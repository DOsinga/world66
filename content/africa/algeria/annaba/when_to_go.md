---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

Annaba has a Mediterranean climate — hot, dry summers and mild, wet winters. Spring (April–May) and autumn (September–October) are the most comfortable times to visit, with temperatures in the low to mid-20s Celsius and limited rain. July and August are hot, often exceeding 35°C, and the city's beaches fill with Algerian tourists escaping the inland heat. Winter is mild by North African standards — rarely below 5°C — but can be grey and wet.

The city has no particular festival calendar that would influence timing. Ramadan, whose dates shift annually, affects restaurant and cafe hours: most places are closed during daylight hours and lively at night.
