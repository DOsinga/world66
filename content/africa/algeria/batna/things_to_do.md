title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Batna is honest about what it offers: a solid base for exploring the Aurès, a market town with a lively central square, and a functional transit point for Timgad. The things worth seeking out here are the Musée de Batna, the Aurès Mountain scenery to the south, and the Roman ruins at Arris. The main draw — Timgad — is covered in Day Trips.
