---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

Batna's altitude — just over 1,000 metres — gives it a more tolerable summer than the Algerian coast or the Sahara. July and August are hot but rarely brutal, and the evenings cool down noticeably. Spring (April–May) and autumn (September–October) are the best months: mild temperatures, clear skies, and the mountain wildflowers in spring make the Aurès at its most photogenic.

Winter is cold and sometimes snowy. The highlands get real winters — overnight temperatures drop well below zero in January, and the road south into the Aurès can be difficult after heavy snow. If you are coming specifically for Timgad, the site is open year-round, and visiting in winter has the advantage of having it nearly to yourself.

Ramadan affects opening hours and restaurant schedules significantly. Most eating establishments do not serve food during daylight hours, though the evenings become lively.
