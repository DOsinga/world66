The reason most travellers come to Béchar is to push on south into the Saoura. **[Taghit](/africa/algeria/taghit)**, 90 km away on a paved road, is the spectacular dune oasis that pairs a palm grove and a ksar against the towering Erg Occidental — one of Algeria's most photogenic places and an easy day trip if you have transport.

Further out, **Beni Abbès** (another 250 km south) sits on the edge of the same dune sea and has the chapel-and-hermitage of the French priest Charles de Foucauld. The rock art of the **Djebel Béchar** and the **Pinacles de Béchar** — strange wind-eroded sandstone columns in the desert north of the city — can both be done as half-day excursions with a local guide.
