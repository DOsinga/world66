---
title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Béchar is a city to pass through rather than to linger in, but a half-day works for the [Béchar Museum](/africa/algeria/bechar/bechar_museum), the [colonial old quarter](/africa/algeria/bechar/old_quarter) with its arcaded boulevards, and the morning [souk](/africa/algeria/bechar/souk) that draws traders from across the Saoura region. The real highlights are out of town: the dune oases of [Taghit](/africa/algeria/taghit) and Beni Abbès to the south, and the long, scenic train ride down from Oran across the High Plateaux.
