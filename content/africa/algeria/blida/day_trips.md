The two obvious day-trip targets are uphill (Chréa, the Chiffa Gorges, and the cedar forests) or back toward the capital. **Algiers** itself is only 45 minutes away by train, and its Casbah, the Bardo Museum, and the Mediterranean corniche are all easily covered in a day.

**Tipasa**, on the coast an hour west, has one of the most spectacular Roman archaeological sites in North Africa — basilicas, temples, and a theatre overlooking the sea, all UNESCO-listed. Combine with a fish lunch at the small port. From Blida, the easiest route is via the autoroute through Koléa.
