---
title: Getting There
type: section
---

There are essentially two ways into Borj Mokhtar: a flight from the north, or a very long drive across the Sahara. There is no third option, and neither is comfortable.

**By air.** [Bordj Badji Mokhtar Airport](/africa/algeria/borjmokhtar/bordj_badji_mokhtar_airport) (IATA: BMW) has occasional Air Algérie and Tassili Airlines flights from [Adrar](/africa/algeria/adrar), with onward connections to [Algiers](/africa/algeria/algiers). The schedule is sparse — sometimes one or two flights a week — and cancellations are routine. Book early and stay flexible.

**By road from the north.** From [Adrar](/africa/algeria/adrar) the route runs south through Reggane and then a long, mostly empty 650 km piste to Borj Mokhtar. The road is tarmac in stretches and graded sand in others; allow two long days in a 4WD, more if you stop to wait out wind. Fuel is available in Reggane and again in Borj Mokhtar but not in between. Carry food, water, a satellite phone, and spares.

**By road from the south.** Coming up from [Gao](/africa/mali/gao) in Mali, the desert track climbs through Tessalit to the Malian border post and then on to Borj Mokhtar. This crossing has been closed for years at a time, so confirm before you set out.
