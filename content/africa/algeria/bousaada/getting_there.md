---
title: "Getting There"
type: section
---

Bou Saâda is about 250 km south of Algiers via the N8 highway, a journey of around three hours by car. The road passes through M'Sila and climbs through the Atlas before descending to the oasis valley.

There is a small airport (Ain Oussera Airport is the nearest scheduled service, about 80 km to the north). Most visitors arrive by road from Algiers. Shared taxis and buses connect Bou Saâda to M'Sila and Algiers, though the services are not frequent.

From Algiers, the practical options are a rented car or a private taxi. Shared grand taxis from the Algiers terminal are cheaper but require a change at M'Sila.
