---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

The best times to visit are spring (March-April) and autumn (October-November), when temperatures are moderate — typically 20-25°C in the daytime — and the light is excellent. In spring the palm groves are lush and the desert flowers briefly.

Summer is brutal: daytime temperatures regularly reach 40-45°C and the dunes are inaccessible at midday. If you visit in summer, plan all outdoor activities for early morning or after 4pm.

Winter is pleasant enough (10-15°C in the day) but some facilities may operate reduced hours. The date harvest is in autumn, when the markets are at their most interesting.
