---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

Spring (March to May) is the best time to visit. Temperatures are mild, the coastal scenery is green, and the light is good for the open-air ruins. Wildflowers often appear along the roadside between Algiers and Cherchell in March and April.

Summer is hot and dry. The museum is pleasant enough in the heat, but wandering the outdoor sites in July or August is uncomfortable. The town does get some Algerian domestic tourism in summer, though it remains quiet compared to more established beach resorts.

Autumn is another good window — cooler than summer but still warm and largely dry. Winter is mild by North African standards but can bring rain and overcast skies along the coast.

Cherchell has no major festivals, and there is nothing that makes a specific date essential. It works equally well as a day trip at any time of year when the weather cooperates.
