The ruins of the Antonian Roman aqueduct are a small but meaningful trace of Constantine's ancient infrastructure. In a city better known for bridges and ravines, the remains point to an older Roman layer beneath the dramatic modern skyline.

This is not a polished archaeological park, so expectations should be modest. It works best for visitors already interested in Constantine's deep history, or as a quick stop while exploring the city's viewpoints and older quarters.
