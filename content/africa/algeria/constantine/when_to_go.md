---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

Constantine sits at 660 metres on its plateau, which gives it a more temperate climate than coastal Algeria. Summers (June to August) are hot — 35°C and above — but drier than the coast, and evenings are pleasant. Winters (December to February) can be cold enough for frost and occasional snow, which gives the gorge and bridges a genuinely dramatic appearance when covered in light snow. Spring (March to May) and autumn (September to November) are the most comfortable seasons for walking: warm days, cool nights, and the best light.

Algeria is a straightforward country to visit for independent travelers willing to do paperwork: visas are required for most nationalities and must be obtained in advance. The city is conservative in dress expectations; the usual North African courtesies apply.
