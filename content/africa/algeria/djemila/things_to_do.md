---

Djemila is an archaeological site first and foremost — a UNESCO-listed Roman town frozen in remarkable preservation. The key monuments are the [Arch of Caracalla](/africa/algeria/djemila/arch_of_caracalla), the [Old Forum](/africa/algeria/djemila/old_forum), the [Christian Basilica](/africa/algeria/djemila/christian_basilica), and the [Djemila Museum](/africa/algeria/djemila/djemila_museum) with its extraordinary floor mosaics. Plan at least a half day to walk the site properly; the combination of the ruins and the mountain landscape is genuinely stunning.
