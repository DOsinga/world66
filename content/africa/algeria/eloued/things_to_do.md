---
title: Things to Do
type: section
---

El Oued's main draw is the town itself — wandering the alleys of the old city under the white-domed roofs, watching the desert light play across the sand-coloured walls. The [Souf Museum](/africa/algeria/eloued/souf_museum) covers the history and ethnography of the region. The [central market](/africa/algeria/eloued/central_market) is the social heart, busiest on Thursday. Just outside town, the [Sidi Salem mosque](/africa/algeria/eloued/sidi_salem_mosque) and the surrounding [ghout date palm gardens](/africa/algeria/eloued/ghout_gardens) — sunken plantations dug down to the water table — are the iconic Souf valley landscapes. For desert excursions into the [Grand Erg Oriental](/africa/algeria/eloued/grand_erg_oriental), local guides run trips ranging from a few hours to several days.
