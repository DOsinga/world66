---
title: Things to Do
type: section
---

The M'zab Valley's five fortified cities are the reason to come to Ghardaia. Each ksar is a distinct urban organism built to a religious and social logic that Le Corbusier studied — concentric rings of houses narrowing toward a mosque and minaret at the summit. A guide is mandatory for most of the old towns; the Mozabite communities are private and the restrictions are genuine, not performative.
