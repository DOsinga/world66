The city sits in the middle of a region of ghout oasis gardens — palm groves planted in bowl-shaped depressions that reach down to the water table — which produce some of the finest dates in Algeria. El Oued's covered market is active and largely untouched by tourism, selling produce, spices, and textiles to a local clientele."
---

The main Algerian entry point to the Grand Erg Oriental and the capital of El Oued Province. The city's distinctive domed architecture — built to resist sand accumulation — makes it architecturally unlike anywhere else in the Sahara. The surrounding ghout oasis gardens, where date palms are grown in depressions reaching the water table, are unusual and worth seeing. Accommodation is basic but available; guides for erg excursions can be arranged through most hotels.
