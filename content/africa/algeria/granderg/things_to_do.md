---
title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

The Grand Erg Oriental is experienced through its entry points — there is no visitor infrastructure inside the erg itself, only dunes and, eventually, the Tunisian border. From El Oued on the Algerian side, guides and 4WD operators run day trips into the dunes south of the city, which give a real sense of the scale of the erg without requiring a multi-day expedition. The erg begins almost immediately outside El Oued — you can walk from the city edge into dune country in under an hour. Camel treks operating out of Douz, across the border in Tunisia, reach deeper into the erg on overnight itineraries and are better organised for independent travellers.

The most rewarding time in the erg is the two hours around dawn. The dunes are cold (temperatures swing 30 degrees between night and day in winter), the light is directional enough to make the sand ripples dramatic, and the wind that builds through the afternoon has not yet started. If you are staying in a desert camp, wake up early.

The town of El Oued itself is worth a day: the domed architecture (each dome keeps the sand out and the interior cool), the covered souk, the local market that sells dates from the nearby ghout oasis gardens. Touggourt, a larger oasis town to the west, is a useful transit stop with a few colonial-era buildings and a palm-lined promenade around the central square.
