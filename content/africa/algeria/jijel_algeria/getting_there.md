title: Getting There
type: section
---

Jijel Ferhat Abbas Airport sits east of the city and handles limited domestic flights, but schedules are not something to build a tight trip around. Most travellers arrive by road from [Constantine](/africa/algeria/constantine), [Setif](/africa/algeria/setif), [Bejaia](/africa/algeria/bejaia), or [Algiers](/africa/algeria/algiers).

The coastal road from Bejaia is the scenic approach, especially along the corniche, but it is slower than it looks on a map. If you are driving, leave daylight for the curves and viewpoints; if you are using buses or shared taxis, expect a practical trip rather than a comfortable one.
