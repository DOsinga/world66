Taza National Park protects a compact but wonderfully varied stretch of the Jijel coast and mountains: cork oak forest, cliffs, caves, hidden beaches, and the wooded Guerrouch massif. It is one of Algeria's smaller national parks, but it packs a lot into a short distance.

The park is best for travellers who like scenery with some rough edges. Trails and facilities are limited, and you should not expect a polished visitor centre experience. The reward is a rare combination of Mediterranean coast and mountain forest, with Barbary macaques, birdlife, and cooler air away from the beach crowds.
