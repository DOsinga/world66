The [Fort of Santa Cruz](/africa/algeria/oran/santa_cruz_fort), perched on Jebel Murdjadjo above the bay, is Oran's most visible landmark — a 16th-century Spanish fortification with a winding road up and sweeping views over the city. The adjoining [Chapel of Santa Cruz](/africa/algeria/oran/santa_cruz_chapel) is a popular pilgrimage and viewpoint.

In the city itself, the colonial-era [Place du 1er Novembre](/africa/algeria/oran/place_du_1er_novembre) is the heart of the French quarter, surrounded by Haussmannian buildings and the ornate Théâtre Régional. The old Ottoman quarter of [Sidi El Houari](/africa/algeria/oran/sidi_el_houari), with the [Bey's Palace](/africa/algeria/oran/beys_palace) and the [Hassan Pasha Mosque](/africa/algeria/oran/hassan_pasha_mosque), is being slowly restored and offers the most atmospheric walking in the city.

The [Ahmed Zabana Museum](/africa/algeria/oran/ahmed_zabana_museum) is the principal museum, covering Maghrebi prehistory and ethnography. The [Sacred Heart Cathedral](/africa/algeria/oran/sacred_heart_cathedral) — now a public library — and the [Abdellah Ben Salem Mosque](/africa/algeria/oran/abdellah_ben_salem_mosque) (a converted 19th-century synagogue) capture the layered religious history of the city.
