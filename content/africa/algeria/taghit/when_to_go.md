---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

Taghit is best visited between October and April. Summer temperatures in the Sahara regularly exceed 45°C and can reach 50°C, making outdoor activity genuinely dangerous. Winter nights in the desert can drop below freezing, so bring warm layers for evenings.

The ideal time is November to February, when daytime temperatures are warm rather than hot (18–25°C), the light on the dunes is at its most photogenic, and the oasis gardens are at their most vivid. Ramadan, the dates of which shift annually, is observed strictly across Algeria — plan accordingly for food and restaurant hours. Algeria's national holidays are also worth checking, as access to some sites may be restricted.
