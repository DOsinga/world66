Tamanrasset — usually just called "Tam" — is the main town of the deep Algerian Sahara, sitting at about 1,400 metres on the edge of the Hoggar Mountains. It is the travel hub of the south: the starting point for expeditions into the [Hoggar Mountains](/africa/algeria/tamanrasset/hoggar_mountains) volcanic plateau, the Tassili du Hoggar, and the ergs beyond.

The town itself is a dusty, spread-out place with a Tuareg character quite different from northern Algeria. The Hoggar landscape around it is extraordinary — black basalt peaks, sand-filled valleys, and the volcanic pinnacle of [Assekrem](/africa/algeria/tamanrasset/assekrem), where Charles de Foucauld built his hermitage and where the sunrise over the Sahara is one of Algeria's iconic sights. You will need a local guide and a 4x4 for any trip outside town.
