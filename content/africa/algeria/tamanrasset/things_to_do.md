---
title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Tamanrasset itself is a functional desert town without major sights of its own. The attraction is what surrounds it: the volcanic peaks and valleys of the [Hoggar Mountains](/africa/algeria/tamanrasset/hoggar_mountains), best explored on multi-day 4x4 expeditions with a local guide.

The unmissable destination is [Assekrem](/africa/algeria/tamanrasset/assekrem), an 80-kilometre journey on rough tracks to a high mountain pass at 2,780 metres where Charles de Foucauld built his hermitage in 1911. Arrive late afternoon, sleep near the pass, and wake before dawn for the Saharan sunrise — one of the most extraordinary sights in North Africa. Serious walkers can continue to [Mount Tahat](/africa/algeria/tamanrasset/mount_tahat), Algeria's highest peak at 2,908 metres, most easily approached from the Assekrem area.

Further afield and requiring a dedicated expedition, the [Amguid Crater](/africa/algeria/tamanrasset/amguid_crater) is a near-perfect meteorite impact crater about 250 kilometres north of town — remote even by Saharan standards, and visited by very few people.

In town, the market is worth a wander for Tuareg silver jewellery, leather goods, and the distinctive blue and white clothing of the Kel Ahaggar Tuareg. All excursions outside Tamanrasset require a registered guide and permits — arrange these through one of the tour agencies in town before you arrive.
