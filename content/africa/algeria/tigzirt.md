Tigzirt is a small coastal town in [Algeria](/africa/algeria), close to Tizi Ouzou in the Kabyle region. It used to be a popular seaside getaway for Kabyle families, and the stony beach and relaxed atmosphere still draw visitors in summer.

The main historic attraction is the [Basilique Chrétienne](/africa/algeria/tigzirt/basilique_chretienne), a 4th-century Christian basilica in the centre of town with well-preserved mosaic floors. The ancient Roman town of Iomnium occupied this site, and its ruins — forum foundations, columns, and civic buildings — are scattered around the modern settlement. The [beach](/africa/algeria/tigzirt/plage_de_tigzirt) is sheltered and pleasant, best visited from June through September when the weather and sea conditions are reliably good.
