---
title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Timgad is essentially one large open-air museum — the entire site is what you come to see. Budget at least three hours, more in spring when wildflowers soften the ruins. The main street, the theatre, the forum, and Trajan's Arch are all walkable in sequence. The on-site museum is a necessary last stop; don't shortchange it.
