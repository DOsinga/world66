---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

Spring (March to May) is the best time to visit Timgad. The Aurès Mountains are still green, wildflowers grow between the ruins, and temperatures are comfortable for a long walk around the open site — midday highs around 18–22°C. Autumn (September to October) is nearly as good, with clearer skies and fewer visitors.

Summer is hot and dry, with temperatures regularly exceeding 35°C at this 1,000-metre altitude. The site is bearable in early morning but exhausting by midday. Winter brings genuine cold — temperatures can drop below freezing at night — and occasional snow in the mountains. The site stays open but the drive from Batna can be tricky after a storm.
