---
title: When to Go
type: section
---

The cooler months — late October to early April — are the only realistic time. Days then are pleasant (15–25°C), nights cold (often near freezing in January) and the desert sky is clear. The summer heat from May to September is brutal, with daytime highs over 45°C, and sandstorms become more common.

The date harvest in October and the *Sebiba* and *zerda* festivals tied to the local Sufi calendar are particular drawcards. The big regional date festival happens in Timimoun itself, usually in October, and is a good time to be in town — music, markets, and an influx of visitors from across the Gourara.
