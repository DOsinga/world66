---
title: "Getting There"
type: section
---

The realistic route into Tindouf is by air. **Commandant Ferradj Airport**, just north of the town, has scheduled Air Algérie flights to and from Algiers and occasional connections to Oran; the flights run several times a week and are heavily booked by government, military, and aid traffic. Reserve well in advance.

Overland travel to Tindouf is possible but slow and bureaucratically complex. The paved road from [Bechar](/africa/algeria/bechar) runs about 750 kilometres through the open desert; long-distance buses run a few times a week and the journey takes roughly fifteen hours. Foreigners on overland routes need a *permis de voyage* from the wilaya, arranged in advance through a registered agency. The land border with Morocco west of Tindouf has been closed for decades and is not open to civilians; the border with Mauritania to the south can in principle be crossed (the old route through Zouerate) but is rarely used and politically sensitive. Plan for a flight unless you have very specific reasons not to.
