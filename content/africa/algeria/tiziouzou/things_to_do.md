Tizi Ouzou's real attractions lie in its Kabyle cultural heritage and the mountain landscapes that surround it. The city centre offers a handful of worthwhile sights, while the wider region — Djurdjura National Park, traditional hilltop villages, and the nearby coast — provides the material for days of exploration. The filter below covers museums, landmarks, and the natural environment that make this corner of Algeria unlike anywhere else.
