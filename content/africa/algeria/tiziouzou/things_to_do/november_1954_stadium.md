Home ground of JS Kabylie, one of the most successful football clubs in Algerian history and a potent symbol of Kabyle identity. The stadium is named for the date of the start of the Algerian War of Independence — 1 November 1954 — but attending a match here is about more than football. The crowd waves Amazigh flags and sings in Kabyle; the atmosphere is electric and distinctly political, a weekly assertion of Berber cultural pride.

JS Kabylie has won the African Cup Winners' Cup twice and the CAF Cup once, making them one of the most decorated clubs on the continent. If your visit coincides with a home fixture, it is one of the most memorable experiences you can have in the region. Check the Algerian Ligue Professionnelle schedule in advance.
