Touggourt is a Saharan oasis city in eastern [Algeria](/africa/algeria), strung along a chain of palm-fringed salt lakes at the northern edge of the Grand Erg Oriental. For centuries it was a caravan hub connecting the Mediterranean coast with the deep Sahara, and the capital of a small independent kingdom that resisted both Ottoman and French control longer than most.

The [old ksar quarter](/africa/algeria/touggourt/ksar_old_town) is the most interesting part of town — a dense cluster of sand-coloured buildings with a ruined palace and the [tombs of the former kings of the Beni Djellab dynasty](/africa/algeria/touggourt/royal_tombs), beside the historic [Sidi Salim Mosque](/africa/algeria/touggourt/sidi_salim_mosque). The [central market](/africa/algeria/touggourt/central_market) is lively, trading in dates, spices, and desert roses (natural gypsum crystal formations found in the surrounding sand). Touggourt's [palm groves](/africa/algeria/touggourt/palm_groves) are extensive and still produce some of the best Deglet Nour dates in Algeria.

The town is a useful staging point for visits to [El Oued](/africa/algeria/eloued), about 160 km to the east, and for the northern fringes of the [Grand Erg Oriental](/africa/algeria/touggourt/grand_erg_oriental). The landscape between the two cities is extraordinary — flat, sun-blasted plains giving way to the first dunes of the great sand sea. Touggourt is reached by road from [Biskra](/africa/algeria/biskra) (about 230 km south) or by air from [Algiers](/africa/algeria/algiers), with occasional flights on Air Algérie.
