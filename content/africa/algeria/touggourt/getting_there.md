---
title: "Getting There"
type: section
---

Touggourt has a small airport with occasional flights from [Algiers](/africa/algeria/algiers) on Air Algérie — the easiest way in. By road, it is reached from [Biskra](/africa/algeria/biskra) (230 km north on the trans-Saharan highway) or from [El Oued](/africa/algeria/eloued) (160 km east). The railway line from the north terminates in Touggourt, but services are slow and best avoided.

Travel to the southern oases beyond Touggourt — Hassi Messaoud, Ouargla, and onward — requires permits and, for foreigners, often an escorted tour. Check current requirements before setting out.
