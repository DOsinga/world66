The Igreja de Nossa Senhora do Pópulo is the main Catholic church of Benguela's colonial centre, a whitewashed Baroque structure that has been rebuilt and restored several times since the original 17th-century construction. It faces a pleasant square in the old town and is still an active parish church.

The interior has some interesting ecclesiastical art and a calm atmosphere that makes it worth stepping inside for a few minutes. The church square is a good starting point for a walk through the colonial grid of streets surrounding it — the mix of preserved facades, occupied ruins, and new construction tells you a great deal about the pace of Angola's reconstruction.
