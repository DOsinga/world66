---
title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Benguela's main sights cluster around the colonial city centre and the waterfront. The fortress, the churches, and the old railway buildings give the city its historical texture; the renovated promenade and the beaches to the south are where people actually spend their time. For most visitors, Benguela is a two-day city — long enough to see everything worth seeing and short enough to leave wanting more.
