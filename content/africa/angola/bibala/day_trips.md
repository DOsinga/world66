Bibala works best as part of a southern Angola route. [Namibe](/africa/angola/namibe) gives you the desert coast and Atlantic port life, while [Lubango](/africa/angola/lubango) has the stronger highland base, the Tundavala Gap, and the upper end of the Serra da Leba road.
