---
title: When to Go
type: section
---

Cabinda has a tropical climate with two rainy seasons. The short rains fall roughly October to December, and the longer rainy season runs March to May. The dry season — June through September — is the most comfortable time to visit, with lower humidity and clearer forest conditions. This is also when the roads into the Maiombe Forest are most passable.

Temperatures stay warm year-round, typically 25–32°C, with higher humidity during the wet seasons making the heat feel oppressive. The forest is most atmospheric during the rains when the canopy is at its lushest, but access becomes difficult.

There are no major festivals or events specifically oriented towards visitors. If your timing is flexible, aim for July or August.
