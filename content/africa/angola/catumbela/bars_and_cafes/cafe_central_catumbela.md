A daytime cafe that doubles as a bar from mid-afternoon. Come in the morning for bica (strong espresso) and pão com manteiga before the heat builds; return in the afternoon for a cold beer and whatever snacks the counter has on offer. The tables spill onto the pavement and the cafe is a useful meeting point in a town with few obvious landmarks.
