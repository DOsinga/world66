A fishermen's tasca near the estuary end of town, the kind of place that opens at sunrise for the fishing crews and stays open until the last drinker leaves. The walls are decorated with old nets and floats; the beers are cold and cheap. A good spot to catch the late afternoon light over the river and talk to locals who have spent the day on the water.
