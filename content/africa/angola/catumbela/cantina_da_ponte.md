A small cantina near the old railway bridge that serves a short menu of Angolan staples. Muamba de peixe — fish in a palm oil and okra sauce — is the dish to order, ladled over rice with a side of funje. The dining room is basic but the cooking is honest, and the price is right for a filling lunch. Popular with local workers at midday.
