A riverfront spot that makes the most of its location, serving grilled fish pulled from the Catumbela that morning. The house specialty is peixe grelhado com pirão — whole grilled fish with a thick cassava porridge on the side — simple and right. Outdoor tables face the water; go at lunch when the kitchen is at its freshest and the light is good on the river.
