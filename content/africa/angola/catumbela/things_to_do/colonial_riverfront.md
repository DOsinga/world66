Along the northern bank of the Catumbela River, a handful of Portuguese colonial buildings from the late 19th and early 20th centuries survive in varying states of repair. These warehouses and trading houses date from the rubber-boom era, when Catumbela briefly flourished as the staging post for inland trade routes to Bailundo and Bié. The streetscape is modest but atmospheric, especially in the golden late-afternoon light that bounces off the river.
