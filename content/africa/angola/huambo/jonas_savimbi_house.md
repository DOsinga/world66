The ruined house of Jonas Savimbi, the longtime UNITA leader during Angola's civil war, is one of Huambo's more pointed modern-history stops. Bullet marks still scar the walls, and the provincial UNITA office stands nearby.

It is not a polished attraction, but it gives a stark reminder of the war years and why Huambo's recent history still feels close to the surface.
