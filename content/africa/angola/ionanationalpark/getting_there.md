---
title: Getting There
type: section
---

Most access is from [Namibe](/africa/angola/namibe) and Tombwa, with routes continuing south into the park. This is 4x4 territory, and you should get current local information before setting out; tracks, permissions, fuel availability, and security procedures can change.

Independent travel is possible only for well-prepared overlanders. For everyone else, use a local operator or guide who knows the park, the ranger posts, and the current route conditions. Carry more water and fuel than you think you need.
