---
title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

The reason to come to Kissama is the landscape and what wildlife you might see in it. There are no developed visitor facilities beyond the lodge — no organised viewing platforms, no interpretive trails. Game drives in a 4x4, with a guide who knows the tracks, are how the park is seen. Allow at least a full day inside the park; two or three days if you can.
