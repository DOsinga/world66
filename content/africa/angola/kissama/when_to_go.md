---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

The dry season from May to October is the only practical time to visit Kissama. The tracks inside the park become impassable during the rains, the mangroves and grasslands fill with mosquitoes, and the game scatters across the higher ground. Within the dry season, August and September offer the best game viewing — water sources dry up, animals concentrate around remaining pans and the riverbank, and the chances of seeing elephant and buffalo are highest.

The wet season runs roughly November to April. It is not impossible to visit then — birdlife is at its peak with migratory species in residence — but driving the park is unpredictable and many tracks may be closed without warning.
