Estádio do Buraco is Lobito's main football stadium, home to Desportivo do Huambo and other local clubs. The name — "Stadium of the Hole" — comes from its unusual sunken construction: the pitch sits in a natural hollow, giving the stands an enclosed, atmospheric feel that generates real noise on match days.

Football is taken seriously in Angola, and a match at the Buraco is a window into local culture that no museum can replicate. The stadium is about 5 km south of the city centre, in a residential area of the Canata commune. Check local listings or ask at your hotel about fixtures — a weekend afternoon game is worth going out of your way for.
