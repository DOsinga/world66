---
title: Getting There
type: section
---

Lobito is 30 km north of [Benguela](/africa/angola/benguela), connected by a good road. From [Luanda](/africa/angola/luanda), Angola's capital, it is about 520 km by road — roughly six to seven hours in good conditions, though the road quality varies. Shared taxis and minibuses cover the Benguela–Lobito route frequently and cheaply. Lobito Airport (IATA: LLT) has a short runway and limited services; most air arrivals for the region use Benguela's airport instead.
