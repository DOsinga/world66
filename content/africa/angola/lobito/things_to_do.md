title: Things to Do
type: section
---

Lobito's main attraction is walking the [Restinga](/africa/angola/lobito/the_restinga) — the long sandbar that defines the city's geography. The [Port of Lobito](/africa/angola/lobito/port_of_lobito) is the most historically significant site, the terminus of the Benguela Railway that once connected the Atlantic to central Africa's copper belt. The [Estádio do Buraco](/africa/angola/lobito/estadio_do_buraco) hosts local football and gives a sense of the city's passionate sporting culture.
