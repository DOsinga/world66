The National Museum of Anthropology is one of Luanda’s most useful cultural stops, with collections that introduce Angolan masks, tools, ritual objects, musical instruments, and everyday material culture. It gives visitors context that the city’s waterfront and colonial buildings cannot provide on their own.

The museum is modest by international standards, but its subject matter is essential for understanding Angola beyond oil-era Luanda. Go with patience for uneven presentation and focus on the objects, which carry the strongest stories.
