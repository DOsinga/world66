The 1st of May Stadium is Malanje's main sports venue, used by the provincial clubs in the Girabola and Segundona — Angola's top two football divisions. It holds around 25,000 and on match days fills with drums, horns, and impressive vocal support from local fans.

You do not need to be a football enthusiast to enjoy a game here; it is one of the few places in Malanje where the city's collective energy concentrates in one spot. Buy a ticket from a tout outside the gates, sit on the cheap side, and let the noise carry the afternoon.
