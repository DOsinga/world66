---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

The best time to visit Mussulo is during the dry season, which runs roughly from May to October. Temperatures are mild and the sky is often overcast — a characteristic of the Benguela-influenced Luanda coast — but rain is rare and the lagoon is calm. The slight haze that settles over the coast in these months can make the light feel soft and flat, but the beach conditions are reliably good.

The wet season (November to April) brings higher humidity, occasional heavy downpours, and rougher surf. The beaches are less crowded on weekdays during the wet season, which has its own appeal for those who prefer solitude.

Weekends year-round see large numbers of day-trippers from Luanda. If you want a quieter experience, come on a weekday. Public holidays and long weekends in Angola can make Mussulo extremely busy, with boats running continually from Capisoca.
