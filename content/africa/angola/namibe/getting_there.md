---
title: "Getting There"
type: section
---

Namibe has a small airport (Yuri Gagarin Airport) with domestic flights to Luanda. TAAG Angola Airlines operates the route; flights are infrequent so book ahead. The drive from Luanda is about 800 km and takes 10–12 hours on generally good but sometimes rough roads through the interior highlands. From [Lubango](/africa/angola/lubango), the nearest major city, it is about 250 km by road. Car hire in Luanda or Lubango is the most flexible option; a four-wheel drive is strongly recommended if you plan to leave the main road.
