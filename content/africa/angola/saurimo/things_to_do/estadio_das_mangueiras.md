Saurimo's main football ground — named after the mango trees that shade the surrounding streets — holds around 7,000 spectators and is home to Progresso da Lunda Sul, the province's club in the national Girabola league. If a match is on, it is worth attending: Angolan football crowds are passionate and the stadium doubles as a community gathering point. The renovated facility on the Agostinho Neto boulevard is easy to find and the match-day atmosphere gives a glimpse of local life that most visitors miss.
