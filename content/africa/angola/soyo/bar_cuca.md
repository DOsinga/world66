A typical Angolan neighbourhood bar near the town centre, serving cold Cuca beer, Sagres, and soft drinks. The name reflects the drink that dominates the menu at establishments like this across Angola. Plastic chairs, a concrete floor, and a fridge behind the counter are the standard fixtures. In the evenings, it fills with local workers winding down after a shift.

Don't expect cocktails or an extensive menu — this is a place for a cold beer and conversation. Prices are reasonable by Soyo standards.
