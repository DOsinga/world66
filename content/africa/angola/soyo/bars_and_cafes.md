---
title: "Bars and Cafes"
type: section
---

Soyo's bar scene is modest. A few informal bars and canteens serve cold Cuca (the local Angolan beer) and soft drinks, and some operate late into the evening. The atmosphere reflects the town's demographics — predominantly male, oil industry workers and local men — and things can get lively on Friday and Saturday nights. Women travelling alone should be aware of the context, though overt hostility is uncommon.

There are no Western-style cafes. For coffee, your guesthouse kitchen or a general store is the more realistic option.
