---
title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Abomey's main draw is the UNESCO World Heritage [Royal Palaces](/africa/benin/abomey/royal_palaces), which includes the [Abomey Historical Museum](/africa/benin/abomey/historical_museum) within its grounds. The bas-relief carved walls and the royal regalia are the highlight. A guide is strongly recommended — the site has complex history and the museum labels are limited. Outside the palace complex, [vodun temples](/africa/benin/abomey/vodun_temples) are accessible in and around town, including a notable chameleon temple reachable with a local guide.
