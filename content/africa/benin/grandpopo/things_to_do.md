---
title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Grand-Popo's pleasures are unhurried ones — the beach, the river, the voodoo culture, the mangroves. There is no formal tourism infrastructure to speak of, which is part of the appeal. Most of what you want to do requires either walking out the door or hiring a pirogue.

The Bouche du Roy, where the Mono river meets the Atlantic, is the most visited natural site near the town — a wide, wild estuary with fishing villages, mangroves, and good birdwatching accessible by pirogue. The beach itself stretches for kilometres in both directions from town, mostly deserted even in high season.
