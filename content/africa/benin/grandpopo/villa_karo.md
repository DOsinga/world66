Villa Karo is an unusual institution for a town this size — a Finnish-Beninese cultural centre established to promote dialogue between West African and European arts and traditions. It runs workshops, exhibitions, and performances, with particular focus on Vodun heritage, local crafts, and the visual arts of the Mono Department.

For visitors arriving in Grand-Popo without prior knowledge of the region, Villa Karo provides invaluable context. The staff can advise on local ceremonies that are open to respectful visitors, direct you to craftspeople and artists, and explain the cultural landscape in a way that a guide picked up by the roadside usually cannot. It is a good first stop in town.
