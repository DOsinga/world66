---
title: "Eating Out"
type: section
---

Ouidah is a small town and the restaurant scene reflects that — there are no high-end dining destinations, but several reliable spots serve good Beninese cooking. Look for *pâte* (a thick maize porridge), grilled fish from the Atlantic, *poulet bicyclette* (free-range chicken), and the spicy *sauce arachide* (peanut sauce). The Afro-Brazilian heritage is still visible in dishes like *feijão* and *acarajé*, brought back from Bahia by returning families in the 19th century.

A handful of small Lebanese-Beninese cafés near the main square serve sandwiches, *chawarma*, and cold drinks at modest prices.
