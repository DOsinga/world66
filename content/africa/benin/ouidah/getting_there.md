---
title: "Getting There"
type: section
---

Ouidah is 40 km west of Cotonou — Benin's commercial capital and the only city with an international airport. From the **Cadjehoun airport (COO)**, a taxi to Ouidah takes about 90 minutes depending on traffic and costs in the region of CFA 25,000–35,000.

The cheapest option is a shared *bush taxi* or minibus (*tro-tro* equivalent) from Cotonou's Étoile Rouge junction, about CFA 1,500–2,500 per seat. Travel time is similar but the experience is rougher; the taxis leave when full. For day-tripping from Cotonou, hiring a car-and-driver for the day (around CFA 60,000) is the standard pattern.

There is no train service. The road west of Ouidah continues to **Grand-Popo** and on to the Togolese border at Hilakondji, about an hour further.
