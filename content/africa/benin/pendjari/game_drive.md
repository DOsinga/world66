The classic Pendjari experience is a slow loop through the park in a 4WD with a local guide, starting at dawn or in the late afternoon when animals are most active. A typical morning drive from the Batia area runs north on the main central track to one of the waterholes, then either east toward the river country or west toward the Porga end of the park, returning to base by late morning before the heat shuts everything down.

The driving is on rough laterite and sand tracks. Independent self-drive is possible in the dry season for confident 4WD drivers, but most visitors hire a vehicle and guide either through their lodge or directly at the park gate — it is not expensive and the guides know where animals have been seen recently, which matters in a park where wildlife density is lower and sightings depend more on local intel than in East African parks. Plan for two or three full days; one is enough to see the park, but enough chances at lion and elephant generally need more.
