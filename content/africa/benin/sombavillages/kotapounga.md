Kotapounga is a small arrondissement north of Boukoumbé where the country opens out into rolling millet fields and scattered baobabs. The settlement pattern here is even more dispersed than at Koussoukoingou — tata compounds stand alone across the landscape, sometimes a kilometre or more apart, each with its own granaries and shrine. The walking between them is half the point.

This is a less-visited corner of the Somba country and a good choice if you want a quieter feel than the more travelled villages further south. A guide is more or less essential because the compounds are not signposted and a friendly approach to a family relies on someone making the introductions in the right language. Half a day is enough; a full day with a packed lunch and time to sit and talk is better.
