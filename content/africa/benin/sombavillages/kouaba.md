Kouaba is one of the more northerly Somba arrondissements, on the route between the Boukoumbé area and the road back up to Natitingou. The tata compounds here sit in slightly drier country, with thorn trees and more open ground between them, and the families are largely subsistence farmers — millet, sorghum, sesame, a few cattle.

For travellers, Kouaba is usually a brief stop rather than a destination of its own — a compound or two visited on the way somewhere else, with a guide who has cousins in the area. It is a good antidote to the more set-piece tour experience of the southern villages, but only really worth it if you have someone who can make the introductions for you.
