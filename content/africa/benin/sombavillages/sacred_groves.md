title: Sacred groves and ancestor shrines
type: poi
---

Scattered through the Somba country are small fenced groves of mature trees that have never been cut — the sacred groves where ancestors are honoured, sacrifices are made, and certain rituals are performed away from the everyday compound. They are not always obvious from the outside (sometimes just a knot of mature trees in an otherwise cleared landscape), and they are not places where visitors can simply wander in.

If your guide thinks it appropriate, you may be shown the edge of a sacred grove and have it explained — what it is for, who maintains it, what kinds of offerings are made and when. Photographing inside is essentially never permitted, and stepping inside without invitation is a serious breach. The same applies to the small ancestor shrines often built into a corner of a tata compound: ask, look respectfully, do not touch. These are some of the most important elements of Batammariba religious life and they are not for tourist consumption.
