---
title: When to Go
type: section
---

Francistown has a semi-arid climate with hot summers and mild, dry winters. The rains come between November and March, when temperatures regularly exceed 35°C and afternoon thunderstorms are common. The rest of the year is dry and considerably more comfortable.

The best time to visit — or pass through — is April to September. Temperatures are pleasant, roads are in better condition after the rainy season has ended, and the drier air makes driving and outdoor time more enjoyable. Winter nights (June–July) can be surprisingly cold, dropping to single figures, so bring a layer if you're sleeping in a tent or a budget room.

Francistown itself has no major festivals that would anchor a visit. If you're timing a trip around game viewing in Chobe or the Okavango, the dry season (May–October) is the standard recommendation: wildlife concentrates around water sources and vegetation is lower, making animals easier to spot.
