Gaborone's position in southern Botswana puts several worthwhile destinations within reach.

**Mochudi**, 35 kilometres north, is one of the oldest settlements in Botswana and has a good ethnographic museum in the old Dutch Reformed Church — a quick detour if you have a car. **Mokolodi Nature Reserve** (10 km south) and the **Gaborone Game Reserve** (within city limits) are both easy half-day options.

For a longer day trip with a 4WD, **Khutse Game Reserve** (240 km northwest) offers an authentic Kalahari experience with far fewer visitors than the northern parks. The **Kgalagadi Transfrontier Park** on the Namibian border requires an overnight stay but is one of the finest wildlife areas in southern Africa. The **Okavango Delta** and Chobe are too far for a day trip from Gaborone but are natural extensions of a Botswana itinerary.
