---
title: "Shopping"
type: section
---

Gaborone's main shopping areas are the **Main Mall** in the centre — a pedestrianised strip with banks, shops, and diplomatic missions — and the newer **Game City** and **Riverwalk** centres, which have more of the international chains.

For crafts and souvenirs, the **Botswana Craft** shops in the Main Mall area carry basketry, pottery, and carved wooden items from across the country. Botswana is particularly known for its woven baskets, which use a traditional technique from the Okavango region — the geometric patterns and tight weave make them genuinely beautiful objects rather than tourist tat.

The **Green Shop** at the Somarelang Tikologo Eco-Park on Kaunda Road sells goods made from recycled or natural materials by local women's groups. The traditional hut setting is unusual for a city centre shop and the products — skincare, accessories, and home furnishings — are locally made and fairly priced.
