Among the better local options in Maun for a meal that doesn't cost lodge prices. The menu leans on grilled meats and stews with Botswana staples — seswaa (pounded beef), bogobe (sorghum porridge), and morogo (cooked wild spinach) appear alongside the more tourist-oriented chicken and chips. It is one of the few places in Maun where you can eat what locals eat rather than a repackaged safari menu.

The setting is informal and the service straightforward. Recommended for budget travellers who want to try genuine Botswana food rather than the international fare served at most lodges. Prices are well below the riverside establishments.
