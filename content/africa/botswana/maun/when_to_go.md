---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

The dry season from May to October is the best time to visit for wildlife. Water recedes, concentrating animals around remaining pools and the delta waterways. Days are warm and mostly cloudless, nights cool in May and June — bring layers. The delta flood, which originates in the Angolan highlands months earlier, typically peaks in Maun around July, filling channels and making mokoro trips spectacular.

November to April is the green season: the bush is lush and the birds are breeding, but some camp access routes flood and a few camps close entirely. Heat and humidity in December to February can be uncomfortable, with afternoon thunderstorms most days. Prices drop significantly in the wet season and the crowds thin out — experienced travellers who can tolerate the conditions find excellent value.

Peak season is July to September, when game viewing is at its most reliable and temperatures are the most agreeable. Book accommodation and light aircraft well in advance for these months.
