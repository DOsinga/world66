Serowe is one of Botswana's largest traditional villages, set against [rocky hills](/africa/botswana/serowe/serowe_hills) to the east. It was the royal capital of the Bangwato people and home to three of the country's most important leaders: Khama III, Tshekedi Khama, and Seretse Khama, Botswana's first president.

The [Khama III Memorial Museum](/africa/botswana/serowe/khama_iii_memorial_museum) houses exhibits on the Bangwato royal dynasty and the Bessie Head collection of manuscripts — Head, one of Africa's most celebrated writers, lived in Serowe for 24 years and set much of her work here. The surrounding hills offer good walks in the morning or late afternoon light.

From Serowe, travellers can head west to the [Central Kalahari Game Reserve](/africa/botswana/ghanzi/central_kalahari_game_reserve) via [Orapa](/africa/botswana/kalaharidesert/orapa), or continue on to [Maun](/africa/botswana/maun) through the Makgadikgadi Pans. The [Khama Rhino Sanctuary](/africa/botswana/serowe/khama_rhino_sanctuary), one of Botswana's community-run conservation successes, is an easy half-day trip.
