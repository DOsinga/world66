The Khama Rhino Sanctuary lies about 30 km south of Serowe and is one of Botswana's most accessible conservation success stories. Run by a community trust representing eight villages, it protects white and black rhinos on around 4,300 hectares of semi-arid thornbush — habitat similar to what you'd find across much of eastern and southern Botswana.

Game drives can be arranged at the sanctuary entrance, and both rhino species can be seen at fairly close range compared with larger parks. Wildebeest, zebra, giraffe, tsessebe, and ostrich are also present. The accommodation and facilities are simple but functional; a self-catering chalet and campsite are available. The sanctuary is best visited as a half-day trip from Serowe rather than an overnight stay unless you are continuing south.
