Banfora is a small city in southwestern Burkina Faso, and it is surrounded by the best natural scenery in the country. The [Cascades de Karfiguéla](/africa/burkinafaso/banfora/cascades_de_karfiguela), about 12 km from town, are a series of waterfalls tumbling through lush vegetation — a rare sight in this part of the Sahel, and a popular swimming spot. The [Dômes de Fabédougou](/africa/burkinafaso/banfora/domes_de_fabedougou), bizarre rock formations eroded into rounded pillars and mushroom shapes, are nearby and well worth the detour.

[Lac de Tengréla](/africa/burkinafaso/banfora/lac_de_tengrela), a shallow lake about 7 km west of town, has a resident population of hippos that can be observed from pirogues — one of the more surreal wildlife experiences in West Africa. The surrounding area is sugarcane country, greener and more fertile than the arid north, with mango trees and baobabs lining the roads. Further west, near the Mali border, the [Pics de Sindou](/africa/burkinafaso/banfora/sindou_peaks) are a long ridge of needle-shaped sandstone pinnacles — a dramatic full-day excursion.

Banfora is reached by train or bus from [Ouagadougou](/africa/burkinafaso/ouagadougou) (about 8 hours) or from [Bobo-Dioulasso](/africa/burkinafaso/bobodioulasso) (about 2 hours). The town itself is quiet and pleasant, with a busy Saturday market, good street food, and a couple of decent places to stay.
