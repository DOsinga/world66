---
title: Getting There
type: section
---

Banfora is on the main rail line and the main road between [Ouagadougou](/africa/burkinafaso/ouagadougou) and Abidjan — most travellers come either by train from Ouagadougou (around 8 hours) or by bus from [Bobo-Dioulasso](/africa/burkinafaso/bobodioulasso) (about 90 minutes on a good day). Sitarail still runs a slow service between Ouaga and Abidjan that stops in Banfora; check current schedules in town, as the line is sometimes interrupted.

Buses leave Bobo-Dioulasso for Banfora roughly every hour from the main gare routière. Several companies (TCV, Rakieta, STAF) cover the route. The fare is a few thousand CFA. Bush taxis are an option but slower and less comfortable.

Banfora has a small airstrip but no scheduled flights. The nearest international airport is Bobo-Dioulasso, with onward connections via Ouagadougou.
