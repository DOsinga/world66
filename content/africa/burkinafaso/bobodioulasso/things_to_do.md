title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Bobo-Dioulasso's highlights are compact and walkable. The Grande Mosquée and the old Kibidwé quarter are the main draws, along with the Musée Provincial du Houët and the imposing twin-towered cathedral. The city is also a good base for visiting the sacred catfish pools at Dafra, the rock village of Koro, and the Sindou Peaks to the west.
