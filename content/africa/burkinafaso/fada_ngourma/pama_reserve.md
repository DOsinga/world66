Pama Reserve lies southeast of Fada N'gourma and forms part of the larger protected landscape running toward Benin and Niger. It is savanna country rather than a polished safari park, with elephants, hippos, big cats, and hundreds of plant species recorded in the wider reserve.

Treat this as a serious expedition rather than an easy day out. Roads, access, security, and local permissions all need current checking before travel.
