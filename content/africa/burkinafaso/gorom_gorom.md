*Africa > Burkina Faso > Gorom-Gorom*

Gorom-Gorom is a Sahel market town in far northern Burkina Faso, close to Mali and Niger and shaped by Tuareg, Fulani, Songhai, and Mossi trade. Its name is often translated as an invitation to sit down together, which fits a place long used as a meeting point for desert-edge communities.

The reason to come, if conditions allow, is the [Thursday market](/africa/burkinafaso/gorom_gorom/thursday_market). It is one of Burkina Faso's great market gatherings, with livestock, leatherwork, silver, food, cloth, and people arriving from villages across Oudalan. The town itself is dusty and plain, but on market day it has the compressed energy of the Sahel.

Travel here needs a hard safety check. Northern Burkina Faso has suffered repeated attacks and displacement during the country's insurgency, and Gorom-Gorom may be unsafe or unreachable. If you are already travelling with reliable local advice, the nearby [Mare d'Oursi](/africa/burkinafaso/gorom_gorom/mare_doursi) and [Markoye rock engravings](/africa/burkinafaso/gorom_gorom/markoye_rock_engravings) show why the wider Oudalan region once drew adventurous overland travellers.
