Saint Augustine Cathedral is one of Koudougou's central landmarks and the seat of the local Roman Catholic diocese. It is not a grand cathedral by European standards, but it helps explain the city's layered religious life, where Catholic institutions sit alongside mosques, traditional authority, and everyday market culture.

It is most useful as a short stop while walking between the centre and the market. Go respectfully, and avoid interrupting services.
