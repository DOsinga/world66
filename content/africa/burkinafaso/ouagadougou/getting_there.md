---
title: "Getting There"
type: section
---

Thomas Sankara International Airport sits on the southern edge of the city, about 5 km from the centre. Air France flies in from Paris; Royal Air Maroc, Brussels Airlines, Air Algérie and a handful of West African carriers also serve the airport. A taxi to the centre is around 5,000 CFA.

By road, the principal connections are south to [Bobo Dioulasso](/africa/burkinafaso/bobodioulasso) (about 5 hours by bus) and on to the coast through Ghana or Côte d'Ivoire. Eastbound routes to Niger and northbound to Mali have been closed or heavily disrupted for years by jihadist activity — check current advice before attempting either.

Border crossings to Togo and Benin are open and reasonably reliable; both involve long bus journeys with multiple changes.
