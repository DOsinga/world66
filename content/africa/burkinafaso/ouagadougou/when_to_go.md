---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

Ouaga has three seasons. **November to February** is the cool dry season — days in the high 20s to mid-30s, nights cool enough for a jumper, dust on the horizon from the Harmattan wind. This is the best time to visit. February in odd-numbered years brings FESPACO, the pan-African film festival, and turns the city into the capital of African cinema for a week.

**March to May** is the hot dry season. Daytime temperatures regularly exceed 40°C and the air is thick with dust. Avoid this period unless you have to come.

**June to October** is the wet season, with most rain in July and August. The countryside greens up dramatically, the air clears, and afternoon storms cool things down — though the heat and the humidity together can be oppressive.
