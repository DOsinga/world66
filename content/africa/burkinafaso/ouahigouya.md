Ouahigouya is the capital of the Yatenga province in northern Burkina Faso, and the third-largest city in the country. It was once the seat of a powerful Mossi kingdom, and the [Yatenga Naaba](/africa/burkinafaso/ouahigouya/yatenga_naaba_palace) (chief) still holds court and performs traditional ceremonies. The city sits in the dry Sahel zone, flat and dusty, with a [large market](/africa/burkinafaso/ouahigouya/grand_marche) that draws traders from the surrounding villages and from across the border in Mali.

Ouahigouya is not on most tourist itineraries, but for travellers exploring Burkina Faso beyond [Ouagadougou](/africa/burkinafaso/ouagadougou) and [Bobo-Dioulasso](/africa/burkinafaso/bobodioulasso), it offers a glimpse of Sahel town life and Mossi culture. The market is lively and colourful, the people are welcoming, and [the surrounding countryside](/africa/burkinafaso/ouahigouya/surroundings) — eroded laterite hills dotted with baobabs — has a stark beauty. The [Grande Mosquée](/africa/burkinafaso/ouahigouya/grande_mosquee) is the city's most distinctive building.
