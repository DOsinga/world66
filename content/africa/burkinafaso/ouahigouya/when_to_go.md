---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

The best time to visit Ouahigouya is during the cooler dry season, from November to February, when temperatures are bearable (around 25–30°C during the day) and the harmattan wind brings hazy but not unbearable conditions. The hottest period is March to May, when temperatures regularly exceed 40°C — punishing for travel.

The rainy season (June–September) brings relief from the heat and turns the landscape green, but roads can become impassable and travel is harder. The big weekly market and traditional ceremonies tied to the agricultural cycle are worth timing a visit around if you can — ask locally about the schedule of the Yatenga Naaba's public appearances.
