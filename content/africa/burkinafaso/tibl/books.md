**Pascal James Imperato** has written extensively on West African art and culture, with material relevant to the Voltaic basin and the ethnic groups of southern Burkina Faso and northern Ghana; his historical surveys of the region remain useful reference works.

**Susan Domowitz** produced important scholarship on Kassena art and textiles, focusing on the symbolic content of surface decoration in this part of West Africa — directly relevant to what you will see in Tiébélé.

**Africa Overland** by Sian Pritchard-Jones and Bob Gibbons (Trailblazer) covers the practical realities of overlanding through West and Central Africa and includes coverage of Burkina Faso; useful for context on getting around the region.

**Thomas A. Hale**, *Griots and Griottes: Masters of Words and Music* — a study of the West African oral tradition that provides cultural background on the Sahelian and sub-Sahelian societies of which Kassena culture is a part.
