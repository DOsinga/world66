title: Day trips
type: section
---

**Pô** is the nearest town, about 15km north of Tiébélé on the main road to Ouagadougou. It has markets, fuel, basic guesthouses, and services that the village lacks; it is the natural base if you want to spend more than a day in the area.

**Nazinga Game Ranch** lies roughly 50km northwest of Pô and is one of the best places in West Africa to see elephants in the wild. The ranch was established as a conservation and community development project and has resident populations of elephant, buffalo, warthog, and antelope, as well as good birdwatching. It is usually visited as a combination trip with Tiébélé.

**Ouagadougou** is 150km north, about 2.5 hours by road. Most visitors to Tiébélé come from the capital and return the same day; those with more time can spend a couple of nights in Pô and combine both Tiébélé and Nazinga.
