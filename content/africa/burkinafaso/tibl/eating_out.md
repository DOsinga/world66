---
title: Eating out
type: section
---

There are no formal restaurants in Tiébélé. Basic food may be available in the village, and the association can sometimes arrange simple meals with advance notice, but do not count on it. The sensible approach is to bring your own supplies from Pô, the nearest town about 15km north, which has markets and small shops. If you are coming from Ouagadougou on a day trip, pack food and water before you leave the capital.
