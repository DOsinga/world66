The Mausolée du Prince Louis Rwagasore honours Burundi's best-known independence figure, a royal prince and elected prime minister assassinated in 1961 just before independence. For Burundians, the site is not just a monument but a piece of national memory, especially around Independence Day commemorations.

The mausoleum sits above the city, so the visit is as much about the view as the memorial itself. Access and photography rules can change, and the place may feel more official than touristic, so go respectfully and ask locally before assuming you can enter the grounds.
