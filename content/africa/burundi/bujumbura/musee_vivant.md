Musée Vivant is Bujumbura's living museum, a hybrid of cultural display, craft space, garden, and small animal collection. It is not a conventional art or history museum; the point is the mix of Burundian material culture, reconstructed traditional elements, and a close look at regional wildlife in a city setting.

Expect modest facilities and variable conditions rather than a polished international museum. Even so, it is one of the few easy cultural stops in Bujumbura and can be worthwhile with a local guide who can explain the craft displays, traditional house forms, and the role the museum has played in the city's public life.
