title: Things to Do
type: section
---

Gitega is a compact town and its main sights can be seen in a day. The [National Museum](/africa/burundi/gitega/national_museum) is the essential stop — the best collection of Burundian cultural objects in the country, with excellent material on royal drumming traditions. Combined with a visit to the royal drummers if one can be arranged, this takes a morning.

The [Cathedral of Christ the King](/africa/burundi/gitega/christ_the_king_cathedral) on its hill is worth visiting for its position and its historical weight as the seat of the Archbishop of Gitega. The [central market](/africa/burundi/gitega/gitega_central_market) is the other essential stop: it is the heart of the town and gives a truer sense of Gitega's daily character than anything else.

Day trips from Gitega can extend into the surrounding hills — the landscape is rolling, green, and relatively cool. The Source of the Nile at Rutovu is about 80 kilometres south; the drive passes through beautiful highland scenery.
