---
title: Eating Out
type: section
---

Bafoussam's food scene is anchored by Cameroonian highland cooking — cassava, plantain, maize, and an abundance of fresh vegetables from the surrounding farms, combined with grilled meats and freshwater fish. The city has a compact cluster of smarter restaurants around the commercial centre and hotel districts, and a much wider spread of local maquis (open-air eating houses) throughout the residential quarters.
