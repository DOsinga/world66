Palais Dika Akwa is a historic estate in central Douala connected with the Akwa royal family. Its value is in the mix of local Sawa identity, colonial-era history, and architecture that stands apart from the newer commercial city around it.

It works best as a short stop while exploring Akwa and the older parts of Douala, especially if you are interested in the city's pre-independence history.
