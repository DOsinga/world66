Dschang is a university town and hill station in western Cameroon, sitting at around 1,400 metres above sea level in the Menoua region. The altitude keeps temperatures mild and the surrounding landscape green — rolling hills, tea plantations, and forest patches that feel very different from the heat of the lowland cities.

The town has long been a retreat from the heat of the coast and larger cities like Douala, and the cooler climate gives it a pleasant atmosphere. The [Museum of Civilisations](/africa/cameroon/dschang/museum_of_civilisations) documents the art and culture of the western Cameroonian kingdoms. The area is well suited to hiking, and the [Menoua highland countryside](/africa/cameroon/dschang/menoua_highlands_hiking) rewards exploration on foot or by bicycle. The [Lac de Dschang](/africa/cameroon/dschang/lac_de_dschang), a crater lake a short walk from the town centre, is the most accessible natural feature.

A visit to Dschang combines well with a trip to [Foumban](/africa/cameroon/foumban), the historic seat of the Bamoun kingdom, and with hiking in the [Bamenda](/africa/cameroon/bamenda) highlands to the north.
