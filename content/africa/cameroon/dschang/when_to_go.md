---
title: When to Go
type: section
---

The western Cameroon highlands have two distinct seasons. The dry season runs from November to March and is the best time to travel — roads are accessible, hiking is comfortable, and the views across the hills are long and clear. December and January can bring cool evenings and occasional mist, but daytime walking is pleasant.

The rainy season runs from April to October, with the heaviest rain falling between July and September. During this period some rural roads become difficult or impassable after heavy rain, and hiking through the highlands is wet and occasionally muddy. The landscape is at its greenest in the rainy season, and the waterfalls in the Menoua hills are most impressive during or just after the rains.
