Kribi Lighthouse stands near the outlet of the Kineke River, with a white base and red top that make it one of the town's easier landmarks to spot. It was built in 1906 during the German colonial period.

The lighthouse is a short stop rather than a full attraction, but it adds a piece of history to a walk around central Kribi and the waterfront.
