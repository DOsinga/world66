---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

The dry season runs roughly November to April and is by far the best time to visit. The Adamawa plateau's altitude keeps daytime temperatures pleasant — usually 25 to 30 degrees Celsius — and the nights cool enough for a blanket. The dry months are also the time to visit Bénoué or Waza national parks further north, when wildlife concentrates around remaining waterholes.

The wet season (May to October) brings heavy thunderstorms most afternoons and turns the dirt roads to mud. The train from Yaoundé can be delayed by washouts. Avoid August if you can — the rainfall peak makes onward travel north of here very difficult.
