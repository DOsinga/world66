Yaoundé is the capital of [Cameroon](/africa/cameroon), a sprawling city of hills and greenery set at around 750 metres altitude in the south-central part of the country. It is quieter and more administrative than the economic capital [Douala](/africa/cameroon/douala), with a calmer pace and a more agreeable climate thanks to the elevation.

The city has fewer conventional tourist sights than some African capitals, but what it has is genuine. The [Musée National](/africa/cameroon/yaound/musee_national) occupies the old presidential palace and holds an impressive collection of Cameroonian art and artefacts. The [Musée de la Blackitude](/africa/cameroon/yaound/blackitude_museum) is a private institution with thousands of objects documenting African and Afro-diasporic culture. The [Benedictine Monastery of Mont Fébé](/africa/cameroon/yaound/benedictine_monastery) sits on a hill above the city and produces excellent chocolate sold at the monastery shop. The [Marché de Mokolo](/africa/cameroon/yaound/mokolo_market) is one of the largest markets in Central Africa, worth an hour of your time even if you buy nothing. The [Mvog-Betsi Zoo and Botanical Garden](/africa/cameroon/yaound/mvog_betsi_zoo) is the main attraction for families, with gorillas and chimpanzees among its residents.

Yaoundé is also, unavoidably, a stopping point — a place to organise permits, arrange onward transport, and stock up before heading into the rest of the country. But it is worth a day's proper exploration rather than just a transit.
