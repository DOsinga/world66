---
title: "Day Trips"
type: section
---

The most rewarding day trip from Yaoundé is to **Mefou National Park**, about 25km to the south. Run by the international charity Ape Action Africa, Mefou is one of the largest primate sanctuaries on the continent, caring for rescued chimpanzees, gorillas, and monkeys in large forested enclosures. Visits are by guided walk and offer close-up encounters with animals that are otherwise extremely hard to see in the wild. It is a meaningful destination as well as a memorable one, and the entrance fee directly supports the sanctuary's work.

Further afield, the **Benedictine Monastery of Mont Fébé** on the northern edge of the city is easily visited in half a day — the monks produce honey, chocolate, and liqueurs for sale, and the hilltop setting offers sweeping views across Yaoundé's green ridgelines. For something wilder, the **Mbam et Djerem National Park** to the north provides forest elephant habitat and is increasingly visited with guides arranged from Yaoundé, though it requires at least an overnight stay.
