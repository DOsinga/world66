The Ethnographic Museum of Forest People presents material culture from Central African and Cameroonian forest communities, including ornaments, masks, weapons, musical instruments, and other traditional objects.

It is a useful complement to Yaoundé's larger national and Blackitude museums, especially for travellers interested in regional cultures beyond the capital itself.
