---
title: "Getting There"
type: section
---

Yaoundé is served by Nsimalen International Airport, about 8km south of the city centre. Several African carriers and Air France operate routes into Nsimalen, with connections through hubs such as Addis Ababa, Casablanca, Paris, and other major West and Central African cities. The airport is small but functional; shared taxis and private hire cars cover the journey to the city centre in around 20 to 30 minutes, traffic permitting.

By road, Yaoundé is connected to Douala (the commercial capital) by a well-maintained highway of roughly 240km — a journey of three to four hours by bus or bush taxi, longer in heavy traffic. Long-distance buses operated by companies such as Buca Voyages and General Express depart from the main bus stations near the Mvog-Mbi market. Overnight trains between Yaoundé and Ngaoundéré in the north are a useful option for reaching the Adamawa Plateau and the Sahel borderlands.
