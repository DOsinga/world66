Lac Municipal is a small central lake in Yaounde, valued less as a grand attraction than as a patch of water and greenery in a hilly, busy capital. It gives the surrounding neighbourhood a calmer focus, with paths and open views that are welcome between museums, markets, and administrative quarters.

Visit if you are already nearby rather than crossing town only for the lake. Its strength is practical: a short walk, a place to sit, and a quick glimpse of how Yaounde's civic spaces fit into the folds of the city.
