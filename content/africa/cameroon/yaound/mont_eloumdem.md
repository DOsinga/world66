Mont Eloumdem rises on the western side of Yaounde and offers one of the city's accessible upland viewpoints. The appeal is the contrast between the busy capital below and the greener slopes and ridges around it.

It is a better fit for travellers who enjoy urban-edge walks and viewpoints than for those expecting a serviced attraction. Go with local advice on access, timing, and transport, and avoid treating the hill as a casual after-dark excursion.
