The statue of Charles Atangana marks one of Yaounde's best-known historical figures, an Ewondo chief who became a powerful intermediary during German and French colonial rule. The monument is modest, but it points into a complicated chapter of Cameroonian history that shaped the capital's political landscape.

It works best as a quick stop while walking or driving through the central city, especially for travellers interested in public memory rather than grand architecture. Read a little background first; the statue is more meaningful when you understand why Atangana remains a debated figure.
