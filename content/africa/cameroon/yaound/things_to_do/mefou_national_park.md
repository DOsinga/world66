---
title: "Mefou National Park"
type: poi
---

Mefou National Park, about 25km south of Yaoundé, is home to one of the largest chimpanzee and gorilla sanctuaries in Africa. Managed by the international conservation organisation Ape Action Africa, the park rescues primates from the bushmeat trade and the illegal pet industry and rehabilitates them in large, forested enclosures spread across the park's secondary rainforest.

Visits are by guided walk along well-maintained trails, and the proximity to the animals — particularly the chimpanzees — is remarkable. Western lowland gorillas, mandrills, and several monkey species are also resident. The guides are knowledgeable and the experience is substantive rather than merely photogenic: Ape Action Africa is a credible conservation organisation, and the entrance fees go directly into animal care. It is the single most rewarding excursion from the capital, easily combined with the drive down through the peri-urban forest fringe.
