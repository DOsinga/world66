---
title: "Mvog-Betsi Zoo"
type: poi
---

The Mvog-Betsi Zoo is Yaoundé's city zoo, situated in the Mvog-Betsi neighbourhood. It holds a varied collection of Central African wildlife including chimpanzees, gorillas, crocodiles, forest buffalo, and various bird and reptile species. Conditions are modest by international standards, but the zoo remains one of the few places in the city where visitors can see the region's wildlife at close range.

The zoo also functions as a botanical garden of sorts, with mature trees and garden areas that make it a pleasant enough place to spend a morning away from the heat of the city. It is popular with local families at weekends. For travellers who have already visited Mefou, it offers less; but for those pressed for time or based in the city centre, it is a reasonable stop.
