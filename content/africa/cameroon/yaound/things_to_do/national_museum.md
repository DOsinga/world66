---
title: "National Museum of Yaoundé"
type: poi
---

The National Museum of Yaoundé occupies the former Presidential Palace, a grand colonial-era building in the heart of the city. The setting alone is worth the visit — the building retains much of the ceremonial architecture from its days as the official residence, and moving through its halls with an awareness of that history adds a layer to the experience that a purpose-built museum rarely achieves.

Inside, the collections cover traditional art, masks, musical instruments, royal regalia, and objects from across Cameroon's diverse ethnic groups. The country's extraordinary cultural complexity — more than 250 languages and dozens of distinct peoples — is reflected in the range of exhibits, from the beaded thrones of the Grassfields kingdoms to carved figures from the forest belt. Explanatory panels are primarily in French. A visit of a couple of hours is sufficient for most, and the surrounding area is walkable from the centre.
