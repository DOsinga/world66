---
title: "Notre-Dame des Victoires Basilica"
type: poi
---

The Basilica of Notre-Dame des Victoires stands on one of Yaoundé's central hills, its twin towers visible from much of the city. Completed in the early twentieth century during the period of French colonial administration, it remains one of the largest and most prominent churches in Cameroon and a significant landmark in a country where Catholicism took deep root during the missionary era.

The interior is cool and spacious, with stained glass that casts coloured light across the nave in the mornings. Sunday mass is well attended and occasionally accompanied by choral singing. The basilica is an easy walk from the city centre, and the hill it sits on provides good views across the surrounding ridges — a reminder that Yaoundé's topography, built across seven hills, shapes every view and every journey through the city.
