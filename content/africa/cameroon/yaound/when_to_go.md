---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

Yaoundé has an equatorial climate — warm and humid year-round, moderated by its elevation of around 750 metres. The city experiences two rainy seasons: the long rains from April to June, and a shorter wet spell in October and November. These months bring afternoon downpours that can make roads slippery and outdoor sightseeing uncomfortable.

The most comfortable time to visit is the dry season from December to February, when temperatures are warm but not excessive and skies are generally clearer. July and August are also relatively dry, though a brief cool spell can set in during these months. Even in the rains, mornings are usually fine, and the vegetation around the city looks its best when everything is green.
