---
title: When to Go
type: section
---

Yaoundé has a relatively comfortable climate by Central African standards, thanks to its altitude of around 750 metres. Temperatures stay in the 22–28°C range year-round, with little of the intense heat found in coastal Douala.

The city has two rainy seasons: a long one from mid-March to June and a shorter one from September to November. The heaviest rains can make the unpaved streets and steep hillsides difficult to navigate and cause flooding in lower-lying areas. The dry season (December to February and July to August) is the most comfortable time to visit.

December is the driest month and the city is relatively animated with the Christmas season. The period between July and September — the second dry season — is also pleasant and coincides with school holidays, when domestic travel picks up.
