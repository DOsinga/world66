Capela de Nossa Senhora de Fátima is a small chapel on the coast north of Sal Rei, simple, white, and photogenic against Boa Vista's dry landscape and Atlantic blues. Its appeal lies as much in the setting as in the building.

It makes an easy short excursion from town, especially by bike, quad, or taxi. Go outside the harshest midday light if you want the chapel and coastline at their best.
