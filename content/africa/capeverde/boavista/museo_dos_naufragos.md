Museo dos Náufragos is a small maritime museum in Sal Rei, focused on the shipwrecks, sea routes, and ocean stories that shape Boa Vista's identity. On an island where beaches often dominate the conversation, it gives useful context for why the Atlantic has always been both livelihood and danger.

It is a short visit, best combined with a walk around Sal Rei's waterfront and old centre. Come for local texture rather than grand displays: maps, fragments, stories, and the sense that Boa Vista's beauty has always depended on the sea's harder side.
