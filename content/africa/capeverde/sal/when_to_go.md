---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

Sal has a desert climate with very little rainfall and warm temperatures year-round. The trade winds blow consistently from the northeast, which keeps temperatures from becoming oppressive and makes conditions ideal for kitesurfing and windsurfing for most of the year.

**November to May** is the dry season and the most popular time for European visitors, with temperatures around 22–26°C, clear skies, and reliable sunshine. December through February is peak season for package tourism.

**June to October** brings higher temperatures (up to 30°C), calmer wind conditions (good for swimming, less so for kitesurfing), and slightly higher humidity. The Harmattan wind occasionally brings dust from the Sahara in late summer, reducing visibility. This is the low season — prices drop considerably.

The most consistently good weather for all activities is February through April: sunshine, light trade winds, warm water, and the island at moderate capacity.
