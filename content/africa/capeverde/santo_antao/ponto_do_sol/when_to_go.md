---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

Santo Antão is dry most of the year but the rain, when it comes, falls in concentrated bursts between August and October. That is also the only time the green of the north really lives up to its reputation — for the rest of the year the high inland slopes are lush thanks to dew condensing from the trade winds, but the lower hills are brown.

The best months for walking are November through March: temperatures around 25°C, low rainfall, comfortable nights. Avoid the worst of the harmattan haze in February if you can — that is when Saharan dust can make distant views fuzzy.
