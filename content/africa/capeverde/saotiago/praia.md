Praia is the capital of Cape Verde and the largest city in the archipelago — relaxed by African capital standards, with a compact old quarter (the [Platô](/africa/capeverde/saotiago/praia/plato)) perched on a plateau above the harbour. The colonial Portuguese architecture and the central [Praça Alexandre Albuquerque](/africa/capeverde/saotiago/praia/praca_alexandre_albuquerque) give it more charm than most West African capitals manage.

The nightlife is excellent for a city of this size. Cape Verdean music — batuque, funaná, and morna — is alive here, and live music venues are easy to find most nights of the week. [Quintal da Música](/africa/capeverde/saotiago/praia/quintal_da_musica) on the Platô is the most reliable spot. The beaches in town are decent — [Prainha](/africa/capeverde/saotiago/praia/prainha_beach) is the closest — and better ones are within a short taxi ride south.

Getting around Santiago from Praia is easy, and [Cidade Velha](/africa/capeverde/saotiago/praia/cidade_velha_day_trip) — the first European city built in the tropics and a UNESCO World Heritage Site — is just 15 kilometres west. The dramatic interior highlands of Serra Malagueta make for a worthwhile day trip further north.
