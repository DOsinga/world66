Cruz do Papa is a large cross above the coast near Quebra Canela. The monument itself is simple, but the spot gives a useful view over the beach and the southern edge of Praia.

It is best treated as a quick viewpoint rather than a long visit. Combine it with Prainha, Quebra Canela, or a walk along Praia's waterfront.
