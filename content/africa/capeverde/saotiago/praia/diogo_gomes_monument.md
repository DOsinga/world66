The Diogo Gomes Monument stands at the south end of the Platô, honoring the Portuguese explorer associated with Cape Verde's early colonial history. The monument is a quick stop, but the reason to seek it out is the view over Praia do Gamboa and Ilhéu de Santa Maria.
