Fundação Amílcar Cabral is a small cultural centre dedicated to Amílcar Cabral, the independence leader whose ideas shaped Cape Verde and Guinea-Bissau. The centre is most noticeable for its murals and its place in Praia's civic core.

It is a short stop rather than a large museum, but it adds useful context if you are walking between the Plateau's squares, churches, and public buildings. Check locally for current opening hours before making a special trip.
