---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

Praia has a Sahel climate — warm year-round with two distinct seasons. The dry season runs from November to July, with temperatures of 22–28°C, consistent trade winds that keep things comfortable, and almost no rain. This is the best time to visit: clear skies, good swimming, and the sea calm enough for boat trips.

The wet season (August to October) brings occasional heavy rain, particularly in September and October. The humidity rises and the temperature stays hot. It is not unpleasant, but the landscape turns greener and the air heavier. Most flights from Europe come in regardless of season, and hotels rarely fill up except during the Carnaval period in February or major national holidays.
