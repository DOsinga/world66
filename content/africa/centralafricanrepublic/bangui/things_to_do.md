---
title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Bangui is not an easy city, but it rewards those who make the effort. The Oubangui riverside, the Musée de Boganda, the cathedral, and the Marché Central are the core attractions. The city moves at an unhurried pace, and much of the pleasure of being here is simply sitting in a riverside café watching the ferries and pirogues cross to the DRC. Check the current security situation carefully before visiting.
