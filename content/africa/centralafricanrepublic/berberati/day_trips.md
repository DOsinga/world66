---
title: "Day Trips"
type: section
---

The main reason to make the effort of reaching Berberati is the surrounding landscape — the forest-savanna transition zone and, beyond it, some of the finest equatorial rainforest in Africa. The city serves as a practical base for exploring the southwest corner of the CAR, though "day trips" here require flexibility: roads are rough, distances are real, and the concept of a timetable is loose.

The most significant destination in the region — the Dzanga-Sangha Special Reserve and the small town of Bayanga — is around 200 kilometres south of Berberati on a forest road and is more of a multi-day excursion than a day trip, but it is the journey most visitors come here to make. Shorter excursions into the forest fringe closer to town offer a taste of the landscape without committing to the full journey south.
