The Mambéré River flows through the prefecture that takes its name from it, passing through forested terrain west and south of Berberati before joining the Kadéï River near the Cameroon border. The river is the defining natural feature of the region and offers a different perspective on the landscape from the city.

Short excursions to the river bank near Berberati are possible and give a sense of the forest-savanna mosaic that characterizes this part of the CAR. The river is used by local communities for fishing, washing, and transport, and the banks support riparian forest distinct from the surrounding savanna. In the dry season, the river level drops and sandy banks become accessible. Access is by moto-taxi or hired vehicle to the nearest road point, followed by a short walk.

Ask locally or through the Catholic mission for current guidance on which sections of the river are easiest to reach and safe to visit.
