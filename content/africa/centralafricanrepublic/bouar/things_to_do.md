---
title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Almost nobody comes to Bouar as a destination. Most travellers are passing through on the overland route between Cameroon and Bangui and stop for the night because Bouar is roughly halfway. What there is to see, however, is genuinely unusual: the **Tazunu megaliths**, a scattered field of 4-tonne granite standing stones erected by an unknown civilisation more than 5,000 years ago, hidden in the grasslands a short drive outside town. They are among the most significant pre-historic monuments in Central Africa, and almost entirely unvisited.
