type: section
---

Abeche rewards the patient traveler willing to look past its rough edges. The surviving monuments of the Ouaddai Kingdom — the sultan's palace, the grand mosque, the ruins of Ouara — give the city a historical weight that few places in the Sahel can match. The market and the old souq are the living counterpart: places where the desert's trading culture continues largely as it has for centuries.
