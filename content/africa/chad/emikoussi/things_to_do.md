---

Emi Koussi is not a destination with attractions in the conventional sense — it is itself the attraction. The [summit climb](/africa/chad/emikoussi/summit_climb) is the primary goal, ascending to 3,445m across volcanic scree and lava fields. The [Trou au Natron caldera](/africa/chad/emikoussi/trou_au_natron) is the most visually dramatic feature: a vast depression within the main caldera, coloured white and orange by mineral deposits.

Getting here requires a full expedition with 4x4 vehicles, experienced guides, all fuel carried from the south, and satellite communications. The [Tibesti rock art](/africa/chad/emikoussi/tibesti_rock_art) sites en route add prehistoric dimension to the landscape. The entire experience is for serious adventurers with expedition experience; it is one of the most challenging and rewarding destinations in Africa.
