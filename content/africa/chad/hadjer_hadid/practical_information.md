title: Practical Information
type: section
---

Hadjer Hadid is in the Sila region of eastern Chad, near the border with Sudan. Security conditions in this area are volatile and have been for decades; check current travel advisories from your government before any travel to eastern Chad. Most Western governments advise against travel to this region.

The area is accessible by road from Abéché, the main city in eastern Chad, which has an airport with connections to N'Djamena. The roads in this area are unpaved and can become impassable during the rainy season (July to September). There are no facilities for independent travelers — no hotels, restaurants, or tourist infrastructure. Travel is only realistic with a supporting organisation and appropriate logistics.
