---
title: Things to Do
type: section
---

Hadjer Hadid is not a destination travelers visit by choice. Those who come are typically aid workers, journalists, or humanitarian personnel. The surrounding landscape is striking in its way — flat semi-arid savanna that turns briefly green during the July-to-September rains — but there are no formal attractions. The notable feature of the area is the large refugee camp complex that has existed here since 2003, housing Sudanese refugees who fled the Darfur conflict.
