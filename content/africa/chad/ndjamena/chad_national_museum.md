Chad National Museum is the main place in N'Djamena to get a compact look at the country's deep history. The collection is smaller than it once was, after looting during civil conflict, but visitors can still see Sao artifacts and other historical material.

It is not a polished museum by European standards, yet it matters because few places in the city explain Chad's archaeology and past so directly. Go with flexible expectations and check current opening conditions locally.
