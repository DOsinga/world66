---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

The best time to visit N'Djamena is the cool dry season from November to February. Temperatures are manageable — warm but not brutal — and the Harmattan wind from the Sahara keeps the air dry and the skies hazy. This is also when the rest of Chad is most accessible, so it is the logical window for anyone planning to travel beyond the capital.

March to May is intensely hot: temperatures regularly exceed 40°C and the city is at its most gruelling. The rainy season runs from June to September, bringing humidity, occasional flooding, and road conditions that make movement difficult. October sees the rains ease and the landscape briefly green up, but the heat remains. Security in N'Djamena is unpredictable year-round and is not tied to seasons; check current advice before travelling regardless of when you plan to go.
