---
title: When to Go
type: section
---

**November to February** is the only practical window. Daytime temperatures drop to a manageable 20–28°C, nights can be cold (near freezing in December and January), and the light in the early morning and late afternoon is exceptional for photography.

Avoid March through October. By April the heat is punishing; by June temperatures can exceed 45°C in the shade, making overland travel genuinely dangerous and mechanical failures potentially fatal. There is no shade outside the palm groves by the lakes.

There are no significant local festivals or events that affect timing — the decision is purely about climate and road conditions.
