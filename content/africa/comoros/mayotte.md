Mayotte is geographically part of the Comoros archipelago but politically part of France -- it voted to remain French when the other islands chose independence in 1974, and became a full French department in 2011. This creates a surreal contrast: European-standard roads and supermarkets sit alongside traditional villages and one of the most spectacular [lagoons](/africa/comoros/mayotte/mayotte_lagoon) on earth.

The island consists of two parts, Grande-Terre and Petite-Terre, connected by a ferry. The main town, [Mamoudzou](/africa/comoros/mayotte/mamoudzou), is on Grande-Terre. The administrative center, [Dzaoudzi](/africa/comoros/mayotte/dzaoudzi), sits on Petite-Terre alongside the airport.

Mayotte's [lagoon](/africa/comoros/mayotte/mayotte_lagoon) is one of the largest in the world, covering more than 1,000 square kilometers and protected by a barrier reef over 160 kilometers long. The [diving and snorkeling](/africa/comoros/mayotte/snorkeling_diving) are outstanding -- the lagoon shelters sea turtles, dolphins, manta rays, and from July to October, [humpback whales](/africa/comoros/mayotte/whale_watching). This is one of the few places in the world where you can reliably swim with whale calves in shallow water. The beach at [Plage de N'Gouja](/africa/comoros/mayotte/ngouja_beach) is the best base for marine wildlife encounters.

On land, [Mount Bénara](/africa/comoros/mayotte/mount_bénara) at 660 meters offers the island's best hiking through patches of intact rainforest. The island rewards slow travel -- the roads are good and two hours of driving covers most of Grande-Terre, but stopping in villages and following the smell of ylang-ylang through the hills takes considerably longer.

Because Mayotte is French territory, the visa requirements, currency (euro), and infrastructure are completely different from the rest of the Comoros. Flights connect Mayotte with Reunion, Paris, and Moroni on [Ngazidja](/africa/comoros/ngazidja). It is significantly more expensive than the other Comoros islands but also much easier to navigate.
