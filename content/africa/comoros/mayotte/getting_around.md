---
title: "Getting Around"
type: section
---

Getting around Mayotte requires some planning, as public transport is limited and the island's road network, while in good condition, doesn't lend itself to walking between sights.

**Taxi-brousse** (shared taxi) is the main form of public transport. These minibuses run between towns on Grande-Terre, are cheap, and run frequently during the day. You flag them down on the road. However, services thin out in the late afternoon and are unreliable on Sundays.

**Car rental** is the most practical option for independent travelers. Roads are well-surfaced by African standards and driving is on the right (French rules apply). Petrol is available throughout the island. Rental agencies are based at the Dzaoudzi-Pamandzi airport and in Mamoudzou.

**The ferry** between Mamoudzou (Grande-Terre) and Dzaoudzi (Petite-Terre) runs continuously throughout the day and takes about ten minutes. Fare is a couple of euros each way.

**Water taxis** can take you from Mamoudzou to various points along the lagoon and to offshore islets (îlots). Agree a price before departing. Some of the best snorkeling spots are only accessible by boat.
