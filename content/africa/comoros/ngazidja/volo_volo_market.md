Volo Volo Market is Moroni's main market and one of the best places on Ngazidja to see daily island life up close. Mornings are the liveliest time, with produce, household goods, spices, and small traders filling the lanes.

Come for the atmosphere more than for a polished shopping experience, and keep valuables close in the busiest parts of the market.
