Snorkeling around Nioumachoua is the simplest way to see the marine park at close range. Reefs lie between the village and the islets, with fish life, coral heads, and clear water when conditions are calm.

Arrange a local boat and ask about the best spot for the day. Coral bleaching and erosion have affected parts of Mohéli, so local guidance matters.
