Nioumachoua Beach is not a groomed resort strip. It is a village shore, with boats, children, fishermen, baobabs, and views toward the islets. That makes it more interesting than a cleaner but emptier beach.

Come late in the day for the best light and the easiest sense of the village's rhythm. Boat trips and reef outings are usually arranged from around here.
