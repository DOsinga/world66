Brazzaville's Corniche is the city's most obvious place to meet the Congo River. The promenade gives you views across the water toward Kinshasa, a reminder that two national capitals face each other here across one of Africa's great rivers.

Come for a walk rather than a checklist attraction. The light, river traffic, and skyline across the water make the Corniche a useful orientation point, and it helps explain Brazzaville's geography more clearly than any map.
