---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

Brazzaville sits just south of the equator and has a broadly equatorial climate with two wet and two dry seasons. The main dry season runs from June to August — the best time to visit, with lower humidity and reliable weather. A shorter dry season occurs around December to January. The long rains fall from October to December; the short rains from March to May. Neither season makes travel impossible, but the wet months can make the unpaved roads around the city difficult.

Temperatures are moderated by the altitude — Brazzaville sits at around 300 metres — and rarely exceed 35°C even in the hot season. The humidity is the more significant factor. April through May is the hottest and most humid period and is best avoided if you have a choice.
