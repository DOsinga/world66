Dolisie is a city in the Niari Valley of the [Republic of the Congo](/africa/congobrazzaville), inland from [Pointe-Noire](/africa/congobrazzaville/pointenoire) and on the Congo-Ocean Railway to [Brazzaville](/africa/congobrazzaville/brazzaville). It was called Loubomo for part of the late 20th century, but the older name returned in 1991.

The city is tied to the forests around it. Logging has long driven the local economy, and the nickname "green gold" fits a place where timber, red earth, and humid hills matter more than conventional sightseeing.

Dolisie is not an easy tourist city. Roads can be rough, power cuts are part of local life, and foreign travellers are still unusual enough to attract attention. But for overland travellers in southern Congo-Brazzaville, it is a real stop on the rail and road corridor rather than just a name on the map.
