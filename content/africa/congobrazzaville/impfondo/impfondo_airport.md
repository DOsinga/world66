The airport serves Impfondo with limited scheduled flights, primarily from Brazzaville operated by small carriers. Service is infrequent and schedules should be verified locally before any travel plan is fixed around them. Cargo planes occasionally provide additional options.

The alternative is the river route down the Likouala aux Herbes and then the Congo River to Brazzaville — a journey measured in days or weeks depending on vessel and connections. Most foreign visitors with a specific purpose in Impfondo fly in, often on missionary or NGO aircraft that operate separately from commercial schedules.
