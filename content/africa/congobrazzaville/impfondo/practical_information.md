Impfondo is not a destination for travellers without significant experience in remote Central Africa. Electricity is unreliable, potable water must be treated, and the nearest hospital with reliable capability is in Brazzaville. Mobile network coverage exists but is patchy. French is the official language; Lingala is the primary language on the street.

Bring all essential medications, a good water filter, cash in CFA francs (there are no reliable ATMs), and enough food provisions for longer than you plan to stay — flight cancellations are common and river journeys run to their own schedule. A good relationship with local missionary or NGO contacts before arriving will make the visit far more manageable.
