Impfondo has almost no conventional tourist infrastructure. The Ubangi River is the main attraction — wide, slow, and the border with the Democratic Republic of the Congo. Dugout canoes and pirogues work the river constantly, and watching the river traffic from the bank is the closest thing to an organised activity the town offers. The cathedral is the most significant building in the centre. The surrounding Likouala swamp forest, which extends north and east, contains some of the least-disturbed rainforest in Africa, including confirmed populations of forest elephants and western lowland gorillas.
