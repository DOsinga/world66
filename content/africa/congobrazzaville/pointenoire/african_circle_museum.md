The African Circle Museum gives Pointe-Noire a compact cultural stop beyond the beach and railway station. Housed near the Lumumba roundabout, it presents masks, local history, and material culture in a building tied to the city's colonial-era social life.

It is not a large museum, but in a city with few formal visitor sights, that works in its favour. Drop in to put some context around Pointe-Noire's coastal, railway, and Congolese identities before heading back to the seafront.
