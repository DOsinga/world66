---
title: "Day Trips"
type: section
---

The standout day trip is north along the coast to **Diosso** and **Loango Bay**, about 25 kilometres from the city. The Gorges of Diosso are a series of dramatic red-laterite ravines dropping from the plateau toward the sea; the small regional museum at Diosso village covers the kings of Loango; and the long sandy beach of Loango Bay, just below, has the historical weight of having been one of the great slave-trade embarkation points of the Atlantic. The three sights work together as a single half-day expedition.

Going inland, the road climbs into the Mayombe forest — the dense, hilly, rain-soaked country between the coast and the Niari valley, with patches of primary forest, small villages and the occasional wildlife sighting. The **Tchimpounga Chimpanzee Sanctuary**, run by the Jane Goodall Institute, is around 50 kilometres north of the city and accepts limited visitor groups by advance booking only — one of the most worthwhile half-days possible from Pointe-Noire.
