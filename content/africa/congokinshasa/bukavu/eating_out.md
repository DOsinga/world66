---
title: "Eating Out"
type: section
---

Bukavu's restaurants reflect the city's complicated demographics: Congolese stalls and fish grills, Indian and Lebanese trading-family restaurants, a small Belgian-era patisserie tradition, and a clutch of newer cafes catering to the NGO and UN staff posted here. Lake Kivu fish — tilapia, sambaza, and capitaine — appears on almost every menu.
