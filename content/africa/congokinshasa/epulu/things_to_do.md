type: section
---

Epulu offers two core experiences: the Okapi Conservation Project station, where semi-habituated okapi can be seen in forest enclosures, and access to the wider Ituri Forest, one of the most biodiverse ecosystems in Africa. Everything here requires patience, flexibility, and awareness of the security situation. This is not a polished ecotourism destination — it is a genuine encounter with equatorial Africa, rewarding precisely because it is difficult.
