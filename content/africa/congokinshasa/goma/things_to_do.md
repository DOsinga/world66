title: Things to Do
type: section
---

Goma's headline experience is the climb up [Mount Nyiragongo](/africa/congokinshasa/goma/mount_nyiragongo) to look down into one of the world's largest active lava lakes — when Virunga National Park is open and the volcano is judged safe. Below the volcano, the [lava field](/africa/congokinshasa/goma/lava_fields) from the 2002 eruption still cuts through the city, a black scar fused into the streets. Walk down to the [shore of Lake Kivu](/africa/congokinshasa/goma/lake_kivu) for the sunset and a cold Primus at one of the small lakeside bars, and cross the border on foot into [Gisenyi](/africa/rwanda/gisenyi) for a striking change of mood.
