---
title: "Bars and Cafes"
type: section
---

Kananga's bar scene is small and local. A handful of simple bars serve the city's residents in the evenings, with cold Primus or Skol — the DRC's ubiquitous lagers — and palm wine for those who prefer the traditional option. There are no cocktail bars or tourist-facing establishments; the bars here are neighbourhood places where people come to drink, talk, and listen to music.
