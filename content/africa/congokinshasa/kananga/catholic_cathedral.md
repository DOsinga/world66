An imposing Belgian colonial-era structure and one of the most substantial buildings in Kananga, the Catholic cathedral anchors a wider mission complex that includes schools and a hospital dating back to the early twentieth century. The mission reflects the long and complicated history of Belgian missionary activity in the Kasai region. The complex remains active and the cathedral is used for regular services.
