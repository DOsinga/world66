---
title: "Day Trips"
type: section
---

"Day trips" from Kananga is something of a relative term given the infrastructure. Nothing here is easy, and what would be a short excursion elsewhere may take most of a day just to reach.

**Surrounding villages** are the most accessible option. A moto-taxi can get you to villages within 20–30km of the city, where Luba and Lulua communities continue traditional ways of life. There are no organised tours; the best approach is to hire a moto driver who knows the area and is willing to guide you. Ask at your guesthouse.

**Kasai river areas** offer fishing communities and river life that is quite different from the urban experience of Kananga. The Kasai and its tributaries support a significant population, and the riverbanks are active with boats, nets, and the commerce of a river economy.

**Mbuji-Mayi** is the DRC's diamond mining capital and the largest city in the Kasai region, roughly 200km east of Kananga. It is a significant place in its own right, but road access is difficult and slow. If you are planning to visit both cities, fly between them rather than attempting the road.
