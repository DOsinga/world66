---
title: "Eating Out"
type: section
---

Formal restaurants are scarce in Kananga. Eating here means street food and small neighbourhood places, mostly clustered around the market. The food is simple, cheap, and authentically Congolese. Expect fufu with saka-saka, grilled fish, and plantain as staples. Hygiene standards vary; stick to freshly cooked food and busy stalls.
