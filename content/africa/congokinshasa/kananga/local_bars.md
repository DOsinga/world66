Small bars scattered across the city serve Primus and Skol beer — the DRC's most common lagers — alongside palm wine. These are entirely local spaces: no menus, no pretension, just cold drinks and company. Evenings are the time to go. The social atmosphere is welcoming, and a shared drink is a reliable way to start a conversation.
