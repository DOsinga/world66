Kananga's central market is the genuine pulse of the city: colourful, chaotic, and crammed with traders selling everything from fresh produce and dried fish to printed textiles and household goods. Spending time here is a window into Kasai life in a way that no formal sight can offer. Go in the morning when activity peaks.
