title: "Lulua River"
type: poi
latitude: -5.910
longitude: 22.415
---

The Lulua River flows near the city and the surrounding area provides a rare patch of green space. Residents come here to wash, fish, and socialise, and there is a quiet rhythm to the riverbank life that offers a contrast to the noise of the city centre. Not a developed attraction in any sense, but a pleasant place to sit and watch.
