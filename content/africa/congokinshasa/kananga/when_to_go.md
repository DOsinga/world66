---
title: "When To Go"
type: section
---

The dry season, running roughly from May to September, is by far the better time to visit. Roads in and around Kananga become impassable during the wet season (October to April) — what is already a difficult overland journey becomes impossible, and even flights, which operate on a good day, are more likely to be cancelled or disrupted when conditions are bad.

If you have any flexibility at all, plan around the dry season. Temperatures are broadly similar year-round (hot and humid), so climate is less of a factor than access.
