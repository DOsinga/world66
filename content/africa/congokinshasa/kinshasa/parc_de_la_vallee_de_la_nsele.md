Parc de la Vallée de la N'Sele is a drive-through wildlife park on the eastern side of Kinshasa, with lions, giraffes and other large animals in a setting that feels more managed safari park than wild reserve. It is a family-friendly day trip rather than a wilderness experience.

The park also has a restaurant, swimming pool and a pedestrian suspension bridge over the Nsele River, so it works best as a half-day outing from the city.
