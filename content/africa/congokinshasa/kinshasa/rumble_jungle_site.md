The Stade Tata Raphaël -- named after a former mayor of Kinshasa -- is an older stadium in the Kasa-Vubu commune that would be entirely unremarkable were it not for one night in 1974. This is where Muhammad Ali defeated George Foreman in what was billed as the "Rumble in the Jungle," one of the most famous sporting events of the twentieth century and one of the defining moments of Ali's career.

The stadium is not a tourist attraction in any organised sense -- there are no plaques, no visitor centre, no boxing museum. It still hosts football matches and occasionally other events, and it looks like what it is: an aging African stadium past its prime. But standing there, knowing what happened on this ground half a century ago, gives the place a weight that no monument could replicate. It is worth the trip for anyone who cares about boxing, about Ali, or about the improbable history that a city like Kinshasa can accumulate.
