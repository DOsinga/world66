---
title: "Shopping"
type: section
---

Kinshasa's best shopping is in its markets, where you can find traditional crafts alongside the chaotic abundance of everyday Congolese commerce. The most tourist-friendly of these is the **Marché de la Liberté** in Gombe, where vendors sell woodcarvings, masks, woven baskets, soapstone figures, and an array of Central African craft objects. Quality varies enormously, and bargaining is expected -- the first price is a starting point, not an offer.

**Congolese wood carvings** are the most distinctive thing to bring home: figures, masks, and decorative objects that range from tourist reproductions to genuinely fine pieces. Take time to look carefully and you will find the latter. **Kitenge fabric** -- the brightly patterned wax-print cotton used across Central and West Africa -- is sold by the metre at very reasonable prices and makes for both a practical and a beautiful souvenir. Look for it at the Marché Central and at fabric shops throughout Gombe.

**Music recordings** are another Kinshasa speciality. Congolese rumba and ndombolo are global genres, but the local market has recordings you won't find anywhere else -- live sets, regional artists, reissues of classic Franco albums. Dig around in the music stalls at the Marché Central and you will almost certainly find something worth taking home. The **Zone de Libre-Echange** area has additional vendors selling a mixture of goods, though navigating it requires patience and a willingness to bargain for everything.
