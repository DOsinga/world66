Kinshasa rewards the curious and punishes the careless. Its sights are not the tidy monuments of a European capital but a living city of enormous intensity: markets where you can buy anything, neighbourhoods alive with music that changed the world, museums housing some of the finest traditional art in Africa, and river views that remind you this is one of the world's great waterways.

The safest way to explore is with a local guide or a driver your hotel can vouch for. Do not walk alone in unfamiliar areas, and avoid carrying anything valuable visibly.
