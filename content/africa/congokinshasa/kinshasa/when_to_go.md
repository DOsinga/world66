---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

Kinshasa sits near the equator and is hot year-round, with temperatures hovering between 25°C and 33°C regardless of season. The difference between seasons is measured in rain rather than temperature.

**The main dry season** runs from June to August, and this is the best time to visit. Roads are more passable, day trips are easier, and the air is clearer. **A shorter dry season** falls between December and February, which is also a reasonable time to visit if the main dry season doesn't suit your plans.

**Avoid April to May and October to November** if you can -- these are the heaviest rainy periods, when roads outside the city can become impassable, humidity is at its peak, and flooding is possible in the lower-lying communes. Rain in Kinshasa is not a gentle drizzle; when the sky opens, it opens completely.

Even in the dry season, afternoon downpours are possible. Bring lightweight, quick-dry clothing for the heat, and a light waterproof for unexpected rain. The city is also subject to harmattan dust winds from time to time, which can reduce visibility and make the air gritty.
