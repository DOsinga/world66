---
title: Things to Do
type: section
---

Kisangani is not a tourist town in any conventional sense — most foreigners who come here are aid workers, missionaries, or researchers. But for the determined traveller the city has real interest.

The genuine sight is Boyoma Falls (Stanley Falls) and the Wagenya fishermen who work the lowest cataract using traditional basket traps — one of the most striking river scenes in Africa. The central colonial-era district, with the cathedral and the slowly decaying Belgian buildings, gives a sense of the city's former importance. And the riverfront on the Congo itself is endlessly absorbing. Hire a local guide; do not wander alone.
