title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Lubumbashi is not a classic tourist destination, but it rewards the curious traveller who ventures this far south. The [National Museum](/africa/congokinshasa/lubumbashi/national_museum_of_lubumbashi) holds the best collection of Katanga's traditional art and archaeology. The [Cathedral of Saints Peter and Paul](/africa/congokinshasa/lubumbashi/sts_peter_paul_cathedral) dominates the city centre with Flemish Gothic authority. For football, the [TP Mazembe stadium](/africa/congokinshasa/lubumbashi/stade_tp_mazembe) is the home ground of one of Africa's most successful clubs.
