---
title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Mbandaka is not a conventional tourist destination — it's a frontier city on the equator, and arriving here requires effort and tolerance for discomfort. That said, the [Eala Botanical Garden](/africa/congokinshasa/mbandaka/eala_botanical_garden) is a genuine surprise, the [Equator Monument](/africa/congokinshasa/mbandaka/equator_monument) marks the literal crossing of the earth's midline, and the [Congo River waterfront](/africa/congokinshasa/mbandaka/congo_river_waterfront) at dawn is worth the journey on its own. The Central Market gives you the most concentrated sense of daily life here.
