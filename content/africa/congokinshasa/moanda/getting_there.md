---
title: "Getting There"
type: section
---

Moanda is reached by road through Kongo Central, usually via Boma. The trip requires patience: road conditions and security advice can change, and travellers should check locally before setting out.

There is no useful public transport network for independent sightseeing around the coast. Arrange a vehicle in advance, preferably with a driver who knows the road to Banana and the mangrove areas.
