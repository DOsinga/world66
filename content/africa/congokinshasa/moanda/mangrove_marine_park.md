The Mangrove Marine Park protects the brackish channels and muddy islands around the Congo River mouth. It is the ecological counterweight to Moanda's oil installations and beach traffic: a low, green world of mangrove roots, birds, fish nurseries, and tidal water.

Visits need local arrangement rather than casual wandering. Ask in Moanda about current access, boat availability, and conservation rules before going. The landscape is subtle rather than spectacular, but it explains why this short strip of coast matters.
