---
title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Djibouti City rewards patient exploration. The old European Quarter's decaying colonial streetscapes and the frenetic energy of the Central Market are the two poles of the city's character, and between them lies a compact downtown that can be walked end to end in an afternoon. The port waterfront, the mosques, and the lively khat market round out a city that punches above its weight for a capital of just a few hundred thousand people.
