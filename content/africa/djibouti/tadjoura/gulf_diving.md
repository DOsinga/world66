The Gulf of Tadjoura is one of the best diving locations in the Horn of Africa, with dive sites along both shores of the gulf offering coral walls, drop-offs to twenty metres, and a variety of large marine life. Sites like Ras Ali, Ras Ouan, and Tombant Point feature good coral coverage, strong schools of trevally and barracuda, giant reef rays, and regular sightings of sea turtles.

The headline attraction is the whale sharks. Between November and February, whale sharks feed in the gulf waters near Tadjoura and the offshore island of Moucha, and diving or snorkelling alongside them is a reliable possibility during those months. The water is warm year-round and visibility is generally good. Local dive operators based in Tadjoura and Djibouti City arrange day trips and overnight boat charters to the best sites.
