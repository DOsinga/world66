---
title: "Bars and Cafes"
type: section
---

Ahwa — traditional Egyptian coffeehouses — are the heartbeat of social life for Cairo's male population. You will find them on every street, serving strong Turkish coffee, mint tea, and karkadeh (hibiscus drink) to men playing backgammon and cards. The El-Fishawy cafe in Khan el-Khalili has been open since 1773 and is the most famous, though it is now primarily a tourist destination.

For bars serving alcohol, Zamalek has the highest concentration: the Deals bar, the Cairo Jazz Club, and various hotel bars. Downtown Cairo has some atmospheric old-school establishments. The Sequoia restaurant and bar on the northern tip of Zamalek island has spectacular Nile views. Beer (Stella and Sakara are the local brands) and imported spirits are available in licensed establishments.
