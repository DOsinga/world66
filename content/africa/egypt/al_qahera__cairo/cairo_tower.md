Cairo Tower stands 187 metres tall on Gezira Island in the Nile, making it the tallest structure in North Africa at the time of its construction in 1961. Designed by Egyptian architect Naoum Shebib, it is clad in a lattice of precast concrete shaped to resemble the papyrus plant, an ancient Egyptian motif adapted to modernist form.

The rotating restaurant and observation deck at the top offer the best panoramic views in Cairo — on a clear day you can see the Pyramids of Giza to the southwest, the Eastern Desert to the east, and the full sprawl of the city along the Nile. It is most impressive at sunset and after dark. The tower is on Gezira Island in central Cairo, easily reached from Zamalek or Garden City.
