The most obvious day trip from Cairo is Alexandria — Egypt's Mediterranean city, two hours by train. Luxor and Aswan require overnight trips rather than day trips. The Sinai Peninsula (Sharm el-Sheikh, the St. Catherine's Monastery area) is a long drive or a short flight.

Within easy reach by car or taxi: Saqqara and Memphis (the Old Kingdom necropolis and ancient capital ruins, 30km south), Dahshur (the Bent and Red pyramids, 40km south), and the Fayyum Oasis (a large lake and garden area, about 90km southwest — one of Egypt's most pleasant escapes from the city heat).
