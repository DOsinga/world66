---
title: "Eating Out"
type: section
---

Cairo's food scene ranges from street food eaten standing up to fine-dining restaurants overlooking the Nile. Egyptian cuisine is hearty and centred on legumes, bread, grilled meats, and mezze dishes. The staples are ful medames (slow-cooked fava beans), koshari (a vegetarian mix of lentils, pasta, and rice smothered in tomato sauce and fried onions), ta'meya (Egyptian falafel made with fava beans rather than chickpeas), and grilled kofta.

Koshary Abu Tarek near Tahrir Square is the famous specialist for koshari, serving it in a frantically busy dining room at all hours. For grilled meats, the restaurants along Mohamed Ali Street in Islamic Cairo are long-established and reliable. The Nile-view restaurants in Zamalek, Dokki, and the south bank of the island offer atmosphere with generally good-quality Egyptian and Lebanese food.
