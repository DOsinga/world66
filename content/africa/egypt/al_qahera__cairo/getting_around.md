---
title: "Getting Around"
type: section
---

Cairo's metro is clean, cheap, and air-conditioned — a welcome refuge from the traffic. Three lines cover the city reasonably well, including stops at Tahrir Square, the Egyptian Museum area, Old Cairo (Mar Girgis), and Giza. It does not serve the Giza pyramids plateau directly; taxis or ride-shares cover the last stretch.

Taxis are plentiful but meters are rarely used; agree on a price before getting in, or use the Uber or Careem app for metered rides. Traffic in Cairo is famously terrible, particularly in the city centre and on the bridges at morning and evening rush hours. Walking is the best way to explore Islamic Cairo and Downtown, though the heat makes long walks impractical in summer.
