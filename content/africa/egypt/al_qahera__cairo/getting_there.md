---
title: "Getting There"
type: section
---

Cairo International Airport is Egypt's main international gateway, with flights from most major cities worldwide. Terminal 2 (T2) handles most international carriers; Terminal 3 (T3) handles EgyptAir and some partners. The airport is northeast of the city, about 20 kilometres from Tahrir Square. Taxis and ride-shares take 30-60 minutes depending on traffic; there is no metro connection to the airport.

Trains from Alexandria (2 hours) and from Upper Egypt (Luxor, Aswan) arrive at Ramses Station in central Cairo. Sleeper trains to Luxor and Aswan depart from here in the evening. Long-distance buses use several terminals around the city — the most convenient is the Turgoman Terminal near Downtown.
