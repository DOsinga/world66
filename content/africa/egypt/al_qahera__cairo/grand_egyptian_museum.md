The Grand Egyptian Museum (GEM), opened in 2023 at the foot of the Giza plateau, is the largest archaeological museum in the world. Built to house the overflowing collections of the old Egyptian Museum on Tahrir Square and to present Egypt's ancient heritage in modern, conservation-standard facilities, it covers 480,000 square metres of building and grounds.

The Tutankhamun galleries are the centrepiece: all 5,000 objects from the young pharaoh's tomb are displayed together, arranged as they were found in the burial chambers. Elsewhere, colossal statues — including a massive statue of Ramses II in the entrance hall — set the scale. The museum is adjacent to the Giza pyramids and can be combined with a visit to the plateau in a full day. Buy tickets in advance, as entry numbers are controlled.
