---
title: "Shopping"
type: section
---

Khan el-Khalili is the obvious starting point for shopping — spices, gold and silver jewellery, papyrus, alabaster, copper and brass wares, perfume oils, and every category of souvenir. Prices are negotiable; the first offer is never the right one. For fixed-price quality handicrafts, the Egyptian Handicrafts stores operated by the government are less exciting but more reliable.

For everyday goods, the Ataba market area near Downtown Cairo sells an enormous range at street prices. The shops along Mohamed Ali Street specialise in musical instruments — particularly the oud (lute) — and music-related items. For books, the Diwan bookshop in Zamalek and the old book market along the road to Azhar mosque are both good. Gold is sold by weight in the gold market within Khan el-Khalili; the markup for craftsmanship is negotiable.
