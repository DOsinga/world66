---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

The best months to visit Cairo are October through April. Spring (March-April) and autumn (October-November) are the most comfortable, with temperatures in the low 20s Celsius. Winter (December-February) is cool and pleasant for sightseeing, though evenings can be chilly. Summer (June-August) is brutally hot — temperatures regularly exceed 40°C — and visiting the open sites like the Pyramids becomes an endurance test.

Ramadan, which moves through the calendar year, transforms Cairo's rhythm: restaurants close during the day, but the city comes alive at night with iftar gatherings and extended social life. The Eid al-Fitr holiday at the end of Ramadan sees the city very busy. The quietest periods for tourism (and thus the most pleasant for independent visitors) are typically October-November and February-March.
