Zamalek is an elegant residential island in the Nile — part of Gezira Island — that has been home to Cairo's expatriate community, diplomatic missions, and upper-class Cairenes for over a century. Its tree-lined streets, European-era apartment buildings, and riverside promenades feel distinctly different from the chaos of the mainland city.

The neighbourhood has the highest concentration of bars, restaurants, and boutiques in Cairo. The Cairo Opera House complex on the southern part of the island hosts classical music, opera, and ballet performances. Several galleries and the Cairo Jazz Club are nearby. The 19th-century villas and art nouveau apartment buildings along the Nile corniche are worth a leisurely walk, particularly in the late afternoon.
