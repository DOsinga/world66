A small group of Hellenistic-era rock-cut tombs on the Anfushi peninsula in Bahary, less visited than the more dramatic Kom el-Shoqafa but historically just as important. The tombs date to the 3rd-2nd century BC and contain unusually well-preserved painted decoration — geometric panels, mythological scenes, and trompe-l'œil rendering of the marble that the actual tombs could not afford.

The site is small — you can see it in 30-45 minutes — and rarely has more than a handful of visitors. Easy to combine with Qaitbay Citadel and a walk through Bahary on the same morning. Open daily; modest entry fee; bring small change for the guard, who occasionally gives unofficial explanations in broken English.
