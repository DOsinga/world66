A solemn day trip 110 km west of Alexandria, along the Mediterranean coast road, to the three war cemeteries from the 1942 Battle of El Alamein. The **Commonwealth War Cemetery**, the largest of the three, holds 7,367 graves of Allied dead in immaculately maintained ranks across a windy plateau. The smaller **German** and **Italian** memorials nearby are no less moving — the German memorial is an austere octagonal stone enclosure inscribed with the names of the missing; the Italian is a tall white marble tower visible from the highway.

A small but well-curated **military museum** in the town displays uniforms, weapons, vehicles, and personal effects from all the combatants. The visit takes a full day from Alexandria (4 hours of driving total). Day-trip cars are easily hired from any Alexandria hotel for around 1,200–1,500 EGP. The cemeteries are open daily; the museum is closed Tuesdays.
