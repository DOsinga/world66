A theatrical road bridge that curves over the bay at Stanley, in the eastern part of the corniche. The bridge has four decorative stone-faced piers topped with classical kiosks — a deliberate gesture to Alexandria's cosmopolitan past. The bay below is now the city's most-popular casual beach, with restaurants and cafes on both shores.

The bridge is best seen at sunset, when the kiosks catch the last light and the bay below fills with families. Walking across the pedestrian footway is part of the experience. The **Stanley Beach** below is one of the cleanest urban beaches in Alexandria, though crowded in summer. Several seafood and cafe-restaurants — most notably **Greek Club** and **Fish Market Stanley** — have outdoor terraces on the bay.
