Ferial Gardens sit at the southern end of Aswan's Corniche, making them an easy pause on a Nile-side walk. The gardens are simple, but the shade, flowers, and river views make them more useful than a generic urban park.

Come here for a slow break rather than a major sight: a place to sit, look across the water, and cool down between the Nubian Museum, Elephantine Island, and the old Cataract Hotel area.
