Bawiti Museum is a small local stop for the finds that make Bahariya more than a desert base. Its displays focus on preserved bodies from nearby tombs and a scatter of oasis artefacts, giving useful context before you head out to the Pharaonic tombs and the Valley of the Golden Mummies.
