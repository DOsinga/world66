Bahariya Oasis sits above a vast underground water system that keeps the oasis springs flowing even in this hyper-arid environment. The most popular bathing spot is Bir El-Ghaba ("Forest Well"), an outdoor pool fed by a hot sulphur spring at around 35–40°C, surrounded by palm trees about 15 km north of Bawiti. The water is sulphurous and has a slightly milky appearance; locals attribute various therapeutic properties to it.

A cooler spring pool near the main road (Bir El-Mattar) offers bathing at around 28°C, pleasanter in summer. Both springs are accessible by tuk-tuk or taxi from Bawiti. Swimming dress is conservative at both — these are family bathing spots, not tourist beach facilities. Visiting in the afternoon on a weekday is quieter; weekends bring Egyptian families in considerable numbers.
