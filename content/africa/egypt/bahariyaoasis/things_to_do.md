Bahariya balances archaeology and desert landscape in equal measure. The [Valley of the Golden Mummies](/africa/egypt/bahariyaoasis/valley_of_golden_mummies) is the headline archaeological discovery — a Greco-Roman necropolis that may hold 10,000 burials, with a sample of gilded mummies displayed in the town museum. The [Temple of Alexander the Great](/africa/egypt/bahariyaoasis/alexander_temple) and the [Pharaonic tombs](/africa/egypt/bahariyaoasis/pharaonic_tombs) of Bannentiu and Zed-Amun-Ef-Ankh add layers going back 2,500 years.

For the desert, the [Black Desert](/africa/egypt/bahariyaoasis/black_desert) south of town is the dramatic volcanic landscape, while the [White Desert](/africa/egypt/bahariyaoasis/white_desert) — 120 km south — is where chalk formations sculpted by wind erosion create one of the most surreal landscapes on earth. The [Crystal Mountain](/africa/egypt/bahariyaoasis/crystal_mountain) is a convenient stop between the two. After a day in the desert, the [hot springs](/africa/egypt/bahariyaoasis/hot_springs) at Bir El-Ghaba provide sulphurous outdoor bathing under the palms.
