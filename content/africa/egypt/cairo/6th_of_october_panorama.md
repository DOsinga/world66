The 6th of October Panorama is Cairo's museum and memorial to the 1973 war with Israel. Aircraft, tanks, and other military hardware stand outside, while the main building uses murals, film, and staged presentation to tell Egypt's official version of the conflict.

It is worth seeing if you are interested in modern Egyptian history or state memory. The tone is patriotic, but that is part of what makes the place revealing.
