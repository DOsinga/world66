Cairo Tower stands on Gezira Island in the Nile, a 187-metre concrete lattice tower built in 1961 in the form of a stylized lotus flower. It offers the best panoramic view of Cairo available to ordinary visitors — on a clear day you can see the Pyramids to the west and the Muqattam Hills to the east, with the chaotic city grid stretching between them.

There is a revolving restaurant at the top and an observation deck below it. The best time to visit is late afternoon, when the light softens the city's dusty sprawl and the minarets catch the sun. Ticket prices are charged separately for the observatory and the restaurant.
