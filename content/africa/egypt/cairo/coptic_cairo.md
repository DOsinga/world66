The walled area in Old Cairo (Masr al-Qadima) that has been the heart of Egypt's Christian community since the 4th century. The Hanging Church (built over a Roman gatehouse), the Church of St Sergius and Bacchus (believed to be where the Holy Family sheltered), and the Ben Ezra Synagogue (where Moses was supposedly found in the bulrushes) are all here within a few hundred metres. The Coptic Museum holds the finest collection of Coptic art in the world. Reachable by Metro on Line 1 (Mar Girgis station).
