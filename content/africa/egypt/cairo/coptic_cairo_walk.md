A walking tour of Coptic Cairo covers the most concentrated collection of early Christian monuments in Egypt in a compact area of Old Cairo (Masr el-Qadima). The standard circuit takes about two hours: the Hanging Church (Al-Mu'allaqa), built on the gates of the Roman Fortress of Babylon; the Coptic Museum (Egypt's finest collection of Christian art); the Abu Serga Church (where the Holy Family is said to have sheltered during the Flight to Egypt); the Ben Ezra Synagogue (built on the site where Moses was found); and the Convent of St George.

The quarter is largely pedestrianised and relatively quiet compared to the rest of Cairo. Early morning is best — the tour groups tend to arrive mid-morning. The Coptic Museum is the highlight for art: the textiles, ivories, icons, and manuscripts represent 1,500 years of Egyptian Christian artistic production.
