The Gamaa Bridge (University Bridge) connects Dokki and Giza to the Garden City and downtown area of the east bank, and the walk across offers good views of the Nile and Cairo's skyline. The bridge is primarily a traffic crossing but the pedestrian walkway gives a rare mid-river perspective on the city — a useful vantage point for photography of both banks simultaneously.

The bridge provides the most direct walking connection between Dokki's museums (Cairo Zoo, Orman Garden, Mahmoud Khalil Museum) and Garden City's embassy district. The walk takes about 15 minutes across the bridge. The Nile at this crossing is at its widest, and on a clear morning the view upstream toward the Gezira Island is one of the better urban river scenes in Cairo.
