The Shooting Club in Dokki is one of Cairo's most established private sports clubs — a large grounds with tennis courts, swimming pools, sports facilities, and several restaurants and cafés, all set within a walled compound. Founded in 1908, it has served Cairo's elite and upper-middle class for over a century and remains a social hub for Giza's professional community.

Day membership is available, making the pool and restaurants accessible to visitors for a daily fee. The indoor and outdoor pools are well-maintained and represent one of the better swimming options in central Cairo during summer. The restaurants are reliable for lunch and are much cheaper than hotel equivalents. The grounds also serve as a popular evening walking circuit.
