The Sofitel Cairo Nile El Gezirah occupies the southern tip of Gezira Island — the best possible location for Nile views, with the river on three sides and views towards central Cairo in every direction. For non-guests, the hotel's restaurants and rooftop bar are the attraction: the **Baraka** rooftop lounge has 360-degree views that are among the best in Cairo, the swimming pool terrace is available to day members, and the breakfast buffet is frequently mentioned as the best in the city.

The hotel's position on the island makes it easily accessible from both the Zamalek side (north) and the Dokki/Garden City side (south). The architecture is typically Sofitel grand-hotel style, but the views compensate for any generic quality in the interior design. Reserve the rooftop bar in advance for Friday evenings.
