The Kempinski Nile Hotel Cairo is a luxury hotel on the Garden City corniche offering excellent Nile views and reliable high-end dining for non-guests. The hotel's main restaurant and the rooftop facilities are accessible with a reservation, and the lobby bar is a convenient air-conditioned meeting point in this part of the city. The waterfront terrace gives a different view of the Nile than the more popular Four Seasons location.

The hotel is used as a base by journalists and diplomats working in the area, and its facilities — spa, pool, business centre — are available to day users on certain terms. The Sunday brunch is popular with the expat community. Location is convenient for the Egyptian Museum (15 minutes walk north along the corniche) and the Parliament and government ministry area.
