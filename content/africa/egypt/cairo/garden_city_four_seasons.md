The Four Seasons Cairo at Nile Plaza is the city's most reliably luxurious hotel — a 30-storey tower on the Garden City waterfront with unobstructed Nile views from most rooms. For non-guests, the interest is in the restaurants and terrace: the hotel has several dining options including the **Bella** Italian restaurant, a poolside bar, and the famous **roof terrace** with views down the Nile that are among the best in Cairo.

The hotel bar (one of the few reliably stocked bars in central Cairo) is a refuge when the city's intensity becomes overwhelming. Food prices are high by Cairo standards but moderate by Five Star global standards. The spa is good and open to day visitors by appointment. Security is strict; you'll need to show ID and state your purpose at the gate.
