The antique and curio shops clustered along Mohammed Farid Street and the surrounding lanes on the Garden City-downtown border are Cairo's best area for hunting old Egyptian objects: silver jewellery, brass lamps, old prints and maps of Egypt, Orientalist paintings, Coptic textiles, and the general curiosities of a city with six thousand years of continuous occupation. Quality and authenticity vary widely; prices are negotiable and sellers expect to bargain.

The most interesting shops tend to be in the older buildings rather than the newer shopfronts. Several dealers specialise in specific categories: one for old Egyptian currency and stamps, another for early 20th-century photographs, another for Orientalist oils. The experience of browsing here — in cluttered, slightly airless rooms with decades of accumulated objects — is unlike any modern antique market.
