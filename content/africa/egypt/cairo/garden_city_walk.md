A walk through Garden City at dusk is one of Cairo's most pleasant experiences — the neighbourhood's curving streets (designed to prevent the Egyptian cavalry from charging, according to local legend) slow you down, the mature trees filter the late afternoon light, and the mix of art nouveau villas, embassies, and 1960s apartment blocks creates an atmosphere unlike anywhere else in the city. The traffic is minimal by Cairo standards.

The walk takes 20–30 minutes at a gentle pace. Start from the Semiramis Intercontinental hotel end (Corniche el-Nil), walk up through the embassy district (you'll pass the US Embassy and several others), and emerge at the southern end of Tahrir Square. The contrast between the quiet, tree-shaded streets of Garden City and the chaos of Tahrir is immediate and instructive about how Cairo's neighbourhoods can exist in entirely different worlds.
