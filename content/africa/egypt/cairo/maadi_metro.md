Maadi Metro station is on Line 1 (the oldest line, running north-south through the eastern city), providing a fast and reliable link from the southern suburbs to central Cairo. From Maadi, it's 7 stops (about 20 minutes) to Sadat station at Tahrir Square, and another 2 stops to Ramses station in northern downtown. The station is at the bottom of Road 9, making it the natural arrival and departure point for the neighbourhood.

The metro from Maadi to downtown costs a few Egyptian pounds and is significantly faster than driving during peak hours — Cairo's traffic is notoriously dense, and the metro avoids it entirely. The stations are air-conditioned, which matters in summer. Line 1 also continues north to Heliopolis (the other Line 1 branch) for a direct cross-city connection.
