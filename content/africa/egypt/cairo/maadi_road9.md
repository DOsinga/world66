Road 9 is Maadi's main commercial and dining street — a tree-lined boulevard running parallel to the Nile that functions as the neighbourhood's social centre. The restaurants range from proper Lebanese and Italian to Egyptian comfort food, with a handful of wine bars (Maadi has the best wine selection outside of Zamalek) and specialty coffee shops filling the gaps. The crowd is predominantly expats and upper-middle-class Egyptians.

The street is best experienced in the evening, when the outdoor seating fills up and the relative calm of Maadi contrasts sharply with downtown. **Deals** (the bar), **La Piazza**, and **L'Aubergine** (a Maadi branch) are consistent performers. Road 9 connects to the Nile corniche, making an easy evening combination of a riverside walk and dinner.
