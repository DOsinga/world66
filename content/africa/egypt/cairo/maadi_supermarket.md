Maadi has the best supermarket infrastructure in Cairo for visitors seeking imported food, decent wine, and international products. **Seoudi Market** (the premium local chain) has well-stocked branches with imported cheese, cold cuts, international condiments, and a better-than-average Egyptian wine selection. Several small delis and specialty food shops on Road 9 carry items difficult to find elsewhere in the city.

For visitors on extended stays, shopping in Maadi is significantly easier than elsewhere in Cairo: the English-language labelling, the familiar product ranges, and the air-conditioned supermarket environment make provisioning straightforward. The wine selection — mainly Egyptian bottles with occasional imports — is reliable for everyday drinking even if the range is limited by European standards.
