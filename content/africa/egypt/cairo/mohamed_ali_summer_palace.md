Mohamed Ali Summer Palace was built in 1821 for Mohamed Ali Pasha, the ruler often credited with founding modern Egypt. The palace mixes Ottoman, Islamic, and European design, and its surviving halls, museum displays, gardens, and fountains make it a worthwhile northern Cairo stop for visitors interested in 19th-century royal architecture.
