Mohandiseen's bar scene — centred on the streets around Gameat el-Dowal el-Arabeyya — is the most accessible in Cairo for visitors who want to drink with Egyptians rather than expats. The bars range from unpretentious local spots serving cheap beer and argileh (shisha) to a few more established venues with DJs and a younger crowd. Alcohol is more freely available here than in many Cairo neighbourhoods, and prices are lower than in Zamalek or Maadi.

The atmosphere changes significantly between weeknights and weekends: weeknights are local regulars, weekends draw larger crowds from across the city. Several venues have rooftop seating with views over the dense urban fabric. Getting here from central Cairo requires a bridge crossing — a taxi or Uber from downtown takes 15–20 minutes depending on traffic.
