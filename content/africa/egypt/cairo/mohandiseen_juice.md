Mohandiseen is one of the best areas in Cairo for fresh juice — a tradition that's alive across the city but particularly well-represented here, where juice stalls on nearly every corner serve freshly squeezed mango, guava, strawberry, sugarcane, and mixed fruit cocktails for a few Egyptian pounds. The quality is high when the fruit is seasonal; mango season (June-August) in particular produces glasses of extraordinary density and sweetness.

The stalls are most active in the afternoon and evening. Look for places with fresh whole fruit visible behind the counter, a high turnover, and a queue of Egyptian customers — these three signals reliably indicate quality. Coconut milk, tamarind juice, and fresh mint lemonade are also widely available. A large glass costs LE 20–40 depending on the fruit.
