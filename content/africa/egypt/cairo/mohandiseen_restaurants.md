The restaurant streets of Mohandiseen — centred on Shehab Street, Gameat el-Dowal el-Arabeyya Street, and the connecting lanes — form one of Cairo's busiest dining districts, almost entirely oriented towards local Egyptian middle-class customers rather than tourists. The cuisine is mainly Egyptian and Lebanese: grills, mezze, shawarma, and full dinner menus at prices well below downtown hotel restaurants.

**Abou El Sid** (Egyptian food), **El Abd** (pastries and ice cream), and various Lebanese chains have establishments here. The juice bars are excellent; freshly squeezed mango, guava, and sugarcane juice is available at stalls on most corners. The area is busiest after 9 pm on weekdays, when Egyptian dinner culture means the restaurant rush is just starting.
