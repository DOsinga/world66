The shopping streets of Mohandiseen — centred on Gameat el-Dowal el-Arabeyya and the parallel commercial lanes — form one of Cairo's most complete retail districts for everyday Egyptian commerce: clothing shops, electronics, pharmacies, mobile phone dealers, shoe shops, and Arabic music stores fill the ground floors of every apartment building. The commerce is predominantly Egyptian-oriented rather than tourist-aimed, making it a useful place to buy practical items at local prices.

The **Lebanon Street** area has a concentration of fashion boutiques and gift shops carrying products that are genuinely difficult to find elsewhere. Several good Arabic sweet shops sell basbousa, konafa, and om ali — much cheaper than the hotel versions. The streets are busiest in the late afternoon and early evening.
