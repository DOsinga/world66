Mori Sushi is consistently ranked among Cairo's best sushi, and the Maadi branch — on Road 9, in what's effectively the brand's home turf — is one of more than a dozen locations across the city. It's not a fusion novelty restaurant; the menu sticks closely to traditional Japanese sushi and sashimi, executed well.

A reliable choice if you want a proper sit-down meal on the Road 9 strip without venturing into the heavier Egyptian dishes nearby.
