The stretch of Nile corniche running along Garden City's western edge is one of the most pleasant sections of Cairo's riverfront walk — quieter than the Zamalek corniche, with fewer vendors and more space. The Four Seasons and Kempinski hotels have terraces directly overlooking the water, and the walkway itself offers clear views across to the west bank. Feluccas (traditional sailing boats) can be hired from the small docks along this stretch.

In the early morning and late evening, the corniche is used for jogging and walking by Garden City residents. The felucca rides at sunset are a genuine pleasure — an hour on the river looking back at the city costs around LE 200-300 and provides a perspective on Cairo that no land-based itinerary can replicate. Book through the hotel concierge or directly with the boatmen.
