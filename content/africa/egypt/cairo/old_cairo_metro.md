Mar Girgis station (named after Saint George, the patron of Coptic Cairo) is one of the most visually interesting metro stations in Cairo — the walls are decorated with Coptic mosaic art, appropriate to the station's location directly above the Coptic quarter. The station provides the easiest access to Coptic Cairo from downtown (one stop south on Line 1 from Sadat) and is a practical starting point for any visit to the old quarter.

The station itself is worth pausing at before descending to the street: the Coptic iconographic motifs in the mosaics give an introduction to the visual vocabulary you'll encounter in the churches above. From the station exit, the entrance to the Coptic compound is a five-minute walk south along the road.
