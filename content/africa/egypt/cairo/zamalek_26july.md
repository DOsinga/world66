26th July Street is Zamalek's main artery — a commercial strip running east-west through the island with the neighbourhood's best concentration of restaurants, wine shops, international bookstores, and upmarket food retail. It's the most accessible part of Zamalek and the place to come if you want Cairo's best selection of imported wine (several shops here stock what passes for a reasonable selection given Egypt's restrictions), a proper cappuccino, or a meal in an air-conditioned restaurant that won't test your tolerance for uncertainty.

Several of Cairo's most reliable restaurants are on or just off 26th July Street: French bistros, Lebanese mezze, and one or two places doing decent Egyptian food for an international palate. The bookshop **Diwan** (one of the best in the Arab world) has a branch here. The street is busiest in the evenings and on weekends.
