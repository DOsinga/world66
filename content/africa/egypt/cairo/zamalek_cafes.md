Zamalek's café scene is the most developed in Cairo — a reflection of the neighbourhood's resident population of diplomats, journalists, NGO workers, and artists who need reliable wifi, good coffee, and somewhere to sit for extended periods. The cafés are concentrated around the intersection of Brazil Street and the surrounding lanes, with a secondary cluster near the Cairo Tower.

**Diwan Bookstore café** is the intellectual heart of the scene — a large bookshop with a café attached, where you can browse Arabic and English literature with a decent flat white. Several specialty coffee shops nearby have invested in proper equipment and sourcing. The café strip is best in the morning; by afternoon it's busier and seats are harder to find. Most places have outdoor seating that becomes genuinely pleasant from October to April.
