Zamalek has Cairo's best selection of bars — a function of its cosmopolitan residential character and the relative concentration of embassies, NGOs, and international businesses in the neighbourhood. The bar scene is spread across several streets, with a concentration on Shagaret el-Dor Street and the surrounding lanes. The quality of wine available here is higher than elsewhere in Cairo, though the city's import restrictions mean "best in Cairo" is a low bar by international standards.

The atmosphere varies: some spots are genuinely good international-standard cocktail bars, others are more Egyptian in character — argileh, beer, and football on a screen. The mix is part of the experience. Several roof terraces have excellent views. Zamalek's bars tend to close later than the rest of Cairo — midnight to 2 am is common on weekends.
