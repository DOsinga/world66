Zooba takes Egyptian street-food staples — koshary, ful, ta'meya — and serves them in a colourful, design-led space without losing what makes them good in the first place. The Maadi branch is part of a small chain with locations across Cairo, but it remains a reliable, comfortable introduction to Egyptian street food for visitors who want the flavours without the street-cart logistics.
