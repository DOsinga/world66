The El Alamein Military Museum covers the North African campaign and the battles fought around the town. Its displays include uniforms, maps, weapons, vehicles, and outdoor military hardware from the armies that fought here.

Visit it before or after the cemetery. The museum gives the broad military outline; the graves give the history its weight.
