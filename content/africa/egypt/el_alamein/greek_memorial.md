The Greek Memorial at El Alamein commemorates Greek soldiers killed in the North African campaign. It is shaped like a small classical temple, with walls bearing the names of the dead.

It is usually seen as part of a wider circuit of El Alamein's war cemeteries and memorials rather than as a stand-alone destination.
