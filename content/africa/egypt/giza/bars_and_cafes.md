---
title: "Bars and Cafes"
type: section
---

Giza's cafe culture runs on strong tea, thick coffee, and the meditative rhythm of the shisha pipe. The Nile corniche side of the city has proper riverfront cafes; near the pyramids the options are hotel bars and tourist-oriented terraces. For the real ahwa experience — traditional Egyptian coffeehouses — head into the residential neighbourhoods away from the main drag.
