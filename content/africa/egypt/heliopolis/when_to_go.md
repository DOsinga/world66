---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

October through April is the best time to visit Heliopolis and Cairo generally. Cairo summers are genuinely brutal — temperatures regularly hit 38–40°C, and the city's heat island effect makes it worse. That said, Heliopolis is more walkable than most of Cairo in summer because of its arcaded streets and tree cover; if you are passing through on a layover in July, it is not impossible, just hot.

The neighbourhood is worth visiting year-round since most of the appeal — the streets, the architecture, the cafés under the arcades — is shaded and walkable in any season. The Baron Empain Palace has opening hours that vary and has occasionally been closed for maintenance since its restoration; check the current situation before making it the centrepiece of your day.

Ramadan brings a special atmosphere to the street food scene after sunset, when the whole neighbourhood comes alive with iftar gatherings and the streets fill with food vendors. If you happen to be in Cairo during Ramadan, Heliopolis in the evening is worth the trip.
