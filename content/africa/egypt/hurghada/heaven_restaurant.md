A reliable Egyptian sit-down restaurant on the Sheraton Road, popular with tour groups but better than that reputation would suggest. The menu runs the standard local repertoire — molokhia, stuffed pigeon, mixed grills, sayadiya rice with fish — at portion sizes designed for European appetites. The set menus are good value for an introductory dinner; à la carte gives more honest dishes. Service is brisk; the air conditioning works.
