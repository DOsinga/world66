The bar end of the Papas Beach complex on the Sakkala strip — same stretch of sand as Hed Kandi and Ministry of Sound, but a louder, earlier and more accessible scene. Outdoor tables on the sand, two-for-one happy hour cocktails, beachfront DJs and the smell of grilled chicken from the kitchen behind. It is unambiguously a tourist bar, and it works on those terms: this is where the package crowd starts the evening before moving on to the clubs next door.
