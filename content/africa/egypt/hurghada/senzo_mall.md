The largest shopping mall in Hurghada sits a few kilometres south of central Sakkala on the way to Magawish. Two air-conditioned floors hold the usual international brand suspects — H&M, Mango, Bershka, Adidas — alongside a Spinneys supermarket, a cinema showing English-language films a few weeks behind release, a food court and a small play area for children. Not a destination in itself, but a practical stop if you forgot something, want to escape the afternoon heat, or need familiar Western groceries for a self-catering apartment. Free shuttle buses run from many of the southern resorts.
