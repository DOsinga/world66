The Ramesseum is the mortuary temple of Ramesses II on Luxor's West Bank. Its best-known feature is the fallen colossal statue that helped inspire Shelley's "Ozymandias", but the site also has temple walls, courts and reliefs that make it a rewarding stop between the West Bank's better-known tombs.
