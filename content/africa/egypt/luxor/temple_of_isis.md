The Temple of Isis is a Greco-Roman temple on Luxor's West Bank. It is a smaller archaeological stop than the famous royal tombs and mortuary temples, but it adds useful range to a day focused on ancient religious sites.

The temple is best visited with other West Bank sights, especially if you are already spending time around the lesser-known ruins rather than only the headline monuments.
