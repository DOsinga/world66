The Tomb of Merneptah, KV8, is one of the royal tombs in the Valley of the Kings. Flooding damaged parts of the tomb over the centuries, but its long corridors, surviving reliefs, and association with the son of Ramesses II make it a worthwhile addition if it is open during your visit.
