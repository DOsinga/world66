The Tomb of Ramose is one of the decorated nobles' tombs on Luxor's West Bank. Ramose was a high official, and the chapel scenes show him before royal and divine figures, including Amenhotep I, Ahmose Nefertari, Horemheb, and the Theban triad.

It is a smaller stop than the royal tombs, but the reliefs and painted scenes make it rewarding for travellers who want to look beyond the standard Valley of the Kings circuit.
