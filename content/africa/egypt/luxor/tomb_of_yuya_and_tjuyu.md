The Tomb of Yuya and Tjuyu, also known as KV46, belonged to the parents of Queen Tiye. Discovered in 1905, it was one of the richest and best-preserved tombs found in the Valley of the Kings before Tutankhamun's tomb changed the scale of comparison.
