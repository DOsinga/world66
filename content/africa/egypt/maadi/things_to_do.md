Maadi is a residential neighbourhood and expat hub rather than a sightseeing destination. What draws visitors is the atmosphere — the wide, shaded streets, the relative calm, and the sense of being in a functioning, liveable part of a city that can feel overwhelming elsewhere. The [Maadi Sporting Club](/africa/egypt/maadi/maadi_sporting_club) is the social centre of the district. The [Maadi Community Church](/africa/egypt/maadi/maadi_community_church) serves the international congregation. To the south, the Corniche traces the Nile bank toward the countryside.
