---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

Mansoura's Delta climate is most comfortable from October through April. Winters are mild rather than cold — daytime temperatures in December and January sit around 15–18°C — and the light is pleasant. Summers are hot and humid, with temperatures regularly reaching 35°C or above and the air heavy with the moisture of the Nile Delta. The city functions year-round as a working commercial and university centre; it has no tourist season in the conventional sense.

The most animated time to visit is during the academic year (September through June), when the university is in session and the streets around the campus are full. During summer the student population largely disperses, which makes the city feel noticeably quieter. Ramadan transforms the rhythm of daily life — restaurants close during the day but the streets come alive after sunset, with particularly festive atmosphere around the corniche. If you want to visit during Ramadan, adjust your schedule to the evening hours and expect slower service for anything that requires bureaucracy.
