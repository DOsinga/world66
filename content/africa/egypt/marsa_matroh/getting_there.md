---
title: "Getting There"
type: section
---

Marsa Matruh has its own airport, **Mersa Matruh Airport**, which handles seasonal charter flights and occasional domestic Egyptian routes, though service is limited and variable. Check current schedules as flights often only operate in summer.

By road from **Alexandria**, the journey is about 290 kilometres along the coastal highway — roughly three hours by car or microbus. Microbuses depart from Sidi Gaber and Moharam Bek stations in Alexandria throughout the day. From **Cairo**, the bus journey via Alexandria takes around six to seven hours; regular services run from Cairo's Turgoman station.

**By train**, there is a railway line connecting Marsa Matruh to Alexandria, with a few departures daily in each direction. The journey takes roughly four to five hours. Check current timetables at the station, as services are reduced outside summer.
