---
title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Marsa Matruh's main business is beach-going, but the stretch of coastline west of town has several distinct beaches and coves worth visiting individually, and the World War II history gives the region a second dimension that many visitors overlook. Rommel's Cave Museum is unexpectedly engaging, and the British Cemetery is one of the most affecting war cemeteries in Egypt.
