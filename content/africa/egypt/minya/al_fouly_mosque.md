Al-Fouly Mosque is a historic mosque on the Nile side of Minya. Its riverside position makes it part of the city's skyline, and the building is tied to local traditions of Islamic calligraphy.

It is a short, worthwhile stop when walking the Corniche or exploring central Minya.
