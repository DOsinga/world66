The Port Said Cultural Entertainment Center, known locally as the Port Said Opera House, is the city's main venue for theatre, music, and cultural events. It stages performances throughout the year, including opera, classical concerts, and Egyptian theatrical productions.

For a city of its size, Port Said has a surprisingly active cultural life — a legacy of its cosmopolitan 19th-century origins when the city had a large European population and the amenities to match. Check local listings for what is on during your visit; a performance here is a good way to spend an evening.
