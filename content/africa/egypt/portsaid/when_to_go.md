---
title: When to Go
type: section
---

The best time to visit Port Said is October to April, when the Mediterranean climate makes walking around comfortable. Summers are hot and humid — the city's position between the sea and the lake means the air rarely dries out, and July and August temperatures regularly reach 35°C or above. The city is busiest during Egyptian school holidays, when domestic tourists from Cairo and the Delta swell the population considerably.

Winter (December to February) is mild by European standards, with daytime temperatures around 15–20°C and occasional rain. The corniche and the old European quarter are best explored at this time of year, when the heat is not a factor. The canal traffic continues year-round regardless of season — the ships do not take holidays.
