One of the most popular spots in town for Egyptian home cooking. The menu runs to hawawshi (spiced minced meat in crispy bread), stuffed pigeon, baba ganoush, and the kind of mezze spread that makes an entire meal on its own. Portions are generous and prices are firmly local. Come hungry and with time to spare — this isn't a quick-service operation.
