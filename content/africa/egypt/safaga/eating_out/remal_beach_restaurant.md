A seafood restaurant with the Red Sea as its backdrop. Grilled fish is the main draw — order whatever was caught that day rather than picking from the written menu and you'll eat better. Solid Egyptian staples round out the offering: rice, salads, flatbread, and the usual array of dips. Tables on the terrace are worth waiting for at sunset.
