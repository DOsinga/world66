---
title: "Day Trips"
type: section
---

Several of Sharm's best-known destinations sit outside the city itself. [Ras Mohammed National Park](/africa/egypt/sharmelsheikh/ras_mohammed) is the obvious day excursion for divers and snorkellers. Inland, [Mount Sinai](/africa/egypt/sharmelsheikh/mount_sinai) and [St. Catherine's Monastery](/africa/egypt/sharmelsheikh/st_catherine_monastery) are usually visited together on a long night-and-morning trip from the resorts; the [Coloured Canyon](/africa/egypt/sharmelsheikh/coloured_canyon), [Blue Desert](/africa/egypt/sharmelsheikh/blue_desert), and [End of the World Cinema](/africa/egypt/sharmelsheikh/end_of_world_cinema) are typical stops on a 4x4 desert circuit.

The town of [Dahab](/africa/egypt/dahab), 90 minutes north up the Gulf of Aqaba, makes a worthwhile day or overnight trip — much smaller, more relaxed, and still a serious diving destination. [Hurghada](/africa/egypt/hurghada), across the Gulf of Suez by ferry, is the equivalent resort on the African mainland and is mostly useful as a transit point.
