Hadaba sits on the plateau above the old lighthouse, looking south over Ras Mohammed and west across the Gulf of Suez. The neighbourhood was developed later than Naama Bay and feels noticeably calmer — wider streets, lower buildings, more locals, and a handful of restaurants and bars that draw a more relaxed crowd than the central strip.

The cliff edge holds some of the best sunset views in Sharm. The legendary [Farsha Cafe](/africa/egypt/sharmelsheikh/farsha_cafe), built down a series of cushioned terraces into the rock, is the obvious spot for a sundown drink. Several other cliff-edge bars have followed the same template with varying success. The beach below Hadaba is rocky but the reef offshore is excellent for snorkelling.
