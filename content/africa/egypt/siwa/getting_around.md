---
title: "Getting Around"
type: section
---

**Bicycle** is the standard. Every guesthouse rents bikes for around 50 EGP a day, and the oasis is mostly flat. Cleopatra's Spring, the Temple of the Oracle, the Mountain of the Dead, and Fatnas Island are all reachable on a single day's ride.

**Donkey carts** (*caretta*) operate as informal taxis around town. Agree the price first — 30–50 EGP for a short hop is fair.

For desert excursions you'll need a **4WD with a local driver**: guesthouses arrange these on demand. Solo travel into the Great Sand Sea is forbidden and dangerous; the sand swallows ordinary vehicles within minutes.
