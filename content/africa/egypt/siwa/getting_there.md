---
title: "Getting There"
type: section
---

Siwa is genuinely remote. There is no airport and no railway; everything arrives by road across hundreds of kilometres of empty desert.

**From Cairo**: a daily East Delta Transport bus runs from Turgoman station, taking 10–12 hours. It's cheap and not luxurious. Many travellers prefer to break the journey at **Marsa Matruh** on the Mediterranean coast, then catch a connection south (4 hours, several daily buses).

**From Alexandria**: a direct bus runs once daily, 8–10 hours via Marsa Matruh.

A shared minibus from Marsa Matruh is faster than the bus but more expensive. Private cars and drivers can be hired from Cairo agencies for around $200-300 one way; useful if you're combining Siwa with the White Desert.

Foreigners crossing the Libyan border at Salloum sometimes route via Siwa, but the road south of Siwa to Bahariya is a restricted military zone and requires permits arranged days in advance.
