---
title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Annobon's attractions are almost entirely natural. The island is a single shield volcano draped in cloud forest, with three distinct peaks rising steeply from the sea. [Quioveo](/africa/equatorialguinea/annobon/quioveo), the highest point at around 655 metres, can be climbed through dense tropical vegetation — a challenging but rewarding ascent with sweeping views of the ocean and the crater lake below. The surrounding waters are exceptionally clear and rich in marine life, making the island a genuine diving destination for those who can get here.

[San Antonio de Palé](/africa/equatorialguinea/annobon/san_antonio_de_pale), the only settlement of any size, has a Portuguese-era church that reflects the island's four centuries of colonial history. The beaches around the village are calm and largely empty. The whole island can be explored on foot in a day or two.
