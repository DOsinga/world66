---

Malabo is a small city with limited tourist infrastructure, but it has real character for those who seek it out. The colonial-era architecture along the waterfront reflects Spanish rule, and [St. Elizabeth's Cathedral](/africa/equatorialguinea/malabo/st_elizabeths_cathedral) is the most impressive building in the city centre. The [Malabo Market](/africa/equatorialguinea/malabo/malabo_market) is the best place to observe everyday life. For context on the country's troubled recent history, the notorious [Black Beach Prison](/africa/equatorialguinea/malabo/black_beach_prison) looms on the edge of the city — not a tourist attraction, but an unavoidable presence.

Day trips to the slopes of Pico Basile, the highest peak in Equatorial Guinea, are possible for those with transport and a local guide. The road south toward Riaba passes through banana plantations and the green Moka Valley with its crater lake — the most scenic drive on the island.
