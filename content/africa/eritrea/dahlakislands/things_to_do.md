title: Things to Do
type: section
---

There are no resorts, beach clubs, or marked trails out here. The archipelago is a national park reached only by chartered boat from [Massawa](/africa/eritrea/massawa), and the days follow the rhythm of the sea: dive in the morning, sail to the next island, swim, eat fish grilled on the deck, sleep under the stars. The reefs and the empty islands are the attraction.
