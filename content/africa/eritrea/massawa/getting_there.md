---
title: "Getting There"
type: section
---

Massawa is 115 km from [Asmara](/africa/eritrea/asmara) via a spectacular mountain road that descends from 2,300 metres to sea level. The drive takes around two to three hours; the road winds through dramatic escarpment scenery and is one of the most striking in the Horn of Africa.

**By road:** Shared taxis and minibuses run between Asmara's main bus station and Massawa throughout the day. The journey takes two to three hours. Private taxis can also be arranged.

**By train (historic):** The Italian-built railway linking Massawa and Asmara has been partially restored as a heritage attraction. A short section now operates for tourists, though not as a regular transport service.

Massawa has no commercial airport. The nearest is Asmara International Airport.
