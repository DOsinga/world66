---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

Massawa is famously one of the hottest cities on earth. Between May and September temperatures regularly exceed 40°C and humidity is extreme; the city is close to uninhabitable for unacclimatised visitors during these months. Even the Eritreans who live here retreat to [Asmara](/africa/eritrea/asmara) in the worst of the heat.

The best time to visit is between October and April, when temperatures are more bearable — typically 25–35°C with lower humidity. November to February is the most comfortable. Christmas and Eritrean Orthodox Christmas (7 January) see large numbers of Eritreans visiting; Easter weekend is the major domestic holiday.

The sea is warm year-round. Visibility for diving and snorkelling is generally best from October through May.
