---
title: Getting There
type: section
---

Senafe is reached by road from Asmara through the southern highlands, usually with a permit for travel outside the capital. Public transport exists for local movement, but travellers who want to visit Qohaito and Matara should arrange a vehicle and driver in advance.

Because Senafe sits close to the Ethiopian border, travel conditions can shift. Check current permit rules in Asmara before setting out.
