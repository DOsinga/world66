The golden Lion of Judah, symbol of the Ethiopian imperial dynasty and of Haile Selassie's Rastafarian following, stands near Meskel Square in central Addis. The bronze statue represents the ancient connection between the Ethiopian royal line and the Tribe of Judah as described in biblical tradition.

The statue's history of seizure and return makes it one of the most charged objects in the city. For Rastafarians worldwide, the Lion of Judah is one of the most sacred symbols of their faith, and the monument is a pilgrimage destination for Rastafarian visitors from Jamaica, the Caribbean, and beyond. It is freely accessible at all times.
