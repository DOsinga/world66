Filwoha Hot Springs sit in the upper Kudu Valley, where hot water emerges among palms and dry-country vegetation. The setting is the attraction: an oasis-like pocket in a park better known for acacia scrub and volcanic plains.

The springs are usually visited as part of a longer park circuit rather than as a stand-alone trip. Ask locally about access and conditions before setting off, because tracks and park management arrangements can change. Bring water, shade, and patience.
