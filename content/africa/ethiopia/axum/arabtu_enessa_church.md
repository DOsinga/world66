Arabtu Enessa Church is a small church near the St Mary of Zion complex, known for colorful interior murals. It is a quieter companion stop to Axum's major religious and archaeological sights.

Visit it while walking between St Mary of Zion, the stelae fields, and the nearby museums. The appeal is the painted interior rather than a large monument.
