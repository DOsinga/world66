The Judith, or Gudit, Stelae Field is a smaller and rougher group of Aksumite stelae south-west of Axum's main archaeological core. It sits across the road from Dungur Palace, making it easy to add while exploring the ruins outside the town centre.

The stones are less impressive than the Northern Stelae Field, but they help show how widely the ancient funerary landscape spread around Axum.
