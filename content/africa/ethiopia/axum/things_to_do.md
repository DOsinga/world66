---
title: Things to Do
type: section
---

Axum's sites are concentrated and walkable. The [Northern Stelae Field](/africa/ethiopia/axum/northern_stelae_field) and the [Cathedral of St Mary of Zion](/africa/ethiopia/axum/st_mary_of_zion) are within a few minutes of each other at the centre of town. The [Dungur Palace](/africa/ethiopia/axum/dungur_palace) is a short walk west, and the [Tombs of Kaleb and Gebre Meskel](/africa/ethiopia/axum/tombs_of_kaleb) sit on a hill 1.8 kilometres northeast. A full day is enough to visit all the major sites; two days allows more time at the museum and for the day trip to [Yeha](/africa/ethiopia/axum/yeha_temple).
