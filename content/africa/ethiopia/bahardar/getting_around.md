---
title: Getting Around
type: section
---

The central city is manageable by bajaj, minibus, taxi, and on foot near the lakefront. For Blue Nile Falls, use a hired car, minibus to Tis Abay, or a guide-arranged transfer.

Lake Tana monastery trips are by boat. Prices depend on the boat size, engine, route, and how many monasteries you include, so agree the plan before leaving the shore.
