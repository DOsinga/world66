title: Things to Do
type: section
---

The Danakil's sights are its geological extremes. [Dallol](/africa/ethiopia/danakilsdepression/dallol) is the centrepiece — the acid pools and mineral colours of the hydrothermal field are unlike anything else on earth. The [Lake Asale salt flats](/africa/ethiopia/danakilsdepression/lake_asale) and the ancient camel salt caravans that cross them connect this alien landscape to one of the world's oldest continuous trading traditions. The night ascent to [Erta Ale](/africa/ethiopia/danakilsdepression/erta_ale) for a view of the permanently active lava lake is one of the most extraordinary experiences available to any traveller. The [Gaet'ale Pond](/africa/ethiopia/danakilsdepression/gaetale_pond) near Dallol is the saltiest body of water you are likely to encounter. All of these are accessible only through organised tours departing from [Mekele](/africa/ethiopia/mekele).
