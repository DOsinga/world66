---
title: "Getting There"
type: section
---

**Dire Dawa Airport (DIR)** is 3 kilometres west of the centre, with several Ethiopian Airlines flights a day to and from Addis Ababa (about an hour). It is by far the easiest way in.

**The new Addis Ababa–Djibouti Railway** runs through Dire Dawa with Chinese-built electric passenger trains a few times a week — about 8 hours to Addis or 2 hours to Djibouti. The new station is north of the centre; check current schedules locally.

**By road**, Addis Ababa is about 450 kilometres west on a tarred but slow highway (10–12 hours by bus). [Harar](/africa/ethiopia/harrar) is 50 kilometres south, an hour by shared minibus from the Megala bus stand. The road to Djibouti is open but the border can be tedious.
