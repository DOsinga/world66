---
title: Getting Around
type: section
---

Gondar's central sights — the Royal Enclosure, Debre Berhan Selassie church, and the Fasilidas Baths — are all within walking distance of each other and of most hotels. The enclosure is a short walk from the central Piazza; the church is about 1.5km further northeast.

**Bajaj** (auto-rickshaws) are the standard local transport for getting across town quickly. Fares are cheap by any standard and negotiated before you get in; a ride from the Piazza to the church should cost a few birr.

**Taxis** (usually blue-and-white minivans) cover longer distances, including the run to Azezo for the airport. Agree the price before departure.

**On foot:** The city centre is walkable and most hotels place guests within easy reach of the main sights. The streets are hilly — Gondar sits on a ridge — and the going gets steeper as you head toward the church, but nothing is arduous.

For **day trips to the Simien Mountains**, guides and vehicle hire are arranged through hotels or the Ethiopian Wildlife Conservation Authority office. Most visitors hire a 4WD through their hotel; this is the most practical option given road conditions.
