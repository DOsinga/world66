title: "Bars and Cafes"
type: section
---

Coffee is the point in Jimma. The [Biqilti Jimma Cafe](/africa/ethiopia/jimma/biqilti_cafe) is well regarded for its traditional coffee ceremony and is one of the more welcoming spots in the city for visitors wanting to understand the ritual properly. [Moonlight Cafe](/africa/ethiopia/jimma/moonlight_cafe) in the centre is a good option for a quick cup and something sweet in the evening. For fresh juice — Jimma's highland location means excellent fruit — [Mule's Juice](/africa/ethiopia/jimma/mules_juice) is a local favourite.
