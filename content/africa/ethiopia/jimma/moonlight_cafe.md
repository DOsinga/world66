Moonlight Cafe is a central spot in Jimma for an evening coffee or a snack, popular with locals heading out after work or students from the nearby campus. The menu runs to coffee, tea, soft drinks, and a selection of pastries and light snacks. It is not a place for a full meal but works well as a meeting point or a spot to sit and watch the city's evening activity.

The cafe is within easy walking distance of most central accommodation and has outdoor seating that catches whatever breeze is going on warm evenings. Service is friendly and the coffee — unsurprisingly, given where you are — is reliable.
