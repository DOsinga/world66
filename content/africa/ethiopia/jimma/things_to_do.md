---
title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

The [Palace of Abba Jifar](/africa/ethiopia/jimma/aba_jifar_palace) is the city's defining attraction — a 19th-century royal compound on a hill north of town that gives the best surviving glimpse of the pre-imperial Oromo kingdoms. The [Jimma Museum](/africa/ethiopia/jimma/jimma_museum), housed in a separate building within the palace grounds, holds royal artefacts, traditional weapons, and Oromo ceremonial objects.

In the city centre, the [Coffee Pot Monument](/africa/ethiopia/jimma/coffee_pot_monument) is the unavoidable symbol of Jimma's identity as the heartland of coffee. [Awetu Park](/africa/ethiopia/jimma/awetu_park) along the Awetu River is the best green space in town. [Jimma University](/africa/ethiopia/jimma/jimma_university) is one of the most respected institutions in Ethiopia and its large campus, with colonial-era buildings at its core, is worth wandering.

[Lake Boye](/africa/ethiopia/jimma/lake_boye), a reservoir a few kilometres south of town, is the top birdwatching spot in the area — pelicans, grebes, and various herons gather in numbers. The old village of [Jiren](/africa/ethiopia/jimma/jiren), once the royal capital of the Kingdom of Jimma, sits on the city's northern edge and still has a few structures dating from the kingdom era.

[Thursday market](/africa/ethiopia/jimma/thursday_market) is the main market day and draws traders and farmers from across the surrounding highlands. Coffee, spices, and hand-woven baskets are the things to look for.
