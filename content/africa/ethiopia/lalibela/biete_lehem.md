Biete Lehem — the House of Bread — is a small underground church carved into the rock adjacent to Biete Gabriel-Rufael in the southeastern cluster at Lalibela. It is one of the eleven canonical rock-hewn churches and shares a courtyard with its larger neighbours, though it is rarely described in isolation and is often treated as an annex of Biete Gabriel-Rufael. The two structures are connected by a passage and served by the same priestly community.

The church is compact and unadorned compared to the more famous members of the complex, but the quality of its carving is consistent with the rest — the columns and ceiling are cut with precision from the parent rock. Biete Lehem is typically visited as part of the full tour of the southeastern group, and its modest scale makes it one of the more intimate spaces in the complex. Standing inside in the half-dark, while incense from a neighbouring church drifts through the passage, is as good an introduction to the sensory atmosphere of Lalibela as any of the grander churches.
