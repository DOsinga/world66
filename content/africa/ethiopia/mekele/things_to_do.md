---
title: Things to Do
type: section
---

Mekele's main cultural draw is the [Atse Yohannes Palace Museum](/africa/ethiopia/mekele/atse_yohannes_palace), where Emperor Yohannes IV's 19th-century residence has been turned into an impressive collection of imperial artifacts and church treasures. The city market is a good place to watch daily life and pick up local produce and textiles.

The city is primarily a base for two of Ethiopia's most extraordinary experiences: tours to the [Danakil Depression](/africa/ethiopia/mekele/danakil_depression_tours) and day trips to the [Tigray rock-hewn churches](/africa/ethiopia/mekele/tigray_rock_churches) scattered through the highland cliffs west of the city. Both require some planning but are well worth the effort.
