---
title: When to Go
type: section
---

The best time to visit Mekele and Tigray is October to February, when temperatures are mild (averaging around 15-22°C at Mekele's 2,000-metre altitude) and the roads are dry. The short rains fall in April and May; the main rains (kiremt) run from June through September, making roads muddy and some rural areas inaccessible, and raising daytime temperatures significantly. The Danakil Depression is best visited October through March — in the summer months it becomes dangerously hot even by Danakil standards.

The Ethiopian Orthodox festival of Timkat (Epiphany) is celebrated in January with particular fervour in Tigray. If you are in the region in late September, Meskel (the Finding of the True Cross) is marked with bonfires and processions across Ethiopia. Both are worth seeing if your timing aligns.
