title: Things to Do
type: section
---

The Simien Mountains are a hiking and wildlife destination above all else. The main escarpment trail from Debark connects [Sankaber](/africa/ethiopia/simienmountains/sankaber), Geech, and [Chennek](/africa/ethiopia/simienmountains/chennek) camps over three to four days of walking, with spectacular gorge views and [gelada baboon](/africa/ethiopia/simienmountains/gelada_baboons) encounters throughout. From Chennek, fit trekkers can ascend [Ras Dashen](/africa/ethiopia/simienmountains/ras_dashen) — Ethiopia's highest peak — or the more accessible [Mount Bwahit](/africa/ethiopia/simienmountains/bwahit).

Wildlife watching is the other main draw. [Geladas](/africa/ethiopia/simienmountains/gelada_baboons) are easily seen in large numbers. [Walia ibex](/africa/ethiopia/simienmountains/walia_ibex) are best viewed at Chennek on the steep escarpment faces. [Ethiopian wolves](/africa/ethiopia/simienmountains/ethiopian_wolf) are occasionally seen hunting in the high moorland. [Lammergeiers](/africa/ethiopia/simienmountains/lammergeier) soar along the escarpment walls throughout the day.

All treks require a licensed guide and armed scout, arranged through the park office in [Debark](/africa/ethiopia/simienmountains/debark).
