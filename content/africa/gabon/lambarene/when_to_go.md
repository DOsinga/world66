---
title: When to Go
type: section
---

Lambarene sits almost on the equator and is hot and humid year-round, with daytime temperatures usually in the high twenties to low thirties Celsius. The long dry season runs from June to September and is the best time to come — the roads are passable, the rivers and lakes are easier to navigate, and the bugs are slightly less ferocious. The short dry season in late December and January is also a good window.

The two rainy seasons (October–November and February–May) bring spectacular afternoon storms but can make the road from Libreville slow and the pirogue trips uncomfortable. The lakes are at their lowest, and most wildlife-friendly, late in the dry season.
