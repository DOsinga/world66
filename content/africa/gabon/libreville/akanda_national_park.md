Akanda National Park covers the mangroves and tidal mudflats north of Libreville, between the Moka and Mondah estuaries. It is the smallest of Gabon's national parks but biologically important — a major staging post on the East Atlantic flyway for migrating waterbirds, with large flocks of pelicans, flamingos and sandpipers.

Access is by boat from the city or from the village of Cap Estérias. Birding guides can be arranged through specialist operators in Libreville. A morning is enough.
