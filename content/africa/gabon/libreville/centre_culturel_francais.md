The French cultural institute in Libreville runs a year-round programme of exhibitions, film screenings, concerts and the occasional dance performance. The building has a courtyard café, a library and a small bookshop. Check the schedule on the day — there is often something on most weeknights, and it is among the few places where Gabonese contemporary artists are reliably visible.
