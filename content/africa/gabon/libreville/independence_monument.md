The Monument de l'Indépendance commemorates Gabon's break from France on 17 August 1960. It stands on the open plaza behind the National Assembly. The monument itself is modest; the plaza is the venue for the annual independence-day parade and a useful waypoint for orienting yourself on the waterfront.
