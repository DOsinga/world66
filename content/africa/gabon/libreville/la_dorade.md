La Dorade at the Hotel Alize is one of the more accessible places in town to try Gabonese cooking in a sit-down setting. The kitchen does poulet nyembwe — chicken in palm-nut sauce — properly, along with grilled fish and a few French standards. Solid value for what you get; the dining room is air-conditioned and quiet.
