La Paillote on Boulevard de l'Independance is the expat seafood mainstay — grilled prawns, brochettes of dorade, a few French meat dishes, and a long wine list. The terrace catches the breeze off the estuary and the kitchen is reliable. Popular with the oil-and-gas crowd at lunch and dinner; book ahead at weekends.
