Lope National Park is the most accessible of Gabon's big inland parks and a UNESCO World Heritage Site, recognised for its unusual mosaic of savanna and dense rainforest. The Trans-Gabon railway from Libreville drops you at the small station of Lopé in around eight hours; the ride is part of the experience.

Wildlife highlights include the world's largest known troops of mandrills, easily seen on safari drives across the savanna, plus forest elephants, gorillas and chimpanzees in the deeper forest. Lodges and guides should be booked well in advance.
