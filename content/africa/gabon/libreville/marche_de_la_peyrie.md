A smaller, more central market than Mont-Bouet. La Peyrie is mostly fresh produce, fish and household goods, with a row of cooked-food stalls along one side that fill up at lunchtime with office workers from the surrounding ministries. Easy stop if you want a market experience without committing to the full Mont-Bouet plunge.
