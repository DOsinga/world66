Nombakélé is the commercial heart of central Libreville — the blocks between the old market, the cathedral, and the harbour. By day it is jammed with shops, banks, and small restaurants catering to office workers; at night it empties out and feels a little eerie. Most of the city's older colonial-era buildings, what survives of them, are here.
