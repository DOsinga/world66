Notre Dame de Lourdes is a small Catholic church in Libreville with a calm blue-and-white interior. The hand-painted front facade and the retable behind the altar give it a softer, more intimate character than the city's larger churches.

It is worth a short look if you are already exploring central Libreville's churches and markets. Visit quietly, especially if a service or private prayer is taking place.
