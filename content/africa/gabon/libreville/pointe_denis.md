Pointe Denis is the long sandy spit across the Gabon Estuary from Libreville. Speedboats and pirogues run from the city port; the crossing takes around thirty minutes. The water on the estuary side is calm and warm, the Atlantic side rougher; a handful of low-key beach lodges and restaurants are spread along the spit.

Between October and March, leatherback and olive ridley turtles nest on the Atlantic-facing beaches. Forest elephants from neighbouring Pongara National Park occasionally cross to the beach at night. Day trippers usually go for lunch and the sand; an overnight stay opens up the wildlife.
