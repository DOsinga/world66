Pongara National Park covers the southern shore of the Gabon Estuary opposite Libreville, including the long Atlantic-facing beach south of Pointe Denis. It is one of the most important nesting sites in the world for leatherback turtles, which haul ashore between November and February to lay eggs.

Most visits combine a stay at one of the small ecolodges in or near the park with night-time guided beach walks during the nesting season. Outside that window, the park is still worth a visit for forest elephants and the empty beaches.
