---
title: When to go
type: section
---

The dry season runs roughly June to September and is the most comfortable time to be in the city. The long rainy season from October to mid-December and the short one from February to May bring intense afternoon storms but rarely all-day rain.

For Pongara's leatherback turtles, plan to be in town between November and February. For Lopé and the inland parks, the drier months from June to September are the easiest for travel.
