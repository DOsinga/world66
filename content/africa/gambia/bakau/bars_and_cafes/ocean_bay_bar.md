A beach bar near Cape Point with plastic chairs on the sand, cold Julbrew, and an unobstructed view of the Atlantic. The kind of place that fills slowly through the afternoon and empties only when the last customers leave. Sunsets here are straightforward and good. No cocktail list; no pretension. The bar serves beer, soft drinks, and sometimes grilled fish if you ask and are prepared to wait.
