---
title: When to Go
type: section
---

The dry season runs from November to May and is the time most visitors arrive. Temperatures are warm but manageable — typically 25–30°C on the coast — and the skies are clear. The harmattan, a dry dusty wind from the Sahara, can reduce visibility between December and February, but it rarely affects comfort significantly. The light in this period is strong and good for photography.

The rainy season, from June to October, brings heavy downpours and high humidity, and most European package tourists avoid it. But it has real attractions: the landscape turns green, prices drop substantially, and the birdwatching improves dramatically. Migratory species arrive from Europe and the north, and the wetlands and stream areas come alive. Serious birders often prefer this period precisely because tourist numbers are low and the birds are abundant.

November and December are the sweet spot for most visitors: the rains have just ended, the bush is still green, bird numbers are high, and the season hasn't peaked enough to crowd the beaches or push prices up.
