title: Getting There
type: section
tags: [getting_there]
---

Aburi is about 35 kilometres north of Accra, a journey that takes between 45 minutes and two hours depending on Accra traffic. Shared taxis and tro-tros from Accra's Madina lorry station run regularly to Aburi — look for vehicles marked "Aburi" or "Akuapem." The road climbs steeply through the ridge, offering good views as you approach the town.

Private taxis from central Accra will negotiate a return fare that includes waiting time while you visit the gardens; agree on this in advance. Driving yourself gives you the flexibility to stop at the woodcarving stalls along the ridge road, which is the main advantage of having your own transport.
