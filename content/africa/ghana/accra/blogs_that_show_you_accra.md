Accra is badly served by the internet. Search it and you get the same six attractions, mostly written by people who spent two nights in Osu. These five do better — Makola Market as a place you shop rather than photograph, Jamestown beyond the lighthouse and the street-art festival, the fabric shops, the beaches, the day trip to Cape Coast, and the everyday business of living in the city rather than visiting it.

It's a shorter list than the others here, and that's the honest state of it. Ghana's travel-writing scene deserves more than it has; if you know a blog we've missed, tell us.
