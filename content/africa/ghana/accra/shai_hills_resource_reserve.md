Shai Hills Resource Reserve is one of the easiest places near Accra to trade traffic for
savanna, rocky outcrops, and wildlife. The reserve protects dry coastal plains and low
hills, with baboons, antelope, birds, and caves linked to the Shai people who once lived
here.

It is a practical half-day or day trip rather than a wilderness expedition. A guide
helps make sense of the history and the trails, and the heat can be punishing by midday.
Go early, bring water, and treat the reserve as a compact introduction to Ghana's drier
landscapes.
