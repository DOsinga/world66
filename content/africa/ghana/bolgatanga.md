Bolgatanga -- "Bolga" to everyone -- is the capital of the Upper East Region and the main town in Ghana's far north. It is hot, dusty, and distinctly different from the south, with a strong Muslim influence and Sahelian architecture of flat-roofed mud compounds.

The main draw is the colourful [central market](/africa/ghana/bolgatanga/bolgatanga_market), one of the best in northern Ghana, known for hand-woven Bolga baskets (the round, brightly coloured ones now exported worldwide) and leather goods. The market is busiest on Wednesdays and Saturdays. The [Upper East Regional Museum](/africa/ghana/bolgatanga/upper_east_museum) offers useful context on the region's cultures and crafts.

Bolgatanga is the base for visiting [Paga](/africa/ghana/paga) and its [sacred crocodile ponds](/africa/ghana/bolgatanga/paga_crocodile_ponds) at the Burkina Faso border, and for exploring the Tongo Hills with the [Tengzug Shrine](/africa/ghana/bolgatanga/tengzug_shrine) -- a dramatic cluster of boulders considered sacred by the Talensi people.
