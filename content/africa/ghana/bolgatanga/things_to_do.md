title: Things to Do
type: section
---

Bolgatanga's appeal is cultural and market-based rather than monumental. The [central market](/africa/ghana/bolgatanga/bolgatanga_market) is the most rewarding place to spend time, particularly on Wednesdays and Saturdays. Outside town, the [Tengzug Shrine](/africa/ghana/bolgatanga/tengzug_shrine) in the Tongo Hills is one of northern Ghana's most significant traditional sites, and [Paga](/africa/ghana/paga) with its sacred crocodile ponds is an easy day trip to the Burkina Faso border.
