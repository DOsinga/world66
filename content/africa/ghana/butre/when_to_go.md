---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

The Western Region has two rainy seasons: a heavier one from April through July and a lighter one in September and October. The best time to visit Butre is during the dry months from November to March, when the beach is dependable and the dirt track from the main road stays passable.

Temperatures vary little through the year — daytime highs sit around 30 degrees Celsius and the sea is warm. The harmattan, a dry dust-laden wind from the Sahara, can dull the sky from December to February but rarely bothers the coast much. Avoid the heart of the rainy season unless you do not mind the road turning to red mud.
