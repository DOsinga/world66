---
title: Beaches
type: section
---

The beaches around Cape Three Points are beautiful but not tame. Expect open Atlantic swell, rocks in places, and rip currents. They are better for long walks, body surfing with care, and watching fishing canoes than for casual swimming.

Turtle nesting can happen along this coast in season. If you see tracks, nests, or hatchlings, keep distance and avoid lights on the sand at night.
