Cape Three Points village is small, sandy, and practical: canoes, nets, simple bars, and the daily rhythm of fishing rather than tourist infrastructure. It is worth a wander if you come for the lighthouse, especially in the morning or late afternoon.

Facilities are limited. Bring water and cash, and avoid photographing the beach work at close range without asking. The village is most rewarding when treated as a living place, not as scenery attached to the lighthouse.
