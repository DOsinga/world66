---
title: "Getting There"
type: section
---

Cape Coast lies on the main coastal highway between [Accra](/africa/ghana/accra) and [Takoradi](/africa/ghana/takoradi), about 160 km west of the capital. The nearest international airport is Accra's Kotoka.

**By bus from Accra** — STC, VIP and Metro Mass run frequent air-conditioned services from Kaneshie or Circle stations in Accra to Cape Coast. Journey time is around three hours in good traffic, longer at weekends or holidays.

**By tro-tro** — Shared minibuses from Kaneshie in Accra are cheaper but slower and more cramped. They leave when full from early morning until late afternoon.

**By car** — A pleasant 3-hour drive along the N1 coastal road. The road is in reasonable shape but features the usual hazards: speed bumps, market towns, slow trucks. Most travellers hire a car with a driver for the day or longer.

**Onward** — Tro-tros and shared taxis run constantly to [Elmina](/africa/ghana/elmina) (15 km) and on to [Takoradi](/africa/ghana/takoradi) (1.5 hours). For Kumasi, change at Cape Coast tro-tro station or take a long-distance bus.
