---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

Cape Coast has a tropical climate with two rainy seasons. The dry season from **November to March** is the best time to visit — warm days (28-32 °C), low humidity, and the harmattan wind from the Sahara that brings hazy skies in December and January.

**April to mid-July** is the main rainy season, with heavy thundery showers most afternoons but few full washouts. A second shorter rainy spell hits in **September and October**. The sea is often rougher and the canopy walkway at Kakum more impressive in the wet months.

The standout event is the **Oguaa Fetu Afahye** in early September — Ghana's most spectacular Fante festival, with chiefs paraded through town in palanquins, asafo company flags flying, and a week of music and ritual. **Pan-African Historical Theatre Festival (PANAFEST)**, held biennially in July or August, brings African diasporans from across the world for performances and ceremonies at the castle. Both are worth timing for; both fill the town.
