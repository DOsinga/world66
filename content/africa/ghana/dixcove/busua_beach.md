The long sweep of white sand a fifteen-minute walk through the bush from Dixcove. Busua faces the open Gulf of Guinea, but a rock reef offshore breaks the worst of the swell — the result is one of the calmer swimming beaches in Ghana, and one of the few places on the coast where surf instructors operate.

The village strung along the beach has a handful of beach bars, mid-range guesthouses, and a couple of restaurants serving grilled fish, jollof rice, and cold Star beer. It is busier and slightly more touristed than Dixcove proper but still small, and the long stretch of beach absorbs everyone. The path between the two villages takes you through a shaded patch of bush — local kids walk it daily and will happily guide you if you look lost.
