Kokrobite is the closest beach escape from [Accra](/africa/ghana/accra), about 30 km west of the capital along the coast road. It is a fishing village that has quietly become the place Accra residents and backpackers come to decompress — wide sandy beach, cold beer, and drumming into the night.

The [Academy of African Music and Arts (AAMA)](/africa/ghana/kokrobite/aama), founded by master drummer Mustapha Tettey Addy, is the cultural anchor of the village. It runs drumming and dance workshops for all levels, and performances happen regularly. [Big Milly's Backyard](/africa/ghana/kokrobite/big_millys), right on the beach, has long been the social hub — a sprawling compound with simple rooms, a bar, and a fire pit that tends to attract an interesting mix of travellers. [The beach](/africa/ghana/kokrobite/kokrobite_beach) itself is wide and pleasant, but the currents can be strong, so swim with care.

Kokrobite works as a day trip or a relaxed overnight. Tro-tros run from Kaneshie station in Accra and drop you at the village junction, a short walk from the beach.
