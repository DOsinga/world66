---
title: "Day Trips"
type: section
---

Kumasi makes a fine base for excursions into the Ashanti hinterland. **Bonwire** is the classic kente-weaving village, half an hour northeast — go early, watch the looms in action, buy directly. **Lake Bosumtwi**, a sacred crater lake an hour south, is the place to swim, hire a wooden plank-boat from local fishermen, and watch a sunset over the rim.

For something darker, the **Adanwomase** kente village offers a quieter, less commercial alternative to Bonwire, and the **Ntonso** village further north is the home of adinkra cloth — the symbol-stamped fabric used in funerals across Ghana. All three can be combined into a long single-day loop.
