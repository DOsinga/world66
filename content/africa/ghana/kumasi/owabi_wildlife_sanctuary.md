Owabi Wildlife Sanctuary protects wetlands and forest northwest of Kumasi, with birds, butterflies, antelope, bushbuck, bushpig, and monkeys. Guided walks can usually be arranged at the visitor centre, and the Bamboo Temple is one of the prettiest spots.
