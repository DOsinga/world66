---
title: When to Go
type: section
---

The dry season (November to March) is the best time to visit — the canoe journey is more pleasant, road access is easier, and the risk of getting caught in rain is lower. The main rainy season runs April to June with a second wet period in September to October; during heavy rain the roads to Beyin can become difficult and the papyrus swamp flooding alters the canoe route.

The area is hot and humid year-round given its proximity to the coast. Bring sun protection, insect repellent, and light clothing. Malaria prophylaxis is advisable — the lake environment has a high mosquito presence.
