Navrongo is a small town in the Upper East Region of [Ghana](/africa/ghana), close to the border with [Burkina Faso](/africa/burkinafaso). It serves as the capital of the Kassena-Nankana District and sits in the dry, flat savanna landscape of Ghana's far north. Most travelers pass through on their way to [Paga](/africa/ghana/paga) or onward to Burkina, but the town has enough character to warrant a stop.

The real draw is the annual Fire Festival (Bugum), a spectacular celebration held around the start of the dry season. Locals carry torches through the streets in a tradition rooted in local legend, and visitors are welcome to join in. It is one of the most dramatic festivals in northern Ghana.

Navrongo is also home to the [Cathedral Basilica of Our Lady of Seven Sorrows](/africa/ghana/navrongo/our_lady_cathedral), one of the oldest Catholic churches in Ghana. The interior is decorated with traditional Kassena-style painted murals that blend local artistic traditions with Christian imagery -- well worth a visit even if churches are not usually your thing. The town's [market](/africa/ghana/navrongo/navrongo_market) is lively and gives a good sense of everyday life in the Upper East. The surrounding villages are known for their [Kassena compound houses](/africa/ghana/navrongo/kassena_compound), painted in bold geometric patterns that are unique to this part of West Africa.
