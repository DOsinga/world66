---
title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Sekondi's sights cluster in the old colonial quarter on the headland: a Dutch fort turned lighthouse, the British-built Fort Sekondi, and the crumbling European Town of merchant houses below. A morning is enough to walk the lot. Add the Bisa Aberwa Museum on the way in for a strong introduction to Akan and Pan-African history before you reach the coast.
