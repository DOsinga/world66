story: Sunyani's Roman Catholic Cathedral is not what you expect from a cathedral in a Ghanaian regional capital — it's a bold, modernist structure with a dramatically sweeping roof, built in the late 20th century and designed to stand out rather than blend in. The Diocese of Sunyani was established in 1971, and the cathedral reflects the ambitions of a young diocese building its presence in a region where Christianity and traditional beliefs have long coexisted. The site sits on a hillock and is visible from several blocks away.
---

The Roman Catholic Cathedral is the most architecturally distinctive building in Sunyani, a modernist church with an unusual roof profile that dominates the local skyline. It's the seat of the Diocese of Sunyani and hosts large congregations for Sunday Mass. The interior is spacious and cool, a welcome refuge from the midday heat.

The cathedral grounds are well maintained and provide a quiet spot to sit outside busy market hours. The surrounding area has several mission schools and institutions associated with the Catholic church, which has a long presence in the Brong Ahafo region. Visitors are welcome during opening hours and for services.
