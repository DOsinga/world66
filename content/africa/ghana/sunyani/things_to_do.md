---
title: Things to Do in Sunyani
type: section
---

Sunyani itself is pleasant but quiet — the [Roman Catholic Cathedral](/africa/ghana/sunyani/catholic_cathedral) is the most striking building in town, and the [central market](/africa/ghana/sunyani/central_market) is worth a visit. The main reason to come here is the exceptional day trips within easy reach: the [Boabeng-Fiema Monkey Sanctuary](/africa/ghana/sunyani/boabeng_fiema_monkey_sanctuary) is one of Ghana's most unusual wildlife experiences, and the [Kintampo Waterfalls](/africa/ghana/sunyani/kintampo_waterfalls) are a straightforward excursion to the north.
