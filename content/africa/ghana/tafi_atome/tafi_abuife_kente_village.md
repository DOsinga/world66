Tafi Abuife is a short trip from Tafi Atome and is known for Ewe kente weaving. The long looms and narrow cloth strips are the main thing to see, and finished cloth is usually available to buy directly from weavers.

It is not a polished museum stop. Go because you want to see a working craft village and ask questions, not because you expect a fixed exhibition.
