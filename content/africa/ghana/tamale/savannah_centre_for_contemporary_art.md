The Savannah Centre for Contemporary Art gives Tamale a serious contemporary art stop, with exhibitions and research that connect northern Ghana to wider African and international art conversations. It is a useful place to see work that is not filtered through the souvenir market or the usual tourist craft circuit.

For travellers, the centre adds a different layer to Tamale. The city is often treated as a gateway to Mole National Park or the north, but SCCA shows it as a place with its own cultural institutions and creative debates. Check the current exhibition schedule before making a special trip.
