---
title: When to Go
type: section
---

The Upper West Region has a single rainy season from May to September. The best time to visit is November through February, when the harmattan — the dry dusty wind from the Sahara — keeps temperatures manageable and the days are clear. From November the landscape is still green enough to be attractive; by March it has dried to a uniform dusty brown.

Visiting during the rains is possible but roads to the village mosques and out to Wechiau can become difficult after heavy downpours. The Black Volta is higher in the wet season, which can actually make the hippo sanctuary more atmospheric, but access can be tricky. Avoid March and April if you are sensitive to heat — temperatures regularly exceed 40°C before the rains arrive.
