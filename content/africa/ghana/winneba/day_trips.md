Winneba is a useful stopping point between [Accra](/africa/ghana/accra) and [Cape Coast](/africa/ghana/capecoast). Cape Coast and [Elmina](/africa/ghana/elmina), with their UNESCO-listed slave-trading castles, are about an hour west along the coast and easily combined as a full day. [Kokrobite](/africa/ghana/kokrobite), the small beach village with the Academy of African Music, is around 40 minutes back toward Accra.
