---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

The single best time to be in Winneba is the **first weekend of May**, when the Aboakyere festival turns the town into a giant traditional pageant — drumming, processions, the deer-hunt itself, and a public durbar at the chief's palace. Book a room weeks ahead and expect higher prices.

**Christmas and New Year** are another lively window — the Effutu's masquerade-style fancy dress competitions fill the streets between 25 December and 2 January. Outside the festivals the town is calm and easy. The **dry season** runs roughly from November to March, with cooler nights and clearer skies; **April to October** brings the main rains, with heaviest downpours in June. Avoid the harmattan haze of December and January if you want to see the coast clearly.
