---
title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

The Bijagos Islands are not a sightseeing destination in the conventional sense — there are no monuments, museums, or attractions to tick off. What's here is nature and culture: the ocean hippos of Orango, sea turtle nesting beaches, extraordinary birdlife, and the living traditions of the Bijago people. The best approach is to base yourself on Bubaque and arrange boat trips to the outer islands.
