The Palais de la Culture d'Abidjan is the city's main venue for theater, dance, music, and cultural events. Built in the 1970s on the edge of the Ébrié Lagoon, it hosts performances by both Ivorian artists and visiting international companies. The main hall seats over a thousand.

Performances here offer the best introduction to Ivorian music, theater, and traditional dance for visitors who want to go beyond restaurants and sightseeing. The building's lagoon-side setting is pleasant in the early evening; check listings at the time of your visit through the venue or local press.
