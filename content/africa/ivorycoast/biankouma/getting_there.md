---
title: "Getting There"
type: section
---

Biankouma is about 40 km north of [Man](/africa/ivorycoast/man) on a road that continues to Touba. From Man, shared taxis (bush taxis) make the run regularly and the journey takes about an hour depending on road conditions. The road is paved for most of its length but can deteriorate in the rainy season.

There is no direct transport from Abidjan to Biankouma; the approach is always via Man, which is itself best reached by bus from Abidjan (the overnight bus is the standard option) or by flying into Man Airport on Air Côte d'Ivoire.
