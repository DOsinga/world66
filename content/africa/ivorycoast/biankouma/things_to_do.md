---
title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Biankouma is a small Dan village in the mountains of western Ivory Coast, best visited on a Sunday when the Goua acrobatic dance performers may be active. The old town's decorated houses and the proximity to Mont Sângbé National Park are the other draws. A visit requires some patience and cultural sensitivity — ask permission from the village chief before exploring, and never photograph fetish objects.
