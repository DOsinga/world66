Bingerville is about 15 km east of [Abidjan](/africa/ivorycoast/abidjan), a charming town on a plateau overlooking the lagoon. Until the 1930s it served as the capital of Côte d'Ivoire.

The major attractions include the old [Botanical Garden](/africa/ivorycoast/bingerville/botanical_garden), the [Governor's Palace](/africa/ivorycoast/bingerville/governors_palace), and the [Centre de Céramique](/africa/ivorycoast/bingerville/centre_de_ceramique) — a working sculpture school whose gallery is the best place in the country to buy contemporary Ivorian art. From Bingerville, a 25 km dirt road takes you to [Grand Bassam](/africa/ivorycoast/grandbassam), where the French first set up their colonial capital.
