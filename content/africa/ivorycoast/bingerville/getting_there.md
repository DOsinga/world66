---
title: Getting There
type: section
---

Bingerville is reached from [Abidjan](/africa/ivorycoast/abidjan) along Boulevard Mitterrand, the lagoon-side road that runs east from the Plateau. A taxi from central Abidjan takes 30–45 minutes depending on traffic and should cost a few thousand CFA. Shared taxis and woro-woro minibuses run the same route for far less.

There is no train and no airport in Bingerville itself — Félix-Houphouët-Boigny International Airport in Abidjan, about 25 km west, is the gateway.
