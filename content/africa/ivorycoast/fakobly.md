Fakobly is a small village 9 km from [Tieni-Siabli](/africa/ivorycoast/tienisiabli) and about 25 km from [Man](/africa/ivorycoast/man), in the far west of [Ivory Coast](/africa/ivorycoast). It lies on the banks of a tributary of the Sassandra River, where hippos and crocodiles can be spotted — you can [hire a pirogue](/africa/ivorycoast/fakobly/sassandra_pirogue) for a short trip on the water. The local Wobé women perform a traditional dance called the [temate](/africa/ivorycoast/fakobly/wobe_temate_dance), though it is often hard to find out in advance when there will be a performance.
