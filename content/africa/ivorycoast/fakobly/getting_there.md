Fakobly sits about 25 km from [Man](/africa/ivorycoast/man), the main city of western Ivory Coast and the logical base for exploring this region. The road from Man runs west through Tieni-Siabli; Fakobly is about 9 km from Tieni-Siabli.

Transport options are limited. Bush taxis run between Man and the western villages when they have enough passengers — you can find them at Man's gare routière. A private taxi or moto-taxi from Man is the more reliable option and gives you flexibility on timing. The roads are passable in the dry season; in the rainy season (June–October) conditions can deteriorate and some stretches become difficult.
