Gouessesso is a small Yacouba village 12 km west of [Biankouma](/africa/ivorycoast/biankouma) and about 50 km from [Man](/africa/ivorycoast/man), in the hilly west of [Ivory Coast](/africa/ivorycoast). The village is known for Les Lianes, a small lodge that blends into its surroundings so well that staying there feels less like tourism and more like a quiet immersion in traditional village life. If you are looking for an authentic encounter with rural West Africa without the self-consciousness that often comes with it, this is a good place to start.

The region around Gouessesso is the cultural heartland of the Dan (Yacouba) people, internationally famous for their [acrobatic stilt-dancers](/africa/ivorycoast/gouessesso/yacouba_stilt_dancers) and the carved wooden masks that inspired Picasso and the early modernists. The land itself is gentle hill country — rice paddies in the valleys, patches of forest on the slopes, with [Mount Tonkoui](/africa/ivorycoast/gouessesso/mount_tonkoui), the country's second highest peak, rising to the south. Days here are spent on long walks, conversations with villagers, and an unhurried pace that is increasingly hard to find anywhere else.
