Gouessesso sits in the heart of the Yacouba country between Biankouma and Man, both worth a visit. [Biankouma](/africa/ivorycoast/biankouma), 12 km east, has a bustling weekly market and is the nearest administrative centre. [Man](/africa/ivorycoast/man), 50 km southeast, is the regional capital — home to the famous Cascade waterfalls, the Dent de Man (a striking tooth-shaped peak), and the largest collection of mask-related cultural sites in the country.

Further west, the village of **Fakobly** (40 km) is one of the centres of Yacouba blacksmithing and mask-carving. The **Sacred Forest of Gouéné**, a small protected grove near Biankouma, is the site of important Yacouba initiation rituals — visitors can sometimes enter the outer edges with a local guide.
