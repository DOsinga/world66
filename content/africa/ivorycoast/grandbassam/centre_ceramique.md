Centre Céramique is a small museum and exhibition space devoted to Ivorian ceramics. Grand-Bassam has a long craft tradition, and the centre gives that industry a visitor-facing stop beyond the colonial buildings and beach.

Expect a modest place rather than a major museum: displays of traditional ceramics, changing work by local artisans, and a useful look at one of the town's main crafts. It pairs well with the nearby old town sights.
