---
title: Bars and cafes
type: section
---

Evening drinking in Man centres on the maquis and small neighbourhood bars. The social atmosphere is easy and unhurried. Local beers — Bock and Flag are the standards — are served cold; palm wine (bangui) is available in the smaller local bars and is worth trying if you can find a fresh batch. There is no particular bar district; the action is spread around the neighbourhoods.
