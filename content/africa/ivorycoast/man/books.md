**Waiting for the Wild Beasts to Vote** by Ahmadou Kourouma (1998) — The great Ivorian novel. A savage political satire set in post-independence West Africa, told through the life of a dictator by his griot. Kourouma, who was from the Man region himself, is the towering figure of Ivorian literature and this is his masterpiece.

**The Identity Card** (*La Carte d'identité*) by Jean-Marie Adiaffi (1980) — Another major work of Ivorian fiction, exploring the absurdities of colonialism and identity through a man's quest for his identity papers. Vivid, angry, and formally inventive.

**Penguin Book of Modern African Poetry** edited by Gerald Moore and Ulli Beier — A solid anthology spanning the continent, useful for getting a feel for the literary landscape that produced Kourouma and Adiaffi.

**West Africa** (Lonely Planet or Bradt regional guide) — For practical information on the wider region; the Man section in any recent edition covers the key practicalities for the hills and the liana bridges.
