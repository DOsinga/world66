---
title: Town restaurants
type: poi
---

A handful of sit-down restaurants near the town centre offer a step up in formality and price from the maquis. The menus tend to combine Ivorian dishes with occasional Lebanese or French influences — the French colonial legacy persists in Ivorian restaurant culture, particularly in towns that once attracted expatriates and aid workers. Grilled meats, rice, and stews are the reliable options; fish is often good in this part of the country.
