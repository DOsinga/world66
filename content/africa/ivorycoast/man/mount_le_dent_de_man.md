Le Dent de Man is the tooth-shaped mountain that gives Man its most recognisable skyline. Its two peaks rise to 881 metres, close enough to town to feel like part of the city but rugged enough to make the climb memorable.

Go for the hike and the views over the green hills around Man. The mountain is a better stop with a local guide, especially if you are not used to the trails.
