Among the most distinctive sights in Ivory Coast, the liana bridges of the Man region are traditional suspension bridges woven from jungle vines by the Dan (Yacouba) people. Some span considerable gorges and remain in active use by local communities. According to local tradition, the bridges were built by night with supernatural assistance — a story that gives them an added layer of cultural resonance. They are maintained as part of the living cultural landscape rather than as museum pieces, which makes them all the more remarkable. Several bridges are accessible from Man, often via short walks from the roadside.
