---
title: Getting There
type: section
---

San-Pédro is around 340km southwest of Abidjan by road — a journey of roughly 5–6 hours by shared taxi or bus. The road through Sassandra and along the coast is scenic but can be slow. Several companies run direct minibus services from Abidjan's Adjamé terminal; book early as services fill up.

San-Pédro Airport (IATA: SPY) has irregular domestic services, though flight frequency and operators change; check current availability closer to your trip. The airport is about 4km from the city centre. There is no rail connection.

From the west, bush taxis run along the coastal route from Tabou near the Liberian border, though this road is rough and journey times variable.
