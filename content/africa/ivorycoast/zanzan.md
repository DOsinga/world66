Zanzan is the far northeast of Côte d'Ivoire, a region of savanna roads, trading towns, and long distances toward Ghana and Burkina Faso. It is not the country's easy tourist face. You come here for old caravan history, borderland culture, and the feeling of being well beyond the Abidjan-to-Grand-Bassam circuit.

[Bondoukou](/africa/ivorycoast/bondoukou) is the best base and the obvious starting point, with old mosques, markets, and masquerade traditions that still give the town real texture. North and west, the landscape opens toward Bouna and [Comoé National Park](/africa/ivorycoast/zanzan/comoe_national_park), one of West Africa's great protected areas but a destination that requires planning, patience, and current local advice.

Travel in Zanzan is slow. Roads can be tiring, public transport is irregular, and distances are larger than they first appear. That is also why the region feels rewarding: the highlights are not staged for quick consumption, and the best moments often come from markets, roadside meals, and conversations in towns that see few foreign travelers.

## Destinations

- [Bondoukou](/africa/ivorycoast/bondoukou) -- historic trading town and the region's most interesting base
