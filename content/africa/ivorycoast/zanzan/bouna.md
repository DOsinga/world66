Bouna is a dry, northern town with more practical value than obvious sightseeing polish. It sits closer to the Burkina Faso borderlands and can be useful for travelers moving toward Comoé National Park or exploring the less-visited north of Zanzan.

The town's interest is in its borderland feel: markets, transport yards, Sahelian influences, and the sense that Côte d'Ivoire is beginning to shade into a different West Africa. Come prepared for simple facilities and long onward journeys.
