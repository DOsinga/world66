Comoé National Park is the big wilderness draw of Zanzan, a huge protected area of savanna, riverine forest, and seasonal grassland. It has suffered from poaching and conflict over the years, so visitors should check current access and conservation conditions before building a trip around it.

When visits are possible, the appeal is scale rather than easy sightings. The park feels remote, dry, and open, with the Comoé River forming a green line through the landscape. Go with proper guidance, enough time, and a realistic understanding that this is not a polished safari circuit.
