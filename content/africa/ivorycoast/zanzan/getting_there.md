---
title: Getting There
type: section
---

Bondoukou is the usual entry point, reached by road from Abidjan via Abengourou or from the Ghana border around Sampa. Buses and shared taxis run, but journeys are long and departure times can be elastic.

For Comoé National Park or smaller towns such as Bouna and Tanda, ask locally before setting out. Road conditions, security advice, and park access arrangements can change.
