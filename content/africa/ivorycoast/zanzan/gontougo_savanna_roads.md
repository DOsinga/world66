The roads through Gontougo are not a formal attraction, but they are a real part of the Zanzan experience. Between towns you pass cashew trees, small markets, roadside food stalls, and villages where traffic thins out quickly.

Travelers with a private vehicle will enjoy this more than those squeezed into a hot minibus. Stop when it makes sense, buy fruit or grilled meat where there is a crowd, and remember that the journey is often more revealing than the named destination.
