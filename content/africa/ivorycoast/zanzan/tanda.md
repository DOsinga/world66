Tanda is a modest town in the Gontougo part of Zanzan, useful mainly as a road stop and local market centre. It will not detain most travelers for long, but it helps break up the geography between Bondoukou and the routes south and west.

If you are moving through by shared taxi, the market area is the most likely place to find food, onward transport, and the ordinary bustle that makes these interior towns more interesting than they look from a highway.
