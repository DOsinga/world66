Most camps and lodges in and around Amboseli can arrange visits to Maasai villages (manyattas) on the park boundaries. The Maasai of the Amboseli area are semi-nomadic pastoralists who have coexisted with — and sometimes clashed with — park management since the reserve was created. A properly arranged community visit, rather than a tourist-trap performance, gives genuine insight into a lifestyle organised around cattle herding, seasonal movement, and a deeply layered traditional culture.

Visits typically include a demonstration of the adumu — the famous jumping dance — and a tour of the thorn-bush enclosure with its low mud-and-dung houses. You will be shown how fire is made by friction, hear about the Maasai age-grade system, and given the chance to look at and buy beadwork and other crafts. Choose lodges and camps that run these visits in partnership with specific communities and share revenue directly — those visits tend to be more authentic and less rehearsed.
