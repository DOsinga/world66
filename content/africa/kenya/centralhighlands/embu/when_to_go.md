---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

Embu's altitude — around 1,450 metres — keeps temperatures moderate year-round, typically between 12°C and 26°C. The area has two rainy seasons: the long rains from March to May and the short rains in October and November. The dry seasons (June to September, and December to February) are the most comfortable times to visit and the best periods for driving the mountain roads and visiting Mwea National Reserve, where wildlife concentrates around the rivers.

The driest months — July and August — are also the coolest, with clear mornings that give the best views of Mount Kenya's peaks from the Embu slopes. If you are using Embu as a base for the Chogoria approach to the mountain, plan for June through October, avoiding the long rains when the forest tracks become difficult.
