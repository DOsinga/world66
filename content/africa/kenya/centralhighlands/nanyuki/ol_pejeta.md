The largest black rhino sanctuary in East Africa, Ol Pejeta also has lions, cheetahs, elephants, and a chimp sanctuary for rescued animals. The conservancy covers 90,000 acres of open grassland and acacia savannah on the Laikipia Plateau, with Mount Kenya as a constant backdrop. Game drives, night drives, and guided walks are all available. The conservancy's anti-poaching work has made it one of the most carefully managed wildlife areas in Kenya.
