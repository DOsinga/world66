title: Things to Do
type: section
---

Runyenjes sits in the green highlands south of Mount Kenya, surrounded by small farms growing coffee, tea, and bananas. The town itself is a market hub, and its weekly open-air market is worth timing a visit around — traders from across Embu County bring fresh produce, livestock, and handmade goods. Beyond the town, the Kirimiri Forest and the twin Nthenge Njeru Falls are the main natural draws. The ancestral Mwene Ndega grove, about 3 km from town, is an important cultural site for the Embu people.
