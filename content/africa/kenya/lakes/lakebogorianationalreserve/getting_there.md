---
title: Getting There
type: section
---

Lake Bogoria lies north of Nakuru and south of Lake Baringo. Most travelers come by private vehicle or safari vehicle via Marigat or Mogotio, often combining the reserve with Baringo on the same loop.

Public transport can get you to nearby towns, but it is awkward for the reserve itself. If you do not have your own vehicle, arrange a driver locally and confirm the gate, road condition, and waiting time before leaving.
