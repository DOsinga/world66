Lake Naivasha is one of Kenya's few freshwater lakes, sitting at about 1,880 metres in the Rift Valley northwest of Nairobi. The lake and its shores support a rich concentration of wildlife — [hippos are common in the shallows](/africa/kenya/lakes/lakenaivasha/hippo_watching), and the birdlife is exceptional, with over 400 species recorded in the area.

The lake makes a good base for exploring the southern Rift Valley. [Hell's Gate National Park](/africa/kenya/nationalparksandreserves/hellsgatenp) is nearby and accessible on foot or by bicycle, and the region is dotted with flower farms that produce a significant share of Europe's cut flowers. [Crescent Island](/africa/kenya/lakes/lakenaivasha/crescent_island) and [Elsamere](/africa/kenya/lakes/lakenaivasha/elsamere) are the standout stops on the south shore.
