Elsamere was the lakeside home of Joy Adamson, author of *Born Free*, the book and film about raising and releasing the lioness Elsa into the wild. The house, set in beautiful gardens on the south shore of Lake Naivasha, is now a conservation centre and museum. You can visit for afternoon tea, which is served on the lawn with black-and-white colobus monkeys dropping from the trees above.

The small museum inside contains Adamson's paintings, personal effects, and exhibits about her work with lions, cheetahs, and leopards. A documentary film about her life is screened during visits. It is about 2 km past Fisherman's Camp on the Lake Road — straightforward to reach by car or bicycle.
