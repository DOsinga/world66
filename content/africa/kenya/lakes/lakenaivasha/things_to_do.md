The lake and its surroundings offer a mix of wildlife viewing, walking, and history.

[Crater Lake](/africa/kenya/lakes/lakenaivasha/crater_lake), on the western side, sits inside a small game sanctuary — a peaceful spot with colobus monkeys and good birdwatching. [Crescent Island](/africa/kenya/lakes/lakenaivasha/crescent_island), reachable by boat from the lakeshore, is a private wildlife sanctuary where you can walk freely among zebras, giraffes, and wildebeest with no fences.

[Elsamere](/africa/kenya/lakes/lakenaivasha/elsamere), the former home of Joy Adamson (author of *Born Free*), is now a conservation centre about 2 km past Fisherman's Camp on the Lake Road. It is open to the public for afternoon tea and has a small museum about Adamson's work with lions and cheetahs.
