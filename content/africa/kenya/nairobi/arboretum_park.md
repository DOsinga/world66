Nairobi Arboretum is a leafy public park west of the city centre, with walking paths, old trees, lawns, and a calmer feel than the busiest central parks.

It works best as a short break from traffic and errands rather than a major expedition. Go in daylight, bring water, and treat it as a relaxed city nature stop.
