Brew Bistro is the best place in Nairobi to drink craft beer — a rooftop bar and restaurant in the heart of Westlands with a wide selection of locally brewed ales, lagers, and stouts alongside international bottles. The rooftop terrace has a view over the Westlands neighbourhood and the hills beyond, and is particularly good at sunset.

The food menu is gastro-pub in style: burgers, wood-fired pizzas, and bar snacks to accompany the beer. The atmosphere is lively but not deafening, which makes it a useful option for conversation. It is popular with Nairobi's young professional crowd and with expats from the nearby diplomatic zone.

Located in Westlands, on the top floor of a building a short walk from the Westlands roundabout.
