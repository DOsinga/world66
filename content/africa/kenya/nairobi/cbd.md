Nairobi's Central Business District is the brash, energetic core of the city — a grid of high-rise towers, colonial-era facades, and constant foot traffic between Kenyatta Avenue and Moi Avenue. At its centre stands the cylindrical **Kenyatta International Convention Centre**, for decades the country's tallest building, with a rooftop viewing deck and helipad open to visitors for the best panorama in Nairobi.

The district mixes commerce and culture. The covered **City Market** trades in crafts, flowers, and curios; the open-air **Maasai Market** sets up for beadwork and carvings; and the green relief of **Uhuru Park** spreads along the western edge beside the old **Railway Museum**. Just across the river on Museum Hill, the **Nairobi National Museum** anchors the city's cultural offering.

It is busy and occasionally chaotic — keep your wits about you after dark — but by day it is the place to feel the pulse of the capital.
