Several excellent day trips are possible from Nairobi. **Lake Nakuru** (about 2 hours north-west) is known for its flamingos and is one of the best places to see black and white rhino. **Lake Naivasha** (1.5 hours) offers boat trips to see hippos and a wide variety of birds from the water's edge. **Amboseli National Park** (4 hours south) gives close views of Kilimanjaro and large elephant herds, better as an overnight stay but feasible in a long day with an early start.

The **Hell's Gate National Park** near Naivasha allows cycling through gorges and geothermal landscape — unusual for an African park in that you can do it without a vehicle. **Ol Doinyo Sabuk National Park**, one hour from the city, is a quick and undervisited escape into forested hills with buffalo and colobus monkeys.
