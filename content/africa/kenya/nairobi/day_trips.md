You barely have to leave the city for your first taste of the bush. [Nairobi National Park](/africa/kenya/nationalparksandreserves/nairobinp) sits just 7 km from downtown, the only major wildlife reserve inside a capital anywhere in the world — lions, rhinos, and giraffes on open plains with the skyline behind them. A half-day morning drive before an afternoon flight is entirely feasible.

Further afield, several excellent full-day trips are possible. [Lake Nakuru National Park](/africa/kenya/nationalparksandreserves/lakenakurunp) (about 2 hours north-west) is known for its flamingos and is one of the best places in Kenya to see both black and white rhino. [Lake Naivasha](/africa/kenya/lakes/lakenaivasha) (1.5 hours) offers boat trips to see hippos and a wide variety of birds from the water's edge. [Amboseli National Park](/africa/kenya/amboseli) (4 hours south) gives close views of Kilimanjaro and large elephant herds — better as an overnight stay, but feasible in a long day with an early start.

The [Hell's Gate National Park](/africa/kenya/nationalparksandreserves/hellsgatenp) near Naivasha lets you cycle through gorges and geothermal landscape — unusual for an African park in that you can do it without a vehicle. Ol Doinyo Sabuk National Park, one hour from the city, is a quick and undervisited escape into forested hills with buffalo and colobus monkeys.
