---
title: "Eating Out"
type: section
---

Nairobi has a serious restaurant scene, particularly in Westlands and Karen. The city's cosmopolitan population means Indian, Chinese, Japanese, Lebanese, and Ethiopian food are all available to a standard well above what you'd find in most East African cities.

For cheap Kenyan street food, look for *hotelies* (small local restaurants) serving ugali (stiff maize porridge), nyama choma (grilled goat or beef), sukuma wiki (collard greens), and githeri (beans and maize). A full meal costs under KSh 200. Choose one that is busy at mealtimes — the food turns over quickly and is always fresh.

Notable restaurants include the [Carnivore](/africa/kenya/nairobi/carnivore_restaurant) (game meats on swords, a genuine institution), [Tamarind](/africa/kenya/nairobi/tamarind_restaurant) (Swahili Coast seafood), [Java House](/africa/kenya/nairobi/java_house) (the city's reliable café chain), [About Thyme](/africa/kenya/nairobi/about_thyme) (garden restaurant in Lavington), and [Tamambo](/africa/kenya/nairobi/tamambo_restaurant) (Karen outdoor terrace).
