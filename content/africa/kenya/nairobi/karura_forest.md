Karura Forest is one of Nairobi's best city escapes, a large protected forest with walking and cycling trails, picnic areas, caves, streams, and a waterfall. It feels surprisingly quiet for somewhere so close to the city.

The forest works well for a half-day outdoors without committing to a full safari-style excursion. Main trails are signposted, and bike hire is often available near the entrances.
