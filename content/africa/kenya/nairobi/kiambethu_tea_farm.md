Kiambethu Tea Farm is a gentle day trip into the green highlands of Limuru, where tea fields roll away from an old farmhouse garden. The standard visit explains how tea is grown and processed, then usually settles into lunch, garden views, and a short walk through patches of indigenous forest.

It is not wild Kenya, and it is not trying to be. The pleasure is the contrast with Nairobi: cooler air, orderly tea rows, colobus monkeys in the trees, and a slower colonial-farm rhythm less than an hour from the city when traffic behaves.
