A rotating street market held at different locations on different days of the week — Westlands on Fridays, the Village Market on Fridays also, and near the City Square on Tuesdays and Thursdays. The vendors are primarily Maasai artisans selling beadwork, shukas (blankets), carved animals, and leather goods. Bargaining is expected and good-natured. The Village Market location is the most accessible and sells the widest range; check current locations as they shift.
