Nairobi Gallery is a central exhibition space near Uhuru Park, used for changing art and cultural shows rather than a single permanent collection. What you see depends on the current programme, so it is worth checking before crossing town.

Its location makes it easy to combine with the Kenyatta International Conference Centre, City Market, or a short walk through the CBD.
