Nairobi Mamba Village is a family-oriented animal park southwest of the city centre, best known for its Nile crocodiles. The appeal is straightforward: crocodile feeding, tortoises, ostriches, boat rides, and a few other light attractions bundled into one easy stop.

It is less essential than Nairobi National Park or the Giraffe Centre, but it can work well if you are travelling with children or want a low-key wildlife stop near Langata. Sunday afternoon crocodile feeding is the main event.
