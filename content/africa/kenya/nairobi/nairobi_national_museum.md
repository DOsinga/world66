The main national museum on Museum Hill, holding collections of Kenyan prehistory, natural history, cultural history, and contemporary art. The anthropology galleries cover Kenya's 44 official ethnic communities; the geology section includes fossils from the Great Rift Valley that pushed back human origins by millions of years. The Joy Adamson Gallery (Born Free) is worth a look, and the snake park in the grounds is the only place in Nairobi you're guaranteed to see one. The café in the courtyard is decent.
