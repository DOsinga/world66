---
title: "Shopping"
type: section
---

The best place to shop in Nairobi is the **City Market** on Muindi Mbingu Street, a covered market selling crafts, beadwork, carvings, and textiles — vendors here are persistent but prices are negotiable. **Maasai Market** runs on rotating days at different outdoor venues around the city and is the best source for Maasai jewellery, blankets, and kikoi fabric.

The **Village Market** in Gigiri is a modern mall popular with the diplomatic community, well-stocked with international brands and a good supermarket. **Westgate Mall** in Westlands and **The Hub Karen** are the other major shopping centres.
