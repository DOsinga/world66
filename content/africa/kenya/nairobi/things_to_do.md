title: "Things to do"
type: section
---

Nairobi punches well above its weight for a city of its size. The wildlife is the obvious draw — lions and rhinos within the city limits is not something most capitals can offer — but the museum, the arts scene, and the community tours all reward time spent here.

Key highlights include the [Nairobi National Park](/africa/kenya/nairobi/nairobi_national_park), the [David Sheldrick Wildlife Trust](/africa/kenya/nairobi/david_sheldrick_wildlife_trust), the [Giraffe Centre](/africa/kenya/nairobi/giraffe_centre), the [Karen Blixen Museum](/africa/kenya/nairobi/karen_blixen_museum), the [Nairobi National Museum](/africa/kenya/nairobi/nairobi_national_museum), the [Railway Museum](/africa/kenya/nairobi/railwaymuseum), and the [Bomas of Kenya](/africa/kenya/nairobi/bomas_of_kenya).
