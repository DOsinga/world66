Uhuru Gardens is one of Nairobi's key national-memory sites. The park is associated with Kenya's independence ceremonies, including the raising of the national flag in 1963, and its monuments frame a broad open space rather than a conventional museum visit.

It is most meaningful if you come with some context for Kenya's independence story, because the site can otherwise feel sparse. Combined with the Nairobi National Museum or the city centre monuments, it helps connect modern Nairobi to the political moment that created the republic.
