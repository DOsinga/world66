---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

Nairobi's high altitude — about 1,660m above sea level — keeps the climate mild year-round. Temperatures rarely exceed 28°C in the warmest months or drop below 10°C at night in the coldest. The sun is strong at this elevation; don't underestimate it.

Kenya has two rainy seasons: the **long rains** from March to May (April is often the wettest month), and the **short rains** from October to December. Both rainy seasons bring afternoon downpours rather than all-day grey skies — mornings are often clear and sunny even in the wettest months. The **dry seasons** (January–February and June–September) are the most reliable for wildlife viewing and outdoor activities.

The best time for a Nairobi National Park visit is **June to September**, when animals concentrate around shrinking water sources. The wildebeest migration in the Masai Mara — a common add-on for Nairobi visitors — peaks in July to October.

The **Nairobi International Book Fair** takes place in September. **Madaraka Day** (June 1) and **Jamhuri Day** (December 12) are the major national holidays; the city fills with parades and public celebrations. Horse racing at the Ngong Racecourse takes place most Sundays between April and July.
