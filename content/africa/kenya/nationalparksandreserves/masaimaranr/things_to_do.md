---
title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Game drives are the heart of any Mara visit, typically two per day: an early-morning drive (leaving camp before sunrise) to catch predators on the hunt and the light on the plains, and a late-afternoon drive as the animals stir again after the midday heat. During July to October, spend as much time as possible near the Mara River and its main crossing points — the wildebeest crossings are unpredictable and you may wait for hours before a herd commits.

Beyond the standard game drive, two splurges are worth it: a sunrise **hot-air balloon ride** over the plains (around 450 USD per person, ending in a champagne bush breakfast), and a visit to a **Maasai village** to meet the community whose land surrounds and includes the reserve. Walking safaris with a Maasai guide offer an entirely different perspective on the ecosystem.

Staying in the **conservancies** (Mara North, Olare Motorogi, Naboisho) rather than the official reserve gives access to off-road driving and night game drives — neither of which is permitted in the main park. The wildlife density is comparable and the exclusivity can be remarkable.
