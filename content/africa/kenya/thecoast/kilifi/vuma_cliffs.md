Vuma Cliffs are a rough, sharp-edged stretch of coral coast near Kilifi, where waves hit the rock hard at high tide. The draw is the view: open sea, spray, and a wilder feel than the town's gentler creek and beach stops.

Wear solid shoes, especially if you plan to walk along the coral rock. It is best treated as a short scenic stop rather than a swimming place.
