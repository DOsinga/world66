---
title: Bars and Cafes
type: section
---

Lamu is a Muslim island and alcohol is sold in very few places. The main exception is **Peponi's** in Shela, whose terrace bar is the social hub of the foreign community and serves cold beer, gin and tonics, and reasonable wine to non-guests as well as guests. The Pool Bar at Lamu House Hotel in town also serves alcohol discreetly.

For tea and coffee, the seafront has half a dozen options. **Whispers Coffee Shop** is the long-standing favourite — Italian espresso, fresh juice, and a shady inner courtyard a couple of streets back from the harbour. **Hapa Hapa** on the waterfront does excellent spiced Swahili coffee. The **Lamu Palace Hotel** terrace is a good spot for a sundowner soft drink.
