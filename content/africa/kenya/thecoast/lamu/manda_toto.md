Manda Toto is a small uninhabited island off Manda, reached by chartered boat from Lamu or, at low tide, from Manda itself. Its appeal is simple: empty sand, clear water, and good snorkelling without the bustle of Shela or Lamu town.

Bring water, food, shade, and a clear pickup arrangement with your boatman. There are no services, which is exactly why the trip works.
