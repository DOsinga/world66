---
title: When to Go
type: section
---

The Lamu archipelago has two distinct seasons. The **kaskazi** (December to March) brings the northeast monsoon — hot, dry days, mostly clear skies, calm seas, and easy dhow sailing. This is peak season and the busiest time, especially around New Year.

The **kusi** (June to September) is the southwest monsoon — cooler air, choppier water, and a stretch of windier afternoons that make beach time less pleasant but sailing exciting. April, May, and November are the long-rains shoulder, when accommodation prices drop sharply and the island is at its quietest.

The single best date to visit is the **Maulidi festival** in the third Islamic month — a week of qaswida singing, dhow races, donkey races, and processions through the streets. Dates shift with the Islamic calendar; check before booking.
