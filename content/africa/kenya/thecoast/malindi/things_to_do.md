type: section
---

Malindi's main attractions are split between the sea and the forest. The [Malindi Marine National Park](/africa/kenya/thecoast/malindi/malindi_marine_national_park) offers snorkelling and diving on protected coral reefs just off the coast. Inland, the [Gedi Ruins](/africa/kenya/thecoast/malindi/gedi_ruins) are the remains of a medieval Swahili town, one of the most atmospheric historical sites on the Kenyan coast. The [Vasco da Gama Pillar](/africa/kenya/thecoast/malindi/vasco_da_gama_pillar) stands on the headland as a reminder that Malindi was already an established trading port when the Portuguese arrived in 1498.
