Casablanca is the long-running open-air bar on Mnazi Moja Road just off Moi Avenue: cold Tuskers, plastic chairs, a kitchen that does decent fries and grilled fish, and an upstairs floor where things turn into a club after midnight. The crowd is mixed and very local, and the music ricochets between Bongo Flava, Afrobeats, and the occasional vintage Lingala. Not glamorous, but reliable.
