Commonwealth War Cemetery Mbaraki is a sober, well-kept pocket of memory in a city better known for Fort Jesus, beaches, and Swahili streets. The graves record soldiers and service personnel connected with East Africa’s wartime campaigns and the Indian Ocean port that Mombasa became.

It is not a major sightseeing stop, but it is worth a quiet detour if you are interested in military history or the less visible layers of the coast. Go respectfully, read the headstones, and combine it with nearby island sights rather than treating it as a standalone outing.
