A scruffy, brightly-lit Swahili cafe on Kibokoni Road in Old Town that draws in everyone from rickshaw drivers to off-duty civil servants. The biryani is the dish to order — a long-cooked rice dish with the meat layered through it, served with raita and pickled limes — and the *mahamri* (slightly sweet, cardamom-perfumed Swahili doughnuts) and *viazi karai* (battered potatoes with tamarind sauce) are the breakfast classics. Everything is cheap, the queues move fast, and the food is consistently good.
