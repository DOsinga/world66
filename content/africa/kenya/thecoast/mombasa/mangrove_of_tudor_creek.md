The mangroves around Tudor Creek are a quieter natural side of Mombasa, where the Kombeni and Tsalu rivers meet tidal forest. Visits are informal: arrange a local boat from Cape Kiberamni or Junda Creek and go for the creek scenery, birdlife, and the contrast with the busy city coast.
