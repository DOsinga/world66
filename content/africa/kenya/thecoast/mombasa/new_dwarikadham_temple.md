New Dwarikadham Temple is a large Krishna Consciousness temple complex in Mombasa. The complex serves the local community and includes temple spaces along with a vegetarian restaurant.

For visitors, it is a short cultural stop rather than a full sightseeing anchor. Dress modestly, be respectful during worship, and check locally for the best visiting times.
