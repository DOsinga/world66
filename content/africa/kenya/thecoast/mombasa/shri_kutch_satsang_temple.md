Shri Kutch Satsang Temple is a Hindu mandir in central Mombasa, built by the local Kutchi community in the 1950s. It is notable as an early Swaminarayan temple outside India and part of the city's long Indian Ocean trading and migration story.

The temple is a short stop rather than a major sight, but it pairs well with Mombasa's Jain Temple, old commercial streets, and other traces of the city's South Asian communities.
