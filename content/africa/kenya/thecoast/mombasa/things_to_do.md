---
title: Things to do
type: section
---

Mombasa rewards a slow wander as much as a tick-list. **Fort Jesus** and the alleys of **Mombasa Old Town** below it are the obvious starting point — a Portuguese fortress, a UNESCO World Heritage Site, and a working Swahili-Arab quarter still trading in spices and carved wooden doors. The **Likoni Ferry** crossing is its own experience, and the **Mombasa Tusks** on Moi Avenue are the city's announcement of itself. Out on the mainland, **Haller Park** and **Mamba Village** in Nyali are the family circuit, and the **Bombolulu Workshops** are the place to actually meet the people making the souvenirs.
