Wild Waters Amusement Park is a modern water park in the Nyali area, with slides, pools, and family-oriented entertainment. It is a practical break from Mombasa's heat and beach routine, especially for travellers with children.

There are also arcade and entertainment facilities on site, so it works best as a half-day family outing rather than a quick sightseeing stop.
