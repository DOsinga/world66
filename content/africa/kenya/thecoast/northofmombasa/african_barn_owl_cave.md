African Barn Owl Cave is a small karst cave north of Mombasa, known for stalactites, stalagmites, and the owls that shelter in the dark chambers. The cave sits about 12 metres underground and is usually visited with a local guide.

It is a rough-edged stop rather than a polished attraction: signage can be poor, and you may need to ask locally for the exact approach. That is part of the appeal if you are exploring the coast beyond the beach resorts.
