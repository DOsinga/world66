Mtwapa Creek is a tidal inlet on the coast north of Mombasa, edged with tropical vegetation, small marinas, and quiet birdwatching spots. It works best as a slow nature stop: walk or cycle around the creek, look for coastal birds, and stay for the softer evening light on the water.
