title: Beaches
type: section
---

Three distinct beaches stretch south from the Likoni ferry: Shelly Beach (closest to Mombasa, convenient for day trips), [Tiwi Beach](/africa/kenya/thecoast/southofmombasa/tiwi_beach) (quiet and uncrowded, good for snorkelling), and [Diani Beach](/africa/kenya/thecoast/southofmombasa/diani_beach) (the main event, 25 kilometres of brilliant white sand with the full range of water sports). Each has a different character; together they give a range of options from day-trippers to long-stayers.
