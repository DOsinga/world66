---
title: Eating Out
type: section
---

Eating on the south coast centres on Diani Beach, where a strip of restaurants caters to the tourist crowd with a range that runs from grilled seafood and Indian Ocean fish to pizza and international menus. Outside Diani, options thin out quickly — Ukunda town has local joints serving rice, beans, and grilled meat at low prices, and Shimoni has a couple of simple places near the ferry jetty. The standout meal on the whole south coast is the seafood lunch served at Mpunguti Restaurant on Wasini Island, included in most Kisite Marine Park day trips.
