A relaxed beachfront restaurant at the northern end of Diani Beach, popular with long-term residents and repeat visitors for its combination of reliable food, cold Tusker, and a direct view over the lagoon. The kitchen turns out grilled fish, calamari, prawn dishes, and burgers. Tables are right on the sand at high tide; a raised terrace provides shelter when needed.

The crowd is a mix of beach tourists, expats, and the kite-surfing community that congregates at this end of the beach. It works as well for a long lunch as for an evening meal. Service is unhurried — bring something to read if you are in a rush.
