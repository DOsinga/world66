At the quieter southern end of Diani Beach, Nomad is attached to the Nomad Beach Hotel and open to non-guests. The bar sits close to the water, with a wide beach in front and a reasonable level of shade. It is less crowded than the bars in the middle section of Diani and has a slightly calmer atmosphere as a result.

Cold beers, cocktails, and fresh juices are the staples. It works well as a mid-afternoon stop on a beach walk along the length of Diani. The kitchen serves light food — sandwiches, salads, and grilled snacks — through the day.
