A national reserve covering hilly coastal forest about 30 kilometres inland from Diani Beach. Shimba Hills is the most accessible wildlife destination from the south coast and makes an excellent half-day or full-day excursion. The forest is lush and green year-round, very different in character from the open savanna of the Masai Mara.

The main attraction is the sable antelope — rare and handsome animals that are not found in any other Kenyan reserve. Forest elephants, buffalo, leopard, and a wide range of birds complete the wildlife list. The forest canopy walk above the treetops is a highlight. Most lodges and tour operators in Diani offer day trips to Shimba Hills; it can also be reached independently by matatu from Ukunda.
