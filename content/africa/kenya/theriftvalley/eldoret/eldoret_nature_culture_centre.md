Eldoret Nature & Culture Centre is a small wildlife and cultural stop on the edge of town. It has animals such as lions, ostriches, hyenas, and snakes, along with reconstructed traditional houses and displays on local culture.

It is not a substitute for a national park safari, but it gives families and short-stay visitors a compact introduction to wildlife and regional heritage without leaving Eldoret for a full day.
