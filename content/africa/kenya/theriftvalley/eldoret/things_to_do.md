---
title: Things to Do
type: section
---

Eldoret's main draw is its running culture — this is where Kenya's distance-running legend was built. The stadium named after Kipchoge Keino anchors the city's athletic identity, and early morning runs with local athletes on the roads around town are possible for visitors who seek them out. Beyond that, the Uasin Gishu plateau offers wheat farming landscapes and a few wildlife and heritage sites worth visiting.
