---
title: When to Go
type: section
---

Eldoret's highland position (about 2,100 metres) keeps the climate mild year-round — warm by day and cool at night, with temperatures rarely exceeding 25°C. The long rains run from March to May; the short rains from October to December. Both periods make the roads wetter but the countryside greener.

The dry seasons (January–February and June–September) offer the most comfortable conditions for travel. The July–August dry season coincides with the best conditions for seeing the plateau farmlands and visiting Iten. There is no exceptional reason to time a visit beyond avoiding the heaviest rains.
