Kakamega is a small town in Western Province, about 65 km north of [Kisumu](/africa/kenya/kisumu). It is the base for trips into the [Kakamega Forest](/africa/kenya/westernkenya/kakamega/kakamega_forest), the easternmost remnant of the great Guineo-Congolian rainforest that once stretched across central Africa. The forest is home to over 380 bird species, colobus and blue monkeys, and a remarkable variety of butterflies and plants found nowhere else in Kenya.

The town itself is not much to look at, but matatus run frequently south to Kisumu and north to Kitale, making it an easy stop on a western Kenya circuit. Be ready for persistent touts at the bus stand — they have a reputation as the most aggressive in the country. Forest visitors usually base at one of the entry stations, [Buyangu](/africa/kenya/westernkenya/kakamega/buyangu_hill) on the north or [Isecheno](/africa/kenya/westernkenya/kakamega/isecheno) on the south, or at the heritage cabin of [Rondo Retreat](/africa/kenya/westernkenya/kakamega/rondo_retreat) inside the forest itself. For a glimpse of distinctively Luhya life, the [Crying Stone of Ilesi](/africa/kenya/westernkenya/kakamega/crying_stone) west of town and the Saturday morning [bullfights](/africa/kenya/westernkenya/kakamega/bullfighting) held in the surrounding villages are worth timing a visit around.
