Kisii is the main town of the Gusii Highlands in southwestern [Kenya](/africa/kenya), a busy commercial centre serving the surrounding tea and coffee country. It sits at around 1,700 metres, which gives it a cooler, greener climate than much of Kenya — the hills around town are intensely cultivated, with terraced fields climbing the slopes in every direction.

The town itself is not a tourist destination, but it is a useful base for exploring [western Kenya](/africa/kenya/westernkenya). The region is known for its soapstone carvings, quarried and carved in the village of [Tabaka](/africa/kenya/westernkenya/kisii/tabaka_soapstone) about 20 km northwest of town. Kisii is also a common stopover on the route between [Nairobi](/africa/kenya/nairobi) and Lake Victoria.

Other things worth a look around town include the busy [municipal market](/africa/kenya/westernkenya/kisii/kisii_municipal_market) and the views from the [Manga Ridge](/africa/kenya/westernkenya/kisii/manga_ridge) to the north. A longer trip out to the [Sondu Miriu gorge](/africa/kenya/westernkenya/kisii/sondu_miriu_gorge) shows the wilder, less farmed edge of the highlands.
