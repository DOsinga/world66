---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

Kisii's altitude (around 1,700 m) keeps it cooler than the lake basin, with daytime temperatures generally between 18 and 28°C all year. The rains come in two seasons: the long rains from March to May and the short rains in October and November. The hills are at their greenest just after the long rains, in June, when the tea estates are at their best.

The drier months — January and February, then July through September — are easiest for road travel. Some of the unpaved tracks out to Tabaka and the Sondu Miriu gorge become awkward in the wet, though Kisii itself remains accessible year-round. Avoid the worst of the long rains if you want to walk the Manga Ridge.
