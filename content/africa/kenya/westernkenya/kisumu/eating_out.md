---
title: Eating Out
type: section
---

Kisumu eats well for a city its size. Fresh tilapia and Nile perch from Lake Victoria are on every menu — fried whole or grilled — and ugali with sukuma wiki (collard greens) is the staple accompaniment. The central market area has cheap lunch spots; the lakefront restaurants are more expensive but worth it for a fish meal with a view.
