---
title: Getting Around
type: section
---

Boda bodas (motorcycle taxis) are the fastest and cheapest way around Kisumu. Agree on a price before getting on. Tuk-tuks are plentiful in the centre and slightly more comfortable for short trips. Matatus run set routes for a few shillings. Walking the city centre is feasible; the lake and outlying sights like Hippo Point and Dunga Beach require a ride.
