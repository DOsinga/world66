Kibuye Market is one of Kisumu's busiest open-air markets, drawing traders and shoppers from the city and the surrounding lake region. It is not a curated craft market; it is a working place of produce, clothes, household goods, repair stalls, noise, and negotiation.

Go with curiosity and a light footprint. Keep valuables secure, ask before photographing people, and treat the market as a way to see Kisumu's everyday economy rather than as a souvenir stop designed for visitors.
