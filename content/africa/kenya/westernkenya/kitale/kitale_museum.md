The Kitale Museum sits in pleasant grounds on the edge of the town centre and covers the natural and cultural history of the western Kenya highlands. The main building houses ethnographic displays from the region's communities alongside natural history exhibits focused on the flora and fauna of Mount Elgon and the surrounding highlands.

The museum grounds include a nature trail that winds through a patch of indigenous forest — home to some of Kenya's western highland bird species that are hard to see elsewhere. An agroforestry demonstration project adjoins the museum. Allow a couple of hours for a thorough visit. The museum is run by the National Museums of Kenya and is open daily except public holidays.
