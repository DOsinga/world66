Treasures of Africa Museum is a small cultural museum in Kitale built around a collection of regional artefacts assembled by a British colonial administrator. The displays focus on material culture from western Kenya and nearby communities.

It pairs naturally with Kitale Museum if you are using the town as a base for Mount Elgon or the surrounding highlands.
