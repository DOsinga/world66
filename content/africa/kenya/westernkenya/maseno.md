Maseno is a small town in western [Kenya](/africa/kenya), sitting almost exactly on the equator near the shores of Lake Victoria. It is best known as an educational centre — [Maseno University](/africa/kenya/westernkenya/maseno/maseno_university) and [Maseno School](/africa/kenya/westernkenya/maseno/maseno_school), one of the oldest schools in East Africa (founded in 1906), are both here.

The town itself is quiet and unassuming, surrounded by green, hilly countryside. It makes a possible stop when travelling between [Kisumu](/africa/kenya/westernkenya/kisumu) on Lake Victoria and Kakamega Forest, one of the last remnants of tropical rainforest in Kenya. The university campus straddles the equator, making it one of the few places in the world where you can literally stand on the line dividing the northern and southern hemispheres.
