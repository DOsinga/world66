Mungoma Caves are tied to Maragoli tradition as the home of Mulogoli, the community's ancestor. The cave system is dark and confusing enough that first-time visitors should not try to wander in alone.

Go with an experienced local guide and wear shoes that can handle rough, damp ground. The draw is less a polished show cave than a place where landscape, oral history, and local identity meet.
