title: Things to Do
type: section
---

Pony trekking is the heart of what Malealea offers. The Malealea Lodge arranges treks of all lengths — a three-hour circuit suited to beginners, full-day rides to viewpoints and waterfalls, and multi-day expeditions that stop overnight in remote Basotho villages reachable by no other means. The horses are sure-footed Basotho ponies, bred for the highlands, and the guides know every trail.

Hiking and mountain biking are well-catered alternatives. Rock art sites — Bushman paintings left by San hunter-gatherers before the Basotho settled the highlands — are within day walking distance, and the landscape is dramatic enough to justify a long walk for its own sake. The Gates of Paradise Pass at the village entrance is itself a striking formation: a narrow rock gateway opening into the valley.

The Malealea Lodge organizes community tours taking in the small village museum, a visit to a local chief, and a traditional sorghum beer brewery. Evenings bring communal dinners and often Basotho traditional dancing performed by community members.
