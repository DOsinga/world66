---
title: When to Go
type: section
---

The best months to visit are April to September — Lesotho's autumn and winter. Days are clear, cold at altitude, and the trails are dry and firm. Winter nights (June–August) drop well below freezing at Malealea's 2,000-metre elevation, so bring proper warm layers even if you visit in summer.

October to March brings the rainy season. The landscape turns lush and green, waterfalls run full, and the mountain wildflowers are at their best — but mountain trails become muddy and river crossings can be impassable after heavy rain. Check conditions with the lodge before setting out on multi-day treks in the wet season.

Easter weekend draws a large crowd to Malealea and the lodge fills fast; book well in advance if you plan to visit then.
