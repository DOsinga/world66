Maseru is a good base for short trips into the highlands and along the Caledon River. **Thaba Bosiu**, the mountain fortress of King Moshoeshoe I, is the essential single trip — 25 km east, easily managed in half a day with a guide.

**Roma**, the university town 35 km south-east, has a striking sandstone Catholic seminary and a colonial-era hospital. **Morija**, 40 km south, holds the country's best museum and the country's main arts festival each September. For something more demanding, the southern road into the Senqu (Orange) River valley reaches the **Maletsunyane Falls** at Semonkong in about three hours.
