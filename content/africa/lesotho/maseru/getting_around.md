---
title: Getting Around
type: section
---

Central Maseru is walkable in a couple of hours, and most of the city's hotels, restaurants and government buildings cluster along Kingsway and the streets that branch off it. For longer trips around town, **shared minibus taxis** (M10–15) work the main routes from a chaotic rank below the main market.

Metered taxis are scarce; **regular taxis** can be ordered through your hotel or the e-hailing apps (Yango and Bolt operate in Maseru). For day trips out of the city — Thaba Bosiu, Roma, Morija — your best bet is a private car or a half-day tour from one of the city hotels. Hire cars from South Africa generally cross the border without trouble; check insurance covers Lesotho.
