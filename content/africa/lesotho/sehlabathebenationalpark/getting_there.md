---
title: Getting There
type: section
---

The park lies in Qacha's Nek District in far southeastern Lesotho. Most visitors need a sturdy vehicle, local advice, and plenty of time; roads can be rough, slow, and affected by rain or snow.

Trips can be arranged from Maseru through specialist operators, but this is a long journey rather than a quick excursion. If approaching from South Africa via the Drakensberg side, check border and trail conditions before relying on Bushman's Nek.
