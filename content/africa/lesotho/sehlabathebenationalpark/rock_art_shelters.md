Sehlabathebe has documented rock-art sites, part of the same cultural landscape that makes the Drakensberg famous. These shelters are not museum displays; they sit in the open mountain environment, vulnerable to weather and careless visitors.

Go with a guide if you can. Do not touch the paintings, wet them for photographs, or mark the rock around them. The best visit is quiet and brief, with the shelter left exactly as you found it.
