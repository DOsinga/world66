Sehlabathebe Airstrip is not a tourist sight in the usual sense, but it says a lot about the area's remoteness. In mountain regions like this, small airstrips can matter for access, emergencies, and occasional charter movements.

Most travelers will arrive by road, not air. Still, the airstrip is a useful landmark when planning routes around Mavuka, the park entrance, and the wider Qacha's Nek highlands.
