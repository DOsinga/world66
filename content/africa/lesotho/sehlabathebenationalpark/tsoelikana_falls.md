Tsoelikana Falls is usually described as the park's largest waterfall and one of its classic hiking targets. The route crosses open plateau country, so the walk is as much a part of the experience as the fall itself.

Ask at the park or lodge for current route advice before setting out. Water levels, mist, and ground conditions can change the feel of the walk quickly, and the lack of crowds means you should not rely on simply following other hikers.
