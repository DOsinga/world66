---
title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Buchanan's attractions are mostly about atmosphere — long Atlantic beaches, a working iron-ore port, and a handful of nineteenth-century settler buildings across the river in Edina. A day or two is usually enough.

The **Port of Buchanan** is the biggest thing in town: a deep-water harbour built by LAMCO in the 1960s for shipping iron ore from the Nimba mountains, now repurposed for general cargo and a renewed iron-ore export operation. Walking the long, mostly empty **Buchanan Beach** is the obvious thing to do at sunset. Across the Saint John River, the old settler town of **Edina** is worth a half-day visit for its handful of surviving timber houses and the sense of an older Liberia.
