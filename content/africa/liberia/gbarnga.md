*Africa > [Liberia](/africa/liberia) > Gbarnga*

Gbarnga is the capital of Bong County and the main inland city of [Liberia](/africa/liberia) — a busy market and transit town roughly 180 km northeast of [Monrovia](/africa/liberia/monrovia) along one of the better roads in the country. It sits in the rolling green hill country of the central highlands, surrounded by Kpelle villages, rubber plantations and small-scale farms.

For travelers, Gbarnga is mostly a stop on the way to somewhere else — north into Nimba County and on towards Guinea, or as the jumping-off point for the spectacular [Kpatawee Waterfall](/africa/liberia/gbarnga/kpatawee_waterfall), a triple-cascade falls hidden in the forest about 50 km west of town. But the city has its own quiet character: a long main road lined with shops and shared-taxi stands, a sizeable market that swells on weekly market days, and the looming [Holy Spirit Cathedral](/africa/liberia/gbarnga/holy_spirit_cathedral) which serves as the seat of the local Catholic diocese.

Gbarnga was at the centre of some of the heaviest fighting during the first Liberian civil war — Charles Taylor used it as his de facto capital for several years in the early 1990s, and the [Gbarnga Central Prison](/africa/liberia/gbarnga/gbarnga_central_prison) and other landmarks from that period are still visible. Today the town is rebuilding steadily; [Cuttington University](/africa/liberia/gbarnga/cuttington_university), the oldest private university in West Africa, sits a few kilometres east and brings a steady student population to the area.

Don't come to Gbarnga expecting tourist infrastructure — it is a working Liberian town. But for travelers wanting to see the country away from the coast, it is an easy day's journey from Monrovia and an honest introduction to the central highlands.
