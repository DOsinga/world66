---
title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

The main draw in the Gbarnga area is the **Kpatawee Waterfall**, a beautiful three-tier cascade about 50 km west of town. In Gbarnga itself, the **Holy Spirit Cathedral** is the most prominent landmark, and the central **Gbarnga Market** is the busiest in the central highlands. A few kilometres east, **Cuttington University** has a small museum of Liberian ethnography that is worth a visit when open.

For those interested in recent history, the town's central prison and the few visible remnants from its years as Charles Taylor's wartime headquarters give a sobering counterpoint to the bustle of the modern market.
