Greenville is the capital of Sinoe County in southeastern [Liberia](/africa/liberia), about 350 km from Monrovia — the first 100 km paved, the rest dirt road. The journey is an adventure in itself and can take the better part of a day.

The town was founded as a settlement for freed American slaves in the 1830s, and some of the [original colonial-era buildings](/africa/liberia/greenville/colonial_quarter) still stand — wide-verandaed timber houses raised above the ground, in a style the settlers brought from the American South. The population is predominantly Kru and Bassa, and the town serves as a small port and regional hub on the [Sinoe lagoon](/africa/liberia/greenville/sinoe_lagoon).

Greenville is remote and facilities are basic, but it offers a genuine glimpse of a part of Liberia that few outsiders see. [Sapo National Park](/africa/liberia/greenville/sapo_national_park), Liberia's only national park, is reachable from here and holds pygmy hippos, forest elephants and one of the largest intact rainforests in West Africa.
