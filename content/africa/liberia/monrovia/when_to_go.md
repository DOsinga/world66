---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

Liberia has a pronounced wet season (May to October) when rainfall can be very heavy and roads outside the city become difficult. The dry season (November to April) is the more comfortable time to visit, with lower humidity and manageable temperatures. December to February is the coolest and most pleasant period.

The Harmattan wind blows from the Sahara between December and February, carrying dry, dusty air that reduces visibility and coats everything with a fine film of reddish dust. It is still an acceptable time to visit — just keep expectations low for clear photographs.

If you plan to visit Sapo National Park, the dry season is essential — the park becomes largely inaccessible during heavy rains.
