---
title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Robertsport's draws are the **surf breaks** along the cape — **Cottons Point**, **Fisherman's Point**, **Inner Cottons**, **Cassava** and **Shipwrecks** — and the empty Atlantic beaches that link them. Beyond surfing, climb **Cape Mount** for sweeping views over the lagoon and ocean, take a pirogue across **Lake Piso** for birdwatching and a swim in calmer water, and wander the ruins of the **Tubman Center of African Culture** and the older nineteenth-century settler buildings.

The town's curious **ghost ship** — a rusting oil tanker that grounded itself on a remote beach further up the coast in 2009 — is a slightly grim but compelling sight if you can find a local with a boat or vehicle to get you there.
