---
title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Sanniquellie itself is small — the main sight is the **Sanniquellie Declaration Monument** marking the 1959 summit of Tubman, Touré and Nkrumah that paved the way for African unity. Beyond town, the real attractions of Nimba County are within reach: the planned mining town of **Yekepa** with its strange mix of abandoned LAMCO modernism and active iron ore operations, the dramatic ridge of **Mount Nimba** on the Guinean border (a UNESCO Biosphere Reserve), and the surviving rainforest of the **Nimba National Forest** on its lower slopes.
