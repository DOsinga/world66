---
title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Sapo is not a park for casual sightseeing — its appeal is the forest itself and the chance of encounters with **pygmy hippopotamus**, **forest elephants**, **chimpanzees** and the rich birdlife of the Upper Guinean rainforest. Treks of two to five days from the main ranger station at **Jalay's Town** are the standard way to see it. The **Sinoe River** and other forest watercourses are good spots for wildlife sightings and for an idea of how a really intact West African rainforest looks and sounds.
