title: Things to Do
type: section
---

Awjila is a small place — half a day is enough to see the village itself. The real reason to come is the [Atiq Mosque](/africa/libya/awjila/atiq_mosque), one of the oldest and most unusual mosques in the Sahara, and a slow wander through the [old town](/africa/libya/awjila/old_town) and surrounding [palm groves](/africa/libya/awjila/palm_groves). Make time for a short drive into the [surrounding desert](/africa/libya/awjila/sahara_landscape) at sunrise or sunset.
