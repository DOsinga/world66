The Derna waterfalls lie south of the city in the Jebel Akhdar foothills, on a seasonal tributary near Wadi Derna. They are one of the few places in Libya where fresh water, greenery and exposed limestone meet in a recognisably mountain setting.

Access depends on road and security conditions, and the area should be checked locally before setting out. If reachable, it is best visited as a short excursion from the city rather than an independent wander into the wadi.
