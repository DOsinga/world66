Derna's harbour and seafront show the city's working Mediterranean side. Fishing boats, port activity and the open coast give a different feel from the tight wadi streets inland.

The seafront can be a useful place to understand the city's geography: sea in front, mountains behind and the wadi cutting through the centre. Check local advice before walking near port infrastructure or flood-affected areas.
