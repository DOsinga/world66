---
title: When to Go
type: section
---

Derna's position near the Jebel Akhdar gives it a wetter, greener feel than most Libyan cities. Spring can be the most attractive season, with milder temperatures and more life in the hills.

Heavy rain is not just inconvenient here; the wadi system has a history of dangerous flooding. Avoid travelling during storm periods, and check local warnings carefully.
