Abu Zar al Ghifari Mosque is one of the most visible landmarks in modern El Merj. Its pale exterior and simple profile stand out against the low-rise rebuilt city.

This is an active religious building rather than a tourist monument. Dress modestly, avoid prayer times unless invited, and ask before photographing people.
