  The Nafusa Mountains have long been one of Libya's Amazigh heartlands. In towns like Yafran, language and local architecture carry a history that is older and more complicated than the modern state's coastal politics.
---

The older parts of Yafran are worth a slow look for their stone lanes, simple houses, and mountain-town layout. This is not a restored museum quarter; it is a lived-in place where the interest comes from texture rather than grand monuments.

The best approach is to walk with someone local who can explain what you are seeing and keep you out of private spaces. Look especially at how houses use stone, shade, and slope to deal with the mountain climate.
