Misurata is Libya's third largest city, with a population of around 300,000. It sits on the site of an ancient Phoenician trading post, some 200 km east of [Tripoli](/africa/libya/tripoli) and 825 km west of [Benghazi](/africa/libya/benghazi).

The city was one of the main battlegrounds of the 2011 revolution — a prolonged and bloody siege left parts of it heavily damaged. The [War Museum](/africa/libya/misurata/war_museum) documents that siege in raw detail, with captured weapons and personal testimony from the defenders. The city has since been substantially rebuilt and is a busy commercial hub for western Libya, centered on its important Mediterranean port.

There is little specifically for tourists here, but it is a natural stopping point on the coastal road between the two main cities. The [old town](/africa/libya/misurata/old_town) area and its souq retain some character. Security conditions have been variable since 2011 — check current advisories before travelling.
