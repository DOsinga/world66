type: section
---

Misurata is primarily a commercial and port city rather than a tourist destination, and the damage and disruption from the 2011 conflict has meant that the city's infrastructure and tourism facilities remain limited. For travelers passing through on the coastal route, the old town area retains some character, and the War Museum is a significant record of the 2011 siege. The city has a Mediterranean coastline with beaches, though access and safety vary. Check current conditions before visiting — Libya has experienced periodic conflict since 2011.
