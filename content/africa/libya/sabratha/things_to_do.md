title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Sabratha's entire appeal is the ancient city. The theatre is the headline — one of the best-preserved Roman stage buildings in the world — but the surrounding ruins of the forum, temples, and baths reward an unhurried walk through. Allow two to three hours for a full visit, more if you linger in the museum.

Access and safety depend heavily on the current security situation in Libya. Check conditions carefully before attempting to visit.
