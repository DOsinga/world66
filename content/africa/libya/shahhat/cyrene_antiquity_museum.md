The Cyrene Antiquity Museum is a small archaeological museum connected to the ancient site. When open, it helps make sense of the ruins by gathering sculpture, inscriptions, and finds from the surrounding area.

Opening conditions can be unpredictable, so treat the museum as a bonus rather than the anchor of your visit. If you do get inside, look for objects that show Cyrene's hybrid identity: Greek foundations, Roman wealth, and a Cyrenaican landscape that shaped both.
