title: Getting There
type: section
---

Shahhat is in eastern Libya's Jebel Akhdar, about 24 km east of Bayda and inland from [Apollonia](/africa/libya/apollonia). The nearest practical approach is by road as part of an organised itinerary through Cyrenaica.

Do not assume that archaeological sites in eastern Libya are freely accessible at any given moment. Check security conditions, permissions, and local escort requirements before building plans around Shahhat.
