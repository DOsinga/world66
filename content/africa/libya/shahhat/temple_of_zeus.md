The Temple of Zeus is Cyrene's most monumental ruin, with massive column drums and reconstructed elements giving a sense of the scale the sanctuary once had. Even fragmented, it feels ambitious and imposing.

Its setting is part of the drama. The temple stands apart from the densest part of the ancient city, so the approach gives you time to absorb its size. Bring water and sun protection; this is not a shaded museum experience.
