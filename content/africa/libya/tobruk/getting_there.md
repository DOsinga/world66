title: Getting There
type: section
---

Tobruk lies in eastern Libya, closer to the Egyptian border than to [Benghazi](/africa/libya/benghazi). Road journeys usually form part of a longer Cyrenaica itinerary, sometimes continuing toward the border when conditions allow.

The city has an airport and a port, but visitor logistics depend heavily on current security conditions, permits, and the availability of licensed operators. Confirm arrangements locally rather than relying on old schedules.
