The two principal reasons most people travel to Libya are both within day-trip range of Tripoli. [Sabratha](/africa/libya/sabratha) is 65km west — a Roman theatre in near-perfect condition standing right at the sea. [Leptis Magna](/africa/libya/leptismagna) is 130km east and on a different scale entirely: arguably the best-preserved Roman city outside Italy, with a forum, a basilica, a circus, and baths spread across a vast site. Both require a licensed guide and advance planning given the security situation, but both are extraordinary.
