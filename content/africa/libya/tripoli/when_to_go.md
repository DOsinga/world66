---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

The best months to visit Tripoli are October through April, when temperatures are comfortable — typically 15–25°C — and the Mediterranean light is clear. Summer (June–September) brings intense heat, with temperatures regularly exceeding 40°C. The ghibli, a hot desert wind from the south, can push temperatures even higher for days at a time and fills the air with fine sand.

Ramadan affects opening hours significantly. Restaurants close during daylight hours, and some sites have reduced access. Travel during Ramadan is possible but requires flexibility. Eid al-Fitr and Eid al-Adha are major holidays when businesses and government offices shut entirely.

The security situation in Tripoli is the more significant variable than the weather — monitor advisories from your government closely regardless of when you plan to visit.
