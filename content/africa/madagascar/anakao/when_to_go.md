---
title: When to Go
type: section
---

The best time to visit Anakao is during the dry season, from April to November. The weather is clear and calm, the sea is flat enough for pirogue excursions, and the snorkelling visibility is at its best. July to September is ideal if you want to see humpback whales — the migration brings large numbers close to the coast during these months.

December to March brings the cyclone season and significantly rougher conditions. Boat crossings can be cancelled for days at a time when the weather turns, and the journey from Toliara becomes more difficult. Accommodation options are limited at any time of year, so booking ahead is advisable for the dry season.
