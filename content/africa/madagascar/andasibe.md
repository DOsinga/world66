Andasibe is the closest national park area to Madagascar's capital and one of the island's most rewarding wildlife destinations. The name refers both to a small railway town and to the complex of protected rainforest around it — specifically the Analamazoatra Special Reserve and the larger Mantadia National Park, together designated Andasibe-Mantadia. The drive east from [Antananarivo](/africa/madagascar/antananarivo) on Route Nationale 2 takes about three hours through hill rice paddies and eucalyptus plantations before dropping into dense rainforest.

The star attraction is the [indri](/africa/madagascar/andasibe/indri_lemur), Madagascar's largest lemur, a black-and-white creature that can weigh over seven kilograms and carries an extraordinary voice — a haunting, wailing call that carries through the forest for more than a kilometre. Indri live in small family groups and vocalize most reliably in the early morning. The [Analamazoatra Reserve](/africa/madagascar/andasibe/analamazoatra_reserve), a compact 1,500-hectare block right next to the village, is the best place to find them with a guide. Mantadia, accessible by vehicle further into the park, is wilder and less visited, with denser forest and more species of chameleon.

The forest holds eleven lemur species in total, including the [diademed sifaka](/africa/madagascar/andasibe/diademed_sifaka), black-and-white ruffed lemur, woolly lemur, and the nocturnal aye-aye. Over 100 bird species have been recorded here, including several endemics. Chameleons are particularly easy to spot on night walks — guides sweep torches along branches where the lizards glow white in the beam. The climate is cool and wet year-round, with an average temperature around 20°C and rain on about 200 days a year.

There is a small cluster of guesthouses and lodges in and around the village of Andasibe, ranging from budget rooms to more comfortable eco-lodges in the forest itself. All park visits require a licensed guide, which can be arranged at the park entrance. The [Vakona Lodge](/africa/madagascar/andasibe/vakona_lodge) area nearby has a small lemur island where habituated individuals can be observed at close range.
