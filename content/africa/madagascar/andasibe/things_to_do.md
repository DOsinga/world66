---
title: Things to Do
type: section
---

Andasibe is first and foremost a wildlife destination. The core activity is guided walks in the rainforest — morning walks for lemurs, evening and night walks for chameleons, frogs, and nocturnal lemurs. Every park visit requires a licensed guide, hired at the park entrances.

The [Analamazoatra Reserve](/africa/madagascar/andasibe/analamazoatra_reserve) is the place to start: compact, well-guided, and virtually guaranteed to show you [indri](/africa/madagascar/andasibe/indri_lemur) on a morning walk. For a more immersive experience, [Mantadia National Park](/africa/madagascar/andasibe/mantadia_national_park) requires a vehicle and a full day but offers wilder forest and better chances of [diademed sifaka](/africa/madagascar/andasibe/diademed_sifaka). Night walks in Analamazoatra are outstanding for [chameleons](/africa/madagascar/andasibe/chameleon_trail) and nocturnal lemurs. [Birding](/africa/madagascar/andasibe/birding) rewards early risers with a high density of Madagascar endemics. The managed [Lemur Island](/africa/madagascar/andasibe/vakona_lodge) at Vakona Lodge offers close contact with habituated animals — a different experience from wild viewing, but popular and worthwhile.
