The Vakona Forest Lodge operates a small private reserve and a feature called Lemur Island — an area popular with tour groups who want guaranteed close contact with habituated lemurs. Small boats ferry visitors to an island where lemurs roam freely and approach for food. It is unambiguously a managed tourist experience, quite different from tracking wild lemurs in the park, but the opportunity to observe animal behaviour and individual personalities at close range has real educational value.

The lodge itself sits in private forest adjacent to the national park and offers guided walks through their own reserve, which can supplement a morning in Analamazoatra with a quieter afternoon circuit. The forest on the lodge property harbours several of the same species found in the park and gets significantly less visitor pressure. For travellers who want a comfortable base with en-suite rooms and a restaurant, Vakona is one of the better options in the area.
