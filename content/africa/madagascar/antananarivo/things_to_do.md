---
title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Antananarivo rewards visitors who slow down and look past the traffic. The ruined Rova crowns the highest hill, jacaranda trees ring Lake Anosy in October, and the sprawling Analakely market pulses with everyday Malagasy life. Day trips reach the UNESCO-listed royal hill of Ambohimanga and lemur reserves in the surrounding countryside. The city's cafe and restaurant scene is small but genuine, shaped by the Malagasy–French hybrid culture that defines Tana's character.
