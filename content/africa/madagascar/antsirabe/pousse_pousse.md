The pousse-pousse — a two-wheeled cart pulled by a running man — is the defining sight and sound of Antsirabe. Hundreds of them operate throughout the town, and arriving visitors are met immediately by a crowd of drivers competing for custom. The competition keeps prices low, and a short ride across town costs almost nothing by any Western measure.

It is worth taking at least one ride for the experience: the speed, the skill of navigation through traffic and pedestrians, the calls between drivers, and the view from the slightly elevated seat. Choose a young driver if you want to move quickly. The drivers are almost always young men from the surrounding highlands trying to earn enough to send money home, and the fare — though low — is real income. Tipping is appreciated and not expected in the transaction itself.
