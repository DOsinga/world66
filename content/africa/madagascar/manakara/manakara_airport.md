Manakara Airport is a minor practical landmark rather than a sightseeing stop, but it says something about the town's geography. The runway sits close to the coast and near the rail corridor, a reminder that Manakara has always been defined by awkward connections.

Do not build a trip around flights unless you have fresh confirmation. For most independent travellers, the airport is useful mainly as a point of orientation on the north side of town.
