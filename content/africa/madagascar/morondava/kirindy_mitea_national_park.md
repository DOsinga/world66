Kirindy Mitea National Park protects one of western Madagascar's richest mixes of dry forest, baobabs, lakes, dunes, and coastal habitat. From Morondava it is a serious excursion rather than a casual stroll, but the reward is a quieter, wilder counterpoint to the famous Avenue of the Baobabs.

The park is strongest for travelers who care about landscapes and endemic wildlife. Access can be rough and conditions seasonal, so arrange the trip locally and do not underestimate distances on Madagascar's west-coast roads.
