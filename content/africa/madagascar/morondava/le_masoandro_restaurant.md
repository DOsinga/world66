Le Masoandro Restaurant at Chez Maggie is a beachside place in Nosy Kely,
known for seafood and sunset views over the Mozambique Channel. It is on the
more expensive end for Morondava, but the setting under the palms is part of
the appeal.

West-facing and located directly on the beach, Le Masoandro Restaurant
and Bar is one of the better spots in town to catch the evening light.
