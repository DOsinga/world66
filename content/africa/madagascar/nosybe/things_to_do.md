title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Nosy Be is built around its beaches and the warm Indian Ocean, but there is more to it than sand. Lemurs in primary rainforest, a sacred volcanic crater lake, and a string of smaller islands a short pirogue ride away — the island repays a few days of unhurried exploration. Hell-Ville, the main town, supplies the boats, the bookings and the practical services.
