Ranomafana is both a village and a rainforest destination, but travellers come for the forest. The national park protects a wet, steep, tangled slice of eastern Madagascar where mist hangs in the valleys and lemurs move through bamboo, palms, and mossy trees.

This is one of the most accessible places in the country to look for rainforest wildlife. [Ranomafana National Park](/africa/madagascar/ranomafana/ranomafana_national_park) is famous for bamboo lemurs, including the golden bamboo lemur whose scientific discovery helped push the area toward protection. Guided walks are compulsory and worthwhile; the forest is dense, slippery, and much more alive when someone can read the sounds and movement.

The village sits outside the park and is known for its [thermal pool](/africa/madagascar/ranomafana/thermal_pool), a very welcome idea after wet trails. Nearby [Andriamamovoka Falls](/africa/madagascar/ranomafana/andriamamovoka_falls), [Centre ValBio](/africa/madagascar/ranomafana/centre_valbio), and the [Namorona River](/africa/madagascar/ranomafana/namorona_river) round out the picture: Ranomafana is not just a checklist of lemurs, but a whole damp mountain landscape.
