Andriamamovoka Falls is a worthwhile short outing near Ranomafana, especially if you want a break from forest trails focused on wildlife. The falls sit in the same wet, steep landscape that makes the national park so lush.

Go with local advice on access, because paths and conditions can change after rain. The reward is not a huge spectacle so much as a good sense of the water-shaped terrain around Ranomafana.
