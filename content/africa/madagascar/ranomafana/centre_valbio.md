Centre ValBio is a research and conservation centre near Ranomafana National Park. For travellers, its value is in showing that the forest is not just scenery: it is a living laboratory, a conservation problem, and a source of local livelihoods.

Access for casual visitors can vary, so check before turning up. Even from the outside, the centre helps explain why Ranomafana matters internationally and why guided tourism here is tied so closely to science and protection.
