Ranomafana means "hot water", and the village thermal pool is the most literal expression of the name. It is not a luxury spa; it is a simple public bath fed by warm mineral water, and that simplicity is exactly why it works.

After a damp forest walk, a soak here feels earned. Bring a towel, keep expectations practical, and check locally for current opening arrangements before promising yourself a post-hike bath.
