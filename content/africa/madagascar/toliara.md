Toliara, still widely called Tulear, is the hot, dusty capital of Madagascar's southwest. It is the end of the classic RN7 route from the highlands and the gateway to a very different island: thorn forest, baobabs, Vezo fishing villages, reef lagoons, and huge dry skies.

The city itself is useful rather than beautiful, though it has more character than a quick transit stop suggests. Pousse-pousse fill the streets, the market is busy, and the coast gives everything a bleached, salty edge. [Musee Cedratom](/africa/madagascar/toliara/musee_cedratom) is the best in-town stop for context, while the [Toliara waterfront](/africa/madagascar/toliara/toliara_waterfront) is worth a late-day wander.

Most of the strongest sights are just outside the city. The [Arboretum d'Antsokay](/africa/madagascar/toliara/arboretum_d_antsokay) is the essential introduction to the spiny forest, [Reniala Reserve](/africa/madagascar/toliara/reniala_reserve) adds baobabs and birdlife near Ifaty, and the [Fiherenana River](/africa/madagascar/toliara/fiherenana_river) marks the dry country around town. Toliara also launches trips to [Anakao](/africa/madagascar/anakao), which is where the coast starts to feel like a proper escape.
