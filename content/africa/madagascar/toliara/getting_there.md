title: Getting There
type: section
---

Toliara is the southern end of the classic RN7 road journey from [Antananarivo](/africa/madagascar/antananarivo), [Antsirabe](/africa/madagascar/antsirabe), [Fianarantsoa](/africa/madagascar/fianarantsoa), and [Isalo](/africa/madagascar/isalo). Taxi-brousse trips are long and tiring; private vehicles make the route far more manageable.

The airport has flights to Antananarivo when schedules cooperate. For [Anakao](/africa/madagascar/anakao), arrange boat transfers or overland-and-boat combinations in town rather than assuming you can improvise easily at the last minute.
