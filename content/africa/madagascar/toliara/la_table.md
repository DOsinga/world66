La Table is a small flat-topped hill outside Toliara, beyond the Arboretum d'Antsokay. The short climb gives wide views over the dry landscape around the city and makes an easy add-on to the arboretum.

It is not a full hike, but the viewpoint is a good break if you have your own transport or do not mind the walk from the arboretum area.
