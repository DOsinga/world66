Musee Cedratom is Toliara's most useful cultural stop, especially if you want a little context before heading into Vezo fishing country or the dry interior. Collections and displays cover regional life, natural history, and the cultures of Madagascar's southwest.

Do not expect a big-city museum. Its value is local: a quiet hour that helps connect the city outside with the people, landscapes, and traditions of the surrounding region.
