The Tortoise Reserve near Ifaty protects radiated and spider tortoises that have been seized from trafficking or removed from the wild. Guided visits lead past enclosures with tortoises of different ages and explain the animals' role in the spiny forest ecosystem.

It is a focused, worthwhile stop if you are visiting Ifaty or the nearby reserves around Toliara.
