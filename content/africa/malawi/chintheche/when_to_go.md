---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

The best time to visit Chintheche is during the dry season, from May to October. Skies are clear, humidity is lower, and the roads are reliably passable. Lake Malawi is calm and perfect for swimming throughout this period, and underwater visibility for snorkelling is at its best.

November to April is the rainy season. Heavy downpours are common in the afternoons, and the red dirt roads can become muddy and difficult. That said, the landscape turns intensely green, temperatures remain warm, and accommodation prices drop. Serious divers and snorkellers sometimes prefer January to March when phytoplankton blooms clear up, giving exceptional underwater clarity close to shore.

Temperatures along the lakeshore are warm year-round, typically between 20°C and 32°C. The altitude is low enough that nights stay pleasant rather than cold.
