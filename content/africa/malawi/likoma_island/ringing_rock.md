Ringing Rock is a concave boulder on Likoma's northwestern shore that gives a bell-like sound when tapped with a stone. Local guides from the nearby village often take visitors there and ask for a community payment.

It is a small curiosity rather than a major sight, but it makes a useful reason to walk beyond the cathedral and beaches.
