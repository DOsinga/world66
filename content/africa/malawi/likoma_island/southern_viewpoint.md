This small rocky viewpoint above the south coast is a good target for a walk or moto-taxi ride if you are staying on the island's western side. The view runs over the lake, the pale beaches below, and the Mozambican shore beyond.

It is modest, but on Likoma the landscape works by accumulation: lake light, granite, sand, and the feeling of being a long way from the mainland.
