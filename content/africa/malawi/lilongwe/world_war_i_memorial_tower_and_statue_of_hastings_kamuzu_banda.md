The World War I Memorial Tower and the statue of Hastings Kamuzu Banda form one of Lilongwe's most visible ceremonial landmarks. The site combines imperial war memory with the post-independence figure who dominated Malawi's early political life.

It is not a museum-style stop, but it is a useful landmark for understanding Capital Hill and the official side of the city. Visitors interested in Malawi's public monuments can combine it with nearby government buildings and the quieter avenues around the administrative district.
