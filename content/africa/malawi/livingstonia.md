Livingstonia sits high above Lake Malawi on the escarpment north of [Nkhata Bay](/africa/malawi/nkhata_bay), cool, quiet, and improbable after the heat of the lakeshore. It is not a conventional town so much as a mission settlement spread along red-dirt roads, with brick buildings, schools, churches, and views that drop away toward the lake.

The place was established by Scottish missionaries in the late nineteenth century after earlier lake-level sites proved unhealthy. That history is still visible at the [Stone House Museum](/africa/malawi/livingstonia/stone_house_museum), [Livingstonia Mission Church](/africa/malawi/livingstonia/livingstonia_mission_church), and [David Gordon Memorial Hospital](/africa/malawi/livingstonia/david_gordon_memorial_hospital). It is a complicated legacy, but one that shaped education, medicine, printing, and politics across northern Malawi.

Nature is just as important as history. [Manchewe Falls](/africa/malawi/livingstonia/manchewe_falls) plunges off the escarpment nearby, while [Lukwe Camp](/africa/malawi/livingstonia/lukwe_camp) gives some of the finest views in the area. Getting here is the adventure: the road from Chitimba is steep and rough, and hiking up is hard work, but arriving slowly is the right way to meet Livingstonia.
