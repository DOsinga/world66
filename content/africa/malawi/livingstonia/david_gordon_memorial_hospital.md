David Gordon Memorial Hospital is part of the mission landscape rather than a tourist attraction in the usual sense. Its historic buildings and continued medical role show why Livingstonia mattered beyond worship: health care was central to the settlement's influence across the north.

Be respectful if you walk nearby. This is a working institution, not an abandoned relic, and visitors should keep photography and curiosity secondary to the hospital's daily business.
