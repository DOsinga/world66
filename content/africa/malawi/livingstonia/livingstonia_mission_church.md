Livingstonia Mission Church is the visual centre of the old mission settlement, a red-brick landmark that still anchors the town. Its architecture is plain but confident, and it makes clear how ambitious the mission project was in this remote highland setting.

Ask locally about access before wandering inside, especially if services or school activities are underway. The church is most interesting when visited with the museum and hospital, because together they show how religion, education, and medicine were tied together here.
