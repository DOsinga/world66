Lukwe Camp sits near the escarpment edge, close to Manchewe Falls, and is one of the best places around Livingstonia for a long view over the lake country. Even if you are not staying, it can be a good stop for food, a drink, or a pause after hiking.

The appeal is simple: cool air, gardens, valley slopes, and a sense of height after the climb from Chitimba. It also makes a practical base if you want to explore Livingstonia without rushing back down the escarpment the same day.
