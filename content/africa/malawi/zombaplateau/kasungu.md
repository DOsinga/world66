Kasungu is a market town in central Malawi, 124km north of [Lilongwe](/africa/malawi/lilongwe) on the M1 highway. It sits on the fertile Kasungu-Lilongwe plain, surrounded by tobacco farms — tobacco has long been the economic engine here.

The town is best known as the home of Malawi's first president, Dr Hastings Kamuzu Banda, who built the [Kamuzu Academy](/africa/malawi/zombaplateau/kasungu/kamuzu_academy) nearby — a grammar school modelled on Eton that is still sometimes called "the Eton of Africa." To the west, [Kasungu National Park](/africa/malawi/zombaplateau/kasungu/kasungu_national_park) protects a large area of miombo woodland, with [Lifupa Lodge](/africa/malawi/zombaplateau/kasungu/lifupa_lodge) as the main base for visitors.

If you are in the area during a ceremony or festival, look out for the [Gule Wamkulu](/africa/malawi/zombaplateau/kasungu/gule_wamkulu) — the masked dance tradition of the Chewa people, recognised by UNESCO. The dancers in their elaborate costumes emerge from the bush to perform at funerals, initiations, and other important occasions.
