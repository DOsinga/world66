BCEAO Tower is one of Bamako's clearest skyline markers and among the tallest buildings in Mali. Its Neo-Sudanic design borrows forms from the Sahelian mosque architecture of Djenné and Timbuktu, turning a modern office tower into a local architectural landmark.

You are mostly looking from outside, but it is a useful point of reference when reading Bamako's cityscape.
