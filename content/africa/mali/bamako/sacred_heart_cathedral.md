Sacred Heart Cathedral is one of central Bamako's clearest colonial-era landmarks, a Catholic church with twin towers and a calm presence near the city's busier streets. It adds religious and architectural variety to a capital better known to travellers for markets, museums, and river life.

The cathedral is worth a respectful short visit if you are already in the centre. Check local conditions before moving around Bamako, and treat the building as an active place of worship rather than a sightseeing stage.
