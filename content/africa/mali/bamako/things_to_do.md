---
title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Bamako isn't packed with formal sights, but what it has is worth your time. The [National Museum](/africa/mali/bamako/national_museum) is one of the best in West Africa, covering the civilizations of the Niger Valley with real depth. The [Plateau Viewpoint](/africa/mali/bamako/plateau_viewpoint) gives a good sense of the scale of the place — the river, the hills, the sprawl below.

The [Grand Mosque](/africa/mali/bamako/grand_mosque) in the Bozola neighbourhood is worth seeing for its architecture and the Friday congregation that spills into the surrounding streets. The [Grand Marché](/africa/mali/bamako/grand_marche) — vast, chaotic, and genuinely good — is where you'll find Malian bogolan cloth and the best street food in the city. The [Palais de la Culture](/africa/mali/bamako/palais_de_la_culture) is the place to hear live Malian music in a proper setting.

At dusk, walk across the [Pont des Martyrs](/africa/mali/bamako/martyrs_bridge) for views of the Niger and the pirogues working the water. The [Parc Zoologique et Botanique](/africa/mali/bamako/zoo_jardin) provides rare green space in the city and is worth an hour with or without children.
