---
title: When to Go
type: section
---

Bamako has two seasons: a wet season (June to September) and a long dry season (October to May). The best time to visit is November to February, when temperatures are more bearable — daytime highs around 30°C — and the Harmattan wind from the Sahara occasionally blows through, bringing dusty but dry air.

March to May is very hot, with temperatures regularly exceeding 40°C in the city. June to September is slightly cooler due to cloud cover and rain, but the humidity rises and the roads out of the city can become impassable in places. If you're planning any travel beyond Bamako into the rest of Mali, the dry season is essential.

The Festival sur le Niger, held in Ségou (about 250 km northeast), usually takes place in early February and is one of the best music festivals in West Africa. Many visitors time a Bamako trip around it.
