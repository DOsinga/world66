---
title: Things to Do
type: section
---

The main sights in Djenné are the [Great Mosque](/africa/mali/djenne/great_mosque), the [Monday Market](/africa/mali/djenne/market), and the [Old Town](/africa/mali/djenne/old_town) itself. Walking the narrow streets with a local guide is the best way to discover the [madrassas](/africa/mali/djenne/madrassas), traditional houses, and quiet corners that you would miss on your own. Don't skip the archaeological mound of [Djenné-Djenno](/africa/mali/djenne/djenne_djenno) just outside town, or a late-afternoon walk down to the [Bani River](/africa/mali/djenne/bani_river).
