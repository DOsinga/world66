---
title: Things to Do
type: section
---

Djiguibombo's main draw is the escarpment itself. The village sits at the base of the Bandiagara Escarpment, and from here the classic trekking routes fan out along the cliff face to a string of ancient Dogon villages. The walking is not technically demanding, but the heat and the distance mean you should treat even a one-day hike with respect.

The most rewarding destination is [Teli](/africa/mali/dogoncountry/djiguibombo/teli_village), a few hours south along the cliff. Its setting is among the most dramatic on the entire escarpment — ancient granaries built into the rockface hundreds of metres above the village floor, and a dramatic drop to the plains below. [Ende](/africa/mali/dogoncountry/djiguibombo/ende_village) is closer and more accessible, a gentler introduction to escarpment village life. [Kani Kombole](/africa/mali/dogoncountry/djiguibombo/kani_kombole_village) lies further south and is typically an overnight stop on multi-day treks.

The [escarpment trek routes](/africa/mali/dogoncountry/djiguibombo/bandiagara_escarpment_trek) from Djiguibombo can be done as day hikes or strung together over two to four days, sleeping in village guesthouses along the way. A local guide is strongly recommended — not only for navigation but for the cultural context and introductions that make the experience worthwhile.
