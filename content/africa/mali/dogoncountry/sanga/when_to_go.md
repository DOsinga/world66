---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

The Dogon country is best visited in the cooler dry season, from **November to February**. Temperatures are bearable (20–30°C in the day), the skies are clear, and the paths along the escarpment are dry. From March onwards it gets hot quickly, and the summer rainy season (June–September) makes trekking difficult — paths become muddy, some sections are impassable, and the humidity is oppressive.

The five-day Dogon market cycle is worth checking before you arrive, as the Sanga market is one of the highlights of a visit.

**2027 note**: The Dogon Sigui ceremony — the most important ritual in the Dogon calendar, held only once every 60 years — begins in 2027. The ceremony lasts several years, with masked processions visiting each village in sequence. The last Sigui ran from 1967 to 1973. If you are planning a visit in the late 2020s, this is worth researching and potentially timing your trip around.

**Security**: The broader Mopti region of Mali has faced serious security concerns since 2012. The situation in and around the Bandiagara Escarpment has varied — some periods and areas have been accessible to tourists, others not. Always check current government travel advisories before planning a trip, and arrange travel with a reputable local guide who knows the current conditions.
