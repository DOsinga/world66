title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Kayes is not a tourist destination in any conventional sense — the infrastructure for visitors is minimal, travel here requires genuine effort, and the heat alone is enough to discourage casual exploration. But for those who come, the combination of dramatic colonial history, the Senegal River, and the extraordinary Félou Falls makes it a genuinely interesting place.

Mali's security situation has been difficult in recent years; check current travel advisories carefully before planning any visit to the country.
