Atar itself is a market town and gateway rather than a destination in its own right. The [old quarter](/africa/mauritania/atar/old_quarter) has traditional mud-brick architecture worth a look, and the [weekly market](/africa/mauritania/atar/weekly_market) on Mondays is lively and authentic. The [Agadir of Atar](/africa/mauritania/atar/agadir_d_atar) — a ruined fortified granary on the plateau above the town — gives good views over the surrounding desert.

The real attractions are in the surrounding region. [Chinguetti](/africa/mauritania/chinguetti) is 90km northeast across the plateau — a holy city of the Sahara with crumbling medieval library towers. [Terjit oasis](/africa/mauritania/terjit) is a lush palm-lined gorge fed by desert springs, about 45km south. The nearby desert landscapes — dune fields, rocky plateaux, ancient caravan routes — are best explored with a local guide and a 4WD vehicle.
