---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

The best time to visit Atar and the Adrar region is October to March. Daytime temperatures are pleasant — typically 20–28°C — and the nights can be cool enough for a blanket. This is also the main tourist season when local guides and 4WD vehicle hire are most readily available.

Avoid June to September. Temperatures regularly exceed 45°C and can approach 50°C in July and August. Heat of this magnitude makes outdoor activity dangerous, not merely uncomfortable. Very little tourism operates during these months.

April and May are transitional — warm but manageable, with occasional sandstorms (harmattan). The light in April is clear and good for photography. Ramadan (dates vary by year) slows things down considerably: restaurants keep restricted hours and the pace of life changes. Respect this, and use it as an opportunity to observe an important aspect of Mauritanian culture.
