---
title: Things to Do
type: section
---

Cape Tagarit rewards the self-sufficient adventurer. The clear Atlantic water makes for good snorkelling — turtles, large reef fish, and abundant sea life close to shore. Fishing from the beach is productive, with sea bass and sea bream the most likely catches; bring your own tackle. The surrounding coast and shallow lagoons are extraordinary for birdwatching, especially from October through March when the Atlantic flyway brings millions of migratory birds south from Europe and the Arctic. The complete absence of development means camping under a vast desert sky is the main evening activity; the isolation and the stars are reasons enough to make the journey.
