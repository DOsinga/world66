title: Getting There

Koumbi Saleh sits in the remote southeastern corner of Mauritania, roughly 70 kilometres north of the border with Mali. The nearest town of any size is Timbedra, about 50 kilometres to the south on the main paved road that links Ayoun al Atrous with Nema. From Timbedra a dirt track leads north to the site; this final stretch requires a 4WD vehicle, especially after rain.

From Nouakchott, the journey takes the better part of two days by road. Shared bush taxis run from Nouakchott to Ayoun al Atrous and onward to Nema, stopping in Timbedra. From Timbedra you will need to negotiate transport — local drivers know the site and can take you there for a fee. Hiring a vehicle in Timbedra or arranging one through a Nouakchott travel agency is the most reliable approach.

There is no public transport to the site itself. Fuel and supplies should be topped up in Timbedra. A guide familiar with the area is strongly recommended, both for navigating the track and for understanding what you're looking at once you arrive.
