type: section
---

Néma is not a sightseeing town. The pleasure of being here is in the simple fact of being at the end of the road — a small Saharan outpost where the Route de l'Espoir runs out and the desert opens up east into Mali. Spend a morning at the market, walk the dusty grid of streets, and watch how a Saharan border town actually lives.
