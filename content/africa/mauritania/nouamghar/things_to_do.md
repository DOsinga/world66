The two reasons to come here are linked: the [Banc d'Arguin National Park](/africa/mauritania/nouamghar/banc_darguin) and the [Imragen fishing communities](/africa/mauritania/nouamghar/imragen_fishing) that live inside it. The neighbouring village of [El Mamghar](/africa/mauritania/nouamghar/el_mamghar) is another Imragen settlement worth visiting if you have a 4x4 and a guide.
