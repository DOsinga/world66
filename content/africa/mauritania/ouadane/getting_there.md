Ouadane is about 240 kilometres northeast of [Atar](/africa/mauritania/atar) and around 120 kilometres from [Chinguetti](/africa/mauritania/chinguetti). Despite the shorter distance, Chinguetti is not a reliable base for getting here — most vehicles in the Adrar go through Atar, which is the regional transport hub.

**From Atar**, occasional 4WD taxis and Land Cruisers make the run to Ouadane when there is enough demand. There is no fixed schedule; ask around the garage and be prepared to wait a day or two. The journey takes roughly four to five hours on piste.

**From Chinguetti**, the direct route to Ouadane crosses open desert and requires a guide. Do not attempt it without one — the piste is unmarked in places and the consequences of getting lost in the Adrar in summer are serious. Some travellers hire a guide and vehicle for a loop that covers both towns and takes two to three days.

**Self-drive** is possible with a well-equipped 4WD, GPS, satellite phone or PLB, and sufficient water and fuel. The piste from Atar is more straightforward than the Chinguetti route. A local guide is still strongly recommended for either approach.
