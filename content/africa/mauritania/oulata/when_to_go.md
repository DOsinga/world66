---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

The Sahara does not do mild. Oualata in summer (June to September) sees temperatures regularly above 45°C, and travel to the town becomes extremely difficult or impossible when the rainy season turns the desert tracks to mud. The best months are November to February, when daytime temperatures are bearable and nights can be cool.

October and March are transitional — increasingly hot but the tracks are usually passable. Avoid July and August entirely if possible: the heat is dangerous, the tracks can be flooded, and any 4WD journey becomes a genuine ordeal.
