---
title: "Day Trips"
type: section
---

Rosso's best day trip is also its most obvious one: cross the river into Senegal and spend time in **Saint-Louis**, about an hour south by road. Saint-Louis is one of West Africa's most atmospheric cities — a UNESCO-listed island town of faded colonial architecture, narrow streets, and pelican-filled beaches. It is a genuine destination in its own right and far more rewarding than Rosso for anyone with time to spare. The border crossing takes some patience, but Saint-Louis is well worth the effort.

Within Mauritania, the delta of the **Senegal River** stretches west toward the Atlantic and is an important wetland and bird habitat, though reaching it from Rosso requires your own transport. Nouakchott is around four hours north — close enough for a there-and-back if you have a vehicle, though most travellers treat the two towns as separate stops rather than a day-trip pair.
