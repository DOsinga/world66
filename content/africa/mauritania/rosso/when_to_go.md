---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

Rosso sits in the Sahel, and the climate is harsh for much of the year. The hot dry season runs from March to June, when temperatures regularly exceed 40 °C and the harmattan wind carries dust in from the Sahara. July to September brings the brief rainy season — humidity rises, roads can flood, and the river swells considerably. October through February is the most comfortable period: cooler temperatures, lower humidity, and clear skies. If you are simply passing through for the border crossing, the season matters less — the ferry runs year-round.
