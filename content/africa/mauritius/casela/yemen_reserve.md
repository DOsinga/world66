Yemen Reserve is a wildlife and landscape area near Casela, known for deer, Mauritian fauna, and open views toward the sea. It is a quieter nature stop than the island's headline reserves, but the combination of animals, rustic kiosks, and sunset views makes it worth considering when already in the area.
