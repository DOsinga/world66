The small cafe at the Seven Coloured Earth site serves cold drinks, coffee, and light snacks to visitors arriving at the main attraction. It is nothing special in itself — a simple tourist cafe attached to a popular site — but it is the most convenient spot for a cold drink after walking the dune viewpoint and the waterfall overlook in the midday heat. Fresh coconut water and sugarcane juice are usually available alongside the standard options.

Arrive early if you want to sit without queuing. By late morning on busy days the terrace fills up quickly with tour groups.
