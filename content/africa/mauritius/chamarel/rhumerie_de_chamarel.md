story: >
  Chamarel produces what is called "agricultural rum" (rhum agricole) — distilled directly from fresh sugar cane juice rather than from molasses, the by-product used in most rum production. The distinction matters enormously to rum enthusiasts: agricole rum retains the grassy, fresh character of the cane itself, giving it a flavour quite unlike the sweeter industrial rums made from molasses.
---

The Rhumerie de Chamarel is one of Mauritius's most acclaimed rum producers, making small-batch agricole rum on a hillside in the Black River district. Guided tours walk you through the production process — from the crushing of local sugar cane to fermentation, distillation in copper pot stills, and ageing in French oak barrels. The views from the distillery over the surrounding cane fields and down toward the coast are excellent.

The tour ends with a tasting of several expressions: white rum fresh off the still, aged rums at different stages, and flavoured varieties infused with vanilla, ginger, and other island spices. The rum here has won international awards and the production is genuinely artisanal — worth taking seriously even if you are not normally a rum drinker. Booking tours in advance is recommended, especially in the November–April high season.
