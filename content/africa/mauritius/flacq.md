Flacq (also written as Centre de Flacq) is the main town on the east coast of Mauritius. It hosts the island's largest outdoor market on Sundays and Wednesdays — the [Flacq Sunday Market](/africa/mauritius/flacq/flacq_market) is a genuine local institution, good for cheap produce, street food, and the kind of atmosphere that disappears inside resort grounds.

The east coast's two best attractions are both close by. [Belle Mare beach](/africa/mauritius/flacq/belle_mare_beach), a few kilometres north, is one of the finest stretches of white sand on the island, protected by the outer reef and rarely crowded. [Île aux Cerfs](/africa/mauritius/flacq/ile_aux_cerfs), a small island in the lagoon off Trou d'Eau Douce to the south, is reached by boat in ten minutes and offers excellent swimming and snorkelling in translucent lagoon water.
