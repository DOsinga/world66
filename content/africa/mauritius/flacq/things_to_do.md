---
title: Things to Do
type: section
---

Flacq's own appeal is the market and the low-key local atmosphere. The main attractions — Belle Mare beach and Île aux Cerfs — are nearby rather than in the town itself, and they are among the best on the island. If you are staying on the east coast, you will spend time in or near Flacq whether you plan to or not; the market gives the area a genuine local texture that the resort hotels nearby lack.
