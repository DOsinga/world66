---
title: When to Go
type: section
---

Mauritius has a tropical climate with two main seasons. The warm, humid **summer** runs from November to April and brings the risk of cyclones (primarily January–March). The east coast, where Flacq sits, takes the full force of trade winds and is more exposed to rain during this period. Seas can be rough, though the lagoon beaches remain protected.

The **dry season** from May to October is the best time to visit the east coast. Temperatures are cooler (24–28°C), humidity is lower, and the sea is calm. June and July can be slightly windy on the east coast but the weather is consistently pleasant. This is peak tourist season — prices are higher and the popular beaches fill up at weekends.
