Grand Gaube is a quiet fishing village on the northeast coast, about 15 kilometres from [Grand Baie](/africa/mauritius/grandbaie). It has a [sheltered lagoon and working fishing harbour](/africa/mauritius/grand_gaube/grand_gaube_lagoon) with calm water and fine views to the offshore islands — Coin de Mire (Gunner's Quoin), Flat Island, Round Island, and Snake Island — which can be visited by [boat excursion](/africa/mauritius/grand_gaube/northern_islands_boat_trip). The village is less touristy than the north coast resorts and has a more authentic feel. [Cap Malheureux](/africa/mauritius/grand_gaube/cap_malheureux_church), 5 kilometres to the west, has a picturesque red-roofed church that is one of the most photographed landmarks on the island, and the large [Goodlands market](/africa/mauritius/grand_gaube/goodlands_market) just inland is the biggest in the north of the island.
