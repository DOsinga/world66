---
title: Getting There
type: section
---

Grand Gaube sits at the end of the coastal road that runs north from Port Louis through [Grand Baie](/africa/mauritius/grandbaie) and Cap Malheureux. The drive from the airport, on the south of the island, takes around an hour and a half; from Port Louis around forty-five minutes.

Local buses connect Grand Gaube with Goodlands and Grand Baie roughly every half-hour during the day. A hire car is the easiest way to combine the village with the wider north coast.
