---
title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Grand Baie is primarily a base for activities elsewhere on the water — catamaran trips to the northern islands, dive boats, deep-sea fishing — rather than a place stuffed with sights. The bay itself is shallow and protected, good for paddleboarding and easy snorkelling around the reef edges. **La Cuvette Beach** at the western end of the village is the prettiest public stretch of sand.

For anyone interested in Mauritian culture beyond the resort strip, the Hindu temple at Triolet a few kilometres inland is the largest on the island and entirely unconnected to the tourist circuit. The waterfront itself is worth wandering at sunset, when the catamarans come back in and the front-line restaurants fill up.
