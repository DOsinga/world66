---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

Mauritius is in the southern hemisphere, so the seasons are reversed from Europe. **May to October** is the cool, dry, slightly windier winter — best for hiking, sightseeing, and big-game fishing (marlin and tuna peak November to March, but the seas are calmer in winter). **November to April** is hot, humid, and increasingly wet; cyclones occasionally cross the island between January and March.

For Grand Baie specifically, the **September to December** window is the sweet spot: warm enough for the water, dry enough for catamaran trips, and not yet into the high-season Christmas peak when prices double and the bay fills up. Avoid the French and South African school holidays in late December and July if you can.
