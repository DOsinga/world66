---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

Mauritius has two seasons. Summer (November to April) is hot and humid, with temperatures in the low 30s and periodic cyclone activity, particularly between January and March. The sea is at its warmest and the island is green, but the south coast can get rough weather. Blue Bay's lagoon is naturally sheltered, so snorkelling is usually possible year-round.

Winter (May to October) is the more comfortable time to visit: warm, drier, and with good trade winds keeping temperatures in the mid-20s. The south and east coasts can be windier than the north in winter, but the clarity of the sea is at its best. July and August are peak European holiday season and prices rise accordingly. May, June, September, and October offer better value and good conditions.
