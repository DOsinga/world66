---
title: Things to Do
type: section
---

Moka's appeal is understated and genuine. The Eureka colonial mansion is the main attraction, and it is one of the most rewarding heritage sites on the island. Beyond that, the mountains of the Moka Range invite hiking — Le Pouce in particular offers panoramic views over the whole island. This is where Mauritians come on weekends to escape the coast; the pace is slow and the atmosphere is distinctly local.
