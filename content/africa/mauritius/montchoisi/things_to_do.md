---
title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Mont Choisi's main draw is the beach itself — wide, sheltered, and backed by casuarina trees that provide natural shade. The calm, shallow water suits families and relaxed swimming. The real pleasure is the quietness: even in high season, weekday mornings can feel almost deserted compared to the busier stretch at nearby [Grand Baie](/africa/mauritius/grandbaie).

For more active options, the [Grand Baie](/africa/mauritius/grandbaie) boat excursion operators are a short drive away, offering day trips to the northern offshore islands — Île Plate, Gabriel, and Coin de Mire — with snorkelling, dolphin watching, and picnics on the beach. The village of Trou-aux-Biches, a few kilometres south, has the best snorkelling on the north coast and a calmer atmosphere than Grand Baie. Mont Choisi itself is better for relaxing than sightseeing.
