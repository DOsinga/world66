Pereybere is a compact beach village on the north coast of [Mauritius](/africa/mauritius), squeezed between busy [Grand Baie](/africa/mauritius/grandbaie) and quieter Cap Malheureux. It is not remote or undiscovered, but it gets the balance right: a proper public beach, small restaurants and snack counters, and enough village life to keep it from feeling like a resort compound.

The main draw is [Pereybere Public Beach](/africa/mauritius/pereybere/pereybere_public_beach), a small arc of white sand with clear, calm water. It is excellent for swimming and easy for families, though it can feel crowded on weekends and holidays. Walk north and the coast becomes rockier and quieter toward [Bain Boeuf Public Beach](/africa/mauritius/pereybere/bain_boeuf_public_beach), with views across to Coin de Mire.

Pereybere works well if you want the north-coast conveniences without sleeping in the middle of Grand Baie's nightlife strip. You can be in Grand Baie in a few minutes by bus or taxi, reach the red-roofed church at Cap Malheureux just as quickly, and still come back to a beach that feels local in the early morning.
