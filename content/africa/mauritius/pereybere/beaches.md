---
title: Beaches
type: section
---

Pereybere's beach scene is compact but very usable. [Pereybere Public Beach](/africa/mauritius/pereybere/pereybere_public_beach) is the obvious choice for swimming: sandy, sheltered, and close to food stalls. It is small, so arrive early if you want shade or a quieter patch of sand.

For more space, walk or drive north to [Bain Boeuf Public Beach](/africa/mauritius/pereybere/bain_boeuf_public_beach). It is less polished and not as convenient for a full beach day, but the views toward Coin de Mire are excellent and the pace is calmer.
