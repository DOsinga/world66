Pereybere is an easy base for the north of Mauritius. Grand Baie has the boat trips, dive operators, restaurants, and nightlife; Grand Gaube and Cap Malheureux are quieter coastal villages with big views toward the northern islands; Mont Choisi has one of the north coast's widest beaches.
