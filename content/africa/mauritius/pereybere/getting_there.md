---
title: "Getting There"
type: section
---

Pereybere is on the north-coast road between [Grand Baie](/africa/mauritius/grandbaie) and Cap Malheureux. Buses from Port Louis and Grand Baie run along this corridor during the day, though taxis are easier if you are carrying beach gear or arriving after dark.

Driving from Sir Seewoosagur Ramgoolam International Airport usually takes around an hour and a half, longer if traffic is heavy near Port Louis. Once you are in the north, the distances are short: Grand Baie is only a few minutes away by taxi or local bus.
