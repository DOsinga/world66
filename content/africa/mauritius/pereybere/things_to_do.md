---
title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Pereybere is about water, food, and short north-coast outings rather than formal sightseeing. Start with [Pereybere Public Beach](/africa/mauritius/pereybere/pereybere_public_beach), then use the village as a quieter base for Grand Baie's boat trips and Cap Malheureux's coastal views.

If you want a less crowded swim, [Bain Boeuf Public Beach](/africa/mauritius/pereybere/bain_boeuf_public_beach) is a good walk or short ride north. For snorkelling tours, diving, catamaran trips to the northern islands, and nightlife, go to [Grand Baie](/africa/mauritius/grandbaie); Pereybere's strength is coming back somewhere smaller afterward.
