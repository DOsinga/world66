Aapravasi Ghat is the landing point where Indian indentured workers stepped onto Mauritian soil, a small stone complex on the harbour front declared a UNESCO World Heritage Site in 2006. The buildings that survive — a hospital block, the immigration sheds, the original stone steps from the water — were where new arrivals were processed, examined, and assigned to plantations.

The site is sobering and well presented. The interpretation centre tells the story of the indenture system across the empire, with photographs, ship manifests, and personal artefacts. Allow an hour. Entry is free. Closed Sundays.
