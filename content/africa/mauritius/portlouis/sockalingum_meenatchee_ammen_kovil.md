Sockalingum Meenatchee Ammen Kovil is a Tamil Hindu temple with the bright sculptural detail that makes South Indian temple architecture so immediately recognisable. Its gopuram is crowded with painted deities, guardians, and mythic scenes, a vivid contrast with the port roads and city traffic around it.

Visit respectfully, remove shoes where required, and avoid interrupting worship. The temple is a short stop rather than a half-day sight, but it adds an important Hindu thread to Port Louis' mix of mosques, churches, markets, and colonial buildings.
