---
title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Souillac's attractions are natural rather than cultural — a dramatic coastline of volcanic rock, a waterfall accessible through sugar cane fields, and peaceful gardens. None of them require more than a half day, which makes Souillac a natural stop on a circuit of the south coast rather than a destination in itself. The combination of Gris-Gris cliffs, Rochester Falls, and the Telfair Gardens makes a satisfying morning.
