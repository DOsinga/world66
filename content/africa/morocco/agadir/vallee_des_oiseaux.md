Vallée des Oiseaux is a small bird park and zoo in central Agadir, with walk-through bird enclosures and a few other animals. It is modest, but easy to pair with a short city walk.

Families get the most out of it: children can see birds at close range and use the small playground, while adults will mainly value the shade and quick change of pace from the beach and souq.
