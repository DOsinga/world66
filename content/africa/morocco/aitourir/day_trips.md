Ait Ourir is best treated as a base for short trips into the Atlas foothills or a coffee stop between Marrakesh and the south. The villages of Tighedouine and Sidi Rahal lie within an hour, and the road over the [Tizi n'Tichka pass](/africa/morocco/tizi_n_tichka_pass) starts climbing in earnest just a few kilometres east, carrying you over the High Atlas toward Telouet, Ouarzazate, and Aït Benhaddou.
