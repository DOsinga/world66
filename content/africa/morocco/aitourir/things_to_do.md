---
title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Ait Ourir is a stopover town, not a destination, but a couple of things are worth your time if you're passing through. The Tuesday souk is the headline event — one of the busiest weekly markets in the Marrakesh hinterland. Outside market day, the Oued Zat valley and the road climbing toward the Atlas reward a short detour.
