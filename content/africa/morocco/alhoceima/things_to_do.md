title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Al Hoceima's attractions are mostly natural — beaches, cliffs, and the national park — with some history woven in from the Spanish colonial period and the 1920s Rif rebellion.

The [Plage Quemado](/africa/morocco/alhoceima/plage_quemado) is the town beach, dramatically situated between sea cliffs in the bay. For more space and quieter water, head out to [Plage Sfiha](/africa/morocco/alhoceima/plage_sfiha) or the coves within [Al Hoceima National Park](/africa/morocco/alhoceima/al_hoceima_national_park) to the west. The park protects a stretch of rugged coastline with sea caves, griffon vultures, and one of the few remaining populations of Barbary macaque in northern Morocco.

The [Peñón de Alhucemas](/africa/morocco/alhoceima/penon_de_alhucemas) — a tiny Spanish-controlled island visible from the town beach — is technically still sovereign Spanish territory, a curiosity that makes Al Hoceima the only place in Morocco where you can watch a foreign flag fly from a medieval fortification a few hundred metres offshore.
