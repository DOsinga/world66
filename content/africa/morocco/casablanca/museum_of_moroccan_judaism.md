The Museum of Moroccan Judaism is one of Casablanca's most valuable cultural stops, and unusual in the wider Arab world. Its collection covers synagogue furnishings, clothing, photographs, jewellery, manuscripts, and domestic objects from Morocco's Jewish communities, treating them as part of the country's own history rather than a footnote.

The building is modest and sits away from the obvious tourist circuit, but it rewards the detour. Go before exploring the Habous quarter or the old medina; it changes the way you read Casablanca's layered urban and religious history.
