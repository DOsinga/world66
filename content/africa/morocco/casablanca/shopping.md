---
title: "Shopping"
type: section
---

Casablanca is not the place to buy traditional Moroccan handicrafts at their finest — Fes and Marrakesh do that better — but it has its own shopping identity.

The **Habous Quarter** (New Medina) has the most concentrated selection of crafts: babouches, pottery, brassware, textiles, and djellabas in traditional workshops with somewhat lower pressure than the imperial city souks. The bookshops here also have good selections of French and Arabic titles.

**Morocco Mall** in Ain Diab and **Twin Center** on Boulevard Zerktouni are the city's main modern shopping centres, with international brands and a broad range of goods. The malls are popular with Casablancans and give a sense of the city's contemporary commercial life.

For everyday shopping, the **Central Market** (Marché Central) near the port has excellent fresh food — particularly fish and produce — and is the best food market in the city.
