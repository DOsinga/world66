The Shrine of Sidi Abderrahman sits on a small rocky island beyond the Corniche, reached only at low tide. Non-Muslim visitors cannot enter the shrine itself, but the crossing, the sea views, and the little cluster around it make this one of Casablanca's more unusual coastal stops.
