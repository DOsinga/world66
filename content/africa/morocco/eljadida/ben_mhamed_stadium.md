El Jadida's main football stadium, home to Difaâ El Jadidi, one of Morocco's older football clubs. If you happen to be in town during a home match, it's worth attending — Moroccan football crowds are enthusiastic and the atmosphere in the local league is unpretentious compared to the polished experience of a big European game. Tickets are inexpensive and usually available at the gate.

The stadium sits just south of the city centre, within easy walking distance of the beach and the Portuguese medina. It holds around 10,000 spectators.
