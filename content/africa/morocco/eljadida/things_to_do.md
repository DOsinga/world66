title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

El Jadida rewards slow exploration. The [Cité Portugaise](/africa/morocco/eljadida/cite_portugaise) is the UNESCO-listed core of the old town — walk the ramparts, find the [Portuguese Cistern](/africa/morocco/eljadida/portuguese_cistern), and watch for the [Community Oven](/africa/morocco/eljadida/community_oven) tucked inside the fortress walls. The [beach](/africa/morocco/eljadida/el_jadida_beach) stretches south from the old walls for several kilometres. In summer, it fills with Moroccans from Casablanca; the rest of the year it belongs mostly to locals.
