---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

Visit between October and April. The Sahara summers are brutal — Erfoud sees average highs above 40 °C in July and August, with sandstorm conditions not uncommon, and most of the desert excursions from town become unpleasant or unsafe. October to early November is the date harvest, with the **Festival des Dattes** taking over the centre for a long weekend of music, processions, and tasting.

December and January are cold at night — drop close to freezing in the dunes — but the days are crisp and the light is at its best. February through April is the most reliable window: warm days, cool nights, clear skies, and dunes still in their winter colour.
