Near the covered market, Café Atlas fills up early with traders and market-goers. Breakfasts here are worth making time for: msemen with argan oil and honey, a glass of café cassé (coffee cut with hot milk), and sometimes beghrir — the spongy semolina pancakes that soak up butter and honey better than anything else. Come between seven and nine for the best selection, before the morning trade winds down.
