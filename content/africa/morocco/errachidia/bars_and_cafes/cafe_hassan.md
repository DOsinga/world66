A classic Moroccan cafe on the main square, Café Hassan has the corner spot that catches both morning sun and the evening breeze. Mint tea is brewed properly here — loose gunpowder green tea, a fistful of fresh mint, and enough sugar to stand a spoon in. Fresh orange juice is pressed to order. The terrace is the right place to sit and watch Errachidia go about its business over a slow pot of tea.
