A popular stop near the bus station, the Ziz handles the constant flow of travellers passing through on routes between Fes and the desert. The menu covers the basics — brochettes, merguez, kefta, and tagines — and the kitchen keeps long hours, which is useful if you arrive on a late bus. The terrace out front is a decent place to watch the evening traffic on the main road while waiting for your order.
