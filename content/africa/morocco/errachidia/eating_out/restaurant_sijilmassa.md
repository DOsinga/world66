One of the more reliable restaurants on Avenue Mohammed V, Sijilmassa does a steady trade in lamb tagine, chicken with preserved lemon, and the ubiquitous harira that appears on every table in Morocco as a free starter. The dining room is plain but clean, service is prompt, and the bread arrives warm. Good value for a filling meal before or after the drive through the Ziz Gorges.
