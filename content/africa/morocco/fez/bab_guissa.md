The northern gate of Fes el-Bali gets a fraction of the attention that [Bab Boujeloud](/africa/morocco/fez/bab_boujeloud) receives, which is part of its appeal. There are no postcard sellers here, no rooftop cafes angling for your business — just a massive gate in the city wall and the road climbing up toward the hills beyond. The gate itself dates to the Almohad period, though it has been rebuilt several times since.

Bab Guissa is the natural starting point for the walk up to the [Merinid Tombs](/africa/morocco/fez/merinid_tombs) and [Borj Nord](/africa/morocco/fez/borj_nord). The road winds uphill through a quieter residential neighbourhood, and the views back over the medina improve with every turn. If you are coming from deep inside the medina, the walk up to the gate itself is a useful orientation exercise — you pass through increasingly quiet streets until you hit the city wall and suddenly have sky above you again.
