A squat lookout tower near [Al-Qarawiyyin](/africa/morocco/fez/al_qarawiyyin) that offers something rare in the medina: perspective. From the rooftop terrace you get a full 360-degree view across the rooftops of Fes el-Bali — satellite dishes, minarets, laundry lines, and the surrounding hills all at once. It is one of the few elevated vantage points inside the medina walls, rather than from the hills above.

The tower is sometimes called the Trumpet Tower because a horn was sounded from here to mark the end of the daily fast during Ramadan. A small fee gets you up the stairs. The best time to visit is late afternoon, when the light turns warm and the call to prayer echoes from every direction. The tower is just north of the Qarawiyyin complex — ask for directions locally, as it is easy to walk past.
