Café Clock is the social hub of the medina for visitors and Fez's small expat community. It occupies a beautifully restored riad on Talaa Kebira, spread across three floors with a rooftop terrace that looks out over the rooftops of Fes el-Bali. The signature dish is the camel burger, which is genuinely good and not just a gimmick. The rest of the menu mixes Moroccan and international comfort food — the shakshuka at brunch is solid, and they do proper coffee, which is not a given in the medina.

Beyond the food, Clock functions as a cultural centre. There are regular storytelling nights in Darija and English, live gnawa music sessions, and occasional calligraphy workshops. The rooftop fills up around sunset, so arrive early if you want a good spot. Service can be slow when it is busy, but that is part of the deal — this is a place to settle in for a couple of hours, not grab a quick meal.
