A tiny place near the start of Talaa Seghira, just inside [Bab Boujeloud](/africa/morocco/fez/bab_boujeloud), with a few tables and a kitchen you can see into from the street. This is where locals eat, and the prices reflect it — tagines run 30-40 dirham, and a full meal with bread and mint tea will cost you under 60. The chicken tagine with preserved lemon and olives is the best bet, cooked slowly in the traditional conical pot.

There is no menu in the usual sense. Rachid or whoever is cooking will tell you what is available that day, and you pick. Do not expect ambiance — the plastic chairs, fluorescent lighting, and Arabic television in the background are part of the package. What you get instead is honest Fassi home cooking at prices that make the riad restaurants look absurd. Come at lunchtime when the tagines are freshest.
