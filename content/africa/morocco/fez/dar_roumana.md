Dar Roumana is a riad restaurant that serves a set five-course dinner that changes daily. The cooking is refined Moroccan — traditional recipes executed with real precision and better ingredients than you will find in most medina restaurants. The pomegranate chicken (poulet à la grenade) is the dish people talk about, and rightly so, but everything from the briouats to the desserts is carefully done. Meals are served in the courtyard or in a small dining room, and the atmosphere is intimate without being stuffy.

You must book ahead, ideally a day or two in advance, as there are limited seats and the kitchen prepares for the number of reservations. The set menu runs around 450-550 dirham per person including multiple courses. Dar Roumana is also a guesthouse, but you do not need to be staying there to eat. It is tucked away in the Zkak Roumane neighbourhood, which is quieter than the main medina thoroughfares — ask them for directions when you book, or they can send someone to meet you.
