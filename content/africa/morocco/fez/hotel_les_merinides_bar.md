The terrace bar at Hotel Les Mérinides sits on the hills north of the medina, near the [Merinid Tombs](/africa/morocco/fez/merinid_tombs), and has a panoramic view over the whole of [Fes el-Bali](/africa/morocco/fez/fes_el_bali). At sunset, when the light turns the medina golden and the call to prayer echoes from dozens of minarets simultaneously, it is one of the finest views in Morocco. You do not need to be a hotel guest to visit the terrace bar.

The drinks are overpriced — expect to pay 80-100 dirham for a beer, roughly double what you would pay in the Ville Nouvelle — and the bar itself is nothing special in terms of decor or service. But you are paying for the view, and it is worth it once. Go about an hour before sunset, order something, and stay for the show. A petit taxi from the medina gates costs around 15-20 dirham each way.
