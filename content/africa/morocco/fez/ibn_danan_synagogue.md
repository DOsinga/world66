In the heart of the Mellah, the old Jewish quarter of [Fes el-Jdid](/africa/morocco/fez/fes_el_jdid), this small synagogue is a quiet reminder that Fez was once home to a large and influential Jewish community. The painted wooden galleries and carved plaster walls have been restored to something close to their original state. A caretaker will let you in and show you around — a small tip is expected.

The real discovery is the underground mikveh, reached by a steep wooden staircase. The spring-fed bath is still there in the dimness below street level, remarkably intact. The synagogue sits on Derb el-Fassiyyin in the Mellah, not far from the Jewish cemetery. It is easy to miss — look for a modest doorway with a Star of David. Ask at the nearby Habarim Synagogue if it is locked.
