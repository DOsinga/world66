After hours in the medina, Jnan Sbil feels like stepping into another climate. These royal gardens sit in the valley between Fes el-Bali and [Fes el-Jdid](/africa/morocco/fez/fes_el_jdid), and they are the closest thing Fez has to a park. Tall palms, bamboo groves, fountains, and a lake with ducks — the shade alone is worth the detour on a hot afternoon.

The gardens were originally laid out in the eighteenth century for the Alaouite sultans but fell into neglect. A thorough restoration in 2011 brought them back to life, and they are now open to the public and well maintained. Local families come here in the early evening, and it is a pleasant place to sit on a bench and regroup before heading back into the labyrinth. The entrance is near [Bab Boujeloud](/africa/morocco/fez/bab_boujeloud), on the road between the two medinas.
