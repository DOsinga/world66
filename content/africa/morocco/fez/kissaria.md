The Kissaria is the covered market at the commercial heart of Fes el-Bali, wedged between Al-Qarawiyyin and the Zaouia of Moulay Idriss II. This is where Fassi families themselves come to shop for special occasions — weddings, religious holidays — and the goods reflect that. The narrow lanes are lined with bolts of silk and brocade, embroidered kaftans, djellabas in fine wool, and traditional wedding accessories. It feels more like a department store than a bazaar, with fixed or semi-fixed prices and less of the theatrical haggling you get in the outer souks. The quality is higher than most of what you will find elsewhere in the medina, and so are the prices, but you are buying what the locals buy.
