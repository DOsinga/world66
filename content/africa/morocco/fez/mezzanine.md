Mezzanine is one of the very few places in the old city where you can sit on a rooftop with a cocktail. Alcohol is not common in the medina — most restaurants here are dry — so a bar with mojitos, wine, and mixed drinks in a stylish setting near Rcif is genuinely unusual. The rooftop terrace has views over the neighbourhood, the drinks are competently made, and the atmosphere skews young and international.

The cocktail list is short but covers the basics, and they pour reasonably. Expect to pay 70-90 dirham for a cocktail. The food menu is limited — come for drinks, not dinner. The location near Rcif means you can combine it with a meal at [The Ruined Garden](/africa/morocco/fez/the_ruined_garden) or street food at [Bab Rcif](/africa/morocco/fez/thami_street_food). On warm evenings the terrace fills up, so arriving before 8pm is wise.
