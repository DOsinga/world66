The Mosque and Mausoleum of Sidi Ahmad al-Tijani is one of Fez's important Sufi pilgrimage sites. Sidi Ahmad al-Tijani founded the Tijaniyya order and died in Fez in 1815, giving the shrine meaning well beyond Morocco.

The site sits in the old medina, close to the craft streets and religious landmarks around Fes el-Bali. Non-Muslim visitors should expect access limits, but the exterior and the flow of pilgrims still make it a meaningful stop while walking this part of the city.
