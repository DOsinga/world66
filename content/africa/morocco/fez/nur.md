Nur is the best restaurant in Fez and one of the best in Morocco. Chef Najat Kaanache trained at elBulli, Noma, and a string of other top kitchens before returning home to cook Moroccan food through a modern lens. The result is a tasting menu that uses traditional ingredients — saffron, preserved lemon, ras el hanout, argan oil — in ways that are genuinely surprising. A recent menu included deconstructed pastilla, lamb confit with seven-vegetable couscous broth, and a saffron panna cotta that was worth the trip on its own.

This is fine dining by any international standard, and priced accordingly — expect to pay 800-1200 dirham per person for the tasting menu, which is expensive for Fez but reasonable compared to restaurants of this calibre elsewhere. The dining room is intimate, set in a restored riad with maybe twenty seats. Book well in advance, as Nur fills up most nights. Dress is smart casual. If you eat at one restaurant in Fez where the bill makes you wince slightly, make it this one.
