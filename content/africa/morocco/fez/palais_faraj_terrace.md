The terrace at Palais Faraj has what may be the single best view in Fez. Perched high on the northern edge of the medina near Bab Guissa, it looks down over the entire old city — a dense carpet of rooftops, minarets, and satellite dishes stretching to the hills beyond. They serve cocktails, Moroccan wine (the Volubilia rosé is decent), and non-alcoholic drinks. The setting is a luxury hotel, so expect polished service and prices to match — cocktails run 100-130 dirham.

This is not an everyday bar. It is the kind of place you go once, ideally at sunset, to sit in a beautiful spot and appreciate the scale of the medina below. The terrace is open to non-guests, but it helps to dress reasonably well — this is a five-star hotel, not a backpacker rooftop. If the [Les Mérinides](/africa/morocco/fez/hotel_les_merinides_bar) terrace gives you the distant panorama, Palais Faraj gives you the intimate overhead view. Both are worth one visit.
