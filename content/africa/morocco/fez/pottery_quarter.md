The pottery workshops of Ain Nokbi sit outside the medina walls to the east, spread across a hillside where the kilns have room to breathe. This is where the distinctive blue-and-white Fez ceramics are made, from the raw clay to the finished glazed product, and several workshops welcome visitors to watch the whole process: potters throwing on kick-wheels, painters applying the intricate geometric designs freehand, and the massive wood-fired kilns where everything is fired. The large cooperative at the top of the hill is the most visited and has a showroom the size of a warehouse, but smaller family workshops nearby offer the same quality with less pressure. Buying direct here is significantly cheaper than in the medina — a set of painted bowls that costs 400 dirhams in the souks might be 150 here. A petit taxi from Bab Boujeloud takes ten minutes.
