A straightforward Moroccan restaurant near [Bab Boujeloud](/africa/morocco/fez/bab_boujeloud) that does the basics well at reasonable prices. The pastilla — the layered pigeon pie with almonds and cinnamon sugar that Fez is famous for — is properly made here and worth ordering. The harira soup is thick and warming, the lamb tagine with prunes is reliable, and portions are generous. Expect to pay around 60-90 dirham for a main course.

Bouayad does not have the atmosphere of the riad restaurants or the street credibility of the hole-in-the-wall places, but it sits in a useful middle ground: decent food, clean, and affordable. It is popular with Moroccan families as well as visitors, which is generally a good sign. The location near the main entrance to Fes el-Bali makes it a convenient choice before or after a long day navigating the medina.
