If you are staying in the [Ville Nouvelle](/africa/morocco/fez/ville_nouvelle) and want Moroccan food without trekking into the medina, Dar Tajine is a reliable choice on Avenue Ahmed Chaouki. The tagines are well-made — the lamb with prunes and almonds is particularly good — and the couscous on Fridays follows the local tradition of the weekly family couscous meal. Prices are moderate, with mains around 70-100 dirham.

The setting is a pleasant enough restaurant dining room, nothing like the atmospheric riad courtyard places in Fes el-Bali, but that is not the point. This is where you go when you want a proper sit-down Moroccan meal without the navigation challenge or the tourist markup of the medina. Service is friendly and unhurried. It is also considerably easier to find than most medina restaurants, which counts for something after a long day.
