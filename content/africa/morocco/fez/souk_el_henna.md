A small, tree-shaded square tucked away from the main medina thoroughfares, Souk el-Henna has a calmer atmosphere than the frenetic commercial streets nearby. The stalls here sell henna powder, kohl, traditional herbal remedies, and handmade soaps, alongside a good selection of the blue-and-white Fez ceramics — bowls, plates, and tiles — at slightly better prices than the tourist-facing shops. Vendors are less pushy here, and you can browse without being pulled into every doorway. It is a pleasant spot to pause, and a useful place to pick up ceramics without making the trip out to the pottery quarter.
