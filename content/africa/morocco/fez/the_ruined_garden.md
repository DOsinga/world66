The Ruined Garden sits in the courtyard of a partially restored riad near Rcif, where fig and orange trees grow among old stone walls open to the sky. It is one of the most pleasant places to eat in Fez, combining a Moroccan-European menu with a setting that feels genuinely peaceful — no small feat in the medina. The menu changes regularly depending on what is available, but expect dishes like slow-cooked lamb with prunes, beetroot and goat cheese salad, and good homemade bread.

Portions are generous, prices are moderate by restaurant standards (mains around 80-120 dirham), and the cooking is consistently careful. The garden courtyard is the main draw — on a warm afternoon, eating lunch under the fig trees with birdsong overhead is about as good as it gets. Reservations are strongly recommended, especially for dinner and on weekends. Finding the entrance takes some navigating through narrow alleys near Rcif, but it is signposted once you are close.
