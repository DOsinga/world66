---
title: Things to Do
type: section
---

The High Atlas rewards every kind of traveller: trekkers come for Toubkal and the Berber villages, skiers head to Oukaïmeden in winter, and day-trippers from Marrakech drive up to the Ourika Valley for the air alone. The range has few tourist traps — most of it is working agricultural land and mountain pasture, and you feel that.
