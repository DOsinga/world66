---
title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Ifrane's appeal is overwhelmingly outdoors. The [cedar forests](cedar_forest_barbary_macaques) that surround the town are home to Barbary macaques and some excellent walking. In winter, the [Michlifen ski resort](michlifen_ski_resort) draws Moroccan families up the mountain for a day on the slopes — skiing in Morocco surprises nearly everyone who hasn't been. [Jbel El Koudiate](jbel_el_koudiate), an extinct volcanic shield west of town, rewards a short hike with views over the Atlas. [Al Akhawayn University](al_akhawayn_university) gives the town an unusual intellectual energy; the campus is attractive and open to visitors. In town, the stone [Lion of Ifrane](lion_of_ifrane) statue in the park is the town's most photographed landmark.
