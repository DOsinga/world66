---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

Imilchil is a high-altitude village and the seasons matter. From November to April the plateau is often under snow, the unpaved approach roads can be impassable, and night-time temperatures fall well below freezing. Many auberges close in winter.

The best window is **May to early October**, when the days are warm, the lakes are full from the spring melt, and the high pastures are green. Bring warm clothes anyway — even in August, dawn at 2,200 metres is cold.

The big draw is the **Moussem des Fiançailles** in late August or September. Dates are fixed locally and shift from year to year; ask in advance, because accommodation is overrun during the festival and you need to plan ahead. **Saturday is market day** — make sure your visit includes one.
