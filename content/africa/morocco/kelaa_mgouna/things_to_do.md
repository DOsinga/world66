Kelaa M'Gouna's appeal is largely natural and seasonal. The rose harvest in May is the spectacle most visitors come for, but the trekking routes into the M'Goun massif are accessible year-round and draw walkers serious about the High Atlas backcountry. The town itself is small, and the main things to do are out in the valley.
