The Agdal district offers some of the best views of the High Atlas mountains from within Marrakesh — the city's famous backdrop of snow-capped peaks visible in winter and spring above the southern walls. The mountains are closest here: Jebel Toubkal, at 4,167 metres the highest peak in North Africa, is visible on clear days as a distinct summit above the Atlas range.

The best viewpoints are from the roads immediately south of the Agdal walls, where there is nothing between you and the mountains except the flat agricultural plain of the Haouz. The view is at its finest in winter after snowfall and in late afternoon when the light turns the snow golden. The combination of the ancient gardens, the ochre city walls, and the high mountains behind them is the defining image of Marrakesh in every direction.
