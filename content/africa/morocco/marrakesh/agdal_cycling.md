The roads around the perimeter of the Agdal — running south from the Kasbah walls through the outer residential districts — are among the more pleasant cycling routes in Marrakesh. The traffic is lighter than in the city centre, the road surface generally good, and the scale of the landscape — the long straight roads with the garden walls on one side and the Atlas mountains visible at the end — is distinctly different from the compressed scale of the Medina.

Several bicycle hire shops operate in Gueliz and the Medina. A morning ride around the Agdal, stopping to look through the gates at the orchards within when they are visible, and continuing south to see the outer walls in their landscape context, takes about two hours at an easy pace. The ride works best in winter or spring when the temperatures are moderate.
