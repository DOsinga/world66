The modern residential district that shares the Agdal name with the royal gardens has grown up around the garden walls in the southern part of the city. The neighbourhood is quieter and more spacious than the historic centre, with apartment buildings and villas housing a middle-class Marrakshi population that values its distance from the tourist areas.

The Agdal district has several good neighbourhood restaurants, a daily market, and the kind of local cafes that serve businesspeople and residents. The broad avenues here were developed from the 1970s onwards and have a New Town feeling — modern Morocco rather than historic Morocco — but the proximity to the royal gardens and the Atlas mountains visible from the southern streets give the area a quality that compensates for its architectural modesty.
