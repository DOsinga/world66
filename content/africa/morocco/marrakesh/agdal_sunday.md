The Agdal royal gardens open to the public on Fridays and Sundays when the Moroccan royal family is not in residence — which in practice means most of the year except during official visits and the summer season. The opening is free and draws a large, local crowd: Marrakshi families who come to picnic, children to play, and couples to walk in the shade of the olive groves.

The experience of the Agdal on a Sunday afternoon is one of the most genuinely Moroccan activities available to visitors in Marrakesh — not a tourist attraction but a public park that happens to be magnificent. Bring a picnic, hire a pedalo on the lake, and spend several hours doing what Marrakshis do on their day off. Arriving before midday ensures the best spot under the olives before the crowds arrive.
