A 5-acre contemporary art garden 27 km south of Marrakesh on the road to Ourika, mixing botanical collections from across the world with sculptures, mosaics, and installations. The garden is laid out as a wandering path through bamboo groves, palm walks, cactus beds, and water features, with art emerging from the vegetation at intervals.

A free shuttle bus runs from the Theatro nightclub in Marrakesh in the morning, returning in the afternoon. Allow three hours including travel. The on-site café is good.
