On clear winter and spring days, the snow line of the High Atlas runs along the horizon south of the city — visible from any high rooftop in the medina and most strikingly from the terraces around the Koutoubia. The view often catches first-time visitors off guard because Marrakesh feels and looks so flat at street level. The best sunset perches are the rooftop bars at El Fenn and Le Salama, and the parapet of the Le Jardin Secret tower.
