One of the more imposing of the medina's surviving gates, on the western side of the walls. Bab Doukkala is also a useful orientation point: the adjacent square is a major stop for buses to Casablanca, and the souk immediately inside the gate (Souk Bab Doukkala) is a local market where Marrakshis still do their daily shopping — much less touristy than the central souks.
