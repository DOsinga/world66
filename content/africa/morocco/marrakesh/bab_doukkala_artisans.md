The souk immediately south of the Bab Doukkala market is a working neighbourhood commercial area — fabric and clothing shops, household goods, small hardware stores — that serves the residential medina around it rather than tourists. The transition from the market stalls near the gate to the deeper souk lanes involves progressively more fabric, more tailoring, and the kind of practical commerce that underwrites Moroccan daily life.

Several fabric sellers in this area specialise in djellaba cloth and traditional Moroccan textile work — the raw material that Moroccan tailors transform into the robes and caftans worn throughout the country. Buying fabric here and having it made up by one of the nearby tailors is a worthwhile way to acquire genuinely Moroccan clothing at prices that reflect local rather than tourist economics.
