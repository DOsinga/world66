The Bab Doukkala area has several neighbourhood hammams that have operated continuously for generations — traditional bathhouses that serve the local population and are willing to admit visitors who come without expectation of a tourist experience. The procedure is the same as any hammam (hot room, scrub, soap), but the context is a working neighbourhood facility: simple, functional, and considerably cheaper than the tourist hammams of the Medina or the hotel spas of Hivernage.

Your riad host, if staying nearby, can direct you to the nearest neighbourhood hammam and advise on timing (men and women typically use separate hammams or use the same hammam at different hours). Bringing your own kessa scrub mitt, a change of clothing, and a small towel is advisable. The experience at a genuine neighbourhood hammam — the steam, the physical scrub, the relaxed hour afterwards — is one of the most distinctly Moroccan things you can do in the city.
