The Bab Doukkala area has become one of the more interesting addresses in the medina for riad stays — the lower tourist density compared to the Kasbah or Mouassine quarters means prices tend to be more reasonable, and the neighbourhood provides a more authentic context for the riad experience. Several well-regarded riads have opened here in the past decade, restoring significant 19th-century houses.

Staying in Bab Doukkala puts you within walking distance of the main souks (fifteen minutes south) and the Mouassine mosque fountains, while remaining in a neighbourhood where local life continues largely undisturbed by tourism. The northern walls and the medina's quieter gardens are nearby. For repeat visitors to Marrakesh who have already experienced the standard riad circuits, Bab Doukkala offers a useful alternative perspective.
