The northern section of the medina walls between Bab Doukkala and Bab el-Khemis (the Thursday Market gate) is the least visited stretch of Marrakesh's 19-kilometre ramparts — a long, unbroken section of original pisé construction in good condition, running through a quieter part of the city away from the major tourist sites.

Walking the outside of the northern walls — a flat, forty-minute stroll — gives a perspective on the medina as a fortified organism: the towers at regular intervals, the repair patches from different centuries, the relationship between the walls and the gardens outside them. The Bab el-Khemis flea market beyond the next gate is a good destination for the end of this walk: a genuinely local market for second-hand goods, spare parts, and recycled materials that convenes weekly and has no tourist orientation.
