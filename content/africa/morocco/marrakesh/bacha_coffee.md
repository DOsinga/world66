A coffee shop and roastery in the restored kitchens of the Dar el Bacha palace, with white marble, polished brass, and a serious approach to single-origin Arabicas. The list runs to over a hundred coffees from around the world and each can be ordered in the French press-style 1910 method. Pastries, sandwiches, and a few light dishes from a short menu. One of the calmest corners of the medina.
