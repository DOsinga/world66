The Marrakesh branch of the Café Clock that started in Fez — a contemporary café in a restored kasbah house south of the Bahia Palace, with a programme of Moroccan music, storytelling, and calligraphy evenings alongside a short modern-Moroccan menu. The famous camel burger is the menu's signature but the cooking generally is the best of the riad-café options. Useful for an evening event in the medina.
