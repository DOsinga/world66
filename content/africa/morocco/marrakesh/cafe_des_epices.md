A two-storey café on the corner of Rahba Kedima, with a rooftop terrace looking over the spice square and the rooftops of the souk. The drinks are simple — mint tea, fresh juices, coffee — and the food short and reliable: olive-oil and tomato bruschettas, salads, sandwiches. A useful pause halfway through a morning in the souks.
