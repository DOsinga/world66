A historic walled garden between Jemaa el-Fna and Gueliz, dating from the eighteenth century and redesigned in the 2000s with free wifi terminals scattered among the orange trees (hence the name). The cybernetics aspect has aged poorly but the park itself is one of the most pleasant green spaces in central Marrakesh, with shaded benches, calligraphic sculptures, and well-kept lawns.
