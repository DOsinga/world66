Design & Co spreads across three floors of a Sidi Ghanem warehouse, mixing contemporary gallery and design furniture showroom in a format that suits the district's hybrid character. Around 45 international artists are represented at any given time, working across painting, sculpture, photography, and design objects. The ground floor tends toward gallery presentation; the upper levels lean more toward furniture and decorative work.

The selection spans multiple movements and price points, making it accessible to both serious collectors and visitors looking for a well-made object to bring home. Open Monday to Friday 10am–6pm, Saturday 10am–4pm, closed Sunday.
