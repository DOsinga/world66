The Es Saadi is a palace-scale hotel in the Hivernage district with a history stretching back to the 1950s, when it was the address of choice for the French Protectorate's social world. The hotel's art deco and Moorish architecture, set in extensive gardens with rose beds and fountains, makes it a compelling place even for non-guests.

The main terrace operates as a public café and is a meeting point for Marrakshi society on weekend evenings. The adjacent casino — one of the few operating in the city — adds a distinct energy after dark. The gardens can be visited independently; the hotel is a useful orientation point for exploring Hivernage's quiet streets and planted avenues.
