Galerie Pop operates on a different logic from the district's more formal galleries: it's a shared creative loft where artists, artisans, and designers each curate their own corner of a large white industrial space. The result is part gallery, part bazaar, part design market — ceramics beside vintage furniture, handmade textiles beside contemporary lighting, fashion pieces alongside children's objects.

The emphasis is on handmade and one-off work rather than mass production. Some makers are local Moroccan craftspeople working traditional forms; others are international designers drawn to Marrakesh for the craft traditions and the light. What links them is a resistance to the kind of tourist-market reproduction that fills the medina souks. Worth wandering through even if you're not buying.
