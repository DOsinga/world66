A grand 1920s colonial café in Gueliz, restored as a brasserie and bar serving French-Moroccan classics in a wood-panelled dining room with brass fans, leather banquettes, and white-jacketed waiters. The food is reasonable and the setting genuinely time-warp. A useful destination for an evening out of the medina.
