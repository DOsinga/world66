Gueliz has become the centre of Marrakesh's contemporary art scene, with several notable galleries clustered around the streets off Avenue Mohammed V. The David Bloch Gallery, Matisse Art Gallery, and a number of newer spaces show work by Moroccan artists alongside international figures — the quality is generally high and admission is usually free.

The art scene here reflects a particular Marrakesh cultural moment: international collectors and riad owners have driven demand for serious contemporary work, while Moroccan artists have found in Marrakesh a market that does not exist in Casablanca or Rabat. The galleries in Gueliz are a genuine cultural destination, not tourist traps. Opening hours tend to be afternoon-focused; Tuesdays are often closed.
