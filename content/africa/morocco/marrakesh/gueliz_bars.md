Gueliz is where Marrakesh's bar scene is concentrated — the new town's French-era architecture and alcohol licensing make it the natural home for cafes, wine bars, and cocktail venues. The terrasses on Avenue Mohammed V fill in the evenings with a mix of Moroccan professionals, expats, and visitors who want a drink in a non-hotel setting.

The bars range from simple neighbourhood establishments serving Flag beer and local wine to more ambitious cocktail bars with lists of Moroccan spirits. Several of the better wine bars have good selections of Moroccan wine — the Atlas, Ouled Thaleb, and CB appellations are worth trying if you haven't encountered Moroccan wine before. The bar scene in Gueliz is less developed than Casablanca's or Rabat's, but it is the most vivid in Marrakesh.
