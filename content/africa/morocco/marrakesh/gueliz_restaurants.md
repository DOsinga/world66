Gueliz has the most reliable concentration of consistently good restaurants in Marrakesh — without the tourist premium of the medina palace restaurants or the variable quality of riad dining. The streets around Rue de la Liberté and the side streets off Avenue Mohammed V have French bistros, Italian restaurants, and Moroccan establishments at prices that reflect a local as much as a tourist clientele.

The key advantage of eating in Gueliz is wine. Unlike the medina, where alcohol licensing is difficult, Gueliz restaurants routinely have good wine lists. The food quality at the better Gueliz establishments is high — several have real chefs rather than hotel kitchen assembly — and the service is generally less theatrical than the medina palace restaurants. For a serious dinner outside the tourist economy, Gueliz is the place to look.
