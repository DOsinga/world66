Gueliz offers a different kind of Marrakesh shopping from the souk experience: fixed prices, air conditioning, and goods aimed at a middle-class Moroccan and international market. The main shopping streets around Rue de la Liberté and the Carré Eden mall have Moroccan and international brands, bookshops, music stores, and the kind of lifestyle shops that have colonised the neighbourhood alongside the art galleries.

Several shops in Gueliz specialise in curated Moroccan crafts — argan oil products, quality babouches and leather goods, contemporary takes on traditional textiles — at prices that are higher than the souks but without the negotiation and uncertainty. For anyone who wants quality Moroccan goods with a fixed price and no pressure, Gueliz is a useful complement to souk shopping.
