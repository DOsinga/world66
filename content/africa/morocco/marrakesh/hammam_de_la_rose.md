One of the more reliable hammams aimed at visitors — clean, professional, and not the cheapest. Treatments run from a basic gommage (black-soap scrub and rinse) to longer rituals with argan oil massage. Booking ahead is recommended. For a more local experience, the unisex but separate-hours public hammams near the souks cost a tenth as much and are part of weekly life for medina residents.
