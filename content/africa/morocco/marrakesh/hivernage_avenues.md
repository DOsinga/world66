The tree-lined avenues of Hivernage — planted with orange, jacaranda, and Washingtonia palms by the French Protectorate administration — are the defining character of the neighbourhood and one of the more pleasant walking environments in Marrakesh. Unlike the Medina lanes, which are designed to disorient, or the broad traffic arteries of Gueliz, Hivernage's avenues are shaded, quiet, and easy to navigate.

An early-morning walk through Hivernage, from the Koutoubia towards the Menara Gardens, takes about forty minutes and passes through gardens, walled hotel grounds, and residential streets where the city's wealthier Marrakshi families live. The district has a pace entirely its own — unhurried and insulated from both the tourist intensity of the Medina and the commercial bustle of Gueliz.
