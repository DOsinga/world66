Hivernage's defining quality is its greenery — the neighbourhood is essentially a collection of walled gardens with buildings inside them. The private gardens of the grand hotels and villas are invisible to the street but collectively create a microclimate distinctly cooler than the stone-paved medina. On summer days when central Marrakesh is genuinely hot, Hivernage can be 5–8 degrees cooler due to the transpiration of its mature trees.

The relationship between garden and building — borrowed from the riad tradition but scaled up to European villa proportions — gives Hivernage its distinctive character. Walking the neighbourhood's avenues in the morning, when residents exercise their dogs and the garden smells carry through the gates, is the best introduction to a part of Marrakesh that exists primarily for the comfort of those who live and stay here.
