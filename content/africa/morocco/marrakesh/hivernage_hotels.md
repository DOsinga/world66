Hivernage contains some of Marrakesh's grandest hotels — the Mamounia, the Es Saadi, and several newer luxury properties — set in extensive gardens that form a green buffer between the neighbourhood's quiet streets and the bustle of the medina. The hotels themselves are, in several cases, architectural landmarks: the Mamounia's art deco interiors, designed when Winston Churchill stayed here regularly, are among the most celebrated hotel spaces in Africa.

The Mamounia's garden bar is open to non-guests and offers an afternoon experience — tea, cocktails, or simply a walk in the grounds — that is worth the price of entry. The Es Saadi, slightly further into Hivernage, operates a casino and has a lively terrace that attracts Marrakshi society on weekend evenings. Several other hotels in the area have spa facilities open to day visitors at significant but reasonable cost.
