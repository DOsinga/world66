Hivernage is home to Marrakesh's main nightclub district — a concentration of upscale clubs and late-night bars that operate until dawn and attract a mixed crowd of wealthy Moroccans, expatriates, and tourists staying in the nearby hotels. The clubs here are a world away from the neighbourhood's daytime calm: bouncers on doors, dress codes, and sound systems at significant volume.

The club scene in Hivernage orbits around the Es Saadi hotel and several purpose-built venues on the main avenues. The music is primarily Western EDM and Arabic pop, the crowd is younger and more affluent than the city's general tourist population, and the prices reflect both. Getting in requires appropriate dress and often a reservation; getting out before 3am suggests you arrived too early. This is genuine nightlife rather than a tourist entertainment product.
