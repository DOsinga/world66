Hivernage has a small but high-quality restaurant scene anchored by the hotel restaurants of the Mamounia and Es Saadi, and supplemented by independent establishments that serve the neighbourhood's wealthy residents and hotel guests. The cooking here tends towards Franco-Moroccan fusion rather than traditional Moroccan — the clientele expects wine lists and international menus.

The best independent restaurants in Hivernage tend to be in the streets immediately south of the Koutoubia, where several long-established establishments have cultivated a loyal following among Marrakesh's international community. The food is reliably good, the atmosphere relaxed, and the prices significant but not outrageous by international standards. For a serious evening meal outside the medina, Hivernage is the best of the city's alternatives.
