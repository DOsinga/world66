Hivernage contains the highest concentration of luxury spa facilities in Marrakesh — several of the grand hotels have invested heavily in spa and wellness facilities that are open to day visitors. The Mamounia's spa, the Es Saadi's spa and hammam complex, and several boutique hotel spas offer a range of traditional Moroccan treatments (hammam, rhassoul clay, kessa scrub) in settings considerably more elaborate than the neighbourhood hammams.

The difference between a hotel spa hammam and a neighbourhood hammam is largely one of context rather than technique — the procedures are similar, but the hotel version adds ambient lighting, scented oils, trained therapists, and prices that reflect all of the above. For those who want a hammam experience without the uncertainty of navigating a neighbourhood bathhouse, the hotel spas of Hivernage are the obvious choice.
