The lanes between Bab Agnaou and the Saadian Tombs contain a cluster of artisan workshops where traditional crafts — leatherwork, metalwork, woodcarving — are produced and sold in the same space. These are generally working workshops rather than tourist shops: the craftsmen are actually making things, and the goods on sale reflect production rather than marketing.

Prices here are lower than in the main souks, partly because the rents are lower and partly because the customer base is more mixed — Moroccan buyers as well as tourists. Negotiation is expected but the opening prices tend to be more realistic. The Kasbah workshops particularly specialise in brasswork and decorative objects for riads, which has made the area a destination for the interior design trade.
