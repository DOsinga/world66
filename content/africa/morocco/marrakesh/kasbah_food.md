The Kasbah quarter has developed a small but good restaurant scene that serves visitors to the Saadian Tombs without the aggressive touts of the main medina. The cafes around the Kasbah square tend to have reasonable food at honest prices — tagines, couscous, pastilla — and rooftop terraces with views of the mosque minaret.

Several riad restaurants in the Kasbah open their courtyard dining to non-guests in the evening, offering a more intimate experience than the tourist palace restaurants further north. The Kasbah night market on the square is smaller and quieter than Jemaa el-Fnaa but runs similar stalls — grilled meats, harira soup, fresh fruit — with fewer people targeting tourists.
