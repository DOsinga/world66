The Kasbah quarter has several traditional hammams — neighbourhood bathhouses that have operated continuously for centuries and continue to serve the local population alongside tourists. Unlike the tourist-oriented hammams of the northern medina, the Kasbah hammams have a mixed clientele of Marrakshi residents who use them weekly, as they have always done.

A visit to a neighbourhood hammam is a more authentic experience than the tourist spa versions: simpler facilities, lower prices, and the rhythm of an institution built into local life. The procedure is the same — steam room, kessa scrub with a mitt, savon beldi (black olive soap) — but the context is a working bathhouse rather than a curated wellness experience. Staff at your riad can direct you to the nearest neighbourhood hammam and advise on timing.
