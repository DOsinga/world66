The streets immediately around the Saadian Tombs and the Kasbah Mosque contain a concentration of restored riads that have become, in recent years, among the most desirable addresses in Marrakesh. The Kasbah quarter has a particular advantage over other medina neighbourhoods: it is quieter, has fewer motorbikes, and the scale of the lanes is more intimate than the commercial streets of the souks.

Walking through the Kasbah residential lanes — especially in the early morning or after the Saadian Tombs close in the evening — gives a sense of the medina as a place people actually live. The smell of bread from a small bakery, a craftsman working leather in a doorway, children heading to school — the Kasbah has retained this quotidian life while also hosting sophisticated riads that attract experienced Marrakesh visitors.
