The Kasbah quarter has its own small covered market where residents buy food, household goods, and clothing. This is a functional neighbourhood market rather than a tourist attraction — fruit and vegetables, meat, dry goods — with prices in local currency and sellers who are not especially interested in visitors.

For those who have spent time in the main souks and want to see how Marrakshis actually shop, the Kasbah market is instructive. The contrast with the tourist-oriented souk experience is significant: no touts, no fixed pricing for tourists, no carpets or lanterns. Just an everyday market in a city where the daily shopping still happens in covered lanes rather than supermarkets.
