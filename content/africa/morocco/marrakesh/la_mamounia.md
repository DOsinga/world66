La Mamounia is one of the great hotels of the world — a property that has been receiving guests since 1923 in the grounds of a royal garden dating from the 18th century. Churchill painted here, the Rolling Stones stayed in the 1970s, and every significant visitor to Marrakesh of the past century has at some point passed through its doors. The hotel was comprehensively renovated in 2009 by designer Jacques Garcia, keeping the art deco bones while adding a layer of luxury that sets a high standard for the city.

Even if you are not staying here, La Mamounia is worth visiting. The garden — nine hectares of olive trees, rose gardens, and fountains — is open to guests for afternoon tea, cocktails, or dinner in one of the several restaurants. The architecture, particularly the great hall and the Churchill Bar, rewards unhurried exploration. Going is a rare case where the experience fully justifies the price.
