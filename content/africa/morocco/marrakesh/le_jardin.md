A green courtyard restaurant inside a sixteenth-century riad in the souks, with old turtles wandering between the tables and bird cages hanging from the trees. The menu is short and modern-Moroccan; the appeal is the cool, quiet courtyard as a refuge from the lanes outside. A good lunch stop midway through a souk crawl.
