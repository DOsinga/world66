A rare licensed bar inside the medina, on the upper floors of a converted house north of Jemaa el-Fna. Decorated in over-the-top Orientalist style, with a roof terrace overlooking the Koutoubia minaret. The cocktails are fine and the view at sunset is the main reason to come. The restaurant downstairs serves competent Moroccan-French food.
