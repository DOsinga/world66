The most visited tourist attraction in Morocco, and worth the queue. The 1.6-hectare garden in the Gueliz district is densely planted with cactus, palm, bougainvillea, and bamboo around a cobalt-blue art-deco studio building, with bright yellow planters set against the blue walls. The plant collection is large and well-labelled, drawn from five continents.

The on-site Berber Museum, in Jacques Majorelle's former villa, is small but excellent — a tight overview of Berber textiles, jewellery, and ceremonial objects from Saint Laurent's collection. The separate Yves Saint Laurent Museum, a five-minute walk away, has its own ticket. Buy timed-entry tickets online: walk-ups can queue for hours in high season.
