Marrakech Fine Food occupies a 200 m² space that blurs the line between specialty food shop and café. The shelves hold globally sourced coffees, teas, chocolates, spices, dates, and prepared foods; the counter serves espresso drinks made with beans roasted in-house, alongside pastries and viennoiseries produced in collaboration with the Joël Robuchon group. The result is a morning coffee and lunch stop of real quality in a district that otherwise requires a taxi ride back to Guéliz for anything comparable.

Sandwiches, salads, and all-day snacks round out the menu. It has become a mid-morning gathering point for the Sidi Ghanem design community — architects, gallery owners, and showroom staff — which makes it a good place to get a feel for the district even before exploring the surrounding workshops.
