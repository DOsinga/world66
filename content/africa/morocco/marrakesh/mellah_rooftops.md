The Mellah's distinctive multi-storey houses, with their projecting upper floors and roof terraces, give the quarter a vertical dimension that is unusual in the medina. Several cafes and restaurants in the area have rooftop terraces that look across the tightly packed Mellah roofscape towards the Koutoubia minaret to the west and the mountains beyond.

The rooftop view over the Mellah — densely packed terraces with the Bahia Palace gardens visible below and the medina's minarets rising above — is one of the better urban panoramas in Marrakesh. The clay-tiled roofs of the old Mellah houses have a different texture and colour from the flat terraces of the rest of the medina, giving the view a distinctive quality that rewards those who seek it out.
