A small museum opened in 2018, the first of its kind in North Africa, devoted to the cultural and artistic achievements of Moroccan women. Rotating exhibitions cover everything from Berber tattoo traditions to contemporary photography, weaving, and music. The space is intimate — three floors of a renovated riad — and the labelling is bilingual French and English. Allow forty-five minutes.
