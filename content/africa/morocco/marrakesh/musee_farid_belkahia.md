Musee Farid Belkahia is devoted to one of Morocco's major modern artists, with displays that follow different periods of his work. It is a quieter counterpoint to the medina's palaces, gardens, and craft markets.

Come if you are interested in contemporary Moroccan art, or if you want a Marrakesh museum that looks beyond the usual historic circuit.
