Hot-air balloon flights over Marrakesh launch from the edge of the Palmeraie before sunrise and drift south over the palm grove, the city's rooftops, and the agricultural plain towards the Atlas mountains. The view from above — the Medina's minarets and flat rooftops, the pink city walls, the green Palmeraie, and the white peaks of the Atlas in the distance — is genuinely extraordinary and available in very few other ways.

Flights last approximately an hour and operate October through April when the weather is most reliable. Several operators are based in the Palmeraie; the experience is weather-dependent and occasionally cancelled at short notice. The flight typically ends with a Berber breakfast in the open country south of the city, which is itself a pleasant experience. This is expensive by Marrakesh standards but represents reasonable value as a once-in-a-lifetime perspective on the city and landscape.
