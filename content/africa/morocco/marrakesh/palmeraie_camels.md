Camel rides in the Palmeraie are a well-established tourist activity, with operators stationed along the main Route de Fès track through the grove. The circuits last between thirty minutes and two hours and follow the piste roads through the denser parts of the grove. The camels are handled by Berber guides who also manage the logistics and often provide commentary about the grove and its history.

The experience is pleasant if modest — camels move slowly and the Palmeraie at ground level is less dramatic than it appears from above. The grove's density has thinned considerably due to the Red Palm Weevil and urban encroachment, so the landscape is more open than photographs from the 1980s suggest. Animal welfare standards at the Palmeraie operators vary; choosing an operator with well-maintained, not underfed animals is worth brief inspection before booking.
