The Palmeraie is best experienced by bicycle — the sandy piste roads that wind through the groves are largely traffic-free and the flat terrain is easy cycling. Several operators near the Route de Fès junction rent bicycles and can suggest routes through the grove, ranging from a two-hour circuit to a full-day exploration that takes in the outer edges where the palms give way to cultivated fields.

The experience changes significantly between morning and afternoon. Early morning in the grove — before the tour groups arrive with their quads and camels — the Palmeraie has a genuinely peaceful quality: the sound of birds, the smell of earth and palms, the occasional glimpse of a riad garden through an open gate. By midday the dust of the quad bikes and the commercial activity around the main track make the atmosphere considerably less serene.
