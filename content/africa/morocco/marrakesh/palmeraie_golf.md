The Palmeraie Golf Palace is one of several golf courses in the Marrakesh area, occupying a large estate on the northern edge of the palm grove. The eighteen-hole course winds through the palms and is irrigated to a standard unusual in an arid country. The complex includes the golf course, a large hotel, tennis courts, and pool facilities.

Golf is a significant part of the Marrakesh tourist economy, with several courses — the Royal Golf Club (founded 1927 by the French), the Amelkis, and others — catering to a European market that visits specifically for warm-weather golf. The Palmeraie Golf Palace is the most accessible from the Medina. For non-golfers, the course's estate provides a pleasant cycling circuit when closed to play.
