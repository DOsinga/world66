The Palmeraie's main attraction for visitors is its extraordinary concentration of luxury accommodation — some of the most celebrated boutique riads and resort hotels in Morocco, hidden behind unmarked walls along sandy tracks through the palm groves. The properties range from intimate six-room riads with private pools to sprawling resort complexes with multiple restaurants, golf courses, and spa facilities.

Staying in the Palmeraie involves a trade-off: the seclusion and luxury are extraordinary, but reaching the Medina requires a taxi journey of fifteen minutes or more. Several properties run shuttle services to the centre. The most interesting Palmeraie hotels tend to be the smaller owner-managed riads rather than the international brand properties, which could be anywhere.
