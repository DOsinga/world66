Quad biking in the Palmeraie is one of the most popular tourist activities in Marrakesh — dozens of operators run circuits through the palm groves on quad bikes and buggies, and the noise and dust generated by the industry have become one of the main complaints about the Palmeraie experience. The activity is not for everyone, but for those who want a fast, outdoor afternoon activity it delivers straightforwardly.

The standard circuit runs for two to three hours through the grove and often includes a stop at a Berber village for mint tea. Prices vary significantly between operators; booking through your hotel usually means a premium. The best quads are along the Route de Fès north of the city. Going in the late afternoon rather than the midday heat is advisable — both for comfort and because the light through the palms is considerably more interesting.
