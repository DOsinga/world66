Several of the Palmeraie's restaurant estates offer lunch in settings of extraordinary beauty — courtyard pools, mature gardens, tables under the palms — that justify a taxi journey from the city centre for a long afternoon meal. The cooking at the better establishments ranges from traditional Moroccan palace cuisine (pastilla, lamb mechui, couscous) to contemporary Mediterranean menus.

The lunch estates tend to require advance reservation and sometimes have minimum spend requirements. The format — arriving at midday, eating slowly through several courses, spending the afternoon by a pool or in a garden — suits a long visit to Marrakesh rather than a short weekend break. Several estates offer a combination of lunch and an afternoon spa or hammam session that makes a full day of it.
