The Palmeraie's resort hotels have invested heavily in spa facilities that are available to day visitors as well as hotel guests. The hammam and wellness packages here typically combine the traditional Moroccan hammam sequence (heat room, kessa scrub, black soap) with spa additions — massages, facials, argan oil treatments — in settings of considerable elaborateness.

The Palmeraie spa experience is at the higher end of the Marrakesh wellness market. What you are paying for, beyond the treatments themselves, is the garden setting: the ability to move between the hammam and a private pool surrounded by palms is genuinely different from a city spa. Day packages typically include lunch, afternoon tea, and use of the pool and gardens, making them a full-day commitment.
