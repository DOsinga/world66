The Palmeraie at sunrise is the grove at its best — the low light picking out the textures of the palms, the mist over the irrigated fields, the mountains beginning to clarify on the horizon. The tourist operations have not started, the quad bikes are silent, and the grove is occupied only by early-morning dog walkers and the occasional donkey cart carrying produce from the farms.

Several hotels in the Palmeraie offer sunrise horseback rides or balloon flights that begin early enough to catch the best light. A hot-air balloon over the Palmeraie at dawn — looking down at the grove with the Medina's minarets and the Atlas behind it — is one of the genuinely outstanding tourist experiences available in Marrakesh. The balloon operators are based on the edge of the grove and the flights typically run October to April when the weather is most reliable.
