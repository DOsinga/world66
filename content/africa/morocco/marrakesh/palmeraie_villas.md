The Palmeraie has become over the past two decades a showcase for contemporary Moroccan riad architecture scaled up to villa size — large private properties built by European and Moroccan owners who wanted the riad experience (courtyard, garden, pool) in a setting with more space and privacy than the Medina allows. Many of these villas are rentable as private property and represent some of the best accommodation in Morocco.

The architecture ranges from traditional Moroccan pastiche to seriously ambitious contemporary work. Several internationally significant architects have built in the Palmeraie, and the range of approaches — from orthodox Moroccan to radical contemporary — makes the grove an interesting field for architectural tourism, even if most of the best buildings are behind walls and not publicly visible.
