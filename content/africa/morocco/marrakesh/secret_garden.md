A restored seventeenth-century riad-garden complex on the Mouassine road, reopened to the public in 2016 after a long restoration. Two gardens — an exotic one with cacti, palms, and bougainvillea, and a more formal Islamic four-part garden of orange and olive trees — sit either side of an original water system fed from the Atlas. A small entrance tower can be climbed for a high view across the medina rooftops to the Koutoubia.
