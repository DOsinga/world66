Semlalia contains several of Marrakesh's better mid-range hotels — three and four-star properties that offer good value compared to the medina riads or Hivernage grand hotels. The location between the old city and Gueliz is practical: the Medina is a ten-minute walk and taxis are plentiful.

Staying in Semlalia rather than the Medina has practical advantages: quieter streets, easier vehicle access, fewer touts, and a more relaxed pace. The trade-off is losing the experience of waking up in the old city and hearing the dawn call to prayer resonate off the walls. For visitors who want to visit the Medina but sleep in relative calm, or who are travelling with children or mobility considerations, Semlalia's hotels are worth considering.
