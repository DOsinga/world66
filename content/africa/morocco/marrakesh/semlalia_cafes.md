The streets around the Cyber Park and the northern edge of the Koutoubia have a cluster of cafes and Moroccan patisseries that serve a local clientele of students, office workers, and residents rather than tourists. The coffee and mint tea here are consistently good, the pastries excellent — baklava, briouats, and msemen alongside French-style viennoiseries — and the prices reflect a neighbourhood that is not primarily tourist.

The Semlalia cafes offer a useful mid-morning break between exploring the medina and heading north to Gueliz. The atmosphere is pleasant and unhurried; tables tend to turn slowly on weekend mornings when families come for breakfast. The street patisseries that sell directly from trays are even more affordable and give a good sense of the everyday Moroccan sugar economy.
