Semlalia has a good selection of neighbourhood restaurants serving simple Moroccan food at local prices — tagines, couscous on Fridays, grilled fish, and the daily menu (menu du jour) format that gives you three courses for a fixed price around lunchtime. These are largely unknown to tourists, which keeps the prices honest and the clientele predominantly Moroccan.

Several restaurants in Semlalia specialise in food for the office lunch trade — quick, filling, well-priced. The food is typically better than similar-priced options in the medina because the competition is from neighbouring Moroccan restaurants rather than other tourist-oriented establishments. Walking into any restaurant that appears to have a Moroccan clientele and ordering the daily menu is a reliable strategy.
