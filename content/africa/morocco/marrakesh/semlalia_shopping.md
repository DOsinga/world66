Semlalia's main commercial street has the practical shops of a neighbourhood that does not depend on tourism: fabric shops, tailors, small supermarkets, hardware stores, and the mobile phone repair kiosks that have become ubiquitous in Moroccan cities. The goods here are for Marrakshis rather than visitors, and the experience of shopping in Semlalia — for sunscreen, a phone charger, a pair of trousers — is considerably less fraught than in the Medina souks.

The neighbourhood also has several argane oil and Moroccan beauty product shops that sell at local prices rather than the tourist premium charged in Gueliz boutiques. The quality is often the same — some of the products come from the same cooperatives — but the packaging is less elegant and the price considerably lower.
