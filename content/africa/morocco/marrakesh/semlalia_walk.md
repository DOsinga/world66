The walk between the Koutoubia Mosque and the centre of Gueliz via the Semlalia district takes about twenty minutes on foot and is one of the better urban walks in Marrakesh — a transition from the ochre stone of the old city through the green breathing space of the Cyber Park and into the art deco streetscape of the new town. The Koutoubia minaret remains visible through much of the walk, acting as a landmark and orientation point.

The route passes through the transition zone where Marrakesh shifts from medieval to modern: old walls giving way to apartment buildings, donkey carts giving way to taxis, medina lanes widening into boulevards. The Semlalia segment of this walk — the Arset Moulay Abdeslam garden, the quiet streets behind it — is the most pleasant interlude. Walking this route in both directions at different times of day gives a useful overview of how the city's layers relate to each other.
