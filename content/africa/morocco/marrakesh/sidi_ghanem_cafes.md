Sidi Ghanem's transformation into a design district has brought with it the cafes that creative districts everywhere require. Several good coffee shops have opened along the main strip, serving the designers, buyers, and creative professionals who work in the quarter. The food ranges from excellent Moroccan pastries to contemporary brunch menus, and the coffee is consistently better than you will find in most of the Medina.

The cafes in Sidi Ghanem have a particular atmosphere — relaxed, professional, with a design-conscious clientele — that is quite different from either the tourist terrace cafes of Jemaa el-Fnaa or the neighbourhood cafes of Gueliz. Spending an hour or two here between showrooms is a pleasant alternative to the intensity of the Medina.
