Moroccan furniture design has evolved significantly in the past two decades, moving from the heavy carved cedar pieces that furnished traditional riads to a more varied contemporary production that uses cedar, walnut, metal, and leather in hybrid forms. Sidi Ghanem is where this contemporary furniture is produced and sold.

The furniture workshops and showrooms here cater primarily to the professional market — hotels, riad owners, interior designers — but welcome individual buyers. The pieces are typically made to order with delivery arrangements; several studios will ship internationally. The quality and design sensibility is considerably higher than the medina craft shops, and for those outfitting a Moroccan property or looking for significant pieces to bring home, a day in Sidi Ghanem comparing workshops is the most efficient approach.
