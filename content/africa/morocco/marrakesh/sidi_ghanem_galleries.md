Several contemporary art galleries have established themselves in Sidi Ghanem alongside the design showrooms, drawn by the large industrial spaces and the proximity to a design-oriented clientele. The work shown here tends towards contemporary Moroccan and North African art — painting, sculpture, photography, and installation — with occasional international group shows.

The gallery scene in Sidi Ghanem is less visible to casual tourists than the Gueliz galleries but often shows more ambitious work. The spaces are larger, allowing for installation pieces and large-format work that the Gueliz galleries cannot accommodate. Opening hours are typically Tuesday to Saturday afternoons; several galleries close during the August heat. A call ahead before visiting is advisable.
