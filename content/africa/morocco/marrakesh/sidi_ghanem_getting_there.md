Sidi Ghanem is located about 5 kilometres west of the Medina along the Route de Safi — too far to walk comfortably from the city centre but an easy taxi or ride-share journey of ten to fifteen minutes. Taxis from the medina typically cost 20–30 dirhams. Several operators run day trips to Sidi Ghanem combined with other western suburbs destinations (Majorelle Garden, the souks of Gueliz).

The district is spread along a kilometre or two of industrial road, so having a plan before arriving is worthwhile. The main concentration of showrooms is in the first section of the Route de Safi after the turn-off from the main ring road. Arriving by taxi allows the driver to navigate; cycling from Gueliz is possible along the Route de Safi but the road is not particularly pleasant for cyclists. The district is at its most active Tuesday through Saturday, roughly 10am to 6pm.
