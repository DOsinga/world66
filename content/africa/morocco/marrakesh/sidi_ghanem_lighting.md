Marrakesh's signature export may be the pierced-metal lantern — the brass and copper lamps that hang in every riad and fill every souk. In the souks these are produced industrially; in Sidi Ghanem, a cluster of lighting studios produces the high-end version: larger pieces, more complex patterns, better materials, and designs that range from traditional Moroccan to contemporary geometric work.

Several studios in Sidi Ghanem have international reputations for architectural lighting — their work appears in boutique hotels across Morocco and in interior design projects from Paris to Dubai. The showrooms display both the production version and the custom-project portfolio. Even for those not in the market for a major lighting installation, the studios are interesting to visit as a demonstration of how traditional Moroccan craft translates into high-end contemporary design.
