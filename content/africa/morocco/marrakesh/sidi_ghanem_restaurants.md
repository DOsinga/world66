The restaurants that have opened in Sidi Ghanem cater to the lunch trade from the surrounding design businesses and showrooms — reliably good food at business lunch prices, with wine lists that reflect a clientele that expects to drink at midday. The cooking tends to be Moroccan-European fusion or straightforward Moroccan in pleasant settings.

Several of the better Sidi Ghanem restaurants have become destinations in their own right — places that attract people from the city centre specifically for a long lunch in a relaxed environment away from the tourist intensity. The evening scene is quieter, as the design district largely closes after business hours, but a few restaurants stay open late enough for dinner. The taxi journey from the Medina (fifteen minutes, minimal cost) is not a deterrent for those who have discovered the quarter.
