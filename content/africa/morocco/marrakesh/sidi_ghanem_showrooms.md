The main strip of Sidi Ghanem along the Route de Safi contains a concentration of design showrooms that have made this former industrial zone the most interesting shopping destination in Marrakesh for home furnishings and contemporary craft. The showrooms occupy converted warehouses and purpose-built retail spaces, selling furniture, ceramics, textiles, and lighting designed and produced in Morocco.

The quality is generally high and the aesthetic ranges from traditional Moroccan craft reimagined in contemporary form to international minimalism with Moroccan materials. Several of Morocco's best-known designers — including the ceramicist Maryam Montague and various furniture designers who supply the international hotel market — have flagship showrooms here. Prices are higher than the souks but the goods are more reliably made and the provenance is traceable.
