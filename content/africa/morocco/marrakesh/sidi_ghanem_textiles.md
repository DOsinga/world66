The textile ateliers of Sidi Ghanem produce some of the most interesting contemporary Moroccan fabric work — reinterpreting traditional Berber weaving, Moroccan embroidery, and kilim patterns through contemporary design lenses. Several studios have attracted significant international attention and supply high-end hotels and interior design clients across Europe and the Gulf.

The fabrics produced here — cushion covers, throws, rugs, curtains — are available to individual buyers at the studio showrooms. The prices are significantly higher than the medina shops but the quality is reliably different: hand-woven rather than machine-made, natural dyes where claimed, and designs that are genuinely original rather than reproductions of tourist-market patterns. For those furnishing a home or looking for serious craft gifts, Sidi Ghanem's textile studios are worth the taxi journey.
