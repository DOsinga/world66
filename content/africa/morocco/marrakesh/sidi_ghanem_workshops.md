The workshops behind the showrooms of Sidi Ghanem are where the design district's output is actually produced — ceramicists, woodworkers, metalworkers, and textile designers working in spaces that are large enough for serious production. Several workshops are open to visitors who want to see how the goods are made, and the contrast with the performance-craft of the medina workshops — where production is staged for tourists — is striking.

The ceramics workshops in particular are worth visiting. The pottery tradition in Morocco is ancient but the interpretation varies enormously, and watching a contemporary Moroccan ceramicist work through their process — throwing, glazing, firing — gives a different picture of Moroccan craft than the souvenir shops of the Medina. Some workshops sell directly from the production floor at prices that reflect this.
