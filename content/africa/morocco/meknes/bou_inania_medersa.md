The Bou Inania Medersa is the finest medieval building in Meknes and one of the best examples of Marinid architecture in Morocco. Built in the 14th century as a Koranic school and student residence, it is decorated with an extraordinary combination of carved cedarwood screens, zellige tilework, and intricate stucco panels that cover virtually every surface. The courtyard is the finest room, with a marble fountain at its centre and carved galleries on two levels.

The medersa is open to non-Muslims, which is rare for a religious building in Morocco. A small entrance fee is charged. Climb to the roof terrace for views over the medina and the minaret of the adjacent mosque. Allow about an hour to do it justice.
