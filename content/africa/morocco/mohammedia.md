Mohammedia is a coastal city midway between [Casablanca](/africa/morocco/casablanca) (30 km south) and [Rabat](/africa/morocco/rabat) (60 km north). It began as a small Portuguese trading post and was known as Fedala until Moroccan independence. Today it serves largely as a beach getaway for Casablanca residents, with a long sandy coastline and a more relaxed pace than its larger neighbour.

The town has a pleasant [corniche](/africa/morocco/mohammedia/corniche), a small [kasbah](/africa/morocco/mohammedia/kasbah) near the [port](/africa/morocco/mohammedia/port), and one of Morocco's most distinguished links courses at the [Royal Golf Mohammedia](/africa/morocco/mohammedia/royal_golf_mohammedia). The beach is popular but can be rough — the Atlantic swells here are strong. Birders should look in on the marshes of the [Oued Mellah](/africa/morocco/mohammedia/oued_mellah) just west of town. Mohammedia is also home to Morocco's largest oil refinery, which sits at the edge of town and is hard to miss.

For travellers, Mohammedia is more of a lunch stop or half-day detour than a destination in its own right, but the [fish grills at the port](/africa/morocco/mohammedia/port_fish_grills) are excellent and considerably cheaper than Casablanca.
