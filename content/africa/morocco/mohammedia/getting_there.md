---
title: "Getting There"
type: section
---

Mohammedia sits on the main coastal axis between Casablanca and Rabat, which makes it one of the easiest places to reach in Morocco. The A1 motorway has its own Mohammedia exit; the drive from central Casablanca is about 30 km north and from Rabat about 60 km south.

ONCF trains on the Casablanca–Rabat–Kenitra line stop at Mohammedia station roughly every 30 minutes during the day, taking 20 minutes from Casa Voyageurs and 40 minutes from Rabat Ville. The station is a short taxi ride from the corniche.
