The Royal Golf Mohammedia is an 18-hole parkland course at the edge of town, set among mature eucalyptus and palm trees with the Atlantic in the background. The layout is flat and walkable, with several water hazards fed by the Oued Mellah and tight fairways that punish a wayward driver.

Green fees are modest by European standards and visitors can play without club membership; reserve a tee time a day in advance through the clubhouse. There is a decent restaurant on the terrace overlooking the 18th green that serves Moroccan and French dishes — open to non-golfers as well.
