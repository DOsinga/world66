The Roman ruins of [Volubilis](/africa/morocco/volubilis) lie about four kilometres downhill — close enough to walk in an hour, or a 50-dirham grand taxi ride. The imperial city of [Meknes](/africa/morocco/meknes), about 30 kilometres south, can be reached by shared grand taxi in under an hour. Many visitors base themselves in Meknes and do both Volubilis and Moulay Idriss as a single circular day trip.
