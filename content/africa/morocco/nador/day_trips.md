Nador's easiest day trip is [Melilla](/europe/spain/melilla), the Spanish enclave just along the coast, where Modernista architecture and fortress walls make a sharp contrast with the Moroccan Rif. [Al Hoceima](/africa/morocco/alhoceima) is farther west and deserves more than a day, but the coastal road gives a good taste of the Mediterranean Rif if you have your own transport.
