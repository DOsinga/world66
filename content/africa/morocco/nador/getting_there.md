---
title: Getting There
type: section
---

Nador has an airport south of town with domestic flights and seasonal European services aimed at the Moroccan diaspora. The city is also on Morocco's rail network, with trains running inland toward Taourirt for connections to Oujda, Fez, and the rest of the country.

Grand taxis and buses link Nador with [Al Hoceima](/africa/morocco/alhoceima), [Oujda](/africa/morocco/oujda), and the border crossing for [Melilla](/europe/spain/melilla). If you are arriving by ferry through Melilla, Nador is the natural Moroccan onward stop.
