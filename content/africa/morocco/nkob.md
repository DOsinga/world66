Nkob is a small village in the southern Draa Valley, about 160 km south of Ouarzazate on the road to Rissani and the Sahara. What makes it remarkable is the extraordinary density of [kasbahs](/africa/morocco/nkob/kasbahs_of_nkob) — around 45 of them, in varying states of preservation, rising above the palmeries and the semi-arid plateau. The village has been proposed for UNESCO World Heritage listing.

Most travellers pass through on the route between Ouarzazate and the Erg Chebbi dunes, but Nkob rewards a longer stop. The surrounding [Jebel Saghro](/africa/morocco/nkob/jebel_saghro) offers some of the wildest trekking in southern Morocco, and the two main [palmeries](/africa/morocco/nkob/palmery_of_nkob) give the village a lush quality that contrasts sharply with the rocky desert landscape outside. The restored [Kasbah Baha Baha](/africa/morocco/nkob/kasbah_baha_baha) is the easiest place to step inside one of the village's mud-brick fortified houses.
