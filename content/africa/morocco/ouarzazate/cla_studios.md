CLA Studios (Cinéma Les Atlas) is Ouarzazate's second major film production facility, located on the southeastern edge of town on the road toward Zagora. Smaller than the Atlas Film Studios but more frequently active, CLA has provided sets and locations for European and American productions including Asterix and Obelix: Mission Cleopatra and various French television films. The studio's standing sets include a large Egyptian street and a traditional Moroccan market quarter. When not in active production, guided tours are available — check availability locally as schedules are irregular.
