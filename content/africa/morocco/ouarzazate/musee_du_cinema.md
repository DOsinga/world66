Musée du Cinéma is a small film museum opposite Kasbah Taourirt, using old sets, props and production material to tell Ouarzazate's movie-making story. It is modest and not every display is well labelled, but it is a convenient in-town alternative if you do not have time to visit the larger studio sites outside Ouarzazate.
