Ouirgane is a small valley town nestled in the High Atlas mountains, about 60 kilometres south of [Marrakech](/africa/morocco/marrakech). It makes a natural stopping point before or after driving the Tizi n'Test pass — a spectacular and terrifying sequence of hairpin bends over the mountains to [Taroudant](/africa/morocco/taroudant) and the Souss Valley.

The valley itself is green and quiet, a world away from the bustle of Marrakech. It is a pleasant base for [hiking in the Atlas](/africa/morocco/ouirgane/hiking_in_the_atlas), mountain biking and horse riding in the surrounding hills, with trails leading through Berber villages and walnut groves.

The centrepiece of the village is [Lake Yacoub el Mansour](/africa/morocco/ouirgane/lake_yacoub_el_mansour), a reservoir created by the Ouirgane dam in 2008, its green-blue water striking against the dry mountains. About an hour's drive south on the Tizi n'Test road lies the remarkable [Tin Mal Mosque](/africa/morocco/ouirgane/tin_mal_mosque) — built in 1156, open to non-Muslims, and the direct architectural prototype for Marrakech's Koutoubia.
