Dar Sebti is a small ethnographic and craft museum housed in a restored merchant's house in the medina. The collections cover the dress, jewellery, and domestic objects of the eastern region — distinctive for their Berber and Algerian influences — alongside Andalusian musical instruments. The building itself, with its painted plasterwork and central patio, is the most accessible example of Oujda's domestic architecture.
