Oukaimeden is Africa's highest ski resort, sitting at around 2,600 metres in the High Atlas mountains, just 90 minutes from Marrakech. It draws a quirky mix of Moroccan families, French expats, and curious travellers who find it hard to believe you can ski in North Africa. The runs aren't long or technically demanding — this is more novelty than serious skiing — but the setting, with Atlas peaks all around and a village that feels genuinely Moroccan, is unlike any other ski resort on the continent. Ski and snowboard equipment is available to rent at the base.

Outside of ski season (roughly December through March), the plateau and surrounding trails make for excellent hiking and mountain biking. The area also has some of the oldest [rock carvings](/africa/morocco/oukaimeden/rock_carvings) in Africa, scattered across the surrounding hillsides — depictions of elephants, ibex, and cattle from a time when the Atlas climate was far wetter. The [Oukaimeden Observatory](/africa/morocco/oukaimeden/oukaimeden_observatory) on the plateau takes advantage of the high-altitude clear skies, and the night sky here is among the best in Morocco. The [Tacheddirt Valley](/africa/morocco/oukaimeden/tacheddirt_valley) below the resort connects to the wider High Atlas trekking network and the approaches to Jebel Toubkal.
