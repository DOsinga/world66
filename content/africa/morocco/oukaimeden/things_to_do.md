---
title: "Things to Do in Oukaimeden"
type: section
---

Oukaimeden offers two very different experiences depending on the season. In winter it is a ski resort — Africa's highest — with runs across a snowfield at 2,600 metres. Outside of winter, the plateau is ideal for hiking, mountain biking, and exploring the High Atlas landscape. Year-round, the prehistoric rock carvings scattered across the surrounding hillsides are worth seeking out.

The [Oukaimeden Ski Area](/africa/morocco/oukaimeden/ski_area) has lifts and equipment rental available during the ski season (December to March). The [Oukaimeden Observatory](/africa/morocco/oukaimeden/oukaimeden_observatory) uses the high-altitude clear skies for astronomical research. The [Rock Carvings of Oukaimeden](/africa/morocco/oukaimeden/rock_carvings) are one of the richest collections of prehistoric art in North Africa, scattered across the rocky slopes just outside the resort.

For serious trekkers, the [Tacheddirt Valley](/africa/morocco/oukaimeden/tacheddirt_valley) below the plateau leads to remote Berber villages and connects to longer Atlas routes toward Jebel Toubkal.
