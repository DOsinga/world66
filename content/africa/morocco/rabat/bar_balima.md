In the basement of the Hotel Balima on Avenue Mohammed V, directly opposite the Parliament. Known to locals as the "bat cave" for its underground setting. A Rabat institution — the bar where politicians, journalists, and expats have been drinking since the Protectorate era. Beer and spirits; prices are mid-range for a hotel bar.
