---
title: "Beaches"
type: section
---

The Atlantic coast south of Rabat has a string of beaches, most reachable by taxi or bus within 30 minutes. The city beach at Rabat itself is crowded in summer; the beaches further south are better.

**Temara Beach** (15 km south) is the most popular family beach — wide, sandy, and relatively calm. The small resort town of Temara has hotels and restaurants.

**Plage des Nations** (Sidi Bouknadel, 15 km north) is quieter, named for the diplomats who used to swim here. Be aware that the currents outside the central lifeguarded strip are dangerous — the Atlantic here is not the Mediterranean.

**Mehdia Beach** (30 km north) attracts surfers. The old Portuguese fort, the Borj el Kebir, overlooks the beach from the cliff above the Sebou river mouth. Less commercial than Temara.
