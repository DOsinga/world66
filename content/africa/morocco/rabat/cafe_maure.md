Inside the Kasbah des Oudaya, perched above the point where the Bou Regreg meets the Atlantic. Serves mint tea, coffee, and Moroccan pastries — no alcohol. The terrace is one of the best places to sit in Rabat: blue-painted walls, the estuary below, and on clear days a view to Salé across the water. Gets busy on weekend afternoons; arrive early for a terrace table.
