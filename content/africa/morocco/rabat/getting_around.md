---
title: "Getting Around"
type: section
---

**On foot** — The medina, Kasbah des Oudaya, Hassan Tower, and the Ville Nouvelle are all walkable from each other. This compact core takes about 30 minutes to cross end to end.

**Petit taxis** — Blue petit taxis go anywhere in the city. They are metered (insist on the meter, or fix a price first). A ride across town should cost 20–40 dirhams. Chellah and Agdal are a short taxi ride from the centre.

**Tram** — Rabat's modern tramway runs through the Ville Nouvelle and connects the city to Salé across the Bou Regreg. Cheap and reliable; buy a ticket at the stop.

**To Salé** — Take the tram across the Hassan II Bridge, or take the old ferry from near the Kasbah des Oudaya. Grand taxis also cover the route; as always in Morocco, make sure you're sharing and not renting the whole vehicle.
