Across the Bou Regreg in Sale, the Marinid Madrasa of Abu al-Hasan is one of the most rewarding small monuments in the Rabat area. Built in the 14th century beside the Grand Mosque of Sale, it preserves the intimate scale and decorative precision of Marinid religious architecture.

The pleasure is in the details: carved cedar, zellige tilework, plaster bands, and the quiet courtyard geometry that turns a compact school into something elegant. It pairs naturally with a wander through Sale's medina, which feels more local and less polished than Rabat's main tourist circuit.
