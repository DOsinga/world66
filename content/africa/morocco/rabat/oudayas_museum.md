Inside the kasbah walls, this small museum occupies a beautifully restored 17th-century Alaouite palace. Displays cover traditional Moroccan dress, jewellery, musical instruments, and household objects from across the country. The building itself — a central courtyard with an ornamental pool, cedar-panelled rooms, and carved plasterwork — is as interesting as the collection. Entrance around 10 dirhams. The ticket also covers the adjoining Andalusian Garden.
