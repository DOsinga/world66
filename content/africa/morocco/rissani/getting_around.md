---
title: "Getting Around"
type: section
---

Rissani is small enough to walk in twenty minutes end to end, but the ksour and Sijilmassa are spread over a few kilometres of palm groves on the edge of town. The **circuit touristique** is signposted and can be driven, cycled, or walked; expect three to four hours on foot if you want to look at anything.

**Guides and taxis.** Petit taxis loiter around the souk gates and will run you out to the circuit for a negotiated fare (around 100 dirhams for a two-hour loop). A local guide — easily arranged at any guesthouse — is genuinely worth it: the buildings are not signposted, the context is half the experience, and a guide will get you into ksour you would not otherwise visit.

**Bike.** A handful of guesthouses rent bicycles, which is the most pleasant way to do the circuit in cool weather.

**Onward transport.** Shared *grand taxis* run from the souk to Erfoud, Merzouga, and Errachidia. Negotiate the fare before you get in.
