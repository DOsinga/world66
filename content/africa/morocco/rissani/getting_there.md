---
title: "Getting There"
type: section
---

Rissani is the end of the line — the last town of any size before the Algerian border and the Sahara. The road in from the north passes through **Errachidia** and **Erfoud** and follows the Ziz River through one of the great palmeries of Morocco. The scenery is the main reward; a straightforward drive becomes a half-day if you stop.

**By road.** The N13 from Errachidia is the main route in; the drive from Errachidia takes around an hour. From Marrakech the drive is long (around eight to nine hours) and crosses the High Atlas via the Tizi n'Tichka pass — a stunning route but not one to attempt at night. From Fez the route comes via Midelt and is similar in time.

**By bus.** CTM and Supratours run daily buses from Errachidia, Meknes, and Fez. The bus station is just north of the souk.

**By air.** The nearest airport is Errachidia (ERH), with limited domestic flights to Casablanca. Most visitors arrive overland from Fez or Marrakech.

**Onward.** From Rissani the road continues south to **Merzouga** and the dunes of Erg Chebbi (45 km), or west via the Drâa Valley to **Zagora** and **Ouarzazate** — a long, beautiful day's drive across the southern edge of the Atlas.
