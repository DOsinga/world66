---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

Rissani is on the edge of the Sahara and the climate is unforgiving for half the year. **October to April** is the only sensible window — daytime temperatures are warm but not punishing and the nights can be cold enough to want a fleece. The desert excursions south to **Merzouga** are at their best in November and February when the air is clearest.

**May to September** is genuinely difficult: daytime temperatures of 45°C and above are routine in midsummer and the wind off the Sahara turns hot and gritty. Most of the trans-Saharan tour groups pull out completely by mid-June.

The **date harvest** runs in October and is the most interesting time of year for the souk: trucks rattle in from the surrounding oases laden with crates of medjool dates and the auctions in the date market run all morning. The **Moussem of Moulay Ali Cherif** is held in late summer or early autumn (the date shifts with the Islamic calendar) and draws pilgrims from across Morocco; lodging in Rissani is impossible without an advance booking during the festival.
