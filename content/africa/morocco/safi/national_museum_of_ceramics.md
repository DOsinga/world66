Safi is Morocco's great pottery city, and the National Museum of Ceramics gives that craft a proper frame. Housed in the old Kechla fortress, the museum links the city's workshops to a wider Moroccan ceramic tradition of glazes, tiles, vessels, and decorative forms.

It is the natural companion to a walk up Colline des Potiers, where the craft is still made and sold. The museum adds context before or after the workshop lanes: why Safi's clay matters, how styles developed, and how everyday pots became one of the city's defining arts.
