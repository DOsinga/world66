Mnar Park is a family-oriented water park east of Tangier, near Cap Malabata. Its slides, pools, and open coastal views make it a simple half-day escape when the city feels hot and crowded.

It is best treated as a practical family stop, not a cultural sight. Check current opening times before heading out, especially outside the summer season.
