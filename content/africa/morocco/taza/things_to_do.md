---
title: Things to Do in Taza
type: section
---

Taza's sights are concentrated in **Taza-Haut**, the old medina on the rocky spur above the Ville Nouvelle. The most rewarding approach is to walk the [medina walls](/africa/morocco/taza/the_medina_walls) circuit, which takes in the main viewpoints — [Bab Er Rih](/africa/morocco/taza/bab_er_rih), [Bab Zitouna](/africa/morocco/taza/bab_zitouna), and [Tour Es-Sarragine](/africa/morocco/taza/tour_essarragine) — while giving a sense of the fortifications that made Taza strategically important for centuries.

The [Grande Mosquée](/africa/morocco/taza/grande_mosquee) at the heart of the medina is the most important historic building in Taza — one of the oldest Almohad mosques in Morocco, founded in 1135 — though it is not accessible to non-Muslims. The medina lanes around it, including the souk area and the artisan quarter, are worth exploring.

Outside the city, the [Friouato Caves](/africa/morocco/taza/friouato_caves) and [Djebel Tazzeka National Park](/africa/morocco/taza/djebel_tazzeka) about 20–30 kilometres south provide the main day-trip options.
