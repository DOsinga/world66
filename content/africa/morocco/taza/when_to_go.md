---
title: When to Go to Taza
type: section
---

Taza has a semi-arid climate tempered by its altitude — the medina sits at around 600 metres. Summers are hot (35°C in July) but more bearable than the coastal plains, and the evenings cool quickly. Winters are cold and occasionally wet, with snow visible on the Middle Atlas to the south.

**Spring (March–May)** is the most pleasant time: the valley below the medina is green from winter rains, temperatures are comfortable (15–25°C), and the countryside around Djebel Tazzeka is at its best for walking. **Autumn (September–October)** is also good and quieter.

**Summer** brings heat but the mornings are cool enough for medina exploration. The Friouato Caves maintain a constant cool temperature year-round and are especially welcome in July and August. **Winter** requires warm clothing in the medina; the Taza Gap is known for cold winds.

As a working town rather than a tourist destination, Taza is busy throughout the year with the rhythms of local commerce and life — there is no "tourist season."
