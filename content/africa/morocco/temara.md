Temara is a coastal city directly south of [Rabat](/africa/morocco/rabat), essentially a southern suburb of the capital. Its main draw for visitors is [Temara Beach](/africa/morocco/temara/temara_beach), a long stretch of Atlantic sand popular with Rabatis on summer weekends. The beach can get crowded but has a relaxed atmosphere and a string of cafes along the promenade.

The city has Almohad-era origins — Sultan Abd al-Mumin founded it in the 12th century — and the [old kasbah walls](/africa/morocco/temara/kasbah_walls) built by Moulay Ismail still stand. The [Rabat Zoo](/africa/morocco/temara/rabat_zoo), technically within Temara's limits, is notable for its Barbary lion breeding programme. Beyond these attractions, Temara is primarily residential and makes a convenient and cheaper alternative base to Rabat if you have a car.
