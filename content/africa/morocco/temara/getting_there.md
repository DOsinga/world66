title: Getting There

---

Temara is about 12 kilometres south of [Rabat](/africa/morocco/rabat) and is easily reached from the capital by shared taxi (petit taxi or grand taxi) from Rabat's central taxi stands. The journey takes 20-30 minutes outside rush hour. A train on the ONCF line between Rabat and Casablanca stops at Temara (Gare de Témara), though the station is not centrally located and a short taxi ride is needed to reach the beach.

By car, Temara is directly south on the main coast road. Parking near the beach is available but can fill up on summer weekends.
