Temara's main attraction is its Atlantic beach, which is quieter and less developed than the beaches near Casablanca. The old kasbah walls are worth a look for those interested in Morocco's medieval fortifications. The Rabat Zoo, technically within Temara's limits, is a significant attraction for families. The town is most usefully visited as a day trip from Rabat or as a base when accommodation in the capital is full or expensive.
