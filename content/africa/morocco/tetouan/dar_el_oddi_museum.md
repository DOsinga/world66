Dar El Oddi is a restored family house from the 1920s, reopened as a small museum after years of decay. Its permanent exhibition, "Visions of Tetouan", uses old images and documents to show the city's streets, landscapes, and urban life.

The house itself is part of the appeal, giving a quieter look at Tetouan's domestic architecture away from the busier gates and craft schools.
