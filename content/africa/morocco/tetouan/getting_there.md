title: Getting There
type: section
---

There is no train station in Tetouan. Grand taxis and CTM buses run frequently from the bus station south of the centre to [Tangier](/africa/morocco/tangier) (about an hour), [Chefchaouen](/africa/morocco/chefchaouen) (around 90 minutes), and [Ceuta](/africa/morocco/ceuta) at the Spanish border (45 minutes). Connecting through Ceuta is the most common arrival point for travellers coming from southern Spain.

Tetouan's small airport, Sania Ramel, lies northeast of town and handles a handful of seasonal flights, mostly to Spain. For most travellers, flying into Tangier and continuing by bus or grand taxi is more practical.
