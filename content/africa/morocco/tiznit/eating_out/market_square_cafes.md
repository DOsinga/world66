type: poi

The cafés and small restaurants around the main place serve cheap tagines, harira soup, brochettes, and bread to a largely local crowd. Prices are very low and the atmosphere is entirely unaffected by tourism. Good for a quick lunch while waiting for a bus or grand taxi. Most places open early and stay busy through the afternoon.
