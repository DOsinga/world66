---
title: "Getting There"
type: section
---

Tiznit has no train service. Regular buses and grands taxis run from Agadir (1.5–2 hours north), most departing from Inezgane bus station on the southern edge of Agadir. CTM and several other intercity bus companies pass through Tiznit on routes between Agadir and the south.

Buses and taxis also run to Tafraoute in the Anti-Atlas (2.5 hours east), Sidi Ifni on the coast (1.5 hours south), and Guelmim further south towards the Sahara. Grands taxis to surrounding towns depart from the market square.
