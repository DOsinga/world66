The ancient spring that gave Tiznit its reason to exist, the Source Bleue sits inside the medina and has been drawing water since before the town's walls were built. It is now a small plaza and café area rather than a working spring, but the spot retains a quiet, shaded character. A good place to rest mid-afternoon before continuing through the medina's lanes.
