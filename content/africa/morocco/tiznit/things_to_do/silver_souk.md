A covered market of silversmiths making and selling traditional Berber jewellery — fibulas, bracelets, anklets — using techniques passed down through generations. Tiznit has been Morocco's centre for Berber silver work for centuries, and the souk here offers some of the best prices in the country. You can watch craftsmen at work shaping and engraving the metal, and most stalls are open to straightforward bargaining without high-pressure selling.
