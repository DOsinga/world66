---
title: "Eating Out"
type: section
---

Beira's food is built on Mozambican basics — peri-peri prawns, *matapa* (cassava leaves cooked in coconut milk and peanuts), grilled fish, and *frango à zambeziana* (chicken in lemon, garlic, and chilli). The Portuguese inheritance shows in the bread, the coffee, and the lingering availability of decent grilled meat in older restaurants.

**Clube Náutico** by the seafront serves grilled prawns and chicken with sea views; reliable for an evening dinner. **Take Mar**, also near the beach, is the standard sundowner location — cold Manica beer, grilled fish, plastic furniture, often live music on weekends. **Restaurante Miramar** does proper Portuguese-style fish, including *bacalhau*, in a less informal setting. For something simple at lunchtime, the corner cafés around Praça da Independência serve *pãozinho* (small bread rolls) with cheese, ham, or grilled chouriço.

**Pirï pirï prawns** are Beira's signature dish. The sauce varies wildly between restaurants — some places are eye-watering, others build the heat slowly. Ask for "*médio*" if you want it manageable.
