---
title: "Getting Around"
type: section
---

Beira is flat, hot, and spread out. Distances within the city are larger than the map suggests; the old centre and the Macuti beach are several kilometres apart.

**Taxis** are the usual way of getting around. The cars are unmetered — agree the fare before you start. Short hops in the centre run 100–200 meticais; longer trips between centre and the Macuti area or the airport are 300–500. Drivers gather at the railway station, the airport, and outside hotels.

**Chapas** (shared minibuses) run all the main routes and cost a few meticais. They are crammed, fast, and standard for local people but uncomfortable with luggage. Useful for the journey down the Macuti beach road.

**Walking** is fine in the centre during cool hours; avoid in the heat of midday. Walking at night is not safe — take a taxi after dark, even for short distances.
