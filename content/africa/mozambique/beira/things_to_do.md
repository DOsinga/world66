title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Beira is not a city for tourist sights so much as a city to walk through. The colonial centre, the port, and the long beach all have their atmosphere. Half a day is enough to see the main things at an unhurried pace.

The [Cathedral of Our Lady of the Rosary](/africa/mozambique/beira/cathedral) is the city's anchoring monument, built partly from stones of the sixteenth-century Portuguese fort of São Caetano at Sofala. The [Beira Railway Station](/africa/mozambique/beira/railway_station) is a fine piece of late colonial architecture and still works as the terminus of the Beira-Bulawayo line. The [Macuti Lighthouse and Shipwreck](/africa/mozambique/beira/macuti_lighthouse) at the northern point is the city's photogenic icon — a rusting ship beached directly in front of a century-old lighthouse. The famously decaying [Grande Hotel](/africa/mozambique/beira/grande_hotel), once the largest in Africa and now home to several thousand squatters, is at the southern edge of town.
