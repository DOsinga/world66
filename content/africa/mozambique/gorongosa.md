Gorongosa National Park is one of Africa's great restoration stories. Wildlife was devastated during Mozambique's civil war, but the park has spent decades bringing back buffalo, elephant, lion, hippo, waterbuck, and the ecological balance that once made it famous.

The landscape is unusually varied. [Lake Urema](/africa/mozambique/gorongosa/lake_urema) sits in the Rift Valley floodplain, drawing waterbirds and hippos; palm savanna and fever-tree woodland spread around it; and [Mount Gorongosa](/africa/mozambique/gorongosa/mount_gorongosa) rises to the northwest, feeding the park's rivers. This mix of wetland, grassland, and mountain is what makes Gorongosa feel different from the drier safari country farther south.

The main base is [Chitengo](/africa/mozambique/gorongosa/chitengo), reached by road from [Beira](/africa/mozambique/beira). Gorongosa is not as slick as Kruger or the Serengeti, and that is part of the point: roads can be rough, sightings are earned, and the science and community work are as central to the experience as the game drives.
