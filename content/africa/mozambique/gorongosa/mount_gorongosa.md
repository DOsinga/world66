Mount Gorongosa rises northwest of the floodplain and changes the whole park. Its higher, wetter slopes support forest, coffee-growing communities, and streams that feed the lowland ecosystem.

Visits to the mountain are more community and landscape focused than classic safari. If available, a guided excursion gives a valuable counterpoint to the floodplain: cooler air, cultivated slopes, forest patches, and a clearer sense of how people and park boundaries meet.
