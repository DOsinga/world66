---
title: When to Go
type: section
---

The dry season runs from May to October -- this is the time to visit, with cool nights, blue skies, and reliable conditions for dhow and kayak trips. July and August are the busiest months at the lodges.

The wet season, December to April, brings heavy rain and the risk of cyclones; some operators close entirely during this period. Mosquitoes are present year-round; bring repellent and a good net even if the lodge provides one. The water is warm enough for swimming throughout the year.
