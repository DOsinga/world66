---
title: When to Go
type: section
---

The best time to visit Inhambane is April to November, when the weather is dry and warm. The cool dry season from May to August is ideal: temperatures are pleasant (22–27°C), humidity is low, and the rain is rare. October and November bring pre-rains humidity and heat.

December to March is the rainy season — heavy downpours, high humidity, and the risk of cyclones. Malaria risk is highest during the wet season; take prophylactics year-round. The whale shark season at nearby Tofo runs roughly October to March, which overlaps with the less pleasant weather. If whale sharks are the draw, plan for October or November when the season opens but before the worst of the rains.
