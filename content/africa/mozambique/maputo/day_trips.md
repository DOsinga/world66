Most travellers use Maputo as a launchpad rather than a destination in itself. The easiest escape is **Inhaca Island**, two hours across the bay by ferry, with good beaches and a marine reserve. South of the bay, **Ponta do Ouro** on the South African border is the country's main dolphin-diving and surfing village. North up the coast lie the long beaches around **Xai-Xai** and the larger town of **Inhambane**, gateway to the celebrated diving at Tofo.

Inland, the **Maputo Special Reserve** (formerly Maputo Elephant Reserve), about 80 km south, has a small but recovering population of forest elephants, antelope and good birding in the coastal dunes — newly accessible by paved road and reachable as a long day trip with a 4x4.
