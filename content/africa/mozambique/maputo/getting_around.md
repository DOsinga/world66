---
title: "Getting Around"
type: section
---

The central Baixa and the Polana quarter are walkable by day — flat enough, gridded for the most part, and pleasant under the jacarandas. After dark, take a taxi.

**Taxis** are everywhere but rarely metered; agree the fare before getting in. A trip across the centre is typically 200–400 meticais. **Bolt** works in Maputo and removes the haggling. The **chapas** (shared minibuses) are cheap and constant but require local knowledge — they run on fixed routes shouted by the conductor leaning out the door, and they go where they go.

The new **Maputo–Katembe bridge**, opened in 2018, finally connects the city to its southern shore by road; before then everyone took the ferry. Driving is on the **left**, as in South Africa.
