---
title: "Getting There"
type: section
---

**By air.** Maputo International Airport (MPM) sits 3 km north-west of the centre. LAM, South African Airways, TAP Portugal, Ethiopian, Kenya Airways, Qatar Airways and Turkish Airlines fly in. The most useful connections are the short hops from **Johannesburg** (around 50 minutes) and from Lisbon and Dubai for long-haul. A metered taxi to the centre costs around 600 meticais; agree the price before getting in if there is no meter.

**Overland from South Africa.** The Lebombo / Ressano Garcia border post on the N4 is the main road crossing — open 06:00 to 24:00. **Cheetah Express**, **Intercape**, **City to City** and other long-distance buses run daily from Johannesburg and Pretoria; the trip takes 8–10 hours including the border. The slower but more interesting alternative is the **Shosholoza Meyl** rail service from Johannesburg via Komatipoort, plus a local train onwards.

**Overland from Eswatini.** The Goba border is closer and quieter; minibus taxis run from Mbabane and Manzini.

**By boat.** The ferry to **Inhaca Island** leaves from the central catembe terminal twice a day in season.
