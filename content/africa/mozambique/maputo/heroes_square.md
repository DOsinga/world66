The **Praça dos Heróis** ("Heroes' Square") sits at the western end of Avenida Acordos de Lusaka, a large circular plaza dominated by a six-pointed star-shaped white marble structure. The shell encloses the **Mausoleum of the Heroes of the Nation**, where the remains of Mozambique's independence leaders lie behind a glass wall. The square is the formal setting for state ceremonies on independence day and other national holidays.

The crypt is not always open to walk-up visitors, but the exterior can be viewed at any time. The square is somewhat out of the way — a 4 km taxi ride from the centre — but it is worth combining with a visit to the **Aeroporto Internacional** monument or the nearby market.
