The **Museu Nacional de Arte** (MUSART) on Avenida Ho Chi Minh is the most rewarding museum in Maputo and the best single introduction to 20th-century Mozambican art. The collection is built around the country's two giants — **Malangatana Ngwenya** and **Naguib Elias Abdula** — and extends to a wider catalogue of painters and sculptors from the FRELIMO and post-independence period: Bertina Lopes, Roberto Chichorro, Idasse Tembe, and the makaranga sculptors who work in scrap metal.

The building itself is a modest converted warehouse, with whitewashed walls that let the colour of the paintings do the work. Allow an hour. The museum opens Tuesday to Friday 10:00–17:00 and Saturday 14:00–18:00; the entrance fee is small.
