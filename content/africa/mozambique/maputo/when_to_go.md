---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

Maputo sits just inside the tropics and has two seasons: a hot, humid wet summer from November to March, and a warm, dry winter from May to September. **April–September** is the most comfortable time to visit — daytime temperatures of 24–28°C, low humidity, almost no rain, and the bay calm enough for the ferry to Inhaca to run reliably. Avoid **January and February** if you can: this is cyclone season for southern Mozambique, and rainfall can paralyse the city's drainage for days.

The big cultural events worth timing a trip around are the **AZGO music festival** in late May, which brings African and international acts to Maputo over a long weekend, and the **DOCKANEMA** documentary film festival in September. The independence holiday on **25 June** is a good day to be in town — the city dresses up and bars stay open late.
