Fort São Lourenço sits on a small islet off Mozambique Island, low and lonely compared with the great fortress at the northern tip. It was part of the wider defensive system controlling the approaches to the harbour.

You see it best from the water or from the island's southern edge. If local boatmen are running trips, it makes a good short excursion, especially in calm weather when the channel is bright and clear.
