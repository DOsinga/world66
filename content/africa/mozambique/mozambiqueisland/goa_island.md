Goa Island lies offshore from Mozambique Island, a low coral islet with a lighthouse and a very different mood from the crowded historic town. It is the kind of excursion that makes sense when the sea is calm and you want open water after a day among stone walls.

Boat trips depend on local conditions and negotiation with island boatmen. Bring water, sun protection, and realistic expectations: this is a simple outing, not a packaged resort experience.
