The bridge from Lumbo is the island's modern lifeline, a narrow concrete approach that turns Mozambique Island from a boat-dependent outpost into a road destination. It is not beautiful in itself, but the crossing is memorable.

Arriving over the bridge gives the best first sense of the island's scale: long, low, and barely above the water. Pause on the mainland side if you can, because the view back toward the old town helps explain why this small strip of coral mattered so much.
