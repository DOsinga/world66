The Museum of Sacred Art is a small but worthwhile stop near the Palace of São Paulo. It gathers church objects, statues, vestments, and liturgical pieces from the island's long Catholic history.

The appeal is not volume but intimacy. After the scale of the fortress and palace, these smaller objects bring the island's religious life down to human size: processions, chapels, patron saints, and the everyday material culture of faith.
