The Palace of São Paulo was the governor's residence and later became the island's main museum complex. Its arcades, courtyards, and rooms of Indo-Portuguese furniture show the official face of the old colonial capital.

It is one of the best places to get context before wandering the streets. The building itself is as important as the collection: high ceilings, shaded galleries, and sea air moving through rooms that once governed a long stretch of the East African coast.
