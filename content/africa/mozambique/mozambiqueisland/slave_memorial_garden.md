  Mozambique Island was tied into Indian Ocean slave routes as well as trade in gold,
  ivory, cloth, and spices. The memorial is modest, but it points to a history that
  can be too easily hidden behind the romance of ruined colonial architecture.
---

The Slave Memorial Garden is a quiet, necessary counterweight to the island's forts and palaces. It asks you to remember that Mozambique Island's wealth was built not only on trade and administration, but also on coercion and human trafficking.

Do not expect a large museum-style interpretation. The value is in stopping, reading the island more critically, and then carrying that awareness back into the old streets.
