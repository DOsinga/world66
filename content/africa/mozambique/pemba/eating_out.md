---
title: "Eating Out"
type: section
---

Pemba eats fish. Grilled prawns, lobster and reef fish are everywhere along the Wimbi Beach strip; the best are simply seared with garlic, lemon and piri-piri. Down in the Baixa, simple Mozambican lunch spots serve matapa (cassava leaves with peanut and coconut), xima (maize porridge) and grilled chicken at a fraction of the beach prices.
