Quelimane is the capital of Zambezia province, sitting in the delta of the [Bons Sinais River](/africa/mozambique/quelimane/bons_sinais_river) near the Indian Ocean coast. It's a working port city rather than a tourist destination — most travellers pass through on the way between Beira and Nampula.

The city has a relaxed, slightly faded feel, with some attractive Portuguese-era architecture including the [Old Cathedral](/africa/mozambique/quelimane/old_cathedral) — built in the 1770s and one of the oldest churches in Mozambique. The [Mercado Municipal](/africa/mozambique/quelimane/mercado_municipal) is the most vivid place in the city for an hour's walk, stacked with cassava, dried fish, and tropical produce. The surrounding area includes coconut plantations, mangrove channels, and [Zalala Beach](/africa/mozambique/quelimane/zalala_beach), about 30 km east of town on a dirt road — 7 km of empty Indian Ocean sand backed by palms and largely deserted on weekdays.

It's a useful stop for breaking up the long journey north, and the combination of colonial architecture, local market, river estuary, and a beach nearby gives it enough for two days if you're not rushing.
