---
title: "Getting There"
type: section
---

**By air** is the most practical approach. Quelimane Airport (UEL) has regular connections to Maputo on LAM Mozambique Airlines, and there are also connections via Beira. The airport is a short taxi ride from the city centre.

**By road**, Quelimane sits on the main north–south highway connecting Beira (450 km south) and Nampula (550 km north). The roads are paved but in variable condition, and long-distance travel by bus or chapa (shared minibus) is slow — allow a full day from Beira or Nampula. The Licungo River crossing north of the city has a ferry that can cause delays.

**By ferry**, boat connections along the coast were historically important but are now unreliable and not recommended as a primary transport option. The Zambezi delta geography makes road access indirect; the airport is almost always the better choice for travellers with limited time.
