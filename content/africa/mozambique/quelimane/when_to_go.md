---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

Quelimane has a tropical climate with a distinct wet and dry season. The dry season runs roughly from April to November — this is the best time to visit, with lower humidity, better road conditions to Zalala Beach, and clear weather. Temperatures are warm year-round, typically 25–30°C.

The wet season (December to March) brings heavy rain and high humidity. Roads deteriorate, and the dirt track to Zalala becomes difficult in a standard vehicle. Cyclone risk is real from January to March — the Mozambican coast has been hit by significant storms in recent years. Avoid planning travel during this period unless necessary.
