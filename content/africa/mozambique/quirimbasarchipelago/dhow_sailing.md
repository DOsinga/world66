The Quirimbas Archipelago is best explored by dhow — the traditional lateen-rigged sailing vessel that has been the working transport of this coast for a thousand years. Local fishermen and traders still use dhows to move between the islands, and several operators based on Ibo offer guided dhow excursions to the uninhabited islands and sandbanks of the southern archipelago.

A typical dhow day trip from Ibo visits two or three small islands, with snorkelling on the reef, a beach lunch, and a stop at a fishing village. Overnight dhow trips, sleeping on deck under the stars, are possible with advance arrangement from Ibo's lodges. The experience of sailing through the mangrove channels and across the turquoise lagoon in a traditional vessel, with the white sail above and the drone of wind and water — this is the Quirimbas at its best.
