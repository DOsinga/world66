---
title: Getting There
type: section
---

The gateway to the Quirimbas Archipelago is **[Pemba](/africa/mozambique/pemba)**, the provincial capital of Cabo Delgado, served by daily flights from Maputo (about 1 hour 40 minutes). LAM Mozambique operates regular services; check current schedules as frequencies change.

From Pemba, getting to the islands requires either a speedboat (1.5–3 hours to Ibo depending on sea conditions), a local dhow (half a day to a full day), or a charter flight (15–30 minutes). Most lodges on Ibo and the northern islands arrange transfers from Pemba airport. Individual travellers without a lodge booking can hire a speedboat from Pemba waterfront or arrange a dhow from the fishing village at Wimbi Beach.

**Security note**: The northern part of Cabo Delgado province has experienced a serious insurgency since 2017, particularly around Mocímboa da Praia and Macomia to the north. The Pemba and Ibo area has been less directly affected, but the security situation can change. Check current travel advisories from your government before booking.
