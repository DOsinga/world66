---
title: When to Go
type: section
---

The best time to visit is during the dry season, from **May to October**. June, July, and August offer the most reliable weather: low humidity, light winds from the south, and excellent diving visibility. This is also the period when **humpback whales** (July to October) congregate in the channels between the islands, and boat trips for whale watching are available from Ibo.

The wet season runs November to April. December and January are the hottest and most humid months; the cyclone season peaks between January and March and can make sea crossings uncomfortable or impossible. That said, **whale shark** aggregations occur November to March, and operators from Ibo run snorkelling trips with these animals when they are present.

**April and May** (the tail of the wet season) can offer good value: the rains have mostly finished by May, the vegetation is green, and lodge prices are lower than in the dry-season peak. The sea is typically calm by late April.
