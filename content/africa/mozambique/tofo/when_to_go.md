---
title: When to Go
type: section
---

Tofo has two main seasons, and the best time to visit depends entirely on what you want to see.

**For whale sharks:** October to March, peaking in November to February. The bay's seasonal plankton blooms are at their richest during these months, drawing the largest concentrations of whale sharks.

**For whale watching:** June to November, peaking in August to October, when humpbacks are migrating through the Mozambique Channel.

**For surfing:** May to August, when Indian Ocean groundswells are most consistent.

**For beach and general travel:** April to October is the dry season and the most comfortable for travel in the region. Temperatures are warm but not extreme, and the roads (often rough dirt) are at their most passable.

**Avoid December to February** if you're sensitive to heat and humidity. This is also the period when cyclones form in the Indian Ocean, and while Tofo is not always in their path, conditions can deteriorate rapidly. The Mozambique coast has been hit by serious cyclones in recent years; check forecasts carefully if travelling during this window.

**Peak season** for visitors is July to August (South African school holidays) and December to January. Accommodation fills up and prices rise. Book ahead.
