---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

The best time to visit Vilanculos is from April to November, when the weather is dry and clear and the Indian Ocean is at its calmest. This is also the season for the best snorkelling and diving visibility in the archipelago.

December to March is the wet season, and while temperatures remain warm, heavy rain and occasional cyclones can make the offshore waters rough and choppy. Some island lodges close entirely during this period. Water visibility for diving and snorkelling drops significantly after heavy rain.

June and July are the coolest months — pleasant for walking and exploring the town, though the water temperature drops enough that wetsuits are useful for longer snorkelling sessions. August and September are often considered the peak months, combining warm temperatures with calm seas and good visibility.
