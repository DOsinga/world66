Fischer's Pan sits at the eastern end of Etosha, near the Namutoni camp. It's a shallow seasonal pan — smaller and shallower than the main Etosha Pan — that fills with water in good rainy seasons. When it does, it becomes one of the best birdwatching spots in southern Africa.

In dry conditions, the pan's surrounding woodland is excellent for elephant, giraffe, and the dense zebra herds that characterise the eastern end of the park. The road around the pan is one of the most scenic drives in Etosha, with views across open water or shimmering salt in one direction and dense mopane in the other.
