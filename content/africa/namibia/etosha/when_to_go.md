---
title: When to Go
type: section
---

The dry season — May through October — is the best time to visit Etosha by far. As the waterholes become the only source of water for hundreds of kilometres, wildlife concentrations build to extraordinary levels. September and October are peak months: the vegetation is dry and open (so you can see further), temperatures are manageable (30°C range), and every waterhole is in use around the clock.

The wet season (November to April) is more complicated. Rains green up the landscape, animals disperse into the bush, and the roads in the western section can become impassable. However, summer brings migratory birds, possible flamingo sightings on the pan, and lower visitor numbers. The main rest camps remain open year-round.

Avoid July and August school holidays if you want the rest camps to yourself — these months are busy with South African and European visitors, and booking ahead is essential.
