Keetmanshoop is the practical capital of southern Namibia, a hot, dry road town where the B1 highway meets the routes toward Luderitz, the Kalahari, and the South African border. The town itself is modest, but it earns a place in the guide because the surrounding landscape is pure southern Namibia: quiver trees, black dolerite boulders, fossils, and long desert distances.

Most travellers use Keetmanshoop as a night stop between [Windhoek](/africa/namibia/windhoek), [Luderitz](/africa/namibia/luderitz), and Fish River Canyon. That can make it sound like a service town, but the nearby [Quiver Tree Forest](/africa/namibia/keetmanshoop/quiver_tree_forest) and [Giant's Playground](/africa/namibia/keetmanshoop/giants_playground) are genuinely worth time, especially in the low light of early morning or late afternoon.

The town also has one of Namibia's older stone mission churches, now used as the [Keetmanshoop Museum](/africa/namibia/keetmanshoop/keetmanshoop_museum). Do not expect a polished city break. Keetmanshoop is dusty, functional, and spread out, but it gives you a good introduction to the harsh beauty of the far south.
