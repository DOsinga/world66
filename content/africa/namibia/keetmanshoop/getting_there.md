title: Getting There
type: section
---

Keetmanshoop sits on Namibia's B1 highway, about 500km south of [Windhoek](/africa/namibia/windhoek) and roughly 340km north of the South African border at Noordoewer. The B4 branches west toward [Luderitz](/africa/namibia/luderitz), making the town a common overnight stop on southern Namibia road trips.

Long-distance buses and shuttles may stop here, but a rental car is the most useful way to visit the surrounding sights. Fuel up before leaving town; distances in the south are long and services thin out quickly.
