Keetmanshoop Museum occupies the old Rhenish Mission Church, a stone building from 1895 and one of the town's key historic landmarks. The displays cover local Nama history, early settlers, and the mission period.

It is a modest museum, but worth a short visit if you are staying in town. The building itself is the main draw, especially as a contrast to the hot, practical streets around it.
