---
title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Outjo's main function is as a service stop for Etosha-bound travellers, but the town itself and its surroundings offer a few worthwhile stops. The [Khorab Memorial](/africa/namibia/outjo/khorab_memorial) outside town marks the spot where Germany surrendered South West Africa in 1915. The [Outjo Museum](/africa/namibia/outjo/outjo_museum) tells the history of the colonial period in the region. The [Töpfers Konditorei](/africa/namibia/outjo/topfers_konditorei) bakery is famous across Namibia for its German pastries.

For wildlife, the [Waterberg Plateau National Park](/africa/namibia/outjo/waterberg_plateau) is about 120km south-east — a table mountain with a very different ecosystem from the Etosha plain, home to white rhinos, sable antelope, and roan antelope. It makes a worthwhile overnight detour if you have the time.
