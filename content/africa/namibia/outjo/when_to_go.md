---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

The best time to visit Outjo — and Etosha — is during the dry season, from May to October. This is when wildlife concentrates around waterholes (the vegetation is sparse so animals are easier to see), the roads are in good condition, and the days are warm but not oppressively hot. August through October is the hottest and driest part of the dry season; the waterholes are at their most productive for viewing.

The rainy season (November–April) brings green vegetation and spectacular thunderstorms, but some tracks become impassable and wildlife disperses across the park. Temperatures in Outjo in summer (December–February) can exceed 40°C. If visiting in the wet season, keep expectations for Etosha wildlife sightings modest; the experience is more about landscape and birdlife.

Night temperatures in the dry season can drop near zero in June and July at altitude; pack accordingly.
