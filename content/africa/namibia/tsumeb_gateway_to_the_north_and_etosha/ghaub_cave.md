Ghaub Cave is a guided cave visit on the historic Ghaub mission farm. The cave was rediscovered in 1913 by missionary Heinrich Vedder, and the wet limestone landscape around it is unusual for this part of Namibia.

The cave tour is active rather than polished: expect narrow sections, crawling or sliding in places, and a visit that suits travellers with decent fitness.
