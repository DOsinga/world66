---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

Namibia's north has a simple two-season climate. The **dry season** (May to October) is the best time to visit Tsumeb and Etosha. Rain is rare, days are warm and sunny, and the roads are in their best condition. Wildlife congregates around the Etosha waterholes in July and August, making this the peak game-viewing period.

The **wet season** (November to April) brings afternoon thunderstorms, mainly heavy and short-lived. Temperatures rise and the landscape turns green. Etosha is less visited — animals scatter from the waterholes — but the birdlife is exceptional. Roads in Tsumeb itself remain fine, but some farm tracks become impassable.

There is no compelling reason to avoid Tsumeb at any particular time of year: it is a transit town first and foremost. If you are timing an Etosha visit, aim for June through September for the best wildlife viewing.
