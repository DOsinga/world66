---
title: "Eating Out"
type: section
---

Walvis Bay's restaurant scene is more pragmatic than Swakopmund's just up the coast, but the few good places use the same superb cold-water seafood — fresh oysters from the Lüderitz farms, kingklip, hake, calamari — and the few standouts are worth the trip.

**The Raft** (on stilts over the lagoon) is the showcase restaurant, with the best views in town. **Anchors at the Jetty** and **Crazy Mama's** are good middle-range options. The waterfront promenade has a cluster of cheap takeaway places for fish and chips. Local craft beers from Camelthorn Brewery in Windhoek are widely available; the wines are mostly South African.
