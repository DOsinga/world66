---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

The Benguela current keeps Walvis Bay cool year-round — temperatures rarely rise above 25°C even in summer or drop below 10°C in winter. The cold Atlantic also generates heavy morning fog that usually burns off by midday but can occasionally linger and disrupt boat tours.

Birdwatching is good year-round but the lagoon is at its absolute peak from October to March when flamingos breed and migrant waders arrive in massive numbers. **Whales** (humpback and southern right) pass the coast between August and October. The desert is best avoided in midsummer afternoons (December to February) when the sand becomes unbearably hot; otherwise, all seasons are pleasant. The wind picks up most afternoons, sometimes uncomfortably, especially September to November.
