---
title: 'Shopping'
type: section
---

Windhoek is the best place in Namibia to pick up crafts and souvenirs — the selection is wider than at any of the smaller towns, and prices are usually fairer than at lodge gift shops in the desert.

**Post Street Mall**, the pedestrianised stretch in the centre, has a long open-air craft market with wood carvings, beadwork, ostrich-egg jewelry, and printed cloth. Bargaining is expected; assume around 60-70 percent of the asking price.

**Namibia Craft Centre**, in the Old Breweries on Tal Street, gathers around 40 small producers under one roof — basketry from the Caprivi, San bushman crafts, contemporary jewelry, photography. Prices are fixed but quality is higher than the street markets. Excellent for serious gifts.

**Penduka**, on the edge of Goreangab Dam in Katutura, is a women's co-operative selling embroidery, baskets, and pottery. The setting is pleasant and the project trains and employs disadvantaged women from the township. A short taxi ride from the centre.

For everyday shopping, the **Maerua Mall** and the **Wernhil Park Mall** are full-service air-conditioned shopping centres with supermarkets and chain stores — useful for stocking up before a long road trip.
