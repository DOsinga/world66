---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

The Sahara climate dictates everything. From November to February, temperatures are tolerable — daytime highs around 25–30°C, nights that can be genuinely cold, especially in the desert beyond town. This is the window for any serious overland travel northward.

From March onward the heat builds rapidly. By May, June, and July, daytime temperatures exceed 45°C and crossing the Sahara becomes a serious endurance exercise. The harmattan wind blows fine dust through everything in the early months of the year. There is essentially no rain.

If you are transiting through, aim for November to February. If you are waiting for a border opening or vehicle repairs, prepare for whatever the season brings — there is no good time to be stuck in Arlit.
