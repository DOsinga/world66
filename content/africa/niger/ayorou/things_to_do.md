Ayorou is about the river rather than monuments. Time a visit for the [Sunday market](/africa/niger/ayorou/sunday_market), then take a [pirogue out to the islands and hippo channels](/africa/niger/ayorou/hippo_pirogue_trip) if local conditions are good. The [riverfront](/africa/niger/ayorou/niger_riverfront) is the best place to understand how the town works.
