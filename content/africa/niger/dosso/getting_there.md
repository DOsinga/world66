---
title: Getting There
type: section
---

Dosso sits on Niger's main paved east-west road. Buses, minibuses, and bush taxis link it with [Niamey](/africa/niger/niamey), about two hours west in good conditions, and continue east toward [Maradi](/africa/niger/maradi) and [Zinder](/africa/niger/zinder).

Services leave when full rather than on a strict timetable. If you are using Dosso as a stopover, arrive in daylight and confirm onward transport before settling in.
