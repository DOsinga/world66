Maradi is Niger's great Hausa trading city, a hot, dusty, energetic place close to the Nigerian border and tied by commerce to [Kano](/africa/nigeria/kano), Katsina, and [Zinder](/africa/niger/zinder). It is the second-largest city in Niger, but it feels less like an administrative capital than a market organism: trucks, livestock, grain, textiles, and cross-border traders all feeding the same daily rhythm.

The city has deep historical roots. After the Fulani jihad changed the balance of power in Hausaland, Maradi became associated with the Katsina ruling line in exile, and that traditional identity still gives the city a certain pride. You will not find many formal tourist sights, but the markets, mosques, and Hausa street life make it one of the more interesting stops on Niger's southern corridor.

Maradi works best for travellers who are already crossing Niger by road. Spend a morning at the [Kasouwa Matta](/africa/niger/maradi/kasouwa_matta), look around the central market quarter, and use the city as a practical base before continuing west to [Dosso](/africa/niger/dosso) and [Niamey](/africa/niger/niamey) or east to [Zinder](/africa/niger/zinder).
