---
title: Getting There
type: section
---

Maradi is on Niger's main paved southern highway, with regular buses and bush taxis to [Zinder](/africa/niger/zinder), [Dosso](/africa/niger/dosso), and [Niamey](/africa/niger/niamey). Road travel is long but straightforward by Nigerien standards.

The Nigerian border is close, and Maradi has strong commercial links with Katsina and Kano. Check current border and security conditions before planning cross-border travel; this part of the Sahel changes quickly.
