Maradi's Grand Marché fills a walled two-block area in the centre of town and stays busy through the week. Friday is the liveliest day, when traders and shoppers from the wider region crowd the gates, stalls, and lanes; come early, keep small CFA notes handy, and expect the market to feel practical rather than staged.
