The Grand Marché of Niamey is the largest market in Niger and the commercial heart of the capital. It occupies a multi-storey building in the city centre but spills out onto the surrounding streets, which are lined with vendors, traders, and the incessant movement of goods. The market covers a wide range of products — imported Chinese goods, local crafts, spices, kola nuts, textiles, and silver jewellery — and navigating it takes patience and a willingness to get turned around.

The textile and fabric sections are particularly good for wax-print cloth and indigo-dyed material sold by the yard. The produce sections are a reliable place to observe daily Niamey life, with Hausa, Zarma, Tuareg, and Fulani vendors all working alongside each other. Go in the morning when it is cooler and the market is at its most active. Watch your pockets, especially near the entrances where crowds are thickest.
