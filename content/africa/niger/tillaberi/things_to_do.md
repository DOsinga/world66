---
title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Tillaberi does not have a long list of conventional sights. The reasons to stop are the weekly market, the possibility of seeing giraffes on the river banks, and a general atmosphere of unhurried Sahelian life along the Niger River. It is a transit town for those making the long journey between Niamey and Gao, and the majority of visitors are passing through rather than staying.
