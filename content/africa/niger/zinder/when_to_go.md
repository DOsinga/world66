---
title: When to Go
type: section
---

Zinder sits in the Sahel — semi-arid, hot, and seasonal. The best time to visit is the dry season, from October to March, when temperatures are more bearable and roads are reliable. November and December are the most pleasant months: cool nights, clear skies, and manageable daytime heat (25–30°C). The harmattan wind blows from the northeast in December and January, carrying dust from the Sahara that can reduce visibility and coat everything in a fine grit.

The rainy season runs from June to September. Temperatures drop slightly but humidity rises, and roads — particularly the track north toward Agadez — can become impassable after heavy rain. The landscape turns briefly green during this period, and the market activity picks up as agricultural produce comes in from the surrounding region.

Avoid visiting in March to May, when the dry heat peaks and temperatures regularly exceed 40°C. This is the most gruelling period, and not ideal for walking the old quarters.
