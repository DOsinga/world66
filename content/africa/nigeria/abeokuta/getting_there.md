---
title: "Getting There"
type: section
---

Abeokuta is about 100 km north of [Lagos](/africa/nigeria/lagos), an easy 2–3 hour drive on the Lagos-Abeokuta Expressway (longer in Lagos rush-hour traffic). The road is mostly fine although patchy in places and best driven during daylight. Hired cars and drivers are widely available in Lagos; for solo travel, intercity minibus services depart from the Mile 2 motor park.

The Lagos-Ibadan **standard-gauge railway**, opened in 2021, has a station at Abeokuta and offers comfortable air-conditioned service from Lagos in around two hours. It is the most pleasant way to make the trip and avoids the worst of the expressway traffic. **Murtala Muhammed International Airport** in Lagos is the nearest air gateway, about 90 minutes from Abeokuta by road or rail.
