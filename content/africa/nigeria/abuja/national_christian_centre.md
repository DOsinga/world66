The National Christian Centre occupies a prominent site in the Central Business District of Abuja, not far from Eagle Square. A non-denominational church intended to serve the whole of Nigeria's Christian community, it was built as a national institution alongside the National Mosque — the two together were a deliberate statement about Nigeria's identity as a multi-faith state.

The building is architecturally striking — a modern structure with a large auditorium and distinctive copper-coloured dome. Visitors are generally welcome to look around outside services; the grounds are well-maintained and give a good view of the church's scale. Inside, the main sanctuary seats several thousand and is used for major state occasions and national religious events. Worth a brief stop if you're already in the Central District.
