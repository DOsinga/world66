---
title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Abuja is light on traditional tourist sights — it's a planned city, not an ancient one. The main draws are the dramatic landscape, a handful of cultural institutions, and easy access to [Zuma Rock](/africa/nigeria/abuja/zuma_rock) outside the city. [Eagle Square](/africa/nigeria/abuja/eagle_square), the national ceremonial ground, is worth a look if you're nearby, and the [Nigerian National Mosque](/africa/nigeria/abuja/nigerian_national_mosque) and [National Christian Centre](/africa/nigeria/abuja/national_christian_centre) together make a good half-morning tour of the Central District.

The [Aso Rock](/africa/nigeria/abuja/aso_rock) monolith looms over the Presidential Villa and is the defining feature of Abuja's skyline. You can't access it directly, but it's hard to avoid. For a more grounded experience of the city, head to [Wuse Market](/africa/nigeria/abuja/wuse_market) — the busiest market in the capital and the place to eat proper Nigerian food.

The [Millennium Tower](/africa/nigeria/abuja/millennium_tower) project in the Central District is an ongoing curiosity — a grand prestige building that has been under construction for years with no clear end date. The [Abuja Arts and Culture Centre](/africa/nigeria/abuja/abuja_arts_council) has rotating exhibitions of Nigerian art when it's open. [Maitama](/africa/nigeria/abuja/maitama) is the diplomatic district, pleasant for an evening meal if your budget allows.
