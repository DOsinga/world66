Thought Pyramid Art Centre is one of Abuja's better places to see contemporary Nigerian art in a focused gallery setting. Exhibitions change, but the centre is known for showing painting, sculpture, photography, and mixed-media work from established and emerging artists.

It is a useful stop because Abuja can feel more administrative than cultural at first glance. A visit here gives the city some creative texture and pairs well with other central sights such as the National Mosque and Eagle Square.
