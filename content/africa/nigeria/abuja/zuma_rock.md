Zuma Rock is an enormous granite monolith about 80 km north of Abuja on the main road to Kaduna (the A2). The rock rises roughly 300 metres above the surrounding plain and is virtually impossible to miss if you're driving north — it appears suddenly at a bend in the road and dominates the landscape for miles around.

You can stop at the base for photographs. There's no formal entrance or visitor infrastructure — a small village and several souvenir stalls at the roadside are about all you'll find. The rock itself is not climbable in the traditional sense, and local communities request that visitors treat the site respectfully. The drive from Abuja takes about 90 minutes. It makes a natural stop if you're already heading toward Kaduna, or a worthwhile half-day excursion on its own.
