Igbo Olodumare is a forest area near Akure, known locally for its thick evergreen vegetation and quiet picnic setting. It is the kind of place to visit for a short nature break rather than a formal attraction with heavy infrastructure.

Go with local directions and realistic expectations: the appeal is the forest atmosphere, shade, and space away from the city.
