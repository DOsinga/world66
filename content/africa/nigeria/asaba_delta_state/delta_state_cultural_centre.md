The Delta State Cultural Centre in Asaba houses exhibits on the arts, crafts, and traditions of Delta State's many ethnic communities, along with a performance space used for government events and cultural shows. The collection includes traditional costumes, musical instruments, carved objects, and photographs documenting the state's communities and festivals.

It is a modest institution by international standards but a worthwhile stop for anyone wanting context on the region's cultures before exploring further. Check what events or performances are scheduled — the centre occasionally hosts traditional music and dance groups that are far more engaging than the static displays. The centre is typically open on weekdays during business hours; entry fees are minimal.
