The Emotan Statue stands at the heart of the old market area, on the spot where the real Emotan is said to have sold her goods in the 15th century. Emotan was a market trader who repeatedly warned the Edo king, Oba Ewuare the Great, of plots against his life and safety. She became a figure of loyalty, courage, and protection — the city's patron saint in all but name.

The statue is Benin City's most recognized piece of public art and one of its most historically loaded. It represents not just Emotan but the deep cultural memory of the Edo people. The surrounding market area is busy and atmospheric, a good place to experience the city at its most alive. If you visit during a ceremony, you may witness the formal homage paid here before proceedings begin elsewhere.
