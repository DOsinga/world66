The Holy Aruosa Cathedral is a remarkable institution that reflects the Edo people's capacity to absorb and reshape outside influences. The Aruosa church blends Christian worship with traditional Edo spiritual practices in a way that is neither syncretism nor compromise — it is its own thing, deeply rooted in the Benin royal tradition.

The cathedral's origins are linked to Portuguese contact in the 15th century, and it maintains practices and objects — including royal regalia — that connect it directly to the old Benin court. Visiting requires sensitivity and appropriate dress; this is an active place of worship, not a tourist site. But for anyone interested in the deep currents of Edo culture, it is one of the most interesting stops in the city. Ask locally about the protocols for visitors.
