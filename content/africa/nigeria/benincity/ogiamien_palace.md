Ogiamien Palace is a small but meaningful stop in Benin City, valued less for spectacle than for continuity. Local tradition links the palace with one of the few structures to survive the British punitive expedition of 1897, making it a counterpoint to the better-known royal palace and museum collections.

Visitor access can be informal, so ask locally and be respectful if the compound is in use. For travellers interested in Benin history, it adds another layer to the story of a city often reduced to bronzes and conquest.
