Finima Beach is Bonny's main Atlantic shore. It is a broad, open beach used for recreation and events, and it gives a very different feel from the enclosed creeks and industrial edges seen on the way in.

Visit with local advice on tides, access, and safety. The beach is best treated as part of a Bonny day with Finima Nature Park rather than a stand-alone resort stop.
