---
title: When to Go
type: section
---

Calabar's climate is tropical — hot and humid year-round, with two rainy seasons (April–June and September–November). The dry season from November to March is generally the most comfortable time to visit, and December is peak season thanks to the Calabar Carnival.

If you are visiting specifically for the carnival, book everything — flights, hotels, everything — months in advance. The city's accommodation is completely overwhelmed during the festival fortnight in December. If you want to visit Cross River National Park, the dry season (November–February) is best for trails and wildlife movement, when the forest floor is accessible and the rivers are lower.
