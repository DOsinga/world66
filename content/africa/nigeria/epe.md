Epe is an Ijebu-Yoruba town on the north side of Lekki Lagoon, far enough from central [Lagos](/africa/nigeria/lagos) to feel like a different Lagos State. Its economy has long turned on fishing, farming, and trade, and the lagoon is the main fact of local life.

For travellers, Epe is more of a local stop than a polished destination. It gives a look at the eastern lagoon belt, where fish markets, waterside traffic, and Ijebu culture sit outside the familiar Lagos Island and Lekki story.

Come with modest expectations and local guidance. Epe is useful if you are exploring beyond the city or tracing the lagoon communities east of Lagos, but it is not a place to build a first Nigeria itinerary around.
