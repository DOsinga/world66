Ado Awaye is a strong day trip from Ibadan for travellers who want landscape, exertion, and local belief in the same outing. The climb leads through rocky hills to Iyake Lake, often described as one of Africa's rare suspended lakes because it sits high on the mountain rather than in a valley.

The route is more than a viewpoint walk. Shrines, caves, boulders, and stories tied to the old Oyo world give the place a cultural weight that a simple hiking trail would not have. Go with a local guide, start early, and bring more water than you think you need; the heat on the exposed rock can be punishing.
