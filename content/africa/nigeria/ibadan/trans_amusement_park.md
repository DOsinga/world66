Trans-Amusement Park is Ibadan's main family amusement park, with rides such as bumper cars, a Ferris wheel, merry-go-rounds, flying chairs, and other fairground attractions.

It is aimed mostly at families and younger visitors rather than sightseers, but it gives children a clear change of pace from Ibadan's museums, markets, and historic landmarks.
