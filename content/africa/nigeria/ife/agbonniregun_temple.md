Agbonniregun Temple is a shrine associated with Orunmila, the Yoruba orisha of wisdom, knowledge, and divination. In Ile-Ife, where sacred groves, royal sites, and origin traditions sit close together, it belongs to the living religious landscape rather than to the museum world.

Visitors should approach with respect and local guidance. This is not a polished attraction with interpretive panels; it is a sacred place where permission, dress, and behaviour matter. Its interest is strongest for travellers already trying to understand Ile-Ife's role in Yoruba cosmology.
