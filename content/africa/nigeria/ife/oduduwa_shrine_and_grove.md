The Oduduwa Shrine and Grove is one of Ile-Ife's important Yoruba sacred sites, associated with Oduduwa, the legendary ancestor of the Yoruba people. It is a meaningful stop for visitors interested in the city's religious traditions and its role in Yoruba history.
