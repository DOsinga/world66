Ori Olokun points visitors toward one of Ile-Ife's great cultural claims: the astonishing naturalism of its ancient metal heads. The name is associated with the famous Ife head tradition and with debates over excavation, removal, and the meaning of Yoruba royal art.

Treat it as a companion stop to the Ife National Museum rather than a substitute for it. The museum gives the stronger context, but seeing Ori Olokun in town helps connect the art to Ile-Ife's living identity as a sacred and royal centre.
