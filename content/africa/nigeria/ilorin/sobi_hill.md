Sobi Hill is the most visible natural landmark near Ilorin, a rocky outcrop rising above the flat savanna north of the city. The climb is straightforward — a well-worn path leads to the summit — and the view from the top extends across the red-earthed landscape of Kwara State.

The hill has some significance in local Yoruba tradition as a landmark associated with the pre-emirate history of the area. It is a popular spot for an early morning or late afternoon walk among residents seeking some outdoor space, and it provides one of the few panoramic views available near this flat-lying city.
