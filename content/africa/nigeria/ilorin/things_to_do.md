---
title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Ilorin's main attractions cluster around its traditional and Islamic heritage — the Emir's palace complex, the old mosques, and the rhythms of the central market. Sobi Hill to the north offers views over the city and some green space. The University of Ilorin campus provides another contrast, a modern institution that has shaped the city's intellectual life since the 1970s.
