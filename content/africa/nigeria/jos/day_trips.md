---
title: "Day Trips"
type: section
---

The Jos Plateau is one of the most distinctive landscapes in Nigeria, and the day trips are part of the reason to come. **Riyom Rock** — the stacked granite stones once on the 100-naira note — is the obvious one, an easy half-day to the south. **Assop Falls** on the Abuja road and **Kurra Falls** to the south-east are both good half- or full-day outings, best in or just after the rainy season.

Longer trips include **Wase Rock**, the dramatic volcanic plug in the south-east of the state (a full day, with attention to security), and the **Pandam Wildlife Park**, a less-visited reserve in the lowlands south of the plateau. The old tin-mining country south of Jos — Bukuru, Barkin Ladi, Vom — is interesting in its own right for the post-industrial landscape. As always in Plateau State, take advice on the current security situation before any longer outing.
