---
title: "Getting There"
type: section
---

**Yakubu Gowon Airport (JOS)** is a 30-minute drive south of the city, served by domestic flights from Lagos and Abuja. Frequencies vary — at times there have been daily Air Peace or Aero Contractors flights, at others the schedule has been thinner. Check current schedules close to the trip.

By road, **Abuja is around four hours** west via the A237 — the most common overland route for travellers. Taxis and shared minibuses run the route, but most visitors prefer to hire a car with a driver. **Kaduna** is around five hours north. Long-distance buses connect Jos to most major Nigerian cities, but the road network is variable; for any longer overland trip take advice on current conditions before you set out.
