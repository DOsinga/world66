Jos Museum and Zoo is part of the broader Jos Museum Complex, with collections covering Nigerian history, crafts, archaeology, and older vehicles, plus family-oriented animal exhibits. The museum side is the stronger reason to visit, especially for travellers interested in Plateau State and Nigeria's cultural history.

Expect a modest, locally run experience rather than a polished national museum. It pairs well with the nearby traditional architecture displays in the same museum area.
