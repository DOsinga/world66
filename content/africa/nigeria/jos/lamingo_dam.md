Lamingo Dam is a small reservoir east of Jos, tied to the area's long history of tin mining. Local accounts connect it to old mining pits and underground water channels that were later shaped into a dam.

It is a modest sight, but the setting gives a glimpse of how mining changed the landscape around Jos. Go for the view and the local story rather than expecting a major attraction.
