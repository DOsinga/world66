The Lugard Footbridge, now located in the General Hassan Katsina Park along the Kaduna River, is one of the oldest iron structures in northern Nigeria. Built in 1904 and subsequently relocated to Kaduna from the original capital at Zungeru, it is an unassuming piece of colonial-era engineering — iron handrails, wire gauze, concrete pillars — that has survived more than a century and several changes of political purpose.

The park around the bridge, known as the River Gardens, makes for a pleasant afternoon. There is a swimming pool, benches along the riverbank, and a calm atmosphere that is a genuine contrast to the city's busier streets. The old footbridge adds a melancholy historical texture to what is otherwise a local leisure spot.
