The National Commission for Museums and Monuments in Kaduna has historical and archaeological material connected with Kaduna and northern Nigeria. It is a useful stop for context before visiting the city's older civic and religious landmarks.

Expect a local museum rather than a polished national showcase, with the main value in its artefacts and regional history.
