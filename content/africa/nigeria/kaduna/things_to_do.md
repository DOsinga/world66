---
title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Kaduna is not a conventional tourist city, but it has a distinctive mix of colonial history, political significance, and living cultural complexity that rewards a day or two of exploration. The colonial-era buildings in the north of the city carry real weight — [Lugard Hall](/africa/nigeria/kaduna/lugard_hall) is one of the most significant legislative buildings in Nigerian history, and [Arewa House](/africa/nigeria/kaduna/arewa_house) is a serious museum of northern Nigerian political heritage. The [River Gardens and Lugard Footbridge](/africa/nigeria/kaduna/lugard_footbridge) along the Kaduna River make for a pleasant afternoon. The [Central Market](/africa/nigeria/kaduna/kaduna_central_market) is enormous and worth a morning. And if you have transport, [Kajuru Castle](/africa/nigeria/kaduna/kajuru_castle) in the hills south of the city is one of the more inexplicable things in Nigeria — a medieval German fortress, built in the 1980s, that is now a resort.
