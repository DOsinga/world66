Lagos is one of the fastest-growing cities in the world — some 21 million people now, give or take a few million nobody is sure about — and easily the most exhausting. It sprawls across islands and lagoons at the mouth of the Ogun River, a commercial engine that runs on traffic jams, generator fumes, Nollywood, and Afrobeats. You do not come here for ease. You come because it is the closest thing Africa has to a New York, and because the energy, music, food, and hustle of the place are unlike anywhere else.

Most visitors stay on [Victoria Island](/africa/nigeria/lagos/victoria_island) or [Ikoyi](/africa/nigeria/lagos/ikoyi), the gated green islands where the embassies and expat hotels are. [Lagos Island](/africa/nigeria/lagos/lagos_island), just across the bridge, is the old business and trading heart; **Marina** and **Broad Street** still have the old colonial buildings, though most visitors find the density and hustle too intense without a guide. [Lekki](/africa/nigeria/lagos/lekki), further east, is the newer upmarket strip.

The city is not really about sights. The [Nigerian National Museum](/africa/nigeria/lagos/national_museum) on Lagos Island has a decent collection of bronzes and masks. The [Nike Art Gallery](/africa/nigeria/lagos/nike_art_gallery) in Lekki is the best place to see contemporary Nigerian art. The [Lekki Conservation Centre](/africa/nigeria/lagos/lekki_conservation_centre) has a canopy walkway through swamp forest. [Bar Beach](/africa/nigeria/lagos/bar_beach) and the beaches east at [Tarkwa Bay](/africa/nigeria/lagos/tarkwa_bay) (ferry only) and [Lekki](/africa/nigeria/lagos/elegushi_beach) are the local escape on weekends.

What Lagos really does better than anywhere in Africa is nightlife and live music. [The Shrine](/africa/nigeria/lagos/new_afrika_shrine) in Ikeja, the venue run by Femi and Yeni Kuti in memory of their father Fela, is the single best club experience on the continent. The [Kalakuta Republic Museum](/africa/nigeria/lagos/kalakuta_museum) next door preserves Fela's last house. [Freedom Park](/africa/nigeria/lagos/freedom_park) on Lagos Island hosts regular concerts in a former colonial prison. Ask, go late, stay later.

A note on Lagos traffic: plan for hours on the road. A driver and decent air conditioning change the experience entirely.
