One of the largest open markets in West Africa, on Lagos Island near the central business district. Balogun is primarily a textile and clothing market: ankara print fabrics, lace, aso-oke weave, ready-made garments, and tailoring services occupy hundreds of stalls across a labyrinthine network of streets and covered passages. The noise, density, and pace are overwhelming for a first-time visitor — that's precisely what makes it extraordinary. Bring a local guide if you want to navigate it properly and bargain effectively.
