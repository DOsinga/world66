---
title: Bars and Cafes
type: section
---

Lagos nightlife is what the city is really about. Most of it happens on Victoria Island and in Lekki, with the live-music heart still in Ikeja around the [New Afrika Shrine](/africa/nigeria/lagos/new_afrika_shrine). Open-air bars like [Bottles](/africa/nigeria/lagos/bottles) on VI run late and loud; [Jazzhole](/africa/nigeria/lagos/jazzhole) in Ikoyi is the more civilised, jazz- and book-loving counterpart. Cafes are concentrated on VI and Ikoyi — [Cafe Neo](/africa/nigeria/lagos/cafe_neo) for coffee, [Terra Kulture](/africa/nigeria/lagos/terra_kulture) for an espresso between gallery and theatre, and a dozen bakeries scattered through the leafy streets. Ask the day before you arrive where the party is; the venues change quickly.
