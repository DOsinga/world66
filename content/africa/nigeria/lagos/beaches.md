title: Beaches
type: section
---

Lagos sits on a coastal sand-spit and a long Atlantic beach runs the entire south edge of the metropolitan area. [Bar Beach](/africa/nigeria/lagos/bar_beach) on Victoria Island used to be the centre of weekend beach life until coastal erosion narrowed it to a strip; the Eko Atlantic seawall now protects what's left. [Tarkwa Bay](/africa/nigeria/lagos/tarkwa_bay), accessible only by boat from the VI jetties, is the calm and sheltered alternative inside the harbour. East along the **Lekki Peninsula** the beaches go on for kilometres — [Elegushi](/africa/nigeria/lagos/elegushi_beach), **La Campagne Tropicana**, **Eleko** — each privately operated and charging a small entry fee, with music, suya, horse rides, and rough surf. The Atlantic surf along this whole coast is heavy and there are strong rip currents; swim only where lifeguards are stationed.
