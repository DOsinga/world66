Cafe Neo is the local coffee chain that finally gave Lagos a respectable cup — Ethiopian and Rwandan single-origin beans, espresso machines that work, and air-conditioned outposts on Victoria Island, Ikoyi, and Lekki. The Mosaic Mall branch on VI is the busiest, with a steady crowd of laptop workers, free wifi, and a passable pastry counter. A useful refuge from the heat and the traffic.
