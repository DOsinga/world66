---
title: Day Trips
type: section
---

The classic long day trip is [Badagry](/africa/nigeria/lagos/badagry), two hours west of the city — the small Atlantic port that was one of the largest slave-trade ports in West Africa for three centuries, with a chain of small museums and the **Point of No Return** on the beach where the slave ships loaded. Closer to home, [Lufasi Park](/africa/nigeria/lagos/lufasi_park) and **La Campagne Tropicana Beach Resort** on the Lekki-Epe Expressway make decent half-day escapes; and the wildlife and forest of the [Lekki Conservation Centre](/africa/nigeria/lagos/lekki_conservation_centre) is the obvious in-city green walk. For a longer trip, **Abeokuta** (two hours north) has the Olumo Rock and the Adire-cloth markets.
