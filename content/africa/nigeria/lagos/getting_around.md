---
title: Getting Around
type: section
---

Lagos traffic is the central fact of life in the city. Plan everything around it — leave early or leave very late.

**Cars and apps**: Uber and Bolt both operate citywide and are by far the most pleasant way to move around. **LagRide** is the city-backed alternative. Confirm the route and the rate before getting in. For longer trips, hiring a driver-and-car for the day is reasonable value.

**BRT** (Bus Rapid Transit): the dedicated red BRT buses on the **Ikorodu–TBS** corridor and the **Oshodi–Abule-Egba** corridor are clean, air-conditioned and surprisingly fast. They beat sitting in your own car. Pay with a Cowry Card.

**Ferries**: the network of public ferries across Lagos Lagoon (Lagos Ferry Service, LagFerry) connects the islands and the mainland — a useful alternative to the bridges at rush hour. The Marina–Apapa and CMS–Ikorodu lines are the main routes.

**Danfos and keke**: the yellow minibuses (*danfos*) and three-wheelers (*keke*) are everywhere, cheap, and chaotic. Useful for short distances if you know where you're going; otherwise stick to apps.

**Walking** is fine inside the gated areas of Victoria Island, Ikoyi, and Lekki; it is not really an option in the older parts of Lagos Island except with a guide.
