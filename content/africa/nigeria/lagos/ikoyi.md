Ikoyi is the quietest of the central islands — a leafy residential district built on land reclaimed in the colonial era, where many of the early 20th-century colonial bungalows still stand under big mango trees. Originally a whites-only neighbourhood, it is now home to many of the city's wealthier residents, embassies, schools, and the **Lagos Country Club**. The **Banana Island** annex on the east side is one of the most expensive pieces of real estate in Africa. For visitors, Ikoyi is mostly a place to walk in the cooler late afternoon, eat at a few of the better restaurants, and visit the **Kalakuta Republic Museum** in the original Fela Kuti house.
