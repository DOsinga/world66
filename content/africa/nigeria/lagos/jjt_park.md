JJT Park is a landscaped public park beside the Lagos State Secretariat in Alausa. It is named for three former Lagos governors: Mobolaji Johnson, Lateef Jakande, and Bola Tinubu.

The park is more polished than many open spaces in the city, with lawns, paths, seating, and monuments. It is a pleasant stop if you are already in Ikeja or Alausa, rather than a reason to cross Lagos on its own.
