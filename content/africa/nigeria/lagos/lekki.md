Lekki is the long, narrow peninsula that runs east from Victoria Island along the Atlantic coast — a sandbar that holds most of the city's new-money development, the **Lekki Conservation Centre**, the **Nike Art Gallery**, the **Lekki Market**, and a string of weekend beaches (Elegushi, La Campagne Tropicana). Traffic on the **Lekki-Epe Expressway** is famous — count on hours on a Friday evening — and on the new toll roads it is still the major link to the east. For visitors, the interesting things are mostly clustered around the first 10 km out from VI.
