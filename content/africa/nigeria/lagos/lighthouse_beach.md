Lighthouse Beach is named for the old lighthouse guarding the entrance to Lagos harbour. It sits along the same sandy coast as Tarkwa Bay, but feels more like a walk-and-explore beach than a tightly managed resort stop.

The lighthouse can be seen from the beach, and the shoreline walk between Tarkwa Bay and Lighthouse Beach is the main reason to come. Check local advice before setting out, as access and beach conditions around Lagos can change.
