Lighthouse Beach sits near the entrance to Lagos harbour and takes its name from the old lighthouse visible from the shore. The walk along the coast between Tarkwa Bay and Lighthouse Beach is the main appeal, mixing beach scenery with views of the harbour approaches.

Conditions can vary, so treat it as a coastal walk and beach stop rather than a polished resort beach. It pairs naturally with Tarkwa Bay for visitors already making the trip out to the waterfront.
