The Lekki Urban Forest and Animal Shelter Initiative (Lufasi) Park is a 20-hectare patch of conserved coastal forest about 30 km east of Victoria Island on the Lekki-Epe Expressway. It functions as a private wildlife sanctuary and an environmental-education centre — rescued monkeys, snakes, tortoises, a small bird collection, and a canopy walk through the trees. Quieter and less developed than the better-known Lekki Conservation Centre, with more sense of being in a real forest. A good half-day with children.
