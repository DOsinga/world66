The National Gallery of Modern Art in Lagos focuses on modern Nigerian art, including portraiture, sculpture, ceramics, and textile work. The collection draws on traditions from Yoruba, Hausa, Igbo, and other Nigerian cultures while showing how artists have adapted those traditions in contemporary forms.

It is a useful stop for travellers who want more than beach and market time in Lagos, especially if paired with the National Museum or one of the city's private galleries.
