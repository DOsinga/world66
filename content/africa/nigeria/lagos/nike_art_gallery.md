Founded by textile artist Nike Davies-Okundaye in Lekki, this private gallery holds over 8,000 works across five floors — paintings, textiles, traditional art, and sculpture from across Nigeria and West Africa. Entry is free. The top floor has a workshop where you can try traditional adire (indigo-resist dyeing) and weaving. The gallery café serves Nigerian food. Not a museum in the formal sense — it's a working studio and family home as much as a cultural institution, which makes it more interesting than the alternative.
