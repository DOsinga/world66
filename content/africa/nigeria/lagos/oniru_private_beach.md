Oniru Private Beach is one of the easier Atlantic beaches to reach from Victoria Island and central Lagos. It is a managed beach rather than a wild stretch of coast, but it gives visitors sand, surf, and open views toward the Eko Atlantic development without a long drive out of the city.

Expect crowds and a lively local beach scene, especially on weekends. The water can be rough, so the main appeal is the seaside setting, people-watching, and sunset rather than serious swimming.
