A arts complex on Victoria Island housing a theatre, gallery, language school, restaurant, and bookshop. Terra Kulture runs one of the most consistent performance programmes in Lagos — Yoruba, Hausa, and English language theatre, contemporary dance, and music. The gallery represents Nigerian artists. The restaurant serves traditional dishes from the country's main regional cuisines. It's the clearest example of what a sustained Nigerian arts institution looks like, and the weekend performances are often sold out.
