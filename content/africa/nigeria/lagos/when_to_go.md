---
title: When to Go
type: section
---

Lagos is hot all year — temperatures rarely below 22°C, rarely above 33°C — and the choice is really between dry and wet.

**Dry season (December to March)** is the most comfortable time to visit, though the *harmattan* haze from December into January reduces visibility and coats everything in a fine red Saharan dust. The cool, dry weeks of January and February are also when most of the big music and arts events happen — Felabration in October, the **Lagos Theatre Festival** in February, **Eko Atlantic Festival**, and the annual **Lagos Jazz Series**.

**Wet season (April to October)** brings heavy bursts of rain, especially in June and September, that flood the lower streets and slow the traffic further. The temperatures are slightly cooler.

**August is the lull** between the two rainy peaks and is often a surprisingly pleasant time to visit. Avoid arriving on a Friday afternoon if you can: the city seizes up at the start of the weekend.
