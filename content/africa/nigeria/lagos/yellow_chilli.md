One of the most reliable proper-Nigerian restaurants in Lagos: Yellow Chilli has been serving classic Nigerian cooking to expats, returnees, and politicians for over fifteen years. Order the jollof rice (the menu does it three ways — coastal, Yoruba, and party-style), goat-meat pepper soup, egusi with pounded yam, or the suya platter. The dining room is bright and air-conditioned, the service is brisk, and the food is the most consistent in town.
