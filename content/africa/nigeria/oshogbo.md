Oshogbo is the art capital of Nigeria. The city is filled with art studios that produce some of Africa's most inspiring works. The [Oja Oba market](/africa/nigeria/oshogbo/oja_oba_market) is a good place to shop for souvenirs, especially if you're into juju. The market is across the street from the Oba's palace.

The highlight of Oshogbo is the [Osun-Osogbo Sacred Grove](/africa/nigeria/oshogbo/osun_osogbo_sacred_grove), a UNESCO World Heritage Site on the outskirts of town. Austrian artist Suzanne Wenger spent decades restoring and creating monumental sculptures among the traditional Yoruba shrines here. The annual Osun festival in August draws huge crowds.

Oshogbo also has a vibrant contemporary art scene rooted in the Oshogbo Art Movement that emerged in the 1960s. The [Nike Art Gallery](/africa/nigeria/oshogbo/nike_art_gallery) is one of the finest collections of Yoruba traditional art in Nigeria, and the [Adunni Olorisha Trust](/africa/nigeria/oshogbo/adunni_olorisha_trust) continues Suzanne Wenger's legacy. The city sits in Osun State, about 80 km northeast of Ibadan.
