African Heritage Gallery is a small Oshogbo art stop with work by a noted local artist alongside other African art forms. It is not as substantial as the Nike Art Gallery, but it fits the city's wider reputation for Yoruba art, workshops, and sacred-grove culture.

Go if you are already spending time with Oshogbo's art scene and want another local collection rather than a formal museum experience.
