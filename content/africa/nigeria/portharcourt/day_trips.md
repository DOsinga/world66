---
title: Day Trips from Port Harcourt
type: section
---

The most accessible day trip from Port Harcourt is the ferry crossing to **Bonny Island**, roughly 40 minutes by speedboat from the Bonny Waterside. The island has an interesting history as one of the major slave-trading ports of West Africa in the eighteenth century, and it retains some colonial-era architecture alongside the massive LNG export facility that dominates modern Bonny. [Finima Nature Park](/africa/nigeria/portharcourt/finima_nature_park) on the island's southern tip offers guided nature walks and birdwatching.

**Okrika**, accessible by boat north along the Bonny River, is a historic Ijaw town built on islands in the delta. The journey through the waterways is the attraction as much as the destination itself — the landscape of creeks, mangroves, and stilt villages is striking. In Okrika, the church of St Peter is an early colonial-era building worth seeing.

**Obudu** in Cross River State is a longer drive — four to five hours on good roads — but the Obudu Mountain Resort sits at altitude in the Sankwala Mountains and offers a dramatically different climate from Port Harcourt's lowland heat. It is better as an overnight trip than a day trip.
