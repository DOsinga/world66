---
title: Shopping in Port Harcourt
type: section
---

Port Harcourt's shopping divides between the traditional open-air markets and the newer malls and supermarkets that have appeared as the city's middle class has grown. For visitors interested in Nigerian goods — fabrics, crafts, food — the markets are where to go.

[Mile 1 Market](/africa/nigeria/portharcourt/mile_1_market) is the city's largest and most comprehensive traditional market, spread across the city centre. It is intense and crowded but unbeatable for Ankara prints, traditional dress, spices, and fresh produce. Mile 3 Market nearby is smaller but less congested and has a good mix of food and household goods. [Oil Mill Market](/africa/nigeria/portharcourt/oil_mill_market) in the northern part of the city serves the Rumuokoro area and is less overwhelming for those new to Nigerian market shopping.

For a more controlled shopping experience, Aba Road and Rumuola Road have a string of supermarkets, electronics shops, and clothing retailers. Park N Shop, Choice Supermarket, and De Giant Supermarket are useful for provisions, imported goods, and anything you need but cannot find at the markets.
