The Waziri Jumaidu History and Culture Bureau is Sokoto's main local-history museum. Its collections focus on the Sokoto people, with cultural relics, historic objects, and displays that help explain the city beyond its markets, mosques, and palace.

The presentation is simple, but the museum is a useful stop if you want context for Sokoto's role as a northern Nigerian religious and cultural centre.
