The National War Museum is one of Nigeria's most important historical museums. It displays weapons, photographs, aircraft, tanks, and other military hardware from Nigerian military history, with the Civil War period at the centre of the visit.

The subject is painful and politically loaded, so come prepared to read critically rather than passively. The outdoor exhibits are striking, and the museum site is also associated with wartime Voice of Biafra radio transmissions.
