Falcorp Mangrove Park and Mini Zoo combines a small wildlife attraction with displays connected to the Warri Kingdom. Visitors can see the royal cemetery, the Royal Museum, the Erejuwa Gallery, and animal enclosures with fish, crocodiles, primates, and snakes.

The park also offers outdoor activities such as canoeing, fishing, and short walks, so it is most useful for families or travellers looking for a broad, low-key stop outside central Warri.
