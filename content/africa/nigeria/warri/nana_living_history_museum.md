Nana Living History Museum preserves the memory of Nana Olomu, the powerful Itsekiri merchant and political figure whose influence over palm oil trade made him one of the Niger Delta's most important 19th-century leaders. For travellers, it is a rare structured window into Delta history beyond oil, ports and contemporary commerce.

The museum is best approached with patience, as visitor infrastructure in this part of Nigeria can be uneven. Its value lies in the local story: trade networks, colonial pressure, Itsekiri authority and the way one household history opens onto the wider Niger Delta.
