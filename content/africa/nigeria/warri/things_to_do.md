---
title: "Things to Do in Warri"
type: section
---

Warri is not a conventional tourist city, but it has genuine character. The [Palace of the Olu of Warri](/africa/nigeria/warri/olu_palace) is the most historically significant landmark — the Itsekiri kingdom predates European contact. The [Delta Steel Complex](/africa/nigeria/warri/delta_steel_complex) at Ovwian Aladja gives a visceral picture of the oil-era industrial delta. [Warri Stadium](/africa/nigeria/warri/warri_stadium) hosts Warri Wolves; a home match is worth attending for the atmosphere. The [Efferun Market](/africa/nigeria/warri/efferun_market) is the city's most active daily market. The best day trip is [Abraka on the Ethiope River](/africa/nigeria/warri/ethiope_river), 30 km north, with natural spring-fed swimming.
