Wikki Warm Springs is the classic non-wildlife attraction inside Yankari, a clear spring-fed pool where the water stays pleasantly warm year-round. After the heat and dust of a game drive, slipping into the water under the trees feels like the reserve's great practical luxury.

It is popular with Nigerian visitors and can be lively, especially on weekends and holidays. Bring swimwear, keep an eye on belongings, and treat it as part of the Yankari experience rather than a separate excursion.
