The Zaria City Wall marks the scale and importance of old Zazzau, one of the historic Hausa city-states. Even where the walls survive only in fragments, they help visitors read Zaria as a fortified royal and trading centre rather than just a modern university city.

A visit is strongest with local guidance, since the remains are spread out and context matters. Pair the walls with the emirate landmarks and old city areas for a fuller sense of northern Nigerian history.
