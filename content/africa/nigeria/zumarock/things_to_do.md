---
title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Zuma Rock is essentially one big sight — the monolith itself. There are no formal hiking trails or visitor centre at the base, and serious climbing requires permits and a guide arranged through the local Gbagyi community, who consider parts of the rock sacred. Most travellers simply pull off the Abuja–Kaduna expressway at one of the viewpoints, take in the scale of the thing, and continue on. Local touts will offer to walk you partway up the lower slopes for a small fee.

A few smaller sights cluster around the rock and the road north from Abuja. Bring water — the savannah here is hot and dry for much of the year.
