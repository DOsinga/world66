Dolomieu Crater is the main summit crater of Piton de la Fournaise, Réunion's active shield volcano. The crater is about a kilometre across and far deeper than the smaller summit craters nearby, with sheer dark walls that show how much the volcano has changed since the crater first opened in 1791.

The classic visit is the marked hike across the Enclos Fouqué from Pas de Bellecombe. Conditions can change quickly, and the area may close during eruptions or dangerous gas conditions, but in clear weather the view into the crater is one of Réunion's great volcanic sights.
