Mascarin Botanical Garden sits above Saint-Leu in Colimaçons, where the air is cooler and the sea drops away below. The collections focus on Réunion and the wider Mascarene islands, with useful sections on endemic plants, old crops, palms, orchids, and dry-zone species.
