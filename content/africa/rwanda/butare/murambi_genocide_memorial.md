Murambi Genocide Memorial Centre is one of Rwanda's most difficult and important memorials. The site was a technical school where thousands of Tutsi civilians were killed in 1994, and its exhibitions confront the massacre directly, including preserved remains and rooms that many visitors find harrowing.

It is not a casual sightseeing stop, but it is essential for travellers trying to understand modern Rwanda beyond museums and landscapes. Go with time, respect the rules on photography and conduct, and expect the visit to stay with you.
