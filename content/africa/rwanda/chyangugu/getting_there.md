---
title: Getting There
type: section
---

**By air**: Kamembe International Airport (KME) is 3 km north of the town centre and has scheduled flights to Kigali. RwandAir operates this route and journey time is under an hour. The airport is small but reliable.

**By road from Kigali**: The journey takes 4–5 hours by bus or minibus. Several companies run daily services; the road via Butare (now Huye) passes through the tea-growing highlands and is scenic. The last section before Cyangugu descends steeply toward the lake.

**Via Nyungwe**: The road from Butare through Nyungwe National Park is spectacular but slow — allow extra time, especially in the wet season. Shared minibuses pass through the park en route to Cyangugu.

**From the DRC**: The border crossing from Bukavu is open during daylight hours. Visas for Rwanda are available on arrival; DRC visas must be arranged in advance.
