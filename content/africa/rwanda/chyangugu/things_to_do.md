---
title: Things to Do
type: section
---

Cyangugu rewards visitors with extraordinary natural settings — a rainforest that ranks among Africa's finest, a lake full of light and colour, a wetland teeming with hippos and crocodiles — all within an easy hour or two of each other. The main draw is [Nyungwe National Park](/africa/rwanda/nyungwe_national_park) to the east, but Rusizi National Park and Lake Kivu itself offer compelling reasons to linger in town.
