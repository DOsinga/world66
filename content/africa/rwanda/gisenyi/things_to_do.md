---

Gisenyi's main draw is its position — it is the departure point for gorilla trekking in [Volcanoes National Park](/africa/rwanda/volcanoesnationalpark), a short and spectacular drive away. The town itself offers lake swimming, kayaking, and easy access to the Congo Nile Trail. The cross-border view to the smoking cone of Nyiragongo adds a permanent dramatic backdrop.
