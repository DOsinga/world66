---
title: When to Go
type: section
---

Rwanda's western province has two dry seasons: the main dry season from June to September and a shorter one in January and February. These are the best times to trek in Volcanoes National Park — the forest trails are drier and the gorillas are more easily tracked. The rainy seasons (March to May, October to November) bring lush scenery and fewer tourists but muddier tracks and more difficult trekking.

The town itself is pleasant year-round — the lake moderates temperatures, keeping them between 18°C at night and 26°C during the day. The rains come in short afternoon showers rather than all-day downpours.
