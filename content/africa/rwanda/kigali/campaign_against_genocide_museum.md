The Campaign Against Genocide Museum in Kigali presents the military and political campaign that ended the 1994 genocide against the Tutsi. It is a more specific and strategic counterpart to the Kigali Genocide Memorial, focusing on the RPF campaign and the wider conflict context.

The subject is heavy, but the museum helps visitors understand why modern Rwanda's official memory is framed the way it is. It is best approached with time, patience, and sensitivity.
