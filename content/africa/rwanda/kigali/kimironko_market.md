Kimironko Market is Kigali's largest and most bustling market, a sprawling complex in the eastern part of the city that sells everything from fresh produce and fish to second-hand clothes and household goods. It is the main market for the city's residents and one of the best places to see the commercial engine of Kigali running at full speed.

The fabric and tailoring section is particularly good — you can buy printed Kanga cloth and have it made up by a tailor within a day or two. Prices are significantly lower than in the tourist-facing shops in Kiyovu, and a bit of patient negotiation is expected. The market is busiest on weekends and early mornings when the produce is freshest. The surrounding streets have cheap local restaurants serving brochettes and bean stews.
