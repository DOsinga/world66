Nyamirambo is Kigali's most vibrant and authentic neighbourhood — a dense, predominantly Muslim district in the western hills that feels different from the polished government quarters elsewhere in the city. The streets are narrow and alive: tailors work from open-fronted shops, mosques punctuate the skyline, and food vendors set up at dusk alongside bars and small restaurants. This is where Kigali eats cheaply and stays up late.

Walking through Nyamirambo with a guide from the Nyamirambo Women's Center is one of the best things you can do in Kigali. The centre runs neighbourhood walks that take in the market, the craft sellers, the historic Jewish neighbourhood nearby, and the rooftop views across the city. The evening walk ends with a meal prepared by the women's cooperative, which is excellent.
