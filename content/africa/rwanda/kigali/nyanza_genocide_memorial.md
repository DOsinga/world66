Nyanza Genocide Memorial marks the site of mass graves where thousands of Tutsi civilians were killed during the 1994 genocide after the UN guards protecting them were withdrawn. The memorial is a sober stop southeast of central Kigali and is best visited with time to reflect rather than as a quick photo stop.
