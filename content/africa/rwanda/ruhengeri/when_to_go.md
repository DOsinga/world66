---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

The dry seasons — June to September and December to February — are the best times for gorilla trekking and hiking. The trails are drier, views of the volcanoes are clearer, and the overall experience is more comfortable. The gorillas can be tracked year-round — they don't migrate — but the wet seasons (March-May and October-November) make the trails muddy and the hikes more demanding.

High season (June-September) sees the most visitors and the highest demand for gorilla permits, which must be booked well in advance through the Rwanda Development Board. Prices are higher in high season. The low season offers the same wildlife experience at somewhat lower costs and with less competition for popular accommodation.
