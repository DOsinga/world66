---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

São Tomé has a tropical climate with two dry seasons and two wet seasons. The main dry season (gravana) runs from June to September — the best time to visit, with lower humidity, clearer skies, and easier road conditions for exploring the interior. The shorter dry season (gravanito) falls in January and February.

The rainy seasons bring heavy downpours, particularly in the southern part of the island where the mountains force moisture from the prevailing winds. Even in the rainy season, mornings are often clear; rain typically arrives in the afternoons. Average temperatures stay between 24–30°C year-round at sea level, cooler in the interior highlands. The island is malaria-affected; take appropriate precautions regardless of when you travel.
