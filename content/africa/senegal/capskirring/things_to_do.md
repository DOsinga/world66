---
title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Cap Skirring's rhythm is slow and beach-focused. The main beach is the draw for most visitors, but pirogue trips into the mangrove bolongs and excursions to Diola villages add a cultural dimension that distinguishes a stay here from a generic resort. The Casamance region has its own distinct identity — linguistically, culturally, and historically separate from northern Senegal — and it is worth engaging with that rather than just lying on the sand.
