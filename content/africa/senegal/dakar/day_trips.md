Lac Retba — **Pink Lake** — is the obvious day trip, an hour's drive northeast, where a cyanobacterium turns the water a bubblegum pink in the dry season; salt collectors work the lake in wooden pirogues. The fishing village of Yoff at the northern tip of the peninsula has a Layène Sufi shrine and a working beach. Further out, Keur Moussa Monastery holds Sunday mass set to kora music — an unexpected and beautiful thing.

The [Île de Gorée](/africa/senegal/iledegoree) is barely a day trip — a twenty-minute ferry from the port — but it is the essential excursion from Dakar. The resort town of [Saly](/africa/senegal/saly) and the inland market town of [Thiès](/africa/senegal/thies) are both feasible if you have your own transport or a willing taxi driver.
