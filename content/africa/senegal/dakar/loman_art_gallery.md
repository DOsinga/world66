Loman Art Gallery is a personal, artist-led space rather than a formal museum. It shows contemporary work by Senegalese and other African artists, often in a relaxed setting that may also include a cafe, garden, or informal events depending on the season.

It is best for travellers already interested in Dakar's art scene, especially if they have seen the larger museums and want something more intimate. Check ahead before crossing town; small galleries can keep irregular hours between exhibitions.
