A few minutes by pirogue from the village of N'Gor in the north of the peninsula, N'Gor Island is the easiest day-trip swim from Dakar — a small, mostly residential island with two coves of bright sand. Local boatmen shuttle across from the beach as fast as they can fill the boats; the trip takes five minutes and costs a few hundred CFA each way.

The island is best known internationally for the right-hand reef break off its north side, immortalised in the 1966 surf film *The Endless Summer* and still rated one of the best waves in West Africa. The two beaches on the inland side are quiet, safe for swimming and have a couple of simple restaurants serving grilled fish. Bring cash; nothing is expensive.
