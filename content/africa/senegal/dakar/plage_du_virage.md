Plage du Virage is one of Dakar's practical city beaches, set on the northwestern coast where Atlantic swell, sand, and urban life meet. It is popular with local swimmers, surfers, and people escaping the heat without making a full trip out of town.

Conditions vary, and currents can be serious, so watch what locals are doing before entering the water. For travellers it works best as a short beach break paired with Les Almadies, Ngor, or other sights on the peninsula.
