title: Things to Do
type: section
---

Dakar's sights are spread around the peninsula — the old colonial centre around [Place de l'Indépendance](/africa/senegal/dakar/place_de_lindependance), the [Grande Mosquée](/africa/senegal/dakar/grande_mosquee) on the Medina side, the [African Renaissance Monument](/africa/senegal/dakar/african_renaissance_monument) looming above Ouakam, and the soul of it all, [Île de Gorée](/africa/senegal/iledegoree), half an hour away by ferry. The vast new [Museum of Black Civilisations](/africa/senegal/dakar/museum_of_black_civilisations) and the older [IFAN Museum](/africa/senegal/dakar/ifan_museum) on Place Soweto cover the African art and history side. For a sunset walk, head to the [Mosque of the Divinity](/africa/senegal/dakar/mosque_of_the_divinity) on the rocks at Ouakam, or watch the pirogues unload the catch at [Soumbédioune](/africa/senegal/dakar/soumbedioune) on the Corniche.
