Dedicated to the history and contemporary lives of Senegalese women, the Henriette-Bathily Museum displays photographs, clothing, household objects, and rotating exhibitions of work by women artists. The collection emphasises the everyday — cooking utensils, jewellery, ceremonial dress — and traces women's roles across different ethnic groups in Senegal.

It is a small, thoughtfully arranged museum that adds a quieter and more personal note to the heavier history elsewhere on the island.
