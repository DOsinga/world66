---
title: "Getting Around"
type: section
---

Within Saly, the resort is walkable for those staying in the main hotel zone. Tuk-tuks and horse-drawn carts operate along the main road as informal taxis and are cheap for short hops. For day trips to Mbour, Bandia, or Joal-Fadiouth, taxis can be hired for the day — agree a price in advance.

Car hire is available through hotels or local agencies if you want to explore the Petite Côte independently. Driving is straightforward on the main roads, though secondary tracks can be rough. Motorbike taxis also operate within Saly.
