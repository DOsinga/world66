Beach horse riding is one of Saly's most popular activities and one of the most enjoyable ways to experience the coast. Stables operate along the beach, offering rides ranging from a short guided canter to longer expeditions up the beach at sunset. The horses are calm and well-suited to riders of all levels.

The most popular option is the late-afternoon ride timed for sunset over the Atlantic. Several operators work directly on the beach — you'll be approached by handlers near the main hotel zone. Prices are negotiable and generally reasonable. For longer or more serious rides, it's worth asking your hotel to arrange a reputable stable in advance rather than booking on the spot.
