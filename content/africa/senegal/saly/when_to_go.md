---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

The best time to visit is November to April — the dry season, when temperatures are warm but not oppressive (around 25–30°C), the sea is calm, and rain is essentially absent. December to February is peak season, when European visitors arrive in numbers and hotel prices are at their highest.

May to October is the wet season. Humidity is high, afternoon rains can be heavy, and the ocean can become rough. The resort is noticeably quieter and many hotels drop their prices significantly, which makes it reasonable value for those who don't mind the weather. Temperatures remain warm year-round; Saly never gets cold.
