---

Touba is a pilgrimage city, and the experience of visiting is shaped by that fact. The Grand Mosque dominates everything — physically and spiritually. Beyond it, the city's distinctiveness comes from its self-governed, faith-shaped character: the prohibition on alcohol and tobacco, the density of Mouride symbols and imagery, the busy markets funded by the global Mouride trade network.
