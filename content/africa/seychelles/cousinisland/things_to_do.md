Cousin Island is a nature reserve, and virtually everything you do here is wildlife-focused. A trained conservation guide accompanies every group — there is no independent wandering — which means you will see far more than you would on your own. The guides know every nest and burrow on the island, and the birds and reptiles show almost no fear of humans, having lived alongside conservation workers for decades.

The island is open Monday to Friday, 9:45am to 12:30pm. Visits are organised by boat trip from [Praslin](/africa/seychelles/praslin), taking about 15 minutes. The entry fee is SCR 600 for non-residents. Bring water, sun protection, and mosquito repellent — there are no facilities on the island.
