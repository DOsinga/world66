Mission Lodge is a historic site in the mountains of Mahé, where a school was founded for the children of liberated slaves. The ruins are modest, but the setting makes the stop memorable, with forest around the old mission site and broad views across the island.

It is an easy addition to a drive or hike through Morne Seychellois National Park, especially for travellers interested in Seychelles history beyond the beaches.
