---
title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Mahe's highlights divide between Victoria and the island's beaches, forests, and gardens. The [Victoria Market](/africa/seychelles/mahe/victoria_market) is the liveliest place in the Seychelles capital on weekday mornings. The [Morne Seychellois National Park](/africa/seychelles/mahe/morne_seychellois_national_park) covers nearly a third of the island and contains good hiking trails through cloud forest. For beaches, [Beau Vallon](/africa/seychelles/mahe/beau_vallon) on the northwest coast is the busiest and most accessible; [Anse Intendance](/africa/seychelles/mahe/anse_intendance) in the south is among the most beautiful.

The [Botanical Gardens](/africa/seychelles/mahe/botanical_gardens) in Victoria are worth a morning, especially for the giant tortoises and the endemic coco de mer palms. [Le Jardin du Roi](/africa/seychelles/mahe/le_jardin_du_roi) is a spice garden 20 minutes south of Victoria that covers 85 acres of hillside with cinnamon, vanilla, and nutmeg trees. The [Domaine de Val des Prés](/africa/seychelles/mahe/domaine_de_val_des_pres) in Au Cap presents traditional Creole crafts and architecture.
