---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

Mahe has two seasons. The northwest monsoon (November to March) brings warmer, calmer weather on the west coast beaches like Beau Vallon — this is peak tourist season, with December and January the busiest months. The southeast trade winds (May to September) bring rougher seas on the south and east coasts; beaches like Anse Intendance can have strong surf during this period.

There is no bad time to visit by European standards — temperatures hover around 27–30°C year-round, and even the wetter months get plenty of dry days. The shoulder months of April and October offer a good balance: fewer visitors, reasonable prices, and generally calm seas. Diving is year-round, with the best visibility during the calmer months.
