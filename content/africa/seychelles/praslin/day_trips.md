Praslin is the natural hub for exploring the inner islands of the Seychelles. La Digue — home to Anse Source d'Argent, one of the most photographed beaches in the world — is 30 minutes by ferry. Cousin Island, a nature reserve of extraordinary bird density, is 15 minutes by boat.
