Bo is Sierra Leone's second largest city and the capital of the Southern Province, a sprawling commercial hub about four hours east of [Freetown](/africa/sierraleone/freetown) by road. It is the main market town for the south and east of the country, and its busy [central market](/africa/sierraleone/bo/central_market) is the economic engine of the region. Bo was one of the first towns in Sierra Leone to have a secondary school — the Bo Government Secondary School, founded in 1906 — and it retains a reputation as an education centre, with several secondary schools and technical institutes drawing students from across the south.

The city is not a tourist destination in the conventional sense, but it is a practical base if you are heading to the Gola Rainforest or the diamond-mining areas of Kenema and Kono. The atmosphere is lively and welcoming. Infrastructure is improving but still rough — expect power outages and unpaved roads in many neighbourhoods. Bo was heavily affected by the civil war of the 1990s but has recovered with considerable energy.

The [Immaculate Heart of Mary Cathedral](/africa/sierraleone/bo/ihmcathedral) in the town centre is the seat of the Roman Catholic Diocese of Bo, established in 2011. It is one of the more substantial buildings in the city and worth a look if you are passing. The diamond trade that operates out of nearby Kenema and Kono passes through Bo's markets and gives the commercial district a particular intensity.
