The Banana Islands sit off the southern end of the Freetown Peninsula, with beaches that feel much farther from the capital than the map suggests. The appeal is simple: quiet sand, warm water, fishing-village life, and a slower rhythm than Lumley or Lakka.

This is a day trip or overnight excursion rather than a quick swim from town. Arrange boat transport carefully, ask about sea conditions, and bring what you need; facilities are limited compared with the peninsula beaches.
