Hamilton Beach sits along the string of coves and sandy breaks west of central Freetown, where the peninsula begins to feel greener and less urban. It is smaller and quieter than the better-known Lumley or River No. 2 beaches, which makes it appealing if you are more interested in space and sea air than a busy beach scene.

Facilities can be limited, and conditions change with weather and tide, so go with realistic expectations. It works best as part of a beach-hopping day along the peninsula rather than as the single reason to cross town.
