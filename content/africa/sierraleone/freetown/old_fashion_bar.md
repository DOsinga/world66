Old Fashion is one of the longer-running open-air bars on Lumley Beach. Wooden benches, music from a stack of speakers, cold Star beer, and a relaxed crowd of locals and a few expats. Good place for a sundowner.

Food is simple — grilled fish or chicken with chips. Cash only.
