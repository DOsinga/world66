title: "Shopping"
type: section
---

Freetown's main craft market is the [Big Market](/africa/sierraleone/freetown/big_market) on Wallace Johnson Street — gara cloth (indigo-dyed cotton), basketry, beads, and country cloth at negotiable prices. For everyday goods and produce, [King Jimmy Market](/africa/sierraleone/freetown/king_jimmy_market) by the bay is the busiest and most atmospheric option, though it is intense and not for everyone.

The supermarkets in Aberdeen and Lumley — Choithram, Monoprix, and a few Lebanese-owned chains — stock most imported food and basic supplies. Cash is dominant; card acceptance is improving but unreliable away from the main hotels.
