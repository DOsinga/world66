The small whitewashed Methodist church in Maroon Town was founded by Jamaican Maroons — escaped slaves who had fought the British in Jamaica and were eventually deported to Freetown in 1800. It is one of the oldest churches in Sierra Leone and a focus for Krio heritage.

The building is plain and easy to miss if you do not know to look for it. Inside, simple wooden pews face a modest pulpit. Easiest to find with a local guide or by asking for "the Maroon church" on Liverpool Street.
