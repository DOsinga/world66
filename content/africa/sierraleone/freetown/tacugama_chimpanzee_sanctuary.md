The Tacugama Chimpanzee Sanctuary occupies 100 acres of rainforest in the Western Area Peninsula National Park above Freetown. It rehabilitates chimpanzees confiscated from the illegal pet and bushmeat trades and runs an active research and education programme. The sanctuary is one of Sierra Leone's most rewarding day trips and a rare opportunity to see the species in something close to wild conditions.

Public tours run several times a day, typically morning and mid-afternoon. Lodges in the forest allow overnight stays. The drive up from Freetown takes around 45 minutes on a rough road and is best arranged through a local taxi or tour operator. Book the tour in advance.
