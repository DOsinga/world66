The forested interior of the peninsula is protected as a national park, covering some 17,000 hectares of evergreen rainforest along the ridge that runs from Freetown south to River No. 2. The flagship attraction is the **Tacugama Chimpanzee Sanctuary** on the park's northern edge, where you can take an hour-long guided walk past the enclosures of rescued chimps.

For more serious walking, the park has marked trails to **Charlotte Falls** and the **Picket Hill** viewpoint, both accessible from Regent village above Freetown. Guides at the park headquarters arrange visits with a day's notice. The forest is hot and humid; start early and bring water.
