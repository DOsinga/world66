---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

The dry season runs **November to April** and is the only sensible window for a beach trip. Days are hot, the sea is calm, and the famous Saharan harmattan haze settles over the peninsula in January and February. The earliest months are the freshest; by April the heat is genuinely sticky.

The rainy season from **May to October** is intense — the peninsula sits in one of the wettest stretches of West Africa, and August can deliver more than a metre of rain in a single month. Many beach lodges close for the duration. Surfers, however, get their best swell at the shoulder of the wet season.
