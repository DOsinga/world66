Makeni is the natural staging point for the north of Sierra Leone. **Kabala** is the closest major day trip, a hill town two hours further north with cooler air and walks up to Wara Wara mountain. With more time, the Outamba-Kilimi National Park, on the Guinea border, offers chimpanzees, hippos, and a network of forest rivers — plan on at least two days for the round trip.

For the truly ambitious, **Mount Bintumani** in the Loma Mountains is the highest peak in West Africa west of Cameroon, at 1,945 m. Reaching the base requires a long drive on rough tracks, and the climb itself takes two to three days with a local guide. It is one of the great unsung treks of the region.
