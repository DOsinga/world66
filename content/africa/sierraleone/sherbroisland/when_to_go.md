---
title: When to Go
type: section
---

The dry season, from November to April, is by far the better time to visit. Roads (such as they are) become impassable during the rains, boat crossings are rougher, and the island's mangrove channels are harder to navigate. The peak of the dry season in January and February brings pleasantly warm temperatures and reliable transport conditions.

Birdwatching is particularly rewarding during the dry season when migratory species from Europe and northern Africa stop over in the mangroves and mudflats. The fishing season follows similar patterns — the dry season catch in the offshore waters is the best of the year. Avoid the heaviest part of the wet season (July and August) unless you have a specific reason and a lot of flexibility.
