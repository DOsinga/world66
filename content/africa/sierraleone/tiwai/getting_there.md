---
title: "Getting There"
type: section
---

Tiwai is in southern Sierra Leone, reached by road and a short river crossing. From [Freetown](/africa/sierraleone/freetown), most travellers drive via [Bo](/africa/sierraleone/bo) and Potoru or Kambama; allow a full day because the later sections can be slow and rough.

Public transport is not practical for a short visit. Arrange a 4WD and driver through a local operator or the island management, and confirm the boat transfer before leaving town. In the rainy season, road conditions can change quickly.
