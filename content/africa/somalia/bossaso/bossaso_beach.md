The Gulf of Aden coastline around Bossaso offers stretches of relatively undeveloped beach with clear water and good visibility. The beaches east of the port are the most accessible, reachable by vehicle on a dirt road running along the coastal flat. The water is warm year-round and the seabed is sandy near shore, though the coast becomes rockier further east.

The best time for swimming is October to March, when the northeast monsoon keeps the sea calm and the coastal air is cooler. During the summer khareef season (June to September), winds pick up and the sea can be rough. Facilities are essentially nonexistent — bring everything you need. Local advice on current conditions and safety in specific locations is essential before visiting.
