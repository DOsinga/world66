title: "Practical Information"
type: section
---

Hobyo lies in the Galmudug state of central Somalia, on the Indian Ocean coast some 500 km north-east of Mogadishu. The town is the administrative centre of the Hobyo district and is connected to the main coastal road by a partly graded track.

Security conditions in central Somalia have been volatile for decades. Al-Shabaab remains active in parts of Galmudug, clan tensions occasionally flare, and the wider area was historically the heartland of Somali piracy. There is no consular presence, no formal hotels, and no reliable medical evacuation. Western governments uniformly advise against all travel.

For independent travellers Hobyo is effectively off-limits. The information here is a record of what the town is, not an invitation to visit.
