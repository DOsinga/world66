Almnara Tower is one of Mogadishu's medieval remnants, a small but telling survivor from the city's long Indian Ocean trading history. It is not a polished monument, but it helps make sense of the older city beneath the modern capital.

Conditions in Mogadishu can change quickly, so treat this as a stop to make only with current local advice and reliable transport.
