Mogadishu Peace Park is one of the city's best-known public green spaces, opened in 2016 as a carefully landscaped place for families, photos, and a break from the surrounding streets. Its appeal is simple but real: lawns, shade, paths, and a public mood that feels more relaxed than much of central Mogadishu.

Security conditions in the city change, so check local advice before visiting and go with people who know the current situation.
