The Hawd is the broad semi-arid plateau that stretches south and east from Baligubadle into Ethiopia's Somali Region. It is grazing country — flat, lightly wooded with acacia and thorn scrub, and dotted with the seasonal water holes that determine where herders move.

Drive any distance south of Baligubadle and you will share the road with camels, sheep, and goats being walked along the edges. After rain the land turns briefly green and the herders set up temporary camps; in the dry season the air is dust and the herds gather around the wells.
