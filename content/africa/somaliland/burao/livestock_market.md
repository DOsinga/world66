The livestock market is the economic heart of Burao and the most vivid spectacle the city has to offer. On market days — typically Tuesday and Saturday mornings — the market fills with camels, goats, and sheep driven in from the surrounding countryside by Somali pastoralists. The scale is impressive: Burao's market is one of the largest camel markets in the Horn of Africa, and the animals command serious prices given that camels are a form of wealth, transport, and food all at once.

Visitors are rare enough that your presence will attract attention, but not hostility. Dress conservatively and bring a local guide or fixer if you can — it makes navigation easier and conversations possible. The market is at its busiest in the early morning and winds down by midday.
