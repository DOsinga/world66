  Burao holds a particular place in Somaliland's founding story: it was here on 18 May 1991 that the Somali National Movement declared independence from Somalia. The date is commemorated each year as Somaliland's national day, and the city's monuments trace the arc of a liberation struggle that lasted a decade and cost tens of thousands of lives.
---

Burao played a pivotal role in Somaliland's war of independence against the Siad Barre regime, and several monuments and sites around the city mark that history. The Somali National Movement (SNM) was active in the region throughout the 1980s, and the city suffered heavily during the government's crackdown — much of it was destroyed in 1988. The visible scars of that period have largely been rebuilt over, but plaques, memorials, and the memory of survivors make Burao a place where recent history feels close.

For travellers interested in this story, the best approach is to find a local guide who can provide context and direct you to relevant sites. The history is not well-documented in English-language sources, but it is well-remembered by residents.
