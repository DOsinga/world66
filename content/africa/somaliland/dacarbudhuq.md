Dacarbudhuq is the largest town on the road between [Hargeisa](/africa/somaliland/hargeisa) and [Berbera](/africa/somaliland/berbera), sitting on the main highway that connects Somaliland's capital to its principal port. Most travellers pass through without stopping, but the town has a certain significance — a [monument to Omar Kujoog](/africa/somaliland/dacarbudhuq/omar_kujoog_monument) commemorates his defence of Dacarbudhuq against the Italian advance towards Hargeisa during the colonial era.

A small [daily market](/africa/somaliland/dacarbudhuq/dacarbudhuq_market) is reason enough to stop for half an hour and watch the town go about its business — a glass of cardamom shaah, a quick look around, and onwards.
