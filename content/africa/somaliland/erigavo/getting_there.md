Erigavo Airport (IATA: ERA) sits a few kilometres north of town and receives occasional flights from Hargeisa operated by small regional carriers. Services are infrequent and schedules shift; confirm availability directly with operators before planning around air travel. The airport serves a town with no significant tourist infrastructure, so flight options are limited and can disappear without notice.

By road, Erigavo is roughly 500 kilometres from Hargeisa and can also be approached from Berbera on the coast. Neither route is straightforward: the roads are unpaved for significant stretches, and the final climb up into the highlands involves sections that require a high-clearance 4WD. The journey from Hargeisa takes around eight to ten hours in good conditions. Shared taxis and minibuses run the route but stop frequently and can take considerably longer. If you are not travelling with your own vehicle, hiring a car and driver in Hargeisa or Berbera is the practical option — one who knows the route and whose vehicle can handle it.
