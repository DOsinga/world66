Jama Mosque is one of the clearest landmarks in central Hargeisa, standing east of the main market area and woven into the city's daily rhythm. It is most atmospheric around Friday prayers, when the surrounding streets fill and the mosque becomes a visible centre of public life.

Non-Muslim visitors should be discreet and should not treat prayer time as a spectacle. Even from outside, the mosque helps orient a walk through Hargeisa: market, traffic, money changers, tea stalls, and the city's religious life all sit close together here.
