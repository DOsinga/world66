Naasa Hablood are the twin conical hills outside Hargeisa, visible from a distance and often treated as one of the capital's natural symbols. Their Somali name refers to their distinctive paired shape, and they sit in the open country between the city and the Berbera corridor.

They are more of a viewpoint and landmark than a developed attraction. Go with a local driver or guide, especially if you want to hike or photograph around the hills, and combine the outing with a wider trip outside Hargeisa rather than making it your only excursion.
