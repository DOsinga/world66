Saryan Museum is a small independent cultural museum in Hargeisa, focused on Somali heritage, everyday objects, photographs, and memory. In a city where formal museum infrastructure is limited, that alone makes it a useful stop for travellers trying to understand Somaliland beyond street impressions.

Expect a modest, personal museum rather than a polished national institution. Its value lies in conversation, context, and the chance to see how local people are collecting and presenting their own recent and older history.
