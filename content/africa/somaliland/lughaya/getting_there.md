---
title: "Getting There"
type: section
---

Lughaya is remote even by Somaliland standards. The nearest airport with scheduled flights is **Berbera** (BBO), about 200 km east, served from Hargeisa, Dubai, and Addis Ababa. Hargeisa airport (HGA) is further inland but has more flights.

From Berbera or Hargeisa to Lughaya is a long drive on poor roads — typically a full day with a 4×4, longer in the wet seasons. Public transport (shared minibuses, *qansaaha*) runs irregularly; most visitors arrange a private vehicle with a driver who knows the route. The crossing into the Awdal Region passes through several local-administration checkpoints; carry your passport, your Somaliland visa, and a Somaliland-issued security travel permit (the SPU, **Special Protection Unit**, is mandatory for foreign travel in much of the region — arrange this through your hotel or guide in Hargeisa before setting out).

Travel to Lughaya without a local fixer is not advisable. Conditions on the ground change quickly; consult your embassy's current travel advice.
