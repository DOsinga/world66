The biggest shopping centre on the KwaZulu-Natal South Coast, Galleria Mall sits just off the N2 highway between Amanzimtoti and Athlone Park. Major South African chains — Woolworths, Checkers, Pick n Pay, Edgars — anchor more than 200 shops, plus a Ster-Kinekor cinema and the usual range of food courts.

Useful as a rainy-day option and as the closest reliable place to stock up on groceries for a self-catering holiday. Free parking. The mall replaced an earlier shopping centre and has been the main retail hub for the South Coast since opening in 2008.
