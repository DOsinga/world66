The Pig and Whistle is Bathurst's social and historical centre, a pub and inn on Kowie Road that claims to be South Africa's oldest continuously licensed pub. The building and bar carry much of the village's 1820 settler atmosphere.

World66 does not cover accommodation here; come for a drink, a meal, or simply to see the pub that gives Bathurst much of its character. It is the kind of place where the stop matters more if you give it time.
