Cape Town has the most beautiful of settings, wedged between the Atlantic and the flat-topped massif of [Table Mountain](/africa/southafrica/capetown/tablemountain). Combine that with a Mediterranean climate, a layered colonial and post-apartheid history, and one of the most varied collections of sights in any African city, and you have one of the world's great travel destinations.

Established as a Dutch East India Company supply station in 1652, Cape Town has a rich and often painful history that shows in its architecture and its public space. The [Castle of Good Hope](/africa/southafrica/capetown/castle_good_hope) is the oldest colonial building in South Africa; the [Slave Lodge](/africa/southafrica/capetown/slave_lodge) and the [District Six Museum](/africa/southafrica/capetown/district_six_museum) record the cost of that history; the [Cape Town City Hall](/africa/southafrica/capetown/cape_town_city_hall) is where Mandela gave his first free speech in 1990. The colourful [Bo-Kaap](/africa/southafrica/capetown/bokaap) quarter and the leafy [Company's Garden](/africa/southafrica/capetown/companys_garden) are easy walks from the centre.

The [V&A Waterfront](/africa/southafrica/capetown/victoria__alfred) is the obvious tourist hub, with boats to [Robben Island](/africa/southafrica/capetown/robbenisland), the [Two Oceans Aquarium](/africa/southafrica/capetown/two_oceans_aquarium), and the spectacular [Zeitz MOCAA](/africa/southafrica/capetown/zeitz_mocaa) contemporary art museum in a converted grain silo. For wine, [Groot Constantia](/africa/southafrica/capetown/grootconstantia) is within the city and the [Stellenbosch and Franschhoek wine country](/africa/southafrica/capetown/winelands_day_trip) is an hour east.

Near Cape Town are some of the country's finest beaches — [Camps Bay](/africa/southafrica/capetown/camps_bay) and [Clifton](/africa/southafrica/capetown/clifton_beaches) for the Atlantic glamour, [Boulders Beach](/africa/southafrica/capetown/boulders_beach) for the resident penguins, and [Bloubergstrand](/africa/southafrica/capetown/bloubergstrand) for the classic Table Mountain view back across the bay.

If you are into hiking, Table Mountain, [Lion's Head](/africa/southafrica/capetown/lions_head), [Devil's Peak](/africa/southafrica/capetown/devils_peak) and the [Cape Point](/africa/southafrica/capetown/cape_point) reserve at the tip of the peninsula offer some of the best urban walking anywhere. [Kirstenbosch](/africa/southafrica/capetown/kirstenbosch), on the mountain's eastern flank, is one of the world's great botanical gardens.
