A colony of some 3,000 African penguins living among the granite boulders of a sheltered cove near Simon's Town. The penguins are completely unruffled by visitors and will walk past you on the path; in season (October to January) chicks waddle around the boulder fields. Entry through the Table Mountain National Park network; the boardwalk takes you close without disturbing nesting sites. Combine with a trip to Cape Point and the Cape of Good Hope, 30 minutes further south.
