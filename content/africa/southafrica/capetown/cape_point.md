A sheer headland of shale cliffs dropping 200 metres into the sea at the southwestern corner of Africa. The old lighthouse at the top, reached by a funicular or a steep path, gives views across False Bay to the east and the Atlantic to the west. The flying time to Rio de Janeiro is 5,700 kilometres across open ocean. The Cape of Good Hope, a short walk away, is the most southwesterly point on the continent — not the most southerly, despite the sign. The surrounding fynbos is exceptional in flower.
