Cape Town Science Centre is an interactive science museum in Observatory, aimed especially at families and school-age children. Hands-on displays cover puzzles, building, technology, and everyday science rather than static cases.

It is a useful indoor stop when travelling with children, and it pairs well with other Observatory and Woodstock sights.
