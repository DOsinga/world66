title: Day Trips
type: section
---

The Cape Peninsula and the Winelands give Cape Town an unusually rich set of day trips. South along the Atlantic, [Hout Bay](/africa/southafrica/houtbay) is a fishing village with a working harbour and seal-watching boats, and the western end of [Chapman's Peak Drive](/africa/southafrica/chapmans_peak) — nine cliff-edge kilometres carved into the mountainside on the way to Noordhoek. Around the False Bay side, [Simon's Town](/africa/southafrica/simonstown) is a Victorian naval town within walking distance of the penguin colony at [Boulders Beach](/africa/southafrica/capetown/boulders_beach), while [Cape Point and the Cape of Good Hope](/africa/southafrica/cape_point) reserve mark the dramatic, wind-blown tip of the peninsula. A full loop of the peninsula takes a relaxed day.

Inland, the [Cape Winelands](/africa/southafrica/winelands) are an hour east, with [Stellenbosch](/africa/southafrica/stellenbosch) and [Franschhoek](/africa/southafrica/franschhoek) as the two historic centres — Cape Dutch architecture, good restaurants and dozens of estates open for tasting. A day trip is feasible but tight; many visitors take a guided tour to avoid driving between tastings. Closer to home, [Robben Island](/africa/southafrica/capetown/robbenisland) is technically within Cape Town but requires a ferry and the better part of a day, so book well ahead.
