The Cape Peninsula and the Winelands give Cape Town an unusually rich set of day trips. [Stellenbosch](/africa/southafrica/stellenbosch) and [Franschhoek](/africa/southafrica/franschhoek) are each about an hour east and are the two centres of the Cape Winelands — historic towns with good restaurants, Cape Dutch architecture and dozens of estates open for tasting. [Simon's Town](/africa/southafrica/capetown/simons_town), at the far end of False Bay, takes about 45 minutes by car and combines the penguin colony at Boulders Beach with a Victorian naval-town character. [Robben Island](/africa/southafrica/capetown/robbenisland) is technically within Cape Town but requires a ferry and half a day; book well ahead.
