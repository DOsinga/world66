Nevernew is one of the longer-running vintage shops on Lower Main Road, packed tight with retro skirts, band t-shirts, Doc Martens and an ever-changing rail of secondhand and new hippie-style clothing. Stock turns over quickly — staff bring in new pieces most weeks — so regulars treat it as a place to check back on rather than a one-time browse.

It sits among a dense run of thrift and vintage stores on this stretch of Lower Main Road, making it an easy stop on a longer crawl through Observatory's secondhand shopping scene. Cash and card both accepted; expect to dig a little to find the good pieces.
