Simon's Town is the historic Royal Navy base on the False Bay side of the peninsula, with a Victorian main street still lined with naval-themed shops, pubs and small museums. It has been the South African Navy's main base since the British handed over the dockyard in 1957, and the harbour is dominated by grey warships and frigates.

The town's most beloved character is Just Nuisance, a Great Dane and the only dog ever officially enlisted in the Royal Navy, who is commemorated by a bronze statue on Jubilee Square. The South African Naval Museum, the Heritage Museum and the easy walk south to Boulders Beach round out a half-day visit. The Metrorail train from central Cape Town runs all the way here, hugging the False Bay shoreline.
