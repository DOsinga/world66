The Armchair Theatre has been a fixture of Observatory's nightlife since 1998, running a packed weekly programme out of its Lower Main Road space that mixes live music with stand-up comedy, karaoke, quiz nights and the occasional film screening or exhibition. It functions as both a bar and a small venue, with a stage end and a more conversational front bar.

The weekly line-up changes but typically includes comedy nights midweek, karaoke on Thursdays and live bands on Fridays and Saturdays — check the calendar before heading down since the format shifts night to night. It's an easy walk from the rest of Lower Main Road's bars and restaurants, and works well as a stop on a longer evening through the neighbourhood.
