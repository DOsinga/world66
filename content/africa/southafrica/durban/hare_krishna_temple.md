Durban's Hare Krishna Temple, formally the Sri Sri Radha Radhanath Temple of Understanding, is one of the city's most distinctive religious buildings. Its lotus-like form rises from landscaped grounds in Chatsworth, reflecting the deep Indian presence south of the city centre.

The temple is open to respectful visitors outside major services, and the vegetarian restaurant is part of the draw. It is a little out of the central tourist circuit, but worthwhile if you are interested in Durban's Hindu and Indian cultural landscape.
