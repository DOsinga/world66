The Inanda Heritage Route runs through three of the most important sites in 20th-century South African history, all in the township of Inanda 30km north-west of Durban. **Phoenix Settlement** was the home where Mahatma Gandhi developed his philosophy of *satyagraha* (non-violent resistance) between 1904 and 1914; the small museum on site is excellent. **Ohlange Institute** was founded in 1900 by John Dube, the first president of the ANC, and is where Nelson Mandela cast his vote in the 1994 election. **Inanda Seminary** was the first secondary school for African girls in southern Africa.

The route is best done with a guide — both for context and because Inanda is not somewhere to wander alone. Several Durban tour operators run half-day Inanda trips for around R600.
