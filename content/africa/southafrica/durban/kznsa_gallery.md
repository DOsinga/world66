KZNSA Gallery, run by the KwaZulu-Natal Society of Arts, is one of Durban's better independent art spaces. It focuses on contemporary South African work, with exhibitions, a shop, and a cafe that make it easy to linger.

The gallery is a good reason to head into Glenwood, especially if you want a break from beaches, malls, and big civic museums. Its programme changes often, so the exact reward depends on what is hanging when you visit.
