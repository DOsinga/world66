MINITOWN is a beachfront miniature park that turns Durban into a walkable model city, with scaled-down buildings, railways, harbour scenes, and local landmarks. It is old-school family tourism, but that is part of its charm: simple, visual, and very easy to understand.

The park is best for families or for travelers who enjoy slightly nostalgic civic attractions. It also gives a quick overview of Durban's built identity before you head back to the real city, from the beachfront and harbour to the high-rise skyline behind it.
