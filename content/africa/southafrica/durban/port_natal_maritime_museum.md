Port Natal Maritime Museum sits by Durban harbour and uses preserved vessels, ship models, and maritime displays to tell the story of the port. The setting matters: Durban's identity is inseparable from its working harbour, and the museum makes that connection tangible.

It is a modest museum, but a useful stop if you are interested in ships, port history, or the older waterfront. Pair it with the nearby city-hall museums or a harbour-focused walk.
