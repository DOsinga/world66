The Bluff is the long forested headland that shelters Durban's harbour from the south, rising to almost 100 metres above the sea. The seaward side has the **Bluff Headland** nature reserve, a stretch of coastal forest and grassland with monkeys, bushpigs and views down the wild south coast.

Below the headland, the Bluff suburb has quieter beaches than the Golden Mile — particularly **Brighton Beach** and the surfers' favourite **Anstey's** — and a more low-key local atmosphere. Watch the container ships round the South Pier from the Bluff Yacht Club for one of the best views in the city.
