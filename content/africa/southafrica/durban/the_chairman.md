The Chairman is the dressed-up jazz lounge in Durban's old Point quarter — vintage furniture, soft red lighting, live bands four nights a week, and a strict over-25 door policy that keeps the crowd grown-up. The drinks list runs to cocktails and wine, the food is small-plate, and the music is the point.

Reservations essential at weekends. The Point can feel deserted on the way in — order an Uber rather than wander the surrounding streets.
