---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

Durban is subtropical and hot all year. The **best months are April to October** — the dry season, with sunny days at around 22-26°C and warm sea temperatures. June and July are the coolest, the surf is at its best, and the **Sardine Run** brings whales, sharks and millions of seabirds along the coast.

**Summer (December–March)** is hot, sticky and rainy, often with afternoon thunderstorms; humidity can be punishing. December is the South African school-holiday season and the beachfront is at its busiest. The annual **Comrades Marathon** between Durban and Pietermaritzburg runs in June and fills the city; book accommodation well ahead.
