East London is a working Eastern Cape port city with a better coastline than its name prepares you for. The centre can feel worn, but the city has long beaches, surf breaks, river mouths and a natural harbour, all wrapped into the daily life of Buffalo City.

The easy pleasures are along the coast. [Nahoon Beach](/africa/southafrica/east_london/nahoon_beach) is the best-known surf and sand stop, while [Orient Beach](/africa/southafrica/east_london/orient_beach) keeps you closer to the old beachfront and harbour. The city is not glossy, and that is part of the point: East London is more useful, lived-in and local than a resort town.

Do not skip the [East London Museum](/africa/southafrica/east_london/east_london_museum). Its coelacanth story, natural history and regional collections make it one of the strongest small-city museums in South Africa. Add the [Ann Bryant Art Gallery](/africa/southafrica/east_london/ann_bryant_art_gallery) if you want a quieter cultural stop away from the beach.
