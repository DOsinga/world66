The Franschhoek Motor Museum is a polished collection of historic cars, motorcycles, bicycles, and motoring memorabilia displayed on the L'Ormarins estate. Even travellers with only a mild interest in cars tend to enjoy it, because the vehicles are presented as design, engineering, and social history rather than just horsepower.

It sits outside the village, so plan it as a deliberate excursion rather than a quick stroll from the main street. The setting helps enormously: vineyards, mountains, and immaculate museum halls make this feel more like a Winelands outing than a garage visit.
