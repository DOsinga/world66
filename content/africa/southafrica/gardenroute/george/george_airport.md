story: "George Airport is the main gateway to the eastern Garden Route, with multiple daily flights to Cape Town and Johannesburg on South African Airways, FlySafair, and Kulula. The airport is small — you can walk to your car hire from the terminal in under 3 minutes — and remarkably unstressed compared to the main South African airports. Flying in rather than driving the 400 kilometres from Cape Town saves half a day and is often cheaper than it looks."
---

The main airport serving the Garden Route, with regular flights to Cape Town (60 minutes) and Johannesburg (90 minutes). Car hire is available at the terminal from the major South African operators. The airport is 10 kilometres west of central George; taxis and shuttles meet most flights.
