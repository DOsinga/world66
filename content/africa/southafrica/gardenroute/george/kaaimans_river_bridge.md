A historic steel railway bridge over the Kaaimans River gorge, about halfway between George and Wilderness on the Garden Route. The bridge carried the Outeniqua Choo-Tjoe steam train on what was one of the most scenic rail sections in South Africa. The train no longer runs but the bridge is preserved; the best views are from the N2 lookout or from the beach at Wilderness.
