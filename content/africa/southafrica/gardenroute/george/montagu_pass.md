story: "Montagu Pass is the original 19th-century mountain road over the Outeniqua range, now bypassed by the modern Outeniqua Pass but preserved as a scenic gravel route. The road was built by convict labour in 1847 under the direction of engineer Andrew Bain — the same man responsible for several other mountain passes in the Western Cape. At 12 kilometres it is manageable for most vehicles in dry conditions. The pass snakes through indigenous yellowwood forest, and the views from the upper section over George and the coast are better than those from the modern tar road."
---

A 19th-century gravel mountain pass over the Outeniqua range, now a heritage route running parallel to the modern Outeniqua Pass. Built in 1847, the road is narrow but passable in a normal car in dry conditions. The forest is denser than on the modern pass and the road sees almost no traffic — a good alternative route for those who want to slow down through the mountains.
