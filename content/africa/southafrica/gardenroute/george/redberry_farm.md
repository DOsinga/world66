Redberry Farm is a family-oriented farm attraction outside George, known for strawberry picking, casual food and outdoor activities. It is a light, easy stop rather than a major sight, but it fits the Garden Route's slower family-travel rhythm.

The farm is most worthwhile in season or when children need space to run between longer drives. Treat it as a relaxed break on the edge of George, not as a substitute for the area's beaches, passes and forest walks.
