The Cape St Blaize Trail follows the cliffs around the point, starting near the lighthouse and the cave below it. It is one of the simplest ways to get Mossel Bay's best coastal views without leaving town.

The path is exposed, so take water and avoid pushing on in bad wind or heat. In whale season, stop often and scan the water rather than treating it as only a fitness walk.
