Birds of Eden is a huge mesh-covered sanctuary built over a pocket of indigenous forest near The Crags. Walkways pass through the aviary at different levels, so you see birds in the canopy, beside streams, and around the forest floor.

It is a managed sanctuary rather than wild birding, but the scale is impressive and it pairs naturally with Monkeyland next door.
