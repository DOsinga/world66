Lookout Beach is one of the easiest beaches to love in Plett: wide, open, and close enough to town for a quick swim, surf check, or long walk. The views take in the bay and the river mouth, with Robberg closing the horizon to the south.

As elsewhere on this coast, swim with care and pay attention to lifeguard flags and surf conditions.
