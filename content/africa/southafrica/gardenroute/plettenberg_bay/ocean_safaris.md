Ocean Safaris runs boat trips into Plettenberg Bay, where seals are reliable and dolphins are common. Southern right and humpback whales are seasonal, while Bryde's whales may appear at other times.

Conditions matter, so treat it as a weather-dependent outing and book for your first clear day rather than your last morning in town.
