Bloukrans Bridge carries the N2 across a deep gorge east of Storms River. Even if you have no intention of jumping from it, the bridge is worth a short stop for the scale of the ravine and the sweep of the concrete arch.

The bungy jump is the famous activity here, and it is run as a commercial operation from the bridge complex. Spectators can still get a good sense of the drop without signing up for the leap.
