The Tsitsikamma Canopy Tour sends you along a series of zip-lines and platforms high in the indigenous forest. It is the polished, accessible adventure activity in Storms River: enough height to feel exciting, but structured and guided rather than rough.

The best part is the change of viewpoint. From the platforms the forest stops being a wall of green beside the road and becomes a layered place of trunks, ferns, birds, and filtered light.
