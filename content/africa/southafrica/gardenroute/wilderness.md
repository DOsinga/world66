Wilderness sits on a long, wide [beach](/africa/southafrica/gardenroute/wilderness/wilderness_beach) between the Kaaimans River mouth and the Touw River lagoon, roughly halfway along the [Garden Route](/africa/southafrica/gardenroute). The name is apt — the setting is genuinely wild, backed by forested hills and fronted by the Indian Ocean.

The [Wilderness Section of the Garden Route National Park](/africa/southafrica/gardenroute/wilderness/wilderness_section) protects a chain of lakes, rivers and estuaries behind the coast. You can canoe up the [Touw River](/africa/southafrica/gardenroute/wilderness/touw_river_lagoon) to a series of waterfalls on the [Half-Collared Kingfisher Trail](/africa/southafrica/gardenroute/wilderness/half_collared_kingfisher_trail), go hiking through indigenous forest, or just swim in the warm ocean. [Paragliding](/africa/southafrica/gardenroute/wilderness/paragliding) is also a draw. The [Map of Africa](/africa/southafrica/gardenroute/wilderness/map_of_africa) viewpoint, a few kilometres inland, offers a striking aerial perspective of a river meander that looks uncannily like the continent.
