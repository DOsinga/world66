---
title: Day Trips
type: section
---

The Magaliesburg valley sits at the western end of one of South Africa's most concentrated clusters of significant places. The **Cradle of Humankind**, a UNESCO World Heritage Site, is forty minutes east — the **Sterkfontein Caves** within it are where some of the most important early hominid fossils have been excavated. The **Maropeng Visitor Centre** at the cradle interprets the discoveries.

**Hartbeespoort Dam**, about half an hour east, is a sprawling weekend-leisure lake with cable car, aerial cableway up the Magaliesberg, and a slightly tacky chain of resort hotels and curio shops. **Sun City** and **Pilanesberg National Park** are within a two-hour drive west — a longer day trip but doable, especially if you want one day of big-five game viewing without committing to a multi-day safari.

For active travellers, the bigger end of the **Magaliesberg trail system** (the Highveld Trail) starts about an hour east near Pretoria and runs multiple-day routes along the spine of the range.
