Lesedi Cultural Village is a visitor-focused cultural village near the Magaliesberg, presenting Zulu, Sotho, Pedi, Xhosa, and Ndebele homesteads, dance, and food. It is more staged than a living village, but it can be a useful introduction to several South African cultural traditions in one stop.
