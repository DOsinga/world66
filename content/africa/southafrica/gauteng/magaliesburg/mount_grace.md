Mount Grace is the grandest of the country-house hotels in the Magaliesburg valley — a thatched estate of cottages and gardens spread across the foothills, four kilometres west of the town. The restaurant is open to non-guests for the long Sunday lunch, which is the appropriate Magaliesburg meal.

The kitchen does a contemporary South African menu — venison, springbok, local trout, Karoo lamb — in a glassed-in dining room overlooking the gardens. Bookings are essential for Sundays. The hotel runs a spa that is also open to day visitors and is a fair option if you want a longer-form weekend break.
