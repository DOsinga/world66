---
title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Graaff-Reinet splits neatly between town and landscape. The Valley of Desolation is the essential outing, with Camdeboo National Park wrapping around the town and giving the Karoo its scale. Back in the centre, the best sights are close enough to walk: the Dutch Reformed Church, Reinet House, small museums and streets of Cape Dutch and Victorian buildings.
