Hout Bay ("Wood Bay") is a fishing village and suburb about 20 km south of [Cape Town](/africa/southafrica/capetown) city centre, tucked into a dramatic valley between the mountains of the Cape Peninsula. The harbour is active — fishing boats, a seal colony on [Duiker Island](/africa/southafrica/houtbay/duiker_island) just offshore, and the bustle of the waterfront [Harbour Market](/africa/southafrica/houtbay/hout_bay_harbour_market).

The [Chapman's Peak Drive](/africa/southafrica/houtbay/chapmans_peak), from Hout Bay to Noordhoek, is one of the most spectacular coastal drives in South Africa — nine kilometres blasted into the cliff face above a sheer drop to the Atlantic. [The Sentinel](/africa/southafrica/houtbay/the_sentinel) headland to the west can be hiked for views across the bay, while the valley floor holds the [Hout Bay Museum](/africa/southafrica/houtbay/hout_bay_museum), [World of Birds](/africa/southafrica/houtbay/world_of_birds), and the peculiar [Lichtenstein Castle](/africa/southafrica/houtbay/lichtenstein_castle) on the hillside above. The township of [Imizamo Yethu](/africa/southafrica/houtbay/imizamo_yethu) to the north of the valley is best understood through a guided community tour. [Sandy Bay](/africa/southafrica/houtbay/sandy_bay), a secluded naturist beach, is a 30-minute walk from neighbouring Llandudno.
