Aston Bay is the quieter coastal edge of Jeffreys Bay, with long sandy beaches backed by protected natural areas rather than the surf-town bustle. It is a good place for a slow walk, birdwatching, and a look at the softer side of the coast, where tortoises, small antelope, rabbits, and mongoose are part of the local landscape.
