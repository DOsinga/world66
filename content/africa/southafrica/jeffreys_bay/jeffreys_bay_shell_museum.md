The Shell Museum is a small but fitting stop in a town where beachcombing is part of the local identity. Its displays cover shells, coastal life, shipwreck material, and pieces of Jeffreys Bay's fishing and holiday history.

It is near the tourist office and main beach, so it works well as a short stop between swims, surf checks, or a walk along Da Gama Road.
