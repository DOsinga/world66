Noorsekloof is a narrow strip of valley bushveld tucked into the north side of town. The trail is short, roughly three kilometres, and follows the kloof through birdy coastal vegetation rather than big scenery.

It is best as a morning leg-stretch before the beach wind comes up. Wear proper shoes after rain, when the streamside sections can be muddy.
