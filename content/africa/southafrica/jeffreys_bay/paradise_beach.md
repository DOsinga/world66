Paradise Beach is the quieter stretch south of Jeffreys Bay, with pale sand running toward the Kromme River and Cape St Francis beyond it. It is the place to go when you want space rather than surf-town bustle.

The beach is good for long walks, kite flying, and watching seabirds, but treat swimming conditions with care. The coast here can feel remote quickly.
