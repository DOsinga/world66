title: "Eating Out"
type: section
---

Johannesburg's restaurant scene is split between the northern suburbs and [Maboneng](/africa/southafrica/johannesburg/maboneng) downtown. [Rosebank](/africa/southafrica/johannesburg/rosebank), [Sandton](/africa/southafrica/johannesburg/sandton), and [Melville](/africa/southafrica/johannesburg/melville) have the density of good restaurants; Maboneng has the edge and atmosphere. South African beef is excellent everywhere — the highveld climate and grass-fed farming produce some of the best steaks on the continent.

Braai (barbecue) culture is central to South African food identity, but the city also has a good range of other cuisines: Mozambican seafood and peri-peri are standards on most menus; Cape Malay cooking shows up in better restaurants; and the pan-African options are worth exploring. For straightforward, excellent value, look for the Portuguese-influenced churrasco grills that serve chicken and pork from charcoal fires.
