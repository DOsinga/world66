The Sophiatown Heritage & Cultural Centre tells the story of one of Johannesburg's most important lost neighbourhoods. Sophiatown was a mixed, creative, politically charged district until 1955, when apartheid authorities began forcibly removing more than 60,000 residents and replacing it with a whites-only suburb.

The centre is a useful stop for understanding the writers, musicians, activists, clergy, and ordinary families who made Sophiatown famous before the removals. It pairs well with Constitution Hill and the Apartheid Museum for a fuller picture of 20th-century Johannesburg.
