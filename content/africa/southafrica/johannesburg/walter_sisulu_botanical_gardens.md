Walter Sisulu Botanical Gardens is one of Johannesburg's best green escapes, with lawns, rocky ridges, and good birdlife on the edge of the city. It is especially known for the Verreaux's eagles that nest near the waterfall, and it is an easy place for a picnic or a slow walk.
