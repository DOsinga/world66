---
title: When to Go
type: section
---

The Northern Cape has a semi-arid climate with warm, dry winters and hot summers. Most visitors are most comfortable from May through September, when daytime temperatures sit around 18–22°C and the nights are clear and cool. Winter days are pleasant for walking around the outdoor attractions; the heat of midsummer makes prolonged outdoor activity uncomfortable.

Summer (November through February) can be fierce — temperatures regularly exceed 38°C, and the diamond-country landscape offers little shade. Rain is possible in summer but Kimberley averages only around 400mm annually, and it rarely disrupts plans for long. Spring (September–October) and autumn (March–April) are good shoulder periods, warm without the extremes.

There are no major seasonal events that would make one time of year dramatically preferable. The mines and museums are open year-round.
