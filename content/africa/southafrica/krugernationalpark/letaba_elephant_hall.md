Letaba Elephant Hall is one of Kruger National Park's best interpretive stops, using skulls, tusks, and displays to explain elephant biology, ecology, and individual famous tuskers. It turns a rest-camp break into useful context for what you may see on the roads.

Visit it while staying at or passing through Letaba Rest Camp. The hall is especially worthwhile for families and first-time safari travellers who want to understand elephant behaviour before returning to game drives.
