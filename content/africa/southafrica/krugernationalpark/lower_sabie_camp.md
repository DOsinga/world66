Lower Sabie is one of Kruger's most beloved camps, set on the banks of the Sabie River near where it approaches the Mozambique border. The setting is excellent — game comes to the river in front of the camp, and the terrace restaurant is a fine place to watch elephants drinking at dusk. The camp is smaller and more intimate than Skukuza, with a strong sense of the bush right outside the fence.

The roads around Lower Sabie are consistently productive. The river roads east toward Crocodile Bridge and west toward Skukuza are reliable for lion and leopard, and the more open terrain around the H10 road (which links Lower Sabie to the Satara area) is good for cheetah. The dam outside the camp fence attracts hippos and crocodiles year-round and is excellent for waterbirds. Book well in advance — Lower Sabie is one of the most popular camps in the park.
