Ratel Pan is a west-facing hide above the Timbavati River near Piet Grobler Dam. The position is especially good in the morning, when animals come down to the water and the light is still soft.

It is a selective stop rather than a headline Kruger sight, but patient visitors may see lions, kingfishers, and other wildlife without leaving the vehicle route for long.
