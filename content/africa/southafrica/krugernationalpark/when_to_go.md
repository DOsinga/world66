---
title: When to Go
type: section
---

The dry season — May to October — is the best time for game viewing. Vegetation thins, water sources reduce to predictable points, and animals concentrate in accessible areas. Winter nights (June and July) can be surprisingly cold at altitude, but days are clear and excellent for driving. The peak months of July and August bring more visitors and campsite bookings must be made months in advance.

The wet season (November to April) brings lush green vegetation, baby animals, and the migratory birds that make Kruger a world-class birding destination. The downside is thick bush that makes spotting harder. The roads remain passable throughout the year, as most main routes are tarred.
