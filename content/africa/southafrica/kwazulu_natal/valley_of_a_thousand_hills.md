The Valley of a Thousand Hills is a deeply folded landscape west of Durban, where the Umgeni River has carved through the Natal Midlands over millions of years, leaving behind an endless series of ridges and valleys covered in grassland and indigenous bush. It is one of KwaZulu-Natal's most scenic areas and feels very different from the coast, quieter and more traditional.

The main road through the valley, the Old Main Road between Durban and Pietermaritzburg, passes through Botha's Hill and Drummond — good places to stop for lunch or to visit craft markets and the PheZulu Safari Park, which offers demonstrations of Zulu dance, a small traditional homestead, and a crocodile park. It's accessible rather than authentic, but gives an easy introduction to Zulu cultural traditions.

The valley makes a good half-day excursion from Durban, best combined with a drive to Pietermaritzburg and the Natal Midlands beyond.
