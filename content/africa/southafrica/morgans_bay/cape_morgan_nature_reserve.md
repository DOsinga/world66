Cape Morgan Nature Reserve protects the green coastal strip east of the village, between the lagoon, forest, and surf. It is the place to stretch a Morgan's Bay visit beyond the beach, with birds in the riverine forest and paths that lead toward wilder coast.

The reserve also gives context to the village. Morgan's Bay is not just a beach stop; it is a small settlement pressed between farmland, forest, and the first real edge of the Wild Coast.
