Morgan Bay Beach is a broad, easy strip of sand with the Inchara River lagoon tucked behind it. The lagoon is the gentler choice for children, paddling, and lazy swims, while the open beach gives you the long coastal walk and the sound of the surf.

Holiday periods are lively, but outside school breaks it can feel wonderfully unhurried. Lifeguard cover is seasonal, so check conditions locally before swimming in the sea.
