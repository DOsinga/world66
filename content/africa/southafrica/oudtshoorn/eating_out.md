---
title: Eating Out
type: section
---

Oudtshoorn's restaurant scene punches above its size, partly because it gets steady tourist traffic from the cave-and-ostrich circuit and partly because the local produce is excellent. Ostrich is on almost every menu — it tastes like lean beef, works well as a steak, and is worth trying at least once.

**Jemima's** on Baron van Rheede Street is the standby for Karoo cooking done properly: ostrich fillet, kudu pie, and waterblommetjie bredie (a stew of water lily flowers native to the Western Cape). The atmosphere is relaxed and local, and the portions are large. **The Nostalgie Restaurant** at the Pinehurst guesthouse is more formal and good for a special occasion. **Godfather's** near the town centre is popular for pizzas and offers a more relaxed mid-range option. For coffee and light meals, the bakeries on Voortrekker Road open early and are where the locals eat breakfast.
