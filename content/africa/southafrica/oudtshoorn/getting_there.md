---
title: Getting There
type: section
---

Oudtshoorn is 360 kilometres east of Cape Town along the N2 and then the N12 through the Outeniqua Mountains — about four hours by car. The Huguenot Tunnel near Paarl shortens the journey slightly. The more scenic route goes over the Outeniqua Pass at George, which adds a few minutes but is considerably more spectacular.

**By car** is by far the most practical option, as you will need wheels to reach the caves and farms. **Intercape** and **Translux** buses run from Cape Town and from Port Elizabeth, stopping at the town centre. The journey from Cape Town takes around five to six hours. There is a small airport at Oudtshoorn with irregular scheduled services — check current schedules before relying on it.
