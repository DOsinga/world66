Paternoster is a small fishing village turned holiday town on South Africa's West Coast. The best of it is simple: whitewashed cottages, cold Atlantic light, fishing boats on the beach, crayfish season, and the rocky Cape Columbine coast just beyond the last houses.

The village is no longer undiscovered, and weekends can be busy with Cape Town visitors. Even so, Paternoster has kept more character than many coastal resorts. The old part of town is still low, white, and walkable, with sea views down sandy lanes and restaurants tucked into former fishermen's cottages.

The reason to stay longer than lunch is [Cape Columbine Nature Reserve](/africa/southafrica/paternoster/cape_columbine_nature_reserve). It protects a rough stretch of granite, fynbos, coves, and open sea, with the [Cape Columbine Lighthouse](/africa/southafrica/paternoster/cape_columbine_lighthouse) standing above it. In late winter and spring, wildflowers brighten the surrounding veld.

Paternoster is best for slow travellers: walk the beach, eat seafood, drive the gravel road to the lighthouse, and watch the colour change over the Atlantic at sunset.
