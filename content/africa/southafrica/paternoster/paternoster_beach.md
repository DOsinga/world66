Paternoster Beach curves along the front of the village, close enough that you can step from a restaurant or cottage straight onto the sand. Fishing boats still land here, and that working edge keeps the beach from feeling like a resort strip.

Swimming is for the hardy because the Benguela current keeps the water cold. Walking is the better use of the beach, especially toward sunset.
