The old village is the part of Paternoster that makes the place worth stopping for. Low white cottages, narrow lanes, small galleries, seafood restaurants, and glimpses of fishing boats give it a strong West Coast feel.

It is small enough to explore on foot. Go early or late, when the day-trippers thin out and the light on the white walls is softer.
