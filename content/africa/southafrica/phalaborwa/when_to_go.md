---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

The dry winter months from May to September are the best time for wildlife viewing — grass is short, water sources concentrate animals, and temperatures are pleasant during the day. Nights can be cold, especially in June and July. The summer rainy season (November to March) brings heat and humidity, the bush turns spectacularly green, and baby animals appear in abundance, but tall grass makes spotting harder and malaria risk is higher.

Phalaborwa is in a malaria zone year-round. Take prophylactics and use a strong repellent regardless of season, but be especially diligent during and after the rains.
