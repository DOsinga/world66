Austin Roberts Bird Sanctuary is a pocket of wetland and bushveld in Nieuw Muckleneuk, close enough to central Pretoria to slip into a city day. It protects a series of dams and reed beds where herons, weavers, ducks, and other waterbirds gather, with hides that let you watch without much fuss.

The reserve is not a wilderness experience, and that is part of its appeal. It is a calm, accessible place for an hour or two between heavier sights, particularly for birders or families who need shade, water, and something alive to look at. Early morning is the best time to visit.
