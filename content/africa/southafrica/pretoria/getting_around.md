---
title: "Getting Around"
type: section
---

The **Gautrain** runs between Pretoria station and Hatfield (for the embassies and university area) with several stops including Menlyn. It's by far the safest and most reliable option for the central corridor.

**Taxis** are metered (Uber is widely available and preferred by most visitors and locals over street taxis). Do not flag down unmarked vehicles. **City buses** cover the main arteries but schedules are unreliable. Most of Pretoria's key attractions are spread across the city and not easily connected on foot — a car or Uber is the practical option for a full day of sightseeing.

**Walking** in the central city (Church Square area) is fine in the daytime. Avoid walking in the CBD after dark. The **Arcadia** and **Hatfield** suburbs are safer and have more restaurant and bar options for evenings.
