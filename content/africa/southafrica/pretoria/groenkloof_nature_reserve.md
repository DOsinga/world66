Groenkloof Nature Reserve is one of Pretoria's best escapes: a stretch of bushveld close enough to the city for a half-day walk, ride, or picnic. The reserve protects rocky ridges, grassland, and wooded valleys, with zebra, giraffe, antelope, and plenty of birdlife often seen from the trails.

It is not Kruger, and it does not pretend to be. Its appeal is that you can leave the traffic behind quickly and get a real taste of the Highveld outdoors without planning a long expedition. Go early for cooler weather and better wildlife sightings.
