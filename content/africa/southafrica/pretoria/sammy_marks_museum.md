Sammy Marks Museum occupies the grand Victorian house of the Lithuanian-born industrialist who became one of the South African Republic's most important entrepreneurs. Rooms are preserved with period furniture, decorative art, and domestic details that make the site feel more like a lived-in estate than a conventional display hall.

The museum is a worthwhile Pretoria-side trip for travelers interested in mining wealth, colonial-era society, and the private lives behind South Africa's late-19th-century boom. Its distance from the city centre means it is best planned as a deliberate outing, not a quick drop-in.
