title: Things to Do
type: section
---

Pretoria's main sights are concentrated in two clusters. In the **city centre**, [Church Square](/africa/southafrica/pretoria/church_square) is the historic heart — flanked by the Palace of Justice and the statue of Paul Kruger — with the [Kruger House](/africa/southafrica/pretoria/kruger_house), [Melrose House](/africa/southafrica/pretoria/melrose_house), and the [Ditsong National Museum of Natural History](/africa/southafrica/pretoria/national_museum_of_natural_history) all within walking distance. A short drive east, the [Union Buildings](/africa/southafrica/pretoria/union_buildings) sit on Meintjieskop hill above the city with Nelson Mandela's enormous bronze statue looking out over the amphitheatre lawns. The [Pretoria National Botanical Garden](/africa/southafrica/pretoria/pretoria_botanical_garden) (out in Brummeria) holds 50 hectares of indigenous South African flora and is a good retreat from the city. South of the centre, the [Voortrekker Monument](/africa/southafrica/pretoria/voortrekker_monument) on Monument Hill is a loaded but unmissable piece of Afrikaner heritage, with sweeping views from the dome. Directly opposite it, [Freedom Park](/africa/southafrica/pretoria/freedom_park) is the post-apartheid counter-memorial built on the adjacent hill. For families, the [National Zoological Gardens](/africa/southafrica/pretoria/national_zoological_gardens) on Boom Street is one of the best zoos on the continent.
