---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

Pretoria has a highveld climate: warm summers (November–March) with frequent afternoon thunderstorms, and dry, mild winters (June–August) with cold nights but usually sunny days. Summer temperatures reach 30°C but the air is fresh at 1,400 metres elevation. It rarely gets too hot to be comfortable.

The city is famous for its **jacaranda trees** — over 70,000 of them line the streets. They bloom in **October and early November**, turning entire avenues purple. This is a genuinely spectacular time to visit; the Union Buildings gardens and Herbert Baker Street are especially photographed. Book accommodation well in advance for Jacaranda season.

**Winter** (July–August) is the driest and most reliably sunny period, ideal for outdoor sightseeing. Rugby season runs April–October, and catching a Bulls match at Loftus Versfeld is worth timing a trip around.
