---
title: "Eating Out"
type: section
---

Vilakazi Street in Orlando West has become a restaurant strip, and the food along it ranges from decent to tourist-oriented. For a more authentic experience, Soweto has a thriving culture of shebeens (informal bars with food) and traditional restaurants serving South African staples like braai (barbecue), pap and vleis (maize porridge and meat), and oxtail stew. The food scene is improving rapidly as Soweto develops its tourism economy.
