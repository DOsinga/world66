---
title: "Getting There"
type: section
---

Soweto is about 16 kilometres southwest of central Johannesburg. The easiest way to visit independently is by Uber or metered taxi — the drive from central Johannesburg or Sandton takes 30–45 minutes depending on traffic. Many visitors join an organised township tour, which provides transport, a guide with historical knowledge, and access to places that might be harder to find independently.

The Rea Vaya Bus Rapid Transit (BRT) system connects Soweto to central Johannesburg, with the main Soweto BRT terminal at Chris Hani Baragwanath Hospital. From Johannesburg Park Station, buses run regularly along the BRT corridor. The journey takes about 45 minutes on the BRT.

Driving is possible; parking is available near the main sites on Vilakazi Street. Exercise normal urban caution — Soweto is no more dangerous than most parts of Johannesburg when you are with a group or on a recognised tourist route.
