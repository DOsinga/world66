Mandela House is the modest four-room home on Vilakazi Street in Orlando West where Nelson Mandela lived from 1946 until his arrest and imprisonment in 1962. Now a national museum, it is one of the most visited sites in South Africa — both for its connection to Mandela and for its role in preserving the history of the anti-apartheid movement.

The museum has been carefully preserved with period furniture and personal artefacts, giving a vivid impression of the modest circumstances of Mandela's domestic life before his 27-year imprisonment. Photographs, documents, and audio-visual exhibits tell the story of the family's years here and of Mandela's underground activities leading up to his arrest. The street outside — Vilakazi Street — is remarkable in being the only street in the world to have housed two Nobel Peace Prize laureates: Mandela at number 8115 and Desmond Tutu further along. Entry fee around R80. Open daily.
