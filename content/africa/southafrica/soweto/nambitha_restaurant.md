Nambitha is one of the better restaurants on Vilakazi Street, serving traditional South African food in a setting that gives visitors a relaxed place to eat after visiting the nearby museums. The menu covers South African favourites — braai meat, pap and chakalaka (a vegetable relish), offal dishes, and slow-cooked beef — alongside some more accessible options for those less adventurous with new cuisines.

The restaurant is popular with tour groups and individual visitors alike. It's a good place for lunch between visiting Mandela House and the Hector Pieterson Museum. Prices are reasonable, and the setting includes a courtyard that is pleasant in good weather.
