Orlando Stadium is the principal football ground in Soweto and one of the oldest stadiums in South Africa, first established in 1959 and substantially redeveloped. It is the home ground of Orlando Pirates, one of South Africa's most popular and successful football clubs, founded in Soweto in 1937. Matches between Pirates and their Soweto rivals Kaizer Chiefs — known as the Soweto Derby — are among the most passionate football occasions in Africa.

The stadium holds around 40,000 spectators and has hosted major events including African Champions League matches. If you are in Soweto during the football season (August to May), attending a Pirates home game is an excellent way to experience the city's energy. Check the PSL (Premier Soccer League) schedule for fixture dates.
