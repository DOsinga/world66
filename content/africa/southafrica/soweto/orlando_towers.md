  The two towers of the Orlando Power Station, commissioned in 1939, powered Johannesburg for decades. After the power station was decommissioned in 1998, the towers were acquired by a private company and reimagined as an extreme sports venue — one of the most distinctive repurposings of industrial infrastructure anywhere in Africa. The Orlando Towers are two giant decommissioned cooling towers from the old Orlando Power Station, transformed into one of Soweto's most unexpected attractions. The towers have been painted with vivid murals — a different design on each — and are visible from kilometres away across the flat Soweto landscape. They now house a bungee jumping operation (the first urban bungee jump in the world, from the bridge between the two towers), a climbing wall, and an entertainment complex at ground level.

The bungee jump (65 metres) is one of the most exhilarating in South Africa, offering a view directly into the bowl of the cooling towers. The surrounding complex has a market, restaurants, and regular events. Even if you don't jump, the towers are worth seeing as a piece of industrial reuse and a statement about Soweto's energy and reinvention.
