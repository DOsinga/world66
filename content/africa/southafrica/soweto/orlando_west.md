Orlando West is the most historically significant neighbourhood in Soweto — and arguably in South Africa. This is where the Hector Pieterson Museum and Memorial stand, where Mandela lived on Vilakazi Street, and where Archbishop Desmond Tutu made his home. The street names here carry the full weight of the anti-apartheid struggle.

Beyond the memorial sites, Orlando West is a pleasant, established neighbourhood with tree-lined streets and houses that reflect the range of Soweto society, from simple workers' cottages to more substantial middle-class homes. Vilakazi Street, lined with restaurants and craft shops, is now a tourist strip — but turn a corner in any direction and you are back in an ordinary, working neighbourhood. That contrast is part of the point.
