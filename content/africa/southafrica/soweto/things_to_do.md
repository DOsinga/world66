---
title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Soweto's attractions divide into the historical apartheid-era memorial sites concentrated in Orlando West, and the living culture of the township — its restaurants, shebeens, township tours, football stadium, and the extraordinary art on the Orlando Towers. The two make for very different but complementary visits: one about memory and resistance, the other about the energy of the city Soweto has become.

Most visitors take a guided tour that combines the main memorial sites. This provides historical context and ensures local guides benefit from tourism. A half-day covers the core sights; a full day allows for meals and deeper exploration.
