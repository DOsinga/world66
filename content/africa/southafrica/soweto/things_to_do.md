title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Soweto's main attractions are its apartheid-era memorial sites, concentrated in the [Orlando West](/africa/southafrica/soweto/orlando_west) neighbourhood. Most visitors take a guided tour — either walking or by bicycle — which provides essential context and supports local guides. The key sites can be covered in a half day: the [Hector Pieterson Museum](/africa/southafrica/soweto/hector_pieterson_museum), [Mandela House](/africa/southafrica/soweto/mandela_house), and the [Tutu House](/africa/southafrica/soweto/tutu_house) on [Vilakazi Street](/africa/southafrica/soweto/vilakazi_street). Further afield, [Regina Mundi Church](/africa/southafrica/soweto/regina_mundi_church) and the [Orlando Towers](/africa/southafrica/soweto/orlando_towers) are worth adding if you have a full day.
