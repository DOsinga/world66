The Tutu House on Vilakazi Street is the home where Archbishop Desmond Tutu lived during his years as one of South Africa's most prominent anti-apartheid voices. The house is notable for its position on the same street as Mandela's house — making Vilakazi Street, in Orlando West, arguably the most historically significant street in South Africa.

The house is not a museum but can be viewed from the street. Desmond Tutu, who won the Nobel Peace Prize in 1984 for his non-violent resistance to apartheid and his advocacy of peaceful transition, used this home as his base during the most turbulent years of the 1980s. His role in the Truth and Reconciliation Commission after the transition to democracy further cemented his place in history. Visiting Vilakazi Street gives a tangible sense of the remarkable concentration of resistance that this single neighbourhood sustained.
