Walter Sisulu Square marks the place where the Congress of the People adopted the Freedom Charter in 1955, setting out a vision of equal rights that became central to the anti-apartheid struggle. The square is named for Walter Sisulu, one of the ANC leaders later imprisoned on Robben Island.

The site is most meaningful with some context, but it belongs on any Soweto route focused on Mandela, the ANC, and the long fight against apartheid.
