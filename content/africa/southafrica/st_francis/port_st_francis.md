Port St Francis keeps the town from feeling like a purely holiday-home coast. Squid boats work from the harbour, and the quayside has a practical, salty edge that contrasts with the white canal houses inland.

It is a good place to pause for lunch, watch boats, and get a sense of the local economy beyond beach leisure. The harbour is also the departure point for some fishing and sea trips when conditions allow.
