Seal Point is the surf name most travellers hear around Cape St Francis. The break sits near the lighthouse, with clean lines when the swell and wind behave, and a coastal setting that is enjoyable even if you never get in the water.

Conditions matter, and this is not a beginner beach by default. Non-surfers can still make the stop for the atmosphere: boards on car roofs, spray over the rocks, and a long horizon that feels properly exposed.
