Stellenbosch is the heart of South Africa's wine country, a handsome university town of Cape Dutch architecture, oak-lined streets and more than 150 wine estates within easy driving distance. It is less than an hour from [Cape Town](/africa/southafrica/capetown) and makes an easy day trip, though spending a night or two lets you properly explore the valleys.

The town centre is walkable and attractive — [Dorp Street](/africa/southafrica/stellenbosch/dorp_street) is one of the finest historic streetscapes in South Africa, with whitewashed gabled buildings, wine bars, and galleries shaded by oak trees. The [Village Museum](/africa/southafrica/stellenbosch/stellenbosch_museum) is one of the best local history museums in the country. The [university botanical garden](/africa/southafrica/stellenbosch/stellenbosch_university_botanical_garden) is a quiet green space in the town centre.

The [wine route](/africa/southafrica/stellenbosch/wine_estates) stretches into the surrounding [Jonkershoek Valley](/africa/southafrica/stellenbosch/jonkershoek_valley) and Simonsberg valleys, where you can taste some of the country's best reds — particularly Pinotage and Cabernet Sauvignon. Autumn (March to May) is the most beautiful time to visit, when the vineyards turn red and gold and the harvest is underway.
