---
title: Eating Out
type: section
---

Stellenbosch has one of the best restaurant scenes in South Africa outside Cape Town, driven by the steady flow of wine tourists and a prosperous university town population. The best places are generally either on or near Dorp Street or on wine estates a short drive from town.

**Overture** at Hidden Valley estate is consistently rated among South Africa's top ten restaurants — modern wine-country cuisine with estate-grown produce and an exceptional view. Booking well in advance is essential. **Terroir at Kleine Zalze** is similarly serious and similarly booked up. For something more casual in town, **Wijnhuis** on Church Street has a good wine list (the whole point is the selection by the glass) and solid bistro cooking. **Mozaiek** at Babylonstoren farm, about 20 kilometres from town, is worth the drive for lunch — the gardens are spectacular.

For coffee and cake, the **Deli at Spier** wine estate is reliable and has good outdoor seating. **Schoon** on Dorp Street is the spot for sourdough and excellent coffee.
