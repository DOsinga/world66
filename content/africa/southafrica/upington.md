Upington is a green interruption in a very dry part of South Africa. The Orange River runs through town, feeding vineyards, date palms, gardens, and a strip of shade before the road pushes back into the Kalahari.

The town is not beautiful in a polished way, but it has a strong local rhythm. Start at the [Kalahari-Oranje Museum](/africa/southafrica/upington/kalahari_oranje_museum), housed in the old mission station, then follow the river mood through the palms, wine cellars, and a drink on [Sakkie se Arkie](/africa/southafrica/upington/sakkie_se_arkie).

Upington also works as a base for the wider Green Kalahari. [Spitskop Nature Reserve](/africa/southafrica/upington/spitskop_nature_reserve) is close enough for a short drive among desert-adapted wildlife, while Augrabies Falls and the Kgalagadi are the longer, bigger prizes west and north.
