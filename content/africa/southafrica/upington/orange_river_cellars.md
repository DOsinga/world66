Orange River Cellars gives Upington one of its most surprising travel angles: wine made along the edge of the Kalahari. The cooperative draws from vineyards irrigated by the Orange River, where grapes, raisins, and wine are part of the same agricultural story.

Come for a tasting rather than a grand Cape Winelands fantasy. The pleasure is in understanding how much green life this river supports in a hard, hot landscape.
