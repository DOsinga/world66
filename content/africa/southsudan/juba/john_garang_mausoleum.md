The John Garang Mausoleum is Juba's most important political landmark. Garang led the Sudan People's Liberation Movement through decades of war and died in a helicopter crash in 2005, before South Sudan became independent.

The site is guarded and access can vary, but even seeing the square and statue gives context for the country around you. This is not grand tourism; it is a place where a young state tells its founding story.
