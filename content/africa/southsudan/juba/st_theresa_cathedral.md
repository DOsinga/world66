St Theresa Cathedral is one of Juba's clearest old landmarks, built in 1952 and later made the cathedral of the Roman Catholic Archdiocese of Juba. Its value is not just architectural; churches have been places of refuge and public life through South Sudan's long conflicts.

Visit respectfully and with local advice. Services and security conditions matter more here than any sightseeing timetable.
