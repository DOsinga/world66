The White Nile is Juba's best natural anchor. From the riverside you get a softer view of the city: water, boats, birds, and the sense that this dusty capital is tied into a much larger African geography.

Short boat trips can sometimes be arranged, but treat them as locally organised outings rather than a fixed tourist product. Safety, weather, and the reliability of the operator matter.
