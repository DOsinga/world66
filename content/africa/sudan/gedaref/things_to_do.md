---
title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Gedaref is not a city you visit for monuments. It is a working agricultural and trading town on the eastern plains, and what is worth seeing is bound up with that life — the grain market, the surrounding farmland, and the unusual mix of peoples who built the place. Wander the market in the cool early morning, drink tea where the traders drink it, and head out into the Butana for a sense of what feeds Sudan.
