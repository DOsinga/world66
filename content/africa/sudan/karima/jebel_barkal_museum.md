The Jebel Barkal Museum is modest, but it helps anchor the ruins in a wider story. If it is open, start here before walking the temples: a little context goes a long way when the landscape outside is mostly broken stone and sand.

Opening conditions can be irregular, especially during periods of unrest. Ask locally rather than building a tight schedule around it.
