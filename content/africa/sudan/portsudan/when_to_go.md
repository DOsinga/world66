---
title: When to Go
type: section
---

The Red Sea diving is best between October and May, when visibility is highest and surface conditions are calmest. June through September brings heat, wind, and reduced visibility — liveaboard operators tend to pause operations in summer.

Port Sudan is hot year-round, with temperatures regularly exceeding 40°C in summer. Winter (December to February) is the most comfortable time for any land activity.

**Important:** As of 2024, Sudan is experiencing an active civil war. Travel is effectively impossible for most international visitors and is not recommended. Monitor the situation through your government's travel advisory service before making any plans.
