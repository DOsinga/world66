The Royal Swazi Spa is an entertainment complex that has been the valley's main upmarket destination since the 1980s. It contains a casino, an 18-hole golf course, a spa, and several restaurants — a combination that made it unusual in the region when it was built, and that still draws visitors from Johannesburg looking for a weekend across the border.

The casino operates daily and attracts a mix of South African tourists and locals. The golf course is well maintained and playable year-round; visitors can pay to use it by the round. The spa offers standard treatments at prices lower than equivalent facilities in South Africa. It is worth knowing about as a facility if you need it, though it represents a different Eswatini from the wildlife sanctuary and the craft markets.
