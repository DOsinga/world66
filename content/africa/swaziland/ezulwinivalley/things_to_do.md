---
title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

The Ezulwini Valley is not a single attraction but a corridor of them, strung along the MR103 road between Mbabane and Lobamba. The highlights are the wildlife sanctuary, the Mantenga cultural site and falls, the royal residences, and the craft markets. A car makes it far more usable — the distances are manageable but the roadside attractions are spread out.
