King Sobhuza II Memorial Park honours the king who led Swaziland, now Eswatini, through independence and ruled until 1982. The grounds include a glass mausoleum on the spot where his body lay in state and a small museum about his reign.

It is close to Lobamba's other national institutions, so it fits naturally with the National Museum and Ludzidzini Royal Village.
