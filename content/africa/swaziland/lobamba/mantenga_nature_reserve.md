Mantenga Nature Reserve sits in the Ezulwini Valley below steep grassland ridges, close enough to Lobamba to be an easy half-day. The main natural draw is Mantenga Falls, reached by a short walk through riverine forest, with views up toward the surrounding hills.

The reserve is often combined with the nearby cultural village, where dance performances introduce visitors to Swazi traditions in a staged but still useful way. Come for the mix: a bit of walking, a waterfall, valley scenery, and cultural context without a long drive.
