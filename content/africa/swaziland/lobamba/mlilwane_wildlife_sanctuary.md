Mlilwane Wildlife Sanctuary is one of the easiest and most rewarding nature escapes in the Ezulwini Valley. Because there are no big cats in the main visitor areas, you can walk, cycle, or ride through open country with zebra, wildebeest, warthog, antelope, and prolific birdlife around you.

The sanctuary has a gentler feel than the big-game parks farther east, and that is its strength. Spend a few hours on the trails, climb to a viewpoint if the weather is clear, and linger for the sense of space that Lobamba itself only hints at.
