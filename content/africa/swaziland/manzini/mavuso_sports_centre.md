The Mavuso Sports Centre is a multi-use venue that doubles as the country's main events facility for trade shows, large gatherings, and sporting competitions. The main stadium holds several thousand people and is used for football, athletics, and national events. It is the site of the annual Swaziland Trade Fair, held in August, which is one of the biggest commercial events in the country.

For visitors, the Trade Fair in August is the main reason to seek the venue out; at other times it functions as a conventional sports ground and is only worth a visit if there is a specific event scheduled.
