Indingilizi Gallery is the best place in Mbabane to see and buy contemporary Swazi art and craft. Founded in 1982, it occupies a converted building near the city centre and carries a thoughtfully curated selection of paintings, sculpture, weaving, woodwork, and ceramics — a genuine cross-section of what Swazi artists are producing.

The gallery's owners have long championed the idea that traditional Swazi craft is art, not souvenir, and the curation reflects that. Pieces range from affordable prints to more substantial works by established artists. This is a much better place to buy a meaningful souvenir than any of the craft markets, and the quality difference is obvious. Even if you are not buying, it is worth half an hour to browse.

The gallery is small and the stock changes regularly. Opening hours can be irregular on weekends — it is safest to aim for weekday mornings.
