---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

Mbabane's highland location (1,200 metres) means the climate is milder than most of southern Africa. Summers (November to March) are warm and wet — temperatures reach the mid-20s Celsius, and afternoon thunderstorms are common. This is also when the surrounding hills are greenest. Winters (June to August) are dry and can be cold, especially at night, with occasional frosts. Snowfall at this altitude is rare but not impossible.

The best time to visit is in the dry season (April to September), when the weather is clear and pleasant and the roads to the nature reserves are most reliable. September and October bring spring wildflowers to the surrounding grasslands. The Umhlanga ceremony (Reed Dance), usually held in late August or early September, draws tens of thousands of people to the royal residence at Ludzidzini — a spectacular cultural event if you happen to be in the country at the right time.
