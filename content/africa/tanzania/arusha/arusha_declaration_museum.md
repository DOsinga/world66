The Arusha Declaration Museum explains the 1967 declaration that set Tanzania on a path toward Ujamaa, Julius Nyerere's version of African socialism. It gives useful context for the monument nearby and for the politics that shaped Tanzania after independence.

The museum is small, but it is one of the better stops in central Arusha for travellers interested in modern East African history.
