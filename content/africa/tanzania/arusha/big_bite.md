Big Bite, on Sokoine Road, is one of Arusha's most reliable lunch stops — a local fast-food operation serving nyama choma (grilled meat), chips, and cold drinks at very reasonable prices. It is the kind of place where Arusha residents actually eat, not just tourists, which keeps the food honest and the portions substantial.

The grilled chicken and beef are the best options; order by weight and point to what you want. The ugali (maize porridge) served alongside is the staple starch — try it with a meat sauce or sukuma wiki (braised greens). Seating is basic but plentiful, and the turnover is fast. Budget about 5-8 USD for a full meal with a drink.
