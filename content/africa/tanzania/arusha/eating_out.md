---
title: "Eating Out"
type: section
---

Arusha has a range of eating options for a Tanzanian city. The expat and safari crowd supports several international restaurants around Boma Road. Indian food is excellent — reflecting Tanzania's South Asian population — with curries, samosas, and chapati available at [Khan's Barbecue](/africa/tanzania/arusha/the_chinese_restaurant) and similar spots around Mosque Street and India Street at very low prices. [Via Via](/africa/tanzania/arusha/via_via) in the gardens behind the Natural History Museum is reliable for both local and European dishes, and doubles as the main social hub for travellers. For local food at near-nothing prices, [Big Bite](/africa/tanzania/arusha/big_bite) on Sokoine Road serves nyama choma, ugali, and grilled chicken at budget prices.
