Triniti occupies a three-storey block on Toure Drive — a restaurant on the ground floor, a long bar and pool tables on the first, an enclosed dance club on the top. Weekday evenings the place ticks over as a neighbourhood bar; Friday and Saturday the top floor turns into one of the busier dance venues in Dar, with a mix of bongo flava, Afrobeats, and house from local DJs.

Door policy is informal but smart-casual is the safest bet, and prices climb the higher up the building you go. Bajaji rather than walking after dark; the road is busy with traffic but unpleasant for pedestrians late at night.
