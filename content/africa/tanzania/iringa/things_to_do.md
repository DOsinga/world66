title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Iringa itself can be walked in an afternoon: the market, [Neema Crafts](/africa/tanzania/iringa/neema_crafts), and the climb up [Gangilonga Rock](/africa/tanzania/iringa/gangilonga_rock) for the view. The real reasons to stop here are within an easy day's drive: the [Isimila Stone Age Site](/africa/tanzania/iringa/isimila_stone_age_site) and [Kalenga](/africa/tanzania/iringa/kalenga), where the Hehe chief Mkwawa held off the Germans for seven years before being beheaded.
