---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

The dry season from June to October is the best time to visit. Roads into the rock art sites and across to the Haubi badlands become difficult or impassable during the long rains (March to May) and can be treacherous in the short rains (November to December). The main road north to Babati and south to Dodoma is also affected, and bus cancellations are common in heavy downpour periods.

The rock paintings are best visited in the cooler dry-season months — walking between sites under a June sky is pleasant; doing so in February heat is not. Temperatures on the Central Plateau are moderate compared to the coast: Kondoa sits at around 1,200 metres and nights can be cold from June to August, dropping to around 10°C.

There are no significant festivals or events that would make one time of year preferable to another from a cultural perspective. Market days in the surrounding villages follow a weekly rotation, and asking locals which village has its market on a given day can add an interesting dimension to a longer stay.
