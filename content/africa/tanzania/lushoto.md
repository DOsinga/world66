**Lushoto** is a small town in the Usambara Mountains of northeastern [Tanzania](/africa/tanzania), set in a green, hilly landscape at about 1,400 metres. It makes a refreshing break from the heat of the coast — the air is cool, the scenery lush, and the hiking excellent. The town was a German colonial hill station under the name Wilhelmstal, and the [Lutheran church](/africa/tanzania/lushoto/st_eugene_lutheran) on the main street is one of several reminders of the period.

The Western Usambaras around Lushoto offer walks through montane rainforest, past waterfalls, and through villages where people farm the steep hillsides. The classic short walk is out to the [Irente Viewpoint](/africa/tanzania/lushoto/irente_viewpoint), where the escarpment drops a thousand metres to the Maasai plains; the longer hikes head north through [Magamba Forest](/africa/tanzania/lushoto/magamba_forest) and on to the ridge-top village of [Mtae](/africa/tanzania/lushoto/mtae_village). Several local guides run ecological tours that combine hiking with community visits.

Lushoto is about an hour north of Mombo, roughly halfway between [Dar es Salaam](/africa/tanzania/daressalaam) and the northern safari circuit. Time a visit around the big [market](/africa/tanzania/lushoto/lushoto_market) days, eat lunch at [Irente Farm Lodge](/africa/tanzania/lushoto/irente_farm_lodge), and detour down to [Soni Falls](/africa/tanzania/lushoto/soni_falls) on the way back to the highway.
