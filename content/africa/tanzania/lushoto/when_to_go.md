---
title: When to Go
type: section
---

The dry season — June to September — is the best time: cool but not cold (10–22 °C), clear views, dry trails. The short rains in November can bring brief afternoon storms but the landscape is at its greenest then. The long rains from late March through May make many of the forest tracks impassable.

Pack a sweater; nights at 1,400 metres are sharper than visitors expect, and the local hotels do not always have hot water.
