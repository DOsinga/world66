---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

**October to March** is the prime season. This is when whale sharks congregate in Chole Bay, the sea is calm, and the rains are minimal. December through February is peak season; book flights and lodges months in advance.

**April and May** bring the long rains (masika). Many lodges close, flights become unreliable, and the seas can be rough. Avoid this period.

**June to September** is the short dry season. The whale sharks have typically moved on, but diving conditions can be excellent — calm seas, good visibility, and fewer visitors. This is the best time to come if marine life other than whale sharks is your priority, and prices are lower.

**November** is increasingly popular: the whale sharks have arrived, prices are not yet at peak, and the island is relatively quiet.
