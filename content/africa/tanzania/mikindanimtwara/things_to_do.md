Mikindani-Mtwara rewards explorers willing to make the effort to reach it. The Old Boma is the standout sight — a restored German colonial fort with an extraordinary Livingstone connection. The old town of Mikindani is one of the best-preserved Swahili coastal townscapes on the Tanzanian coast. And offshore, the Mnazi Bay Marine Park offers some of the least-visited diving in East Africa.
