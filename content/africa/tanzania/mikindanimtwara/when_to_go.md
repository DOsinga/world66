---
title: When to Go
type: section
tags: [when_to_go]
---

The southern Tanzanian coast has two rainy seasons: a long rains from March to May and shorter rains in November. The dry season from June to October is the best time to visit — humidity drops, the roads are passable, and dive visibility in Mnazi Bay is at its best.

July and August are peak months for clear water and comfortable temperatures. The shoulder months of June and October are also good. Avoid the long rains if you're planning to travel overland — some roads can become impassable.
