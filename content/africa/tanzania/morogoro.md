Morogoro sits on the main inland road and rail line from [Dar es Salaam](/africa/tanzania/daressalaam), but it is more than a place to change buses. The city lies hard against the green wall of the [Uluguru Mountains](/africa/tanzania/morogoro/uluguru_mountains), one of the Eastern Arc ranges, and the slopes begin close enough that a day hike can start from town.

The city itself is busy, practical and unpolished. The [Chief Kingalu Market](/africa/tanzania/morogoro/chief_kingalu_market) is the best place to feel Morogoro at street level, while the [Hindu Mandal Temple](/africa/tanzania/morogoro/hindu_mandal_temple) hints at the Indian Ocean trading world that reaches inland along Tanzania's transport routes.

Morogoro works best for travellers who like a real town around them: daladalas, produce markets, university students, and mountains at the end of the street. It is also a good soft landing before heading deeper into central or southern Tanzania.
