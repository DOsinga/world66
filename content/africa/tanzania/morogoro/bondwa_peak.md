Bondwa Peak is one of the classic Uluguru day hikes from Morogoro. The summit is high enough to feel like a proper climb, but close enough to town that fit walkers can do it as a long day with a guide.

Clouds often build around the ridge, so start early. In clear weather the reward is a wide view over Morogoro and the plains beyond; in mist, the forest itself becomes the point.
