---
title: "Bars and Cafes"
type: section
---

Mwanza is not a nightlife town in the Dar es Salaam sense, but the lakeshore comes alive in the evenings. The **Mwanza Yacht Club** bar on Capripoint is the standard sundowner spot for the international community: cold beer, simple food, a swimming dock, and an unobstructed view of the lake turning pink at six. Day visitors pay a small fee.

In the city centre, the bars around the **Tilapia Hotel** and along **Kenyatta Road** are busier, louder, and more local: bottles of Kilimanjaro and Serengeti lager, *bongo flava* on the speakers, and crowds spilling out onto the pavement. **Bulldog Pub** in the centre is the long-running expat bar with a pool table and televised football. For coffee, a handful of cafes in the centre — including the small **Salma** chain — do reasonable espresso and pastries.

The largest weekend club nights happen at venues around **Nyakato** north of town; Friday and Saturday they run until very late. Take a taxi back to your hotel — the central streets are not a place to walk alone at night.
