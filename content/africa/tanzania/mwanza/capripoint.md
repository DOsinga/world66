Capripoint is the rounded peninsula that pokes out into Lake Victoria immediately south of central Mwanza. The peninsula has long been the city's most desirable residential address — Indian-owned houses, German colonial bungalows under the trees, and a few discreet hotels. The lakeshore road around the peninsula is the most pleasant walk in the city, particularly in the late afternoon when the heat eases.

The **Mwanza Yacht Club** sits towards the tip of the peninsula and welcomes day visitors for a modest fee — its lakeshore bar is one of the city's standard sundowner spots, and it has a small swimming dock. From the Yacht Club's beach there is a clear view across to Saanane Island and the boats coming in and out of the harbour. There are no formal sights as such; the appeal is the texture and the air, both noticeably cleaner here than in the dusty downtown.
