The two obvious targets from Mwanza are very different. **Bukoba**, on the western shore of Lake Victoria, can be reached in a day by air but is much better as an overnight on the **MV Victoria** ferry, returning the following night. The **western corridor of the Serengeti** — entered at the Ndabaka Gate about three hours east of Mwanza — gives access to a less-visited part of the park and to the river crossings of the great migration in May to July.

Closer to the city, the **Bujora Sukuma Museum** at Kisesa is a half-day rather than a day. **Saanane Island National Park** is half a day. The lake fishing villages around **Igombe** and **Nyamatala** to the south of the city are quiet and pleasant for a longer half-day with a hired car.
