---
title: "Getting Around"
type: section
---

Central Mwanza is walkable but spread out, and the climate makes long walks at midday unpleasant. The standard local transport is the **dala-dala** — small minibus, fixed routes, very cheap — and **bajaji** (auto-rickshaw), which work well for short hops in the centre and to Capripoint. **Boda-boda** motorbike taxis are faster but less safe and best avoided unless you are confident on the back.

Standard **taxis** are easy to find at the airport, the bus stand, and outside the larger hotels; agree the price before getting in. For tourists, a half-day with a hired car and driver from the hotel is the most efficient way to combine Bujora, the fish market, and Saanane in a single outing.

For Saanane Island and other lake destinations, **hire boats** are arranged at the small jetty by the National Park office. There is no formal water taxi system across the lake within the city.
