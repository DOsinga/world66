---
title: "Getting There"
type: section
---

**By air:** Mwanza Airport (MWZ) is about 10 km north of the city centre and is the main reason most travellers actually reach Mwanza. **Precision Air**, **Air Tanzania**, and **Auric Air** run multiple daily flights to [Dar es Salaam](/africa/tanzania/daressalaam) (about 90 minutes), and there are regular services to [Arusha](/africa/tanzania/arusha), Kilimanjaro, [Bukoba](/africa/tanzania/bukoba), and seasonal connections to Entebbe in Uganda. Taxis to town cost about $10.

**By bus:** Buses connect Mwanza to most major Tanzanian cities but the journeys are long and rough. Dar es Salaam is around 18 hours, Arusha around 14 hours via the southern route, [Moshi](/africa/tanzania/moshi) around 16 hours. **Kampala Coach** and other operators run cross-border services to Kampala in Uganda (around 12 hours), passing the western shore of the lake. Bukoba is reachable by a long road journey or — better — by the **MV Victoria** boat (see below).

**By boat:** The classic crossing of Lake Victoria to Bukoba on the **MV Victoria** runs overnight several times a week. Cabins are basic but adequate, and the passage at sunrise — with the lake mist clearing as you approach the western shore — is one of the most atmospheric journeys in East Africa. Buy tickets at the port a day or two in advance.

**By road from the Serengeti:** Mwanza's airport is the easiest access point for the western corridor of the [Serengeti](/africa/tanzania/serengeti). The Ndabaka Gate is about 145 km east of the city, around three hours by road.
