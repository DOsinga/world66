---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

Mwanza sits just south of the equator and only a little above sea level, so it is warm all year — daytime temperatures around 27–30°C, nights mid-teens. The major variable is rain.

The **long rains** run from March through May and can be heavy enough to make some dirt roads impassable; the western corridor of the Serengeti is best avoided then. The **short rains** in November and December are lighter and rarely disruptive. The **dry season from June through September** is the most comfortable time — cool, sunny, low humidity — and overlaps with the wildebeest migration crossings in the western corridor that bring most safari travellers through Mwanza. October is still pleasant and slightly cheaper. January and February are hot and dry and the lake water level drops, exposing more of the shore.
