Elephant Cave is a short but rewarding hike near the Ngorongoro Conservation Area, where elephants have carved mineral-rich earth from the cave walls over generations. The result is part wildlife site, part small cultural landmark, with a trail that climbs through forest and open slopes used by animals coming to lick the salty soil.

The loop takes about two and a half hours and gains around 500 metres, so it is more of a walk with a workout than a quick photo stop. Go with a local guide, carry water, and treat it as a good half-day break from vehicle-based safari days.
