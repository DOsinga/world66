Empakaai Crater is a remote volcanic caldera in the Ngorongoro Conservation Area, with steep green walls dropping to a salt lake. The colour of the lake shifts with the light, and guided hikes into the crater offer a quieter contrast to the main Ngorongoro safari circuit.
