---
title: Things to Do
type: section
---

Njombe is more useful as a base than a destination in its own right. The town itself has a couple of mission churches, a Saturday market, and excellent cool air. The proper attractions are around it: the **tea and wattle plantations** that wrap the plateau, the **Kitulo National Park** wildflower fields three hours away by car, and the **Lupembe escarpment** with its steep drop down into the Kilombero plain.
