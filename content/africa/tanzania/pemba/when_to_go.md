---
title: When to Go
type: section
---

Pemba has two rainy seasons: the long rains from late March through May and the shorter masika from November through early December. The long rains are heavy and can shut down the dirt roads and the smaller ferry services for days at a time — avoid April unless your plans are flexible.

The dry months from June through October are the right window, with cooler nights, calm seas, and the best diving visibility. January and February are also fine: hot and dry, with the highest water temperatures of the year for diving. Avoid Ramadan if you can — most restaurants close during the day and the towns slow noticeably.
