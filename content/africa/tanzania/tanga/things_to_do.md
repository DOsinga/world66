---
title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Tanga is a slow town and the sights match that pace. A walk through the colonial-era streets, an hour at the **Tanga Yacht Club** for a sundowner, a half-day at the **Amboni Caves** outside town, and a longer trip out to the **Tongoni Ruins** down the coast covers the city's headline attractions. Boat trips into the bay to **Toten Island** (Tanga Island) are an obvious add-on.
