Shume is a small town in the heart of the Western Usambara Mountains, surrounded by a large government forest plantation mixed with remnant natural forest. The Shume-Magamba Forest Reserve, one of the largest protected forest areas in the Western Usambaras, begins here. Walking trails through both plantation and natural forest give access to some of the best remaining wildlife habitat in the range.

The Shume area is known to birders for the Usambara eagle-owl — a rare endemic owl that roosts in the natural forest sections. The walk from Lushoto to Shume and back (or continuing over the ridge) is a classic full-day route that passes through four or five distinct vegetation zones.
