---
title: When to Go
type: section
---

The Usambara Mountains have two rainy seasons: the long rains from March to May and shorter rains in November. During the rains the landscape is lush and green but the paths can be slippery, and cloud frequently obscures the escarpment views. The forests are most alive in the rains.

The dry seasons (June to October and December to February) offer the most reliable walking weather. June to October is the main tourist season, though even then the mountains are uncrowded by East African standards. Temperatures are pleasant year-round — cool evenings at altitude even in summer.

The combination of Usambara Mountains with the Tanzanian northern safari circuit works well: the mountains sit between Dar es Salaam and the Moshi/Arusha area, making a break in Lushoto a natural addition to either direction.
