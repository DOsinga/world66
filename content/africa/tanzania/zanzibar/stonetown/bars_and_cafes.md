---
title: "Bars and Cafes"
type: section
---

Zanzibar is officially Muslim and alcohol is not sold openly on the streets, but the tourist hotels and a handful of dedicated bars pour freely. The classic sundowner is at **Africa House Hotel terrace** on the Shangani waterfront — the old English Club, with a wraparound first-floor terrace that catches the sunset over the dhow harbour.

**6 Degrees South**, two doors along, is more modern: a tapas-style bar with the same view at a slightly higher price. **Mercury's** on Mizingani Road, named for the local hero, is the after-dinner option, with live music most evenings and tables on the seawall facing Forodhani. For coffee and breakfast, head to **Stone Town Cafe** near Christ Church or **Mercury's** in the morning before it switches to evening trade.

Local **Konyagi** (sugarcane gin) and **Kilimanjaro** beer are the staples; a small selection of South African wines is available at the better restaurants.
