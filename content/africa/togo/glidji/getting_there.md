---
title: "Getting There"
type: section
---

Glidji is about 4 km north of [Aného](/africa/togo/aneho) on a short paved road and is easily reached on foot, by motorbike taxi (zem), or by shared taxi. From [Lomé](/africa/togo/lome), it is roughly 45 km east along the coast road towards the Benin border — about an hour by bush taxi or hired car, with the turn-off to Glidji a few hundred metres before Aného.

During the Epe-Ekpe festival in September the route is jammed with pilgrims and traffic; allow extra time and consider basing yourself in Aného rather than trying to reach Glidji on the day.
