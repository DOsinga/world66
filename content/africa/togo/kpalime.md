Kpalimé sits in the coffee and cocoa-growing hills of western Togo, about two hours northwest of [Lomé](/africa/togo/lome). The surrounding landscape is the greenest and most scenic in the country — lush hills, waterfalls, and dense vegetation that feels a world away from the dry Sahelian north.

The area is ideal for hiking. [Mount Agou](/africa/togo/kpalime/mount_agou), Togo's highest peak at 986 metres, is just 20 km away and can be climbed in a half day. The villages around [Klouto](/africa/togo/kpalime/klouto_forest), north of town, are the starting point for walks through forest full of butterflies and birdsong — guides are easy to find (or they find you). [Kpimé Falls](/africa/togo/kpalime/kpime_falls) is the most accessible of several waterfalls in the hills.

In town, the [market](/africa/togo/kpalime/marche_kpalime) on Rue du Marché is worth a visit, and the [Centre Artisanal](/africa/togo/kpalime/centre_artisanal) about 5 km north on the road to Klouto sells locally made batik cloth, wood carvings, and other crafts. On the same road, the colonial-era folly [Château Viale](/africa/togo/kpalime/chateau_viale) is one of West Africa's stranger landmarks. Kpalimé has a relaxed, friendly atmosphere and enough simple guesthouses and restaurants to make it a comfortable base for exploring the region.
