Musée International du Golfe de Guinée is a small beachside museum with a strong collection of West African art and ceremonial objects. The displays include masks, carved chairs, funerary objects, and older pieces from across the Gulf of Guinea region.

It sits a little outside the centre, so it works best for travellers who already have an interest in regional art or who want a quieter counterpoint to Lomé's markets. Check locally for the current name and opening hours, as the museum has also been listed as the Musée International Art d'Afrique.
