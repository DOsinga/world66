---
title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Lomé is not packed with formal tourist attractions, but what it has is genuinely interesting. The Akodéssewa Fetish Market is the main draw for visitors, a window into voodoo traditions that is unlike anything you will find elsewhere in West Africa. The Togo National Museum offers a quieter look at Togolese culture and history. The Grand Marché is the city's commercial heart, run by the legendary Nana Benz; the seafront Boulevard du Mono is the most agreeable promenade in any West African capital. Beyond that, much of the pleasure in Lomé comes from simply walking the streets — the lived-in Ewe quarter of Bè around its sacred lagoon, the busy maquis of Dékon — and from watching the Atlantic break along the wide city beach.
