---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

Lomé sits on the Gulf of Guinea coast and is hot and humid year-round. The most pleasant months are **November to February**, when the harmattan brings a few weeks of dry, slightly hazy air, daytime highs in the low 30s and cool nights. **March to May** is hot and increasingly humid; **June and July** are the main rainy season, with afternoon downpours that flood the unpaved streets but rarely last long.

The biggest event of the year is the **Festival Filbleu** in March, the most important African film festival in the country. **Independence Day** on 27 April brings parades on Place de l'Indépendance. **Epé Ekpé**, the traditional Guin new year, is celebrated in [Aného](/africa/togo/aneho) in early September and is worth a day trip from Lomé.
