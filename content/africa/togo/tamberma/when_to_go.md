---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

The dry season — November through March — is the comfortable time to visit. Tracks are passable, the harmattan haze gives the landscape a softer light, and the millet harvest is over so the compounds are at their fullest. December and January nights can be surprisingly cool; bring a layer. The rainy season (June to September) makes the dirt roads difficult and many compounds are closed up; avoid.
