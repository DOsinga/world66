Béja's best argument as a stop is its position halfway between two of Tunisia's great Roman sites. **Bulla Regia**, with its astonishing underground villas, lies 50 km west. **Dougga**, the spectacular hilltop Roman city often called the best-preserved in North Africa, lies 50 km south. Either can be done as a half-day trip; both together make a full day. A car or a hired taxi for the day is the practical way to manage it — public transport between the sites is patchy and slow.
