---
title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Bulla Regia is a single archaeological site rather than a town with multiple districts. The visit consists of exploring the above-ground ruins — the forum, temples, and baths — and descending into the underground villas that are the site's unique feature. Allow two to three hours for a thorough visit. There is no shade once you leave the underground rooms, so visit early in the day during summer months and bring water.
