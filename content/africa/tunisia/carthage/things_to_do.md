---
title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Carthage's archaeological sites are spread out — the whole UNESCO-listed area covers several square kilometres of suburban Tunis. The standard approach is to buy the multi-site ticket at any of the main entrances (Byrsa, the Antonine Baths, the Tophet, the Punic Ports) and tackle them as a half-day or full-day circuit.

For a focused half-day: start at the **Byrsa Hill** and **Carthage National Museum** for context, walk down to the **Antonine Baths** for the most photogenic ruins, then drop south to the **Punic Ports** and **Tophet** for the city's most unsettling history. A full day adds the **Amphitheatre**, **Circus**, **Cisterns of La Malga**, and the **Damous El Karita** and **Saint-Cyprien** basilicas.

Finish with **Sidi Bou Said** on the cliff just to the north — the blue-and-white village is touristy but genuinely beautiful, and a cold drink at one of the cliff-top cafés is the ideal way to end a hot day among the ruins.
