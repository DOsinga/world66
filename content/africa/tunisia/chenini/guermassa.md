Guermassa is an abandoned hilltop village crowned by a ruined ksar, with wide views across the valleys near Chenini. It takes more effort than the better-known ksour, but the quiet setting and ridge-top position make it a worthwhile stop for travellers exploring the Tataouine hill villages by car or on foot.
