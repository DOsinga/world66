---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

Southern Tunisia is hot. In July and August, temperatures on the ridge at Chenini can reach 40°C or more, and the exposed rock amplifies the heat. The walk to the mosque becomes genuinely unpleasant in the middle of the day during high summer — though early mornings are still manageable if you start before 8am.

The best months are October to April. Spring (March and April) is particularly good: comfortable temperatures, occasional wildflowers in the rocky crevices, and good light for photography. Winter days are mild and sunny, though nights can be cold, and the occasional winter rain turns the track up to the village muddy.

Ramadan affects opening hours and the availability of food and drink during the day; the village is quieter and the atmosphere more contemplative, which some visitors prefer. Avoid major Tunisian public holidays if you dislike crowds — Chenini is a popular domestic day trip from Tataouine and from the coastal resorts.
