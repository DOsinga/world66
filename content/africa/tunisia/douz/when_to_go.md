---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

Avoid Douz in summer — July and August temperatures regularly exceed 40°C, and the heat makes any time in the desert genuinely dangerous. The ideal months are October through April, when daytime temperatures are comfortable (15–25°C) and nights are cool but not unbearably cold.

The International Festival of the Sahara in late November or December is the peak event, and the town fills up significantly. Otherwise, spring and autumn see the most visitors. Winter is quiet and the desert nights can drop below freezing — bring warm layers if sleeping under the stars.
