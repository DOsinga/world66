---
title: "Getting There"
type: section
---

Gabès is on the main coastal road and rail line between Tunis and Tunisia's south. Trains run several times a day to Tunis (around 6 hours), Sfax, and Sousse; the line currently ends at Gabès, so anything further south is by road. The station is in the centre of town.

Louages (shared taxis) are the fastest way south to **[Medenine](/africa/tunisia/medenine)** and **[Tataouine](/africa/tunisia/tataouine)**, west to **[Douz](/africa/tunisia/douz)** via Kebili, and on to the Sahara. The louage and bus stations sit on opposite sides of the city — ask before heading out. From Gabès airport just south of town there are occasional flights to Tunis, but the train and road connections are usually a better bet.
