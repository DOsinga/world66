---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

Spring (March to May) and autumn (September to November) are the right times to visit Gafsa. Temperatures sit between 18 and 28°C, the light is clear, and the city is in its normal working rhythm rather than baking under the pressure of a Saharan summer. These are also the best months for continuing south toward Tozeur and the Chott el-Jerid — the road trips that bracket a Gafsa stop are far more pleasant when you are not running the air conditioning flat out.

Summer in Gafsa is genuinely harsh. Temperatures regularly exceed 40°C, the streets empty out in the middle of the day, and visiting any outdoor site before 9am or after 5pm becomes a practical necessity rather than a preference. The Roman pools remain popular with locals precisely because the spring-fed water stays cooler than the air, but extended sightseeing in July or August is uncomfortable. Winter is mild enough — 10 to 18°C — and quiet, though some facilities in smaller surrounding towns operate on reduced hours.
