**The Monuments of the Tataouine Region** — various academic surveys of the ksour architecture of the Tunisian south exist in French; worth searching for if you read French and want detail on the individual structures and their history.

**Handbook to Life in the Medieval World** by Madeleine Pelner Cosman and Linda Gale Jones — covers Berber and North African Islamic civilisation including fortified architecture; provides useful background on the social function of the ksar tradition.

**Tunisia** by various authors (Lonely Planet, Rough Guide, or Bradt) — a practical travel guide covering the Tataouine region, ksour circuit, and logistics for the south. The Bradt guide to Tunisia has consistently strong coverage of the southern region.

**The Illustrated Star Wars Universe** by Kevin J. Anderson and Ralph McQuarrie — for the production design and location context behind the Tatooine scenes; useful for understanding why Ksar Hadada and the Tataouine region were chosen as filming locations.
