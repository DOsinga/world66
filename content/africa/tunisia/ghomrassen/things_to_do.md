title: Things to do
type: section
---

Ghomrassen rewards visitors who come for the landscape and the architecture rather than organised tourism. The town sits in a dramatic setting of eroded limestone gorges and rocky hillsides, and that landscape is as much an attraction as any single sight.

The two focal points are [Ksar Hadada](/africa/tunisia/ghomrassen/things_to_do/ksar_hadada), a well-preserved Berber fortified granary that appeared as Mos Espa in Star Wars Episode I: The Phantom Menace, and the [cliff sanctuary](/africa/tunisia/ghomrassen/things_to_do/cliff_sanctuary) perched on the rocky crags directly above the town centre. The [surrounding ksour landscape](/africa/tunisia/ghomrassen/things_to_do/ksour_landscape) extends well beyond the town itself and repays exploration if you have a vehicle.

Facilities are limited. There are no tour operators, no ticketing systems, and no queues. This is part of the appeal.
