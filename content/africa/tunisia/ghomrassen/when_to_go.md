---
title: When to go
type: section
---

October to March is the best time to visit. Temperatures in this arid inland region are comfortable during the day and cool at night; the light in winter is clear and good for photography of the gorge landscape. Spring (March–April) can be pleasant but temperatures start climbing quickly.

July and August are harsh. The desert heat pushes daytime temperatures well above 40°C, and there is no shade in the gorges. If you are travelling in summer, start early in the morning and be finished outdoors by midday.

The Tataouine region has hosted a Star Wars Film Location Festival in past years — usually in spring — which brings a temporary influx of visitors to Tataouine and the surrounding area, including Ghomrassen. Check current schedules if this is relevant to your plans.

The landscape is dramatic year-round, and the ksour do not change with the season. The main variable is whether the heat allows comfortable exploration.
