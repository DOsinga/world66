---
title: "Getting There"
type: section
---

The nearest airport is Tunis-Carthage International Airport, about 65 km north of Hammamet. Enfidha-Hammamet International Airport (Monastir) is closer at about 80 km south and handles many charter flights from Europe directly. Most package holiday operators fly into Enfidha.

From Tunis, trains run to Bir Bou Rekba (on the Sfax line), from where a change to the Hammamet local service gives access to the resort. The journey takes about 90 minutes total. Louage taxis from Tunis bus stations are faster (about 1 hour) and more convenient. From Enfidha airport, taxis or organised transfers to Hammamet take about 45 minutes.
