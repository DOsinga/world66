---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

April to June and September to October are the best months — warm enough for swimming (sea temperature 20–24°C), fewer crowds than summer, and prices noticeably lower. July and August are peak season: hot (30–35°C), crowded, and expensive. The beach is still enjoyable but the resort is overwhelmed with European package tourists.

November to March is mild by North African standards (15–20°C) but cool enough that beach holidays feel marginal. Some hotels close or reduce services in the off-season. The Medina and old town are pleasant year-round; winter is a good time for sightseeing without the heat.
