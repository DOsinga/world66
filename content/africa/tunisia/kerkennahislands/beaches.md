---
title: "Beaches"
type: section
---

The whole coastline of both islands is essentially one continuous beach — long stretches of pale sand sloping gradually into very shallow water. You can walk a hundred metres out from shore in some places before it reaches your knees.

The most popular and accessible beach is at **[Sidi Frej](/africa/tunisia/kerkennahislands/sidi_frej)**, where most of the hotels are concentrated. **Ouled Ezzeddine** on the northern coast of Chergui has a wilder feel and is often deserted. **Sidi Founkhal** in the south-east has good swimming and a few simple beach cafés in summer. None of the beaches have lifeguards; the shallow water makes most safe, but the open Mediterranean side can be choppy when the wind picks up.
