La Marsa sits at the northern end of the TGM line, and the two great day-trip destinations are a few minutes' train ride away. **[Sidi Bou Said](/africa/tunisia/sidibousaid)**, the famous blue-and-white village above the cliffs, is one stop south — ten minutes by train and an essential half-day. **[Carthage](/africa/tunisia/carthage)**, with its scattered Punic and Roman ruins, is the next stop along and can fill a full day. Central **[Tunis](/africa/tunisia/tunis)** itself, with its great Medina, the Bardo Museum, and the modern Habib Bourguiba avenue, is a 30-minute ride south.
