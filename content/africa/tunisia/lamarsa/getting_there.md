---
title: "Getting There"
type: section
---

The easiest way to reach La Marsa from Tunis is the **TGM** light-rail line, which runs from the central Tunis-Marine terminal every 10 to 20 minutes. The journey takes about 30 minutes and passes through Carthage and **[Sidi Bou Said](/africa/tunisia/sidibousaid)** on the way. The La Marsa terminus is right beside the corniche.

By taxi from central Tunis the trip takes around 20 minutes outside rush hour. The airport at Tunis-Carthage is about 15 minutes away by taxi. Buses run from Tunis to La Marsa but are slower and less comfortable than the train.
