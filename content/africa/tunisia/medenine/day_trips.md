Medenine's location makes it a sensible base for the ksour country and the Berber villages of the southeast. **[Tataouine](/africa/tunisia/tataouine)**, 50 km south, is the larger sister town and the gateway to the most spectacular ksour. **[Chenini](/africa/tunisia/chenini)** and **[Ghomrassen](/africa/tunisia/ghomrassen)** — the famous cliff-side and cave-dwelling Berber villages — are an hour or so further south and well worth the drive. The island of **[Jerba](/africa/tunisia/jerba)**, reached by causeway or short ferry, is 90 minutes east. Hiring a taxi or car for the day is the practical way to combine several of these stops.
