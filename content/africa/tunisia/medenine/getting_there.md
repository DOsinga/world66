---
title: "Getting There"
type: section
---

Medenine sits on the main road from **[Gabes](/africa/tunisia/gabes)** (75 km north) to the Libyan border and Tataouine, and on the route to the island of **[Jerba](/africa/tunisia/jerba)** to the east. There is no train station; long-distance buses and louages run frequently from Gabes, Tataouine (45 minutes south), Jerba (90 minutes east), and Tunis (around 8 hours north).

The bus and louage stations sit on opposite sides of the town centre. For local trips to Metameur, Ksar Hadada, **[Chenini](/africa/tunisia/chenini)**, and other nearby villages, you will need to hire a taxi for the day or rent a car. A few of the larger hotels in Tataouine and on Jerba arrange day trips that take in several ksour and pass through Medenine.
