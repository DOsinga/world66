---
title: "Getting There"
type: section
---

Moknine is easily reached from Monastir (20 km) and Mahdia (25 km) by louage (shared taxi) or the Sahel metro — the light rail network connecting the coastal towns of the Monastir governorate. The Sahel metro station is close to the centre of town. From Sousse (35 km), louages run regularly and are the fastest option.

The nearest airport is Monastir Habib Bourguiba International, which operates charter and low-cost flights from European cities. From the airport, louages or taxis connect to Moknine in about 30 minutes.
