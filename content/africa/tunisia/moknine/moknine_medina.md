Moknine's old medina is a compact walled town with the characteristic architecture of the Sahel interior — whitewashed cube houses with blue or green woodwork, narrow lanes, and a central mosque. It is not a major tourist sight but is well-preserved and makes for an authentic look at a small Tunisian town that functions as a real community rather than as a museum piece.

The medina is small enough to explore on foot in an hour. The covered market (souk) at its centre is active on market days and sells produce, household goods, and the locally made pottery. The Great Mosque is the architectural anchor of the old town, though its interior is not open to non-Muslims.
