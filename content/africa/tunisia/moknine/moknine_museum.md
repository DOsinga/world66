Moknine has a small regional museum focused on the town's craft heritage, particularly its pottery tradition. The collection includes examples of traditional Sahel ceramics from different periods, along with artefacts documenting daily life in the region. The museum is housed in a distinctive building at the edge of the old town.

For those interested in traditional crafts, the museum provides useful context for understanding the pottery workshops scattered around the town. Exhibits explain the techniques, materials, and history of Sahel pottery, from its pre-Islamic roots to the present day. Entry is inexpensive.
