The Museum of Folk Arts and Traditions of Monastir is a compact collection of traditional Tunisian costume, jewellery, woven household goods, and copperware. It is not a large museum, but it adds useful context to the medina and the better-known ribat nearby.

Visit it as part of a short circuit through central Monastir rather than as a stand-alone destination.
