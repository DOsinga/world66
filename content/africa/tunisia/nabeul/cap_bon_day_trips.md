Nabeul sits in the middle of Cap Bon, one of Tunisia's most varied and accessible peninsulas. The Roman ruins at Kerkouane — a UNESCO World Heritage Site and the only intact Punic town surviving from antiquity — are about 40 km north along the coast and well worth a half-day. The drive along the cape passes through orange and lemon groves, vineyards, and small fishing villages.

[Hammamet](/africa/tunisia/hammamet), the main beach resort town on Cap Bon, is 12 km south of Nabeul and easily combined in a day. The old medina at Hammamet, enclosed in walls overlooking the sea, is among the best-preserved in Tunisia. The cape's wine-producing villages — El Haouaria at the tip of the peninsula is a good target — are accessible by car and provide a contrast to the coastal resort strip.
