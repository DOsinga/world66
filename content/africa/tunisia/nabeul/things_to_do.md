Nabeul's identity is tied to ceramics, and the pottery workshops and market are the primary draw. Beyond the craft tradition, the [Nabeul Museum](/africa/tunisia/nabeul/nabeul_museum) houses important archaeological finds from the Roman site at Kerkouane and from the broader Cap Bon area. The medina and the beach round out what is, for most visitors, a half-day or full-day stop rather than a destination in itself.
