---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

October to April is the comfortable season. October is also the date harvest, busy but atmospheric. Summer is brutally hot — daytime temperatures of 45 °C are normal — and most visitors avoid June, July, and August. Winter mornings can be surprisingly chilly; pack a layer. The Sahara Festival of Tozeur, in November or December, is worth timing for if you can.
