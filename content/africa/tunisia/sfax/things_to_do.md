---
title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Sfax rewards a full day of walking without any tourist crowds to contend with. The **Medina** is the centre of attention — a walled old city of extraordinary vitality, still functioning as a genuine commercial and residential area rather than a sanitised museum. Enter through one of the historic gates, such as **Bab Diwan** (the sea gate) or **Bab Charki** (the eastern gate), and lose yourself in the network of souks.

Within the medina, the **Great Mosque of Sfax** is the most important religious building — its minaret dates to the 9th century and is one of the earliest surviving Islamic structures in the Maghreb. The **Dar Jellouli Museum**, housed in a fine 17th-century mansion, displays Sfax's regional arts and folk traditions including embroidery, costume, jewellery, and woodwork. The **Kasbah of Sfax** occupies the southwestern corner of the old walls and contains the **Sfax Archaeological Museum**, which holds Roman mosaics and antiquities from the surrounding region.

The **Souk El Haddadine** (blacksmiths' market) is one of the most authentic craft souks in Tunisia — the sound of hammering metal fills the narrow lanes. The working harbour is worth an afternoon visit, especially in the late afternoon when the fishing fleet returns.
