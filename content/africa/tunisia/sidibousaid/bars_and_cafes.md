---
title: Bars and Cafes
type: section
---

Sidi Bou Said's cafe culture is central to the place. Tea houses line the main street, and the [Cafe des Nattes](/africa/tunisia/sidibousaid/cafe_des_nattes) at the top of the hill is a must. Being a conservative Muslim village, alcohol is not widely available, but the rooftop tea terraces more than compensate.
