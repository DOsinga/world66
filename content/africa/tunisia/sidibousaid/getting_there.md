---
title: Getting There
type: section
---

The easiest way to reach Sidi Bou Said from [Tunis](/africa/tunisia/tunis) is the TGM commuter train, which runs roughly every 20–30 minutes from the Tunis Marine station near the city centre. The journey takes about 35 minutes and costs a few dinars. Get off at Sidi Bou Said station — the village is a short steep walk uphill from the platform.

Taxis from central Tunis take 20–30 minutes depending on traffic. The TGM also connects to [Carthage](/africa/tunisia/carthage), one stop before Sidi Bou Said, making it easy to combine both in a single day trip.
