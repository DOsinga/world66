---
title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Tabarka's greatest draw is beneath the water. The rocky coastline and clear Mediterranean waters host some of the best diving in Tunisia, with coral formations, sea fans and a good variety of fish. Several dive centres operate from the port.

Above the waterline, the town and its surroundings offer Roman history, a dramatic Genoese fortress and decent hiking through the Kroumirie forest.
