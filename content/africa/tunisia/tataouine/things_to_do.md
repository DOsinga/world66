---
title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

The main draw of Tataouine is not the town itself but the extraordinary landscape of fortified Berber granaries — ksour — scattered across the hills to the south and west. Ksar Ouled Soltane, Chenini, Ksar Hadada, and Douiret can each fill half a day; together they make a compelling two-day circuit. In town, the Thursday market is the one not-to-miss experience — arrive early before the heat builds.
