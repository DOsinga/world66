---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

October to April is the comfortable window. Summer (June to August) is brutal — temperatures regularly exceed 40°C in the shade, and the surrounding stony desert reflects the heat back at you. Spring (March to May) and autumn (September to November) are ideal: warm days, cool evenings, and the landscape at its most photogenic. The Thursday market runs year-round regardless of season, drawing traders from across the region.

Ramadan affects opening hours for restaurants and some sites; plan accordingly.
