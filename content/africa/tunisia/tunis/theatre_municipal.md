Théâtre Municipal is a white Art Deco theatre on central Avenue Habib Bourguiba. Even if you are not attending a performance, the facade is one of the avenue's most distinctive pieces of early 20th-century architecture.

It is a quick but worthwhile stop while walking between the cathedral, the central market, and the entrance to the medina.
