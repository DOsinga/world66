---
title: "Getting There"
type: section
---

Utique sits about 35 km northwest of [Tunis](/africa/tunisia/tunis), off the GP8 road towards [Bizerte](/africa/tunisia/bizerte). There is no direct public transport to the site itself.

The simplest option is to hire a taxi from Tunis for the round trip — agree on the fare in advance, including a wait of an hour or two at the site. A louage (shared minibus) from Tunis to Henchir Bou Chateur or to Utique-village will get you close, but you will still have a couple of kilometres to walk to the archaeological park.

The site combines naturally with a day trip to Bizerte or with Carthage on the way back into Tunis. If you have a rental car, parking is informal — pull off the road near the entrance.
