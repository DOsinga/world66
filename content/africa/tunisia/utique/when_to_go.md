---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

Spring (March to May) and autumn (September to early November) are ideal. The site is unshaded and lies on a low rise where afternoons in midsummer can be punishing — temperatures above 35 C are common in July and August.

Winter is mild but can be rainy, and the unsurfaced paths through the ruins turn into mud. Mornings are best year-round: the light is softer, the mosaics show their colour, and you will likely have the place to yourself. Avoid Mondays, when the small museum may be closed.
