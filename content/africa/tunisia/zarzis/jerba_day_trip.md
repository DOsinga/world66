---

Jerba is the most obvious excursion from Zarzis, 20 minutes away by road across the ancient Roman causeway. The island has more tourism infrastructure than Zarzis and more to see: the El Ghriba synagogue, the Houmt Souk old town with its Ottoman fort, and excellent fish restaurants. Zarzis makes a quieter base than the island's resort zone if you want to visit Jerba's sights without staying inside the tourist bubble.
