---
title: When to Go
type: section
---

Bwindi is a year-round destination, but conditions vary considerably. The two dry seasons — June to August and December to February — are the most popular times to visit. Trails are drier and easier, visibility in the forest is better, and the weather is generally more reliable. These are also the busiest months, and permits sell out far in advance.

The rainy seasons (March–May and September–November) are muddier and more challenging, but the forest is vivid green, gorilla permits are easier to obtain at short notice, and accommodation prices are sometimes lower. The rain usually comes in bursts rather than all day, and properly waterproofed gear deals with it well.

Temperatures at Bwindi are mild year-round — the altitude (1,190 to 2,607 metres) keeps things cool. Mornings can be cold enough for a fleece, and the forest interior stays cool throughout the day.
