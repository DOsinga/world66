Entebbe is a quiet lakeside town on the northern shore of Lake Victoria, 37 kilometres southwest of Kampala. It serves as Uganda's main international gateway — Entebbe International Airport handles virtually all international flights — and many travellers pass through on their way to Kampala and the national parks. Those who stay longer find a genuinely pleasant town: cooler than Kampala, green, unhurried, and set amid tropical gardens and lake beaches.

The [Entebbe Botanical Gardens](/africa/uganda/entebbe/entebbe_botanical_gardens), 100 acres of landscaped grounds bordering a kilometre of Lake Victoria shoreline, are one of the most beautiful green spaces in East Africa. They contain over 300 plant species and were used as a filming location for the original Tarzan films. The [Uganda Wildlife Education Centre](/africa/uganda/entebbe/zoo) nearby is a rehabilitation zoo with over 200 animals — shoebill storks, chimpanzees, lions, and more — in spacious enclosures, accessible for a few dollars.

[Lido Beach](/africa/uganda/entebbe/lido_beach) is the most popular of Entebbe's lake beaches; [Aero Beach](/africa/uganda/entebbe/aero_beach) is scattered with retired aircraft, making it an unusual spot. Several resort hotels allow non-guests to use their pools and beach facilities for a small fee. Boats make day and overnight trips to the Ssese Islands archipelago and to [Ngamba Island](/africa/uganda/entebbe/ngamba_island), a chimpanzee sanctuary run by the Uganda Wildlife Conservation Trust. The quiet [Kigungu landing site](/africa/uganda/entebbe/kigungu_landing_site) on the western shore of the peninsula marks where the first Catholic missionaries arrived in Uganda in 1879.
