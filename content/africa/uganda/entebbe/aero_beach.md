Aero Beach is Entebbe's most unusual lakeside spot: a resort and beach area dotted with decommissioned aircraft — retired planes left in place as permanent installations, giving the grounds the feel of an outdoor aviation museum crossed with a beach bar. The combination of retired fuselages, lake views, and plastic chairs is peculiarly appealing.

The beach itself is a stretch of Lake Victoria shoreline where people swim, barbecue, and drink in the shade of the old planes. It's popular with Kampala weekenders looking for a lake escape and with travellers with time to kill before or after a flight from nearby Entebbe International Airport. The Atlas Obscura community has documented it as one of East Africa's stranger attractions, and the description holds up in person.
