Ngamba Island, about 23 km southeast of Entebbe on Lake Victoria, is a 100-acre forested island that serves as a sanctuary for rescued and orphaned chimpanzees managed by the Chimpanzee Sanctuary and Wildlife Conservation Trust. Around 50 chimpanzees live here, all of them rescued from the illegal wildlife trade.

Visits to the island involve a boat journey of about an hour from Entebbe, a guided walk along the island's forest edges, and timed feeding sessions where the chimpanzees emerge from the interior forest to receive fruit — the closest safe viewing distance is modest, but the animals are habituated and the interactions are remarkable. Half-day, full-day, and overnight visits are available; booking in advance is essential. The Uganda Wildlife Education Centre in Entebbe can help with arrangements, or contact the Chimpanzee Trust directly.
