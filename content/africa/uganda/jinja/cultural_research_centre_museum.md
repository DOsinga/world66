The Cultural Research Centre Museum in Jinja focuses on the history and culture of the Busoga kingdom, the traditional kingdom whose territory surrounds the city. Exhibits cover the Basoga people's history, royal regalia, traditional instruments, and the social customs that have shaped life in this part of Uganda for centuries.

It's a modest museum by international standards, but it fills a gap that larger Uganda museums tend to overlook. Jinja sits at the heart of Busoga territory, and understanding the kingdom adds real depth to the town's story — especially when you consider that the land around the Source of the Nile has been culturally and politically significant long before Europeans arrived.
