---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

Jinja is worth visiting year-round, but two dry seasons offer the best conditions. The main dry season runs from June to August, and a shorter dry spell falls in December and January. During these months the roads are good, the sky is clear, and the river — if anything — is running at a pace that suits white-water activities.

The long rains fall between March and May, and the shorter rains in October and November. Rafting and kayaking continue through the wet season without much disruption, since the river is already full and rainfall just makes it more dramatic. The roads around Bujagali can get muddy and the Mabira Forest access track can be slow. If you're combining Jinja with a safari in western Uganda, the dry seasons are a better bet for road travel across the country.

There's no distinct high season for Jinja in the way there is for beach or safari destinations. The adventure operators run trips daily throughout the year and rarely fill up completely except around Christmas and Easter when Ugandan domestic tourism peaks.
