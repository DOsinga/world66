Nommo Gallery is a small but useful stop for seeing Ugandan painting, sculpture, and craft outside the market-and-souvenir circuit. It sits in central Kampala in a quieter compound than the streets around it, which makes the visit feel like a pause as much as a gallery stop.

Exhibitions and stock change, so come with curiosity rather than a checklist. The gallery is best paired with nearby Nakasero, the National Theatre area, or a wider look at Kampala's public institutions; it gives a more contemporary counterpoint to the National Museum and the city's older religious landmarks.
