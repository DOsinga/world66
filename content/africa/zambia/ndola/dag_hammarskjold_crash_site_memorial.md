This memorial marks the site where UN Secretary-General Dag Hammarskjöld died in a plane crash in 1961 while travelling on a peace mission during the Congo crisis. The grounds include a small museum and garden, making it a sober but worthwhile stop for anyone interested in modern African history or the early years of the United Nations.
