---
title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Ndola is an industrial city, not a tourist destination, but it has a few genuine points of interest — particularly for those interested in the history of the Copperbelt, the Dag Hammarskjöld crash site, and the legacy of the slave trade. The central market is lively and worth a wander; the Copperbelt Museum provides useful context for the region.
