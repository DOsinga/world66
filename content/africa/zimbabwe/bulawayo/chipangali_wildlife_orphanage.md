Chipangali Wildlife Orphanage lies on the southeastern edge of Bulawayo and has been taking in injured, orphaned, abandoned, and confiscated wild animals since 1973. It is not a polished safari experience; the point is rescue and rehabilitation first, with education and research folded into the work.

For travellers, Chipangali is most worthwhile if you want to understand the messy, practical side of wildlife conservation in Zimbabwe. Some animals can be released, others cannot, and the visit is a reminder that human-wildlife conflict, poaching, pet keeping, and road injuries all have consequences long after the headline story has passed.
