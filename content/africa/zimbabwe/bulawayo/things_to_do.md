---
title: Things to Do
type: section
---

Bulawayo is a city with more substance than its secondary status might suggest. The Natural History Museum is one of the finest natural history collections in southern Africa. The Railway Museum celebrates the city's deep connection to the iron road that shaped its economy for a century. And Matobo National Park, thirty kilometres south, offers one of the most dramatic and wildlife-rich landscapes in Zimbabwe. Most travelers who stop here find they wish they had stayed longer.
