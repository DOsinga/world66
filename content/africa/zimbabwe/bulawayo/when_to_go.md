---
title: When to Go
type: section
---

Bulawayo is on the Zimbabwean highveld at about 1,340 metres elevation, which keeps temperatures more moderate than the lower-lying Zambezi Valley or the lowveld. Summers (November to March) are warm to hot with afternoon thunderstorms — temperatures can reach 35°C but rarely become oppressive. Winters (May to August) are dry and sunny with cold nights; days are typically 20–25°C and excellent for walking and wildlife viewing.

The dry season — May to October — is generally the best time to visit Matobo National Park, since vegetation is less dense and wildlife concentrates around water sources. Wildlife viewing is good year-round in Matobo compared to some parks because the kopje landscape remains accessible regardless of season.
