Mutare is the largest town in Zimbabwe's Eastern Highlands, situated in a narrow valley near the Mozambique border with forested mountains rising on all sides. It is the commercial centre for the region and a practical base for exploring some of Zimbabwe's most spectacular highland scenery. The Eastern Highlands as a whole are among the best birdwatching destinations in southern Africa and among the least visited.

South of town, the [Vumba Mountains and Bvumba Botanical Gardens](/africa/zimbabwe/easternhighlands/mutare/bvumba_mountains) are the main draws — mist-wreathed montane forest that holds orchids, ferns, and forest birds found nowhere else in Zimbabwe. North of Mutare, [Nyanga National Park](/africa/zimbabwe/easternhighlands/mutare/nyanga) contains the country's highest peak (Mount Nyangani, 2,592m) and the Mtarazi Falls — the second tallest waterfall in Africa — dropping dramatically into the Honde Valley below. Further south, [Chimanimani National Park](/africa/zimbabwe/easternhighlands/mutare/chimanimani) offers one of Zimbabwe's finest multi-day walks through a remote quartzite mountain range.

In town, the [Sakubva market](/africa/zimbabwe/easternhighlands/mutare/sakubva_market) near the main bus terminus is a lively place to pick up locally made stone and wooden carvings at prices well below tourist outlets elsewhere. The [Mutare Museum](/africa/zimbabwe/easternhighlands/mutare/mutare_museum) covers the region's natural history and cultural heritage and is worth an hour before heading into the hills.
