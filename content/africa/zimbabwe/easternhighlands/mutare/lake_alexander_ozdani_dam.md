Lake Alexander, also known as Ozdani Dam, is a scenic reservoir outside Mutare with views over the surrounding hills. It is used for fishing, rowing, yachting, and quiet picnics, with simple shelters and facilities around the water.

It works best as a half-day escape from town, especially for travellers with their own transport. The appeal is the setting and slower pace rather than a formal attraction.
