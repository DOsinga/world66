Nyanga National Park, about 100km north of Mutare, contains Zimbabwe's highest peak (Mount Nyangani, 2,592m) and the Mtarazi Falls, which drop 762m into the Honde Valley — the second highest waterfall in Africa. The national park also has Zimbabwe's only trout fishing streams and a landscape of rolling grassland, forest patches, and rocky kopjes quite unlike the rest of the country.

The drive from Mutare through the Nyanga highlands is excellent: the road climbs through tea and coffee estates and commercial forest to a plateau with wide views in all directions. Mount Nyangani can be hiked in a day with an early start (the summit is a 2-3 hour walk from the car park). The Mtarazi Falls viewpoint requires a separate approach via the Honde Valley road, which descends dramatically from the escarpment.
