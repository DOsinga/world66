---
title: "Getting There"
type: section
---

Great Zimbabwe is 27 km from Masvingo, the nearest city, and about 290 km south of [Harare](/africa/zimbabwe/harare) via the A4 highway. The drive from Harare takes 3–4 hours on generally good road.

**From Masvingo.** The site is signposted from the main road south of town. Shared taxis (kombis) run between Masvingo and the ruins; the journey takes about 30 minutes. Taxis from Masvingo to the site cost a small flat rate and can be arranged to wait or collect you at a fixed time.

**From Harare.** Buses run from Harare's Mbare bus terminus to Masvingo regularly throughout the day; the journey takes 3–4 hours. From Masvingo take a kombi or taxi to the ruins. If you are driving, the route is straightforward on the A4 south from Harare through Chivhu to Masvingo.

**Accommodation.** The Great Zimbabwe Hotel is immediately adjacent to the ruins — staying there means you can visit at opening time before the day-trip crowds arrive, which is well worth doing. There are also options in Masvingo itself.
