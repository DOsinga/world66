---
title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Great Zimbabwe divides into three main zones spread across a wide, hilly landscape. Allow at least half a day — ideally more — to explore all three properly. The Hill Complex requires a reasonable level of fitness to reach; the Great Enclosure below it is the most photographed section; and the Valley Ruins between them are the least visited but reward close attention. A guide is not required but adds considerably to the experience.
