The Eastgate Centre on Robert Mugabe Road is a shopping centre and office block whose design made architectural history. Completed in 1996 and designed by Zimbabwean architect Mick Pearce in association with Arup, the building regulates its temperature through a passive ventilation system modelled on the self-cooling mounds built by African termites.

Cool air enters through vents at the base, is warmed by the building's activities during the day, and rises to escape through chimneys at the top — the same basic principle used by termites in their colonies. The result is a building that maintains comfortable temperatures through Harare's hot seasons without the energy cost of conventional air conditioning. Worth a look even if architecture isn't usually your thing.
