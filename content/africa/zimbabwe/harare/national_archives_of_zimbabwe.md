The National Archives of Zimbabwe is more than a research institution. Its public collections include historical photographs, documents, maps, and displays that help make sense of Zimbabwe's colonial and post-colonial history.

Casual visitors should not expect the polish of a major museum, but the material can be fascinating if you are interested in the country beyond wildlife and landscapes. It pairs well with the National Gallery and other central Harare cultural stops.
