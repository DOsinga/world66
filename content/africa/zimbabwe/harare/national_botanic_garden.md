The National Botanic Garden is one of Harare's best green pauses, a large garden north of the centre with collections focused on Zimbabwean and regional African plant life. It is also tied to the National Herbarium, so the place has a scientific purpose beyond pleasant shade.

Come for a walk, birding, or a quiet break from traffic rather than for formal flower beds. The most interesting sections are those that evoke Zimbabwe's own woodland and savanna habitats, giving travellers a preview of landscapes they may meet beyond the capital.
