The Kopje is the granite hill that rises above central Harare and helped shape the early colonial town. Its height made it a lookout and reference point, and it remains one of the few places where you can get a quick physical sense of the city's position on the highveld.

The site is more historic viewpoint than polished attraction. Visit in daylight, preferably with local advice on current access and safety, and treat it as a short stop that connects Harare's geography with its origin story.
