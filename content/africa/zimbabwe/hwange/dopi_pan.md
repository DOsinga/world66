Dopi Pan lies along one of the fossil river systems that give Hwange its pattern of vleis, pans, and slightly richer grazing. It is a useful stop on longer drives from Main Camp, especially when the dry season pulls animals toward every reliable water source.

Expect a slower, less theatrical experience than at Nyamandhlovu. That is not a bad thing. Dopi is good for reading the landscape: elephant tracks in the dust, antelope moving between shade and water, and birds working the pan margins. It pairs well with the other southern pans if you have a full day.
