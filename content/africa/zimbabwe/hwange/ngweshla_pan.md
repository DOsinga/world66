Ngweshla Pan is one of the strongest game-viewing areas in southern Hwange. The open grassland around the pan gives better visibility than dense woodland, and the mix of water, grazing, and cover pulls in zebra, wildebeest, impala, giraffe, and large elephant herds in the dry season.

Predators follow. Lion are regularly seen around Ngweshla, and wild dog sometimes hunt the wider vlei system. The pan is far enough from Main Camp that it works best as a planned drive with an early start, not an afterthought. In the late dry season, the dust, light, and concentration of animals can be spectacular.
