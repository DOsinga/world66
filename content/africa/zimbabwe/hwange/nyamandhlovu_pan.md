Nyamandhlovu Pan is one of Hwange's classic wildlife stops, close enough to Main Camp for an easy morning or late-afternoon drive. A raised viewing platform overlooks the waterhole, giving a safe, patient way to watch animals come and go without edging the vehicle closer every few minutes.

In the late dry season, elephants dominate the scene. Herds arrive in waves, drink, dust themselves, jostle around the water, and move off into the teak woodland. Giraffe, zebra, impala, kudu, and baboons are regular, and predators patrol the area when prey numbers build. Bring binoculars and stay longer than your first instinct; the waterhole changes by the minute.
