---
title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Kwekwe earns its place on the map through its mining history. The **Globe and Phoenix Mine** site preserves the Paper House and a small museum of the operation that built the town. The **Kwekwe Sports Club** is the local social heart, with a renovated international cricket ground.

Out of town, the **Sebakwe Recreational Park** to the east offers a quiet day among lake, antelope, and miombo woodland, while the satellite town of **Redcliff** with its hibernating steelworks gives a window into Zimbabwe's industrial past.
