---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

Kwekwe lies on the central Zimbabwean plateau at around 1,200 metres, which gives it a temperate climate by African standards. The dry winter months — May to August — are the most comfortable: warm sunny days around 22°C and cool nights down to single digits. The bush is browner but the roads are dry.

October is the hottest month, with temperatures pushing into the mid-30s; afternoon storms break the heat through the summer rainy season from November to March. Avoid the peak of the wet season if you want to drive on minor roads or visit Sebakwe.
