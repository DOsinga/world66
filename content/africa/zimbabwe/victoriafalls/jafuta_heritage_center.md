Jafuta Heritage Center is a small museum focused on the cultural heritage of the Shona, Ndebele, Tonga, and Lozi peoples. It is not a blockbuster stop, but it adds useful human context to a Victoria Falls visit that can otherwise become all viewpoints and activities.

Go expecting a compact collection rather than a long museum afternoon. It works best as a quiet add-on when you want to understand more of the region around the falls.
