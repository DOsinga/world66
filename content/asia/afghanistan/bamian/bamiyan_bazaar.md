The bazaar that runs along the main street of Bamiyan town is the heart of everyday life in the valley. Small shops sell carpets, fresh bread, dried apricots, walnuts, mulberries, copper, plastic goods, and clothing; *chaikhanas* (tea-houses) serve green tea, *qabili pulao* (rice with carrots and raisins) and *mantu* dumplings. The bazaar is one of the few places in the country where you can stop for tea in a Hazara teahouse and watch the day pass — a quietly hospitable place, mostly Hazara, very used to foreign visitors.
