title: Day Trips
type: section
---

The two essential trips from Bamiyan town are both possible as long days. [Band-e Amir](/asia/afghanistan/bamian/band_e_amir), Afghanistan's first national park, sits about 75 km west — three to four hours of slow driving each way over a rough mountain road, but the six turquoise travertine-dammed lakes at 3,000 metres are one of Afghanistan's great sights. [Shahr-e-Zohak](/asia/afghanistan/bamian/shahr_e_zohak), the red-stone Ghorid fortress at the eastern entrance to the valley, makes a half-day with a steep walk up to the citadel. Closer in, the [Foladi](/asia/afghanistan/bamian/foladi_valley) and [Kakrak](/asia/afghanistan/bamian/kakrak_valley) side valleys give a quieter look at Hazara village life and a third, smaller Buddha niche.
