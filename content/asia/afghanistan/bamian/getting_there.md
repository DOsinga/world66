---
title: Getting There
type: section
---

The road from [Kabul](/asia/afghanistan/kabul) to Bamiyan is about 230 km but takes between 6 and 10 hours depending on the route and the security situation. There are two roads:

**The southern route via Wardak and Maidan Shahr** is shorter, paved for most of the way, but historically more dangerous because of Taliban presence south of Kabul.

**The northern route via Parwan, the Salang/Shibar Pass, and Doshi** is longer (about 8–10 hours), unpaved in places, but historically safer and considered more scenic — through dramatic Hindu Kush passes.

A small **Bamyan Airport** (BIN) operates flights to and from Kabul — when conditions permit, this is the easier option. Operated under various names over the years; check schedules close to your travel date.

**Drive yourself only with local knowledge.** A driver-and-vehicle hire through a reputable Kabul agency is the standard arrangement for foreign visitors; it includes security risk assessment and route planning. Conditions and routes change quickly; check the situation in the days before you travel.
