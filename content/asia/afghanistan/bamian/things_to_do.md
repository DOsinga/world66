The Bamiyan valley is one of the great archaeological landscapes of the Silk Road. The empty niches of the [Buddhas of Bamiyan](/asia/afghanistan/bamian/buddhas_of_bamiyan) — destroyed in 2001 — still dominate the cliffs, surrounded by hundreds of cave dwellings and meditation cells used by Buddhist monks from the 4th century onwards. Above the town, [Shahr-e-Gholghola](/asia/afghanistan/bamian/shahr_e_gholghola), the "City of Screams," is the ruined Ghorid citadel that Genghis Khan reduced in 1221. West of the town, the six turquoise lakes of [Band-e Amir](/asia/afghanistan/bamian/band_e_amir) make up Afghanistan's first national park.
