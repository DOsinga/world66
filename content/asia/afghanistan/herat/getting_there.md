---
title: "Getting There"
type: section
---

Herat has an international airport (Herat Airport, IATA: HEA) with connections to Kabul and, historically, to regional cities in Iran, India, and the Gulf. Under Taliban rule since 2021, international flight connectivity has been severely reduced and remains uncertain.

**Overland from Iran:** The main border crossing into Afghanistan from Iran is at Islam Qala, about 120km west of Herat on the road from Mashhad. This route was historically popular with overland travellers; its current status and safety should be verified before travel.

**Overland from Kabul:** The road from Kabul to Herat crosses some of the most remote and historically unsafe terrain in Afghanistan, a journey of some 1,100km. Travel on this route is not recommended for independent travellers.

**Note:** Afghanistan is currently under Taliban control. Travel advisories from most governments strongly advise against all travel to Afghanistan. Conditions change rapidly; consult your government's travel advisory before making any plans.
