Herat's Military Museum holds a heavy collection of weapons and equipment left behind by Afghanistan's long sequence of wars and invasions. The displays are blunt rather than polished, with an upstairs section focused on the Soviet invasion.

It is a sobering stop for travellers trying to understand the city's recent history beyond its mosques, minarets, and Timurid architecture.
