Takht-e Safar is an old hillside park on the edge of Herat, associated with views, picnics, and the long local habit of escaping the city walls for cooler air. Its appeal is not a single monument but the setting: dry hills, a sense of space, and Herat spread below.

Current access and safety conditions should be checked carefully before any visit. When conditions allow, it is a gentle complement to the city's mosques, shrines, and citadel.
