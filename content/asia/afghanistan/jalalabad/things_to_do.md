Jalalabad is not a tourist destination in the conventional sense — Afghanistan has been under Taliban rule since 2021, and travel is not recommended for most visitors. This section documents what the city contains historically and geographically, for reference and for the day when conditions change.

The area's most significant historical sites are Buddhist — Jalalabad sits in the ancient region of Nagarahara, which was a major centre of early Buddhism. The [Ahin Posh stupa](/asia/afghanistan/jalalabad/ahin_posh) is the most accessible remnant of this. The city itself has Mughal-era roots and a lush, semi-tropical character rare in Afghanistan.
