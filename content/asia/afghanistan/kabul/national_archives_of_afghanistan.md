The National Archives of Afghanistan preserves historic documents, manuscripts, and records from Afghan history. A small museum display makes part of the collection accessible to visitors.

It is a focused stop rather than a large museum, best suited to travellers with an interest in Afghan history, archives, or old manuscripts.
