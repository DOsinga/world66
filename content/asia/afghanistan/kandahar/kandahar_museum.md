Kandahar Museum is a small cultural museum with paintings by Ghiyassuddin, one of Afghanistan's important modern painters, among its main draws. The collection gives a rare indoor cultural stop in a city better known to travellers for shrines, bazaars, and difficult recent history.

Check local conditions and opening arrangements before making plans. In Kandahar, practical access can matter as much as the attraction itself.
