title: Practical Information
type: section
---

**Security.** Kunduz has changed hands repeatedly during the past two decades of war and remains volatile. Most foreign governments advise against all travel to Afghanistan, and to Kunduz in particular. Independent tourism is not realistic at the time of writing.

**Visas and registration.** Visas for Afghanistan are not issued routinely to most nationalities and the situation changes frequently. Anyone considering travel needs current advice from a serious source — an experienced fixer, an NGO with people on the ground, or a journalist who has been recently.

**Language.** Dari (Afghan Persian) is the lingua franca; Pashto, Uzbek and Turkmen are also spoken in the surrounding districts.

**Climate.** Hot, dry summers with daytime temperatures often above 40 °C, and cold winters with occasional snow. Spring is short and pleasant; autumn is the best season, with cooler air and the melon harvest in the markets.
