title: Things to Do
type: section
---

Maimana is not a city built for sightseeing in the conventional sense — there are no famous monuments, no major museums, no carefully preserved historic quarter. What it offers is the texture of a remote northwestern Afghan town that has changed less than most: a working bazaar known for its carpets, a Friday Mosque at its centre, and the dry hills of Faryab Province rising in every direction. For visitors who somehow find their way here, the rewards are atmospheric rather than monumental.
