---
title: "Getting There"
type: section
---

Mazar-i-Sharif has an international airport with intermittent service from Kabul, Istanbul, Dubai, and at times Tashkent. Schedules change frequently — confirm before you travel.

By road, Mazar lies on the highway between Kabul (around 425 km south, over the Salang Pass) and Hairatan on the Uzbek border (around 60 km north). The Friendship Bridge at Hairatan is the main land crossing into Uzbekistan, leading to Termez. The road from Kabul is sometimes closed by snow at the Salang or by security incidents; the Uzbek crossing is more reliable but visa requirements are strict in both directions.

Internal flights and shared taxis connect Mazar with Kunduz, Herat, and Kabul. Foreign travellers will normally need a local fixer to negotiate transport, checkpoints, and accommodation.
