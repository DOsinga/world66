Jidd Hafs is a residential suburb sitting just west of Manama, Bahrain's capital. It is one of the oldest settled areas on the island and served as Bahrain's capital city before Manama took that role — traces of that longer history show in its older neighbourhoods and traditional architecture amid the modern sprawl.

Today Jidd Hafs is a quiet, largely residential area without major tourist sights. It is useful mainly as an affordable base for exploring [Manama](/asia/bahrain/manama) and northern Bahrain, with public transport connections into the capital running regularly. The one genuine point of historical interest nearby is the [Khamis Mosque](/asia/bahrain/jid_hafs/khamis_mosque) in the adjacent village of Khamis — believed to be the oldest mosque in Bahrain, with minarets dating to the 14th and 15th centuries.
