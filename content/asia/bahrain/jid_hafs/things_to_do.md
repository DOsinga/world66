---
title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Jidd Hafs offers little in the way of conventional tourist sights — it is a residential suburb and functions as one. The main reason to base yourself here is proximity to [Manama](/asia/bahrain/manama) and the lower accommodation prices compared to the capital. That said, the [Khamis Mosque](/asia/bahrain/jid_hafs/khamis_mosque), believed to be the oldest mosque in Bahrain, sits just south of the suburb and is genuinely worth the short detour.
