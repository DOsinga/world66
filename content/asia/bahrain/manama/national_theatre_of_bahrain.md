The National Theatre of Bahrain stands on the waterfront beside the Bahrain National Museum, a polished modern building meant to echo the kingdom's relationship with the sea. Its broad roof and glassy setting make it one of Manama's more photogenic contemporary landmarks.

You will get the most from it if you attend a performance, but the exterior is still worth seeing while visiting the museum district. Check the programme in advance; access inside depends on events, and the building is more cultural venue than drop-in museum.
