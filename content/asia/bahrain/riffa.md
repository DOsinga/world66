Riffa is [Bahrain](/asia/bahrain)'s second-largest city, spread across the low hills of the Central Governorate south of [Manama](/asia/bahrain/manama). The city is divided into East and West Riffa, with the western half home to many of the royal family's palaces — you will spot their walled compounds and manicured grounds as you drive through. The main historical attraction is [Riffa Fort](/asia/bahrain/riffa/riffa_fort), also known as Sheikh Salman bin Ahmed Al Fateh Fort, a nineteenth-century stronghold perched on a hilltop with views across the surrounding palm groves and residential districts.

Modern Riffa is largely a residential city, with shopping malls, schools, and the sprawling Riffa Views development. The [Bahrain International Circuit](/asia/bahrain/riffa/bahrain_international_circuit), home to the Formula 1 Bahrain Grand Prix, lies just to the south in Sakhir, and on race weekends the area buzzes with international visitors. A short drive north of Riffa, the village of [A'ali](/asia/bahrain/riffa/aali_burial_mounds) sits among the UNESCO-listed Dilmun burial mounds — one of the largest ancient cemeteries in the world, and a startling reminder that this quiet island was once a major trading civilisation. For everyday life, Riffa offers a quieter, more local alternative to the bustle of Manama, with a handful of good Arabic restaurants and the Friday morning market worth a browse.
