---
title: "Getting Around"
type: section
---

Riffa is not walkable in the conventional sense — it is a sprawling residential city built around the car. Taxis and ride-share apps (Careem and Uber both operate in Bahrain) are the practical way to get around. The city is about 15 kilometres south of [Manama](/asia/bahrain/manama) via the main highway, and taxis between the two run frequently and are reasonably priced.

The Bahrain International Circuit at Sakhir is a further 10 kilometres south of Riffa. During Grand Prix weekend a shuttle service operates from Manama; at other times a taxi is the only option unless you have a rental car.
