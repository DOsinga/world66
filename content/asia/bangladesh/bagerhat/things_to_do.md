---
title: Things to Do
type: section
---

Bagerhat's sights are spread west of the modern town along the old mosque-city landscape of Khalifatabad. The Sixty Dome Mosque is the essential stop, but the trip works best if you also visit Khan Jahan Ali's mausoleum and one or two of the smaller brick mosques nearby. Go early or late in the day if you want softer light and less heat.
