Bangladesh Railway Museum preserves equipment, signs, lamps, uniforms, signalling gear and other objects from the Assam Bengal, Eastern Bengal, Pakistan and Bangladesh railway eras.

It is a specialist museum, but a worthwhile stop if you are interested in rail history or in how Chittagong was linked to the wider region by train.
