Bistaar is an independent arts complex in Chittagong, the kind of small cultural space that can tell you more about a city than another monument. It hosts exhibitions, talks, performances, workshops, and community arts events, with a focus on contemporary creative life rather than polished tourist display.

Check what is on before you go, because its value depends heavily on programming. When active, it is a good way to meet Chittagong as a living city of artists, students, and organisers, not only as a port with beaches, shrines, and colonial remnants.
