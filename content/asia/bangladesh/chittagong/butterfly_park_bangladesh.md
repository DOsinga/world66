Butterfly Park Bangladesh is a family-oriented garden attraction near Chittagong, built around butterfly displays, landscaped paths, and small recreational areas. It offers a softer, greener outing than the city's port roads and busy commercial districts.

The park is most useful for families or travellers with extra time in Chittagong rather than for those trying to see only the city's strongest sights. Go in daylight and treat it as a relaxed nature-themed break.
