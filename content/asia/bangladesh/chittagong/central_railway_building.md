The Central Railway Building, usually shortened to CRB, is one of Chittagong's more atmospheric colonial-era landmarks. Its hilltop grounds are green and open by city standards, with old railway offices, bungalows, and a broad public space used for Bengali New Year and other festivals.

Come for a walk rather than a formal tour. The buildings are still part of the railway world, but the setting gives a good feel for Chittagong's older civic landscape.
