Chandanpura Nachghar is an old dance hall and mansion fragment in one of Chittagong's historic neighbourhoods. Its appeal is architectural: arched openings, brickwork, and decorative traces from a period when local elites built houses that mixed Bengali, Mughal, and colonial influences.

The building is not polished as a visitor attraction, and that is part of the point. It is a stop for travellers interested in the city's older urban fabric, best combined with nearby mosques, shrines, and streets rather than treated as a standalone museum.
