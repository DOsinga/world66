Chatteshwari Temple is one of Chittagong's better-known Hindu shrines, set on a small rise rather than hidden deep in the commercial streets. The temple is dedicated to Chatteshwari Kali, and the name is closely tied to the old identity of the city.

It is not a large complex, but it adds an important religious and cultural layer to a city better known to travellers for its port, markets, and hill views. Visit respectfully, especially during worship, and expect a working shrine rather than a polished monument.
