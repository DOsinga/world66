Chintamani Library preserves rare palm-leaf manuscripts and old religious texts in Pali, Burmese, and Sanskrit. The collection is small but unusual, with classical literature and religious works that give context to Chittagong's Buddhist heritage.

It is best treated as a short, focused stop for visitors already exploring the city's older temples, mosques, and museums rather than as a large museum visit.
