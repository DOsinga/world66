---

A man-made lake set among the hills on the northern edge of Chittagong, surrounded by an amusement park and a small zoo. The lake itself is the more interesting part — paddle boats and a small ferry cross to picnic areas on wooded hills, and the views back over the lake from the higher trails are good.

The amusement park rides are aimed at families and Bangladeshi day-trippers, who pack the place on holidays. The lake is most pleasant on weekday mornings.
