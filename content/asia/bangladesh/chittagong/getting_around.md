---
title: "Getting Around"
type: section
---

Chittagong has no metro system; getting around relies on a mix of CNG auto-rickshaws, buses, taxis and (increasingly) Uber.

**CNG (compressed-natural-gas auto-rickshaws)** are the standard mode of transport. Drivers often refuse to use the meter — agree the fare before you set off, around 50-100 taka for short trips, 200-300 taka across town.

**Uber and Pathao** ride-hailing apps work in the city; cars and motorbike pillion rides are usually cheaper than CNGs and avoid haggling.

**City buses** are crowded and the routes are hard to figure out as a visitor. Avoid in rush hours.

**Walking** is workable in the central commercial areas but the pavement quality is poor and the air is bad — masks are advisable for the worst weeks of winter smog.
