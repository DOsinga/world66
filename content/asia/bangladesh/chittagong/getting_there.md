---
title: "Getting There"
type: section
---

**By air**: Chittagong's Shah Amanat International Airport (CGP) has flights from [Dhaka](/asia/bangladesh/dhaka) (about 45 minutes), the Gulf states (Doha, Dubai, Sharjah, Muscat) and a handful of regional destinations. Internal flights from Dhaka are the easiest way to arrive if you are short on time.

**By rail**: Chattogram Railway Station is one of the busiest in Bangladesh. The overnight trains from Dhaka (notably the *Subarna Express* and the *Mahanagar Express*) take 6-7 hours and have AC sleeper berths. Daytime trains are slower.

**By road**: The Dhaka-Chittagong Highway is the country's busiest road link. Long-distance buses leave every few minutes from both ends and take 6-8 hours depending on traffic. Several private companies — Green Line, Hanif Enterprise, Shyamoli — offer comfortable AC coaches.

**By sea**: The MV Bay Cruise occasionally runs from Dhaka to Chittagong; check current operations. International passenger ferries no longer use Chittagong.
