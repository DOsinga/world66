Iskcon Prabartak Sri Krishna Temple is a large Hindu temple in Chittagong, built with Makrana marble and detailed woodwork. Its size and polished materials make it stand out among the city's religious sites, and it offers a useful contrast to older shrines such as Chatteshwari Temple.

Visit respectfully, especially during worship or festivals. The temple is a working religious site, not just a monument.
