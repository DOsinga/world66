Masjid-e-Siraj ud-Daulah is one of Chittagong's older mosques, linked to the wave of mosque building that followed Mughal control of the city in the 17th century.

It is a small historic stop rather than a major monument, but it adds context to the old religious landscape around central Chittagong.
