Shadhinata Complex is a theme park built around replicas of Bangladesh's best-known buildings and monuments. Models include sights such as the Jatiya Sangsad Bhaban, Ahsan Manzil, Curzon Hall, Kantajew Temple, and other national landmarks.

It is a family-oriented stop rather than a substitute for seeing the originals, but it gives a quick visual tour of the country from a Chittagong base.
