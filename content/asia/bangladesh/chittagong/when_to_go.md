---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

**November to February** is the best time to visit Chittagong. Daytime temperatures hover around 25-28°C, the humidity drops, and there is little rain. The smoggy winter haze is less of a problem here than in Dhaka because of the coastal breeze.

**March to May** is hot and increasingly humid (30-35°C). Manageable but tiring.

**June to October** is the monsoon. Heavy rains, frequent flooding, and possible cyclones in the Bay of Bengal — particularly dangerous late in the season. Travel to Cox's Bazar and the Hill Tracts is awkward at best during the monsoon.

The **Pohela Boishakh** (Bengali New Year) on 14 April brings cultural events to DC Hill and elsewhere across the city. The Catholic feast of **Christmas** is celebrated with particular enthusiasm in the Firingi Bazar quarter.
