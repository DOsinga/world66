The Zia Memorial Museum occupies Chittagong's former Circuit House, a colonial-era building that became part of Bangladesh's modern political history after President Ziaur Rahman was assassinated there in 1981. The museum is therefore as much about place as exhibits.

It will appeal most to travellers trying to understand Bangladesh beyond markets and river ports. The displays are modest, but the building's role in the country's post-independence story gives the stop more weight than a routine local museum.
