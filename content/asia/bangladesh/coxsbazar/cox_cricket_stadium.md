A modern international cricket ground set just inland from the beach, built to Test match standard and one of the more scenically located cricket venues in the subcontinent. Cox's Bazar hosted its first international matches here in the early 2020s, and the stadium has since become part of the Bangladesh Cricket Board's regular rotation.

Even if there is no match during your visit, the stadium is worth a look from outside. During Bangladesh home series, tickets for domestic fixtures are affordable and the atmosphere is lively. The ground has relatively compact stands and good sightlines. Check the BCB website for upcoming fixtures — the stadium has limited traffic from international fixtures but sees reasonable domestic action during the Bangladesh Premier League and national team training blocks.
