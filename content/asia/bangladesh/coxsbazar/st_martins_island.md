Bangladesh's only coral island, lying 9 kilometres south of the Teknaf peninsula at the southern tip of the country. The island is small — about 8 square kilometres — with coconut palms, a sandy shore, and a fringing coral reef. The water is clear and the pace is slow; this is a genuinely different experience from the mainland.

Boats run daily from Teknaf during the tourist season, roughly November through March; the journey takes two to three hours each way. In the monsoon season (April to October) the sea is too rough for regular boat service and the island effectively closes. Day trips are possible but an overnight stay allows you to experience the island in the evening after the day visitors have gone. Accommodation is simple. The reef snorkelling is worth doing even though the coral has seen better days — visibility is usually good and the fish life is still reasonable by South Asian standards.
