Bahadur Shah Park is a historic open space in Old Dhaka, surrounded by dense streets and several older civic, religious, and educational buildings. It was once Victoria Park, a central public space in the colonial city.

The park is best known for its memorials connected with the 1857 uprising, when rebels and civilians were executed here. It is not a quiet garden, but it gives a compact view of Old Dhaka's layered political history.
