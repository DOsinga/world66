Dhakeshwari Temple is Bangladesh's national Hindu temple and one of the most important religious sites in Dhaka. The temple is traditionally linked to the 12th century and remains a living place of worship, especially during Durga Puja.

It pairs naturally with Lalbagh Fort and the lanes of Old Dhaka, giving a fuller picture of the city's layered religious history.
