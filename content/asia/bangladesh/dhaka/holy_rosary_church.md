Holy Rosary Church is one of Dhaka's older Christian landmarks, with roots in the Portuguese Catholic community of the 17th century. Its bright repainting and later additions make it less austere than many colonial churches, but the history is still the reason to come.

The church is a short, quiet stop rather than a major sight. It is most interesting if you are tracing the layered religious history of old and central Dhaka.
