The Mausoleum of Three Leaders holds the graves of A. K. Fazlul Huq, Huseyn Shaheed Suhrawardy, and Khawaja Nazimuddin, three major political figures from Bengal's 20th-century history.

Its position near Curzon Hall and Suhrawardy Udyan makes it a natural short stop while exploring central Dhaka's civic and university landmarks.
