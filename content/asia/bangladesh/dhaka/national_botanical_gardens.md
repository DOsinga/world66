The National Botanical Gardens cover a large green tract in Mirpur, offering a rare break from Dhaka's traffic and noise. The grounds are best for an unhurried walk among trees, ponds, and plant collections, and they sit next to Dhaka Zoo, so the two can be combined into a longer family-friendly outing.
