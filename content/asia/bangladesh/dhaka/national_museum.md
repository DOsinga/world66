Bangladesh National Museum is the broadest introduction to the country's history and culture in Dhaka. Its collections include sculpture, painting, manuscripts, coins, arms, textiles, and archaeological material from Hindu, Buddhist, and Muslim periods, making it a useful first stop before diving into the city's older quarters and independence sites.
