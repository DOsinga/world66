Dhaka's National Museum of Science and Technology is an educational museum with displays on science, technology, and modern discovery. Its weekend starwatching sessions add a hands-on reason to go beyond the permanent exhibits.

It is best treated as a practical family or school-age stop, especially for visitors staying in northern Dhaka or combining it with the nearby planetarium and National Assembly area.
