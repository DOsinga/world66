Ramakrishna Mission is a Hindu temple and community complex in central Dhaka, founded in 1916. The site is known for Indian-influenced architecture and for its role as a long-running religious and cultural institution.

It is a worthwhile short stop for visitors interested in Dhaka's religious mix, especially when combined with nearby older-city sights such as Baldha Garden, Shankhari Bazaar, and the historic churches and mosques around Old Dhaka.
