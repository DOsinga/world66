Ramna Green is one of central Dhaka's largest open spaces, a broad parkland area with roots in Mughal gardens and old tombs. In a dense, noisy city, its lawns and tree cover give it real breathing room.

It sits near several important civic and cultural sights, so it fits naturally into a central Dhaka walk. Go in daylight and expect a busy local park rather than a manicured showpiece.
