Saat Masjid is a 15th-century mosque known for its seven white domes and compact Mughal-era form. It is one of Dhaka's older religious buildings and a useful stop for visitors tracing the city's pre-colonial architecture.

The mosque is still a place of worship, so visit respectfully and avoid prayer times if you only want to look at the building.
