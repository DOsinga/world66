Dinajpur is a north Bangladeshi town that looks ordinary at first, but has a stronger set of day-trip anchors than many larger places. Its appeal is in old brick, terracotta, water, and the slow rhythm of a provincial town close to the Indian border.

The outstanding sight is [Kantajew Temple](/asia/bangladesh/dinajpur/kantajew_temple), one of Bangladesh's finest terracotta Hindu temples. Closer to town, [Dinajpur Rajbari](/asia/bangladesh/dinajpur/dinajpur_rajbari) gives you the remains of a zamindar palace complex, while [Ramsagar National Park](/asia/bangladesh/dinajpur/ramsagar_national_park) centres on a large 18th-century tank surrounded by trees.

Dinajpur is not a polished tourist town. Come if you are already travelling through northern Bangladesh and want a day with real local texture rather than another bus connection.
