Central Park and Zoo is a large green area by the Padma River, with lawns, trees, and a small zoo. It is one of Rajshahi's easier family stops and gives a break from the denser parts of the city.

The river setting is part of the appeal, so combine it with time along the nearby embankment if the weather is comfortable.
