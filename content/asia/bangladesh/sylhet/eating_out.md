---
title: "Eating Out"
type: section
---

Sylhet's cuisine is distinctive within Bangladesh — milder, more sour, and with a particular love of fish (especially the river fish *boal* and *chital*) and the small fermented limes called *satkora*. The expatriate connection to London means that "Indian" restaurants in Britain owe much of their character to Sylheti cooks.

**Panshi Restaurant** — A legendary local eatery on Jallarpar Road, famous for its boal fish curry, chicken roast, and a wide variety of bhortas (mashed vegetable dips). Always packed at lunchtime; arrive early or expect to wait. Cheap and excellent.

**Pansi Restaurant** (separate establishment) — Another classic on the same street with similar food and similar queues; locals argue endlessly about which is better.

**Woondaal** and **Buffet Park** — Modern restaurants near Zindabazar with Chinese, Thai, and continental menus aimed at the city's middle class. Cleaner and air-conditioned, less authentic.

**Tea** — The Nilkantha Tea Cabin in Srimangal (the famous seven-colour tea) is worth a day trip in itself.
