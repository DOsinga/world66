---
title: "Shopping"
type: section
---

Sylhet's shopping is concentrated in **Zindabazar**, **Bondorbazar**, and **Lalbazar** — the dense old commercial streets around the Keane Bridge and dargah, where you'll find everything from cheap electronics to clothing, gold jewellery, and traditional crafts.

The best local buys are:

- **Manipuri textiles** — woven sarees and shawls from the Manipuri community around Sylhet, with distinctive geometric patterns. Cooperatives sell direct.
- **Shitalpati mats** — cool, silky woven mats from the *murta* reed, a Sylhet speciality.
- **Tea** — fresh from the estates, far better than what is exported. Tin packs make good gifts.
- **Cane and bamboo craft** — baskets, lamps, and furniture from the rural traditions of the region.

**Blue Water Shopping City** is the largest modern mall, with international brands and a food court. Prices are higher than the bazaars but no haggling.
