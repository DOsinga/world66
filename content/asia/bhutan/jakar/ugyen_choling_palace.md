Ogyen Choling sits high above the Tang Valley, far enough from Jakar that the journey feels like part of the visit. The manor complex grew from a religious site associated with Dorji Lingpa and later became one of Bhutan's great family estates; its central tower now houses a private museum on household life, ritual objects, and the old political world of Bumthang.

It is not a quick tick-box sight. The reward is the slow approach through the valley, the traditional buildings clustered around the old manor, and a museum that explains Bhutan through rooms, tools, textiles, and family memory rather than through a national showcase. If you have time for only one longer excursion from Jakar beyond the main temples, this is one of the most distinctive.
