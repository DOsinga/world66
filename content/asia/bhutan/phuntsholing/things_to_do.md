---
title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Phuntsholing's sights are compact and can be covered in a morning. The [Bhutan Gate](#bhutan_gate) is the obvious starting point — the ornate archway marking the border is one of the most photographed spots in the country. A short walk into town brings you to [Zangto Pelri Lhakhang](#zangto_pelri_lhakhang), a serene temple rising above the market area. For those with more time, [Kharbandi Gompa](#kharbandi_gompa) sits above town on the Thimphu road, offering sweeping views over the plains, and the [Amo Chhu Crocodile Breeding Centre](#amo_chhu_crocodile_breeding_centre) beside the river is worth an hour if you have never seen a gharial up close.
