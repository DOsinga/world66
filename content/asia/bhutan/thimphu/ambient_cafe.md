A small, relaxed cafe in central Thimphu serving filter coffee, fresh juices, toasted sandwiches, and a simple breakfast menu. The interior is quieter than most cafes near the town centre, with wooden furniture and local artwork on the walls. Opens early, making it a good option before the sightseeing circuit begins. The coffee is made from locally grown beans from southern Bhutan, where small-scale coffee cultivation has been developing since the 2010s.
