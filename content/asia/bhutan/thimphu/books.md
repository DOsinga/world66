title: Books
type: section
---

Bhutan has attracted thoughtful writers since it opened cautiously to tourism in the 1970s. Two useful starting points are Jamie Zeppa's *Beyond the Sky and the Earth* (1999), a memoir of teaching in rural Bhutan that remains one of the most honest accounts of life in the kingdom, and Linda Leaming's *A Field Guide to Happiness* (2014), which covers daily life in Thimphu. For a darker angle, Kunzang Choden's *Chilli and Cheese* is a collection of Bhutanese short stories by the country's first female novelist in English. Karma Ura's *The Hero with a Thousand Eyes* reconstructs a 17th-century Bhutanese epic and gives unusual access to the historical worldview behind the dzong architecture that dominates the capital.
