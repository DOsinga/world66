---
title: Day Trips
type: section
---

Several good day excursions are possible from Thimphu. Paro — with its famous Tiger's Nest monastery — is 54 km away on a good road (about 1.5 hours). Punakha Dzong, considered the most beautiful fortress-monastery in Bhutan, is a two-hour drive over the Dochu La pass. Simtokha Dzong is just 5 km south of the city. All these trips require being arranged through your licensed Bhutanese tour operator as part of your permit package.
