Several good day excursions are possible from Thimphu, all of which must be arranged through your licensed Bhutanese tour operator as part of your permit package.

[Paro](/asia/bhutan/paro) is the obvious first choice — a 54 km drive west (about 1.5 hours) to the valley that holds Taktsang, the Tiger's Nest monastery clinging to a cliff face above the pine forest. The town has its own dzong and a clutch of medieval temples, and Bhutan's international airport sits at the valley floor.

[Punakha](/asia/bhutan/punakha), the old winter capital, is a two-hour drive over the Dochu La pass. Punakha Dzong, set at the confluence of the Pho Chhu and Mo Chhu rivers, is the most beautiful fortress-monastery in the country, and the drive there through rhododendron forest is as memorable as the destination.

Closer to the city, [Dochu La Pass](/asia/bhutan/thimphu/dochu_la) at 3,100 metres makes a worthwhile half-day stop in its own right, its 108 chortens framing the high Himalaya on clear winter mornings. [Simtokha Dzong](/asia/bhutan/thimphu/simtokha_dzong), the oldest dzong in Bhutan, is just 5 km south of the centre and easily combined with any trip down the valley.
