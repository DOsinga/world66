A small shop near the town centre selling a well-edited range of Bhutanese products: locally distilled spirits, flavoured teas, handmade paper goods, and a selection of locally produced food items. Useful for stocking up on things that are genuinely made in Bhutan rather than Nepal — the labelling is clear about origin. Red Panda beer and Bhutan Mist whisky are available to take home. Reasonable prices; friendly staff who can explain the products.
