The principal street of Thimphu, running south from the Memorial Chorten to the clock tower square and beyond. Norzin Lam is the best walk for understanding the city's daily life: monks in maroon robes pass office workers in traditional gho, tourists negotiate with handicraft sellers, and the traffic moves with notable patience for a capital city. The street-level shops sell everything from mobile phones to Bhutanese boots. In the evenings, food stalls appear selling steamed momos, and the pavement becomes a slow promenade.
