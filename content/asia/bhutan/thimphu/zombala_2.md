A small, busy local restaurant in central Thimphu known for cheap, filling Bhutanese and Tibetan food. The menu covers beef momos, thukpa (noodle soup), and a rotating daily special of rice and curry. Tables are shared and turnover is fast. Popular with students, monks, and office workers on lunch breaks. Prices are significantly lower than at tourist-oriented restaurants, and the food is consistently good. Cash only; expect to wait for a seat at peak times.
