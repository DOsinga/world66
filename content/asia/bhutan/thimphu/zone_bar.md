A sports bar above the Changlimithang stadium, one of the main gathering points for Thimphu's expat community and young Bhutanese professionals. The menu covers burgers, pizza, steaks, and some Bhutanese dishes. Screens show major football, cricket, and tennis matches. The atmosphere is lively on evenings when big games are on, and the rooftop section has views over the stadium and the hills beyond. More casual in atmosphere than most places in the city, and open late by Thimphu standards.
