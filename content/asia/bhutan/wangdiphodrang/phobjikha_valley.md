The Phobjikha Valley is a broad U-shaped glacial valley at around 3,000 metres, about 70 km east of Wangdue Phodrang town. From late October to mid-February it becomes the wintering ground for several hundred **black-necked cranes** that migrate down from the Tibetan plateau.

The Royal Society for the Protection of Nature (RSPN) runs a small visitor and information centre at the edge of the wetland, with viewing scopes and exhibits on the cranes' biology and the conservation programme. The hillside above the valley is dominated by **Gangtey Goemba**, a 17th-century monastery whose courtyard offers the panoramic view of the valley that ends up in most photographs. Several short trails — the Gangtey Nature Trail being the easiest — let you walk down through the pine forests to the valley floor.
