Jerudong Park is Brunei's famous amusement park, built on a scale that made it part playground, part national curiosity. Its glory days are behind it, and that is worth saying plainly: some travellers will find it faded, while families may still enjoy the rides, water play, and evening atmosphere.

Check opening times before going, because the park operates more as an afternoon and evening outing than a full-day theme park. It is best for travellers with children, amusement-park completists, or anyone curious about Brunei's 1990s oil-boom ambition in physical form.
