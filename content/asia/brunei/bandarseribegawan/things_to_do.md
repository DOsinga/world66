---
title: Things to Do
type: section
---

Bandar Seri Begawan's highlights are spread across a compact city centre — two of the world's most photogenic mosques, a sprawling water village that has been continuously inhabited for centuries, a surprisingly engrossing museum, and accessible rainforest and river trips on the edge of town. The city rewards a slow pace. Walk the waterfront in the early morning, visit a mosque mid-morning before prayers, eat at the night market after dark.
