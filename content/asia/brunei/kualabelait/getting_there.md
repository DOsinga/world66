---
title: Getting There
type: section
---

Kuala Belait is 100 km southwest of [Bandar Seri Begawan](/asia/brunei/bandarseribegawan) along the coastal highway. The drive takes around 90 minutes on good roads.

Bus services run between BSB's main bus terminal and Kuala Belait several times daily; the journey takes around two hours and is very cheap. Buses also connect to the Sarawak border crossing at Sungai Tujuh/Miri — you will typically need to change in Seria, 10 km up the road from KB.

For those continuing to [Miri](/asia/malaysia/sarawak/miri) in Malaysia, the border crossing at Sungai Tujuh is around 20 km south of Kuala Belait. Taxis from KB to the border are the easiest option.
