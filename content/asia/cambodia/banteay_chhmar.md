Banteay Chhmar is a village and temple complex in northwestern [Cambodia](/asia/cambodia), close to the Thai border and well beyond the easy Angkor circuit. The ruins date from the Angkorian period, but the mood is very different from [Siem Reap](/asia/cambodia/siemreap): fewer visitors, more silence, and a stronger sense that the jungle is still negotiating with the stone.

The main draw is [Banteay Chhmar Temple](/asia/cambodia/banteay_chhmar/banteay_chhmar_temple), one of the great Khmer temple complexes outside Angkor. Its bas-reliefs, face towers, broken galleries, and satellite shrines reward slow wandering. The village beside it provides simple services, homestays, guides, and community tourism rather than polished resort infrastructure.

This is a place for travellers who can handle rough edges. Access is usually from Sisophon, and you should check current border-area security advice before committing to the trip.
