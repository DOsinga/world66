Meborn Baray is the large reservoir associated with the Banteay Chhmar complex. It once helped support the settlement and temple system; today it is a quiet open-water landscape used by birds and local villagers.

It is close enough to reach on foot or by bicycle from the community tourism office. In the dry season, the island temple in the middle may be reachable.
