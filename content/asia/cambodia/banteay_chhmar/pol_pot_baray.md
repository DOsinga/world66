Pol Pot Baray is a reservoir north of Banteay Chhmar, built during the Khmer Rouge period under brutal conditions. Today it is an important local water source and a stark reminder of more recent history beyond the area's ancient temples.

It is usually reached by moto, and access can be difficult in the rainy season. The stop is most meaningful for visitors already exploring the Banteay Chhmar area in depth.
