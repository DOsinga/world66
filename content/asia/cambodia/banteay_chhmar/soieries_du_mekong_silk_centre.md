Soieries du Mekong Silk Centre adds a living craft stop to a temple-heavy visit. The workshop trains local women in silk weaving, and visitors can usually see part of the process before browsing the finished products.

It is a useful place to spend money locally. The work is more interesting than the usual souvenir table, and it helps connect the temple visit to the village around it.
