---
title: Eating Out
type: section
---

Battambang has a small but lively restaurant scene that has grown with the town's popularity among backpackers and independent travellers. The night market along the riverside is the easiest option for a cheap meal — grilled meats, fresh noodles, and Cambodian curries served at plastic tables under the stars.

**Jaan Bai** (The Rice Bowl) is the most-recommended restaurant in town, run as a social enterprise training young Cambodians in hospitality. The menu covers Cambodian home cooking and some Western dishes, and the food is genuinely good. **Nary Kitchen** and **The Lonely Tree Cafe** are popular spots for Khmer cuisine at budget prices with outdoor seating.

For something more substantial, the **White Rose restaurant** in the centre has been serving Cambodian and Thai food to travellers for years. The riverside restaurants south of the old market are fine for a beer and a plate of rice at sunset.
