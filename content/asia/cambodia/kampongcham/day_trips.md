Kampong Cham makes a good midway stop on the long road north from **Phnom Penh** to **Kratie** and the **Irrawaddy dolphin** stretch of the Mekong. Closer to town, the older brick sanctuaries of **Wat Hanchey** make a good half-day trip upriver, and the wooden pagoda of **Wat Maha Leap** with its silk-weaving villages is an easy detour to the south.
