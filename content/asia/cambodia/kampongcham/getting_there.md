---
title: Getting There
type: section
---

Kampong Cham is on **National Road 7**, the main highway from Phnom Penh towards Laos and Vietnam, about 120 kilometres northeast of the capital — three hours by road on a good day. Frequent **minibuses and shared taxis** run from Phnom Penh's Central Market and Sorya Mall stations through the day; comfortable air-conditioned **Giant Ibis** buses run twice daily. Coming from the north, buses from **Kratie** and **Stung Treng** stop here, and there are direct minivans from **Siem Reap** several times a day.

Slow boats up the Mekong from Phnom Penh ran for a few years in the 2010s but no longer operate on a regular schedule. Private boat charters can still be arranged for groups.
