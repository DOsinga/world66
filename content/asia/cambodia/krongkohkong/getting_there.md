---
title: "Getting There"
type: section
---

Koh Kong sits at the Cambodian end of the Cham Yeam border crossing with Thailand, 9 kilometres from Hat Lek on the Thai side. From [Koh Chang](/asia/thailand/kohchang) or Trat, a minivan to the border plus a tuk-tuk in town takes around three hours.

From inside Cambodia, the road south from [Phnom Penh](/asia/cambodia/phnompenh) (about 6 hours by bus) and the road from [Sihanoukville](/asia/cambodia/sihanoukville) (about 4 hours) both arrive at the river bridge. There are no scheduled flights — the small Koh Kong airport handles charters and military traffic only.
