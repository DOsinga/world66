The Indigenous Peoples' Market in Sen Monorom operates primarily in the early morning, when Bunong and other highland community members come to trade produce, handicrafts, and goods from the surrounding villages. The market runs along a side street near the town centre and is one of the most authentic local markets in northeastern Cambodia.

The goods on offer range from fresh vegetables, forest products, and herbs to traditional textiles, baskets, and small carved items. The woven cloth produced by Bunong women using traditional patterns and natural dyes is of particular quality and interest. Arrive early — by 8am most of the interesting traders have set up; by 10am the market is winding down. Negotiating respectfully and buying directly from the maker is the norm.
