Leng Khin Waterfall is a single-drop cascade of about 25 metres, located about 45 minutes north of Sen Monorom followed by a short uphill walk. The falls drop into a deep pool suitable for swimming, and the forest around them is more intact than the area around the more accessible Bou Sra. It sees fewer visitors than the province's other main waterfalls, which is a recommendation in itself.

Reaching Leng Khin requires either a rented motorbike and comfortable navigation of forest tracks, or arrangement through a guesthouse or tour operator in Sen Monorom. A tuk-tuk can also make the trip on the main road section, with the final walk on foot. Combine with a stop at a pepper or coffee farm in the area for a full day out.
