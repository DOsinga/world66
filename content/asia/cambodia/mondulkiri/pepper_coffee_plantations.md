Mondulkiri is one of Cambodia's more productive agricultural regions for highland crops: pepper, coffee, and more recently cardamom are all cultivated in the hills around Sen Monorom. The volcanic red soil and cooler temperatures create conditions similar to the highland growing regions of Vietnam, and several small family-run plantations welcome visitors for tastings and brief explanations of cultivation methods.

Most day trips to Bou Sra Waterfall pass by one or more plantations, and stopping to taste freshly ground Mondulkiri coffee and compare it with the white and black peppercorns grown on the same farm is a straightforward addition to any trip. The plantation owners typically speak some English or work with a guide. Coffee and pepper make genuinely good souvenirs — locally roasted coffee particularly, which is rarely exported.
