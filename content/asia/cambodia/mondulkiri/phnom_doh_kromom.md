Phnom Doh Kromom is a pair of hills just north of Sen Monorom town, the most prominent landmarks visible from the centre of the provincial capital. A trail leads to the top, taking about 45 minutes each way, passing through the characteristic Mondulkiri landscape of pine forest mixed with deciduous trees. The summit provides panoramic views over Sen Monorom and the rolling highland terrain.

The hills are considered sacred by the Bunong community and visitors should behave respectfully, particularly near any shrines or offerings at the summit. The walk is suitable for anyone in reasonable fitness and does not require a guide, though hiring a local guide adds cultural context. Go in the late afternoon for the best light and cooler temperatures.
