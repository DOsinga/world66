---
title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

The two central activities are elephant experiences and waterfall visits, and most itineraries combine them with a community trek to a Bunong village and some exploration of the highland scenery around Sen Monorom. The town itself is small and walkable; a single afternoon is enough to cover the market, the central stupa, and the views from the surrounding hillsides.

Day trips to Bou Sra Waterfall typically stop at pepper and coffee plantations on the way, and the Dak Dam area to the north has several smaller waterfalls and good forest scenery. For those wanting something more adventurous, multi-day motorcycle trips are possible on roads that become more forest track than highway.
