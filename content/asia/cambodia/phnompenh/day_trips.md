---
title: "Day Trips"
type: section
---

Phnom Penh has fewer obvious day trips than Siem Reap, but a couple of half-days will get you outside the city. The Killing Fields of Choeung Ek (15 km south) are usually combined with Tuol Sleng into a single sobering morning. The Mekong island of **Koh Dach**, 30 minutes by ferry from the city, is a low-key day of silk weaving and rural cycling. The 11th-century pre-Angkorian temple of **Phnom Chisor**, 60 km south, is a long climb in the heat but rewarding for archaeology enthusiasts. The riverside town of **Udong**, 40 km north, was Cambodia's capital before Phnom Penh and has a small cluster of temples on a hill.

For anyone with more time, **Kampot** (3 hours south by road) and the colonial hill station of **Bokor** make a worthwhile overnight, as does the riverside town of **Kratie** (5–6 hours north) for a chance to see the freshwater Irrawaddy dolphins.
