---
title: "Eating Out"
type: section
---

Ban Lung's restaurant scene is basic but improving. Most travellers eat at the half-dozen guesthouse restaurants on the main road, all of which do the standard Cambodian-and-Western menu — *amok*, *lok lak*, banana pancakes, fried noodles — competently rather than memorably. The market is where to look for genuine local food: hilltribe rice porridge for breakfast, grilled river fish for lunch.
