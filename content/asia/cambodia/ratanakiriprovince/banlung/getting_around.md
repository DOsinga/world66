---
title: "Getting Around"
type: section
---

The single best way to see Ratanakiri is by **motorbike** — most guesthouses rent automatic 110cc scooters for USD 6–10 a day. The roads to Yeak Laom, the waterfalls, and the closer villages are red-dirt tracks, mostly passable in the dry season but tricky and sometimes impassable after heavy rain.

If you would rather not ride yourself, **moto-taxis** can be hired by the half-day or full day; rates are negotiable but expect USD 15–25 a day plus fuel. For longer excursions, hire a 4x4 with driver from a guesthouse — Virachey treks and the Voen Sai trip really require one. Tuk-tuks operate within Ban Lung itself but are scarce.
