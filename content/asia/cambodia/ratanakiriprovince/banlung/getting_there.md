---
title: "Getting There"
type: section
---

Ban Lung is genuinely remote. The standard overland approach is by **bus from Phnom Penh** via Stung Treng — 12 to 14 hours on roads that range from acceptable to terrible. Several companies run overnight services; pick one that uses minibuses rather than the cramped local buses if you can.

From the south, you can also reach Ban Lung from **Kratié** (about 8 hours) or **Sen Monorom** in Mondulkiri (rough cross-country dirt road, 8–10 hours and not always passable in the wet season). The road from the Lao border at Voen Kham (south of Pakse) makes Ban Lung a feasible entry from Laos.

**Ratanakiri Airport** outside town has irregular flights to Phnom Penh — schedules change often.
