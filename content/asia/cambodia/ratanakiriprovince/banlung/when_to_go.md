---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

**November to February** is the best time — dry, relatively cool (mornings can drop to 18°C), with manageable roads and clear views from the waterfalls. This is also peak season; book ahead at the better guesthouses.

**March to May** brings serious heat (35°C+) and dust; the waterfalls dwindle and the crater lake is at its swimmable best.

**June to October** is the wet season. The forest turns lush, the waterfalls thunder, and the roads turn to red mud — getting between sites can become a serious adventure. Some routes (the Voen Sai cemeteries, parts of Virachey) become inaccessible.
