Angkor Botanical Garden gives Siem Reap a gentler green stop away from temple circuits, markets, and hotel pools. The garden is designed around Cambodian plants, shaded walks, ponds, and educational displays, making it a useful reset when the heat and stone of Angkor start to blur together.

It is not a substitute for the temples, of course, but it works well on a slower day or with children who need space to wander. Go early or late, and treat it as a calm hour in the city’s expanding cultural landscape rather than a headline attraction.
