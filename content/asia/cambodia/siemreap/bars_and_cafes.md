---
title: Bars and Cafes in Siem Reap
type: section
---

Siem Reap has a lively bar and cafe scene concentrated around Pub Street and the Old Market, but increasingly spread through the Kandal Village and Wat Bo neighbourhoods to the east.

**[Pub Street](/asia/cambodia/siemreap/pub_street)** (Street 8) is the main nightlife strip: bar-heavy, loud, and busy from 6pm until well after midnight. The **Angkor What?** bar at the western end is the city's oldest and still a first-stop institution. For a quieter cocktail, **[Miss Wong](/asia/cambodia/siemreap/miss_wong)** in The Lane behind Pub Street is the best option in the area — a 1930s Shanghai-styled bar with genuinely well-made drinks.

The **Kandal Village** area around Hap Guan Street and Street 9 has better options for those who want good coffee or a craft cocktail without crowd noise. Several rooftop bars have opened along the river promenade and are particularly pleasant at sunset.

For coffee, the city has developed a serious cafe culture. Look for Cambodian single-origin coffee — the country's coffee-growing north is producing increasingly distinctive beans.
