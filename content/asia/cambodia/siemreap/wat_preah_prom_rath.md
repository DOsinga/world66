Wat Preah Prom Rath is a bright, active temple compound near the centre of Siem Reap, with lotus-like modern architecture layered over a much older local story. Look for the reclining Buddha behind the main hall and the gate reliefs, where Khmer and Buddhist imagery meet in a way that feels more local than museum-like.
