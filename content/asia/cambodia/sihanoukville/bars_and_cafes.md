---
title: Bars and Cafes
type: section
---

The nightlife in Sihanoukville is heavy on Chinese-run casino bars and a thinner survivor population of older expat and backpacker hangouts.

**Otres** is where the more characterful evenings happen — beachfront bars with hammocks, fire shows, and a quietly weather-beaten crowd. **Big Easy** is the longest-running expat bar in the city centre and is still going. **Led Zephyr** and **Coasters** on Serendipity Beach Road catch a younger backpacker crowd.

For coffee, the **Kampot Pepper Cafe** on the road into town does a decent cup, and several Vietnamese-run cafes around Independence Square serve excellent iced drip coffee at low prices.
