The Ancient City Han Tombs are a protected archaeological site on the edge of Huaibei. The tombs are valued for what they show about Han dynasty burial architecture, including chamber layouts and carved brick and stone decoration.

This is a specialist stop rather than a polished headline sight, but it is worth seeking out if you are interested in early Chinese tombs and the older history of northern Anhui.
