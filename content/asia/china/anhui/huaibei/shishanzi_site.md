Shishanzi Site is a Neolithic archaeological site in the Huaibei area, protected for its importance to the study of early cultures in northern Anhui and the Huai River basin.

The site is best suited to travellers with a strong interest in archaeology. Facilities can be limited, so treat it as a planned cultural stop rather than a casual city-centre attraction.
