Sui-Tang Grand Canal Ancient Town is Huaibei's attempt to turn its canal history into a walkable heritage quarter. The district uses reconstructed streets, water, gates, and evening lighting to evoke the period when canal transport shaped towns across eastern China.

It is a managed scenic area rather than an untouched old town, so adjust expectations accordingly. The value is highest in the evening, when lights and reflections make the setting more theatrical. For travellers already in Huaibei, it gives a more atmospheric outing than the city's practical modern centre.
