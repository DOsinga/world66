Suixi Old Street Ancient Building Complex is a protected historic street near Huaibei. The bluestone-paved street runs for several hundred meters and preserves older shopfronts and buildings from the town's trading past.

It is best approached as a short heritage walk, with the interest in the surviving street pattern and architecture rather than any single monument.
