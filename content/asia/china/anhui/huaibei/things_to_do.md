---
title: Things to Do
type: section
---

Huaibei's attractions sit at the intersection of coal-country reinvention and genuine archaeological depth. The city's best arguments for a visit are the Han dynasty stone-carved tomb pictorial slabs in the museum, the ruins of the ancient Linhuan city that served as a Tang dynasty regional capital, and the Longjishan scenic ridge with its panoramic sky road above the North China Plain. Xiangshan Park, where the temple sits above the city on a forested hillside, gives the best overview of the urban landscape.
