Ciguang Pavilion, also known as Mercy Light Temple, sits at the lower end of Huangshan's front mountain route. For many walkers it is less a quiet temple visit than the point where the mountain experience begins, with paths and cable-car access leading up toward the granite peaks.

The pavilion is worth noticing before you rush uphill. Its long association with Huangshan travel, temple architecture, and position at the edge of the climb make it a useful threshold between the resort infrastructure below and the famous pine-and-rock scenery above.
