The Li Bai Cultural Tourism Area centres on the tomb and memorial landscape of Li Bai, the great Tang poet whose final years are tied to Dangtu, southeast of Ma'anshan. The complex includes memorial halls, stele, poetry references, and the green foothills traditionally associated with his burial place.

It is a specialist stop, but a good one if Ma'anshan's Li Bai connection is what brought you here. Read a few poems before going; otherwise the site can feel like a formal memorial park rather than a place alive with literary memory.
