Taihu Mount National Forest Park is a green day-trip area outside Ma'anshan, combining wooded hills, temple stops, and viewpoints over the surrounding countryside. It is a straightforward escape from the industrial river-city setting.

The park is most useful for travelers already in Ma'anshan who want a half-day outdoors. Expect a developed Chinese forest-park experience, with paths and scenic-area infrastructure rather than remote hiking.
