Tongling Happy World is a local theme park with amusement rides and family entertainment. It is a light-hearted break from Tongling's museums, lake scenery, and copper-industry sights.

This is mainly useful for families travelling with children or anyone spending more than a quick pass through the city. Do not expect a major destination park; think of it as an easy local outing.
