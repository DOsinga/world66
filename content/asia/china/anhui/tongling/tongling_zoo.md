Tongling Zoo is a family-oriented stop with animal exhibits, several display areas, and simple interactive activities. It is most useful for travellers with children or for anyone looking for an easy outdoor break within the city.

Do not treat it as Tongling's main cultural sight, but it can pair well with nearby parks or the Tongling Museum if you have extra time in town.
