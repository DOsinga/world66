Fantawild Adventure is Wuhu's big theme-park outing, with roller coasters, family rides, indoor attractions, and stage shows aimed squarely at domestic visitors. It is not a historic sight, but it is one of the city's most substantial leisure attractions and a practical choice if you are travelling with children.

Expect crowds during school holidays and weekends. For most travellers it sits below Mirror Lake and the riverfront, but families may find it the easiest full-day activity in Wuhu.
