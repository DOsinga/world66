Maren Qifeng Scenic Area is a mountain landscape south of Wuhu with trails, rocky peaks, river rafting, and a glass bridge. The draw is the mix of Anhui scenery and built viewpoints, with enough to fill a half-day trip from the city.

Go for the views and walking rather than a quick photo stop. The area is outside central Wuhu, so it needs planning around transport and weather.
