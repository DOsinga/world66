Badachu is a hillside complex of eight Buddhist temples and monasteries at the foot of Beijing's Western Hills. The restored temples, wooded paths, and city views make it a quieter alternative to the capital's busiest central sights.

Plan it as a half-day outing if you want temple architecture, a walk in the hills, and a break from the denser parts of Beijing.
