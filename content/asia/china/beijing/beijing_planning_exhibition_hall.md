The Beijing Planning Exhibition Hall is a museum near Qianmen that explains the city's growth through maps, historic photographs, planning displays, and a large scale model of central Beijing. It is a useful stop for visitors who want to understand the layout of the capital before walking the old city or comparing it with the newer districts.
