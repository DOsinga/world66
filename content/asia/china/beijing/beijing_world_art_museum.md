Beijing World Art Museum is an art museum beside the China Millennium Monument, with exhibitions covering calligraphy, painting, and international art. The monument's large sundial-like form is part of the visit, and the site is a good western Beijing stop when combined with Yuyuantan Park or the Capital Museum.
