Bussiere Garden is a small Chinese garden built in 1923 by the French physician Jean Jerome Augustin Bussiere. It now serves as a Sino-French cultural exchange site, with historic grounds that make it a quieter stop in outer Haidian for visitors interested in Beijing's foreign community history.
