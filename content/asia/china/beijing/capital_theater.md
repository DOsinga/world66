Capital Theater is one of central Beijing's established stages for spoken drama, used by companies including the Beijing People's Art Theater. The programme leans toward contemporary and classical Chinese plays, so it is best for visitors who want a local theatre night rather than a tourist show.

The language barrier is real, but the building's location near Wangfujing makes it easy to pair with an evening in the centre. Check the current programme before going, as performances vary by season.
