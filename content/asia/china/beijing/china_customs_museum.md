The China Customs Museum is a national museum run by China's customs administration. Its displays cover the history of customs work, border controls, trade, and smuggling cases, making it a more specialised stop than Beijing's larger history museums.

It is best for travellers with an interest in modern institutions, trade, or administrative history.
