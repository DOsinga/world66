The Art Gallery and Library of China National Academy of Painting is a specialist stop for Chinese painting and related exhibitions. One unusual feature is an old Huizhou-style building moved here from Anhui, giving visitors a look at a regional architectural tradition that is otherwise less convenient to see from Beijing. Visits may require advance appointment.
