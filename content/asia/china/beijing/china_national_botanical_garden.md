China National Botanical Garden spreads across the western edge of Beijing near Fragrant Hills. It offers large planted grounds, seasonal flowers, greenhouses, and quieter wooded corners, including Cherry Glen, where Reginald Johnston, tutor to the last emperor Puyi, once had a villa.
