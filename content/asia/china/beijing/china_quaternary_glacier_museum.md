China Quaternary Glacier Museum is a specialist geology museum in western Beijing, focused on glaciers, landforms, and the Quaternary period.

It is a niche stop, but worthwhile for visitors with an interest in earth science or for families looking for a quieter museum outside Beijing's central cluster.
