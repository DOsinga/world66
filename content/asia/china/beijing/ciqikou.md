A specialist ceramics market in southeast Beijing dealing in porcelain from across China — Jingdezhen blue and white, Longquan celadon, Yixing purple clay teapots, and a wide range of reproduction antique pieces. The quality varies considerably between stalls, but the better vendors at the rear of the market stock genuine craftwork from named kilns at prices that compare favourably with gallery retail. A good place to buy quality teaware if you know what to look for.

Bargaining is standard. Bring photographs of what you want if you have specific requirements; the vendors can often source items not on display. The market is in the south of the city, not far from Panjiayuan; combining both markets in one trip makes sense. Access via subway (Line 10, Jinsong station) and a short taxi.
