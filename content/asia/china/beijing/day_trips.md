The Great Wall is the obvious day trip from Beijing, and the choice of section matters. [Mutianyu](/asia/china/beijing/great_wall_mutianyu) (70 km northeast) is the most practical restored section. [Simatai](/asia/china/beijing/simatai) and [Jinshanling](/asia/china/beijing/jinshanling) (130 km northeast) are wilder and better for serious hikers; the three-hour walk between them is the best Wall experience available from the city. The [Ming Tombs](/asia/china/beijing/ming_tombs) are in the same northern corridor as Mutianyu and worth combining.

The [Old Summer Palace](/asia/china/beijing/old_summer_palace) (Yuanmingyuan) is reachable by subway and can be combined with the [Summer Palace](/asia/china/beijing/summer_palace) in the same northwest corner of the city.
