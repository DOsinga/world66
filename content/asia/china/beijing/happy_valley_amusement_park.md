Happy Valley Amusement Park is one of Beijing's largest theme parks, with dozens of rides spread across themed areas. It is a full-day family stop rather than a cultural sight, useful if you want roller coasters and staged entertainment after several days of temples, museums, and palace grounds.

Expect crowds during weekends and school holidays, and check ride operations before making the trip across town.
