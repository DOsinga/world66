Jingtailing is the tomb of the Jingtai Emperor, a Ming ruler whose short and troubled reign ended with his removal from power. It is a quieter imperial tomb than the main Ming Tombs complex, but worthwhile if you are tracing Beijing's dynastic history in detail.
