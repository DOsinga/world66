The last home of Mei Lanfang, the most celebrated Peking Opera performer of the twentieth century and the artist who carried the form to Western audiences, sits in a quiet courtyard house off Huguosi Street near the Houhai lakes. Mei specialised in the *dan* female roles, and the rooms are kept much as he left them at his death in 1961 — furniture, costumes, photographs, and stage memorabilia among them.

It is a small, low-key museum that will mean most to those with some interest in Chinese theatre, but the preserved siheyuan itself is a pleasant example of a well-to-do courtyard residence. An easy addition to a walk around the Shichahai lakes.
