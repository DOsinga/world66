The Military Museum of the Chinese People's Revolution presents the official Chinese account of military history, with large halls of weapons, aircraft, boats, rockets, missiles, and armoured vehicles. Some of the displays cover captured foreign equipment, including items from the Korean War.

English interpretation is limited, but the hardware is impressive and the museum gives a useful look at how modern China tells its own military story.
