Niujie Mosque is Beijing's oldest and largest mosque, founded in the 10th century and rebuilt over many later dynasties. The complex looks Chinese at first glance, with palace-style roofs and courtyards, but the details mix in Arabic calligraphy and Islamic decoration. The surrounding Niujie area remains one of Beijing's clearest Muslim neighborhoods, so combine the mosque with a walk past the halal shops and restaurants nearby.
