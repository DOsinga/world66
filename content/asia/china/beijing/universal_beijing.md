Universal Beijing is the first Universal Studios theme park in China, opened in 2021 in Tongzhou. The resort is divided into themed zones including Hollywood Boulevard, Jurassic World Isla Nublar, Transformers: Metrobase, The Wizarding World of Harry Potter, Kung Fu Panda Land of Awesomeness, Minion Land, and WaterWorld.

It is a full-day family attraction rather than a casual Beijing sight. Check ticketing, transport, and opening hours before going, since the park sits well east of the central city and needs deliberate planning.
