A short lane branching off the Wangfujing shopping street, hung with red lanterns and lined with stalls, the snack street is best known for the shock-value skewers its vendors wave at passing tourists: scorpions (sometimes still wriggling), starfish, seahorses, and assorted insects, alongside more ordinary lamb kebabs, candied haws, and noodles.

It is frankly a tourist spectacle rather than a place locals eat, and the genuinely good street food is elsewhere in the city — but the theatre of it, especially after dark when the lanterns are lit, is hard to resist for a first-time visitor. Come to look and photograph; eat lightly.
