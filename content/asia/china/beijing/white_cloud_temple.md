White Cloud Temple is one of Beijing's most important Taoist temples, associated with Qiu Chuji and the Quanzhen tradition. Its halls, courtyards, ritual objects, and active religious life make it a strong stop for visitors interested in Chinese sacred sites beyond the imperial monuments.
