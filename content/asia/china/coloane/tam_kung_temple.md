The Tam Kung Temple, built in 1862 on the southern shore of Coloane, is dedicated to a Taoist deity venerated by seafarers throughout Guangdong and Macau. The temple is modest in scale but rich in atmosphere — incense smoke, red lanterns, and the particular quiet of a working place of worship that has been here for over 160 years.

The standout object is the whale bone dragon boat on the main altar: a long carved bone decorated with coloured silk banners, its origin unknown, its presence unexplained. The temple also holds a bronze spouting bowl — a resonating vessel that produces a standing wave when you rub the handles in the right way. It is one of the genuine curiosities of Coloane and well worth the short walk from the village square.
