Fujian Science and Technology Museum is a practical indoor stop near Wuyi Square, especially useful if you are travelling with children or need a rainy-day break from temples and old lanes. Exhibits lean hands-on, with physics, robotics, natural science, and space displays.

It is not the first museum to choose in Fuzhou if you only have one day; the Fujian Museum gives a stronger sense of the province. But for families, science fans, or anyone staying near the centre, it earns its place as an easy, low-friction visit.
