Fuzhou Art Museum occupies the former residence of Huang Peisong, a senior official from the late Qing period. The building gives the museum an added historical layer, with art displays set inside an old official residence rather than a neutral gallery box.

It is a useful stop for visitors already exploring the Yantai Mountain area or Fuzhou's older architectural pockets.
