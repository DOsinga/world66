Hualin Temple is not the biggest temple in Fuzhou, but it has one of the city's strongest architectural claims. Its main hall is an unusually early timber structure, a reminder that old Chinese buildings survived by repair, rebuilding, and stubborn continuity as much as by untouched preservation.

The compound is best approached slowly. Look at the proportions of the hall, the bracket sets, and the way the timber frame carries the roof; it is a different pleasure from the incense-heavy bustle of larger temples. Pair it with the nearby historic lanes if you want a compact walk through older Fuzhou.
