Luoxing Pagoda Park centres on Luoxing Pagoda, a landmark on the Min River near Mawei. The hilltop setting gives broad views over the river, shipyard area and the older harbour landscape east of Fuzhou.

It is a worthwhile stop for visitors already exploring Mawei's maritime history, with paths and viewpoints around the pagoda.
