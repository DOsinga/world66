Luoxing Pagoda Park sits in Mawei, southeast of central Fuzhou, around the old Luoxing Pagoda. The park has paths up a small hill to the base of the pagoda, with views over the river and the historic shipyard district nearby.

It is best folded into a Mawei outing rather than treated as a central Fuzhou sight. The appeal is the mix of river views, naval history and a quieter district that feels different from the city centre.
