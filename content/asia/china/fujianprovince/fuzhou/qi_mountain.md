Qi Mountain is a forested mountain park outside Fuzhou, known for waterfalls, wide views over the surrounding ranges, and a suspension bridge that makes the scenery feel more dramatic. The area is a good change of pace from temples and city streets.

Come prepared for walking and changing weather. The reward is a greener, cooler side of the Fuzhou area, with viewpoints that make the trip worthwhile on a clear day.
