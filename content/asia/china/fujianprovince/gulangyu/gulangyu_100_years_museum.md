Gulangyu 100 Years Museum focuses on the island's colonial-era foreign residents and treaty-port history. The museum occupies a former villa once linked to the Singapore-based OCBC Bank, which fits the subject well.

It is a useful companion to Gulangyu's architecture walks. Visit if you want more context for the villas, churches, consulates, and foreign institutions that shaped the island's unusual streetscape.
