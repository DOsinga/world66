Gulangyu Organ Museum is housed in the Trigram House, one of the island's landmark colonial-era buildings. The collection focuses on pipe organs and other historic organs, matching Gulangyu's wider reputation as China's "Piano Island."

It is an easy addition to a walking route around northern Gulangyu and pairs well with the Piano Museum and the island's preserved international-settlement architecture.
