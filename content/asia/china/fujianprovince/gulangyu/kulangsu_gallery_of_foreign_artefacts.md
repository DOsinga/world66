The Kulangsu Gallery of Foreign Artefacts from the Palace Museum Collection occupies a 19th-century heritage building that once housed the Hope Hospital and Nurses' School. The collection comes from the Palace Museum in Beijing and focuses on foreign objects that entered the imperial collection.

It is a good fit for Gulangyu, where colonial villas and treaty-port history already shape much of the island's character. Visit for both the displays and the building itself.
