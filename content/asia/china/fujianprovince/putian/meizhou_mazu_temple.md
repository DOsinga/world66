Meizhou Mazu Temple is the great pilgrimage focus of Putian's island coast, associated
with Mazu, the sea goddess worshipped across Fujian, Taiwan, and Chinese maritime
communities worldwide. For travelers, it is a vivid reminder that Putian's influence
travelled by boat as much as by road.

The temple is best understood as a living pilgrimage site, not only a historic monument.
Ferries, incense, festival crowds, and sea views are all part of the experience. Go with
time for Meizhou Island itself, because the setting explains why a protector of sailors
matters here.
