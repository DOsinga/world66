Quanzhou Tianhou Temple is one of the city’s clearest reminders that Quanzhou was once a great port. Dedicated to Mazu, the protector of sailors, the temple served merchants, seafarers, and migrant communities whose lives depended on the sea routes linking Fujian with Southeast Asia and beyond.

The complex is busy with worship rather than frozen as a museum. Incense, carved beams, guardian figures, and tiled roofs give it the dense visual language of Minnan temples, while its location near the old commercial city makes it easy to combine with Qingjing Mosque and the maritime museum. It is a small site with a large historical reach.
