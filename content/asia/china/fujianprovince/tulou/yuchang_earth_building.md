Yuchang Earth Building is one of the most memorable Fujian tulou, famous for its visibly leaning internal wooden columns and long history within the Hakka earth-building tradition. Its irregularity makes the structure feel alive rather than museum-perfect.

It is a strong stop for anyone visiting the Nanjing Tulou area, especially when paired with nearby clusters such as Tianluokeng. Respect residents and local rules: many tulou are heritage sites, but they are also connected to lived community space.
