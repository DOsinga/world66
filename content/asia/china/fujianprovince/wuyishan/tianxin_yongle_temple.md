Tianxin Yongle Temple is a quiet Buddhist temple in the Wuyi Mountains, close to the tea cliffs and walking routes that bring most visitors into this part of the park. The courtyards are simple and peaceful, with a tea room that fits the mountain's Da Hong Pao culture.

Lunch with the monks is sometimes possible by donation, but the main reason to come is the calm setting. It makes a good pause between busier scenic stops such as Wuyi Palace, Thread of Sky, and the tea-growing cliffs.
