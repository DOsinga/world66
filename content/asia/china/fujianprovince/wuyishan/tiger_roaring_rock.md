Tiger Roaring Rock is one of Wuyi Shan's rugged sandstone viewpoints, set among steep paths, forest, and cliff scenery. The highest point is sometimes closed, but the surrounding trails still give good views into the rocky heart of the scenic area.

It works best as part of a longer walk between Thread of Sky and Jade Beauty Peak, rather than as a standalone stop.
