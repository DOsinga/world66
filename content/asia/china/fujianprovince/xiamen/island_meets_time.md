Island Meets Time is a theme park near Xiamen's International Cruise Terminal, built around southern Fujian heritage. The themed streets, performance zones, folk games, costumed characters, and evening light shows make it more of a half-day family stop than a quick photo call.

It works best if you want a staged, easy introduction to Minnan culture rather than another museum or temple visit.
