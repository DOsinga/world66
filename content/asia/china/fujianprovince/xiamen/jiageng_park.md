Jiageng Park, also known as Kah Kee Park, commemorates Tan Kah Kee, the overseas Chinese entrepreneur and philanthropist who founded Xiamen University and Jimei University. The park combines waterfront paths, memorial architecture, and views across Jimei.

It is a good stop if you are already visiting Jimei and want to understand the district's links to Tan Kah Kee's educational legacy.
