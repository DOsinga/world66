Wuyuan Bay Wetland Park is Xiamen's largest wetland park, covering roughly 85 hectares around the bay. Boardwalks and paths make it a useful change of pace from the island's temples, shopping streets, and beach roads.

Come for a quiet walk, water views, and birdwatching rather than formal sightseeing.
