Xiamen Garden Expo Park is a large landscaped park in Jimei, built across exhibition islands, ecological islands, and peninsulas. The grounds combine formal garden displays with lake views and broad walking areas.

It is a good slower stop outside central Xiamen, especially for travelers who want gardens and open space rather than another dense urban attraction.
