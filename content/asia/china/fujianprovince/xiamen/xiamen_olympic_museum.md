Xiamen Olympic Museum is a specialist museum devoted to the Olympic Games, opened with approval from the International Olympic Committee and the China Olympic Committee. Exhibits cover Olympic history, symbols, and memorabilia rather than Xiamen itself.

It is a niche stop, but worthwhile for sports-history fans or anyone travelling with children who would rather see medals and mascots than another temple.
