This small courtyard site preserves a two-storey brick-and-wood building connected with early Communist Party organisation in Fujian. Exhibits cover revolutionary activity in the region, including party work in white areas, Soviet areas, and the guerrilla-war years.

It is a specialist history stop, but its compact scale makes it easy to include while exploring central Zhangzhou.
