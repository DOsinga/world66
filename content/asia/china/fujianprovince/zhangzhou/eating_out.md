---
title: Eating Out
type: section
---

Zhangzhou's food identity is older and more rooted than Xiamen's — this is where lor mee originated, where the oyster omelette style is lighter and crispier, and where the white tea and black tea traditions of Fujian reach the coast. The best eating is concentrated in the old city lanes near the Jiulong River and in the night market around Shengping Road.
