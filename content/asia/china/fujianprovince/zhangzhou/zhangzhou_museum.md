Zhangzhou Museum gives travellers a compact overview of a city often overshadowed by Xiamen and the Fujian tulou routes. Exhibits cover local history, folk culture, and the regional traditions that shaped this part of southern Fujian.

It is a sensible first stop if you are using Zhangzhou as more than a transit point. The museum helps connect the city to nearby temples, old streets, and rural heritage sites.
