The Museum of Xuanzang's Pilgrimage tells the story of the 7th-century Buddhist monk who travelled from China to India and returned with scriptures that reshaped Chinese Buddhism. His journey later became one of the main inspirations for *Journey to the West*.

For travellers crossing the Hexi Corridor, the museum gives useful context for Guazhou's Silk Road setting. It is a good companion to the region's Buddhist cave sites and desert ruins, especially if you are already visiting Suoyang City or the Yulin Caves.
