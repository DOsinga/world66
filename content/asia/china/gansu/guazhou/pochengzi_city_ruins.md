Pochengzi City Ruins are the remains of Changlecheng, an old county seat founded during the Han Dynasty and abandoned by the Tang period. Like many Silk Road sites around Guazhou, the attraction is the sense of a settlement reduced to earth, walls, and desert distance.

This is for travellers already interested in the region's archaeology, not a polished headline sight. It works best as part of a wider Guazhou circuit with Suoyang City, Qiaowan Fort, or the Yulin Caves.
