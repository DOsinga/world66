This memorial in Guazhou commemorates the Red Western Route Army and the Battle of Anxi, fought here in 1937 during the Chinese Civil War and the Long March period. It is a focused local history stop rather than a major museum, but it helps explain why this small desert town appears in modern Chinese revolutionary memory.
