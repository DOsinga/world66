Zhang Zhi Park is dedicated to Zhang Zhi, the second-century calligrapher associated with perfecting cursive Chinese calligraphy. The park, formally the Cultural Production Park of the Cursive Calligraphy Master's Hometown, includes a museum about his life and work.
