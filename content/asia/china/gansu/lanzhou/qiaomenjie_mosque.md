Qiaomenjie Mosque stands in a Hui neighbourhood south of Zhongshan Bridge. Tradition traces worship on the site back to the Ming dynasty, and the mosque has been listed as a provincial cultural relic, making it a worthwhile detour while exploring central Lanzhou's Yellow River sights.
