White Cloud Temple, also known as Luzu Temple, is a Taoist temple on the south bank of the Yellow River in Lanzhou. It is dedicated to Lu Dongbin and sits near Zhongshan Bridge, making it easy to combine with a riverside walk.

The temple is a modest stop, but it adds religious and local texture to the central Lanzhou riverfront.
