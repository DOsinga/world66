Tianshui Folk Culture Village presents traditional handicrafts, folk dances, and regional drama from Gansu. Visitors can watch demonstrations and, in some cases, take part in simple craft or performance activities.

It is a useful family-friendly stop for travellers who want a cultural activity between Tianshui's temples, museums, and the Maijishan Grottoes.
