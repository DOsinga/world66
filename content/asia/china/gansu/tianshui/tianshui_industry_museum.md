Tianshui Industry Museum occupies a former electrical equipment factory and focuses on the city's industrial past. The displays of old machinery and factory history are a useful counterpoint to Tianshui's temples and grottoes, especially for visitors interested in how inland Chinese cities developed in the 20th century.
