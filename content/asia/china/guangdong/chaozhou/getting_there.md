---
title: Getting There
type: section
---

Chaozhou does not have its own high-speed rail station; the nearest is Chaoshan Station, which serves both Chaozhou and [Shantou](/asia/china/guangdong/shantou) and is located between the two cities. From Guangzhou South Station, the journey to Chaoshan takes about two hours on a G-train. Local buses and taxis connect Chaoshan Station to Chaozhou city centre in about forty-five minutes.

From Shantou, buses run frequently and take about an hour. The combination of the two cities — Shantou for its modern energy and Chaozhou for its old city — makes an excellent base for exploring the Teochew heartland.
