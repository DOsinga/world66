The China Agarwood Culture Museum is devoted to agarwood, the fragrant resinous wood prized across China and Southeast Asia for incense, carving, medicine, and luxury objects. The displays explain how the wood forms, how it is graded, and why it became valuable.

It is a specialist museum, but a useful stop if you are interested in Chinese material culture or want a Dongguan sight beyond the better-known gardens and Opium War sites.
