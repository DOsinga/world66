The Dongguan Numismatic Museum is run by Dongguan Rural Commercial Bank and displays old coins, banknotes, and material connected with local banking history. It is a niche museum, but useful for visitors interested in Chinese currency, trade, and regional economic history.

Guided tours may be available, including in Mandarin, Cantonese, and English.
