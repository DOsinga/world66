Dongguan Xiangshi Zoo is a family-oriented zoo in Dongguan with animal exhibits and scheduled performances.

It is most useful for visitors travelling with children or looking for a simple day out away from the city centre. Check the current show times and transport options before setting out, as it sits well outside central Dongguan.
