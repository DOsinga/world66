The Guangdong Memorial Hall of the East River Column tells the story of the Dongjiang Column, a Communist-affiliated guerrilla force that fought Japanese forces during the Second World War and later took part in the Chinese Civil War.

The museum stands on a hill above Dawangling, where the column had its main base from 1940 to 1943. Several surviving village buildings connected with the base can also be visited.
