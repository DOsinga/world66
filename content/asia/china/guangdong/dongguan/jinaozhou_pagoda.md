Jin'aozhou Pagoda is a late Ming-era pagoda built between 1597 and 1624 and restored to its present form in the 18th century. It is noted for its long survival and slight, stable lean.

The pagoda is a compact historic stop in Dongguan, best combined with other older sights such as Keyuan Garden.
