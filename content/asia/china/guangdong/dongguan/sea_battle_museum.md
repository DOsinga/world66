The Sea Battle Museum is the main museum for the Opium Wars around Humen. Its exhibits cover the Qing background, the conflict itself, and its aftermath from the official Chinese historical view.

It pairs naturally with nearby Weiyuan Fort and Lin Zexu Memorial Museum, making this part of Dongguan the strongest stop for visitors interested in nineteenth-century history.
