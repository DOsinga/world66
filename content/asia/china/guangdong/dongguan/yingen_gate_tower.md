Ying'en Gate Tower is the last surviving piece of Dongguan's old city wall, built in the Ming dynasty in 1384. Modern Dongguan can feel defined by factories and wide roads, so this compact remnant is a useful reminder of the older walled city.

It is a quick stop rather than a long visit, but worth seeking out if you are tracing Dongguan's historic core.
