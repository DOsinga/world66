Chen Village Flower World is a large flower-growing and display area in Chencun, Shunde, long associated with Foshan's horticultural trade. The appeal is simple: rows of nurseries, seasonal blooms, and enough colour to make it more than a quick shopping stop.

It works best if you are already exploring Shunde or Foshan's quieter suburbs. Go during the day, when the flower markets and display gardens are active.
