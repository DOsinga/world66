Fengjian Water Village is an old canal settlement in Shunde, with stone bridges, narrow lanes, and waterside houses. Its history is often traced back many centuries, and the village still feels most atmospheric when explored on foot away from the busiest times.

Weekends can be crowded, while weekdays are calmer. During the Dragon Boat Festival, the waterways become part of local boat-racing celebrations.
