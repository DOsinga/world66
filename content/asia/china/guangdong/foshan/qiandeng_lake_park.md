Qiandeng Lake Park shows the modern side of Foshan, with water, bridges, landscaped paths, and surrounding towers reflected in the lake. It is not an old temple or kiln site, but it is a useful contrast to the city's historic core.

Go in the late afternoon or evening, when the light is softer and the park becomes a local promenade. It is a pleasant place to see contemporary urban life in Foshan after a day spent around ancestral halls, gardens, and ceramic history.
