Sanshui Forest Park is a large green escape in Foshan's Sanshui District. Its mix of lake paths, wooded slopes, a Confucian temple, and a giant reclining Buddha makes it more varied than a simple city park.

It is best treated as a relaxed family outing or a change of pace from Foshan's older urban temples and gardens.
