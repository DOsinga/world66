Chimelong International Circus is the big-ticket evening show in Guangzhou's Panyu entertainment district, with acrobats, aerial acts, dancers, and heavy stage effects packed into a fast 90-minute performance. It is polished, loud, and built for spectacle rather than subtlety.

The circus makes most sense if you are already spending time around the Chimelong resorts or travelling with children. Book ahead on busy weekends and holidays, when the area fills quickly.
