This modest compound in Huadu preserves the former home of Hong Xiuquan, the Taiping Rebellion leader whose movement shook late Qing China. It is most useful for travellers with an interest in 19th-century Chinese history, linking family rooms and local exhibits to one of the country's largest civil wars.
