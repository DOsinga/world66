Grandview Museum of Natural Science is a private natural history museum in Tianhe. The collection focuses on rocks, fossils, and dinosaur specimens, making it an easy indoor stop for families and science-minded visitors.

It is not Guangzhou's most essential museum, but it is useful if you are nearby in the shopping and business district and want something more educational than another mall walk.
