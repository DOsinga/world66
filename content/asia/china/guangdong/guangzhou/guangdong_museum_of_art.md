Guangdong Museum of Art focuses on modern and contemporary Chinese art, with particular strength in artists from Guangdong. It is a useful counterpoint to Guangzhou's older temples and colonial streets, showing the city's more recent cultural confidence.

Entry arrangements can vary by exhibition, and ID may be needed for free tickets. Combine it with a walk on Ersha Island or the Pearl River cultural district.
