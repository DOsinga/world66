This museum occupies a site tied to Sun Yat-sen, who was sworn in here as president of the Republic of China in 1921. The exhibits trace Guangdong's revolutionary history from the Opium War through the founding of modern China, making it a worthwhile stop for visitors trying to understand Guangzhou's political past.
