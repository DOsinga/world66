Guangzhou City Art Museum is a large art gallery near Yuexiu Park, with holdings that include calligraphy, traditional Chinese painting, sculpture, and Tibetan Buddhist thangka art.

It is a strong add-on to the Yuexiu Park and Nanyue King Tomb area, especially for visitors who want a quieter museum stop focused on Chinese visual culture.
