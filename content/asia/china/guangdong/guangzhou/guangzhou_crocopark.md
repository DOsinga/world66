Guangzhou CrocoPark is a crocodile-focused family attraction in Panyu, with live animals, photo stops, and themed displays built around one very specific reptile.

It is not a classic cultural sight, but it can be a useful half-day option for families who have already seen Guangzhou's main temples, museums, and riverfront landmarks.
