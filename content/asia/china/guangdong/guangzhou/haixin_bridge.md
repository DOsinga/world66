Haixin Bridge is Guangzhou's pedestrian bridge over the Pearl River, linking Ersha Island with the Tianhe side of the river. Opened in 2022, it has sweeping white curves and clear views toward the Canton Tower and the riverside skyline.

It is most worthwhile around dusk or after dark, when the riverfront lights come on. The bridge works well as part of a walk between the city's modern cultural district, Ersha Island, and the Pearl River promenades.
