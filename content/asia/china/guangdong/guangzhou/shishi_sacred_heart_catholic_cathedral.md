Shishi Sacred Heart Catholic Cathedral is one of Guangzhou's oldest church buildings and one of the largest Gothic Revival churches in southern China. Construction began in the 1860s, and the stone exterior still stands out in the old city.

The cathedral remains an active place of worship, so visit respectfully and check opening times before making a special trip.
