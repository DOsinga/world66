The Temple of 500 Genii is tied to the story of Bodhidharma, the Indian monk traditionally credited with bringing Chan Buddhism to China. It is a worthwhile Liwan temple stop for travellers interested in Guangzhou's older Buddhist history beyond the better-known Six Banyan and Guangxiao temples.
