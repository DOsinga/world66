---
title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Guangzhou rewards exploration across several distinct zones: the historic Yuexiu district in the centre, Shamian Island's colonial enclave in the southwest, and the gleaming modern towers of Tianhe and Zhujiang New Town across the Pearl River. The city is too big to cover comprehensively in a short visit — focus on the old town and the Pearl River promenade, then cross the river to see the Canton Tower and the Opera House.
