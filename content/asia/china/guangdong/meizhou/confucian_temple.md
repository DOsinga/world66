Meizhou Confucian Temple adds a scholarly and civic strand to a city usually introduced through Hakka culture. Its halls and courtyards point to the old examination-world values that shaped elite life across inland Guangdong.

It is not as essential as the Hakka Museum or the outlying ancestral landscapes, but it makes a worthwhile central stop. Visit it as part of a walk through older Meizhou, where temples, schools, and clan memory overlap rather than sit in separate categories.
