The Thousand Buddha Pagoda Temple is one of Meizhou's most visible Buddhist sites, centred on a pagoda and temple complex that gives the city a vertical landmark as well as an active place of worship. It is a useful stop for travellers trying to balance Hakka cultural sites with religious architecture.

The visit is mainly about atmosphere, views, and the contrast between temple space and the surrounding city. Go respectfully, especially during active worship periods, and treat it as a living religious site rather than just a viewpoint or photo backdrop.
