---
title: Things to Do
type: section
---

Qingyuan's main draws are its gorge parks north of the city, where water-carved limestone scenery meets the region's unusual brand of "water park meets nature park" tourism. The Feilai Temple, perched on a cliff above the Bei River, is the city's landmark sight. Beyond the tourist circuit, the Bei River itself offers boat trips, and the surrounding hills contain hot spring resorts popular with Guangzhou weekenders.
