Artron Art Center is a large art and publishing complex in Nanshan, best known for the Artron Wall: a huge wall of art books that has become one of Shenzhen's more unusual interiors. It suits the city well, sitting somewhere between gallery, design showcase, and cultural-tech statement.

Come if you are interested in contemporary Chinese art, design, or the way Shenzhen presents culture through scale and spectacle. It is less central than OCT-LOFT but pairs well with other Nanshan stops.
