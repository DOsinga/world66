The China Merchants History Museum tells the story of China Merchants Group, the state-owned company closely tied to the development of Shekou and modern Shenzhen. The exhibits are corporate in origin, but they also explain how this part of the city changed during China's reform era.

It is a niche stop, best for visitors already interested in Shenzhen's port, industry, and urban history rather than for a first pass through the city.
