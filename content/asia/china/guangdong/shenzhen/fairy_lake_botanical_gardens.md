Fairy Lake Botanical Gardens is one of Shenzhen's strongest green escapes, spread across foothills, valleys, streams, and lakes on the east side of the city. The gardens are large enough to feel like a day out rather than a quick urban park.

Come for the plant collections, lake views, and the chance to walk into the hills without leaving Shenzhen.
