Happy Valley is Shenzhen's large amusement park in the Overseas Chinese Town area, close to Window of the World and Splendid China. It is divided into themed zones with roller coasters, water rides, family attractions, stage shows, and the Playa Maya water park.

The park is a full half-day or day out rather than a quick sightseeing stop. It works best for families and visitors who want a break from museums, shopping, and city walks.
