Hezheng Art Museum is a private art museum in Shenzhen, founded in 2016 by businessman Li Weiqing. Its displays include Chinese paintings, calligraphy, ceramics, sculpture, and other works from private collections.

It is a specialist stop for visitors with an interest in Chinese art, especially if already exploring western Shenzhen.
