Hong Fa Buddhist Temple is a busy pilgrimage temple on the slopes of Wutong Mountain, within the Fairy Lake Botanical Gardens area. It is not especially old, but its hillside setting and constant flow of worshippers make it one of Shenzhen's more atmospheric religious sites.

It pairs naturally with a visit to Fairy Lake Botanical Gardens; allow extra time for the climb and crowds on festival days.
