Lianhuashan Park is Shenzhen's central green space — a large urban park spread across a low hill in Futian District, overlooking the CBD from the north. The hilltop affords one of the best free views in the city: looking south, you see the Futian Central Business District with the Ping An Finance Centre dominating the skyline, and beyond it the hills of Hong Kong's New Territories.

At the summit stands a large bronze statue of Deng Xiaoping, the architect of China's economic opening and the man responsible for designating Shenzhen as the country's first Special Economic Zone in 1980. The statue is a pilgrimage site for visitors from across China and is especially busy on national holidays.

The park itself is well maintained with walking paths, a lake, and areas where locals practise tai chi and fly kites in the mornings. Entry is free. The Shenzhen Museum and Civic Center are a short walk to the south.
