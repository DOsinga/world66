Lixin Lake is a landscaped reservoir in northeastern Shenzhen, surrounded by parks, trees, and well-kept walking paths. It is not one of the city's essential sights, but it is a pleasant local green space if you are already in this part of Shenzhen and want an easy lakeside walk.
