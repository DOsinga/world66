Nanshan Museum covers the history of Shenzhen's Nanshan district, with archaeological finds and local-history exhibits that show there was much more here before the city's modern boom. It is a useful stop if you are already exploring Nanshan or Shekou and want a break from malls, theme parks, and waterfront development.
