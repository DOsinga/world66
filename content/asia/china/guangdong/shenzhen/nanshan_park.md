Nanshan Park climbs the forested hill above Shekou, with mostly stair-stepped trails and broad views from the 336 m summit. Allow two to three hours if you are walking to the top and back; on a clear day the reward is a sweep across Shenzhen, the bay, and Hong Kong's New Territories.
