OCT Art and Design Gallery occupies a restored industrial building in Shenzhen's OCT area. The gallery focuses on industrial, domestic, fashion, and graphic design, with changing exhibitions that show why Shenzhen has become one of China's main design centres.

It is most useful if you are already visiting OCT-LOFT, Window of the World, or the surrounding creative district, but the building and exhibitions give the area a more contemporary edge than the theme parks nearby.
