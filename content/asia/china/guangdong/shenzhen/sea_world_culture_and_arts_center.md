Sea World Culture and Arts Center is a major design and culture venue in Shekou. The building hosts design exhibitions, cultural programming, and galleries near the Sea World shopping and entertainment district.

It is most useful for visitors interested in contemporary design or looking for a quieter cultural stop in Shenzhen's southwest.
