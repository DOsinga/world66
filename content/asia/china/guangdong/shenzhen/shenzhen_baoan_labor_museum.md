Shenzhen Bao'an Labor Museum tells the story of the migrant workers who built the factories, roads, and housing that turned Shenzhen from border towns into a megacity. It is a useful counterweight to the city's theme parks and skyline viewpoints.

The focus is on Bao'an district and the everyday labor behind Shenzhen's boom. Come here if you want to understand the human side of the city's manufacturing history rather than just its speed and scale.
