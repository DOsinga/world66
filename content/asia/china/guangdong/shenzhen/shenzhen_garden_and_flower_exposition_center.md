Shenzhen Garden and Flower Exposition Center is a large landscaped park created for an international garden exhibition. Its grounds mix Chinese and international garden styles, hothouses, a waterfall, and a hilltop pagoda with broad views on clear days.

It is one of Shenzhen's better green stops if you want a long walk rather than another mall or theme park.
