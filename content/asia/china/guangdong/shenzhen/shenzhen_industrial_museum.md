Shenzhen Industrial Museum tells the story of the city's manufacturing, design, and technology growth, from special economic zone to global hardware hub. The exhibits are most useful for visitors who want context for the factories, electronics markets, and rapid urban change that define modern Shenzhen.

Solo visitors can usually enter without advance booking, while groups may need to arrange a visit ahead of time.
