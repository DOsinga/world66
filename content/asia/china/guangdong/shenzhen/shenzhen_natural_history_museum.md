Shenzhen Natural History Museum is a large science museum in the city's eastern districts, built around natural history, geology, fossils, and the biodiversity of South China. It is designed for hands-on family visits rather than quiet specialist study.

The museum is a long ride from central Shenzhen, so it makes most sense if you are already heading east or travelling with children who want a break from malls and city viewpoints.
