---

Shenzhen rewards exploration in unexpected ways. The obvious starting point is [Dafen Oil Painting Village](/asia/china/guangdong/shenzhen/dafen_oil_painting_village), where thousands of artists produce copies of famous works at astonishing speed and quality. The [OCT-LOFT Creative Culture Park](/asia/china/guangdong/shenzhen/oct_loft) is the counterpoint — repurposed factory buildings that now house galleries, independent shops, and weekend markets. For the best single view of the city, head to the top of the [Ping An Finance Centre](/asia/china/guangdong/shenzhen/ping_an_finance_centre), the fifth-tallest building in the world.

[Lianhuashan Park](/asia/china/guangdong/shenzhen/lianhuashan_park) offers a rare green escape in the middle of Futian, with a large bronze Deng Xiaoping statue watching over the city he conjured into existence. The [Shenzhen Museum](/asia/china/guangdong/shenzhen/shenzhen_museum) covers the city's transformation from fishing village to megacity — the scale of change compressed into just four decades makes it genuinely compelling. [Huaqiangbei Electronics Market](/asia/china/guangdong/shenzhen/huaqiangbei_electronics_market) is chaotic, overwhelming, and unlike anywhere else on earth — floors of components, gadgets, and improbable knockoffs. [Window of the World](/asia/china/guangdong/shenzhen/window_of_the_world) is gloriously kitsch: 130 scaled-down world landmarks crammed into a single theme park.
