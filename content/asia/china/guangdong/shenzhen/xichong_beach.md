Xichong Beach is one of the more remote beaches on Shenzhen's eastern coast, well beyond the busier Dameisha area. It is less built-up than many city beaches, with cleaner sand, open sea views, and a quieter feel.

The distance from central Shenzhen is part of the appeal and part of the planning problem. Treat it as a deliberate day trip, not a quick stop between urban sights.
