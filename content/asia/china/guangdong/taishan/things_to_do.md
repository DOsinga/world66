title: Things to Do
type: section
---

Taishan's attractions centre on the diaspora story: the local museum, the Overseas Chinese Museum, and the emigrant villages scattered across the county. Shangchuan Island, reached by ferry, adds a beach and Catholic pilgrimage dimension. The diaolou watchtowers in the surrounding countryside, while less concentrated than Kaiping's UNESCO-listed clusters, reward exploration for those interested in the architectural legacy of the overseas Chinese connection.
