This former police station is a surviving piece of Zhanjiang's Guangzhouwan period, when the area was leased to France. Built in 1900, shortly after the colony was established, it later continued in use as a Chinese police station until 2013.

The building is worth a look as part of Zhanjiang's scattered colonial-era architecture, especially if you are already visiting the nearby Cathedral of Saint Victor.
