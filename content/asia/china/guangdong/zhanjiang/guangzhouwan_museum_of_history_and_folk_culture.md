The Guangzhouwan Museum of History and Folk Culture is a small museum focused on Zhanjiang's period as the French-leased territory of Guangzhouwan from 1898 to 1945. Exhibits cover colonial administration, local life, and the city's hybrid architectural traces.

Visitor numbers are limited, so it is worth checking current access before making a special trip. Pair it with Zhanjiang's other French-era buildings if you are interested in this chapter of Guangdong history.
