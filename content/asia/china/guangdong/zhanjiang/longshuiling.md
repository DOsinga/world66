Longshuiling is a low hill on Donghai Island with wide views over fields, coastline, and the approaches to Zhanjiang Port. It is more a local lookout than a major mountain, but the mix of sea views, greenery, and old military tunnels gives it enough interest for travelers already exploring the island.
