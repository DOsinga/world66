---
title: Things to Do
type: section
---

Zhaoqing's main draw is the karst lake scenery of Star Lake (Qixingyan), where seven rocky peaks rise from the water in a pattern that mirrors the Big Dipper. Dinghu Mountain, twenty kilometres northeast, offers genuine hiking in old-growth forest with temple complexes built into the hillside. The city's own ancient walls and the Matteo Ricci historical connection reward a half-day of urban exploration.
