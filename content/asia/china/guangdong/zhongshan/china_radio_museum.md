The China Radio Museum in Zhongshan displays part of a large collection of historic radios, with sets ranging across many periods and styles. Only a fraction of the collection is shown at one time, but the variety is the point.

Captions are mainly in Chinese, though the objects themselves make the museum accessible to visitors interested in design, technology, or broadcasting history.
