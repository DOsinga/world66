title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

The [Sun Yat-sen Former Residence](/asia/china/guangdong/zhongshan/sun_yat_sen_former_residence) at Cuiheng Village is the essential stop: the preserved farmhouse where he was born sits within a larger museum complex that covers his life and the Republican revolution he led. The [Sun Yat-sen Memorial Hall](/asia/china/guangdong/zhongshan/sun_yat_sen_memorial_hall) in the city centre is a large ceremonial building housing historical exhibits. [Wugui Mountain](/asia/china/guangdong/zhongshan/wugui_mountain) on the eastern side of the city offers hiking trails through forested hills and is one of the few green escapes within easy reach. The city's chrysanthemum festival runs annually in mid-autumn, when [Zhongshan Park](/asia/china/guangdong/zhongshan/zhongshan_park) and gardens across the city display hundreds of varieties.
