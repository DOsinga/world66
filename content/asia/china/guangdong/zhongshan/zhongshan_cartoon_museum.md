Zhongshan Cartoon Museum highlights the city's connection with several important Chinese cartoonists. The displays include work by local artists as well as cartoonists from elsewhere in China, making it a focused stop for visitors interested in modern popular art and illustration.

It sits near Yixian Lake Park and can be folded into a short central Zhongshan museum and park walk.
