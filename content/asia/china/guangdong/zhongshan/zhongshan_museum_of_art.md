Zhongshan Museum of Art is the city's main public art museum, with exhibitions that move between
Chinese painting, calligraphy, regional artists, and contemporary work. It adds a cultural stop to a
city better known to outsiders for Sun Yat-sen connections and Pearl River Delta industry.

Check the current exhibition before making a special trip, since the value depends on what is
showing. If you are already in central Zhongshan, it is a calm place to see how the city presents
its own artistic life.
