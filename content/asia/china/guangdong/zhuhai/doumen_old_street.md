Doumen Old Street is a historic commercial precinct west of central Zhuhai, with old shopfronts and trading buildings from the late 19th and early 20th centuries. It recalls the period when Doumen was an important local trading post rather than a quiet district on the edge of a booming city.

Come for a short walk rather than a single monument. The appeal is in the scale of the street, the surviving facades, and the contrast with Zhuhai's newer waterfront development.
