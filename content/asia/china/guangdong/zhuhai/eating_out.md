---
title: Eating Out
type: section
---

Zhuhai's food scene benefits from its proximity to both Hong Kong and Macau and from direct access to the seafood of the South China Sea. The island fishing villages are the best source of genuinely local seafood, cooked simply. The Gongbei district has the widest range of restaurants, covering Cantonese, Macanese, and other Chinese regional cuisines in close proximity.
