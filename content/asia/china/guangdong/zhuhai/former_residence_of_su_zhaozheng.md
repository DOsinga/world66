The former residence of Su Zhaozheng preserves the home of a labour organiser who became an important early figure in Chinese communist politics. The museum is small and the interpretation is mainly in Chinese, but the old village setting and oyster-shell walls make it a worthwhile detour for visitors interested in Zhuhai beyond the waterfront.
