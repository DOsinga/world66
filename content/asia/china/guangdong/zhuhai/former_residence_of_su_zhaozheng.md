The former residence of Su Zhaozheng is a small museum in the home of the early 20th-century communist activist. Exhibits cover local history, Su's life and his role in labour organising around Hong Kong.

English information is limited, but the old house, oyster-shell wall and nearby historic buildings make it a worthwhile stop for visitors interested in Zhuhai's older villages.
