---
title: Getting There
type: section
---

Zhuhai is connected to the national high-speed rail network at Zhuhai North Station, with trains to Guangzhou South taking about 45 minutes. The city also has its own airport, Zhuhai Jinwan Airport, with domestic flights to major Chinese cities. From Hong Kong, the Hong Kong-Zhuhai-Macau Bridge shuttle buses provide a direct connection in about 40 minutes from the Hong Kong International Airport.

The Gongbei border crossing connects Zhuhai directly to Macau by foot — a ten-minute walk. Ferries run between Zhuhai and Hong Kong (about 70 minutes). The ferry terminal near the Gongbei border also operates services to various Pearl River Delta cities. For the outer islands, ferries depart from the Jiuzhou Port and other piers in the city.
