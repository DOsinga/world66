Guyuan Museum of Art is a specialised Zhuhai museum named for Gu Yuan, the influential twentieth-century printmaker who was born in the area. Its focus gives the city a cultural stop beyond seaside promenades, border crossings, and theme-park scale attractions.

The museum is most rewarding if you have an interest in Chinese modern art, woodcuts, or regional artists. Even for casual visitors, it can be a calm indoor break and a useful reminder that Zhuhai is not only a resort-and-Macau gateway.
