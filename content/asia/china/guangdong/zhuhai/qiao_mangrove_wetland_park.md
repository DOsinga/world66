Qi'ao Mangrove Forest Wetland Park protects a coastal wetland on Qi'ao Island, away from Zhuhai's denser border and waterfront districts. Boardwalks and viewing areas make the mangroves accessible without turning the place into a manicured city park.

It is best for travellers who want a nature break and do not mind the distance from central Zhuhai. Birds, tidal water, and the island setting are the draw, so time the visit with weather and transport in mind.
