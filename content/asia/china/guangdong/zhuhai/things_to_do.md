type: section
---

Zhuhai's main character comes from its coastline, its proximity to Macau, and its self-conscious decision to develop differently from Shenzhen. The Lovers' Road promenade, the Chimelong theme park complex, and the Hong Kong-Zhuhai-Macau Bridge offer three very different experiences of the same city. The fishing villages on the outer islands and the Gongbei border district add further layers.
