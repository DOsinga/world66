Zhuhai Museum gives the city a useful interpretive stop, especially for visitors who otherwise encounter Zhuhai mainly as beaches, islands, and the Macau border. Exhibits typically frame the area's fishing villages, coastal geography, and rapid transformation into a Special Economic Zone city.

It is a practical indoor stop rather than a destination museum, but it helps make sense of Zhuhai's unusual identity: part resort city, part border city, part Pearl River Delta growth story.
