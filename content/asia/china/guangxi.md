Guangxi Zhuang Autonomous Region in southern China contains some of the most recognisable landscape in the world. The karst geography here — thousands of near-vertical limestone peaks rising from flat river plains — is the archetype of classical Chinese painting, reproduced on the back of the 20-yuan note and in a thousand ink-wash scrolls. Seeing it in person remains startling, however familiar the image.

[Guilin](/asia/china/guangxi/guilin) is the main city and gateway, but most travellers quickly move on to [Yangshuo](/asia/china/guangxi/yangshuo), the small town downstream where the karst scenery is more accessible and the atmosphere more relaxed. North of Guilin, the [Longji Rice Terraces](/asia/china/guangxi/longji) cascade down steep hillsides in the villages of the Zhuang and Yao minorities — among the most photogenic agricultural landscapes in China. The regional capital [Nanning](/asia/china/guangxi/nanning) is the least scenic but most practical city, an increasingly prosperous place that serves as the main gateway to Vietnam. Far to the southwest near the Vietnamese border, [Detian Waterfall](/asia/china/guangxi/detian) — Asia's largest transboundary waterfall — rewards the long journey to reach it.
