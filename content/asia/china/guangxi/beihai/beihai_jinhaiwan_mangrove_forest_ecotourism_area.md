Beihai Jinhaiwan Mangrove Forest Ecotourism Area protects a large mangrove wetland southeast of the city. Boardwalks, birdlife, tidal scenery, and a museum about the Tanka boat people make it a useful nature break from Beihai's urban waterfront.
