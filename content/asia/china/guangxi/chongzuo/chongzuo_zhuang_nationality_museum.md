Chongzuo Zhuang Nationality Museum is a large local museum with exhibits on Zhuang culture as well as a broader natural history section focused on the landscapes and wildlife of the Chongzuo area. It is one of the more useful indoor stops for understanding the region before visiting the surrounding karst and border country.
