The Eighth Route Army Guilin Office Museum covers the Communist army unit's work in Guilin during the Second World War. Exhibits explain the wartime political and military activity that passed through the city, with photographs, documents, and period rooms.

It is a focused history museum, worth adding if you are already exploring central Guilin's older streets and want more context beyond the city's karst scenery.
