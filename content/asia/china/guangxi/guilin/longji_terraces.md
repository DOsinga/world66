The Longji Rice Terraces (Dragon's Backbone Terraces) lie about 80 km north of Guilin near the town of Longsheng, spread across steep mountainsides at altitudes of up to 1,100 metres. The terraces are worked by Zhuang and Yao minority villages and have been in continuous cultivation since the Yuan dynasty — the water channels that feed each tier from the one above are still functioning after 700 years.

The views from the ridge viewpoints (reached on foot or by a combination of cable car and walking) are extraordinary, especially in the planting season (April to May) when the flooded terraces reflect the sky, and at harvest (September to October) when the hillsides turn gold. Staying overnight in one of the village guesthouses gives you the best light. Allow a full day from Guilin; buses run from Guilin bus station.
