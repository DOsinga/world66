Longhua Temple belongs to the cluster of Buddhist sites on and around West Mountain, the wooded rise that gives Guiping its strongest travel appeal. The temple is not as famous internationally as the mountain itself, but it adds ritual life and architecture to what could otherwise be just a scenic climb.

Visit it as part of a wider West Mountain walk, allowing time for the slopes, pavilions, and views rather than treating the temple as an isolated checklist stop. The combination of greenery and incense is the reason to come.
