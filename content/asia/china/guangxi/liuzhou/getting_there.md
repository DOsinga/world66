title: Getting There
type: section
---

Liuzhou sits on the main rail line between [Guilin](/asia/china/guangxi/guilin) and [Nanning](/asia/china/guangxi/nanning), making it an easy stop on any journey between the two. High-speed G-trains run frequently — the journey from Guilin takes about 45 minutes, from Nanning about 50 minutes. The main railway station, Liuzhou Station, is in the city centre.

The city also has connections to [Guiyang](/asia/china/guizhou/guiyang) and Guangzhou, making it a reasonable hub for moving between Guangxi and the provinces to the north and east. Long-distance buses serve destinations that the high-speed rail doesn't reach.

Within the city, taxis and rideshare apps (DiDi) are the practical transport. The karst peaks and Liu River make cycling an attractive option for getting around — bikes are available through shared bike schemes.
