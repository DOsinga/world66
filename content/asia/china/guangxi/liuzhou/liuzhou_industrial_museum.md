Liuzhou Industrial Museum tells the story of a city better known inside China for manufacturing than for classic sightseeing. Exhibits on machinery, vehicles, and local industry help explain why Liuzhou feels more workaday and modern than the karst postcard towns elsewhere in Guangxi.

It is a good choice if you like cities with an economic backstory, or if rain pushes you indoors. Pair it with the military museum or riverfront walks for a fuller picture of Liuzhou beyond luosifen and parks.
