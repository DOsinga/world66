Liuzhou Museum gives the city's story a useful frame, from prehistoric finds through later urban history and the cultures of Guangxi's ethnic minorities. It is a stronger first museum stop than the more specialised industrial and military museums, especially if you are trying to understand Liuzhou before heading into its parks, riverfront, and food streets.
