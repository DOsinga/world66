Malushan Rare Stones Exposition Garden is a landscaped park built around Liuzhou's local enthusiasm for unusual stones. Paths lead past large rock specimens in the open air, while the small museum section holds the rarer and more delicate pieces.

It is a niche stop, but a worthwhile one if you want something distinctly local beyond Liuzhou's better-known industrial museums, river views, and luosifen food culture.
