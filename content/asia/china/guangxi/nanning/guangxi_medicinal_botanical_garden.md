Guangxi Medicinal Botanical Garden is a distinctive Nanning attraction because it treats plants as medicine, culture, and ecology rather than simply decoration. The garden's collections focus on medicinal species and the plant knowledge of southern China, giving it a clearer identity than a generic city park.

It is best for travellers interested in botany, traditional medicine, or a quieter outdoor break from central Nanning. The grounds are large enough to justify a dedicated visit, especially in seasons when the plantings are at their most active.
