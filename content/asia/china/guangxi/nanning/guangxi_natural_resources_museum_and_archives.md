The Guangxi Natural Resources Museum and Archives covers the region's landscapes, minerals, fossils, and geological history. Its exhibition halls are useful for understanding the karst scenery, mineral resources, and natural setting that shape much of Guangxi.

It is best for visitors with an interest in natural history or geology, especially before heading farther into the province.
