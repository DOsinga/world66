Kunlun Pass is a historic mountain pass northeast of Nanning, best known today for its memorial landscape tied to wartime fighting in Guangxi. The site combines road history, battlefield memory, and hill-country scenery in a way that differs sharply from Nanning's urban parks.

It is a specialist day trip rather than a casual city sight, but worthwhile for travelers interested in modern Chinese history or regional landscapes. Because it is well outside the center, check transport and opening details before committing the time.
