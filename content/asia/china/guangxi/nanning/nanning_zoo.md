Nanning Zoo is the city's main family wildlife attraction, with African and Asian animals, reptiles, birds, pandas, and aquarium exhibits spread through a large park-like site. It is a practical half-day option with children, especially if you want something less museum-focused than central Nanning's cultural stops.
