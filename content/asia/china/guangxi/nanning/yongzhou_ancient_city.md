Yongzhou Ancient City is a central Nanning heritage area around Xingning Road, Minsheng Road, Jiefang Road, Golden Lion Alley, and Silver Lion Alley. It is best treated as a short walk through old lanes, shops, and surviving fragments of the historic city.
