Yongzhou Ancient City is the old commercial heart of Nanning, centred on pedestrian streets and narrow lanes with blue-brick walls and black-tiled roofs. The area mixes restored streetscapes with historic sites such as the City God Temple and Deng Yingchao Memorial Hall.
