Drippy Rock is a coastal spot on Weizhou Island named for cliffs that continue to seep water after rain. The draw is the mix of rock formations, beach scenery, and sea views rather than a built attraction.

It is best folded into a wider loop around Weizhou's volcanic coast, with time for the nearby beaches and simple on-beach food stalls.
