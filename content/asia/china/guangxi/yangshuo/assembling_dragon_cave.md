Assembling Dragon Cave is a small show cave in the Yangshuo karst area, with illuminated limestone formations and an easy visitor route. It is not the region's most essential landscape, but it gives a quick underground variation on the peaks and rivers outside town.

The cave is best treated as a short add-on if you are already exploring the Yulong River or nearby countryside. Travelers with limited time should still put outdoor karst scenery first.
