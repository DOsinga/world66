Guiyang Happy World is a large amusement park in the Baiyun district, with roller coasters, family rides, and the bright, high-volume atmosphere typical of Chinese urban theme parks. It is not the reason most travellers come to Guizhou, but it is a real local leisure destination.

Consider it if you are travelling with children, living in the city for a while, or want a day that has nothing to do with caves, karst, or ethnic-village tourism. Check current opening hours and ticketing before going, since operations can shift by season and school holidays.
