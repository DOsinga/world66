Huaxi Park sits south of central Guiyang, where streams, low karst hills, bridges, and planted gardens give the city a softer edge. It has long been a favourite local escape, and it makes sense if you want scenery without committing to a full day in the countryside.

The appeal is gentle rather than dramatic. Walk the paths along the water, cross the small bridges, and use the park as a pause between Guiyang's denser urban sights and bigger Guizhou landscapes farther out.
