Zhenshan Village is a Bouyei minority village near Guiyang, known for stone houses, narrow lanes, and a lakeside-hill setting that feels far older than the provincial capital. It is one of the more approachable cultural day trips from the city.

Tourism has reached the village, but the stone architecture and landscape still make the visit worthwhile. Go with time to walk beyond the entrance area and connect it with nearby Tianhe Lake or Huaxi sights.
