This waterfall cluster includes Broken Bridge Falls, Raya Falls, and the 68-step plunge waterfall. The area is known for varied cascades, from broad curtains of water to stepped drops that break the flow into many smaller falls.

It is a worthwhile nature stop in Libo County for visitors already exploring the county's karst scenery, bridges, and forested water routes.
