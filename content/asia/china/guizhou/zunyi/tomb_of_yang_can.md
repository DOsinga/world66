The Tomb of Yang Can is a specialized but worthwhile historical site for travelers with time in Zunyi. Its interest lies in the surviving stonework and funerary setting, which point to the region's older elite history before Zunyi became known primarily for twentieth-century revolutionary narratives.

This is not a polished headline museum, so expectations matter. It rewards visitors who enjoy archaeological sites, carved details, and quieter local history, and it pairs best with other Zunyi stops rather than as a long single-purpose outing.
