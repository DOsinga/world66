Zunyi Museum is a useful first stop for understanding why this Guizhou city matters beyond its modern streets. Its displays cover local history, regional culture, and the revolutionary period that made Zunyi a major name in Chinese political memory.

The museum pairs naturally with the Zunyi Conference Site. Visit it when you want background before walking the older streets, or when weather makes an indoor sight more appealing.
