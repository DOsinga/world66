Baisha Meteor Crater is regarded as the first meteor crater identified in China. The site is heavily covered in vegetation, so the shape is not as obvious as at desert craters, but that is part of its odd appeal: you are looking for geological history hidden under tropical growth.

It is a niche stop and best suited to travellers already exploring inland Hainan. Do not expect a polished visitor centre or dramatic bare-rock crater.
