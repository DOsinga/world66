---
title: "Getting There"
type: section
---

Dadonghai sits about 3 km south-east of central [Sanya](/asia/china/hainanisland/sanya), so reaching it means first getting to Sanya. Sanya Phoenix International Airport (SYX), 15 km west of the city, has direct flights from most major Chinese cities and a growing list of international ones — Hong Kong, Seoul, Moscow, Singapore. From the airport, the metered taxi to Dadonghai runs around 80-100 yuan, or take Bus 8 to the centre and change.

The Hainan High Speed Rail loops the island and connects Sanya to Haikou in about 1.5 hours — a scenic ride along the coast. From Sanya railway station, take a local taxi (about 30 yuan) to Dadonghai. From Sanya city centre, public Bus 2 and Bus 8 run to Dadonghai every few minutes for 2 yuan, or take a taxi for about 20 yuan.
