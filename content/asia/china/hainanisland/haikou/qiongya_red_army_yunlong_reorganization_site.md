The Qiongya Red Army Yunlong Reorganization Site is a revolutionary-history stop outside central Haikou, tied to the reorganization of Communist forces on Hainan. It is a niche attraction, but it adds historical depth beyond the city's arcade streets and volcanic landscapes.

The site is best for travelers with an interest in modern Chinese history or those already exploring Haikou's outlying districts. English interpretation may be limited, so some background reading helps.
