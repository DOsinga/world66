Teddy Bear Museum Haikou is a family-oriented branch of the Jeju-based teddy bear museum chain. Displays include teddy-bear scenes based on Silk Road cities, antique bears, and bear-themed artwork, with the expected shop at the end. It is light rather than essential, but useful for families or anyone looking for an indoor stop near Haikou's outlying attractions.
