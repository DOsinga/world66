Tianhou Temple is one of Haikou's oldest temples, dedicated to Mazu, the sea goddess also known as Tianhou. Its history reaches back more than 700 years, linking it to the city's old maritime life.

The temple is a natural stop while walking around the Bo'ai Road and old street area, especially if you are interested in Hainan's coastal trading culture.
