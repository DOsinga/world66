Wormhole Library is a sculptural seaside library and public reading space on Haikou Bay, designed by MAD Architects. Its white concrete curves and openings make it as much an architectural stop as a place to browse books, with views across the water and a calm setting away from the city centre.

Visits may require advance booking through the official Chinese-language site or WeChat account, so check before heading out.
