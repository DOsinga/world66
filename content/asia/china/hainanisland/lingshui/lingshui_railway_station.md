Lingshui Railway Station places the county within easy reach of both Haikou (about an hour by high-speed rail) and Sanya (about 20 minutes). The station is on the east-coast branch of the Hainan Ring Railway, which circles the entire island at high speed. For travellers exploring the Hainan coast without a car, the station makes Lingshui a natural base: the beach at Perfume Bay is 20 minutes by taxi from the station, and Nanwan Monkey Island is about 40 minutes.

The station building itself is modest but well-maintained, with taxis and private car services outside. The ring railway has transformed travel on Hainan since its completion in 2015 — what once required a car or multi-hour bus journey now takes minutes by train.
