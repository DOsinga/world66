Mytikas Water Park is Lingshui's main water park, opened in 2018 near the coast. It is a family-focused stop with pools and slides, though parts of the site have continued to develop since opening.

Check current hours before going, especially outside holiday periods, and treat it as a half-day break from beaches and sightseeing.
