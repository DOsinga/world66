---
title: "Getting There"
type: section
---

Luhuitou is just south of central Sanya across the small inlet. A taxi from Sanya Bay or Dadonghai takes about ten minutes; city buses run regularly to the foot of the peninsula. The chairlift up to the park starts from the harbour-side car park.

Sanya is reached by frequent flights to Sanya Phoenix International Airport, by the high-speed train that runs around the perimeter of Hainan, and by long-distance buses from Haikou.
