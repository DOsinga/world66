---

Sanya's beaches each have a different character, and choosing where to stay matters as much as choosing when to go.

**[Yalong Bay](/asia/china/hainanisland/sanya/yalong_bay)** is the most upmarket — a 7.5-kilometre crescent of fine white sand 25 kilometres east of the city, ringed by international resort hotels. The water is the clearest of any beach in the area.

**[Dadonghai](/asia/china/hainanisland/sanya/dadonghai_beach)**, 2 km from downtown, is the most central beach. It is busy, bordered by a tight strip of hotels and restaurants, and the easiest beach to reach if you are basing yourself in town.

**[Sanya Bay](/asia/china/hainanisland/sanya/sanya_bay)** is the longest beach, stretching 22 km along the city's western edge. Less developed than Yalong, with a coconut-palm promenade that is the city's main evening walking route.

**[Haitang Bay](/asia/china/hainanisland/sanya/haitang_bay)**, 35 km east, is the newest resort strip — sprawling, dominated by very large international hotels, and home to Atlantis Sanya and the Sanya International Duty Free Mall.

For diving and snorkelling, take the ferry from Haitang Bay out to **[Wuzhizhou Island](/asia/china/hainanisland/sanya/wuzhizhou_island)**, where the reefs are in better shape than on the developed mainland coast.
