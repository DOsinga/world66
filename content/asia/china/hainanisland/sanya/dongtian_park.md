Dongtian Park, also known as Daxiao Dongtian, is a coastal scenic area west of Sanya
where Taoist associations meet sea cliffs, caves, palms, and old stone inscriptions. It
offers a more historic and ceremonial coastline than the city's resort beaches.

The park is easy to fold into a western Sanya day with Nanshan or Tianya Haijiao. Go for
a walkable mix of ocean views, small shrines, and weathered rocks rather than untouched
nature. It is a managed scenic area, but the setting is strong enough to carry the
visit.
