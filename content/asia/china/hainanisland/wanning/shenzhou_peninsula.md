Shenzhou Peninsula is one of Wanning's main coastal resort zones, a broad finger of land with beaches, palms, golf development, and sea views on several sides. It is not untouched Hainan, but it is a useful base if you want beach time without staying in busier Sanya.

The appeal is the setting: wide skies, soft sand, and enough space to walk or cycle between resort pockets. Come for a relaxed half-day by the water, then balance it with Wanning's surf beaches and inland tropical gardens.
