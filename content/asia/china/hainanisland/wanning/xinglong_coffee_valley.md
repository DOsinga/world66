Xinglong Coffee Valley turns one of Wanning's best-known crops into an easy visitor
stop. The area around Xinglong has long been associated with overseas Chinese farms and
tropical agriculture, and coffee is one of the more enjoyable ways to taste that
history.

Come for a relaxed change from beaches: plantations, tasting rooms, and a look at how
coffee fits into Hainan's humid lowland landscape. It pairs naturally with Xinglong's
botanical garden or overseas Chinese museum if you are spending a day inland.
