Gaolong Bay is one of Wenchangs accessible coastal stretches, a long curve of sand and water south of the city. It is less distinctive than the coconut groves and old streets, but it gives the Wenchang page a proper beach stop for travellers staying in the area.

Expect a developed, easygoing beach rather than a remote tropical escape. It is best for a walk, a sea breeze, and a break from temple and town visits, with swimming conditions depending on weather and local warnings.
