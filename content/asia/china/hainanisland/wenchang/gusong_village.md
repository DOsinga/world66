Gusong Village is a traditional Hainanese village near the coast east of Wenchang. Its appeal is low-key: old village houses, a quiet rural setting, and access to nearby undeveloped beaches.

It is not a polished heritage attraction, but it can be worthwhile for travellers exploring the Tonggu Ridge and eastern Wenchang coast who want a less commercial village stop.
