Tonggu Ridge is one of Wenchang's stronger natural viewpoints, rising above the Hainan east coast with sea, bay, and headland views. The combination of tropical vegetation and open coastal scenery makes it a more varied outing than a simple beach stop.

It is best visited in clear weather, when the viewpoint earns the climb or drive. Pair it with nearby coast or space-launch-related sights around Wenchang, and bring sun protection because exposed sections can be hot and bright.
