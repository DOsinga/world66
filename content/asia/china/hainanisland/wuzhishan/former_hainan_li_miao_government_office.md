This former government office is a small but unusual Wuzhishan landmark, built in 1954 for the Hainan Li Miao Autonomous Prefecture. The building is not normally open to visitors, but its exterior mixes traditional Chinese forms with Western neoclassical details in a way that stands out in the city centre.

Treat it as a quick architecture stop while walking between the market, the bus station, and the Hainan Nationalities Museum.
