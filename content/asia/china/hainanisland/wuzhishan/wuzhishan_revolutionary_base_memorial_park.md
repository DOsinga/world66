Wuzhishan Revolutionary Base Memorial Park marks a former People's Liberation Army base used from 1948 to 1950 during the final phase of the Chinese Civil War. The site includes a tall monument, graves of Communist martyrs, and a museum about the base.

Information is mainly in Chinese, so the visit is most rewarding if you have some background in modern Chinese history or a translation app handy.
