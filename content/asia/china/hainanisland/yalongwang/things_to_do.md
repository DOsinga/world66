Yalong Bay is a resort strip, not a town, and most of what you do here is swim, walk on the beach, or take a half-day excursion into the protected forest park behind. The handful of attractions clusters at the eastern end of the bay around the Yalong Bay Plaza and the entrance to the Tropical Paradise Forest Park.
