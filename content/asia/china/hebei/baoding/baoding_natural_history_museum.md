Baoding Natural History Museum gives the city a science stop beyond its better-known imperial offices and historic sites. Exhibits focus on natural history themes such as fossils, geology, animals, and environmental displays, with a layout that works especially well for families.

It is not the main reason to come to Baoding, but it adds variety if you are spending more than a quick transit day here. Check opening hours before crossing town, as local museum schedules in China can be less forgiving than their official-looking buildings suggest.
