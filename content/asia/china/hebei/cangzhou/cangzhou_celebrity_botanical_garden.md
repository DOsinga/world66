Cangzhou Celebrity Botanical Garden mixes a city park, plant collections, and local history displays. Its Celebrity Avenue is lined with bronze sculptures of notable figures from Cangzhou, while the Hall of Fame presents portraits, objects, books, images, and video material connected to the region.

The garden is not just a green space. It is useful if you want an easy outdoor stop that also explains how Cangzhou tells its own story through writers, officials, martial artists, and other local figures.
