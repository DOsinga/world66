Cangzhou City Mosque, also known as Beida Temple, is one of Hebei's protected historic religious sites and is often listed among China's notable old mosques. Its gate and traditional Chinese-Islamic architecture make it a worthwhile stop when exploring Cangzhou's older urban core.
