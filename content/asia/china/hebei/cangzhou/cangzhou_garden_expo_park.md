Cangzhou Garden Expo Park was built for the Hebei Garden Expo beside the Beijing-Hangzhou Grand Canal. The grounds bring together themed garden areas, water features, and displays drawing on Cangzhou and surrounding regional culture.

It works best as an easy outdoor stop if you want a landscaped break from the city's museums and historic sites.
