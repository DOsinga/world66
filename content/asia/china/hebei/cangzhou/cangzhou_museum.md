Cangzhou Museum is the city's main historical museum, a useful place to make sense of a destination better known for martial arts traditions and the Iron Lion. Exhibits cover local archaeology, canal history, folk culture, and the region's place in Hebei.

It is the right first stop if you want context before visiting scattered sites around the city. The museum also helps show that Cangzhou's identity is broader than a single monument or kung fu reputation.
