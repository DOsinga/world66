Ji Xiaolan Tomb marks the burial place of the Qing-dynasty scholar, writer, and imperial official Ji Yun, better known by his courtesy name Ji Xiaolan. The low brick tomb and surrounding cemetery are modest, but they give Cangzhou a direct link to one of late imperial China's best-known literary figures.

It is a specialist stop rather than a headline sight. Go if you are interested in Qing history or want to round out Cangzhou beyond the Iron Lion and canal heritage.
