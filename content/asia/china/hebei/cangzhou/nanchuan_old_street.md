Nanchuan Old Street is a restored heritage area in Cangzhou's Yunhe District, between Nanhu Lake and the Grand Canal. Its reconstructed Ming- and Qing-style courtyards, landmark towers, food stalls, and craft shops make it a useful place to get a quick feel for the city's canal culture.
