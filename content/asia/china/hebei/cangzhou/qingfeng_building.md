Qingfeng Building is a modern reconstruction of a much older tower on the bank of the Grand Canal. The present five-storey building dates from 1992, but its Jin-dynasty reference and canal-side position make it a useful stop when walking Cangzhou's heritage corridor.

Come for the setting rather than great age. It works best paired with the Confucian Temple, Nanchuan Old Street, and other central Cangzhou sights.
