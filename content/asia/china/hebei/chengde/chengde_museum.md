Chengde Museum helps frame the city beyond the Mountain Resort and outlying temples. Its
collections introduce imperial, regional, and ethnic histories tied to the Qing court's
summer capital and the surrounding Hebei landscape.

Visit early in a Chengde stay if you want context before tackling the larger heritage
sites. It is a conventional museum stop, but in a city with such layered imperial
geography, that context is genuinely useful.
