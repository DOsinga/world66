When the Kangxi Emperor first laid out the Mountain Resort he selected and named thirty-six "scenic spots" — particular views, pavilions, and pieces of garden that he wanted to be experienced in a particular order. His grandson Qianlong added another thirty-six. Together they form a kind of imperial walking tour of the park.

A handful of the spots have been carefully restored and are signed in English: the **Misty Rain Pavilion** on its lake island, the **Wenjin Pavilion** that once held one of the four copies of the *Siku Quanshu* imperial library, the **Yongyou Pagoda** rising over the northern grasslands. Picking up a map at the gate and finding even five or six of them is a satisfying way to organise a day inside the resort — better than just walking until your feet hurt.
