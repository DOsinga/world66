---
title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Chengde's main draw is imperial-era architecture on a grand scale. The [Mountain Resort](/asia/china/hebei/chengde/mountain_resort) alone could fill a full day; add the [Putuo Zongcheng](/asia/china/hebei/chengde/putuo_zongcheng_temple), [Puning](/asia/china/hebei/chengde/puning_temple), [Xumi Fushou](/asia/china/hebei/chengde/xumi_fushou_temple), and [Pule](/asia/china/hebei/chengde/pule_temple) temples and you have two days of serious sightseeing. The smaller outer temples — [Anyuan](/asia/china/hebei/chengde/anyuan_temple) and [Shuxiang](/asia/china/hebei/chengde/shuxiang_temple) — are worth seeking out for their quieter atmosphere. The rock formation at [Sledgehammer Peak](/asia/china/hebei/chengde/sledgehammer_peak) makes a satisfying half-day trip out of the city centre for the views back over the valley.
