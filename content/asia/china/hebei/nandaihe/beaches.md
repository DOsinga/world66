---
title: Beaches
type: section
---

The Nandaihe coastline extends for roughly 17 kilometres, and the beaches are broad and sandy with a gentle gradient into the water. The main resort area is backed by hotels and restaurants and is oriented toward families: the water is shallow and calm enough for children, and the beach is wide enough that it rarely feels crowded even in peak season.

The sand takes on a faint golden colour in direct sun. It is nothing exotic, but it is clean and comfortable, and the Bohai Sea here is warmer than its northern exposure might suggest. Jellyfish can be a seasonal nuisance in mid-summer.
