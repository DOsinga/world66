Nandaihe is at the western end of a coastal strip that includes two historically significant sites. Beidaihe, immediately to the north, has been a resort since the 1890s and carries its own political backstory. Shanhaiguan, about 25 kilometres further northeast, is where the Great Wall meets the Bohai Sea at the Dragon Head Rock — one of the defining images in Chinese landscape.

The combination of wall, beach, and the quieter resort at Nandaihe makes the Qinhuangdao coast one of the more varied short stretches of Chinese coastline accessible from Beijing in under three hours by high-speed train.
