---
title: Eating Out
type: section
---

Seafood is the dominant cuisine along the Nandaihe waterfront. Stalls and restaurants along the beach road offer crab, clams, shrimp, and sea cucumber, typically priced by weight and displayed live in tanks outside. The seasonal nature of the resort means prices are slightly inflated compared to everyday Hebei cooking, but the freshness is genuine.

Look for places that price visibly and have good turnover rather than approaching anyone who flags you down aggressively. The best seafood is found in the side streets just off the main beach road rather than directly on the waterfront where tourist surcharges are steepest.
