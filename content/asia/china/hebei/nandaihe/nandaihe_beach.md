The main beach at Nandaihe, sometimes called the Golden Beach for the colour of its sand at certain hours, runs for several kilometres along the developed resort front. It is the quieter alternative to Beidaihe just across the Daihe River to the north: the same sandy Bohai coast, the same calm shallow water, but without the layered political mythology that makes Beidaihe a destination in its own right.

The beach is equipped with the standard Chinese summer resort infrastructure — sun umbrellas, snack stands, changing facilities, and the occasional water sports operator offering banana boats and jet ski rides. The pace is genuinely relaxed. Families from Beijing and Tianjin make up the majority of visitors, and the whole atmosphere is oriented toward children in the water and adults eating seafood.

The season runs from June through September, with July and August being the busiest months. Outside those months the facilities close and the coast has a deserted, wind-scoured quality that some visitors find more interesting than the summer crowds.
