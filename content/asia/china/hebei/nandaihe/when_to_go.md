---
title: When to Go
type: section
---

Nandaihe is a seasonal resort. The beach season runs from late June through early September, with July and August the peak months when hotels fill and seafood restaurants are at their busiest. Water temperatures are comfortable for swimming through most of August.

Outside the beach season, most facilities close entirely and the town has a deserted, off-season character. Spring and autumn visits are possible if you have independent transport and do not mind that most restaurants and hotels will be shuttered. Winter is cold and bleak in the way that all northern Chinese coastal towns become in January, but there is a particular bleakness to a closed resort that some visitors find worth experiencing.
