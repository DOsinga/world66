Shanhaiguan is the eastern terminus of the Great Wall, where the fortifications march down from the mountains and plunge straight into the Bohai Sea at a spot called [Old Dragon's Head](/asia/china/hebei/shanhaiguan/old_dragons_head). The walled town itself, with its massive east gate inscribed [First Pass Under Heaven](/asia/china/hebei/shanhaiguan/first_pass), was one of the most strategically important fortresses in Chinese history — the pass between the mountains and the sea that controlled access to the northeastern plains.

The town is compact and can be walked in an afternoon. The restored [city walls](/asia/china/hebei/shanhaiguan/city_walls) are impressive, and the stretch of Great Wall climbing into the Yanshan Mountains behind the town at [Jiao Mountain](/asia/china/hebei/shanhaiguan/jiao_mountain) offers good hiking with far fewer crowds than the sections near Beijing. The [Great Wall Museum](/asia/china/hebei/shanhaiguan/great_wall_museum) gives essential context, and the legendary [Mengjiangnü Temple](/asia/china/hebei/shanhaiguan/mengjiangnu_temple) — a folk shrine to the woman whose tears, in the old story, brought down a section of the wall — sits east of town. Shanhaiguan is easily reached by train from Beijing (about two hours by high-speed rail) or as a side trip from the nearby beach resort of Beidaihe.
