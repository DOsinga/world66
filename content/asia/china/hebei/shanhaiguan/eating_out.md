---
title: "Eating Out"
type: section
---

Shanhaiguan's food is northern Chinese with strong seaside notes. The town is famous for **da gao** — a sweet brown-sugar steamed cake sold from street stalls — and for **biscuit cakes** (*shaobing*) and braised donkey meat sandwiches in the local Hebei style. Down on the coast at Old Dragon's Head and the Bohai Sea villages, the focus shifts to **seafood**: grilled flatfish, salt-and-pepper crab, scallops, and the famous **Qinhuangdao prawns**. Cold Yanjing or Snow beer is the usual accompaniment.
