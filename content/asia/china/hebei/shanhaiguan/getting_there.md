---
title: "Getting There"
type: section
---

**By train.** Shanhaiguan railway station, on the original Beijing–Shenyang line, is a five-minute walk from the south gate of the old town. High-speed CRH trains from **Beijing South** take about two hours; slower D-class and K-trains take three to four. Trains continue north-east into Liaoning and toward the North Korean border.

**From Beidaihe and Qinhuangdao.** Shanhaiguan is now a district of the larger **Qinhuangdao** prefecture-level city. From the main Qinhuangdao high-speed station, a local train or taxi takes about 30 minutes. The popular beach resort of **Beidaihe**, 25 km south, is connected by frequent local trains and a constant flow of taxis.

**By air.** Qinhuangdao Shanhaiguan Airport (BPE) is just outside town with limited domestic flights; most visitors arrive via the train or fly into Beijing and take the train onward.

**By road.** The G1 Beijing–Harbin expressway passes Shanhaiguan; private hire from Beijing takes about three hours. Long-distance buses from Beijing run several times a day.
