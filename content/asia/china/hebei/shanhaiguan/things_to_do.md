---
title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Shanhaiguan is a compact walled town that can be covered on foot in a long afternoon. The east gate (**First Pass Under Heaven**), the south and north city walls, the small old streets, and the Great Wall Museum are within ten minutes' walk of one another. The two most impressive sights — **Old Dragon's Head**, where the Great Wall enters the sea, and **Jiao Mountain**, the first peak of the mountain Great Wall — are 5–7 km out of town and need a taxi or bus.
