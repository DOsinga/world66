---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

Shanhaiguan has a sharp continental climate moderated by the sea. **Summer (June–August)** is warm (25–30°C) and the only time when the beaches at Old Dragon's Head are usable; it is also peak Chinese domestic-tourism season, with crowds. **Autumn (September–October)** is the best time to visit — warm dry days, cool nights, low humidity, the mountains turning yellow and red, and the early-October national holiday week aside, manageable crowds.

**Winter (November–March)** is cold (-5 to 5°C) but very clear, with frequent dustings of snow on the wall — the most evocative season for photographs. **Spring (April–May)** is short and prone to spring dust storms blowing in from Mongolia; check the air-quality forecast before planning a Jiao Mountain hike.

The pass is busiest during **Chinese national holidays** (Spring Festival in January/February, Labour Day in early May, National Day from October 1–7). Avoid those weeks if you can.
