North China Military Martyrs Cemetery is a large memorial park dedicated to people who died during the war against Japan. Its most unusual draw for foreign visitors is the set of memorials connected to Norman Bethune of Canada and Dwarkanath Kotnis of India, both doctors remembered in China for their wartime service.

The grounds are solemn rather than showy, with monuments, trees, and broad paths. It is a worthwhile stop if you are interested in modern Chinese history or in the international stories tied to Shijiazhuang.
