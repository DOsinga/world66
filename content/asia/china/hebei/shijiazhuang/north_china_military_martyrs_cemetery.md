North China Military Martyrs Cemetery is a large memorial park in Shijiazhuang dedicated to people who died in the anti-Japanese war. Notable sections include memorials connected with Norman Bethune and Dwarkanath Kotnis, giving the site international as well as Chinese historical interest.
