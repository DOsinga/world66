Anti-seismic Monument Square is one of Tangshan's central memory sites, tied to the catastrophic 1976 earthquake and the city's reconstruction afterward. It is not just a traffic-facing monument; it is part of how Tangshan presents its modern identity.

Pair it with the earthquake memorial museum for fuller context. The square itself is a short stop, but it helps explain why disaster, resilience, and rebuilding are so prominent in Tangshan's civic landscape.
