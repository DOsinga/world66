Datang Xingguo Temple is a Tang Dynasty-inspired Buddhist complex in Tangshan. Its main axis links the Mountain Gate, Hall of the Heavenly Kings, and Great Hero Hall, giving visitors a clear sense of the temple's formal layout.

It is a quieter architectural and religious stop, best paired with Tangshan Museum or Kailuan National Mine Park.
