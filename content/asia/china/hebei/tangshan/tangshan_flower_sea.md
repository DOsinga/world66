Tangshan Flower Sea is a seasonal flower-viewing area where large plantings turn into broad fields of colour in spring and summer. It is built for strolling, photography, and a slower outdoor break from the city's museums and memorials.

Come for the flowers rather than a formal botanical garden. The visit works best in bloom season, when the scale of the planting is the main attraction.
