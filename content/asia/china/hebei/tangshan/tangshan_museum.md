Tangshan Museum broadens the citys story beyond the famous 1976 earthquake. Its exhibits cover local history, ceramics, industry, folk culture, and the urban rebuilding that made Tangshan a symbol of recovery in modern China.

Visit it with the earthquake memorial museum if you can. One explains the disaster; the other gives the city enough before-and-after context to feel like a real place rather than a single tragic event.
