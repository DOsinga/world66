Tangshan Shadow Puppet Theme Park is dedicated to the city's traditional shadow puppet art, one of eastern Hebei's best-known folk traditions. Exhibits explain the craft and its local history, while performances show how the carved figures, music, and narration work together.

It is a family-friendly cultural stop rather than a major museum. It makes most sense for visitors who want to see a living performance tradition alongside Tangshan's earthquake and industrial history sites.
