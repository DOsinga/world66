Tangshan Zoo is a large family attraction with pandas, big cats, birds, and other animal exhibits in landscaped grounds. It is best suited to travellers with children or anyone looking for an easy half-day stop away from the city's industrial history sights.

The zoo's scale and range of animals make it one of Tangshan's more straightforward visitor attractions.
