Genting Resort Secret Garden is one of Chongli's major ski areas and became
internationally visible as a Beijing 2022 Winter Olympics venue. For Zhangjiakou
travelers, it represents the region's newer identity as China's most developed winter-
sports hub.

In winter the appeal is obvious: lifts, snow, and mountain resort infrastructure.
Outside ski season, check what is actually open before going. It is best treated as a
specific stop within the broader Chongli ski district rather than a general sightseeing
detour.
