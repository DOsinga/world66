The Flood Control Monument stands by the Songhua River at the end of Harbin's Central Street, commemorating the city's struggle against major floods. Its position makes it one of the natural finishing points for a walk through the old commercial district.

The monument is less moving as sculpture than as urban geography. It marks the meeting of Harbin's riverfront, winter promenades, and civic memory, and it helps explain why the Songhua is more than a backdrop here.
