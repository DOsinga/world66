The Northeast China Revolutionary Martyrs' Memorial Hall is a serious, state-style history museum rather than a light sightseeing stop. Its focus is the revolutionary and anti-Japanese history of the region, with displays that place Harbin inside the wider story of conflict and resistance in Manchuria.

For most visitors it works best as a specialist addition after the better-known Harbin sights. If you are interested in twentieth-century Chinese history, it adds context that the ice festival, Russian architecture, and riverfront cannot provide on their own.
