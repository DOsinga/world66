The Temple of Heavenly Bliss, better known locally as Jile Temple, is Harbin's major Buddhist temple complex. Its yellow walls, tiered roofs, incense smoke, and orderly courtyards feel quite different from the Russian-influenced architecture that dominates the city's tourist image.

The temple is active rather than merely decorative, so visit with the same courtesy you would bring to any place of worship. The main halls contain large Buddha images and painted details, while the courtyards offer a calm pause from Harbin's traffic. It pairs well with the nearby Confucian temple area for a broader view of the city's religious architecture.
