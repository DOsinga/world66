Beihong Village is one of China's northernmost settlements, known for traditional wooden houses, forest scenery, and the slow rural feel of the far northeast. It is often visited as part of a Mohe-area trip focused on border landscapes and Arctic Village sights.

Winter brings the strongest atmosphere, with snow, dark skies, and a chance of aurora viewing, though the village is remote enough that visitors should plan transport carefully.
