---
title: "Getting There"
type: section
---

Mohe is genuinely remote. By the standards of Chinese domestic travel, reaching it requires serious planning.

**By air:** Mohe Gulian Airport has flights to and from [Harbin](/asia/china/heilongjiang/harbin) and a handful of other Chinese cities. Flights are infrequent and should be booked well in advance, particularly during the winter aurora season and the summer solstice period when demand spikes.

**By train:** The train from Harbin takes approximately 12 hours. This is the classic approach and gives a real sense of the journey through the boreal forest. Sleeper berths are available and recommended — the train is the most atmospheric way to arrive, especially if you can time the last hours to arrive in daylight and watch the forest thicken as you move north.

**By road:** Long-distance buses connect Mohe to other Heilongjiang cities, but journey times are long and road conditions in winter can be difficult. Self-driving is possible in summer but winter driving on provincial roads here requires experience and preparation.
