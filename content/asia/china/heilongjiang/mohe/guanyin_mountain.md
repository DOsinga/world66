Guanyin Mountain is a forested pilgrimage and viewpoint area marked by a tall statue of Guanyin. The setting is quiet and expansive, with the statue looking out over the surrounding northern landscape.

Come for the combination of religious atmosphere, easy walking, and views. It fits best as part of a wider Mohe-area trip rather than a quick stop in town.
