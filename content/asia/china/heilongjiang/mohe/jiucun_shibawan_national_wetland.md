Jiucun Shibawan National Wetland protects a far-northern river-meander landscape near Mohe, where wetlands, forest, and open skies give travellers a strong sense of China's Arctic-edge geography. It fits naturally with Mohe's appeal as a place of cold extremes, northern lights lore, and borderland scenery.

The wetland is best visited as part of a wider Mohe itinerary rather than as a quick urban sight. Weather, season, and transport matter greatly here, so plan with current local information.
