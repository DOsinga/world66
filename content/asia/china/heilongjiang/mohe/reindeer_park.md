Reindeer Park near Mohe gives visitors a chance to see domesticated reindeer in China's far north. The park is tied to Ewenki herding culture, so it is most interesting when treated as a cultural stop rather than just a photo stop.

Visits usually include feeding the reindeer and learning a little about how the animals are kept in the Arctic environment. It pairs naturally with the nearby Santa Claus Village scenic area.
