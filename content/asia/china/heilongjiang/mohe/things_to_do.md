title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Mohe's attractions are inseparable from its extreme geography. The appeal is twofold: the aurora borealis in winter and the near-midnight sun in summer. Everything else — the river views, the village at the northern tip — amplifies these two phenomena. This is not a city of museums and monuments; it is a place where the sky and the latitude do all the work.
