Mudanjiang is the gateway to eastern Heilongjiang, a mid-sized city in the Mudan River valley that most visitors treat as a base for the surrounding landscapes rather than as a destination itself. That is fair: the city is pleasant but not remarkable. What lies around it, however, is excellent.

**[Jingpo Lake](/asia/china/heilongjiang/mudanjiang/jingpo_lake)** (Jingpohu), 90 kilometres south in the county of Ning'an, is the main reason to make the trip. China's largest mountain barrier lake — formed when lava flows dammed a river valley tens of thousands of years ago — stretches 45 kilometres through forested hills. The name means "Mirror Lake," which is accurate on calm days when the pine-clad shore reflects perfectly in the dark water. Boat trips, hiking trails, and a famous waterfall (Diaoshuilou, one of the largest volcanic waterfalls in Asia) fill a full day easily. In summer the lake is busy with Chinese tourists; in autumn the larch and birch forest around it turn vivid yellow.

**[Snow Town](/asia/china/heilongjiang/mudanjiang/snow_town)** (Xuexiang), in the mountains about 200 kilometres southwest of Mudanjiang, is one of the most-photographed winter landscapes in China: a small mountain village where the snow falls so heavily from late October that the rooftops are buried under enormous mushroom-shaped drifts, and the pine trees are bowed double. It looks like a children's illustration of winter and is genuinely beautiful. Getting there in winter involves some planning, but tour operators in Mudanjiang and [Harbin](/asia/china/heilongjiang/harbin) run regular trips.

The **[Bohai State site](/asia/china/heilongjiang/mudanjiang/bohai_state_site)** near Ning'an preserves the ruins of the capital of the Bohai Kingdom, which controlled much of northeast Asia from the 7th to 10th centuries before its destruction by the Khitan. The site museum has good exhibits on a kingdom that is largely forgotten outside the region.

Mudanjiang connects to [Harbin](/asia/china/heilongjiang/harbin) by high-speed rail in under two hours, and has direct train connections to Beijing.
