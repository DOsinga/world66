---
title: "Getting There"
type: section
---

Suifenhe is most commonly reached from Mudanjiang, the nearest major city.

**By bus:** Buses between Suifenhe and [Mudanjiang](/asia/china/heilongjiang/mudanjiang) run regularly throughout the day. The journey takes two to three hours on provincial roads through hilly forested terrain. This is the standard approach for most visitors.

**By train:** Local train services connect Suifenhe to Mudanjiang and onward to the main Heilongjiang rail network. Journey times are similar to the bus, sometimes longer. For travellers crossing into Russia, the twice-weekly international train service connects Suifenhe to Vladivostok, though this requires a valid Russian visa obtained in advance.

**By road:** The road from Mudanjiang is in reasonable condition and self-driving is practical. Winter driving requires appropriate preparation.
