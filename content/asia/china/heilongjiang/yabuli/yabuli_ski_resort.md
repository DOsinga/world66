Yabuli Ski Resort is China's largest and most established alpine ski area, spread across the slopes of Daqingshan Mountain in the Zhangguangcai Range. The resort has over a dozen runs of varying difficulty, from wide easy slopes for beginners to the competition-standard descent that hosted the 1996 Asian Winter Games. The vertical drop and run lengths are respectable by domestic standards, and the snow cover from November through March is reliable given the continental Manchurian climate.

The lift system has been progressively upgraded and can handle the peak crowds that arrive at Chinese New Year and major holiday weekends. Outside of those peaks, the resort is surprisingly relaxed — mid-week in January or February gives good conditions with short queues. Equipment rental is available at the base and is of reasonable quality. Ski instruction is available in Mandarin at most skill levels; English-speaking instructors are rarer but can usually be arranged with advance notice. The resort accommodation ranges from budget dormitory bunks to reasonably comfortable hotels, and the shuttle buses from Harbin make multi-day stays straightforward.
