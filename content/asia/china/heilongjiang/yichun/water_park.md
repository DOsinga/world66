Water Park is a riverside park in Yichun that changes character with the season. In summer it is a simple place to walk by the river, while in winter it becomes one of the city's main ice-carving spots.

The winter sculptures are the reason to make a deliberate stop. Visit after dark if lighting is in place, and dress for a slow outdoor walk in severe cold.
