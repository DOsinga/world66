Yuefei Temple near Tangyin is dedicated to Yue Fei, the Song-dynasty general whose loyalty became a major theme in Chinese historical memory. For travelers based in Anyang, it is a meaningful excursion beyond the city's Shang and oracle-bone sites.

The temple is most rewarding if you have some interest in Chinese history and heroic cults. Its halls, inscriptions, and memorial atmosphere make it more than a generic out-of-town temple stop.
