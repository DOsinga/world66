---
title: Eating Out
type: section
---

Kaifeng has a rich street food tradition that claims its roots in the Song dynasty, when the city was famous for the variety and quality of its food vendors. The night market near Gulou Square is the centre of the evening scene; local specialities to seek out include the city's signature steamed buns, osmanthus rice cakes, five-spice beef soup, and the deep-fried flat bread known as guokui.
