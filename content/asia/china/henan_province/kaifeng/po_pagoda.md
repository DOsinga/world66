Po Pagoda is a stocky hexagonal Buddhist pagoda dating from 974. It once stood nine storeys high, but today its three remaining storeys are covered with large tiles showing Buddhist figures.

It sees fewer visitors than Kaifeng's better-known sights. If the internal stairway is open, it may be possible to climb inside.
