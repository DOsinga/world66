Dingding Gate Ruins Museum presents one of Luoyang's major archaeological sites: the remains of a monumental city gate from the Sui and Tang capital. It helps make the scale of ancient Luoyang visible in a city where much history lies under modern streets.

The museum is most rewarding for travellers already interested in Chinese capitals, archaeology, or the Tang dynasty. Pair it with Luoyang Museum and the old city sights for a fuller picture.
