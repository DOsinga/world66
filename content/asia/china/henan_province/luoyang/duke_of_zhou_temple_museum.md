The Duke of Zhou Temple Museum is a historic temple and museum complex in Luoyang connected with the statesman traditionally associated with early Zhou ritual and governance. It broadens the city's heritage beyond the headline Buddhist and imperial sites.

The visit is most rewarding for travelers interested in classical Chinese history and ritual culture. It is a quieter stop than Longmen or White Horse Temple, so allow time to read the context rather than rushing through the courtyards.
