Luoyang Go Museum is a private museum devoted to go, the classic Chinese board game known locally as weiqi. The collection is niche, but it gives a focused look at a game with deep roots in Chinese literati culture, strategy, and social life.

It is best for travellers who already have some curiosity about traditional games or want a small cultural stop away from Luoyang's headline archaeological sites.
