The Museum of Luoyang Eastern Zhou Royal Horse and Chariot Pits is built around the archaeological remains themselves, not just objects in cases. Its most striking display is the chariot pit, where horses and vehicles connected to Eastern Zhou royal power are preserved in situ.

Luoyang has so many big-ticket Buddhist and dynastic sights that this smaller museum can be missed, but it fills an important gap. It puts the ancient royal city under your feet and gives a tangible sense of ritual, status, and transport long before the later capitals.
