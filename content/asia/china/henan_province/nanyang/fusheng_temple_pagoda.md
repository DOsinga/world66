Fusheng Temple Pagoda is a Song-dynasty Buddhist tower that once rose to 13 storeys before war damage cut it down. The temple around it has disappeared, but the pagoda still gives a useful glimpse of older Nanyang-area religious architecture; artefacts found during restoration are displayed at the Henan Provincial Museum in Zhengzhou.
