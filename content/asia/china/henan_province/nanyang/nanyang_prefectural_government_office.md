The Nanyang Prefectural Government Office is a restored yamen, the kind of official compound where imperial administrators heard cases, issued orders, and staged the rituals of local authority. Its gates, halls, and courtyards make the old bureaucracy legible in a way that ordinary museum displays often do not.

It is a strong stop for travelers interested in Chinese urban history, especially because Nanyang's better-known sights are spread between temples, markets, and figures such as Zhang Heng. Here the focus is civic power: how a regional city was governed and how architecture reinforced that hierarchy.
