Ping Ding Mountain is the flat-topped hill that gives Pingdingshan its name. The climb is straightforward, and the summit is valued for its broad view over the city and the surrounding plain.

It is a simple outdoor stop rather than a major scenic area, but it gives the city a clear landmark and a good reason to get above street level.
