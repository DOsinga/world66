---
title: Getting There
type: section
---

Xinxiang sits on the main Beijing–Guangzhou high-speed rail corridor, making it easily accessible from both Zhengzhou (30–40 minutes south) and Beijing (about 2.5 hours north). The Xinxiang East Railway Station handles all high-speed services. From the station, buses and taxis reach the city centre in twenty minutes.

For the Taihang mountain sites in Huixian County, take a local bus or hire a car from Xinxiang north toward Huixian. Wanxianshan Scenic Area is about 80 kilometres from the city, taking roughly ninety minutes by road. Tour buses from Xinxiang and direct services from Zhengzhou operate on weekends and public holidays in peak season. The easiest way to visit Guoliang and the gorges independently is to hire a driver for the day in either Xinxiang or Zhengzhou.
