---
title: Things to Do
type: section
---

Most of what draws visitors to Xinxiang lies north of the city, in Huixian County where the plains abruptly give way to the Taihang escarpment. The Guoliang Tunnel and the Baligou gorges are the main attractions, both within the Wanxianshan Scenic Area — plan for a full day in the mountains rather than a rushed half-day.
