Qinglongxia is a quieter scenic area in the Yuntaishan system, centred on Fenglinxia Reservoir and its high dam. The usual visit combines shuttle-bus viewpoints, reservoir boat rides, and walking trails down through the gorge, with fewer crowds than the park's better-known canyon routes.
