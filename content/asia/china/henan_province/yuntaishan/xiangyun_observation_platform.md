Xiangyun Observation Platform is a glass-bottom viewpoint above the valley and reservoir scenery of Yuntaishan. The exposed walkway is built for the view as much as the thrill, with visitors clipped to a side cable on parts of the route.

It is a short, high-adrenaline stop within the wider mountain area rather than a standalone day trip.
