China Jun Porcelain Cultural Park is a large cultural complex devoted to Yuzhou's Jun ware tradition. It includes display areas, porcelain-themed architecture, and spaces focused on the craft and history of Jun ceramics.

It is most useful for travellers who want to understand Yuzhou through its best-known art form, especially when combined with the Jun Royal Kiln Site Museum.
