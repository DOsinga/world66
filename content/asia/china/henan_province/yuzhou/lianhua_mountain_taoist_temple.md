Lianhua Mountain Taoist Temple combines a mountain walk with a working religious site. Visitors come for the temple, the hillside setting, and the open views across the surrounding countryside.

It is a good counterpoint to Yuzhou's kiln history: less polished, more rural, and best visited when you have time for a slow walk rather than a quick photo stop.
