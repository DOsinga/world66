This Ming dynasty royal mausoleum holds the tomb of Zhu Youdun, King Ding of Zhou. The draw is the combination of preserved burial architecture, old trees, and a peaceful setting that gives a sense of traditional Chinese mausoleum culture outside Yuzhou's busier sights.
