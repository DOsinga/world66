Yuzhou Cultural and Creative Market is a weekend market for local crafts, artwork, and small handmade goods. It is especially useful if you are interested in Yuzhou's ceramic traditions but want something livelier than a museum shop.

Expect pottery, souvenirs, food stalls, and occasional live music. Go for the atmosphere and browsing rather than for a polished attraction.
