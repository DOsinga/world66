Yuzhou Forest Botanical Garden is a large green escape on the edge of the city, with tree collections, walking paths, viewing platforms, and space for families to spread out. It is more relaxed than formal, and works best as a slow walk rather than a checklist sight.

The garden is also used for nature education and occasional exhibitions, so it can be livelier during local events. Come for fresh air, shade, and a softer view of Yuzhou than the streets give you.
