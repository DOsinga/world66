Yuzhou Museum covers the city's long history, with exhibits on local pottery, regional culture, and artifacts connected to the Zhou period. It is a sensible first stop before visiting Yuzhou's kiln sites and older temples.

The museum is most useful for travellers who want background on why ceramics matter so much to the city.
