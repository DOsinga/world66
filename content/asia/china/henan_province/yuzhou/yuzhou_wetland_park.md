Yuzhou Wetland Park is a quiet nature stop on the edge of the city, with wetland plants, birdlife, and walking paths. It is a good place to slow down between Yuzhou's older ceramic and temple sights.

The park also has displays about local conservation. It is most rewarding in mild weather or during bird activity, when the wetlands feel less like a standard city park.
