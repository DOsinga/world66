Zhengzhou is the capital of [Henan Province](/asia/china/henan_province), a sprawling transport hub and industrial city that most travellers pass through on the way to somewhere else. The city itself is not beautiful, but it sits at a crossroads of Chinese history — [Shang Dynasty ruins](/asia/china/henan_province/zhengzhou/shang_city_ruins) lie beneath the modern streets, and some of China's most important ancient sites are within day-trip distance.

The big draw is Mount Songshan, about 90 minutes southwest, the central and most revered of China's five sacred mountains. At its foot sits the [Shaolin Monastery](/asia/china/henan_province/zhengzhou/shaolin_monastery), founded in 495 AD, birthplace of Chan (Zen) Buddhism and the legendary Shaolin martial arts. The monastery's Thousand Buddha Hall contains Tang Dynasty murals, and the Pagoda Forest nearby — over 220 memorial pagodas spanning a thousand years — is an extraordinary sight.

In the city, the [Henan Provincial Museum](/asia/china/henan_province/zhengzhou/henan_museum) houses one of China's finest collections of ancient bronzes, jades, and ceramics, with artefacts spanning from the Neolithic to the Qing Dynasty. The [Shang Dynasty city wall](/asia/china/henan_province/zhengzhou/shang_city_ruins) — 3,500 years old, unearthed in 1955 — is still partially visible in several places around the modern city. The [Yellow River Scenic Area](/asia/china/henan_province/zhengzhou/yellow_river_scenic_area), 30 kilometres north, is where the mother river of Chinese civilisation emerges from the loess hills and spreads across the North China Plain.

Zhengzhou is a major rail junction — the crossroads of the Beijing-Guangzhou and Lianyungang-Lanzhou lines — making it easy to reach from almost anywhere in China. High-speed rail connects it to Beijing in about 2.5 hours and Xi'an in under 2 hours.
