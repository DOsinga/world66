The Yellow River Museum gives Zhengzhou a subject bigger than the city itself. Its exhibits trace the river's geography, floods, sediment, hydraulic engineering, and cultural role, which is useful context before visiting the Yellow River Scenic Area north of town.

This is a museum for travelers who like infrastructure and environmental history as much as temples and dynasties. The Yellow River has shaped northern China through both fertility and disaster, and the museum makes that long, difficult relationship easier to grasp.
