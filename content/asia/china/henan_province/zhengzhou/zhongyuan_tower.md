Zhongyuan Tower is one of Zhengzhou's most visible modern landmarks, a broadcasting and observation tower rising above the flatter cityscape. The attraction is straightforward: height, views, and the chance to see the scale of a provincial capital that many travellers otherwise use only as a transport hub.

Go on a clear day or skip it; haze can turn the view into an exercise in imagination. When visibility cooperates, the tower helps orient you to Zhengzhou's spread and its mix of new districts, roads, and older urban pockets.
