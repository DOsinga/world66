Aberdeen Wholesale Fish Market is one of Hong Kong's major fish markets, tied to the harbour and fishing-trade history of Aberdeen. Visitors can see live seafood in the market area and, around the adjacent wharves, the working side of the trade.

It is most interesting early in the day and for travellers who want a more functional food-market stop rather than a polished shopping experience. Some nearby seafood restaurants can prepare fish bought in the area.
