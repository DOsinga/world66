Central Plaza is one of Wan Chai's most visible skyscrapers, once the tallest building in Hong Kong. During business hours, visitors can usually ride to the 46th-floor sky lobby for a free harbour and island view, making it a useful indoor viewpoint as well as a landmark on the skyline.
