City Gallery is a small civic museum focused on how Hong Kong plans, builds, and manages its dense urban landscape. Interactive displays explain major infrastructure projects, harbourfront changes, transport links, and future development ideas.

It is most useful for visitors who enjoy cities as systems: maps, models, reclamation, rail lines, skyline changes, and the practical choices behind Hong Kong's vertical form.
