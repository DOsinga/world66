Dialogue in the Dark is an unusual guided exhibition where you move through Hong Kong-themed environments in complete darkness. The point is to rely on non-visual senses, usually with help from a visually impaired guide.

Tours take about 75 minutes. Reserve ahead if you need English or Mandarin rather than Cantonese.
