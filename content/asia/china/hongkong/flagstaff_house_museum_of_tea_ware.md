Flagstaff House Museum of Tea Ware is a quiet museum in Hong Kong Park, housed in one of the oldest surviving colonial buildings in the city. Its collection ranges from ancient Chinese tea vessels to modern ceramics, giving a compact introduction to tea culture without taking over half a day.

It works well as a short stop while walking between Admiralty, Central, and the park's gardens. The setting is half the appeal: a historic house surrounded by greenery in the middle of Hong Kong's densest business district.
