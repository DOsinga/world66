Haw Par Mansion was built for Aw Boon Haw, the Burmese-born Chinese entrepreneur behind Tiger Balm. The mansion's Chinese Renaissance architecture makes it one of Hong Kong's more unusual historic houses, though visits are limited and tours have usually been offered only on selected weekends.
