Hong Kong Correctional Services Museum is a specialist museum in Stanley covering the history of prisons and penal administration in Hong Kong. Displays include mock prison cells, a mock gallows, photographs, documents, and correctional-service artifacts.

It is an unusual add-on if you are already visiting Stanley, especially for travelers interested in Hong Kong's colonial institutions.
