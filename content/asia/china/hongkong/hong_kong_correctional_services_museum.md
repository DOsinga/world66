The Hong Kong Correctional Services Museum is a small but memorable museum above Stanley, tracing the territory's prison system from colonial punishment to modern correctional administration. The displays include historical photographs, documents, uniforms, a mock gallows, and reconstructed prison cells.

It is a specialist stop, but a good one if you are already visiting Stanley Market or the south side of Hong Kong Island. The subject matter is sober, and the museum gives useful context to nearby Stanley Prison, one of Hong Kong's long-running institutions.
