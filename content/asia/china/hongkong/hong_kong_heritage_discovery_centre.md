Hong Kong Heritage Discovery Centre occupies former British military barracks in Kowloon Park. The building is part of the appeal, and the exhibitions introduce Hong Kong's archaeology, built heritage, and conservation work.

It is a quiet, useful stop when walking through Tsim Sha Tsui or Kowloon Park.
