The Hong Kong Museum of Art sits on the Tsim Sha Tsui waterfront and pairs strong harbour views with collections of Chinese painting, ceramics, antiquities, and Hong Kong art. The museum is especially good for seeing how local artists have worked between Chinese traditions and the city's British colonial history.

Allow time for the temporary exhibitions as well as the permanent galleries, then step outside for one of Kowloon's best views across Victoria Harbour.
