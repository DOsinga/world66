The Hong Kong Racing Museum sits at Happy Valley Racecourse and explains how horse racing became one of the city's great public rituals. The displays cover the racecourse, famous horses and jockeys, betting culture, and the role of the Hong Kong Jockey Club.

It is a compact museum, best combined with a walk around Happy Valley or a race night if one is scheduled. Even if you are not a racing fan, it gives a useful window into a sport that has shaped Hong Kong's social life and philanthropy for generations.
