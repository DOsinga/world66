Hong Kong Space Museum is a family-friendly science museum on the Tsim Sha Tsui waterfront, easy to recognize by its domed planetarium. Exhibits cover space flight, astronomy, and lunar exploration, with hands-on simulators mixed in with static displays.

The planetarium shows are the main reason to plan ahead. Most sessions are in Cantonese, but English options are sometimes available.
