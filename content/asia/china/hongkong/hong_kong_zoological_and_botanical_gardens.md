Hong Kong Zoological and Botanical Gardens is a free hillside garden above Central, with shaded paths, mature trees, birds, reptiles, primates, and a small mammal collection. It works well as a quiet break from the city centre, especially with children or anyone interested in old Hong Kong's colonial-era public gardens.
