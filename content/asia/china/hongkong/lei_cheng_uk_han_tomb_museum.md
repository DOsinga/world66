Lei Cheng Uk Han Tomb Museum protects a brick tomb from the Eastern Han dynasty, discovered in 1955. The small exhibition hall explains the tomb and displays pottery and artifacts found during the excavation.

It is a compact stop, but worthwhile if you are in Sham Shui Po or want an older layer of Hong Kong history than the city usually shows at street level.
