Lung Fu Shan Country Park is a small, accessible patch of green on the slopes west of the Peak. It gives you a quick escape from Central and Sheung Wan without committing to a full day in the hills.

The paths lead through woodland, picnic spots, and old military remains, including Pinewood Battery from the Second World War. The reward is not wilderness, but a quiet walk with harbour views and a useful link into the larger Peak trail network.
