M+ is Hong Kong's major museum for contemporary visual culture, covering visual art, design, architecture, moving image, and related fields. Its galleries mix Hong Kong, Chinese, Asian, and global work in a large purpose-built building on the West Kowloon waterfront.

The museum is a strong stop for modern art and design, and the harbour setting makes it easy to combine with a walk through the West Kowloon Cultural District.
