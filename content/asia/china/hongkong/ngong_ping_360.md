Ngong Ping 360 is the cable car linking Tung Chung with Ngong Ping on Lantau Island. The 5.7-km ride gives wide views over the airport, forested hills, the South China Sea, and the approach to the Tian Tan Buddha.

At the upper station, Ngong Ping Village is a convenient starting point for Po Lin Monastery, the Big Buddha, and walks into the surrounding hills.
