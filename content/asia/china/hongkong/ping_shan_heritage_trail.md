The Ping Shan Heritage Trail is a short walk through one of Hong Kong's oldest settled areas. The route links clan halls, study halls, old village buildings, and the Tsui Sing Lau Pagoda, giving a compact look at the Tang clan heritage of the northwestern New Territories.

The trail is easy to follow and works well as a half-day cultural stop away from the denser parts of Hong Kong.
