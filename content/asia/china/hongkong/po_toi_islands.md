The Po Toi Islands sit south of Hong Kong Island and feel much farther from the city than they are. The main island is known for weathered rock formations, open sea views, quiet paths and a small fishing-village atmosphere that makes a good antidote to Hong Kong's density.

Come for a half-day of walking and seafood rather than beaches or nightlife. Ferry service is limited, so check the return times before you settle into lunch.
