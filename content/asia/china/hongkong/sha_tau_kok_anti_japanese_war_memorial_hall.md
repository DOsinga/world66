Sha Tau Kok Anti-Japanese War Memorial Hall is a small World War II museum in the former Luo family home near the Hong Kong-Shenzhen border. The house was used by Communist-led guerrilla fighters who crossed from the mainland to resist the Japanese occupation.

Visits are by advance booking and usually for groups, but the museum is outside the Frontier Closed Area, so no border-area permit is needed.
