Stanley Main Beach is one of Hong Kong Island's better-known recreation beaches, popular with windsurfers and dragon boat teams. It has the practical facilities that make a beach stop easy: changing rooms, showers, toilets, barbecue areas, shark nets, and a small cafe nearby.
