Tai O is a traditional fishing village on Lantau Island, known for its stilt houses over the tidal channels. The lanes, bridges, seafood shops, and waterfront views make it one of Hong Kong's most distinctive heritage areas.

It is worth visiting slowly, with time to walk the village, look over the water, and sample the local dried seafood snacks.
