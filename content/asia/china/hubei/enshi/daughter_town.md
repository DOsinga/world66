Daughter Town is a purpose-built Tujia-themed visitor district in Enshi, mixing restaurants, craft stalls, performance spaces, and photogenic streets. It is not an untouched old village, but it does package regional culture in a way that is easy to visit after dark.

Go with the right expectations: this is a lively, commercial folk-culture quarter rather than a quiet heritage site. It works best in the evening, when lights, snacks, and performances make it more engaging than a daytime walk-through.
