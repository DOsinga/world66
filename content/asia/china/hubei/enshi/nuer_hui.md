Nuer Hui is Enshi's best-known Tujia festival, held around the seventh to twelfth days of the seventh lunar month. It is often described as a local Valentine's festival: songs, courtship rituals, and public performances turn Tujia matchmaking traditions into a visitor-friendly event.

The festival is worth seeking out if your timing lines up, especially alongside Daughter Town and Enshi's other cultural sights. Dates follow the lunar calendar, so check locally before building a trip around it.
