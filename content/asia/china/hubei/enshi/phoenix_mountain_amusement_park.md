Phoenix Mountain Amusement Park is Enshi's main family amusement park, with rides ranging from roller coasters and a Ferris wheel to bumper cars and smaller children's attractions. It is a straightforward funfair-style stop rather than a cultural sight.

Families with children may find it useful on a spare afternoon in Enshi, especially if the weather is not right for the region's bigger outdoor trips.
