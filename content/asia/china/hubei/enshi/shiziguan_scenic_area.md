Shiziguan Scenic Area is a canyon-and-river landscape south of Enshi, in Xuan'en County. Its modern calling card is the floating bridge laid across the water, where cars and visitors appear to skim the surface between steep green slopes.

The bridge is the quick visual hook, but the area works best as a half-day scenic outing rather than a single-photo stop. Expect managed viewpoints, water scenery, and the usual Chinese scenic-area infrastructure; go on a clear day if you want the river colour that makes the place look almost unreal in photographs.
