Dakou National Forest Park is a mountain-green-space excursion near Jingmen, with forest paths, ridges, and scattered cultural sights. It gives the city a credible outdoor option for travelers who have already seen the central historic stops.

The park is set up for local and regional visitors, so expect a managed scenic area rather than backcountry walking. It is best approached as a half-day nature break with private transport.
