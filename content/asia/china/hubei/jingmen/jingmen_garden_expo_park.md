Jingmen Garden Expo Park is an ecological park built on a former mining site. It combines themed gardens, Chu-culture displays, a nature museum, children's areas, interactive installations, and night light shows.

It is most useful for families or visitors looking for a landscaped outdoor stop close to the city.
