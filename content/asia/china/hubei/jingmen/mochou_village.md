Mochou Village is a Chu-culture themed attraction with craft experiences, folk performances, boat rides, and Chu-style architecture. The area also has a literature and art museum, cultural displays, and places to eat.

It is a family-friendly stop rather than a major historic site, but it gives visitors near Jingmen an easy way to sample local heritage in one place.
