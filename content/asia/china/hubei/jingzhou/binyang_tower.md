Binyang Tower stands on Jingzhou's old city wall, one of the clearest reminders of the city's long strategic role on the middle Yangtze. The tower and wall section give visitors a tangible focus for understanding Jingzhou as a fortified historic city.

It is best visited as part of a walk along the wall and nearby old-town streets. The site is more rewarding if you allow time for the defensive setting rather than treating the tower as a quick isolated photo.
