Wanshou Park is a cultural stop as much as a green one. Its main sight is Wanshou Pagoda, a seven-storey Ming-era tower with carved brickwork and Buddhist figures inside. The park also includes Guanyin Rock and river views, making it a worthwhile detour beyond Jingzhou's better-known city walls and museum.
