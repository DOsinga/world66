Zhang Juzheng Former Residence is a reconstructed Ming- and Qing-style courtyard complex dedicated to the powerful Ming dynasty statesman. The site includes halls, gardens, and exhibits that explain Zhang's life and political career.

It is an easy add-on while exploring Jingzhou's old city walls and historic core.
