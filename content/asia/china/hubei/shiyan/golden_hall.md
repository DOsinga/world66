The Golden Hall is one of the great summit landmarks of Wudang Mountain, the Taoist mountain complex associated with Zhenwu worship and martial arts legend. The small bronze hall sits high on the mountain and forms the symbolic climax of the pilgrimage route, reached after a sequence of gates, stairs, and temple terraces.

It is not a standalone city sight in Shiyan; it belongs to the Wudang Mountain excursion. That context is what makes it worth the effort. Go prepared for crowds during holidays and for steep walking even if you use the mountain transport system for part of the climb.
