The Wudang Mountains are Shiyan's great travel draw: a sacred Taoist range where
temples, palaces, stairways, and forested peaks create one of China's most atmospheric
mountain-cultural landscapes. The area is also closely associated with internal martial
arts, giving it a reputation that extends far beyond Hubei.

Do not treat Wudang as a single viewpoint. The best visit takes a full day at minimum,
and an overnight stay lets you move more slowly between cable cars, paths, palaces, and
sunrise points. The mixture of religion, architecture, and mountain air is what makes it
exceptional.
