---
title: When to Go
type: section
---

The Wudang Mountains are accessible year-round but are at their best in spring (April to May) and autumn (September to October). Spring brings mild temperatures and wildflowers on the lower slopes; autumn brings clear skies, lower humidity, and golden forest colour. Summer is warm on the lower trails but cool at the summit, and the mountain is often shrouded in mist — atmospheric if you embrace it, frustrating if you came for views.

Winter transforms the complex with snow on the temple roofs and ice on the stone paths, creating images that look like classical Chinese paintings come to life. The upper paths require care in icy conditions, and some areas may be temporarily closed. Crowds peak during Chinese national holidays (Golden Week in October is particularly busy); weekday visits at any time of year are noticeably quieter.
