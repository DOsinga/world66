---
title: "Bars and Cafes"
type: section
---

Wuhan's bar scene is concentrated in three areas: the streets behind the Hankou riverfront (especially Lugou Bridge Road and the lanes off Liji North Road), the Tanhualin arts district in Wuchang, and the Optics Valley student quarter. The vibe ranges from concession-era cocktail bars to noisy student dives. Coffee shops are everywhere — local chains like Lucky Coffee and Manner now sit alongside the global brands.
