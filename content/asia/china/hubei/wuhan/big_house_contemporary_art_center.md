Big House Contemporary Art Center is a small contemporary art space in Wuchang. It is mainly worth considering for current exhibitions rather than for a fixed collection.

Weekend visits are usually simpler, while weekday visits may require booking ahead. Check the current programme before making a special trip, and combine it with nearby Wuchang sights if an exhibition interests you.
