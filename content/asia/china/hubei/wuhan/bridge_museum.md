Wuhan's Bridge Museum opened in 2019 and focuses on bridge building in a city defined by the Yangtze and Han rivers. Exhibits cover the history, engineering, and construction techniques behind major Chinese bridges.

It is a specialist museum, but a good stop for visitors interested in infrastructure or Wuhan's river crossings. It fits naturally with other Hanyang sights.
