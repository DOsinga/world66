The CCP Fifth Congress Site Memorial occupies a 1918 school building in Wuchang. From 1922 to 1927 it was associated with Chen Tanqiu, one of the founding figures of the Chinese Communist Party, and in 1927 it hosted the party's fifth congress.

The building is now part of the Wuhan Revolution Museum network. Exhibits cover the early party period, and several rooms have been restored to show how they looked during the congress.
