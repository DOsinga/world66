The CCP Party Discipline Construction History Museum is a branch of the Wuhan Revolution Museum in Wuchang. The focus is narrow, but it adds context to the revolutionary history clustered around Snake Hill and Yellow Crane Tower.

Expect mostly Chinese signage outside, with some English explanation inside. It works best as a short stop for travellers already exploring Wuhan's political and revolutionary sites.
