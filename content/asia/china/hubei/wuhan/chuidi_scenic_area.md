Chuidi Scenic Area, also known as Ma'anshan Forest Park, is part of the larger East Lake landscape on Wuhan's eastern side. The area mixes wooded hills, walking and cycling paths, lake edges, gazebos, and viewpoints over Yujia Lake.

It is a good choice when you want East Lake scenery with more room to wander than the busier central sections. The paths make it easy to build a half-day walk or bike ride, especially if combined with nearby Moshan or Wuhan Botanical Garden.
