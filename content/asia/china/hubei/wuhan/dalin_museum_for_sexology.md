The Dalin Museum for Sexology is one of China's rare museums devoted to sexology and erotic culture. Founded by scholar Liu Dalin, it displays historical objects and images that show how sexuality has been represented in Chinese art and daily life.

It is a niche museum, but that is exactly why it is interesting. Visitors who like offbeat collections will find something here that Wuhan's larger museums do not cover.
