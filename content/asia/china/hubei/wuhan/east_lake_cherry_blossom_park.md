East Lake Cherry Blossom Park is one of Wuhan's better places for spring blossom viewing, especially if the crowds around Wuhan University feel too intense. Outside the short flowering season it is a quieter corner of the wider East Lake area, best paired with Moshan or the lakeside paths nearby.
