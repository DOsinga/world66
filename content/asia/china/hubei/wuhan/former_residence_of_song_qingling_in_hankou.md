This late-19th-century Russian-style house is tied to one of modern China's best-known political families. Song Qingling, Sun Yat-sen's widow, lived here in the 1920s while working with the Kuomintang government in Wuhan.

The rooms have been restored with period details and old photographs, so the visit works both as a small house museum and as a window into Hankou's treaty-port streets.
