This modest Hankou building marks one of the key organizing points of the 1923 Beijing-Hankou railway strike. Union leaders met here after their Zhengzhou office was closed by the warlord Wu Peifu, then planned the walkout that was crushed a few days later.

It is mainly for travelers interested in China's labor and revolutionary history, but it fits well with Wuhan's other old railway, concession, and political-history sites.
