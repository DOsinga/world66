The Former Site of Wuhan Nationalist Government is a 1917 building originally built for the Nanyang Brothers Tobacco Company. In 1927 it briefly served as the seat of the left-wing Wuhan Nationalist Government, separate from Chiang Kai-shek's government.

A museum on the third floor explains the building's political role and the short-lived government that operated here. It is a useful stop for visitors interested in Republican-era Chinese history.
