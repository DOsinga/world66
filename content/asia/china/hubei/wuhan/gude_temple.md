Gude Temple is one of Wuhan's most unusual Buddhist temples, with a design that mixes Chinese, Southeast Asian, Middle Eastern, and European influences. Founded in the 19th century and expanded in the early 20th, it is worth visiting as much for its architecture as for its religious role.
