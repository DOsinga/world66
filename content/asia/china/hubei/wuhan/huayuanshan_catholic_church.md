Huayuanshan Catholic Church is a historic church in Wuchang, close to Snake Hill and the old city core. Its brickwork and quiet compound make it a worthwhile stop for visitors tracing Wuhan's treaty-port, mission, and republican-era architecture beyond the better-known riverfront sights.
