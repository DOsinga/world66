The James Jackson Museum is an art and design museum inside the former James Jackson Stadium, one of China's oldest modern indoor sports buildings. The stadium dates from 1921 and is named for James Jackson, a British theology professor connected with the site.

It is a useful stop in Wuchang if you are interested in early modern architecture as well as changing exhibitions.
