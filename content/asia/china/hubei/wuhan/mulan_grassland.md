Mulan Grassland is a large themed grassland park north of Wuhan, linked by legend to Hua Mulan's riding and archery training. The modern attraction mixes open scenery with family activities such as horse riding, archery, rowing, rafting, and seasonal shows.

It is a day-out destination rather than a central Wuhan sight. Go for the combination of landscape, legend, and activities, not for quiet wilderness.
