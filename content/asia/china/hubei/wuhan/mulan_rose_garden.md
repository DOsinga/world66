Mulan Rose Garden is a large flower garden in northern Wuhan, with more than 160 rose varieties spread across extensive grounds. The main bloom begins in March or April, and flowers can usually be seen through much of the warmer season, often into November.

It is a seasonal outing rather than a central-city sight, so it makes most sense for visitors with extra time in Wuhan or a specific interest in gardens and flower displays.
