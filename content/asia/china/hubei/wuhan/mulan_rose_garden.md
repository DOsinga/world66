Mulan Rose Garden is a large flower garden north of central Wuhan, planted with more than 160 varieties of roses across a broad landscaped site. The main bloom begins around March or April and can continue in waves into November.

It is a seasonal stop, so timing matters. Come when the roses are out and it works as a colourful half-day escape from the city; outside the flowering months it is much less compelling.
