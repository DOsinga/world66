The Museum of Zhang Zhidong tells the story of the late Qing official closely tied to Wuhan's industrial rise. Zhang promoted limited reform and helped establish the Hanyang Iron Works, the Hubei Arsenal, and other modern factories that reshaped the city. The museum is also known for its striking contemporary design, so it works even for visitors with only a passing interest in the history.
