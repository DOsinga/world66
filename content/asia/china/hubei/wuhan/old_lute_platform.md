Old Lute Platform is a memorial to Yu Boya and Zhong Ziqi, whose friendship became one of China's best-known stories about music and understanding. The original platform is said to date back to the Song dynasty, though the present structure has been rebuilt more than once.

It is a quiet, literary sort of sight rather than a blockbuster. Visit while exploring Hanyang, especially if you like places where a compact monument opens onto a much older cultural story.
