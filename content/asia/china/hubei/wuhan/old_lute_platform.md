Old Lute Platform is a memorial site tied to the story of Yu Boya and Zhong Ziqi, whose friendship is one of China's classic tales of musical understanding. The present platform is a rebuilt landmark, but it remains a meaningful stop in Hanyang for visitors following Wuhan's older cultural sites.
