Panlongcheng National Archaeological Park protects one of the largest known sites of the Bronze Age Erligang culture, which flourished in central China in the second millennium BC. The site museum displays finds from the ancient city and helps make sense of the excavated remains.

It sits north of central Wuhan and usually requires planning ahead. Visitors may need to book before visiting, so check current arrangements before making the trip.
