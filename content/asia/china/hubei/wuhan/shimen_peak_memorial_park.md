Shimen Peak Memorial Park is a large cemetery on the slopes of Stone Gate Peak, but its public art makes it more than a burial ground. The foothills are dotted with sculpture and memorial pieces, giving the eastern edge of Wuhan an unusual mix of cemetery landscape and outdoor art walk.
