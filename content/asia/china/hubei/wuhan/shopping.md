---
title: "Shopping"
type: section
---

Wuhan's main shopping districts are Jianghan Road in Hankou (the pedestrianised commercial spine of the city, lined with department stores and snack vendors), Xudong Plaza in Wuchang (a vast mall complex catering to the eastern part of the city), and the malls of Optics Valley for cheap student-oriented gear. For souvenirs, the gift shops at the Yellow Crane Tower and Hubei Provincial Museum stock the better-quality items — lacquerware, embroidery, and bronze-bell replicas.
