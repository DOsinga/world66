---
title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Wuhan is a city of three towns at the confluence of two rivers, and the things to do are split between the three. **Wuchang**, on the south bank of the Yangtze, holds the Yellow Crane Tower, East Lake, and the Hubei Provincial Museum. **Hankou**, on the north bank, has the old treaty-port concession districts and the heart of the night-food scene. **Hanyang**, the smallest of the three, holds the Guiyuan Temple and the Qingchuan Pavilion looking back across to Snake Hill. Allow at least two full days and a long lunch.
