Wuhan Flower Expo Park is a large flower and garden park in the southern part of Wuhan. The planted area is extensive, and the park is at its best during seasonal flower displays, especially around the spring flower fair.

It is a better fit for travellers who have extra time in Wuhan or are already heading toward Jiangxia. Come for open-air colour and a relaxed walk rather than for a major museum-style attraction.
