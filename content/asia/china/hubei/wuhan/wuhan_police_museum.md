Wuhan Police Museum displays artifacts from the history of policing in Wuhan, including material connected to Hankou's concession-era institutions. The building itself is part of the appeal: it occupies the former police headquarters of the German Concession, built in the early 20th century.

It is a niche museum, but worthwhile for visitors already exploring Hankou's old treaty-port architecture.
