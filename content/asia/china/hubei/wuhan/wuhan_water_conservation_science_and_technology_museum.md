Wuhan Water Conservation Science and Technology Museum is a specialist museum devoted to water management, flood control, and river engineering. The theme is especially relevant in Wuhan, a city shaped by the Yangtze, Han River, lakes, and repeated flood-control works.

It is a niche stop rather than a first priority, but it can be useful for visitors interested in engineering, urban water systems, or the way Wuhan lives with its rivers.
