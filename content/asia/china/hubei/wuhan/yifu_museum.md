The Yifu Museum at China University of Geosciences is strongest on minerals, fossils, and earth science. Its mineral collection is large, and the dinosaur skeleton displays make it more approachable than a purely academic university museum.

It is most useful if you are already in eastern Wuhan or have a specific interest in geology and natural history. For families, it can be a good change of pace from temples, lake parks, and revolutionary history sites.
