Zhongshan Warship Museum displays the restored Zhongshan, a Chinese warship sunk during the Battle of Wuhan in 1938 and raised from the Yangtze in 1997. Exhibits cover the ship, its recovery, and the wider wartime history around Wuhan. A memorial and outdoor sculptures around the lake add context to the main museum hall.
