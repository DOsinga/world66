Guangde Temple traces its history to the Tang dynasty and was renamed during the Ming dynasty. The complex shows Ming and Qing architectural forms, with highlights including the Mahavira Hall, Sutra Library, and the brick-and-stone Duobao Pagoda.

It is a strong stop for visitors interested in Xiangyang's religious history, especially when paired with nearby Gulongzhong and the old city walls.
