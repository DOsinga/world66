Mi Gong Temple commemorates Mi Fu, the Northern Song calligrapher and painter. The temple dates back to the Yuan period and preserves Qing-style architecture, stone inscriptions, and rubbings connected with Mi Fu's work.

It is one of Xiangyang's better stops for traditional Chinese calligraphy and local cultural history, especially when paired with the old city walls and Xiangyang Museum.
