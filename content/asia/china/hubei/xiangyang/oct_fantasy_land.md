OCT Fantasy Land is Xiangyang's big contemporary theme-park attraction, built for rides, shows, and family entertainment rather than historic atmosphere. It broadens the city's visitor offer beyond walls, rivers, and Three Kingdoms associations.

The value is straightforward: go if you want a day of amusement-park energy, especially with children or teenagers. It is not the place to understand old Xiangyang, but it is a real part of how the modern city entertains itself.
