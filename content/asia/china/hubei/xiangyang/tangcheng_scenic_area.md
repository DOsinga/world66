Tangcheng Scenic Area is a large Tang-style architectural complex on the edge of Xiangyang. Its gates, towers, palaces and temple buildings were designed with film production in mind, so the appeal is visual rather than archaeological. Come for the scale, the photo opportunities and the chance to walk through a stylised version of imperial-era urban scenery.
