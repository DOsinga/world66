Tribe of the Three Gorges is a scenic area west of Yichang that packages Yangtze gorge landscapes with reconstructed village scenes, river views, and folk-culture performances. It is designed for visitors who want a more accessible gorge experience than a full cruise.

The site is commercialized, but the setting along the water and cliffs gives it real scenic value. It works best as a half-day excursion from Yichang for travelers interested in the Three Gorges landscape beyond the dam itself.
