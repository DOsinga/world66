Yichang Museum is the city's main cultural museum and a useful grounding point before or after a Yangtze River trip. Exhibits cover regional archaeology, Ba culture, local history, and the geography of the Three Gorges area.

For travellers using Yichang mainly as a cruise gateway, the museum helps the city feel less like a transfer point. It is also a sensible indoor choice when weather or river schedules disrupt plans.
