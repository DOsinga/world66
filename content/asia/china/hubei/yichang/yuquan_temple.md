Yuquan Temple is a historic Buddhist site near Dangyang, west of Yichang, and one of the more substantial temple excursions in the region. Its importance is tied to Chinese Buddhist history, especially the spread of Tiantai thought, rather than to a single photogenic landmark.

For travellers based in Yichang, the temple makes sense if you are looking beyond the Three Gorges Dam and river cruises. Expect courtyards, halls, old trees, incense, and a quieter pace than the Yangtze's heavily managed headline sights. It is best reached with private transport or a locally arranged trip.
