---
title: Bars and Cafes
type: section
---

Changsha has a nightlife culture that surprises visitors expecting a sedate provincial capital. The city's entertainment industry was turbocharged by the massive success of Hunan Satellite Television's reality and variety shows — including the long-running singing competition "Singer" — which brought celebrities and cultural attention to the city over two decades. The result is a bar and club scene with genuine local identity rather than imitation Shanghai or Beijing.

The Jiefang West Road area is the main nightlife concentration, with bars, clubs, and live music venues operating until early morning. The riverfront bars along the Xiangjiang embankment offer evening views and a slightly more relaxed atmosphere. For daytime coffee, the neighbourhoods around Yuelu University (near the academy) have a dense concentration of cafes serving Hunan University students and faculty.
