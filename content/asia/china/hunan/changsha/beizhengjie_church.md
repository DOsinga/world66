The Beizhengjie Christian Church on Huangxing North Road is Changsha's most architecturally distinctive non-Chinese building — a neo-Gothic Protestant church completed in 1921 by the American Episcopal mission and still in active use. The pointed arches, buttresses, and tower are a genuine oddity in the streetscape of an otherwise densely Chinese urban fabric, and the building's survival through the twentieth century's political disruptions is remarkable.

The church holds Sunday services in Mandarin and is open to visitors outside service hours. Inside, the Gothic proportions create an atmosphere quite different from anything else in Changsha — the height, the coloured light through pointed windows, and the silence stand apart from the city's usual intensity. The surrounding neighbourhood on Huangxing North Road is a mix of old shophouses and new development; the church's survival in its original form makes it all the more striking. Free to visit; respectful dress and behaviour expected during services.
