Changsha Ecological Zoo is a large animal park outside the central city, designed as a family-oriented wildlife and leisure destination. It offers a different kind of outing from Changsha's riverfront, museums, and old streets.

For most travellers it is mainly useful with children or a strong interest in zoos. Check transport and opening conditions before going, since it is not a quick central attraction.
