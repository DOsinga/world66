Changsha Museum is one of the city's main introductions to local history, using archaeological finds, historical displays, and urban interpretation to explain the capital of Hunan. It is especially useful because Changsha's rapid modern growth can hide its older layers.

Visit before or after Hunan Museum depending on how deep you want to go. Changsha Museum is more city-focused, giving context to streets, districts, and sites that might otherwise feel disconnected.
