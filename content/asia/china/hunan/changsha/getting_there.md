---
title: Getting There
type: section
---

Changsha is one of the best-connected cities in central China. Changsha Huanghua International Airport handles international flights to Southeast Asia and domestic connections to all major Chinese cities; it is about 25 kilometres east of the city centre, connected by metro Line 6 (about 50 minutes, ¥15–20). High-speed rail from Beijing takes around five hours; from Shanghai around four and a half hours; from Guangzhou about two hours; from [Wuhan](/asia/china/hubei/wuhan) about two hours. The main high-speed station is Changsha South, in the southeastern suburbs.

For travel within Hunan, Changsha is the hub. [Shaoshan](/asia/china/hunan/shaoshan) (Mao's birthplace) is 60 kilometres away and accessible in about 30 minutes by high-speed rail. [Zhangjiajie](/asia/china/hunan/zhangjiajie) is around three hours by high-speed train. [Changde](/asia/china/hunan/changde) is 90 minutes. [Yueyang](/asia/china/hunan/yueyang), on Dongting Lake, is 30 minutes north.
