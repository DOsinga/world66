Hunan First Normal University is where Mao Zedong received his formal education, studying here from 1913 to 1918. The campus has been preserved as a heritage site alongside the functioning university, with a museum documenting Mao's student years in Changsha and the broader history of progressive education in Hunan during the Republican period. The restored school buildings are in a traditional Chinese architectural style that makes the campus genuinely worth walking, independent of the Mao association.

The museum covers Mao's early intellectual development — his reading, his friendships with fellow students who would later become revolutionary figures, his early political writings, and the Hunanese intellectual climate of the 1910s that shaped his thinking. For anyone interested in the origins of the Chinese Communist movement, this is more revealing than the purely devotional sites: it places Mao in a specific intellectual and social context rather than presenting him as an inevitable historical force. Entry is free; the site is in the southwestern part of the old city.
