Longping Rice Museum turns one of Hunans defining crops into a surprisingly broad story. The displays connect rice farming, food culture, rural technology, and the work of Yuan Longping, the scientist whose hybrid-rice research became a major Chinese agricultural achievement.

It is a good counterpoint to Changshas revolutionary and entertainment-heavy sights. Travelers with an interest in food, science, or the countryside behind Hunan cuisine will get more out of it than casual museum-hoppers.
