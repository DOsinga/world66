Yuelu Academy, on the lower slopes of Yuelu Mountain on the west bank of the Xiang River, is one of the oldest and most celebrated educational institutions in China. Founded in 976 AD, it was a major centre of Confucian scholarship during the Song dynasty and continues to operate within the grounds of Hunan University — one of the few places in China where ancient academy architecture has survived continuously in its original function.

The buildings are arranged in the traditional academic compound style: lecture halls, dormitories, a library, and gardens connected by covered walkways. The main lecture hall contains calligraphic plaques including the famous "Realising the Way" inscription written by the Song philosopher Zhu Xi, who taught here in the twelfth century. The gardens are particularly fine in autumn when the ginkgo trees turn gold. Entry costs around ¥20; the academy is open daily. It is a ten-minute walk from the base of the Yuelu Mountain cable car, and most visitors combine the two.
