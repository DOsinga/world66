Dwarf Bridge is a high suspension bridge over a steep valley near Dehang, where fog often collects below the deck. Visitors cannot walk across the expressway bridge itself, but the scenic area has viewing platforms, sightseeing transport, and an elevator that make it possible to see the span and gorge up close.
