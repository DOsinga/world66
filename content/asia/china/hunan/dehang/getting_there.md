---
title: Getting There
type: section
---

Dehang is reached by bus from Jishou (about 45 minutes, ¥15–20) or from [Fenghuang](/asia/china/hunan/fenghuang) (about an hour). Jishou is on the high-speed rail line connecting [Changsha](/asia/china/hunan/changsha) and Chongqing via Zhangjiajie — a journey of about 2.5 hours from Changsha. There is no rail connection directly to Dehang.

The most efficient circuit of western Hunan visits Jishou as a base, with day trips to Dehang (north) and Fenghuang (south). Alternatively, a two-night loop combining Dehang and Fenghuang with an overnight in each gives a more rounded picture of Xiangxi than either destination alone provides.
