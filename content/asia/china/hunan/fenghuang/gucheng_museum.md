Gucheng Museum is a compact stop inside Fenghuang's old town, useful for putting the riverside lanes, stilt houses, and regional culture into context. It gives visitors a slower, indoor counterpoint to the town's busy bridges and waterfront walks.

The museum is best treated as part of a broader old-town circuit rather than as a standalone reason to come. If Fenghuang feels overly theatrical at street level, a short museum visit can help reconnect the setting with local history and identity.
