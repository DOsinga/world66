---
title: Things to Do
type: section
---

Fenghuang rewards early mornings and late evenings. The old town at seven in the morning, before the tour groups arrive, is a different place from the same streets at noon — quieter, more authentic in feel, the wooden buildings golden in early light and the river empty except for local fishermen. Plan to arrive in the afternoon, explore the evening, and get up early. Two nights are better than one.
