Furong — ancient name Wangcun, current official name Furong Ancient Town — sits on cliff faces above a waterfall that drops into the You River in Yongshun County, northwestern [Hunan](/asia/china/hunan). The town was renamed after the 1986 film "Hibiscus Town" (Furong Zhen), directed by Xie Jin and starring Liu Xiaoqing and Jiang Wen, which used the setting as a backdrop for a story about ordinary lives destroyed and rebuilt across the upheavals of Maoist-era campaigns. The film is one of the most important Chinese works of the reform era and brought international attention to a town that had previously been known mainly to people who lived near it.

The [waterfall](/asia/china/hunan/furong/furong_waterfall) is the defining physical fact of the place. A two-tiered fall drops more than sixty metres into a pool below the old commercial street, which was built on the hillside above and below the falls on stilted wooden structures typical of [Tujia architecture](/asia/china/hunan/furong/tujia_architecture). The [ancient street](/asia/china/hunan/furong/ancient_street) itself — stone-paved, about 1,000 metres long, flanked by two- and three-storey timber buildings — climbs steeply from the river level to the plateau above, with the waterfall audible and partially visible throughout. At night, lit by lanterns, the street has a quality that goes some distance toward explaining why it appealed to a filmmaker. The [film site](/asia/china/hunan/furong/hibiscus_town_film_site), including the original tofu stall, is preserved near the bottom of the street.

The Tujia people are the original inhabitants of this landscape, and elements of Tujia material culture — [brocade weaving](/asia/china/hunan/furong/tujia_brocade), lacquerwork, carved wooden architecture — are visible in the workshops and stalls along the street, though the distinction between locally produced craft and tourist-market product requires attention to identify. The surrounding county contains additional Tujia villages without tourism infrastructure that reward exploration.

Furong is midway between [Fenghuang](/asia/china/hunan/fenghuang) and [Zhangjiajie](/asia/china/hunan/zhangjiajie), making it a natural stop on a circuit of western Hunan. A high-speed railway connection opened in 2021 substantially improved access; it is also reachable by road from [Laosicheng](/asia/china/hunan/laosicheng), 50 kilometres to the southeast.
