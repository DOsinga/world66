Qianyang Ancient City, about 60 kilometres northeast of Huaihua, is a 2,200-year-old settlement with surviving Ming and Qing dynasty streets that remains largely off the standard tourist circuit. Where [Fenghuang](/asia/china/hunan/fenghuang) has been packaged and priced, Qianyang still functions as a normal Hunan town — the old streets are commercial rather than performative, and the residents going about their business outnumber the visitors by a significant margin.

The historic core contains well-preserved guild halls, clan ancestral temples, and arched stone bridges, all in the dense residential street pattern typical of prosperous Ming and Qing settlements. The riverfront section is particularly atmospheric. A day trip from Huaihua makes the most sense; the journey takes about an hour by bus. The lack of English signage is part of the experience rather than a problem, and the Hunan cooking here is excellent.
