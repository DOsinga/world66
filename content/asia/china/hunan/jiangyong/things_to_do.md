title: Things to Do
type: section
---

Jiangyong's appeal is specific and compelling: the Nüshu museum is the only place in the world to see manuscripts and artefacts of the women's script in context, and the surrounding villages have a quiet agricultural beauty that rewards slow exploration. This is not a destination for those seeking convenience — distances are real, English is absent, and the infrastructure is modest. For those who care about the story, it is entirely worth it.
