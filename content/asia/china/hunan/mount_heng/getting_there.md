---
title: Getting There
type: section
---

The most convenient access is by high-speed train to Hengshan West station (also called Nanyue station), from which Nanyue town at the base of the mountain is a short taxi or bus ride. From [Changsha](/asia/china/hunan/changsha) the train takes about 30 minutes; from Guangzhou about two hours. [Hengyang](/asia/china/hunan/hengyang) is the nearest major city, about 45 minutes by bus or shared taxi from Nanyue.

Within the mountain park, cable cars run from Nanyue town to mid-mountain and from mid-mountain toward the upper section. The park entrance ticket (¥80 off-season, ¥120 in summer) includes internal shuttle buses. A full day is needed for the summit; starting early avoids the worst of the midday crowds and gives the best chance of clear summit views.
