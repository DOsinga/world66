Huixian Bridge is a small but memorable feature on Mount Heng: a rock wedged into the mountain landscape so that it forms a narrow natural bridge. It is not the grandest sight on the sacred mountain, but it gives hikers a more intimate encounter with the terrain.

The appeal is the detour. While many visitors concentrate on temples and the summit, this spot pulls you onto quieter paths where the mountain feels less processed. Take care in wet weather, since the romance of the bridge depends on exactly the kind of stone footing that can turn slick.
