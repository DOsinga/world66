---
title: Things to Do
type: section
---

Tianmen Mountain is a full-day experience structured around a sequence of spectacles: the cable car from the city, the mountain road with 99 hairpin bends, the Heaven Gate Cave and its 999 steps, the glass walkways on the cliff edges, and the forested plateau with its small temples. Each of these elements is different enough from the others that the day never feels repetitive — though the crowds on weekends can be substantial.
