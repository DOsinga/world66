---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

Wulingyuan is open year-round but the experience varies significantly by season. Autumn (September to November) gives the best combination of clear skies, moderate temperatures, and manageable crowds. Spring (March to May) brings mist and blooming vegetation, with fog most frequent in April — the fog is beautiful but obscures the views; it also reduces the crowds. Summer is hot, humid, and at peak occupancy during July and August and the Golden Week national holidays; the park walkways become genuinely congested.

Winter is the least visited season and can be unexpectedly rewarding: snow on the columns is a famous visual that photographers travel specifically to capture, and the absence of crowds makes the quieter trails accessible. The park doesn't close but cable cars may suspend operations in high wind or ice; checking conditions before arrival is advisable. Weekday visits at any season reduce crowds significantly compared to weekends.
