Wulongzhai, formerly called Black Dragon Walled City, is a rugged scenic area in Yangjiajie within the Wulingyuan landscape. The route is known for steep paths, narrow rock passages, and viewpoints rather than polished facilities.

It is best treated as a hiking stop for visitors who want a rougher corner of Wulingyuan beyond the most famous viewing platforms.
