Cycling along Dongting Lake gives Yueyang visitors a slower way to experience the vast wetland landscape that shapes the city. The lake edge, bridges, fishing activity, and changing water levels make the ride feel different from a standard urban bike route.

Routes and bike access can vary, so this works best with current local advice rather than a fixed checklist. It is most rewarding in fair weather and pairs naturally with Yueyang Tower or Junshan Island plans.
