The Yueyang Intangible Cultural Heritage Center introduces local crafts and traditions from the Yueyang area, including paper-cutting, bamboo weaving and traditional dyeing. It is a modest cultural stop rather than a major museum.

The center is most worthwhile for travellers interested in hands-on craft traditions or looking for context beyond Yueyang Tower and Dongting Lake. Pair it with Yueyang Museum for a fuller view of the city's local culture.
