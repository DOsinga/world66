The [Wulingyuan](/asia/china/hunan/wulingyuan) World Heritage area encompasses more than 3,000 quartzite sandstone columns rising from forested gorges, four major scenic areas, and a complete tourist infrastructure of cable cars, internal buses, and the extraordinary Bailong Elevator. Most visitors buy a multi-day ticket (around ¥227 for four consecutive days) and spend two or three full days working through the main areas.

The standard itinerary covers Huangshizhai viewpoints on day one, Golden Whip Stream valley walk and the Bailong Elevator on day two, and Yuanjiajie or Tianzi Mountain on day three. Each area offers a distinct experience: Huangshizhai provides the panoramic overview, Golden Whip Stream the intimate valley-level perspective, Yuanjiajie the Avatar peak, and Tianzi Mountain the highest viewpoints with sea-of-cloud views when conditions allow. Baofeng Lake and Huanglong Cave are worthwhile additions outside the main park boundary. The park entrance is at Wulingyuan village, 50 kilometres north of Zhangjiajie city.
