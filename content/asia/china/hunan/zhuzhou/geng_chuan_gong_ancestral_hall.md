Geng Chuan Gong Ancestral Hall is a Qing-era clan shrine in Liling, first built in 1861 and later associated with Geng Biao, the military officer and diplomat born here. The complex is a provincial-level protected cultural relic, with traditional halls and displays tied to local farming culture.

It is a specialist stop rather than a must-see for every Zhuzhou visitor, but it is worth keeping on the map for travellers interested in local history beyond the main city centre.
