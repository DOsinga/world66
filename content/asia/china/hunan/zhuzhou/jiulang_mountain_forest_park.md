Jiulang Mountain Forest Park covers the highest peak in the urban Changsha-Zhuzhou-Xiangtan area, with forest paths and broad views over Zhuzhou. It is most rewarding in clear weather, especially around sunrise or sunset when the city and surrounding hills are easier to pick out.
