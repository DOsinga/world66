Liling Ceramic Museum is the most direct way to understand why this county-level city
east of Zhuzhou is known for porcelain. The displays trace local ceramic production from
older kiln work to the bright underglaze porcelain associated with modern Liling.

It is a specialist stop, but a worthwhile one if you are using Zhuzhou as a base and
want something more distinctive than another urban park. Pair it with a walk through
Liling's ceramic districts or shops, where the craft is still a working industry rather
than only a museum subject.
