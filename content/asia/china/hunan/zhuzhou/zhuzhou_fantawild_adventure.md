Zhuzhou Fantawild Adventure is a large theme park with science-fiction, animation, and high-tech show rides as its main draw. Major attractions include roller coasters, flying simulators, and dinosaur-themed indoor rides, along with stage performances. It sits outside central Zhuzhou and works best as a half-day or full-day family outing.
