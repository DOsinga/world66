Zhuzhou Fantawild Dreamland is a large theme park built around Chinese mythology, animation, and family rides. Its headline attractions include roller coasters, indoor shows, water effects, and seasonal festival events. It is outside the city center, so plan it as a half-day or full-day outing rather than a quick stop between downtown sights.
