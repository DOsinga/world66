  The television tower was completed in 1997 and at 260 metres was for several years the tallest structure in Hunan. ---

The Zhuzhou Television Tower rises 260 metres from the Shennongcheng hill, visible from most of the city. It does not have a public observation deck, so its value to visitors is entirely visual — as a landmark for orientation and as a recognisable element in the city's skyline when seen from the Xiang River waterfront.

The tower's proximity to the Shennong Tower means both structures appear together in panoramic photographs from the river, an unintentional but visually interesting pairing of traditional architectural symbolism and industrial-era function. For those interested in Chinese urban form, the Shennongcheng hill as a whole — with its mythological park, television infrastructure, and city views — is a compact example of how mid-sized Chinese cities negotiate the relationship between commemoration and practical development.
