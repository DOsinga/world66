Hohhot is the natural base for day trips and overnight excursions across central Inner Mongolia. The grassland destinations — Xilamuren, Gegentela, Huitengxile — are all within reach, and the historical sites of the Hohhot suburban area (Wang Zhaojun Mausoleum, White Pagoda, Shengle ruins) fill a half-day each. For longer excursions, Ordos and its Genghis Khan Mausoleum are about three hours south, and Erlian to the north provides the border crossing experience.
