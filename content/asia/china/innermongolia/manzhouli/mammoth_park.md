Mammoth Park is one of Manzhouli's deliberately theatrical attractions, using oversized prehistoric-animal sculptures to turn the grassland edge of the city into a photo-friendly stop. The setting fits Manzhouli's taste for big, playful public spaces rather than subtle museum interpretation.

It is best treated as a short outing, especially for families or anyone already visiting the city's Russian-style squares and border-themed sights. The appeal is visual and light-hearted: broad skies, open lawns, and enormous mammoth figures against the steppe landscape.
