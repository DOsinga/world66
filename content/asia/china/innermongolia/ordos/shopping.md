title: Shopping
type: section
---

Ordos is the home of Chinese cashmere. The Erdos brand originated here and the factory outlet stores in Dongsheng offer the best prices in China on quality cashmere products. Beyond the brand name, smaller workshops in the wholesale district sell unbranded cashmere at even lower prices. The Sunday market in Dongsheng covers a wider range of goods including Mongolian handicrafts and regional food products.
