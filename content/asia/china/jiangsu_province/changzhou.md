Changzhou (常州) sits between Shanghai and Nanjing on one of China's busiest rail lines, and most travellers pass through without stopping. That is understandable — Changzhou is not a city built for tourism — but it rewards a day's exploration more than its reputation suggests. It is a working Chinese city of about four million people, compact enough to get around on foot or by metro, with a handful of genuinely interesting sites and without the hordes that follow visitors around Suzhou or Hangzhou.

The main sight is [Tianning Temple](/asia/china/jiangsu_province/changzhou/tianning_temple), a large Buddhist complex dominated by a thirteen-storey timber pagoda that is one of the tallest wooden structures in China. It is impressive in scale and less commercialised than many comparable temple complexes; weekday mornings, when monks are going about their practice and the incense smoke drifts across empty courtyards, it is genuinely atmospheric. [Dongpo Park](/asia/china/jiangsu_province/changzhou/dongpo_park), on the Grand Canal, is free to enter and named after the Song dynasty poet Su Dongpo, who was briefly posted here; the canal section at its edge is one of the more pleasant stretches of the waterway accessible from a city centre anywhere in Jiangsu.

Changzhou has a small claim on culinary fame: the [sesame bing](/asia/china/jiangsu_province/changzhou/changzhou_sesame_bing), a flaky round pastry filled with either sweet or salty fillings, is a local specialty worth seeking out from any streetside vendor. The city is also known historically for handmade combs — an industry that apparently stretches back two thousand years — and [Canal 5](/asia/china/jiangsu_province/changzhou/canal_5), a converted textile mill complex near the Grand Canal, hosts studios, galleries, and a decent market at weekends.

If you are moving along the Shanghai–Nanjing corridor and want to break the journey somewhere that functions as an actual city rather than a museum piece, Changzhou is the best option. High-speed trains connect it to both endpoints in under an hour.
