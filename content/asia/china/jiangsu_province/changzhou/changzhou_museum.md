Changzhou Museum is the city's main general museum, with exhibits that connect local archaeology, history, art, and natural specimens. For visitors using Changzhou as more than a transit stop, it is the clearest indoor primer on the city and surrounding region.

The museum works well early in a visit because it gives context for temples, canals, and older neighbourhoods elsewhere in town. Allow time for changing exhibitions if they are open during your visit.
