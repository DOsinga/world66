---
title: Eating Out
type: section
---

Changzhou has a distinct culinary identity within Jiangsu's crowded food landscape. The sesame bing — a flaky round pastry with sweet or savoury fillings, baked against the walls of a cylindrical oven — is the city's signature street food and worth seeking from any busy vendor. The Grand Canal area and the streets around Tianning Temple have the best concentration of local-style restaurants.
