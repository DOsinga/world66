Hongmei Park is one of central Changzhou's main green spaces, wrapped around the Tianning Temple area with ponds, gardens, pavilions, and shaded paths. It gives visitors an easy place to slow down between the city's temple, museum, and shopping districts.

The park is most appealing as a combined landscape-and-temple walk rather than as a standalone destination. Its central location and local popularity make it a good place to see everyday Changzhou alongside more formal sightseeing.
