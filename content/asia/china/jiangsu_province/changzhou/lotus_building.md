The Lotus Building is Changzhou's most recognizable contemporary architectural landmark, designed as a lotus-flower form rising from an artificial lake beside People's Park. Its sculptural exterior is the main draw, especially when seen across the water or lit in the evening.

It is a useful stop for travellers interested in modern Chinese civic architecture or photography. The building's function is administrative, so the visit is primarily about the exterior, the park setting, and the contrast with Changzhou's older temples and canals.
