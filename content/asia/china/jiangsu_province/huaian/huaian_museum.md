Huai'an Museum is the city's general museum, presenting local history, cultural relics, and regional context beyond the better-known Zhou Enlai sites. It is a useful indoor stop for understanding the city as more than a single historical association.

The museum is best early in a visit or on a rainy day. Because it is outside some tourist circuits, confirm opening information locally before travelling across town.
