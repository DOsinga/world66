Journey to the West Amusement Park draws on Huai'an's connection with Wu Cheng'en, the author traditionally associated with the classic novel. The theme gives the park a local literary hook rather than making it just another ride complex.

It sits outside the central heritage circuit, so it is mainly for families, theme-park fans or visitors especially interested in Journey to the West culture. Check current opening details before making the trip.
