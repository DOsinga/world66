Wentong Pagoda is a Tang dynasty brick pagoda in the old city of Huai'an, one of the oldest standing buildings in Jiangsu province. It is seven storeys tall, built in the classic Chinese brick-pagoda style with projecting eaves at each level, and despite its age remains structurally sound and visually striking. The pagoda has survived repeated floods, rebellions, and the general destruction of Huai'an's old city fabric over the centuries.

The pagoda stands in a small park that provides a respectful setting without over-elaborating the site. It is not open to climb but can be appreciated from the exterior and the surrounding grounds. The visit is brief — fifteen to twenty minutes — and pairs naturally with the nearby Zhenhuai Building and the old canal quarter.
