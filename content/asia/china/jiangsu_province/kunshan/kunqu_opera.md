Kunqu — kun opera — is the classical musical theatre form that originated in Kunshan county and gave the genre its name. Inscribed by UNESCO as an intangible cultural heritage in 2001, it is one of the oldest surviving theatrical traditions in the world, combining song, speech, dance, and acrobatics in a formal style that evolved over five centuries. The pacing is deliberate, the costumes elaborate, and the vocal technique entirely unlike anything in the Western theatrical tradition.

Performances are held periodically at venues in Kunshan and at the Kunshan Opera Museum near Tinglin Garden, which provides good contextual displays about the form's history and technique. For visitors interested in traditional Chinese performance culture, Kunshan offers access to a living tradition at its place of origin rather than through the more commercial channels available in Shanghai. Check local listings or the museum for upcoming performances.
