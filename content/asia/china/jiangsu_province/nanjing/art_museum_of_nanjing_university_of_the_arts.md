The Art Museum of Nanjing University of the Arts is a contemporary exhibition space tied to one of China's major arts universities. Its galleries show work by faculty, students, and visiting artists, with a program that mixes exhibitions, lectures, and cultural events.

It is most useful for visitors already interested in contemporary art or exploring Nanjing beyond the main historic sights.
