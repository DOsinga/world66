Jiefang Gate is one of the newer openings in Nanjing's great Ming city wall, cut near Xuanwu Lake and now used as a practical entry point into one of the city's best walking areas. It lacks the age of Zhonghua Gate, but it sits in a rewarding position where wall, lake, and parkland meet.

Use it as part of a longer circuit rather than a single destination. From here you can walk the wall sections, drop down toward Xuanwu Lake, or continue into the old northern approaches to the city. It is a good reminder that Nanjing's walls are not just monuments; they still shape how the city is crossed.
