The Liji Alley Comfort Station Site is a difficult but important memorial in Nanjing, preserving
buildings connected to the wartime Japanese military sexual slavery system. Exhibits document
survivor testimony, archival evidence, and the history of the site itself.

It is not an easy visit, and it should be approached with the same seriousness as Nanjing's other
war memory sites. For travelers interested in 20th-century history, it adds a specific and human
chapter that larger museums can only summarize.
