Nanjing Museum of Paleontology is a compact science museum with a strong fossil focus. Its displays cover dinosaurs, ancient plants, early animals, and the deep geological record that underlies the Yangtze region and China more broadly.

It is especially useful for families or travelers who want a change from imperial walls and Republican history. The location near Jiming Temple and Xuanwu Lake makes it easy to fold into a varied half-day.
