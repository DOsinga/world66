The Taiping Kingdom History Museum focuses on one of nineteenth-century China's most consequential rebellions, using Nanjing's own role as the Taiping capital to ground the story. Exhibits cover political, military, religious, and social dimensions of the movement.

It is a worthwhile specialist museum for visitors already exploring Nanjing's layered history. The surrounding historic setting also makes it a calmer stop than some of the city's larger museums.
