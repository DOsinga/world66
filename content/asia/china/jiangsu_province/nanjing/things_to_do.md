---
title: Things to Do
type: section
---

[Purple Mountain](/asia/china/jiangsu_province/nanjing/purple_mountain) holds most of Nanjing's major historical sites within a single forested hillside: the [Sun Yat-sen Mausoleum](/asia/china/jiangsu_province/nanjing/sun_yatsen_mausoleum), the [Ming Xiaoling Tombs](/asia/china/jiangsu_province/nanjing/ming_xiaoling_tombs) with their stone spirit way, [Linggu Temple and its Beamless Hall](/asia/china/jiangsu_province/nanjing/linggu_temple), the [Nanjing Museum](/asia/china/jiangsu_province/nanjing/nanjing_museum), [Plum Blossom Hill](/asia/china/jiangsu_province/nanjing/plum_blossom_hill), and [Meiling Palace](/asia/china/jiangsu_province/nanjing/meiling_palace) are all here.

In the old city, the [Ming City Wall](/asia/china/jiangsu_province/nanjing/nanjing_city_wall) and [Zhonghua Gate](/asia/china/jiangsu_province/nanjing/zhonghua_gate) show the Hongwu Emperor's defensive engineering at its most impressive. The [Presidential Palace](/asia/china/jiangsu_province/nanjing/presidential_palace) covers the Republican period. The [Nanjing Massacre Memorial Hall](/asia/china/jiangsu_province/nanjing/nanjing_massacre_memorial) is harrowing and essential. The [John Rabe House](/asia/china/jiangsu_province/nanjing/john_rabe_house) provides a more intimate perspective on 1937.

The [Confucius Temple and Qinhuai River](/asia/china/jiangsu_province/nanjing/confucius_temple) area is the entertainment and food heart of the old city — best at night. [Mochou Lake Park](/asia/china/jiangsu_province/nanjing/mochou_lake_park), [Xuanwu Lake Park](/asia/china/jiangsu_province/nanjing/xuanwu_lake_park), and [Jiming Temple](/asia/china/jiangsu_province/nanjing/jiming_temple) are the green spaces that locals use daily. [Librairie Avant-Garde](/asia/china/jiangsu_province/nanjing/librairie_avant_garde) is one of China's great bookshops. The [Jiangning Weaving Museum](/asia/china/jiangsu_province/nanjing/nanjing_jiangning_weaving_museum) covers the city's silk tradition in a prize-winning building.

The [Drum Tower](/asia/china/jiangsu_province/nanjing/drum_tower), [Chaotian Palace](/asia/china/jiangsu_province/nanjing/chaotian_palace), [Cathedral of the Immaculate Conception](/asia/china/jiangsu_province/nanjing/cathedral_immaculate_conception), and the [Zheng He Memorial](/asia/china/jiangsu_province/nanjing/zheng_he_memorial) fill a serious Nanjing itinerary. For further afield, [Foding Palace at Niushou Mountain](/asia/china/jiangsu_province/nanjing/foding_palace) and [Tangshan Hot Springs](/asia/china/jiangsu_province/nanjing/tangshan_hot_springs) are popular half-day trips within the city limits.
