---
title: Things to Do
type: section
---

Nantong's sights are spread between the old city, the Haohe River waterfront, and the scenic hills to the north. The city's museum culture is genuinely strong — the Nantong Museum is the oldest purpose-built public museum in China and the Textile Museum is among the best of its kind — and the Langshan hill complex offers views and temple atmosphere within easy reach of the centre.
