Luomahu Happy World is a family amusement area near Luoma Lake, with climbing, play structures, themed areas and other child-friendly activities. It is mainly useful for families who need an easy half-day outing rather than a cultural sight.

It pairs naturally with a lake visit or other stops on Suqian's northern side. Check opening times locally before making a special trip, as attractions of this kind can change schedules outside school holidays.
