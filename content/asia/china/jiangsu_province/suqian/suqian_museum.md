Suqian Museum covers the city's history, folk culture, and social change, with exhibits on local customs, economic development, and regional art. It is a useful first stop for travellers trying to understand Suqian beyond its better-known Xiang Yu associations.

The museum is most worthwhile if you read some of the historical context as you go. Pair it with Xiang Yu's Ancestral Home for a fuller picture of the city's place in Jiangsu history.
