Zaohe Longyun City is a cultural attraction in Suqian with exhibits on water conservancy, shipping history, and the Grand Canal, along with family-oriented theatre and entertainment facilities. It is a modern, managed sight rather than an old neighbourhood, but it gives useful context for the canals and waterways that shaped the area.

Allow time for the museum sections if you are more interested in history than the entertainment side.
