Mountain Villa with Embracing Beauty is one of Suzhou's classical gardens and part of the city's UNESCO-listed garden tradition. It is small, but its artificial mountain is a masterpiece of compressed landscape design, making the space feel far larger and wilder than its footprint.

Visit slowly. The garden rewards shifts in angle, framed views, and the Chinese art of making rock, water, buildings, and planting suggest an entire mountain world inside a city block.
