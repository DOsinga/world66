Suzhou Pingtan Museum introduces the city's own storytelling and ballad tradition, performed in the Suzhou dialect with spare musical accompaniment. It is a useful stop if you want to understand Suzhou as a living cultural city, not only as a set of gardens and canals.

The museum is most rewarding when there is a live performance or demonstration. Even a short visit gives helpful context before hearing pingtan in a teahouse or performance venue.
