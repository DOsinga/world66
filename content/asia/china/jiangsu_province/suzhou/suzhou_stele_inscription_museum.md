Suzhou Stele Inscription Museum is housed in the former Confucian Temple and focuses on carved stone inscriptions, calligraphy, and local history. It is quieter than Suzhou's garden and silk museums, but the collection of ancient steles gives a useful window into the city's scholarly culture.
