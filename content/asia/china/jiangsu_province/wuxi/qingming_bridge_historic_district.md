The Qingming Bridge area is the most substantial remaining section of Wuxi's historic canal architecture — a neighbourhood of whitewashed walls, black-tiled roofs, and narrow stone alleys that line both banks of the ancient Grand Canal. Unlike more heavily restored canal districts elsewhere in Jiangsu, this area retains a degree of authentic disorder: the buildings are not all the same height, the alleys are genuinely narrow, and not every ground-floor space has been converted into a tourist business.

The best way to explore it is on foot in the morning, when local life is most visible — the produce vendors, the tea drinkers, the cats. Several small bridges cross the canal at intervals, each offering a slightly different angle on the same scene. The area is best visited on weekday mornings; weekend afternoons bring enough visitors to crowd the lanes.
