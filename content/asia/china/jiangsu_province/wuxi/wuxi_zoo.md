Wuxi Zoo is a large family-oriented attraction on the western side of the city, combining animal enclosures with amusement-park elements. It is not the first stop for travellers chasing old canals and lakeside scenery, but it is a practical option if you are in Wuxi with children or want a low-pressure day away from temples and museums.

The zoo is best approached honestly: go for an easy local outing rather than a rare wildlife experience. Its travel value comes from the scale of the grounds and the glimpse it gives of how Wuxi families spend a weekend outside the historic centre.
