The Guishan Han Dynasty Tombs hold the burial chambers of Liu Zhu, a ruler of the ancient principality of Chu, and his wife. The site gives Xuzhou a strong imperial-history stop, with underground stone chambers that are more atmospheric than their brief description suggests.
