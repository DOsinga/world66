Xuzhou Aquarium is a large aquarium near Yunlong Lake, with displays of freshwater and marine species from China and beyond. It is a useful family stop and a good indoor option when the weather makes the lakefront less appealing.

Combine it with a walk around Yunlong Lake if you want to make a half-day of this side of the city.
