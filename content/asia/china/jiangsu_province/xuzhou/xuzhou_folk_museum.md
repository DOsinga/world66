Xuzhou Folk Museum occupies historic courtyard houses on Hubu Hill, including the Zhai Family Compound and Yu Family Compound. Exhibits focus on daily life during the Ming and Qing dynasties, and the museum also manages several nearby historic houses that are open to visitors.
