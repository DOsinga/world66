---
title: Eating Out
type: section
---

Yancheng's food is shaped by coast and estuary. The standout local ingredient is Dafeng crab, caught in the coastal shallows and prized for its clean sweetness. Saltwater duck brined in locally harvested sea salt has a leaner quality than the Nanjing version. The city's seafood is reliably fresh and notably cheaper than in the larger cities to the south.
