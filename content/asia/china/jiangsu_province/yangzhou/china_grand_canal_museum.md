The China Grand Canal Museum is a major modern museum for one of China’s defining infrastructure stories. Yangzhou’s fortunes were tied to the Grand Canal for centuries, and the museum uses models, artefacts, reconstructions, and large galleries to explain how that waterway moved grain, officials, merchants, and ideas.

It is especially worthwhile because the subject is not just local history. The canal shaped imperial administration and urban life across eastern China, so this museum gives Yangzhou a national frame and makes a good counterweight to the city’s gardens and old merchant quarters.
