Zhuyuwan Scenic Area spreads across a broad green pocket northeast of central Yangzhou, combining gardens, lake scenery and the city zoo. It is a more spacious, family-oriented outing than Yangzhou's compact classical gardens.

The draw is the mix of paths, water, seasonal flowers and animal exhibits rather than a single must-see monument. It works best for travelers staying long enough in Yangzhou to want an outdoor break beyond Slender West Lake and the old quarter.
