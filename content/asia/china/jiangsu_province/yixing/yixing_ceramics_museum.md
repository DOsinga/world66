The Yixing Ceramics Museum, in the Dingshu area about 20 kilometres south of the main city, is the definitive place to understand the zisha clay tradition. The permanent collection ranges from Song Dynasty proto-teapots through the Ming and Qing masters who defined the form's ideal proportions to contemporary potters working in the same techniques. The examples here include pieces by the most celebrated historical makers — names like Shi Dabin and Chen Mingyuan — alongside explanations of what makes their work exceptional.

The museum is free to enter and well-organised. The shop sells reputable pieces, though the price range is wide and the most serious collectors go directly to the workshops. The surrounding Dingshu district is lined with pottery studios, galleries, and markets where you can watch potters at work. Budget at least half a day if you intend to browse the workshops seriously.
