Nanshan Park gives Zhenjiang a green, hilly counterpoint to its river and temple sights. Paths climb through wooded slopes, pavilions, and scenic spots that are especially welcome when the city feels busy or humid.

It is a proper park outing, not just a pocket of grass. Allow time to wander rather than aiming for a single viewpoint, and combine it with the citys better-known hills if you want a fuller sense of Zhenjiangs landscape.
