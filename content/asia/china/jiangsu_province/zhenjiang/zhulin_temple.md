Zhulin Temple, the "Bamboo Forest Temple", sits in a valley near Zhaoyin Mountain, also known as Bamboo Hill. The surrounding bamboo gives the temple its name and hides it from view until you are close.

It is a calm stop for visitors exploring Zhenjiang's hills and parks, combining a temple visit with a short nature outing.
