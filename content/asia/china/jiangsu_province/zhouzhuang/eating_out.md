Zhouzhuang's food is rooted in Jiangnan freshwater cooking — river fish, lake shrimp, and pork prepared in the sweet-savory style of the region. The signature dish is Wansan pork hock, a whole knuckle braised until the skin collapses into gelatin. Most restaurants are concentrated along the main pedestrian lanes and in the area near the Shen Family Mansion.
