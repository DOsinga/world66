Ganzhou is firmly in Hakka culinary territory, which means robust, warming food built around preserved ingredients, salt, and slow cooking. The flavours here are noticeably more intense than the lighter Cantonese style to the south. Rice noodles are the staple; pork and fresh river fish dominate the protein side. The old town area has clusters of local restaurants, and the morning market near the old bus station is worth visiting for breakfast.
