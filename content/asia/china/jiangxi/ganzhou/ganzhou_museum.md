Ganzhou Museum is a practical introduction to a city with unusually deep Song-dynasty and Hakka layers. Its galleries help connect the old walls, drainage system, and river setting to the wider history of southern Jiangxi.

Use it as context before walking the historic core. The museum is especially worthwhile if you want to understand Ganzhou as more than a stop between better-known Chinese destinations.
