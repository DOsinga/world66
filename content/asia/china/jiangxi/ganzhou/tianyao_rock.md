Tianyao Rock Scenic Area is one of Ganzhou's easiest natural breaks from the city, known for rock formations, green hills, and views over the surrounding landscape. Paths lead through the scenic area to caves, cliffs, and lookouts.

It is a good choice when you want something more active than the old-town sights without leaving Ganzhou for a full day. Go in clear weather if the views are the main draw.
