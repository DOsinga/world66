---

Jingdezhen's restaurant scene has expanded considerably with the influx of young artists, students, and foreign ceramicists. The area around Taoxichuan has the most diverse options, with cafés, ramen shops, hotpot restaurants, and small local places serving Jiangxi-style rice and noodle dishes. The old city area near the Zhushan site has cheaper, more traditional options.
