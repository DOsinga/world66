Jingdezhen has its own high-speed rail station on the Hangzhou–Nanchang High-Speed Railway, with trains to Hangzhou in about 90 minutes and to Nanchang in under an hour. From [Mount Longhu](/asia/china/jiangxi/mount_longhu) (via Yingtan), the journey takes about 90 minutes. Jingdezhen also has a small domestic airport with flights to Beijing, Shanghai, and Guangzhou.

City buses and taxis cover the main destinations within the urban area; shared taxis are the easiest option for Sanbao Village.
