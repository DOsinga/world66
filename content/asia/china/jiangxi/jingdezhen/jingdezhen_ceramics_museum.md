Jingdezhen Ceramics Museum is a key stop in the city most closely associated with
Chinese porcelain. Its displays help turn the name Jingdezhen from a label on ceramics
into a history of kilns, techniques, trade, taste, and imperial demand.

Visit before or after the kiln sites and studio districts, depending on whether you
prefer context first or objects first. Either way, the museum gives structure to a city
where ceramics are everywhere.
