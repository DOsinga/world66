Samryu Temple Old Street is an old riverside street in Jingdezhen, near the meeting of the Changjiang and Xihe rivers. The area grew around small settlements and temple sites, and it gives a quieter look at the city's older urban fabric away from the best-known ceramic museums and markets.

It is a modest heritage walk rather than a single monument, best paired with nearby kiln and porcelain sights.
