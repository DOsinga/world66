Sanbao Village (三宝村) sits in a quiet valley about eight kilometres south of Jingdezhen's city centre, where bamboo and rice paddies surround a scatter of studios, workshops, and galleries converted from old rural buildings. It is quieter and more intimate than Taoxichuan — less urban, with a pace that suits extended exploration rather than a quick walk-through.

International ceramic artists have been based here since the late 1990s, making it one of China's longest-established creative village projects. Some studios are open to visitors and occasional workshops are available. The walk through the valley between studios, with kilns visible in gardens and ceramic shards used decoratively in walls, makes the relationship between the landscape and the craft feel particularly clear. Shared taxis from central Jingdezhen make the journey in about twenty minutes.
