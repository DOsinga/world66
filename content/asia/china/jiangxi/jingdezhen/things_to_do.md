Jingdezhen is one of those cities where the main attraction and the main industry are the same thing. Whether you engage with ceramics at the industrial history level (the Ancient Kiln museum), the contemporary art level (Taoxichuan), or the hands-on level (the many studios offering classes), you will find yourself somewhere interesting. A day covers the essentials; a week is not too long if you are serious about ceramics.
