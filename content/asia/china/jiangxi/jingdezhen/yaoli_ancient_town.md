Yaoli Ancient Town is a historic porcelain and tea town in the forested hills northeast of Jingdezhen. It is known for old Gan-style architecture, narrow lanes, and its long link with the raw materials and craft traditions behind Jingdezhen ceramics.

It works best as a half-day or day trip if you want to see the wider landscape behind the porcelain industry, not just the kilns and museums in the city.
