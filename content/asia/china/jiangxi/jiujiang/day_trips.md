The most obvious day trip from Jiujiang is Lushan, which rises from the plain just south of the city and can be reached in under half an hour by bus. The mountain's hiking, colonial-era villas, and historical associations with both Chiang Kai-shek and Mao Zedong can fill a full day or more; those with more time should consider staying overnight on the mountain.
