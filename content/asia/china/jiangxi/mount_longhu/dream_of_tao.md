Dream of Tao is a large outdoor night performance at Mount Longhu, using the cliffs and water as its stage. The show draws on Taoist imagery, with lighting, music, and choreographed scenes set against the park's natural backdrop.

It is ticketed separately from the main scenic area. Go if you are staying late in Longhu and want a staged evening experience; skip it if you came mainly for the temples, river, and Danxia landscape.
