Elephant Trunk Hill is a Longhu rock formation whose gap and overhang give it the profile of an elephant. It also makes a useful walking point: boardwalks run along the cliffs toward Fairy Pool, while rougher paths continue through quieter rocks and valleys toward Xianshui Rock.
