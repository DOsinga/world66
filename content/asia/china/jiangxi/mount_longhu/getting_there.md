Mount Longhu is most easily reached from Yingtan, which has a high-speed rail station on the Hangzhou–Shenzhen and Shanghai–Kunming lines. Trains from Nanchang to Yingtan take about 30 minutes; from [Jingdezhen](/asia/china/jiangxi/jingdezhen) it is about 90 minutes. From Yingtan Station, local buses and shared taxis reach the mountain entrance in about 30 minutes.

The main entrance area is at Shangqing village; the boat trip and most trails depart from here. The mountain is easily combined with Jingdezhen as a two-day itinerary with a night in the village or in Yingtan.
