Giant Python Rock (巨蟒出山) is Sanqingshan's most distinctive single formation — a 128-metre-tall granite pillar jutting from the cliff face at an acute angle, its tip capped with a gnarled pine in the manner of classical Chinese landscape paintings. The name needs no explanation once you see it: the column's shape, angle, and the pine tree at its crown make the identification immediate.

The formation is visible from the main walking circuit at several points; the best viewing angle is from a fixed observation platform on the opposite cliff face, which puts you at approximately the same elevation as the midpoint of the pillar. Allow some time at the platform — the formation changes character considerably depending on the light, and the addition of cloud or mist in the valley below transforms the scene entirely.
