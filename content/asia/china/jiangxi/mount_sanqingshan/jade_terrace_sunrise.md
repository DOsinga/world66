Jade Terrace (玉台) is the main sunrise viewpoint on Sanqingshan, reached either by staying overnight on the mountain or by arriving before dawn on the ropeway from the West Gate. The terrace itself is a wide platform of stone cut into the mountain's upper ridge, looking east over a complex of peaks and ravines. When the conditions are right — low cloud filling the valleys, peaks emerging above the mist — this is one of the more spectacular mountain views in Jiangxi.

The sunrise experience requires commitment: ropeway starts at 6am (buy tickets in advance), the walk from the cable car to the platform takes 30 minutes, and the cloud conditions needed for the best views cannot be predicted more than the night before. The trade-off is that the mountain is quieter in the early morning, and even on clear days without dramatic cloud effects, the light on the granite formations is best in the hour after sunrise.
