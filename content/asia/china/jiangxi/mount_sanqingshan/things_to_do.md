Sanqingshan's trail system is divided into several circuits of varying lengths, all connected by the same iron-railing-and-concrete-path infrastructure that clings to the cliff faces. The South Gate circuit covers the most dramatic formations and the best Sanqinggong Temple access; the West Gate circuit offers the highest viewpoints and the sunrise platform. Most visitors come for a single day, but the mountain rewards two days if you want to cover the full network without rushing.
