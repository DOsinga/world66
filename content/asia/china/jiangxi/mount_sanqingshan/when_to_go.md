---
title: When to Go
type: section
tags: [when_to_go]
---

Sanqingshan is a mountain for all seasons, but each offers a different experience. Late April and May bring the rhododendron bloom — the most popular and most crowded period. Summer is warm, often misty, and occasionally prone to heavy afternoon thunderstorms that can close exposed sections of trail. Autumn (October to November) offers clear skies, autumn colour on the deciduous trees, and thinner crowds than spring. Winter brings snow and ice that transform the granite formations dramatically, though some paths are closed for safety and access is more restricted.

The mountain is best visited on weekdays regardless of season — weekend crowds are substantial, particularly on the most scenic circuits. Early morning access (taking the first ropeway at 6am) is the most reliably good strategy for seeing the mountain in good light with fewer people.
