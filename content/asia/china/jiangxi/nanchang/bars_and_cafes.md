---
title: Bars and Cafes
type: section
---

Nanchang's nightlife clusters around the university districts in the west and the riverside developments near the Pavilion of Prince Teng. The Yanjiang Road strip along the Gan River has bars and outdoor seating with views of the nightly LED light show projected onto the west bank towers. Younger crowds gravitate toward the Pedestrian Street area in Donghu district, where cafes and bubble tea shops shade into cocktail bars after dark.
