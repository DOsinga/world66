Nanchang makes a good base for the region. [Poyang Lake](/asia/china/jiangxi/poyang_lake), China's largest freshwater lake and an outstanding winter birdwatching destination, is about 80 kilometers north and easily reached by bus or car. [Lushan](/asia/china/jiangxi/lushan), the ancient mountain resort with views over the Yangtze plain and a collection of colonial-era villas, is reachable in around two hours. [Jingdezhen](/asia/china/jiangxi/jingdezhen), the porcelain capital with excellent museums and active kilns, is about two and a half hours by high-speed train.
