---
title: Shopping
type: section
---

Nanchang's best shopping is for regional specialties: Jingdezhen porcelain at the wholesale markets, Jiangxi green tea, and dried chilli products. The central Pedestrian Street in Donghu offers standard Chinese mall retail, while the Shengjin Tower area and Wanshou Palace Street have more locally distinctive options. See the Nanchang Porcelain Market and Nanchang Shopping Districts entries for specifics.
