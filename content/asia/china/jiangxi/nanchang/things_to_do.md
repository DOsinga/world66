title: Things to Do
type: section
---

Nanchang's sights divide neatly between its revolutionary heritage and its classical Chinese landmarks. The August 1st Uprising Memorial Museum and Bayi Square form the ceremonial spine of the modern city, while the Pavilion of Prince Teng on the Gan River represents its deep pre-revolutionary past. Honghu Lake and the parks scattered across the city offer relief on humid summer afternoons.
