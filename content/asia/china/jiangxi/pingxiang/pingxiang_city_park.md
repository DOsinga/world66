Pingxiang's main urban cultural park provides the standard amenities of a Chinese provincial city green space — lake, walking paths, pavilions, outdoor exercise equipment — along with a small museum covering the city's industrial heritage and its connection to the regional revolutionary history. The park is pleasant rather than remarkable, and its main value is as a orientation point for understanding how Pingxiang sees itself in the context of its coal-industry past and post-industrial present.

The surrounding urban area has been significantly redeveloped as the city's economy transitions away from heavy industry, and the park is part of a broader effort to rebrand Pingxiang as an environmental and outdoor tourism destination rather than an industrial center.
