---
title: Getting There
type: section
---

Ruijin is on the Ganzhou-Shenzhen high-speed rail line, making it easily accessible from Ganzhou (about 30 minutes) and Shenzhen (around 2.5 hours). From [Nanchang](/asia/china/jiangxi/nanchang), the journey requires changing trains at Ganzhou; the total journey is around three hours. There is no direct service to Ruijin from Shanghai or eastern cities without a connection.

By bus from Ganzhou, the trip takes about two hours on the expressway. Within Ruijin, taxis and local buses reach the main sites; the Yeping complex is about 7 kilometers northwest of the city center and is best reached by taxi.
