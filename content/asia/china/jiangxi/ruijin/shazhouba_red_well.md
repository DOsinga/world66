Shazhouba Red Well is one of Ruijin's core revolutionary-history stops, connected to the period when the Chinese Soviet Republic was based in the area. The well is remembered in Communist Party history as a practical project associated with Mao Zedong and the local villagers, and it has become a symbol of the "serve the people" story told across Ruijin's red-tourism sites.

The site is modest if you judge it only as a well, but it makes sense in combination with Yeping and the Ruijin Revolutionary History Museum. This is a place where the political narrative matters as much as the object itself. Visit it as part of a circuit through the Chinese Soviet Republic sites rather than as a standalone scenic attraction.
