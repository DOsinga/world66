---
title: Things to Do
type: section
---

Wuyuan's main draw is the cluster of ancient Huizhou-style villages scattered through its valleys. Each village has a distinct character: Likeng is most accessible, Sixi Yancun is quieter, Xiaoqi is the most unspoiled. Beyond the villages, Wuyuan's tea fields and forested hills reward those willing to venture off the main tourist routes. Spring rapeseed blossom season transforms the valley floors into a sea of yellow — beautiful but accompanied by large tour crowds.
