Momoge National Nature Reserve protects a large wetland landscape of swamps, islands, rivers, and grassland near Baicheng. It is best known for birdlife, including cranes during the spring and autumn migrations.

The reserve is most rewarding for patient visitors with binoculars and time to look across the wetlands rather than for a quick city stop.
