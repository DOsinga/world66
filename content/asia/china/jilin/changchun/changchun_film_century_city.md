Changchun Film Century City is a large film-themed amusement park run by the Changchun Film Group Corporation. It mixes rides, studio-style sets, and film effects attractions, making it one of the city's more family-oriented stops.

It is outside the central city, so plan it as a half-day outing rather than something to squeeze between downtown museums. It works best for travellers interested in Changchun's film industry or visiting with children.
