Chunyi Road is Changchun's main upscale commercial street, lined with department stores, boutiques, restaurants, and the flagship outlets of Chinese and international brands. The street functions as the city's shopping spine and social parade ground — the place where Changchun's residents come to see and be seen.

The architecture along the street mixes Manchukuo-era buildings with modern commercial construction, and some of the older facades are worth looking at above the ground-floor shop fronts. The street is crowded on weekends and the energy is representative of contemporary Chinese commercial city life: noisy, colourful, purposeful. For visitors, it provides an hour's window into how a northeastern Chinese city's residents actually spend their leisure time and money.
