The most obvious day trip from Changchun is Jilin City, forty minutes east by high-speed train — the Songhua River, the Confucian Temple, and the Jilin City Museum make a comfortable day. Changbaishan is reachable in a very long day with an early start, though staying overnight in Baihe is more comfortable if you want time to see the summit properly.
