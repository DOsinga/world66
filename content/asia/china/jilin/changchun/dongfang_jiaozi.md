Dongfang Jiaozi Wang — "East Dumpling King" — is one of Changchun's most reliable and well-known dumpling restaurants, representing the northeastern Chinese dumpling tradition at its best. The dumplings here are large, the skins substantial, and the fillings built around pork, lamb, and seafood combinations that reflect the region's cooking traditions.

Northeastern dumplings differ from their southern counterparts in scale and filling density — these are eating food, not refined bites. The standard procedure is to order by quantity (usually by liang, or 50-gram portions), add vinegar and chilli oil from the condiment station, and work through them with the house soup alongside. The restaurant is busy at most meal times and the turnover is fast.
