Jilin Province Arts and Crafts Museum adds a craft-focused stop to Changchun's museum landscape. It highlights decorative traditions, regional workmanship, and contemporary applied arts rather than the political history for which the city is often known.

It is a secondary museum, but useful for travellers interested in material culture and local making. The visit is best kept flexible, since exhibitions and displays may change.
