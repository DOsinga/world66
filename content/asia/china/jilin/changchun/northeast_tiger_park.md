The Northeast Tiger Park, also called the Siberian Tiger Park, houses over 100 Amur (Siberian) tigers in a large fenced facility on Changchun's northern edge. It is one of the largest captive tiger facilities in the world and attracts significant visitor numbers — including many Chinese tourists who come specifically to see the animals at close range from buses that drive through the enclosures.

The experience is powerful in a complicated way. Seeing a Siberian tiger up close — a genuinely large, powerful animal whose wild population is critically small — generates the same mixture of awe and unease that captive wildlife always produces. The facilities here have been controversial: conditions, feeding practices, and the balance between conservation value and entertainment have all been questioned. Research the current situation before visiting, as standards and practices change over time. The park does participate in breeding programs that contribute to species preservation, but whether the visitor experience is compatible with genuine wildlife values is a question you will need to answer for yourself.
