Beishan Park on the western edge of Jilin City is the city's most atmospheric park — a hilly landscape with active temple complexes, seasonal lotus displays, and in winter a significant contribution to the ice and snow scenery that defines the Jilin cold season. The park covers several hills with Taoist and Buddhist temples distributed across them, connected by paths and steps that make for a varied walk.

The temples are genuinely active: incense is burning, monks and nuns are present, and the religious year brings regular ceremonies and festivals. The spring temple fair is the most significant event, a multi-day gathering that draws pilgrims and visitors from across the province and has a centuries-long tradition. Autumn brings chrysanthemum displays that are another cultural fixture.

In winter, the park's frozen ponds and snow-covered paths add to the general ice scenery of the Jilin winter. The combination of religious sites, seasonal gardens, and winter landscape makes Beishan one of the most complete urban parks in northeast China.
