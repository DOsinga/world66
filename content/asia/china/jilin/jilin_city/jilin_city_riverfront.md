The Songhua River embankment walkway is the social heart of Jilin City — a paved riverside promenade that stretches for several kilometres along the city's riverfront and functions as the city's living room in both summer and winter. In summer, families stroll, vendors operate, and boats depart for lake excursions. In winter, it is the front-row seat for the rime ice spectacle that defines the city's international identity.

The embankment is at its most active in the early morning, when photographers set up for the dawn light on the rime ice, elderly residents do group exercise, and the river sends steam into the winter air. An early morning walk here in December or January is the single experience most specific to Jilin City — nowhere else in China looks or feels quite like this. In the other seasons, the embankment is a pleasant urban waterfront with the particular character of a northeastern Chinese city that has built its identity around its river.
