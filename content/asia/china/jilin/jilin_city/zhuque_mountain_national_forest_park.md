Zhuque Mountain National Forest Park is a forested mountain area south of Jilin City, with hiking trails, temples, and seasonal outdoor activities. It is one of the city's easier nature escapes when you want hills and fresh air without leaving the region.

The park also has food and basic visitor facilities, so it can work as a half-day outing.
