Mengdumei Ski Resort is the main winter-sports outing for Yanji, set in the hills outside the city rather than in the urban centre. It is not a destination ski resort in the Alpine sense, but it gives travellers in Yanbian a practical way to turn the long northeastern winter into an activity day.

The slopes suit beginners and casual skiers more than experts, with equipment rental and day-use facilities aimed at local families and weekend visitors. Conditions depend heavily on the season, so check recent snow and opening information before making the trip. In winter it pairs well with Yanji's Korean food scene after a cold day outside.
