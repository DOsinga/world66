The Yanji Dinosaur Museum opened in 2021 following the discovery of fossils during urban construction work in 2016. The exhibits are recent and well-presented, with full skeletons and detailed explanations of the finds in the Yanbian region. The museum is small compared to the major natural history collections in Beijing or Shenyang, but the recency of both the discovery and the facility gives it an immediacy that older collections sometimes lack.

For families or for anyone with an interest in palaeontology, it is a good stop alongside the city's cultural sites. The museum also provides some geological context for the wider Changbai mountain region, which has unusual volcanic and tectonic history that helps explain why fossils turn up here at all.
