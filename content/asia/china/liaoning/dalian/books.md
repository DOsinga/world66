**Lonely Planet China** — The standard practical guide covers Dalian and the Liaoning coast in its northeast China chapter, with current transport and accommodation information.

**The Great Game** by Peter Hopkirk — Background on the Russian imperial expansion into northeast Asia that shaped Dalian's history: the Russian lease of the Liaodong Peninsula, the construction of Port Arthur, and the eventual confrontation with Japan.

**Manchuria: Its People, Resources and Recent History** by Alexander Hosie — A detailed early 20th-century account of the region Dalian served as a gateway to, capturing the period when Russian and Japanese interests collided here.

**Waiting** by Ha Jin — Booker Prize-winning novel set in a northeast China military hospital over several decades. Not set in Dalian specifically, but captures the character of the region — its restraint, its winters, its particular mix of resignation and endurance — better than almost any other work of fiction.
