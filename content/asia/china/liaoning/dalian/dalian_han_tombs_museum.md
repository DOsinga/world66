Dalian Han Tombs Museum displays objects excavated from Han dynasty tombs in the surrounding area. The museum gives useful context for a part of Dalian's history that is easy to miss among the city's better-known port, beach, and Russian-Japanese heritage sights.

It is a worthwhile stop for visitors with an interest in archaeology or early imperial China, especially when exploring the Ganjingzi side of the city.
