The streets around Zhongshan Square and the central business district have a dense concentration of restaurants covering both northeast Chinese cooking and Dalian's coastal specialities. Look for jiaohuaji (beggar's chicken, slow-cooked in clay), scallop dishes, and sea urchin roe on rice. Korean and Japanese restaurants are common throughout the centre, reflecting the city's close historical and commercial ties with both countries.
