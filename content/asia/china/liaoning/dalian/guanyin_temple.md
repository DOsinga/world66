Guanyin Temple is a Ming-dynasty Buddhist temple in Jinzhou, north of central Dalian. It is one of the better-known temples in southern Liaoning and becomes especially busy during Spring Festival and temple celebrations.

The temple is outside the central Dalian sightseeing cluster, so it makes most sense if you are already visiting Jinzhou or want a more local religious site away from the downtown squares.
