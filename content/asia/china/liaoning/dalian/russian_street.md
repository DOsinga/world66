Russian Street is one of the easier places to read Dalian's treaty-port past in the present-day city. Some of the façades are modern reconstructions and the souvenir stalls lean touristy, but a walk here still gives a useful feel for the Russian and Japanese layers that shaped old Dalian.

The best reason to come is not the shopping. Use the main street as a starting point, then wander the side streets where a few more convincing old buildings survive.
