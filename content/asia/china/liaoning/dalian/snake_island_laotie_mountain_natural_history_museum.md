The Snake Island & Laotie Mountain Natural History Museum explains the flora, fauna, and protected landscapes of the Snake Island and Laotie Mountain Nature Reserve near Lushunkou. It is most useful as context before visiting the reserve area or for travellers interested in the region's unusual wildlife.
