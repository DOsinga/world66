Sun Asia Ocean World is a large aquarium complex near Xinghai Bay, with marine tanks, polar animals, and family-focused shows. It is separate from Tiger Beach Ocean Park, Dalian's other big marine attraction.

The naming can be confusing, as Sun Asia may refer to one section or the wider complex. Check the ticket options before buying, especially if you only want the aquarium rather than the full set of attractions.
