Historic coloured trams still running through the city centre, a legacy of the Russian colonial period and one of the few surviving urban tram systems in China. Three lines operate with a flat fare, connecting the main squares and colonial-era streets. Slow enough to watch the city at a human pace. Hop on and off around the colonial district rather than taking taxis — it is part of the Dalian experience.
