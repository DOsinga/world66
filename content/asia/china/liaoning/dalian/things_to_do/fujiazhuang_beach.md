title: "Fujiazhuang Beach"
type: poi
latitude: 38.857
longitude: 121.593
---

The best swimming beach in Dalian, on the southern coast between Baiyunshan and Xiuyuefeng parks. Clean sand, organised facilities, and better water quality than the more tourist-focused Tiger Beach. Packed in July and August with domestic tourists, but pleasant in June and September when the weather is still warm and the crowds thin. About 30 minutes from the city centre by bus or taxi.
