---
title: "Zhongshan Square"
type: poi
latitude: 38.913
longitude: 121.644
---

The centrepiece of Dalian's colonial urban planning: a large circular boulevard with ten symmetrical Beaux-Arts buildings radiating outward from the centre. The tram lines converge here, and in summer evenings hundreds of people gather to practise ballroom dancing. Best seen at dawn or dusk when the light catches the facades. Originally laid out during the Japanese occupation as Ōhiroba (Great Square).
