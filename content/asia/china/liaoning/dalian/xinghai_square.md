title: Xinghai Square
type: poi
---

One of the world's largest public squares at 1.1 million square metres, set on the oceanfront overlooking Xinghai Bay. The views across the bay are wide and open — unusual for a city this size. Popular with locals for morning tai chi, kite flying, and evening walks. The scale is genuinely startling. The adjacent Xinghai Park and convention centre make this the civic heart of the southwestern city.
