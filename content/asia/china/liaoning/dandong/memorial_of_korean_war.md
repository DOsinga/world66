The Memorial of the War to Resist US Aggression and Aid Korea is China's only national museum dedicated to the Korean War, and it presents the conflict from a perspective rarely encountered in Western travel. The collection spans three floors and includes personal effects of soldiers, command maps, captured military equipment, and documentary photographs. The roster of Chinese casualties — listed by name on memorial walls — gives the scale of the intervention a weight that statistics alone cannot convey.

Outside the museum building, a courtyard displays hardware from the conflict: MiG-15 fighters, Soviet-made artillery pieces, and a captured American tank. The exhibits inside are in Chinese with limited English labelling, but the documentary photographs and physical artefacts communicate effectively regardless of language. Admission is free with a passport. The museum is about 1.5 kilometres from the Broken Bridge, walkable along the riverfront.
