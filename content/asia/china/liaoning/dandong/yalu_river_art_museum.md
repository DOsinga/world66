Yalu River Art Museum is a small art museum in Dandong that focuses on one exhibition at a time, changing shows only once a year. It is a useful cultural stop for visitors already spending time along the Yalu River, especially when the current exhibition matches your interests.

Winter and early-spring visits may require advance booking, so check the museum's current instructions before going.
