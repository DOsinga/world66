Wang Erlie Memorial Hall tells the story of Wang Erlie, a Liaoyang-born Qing dynasty official who rose through the imperial examination system. Exhibits cover his life, career, and local legacy, with some English translations.

It is a useful small museum for travelers already exploring Liaoyang's historic core.
