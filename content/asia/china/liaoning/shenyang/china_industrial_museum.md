The Industrial Museum of China belongs in Shenyang because the city was one of the heavy-industrial engines of northeast China. The museum occupies the Tiexi district, where factories, machine tools, foundries, and worker culture shaped much of modern Shenyang's identity.

This is a strong stop if you are interested in twentieth-century China beyond palaces and imperial tombs. Expect large machinery, industrial displays, and a story about how the old manufacturing base became both a source of pride and a problem to reinvent.
