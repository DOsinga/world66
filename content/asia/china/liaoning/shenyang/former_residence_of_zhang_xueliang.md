The Former Residence of Zhang Xueliang is one of Shenyang's important modern-history sites. Zhang, the Manchurian warlord known as the Young Marshal, played a decisive role in the politics of Republican China and the famous Xi'an Incident.

The residence complex gives that history a physical setting through courtyards, rooms, and displays rather than abstract dates. It is a strong stop for travelers who want Shenyang beyond imperial palaces and industrial memory.
