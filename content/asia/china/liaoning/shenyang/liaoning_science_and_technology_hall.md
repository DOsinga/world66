Liaoning Science and Technology Hall is a large modern science museum in Shenyang's Hunnan district, with interactive exhibits aimed at making physics, technology, and natural science accessible. It is away from the older imperial core, but it broadens the city's museum offering beyond palaces, tombs, and war history.

The museum is most useful for families, science-minded travelers, or anyone spending time in southern Shenyang. Because it sits well outside the central sightseeing cluster, check transport time before adding it to a short visit.
