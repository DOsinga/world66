The Paleontological Museum of Liaoning is one of Shenyang's stronger specialist museums because the province's fossil record is internationally important. Liaoning's feathered dinosaurs, early birds, and other Mesozoic finds changed how scientists think about dinosaur evolution.

The museum is best for travellers with an interest in natural history, families with children, or anyone wanting a break from imperial and modern history sights. Labels may vary in English depth, but the fossils carry the story clearly.
