Sacred Heart of Jesus Cathedral is Shenyang's main Catholic cathedral, a twin-spired brick landmark that stands out from the surrounding commercial streets. Its European Gothic Revival form is a reminder of the city's international and missionary-era layers.

The cathedral is a worthwhile architectural stop even for visitors who are not seeking out churches. It is close enough to central Shenyang sightseeing to fold into a broader walk, and its scale makes it one of the city's more visible religious buildings.
