Shenyang Horticultural Exposition Garden is a vast landscaped park created from the city's international garden expo grounds. It offers themed gardens, lakes, bridges, pavilions, and long walks that show a softer side of Shenyang than palaces, tombs, and heavy industry.

Its scale means you should not try to see every corner. Pick a season when flowers or autumn colour are doing some work, wear comfortable shoes, and treat it as a half-day escape from the dense city.
