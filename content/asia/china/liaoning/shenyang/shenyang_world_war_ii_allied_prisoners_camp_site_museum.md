This museum preserves the site of a wartime prisoner-of-war camp in Shenyang, where Allied soldiers and civilians were held under Japanese occupation. It is a harder visit than the imperial palace or the Qing tombs, but it fills in a part of the city's twentieth-century history that is easy to miss.

Expect a memorial tone rather than spectacle. The exhibits are most meaningful if you already have some context for the Pacific War and Manchuria's occupation history; even without that, the preserved site gives the story a physical weight that ordinary museum cases often lack.
