Taiqing Palace is a Taoist temple complex in Shenyang, offering a quieter religious counterpoint to the city's imperial palace and war-history museums. Its halls, courtyards, and incense give a sense of older urban spiritual life that survives among the modern streets.

It is not the city's single essential sight, but it belongs in a rounded Shenyang itinerary. Visit slowly, step aside during worship, and pair it with nearby central sights rather than making it a standalone excursion.
