Avenida da República is a tree-lined road around the southern end of the Macau Peninsula, known for Portuguese-era residences and views across Nam Van and Sai Van lakes. It shows a quieter, more residential side of Macau's colonial architecture.

The avenue is best treated as a walking detour near A-Ma Temple and the waterfront rather than a standalone sight. Its appeal is the combination of old houses, lake views, and the curve of the road around the peninsula.
