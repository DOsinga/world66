Cemetério de São Miguel Arcanjo is a small Catholic cemetery that shows Macau's Portuguese side in a quiet, intimate way. Angels, tiled details, and weathered family tombs make it a worthwhile pause if you enjoy old cemeteries and colonial-era traces.

It is not a headline sight, but it pairs well with a slow walk through the historic peninsula.
