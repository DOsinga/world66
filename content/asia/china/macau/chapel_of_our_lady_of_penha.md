The Chapel of Our Lady of Penha stands on a steep hill at the southern end of Macau Peninsula. The chapel is small, but the hill is one of the best places to look over central Macau, Nam Van and Sai Van lakes, Macau Tower, the bridges to Taipa, and the Pearl River.

It is a good stop when you want a quieter view above the old city and do not mind the climb or taxi ride up.
