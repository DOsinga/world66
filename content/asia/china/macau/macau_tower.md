Macau Tower rises 338 metres above the southern tip of the peninsula and is the most visible landmark on the city skyline. The observation deck on the 58th floor offers a 360-degree view that takes in the bridges to Taipa, the Cotai Strip, and on a clear day the mountains of Guangdong province. On the outdoor walkway one level below, you can walk around the outside of the tower with nothing but a harness between you and the harbour far below.

The real draw for thrill-seekers is the bungee jump from the external walkway at 233 metres — the world's highest commercial jump — plus a skywalk and various other adrenaline experiences. For calmer visitors, there are two revolving restaurants and a shopping mall at the base. The tower is a 10-minute taxi ride or bus ride from the old city centre.
