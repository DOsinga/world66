Mandarin House is a large 19th-century Chinese residential compound associated with the reformist thinker Zheng Guanying. Its courtyards, timber screens, tiled roofs, and layered family spaces show a side of old Macau that is quieter than the nearby Portuguese churches and squares.

The house is a strong stop for anyone following Macau's UNESCO-listed historic centre beyond the most crowded landmarks.
