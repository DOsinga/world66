St. Augustine's Square is a compact heritage corner of old Macau, framed by St. Augustine's Church, Dom Pedro V Theatre, St. Joseph's Seminary and Church, and the Robert Ho Tung Library. It works well as a short walking stop between the more famous sights of the peninsula.
