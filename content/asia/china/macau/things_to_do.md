---
title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Macau's UNESCO-listed historic centre is compact enough to walk across in an afternoon. The must-sees are the [Ruins of São Paulo](/asia/china/macau/ruins_of_sao_paulo), [Monte Fort](/asia/china/macau/monte_fort) (which also houses the [Macau Museum](/asia/china/macau/macau_museum)), [Senado Square](/asia/china/macau/senado_square), [A-Ma Temple](/asia/china/macau/a_ma_temple), and [St Dominic's Church](/asia/china/macau/st_dominics_church). The [Guia Fortress](/asia/china/macau/guia_fortress) on the peninsula's highest hill has the best views in Macau and the oldest Western-built lighthouse on the Chinese coast. For the other Macau, the [Cotai Strip](/asia/china/macau/cotai_strip) with the [Venetian Macao](/asia/china/macau/venetian_macao) at its heart is unmissable for the spectacle alone. [Taipa Village](/asia/china/macau/taipa_village) and [Coloane Village](/asia/china/macau/coloane_village) give a quieter, more local side of the territory. The [Macau Tower](/asia/china/macau/macau_tower) offers the world's highest commercial bungee jump for those inclined.
