The Wine Museum and Grand Prix Museum share a building north of Macau's centre, pairing two very different parts of the city's Portuguese and modern identity. The wine side explains Portuguese wine regions and traditions, while the Grand Prix exhibits focus on Macau's street-circuit racing history.

It is a useful rainy-day stop rather than a reason to cross town on its own. Motorsport fans will get the most from the Grand Prix displays.
