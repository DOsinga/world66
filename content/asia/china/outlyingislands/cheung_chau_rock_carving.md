The Cheung Chau Rock Carving is a small prehistoric carving cut into the rock near the island's shore. Its exact age is uncertain, but the patterns are usually compared with Bronze Age decoration found elsewhere in the region.

Do not expect a large site. The appeal is in finding a rare trace of Hong Kong's much older human landscape while walking around an island better known for ferries, seafood, beaches, and the Bun Festival.
