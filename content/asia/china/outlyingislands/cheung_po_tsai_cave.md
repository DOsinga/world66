Cheung Po Tsai Cave is a small cave on Cheung Chau linked by legend to the 19th-century pirate Cheung Po Tsai. Local stories say he hid treasure here, though none has ever been found.

The cave is more atmospheric than grand, but it makes a good goal for a short walk around the island's quieter coast.
