Finger Hill is Peng Chau's best short walk, rising above the island with open views toward Lantau, Hong Kong Disneyland, and the Tsing Ma Bridge. The climb is brief but steep enough to feel like a proper outing, and the lookout gives a clear sense of how the small outlying island sits between the busier parts of Hong Kong.
