Hung Shing Temple is a small Cheung Chau temple first built in 1813 and repaired several times since. It is a modest but worthwhile stop for travellers walking the island's village lanes and waterfront paths.

Pair it with Cheung Chau's other temples, beaches, and old rock carvings rather than crossing from Hong Kong Island for this temple alone.
