Hung Shing Ye Beach is one of Lamma Island's easiest beach stops, close enough to Yung Shue Wan to reach on foot after the ferry. The sand and water are pleasant, though the nearby power station keeps the view from being postcard-perfect.

It works well as part of a relaxed Lamma walk: swim, have a snack, then continue along the island trail. On weekends it can be busy, but it still feels far removed from central Hong Kong.
