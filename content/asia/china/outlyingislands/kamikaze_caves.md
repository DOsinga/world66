The Kamikaze Caves near Sok Kwu Wan on Lamma Island were dug by Japanese forces during World War II to shelter speedboats intended for surprise attacks. The caves are simple, but they add a sharp historical note to the island's walking routes.

They are worth a short look if you are already hiking around Sok Kwu Wan, rather than a trip on their own.
