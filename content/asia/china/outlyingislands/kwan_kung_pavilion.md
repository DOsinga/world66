Kwan Kung Pavilion is a small hilltop temple on Cheung Chau, dedicated to the general Guan Yu, also known as Kwan Kung. It is a modest stop rather than a major sight, but the climb gives a different angle on the island beyond the harbour, seafood lanes, and beaches.
