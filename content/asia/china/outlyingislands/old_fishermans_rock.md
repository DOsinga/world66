Old Fisherman's Rock is a small coastal rock formation off the northeast side of Peng Chau. It sits on a sandy islet and can be reached across a shallow sandbar when conditions allow.

It is a modest stop rather than a major sight, but it gives a good reason to walk beyond the village lanes and see the quieter shoreline of Peng Chau. Check the tide before crossing, and do not attempt it when the sandbar is covered.
