Sai Wan Tin Hau Temple is a small sea-facing temple on Cheung Chau, dedicated to Tin Hau, the goddess traditionally honoured by fishermen and coastal communities. It is modest, but the setting gives it more presence than its size suggests.

The temple works best as part of a walk around the island rather than as a destination on its own. Stop for the sea views, the incense, and the reminder that Cheung Chau was a fishing community long before it became a weekend escape.
