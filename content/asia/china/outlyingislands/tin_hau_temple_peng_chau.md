Peng Chau's Tin Hau Temple dates from 1792 and is dedicated to the sea goddess long venerated by fishing communities around Hong Kong. Inside is a whale rib, while outside a stone monument warns of the pirates who once troubled these waters.

It is a small stop, but it fits naturally into a slow walk around Peng Chau village and helps explain the island's maritime past.
