Yuk Hui Temple is one of Cheung Chau's best-known temples and the focus of the island's annual Bun Festival. The temple is dedicated to Pak Tai, the Taoist deity traditionally associated with protection from plague and danger at sea.

The building is small but richly detailed, with incense coils, carved roof figures, and festival decorations that make it a natural stop while walking between the ferry pier, the village lanes, and Tung Wan Beach.
