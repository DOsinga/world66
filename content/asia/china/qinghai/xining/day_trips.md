Xining is the main hub for exploring Qinghai Province. The most popular day trip is Qinghai Lake (two hours by car), and many visitors combine this with a stop at the Chaka Salt Lake (two hours further west). Kumbum Monastery in Huangzhong is 25 kilometres south and easily reached for a half-day. Tongren (Rebkong) is about four hours and is better treated as an overnight, though it can be done as a long day in a hired car.
