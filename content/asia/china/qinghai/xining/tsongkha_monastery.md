Tsongkha Monastery is a small Tibetan Buddhist monastery in Xining with more historical weight than its size suggests. It is associated with the 10th Panchen Lama's 1951 meeting with Xi Zhongxun, then a senior official in northwest China.

The monastery is a short stop for travelers interested in Xining's Tibetan Buddhist connections.
