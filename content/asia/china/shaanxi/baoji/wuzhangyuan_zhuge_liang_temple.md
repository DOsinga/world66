Wuzhangyuan Zhuge Liang Temple marks a landscape strongly associated with the final northern campaign of Zhuge Liang, the strategist whose reputation dominates Three Kingdoms memory. The temple and surrounding plain are most meaningful if you know the history, but even casual visitors will understand why the site became a place of commemoration.

It is outside central Baoji, so treat it as a targeted historical excursion rather than a casual city stop. Pairing it with other western Shaanxi sights makes the travel time easier to justify.
