---
title: When to Go
type: section
---

Hanzhong has a humid subtropical climate, significantly warmer and wetter than the rest of Shaanxi. Winters are mild by Chinese standards — rarely dropping below freezing in the valley floor — and summers are hot and humid. The Qinling Mountains to the north block cold winter winds and trap monsoon moisture.

**Spring (March–April)** is the peak tourist season, when the rapeseed fields are in bloom across the Han River valley. Accommodation fills up on weekends during this period and prices rise. Book ahead.

**Autumn (September–November)** is the other good season: cooler temperatures, clear skies, and the trees of the Qinling slopes changing colour. The Three Kingdoms sites are at their most atmospheric.

**Summer (June–August)** is hot, humid, and often rainy. The mountain scenery remains attractive but the valley heat can be enervating. **Winter (December–February)** is the quietest and cheapest time; the historical sites are largely uncrowded and the climate is tolerable, though some mountain roads may close after snowfall.
