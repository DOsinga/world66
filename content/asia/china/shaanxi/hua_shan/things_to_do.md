---
title: Things to Do
type: section
---

Hua Shan offers five peaks connected by paths ranging from strenuous to genuinely terrifying. The standard approach is the North Trail on foot or by cable car, then a traverse of the ridge. Key sites include the Changkong Zhandao plank walk, the East Peak sunrise platform, and several Taoist temples. Most serious walkers take two days and sleep on the mountain.
