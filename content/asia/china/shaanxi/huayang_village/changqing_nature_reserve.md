Changqing National Nature Reserve occupies a large stretch of forested mountain terrain in the Qinling range, about 30 minutes by road from Huayang village. It was established partly to protect the rare Qinling giant panda — a subspecies found only in these mountains — along with golden snub-nosed monkeys, golden takin (a large bovid with a golden coat), and the crested ibis, one of China's most endangered birds.

The reserve is not a tourist park with managed viewing areas. Wildlife encounters depend on luck, season, and how far into the forest you go. Spring and autumn are the best times for panda sightings when the animals move to lower elevations. The reserve accepts a limited number of visitors and arranges guided walks with staff. Entry must be arranged in advance through the reserve administration or through the hotel in Huayang village, which can also help organise transport.
