---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

The best time to visit Huayang is **spring (April–May)** or **autumn (September–October)**. These months bring comfortable temperatures, clear mountain air, and the best chances of seeing wildlife in the lower forest zones — pandas descend to lower elevations when bamboo is most accessible in spring.

**Summer** is warm and can be wet. The forest is at its most lush and green, but leeches are a nuisance on forest paths and cloud cover is frequent. **Winter** brings snow to the mountains and the village has the feel of a place largely cut off from the outside world. The reserve's activities are limited and road access can be difficult after heavy snowfall.

October is arguably the best single month — the forest colour is spectacular, the weather is dry, and the wildlife is active before winter. Chinese national holiday (first week of October) brings more visitors than usual; the week before or after is quieter.
