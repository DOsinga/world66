Shenmu Museum is a local-history anchor for a county-level city with deep northern Shaanxi roots. Its displays help connect the area's archaeology, frontier geography, and regional culture to a place many travellers otherwise pass through quickly.

This is a secondary POI, but a useful one for visitors already in Shenmu. It provides context before exploring temples, old-town traces, or the wider landscapes near the Ordos edge.
