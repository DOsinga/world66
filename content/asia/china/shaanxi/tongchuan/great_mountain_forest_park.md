Great Mountain Forest Park is a forested hiking area near Tongchuan, with trails, viewpoints and camping areas. Spring and autumn are the best times to visit, when the trees and mountain views are at their most attractive.

It is a straightforward nature stop for visitors with time outside the city centre, especially if you want a walk rather than another temple or museum.
