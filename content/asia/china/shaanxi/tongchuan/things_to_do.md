title: Things to Do
type: section
---

Tongchuan's two main attractions are complementary: Yaowang Mountain combines Tang Dynasty religious history with fine views over the plateau, while the Yaozhou Kiln Museum anchors a millennium-long ceramic tradition in its original landscape. Chenlu Ancient Town adds a living craft village to the itinerary. The combination makes Tongchuan more than a day trip from Xi'an.
