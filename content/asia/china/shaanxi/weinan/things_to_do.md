Weinan prefecture's real draw is its archaeology and landscapes. [Mount Hua](/asia/china/shaanxi/weinan/mount_hua) is the unmissable destination — one of China's five sacred mountains and among the most dramatic in the country. The [Qianling Mausoleum](/asia/china/shaanxi/weinan/qianling_mausoleum) and [Zhaoling Mausoleum](/asia/china/shaanxi/weinan/zhaoling_mausoleum) represent the Tang imperial tombs at their most accessible, while [Dangjia Village](/asia/china/shaanxi/weinan/dangjia_village) in Hancheng preserves the most complete courtyard-house streetscape in northern China. The [Sima Qian Temple and Tomb](/asia/china/shaanxi/weinan/sima_qian_temple) outside Hancheng commemorates China's greatest historian with a setting as dramatic as his prose.
