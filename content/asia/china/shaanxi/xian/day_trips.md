---
title: Day Trips
type: section
---

Most of Xi'an's biggest sights are technically day trips — the [Terracotta Warriors](/asia/china/shaanxi/xian/terracotta_warriors), [Huaqing Pool](/asia/china/shaanxi/xian/huaqing_pool), and [Mausoleum of Qin Shi Huang](/asia/china/shaanxi/xian/qin_shi_huang_mausoleum) are 40 km east, easily combined as a single morning–afternoon outing. The [Yangling Mausoleum](/asia/china/shaanxi/xian/yangling_mausoleum) is a quieter half-day to the north.

Further out, [Mount Hua](/asia/china/shaanxi/xian/mount_hua) (120 km east) and [Famen Temple](/asia/china/shaanxi/xian/famen_temple) (120 km west) are full-day trips, both reachable by direct high-speed train or bus. The [Qianling Mausoleum](/asia/china/shaanxi/xian/qianling_mausoleum) is often combined with Famen on the same circuit. With longer time, the well-preserved Ming-era walled town of [Pingyao](/asia/china/shanxi/pingyao) is a worthwhile overnight, four hours away by train.
