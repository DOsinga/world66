---
title: Getting Around
type: section
---

The Xi'an Metro is the easiest way to move around the modern city. The most useful lines for travellers are Line 1 (east–west under the city, from the railway station via the Bell Tower to the West Gate area) and Line 2 (north–south through the Bell Tower, connecting Xi'an North high-speed rail station to the south of the city). Trains run roughly 6am to 11pm; signage is bilingual.

Inside the city walls, the easiest way to see the historic core is on foot or by bicycle. You can rent a bike on top of the [City Walls](/asia/china/shaanxi/xian/city_walls) for the wall circuit (about two hours for the full 14 km), and bike-share apps work throughout the centre.

For sights outside the walls — Big Wild Goose Pagoda, Shaanxi History Museum — taxis are cheap and plentiful, but ride-hailing apps (Didi) are usually quicker than flagging one down. For the [Terracotta Warriors](/asia/china/shaanxi/xian/terracotta_warriors), [Huaqing Pool](/asia/china/shaanxi/xian/huaqing_pool), and Lintong, the easiest option is the dedicated tourist bus 5(306) from the east square of Xi'an railway station; it costs less than a taxi and stops at all three.
