The Hui Muslim Quarter is a dense web of lanes north of Xi'an's Drum Tower, with snack stalls, souvenir shops, and Hui community life packed into a very walkable area. It is one of the easiest places in the city to sample local street food while moving between the Bell Tower, Drum Tower, and Great Mosque.
