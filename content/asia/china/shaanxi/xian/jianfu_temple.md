Jianfu Temple was founded in 684 to commemorate the death of the Tang emperor Gaozong. The [Small Wild Goose Pagoda](/asia/china/shaanxi/xian/small_wild_goose_pagoda) was built in its grounds twenty years later and now stands at the back of the complex; the temple buildings sit between it and the front gate. Most of what survives is a Ming-era reconstruction, but a handful of Tang foundation stones and a 9th-century stone tortoise remain. The temple is reached on the same ticket as the pagoda and the Xi'an Museum.
