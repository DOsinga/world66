Qinling National Botanical Garden sits south of Xian where the city gives way to the
Qinling Mountains, the natural divide between northern and southern China. Its scale is
the main surprise: this is not a small urban garden but a landscape of plant
collections, wooded slopes, and mountain views.

The garden is best for travelers who want a quieter day outside Xian after the heavy
historical circuit of walls, pagodas, and imperial tombs. Spring blossom and autumn
color are the most rewarding seasons, while summer heat makes shade and water worth
planning around.
