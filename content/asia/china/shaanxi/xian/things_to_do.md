title: Things to Do

---

Xi'an was the capital of China for eleven dynasties and the eastern terminus of the Silk Road. The headline sight is of course the [Terracotta Warriors](/asia/china/shaanxi/xian/terracotta_warriors), 40 km east of the city, but the historic core itself rewards two or three days of slow exploration.

Inside the Ming-era [city walls](/asia/china/shaanxi/xian/city_walls) — best ridden by rented bicycle around the full 14-km circuit — the [Bell Tower](/asia/china/shaanxi/xian/bell_tower) and [Drum Tower](/asia/china/shaanxi/xian/drum_tower) mark the geographic centre, and the [Muslim Quarter](/asia/china/shaanxi/xian/muslim_quarter) immediately to their west is the city's beating heart for food and atmosphere. The [Stele Forest](/asia/china/shaanxi/xian/stele_forest) on Shuyuanmen is the city's most underrated museum.

South of the walls, the Tang dynasty pagodas at the [Big Wild Goose](/asia/china/shaanxi/xian/big_wild_goose_pagoda) and [Small Wild Goose](/asia/china/shaanxi/xian/small_wild_goose_pagoda) temples mark the Tang capital's old precincts, and the nearby [Shaanxi History Museum](/asia/china/shaanxi/xian/shaanxi_history_museum) is one of the great museums of China. Allow a full day for the warriors, a half day for the Stele Forest and city walls, a half day for the Muslim Quarter and the towers, and a full day for the south-of-walls museum-and-pagoda district.
