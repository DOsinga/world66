Xi'an Art Museum gives the city a contemporary cultural stop in a destination better known for imperial tombs, pagodas, and Tang-era spectacle. Its exhibitions change, often focusing on modern and contemporary Chinese art rather than the ancient material that dominates most Xi'an itineraries.

It is worth checking the current programme before making the trip, especially because Qujiang already draws visitors for other sights. When the exhibition is strong, the museum adds a useful modern layer to Xi'an's heavily historical image.
