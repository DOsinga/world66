title: Things to Do
type: section
---

Xianyang's main draw is its Han imperial tombs. The [Yangling Mausoleum](/asia/china/shaanxi/xianyang/yangling_mausoleum) of Emperor Jingdi is the most accessible and most rewarding, with a glass-floor museum built over excavation pits containing thousands of miniature terracotta figures. The [Maoling Mausoleum](/asia/china/shaanxi/xianyang/maoling_mausoleum) of Emperor Wu is the largest Tang imperial burial mound in the region, and its associated museum holds six exceptional Han stone animal sculptures from the tomb of the general Huo Qubing. The [Xianyang Museum](/asia/china/shaanxi/xianyang/xianyang_museum) in the old city centre houses Qin Dynasty relics and an important collection of Han ceramic figures.
