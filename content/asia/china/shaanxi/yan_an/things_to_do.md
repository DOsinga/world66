Yan'an's sights divide into revolutionary history and landscape. The cave dwelling complexes at [Zaoyuan (Date Park)](/asia/china/shaanxi/yan_an/zaoyuan_revolutionary_site) and [Yangjialing](/asia/china/shaanxi/yan_an/yangjialing_revolutionary_site) were home to the Communist Party leadership between 1935 and 1948; the original furnishings survive in many caves. [Pagoda Mountain](/asia/china/shaanxi/yan_an/pagoda_mountain) (Baota Shan) is the city's visual symbol, with panoramic views over the characteristic loess-plateau landscape of ravines and cave dwellings. The [Yan'an Revolutionary Memorial Museum](/asia/china/shaanxi/yan_an/revolutionary_memorial_museum) provides the historical framework for all of it — a large, well-funded institution that documents the base-area years with unusual thoroughness.
