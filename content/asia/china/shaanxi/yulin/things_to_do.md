Yulin's sights combine Ming Great Wall heritage with striking desert landscape. The [Zhenbei Platform](/asia/china/shaanxi/yulin/zhenbei_platform) (Zhenbeitai), north of the city, is the largest surviving beacon tower on the entire Ming Wall, with the desert rolling north from its base. [Hongshi Gorge](/asia/china/shaanxi/yulin/hongshi_gorge), east of town, cuts through red sandstone cliffs pocked with cave dwellings and covered in carved inscriptions. [Lake Hongjiannao](/asia/china/shaanxi/yulin/lake_hongjiannao), the largest desert freshwater lake in China, sits incongruously in the Mu Us Desert northwest of the city. The ancient city site of [Shimao](/asia/china/shaanxi/yulin/shimao_ruins), in Shenmu County, is one of the most significant Bronze Age discoveries in China and still being excavated.
