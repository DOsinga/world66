Yulin City Museum introduces the history and culture of northern Shaanxi, with archaeological finds, local folk material, historical objects, and photographs from the Yulin area. It is a useful stop for understanding the frontier setting behind nearby sights such as Zhenbei Platform, Hongshi Gorge, and the old city streets.

The museum is most worthwhile early in a Yulin visit, since it gives context for the region's Ming-era military history, loess landscape, and local traditions. Check current opening hours before going.
