Yulin Old Street is one of the better places in the city to see its older street life and northern Shaanxi character at ground level. The area mixes traditional storefronts, everyday commerce, and the cityscape that surrounds Yulin's towers and old defensive landmarks.

It works best as a short walk through central Yulin before visiting Kaige Tower, the Drum Tower, Hongshi Gorge, or Zhenbei Platform.
