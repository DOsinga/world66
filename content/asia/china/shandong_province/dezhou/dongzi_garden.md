Dezhou Dongzi Garden Scenic Area is a cultural park built around the story of Dongzi's reading platform. The grounds use traditional-style buildings, water, and garden spaces to present Confucian themes in an urban park setting.

It is one of Dezhou's more useful sightseeing stops, especially if you want something more substantial than a short monument visit.
