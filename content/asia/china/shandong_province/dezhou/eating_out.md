type: section
---

Dezhou's culinary identity is inseparable from its braised chicken. The [Dezhou Braised Chicken](/asia/china/shandong_province/dezhou/dezhou_braised_chicken) stalls and restaurants around the central market are the obvious first stop. Beyond the chicken, Dezhou shares in Shandong's tradition of wheat-based foods — thick hand-cut noodles, stuffed buns, and large flatbreads — and the city's position on the old Grand Canal means it has a secondary tradition of freshwater fish dishes.
