Haidao Jinshan Temple, in Qingyun County in the eastern part of Dezhou prefecture, is one of the larger Buddhist temple complexes in northern China and a significant pilgrimage destination for Shandong and Hebei devotees. The temple's origins are traditionally dated to the Tang Dynasty, though most of the surviving architecture is Ming and Qing. The main complex contains a sequence of courtyards and halls culminating in a large main worship hall, with a pagoda and subsidiary halls arranged to the sides.

The temple draws primarily Chinese pilgrims rather than foreign tourists, and it operates as an active religious institution rather than a heritage site. The atmosphere is accordingly different from the more museified temples in major cities: incense burns continuously, monks conduct regular services, and the courtyards have the purposeful bustle of a place in use. Festival days — particularly the Guanyin festival in the fourth lunar month — bring large numbers of pilgrims and are worth seeing if your timing aligns.

Getting to Haidao Jinshan Temple from Dezhou city requires a bus or private transport to Qingyun County, then a local connection; the journey takes about ninety minutes each way.
