title: Things to Do
type: section
---

Dezhou's most distinctive sight is the [Tomb of Sultan Paduka Pahala](/asia/china/shandong_province/dezhou/sulu_sultan_tomb) — the only tomb of a foreign monarch on Chinese soil, maintained continuously for over six hundred years and still the subject of active Filipino-Chinese heritage diplomacy. [Haidao Jinshan Temple](/asia/china/shandong_province/dezhou/haidao_jinshan_temple), one of northern China's significant Buddhist pilgrimage centres, draws visitors for its scale and atmosphere rather than its age. Beyond the historical sites, [Dezhou Braised Chicken](/asia/china/shandong_province/dezhou/dezhou_braised_chicken) is reason enough for a stop: eating it at source is a different experience from the vacuum-packed version sold at stations across China.
