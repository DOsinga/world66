The Dongying City Museum covers the region's long history from Neolithic settlement through the Longshan and Shang Bronze Age cultures to the Han Dynasty and beyond, with particular attention to the role of the Yellow River in shaping local civilisation. The collections include pottery, bronzes, and Han-era tomb artefacts excavated from the delta region — evidence that the wetlands now preserved as nature reserve were once populated agricultural land before the river shifted again.

The museum also addresses the more recent transformation of the region by the oil industry, bridging ancient and modern in a way that makes the city's peculiar character more legible. The building itself is large and modern, with well-lit galleries. It is a useful orientation to the region if you are spending more than a day around Dongying.
