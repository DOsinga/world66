Guangrao County, south of Dongying city, holds a Song Dynasty Guandi Temple whose main wooden hall is considered one of the oldest surviving timber structures in Shandong. Guandi temples — dedicated to Guan Yu, the deified Han Dynasty general who became the god of war and brotherhood — are common across China, but pre-Song examples are rare, and the Guangrao hall's age and structural integrity make it notable even for dedicated students of Chinese timber architecture.

The county town itself is unremarkable, but the temple's wooden brackets, columns, and roof structure reward the attention of anyone interested in pre-modern construction techniques. The contrast with the relentlessly modern streetscape of Dongying city is itself part of the interest. Guangrao is about 40 kilometres south of Dongying's city centre, accessible by local bus or taxi.
