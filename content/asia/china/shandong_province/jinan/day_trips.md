Jinan's position on the main Beijing–Shanghai rail corridor makes it an unusually convenient base for day trips. [Mount Tai](/asia/china/shandong_province/mount_tai) is 20 minutes by high-speed train to Tai'an, from which buses run directly to the mountain base — a climb-and-cable-car-back day is perfectly feasible. [Qufu](/asia/china/shandong_province/qufu) is 30 minutes by high-speed rail, making the Three Kong Sites an easy half-day excursion. [Tai'an](/asia/china/shandong_province/taian) is a pleasant smaller city worth a look in its own right beyond the mountain connection.
