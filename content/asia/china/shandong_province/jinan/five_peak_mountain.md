Five Peak Mountain is a large scenic park southeast of Changqing, on the outer edge of Jinan. The area combines wooded slopes with temple buildings and pavilions, making it more varied than a simple hill walk.

It is best for travelers with time to go beyond central Jinan's springs and old streets. Expect a nature-focused outing with cultural stops along the way.
