The Folk Art Paradise Museum occupies the former outpatient clinic of the Shandong Red Swastika Society, a Chinese relief organisation similar in purpose to the Red Cross. The building now introduces Chinese folk art rather than medical history.

It is a modest museum, but useful if you are already exploring Jinan's old German quarter and want a quieter cultural stop between the larger sights.
