Huayang Palace is a Taoist temple complex that rewards travellers who have already seen Jinan's headline springs and want something quieter. Its appeal is in the layered courtyards, incense-darkened halls, and carved details rather than a single spectacular view.

The palace sits a little away from the busiest tourist circuit, which helps. Visit with modest expectations and enough time to look at the buildings closely; it is a good small stop for anyone interested in religious architecture or old Jinan beyond Daming Lake.
