Jinan Archaeological Museum is a small museum on the city's archaeological work and discoveries. It is not one of Jinan's headline sights, but it helps explain the older layers beneath a city better known to visitors for springs, lakes, and Qing-era streets.

It makes the most sense as an add-on if you are already nearby or have a specific interest in local history.
