Jinan Art Museum is a modern art museum in western Jinan, near the junction of Lashanhe West Road and Weihai Road. It hosts changing exhibitions of contemporary art and gives the newer west side of the city a cultural stop beyond the older spring and lake districts.

It is most useful for visitors with a specific interest in local art or anyone staying nearby. Check the current exhibitions before making a long trip across town.
