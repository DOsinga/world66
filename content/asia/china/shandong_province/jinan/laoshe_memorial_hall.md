Laoshe Memorial Hall is a small museum in the former Jinan residence of Lao She, one of modern China's best-known writers. The rooms focus on his time in the city and the early-20th-century world that shaped his fiction.

It is a quiet, specialist stop rather than a headline sight, but it adds a literary layer to Jinan's spring parks and old streets.
