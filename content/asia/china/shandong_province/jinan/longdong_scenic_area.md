Longdong Scenic Area gives Jinan a rougher natural edge than the spring parks in the city centre. The draw is a valley of cliffs, caves, trees, and stone paths, with a name that evokes the dragon legends attached to the landscape.

It is a good choice when you want a half-day outdoors without leaving the Jinan area completely. Expect stairs and uneven ground, and go for rock, shade, and views rather than manicured garden scenery.
