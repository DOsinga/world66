Minghu Ju is a Qing-dynasty theatre near Daming Lake where visitors can see traditional opera in a teahouse setting. It is a good fit for an evening when you want a cultural stop that is more local than another museum.

The format is informal: tea, food, and performance share the same old theatre space. Check the current programme before making a special trip.
