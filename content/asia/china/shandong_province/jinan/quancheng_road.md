Quancheng Road is the main east–west artery of Jinan's old city, running from the Baotu Spring area east through the historic spring district and commercial center. The street has been pedestrianised in its western section, and the combination of old architecture, spring water channels, and dense commercial activity makes it the most characteristic urban experience in Jinan.

The evening scene on Quancheng Road's pedestrian section is particularly good — street food vendors, small shops, and crowds of locals doing what Chinese urban populations do best: eating, shopping, and occupying public space with cheerful purpose. The shops sell Jinan specialties (rose cakes, lotus-root products, spring-themed gifts), and the food stalls offer a broad survey of Shandong snacks. This is not a tourist fabrication — the street is genuinely busy with local residents.
