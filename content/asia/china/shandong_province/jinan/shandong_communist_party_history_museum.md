The Shandong Communist Party History Museum is a specialist museum in central Jinan, with exhibition halls covering the Communist Party's development in Shandong Province.

It will mainly appeal to visitors interested in modern Chinese political history, but its central location makes it easy to combine with Jinan's old-city springs and nearby historic streets.
