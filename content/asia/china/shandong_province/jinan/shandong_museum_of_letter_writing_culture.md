The Shandong Museum of Letter Writing Culture is a small specialist museum devoted to the history of correspondence. Exhibits cover ancient and modern letter writing, with examples connected to well-known people from Shandong.

It is a niche stop, but worthwhile for visitors already exploring Jinan's cultural district and interested in calligraphy, paper culture, and everyday written history.
