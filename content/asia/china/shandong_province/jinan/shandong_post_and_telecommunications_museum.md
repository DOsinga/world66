The Shandong Post and Telecommunications Museum occupies part of the China Unicom building on the site of the former Shandong Postal Administration Office. Its displays cover the development of postal and communications networks in the province.

It is a niche museum, but it fits well into a central Jinan walk that also takes in the nearby cathedral, mosque, Confucius temple, and old commercial streets.
