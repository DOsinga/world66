Shandong University Museum displays collections from the university's archaeology and history departments. Exhibits include ancient Chinese writing, archaeological finds, and material connected with the long history of Shandong.

It is most worthwhile for visitors with a specific interest in archaeology or Chinese historical culture. Check current access before going, as university museums can have limited public hours.
