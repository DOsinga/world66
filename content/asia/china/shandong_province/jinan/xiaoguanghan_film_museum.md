Xiaoguanghan Film Museum occupies the former Jinan Xiaoguanghan Theater, regarded as the city's earliest cinema. The small museum focuses on film history and the building's role in Jinan's early cinema culture.

It fits well into a walk through the old city, close to the Confucius Temple, Great Mosque, historic churches, springs, and other compact sights around central Jinan.
