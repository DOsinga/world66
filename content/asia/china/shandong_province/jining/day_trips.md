[Qufu](/asia/china/shandong_province/qufu) is the obvious day trip from Jining — about 30 minutes by bus or taxi, 20 minutes on the high-speed rail from Yanzhou. The Mencius Temple in Zoucheng (45 minutes south) makes a complementary half-day to Qufu. The [Weishan Lake](/asia/china/shandong_province/jining/weishan_lake) wetlands are 1.5 hours south by bus and worth a half-day for waterbirds and lotus flowers.
