Shangjiushan Ancient Village is a traditional stone village near Zoucheng, with lanes and buildings dating mainly from the Ming and Qing periods. It is a good stop for seeing older rural architecture in southwestern Shandong.

The appeal is the village setting rather than a single monument, so allow time to walk the lanes and look at the stone houses, courtyards, and surrounding countryside.
