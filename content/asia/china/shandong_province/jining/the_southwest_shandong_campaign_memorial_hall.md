The Southwest Shandong Campaign Memorial Hall commemorates the Battle of Southwest Shandong and the martyrs buried around the Yangshan battlefield. The large memorial area includes exhibition halls, a panorama gallery, monuments, weapon displays, and cemetery grounds.
