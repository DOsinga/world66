Tianfo Tourist Area is an outlying Linyi scenic area built around Buddhist imagery, hillside paths, and viewpoints. It fits the regional pattern of mountain parks where religious iconography and landscape are experienced together.

It is a secondary sight, best for travelers with extra time or private transport. If your Linyi stop is short, prioritize the city's stronger museums and historic sites first.
