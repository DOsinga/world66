Blossom Valley is a quieter scenic valley on Mount Tai, known for streams, forest, seasonal flowers, and clearer water than the busier summit routes. It is a good choice for visitors who want mountain scenery with fewer crowds than the main stairway.

The valley is best treated as a nature walk within the wider Mount Tai area. Spring blossoms and autumn colour are the strongest reasons to choose this side route.
