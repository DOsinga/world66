Badaguan is Qingdao at its most graceful: a leafy district of villas, embassies, and European-influenced architecture laid out near the sea. The name refers to eight old passes, and the streets still feel like a deliberate collection of moods rather than a normal grid.

Walk slowly and look at the houses, trees, and glimpses of beach between the lanes. It overlaps with Qingdao's German-era story but has its own softer, residential character.
