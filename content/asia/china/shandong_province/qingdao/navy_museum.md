The PLA Navy Museum is one of Qingdao's most distinctive museums, fitting for a city with a long naval identity and a major modern naval base. The collection includes ship displays, weapons, uniforms, aircraft, and exhibits tracing the development of China's navy in the 20th and 21st centuries.

The outdoor hardware is the main draw for most visitors, especially if you are interested in military history or engineering. It also broadens the usual Qingdao itinerary beyond German colonial architecture, beaches, and beer, showing the city's strategic role on the Yellow Sea.
