Qingdao German Prison Site Museum occupies a colonial-era prison first built during the German lease of Qingdao. The exhibits are blunt about the city's occupation history, moving from the German period into Japanese rule and later political imprisonment.

The cells, basement rooms, and recreated interrogation spaces make it a darker visit than Qingdao's villas and beaches, but it adds useful context to the old German quarter. Go when you want a more serious piece of the city's history.
