Dongyi Town is a purpose-built cultural district in Rizhao, mixing old-style streets, food stalls, shops, and references to the region's Dongyi heritage. It is not an untouched historic quarter, but it gives visitors an easy place to walk, snack, and see how the city packages its local identity.

Go in the evening if you want the livelier version, when lights, food vendors, and crowds make the area feel more like a promenade than a museum. It works best as a relaxed addition to Rizhao's beaches and waterfront rather than a standalone reason to visit the city.
