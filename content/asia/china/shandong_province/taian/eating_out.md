---
title: "Eating Out"
type: section
---

Tai'an's food is straight Shandong cuisine — heavier and saltier than the food further south, built on wheat noodles, dumplings, and braised meat. Local specialities lean on what grows on the mountain: tofu made with Mount Tai spring water (said to be the best in China for the purpose), wild mushrooms, and a particular variety of grey-green vegetables called "red gizzard cabbage".

The cluster of restaurants along [Tongtian Street](/asia/china/shandong_province/taian/tongtian_street) is the easiest place to find a [Mount Tai tofu](/asia/china/shandong_province/taian/mount_tai_tofu) meal or a bowl of [hand-pulled noodles](/asia/china/shandong_province/taian/hand_pulled_noodles) after the descent. For something more formal, the dining rooms of the larger hotels in town serve banquet-style Shandong cooking.
