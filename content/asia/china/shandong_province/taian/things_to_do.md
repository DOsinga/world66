---
title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Almost everyone here is on their way up [Mount Tai](/asia/china/shandong_province/taian/mount_tai), the most revered of the Five Sacred Mountains and a UNESCO World Heritage Site. The traditional pilgrimage begins at [Dai Temple](/asia/china/shandong_province/taian/dai_temple) in the centre of town, follows the long ceremonial avenue north, and continues up the staircase past [Red Gate](/asia/china/shandong_province/taian/red_gate), the [Doumu Palace](/asia/china/shandong_province/taian/doumu_palace), and the [Middle Heavenly Gate](/asia/china/shandong_province/taian/midway_gate) to the [Jade Emperor Peak](/asia/china/shandong_province/taian/jade_emperor_peak) at the summit.

The classic route is to start before dawn and walk up overnight for sunrise from the peak — the experience that has drawn pilgrims and poets for two thousand years. A bus to the midway gate and the cable car from there to the summit is the modern alternative. Either way, allow most of a day and bring something warm: the summit is several degrees colder than the base, and the wind can be fierce.

In town, the [Sutra Stone Valley](/asia/china/shandong_province/taian/sutra_stone_valley) and the [Pumen Temple](/asia/china/shandong_province/taian/pumen_temple) are worth a half-day, and the lively [Tongtian Street](/asia/china/shandong_province/taian/tongtian_street) tourist strip between Dai Temple and the mountain is the easiest place to eat and shop after the climb.
