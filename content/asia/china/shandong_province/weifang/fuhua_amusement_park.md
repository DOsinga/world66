Fuhua Amusement Park is Weifang's main family amusement park, known locally for large rides and roller coasters. It is a straightforward day-out option if you are travelling with children or want a break from museums, kite culture, and city sightseeing.

Check current opening times before going, as ride schedules and seasonal hours can matter more here than at the city's historic sights.
