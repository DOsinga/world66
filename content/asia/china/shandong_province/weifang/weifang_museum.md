Weifang Museum is the city's broad civic museum, useful for putting the kite capital into a wider Shandong context. Its exhibitions cover local archaeology, regional history, folk traditions, and the urban story of Weifang beyond the better-known kite festival.

It is a good indoor counterpoint to Weifang's craft villages and kite-focused sights. The museum works best for visitors who want background before exploring the city's old commercial quarters, festival culture, and surrounding heritage sites.
