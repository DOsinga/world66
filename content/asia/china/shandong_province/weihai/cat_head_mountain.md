Cat Head Mountain is a small coastal hill named for a rock profile said to resemble a cat's head. It is not a major hike, but it gives a quick look at Weihai's rocky shoreline and makes a pleasant short stop if you are already exploring the coast east of the city.

The appeal is simple: sea air, a compact climb, and a viewpoint with a local name that is easy to remember.
