The First World War Chinese Laborers Memorial is a focused memorial to the Chinese workers who served behind the lines in Europe during the First World War. Weihai is an apt place for it, given the region's links to recruitment and maritime routes.

It is a quiet, reflective stop rather than a headline attraction. Visit if you are interested in the less familiar global history of the war or want a meaningful addition to Weihai's coastal and island sights.
