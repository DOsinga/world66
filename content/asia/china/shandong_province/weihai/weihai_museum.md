Weihai Museum is a broad local-history museum for a coastal city better known to travellers for Liugong Island, Korean connections, and seaside scenery. Its exhibitions help organise the region's maritime, military, and civic history into a more coherent narrative.

This is a supporting stop, not Weihai's main attraction. It is most useful in poor weather or for visitors who want background before heading to Liugong Island and the First Sino-Japanese War sites.
