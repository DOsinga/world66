Mount Kunyu is the main mountain escape for the Yantai area, a forested scenic region associated with Taoist culture, temples, streams, and ridge walks. It gives the Shandong coast an inland counterweight, trading beaches and wine country for cooler wooded slopes.

The area is broad enough that planning matters: choose a specific gate or route rather than simply aiming for the mountain. In good weather it works as a full day trip for hiking and temple scenery, especially for travelers who want Yantai beyond the waterfront.
