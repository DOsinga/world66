---
title: Things to Do
type: section
---

Yantai combines beach resort pleasures with a distinctive wine culture and genuine treaty-port history. The hilltop consulate district is quieter and less commercialized than Qingdao's German quarter, and the Changyu Wine Culture Museum is one of the more interesting industrial-heritage attractions in Shandong. The Changdao Islands off the coast add an excursion option for those who can spare an extra day.
