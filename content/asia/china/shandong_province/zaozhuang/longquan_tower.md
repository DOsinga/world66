Longquan Tower is a tall brick pagoda in Tengzhou, counted among the area's traditional scenic sights. Its pavilion-style structure and blue-brick exterior are linked to Song and Yuan dynasty Buddhist architecture, and the tower remains one of the more distinctive historic landmarks in the Zaozhuang area.

The site is most worthwhile for visitors interested in old Chinese pagodas and regional Buddhist history.
