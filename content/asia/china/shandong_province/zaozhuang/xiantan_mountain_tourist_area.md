Xiantan Mountain Tourist Area is a scenic area outside Zaozhuang with wooded hills, springs, rocks, caves, and cultural remains. It has been developed with walking areas, viewing spots, and leisure facilities.

The draw is the mix of natural scenery and local history rather than a single monument. It is most useful for visitors with extra time in the Zaozhuang area who want a green day trip.
