Zaozhuang Guanshi Liuyuan Scenic Area is a countryside attraction east of the city, combining old sandalwood groves, small temples, walking paths, and seasonal pomegranate orchards. It is the kind of place to approach slowly, with more value in the landscape and local atmosphere than in a single monument.

The best time to visit is around pomegranate season, when the orchards are active and the rural setting gives a clearer contrast to Zaozhuang's industrial core.
