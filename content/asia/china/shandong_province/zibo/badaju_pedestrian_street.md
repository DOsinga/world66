Badaju Pedestrian Street is a compact walking area lined with older buildings, snack stalls, craft shops, and long-running local stores. It is more about atmosphere than a single landmark.

For a first visit to Zibo, it works well as a low-key introduction to the city's everyday food culture before or after the larger museums and ancient sites.
