The China Ceramics and Glazed Glass Museum reflects Zibo's long association with ceramics, kiln culture and colored glaze. Its displays give the city a strong specialist museum, with historical pieces and contemporary craft connecting local industry to Chinese decorative art.

For travelers in Zibo, this is one of the clearest reasons to stop beyond general urban sightseeing. Allow enough time to see both the ceramics and glazed-glass sections, since together they explain why the museum belongs here rather than in a generic provincial collection.
