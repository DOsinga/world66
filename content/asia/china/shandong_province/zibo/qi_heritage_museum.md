Qi Heritage Museum in Linzi focuses on the ancient State of Qi, whose capital lay in
this part of today's Zibo. For travelers, it helps connect the region's ruins, walls,
and place names to a real political and cultural power of early China.

The museum is most useful if you are already interested in Chinese history or are
visiting Linzi's archaeological sites. It is not as instantly legible as a ceramics
museum, but it gives Zibo a deeper identity than industry and barbecue.
