Red Leaf Persimmon Rock Tourism Area is a mountain-valley scenic area near Boshan, best known for its autumn colour. The slopes and village buildings are especially photogenic when the persimmon trees and red leaves are at their peak from early September through November.

The area mixes rural scenery with stone-and-tile buildings and preserved Qing-era structures, so it works best as an unhurried countryside stop rather than a quick roadside viewpoint.
