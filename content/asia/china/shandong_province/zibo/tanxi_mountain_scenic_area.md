Tanxi Mountain Scenic Area is a mountain walking area with red-leaf forests, stone paths, caves, temples, and a high glass walkway. The downhill route from Longfeng Terrace toward the glass walkway is the usual scenic line, especially in autumn or near sunset when the ridges catch the light.
