---
title: Things to Do
type: section
---

Zibo rewards visitors willing to move around its sprawling geography. The Linzi district in the east holds the ancient Qi capital's ruins and dedicated museums; Zhoucun in the northwest preserves intact Ming and Qing commercial streetscapes; Boshan in the south has ceramics and glassware traditions going back to the Ming Dynasty. And the barbecue, which went viral in 2023, is genuinely worth the trip.
