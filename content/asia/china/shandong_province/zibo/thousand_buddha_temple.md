Thousand Buddha Temple sits near Zhoucun's old commercial quarter, easy to miss beside the busier streets around it. Step inside and it opens into a compact temple garden with six halls and a mix of Buddhist, Taoist, and Confucian elements.

It is best visited together with Zhoucun Ancient Town. The contrast is the point: market-town bustle outside, a quieter religious compound within a few steps.
