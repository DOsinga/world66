Yuanshan National Forest Park is Zibo's national forest park, with wooded hills and several developed scenic areas. The park includes sections tied to the old Great Wall of Qi, a folk custom garden, and family-oriented attractions such as Dinosaur Valley.

It is more of a local day out than a headline Shandong sight, but it is worth considering if you are staying in Zibo and want forest scenery close to the city.
