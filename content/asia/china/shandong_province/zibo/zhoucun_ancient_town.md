Zhoucun Ancient Commercial Street is one of the better-preserved examples of a late Ming and Qing dynasty market town in northern China — an area of streets, shophouses, warehouses, and guild halls that survived the 20th century largely intact because it was absorbed into Zibo's industrial urban fabric rather than demolished and redeveloped.

The main streets — Dajie, Sishijie, and Yinzijie — retain their original stone paving and the ground-floor shopfront architecture that made this one of the major trading hubs for silk, cotton, and manufactured goods in the 18th and 19th centuries. Several buildings have been converted to restaurants, craft shops, and small museums; others survive in their original commercial function. The Thousand Buddha Temple nearby adds a religious dimension. Zhoucun is in the western part of Zibo city, accessible by bus.
