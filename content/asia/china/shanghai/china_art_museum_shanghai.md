China Art Museum Shanghai occupies the former China Pavilion from Expo 2010, a huge red building now used for modern and contemporary Chinese art. The strongest draws are the upper-floor galleries and the digital version of Along the River During the Qingming Festival, a long animated remake of the classic scroll painting.
