The civic heart of Shanghai, centred on the vast open space of **People's Square** — formerly the Shanghai Racecourse under the British concession, then a military parade ground, now a public park surrounded by the city's main cultural and government institutions. The square and its surroundings feel more formal than the cafe-dense neighbourhoods nearby, but the density of museums and the metro interchange beneath it make it an unavoidable part of any serious visit.

The **Shanghai Museum** on the south side holds one of the world's great collections of Chinese art — bronzes, ceramics, calligraphy, and furniture across dozens of galleries. The **Shanghai Urban Planning Exhibition Hall** has an extraordinary scale model of the city's entire urban area. The **Shanghai Grand Theatre** is the city's main opera and ballet venue. **Nanjing Road pedestrian street**, running east from the square, is the main retail artery.
