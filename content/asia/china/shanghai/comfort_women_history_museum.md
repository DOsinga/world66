The Chinese "Comfort Women" History Museum documents the women forced into sexual slavery by the Japanese military during the Second World War. It is a sober, difficult museum focused on testimony, records, and public memory.

Visit with time to absorb the subject matter. It is most relevant for travellers interested in Shanghai's wartime history and the wider history of East Asia in the 20th century.
