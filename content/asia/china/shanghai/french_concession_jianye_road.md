title: Jianye Road (Local Market Street)
type: poi
---

A local neighbourhood street in the French Concession running between Huaihai Road and Fuxing Road that retains its function as a market and provisioning street for residents rather than a tourist-oriented commercial district. Jianye Road has fresh produce stalls, a wet market section, bakeries, and the small hardware and household goods shops that serve daily life in the surrounding residential blocks.

Visiting this street gives a direct experience of how the French Concession functions as a living neighbourhood rather than an architectural exhibit. The morning hours (7–10 am) are the most active; the afternoon is quieter. The stalls and shops on Jianye Road are a good complement to a walk around the more visited architectural streets nearby.
