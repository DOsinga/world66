Gucun Park is a large northern Shanghai park best known for its cherry trees and spring blossom festival. Beyond the seasonal draw, it has broad walking areas and family attractions, including a dinosaur-themed amusement area. It is not central, but it can be worth the trip during blossom season or for families staying in Baoshan.
