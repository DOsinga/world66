Huangpu Park sits at the northern tip of the Bund, where Suzhou Creek meets the Huangpu River. The park is small, but its riverfront position and the Bund Historical Museum make it a useful stop for understanding the colonial-era waterfront and its later reinvention as one of Shanghai's main promenades.
