The neighbourhood north of the French Concession and west of the Bund, anchored by the **Jing'an Temple** — a working Buddhist monastery surrounded by a wall of luxury retail, which says something about the district's character. Jing'an is Shanghai's most concentrated luxury shopping area: Nanjing West Road and the Jing'an Kerry Centre carry every international brand worth naming. But behind the malls, the streets of old lilong housing and the tree-lined lanes near Changshu Road offer a different pace.

The **M50 Creative Park** on Moganshan Road, in a cluster of former mill buildings, is the best place to browse contemporary Chinese art galleries. **Jade Buddha Temple** to the north is one of the city's most visited religious sites, its two jade Buddha statues — the larger one over a metre tall — brought from Burma in 1882. **Maoming Road** and **Yuyuan Road** have good independent cafes and bars at a scale that Nanjing West Road doesn't offer.
