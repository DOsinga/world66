Jing'an Park is a small green pause in one of Shanghai's busiest central districts. Paths curve around water and planting, and the park is often at its best when local residents are dancing, exercising, or playing board games in the corners.

Come here between temples, malls, and metro rides rather than as a major sight. It gives a quick, useful glimpse of everyday public life in dense central Shanghai.
