Jinjiang Amusement Park is an older Shanghai amusement park, useful mainly for families and for the 108-metre Ferris wheel. On a clear day the wheel gives a broad view across the southern side of the city.

It is not as polished as Shanghai's newer theme parks, but it still works as a low-key family stop when you want rides rather than another museum or temple.
