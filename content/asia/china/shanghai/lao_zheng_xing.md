One of the oldest restaurants in Shanghai, opened in 1862 and serving the traditional sweet, dark, oily *benbang cai* — Shanghainese home cooking. Order the **red-braised pork belly** (hong shao rou), **drunken crab** marinated in Shaoxing wine (in season, autumn), **eel fried with garlic**, and the **scallion oil noodles**. Prices are reasonable, the room is brightly lit and noisy in the proper Chinese style, and the staff have largely forgotten that anyone might want a menu in English. Use a translation app.

Several branches around the city — this Fuzhou Road location is the original.
