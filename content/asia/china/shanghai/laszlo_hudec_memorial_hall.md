The László Hudec Memorial Hall occupies the former family home of the Hungarian-Slovak architect who helped shape early 20th-century Shanghai. Small exhibits explain Hudec's life and point visitors toward some of his better-known buildings around the city.

It is most useful as context before exploring Shanghai's Art Deco and historic residential districts.
