Long Museum West Bund is the riverside branch of the private museum founded by collectors Liu Yiqian and Wang Wei. It shows Chinese and international contemporary art in a large industrial-style building on Shanghai's West Bund, with rotating exhibitions rather than a fixed route through a permanent collection.
