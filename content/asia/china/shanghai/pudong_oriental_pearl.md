The 1994 television tower that was Pudong's first supertall structure and is still the most recognisable element of the Shanghai skyline from the Bund — the two pink spheres connected by cylindrical sections have become the default image of the city. The tower has been largely superseded aesthetically and structurally by the subsequent Lujiazui towers, but the Shanghai History Museum at its base is one of the better city history museums in China.

The museum (separate from the tower admission) traces Shanghai's history from Ming dynasty trading port through the concession era, the Japanese occupation, the 1949 revolution, and the Reform era development. The diorama reconstructions of 1930s street scenes are particularly detailed. Open daily; museum admission is separate from the tower observation.
