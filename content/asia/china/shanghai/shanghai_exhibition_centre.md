Shanghai Exhibition Centre is a grand 1950s landmark in Jing'an, built in a Soviet-influenced style and once the tallest building in Shanghai. It still hosts exhibitions and events, but the main reason to stop is the architecture and the way it anchors this part of Nanjing West Road.
