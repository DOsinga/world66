Shanghai Expo Park occupies part of the former 2010 World Expo grounds along the Huangpu River. The event is gone, but the area still has broad riverside walks, leftover pavilions, and the landmark China Pavilion, now used for cultural exhibitions. It is best combined with nearby museums and the South Bund waterfront.
