Soong Ching-ling's Former Residence is a preserved home museum dedicated to Sun Yat-sen's wife, a major political figure in her own right. Soong was educated in the United States, active in Kuomintang politics, and later remained in mainland China after 1949.

The house displays photographs, personal objects, and state-era artifacts, with well-kept grounds and a garage holding several official cars. It is a strong stop for visitors interested in 20th-century Chinese history.
