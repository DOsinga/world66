A former British racing magnate's estate from 1917, now a luxury hotel, the Ruijin Guesthouse on Ruijin 2nd Road maintains a large garden compound in the heart of the French Concession. The grounds — several hectares of mature trees, classical Chinese-Western garden plantings, and preserved 1920s villa buildings — are accessible to the public as a hotel garden.

The compound is one of the few places in central Shanghai where the pre-war garden estates of the concession era are visible in something approaching their original condition. The garden café is open to non-guests. Ruijin 2nd Road at Fuxing Road; Metro Shanxi Road South or Dapuqiao.
