Yuncaitang Spa is a large Japanese-style bath and relaxation complex in Songjiang. The entry ticket covers separate men's and women's bathing areas, saunas, jacuzzis, pools, heated pebble beds, relaxation rooms, and a children's play area, with massages and food available for extra cost.
