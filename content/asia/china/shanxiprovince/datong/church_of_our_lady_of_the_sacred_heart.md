The Church of Our Lady of the Sacred Heart is Datong's Catholic cathedral and one of the old city's more unusual landmarks. First built in 1891, it was burned during the Boxer uprising, rebuilt in 1906, damaged again during the Cultural Revolution, and later restored.

It remains an active church and the seat of the Catholic Diocese of Datong.
