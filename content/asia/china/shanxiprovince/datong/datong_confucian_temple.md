Datong Confucian Temple is a Ming-era temple complex dedicated to Confucius, with halls, gates, service buildings, and relocated historic structures. Datong was among the early cities outside Qufu to establish a temple for Confucian worship.

The site complements Datong's Buddhist temples and city walls, giving a broader view of the city's religious and civic history.
