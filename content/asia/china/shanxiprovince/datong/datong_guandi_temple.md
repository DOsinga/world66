Datong Guandi Temple is dedicated to Guan Yu, the deified general revered as Guandi. Its Martial Saint Hall is one of the few Yuan-dynasty buildings left in Datong's old city, with later Qing additions giving the complex a layered feel.

Inside are murals, carved wooden shrine work, and modern statues that keep the temple from feeling like an empty shell. It is a strong stop for visitors already exploring Datong's old city temples and walls.
