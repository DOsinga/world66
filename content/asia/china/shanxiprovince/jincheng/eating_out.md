---
title: "Eating Out"
type: section
---

Jincheng cooking shares the general Shanxi repertoire — noodles, lamb, vinegar — with some local variations from the Taihang Mountain area. The city has a range of straightforward restaurants around the commercial centre, and the villages near the Huangcheng compound have simple farmhouse restaurants serving home-style mountain food, best in season with fresh vegetables and foraged mushrooms in autumn.
