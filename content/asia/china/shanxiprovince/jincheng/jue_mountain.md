Jue Mountain, also called Jade Mountain, is part of the southern Taihang range near Jincheng. The draw is the mountain scenery: cliffs, forested slopes, temple sites, and paths that make a solid day out from the city.

The coordinates in older guide listings are approximate, so confirm transport locally before setting out. Go for the landscape rather than expecting a polished urban sight.
