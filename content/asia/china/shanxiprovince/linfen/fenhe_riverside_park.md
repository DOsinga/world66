Fenhe Riverside Park follows the river through Linfen with paths, wetlands, and open green space that give the city a useful break from traffic. It is a good place for a walk, especially in the early evening when local families come out.

Some entrances have bike-rental vendors, making the riverbank an easy low-effort ride rather than a formal sightseeing stop. Come for fresh air and a slower look at the city.
