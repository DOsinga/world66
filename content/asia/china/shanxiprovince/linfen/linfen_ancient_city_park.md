Linfen Ancient City Park is a landscaped city park that nods to the old walled-city history of Linfen with lakes, bridges, sculptures, and recreated historic themes. It is more a local promenade than an archaeological site.

Go for an easy walk, especially in the evening when city parks in China fill with families, dancers, and people taking the air. The historical styling gives the park a little more character than a plain green space.
