Qianfo Temple is a historic Buddhist temple near Linfen, noted for old architecture, Buddha statues, and murals. It is a worthwhile stop for travellers interested in Buddhist art and the region's religious history.

The temple is outside the city centre, so it fits best into a broader day of temple and heritage sights around Linfen.
