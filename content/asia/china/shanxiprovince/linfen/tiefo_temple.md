Tiefo Temple, also called Dayun Temple, is a historic Buddhist temple in central Linfen. Its best-known features are a six-tier glazed pagoda and a large iron-cast head of Sakyamuni Buddha linked to the Tang dynasty. It is a compact but worthwhile stop for travellers following Linfen's older religious and archaeological sites.
