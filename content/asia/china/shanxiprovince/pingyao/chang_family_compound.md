Chang Family Compound is one of Shanxi's grand merchant-residence complexes, built by a
wealthy family whose courtyards, halls, gardens, and carved details show the confidence
of the province's banking and trading era. It is less internationally famous than
Pingyao, but it belongs to the same world of commerce and courtyard architecture.

The compound is best for travelers who have already warmed to Pingyao's lanes and want
to see how merchant wealth played out on a family estate scale. Give yourself time to
look at the woodwork, brick carving, and spatial rhythm; the pleasure is in accumulated
detail.
