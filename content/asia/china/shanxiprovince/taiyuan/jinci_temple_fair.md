The Jinci Temple Fair is held on the third day of the third lunar month (usually March or April) and has been one of the main popular festivals in the Taiyuan area for over a thousand years. The fair combines agricultural ritual — offerings to the spring deity who controls the water that irrigates the surrounding fields — with the full range of Chinese temple fair activities: folk performances, theatrical performances, food vendors, craft markets, and the general crowd energy of a festival that has been happening in the same place for generations.

On festival days the crowds at Jinci are intense and the experience is fundamentally different from an ordinary heritage visit. The folk opera performances (typically Jin opera, the local Shanxi style) are staged on the traditional stage platform facing the Hall of the Holy Mother. For visitors interested in living ritual tradition rather than museum-style heritage, the fair is the most authentic version of what Jinci was originally for.
