Shanxi Museum of the War of Resistance Against Japanese Aggression focuses on the province's role in the Second Sino-Japanese War. Its subject is narrower than Shanxi Museum, but the local wartime angle gives it a distinct place in Taiyuan's museum landscape.

The museum is best for travellers with a serious interest in modern Chinese history. Expect a commemorative tone and allow time for translation gaps if you do not read Chinese.
