The Shanxi Grand Theatre is the main performing arts venue in Taiyuan, a large modern concert hall and opera house on the western side of the city that hosts national touring productions, local opera companies, and international performances. The building is architecturally ambitious — part of the broader civic redevelopment along the Fen River — and the interior acoustics are well-regarded.

Shanxi opera (Jin opera or Jinju) is one of the major regional opera traditions in northern China, with a history of several hundred years and a performance style characterised by bold percussion and high falsetto voices. Performances of Jinju are staged at the Grand Theatre and at the smaller Shanxi Opera House in the city centre, typically on weekend evenings. Attending a performance — even without understanding the libretto — is a vivid cultural experience and a specific Taiyuan pleasure that most visitors miss.
