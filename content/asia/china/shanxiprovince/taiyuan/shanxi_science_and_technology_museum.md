Shanxi Science and Technology Museum is a large modern science centre in Taiyuan, built around interactive displays, demonstrations, and education rather than historical collections. It is strongest for families and travellers who enjoy hands-on museums.

The museum also gives a useful contrast to Shanxi's coal, temples, and ancient architecture. It is not the first stop for most visitors, but it broadens Taiyuan's museum circuit and can rescue a cold or rainy day.
