Haizhou Guan Di Temple is one of China's most important temples dedicated to Guan Yu, who was born nearby. The large complex dates back to the Sui dynasty and has palace-like halls, gardens, rockeries, ceremonial gates, and the Spring and Autumn Building at the rear.
