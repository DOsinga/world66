---
title: Getting Around
type: section
---

Getting around Aba Prefecture requires planning. The distances between the major sights are large, roads are mountain highways, and public transport is limited outside of the main routes.

**Local buses and minibuses** run between the main towns — Aba, Songpan, Zoige, Maerkang — but infrequently and not on fixed timetables. Be prepared to wait or share a ride with whoever is going your direction.

**Shared taxis** fill the gap between bus routes and are how locals move around. Negotiate the fare before getting in.

**Hiring a driver** for 2–4 days is the most sensible way to explore the prefecture if you have a specific itinerary. Drivers and vehicles can be arranged in Songpan, which is the main hub for independent travellers. Expect to pay 400–600 RMB per day depending on distances.

**Inside Jiuzhaigou**, the park operates a shuttle bus system along the Y-shaped valley — private vehicles are not permitted. Ticket prices include the shuttles.
