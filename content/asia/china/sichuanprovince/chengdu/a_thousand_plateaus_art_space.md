A Thousand Plateaus Art Space is a contemporary gallery in Chengdu, part of the city's quieter but increasingly serious art scene. It focuses on current Chinese artists rather than the historic and archaeological material that dominates many Sichuan itineraries.

The value depends on the exhibition calendar, but that is true of most contemporary galleries. If you are already exploring Chengdu beyond pandas and teahouses, it offers a useful glimpse of the city's present-day cultural life.
