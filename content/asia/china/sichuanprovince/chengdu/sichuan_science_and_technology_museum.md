Sichuan Science and Technology Museum is a large interactive museum near Tianfu Square, with exhibits on science, space, mathematics, robotics, physics, aerodynamics, and Sichuan's waterways. It is especially useful for families or for a rainy-day break in the centre of Chengdu.

The displays are hands-on, and many explanations are in both Chinese and English.
