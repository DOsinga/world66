Sichuan University's Huaxi Campus, east of the Jinjiang River, is one of the most architecturally distinguished university campuses in China. Founded by American missionaries in 1910, the original campus buildings blend Western academic architecture with Chinese decorative elements in a way that is both historically interesting and visually appealing.

The university's museums are open to the public: the Sichuan University Museum has a solid collection covering Sichuan archaeology, folklore, and ethnography; the Huaxi Medical History Museum documents the evolution of medicine in western China. The campus grounds are pleasant to walk through, with large trees and old stone paths. Located in the Jinjiang District, accessible by Metro Line 3.
