Yongling Mausoleum is the tomb of Wang Jian, founder of the Former Shu kingdom during the Five
Dynasties period. The low mound hides a stone burial chamber decorated with musicians and
attendants, a reminder that Chengdu's history runs much deeper than pandas, hotpot, and teahouses.

It is a good counterpoint to Chengdu's busier temples and museums. The site is quiet, central enough
to combine with other stops, and especially worthwhile if you enjoy early medieval Chinese history
or want a less crowded historical sight inside the city.
