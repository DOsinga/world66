The 816 Nuclear Military Plant is a vast underground Cold War complex built for a Chinese nuclear weapons programme that was cancelled before production began. Its tunnels, caverns, and machinery halls are now open to visitors, making it one of Chongqing's stranger and more memorable industrial sights.
