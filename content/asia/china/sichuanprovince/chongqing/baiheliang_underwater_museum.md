Baiheliang Underwater Museum protects a rare Yangtze River archaeological site that was submerged after the Three Gorges Dam raised water levels. The museum lets visitors see White Crane Ridge, where carved inscriptions recorded river levels from the Tang dynasty onward, through an underwater viewing system. It is one of Fuling's strongest reasons to make the trip from central Chongqing.
