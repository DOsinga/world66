  Shaping Park contains a large lake, formal gardens, and — at its northern end — a memorial to the Cultural Revolution victims executed in the park between 1966 and 1969. Shaping Park is a large municipal park in the Shapingba district of Chongqing, popular with local families for its lake, gardens, and outdoor recreational areas. The main lake hosts paddle boats and is surrounded by walking paths and pavilions where elderly residents play cards and mahjong.

At the northern edge of the park is a memorial cemetery to victims of the Cultural Revolution — one of the most unusual and historically significant sites in Chongqing. The cemetery contains the graves of people killed in the factional Red Guard conflicts of the late 1960s, when different Revolutionary factions fought pitched battles in the city. The memorial is sobering and unusual for its frankness about this period.
