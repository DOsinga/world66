The Chongqing Urban Planning Exhibition Hall occupies a building adjacent to the Three Gorges Museum and provides a comprehensive overview of the city's past, present, and planned future. The scale model on the ground floor is the main attraction — a detailed recreation of the entire municipality covering 82,000 square kilometres, from the dense city centre to the rural districts near the Tibet border.

For visitors trying to understand the sheer size and complexity of Chongqing — at 30 million people, far larger than most countries — the exhibition helps. It also shows the ambition of the city's current development plans, with new districts, bridges, and infrastructure projects laid out in the model like an architectural dream. Admission is usually free.
