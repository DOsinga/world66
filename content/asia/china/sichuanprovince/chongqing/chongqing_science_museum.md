The Chongqing Science and Technology Museum is a large modern facility on the Yuzhong peninsula near the People's Auditorium. Its interactive exhibits cover physics, astronomy, engineering, and the history of technology, making it primarily a destination for families with children.

For adult visitors, the most interesting sections deal with the history of Chongqing's industrial development — from the weapons factories relocated here during the Anti-Japanese War, which transformed Chongqing into a major manufacturing city, to the modern automobile, motorcycle, and electronics industries that have made it one of China's largest industrial municipalities. The rooftop observation deck has excellent views. Admission is free.
