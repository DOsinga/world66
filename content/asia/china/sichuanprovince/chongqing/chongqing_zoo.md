  Chongqing Zoo has one of the largest captive populations of giant pandas in China outside the Chengdu Research Base — a legacy of its geographic position in the pandas' native habitat range. Chongqing Zoo is a large facility in the southwestern part of the city, home to giant pandas, Siberian tigers, African lions, and a wide range of other species. It is popular with Chongqing families and somewhat less polished than the dedicated panda research centres, but it offers a genuine chance to see the pandas in a relatively uncrowded setting.

The zoo grounds are pleasant and hilly, which is fitting in a city defined by its topography. The giant panda enclosure is the main draw for visitors, and the animals are visible for most of the day. Combined with the adjacent Chongqing Botanical Garden, the zoo makes for a half-day or full day out. Easy to reach by metro.
