Jiangbei District, on the north bank of the Jialing River, is Chongqing's most international neighbourhood and its financial centre. The Jiangbei Zuì area around the junction of the Jialing and Yangtze rivers has grown into a forest of skyscrapers and is home to the city's newest luxury hotels, high-end restaurants, and shopping centres.

The riverfront promenade offers exceptional views back across to the historic peninsula and the illuminated towers of central Chongqing. Jiangbei is also where you will find the Airport Rail Link terminal and many of the city's newer cultural venues. For travellers staying near the airport, this district is the most convenient base for exploring the city.
