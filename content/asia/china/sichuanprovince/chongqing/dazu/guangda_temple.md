Guangda Temple is one of the Buddhist sites around Dazu, where temple architecture and carved religious imagery sit close together. It is not as famous as the headline rock carving areas, but it helps fill in the wider sacred landscape.

The temple is best visited as part of a broader Dazu itinerary. Its appeal is in the details: old Buddhist art, quiet courtyards, and the way it connects the main carving sites to the living religious setting around them.
