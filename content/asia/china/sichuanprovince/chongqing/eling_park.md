  Eling Park's most unusual feature is a pavilion built at the exact confluence of the Jialing and Yangtze rivers — a spot where you can watch two great rivers meet, their waters visibly different in colour and temperature where they first mingle. The Jialing runs clear and blue-green; the Yangtze runs brown with silt. The two streams run side by side for some distance before finally mixing, a phenomenon visible from the park's elevated vantage point.
---

Eling Park sits on a peninsula hill between the Jialing and Yangtze rivers, offering what many consider the best panoramic views in central Chongqing. The viewing pavilion looks out over the confluence of the two rivers and the city spread across the hills beyond — the classic image of Chongqing as the Mountain City.

The park itself is a pleasant green space popular with local families, with teahouses, walking paths, and shaded benches. It is less touristed than some other viewpoints and the atmosphere is relaxed. The park is historically significant as well — it contains the former wartime garden residence of Chiang Kai-shek, now a small museum. The morning light on the rivers is excellent for photography.
