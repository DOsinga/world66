  The Jialing River flows out of the mountains of northern Sichuan, running green and clear — noticeably different in colour and temperature from the brown Yangtze it meets at Chaotianmen. The contrast is most visible in winter and spring, when the rivers are at different levels and the colours remain distinct for kilometres downstream of the confluence. River monitoring stations track the exact point where the waters merge as a measure of upstream conditions.
---

The Jialing River Promenade runs along the north bank of the Jialing where it skirts the Yuzhong peninsula, offering a walking route between the Hongya Cave district and Chaotianmen Square. The promenade is popular in the evenings and gives views across the river to the Jiangbei District towers on the north bank.

A series of public spaces, gardens, and waterfront restaurants lines the route. The stretch near Hongya Cave is the most lively, with open-air food stalls and performance areas set up in the evenings during summer. In winter, the morning mist on the river creates atmospheric conditions for photography.
