Liziba station on Chongqing Metro Line 2 is worth visiting for a reason that has nothing to do with where you are going: the metro line passes through a 19-storey apartment building. The elevated viaduct enters the building, a full metro station operates inside floors 6 to 8, and the train exits the other side. Sound insulation was installed when the building was constructed around the existing elevated line, and residents claim they have adapted to it.

The station is easily reached from central Chongqing and is best appreciated from outside the building, where you can watch trains disappear into what appears to be an ordinary residential tower. The local attitude to this arrangement — matter-of-fact, entirely unsurprised — says something about how Chongqing has approached the challenge of building a city on vertical terrain.
