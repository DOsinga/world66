Nanshan is a forested ridge south of the Yangtze River, rising to about 295 metres and offering some of the most celebrated views over Chongqing. The One Tree Hill (一棵树观景台, Yī Kē Shù Guānjǐng Tái) viewpoint on the ridge is particularly popular for dawn and dusk views, when the city lights spread below in a carpet that stretches to the horizon.

The scenic area includes botanical gardens, several old villas from the wartime period (including the former residence of Song Meiling, Chiang Kai-shek's wife), and extensive walking trails through pine and camphor forest. The cable car from the city makes the ascent easy. The area is at its most dramatic in autumn and winter, when the fog rolls in from the Yangtze and the city appears as a pattern of lights in the grey below.
