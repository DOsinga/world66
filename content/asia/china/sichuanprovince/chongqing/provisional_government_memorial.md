Between 1940 and 1945, Chongqing hosted the Provisional Government of the Republic of Korea — the Korean government-in-exile that operated in China while Japan occupied the Korean peninsula. The memorial hall preserves the building where this government operated, telling the story of Korean independence activists and the Korean Liberation Army that fought alongside Chinese and American forces.

The exhibition is modest in scale but historically significant, particularly for visitors interested in Korean history. Bilingual signage in Chinese and Korean makes it accessible, and there is some English text as well. The site is a reminder that wartime Chongqing was genuinely cosmopolitan — the provisional capital hosted diplomatic missions, refugee governments, and foreign military commands from across the Allied world.
