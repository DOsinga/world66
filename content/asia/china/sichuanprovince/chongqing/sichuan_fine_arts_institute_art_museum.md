The Art Museum of Sichuan Fine Arts Institute, also known as the Luo Zhongli Art Museum, is one of Chongqing's larger art museums. It sits on the institute's Huxi Campus and presents modern and contemporary Chinese art, with a link to painter Luo Zhongli, one of the school's best-known graduates.

The museum is most useful for visitors who want to see Chongqing's art scene beyond the central riverfront sights. Check opening hours before making the trip out to the university district.
