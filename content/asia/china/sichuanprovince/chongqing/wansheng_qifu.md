  The Heishan Valley Scenic Area in Wansheng contains a section of the Shuanglong (Double Dragon) underground cave system — a chain of interconnected caverns carved by an underground river through limestone. The caves are 8 kilometres in accessible length, with stalactite formations, underground lakes, and a waterfall visible from walkways inside. It is one of the least-visited major cave systems near Chongqing and sees far fewer tourists than Furong Cave in Wulong.

Wansheng Economic Development Zone, about 100 kilometres south of central Chongqing in the coal-rich mountains near the Guizhou border, is home to a cluster of scenic areas including karst gorges, waterfalls, and cave systems. It is primarily a domestic tourism destination but is accessible from Chongqing by bus.

The Heishan Valley Scenic Area offers karst gorge scenery and cave access in a relatively unspoiled setting. The Shuanglong Cave system is impressive for its scale and its underground waterway. The area is significantly cooler than Chongqing city in summer — a practical advantage for heat-weary visitors — and the surrounding mountains are forested and attractive for hiking.
