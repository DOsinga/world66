Yuzhong is the historic peninsula at the heart of Chongqing, enclosed by the Jialing River to the north and the Yangtze to the south. This is where the original city developed, where the wartime capital was concentrated, and where most of the major museums, monuments, and commercial districts are found today.

The district is defined by its dramatic topography — streets climb steeply between river levels and ridge tops, connected by outdoor escalators (the longest system in China), lifts embedded in cliff faces, and a web of elevated walkways. Walking Yuzhong requires comfortable shoes and the acceptance that every destination involves significant climbing. The reward is a city that works vertically in ways no other urban centre quite matches.
