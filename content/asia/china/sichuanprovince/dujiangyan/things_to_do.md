Dujiangyan's centrepiece is the [Dujiangyan Irrigation System](/asia/china/sichuanprovince/dujiangyan/dujiangyan_irrigation_system), one of the great engineering achievements of antiquity and a UNESCO World Heritage Site still in active use after 2,300 years. The [Erwang Temple](/asia/china/sichuanprovince/dujiangyan/erwang_temple) sits on the hill above, honouring the father-and-son engineers who built it. Fifteen kilometres to the south, [Qingcheng Mountain](/asia/china/sichuanprovince/dujiangyan/qingcheng_mountain) offers a quieter alternative to the busy irrigation park: a forested Daoist sacred mountain with ancient temples scattered along its paths. The city also has its own [giant panda base](/asia/china/sichuanprovince/dujiangyan/dujiangyan_panda_base), operated by the China Conservation and Research Center for Giant Panda, with volunteer opportunities not available at the Chengdu facilities.
