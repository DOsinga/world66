---
title: Getting There
type: section
---

Leshan is about two hours south of Chengdu by bus. Buses depart from Chengdu's Xinnanmen bus station or the high-speed rail station area throughout the day. The journey by high-speed rail to Leshan station takes around 40 minutes; regular buses take closer to two hours depending on traffic.

From [Mount Emei](/asia/china/sichuanprovince/mountemei), Leshan is a straightforward connection — buses run between the two tourist sites regularly, and the two are commonly visited together in a single trip from Chengdu. The Giant Buddha and Mount Emei share UNESCO World Heritage Site status, inscribed together in 1996.

There is no significant air connection at Leshan; most visitors arrive via Chengdu.
