Luocheng Ancient Town is best known for its extraordinary boat-shaped main street, a long covered market lane whose raised central line and flanking eaves give the impression of a wooden vessel. The form is not just a visual trick: it creates shade, gathering space, and a strong sense of enclosure that makes the old street feel theatrical even on an ordinary market day.

The town is a worthwhile detour from Leshan for travelers interested in vernacular architecture and older Sichuan town life. It is smaller and less polished than the province's headline ancient towns, which is part of the appeal. Come for a walk, tea, and street food rather than a checklist of monuments.
