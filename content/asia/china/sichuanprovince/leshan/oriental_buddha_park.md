Oriental Buddha Park is a Buddhist-themed sculpture and grotto park near Leshan's famous Giant Buddha area. It expands the visit with carved figures, caves, and religious imagery that echo older Sichuan Buddhist traditions.

It is not as essential as the Giant Buddha itself, but it can add worthwhile depth for visitors with extra time. Expect a constructed attraction with religious art rather than an untouched ancient site.
