Pingqiang Little Three Gorges is a gentler Leshan river landscape, named for a sequence of smaller gorges on the Pingqiang River. It does not compete with the Giant Buddha for monumentality; its value is in water, cliffs, and a quieter look at the countryside around the city.

Consider it if you have already seen the main Buddha area or want a slower excursion. Boat availability and local conditions matter, so arrange details locally rather than assuming it operates like a fixed urban attraction.
