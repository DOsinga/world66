Baoguo Temple is one of the main temple stops around Mount Emei. It is known for its large bronze bell, traditionally said to be audible many kilometres away when rung.

The temple makes a good introduction before heading farther up the mountain, especially for visitors interested in Emei Shan's Buddhist heritage rather than only its summit views.
