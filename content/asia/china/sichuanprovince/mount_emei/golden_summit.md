Golden Summit is the high reward of Mount Emei: a dramatic peak crowned by the huge multi-faced statue of Samantabhadra and ringed, on clear days, by views over ridges and cloud. It is one of the mountain's classic pilgrimage sights, and the climb from Baoguo feels like a proper arrival when the summit finally opens out.
