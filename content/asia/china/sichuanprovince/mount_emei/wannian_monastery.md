Wannian Monastery is one of Mount Emei's major Buddhist temples and a natural break on the climb through the mountain's forested middle slopes. Its best-known treasure is the bronze statue of Puxian Bodhisattva seated on a white elephant, a powerful image in the mountain's devotional landscape.

Some of the complex has been rebuilt, but the atmosphere still works: incense, damp stone, monks moving through the courtyards, and cloud forest pressing close around the halls. It is worth planning into a slower Emei Shan itinerary rather than rushing past.
