---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

Panzhihua's microclimate is one of its most interesting features. The city sits in a deep valley where the surrounding mountains trap warmth, giving it a subtropical climate at a latitude that would normally be temperate. Winters (November–March) are mild and sunny — often reaching 20°C — which is why Panzhihua markets itself as a winter resort for Sichuan residents escaping the grey and damp of Chengdu.

Summer (June–September) brings heat and occasional heavy rain. The mountains around the city cool down significantly above 2,000 metres, making the Gesala area a viable summer escape from the valley. The Yi Torch Festival typically falls in late July.

Spring and autumn are pleasant but brief. The mango and litchi harvest (June–August) is a local event worth timing a visit around if you are passing through.
