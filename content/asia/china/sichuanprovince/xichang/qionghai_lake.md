  Qionghai Lake is the second-largest freshwater lake in Sichuan and is remarkable for its position: at 1,510 metres above sea level, ringed by mountains, with the city's permanent warmth and clear skies making the 35-kilometre cycling circuit around it usable eleven months of the year. The wetland on the lake's northwest shore was recognised as a National Wetland Park in 2014, and the bird populations — particularly in the migratory season — justify the designation.
---

Qionghai Lake sits immediately east of the city and defines Xichang's outdoor character more than any other feature. The 40-kilometre cycling route along its shores is flat, well-maintained, and one of the better urban cycling circuits in Sichuan — passing through wetland sections, small fishing villages, and the Dashiban ancient village area, with mountain views across the water throughout.

The wetland park on the northwestern shore has boardwalks through reed beds and viewing platforms over the water. Bird populations include resident species and winter migrants, and the park is particularly rich in waterfowl between October and March. The lake's surface is large enough to feel genuinely expansive rather than merely ornamental, and on clear days the reflection of Lushan Mountain in the water provides the classical Chinese landscape painting composition that the lake's popularity with painters suggests is not accidental.
