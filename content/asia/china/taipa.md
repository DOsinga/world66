Taipa is one of the two main islands that make up Macau's territory alongside the peninsula. It is now largely continuous with Macau city via the Cotai Strip — the reclaimed land between Taipa and [Coloane](/asia/china/coloane) that has become a dense concentration of casino resorts. The original village of Taipa, however, retains a pleasant Portuguese colonial character that is worth seeking out.

The [Taipa Houses Museum](/asia/china/taipa/taipa_houses_museum) is a row of five restored Portuguese villas overlooking a garden, offering a glimpse of how Macau's Portuguese community lived in the early 20th century. Nearby, the [Church of Our Lady of Carmel](/asia/china/taipa/our_lady_of_carmel) and several Tin Hau temples reflect the island's layered religious heritage. The village lanes — particularly [Rua do Cunha](/asia/china/taipa/rua_do_cunha) — have a good selection of traditional Macanese restaurants, making [Taipa Village](/asia/china/taipa/taipa_village) a welcome contrast to the casino strip.
