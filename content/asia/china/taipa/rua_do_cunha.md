Rua do Cunha, known colloquially as "Food Street," is the main pedestrian lane through Taipa Village and the most concentrated eating experience in Macau outside the casino buffets. The narrow lane is lined on both sides with snack stalls selling the Macanese specialities that distinguish the territory from mainland China and Hong Kong: pork chop buns, egg tarts, almond cookies, beef jerky, and the fennel-spiked pork sausage called chouriço.

Most stalls have been operating for decades and some approach legendary status. The egg tarts here — with a flaky pastry shell rather than the crisper Hong Kong style — are among the best in the territory. The crowds in the lane are intense on weekend afternoons; if you want to eat without fighting your way through tour groups, come on a weekday morning when the stalls are setting up.
