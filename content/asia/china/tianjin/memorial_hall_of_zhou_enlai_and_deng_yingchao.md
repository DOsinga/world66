This memorial museum follows the lives of Zhou Enlai and Deng Yingchao, two major figures in 20th-century Chinese politics who both had deep connections with Tianjin. The displays lean heavily on photographs, documents, personal objects, and revolutionary history.

It is most rewarding if you already have some interest in modern Chinese history. Among the more tangible exhibits are Zhou's car, printing equipment linked to the May Fourth era, and material from the couple's long public careers.
