Tianjin Art Museum is part of the city's modern cultural centre, with exhibitions focused on Chinese art and changing temporary shows. It is a useful indoor stop if you are exploring Tianjin beyond the concession streets and riverfront. Check current entry rules before going, as visits may require advance booking through WeChat.
