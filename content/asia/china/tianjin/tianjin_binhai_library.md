The Tianjin Binhai Library, designed by Dutch firm MVRDV and opened in 2017, is one of the most extraordinary public buildings in China. The centrepiece is a vast spherical auditorium wrapped in concentric rings of white bookshelves that climb from floor to ceiling on all sides, creating a dizzying, almost surreal interior. The building went viral worldwide shortly after opening.

The library is part of the Binhai Cultural Centre development, about 50 km east of central Tianjin in the Binhai New Area. Getting here takes about an hour from the city centre by metro and bus or taxi, so visiting makes most sense combined with other Binhai destinations. The building is free to enter and genuinely worth the journey for anyone interested in architecture — the interior is even more striking in person than in photographs.
