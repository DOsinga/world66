Tianjin Drum Tower stands at the centre of the old walled city. The original Ming-era tower was destroyed during the Cultural Revolution, but the rebuilt structure anchors a lively historic district and gives a useful sense of the old city's layout. It works best as part of a walk through the surrounding lanes and shops.
