Tianjin Haichang Polar Ocean World is one of Binhai's big family attractions, with penguins, seals, polar-themed exhibits, and large marine tanks under one roof. It is a useful stop if you are already visiting the Binhai Cultural Centre area and want something more kid-friendly than the architecture sights.
