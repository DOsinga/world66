Tianjin TEDA Aircraft Carrier Theme Park is built around the Kiev, a decommissioned Soviet aircraft carrier from 1970. Visitors can board the carrier and see naval displays that also include a destroyer and submarine.

The park mixes military-history exhibits with staged shows and family attractions, so it works best for visitors interested in Cold War hardware rather than a quiet museum visit.
