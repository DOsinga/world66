---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

Tianjin has a continental climate with four distinct seasons. **Spring (April–May)** and **autumn (September–October)** are the most comfortable times to visit — warm days, low humidity, and clear skies. Spring can be dusty from sandstorms blowing in from Mongolia, but this usually clears by late April.

**Summer (June–August)** is hot and humid, with temperatures regularly above 35°C and occasional heavy rain. Winters are cold and dry, with temperatures often dropping well below freezing and occasional snow. If you are visiting Beijing and considering a day trip to Tianjin, spring and autumn give the best conditions for walking the concession districts.

The **Chinese New Year** period (late January–February) shuts many businesses but the city is festive and lively. The **Mazu Birthday Festival** on the 23rd day of the third lunar month (April or May) brings traditional performances to Ancient Culture Street and the Tianhou Temple.
