---
title: Getting There
type: section
---

Gyantse is typically reached by road from [Lhasa](/asia/china/tibet/lhasa) (around 270 km to the northeast) or from [Shigatse](/asia/china/tibet/shigatse) (90 km to the northwest). The journey from Lhasa takes roughly six to eight hours by vehicle and passes through spectacular high-altitude scenery, including the turquoise Yamdrok Lake at around 4,400 metres.

All foreign visitors to Tibet require a Tibet Travel Permit in addition to a Chinese visa, and must travel with a licensed guide and registered vehicle. Independent travel in Tibet is not permitted — all transport arrangements go through a licensed Tibetan travel agency.

Most visitors pass through Gyantse as part of an organised overland tour of Tibet, stopping for a half-day or overnight before continuing to Shigatse or toward the Nepali border.
