The Yak Museum of Tibet is devoted to the animal that has shaped Tibetan transport, food, clothing, and high-altitude life for centuries. It is a focused museum, but the subject is central enough to Tibetan culture that it makes sense after the larger religious and palace sights.

Visitor numbers are limited, so check current access before making the trip across town.
