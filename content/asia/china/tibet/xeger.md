**Xeger** (also spelled Shelkar or New Tingri) is a small town at 4,350 metres in southern Tibet, built at the foot of the ruins of [Xegar Dzong](/asia/china/tibet/xeger/xegar_dzong), an old hilltop fortress. It sits just off the Friendship Highway between [Shigatse](/asia/china/tibet/shigatse) and the Nepalese border.

The town is primarily a stopping point for expeditions heading to Mount Everest — the [turnoff to Everest Base Camp](/asia/china/tibet/xeger/everest_base_camp_turnoff) is about 10 km west. The [Pang La pass](/asia/china/tibet/xeger/pang_la_pass) on that road, at 5,050 metres, offers on clear mornings a panorama of five 8,000-metre peaks at once. The [dzong ruins](/asia/china/tibet/xeger/xegar_dzong) above the town are worth the 45-minute climb for views across the Tingri plain. At this altitude, take it easy — the thin air is felt immediately, and acute mountain sickness is a real risk for anyone who has not had adequate acclimatisation time.
