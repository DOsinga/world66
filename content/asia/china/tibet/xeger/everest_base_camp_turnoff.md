The turnoff from the Friendship Highway to Everest Base Camp (Chinese side) is about 10 km west of Xeger. The road climbs through the Pang La pass, crosses a plateau, and eventually reaches Rongbuk Monastery and then the base camp at 5,150 metres — a total of about 70 km of rough paved and dirt road from the highway.

The journey takes about two hours from Xeger. A Tibet Tourism Bureau permit is required in addition to the standard Tibet Autonomous Region permit, and these must be arranged in Lhasa or at a registered travel agency before setting out. Independent travel to the base camp road in a rented vehicle is not permitted; the route requires a registered guide and vehicle.
