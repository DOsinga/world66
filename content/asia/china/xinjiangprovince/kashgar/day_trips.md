Kashgar is the best base in western China for exploring the surrounding landscape and Silk Road sites. The Karakoram Highway south offers some of the most dramatic mountain scenery on earth within a few hours of the city. The Southern Silk Road to the east, toward Yarkand and Hotan, passes through oasis towns with their own histories and handicraft traditions.
