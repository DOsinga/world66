Ayding Lake sits in the Turpan Depression, a stark salt-lake basin often cited among the lowest places in China. The attraction is the desert geography: heat, glare, salt flats, and a sense of how extreme the Turpan landscape can be.

This is a specialist landscape stop, not a lush lake outing. Go with transport arranged, carry water, and avoid treating the midday heat as a minor inconvenience.
