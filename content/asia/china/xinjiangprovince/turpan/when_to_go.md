---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

Turpan has one of the most extreme climates in China — the Turpan Depression, at 154 metres below sea level, is the second-lowest land point in Asia and one of the hottest. Summer (June through August) regularly exceeds 45°C, with overnight lows still in the 30s. Even with shade and the legendary local grapes and watermelons, midday outside is brutal.

The most comfortable windows are **April–May** and **late September–October**, when daytime temperatures are 20–30°C and the desert basin is at its most photogenic. Late September coincides with the grape harvest and the annual Turpan Grape Festival.

**Winter (December–February)** is cold and quiet, with daytime highs near or below freezing. Many smaller sites close or run reduced hours. Spring sandstorms occasionally blow in from late March into May; check the forecast.
