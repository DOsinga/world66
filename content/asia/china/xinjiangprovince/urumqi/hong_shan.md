Hong Shan — Red Mountain — is the most prominent natural feature in central Ürümqi, a sandstone hill rising above the surrounding urban grid with a distinctive Qing dynasty pagoda on its summit. The climb is short, 20 to 30 minutes at a gentle pace up paved paths, and the view from the top is the best in the city: a panorama of Ürümqi's sprawl, with the Tian Shan range visible on clear days to the south, and the Junggar Basin stretching north toward Kazakhstan.

It is a popular spot for morning exercise, and you are likely to share the paths with older locals doing tai chi or walking fast laps. The park around the base is well maintained, with pavilions and a small lake. It is free to enter the park and modest to climb to the pagoda itself. As a half-morning outing combined with breakfast in the nearby streets, it is one of the most pleasant ways to begin a day in Ürümqi.
