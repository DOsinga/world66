Hanren Street Grand Bazaar is a lively local market where Yining's Kazakh, Uyghur, and Chinese influences are easy to see in one place. Stalls sell embroidery, carpets, dried fruit, spices, and everyday goods.

It is best visited slowly, with time to browse and bargain politely. The appeal is less any single stall than the dense market atmosphere.
