The Ili Kazakh Autonomous Prefecture Museum is one of the main museums in Xinjiang, with archaeological and ethnological collections from across the Ili Valley. It is a useful stop for context before visiting Kazanchi and the surrounding region, especially for its material on the valley's Kazakh, Uyghur, Hui, and Russian-influenced cultures.
