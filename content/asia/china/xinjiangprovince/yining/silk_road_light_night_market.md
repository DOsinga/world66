Silk Road Light Night Market is one of Yining's livelier evening stops, with food stalls, lights, and the easy bustle that makes a night market more than a place to eat. Lamb skewers, noodles, and local snacks are the main draw.

Come after dark rather than in the afternoon. It works best as a low-key evening wander, especially if you want a quick taste of local street food before heading back across town.
