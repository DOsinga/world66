type: section
---

Yining's best assets are its landscapes and its layered cultural character. The Kazanchi district in the city, the lavender fields of Huocheng County, the Nalati Grasslands, and the road to Sayram Lake through Guozigou valley give four completely different experiences within a day's travel of the city. The appeal is in the combination rather than any single landmark.
