Shibao Mountain is a rugged temple-and-scenery area known for strange rock formations and old religious sites. Baoxiang Temple, Jinding Temple, and other buildings from the Yuan, Ming, and Qing periods sit among the cliffs and forest.

It is a strong day trip for travelers who want a more rural historic landscape beyond Dali's old town and lakefront villages.
