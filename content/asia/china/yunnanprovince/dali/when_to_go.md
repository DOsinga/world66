---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

Dali sits at 2,000 metres and is famous in China for its mild climate — locals call it the city of 'wind, flowers, snow, and moon'. It is comfortable to visit any time of year, but the seasons differ.

**March to May** is arguably the best — clear skies, wildflowers on Cangshan, water and air both pleasant. **June to August** is the wet season, with afternoon thunderstorms and occasional heavy rain; it is also the warmest and the busiest, as Chinese domestic tourism peaks. **September to November** is mild and dry, with good visibility for mountain views. **December to February** is cool but not cold — daytime temperatures around 12–15°C, dropping toward freezing at night. Snow occasionally dusts the Cangshan summits in winter, which is the source of the 'snow' in the local motto.

Avoid the Chinese national holidays — May Day (1–5 May), National Day (1–7 October), and Lunar New Year — when the old town is packed and accommodation triples in price.
