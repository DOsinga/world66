The Burma Road History Museum looks at one of Kunming's most important wartime connections: the road that linked southwest China with Burma and became a vital supply route during the Second World War. The subject is bigger than the building, touching on engineers, soldiers, truck convoys, and the geography that made Yunnan strategically crucial.

It is a specialist museum, so it works best for travellers already interested in modern Chinese history or the old overland routes into Southeast Asia. Even a short visit helps explain why Kunming's position mattered far beyond its pleasant climate and lake scenery.
