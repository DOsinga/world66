Kunming North Station is the city terminus of the old meter-gauge Kunming-Hekou railway. The adjoining Yunnan Railway Museum covers the line's engineering, rolling stock, and role in linking Yunnan with Vietnam.

It is a useful stop for rail enthusiasts and for anyone interested in how geography shaped transport in southwest China.
