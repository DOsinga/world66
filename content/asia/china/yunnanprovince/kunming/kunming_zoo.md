Kunming Zoo is a long-running city zoo near Yuantong Temple, with animal enclosures, a peacock garden, a butterfly area, an aviary, and an aquarium. It is most pleasant in spring, when the grounds fill with blossoms, especially cherry trees and weeping Chinese crab apples.
