Victory over Japan Memorial Hall was completed in 1947, soon after the end of the Second World War, and is one of China's early postwar memorial commissions. It stands as a national heritage site in central Kunming.

The hall is a concise history stop rather than a long museum visit. Pair it with nearby Wuhua District sights such as Green Lake and the Yunnan Military Academy.
