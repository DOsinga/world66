Yunnan Railway Museum tells the story of the narrow-gauge lines that linked Kunming and Yunnan with Vietnam and the wider region. The indoor section has maps, models, documents, and bilingual displays, while the trainshed shows locomotives and rolling stock from different periods and countries.

It is a specialist museum, but a rewarding one if you are interested in railways, border history, or the French-Chinese engineering projects that shaped modern Yunnan.
