Yunnan Wildlife Park is a large zoological park on Kunming's northern side, with animal exhibits that lean into the province's biodiversity. It is not a wilderness experience, but it does give families and casual visitors an accessible look at regional wildlife themes.

The park is a secondary Kunming attraction, best suited to travellers with children or extra time after the city's stronger cultural and landscape sights. Check transport and opening details before going, since it sits well outside the most walkable central districts.
