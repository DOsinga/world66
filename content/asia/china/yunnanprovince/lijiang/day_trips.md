Lijiang is one of the best-placed bases in Yunnan for reaching the dramatic high country of the northwest. Two of the most rewarding destinations — [Tiger Leaping Gorge](/asia/china/yunnanprovince/tiger_leaping_gorge) and [Shangri-La](/asia/china/yunnanprovince/shangrila) — are within a half-day's travel, and most travellers use Lijiang as a staging point rather than a destination in isolation.

**Tiger Leaping Gorge** (about 90 minutes north by bus) is one of the deepest gorges in the world, and the high trail along its north rim is consistently rated among the finest multi-day walks in China. The classic route takes two days with an overnight at a guesthouse mid-trail. It is possible as a day trip to the gorge entrance, but doing the trek requires at least one night.

**Shangri-La** is 4–5 hours north by bus. Most travellers spend a night or two there rather than doing it as a day trip, but the journey itself — past high Tibetan grasslands and dramatic mountain scenery — is worthwhile. Songzanlin Monastery, the largest Tibetan Buddhist monastery in Yunnan, is the main sight.

**Shaxi**, a beautifully preserved market town on the ancient Tea Horse Road, is about 3 hours south. Its Friday market is genuine rather than staged, and the old caravan square (Sideng Square) is a UNESCO-listed monument. Shaxi sees a fraction of the tourist traffic that passes through Lijiang.
