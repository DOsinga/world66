Dongba Valley sits north of Lijiang on the way toward Jade Dragon Snow Mountain, presenting Naxi culture, mountain scenery, and constructed visitor trails in one package. It is more managed than a village walk, but it can help travellers understand the Dongba religious and artistic identity that threads through the region.

The setting is the strongest part: cliffs, water, and the mountain approach give the visit a different mood from Lijiang's old-town lanes. Go early if combining it with other sights on the snow-mountain road.
