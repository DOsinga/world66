Gold Dragon Bridge is a historic stone bridge north of Lijiang, valued less as a modern crossing than as a reminder of the region's older routes, river landscapes, and Naxi-area settlement patterns. Its distance from the old town means it feels more like a countryside excursion than an urban landmark.

It is worth considering if you are already exploring beyond central Lijiang and want a quieter heritage stop. The bridge is not essential for a first visit, but it adds texture to the region's network of villages, streams, and old roads.
