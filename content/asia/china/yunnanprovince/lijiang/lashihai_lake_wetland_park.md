Lashihai Lake Wetland Park spreads across a high basin west of Lijiang, where water, grassland and villages sit below the snowy mountains. The lake is known for migratory birds and for the open rural scenery that contrasts with the lanes of Lijiang old town.

Tourism here can be uneven, with horse rides and packaged outings varying in quality. Go for the landscape and birdlife rather than a scripted attraction, and be clear about prices before accepting rides or extras.
