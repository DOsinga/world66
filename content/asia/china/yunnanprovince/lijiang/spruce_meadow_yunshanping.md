Spruce Meadow, or Yunshanping, is a high meadow on the eastern side of Jade Dragon Snow Mountain. The grassland sits inside spruce forest at about 3,240 m, with views toward the snowy peaks and paths through a landscape tied to Naxi love legends.

It is usually visited as part of the Jade Dragon Snow Mountain area. The cableway makes access easier, but the altitude can still be tiring, so give yourself time and bring warm layers.
