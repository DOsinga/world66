The Daying River is one of the landscape features that gives Ruili its border-town character, running through a subtropical area shaped by Dai culture, trade routes, and proximity to Myanmar. Riverside views and bridges help orient visitors in a city where the frontier is never far away.

It is best approached as part of a wider Ruili wander rather than as a single formal attraction. The river adds atmosphere to visits that also include markets, border sights, and nearby parks.
