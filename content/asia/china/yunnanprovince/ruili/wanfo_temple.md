Wanfo Temple is one of Ruili's visible Buddhist landmarks, with Southeast Asian influences that fit the city's position on the Myanmar border. It is a calm stop amid a town better known for jade trading and cross-border commerce.

The temple is most interesting for the way it shows Ruili's cultural mix: Chinese administration, Dai traditions, Burmese connections, and Theravada Buddhist forms all close together. Visit respectfully and treat it as an active religious site, not just a photo stop.
