The biggest day-trip pull from Shangri-La is **Tiger Leaping Gorge**, two hours south on the road to [Lijiang](/asia/china/yunnanprovince/lijiang) — one of the deepest river gorges in the world, with an excellent two-day high trail along its northern rim. Going on foot is the way to do it: from the trailhead at Qiaotou you walk over two days to Tina's Guesthouse at the gorge bottom, with simple guesthouses along the route.

For shorter trips, **Pudacuo National Park** and **Napa Lake** are both within an hour, **Shika Snow Mountain** is at the town's back door, and the **Songzanlin Monastery** is twenty minutes from the Old Town and gets nearly every visitor.

Pushing on, the road north to **Deqin** and the Meili Snow Mountain (about eight hours) is the gateway to the eastern Tibetan plateau. Most travellers continue this way rather than treating Deqin as a day trip — there is plenty to do up there.
