---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

Shangri-La is at altitude and the weather is more mountain than south China. **Summer (June–September)** has the best temperatures — 15–22°C during the day — and the wildflowers on the grasslands are at their peak. It is also the wettest time; expect afternoon rain, particularly in July and August, and occasional roadblocks from landslides on the road north.

**Spring and autumn** are reliable shoulder seasons. May has wildflowers without the summer crowds; October has clear, dry weather and the autumn colours on the slopes; both are good times for hiking.

**Winter (December–February)** is cold — below freezing most nights, and well below by day in January — but it is the season for black-necked cranes at Napa Lake and the views are at their clearest. Snow can close the road over the passes to Deqin for days at a time; check before you commit to a route north.

The Horse Racing Festival, usually in late May or early June, is the major local event — Tibetan riders from across the region come to compete on the grassland south of town.
