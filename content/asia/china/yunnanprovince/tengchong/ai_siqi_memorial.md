The Ai Siqi Memorial Hall is a small museum dedicated to the Tengchong-born philosopher and sociologist. Exhibits cover his life, writings, and place in modern Chinese thought, making it a useful stop for visitors already exploring Heshun and Tengchong's historic sites.
