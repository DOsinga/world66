Tengchong Confucius Temple is a reminder that the city was not only a frontier trading post and wartime battleground, but also a county seat with the institutions of traditional Chinese learning. Its halls, courtyards, and ceremonial layout follow the grammar of Confucian civic architecture.

The temple is a useful in-town stop between Tengchong's more famous hot springs, volcanic landscapes, and Second World War memorials. Visit for a quieter look at local history and the scholar-official culture that shaped old county towns across Yunnan.
