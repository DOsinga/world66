Yiluo Pool is a small scenic lake on the south side of Yiluo Park, useful as a gentle pause while exploring Tengchong. The area folds together water views, a Buddhist temple, a pagoda, and World War II memorials, so it works best as part of a wider walk rather than as a single destination.
