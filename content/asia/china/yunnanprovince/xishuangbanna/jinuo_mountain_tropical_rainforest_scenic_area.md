Jinuo Mountain Tropical Rainforest Scenic Area combines dense Xishuangbanna forest with visits focused on Jinuo culture. The appeal is the setting as much as the displays: humid paths, thick greenery and a look at one of Yunnan's smaller ethnic groups in its home landscape.
