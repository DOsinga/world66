Mengjinglai Scenic Area is a living Dai village near the China-Myanmar border. Traditional stilt houses, wells, temples, weaving, farming, and sugar-making are still part of daily life, so the visit feels less like a single monument and more like a compact cultural landscape.

It is a useful stop for travelers using Xishuangbanna as a base for Dai villages and border-country excursions.
