Holy Lake is a high mountain lake south of Yubeng, reached by a demanding full-day hike from Lower Yubeng. The route climbs through forest and alpine terrain, and the lake is best suited to well-prepared hikers who can handle a long, steep trail and fast-changing mountain weather.
