Yuxi Kiln Site preserves the remains of an old ceramics kiln believed to date from the late Yuan or early Ming dynasty. It is a small, specialist stop, but it adds useful depth to Yuxi's ceramic and craft history.

Visit if you are already exploring Yuxi's historic sights or have a particular interest in ceramics. Pair it with Yuxi Museum for a fuller sense of the area's past.
