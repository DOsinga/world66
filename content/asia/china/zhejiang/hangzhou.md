Hangzhou is one of China's most celebrated cities, famous since Marco Polo reportedly called it the finest city in the world. The claim still has some force: [West Lake](/asia/china/zhejiang/hangzhou/west_lake), right at the city's centre, is a genuinely beautiful expanse of water ringed by pagodas, willow-lined causeways and wooded hills. It's been a place of literary and artistic pilgrimage for a thousand years, and even with the crowds it manages to feel serene in the early morning.

The lake's surroundings reward slow exploration on foot or by bike. The Su Causeway, [Broken Bridge](/asia/china/zhejiang/hangzhou/west_lake), and [Leifeng Pagoda](/asia/china/zhejiang/hangzhou/leifeng_pagoda) are the classic sights, but the gardens and tea plantations in the hills above the western shore are just as good. The [Longjing (Dragon Well) tea village](/asia/china/zhejiang/hangzhou/longjing_tea) grown here is among the finest green tea in China — a walk through the terraced fields and a cup at a farmhouse teahouse is something you won't want to skip. The [China National Tea Museum](/asia/china/zhejiang/hangzhou/china_national_tea_museum) in the tea hills explains the whole story.

The [Yue Fei Temple](/asia/china/zhejiang/hangzhou/yue_fei_temple) on the northern shore is a monument to one of China's most beloved military heroes and one of the most visited historic sites in the country. The [Zhejiang Provincial Museum](/asia/china/zhejiang/hangzhou/zhejiang_provincial_museum) on Lone Hill island has Neolithic jade that predates anything most museums can offer. Beyond the lake, Hangzhou has a strong old-town area around [Hefang Street](/asia/china/zhejiang/hangzhou/hefang_street), the impressive Song Dynasty [Six Harmonies Pagoda](/asia/china/zhejiang/hangzhou/six_harmonies_pagoda) at the Qiantang River, and a well-regarded local cuisine built around river fish and the local tea.

It's under an hour from Shanghai by high-speed train, which makes it the most popular day trip from that city — aim to stay overnight if you can to see it without the worst of the crowds.
