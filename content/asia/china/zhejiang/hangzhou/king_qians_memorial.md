King Qian's Memorial stands near the south end of West Lake and commemorates the rulers of the Wuyue Kingdom, which made Hangzhou an important regional capital before the Song dynasty.

It is a quiet historical stop to add to a West Lake walk, especially for visitors interested in the city's pre-imperial and early medieval history.
