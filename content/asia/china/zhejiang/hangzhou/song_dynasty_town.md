Song Dynasty Town, often known as Songcheng, is a large historical theme park on the southwestern side of Hangzhou. It recreates a theatrical version of Song-era streets, shops, gates, and folk scenes, with the emphasis less on quiet authenticity and more on scale, performance, and crowd-pleasing spectacle.

The headline draw is the big stage show, which mixes dance, acrobatics, water effects, and Hangzhou history into a polished tourist production. It is a very different experience from West Lake or Lingyin Temple, but it suits travelers who want an evening show or a family-friendly break from classical sightseeing.
