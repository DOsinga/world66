---
title: Things to Do
type: section
---

Hengdian's entire appeal rests on the film studio complex, but within that complex there is more to see than a single afternoon suggests. The main draw is wandering between active shooting locations and the permanent set pieces — the Forbidden City replica, the Qin palace, the Ming-dynasty streets — but the human spectacle of thousands of extras and would-be actors living and working in a purpose-built film town has its own fascination.
