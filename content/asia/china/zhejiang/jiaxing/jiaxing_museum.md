Jiaxing Museum is the city's main general museum, with collections covering local archaeology, history, and culture. The displays give useful background before or after visiting South Lake, Meiwan Street, and the city's revolutionary-history sites.

The museum is a practical stop for understanding Jiaxing beyond its best-known lake scenery and Communist Party history.
