The Nanhu Revolutionary Memorial Hall is Jiaxing's key modern-history museum, tied to the South Lake story in the founding narrative of the Chinese Communist Party. The newer branch gives the subject more space and a formal museum setting.

It is a strong complement to South Lake itself. Visit the museum first if you want the political context, then walk the lakefront to see why this otherwise gentle water landscape carries national symbolic weight.
