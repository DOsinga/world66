Chao Yindong is a coastal rock site on Mount Putuo, known for cliffs carved with characters attributed to the Kangxi era of the Qing dynasty.

It is a short scenic stop rather than a full destination, but it fits naturally into a Mount Putuo visit that links the island's temples, sea cliffs, and pilgrimage paths.
