Baiyun Manor is a quiet historic house in Ningbo, with origins in the 1300s and a later life as an exclusive family school. Wooden plaques explain the family and its students in Chinese and English, while canals and a small garden make the visit more peaceful than most central-city sights.

It is best for travellers who enjoy low-key historic houses rather than headline monuments. The setting is calm, but the appeal depends on being interested in the family history and old Ningbo.
