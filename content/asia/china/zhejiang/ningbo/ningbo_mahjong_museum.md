The Ningbo Mahjong Museum occupies the former home of Chen Yumen, who is credited with shaping modern mahjong. The displays are modest, but they give useful context to a game that spread from eastern China into homes, clubs, and gambling rooms around the world.

It works best as a short stop if you are already exploring central Ningbo and want something more specific than another general history museum.
