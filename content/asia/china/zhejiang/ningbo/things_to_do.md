---
title: Things to Do
type: section
---

Ningbo is a city of layered histories — ancient ports, Ming dynasty libraries, colonial treaty-port architecture, and modern industrial sprawl. The historic core around TianYi Pavilion and the old Yong River waterfront is compact and walkable, and the city's museums rank among the best in Zhejiang. Day trips to the Zhoushan archipelago and Mount Putuo extend the range considerably.
