Tianning Temple Pagoda is an old brick pagoda from a former temple in central Ningbo. Today it stands in a tight urban setting between the sidewalk and road, which makes its survival more striking than its scale.

It is a quick architectural stop near the older parts of the city rather than a full temple visit.
