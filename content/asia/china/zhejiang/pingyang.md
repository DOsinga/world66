Pingyang is a county-level destination south of [Wenzhou](/asia/china/zhejiang/wenzhou), with its main towns at Kunyang and Aojiang. The urban centre is useful rather than beautiful, but the county around it has enough substance to justify a detour if you are already travelling through southern Zhejiang.

The strongest reason to come is the contrast packed into a small area. Offshore, the [Nanji Islands](/asia/china/zhejiang/pingyang/nanji_islands) have beaches, clear water, seafood, and protected marine life. Inland, [South Yandang Mountain](/asia/china/zhejiang/pingyang/south_yandang_mountain) offers crags, streams, temples, and old paths. In the hills, [Shunxi Ancient Village](/asia/china/zhejiang/pingyang/shunxi_ancient_village) keeps a quieter architectural story alive.

Pingyang is not a polished international stop. English is limited, distances inside the county are larger than they look on a map, and Nanji usually needs a ferry plan rather than a casual afternoon. With a little effort, though, it gives you a useful slice of coastal Zhejiang beyond Hangzhou and the better-known water towns.
