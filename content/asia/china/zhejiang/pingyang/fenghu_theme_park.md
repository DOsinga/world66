Fenghu Theme Park is a lakefront park in Pingyang, with paths, open water, and mountain views around Fenghu Lake. It is a low-key place rather than a thrill park: come for a walk, a cycle, or a quiet evening by the water.

The park is at its best near sunset, when the lake gives the town a softer edge and locals come out for an easy stroll.
