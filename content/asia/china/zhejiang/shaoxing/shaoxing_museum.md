Shaoxing Museum is a useful overview stop for a city dense with literary sites, canals, gardens, and wine culture. Its collections introduce local archaeology and regional history, giving context before visiting Lu Xun's Native Place or Shen Garden.

It is most valuable early in a Shaoxing visit. The museum will not replace the atmosphere of the old lanes, but it helps connect the city's individual sights into a longer historical story.
