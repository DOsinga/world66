---
title: Things to Do
type: section
---

Shaoxing's historic district is compact and best explored on foot or by the traditional black-awning boat (*wupeng chuan*) that has been the city's signature transport for centuries. The canal system, the literary heritage, and the preserved old lanes around the Lu Xun quarter are the city's main attractions; the rice wine culture adds a vivid and distinctive food and drink dimension.
