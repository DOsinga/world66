A-Thousand-Feet Waterfall is part of the Qiongtai Fairy Valley scenic area on Tiantai Mountain. The falls drop in two stages into a pool deep in the gorge, making it a worthwhile nature stop for visitors already exploring the mountain's temples and trails.

It is best treated as part of a Tiantai Mountain day out rather than a separate trip.
