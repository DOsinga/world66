Chicheng Scenic Area is a conglomerate hill near Tiantai, marked by cliffs, caves, temples, monasteries, and a pagoda above the town. It is one of the easier scenic stops in the Tiantaishan area and combines religious sites with short climbs and views.

Access can vary when restoration work is under way, so check locally before planning a long visit. Even a partial visit is useful if you want a scenic stop close to town.
