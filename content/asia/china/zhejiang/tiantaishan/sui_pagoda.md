Sui Pagoda is a nine-storey hexagonal brick pagoda near Guoqing Temple on Tiantai Mountain. Built in the Sui dynasty and standing about 59 metres high, it is one of China's older surviving brick pagodas. The wooden roof structures have long since burned away, leaving the brick tower stark and weathered.
