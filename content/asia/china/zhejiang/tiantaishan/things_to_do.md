title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Tiantai Mountain rewards slow exploration. The main monastic complex at [Guoqing Temple](guoqing_temple) anchors the visit, but the mountain repays those who push further up the trails to the waterfalls and summit plateau. A full day covers the monastery and the lower forest; two days lets you reach the quieter upper reaches and explore some of the smaller shrines scattered along the ridges.
