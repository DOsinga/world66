Wenzhou is the third city of [Zhejiang](/asia/china/zhejiang) Province and one of the economic powerhouses of the region. Famed for its businessmen and women (and the *Wenzhou model* of private enterprise), Wenzhou is frequently used by Western media as a barometer of the Chinese export economy — among other things, the city manufactures around 90% of the world's spectacles and massive quantities of shoes.

Wenzhou is not on many China tour itineraries and, despite being a cultural hotspot, it is not the most foreigner-friendly of cities. However, there are plenty of things to do and see should you find yourself with time on your hands. The city sits on the [Ou River](/asia/china/zhejiang/wenzhou/ou_river_waterfront) with mountains behind it, and the nearby [Yandang Mountains](/asia/china/zhejiang/yandangshan) are one of the more dramatic geological landscapes in eastern China. [Jiangxin Island](/asia/china/zhejiang/wenzhou/jiangxin_island) in the middle of the river offers ancient pagodas and city views, and Wenzhou's remarkable community of Christians — it is known as "China's Jerusalem" — has left a striking architectural legacy in the form of [St. Paul's Cathedral](/asia/china/zhejiang/wenzhou/st_pauls_cathedral) and numerous other churches.
