Epoch Art Museum gives Wenzhou a contemporary-art stop beyond the city's stronger historical and civic museums. Its exhibitions focus on modern Chinese visual culture, changing shows, and a purpose-built gallery setting rather than a fixed encyclopaedic collection.

Check the current programme before crossing town, because a visit depends heavily on what is installed. When the exhibitions are active, it is a useful addition for travellers who want to see Wenzhou as a living commercial city rather than only through temples, islands, and older streets.
