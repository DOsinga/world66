---
title: "Getting Around"
type: section
---

Within Wenzhou, city buses and a developing metro system cover the main areas, but taxis and ride-hailing apps (Didi) are the most practical option for moving between sights. The city centre is compact enough for walking around the Ou River waterfront and into Jiangxin Island. For Yandang Mountains and Dongtou, buses and trains are available from the main bus station and Wenzhou South station respectively. No English-language navigation support should be assumed — a translation app and downloaded offline maps are essential tools for independent travel here.
