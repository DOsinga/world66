Jingshan Park is a large green hill park overlooking Wenzhou and the Huanglong area. It is a straightforward place for a walk, with trees, paths, and viewpoints that give a break from the dense city below.

The park is most useful for travellers who want an easy outdoor stop within Wenzhou rather than a full mountain excursion.
