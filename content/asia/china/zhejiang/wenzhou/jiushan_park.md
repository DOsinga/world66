Jiushan Park is a compact city park with a lake, paths, and a tall pagoda that gives broad views over Wenzhou. It is not a major destination by itself, but it is a pleasant central stop when you want greenery and a quick viewpoint.

The park is most worthwhile if you are already exploring central Wenzhou and want an easy break from the surrounding streets.
