The Ou River defines the geography of Wenzhou, flowing east through the city before reaching the coast. The waterfront promenade along the southern bank offers the classic view of Jiangxin Island and the East Pagoda, and is a genuine gathering place for local life — older residents doing tai chi at dawn, families walking in the evening, fishing boats and commercial traffic on the water.

The promenade stretches for several kilometres and is well-maintained, with the ferry pier for Jiangxin Island accessible midway along. Wenzhou's commercial skyline makes an effective backdrop, a reminder that this is one of China's wealthiest mid-sized cities — the glass towers and the ancient pagoda visible from the same viewpoint tells the story of modern Wenzhou concisely.
