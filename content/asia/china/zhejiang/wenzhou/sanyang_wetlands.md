Sanyang Wetlands is one of Wenzhou's most distinctive urban nature areas, a watery landscape of channels, islands, reeds and village traces inside the expanding city. Boardwalks and boat routes make the wetland accessible without erasing its low, amphibious character.

It is a strong counterpoint to Wenzhou's commercial energy and mountain day trips. Go for reflections, birdlife and the sense of a delta city still negotiating with water.
