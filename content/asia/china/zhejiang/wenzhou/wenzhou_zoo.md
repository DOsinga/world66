Wenzhou Zoo is a family-oriented stop in the hills west of the city, often combined with the surrounding parkland. The animal collection and grounds make it a straightforward half-day for travelers with children or anyone looking for an easy outdoor break.

It is not Wenzhous most distinctive sight, but it fills a useful role in a city where many highlights are temples, islands, wetlands, or mountain excursions. Go for a relaxed local outing rather than a major zoological collection.
