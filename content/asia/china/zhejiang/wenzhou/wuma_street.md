Wuma Street is Wenzhou's best-known pedestrian street, a busy central strip of snack shops, local brands, and evening crowds. It is the place to go when you want a quick taste of the city without planning too hard.

The draw is the street life rather than any single monument. Come hungry, wander slowly, and use it as an introduction to Wenzhou's local snacks and small-shop culture.
