Xikou is a small town in the mountains south of Ningbo, best known as the birthplace of Chiang Kai-shek. His [former residences and ancestral halls](/asia/china/zhejiang/xikou/chiang_birthplace) are scattered through the town and draw a steady stream of Chinese visitors, though few foreign tourists make the trip.

The setting is genuinely beautiful — the Shanxi River runs through the centre, flanked by [old stone buildings](/asia/china/zhejiang/xikou/xikou_old_street) and dense green hills. The [Xuedou Temple complex](/asia/china/zhejiang/xikou/xuedou_temple) nearby is one of the largest Buddhist monasteries in eastern China, and a 33-metre seated Maitreya Buddha was added in 2008. The mountain trails around Xuedou offer good half-day hikes with views over the valley, passing two exceptional waterfalls.
