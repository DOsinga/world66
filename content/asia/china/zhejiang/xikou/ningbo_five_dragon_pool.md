Ningbo Five Dragon Pool is a mountain park with small waterfalls, scenic trails, and an old temple in the hills west of Ningbo. The walks are the main reason to come, with views opening up across the surrounding ranges.

Visitors who do not want the full climb can use local taxi service for easier access to the higher viewpoints.
