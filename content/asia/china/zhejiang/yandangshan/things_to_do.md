title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

The Yandang Mountains are organized into several separately ticketed scenic areas, each requiring two to four hours to explore properly. [Lingfeng](lingfeng_scenic_area) has the most dramatic rock formations and the cave temple; [Dalongqiu](dalongqiu_waterfall) has the highest waterfall; [Lingyan](lingyan_scenic_area) has the theatrical cliffs and the Nine-Storey Temple. Most visitors combine two or three areas in a full day. An overnight in the valley gives time to explore the quieter outer sections that see few visitors.
