Xianqiao Bridge Scenic Area is one of Yandangshan's quieter scenic zones, centred on a natural bridge and surrounded by the usual Yandang mix of cliffs, caves, and weathered rock forms. It is less famous than Lingfeng or Lingyan, which can be a good thing if you want the mountains with fewer tour groups.

The scenery is the main draw rather than any single monument. Come prepared for paths, steps, and changing weather, as with the rest of the Yandang Mountains.
