---
title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Zhoushan's attractions are spread across an archipelago of over 1,300 islands. [Mount Putuo](mount_putuo) on Putuoshan Island is the main draw for most visitors — a sacred Buddhist mountain with forested trails, temple complexes, and a genuine pilgrimage atmosphere. [Shenjiamen Fish Market](shenjiamen_fish_market) on Putuo Island shows the other face of the archipelago: the industrial seafood port that makes Zhoushan one of the world's most important fishing hubs. For beaches, [Zhujiajian Island](zhujiajian_island) has the best of Zhejiang's sand; for remoteness, the [Shengsi Islands](shengsi_islands) at the outer edge of the archipelago offer a very different experience from the well-visited inner islands.
