Pernera Beach is a small beach near Ayia Napa with an unusual underwater draw: the MUSAN underwater sculpture gallery by Jason deCaires Taylor lies offshore and can be explored by divers. It is most interesting for visitors planning time in the water rather than only a quick beach stop.
