The sea caves near Cape Greco are one of the prettiest pieces of coast around Ayia Napa. The limestone has been cut into arches, shelves, and low caves above water so clear that boats seem to hover over the seabed. It is an easy stop by car, bicycle, or coastal walk from the resort strip.

Go early or late if you want the cliffs without crowds, and be cautious around the edges: the rock is sharp, the drops are real, and summer heat can make the exposed walk punishing. Boat trips give the best view back toward the caves, while confident swimmers can enjoy the water in calm conditions.
