Thalassa Municipal Museum gives Ayia Napa a cultural stop away from beaches, bars, and boat trips.
Its exhibits focus on the sea: marine life, archaeology, ship models, and the long relationship
between Cyprus and the eastern Mediterranean.

The museum is compact, air-conditioned, and easy to fit into the middle of a hot day. It works
especially well for families or travelers who want a little context before heading back to the
harbour and beaches.
