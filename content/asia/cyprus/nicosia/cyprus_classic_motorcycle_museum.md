The Cyprus Classic Motorcycle Museum is a small private museum in Nicosia's old city. Its collection includes about 150 classic motorcycles, many of them British, dating from the early 20th century to the 1980s.

It is a compact but distinctive stop for transport fans, and easy to combine with a walk through the old city.
