The Cyprus Museum of Natural History focuses on the animal life of Cyprus and the wider Mediterranean, with mammals, marine life, insects, fossils, and displays aimed at families. It is outside the old centre, so it is best treated as a targeted museum stop rather than a casual walk-by sight.
