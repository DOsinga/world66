The Cyprus Postal Museum traces the island's postal history from the Venetian period onward. Its collection includes stamps, postal markings, and material that shows how Cyprus was connected to wider Mediterranean routes.

It is a small museum, but a useful stop for visitors who enjoy specialist collections or are already walking through old Nicosia.
