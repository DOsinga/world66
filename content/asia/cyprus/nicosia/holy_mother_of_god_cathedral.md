Holy Mother of God Cathedral is an Armenian church in Nicosia, adding a quieter but meaningful stop to the city's mix of Orthodox, Catholic, Muslim, and Armenian heritage. Its value lies in how it broadens the story of the divided capital beyond the most visited monuments.

It is worth seeking out if you are walking the older quarters and paying attention to minority communities and sacred architecture. Check access before relying on an interior visit, as smaller religious sites may not keep tourist hours.
