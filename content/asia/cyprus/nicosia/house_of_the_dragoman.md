The House of the Dragoman Hadjigeorgakis Kornesios is a restored 18th-century mansion that now serves as an ethnological museum. Its rooms, courtyard, and domestic displays show how an elite Ottoman-era household in Nicosia lived, and it pairs well with the nearby folk and Byzantine museums.
