Enkomi is an archaeological site west of Famagusta with roots in the Bronze Age city often linked to ancient Alashiya. It is a quieter, more fragmentary experience than Salamis, but it reaches further back into Cyprus's eastern Mediterranean history.

Come if you like ruins that require imagination. The site is best understood as part of a wider Famagusta archaeology day, with Salamis, the city walls, and the coast helping fill in the long human geography of this corner of Cyprus.
