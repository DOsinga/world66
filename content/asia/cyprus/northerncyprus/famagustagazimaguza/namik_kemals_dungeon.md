Namik Kemal's Dungeon is a small historic site in the old palace complex, remembered for the Ottoman poet and playwright Namik Kemal, who was imprisoned here from 1873 to 1876 after writing a politically sensitive play. The rooms are modest, but the site adds a literary and political layer to Famagusta's Venetian and Ottoman core.
