Palm Beach is Famagusta's most charged seaside spot: a strip of sand beside the sealed and partly reopened district of Varosha, once one of Cyprus's most fashionable resort areas. The water and beach are attractive in their own right, but the empty high-rises behind the fences make the place feel unlike an ordinary Mediterranean swim stop.

Come in daylight, respect any posted restrictions, and treat the area as both beach and historical landscape. It can be an unsettling visit, but that contrast is exactly why it belongs on a Famagusta itinerary: few places show the island's divided modern history so physically.
