St Barnabas Monastery is one of the most worthwhile excursions from Famagusta, combining a monastic complex, icon displays, and archaeological context in a quiet rural setting. It is associated with the tradition of St Barnabas, an important figure in Cypriot Christianity.

The site pairs naturally with Salamis and other antiquities north of the walled city. It is a strong stop for travelers interested in religious history, icons, and the layered Greek, Roman, Byzantine, and later heritage of eastern Cyprus.
