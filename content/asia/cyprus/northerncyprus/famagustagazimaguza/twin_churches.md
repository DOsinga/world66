The Twin Churches are a pair of medieval churches in Famagusta's old city. The larger church was built for the Knights Hospitaller in the early 13th century, while the smaller one was built for the Knights Templar around 1300. They are a compact stop while walking between the city's larger Gothic and Venetian monuments.
