The main restaurant inside the Büyük Han, with tables in the central courtyard around the raised mescit and fountain. The menu runs to Cypriot mezedes, grilled meats, and the local sheftalia (caul-wrapped grilled meat sausages).

The setting is the draw. Service is slow and prices a little above neighbourhood averages, but eating in one of the finest Ottoman buildings on the island is fair compensation.
