A short white tower on the headland above the archaeological park, built by the British in 1888 and still in service. It is not open to the public, but the grassy slope around it is one of the best free viewpoints on this stretch of coast — the sea on one side, the ruins of Nea Paphos on the other.

Walk up at golden hour. The light off the sea is what brings the photographers.
