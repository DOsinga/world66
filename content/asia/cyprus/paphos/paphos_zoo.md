Paphos Zoo is a family-oriented animal park outside the main archaeological and harbour areas of Paphos. It is best known for bird collections and a broad mix of animals, making it an alternative to beach and ruin-focused days.

This is mainly a practical family outing rather than a core cultural sight. Check transport, weather, and current animal-show schedules before building a day around it.
