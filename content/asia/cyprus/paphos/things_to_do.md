title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Paphos has one of the best archaeological parks in the Mediterranean. The Roman mosaic floors in the [House of Dionysus](/asia/cyprus/paphos/houseofdionysus) and the surrounding villas in the [Paphos Archaeological Park](/asia/cyprus/paphos/paphos_archaeological_park) are extraordinary — some of the finest in situ anywhere in the Roman world — and a short walk away lie the rock-cut [Tombs of the Kings](/asia/cyprus/paphos/tombsofthekings) from the Hellenistic period. The [Byzantine Museum](/asia/cyprus/paphos/byzantinemuseum) and the [Paphos Archaeological Museum](/asia/cyprus/paphos/paphos_archaeological_museum) hold serious collections, and the squat [Paphos Castle](/asia/cyprus/paphos/paphos_castle) at the end of the harbour is the obvious starting point for an evening walk. Further afield: the hillside birthplace of Aphrodite at Petra tou Romiou and the Akamas Peninsula, the last wild corner of Cyprus.
