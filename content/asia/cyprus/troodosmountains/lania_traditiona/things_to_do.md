---
title: Things to Do
type: section
---

Lania is small enough to walk in an hour and worth slowing down for far longer. The point of being here is the texture — the stone houses, the bougainvillea spilling from the walls, the wine cellars open onto the lanes — rather than the inventory of sights. That said, a handful of things are worth seeking out: the village's old wine press, the church in the central square, and the giant royal oak on the edge of the village.
