Perched on the southern slopes of the Troodos mountains at around 1,100 metres, Pano Platres is the most popular mountain resort in Cyprus. The air is noticeably cooler here than on the coast, and the surrounding pine forests give the village a quite different character from the sun-baked lowlands.

In summer, Cypriots head up to escape the coastal heat. In spring and autumn, visitors arrive for the walking trails — the [Caledonian Waterfall trail](/asia/cyprus/troodosmountains/pano_platres/caledonian_waterfall) and the Millomeris trail are particular favourites, and the Artemis trail circles the peak of [Mount Olympos](/asia/cyprus/troodosmountains/pano_platres/mount_olympos) nearby. When winter arrives, the village sits within easy reach of the Troodos ski centre. [Trooditissa Monastery](/asia/cyprus/troodosmountains/pano_platres/trooditissa_monastery), set in a steep pine valley above the village, is one of the most atmospheric religious sites in Cyprus.
