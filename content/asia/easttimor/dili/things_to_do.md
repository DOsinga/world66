Dili is a small capital and you can see the headline sights in a long day: the seafront from the Government Palace to Cristo Rei, the resistance museums that explain why this country exists, and a swim or a dive off the city beach. Most of what is interesting sits within a couple of kilometres of Avenida de Portugal.
