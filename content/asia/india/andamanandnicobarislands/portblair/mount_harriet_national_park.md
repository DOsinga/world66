Mount Harriet National Park protects forested hills across the water from Port Blair. It is a good change of pace from the city's museums and colonial sites, with greenery, birdlife, and viewpoints over the Andaman landscape.

The park is named for Harriet Tytler, who documented monuments in Delhi in the 19th century. Check transport and permit details locally before setting out.
