title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Port Blair's strongest sights mix prison history, harbour ruins, and Andaman natural history. Start with the [Cellular Jail](/asia/india/andamanandnicobarislands/portblair/cellular_jail), take the boat to [Netaji Subhas Chandra Bose Island](/asia/india/andamanandnicobarislands/portblair/netaji_subhas_chandra_bose_island), and use [Chatham Saw Mill](/asia/india/andamanandnicobarislands/portblair/chatham_saw_mill) or the [Samudrika Naval Marine Museum](/asia/india/andamanandnicobarislands/portblair/samudrika_naval_marine_museum) for a shorter stop.
