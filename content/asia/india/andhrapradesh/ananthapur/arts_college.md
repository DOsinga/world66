The Arts College in Anantapur is one of the town's more recognisable institutional buildings, tied to the older Government College campus. Its colonial-era academic architecture stands out in a city better known to travellers as a base for Lepakshi, Gooty, and Puttaparthi excursions.

This is an exterior architectural stop, not a museum. It is worthwhile if you are already walking central Anantapur and want a sense of the civic institutions that shaped the town in the twentieth century.
