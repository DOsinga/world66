---
title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

The [Sita Ramachandraswamy Temple](/asia/india/andhrapradesh/bhadrachalam/sita_ramachandraswamy_temple) is the town's entire reason for being — plan your visit around it. Beyond the temple, the [Godavari River ghats](/asia/india/andhrapradesh/bhadrachalam/godavari_river_ghats) offer a peaceful counterpoint to the pilgrimage crowds, and the boat trip to the [Papikondalu Hills](/asia/india/andhrapradesh/bhadrachalam/papikondalu_hills) is one of the finest river journeys in Telangana. The day excursion to [Parnasala](/asia/india/andhrapradesh/bhadrachalam/parnasala) takes you through tribal villages to the legendary site of Sita's abduction.
