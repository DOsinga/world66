---
title: Beaches
type: section
---

Chirala sits directly on the Bay of Bengal and has good beach access. The town beach is largely the preserve of local fishing communities — colourful catamarans, morning fish auctions, and an absence of tourist infrastructure. [Suryalanka Beach](/asia/india/andhrapradesh/chirala/suryalanka_beach), 12 km south, is cleaner and more accessible for swimming during the winter months (October to March), when the sea is calm. Andhra Pradesh Tourism operates a resort there.

The water is warm year-round, but the monsoon (June to September) brings rough surf and rip currents — swimming is not advisable during that period. The coastal landscape south of town is attractive: casuarina forests, low dunes, and salt flats that attract migratory birds in winter.
