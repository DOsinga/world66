Nehru Park is a small public park in central Cuddapah with lawns, walking paths, and play equipment. The morning crowd is older residents on exercise walks; the late afternoon brings families. It is not a destination in itself but a useful pause if you are travelling with children or wandering on foot in the heat of the day.
