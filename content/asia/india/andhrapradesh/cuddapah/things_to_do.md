---
title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Cuddapah's sights are modest in scale but varied in character — Sufi dargahs, a 17th-century Hindu temple, a Gothic Catholic cathedral, a colonial-era British cemetery, and a small archaeological museum. None of these are show-stoppers in their own right, but as a half-day on foot they give a strong sense of Rayalaseema's overlapping religious and political histories. The Seven Roads junction at the centre of the old town is the obvious place to start.
