The Ashtalakshmi temple on the edge of Eluru is a relatively modern construction, built in the South Indian style with a tall ornate gopuram covered in painted figures. It is dedicated to the eight aspects of Lakshmi, the goddess of wealth and prosperity, with each form housed in its own shrine around the central sanctum.

The temple is busy on Fridays and during the Varalakshmi Vratham festival in the monsoon, when women in saris fill the courtyards to perform the ritual fast.
