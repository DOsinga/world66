Balaji Temple at Chilkur is an important Hindu pilgrimage site on the outskirts of Hyderabad. It is dedicated to Venkateswara and is especially known among devotees praying for success with overseas visas.

The temple has an unusual reputation for refusing donations and VIP privileges. Devotees traditionally walk circuits around the shrine, returning for many more once a wish has been fulfilled.
