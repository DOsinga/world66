The Banjara Hills and Jubilee Hills strip — roughly the Road No. 10 and Road No. 12 area — is where Hyderabad's working-in-tech crowd eats, drinks, and meets. Specialty coffee shops, rooftop bars, craft beer taprooms, and a dense concentration of restaurants make this the obvious destination for evenings after a day of Old City sightseeing. The contrast with the Charminar area is stark: this could be a prosperous neighbourhood in any large Indian city, polished and air-conditioned, with valet parking outside the better restaurants.
