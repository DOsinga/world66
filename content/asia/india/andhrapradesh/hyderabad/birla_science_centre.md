Birla Science Centre combines a planetarium, science museum, dinosaur display, and art gallery near Birla Mandir. The museum is uneven, but the planetarium show, Kotasaurus skeleton, and Nirmala Birla art collection make it a useful stop for families or anyone looking for an indoor science-focused visit in central Hyderabad.
