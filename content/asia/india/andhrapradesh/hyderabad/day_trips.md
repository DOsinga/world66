---
title: "Day Trips"
type: section
---

**Warangal** (140km northeast, around 3 hours by train or road) was the capital of the Kakatiya dynasty before Hyderabad existed. The 13th-century Warangal Fort and the **Thousand Pillar Temple** — with its intricate basalt carvings — are among the finest examples of Kakatiya architecture. A direct train makes this feasible as a long day trip.

**Nagarjunakonda** (160km southeast) is a Buddhist archaeological site of the first importance, now largely submerged by the Nagarjuna Sagar reservoir. A boat takes visitors to the island museum where sculptures and ruins were relocated before the dam was built. The setting — water, hills, and ancient stonework — is striking.

**Bidar** (130km north, just across the Karnataka border) was the capital of the Bahmani Sultanate in the 15th century and has some of the most undervisited medieval architecture in the Deccan: the Bidar Fort, the Mahmud Gawan Madrasa, and the Bahmani Tombs. Far quieter than Hyderabad's main sites.

**Ramoji Film City** (30km east) is the world's largest integrated film studio complex, a Guinness record holder. Largely aimed at Indian families and film fans, with studio tours, sets from famous Telugu and Hindi films, and theme-park elements. Not for everyone, but very popular.
