Hussain Shah Wali Dargah is the mausoleum of a Sufi saint associated with the Qutb Shahi dynasty. The shrine is a quieter historic stop west of central Hyderabad, especially for visitors already exploring Golconda Fort and the Qutb Shahi tombs.
