Ibrahim Quli Qutb Shah Mosque is a historic mosque near Golconda Fort, associated with the fourth ruler of the Golconda Sultanate. It adds a smaller religious and architectural stop to the area around the fort.

Visit it as part of a wider Golconda outing rather than as a standalone trip across Hyderabad.
