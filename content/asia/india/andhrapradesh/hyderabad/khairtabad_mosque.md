Khairtabad Mosque dates to the early 17th century and is linked to Khairunisa Begum, daughter of Sultan Muhammad Qutb Shah. Its age and Qutb Shahi associations make it more than a neighborhood mosque, even if it is not one of Hyderabad's grand headline monuments.

It is a short architectural and historical stop for visitors already exploring central Hyderabad.
