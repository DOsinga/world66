Khursheed Jah Devdi is a fading Paigah noble's palace in the Old City, built with a European face quite unlike Hyderabad's better-known Indo-Islamic monuments. The building has suffered from neglect, but its colonnades, garden setting, and slightly melancholy grandeur make it worth a look if you are already exploring the Charminar side of town.
