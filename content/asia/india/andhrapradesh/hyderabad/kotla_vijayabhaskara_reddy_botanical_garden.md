Kotla Vijayabhaskara Reddy Botanical Garden is a large green space in Hyderabad's western technology corridor. Its marked sections include bamboo and palm collections, and the paved paths make it a useful stop for travellers who want a quieter break from the city's forts, tombs, and traffic.
