Kotla Vijayabhaskara Reddy Botanical Garden is a broad green break from Hyderabad's traffic, with paved paths through bamboo, palms, flowering beds, and labelled plant collections. It is most useful as a quiet walk or picnic stop if you are already in Cyberabad or have had your fill of forts and old-city crowds.
