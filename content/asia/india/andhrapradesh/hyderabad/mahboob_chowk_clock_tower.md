Mahboob Chowk Clock Tower is a five-storey clock tower from 1892 in Hyderabad's Old City. It stands in a small garden near the Charminar and Laad Bazaar, with clock faces on all four sides and a busy market setting around it.

It is a short stop rather than a long visit, but it adds another layer of late-19th-century architecture to an Old City walk.
