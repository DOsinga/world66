Moula Ali Dargah is a hilltop shrine reached by a long flight of steps in eastern Hyderabad. The site is linked to a sacred stone impression associated with Hazrat Ali and was developed under the Qutb Shahi rulers, with a mosque, pavilion and ceremonial buildings around the shrine. The climb is part of the visit, ending with wide views over the city.
