Mrugavani National Park protects a pocket of dry scrub and woodland on the edge of Hyderabad, with birds, reptiles, small mammals, and native plants that are easy to miss in the city's built-up sprawl. It is a modest national park, but useful if you want a nature break without leaving the metropolitan area.

Come early for better bird activity and cooler weather. The park is most rewarding as a short walk or family outing rather than a full wildlife safari.
