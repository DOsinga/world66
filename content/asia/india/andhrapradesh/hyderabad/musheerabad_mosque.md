Musheerabad Mosque is a 16th-century Qutb Shahi mosque built under Ibrahim Quli Qutb Shah of Golconda. Its five arches and corner minarets echo other historic mosques from the period, giving the northern side of Hyderabad a worthwhile architectural stop.
