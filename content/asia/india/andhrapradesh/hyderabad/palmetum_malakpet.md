The Palmetum in Malakpet is a small botanical garden with a narrow focus: palms. Its collection covers all six subfamilies of the palm family, with around 120 varieties planted across the grounds.

It is a quiet, specialist stop rather than one of Hyderabad's grand sights. Come if you like gardens, botany, or need a green pause away from the busier Old City landmarks.
