Premamati Mosque is a 16th-century mosque built during the reign of Ibrahim Quli Qutb Shah. It was intended to serve a planned city west of Golconda Fort, but that city was abandoned after water proved unreliable.

The mosque is a quieter historic stop near the Golconda and Qutb Shahi sites, useful for seeing more of the area's Qutb Shahi architecture.
