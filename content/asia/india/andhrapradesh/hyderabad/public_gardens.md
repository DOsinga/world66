Hyderabad's Public Gardens, also known as Bagh-e-Aam, are one of the city's long-established public green spaces. The gardens sit near several civic and cultural buildings, including the State Legislative Assembly, Jubilee Hall, and the State Archaeological Museum.

They are a relaxed stop for a break between museums and central Hyderabad sights. The appeal is the mix of shade, old public architecture, and everyday city life rather than formal landscaping.
