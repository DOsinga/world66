Purana Pul is the oldest bridge in Hyderabad, built across the Musi River in 1578 during the Qutb Shahi period. It linked Golconda with the growing city of Hyderabad and remains one of the older surviving pieces of the Old City's fabric.

The bridge is not a polished attraction, but it is a worthwhile historical marker if you are already exploring the Old City, Charminar, and the nearby markets.
