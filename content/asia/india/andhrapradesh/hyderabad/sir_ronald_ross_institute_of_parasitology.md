This small Osmania University museum marks the laboratory where Ronald Ross proved that malaria is transmitted by female Anopheles mosquitoes. Old photographs, research diagrams, a bust, and a commemorative plaque make it a specialist stop, but it is a meaningful one if you are interested in medical history.
