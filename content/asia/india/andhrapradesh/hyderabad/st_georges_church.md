St George's Church is one of Hyderabad's oldest churches, built in 1844 as an Anglican church and now part of the Church of South India. Its simple colonial-era architecture gives a different angle on the city's 19th-century history.

It is close enough to the older centre to combine with a wider walk through Abids and the surrounding commercial streets.
