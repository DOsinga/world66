Sudha Car Museum is a quirky private museum devoted to handmade and modified vehicles created by Kanyaboyina Sudhakar Yadav. The collection includes vintage cars, bikes, and a large number of novelty vehicles shaped like everyday objects.

It is light-hearted rather than polished, but that is the appeal. Families and visitors who enjoy oddball museums will get the most out of it, and it is an easy add-on when visiting sights in southern Hyderabad.
