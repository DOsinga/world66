---
title: Beaches near Machilipatnam
type: section
---

The Bay of Bengal coastline around Machilipatnam offers long, uncrowded beaches, though the sea is often rough and swimming conditions vary by season. The main beach accessible from the city is at Manginapudi, about 10 km south. The best time to visit is from October to March, when the weather is cooler and the Bay is calmer.
