---
title: Things to Do in Machilipatnam
type: section
---

Machilipatnam rewards the traveler who arrives curious about Kalamkari textiles, colonial history, or coastal life on the Krishna delta. The town itself is compact and manageable on foot or by auto-rickshaw. The harbour area and the fort remnants speak to the city's long commercial past, while the villages on the outskirts are where the Kalamkari craft tradition stays alive.
