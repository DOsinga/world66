---
title: When to Go in Machilipatnam
type: section
---

The best time to visit Machilipatnam is from October to February. The weather is dry, temperatures are pleasant (20–30°C), and the Bay of Bengal is calm enough for the beach. From June to September, the monsoon brings heavy rain and occasional flooding in the low-lying areas of the city; travel is possible but less comfortable. March to May is hot and humid, with temperatures pushing above 40°C.

The Kalamkari workshops in Pedana operate year-round, though artisans work longer hours in the cooler months. If you want to see the full drying and washing process, a dry sunny day is essential — the craft depends on sunlight.
