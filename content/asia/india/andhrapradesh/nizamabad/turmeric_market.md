The turmeric market (mandi) in Nizamabad is one of the major agricultural trading hubs for turmeric in India. The surrounding district produces large quantities of the spice, and the market is where farmers bring their crop to sell to traders who supply spice processors and exporters across the country. If you visit during the harvest season (typically January to April), the market is a vivid spectacle of golden-yellow sacks, busy traders, and the pervasive scent of turmeric.

Even outside the peak season the market area is active and worth visiting to understand the agricultural economy that underpins this part of Telangana. This is not a tourist attraction in any formal sense — it is a working commodity market — but visitors are generally tolerated and the colour and activity make it memorable.
