---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

The coastal Andhra climate is hot and humid for most of the year. The best window for visiting Prakasham is **November to February**, when daytime temperatures sit in the high twenties Celsius and the nights are pleasant.

March through June is brutally hot, with temperatures regularly above 40°C and stiff sea breezes only taking the edge off. The south-west monsoon brings rain from June to September, and the north-east monsoon — which is more important here than on most of India's west coast — adds further showers in October and November. Cyclones occasionally make landfall along this stretch of coast in the late monsoon; check the forecast if travelling between September and December.

Temple festivals — particularly at the Sai Baba temple in Ongole and at the local Venkateswara shrines — can transform sleepy sites into crowded pilgrimage centres for a few days at a time.
