The ISKCON Radha-Gopinath temple stands close to the Godavari riverfront, making it easy to combine with Pushkar Ghat. The complex is newer than Rajahmundry's older shrines, but it is active, tidy, and welcoming to visitors who want a calm stop between the river and the town centre.

As with any working temple, dress modestly and avoid intrusive photography during worship.
