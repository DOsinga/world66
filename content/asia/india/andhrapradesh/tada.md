Tada is a small Andhra Pradesh town just north of the Tamil Nadu border, on the highway and railway line between [Chennai](/asia/india/tamilnadu/chennai) and [Nellore](/asia/india/andhrapradesh/nellore). The town itself is plain and practical, but it has one good reason to appear on a travel map: it is the usual jumping-off point for [Tada Falls](/asia/india/andhrapradesh/tada/tada_falls), also called Ubbalamadugu Falls.

The falls sit inland in the Siddulaiah Kona forest, reached by a rough trek along streams and boulders. This makes Tada popular with weekend walkers from Chennai, especially after the monsoon when there is enough water to justify the effort. Pulicat Lake lies to the east, while Sullurpeta and Sriharikota are further up the coast.

Tada is easy to reach by local train from Chennai and by buses running between Chennai, Tirupati, and Nellore. Treat it as a simple base rather than a town to linger in.
