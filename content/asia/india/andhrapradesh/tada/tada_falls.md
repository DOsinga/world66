Tada Falls, or Ubbalamadugu Falls, is the reason most travellers stop in Tada. The falls drop through forested hills west of town, and the approach is more important than the final cascade: a rough walk over rocks, through scrub, and beside clear pools that fill properly after the rains.

This is not a manicured picnic spot. Wear shoes that can handle wet stone, take food and water, and expect the trail to be hot outside the cooler months. From Tada, local transport can get you part of the way toward Varadaiahpalem, but arranging a vehicle makes the day much easier.
