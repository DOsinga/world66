---
title: "Day Trips"
type: section
---

The area around Tirupati has several worthwhile sites that pilgrims often visit before or after Tirumala.

**Sri Kalahasti**, 36 km east, is one of the five major Shaivite Pancha Bhuta Sthala temples — representing the element of air. The temple is set in a dramatic gorge, the icon is naturally ventilated by a hollow in the rock, and it is also one of the most important Rahu-Ketu sthalams in India.

**Chandragiri**, 15 km west of Tirupati, was the last capital of the Vijayanagar Empire. The Raja Mahal palace inside the fort houses a small archaeology museum; the views over the surrounding hills are excellent.

**Sri Venkateswara National Park** covers the forested hills around Tirumala. Talakona waterfall, the tallest in Andhra Pradesh at 270 feet, is the most-visited part. Trekking permits are required from the forest department.

**Tiruchanur**, 5 km south of Tirupati, has the Padmavati Temple — dedicated to Venkateswara's consort. Most pilgrims visit before going up to Tirumala, as local tradition holds that worship at Tirumala is incomplete without honouring the goddess at Tiruchanur first.
