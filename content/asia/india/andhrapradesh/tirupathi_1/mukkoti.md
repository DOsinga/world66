Mukkoti is a Shiva temple near Kaloor village, about 12 km from Tirupati. The shrine is dedicated to Sri Agastheeswarar Swamy, with Goddess Aanandavalli also worshipped here.

It is a smaller, quieter stop than Tirupati's major pilgrimage temples. Come if you are exploring the religious sites around the city and want something more local than the main temple circuit.
