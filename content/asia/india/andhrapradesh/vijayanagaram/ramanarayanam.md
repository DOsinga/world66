Ramanarayanam is a modern temple complex outside Vizianagaram, built around the story of the Ramayana. The 15-acre site is laid out in the shape of a bow and arrow, which makes it more theatrical than the city's older royal-era sights.

It draws pilgrims and families in large numbers, so expect a devotional atmosphere rather than a quiet archaeological stop. For visitors, the interest is the scale, the narrative layout, and the way a new religious attraction has become part of the city's travel circuit.
