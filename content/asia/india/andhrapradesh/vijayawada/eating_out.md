---
title: "Eating Out"
type: section
---

Vijayawada is the heart of coastal Andhra cuisine, and the food is fiery, oily, and unapologetically tamarind-and-chilli forward. Pickles (avakaya, gongura) are taken seriously. The local meal is built around rice, sambar, and a sequence of curries; the gongura mutton at any decent Andhra mess is reason enough to stop in the city.
