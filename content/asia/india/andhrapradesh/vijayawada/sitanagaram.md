Sitanagaram sits on the bank of the Krishna River near Vijayawada, with a broad sandy riverside used for recreation and local outings. The Someswara Swamy temple gives the place its religious pull, and local tradition links the name to Rama grieving for Sita.

This is a modest riverside stop, best combined with Vijayawada's temples and river sights. Come for the setting and the local rhythm rather than expecting a major monument.
