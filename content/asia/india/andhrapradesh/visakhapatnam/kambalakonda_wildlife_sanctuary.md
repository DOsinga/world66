Kambalakonda Wildlife Sanctuary protects a stretch of dry evergreen forest and scrub on the edge of Visakhapatnam. It gives the city a rougher natural setting beyond beaches, hill parks, and naval museums.

Expect a simple outdoor visit rather than a safari. Trails, viewpoints, and the chance of birds or small wildlife make it worthwhile if you want to see the landscape that frames the city from inland.
