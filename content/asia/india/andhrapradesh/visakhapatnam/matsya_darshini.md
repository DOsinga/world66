Matsya Darshini is a small aquarium near the Visakhapatnam seafront, with marine and freshwater fish displays. The shark-mouth exit is its most memorable feature, and a handicrafts shop sits above the aquarium. It is a modest stop, but useful for families or for filling time while visiting the nearby beach museums.
