The Sea Harrier Museum adds another military-machine stop to Visakhapatnam's seafront, complementing the better-known submarine museum nearby. Its focus is a retired naval jump jet, which gives aviation enthusiasts a reason to pause on the beach road.

It is a compact museum, not a full naval aviation history course. The appeal is the object itself and the way Vizag's identity as a naval city becomes visible through museums, memorials, ships, and aircraft along the coast.
