Shilparamam Jathara is a crafts-and-culture complex in Visakhapatnam, modelled on the Shilparamam idea of giving artisans a dedicated place to sell and demonstrate regional work. It is most useful when stalls, fairs, or performances are active.

The draw is not architecture so much as browsing: textiles, handicrafts, small food stalls, and occasional cultural programming. Check local timing if you can, because the experience depends heavily on how many vendors and events are running.
