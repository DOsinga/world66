The UH3H Helicopter Museum displays a decommissioned Indian naval helicopter on Visakhapatnam's Beach Road, close to the city's better-known submarine and aircraft museums. It is a compact, hands-on addition to the seafront museum cluster.

The appeal is strongest for families and visitors interested in military hardware. You can see the aircraft at close range and pair it easily with the nearby INS Kurusura Submarine Museum, turning a beach promenade walk into a short aviation-and-naval history circuit.
