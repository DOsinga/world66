The Victory at Sea Memorial stands on Beach Road facing the Bay of Bengal, commemorating Indian naval action in the 1971 war. It is a compact stop rather than a long visit, but the setting gives it weight: surf on one side, traffic and city life on the other, and the submarine museum close by.

Come while walking the Ramakrishna Beach promenade. The memorial helps tie together Visakhapatnam's identity as both a beach city and a naval city, especially when combined with INS Kursura and the maritime exhibits nearby.
