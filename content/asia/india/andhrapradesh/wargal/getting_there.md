---
title: "Getting There"
type: section
---

Wargal is about 60 kilometres north of Hyderabad on the Siddipet-Karimnagar highway (NH163). By road from Hyderabad, the journey takes roughly an hour and a half, passing through the Sangareddy and Medak districts. Buses from Hyderabad's Jubilee Bus Station (Secunderabad) run regularly toward Siddipet and stop at Wargal, taking around two hours. If you are coming by car, the highway is well-maintained and signposted.

The most practical approach is a half-day trip from Hyderabad, timed to arrive when the temple is open for darshan in the morning or evening. Many visitors combine Wargal with Nachagiri Narasimha Swamy Temple, which lies about 15 kilometres further along the same highway.
