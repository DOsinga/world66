---
title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Wargal is a pilgrimage destination, and the [Saraswati Temple](/asia/india/andhrapradesh/wargal/saraswati_temple) on the hill is the reason visitors come. The temple complex also includes shrines to Vigneshwara (Ganesha) and Saniswara, and a Veda Pathasala where children can be seen reciting ancient texts. The [Nachagiri Narasimha Swamy Temple](/asia/india/andhrapradesh/wargal/nachagiri_temple) with its self-manifested Narasimha idol is about 15 km away and can be combined with a visit.
