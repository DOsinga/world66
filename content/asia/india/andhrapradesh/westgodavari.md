West Godavari is a district in [Andhra Pradesh](/asia/india/andhrapradesh) centred on the fertile delta of the Godavari River as it fans out toward the Bay of Bengal. It is one of the most productive rice-growing regions in India, and the landscape is lush and green — a network of canals, palm groves, and temple towns quite different from the drier interior.

The main town of interest is Bhimavaram, known for the [Somarama temple](/asia/india/andhrapradesh/westgodavari/somarama_temple) — one of the five holy Pancharama Kshetras dedicated to Shiva. Further east, [Narsapur](/asia/india/andhrapradesh/westgodavari/narsapur_lace_industry) is the centre of a lace-making industry that employs tens of thousands of women in the villages of the delta, producing work that is exported worldwide. The [Godavari backwaters](/asia/india/andhrapradesh/westgodavari/godavari_delta_backwaters) around Narsapur offer boat trips through a landscape of canals and palm groves similar to — but less touristed than — the Kerala backwaters.

Near Eluru (formerly in West Godavari district), the [Guntupalli Buddhist cave temples](/asia/india/andhrapradesh/westgodavari/guntupalli_buddhist_monuments) date to the 3rd century BCE and contain what may be the oldest rock-cut circular chaitya hall in India. The site is rarely visited and atmospheric.
