---
title: Things to Do
type: section
---

West Godavari district's main draws are its religious sites and its river landscape. The Godavari delta, where the river splits into multiple channels as it approaches the Bay of Bengal, creates an intricate geography of canals, islands, and palm-fringed riverbanks that is unlike anything in the drier interior of Andhra Pradesh. The Pancharama temples — five ancient Shiva shrines scattered across the delta region — are important pilgrimage sites with real architectural and historical interest.

The district capital Bhimavaram is the most convenient base. The Guntupalli cave temples near Eluru are a rarely visited Buddhist site of considerable antiquity and beauty.
