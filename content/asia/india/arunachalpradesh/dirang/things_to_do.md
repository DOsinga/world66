---
title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Dirang is small but has more going on than its size suggests. The old stone Dzong in the centre of town is the headline, but the Sangti Valley a few kilometres away, the Yak Research Centre, and the hot springs all reward an extra day. Most visitors treat Dirang as a stop between Bomdila and Tawang — staying two nights instead of one is the simplest way to see all of it.
