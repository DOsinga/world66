---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

The road to Tawang via Dirang is open all year but can be blocked by snow at Sela Pass in mid-winter. Best months for driving and clear views are **October–November** and **March–May**.

**Winter (December–February)** is genuinely cold — temperatures drop below freezing — but it is also when the black-necked cranes are in Sangti Valley, and when the mountains around Tawang are spectacular under snow. Pack accordingly.

**Monsoon (June–August)** brings landslides on the road up from Tezpur. Avoid if you can.
