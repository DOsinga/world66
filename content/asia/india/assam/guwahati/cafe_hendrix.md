A relaxed cafe-bar near Six Mile with a rock and blues soundtrack and walls lined with vinyl records and concert posters. The kitchen does Italian-leaning comfort food — pasta, pizza, burgers, sizzlers — and the bar is well-stocked with imported beer and decent cocktails.

Live music nights on weekends, often featuring local rock bands; the northeast has a strong indie music scene and Hendrix is one of the venues. Mid-range prices (around ₹700 per head); reservations advisable on Friday and Saturday.
