The best day trips out of Guwahati combine quickly. **Hajo**, 30 km northwest, is a small town where Hindu, Buddhist and Muslim shrines share the same hilltop — Hayagriva Madhava Temple, Poa Mecca and a small Buddhist chaitya. **Sualkuchi**, the silk-weaving town on the north bank, is half an hour further; you can visit weaving families and watch muga and eri silk being made. **Pobitora Wildlife Sanctuary**, 50 km east, has the highest density of one-horned rhinos in the world — a morning jeep safari is more or less guaranteed sightings.

For a longer day, **Shillong** in Meghalaya is three hours away through the hills. **Kaziranga National Park** is reachable as an overnight rather than a day trip — five hours each way.
