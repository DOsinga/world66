Doul Govinda Temple is a Krishna temple on the north bank of the Brahmaputra, best known for its five-day Holi celebrations in February or March. Outside festival time, it still makes a pleasant temple visit, often paired with the river crossing and the sandy Brahmaputra banks between November and April.
