Garbhanga Wildlife Sanctuary lies on the southern edge of Guwahati, where the city gives way to forested hills. It is valued for elephant habitat, birdlife, and the chance to see a wilder side of Assam without travelling far from the urban centre.

Access and conditions can be uneven, so do not approach it like a manicured city park. Go with local information, respect restrictions, and consider it a nature excursion for travellers who already understand that wildlife sightings are never guaranteed.
