---
title: Getting There
type: section
---

Guwahati is the entry point for the entire northeast. **Lokpriya Gopinath Bordoloi International Airport** (Borjhar, 25 km west of the centre) has direct flights from Delhi, Mumbai, Kolkata, Bangalore, Bangkok, Dhaka and Singapore, plus regional connections to Imphal, Aizawl, Agartala, Itanagar and Shillong. A pre-paid taxi from the airport to central Guwahati costs around ₹700.

**Guwahati Railway Station** is in Paltan Bazaar in the city centre. Major trains include the Rajdhani from Delhi (about 30 hours) and several Kolkata trains (about 20 hours). All trains heading further into the northeast — Dibrugarh, Silchar, Naharlagun, Agartala — pass through here.

Long-distance buses run to Shillong (3 hours), Siliguri (12 hours), Tezpur and Jorhat from the inter-state bus terminal at Paltan Bazaar. Shared sumos to Shillong are faster and only slightly more expensive than the bus.
