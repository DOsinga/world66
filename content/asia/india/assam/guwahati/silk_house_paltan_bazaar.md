The cluster of silk and textile shops around Paltan Bazaar — the streets between the railway station and the bus stand — is where most Assamese go to buy mekhela chador, the two-piece traditional dress. Several shops specialise in muga and pat silks; the famous golden muga, naturally produced and unique to Assam, is the headline material. Prices range from ₹2,000 for a simple cotton mekhela to ₹50,000+ for a fine muga set.

Quality varies and shopping requires patience and a willingness to bargain. Try Pratiksha Silk Palace and Daud Silk House for established names. Many shops will fit and tailor on the spot if you have a few days.
