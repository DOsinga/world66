Sualkuchi is the classic craft excursion from Guwahati, a riverside town famous for Assamese silk weaving. Looms, yarn shops, and small workshops make the place feel industrious rather than staged, and the best visits are about watching the work as much as buying fabric.

It lies outside the city, so plan it as a half-day trip with transport arranged in advance. If you are interested in textiles, ask about muga, eri, and pat silk, and remember that the most interesting part is the chain of skill behind the finished sari or scarf.
