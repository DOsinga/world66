Tirupati Balaji Temple is a quiet Hindu temple south of central Guwahati, modelled on the famous Venkateswara temple in Tirupati. Its white gateway, orderly courtyards, and peaceful setting make it a good stop if you are exploring the Dispur side of the city.

The temple is mainly a place of worship rather than a grand monument, so visit with the usual respect for prayer spaces. It works best as a short, calm break from Guwahati's busier markets and traffic.
