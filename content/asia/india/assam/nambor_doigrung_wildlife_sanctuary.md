Nambor-Doigrung Wildlife Sanctuary protects forest and wetland habitat east of Kaziranga, with hot springs and chances to see rhino and other wildlife. It is a more out-of-the-way Assam nature stop, so go with local transport plans rather than expecting easy city-style access.
