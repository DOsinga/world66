---
title: Things to Do
type: section
---

Almost everything worth doing at Pobitora centres on the wildlife sanctuary. Rhino sightings are virtually guaranteed on jeep safaris, and the floodplain landscape — tall grass, water holes, open grassland — is evocative of a wilder Assam that has otherwise been heavily converted to agriculture. Birdwatching is excellent around the water bodies at the sanctuary's edge.
