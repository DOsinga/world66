Bhairabi Temple stands near the eastern side of the city, with old carved stones and views toward the Brahmaputra and Kolia Bhomora Setu. It is dedicated to the goddess Bhairavi and is one of the more atmospheric religious stops around Tezpur.

The temple is modest, but it pairs naturally with Bamuni Hills and the river-facing sights nearby. Come with time to sit rather than treating it as a quick checklist item.
