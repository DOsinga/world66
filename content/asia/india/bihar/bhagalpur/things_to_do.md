title: Things to Do
type: section
---

Bhagalpur's two main draws are its silk-weaving heritage and the Gangetic river dolphins. The [Vikramshila Gangetic Dolphin Sanctuary](/asia/india/bihar/bhagalpur/vikramshila_dolphin_sanctuary) preserves one of the largest remaining populations of the endangered river dolphin in a stretch of the Ganges east of the city. The weavers of [Nathnagar](/asia/india/bihar/bhagalpur/nathnagar_silk_weavers) have been producing Tussar (wild silk) for centuries, and visiting the workshops is a genuine local experience.

The [Bhagalpur Museum](/asia/india/bihar/bhagalpur/bhagalpur_museum) holds a useful collection of regional archaeological finds and sculptures. The most worthwhile day trip from the city is [Vikramshila Mahavihara](/asia/india/bihar/bhagalpur/vikramshila_mahavihara), ruins of a 9th-century Buddhist university about 50km east near Kahalgaon.
