[Bodh Gaya](/asia/india/bihar/bodhgaya), where the Buddha attained enlightenment under the Bodhi tree, is the most worthwhile single trip from Patna. It is about 100 km south and takes 2-3 hours by train or hired car; many visitors do it in a day, though staying overnight allows time to see the modern temples built by the Sri Lankan, Thai, and Japanese sanghas.

The ruins of **Nalanda**, the great Mahavihara that was the world's first residential university, are 90 km southeast, easily combined with Bodh Gaya or visited separately. **Vaishali**, 50 km north across the Ganges, is where the Buddha gave his last sermon and where the Second Buddhist Council was held; the Ashoka pillar there is one of only two still standing on its original site.
