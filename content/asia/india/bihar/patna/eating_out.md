---
title: Eating Out
type: section
---

Bihari food is unfussy, vegetarian-leaning, and based on local grains: gram flour, mustard oil, and the prized aromatic rice of the Ganges plain. The signature dish is **litti chokha** -- balls of wheat flour stuffed with roasted gram, cooked over coals, and served with mashed aubergine and tomato. Eat it at a roadside stall around Boring Road or in the bazaars of Patna City; it should arrive smoky and brittle.

Beyond litti, look for **sattu paratha** (flatbread stuffed with roasted chickpea flour), **dal pitha** (steamed dumplings), and the river fish curries cooked in mustard. Maurya Lok complex in the centre has reliable mid-range restaurants; the bigger hotels along Fraser Road serve Mughlai and continental food. For sweets, **khaja** -- crisp layered pastry soaked in syrup -- is the Bihari speciality.
