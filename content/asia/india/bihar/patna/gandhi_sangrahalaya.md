Gandhi Sangrahalaya is Patna's museum on Mahatma Gandhi, with photographs, manuscripts, quotations, paintings, and sculpture tracing the main phases of his life. The library and exhibition rooms make it a worthwhile stop if you want more context on Gandhi's politics and public memory in Bihar.
