Indira Gandhi Planetarium is one of Patna's main science attractions, with regular astronomy shows and exhibitions aimed at both adults and children. Its dome theatre makes it a useful indoor stop, especially in hot or wet weather.

Check the current show schedule before going, as the visit is much better when timed around a screening.
