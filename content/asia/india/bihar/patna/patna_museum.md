The older of Patna's two museums, the Patna Museum opened in 1917 in an Indo-Saracenic building near Buddha Smriti Park. When the Bihar Museum opened in 2015, many of its star objects -- including the Didarganj Yakshi -- moved to the new building, but the Patna Museum still holds the Buddha relic casket from Vaishali, Gandharan reliefs, terracotta from Sunga sites, and a gallery of British-era paintings.

The interior is dusty and labelling is inconsistent, but the collection remains rich and the building itself, with its arched corridors and inner courtyards, is worth the visit. Closed on Mondays.
