The Church of Our Lady of the Sea is part of Daman's Portuguese coastal inheritance, set within the old fort area where churches, walls, and sea-facing lanes still carry the shape of the colonial port. Its appeal is less about a single dramatic feature and more about how it fits into a compact historic walk through Nani Daman.

Visit it together with the fortifications and nearby waterfront rather than as a stand-alone detour. The whitewashed exterior, quiet interior, and sea air make it a useful pause in a town where the strongest travel experience is piecing together the old Portuguese enclave from surviving churches, gates, and walls.
