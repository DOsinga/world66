Daman Lighthouse is one of the easiest visual anchors in Moti Daman, rising near the fort and the seafront. It is not a complicated sight, but in a small coastal destination the combination of fort walls, open water, and sunset views earns its place.

Come late in the day if you can. The lighthouse area makes most sense as part of a loop through Moti Daman Fort, the churches, and the waterfront, when the Portuguese-era townscape and the Arabian Sea can be read together.
