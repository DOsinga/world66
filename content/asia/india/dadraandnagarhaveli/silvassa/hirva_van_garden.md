Hirva Van Garden is a landscaped green space near Silvassa, built around lawns, small waterfalls, and shaded walking paths. It is the sort of place that works best as an easy breather from the town's heat and traffic rather than as a major standalone attraction.

For families and slow travellers it can be a pleasant stop, especially in the cooler parts of the day. Pair it with Silvassa's other gardens and the Tribal Cultural Museum to get a broader sense of why visitors use the town as a relaxed weekend base.
