The Chapel of Our Lady of Rosary is one of Daman's small but rewarding Portuguese-era churches. Set within the old fort precinct, it carries the layered feel of a coastal colony: defensive walls outside, Catholic devotion and carved detail within.

The chapel is best visited while exploring Moti Daman Fort, where the lanes and churches make more sense together than as isolated stops. It is a compact sight, but it gives Daman's colonial history a human scale.
