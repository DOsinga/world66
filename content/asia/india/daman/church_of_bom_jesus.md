The Church of Bom Jesus is one of the clearest reminders of Daman's Portuguese past. Set within the old fort area, the church dates from the colonial period and keeps a quiet, whitewashed dignity that contrasts with the busier streets outside. Its carved wooden altar and interior details are the main draw.

Daman's old churches are not huge, but they give the town its particular character: Catholic, coastal, and historically tied to Goa and the wider Portuguese Indian world. Visit Bom Jesus while walking the fort, and take a little time inside rather than treating it as a quick facade stop.
