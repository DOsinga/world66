The Dominican Monastery is one of Daman's most evocative Portuguese remnants, a roofless ruin whose arches and stone walls still suggest the scale of the old religious complex. It is less polished than the town's churches, which is exactly why it is worth a look.

Come for the atmosphere rather than museum interpretation. The monastery sits naturally with a walk around the fort area, where Daman's small colonial core makes more sense when you see the churches, walls, and ruins together.
