Fort Jerome, also called Nani Daman Fort, stands at the northern side of the Daman Ganga River mouth. It is smaller than the great fort at Moti Daman, but easier to fold into a short visit, with thick walls, a gateway, and traces of the Portuguese period that shaped this coast for centuries.

The fort is most useful as a starting point for understanding Daman's layout. From here you can look across the river toward Moti Daman, then wander through the lanes around Nani Daman's churches and waterfront. Go for atmosphere rather than pristine restoration; the charm is in the weathered masonry and the river setting.
