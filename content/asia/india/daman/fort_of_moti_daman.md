The Fort of Moti Daman is the heavyweight Portuguese monument in Daman, a large walled enclosure rather than a single picturesque ruin. Bastions, gateways, churches, government buildings, and quiet streets sit inside the walls, making it feel like a fortified town that has kept working.

Walk the ramparts and lanes slowly. The fort rewards attention to scale: the thick walls facing the Daman Ganga, the old churches inside, and the contrast between colonial masonry and the everyday administrative life still going on around it.
