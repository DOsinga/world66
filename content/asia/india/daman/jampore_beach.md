Jampore Beach is Daman's quieter southern beach, known for its long stretch of dark sand and open coastal views. It is not a pristine tropical beach, but it gives the town a relaxed shoreline escape away from the denser fort and market areas.

Go for a walk, sunset, or a simple beach break rather than serious swimming. The beach works best when folded into a Daman day that also includes Moti Daman Fort and the old Portuguese-era streets.
