Ajmal Khan Park is the central green lung of Karol Bagh, a pleasant urban park in the middle of one of Delhi's most densely commercial neighbourhoods. The park is well maintained with lawns, walking paths, and a small children's play area. It fills up in the evenings with local residents escaping the noise and crowds of the surrounding shopping streets.

The park is a useful orientation point in Karol Bagh — the neighbourhood's main shopping areas radiate out from the area around it. Early mornings see yoga practitioners and speed-walkers; evenings bring families and vendors selling chaat and ice cream from carts along the perimeter. A welcome respite from the relentless commerce of Karol Bagh's main bazaar.
