Atagh Khan's Tomb is a Mughal-era tomb in the Nizamuddin area, associated with a general in Emperor Humayun's army. It sits close to the dense historic lanes around Hazrat Nizamuddin, where Sufi shrines, old tombs, and mosques crowd together.

The tomb is not one of Delhi's headline monuments, but it rewards travellers already exploring Nizamuddin with another layer of Mughal history.
