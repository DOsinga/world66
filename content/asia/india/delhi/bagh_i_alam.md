Bagh I Alam ka Gumbad is a Lodi-era tomb in South Delhi, built from local stone with red sandstone and blue tile details. It is a quiet, atmospheric stop for visitors tracing Delhi's pre-Mughal monuments around Hauz Khas and the old urban villages nearby.
