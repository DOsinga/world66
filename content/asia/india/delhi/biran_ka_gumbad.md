Biran ka Gumbad is a Lodi-period tomb near Hauz Khas, built with arched niches and the heavy, compact form typical of late Delhi Sultanate funerary architecture. It is a short stop rather than a major sight, but it fits well into a walk linking Hauz Khas Complex, Deer Park, and the area's other medieval tombs.
