The Cathedral Church of the Redemption is the cathedral of the Church of North India's Delhi diocese. Henry Medd designed it in the late colonial period, and its calm, pale interior feels far removed from the traffic around New Delhi's government quarter.

Look for the stained glass, the restrained arches, and the way the building handles heat and light without heavy ornament.
