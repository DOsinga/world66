Chittaranjan Park Kali Mandir is a Bengali temple complex on a small hill in South Delhi. The main Kali shrine is joined by temples to Shiva and Radha Krishna, and the compound becomes especially lively during Durga Puja.

Outside festival time it is a quieter stop, useful if you want to see the Bengali cultural life that gives Chittaranjan Park its distinct character.
