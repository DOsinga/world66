Connaught Place — the circular colonnaded commercial hub of Lutyens' Delhi — has one of the highest concentrations of restaurants in the capital, spanning everything from heritage establishments to fast-food chains. Wenger's Bakery (1926), United Coffee House (1942), and Veda are among the most venerable addresses, while the inner and outer circles of the colonnade house dozens of bars, cafés, and restaurants across price points.

The basement level of the central park beneath the plaza houses the Palika Bazaar market and several food courts. The area is busiest at lunchtime when office workers descend from the surrounding buildings, and on weekend evenings when the restaurants and bars are at their most social. The location is central to all parts of Delhi and the metro connections make it easy to reach from anywhere in the city.
