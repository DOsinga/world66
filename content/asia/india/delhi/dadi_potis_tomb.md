Dadi Poti's Tomb is a pair of medieval tombs, one large and one small, known locally as the grandmother and granddaughter tombs. The larger tomb appears three-storey from outside but holds a single chamber with several graves.

The architecture points to two different periods: the larger tomb to the Tughlaq era and the smaller, sloping-walled tomb to the Lodi period. It is a modest stop, but worthwhile if you are tracing Delhi's scattered medieval remains.
