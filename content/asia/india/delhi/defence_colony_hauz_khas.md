The stretch of Lodhi Road and its surrounding lanes between Lodi Gardens and Defence Colony has become one of Delhi's most interesting dining destinations. The combination of upscale residential neighbourhood, proximity to the diplomatic enclave, and a sequence of well-regarded restaurants makes the area a natural circuit for food-focused visitors. Restaurants here tend toward careful, ingredient-led Indian cooking rather than the tourist-oriented fare found near monuments.

Establishments like Café Lota (attached to the National Crafts Museum), the Indian Accent (consistently ranked India's best restaurant), and a string of mid-range cafés have clustered in this zone. The walk from Lodi Gardens through the dining strip to Defence Colony Market covers roughly two kilometres and passes through some of the most pleasant residential streets in Delhi.
