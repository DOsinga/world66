Dilli Haat INA is a permanent crafts and food bazaar in South Delhi designed to give rural artisans from across India a direct retail platform. The open-air market rotates stalls among craftspeople from different Indian states on a fortnightly basis, ensuring the stock continuously changes. You'll find Rajasthani block prints, Kashmiri papier-mâché, Madhubani paintings, tribal jewellery, and bamboo furniture alongside regional food stalls representing every major Indian cuisine.

The market is run by the Delhi Tourism Corporation and charges a modest entry fee. It opens daily from 11am to 10pm. The quality is generally higher and the atmosphere more relaxed than the city's commercial shopping areas. It's particularly good for authenticated handicrafts at fair prices — stallholders are working artisans rather than traders, so you can often see the work being made.
