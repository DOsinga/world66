The Dr. Ambedkar National Memorial honours B. R. Ambedkar, the anti-caste leader and chief architect of India's constitution. The building is shaped like an open book and includes Buddhist-inspired features such as an Ashoka pillar, Bodhi tree, fountains, and Sanchi-style gateways.
