The Garden of Five Senses is a landscaped garden in south Delhi, partly built over rocky ground near Mehrauli. Its themed areas include Mughal-style planting, lily pools, bamboo courts, herb gardens, and sculpture-filled paths.

It is more curated than wild, but it makes a pleasant break from Delhi's tombs and traffic, especially when paired with the Qutb complex or Mehrauli Archaeological Park nearby.
