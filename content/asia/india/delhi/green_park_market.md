Green Park Market is a busy neighbourhood shopping street just south of Hauz Khas, popular with students and young professionals from the nearby residential colonies. The market has a good mix of affordable clothing stores, stationery shops, bakeries, and casual restaurants. The atmosphere is lively without the tourist-oriented character of Hauz Khas Village itself.

Several of Delhi's popular casual dining chains have branches here, alongside independent cafés and South Indian restaurants that draw in the IIT Delhi community nearby. It is a more down-to-earth and budget-friendly alternative to the restaurant scene in Hauz Khas Village, a short walk away.
