Lajpat Nagar Central Market is one of South Delhi's most popular all-round shopping destinations, a large outdoor market catering to middle-class residents with a broad range of clothing, textiles, accessories, and household goods. The Central Market is the main draw, with hundreds of shops selling readymade garments and fabrics, while the surrounding lanes extend the shopping into specialist areas for jewellery, shoes, and homewares.

The market is particularly well-known for its selection of traditional Indian women's wear — suits, sarees, and dupattas — at affordable prices. Saturday and Sunday afternoons are the busiest times. The market is well-served by the Lajpat Nagar metro station on the Pink Line and makes a practical combination with the nearby Defence Colony food scene.
