Gurdwara Dam Dama Sahib is a Sikh place of worship near Humayun's Tomb. The site is linked with Guru Gobind Singh and was developed in the late 18th century, with later buildings added for priests and pilgrims.

It is most worth visiting as part of a Nizamuddin walk, where the layers of Mughal tombs, shrines, gardens, and living places of worship sit unusually close together.
