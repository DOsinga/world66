Gurdwara Mata Sundri is a Sikh shrine dedicated to Mata Sundri, wife of Guru Gobind Singh. The building combines a square-domed sanctum, arched details, and a marble-paved gallery, and offers a quieter religious stop in central Delhi away from the city's headline monuments.
