This Hanuman Temple near Connaught Place is an old Hindu shrine with an unusual crescent moon fixed on its spire, a detail more often associated with Islamic architecture. It is a compact but busy religious stop in central Delhi, worth visiting respectfully if you are already exploring the area around Jantar Mantar and Bangla Sahib.
