Hauz Khas Village has one of Delhi's most active concentrations of contemporary art galleries, spread through the multi-level medieval buildings overlooking the Hauz Khas tank. Galleries such as Nature Morte and Shrine Empire have been instrumental in building India's contemporary art market over the past two decades, and the village's combination of old architecture and new art creates a distinctive atmosphere.

Most galleries are open Tuesday through Sunday from 11am to 7pm. Entry is free. The village's restaurant and café scene makes it easy to combine gallery hopping with a meal. The galleries tend to be more experimental and internationally connected than those in more institutional museum settings. New solo and group shows open roughly monthly.
