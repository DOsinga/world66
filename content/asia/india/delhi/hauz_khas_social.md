Social at Hauz Khas Village was one of the venues that helped establish the neighbourhood as Delhi's most fashionable nightlife destination. Part of a national bar and co-working space chain, the Hauz Khas branch occupies a multi-level space overlooking the medieval ruins of the Hauz Khas tank — giving it one of the most dramatic bar views in any Indian city.

The bar serves craft cocktails, beer, and a solid menu of Indian-accented sharing plates. The crowd skews young and creative. The venue doubles as a co-working space during the day, transitioning to a bar and live music space in the evenings. Thursday through Saturday nights are lively and booking ahead is advisable.
