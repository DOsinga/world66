INA Market is South Delhi's most popular fresh produce and international food market, a sprawling complex near the Dilli Haat INA metro station. The market has a large section of regular vegetable and spice vendors alongside an unusual collection of stalls selling imported foods, international cheeses, dried fruits, and specialty ingredients that are hard to find elsewhere in the city. It draws both local residents and expats from the nearby diplomatic enclave.

The fish and meat section is active early in the morning when the freshest stock arrives. A small food court attached to the market serves good South Indian breakfasts. The combination of INA Market and the adjacent Dilli Haat craft market makes this corner of South Delhi worth a morning of exploration.
