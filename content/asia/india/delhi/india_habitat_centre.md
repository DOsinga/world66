The India Habitat Centre is one of New Delhi's finest multipurpose cultural complexes, designed by architect Joseph Allen Stein and opened in 1993. The campus blends indoor and outdoor spaces with gardens, water features, and public art installations, creating a green oasis near Lodhi Road. It houses art galleries, an auditorium, a film screening room, restaurants, and a library.

The Visual Arts Gallery hosts rotating exhibitions of contemporary Indian art, and the auditorium presents theatre, classical music, and dance performances throughout the year. The complex is open to the public and its café terraces are popular with Delhi's professional crowd. It's one of the best places in the city to catch a high-quality cultural event at an affordable price.
