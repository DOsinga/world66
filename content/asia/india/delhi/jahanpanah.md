Jahanpanah was the fourth medieval city of Delhi, founded by Muhammad bin Tughlaq in the 14th century. The scattered remains include Adilabad Fort, Begumpur Mosque, Bijay Mandal, Serai Shaji Mahal, and other Tughlaq-era structures.

The area is not a single polished monument, so it suits travellers who enjoy piecing together Delhi's older layers across several stops. Combine it with nearby South Delhi historic sites rather than treating it as a quick photo stop.
