A densely commercial neighbourhood in West Delhi, famous for being Delhi's main wholesale and retail market for textiles, jewellery, shoes, and electronic goods. **Ajmal Khan Road** is the main commercial artery, several kilometres of shops selling everything from wedding saris to cheap electronics. The neighbourhood is loud, crowded, and entirely functional — people come here to shop rather than to sightsee.

Karol Bagh is also one of the more accessible neighbourhoods for budget travellers, with a wide range of guesthouses and hotels, particularly around Pusa Road and the Karol Bagh metro station. The food in the neighbourhood reflects its position as a transit point: **Roshan di Kulfi** is the most famous sweet shop, but the street food stalls along Ajmal Khan Road and the surrounding lanes are reliably good and cheap.
