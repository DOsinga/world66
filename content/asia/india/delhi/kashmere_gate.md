Kashmere Gate is one of Old Delhi's surviving city gates and a charged site from the 1857 rebellion. The structure is not as complete as Delhi's great Mughal monuments, but the battle scars and memorial plaques make it a worthwhile stop for anyone following the city's colonial history.
