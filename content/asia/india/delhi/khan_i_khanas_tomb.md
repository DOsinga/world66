Khan-i-Khana's Tomb was built in 1598 for the wife of Abdul Rahim Khan-i-Khana, one of Akbar's celebrated courtiers and poets. Khan-i-Khana was later buried here himself in 1627.

The Mughal tomb sits close to Humayun's Tomb and Nizamuddin, making it a useful stop for visitors tracing Delhi's 16th- and 17th-century architecture.
