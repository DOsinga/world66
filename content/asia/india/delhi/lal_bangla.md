Lal Bangla is a pair of Mughal-era mausoleums associated with Lal Kunwar, wife of Emperor Jahandar Shah, and her daughter Begum Jaan. The tombs are a quieter stop in a part of Delhi dense with Mughal and colonial history.

Come for a short historical detour rather than a major monument. It pairs best with nearby Nizamuddin and Old Fort sights.
