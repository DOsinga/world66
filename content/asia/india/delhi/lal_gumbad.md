Lal Gumbad is a 14th-century tomb from the Tughlaq period, built for Shaikh Kabir-ud-Din Auliya. Its red sandstone and battered walls make it one of Delhi's older surviving monuments, quieter than the famous tombs but still full of character.

The site rewards travellers who enjoy Delhi's scattered medieval remains. It is best combined with other south Delhi stops rather than treated as a stand-alone expedition.
