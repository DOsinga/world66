The Claridges is Delhi's most distinguished heritage hotel, a colonial-era building on Aurangzeb Road in the heart of Lutyens' Delhi that has been operating since 1952. The white neoclassical facade, wide verandahs, and mature gardens preserve the atmosphere of imperial New Delhi. Even non-guests can visit the hotel's acclaimed Dhaba restaurant (casual Indian street food elevated to fine dining) or take afternoon tea in the lobby.

The hotel's Sevilla restaurant is one of Delhi's better Spanish restaurants, and the bar serves classic cocktails in an atmosphere of unhurried colonial elegance. A walk through the surrounding streets — wide, tree-lined roads with bungalows and embassy residences — gives a sense of the architectural ambitions of Lutyens' planned capital.
