Meharchand Market is a boutique shopping street in Lodhi Colony, popular with design-conscious Delhi residents for its mix of antique dealers, independent home décor stores, fashion boutiques, and cafés. The market runs along a pleasant stretch of road adjacent to the Lodhi Colony residential area and has developed a reputation for curated lifestyle shopping that differs from the mass-market character of most Delhi bazaars.

Several excellent cafés line the street, making it a good pit stop between exploring Lodi Gardens and the India Habitat Centre. The antique shops in particular stock interesting colonial-era furniture, vintage prints, and Indian art objects. It is best visited on weekday mornings when it is quieter.
