Mehrauli village is one of Delhi's eight historical cities and one of the oldest continuously inhabited urban settlements in India, dating from the 10th-century Tomara and Chahamana dynasties. The Phool Walon Ki Sair (Procession of the Flower Sellers) festival held each autumn in Mehrauli is one of Delhi's most cherished Hindu-Muslim syncretic cultural events, commemorating a tradition dating from the Mughal period.

A heritage walk through the village covers the Qutb Minar complex, the Yogmaya Temple, the Adham Khan Tomb, the Rajon ki Baoli stepwell, and dozens of smaller medieval structures within a radius of two kilometres. The Mehrauli Archaeological Park to the south extends the walk further. Independent walking or a guided tour through organisations like DelhiByFoot makes for a rewarding half-day of historical exploration.
