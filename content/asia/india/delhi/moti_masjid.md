Moti Masjid is a small 18th-century Mughal mosque near the dargah of Qutbuddin Bakhtiar Kaki in Mehrauli. Its name refers to the white marble facing, and the mosque preserves the late Mughal tradition of building intimate marble prayer spaces within larger religious complexes.

It is best seen while exploring Mehrauli's dense cluster of tombs, mosques, stepwells, and ruins.
