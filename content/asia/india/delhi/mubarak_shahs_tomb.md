Mubarak Shah's Tomb is a damaged Sayyid-period monument whose surviving west and south gates hint at a once larger complex. The area also has several Lodi-era tombs and a baoli, making it a worthwhile stop for visitors exploring Delhi's lesser-known medieval remains.
