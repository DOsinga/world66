Najaf Khan's Tomb is a quiet Mughal-era memorial south of Lodi Road, dedicated to the Persian adventurer and military commander who rose to power in 18th-century Delhi. The tomb is modest compared with Humayun's Tomb or Safdarjung's Tomb, but it rewards a short detour for its calm garden setting.

Visit while exploring the Lodi Colony and Lodi Gardens area, where several small tombs and later monuments sit within walking distance of each other.
