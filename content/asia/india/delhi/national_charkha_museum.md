The National Charkha Museum is a small museum dedicated to the spinning wheel and its place in India's independence movement. Its most visible feature is a giant stainless-steel charkha, billed as the world's largest spinning wheel. The museum is close to Connaught Place, so it is easiest to treat as a short add-on while exploring central New Delhi.
