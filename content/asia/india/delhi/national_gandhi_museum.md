The National Gandhi Museum stands near Raj Ghat and focuses on Mahatma Gandhi's life, work, and assassination. Its galleries display photographs, paintings, personal objects, and documents, including one of Gandhi's walking sticks and clothing connected with his final day.

The museum is a useful companion to Raj Ghat rather than a large stand-alone museum. Come for context on Gandhi's public campaigns, the independence movement, and the way his memory is presented in modern India.
