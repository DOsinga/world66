Delhi's National Rail Museum is a strong stop for anyone interested in India's railway history. The collection includes historic locomotives, saloon cars used by princely states and British viceroys, and exhibits that show how railways tied the subcontinent together.

It is also one of Delhi's better family museums, with enough large hardware outside to keep children interested even before the indoor displays.
