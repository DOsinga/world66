The National Science Centre is a hands-on science museum near Purana Qila, with galleries on dinosaurs, DNA, energy, and Indian science and technology. It is especially useful for families and anyone travelling with children who need a break from forts and monuments.

The displays are broad rather than polished in the style of a new museum, but the interactive exhibits make it one of Delhi's better rainy-day or hot-afternoon stops.
