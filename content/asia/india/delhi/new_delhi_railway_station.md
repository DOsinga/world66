New Delhi Railway Station is the main rail hub for the capital and the gateway through which most overland travellers first encounter central Delhi. The station itself is a logistical behemoth handling hundreds of trains and hundreds of thousands of passengers daily, but the area directly to its west — the Paharganj Main Bazaar — is one of the classic budget traveller districts of South Asia.

The Paharganj strip runs west from the station's main entrance, lined with guesthouses, street food stalls, travel agents, and shops selling everything from backpacks to tablas. The food scene mixes north Indian dhabas with Israeli-style falafel and banana pancake cafes, reflecting the neighbourhood's decades as an international backpacker hub. Despite its scruffy reputation it is a functional, affordable base for exploring Delhi's major sights.
