The area around Hazrat Nizamuddin Railway Station is one of Delhi's most atmospheric transport hubs, mixing the activity of a major rail interchange with the devotional atmosphere of the nearby dargah. The station serves many of Delhi's most important long-distance trains including the Rajdhani and Shatabdi express services to Agra, Mumbai, and Kolkata. Several old dhabas near the station entrance have been serving travellers for decades.

The food at the stalls around the station is particularly good: kebabs, nihari (slow-cooked mutton), and puri-sabzi from vendors who know their customers have limited time and hunger sharpened by travel. The Mughlai food tradition of Nizamuddin is one of Delhi's finest, and a meal here after visiting the dargah is one of the better cheap food experiences in the city.
