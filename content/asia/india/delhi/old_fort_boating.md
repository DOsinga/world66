type: poi

The moat surrounding Purana Qila (Old Fort) has been landscaped into a pleasant boating lake and children's park, making it a popular destination for Delhi families on weekends. Pedal boats and rowboats are available for hire at affordable prices. The lake is surrounded by flowering trees and the ancient walls of the fort create a dramatic backdrop for the activity.

A small amusement area with rides for younger children and a snack bar make this a full family outing option. The lake is at its best in the cooler months from October to March. The combination of boating with a visit to the fort and adjacent National Zoological Park fills an enjoyable half-day in this part of Delhi.
