Sunder Nagar Market is a small, elegant antiques and crafts market near the Pandara Road area of central Delhi, popular with collectors, interior designers, and visitors looking for quality Indian antiques. The compact market has around 30 to 40 shops selling Mughal-period miniature paintings, old bronzes, tribal artefacts, colonial-era curios, silver jewellery, and traditional handicrafts — a more curated selection than the larger flea markets.

The market operates Tuesday through Sunday and is best visited in the late morning when all shops are open. Prices reflect the quality and provenance of the items, and the proprietors are generally knowledgeable. It makes a good complement to the National Museum nearby, contextualising objects in the collection with things you can actually purchase.
