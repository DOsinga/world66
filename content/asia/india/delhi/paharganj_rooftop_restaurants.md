The rooftop cafés and restaurants of Paharganj have been feeding international travellers for decades, and several classics have survived the neighbourhood's relentless churn of guesthouses. The upper floors of hotels along the Main Bazaar open onto terraces with views over the roofscape of central Delhi toward the domes and towers visible on the horizon — good for people-watching and watching the kites wheel over the city below.

The food is a globalised version of traveller classics: Israeli salads, banana porridge, thalis, lassi, and pizzas all appear on the same laminated menus. Standards vary, but the best kitchens are genuinely good and the prices are among the lowest in the city. Madan Café and Sonu Chat Bhandar on the main strip have loyal followings. Best at breakfast and lunch before the midday heat sets in.
