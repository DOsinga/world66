Parliament Museum tells the story of India's parliamentary democracy, with exhibits on the freedom struggle, constitution-making, and the working of the Lok Sabha. It also holds gifts and ceremonial objects received from foreign delegates.

It is most useful for travellers with a strong interest in modern Indian history and government. Check access rules before going, as security and opening arrangements around the Parliament complex can change.
