The Prime Ministers' Museum & Library occupies the Teen Murti complex, once the residence of Jawaharlal Nehru. The site combines material on Nehru, India's post-independence prime ministers, a major research library, and a planetarium, making it one of New Delhi's stronger stops for modern Indian history.
