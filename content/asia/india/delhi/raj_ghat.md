Raj Ghat is the simple black-marble memorial marking the place where Mahatma Gandhi was cremated in 1948. The platform sits in a quiet walled garden near the Yamuna, with an eternal flame and paths that make the visit feel more like a pause than a monument checklist.

Security and closures can tighten around national holidays and Gandhi anniversaries. Combine it with nearby Old Delhi sights if you want a fuller sense of the city around the memorial.
