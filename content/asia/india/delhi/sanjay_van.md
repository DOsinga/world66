Sanjay Van is a wooded stretch of Delhi's Aravalli ridge, with rough trails and a much wilder feel than the city's formal gardens. The Qutub Institutional Area and Neela Hauz Bridge entrances are among the simpler access points, but bring water and sturdy shoes because facilities inside are limited.
