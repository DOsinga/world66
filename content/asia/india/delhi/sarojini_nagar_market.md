Sarojini Nagar Market is one of Delhi's best-known discount shopping destinations, a sprawling outdoor market where surplus export garments, fashion accessories, and household goods are sold at prices that attract bargain hunters from across the city. The market is particularly famous for export-overrun clothing — branded and designer items that didn't make it into the supply chain — sold at a fraction of their original price.

The market is always busy and at its most chaotic on weekends. The narrow lanes are packed with colourful stalls and the haggling culture is alive here more than in most Delhi markets. It's best visited on a weekday morning. Bring cash as most vendors do not accept cards, and leave valuables at home as the crowds can be dense.
