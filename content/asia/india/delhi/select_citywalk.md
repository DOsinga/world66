Select CityWalk in Saket is consistently rated among India's best shopping malls, located just south of the Hauz Khas area near the South Delhi ridge. The mall houses international brands alongside Indian fashion labels, a good food court, a multiplex cinema, and a wide range of casual and upmarket dining options. Its open-air design with a central plaza makes it feel less claustrophobic than many Indian malls.

It is popular with South Delhi residents as a social hub as much as a shopping destination. The attached PVR cinema screens mainstream Bollywood and Hollywood films as well as art house titles on dedicated screens. Easily reached by auto or metro from central Delhi.
