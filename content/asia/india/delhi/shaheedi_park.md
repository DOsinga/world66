Shaheedi Park is an open-air sculpture museum focused on Indian history and public memory. Its large collection includes bas-reliefs, busts, and full statues of freedom fighters, soldiers, thinkers, rulers, sages, and deities.

The park is most useful as an outdoor history stop near central Delhi's museums and monuments, especially for visitors interested in how national figures are represented in public art.
