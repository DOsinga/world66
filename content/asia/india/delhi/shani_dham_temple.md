Shani Dham Temple is a Hindu temple in south Delhi known for its large natural rock statue of Shani, the deity associated with Saturn. The temple draws devotees, especially on Saturdays, and has a more local devotional feel than Delhi's major headline monuments.

It is most useful as an add-on for travellers already exploring the Mehrauli area rather than as a stand-alone cross-city trip.
