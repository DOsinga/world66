Sri Sri Radha Parthasarathi Mandir is Delhi's ISKCON temple, dedicated to Krishna and Radha in the form of Radha Parthasarathi. Alongside the temple halls, the complex has devotional exhibits, multimedia presentations, and a lively stream of worshippers.

It is a useful stop for families and for travelers interested in contemporary Hindu devotional life, with food available at Govinda's restaurant on site.
