St Peter's Cathedral is the Delhi headquarters of the Jacobite Syrian Orthodox Church. Its architecture blends Oriental church forms with modern elements, giving it a different character from many of central Delhi's better-known religious buildings.

It is a focused stop for visitors interested in Delhi's Christian communities and the variety of religious architecture in the city.
