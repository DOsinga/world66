The Sulabh International Museum of Toilets is one of Delhi's stranger specialist museums, but it has a serious subject: the history of sanitation and the social problems created by its absence. Displays trace toilet design and hygiene practices across different periods and countries.

It is a deliberate detour rather than a casual old-city stop. Go if you like unusual museums, design history, or the practical side of public health that most travel guides skip.
