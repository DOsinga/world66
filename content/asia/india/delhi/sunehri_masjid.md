Sunehri Masjid is an 18th-century mosque in Old Delhi, built by the Mughal noble Roshan-ud-Daula during the reign of Muhammad Shah. Its name means "golden mosque", a reminder of the gilded domes that once made it stand out above the crowded streets.

It is a compact historic stop near Chandni Chowk, best visited as part of a walk through Old Delhi's markets, shrines, and narrow lanes.
