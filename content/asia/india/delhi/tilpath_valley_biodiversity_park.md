Tilpath Valley Biodiversity Park is a restored green area northwest of Asola Bhatti Wildlife Sanctuary. Developed from 2015 to 2018, it is best suited to visitors looking for a quieter nature stop on Delhi's southern edge rather than another monument-heavy itinerary.
