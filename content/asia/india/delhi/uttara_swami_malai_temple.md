Uttara Swami Malai Temple, also known as Malai Mandir, is a South Indian-style Hindu temple in South Delhi dedicated to Lord Swaminatha. The main shrine is built entirely of granite and draws on Chola temple forms.

It is most rewarding for visitors interested in Delhi's many regional religious communities, as the complex feels quite different from the Mughal and north Indian monuments that dominate most sightseeing routes.
