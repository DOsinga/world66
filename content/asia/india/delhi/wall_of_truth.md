The Wall of Truth is a memorial to Sikhs killed during the 1984 anti-Sikh riots. It also remembers Sikh soldiers and the Hindu and Muslim neighbours who were killed while trying to protect Sikhs during the violence.

Its central location makes it a sober stop for travellers interested in modern Delhi history.
