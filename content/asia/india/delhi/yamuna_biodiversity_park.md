Yamuna Biodiversity Park is a restored wetland and woodland habitat in North Delhi, created to bring back some of the river plain's native ecology. It is best known for birds, with both resident and migratory species using the lakes, grassland, and reed beds.

It is not central Delhi's easiest sight, but it is a good change of pace if you want green space and wildlife rather than tombs, bazaars, and traffic.
