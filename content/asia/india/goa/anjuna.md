[Anjuna Beach](/asia/india/goa/anjuna/anjuna_beach) is one of the most popular beach villages in north [Goa](/asia/india/goa), known since the 1960s as a gathering point for the international hippie trail. The all-night beach parties with trance music still draw a diverse crowd from around the world, though the scene has mellowed considerably from its wilder days — [Shiva Valley](/asia/india/goa/anjuna/shivas_bar) at the south end of the beach is the most consistent of the survivors.

The [Wednesday flea market](/asia/india/goa/anjuna/anjuna_flea_market) is the best reason to leave the beach for a few hours — go early, ideally by 7am, before the heat and the crowds build. You will find everything from Kashmiri textiles to Tibetan jewellery to second-hand paperbacks. The quieter [Saturday Night Market](/asia/india/goa/anjuna/saturday_night_market) at nearby Arpora is its evening cousin.

The [Albuquerque Mansion](/asia/india/goa/anjuna/albuquerque_mansion), built in the 1920s and modelled on the palace of the Sultan of [Zanzibar](/africa/tanzania/zanzibar), is worth a detour for its faded grandeur. A short walk north brings you to the ruins of [Chapora Fort](/asia/india/goa/anjuna/chapora_fort), one of the best sunset spots on the coast.
