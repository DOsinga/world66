---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

The Goan high season runs **mid-November to mid-February**: dry, sunny, daytime highs around 30°C, nights cool enough for a shirt. This is when Calangute fills up — book everything, expect Christmas and New Year to be packed and expensive.

**March to May** is hotter, stickier and quieter, with prices noticeably lower; the sea is still swimmable. **June to September** is the monsoon — the beach shacks are dismantled, many restaurants close, and the sea turns brown and dangerous, but the inland landscape erupts in green. The October "shoulder" is the underrated month: shacks rebuilding, prices low, weather almost dry.

If you can avoid the **Christmas–New Year** week and **mid-December** Sunburn Festival weeks, do.
