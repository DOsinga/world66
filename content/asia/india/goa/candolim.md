Candolim is one of the quieter beach stretches along North Goa's coast, sitting between the busier [Calangute](/asia/india/goa/calangute) to the south and the walls of [Fort Aguada](/asia/india/goa/candolim/fort_aguada) to the north. The beach is long and wide, backed by a mix of shacks, guesthouses, and the occasional colonial-era villa half-hidden behind palm trees.

The area draws visitors who want Goa's beach life without the full-throttle party scene of Baga or [Anjuna](/asia/india/goa/anjuna). [Candolim Beach](/asia/india/goa/candolim/candolim_beach) and its shacks serve fresh seafood and cold Kingfishers, and the main road running parallel to the coast has a good concentration of restaurants, from cheap Goan thalis to more polished places. [Fort Aguada](/asia/india/goa/candolim/fort_aguada), built by the Portuguese in 1612 to guard the mouth of the Mandovi River, sits at the southern tip of the peninsula and is an easy walk down [Sinquerim Beach](/asia/india/goa/candolim/sinquerim_beach). The old [Aguada Lighthouse](/asia/india/goa/candolim/aguada_lighthouse) and the [Fort Aguada Jail Museum](/asia/india/goa/candolim/fort_aguada_jail_museum) sit within the same complex and are worth combining into a single morning.
