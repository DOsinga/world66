---
title: "Getting Around"
type: section
---

Candolim is strung out along a single main road parallel to the beach. Walking it from end to end takes 30-40 minutes; tuk-tuks are everywhere for short hops.

**Scooter rental** is the standard way to explore the wider area — expect to pay around 400-600 rupees a day. Get a helmet, check the brakes, and remember that Goan traffic is unforgiving. Petrol stations are scarce so fill up before any longer trip.

**Taxis** are easy to find at the main beach entrance and tuk-tuks line the main road. Both have set "tourist" prices that locals do not pay — agree the fare before you set off. The Goa Miles app gives metered fares for taxis if you prefer the certainty.

For trips to Panaji, Calangute, or Mapusa, **local buses** run along the main road for next to nothing, though schedules are loose.
