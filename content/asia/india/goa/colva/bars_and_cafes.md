---
title: "Bars and Cafes"
type: section
---

Colva's bar scene is low-key by Goa standards. The beach shacks shift into bar mode after sunset, serving cold beer and local feni to a relaxed crowd. There are a handful of dedicated bars in the village that stay open later and draw a mix of domestic tourists and long-stay visitors. The pace is nothing like the clubs and beach parties of North Goa — which is exactly the point.
