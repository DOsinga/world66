One of the more established venues on the busy beach strip, Leda Lounge operates as both restaurant and bar from midday into the evening. The seafood platters — a spread of grilled fish, prawns, and calamari — are the thing to order, and the Goan fish curry is reliably good. The setting is the standard Colva arrangement: shaded tables facing the sand, beach breeze, slow pace. A reasonable choice if you want something slightly more structured than the basic shacks.
