---
title: Things to Do in Mapusa
type: section
---

Mapusa is a working town, not a resort. Its main attraction is its market, and the best way to experience the place is to arrive on a Friday morning, wander through the bazaar, eat something from one of the stalls, and head out to the beaches in the afternoon. The churches, the theatre, and the surroundings offer more if you have the time.
