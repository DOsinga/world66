Margao rewards a full day rather than a quick transit stop. The old Catholic quarter around the [Church of the Holy Spirit](/asia/india/goa/margao/church_of_the_holy_spirit) and the [Sat Burzam Ghor](/asia/india/goa/margao/sat_burzam_ghor) mansion can be covered on foot in a morning; the [covered market](/asia/india/goa/margao/margao_covered_market) is best visited early. The [Goa Chitra Museum](/asia/india/goa/margao/goa_chitra_museum) in Benaulim, a short autorickshaw ride away, is worth two to three hours on its own.
