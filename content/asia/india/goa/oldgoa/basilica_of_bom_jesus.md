The Basilica of Bom Jesus is the most important church in Goa and one of the finest examples of Baroque architecture in Asia. Completed in 1605, it is dedicated to the Infant Jesus and holds the mortal remains of St Francis Xavier — the Jesuit missionary who brought Christianity to India, Japan, and much of East Asia — in an ornate silver and glass casket raised on an elaborate mausoleum inside the right transept.

The exterior is imposing: the facade combines Doric, Corinthian, and Composite columns with basalt inlay work, and the interior is dark and richly carved. The side chapel and the sacristy are particularly fine. The church was declared a Minor Basilica in 1946 and is protected as part of the UNESCO World Heritage Site. Entry is free; photography inside is restricted.
