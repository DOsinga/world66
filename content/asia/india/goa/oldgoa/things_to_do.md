Old Goa is a compact site — most of the major monuments are within walking distance of each other. Start at the Basilica of Bom Jesus, then cross the road to the Se Cathedral and the Church of St Francis of Assisi (which contains the Archaeological Museum). From there, the short climb to Holy Hill takes you to the Convent of St Monica, the Museum of Christian Art, and the ruins of St Augustine's Church. Allow at least three hours for a thorough visit; four if you want to sit in the buildings and take in the atmosphere properly.
