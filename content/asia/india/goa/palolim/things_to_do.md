---
title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Palolem is a beach village, and most of the day's plan revolves around water — swimming the half-moon bay, kayaking out to the small offshore islands, walking south to quieter Patnem, or taking a boat to look for dolphins at dawn. Inland, a short hike up the headland leads to small Hindu shrines tucked among rocks and palms.
