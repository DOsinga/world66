---
title: "Bars and Cafes"
type: section
---

Goa drinks more freely than most Indian states — alcohol is cheap, taxed lightly, and consumed openly — and Panaji's drinking scene is among the most relaxed in the country. The classic place to drink is São Tomé Square, where **Joseph Bar** (Goa's oldest tavern) and a handful of newer competitors keep a small crowd standing on the cobbles outside well into the night. *Feni*, the local cashew or coconut spirit, is the default short; Kings beer or a Sula white is the long drink.

For coffee, the small **Café Bhonsle** near the bus stand has been a Panaji institution since 1947 — strong filter coffee, sweet sticky buns, and a working-class breakfast crowd. **Black Sheep Bistro** and several Fontainhas places now serve flat whites and *bossa nova* for a different crowd.
