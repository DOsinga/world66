The most essential day trip from Panaji is **Old Goa**, the abandoned former Portuguese capital nine kilometres upriver. The complex of 16th- and 17th-century churches and cathedrals — Bom Jesus (with the relics of St Francis Xavier), Sé Cathedral, St Cajetan — is a UNESCO World Heritage Site and one of the most extraordinary colonial-era ensembles anywhere in Asia. Half a day at least.

The North Goa beach belt — **Calangute, Baga, Anjuna**, and the smaller villages stretching up to Arambol — is twenty to forty minutes by road. **Mapusa**, the market town inland from the beaches, has Goa's best Friday morning market. South of Panaji, **Margao** is the bigger commercial town and the gateway to the quieter southern beaches of Colva and Palolem.
