---
title: "Eating Out"
type: section
---

Panaji's food is the Goan-Portuguese hybrid at its richest: vindaloo (from the Portuguese *vinho d'alhos*, garlic-and-wine), pork sorpotel, prawn balchao, chicken xacuti, the fish curry-rice that is the daily lunch of most of the state, the dense slow-cooked beef *bafat*, and the inescapable *bebinca* layer cake for dessert. Pair with a bottle of Goan *feni* — cashew or coconut — or local Kings beer.

The atmospheric restaurants cluster in **Fontainhas** and São Tomé, in restored Portuguese houses with shaded verandas. The cheaper local eating is along **18th June Road** and around the market, with thali joints serving fish curry-rice at lunchtime for very little money. The hotel and resort restaurants out at Miramar lean upmarket but rarely beat the city-centre kitchens for taste.
