---
title: "Getting There"
type: section
---

**By air.** Goa has two airports. **Dabolim** (GOI), 30 km south of Panaji, is the older airport — still the busy domestic terminal, also used for some international flights. **Manohar International Airport** (GOX) at Mopa, 35 km north, opened in 2022 and now handles most international charter and scheduled flights. Pre-paid taxis from either airport cost around ₹1,000 to Panaji; the drive is about an hour from each.

**By train.** Goa's main railway station is **Madgaon (Margao)**, 30 km south of Panaji — Konkan Railway trains from Mumbai (8–10 hours), Mangalore, Pune, and points across India. From Madgaon, take a bus or pre-paid taxi to Panaji. The smaller Karmali station, closer to Panaji, also takes some Konkan Railway services.

**By bus.** The Kadamba bus stand in central Panaji runs frequent buses to Mumbai (overnight, 12 hours), Bangalore (10 hours), Mangalore, Hampi, and Pune. KSRTC and KTC are the main operators; sleeper buses are reasonably comfortable.

**By road.** NH66 runs the length of the Konkan coast; NH4A connects Goa to Bangalore. Driving in Goa is easier than in most Indian states; rental scooters and small cars are widely available.
