Goa Science Centre and Planetarium is a family-oriented science museum by Miramar Beach. It has interactive science exhibits and a small theatre that screens 3D science films.

It is best treated as a practical rainy-day or family stop rather than a classic Goan sight, but its beachside location makes it easy to combine with Miramar and Dona Paula.
