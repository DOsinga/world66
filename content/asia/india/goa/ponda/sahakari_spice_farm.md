Sahakari Spice Farm is the most straightforward way to turn Ponda's spice-country reputation into an actual visit. Tours usually focus on pepper, cardamom, nutmeg, cashew, and other crops, followed by a Goan meal. It is touristy, but still useful if you want to understand the plants behind the flavours on the table.
