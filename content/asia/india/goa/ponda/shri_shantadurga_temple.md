Shri Shantadurga Temple is one of the key reasons to come inland from the coast. The temple stands at Kavlem, with a warm-coloured facade, arched windows, lamp tower, and the kind of layered history that explains Ponda's role as a refuge for Hindu worship during Portuguese rule. It is an active place of devotion, so dress and move accordingly.
