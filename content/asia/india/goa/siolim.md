Siolim is a quiet village on the northern bank of the Chapora River, about 7 km from [Mapusa](/asia/india/goa/mapusa). It sits at a natural crossing point that once served ferries between North Goa's beaches and the interior, and that history gives the village its unhurried, between-places character.

The Wednesday market at the old Ferry Terminal is worth a visit — a mix of local produce and goods that draws villagers from across the area. [St. Anthony's Church](/asia/india/goa/siolim/st_anthonys_church) and the Sateri Temple are the main landmarks. A short drive west brings you to [Chapora Fort](/asia/india/goa/siolim/chapora_fort), whose crumbling ramparts offer one of the finest panoramic views in North Goa. Most of the northern beaches — Vagator, Anjuna, Morjim — are within a 10-minute drive.
