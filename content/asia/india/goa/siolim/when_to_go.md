---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

The best time to visit Siolim and North Goa is **November through February**, when the monsoon has cleared, the air is dry, and temperatures are pleasant (25–30°C). The beaches are at their best and the village comes alive with visitors and seasonal restaurants.

March and April are hotter and more crowded as Easter approaches. The **monsoon runs from June through September** — Siolim gets heavy rain, the sea is rough and off-limits for swimming, and many businesses close. The quiet and the lush greenery have their own appeal for those who don't mind the rain.

The **feast of St. Anthony** (June 13) falls during the monsoon but is celebrated regardless of the weather with a procession through the village.
