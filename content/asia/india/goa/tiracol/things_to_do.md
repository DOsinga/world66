---
title: 'Things to Do'
type: section
---

Tiracol is a one-fort village with a couple of beaches and a ferry crossing. The fort itself, the small Portuguese church inside its walls, and the dramatic estuary headland are the headline sights; the empty rocks-and-sand beach below and the short hop across to Querim's much longer beach add another half-day. Almost everyone who comes either drives across the Tiracol bridge from north Goa or takes the small ferry from Querim to Tiracol jetty.
