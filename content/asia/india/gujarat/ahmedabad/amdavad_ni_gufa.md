Amdavad ni Gufa is an underground art gallery near Gujarat University, designed by Balkrishna Doshi to show works by M. F. Husain. The domed, cave-like space makes the architecture as much of a draw as the art.

The garden and cafe above ground make it an easy stop while exploring Navrangpura and Ahmedabad's modern architecture.
