The Auto World Vintage Car Museum holds the private collection of the late industrialist **Pranlal Bhogilal** — over 100 cars, motorcycles, and royal carriages including Rolls-Royces of the Maharajas of Gujarat, vintage Bentleys, and a 1923 Chrysler that belonged to the Aga Khan. The collection is housed in a converted farmhouse 12 kilometres north of central Ahmedabad on the Gandhinagar road.

Some cars are driveable and the museum offers short rides in vintage Rolls-Royces for a hefty fee. Allow 90 minutes. Closed on Tuesdays.
