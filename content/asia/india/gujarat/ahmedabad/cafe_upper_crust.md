Upper Crust has been one of Ahmedabad's more upmarket cafes since the late 1990s — wood-panelled, with a serious cake counter, real espresso, and a small menu of European mains (lasagne, sandwiches, soups). It is the spot to read the paper or work for a few hours without being hurried. Branches on C.G. Road, Bodakdev, and at the airport.
