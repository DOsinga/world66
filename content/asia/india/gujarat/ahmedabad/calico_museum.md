The Calico Museum of Textiles is one of the world's great textile collections — five centuries of Indian fabrics, costumes, temple hangings, royal tents, and embroidered shawls displayed in a beautifully carved wooden haveli inside the Sarabhai family compound. Highlights include the 17th-century Mughal court hangings, the **kalamkari** painted hangings from Tamil Nadu, the gold-thread **zardozi** dress of the Maratha rulers, and a tented hall recreating a Mughal court reception.

Visits are by guided tour only and capped at 20 people. The morning tour at 10:30 focuses on religious textiles; the afternoon tour at 2:45 covers secular and court textiles. Reservations open at 10am the day before — call ahead. No photography is allowed and the no-bag policy is strict.
