The most popular short day trips from Ahmedabad are northbound. The **[Adalaj Stepwell](/asia/india/gujarat/ahmedabad/adalaj_stepwell)** at 18 km and the giant **[Akshardham temple](/asia/india/gujarat/ahmedabad/akshardham_gandhinagar)** in Gandhinagar can be combined in a half day. The **[Thol Lake Bird Sanctuary](/asia/india/gujarat/ahmedabad/thol_lake)** at 30 km is the local birding stop, with winter flocks of flamingos and migratory waterfowl.

For longer trips, **[Lothal](/asia/india/gujarat/lothal)** — the 4,000-year-old Indus Valley dockyard — lies 78 km southwest. **[Champaner-Pavagadh](/asia/india/gujarat/champaner)** UNESCO archaeological park is 150 km east and combines well with the city of **[Vadodara](/asia/india/gujarat/vadodara)** for an overnight. **Modhera Sun Temple** at 100 km north makes a good full-day excursion combined with the **Patan Stepwell** (Rani-ki-Vav) — both World Heritage sites.
