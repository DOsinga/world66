---
title: "Getting Around"
type: section
---

Ahmedabad has a growing **metro** with two lines crossing the city — useful for the long east-west commute from the airport area to Vastrapur and out to APMC. The flat fare and air-conditioning make it the easiest way to cover distance.

For everything else, **auto-rickshaws** are the standard. Drivers will quote tourist prices: insist on the meter, or agree a fare in advance — about ₹30 per kilometre is reasonable. **Uber** and **Ola** both operate and remove the negotiation. The **BRTS** (Bus Rapid Transit) is fast on dedicated lanes but the routes take some study.

The old walled city is best on foot — the streets are too narrow for cars, the cycle-rickshaws too rough, and walking is the only way to see the pols properly. Wear comfortable shoes and bring water.
