Gopi has been doing the Gujarati thali for over fifty years and remains one of the city's most reliable. The unlimited thali is the standard format — small bowls of dal, kadhi, two or three shaaks, rotli, rice, papad, pickle, and sweets — but the quality is consistently good and the staff cheerful about endless refills. The dining hall is plain, air-conditioned, and gets busy at lunch. Cash only at the door.
