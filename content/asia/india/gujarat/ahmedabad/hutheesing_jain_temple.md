The Hutheesing Temple, completed in 1848 by Sheth Hutheesing Kesarising, a wealthy Ahmedabad merchant, is one of the finest 19th-century Jain temples in Gujarat. The plan follows classical western-Indian temple architecture — a central hall with 52 small shrines around the perimeter, all in carved white marble. The main shrine houses **Dharmanath**, the 15th Jain Tirthankara.

The temple is active and worshippers expect quiet and respect — non-Jains are welcome but should leave leather goods (belts, wallets) at the entrance. Photography is limited. A separate **Manastambha** column, added in 2000, stands in the courtyard.
