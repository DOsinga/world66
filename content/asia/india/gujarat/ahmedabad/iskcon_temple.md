ISKCON Temple Ahmedabad is a large Krishna temple in the city's western suburbs, dedicated to Radha and Krishna. The complex is busiest during evening prayers and festival periods, when devotional music and food offerings draw a steady local crowd.

It is a useful stop if you are staying in New West Ahmedabad or want to see the city's contemporary Hindu devotional scene beyond the old walled-city temples.
