The graves are still part of the dining room and are tended daily."
---

Lucky is the famous Lal Darwaja cafe where you eat your bun-and-tea with twelve medieval Muslim graves in the middle of the room. The food is unremarkable Iranian-Indian cafe fare: tea, paneer keema, akoori scrambled eggs, soft white buns. The reason to come is the atmosphere — a small, cluttered cafe with old photographs, a 1960s air-conditioner, and the painted graves under glass-topped tables.

A small **M.F. Husain mural** in the back room is owner Krishanan's prized possession, painted in the 1990s when the artist used the cafe as his Ahmedabad office.
