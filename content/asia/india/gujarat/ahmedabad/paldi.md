Paldi is the southwestern district of Ahmedabad, just below Ellisbridge, and is the city's modernist architecture trail. The **Sanskar Kendra** (Le Corbusier, 1957), the **Sangath** studio of Balkrishna Doshi (1981, where Doshi worked until his death in 2023), the **National Institute of Design** campus by Gautam Sarabhai, and the **Tagore Hall** are all within walking distance. The district also contains the **Vasna Barrage**, which dams the Sabarmati for the riverfront.

The neighbourhood is residential and quiet, with a few good cafes and the **Vasna Cremation Ground**, designed by Doshi in the 1980s and now considered one of his masterpieces.
