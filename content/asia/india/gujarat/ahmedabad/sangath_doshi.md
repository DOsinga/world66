Sangath — meaning **moving together** in Sanskrit — is the studio Balkrishna Doshi built for himself in 1981 and continued to work in until his death. The half-buried vaulted ceramic-tiled roof, terraced gardens, and informal sunken meeting spaces became one of his most influential designs and an explicit alternative to International Modernism. The building was added to the Pritzker citation when Doshi won the prize in 2018.

The office is still active and visitors can request a brief look from the gate. The building reads as a series of grass mounds with white-tile vaults — almost agricultural — until the sliding glass doors reveal an interior packed with Indian craft furniture and Doshi's drawings.
