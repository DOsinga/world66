Vastrapur Lake is an urban lake and popular evening gathering spot in Ahmedabad's western districts. A walking path circles the water, with lawns, gardens, an open-air theatre, and space for occasional cultural events.

It is most useful as a relaxed local stop rather than a major sightseeing destination.
