Vishalla was opened in 1978 by the late industrialist **Surendra Patel** as a recreation of a Gujarati village dining experience. Diners sit on the floor on woven mats, are served on **palas** leaf-plates by waiters in traditional dress, and eat the standard Gujarati thali while a string of performances — puppet shows, folk dance, classical music — runs in the open courtyards. There is also an excellent and free **Vechaar Utensils Museum** on the same site, with over 4,500 traditional kitchen and water utensils from across India.

The whole place is theatrical but the food is genuinely good. Lunch and dinner sittings; book ahead. About 40 minutes' drive from the centre.
