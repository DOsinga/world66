Arasuri Ambaji Shaktipith Temple is the reason Ambaji exists as a major pilgrimage town. It is one of the important Shaktipeeth shrines, associated with the goddess Amba, and draws worshippers from Gujarat, Rajasthan, and farther across India.

The approach is as much part of the visit as the sanctum. Lanes around the temple are thick with flower sellers, prasad stalls, pilgrims, and small shops selling devotional objects. Expect queues on ordinary days and heavy crowds around full moon dates and major festivals. Dress modestly, leave cameras where required, and give yourself time to move with the rhythm of the place rather than against it.
