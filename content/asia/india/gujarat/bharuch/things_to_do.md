---
title: Things to Do
type: section
---

Bharuch rewards a few hours of exploration: the fort on the hill above town, the Narmada riverfront, and the old bazaar quarter make a compact circuit. The city's real draw is its ancient trading history — this was a port known to Greeks, Romans, and Arab navigators — rather than any single monument. Most visitors pass through en route to the Narmada dam sites or the coastal resorts further south.
