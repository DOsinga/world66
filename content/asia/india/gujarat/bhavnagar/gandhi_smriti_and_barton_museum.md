Gandhi Smriti is Bhavnagar's main civic museum stop, close to the central clock tower. Its Gandhi connection matters because Gandhi studied at Samaldas College in Bhavnagar before leaving India for legal studies in London.

The attached Barton Museum broadens the visit with regional material, including archaeological finds and local collections. It is not a grand national museum, but it gives the city a cultural centre of gravity.
