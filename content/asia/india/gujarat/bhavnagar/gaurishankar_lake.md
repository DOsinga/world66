Gaurishankar Lake, often called Bor Talav, is Bhavnagar's old reservoir and a popular local evening spot. It sits beside Victoria Park, so the lake and forest work best as a single easy outing.

Do not expect wilderness. Come for the sunset, the everyday local scene, and the contrast with the dense city nearby.
