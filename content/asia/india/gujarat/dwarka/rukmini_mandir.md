Rukmini Mandir sits outside the main bustle of Dwarka, which gives it a different feel from the town's central pilgrim circuit. The temple is dedicated to Rukmini, Krishna's consort, and its carved exterior rewards a closer look before you step inside.

It is a natural addition to a Dwarka temple round rather than a standalone journey. The slight distance from the main shrine helps, though: the visit feels calmer, and the temple's mythology adds another strand to the city's Krishna-focused landscape.
