Indroda Dinosaur & Fossil Park is Gandhinagar's best-known science stop, built around Gujarat's fossil finds and dinosaur nesting sites. The displays include fossilized eggs, tracks, bones, and life-size dinosaur models, which makes it especially strong for children. It is more educational park than polished museum, but the subject matter is memorable.
