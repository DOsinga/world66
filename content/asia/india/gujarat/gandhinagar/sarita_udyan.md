Sarita Udyan is one of Gandhinagar's relaxed green spaces, set near the Sabarmati and used by locals for walks, picnics, and unhurried evenings. In a planned capital of broad roads and government sectors, that softness matters.

It is not a destination to cross Gujarat for, but it works well if you are already in Gandhinagar and want a simple break outdoors. Visit in the cooler parts of the day, when families and walkers give the park its everyday character.
