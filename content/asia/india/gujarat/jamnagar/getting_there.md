---
title: "Getting There"
type: section
---

**By air.** Jamnagar Airport has limited domestic flights, mostly to [Mumbai](/asia/india/maharashtra/mumbai). For most travellers, Rajkot (130 km east) is the bigger hub with more frequent connections.

**By train.** Jamnagar is on the western line between Ahmedabad and Okha, and trains from Ahmedabad take 6–7 hours. Direct overnight trains from Mumbai run several times a week and take around 16 hours. The station is in the centre of town, a short rickshaw ride from most hotels.

**By bus.** Long-distance buses connect Jamnagar to all major towns in Gujarat: Ahmedabad (6 hours), Rajkot (2.5 hours), Dwarka (3 hours), Junagadh (4 hours).

**By road.** The drive from Ahmedabad takes about 5 hours along NH947. The road is largely good four-lane highway.
