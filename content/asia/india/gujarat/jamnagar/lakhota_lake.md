The Ranmal Lake, more commonly called the Lakhota Lake, is an artificial reservoir in the centre of old Jamnagar, with a small fortified palace on an island in the middle. The palace is connected to the shore by a narrow causeway and now houses a small museum of Jadeja dynasty objects: weapons, miniature paintings, royal portraits.

The walk around the lake at dawn or dusk is the main draw — it is a working public space used by morning walkers, families, and the inevitable mass of pigeons. The pavilions on the shore, the small Bhujio Kotho watchtower nearby, and the old city alleys leading off the lakefront give a sense of pre-industrial Jamnagar that the surrounding sprawl has otherwise erased.
