Pratap Vilas Palace is one of Jamnagar's grand royal landmarks, built with a mix of Indian palace planning and European classical details. Its domes, columns, and generous grounds make it stand apart from the more compact old-city sights.

Access can be limited, so the palace is often appreciated from outside rather than as a full interior tour. Even so, it is worth noting for the way it reflects Jamnagar's princely-state history and early 20th-century taste.
