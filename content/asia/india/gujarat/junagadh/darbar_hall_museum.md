Darbar Hall Museum gives Junagadh's princely history a tangible setting. The former court space displays portraits, arms, textiles, chandeliers, and royal objects from the Nawabi period, letting you see how power presented itself before independence and accession changed the city's role.

It is a good indoor stop between Junagadh's forts, stepwells, and religious sites. The museum is not large, but its rooms add useful context to a city where Islamic courtly architecture and Gujarati pilgrimage routes sit unusually close together.
