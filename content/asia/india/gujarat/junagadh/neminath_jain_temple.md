Neminath Jain Temple is one of the great rewards of the climb up Mount Girnar. Dedicated to the 22nd Tirthankara, it anchors the Jain temple cluster on the mountain and carries the weight of centuries of pilgrimage.

The ascent is part of the experience, not just the access route. Start early, carry water, and give yourself time at the top; the temple, the views, and the stream of pilgrims make this one of Junagadh's defining sights.
