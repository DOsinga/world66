---
title: "Eating Out"
type: section
---

Kutchi food is almost entirely vegetarian and has a distinct identity within Gujarati cuisine — bajra (millet) rotlas instead of wheat, plenty of milk and ghee, a love of sweet–salty contrasts. The local **thali** is the easiest way in: a metal plate piled with rotla, dal, kadhi, several vegetable preparations, jaggery, ghee, and one or two sweets.

**Hotel Prince** on Station Road and **Toral Hotel** near the centre both do well-regarded unlimited Gujarati thalis at lunch and dinner. **Sankalp** offers a more refined South Indian alternative. **Green Rock**, on the road south to the airport, is a long-standing favourite for late-night Punjabi and Chinese. For local sweets, **Geeta Bhel** and the dozens of stalls around the Bhid Gate produce excellent fafda, jalebi and aamras (in mango season). Eating out in Bhuj is cheap; a thali rarely costs more than ₹250.
