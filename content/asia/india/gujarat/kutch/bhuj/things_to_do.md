---
title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Bhuj's two great surviving palaces sit side by side in the **Darbargadh** complex — the 18th-century **Aina Mahal** with its Venetian glass interiors and the heavily damaged but still imposing **Prag Mahal**. The **Kutch Museum**, the oldest in Gujarat, has fine displays of regional embroidery and silverwork. **Hamirsar Lake** in the centre of town is a pleasant walk at sunset, ringed by ghats and small temples. Outside the city, the **Smritivan Earthquake Memorial** on Bhujia Hill is one of the most affecting modern museums in India, and the surrounding **craft villages** of Kutch are the real reason most travellers come — each producing its own distinctive embroidery, mirror work or block-printed cotton.
