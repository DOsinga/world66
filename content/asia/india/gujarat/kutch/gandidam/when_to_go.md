---
title: When to Go
type: section
---

The best time to visit Gandhidham and the surrounding Kutch region is between October and March, when temperatures are comfortable and the evenings are cool. April and May bring intense heat, with temperatures often reaching 40°C or more, while the monsoon from July to September brings humidity and occasional flooding to the low-lying areas around the port.

The Rann Utsav festival, held in the salt flats of the Rann of Kutch between November and February, draws large numbers of visitors to the district. Book accommodation and train tickets well in advance if you are travelling during this period, as Gandhidham Junction becomes one of the busiest entry points to the festival grounds.
