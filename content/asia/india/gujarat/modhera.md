Modhera is a small village in Gujarat's Mehsana district, about 99 km from [Ahmedabad](/asia/india/gujarat/ahmedabad), famous almost entirely for one extraordinary monument: the [Sun Temple](/asia/india/gujarat/modhera/sun_temple). Built in 1026 AD by the Solanki king Bhimdev I, it is one of the finest examples of medieval Indian temple architecture, with walls covered in extraordinary carved sculptures of gods, goddesses, and scenes from everyday life.

In front of the temple, the [Surya Kund](/asia/india/gujarat/modhera/surya_kund) is a vast stepped tank surrounded by over a hundred small shrine niches. The reflection of the temple in its still water on a morning visit is one of Gujarat's most memorable sights. The complex also includes the [Modhera Vav](/asia/india/gujarat/modhera/modhera_vav), a carved stepwell typical of the Solanki tradition, and a short walk away, the [Modheshwari Mata Temple](/asia/india/gujarat/modhera/modheshwari_temple), an active shrine that has been serving the local Modh Brahmin community for centuries.

Modhera makes an excellent day trip from Ahmedabad, ideally combined with [Patan](/asia/india/gujarat/patan) — home to the spectacular Rani ki Vav stepwell — and [Siddhpur](/asia/india/gujarat/siddhpur), all on the same route north.
