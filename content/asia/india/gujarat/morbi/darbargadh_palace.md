Darbargadh Palace is part of Morbi's princely-state core, a reminder that the town's architectural interest goes well beyond ceramics and bridges. Its carved gateways, courtyards, and palace-front details give a more intimate view of local royal taste than the grander New Palace.

Condition and access can vary, so treat it as a historic exterior and neighbourhood walk unless you confirm otherwise locally. Even from outside, it helps tie together Morbi's unusual mix of Rajput, colonial, and mercantile architecture.
