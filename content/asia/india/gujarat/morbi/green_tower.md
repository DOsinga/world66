Green Tower is a small but distinctive Morbi landmark, built in a European style and topped with a three-level cast-iron frame and dome. It fits the city's wider collection of palace, bridge, and civic architecture.

It is worth a quick look while walking between the old town sights, especially if you are already visiting the nearby bridge and palace area.
