Patan is almost always visited as one stop on a longer day from Ahmedabad. The Sun Temple at [Modhera](/asia/india/gujarat/modhera) is half an hour south, and the ruined Rudra Mahalaya at [Siddhpur](/asia/india/gujarat/siddhpur) sits 30 km north on the way to Mount Abu.
