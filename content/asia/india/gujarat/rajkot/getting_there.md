---

Rajkot Airport (RAJ) handles domestic flights to Mumbai, Delhi, Bangalore and a handful of other Indian cities. A new larger airport — Hirasar International — opened to the south of the city in 2023 and is gradually taking over operations. Check which airport your flight uses before booking a taxi.

The railway station is in the centre of the city and is well-connected by overnight and daytime trains to Ahmedabad, Mumbai, Delhi and elsewhere. Long-distance buses leave from the ST stand near the centre. Shared taxis and intercity vans run to [Ahmedabad](/asia/india/gujarat/ahmedabad), [Jamnagar](/asia/india/gujarat/jamnagar), [Junagadh](/asia/india/gujarat/junagadh) and Diu.
