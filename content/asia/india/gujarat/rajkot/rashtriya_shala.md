Rashtriya Shala was founded in the orbit of Mahatma Gandhi's educational and swadeshi ideas, and it still feels more like an institution than a polished museum. The campus is associated with khadi, handcrafts, and the nationalist belief that education should be tied to practical work.

For travelers, its value is in giving Rajkot a deeper context than palaces and colonial buildings alone. Visit with patience: the rewards are in the story of local reform, independence politics, and craft traditions, not in blockbuster displays.
