The Rotary Dolls Museum is an unexpectedly global stop in Rajkot. Its collection uses dolls, costumes and miniature settings to introduce clothing, festivals and domestic scenes from many countries, making it more about cultural comparison than toy nostalgia.

It is especially good with children, but adults who enjoy folk dress and material culture will also find details to linger over. Rajkot's major Gandhi sites are still the priority; this museum is a lighter, colourful addition when you have another hour in the centre.
