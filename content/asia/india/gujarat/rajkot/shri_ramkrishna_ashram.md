Shri Ramkrishna Ashram is a quiet spiritual and cultural stop in Rajkot. The ashram has a permanent exhibition on the life of Ramakrishna, along with a public library and an Ayurvedic centre.

It is most worthwhile for visitors interested in modern Hindu reform movements or looking for a calmer break from Rajkot's busier sights.
