---
title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Rajkot is not a sightseeing city. A day or two is enough to see the Gandhi sites, the city museum, and the palace before moving on to the rest of Saurashtra. The Kaba Gandhi No Delo, where Mohandas Gandhi spent part of his childhood, is the clear headliner; the more recent Mahatma Gandhi Museum at his old school complements it well.
