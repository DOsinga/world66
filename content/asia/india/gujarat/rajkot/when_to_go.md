---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

October to March is the only comfortable window. Daytime temperatures sit in the low-to-mid 20s Celsius through these months, with cool evenings and almost no rain. December and January can be cold enough at night to want a jacket.

The pre-monsoon (April–June) is brutal: 40°C+ days are routine, the air is dusty, and there is no useful relief until nightfall. The monsoon (July–September) cools the temperature but brings unpredictable downpours. **Navratri** in late September or October — nine nights of garba dancing — is one of the most exuberant festivals in Gujarat and Rajkot is one of its centres; consider timing a visit around it if you don't mind crowds and noisy nights.
