Sunrise Point is one of Saputara's straightforward hill-station pleasures: a high lookout where the day starts over the wooded Dang landscape. The view is not complicated, but the combination of cool air, hazy ridges, and early light is exactly why people come to Saputara.

Go early and give yourself time to walk rather than rush up for a photograph. In monsoon and just after it, the slopes are greener and the valley mist makes the viewpoint feel far more atmospheric than in the dry season.
