Sidhheshwar Mahadev Temple and the Moksha Peepal tree are part of Siddhpur's ritual landscape near the Saraswati river. The riverfront has been developed for religious rites, so this is most meaningful when visited with Bindu Sarovar and the town's other pilgrimage sites.
