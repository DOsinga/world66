Siddhpur rewards slow exploration. The two main draws — the Rudra Mahalaya ruins and the Bohra Vads — are both within easy walking distance of each other in the town centre. The sacred tank of Bindu Sarovar and the small Shristhal Museum round out a half-day visit, and the whole town can comfortably be combined with Modhera and Patan on a single day trip from Ahmedabad.
