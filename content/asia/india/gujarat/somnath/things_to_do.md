type: section
---

Somnath's defining sight is the [Somnath Temple](/asia/india/gujarat/somnath/somnath_temple) itself — one of India's twelve jyotirlingas, rebuilt seven times on the same spot after successive destructions. The [Bhalka Tirtha](/asia/india/gujarat/somnath/bhalka_tirtha) nearby marks where, according to Hindu tradition, Krishna was accidentally shot with an arrow and left his mortal body. The [Somnath Museum](/asia/india/gujarat/somnath/somnath_museum) holds fragments from the medieval temple destroyed by Mahmud of Ghazni in 1026.

For those wanting to linger, the coast around Somnath is scenic in the late afternoon, and [Veraval](/asia/india/gujarat/somnath/veraval_fishing_harbour) a few kilometres away has one of the largest fishing harbours on the Gujarat coast — raw and pungent, but a vivid contrast with the refined pilgrimage town up the road.
