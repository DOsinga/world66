---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

Somnath is in coastal Gujarat and is generally hot. The most comfortable months are **October to February**, when temperatures are mild (20–28°C) and the sea breeze makes the coastal setting pleasant. This is also when pilgrimage traffic is highest, particularly around Mahashivaratri (February/March), when the temple hosts large gatherings.

**March to May** is hot and increasingly uncomfortable. **The monsoon** (June to September) brings heavy rain and the sea can be rough, but the town is quiet and the temple uncrowded. The surrounding landscape turns briefly green.

The **Kartik Purnima** fair, held on the full moon in November, attracts large numbers of pilgrims and is the most festive time to visit.
