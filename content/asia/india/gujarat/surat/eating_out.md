title: Eating Out
type: section
---

Surat is widely considered to have the best street food in Gujarat, and many Gujaratis who have moved to other cities will cite Surti food as what they miss most. The specialities are specific to this city: locho (a soft, steamed gram-flour snack), khaman (steamed chickpea cake), undhiyu (winter mixed vegetable curry), and the various mithai (sweets) from the Chowk area. The Bombay Market area and Saroli are good areas to start.
