Science Centre Surat is the city's main hands-on science and learning complex, with galleries, a planetarium, and displays aimed especially at children and students. It gives Surat a practical indoor option beyond textile, diamond, and old-city themes.

It is best for families, school-age travellers, or anyone needing an air-conditioned break with something to do. Check show times for the planetarium before arranging your day around it.
