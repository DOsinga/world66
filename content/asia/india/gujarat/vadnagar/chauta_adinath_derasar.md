Chauta Adinath Derasar is a Jain temple in Vadnagar, tied to the tradition that sees the town as an ancient foothill of Shatrunjaya at Palitana. It is a quieter stop than Hatkeshwar Mahadev Temple, but it adds another religious strand to the old town.

Visit respectfully and keep the stop brief unless worship or temple architecture is a particular interest. It fits well into a slow walk between Vadnagar's lake, gates, and archaeological sites.
