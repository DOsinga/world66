Jogida ni Gufa is a hilltop group of rock shelters and Buddhist carvings reached by a short trek. The site is rough-edged and not heavily interpreted, but the combination of sculpture, stone, and hill views makes it rewarding if you are already exploring Taranga and Vadnagar's older religious sites.

Wear shoes with grip and bring water; the climb is part of the visit.
