The Tana Riri Shrines commemorate Tana and Riri, two legendary musician sisters associated with the Mughal-era story of Tansen and Vadnagar. The site is modest, but it gives a local cultural thread to a town better known for gates, temples, wells, and archaeological remains.

The shrines are most rewarding if you are already exploring Vadnagar's old sites. They are also linked to an annual classical music festival held in the sisters' memory.
