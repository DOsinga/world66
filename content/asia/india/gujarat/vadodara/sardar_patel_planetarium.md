Sardar Patel Planetarium is a science attraction in Sayaji Baug, close to Baroda Museum and other central Vadodara sights. The pyramid-like building includes a planetarium chamber, exhibition space, an amphitheatre, and an observatory.

It is most useful for families or travelers with an interest in astronomy. Check show times before making a special trip, since the planetarium experience depends on the scheduled programs.
