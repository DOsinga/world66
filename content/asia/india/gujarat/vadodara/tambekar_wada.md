Tambekar Wada is one of Vadodara’s more intimate heritage buildings, a Maratha-era mansion known for painted walls rather than royal scale. Its murals show mythological scenes, courtly imagery, and decorative flourishes that bring domestic architecture into the city’s art story.

The wada is best visited by travellers who already have an appetite for old houses, craft, and painted interiors. It is quieter than Laxmi Vilas Palace and less obvious than the museums, but that smaller scale is exactly what makes it valuable.
