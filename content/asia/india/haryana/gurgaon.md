Gurgaon, officially Gurugram, is the city that shows what happened when Delhi's edge met private capital at full speed. Glass office parks, gated towers, shopping malls, expressways, and metro stations sit beside old village lanes, devotional shrines, and dusty Haryana fields that have not quite disappeared.

It is not an easy city to love in the old travel-guide sense. Distances are awkward, walking is often unpleasant, and the skyline can feel built for cars and corporate badges. But Gurgaon has energy, food, nightlife, and a surprisingly good set of green escapes if you know where to look. The [Aravali Biodiversity Park](/asia/india/haryana/gurgaon/aravali_biodiversity_park) is the best corrective to the city's concrete reputation, while [Basai Wetland](/asia/india/haryana/gurgaon/basai_wetland) and [Sultanpur National Park](/asia/india/haryana/gurgaon/sultanpur_national_park) make the district genuinely rewarding for birders.

For urban Gurgaon, [Cyber City](/asia/india/haryana/gurgaon/cyber_city) is the obvious face of the new city: offices by day, restaurants and bars after work. The older religious layer survives at [Sheetla Mata Mandir](/asia/india/haryana/gurgaon/sheetla_mata_mandir) and [Dronacharya Temple](/asia/india/haryana/gurgaon/dronacharya_temple). If you want one specialist museum, choose [Museo Camera](/asia/india/haryana/gurgaon/museo_camera), an unexpectedly strong photography collection in a city better known for real estate.
