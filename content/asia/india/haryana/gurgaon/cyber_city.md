Cyber City is the most recognisable face of new Gurgaon: office towers, corporate campuses, elevated metro tracks, and polished plazas just across from Delhi's airport zone. It is not historic, but it explains the city's boom better than any monument could.

For travellers, the draw is Cyber Hub, the restaurant and bar cluster embedded in the business district. Come in the evening when the office crowds loosen up, prices rise, and Gurgaon briefly feels like a proper urban centre rather than a scatter of sectors.
