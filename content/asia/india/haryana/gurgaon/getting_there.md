---
title: Getting There
type: section
---

Gurgaon is immediately southwest of Delhi and is easiest to reach from Indira Gandhi International Airport, which is closer to many Gurgaon hotels and offices than central Delhi is. The Delhi Metro Yellow Line runs to Millennium City Centre Gurugram, with Rapid Metro connections through Cyber City and nearby business districts.

Road access is via NH48 and the Delhi-Gurgaon Expressway, though traffic can be punishing at office hours. Gurgaon railway station is useful for some regional trains, but most long-distance rail travellers use New Delhi, Delhi Sarai Rohilla, or Hazrat Nizamuddin.
