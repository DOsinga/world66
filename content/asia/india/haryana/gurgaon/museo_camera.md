Museo Camera is one of Gurgaon's better cultural surprises: a museum devoted to photography, cameras, and the history of image-making. In a city short on classic museums, it gives visitors something focused and genuinely different from malls and office districts.

The collection is strongest if you have even a passing interest in cameras, photojournalism, or visual culture. Check opening times before going, as specialist museums in Indian cities can keep more variable hours than the big national institutions.
