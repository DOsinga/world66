Sheetla Mata Mandir is one of the main pilgrimage sites in old Gurgaon, dedicated to the goddess Sheetla. It can be crowded, noisy, and deeply local, especially during festivals and auspicious days, which is precisely why it makes a useful contrast to Cyber City.

Dress modestly, expect queues at busy times, and leave shoes where instructed. The temple is not about architectural grandeur; it is about living devotion in a city that often pretends it was born with the office tower.
