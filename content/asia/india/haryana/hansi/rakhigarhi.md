Rakhigarhi is one of the most important Indus Valley Civilisation sites in India, spread around a present-day village near Hansi. Excavations have uncovered traces of fire altars, drainage, storage, tools, toys, and sculpture, with much of the ancient settlement still unexcavated.

It is a serious archaeology stop rather than a polished visitor attraction. Come for the scale of the history, and pair it with Hansi's forts and shrines if you are exploring this part of Haryana.
