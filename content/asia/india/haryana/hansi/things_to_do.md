---
title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Hansi's surviving heritage is mostly a single afternoon's worth: the great mud-and-brick mound of Asigarh Fort with George Thomas's gate, a couple of historic mosques and Jain shrines around the old town, the surviving city gates that still mark the edges of the medieval walled town, and the bazaars in between. Pair it with a stop in Hisar and you have a useful day of Haryana history off the highway.
