Jind has several ancient ponds and tanks associated with Mahabharata traditions, of which the Brahm Kund is among the oldest. The kund (tank) is used for ritual bathing and is ringed by small temples. The setting is typical of Haryana's religious geography — modest in appearance, significant in local belief.

The area around the kund is quiet except on festival days, when it fills with pilgrims performing shraddha (ancestor propitiation) rituals. A visit here alongside Rani Talab gives a good sense of how Jind functions as a regional pilgrimage town serving the dense agricultural communities of central Haryana.
