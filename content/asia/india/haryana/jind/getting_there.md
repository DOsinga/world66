---
title: Getting There
type: section
---

Jind is well-connected to the major cities of north India. By train, the Jind Junction railway station sits on the Ferozepur line and has regular services to Delhi (around 2.5–3 hours), Chandigarh (around 2 hours), and Hisar. Several daily trains run to Delhi's Sarai Rohilla or Old Delhi stations. Check current timetables on the Indian Railways website or at the station, as schedules change seasonally.

By road, Jind is about 155 kilometers northwest of Delhi, accessible via National Highway 152. Haryana Roadways buses run frequently from Delhi's ISBT Kashmere Gate, with a journey time of roughly 3–4 hours depending on traffic. Private bus services and shared taxis are also available from Rohtak, Chandigarh, and Hisar.

There is no airport at Jind. The nearest airport is Chandigarh (about 130 km) or Indira Gandhi International Airport, New Delhi.
