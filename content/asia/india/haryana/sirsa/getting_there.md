---
title: Getting There
type: section
---

Sirsa is linked by road to Hisar, Fatehabad, Bathinda, and Hanumangarh, making it a useful break on routes between western Haryana, Punjab, and northern Rajasthan. Buses are the most common public transport option for nearby towns.

Sirsa railway station handles regional services, but for wider rail connections travellers often route through larger junctions such as Hisar, Bathinda, or Delhi depending on direction.
