Bir Billing is one of the world's top paragliding destinations, but there's plenty more to explore on the ground.

**Paragliding**

The [Billing launch site](/asia/india/himachalpradesh/bir_billing/billing_launch_site) at 2,400 metres is the heart of the operation. Tandem flights are available for anyone — no experience needed — and the views from the meadow are worth the hour-long cab ride even if you never leave the ground. The flying season peaks from October through November.

**Monasteries and spiritual sites**

[Sherab Ling monastery](/asia/india/himachalpradesh/bir_billing/sherab_ling_monastery) in the pine forest between Bir and Baijnath is the largest and most impressive, with its golden Maitreya and line of stupas. The [Deer Park Institute](/asia/india/himachalpradesh/bir_billing/deer_park_institute) in the Tibetan Colony runs seminars on Buddhist philosophy and Indian wisdom traditions. Several smaller gompa are scattered through the colony and easy to visit on foot.

**Baijnath temple**

The [Baijnath Shiva temple](/asia/india/himachalpradesh/bir_billing/baijnath_shiva_temple) dates to 1204 CE and is one of the finest examples of medieval Nagara temple architecture in Himachal Pradesh. It's 16 km from Bir by road, or a longer but beautiful walk via the Sherab Ling stupa trail.

**Tea gardens**

The [Bir Tea Factory](/asia/india/himachalpradesh/bir_billing/bir_tea_factory) offers informal tours during processing season and sells Kangra first-flush tea at factory prices. The tea gardens on the lower Dhauladhar slopes make for a gentle walk at any time of year.

**Upper Bir**

The river above the main village has natural pools for swimming in summer. Follow the main road uphill and then drop down toward the water — a popular afternoon escape from the valley heat.
