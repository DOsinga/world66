---
title: When to Go
type: section
---

Dharamsala has two good seasons and two difficult ones. **March to June** is the best time: spring brings rhododendrons in bloom on the hillsides, temperatures at McLeod Ganj are comfortable (15–25°C), and the Dhauladhar peaks are clear of cloud most days. The Triund trek is open and the Dalai Lama's teaching schedule is often active in spring.

**October and November** are also excellent — post-monsoon, the air is clear, the mountains visible, and the crowds from summer are gone. **Tibetan New Year (Losar)**, usually in February or March, brings celebrations to McLeod Ganj that are worth timing a visit around.

**July and August** bring heavy monsoon rain. The Triund trail becomes slippery and occasionally dangerous, and views of the mountains disappear for weeks at a time. Some travellers prefer the lush green landscape and empty guesthouses enough to go anyway.

**December and January** are cold at this altitude — temperatures drop to near zero at night — and snow occasionally closes higher trails. The town is quiet but the Tibetan New Year preparations begin, and the Dalai Lama often gives winter teachings.
