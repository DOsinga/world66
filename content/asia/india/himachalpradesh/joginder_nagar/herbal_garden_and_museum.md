Joginder Nagar's Herbal Garden and Museum is a useful stop for anyone interested in Himalayan medicinal plants. The garden is close to the bus stand and is reached by Cinema Road, making it easy to add to a short walk around town.

The museum focuses on rare herbs and medicinal plants rather than showy landscaping. It works best as a quiet, local counterpoint to the valley views and railway heritage around Joginder Nagar.
