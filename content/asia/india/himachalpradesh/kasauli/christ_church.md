Christ Church is Kasauli's main piece of colonial architecture, a small Gothic-style church close to the bus stand and Mall Road. Trees soften the stonework and make the church feel more settled than grand.

It is worth seeing because it anchors the old hill-station story. Pair it with a slow walk through the centre rather than treating it as a standalone destination.
