The Kalatop Khajjiar Wildlife Sanctuary is a 30 sq km belt of deodar and oak forest that wraps around the meadow and stretches west toward [Dalhousie](/asia/india/himachalpradesh/dalhousie). It protects barking deer, Himalayan black bear, leopards, monals and koklas pheasants, though the bigger animals stay deep in the forest and most visitors only see langurs and a lot of birds.

A clear, well-maintained forest road runs the 10 km from Khajjiar to the Kalatop rest house, and walking it makes for one of the best half-day hikes in the area. The path is shaded the whole way, gains very little elevation, and emerges at a viewpoint over the Ravi valley. A small entry fee is collected at the sanctuary gate; carry water, there is none on the way.
