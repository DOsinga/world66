Bijli Mahadev — the 'lightning Shiva' — sits on a ridge at around 2,400 metres, with one of the great panoramic views in the Kullu valley. On clear days you can see the Beas and the Parvati valley meeting below, with snow peaks stretching away to the north and east.

The climb is about three kilometres on a stepped path from the road end at Chansari, taking 90 minutes to two hours one way. Go early; clouds usually build by midday and the view is the whole point.
