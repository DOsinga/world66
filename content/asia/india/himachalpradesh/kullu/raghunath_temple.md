The Raghunath temple is the spiritual heart of Kullu, a modest two-storey structure of stone and wood on a hill above the bazaar. The deity housed here is the focus of the Kullu Dussehra, the festival that brings the valley together every October.

The temple is open to all and the priests are generally welcoming. There is little ornament — what makes the place is its standing as the supreme god of Kullu, and the long ritual calendar that organises the year around its courtyard.
