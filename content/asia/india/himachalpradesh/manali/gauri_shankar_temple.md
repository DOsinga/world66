Gauri Shankar Temple is a small but finely proportioned stone temple near Naggar, south of Manali in the Kullu Valley. Dedicated to Shiva, it is known for its compact shikhara form and carved stonework.

The temple works best as part of a Naggar excursion, paired with Naggar Castle, village lanes, and valley views. It is quieter than Manali's central temples and gives a stronger sense of the older Kullu architectural tradition.
