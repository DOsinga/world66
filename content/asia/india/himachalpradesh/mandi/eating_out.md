---
title: "Eating Out"
type: section
---

Mandi does not have a fine-dining scene to speak of, and that is part of its charm. Eating is a matter of small dhabas around the Indira Market and the slightly more comfortable restaurants in the heritage hotels.

The local dishes to try are **Mandyali dham** (a special-occasion vegetarian thali of rice, dal, kadhi, chana madra and meetha rice, served on leaf plates at weddings and festivals), **siddu** (a steamed wheat dumpling with savoury walnut or sweet poppyseed filling, eaten with ghee and dal), and the sour-spicy **chana madra** (chickpeas in yoghurt with whole spices, the signature Himachali curry).

The **Raj Mahal Palace** dining room is the formal option, with a passable Himachali set menu in the evenings. **Hotel Mayfair** and the small dhabas on Seri Bazaar do reliable, cheap thalis at lunch. For street food, **Indira Market** has stalls selling siddu and bhature; ask for siddu with walnuts (akhrot) if you see it on the menu.
