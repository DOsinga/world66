---
title: "Getting There"
type: section
---

Mandi is a major junction on the road network of Himachal Pradesh, with frequent buses to almost everywhere in the state. From **Delhi**, it is about 11 hours by overnight Volvo bus (HRTC and private operators run several daily services); from **Chandigarh**, six hours; from **Manali**, four hours via the Pandoh dam.

The **nearest railway** is Joginder Nagar, 55 km away, on the narrow-gauge line from Pathankot. It is slow and atmospheric but adds half a day to any journey. The standard-gauge railhead is Una, three hours away.

**Bhuntar Airport** near Kullu is the closest commercial airport, 60 km north, with a small number of flights from Delhi that are unreliable in poor weather. The bigger airports at **Dharamshala** and **Chandigarh** are both four hours away. Most travellers reach Mandi by road as part of a longer Himachal trip, and the town is the natural stop for breakfast on the way from Chandigarh to Manali.
