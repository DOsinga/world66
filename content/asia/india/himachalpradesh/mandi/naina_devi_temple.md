Naina Devi Temple is reached by a walk from Rewalsar, with Buddhist caves and small lakes nearby depending on the route. The approach is part of the appeal, turning the temple into a short hill excursion rather than only a shrine visit.

It is best for travellers spending time around Rewalsar Lake who want a more active stop in the surrounding hills.
