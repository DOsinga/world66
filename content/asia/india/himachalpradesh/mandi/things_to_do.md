---
title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Mandi is a temple town first and a hill town second. The eighty-one stone temples scattered across the old town are the main draw: most are small, single-cell shrines from the 16th–17th centuries, carved in the sikhara style with figures from the Ramayana and Mahabharata on the outer walls. The five essential stops are **Bhootnath**, the riverside Shiva temple at the heart of the bazaar; **Trilokinath**, the carved 1520 temple on the south bank; **Panchvaktra**, a five-faced Shiva temple at the confluence of the rivers; **Mata Kuan Rani**, the small "Princess of the Well" temple by the river; and **Tarna Devi**, the goddess temple on the hill that gives the best view in town.

Beyond the temples, the old **Indira Market** square below the Bhootnath is the social heart of the town, with carved wooden balconies overhanging the narrow lanes. The colonial-era **Victoria Bridge** and the ruined **Raj Mahal Palace** sit a short walk apart on the river. Allow a full day for the temples; the day trips out to **Rewalsar** and **Prashar Lake** deserve another.
