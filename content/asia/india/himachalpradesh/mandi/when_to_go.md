---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

Mandi is at a lowish 800 metres for a Himachal town, which means summers can be hot — temperatures climb into the mid-30s in May and June, and the air in the valley sits still and heavy. The town is at its best in **March–April** (warm days, the rhododendron in flower on the hills, the Shivratri Fair if dates line up) and **October–November** (clear post-monsoon air, snow returning to the high ridges visible from town).

The **Shivratri Fair** in February or March (dates set by the Hindu lunar calendar) is the single best time to visit if you can plan for it: a week-long procession of village deities, hundreds of palanquins arriving over the mountains, the whole town strung with bunting. Hotel space disappears six months in advance.

The **monsoon** (mid-June to early September) is wet but not unpleasant; the river runs full and the temples look dramatic in low cloud. The roads to Rewalsar and Prashar can wash out after heavy rain. Winter is cold (sub-zero at night in January) but rarely snowy in town itself; the high passes north of Mandi close.
