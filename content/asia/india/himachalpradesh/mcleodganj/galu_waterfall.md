Galu Waterfall is a short but steep hike above Bhagsu, reached by climbing toward the saddle behind Haini and then following a trail with smaller ups and downs. The waterfall has cascades and swimming holes, with a few basic cafes along the way.

It works best as a half-day walk from McLeod Ganj or Bhagsu rather than as a standalone trip from lower Dharamsala.
