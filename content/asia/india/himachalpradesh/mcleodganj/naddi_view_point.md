Naddi View Point looks across to the snow-covered Dhauladhar range from the hills above McLeod Ganj. On a clear day you can pick out Triund and the higher ridges beyond it, with the Kangra Valley dropping away below.

The viewpoint is easy to reach by taxi or rickshaw, and it can also be worked into a gentle walk from Dharamkot. Go early or late for clearer light and fewer visitors.
