Nako is a high village in Kinnaur, close to the old routes toward Spiti and Tibet, and it feels very far from the hill-station version of Himachal Pradesh. Stone houses, prayer flags, barley fields, and bare brown mountains give the place a spare beauty.

The two anchors are simple but strong: [Nako Monastery](/asia/india/himachalpradesh/nako/nako_monastery), with old Buddhist temples and murals, and [Nako Lake](/asia/india/himachalpradesh/nako/nako_lake), a small willow-edged lake beside the village. The best third sight is the village itself: lanes, chortens, flat-roofed houses, and views toward the Trans-Himalaya.

Nako is a place to slow down, not to consume. It sits at roughly 3,600 metres, so arrive gently, drink water, and do not plan a hard walk on your first afternoon. If you have the energy, the short climbs above the lake give the village its proper setting.
