Nako Monastery is the village's main cultural sight, a compact group of old Buddhist temples in the middle of the settlement. The buildings are plain from outside, but inside are murals, sculptures, and a quiet sense of age that fits the high, dry landscape around them.

Go respectfully and expect simplicity. This is not a polished museum-monastery; it is a living religious place in a remote village, and some older elements may look worn. That worn quality is part of the truth of Nako, not a flaw to be edited out.
