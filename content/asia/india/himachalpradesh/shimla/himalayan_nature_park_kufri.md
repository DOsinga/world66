Himalayan Nature Park Kufri is a wildlife park above Shimla focused on Himalayan species. Its animals include musk deer and snow leopards, giving visitors a compact look at high-altitude fauna that is otherwise hard to see.

The park is a natural fit with a day trip to Kufri, especially for families or visitors who want an easy nature stop rather than a long hike.
