The Photo Art Gallery displays thousands of photographs of Himachal Pradesh by Inder Pal, covering the state's landscapes, people, culture, flora, and fauna. It is a focused stop for visitors who want a visual introduction to the region beyond Shimla's colonial core.

The gallery is best combined with nearby walks around Potter's Hill or other sights on Shimla's western side.
