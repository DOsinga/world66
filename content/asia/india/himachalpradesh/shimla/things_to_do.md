---
title: Things to Do
type: section
---

Shimla's attractions divide between its colonial heritage — the Mall, Christ Church, Viceregal Lodge, and Gaiety Theatre — and the surrounding landscape, which offers hiking, mountain railway rides, and excursions into the Himalayan foothills. The town is compact enough that the main cultural sites are walkable from the centre, and the Kalka-Shimla Railway alone justifies a significant detour.
