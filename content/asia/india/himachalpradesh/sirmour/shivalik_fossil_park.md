Shivalik Fossil Park is a small fossil museum and outdoor display area devoted to the prehistoric animals of the Siwalik hills. The collection includes skeletal remains and life-size models of extinct mammals, giving a useful sense of the creatures that once lived in this part of northern India.

It is a modest place rather than a polished big-city museum, but it makes a worthwhile stop if you are travelling through Sirmour and have an interest in fossils, geology or natural history.
