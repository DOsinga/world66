Kaza is the administrative headquarters and main settlement of Spiti Valley, a market town at 3,800 metres with guesthouses, restaurants, a few ATMs (bring extra cash), a post office, and most of the logistics infrastructure you need for exploring the valley. It has two parts: Old Kaza, with older houses and a small monastery, and New Kaza, the newer commercial area with most of the shops and accommodation.

The town itself is not a destination but a base. From here, the valley's monasteries, villages, and trekking routes fan out in all directions, and Kaza has enough to fill a rest day — the local market sells vegetables, handicrafts, and provisions, and a handful of good Spitian restaurants serve the region's simple but satisfying food: thukpa (noodle soup), momos, and butter tea. The Spiti Ecosphere organisation, based in Kaza, runs excellent community-based tourism programs and can arrange home-stays in valley villages.
