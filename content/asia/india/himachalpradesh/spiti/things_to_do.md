---
title: Things to Do
type: section
---

Spiti Valley rewards those who take their time. The major monasteries — [Key](/asia/india/himachalpradesh/spiti/key_monastery), [Tabo](/asia/india/himachalpradesh/spiti/tabo_monastery), and [Dhankar](/asia/india/himachalpradesh/spiti/dhankar_monastery) — are all within a day's drive of Kaza and deserve full visits rather than quick stops. The fossil villages of [Langza](/asia/india/himachalpradesh/spiti/langza) and [Hikkim](/asia/india/himachalpradesh/spiti/hikkim) make for an excellent morning circuit. [Chandratal Lake](/asia/india/himachalpradesh/spiti/chandratal_lake) and the [Kunzum Pass](/asia/india/himachalpradesh/spiti/kunzum_pass) are worth the drive to the head of the valley. Trekkers can access the [Pin Valley National Park](/asia/india/himachalpradesh/spiti/pin_valley) for multi-day routes and wildlife watching.
