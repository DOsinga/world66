Anantnag is the main town of the southern Kashmir Valley, about 55 km south of [Srinagar](/asia/india/jammuandkashmir/srinagar) on the [Jhelum River](/asia/india/jammuandkashmir/anantnag/jhelum_river). Known also as Islamabad in older maps, it serves as the gateway district for some of Kashmir's most visited sights in the surrounding countryside.

The [Martand Sun Temple](/asia/india/jammuandkashmir/anantnag/martand_sun_temple), an 8th-century ruined Hindu temple on a plateau with views across the valley, is one of the most significant archaeological sites in Kashmir. [Achabal](/asia/india/jammuandkashmir/anantnag/achabal_gardens), a Mughal garden with natural spring sources, and [Kokernag](/asia/india/jammuandkashmir/anantnag/kokernag), a botanical garden built around the largest spring source in the Kashmir Valley, are both within easy reach. The [Verinag Garden](/asia/india/jammuandkashmir/anantnag/verinag_garden), at the source of the Jhelum River itself, is another Mughal-era site in the district.

The district is predominantly agricultural — apple orchards, rice fields, and saffron cultivation — and the landscapes around the valley are attractive. [Pahalgam](/asia/india/jammuandkashmir/pahalgam), the mountain resort at the head of the [Lidder Valley](/asia/india/jammuandkashmir/anantnag/lidder_valley), is within this district and is one of Kashmir's most popular destinations.
