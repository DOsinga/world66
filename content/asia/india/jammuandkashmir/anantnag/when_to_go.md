---
title: When to Go
type: section
---

The Kashmir Valley has four real seasons, and Anantnag follows the valley pattern closely.

**Spring (March to May)** is the prettiest time — orchards in blossom around the town, cool air, the gardens at Achabal and Verinag at their best. Bring a warm layer for the evenings.

**Summer (June to August)** is warm and dry, with daytime temperatures in the mid-twenties. This is peak season for trips up to Pahalgam and the Lidder Valley, and roads, lodgings, and gardens are at their busiest.

**Autumn (September to November)** is the most beautiful season — the chenar trees turn deep red and gold, the saffron harvest happens in late October around Pampore, and the air is crisp. Late October to early November is a small window worth planning around.

**Winter (December to February)** is cold and snowy, with daytime temperatures often below freezing. The gardens are closed or empty, but a few days of snow on the Martand Sun Temple is striking if you do not mind the cold.
