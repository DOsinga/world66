---
title: "Eating Out"
type: section
---

Baramulla's food is the Kashmiri food of the rest of the valley: rich Wazwan-influenced lamb dishes, lots of dried apricot and saffron, mutton kebabs, rajma-chawal, and the local fast food staple, the **kanti** (mutton chunks with onions and chillies served with naan).

There are no destination restaurants in town, but the **bazaars near the river** have several reliable bakeries turning out hot **kashmiri naan**, **lavasa** and **kulcha** in clay tandoors. A handful of plain dhabas on the main road do mutton rogan josh, yakhni and dal — order them with the local short-grain rice. For a sit-down meal, the small restaurants attached to the better hotels along the bypass road are the safest bet.
