Bagh-e-Bahu spreads below Bahu Fort as a terraced garden looking over the Tawi River and Jammu city. It is more polished than wild, with lawns, flowerbeds, fountains, and viewpoints that make it a popular family stop as well as a sightseeing break.

The garden works best in the cooler parts of the day, especially if you are already visiting the fort and temple above it. It gives Jammu a softer, more panoramic pause between shrines, traffic, and bazaar streets.
