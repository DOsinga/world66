Raghunath Temple is the dominant religious landmark of Jammu, a compound of seven connected temples dedicated primarily to Lord Rama. Construction began under Maharaja Gulab Singh in 1835 and was completed by his son Ranbir Singh in 1860. The main shrine is dedicated to Rama; the satellite temples are devoted to various forms of Vishnu and other deities associated with the Ramayana.

The interior covering of gold foil gives the complex an unusually rich visual effect — walls, columns, and niches all shimmer. Non-Hindus are welcome. The temple sits in the heart of the old city bazaar, so the surrounding streets are worth exploring before or after a visit. Arrive early in the morning or at dusk for the aarti ceremony.
