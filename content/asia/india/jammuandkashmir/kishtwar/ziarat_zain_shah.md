The most venerated Sufi shrine in the Kishtwar area, dedicated to the saint Zain Shah Sahib. The ziarat draws pilgrims from across the region — both Muslim and Hindu visitors come to pay respects, reflecting the syncretic religious culture that historically characterized the Kashmir hills.

The shrine is a quiet, atmospheric place to visit outside of the main festival days. The courtyard has large chinar trees and a sense of calm that contrasts with the busy bazaar nearby. Remove footwear before entering.
