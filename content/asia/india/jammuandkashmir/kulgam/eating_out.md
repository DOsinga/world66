---
title: 'Eating Out'
type: section
---

Kulgam is not a place to come for cosmopolitan dining. The town has a handful of small Kashmiri dhabas along the main road, serving the same dishes you would find anywhere in the valley but at half the price of Srinagar's tourist restaurants.

Try **wazwan** — the traditional Kashmiri multi-course meat feast — at one of the family-run places near the bus stand. A simpler everyday meal is **rogan josh** (lamb in a red gravy of Kashmiri chillies and fennel), **goshtaba** (pounded lamb meatballs in yogurt), or **dum aloo** (potatoes in a spiced sauce) with steamed rice. Vegetarians should ask for **haakh** (collard greens) or **nadru** (lotus root) preparations.

For tea, the local **kahwa** — green tea with cardamom, cinnamon, almond, and saffron — is excellent and served everywhere. **Noon chai**, a pink salty tea, is the everyday Kashmiri breakfast drink and an acquired taste.

The road from Kulgam to Aharbal passes orchards and roadside stalls selling apples in season; bring a few for the walk.
