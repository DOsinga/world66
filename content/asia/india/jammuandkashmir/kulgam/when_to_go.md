---
title: 'When to Go'
type: section
---

Kulgam sits at 1,739 metres, lower than Pahalgam or Gulmarg, so the seasons are milder but still distinctly four-season.

**Spring (March-May)** is the prettiest time. The apple and almond orchards across the district come into blossom from late March; the rice paddies start to flood and shimmer green. Temperatures sit pleasantly in the low 20s C by day, cool at night. Aharbal Falls are at full flow with the snowmelt.

**Summer (June-August)** brings warm, dry weather with daytime temperatures around 25-30°C. This is the season to trek to Konsarnag: the high meadows are open, the lake free of ice, and Gujjar herders move up with their flocks. Book trips in advance — this is also the peak season for domestic tourism in the valley.

**Autumn (September-November)** is the second great season. The chinar trees that line the river turn fiery red and gold; the air is crisp and clear; the harvest of rice and apples is in full swing. Wonderful for photography, less crowded than summer.

**Winter (December-February)** is cold — temperatures drop below freezing, snow blankets the higher passes, and Aharbal sometimes partly freezes. The lake trek is impossible. Most tourists ski at Gulmarg instead, but a peaceful, snowy Kulgam has its own appeal if you can take the cold.
