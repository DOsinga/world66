---
title: Eating Out
type: section
---

Leh's restaurant scene has evolved considerably as tourist numbers have grown, and the town now has a solid spread of options alongside the standard traveller staples. Most places are concentrated along the main bazaar and in the Changspa tourist village.

**Bon Appétit** on Fort Road is one of the better all-rounders — wood-fired pizzas, good pasta, and a range of Indian dishes that actually taste the way they should. The terrace has views up toward the palace.

**Tibetan Kitchen** near the market is the place for Tibetan food: thukpa (noodle soup), thenthuk (hand-pulled noodle soup), momos (dumplings), and skyu (a hearty stew of root vegetables and pasta). Simple, cheap, and warming after a cold day.

**Lamayuru Restaurant** in the centre is a solid choice for Indian standards — dal makhani, paneer dishes, and tandoor breads — as well as Ladakhi specialities including chhurpe (dried yak cheese) and butter tea if you want to try it once.

**Alchi Kitchen** on Fort Road, run by a woman from Alchi village, serves authentic Ladakhi and Tibetan food made with local ingredients: nettle soup, buckwheat pancakes, and hand-rolled pasta. One of the most interesting meals in town.

**Wonderland Restaurant** in Changspa has a popular rooftop, good coffee, and reliable breakfast options — eggs, porridge, and local bread — that are welcome after acclimatising on a bland diet your first day.
