Gurudwara Pathar Sahib stands on the road west of Leh, in the dry, high landscape where Ladakh's distances feel larger than the map suggests. The shrine is centred on a boulder associated with Guru Nanak, and it draws Sikh pilgrims, army personnel, and curious travelers alike.

It is an easy stop if you are heading toward Magnetic Hill or the Zanskar-Indus confluence. As at any gurudwara, cover your head, move quietly, and accept the place first as a living shrine rather than a roadside attraction.
