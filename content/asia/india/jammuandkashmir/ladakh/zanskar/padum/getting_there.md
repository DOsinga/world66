---
title: Getting There
type: section
---

Padum, the main town of Zanskar, is about 480 kilometres from Leh by road. The most common summer approach is via **Kargil**, a 230-kilometre drive from Leh on the Srinagar highway, followed by the 230-kilometre stretch to Padum over the Pensi La (4,400m). The total journey takes 2 to 3 days, either by public bus or hired jeep; the Kargil-to-Padum leg alone takes 8 to 10 hours on a rough road and is not done in a single day by most travellers.

A second route goes via the **Zanskar Road from Leh**, constructed in recent years through the Zanskar Gorge south of Nimmu. When fully complete, this road will cut the journey from Leh to Padum to a single long day. As of 2024, sections remain incomplete and the route is used mainly by local vehicles and 4WDs; check current conditions before setting out.

Flying to Leh and then hiring a jeep is the fastest option. Budget several days for the overland leg. Public buses from Kargil to Padum run a few times per week in summer; they are cheap but very slow.

In winter, access to Zanskar is by the Chadar Trek (10-14 days on foot along the frozen river) or by helicopter from Leh (irregular, expensive, and weather-dependent). There is a small airstrip at Padum but it is not served by regular commercial flights.
