title: Things to Do
type: section
---

Zanskar's attractions are its monasteries, its trekking routes, and the extraordinary landscape that holds them together. The valley sits far above the clouds, enclosed by peaks that rarely dip below 5,000 metres, and the quality of light here — thin, clear, pitiless — makes everything look impossibly sharp. Whether you are visiting the ancient gompas or walking a high pass, the altitude will remind you constantly that you are somewhere exceptional.
