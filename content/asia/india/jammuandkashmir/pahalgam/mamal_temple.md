Mamal Temple (also called Mamaleshwar Temple) is a Hindu Shiva temple on the banks of the Lidder River in Pahalgam town, one of the oldest religious sites in the Kashmir Valley. The temple complex is small but well-maintained, with a main shrine and several subsidiary structures. It is an active place of worship, particularly visited by Hindu pilgrims who include it as a stop before or after the Amarnath pilgrimage.

The riverside location is attractive, with the fast-flowing Lidder just below the temple terrace and good views up the valley. The temple is open daily; visitors of all faiths are welcome. Remove shoes before entering the shrine area.
