Patnitop is a hill station in the Udhampur district of Jammu & Kashmir, perched at 2,024 metres on the Patnitop plateau in the Shivalik range. It sits directly on the Jammu–Srinagar National Highway (NH44), about 110 km from Jammu — close enough to serve as a summer escape from the plains heat, remote enough to feel genuinely quiet on weekdays. The plateau is open meadow ringed by dense pine and deodar forest, with the Chenab valley dropping away to the south.

The main draws are the views and the walking. [Nathatop](/asia/india/jammuandkashmir/patnitop/nathatop), 3 km further up at around 2,700 m, gives panoramic views of the Pir Panjal range and the Chenab gorge. Trails through the forest connect the plateau to the ridgeline and make for good half-day [trekking](/asia/india/jammuandkashmir/patnitop/trekking). [Sanasar](/asia/india/jammuandkashmir/patnitop/sanasar), a bowl-shaped meadow 20 km east, is the area's paragliding centre and has a nine-hole golf course. [Sudh Mahadev Temple](/asia/india/jammuandkashmir/patnitop/sudh_mahadev_temple), an ancient Shiva pilgrimage site in a forested valley about 25 km away, is worth a half-day trip.

In winter, when the plateau receives snow from December through February, a small [ski area](/asia/india/jammuandkashmir/patnitop/skiing) operates on the slopes. The runs are short and gentle — beginner terrain — but the setting is attractive and equipment is available for hire. The road from Jammu can close during heavy snowfall.
