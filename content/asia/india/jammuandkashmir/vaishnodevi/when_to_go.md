---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

The shrine is open year-round, but the experience varies enormously by season. The pilgrimage is most pleasant from **March to October** when the weather on the mountain is mild. **April and October–November** correspond to **Navratri**, the two annual nine-night festivals of the goddess — these are the busiest periods, with single-day pilgrim counts that can exceed 30,000 and queue waits of many hours for darshan.

**Summer (May–June)** is hot at the base in Katra (over 35°C) but cool at the cave; the climb is sweat-drenched but doable. **July–August** brings the monsoon, with landslides occasionally closing parts of the trail. **December–February** brings snow on the upper sections and below-freezing nights — fewer pilgrims, but you may not be able to reach Bhairav Nath at all.

The **busiest days** are weekends and the new-moon days; pick a weekday in shoulder season for the shortest queues.
