---
title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Deoghar's sights are tied to pilgrimage, but they are not all the same experience. Start with [Baba Baidyanath Dham](/asia/india/jharkhand/deoghar/baba_baidyanath_dham), the crowded Shiva temple complex that defines the city. [Naulakha Temple](/asia/india/jharkhand/deoghar/naulakha_temple) is quieter and architecturally more composed, while [Trikut Pahar](/asia/india/jharkhand/deoghar/trikut_pahar) takes you out to the hills for a half-day of views, shrines, and walking.
