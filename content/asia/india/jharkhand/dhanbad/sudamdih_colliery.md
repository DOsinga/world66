Sudamdih Colliery is one of the places where Dhanbad's coal story stops being abstract. It sits in the wider Jharia coalfield, the coking-coal belt that made the city nationally important.

Access to working mines needs permission and should be arranged locally, so do not treat this as an open walk-in sight. Even from the surrounding roads, the industrial landscape helps explain why Dhanbad feels so different from India's temple towns and hill stations.
