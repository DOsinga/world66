Ranchi is the capital of [Jharkhand](/asia/india/jharkhand), set high on the Chota Nagpur Plateau rather than down in the hotter Gangetic plains. That elevation gives it cooler evenings, red soil, patches of forest, and a very different feel from the bigger cities of eastern India.

The city itself is busy and uneven, but it has enough texture for a spare day. [Tagore Hill](/asia/india/jharkhand/ranchi/tagore_hill) and [Jagannath Temple](/asia/india/jharkhand/ranchi/jagannath_temple) give the urban skyline its best viewpoints, while the [Rock Garden](/asia/india/jharkhand/ranchi/rock_garden) is a popular local outing above Kanke Dam.

Ranchi's stronger claim is as a base for waterfalls. [Hundru Falls](/asia/india/jharkhand/ranchi/hundru_falls), [Dassam Falls](/asia/india/jharkhand/ranchi/dassam_falls), and Jonha Falls sit outside town in the plateau country and are at their most dramatic after the monsoon. Go with local advice in the rains: the same water that makes them impressive also makes rocks and currents dangerous.
