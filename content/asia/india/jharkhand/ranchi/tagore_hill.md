Tagore Hill rises above Morabadi and gives one of the clearest views over Ranchi. It is associated with Jyotirindranath Tagore, elder brother of Rabindranath Tagore, who spent time here in the early 20th century.

The steps and upkeep can be rough, but the hill is still worth the climb near sunset, when the plateau light softens and the city spreads out below. Go in daylight and keep expectations simple: it is a viewpoint with history, not a polished monument.
