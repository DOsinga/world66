The Tribal Research Institute and Museum introduces the cultures of Jharkhand's many Adivasi communities. Displays cover the state's 32 tribal groups, with material on daily life, crafts, ritual objects, and social traditions that are often missing from broader Indian museum narratives.

It is a useful context stop in Ranchi, especially before travelling into the surrounding plateau and waterfall country. The museum is modest, but it helps explain why Jharkhand has such a distinct cultural identity within eastern India.
