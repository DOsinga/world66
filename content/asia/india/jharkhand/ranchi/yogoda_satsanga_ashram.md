Yogoda Satsanga Ashram is linked with Paramahansa Yogananda, the author of *Autobiography of a Yogi*, who began his life's work in Ranchi. The grounds are quiet and green, offering a calm break from the city as well as a place of interest for readers of Yogananda's work.

It is a contemplative stop rather than a conventional monument, so visit with time to slow down.
