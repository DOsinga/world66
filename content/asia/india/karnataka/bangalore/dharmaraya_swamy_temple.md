Dharmaraya Swamy Temple is one of old Bangalore's distinctive shrines, linked to the city's Pete area and the annual Karaga festival. The temple is dedicated to Dharmaraya and Draupadi, a rare pairing that makes it stand out from the city's many older Hindu temples.
