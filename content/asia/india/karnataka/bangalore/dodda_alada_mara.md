Dodda Alada Mara, the Big Banyan Tree, is a huge centuries-old banyan west of Bangalore. The tree spreads across several acres, with aerial roots forming a little shaded maze; it is a simple outing, but a memorable one if you want a break from the city's traffic and concrete.
