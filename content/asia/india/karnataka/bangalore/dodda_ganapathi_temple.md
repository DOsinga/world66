Dodda Ganapathi Temple is one of Basavanagudi's older temples, standing just beside the Bull Temple. Its main draw is the large monolithic image of Ganesha, a local landmark with deep neighborhood roots.

It is a compact stop, but it makes sense as part of a Basavanagudi walk that also takes in the Bull Temple, Bugle Rock, and nearby food streets.
