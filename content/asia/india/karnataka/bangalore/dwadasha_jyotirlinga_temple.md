Dwadasha Jyotirlinga Temple is a Shiva temple with 12 lingams representing the major Jyotirlinga pilgrimage shrines of India. It is mainly a devotional stop, but the grouped shrines make it a useful place to understand one of Shaivism's important pilgrimage traditions without leaving Bangalore.
