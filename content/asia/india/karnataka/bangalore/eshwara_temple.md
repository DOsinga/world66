Eshwara Temple is a Chola-era Shiva temple in Bangalore, with an inscription from 1050 recording grants made during the reign of Rajendra Chola. It is a compact heritage stop rather than a major monument, but the age of the inscription and the survival of a medieval temple inside the modern city make it worth seeking out for history-minded visitors.
