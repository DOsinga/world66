Gandhi Bhavan is a small centre devoted to Mahatma Gandhi, with photographs, letters, a library, and spaces used for talks and meetings on Gandhian ideas. It is not one of Bangalore's grand sights, but it adds useful political and social context.

Visit if you are already exploring the central civic area around Vidhana Soudha, Cubbon Park, and the museums.
