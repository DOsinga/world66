Government Aquarium is a small but central stop on the edge of Cubbon Park, with tanks of freshwater and marine fish ranging from mahseer and Indian tiger barb to gourami, goldfish and eels. It is not a slick modern aquarium, but it is easy to pair with the museums and gardens nearby.
