Holy Trinity Church is one of Bangalore's major colonial-era churches, built in 1851 for the British regiment stationed in the city. Its English Renaissance style, large nave, and military history make it more than a routine parish church.

The church remains an active place of worship and a useful stop for understanding the old cantonment side of Bangalore.
