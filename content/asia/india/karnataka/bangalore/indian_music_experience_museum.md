The Indian Music Experience Museum is an interactive museum devoted to Indian music, from classical traditions to film songs and contemporary sound. It is hands-on enough for families, but detailed enough for visitors who want more than a quick display of instruments.

There is also a learning centre attached to the museum, so check the programme if you are interested in workshops or live events.
