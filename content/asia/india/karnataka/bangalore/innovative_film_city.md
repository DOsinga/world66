Innovative Film City is a family entertainment complex on the Bengaluru-Mysuru road, combining amusement-park attractions with themed exhibits such as wax figures, dinosaur models, and fossil displays. It is outside central Bangalore and best treated as a half-day outing for families rather than a quick city sight.
