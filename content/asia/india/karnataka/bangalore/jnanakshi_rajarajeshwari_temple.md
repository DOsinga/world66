Jnanakshi Rajarajeshwari Temple is a large Hindu temple in western Bangalore dedicated to Goddess Parvati as Raja Rajeshwari. Built in a traditional stone style, it is spacious, active, and calmer than many of the city's busier central shrines.

It is most worthwhile for travellers interested in Bangalore's living Hindu temples rather than only its colonial and museum sights.
