Karnataka Chitrakala Parishath is one of Bangalore's main visual-arts institutions, with galleries for painting, sculpture, folk art, and rotating exhibitions. The permanent collection includes Mysore paintings, leather puppets, and other regional material that gives the complex a clear local focus.

It is worth checking what temporary shows or craft fairs are on before visiting. Even between major events, it offers a useful cultural stop north of the city centre and a quieter contrast to Bangalore's better-known temples, parks, and colonial-era buildings.
