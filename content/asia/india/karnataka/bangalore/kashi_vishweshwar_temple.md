Kashi Vishweshwar Temple is an old Shiva temple in eastern Bangalore, associated with the Western Ganga period. Its appeal is the contrast: a pocket of older stone, ritual, and quiet inside a part of the city better known for tech offices and traffic.

It is a modest stop, but worthwhile if you are already exploring the eastern side of Bangalore or are interested in the city's pre-modern religious sites.
