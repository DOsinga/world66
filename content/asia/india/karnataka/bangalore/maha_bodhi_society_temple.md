The Maha Bodhi Society Temple is a calm Buddhist compound in central Bangalore. Its main shrine is a modern structure inspired by the Mahabodhi Temple at Bodh Gaya, and visitors are welcome to walk through the peaceful grounds.

The temple also runs charitable and educational work, but the reason to stop here is the quiet shrine atmosphere, especially if you are already visiting the older civic and religious sights around the city centre.
