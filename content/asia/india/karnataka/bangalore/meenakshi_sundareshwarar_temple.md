Meenakshi Sundareshwarar Temple brings a slice of Tamil temple architecture to southern Bangalore, with a spacious stone complex dedicated to Meenakshi Amman and Sundareshwarar. It is a calm stop if you are already exploring this side of the city and want a living temple rather than another museum or park.
