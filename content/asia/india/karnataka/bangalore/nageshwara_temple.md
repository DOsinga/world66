Nageshwara Temple is one of Bangalore's older sacred sites, with origins in the Western Ganga period and later additions under the Cholas, Hoysalas, and Vijayanagara rulers. The temple is best known for an inscription with the earliest known reference to Bengaluru, but the stone halls, Nandi image, and Shiva shrine make it more than a footnote.
