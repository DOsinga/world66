The NIMHANS Brain Museum is one of Bangalore's odder museums: a medical collection built around hundreds of preserved human brain specimens. It is run by the National Institute of Mental Health and Neurosciences, so the focus is education rather than spectacle.

For visitors with a strong stomach and an interest in science, it is memorable in a way few city museums are. The displays explain brain anatomy and disease in direct, practical terms, and the collection gives the subject a physical reality textbooks cannot match.
