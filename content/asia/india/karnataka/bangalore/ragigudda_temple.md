Ragigudda Sri Prasanna Anjaneyaswamy Temple is a well-known Hanuman temple set on a rocky hill in south Bangalore. The climb and the hilltop setting give it more presence than many neighbourhood temples, with smaller shrines to Shiva and Ganesha within the complex.

It can be very crowded around Hanuman Jayanti, but outside major festival days it is a calmer local pilgrimage stop. Saturday midday meals draw devotees, and the views over the surrounding neighbourhood are part of the visit.
