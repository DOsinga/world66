Ramakrishna Math in Gandhi Bazaar is a calm ashram campus with a large meditation hall, gardens, and shrines connected with the Ramakrishna movement. It is less about spectacle than atmosphere: visitors come for the quiet grounds, the devotional setting, and a break from the surrounding city.

The preserved spots associated with Sarada Devi and Swami Vivekananda add interest for travellers following modern Hindu reform movements.
