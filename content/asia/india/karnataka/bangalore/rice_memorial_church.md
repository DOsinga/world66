Rice Memorial Church is a 1904 stone church in Bangalore Pete, named for missionary and scholar Benjamin Holt Rice. Its European Classical style stands out in the older market district: Tuscan columns, pediments, and arched windows give the building a formal, almost civic presence.

It is a short architectural stop rather than a long visit, but it adds a useful layer to a walk through the older part of central Bangalore.
