Shivoham Shiva Temple is a modern temple complex best known for its large Shiva statue set amid artificial mountain scenery and cave-like walk-through displays. It is especially busy around Maha Shivaratri.

The presentation is more theatrical than ancient, and visitors are steered toward paid darshan packages. Still, it is a distinctive Bangalore stop if you are interested in contemporary popular devotion.
