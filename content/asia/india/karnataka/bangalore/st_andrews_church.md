St. Andrew's Church is Bangalore's old Scottish Kirk, a Gothic-style Presbyterian church just off Cubbon Road. The steep tiled roof, pointed windows, and polished memorial plaques give it a quieter, more northern-European feel than many of the city's colonial churches.

The stained glass and pipe organ are the main reasons to step inside if it is open. It is an easy detour while walking between MG Road, Cubbon Park, and the older administrative quarter.
