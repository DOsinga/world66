type: section

Bangalore's sights range from Vijayanagara-era temples and the grand Hoysala-inspired Legislative Assembly to colonial-era parks, fine art galleries, and a pair of botanical gardens that have been growing since the 18th century. Cubbon Park sits at the centre of all of it, and half the museums in the city are within walking distance of its bandstand.
