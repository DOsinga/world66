Turahalli Forest is one of Bengaluru's more accessible dry scrub and eucalyptus woodland patches, popular with cyclists, walkers, and birders in the city's south. After the monsoon, small seasonal blooms and greener undergrowth soften what is otherwise a dry, open landscape.

It is a local nature escape rather than a deep forest. Go early, bring water, and treat it as a morning outdoors before the traffic and heat build.
