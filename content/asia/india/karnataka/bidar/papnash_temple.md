Papnash Temple is a Shiva temple on the edge of Bidar, associated in local tradition with Lord Rama's return from Lanka. Pilgrims come here for the sacred tank and the belief that the place washes away sin.

It is not as visually dramatic as Bidar Fort, but it adds a different side of the city: living pilgrimage, local ritual, and a quieter stop away from the main fort circuit.
