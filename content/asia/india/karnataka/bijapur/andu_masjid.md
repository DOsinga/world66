Andu Masjid is a small but distinctive Indo-Islamic mosque from 1608-09, built under Ibrahim Adil Shah II. Its two-level structure, open terrace, ribbed central dome, and corner minarets make it a worthwhile addition to a circuit of Bijapur's better-known monuments.
