Bahamani Gate is one of the old gateways of Bijapur's fortified city, part of the defensive circuit around the Adil Shahi capital. Its value lies less in decoration than in the scale and logic of the walls, bastions, and controlled entries that once shaped movement through the city.

Visit it as part of a wider Bijapur fort and old-city circuit rather than as a single stop. The gate helps connect the famous domes and mosques to the military landscape that protected them.
