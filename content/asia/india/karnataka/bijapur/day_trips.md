Bijapur is a useful base for the Chalukyan sites of north Karnataka, all of which lie within reasonable driving distance.

[Badami](/asia/india/karnataka/badami), about 130 kilometres south, has the famous rock-cut Hindu and Jain cave temples carved into a red sandstone cliff in the 6th century — strikingly different from anything in Bijapur and a different era entirely. Allow a full day; the temples and the lake are worth a slow visit.

The temple cluster at **Pattadakal** (UNESCO World Heritage) is a further 30 kilometres beyond Badami and an easy combined trip.

[Hampi](/asia/india/karnataka/hampi), capital of the Vijayanagara empire that the Adil Shahis helped to destroy in 1565, is about 280 kilometres south. It is too far for a comfortable day trip and is better treated as a separate two-day stop — but the connection between the two cities is well worth thinking about, as the wealth of Bijapur came in part from the sack of Vijayanagara.
