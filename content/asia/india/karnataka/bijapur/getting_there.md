---
title: "Getting There"
type: section
---

Bijapur (Vijayapura on the railway timetables) is on the main rail line between Solapur and Hubli and is well connected. Daily trains run from **Mumbai** (around 12 hours), **Bangalore** (12–14 hours), **Hyderabad** (8–10 hours), and **Pune**. The station is about 2 kilometres east of the old city; auto-rickshaws meet the trains.

By road, Bijapur is roughly four hours from **Solapur**, five from **Hubli**, and eight from **Hyderabad** or **Bangalore**. The state-run KSRTC and a number of private operators run buses on all the main routes; the central bus station is at the south edge of the old city.

The nearest airports are at **Belgavi** (about 200 km) and **Hubli** (260 km), both with daily flights from Bangalore and Mumbai.
