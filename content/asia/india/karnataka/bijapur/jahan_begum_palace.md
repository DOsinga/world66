Jahan Begum Palace is one of Bijapur's quieter Adil Shahi-era ruins, overshadowed by Gol Gumbaz, Ibrahim Rauza, and the great mosques. Its value is less in a perfect monument than in the way it expands the city's old royal landscape.

Stop if you are already moving through the citadel and lesser ruins with a driver or guide. The site rewards travellers who like fragments, masonry, and atmosphere, but it is not the first place to send someone with only a few hours in Bijapur.
