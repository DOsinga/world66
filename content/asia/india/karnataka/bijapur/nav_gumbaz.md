A complex of nine modest domed tombs ("Nav Gumbaz" means "nine domes") arranged in a cluster east of the old city. They were built in the late 17th century for various royal officials. None is individually outstanding, but the group seen together gives a good sense of how the Adil Shahi sense of monumental architecture filtered down to the noble class.

The site is somewhat overgrown and rarely visited. Worth a quick stop on the way to Gol Gumbaz, which is just down the road.
