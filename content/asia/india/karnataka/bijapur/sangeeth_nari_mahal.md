Sangeeth Nari Mahal is a ruined palace at Navraspur, outside the main cluster of Bijapur monuments. It is associated with the Adil Shahi court and the musical, poetic world of Ibrahim Adil Shah II, whose ambitions for Navraspur reached beyond ordinary palace building.

The remains are fragmentary, so come for atmosphere and context rather than perfect preservation. It is best combined with other outlying Bijapur sites and with a driver who knows the local roads; on its own it may feel too slight for a long detour.
