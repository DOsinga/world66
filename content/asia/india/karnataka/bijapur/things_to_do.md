---
title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

The whole reason to come to Bijapur is the architecture of the Adil Shahi sultanate, scattered across the old city. A full day will let you cover the major monuments at an unhurried pace. The big three — [Gol Gumbaz](/asia/india/karnataka/bijapur/gol_gumbaz), [Ibrahim Rauza](/asia/india/karnataka/bijapur/ibrahim_rauza), and the [Jama Masjid](/asia/india/karnataka/bijapur/jama_masjid) — are essential. After those, the unfinished tomb of [Bara Kaman](/asia/india/karnataka/bijapur/bara_kaman) and the giant Persian cannon [Malik-E-Maidan](/asia/india/karnataka/bijapur/malik_e_maidan) are the standout secondary sights.

Most monuments charge a small fee (some are free, some 25 rupees, the Gol Gumbaz around 300 rupees for foreigners). The Archaeological Survey of India maintains them; their attendants generally speak good English and the captions are clear.
