---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

The best time to visit Bijapur is **November through February**, when daytime temperatures are pleasant (20–28°C) and the air is dry. December and January are the coolest months and the most comfortable for the long walks the monuments demand.

**March through May** is brutally hot — daytime temperatures regularly above 40°C, the stone pavements unbearable by mid-morning. Visit the monuments before 10 am or after 4 pm if travelling in this period.

**June through September** brings the southwest monsoon, with afternoon storms. The weather is dramatic and the temperature drops, but the heavier rain showers can disrupt monument visits. October is a transition month, increasingly pleasant.
