---
title: "Getting There"
type: section
---

Chitradurga is on the Bangalore–Pune highway (NH48), 200km north of Bangalore and 60km south of Davangere.

**By road:** Most people drive or take a bus from Bangalore (about 3.5 hours) or Davangere (about an hour). Direct buses run frequently from Bangalore's Majestic bus stand. The KSRTC operates regular services.

**By rail:** Chitradurga railway station has services from Bangalore (overnight trains as well as day trains), Hubli, and Davangere. The station is about 2km from the fort.

**By air:** The nearest airport is Bangalore (Kempegowda International, 200km). No scheduled flights serve Chitradurga directly.
