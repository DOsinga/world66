title: "Eating Out"
type: section
---

Kodava cuisine is one of the most distinctive in South India — pork is a staple (unusual for Hindu cuisine), bamboo shoots and wild greens are common, and the cooking leans on the kachampuli, a sour reduction made from local Garcinia gummi-gutta fruit.

The dishes to look for: **pandi curry** (pork in kachampuli and roasted spice), **akki roti** (flat rice bread), **noolputtu** (steamed rice strings), and **kadambuttu** (steamed rice balls). Many homestays serve a full Kodava meal — book ahead.
