---
title: "Getting Around"
type: section
---

Coorg is a road-trip destination. Distances between the main sights are not large in kilometres, but the roads are slow — count an hour for every 30km, and slower in the rain.

The most flexible option is a **hired car with driver** out of Madikeri or Bangalore — expect ₹2,500-4,000 a day. **Self-drive** is possible but the estate roads are narrow and unsigned; have a working map app.

**Local buses** run between Madikeri and the larger villages (Bhagamandala, Virajpet, Kushalnagar) for next to nothing, but they are slow and infrequent. Within Madikeri itself, **tuk-tuks** handle everything in town.

Many homestays will arrange jeep transfers to specific sights like Mandalpatti or Tadiandamol, which is usually cheaper than booking from outside.
