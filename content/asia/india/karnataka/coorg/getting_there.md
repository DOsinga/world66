---
title: "Getting There"
type: section
---

Coorg has no airport and no railway station within the district. You get here by road from one of three directions.

**From Bangalore**, the drive is around 250km and takes six to seven hours via Mysore. Daily buses run from Majestic bus stand to Madikeri, taking about eight hours.

**From Mysore**, it is a much easier 120km — three hours by car, four by bus. This is the standard approach for most travellers.

**From Mangalore** on the coast, the drive is about 140km up the Western Ghats — slow, twisty, beautiful, and prone to landslides during monsoon.

The nearest airports are **Mangalore** (135km) and **Mysore** (130km); Bangalore's Kempegowda International is further but has more flights.
