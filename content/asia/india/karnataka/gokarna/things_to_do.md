title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

The main activities are beach walking and beach-hopping. The trail from town over the headland to [Kudle Beach](/asia/india/karnataka/gokarna/kudle_beach), then to [Om Beach](/asia/india/karnataka/gokarna/om_beach), then over the cliff to [Half Moon Beach](/asia/india/karnataka/gokarna/half_moon_beach), and finally to [Paradise Beach](/asia/india/karnataka/gokarna/paradise_beach) is a rewarding half-day walk. Sections involve scrambling over rocks; wear decent footwear.

The [Mahabaleshwara Temple](/asia/india/karnataka/gokarna/mahabaleshwara_temple) in the town centre is worth visiting even for non-pilgrims — [Car Street](/asia/india/karnataka/gokarna/car_street) around it is atmospheric with its priests' houses and small shops. The [Kotiteertha Tank](/asia/india/karnataka/gokarna/kotiteertha_tank), where pilgrims bathe, is nearby, and a short walk brings you to the [Maha Ganapati Temple](/asia/india/karnataka/gokarna/maha_ganapati_temple) and [Bhadrakali Temple](/asia/india/karnataka/gokarna/bhadrakali_temple).
