---
title: Getting There
type: section
---

Hubballi Junction is one of the major railway hubs of Karnataka, which is the main reason most visitors find themselves here. Direct trains connect Hubli to Bengaluru (about 6 hours on the fastest services), Mumbai (around 12 hours on the Goa-Mumbai corridor), and Hyderabad. For Goa, take a train to Londa and change to the Konkan Railway — or take a direct bus, which is often faster and more frequent.

The Rani Channamma Airport (HBX), named after the 19th-century queen of Kittur, serves a handful of domestic routes including flights to Bengaluru, Mumbai, and Hyderabad. Schedules change frequently; check before planning an air arrival.

By road, Hubli is about 5 hours from Bengaluru on NH48 and roughly 4 hours from Panaji in Goa via NH4A. The city is on a flat section of the Deccan, so road journeys here are less dramatic than elsewhere in Karnataka but also more straightforward.
