Indira Gandhi Glass House Garden is one of Hubli's most useful breathing spaces, a central park built around a glasshouse and open lawns. It is a simple urban garden rather than a botanical showpiece, but in a busy commercial city that simplicity is exactly the point.

Local families come for evening walks, children use the play areas, and the grounds occasionally host flower shows and civic events. If you are passing through Hubli, it is a practical place to reset between markets, temples, and bus or rail connections.
