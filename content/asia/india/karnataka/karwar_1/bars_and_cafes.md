---
title: "Bars and Cafes"
type: section
---

Karwar is a conservative town and nightlife is minimal. A few permit rooms attached to hotels serve beer and the local spirit toddy, but they are functional rather than atmospheric. More pleasant is the habit of sitting out at one of the tea stalls along the beach promenade at dusk, when the fishing boats return and the light on the river mouth turns agreeable. The KSTDC resort at Devbagh has a bar for guests.
