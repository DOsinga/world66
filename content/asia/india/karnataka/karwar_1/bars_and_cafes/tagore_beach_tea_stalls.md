A row of simple tea stalls lines the Tagore Beach promenade, selling cutting chai, fresh coconut water, and fried snacks — bhajis and boiled groundnuts mainly. These are the places where Karwar slows down of an evening. Locals pull up plastic chairs, fishing boats come in across the bay, and there is nothing much to do except watch the sky change colour over the water. No alcohol, no music — just chai and a view that costs nothing.
