Kamat is a no-frills South Indian restaurant serving vegetarian meals throughout the day. The morning idli-vada-sambar routine draws locals on their way to work, and at lunch the thali — rice, dal, sambar, rasam, and a couple of curries — is filling and fairly priced. The place is straightforward and popular enough that the food keeps moving, which generally means it is fresh.
