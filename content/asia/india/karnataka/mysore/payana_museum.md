Payana Museum is a transport and travel museum on the outskirts of Mysore, built around vintage vehicles and the romance of road journeys. It is a lighter stop than the palace or Chamundi Hill, but the collection gives car enthusiasts and families something different from the city’s standard heritage circuit.

The museum works best if you have your own transport or are already planning an outing beyond the centre. Expect polished nostalgia rather than deep scholarship: cars, signs, travel objects, and the pleasure of seeing how mobility became part of modern Indian life.
