Tipu's Drop is the dramatic cliff point associated with Tipu Sultan's hilltop retreat and the darker legend of condemned prisoners being thrown from the edge. The story is grim, but the view is the immediate reason to stop here.

Treat the edges seriously, especially when the hill is wet or crowded. The best light is early, when the plains below are still hazy and the heat has not built up.
