Agumbe, about 65 km west of Shimoga, sits at the crest of the Western Ghats and is one of the wettest places in India. The Agumbe Rainforest Research Station, established by herpetologist Romulus Whitaker, has made this small village a centre for king cobra research — the snakes are a common sight in the forests here, and the station does public education about their conservation.

The ghats viewpoint above the village is famous for sunsets; the forest trails reward early morning walks for birds and wildlife. Sunset Point above the village looks west over the coastal plains and is popular with locals. The road from Shimoga descends dramatically through hairpin bends into increasingly dense forest — the drive itself is part of the experience. Accommodation is limited; most people visit as a day trip from Shimoga or Udupi.
