Shimoga's real value is as a base for exploring the surrounding Malnad region. The bus stand connects to all the major destinations; a private car or motorcycle gives more flexibility for the forest roads.

[Jog Falls](/asia/india/karnataka/shimoga/jog_falls) is the headline attraction — one of India's highest waterfalls, about 100 km northwest. [Agumbe](/asia/india/karnataka/shimoga/agumbe) in the Ghats is a rainforest village famous for king cobras and monsoon rain, 65 km west. [Sigandur](/asia/india/karnataka/shimoga/sigandur), on the backwaters of the Sharavathi reservoir, combines a boat crossing with a well-known temple and is worth combining with a Jog Falls trip. [Kodachadri](/asia/india/karnataka/shimoga/kodachadri) is a moderately demanding trek to a forested peak with views into the Ghats, about 95 km southwest.
