Shimoga's real value is as a base for exploring the surrounding Malnad region. The bus stand connects to all the major destinations; a private car or motorcycle gives more flexibility for the forest roads.

[Jog Falls](/asia/india/karnataka/jog_falls) is the headline attraction — one of India's highest waterfalls, about 100 km northwest. The rainforest village of [Agumbe](/asia/india/karnataka/agumbe) in the Ghats, 65 km west, is famous for king cobras and some of the heaviest monsoon rain in India. [Sigandur](/asia/india/karnataka/shimoga/sigandur), on the backwaters of the Sharavathi reservoir, combines a boat crossing with a well-known temple and is worth combining with a Jog Falls trip. [Kodachadri](/asia/india/karnataka/kodachadri) is a moderately demanding trek to a forested peak with views into the Ghats, about 95 km southwest.
