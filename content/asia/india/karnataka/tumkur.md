Tumkur sits 70 km northwest of Bangalore on the Pune highway, at the edge of the Devarayanadurga Hills. It is a busy commercial town and the administrative seat of Tumkur District, known for its markets in coconut, tobacco, and vegetable oil.

The town itself is not a major tourist destination, but it makes a reasonable stop if you are travelling between Bangalore and the northern Karnataka interior. The [Siddhaganga Matha](/asia/india/karnataka/tumkur/siddhaganga_matha), a large Lingayat monastery 5 km from town, is worth a visit — it runs one of India's largest free residential schools and the grounds are peaceful. The Devarayanadurga hill fort, about 15 km south, has two ancient temples and good views of the surrounding countryside.

Other day-trip options from Tumkur include the massive granite monolith at Madhugiri (claimed to be Asia's second-largest), the Markonahalli dam, and the historic fort town of Sira.
