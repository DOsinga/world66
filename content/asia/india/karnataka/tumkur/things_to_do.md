---
title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Tumkur is not a tourist city, but it is a useful base for exploring some genuinely worthwhile sites in the surrounding district. The Siddhaganga Matha monastery just outside town is one of the most important Lingayat institutions in Karnataka, while Devarayanadurga and Madhugiri offer hill forts, temples, and dramatic granite landscapes within an hour's drive.
