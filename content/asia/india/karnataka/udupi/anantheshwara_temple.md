Anantheshwara Temple stands close to Udupi's famous Sri Krishna Matha and is one of the older sacred sites in the temple quarter. Dedicated to Shiva, it is woven into the same tight network of mutts, lanes, tanks, and shrines that gives central Udupi its pilgrim-town character.

Travellers often come to Udupi for the Krishna temple and move on too quickly. This temple is a good reason to slow down: the atmosphere is quieter, the stonework and ritual rhythm feel older, and the surrounding streets show how deeply religious life shapes the town's daily routine.
