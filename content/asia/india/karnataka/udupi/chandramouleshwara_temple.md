Chandramouleshwara Temple is one of Udupi's old Shiva temples, standing close to the better-known Krishna Matha in the town's sacred centre. Its scale is modest, but its age and position make it part of the temple circuit that gives Udupi its identity as a pilgrimage town rather than just a coastal stop.

The best way to visit is on foot, moving slowly between the nearby shrines, temple tanks, and lanes around Car Street. Chandramouleshwara is still an active place of worship, so the experience is less about sightseeing display and more about watching the rhythm of lamps, bells, offerings, and pilgrims moving through a compact sacred neighbourhood.
