Kapu Beach is one of the strongest coastal outings from Udupi, a broad Arabian Sea beach known for its lighthouse, black rocks, and sunset light. It is far enough from the temple-town centre to feel like a separate excursion, but close enough to work as an easy half-day trip.

The lighthouse is the visual marker, and the beach below it is the place to linger. Swimming conditions vary with season and surf, so treat the sea with caution and come mainly for the walk, photographs, and the change from Udupi's inland temple rhythm.
