Malpe Beach is Udupi's easiest seaside escape, a broad stretch of sand on the Arabian Sea a short ride west of town. It is more developed than the quieter beaches further down the coast, but that also means food stalls, simple facilities, and regular activity around the harbour.

The beach is especially useful because boats for St Mary's Islands usually leave from Malpe. Come early for the cleanest light and cooler sand, or late in the afternoon when local families arrive and the fishing boats give the shoreline a proper coastal Karnataka feel.
