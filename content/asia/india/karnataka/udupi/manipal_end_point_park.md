Manipal End-point Park is a clifftop viewpoint above the Suwarna River, close to the university town of Manipal. The appeal is simple: broad views over river country, distant hills, and, on clear days, toward the coast.

It is a good low-key stop if you are already in Udupi or Manipal and want an easy evening walk with a view. The park is especially popular with students, so expect more local life than formal sightseeing.
