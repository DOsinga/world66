Pajaka Kshetra is a pilgrimage site near Udupi, revered as the birthplace of the philosopher Madhvacharya. The complex includes a Durga temple, a memorial to Madhvacharya, and sacred ponds used by pilgrims.

It is most rewarding for visitors already interested in Udupi's religious history and the Dvaita tradition.
