Sri Krishna Temple and Mutt is the spiritual centre of Udupi and the reason many Indian travellers know the town at all. The temple is associated with the Dvaita philosopher Madhvacharya and remains an active pilgrimage complex rather than a museum piece.

Visitors should come with patience and respect for temple routines. The surrounding lanes, mutts, food traditions, and devotional atmosphere make this one of coastal Karnataka's strongest cultural stops.
