St Mary's Island is a small island group off Malpe known for its columnar basalt formations, an unusual geological sight on this stretch of the Karnataka coast. The dark, angular rock against the Arabian Sea gives the place a sharper look than a standard beach outing.

Boat access is weather-dependent and usually runs from the Malpe side, so check conditions before planning around it. The island is best for walking, photography, and geology-minded curiosity rather than swimming; bring shade and water if you go in the hotter part of the day.
