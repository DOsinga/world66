Ambalappuzha Sree Krishna Swamy Temple is a major temple south of Alappuzha, known across Kerala for its Krishna shrine and traditional palpayasam offering. It is far enough from town to feel like a deliberate excursion, but it belongs naturally to an Alappuzha itinerary.

The temple is active rather than museum-like, so dress and behave accordingly. Travellers come for the ritual atmosphere, the Kerala temple architecture, and the chance to see a religious landmark that is important locally, not just photogenic.
