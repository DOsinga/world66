---
title: "Getting There"
type: section
---

Alappuzha is 84 km north of Thiruvananthapuram and 60 km south of [Kochi](/asia/india/kerala/kochi). There is no airport; the nearest is Kochi International Airport (about 75 km by road).

**By train**: Alappuzha railway station is on the main coastal line between Thiruvananthapuram and Ernakulam (Kochi). Several express and passenger trains stop here daily; the journey from Kochi takes about 1.5 hours, from Thiruvananthapuram around 2.5 hours.

**By bus**: Regular government and private buses connect Alappuzha to Kochi, Thiruvananthapuram, [Kottayam](/asia/india/kerala/kottayam), and [Kollam](/asia/india/kerala/kollam). The KSRTC bus stand is near the town centre.

**By ferry**: The eight-hour public ferry from [Kollam](/asia/india/kerala/kollam) to Alappuzha is the most scenic way to arrive and one of Kerala's great journeys. Ferries run once daily.
