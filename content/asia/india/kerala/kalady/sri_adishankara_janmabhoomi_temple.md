Sri Adishankara Janmabhoomi Temple is Kalady's central pilgrimage site. The complex stands near the Periyar and is associated with the Sringeri Sharada Peetham, with shrines to Shankara and Sharadamba and a devotional rhythm that matters more than architectural show.

Come respectfully and give yourself time. This is an active religious place, and the most meaningful moments are often small ones: lamps, chanting, and pilgrims pausing by the river.
