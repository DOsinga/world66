Bastion Bungalow is an Indo-Dutch heritage building in Fort Kochi, built on the site of the old Stromsburg Fort. Its broad verandas, thick walls, and colonial proportions make it one of the area's better architectural survivals.

The building now serves as the District Heritage Museum, with exhibits that add context to Fort Kochi's layered Portuguese, Dutch, and British past.
