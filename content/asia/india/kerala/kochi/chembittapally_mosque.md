Chembittapally Mosque is known for its large copper-roofed domes, which give the mosque its name. Its gates open toward the lanes leading to Panayapilly, Goldenmukku, Jew Street, and Mattancherry Bazaar, so it fits naturally into a walk through old Kochi's layered religious and trading quarters.
