Chottanikkara Devi Temple is one of the major goddess temples around Kochi, drawing steady streams of pilgrims through the day. It is especially busy during the Makam festival, but even outside festival season the rituals, lamps, and crowds give a strong sense of living temple culture rather than a monument kept only for visitors.
