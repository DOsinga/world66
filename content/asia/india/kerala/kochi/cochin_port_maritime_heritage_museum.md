The Cochin Port Maritime Heritage Museum tells the story of Kochi's modern harbour from a former port engineer's residence on Willingdon Island. Its strongest material is a collection of old photographs showing how the port city grew around dredging, shipping, and the creation of the island itself.

It is a small, focused stop rather than a major museum, but it adds useful context before or after exploring Fort Kochi and Mattancherry.
