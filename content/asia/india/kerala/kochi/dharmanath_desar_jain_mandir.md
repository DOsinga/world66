Dharmanath Desar Jain Mandir, often called the Gujarati Mandir, reflects the Jain and Gujarati trading community that settled in Kochi centuries ago. The temple is still active, so visit quietly and look for the carved details and calm courtyard rather than expecting a museum-like sight.
