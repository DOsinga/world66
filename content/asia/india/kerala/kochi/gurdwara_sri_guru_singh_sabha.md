Gurdwara Sri Guru Singh Sabha is Kerala's only Sikh gurdwara, established by Kochi's Punjabi community in 1955. It is a modest but distinctive religious site in a city better known for churches, mosques, temples, and synagogues.

The main reason to visit is to see another layer of Kochi's long multicultural story. Special rites and langar are held on weekends; visitors should dress and behave respectfully as in any active place of worship.
