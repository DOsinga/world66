Kumbalangi Integrated Tourism Village is a community tourism project on the backwaters south of central Kochi. It gives visitors a structured way to see fishing, coir work, crab farming, and village life without turning the place into a theme park.

The visit is most worthwhile if you are interested in Kerala's everyday backwater economy rather than another monument. Go with a local guide or arranged visit, and treat it as a cultural stop, not a quick photo detour.
