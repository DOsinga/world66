Matsyafed Milky-way Aqua-Tourist Village turns a working shrimp farm into a low-key visitor stop on Kochi's backwater edge. You can fish for prawns, crabs, and shrimp, canoe through the mangrove-fringed water, and get a close look at the aquaculture that shapes this part of Kerala.

It is best treated as a relaxed family outing rather than a major sight, but it adds a different texture to a Kochi trip built around Fort Kochi and Mattancherry.
