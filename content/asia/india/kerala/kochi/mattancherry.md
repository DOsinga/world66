Mattancherry is the old spice-trading quarter of Kochi, immediately south of Fort Kochi, and gives a more commercial, less touristic picture of the city. The main lane — Jew Town Road — is lined with antique shops, spice merchants, and wholesale warehouses dealing in pepper, cardamom, and other Kerala spices. The smells are extraordinary.

The [Mattancherry Palace](/asia/india/kerala/kochi/mattancherry_palace) and the [Paradesi Synagogue](/asia/india/kerala/kochi/paradesi_synagogue) are both in this neighbourhood and can be visited together in a half-day walk. The antique shops are genuinely interesting — old bronze lamps, wooden furniture, maps, and colonial-era curiosities jostle for space in narrow shopfronts. Bargaining is expected.
