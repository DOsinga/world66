The National Shrine Basilica of Our Lady of Ransom, also known as Vallarpadam Church, is a major Christian pilgrimage site in Kochi. Its venerated image of the Virgin Mary and Infant Jesus is traditionally linked to Portuguese merchants in the early 16th century.

The basilica is worth visiting for its religious importance and for the story of the image surviving the flood that destroyed the older Portuguese church in 1676.
