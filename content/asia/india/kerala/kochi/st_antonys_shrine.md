St. Antony's Shrine in Kaloor is one of Ernakulam's busy Catholic pilgrimage stops. Devotees come through at all hours, and the place is more interesting for its steady movement of local worshippers than for architecture alone.

Visit respectfully and keep it brief if services are under way. It works best as a glimpse of living faith in modern Kochi, away from the better-known colonial churches of Fort Kochi.
