St. George's Syro-Malabar Forane Church, often associated with Edappally, is one of the best-known Christian pilgrimage churches in the Kochi area. The church draws both Christian and non-Christian visitors, especially for devotions linked to healing traditions.

It is most useful for travelers interested in Kerala's long Christian history and the living religious life of Ernakulam beyond the better-known colonial churches of Fort Kochi.
