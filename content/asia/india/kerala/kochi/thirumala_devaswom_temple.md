Thirumala Devaswom Temple is a Gowda Saraswat Brahmin temple dedicated to Lord Venkateswara. It is known for its large brass bell and copper-roofed north gopuram, both of which make it stand out among Kochi's many religious sites.

The temple is close enough to Mattancherry and Fort Kochi to combine with the area's synagogue, palace, churches, and maritime heritage stops.
