Thrikkakara Temple is one of the key temples associated with Kerala's Onam festival and is dedicated to Vamana, an incarnation of Vishnu. The temple becomes especially lively during Onam, with several days of rituals and cultural performances.

Outside the festival season it is a quieter religious site, but still an important stop for visitors interested in Kerala's temple traditions.
