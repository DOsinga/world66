title: Bars and Cafes
type: section
tags: [bars_and_cafes]
---

Kerala is not a great drinking state — alcohol is taxed heavily and government-controlled liquor shops operate limited hours. In Kovalam, however, the beach resort setting means most restaurants on the tourist strip have beer and spirits licenses. The bars are attached to restaurants rather than being standalone operations, and the evenings here are relaxed rather than lively.
