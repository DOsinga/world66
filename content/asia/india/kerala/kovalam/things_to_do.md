Kovalam's main activity is the beach — specifically the choice of which beach suits your mood. Beyond that, Ayurvedic treatments are the other anchor of a stay here, with everything from quick oil massages to week-long panchakarma programmes. The fishing village of Vizhinjam, a short walk south, and the historic Halcyon Castle add some depth to what is otherwise a resort experience.
