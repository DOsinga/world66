---
title: Getting There
type: section
---

Kumarakom sits about 16 km west of [Kottayam](/asia/india/kerala/kottayam), which is the nearest railway hub and the most practical arrival point. Trains from Trivandrum, Kochi, and as far north as Mumbai stop at Kottayam. From the station, auto-rickshaws and taxis run to Kumarakom in 30 minutes; homestays and resorts usually arrange pick-ups.

The nearest international airport is Kochi (COK), 85 km north — about two hours by taxi. Trivandrum (TRV) is around 160 km south, about four hours by road. A more characterful route is by boat from [Alappuzha](/asia/india/kerala/alappuzha) across Vembanad Lake; a ferry service runs daily and a private houseboat transfer can be arranged.
