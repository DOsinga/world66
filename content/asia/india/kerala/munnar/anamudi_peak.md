Anamudi, at 2,695 metres the highest peak in South India, stands within the boundaries of Eravikulam National Park, about 15 kilometres from Munnar. It is a substantial summit by the standards of the peninsular hills — higher than anything in Sri Lanka or peninsular Malaysia — and the high-altitude grasslands around it support the Nilgiri tahr population that the park was established to protect.

Climbing Anamudi requires a forest department permit, which must be arranged in advance through the park authorities in Munnar. The climb is not technically difficult but is long and should be attempted in dry weather; the summit is often shrouded in cloud by midday. Only a limited number of permits are issued each day. The park closes Anamudi access entirely during the calving season (February to March). For most visitors, seeing the peak from the Eravikulam observation areas is enough — it dominates the skyline in the way that South India's highest mountain should.
