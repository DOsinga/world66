Blossom Park is a landscaped garden and recreation area near Munnar town, with lawns, flowers, paths, and views toward the surrounding tea hills. It is not wild Kerala, but it is an easy breather if you are based in town with children or limited time.

Use it as a light stop between the tea museum, town centre, and nearby viewpoints. The park's value is convenience: a soft, green place to pause before heading back into the steeper country around Munnar.
