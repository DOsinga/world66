Chinnar Wildlife Sanctuary is a different side of the Munnar region: drier, thornier,
and more open than the tea hills most visitors picture first. It protects forest, scrub,
river valleys, and wildlife habitat on the Kerala-Tamil Nadu border, including the
endangered grizzled giant squirrel.

This is a place for guided walks, wildlife patience, and a slower day in the Western
Ghats. Do not expect guaranteed animal sightings, but do expect a landscape that
broadens your sense of Munnar beyond plantations and viewpoints. Check forest-department
rules before planning treks.
