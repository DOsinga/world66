The Floriculture Centre is a small, well-kept garden near Munnar with beds of seasonal flowers, ornamental plants, and a few herbal displays. The tea-covered hills behind it are part of the appeal, especially when the flowers are in bloom.

It is not a major sight, but it makes an easy, low-effort stop for photos or a short break between Munnar's viewpoints, tea estates, and national park visits.
