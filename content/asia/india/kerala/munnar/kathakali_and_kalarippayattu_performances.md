Kathakali and Kalarippayattu performances give Munnar visitors an accessible introduction to two Kerala traditions: the stylised dance-drama of Kathakali and the athletic martial art of Kalarippayattu. In a hill station where many days revolve around tea estates and viewpoints, an evening performance adds a useful cultural layer.

These shows are staged for visitors, so expectations should be realistic, but they can still be worthwhile when presented with care. Arrive early if makeup demonstration or seating choice matters, and confirm the current venue and schedule locally.
