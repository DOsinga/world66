Palakkad is Kerala's inland gateway, sitting in the Palghat Gap where the Western Ghats open toward Tamil Nadu. It is less lush and less tourist-shaped than the coast or the hill stations, but that makes it useful: a real market town with rail connections, temple streets, paddy fields and hot, dry afternoons that feel more Tamil than Keralan.

The centre is anchored by [Palakkad Fort](/asia/india/kerala/palakkad/palakkad_fort), a well-preserved 18th-century stronghold often associated with Hyder Ali and Tipu Sultan. North of town, [Malampuzha Dam](/asia/india/kerala/palakkad/malampuzha_dam) and its gardens make the standard local outing, while [Kalpathy](/asia/india/kerala/palakkad/kalpathy) preserves an old agraharam quarter of tiled houses, temple lanes and an important chariot festival.

Palakkad is also the practical base for [Silent Valley National Park](/asia/india/kerala/silent_valley_national_park), though the park itself needs advance planning and an early start. Don't expect a polished destination. Come for a day or two if you are crossing between Kerala and Tamil Nadu, if you care about the cultural borderland, or if you need a sensible launch point for the forests north of town.
