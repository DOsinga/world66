Thalassery Pier, or Kadal Palam, is the old sea bridge built for loading goods between shore and ships. It is a simple industrial relic, but it helps explain why this modest town mattered: pepper, timber, and colonial trade all moved through this coastline.

The pier pairs naturally with the fort and Overbury's Folly. Treat it as part of the town's harbour story rather than a polished attraction.
