The region around Thiruvananthapuram is rich in day trip options. Kovalam, the famous beach resort with its crescent of sand and lighthouse, is 16 km south and easily reached by taxi or auto. Varkala, with its distinctive cliff beach and backpacker ambiance, is about 50 km north — comfortable as a day trip or a two-day excursion. Kollam (Quilon) is about 70 km north on the coast, the southern gateway to the Kerala backwaters.
