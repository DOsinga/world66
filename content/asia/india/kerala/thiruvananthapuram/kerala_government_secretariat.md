The Kerala Government Secretariat is the main administrative landmark of Thiruvananthapuram, a long colonial-era building that anchors the city's civic core. Its arcaded facade and formal grounds make it one of the clearest architectural statements along the central government axis.

Most travelers will see it from outside rather than tour the interior. It is worth a short stop while moving between museums, temples, and the Palayam area, especially if you are interested in how princely-state and colonial architecture shaped the modern Kerala capital.
