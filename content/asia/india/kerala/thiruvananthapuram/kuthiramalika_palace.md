Kuthiramalika Palace, beside Padmanabhaswamy Temple, is a Travancore palace known for its carved wooden horse brackets and traditional Kerala architecture. The palace museum displays royal antiques and is one of the most useful stops for understanding Thiruvananthapuram's old royal quarter.
