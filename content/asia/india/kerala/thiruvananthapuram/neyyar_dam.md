Neyyar Dam is a reservoir and forest-edge outing in the hills southeast of Thiruvananthapuram, often combined with boating, picnic stops, and visits toward the Neyyar wildlife sanctuary area. It offers a greener day trip than the city's museums, temples, and beaches.

The dam is best approached as a half-day or full-day excursion, depending on transport and whether you add nearby nature activities. Check current access and boat operations, especially outside peak visitor periods.
