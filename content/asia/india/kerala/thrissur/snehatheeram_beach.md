Snehatheeram Beach is a managed beach park at Thalikkulam, west of Thrissur, with a broad stretch of Arabian Sea sand and a landscaped visitor area. It is one of the easier coastal outings from the city, especially for families and sunset trips.

The beach is more organized than wild: expect local crowds, snack stalls, and a social evening atmosphere rather than solitude. Swimming conditions can vary with the season, so treat the water cautiously and come mainly for the coastal air and sunset.
