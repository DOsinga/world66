Thrissur Zoo is a family-friendly stop near the city's museum quarter, with animal enclosures, a snake house, and shaded grounds that work well for a slower break between temples and museums.

It is most useful for visitors travelling with children, but the central location also makes it easy to combine with the Archaeological and Art Museum next door.
