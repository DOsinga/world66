Vaidyaratnam Ayurveda Museum presents the history, tools, and traditions of Ayurveda in Kerala. The museum explains the development of this medical tradition through displays on ingredients, preparation methods, treatment rooms, and the families and institutions that preserved the practice around Thrissur.
