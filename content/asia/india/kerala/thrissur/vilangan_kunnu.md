Vilangan Kunnu is a hilltop recreation area on the edge of Thrissur, developed with walking paths, play areas, and viewpoints. Locals come for the breeze and the broad views over the surrounding countryside, especially in the late afternoon.

It is most useful as a relaxed family stop or sunset viewpoint rather than a formal sight. Bring water, and expect a short climb or walk depending on where you enter.
