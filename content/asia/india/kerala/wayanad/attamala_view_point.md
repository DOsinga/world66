Attamala View Point looks out over Wayanad's valleys, tea estates, and forested hills. Mornings are best, when mist still hangs in the folds of the hills and the light is soft enough for long views.

The glass-bridge attraction has made the viewpoint more popular, so expect a busier, more commercial feel than at a quiet roadside overlook.
