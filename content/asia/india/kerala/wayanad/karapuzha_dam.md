Karapuzha Dam is a scenic reservoir stop in Wayanad, backed by low hills and open water. It is quieter than the district's best-known waterfalls and caves, and is mainly worth visiting for the view.

Late afternoon is the best time to come, when the light softens and the reservoir makes an easy break between longer drives through Wayanad.
