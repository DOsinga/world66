Kuruva Dweep is a cluster of forested river islands on the Kabini tributaries, covering roughly 950 acres of evergreen vegetation. The appeal is simple: quiet paths, bamboo, birdlife, and the feeling of being away from Wayanad's busier road stops.

Access is usually controlled to protect the fragile habitat, so check local conditions before setting out. When open, it is best treated as a slow nature visit rather than a quick photo stop.
