Meenmutty Falls is one of Wayanad's bigger waterfall sights, dropping in three tiers on the slopes of the Banasura hills. The falls are at their most impressive after rain, when the water spreads across the rock face and the forest feels properly wet and green. Access can involve rough paths, so check local conditions before setting out.
