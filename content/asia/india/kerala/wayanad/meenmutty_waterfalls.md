Meenmutty Waterfalls is a forest waterfall in Wayanad reached by a fairly demanding walk through coffee, tea, and jungle terrain. The falls descend in stages, and visitors usually go as far as access and seasonal conditions allow.

This is not a casual roadside waterfall. You need decent shoes, water, and often a local guide or forest entry ticket, but the reward is a wilder, wetter Wayanad experience than the viewpoints along the main roads.
