Pookode Lake is a small, green-fringed lake in Wayanad, known for boating and easy walks near the water. The surrounding forest, lotus growth, and freshwater fish make it a gentle family stop rather than a hard hike.

It is a useful contrast to Wayanad's caves, peaks, and waterfalls. Visit early or outside peak holiday hours if you want the lakeside at its quietest.
