Puliyarmala Jain Temple is a historic Jain temple near Kalpetta, dedicated to Anantnath Swami. Its Dravidian-Hoysala style, stonework, and Jain sculptures make it one of Wayanad's more interesting religious sites.

The temple is also tied to local history: Tipu Sultan is said to have used it for storing arms and ammunition, which adds another layer to the visit.
