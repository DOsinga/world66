---
title: Things to Do
type: section
---

Wayanad's main draws are outdoor — trekking, wildlife spotting, and exploring the caves and waterfalls tucked into the forest. The tribal culture of the Paniya, Kurichiya, and Adivasi communities adds a dimension you will not find in most Kerala hill destinations.

Most treks and sanctuary visits require advance booking; organize safaris and permits a day in advance if possible, especially during the October-May high season.
