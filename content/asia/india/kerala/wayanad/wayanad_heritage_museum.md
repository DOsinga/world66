Wayanad Heritage Museum is a compact museum in Ambalavayal, useful for understanding the district beyond its hills and waterfalls. The galleries cover local archaeology, farming life, ritual objects, tribal communities, and hero stones from the region's older past.

It is not a long visit, but it adds good context before or after Edakkal Caves. Allow about half an hour unless you are especially interested in Kerala's upland cultures.
