Wayanad Wildlife Sanctuary is part of the Nilgiri Biosphere Reserve and links with the Nagarhole, Bandipur, and Mudumalai protected areas. The sanctuary is known for elephants, deer, wild boar, birds, and forest landscapes, with visits usually arranged through the designated entry points and forest department rules.
