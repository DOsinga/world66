---
title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Kavaratti is a small island, and its attractions reflect that: the water is the main event, with snorkelling and diving over coral reefs that rank among India's finest. On land, the [Ujra Mosque](#ujra_mosque) with its remarkable carved driftwood entrance is the most architecturally significant building in Lakshadweep, and the [Kavaratti Aquarium](#kavaratti_aquarium) gives a sense of the reef ecosystem without getting wet.

The island's real pleasures are unstructured: walking the beach in the early morning before the heat builds, watching fishing boats land their catch, and finding a patch of shallow lagoon water to yourself.
