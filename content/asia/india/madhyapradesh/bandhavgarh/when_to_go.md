---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

The park is open from **October to June** and closes completely during the monsoon (July to September) when the roads become impassable. **February to May** is the best period for tiger sightings: the dry season concentrates animals around the remaining waterholes, and the trees lose their leaves, making it easier to spot wildlife. Temperatures in April and May are fierce — above 40°C at midday — but tigers are more visible, and this is when the most dramatic encounters happen.

**October and November** offer a gentler introduction: the forest is green after the rains, bird life is prolific, and the heat is manageable. December and January are cool (it can drop to 5°C overnight near Tala), and the park is less busy than in peak season. Book safaris well in advance for February through April — this is when Bandhavgarh attracts the most visitors.
