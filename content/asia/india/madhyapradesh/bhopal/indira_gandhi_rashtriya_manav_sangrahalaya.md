Indira Gandhi Rashtriya Manav Sangrahalaya is one of Bhopal's most important museums, and one of India's more ambitious anthropology institutions. Its open-air displays, reconstructed dwellings, and exhibitions explore tribal, folk, and regional cultures across the country.

Give it time. The museum is large, and its outdoor setting makes it feel closer to a cultural landscape than a single building. It pairs naturally with the nearby lake and other Bhopal museums.
