The Regional Museum of Natural History in Bhopal is a branch of India's National Museum of Natural History. Its displays focus on the natural environment, wildlife, and environmental protection, with an informal education role rather than a grand museum collection.

It is a useful family stop or rainy-day option, especially for visitors already exploring Bhopal's museum circuit around the lakes and cultural institutions.
