---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

The best time to visit Bhopal is October to March, when the weather is dry and cool — December and January have pleasant days around 20°C and cool nights. The monsoon runs from July to September and brings heavy rain; the city becomes green and the lakes fill, but outdoor exploration is difficult.

April and May are uncomfortably hot by any standard, with temperatures regularly exceeding 40°C. If you are combining Bhopal with [Sanchi](/asia/india/madhyapradesh/sanchi), the winter months are also the most comfortable for the Sanchi ruins, which have no shade.
