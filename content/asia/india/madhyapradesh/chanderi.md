Chanderi is a small Bundelkhand town with a surprisingly dense layer of history. Fort walls climb the hills, medieval gates break through the rock, mosques and palaces sit among the lanes, and Jain shrines are cut into the surrounding slopes.

The town is also one of India's great handloom names. Chanderi saris are known for their light silk-cotton weave and zari work, and the craft still gives the place a living identity rather than leaving it as another ruin town. That mix is what makes Chanderi worth the detour: monuments for the morning, weavers and market lanes for the afternoon.

Chanderi is not as easy to reach as Gwalior, Orchha, or Khajuraho, and that is part of the appeal. Come if you like compact historic towns where the best sights are close enough to explore slowly.
