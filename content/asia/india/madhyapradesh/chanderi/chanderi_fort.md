Chanderi Fort, also called Kirti Durg, is the natural starting point for the town. Its walls run across the hill above Chanderi, with gates, bastions, and open views over the old settlement and surrounding countryside.

The fort's scale is what makes it memorable. You are not just looking at one gate or courtyard, but at a long defensive line that explains why Chanderi mattered on the routes between Malwa and Bundelkhand.
