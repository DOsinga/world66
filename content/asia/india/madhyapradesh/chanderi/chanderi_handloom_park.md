Chanderi's handloom tradition is not a side note; it is the craft that gives the town its modern reputation. The saris are usually light silk-cotton pieces with fine zari work, woven in patterns that have become famous across India.

The handloom park and local weaving outlets are useful places to understand the process before buying. Ask to see the looms if possible, and do not treat the visit as only a souvenir stop.
