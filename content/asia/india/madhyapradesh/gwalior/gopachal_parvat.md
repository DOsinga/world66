Gopachal Parvat refers to the rock-cut Jain figures carved into the slopes around Gwalior Fort. Some are monumental, some badly damaged, and some easy to miss if you are focused only on reaching the top. Together they make the ascent feel like a pilgrimage landscape as much as a military approach.

The figures are best seen on the way up or down from the fort, when you can stop and look without making a separate excursion. Wear decent shoes: the roadside viewpoints and paths can be uneven, and the sun on the rock is fierce.
