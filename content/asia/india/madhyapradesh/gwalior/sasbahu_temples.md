The Sasbahu Temples are among the most rewarding stops on the fort plateau. They are partly ruined, but the carving is still delicate and the setting is superb, with views across the city and enough space to appreciate the twin structures without being crushed by crowds.

They sit away from the main palace, so do not skip them if you have already paid your fort entry. The temples are especially good for travellers who enjoy architectural detail: pillars, ceiling panels, miniature figures and the way the surviving stonework filters light.
