Annapurna Temple is one of Indore's better-known devotional stops, dedicated to the goddess associated with food and nourishment. Its colourful gateway, sculptural detail, and steady stream of worshippers give it a lively feel, especially during festivals and busy prayer times.

It is not as distinctive as Kanch Mandir or as atmospheric as the night food markets, but it belongs on a rounded Indore itinerary. Visit respectfully, leave shoes where indicated, and give yourself time to watch how the temple functions as a neighbourhood anchor as much as a monument.
