Gomatgiri is a Jain pilgrimage complex on a hill west of Indore, known for its white shrines, small temples, and a statue of Bahubali set against open sky. The site is modern rather than ancient, but its hilltop position gives it a calmness that is hard to find in the city centre.

It is most worthwhile around sunrise or late afternoon, when the light is softer and the views back toward Indore are clearer. Visitors should dress and behave as they would at any active religious site; the reward is a quiet devotional landscape rather than an archaeological monument.
