Venkatesh Temple is a Vaishnavite temple in central Indore, associated locally with the city's Rath Yatra procession. It is not as visually unusual as Kanch Mandir or as prominent as Rajwada, but it is a meaningful stop for travellers interested in Indore's living religious sites.

Visit respectfully and expect an active neighbourhood temple rather than a museum. It is easiest to combine with other old-city sights and markets nearby.
