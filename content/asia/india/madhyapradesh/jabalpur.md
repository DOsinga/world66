Jabalpur is a large city in central Madhya Pradesh, sitting on the upper Narmada River. It is not a tourist destination in itself, but it serves as the gateway to some of the most dramatic landscapes in central India.

The main attraction nearby is the [Marble Rocks at Bhedaghat](/asia/india/madhyapradesh/jabalpur/bhedaghat), about 25 km south of the city. The Narmada flows through a narrow gorge of white marble cliffs that rise up to 30 metres on both sides. Boat rides through the gorge — particularly by moonlight — are spectacular. At the end of the gorge, the [Dhuandhar Falls](/asia/india/madhyapradesh/jabalpur/dhuandhar_falls) drop the river about 10 metres in a roar of spray.

Jabalpur is also a base for visiting Kanha National Park and Bandhavgarh National Park, two of the best tiger reserves in India. Both are 4-6 hours by road from the city.

The city itself has a few points of interest: [Madan Mahal Fort](/asia/india/madhyapradesh/jabalpur/madan_mahal_fort), perched on a rocky hilltop, and the [Rani Durgavati Museum](/asia/india/madhyapradesh/jabalpur/rani_durgavati_museum). The [Hanumantal lake and Jain temple](/asia/india/madhyapradesh/jabalpur/hanumantal_lake) area is pleasant for an early morning walk. Jabalpur is well connected by train to Delhi, Mumbai, and Varanasi.
