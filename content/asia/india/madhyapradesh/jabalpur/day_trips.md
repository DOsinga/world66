title: "Day Trips"
type: section
---

**Kanha National Park** is one of India's premier tiger reserves, about 180 km east of Jabalpur — a 4-hour drive to Mukki or Khatia gate. It was the inspiration for Rudyard Kipling's Jungle Book and has some of the best-managed wildlife safari infrastructure in the country. Best visited between November and April.

**Bandhavgarh National Park** is about 200 km northeast of Jabalpur, reachable in 4–5 hours. It has one of the highest tiger densities in India and excellent chances of sightings on a morning or afternoon jeep safari.

**Bhedaghat and the Marble Rocks** are the obvious near-Jabalpur excursion — 25 km south, easily done in a half-day. The gorge boat trip plus Dhuandhar Falls makes a complete morning.

**Bargi Dam** on the Narmada, about 30 km north, offers a large lake, boating, and bird-watching — a relaxed alternative if the main sights have been covered.
