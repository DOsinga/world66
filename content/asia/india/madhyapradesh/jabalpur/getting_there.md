---
title: "Getting There"
type: section
---

Jabalpur is well connected by train from Delhi (Jabalpur Express, about 12 hours), Mumbai (about 16 hours), and Varanasi (about 9 hours). Jabalpur Junction is the main station, centrally located and served by many express trains.

Jabalpur Airport (JLR) has domestic connections to Delhi and Mumbai, with Air India and IndiGo operating regular flights.

By road, Jabalpur sits on NH 12 (Delhi–Raipur) and is accessible from Nagpur (about 3.5 hours), Bhopal (about 4.5 hours), and Varanasi (about 8 hours).
