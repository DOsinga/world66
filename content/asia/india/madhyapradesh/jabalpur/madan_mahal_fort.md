Madan Mahal Fort perches on a granite hilltop in the northern part of Jabalpur, an 11th-century Rajput fortification associated with the Gond queen Rani Durgavati. The fort is modest in scale but dramatic in position — reached by a short climb up rocky steps, it gives panoramic views over the city and the surrounding landscape.

The structure includes a palace hall, watch towers, and a stable carved directly out of the rock. The isolation of the hilltop — accessible only on foot from one approach — made it a useful refuge and watchtower. The Archaeological Survey of India maintains the site. The views from the top at sunrise or in the late afternoon are particularly good. The fort combines well with a visit to the Rani Durgavati Museum in the same trip.
