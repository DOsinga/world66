Tilwara Ghat is a Narmada river ghat near Jabalpur with historical associations that include memorial activity connected to Mahatma Gandhi. The river setting makes it both a place of local devotion and a quieter heritage stop outside the city's busier core.

It is best visited as part of a circuit that also includes Jabalpur's better-known river and rock landscapes. Go respectfully, especially during rituals or local gatherings, and do not expect heavy tourist infrastructure.
