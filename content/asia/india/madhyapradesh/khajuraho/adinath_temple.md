Adinath Temple is one of the Jain temples in Khajuraho's eastern group, dedicated to the first Jain tirthankara. It is smaller than the headline western temples, but the sculpted figures, including graceful yakshis, make it worth adding if you are exploring beyond Khajuraho's most famous cluster.
