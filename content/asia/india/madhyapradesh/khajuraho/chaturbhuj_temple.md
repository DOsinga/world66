Chaturbhuj Temple lies in Khajuraho's southern group, away from the dense circuit around the famous western temples. Its name refers to the four-armed form of Vishnu, and the tall image inside gives the shrine a calm, vertical focus.

The temple is plainer than Khajuraho's most celebrated monuments, but that also makes it easier to look closely without jostling through a crowd. Include it if you have time beyond the western group and want to understand how the temple landscape spreads across the town.
