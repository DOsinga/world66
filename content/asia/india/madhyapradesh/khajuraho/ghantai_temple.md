Ghantai Temple is a Jain temple ruin in Khajuraho, known for carved panels showing the sixteen dreams of Mahavira's mother and a Jain goddess riding Garuda. It is less complete than the headline Khajuraho temples, but worthwhile for visitors following the Jain side of the temple group.
