title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Mandsaur's interest lies primarily in its archaeology and its religious heritage. The Pashupatinath Temple on the Shivna River is the spiritual heart of the town, drawing pilgrims year-round. Beyond the temple, the district contains remnants of Gupta-period and medieval civilisations — scattered rock inscriptions, ancient pillars, and the rock-cut Dharmarajeshwara Temple in the surrounding countryside.

The town itself is modest but relaxed. Most visitors treat it as a half-day or full-day stop between Ujjain and Udaipur, or as a base for exploring the rural Malwa plateau.
