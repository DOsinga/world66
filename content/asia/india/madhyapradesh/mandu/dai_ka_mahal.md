Dai Ka Mahal is a smaller monument south of the central groups, useful if you are following a wider circuit around Sagar Talab and the southern road. It is not an essential first stop, but it gives Mandu's quieter ruins their texture.

The pleasure here is in the setting and the accumulation of fragments. Mandu is at its best when you let the secondary monuments fill the gaps between the headline sites.
