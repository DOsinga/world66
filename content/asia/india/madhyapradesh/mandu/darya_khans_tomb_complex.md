Darya Khan's Tomb Complex is a walled group of Mandu monuments centred on the massive tomb of Darya Khan, a minister in the court of Mahmud Khalji II. The complex also includes a mosque, another tomb, the Lal Sarai, and the Somvati Kund.

It is less famous than Mandu's headline palaces, which makes it a good place to slow down and look at the architecture without the same crowds. The mix of tombs, water, and enclosed courtyards gives the site a strong sense of place.
