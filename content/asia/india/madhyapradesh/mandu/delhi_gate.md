Delhi Gate is the southernmost of Mandu's northern gates, just north of Gada Shah's Shop. The large gateway once combined several arches, guard rooms, and watchtowers, and it likely served as a royal entry route for the Delhi Sultans and the Mughals. Parts have collapsed, but the surviving mass of the gate still makes a strong stop while exploring Mandu's ruins.
