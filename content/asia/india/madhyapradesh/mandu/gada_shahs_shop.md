Gada Shah's Shop is a massive two-storey structure in Mandu whose original purpose is still uncertain. The name suggests a shop, but the building's scale and layout make it feel closer to a civic or elite compound.

The ruin is worth a look for its size, its surviving masonry, and the remains of stepwells and an octagonal structure behind it. It works best as one stop in a broader circuit of Mandu's palace and mosque ruins.
