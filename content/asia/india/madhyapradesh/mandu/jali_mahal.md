Jali Mahal is a small mausoleum on a mound south of Sagar Talao. Its name refers to the decorative stone latticework, even though the building is not a palace in the usual sense.

It is a secondary stop, but a good one if you are tracing Mandu's quieter ruins between the better-known palaces, tanks, and pavilions.
