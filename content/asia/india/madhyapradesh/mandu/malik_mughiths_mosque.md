Malik Mughith's Mosque is a 15th-century ruined mosque near Sagar Talao, paired with a caravanserai across the road. Its entrance leads into a courtyard lined with arched passages, with surviving domes and carved columns on the western side.

The mosque is a good stop on Mandu's quieter southern circuit, where the pleasure is in moving between lakes, gateways, tombs, and half-ruined monuments.
