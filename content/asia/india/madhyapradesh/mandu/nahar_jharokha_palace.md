Nahar Jharokha Palace is a ruined palace near Hindola Mahal. Its name means "tiger window", after a once-famous window detail, though the palace roof has long since collapsed.

Come for the atmosphere of Mandu's scattered ruins: broken walls, surviving openings, and a sense of how much royal architecture once filled this plateau.
