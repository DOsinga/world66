Rani Roopmati Pavilion sits on the southern edge of Mandu's plateau, where the land falls away toward the plains. The building itself is simple, with twin pavilions and long views, but the setting makes it one of the most memorable places in town.

Come late in the afternoon if you can. The light softens the ravines, the plateau cools, and the old story of Roopmati and Baz Bahadur becomes easier to understand as landscape rather than legend.
