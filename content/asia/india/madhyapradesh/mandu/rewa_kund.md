Rewa Kund is a stepped tank near Baz Bahadur Palace, traditionally linked to Rani Roopmati and the Narmada. It is a smaller, quieter stop than Mandu's famous palaces, but it helps explain how water shaped life on the plateau.

Visit it as part of the southern group. The tank, palace, and pavilion together make the landscape legible: water below, courtly buildings on the slope, and the long drop toward the plains beyond.
