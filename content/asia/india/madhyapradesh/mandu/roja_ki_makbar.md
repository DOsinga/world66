Roja ki Makbar is a small domed tomb associated with Khadija Bibi, a woman Sufi saint. The dark stone structure has an arched western entrance and several graves inside.

It is reached by a dirt road near Darya Khan's Tomb Complex and is best seen as part of a circuit of Mandu's quieter tombs and ruined monuments.
