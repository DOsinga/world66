The Tomb of Hoshang Shah is one of Mandu's key monuments and is often described as the first major marble tomb in India. Completed in the 15th century, it sits in a walled complex near the Jami Masjid.

The building is compact but finely proportioned, with a white marble dome, carved screens, and a quiet courtyard setting. It is an essential stop in Mandu's central monument group.
