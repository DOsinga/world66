Omkareshwar is a pilgrimage town on the Narmada, built around Mandhata island and one of the twelve jyotirlinga shrines of Shiva. The setting is the point: bridges, ghats, temple bells, saffron-robed pilgrims, and the river curling around an island that tradition reads as the sacred syllable Om.

The main shrine, [Omkareshwar Temple](/asia/india/madhyapradesh/omkareshwar/omkareshwar_temple), draws most visitors, but the town works best as a river circuit rather than a single queue. Cross to the south bank for [Mamleshwar Temple](/asia/india/madhyapradesh/omkareshwar/mamleshwar_temple), walk sections of the island parikrama, pause at [Siddhanath Temple](/asia/india/madhyapradesh/omkareshwar/siddhanath_temple), and spend time on the [Narmada Ghats](/asia/india/madhyapradesh/omkareshwar/narmada_ghats) when the light softens.

Omkareshwar is easy to combine with [Indore](/asia/india/madhyapradesh/indore), Maheshwar, and [Ujjain](/asia/india/madhyapradesh/ujjain) on a central India temple circuit. It is not polished, and the lanes can feel intense on festival days, but the river gives the town a gravity that is hard to fake.
