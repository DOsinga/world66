---
title: Getting There
type: section
---

Indore is the easiest gateway, roughly 75 to 80 km away by road. Buses and shared vehicles also connect Omkareshwar with Khandwa and nearby towns, though journey times vary with road work and festival traffic.

The nearest rail options are on regional lines around Khandwa and Omkareshwar Road, but most travellers still arrive by road.
