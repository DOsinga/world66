Siddhanath Temple is one of the better architectural stops on Mandhata island, with weathered stonework and a raised platform that feels older and quieter than the main bazaar-side shrines.

The temple is worth including on the island walk rather than treating Omkareshwar as a single-temple visit. Look for the carved details and the way the building sits above the river landscape.
