Phool Bagh is a small historic garden near the main temple area, easy to miss if you are focused only on the fort. It gives a quieter glimpse of courtly leisure: channels, pavilions, and shaded spaces intended to cool the Bundelkhand heat.

Treat it as a short pause between the busier monuments. It is not Orchha's headline sight, but it helps round out the town beyond palaces and temples.
