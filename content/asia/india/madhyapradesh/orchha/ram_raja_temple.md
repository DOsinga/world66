Ram Raja Temple is Orchha's living centre, busy with pilgrims and local worshippers throughout the day. The building began life as a palace, which helps explain its unusual form and the royal language of its rituals.

The atmosphere is the reason to visit. Expect crowds, bells, security, and sudden changes of pace around aarti, especially during festivals. Dress and behave as you would in an active temple rather than a museum.
