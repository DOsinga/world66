Purani Basti is the old quarter of Raipur, wedged into the southern part of the city centre. The lanes here are older and narrower than the rest of the city, and the buildings — a mix of carved wooden havelis, merchant houses, and modest temples — give a sense of what Raipur looked like before its rapid modern growth. The Dudhadhari Math and Mahamaya Temple both sit within the neighbourhood, making Purani Basti the natural area for a temple-and-heritage walk. Early morning is the best time: the light is good, the streets are relatively quiet, and the daily rituals at the temples are in full swing. Give yourself an hour to wander without a specific destination.
