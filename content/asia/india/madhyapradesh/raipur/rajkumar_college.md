Rajkumar College is one of the oldest and most architecturally impressive institutions in Raipur. The campus dates to 1882, when the British established it to educate the sons of the ruling families of central India's many princely states. The main buildings are fine examples of Victorian Gothic institutional architecture transplanted to the Indian plains — red brick, arched verandahs, and a general air of muscular imperial confidence. The college is a working school and not formally open to visitors, but the exterior and campus gates are worth a look if you're passing by. The building stands as an unusually intact piece of the colonial administrative landscape.
