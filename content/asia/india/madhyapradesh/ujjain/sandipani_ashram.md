Sandipani Ashram is one of Ujjain's important pilgrimage stops, traditionally associated with the education of Krishna, Balarama, and Sudama under Guru Sandipani. The site combines mythic geography with the everyday rhythm of a living ashram.

It is calmer than the crush around Mahakaleshwar and Ram Ghat, making it a useful counterpoint in the city. Give yourself time for the temple precinct, the sacred tank, and the stories that local guides attach to the place.
