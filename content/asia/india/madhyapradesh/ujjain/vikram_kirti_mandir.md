Vikram Kirti Mandir is a cultural complex with an archaeological museum, art gallery, auditorium, and the Scindia Oriental Research Institute. Its collections include manuscripts, paintings, inscriptions, copper plates, fossils, and objects tied to the history of Ujjain and the wider Malwa region.

It is a useful stop if you want context beyond the temple circuit, especially for Ujjain's older literary, artistic, and archaeological traditions.
