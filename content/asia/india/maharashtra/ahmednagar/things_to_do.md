title: Things to Do
type: section
---

Ahmednagar's attractions cluster around its Nizam Shahi history and its connections to the Indian independence movement. The fort is the centrepiece, but the Meher Baba pilgrimage sites draw visitors from across the world, and the old city bazaars have the texture of authentic Deccan street life. It's not a destination for the casual tourist, but for those who make the effort, it rewards genuine curiosity.
