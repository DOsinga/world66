The main beach in Alibag town runs directly in front of the Kolaba Fort and stretches for about 2 km along the seafront. It's a working beach — fishing boats pull up here and the sand is not as clean as some of the more secluded options further north or south — but it's convenient, lively, and the backdrop of the fort rising from the water is hard to beat at sunset.

Horse rides, jet skis, and food stalls are available along the beach. Swim here only when conditions are calm; the undertow can be strong. The beach is at its best in the evening when the fort is lit and locals come out to walk the shoreline.
