---
title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

The main draw of Alibag is its string of beaches and the old Maratha and Portuguese forts that punctuate the Konkan coastline. Most visitors base themselves in or near Alibag town and make day trips to the more distant beaches and the spectacular Murud-Janjira fort. The sea is swimmable between October and May; the monsoon (June to September) makes the coast rough and often dangerous.
