---
title: "Eating Out"
type: section
---

Eating in Alibaug means seafood and Konkani cooking — fish curry with rice, prawn koliwada, fried bombil, sol kadhi to drink. The roadside shacks at Nagaon and Kihim are the cheapest and freshest; central Alibaug has a handful of more polished restaurants. Most places are unpretentious and reasonably priced.
