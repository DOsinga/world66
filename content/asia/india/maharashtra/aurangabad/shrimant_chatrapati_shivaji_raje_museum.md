Shrimant Chatrapati Shivaji Raje Museum is devoted to Shivaji, the 17th-century Maratha ruler whose memory still carries enormous weight in Maharashtra. The displays include objects and reproductions linked to his life, campaigns, and courtly world.

It is a local history stop rather than a grand national museum, but it adds useful context before or after visiting Aurangabad's forts, gates, and Mughal-era monuments.
