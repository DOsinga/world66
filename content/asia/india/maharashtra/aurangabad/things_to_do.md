---
title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Aurangabad's things to do divide neatly into two categories: the ancient monuments within the city itself — Mughal gateways, a Buddhist water-mill complex, and the hilltop Buddhist caves just outside town — and the day trips to Ellora and Ajanta, which are among the most extraordinary archaeological sites on earth. Give the city itself at least half a day before or after the caves.
