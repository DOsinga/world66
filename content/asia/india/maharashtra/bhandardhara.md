Bhandardhara is a hill station and reservoir in the Sahyadri range of [Maharashtra](/asia/india/maharashtra), about four hours' drive from [Mumbai](/asia/india/maharashtra/mumbai). During the monsoon the landscape transforms — cloud-wrapped mountains, waterfalls everywhere, and the [Arthur Lake](/asia/india/maharashtra/bhandardhara/arthur_lake) reservoir brimming against the Wilson Dam. It is one of the best weekend escapes from the city.

[Kalsubai Peak](/asia/india/maharashtra/bhandardhara/kalsubai_peak), the highest point in Maharashtra at 1,646 metres, lies nearby and is a popular trek. [Ratangad Fort](/asia/india/maharashtra/bhandardhara/ratangad_fort) offers another good hike with panoramic views of the surrounding valleys, and the [Amruteshwar Temple](/asia/india/maharashtra/bhandardhara/amruteshwar_temple) in the village below is one of the finest 12th-century temples in Maharashtra. The [Umbrella Falls](/asia/india/maharashtra/bhandardhara/umbrella_waterfall) on the Pravara River are particularly dramatic from July to September.

The MTDC resort overlooks the lake and is the most convenient place to stay. Budget travellers will find more options in Igatpuri, 35 km away and well connected by rail on the Mumbai-Nashik line.
