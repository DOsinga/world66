Chiplun is the main inland town of Ratnagiri district, set on the Vashishti River where the Konkan coast begins to climb toward the Sahyadri hills. It is not a beach town, and that is the point. Chiplun is greener, quieter, and more river-focused than the coast, with mango orchards, temple villages, backwater edges, and monsoon waterfalls close by.

The town makes a useful break on the Mumbai-Goa highway or Konkan Railway, especially if you want to move between the coast and the Ghats. The essential local stops are [Parshuram Temple](/asia/india/maharashtra/chiplun/parshuram_temple), the [Vashishti River](/asia/india/maharashtra/chiplun/vashishti_river), and [Gowalkot Fort](/asia/india/maharashtra/chiplun/gowalkot_fort). With more time, the hills around Koyna and the Dervan complex make good excursions.

Chiplun is at its best in and just after the monsoon, when the valleys turn intensely green. In the dry season it becomes more of a practical base than a destination in itself, but the food, river air, and easy access to the Sahyadris still make it worth a stop.
