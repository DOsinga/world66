---
title: Getting There
type: section
---

Chiplun sits on the Mumbai-Goa highway and on the Konkan Railway. Trains connect it with Mumbai, Ratnagiri, Goa, and points farther south, while buses and private vehicles use the highway through the river valley.

Road travel is scenic but slow in the monsoon, when landslides, repairs, and heavy rain can affect the Ghats and coastal routes.
