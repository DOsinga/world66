Gowalkot Fort stands on the south bank of the Vashishti River west of Chiplun. It is a small fort rather than a grand citadel, but the river setting gives it character and explains its strategic purpose.

Go for the view and the sense of the river landscape. The fort is best paired with a drive along the Vashishti rather than treated as a long standalone visit.
