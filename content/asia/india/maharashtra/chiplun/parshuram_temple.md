Parshuram Temple stands on a hill above Chiplun, one of the town's most important pilgrimage sites. The temple is dedicated to Parashurama, the warrior-sage whose legend is deeply tied to the Konkan coast.

The setting is part of the appeal. Visit early or late for cooler weather and better views over the surrounding hills and river country.
