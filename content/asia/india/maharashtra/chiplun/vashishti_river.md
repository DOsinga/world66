The Vashishti River is Chiplun's defining landscape feature, wider and slower here than the mountain streams that feed it. It gives the town its humid, backwater feel and separates it from the beach settlements farther west.

Look for river views around Gowalkot and the bridges rather than expecting a single formal promenade. In the monsoon and post-monsoon months, the river and surrounding hills carry most of Chiplun's beauty.
