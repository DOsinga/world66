Dashavatara Cave, or Cave 15, is one of the Ellora caves where it pays to slow down. It is a two-storey excavation with a complex history, probably begun with a Buddhist plan and later completed as a Hindu shrine under Rashtrakuta patronage.

The reward is in the carving. Look for the vigorous panels of Shiva, Vishnu, and epic scenes, and notice how different the building feels from the single overwhelming drama of Kailasa nearby. Dashavatara is less famous than Cave 16, but it helps explain Ellora as a layered workshop rather than a one-monument site.
