Dhumar Lena is Cave 29 at Ellora, one of the major Hindu caves in the complex and a good reminder that Ellora is much more than the famous Kailasa temple. The shrine is cut with a broad, muscular scale, and its Shaivite reliefs have a physical force that suits the dark interior.

It is worth saving some attention for this cave rather than arriving exhausted after the marquee sights. The layout, sculptural panels, and heavy rock-cut atmosphere make it one of Ellora's most impressive secondary stops, especially for travellers interested in how Indian cave architecture moved from Buddhist monastic spaces into Hindu temple forms.
