Grishneshwar Temple is an 18th-century Shiva temple near the Ellora Caves and one of Maharashtra's Jyotirlinga sites. Its carved stonework makes it more than a quick religious stop.

The temple is close enough to the caves to combine in the same visit, but it has its own pull for pilgrims and travellers interested in Deccan temple architecture.
