The Jain group at Ellora, caves 30 to 34, is smaller than the Hindu and Buddhist sections but beautifully detailed. The shrines are carved into the same basalt cliff and show Jain tirthankaras, seated figures, pillars, and ornamental work with a precision that rewards close looking.

Do not skip them after Kailasa Temple, even if your energy is fading. Their quieter scale changes the mood of Ellora, showing how three religious traditions shared the same cliff while expressing very different ideas of space, devotion, and restraint.
