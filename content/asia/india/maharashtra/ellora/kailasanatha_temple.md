Kailasanatha Temple, usually called Kailasa Temple, is the showpiece of Ellora. Rather than being built upward from blocks, it was carved downward from a single mass of basalt, creating a freestanding temple complex, courtyards, bridges, shrines, and sculpted panels from the living cliff.

The scale is difficult to grasp until you walk around it. Elephants seem to hold up the base, scenes from Hindu epics run across the walls, and the central shrine rises like a mountain inside the excavated court. It is one of India's greatest rock-cut monuments and the essential stop at Ellora.
