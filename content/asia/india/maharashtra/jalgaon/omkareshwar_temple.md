The Omkareshwar Temple is Jalgaon's principal Shiva temple, located in the older quarter of town near the main bazaar. It draws local worshippers throughout the week, with particular activity on Monday mornings and during the major Hindu festivals, and is worth a brief visit to see the everyday religious life of the city before heading out to the caves.

The temple is active and welcoming to visitors who dress modestly and remove their shoes at the entrance. The surrounding lanes of the old town are pleasant to wander — small workshops, vegetable sellers, and shops trading in the agricultural goods that sustain this market town.
