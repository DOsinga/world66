---
title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Jalgaon's own sights are modest; the caves in the surrounding region are the real draw. The [Ajanta Caves](/asia/india/maharashtra/jalgaon/ajanta_caves) — a UNESCO World Heritage Site 60 km to the northeast — are the reason most travellers stop here at all. Within town, the [Omkareshwar Temple](/asia/india/maharashtra/jalgaon/omkareshwar_temple) in the city centre is worth a quick visit, and [Shirsoli Lake](/asia/india/maharashtra/jalgaon/shirsoli_lake), 6 km out on the Pachora road, makes a pleasant early-morning stop for birdwatchers. The [Jalgaon Bazaar](/asia/india/maharashtra/jalgaon/jalgaon_bazaar) — particularly the gold market and the agricultural trading lanes east of the railway station — reflects the town's character as a prosperous Khandesh market centre. But most visitors spend their time here planning excursions rather than exploring the town itself.
