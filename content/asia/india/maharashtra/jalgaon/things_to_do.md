---
title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Jalgaon's own sights are modest; the caves in the surrounding region are the real draw. The [Ajanta Caves](/asia/india/maharashtra/jalgaon/ajanta_caves) — a UNESCO World Heritage Site 60 km to the northeast — are the reason most travellers stop here at all. Within town, the [Omkareshwar Temple](/asia/india/maharashtra/jalgaon/omkareshwar_temple) in the city centre is worth a quick visit, and [Shirsoli Lake](/asia/india/maharashtra/jalgaon/shirsoli_lake), 6 km out on the Pachora road, makes a pleasant early-morning stop for birdwatchers. The [Jalgaon Bazaar](/asia/india/maharashtra/jalgaon/jalgaon_bazaar) — particularly the gold market and the agricultural trading lanes east of the railway station — reflects the town's character as a prosperous Khandesh market centre.

The [Gandhi Research Foundation](/asia/india/maharashtra/jalgaon/gandhi_research_institute) on the edge of town is a serious institution with a museum and library open to visitors — a surprising resource for a city of this size. If you have transport, [Muktainagar](/asia/india/maharashtra/jalgaon/muktai_temple) to the west is a significant pilgrimage town for the Warkari tradition, while [Yaval Wildlife Sanctuary](/asia/india/maharashtra/jalgaon/yaval_wildlife_sanctuary) in the hills to the north offers leopard and birdwatching in dry deciduous forest.
