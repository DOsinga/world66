Ladghar is a low-key beach village on the Konkan coast north of Dapoli, the kind of place where the rhythm is still shaped by fishing boats, coconut palms, and evening walks on the sand. The beach is sometimes nicknamed for its reddish tones at sunset, though the real appeal is its unhurried scale.

It is best visited as part of a beach-hopping loop around Dapoli, along with Murud, Karde, and Anjarle. Facilities are simple, so come for a swim, a seafood meal, or a quiet hour by the water rather than a polished resort experience.
