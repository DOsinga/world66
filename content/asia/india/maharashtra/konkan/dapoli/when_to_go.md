---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

The best time to visit Dapoli is October to February, when the monsoon has ended, the vegetation is still green from the rains, and the temperatures are comfortable — typically 20–28°C. The beaches are calm and swimmable. March and April are warmer and drier; May brings heat and humidity ahead of the monsoon.

The monsoon (June to September) transforms the Konkan coast dramatically. The Western Ghats behind Dapoli turn intensely green, waterfalls appear on every hillside, and the countryside is beautiful. However, the sea is rough and swimming is dangerous; many roads can become difficult to access after heavy rain. Some guesthouses close for the season.

Diwali and Ganesh Chaturthi bring domestic visitors in significant numbers; book accommodation well ahead during these festivals.
