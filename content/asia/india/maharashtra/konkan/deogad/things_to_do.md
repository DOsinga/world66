---
title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Deogad's draws are simple: a Maratha-era fort on a wave-battered headland, an old lighthouse with the best view on the coast, a long quiet beach south of town, and the temples scattered along the shore. None of it takes much time, but the slow Konkan pace is part of the pleasure.

If you are here for the mangoes, time your trip for late April or early May, when the Alphonso harvest is at its peak and the orchards on the surrounding plateau will sell direct.
