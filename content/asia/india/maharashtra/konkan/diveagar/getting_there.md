---
title: "Getting There"
type: section
---

Diveagar is around 170 km from Mumbai and 200 km from Pune. Most people drive.

**By car from Mumbai**, take NH66 (the Mumbai-Goa highway) south to Mangaon, then turn west on SH97 to Mhasla and on to Diveagar. The road is reasonable; allow four to five hours including a stop. The last stretch, after Mhasla, is narrow and twists through the hills — slow but pretty.

**By car from Pune**, the route is through Tamhini Ghat and Mangaon. The ghat road is spectacular in the monsoon and dangerous in heavy rain; in dry months it cuts the drive to about four hours.

**By bus**, MSRTC runs services from Mumbai (Borivali, Dadar) and Pune to Mhasla, with local connections on to Diveagar. The Mumbai-Diveagar overnight option is grim but cheap. Private operators run Volvo coaches to Mhasla on weekends.

**The Ro-Ro ferry** from Mandwa to Mumbai (and the catamaran from Gateway of India to Mandwa) lets you skip the worst of the Mumbai traffic if you are coming from the city; from Mandwa it is about three hours by car to Diveagar.

The nearest railhead is **Mangaon** on the Konkan Railway (Mumbai-Goa line), about 50 km away, with auto-rickshaws and taxis available outside the station.
