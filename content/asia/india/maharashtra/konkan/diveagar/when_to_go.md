---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

**October to February** is the obvious sweet spot. The sea is calm, the humidity has dropped, the daytime temperature sits in the high twenties, and Mumbai weekenders fill up the homestays — book ahead for any weekend, especially Diwali and Christmas-New Year. Weekdays in this stretch are blissfully quiet.

**March to May** gets hot and sticky, but the beach stays usable and the crowds thin out. Pack for 32°C and high humidity.

**June to September** is the monsoon. The sea is dangerous, swimming is out, and most homestays drop their rates by half. If you like the look of a deserted Konkan beach in the rain, with palms thrashing and the village mostly indoors, this is the time — but it is not a sunbathing trip.

The big local event is **Ganesh Chaturthi** in August or September, when the Suvarna Ganesh temple is packed and the village is loud and joyful. Worth timing if you can handle the rain.
