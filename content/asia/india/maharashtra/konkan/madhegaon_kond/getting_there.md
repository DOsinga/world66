---
title: Getting There
type: section
---

Madhegaon Kond is in the Lanja taluka of Ratnagiri district. The nearest railway station on the Konkan Railway is Lanja Road (also listed as Lanja), about 8 kilometres away. The Konkan Railway runs along the coast between Mumbai and Mangalore with spectacular engineering — tunnels through the Western Ghats and bridges over the rivers — and even if you are not stopping at Madhegaon Kond, the journey itself is worth doing.

From Lanja station, local buses and shared jeeps run into the surrounding villages. Ratnagiri city, the district headquarters, is about 40 kilometres south and is the closest town with hotels, ATMs, and onward connections. By road from Ratnagiri, take the NH166 north and branch off toward Lanja. Chiplun, another useful town with better facilities, is about 35 kilometres north.

If you are exploring the Konkan coast by car or motorcycle, the rural roads in this area are well surfaced by local standards but narrow, and the monsoon season (June to September) can make some stretches slippery. Outside of monsoon the drives are spectacular.
