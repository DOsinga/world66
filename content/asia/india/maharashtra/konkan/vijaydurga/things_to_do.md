title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Vijaydurga's highlight is its [fort](/asia/india/maharashtra/konkan/vijaydurga/vijaydurga_fort), a triple-walled sea fortification on a rocky promontory at the mouth of the Waghotan Creek. The fort is well-preserved and the views from the ramparts are excellent. The [Rameshwar Temple](/asia/india/maharashtra/konkan/vijaydurga/rameshwar_temple) at Rameshwar Wadi, a couple of kilometres along the coast, is a popular pilgrimage site where a freshwater spring meets the sea. The [Waghotan Creek estuary](/asia/india/maharashtra/konkan/vijaydurga/waghotan_creek) is one of the best spots on the Konkan coast for dolphins, especially in the early morning from October to May.
