For alcohol in Lonavala, the resort hotels are effectively the only option. **Della Resorts** and **Fariyas Resort** both have bars serving beer, spirits, and cocktails, primarily to their own guests but generally accessible to visitors eating in the hotel restaurant. **Rhythm Lonavala** follows the same pattern.

These are not destination bars — they are hotel bars serving Mumbai and Pune weekenders who want a drink in a comfortable setting. Prices are resort-level (notably higher than what you'd pay in Pune). On a monsoon Friday or Saturday evening they fill up quickly with weekend arrivals, so walk-in access can be difficult without a reservation for dinner.

If you're not staying at a resort and want alcohol, plan your evening meal at one of these properties and factor in the wait. Otherwise Lonavala is effectively dry.
