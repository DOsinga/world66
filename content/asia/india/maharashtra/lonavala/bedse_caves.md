The Bedse Caves are a quiet 1st-century BCE Buddhist rock-cut cave complex near Kamshet. The site has a chaitya hall with a stupa, a simple vihara, and a short uphill approach that keeps it much less crowded than the better-known Karla and Bhaja caves.
