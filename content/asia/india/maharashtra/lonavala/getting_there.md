---
title: "Getting There"
type: section
---

**By train** is the easiest and most enjoyable way to arrive. Lonavala is on the **Central Railway** main line — the old British-era Bombay–Pune railway that climbs the ghats via the famous Bhor Ghat section. From **Mumbai CST** (Chhatrapati Shivaji Maharaj Terminus), the journey takes around 2 hours; from **Pune**, about 1 hour. Several named expresses stop here — the **Deccan Express** and **Indrayani Express** are the traditional options — alongside commuter and intercity services that run throughout the day. The climb through the ghats in the monsoon, with waterfalls visible from the carriage windows, is worth the trip in its own right. Book on the IRCTC website or app; seats fill up fast on weekend mornings.

**By road**, Lonavala is about 90 km from Mumbai and 65 km from Pune via the **Mumbai–Pune Expressway** (NH48). The expressway journey takes around 1.5 hours from Mumbai and 1 hour from Pune in light traffic; significantly longer on Friday evenings and monsoon weekends when the expressway backs up badly. Private taxis, app-based cabs, and MSRTC buses all run this route.

**Nearest airport**: **Mumbai (BOM)**, about 100 km from Lonavala, is the main international hub for this region. Pune airport (PNQ) is closer (65 km) and serves a growing number of domestic and international routes.
