Lion's Point is one of Lonavala's busy but genuinely scenic lookouts, set on the road toward Aamby Valley. In the monsoon the surrounding cliffs turn green, seasonal waterfalls appear, and the view has the dramatic, misty quality that makes the hill stations above Mumbai and Pune so popular.

Expect crowds, snack stalls, and traffic at peak times. The best approach is to go early or on a clear weekday, enjoy the valley view, and combine it with nearby Tiger's Leap rather than treating it as a long standalone excursion.
