Shivlinga is a quiet viewpoint near the road to Tiger's Leap, looking across the deep valleys and ridges of the Sahyadris. It is less famous than Tiger's Leap or Lion's Point, which is part of the appeal.

The stop is simple: views, a few snack stalls, and a calmer place to take in the surrounding hills.
