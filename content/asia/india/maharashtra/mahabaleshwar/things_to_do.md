---
title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Mahabaleshwar's main attractions are its viewpoints, its old temple, and the surrounding countryside. The viewpoints are the reason most visitors come, and the best of them reward the early morning — hire a taxi from the main bazaar for a half-day circuit, starting with Wilson Point for sunrise and following the plateau rim to Arthur's Seat, Kate's Point, Elephant's Head Point, and Bombay Point for sunset.

[Arthur's Seat](/asia/india/maharashtra/mahabaleshwar/arthurs_seat) is the most dramatic of the viewpoints — the sheer drop below it into the Konkan plains is about 1,200 metres. [Wilson Point](/asia/india/maharashtra/mahabaleshwar/wilson_point) is the highest point of the plateau and the place to come for sunrise, arriving before dawn if you want the view to yourself. [Venna Lake](/asia/india/maharashtra/mahabaleshwar/venna_lake) is the lower-key family attraction: rowboats, horses, and an atmosphere closer to a village fair than a nature experience.

The [Old Mahabaleshwar Temple](/asia/india/maharashtra/mahabaleshwar/mahabaleshwar_temple) is a significant Shiva temple and one of the source points of the Krishna River. The [Pratapgad Fort](/asia/india/maharashtra/mahabaleshwar/pratapgad_fort) at 24 km distance is the best day trip from the hill station — a major Maratha fort with a famous story attached.

Strawberry picking at the farms around Panchgani and at Mapro Garden is popular with families. The berry season runs from November to April.
