---
title: "Getting There"
type: section
---

Malkapur is a stop on the **Central Railway's Mumbai–Nagpur main line**. Trains from Mumbai and Pune stop here daily — the Maharashtra Express, Azad Hind Express, and several Howrah-bound services all serve the route. From Mumbai the journey is around 9–10 hours; from Pune, about 7.

By **road**, Malkapur is on National Highway 6 between Akola and Jalgaon. Buses connect the town to Mumbai, Pune, [Aurangabad](/asia/india/maharashtra/aurangabad), and Nagpur, but the train is more comfortable on the long routes.

To reach **Lonar** from Malkapur is around 100 km by road, three to four hours each way. There are state-transport buses, but most travellers hire a car or taxi for the day. Plan to leave very early; an overnight at Lonar saves a long return trip.
