---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

**October to February** is the best window. Temperatures are bearable (daytime highs 25–30°C, cool nights), the post-monsoon landscape is green, and migratory birds are on the Lonar lake. December and January are the most comfortable.

**March to May** is dry and very hot — temperatures regularly above 40°C — and the lake levels drop. Travel is uncomfortable but possible if you can move in the early morning.

**June to September** brings the monsoon, which can wash out roads to Lonar and make the crater paths slippery. The landscape is at its greenest, but practically more difficult.
