Matheran — "forest on top" in Marathi — is a tiny hill station at 800 metres elevation, just 111 km from Mumbai and the nearest escape from the city's heat and noise. What makes it special is that no motorised vehicles are allowed. You arrive by toy train on a narrow-gauge line from Neral (or by taxi to the car park at the edge of town), and from there everything is on foot, by horse, or by hand-pulled cart.

The best season is October to May, when the air is cool and clear and the viewpoints offer panoramic views across the Western Ghats. [Charlotte Lake](/asia/india/maharashtra/matheran/charlotte_lake), [Panorama Point](/asia/india/maharashtra/matheran/panorama_point), and Louisa Point are the main walks — none of them strenuous. The town itself is small, with a market street, a handful of basic restaurants, and colonial-era bungalows turned into guesthouses.

The [toy train ride from Neral](/asia/india/maharashtra/matheran/matheran_hill_railway) is part of the experience — 21 km of switchbacks and cliff edges taking about two hours. It is slow, rattling, and thoroughly enjoyable. The line is a UNESCO World Heritage Site.
