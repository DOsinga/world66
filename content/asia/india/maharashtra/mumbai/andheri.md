The largest and most diverse suburb in Mumbai's western corridor, where the city truly becomes a collection of separate urban villages rather than a single centre. Andheri is where the airport sits, where a significant portion of the film production industry is based (the **Film City** studios are nearby in Goregaon), and where the western suburbs have their most fully developed commercial life in the strip running east-west through the suburb.

**Andheri West** — particularly the area around Versova and Aram Nagar — has a significant concentration of actors, directors, and production workers who work in the nearby studios, giving the neighbourhood a particular energy and a good range of restaurants and cafes. **Versova Beach**, at the western end of the suburb, is one of the cleaner beaches in Mumbai after a celebrated community clean-up project beginning in 2015. The suburb also contains the **Sanjay Gandhi National Park**, whose boundaries reach into Andheri and whose leopard population occasionally makes itself known at the urban edges.
