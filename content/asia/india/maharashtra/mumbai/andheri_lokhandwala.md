Lokhandwala Complex in Andheri West is one of Mumbai's most popular suburban shopping and dining destinations, a large residential and commercial development with a main market street lined with shops, restaurants, and entertainment options. The area is known as a hub for Bollywood industry workers — producers, directors, and actors live in the surrounding high-rises — giving it a celebrity-watching character unusual among suburban markets.

The market is best for casual dining: hundreds of restaurants, chaat stalls, and cafés serve the dense residential population. Several good bars and live music venues operate in the area. The Infinity Mall a short distance away provides covered shopping for brands and multiplexes. The area is easily reached by auto-rickshaw from Andheri Station.
