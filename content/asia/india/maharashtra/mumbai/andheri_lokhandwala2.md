The Andheri East commercial area around Marol and Chakala has developed into one of the city's most significant dining and hospitality clusters, driven by the concentration of corporate offices and technology parks in the area around the Seepz Export Processing Zone. The business park culture supports a large range of restaurants, from lunchtime thali canteens to upscale evening dining.

The area around Mathurdas Vasanji Road ("MV Road") in Andheri East has a particularly good concentration of South Indian and North Indian restaurants alongside fast-casual chain outlets serving the office population. For visitors, the area is mainly relevant as a transit zone near the airport, but several reliable dining options make it workable for an overnight stop.
