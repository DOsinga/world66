Andheri West has one of Mumbai's most diverse and affordable restaurant scenes, catering to the large middle-class and film industry population that lives in the area's residential towers and bungalows. The stretch around Versova Road, Oshiwara, and the lanes off Lokhandwala is particularly strong for casual dining at prices significantly lower than Bandra or Colaba.

The area is especially good for South Indian food — dosa and idli restaurants of real quality serve the large communities from Tamil Nadu and Karnataka who live in Andheri. Street food around Andheri Station (West) is consistently excellent, particularly the Maharashtrian vada pav and misal pav stalls. The late-night food culture in Andheri is fuelled by film production schedules and the entertainment industry.
