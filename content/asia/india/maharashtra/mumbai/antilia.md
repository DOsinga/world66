Antilia is the private residence of Mukesh Ambani, but it has become one of Mumbai's most talked-about modern buildings. The 27-storey tower rises above Altamount Road with a deliberately showy design said to draw on the lotus and the sun.

You cannot visit the interior, so this is an exterior-only stop. It is most interesting as a glimpse of contemporary Mumbai wealth and as a contrast to the older bungalows and apartment blocks of Cumballa Hill.
