Babulnath Mandir is a well-known Shiva temple on the slope above Chowpatty, close to Malabar Hill. Reaching the shrine means climbing roughly 100 steps, though an elevator is available. The temple is most atmospheric during active worship, when bells, flowers, and offerings fill the small hilltop precinct.
