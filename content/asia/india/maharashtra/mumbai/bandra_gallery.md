Bandra has gradually developed one of Mumbai's more interesting independent art and culture scenes, with several galleries, alternative performance spaces, and art bookshops operating in the neighbourhood. The congregation of media professionals, designers, and creative industry workers in Bandra West has created a local audience for art that is distinct from the more establishment character of Fort and Colaba.

The Sitara Gallery and several others show emerging Indian artists in welcoming spaces. The neighbourhood's proximity to Mumbai's film and television industry means that video art and cross-media work is particularly well-represented. Independent art events, pop-up exhibitions, and informal performances in garden spaces are a regular feature of Bandra's cultural calendar.
