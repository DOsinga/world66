Jogger's Park on the northern tip of the Bandra seafront is one of Mumbai's most popular morning exercise spaces, a small but beautifully positioned park on the rocks overlooking the sea with views south toward the Bandra Fort. The circular jogging track is consistently busy from 6am to 9am with runners, walkers, and yoga practitioners. The benches facing the sea are popular for sitting and watching the kite surfers and fishing boats that occupy the water.

The park is very small but its elevated position on the headland makes it feel more spacious than it is. The combination of sea air, exercise, and the early morning light on the water makes the park one of the most pleasant spots in Bandra. It is best visited on weekdays to avoid the weekend crowds.
