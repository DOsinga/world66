Pali Village in Bandra West is a charming enclave of converted houses and garden restaurants that has become one of Mumbai's finest casual dining destinations. The neighbourhood's narrow lanes, old Portuguese-era buildings, and mature trees give it a village atmosphere at odds with its sophisticated restaurant scene. Several of Mumbai's most critically-praised independent restaurants operate from Pali Village, many in beautiful garden settings.

The area is at its best in the evening when the garden restaurants fill up with a creative, cosmopolitan crowd. Brunch on weekends is also popular. The cooking ranges from creative Indian to Japanese, Lebanese, and European, with emphasis on ingredient quality and casual presentation. Pali Village has fewer tourists than the Colaba dining strip but matches or exceeds it in overall food quality.
