The Bandra-Worli Sea Link is Mumbai's landmark cable-stayed bridge across Mahim Bay, joining Bandra and Worli and cutting a slow coastal drive to a quick crossing. You cannot walk it, but it is a defining city view from Bandra Fort, Worli Sea Face, and the seafront promenades.
