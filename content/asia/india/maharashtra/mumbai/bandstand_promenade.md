Bandstand Promenade is a seafront walk on the western edge of Bandra, running toward Land's End and Bandra Fort. It is one of the neighbourhood's favourite places for evening strolls, sea air, and people-watching, with apartment blocks and celebrity bungalows set back from the water.

Come late in the day, when the heat eases and the promenade fills with walkers. It pairs well with Mount Mary Church, Bandra Fort, and the old East Indian lanes inland.
