Jio World Centre is Reliance Industries' massive commercial and cultural campus in BKC, a multi-building complex that includes the Jio World Convention Centre (one of India's largest), the Jio World Drive mall, the Nita Mukesh Ambani Cultural Centre (NMACC), and several hotels. The complex aims to position Mumbai as a venue for major international conventions, exhibitions, and cultural events.

The Convention Centre has hosted large-scale trade exhibitions, tech conferences, and international summits. The Cultural Centre presents a year-round programme of performing arts and visual arts. The complex is the most significant single private investment in Mumbai's cultural infrastructure since the NCPA. Located in the heart of BKC with metro connections nearby.
