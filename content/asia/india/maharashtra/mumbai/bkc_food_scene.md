The streets around BKC's central grid have developed an active street food and casual dining scene that coexists with the formal restaurants of the business district. The lanes behind the main corporate campuses have clusters of thali restaurants, Gujarati dhabas, South Indian canteens, and small café operations that serve the thousands of office workers who prefer to eat quickly and cheaply at lunch.

The evening scene near BKC's hotels and malls is more upscale, with several cocktail bars and restaurants targeting the business-travel crowd. The contrast between the formal corporate environment and the informal food ecosystem that has grown up to serve it is characteristically Bombay. Food trucks and pop-up vendors fill the outdoor spaces during lunchtime on weekdays.
