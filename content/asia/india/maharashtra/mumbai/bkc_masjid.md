Bandra East Metro Station (serving Line 3, the underground Mumbai Metro Aqua Line) functions as the main transport gateway to BKC, connecting the business district to the rest of Mumbai's metro network. The station's immediate surroundings have developed a secondary commercial cluster of hotels, restaurants, and service businesses catering to transit traffic.

The BKC area is best understood as an urban precinct built for efficient movement — wide pavements, planned roads, and a comprehensive metro connection distinguish it from the chaotic character of most Mumbai commercial zones. The US Consulate is near the metro station, making the area a regular destination for visa applicants. The walk from Bandra East Station to the main BKC commercial core takes about 15 minutes.
