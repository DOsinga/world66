The Nita Mukesh Ambani Cultural Centre (NMACC) at the Jio World Centre in BKC opened in 2023 as India's most ambitious private arts venue, a multi-disciplinary complex housing a 2,000-seat grand theatre, two smaller performance spaces, and the Art House — India's largest climate-controlled gallery space. The institution aims to present Indian classical arts alongside international contemporary culture at world-class production standards.

The NMACC has hosted major productions from national and international artists since opening, presenting everything from Kathak dance to international ballet. The gallery exhibitions have drawn significant attention for presenting Indian art in the context of global contemporary practice. Tickets are required for performances; the Art House galleries are ticketed. Located in the Jio World Centre complex alongside the Jio World Drive mall and Convention Centre.
