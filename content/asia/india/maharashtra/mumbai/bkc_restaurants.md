Bandra Kurla Complex has developed a vibrant restaurant and café scene catering to its dense population of corporate and diplomatic workers. The streets around the main BKC grid — particularly the area near the Trident Hotel and along the main boulevard — host a variety of restaurants, coffee shops, and fast-casual dining that spans cuisines from Japanese and Korean to Lebanese and pan-Indian.

The lunch hour in BKC sees long queues at popular casual restaurants, while evenings attract the after-work crowd to bars and more relaxed dining. Several top-end restaurants have set up in the area to serve expense-account business diners. The area's wide pavements and relative lack of traffic make it more walkable than most Mumbai restaurant districts.
