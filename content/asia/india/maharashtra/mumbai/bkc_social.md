BKC's evening dining and entertainment scene has expanded significantly as the district has attracted five-star hotels, luxury residences, and large convention facilities. The bars and restaurants in the Trident BKC, St. Regis, and JW Marriott (across the bay at Juhu) serve an evening crowd of business travellers, expats, and affluent South Mumbai residents who make the journey across town for the BKC dining experience.

The Olive Bar and Kitchen in BKC is one of Mumbai's better Mediterranean-influenced restaurants, popular for outdoor dining on warm evenings. Several new-generation cocktail bars have opened in the district's mixed-use buildings. The area's planners have made more room for outdoor seating and casual public space than is typical in Mumbai, giving BKC's evening scene a slightly more European character.
