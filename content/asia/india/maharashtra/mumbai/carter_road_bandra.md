Carter Road Promenade (officially Sangeet Samrat Naushad Ali Marg) is Bandra's most beloved seafront walkway, a 1.25-kilometre path along the Arabian Sea coast lined with cafés, restaurants, and open-air seating. The promenade is particularly animated in the evenings when Bandra's young professional and creative community gathers here to socialise, exercise, and watch the sun set over the sea.

The stretch is packed with restaurants and cafés that spill tables out toward the water. The food options range from casual bhel puri and vada pav stalls to chic cafés. Carter Road's combination of sea air, cafe culture, and the distinctive Bandra character — lively, cosmopolitan, and slightly bohemian — makes it one of the most enjoyable places to spend an evening in Mumbai.
