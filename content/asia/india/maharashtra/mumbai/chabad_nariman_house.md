Chabad Nariman House is a Jewish community centre in Colaba and a memorial site connected with the 2008 Mumbai attacks. Its visitor programme tells the story of Rabbi Gavriel Holtzberg, Rivka Holtzberg, and the centre's role in the Jewish life of the city.

Access is more structured than at a normal museum, and security can be tight. It is worth planning ahead if you want to join the tolerance and memorial tour rather than simply see the exterior.
