The Church of St Andrew and St Columba is Mumbai's old Scottish church, built in 1819 for the city's Presbyterian community. Its Greek Revival lines stand out among the Gothic, Indo-Saracenic, and Art Deco buildings that usually define the Fort area.

It is a small stop, but a good one if you are walking the colonial-era streets north of Colaba. The church helps show how many different communities left their mark on old Bombay.
