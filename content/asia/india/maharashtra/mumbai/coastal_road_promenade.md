The Coastal Road Promenade gives Mumbai a long new waterfront walk between Worli and Malabar Hill. It is more concrete than leafy, so do not expect the romance of Marine Drive, but the sea views and skyline angles make it worthwhile if you are already exploring this stretch of the city.
