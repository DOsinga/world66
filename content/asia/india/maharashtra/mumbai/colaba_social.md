Colaba has the most concentrated café, bar, and restaurant scene in South Mumbai, built around the Colaba Causeway and the lanes behind it. The area caters to tourists, expats, and affluent South Mumbai residents with a range of options from backpacker budget spots to upscale hotel bars. Leopold Café, one of Mumbai's most famous establishments, is here alongside newer venues that continue to update the neighbourhood's food and drink offer.

The Hotel Apollo bar on Strand Cinema Road is a classic Parsi bar that has been serving affordable drinks to sailors, merchants, and locals for decades. Indigo in Mandlik Marg is one of Mumbai's best-regarded continental restaurants. The overall character of Colaba's dining scene is more international and tourist-facing than anywhere else in Mumbai, making it a convenient starting point for visitors new to the city.
