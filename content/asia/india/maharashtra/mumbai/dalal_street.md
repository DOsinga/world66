Dalal Street is Mumbai's historic financial heart, a narrow lane closely tied to the Bombay Stock Exchange and the city's stock-market culture. Brokers gathered in this area from the 1850s, and the Native Share and Stock Brokers' Association, later the BSE, was founded in 1875.

It is a quick stop rather than a long visit, but it adds useful context while walking through Fort's heritage streets and business district.
