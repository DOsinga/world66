Dharavi is one of the largest informal settlements in Asia and one of the most misrepresented. Bollywood and Slumdog Millionaire have made it famous; the reality is more nuanced. Inside the 2.4 square kilometres live an estimated one million people and a dense economy: pottery kilns, leather tanneries, garment workshops, and an astonishing recycling operation that processes plastic, metal, and paper from across Mumbai. Visit with a responsible local guide rather than a standard tour — several social enterprises run walks that go to workshops and homes with proper community permission.
