Dharavi's food scene reflects its extraordinary demographic diversity — the settlement draws workers and residents from across India, and their food traditions coexist in a concentrated area. The main street has vendors selling everything from Tamil breakfast dosas and Uttar Pradesh-style kachoris to Hyderabadi biryani and Gujarat-style dal-dhokla, often from the same lane. The food is primarily home-cooking culture brought to the street.

For visitors arriving on guided tours, the tour operators typically arrange food stops with local vendors as part of the experience. The self-catering culture of Dharavi means that the food available at the street level reflects what residents eat at home rather than a tourist-oriented offering — making it notably more authentic than street food in tourist areas.
