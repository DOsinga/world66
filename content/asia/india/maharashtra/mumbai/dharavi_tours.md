Dharavi is one of Asia's largest informal settlements and one of Mumbai's most misunderstood neighbourhoods — a dense, self-organising community with a thriving economy of leather tanneries, pottery workshops, recycling industries, and food production. Guided tours led by Dharavi residents (offered by organisations like Reality Tours and Slum God Travel) give visitors direct exposure to the neighbourhood's commercial life and complex social fabric without voyeurism.

The tours are conducted on foot through the industrial and residential sections, with guides explaining the history, economics, and everyday life of the community. Photography is generally not permitted inside residential areas. The tours offer a genuine counterpoint to the city-of-towers narrative of modern Mumbai and emphasise the economic contribution of Dharavi's estimated 300 cottage industries. Book ahead through reputable local tour operators.
