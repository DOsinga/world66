The world's largest outdoor laundry: a vast open plot near Mahalaxmi station with 800 concrete wash pens where dhobis have washed clothes for Mumbai's hotels, hospitals, and residents since the 1890s. From the bridge above, the scale of it becomes clear — colour-sorted piles of washing, the rhythmic slapping of cloth on stone, and the clotheslines stretching across the rooftops. Early morning is best, when the light is good and the work is at its busiest. The view from Mahalaxmi Bridge is free.
