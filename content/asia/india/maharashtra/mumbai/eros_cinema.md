Eros Cinema is one of Mumbai's best-known Art Deco landmarks, standing near Churchgate with a curved facade and streamlined 1930s detailing. It opened in 1938 and forms part of the city's remarkable Art Deco streetscape around the Oval Maidan and Marine Drive.

The cinema has had periods of closure and renovation, so treat it primarily as an architectural stop rather than a guaranteed film outing. It is easy to see while walking between Churchgate, the Oval Maidan, and the Fort area's colonial buildings.
