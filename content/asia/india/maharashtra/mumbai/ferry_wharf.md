Ferry Wharf is an old harbour area in Mumbai, now used by smaller boats, barges and ferries to Uran and nearby parts of Navi Mumbai. It is best known to visitors for its wholesale fish market.

Come early if you want to see the market at work. The wharf is also one of the city's immersion points during Ganesh Chaturthi.
