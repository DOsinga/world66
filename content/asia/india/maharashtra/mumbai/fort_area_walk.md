The Fort area is the historical core of Bombay, where the original British East India Company settlement grew in the 18th and 19th centuries. A walk through the Victorian Gothic and Indo-Saracenic buildings of Fort and Churchgate forms one of Asia's finest heritage architecture trails. The ensemble of buildings around the Chhatrapati Shivaji Terminus, the High Court, the University of Mumbai, and the Old Secretariat was collectively inscribed as a UNESCO World Heritage Site in 2018.

Key buildings include the Chhatrapati Shivaji Maharaj Vastu Sangrahalaya (Prince of Wales Museum), the Rajabai Clock Tower, the Bombay High Court, the University Library, and the General Post Office. Free maps for self-guided heritage walks are available from the Kala Ghoda Arts Association nearby. The Kala Ghoda area, adjacent to the heritage district, is Mumbai's art and culture hub.
