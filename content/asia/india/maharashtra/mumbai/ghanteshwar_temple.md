Ghanteshwar Temple is a small Hanuman temple in Bandra known for its bells. Devotees traditionally offer a bell when a prayer or request is fulfilled, giving the shrine a distinctive look and sound.

It is not one of Mumbai's grand monuments, but it is a specific, living neighbourhood temple and a good short detour if you are already exploring Bandra's lanes and churches.
