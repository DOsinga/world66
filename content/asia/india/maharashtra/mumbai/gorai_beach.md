Gorai Beach is a northern Mumbai beach reached via the Borivali-Gorai ferry, popular with families and weekend groups who want a looser, less central beach day than Juhu. The sand and water are not pristine, but the setting feels more open than much of the city coast.

It is best for a breezy walk, sunset, or a low-key outing combined with the nearby villages and amusement parks. Avoid expecting a quiet wilderness beach on holidays, when it fills quickly.
