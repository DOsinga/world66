High Street Phoenix is one of Mumbai's most successful urban regeneration projects, converting a defunct cotton mill complex in Lower Parel into a sprawling retail, dining, and entertainment hub. The Phoenix Mills site mixes a large shopping mall (High Street Phoenix), a luxury hotel (St. Regis), multiple restaurant clusters (Palladium and the attached open-air area), and a cinema multiplex within the shell of the old industrial buildings.

The complex has anchored the transformation of Lower Parel from a declining industrial zone into one of Mumbai's most fashionable commercial and dining areas. The restaurants range from reliable chains to several of Mumbai's better independent kitchens. The weekend farmer's market in the outdoor plaza is popular with expats and local food enthusiasts.
