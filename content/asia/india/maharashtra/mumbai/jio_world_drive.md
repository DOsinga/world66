Jio World Drive is a large luxury and lifestyle mall in BKC opened in 2021, positioned as one of Mumbai's most upscale retail destinations. The mall houses international luxury brands, flagship stores of major Indian fashion labels, a well-curated food hall, and a multiplex cinema. Its design incorporates outdoor plazas and tree-shaded walkways that give it a more pleasant atmosphere than typical Indian malls.

The mall's food hall on the upper level is one of the best in Mumbai for a high-quality sit-down meal, with several well-regarded independent restaurant concepts alongside established chains. Adjacent to the mall, Jio World Garden provides an outdoor events venue that hosts concerts, exhibitions, and cultural programmes.
