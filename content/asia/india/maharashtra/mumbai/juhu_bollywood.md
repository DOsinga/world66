Juhu is Mumbai's most celebrity-dense neighbourhood, home to dozens of Bollywood actors, directors, and producers who have built mansions behind high walls along the beachfront roads. The area is the residential heart of the Hindi film industry — Amitabh Bachchan, Hrithik Roshan, and many other stars live on or near Juhu Tara Road. The neighbourhood has a quiet, suburban character compared to the bustle of Bandra.

Several of Mumbai's best old-school Hindi restaurants and traditional Gujarati eateries operate in Juhu, catering to the film industry crowd and local residents. The beach at Juhu provides a casual gathering spot where Bollywood's version of celebrity daily life unfolds — jogging, evening walks, and the occasional public appearance. Film studios in Andheri nearby are where most of the actual work happens.
