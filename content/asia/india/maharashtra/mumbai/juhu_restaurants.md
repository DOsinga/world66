The hotel and restaurant strip along Juhu-Tara Road and the streets behind Juhu Beach is one of Mumbai's oldest tourist dining districts, originally developed for visitors to Juhu's colonial-era beach hotels. The area remains popular for casual seafood dining, ice cream parlours, and the traditional Mumbaikar beach holiday experience. JW Marriott and Novotel on the beachfront attract international visitors alongside the older establishments.

The street food around Juhu Beach — particularly the beach bhel and pani puri vendors in the evening — is among Mumbai's most beloved. Several established Gujarati restaurants in the area serve excellent vegetarian thalis. The neighbourhood's culinary scene is less fashionable than Bandra's but more diverse and affordable.
