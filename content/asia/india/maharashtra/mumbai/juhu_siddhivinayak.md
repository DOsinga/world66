Vile Parle, the suburb immediately east of Juhu, has an excellent and underrated restaurant scene built around the Gujarati vegetarian tradition that dominates the neighbourhood's community character. The area around Vile Parle Station (West) has dozens of Gujarati thali restaurants, farsan (snack food) shops, and sweet shops that represent the best of Mumbai's Gujarati food culture at very low prices.

Mithi Chai, Gopal's, and several unnamed farsan shops make Vile Parle West worth seeking out for anyone interested in Gujarati food. The area is also good for the Irani café tradition — cafés that were established by Iranian Zoroastrian immigrants in the early 20th century and still serve brun maska (bread and butter) and chai with characteristic colonial-era furniture and faded charm.
