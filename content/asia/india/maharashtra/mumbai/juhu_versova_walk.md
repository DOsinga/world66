title: Versova to Juhu Beach Walk
type: poi
---

The shoreline walk from Versova Beach south to Juhu Beach is one of Mumbai's best informal coastal walks, covering about 5 kilometres along the Arabian Sea. The route passes through the Versova fishing village, crosses the narrow Versova creek, and follows the beach to Juhu. At low tide the walk is straightforward; at high tide sections may be impassable.

The walk offers views of the fishing boat traffic in and out of Versova, the open sea, and the Bandra-Worli Sea Link visible to the south. It is best done in the early morning when the light is good and the heat manageable. The walk ends near the JW Marriott and ISKCON temple at Juhu, where refreshments are easily found.
