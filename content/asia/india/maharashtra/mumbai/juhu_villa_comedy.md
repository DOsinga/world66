Juhu's beachfront hotel strip includes properties ranging from international five-stars (JW Marriott, Novotel) to classic Bollywood-era beach hotels that have been receiving film industry guests since the 1950s and 60s. The JW Marriott has one of the best-positioned properties in Mumbai, sitting directly on Juhu Beach with sea-facing rooms and a beach access pool. Several of its restaurants are reliable for a quality meal in the Juhu area.

The older Sun-n-Sand Hotel on the beachfront has been a fixture of Mumbai's leisure scene since 1962, frequented by three generations of Bollywood celebrities. For visitors, the hotel area provides good dining and bar options in easy walking distance of Prithvi Theatre and the ISKCON temple, making the Juhu beachfront a self-contained destination for an evening.
