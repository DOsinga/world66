The cluster of mills and warehouses between Lower Parel and Worli has become Mumbai's most dynamic restaurant and bar district, with dozens of establishments operating in converted industrial buildings. The area around Kamala Mills, Lower Parel Station, and Senapati Bapat Marg hosts rooftop bars, high-concept restaurants, and casual cafés that attract a young, affluent crowd. The neighbourhood has the post-industrial character and high ceilings of a New York meatpacking district.

Key places include Masque (consistently among India's best restaurants), Bombay Canteen, and a constantly changing roster of bars in converted mill buildings. The area is busiest Thursday through Saturday evenings. It is particularly good for Mumbai's cocktail culture, with several dedicated cocktail bars having established themselves here.
