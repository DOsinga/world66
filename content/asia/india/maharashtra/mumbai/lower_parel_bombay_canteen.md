Bombay Canteen is one of the most influential restaurants in contemporary India, a Lower Parel institution that opened in 2015 and redefined what Indian casual dining could be. The menu draws on the full spectrum of Indian regional cuisines — Goan, Parsi, Chettinad, Bengali, Malvani — reinterpreting classic dishes with careful sourcing and updated technique without losing their essential character. The bar programme is equally strong.

The restaurant occupies a converted mill building with an open kitchen and warm industrial-style interior. It is consistently busy, particularly at lunch on weekdays and at dinner on weekends — reservations are essential. The Bombay Canteen's influence on the Indian restaurant scene has been significant; it is widely credited with encouraging a new generation of Indian restaurants to take Indian food seriously on its own terms.
