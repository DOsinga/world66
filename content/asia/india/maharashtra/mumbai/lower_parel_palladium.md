Palladium is the luxury shopping arm of the High Street Phoenix complex in Lower Parel, housing India's most concentrated collection of international luxury brands alongside high-end Indian designers. Hermès, Louis Vuitton, Burberry, and other global names sit alongside Indian fashion houses in a well-maintained mall environment. The St. Regis Mumbai hotel adjoins the complex, and the area has a generally upscale atmosphere.

The attached open-air extension hosts premium casual dining and alfresco restaurants on a landscaped terrace, making it one of the more pleasant places in Mumbai to eat outside. The complex as a whole — combining luxury shopping, mid-market shopping (High Street Phoenix), restaurants, a hotel, and a cinema — makes it possible to spend an entire day here across price points.
