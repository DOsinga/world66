Maharashtra Police Headquarters occupies the former Royal Alfred Sailors' Home, an 1876 Gothic building designed by Frederick William Stevens. The Neptune figure, carved details, and restored facade make it a worthwhile architecture stop while walking between Colaba and Kala Ghoda.
