Mahim Fort stands at a strategic point on Mahim Bay, looking toward Worli and Bandra. Its origins are unclear, but the site was contested for centuries because it controlled a key approach along Mumbai's coast.

The fort is in poor condition, so this is more of a history stop than a polished monument. The surviving walls and bay views still make the location worth a look for visitors tracing Mumbai's older defensive sites.
