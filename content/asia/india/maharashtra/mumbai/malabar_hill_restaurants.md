type: poi

Malabar Hill's residential character means its restaurant scene is centred on a few neighbourhood institutions rather than a bustling dining strip. The Copper Chimney in the area is a veteran of Mumbai's Mughlai food scene. The Oberoi Hotel near Nariman Point, at the base of the hill, has long maintained one of Mumbai's finest dining rooms. The Parsi restaurants of Tardeo nearby — which specialize in the Parsi-Irani café tradition — are excellent for the distinctive cuisine that the Parsi community brought from Persia.

Britannia & Co. in the Fort area (a short auto ride away) is a Bawi-run Parsi restaurant open since 1923 that is perhaps the finest place in Mumbai to eat dhansak (lamb and lentil stew) and berry pulao. The Malabar Hill area suits a leisurely lunch followed by an afternoon walk through the parks rather than a rushed dining visit.
