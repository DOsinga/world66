The Malabar Hill ridge road offers some of Mumbai's finest views, with tree-shaded walkways running past colonial bungalows, the Hanging Gardens, Kamala Nehru Park, and the Banganga Tank. The neighbourhood is home to Maharashtra's Chief Minister's residence (Varsha) and several palatial houses, giving the area a quiet, residential character unusual for Mumbai. The views from the ridge across the Arabian Sea and Back Bay are exceptional at dusk.

The full walk from Marine Drive up to the Walkeshwar Temple and Banganga Tank takes about two hours and combines urban views with pockets of genuine quiet. The neighbourhood's narrow lanes and leafy footpaths are a reminder of what Bombay looked like before high-rises dominated. Early mornings bring serious walkers; evenings attract couples watching the sunset.
