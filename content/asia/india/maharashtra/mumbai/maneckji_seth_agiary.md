Maneckji Seth Agiary is one of Mumbai's oldest Zoroastrian fire temples, tucked into the Fort area near some of the city's grandest colonial buildings. Non-Parsis cannot enter the temple itself, but the exterior is still worth a look for its unusual Persian-Greek Revival details and the lamassus figures guarding the entrance.
