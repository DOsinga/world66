Marvé Beach is a narrow, local stretch of coast in Mumbai's northern suburbs, more useful for a breezy walk or watching small-scale fishing than for a swim. Currents and sinking sand make the water unsafe, and the nearby naval base means parts of the beach can feel restricted.
