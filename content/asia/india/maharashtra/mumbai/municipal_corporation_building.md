Mumbai's Municipal Corporation Building is a major Victorian Gothic landmark beside Chhatrapati Shivaji Terminus. Completed in 1893, its V-shaped plan, tall tower, dome, and sculptural details make it part of the same grand colonial streetscape as the station.

It remains a working civic headquarters, so the main reason to stop is the exterior. The building is easiest to appreciate as part of a Fort-area architecture walk.
