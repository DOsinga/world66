The National Museum of Indian Cinema tells the story of Indian film, from silent-era equipment and posters to the studios, stars, and technology that shaped the industry. It is a useful stop if you want more context for Mumbai's film culture than the usual Bollywood shorthand.

The museum uses a mix of artefacts, screens, and interactive displays, so it works best as a short indoor visit rather than a grand old museum. It sits near Peddar Road and can be paired with Haji Ali, Mahalaxmi, or Malabar Hill.
