Nehru Planetarium is Mumbai's main astronomy venue, next to the Nehru Science Centre in Worli. Its dome shows cover the night sky, space science, and astronomy in a format that works well for families and curious adults.

The equipment and presentation are not the reason to cross India, but in Mumbai it is a solid rainy-day stop and a useful counterpoint to the city's art, colonial architecture, and sea-facing promenades.
