Nehru Science Centre is a large science museum with hands-on exhibits and displays aimed at families and school groups. It also includes "Discovery of India", a show on Indian history named after Jawaharlal Nehru's book.

It is a useful rainy-day or family stop in central Mumbai, especially if you want a museum that is more interactive than the city's art and heritage collections.
