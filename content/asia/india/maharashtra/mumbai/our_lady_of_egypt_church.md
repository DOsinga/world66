Our Lady of Egypt Church is one of Mumbai's older Catholic churches, founded in 1606 before British rule in the city. Its long history makes it a worthwhile stop for visitors tracing the Portuguese-era Christian heritage around Bandra, Kalina, and nearby suburbs.

The church is an active parish, so visits are best kept quiet and respectful, especially during services.
