The Port Trust War Memorial recalls the World War I personnel who passed through Mumbai's docks on their way to and from service. Its position near the old port gives it context that is easy to miss among the larger colonial buildings of the Fort area.

It is a short, sober stop rather than a major museum. Pair it with a walk through Fort and Ballard Estate, where the city's port history is still visible in the street plan and architecture.
