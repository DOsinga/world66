Prabhadevi Temple is a small but historic Hindu shrine dedicated to the goddess Prabhavati. The idol is said to date from the 12th century, while the present temple building was constructed in 1715. It is a worthwhile stop for visitors interested in Mumbai's older religious sites beyond the better-known seafront temples.
