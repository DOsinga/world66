Rajgruha was the Mumbai home of Dr B. R. Ambedkar, the jurist, social reformer, and chief architect of India's constitution. The ground floor now functions as a heritage museum, while the house is also remembered for Ambedkar's enormous personal library.

This is a serious stop for travellers interested in modern Indian history and Dalit politics. It is more about context and memory than display spectacle, but the association with Ambedkar gives the house real weight.
