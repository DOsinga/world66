Sewri Flamingo Point overlooks the mudflats where large numbers of flamingos gather in winter. The birds feed here after migrating from the Rann of Kutch, and the scene can be impressive when the tide and season are right.

Bring binoculars if you have them, and check local timing before making a special trip.
