Shaare Rason Synagogue was built in 1843 for Mumbai's Bene Israel Jewish community. Its compound gate, Hebrew inscriptions, Star of David details, tiled roofs, and brass-fitted central pavilion make it one of the city's more distinctive religious heritage sites.
