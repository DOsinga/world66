Shri Siddhivinayak Temple is one of Mumbai's most important Hindu temples, dedicated to Ganesha. It draws steady crowds of worshippers, with Tuesday especially busy, and is worth a deliberate stop for visitors interested in the city's living religious life rather than only its colonial landmarks.
