Shri Swaminarayan Mandir is a tri-spired temple in Bhuleshwar, dedicated to Laxminarayan Dev, Ghanshyam Maharaj, and Radha Krishna. The temple belongs to the Swaminarayan tradition and is one of the more distinctive Hindu shrines in this crowded part of south Mumbai.

It is best visited as part of a walk through Bhuleshwar's temple streets and markets. The area is busy and dense, but the mandir's carved details and devotional energy make it stand out from the surrounding traffic and shopfronts.
