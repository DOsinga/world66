Veermata Jijabai Bhosale Udyan, long known as Victoria Gardens, is Mumbai's historic zoo and garden in Byculla. It pairs animal enclosures and shaded grounds with the adjoining Dr Bhau Daji Lad Museum, making the area a useful family stop.
