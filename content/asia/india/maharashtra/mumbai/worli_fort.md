Worli Fort is a small, weathered sea fort above Mahim Bay, built by the British in the 17th century when Mumbai was still a chain of islands. The structure is rough and partly absorbed into the surrounding village, but the ramparts and bay views make it worthwhile for travellers tracing the city's older coastal defences.
