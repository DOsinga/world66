The walk from Worli Sea Face south through Prabhadevi to Dadar is one of Mumbai's most rewarding urban itineraries, passing through layers of the city's architectural and social history. The route takes in the Art Deco housing of the Worli colony, the enormous Siddhivinayak Temple (one of Mumbai's most visited Hindu temples), and the Prabhadevi residential area — a middle-class Maharashtrian neighbourhood largely unchanged from the 1960s.

The Siddhivinayak Temple dedicated to Ganesh draws over 25,000 devotees on Tuesdays and is among Mumbai's most important religious sites. The queue system means entry is controlled but orderly. The surrounding area fills with flower vendors, prasad shops, and food stalls on festival days.
