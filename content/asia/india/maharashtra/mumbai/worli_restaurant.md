type: poi

Worli has one of Mumbai's highest concentrations of five-star hotels — the Four Seasons, Crown Plaza, and ITC Grand Central are all in the neighbourhood — and their restaurants and bars draw a well-heeled clientele. The rooftop bar at the Four Seasons offers one of the best views of the Sea Link and the Arabian Sea in the city. The hotel's San:Qi restaurant is a reliable destination for pan-Asian food.

Beyond the hotels, the peninsula's residential neighbourhoods have developed a good independent café and casual dining scene catering to the young professional families who have moved in as the area regenerated. The combination of waterfront access, good transport links, and improving amenities has made Worli one of the more desirable addresses in modern Mumbai.
