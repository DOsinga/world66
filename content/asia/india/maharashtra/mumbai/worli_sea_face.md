Worli Sea Face is a long promenade running along the western edge of the Worli peninsula, facing the Arabian Sea. The walkway offers continuous sea views and is a popular spot for evening walks, jogging, and watching the sunset behind the distant Bandra-Worli Sea Link. The adjacent Worli Koliwada — one of the city's original Koli fishing villages — gives the area a particular authenticity that contrasts with the towers and corporate campuses now surrounding it.

The Worli Fort, an 18th-century British bastion, sits at the northern end of the sea face and is accessible to walk around. The fishing boats of Worli Koliwada pulled up on the beach below the fort make for excellent photography. At the southern end, the entrance to the Bandra-Worli Sea Link creates a dramatic skyline view.
