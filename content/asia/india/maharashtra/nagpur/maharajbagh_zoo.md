Maharajbagh Zoo is Nagpur's older city-centre zoo, set in a garden landscape close to the main urban sights. It keeps animals such as leopards, lions, tigers, peacocks, and other birds and mammals.

It is a modest family stop rather than a major wildlife attraction, but its central location makes it easy to combine with the museum and other sights around Sitabuldi.
