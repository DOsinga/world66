---

Nagpur's most significant attraction is [Deekshabhoomi](/asia/india/maharashtra/nagpur/deekshabhoomi), the vast white stupa where Dr. B.R. Ambedkar led the mass conversion of hundreds of thousands of Dalits to Buddhism on 14 October 1956 — one of the defining events of modern India. The [Zero Mile Stone](/asia/india/maharashtra/nagpur/zero_mile_stone) nearby marks the surveyed centre of British India. The [Nagpur Central Museum](/asia/india/maharashtra/nagpur/nagpur_central_museum) — locally called Ajab Bangala or "Wonderful Bungalow" — holds prehistoric fossils, Nagpur Kingdom armour, and natural history collections. The [Raman Science Centre](/asia/india/maharashtra/nagpur/raman_science_centre) combines an interactive science museum with a planetarium.

[Sitabuldi Fort](/asia/india/maharashtra/nagpur/sitabuldi_fort) sits on twin hills at the city centre. It's an army installation so access is limited to Sundays and national holidays, but the views over the city repay the visit. [Ambazari Lake](/asia/india/maharashtra/nagpur/ambazari_lake) is the city's most attractive park — a reservoir surrounded by lawns, a small garden, and a children's park, busy on evenings and weekends. [Futala Lake](/asia/india/maharashtra/nagpur/futala_lake) on the western edge of the city is another popular spot, known for its illuminated fountains at night.
