Nandur Madhmeshwar Bird Sanctuary lies outside Nashik where wetland, reservoir, and agricultural landscapes meet. It is best known for migratory waterbirds, and in season the reed beds and open water can be busy with storks, ducks, waders, and raptors.

The sanctuary works well as a half-day nature trip from Nashik, especially for birders traveling with binoculars and patience. Conditions vary strongly by season and water level, so it is worth checking locally before making the journey.
