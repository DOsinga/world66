Sundarnarayan Temple stands near the Godavari River and Ram Kund, dedicated to Vishnu in his Sundarnarayan form. The 18th-century temple is valued for its stone carving, compact proportions, and its place in Nashik's dense sacred geography. One well-known detail is the alignment that lets sunlight fall onto the deities around the spring equinox.

Visit it as part of the Panchavati and riverfront circuit rather than as an isolated stop. The area can be busy with pilgrims, vendors, and traffic, but that is also what makes Nashik's temple quarter feel alive. Remove shoes where required and be respectful with photography.
