Tapovan is a pilgrimage area in Nashik associated with episodes from the Ramayana, set around temples, ghats, and wooded patches near the Godavari. It gives a more spread-out sacred landscape than the dense ritual focus around Ram Kund.

Come for a slow circuit rather than a single building. The value is in how stories, bathing places, shrines, and everyday worship overlap across a neighbourhood that still functions as part of Nashik's religious life.
