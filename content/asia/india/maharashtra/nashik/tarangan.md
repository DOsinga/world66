Tarangan is Nashik's planetarium, named for Yashwantrao Chavan and aimed at school groups, families, and anyone curious about astronomy. Shows are usually offered in several languages, including English, Hindi, and Marathi.

It is a simple indoor stop rather than a major museum, but useful with children or during the hotter part of the day.
