---
title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Panchgani is a walking and scenery town. The must-do is a circuit of **Table Land** at the top of the hill — the second-largest mountain plateau in Asia and a Bollywood favourite. The viewpoints at **Sydney Point** and **Parsi Point** give the classic hill-station vistas across the Krishna Valley, and **Devil's Kitchen**, just below Table Land's southern rim, is a small cave shrine with a Mahabharata legend attached. Closer to the highway, **Mapro Garden** and its rival **Sherbaug** sell every conceivable strawberry product alongside short walks and play areas for children. For a sense of the town's colonial past, walk the lanes around **St Peter's School** and the boarding-school quarter. Further afield, the **Rajpuri Caves** and the ruins of **Kamalgad Fort** are worthwhile short excursions.
