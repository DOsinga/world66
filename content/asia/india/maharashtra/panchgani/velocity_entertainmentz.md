Velocity Entertainmentz is a family amusement park between Panchgani and Mahabaleshwar, known for go-karting and a cluster of rides and games. It is a change of pace from viewpoints, caves, and gardens.

It is most useful for families travelling with children or groups who want an easy activity on the road between the two hill stations.
