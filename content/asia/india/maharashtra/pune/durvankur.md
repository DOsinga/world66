A pure-vegetarian thali specialist in Tilak Road, serving traditional Maharashtrian food on a banana leaf. The thali changes daily but the staples are puran poli, bhakri, the seasonal vegetable, dal-rice, kadhi, and a sweet -- usually shrikhand or a basundi.

It is unpretentious, fast, and one of the best places in Pune to try a complete Maharashtrian home-style meal in one sitting. Open for lunch and dinner. Reservations help on weekends.
