Pune's ISKCON Temple is a large Radha Krishna temple complex in the south of the city. The complex includes the main Radha Krishna temple and a Venkateswara temple, with architecture drawing on North Indian temple forms.

It is a worthwhile stop for visitors interested in contemporary Hindu temple life, devotional music, and one of Pune's larger religious complexes.
