Mahatma Phule Museum is one of Pune's older museums, founded in 1890. Its collections cover industrial products, agriculture, crafts, and everyday technology, giving a practical view of how the region worked rather than a polished art-museum experience.

Go for the old-fashioned displays and local texture. It pairs well with Pune's central heritage sights if you are interested in civic and industrial history.
