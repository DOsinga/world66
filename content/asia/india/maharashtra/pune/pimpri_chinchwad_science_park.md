Pimpri Chinchwad Science Park is a family-friendly science centre on the northern side of the Pune urban area. It combines museum exhibits with an art gallery, auditorium, amphitheatre, and planetarium.

It is most useful with children or on a rainy day, especially if you are staying around Pimpri-Chinchwad rather than central Pune.
