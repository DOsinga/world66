Sarasbaug is a well-known Pune landmark built around an old Peshwa-era lake. Its focus is the Talyatla Ganapati Temple, a Ganesha shrine that remains busy with worshippers and local visitors.

The grounds are best for a short walk and a look at everyday devotional life in the city. It is easy to combine with other central Pune sights.
