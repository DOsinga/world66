Vetal Hill is one of Pune's best-known urban hill walks. The trail climbs through scrub and forest maintained by local nature volunteers, passing the Vetal Baba Mandir on the way up.

The summit area has broad city views, though trees and the radio transmitter block some angles. Go early or late in the day for cooler weather and better light.
