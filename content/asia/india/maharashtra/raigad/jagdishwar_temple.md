A modest stone Shiva temple toward the eastern edge of the Raigad plateau, built by Shivaji during his reign. The bull Nandi faces the *lingam* in the inner sanctum, and an inscription over the doorway records that the temple was completed in 1674, the year of Shivaji's coronation. Outside, a stone platform marks the *samadhi* (cenotaph) where his ashes are said to have been interred after his death in 1680. A statue of Shivaji's dog Waghya, who legend says leapt onto the funeral pyre, stands nearby.

For Maharashtrians the spot is something close to a pilgrimage. Visitors remove their shoes; mornings are quiet, afternoons busy with school groups and devotees.
