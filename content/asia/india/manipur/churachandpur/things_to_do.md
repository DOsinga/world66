---
title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Churachandpur's appeal is specific: it is a window into the Kuki tribal cultures of southern Manipur, and the surrounding forested hills offer quiet walks and river views rarely seen by outsiders. Infrastructure is basic and the town is genuinely off the tourist trail. Those who visit do so to understand a corner of Northeast India that most travellers never reach.

The town market is the most accessible starting point — a place where handicrafts from the hill communities come together in one spot. Beyond town, guided visits to Kuki villages reveal traditional weaving, music, and community life that remains largely intact. The forested ridges and river valleys around Churachandpur are good for walking, though the roads to outlying areas can be rough.
