Memento Museum is a small cultural collection near Kangla Fort, with objects tied to ancient, medieval, and modern Meitei life. It is part museum, part information centre, and part shop, so go with modest expectations rather than expecting a polished state museum.

The value is in the local focus: it gives visitors a quick look at Meitei memory, symbols, and material culture before or after the bigger sights in central Imphal.
