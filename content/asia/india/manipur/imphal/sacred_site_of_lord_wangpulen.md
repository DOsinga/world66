The Sacred Site of Lord Wangpulen is a Meitei shrine dedicated to Wangpulen, also known as Wangbren, a water deity. The site displays sculptures, idols, mythological figures, and cultural objects connected with local religious traditions.

It is best treated as a cultural and sacred stop, with respectful behaviour and modest expectations about visitor facilities.
