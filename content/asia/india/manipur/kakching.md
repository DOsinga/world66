Kakching is the second largest town in Manipur, situated in the southeastern part of the state about 44 kilometres from [Imphal](/asia/india/manipur/imphal) and 70 kilometres from the border with Myanmar. The town was declared the cleanest city in Northeast India in 2018, and it shows — the streets are unusually well-organized for a town of this size, and the market culture is lively without being chaotic.

The [People's Museum](/asia/india/manipur/kakching/peoples_museum) is the cultural centrepiece: nearly 1,200 rare manuscripts, stone-age tools, tribal headgear, and medieval artefacts that reflect the complex heritage of this corner of Manipur. Outside the museum, the [Kakching Garden](/asia/india/manipur/kakching/kakching_garden) at Uyok Ching on the Sekmai River is the town's social heart — a riverside park with a Hindu temple and traditional Meitei shrine sitting side by side.

The [Kakching Market](/asia/india/manipur/kakching/kakching_market) is worth visiting for its Myanmar imports and local produce, especially the fermented bamboo shoot (soibum) and woven bamboo handicrafts. In the evenings, the town's bars serve Yu, a rice wine fermented locally and very similar to Japanese sake — a social tradition that is distinctly Manipuri.
