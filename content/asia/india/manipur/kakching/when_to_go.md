---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

Kakching is most pleasant between October and April, when the monsoon has ended and temperatures are comfortable. The winter months of November to February are cool and dry — good walking weather, with clear skies. The summer months from March to May are warm but manageable at Kakching's elevation.

Avoid the monsoon season from June to September if possible. The region receives heavy rainfall and travel in the hill areas can become difficult. The town itself functions normally, but roads to nearby attractions may be affected.
