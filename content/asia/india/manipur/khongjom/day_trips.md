---
title: "Day Trips"
type: section
---

**Thoubal** is the district headquarters, a few kilometres further along the main road. The town has a lively market and sits beside the Thoubal River, and is a practical base if you want to stay close to Khongjom.

**Loktak Lake**, about 40 km north of Khongjom via Imphal, is the largest freshwater lake in northeastern India and home to the unique floating phumdis — mats of organic matter that support the endangered Sangai (Eld's deer) on Keibul Lamjao National Park, the world's only floating national park. A visit here pairs well with a day trip to Khongjom.

**Imphal** itself is worth spending time in. Ima Keithel (the Mothers' Market), one of the largest all-women markets in Asia, and the Kangla Fort — the former seat of the Manipuri kingdom — provide context for the history you encounter at the Khongjom war memorial.
