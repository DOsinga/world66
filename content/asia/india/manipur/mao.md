Mao is a hill station perched at 1,757 metres on the highway between Dimapur and [Imphal](/asia/india/manipur), roughly halfway through the journey across Manipur's rugged highlands. The town sits in the Senapati district, surrounded by terraced hillsides and thick subtropical forest, and serves as the heartland of the Mao Naga people. The [Mao Gate](/asia/india/manipur/mao/mao_gate), a few kilometres north, marks the border between Manipur and Nagaland and the traditional boundary of Mao Naga territory.

The main reasons to stop here are the encounter with Mao Naga culture — the [surrounding villages](/asia/india/manipur/mao/mao_naga_villages) offer a glimpse of traditional life that few visitors to northeast India ever see — and the [forest walks](/asia/india/manipur/mao/forest_walks) into the subtropical montane forest that covers the surrounding hillsides. The area sits in the Indo-Burma biodiversity hotspot, and the birdwatching is exceptional. The climate is pleasant year-round thanks to the elevation, making it a welcome break from the heat of the lowlands.
