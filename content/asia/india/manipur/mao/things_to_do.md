title: Things to Do
type: section
---

Mao's appeal is less about specific sights and more about the character of the place: a Naga hill town with a distinct culture, dramatic highland scenery, and a position on a key trans-Manipur highway that makes it accessible without being overrun. The activities here are largely outdoor — walks into the surrounding forest, village visits, and the experience of a Naga market day. This is northeast India at its most unhurried.
