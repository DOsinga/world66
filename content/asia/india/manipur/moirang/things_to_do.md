---
title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Moirang is a stop on a Loktak Lake circuit rather than a sit-down destination. The two-hour minimum is the INA Memorial in town, the lake shore at the boat jetty, and a short visit to the Ibudhou Thangjing temple. With a half-day you can add Keibul Lamjao National Park — the world's only floating national park, home of the endangered sangai deer — and a boat trip onto Loktak to see the phumdis, the great mats of vegetation that drift on the lake's surface.
