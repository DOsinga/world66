---
title: Getting There
type: section
---

Moreh is about 110 km from Imphal by road along NH102, via Pallel and Tengnoupal. The road is scenic but sensitive, passing through hill country where security conditions can affect travel.

Use current local advice before leaving Imphal, especially during periods of unrest or heavy rain. Shared vehicles and buses may run when conditions allow, but a private vehicle arranged through reliable local contacts is more practical.
