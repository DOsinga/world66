The Indo-Myanmar Friendship Bridge area is the symbolic edge of Moreh, linking the Indian side with Tamu across the border zone. It is a place to understand geography, not somewhere to wander casually.

Access depends on official rules and security conditions. If you visit, follow local instructions carefully and avoid photographing security personnel or restricted infrastructure.
