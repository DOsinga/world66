Moreh Bazaar is the best place to understand the town without crossing any frontier. Shops and stalls reflect the border economy, with Indian and Myanmar influences mixing in goods, food, and languages.

Go with modest expectations and current local advice. The appeal is observation rather than shopping for souvenirs: who is moving, what is being sold, and how the town's border identity plays out at street level.
