The Moreh Integrated Check Post is the formal border infrastructure south of town, built for customs, immigration, and trade. For travellers, it is less a sightseeing attraction than a visible sign of Moreh's strategic role.

Do not treat it like a monument. Access and photography may be restricted, and the practical value of visiting depends entirely on current border conditions.
