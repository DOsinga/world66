From parts of Moreh you can look toward Tamu and the Kabaw Valley, the Myanmar side that gives the town its larger meaning. The view is not dramatic in a mountain-summit sense, but it is politically and geographically charged.

This is enough for most travellers. Unless the border is officially open and safe, Moreh should be understood from the Indian side, with Tamu kept as context rather than an onward plan.
