Tengnoupal is a small hill town in the Manipur highlands, about 70 km south of [Imphal](/asia/india/manipur/imphal) on the NH102 — the road that follows the old Imphal-Burma route toward Myanmar. It sits near the highest point on this stretch of road, around 1,450 metres above sea level, with wide views over the Manipur valley stretching north toward the capital.

The town is the headquarters of Tengnoupal district and is predominantly inhabited by Kuki-Zo tribal communities. The surrounding hills are densely forested, and the area serves as a staging point for travellers heading to [Moreh](/asia/india/manipur/moreh), the Indian border town about 12 km further south where NH102 crosses into Myanmar.

For anyone travelling the Imphal-Moreh highway, Tengnoupal is where the climbing stops and the views open up. The road itself — its history, its landscape, and what it connects — is reason enough to pause here.
