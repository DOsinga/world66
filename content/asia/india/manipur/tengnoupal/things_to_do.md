---

Tengnoupal's appeal is almost entirely the landscape: the high-altitude light, the forested ridges, and the long drop of the Manipur valley visible from the road. The town itself is small, but the drive up from Imphal — and further down to [Moreh](/asia/india/manipur/moreh) — is one of Manipur's most scenic routes.
