Nohsngithiang Falls — also called the Seven Sisters Waterfall — is a spectacular seven-segmented waterfall near Mawsmai village, about six kilometres from Cherrapunji. The falls drop 315 metres over a broad cliff face into a gorge below, with seven separate streams visible from the viewpoint above when the monsoon is running at full force.

The viewpoint is accessible by road and a short walk. On clear days — more likely in October to March — the views extend south over the plains of Bangladesh. A secondary viewpoint slightly further along the cliff gives a different angle on the falls. The area around the viewpoint has a few small stalls selling snacks and the ubiquitous local pineapple.
