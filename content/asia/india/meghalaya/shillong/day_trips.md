Shillong is the gateway to Meghalaya, and most travellers spend a night or two here before pushing south to the **living root bridges** of [Cherrapunji](/asia/india/meghalaya/cherrapunji) and the village of **Mawlynnong** (often advertised as Asia's cleanest village). Both are doable as long day trips from Shillong but reward an overnight.

**Dawki**, on the Bangladesh border, has the legendarily transparent Umngot river — at the right time of year, the boats appear to be floating on air. **Mawsynram** competes with Cherrapunji for wettest place on earth. **Mawphlang Sacred Grove**, a Khasi sacred forest, is closer in and a good half-day. North, [Guwahati](/asia/india/assam/guwahati) is around three hours by shared taxi or bus.
