Aizawl is a hill capital in the most literal sense: the city runs along a steep ridge, with houses clinging to slopes and views opening suddenly between lanes. It is the practical base for [Mizoram](/asia/india/mizoram), but it also gives the best first taste of Mizo life: church bells, tidy markets, traffic squeezing around impossible bends, and green hills in every direction.

The city is not built for hurried sightseeing. Walk the centre, look down from [Durtlang Hills](/asia/india/mizoram/aizawl/durtlang_hills), and spend time in [Bara Bazar](/asia/india/mizoram/aizawl/bara_bazar), where women vendors sell vegetables, smoked meat, bamboo shoots, and woven shawls. [Mizoram State Museum](/asia/india/mizoram/aizawl/mizoram_state_museum) is small but useful for understanding the state's clans, textiles, weapons, and musical instruments.

On the outskirts, [Solomon's Temple](/asia/india/mizoram/aizawl/solomons_temple) shows the strength of Christianity in modern Mizoram, while [Reiek](/asia/india/mizoram/aizawl/reiek) and [Hmuifang](/asia/india/mizoram/aizawl/hmuifang) make easy escapes into the ridges beyond town. Come with patience: roads are slow, weather changes quickly, and the reward is atmosphere more than a checklist.
