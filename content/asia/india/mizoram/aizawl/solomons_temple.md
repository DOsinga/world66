Solomon's Temple is a large white church on the western outskirts of Aizawl, built by the Kohhran Thianghlim church. Its scale is striking in a city where most buildings are squeezed tightly onto slopes, and the open grounds make it feel unusually spacious.

Visitors come for the architecture, the quiet setting, and the view back toward the hills. Dress respectfully and avoid treating services as a spectacle; Christianity is central to public life in Mizoram, not a curiosity staged for outsiders.
