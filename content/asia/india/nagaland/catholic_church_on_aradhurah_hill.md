The Catholic Church on Aradhurah Hill is one of Kohima's most visible landmarks and among the largest churches in India's predominantly Christian northeast. Its hilltop setting gives it a strong presence above the town.

The main reason to come, beyond the church itself, is the view. From the grounds you get a broad look over Kohima and the surrounding hills.
