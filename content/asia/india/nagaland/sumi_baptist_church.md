Sumi Baptist Church is a huge hill-country church in Zunheboto, built over a decade and able to hold thousands of worshippers. Its scale says a lot about the strength of Baptist Christianity in Nagaland, and the building is one of the most striking modern landmarks in the Sumi Naga heartland.
