Aska is a town in Ganjam District, Odisha, about 40 km from [Berhampur](/asia/india/orissa/berhampur). It is known locally as the "Sugar City," a nickname earned by what is claimed to be one of the oldest sugar mills in Asia — the [Aska Sugar Mill](/asia/india/orissa/aska/aska_sugar_mill) was established here during the British colonial period in 1824.

The [Khambeswari temple](/asia/india/orissa/aska/khambeswari_temple) is the main religious site in town. The most animated time to visit is during the Dandanacha festival (March–April), dedicated to the goddess Maa Kali, which culminates on the final day — the Meru — in a procession that draws large crowds from the surrounding area. The [Lakhari Valley Wildlife Sanctuary](/asia/india/orissa/aska/lakhari_valley_wildlife_sanctuary), about 5km from town, offers forest wildlife and a half-day escape into the Eastern Ghats foothills.
