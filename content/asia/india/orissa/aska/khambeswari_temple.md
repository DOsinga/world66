The Khambeswari temple is the central religious landmark of Aska and the focus of the town's most important annual festival. The temple follows the Kalinga architectural style typical of Odisha — a distinctive curvilinear tower (deul) rising above the inner sanctum, with an assembly hall in front.

The most animated time to visit is during the Dandanacha festival in March or April, when the whole town fills with pilgrims, street vendors, and music. Outside festival season the temple is a quiet, functional place of local worship rather than a major pilgrimage destination.
