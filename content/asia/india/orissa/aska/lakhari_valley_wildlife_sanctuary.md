Lakhari Valley Wildlife Sanctuary lies about 5km from Aska and covers around 185 sq km of hilly deciduous forest in the Eastern Ghats foothills. It is a low-key destination with few visitor facilities — expect basic forest tracks rather than developed wildlife lodges — but for those willing to explore independently, it offers a genuine glimpse of the forest ecosystem of inland Odisha.

Wildlife spotting requires patience and an early start. The best time to visit is between November and March, when the forest is drier and animals congregate more predictably around water sources. Local guides can be arranged in Aska or [Berhampur](/asia/india/orissa/berhampur).
