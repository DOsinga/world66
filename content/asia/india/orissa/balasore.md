Balasore is a coastal district in the far north of Odisha (formerly Orissa), bordered by West Bengal to the north and the Bay of Bengal to the east. The district town is a busy trading centre in a fertile agricultural area, traditionally known as the granary of Odisha for its rice production.

The coastline includes [Chandipur beach](/asia/india/orissa/balasore/chandipur_beach), one of the stranger beaches in India — the sea retreats up to 5 km at low tide, exposing a vast expanse of red sand, then surges back. It is also the site of India's Integrated Test Range missile facility, which means access to the shoreline can occasionally be restricted. [Remuna](/asia/india/orissa/balasore/remuna) and its Khirachora Gopinath temple is a major Vaishnava pilgrimage site about 10 kilometres from town. [Panchalingeswar](/asia/india/orissa/balasore/panchalingeswar) — where five Shiva lingas sit submerged in a forest stream — is worth the 60-kilometre drive into the Nilagiri hills.
