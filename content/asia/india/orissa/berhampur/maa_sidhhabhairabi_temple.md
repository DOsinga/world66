Maa Sidhhabhairabi Temple is a busy Hindu shrine north of Berhampur, drawing devotees from southern Odisha and northern Andhra Pradesh. It is most active on Tuesdays and Sankranti days, when the temple fills with people coming to make offerings and ask for blessings.

The temple is worth pairing with other Berhampur shrines if you are interested in local religious life rather than monumental architecture. Go early if you want the atmosphere without the heaviest crowds.
