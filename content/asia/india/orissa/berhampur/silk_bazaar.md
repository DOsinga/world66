Berhampur is the historic centre of Odisha's silk-weaving industry, and the **Berhampuri Patta** saree — a handloom silk with a distinctive temple-style border (kumbha) — is its signature product. The trade is concentrated in the city's main bazaars: **Annapurna Market** for retail sarees, **Giri Market** and **Chena Bazaar** for wholesale and weavers' shops, and **Urban Bank Road** for upmarket silk shops.

The right approach is unhurried. Sit in a shop, accept the offered tea, and let the shopkeeper unfold half a dozen sarees on the counter. Pure handloom silk is rare and expensive; mill silks and silk-cotton blends are far more common and still beautiful. The 2009 Geographical Indication protects the genuine handloom Berhampuri Patta — ask for it by name if that is what you want.
