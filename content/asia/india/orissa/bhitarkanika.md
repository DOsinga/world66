Bhitarkanika National Park protects one of the great mangrove landscapes of eastern India. Rivers, creeks, mudflats, and forested islands spread out behind the Bay of Bengal, creating a quieter, less famous cousin to the Sundarbans. The stars here are saltwater crocodiles, waterbirds, monitor lizards, and the strange stillness of the mangrove channels.

This is not a place for casual wandering. You travel by boat from entry points such as Gupti or Khola, with forest department rules shaping where you can go. [Dangmal](/asia/india/orissa/bhitarkanika/dangmal) is the usual visitor base inside the park area, while [Gahirmatha Beach](/asia/india/orissa/bhitarkanika/gahirmatha_beach) is known for olive ridley turtle nesting but is tightly controlled during sensitive seasons.

Bhitarkanika suits travellers who like wildlife, wetlands, and a bit of logistical friction. Combine it with [Paradeep](/asia/india/orissa/paradeep), Cuttack, or Bhubaneswar rather than expecting a quick roadside sight.
