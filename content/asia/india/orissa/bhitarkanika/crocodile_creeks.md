  Bhitarkanika has a reputation for very large saltwater crocodiles; some local claims have become legendary, though not all are scientifically verified. The sensible thrill is simpler: this is one of the best places in India to see the species in wild mangrove habitat.
---

Saltwater crocodiles are Bhitarkanika's headline animal. On a good boat trip you may see them lying on exposed mud, sliding off banks, or waiting almost invisibly at creek edges.

Do not lean out for photographs or treat the water like a tame safari pond. These are powerful wild animals, and the park's rules exist for good reason.
