Gahirmatha Beach separates the Bhitarkanika mangroves from the Bay of Bengal and is famous for olive ridley turtles. It is a wild, sensitive coast rather than a swimming beach, and access can be restricted when nesting or hatching is underway.

Ask locally before planning a visit. If entry is closed, respect it; the beach's importance lies precisely in giving the turtles space.
