Aisaneswara Temple is a compact 13th-century Shiva temple near Bhubaneswar's older sacred core. It follows the classic Odia pairing of jagamohana and vimana, and rewards visitors who are already exploring the temple cluster around Lingaraja with another close look at the city's medieval stonework.
