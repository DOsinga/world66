Brahmeswara Temple is one of Bhubaneswar's elegant early medieval temples, built in the mature Kalinga style with a curving tower and richly carved walls. It is smaller than Lingaraja, but the sculptural detail rewards close looking.

The temple is especially useful for travellers trying to understand why Bhubaneswar is called a city of temples. Its quieter compound lets you study the architecture without the pressure and crowds of the city's most active shrines.
