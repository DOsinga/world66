Chandaka Elephant Sanctuary protects forest and reservoirs on the edge of Bhubaneswar. Watch towers such as Kochilaberana, Pitagadia, and Charichhak are used for spotting birds and wildlife, while Deras and Jhumka dams give the reserve some of its most scenic views.

It works best as a nature break from the city's temples and museums. Go with time for slow wildlife watching rather than expecting a quick roadside stop.
