The Chausathi Yogini Temple at Hirapur is one of the most unusual temple visits around Bhubaneswar. Instead of the towering curvilinear form associated with Odisha's famous Shiva temples, this ninth-century shrine is low, circular, and open to the sky, with niches for the Yogini figures around the inner wall.

Its small scale is part of its power. The temple feels intimate and slightly mysterious, especially compared with the busy Lingaraja and Rajarani temple precincts in the city. It is best treated as a short trip from Bhubaneswar for travelers interested in tantric traditions, sculpture, or less crowded sacred sites.
