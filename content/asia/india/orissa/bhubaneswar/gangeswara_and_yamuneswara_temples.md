Gangeswara and Yamuneswara Temples are twin medieval temples in Bhubaneswar dedicated to Ganga and Yamuna. They likely date from the 13th or 14th century, during the Ganga Dynasty, and are built in grey sandstone.

The temples stand near the Ganga Yamuna tank, tied in local legend to Parvati and Shiva. They are a compact stop for visitors tracing the smaller shrines around Bhubaneswar's old temple quarter.
