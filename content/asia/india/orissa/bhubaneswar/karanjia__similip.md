Karanjia is a small town in the Mayurbhanj district of Odisha, serving as the main gateway to [Similipal National Park](/asia/india/orissa/bhubaneswar/karanjia__similip/similipal_national_park) — one of India's largest tiger reserves, spread over 2,750 square kilometres of sal forest, grasslands, and hills.

[Similipal](/asia/india/orissa/bhubaneswar/karanjia__similip/similipal_national_park) is home to Royal Bengal tigers, Asian elephants, gaur, and a wide variety of birdlife, including the rare melanistic (near-black) Bengal tiger found almost nowhere else on earth. The park has two spectacular waterfalls: [Joranda](/asia/india/orissa/bhubaneswar/karanjia__similip/joranda_falls) (150 metres) and [Barehipani](/asia/india/orissa/bhubaneswar/karanjia__similip/barehipani_falls) (399 metres, one of the tallest in India). Visitors can stay in government forest rest houses inside the park, which should be booked in advance through the Similipal Tiger Reserve office in Baripada.

Karanjia is about 230 km from Bhubaneswar and 120 km from Jamshedpur. The town has basic hotels and transport connections. A useful half-day excursion from Karanjia is the medieval temple complex at [Khiching](/asia/india/orissa/bhubaneswar/karanjia__similip/khiching_temple), 25 kilometres away, with its carved goddess and small site museum.
