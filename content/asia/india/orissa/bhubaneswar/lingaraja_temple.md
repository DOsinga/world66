Lingaraja Temple is the central monument of old Bhubaneswar and one of the most important Hindu temples in Odisha. Dedicated to Shiva, it anchors the city's identity as a temple town and shows the mature Kalinga style in its soaring tower, sculpted walls, subsidiary shrines, and enclosed sacred precinct.

Non-Hindus are generally not allowed inside the active temple complex, but the exterior and surrounding old-city lanes are still essential to understanding Bhubaneswar. Visit respectfully, expect crowds during festivals, and combine it with nearby temples and the Bindu Sagar tank for the strongest sense of the historic city.
