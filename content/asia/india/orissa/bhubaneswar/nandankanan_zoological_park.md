Nandankanan Zoological Park is one of the major family attractions around Bhubaneswar, combining a zoo, botanical setting, lake, and safari-style experiences. It is especially associated with white tigers, though the broader grounds are part of the appeal.

The park needs time and a reasonably early start, particularly in hot weather. It is a good counterpoint to Bhubaneswar's temples, giving families and wildlife-minded travellers a full outdoor day.
