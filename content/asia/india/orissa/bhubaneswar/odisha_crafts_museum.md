The Odisha Crafts Museum, also known as Kala Bhoomi, is one of Bhubaneswar's strongest newer cultural attractions. Its galleries and open-air spaces present the state's craft traditions with unusual care, from textiles and tribal art to stone, metal, wood, and painting.

It is a useful counterweight to the city's temple circuit. After seeing Bhubaneswar's ancient architecture, the museum shows how Odisha's visual culture continues through living craft traditions, and the campus itself is pleasant enough to slow down in rather than rush through.
