Odisha State Museum is the main place in Bhubaneswar to put the region's temples and craft traditions into context. Its collections range from stone sculpture and bronzes to palm-leaf manuscripts, coins, weapons, and natural history displays.

The galleries can feel old-fashioned, but the material is rich. Visit before or after the temple circuit: the museum helps explain the iconography and historical depth behind the city's famous Kalinga architecture.
