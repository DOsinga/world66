Rameshwar Temple is an early Bhubaneswar shrine dedicated to Shiva and traditionally linked with Rama and Sita. Built in the 9th century, it shows a transitional phase of Kalinga temple architecture, with a tall vimana, a separate mandap, sculpted panels, and a stepped temple pond within the compound.
