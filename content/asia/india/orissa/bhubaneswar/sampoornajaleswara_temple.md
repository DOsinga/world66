Sampoornajaleswara Temple is a 9th- or 10th-century shrine west of the Lingaraja West Canal in Bhubaneswar's old temple quarter. It is smaller than the headline temples, but the surviving wall ornament makes it a worthwhile stop on a slow walk between the nearby historic shrines.
