Subarnajaleswara Temple is a small 9th- to 10th-century Shiva temple in Bhubaneswar's old temple district. Its name is linked to the goldsmiths who once lived in the area.

The surviving shrine is plain compared with the city's larger temples, but it adds another early medieval stop for visitors walking between the better-known shrines around the Lingaraja area.
