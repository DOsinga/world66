The Suka and Sari temples are smaller stops in Bhubaneswar's old temple district, easy to miss if you rush only between the headline shrines. Their stonework, proportions, and neighbourhood setting make them useful examples of the city's dense Kalinga temple landscape.

Visit them as part of a walking loop around Muktesvara, Lingaraja, and the surrounding tanks. The pleasure is cumulative: each smaller shrine adds another variation in carving, tower form, and ritual use.
