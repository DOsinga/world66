Swarnajaleswara Temple is a small 7th-century shrine in Bhubaneswar's old temple district, north of Kotiteertheswara Temple. It is associated with the Sailodbhava period and preserves early temple forms, even though later development has crowded closely around it.

The setting makes the full building difficult to photograph, but it is worth including on a slow walk through the cluster of historic temples around the old city.
