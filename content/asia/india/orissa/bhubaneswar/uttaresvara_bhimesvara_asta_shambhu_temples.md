The Uttaresvara, Bhimesvara and Asta Shambhu Temples form a small temple cluster on the northern edge of Bindu Sagar. The complex includes several shrines and the Godavari Kund tank, adding another layer to Bhubaneswar's dense Old Town temple landscape.

Some parts have been heavily restored, so this is not the most atmospheric temple stop in the city. It is still useful on a slow walk around Bindu Sagar, especially if you are tracing the smaller shrines between the better-known monuments.
