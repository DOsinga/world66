Chatrapur (also spelled Chhatrapur) is the headquarters of Ganjam District in southern [Odisha](/asia/india/orissa), a small administrative town on the Chennai-Howrah main line. It sits on NH-5 between Berhampur and Bhubaneswar, making it easy to reach by both rail and road. The town itself is quiet and unassuming, but it serves as a base for exploring the southern Odisha coast and the hilly hinterland of Ganjam.

The [Thakurani Yatra festival](/asia/india/orissa/chatrapurganjam/thakurani_yatra), celebrated once every two years, is the big event here — a week-long procession honouring the goddess Thakurani that draws enormous crowds from across the district. The festival features decorated chariots, traditional music, and a palpable sense of community devotion. If your timing coincides, it is worth stopping for.

The Ganjam coast nearby has long stretches of relatively untouched beach, and [Tampara Lake](/asia/india/orissa/chatrapurganjam/tampara_lake) at the mouth of the Rushikulya River is good for birdwatching. The [Taptapani hot springs](/asia/india/orissa/chatrapurganjam/taptapani_hot_springs), about 50 km inland in the Eastern Ghats, are a popular excursion set in cool forest. The Lakhari Valley with its sal forests and tribal villages gives a sense of rural Odisha that few travellers see. Chatrapur has basic facilities but nothing elaborate — Berhampur, about 20 km south, is the nearest city of any size for better dining and services.
