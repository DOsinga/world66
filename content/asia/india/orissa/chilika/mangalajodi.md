Mangalajodi is one of Chilika Lake's best birding bases, a wetland village where winter brings large numbers of migratory birds into the reeds and open water. Small boats move quietly through the channels, which makes the experience more intimate than scanning birds from a distant road.

The best time is the cooler migration season, especially early morning when the light is soft and the wetlands are active. Go with a local boatman or naturalist if you can; the value here is not just seeing birds, but understanding how a former hunting area became a conservation-minded tourism stop.
