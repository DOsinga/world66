The Cuttack Chandi Temple, dedicated to the goddess Chandi, is the most important religious site in the city. The current temple is a relatively recent rebuilding on an old foundation, plain on the outside but always busy with worshippers — Cuttack's residents will tell you the goddess is the real owner of the city, and her blessing is sought for everything from new businesses to weddings.

Non-Hindus are usually allowed in to the outer courtyard. The temple is at its most atmospheric at dawn and at the evening aarti.
