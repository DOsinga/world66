Odisha State Maritime Museum occupies the former River Boat Construction and Repair Workshop, a riverside industrial complex dating to 1866. The setting gives the museum a stronger sense of place than a purpose-built gallery would.

Inside, galleries cover Odisha's maritime history with boat models, ship displays, a boat shed, an aquarium, and 3D shows.
