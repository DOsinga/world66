Dhenkanal is a district town in central [Odisha](/asia/india/orissa), once the seat of a princely state and now a quiet administrative centre surrounded by forested hills. The town itself is modest — the old [Dhenkanal Palace](/asia/india/orissa/dhenkanal/dhenkanal_palace) and [Hanuman Hill](/asia/india/orissa/dhenkanal/hanuman_hill) cover the main sights — but the district holds some of the state's most rewarding detours for travellers willing to get off the main highways.

The [Kapilash Temple](/asia/india/orissa/dhenkanal/kapilash_temple), dedicated to Shiva, sits atop a hill about 25 km from town, reached by a steep climb through dense sal and teak forest. The temple is an important pilgrimage site and the surrounding Kapilash Wildlife Sanctuary is home to elephants, leopards, and a rich variety of birdlife. The [Satkosia Gorge](/asia/india/orissa/dhenkanal/satkosia_gorge) on the Mahanadi River, on the south-western edge of the district, is one of Odisha's most dramatic landscapes — the river cuts through a narrow canyon flanked by steep, forested hillsides, and boat rides through the gorge are possible during the dry season. Further north, [Joranda](/asia/india/orissa/dhenkanal/joranda_monastery) is the spiritual headquarters of the unusual nineteenth-century Mahima Dharma sect.

Dhenkanal district is also known for its dhokra metal castings, a traditional tribal craft made using the lost-wax method. The artisan village of [Sadeibareni](/asia/india/orissa/dhenkanal/sadeibareni_dhokra) produces distinctive figurines and ornaments that make for excellent souvenirs. The town is on the rail line between [Bhubaneswar](/asia/india/orissa/bhubaneswar) and Sambalpur, and regular buses connect it to the state capital about three hours to the southeast.
