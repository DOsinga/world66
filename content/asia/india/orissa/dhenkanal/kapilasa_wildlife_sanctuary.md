Kapilasa Wildlife Sanctuary protects deciduous forest in the hills near Dhenkanal. Elephants, deer, leopards, hyenas, and other wildlife live in the sanctuary, and boating is available in parts of the area.

It is a nature-focused side trip rather than an urban sight. Check local access conditions before going, especially outside the main travel season.
