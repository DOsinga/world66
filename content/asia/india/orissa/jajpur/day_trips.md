Jajpur sits in the middle of one of India's richest Buddhist archaeological zones — the "Diamond Triangle" of [Ratnagiri, Lalitgiri and Udayagiri](/asia/india/orissa/ratnagirilalitgiri), all within an hour's drive south of town. The three excavated mahaviharas preserve stupas, monastery courtyards, carved gateways and hilltop sculptures from the centuries when this was a stronghold of Tantric Buddhism in eastern India.

[Udayagiri](/asia/india/orissa/udayagiri) is the largest of the three, with two separate monastery complexes a few kilometres apart and a famous stepped well; Ratnagiri is the most extensively excavated and has an excellent little site museum. You can comfortably cover two of the three in a single day with a hired car — Lalitgiri sits closest to the highway and is the easiest to add as a third stop.
