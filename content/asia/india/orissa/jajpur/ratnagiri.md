About 25 km south of Jajpur, the ruined mahavihara of Ratnagiri sits on a low hill above paddy fields. It was the most important Buddhist monastery in Odisha and a major centre of Vajrayana (Tantric) Buddhism from roughly the 5th to the 13th century. The excavated complex includes a stupa, two large monasteries with cells and meditation halls, and a magnificent gateway carved with images of Tara, Avalokiteshvara and Heruka deities.

The site museum on the way up the hill is small but worth fifteen minutes: bronzes from the monks' shrines, carved stelae, and a few terracottas that hint at what daily life looked like. Ratnagiri pairs naturally with Udayagiri on the same day; Lalitgiri is closer to the highway and easy to add as a third stop.
