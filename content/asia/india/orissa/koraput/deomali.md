Deomali is the highest peak in Odisha and the strongest landscape trip from Koraput. The road climbs through Eastern Ghats country to a high, open ridge with views over forest, farmland, and low mountain folds.

Go with a local driver who knows the road and weather. The view is best after the monsoon clears the air, though the rains also make the hills intensely green.
