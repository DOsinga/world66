Kolab Dam sits west of Koraput on a broad highland reservoir. It is one of the easier outings from town and works well when you want fresh air and wide water views without committing to a long district drive.

The appeal is simple: hill air, open water, and a break from the traffic in town. It is best around the softer light of morning or late afternoon.
