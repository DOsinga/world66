Maliguda Railway Tunnel is known as one of India's highest broad-gauge railway tunnels, on the Visakhapatnam-Kirandaul line. The surrounding hill railway scenery is the main draw rather than the tunnel alone.

It is a niche stop for rail enthusiasts or travelers already exploring the Koraput area by road. Check local access conditions before making a special trip.
