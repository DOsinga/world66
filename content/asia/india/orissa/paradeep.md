Paradip (also spelled Paradeep) is a major port city on the coast of Odisha, where the Mahanadi River meets the Bay of Bengal. It is one of India's busiest cargo ports, but it also has a surprisingly pleasant [beach](/asia/india/orissa/paradeep/paradeep_beach) and a marine drive that make it worth a stop if you are travelling the Odisha coast.

The beach is wide and largely uncrowded, backed by casuarina groves. The [port](/asia/india/orissa/paradeep/paradeep_port) area itself is interesting to watch — massive container ships come and go. Paradip is about 94 km from Cuttack, with regular bus services making it an easy day trip. The [Mahanadi delta wetlands](/asia/india/orissa/paradeep/mahanadi_delta) surrounding the town are rich in birdlife, and the Bhitarkanika mangroves are a short drive north.
