Netaji Museum occupies the ancestral house of Subhas Chandra Bose in Puri and collects photographs, letters, journals, and personal material connected with the independence leader. It is a quieter counterpoint to the temple and beach circuit, giving the town a modern historical stop.

The museum is modest, but worthwhile if Bose's story interests you or if you want a short cultural visit near the centre of Puri.
