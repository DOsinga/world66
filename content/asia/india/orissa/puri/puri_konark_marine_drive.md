The Puri-Konark Marine Drive is the coastal road linking Puri with Konark, with empty beaches, river mouths, and small temples along the way. It is best treated as a slow detour rather than a transfer: stop where access is clear, keep an eye on surf conditions, and leave time for Ramachandi Temple near the route.
