Lalitgiri is one of Odisha's major Buddhist archaeological sites, set among low hills west of the Ratnagiri-Udayagiri cluster. Excavations have uncovered monastery remains, stupas, sculptures, terracotta objects, and bronze pieces that point to a long history of Buddhist worship and learning here.

It is usually visited with nearby Ratnagiri and Udayagiri as part of a full-day circuit. The setting is quiet and rural, so the appeal is in the ruins, the landscape, and the chance to see one of eastern India's most important early Buddhist sites without large crowds.
