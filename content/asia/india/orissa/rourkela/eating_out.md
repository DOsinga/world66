---
title: Eating Out
type: section
---

Rourkela's food scene reflects its origins as a planned industrial city — there is a sprinkle of north Indian, south Indian, Bengali, and Odia kitchens, all reasonably priced. The standout is **Madhuvan** in Civil Township, a long-running vegetarian thali restaurant where a full plate with three vegetables, dal, rice, rotis and a sweet runs to around 200 rupees. For Bengali and Odia fish curries, head to **Vatika**.

Pure-veg North Indian travellers should look out for **Renuka**, near Sector 2 market, where the dal makhani and stuffed parathas are well above average. Street food clusters around Bisra Chowk in the evening, with chaat carts, momos, and *gupchup* (the Odia version of pani puri) selling till late. For chai and Bengali sweets there are several decent shops on Main Road in Uditnagar.
