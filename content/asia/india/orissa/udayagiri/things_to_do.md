---
title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Udayagiri is primarily a destination for those interested in Buddhist archaeology and Odishan history. The mahavihara complex itself is the main draw, and exploring the excavated monastery, the stepped well, and the hilltop rock sculptures fills a half-day comfortably. The site is quieter and less visited than nearby Ratnagiri, which makes it a more contemplative experience.
