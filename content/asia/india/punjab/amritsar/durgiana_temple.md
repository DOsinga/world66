The Durgiana Temple sits about 2 km from the Golden Temple complex — an easy auto-rickshaw ride. A Hindu temple dedicated to the goddess Durga, it was built in the 1920s in a style that closely mirrors the Golden Temple, with a gilded exterior, a central pool, and a similar approach via a causeway over water.

It receives far fewer visitors than its Sikh counterpart and has a quieter, more contemplative atmosphere. Dress conservatively and remove shoes before entering. The temple is particularly beautiful early in the morning, when the gold catches the light and the crowds are thin.
