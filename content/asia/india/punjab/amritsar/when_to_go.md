---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

Amritsar has a continental climate with hot summers and cold winters. The ideal visiting window is October to March, when temperatures are comfortable and the city is at its most pleasant.

**October to November** is the best time: post-monsoon clarity, mild temperatures (20–28°C), and the Diwali festival in October or November, which sees the Golden Temple illuminated in spectacular fashion — one of the finest sights in India.

**December to February** is cool to cold (5–15°C at night, sometimes touching freezing). The city is busy with domestic pilgrims during this period, and the Wagah Border ceremony continues year-round regardless of temperature.

**March to April** warms up quickly and is pleasant. The harvest festival of Baisakhi is celebrated in April with particular intensity in Amritsar — it was during Baisakhi on 13 April 1919 that the Jallianwala Bagh massacre occurred.

**May to August** is hot (35–45°C in summer) and humid during the monsoon from July to September. Manageable but not comfortable. The monsoon usually brings green countryside and frequent rain to the city itself.
