---
title: "Getting There"
type: section
---

Bathinda is one of the best-connected railway junctions in Punjab — direct trains run to Delhi (6–8 hours), Amritsar (4 hours), Jaipur, Bikaner and Mumbai. The main **Bathinda Junction** station is in the heart of town, walking distance from the fort.

Buses run frequently from Chandigarh (5 hours), [Amritsar](/asia/india/punjab/amritsar) (4 hours) and Delhi (8 hours). The bus stand is just south of the railway station.

**Bathinda Airport** (also called Bhisiana) has limited domestic flights — mainly to Delhi — and is not generally worth the trouble for short-haul travellers; the train is faster and cheaper.
