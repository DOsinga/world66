---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

Bathinda has one of the harsher climates in Punjab — summer temperatures climb past 45°C in May and June and the heat radiates off the brick walls of the old city well into the night. Avoid it.

**October to March** is the only sensible window: warm days, cool evenings, and the Rose Garden in bloom from February. Winter mornings can drop to 4–5°C with fog so thick it cancels trains, so pack a fleece.

If you can time it with **Baisakhi** in mid-April, the pilgrimage to Damdama Sahib is one of the great spectacles of southern Punjab — but expect crowds and a sudden spike in heat.
