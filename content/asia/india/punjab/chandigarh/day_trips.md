Chandigarh sits at the foot of the Shivalik Hills and is the obvious gateway to several worthwhile day trips. The big two are **Shimla**, the old summer capital of British India, three hours' drive into the mountains, and **Pinjore Gardens** (Yadavindra Gardens) just east of the city, a terraced Mughal garden laid out in the 17th century that makes a pleasant half-day escape.

Further afield, **Anandpur Sahib** is one of the holiest places in Sikhism and about two hours by road; the Virasat-e-Khalsa museum here, designed by Moshe Safdie, is one of the most striking modern buildings in northern India. **Amritsar** and the Golden Temple are reachable as a long day trip but really deserve an overnight.
