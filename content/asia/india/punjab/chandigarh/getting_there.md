---
title: "Getting There"
type: section
---

Chandigarh's airport, **Shaheed Bhagat Singh International**, has direct flights from Delhi, Mumbai, Bengaluru, and most major Indian metros, plus a handful of international links to Dubai and Sharjah. It is about 12 km from the city centre, with metered taxis and pre-paid auto-rickshaws at the terminal.

The **Shatabdi Express** from Delhi is the most pleasant way to arrive — about three and a half hours from New Delhi station, with breakfast served on board. Other express trains connect Chandigarh with Amritsar, Lucknow and Kolkata.

By **road**, Chandigarh is around 250 km north of Delhi on the well-maintained NH44 — five to six hours by car or air-conditioned Volvo coach. The Inter-State Bus Terminal in Sector 17 is the central hub; another large terminal sits in Sector 43 and handles long-distance services to Himachal Pradesh and Jammu.
