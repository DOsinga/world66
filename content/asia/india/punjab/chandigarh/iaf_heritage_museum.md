The newest of Chandigarh's museums, opened in 2023 to mark the city's long links with the Indian Air Force (Chandigarh has been an important IAF base since the 1960s). The collection includes uniforms, archive photographs, and a few decommissioned aircraft including a MiG-21 — the type that flew the famous 1971 raid on Tezgaon airfield in then-East Pakistan.

It is not large, but it is enthusiastically curated and a good rainy-afternoon stop. Combine with the Government Museum in the same sector.
