Named after Rabindranath Tagore, the city's main theatre is a modernist concrete pavilion in Sector 18 with a fan-shaped auditorium and a permanent calendar of Punjabi and Hindi plays, classical music recitals and dance recitals. The exterior is a typical Chandigarh statement — bare concrete, sculptural projecting cantilevers — and the venue is a useful gauge of what's happening culturally on any given week.

Tickets are cheap by Western standards and many performances are in English or Hindi. Check the noticeboards outside or the Chandigarh Tourism listings.
