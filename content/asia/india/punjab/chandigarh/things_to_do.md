---
title: "Things to do in Chandigarh"
type: section
---

Chandigarh's main draws are architectural and outdoors. The Capitol Complex and the Rock Garden are the headliners, but the city's parks, lake and museums fill out a comfortable two-day itinerary without much effort.

Start with the **Capitol Complex** and the **Open Hand Monument** — the Le Corbusier core — then walk down through Sector 17 and the **Government Museum** before ending an afternoon at the **Rock Garden** and **Sukhna Lake**. Save the **Rose Garden** for sundown.
