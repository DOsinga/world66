Mubarak Mosque in Qadian is regarded as the first mosque of the Ahmadiyya movement, making it an important pilgrimage and heritage site for Ahmadi Muslims. The complex includes spaces associated with prayer and reflection, including Bait-ul Fikr, Bait-ud-Dua, and Gol Kamra.

For most travellers this is a specialist stop, but for anyone following the religious history of Punjab it is one of the district's more significant sites.
