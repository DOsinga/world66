The Sodal Mandir, in the Doaba Chowk area near Devi Talab, is a smaller shrine but the centre of one of Jalandhar's most distinctive religious traditions. Baba Sodal was a child saint of the Khatri community said to have drowned in the temple tank in the 15th century after a dispute with his mother. The annual Sodal Mela on Anant Chaudas day (September) is the city's largest fair, drawing pilgrims from the Punjabi diaspora as far as Britain and Canada.

The shrine itself is small and not architecturally distinguished, but the tank is the focus and the atmosphere during the mela is genuinely impressive. Outside festival time it is a quiet local stop; combine it with a visit to Devi Talab a kilometre away.
