title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Kapurthala's appeal is almost entirely architectural: a small Punjabi city that a Francophile maharaja tried to remake as a "Paris of the East." The results are scattered around town and best seen on foot or by auto-rickshaw over a few hours. The [Jagatjit Palace](/asia/india/punjab/kapurthala/jagatjit_palace) (now a military school and visible from the road) set the tone; the [Moorish Mosque](/asia/india/punjab/kapurthala/moorish_mosque) is the most extraordinary individual building; the [Panch Mandir](/asia/india/punjab/kapurthala/panch_mandir) offers a striking contrast in the form of five Hindu temples grouped together. The overall effect — French Renaissance beside Mughal-inspired gardens beside Sikh gurdwaras — is genuinely bizarre and worth the detour from [Jalandhar](/asia/india/punjab/jalandhar).
