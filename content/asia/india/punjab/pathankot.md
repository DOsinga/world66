Pathankot is a transport junction in northern Punjab, the gateway to the hill stations and mountain valleys of Himachal Pradesh and [Jammu and Kashmir](/asia/india/jammuandkashmir). Most travellers pass through rather than linger, but it serves as a useful staging point for journeys to [Dalhousie](/asia/india/himachalpradesh/dalhousie), [Kangra](/asia/india/himachalpradesh/kangra), Chamba, and the deeper Himalayas.

The town sits in the foothills where the Punjab plains meet the [Shivalik Hills Foothills](/asia/india/punjab/pathankot/sarkaghat_road_viewpoint), near the Chakki River. [Shahpur Kandi Fort](/asia/india/punjab/pathankot/shahpur_kandi_fort), a few kilometres out, has crumbling ramparts with views over the Ravi River gorge, and the [Madhopur Headworks](/asia/india/punjab/pathankot/madhopur_headworks) — a Victorian-era barrage that still feeds the canals of central Punjab — makes for a quiet excursion on the edge of town.

The district's other highlights lie a little further out: the [Ranjit Sagar Dam](/asia/india/punjab/pathankot/ranjit_sagar_dam) and its wide reservoir, shared between Punjab, Himachal Pradesh, and Jammu and Kashmir, is good for birdwatching in winter. The [Nurpur Fort](/asia/india/punjab/pathankot/nurpur_fort) on the road to Dharamsala is an undervisited Rajput ruin with a fine temple inside. And the [Kangra Valley Railway](/asia/india/punjab/pathankot/kangra_valley_railway), departing from Pathankot Junction on its narrow gauge toward Jogindernagar, is one of the great slow train journeys of the Himalayan foothills.
