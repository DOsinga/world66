Perched on a rocky spur above the Ravi River where it cuts through the Shivalik Hills, Shahpur Kandi Fort is the most dramatic historic site in the Pathankot district. The fort dates to the era of the Pathania Rajput chiefs and occupies a commanding position overlooking the deep river gorge, with views stretching across the green hills toward Himachal Pradesh.

The ruins themselves are atmospheric rather than grand — weathered stone walls, crumbling towers, and overgrown courtyards — but the setting more than compensates. The road down to the river from the fort passes through thick forest, and the Ravi below is wide and clear here before it joins the Ranjit Sagar reservoir. Shahpur Kandi is about eight kilometres from Pathankot town; shared jeeps and autos run there, or you can take a taxi and combine it with the dam.
