A 17th-century Mughal haveli in the Naya Bazar district, said to have been used by visiting emperors during their pilgrimages to the Dargah. The building is now in private hands and only the courtyard is accessible, but the carved facade — pointed cusped arches, deep jharokha balconies, lime plasterwork — is one of the better-preserved examples of Mughal domestic architecture in Ajmer.

Worth looking at as you walk through the bazaar between the Dargah and the Akbari Fort.
