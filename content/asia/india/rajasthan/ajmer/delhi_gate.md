One of the surviving gates of the walled old city, marking the road that led north towards Delhi. The stone arch and its twin bastions are 17th century; through the gate the bazaar runs straight down to the approaches of the Dargah Sharif. Easily seen on the way between Dargah and Ana Sagar Lake.
