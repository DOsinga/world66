It is still one of the most prestigious boys' boarding schools in India."
---

Mayo College sits on the southern outskirts of Ajmer in 87 acres of grounds, dominated by its striking white marble main building. The main building and the small museum inside are sometimes open to visitors with prior permission from the school office; even the exterior, glimpsed from the road, is one of the great pieces of late-19th-century architecture in north India.

The school's old boys include several maharajas and a number of well-known Indian writers and politicians.
