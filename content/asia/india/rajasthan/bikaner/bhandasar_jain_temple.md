The Bhandasar Jain Temple is one of the finest examples of Jain architecture in Rajasthan, tucked into the old city about two kilometres from Junagarh Fort. Its five-storey facade is covered in intricate carvings, and the interiors are painted with gold leaf and richly coloured murals depicting Jain cosmological scenes. The temple is dedicated to the fifth Jain Tirthankara, Sumatinath.

The building is still an active place of worship, so dress conservatively, remove shoes before entering, and check that it's open to visitors before making the journey. The upper floors offer good views over the old city rooftops. It is less visited than the fort and rewards those who seek it out.
