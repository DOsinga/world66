Shivbari Temple is a centuries-old Shiva temple just outside Bikaner, partly surrounded by Shiv Bari Park. The complex is known for its two large bawris, or water reservoirs, and it draws steady local pilgrimage traffic through the year.

It is a modest but worthwhile stop if you are already exploring Bikaner's forts, havelis, and temples by car or auto-rickshaw.
