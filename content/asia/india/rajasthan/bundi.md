Bundi is what Rajasthan must have felt like before the tour buses arrived. This small town, about 40 km from [Kota](/asia/india/rajasthan/kota), sits below the star-shaped [Taragarh Fort](/asia/india/rajasthan/bundi/taragarh_fort) and is filled with crumbling havelis, step-wells, and narrow lanes largely free of souvenir shops.

The [Garh Palace](/asia/india/rajasthan/bundi/garh_palace), clinging to the hillside beneath the fort, contains some of the finest murals in Rajasthan — vivid scenes of court life, hunting, and mythology painted on walls and ceilings. The town has several beautiful step-wells (baoris), with the [Raniji ki Baori](/asia/india/rajasthan/bundi/raniji_ki_baori) being the most impressive — Bundi has over 50 step-wells, more than any other town in Rajasthan. Bundi rewards slow travel: spend a couple of days walking the old streets, watching sunsets from the fort walls, and drifting out to the peaceful [Jait Sagar Lake](/asia/india/rajasthan/bundi/jait_sagar_lake) where Kipling once stayed at the lakeside [Sukh Mahal](/asia/india/rajasthan/bundi/sukh_mahal).
