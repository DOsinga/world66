title: Getting There
type: section
---

Bundi has a small railway station on the Kota–Chittor line with a few direct services from [Jaipur](/asia/india/rajasthan/jaipur) and connections from [Delhi](/asia/india/delhi) and [Mumbai](/asia/india/maharashtra/mumbai). The nearest major junction is **Kota**, about 40 km away, which has frequent trains on the Mumbai–Delhi route. From Kota, buses and shared jeeps run to Bundi throughout the day.

By road, Bundi is about 210 km south of Jaipur (around 4 hours) and 35 km north of Kota. State buses connect it to Ajmer, Chittorgarh, and Kota. Many travellers combine Bundi with a circuit that includes [Chittorgarh](/asia/india/rajasthan/chittorgarh) and [Pushkar](/asia/india/rajasthan/pushkar).
