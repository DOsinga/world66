Lake Nawal Sagar sits in the middle of Bundi, catching reflections of the palace, temples, and blue-washed houses around it. It is less a wilderness lake than an urban mirror, and that makes it central to the town's visual charm.

Walk the edges slowly, especially in morning or late afternoon light. The water level and cleanliness can vary, but the view back toward Bundi's old town is one of the classic reasons to linger here.
