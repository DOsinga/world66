The Kalika Mata Temple is one of the oldest structures within Chittorgarh Fort, originally an eighth-century Sun Temple that was later converted to a shrine of the goddess Kali. The outer walls retain some of their original carved panels in a style characteristic of Pratihara-period architecture. The temple remains an active place of worship, particularly busy on Navratri, the festival of the goddess.

The interior is typically simple — a sanctum with an image of Kali — but the setting within the fort, combined with the temple's complex architectural history, makes it one of the more interesting buildings on the plateau. Shoes must be removed before entering.
