Rana Kumbha's Palace is one of the major ruins inside Chittorgarh Fort and among its oldest palace complexes. The remains include courtyards, stables, temple spaces, and a series of balconies that hint at the scale of the former royal residence.

The palace is tied to several Chittorgarh stories, including the birth of Maharana Udai Singh and legends around Panna Dhay and Rani Padmini. Come for the atmosphere of the ruins as much as the history: the broken stonework gives the fort's dramatic past a physical shape.
