---
title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Hanumangarh's two main attractions are the Bhatner Fort in town and the Kalibangan archaeological site 30 kilometres to the south. Both require some effort to appreciate — Bhatner is partially ruined and lacks signage, and Kalibangan is an active site that requires advance arrangements. But for anyone interested in ancient and medieval history, both are well worth the detour.
