1135 AD is named for the founding year of the Amber kingdom, and it operates inside the Amber Fort complex — a rare opportunity to eat in an actual fort rather than just visit one. The setting is extraordinary: high-arched halls with painted walls, stone floors, and staff in period costume.

The menu focuses on Rajput court cuisine — dishes that allegedly draw on historical recipes from the maharajas' kitchens. The laal maas (red meat curry), safed maas (white meat curry in yogurt), and the game meat preparations are the picks. The quality is generally good, the presentation is theatrical, and the prices are firmly in the upper range.

Lunch here works well as part of a fort visit — you don't need to re-enter after eating. The restaurant gets very busy at peak times; booking ahead is strongly recommended. Entry requires a fort ticket.
