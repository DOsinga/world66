Amrapali Museum is a jewellery museum with thousands of pieces ranging from tribal ornaments in wood and metal to silver, gold, antiques, and decorative curios. The collection is especially useful in Jaipur, where jewellery and craft traditions are part of the city's identity. Allow about an hour if you have an interest in design or traditional Indian workmanship.
