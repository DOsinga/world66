Sanghiji Jain Temple is one of Sanganer's most important religious sites, southeast of central Jaipur. The temple is associated with the Digambara Jain tradition and is known for its carved stone architecture, layered courtyards, and a sanctum that draws devotees as much as sightseers.

It pairs naturally with Sanganer's textile and paper traditions, making the suburb more than a shopping errand. Dress and behave as you would at an active temple; the building is beautiful, but it is not a museum.
