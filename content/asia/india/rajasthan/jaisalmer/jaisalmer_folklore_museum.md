Jaisalmer Folklore Museum focuses on Rajasthan's folk heritage, with displays on local arts, customs, textiles, and everyday objects from the desert region. It is a small museum, but useful if you want context before visiting the havelis and fort.

The evening puppet shows are the highlight, giving the collection a more lively frame than display cases alone.
