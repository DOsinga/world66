Chambal Garden is a riverside garden on the banks of the Chambal, with paths, a pond, and a small suspension bridge. Its animal enclosures and traffic-themed children's park make it more family-oriented than Kota's forts and palaces.

Come for an easy outdoor break rather than a major monument. It works best as a relaxed stop after seeing the older sights closer to the centre.
