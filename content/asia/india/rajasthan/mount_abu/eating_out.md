---
title: "Eating Out"
type: section
---

Mount Abu is overwhelmingly vegetarian, partly because of the Jain temples and partly because much of the town's tourist trade comes from religious Gujaratis. Expect plenty of Gujarati thalis, Rajasthani dal-baati-churma, South Indian dosas, and Punjabi-style chole. Alcohol is restricted but not impossible — a few of the hotels have licensed bars.
