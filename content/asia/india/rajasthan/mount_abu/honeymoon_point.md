A second viewpoint west of town, looking northwest over a broader sweep of the plains. The name supposedly comes from two natural rocks that resemble a man and a woman, but the place is functional rather than romantic — well-trodden paths, a few cafes, busloads of visitors. Quieter than Sunset Point and worth the detour for the wider view.
