---

The small lake in the centre of Mount Abu town is the focal point of an evening stroll. Pedal boats and rowing boats are available for hire from the eastern jetty. The walk around the lake takes about 45 minutes and passes a string of named granite outcrops — Toad Rock and Nun Rock being the most photographed — and the rather kitsch promenade of food stalls and souvenir shops. A statue of Gandhi stands above the lake at Gandhi Ghat, where his ashes were partly immersed.
