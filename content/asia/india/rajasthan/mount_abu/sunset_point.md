A west-facing viewpoint on the edge of the Mount Abu plateau, set up with benches and snack stalls for the daily ritual of watching the sun drop over the Aravalli hills toward the Gujarat plains. Walk or take a horse cart from the centre — about 2 km. It can be crowded with Indian families and a bit of a circus, but the light over the receding ridges is genuinely beautiful. Arrive 30 minutes before sunset.
