The most famous of Mount Abu's named granite outcrops, perched above the south shore of Nakki Lake. From a particular angle it looks unmistakably like a toad about to leap into the water. A short staircase leads up to the rock and you can scramble onto the toad's back for views over the lake and town. Best at sunset when the granite turns warm orange and the air cools enough to make the climb pleasant.
