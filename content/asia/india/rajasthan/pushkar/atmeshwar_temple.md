Atmeshwar Temple is a small Shiva shrine in Pushkar, away from the most crowded ghats and Brahma Temple approach. It is not the town's headline sight, but it gives a quieter glimpse of Pushkar's dense sacred geography, where temples appear in lanes, courtyards, and tucked-away corners.

The visit is short and best folded into a wider walk rather than planned as a separate outing. Go respectfully, expect a local devotional atmosphere, and use it as a reminder that Pushkar is more than its lakefront circuit and market streets.
