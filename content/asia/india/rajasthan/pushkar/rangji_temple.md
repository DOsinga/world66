Rangji Temple stands out in Pushkar because it follows a South Indian temple style rather than the local Rajasthani pattern. Its gopuram-like entrance and Vishnu worship give the town's temple circuit a different rhythm from the Brahma Temple and the smaller shrines around the lake.

It is an easy stop while walking through Pushkar's centre. Come for the contrast in architecture and ritual, not for the scale of a major South Indian temple complex.
