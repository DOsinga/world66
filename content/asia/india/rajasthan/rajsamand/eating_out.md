---
title: "Eating Out"
type: section
---

Rajsamand has a handful of small restaurants and dhabas catering mainly to locals and passing travellers on the Udaipur–Jodhpur highway. Expect simple Rajasthani thalis, dal baati churma, and basic north Indian staples. The options are modest compared to Udaipur, so many day-trippers bring food with them or eat before they arrive.
