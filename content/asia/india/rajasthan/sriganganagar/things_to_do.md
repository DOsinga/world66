---
title: Things to Do
type: section
---

Sri Ganganagar is not a tourist destination and makes no effort to be one. The few reasons to pause here are the Ganga Canal heritage, the archaeological site at Kalibangan nearby, the border atmosphere, and the local Punjabi-Rajasthani market culture. It works best as an overnight stop for travellers crossing to or from Punjab or heading to the Thar desert further south.
