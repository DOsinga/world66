---
title: "Eating Out"
type: section
---

Udaipur eats Rajasthani — *dal baati churma* (lentils with baked wheat balls and crumbled sweet), *gatte ki sabzi* (gram-flour dumplings in curry), *laal maas* (mutton in a fierce red chilli gravy, the Mewar speciality), and the local *thali* that lets you taste a bit of everything. The old city has the highest concentration of restaurants in Rajasthan — most with rooftops, most with views of the lake. The food at many of them is mediocre; we have picked out the ones that actually deliver.
