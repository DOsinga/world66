A small, sociable cafe on the steps above Gangaur Ghat, **Jheel's** does decent espresso, fresh masala chai, smoothies thick enough to stand a spoon in, and a short menu of cheese toasties and homemade cake. The terrace seats look directly down at the lake.

A favourite mid-morning stop for travellers waiting for boats or for the cultural show at Bagore ki Haveli. Free WiFi, good for an hour of catching up on emails before the heat builds.
