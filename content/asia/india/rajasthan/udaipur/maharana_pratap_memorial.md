On the hill of **Moti Magri** ("Pearl Hill") overlooking Fateh Sagar lake stands a bronze equestrian statue of **Maharana Pratap**, the 16th-century Mewar ruler who refused to submit to the Mughal emperor Akbar and spent his life fighting from the hills. The setting is more interesting than the statue itself — landscaped gardens, a small museum on Rajput warfare, and a fine view across the city.

A 20-minute walk up from the entrance. Best at sunset, with the light catching the Aravalli ridges to the west.
