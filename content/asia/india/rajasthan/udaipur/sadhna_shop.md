A women's collective handicraft shop on Fatehpura, **Sadhna** is run by Seva Mandir, an NGO that has been working with tribal women in southern Rajasthan since 1968. The shop sells the women's block-printed and embroidered textiles — table linen, cushion covers, scarves, simple kurtas — at fair, fixed prices.

Quality is consistently good; designs are subtle Rajasthani patterns updated for contemporary use. A genuinely useful place to buy gifts that have a story behind them rather than a hard-sell sales pitch.
