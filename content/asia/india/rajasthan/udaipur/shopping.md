---
title: "Shopping"
type: section
---

Udaipur is one of the best shopping cities in Rajasthan — miniature paintings, mojari leather slippers, block-printed textiles, silver jewellery, and puppets are all produced in or near the city. The main shopping streets are **Bada Bazaar** (the spice and food bazaar north of the Jagdish Temple), **Hathipole** (textiles, silver, and miniature painting workshops), and **Chetak Circle** (modern retail and tailoring).

Prices in tourist-facing shops are very negotiable — start at one-third the asking price. The cooperatives like **Sadhna** (women's collective handlooms) offer fixed prices and reliable quality.
