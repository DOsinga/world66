---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

**October to March** is the only sensible window. December and January nights can be surprisingly cold (down to 6–8°C) but the days are warm, dry, and clear — perfect for walking and for boat rides on the lake.

**April through June** is brutal: temperatures climb to 40–43°C and the heat hangs heavy over the white-washed city. Avoid unless you have to come.

**July to September** brings the monsoon. The Aravalli hills turn briefly green, the lakes fill, and prices drop sharply — but rain can be heavy enough to disrupt sightseeing. The **Mewar Festival** in late March or early April (timed with Gangaur) is one of the best in Rajasthan: parades, dancing, and a procession of decorated boats across Lake Pichola.
