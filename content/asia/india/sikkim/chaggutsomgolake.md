Tsomgo Lake, also written Changu or Tsongmo, is the classic high-altitude excursion from [Gangtok](/asia/india/sikkim/gangtok). The lake sits beside the road to [Nathu La](/asia/india/sikkim/chaggutsomgolake/nathu_la) at about 3,750 metres, ringed by steep slopes that hold snow well into spring. In winter it can freeze hard; in summer the surrounding hills brighten with alpine flowers.

The lake is beautiful, but it is not wilderness. Expect permit checks, jeeps, snack stalls, and decorated yaks for photographs. The trick is to accept the busyness and still look properly: the water changes colour with light and season, and the surrounding military road gives the whole place a frontier edge.

Most visitors combine the lake with [Baba Harbhajan Singh Mandir](/asia/india/sikkim/chaggutsomgolake/baba_harbhajan_singh_mandir), [Kyongnosla Alpine Sanctuary](/asia/india/sikkim/chaggutsomgolake/kyongnosla_alpine_sanctuary), and Nathu La if permits are available. Weather matters. Snow, landslides, or military restrictions can close the road quickly.
