Baba Harbhajan Singh Mandir is a shrine on the high route beyond Tsomgo Lake, usually included on the same jeep excursion. It is simple rather than architecturally grand, but its setting among army roads and bare slopes gives it a particular atmosphere.

The stop matters because it shows how closely faith, memory, and the military frontier are woven together in this part of Sikkim. Dress warmly; the wind can be sharp even when Gangtok feels mild.
