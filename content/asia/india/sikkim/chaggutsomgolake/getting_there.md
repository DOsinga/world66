---
title: Getting There
type: section
---

Tsomgo Lake is reached by shared or reserved jeep from Gangtok, usually as part of a permit-controlled day trip. The road is steep, scenic, and vulnerable to snow, fog, and landslides.

Foreign visitors and Indian citizens have different permit rules in Sikkim, and Nathu La has additional restrictions. Arrange paperwork in Gangtok before setting out rather than assuming it can be solved at the checkpoint.
