Nathu La is the high border pass east of Tsomgo Lake, reached only when permits, weather, and army conditions allow. The drive climbs into a stark landscape of snowfields, military posts, and thin air.

Do not assume it will be included automatically. Permits are limited, foreigners face additional restrictions, and closures are common. If you do get there, take it slowly; the altitude can hit hard.
