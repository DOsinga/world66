Dubdi Monastery, built in 1701, is one of Sikkim's oldest monasteries. It stands above Yuksom at the end of a forested uphill walk, and the combination of history, mountain setting, and quiet approach makes it one of the better cultural stops in the Pelling and Yuksom area.
