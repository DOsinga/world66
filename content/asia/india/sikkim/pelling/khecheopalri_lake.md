Khecheopalri is one of the most sacred lakes in Sikkim, revered by both Buddhists and Hindus. The dark water is perfectly still and, by tradition, no leaf is allowed to remain on the surface — birds are said to remove them. Whether or not you believe the legend, the lake has an atmosphere unlike anywhere else in the region.

The drive from Pelling takes about 45 minutes on winding roads, followed by a short walk through prayer-flag-decorated forest. The lake is surrounded by dense vegetation and the reflections of the forest on the water make it exceptionally photogenic. A small gompa overlooks the shore. Avoid weekends when pilgrim crowds are heaviest.
