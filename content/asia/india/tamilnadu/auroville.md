Auroville is an experimental township north of Pondicherry, founded in 1968 around the idea of a universal city beyond nationality and religion. That can sound abstract until you arrive: red-earth lanes, casuarina shade, quiet workshops, solar kitchens, handmade instruments, reforested plots, and visitors moving between spiritual curiosity and very practical community projects.

The centre of gravity is [Matrimandir](/asia/india/tamilnadu/auroville/matrimandir), the gold-covered meditation chamber at the heart of the plan. Day visitors begin at the [Auroville Visitors Centre](/asia/india/tamilnadu/auroville/auroville_visitors_centre), where the exhibitions explain the ideals before you walk toward the viewing point.

Auroville is not a normal sightseeing town. The best day here mixes the central visit with one or two of the community's working places: [SVARAM Sound Garden](/asia/india/tamilnadu/auroville/svaram_sound_garden) for its tactile sound installations, or the [Auroville Botanical Gardens](/asia/india/tamilnadu/auroville/auroville_botanical_gardens) for the township's long effort to restore tropical dry evergreen forest. Come with patience and check booking rules before setting your heart on the inner chamber.
