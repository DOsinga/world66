Auroville Botanical Gardens turns the township's ecological side into a real place to visit. The gardens cover former eroded land and focus on conserving tropical dry evergreen forest, one of the region's threatened ecosystems.

It is best for travellers who want the slower, less photographed side of Auroville. Visits may require advance contact, so check before making the trip out.
