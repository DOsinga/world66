SVARAM is one of Auroville's more distinctive public experiences: a sound garden where bamboo, stone, metal, and wood are turned into instruments you can touch and hear. It fits the township well, halfway between craft workshop, art space, and quiet play.

Check current tour times before going, as visits are more satisfying when the installations are explained instead of simply glanced at.
