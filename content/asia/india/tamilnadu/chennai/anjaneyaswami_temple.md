Anjaneyaswami Temple in Nanganallur is known for its large Hanuman idol, carved from a single stone and rising about 10 metres inside the shrine. It is an active place of worship rather than a quiet monument, so the best visit is a short one focused on the idol and the steady local devotion around it.
