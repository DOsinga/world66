Anna Nagar Tower Park is built around one of Chennai's tallest park towers, with a spiral ramp rising around the structure. The tower is the landmark, but the wider park also has paths, play areas, an amphitheatre, and small sports facilities.

It is a local, family-friendly stop rather than a headline Chennai sight, and works best if you are already in Anna Nagar or want a break from traffic-heavy sightseeing.
