Arignar Anna Zoological Park, also known as Vandalur Zoo, is one of the largest zoological parks in South Asia. Its animals are displayed in broad, open-moated enclosures, with mammals, birds, and reptiles spread across a large suburban site.

It is a full outing rather than a quick city stop, especially on weekends and around Pongal when crowds are heavier.
