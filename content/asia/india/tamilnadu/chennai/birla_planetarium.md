Birla Planetarium is Chennai's main astronomy centre, with sky shows and exhibits aimed especially at students and families. It sits beside the Periyar Science and Technology Museum, so the two can be combined into a science-focused visit.

The presentation is more educational than flashy, but it is one of the better indoor options in the city when the heat or rain makes outdoor sightseeing less appealing.
