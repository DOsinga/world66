Birla Planetarium is Chennai's main astronomy attraction, built in memory of industrialist B. M. Birla. Its sky shows and science exhibits make it a useful family stop, especially for visitors with an interest in space, astronomy, or hands-on science.

The adjoining Periyar Science and Technology Museum adds more exhibits, so it works best as a combined visit rather than a quick photo stop.
