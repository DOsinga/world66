The Church of Our Lady of Health in Besant Nagar is tied to stories of Marian miracles: an apparition to a shepherd boy, the healing of a buttermilk seller, and the survival of Portuguese sailors in a storm. It remains a pilgrimage church and a useful cultural stop near the coast in south Chennai.
