Descanso Church is a small historic church associated with the tradition of St. Thomas in Chennai. The site is said to mark a resting place used by the apostle while he served local communities, and the present church dates to the 17th century. It is most interesting as part of a Mylapore and San Thome walk, where several of Chennai's old Christian sites sit close together.
