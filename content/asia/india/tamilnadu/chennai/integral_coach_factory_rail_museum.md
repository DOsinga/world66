The Integral Coach Factory is primarily a working Indian Railways manufacturing site, but the visitor interest is its rail museum near Perambur. The displays focus on railway coaches, industrial history, and the role Chennai has played in building rolling stock for the national network.

It is a niche stop, best for railway enthusiasts or anyone curious about the machinery behind India's train system. Pair it with other north Chennai sights rather than crossing the city for it alone.
