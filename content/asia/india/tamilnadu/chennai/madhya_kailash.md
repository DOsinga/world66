Madhya Kailash is a busy Chennai temple dedicated mainly to Lord Ganesha, with shrines to Shiva, Parvati, and Vishnu. It is most rewarding as a living neighbourhood temple, especially for visitors interested in everyday Hindu worship rather than a museum-like monument.
