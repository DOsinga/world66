The MGR Memorial stands on Marina Beach and commemorates M. G. Ramachandran, the film star turned chief minister of Tamil Nadu, along with J. Jayalalithaa. It is more political shrine than quiet monument, and that makes it a telling stop on a walk along Chennai's seafront.
