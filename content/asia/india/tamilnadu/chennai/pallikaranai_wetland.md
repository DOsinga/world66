Pallikaranai Wetland is a Ramsar-listed marsh and one of Chennai's most important surviving natural habitats. It supports rare and threatened species and attracts migratory birds during the right season.

The wetland is not a manicured city park, so come for birdlife and ecology rather than facilities. It is a strong stop for visitors who want to see a wilder side of Chennai.
