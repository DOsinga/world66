Shirdi Sai Baba Temple is one of Chennai's better-known shrines dedicated to Sai Baba of Shirdi. It is a quieter stop than the city's older Dravidian temples, but it draws steady local devotion and gives a different view of Chennai's religious life.

Visit respectfully, especially during prayer times, and combine it with other south Chennai sights if you are already around Besant Nagar, Adyar, or Mylapore.
