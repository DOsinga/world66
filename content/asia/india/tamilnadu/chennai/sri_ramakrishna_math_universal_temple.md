Sri Ramakrishna Math in Mylapore is the oldest Ramakrishna Order centre in South India. The Universal Temple is the main draw for visitors: a calm, carefully designed shrine that blends devotional use with an unusually open architectural language.

The campus grew from the work of Swami Ramakrishnananda, who came to Madras in 1897 at Swami Vivekananda's request. It remains an active religious institution, so visit with the same quiet manners you would bring to a working temple.
