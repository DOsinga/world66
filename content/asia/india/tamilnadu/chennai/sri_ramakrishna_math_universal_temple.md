Sri Ramakrishna Math is a major Mylapore spiritual centre founded after Swami Vivekananda's return from the West. The Universal Temple is the main visitor focus, with a calm prayer hall and architecture designed to feel open to people of different faiths.

It is close enough to Kapaleeswarar Temple and Luz Church to include in a Mylapore walk, especially if you are interested in modern Hindu reform movements.
