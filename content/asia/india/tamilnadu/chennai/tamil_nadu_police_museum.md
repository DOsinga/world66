Tamil Nadu Police Museum traces the state's police history from the colonial period to the present. The exhibits cover uniforms, weapons, communications equipment, and other material from the force's past.

The building is part of the appeal: a two-storey Indo-Saracenic structure with high parapet walls, a wooden spiral stair, Madras terrace roofing, lime-plastered walls, and a Doric-columned entrance portico.
