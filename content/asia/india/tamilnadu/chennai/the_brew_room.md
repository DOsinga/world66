The Brew Room is part of Chennai's emerging specialty coffee scene — a proper third-wave coffee shop in the Adyar neighbourhood that takes its sourcing and preparation seriously. Single-origin beans, pour-over brewing, and knowledgeable staff put it in different territory from the traditional filter coffee houses.

The space is comfortable and air-conditioned, with good wifi and a working atmosphere that makes it popular with the city's creative and tech workers. The espresso-based drinks are well-made; the filter options show off the quality of the beans. A simple food menu covers sandwiches and baked goods.

For visitors staying in the Adyar or T. Nagar area, this is the default option for a good coffee in the morning.
