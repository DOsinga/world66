Thirumalai Thirupathi Devasthanam Temple is Chennai's busy shrine to Lord Venkatachalapathi, maintained by the Tirumala Tirupati Devasthanams. It draws a steady stream of devotees, especially on weekends, when the atmosphere is at its most intense.

For visitors, the interest is less architectural showpiece than living worship. Go with patience, dress respectfully, and expect crowds on Saturdays and Sundays.
