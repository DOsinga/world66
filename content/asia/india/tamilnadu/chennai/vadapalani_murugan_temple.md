Vadapalani Murugan Temple is a popular Chennai temple dedicated to Lord Muruga. Although older in origin, it was renovated in the 1920s and later became especially well known through visits by Tamil cinema stars.

Come for the active devotional atmosphere rather than quiet sightseeing. It is a useful stop if you are exploring beyond the central colonial and Mylapore sights.
