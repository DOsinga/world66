Vivekananda House is the old Ice House near Marina Beach, later associated with Swami Vivekananda after his return from Chicago. Today it is a small museum with displays, photos, a bookshop, and video presentations on his life and teachings, making it a useful stop if you are already walking the northern end of the beach.
