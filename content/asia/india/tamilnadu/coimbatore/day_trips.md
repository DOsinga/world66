---
title: "Day Trips"
type: section
---

Coimbatore's hinterland is excellent. The Nilgiri hills begin within an hour to the north; the Anamalai forests are 50 km south. The Nilgiri Mountain Railway — one of only two rack railways in India and a UNESCO World Heritage Site — departs from Mettupalayam 40 km north, making Ooty accessible without a car. Kodaikanal is a longer half-day journey by bus.
