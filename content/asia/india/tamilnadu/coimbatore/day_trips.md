Coimbatore's hinterland is excellent. The Nilgiri hills begin within an hour to the north, and the [Anamalai Tiger Reserve](/asia/india/tamilnadu/anamalai_tiger_reserve) spreads through the Western Ghats some 50 km to the south, with elephants, the rare Nilgiri tahr, and a chance of tigers. The classic outing is up to [Ooty](/asia/india/tamilnadu/ooty), the old Nilgiri hill station — and the way to arrive is the Nilgiri Mountain Railway, one of only two rack railways in India and a UNESCO World Heritage Site, which departs from Mettupalayam 40 km north and makes the climb without a car. Kodaikanal is a longer half-day journey by bus.
