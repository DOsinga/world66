---
title: "Eating Out"
type: section
---

Coimbatore's food is a hybrid of Tamil Brahmin vegetarian cooking and the Muslim and Kerala influences of its trading communities. Morning tiffin — idli, dosa, pongal, vada — is excellent across the city; the biryani at the old Muslim restaurants in the central area is among the best in Tamil Nadu. Fish and prawn dishes from Kerala appear alongside the standard Tamil vegetarian fare.
