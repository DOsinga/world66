GD Museums reflects Coimbatore's engineering and manufacturing side better than most standard city sights. The collection grew from the legacy of inventor and industrialist G. D. Naidu, with exhibits on automobiles, mechanics, electronics, and practical science.

It is strongest for visitors who like machines, design, and the story of how a textile city became an industrial one. Families usually get more out of it than they expect, because many displays are concrete and visual rather than purely academic.
