Avinashi, 29 km northeast of Coimbatore, is home to the Avinashi Lingeshwara Temple — one of the more important Shiva temples in western Tamil Nadu, mentioned in the ancient Tamil Thevarams. The temple tank is large and well-maintained, with the main gopuram reflected in its water in the mornings.

The temple is active and crowded on festival days, particularly during Shivaratri. It's accessible by bus from Coimbatore in about 45 minutes and can be combined with a visit to nearby Tiruppur for those interested in the textile manufacturing that dominates this part of Tamil Nadu.
