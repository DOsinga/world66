---
title: "Getting There"
type: section
---

Cuddalore is on the main coastal highway and railway between Chennai and Nagapattinam, making it easy to reach from most of Tamil Nadu.

**By train:** Cuddalore Port Junction and Cuddalore town stations are on the Chennai-Mayiladuthurai rail line. Trains run regularly from Chennai (about 3-4 hours), Pondicherry (30-40 minutes), and Chidambaram (about 1 hour).

**By road:** Cuddalore is on the ECR (East Coast Road) and NH32. Regular bus services connect to Chennai (about 3 hours), Pondicherry (30 minutes), and Chidambaram (45 minutes). The TNSTC bus stand in Cuddalore has frequent services in all directions.

**From Pondicherry:** The nearest large city is Pondicherry, just 20 km north. Share autos and buses run the route throughout the day.
