The Tsunami Memorial commemorates the people of Kanyakumari who died in the 2004 Indian Ocean tsunami. The monument stands near the southern shore and uses symbolic forms including a wave, a flame, and hands.

It is a brief but meaningful stop when walking between the seafront sights around Kanyakumari's southern tip.
