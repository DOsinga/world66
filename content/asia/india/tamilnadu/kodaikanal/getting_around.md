---
title: "Getting Around"
type: section
---

Kodaikanal is small enough to walk across in half an hour, but the surrounding sights require transport.

**Walking and cycling** work well for anything near the lake. Bicycles are available for hire on the lakeside road; prices are negotiable and a few hours is usually enough for a full lake circuit and a couple of nearby viewpoints.

**Auto-rickshaws** are available in town and will take you to the main viewpoints. Agree the fare before getting in — there are no meters.

**Taxis** are the best option for the full circuit of Pillar Rocks, Guna Caves, and Dolphin's Nose, which are spread across the hill and awkward to visit independently. A car hired for the day from the taxi stand near the bus stand will cost around ₹1,500-2,000 and will cover all the main sights. The driver will know the route.

The main road between the bus stand and the lake area has tourist-oriented traffic at peak times. Everything else is quiet.
