---
title: "Bars and Cafes"
type: section
---

Kumbakonam is a religious town and a fairly dry one — bars are rare, and the night life is essentially the temple bazaar in the evening. Where the town shines is in its tiffin houses and coffee culture: this is the home of **Kumbakonam Degree Coffee**, widely held to be the best filter coffee in Tamil Nadu, and an institution worth working into your day.
