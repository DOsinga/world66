---
title: "Getting There"
type: section
---

Kumbakonam is well connected within Tamil Nadu, less so internationally — most travellers arrive via Chennai or Trichy.

**By train** — Kumbakonam railway station is on the Chennai–Thanjavur line. Several daily trains run from **Chennai Egmore** (around 7 hours), **Thanjavur** (around 1 hour), and **Trichy** (around 2 hours). The station is a 20-minute walk south of the Mahamaham tank.

**By bus** — The Central Bus Station, just east of the centre, has frequent buses to Thanjavur, Trichy, Chidambaram, Karaikal, and Pondicherry, as well as longer-distance services to Chennai and Madurai.

**By air** — The nearest airport is **Trichy (TRZ)**, around 90 km west, with domestic flights and a handful of international routes. **Chennai (MAA)**, around 280 km north, is the main international gateway.
