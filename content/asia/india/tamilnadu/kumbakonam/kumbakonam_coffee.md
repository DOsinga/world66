Kumbakonam Degree Coffee is a regional speciality with its own near-mythic status: a strong filter coffee made with freshly ground beans (a blend usually around 80% coffee and 20% chicory), full-fat cow's milk, and a generous spoonful of sugar, served frothed back and forth between a metal tumbler and a wider dabarah to cool it to drinking temperature.

There is no single place to drink it — every old-style tiffin restaurant in town will serve a version, and locals will argue passionately about whose is best. **Sri Mangalambika Vilas**, **Sri Krishna Bhavan**, and the various branches of **Mami Mess** are all reliably good. Order it 'degree' and 'less sugar' if you prefer less sweetness. A morning round of one good filter coffee per shop is one of the better ways to walk through the old town.
