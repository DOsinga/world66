The Ramaswamy Temple is one of the more colourful of Kumbakonam's temples, dedicated to Rama and unusual for showing him enthroned with Sita, Lakshmana, and his brothers — a tableau rather than a standing figure. It was built in the seventeenth century by the Nayak rulers of Thanjavur.

The temple's distinctive feature is its **mandapam of 219 sculpted pillars**, each carved with scenes from the Ramayana, and the painted ceiling of the inner hall, with episodes from the epic spread above the worshippers. A series of relief panels around the outer walls continues the narrative. For a non-specialist visitor, this temple is among the easier to read as a story in stone.
