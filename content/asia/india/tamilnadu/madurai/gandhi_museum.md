The Gandhi Memorial Museum is housed in the 17th-century Rani Mangammal palace and is one of five national Gandhi museums in India. The exhibits trace the freedom movement from 1757 onward, with a strong focus on Tamil Nadu and on Gandhi's many visits to Madurai.

The displays are old-fashioned but unexpectedly powerful — large reproduction photographs, hand-written letters, and the dhoti Gandhi wore at his assassination. There is also a small khadi handicrafts shop selling cloth and books. Admission is free; closed Fridays.
