Kazimar Big Mosque and Maqbara is one of Madurai's important Islamic landmarks, a mosque and shrine complex associated with the city's early Muslim community. Its whitewashed architecture and tombs offer a quieter contrast to the great temple streets nearby.

Visitors should dress modestly and be respectful of prayer times. The site is worthwhile because it widens the story of Madurai beyond its famous Hindu monuments, showing the city's long religious and trading connections.
