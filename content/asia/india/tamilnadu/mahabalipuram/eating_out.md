---
title: Eating Out
type: section
---

Mahabalipuram's restaurant strip runs along the beach road and the main bazaar. Most places serve the standard traveller menu of Indian, Chinese, and continental dishes. The local speciality is fresh seafood — the catch comes directly off the beach, and grilled fish or tiger prawn masala at any of the beachside restaurants is reliably good and very cheap by Western standards.

**Moonrakers** on the beach road is one of the older establishments and stays busy for a reason: reliable seafood, cold beer, and a relaxed roof terrace. **Le Yogi** nearby has a good-value thali and is popular with families. For South Indian breakfast — idli, dosa, vada — any of the small local restaurants off the main tourist drag will be better and cheaper than the beach places.
