The monuments are the reason to come. The entire collection — [Shore Temple](/asia/india/tamilnadu/mahabalipuram/shore_temple), [Pancha Rathas](/asia/india/tamilnadu/mahabalipuram/pancha_rathas), [Arjuna's Penance](/asia/india/tamilnadu/mahabalipuram/arjunas_penance), [Krishna's Butterball](/asia/india/tamilnadu/mahabalipuram/krishnas_butterball), [Mahishasuramardini Cave](/asia/india/tamilnadu/mahabalipuram/mahishasuramardini_cave), [Varaha Cave Temple](/asia/india/tamilnadu/mahabalipuram/varaha_cave_temple), and [Krishna Mandapam](/asia/india/tamilnadu/mahabalipuram/krishna_mandapam) — forms the UNESCO World Heritage Site. They are spread across a compact area walkable in a day. Buy a single combined ticket at the Archaeological Survey of India booth near the Shore Temple.

The [Ganesha Ratha](/asia/india/tamilnadu/mahabalipuram/ganesha_ratha) in town is free and still in active use. The nearby [Sthalasayana Perumal Temple](/asia/india/tamilnadu/mahabalipuram/sthalasayana_perumal_temple) (Thirukadalmallai) is one of the 108 Divya Desams — sacred Vishnu temples of the Tamil Vaishnava tradition — and has been a working pilgrimage site for over a millennium. [Tiger Cave](/asia/india/tamilnadu/mahabalipuram/tiger_cave), 3 km north, is off the main tourist circuit and worth the detour for the quiet. [DakshinaChitra](/asia/india/tamilnadu/mahabalipuram/dakshinachitra) is 25 km north on the way to Chennai and merits a stop on the drive in or out.

Stone carving is the town's other speciality — workshops line the main street and the craft market, and many pieces are genuinely made here using techniques continuous with the Pallava tradition.
