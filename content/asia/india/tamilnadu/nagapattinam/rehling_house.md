Rehling's House is one of the notable colonial buildings in Tranquebar, near Fort Dansborg. It once served as the residence of the Danish governor and dates from the first half of the 18th century.

The draw is the white colonnaded facade and the sense of the former Danish settlement's street pattern. It works best as part of a walk around Tranquebar's fort and seafront.
