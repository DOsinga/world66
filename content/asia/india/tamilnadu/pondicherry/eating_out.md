---
title: "Eating Out"
type: section
---

Pondicherry's food scene is one of its genuine pleasures. The French Quarter has a cluster of restaurants serving French-influenced food — crêpes, quiche, baguettes — that are surprisingly authentic; this is not a novelty but a living culinary tradition going back to the colonial era. Tamil food is equally strong: the banana-leaf thali restaurants in the Tamil Quarter (west of the canal) are excellent and cheap.

The best restaurant street is Rue Suffren and its continuation south toward the seafront. The **Le Club** restaurant on Dumas Street is a Pondicherry institution, serving Creole and French cooking in a beautiful courtyard garden. **Surguru** on Lakshmi Nagar Street is the reliable choice for south Indian vegetarian food — the breakfast idli and dosa are particularly good. For French patisserie and coffee, the **Baker Street** café on Rue Suffren has proper croissants and a shaded terrace.
