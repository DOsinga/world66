Pondicherry Lighthouse marks the city's coastal history, with an older French-era lighthouse and a later Indian-built structure reflecting two periods of the town's administration. Its seafront location makes it easy to include while walking the promenade, the French War Memorial, and Rock Beach. The appeal is mostly historical and visual, rather than a long standalone visit.
