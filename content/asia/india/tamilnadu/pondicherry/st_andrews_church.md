St. Andrew's Church is one of Pondicherry's older Roman Catholic churches, with roots in the French colonial period and a Gothic-inspired structure rebuilt after the original 18th-century church. It is a quiet stop for visitors tracing the city's churches and colonial architecture beyond the main White Town streets.
