---
title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Pondicherry's main draw is the [French Quarter (White Town)](/asia/india/tamilnadu/pondicherry/white_town) — a well-preserved colonial grid of painted shutters, bougainvillea, and streets named for French generals. The [Sri Aurobindo Ashram](/asia/india/tamilnadu/pondicherry/sri_aurobindo_ashram) anchors the spiritual life of the town, while [Auroville](/asia/india/tamilnadu/pondicherry/auroville) nearby is its own peculiar experiment in intentional community. Walk the [Rock Beach Promenade](/asia/india/tamilnadu/pondicherry/rock_beach_promenade) at dawn before the crowds arrive. A day in [Pondicherry Botanical Garden](/asia/india/tamilnadu/pondicherry/botanical_garden) or the [Pondicherry Museum](/asia/india/tamilnadu/pondicherry/pondicherry_museum) fills out a rewarding afternoon.
