title: Things to Do
type: section
---

Pudukkottai's main draws are historical. The [Government Museum](/asia/india/tamilnadu/pudukkottai/government_museum) has a strong Chola bronze collection. The standout excursion is to [Sittanavasal](/asia/india/tamilnadu/pudukkottai/sittanavasal), 18 km away, where 7th-century Jain cave frescoes survive in remarkable condition. [Thirumayam Fort](/asia/india/tamilnadu/pudukkottai/thirumayam_fort), 15 km west, combines a 17th-century fort with ancient rock-cut temples.

The town itself is a quiet district capital — useful as a base for exploring the broader Pudukkottai region, which is scattered with minor temples and prehistoric megalithic monuments.
