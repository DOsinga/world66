---
title: "Things to Do in Ramanathapuram"
type: section
---

Ramanathapuram has a handful of sights connected to its past as the capital of the Sethupati rulers, who controlled this coastal region for several centuries. Most visitors are here en route to Rameshwaram and give the town only a brief stop, but the Ramalinga Vilasam Palace and the old town tank repay a few hours of exploration.
