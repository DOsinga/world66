The House of A. P. J. Abdul Kalam is the former family home of India's scientist-president, now partly arranged as a small museum. Photographs, awards, and personal displays trace his path from Rameshwaram to public life; it is modest, but it adds a human note to a town better known for temples and pilgrim rituals.
