The childhood home of Dr. A. P. J. Abdul Kalam has been converted into a small museum about the former Indian president and scientist. Photos, awards, and personal material trace his path from Rameshwaram to public life.

It is close to the centre of town and pairs easily with Rameshwaram's larger religious and coastal sights.
