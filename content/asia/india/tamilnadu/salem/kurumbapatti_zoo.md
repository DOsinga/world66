Kurumbapatti Zoological Park sits at the base of the Shevaroy Hills foothills about 10 km from Salem, making it a convenient half-day trip. It is the second largest zoo in Tamil Nadu after Arignar Anna Zoo in Chennai, with a collection that includes tigers, leopards, bears, and a range of deer and antelope species native to the subcontinent.

The grounds are spacious and reasonably green. The zoo is best visited on weekdays to avoid weekend crowds from Salem. The drive up passes through agricultural land with good views of the hills above. Combined with a trip to Yercaud, it makes a full day out of the city.
