Sri Raja Rajeswari Temple is a hill-country Hindu temple near Yercaud, dedicated to a form of the mother goddess. It is mainly a devotional stop, but travellers based around Salem or Yercaud may find it a quiet contrast to the area's viewpoints and waterfalls.
