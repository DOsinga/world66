Thanjavur is well placed for excursions through the Chola heartland. [Kumbakonam](/asia/india/tamilnadu/kumbakonam) is 38 km northeast and has some of the most remarkable temple tanks and Shiva shrines in the delta. [Srirangam](/asia/india/tamilnadu/srirangam), the great Vaishnavite complex on the Cauvery river island near Trichy, is about 55 km west.

Further afield, Gangaikondacholapuram — 70 km north — holds the second great Chola temple, built by Rajendra Chola I in the 11th century to celebrate his conquests to the Ganges. It receives far fewer visitors than the Brihadisvara and is worth the journey.
