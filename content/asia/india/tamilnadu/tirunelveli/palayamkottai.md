Palayamkottai (literally "encampment fort") grew up around an East India Company fort on the eastern bank of the Thamirabarani in the 18th century and became a major mission centre under the Anglican Church Missionary Society. It is now incorporated into Tirunelveli City but still has a distinct, slightly genteel character — wide cantonment-style streets, mature trees, and a cluster of grand 19th-century buildings.

The most prominent landmarks are the **Holy Trinity Cathedral**, completed in 1826 and still the seat of the CSI Diocese of Tirunelveli, and **St. Xavier's College**, founded by Jesuits in 1923 and one of the better arts colleges in the south. Bishop Caldwell, the great 19th-century Tamil scholar and missionary, lived and worked here for over 50 years.
