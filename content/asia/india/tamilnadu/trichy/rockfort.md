Rockfort is Trichy's signature landmark, a temple-crowned mass of stone rising suddenly above the old city. The climb is by a long flight of steps cut into the rock, passing the Thayumanaswamy Temple before reaching the Ucchi Pillayar shrine at the top.

The reward is the view: the Kaveri and Kollidam rivers, Srirangam's temple towers, and the city spreading below. Go early morning or near sunset; midday heat on the stone can be brutal.
