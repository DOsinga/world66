Valparai is a high tea town in the Anamalai Hills, reached from Pollachi by a long ghat road with hairpin bends, forest edges, and views back over the plains. It sits around 1,100 metres above sea level, cooler and quieter than Tamil Nadu's better-known hill stations.

The town itself is plain, but the surrounding landscape is excellent: tea estates roll across the plateau, [Nallamudi Poonjolai](/asia/india/tamilnadu/valparai/nallamudi_poonjolai) looks out over forested valleys, and the dams at Sholayar and Nirar turn the hills into wide sheets of water. The wider [Anamalai Tiger Reserve](/asia/india/tamilnadu/valparai/anamalai_tiger_reserve) brings real ecological weight, with elephants, hornbills, lion-tailed macaques, and other Western Ghats wildlife moving through the forests around the estates.

Valparai is not a place for nightlife or polished resort comforts. It is best for slow drives, misty mornings, estate walks where permitted, and the sense of being in a working hill landscape rather than a showpiece.
