Aaliyar Dam sits below Valparai in the Anamalai foothills, where the reservoir, surrounding hills, and parkland make it a useful stop on the road up from Pollachi. The site is best treated as a short scenic break rather than a full-day destination, with views over the water and simple family attractions around the dam area.
