Nallamudi Poonjolai is Valparai's classic viewpoint, reached by a short walk through tea estate country after the road approach. The reward is a broad look over the Anamalai slopes, with tea below and forested hills dropping away beyond.

Go early if you can. Mist often makes the view more beautiful, but it can also close the valley completely by afternoon.
