Tripura is a small northeastern state with a surprisingly rich travel mix: royal palaces, Bengali-influenced towns, tribal hill country, lakes, wildlife sanctuaries, and some of India's most intriguing rock carvings. It sits almost wrapped by Bangladesh, which gives it a different feel from the larger Himalayan states to the north.

The capital, Agartala, is the practical base and the place to start, especially for [Ujjayanta Palace](/asia/india/tripura/ujjayanta_palace). From there, the classic circuit runs to [Neermahal](/asia/india/tripura/neermahal), the lake palace at Rudrasagar; [Tripura Sundari Temple](/asia/india/tripura/tripura_sundari_temple) at Udaipur; and [Sepahijala Wildlife Sanctuary](/asia/india/tripura/sepahijala_wildlife_sanctuary). With more time, push north to [Unakoti](/asia/india/tripura/unakoti) or east into the [Jampui Hills](/asia/india/tripura/jampui_hills).

Tripura is not difficult in the way some remote northeastern routes can be, but it does require planning. Public transport exists, yet many of the best sights are scattered; a car and driver can turn a frustrating route into a good one. The reward is a state that still feels overlooked, even by travellers who think they know India well.

## Highlights in Tripura

- [Ujjayanta Palace](/asia/india/tripura/ujjayanta_palace) in Agartala
- [Neermahal](/asia/india/tripura/neermahal) on Rudrasagar Lake
- [Unakoti](/asia/india/tripura/unakoti) in the north
- [Jampui Hills](/asia/india/tripura/jampui_hills) near the Mizoram border
