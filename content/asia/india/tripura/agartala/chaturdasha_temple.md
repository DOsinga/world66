Chaturdasha Temple, or the Temple of Fourteen Gods, is one of Agartala's more important religious sites. It is closely associated with Kharchi Puja, when worship of the fourteen deities brings crowds and a strong festival atmosphere.

The temple is in Old Agartala, away from the main palace cluster. It is most rewarding if you are interested in Tripura's older royal and religious traditions.
