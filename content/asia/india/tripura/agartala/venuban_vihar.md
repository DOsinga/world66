Venuban Vihar is a small Buddhist temple in the Kunjaban area, north of Agartala's centre. It offers a quieter counterpoint to the palace and Hindu temple circuit.

It is not a long stop, but it is worthwhile if you want to see the city's Buddhist side or are already exploring northern Agartala.
