Boxanagar Ruins are a Buddhist archaeological site connected with the early medieval history of eastern Bengal and Tripura. Excavations have revealed the remains of monastic and temple structures, pointing to a period when Buddhist culture was active in this part of northeast India.

The site is best for visitors interested in archaeology and regional history rather than polished museum displays.
