Chaturdasha Temple at Khayerpur is dedicated to fourteen deities worshipped in Tripura's royal tradition. Its form is simple, but its cultural weight is large, especially during the Kharchi Puja festival.

The temple makes a worthwhile short trip from Agartala if you are interested in the older ritual world of the Manikya kingdom. It pairs naturally with Ujjayanta Palace and Kunjaban Palace.
