Debtamura, also called Chabimura, is a riverside rock-carving site in the Gomati district. Large panels of Hindu deities are cut into cliffs above the water, reached by boat when conditions allow.

It is one of Tripura's most atmospheric sights, but logistics can be uneven. Ask locally about boat availability, river levels, and road conditions before building a day around it.
