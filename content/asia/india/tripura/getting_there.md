---
title: Getting There
type: section
---

Agartala is the main gateway, with flights from Kolkata, Guwahati, and other Indian cities. Rail links now connect Tripura with the wider Indian network, though journeys from the rest of India are long.

Road travel within Tripura is usually by bus, shared vehicle, or hired car. Because many attractions sit outside towns, hiring a car for a day or two is often the most efficient way to see the state.
