Tripura Sundari Temple stands at Matabari near Udaipur, around 55 km from Agartala. It is one of Tripura's most important Hindu pilgrimage sites, dedicated to the goddess Tripura Sundari.

The shrine is compact but busy, especially during festivals. Go respectfully, and leave time for nearby Kalyan Sagar, the temple tank where devotees gather after worship.
