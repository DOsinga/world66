Bright End Corner is the easy scenic pause on Almora's western side. The view opens across layered hills, and in clear weather the high Himalayan line appears beyond them.

It is not a grand attraction, but it is exactly the kind of place Almora does well: a short walk or drive from town, a bench or parapet, and enough space to watch the light move across Kumaon.
