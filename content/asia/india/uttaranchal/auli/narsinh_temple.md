Narsinh Temple is one of Joshimath's key pilgrimage stops, dedicated to Vishnu in his Narasimha form. It is a compact, active temple rather than a grand monument, but it matters to travellers following the religious route through Joshimath before heading higher into Auli and the Garhwal Himalaya.
