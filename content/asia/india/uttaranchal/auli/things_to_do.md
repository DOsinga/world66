title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Auli is principally a ski resort in winter and a trekking base in summer. The gondola from Joshimath is the defining experience — one of Asia's longest cable cars, rising through oak and rhododendron forest to arrive at meadows with a wall of Himalayan peaks stretching across the horizon. Everything else is organised around those two activities and that one view.
