The Tibetan Buddhist monastery in Clement Town, on the southern edge of the city, is one of the most important seats of the Nyingma school in exile. The complex includes a working monastery, a school for young monks, and an enormous painted stupa that towers above the surrounding suburbs.

The site is open to visitors and free, though donations support the school. The terrace gardens around the stupa are quiet and well-kept; the late afternoon, when monks are chanting in the temple, is the best time.
