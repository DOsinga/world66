A relaxed restaurant on Rajpur Road serving a long mixed menu — North Indian, Chinese, Continental, all done competently. The dal makhani and butter chicken are the obvious choices; the kebab platter for two is generous and reasonably priced.

The outdoor garden seating, set back from the road, is the main draw — string lights, slow service, and beer that takes a while to arrive but does eventually. Bring patience and a friend.
