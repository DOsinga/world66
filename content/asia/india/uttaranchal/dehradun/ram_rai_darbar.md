Ram Rai Darbar is one of Dehradun's key historic religious sites, centred on the shrine associated with Guru Ram Rai. Its painted walls, courtyards, and festival traditions give the city an older layer that is easy to miss amid traffic, schools, and modern hill-station sprawl.

The complex is most rewarding when visited slowly, with time to look at the murals and the way daily worship still structures the place. Dress modestly and expect a functioning shrine rather than a museum.
