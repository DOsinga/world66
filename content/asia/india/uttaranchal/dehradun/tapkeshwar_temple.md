A small Shiva temple built into a natural cave above the Asan River, six kilometres north of the centre, where a steady drip of mineral-rich water from the limestone roof falls directly onto the central lingam. The name means "the dripping lord."

The cave is small and sometimes crowded, but the setting — a tumbling green stream, a footbridge, a string of stalls selling marigolds — is genuinely pleasant, especially after the monsoon. Easiest to reach by autorickshaw from the Clock Tower.
