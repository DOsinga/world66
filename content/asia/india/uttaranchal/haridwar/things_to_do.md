---
title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Haridwar's life revolves around the Ganges and the ghats. The most important thing you can do here is simply be present at Har Ki Pauri during the evening Ganga Aarti — everything else is secondary. Beyond that, the city offers a remarkable concentration of Hindu temples, ashrams, and pilgrimage sites within a compact, walkable area.
