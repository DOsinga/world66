Kanatal is a small hill station in Uttarakhand, perched at about 2,600 metres on the Chamba–Mussoorie highway, roughly 300 km (six to seven hours by road) from Delhi. It is a quiet place — barely a village — with views of the snow-covered Garhwal Himalayas and dense forests of deodar and oak.

The area is popular as a weekend escape from Delhi, especially in winter when snowfall between December and February transforms the landscape. In warmer months, the forests and meadows are good for walking, and the nearby [Surkanda Devi temple](/asia/india/uttaranchal/kanatal/surkanda_devi) offers a short trek with panoramic mountain views. The [Tehri Dam](/asia/india/uttaranchal/kanatal/tehri_lake) and its reservoir are within day-trip distance.

Kanatal has a handful of resorts and camps but remains low-key compared to Mussoorie or Shimla. That is much of its appeal.
