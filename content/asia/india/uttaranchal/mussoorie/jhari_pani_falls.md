Jhari Pani Falls is a small waterfall outside Mussoorie, reached by road toward Jharipani and then a short walk from the main road. The appeal is less the height of the falls than the easy change of pace from the town's busy mall.

Go after rain, when the water is stronger and the surrounding hills are greener. Wear shoes with grip, as the last stretch can be slippery.
