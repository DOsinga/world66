Eco Cave Garden is a small set of natural-style caves and rock passages on the hillside above Nainital. The attraction is simple: you duck, crawl, and squeeze through short themed caves named after animals, with a few viewpoints and garden paths between them.

It is best for families or anyone travelling with children who need a break from lake walks and viewpoints. Adults looking for a serious geological site may find it lightweight, but as an easy, playful hour in Nainital it earns its place.
