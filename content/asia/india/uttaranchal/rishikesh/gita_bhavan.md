Gita Bhavan is a religious complex in the Swarg Ashram area of Rishikesh, associated with Guru Shri Ram Sukh Daasji. It is best visited as part of a walk through the ashram district, where temples, riverfront ghats, and devotional gatherings give this side of the Ganges much of its character.
