---

Rishikesh organises itself around the Ganges and the ashrams along its banks. The evening [Ganga Aarti](/asia/india/uttaranchal/rishikesh/ganga_aarti) at Parmarth Niketan is the centrepiece of every visitor's day. The two famous bridges — [Laxman Jhula](/asia/india/uttaranchal/rishikesh/laxman_jhula) and [Ram Jhula](/asia/india/uttaranchal/rishikesh/ram_jhula) — are the connective tissue between the different parts of town.

The [Beatles Ashram](/asia/india/uttaranchal/rishikesh/beatles_ashram) is the most visited cultural sight. The [Neelkanth Mahadev Temple](/asia/india/uttaranchal/rishikesh/neelkanth_mahadev) rewards those willing to make the forest trek above town. [White-water rafting](/asia/india/uttaranchal/rishikesh/white_water_rafting) and [yoga](/asia/india/uttaranchal/rishikesh/yoga_centres) are the activities that bring most foreign visitors.
