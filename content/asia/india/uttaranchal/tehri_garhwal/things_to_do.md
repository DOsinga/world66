type: section
---

The district's most unusual sight is the [Tehri Lake](/asia/india/uttaranchal/tehri_garhwal/tehri_lake) — a vast reservoir created when the Tehri Dam flooded the original town, offering water sports and eerie glimpses of submerged structures at low water. The gateway town of [Rishikesh](/asia/india/uttaranchal/rishikesh) at the southern edge of the district is the starting point for both adventure sports and Hindu pilgrimage routes into the Garhwal Himalayas. The royal capital of [Narendra Nagar](/asia/india/uttaranchal/tehri_garhwal/narendra_nagar) sits on a ridge with views over the valley.
