The third Mughal emperor's tomb at Sikandra, 8 kilometres north-west of central Agra, is on a scale to match Akbar's own ambitions. The main mausoleum is a five-storey red sandstone and marble building set in a large *charbagh* garden, with four enormous gateways at the cardinal directions. The south gate is particularly fine, with marble inlay panels and four white minarets.

The interior tomb chamber, painted with floral and geometric motifs, holds Akbar's simple marble cenotaph; his actual grave is in a crypt below. The garden is famous for its blackbuck and resident troops of langur monkeys. It rarely sees the crowds of the Taj — well worth half a day.
