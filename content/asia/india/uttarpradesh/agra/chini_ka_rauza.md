The Chini ka Rauza is the tomb of Afzal Khan Shirazi, a Persian-born scholar and finance minister to Shah Jahan, who died in Lahore in 1639. The building's name — "China Tomb" — comes from the brilliant glazed tilework (*kashi kari*) that once covered its entire exterior. Most of the tiles have fallen away, but the surviving fragments hint at how astonishing the original must have looked.

It is a short walk from I'timad-ud-Daulah on the same eastern bank of the Yamuna. Few people visit; it is a quiet, slightly melancholy spot among the ruins of other Mughal monuments. The keeper will usually let you climb up to the terrace for a small tip.
