---
title: "Day Trips"
type: section
---

The big day trip is **Fatehpur Sikri**, 37 km west, easily combined with the Mughal capital ruins of **Bharatpur** and the **Keoladeo National Park** (a UNESCO wetland and one of India's best bird-watching sites, especially November to February) just across the Rajasthan border.

**Mathura and Vrindavan**, 50 km north, are sacred to the god Krishna and full of working temples, ghats, and pilgrims. The atmosphere is intensely Hindu — Vrindavan in particular is sometimes called the city of widows.

**Gwalior**, 120 km south, is a longer day or overnight trip — a magnificent hilltop fort, Jain rock-cut sculptures along the access road, and the haveli mansions of the Scindia dynasty. The Shatabdi express makes the run in 75 minutes.
