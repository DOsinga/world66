---
title: "Eating Out"
type: section
---

Agra's food is mostly north Indian Mughlai with a few local specialities — *bedai* (puffed deep-fried bread served with potato curry) for breakfast, *petha* (a translucent ash-gourd sweet that the city makes by the tonne and exports across India), and crisp savoury *namkeens* sold in shops around Hari Parbat.

The Tajganj and Fatehabad Road districts have the densest concentration of tourist-friendly restaurants. Sadar Bazaar, in the cantonment area, has more local options and a livelier evening crowd. As anywhere in India, ask for places by reputation, eat what you can see being cooked, and stick to bottled water.
