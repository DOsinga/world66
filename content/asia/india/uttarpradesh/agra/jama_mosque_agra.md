Agra's Jama Masjid, built by Shah Jahan in 1648 and dedicated to his favourite daughter Jahanara, stands directly opposite the Agra Fort. The mosque has a striking façade of red sandstone faced with white marble in zigzag courses, three large domes, and a wide open courtyard that can hold ten thousand worshippers.

Most visitors only see it from the fort. It is worth crossing the road: the interior tile and stone work is finer than the photographs suggest, and the surrounding **Kinari Bazaar** of jewellery, marble inlay, and embroidery shops is one of the liveliest neighbourhoods in old Agra. Modest dress; remove shoes at the entrance.
