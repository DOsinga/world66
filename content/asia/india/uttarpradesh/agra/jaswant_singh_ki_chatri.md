Jaswant Singh ki Chatri is a riverside cenotaph in Agra, built in a Rajput idiom that contrasts with the city's better-known Mughal tombs and forts. Its carved stone, domed canopy, and Yamuna setting make it a rewarding small stop for travelers who have time beyond the Taj Mahal circuit.

The monument is much quieter than Agra's headline sights, which is part of its value. Come for the architecture and the shift in perspective: Agra's heritage was never only imperial Mughal marble.
