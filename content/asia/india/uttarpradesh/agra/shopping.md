title: "Shopping"
type: section
---

Agra's signature crafts are **marble inlay work** (the same *parchin kari* technique used at the Taj — small boxes, table tops, chess sets), **leather** (the Agra cantonment has a long tradition of shoemaking), and **zardozi embroidery** in gold and silver thread. *Petha* sweets are an edible souvenir worth carrying home.

The **Kinari Bazaar** behind the Jama Masjid is the old shopping district; **Sadar Bazaar** in the Cantonment is more modern. The government-run **U.P. Handlooms** emporium on Mahatma Gandhi Road has fixed prices and reliable quality for those uncomfortable bargaining. Be wary of "marble factories" that touts will direct you to — much of what is sold as marble inlay is in fact made of soapstone.
