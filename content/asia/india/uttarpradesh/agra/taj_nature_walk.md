Taj Nature Walk gives Agra a rare pause from gates, crowds, and traffic. The trails run through scrub and riverine vegetation east of the Taj Mahal, with viewpoints that frame the monument from less familiar angles.

It is best early in the morning, when birds are active and the heat has not yet flattened the day. Do not expect wilderness, but do expect a useful breathing space and a different way to approach the Taj landscape.
