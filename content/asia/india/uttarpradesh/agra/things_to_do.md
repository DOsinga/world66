---
title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Agra's monuments are mostly Mughal and mostly within a 10-kilometre square either side of the Yamuna river. The five biggest — Taj Mahal, Agra Fort, Itimad-ud-Daulah, Akbar's Tomb at Sikandra, and Fatehpur Sikri — would fill two long days. Smaller monuments (Mehtab Bagh, Chini ka Rauza, the Tomb of Mariam, John Hessing's Tomb) reward a third. A combined entrance ticket gives a small discount on the major sites.

Most monuments are closed on Fridays except the Taj Mahal, which is closed *only* on Fridays. Plan accordingly. Sunrise at the Taj is the highlight of most visits; book your ticket online to skip the morning queue.
