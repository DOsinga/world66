Aligarh Muslim University is one of the oldest and most significant universities in South Asia, and its campus is the main reason a traveller would come to Aligarh. Founded in 1875 and granted university status in 1920, it now enrolls over 30,000 students across faculties of science, law, medicine, theology, arts and engineering.

The campus architecture is the immediate draw: red sandstone buildings in the Indo-Saracenic style blend Victorian Gothic with Mughal arches and decorative tile work. The **Jama Masjid** at the campus centre is an impressive mosque modelled loosely on the Jama Masjid in Delhi. **Sir Syed Hall** and the old Kennedy Hall are the most photographed buildings. The **Maulana Azad Library**, opened in 1960, is one of the largest university libraries in Asia. Visitors are welcome to walk the grounds and the main roads through campus; entering individual buildings typically requires prior permission.
