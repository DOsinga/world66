Anand Bhawan Museum tells the story of the Nehru family and the Indian independence movement. The collection includes personal correspondence, photographs, books, textiles, and material connected with early 20th-century political life.

Nearby Swaraj Bhawan adds more context, with video shows on the family and the freedom struggle.
