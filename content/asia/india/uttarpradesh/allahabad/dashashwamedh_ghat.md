Dashashwamedh Ghat is one of Prayagraj's smaller sacred riverfront stops, tied by tradition to a yajna performed by Brahma. It does not have the scale or drama of Triveni Sangam, but it adds another layer to the city's dense pilgrimage geography.

Come here as part of a wider circuit of ghats and temples rather than as a stand-alone sight. The interest is in seeing how myth, ritual and the river still shape ordinary corners of the city.
