Khusrau Mirza's Tomb is one of the main monuments in Khusro Bagh, the walled Mughal garden near Prayagraj Junction. The sandstone mausoleum holds the grave of Jahangir's eldest son, with a high dome, corner chhatris, and faded interior frescoes that reward a slow look rather than a quick photograph.
