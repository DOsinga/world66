Mayo Memorial and Town Hall is a 19th-century civic landmark built in memory of Lord Mayo, the Viceroy of India assassinated in 1872. The building is known for its tall tower, Gothic arches, decorative pillars, and large public hall, making it a useful stop for visitors interested in Allahabad's colonial-era architecture.
