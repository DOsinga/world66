Old Naini Bridge is a historic road and rail bridge across the Yamuna, linking Prayagraj with Naini. Its long steel structure is one of the older major bridges in the region and remains a useful landmark near the river.

It is best treated as part of a riverfront or Sangam-area outing rather than a standalone destination. The bridge's scale and age make it worth noticing, especially for travellers interested in engineering or colonial-era infrastructure.
