St. Joseph's Cathedral dates to 1879 and reflects Italian church architecture, with a Dominican-style bell tower and marble statues brought from Italy. It remains one of Prayagraj's notable Christian landmarks.

The cathedral compound also includes older Catholic institutional buildings, so it works best as part of a broader walk through the Civil Lines and university-era colonial quarter.
