---
title: "Getting Around"
type: section
---

**Delhi Metro** reaches the western side of the city — the Blue Line ends at Vaishali and Shaheed Sthal (New Bus Adda), the Red Line at Shaheed Sthal. The Rapid Rail (RRTS) has stations at Sahibabad, Ghaziabad, Guldhar and Duhai.

**Auto-rickshaws** are everywhere and the standard mode of transport for short hops. Insist on the meter or agree the fare first — around 100 rupees from the railway station to Indirapuram.

**Ola and Uber** work in the city and are usually cheaper than the auto-rickshaws if you do not feel like haggling.

**City buses** run on most main roads but are crowded and the routes are hard to figure out as a visitor.

The roads are heavy with traffic at all hours; allow plenty of time for anything in the late afternoon.
