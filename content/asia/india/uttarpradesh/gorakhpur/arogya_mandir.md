Arogya Mandir is a well-known naturopathy and Ayurvedic hospital and research centre near the Gorakhnath Temple complex. Founded in connection with the Nath tradition's long association with traditional medicine and yoga therapy, it attracts patients from across north India seeking treatment for chronic conditions through natural means — diet, yoga, herbal treatment, and fasting cures.

The centre is primarily a working medical institution rather than a tourist attraction, but it has become a point of interest for travellers curious about traditional Indian healing practices. If you have an interest in Ayurveda or yoga therapy beyond the commercial spa version, a visit here offers a more substantive glimpse into how these traditions are applied in a clinical setting.
