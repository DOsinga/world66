Neer Nikunj Water Park is Gorakhpur's main water-park option, with slides, pools, a wave pool, and a few simple amusement rides. It is most useful for families looking for a break from temples, markets, and city traffic.

Check opening hours before going, especially outside school holidays or the hottest months. The appeal is straightforward fun rather than sightseeing.
