St. John's Church is a small but worthwhile stop in Gorakhpur for travellers interested in the city's layered religious history. While Gorakhpur is better known for Gorakhnath Temple, Gita Press, and nearby Buddhist circuits, the church reflects the colonial and Christian presence that also shaped the railway-era city.

The visit is brief and quiet. Go respectfully, avoid interrupting services, and combine it with other central sights rather than making a long standalone journey. Its value lies in adding texture to Gorakhpur's map, where temples, presses, parks, and churches sit closer together than a quick glance suggests.
