---
title: When to Go
type: section
---

Jhansi sits on the Bundelkhand plateau at about 270 metres, and the climate is unforgiving in summer. From April through June the temperature regularly exceeds 45°C — not a good time to walk around fort ramparts in the sun. The monsoon arrives in July and brings relief, but also humidity and the occasional flooded road.

**October to March** is the window. November and December are the most comfortable: warm days, cool evenings, clear skies. January can be cold enough at night to need a jacket. The Orchha day trip is pleasant in this period too — the Betwa River is at a decent level from the monsoon runoff, and the monuments are not baking.

Jhansi hosts a significant **Jhansi Mahotsav** cultural festival in late February, usually around the anniversary of the 1857 revolt, with classical music, dance performances, and a light-and-sound show at the fort. Check local listings as dates shift year to year. The festival draws decent crowds, so book accommodation ahead if you plan to coincide.
