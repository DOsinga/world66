JK Temple, also known as Juggilal Kamlapat Temple, is a polished white temple complex built by the Singhania industrial family. Its architecture mixes modern materials with traditional Hindu temple forms: high shikharas, marble floors, open halls, and shrines dedicated to Radha-Krishna, Lakshmi-Narayan, Ardhanarishvara, Narmadeshwar, and Hanuman.

It is one of the cleanest and most visitor-friendly sights in Kanpur, especially in the evening when the complex is lit and local families come for a stroll. The temple is active, so dress and behave respectfully, but non-Hindu visitors are generally comfortable here.
