Kanpur Memorial Church is a red-brick Gothic church in the old cantonment, built in the late 19th century to commemorate British deaths during the 1857 Siege of Cawnpore. Its spire, stained glass, and memorial tablets are among the clearest surviving pieces of colonial Cawnpore.

The church is quiet and usually lightly visited. It rewards travellers interested in history more than casual sightseers, but it is one of the few places in Kanpur where the city's British past is still physically legible.
