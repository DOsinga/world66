Naveen Market is Kanpur's most convenient central shopping district, with clothing shops, footwear, snacks, small electronics, and everyday services packed into a walkable area. It is easier to handle than the older wholesale bazaars, especially if you are new to the city.

Come in the late afternoon when the market has warmed up but before evening traffic becomes impossible. Bargaining is normal in smaller shops, while branded stores are fixed-price.
