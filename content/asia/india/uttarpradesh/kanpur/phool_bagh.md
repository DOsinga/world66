Phool Bagh is the ceremonial garden at the heart of old Kanpur, a green square framed by colonial-era public buildings and busy roads. The Edward VII Memorial Hall on its edge now houses the Kanpur Sangrahalaya, so the park works as both a breathing space and a starting point for the city's civic history.

It is not manicured in the way a capital-city garden might be, but that is part of the appeal. Office workers, families, students, and street vendors all use the space, giving it the everyday texture of Kanpur rather than the feel of a preserved monument.
