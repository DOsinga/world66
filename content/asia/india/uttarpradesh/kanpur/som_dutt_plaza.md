Som Dutt Plaza is an older commercial complex near the Mall Road and Naveen Market area. It has small shops, offices, and local services rather than the polished feel of a new mall.

For travellers it is mainly useful as part of the central shopping circuit. Combine it with Naveen Market and the surrounding lanes if you need practical purchases or want to see ordinary middle-class Kanpur commerce at work.
