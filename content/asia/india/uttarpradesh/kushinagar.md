Kushinagar is one of the great Buddhist pilgrimage places, but it is quieter and more contemplative than its importance suggests. This is the town where the Buddha is traditionally believed to have entered parinirvana, the final passing beyond rebirth, and the main sights cluster around that memory.

The essential stop is the [Parinirvana Stupa and Temple](/asia/india/uttarpradesh/kushinagar/parinirvana_stupa), where a long reclining Buddha image lies inside a simple brick temple. Nearby, the [Ramabhar Stupa](/asia/india/uttarpradesh/kushinagar/ramabhar_stupa) marks the traditional cremation site, while [Matha Kuar Shrine](/asia/india/uttarpradesh/kushinagar/matha_kuar_shrine) and the [Thai Temple](/asia/india/uttarpradesh/kushinagar/thai_temple) round out the main pilgrimage circuit.

Most travellers reach Kushinagar from [Gorakhpur](/asia/india/uttarpradesh/gorakhpur), about 50 km west, and visit as a day trip. It deserves a slower pace if Buddhist history matters to you: early morning around the temple precincts is still, devotional, and far more rewarding than a hurried bus-stop visit.
