The Parinirvana Stupa and Temple are the reason pilgrims come to Kushinagar. The temple shelters a long reclining Buddha image, traditionally associated with the Buddha's final passing beyond the cycle of rebirth. Behind it rises the brick stupa, restored from the ruins of older Buddhist structures.

The site is simple, and that simplicity is its strength. There is no grand architectural display, just a quiet chamber, flowers, chanting, and pilgrims sitting in contemplation. Come early in the morning if you can; the atmosphere is far more powerful before the heat and tour groups arrive.
