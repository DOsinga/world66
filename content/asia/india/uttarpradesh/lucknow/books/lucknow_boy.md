Vinod Mehta — for decades editor of The Pioneer, The Sunday Observer, and finally Outlook magazine — grew up in 1950s and 1960s Lucknow and his 2011 memoir opens with a long and fond account of the city in the early decades after independence. Hazratganj, the convent schools, the Ganjing tradition, the slowly fading Nawabi half-world: Mehta describes all of it with a sharp, affectionate eye.

The political memoir that takes over the second half of the book is itself worth reading, but for Lucknow specifically the first hundred pages are gold.
