---
title: Day Trips
type: section
---

**Dudhwa National Park**, about five hours' drive north, is the closest major tiger reserve to Lucknow and one of the few places in India where you have a reasonable chance of seeing both tiger and rhinoceros in the same trip. **Nawabganj Bird Sanctuary**, an hour out on the road to Kanpur, is a manageable half-day for birders in winter.

For something nearer, **Bara Banki** (an hour east) has the small but atmospheric **Mahadeva temple** and the **Dewa Sharif** Sufi shrine. **Kakori**, on the western edge of the city, holds the **Kakori Conspiracy** memorial commemorating the 1925 train robbery by Indian revolutionaries. None of these are essential, but Lucknow's other major destinations — Agra, Varanasi, Allahabad — are far enough away to need an overnight rather than a day.
