title: Eating Out
type: section
---

Awadhi cuisine is the culinary high art of north India and Lucknow is its capital. The defining technique is **dum pukht** — slow-cooking in sealed pots over a low fire — and the resulting dishes are subtle, fragrant, and built around spice blends rather than chili heat. The signature plates are **dum biryani** (rice and meat layered and steamed), **galouti kebab** (minced lamb so tender it dissolves on the tongue), **nihari** (slow-cooked lamb shank stew, traditionally eaten at dawn with kulchas), and a generous range of **kormas** and **rogan josh**.

For the cheapest and most authentic meals head to the bazaars: **Tunday Kababi** at Akbari Gate in Chowk for the original galouti, **Idris ki Biryani** in Chowpatiya for the city's best biryani, **Rahim's Nahari Kulcha** in Akbari Gate for nihari at dawn, and the chaat stalls of Aminabad — especially **Royal Cafe** for **basket chaat**. For sit-down dining the better hotels do reliable Awadhi tasting menus.
