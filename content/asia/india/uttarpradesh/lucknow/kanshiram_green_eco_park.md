Manyawar Shri Kanshiram Ji Green Eco Park is part park, part political memorial, and part monumental landscape. Its broad paved axes, stone elephants, lawns, and large-scale forms belong to the same recent public architecture that reshaped several parts of Lucknow.

It is not subtle, but that is why it is interesting. Visit for the scale, the evening light on the stone, and the way contemporary memorial politics sit beside Lucknow's older Nawabi and colonial landmarks.
