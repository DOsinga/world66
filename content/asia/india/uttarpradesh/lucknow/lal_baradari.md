Lal Baradari is one of Lucknow's Nawabi-era buildings, a red-painted audience hall in the Qaiserbagh area. It does not have the scale of the Bara Imambara, but it belongs to the same world of court ceremony, Indo-Islamic design, and theatrical urban space.

Look for it while exploring the older heart of Lucknow rather than as a standalone expedition. The building helps connect the city's better-known monuments into a broader story of rulers, gardens, processions, and the refined culture that still shapes Lucknow's self-image.
