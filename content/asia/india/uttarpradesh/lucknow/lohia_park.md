Dr. Ram Manohar Lohia Park is one of Gomti Nagar's big planned green spaces, laid out with long paths, lawns, water features, and memorial architecture rather than the tight garden style of older Lucknow parks. It is not the city's deepest historical sight, but it gives a useful break from traffic and a look at the modern side of Lucknow's civic landscape.

Come early or near sunset, when walkers and families make the park feel lived-in without the midday heat. It works best as a slow hour between heavier sightseeing stops, especially if you are staying in Gomti Nagar or crossing the city toward the riverfront.
