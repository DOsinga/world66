title: Shopping

---

Lucknow's signature export is **chikankari** — the elegant, almost always white-on-white shadow embroidery on cotton or muslin that has been produced here continuously since at least the Mughal period. The trade is centred on **Aminabad** and the surrounding lanes, with hundreds of small workshops and shops at every price point. **Sewa Chikan** in Aminabad is a fair-trade cooperative selling work done by women artisans; **Lucknow Chikan Bhandar** is the long-established traditional name; the lanes of **Chowk** carry cheaper machine-made versions you should learn to spot.

Beyond chikankari, Lucknow is known for **zardozi** (gold-thread embroidery used on wedding clothes and shoes), **attar** and **ittar** (alcohol-free traditional perfumes, with shops clustered in Akbari Gate near the Imambara), and Awadhi sweets, especially **malai paan** and **chandrakala**. For everyday browsing **Hazratganj** carries the chain stores and bookshops; the old bazaars carry everything else.
