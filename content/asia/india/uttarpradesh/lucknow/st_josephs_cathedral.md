St. Joseph's Cathedral is a large Roman Catholic church in central Lucknow, close to Hazratganj. It is an active parish rather than a museum piece, but its scale makes it one of the city's more visible Christian landmarks.

The church is busiest around Christmas, when services and celebrations draw big crowds. Visit quietly outside service times if you mainly want to see the building.
