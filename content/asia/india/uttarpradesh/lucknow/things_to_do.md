---
title: Things to Do
type: section
---

Lucknow's headline sights are the great Nawabi monuments north of the river: the **Bara Imambara** with its bewildering Bhool Bhulaiya labyrinth, the **Chota Imambara** with its silvered domes, the gateway of the **Rumi Darwaza**, and the **Husainabad Clock Tower**. Together with the **Jama Masjid** and the half-ruined **Sat Khanda** they form a half-day's walking circuit through the old city around Hussainabad.

A second cluster of sights traces the British half of Lucknow's history: the burned-out **Residency**, where the 1857 siege played out; the gardens at **Sikandar Bagh**; the Gothic eccentricity of **La Martinière College**; and the modest old British cantonment around it. Add the modern monuments — the vast pink **Ambedkar Memorial Park**, **Janeshwar Mishra Park**, and the **Indira Gandhi Planetarium** — and you have several full days of wandering before you even reach the bazaars of **Chowk** and **Aminabad**.
