Tunday Kababi is the most famous food name in Lucknow. The original shop is a tiny, smoke-filled hole-in-the-wall in Akbari Gate in Chowk; the family now runs several other branches around the city, but the queues at the original location are testimony to its reputation. The signature dish is the **buffalo galouti kebab** — minced and pounded so finely that the patty melts on contact with the tongue.

Eaten with the very thin, almost translucent **paratha** for which the place is also known, the kebabs cost almost nothing. Go early — the original branch starts at around noon and closes when it runs out — and be ready for a chaotic queue, indifferent service, and one of the best plates of meat anywhere in India. Vegetarians need to look elsewhere.
