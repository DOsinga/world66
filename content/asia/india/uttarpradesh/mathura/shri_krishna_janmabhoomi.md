Shri Krishna Janmabhoomi is the centre of Mathura's pilgrimage map, marking the place traditionally revered as Krishna's birthplace. The complex is busy, heavily secured, and emotionally charged, especially around Janmashtami.

Treat it as a living shrine rather than an architectural stop. Phones and bags may be restricted, queues can be slow, and the best visit is one where you allow enough time to move at the pace of the devotees around you.
