Mathura's essential route runs from [Shri Krishna Janmabhoomi](/asia/india/uttarpradesh/mathura/shri_krishna_janmabhoomi) through the old city to [Dwarkadhish Temple](/asia/india/uttarpradesh/mathura/dwarkadhish_temple), then down to [Vishram Ghat](/asia/india/uttarpradesh/mathura/vishram_ghat) for the Yamuna and evening aarti. Start early if you want the temples before the lanes fill up.

The [Government Museum](/asia/india/uttarpradesh/mathura/government_museum) adds a quieter layer, with sculpture from the Mathura school that explains why the city mattered long before modern pilgrimage buses arrived.
