---
title: Getting There
type: section
---

Noida is best reached by the Delhi Metro. The Blue Line runs west to east across the city through eight stations from Noida Sector 15 to Noida Electronic City. From central Delhi (Connaught Place / Rajiv Chowk), the journey to Sector 18 takes about 45 minutes. The Magenta Line provides an alternative route from south Delhi via Botanical Garden interchange.

By road, the DND Flyway connects Noida directly to south Delhi, and the Noida-Greater Noida Expressway runs south to the airport at Jewar (under construction; scheduled to open in stages). Indira Gandhi International Airport in Delhi is currently the main option, about an hour to 90 minutes by car depending on traffic. Auto-rickshaws and Uber / Ola are widely available within the city.
