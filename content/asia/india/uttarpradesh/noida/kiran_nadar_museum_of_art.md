The Kiran Nadar Museum of Art is one of the best reasons to make a cultural stop in Noida. Its collection focuses on modern and contemporary Indian art, with major painters, photographers, sculptors, and installation artists represented in changing exhibitions rather than a static old-fashioned display.

It is useful for travellers who have already seen Delhi's monuments and want a sharper view of India's current art scene. Check the museum calendar before crossing town: talks, workshops, and special shows often make the visit much more rewarding than simply dropping in cold.
