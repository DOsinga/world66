Rishikesh sits at the foothills of the Himalayas where the Ganges emerges from the mountains into the plains, about 25 kilometres north of Haridwar. It is one of the most important pilgrimage centres in India and has also become one of the world's most popular destinations for yoga and meditation — the Beatles stayed here in 1968 at the [Beatles Ashram](/asia/india/uttarpradesh/rishikesh/beatles_ashram), an act that cemented the town's global reputation.

The two famous suspension bridges — [Laxman Jhula](/asia/india/uttarpradesh/rishikesh/laxman_jhula) and [Ram Jhula](/asia/india/uttarpradesh/rishikesh/ram_jhula) — cross the Ganges between the eastern and western banks. The ghats along the river are busy with sadhus, pilgrims, tourists, and the evening [Ganga Aarti](/asia/india/uttarpradesh/rishikesh/ganga_aarti) ceremony at [Parmarth Niketan](/asia/india/uttarpradesh/rishikesh/parmarth_niketan), which is worth attending. The river here runs cold and fast from the mountains, and [white-water rafting](/asia/india/uttarpradesh/rishikesh/white_water_rafting) on the rapids between Rishikesh and Shivpuri is popular.

[Yoga and meditation centres](/asia/india/uttarpradesh/rishikesh/yoga_centres) are everywhere, ranging from serious ashrams with structured programmes to less rigorous operations aimed at tourists. If you are coming specifically for yoga, research in advance — quality varies enormously.
