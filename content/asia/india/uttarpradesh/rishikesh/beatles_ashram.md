The Maharishi Mahesh Yogi ashram — better known as the Beatles Ashram — sits in a forested area along the Ganges east of town. The complex was abandoned after the Maharishi left India in 1968 and was taken over by the forest department. It reopened as a heritage site in 2015 and the ruins, covered in colourful graffiti murals, have become the most photographed destination in Rishikesh.

The meditation cells where the Beatles practiced are still intact — small round huts in the forest. The main lecture hall, guest accommodation, and kitchen buildings are all accessible. A local guide (easily hired at the entrance) can tell you which room each Beatle stayed in and describe the routines of ashram life during their visit. The site has an unexpectedly atmospheric quality — nature reclaiming a concrete campus — that makes it more compelling than a functioning attraction would be.
