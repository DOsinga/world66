Parmarth Niketan is the largest ashram in Rishikesh, with accommodation for several hundred students and resident sadhus. It sits directly on the Ganges at one of the most atmospheric ghats in Uttarakhand — the fast cold river visible from the courtyard, the Himalayas upstream, the temple bells and incense constant throughout the day.

The ashram offers yoga and meditation classes, spiritual discourses, and longer residential programmes. Drop-in visitors are welcome during daytime hours. The main reason most visitors come is the evening Ganga Aarti ceremony at the ghat in front of the ashram — one of the most atmospheric ritual events in India. Accommodation at the ashram is available; it books up well in advance during high season.
