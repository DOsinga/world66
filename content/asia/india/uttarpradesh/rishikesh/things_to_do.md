---
title: Things to Do
type: section
---

Rishikesh sits where the Ganges emerges from the Himalayas, and everything interesting here relates to that geography: the river, the ashrams along its banks, the white-water rapids, and the footbridge crossings that connect the two sides of town. The Beatles Ashram and the Ganga Aarti ceremony at Parmarth Niketan are the most visited sights; the white-water rafting is the most popular activity.
