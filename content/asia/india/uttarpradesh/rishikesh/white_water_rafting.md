The stretch of the Ganges between Rishikesh and the village of Shivpuri offers some of the most accessible white-water rafting in India. The rapids — graded Class II to Class IV depending on the section — run through a gorge of forested hills that feel remote from the ashram culture of Rishikesh town. The most popular stretch runs about 16 kilometres from Shivpuri to Neem Beach, taking about two to three hours.

Multiple operators in Rishikesh offer half-day and full-day rafting trips, with price and quality varying considerably. The safe season is generally from September to June; avoid the monsoon months (July-August) when the river runs dangerously high and rafting is prohibited. A basic package (boat, guide, paddles, safety equipment) typically costs around ₹600-1000 per person. Book through established operators rather than unlicensed touts who approach you near the bridges.
