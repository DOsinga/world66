Rishikesh has been a centre of yoga and Vedantic study since the late nineteenth century, long before international visitors arrived. The town now has hundreds of yoga schools, ranging from serious institutions with multi-week teacher training programmes to drop-in classes aimed at passing tourists. Quality and approach vary enormously.

The most established schools include Sivananda Ashram (founded 1936 by Swami Sivananda, runs structured residential programmes), Yoga Niketan (traditional Hatha yoga, relatively affordable residential stays), and the Yoga Study Centre at Parmarth Niketan. The main yoga and ashram area is concentrated around Ram Jhula and Swarg Ashram on the eastern bank. A teacher training course (200-hour certification) typically runs three to four weeks; intensive residential packages cost from around ₹30,000-80,000 depending on accommodation and lineage. Day classes for visitors cost ₹300-600.
