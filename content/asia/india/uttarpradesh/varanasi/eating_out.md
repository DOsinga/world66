---
title: "Eating Out"
type: section
---

Varanasi has a long tradition of vegetarian street food. The lanes around the ghats and the Vishwanath Temple area are densely packed with stalls and small restaurants, and the quality is generally high.

**Kashi Chat Bhandar** (Godaulia Chowk) — Varanasi's most famous chaat stall, packed from morning to night. The dahi puri and tamatar chaat (tomato-based) are the things to order. Standing room only at peak hours.

**Deena Chat Bhandar** (Vishwanath Lane) — A strong rival for best chaat in the city. Try the papdi chaat and the litti chokha (a Bihari specialty of roasted wheat balls with spiced vegetable mash).

**Blue Lassi Shop** (near Manikarnika Ghat) — Famous for its thick lassis in dozens of flavors, topped with cream and dried fruit. Iconic, touristy, and genuinely excellent.

**Keshari Restaurant** (Dashashwamedh Road) — Reliable thali restaurant popular with pilgrims and travellers. The Banarasi thali comes with dal, sabzi, rice, roti, and kheer.
