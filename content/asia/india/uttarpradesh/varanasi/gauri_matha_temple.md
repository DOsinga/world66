Gauri Matha Temple is tied to a local Kashi pilgrimage custom: devotees visit before leaving the city, offering shells and asking that the merit of the pilgrimage be completed properly.

It is a small, ritual-focused stop best understood as part of Varanasi's living religious geography rather than as a major architectural sight.
