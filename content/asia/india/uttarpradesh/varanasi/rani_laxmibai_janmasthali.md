Rani Laxmibai Janmasthali marks the birthplace of Rani Laxmibai, one of the best-known figures of the 1857 uprising. The small gallery is centred on a statue of her on horseback, with wall displays tracing her life.

Most written material is in Hindi, but the site is still a useful stop for visitors following Varanasi's lesser-known historic places.
