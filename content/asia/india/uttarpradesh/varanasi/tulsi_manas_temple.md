Tulsi Manas Temple is a modern marble temple with a literary soul, dedicated to the poet-saint Tulsidas and the Ramcharitmanas, his Awadhi retelling of the Ramayana. Verses are inscribed on the walls, so the building works as both shrine and text.

It is calmer than the ghats and easier to visit than some of Varanasi's most crowded sacred sites. Combine it with Durga Temple, Sankat Mochan, or a wider southern Varanasi circuit. The appeal is not age but devotion expressed through language, memory, and polished white stone.
