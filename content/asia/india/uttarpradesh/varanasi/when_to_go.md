---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

**October to March** is the best time to visit. Temperatures are comfortable (15–25°C by day), the air is clearer, and the ghats are at their most photogenic. The Dev Deepawali festival in November (on the full moon after Diwali) is spectacular — every ghat is lit with earthen lamps and the entire riverbank glows.

**April to June** brings intense heat (40°C+), but pilgrim numbers remain high and the city never quiets. The Ganga Dussehra festival in May–June is worth timing a visit around.

**July to September** is monsoon season. The river rises dramatically and can flood the lower ghats entirely. Travel in the lanes becomes difficult. However, the humidity breaks the heat, and the atmosphere of a monsoon Varanasi is unlike anywhere else — the city turns inward, the ghats go quiet, and the smell of incense and river water intensifies.

**Avoid**: Holi weekend brings enormous crowds and some safety concerns for solo women travellers.
