Banke Bihari Temple is the shrine many pilgrims come to Vrindavan to see first. The deity is worshipped as a child form of Krishna, and darshan has its own rhythm: curtains open and close repeatedly, breaking the gaze between devotee and image.

The surrounding lanes are tight, crowded, and full of shops selling flowers, sweets, and offerings. Go early if you want even a little space, keep valuables close, and ignore anyone who tries to turn a simple temple visit into an expensive ritual package.
