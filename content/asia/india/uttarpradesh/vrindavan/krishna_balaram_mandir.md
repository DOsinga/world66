Krishna Balaram Mandir is Vrindavan's best-known ISKCON temple and one of the easiest places for foreign visitors to understand the town's devotional life. The compound is orderly, multilingual, and built around regular kirtan, worship, and prasad.

It lacks the age and raw crush of the old-town temples, but that is also its usefulness. Come here to see global Krishna devotion meeting Braj pilgrimage culture in a form that is clear, active, and welcoming.
