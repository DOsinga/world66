Prem Mandir is modern Vrindavan at full scale: white marble, carved devotional scenes, broad grounds, and an evening light display that draws large crowds. It was completed in 2012, so do not come expecting antiquity.

Its value is theatrical rather than subtle. Visit after dark if you want to see why it has become one of the town's most popular contemporary temples.
