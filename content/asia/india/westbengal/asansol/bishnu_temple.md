Bishnu Temple is a small stone temple near Asansol, set on a raised platform with a Bengal hut-shaped roof and arched entrance. It dates to around the 14th century and is protected by the Archaeological Survey of India.

The temple is still used for worship, with a Narayan shila serving as the deity. Come for the compact medieval architecture rather than a long visit.
