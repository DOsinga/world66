Mazhar of Pir Baharam is a small but history-rich tomb complex in Bardhaman. It includes the grave of the Sufi saint Pir Bahram Sakka, along with the tombs of Sher Afghan Khan and Qutubuddin Koka, who died fighting each other in 1610.
