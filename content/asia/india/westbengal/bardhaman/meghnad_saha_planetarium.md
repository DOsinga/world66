Meghnad Saha Planetarium is a small science attraction in Bardhaman, named for the Indian astrophysicist Meghnad Saha. It opened in 1994 with instruments donated from Japan and runs short astronomy shows when there are enough visitors.

It is most useful for families or anyone with a science interest, especially when combined with nearby Golapbag and other sights on the university side of town.
