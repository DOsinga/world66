---
title: Getting There
type: section
---

Berhampore is on the rail line from Kolkata's Sealdah station toward Lalgola. Berhampore Court and Khagraghat Road are the useful stations, depending on where you are staying and which side of the river you need.

Road travel from Kolkata is possible but slow. For a short trip, the train is usually the simpler choice.
