Residency Cemetery at Babulbona is a modest stop, but it helps explain Berhampore's colonial layer. The graves belong to the old military and administrative world that grew around the British station here.

Do not expect a manicured heritage attraction. Come if you are already following the river-port and colonial history of the district, and treat the place with the quiet it deserves.
