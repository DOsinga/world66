Lalji Temple, also known as Radha Laljiu Temple, was built by the Malla king Bir Singha in 1658. Its laterite stonework and single-pinnacled form make it one of the stronger stops on Bishnupur's temple circuit, close to other major sites such as Jorbangla, Shyamrai, and Rasmancha.
