Madangopal Temple is one of Bishnupur's laterite temple sights, built in 1665 by Shrimoni Devi, wife of Bir Singha. Its pancharatna, or five-pinnacled, form makes it a worthwhile addition to a walk between the town's better-known terracotta temples.
