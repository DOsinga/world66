Murli Mohan Temple is a 17th-century laterite temple east of Madanmohan Temple. Built in the ekaratna style, it stands on a raised platform and belongs to Bishnupur's rich group of Malla-period temples.

It is a worthwhile stop while tracing the town's terracotta and laterite temple circuit, especially for visitors comparing Bishnupur's different temple forms.
