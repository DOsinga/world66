Radha Vinod Temple is a small 17th-century brick temple west of Madanmohan Temple. Built in 1659, it follows the at-chala form, with an eight-sloped roof and arched entrances on the eastern and southern sides.

The terracotta decoration is worn, but enough remains to make it a worthwhile stop while exploring Bishnupur's temple cluster.
