Radhagovinda Temple is one of Bishnupur's laterite and brick temples, built in 1729 by Krishna Singh. It follows the single-pinnacle ek-ratna form and sits near the Jor Mandir complex, making it easy to include on a temple walk through town.

Look for the dedicatory inscription on the southern face and the small chariot-shaped brick structure at the southeastern corner. The temple is less famous than Rasmancha or Jorbangla, but it helps fill in the variety of Bishnupur's Malla-period architecture.
