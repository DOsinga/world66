Adyapeath Temple is a major devotional complex in Baranagar, dedicated to the goddess Adya. Its three-pinnacled form is striking, and the temple draws steady local worship rather than feeling like a preserved monument.

The temple's modern history is tied to Annada Thakur and the contested story of an idol said to have been recovered from Eden Gardens. You do not need to follow every detail of the controversy to visit, but it explains why the shrine has such a strong place in local religious life.
