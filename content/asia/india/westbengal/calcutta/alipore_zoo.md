Alipore Zoo is Kolkata's long-running zoological garden and one of the best-known family attractions in the city. Its grounds include lakes that draw migratory birds, and the zoo is tied to several stories from Indian wildlife history, including the long-lived Aldabra giant tortoise Advaita.
