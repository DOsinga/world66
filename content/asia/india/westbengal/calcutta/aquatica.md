Aquatica is a large water park on Kolkata's eastern edge, useful when the heat makes a full day of sightseeing feel ambitious. It has wave pools, slides, racing rides, and splash areas aimed at families and groups.

It is not a cultural stop, but it can be a practical break for travellers with children or anyone who wants a simple day of pools and rides.
