The Asiatic Society is one of Kolkata's great scholarly institutions, with a historic library and collections that range from manuscripts and images to early sculpture and inscriptions. It is a quieter stop than the nearby Indian Museum, but worthwhile for visitors interested in Bengal's role in Asian studies, archaeology, and colonial-era scholarship.
