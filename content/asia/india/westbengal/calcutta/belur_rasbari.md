Belur Rasbari is a riverfront temple complex built by Purna Chandra Daw in 1790. The complex includes a navaratna temple, six Shiva temples, and a riverside ghat.

It is a quieter heritage stop north of central Kolkata, best for visitors already exploring the Hooghly riverfront or the older temple sites around Belur and Bally.
