Birla Industrial & Technological Museum is a hands-on science and technology museum, opened in 1959 as one of Asia's first popular science museums. Its displays cover industrial machinery, communications, physics, and astronomy, with interactive exhibits that make it especially useful for families and anyone travelling with children.
