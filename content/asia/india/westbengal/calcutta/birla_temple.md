Birla Temple is one of Kolkata's most polished modern temples, built in pale stone with ornate carving and a calm, well-kept setting. It is dedicated to Radha and Krishna and stands out for its scale and finish rather than age.

The temple is most atmospheric around evening prayers, when the lighting and devotional music give the complex more life.
