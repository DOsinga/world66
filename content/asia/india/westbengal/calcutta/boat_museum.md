The Boat Museum presents Bengal's boat heritage through wooden models, from Sundarbans cargo boats and coastal fishing craft to flat-bottomed boats from North Bengal. The building also holds puppet, kantha, and tribal-artifact displays, making it a compact cultural museum stop.
