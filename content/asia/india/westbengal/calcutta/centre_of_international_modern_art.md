The Centre of International Modern Art is a small gallery focused on modern and contemporary art. Its programme changes through the year, with exhibitions that often lean toward Indian modern art and Kolkata's wider art scene.

It is a better stop for visitors already interested in galleries than for a first-time sightseeing route, but it adds useful variety beyond Kolkata's colonial monuments and large museums.
