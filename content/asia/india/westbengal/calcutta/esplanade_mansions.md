Esplanade Mansions is an Art Nouveau landmark from Calcutta's colonial commercial age, once connected with the Jewish businessman David Elias Ezra. The building is now used for offices, so the interest is mostly from the street: look for its ornate facade while walking around Chowringhee and Esplanade.
