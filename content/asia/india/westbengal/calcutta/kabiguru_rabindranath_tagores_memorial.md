Kabiguru Rabindranath Tagore's Memorial is a quiet Hooghly riverfront stop in north Kolkata, dedicated to Bengal's great poet and Nobel laureate. It is modest as a monument, but the setting gives it a lift.

Come for the river view as much as the memorial itself. From here the Hooghly and Howrah Bridge frame the scene, especially in the softer light at the beginning or end of the day.
