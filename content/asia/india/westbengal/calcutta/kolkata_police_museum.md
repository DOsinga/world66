Kolkata Police Museum is a small history museum in north Kolkata, covering the city's police force and its role through colonial and modern periods. Displays include documents, photographs, weapons, and material linked to major cases and political movements.

It is a niche stop, but useful if you are already exploring Bagbazar, College Street, or the older neighbourhoods north of central Kolkata.
