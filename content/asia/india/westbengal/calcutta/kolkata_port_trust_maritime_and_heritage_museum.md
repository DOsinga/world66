The Kolkata Port Trust Maritime and Heritage Museum tells the story of the port that helped shape colonial and modern Kolkata. Models of ships, port equipment, and archival displays show how river trade connected the city to the wider world.

It is a compact, specialist museum, best paired with a walk through BBD Bagh or the riverside. Go for context rather than spectacle.
