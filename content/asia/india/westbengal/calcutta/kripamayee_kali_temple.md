Kripamayee Kali Temple is a navaratna, or nine-pinnacled, temple in Baranagar dedicated to Kali as Kripamayee, "she who is merciful." Its roofline makes it a useful stop for visitors interested in Bengal temple architecture.

The temple complex also includes twelve shrines dedicated to Shiva, giving the site a compact but layered sacred layout.
