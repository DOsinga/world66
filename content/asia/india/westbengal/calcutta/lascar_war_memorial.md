The Lascar War Memorial commemorates the Indian sailors who died while serving on Royal Navy and British merchant ships during World War I. Its Indo-Islamic design makes it one of Kolkata's more distinctive military memorials.

It is a short, worthwhile stop for people exploring the Maidan and the colonial-era monuments around the Hooghly.
