Maa Phire Elo is a museum dedicated to Durga Puja idols and the craft culture around Kolkata's biggest festival. It is most rewarding for visitors who want to understand the artistry that usually appears only during the festival season.

The museum works as a focused cultural stop in south Kolkata, especially for anyone interested in Durga Puja, sculpture, or contemporary Bengali ritual art.
