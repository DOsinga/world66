Magen David Synagogue is one of Kolkata's most striking Jewish landmarks, built in 1884 in a Victorian style with a tall clock tower and ornate interior. It reflects the history of the city's Baghdadi Jewish community and is worth pairing with the nearby old markets and heritage streets north of B.B.D. Bagh.
