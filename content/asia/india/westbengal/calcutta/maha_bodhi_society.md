The Maha Bodhi Society of India occupies a historic building in central Kolkata and is a quiet Theravada Buddhist stop amid the city's busier streets. The upper floor includes a shrine room and library.

It is a modest visit rather than a major monument, but worthwhile for travellers interested in Kolkata's religious mix or the modern Buddhist revival movement.
