Marble Palace is a 19th-century mansion built by Raja Rajendra Mullick and now run as a private museum. Its rooms hold an eccentric collection of paintings, sculpture, furniture, and decorative objects, while the garden and Palladian-style mansion make it one of north Kolkata's more unusual historic-house visits.
