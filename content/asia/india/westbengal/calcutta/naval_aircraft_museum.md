The Naval Aircraft Museum is an open-air aviation museum on Kolkata's eastern side. Its centrepiece is a retired Tupolev Tu-142 anti-submarine aircraft from the Indian Navy, backed by models and displays explaining aircraft parts and design. It is a compact stop, but a good one for families or anyone interested in military aviation.
