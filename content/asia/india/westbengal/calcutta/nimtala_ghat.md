Nimtala Ghat is one of Kolkata's most important cremation ghats and a deeply sacred place for Hindus. Visit with restraint: it is an active ritual site, not a sightseeing set piece, but its religious importance makes it one of the significant places along the Hooghly in north Kolkata.
