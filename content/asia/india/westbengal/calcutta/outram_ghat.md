Outram Ghat is one of Kolkata's livelier riverfront stretches, with views across the Hooghly toward Vidyasagar Setu. It is used for bathing and religious rituals, but also works as a place to stroll, eat something simple, or watch boats move along the water.

The setting is atmospheric rather than polished. Watch for trains along the circular rail track, and consider a short country-boat ride if the river is calm.
