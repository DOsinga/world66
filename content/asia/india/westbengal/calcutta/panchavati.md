Panchavati is a small sacred grove associated with Ramakrishna's spiritual practice in the 1860s. Its five trees, including banyan, peepal, neem, amla, and wood apple, make it a quiet stop for visitors interested in the religious history around Dakshineswar and north Kolkata.
