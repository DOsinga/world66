Pathbari Temple in Baranagar is associated with Chaitanya Mahaprabhu, who is said to have stopped here on his way to Puri in the early 16th century. It is a modest but meaningful Vaishnavite site, mainly worth seeking out if you are interested in Bengal's devotional history or are already exploring north Kolkata.
