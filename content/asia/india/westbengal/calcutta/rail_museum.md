The Rail Museum near Howrah Station covers the railway history of eastern India, with a focus on the station and the locomotives that shaped travel in and out of Kolkata. Its collection includes historic electric and steam locomotives, shunting engines, and other railway equipment.

It is a good short stop for railway enthusiasts or families already crossing the river to Howrah, and it gives useful context for one of India's busiest railway hubs.
