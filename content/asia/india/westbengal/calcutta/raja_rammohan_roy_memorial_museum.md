The Raja Rammohan Roy Memorial Museum presents the life and work of the reformer Raja Rammohan Roy, a central figure in the Bengal Renaissance. The collection includes photographs, sketches, paintings, reproduced writings, period furniture, and dioramas that help place his work in its social and political setting.

It is a worthwhile stop for visitors following Kolkata's intellectual and reform history, especially when combined with nearby north Kolkata heritage sites.
