The Ramakrishna Mission Institute of Culture is a quiet cultural stop with collections tied to Bengal's folk art and intellectual life. Exhibits include textiles, patachitra paintings, terracotta pieces, manuscripts, sculptures, coins, and work by Bengali artists.

It is not a blockbuster museum, but it rewards travellers interested in Kolkata's cultural and religious history, especially beyond the better-known colonial sights around the Maidan.
