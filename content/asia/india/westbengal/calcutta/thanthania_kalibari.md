Thanthania Kalibari is one of Kolkata's older Kali temples and remains an active place of worship. It draws large crowds during Kali Puja, Kaushiki Amavasya, and Phalaharini Amavasya, when the rituals are at their most intense. The temple is a worthwhile stop for visitors interested in Kolkata's living religious traditions.
