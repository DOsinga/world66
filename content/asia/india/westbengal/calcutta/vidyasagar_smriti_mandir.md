Vidyasagar Smriti Mandir preserves the former home of Ishwar Chandra Vidyasagar, the Bengali reformer, educator, and writer. It is a small, specialist stop, but worthwhile for visitors interested in the Bengal Renaissance and Kolkata's 19th-century intellectual history.
