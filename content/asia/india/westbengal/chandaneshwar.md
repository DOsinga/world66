Chandaneshwar is a small temple village just across the Odisha border from West Bengal's Digha-Shankarpur coast. Travellers usually come as a short excursion from [Digha](/asia/india/westbengal/digha) or [Shankarpur](/asia/india/westbengal/shankarpur), not as a destination in its own right.

The focus is the [Chandaneswar Shiva Temple](/asia/india/westbengal/chandaneshwar/chandaneswar_shiva_temple), an active pilgrimage shrine that draws devotees from both Odisha and West Bengal. The surrounding village is simple: flower sellers, prasad stalls, pilgrims, auto-rickshaws, and the usual border-town bustle.

Chandaneshwar is best paired with the coast. [Talasari Beach](/asia/india/westbengal/chandaneshwar/talasari_beach) is close by, with tidal flats and fishing boats, while Digha's hotels and transport make the practical base. Come for the temple atmosphere, then move on; there is little reason to overnight here unless you are attending a festival.
