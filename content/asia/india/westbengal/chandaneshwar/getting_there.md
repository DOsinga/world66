---
title: Getting There
type: section
---

Chandaneshwar is about 8 km from Digha and a little farther from Shankarpur. Auto-rickshaws, hired cars, and local buses make the short run across the state border.

Digha is the practical rail and bus gateway, with trains from Howrah and buses from Kolkata. From Digha station or the beach area, negotiate an auto-rickshaw fare before you start.
