Udaypur Beach sits between Digha and Talasari, close enough to the border that it is often folded into the same short excursion. It is less built-up than central Digha and has a looser, more local feel, with beach shacks and fishing activity depending on the season.

Do not expect a pristine tropical beach. The appeal is space, quieter sand, and a break from Digha's crowds. Swimming conditions vary with tide and weather, so follow local advice before going in.
