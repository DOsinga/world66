Baradebi Bari is a Durga temple in Cooch Behar, notable for using European architectural forms rather than the more familiar temple styles of Bengal. That contrast makes it a worthwhile stop for visitors already exploring the palace and other heritage sights in town.

The temple is especially active during Durga Puja, when it hosts a large fair and draws more local life than on an ordinary day.
