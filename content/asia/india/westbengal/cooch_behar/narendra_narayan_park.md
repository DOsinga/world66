Narendra Narayan Park is a family park in Cooch Behar with gardens, ponds, children's play areas, and a small toy train. It is a relaxed local stop rather than a major sight, but it works well for travellers with children or anyone needing a quiet break near the centre.

Pair it with Cooch Behar Palace and Sagardighi for an easy circuit through the town's main public spaces.
