The Rail Museum is a modest but useful stop if you are interested in how the old Cooch Behar State Railway connected this corner of Bengal to Assam and the Dooars. The building deliberately echoes the local temple style, and the exhibits cover railway life, technology, and regional history.

It is small, so treat it as a short add-on rather than a full museum afternoon.
