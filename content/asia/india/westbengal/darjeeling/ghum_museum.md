Ghum Museum is a compact stop at Ghum station, one of the high points of the Darjeeling Himalayan Railway. Its exhibits and old railway material help explain why the toy train is more than a scenic ride: it is a working piece of hill engineering and colonial-era transport history.

The museum is modest, so it works best when paired with a toy-train ride or a visit to nearby Ghoom Monastery and Batasia Loop. Railway enthusiasts will get the most from it, but even casual visitors gain context for Darjeeling's most famous line.
