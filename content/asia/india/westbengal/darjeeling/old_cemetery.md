Darjeeling's Old Cemetery is a quiet, slightly out-of-the-way stop for travellers interested in the hill station's layered history. Its graves include figures tied to the town's colonial, scholarly, and Himalayan past.

The best-known grave is that of Alexander Csoma de Koros, the Hungarian scholar who became one of the early European students of Tibetan language and literature. The cemetery is more reflective than showy, and rewards a slow walk.
