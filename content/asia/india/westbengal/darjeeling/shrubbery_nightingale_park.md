Shrubbery Nightingale Park is a landscaped hillside park near central Darjeeling, with views across the surrounding slopes and a large statue of Lord Shiva. It is best treated as an easy evening stop rather than a main sight.

During the tourist season the park often hosts cultural performances, which are the main reason to time a visit later in the day.
