Amarabati Park is a landscaped family park in Digha, built around a small lake with gardens, paths, and simple amusements. It is not the reason to travel to Digha, but it gives beach-weary visitors a green, low-pressure place to pause.

The park is most useful for families, evening strolls, and a short break between beach visits. Keep expectations modest: the appeal is local leisure, shade, and a change of pace from the seafront.
