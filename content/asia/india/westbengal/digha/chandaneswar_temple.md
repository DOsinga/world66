Chandaneswar Temple is a Shiva temple just inland from the Digha coast, close to the Odisha border. For many Bengali visitors it is part of the standard Digha trip: beach time, seafood, and a short pilgrimage excursion to a temple that is especially busy during annual festivals.

The site is modest architecturally, but it is useful for understanding Digha as more than a resort strip. Expect crowds, vendors, and a practical devotional atmosphere rather than a quiet archaeological monument.
