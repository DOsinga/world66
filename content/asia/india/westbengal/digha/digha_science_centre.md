Digha Science Centre is a practical family stop in a beach town where weather and crowds can make indoor time valuable. The exhibits are aimed at accessible science learning, with demonstrations and displays that work best for children and school-age visitors.

It is not the reason to come to Digha, but it rounds out a trip that might otherwise be only beach, promenade, and temple. Keep it in reserve for the hottest part of the day or a wet afternoon.
