---
title: "Things to Do in Digha"
type: section
---

Digha is fundamentally a beach resort. The main activity is the beach itself — walking it at sunrise or sunset, sitting under the casuarina trees, and watching the fishing boats at work. Beyond the beach, the [Jagannath Temple](/asia/india/westbengal/digha/jagannath_temple) is the main religious site, while the Marine Aquarium and Research Centre gives a look at Bay of Bengal marine life. Day trips to [Shankarpur](/asia/india/westbengal/digha/shankarpur_beach) and Chandaneshwar add variety.
