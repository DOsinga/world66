---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

October through March is the best season for Digha. The weather is cool and dry, the sea is calm, and the light on the beach is good. December and January are the peak domestic tourist months — comfortable weather but weekend crowds can be overwhelming.

April through June brings increasing heat and humidity. The sea becomes rough and swimming is inadvisable by late May. The monsoon arrives in late June and runs through September: the sea is dangerous, most resorts empty out, and Digha is not worth visiting in this period. The rain and rough sea can be dramatic to observe, but basic facilities are reduced.

The busiest times regardless of weather are public holidays and school holidays in West Bengal — Durga Puja (October) and the winter school break bring particularly large crowds from Kolkata. Book accommodation well in advance if your travel dates coincide with these.
