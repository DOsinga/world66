Bhabani Pathak's Tilla is a local history and legend site in Durgapur, associated in popular memory with the rebel figure Bhabani Pathak. It is not a polished monument, but it carries the kind of regional folklore that gives the industrial city a deeper past.

Visit with modest expectations and an interest in local stories. The site is most worthwhile when paired with Durgapur's river, barrage, and older settlement traces, which together show that the city is not only steel plants and planned neighbourhoods.
