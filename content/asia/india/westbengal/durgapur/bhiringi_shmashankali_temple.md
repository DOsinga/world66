Bhiringi Shmashankali Temple is a Kali temple established in 1852. It is one of Durgapur's older religious sites and draws devotees from outside the city during Kali Puja.

The temple is a worthwhile stop for visitors interested in local worship and the older settlement history around Bhiringi.
