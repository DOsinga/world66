---
title: "Getting Around"
type: section
---

Durgapur is a sprawling planned city — the distances between areas are larger than they look on a map and walking between neighbourhoods is not practical. The station sits in the middle of the city with two exits: the main side for City Center and most of the new townships, and the Market side for Bidhan Nagar and the older ABL/FCI areas. Auto-rickshaws are available from both sides; taxis and shared minibuses mainly operate from the main exit.

**Minibuses** are the main form of public transport and run frequently across most of the city. Most routes pass through City Center. Fares are very low.

**Auto-rickshaws** are slightly cheaper than taxis and useful for shorter hops. The rates are usually negotiable or agreed in advance.

**Taxis** do not use meters — fixed rates by destination are displayed at taxi stands near the station. Car hire with a driver, available at stands around the city, is the most practical option for a day of sightseeing: rates are reasonable and you avoid navigating unfamiliar routes.

Durgapur has no airport. All intercity travel is by road or rail.
