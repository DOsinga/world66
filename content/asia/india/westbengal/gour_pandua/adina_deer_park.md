Adina Deer Park is a small wildlife sanctuary near the Adina Mosque area, with spotted deer, nilgai, birds, butterflies, and seasonal migratory birdlife. It offers a softer nature stop among Gour-Pandua's ruins and Islamic monuments.

It is most useful as a short family-friendly add-on while visiting Adina Mosque, rather than as a destination by itself.
