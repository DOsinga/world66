Ballal Bati is an archaeological site near the old monuments of Gour, excavated in the early 2000s. The name is often linked with Ballal Sen of the Sena dynasty, though interpretations of the site vary.

It is not as immediately impressive as Gour's gateways, mosques, and towers, but it adds depth for visitors interested in the older layers of Bengal's medieval history.
