Dhunichak Masjid is a brick-built mosque in the historic Gaud area, with six domes, arched entrances, octagonal corner turrets, and terracotta ornamentation. Its exact history is uncertain, but the building fits well into a day spent tracing the ruined mosques, gates, and monuments of Gour-Pandua.

It is best visited as part of a wider circuit rather than as a stand-alone trip.
