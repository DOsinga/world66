Eklakhi Mausoleum is a square brick tomb at Pandua, usually linked to Sultan Jalaluddin Muhammad Shah. Its name is said to refer to the cost of construction: one lakh rupees.

The building is compact but richly layered, with thick walls, a central dome, corner turrets, and terracotta ornament. It pairs naturally with nearby Qutb Shahi Mosque and the dargah complexes.
