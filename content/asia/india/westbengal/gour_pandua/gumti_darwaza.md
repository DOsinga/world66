Gumti Darwaza is a small domed gateway near Lukochuri Darwaza in the ruins of Gour. It probably served as a private eastern entrance, and traces of the colourful enamel tiles that once covered its exterior still survive. It is not a stand-alone trip, but it is worth pausing for while moving between Gour's larger mosques, gates, and tombs.
