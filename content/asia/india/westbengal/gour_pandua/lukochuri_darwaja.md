Lukochuri Darwaja, also called the Sahi Gate, stands near the Kadam Rasul Mosque in Gour. The royal gateway was probably built in the 17th century and still shows traces of the stucco and tile decoration that once covered it.

It is a good short stop while linking Gour's larger monuments, especially for visitors tracing the old citadel and palace area.
