Nimasarai Minar is a ruined octagonal tower between Gour and Pandua, traditionally linked to an old roadside inn and watch point. Only the lower portion survives, with projecting spikes and worn brickwork, but it adds a curious extra stop to the area's larger mosque and gateway circuit.
