Qutb Shahi Masjid, locally known as Chota Sona Masjid, is one of the important monuments of Pandua's old Islamic centre. It stands near the shrines associated with Nur Qutb-ul-Alam and Shah Jalal Tabrizi, making this small area one of the most layered parts of the site.

Visit it with Eklakhi Mausoleum and Adina Mosque to get a fuller sense of Pandua's ruined medieval landscape.
