Shahi Niamatullah Mazar is the tomb of Shah Niamatullah, who died in the 17th century and was buried in a structure built under Shah Shuja. The single-domed tomb has arched openings on all four sides and slender corner minarets.

It fits naturally into a circuit of the Gaur monuments, close to Tahkhana, Qadam Rasul Mosque, Firoz Minar, and Dakhil Darwaza.
