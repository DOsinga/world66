The Two Stone Pillars stand on the approach to Gour from Malda and are often the first remains visitors see before reaching the larger gates and mosques. Their carved forms echo details found at Baro Sona Masjid, linking them to the wider Bengal Sultanate landscape.

Treat them as a quick historical marker on a Gour circuit rather than a stand-alone destination. They help set the tone for the scattered ruins that follow.
