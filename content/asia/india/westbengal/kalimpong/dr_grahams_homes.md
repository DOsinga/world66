Dr. Graham's Homes is a boarding school on the slopes of Deolo Hill, established in 1900 by the Scottish missionary Dr. John Anderson Graham. The campus occupies a large area of hillside and contains a substantial collection of colonial-era buildings including a Gothic chapel, staff cottages, playing fields, and orchards. The grounds are generally open to visitors during the day, and the chapel is one of the more atmospheric buildings in Kalimpong.

The school continues to operate as a residential school, and the campus has the slightly time-stopped quality of many hill-station institutions — large trees, well-kept lawns, and the smell of damp stone. From the upper parts of the campus, the views towards the Teesta valley are good. It is best combined with a walk up to Deolo Hill, which rises directly above.
