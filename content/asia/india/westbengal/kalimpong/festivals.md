---
title: "Festivals"
type: section
---

The **Kalimpong Flower Festival** is held each October, coinciding with the peak bloom of gladioli and dahlias in the region's nurseries. Kalimpong produces around 80 percent of India's gladioli, and the festival draws visitors from across West Bengal and beyond. Nurseries open their displays, and the town market fills with cut flowers. The timing overlaps with post-monsoon clear weather, making it the best all-round week to visit.

**Losar**, the Tibetan New Year (usually February–March), is celebrated by the town's sizeable Tibetan and Bhutia communities. The monasteries hold ceremonies and mask dances; the Tharpa Choeling Gompa and Zong Dog Palri Fo-Brang Gompa are the main venues.

**Durga Puja** (October) and **Diwali** are celebrated by the Bengali Hindu community with street illuminations and community events.
