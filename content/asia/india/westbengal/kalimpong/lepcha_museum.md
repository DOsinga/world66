The Lepcha Museum is a small cultural museum rather than a large institutional attraction, but it has a clear purpose in Kalimpong. Its objects, manuscripts, clothing, and household material point toward the Lepcha people, whose history is central to the eastern Himalayan region but often invisible in quick hill-station itineraries.

The museum is best visited with patience and modest expectations. It will appeal most to travellers interested in local cultures, language, and the layers of identity behind Kalimpong's monasteries, schools, and mountain views.
