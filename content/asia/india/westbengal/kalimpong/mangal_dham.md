Mangal Dham is one of Kalimpong's most visible Hindu temple complexes, set away from the Buddhist monasteries that often dominate visitors' itineraries here. Its prayer halls, painted panels, and landscaped grounds are dedicated to Guruji Shri Mangal Das Ji Maharaj.

The complex is worth adding to a Kalimpong circuit because it broadens the town's religious picture. Visit respectfully, allow time to look at the devotional art, and pair it with the gompas and viewpoints to see how many strands of hill culture meet in this small town.
