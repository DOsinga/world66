Butterfly Garden is a small New Town attraction built around a planted dome and a circular water feature. Flowering plants draw a changing mix of butterflies, so it works best as a slow, close-up stop rather than a big set-piece sight.

It fits naturally with the other low-key family stops on Kolkata's eastern edge, especially if you are already visiting Eco Park or the nearby gardens.
