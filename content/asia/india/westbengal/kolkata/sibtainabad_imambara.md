Sibtainabad Imambara is one of Kolkata’s most evocative links with Wajid Ali Shah, the last Nawab of Awadh, who lived in exile in Metiabruz after the British annexed his kingdom. The complex includes his tomb and preserves a strand of Lucknowi culture that took root far from Lucknow.

It sits well outside the usual central Kolkata sightseeing loop, so it rewards travellers who are deliberately looking for layered, less obvious history. Go with time, modest dress, and patience for the journey; the context is as important as the architecture.
