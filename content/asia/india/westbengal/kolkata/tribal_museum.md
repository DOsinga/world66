The Tribal Museum presents material culture from several Indigenous communities in India, including the Great Andamanese, Khasi, Bhil, and Bhotiya peoples. Displays include masks, musical instruments, art, and other objects tied to daily life and ritual practice.

It is a focused museum rather than a large collection, but it is useful for visitors interested in India's tribal cultures.
