The obvious day trip from Krishnanagar is **Nabadwip**, 15 km to the south, the medieval Bengali capital and a major Vaishnava pilgrimage centre tied to the saint Sri Chaitanya. The two towns are commonly combined into a single overnight from Kolkata.
