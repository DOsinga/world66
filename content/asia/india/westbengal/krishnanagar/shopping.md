title: "Shopping"
type: section
---

The clay sculptures of [Ghurni](/asia/india/westbengal/krishnanagar/ghurni) are the main thing to buy in Krishnanagar. Many of the Patra workshops keep finished pieces for sale on shelves at the front; prices are negotiable and the artisans will pack carefully for travel.

Krishnanagar is also known for **sarpuria** and **sarbhaja**, two distinctive Bengali sweets made from the layered cream that forms on the surface of slowly reduced milk. Shops along the main bazaar sell them by weight; they travel for a day or two.
