Pareshnath Shiv Mandir is a small open temple near the reservoir, valued less for architectural grandeur than for its setting and local story. Stone images found during construction of the dam are kept here, and the site is linked in local telling with older Jain and Hindu layers of the area.

It pairs naturally with Musafirana Viewpoint. Together they make the dam circuit feel like more than a drive along water.
