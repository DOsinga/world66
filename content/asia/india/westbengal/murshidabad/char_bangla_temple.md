Char Bangla Temple is a group of four small terracotta temples near Baranagar, north of Murshidabad's main palace area. The richly worked brick panels show scenes from Hindu epics, everyday life, and floral designs, making the complex one of the stronger temple stops in the Murshidabad region.

It sits outside the main Hazarduari cluster, so it is best visited with local transport and combined with other sights on the northern side of town.
