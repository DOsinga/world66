The House of Jagat Seth was the home of the powerful banking family that handled money lending and finance in Murshidabad's nawabi period. The restored complex now works as a museum and also includes a Jain temple dedicated to Parshvanatha.

It is a useful stop for understanding the merchant wealth behind Murshidabad's palaces, mosques, and colonial-era political history.
