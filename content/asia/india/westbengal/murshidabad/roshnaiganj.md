Roshnaiganj holds the tomb of Nawab Shuja-ud-Din Muhammad Khan, one of the rulers who shaped Murshidabad's 18th-century court. The site is quiet rather than polished, but the mosque and tomb make a worthwhile stop for travellers following the old Nawabi history north of town.
