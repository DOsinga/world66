---
title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Most visitors start with Hazarduari Palace and the Nizamat Imambara, then work outward to Katra Masjid, Wasif Manzil, Khushbagh, and the smaller gates and cannons around the old nawab capital. The sights are close enough on a map, but heat and traffic make local transport worthwhile.
