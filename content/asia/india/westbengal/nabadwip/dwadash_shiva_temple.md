Dwadash Shiva Temple is an old Shiva temple in Nabadwip, built in 1835. Its eight-chala roof form and terracotta ornament make it a worthwhile stop for visitors interested in Bengal's temple architecture.

The temple is not large, but it fits well into a walk through Nabadwip's religious sites and ghats. Look closely at the facade details before moving on.
