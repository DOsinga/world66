The principal temple of Chaitanya Mahaprabhu in the town, in the heart of old Nabadwip. The current building is modern but the *vigraha* — the brass image — is one of the oldest Vaishnava deities in Bengal. Devotees come at first light to *darshan* and again for the evening *kirtan*, when the building fills with drums, cymbals, and the long Bengali *Hare Krishna* chant.

The surrounding lanes are dense with smaller maths and the sweet shops famous for *kheer-er chop* and *Nabadwip-er lal doi*. Easy to spend an unhurried morning here.
