The colossal blue-and-gold temple slowly rising at the centre of the Mayapur complex, due for full completion in the late 2020s but already substantially open for worship. Designed in a hybrid Western-Vedic style, its central dome will be one of the largest in the world.

The lower halls are already in use for the main daily *aartis*, the upper levels still under construction. The model rooms describing the planned interior — an immersive depiction of the Vedic cosmos — are open to visitors. A genuinely strange and ambitious project that no architecture-minded traveller in eastern India should miss.
