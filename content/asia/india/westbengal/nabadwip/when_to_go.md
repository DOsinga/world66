---
title: When to Go
type: section
---

October to February is the comfortable window: dry, with daytime temperatures around 25°C. **Rash Utsav** in November is the spectacular local festival — every neighbourhood erects enormous painted idols and the streets are thronged for three days. **Gaura Purnima** in February or March brings the great Parikrama pilgrimage and the largest crowds of the year; book any accommodation weeks ahead.

April to June is hot and humid (35-40°C). The monsoon arrives in late June and lasts through September; the rivers run high and some of the ghats become temporarily impassable. Pilgrim numbers drop in these months, which has its compensations — empty temples, gentler atmosphere.
