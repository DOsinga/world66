Purulia is a district headquarters in western West Bengal, on the drier edge of the state where Bengal begins to shade into the Chota Nagpur plateau. It is not a polished tourist town, but it works as a practical base for the district's hills, lakes, old temples, and tribal villages.

The town itself is quiet and workaday. Saheb Bandh, the large lake near the centre, is the natural pause in the day, while small museums and libraries give a little context to the region. The stronger reason to come is the surrounding countryside, especially if you are interested in Purulia's Chhau dance tradition, rural festivals, and the rocky, dry landscape that feels very different from the riverine Bengal around Kolkata.

Transport is simple by Indian standards: Purulia has rail and bus connections with Kolkata, Asansol, Ranchi, Jamshedpur, and Durgapur. For sights outside town, hire a car for the day.
