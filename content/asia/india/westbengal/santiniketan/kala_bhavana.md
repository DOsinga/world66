Kala Bhavana is the fine arts college that helped make Santiniketan a centre of modern Indian art. Its importance lies as much in the campus environment as in formal galleries: murals, sculptures, studios, and outdoor work make art feel woven into daily life.

Artists such as Nandalal Bose, Benode Behari Mukherjee, and Ramkinkar Baij are part of the story here. Access can vary because it is a working institution, so treat it respectfully rather than as an open museum.
