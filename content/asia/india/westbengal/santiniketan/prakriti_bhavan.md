Prakriti Bhavan is a nature art museum near Santiniketan, with indoor displays of driftwood and dry-wood sculpture and an open-air garden of natural rock forms. Its appeal is the mix of art, landscape, and quiet outdoor space rather than a conventional gallery collection.

It is a useful stop when combining Santiniketan with Ballavpur Wildlife Sanctuary and the surrounding rural edge of town.
