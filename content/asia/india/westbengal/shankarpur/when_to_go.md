---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

The Bengal coast has a sharp seasonal rhythm. **October to February** is the best window — dry, cool, with daytime temperatures in the mid-20s and the sea calm enough for swimming. Christmas week and Bengali New Year (mid-April) are the busiest of the year and rooms are scarce; book ahead.

**March to early May** is hot and sticky but the sea is at its calmest. **Mid-June to September** is monsoon — wind, big surf, periodic tropical storms making landfall, no fishing boats out, and many resorts effectively closed. Beautiful in its own way but not for swimming.
