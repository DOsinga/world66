---
title: Getting There
type: section
---

The nearest railway station is Rampurhat, 6 km from Tarapith, on the Bardhaman–Rampurhat line. Several trains run daily from Kolkata Sealdah; the journey takes around 3–3.5 hours. From Rampurhat, shared autos and taxis cover the last stretch to the temple in 15–20 minutes.

If you are combining Tarapith with [Shantiniketan](/asia/india/westbengal/calcutta) (Tagore's university town), note that Bolpur station is about 45 km south of Rampurhat and both are accessible on the same day trip axis from Kolkata.
