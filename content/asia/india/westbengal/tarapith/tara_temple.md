The central temple of Tarapith is dedicated to Tara, the fierce second Mahavidya, and is among the most revered Shakta pilgrimage sites in Bengal. The inner sanctum holds a small black stone image of the goddess — two feet tall, with silver eyes — in the unusual nursing pose that distinguishes Tarapith's Tara from other forms. The atmosphere inside the temple is dense with incense, marigolds, and the press of devotees; not a place for quiet contemplation, but an extraordinary immersion in living religious practice.

The outer courtyard is ringed with smaller shrines and a large tank where pilgrims bathe before entering. The approach lane from the main road is lined with flower sellers, sweet shops, and stalls selling offerings. Non-Hindu visitors are generally welcome to enter the inner area, though it is worth following the lead of pilgrims around you.
