Bali Botanical Gardens, near Candikuning in the cool Bedugul highlands, is one of Indonesia's official botanical gardens. The altitude makes it a pleasant break from the coast, with large lawns, forested paths, orchids, ferns, and views toward the central mountains.

It is worth allowing time to wander rather than treating it as a quick photo stop. The gardens combine well with Lake Bratan and the Bedugul markets, especially if you want a quieter day in Bali's uplands.
