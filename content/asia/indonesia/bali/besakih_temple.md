Bali's most sacred site, perched at 1,000 metres on the slopes of the active volcano Mount Agung. The complex comprises 23 separate temples, the oldest of which may date to the 10th century. Non-Hindus are not admitted inside the innermost sanctuaries, but the outer areas and the approach through the gates is impressive. Go on a festival day if you can — the temple is most alive when ceremonies are underway, which is most of the time. Hire a guide in the village rather than accepting offers at the gate.
