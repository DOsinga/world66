Nuanu Creative City is a large cultural and leisure complex west of Canggu, mixing outdoor art installations with event spaces, restaurants, and family attractions. It is more planned destination than old Balinese village, but it can be a useful stop if you want contemporary art, an easy wander, or something low-pressure with children.

The site is spread out, so allow time to move between the installations and the beach-club side of the complex. Shuttle buses sometimes run within the grounds.
