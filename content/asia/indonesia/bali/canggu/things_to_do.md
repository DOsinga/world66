---
title: "Things to Do in Canggu"
type: section
---

Canggu's days are structured around the tides. In the morning, surfers check the break and the yoga studios fill up; by midday the rice field cafes are full; by late afternoon the beach clubs are heaving. Stay long enough and you'll find a routine in it. Beyond the beach there are temples, creative markets, and rice paddies that take all of five minutes' walk to reach from the main tourist strip.
