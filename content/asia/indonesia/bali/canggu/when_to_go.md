---
title: When to Go
type: section
---

Canggu has the same seasonal pattern as the rest of southern Bali. The dry season (May to October) is best: the surf is more consistent, the roads are less slippery, and the skies are clearer for sunsets. July and August are peak season with significantly more tourists and higher prices.

The wet season (November to March) brings afternoon rain, lower prices, and a different kind of tourist. The surf can be excellent in the wet season — the swells are bigger and more frequent — but the roads flood briefly during heavy rain and the rice field cafes lose some of their appeal in a downpour. April and May are probably the sweet spot: good conditions, reasonable crowds, prices before peak season.
