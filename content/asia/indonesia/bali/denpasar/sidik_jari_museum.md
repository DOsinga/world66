Sidik Jari Museum is a small private art museum founded by Balinese artist I Gusti Ngurah Gede Pemecutan. It shows his own work alongside pieces by other artists, with a focus on local fine art rather than the larger cultural displays found at Denpasar's main museums.

The museum is a modest stop, but worthwhile for travellers who are already exploring Denpasar's art scene. It also hosts occasional public dance and performance events.
