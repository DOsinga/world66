Jatiluwih is one of Bali's great landscapes: a wide sweep of rice terraces stepping down from the slopes below Mount Batukaru. Come with time to walk rather than just take a photo from the road. The terraces are part of Bali's UNESCO-listed subak irrigation landscape, and the views are best when the fields are green and the clouds stay high.
