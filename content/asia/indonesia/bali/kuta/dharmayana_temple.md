Dharmayana Temple is one of Kuta's oldest and most interesting cultural sights, a Chinese Buddhist temple tucked away from the beach traffic and nightlife. Its red gates, incense, and courtyard details offer a short but real change of rhythm.

It is easy to miss if you only come to Kuta for surf and bars. Visit respectfully, keep expectations modest, and enjoy it as a glimpse of Balis Chinese community and the layered history behind the resort strip.
