Suluban Beach, also called Blue Point Beach, is a small cliff-backed beach near Uluwatu reached by steps and narrow passages through the rock. It is less open and easy than the main south Bali beaches, but the setting is much more dramatic.

Surfers come for the reef break, while non-surfers usually visit for the cave-like approach, the cliff views, and the chance to combine it with Uluwatu Temple or the bars above the surf.
