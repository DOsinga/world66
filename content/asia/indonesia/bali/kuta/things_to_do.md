---
title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Kuta's main attraction is its beach — long, west-facing, and the reason the town exists. Beyond that, the sights are a small mix of memorial sites, water parks, and the launch pad for trips into the rest of Bali. Most travellers spend a day or two here for surf and sunsets, then move on to Ubud or the volcanoes.
