---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

Bali has two seasons: dry, from roughly April to October, and wet, from November to March. Kuta is sunny and warm in both, but the dry season has clearer afternoons, cleaner surf, and the crowds — peak months are July, August, and the Christmas-New Year stretch, when room rates double and the beach fills up.

**Shoulder seasons** — May–June and September–early October — are the sweet spot: warm and dry, fewer tourists, surf still working, and prices noticeably lower. The wet season is not a write-off either; rain usually comes in short tropical bursts late in the day, and you will have the beach to yourself in the mornings.

Watch for **Nyepi**, the Balinese day of silence in March, when the entire island shuts down for twenty-four hours — no flights in or out, no lights, no streets open. Either time your visit around it or be in town a day early to witness the spectacular ogoh-ogoh parades the night before.
