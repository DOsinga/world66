Lovina is a string of small coastal villages along the north coast of [Bali](/asia/indonesia/bali), stretching several kilometres either side of the main settlement of Kalibukbuk. It is the north coast's main tourist area, but feels like a different world from the south — quieter, cheaper, and more laid-back.

The beaches are volcanic black sand, the sea is calm, and the main draw is the morning [dolphin-watching](/asia/indonesia/bali/lovina/dolphin_watching) trips that head out before dawn. The snorkelling is good, particularly around the reef at nearby [Menjangan Island](/asia/indonesia/bali/lovina/menjangan_island_snorkelling). Lovina makes a practical base for visiting the [Banjar hot springs](/asia/indonesia/bali/lovina/banjar_hot_springs), the Buddhist monastery at [Brahma Vihara Arama](/asia/indonesia/bali/lovina/brahma_vihara_arama), and the [waterfalls](/asia/indonesia/bali/lovina/git_git_waterfall) in the hills above the coast.
