Akar is the vegetarian option in Kalibukbuk, set back from the main road in a small garden. The menu runs from raw salads and smoothie bowls to Indonesian classics done without meat. Portions are generous and prices fair.

Good for breakfast after the dolphin trip — they open early and the coffee is properly made.
