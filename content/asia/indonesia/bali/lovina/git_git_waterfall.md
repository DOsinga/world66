Air Terjun Git Git is the best-known waterfall in north Bali, about eleven kilometres south-east of Lovina on the road climbing towards Bedugul. The main fall plunges forty metres in a single drop and there is a swimmable pool at the base — bring a swimsuit if the weather is warm.

The walk from the car park takes around ten minutes through small coffee and clove gardens. There is an entry fee and a chain of small souvenir stalls along the path. Visit early or late in the day; the falls catch the sun for only a short window each morning and the pool is otherwise in shade.
