Munduk Waterfall is one of north Bali's better waterfall walks, reached by a short path from the road rather than visible from the car park. It is most impressive in the wet season, when the water is loud and the valley feels properly lush.

The path is narrow but manageable, and there is usually a small stop nearby for locally grown coffee after the walk.
