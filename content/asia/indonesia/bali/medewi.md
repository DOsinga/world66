Medewi is the Bali people mean when they say the island still has quiet corners. It sits on the west coast, far from the south's club traffic, with black sand, fishing boats, rice fields, and one famous left-hand wave.

The headline is [Medewi Point](/asia/indonesia/bali/medewi/medewi_point), a long, mellow surf break that draws surfers who want room to breathe. Non-surfers still get a good day out of the coast: [Medewi Beach](/asia/indonesia/bali/medewi/medewi_beach) is good for walking and watching the boats, while [Bunut Bolong](/asia/indonesia/bali/medewi/bunut_bolong) makes a strange inland detour through Jembrana's green hills. [Pura Rambut Siwi](/asia/indonesia/bali/negara/pura_rambut_siwi), one of west Bali's strongest sea temples, is close enough to fold into the same day.

Medewi is not for people who need a full menu of sights. It is for surfers, slow travellers, and anyone who wants a beach village that still looks toward Java as much as toward Bali's resort south.
