Negara is the main town of Jembrana, the quieter western end of [Bali](/asia/indonesia/bali). It is not the Bali of beach clubs and villa traffic. The road to Java runs through town, rice fields press close, mosques are common, and the coast is lined with fishing villages and black sand.

The reason to come is the local texture. Jembrana calls itself the land of [Mekepung buffalo racing](/asia/indonesia/bali/negara/mekepung_buffalo_races), a fast, noisy harvest tradition seen almost nowhere else. South of town, [Pengambengan Fishing Port](/asia/indonesia/bali/negara/pengambengan_fishing_port) shows the working coast; east, [Pura Rambut Siwi](/asia/indonesia/bali/negara/pura_rambut_siwi) brings one of west Bali's strongest sea temples to a cliff above the surf.

Negara is best for travellers who have already seen the famous parts of Bali and want the island with fewer layers of tourism between them and daily life. Give it a day by car or motorbike, and it will feel very different from the south.
