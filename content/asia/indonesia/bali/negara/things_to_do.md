---
title: Things to Do
type: section
---

Negara's sights are spread across southern Jembrana, so treat the town as a base rather than a walking destination. The best day combines the coastal temple at Rambut Siwi, fishing boats at Pengambengan, and one of the inland Christian villages or Palasari Dam. If a Mekepung race is scheduled, build the day around it.
