Nusa Lembongan is the gentler member of Bali's southeastern island trio. It sits between mainland [Bali](/asia/indonesia/bali) and wilder [Nusa Penida](/asia/indonesia/bali/nusapenida), close enough for a day trip from [Sanur](/asia/indonesia/bali/sanur) but better with at least one night. The island is small, dry, and low-slung, with lanes narrow enough to keep traffic slow and a coast that shifts from calm bays to wave-battered cliffs in minutes.

The classic Lembongan day loops around the southwest: [Devil's Tear](/asia/indonesia/bali/nusalembongan/devils_tear), [Dream Beach](/asia/indonesia/bali/nusalembongan/dream_beach), and [Sandy Bay](/asia/indonesia/bali/nusalembongan/sandy_bay) are all close together. [Mushroom Bay](/asia/indonesia/bali/nusalembongan/mushroom_bay) is the easiest beach base, while the [Yellow Bridge](/asia/indonesia/bali/nusalembongan/yellow_bridge) connects the island to smaller Nusa Ceningan.

The water is the other reason to come. Boats run to snorkelling sites around Lembongan and Penida, with manta ray trips depending on conditions. Seaweed farming, once the island's main business, still shapes the shallows in places, though tourism has changed the economy fast.

Lembongan is more developed than it used to be, but it has not turned into south Bali. Come for a few quiet days of scooters, swims, reef boats, and early nights, not for nightlife or shopping.
