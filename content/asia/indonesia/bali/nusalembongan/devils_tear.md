Devil's Tear is the island's most dramatic land-based sight: a rough limestone outcrop where Indian Ocean swells hit the cliffs and burst into spray. In the right conditions the blowback rises several metres, turning the whole ledge into a cloud of salt water.

It is also the place on Lembongan where caution matters most. The rocks are sharp, wet, and exposed to unpredictable waves. Watch from a dry distance, keep children away from the edge, and do not step down onto the lower ledges for a photograph.
