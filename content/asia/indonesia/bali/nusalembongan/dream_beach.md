Dream Beach is a small white-sand cove near Devil's Tear, enclosed by low cliffs and open to the swell. It looks idyllic from above, especially in the morning, and it is one of the prettiest beaches on the island.

Swimming depends entirely on conditions. The shore break can be powerful and the currents unforgiving, so treat it as a beach for sunning, paddling, and photographs unless the sea is clearly calm. Cafes above the sand make it an easy stop on the southwest loop.
