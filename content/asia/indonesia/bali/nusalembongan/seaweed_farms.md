Seaweed farming is still part of everyday life on Nusa Lembongan, especially around the southern side of Lembongan Village. At low tide you can see the shallow plots and the simple working methods used to grow seaweed for the cosmetics industry.

It is a quick, low-key stop, but it shows a side of the island beyond beaches and dive boats.
