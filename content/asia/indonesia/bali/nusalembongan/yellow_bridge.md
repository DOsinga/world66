The Yellow Bridge links Nusa Lembongan with Nusa Ceningan across the narrow channel between the islands. It is only wide enough for scooters and pedestrians, which gives it a pleasingly informal feel: temple traffic, surfboards, delivery bikes, and visitors all squeeze across in single file.

The bridge is not a major sight in itself, but it is the hinge of the island pair. Cross it for Ceningan's cliff views, beach bars, and quieter lanes, or stop briefly for photographs of the channel and seaweed plots below.
