Nusa Penida is [Bali](/asia/indonesia/bali)'s dramatic neighbour — a large limestone island rising from the sea southeast of the main island, reachable in 45 minutes by fast boat from Sanur. Until recently it was largely ignored by tourists, visited mainly by Balinese Hindus making pilgrimage to its temples. Then the Instagram age arrived, and images of [Kelingking Beach](/asia/indonesia/bali/nusapenida/kelingking_beach) — a T-rex-shaped cliff plunging to a white beach completely inaccessible by road — went around the world. The island has been overrun ever since.

The crowds are real, but so is the landscape. Nusa Penida is wilder and steeper than mainland Bali, with roads that are genuinely challenging and viewpoints that require significant hiking. [Angel's Billabong](/asia/indonesia/bali/nusapenida/angels_billabong), a natural rock pool at the island's western tip, and [Broken Beach](/asia/indonesia/bali/nusapenida/broken_beach), an arch-enclosed lagoon next to it, are among the most photographed spots in all of Indonesia. The south coast cliffs run for miles, and the views across the Badung Strait to Bali's volcanoes on a clear morning are extraordinary.

The underwater world around Nusa Penida is what brought divers here long before the land-based tourism. The island lies at the intersection of warm and cold ocean currents, which creates the conditions for manta rays, ocean sunfish (mola mola), and an extraordinary density of reef life. [Manta Point](/asia/indonesia/bali/nusapenida/manta_point) on the southwest coast is the mola mola capital of Bali's dive circuit; large mantas are also seen reliably at the cleaning station there. [Crystal Bay](/asia/indonesia/bali/nusapenida/crystal_bay), on the northwest, is the main dive hub and the best beach for swimming.

Most visitors come on a day trip from Sanur or the Gili Islands and see two or three viewpoints. To do the island justice you need at least two nights — one to recover from the roads, one to actually explore. Rent a scooter only if you are confident with seriously steep, rough terrain. Otherwise, hire a local driver for the day.
