Atuh Beach is one of eastern Nusa Penida's signature coves: a pale strip of sand held between high limestone cliffs, with rock stacks and arches breaking up the bright blue water offshore. The view from the rim is as much a part of the visit as the beach itself, especially in the morning when the light reaches into the cove.

Access is by a steep stair path, so it works best as a deliberate stop rather than a casual roadside swim. Bring water, expect uneven steps, and combine it with nearby Diamond Beach viewpoints if you are making the long drive across the island.
