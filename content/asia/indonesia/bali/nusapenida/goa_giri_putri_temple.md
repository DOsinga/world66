Goa Giri Putri is one of Nusa Penida's most memorable inland stops: a Hindu temple inside a large limestone cave, entered through a surprisingly small opening in the hillside. The squeeze at the entrance gives way to a broad cavern used for prayer, ceremonies, and offerings.

It is a working sacred site, so visitors should dress properly, rent or bring a sarong, and move quietly when worshippers are present. The temple makes a useful contrast to the island's cliff viewpoints and beaches, especially on an east-side route that also takes in Atuh, Diamond Beach, or the coastal villages.
