Peguyangan Spring is less a conventional waterfall than a dramatic cliffside pilgrimage: a long run of blue metal stairs drops down the rock face to a spring, small temple area, and sea-facing pools. The ocean view is the draw, but the descent is exposed enough to make the place feel more adventurous than many Nusa Penida stops.

Wear proper shoes and avoid treating it as a casual beach visit. Visitors should dress respectfully around the shrine and budget time for the climb back up, which is the part most people remember after the photos are taken.
