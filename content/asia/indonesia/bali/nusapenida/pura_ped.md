Pura Ped is one of Nusa Penida's important temple complexes, set near the north coast and visited by Balinese Hindu pilgrims as well as island travellers. It gives the island a cultural anchor beyond the beaches, cliffs, and Instagram viewpoints.

Dress properly and follow temple etiquette, including sarong and sash requirements where posted. The temple is most rewarding when treated as a living place of worship, not simply another stop on a scooter loop.
