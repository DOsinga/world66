Suwehan Beach is one of Nusa Penida's wilder east-coast beaches, a pale crescent of sand tucked beneath high limestone cliffs. The descent is part of the experience: a steep stairway drops from the road-end area to the shore, opening suddenly onto blue water, a freestanding rock stack, and a beach that still feels far less packaged than Kelingking or Diamond Beach.

Come early if you care about light and tide. The cliff throws shade across the sand later in the day, and at high tide the beach can shrink dramatically. The road has improved, but this is still a place for confident scooter riders or a hired driver rather than a casual last-minute detour.
