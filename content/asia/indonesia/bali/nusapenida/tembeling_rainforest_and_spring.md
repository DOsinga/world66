Tembeling Rainforest and Spring is one of Nusa Penida's more atmospheric inland-coast combinations: a forested ravine, freshwater pools, and a rocky shoreline reached by a rough descent. It feels greener and more enclosed than the island's famous cliff viewpoints.

The approach can be awkward, especially by scooter, so check conditions before going and do not underestimate the return climb. The reward is a quieter, more textured version of Nusa Penida, with shade, water, and coast in one compact outing.
