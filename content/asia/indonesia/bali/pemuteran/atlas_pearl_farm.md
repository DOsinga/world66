Atlas Pearl Farm sits on the coast at Penyabangan, east of Pemuteran. Visitors can learn how pearls are farmed, see the oyster hatchery, and watch a short explanation of the process before reaching the showroom. It is a modest stop, but more interesting than an ordinary shop if you are curious about the craft behind Bali's pearl trade.
