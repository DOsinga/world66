Pura Luhur Batukaru is one of Bali's key directional temples, set on the slopes of Mount Batukaru. The forested setting and strong pilgrimage role make it feel more solemn than many busier temple stops, and it is worth the detour for travellers exploring Bali beyond the southern resort belt.
