Pura Meduwe Karang is one of North Bali's more interesting temples, dedicated to agricultural deities. The temple is best known for its lively stone carving, including the famous image of a Dutch cyclist that has become one of Bali's copied motifs.

It is a good stop when travelling along the north coast, especially for visitors interested in Balinese temple sculpture beyond the better-known sites around Ubud and the south.
