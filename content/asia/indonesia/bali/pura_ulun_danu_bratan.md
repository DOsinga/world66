Pura Ulun Danu Bratan is one of Bali's defining temple views, standing on the western shore of Lake Bratan in the cool Bedugul highlands. When the water is high, parts of the temple appear to float on the lake, with the mountains behind it.

It is among the island's most photographed sights, so arrive early for quieter paths and clearer views.
