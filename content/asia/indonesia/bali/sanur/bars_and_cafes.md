---
title: Bars and Cafes
type: section
---

Sanur is a daytime cafe town more than a nightlife town. The beachfront has a steady spread of warungs serving fresh juices and Bintang at sunset; the streets back from the beach have proper espresso bars and the digital-nomad coworking cafes. The cocktail scene is concentrated in a handful of beach clubs at the south end. For real nightlife head to Kuta or Seminyak; Sanur winds down around midnight.
