Cafe Batujimbar on Danau Tamblingan has been Sanur's go-to garden cafe since the 1990s — leafy courtyard, ceiling fans, juice bar, and a menu that runs from solid Western breakfasts to *nasi goreng* and excellent Balinese smoked duck. The lunch crowd is local expats with laptops, the weekend brunch is families. Reliable cooking, decent wifi, fresh juices, and unhurried service.
