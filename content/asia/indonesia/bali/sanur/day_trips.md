The fast boats from Sanur Harbour put **Nusa Lembongan** within thirty minutes for snorkelling, mangrove kayaking, and a quieter beach. Half an hour inland brings you to the markets and temples of [Denpasar](/asia/indonesia/bali/denpasar). [Ubud](/asia/indonesia/bali/ubud) — the cultural heart of Bali, with art galleries, the Monkey Forest, and rice-terrace walks — is about an hour's drive north. Closer still, the **Bajra Sandhi Monument** in Denpasar tells the story of the Balinese resistance to colonial rule in a striking lotus-shaped tower.
