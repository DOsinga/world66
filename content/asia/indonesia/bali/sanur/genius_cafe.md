Genius Cafe sits directly on the beach at Pantai Sindhu and serves the digital-nomad set: speed-tested wifi, plug points on every table, cold-brew coffee, smoothie bowls, and a Pilates studio upstairs. The food is healthful and overpriced, but the trade-off is a desk with a sea view and a steady stream of people watching laptops in the breeze. Best at breakfast or late afternoon when the heat eases.
