---
title: Getting There
type: section
---

Sanur is about a 20-minute taxi ride from **Ngurah Rai International Airport** in normal traffic — call it 30–45 minutes at evening rush. Most arrivals take an airport taxi or a pre-booked transfer; Grab and Gojek both operate, though the airport's taxi mafia limits Grab pickup zones. From other parts of Bali, a metered taxi or app ride from Kuta or Ubud is the standard option. The **Sanur Harbour** at the south end of the beach has fast boats to Nusa Lembongan, Nusa Penida, and the Gili Islands.
