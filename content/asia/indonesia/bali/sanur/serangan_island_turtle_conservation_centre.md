The Serangan Island Turtle Conservation Centre is a modest conservation-focused stop near Sanur, connected to efforts to protect sea turtles around Bali. It is not a polished wildlife park, and that distinction is important.

A visit is worthwhile if you approach it as a short educational stop and ask questions about current conservation work. It pairs well with exploring Serangan Island, but it should not be treated as entertainment first and animal welfare second.
