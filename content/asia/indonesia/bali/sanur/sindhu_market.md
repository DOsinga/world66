Pasar Sindhu is Sanur's morning produce market on Jalan Danau Tamblingan — busiest from dawn until about 9am, when the stalls of dragonfruit, lemongrass, chillies, salted fish, and warm coconut sticky rice give way to the day. A small night market section opens late afternoon for hot food: satay, *nasi campur*, *bakso*, and *bubur sumsum* puddings. The cleanest, most photogenic, and least chaotic of Bali's smaller pasars.
