---
title: Things to Do
type: section
---

Sanur's pleasures are deliberate and quiet. The five-kilometre **beachfront promenade** is the spine of any day here — long stroll at sunrise, sit at a warung at noon, swim and snorkel at high tide. The **Le Mayeur Museum** and the **Pura Blanjong** temple anchor the modest sightseeing list. Boats from the harbour run to **Nusa Lembongan** and **Nusa Penida** for half-day or longer escapes, and a short transfer puts the heart of [Denpasar](/asia/indonesia/bali/denpasar) within reach.
