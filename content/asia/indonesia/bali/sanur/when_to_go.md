---
title: When to Go
type: section
---

Sanur's dry season runs from April to October — the cleanest skies, calmest seas, and lowest humidity. **June through August** are the peak months, when the European school holidays push prices up sharply; book accommodation well ahead. The shoulder months of **April–May and September–October** are the sweet spot: warm dry weather, fewer crowds, and lower rates. The wet season (November to March) brings dramatic short downpours rather than constant rain, and Sanur's east-coast position means storms are usually less aggressive than on the south. Avoid New Year and the Idul Fitri Indonesian holiday if you don't like crowds.
