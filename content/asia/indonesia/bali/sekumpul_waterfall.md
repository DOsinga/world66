Sekumpul Waterfall is one of north Bali's most dramatic waterfall sites, with several streams dropping side by side into a jungle valley. The setting is lush, steep and photogenic.

Reaching the falls takes a moderately demanding walk with stairs, slippery paths and river crossings. Wear sturdy footwear and expect local guides to be available near the access points.
