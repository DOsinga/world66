Seminyak is [Bali](/asia/indonesia/bali)'s upmarket beach strip, running north from [Kuta](/asia/indonesia/bali/kuta) and Legian along the west coast. What was once a quiet township is now the island's centre for boutique shopping, international restaurants, and beach clubs — a world away from Kuta's backpacker scene just down the road.

The [beach](/asia/indonesia/bali/seminyak/seminyak_beach) is wide and west-facing, which means good sunsets and reliable surf. [Jalan Kayu Aya](/asia/indonesia/bali/seminyak/jalan_kayu_aya) (known as "Eat Street") and [Jalan Petitenget](/asia/indonesia/bali/seminyak/jalan_petitenget) are the main drags, lined with designer shops, cocktail bars, and restaurants covering everything from Balinese to Japanese to Italian. Seminyak attracts a more affluent crowd than its southern neighbours, along with a large expat community. Prices are among the highest in Bali, but the quality of food and nightlife is noticeably better too.
