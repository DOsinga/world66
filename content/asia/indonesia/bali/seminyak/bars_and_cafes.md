---
title: Bars and Cafes
type: section
---

Seminyak's evening scene revolves around the beach clubs along the dune line — Ku De Ta, Potato Head, La Plancha, Sundays — which open at lunchtime, peak at sunset, and run until late. The cocktails are good, the music is decent, and the sunsets are reliably spectacular.

Inland from the beach there is a smaller circuit of dedicated cocktail bars and brunch cafes, mostly along Eat Street and Jalan Petitenget. The vibe shifts later in the evening to the warehouse clubs on Jalan Dhyana Pura.
