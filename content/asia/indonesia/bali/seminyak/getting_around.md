---
title: Getting Around
type: section
---

Seminyak is small enough that most things are reachable on foot, but the heat and traffic make taxis sensible for anything beyond about ten minutes' walk. **Bluebird** taxis are reliable and metered — accept no substitutes; insist on the meter. **Grab** and **Gojek** ride-hailing apps work well in Seminyak (though parts of Kuta have prohibitions on app pickups; ignore the local touts trying to convince you otherwise).

Scooters can be rented from any of the dozens of small shops along Jalan Raya Seminyak for around 70,000–100,000 rupiah a day. International licence required; helmets compulsory; traffic is unforgiving for the inexperienced.
