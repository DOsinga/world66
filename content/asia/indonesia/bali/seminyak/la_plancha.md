The cheapest, most relaxed sunset venue on Seminyak Beach. La Plancha runs a strip of multicoloured beanbags and parasols on the sand south of Pura Petitenget; the bar serves Bintang, basic cocktails, and Spanish-influenced beach food.

Order a drink and you can claim a beanbag for the rest of the evening. The vibe is loud and a bit chaotic — fire performers, occasional bands, lots of phones — but it captures the original Seminyak feel that the bigger clubs have refined out of existence.
