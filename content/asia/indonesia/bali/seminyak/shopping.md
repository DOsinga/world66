---
title: Shopping
type: section
---

Seminyak is Bali's premier shopping district — a mix of independent Bali-based fashion designers, Australian-led resort wear, and local jewellers. The two main shopping streets are **Jalan Kayu Aya** (Eat Street) and **Jalan Petitenget**, both lined with boutiques running roughly from south to north.

For air-conditioning, **Seminyak Village** mall has three floors of mostly local labels. Avoid Jalan Legian if you are hoping for designer goods — the southern end is overrun with fake-brand bag and DVD shops better suited to Kuta.
