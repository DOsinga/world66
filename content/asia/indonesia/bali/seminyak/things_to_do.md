---
title: Things to Do
type: section
---

Seminyak is not a sightseeing destination so much as a strip of beach, restaurants, and beach clubs. The main activities are sunset-watching from a club lounger, shopping the boutiques along Jalan Kayu Aya and Jalan Petitenget, and getting massages, manicures, or surf lessons.

The two genuine attractions are **Petitenget Temple**, at the north end of the beach, and the sunsets themselves — Seminyak faces directly west and consistently produces some of the most photographed evening skies on the island.
