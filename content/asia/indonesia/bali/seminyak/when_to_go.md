---
title: When to Go
type: section
---

**Dry season (April–October)** is the peak — bright sun, manageable humidity, and ocean clarity that occasionally reaches blue. July and August are the busiest months; rooms book out and beach-club prices rise.

**Wet season (November–March)** brings tropical downpours, usually mid-afternoon and lasting an hour or two. The surf is bigger, the crowds are thinner, and the resorts drop their rates. Sunsets in March, after the rains clear, can be the best of the year.

Note that **Nyepi** (Balinese New Year, March) shuts the whole island down for 24 hours — no traffic, no lights, no flights in or out. Plan around it.
