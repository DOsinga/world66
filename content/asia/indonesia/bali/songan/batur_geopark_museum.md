Batur Geopark Museum explains the volcanic landscape around Mount Batur, Lake Batur, and the wider Pacific Ring of Fire. Exhibits cover local eruptions, volcanic rocks, and the geology that shaped the caldera.

It is a useful stop before or after seeing Mount Batur itself, especially for travellers who want context beyond the viewpoint photos. Displays include films, models, and a telescope trained toward the mountain.
