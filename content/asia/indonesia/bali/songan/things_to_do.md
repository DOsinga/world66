---
title: Things to Do
type: section
---

Songan sits at the caldera floor with Lake Batur on one side and the crater wall rising steeply on the other. The main activities here are walking along the lake shore to the crater-wall temple, climbing the short steep trail to the caldera rim for views down to the lake and across to the ocean, and using the village as a launching point for the pre-dawn Mount Batur sunrise hike. The lake provides fresh fish for local restaurants — grilled lake fish is the signature meal of the area.
