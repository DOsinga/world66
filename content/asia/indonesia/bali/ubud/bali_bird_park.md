Bali Bird Park is a polished wildlife attraction south of Ubud, with landscaped aviaries holding hornbills, parrots, birds of paradise, and other tropical species. It is built for close viewing, so it suits families and visitors who want an easy nature stop without a trek.

The park is commercial, but it is substantial enough to earn its place on an Ubud itinerary if birds interest you. Combine it with nearby villages or craft stops, and check show and feeding times if those matter to your visit.
