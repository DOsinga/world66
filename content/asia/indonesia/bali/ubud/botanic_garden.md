Ubud's Botanic Garden fills a valley north of the centre, making it a pleasant place for a slow walk among tropical planting. It is most rewarding in the morning, before the heat settles into the paths.

Treat it as a quiet nature break rather than one of Ubud's major cultural stops. It pairs well with a day built around the northern side of town.
