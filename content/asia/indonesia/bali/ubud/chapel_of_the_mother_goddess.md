The Chapel of the Mother Goddess, also known through its Rajarajeshwari Tripurasundari dedication, is a small spiritual site outside Ubud's busiest centre. It belongs to the Anand Ashram community and reflects the town's long-running mix of Balinese, Hindu, yoga, and international retreat cultures.

This is not a major temple in the Balinese ceremonial sense, so approach it as a quiet, specific stop rather than a headline sight. It is most worthwhile for travelers already interested in Ubud's spiritual communities and willing to check visiting etiquette before arriving.
