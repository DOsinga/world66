Petulu is known for the white herons that return to roost in the village trees, usually making the late afternoon the best time to visit. The spectacle is simple but memorable: birds streaming back over rice fields and rooftops as the light softens north of Ubud.

It is a gentle nature-focused excursion rather than a formal attraction. Go respectfully, keep noise down around homes and temples, and combine the stop with a ride through the quieter villages outside central Ubud.
