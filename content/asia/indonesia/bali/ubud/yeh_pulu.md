Yeh Pulu is a small archaeological site near Ubud, centred on a long rock relief carved with figures from daily and mythic life. It is quieter and more intimate than Goa Gajah, and the approach through greenery helps it feel less staged than some of Ubud's busier temple stops.

The visit is short, but that is part of its charm. Pair it with Goa Gajah or the rice-field villages east of town, and give yourself time to look closely at the carving rather than treating it as a quick photo stop.
