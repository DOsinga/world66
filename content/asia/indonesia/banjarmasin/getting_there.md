---
title: Getting There
type: section
---

Banjarmasin is served by Syamsudin Noor Airport (BDJ), about 25km from the city centre. Garuda Indonesia and Lion Air operate regular flights from Jakarta (around 1.5 hours) and Surabaya. A taxi from the airport to the city centre takes 30–45 minutes depending on traffic.

There is no rail connection to Banjarmasin. Long-distance buses connect the city to destinations in South and Central Kalimantan. Ferry services operate from the Trisakti port to Java (Surabaya) and Sulawesi; the Surabaya crossing takes about 18 hours and is a cost-effective option for those not in a hurry.
