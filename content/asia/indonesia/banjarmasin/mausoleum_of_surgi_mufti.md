The Mausoleum of Surgi Mufti (Kubah Surgi Mufti) is the tomb of a revered 19th-century Islamic scholar who was the grand mufti of the Banjar Sultanate. The site is a place of Islamic pilgrimage and is considered sacred by the Banjar people. The mausoleum is enclosed in a traditional compound with a distinctive roofed structure over the grave.

Visiting requires modest dress and respectful behaviour. The site is in a river-facing neighbourhood and reflects the deep Islamic heritage of Banjarmasin's culture — the city's Muslim identity, reinforced by the river and market traditions, is central to everything about it. Ask your guesthouse about the best time to visit, as the site can be busier around Islamic holidays.
