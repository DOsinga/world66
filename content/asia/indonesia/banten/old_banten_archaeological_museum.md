Old Banten Archaeological Museum covers the history of the former Banten Sultanate and the old port town, with local archaeological finds and Southeast Asian ceramics. It is a useful context stop before or after visiting the nearby Grand Mosque of Banten, Kaibon Palace and Speelwijk Fortress.
