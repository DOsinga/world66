Sawarna is a small village on the south coast of Banten, hemmed in by jungle hills to the north and the Indian Ocean to the south. It takes effort to reach: from Jakarta the road journey can stretch to six or seven hours, especially on weekends. That distance is part of the point. Sawarna feels like a rougher, less built-up Java beach escape than the resort strips closer to the capital.

The coast is the draw. [Tanjung Layar](/asia/indonesia/banten/sawarna/tanjung_layar) is the village icon, with two dark rock fins standing in the surf like sails. [Ciantir Beach](/asia/indonesia/banten/sawarna/ciantir_beach) is the main sand-and-surf stretch, while [Goa Langir](/asia/indonesia/banten/sawarna/goa_langir) adds limestone cliffs and sea caves to the usual beach day.

Sawarna works best if you want a simple coastal base rather than a polished resort. You walk or ride a motorbike between rice fields, warungs, surf breaks, and rocky coves. Come for the water, the dawn and dusk light, and the feeling that the south coast of Java still has a few edges left.
