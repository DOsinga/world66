---
title: "Getting Around"
type: section
---

Batam has no public transport worth relying on. The island is too spread out to walk, and buses are infrequent and confusing for visitors. **Ride-hailing apps** (Grab, GoJek) work well in Batam and are by far the most practical way to move around. Fares are cheap compared to Singapore. Download both apps before you arrive.

**Taxis** queue at the ferry terminals and ferry ramps; negotiate the fare before you get in, or insist on the meter if available. Terminal taxis are typically more expensive than Grab.

**Car rental** is available if you plan to drive the Barelang Highway to Galang Island or explore the Nongsa coast — a half-day circuit that is hard to do efficiently by ride-hail. Batam follows Indonesian road rules; driving is on the left.

There are **ferry connections** to some of the resort islands (Nongsa Point Marina, for example, runs boat transfers) and to Bintan from Telaga Punggur, a journey of about 30 minutes to Tanjung Pinang.
