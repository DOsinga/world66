One of Batam's best 18-hole courses, designed by Greg Norman and set on rolling hills in the north of the island with long views over the Strait of Malacca toward Singapore. The layout has water hazards on several holes and generous fairways that suit mid-handicap players without being easy. The clubhouse is well-run with a decent restaurant and rental equipment. Green fees are considerably cheaper than anything comparable in Singapore, and packages with ferry transfers and tee times are sold by Singapore travel agents.
