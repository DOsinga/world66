Tanjung Pandan is Belitung's main town and the practical base for visiting the island. It is not a destination in itself, but the town centre has some character: a compact district of Chinese shophouses from the colonial tin-mining era lines the main street, and the waterfront market is lively in the early morning. Most restaurants, guesthouses, and tour operators are based here.

The **Museum Tanjung Pandan** at the edge of town has a collection focused on the island's tin-mining history, including traditional tools, antique tin-ware, and material on the Laskar Pelangi story. The market sells local produce including Belitung pepper (once among the most valued in Asia), dried seafood, and the local *mie Belitung* (noodles in a prawn-and-tofu soup), the island's signature dish.
