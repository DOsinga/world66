Cirebon Waterland Ade Irma Suryani is a family-oriented waterfront park with pools, slides, play areas, food stalls, and sea-cottage style buildings. It is a lighter stop than Cirebon's palaces and mosques, aimed mostly at families travelling with children.

The main reason to come is for a relaxed pool-and-play break within the city, not for a major cultural sight.
