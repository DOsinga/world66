Malanage is the best kind of Flores hot spring: simple, warm, and set in green country rather than built into a spa. Its charm is the meeting of two streams, one hot and mineral-rich, the other cold, so you can shift around until the temperature feels right.

It works well after Bena or a morning viewpoint ride. Facilities are basic, so bring a towel, modest swimwear, and small cash.
