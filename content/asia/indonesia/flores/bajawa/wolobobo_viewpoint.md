Wolobobo is the easy viewpoint above Bajawa, reached by road and best in clear morning or late-afternoon light. From the ridge you get the town, the surrounding hills, and the cone of Mount Inerie in one sweep.

It is a simple stop, but a good one. Go when the clouds are moving rather than settled, and bring a layer; Bajawa's highland air cools quickly once the sun drops.
