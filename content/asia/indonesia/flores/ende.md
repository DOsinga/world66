Ende is the biggest city on [Flores](/asia/indonesia/flores), a workaday port and airport town on the island's south coast. Most travellers pass through on the way to Kelimutu, Sumba, or the long Trans-Flores road, but Ende has more texture than a simple transit stop.

The city has an educated, practical feel, with a university, a hospital, markets, and enough English-speaking young people to make it easier than many smaller Flores towns. It is also closely tied to Indonesian history: Sukarno was exiled here by the Dutch from 1934 to 1938, and the house where he lived is now the [Bung Karno Museum](/asia/indonesia/flores/ende/bung_karno_museum).

Ende's strongest travel role is as an eastern Flores base. Flights link it with other Indonesian cities, ferries run irregularly toward Sumba and Kupang, and buses continue west toward Bajawa and Labuan Bajo or east toward Maumere. If you are crossing Flores overland, Ende is a sensible place to pause, reset, and make the side trip to Kelimutu.
