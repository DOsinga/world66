The Bung Karno Museum preserves the house where Sukarno lived during his Dutch-imposed exile in Ende from 1934 to 1938. The rooms keep old furniture and photographs from the period, giving the place a more personal feel than a formal national museum.

For Indonesian visitors it is a major historical stop. For foreign travellers it is a useful way to understand why this otherwise ordinary Flores city matters in the national story.
