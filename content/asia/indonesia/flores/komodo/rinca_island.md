Rinca Island is the second-largest island in Komodo National Park and the preferred destination for many visitors because it is considerably closer to Labuan Bajo (about 45 minutes by speedboat versus 2 hours to Komodo Island). Dragon sightings at Rinca are at least as reliable as on Komodo, particularly around the ranger station at Loh Buaya where the animals congregate.

Rinca also offers excellent hiking. The hills behind Loh Buaya rise to viewpoints with panoramas across the park's islands, dry savanna, and the surrounding sea. Water buffalo and deer are common, macaques are everywhere, and orange-footed scrubfowl scratch around the forest floor. The combination of dragons, birds, and landscape makes Rinca feel like a more complete wildlife experience than Komodo Island, which is primarily visited for the dragon encounter alone.
