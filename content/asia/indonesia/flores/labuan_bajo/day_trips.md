Most day trips from Labuan Bajo go into [Komodo National Park](/asia/indonesia/komodo): Rinca or Komodo for dragons, Padar for the viewpoint, Pink Beach for snorkelling, and one or two reef stops if conditions allow. Day trips are popular but rushed. If you can afford the time and money, a two- to four-night liveaboard gives a far better sense of the park.
