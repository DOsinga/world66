Mangeruda Hot Spring is a low-key soak in the hills near Bajawa, with warm spring water feeding a couple of simple pools and a stream. The facilities are basic and can feel neglected, but the water is genuinely warm and the stop works well after a day visiting villages or viewpoints around central Flores.
