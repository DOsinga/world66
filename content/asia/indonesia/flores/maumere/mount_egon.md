Mount Egon is the active volcano east of Maumere, with a crater hike and broad views over the coast when conditions are good. It is a proper mountain outing rather than a casual town walk.

Ask locally before setting off. Egon has erupted in recent decades, and gas, weather, or official warnings can make the crater a bad idea even when it looks calm from town.
