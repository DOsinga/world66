Syuru is one of the villages around Agats where visitors can begin to understand Asmat river settlement: wooden walkways, tidal water, canoes, and carving traditions that belong to daily life rather than to a display case. Go with a local contact or guide, and treat photography and home visits as things to ask about, not assume.
