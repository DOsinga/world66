The valley's only town and the practical hub for any trip into the Baliem. The Pasar Jibama market on the edge of town is the most interesting single stop in Wamena itself — Dani farmers come down from the surrounding hills in traditional dress, and the produce on display (sweet potato varieties, fern shoots, freshwater fish, smoked pork) is the staple of the highland diet.

Wamena has the valley's airport, its hospital, the police permit office, basic guesthouses, and a handful of warungs. Don't expect culture inside town — go for the perimeter villages and the trekking routes north and east.
