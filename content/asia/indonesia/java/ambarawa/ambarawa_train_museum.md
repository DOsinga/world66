Old steam locomotives and carriages from the colonial era are on display at this station, formerly known as *Koening Willem I* (opened 1873). Visitors can take a nostalgic ride in wooden carriages pulled by a 1907 locomotive along the scenic Ambarawa–Bedono route through beautiful mountain landscape. The 9-kilometre line is one of the last surviving cog railways in Asia and climbs sharply through bamboo forests and tea plantations.

Steam rides require a minimum group and are best booked a day or two ahead; diesel-hauled trips run more regularly. Even without a ride the open-air collection of two dozen 19th- and early-20th-century engines is worth an hour.
