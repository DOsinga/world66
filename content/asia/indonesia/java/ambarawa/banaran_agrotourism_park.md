Banaran Agrotourism Park is a coffee-plantation attraction east of Ambarawa, operated by state-owned PTPN 9. The setting is the main draw, with plantation views and family activities such as horse riding, swimming, camping, and off-road rides.

It is best treated as a relaxed half-day stop if you are already exploring Ambarawa or Rawa Pening, rather than as a cultural sight on its own.
