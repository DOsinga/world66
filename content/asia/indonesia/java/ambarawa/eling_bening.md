Eling Bening is a landscaped hilltop leisure area above Ambarawa, best known for its wide views over
Rawa Pening lake and the volcanic skyline of Central Java. The site mixes gardens, pools, photo
platforms, and restaurant terraces, so it is more scenic leisure park than natural wilderness.

Its strongest reason to visit is the panorama. On a clear day, the lake, rice fields, and mountains
make this one of the easiest viewpoints in the Ambarawa area, especially for travellers already
visiting the railway museum, Gedong Songo, or Bandungan.
