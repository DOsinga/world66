title: Getting There
type: section
---

Ambarawa sits roughly midway between [Semarang](/asia/indonesia/java/semarang) (40 km north, one hour by car) and [Yogyakarta](/asia/indonesia/java/yogyakarta) (90 km south, two and a half to three hours) on the main central Java road. Buses between the two cities pass through frequently; ask to be dropped at *terminal Bawen* or *terminal Ambarawa*.

Despite the famous Train Museum, the regular rail line through Ambarawa closed decades ago. The heritage trains from the museum run as tourist attractions only — they are not transport.
