**Indonesia Menggugat** by Sukarno. The defence speech Sukarno wrote in 1930 while imprisoned at the [Banceuy Prison](/asia/indonesia/java/bandung/banceuy_prison) in central Bandung. It became one of the founding documents of Indonesian nationalism. Short, fiery, and still widely read.

**This Earth of Mankind** (Bumi Manusia) by Pramoedya Ananta Toer. The first of the Buru Quartet, set in the late-colonial Dutch East Indies. Not Bandung-specific but the best introduction to the Java that Dutch Bandung emerged from — and a book that helps you read the Asia-Afrika Conference of 1955 in its full context.

**Twilight in Jakarta** by Mochtar Lubis. A 1950s political novel by one of Indonesia's great journalists, capturing the texture of independence-era urban Java. Useful background for any visit to Bandung's colonial-modern core.

**The Bandung Connection** by Roeslan Abdulgani. A first-person account of organising the 1955 Bandung Conference, written by Indonesia's foreign minister at the time. The best context for visiting the [Gedung Merdeka](/asia/indonesia/java/bandung/conference_walk) and the Asian-African Conference Museum.
