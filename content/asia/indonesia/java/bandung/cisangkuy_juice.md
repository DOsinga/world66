Jalan Cisangkuy beside Taman Lansia (Elderly People's Park) is the city's juice-and-yoghurt strip, where a row of identical wooden stalls have been competing on freshness for decades. The classic order is strawberry-yoghurt — fresh strawberries from the Ciwidey farms, house yoghurt, a swirl of palm sugar — served in a tall glass with a long spoon.

Most stalls also do *colenak* (grilled fermented cassava with palm sugar), and the surrounding warungs serve cheap nasi goreng. Easy to combine with Gedung Sate just across the park.
