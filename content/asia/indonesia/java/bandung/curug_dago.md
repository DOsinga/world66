The 12-metre Curug Dago waterfall sits in a small forest reserve at the top of the Dago plateau, an easy 15-minute walk from the end of Jalan Dago. The path through bamboo and *karet* (rubber) trees is well marked. The pool below the falls is shallow and pleasant for paddling on a hot day, though it can be busy with families at weekends.

A short way uphill from the main viewing platform are two inscribed stones placed by the Thai royal family in the early 20th century, easy to miss without a guide. The reserve is open daily, with a small entrance fee paid at the gate.
