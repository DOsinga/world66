Dago Dreampark is a sprawling themed park on the upper Dago plateau, designed in a series of photo-friendly themed zones — fairy-tale castle, log-cabin village, hobbit hole, oversized chairs, hot air balloon. It is what it is: a stop optimised for family photos rather than serious sightseeing.

The setting at 1,100 metres is cool and breezy, the lawns are well kept, and the surrounding forested valleys are genuinely scenic. Easy day-trip from Bandung, particularly with kids. Buy a ticket bundle at the gate for access to the rides and zones.
