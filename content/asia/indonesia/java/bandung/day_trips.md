---
title: 'Day Trips'
type: section
---

The volcanic landscape around Bandung is the real draw. [Tangkuban Perahu](/asia/indonesia/java/bandung/tangkuban_perahu), the most-visited destination, is 30km north — an active volcano whose main crater you can reach by road and peer into from the rim, with sulphur vents and a lunar landscape at 2,084 metres. From there it is worth continuing to the [Ciater hot springs](/asia/indonesia/java/bandung/ciater_hot_springs), where natural thermal pools sit in a tea plantation. The scenery on the road through the tea country is excellent.

To the south, [Kawah Putih](/asia/indonesia/java/bandung/kawah_putih) is a striking turquoise sulphuric lake set in a collapsed caldera near Ciwidey, 46km from Bandung. The same area has strawberry farms you can pick from, and [Situ Patenggang](/asia/indonesia/java/bandung/situ_patenggang), a peaceful highland lake nearby, completes the loop. Combine with [Ranca Upas](/asia/indonesia/java/bandung/ranca_upas) for a relaxing day among deer and tea bushes.

Closer to the city, [Maribaya and Tebing Keraton](/asia/indonesia/java/bandung/maribaya) above Dago are an easy half-day for forest walks and a sunrise viewpoint. The [Lembang Floating Market](/asia/indonesia/java/bandung/floating_market) and [Farmhouse Lembang](/asia/indonesia/java/bandung/farmhouse_lembang) are popular family stops on the way north.
