Kebun Begonia is a flower garden in the hills north of Bandung, planted with bright beds of begonias and other ornamental flowers. It is a simple attraction, but the colour, cooler Lembang air, and mountain setting make it an easy stop if you are already exploring the area.

The garden is best for a short visit and photographs rather than a long botanical outing. Combine it with other Lembang sights so the trip out of central Bandung feels worthwhile.
