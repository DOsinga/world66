Maranatha Protestant Church is a 1920s Protestant church in eastern Bandung, designed by F. W. Brinkman and opened in 1926 as the Oosterkerk. Its Dutch colonial-era architecture makes it a useful stop for visitors tracing Bandung's early 20th-century buildings.

It is a brief architectural visit rather than a major sight, but it adds context to Bandung's mix of civic, religious, and colonial design from the same period.
