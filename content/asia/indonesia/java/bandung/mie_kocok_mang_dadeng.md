Mie Kocok is a Bandung invention: thick yellow noodles in a clear beef broth, topped with sliced *kikil* (tendon), bean sprouts, lime, fried shallots, and emping crackers. Mang Dadeng on Jalan Banteng has been turning it out since the 1970s and remains the benchmark.

It is a one-dish operation — you sit, you order, you get a steaming bowl in under a minute. The broth has serious depth, slow-simmered with bones for hours. Add the sambal, squeeze the lime over, and finish with a glass of sweet tea. Open from morning until early afternoon; come before 1pm to avoid the queues and the chance of running out.
