A Dutch-themed pub on the upper end of Braga Street, North Sea Barge has been pouring imported beer to expats, journalists, and curious locals since the 1990s. The interior is what you might expect: dark wood, fishing nets, model ships, a long bar with a row of taps. There is a small menu of pub food — fish and chips, *bitterballen*, schnitzel — but the focus is the beer.

Live music a couple of nights a week, usually rock and blues covers. Useful as a sit-down break on a wider Braga Street wander.
