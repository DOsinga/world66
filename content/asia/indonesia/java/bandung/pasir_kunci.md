Pasir Kunci is a cultural village on the northeastern edge of Bandung, set among paddy fields and foothills. It is intended to preserve and present Sundanese arts, food, and village traditions, making it a useful counterpoint to Bandung's city traffic and shopping streets.

Go when there is a cultural program or performance scheduled; without one, the setting is pleasant but the visit is quieter.
