A peaceful crater-rimmed lake about 50km southwest of Bandung, ringed by tea plantations of the Rancabali estate. The lake sits at 1,600 metres, the air is cool, and the surface is usually unruffled even in the afternoons. Rowboats and paddle boats are for hire by the hour, and a small wooden bridge crosses to a tiny island in the middle. Best combined with [Kawah Putih](/asia/indonesia/java/bandung/kawah_putih), which is fifteen minutes' drive north on the same road.

The Sundanese legend tied to the lake involves two lovers — Ki Santang and Dewi Rengganis — who were separated and met again only after wandering the world; "Patenggang" derives from a Sundanese word meaning "to look for." There is a heart-shaped stone on the shore that locals call Batu Cinta.
