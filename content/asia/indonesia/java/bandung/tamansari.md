Tamansari is the kampung that climbs the slope north of central Bandung between Jalan Wastukencana and the Cikapundung river valley. The neighbourhood is dense, working-class, and visually striking: narrow alleys, tiled rooftops cascading down toward the river, painted houses in a kaleidoscope of colours — an unofficial street art project that has covered entire facades.

Wandering here is rewarding for anyone interested in everyday Indonesian urban life. The 200 Brothers area near the Pasupati Bridge approach is the most photographed stretch. Behave like a guest, ask before photographing inside the alleys.
