---
title: 'Things to Do'
type: section
---

Bandung's sights divide neatly between its Art Deco colonial heritage in the city centre and the volcanic landscape on its northern and southern fringes. In the centre, the [Gedung Sate](/asia/indonesia/java/bandung/gedung_sate) and its surrounding civic buildings are a short walk from [Braga Street](/asia/indonesia/java/bandung/braga_street), the old colonial promenade now lined with cafes and boutiques. The pedestrianised [Asia-Afrika walk](/asia/indonesia/java/bandung/conference_walk) ties them together, with the [Asian-African Conference Museum](/asia/indonesia/java/bandung/asian_african_conference_museum) and the [Hotel Savoy Homann](/asia/indonesia/java/bandung/hotel_savoy_homann) as the obvious centrepieces.

Further out, [Villa Isola](/asia/indonesia/java/bandung/villa_isola) and the [ITB campus](/asia/indonesia/java/bandung/itb_campus) are the best of the early-20th-century architecture beyond the colonial core. The [Geological Museum](/asia/indonesia/java/bandung/geological_museum) explains why the city sits where it does — inside an ancient caldera — and [Saung Angklung Udjo](/asia/indonesia/java/bandung/saung_udjo) is the place to hear the Sundanese bamboo orchestra performed by people who have spent their lives mastering it.

The volcanic landscape is the next layer. [Tangkuban Perahu](/asia/indonesia/java/bandung/tangkuban_perahu) is the most accessible volcano, [Kawah Putih](/asia/indonesia/java/bandung/kawah_putih) the most photogenic crater lake, and the tea plantations around [Ciater](/asia/indonesia/java/bandung/ciater_hot_springs) and [Situ Patenggang](/asia/indonesia/java/bandung/situ_patenggang) offer pleasant high-altitude scenery for a day's drive.
