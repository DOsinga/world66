Batu Karas Beach is the reason most travellers come. The main break is a soft right-hander that suits longboards and surf lessons, with board rentals and instructors clustered close to the sand.

The wave lacks the power advanced surfers usually seek in Indonesia, but it is forgiving and consistent enough for learning. The beach is also pleasant for walking, especially early or near sunset, when fishing boats and surfers share the bay.
