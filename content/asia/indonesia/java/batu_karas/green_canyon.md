Green Canyon, locally known as Cukang Taneuh, is a short trip from Batu Karas and one of the area's best inland sights. Small boats travel up a green river between jungle banks and narrow rock walls, with swimming and cliff-jumping possible when water levels are safe.

The colour and clarity depend heavily on rain. After heavy downpours the water can turn brown for a day or two, so ask locally before setting out.
