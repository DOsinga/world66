Mount Kelud is an active volcano northeast of Blitar, known for forceful eruptions and a crater landscape that can change quickly. When access is open, the trip gives Blitar a dramatic natural counterpoint to its Sukarno and Majapahit history.

Check local conditions before going. Kelud is not a decorative hill; closures, damaged roads, and restricted crater access are normal after periods of activity.
