Sukarno's grave is Blitar's main pilgrimage site: a formal mausoleum complex with the first president's tomb, a museum, and a library. It is busy with Indonesian visitors, school groups, and political memory rather than foreign-tourist gloss.

Come here first if you want Blitar to make sense. The town's national importance is tied less to its streets than to the way Sukarno's story is preserved here.
