Terawang Cave is one of Blora's more straightforward natural sights, reached in the limestone country west of town. The formations are modest by major cave standards, but the setting helps explain how different this dry, wooded part of Java feels from the island's better-known volcanic highlands.

It is best paired with other nearby stops rather than treated as a full-day destination on its own.
