Bogor is a quick escape from [Jakarta](/asia/indonesia/java/jakarta), about an hour south by train. The city is best known for the [Bogor Botanical Gardens (Kebun Raya)](/asia/indonesia/java/bogor/bogor_botanical_gardens), one of the oldest and most impressive tropical gardens in Asia, home to over 15,000 species of trees and plants including the giant *Amorphophallus titanum* (corpse flower). The [Presidential Palace](/asia/indonesia/java/bogor/bogor_palace) sits within the gardens, its grounds roamed by a herd of spotted deer. Bogor gets more rain than almost anywhere in Java, which keeps everything lush and green.

Beyond the gardens, the [Bogor Zoology Museum](/asia/indonesia/java/bogor/zoology_museum) holds one of the largest natural history collections in Southeast Asia — founded in 1894, with particular strength in Indonesian bird specimens. The [Kujang Monument](/asia/indonesia/java/bogor/kujang_monument) at the southern roundabout commemorates the distinctive Sundanese ceremonial dagger that has become the city's symbol. For food, seek out soto Bogor — the city's light, clear beef broth soup — at the warungs near the train station.
