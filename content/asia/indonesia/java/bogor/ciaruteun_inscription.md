The Ciaruteun Inscription is an early stone inscription associated with the Tarumanagara kingdom, one of the important Hindu-Buddhist polities of western Java. Its carved script and footprint symbols make it a compact but significant archaeological stop near Bogor.

It is best for travellers interested in Indonesian history beyond colonial gardens and volcano scenery. The site is outside the central city, so plan transport and combine it with other heritage or countryside stops if possible.
