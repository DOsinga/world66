Prasasti Batutulis is an inscribed stone associated with the old Sunda Kingdom, making it one of Bogor's most important pre-colonial relics. In a city often framed by botanical gardens and Dutch colonial history, it points to a much older Sundanese political landscape.

The site is small, and interpretation may be limited, but the object matters. Pair it with the Bogor Palace and the botanical gardens to see how many layers of power have occupied this cool, strategic city south of Jakarta.
