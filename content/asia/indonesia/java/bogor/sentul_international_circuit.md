Sentul International Circuit is Indonesia's best-known permanent racing track, set in the Sentul area between Bogor and Jakarta. It has hosted major national and regional motorsport events and remains the obvious stop for travellers who follow racing.

The circuit is not a casual walk-up attraction every day, so it only makes sense when there is an event, track activity, or a specific motorsport reason to go. For fans, though, it adds a different side to Bogor beyond gardens, palaces, and mountain air.
