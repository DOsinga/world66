Soto Bogor is the city's distinctive variant of the Indonesian clear broth soup — lighter and more delicate than the Javanese versions, made with beef broth and served with sliced beef, rice cakes, bean sprouts, and a squeeze of lime. Every local has their favourite warung (food stall) that makes it, and opinions on which is best are held with great conviction.

The cheapest and most atmospheric places to eat soto Bogor are the warungs clustered around the Bogor train station and the streets near the Botanical Gardens entrance. Meals are typically eaten standing or at rough plastic tables, and the food arrives within minutes of ordering. If you are coming from Jakarta, arriving hungry is a reasonable strategy.
