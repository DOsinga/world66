Wisata Agro Gunung Mas is a tea estate in the Puncak highlands, surrounded by green slopes and mountain views. Visitors can walk or drive through the plantation tracks, stop at the cafe for estate tea, and, when the factory is operating, see how the leaves are processed.

It is best visited early in the day, before traffic builds on the Puncak road and while the plantation is still active. The open views and cooler air make it a useful break from Bogor or Jakarta.
