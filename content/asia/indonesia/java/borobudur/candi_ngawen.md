Candi Ngawen is a small Buddhist temple complex in the Borobudur area, far quieter than the region's famous monument. Its surviving shrines and carved guardian lions make it a rewarding stop for travellers interested in the wider Kedu Plain temple landscape.

The site is best approached as context rather than competition for Borobudur. It shows how dense the area's sacred geography once was, with smaller temples scattered among villages, fields, and low volcanic horizons.
