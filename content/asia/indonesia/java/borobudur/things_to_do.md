---
title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

The [Borobudur Temple complex](/asia/indonesia/java/borobudur/borobudur_temple) is the overwhelming reason to come here. Walking the galleries from the base to the summit is the experience; the carved relief panels along the lower terraces are among the finest Buddhist narrative sculptures anywhere, and the view from the upper platforms across the Kedu Plain is extraordinary.

[Mendut Temple](/asia/indonesia/java/borobudur/mendut_temple) and [Pawon Temple](/asia/indonesia/java/borobudur/pawon_temple) form a sacred alignment with Borobudur and are worth visiting if you have more than a day. The [Chicken Church](/asia/indonesia/java/borobudur/gereja_ayam) in the forested hills to the west is one of those genuinely odd attractions that defy easy description. The [Kedu Plain](/asia/indonesia/java/borobudur/kedu_plain_viewpoint) is beautiful cycling country — flat, green, volcanic — and exploring by bicycle is an excellent way to spend an afternoon.
