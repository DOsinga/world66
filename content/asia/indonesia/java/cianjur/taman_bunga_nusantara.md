Taman Bunga Nusantara is a large flower garden near Cipanas, with themed gardens, clipped hedges, a maze, a giant floral clock, and a tower for looking over the grounds. It is an easy, colourful stop in the Puncak highlands, especially for families or anyone breaking up the road between Bogor, Cianjur, and Bandung.
