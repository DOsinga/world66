Kejawanan Beach is Cirebon's easiest seaside escape, a low-key stretch of coast used more by local families than by international beach seekers. The appeal is simple: sea air, fishing boats, snacks, sunsets and a break from the heat and traffic of the city.

Do not expect a pristine resort beach. This is a working, everyday waterfront, and that is part of its value for travellers trying to understand Cirebon as a north-coast port city. Go late in the day, when the light softens and the beach is at its most social.
