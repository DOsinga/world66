Strawberry farms line parts of the road through Ciwidey, taking advantage of the cooler upland climate. Most are simple pick-your-own stops with juice, snacks and baskets of fruit rather than polished attractions. They are not worth a special journey alone, but they give the crater-lake day a very Sundanese highland flavour.
