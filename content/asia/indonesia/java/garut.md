*Asia > Indonesia > Java > Garut*

Garut is a Sundanese highland town southeast of [Bandung](/asia/indonesia/java/bandung), surrounded by volcanoes, rice fields, and hot springs. It is not a polished resort town, and that is part of the point: Garut feels local, busy, and lived-in, with the mountains close enough to pull the day out of the streets.

The strongest reason to come is the volcanic country around town. [Mount Papandayan](/asia/indonesia/java/garut/mount_papandayan) has smoking craters, dead forest, and broad alpine meadows that make it one of West Java's most approachable volcano days. Closer to town, [Cipanas](/asia/indonesia/java/garut/cipanas_hot_springs) is the old hot-spring district at the foot of Mount Guntur.

Garut also has a cultural anchor that Bandung lacks: [Cangkuang Temple and Kampung Pulo](/asia/indonesia/java/garut/cangkuang_temple), reached by a short raft crossing in Leles. Add leather workshops and the town's sticky dodol sweets, and Garut clears the bar as a worthwhile highland stop rather than just another Java service town.
