Cipanas is Garut's hot-spring area, set below Mount Guntur just north of town. The appeal is simple: warm mineral water after a volcano walk or a long road trip through West Java.

The facilities vary from basic pools to larger resort-style complexes. Choose for the water and the mountain setting, not for luxury.
