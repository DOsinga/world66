Ancol Dream Park is Jakarta's big waterfront leisure complex, combining Dufan amusement park, Atlantis Water Park, Ocean Dream, Ocean Ecopark, bowling, restaurants, and the city's best-known beach. It is crowded on weekends but remains the main family day out in North Jakarta.
