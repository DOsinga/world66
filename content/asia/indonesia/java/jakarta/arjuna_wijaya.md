Arjuna Wijaya is a dramatic chariot statue near the western side of Merdeka Square, showing Arjuna and Krishna from the Mahabharata. Created by Indonesian sculptor Nyoman Nuarta, it works best as a quick public-art stop while walking between Monas and central Jakarta's government district.
