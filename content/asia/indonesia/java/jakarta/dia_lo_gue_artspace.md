Dia.lo.gue Artspace is a contemporary art centre in South Jakarta, with changing exhibitions and a cafe folded into the same complex. The name plays on the Indonesian words for she or he, you, and me, which suits a place built around conversation as much as display.

The visit depends on what is showing, but it is a useful stop if you want to see Jakarta's current design and art scene away from the big museums.
