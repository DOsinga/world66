Galangan VOC is a surviving piece of old Batavia's maritime trade network, dating to the Dutch East India Company era. The former ship repair yard and trade office is now used commercially, but enough of the historic structure remains to make it a worthwhile stop for visitors tracing Jakarta's colonial waterfront.
