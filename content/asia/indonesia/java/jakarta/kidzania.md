KidZania Jakarta is an indoor role-play city for children, where kids try out jobs and everyday adult tasks in a scaled-down setting. Activities include media, detective work, racing, and factory-style demonstrations.

It is most useful for families with children aged roughly 4 to 16, especially on hot or rainy days when an indoor activity is more practical than sightseeing outside.
