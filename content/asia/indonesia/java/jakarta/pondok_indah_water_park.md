Pondok Indah Water Park is a family-oriented water park in South Jakarta, better for an easy day with children than for serious thrill rides. The slides are modest, but the pools and managed setting make it a practical break from traffic and sightseeing.

It is most useful if you are staying in or near South Jakarta. For visitors based across town, weigh the travel time before making it a main plan for the day.
