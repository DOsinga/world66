Jakarta's Presidential Palace complex includes the Istana Merdeka and Istana Negara, with colonial-era buildings that became the centre of Dutch administration before later serving the Indonesian presidency. The main palace dates back to a late-18th-century mansion.

Most visitors see the complex from outside while exploring the area around Merdeka Square, the National Monument, and Istiqlal Mosque. Its value is in the history and setting rather than in regular interior access.
