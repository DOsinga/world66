The Proclamation Museum marks the place where Soekarno and Hatta read Indonesia's Declaration of Independence on August 17, 1945. Exhibits use dioramas, mock-ups, documents, and propaganda posters to explain the events around independence.

It is a compact but important stop for understanding modern Indonesia, especially if you are already visiting Menteng, the National Monument, or Jakarta's other independence-era sites.
