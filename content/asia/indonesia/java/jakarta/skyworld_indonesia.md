Skyworld Indonesia is a space and astronomy attraction in the Taman Mini Indonesia Indah area. It mixes educational exhibits with family entertainment, including a planetarium-style program, space-themed displays, a 5D cinema, and children's play areas.

The appeal is strongest for families or anyone already spending time at Taman Mini. Treat it as a hands-on science stop rather than a major museum.
