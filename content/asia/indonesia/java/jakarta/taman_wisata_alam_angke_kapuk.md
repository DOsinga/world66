Taman Wisata Alam Angke Kapuk is a mangrove park on Jakarta's north coast, with boardwalks, planted wetland areas, and a chance to see a very different side of the city from its malls and traffic.

It is more managed recreation area than untouched reserve, but the mangroves, water channels, and birdlife make it a worthwhile stop for visitors interested in urban nature.
