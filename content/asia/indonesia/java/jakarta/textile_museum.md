Jakarta's Textile Museum focuses on Indonesian cloth, motifs, dyes, and weaving traditions, with a dedicated batik gallery and displays of textiles from across the archipelago. It is a good complement to the larger national museums because it looks closely at materials, patterns, and craft.

Some visits include hands-on batik-making sessions, and the museum also has looms and information on traditional dye colours. Check current workshop availability before planning around it.
