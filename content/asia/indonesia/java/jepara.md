Jepara sits on Java's north coast, northeast of [Semarang](/asia/indonesia/java/semarang), and has a clearer identity than many towns along this busy shore. It is Indonesia's best-known centre for carved teak furniture, the birthplace of women's education pioneer R.A. Kartini, and one of the mainland ports for boats to [Karimunjawa](/asia/indonesia/java/karimunjawa).

The town itself is not polished. Traffic, workshops, showrooms, and ferry errands all mix together, but that is part of the point. Jepara is a working craft town, not a beach resort pretending to be one.

Give it a day if you are already travelling the north coast of Java. Visit the [Kartini Museum](/asia/indonesia/java/jepara/kartini_museum), look for weaving in [Troso Village](/asia/indonesia/java/jepara/troso_village), and finish at [Bandengan Beach](/asia/indonesia/java/jepara/bandengan_beach) or the harbour if you are catching the Karimunjawa boat.
