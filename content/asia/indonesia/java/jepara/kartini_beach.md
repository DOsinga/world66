Kartini Beach is Jepara's easiest coastal stop, with views toward Panjang Island and boats leaving for Karimunjawa. It is more developed than wild, but useful if you want a simple waterfront walk before or after the ferry.

The beach area also has Kura-Kura Ocean Park, a turtle-shaped family attraction with aquariums and children's exhibits.
