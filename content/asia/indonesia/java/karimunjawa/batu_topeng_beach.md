Batu Topeng Beach is one of Karimunjawa's quieter west-coast beaches, with shallow turquoise water, pale sand, and a line of trees giving shade behind the shore. It is the kind of beach that works best when you do very little: swim, read, and wait for the evening light.

The beach sits close enough to other island stops to fold into a scooter loop, especially with Tanjung Gelam and Bukit Love. Facilities can be simple, so bring what you need and treat it as a scenic pause rather than a serviced resort beach.
