Kemujan Island forms the northern part of the Karimunjawa island group and is linked to the main island by a causeway. It has a slower, more spread-out feel, with mangroves, beaches, and village roads that suit travellers with a motorbike or local transport.

Come here when you want to look beyond the harbour village and standard snorkelling trips. The appeal is not a single monument but the combination of island scenery, coastal stops, and a quieter look at daily life in Karimunjawa.
