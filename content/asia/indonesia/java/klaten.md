Klaten sits in the flat, fertile country between [Yogyakarta](/asia/indonesia/java/yogyakarta) and [Solo](/asia/indonesia/java/solo). Many travelers pass through it on the main road or train line, but the regency rewards a slower day with old temples, rice-field views, and spring-fed swimming pools that are part picnic spot, part local ritual of cooling off.

The best-known ancient site on this side of the plain is [Candi Plaosan](/asia/indonesia/java/klaten/candi_plaosan), a Buddhist complex close to Prambanan but quieter and more atmospheric in the late afternoon. Klaten also has smaller temples such as [Candi Merak](/asia/indonesia/java/klaten/candi_merak), where the reward is not scale but the sense of finding a 10th-century shrine still sitting in village country.

Water is the other reason to come. [Umbul Ponggok](/asia/indonesia/java/klaten/umbul_ponggok) is the famous clear spring pool, with fish, underwater photos, and a carnival feel on busy days. Rowo Jombor and the Pusur river tubing scene make the day less temple-heavy, especially if you are travelling with local friends or children.

Klaten works best as a side trip from Yogyakarta or Solo. Start early for the temple plain, take a long lunch, then save the springs or Rowo Jombor for the hotter part of the day.
