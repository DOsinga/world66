Candi Merak is a small Hindu temple rather than a major headline sight. That is part of its value: it shows how scattered the old temple landscape is around Klaten, beyond the famous Prambanan complex.

The temple is best for travelers who already enjoy ancient Javanese architecture and do not mind a simple village setting. Pair it with a spring stop or the sugar museum rather than making it the whole day.
