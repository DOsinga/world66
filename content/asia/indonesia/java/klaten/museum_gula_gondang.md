Museum Gula Gondang tells the plantation and sugar story of the Klaten plain. The collection includes old sugar-processing equipment and steam locomotives, a reminder that this part of Central Java was shaped not only by temples and courts but also by colonial-era industry.

It is a modest museum, but it makes a useful stop if you are moving by car between Yogyakarta, Klaten, and Solo and want something more local than another roadside meal.
