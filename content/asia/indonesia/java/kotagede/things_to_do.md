---
title: Things to Do
type: section
---

Kotagede rewards an unhurried afternoon. The main loop links the silver workshops along Jalan Kemasan, the Grand Mosque compound, the tomb of Senopati and a handful of well-preserved Joglo (traditional Javanese) houses tucked behind narrow lanes. Pick up a free walking-tour leaflet from the tourist information point near the Pasar Kotagede market.
