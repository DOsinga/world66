Lumajang is not one of Java's polished tourist towns. Its centre is practical and spread out, but the regency around it holds some of East Java's strongest volcanic scenery: huge waterfalls dropping from the Semeru foothills, farming villages on cool high slopes, and back-door views toward the Bromo caldera.

The reason to come is the western and northern countryside rather than the town itself. [Tumpak Sewu Waterfall](/asia/indonesia/java/lumajang/tumpak_sewu_waterfall) is the headline, a great curtain of water in a horseshoe canyon below Mount Semeru. Nearby [Goa Tetes](/asia/indonesia/java/lumajang/goa_tetes) and [Kapas Biru Waterfall](/asia/indonesia/java/lumajang/kapas_biru_waterfall) make this one of Java's best waterfall clusters if you are willing to get wet and muddy.

Lumajang also gives access to [Puncak B29](/asia/indonesia/java/lumajang/puncak_b29), a high village viewpoint over Bromo's sea of sand from a less obvious angle than the standard Probolinggo route. It is still rural and weather-dependent, which is part of the appeal. Come with time, an early start, and shoes that can handle steep paths.
