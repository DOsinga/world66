Kapas Biru is a tall, narrow waterfall south of the Semeru slopes, reached by a steeper and rougher approach than its postcard looks suggest. The fall drops cleanly against dark rock and green forest, with a cooler, less crowded feel than Tumpak Sewu on busy days.

The trail usually takes commitment: dirt paths, bamboo sections, and a final approach that can be slippery after rain. It works well as a second waterfall on a full Pronojiwo day, especially for travellers who want the landscape without quite as many people around them.
