---
title: Eating Out
type: section
---

Malang is one of East Java's best eating cities. **Bakso Malang** — meatball soup with noodles, fried tofu, and crispy wontons — is the local dish and the street version eaten standing up is often the best. **Cwie Mie Malang** (egg noodles in a clear broth with wontons) is the other signature dish. **Rawon**, the dark black beef soup coloured by buah keluak paste, is served throughout East Java but Malang's version is among the finest.

The **Pasar Besar** traditional market is the best place for cheap eating in the morning — nasi pecel (rice with vegetables in peanut sauce), steamed corn, and fried snacks. **Jalan Tumapel** in the centre has numerous bakso stalls that locals rate highly. For something more comfortable, **Toko Oen** on Jalan Basuki Rahmat is the colonial-era institution worth visiting at least once.
