Ijen Boulevard is one of Malang's best streets for a slow walk, with bougainvillea, colonial-era buildings, and several nearby landmarks including the Brawijaya Army Museum and a Catholic church. On Sunday mornings, the car-free event brings food stalls and a local market atmosphere.
