Madakaripura Waterfall drops through a narrow, green-sided gorge in the foothills near Mount Bromo. Visitors usually reach it by road from Sukapura or as a side trip before or after the volcano.

Expect a wet approach through spray and shallow water. It is most rewarding with your own transport or a driver, since it sits away from the main village stops.
