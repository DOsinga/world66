---
title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

The essential experiences at Bromo are the dawn viewpoint and the crater climb, both of which can be done in a single morning. Beyond these, the Tengger caldera rewards exploration: the sea of sand that fills the caldera floor is worth crossing on horseback or on foot, and the crater rim has trails that take you away from the crowds. [Mount Semeru](/asia/indonesia/java/mountbromo/mount_semeru), visible from Bromo as the tallest peak on Java, is a multi-day trek for serious hikers. The villages on the caldera rim — Cemoro Lawang, Ngadisari — offer glimpses of the Tengger Hindu culture that has survived here since the Majapahit era.
