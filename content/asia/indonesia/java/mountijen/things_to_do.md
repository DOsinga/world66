title: Things to Do
type: section
---

Everything at Mount Ijen centres on the volcano. The essential experience is the [blue fire](/asia/indonesia/java/mountijen/blue_fire) — the otherworldly electric flames that burn at the volcanic vents before dawn, visible only in darkness. Reaching them requires a midnight start and the [crater rim hike](/asia/indonesia/java/mountijen/crater_rim_hike), then a descent into the crater itself. Once at the crater floor, you encounter both the [Kawah Ijen](/asia/indonesia/java/mountijen/kawah_ijen) acid lake — the world's largest — and the [sulfur miners](/asia/indonesia/java/mountijen/sulfur_miners) at work, one of the most intense human-labour environments still operating at this scale.

At sunrise, the crater rim offers extraordinary views over the turquoise lake and across the [Ijen plateau](/asia/indonesia/java/mountijen/ijen_plateau) to the coast. A gas mask is essential for crater descent; these can be rented at the Paltuding basecamp.
