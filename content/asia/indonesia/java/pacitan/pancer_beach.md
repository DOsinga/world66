Pancer Beach sits on Pacitan Bay, close enough to town to be the simplest coastal stop. It is known more for surfing and local beach life than for the dramatic rock scenery found farther west.

Use it when you want an easy sunset, a look at the bay, or a quick surf check without spending the day on the coastal roads.
