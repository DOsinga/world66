Tabuhan Cave is known for its musical stone formations: some of the stalactites and stalagmites produce tones when tapped, giving the cave its local reputation as a natural instrument. It is a smaller, stranger stop than Gong Cave.

Treat the formations gently and follow local guidance. The appeal here is the odd detail, not a long expedition.
