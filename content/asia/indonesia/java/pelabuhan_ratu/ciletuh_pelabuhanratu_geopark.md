Ciletuh-Pelabuhanratu Geopark spreads west of town across a dramatic stretch of southern West Java. Its attractions include waterfalls, cliffs, beaches, and an amphitheatre-like landscape facing the Indian Ocean.

The geopark is the strongest reason to base yourself in Pelabuhan Ratu for more than a quick beach stop. Distances are longer than they look on a map, so plan transport carefully rather than assuming you can cover everything casually in one afternoon.
