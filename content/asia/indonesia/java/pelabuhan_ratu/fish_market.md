The fish market sits by the port and is at its best when the day's catch is moving through. It is noisy, wet, and practical, with the kind of direct coastal economy that makes Pelabuhan Ratu feel like a real town rather than a beach set.

Come early, keep out of the workers' way, and expect strong smells. For photographers and food-minded travellers, it is one of the more grounded stops in town.
