Pura Pulaki stands on the coast east of Pemuteran, with black stone, a cliff backdrop, and macaques that make the visit lively. It is one of north Bali's sea temples, part of the island's chain of coastal shrines.

This is an active Hindu temple, not a set piece. Wear a sarong, keep food and loose glasses away from the monkeys, and give worshippers space.
