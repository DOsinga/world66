The BRI Museum in Purwokerto focuses on the origins and development of Bank Rakyat Indonesia, whose roots are closely tied to the city. It is a specialist museum, but it gives a local angle on Indonesian economic and institutional history.

The museum is best for travellers who enjoy civic history or want something beyond nature excursions around Purwokerto. Keep expectations modest and confirm hours before making a special trip.
