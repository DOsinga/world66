---
title: 'Day Trips'
type: section
---

The hills north of Purwokerto are an easy half-day or full day out. **Baturraden** is the closest and most popular — 45 minutes by angkot, with hot springs and waterfalls on the slopes of Mount Slamet. The much wider **Dieng Plateau** to the north-east, three hours by road, is one of the most spectacular places in Central Java with its ancient Hindu temples, sulphur lakes and coloured volcanic terrain; it can just about be done as a long day trip but is better done as an overnight from Wonosobo.

Down on the south coast (about 90 minutes by train or car) the beach at **Cilacap** is more interesting for its grim history than its sand — the offshore island of **Nusa Kambangan** still houses Indonesia's maximum-security prison, and visitors can take ferries to the southern beach of Pasir Putih. Closer to home, the old colonial town of **Banyumas** itself (15 km east) has a small but well-curated museum of regional history in the former bupati's residence.
