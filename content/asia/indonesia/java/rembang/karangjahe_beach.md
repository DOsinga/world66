Karangjahe Beach is a popular local beach in Punjulharjo village, about 7.5 km east of Rembang on the road toward Lasem. It is not a remote tropical beach; it is a family picnic place, with shade trees, snack stalls, mats, and simple beach activities.

That makes it useful. Come in the morning or late afternoon, eat something simple by the water, and treat it as a look at how Rembang families use the coast.
