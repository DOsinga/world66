The R.A. Kartini Museum is the main reason to stop in Rembang itself. It occupies the former residence of the Rembang regent, where Kartini lived after marrying R.M.A.A. Djojoadiningrat and where she continued to write about education, women, and Javanese society.

The museum displays personal objects, family material, and rooms connected with Kartini's life in Rembang. The presentation can be plain, but the place matters: it links one of Indonesia's most important modern voices to an actual house and town.
