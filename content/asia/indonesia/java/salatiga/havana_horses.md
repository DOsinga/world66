Havana Horses is a riding stable near Salatiga, useful for travellers who want a guided outdoor activity rather than another town-centre stop. The appeal is the Central Java countryside: villages, fields, and the volcanic backdrop around Salatiga.

Because it is an operator-led activity, contact ahead and do not assume walk-up rides are available. It makes most sense for riders or families staying in the area, not as a long-distance detour from Semarang or Yogyakarta.
