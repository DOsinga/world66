The Plumpungan Inscription is a small but locally important archaeological marker in Salatiga, often treated as evidence for the area's early historical identity. Its scale is modest, but inscriptions like this are valuable because they connect a modern city to older Javanese polities and settlement patterns.

It is a short stop for history-minded visitors rather than a broad tourist attraction. Pair it with central Salatiga and nearby mountain excursions if you want both local heritage and landscape.
