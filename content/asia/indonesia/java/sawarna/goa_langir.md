Goa Langir is a beach-and-cave stop west of Sawarna village, where pale limestone cliffs meet a quiet stretch of sand. The cave openings and rock walls give it a different mood from the open surf beach at Ciantir.

Go with daylight and decent footwear if you plan to poke around the rocks. The beach is more about exploring, photos, and watching the coast than swimming far offshore.
