Gedung MARBA is a mid-19th-century commercial building in Semarang's Kota Lama. It was associated with Martak Badjened, a Yemen-Indonesian businessman active in export and import trade.

The building is best seen as part of a Kota Lama walk, close to Blenduk Church and other surviving colonial-era architecture.
