Gedung PELNI is an early-20th-century Art Deco Indies building in Semarang's old port district. It was designed by F.J.L. Ghijsels for the Koninklijke Paketvaart Maatschappij, the Dutch colonial shipping company.

The building is most interesting as part of a walk through Kota Lama, where it adds another layer to Semarang's colonial commercial architecture.
