Hutan Wisata Tinjomoyo is a forest recreation area in Semarang that offers a greener break from the city's traffic and lowland heat. It is more local and informal than a national park, but the shade and terrain make it useful for a short outdoor outing.

Travellers should treat it as a modest nature stop rather than a must-see attraction. It works best when paired with other Semarang sights on the southern side of the city.
