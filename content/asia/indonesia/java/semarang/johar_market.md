Johar Market is one of Semarang's great everyday landmarks, a market complex whose concrete halls once showed off a confident modernist answer to tropical heat and crowds. It is still a place of trade first, so expect movement, noise, and practical shopping rather than a staged heritage experience.

For travellers, the interest is the mix of architecture and street life. The market sits close to the old city and the Kauman area, making it easy to fold into a walk through central Semarang. Go in the morning, when the produce, textiles, and food stalls give the building its full working character.
