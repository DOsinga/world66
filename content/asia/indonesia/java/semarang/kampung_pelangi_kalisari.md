Kampung Pelangi Kalisari is a brightly painted village near Semarang's flower market. The area is known for its rainbow-coloured houses and small sustainability projects, making it an easy visual stop close to the city centre.

It works best as a short wander with a camera, especially when paired with nearby central Semarang sights.
