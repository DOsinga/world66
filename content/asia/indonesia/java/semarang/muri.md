MURI, the Indonesian World Records Museum, is one of Semarang's more offbeat stops. Instead of presenting a conventional history collection, it documents record-setting feats, national firsts, and eccentric achievements from across Indonesia.

It is a niche museum, but that is also the appeal. Come for a quick, curious visit if you enjoy unusual local institutions or want something lighter after Semarang's colonial architecture and temples.
