Taman Puri Maerokoco is Semarang's miniature Central Java park, with pavilions and displays representing different parts of the province. It is a consciously packaged attraction, but useful if you want a quick introduction to regional architecture and identity.

The park works best for families or travellers with limited time outside Semarang. Do not expect a substitute for visiting the towns and landscapes represented here; treat it as a sampler and an easy afternoon by the water.
