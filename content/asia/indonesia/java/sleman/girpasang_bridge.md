Girpasang Bridge is a pedestrian suspension bridge in the hills north of Sleman, near the Merapi area. It crosses a steep river valley, with a small gondola nearby and wide views across the surrounding slopes.

The bridge is a light adventure stop rather than a major sight. It makes most sense if you are already exploring Kaliurang, Ullen Sentalu, or the Merapi viewpoints and want a short walk with a bit of height.
