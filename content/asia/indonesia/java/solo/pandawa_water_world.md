Pandawa Water World is a family waterpark south of central Solo. It has swimming pools, slides, play areas, and artificial waves, making it a practical break from temple, palace, and market sightseeing for families with children.

It is a purpose-built leisure stop rather than a cultural sight, but it can be useful on hot days or when travelling with kids who need a few hours of water play.
