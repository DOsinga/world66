The Sheikh Zayed Grand Mosque is the newest major landmark in Solo, opened in 2022 as a gift from the United Arab Emirates at a cost of roughly US$20 million. The design is a deliberate reproduction of the Abu Dhabi original, complete with white marble, ornate tilework in shades of blue, and the four-minaret configuration of the Gulf model.

The mosque is large enough to hold thousands of worshippers and has quickly become a popular attraction for both religious visitors and tourists interested in the architecture. Non-Muslim visitors are welcome at designated times — the interior is as impressive as the exterior, with imported marble and elaborate chandelier work. The mosque is a short ride north of the main palace area.
