Solo Safari is Surakarta’s revived zoo and wildlife park, a family-oriented attraction that has become one of the city’s easier outdoor stops. It mixes animal enclosures, landscaped paths, and light entertainment in a way that suits travellers with children.

It is not the reason to come to Solo, where palaces, batik, and Javanese culture matter more, but it broadens the city’s visitor options. Go with realistic expectations: it is a leisure park as much as a conservation-minded zoo.
