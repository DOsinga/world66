Curug Sawer is the waterfall walk that completes a Situ Gunung visit. The path runs through damp, green forest, and the falls give the bridge excursion a natural endpoint rather than just a photo stop.

Wear shoes that can handle mud and wet stone, especially after rain. The visit is short, but the forest setting makes it one of the better half-day escapes from Sukabumi.
