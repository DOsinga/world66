Mochi Lampion is a useful stop for Sukabumi's best-known snack: soft, chewy mochi with sweet fillings. The style came through local Chinese-Indonesian food culture and has become one of the city's edible calling cards.

It is not a destination restaurant, but it is a good place to pick up a box before a train ride or mountain trip.
