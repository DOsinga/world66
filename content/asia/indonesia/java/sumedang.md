A small Sundanese town between [Bandung](/asia/indonesia/java/bandung) and Cirebon, Sumedang is famous across Java for one thing above all: its tahu. The deep-fried tofu sold by [roadside vendors](/asia/indonesia/java/sumedang/tahu_sumedang_vendors) along the main road is a distinct style — hollow inside, crackling shell outside — that Indonesians across the country recognise as a Sumedang specialty. Stopping to eat a portion with sambal and lontong is essentially mandatory for anyone passing through.

Beyond the tofu, Sumedang rewards a short stop rather than an overnight stay. The [Museum Prabu Geusan Ulun](/asia/indonesia/java/sumedang/museum_prabu_geusan_ulun) holds the crown jewels and weapons of the Sumedang Larang kingdom, the Sundanese successor state that maintained independence for nearly a century after the fall of the Pajajaran Kingdom. It is a small but focused collection, worth an hour of anyone's time. [Gunung Kunci](/asia/indonesia/java/sumedang/gunung_kunci), a wooded hill a short walk from the [alun-alun](/asia/indonesia/java/sumedang/alun_alun_sumedang), still has Dutch-era fortifications embedded in its slopes. None of this justifies extending a trip specifically to Sumedang, but it makes the stop between Bandung and Cirebon more interesting than the usual roadside break.
