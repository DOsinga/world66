---
title: Things to Do
type: section
---

Sumedang's sights are few but worthwhile if you have a couple of hours between Bandung and Cirebon. The Museum Prabu Geusan Ulun is the obvious anchor, flanked by the colonial-era Gunung Kunci hill fort and the alun-alun square. Beyond that, the main activity in town is eating — specifically the tofu that made Sumedang famous across Java.
