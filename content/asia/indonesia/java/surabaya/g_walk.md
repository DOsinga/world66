G-Walk is a night food strip in CitraLand, west Surabaya, with stalls, bazaars and casual places to eat. It gets busiest after dark, when students and local families come out for snacks, drinks and an easy evening wander.

It is not a polished sightseeing stop, but it is useful if you are staying in west Surabaya and want a lively local food scene rather than another mall meal.
