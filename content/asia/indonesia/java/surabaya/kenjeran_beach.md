Kenjeran Beach is Surabaya's best-known city beach, with dark sand, views toward the Suramadu Bridge, and a waterfront that is busiest around sunset. It is more a local outing spot than a resort beach, but the bridge views and evening fountain show make it a worthwhile stop if you are exploring the city's northeast coast.
