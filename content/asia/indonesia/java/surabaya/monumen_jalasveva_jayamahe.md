Monumen Jalasveva Jayamahe stands on Surabaya's northern waterfront, looking out over the harbour as a tribute to the Indonesian Navy. The statue is one of the city's more dramatic modern landmarks, especially for travellers interested in Surabaya's maritime side.

It works best as part of a northern Surabaya loop with Kalimas Harbour, the naval museum, and the old trading districts around Jembatan Merah.
