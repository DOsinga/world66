Museum Kesehatan Dr. Adhyatma is a specialist museum on the history of health and medical treatment in Indonesia. Its displays move from traditional ideas of illness and healing to the development of modern remedies and public health.

It is a niche stop, but a useful one if you want Surabaya's museums to go beyond banks, tobacco, and colonial buildings. Pair it with the old-city sights around Jembatan Merah rather than making it the centre of a day.
