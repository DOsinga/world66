Museum Rudi Isbandi is a small art museum in Surabaya dedicated to the Indonesian painter Rudi Isbandi. It is not one of the city's headline sights, but it adds a useful cultural stop in a city better known to travellers for colonial buildings, markets and revolutionary history.

The museum is best for visitors who are already interested in Indonesian art or who want a quieter alternative to Surabaya's larger, busier attractions. Check current opening arrangements before going, as small private or foundation-run museums can be less predictable than major public institutions.
