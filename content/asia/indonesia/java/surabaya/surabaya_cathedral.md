Surabaya Cathedral, formally Katedral Hati Kudus Yesus, is the seat of the Roman Catholic Diocese of Surabaya. Its history traces the growth of the Catholic community in East Java from an apostolic prefecture in 1928 to a full diocese in 1961. The church is a calm architectural stop in a city better known for commerce, traffic, and revolutionary history.
