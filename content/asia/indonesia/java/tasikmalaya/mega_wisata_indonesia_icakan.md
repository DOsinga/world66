Mega Wisata Indonesia Icakan is a large recreation park east of Tasikmalaya, built around pools, water slides, paddle boats, and family rides. It is not a cultural stop, but it is one of the bigger easy-going day outings in East Parahyangan, especially if you are travelling with children.
