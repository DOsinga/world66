Pacar Beach is one of the easier companions to Kedung Tumpang on a south-coast outing. It has white sand, rocky edges, and, when water levels cooperate, a small waterfall close enough to the beach to make the stop feel different from the next cove down the road.

It is best folded into a beach-hopping day rather than treated as the only goal. Bring water, check local road advice, and assume facilities will be simple.
