Sanggrahan Temple is a modest Buddhist temple site from the Majapahit period. It is close enough to Tulungagung to visit without a major detour, and it pairs naturally with Dadi Temple for anyone interested in the region's pre-Islamic history.

Come with the right expectations. The site is small, but it helps explain why this inland town is more than a staging point for the coast.
