Pangumbahan's surf is part of Ujung Genteng's draw, especially for travelers who want West Java waves without a busy resort scene. The breaks are not beginner-oriented in the way Batu Karas can be, so ask locally and read conditions carefully.

The setting is the appeal: a quiet Indian Ocean coast, turtle habitat nearby, and far fewer distractions than Java's easier beach towns.
