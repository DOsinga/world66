Pangumbahan Turtle Park is the strongest reason to travel all the way to Ujung Genteng. Green turtles nest here throughout the year, with peak nesting often in the middle of the dry season, and wardens protect eggs before releasing hatchlings toward the sea.

The experience is moving because it is small and direct. Keep your distance, follow local instructions, and remember that the beach matters more as habitat than as a photo opportunity.
