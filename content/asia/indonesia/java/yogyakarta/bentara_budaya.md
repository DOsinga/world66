Bentara Budaya is a small but useful cultural centre in Yogyakarta, hosting art exhibitions, performances, talks, and community events. It is the kind of place that helps you see the city as a living arts centre, not only as a base for temples and palace history.

The value depends entirely on the programme, so check current listings before going. When something is on, it can be a rewarding low-key evening or gallery stop, especially for travellers interested in contemporary Indonesian culture.
