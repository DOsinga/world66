Kedai Kebun is a small arts space in Yogyakarta that has long mixed exhibitions, performances, discussion, and cafe life. It fits the city's student and artist energy better than many formal galleries do.

Check the current programme before going, because the appeal depends on what is happening. Even so, it is a useful address if you want to see Yogyakarta's contemporary cultural side beyond the kraton and temples.
