Kampung Atas Air is a waterfront settlement in Balikpapan where houses, lanes, and everyday life extend over the water on stilts and boardwalks. It offers a more local view of the city than malls, oil-industry landmarks, or hotel districts.

Go respectfully: this is a lived-in neighbourhood, not an open-air museum. The interest is in the relationship between tide, timber, boats, and domestic life along the coast, best seen on a slow walk with a local sense of boundaries.
