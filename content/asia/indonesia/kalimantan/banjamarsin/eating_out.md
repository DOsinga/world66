---
title: "Eating Out"
type: section
---

Banjarese food is one of the great regional cuisines of Indonesia and rarely encountered elsewhere. The signature dish is **soto banjar** — a clear, fragrant chicken broth with rice cake, perkedel (potato fritter), boiled egg, and a squeeze of lime. The best place to try it is at the long-running Soto Bang Amat on Jalan Banjar Indah Permai.

Other essentials are **ketupat kandangan**, rice cakes in a spiced fish gravy; **nasi kuning Banjar**, yellow turmeric rice with a peppery sambal; and **soto banjar madihin**, a slightly sweet variant served with martabak. The Siring riverfront has dozens of food stalls in the evening, and the simple grilled-fish warungs around the Sabilal Muhtadin mosque are reliably good. Fruit lovers should not miss the local kasturi, a small wild mango with an intense flavour, in season from late November to January.
