---
title: "Getting There"
type: section
---

Syamsudin Noor International Airport (BDJ) is at Banjarbaru, 25 km south-east of central Banjarmasin and connected by an inexpensive Damri shuttle bus. Daily flights link Banjarmasin with Jakarta, Surabaya, Yogyakarta, Balikpapan, Makassar, and Singapore.

There are no rail connections in Kalimantan; long-distance travel is by bus, river, or air. The Pelni passenger ferry calls at the Port of Trisakti, on the Barito River west of the city, with services to Surabaya and Semarang. Within the city itself, taxis, Grab/Gojek, and pedicabs (becak) cover most needs; the river itself is a useful and atmospheric way to move between the floating market jetties and the old town.
