The Sei Alalak Bridge, opened in 2021 across the Alalak River north of central Banjarmasin, is Indonesia's first curved cable-stayed bridge — an elegant 850-metre arc whose two pylons rise above the riverside warehouses and stilt houses of the northern suburbs.

It is more functional than tourist attraction: the bridge carries the Trans-Kalimantan highway and has cut hours from the old route across the marshes. But it has quickly become a symbol of the modern city, lit a different colour each evening, and is the obvious place to break the journey northward out of Banjarmasin towards Palangkaraya or the Tanjung Puting orangutan reserve.
