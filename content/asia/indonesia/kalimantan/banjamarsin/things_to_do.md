---
title: "Things To Do"
type: section
---

The first morning is the floating market — Lok Baintan is the more rewarding choice for those willing to leave the hotel at 04:30; Kuin is easier and quicker. Combine with a stop at Pulau Kembang for the monkeys and the old Sultan Suriansyah Mosque on the way back.

In the afternoon, the Siring promenade and the Sabilal Muhtadin mosque give a sense of modern Banjarmasin. A second day is well spent on a long boat trip down the Barito to Pulau Kaget for proboscis monkeys, or on a drive out to the Martapura diamond market. For anyone with a few days to spare, the bamboo-rafting trip down the Amandit at Loksado is the standout overnight option.
