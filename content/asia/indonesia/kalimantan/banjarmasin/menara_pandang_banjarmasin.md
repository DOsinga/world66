Menara Pandang Banjarmasin is a simple but useful viewpoint on the Martapura River, giving visitors a quick read on a city built around water. From the tower you can see the riverfront, bridges, boat traffic, and the dense urban fabric that makes Banjarmasin feel different from many other Indonesian cities.

It works best as part of a river walk or an evening wander rather than as a destination on its own. Come for orientation, photos, and a sense of how the city meets the water, then continue toward the floating market connections, mosques, and riverside food stalls that give Banjarmasin its rhythm.
