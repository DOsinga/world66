Batu Banama is a cluster of boulders and rock domes near Tangkiling Hill, valued both for its views and for its place in local Dayak legend. Shrines and offerings around the rocks underline the site's spiritual role.

It works best as part of a trip north of Palangka Raya, especially if combined with nearby nature stops.
