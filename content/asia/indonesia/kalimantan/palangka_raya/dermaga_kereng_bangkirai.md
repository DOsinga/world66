Dermaga Kereng Bangkirai is a blackwater dock area south of Palangka Raya where visitors can walk by the water, watch local boat activity, and arrange kelotok rides toward Sebangau National Park. It works best as a relaxed riverside stop or as the starting point for seeing the peat-swamp landscape beyond the city.
