---
title: "Getting There"
type: section
---

The main gateway is [Tjilik Riwut Airport](/asia/indonesia/kalimantan/palangka_raya/tjilik_riwut_airport), 13 km west of the city, with daily flights to Jakarta, Surabaya, and Banjarmasin. Overland from Banjarmasin (the larger neighbouring capital) is a five- to six-hour bus ride on a paved road — practical, but flights are not much more expensive.
