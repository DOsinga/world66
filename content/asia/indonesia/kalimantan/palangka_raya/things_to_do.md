---
title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

The main reason to come to Palangka Raya is the natural environment. [Sebangau National Park](/asia/indonesia/kalimantan/palangka_raya/sebangau_national_park), a vast expanse of peat swamp forest and black-water rivers, begins just south of the city and is one of the best places in Borneo to see wild orangutans. Further north, the [Nyaru Menteng](/asia/indonesia/kalimantan/palangka_raya/nyaru_menteng) rehabilitation centre is the world's largest orangutan rescue operation. The [Kahayan River](/asia/indonesia/kalimantan/palangka_raya/kahayan_river) running through town is worth exploring by klotok (traditional motorised canoe), preferably as far as the [Dayak villages](/asia/indonesia/kalimantan/palangka_raya/dayak_villages) upstream.

In town itself, the [Balanga Museum](/asia/indonesia/kalimantan/palangka_raya/balanga_museum) is the best introduction to Dayak culture, the [Kahayan Bridge](/asia/indonesia/kalimantan/palangka_raya/kahayan_bridge) is the most photogenic landmark, and the [Sukarno Monument](/asia/indonesia/kalimantan/palangka_raya/sukarno_monument) marks the spot where the city was founded as a planned would-be capital in 1957. The [Darussalam Grand Mosque](/asia/indonesia/kalimantan/palangka_raya/darussalam_grand_mosque) is the major religious building. [Pasar Besar](/asia/indonesia/kalimantan/palangka_raya/pasar_besar) and [Pasar Kahayan](/asia/indonesia/kalimantan/palangka_raya/pasar_kahayan) give the city's daily rhythm.
