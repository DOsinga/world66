---
title: Getting Around
type: section
---

Central Samarinda is navigable on foot if you are based near the waterfront. For longer trips — to the Islamic Centre, Pampang, or Sungai Kunjang port — you will need transport.

**Angkot** (shared minibuses) cover the main routes within the city and are cheap, though the network can be confusing for first-time visitors. Routes are indicated by the number painted on the front. The main angkot terminal near Pasar Pagi is the hub.

**Ojek** (motorcycle taxis) are the fastest way to get around and are found everywhere. Apps like Gojek and Grab work well in Samarinda and make ordering easy; they are generally more reliable than hailing an ojek on the street for a fair price.

**Car rental with driver** is the practical option for visiting Pampang, Tenggarong, or anywhere requiring time-sensitive logistics. Hotels can arrange this. Agree on the price and itinerary before departing.

The **Mahakam ferry** system connects the north and south banks of the river at several crossing points, and taking a public ferry is itself a pleasant ten-minute experience with good views of the city.
