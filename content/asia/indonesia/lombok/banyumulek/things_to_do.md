title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

There is one reason to come to Banyumulek and that is the pottery. The village is small enough to cover on foot in an hour or two: wander the lanes to watch the [pottery workshops](/asia/indonesia/lombok/banyumulek/pottery_workshops) in action, then visit the [Banyumulek Pottery Cooperative](/asia/indonesia/lombok/banyumulek/pottery_cooperative) at the main road for the widest selection and shipping arrangements.

Combine the stop with the weaving village of [Sukarara](/asia/indonesia/lombok/sukarara), about 15 km to the southeast — together the two villages make a satisfying half-day craft circuit out of [Mataram](/asia/indonesia/lombok/mataram).
