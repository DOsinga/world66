Mataram is the capital of [Lombok](/asia/indonesia/lombok), though in practice it is four towns that have merged into one sprawling conurbation: Ampenan, Mataram, Cakranegara (Cakra) and Sweta. Each has its own character.

[Ampenan](/asia/indonesia/lombok/mataram/ampenan), the old port district, is the most atmospheric part of the conurbation, with its faded colonial shophouses and quiet waterfront. The [West Nusa Tenggara State Museum](/asia/indonesia/lombok/mataram/ntb_museum) in Ampenan gives a good overview of Lombok's cultures — Sasak, Balinese Hindu, and Islamic — with a strong textile collection. Cakranegara (Cakra) is the liveliest area and the best base for eating and shopping. It is also home to the [Mayura Water Palace](/asia/indonesia/lombok/mataram/mayura_water_palace) and [Pura Meru](/asia/indonesia/lombok/mataram/pura_meru), Lombok's largest Hindu temple, which face each other across a main road. Mataram proper is the administrative centre, and Sweta (Mandalika Terminal) is the island's main transport hub for buses and bemos.
