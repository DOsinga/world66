Merese Hill is a grassy headland near Kuta Lombok with wide views over the south coast. On a clear day the bays, surf breaks, and open sea are visible from the ridge, making it one of the easiest viewpoints in the Mandalika area.

The hill is especially popular around sunrise and sunset, when the light catches the coast and the walk up is cooler. Wear shoes with some grip, as the paths can be dusty or slippery depending on the season.
