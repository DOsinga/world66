Pantai Putri Nyale is a pretty beach east of Kuta Lombok, known for pale sand, headlands, and the annual nyale sea-worm legend associated with the south coast. It is scenic, but swimming depends heavily on the tide.

At low tide the water pulls far out and the beach is better for walking and views than for a swim.
