Sendang Gile is the first major waterfall below Senaru, reached by a stepped path from the village edge. Water drops in a strong white sheet into a cool, misty hollow, with forest pressing in close around the rock.

It is the easiest of the Senaru waterfalls and the best choice if you only have limited time. You do not need a guide for the main path, though local touting can be persistent; agree clearly on any help before accepting it.
