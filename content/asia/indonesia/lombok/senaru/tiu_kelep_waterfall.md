Tiu Kelep is the wilder waterfall above Sendang Gile, reached by continuing through forest and across shallow streams. The reward is a broad, cold pool and a high curtain of water that throws spray across the whole basin.

The walk is not long, but it is rougher than it looks on a map. Conditions change after rain, and the creek crossings can be slippery. A guide is useful if you are travelling with children, older companions, or anyone uncomfortable on wet rock.
