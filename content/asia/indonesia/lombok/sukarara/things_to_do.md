---
title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Sukarara is a single-purpose stop: come for the textiles. Spend an hour or two wandering the lanes to watch the [Sasak weaving workshops](/asia/indonesia/lombok/sukarara/weaving_workshops) in action, then browse the [weaving cooperatives](/asia/indonesia/lombok/sukarara/weaving_cooperatives) on the main road for the broadest selection and fixed prices.

The pottery village of [Banyumulek](/asia/indonesia/lombok/banyumulek) lies about 15 km to the northwest, and the traditional Sasak settlements of Sade and Rambitan are a short drive south on the road to [Kuta Lombok](/asia/indonesia/lombok/kuta_lombok). A full day comfortably covers all four.
