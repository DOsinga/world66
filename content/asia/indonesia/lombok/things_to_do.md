title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

The big draw is [Mount Rinjani](/asia/indonesia/lombok/rinjani) — the two- to four-day trek to the crater rim and Segara Anak lake is the most memorable experience on the island and requires some preparation. Book a guide through agencies in Senggigi, Mataram, or the trekking villages of Senaru and Sembalun.

The [Gili Islands](/asia/indonesia/lombok/gili_islands) are the most visited part of Lombok. Gili Trawangan is the biggest and most developed, with beach bars and dive schools; Gili Meno is the quietest; Gili Air lies between the two in character. All three have excellent snorkelling with sea turtles a near-certainty in the shallows.

The south coast has Lombok's best surf. [Kuta Lombok](/asia/indonesia/lombok/kuta_lombok) is the base for the crescent bays and breaks to east and west; [Desert Point](/asia/indonesia/lombok/desert_point) at the southwestern tip is one of the most celebrated left-handers in Southeast Asia. The interior has craft villages worth a half-day: [Sukarara](/asia/indonesia/lombok/sukarara) for traditional Sasak weaving, [Banyumulek](/asia/indonesia/lombok/banyumulek) for pottery, and the waterfalls near [Senaru](/asia/indonesia/lombok/senaru_waterfalls) at the foot of Rinjani.
