Fort Amsterdam stands on Ambon's north coast at Hila, a small but important reminder of the island's place in the Dutch spice empire. The fort is modest in scale, yet its position by the sea says plenty about cloves, shipping routes, and the contest for control of Maluku.

It takes effort to reach from central Ambon, so treat it as part of a north-coast outing rather than a quick city sight. The reward is context: colonial walls, village life, and a coastline that makes the history of the spice islands feel less abstract.
