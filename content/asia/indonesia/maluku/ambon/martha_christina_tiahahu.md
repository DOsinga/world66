The Martha Christina Tiahahu Monument stands on a hill in the Karang Panjang area, about ten minutes from downtown Ambon. An eight-metre bronze statue of the young Ambonese freedom fighter, spear raised, gazes out over the city and Ambon Bay below.

The monument is one of the best viewpoints in Ambon, particularly at sunset when the bay and the hills of the Leihitu peninsula are bathed in good light. Martha Christina Tiahahu fought alongside her father against the Dutch colonial forces in 1817 and died in captivity in 1818, aged just 17. She is one of Indonesia's declared National Heroes, and the combination of her story and the view makes this worth the short trip from the city centre.
