Air Belanda Beach is known for the freshwater that seeps up through the sand near the shore. It is a small, curious stop rather than a grand beach, but it pairs naturally with Ora Beach and the north-coast villages if you are already crossing Seram.

The attraction is simple: dig into the sand and cool freshwater appears where you would expect only salt. Bring water shoes or sandals, and check local conditions before swimming.
