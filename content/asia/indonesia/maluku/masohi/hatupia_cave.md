Hatupia Cave is a sea cave and swimming spot near the north coast of Seram. It is one of those places where the attraction depends on timing, local knowledge, and a willingness to move slowly, but the combination of limestone, clear water, and village coast gives it real appeal.

Ask locally before going in. Conditions in caves and coastal pools change, and access is much easier with someone who knows the route.
