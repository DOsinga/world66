Lokong Beach is the easiest named beach stop on Pulau Run. It is not a serviced resort beach, just a quiet stretch of coast where the island's clear Banda water does most of the work.

It fits naturally into a walk across the island. Come prepared for heat, and do not expect shops or beach facilities once you leave the village.
