Nailaka is the small island just off the northeastern side of Pulau Run. It is the prettiest short outing from the village: pale sand, transparent water, and reef close enough to make snorkeling the main reason to go.

Ask locally about a boat and sea conditions before setting out. There is little shade and no real infrastructure, so bring water and sun protection.
