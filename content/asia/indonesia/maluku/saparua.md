Saparua is the largest of the Lease Islands east of Ambon, close enough for a determined day trip but better with a night or two. It has the classic Maluku mix: clove-era history, Christian and Muslim villages, bright water, and tourism that still feels small-scale.

The island's strongest sight is [Fort Duurstede](/asia/indonesia/maluku/saparua/fort_duurstede), tied to the 1817 Pattimura rebellion against Dutch rule. Around it are village stops such as [Baileu Saparua](/asia/indonesia/maluku/saparua/baileu_saparua) and Ouw, known for pottery, while the coast gives you swimming, snorkelling, and boat trips to [Molana Island](/asia/indonesia/maluku/saparua/molana_island).

Saparua is not polished. Boats from Ambon, local transport, and simple facilities all need patience. That is part of why the island works: it still feels like a lived-in Maluku island first and a visitor destination second.
