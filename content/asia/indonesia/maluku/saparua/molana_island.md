Molana Island sits just off Saparua's south-western side, a small coral island with pale beaches and clear water. It is the kind of place that rewards the extra boat arrangement, especially if you want a quieter swim than the beaches on the main island.

Go only when the weather and sea are sensible, and sort out return transport before you leave. Facilities are limited, which is part of the appeal but not something to ignore.
