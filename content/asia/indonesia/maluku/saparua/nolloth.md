Nolloth is a quiet village on Saparua's north-eastern side, known for its old church and friendly village atmosphere. It is not a major sight, but it is a good reminder that the island's interest is spread through its villages rather than concentrated in one resort strip.

Stop here if you are making a full island loop. The road, coast, and village life are the point as much as the church itself.
