Lake Ngade is one of Ternate's most photogenic inland viewpoints, where a small lake, steep green slopes, and the volcanic island of Tidore can align in the same frame. It is a reminder that Ternate's drama is not only in its forts and spice history, but in its compact volcanic geography.

The stop is usually brief, but the view is strong enough to justify the detour around the island. Go in clear weather, since haze and rain can quickly flatten the scene.
