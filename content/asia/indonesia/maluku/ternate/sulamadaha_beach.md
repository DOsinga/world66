Sulamadaha Beach is one of Ternate's favourite coastal escapes, known for clear water, dark volcanic sand, and views toward nearby Hiri Island. The setting makes sense of Ternate as a volcanic island rather than just a historic spice-port town.

It is a relaxed swim-and-snack outing, especially good when the water is calm. Combine it with other stops around the island if you have transport, and bring reef-safe habits if you snorkel or explore the cove.
