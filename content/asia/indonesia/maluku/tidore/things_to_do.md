---
title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

The main pull of Tidore is its history — the island was, with [Ternate](/asia/indonesia/maluku/ternate) across the strait, one of the four great clove sultanates of North Maluku, and the rebuilt [Kadato Kie](/asia/indonesia/maluku/tidore/kadato_kie) palace together with the [Sonyine Malige Museum](/asia/indonesia/maluku/tidore/sonyine_malige_museum) and the [Sultan Nuku Monument](/asia/indonesia/maluku/tidore/sultan_nuku_monument) trace that history clearly. The two Spanish forts above Soasio — [Fort Tahula](/asia/indonesia/maluku/tidore/fort_tahula) and [Fort Tohula](/asia/indonesia/maluku/tidore/fort_tohula) — are short climbs with excellent views and almost no other visitors.

For more active travellers, the climb up [Gunung Kiematubu](/asia/indonesia/maluku/tidore/gunung_kiematubu) is the obvious challenge — a long, steep day from the village of [Gurabunga](/asia/indonesia/maluku/tidore/gurabunga) that finishes at one of the best summit views in eastern Indonesia. Less ambitious days can be spent circling the island on the [ring road](/asia/indonesia/maluku/tidore/ring_road_drive), stopping at the [Akesahu hot springs](/asia/indonesia/maluku/tidore/akesahu_hot_springs), and walking through the [clove gardens](/asia/indonesia/maluku/tidore/clove_gardens) on the middle slopes.

Quieter pleasures include a short charter from [Rum harbour](/asia/indonesia/maluku/tidore/rum_harbour) to [Maitara Island](/asia/indonesia/maluku/tidore/maitara_island) for snorkelling, and a wander through the streets of [Soasio](/asia/indonesia/maluku/tidore/soasio) — its [great mosque](/asia/indonesia/maluku/tidore/great_mosque), its market, its evening seafront walk.
