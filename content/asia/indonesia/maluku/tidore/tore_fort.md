Tore Fort is one of Tidore's old defensive sites, a reminder that this small island once mattered enormously in the clove trade. The ruin is not large, but its hillside position gives it a strong sense of place above the coast.

Come for the combination of history and view. Tidore's forts make most sense together, as fragments of a spice-island story involving local sultanates, European powers, and the sea lanes between volcanoes.
