Pering Mangrove Ecotourism, also written as Pring Mangrove Ecotourism, opened in 2022 as a simple boardwalk attraction. Its wooden pier runs for about a kilometre through the mangroves, making it an easy nature stop on Natuna.

Come for a short walk rather than a full-day outing. The appeal is the setting: mangrove shade, water, and a slower look at the island away from the beaches.
