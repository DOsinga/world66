---
title: "Getting There"
type: section
---

Gunungsitoli is reached by air or sea from Sumatra. Binaka Airport, about 7 km south of the town centre, has several flights daily to Medan (approximately one hour) and less frequent connections to other Sumatran cities. The airport is the quickest and most reliable way to reach Nias.

By sea, ferries depart from Sibolga on the Sumatran west coast to Gunungsitoli harbour. The crossing takes approximately 10 hours and runs several times a week; conditions can be rough during the northwest monsoon (October through March). Slower and cheaper PELNI ferries also serve the route on less frequent schedules.

From Gunungsitoli, most visitors continue south to Teluk Dalam and the surfing areas around Lagundri Bay. The road south through the interior of Nias takes approximately four hours by bus or bemo.
