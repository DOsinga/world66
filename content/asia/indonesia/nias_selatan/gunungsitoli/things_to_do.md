---
title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Gunungsitoli is primarily a transit town. The main reason to linger is the Nias Heritage Museum, which has an impressive collection of megalithic stone carvings and traditional artefacts that give context to everything you'll see elsewhere on the island. The harbour and market area are lively in the morning. Most visitors are passing through on their way south to Teluk Dalam and the surf breaks at Lagundri Bay.
