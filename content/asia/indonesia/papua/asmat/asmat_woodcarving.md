Woodcarving is the great Asmat art form, from ancestor poles and shields to drums and smaller pieces sold in Agats. The best work is not souvenir decoration; it is tied to clan identity, memory, ritual, and the forest.

Ask locally about current carving workshops or festival events. Buying directly and respectfully is better than treating carvings as anonymous curios.
