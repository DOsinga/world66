Manokwari is the capital of West Papua province, set on a bay surrounded by forested hills at the northern edge of the Bird's Head Peninsula. It is a small, pleasant city that sees few tourists but rewards those who come.

The main attraction near town is [Gunung Meja Nature Reserve](/asia/indonesia/papua/manokwari/gunung_meja), a forested hill with trails and views over the city and bay. [Amban Beach](/asia/indonesia/papua/manokwari/amban_beach) is a short ride from the centre. For a longer trip, the twin Anggi Lakes sit high in the [Arfak Mountains](/asia/indonesia/papua/manokwari/arfak_mountains) to the south, surrounded by cool cloud forest — a striking contrast to the tropical coast.

Manokwari has historical significance as the place where Christianity was first introduced to Papua, in 1855, via [Mansinam Island](/asia/indonesia/papua/manokwari/mansinam_island) just offshore. The island is still a pilgrimage site and easy to reach by boat from the harbour.
