Pasir Putih Beach is one of Manokwari's easiest coastal escapes, with pale sand, clear water, and a more inviting swimming setting than the rougher stretches of coast around town. The name simply means White Sand Beach, and that directness suits the place.

It is not a remote wilderness beach, but it is a useful, enjoyable half-day stop if you are based in Manokwari. Come for a swim, a walk, and a slower look at the bay, especially when the weather is settled and the water is calm.
