Taman Burung Aimas is a small bird park outside Sorong, with regional Australasian birds such as cassowaries, crowned pigeons, and cockatoos. It is a simple family stop rather than a major wildlife centre.

It makes the most sense for travelers with extra time in Sorong before or after Raja Ampat connections.
