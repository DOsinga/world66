Tanjung Kasuari Beach is one of Sorong's better-known coastal escapes, north of the city center and often used by travelers with spare time around Raja Ampat transfers. The draw is simple: sand, coconut-fringed shoreline, and a more relaxed setting than the port city.

It is not a polished resort attraction, and conditions can vary, but it gives Sorong a legitimate beach option rather than just transit logistics. Go with modest expectations and enough time for transport back to town.
