Pekanbaru is the capital of Riau, a busy, hot, oil-and-trade city on the Siak River in eastern Sumatra. It is not as atmospheric as Bukittinggi or as famous as Medan, but it has a clear identity: Malay culture, river commerce, big mosques, markets, and a practical position between inland Sumatra and the Strait of Malacca.

Most travellers come through Pekanbaru rather than making it the whole point of a trip. That is fair. But if you have a day, the city gives you a useful look at urban Riau: the domes and wide grounds of [An-Nur Great Mosque](/asia/indonesia/pekanbaru/an_nur_great_mosque), the Malay collections at [Sang Nila Utama Museum](/asia/indonesia/pekanbaru/sang_nila_utama_museum), and the trade buzz of [Pasar Bawah](/asia/indonesia/pekanbaru/pasar_bawah).

The city is spread out and traffic can be tiring, so plan a compact route rather than trying to wander aimlessly. Add the [Soeman Hs Library](/asia/indonesia/pekanbaru/soeman_hs_library) for architecture and local reading culture, then cross or view [Siak IV Bridge](/asia/indonesia/pekanbaru/siak_iv_bridge) to remember that Pekanbaru's story still runs along the river.
