Candi Kalasan is one of the oldest Buddhist temples around Yogyakarta, built in the 8th century under the Sanjaya dynasty. Its carved walls and traces of ancient plaster are quieter than nearby Prambanan, but they show the mixed Hindu-Buddhist world that shaped early Central Java.
