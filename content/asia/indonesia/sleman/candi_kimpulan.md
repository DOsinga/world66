A small Hindu temple from the late 9th or early 10th century, exposed in 2009 during construction work on the campus of the Islamic University of Indonesia in Kaliurang. The temple, which had been buried by Merapi ashfall, was preserved in place and the university library was built around it. Visitors can see it through the glass walls of the library basement.

A reminder that Java's pre-Islamic past lies very thinly beneath the surface — most of the larger candi (temples) in Sleman were also rediscovered like this.
