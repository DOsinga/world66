Baiturrahim Great Mosque anchors Gorontalo's old centre and gives the city a clear civic landmark. Its current form has been rebuilt and expanded, but the site remains tied to the older town around the square and harbour.

It is worth a short stop while walking the centre, especially in the late afternoon when the area is active and the light is softer. Dress modestly and avoid prayer times unless you are there to worship.
