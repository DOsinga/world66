Trans Studio is a large indoor theme park and shopping mall complex in the east of the city. It is one of the biggest indoor theme parks in Southeast Asia, with rides and attractions spread across an air-conditioned space. Worth knowing about if you are travelling with children, or if the heat outside becomes too much; otherwise it is easy to skip in favour of the city's more distinctive attractions.
