Manado is the capital of North Sulawesi and the launching point for [Bunaken National Marine Park](/asia/indonesia/sulawesi/manado/bunaken_national_park), one of the richest coral reef systems on earth. Most visitors come to dive — the underwater walls off Bunaken drop hundreds of metres and teem with turtles, sharks and an astounding variety of reef fish. The city itself is busy and unpretentious, with a predominantly Christian Minahasa population and a cuisine that is unapologetically hot and adventurous.

Beyond the diving, the [Minahasa highlands around Tomohon](/asia/indonesia/sulawesi/manado/tomohon_highland) offer a cooler day trip to volcanic crater lakes and one of the most unusual markets in Southeast Asia. The [Tangkoko Nature Reserve](/asia/indonesia/sulawesi/manado/tangkoko_nature_reserve) on the northeastern peninsula is one of the better places in Sulawesi to see the spectral tarsier and the Sulawesi black macaque. In the city, the [Klenteng Ban Hin Kiong Temple](/asia/indonesia/sulawesi/manado/klenteng_ban_hin_kiong) — the oldest Chinese Buddhist temple in Manado — is worth an hour in the old Chinatown district.
