This regional museum is the best indoor introduction to Central Sulawesi. Exhibits cover Kaili culture, local history, traditional objects, and the megalithic stones of the Bada Valley.

It is not a large museum, but it helps make sense of the province before heading inland or onward to the coast. Go early in the day, when heat and traffic are easier to handle.
