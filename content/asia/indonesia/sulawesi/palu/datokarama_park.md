Datokarama Park gives a broad view over Palu Bay and the city. It is also tied to the memory of the 2018 tsunami, with damaged waterfront structures still part of the visual story.

Come for the view, but do not treat the place as just a photo stop. Palu's recent disasters are not abstract here; they are part of how the city understands its coast.
