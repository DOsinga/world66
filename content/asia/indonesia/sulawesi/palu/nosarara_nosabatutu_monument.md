This hilltop peace monument looks out over Palu and its bay. Its name comes from a Kaili phrase meaning that people are siblings and united, and the site is connected with messages of religious and social peace.

The monument is most useful for the view and for understanding Palu's civic identity. Go in the clearer part of the day if you want photographs over the bay.
