Sou Raja is Palu's old royal house, a wooden traditional residence linked to the local rulers of the Kaili people. It is one of the clearest heritage buildings in a city where much of the interest is spread out and easy to miss.

The visit is short, but worthwhile if you want Palu to feel like a place with its own history rather than just the airport for Central Sulawesi.
