Deri is a Toraja burial site set in a rural landscape north of Rantepao. The tombs are less dramatic than the best-known Toraja sites, but the scenic road there passes villages, bamboo groves, and quiet countryside that make the trip worthwhile for travellers exploring the area in depth.
