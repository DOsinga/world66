---
title: When to Go
type: section
---

The dry season, from June through September, is the best time to visit Tana Toraja. Roads are passable, the highland views are clear, and the absence of rain makes walking between villages pleasant. This period also coincides with the high season for funeral ceremonies, since families prefer to hold them when guests can travel easily. If attending a ceremony is your main aim, July and August are your best odds.

The rainy season from November to March brings heavy downpours that can make roads muddy and some hillside sites difficult to reach, but the rice terraces are at their most vivid green during the growing season and the landscape is spectacular. Ceremonies do still occur in the wet season, just less frequently.

The peak tourist months of July and August bring more visitors and slightly higher accommodation prices, but Tana Toraja never feels overcrowded. Most sites have no entrance queue and the highland pace is always unhurried.
