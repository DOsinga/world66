Cut Nya Dien House is a replica of the home associated with the Acehnese resistance leader Cut Nyak Dhien. The original house was burned during the Aceh War, but the rebuilt house now works as a small museum about her life and the local struggle against Dutch rule.

The site is in Lampisang village, west of Banda Aceh. It pairs well with other Banda Aceh history stops, especially for visitors interested in the city's national heroes and colonial-era past.
