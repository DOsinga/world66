---
title: Getting Around
type: section
---

The central sights are close together, but the heat makes walking less appealing than it looks on a map. Becak motor trishaws, taxis, and ride-hailing apps are the easiest way to move between the mosque, museums, and port.

Traffic is lighter than in most Indonesian provincial capitals. If you rent a scooter, ride conservatively and avoid the busiest roads around prayer times and school hours.
