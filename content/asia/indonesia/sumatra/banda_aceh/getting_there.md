---
title: Getting There
type: section
---

Sultan Iskandar Muda International Airport is southeast of the city and has regular flights from Jakarta and Medan, plus occasional regional links. A taxi or ride-hailing car into the centre normally takes 30 to 45 minutes, depending on traffic.

The Ulee Lheue ferry terminal west of town is the departure point for [Sabang Island](/asia/indonesia/aceh/sabang_island) on Pulau Weh. Fast boats usually take about an hour to Balohan; the slower car ferry takes longer but is useful if you are travelling with a motorcycle.
