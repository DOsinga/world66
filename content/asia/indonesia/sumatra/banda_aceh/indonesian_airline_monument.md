The Indonesian Airline Monument recalls Aceh's role in the first years of the republic, when local donations helped buy an aircraft for the new Indonesian state. It is a modest city-centre monument rather than a major sight, but it adds useful context to Banda Aceh's strong sense of regional pride and national history.
