Belitung Mangrove Park is an ecotourism area near Tanjung Pandan where visitors can walk through mangrove habitat and see a different side of the island away from the granite beaches. The floating restaurant adds a practical stop for a short outing, but the main reason to come is the mangrove landscape itself.
