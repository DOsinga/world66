Gurok Beraye is a waterfall fed from Mount Tajam, Belitung's highest point. The water drops into a broad pool that can be used for swimming when conditions are good.

The trip involves rougher terrain and deep gorges nearby, so it suits visitors looking for a nature outing rather than a quick roadside stop.
