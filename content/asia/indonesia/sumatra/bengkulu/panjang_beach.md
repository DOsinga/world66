Panjang Beach runs for kilometres along the city's coast, backed by casuarina trees rather than the coconut palms many visitors expect in Indonesia. It is better for walking, jogging, sunsets, and food stalls than for swimming, as currents and coral make the water risky.

Come in the late afternoon, when the heat drops and Bengkulu residents gather by the water.
