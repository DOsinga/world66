Berastagi is a Karo highland town in North Sumatra, about two to three hours from [Medan](/asia/indonesia/sumatra/medan) on the road toward [Lake Toba](/asia/indonesia/sumatra/laketoba). Its appeal is simple and strong: cooler air, vegetable farms, fruit markets, and two volcanoes - Sibayak and Sinabung - rising close enough to dominate the skyline.

Most travellers come to climb [Mount Sibayak](/asia/indonesia/sumatra/berastagi/mount_sibayak), a steaming volcanic crater that can be done as a half-day trip with an early start. After the hike, the hot springs at Lau Debuk Debuk are exactly what you want: rough-edged, local, and very welcome on tired legs.

Berastagi is not pretty in a tidy colonial-hill-station way. Traffic can be loud, weekend crowds from Medan are real, and the sights are scattered. Still, the town has a clear mountain identity, and for a spare day in northern Sumatra it easily beats sitting in Medan waiting for the next bus.
