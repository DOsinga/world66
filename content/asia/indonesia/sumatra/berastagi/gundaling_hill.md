Gundaling Hill is the easy panorama above Berastagi, with views toward Mount Sibayak and Mount Sinabung when the weather is clear. It is commercialised, with stalls and weekend crowds, but the volcano skyline is still worth the short detour.

Come early or late for better light and fewer people. If Sinabung is venting, this is one of the simpler places to understand how close Berastagi sits to real volcanic activity.
