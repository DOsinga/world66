Lau Debuk Debuk is the cluster of hot spring pools at the foot of Mount Sibayak. The setting is informal and can be busy with local families, but that is part of the appeal.

It is best after the Sibayak climb, when the sulphur smell, warm water, and mountain views feel earned. Pick a clean-looking pool, keep valuables close, and expect simple facilities.
