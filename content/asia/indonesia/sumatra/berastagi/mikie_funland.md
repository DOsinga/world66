Mikie Funland is a large amusement park outside Berastagi, with a wide mix of rides and family attractions. It is popular with local visitors, especially on weekends and holidays, when the park can be crowded.

For travellers with children, it is one of the easiest family stops in the Berastagi area. Check opening times and promotions before going, since the experience depends heavily on the day and crowd levels.
