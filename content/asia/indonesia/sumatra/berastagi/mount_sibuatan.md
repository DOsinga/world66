Mount Sibuatan is the highest mountain in North Sumatra, rising to 2,457 metres north of Lake Toba. The rainforest climb is hard, usually takes two days, and needs a guide, so it is best for hikers looking for a wilder alternative to the better-known volcanoes around Berastagi.
