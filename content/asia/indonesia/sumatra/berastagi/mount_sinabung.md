Mount Sinabung is the restless volcano west of Berastagi, a serious mountain rather than a casual afternoon walk. When access is allowed, the route starts near Lake Kawar and climbs through farms and jungle to wide views over the Karo highlands. Check the current exclusion zone before setting out, as eruptions can close the whole area without much warning.
