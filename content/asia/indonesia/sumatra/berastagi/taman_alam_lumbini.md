Taman Alam Lumbini is a Buddhist temple complex near Berastagi, best known for its golden pagoda modelled on Yangon's Shwedagon. The complex feels unexpected in the Karo highlands, which is part of its charm.

It is an easy add-on if you have transport and want a change from volcanoes and markets. Dress modestly and treat it as an active religious site, not just a photo stop.
