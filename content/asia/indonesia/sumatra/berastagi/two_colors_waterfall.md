Two Colors Waterfall is a forest waterfall north of Berastagi, named for the pale blue and white tones in its mineral-rich water. Reaching it involves a long trek through rainforest from the Sibolangit area, so it is a real outing rather than a quick roadside stop.

Go with a local guide and start early. The hike, wet ground, and variable conditions make it better suited to active travellers than to anyone looking for an easy waterfall viewpoint.
