The standout is **Lake Maninjau**, a 17-kilometre-long crater lake about two hours west by bus down forty-four numbered hairpin bends. The lake is hot, deep, and ringed by fish farms; you can rent a motorbike at the bottom and circle the rim in a half-day. Stay overnight if you can — sunsets on Maninjau are one of West Sumatra's quiet glories.

Closer to town, **Batusangkar** and the rebuilt Pagaruyung Palace give a primer on Minangkabau royal history; the original burned down after a lightning strike in 2007 but the replica is a fine piece of Minang carpentry. The active volcano **Mount Marapi** is a doable but tough overnight climb from the village of Koto Baru, about an hour south. And the larger city of **Padang**, on the coast, is the gateway to the Mentawai Islands and a separate destination in its own right.
