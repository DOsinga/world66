The House of Bung Hatta is a small heritage museum connected with Mohammad Hatta, Indonesia's first vice president and one of the central figures of the independence movement. Bukittinggi was his birthplace, and the house helps place that national story in a Minangkabau townscape of markets, hills, and colonial-era streets.

The visit is modest in scale, but it gives useful context before or after seeing Bukittinggi's better-known landmarks. Period rooms, family references, and independence-era memory make it most rewarding for travellers interested in modern Indonesian history rather than grand museum displays.
