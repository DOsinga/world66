---
title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

The single most popular activity in the park is [orangutan trekking](/asia/indonesia/sumatra/gununglesuer/orangutan_trekking) out of [Bukit Lawang](/asia/indonesia/sumatra/bukitlawang), where a long-running rehabilitation programme has produced what is probably the most accessible population of [Sumatran orangutans](/asia/indonesia/sumatra/gununglesuer/sumatran_orangutan) anywhere in their range. Treks run from a few hours to several days and almost always include sightings of gibbons, leaf monkeys and macaques as well.

For deeper forest, head west to [Ketambe](/asia/indonesia/sumatra/gununglesuer/ketambe) on the Aceh side. Trips from here climb hard into the interior and offer better chances of finding the rarer wildlife — including the parasitic [Rafflesia](/asia/indonesia/sumatra/gununglesuer/rafflesia) flower, the [Sumatran tiger](/asia/indonesia/sumatra/gununglesuer/sumatran_tiger) (in tracks rather than person), and the occasional elephant. Serious mountaineers can attempt the summit of [Mount Leuser](/asia/indonesia/sumatra/gununglesuer/gunung_leuser) itself from [Kedah](/asia/indonesia/sumatra/gununglesuer/kedah) — a two-week expedition through some of the wettest forest on earth.

The [Bohorok River](/asia/indonesia/sumatra/gununglesuer/bohorok_river) at Bukit Lawang gives tubing and swimming; the [Alas River](/asia/indonesia/sumatra/gununglesuer/alas_river) is one of the great rafting rivers of Southeast Asia, running multi-day trips out of Ketambe. For a quieter alternative to Bukit Lawang, the village of [Tangkahan](/asia/indonesia/sumatra/gununglesuer/tangkahan) has a small herd of working elephants used in park patrols and welcomes visitors to the morning river bath.
