Sibea-bea Hill is a viewpoint above Lake Toba known for its large statue of Jesus Christ and sweeping views over the caldera landscape. The approach road climbs through open highland scenery, so the trip is as much about the lake views as the monument itself.

It is a worthwhile detour on the western side of Lake Toba, especially in clear weather when the ridges and water are visible far below.
