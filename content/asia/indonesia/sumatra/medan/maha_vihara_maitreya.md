Maha Vihara Maitreya is one of Medan's most visible Buddhist landmarks, a broad, modern temple compound in the Cemara Asri area east of the city centre. Its scale is part of the appeal: broad courtyards, bright halls, and calm devotional spaces make it feel quite different from the tighter colonial core around Kesawan.

Come for a quiet look at Medan's Chinese-Buddhist life rather than for ancient architecture. The temple works best paired with other east-side stops, and visitors should dress modestly, keep voices low, and avoid interrupting worshippers who are there for prayer rather than sightseeing.
