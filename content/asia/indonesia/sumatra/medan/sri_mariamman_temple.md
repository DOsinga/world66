Sri Mariamman Temple is the most important Hindu landmark in Medan's Kampung Madras, the old Tamil quarter near the city centre. Built in 1884, it is dedicated to the goddess Mariamman and still anchors religious life for Medan's Tamil Hindu community.

The gopuram over the entrance brings a distinctly South Indian note to a city better known for Malay, Chinese, Dutch, and Batak influences. Visit respectfully, especially during worship or festival periods, and combine it with a walk through the surrounding streets for a fuller sense of Medan's layered communities.
