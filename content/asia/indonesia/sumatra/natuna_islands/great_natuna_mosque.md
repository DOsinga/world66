Great Natuna Mosque, also known as Agung Natuna Mosque, is the main mosque of the Natuna Islands Regency. Completed in 2009, it is a prominent local landmark and an active place of worship.

Visitors should dress modestly, remove shoes before entering, and be mindful around prayer times. It is a worthwhile cultural stop when passing through Ranai.
