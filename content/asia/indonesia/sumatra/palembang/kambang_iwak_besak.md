Kambang Iwak Besak is a central Palembang park built around a lake, with colonial-era origins and a strong role as an everyday gathering place. Joggers, families, food vendors, and evening strollers give it more life than its modest size suggests.

It is not one of Palembang's grand historic sights, but it is useful for feeling the city's daily rhythm away from the Musi riverfront. Go around late afternoon or evening, when the heat eases and the park starts to fill.
