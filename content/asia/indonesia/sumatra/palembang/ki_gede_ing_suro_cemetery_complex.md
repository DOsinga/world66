The Ki Gede Ing Suro Cemetery Complex is a historic burial site connected with Palembang's early Islamic period. Its brick structures and sacred atmosphere give visitors a compact introduction to the city's pre-colonial layers, away from the busier riverfront sights.

It is most rewarding for travelers interested in local history and religious heritage. Visit respectfully, as this remains a cemetery and pilgrimage place rather than a conventional open-air museum.
