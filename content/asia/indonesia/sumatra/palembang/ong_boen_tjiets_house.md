Ong Boen Tjiet's House is a historic house connected with Palembang's Chinese-Indonesian merchant community. Its value is architectural and social: a reminder that the Musi River city was shaped by trade networks as much as by sultanate history.

Access may be limited and should be checked before visiting. If open, it pairs well with Kampung Kapitan, riverfront walks, and Palembang's older religious and trading quarters.
