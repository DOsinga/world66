---
title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Palembang's sights are concentrated along the Musi River — the bridge, the fortress, the mosque, and the museums all sit within walking distance of the north bank. Add a half-day for Bukit Seguntang and the songket weaving workshops of Tangga Buntung, and the city's compact tourist circuit takes a comfortable day and a half.
