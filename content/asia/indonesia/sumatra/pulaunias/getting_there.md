---
title: "Getting There"
type: section
---

Nias has two practical entry points. **By air**, Wings Air and Garuda Citilink run daily flights from **Medan** to Binaka Airport, about 20 kilometres south of Gunungsitoli. The flight is around an hour and is the easiest option — book ahead in surf season.

**By ferry**, the longer but cheaper route is the overnight ASDP ferry from Sibolga, on the west coast of [Sumatra](/asia/indonesia/sumatra), to Gunungsitoli. The crossing takes 10 to 12 hours; cabins are basic but a bunk in a shared cabin or a bench on deck is inexpensive. There is also a faster speedboat service from Sibolga in good weather (about 4 hours), and a small ferry from Padang via Bintuhan to Teluk Dalam.

From Gunungsitoli most travellers heading south arrange a private car or share a Kijang minibus to Teluk Dalam, a winding 3-4 hour drive across the spine of the island.
