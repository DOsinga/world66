Anakalang, about 20 kilometres east of Waikabubak, is arguably the most impressive megalithic site in West Sumba. The village's central plaza is dominated by a collection of stone tomb complexes, each comprising a massive horizontal capstone supported on carved pillars. The tombs are still in active ritual use — offerings are placed at them, and ceremonies mark the anniversaries of the deaths of important ancestors.

The village itself is a traditional Sumbanese uma: high-peaked thatched clan houses arranged around the tombs, with the communal space in the centre. Residents are accustomed to respectful visitors. Ask before entering a house or photographing ceremonies.

Nearby Pasunga has a further cluster of particularly fine and elaborately carved tombs.
