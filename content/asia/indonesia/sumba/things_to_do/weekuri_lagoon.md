Weekuri is a saltwater lagoon on the far northwest coast of Sumba, separated from the open sea by a natural rock barrier. The water is turquoise, calm, and remarkably clear — quite different from the exposed surf beaches elsewhere on the coast. Swimming here feels like being inside an aquarium.

The lagoon is at its best in the morning, before the tour groups arrive from Tambolaka airport. Go early, bring your own food and water (the warungs nearby are basic and expensive relative to what they offer), and allow time to drift around the shallower sections where small fish feed in the sea grass.

The drive from Waikabubak takes about two hours on roads that improve each year but still require a capable vehicle in the wet season. Combine the visit with Pantai Mandorak, a long white sand beach 20 minutes further west, or Bwanna, a clifftop viewpoint with views of the Indian Ocean.
