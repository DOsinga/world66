Hu'u, on Sumbawa's south coast near Dompu, is synonymous with surfing. The reef breaks around Lakey Beach — Lakey Peak chief among them — draw a dedicated surf crowd for their consistent, hollow waves, and the low-key strip of beachfront losmen has a laid-back, end-of-the-road feel.
