title: Things to Do
type: section
---

West Timor's appeal lies in its landscape and culture rather than built attractions. The dry savannahs, limestone caves, and offshore islands around Kupang reward those who explore beyond the port city. The interior is traditional Timorese country: ikat weaving villages, hilltop towns with panoramic views, and a culture shaped by centuries of Portuguese and Dutch contact plus a deep animist bedrock.
