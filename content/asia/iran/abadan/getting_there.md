---
title: "Getting There"
type: section
---

Abadan is in Khuzestan Province, in the extreme southwest of Iran near the Iraqi border. The nearest major city and transport hub is **Ahvaz**, about 130 kilometres to the north by road.

**By air**, Abadan Ayatollah Jami International Airport (IATA: ABD) has domestic flights to Tehran and a few other Iranian cities on Iran Air and Mahan Air, though schedules are infrequent. Flying into Ahvaz and continuing by road is often easier. Ahvaz (AWZ) has better connections.

**By road**, Abadan is connected to Ahvaz by a good highway. Shared taxis and occasional buses run between the two cities. The drive takes two to three hours. From Tehran, overland travel takes the better part of a day.

Foreign visitors require a valid Iranian visa. Travel near the Iraqi border requires care; check current conditions with your embassy and with Iranian travel agencies before visiting.
