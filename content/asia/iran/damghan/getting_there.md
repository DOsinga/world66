---
title: "Getting There"
type: section
---

Damghan sits on the Tehran–Mashhad highway and the parallel rail line, midway between the two cities — about 340 kilometres east of Tehran and 600 kilometres west of Mashhad.

**By bus** — overnight and daytime VIP buses from Tehran take five to six hours. From the eastern bus terminal (Beihaghi), departures every couple of hours.

**By train** — the Tehran–Mashhad night trains stop in Damghan in the small hours. Faster but inconvenient if you want a real night's sleep.

**By car** — the toll motorway is fast and well surfaced. Cars can be rented in Tehran or Semnan; petrol is cheap.
