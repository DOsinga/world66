title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Damghan packs an unusual concentration of early Islamic architecture into a small city. The [Tarikhaneh](/asia/iran/damghan/tarikhaneh_mosque) is one of the oldest surviving mosques in Iran, with the brick piers of a converted Sasanian fire temple still standing. The [Jameh Mosque](/asia/iran/damghan/jameh_mosque) at the centre of the bazaar has a finely decorated Seljuq minaret, and a few hundred metres apart stand the two 11th-century brick tomb towers — the [Pir-e Alamdar](/asia/iran/damghan/pir_e_alamdar) and the [Chehel Dokhtaran](/asia/iran/damghan/chehel_dokhtaran).

For older history, the Bronze Age mound of [Tepe Hissar](/asia/iran/damghan/tepe_hissar) south-east of town was one of the great early-20th-century Iranian archaeology digs. The [bazaar](/asia/iran/damghan/bazaar) is small but pleasant for a wander and the famous Damghan pistachios. Hike up to the ruined [Zard Castle](/asia/iran/damghan/zard_castle) in the foothills for a half-day excursion.
