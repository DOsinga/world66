---
title: "Getting There"
type: section
---

Hormuz Island is reached by boat from **Bandar Abbas** on the Iranian mainland. Regular ferries and speedboats leave from the Shahid Haghani port in Bandar Abbas several times a day. The crossing takes about 30 minutes on a speedboat or 45 minutes on the slower ferry. Tickets are cheap and the service runs throughout the day from early morning to late afternoon.

**Getting to Bandar Abbas** is straightforward from most Iranian cities. Iran Air and other domestic carriers operate regular flights from Tehran, Isfahan, and Shiraz. There is also a railway connection to Bandar Abbas from the national network. The city is the major port and commercial hub of the Persian Gulf coast.

Once on Hormuz, **local transport** consists of motorbike taxis and shared minivans. The island is small enough that a hired motorbike is an efficient way to see the main sights in a day. Negotiate rates in advance.
