Abbasian House is one of Kashan's grand traditional merchant houses, built in the late 18th century with multiple courtyards, reception rooms, windcatchers, and richly decorated plasterwork. Its scale and layered layout show how wealthy families separated public, private, and service spaces in desert-city houses.

It sits close to Kashan's other historic houses and the Sultan Amir Ahmad Bathhouse, making it easy to visit as part of a walking circuit through the old quarter.
