The Holy Shrine of Helal Ali is a large shrine and mosque complex near Kashan, with a broad interior court and graves of Iran-Iraq War martyrs outside. It is a religious site first, so dress and behave accordingly.

It works best as a stop for travellers already exploring the sights north of Kashan, including the Underground City of Ouyi.
