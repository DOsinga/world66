Kashan's Puppet and Toy Museum is a small, charming break from the city's grand merchant houses and historic bathhouses. Its displays focus on Iranian puppets, toys, and play traditions, and workshops are sometimes part of the visit.

It is best treated as a light stop rather than a major museum. Families and anyone interested in folk culture will get the most out of it.
