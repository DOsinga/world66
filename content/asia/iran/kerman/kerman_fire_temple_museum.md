Kerman Fire Temple and Zoroastrian Museum is one of the city's most distinctive cultural stops, focused on a community that shaped Iranian history long before Islam and still has a living presence in Kerman and Yazd. The temple gives the museum a sacred setting rather than a neutral gallery feel.

Inside, displays introduce Zoroastrian customs, clothing, ritual objects, and community history. It is compact, but valuable, especially if your Iran route includes Yazd or if you want Kerman to feel like more than a staging point for desert forts and bazaars.
