Sanati Museum is one of Kerman's quieter cultural stops, focused on the work and legacy of Ali-Akbar Sanati, the Kerman-born artist known for painting and sculpture. It gives the city a different register from caravanserais, bazaars, and desert architecture.

The museum is best for visitors who want a compact indoor stop and a glimpse of modern Iranian art outside Tehran or Shiraz. It is not large, but its local connection makes it more meaningful than a generic gallery.
