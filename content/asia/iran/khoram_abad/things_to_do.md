---
title: Things to Do
type: section
---

Khorramabad's sights are concentrated around the city centre and the Khorramabad River that runs through it. The Falak-ol-Aflak castle is the obvious starting point, but the Sassanid-era stone features along the river add unexpected texture to a visit.

The surrounding Zagros mountain scenery rewards anyone who rents a car or takes a taxi out of the centre. Nearby gorges, springs, and the nomadic Lur communities on their seasonal migrations are the real draw for travellers with time to spare.
