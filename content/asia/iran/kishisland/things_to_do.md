title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Kish punches above its weight for a small island. The [Greek Ship](greek_ship), a rusted cargo vessel stranded in the shallows off the southwest coast since 1966, is one of the more atmospheric sights — photogenic at sunset and good for snorkelling. The [Karizak underground aqueduct](karizak_aqueduct) is genuinely impressive: a 2,500-year-old water system that you can walk through, cool and dimly lit, a contrast to the glare outside. [Harireh Ancient City](harireh_ancient_city) has the remains of a medieval trading city with wind towers and a Portuguese fort.

Scuba diving and snorkelling are worthwhile — the Persian Gulf has decent coral, and the wreck makes for an easy dive. [Dolphin-watching boat trips](dolphin_park) run from the harbour. The [north beaches](kish_beach) are the island's main attraction and reason enough to visit. A 56-kilometre [coastal cycling path](cycling_path) connects the main sights.
