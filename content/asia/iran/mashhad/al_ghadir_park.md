A mid-sized public park in the northern part of Mashhad with sports fields, walking paths, and a small lake. It is less famous than Mellat or Kuhsangi but draws strong local crowds on weekend evenings, when families set up picnic blankets and the air fills with the smell of grilling kebabs.

Accessible by metro and a short walk; suitable for a brief stop on a longer city walk.
