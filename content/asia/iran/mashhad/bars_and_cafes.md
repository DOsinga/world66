---
title: "Bars and Cafes"
type: section
---

Iran is dry; the cafés are about coffee, tea, and people-watching, not alcohol. Mashhad has a growing café scene, particularly in the western neighbourhoods of Sajjad, Ahmadabad, and the lanes around Kuhsangi Park. Expect strong Iranian espresso and inventive teas; many places also serve pastries and light meals.
