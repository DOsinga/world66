A trendy café in the Ahmadabad neighbourhood, Café Dideh draws a young, educated, mostly Mashhadi crowd for coffee, light meals, and weekend brunch. The interior is industrial-chic and the wifi is reliable, which makes it a useful working spot for visiting journalists and travellers.

A useful contrast to the more traditional teahouses near the shrine.
