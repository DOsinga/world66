title: "Day Trips"
type: section
---

Mashhad's hinterland is one of the most rewarding parts of a visit. The literary pilgrimage to [Tus](/asia/iran/mashhad/tus) and the [tomb of Ferdowsi](/asia/iran/mashhad/tomb_of_ferdowsi) takes a morning. The resort villages of [Torghabeh](/asia/iran/mashhad/torghabeh) and [Shandiz](/asia/iran/mashhad/shandiz) make for long afternoon lunches. The mountain village of [Kang](/asia/iran/mashhad/kang_village), the [Akhlamad waterfall](/asia/iran/mashhad/akhlamad_waterfall), and the literary city of [Neyshabur](/asia/iran/mashhad/neyshabur) are all worth a full day each.
