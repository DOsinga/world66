Mashhad's hinterland is one of the most rewarding parts of a visit. The literary pilgrimage to [Tus](/asia/iran/tus), with its ruined citadel and the [tomb of Ferdowsi](/asia/iran/mashhad/tomb_of_ferdowsi), takes a morning; pair it with the city of [Neyshabur](/asia/iran/neyshabur), birthplace of Omar Khayyam, for a full day of Khorasan's poets. The resort towns of [Torghabeh](/asia/iran/torghabeh) and [Shandiz](/asia/iran/shandiz), tucked into the cool foothills of the [Binalud range](/asia/iran/binalud), make for long afternoon lunches by the river. For something quieter, the stepped mountain village of [Kang](/asia/iran/kang) and the [Akhlamad waterfall](/asia/iran/mashhad/akhlamad_waterfall) each reward a full day on their own.
