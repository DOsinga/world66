---
title: "Eating Out"
type: section
---

Mashhad's restaurant scene divides sharply along pilgrim lines: many places near the shrine serve a narrow menu of inexpensive chelo kebab and rice dishes aimed at high-volume daily turnover. For a better and more varied meal, head out into the residential neighbourhoods west of the centre, or up to the resort villages of [Torghabeh](/asia/iran/mashhad/torghabeh) and [Shandiz](/asia/iran/mashhad/shandiz) for the famous Khorasani shishlik.

Local specialities to try: shishlik (huge lamb chops), sholeh mashhadi (a thick lentil-and-meat stew), the dessert sohan (saffron brittle), and the local sweet pistachio nougat.
