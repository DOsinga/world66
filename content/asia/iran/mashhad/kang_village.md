A picturesque mountain village about 50 km west of Mashhad, Kang clings to the side of a steep valley in stepped rows of stone-and-mud houses. The road in is unmade in places but the village itself rewards a slow walk: narrow alleyways, working orchards, and a handful of small teahouses serving local bread and yoghurt.

Best visited in spring when the surrounding orchards are in blossom. Hire a taxi for the day from Mashhad; the round trip including time in the village is six or seven hours.
