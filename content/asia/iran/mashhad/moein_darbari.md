A traditional restaurant near the central Shahid Square, Moein Darbari serves the standard Iranian menu — chelo kebab, juje, dizi, ghormeh sabzi — in a quietly elegant dining hall with carved-wood ceilings. The food is reliably good and the service is patient with non-Persian-speaking visitors.

A useful, characterful lunch stop on a day exploring the shrine and the central bazaar.
