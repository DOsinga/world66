The ancient city of Neyshabur, 115 km west of Mashhad on the Mashhad–Tehran road, was one of the greatest centres of Persian learning in the early Islamic period before being destroyed by the Mongols in 1221. The modern town is small but holds two remarkable tombs — Omar Khayyam and the mystic poet Attar — set in landscaped gardens on the outskirts.

The Neyshabur turquoise mines in the nearby hills have supplied the gemstone for thousands of years and remain one of the largest sources of high-quality turquoise in the world. Bazaars in town sell loose stones and finished jewellery at honest prices.
