A garden complex around the [Tomb of Nader Shah](/asia/iran/mashhad/tomb_of_nader_shah), Saadabad combines the mausoleum with a small museum, a series of bronze military monuments, and pleasant lawns and flowerbeds. It is a popular spot for local families on Friday afternoons and a useful quiet stop between visits to the shrine.

Free entry to the gardens; a small fee for the museum. About 15 minutes' walk from the shrine.
