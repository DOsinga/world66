A small mountain town about 30 km west of Mashhad, Torghabeh has been the city's favourite hot-weather escape for centuries. The setting is cool and green — the foothills of the Binalud range, with poplar groves and orchards along the river — and the main road through town is lined with kebab restaurants where Mashhadis come for the local trout, dizi stew, and saffron rice.

A long evening of eating and walking by the river is the standard itinerary. Combine with Shandiz, a similar resort village a few kilometres further on.
