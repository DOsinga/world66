The ruins of Tus, the medieval city that preceded Mashhad as the regional capital, lie 25 km north and make an obvious half-day trip. The site is dominated by the remnants of an enormous earthen citadel wall — the Arg of Tus — encircling a flat plateau where the city stood until its sacking by Tamerlane.

The main monuments today are the [tomb of Ferdowsi](/asia/iran/mashhad/tomb_of_ferdowsi) and the more modest tomb of the great theologian al-Ghazali nearby. The ride out passes pistachio orchards and modest farming villages.
