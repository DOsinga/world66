Meshkin-Shahr sits to the west of Ardebil and north of Mount Sabalan in the Iranian province of Ardabil. The climate is temperate in summer and harsh in winter; the surrounding highlands are the territory of [Shahsavan pastoral nomads](/asia/iran/meshkinshahr/shahsavan_nomads) who migrate seasonally with their flocks between highland summer pastures and lowland winter grazing grounds.

The area has a respectable collection of historical monuments. The [Castle of Arshoq](/asia/iran/meshkinshahr/arshoq_castle) and the [Tomb of Shaykh Haydar](/asia/iran/meshkinshahr/tomb_shaykh_haydar) — father of the founder of the Safavid dynasty — are the most notable. The slopes of Mount Sabalan hide several mineral springs — Googerdi and Qotoor Sooyee are the best known — used by locals for their therapeutic properties. The [Meshginshahr suspension bridge](/asia/iran/meshkinshahr/suspension_bridge) over the Aras River gorge south of town is a popular outing for local families.

Meshkin-Shahr is not a major tourist destination, but those travelling between Ardebil and the border region will find it a pleasant stop, and the [Shahsavan nomadic culture](/asia/iran/meshkinshahr/shahsavan_nomads) of the surrounding countryside is worth seeking out.
