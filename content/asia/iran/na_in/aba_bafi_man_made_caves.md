The Aba Bafi caves in Muhammadieh, just outside Na'in, are man-made underground spaces that local tradition links to the area's Zoroastrian past. Their east-facing entrances and cool interiors later made them useful as workshops for weaving cloaks and rugs from sheep and camel wool.

Some of the weaving spaces are said to be centuries old, making the visit a mix of local craft history and unusual desert architecture.
