Na'in's old bazaar runs in a curved line through the historic centre, with side passages leading toward mosques, houses, and neighbourhood spaces. It is much quieter than the bazaars of Isfahan or Yazd, and some sections feel half-asleep.

That quiet is part of the point. Walk it for the scale, the brick vaults, and the way it ties the old town together.
