Fatemi House is one of Na'in's grand traditional houses, standing near Narenj Castle and the old bazaar. It belonged to an influential local family and was built with separate spaces for summer and winter living, storage, stables, and formal reception.

Visit for the layout as much as the decoration: the house shows how wealthy desert households managed heat, privacy, and social life through architecture.
