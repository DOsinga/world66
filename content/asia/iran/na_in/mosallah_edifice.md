The Mosallah is an octagonal mausoleum and garden complex south of the old centre, enclosed by high walls and marked by wind towers and a turquoise dome. It is not as essential as the Jameh Mosque, but it rounds out Na'in's desert architecture nicely.

The water reservoir on one side is a reminder that in towns like Na'in, architecture and water management were never separate subjects.
