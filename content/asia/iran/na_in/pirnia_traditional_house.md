Pirnia Traditional House, opposite the Jameh Mosque, is a Safavid-era desert house now used as an ethnology museum. Its rooms, garden, plasterwork, painted decoration, and service spaces show how a wealthy Na'in household handled privacy, climate, storage, and guests.

It pairs naturally with the mosque: one explains the town's public religious architecture, the other its domestic life.
