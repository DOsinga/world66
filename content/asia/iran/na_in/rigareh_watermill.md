Rigareh Watermill is an underground qanat-powered mill near Na'in. Water from the Keykhosrow qanat once filled a deep tank and drove the turbine used for grinding grain.

The engineering is the reason to visit. The mill sits far below ground, reached by a long access corridor, and gives a rare close look at how qanat water systems could power more than irrigation.
