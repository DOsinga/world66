The Apadana Palace is the heart of Persepolis for many visitors: the great audience hall where the Achaemenid kings staged imperial ceremony on a vast architectural scale. Its columns, staircases, and carved reliefs make the empire visible in stone, especially in the processions of tribute-bearers from across the Persian world.

Take time with the stair reliefs rather than treating the palace as a quick ruin. The details of clothing, animals, vessels, and gestures are what make the Apadana extraordinary, turning a royal platform into a carefully choreographed image of power, order, and empire.
