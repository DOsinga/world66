The Gate of All Nations is one of the great threshold moments at Persepolis. Built under Xerxes, it announced the ceremonial entrance to the Achaemenid royal complex with colossal stone guardian figures and inscriptions naming the king and empire.

Do not rush through it on the way to the palace terraces. The gateway is where Persepolis first shifts from ruins into imperial theatre, and the scale of the bulls, doorways, and stone blocks sets up everything that follows.
