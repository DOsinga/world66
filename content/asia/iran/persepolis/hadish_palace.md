Hadish Palace is one of the major palace ruins within Persepolis, traditionally associated with Xerxes. Its remaining stairways, stone platforms, and carved details help visitors understand the scale and organization of the Achaemenid ceremonial terrace.

It should be read as part of the wider Persepolis complex rather than as a standalone monument. Even so, naming it separately helps visitors slow down and distinguish the different palace zones instead of treating the whole site as one blur of ruins.
