Istakhr was one of the important ancient cities of Fars, close to Persepolis but belonging to a later political story as well. Its remains are fragmentary compared with the terraces and reliefs nearby, yet the site helps bridge Achaemenid, Sasanian, and early Islamic history in the same landscape.

It is best for travellers who already care about the region's archaeology. Visit with Persepolis and Naqsh-e Rostam in mind, and the scattered stones begin to read less like leftovers and more like evidence of a long-lived centre of power.
