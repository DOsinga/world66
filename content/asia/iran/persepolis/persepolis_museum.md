The Persepolis Museum occupies part of the reconstructed palace area on the terrace, traditionally identified with the Queen's Quarters. It is small compared with the ruins outside, but it gives context to the Achaemenid site through excavated fragments, inscriptions, architectural pieces, and models that are easy to miss when walking straight from gateway to staircase.

Visit it before or after the main terrace, depending on the heat and crowds. The museum will not replace the reliefs and palace remains, but it helps turn Persepolis from a sequence of spectacular stone surfaces into a working archaeological site with layers of excavation, restoration, and interpretation.
