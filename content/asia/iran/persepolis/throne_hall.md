The Throne Hall, also known as the Hundred-Column Hall, is one of the places at Persepolis where the scale of Achaemenid power becomes physical. Even in ruin, the column bases and doorways suggest a vast covered space built for ceremony, display, and imperial theatre.

It is worth pausing here rather than treating it as just another set of stones between the more famous gates and palaces. Look for the carved doorjambs and the way the hall sits within the larger processional logic of Persepolis: movement, hierarchy, and controlled spectacle.
