The Treasury of Persepolis is one of the places where the ceremonial empire becomes tangible. This was the storage complex for wealth, tribute, administrative records, and precious objects, tucked into the royal terrace rather than presented as a showpiece like the Apadana.

Today the foundations can look austere, so it helps to pause and imagine movement: officials, guards, storerooms, tablets, and the machinery of an empire that had to count as well as impress. It adds a useful practical layer to the grandeur of Persepolis.
