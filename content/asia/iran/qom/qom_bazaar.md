The bazaar surrounding the Fatimah al-Masumah Shrine is a working religious market rather than a tourist attraction, and all the more interesting for it. The covered lanes are stocked with prayer rugs, rosaries, religious texts, incense, chador fabric, and devotional images — the full material culture of Shia pilgrimage.

The food stalls sell *sohan*, Qom's famous brittle toffee made with saffron, rosewater, and pistachios. It is the standard gift to bring home from Qom and comes in dozens of varieties; the better shops will let you taste before you buy. The bazaar is most atmospheric during pilgrimage peaks — particularly the months of Muharram and Ramadan — when the crowds and the ceremony around the shrine intensify everything.
