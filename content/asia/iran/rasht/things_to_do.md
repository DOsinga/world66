---
title: Things to Do
type: section
---

Rasht's best sights are woven into daily life rather than set apart behind gates. Start at [Municipality Square](/asia/iran/rasht/municipality_square), then walk into [Rasht Bazaar](/asia/iran/rasht/rasht_bazaar) while the fish, herbs, and pickles are still fresh. The old centre is compact enough to explore on foot, though traffic around it can be assertive.

For a broader sense of Gilan, make time for the [Gilan Rural Heritage Museum](/asia/iran/rasht/gilan_rural_heritage_museum) in Saravan Forest Park. It is more than a folk display: whole wooden houses from different parts of the province have been dismantled, moved, and reassembled there. [Eynak Lagoon](/asia/iran/rasht/eynak_lagoon), on the western edge of the city, is rougher and less curated, but good for seeing the wetland landscape that surrounds Rasht.
