Sanandaj — historically known as Senna — is the capital of Kurdistan Province in western Iran, set in a mountain valley at about 1,500 metres elevation. The city is the cultural heart of Iranian Kurdistan, and Kurdish language, music, and traditions are everywhere: in the bazaar, in the teahouses, and in the sound of the tanbur lute drifting from doorways in the old quarter.

The city has a scattering of historical sites — the [Khosro Abad Mansion](/asia/iran/sanandaj/khosro_abad_mansion), the [old bazaar](/asia/iran/sanandaj/old_bazaar), and the [Jameh Mosque](/asia/iran/sanandaj/dar_ul_ihsan_mosque) — but the real appeal is the surrounding landscape. [Abidar](/asia/iran/sanandaj/abidar_park) on the edge of town offers views across the city and the mountains beyond. The road south to Marivan and [Lake Zarivar](/asia/iran/sanandaj/lake_zarivar) passes through some of the most beautiful highland scenery in Iran. Sanandaj is also a good base for exploring the Kurdish villages of the Hawraman region, where stepped stone houses cling to steep valley walls.
