Semnan sits on the old Tehran-Mashhad corridor at the southern foot of the Alborz, where mountain air gives way to the dry edge of the Dasht-e Kavir. It is the provincial capital, but it feels quieter and more workaday than Iran's famous historic cities.

The reason to stop is the old centre. The blue-tiled [Semnan Citadel Gate](/asia/iran/semnan/semnan_citadel_gate) is the city's signature monument, a surviving Qajar gateway from a demolished fortification. Nearby, [Semnan Bazaar](/asia/iran/semnan/semnan_bazaar) and the [Jameh Mosque of Semnan](/asia/iran/semnan/jameh_mosque) form the heart of the traditional town, with brickwork that reaches back into the Seljuk and Timurid periods.

Semnan also works as a practical base for the surrounding province. [Mahdishar](/asia/iran/mahdishar) and Shahmirzad climb quickly into cooler Alborz country, while Damghan, Bastam, and the desert routes east and south make sense by road or rail. Stay a night if you enjoy understated Iranian towns with real history and little performance.
