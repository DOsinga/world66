title: Things to Do
type: section
---

Semnan's sights cluster around the old centre. Start at the [Semnan Citadel Gate](/asia/iran/semnan/semnan_citadel_gate), then continue toward [Semnan Bazaar](/asia/iran/semnan/semnan_bazaar) and the [Jameh Mosque](/asia/iran/semnan/jameh_mosque). The distances are short, and the pleasure is in piecing together the remains of an old caravan city now surrounded by modern traffic.

This is not a place that overwhelms you with monuments. It rewards a slower eye: brick geometry, tile panels, old shopfronts, and the sudden view north to the Alborz when the air is clear. For cooler air and mountain scenery, continue west to [Mahdishar](/asia/iran/mahdishar).
