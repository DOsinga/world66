A small café in one of the restored merchant houses of the old quarter, with a few tables set out in the inner courtyard around a tiled pool. Coffee, tea, fresh juices, and a short menu of light meals. The atmosphere — old brick walls, climbing geraniums, the sound of water — is the reason to come.

This is the kind of place travellers stumble into in the heat of an afternoon and end up staying for an hour. Good Wi-Fi, friendly staff, and English-speaking customers from the nearby hostels.
