---
title: "Eating Out"
type: section
---

Shiraz is one of the better eating cities in Iran. The local cuisine leans on fresh herbs, citrus, and slow-cooked stews, and the city's love of food is unselfconscious. Two dishes are local signatures: **kalam polo Shirazi** (rice with cabbage, meatballs, and a long list of herbs) and **Shirazi salad** (cucumber, tomato, onion, mint, lime — eaten everywhere in Iran but said to be best at the source).

You will also find very good kebab, faloodeh (the chilled rice-noodle dessert that originated near here), and excellent fresh bread from neighbourhood bakeries throughout the day. Most restaurants do not serve alcohol; Iranian beer is alcohol-free.
