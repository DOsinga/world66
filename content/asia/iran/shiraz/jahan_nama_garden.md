Jahan Nama Garden is one of Shiraz's old garden landscapes, close to the tomb of Hafez and easy to fold into a northern-city walk. Its pavilions, cypresses, water channels, and enclosed calm are part of the same Persian garden tradition that makes Shiraz feel distinct from Iran's busier cities.

It is less famous than Eram Garden, which helps. Visit when you want a quieter pause between tombs and historic houses, and treat the garden as atmosphere rather than spectacle: shade, geometry, and the old Shirazi habit of making leisure feel cultivated.
