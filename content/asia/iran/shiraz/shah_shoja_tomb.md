Shah Shoja's Tomb is a quieter historical stop in Shiraz, associated with the fourteenth-century Muzaffarid ruler who governed during the age of Hafez. It does not have the emotional pull of the poet's tombs, but it adds another layer to Shiraz's medieval political history.

Visit if you are already exploring beyond the obvious circuit. The tomb is most interesting when you connect it with Hafez, court culture, and the shifting dynasties that made Shiraz a literary and political centre.
