Behnam House is a Qajar-period residence that has been meticulously restored and now
forms part of the Tabriz Islamic Arts University. The house consists of two main
sections — a summer pavilion and a winter pavilion — arranged around a long courtyard
with a central pool.

The decoration is the reason to come: painted stucco ceilings, finely carved wooden
doors, and rooms with stained-glass orosi windows that throw coloured light across
the floors. Because the building is in active university use, visiting hours are
restricted, but the courtyard alone is freely accessible during the day.
