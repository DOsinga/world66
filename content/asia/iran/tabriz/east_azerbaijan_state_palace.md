East Azerbaijan State Palace stands on the long-used seat of provincial power in Tabriz. Earlier governor and crown-prince residences on this site were destroyed by floods in the 1930s, and the present complex now combines government offices with the Azerbaijan Governorship Museum. It is a useful stop for visitors interested in the city's political history and public architecture.
