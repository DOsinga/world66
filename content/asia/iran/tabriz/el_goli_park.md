El Goli, known under the Pahlavis as Shah Goli, is Tabriz's best-loved park — an
artificial square lake with an octagonal pavilion sitting on a raised platform in the
middle, reached by a stone causeway. The site dates from the late 15th century and
was redesigned and expanded several times under the Qajars; the present pavilion is
an early-20th-century reconstruction.

Locals come here in the evenings to walk the perimeter path, hire pedal boats, and
eat at one of the restaurants ringing the lake. There is a small amusement park at
the western end and a teleski up the hillside above. It's a 15-minute taxi ride
from the centre and well worth the trip, particularly around sunset.
