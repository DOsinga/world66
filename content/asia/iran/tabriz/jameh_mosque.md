The Jameh Mosque is the historic Friday mosque of Tabriz and is structurally part
of the Grand Bazaar complex — many visitors pass it on the way through without
realising what it is. The building dates from the Seljuk period in its earliest
form and has been rebuilt several times after earthquakes, most thoroughly during
the Safavid and Qajar eras.

The brick prayer hall is sober and beautifully proportioned, lit only by openings in
the dome. The mihrab is intricately carved and the wooden minbar is a fine example
of late Safavid craftsmanship. Slip in for ten minutes between bazaar visits — it's
a quiet counterpoint to the noise outside.
