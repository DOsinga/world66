Koofteh Tabrizi is the dish you cannot leave Tabriz without trying: a single grapefruit-
or melon-sized meatball made from rice, split peas, ground meat, and a generous
handful of herbs, stuffed with a boiled egg, dried plums or apricots, and walnuts,
and simmered in a tomato-saffron broth. It is served with the broth as a kind of
shared centrepiece, broken open at the table.

Almost every old-fashioned restaurant in the city offers it. Notable spots include
Modern Restaurant in the bazaar district, Khaneh Ma in the historic Mokhberalsaltaneh
House, and several small family-run places along Tarbiat Street. Order one to share
between two — it really is huge.
