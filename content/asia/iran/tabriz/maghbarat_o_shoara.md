Maghbarat-o-Shoara, the Mausoleum of Poets, is a cemetery and memorial complex for writers and poets associated with Tabriz. Its best-known modern burial is Shahriyar, the Azerbaijani poet, and the site gives a useful window into the city's literary culture.
