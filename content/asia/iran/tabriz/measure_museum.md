The Measure Museum occupies the historic Salmasi House, a beautiful Qajar-era
courtyard residence in the old quarter. The collection traces the history of
measurement in Iran — weights, scales, abacuses, rulers, sundials, water clocks,
and a peculiar range of devices for measuring everything from rainfall to body
temperature.

It sounds dry but is genuinely interesting, partly because the building itself is
worth the price of admission. The mirrored hall and the stained-glass windows are
beautifully preserved. Even if measuring devices are not your obsession, you'll come
away with a feel for how a wealthy Tabrizi household lived a century ago.
