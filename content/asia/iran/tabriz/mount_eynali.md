Mount Eynali rises just north of Tabriz as a red wall visible from much of the city. Locals come early in the morning and on weekends to walk the paved trail to the first peak, where the view opens across the city and surrounding mountains.

The upper station area has a small shrine, memorials, windmills, and simple places for tea and snacks. You can hike up from the northern edge of the city or use the cable car when it is running.
