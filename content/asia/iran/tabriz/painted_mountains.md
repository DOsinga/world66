The Painted Mountains, sometimes called Aladaglar (Persian for "coloured mountains"),
are a range of low hills several hours' drive southeast of Tabriz, near the town of
Mahneshan in Zanjan province. Mineral deposits in the sedimentary rock have weathered
into stripes of vivid red, mustard yellow, white, and pale green, producing landscapes
that look genuinely unreal in good light.

Reaching them requires a long day trip with a hired car or driver. The best light is
around sunrise and sunset, when the angle of the sun deepens every colour. Combine
with a stop at the nearby Behestan Castle if you have time. The area is undeveloped —
bring water and food.
