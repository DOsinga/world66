The Maqbarat-ol-Shoara, or Mausoleum of Poets, is a striking modern monument in
the Surkhab district that commemorates more than four hundred poets, mystics, and
scholars buried in the old cemeteries of Tabriz. Among the most famous are Asadi
Tusi, Qatran Tabrizi, and the modern Iranian poet Mohammad-Hossein Shahriar.

The structure itself, completed in 1971, is a series of interlocking concrete arches
that frame an internal hall with the symbolic graves. It's an unusual and powerful
piece of late-modernist architecture in a country better known for its tile-domed
mosques. Worth the short taxi ride from the centre.
