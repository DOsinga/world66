Sa'at Tower, also called the Tabriz Municipality Palace, dominates the central
Sa'at Square. Built between 1934 and 1935 by German engineers in a Bavarian
neoclassical style, it still functions as the city hall and main office of the
municipal government. The clocks on each of the four faces of the tower are visible
from much of the surrounding centre.

The lower floors contain a small museum about the city's history, with photographs
and city-planning models. Even if you skip the museum, the square is the natural
heart of downtown Tabriz — a useful orientation point and a good place to sit and
watch the city go by.
