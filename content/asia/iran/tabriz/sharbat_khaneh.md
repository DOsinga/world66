Sharbat Khaneh occupies a restored Qajar-era house in the old quarter and is one of
the best places in Tabriz to try the city's distinct Azeri cuisine. The menu leans
heavily on local specialities: koofteh Tabrizi (the city's signature giant stuffed
meatball), bonab kebab, dizi sangaki, and several rice dishes you won't find south
of the Aras River.

The setting is half the point — high painted ceilings, stained glass, traditional
seating arranged around a small interior courtyard. Service is unhurried and the
portions are generous. Reservations recommended on weekend evenings.
