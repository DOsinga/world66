The Shohada Mosque ("Mosque of the Martyrs") stands on Shohada Square in the city
centre and serves as one of Tabriz's most important active places of worship. The
building is relatively modern but the dome and minarets follow traditional Persian
patterns and are a familiar element of the skyline.

The square in front of the mosque is a major gathering point during religious
holidays, particularly Muharram, when processions converge here. As a working
mosque, dress modestly and behave respectfully if you go inside — non-Muslim
visitors are generally welcomed.
