St. Mary Armenian Church is a centre of Tabriz's Armenian community, with a small museum attached to the church. A church on this site is said to have been visited by Marco Polo in 1275, and the present interior reflects Armenian church design in its tabernacle and decoration. Visit for a quieter counterpoint to the nearby Blue Mosque and Grand Bazaar.
