---
title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Tabriz's main sights are concentrated in and around the old city centre. The
[Grand Bazaar](/asia/iran/tabriz/grand_bazaar) is the obvious starting point and
could consume an entire day on its own — combine it with stops at the nearby
[Jameh Mosque](/asia/iran/tabriz/jameh_mosque) and the ruined
[Ali Shah Mosque](/asia/iran/tabriz/ali_shah_mosque) right beside it.

A short walk away are the [Blue Mosque](/asia/iran/tabriz/blue_mosque),
the [Azerbaijan Museum](/asia/iran/tabriz/azerbaijan_museum) — Iran's second-most-
important archaeological museum — and the
[Constitution House](/asia/iran/tabriz/constitution_house), where the leaders of
the 1906 revolution organised their resistance. The central
[Sa'at Tower](/asia/iran/tabriz/saat_tower) makes a useful orientation point.

For an evening, take a taxi out to [El Goli Park](/asia/iran/tabriz/el_goli_park),
where Tabrizi families come to walk around the artificial lake. The
[Maqbarat-ol-Shoara](/asia/iran/tabriz/poets_mausoleum) (Mausoleum of Poets) is a
remarkable piece of modernist architecture in the Surkhab district.

Don't miss the day trip to [Kandovan](/asia/iran/tabriz/kandovan), an inhabited
cave village an hour south of the city, and — with more time — the unreal
striped landscape of the [Painted Mountains](/asia/iran/tabriz/painted_mountains).
