Valiasr is the wealthy, modern uptown district of Tabriz, well east of the historic
core. Wide boulevards, glass-fronted apartment blocks, and the city's better hotels
and shopping centres are concentrated here. This is where middle-class Tabrizi
families come for an evening out — the cafes are busier and more contemporary in
feel than anything in the centre.

For travellers, Valiasr matters mostly as a place to find a decent international
meal, a Western-style coffee, or a hotel with reliable hot water. There's no
sightseeing to speak of. The district is well connected to the centre by metro and
by frequent taxis.
