title: "Reading Lolita in Tehran"
type: poi
author: "Azar Nafisi"
tags:
  - books
---

Nafisi's memoir describes the years she spent teaching English literature at the University of Tehran before and after the revolution, and the secret reading group she ran in her apartment in the 1990s — seven women meeting weekly to read Nabokov, Fitzgerald, and James, books that the Islamic Republic found variously immoral or imperialist. The book is both a portrait of Tehran's intellectual life and a meditation on what literature does for people who cannot live freely. The descriptions of the city — the architecture, the street life, the checkpoints — are those of someone who loved Tehran deeply and left it reluctantly.
