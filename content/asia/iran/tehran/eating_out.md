---
title: "Eating Out"
type: section
---

Tehran has one of the most interesting food scenes in the Middle East — Iranian cooking is genuinely extraordinary, and the city's restaurants range from traditional *dizi* houses serving stews in clay pots to contemporary restaurants that pair Persian ingredients with modern techniques. The northern districts have the most variety; the bazaar area has the most character. As a Muslim-majority Islamic Republic, alcohol is not served in restaurants or publicly available, but the food more than compensates.
