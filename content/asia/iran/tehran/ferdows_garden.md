Ferdows Garden, also known as Bagh-e Ferdows, is a Qajar-era garden and mansion in north Tehran. Its shaded grounds and historic building give a more relaxed cultural stop than the capital's larger palace complexes.

The site is also associated with Iran's cinema museum, which makes it especially worthwhile for visitors interested in film, modern culture, or Tehran's northern neighborhoods. It pairs well with Tajrish and the foothill districts.
