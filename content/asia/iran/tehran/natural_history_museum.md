Tehran's Natural History Museum displays specimens and exhibits on geology, zoology, botany, fossils, and the natural world. It is a modest but useful stop for families or visitors who want a break from the city's palace, art, and political history museums.

The exhibits are spread over several levels, so allow enough time to browse rather than treating it as a quick photo stop.
