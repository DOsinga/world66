Vahdat Hall, originally Roudaki Hall, is Tehran's most important formal performing-arts venue. Opened in the late 1960s, it was designed as a serious opera and concert hall rather than a multipurpose auditorium, and it still carries that sense of cultural ambition in its scale and interior form.

The hall matters even if you are not attending a performance, because it sits at the centre of Iran's modern classical music, theatre, and opera history. For most travellers, though, the best reason to come is an actual event. Listings change, and access for foreign visitors can depend on the programme, so check current schedules before making a special trip.
