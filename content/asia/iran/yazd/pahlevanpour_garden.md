Pahlevanpour Garden is one of the historic Persian gardens associated with Yazd province, located in Mehriz where water management, shade, and axial design turn an arid setting into a cultivated refuge. Its pools, trees, and pavilion architecture show why Persian gardens are as much about climate and engineering as beauty.

The garden is a day trip from Yazd rather than an in-town stop, but it is worthwhile for travelers interested in desert architecture and landscape design. Visit with the expectation of a composed historic garden, not a flower-heavy park, and combine it with other Mehriz sights if possible.
