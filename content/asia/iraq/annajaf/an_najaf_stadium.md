A multi-purpose stadium north of the old city, home to Najaf FC of the Iraqi Premier League. Football is taken seriously in southern Iraq, and matches against rivals from Baghdad and Basra draw raucous crowds.

Even outside match days the stadium is worth a quick look for travellers interested in modern Iraqi life — it sits in a part of town dominated by new construction rather than shrines, and the contrast with the old quarter is striking.
