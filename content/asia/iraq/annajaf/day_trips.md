The obvious day trip from Najaf is **Karbala**, eighty kilometres north, site of the shrines of Imam Husayn and his half-brother Abbas — the second great pilgrimage city of Shia Islam. Shared taxis run constantly. Many visitors do Najaf and Karbala back to back as part of a single pilgrimage.

Further north, **Babylon** can be visited as a long day trip if you set out early. The ruins are a shadow of what they once were — much was looted, much was rebuilt in modern brick under Saddam — but standing on the foundations of the Tower of Babel is still something. Closer to home, the ancient city of **Kufa**, with its great mosque, is so close it counts as a half-day rather than a full day trip.
