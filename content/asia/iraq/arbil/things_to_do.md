---
title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Erbil's ancient citadel mound dominates everything else in the city — you can see it from almost everywhere, and it is the reason to come. Below the citadel, the bazaar district and [Qaysari Bazaar](/asia/iraq/arbil/qaysari_bazaar) give a sense of the city's commercial character before oil money changed everything. The [Kurdistan Museum](/asia/iraq/arbil/kurdistan_museum) and [Kurdish Textile Museum](/asia/iraq/arbil/kurdish_textile_museum) both deserve time if you are interested in the region's history and material culture.

For a gentler afternoon, [Sami Abdulrahman Park](/asia/iraq/arbil/sami_abdulrahman_park) in the western part of the city is where Erbil's families come to walk and sit in the evenings. The Christian district of Ankawa, with its churches and pleasant cafes, is a short taxi ride from the centre and has a noticeably different atmosphere from the rest of the city.
