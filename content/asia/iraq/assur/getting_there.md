---
title: Getting There
type: section
---

Assur is approximately 100 km south of Mosul in the Saladin Governorate, accessible by road via the town of Shirqat (Al-Shirqat). The site is on the western bank of the Tigris; the main road approaches from the east and crosses to the site by bridge.

Security conditions in northern Iraq have improved significantly since the defeat of ISIS in 2017–2018, but the area between Mosul and Tikrit saw intense fighting and ongoing instability during that period. Check current security conditions before travelling. Most visitors reach the site from Mosul by private car; there is no public transport directly to the archaeological site. A local guide is advisable both for navigation and for access arrangements with the site authorities.
