---
title: Things to Do
type: section
---

Assur is an archaeological site, not a town in the conventional sense. Visitors come to walk among the ruins of one of the oldest cities in the world, see the excavated remains of Assyrian temples and palaces, and look out over the Tigris from a promontory that has been inhabited for five thousand years. Allow at least two hours on site, more if you have a specialist interest in Assyrian archaeology.
