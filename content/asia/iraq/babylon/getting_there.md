---
title: "Getting There"
type: section
---

The ruins of Babylon are about 90 kilometres south of [Baghdad](/asia/iraq/baghdad), on the western bank of the Euphrates near the modern town of Al Hillah. The most common way to visit is on a day trip from Baghdad, by hired car with driver — roughly two hours each way on a good road. Most travellers arrange this through a local agency, which also handles permits where needed.

The same route continues south to [Karbala](/asia/iraq/karbala) and [An Najaf](/asia/iraq/annajaf), and many tours combine all three sites in two days or a long single day. Public transport (buses and shared taxis from Baghdad) is possible but slow and not always straightforward for foreigners.

Check current security and travel advisories before going — conditions in this part of Iraq change. A guide is strongly recommended both for context and for navigating the site.
