Kahramana is one of Baghdad's recognizable public sculptures, showing the servant girl from the Ali Baba tale pouring boiling oil into jars. The fountain turns a story from the wider Arabic literary imagination into a city landmark, with a mix of folklore, movement, and civic symbolism.

It is a quick stop rather than a major museum visit, but it fits well into a route through central Baghdad's modern monuments and public squares. The sculpture is useful because it shows a softer, storytelling side of the city alongside memorials, mosques, and administrative architecture.
