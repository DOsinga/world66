Mustansiriya Madrasah is a 13th-century Abbasid scholarly complex in Baghdad's old commercial district. Founded in 1227, it is one of the city's most important surviving medieval buildings and pairs well with nearby Al-Mutanabbi Street, Khan Murjan, and the Abbasid Palace.
