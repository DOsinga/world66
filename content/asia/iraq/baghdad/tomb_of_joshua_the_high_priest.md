The Tomb of Joshua the High Priest is one of Baghdad's lesser-known religious heritage sites, remembered in local Jewish history and in accounts of the city's once-diverse sacred geography. It is modest as a visitor sight, but it adds important context to Baghdad beyond its Islamic and Abbasid landmarks.

Practical access can be sensitive and should be checked locally. Treat it as a heritage marker to understand the city's layered communities rather than as a conventional museum-style attraction.
