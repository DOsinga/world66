Zumurrud Khatun Mosque and Mausoleum is one of Baghdad's most distinctive medieval landmarks, known for its conical, ribbed mausoleum tower. The structure is associated with the Abbasid period and stands as a rare visible reminder of the city's deep architectural history.

For visitors able to explore Baghdad with appropriate local guidance, the site offers a different register from modern monuments and riverfront life. Its value is both visual and historical: brickwork, burial architecture, and the memory of the Abbasid capital in one compact place.
