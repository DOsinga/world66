The Armenian Church of the Virgin Mary is one of the older Christian landmarks in central Basra. An Armenian church has stood on this site since the 18th century, while the present building dates from the late 19th century.

It is a modest stop, but it adds useful context to Basra's mixed religious and mercantile past. Check local access and security conditions before visiting.
