The Chaldean Catholic Church of the Virgin Mary is a reminder that Basra's history is not only Islamic, Arab, or mercantile. Christian communities have long been part of southern Iraq's urban fabric, and churches like this preserve a quieter layer of the city's religious life.

Travel conditions in Basra can be complicated, so check local advice before setting out and be respectful around services. The church is most meaningful for travelers interested in Iraq's plural history and the fragile survival of minority heritage in a city better known for oil, ports, and the Shatt al-Arab.
