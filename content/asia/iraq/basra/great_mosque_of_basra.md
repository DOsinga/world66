The Great Mosque of Basra refers to one of the city's most historically important mosque sites, tied to Basra's role as an early Islamic garrison city and intellectual centre. For travellers, its significance lies less in ornament than in the age and weight of the place name.

Basra's surviving heritage can be fragmentary, so any visit needs current local advice and realistic expectations. Still, the mosque belongs in the city's core story alongside the old canals, merchant houses, shrines, and river trade.
