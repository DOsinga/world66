Saint Thomas Chaldean Church is one of the clearest reminders that Basra's history is not only Islamic and mercantile. The church belongs to the Chaldean Catholic community, part of Iraq's long and fragile Christian presence.

Visitors should approach it respectfully and check local conditions before going, as access and security can change. For those already in Basra, the church adds an important human layer to a city better known for the Shatt al-Arab, markets, and oil-era history.
