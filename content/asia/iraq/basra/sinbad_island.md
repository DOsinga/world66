Sinbad Island sits in the Shatt al-Arab landscape that defines Basra, drawing on the city's old association with river journeys, trade, and seafaring stories. It is more a local leisure area than a polished international attraction, but that is part of its interest.

For travellers in Basra, the island offers a way to think about the city from the water rather than only from streets and markets. Conditions and access can change, so check locally before making the trip.
