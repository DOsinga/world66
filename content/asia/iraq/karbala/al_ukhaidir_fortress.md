Al-Ukhaidir Fortress is a monumental early Islamic fortress in the desert southwest of Karbala, built as a fortified station on routes linking Iraq with the wider region. UNESCO's tentative-list description calls it an outstanding defensive construction and highlights its unusual military design within Iraq and the Arab-Islamic world.

The attraction is the scale: thick walls, towers, vaulted spaces, and a sense of isolation that makes the complex feel more like a desert stronghold than a city monument. It is not part of Karbala's pilgrimage core, so visiting requires a dedicated excursion and current local security advice, but architecturally it is one of the region's most rewarding non-shrine sites.
