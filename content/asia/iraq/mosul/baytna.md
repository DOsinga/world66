Baytna is a cultural centre in a restored historic house in Mosul's Old City. Its exhibitions and small concerts make it part of the city's post-war cultural recovery, and the house itself gives visitors another look at the domestic architecture that once filled these lanes.

Programming changes, so check locally before making a special trip. Even when there is no event, it is a useful stop if you are already visiting the nearby al-Nuri Mosque area and old-city landmarks.
