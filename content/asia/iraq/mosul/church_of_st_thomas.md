The Church of St. Thomas is one of Mosul's old historic churches, traditionally linked to St. Thomas the Apostle and the early spread of Christianity eastward.

It is a meaningful stop for visitors tracing Mosul's layered religious history, especially alongside the city's other churches, mosques, shrines, and old-city sites.
