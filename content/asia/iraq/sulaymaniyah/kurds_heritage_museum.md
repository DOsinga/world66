The Kurd's Heritage Museum occupies a traditional building on Mawlawi Street in the old city centre and documents Kurdish material culture, folk traditions, and daily life from the late Ottoman period through the twentieth century. The collection focuses on objects — clothing, tools, weapons, musical instruments, household items — that represent the Kurdish way of life before the upheavals of the modern era.

The museum is smaller and more intimate than the Slemani Museum, and its focus on ethnography rather than archaeology makes it a useful complement. Several of the rooms are arranged as period domestic interiors — a Kurdish house, a workshop, a teahouse — that give a sense of how people actually lived in the region.

Worth visiting alongside the bazaar, which lies close by; the combination gives a good sense of the traditional urban and commercial life of Sulaymaniyah before the oil-driven transformation of the late twentieth century.
