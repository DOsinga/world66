---
title: "Eating Out"
type: section
---

Acre's Arab market and the streets of the old city are the best places to eat. The fish restaurants near the harbour serve the morning's catch, and the hummus places in the market are legendary among Israelis who make the trip just for a bowl. Don't leave without trying *safiha* — a flatbread topped with spiced minced meat from the market stalls.
