Abraham's Well is a heritage site in Be'er Sheva's Old City that commemorates the biblical covenant between Abraham and Abimelech, the story that gives the city its name. The well itself is a restored Ottoman-era well in a small plaza, not the original biblical site, but it has been developed into a visitor attraction with a brief presentation on the Genesis narrative and the ancient water-gathering practices of the Negev.

The surrounding area has been landscaped and a small visitor centre provides context. It is a modest attraction, but for anyone tracing the biblical geography of the Negev, it is a natural stop — the kind of place where a name on the map becomes something you can stand beside.
