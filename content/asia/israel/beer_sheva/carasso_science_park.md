Carasso Science Park is Be'er Sheva's hands-on science museum, set in the Old City with interactive exhibits, outdoor science games, and activities aimed at a wide range of ages. It is especially useful if you are travelling with children and need something more active than another historic building.

The museum also fits the city's modern identity as a university and technology centre. Allow enough time for the outdoor areas as well as the indoor displays.
