Be'er Sheva makes a reasonable base for the northern Negev. The [Negev](/asia/israel/negev) stretches south with remarkable landscapes including Mitzpe Ramon and its enormous erosion crater (makhtesh). Masada and the Dead Sea are about 100 km to the northeast.
