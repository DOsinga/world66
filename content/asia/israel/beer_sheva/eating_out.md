---
title: Eating Out
type: section
---

Be'er Sheva's food scene is driven by its large and diverse immigrant population — Soviet Jewish arrivals from the 1990s brought Eastern European cooking, Ethiopian immigrants have established several good restaurants, and the Bedouin and Mizrahi Jewish populations have shaped a local street food tradition centred on hummus, shakshuka, and grilled meats. The university district has a concentration of affordable cafes and restaurants that cater to students.
