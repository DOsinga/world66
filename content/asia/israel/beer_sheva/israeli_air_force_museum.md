The Israeli Air Force Museum near Be'er Sheva is an outdoor-heavy aviation collection with aircraft, helicopters, and military hardware tied to Israel's air force history. The desert setting gives the rows of planes an especially stark backdrop.

Aviation enthusiasts will get the most from it, but the museum also helps explain how central air power has been to Israel's modern military story. Go prepared for sun and heat, since much of the visit is exposed.
