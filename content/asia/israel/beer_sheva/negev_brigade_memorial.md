The Negev Brigade Memorial is one of Beersheba's most distinctive modern landmarks, a sculptural concrete complex on a hill northeast of the city. Designed as both memorial and landscape, it uses towers, walls, and open forms to frame the desert and the city's edge.

The site is strongest near morning or late afternoon light, when the concrete and surrounding hills gain depth. It is a worthwhile stop for architecture, Israeli history, and views over Beersheba.
