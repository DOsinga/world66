Negev Zoo is Be'er Sheva's long-running animal park, set on the edge of the city with a focus that includes desert wildlife alongside more familiar zoo species. For families, it is one of the easiest outdoor attractions in town.

The zoo is modest compared with major national collections, but its desert setting gives it a local character. Visit early or late in hot weather, and check current opening details before making a special trip.
