The Beersheba Turkish Railway Station is a well-preserved Ottoman building dating from 1915, sitting west of the Old City near the edge of the early town plan. The station was the terminus of a branch line constructed to supply Ottoman forces during World War I and played a direct role in the pivotal Battle of Beersheba in October 1917.

The building has been restored and now serves as a heritage site. The simple stone structure — arched windows, thick walls, a platform canopy — is an evocative piece of the First World War landscape, and the surrounding area still has a quiet, somewhat forsaken quality that suits its history. A small exhibition inside documents the station's wartime role.
