Beth Alpha Synagogue preserves the remains of a 6th-century synagogue at the foot of Mount Gilboa. Its main draw is the mosaic floor, with a zodiac wheel, biblical scenes, and inscriptions that give a vivid glimpse of Jewish village life in the Byzantine period.
