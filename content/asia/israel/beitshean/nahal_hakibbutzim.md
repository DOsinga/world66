Nahal HaKibbutzim is a spring-fed stream in the Beit Shean Valley where the water is deep enough for an easy float on a hot day. It is a simple, low-key stop: bring shoes that can get wet, let the current carry you through the reeds, and combine it with the nearby springs and archaeological sites.
