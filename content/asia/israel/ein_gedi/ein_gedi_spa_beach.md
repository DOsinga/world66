Ein Gedi Spa Beach is one of the Dead Sea stops where visitors come for the mineral-rich mud and the strange floating water as much as for the view. Treat it as a practical bathing stop near Ein Gedi rather than a conventional beach: shade, showers, and access matter more here than sand.
