Ein Gedi Spa Beach is a Dead Sea bathing stop south of the kibbutz, known for mineral-rich black mud and the strange, buoyant swim that draws many visitors to the shore. It is more about the experience than scenery: come prepared for intense sun, salty water, and basic facilities.
