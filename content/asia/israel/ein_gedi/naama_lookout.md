Na'ama Lookout is a viewpoint above Ein Gedi, reached from the area around the field school. The reward is a broad view over the oasis, the desert slopes, and the Dead Sea below.

It is a short stop rather than a full hike, but it gives useful orientation before or after visiting the Ein Gedi reserve, especially in the cooler parts of the day.
