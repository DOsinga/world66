Ein Gedi rewards early arrivals. The nature reserve is compact but the trails are steep and exposed, and by late morning the heat in the canyon can be brutal — especially in summer. Bring plenty of water, wear sun protection, and start walking by 8am if you can. The combination of the nature reserve, the Dead Sea shore, and the kibbutz botanical garden makes for a full day.
