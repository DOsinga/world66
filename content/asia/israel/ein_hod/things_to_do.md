---
title: Things to Do
type: section
---

Ein Hod is primarily a place to wander and absorb — its chief attraction is the village itself, with open studios, galleries, and sculpture tucked between the stone houses at every turn. The Janco Dada Museum and the Nisco Museum give structure to a visit, but don't be in a hurry: the best discoveries are made by following your nose down a side lane and finding yourself in someone's workshop.
