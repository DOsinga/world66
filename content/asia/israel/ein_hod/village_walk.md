The real pleasure of Ein Hod is simply walking through it. The village lanes wind up and down the hillside between stone houses, most of which double as artists' studios. Many residents keep their ground-floor workshops open to passing visitors, and it is entirely normal to walk in, look around, and have a conversation with the artist at work. Painters, sculptors, ceramicists, jewellers, and printmakers all have studios here, and the standard of work is genuinely high.

Sculptures appear at the side of the path, murals cover walls that would otherwise be blank, and small garden installations reward a closer look. The village has a pleasant open-air amphitheatre used for concerts and performances in summer. There are no must-see stops on this walk — the point is to be surprised.
