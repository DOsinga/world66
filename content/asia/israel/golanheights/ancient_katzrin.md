Ancient Katzrin preserves the remains of a Jewish village from the Talmudic period, with a reconstructed house, an ancient synagogue, and displays of domestic tools and agricultural life.

It is one of the Golan Heights' clearest stops for understanding everyday life in the region before the modern town of Katzrin grew nearby.
