Aniam Artists' Village is a small Golan Heights stop where visitors can walk between working studios, galleries, and craft shops. It is best treated as a relaxed art-and-scenery detour rather than a formal museum visit.

The surrounding valley adds to the appeal: a stream, a pool, and the seasonal Ayit waterfall are all within walking distance, with the waterfall at its best after winter rain.
