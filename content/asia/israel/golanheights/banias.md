Banias is one of northern Israel's richest nature stops: a green stream reserve with rushing water, short walking trails, old mills, archaeological remains, and one of the country's strongest waterfalls. The Hermon Stream rises here before joining the Jordan River system, so the landscape feels much lusher than the surrounding basalt plateau.

The main trail links the ancient Paneas ruins, named for the Greek god Pan, with rapids and the waterfall viewpoint. It is an easy half-day outing, especially good in winter and spring when the water is at its fullest.
