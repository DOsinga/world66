Gamla Nature Reserve combines a dramatic archaeological site with one of the Golan Heights' best nature walks. The ruined first-century Jewish town sits on a steep, camel-hump ridge, with remains of fortifications, houses, and an ancient synagogue tied to the revolt against Roman rule.

The reserve is also known for its cliffs, griffon vultures, and waterfall trail. Paths range from short viewpoints to steeper walks down toward the ancient town, so check closing times at the entrance before setting out.
