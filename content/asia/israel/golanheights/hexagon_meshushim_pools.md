The Hexagon Pools, also known as the Meshushim Pools, are a set of natural pools in the Meshushim stream. The attraction is the striking basalt formation around the water: volcanic rock has cooled into clean hexagonal columns that frame the pools.

The site lies within the Yehudiya Forest Nature Reserve and is best treated as a short nature outing rather than a roadside stop. Trails can be hot and exposed, so bring water and check access conditions before setting out.
