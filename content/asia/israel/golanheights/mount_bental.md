Mount Bental is one of the clearest viewpoints in the Golan Heights. From the old military position on the summit you can look west across the volcanic plateau and east toward Syria, with the border landscape laid out in a way that is hard to grasp from maps.

The bunkers and trenches give the mountain a rough, lived-in feel rather than a polished lookout. Go on a clear day if you can, and take the political edge of the place seriously; this is beautiful country, but it is not neutral scenery.
