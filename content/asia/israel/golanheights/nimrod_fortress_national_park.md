Nimrod Fortress crowns a steep ridge in the northern Golan Heights. The 13th-century stronghold was built by Muslim rulers against a feared Crusader attack, and its walls, towers, cisterns, and vaulted rooms still make a dramatic circuit.

The setting is part of the draw: clear days bring wide views over the surrounding hills and valleys.
