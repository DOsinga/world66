Tel Faher was a Syrian military post above the Hula Valley until it was captured by Israel in 1967. The site is now a memorial and battlefield stop, with bunkers, trenches, and wide views down over the fields below.

It is a sobering place rather than a polished attraction. The remaining fortifications make the geography of the Golan immediately clear, especially when combined with other military and historical sites on the plateau.
