Umm el Kanatir is a Byzantine-era Jewish town in the southern Golan Heights, best known for its reconstructed ancient synagogue. The site gives a rare ground-level look at Jewish village life in the region after the Roman period.

The synagogue restoration makes the ruins easier to read than many archaeological sites. It is a strong stop if you are combining Gamla, Golan viewpoints, and older religious sites in the area.
