Wadi Daliyot Reserve is a scenic valley and hiking area leading toward Gamla Nature Reserve. It gives a quieter look at the Golan Heights landscape, with open views, ravines, and a trail approach to the wider reserve area.

It is best for walkers who want a nature stop near Gamla rather than a built attraction. Bring water and check trail conditions before setting out.
