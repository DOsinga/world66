Yehudiya Forest Nature Reserve is one of the Golan Heights' best hiking areas, known for basalt canyons, waterfalls, and natural pools. Routes vary in difficulty, and some require comfort with rough ground, water crossings, and hot exposed sections.

It is worth planning as a proper outdoor day rather than a quick roadside stop. Bring water, sun protection, and footwear that can handle wet rock, and check current trail conditions before choosing a route.
