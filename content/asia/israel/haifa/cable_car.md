A short aerial tramway running between Bat Galim beach and the Stella Maris Monastery on the cliff above. The ride takes only a few minutes but the view over the bay and the Mediterranean from the upper terminus is one of the best in the city.

Useful as a quick way to get to Stella Maris without driving up the long switchbacks. Closed in poor weather.
