Hai-Bar Carmel Nature Reserve is a conservation reserve on Mount Carmel that breeds and protects animals once native to the region, with the long-term aim of returning some species to the wild. It is a good fit for families and for travellers who want a wildlife-focused stop near Haifa's mountain sights.

The reserve is more about conservation than spectacle. Check opening times before setting out, then combine it with other Carmel viewpoints, forest walks, or the university-area museums nearby.
