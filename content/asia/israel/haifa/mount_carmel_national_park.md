Mount Carmel National Park protects the wooded ridge south of Haifa, with walking trails, viewpoints, monastery sites, and Mediterranean forest that feels very different from the city's port and lower neighbourhoods. The park stretches across a broad area, so it works best with a car or a planned hike rather than as a quick city stroll.

Highlights include views from the Carmel ridge, quiet picnic spots, and trails through pine and oak woodland. It is one of the easiest ways to add a proper nature break to a Haifa visit.
