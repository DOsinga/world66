MadaTech, the National Museum of Science, is one of Haifa's strongest family attractions. It occupies a historic Technion building and fills it with hands-on exhibits covering physics, technology, space, energy, and everyday scientific puzzles.

It works well because Haifa is a practical, technical city as much as a scenic one. After the Bahá'í gardens and German Colony views, MadaTech gives children and curious adults a more interactive reason to spend time away from the waterfront.
