Nesher Park is a family-friendly forest park on the edge of Haifa, known for two suspended pedestrian bridges that cross a green ravine. The walk is short, but the bridges, pine-covered slopes, and views toward the Carmel foothills make it more memorable than a standard neighborhood park.

It is a useful nature stop if you have transport and want a half-day outside Haifa's denser urban sights. The paths are straightforward, though summer heat can make an early or late visit more pleasant.
