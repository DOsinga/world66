A 22-storey government building near the port, distinguished by its curved glass facade that gave it its nickname. Completed in 2002, it is one of the most striking pieces of contemporary architecture downtown.

Worth a glance in passing; the building is not open to the public, but its form anchors several of the views from the upper city.
