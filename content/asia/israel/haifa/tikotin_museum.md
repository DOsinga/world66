A small museum on the upper Carmel with a focused permanent collection of Japanese prints, ceramics, and lacquer, supplemented by changing thematic exhibitions. The displays are well presented and the building, set among gardens on the ridge, is itself a pleasure.

Worth combining with a walk along the Louis Promenade for the view down to the bay. Closed Sundays.
