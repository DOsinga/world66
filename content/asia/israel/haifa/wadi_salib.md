A largely abandoned 19th-century stone neighbourhood east of the Old City, depopulated during the 1948 war and the scene of major protests in 1959 by North African Jewish immigrants resettled there. Restoration work has been piecemeal and the area is mostly still ruined.

For an off-the-beaten-track walk through layered urban history it is unmatched, though not somewhere to linger at dusk.
