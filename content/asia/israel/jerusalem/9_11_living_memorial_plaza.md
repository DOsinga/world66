The 9/11 Living Memorial Plaza is a large outdoor memorial on a wooded hill at the western edge of Jerusalem. Its metal sculpture and stone plaques honour the victims of the attacks in the United States, giving the site a tone that is solemn but open to the landscape around it.

It is not on the usual Old City circuit, so it suits travellers with a specific interest in memorials or modern ties between Israel and the United States.
