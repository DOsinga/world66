Agnon House preserves the home and study of S.Y. Agnon, the Nobel Prize-winning Hebrew writer who wrote many of his best-known works here. The museum focuses on his life, library, writing room, and the Jerusalem world that shaped his fiction.

It is a small but meaningful stop for readers and anyone interested in modern Hebrew culture.
