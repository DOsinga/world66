The Armenian Bird Mosaic is a small but beautiful 6th-century floor mosaic just outside Jerusalem's Old City walls. Its birds, vines and geometric patterns were made by Armenian Christian artists, and the survival of the colours gives the place a quiet force that is easy to miss among Jerusalem's larger holy sites.

Visits usually need to be arranged through the Armenian Patriarchate, so this is not a casual walk-in stop. It is worth the effort if you are interested in early Christian art or the Armenian history of Jerusalem.
