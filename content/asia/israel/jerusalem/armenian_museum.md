The Armenian Museum sits near Saint James Cathedral in the Armenian Quarter and tells the religious, cultural, and secular history of Armenians in Jerusalem and beyond. Its collection is compact but well documented, making it approachable even for visitors who do not usually linger in small museums.

The building and courtyard are part of the appeal, with a calm colonnaded setting inside one of the Old City's least hurried quarters.
