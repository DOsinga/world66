Beit HaRav Kook preserves the Jerusalem home of Rabbi Abraham Isaac Kook, the first Ashkenazi chief rabbi of British Mandate Palestine and a major thinker in religious Zionism. Visits are modest in scale, with a short film and printed guide helping explain the rooms and Kook's influence.
