The Ben Hinnom Suspension Bridge crosses the Gehinnom Valley south-west of the Old City. At about 200 metres, it is one of Israel's longest pedestrian suspension bridges and gives open views toward Mount Zion, the valley slopes, and the stone neighbourhoods around Yemin Moshe.

It is a quick but memorable addition to a walk between the western side of the Old City and Jerusalem's newer neighbourhoods. Expect a little wobble, which is part of the point.
