Bible Lands Museum traces the cultures of the ancient Middle East in chronological order, with Egyptian, Minoan, Mesopotamian, Persian, Greek, Roman, and early Christian objects all set in context. It is a strong companion to the nearby Israel Museum, especially if you want archaeology with a clearer regional story.
