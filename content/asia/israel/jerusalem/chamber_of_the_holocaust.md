The Chamber of the Holocaust is a small Holocaust museum on Mount Zion, close to the Tomb of David and Dormition Abbey. Its collection includes artifacts, memorial plaques, anti-Semitic publications, and artwork connected to the history of Jewish communities destroyed in the Holocaust.

It has a different tone from Yad Vashem, with a more religious memorial setting and a tighter, more intimate scale. Combine it with other Mount Zion sights rather than treating it as a stand-alone half-day museum.
