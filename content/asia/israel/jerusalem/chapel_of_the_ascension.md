The Chapel of the Ascension is a small medieval shrine on the Mount of Olives, traditionally identified with the place where Christ ascended into heaven. It is sacred to Christians and Muslims, and now stands within a mosque compound.

The chapel's most unusual feature is a stone said to preserve the imprint of Christ's right foot. The site is modest compared with Jerusalem's major shrines, but its layered religious history makes it worth a short stop while visiting the Mount of Olives.
