The Friends of Zion Museum tells the stories of non-Jewish figures who supported Zionism, aided Jewish communities, or backed the creation of Israel. The presentation is modern and multimedia-heavy, with sound, film, and staged rooms doing much of the work.

It will appeal most to visitors interested in modern Jewish and Israeli history. For others, it is a niche museum rather than an essential Jerusalem stop.
