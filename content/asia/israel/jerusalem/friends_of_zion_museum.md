Friends of Zion Museum presents stories of non-Jewish figures who supported Zionism and Jewish communities. The visit is built around multimedia galleries rather than a traditional object-heavy museum layout.

It is a focused stop in central Jerusalem, best for visitors interested in modern Jewish history, Zionism, and religious-political links around the city.
