The Gush Katif Museum is a small museum about the former Jewish settlements in the Gaza Strip and their evacuation in 2005. Its presentation comes from a right-wing Israeli perspective, so it is best visited with that context in mind.

The museum is a niche stop, but it can be useful for visitors trying to understand how the disengagement from Gaza is remembered by some of the people directly affected by it.
