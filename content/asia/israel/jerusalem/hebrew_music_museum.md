The Hebrew Music Museum presents Jewish musical traditions from different communities and periods, using instruments, sound, and themed rooms to show how music moved with Jewish communities across the world.

It is a useful indoor stop in central Jerusalem, especially if you want a museum that is more focused and hands-on than the city's major history collections. Allow time to listen, not just look.
