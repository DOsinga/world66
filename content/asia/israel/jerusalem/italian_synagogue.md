The Italian Synagogue is the center of Jerusalem's Italian Jewish community, with a richly decorated wooden interior brought from Italy and rebuilt here. The adjoining museum displays Italian Judaica and community art, making it a worthwhile stop for visitors interested in Jewish heritage beyond the Old City.
