Jerusalem Biblical Zoo is one of the city's main family attractions, set in the southwest of Jerusalem with animal habitats, paths, and exhibits that work well for a half-day visit. Its original biblical-animal theme is less central than it once was, but it remains a useful break from the dense historic core.
