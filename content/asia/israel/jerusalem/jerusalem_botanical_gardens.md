Jerusalem Botanical Gardens collects plants from around the world, with outdoor sections arranged by region. Local species are especially interesting because the signs connect them to the area's history, religion, and climate.

The gardens are most pleasant outside the hottest part of summer, when shade and slow wandering matter more.
