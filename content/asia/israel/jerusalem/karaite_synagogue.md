The Karaite Synagogue represents a small Jewish community that follows the Hebrew Bible while rejecting the later rabbinic oral law. Visits are by arranged tour, and Hebrew is the usual tour language, though advance contact may make other arrangements possible.

It is a specialized but worthwhile Old City stop for travellers interested in Jerusalem's many religious communities beyond the best-known holy sites.
