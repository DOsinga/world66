The Last Supper Room, or Cenacle, is the upper room in the David's Tomb compound traditionally associated with the Last Supper. The room's present architecture is medieval rather than first-century, but the site remains an important stop for Christian pilgrims and visitors tracing Jerusalem's layered sacred geography.

It is on Mount Zion, very close to Dormition Abbey and the Old City walls, so it is easy to include on a short walking route.
