Mount Tzova is an archaeological hill in the Jerusalem Hills, with remains linked to the Crusader fortress of Belmont. The site is modest, but the combination of ruins, hillside paths, and views gives it more interest than a simple roadside stop.

It works best as part of a wider outing west of Jerusalem, especially if you are exploring the wooded hills and old villages around Ein Kerem and Tzova.
