The Museum of Islamic Art is housed in the Crusader-era refectory of the Knights Templar in Jerusalem's Old City. The collection focuses on Islamic architectural fragments and art, making it a useful stop for visitors already spending time around the Haram al-Sharif and Western Wall area.
