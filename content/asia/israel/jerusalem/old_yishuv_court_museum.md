The Old Yishuv Court Museum tells the story of Jewish community life in Jerusalem's Jewish Quarter from the 15th century through the 1948 War of Independence. Its rooms focus on domestic life, community institutions, and the pressures of living inside the Old City.

It is a useful stop for visitors who want more local context after seeing the Western Wall and the main Old City sights.
