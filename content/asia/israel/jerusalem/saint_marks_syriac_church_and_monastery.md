Saint Mark's Syriac Church and Monastery is a quiet Christian site in the Old City, traditionally associated with the house of Mary, mother of St Mark. Ring at the gate to enter; the resident monks hold a short weekday vespers service in Syriac, giving the visit a living religious context rather than just another ancient wall to photograph.
