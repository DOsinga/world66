Saint Mary's Hospice is a small set of 12th-century Crusader ruins in the Old City, close to the Temple Mount. The remains are modest, but they add another layer to the dense medieval history around the Muristan and Christian Quarter.

Treat it as a short stop while exploring the Old City rather than a destination on its own.
