Jerusalem's Tax Museum is a small, specialist museum about taxation and public life in Israel and historic Jewish communities. It is not a headline sight, but the subject is more revealing than it first sounds, touching law, society, and daily administration.

Most labels are in Hebrew. Ask about an English tour if you want more than a quick look at the displays.
