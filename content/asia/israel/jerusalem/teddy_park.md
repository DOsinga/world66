Teddy Park sits below Jaffa Gate and is named for Teddy Kollek, Jerusalem's long-serving mayor. Its main draw is a choreographed fountain with hundreds of water jets, making the park a pleasant pause on the edge of the Old City.

It is best visited when the fountain is running, or as a short break between the Old City and nearby western Jerusalem sights.
