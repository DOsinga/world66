The Temple Mount Sifting Project lets visitors help sift archaeological debris connected with major excavations and construction works in Jerusalem. The standard program is hands-on, with guidance on how to sort material and an explanation of finds at the end.

It is more of a participatory archaeology visit than a conventional museum. Check the current location and booking details before going, as the source material and program arrangements have changed over time.
