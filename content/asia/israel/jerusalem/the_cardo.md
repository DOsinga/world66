The Cardo is an excavated section of Jerusalem's main north-south street from the Byzantine period. A 200 m stretch beside the Jewish Quarter shows the broad roadway, shop-lined edges, and surviving columns that once ran through much of the Old City. Part of the site is now a covered shopping arcade, but the old paving and colonnade still make it one of the easiest places to picture Byzantine Jerusalem at street level.
