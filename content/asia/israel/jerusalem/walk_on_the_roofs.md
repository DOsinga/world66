title: A Walk on the Roofs
type: poi

This informal rooftop route above the Old City souk gives a different view of Jerusalem's dense historic core. Stairs near St Mark's Road, Khabad Street, and Muristan Road lead up to sections where you can look over the market lanes, the Church of the Holy Sepulchre, and the Dome of the Rock.
