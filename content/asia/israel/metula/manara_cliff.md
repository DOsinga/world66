Manara Cliff rises above the Hula Valley just south of Kiryat Shmona, with a cable car climbing to broad views over northern Israel and toward Lebanon. The height is the main attraction: on a clear day the valley floor, border hills, and surrounding farmland spread out below you.

The site also has paid activities such as rappelling, climbing walls, and a toboggan run, so it works well for families who want more than a scenic lookout. Prices are not cheap, but the view makes the ride worthwhile if you are already exploring the Finger of Galilee.
