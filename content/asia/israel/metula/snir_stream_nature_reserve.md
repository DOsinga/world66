Snir Stream Nature Reserve protects a lively tributary of the Jordan River, with flowing water, shaded paths, and chances to see wildlife along the stream. The reserve is one of the more appealing nature stops in the Finger of Galilee.

Wear shoes that can handle wet paths and check local conditions before setting out, as water levels and access can change with the season.
