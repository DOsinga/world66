title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Metula's appeal is mostly about its setting rather than specific attractions. The [Canada Centre](/asia/israel/metula/canada_centre) is the famous incongruity — an ice rink and sports complex in the heat of northern Israel — while the [Iyyon Nature Reserve](/asia/israel/metula/iyyon_nature_reserve) provides good walking. The [Fatima Gate](/asia/israel/metula/fatima_gate), the old border crossing into Lebanon, is visible from the edge of town and an interesting piece of recent history.

The [Tel Hai](/asia/israel/metula/tel_hai) site, about 8 km south, is the most historically significant stop in the area — a reconstructed settlement and memorial to one of the defining early moments of Zionist settlement.
