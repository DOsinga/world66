Zippori, also known as Sepphoris, is an archaeological site just northwest of Nazareth. It was once the region's largest city, and its main appeal today is the Roman and Byzantine mosaics, including some of the finest floor mosaics in northern Israel. It makes an easy half-day trip from Nazareth for travellers interested in early Galilee history.
