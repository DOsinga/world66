Zippori is an archaeological site near Nazareth, best known for its Roman and Byzantine mosaics. It was once one of the region's most important cities, and the remains include streets, public buildings, houses, and mosaic floors that show the wealth of the ancient settlement.

The site is a strong half-day trip from Nazareth for visitors interested in archaeology, Roman Galilee, or early Jewish and Christian history. Bring water and sun protection, as much of the visit is exposed.
