Ein Akev is a desert spring in the Zin basin, forming a small deep pool in an otherwise dry landscape. The oasis is the reward for a hike or a rough-road approach, and it is one of the more memorable natural stops near Sde Boker and Avdat.

Access is through a nature reserve and is limited to daytime. Come prepared for desert conditions, and expect the walk back to include a real climb out of the basin.
