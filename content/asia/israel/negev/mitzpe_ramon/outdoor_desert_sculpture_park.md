The Outdoor Desert Sculpture Park is a group of large sculptures set near the rim of Makhtesh Ramon. The pieces sit in open desert rather than a formal gallery, so the crater edge, sky, and empty ground are part of the experience.

It is a short detour from Mitzpe Ramon's central traffic circle and pairs naturally with the crater rim promenade or the visitors center.
