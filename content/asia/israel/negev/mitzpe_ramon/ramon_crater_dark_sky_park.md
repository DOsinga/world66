Ramon Crater is recognised as a Dark Sky Park, and that changes how you should plan a visit. Instead of treating the crater as a quick daytime viewpoint, stay after sunset and let the desert go dark.

On clear nights the stars are the main sight. Join a local astronomy outing if you want telescopes and explanation, or simply find a safe, permitted place away from headlights and give your eyes time to adjust.
