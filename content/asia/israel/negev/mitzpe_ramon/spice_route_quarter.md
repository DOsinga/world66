The Spice Route Quarter, also known locally through its old workshop and hangar spaces, is Mitzpe Ramon's small creative district. Former industrial buildings now hold galleries, studios, desert-tour operators, and casual places to eat or drink.

It will not compete with big-city art districts, but it gives the town texture after you have done the crater viewpoints. Come without a strict checklist and see what is open.
