Mount Ardon rises on the eastern side of Makhtesh Ramon and gives one of the best wild views in the Negev. From the top, the crater opens into a hard, colourful landscape of sandstone, ridges, and empty desert basins.

This is a real hike, not a roadside lookout. Start early, carry plenty of water, and treat the exposed desert seriously, especially outside the cooler months.
