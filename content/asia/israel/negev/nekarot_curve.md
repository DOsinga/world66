Nekarot Curve is a desert hike near Saharonim Spring, where the Nekarot stream cuts a narrow, dramatic bend through the rock on the floor of Makhtesh Ramon. The loop is about 6 km and follows marked trails and dirt roads, giving you a compact taste of the crater's dry, sculpted landscape.

Start early, carry plenty of water, and do not enter the canyon if rain is forecast anywhere upstream. Flash floods in the Negev are rare, fast, and unforgiving.
