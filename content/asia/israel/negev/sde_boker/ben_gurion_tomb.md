David and Paula Ben-Gurion are buried in a simple pair of graves on a bluff above the Zin Valley. The approach is landscaped but restrained, and the view does most of the work: pale cliffs, open desert, and the deep line of the wadi below.

Ibex often graze around the paths, especially early or late in the day. The site is free to enter and is one of the most quietly moving stops in the Negev.
