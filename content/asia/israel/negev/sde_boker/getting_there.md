---
title: "Getting There"
type: section
---

Sde Boker is on the main road through the central Negev, south of Be'er Sheva and north of Mitzpe Ramon. A car is the easiest way to connect the kibbutz, Midreshet Ben-Gurion, Ben-Gurion's tomb, and Ein Avdat in one day. Buses run through the area, but the heat and distances make careful timing important.
