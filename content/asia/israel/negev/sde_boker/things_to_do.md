Sde Boker's best stops are split between Ben-Gurion history and desert landscape. Visit [Ben-Gurion's hut](/asia/israel/negev/sde_boker/ben_gurions_hut), walk the terrace at [Ben-Gurion's tomb](/asia/israel/negev/sde_boker/ben_gurion_tomb), then continue to [Ein Avdat](/asia/israel/negev/sde_boker/ein_avdat) for the canyon hike.
