Shivta National Park protects the ruins of a Byzantine desert city, with churches, houses, streets, and water systems standing in a remote part of the Negev. The setting is part of the appeal: the remains sit in dry country where a city seems unlikely.

It is best visited with a car, as public transport is limited and the nearest bus stop is several kilometres away. Bring water and allow time to wander through the ruined streets and church remains.
