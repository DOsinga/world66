al-Bahr Sea Mosque is the oldest surviving mosque in Jaffa, standing close to the harbour since the 17th century. Its small scale is part of the appeal: it is still tied to the fishermen and sailors who have used it for generations, and it fits naturally into a wander around Old Jaffa.
