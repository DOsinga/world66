Bauhaus Center Tel Aviv is a compact gallery and bookshop devoted to the city's Bauhaus and International Style architecture. It is useful before or after a walk through the White City, with exhibitions, maps, and context that make the apartment blocks around Dizengoff feel less anonymous.

The centre also runs architecture walks, but the main value is orientation. Stop in early if you want to understand why central Tel Aviv looks the way it does.
