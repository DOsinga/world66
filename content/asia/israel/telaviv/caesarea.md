Caesarea is the Roman-and-Crusader coastal city about forty kilometres north of Tel Aviv, half an hour by direct train. The site has Herod the Great's harbour, a Roman amphitheatre still used for concerts, a Crusader fortress, and a long stretch of sandy beach.

The most spectacular single feature is the eight-kilometre Roman aqueduct that runs along the beach a short walk north of the main site. Half a day is enough; the museum, the amphitheatre, and the harbour can be combined. Take the train to Caesarea-Pardes Hanna and the local shuttle the last two kilometres.
