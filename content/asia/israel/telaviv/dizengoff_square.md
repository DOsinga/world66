Dizengoff Square is the architectural centrepiece of the White City — a perfect circular Bauhaus composition of curved buildings ringing a central plaza, named for Zina Dizengoff, wife of Tel Aviv's first mayor.

In the centre is the Fire and Water Fountain by the Israeli kinetic artist Yaacov Agam, an enormous coloured ring that rotates and shoots water and (once an hour during the day) fire. The square is now the standard meeting point in the centre of the city. Free.
