The Etzel 1948 Museum focuses on the Irgun, also known as Etzel, during the 1948 war and especially the fighting around northern Jaffa. Its seafront building marks the area covered by the museum's main story.

It is a specialized history stop, best paired with Neve Tzedek, HaTachana, and a walk toward Jaffa for geographic context.
