Gan HaGat — the Wine Press Garden — is a tiny urban park in central Tel Aviv built around a Byzantine-period wine press cut into the limestone bedrock. The press, around 1,500 years old, sits exposed in the centre of the park, with explanatory boards in Hebrew and English.

It is a small, easily missed spot, but a useful reminder that the apparently new Tel Aviv sits on much older ground.
