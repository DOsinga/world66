Givat HaAliyah Beach is one of the quieter beaches at the southern end of Tel Aviv-Yafo. It is removed from the busiest central beach strip and is a useful option if you want sand, sea, and a calmer local feel.

The beach works best as part of a visit to Jaffa or the southern waterfront, especially for travellers looking for a low-key swim or sunset stop.
