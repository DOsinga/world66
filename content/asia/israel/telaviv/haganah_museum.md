The Haganah Museum tells the story of the Jewish underground defence organisation that became central to Israel's military history. Its displays cover the British Mandate period, the road to independence, and the early years of the state.

Expect weapons, documents, photographs, and other memorabilia rather than a neutral art-museum experience. It is most useful for visitors interested in the political history behind modern Tel Aviv.
