The Ilana Goor Museum is one of the most personal art museums in Israel — a converted Ottoman caravanserai stuffed floor-to-ceiling with the artist's own work alongside her collected work by Picasso, Bacon, Henry Moore, Israeli moderns, and tribal art from West Africa to Papua. The arrangement is closer to a cabinet of curiosities than a contemporary museum.

The rooftop terrace is the bonus: full panoramic view over old Jaffa, the Mediterranean, and the Tel Aviv skyline. Open most days except Saturday morning; small entry fee.
