Joseph Bau House is a small museum devoted to Joseph Bau, an artist and Holocaust survivor whose story is connected with Schindler's List. The museum is run by his daughters, who present his art, humour, and wartime story in a very personal way.

This is not a large institution; it is closer to a guided encounter in a family-run memorial studio. Book or check opening arrangements before making a special trip.
