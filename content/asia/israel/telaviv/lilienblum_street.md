Lilienblum Street is the closest Tel Aviv has to a single bar street. It runs for about four hundred metres between Allenby and Herzl, and the section between Nachalat Binyamin and Allenby is a continuous strip of cocktail bars, wine bars, and small clubs.

The street is at its busiest from about midnight on Thursday and Friday nights. Earlier in the evening it is quieter, with most of the bars functioning as restaurants and serving food. Tel Aviv prices are not cheap; expect serious craft cocktails to cost on a par with London or New York.
