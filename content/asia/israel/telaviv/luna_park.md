Luna Park is Tel Aviv's main amusement park, beside Yarkon Park in the north of the city. The rides are modest by international theme-park standards, but the park is easy to reach and works well for families who want a few hours away from museums and beaches.

Expect child-friendly rides, carnival games, and a couple of roller coasters rather than a destination park worth crossing the country for. It is best treated as a light afternoon stop.
