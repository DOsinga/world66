Meymadion is a large water park near Yarkon Park and Luna Park in north Tel Aviv. It has a range of slides and pools, with options for both children and visitors looking for faster rides.

It is mainly a summer family stop. Lines can be long during Israeli school holidays, so it is best treated as a planned half-day rather than a quick detour.
