Neve Tzedek is the small, low-rise neighbourhood just east of the Tel Aviv beach, immediately north of Jaffa. It is the oldest Jewish neighbourhood in the city and is now its most picturesque — narrow streets, restored late-nineteenth-century houses, and a high concentration of boutique shops, galleries, and cafés.

The Suzanne Dellal Centre, the city's main contemporary dance venue, is at the heart of the neighbourhood. The HaTachana — the old Jaffa railway station — sits on the southern edge, restored as a small shopping and dining complex. Allow at least an hour to wander; longer if you plan to eat or drink.
