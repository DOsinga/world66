Pagoda House is one of Tel Aviv's stranger private houses, built in 1924 with a deliberate mix of Eastern and Western architectural cues. Muslim arches, Greek columns, and a pagoda-like roof all appear in one compact building.

You come for the exterior, not a tour. It is a quick architectural detour near central Tel Aviv, useful if you are tracing the city's early eclectic buildings before the cleaner lines of the White City took over.
