Rabin Square — Kikar Rabin — is the largest open square in central Tel Aviv, the standard venue for political rallies, public gatherings, and seasonal markets. The Tel Aviv City Hall sits on the eastern side, with a low concrete amphitheatre stepping down from it to the square.

The Rabin assassination memorial — sixteen dark basalt boulders marking the spot where the prime minister was shot — sits on the eastern edge of the square at the foot of the City Hall steps. The square is unmissable when walking the central streets and worth a quiet pause.
