Rokach House is an 1887 house in Neve Tzedek, built before Tel Aviv itself existed. The restored building tells the story of the neighbourhood and early Jewish settlement outside Jaffa.

It is a small historic-house visit, but it adds useful context to a walk through Neve Tzedek, especially for visitors interested in Tel Aviv's beginnings.
