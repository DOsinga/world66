Saint Archangel Michael Monastery is a Greek Orthodox site tucked into old Jaffa. The present church was rebuilt in the 1990s after a fire destroyed the older building, and it adds another quiet layer to the port's mix of Christian, Muslim, and Jewish landmarks.
