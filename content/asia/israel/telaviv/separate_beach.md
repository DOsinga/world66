Separate Beach is Tel Aviv's gender-segregated beach, used mainly by religious swimmers who want a more modest setting than the open city beaches. Men and women use it on different weekdays, while Saturday is open to everyone.

The beach is enclosed from the promenade, so it feels more private and less casual than nearby Hilton Beach. Check the current day schedule before making the trip, since the rules matter here more than at most Tel Aviv beaches.
