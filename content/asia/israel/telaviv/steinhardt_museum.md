The Steinhardt Museum opened in 2018 on the Tel Aviv University campus and is the largest natural history museum in the Levant. The collection covers the entire eastern Mediterranean region — preserved animal specimens, fossils, insects, and a particular focus on the bird migration routes that pass through Israel.

The permanent galleries are unusually well designed for a research-led museum: a giant illuminated wall map of bird migration, dioramas of the Mediterranean and Negev habitats, and a serious bone-and-skin study collection. About two hours; closed Saturdays.
