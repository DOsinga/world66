It is a single, quiet, weird thing tucked into the medieval stone of Jaffa.'

The Suspended Orange Tree hangs about three metres above a narrow stone alley in the old quarter, easy to miss if you are not looking for it. Walk west from Kedumim Square along the alleys near the artists' studios and the tree will be over your head. Free.

It is small and slightly strange in the way the best small public art is, and worth the five minutes' detour from the standard old Jaffa walk.
