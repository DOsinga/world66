Tel Aviv Cinematheque is the city's main film centre, with several screening halls and a programme that ranges from Israeli cinema to international retrospectives, festivals, and archive screenings. It is one of the better places in Tel Aviv to catch something beyond the standard multiplex schedule.

Check the current programme before going; many screenings are tied to festivals or one-off series. The central location makes it easy to combine with an evening around Habima Square or Rothschild Boulevard.
