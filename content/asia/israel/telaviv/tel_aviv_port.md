The Tel Aviv Port — Namal Tel Aviv — sits at the northern end of the city, where the Yarkon River empties into the sea. The old industrial warehouses have been converted into restaurants, design shops, gyms, and event spaces, and the curving wooden boardwalk runs the length of the harbour.

The port is busiest on Friday lunchtimes and Saturday afternoons, when families come for the seafood restaurants and the Shuk HaNamal indoor farmers' market. Live music and weekend markets fill the central square in the summer. Easy to reach by bus or scooter along the seafront.
