The White City is the UNESCO-designated zone roughly bounded by Allenby Street, Begin Road, the Yarkon River, and the sea. Within it sit several thousand buildings in the International Style, most three or four storeys, painted white, with rounded balconies, ribbon windows, and flat roofs adapted for the Mediterranean climate.

The Bauhaus Foundation runs walking tours from its small museum on Bialik Street on Friday mornings. The standard route covers Rothschild Boulevard, Dizengoff Square, and the surrounding streets. The Liebling Haus, a fully restored Bauhaus apartment block on Idelson Street, is the best single building to visit if you only have an hour.
