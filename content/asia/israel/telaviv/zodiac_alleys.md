The Zodiac Alleys are a network of restored lanes leading through Old Jaffa toward the port. The alleys are named for the signs of the zodiac and are lined with small galleries and studios.

They are best treated as part of a slow walk through Jaffa, linking the port, old stone lanes, and nearby viewpoints.
