The Church & Monastery of the Apostles is a Greek Orthodox complex on the Sea of Galilee, built on a site associated with earlier Byzantine monastic life. Visitors usually enter by ringing the bell; inside are quiet courtyards, chapels, icons, and lake views that make it a calm counterpoint to the region's busier pilgrimage stops.
