The Church & Monastery of the Apostles is a Greek Orthodox complex near the Sea of Galilee, built over the site of an older Byzantine monastery. The present buildings are mostly 19th-century restorations, but the setting, red domes, and layers of Christian history make it a worthwhile stop around Tiberias.
