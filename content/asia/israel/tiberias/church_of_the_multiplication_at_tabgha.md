The Church of the Multiplication at Tabgha marks the traditional site of the miracle of the loaves and fishes, on the northwestern shore of the Sea of Galilee. Its quiet courtyard and famous mosaic floor make it one of the more atmospheric Christian pilgrimage stops around Tiberias.
