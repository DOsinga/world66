Degania Alef, just south of the Sea of Galilee, is widely known as the first kibbutz. It is a useful stop for understanding the communal farming movement that shaped so much of early modern Israel.

The main visitor draw is Gordon House, a small regional museum covering nature, prehistory, and the story of the kibbutz. It works best as part of a wider day around the southern Sea of Galilee rather than as a long standalone visit.
