The Horns of Hittin are the twin-peaked volcanic hills where Saladin defeated the Crusader army in 1187, changing the course of the struggle for Jerusalem. The site is stark, but the landscape and history make it a worthwhile stop for visitors tracing Crusader-era Galilee.
