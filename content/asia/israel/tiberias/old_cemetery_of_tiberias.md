The Old Cemetery of Tiberias is one of the city's most important religious sites, with graves associated with major Jewish scholars and rabbis. Its position near the Sea of Galilee gives the cemetery a strong sense of place: stone tombs, pilgrimage, and lake light all close together.

Visitors should treat it as an active sacred site, not just a historical curiosity. For those interested in Jewish history, Tiberias's role as a centre of learning becomes much more tangible here than it does from the modern waterfront alone.
