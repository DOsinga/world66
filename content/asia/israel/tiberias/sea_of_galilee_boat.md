The Sea of Galilee Boat, often called the Jesus Boat, is a first-century fishing vessel displayed at Kibbutz Ginosar north of Tiberias. It is not connected to a named biblical figure, but its date and construction make it a rare survival from the world of the lake's ancient fishermen.

The museum is small and focused. It pairs well with Magdala or Mount Arbel if you are spending a half day north of Tiberias.
