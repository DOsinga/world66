St. Peter's Church stands close to the Tiberias waterfront, a small Catholic church tied to the Gospel stories of fishermen on the Sea of Galilee. Its boat-like interior is a deliberate nod to St. Peter and the lake.

It is not one of the grand pilgrimage churches of the region, but it makes sense as part of a slow walk along the shore. Step inside for the cool, quiet interior, then continue north along the promenade.
