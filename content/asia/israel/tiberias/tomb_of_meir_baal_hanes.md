The Tomb of Meir Baal Hanes is a Jewish pilgrimage site south of central Tiberias. The complex has Sephardic and Ashkenazic synagogues, and is especially busy around Pesah Sheni and Lag B'Omer, though pilgrims come to pray throughout the year.
