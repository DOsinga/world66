The Tomb of the Matriarchs is a traditional Jewish burial site on the slopes above Tiberias. It is associated with several biblical women, including Bilhah and Zilpah, and is visited by pilgrims who come for prayer rather than spectacle.

The setting is quiet and residential, with views back toward the lake. Dress modestly, be respectful of worshippers, and do not expect a museum-style presentation.
