Chubu spans the wide waist of Honshu, from the Sea of Japan coast to the Pacific, and rises to the peaks of the Japan Alps. It takes in the industrial city of [Nagoya](/asia/japan/chubu/nagoya), the beautifully preserved samurai and geisha districts of [Kanazawa](/asia/japan/chubu/kanazawacity), the old merchant town of [Takayama](/asia/japan/chubu/takayama), the thatched farmhouses of [Shirakawa-go](/asia/japan/chubu/shirakawa_go), and the northern approaches to Mount Fuji.
