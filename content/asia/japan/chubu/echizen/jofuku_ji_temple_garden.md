Jofuku-ji Temple Garden is a quiet Japanese garden of rocks, sand, moss, and seasonal planting, with views toward Mount Hino. The temple is linked to Taira no Yorimori, and the garden is the draw: moss forms wave-like patterns around a holly mound, while camellias, azaleas, maples, and white holly flowers mark the seasons.
