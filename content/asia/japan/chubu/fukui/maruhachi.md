Maruhachi is a straightforward local place for Fukui staples, especially oroshi soba and sauce katsudon. It is the sort of restaurant to use when you want regional food without turning lunch into a project.

Oroshi soba, served with grated daikon, is a good contrast to the heavier pork cutlet bowl.
