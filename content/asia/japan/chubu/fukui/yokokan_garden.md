Yokokan Garden is Fukui's most graceful central sight, a small Edo-period strolling garden built around a pond and a restored villa. It is not large, but it has the calm, composed feeling that makes a garden worth slowing down for.

Come here after the castle ruins or the history museum. The three stops together make the best short walk in central Fukui.
