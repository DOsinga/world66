Tenjin No Yu is a public bath in Hakuba known for mineral waters and mountain views. Both indoor and outdoor baths are available, and lockers are provided.

It is a useful low-key stop after skiing, hiking, or mountain travel, especially for visitors who want a local onsen-style soak without making a separate trip out of the valley.
