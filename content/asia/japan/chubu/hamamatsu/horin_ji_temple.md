Hōrin-ji Temple was established in 1667 by an invited Chinese Zen master. Its buildings are valued for late Ming dynasty influence, which gives the temple a different feel from many Japanese Zen temples of the same period. It is a quiet, specialist stop, best for visitors already exploring Hamamatsu's temples and historic sites.
