Kamoe Kannon is a bright orange-and-white temple near central Hamamatsu. Its modern look makes it stand apart from older wooden temples, and it is an active place of worship rather than a museum piece.

It is most lively around New Year and other holidays, when local visitors come in formal dress. At quieter times, it is a short detour that pairs easily with Hamamatsu Castle.
