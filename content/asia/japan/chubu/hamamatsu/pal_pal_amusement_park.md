Pal-Pal Amusement Park is a family amusement park in Kanzanji, on the shore of Lake Hamana. The rides include roller coasters, a long raft ride, and a cable car over the lake, so it is most useful if you are travelling with children or want an easy day outside central Hamamatsu. Combine it with the lakeside hot spring area if you are staying nearby.
