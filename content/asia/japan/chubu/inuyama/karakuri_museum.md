The Karakuri Museum sits close to Inuyama Castle and explains the mechanical puppets used in Japanese festival floats and theatre. Displays show how the figures are built and operated, and visitors can try moving a puppet themselves.

It is a modest museum, but a good add-on if you are already visiting the castle and Uraku-en. The hands-on element makes it more memorable than a simple display case stop.
