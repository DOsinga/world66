---
title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Inuyama's attractions cluster in a small area around the castle hill and along the Kiso River, making it easy to see the main sights on foot in a single day. The castle, the Jo-an teahouse in Uraku-en garden, and the Honmachi old townscape are all within ten minutes of each other. Meiji Mura and Little World sit a few kilometres to the east and require separate trips, but both reward the extra travel.
