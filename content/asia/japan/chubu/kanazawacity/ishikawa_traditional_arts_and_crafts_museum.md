The Ishikawa Prefectural Museum of Traditional Arts and Crafts introduces the craft traditions associated with Kanazawa and the wider prefecture. Displays cover dozens of local arts and crafts, making it a useful companion to Kenroku-en, the samurai districts, and Kanazawa's gold-leaf shops.
