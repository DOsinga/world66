Heisenji Hakusan Shrine is the atmospheric counterweight to Katsuyama's dinosaur fame. The old complex is linked to Hakusan worship and has stone paths, tall trees, mossy ground, and the remains of a once powerful religious centre.

It is quiet in a way that rewards patience. Come for a slow walk rather than a quick photo stop.
