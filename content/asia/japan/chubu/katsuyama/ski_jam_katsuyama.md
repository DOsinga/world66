Ski Jam Katsuyama is the area's main winter-sports draw, with ski and snowboard terrain that attracts visitors from Fukui, Kyoto, Osaka, and the wider Hokuriku region. It can be busy on weekends, but it gives Katsuyama a strong winter reason to visit.

Check current lift operations and transport before committing, especially outside the core snow season.
