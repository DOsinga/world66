Uchibuse River French Waterfall is a tiered waterfall area in a scenic park outside central Matsumoto. The falls can be reached quickly from the parking area, while longer paths continue through the wooded valley for visitors who want more of a walk.

The park also has picnic areas, so it works best as a quiet nature break rather than a headline Matsumoto sight.
