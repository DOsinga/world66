Kana-dorii is a large torii gate in Fujiyoshida, set on the old approach used by pilgrims heading toward Mount Fuji. The gate is marked with the characters for Fujisan, and on clear days the mountain rises directly beyond it.

It is a quick stop rather than a full attraction, but the combination of shrine approach, town street, and Fuji view makes it one of the more memorable small sights around Fujiyoshida.
