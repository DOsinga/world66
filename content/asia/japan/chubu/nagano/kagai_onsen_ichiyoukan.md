Kagai Onsen Ichiyoukan is an unusual public bath near Matsushiro, known for long, narrow indoor baths filled with brown mineral water. There are separate indoor baths for men and women, plus mixed outdoor baths, so bring a large towel and check the bathing arrangements before you go.
