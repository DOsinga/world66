Aichi Earth Expo Memorial Park is the large green site east of Nagoya where the 2005 Expo has turned into one of the region's biggest day-trip parks. Its best-known draw is Ghibli Park, with areas based on Studio Ghibli films and the recreated house from *My Neighbor Totoro*.
