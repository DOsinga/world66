Shiroyama Hakusan Shrine stands on the former site of Suemori Castle, giving this quiet neighbourhood shrine a deeper history than its modest scale suggests. Local festivals here feature traditional music and dance.

It is a worthwhile short stop if you are already in eastern Nagoya, especially combined with Nittaiji Temple or Higashiyama Zoo and Botanical Gardens.
