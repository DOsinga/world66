The Showa Era and Toy Museum is a small nostalgia museum with toys, household objects, and everyday items from early modern and mid-20th-century Japan. The collection is strongest on Showa-era daily life, making it a useful rainy-day stop around Wakura Onsen and Nanao.

It works best for families or visitors interested in retro Japanese design and childhood culture.
