---
title: Things to Do
type: section
---

Start in the old port area, where [Perry Road](/asia/japan/chubu/shimoda/perry_road) links cafes, stone storehouses, and [Ryosenji Temple](/asia/japan/chubu/shimoda/ryosenji_temple). It is a short walk, but it gives Shimoda its strongest identity: a small Japanese harbour town caught at a turning point in world history.

For views, take the [Mount Nesugata Ropeway](/asia/japan/chubu/shimoda/mount_nesugata_ropeway) above the station and look back over the bay. In warm weather, leave time for [Shirahama Ohama Beach](/asia/japan/chubu/shimoda/shirahama_ohama_beach), the best reason to treat Shimoda as more than a history stop.
