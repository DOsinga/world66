Shizuoka City Toro Museum interprets the nearby Toro archaeological site, a recreated Yayoi-period village with dwellings, rice fields, and exhibits on daily life. The museum is useful for understanding one of Japan's best-known prehistoric settlement sites.

It is a good stop for families and anyone interested in early Japanese history.
