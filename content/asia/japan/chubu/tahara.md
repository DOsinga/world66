Tahara takes up the Atsumi Peninsula, the long southern arm of Aichi that divides Mikawa Bay from the Pacific before ending at Cape Irago. It is technically a city, but the reason to come is the coast: white lighthouse, wind-cut rocks, surf beaches, flower fields, and wide water on three sides.

The most satisfying stretch is around Cape Irago. [Irago Misaki Lighthouse](/asia/japan/chubu/tahara/irago_misaki_lighthouse) stands at the tip of the peninsula, with [Koijigahama Beach](/asia/japan/chubu/tahara/koijigahama_beach) curving away beside it and the sea-carved [Hii Stone Gates](/asia/japan/chubu/tahara/hii_stone_gates) just along the coast. In winter and early spring, [Irago Nanohana Garden](/asia/japan/chubu/tahara/irago_nanohana_garden) turns the fields yellow with rape blossoms.

Tahara is not a polished old town like Kyoto or Kanazawa. The town centre has [Tahara Castle Ruins](/asia/japan/chubu/tahara/tahara_castle_ruins) and a small museum, while [Mount Zao Observatory](/asia/japan/chubu/tahara/mount_zao_observatory) gives the best inland view over the city, farms, bay, and factories. It works best as a quiet coastal detour from Toyohashi or Nagoya, especially if you have a car or are using the ferry across Ise Bay.
