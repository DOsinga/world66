Mount Zao Observatory is the best place to get Tahara's geography in one look. From the platform you can see the city below, Mikawa Bay to the north, the Pacific side of the peninsula, and the industrial edges that remind you this is still working Aichi.

It is especially good around sunset or after dark, when the factories and town lights give the view more depth. Without a car, check local bus options carefully before you count on it.
