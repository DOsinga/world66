The Karakuri Museum focuses on the mechanical dolls associated with Takayama's festival floats. It also displays a large collection of lion dance masks from across Japan, making it a compact stop for visitors interested in festival craft.

It fits naturally with Sakurayama Hachiman-gu and the nearby Yatai Kaikan float exhibition.
