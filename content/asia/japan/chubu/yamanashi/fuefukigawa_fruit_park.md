Fuefukigawa Fruit Park is a broad hillside park built around Yamanashi's fruit culture. It has glass-domed facilities, orchards, play areas, shops, restaurants, and paths that climb to open views over the Kōfu Basin.

The view is the real reason to go. Late afternoon into evening is best, when the basin lights come on below and the park's domes start to glow.
