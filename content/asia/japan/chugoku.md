The Chugoku region occupies the western tail of Honshu, split by mountains into a sunny Inland Sea coast and a quieter Sea of Japan side. Its best-known city is [Hiroshima](/asia/japan/chugoku/hiroshima), with its Peace Memorial and the floating torii of nearby Miyajima. Elsewhere are the great sand dunes of [Tottori](/asia/japan/chugoku/tottori), the ancient grand shrine of [Izumo](/asia/japan/chugoku/izumo), and the samurai town of [Tsuwano](/asia/japan/chugoku/tsuwano).
