Amazu Shrine is closely tied to Bizen's pottery culture. The approach is lined with Bizen-yaki tiles and zodiac figures, and even the ema prayer plaques are made from local pottery.

It is a small shrine, but the ceramic details make it more distinctive than a routine neighborhood stop.
