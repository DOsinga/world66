Hagi Christian Martyrs Memorial Park preserves the graves of Christians exiled from Nagasaki to Hagi in 1868. A memorial in the park marks the persecution and exile of these believers during the early Meiji period.

It is a quiet, reflective stop rather than a large attraction, but it adds a different layer to Hagi's better-known samurai, castle town, and industrial history sights.
