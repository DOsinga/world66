Hagi Museum is a compact local history museum near Hagi Castle Town. Exhibits cover the city's samurai families, castle-town culture, and figures such as Yoshida Shoin and Takasugi Shinsaku, who helped shape late Edo and early Meiji history.

It is a good first stop before walking the old streets, since the displays help explain why Hagi matters beyond its preserved townscape.
