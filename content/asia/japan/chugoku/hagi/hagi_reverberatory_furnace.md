The Hagi Reverberatory Furnace is not visually grand, but it is historically sharp. Its twin stone chimneys are the remains of an 1850s Choshu experiment in Western-style iron technology, part of the hurried military learning that preceded the Meiji Restoration.

Come here if the industrial-revolution story interests you. Otherwise, it works as a short stop between the Shoin Shrine area and the coast.
