Kasayama is a small active volcano on Hagi's coast, known for broad views over the Sea of Japan. The scale is gentle rather than dramatic, which makes it a good short nature stop from town.

Look for the camellias in February and March, when the slopes add colour to the sea views. It pairs well with Kikugahama Beach and the castle ruins if you want a day that mixes coast, history, and scenery.
