Kikuya House preserves the home of a wealthy Hagi merchant family, with tatami rooms, storehouses, and a small garden that show how the town's merchant elite lived. The displays include thousands of family objects, from dolls and household goods to unexpected modern curios, making it a good stop while walking the old castle town.
