Yamanaka Residence preserves the house and working spaces of a local seafood merchant family. Displays show tools and everyday objects from the business, giving a modest but concrete look at Hagi's coastal trade.

It works best as a short stop alongside the castle town and other preserved houses, rather than as a standalone destination.
