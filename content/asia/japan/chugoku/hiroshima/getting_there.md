---
title: "Getting There"
type: section
---

**Shinkansen** is the obvious choice from eastern Japan. Hiroshima station is on the JR San'yō Shinkansen; the fastest *Nozomi* trains from Shin-Osaka take about 85 minutes (¥9,750), from Tokyo around 4 hours (¥18,380). The *Hikari* and *Kodama* services take longer but are covered by the Japan Rail Pass. From Kyoto, plan on about two hours.

**By air**, Hiroshima Airport is 50km east of the city; limousine buses connect to the city centre in about 45 minutes (¥1,470). Budget carriers including Peach and Jetstar operate domestic routes, but for most visitors the shinkansen is faster door-to-door.

If you are travelling on a JR Pass and want to visit Miyajima, note that the ferry from Miyajimaguchi to Miyajima Island is operated by JR and is free with a valid pass.
