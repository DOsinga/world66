Hiroshima City Manga Library is a specialist collection near the Museum of Contemporary Art, with shelves of Japanese manga and a smaller selection of Western comics. It is most useful for manga fans, readers with some Japanese, or anyone looking for a quieter cultural stop around Hijiyama Park.

The appeal is the breadth of the collection rather than a conventional museum display, so allow time to browse.
