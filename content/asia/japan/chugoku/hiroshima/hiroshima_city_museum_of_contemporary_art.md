Hiroshima City Museum of Contemporary Art occupies a hilltop site in Hijiyama Park and focuses on postwar and contemporary art from Japan and abroad. Its setting gives a quieter cultural perspective away from the Peace Memorial Park axis.

The museum is worth pairing with a walk through the park, especially for visitors who want Hiroshima's cultural life beyond memorial history. Temporary exhibitions shape the experience, so check what is on.
