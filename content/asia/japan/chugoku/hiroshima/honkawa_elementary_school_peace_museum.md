Honkawa Elementary School Peace Museum preserves part of a school building that stood close to the hypocenter of the atomic bombing. Its small collection of photographs and objects is quieter than the main Peace Memorial Museum, but the setting makes the loss of civilian life in Hiroshima painfully specific.
