Inasa Beach is not just a strip of sand near Izumo Taisha; it is part of the shrine landscape, tied to myths of gods arriving on the coast. The small Bentenjima rock just offshore gives the beach a clear focal point, especially at sunset.

Come after visiting the shrine if the weather is kind. The beach is simple, but the combination of sea, mythology, and low evening light makes it one of Izumo's most memorable quiet stops.
