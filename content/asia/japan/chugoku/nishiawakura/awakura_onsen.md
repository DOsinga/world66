Awakura Onsen gives the village its easiest low-key pleasure: a hot soak after a forest walk. The baths are simple rather than grand, but the setting near the river and trees fits Nishiawakura's quiet, woody character.

It works best as a practical finish to the day. If you are travelling by train, it is one of the easier stops to reach on foot from Awakura-Onsen Station.
