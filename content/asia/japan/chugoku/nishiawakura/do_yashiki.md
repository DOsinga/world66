Do Yashiki is a preserved Edo-period house in Nishiawakura, built in the 18th century with a traditional thatched roof. Inside are local artifacts and documents that give the building more context than a simple exterior stop.

It is a small attraction, but a useful one if you want to understand the village's older domestic architecture and rural history.
