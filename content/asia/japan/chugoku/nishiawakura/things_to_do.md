title: Things to Do
type: section
---

Nishiawakura is best treated as a forest day rather than a town-sightseeing day. Start with [Wakasugi Primeval Forest](#wakasugi_primeval_forest) if the trails are open, then leave time for [Awakura Onsen](#awakura_onsen) and one smaller stop such as [Ogaya Shibazakura Park](#ogaya_shibazakura_park) in flower season or [Awakura Shrine](#awakura_shrine) for the old cypress.
