The Ancient Kibi Cultural Properties Center is a small archaeology museum focused on the ancient Kibi Kingdom. Its collection is strongest on clay figurines, burial objects, and other finds from the historic Kibi plain west of central Okayama.

Most interpretation is in Japanese, but the artifacts are clear enough to make the stop useful if you are already visiting Kibitsu Shrine, Kibitsuhiko Shrine, or the nearby burial mounds.
