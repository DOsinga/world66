Animo Museum is a small museum dedicated to Okayama-born marathon runner Yuko Arimori, who won Olympic silver in Barcelona and bronze in Atlanta. Exhibits include her medals and other Olympic memorabilia.

It is a niche stop, but worthwhile for visitors interested in Japanese athletics or Olympic history.
