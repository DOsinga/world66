Bitchu Kokubunji is the surviving provincial temple of the old Bitchu region, best known for its five-storey pagoda built in 1844. It sits along the Kibiji cycling route, making it an easy and worthwhile stop between Okayama's ancient burial mounds and rural temple scenery.
