Kibitsuhiko Shrine stands in the old Kibi plain west of central Okayama, an area dense with legends,
kofun tombs, and early regional history. The shrine is associated with Kibitsuhiko-no-mikoto, the
prince linked to the Momotaro story cycle that Okayama has made part of its identity.

The grounds are quieter than many urban shrines, with a long approach and a sense of space that
rewards combining it with nearby Kibitsu Shrine or cycling through the Kibi Plain. It is a good way
to see Okayama's pre-castle, pre-modern roots outside the city centre.
