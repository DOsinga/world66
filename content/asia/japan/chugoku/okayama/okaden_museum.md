Okaden Museum & Mitooka Eiji Design is a small, cheerful stop for anyone who likes trams, trains, or Japanese transport design. It highlights Okayama's streetcar culture and the work of Eiji Mitooka, whose colourful railway interiors and graphics have become attractions in their own right.

This is a niche museum, not a substitute for Korakuen or Okayama Castle. It works best with children, rail fans, or travellers who enjoy the everyday infrastructure that gives Japanese cities so much of their character.
