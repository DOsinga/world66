Sogen-ji is a Zen temple in Okayama that offers a quieter counterpoint to the city's headline pairing of Korakuen Garden and Okayama Castle. Its grounds, halls, and training atmosphere make it feel more contemplative than decorative.

It is best for travellers who like temples as lived-in places rather than photo backdrops. Be respectful of any practice areas, and use the visit to slow the pace after the busier sights around the castle district.
