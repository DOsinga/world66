Shinonsen is a scattered town on the Sea of Japan side of Hyogo, stitched together from hot-spring villages, small ports, mountains, and river valleys. The name sounds like a tourist slogan, but the onsen history is real: Yumura has been a hot-spring settlement since the ninth century, and its boiling source still sits in the middle of town.

[Yumura Onsen](/asia/japan/chugoku/shinonsen/yumura_onsen) is the main reason to come. Steam rises from the Arayu spring beside the river, visitors cook eggs in the hot water, and the lanes around it keep the slow rhythm of an old onsen town. Nearby [Yumechiyokan Museum](/asia/japan/chugoku/shinonsen/yumechiyokan_museum) explains the television drama that made Yumura known across Japan in the 1980s.

Shinonsen also has a coastal side around Hamasaka, with old houses, fishing culture, and access to the San'in Kaigan landscape. It is a slower choice than Kinosaki Onsen, but that is the point: you come for hot water, sea air, and a town that still feels local.
