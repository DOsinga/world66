Antai-ji is a Soto Zen monastery deep in the mountains east of Shinonsen. It is known less as a sightseeing temple than as a working practice place, where residents combine meditation, farm work, and a strict daily rhythm.

Casual visitors should treat it with care and check current access before going. For travellers seriously interested in Zen practice, it is one of the town's most distinctive anchors.
