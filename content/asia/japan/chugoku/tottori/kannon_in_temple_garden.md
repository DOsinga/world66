Kannon-in is the calm counterpoint to Tottori's dunes and castle hill. The temple garden is built around a pond, with carefully placed stones, trees, and seasonal colour.

It is not a spectacle. That is the point. Come when you want a quiet hour and a more intimate piece of Tottori's old town.
