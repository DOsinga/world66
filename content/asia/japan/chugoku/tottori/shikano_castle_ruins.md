Shikano Castle Ruins preserve the moats and stonework of a former castle site in western Tottori. No original castle buildings survive, but the grounds still show the outline of the old fortification.

The ruins are most appealing in cherry blossom season, when hundreds of trees bloom around the former castle grounds.
