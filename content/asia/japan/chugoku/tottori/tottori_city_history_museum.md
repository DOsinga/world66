Tottori City History Museum uses multimedia exhibits and digital displays to explain the city's local history and culture. It is a useful indoor stop before or after visiting the castle ruins and Kannon-in Temple Garden.

The museum is about 15 minutes from JR Tottori Station by the red bus.
