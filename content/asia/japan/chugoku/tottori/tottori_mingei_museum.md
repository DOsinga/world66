Tottori Mingei Museum of Folkcrafts is a small two-story museum with pottery, glass, textiles, woodwork, and bamboo work. Ushinotoyaki pottery is the highlight of the collection.

Outside the museum, a small hexagonal memorial space for deceased orphans adds an unusual Christian-Buddhist layer, with stained glass giving it a distinctive atmosphere.
