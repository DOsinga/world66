Hori Gardens are the former estate of the Hori family, a little outside central Tsuwano. The grounds combine a main residence, guest house, and a carefully kept garden, giving a quieter view of local family wealth and taste than the town's better-known castle and shrine sights.

Come when you have time to range beyond Tonomachi Street. The appeal is the calm setting and the mix of domestic architecture and garden design, not a quick photo stop.
