Tsuwano Japan Heritage Center is a small museum and information stop that introduces the town's history and landscape. Its main exhibit, "100 views of Tsuwano Then and Now", uses old drawings and modern views to show how the townscape has changed.

Start here if you want context before walking Tonomachi Street, the castle ruins, and Tsuwano's temples and shrines. It is a focused stop, not a full museum afternoon.
