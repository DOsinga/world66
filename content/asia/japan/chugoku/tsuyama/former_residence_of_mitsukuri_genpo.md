The former residence of Mitsukuri Genpo stands on Joto Street, close to Tsuyama's cluster of sites connected with Western learning. Mitsukuri was an important scholar and translator during the period when Japan was first dealing seriously with Western powers.

It is a small stop, but a useful one if you are tracing Tsuyama's intellectual history. Pair it with the Tsuyama Archives of Western Learning nearby, where the context is broader and easier to follow.
