Tsuyama Folk Museum covers the old Mimasaka region through everyday objects, local history, and displays tied to Tsuyama Castle and the town that grew around it. It is a modest museum, but useful for understanding why Tsuyama mattered before it became a quiet inland city.

Visit before or after Kakuzan Park. The castle-site context makes the museum's models and historical material much easier to place.
