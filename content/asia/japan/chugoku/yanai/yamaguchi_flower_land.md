Yamaguchi Flower Land is a gentle add-on rather than a core reason to visit Yanai. The park has themed flower beds, seasonal displays, and family-friendly open space in the hills northwest of town.

Go if the season is right or if you are travelling with children. Otherwise, keep your time for the old merchant district.
