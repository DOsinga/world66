Bentenjima Kaihin Park sits on the edge of Lake Hamana, with a beach, boardwalk, and a view to the red torii standing out in the bay. It is at its liveliest around the early-summer fireworks, but the waterfront setting works as an easy break from central Hamamatsu at any time.
