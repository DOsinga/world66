Hamamatsu City Museum covers the history of Hamamatsu and the surrounding region, with prehistoric fossils, pottery, old maps, and everyday objects from farming and domestic life.

The museum is a useful stop if you want local context beyond Hamamatsu Castle and the musical-instrument museum. Its setting in a quiet park makes it easy to pair with a slower walk through this side of the city.
