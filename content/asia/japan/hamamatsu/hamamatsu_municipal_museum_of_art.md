Hamamatsu Municipal Museum of Art is a compact art museum near Hamamatsu Castle. The programme changes through the year, so the value of a visit depends on the current exhibition rather than a single permanent highlight.

It works best as an easy add-on after the castle or as a rainy-day stop in central Hamamatsu. Check the exhibition schedule before making a special trip.
