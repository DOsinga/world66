Lake Hamana Music Box Museum displays self-playing organs, music boxes, and related instruments from around the world. Short performances are usually held on the hour, and there is a hands-on exhibit for visitors who want more than display cases.

The museum is also worth a look for its setting, with broad views over Lake Hamana from outside.
