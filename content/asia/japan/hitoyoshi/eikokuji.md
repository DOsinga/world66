Eikokuji is better known as the Ghost Temple. Its famous hanging scroll shows a grim ghost, tied to a local story about an apparition at the pond behind the temple.

The temple also has a more grounded historical note: Saigo Takamori used it as a main camp during the Seinan War. Between the story, the pond, and the war connection, it is one of Hitoyoshi's most memorable small sights.
