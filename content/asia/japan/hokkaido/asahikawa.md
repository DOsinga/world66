Asahikawa is [Hokkaido](/asia/japan/hokkaido)'s second city, set inland where several rivers meet below the Daisetsuzan mountains. It is colder, quieter, and less polished than [Sapporo](/asia/japan/hokkaido/sapporo), but it has enough of its own character to be more than a transfer stop.

The headline is [Asahiyama Zoo](/asia/japan/hokkaido/asahikawa/asahiyama_zoo), one of Japan's best-known zoos, built around close-up views of animals in motion. In winter the city also puts on the [Asahikawa Winter Festival](/asia/japan/hokkaido/asahikawa/asahikawa_winter_festival), with huge snow sculptures, ice carving, and a local feel that makes a good contrast to Sapporo's busier festival.

Away from those set pieces, Asahikawa works as a practical central Hokkaido base. The river embankments are good for cycling in summer, [Otokoyama Sake Brewing Museum](/asia/japan/hokkaido/asahikawa/otokoyama_sake_brewing_museum) gives the city a serious sake stop, and trains link it to Biei, Furano, and the Daisetsuzan side of the island.
