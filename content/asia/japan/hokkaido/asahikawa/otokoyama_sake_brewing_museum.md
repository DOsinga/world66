Otokoyama is Asahikawa's best cultural stop for anyone interested in food and drink. The brewery traces its name deep into the Edo period, and the museum displays old tools, scrolls, ukiyo-e prints, and the practical steps behind sake brewing.

The visit is short but worthwhile, especially because tasting and buying are part of the experience. It sits north of the centre and is easiest by bus or taxi.
