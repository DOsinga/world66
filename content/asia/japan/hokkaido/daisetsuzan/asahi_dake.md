Asahi-dake is Hokkaido's highest mountain, rising to 2,290 metres in Daisetsuzan National Park. The ropeway makes it unusually accessible: from the upper station, a short loop crosses a volcanic plateau of ponds, fumaroles, and big mountain views.

The summit hike is a longer, steeper outing from the ropeway, usually taking several hours round trip. Weather changes quickly, so treat it as a real alpine walk even though the first section is easy.
