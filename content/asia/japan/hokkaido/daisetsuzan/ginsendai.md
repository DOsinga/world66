Ginsendai is a popular Daisetsuzan trailhead and autumn-colour viewpoint. The route toward Aka-dake climbs through mountain scenery that turns vivid red and gold in early autumn, making it one of the area's classic seasonal hikes.

The trail is best treated as a mountain outing, with weather, transport, and timing checked before setting out.
