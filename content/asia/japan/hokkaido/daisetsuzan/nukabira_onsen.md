Nukabira Onsen is a small hot spring settlement on the eastern side of the Daisetsuzan area. Its appeal is simple: forested valley scenery, mountain air, and hot baths that feel far removed from Hokkaido's larger resort towns.

The outdoor baths are the reason to come, especially those facing the valley. Check current day-use bathing hours before setting out, since many baths are attached to inns and access can change.
