Hakodate Tropical Botanical Garden is a greenhouse garden in the Yunokawa area, with tropical plants, cacti, banana palms, hibiscus, and coffee plants. It is also known for its monkeys, which draw visitors in winter when they soak in a hot spring bath.

The garden is a light family stop near Yunokawa Onsen, with a playground and a foot bath adding to the visit.
