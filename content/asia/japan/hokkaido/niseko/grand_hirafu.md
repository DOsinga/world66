Grand Hirafu is the busiest and largest of the Niseko United ski areas, with the most developed base village and a broad mix of groomed runs, tree skiing, lessons, rental shops, and night skiing. It is the obvious first stop if you want the full Niseko scene rather than a quieter onsen-side ski day.
