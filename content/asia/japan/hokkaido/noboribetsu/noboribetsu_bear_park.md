Noboribetsu Bear Park sits on Mount Shihorei, reached by ropeway from the hot-spring district. The ride itself is part of the appeal, climbing over forest toward views of Lake Kuttara and the Pacific coast.

The bear enclosures will not be for everyone. If captive wildlife bothers you, take the ropeway views into account before deciding whether this belongs in your day.
