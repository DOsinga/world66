The Oyunumagawa Natural Footbath is Noboribetsu at its most relaxed. A short forest trail leads to a shallow warm stream, with simple wooden platforms where walkers sit and soak their feet.

Bring a small towel if you remember. It is a small stop, but it turns the volcanic landscape into something you feel directly.
