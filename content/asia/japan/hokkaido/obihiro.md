Obihiro is the main city of the Tokachi plain, a broad farming region in eastern [Hokkaido](/asia/japan/hokkaido). It feels open and practical, with long straight streets, big skies, and a food culture built around dairy, sweets, and buta-don pork rice bowls.

The sight that makes Obihiro stand out is [Banei Tokachi](/asia/japan/hokkaido/obihiro/banei_tokachi), a draft-horse race found nowhere else in the world. Enormous horses pull heavy sleds over a short dirt course, and the slow power of it feels more like a rural strength contest than ordinary racing.

The city also has a softer side. [Manabe Garden](/asia/japan/hokkaido/obihiro/manabe_garden) is one of the best-known stops on the Hokkaido Garden Path, [Midorigaoka Park](/asia/japan/hokkaido/obihiro/midorigaoka_park) gives an easy green break near the centre, and [Obihiro Shrine](/asia/japan/hokkaido/obihiro/obihiro_shrine) adds a quiet Shinto stop among the trees. Obihiro is not as dramatic as the national parks, but it is a good place to understand Tokachi rather than just pass through it.
