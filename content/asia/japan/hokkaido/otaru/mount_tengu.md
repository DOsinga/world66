Mount Tengu rises behind Otaru and gives the best broad view of the city, harbour, and Sea of Japan. A ropeway makes it easy to reach the top, where viewpoints, small museums, and walking paths spread around the summit area.

Go late in the day if the sky is clear. The city lights and dark water make the view much stronger than it looks on a flat grey afternoon.
