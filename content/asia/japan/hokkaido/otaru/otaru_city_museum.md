Otaru City Museum is the city's railway and local history museum, with a strong collection of locomotives, snowplows, and railway equipment suited to Hokkaido's hard winters. It is especially good if you are interested in how rail shaped Otaru's port economy.

From spring to autumn, a short steam train ride usually runs on the museum grounds. It is brief, but it makes the place much more fun for families and railway fans.
