The Stained Glass Museum is part of Otaru Art Base and shows English stained glass from the late 19th and early 20th centuries. The pieces once belonged to churches, so the appeal is not just colour and craft but the chance to see a transplanted slice of European religious art in a former Hokkaido port warehouse.
