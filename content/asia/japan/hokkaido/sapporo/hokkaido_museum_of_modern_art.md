The Hokkaido Museum of Modern Art is Sapporo's main modern-art museum, with collections that include Japanese and international works, glass art, and paintings connected to the École de Paris. Temporary exhibitions broaden the focus beyond the permanent collection.

It is a good indoor stop near central Sapporo, especially in winter or on rainy days. Pair it with Odori Park or the Hokkaido University Botanical Garden if you are walking the west side of the centre.
