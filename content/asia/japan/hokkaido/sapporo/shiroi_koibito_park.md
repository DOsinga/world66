Shiroi Koibito Park is Ishiya's chocolate-themed attraction, built around the famous Hokkaido white-chocolate sandwich cookies. The visit mixes a factory view, displays, sweets, restaurants, and deliberately playful decor, making it a light family stop rather than a traditional museum.
