Snow Brand Milk Products Historical Museum stands on the factory site where the company first produced butter in 1925. Exhibits explain dairy farming in Hokkaido and the history of Snow Brand's milk products.

It is a modest but distinctive stop for visitors interested in Sapporo's food industry.
