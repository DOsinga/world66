---

Shiretoko's appeal is almost entirely natural. [Shiretoko Goko Lakes](/asia/japan/hokkaido/shiretoko/shiretoko_goko_lakes) are the most visited attraction — five crater lakes in old-growth forest with boardwalk access. [Kamuiwakka Falls](/asia/japan/hokkaido/shiretoko/kamuiwakka_falls) is a warm volcanic waterfall you can wade up through its pools. The [Cape Shiretoko cruise](/asia/japan/hokkaido/shiretoko/shiretoko_cape_cruise) from Utoro covers the inaccessible cliffs and coastline to the peninsula tip.

Wildlife watching is exceptional year-round: [brown bears](/asia/japan/hokkaido/shiretoko/bear_watching) along the Shiretoko Pass road and coast, [whale watching](/asia/japan/hokkaido/shiretoko/whale_watching) from Rausu in summer, and [drift ice viewing](/asia/japan/hokkaido/shiretoko/drift_ice) with its massive sea eagle concentrations in winter. [Mount Rausu](/asia/japan/hokkaido/shiretoko/mount_rausu) is the peak for serious hikers.
