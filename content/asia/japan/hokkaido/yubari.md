Yubari is one of Hokkaido's stranger and more poignant small cities. It was built on coal, became famous for luxury melons and a film festival, then shrank brutally after the mines closed and the local economy collapsed.

That makes the place more than a curiosity. The [Yubari Coal Mine Museum](/asia/japan/hokkaido/yubari/yubari_coal_mine_museum) explains the industrial city that once stood here, [Takinoue Park](/asia/japan/hokkaido/yubari/takinoue_park) gives the valley a gentler side, and [Yubari Merode](/asia/japan/hokkaido/yubari/yubari_merode) turns the melon into something you can actually taste without buying a department-store gift box.

Yubari works best for travelers already moving through central Hokkaido by car. It is not polished, but it has a clear identity: coal seams, empty streets, mountain weather, and a melon brand famous across Japan.
