Mount Racey, on Mount Reisui above the old Yubari town centre, is a compact ski area rather than a destination resort. Its value is local: it shows how Yubari tried to remake itself after coal, and it can be a convenient winter stop if conditions and operations line up.

Check current opening details before planning around it. Yubari's tourism infrastructure has had a stop-start history, and winter facilities can change faster than guidebook copy.
