---
title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Amanohashidate is built around one extraordinary natural feature — the 3.6 km pine-covered sandbar that stretches across Miyazu Bay. Walking or cycling its length, reaching the viewpoints at each end, and exploring the shrines and temples of the surrounding hills fills a good day. The nearby city of Miyazu adds a feudal castle to the itinerary.
