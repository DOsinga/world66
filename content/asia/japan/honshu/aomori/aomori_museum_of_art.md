The Aomori Museum of Art stands beside Sannai-Maruyama and is worth pairing with the archaeological site. Its collection leans heavily into artists connected to the prefecture, including Yoshitomo Nara and Shiko Munakata, with theatrical work by Chagall as another major draw.

The building's pale, trench-like spaces echo the nearby excavation site, so the museum feels rooted in Aomori rather than dropped in as a generic white box. It is one of the better regional art museums in northern Japan.
