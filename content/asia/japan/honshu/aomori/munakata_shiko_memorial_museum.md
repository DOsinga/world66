The Munakata Shiko Memorial Museum of Art is a small museum devoted to the Aomori-born printmaker Munakata Shiko. His bold woodblock prints are the main draw, with paintings and calligraphy adding useful context.

It is more focused than Aomori's larger museums, but that is part of the appeal. Go if you want a quiet, concentrated look at one of Japan's distinctive modern artists.
