Orihime Shrine sits above central Ashikaga and honours deities linked to the city's old textile industry. The current shrine dates from 1937, after an earlier building burned, and its bright vermilion setting in Orihime Park makes it one of the city's most photogenic stops.

The climb is part of the point. From the shrine grounds you get views back over Ashikaga, which makes it a good companion to Ashikaga School and Bannaji Temple below.
