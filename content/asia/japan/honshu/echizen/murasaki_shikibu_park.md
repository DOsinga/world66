Murasaki Shikibu Park marks the year the author of *The Tale of Genji* spent in Echizen with her father, who had been appointed governor of the old province. The park is laid out as a Heian-style garden, with a golden statue of Murasaki looking back toward Kyoto.

It is a quiet stop rather than a major sight, but it gives Echizen a useful literary thread. The adjacent museum adds more context if you are already interested in Murasaki and the world of court literature.
