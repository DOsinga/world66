Fukui's Bhutan Museum is an unusual small museum devoted to Bhutanese culture, with objects, photographs, and displays on the Himalayan kingdom's landscapes and traditions. It is a niche stop, but that odd specificity is exactly what makes it memorable.

It works best as a short visit if you are already in the area or have an interest in Bhutan. Do not expect a large national museum; expect a focused local collection with a very particular subject.
