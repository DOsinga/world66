Fukui City History Museum gives useful context for a city that has been rebuilt more than once. The displays cover early settlement, the castle-town period, wartime destruction, and the modern city that grew around the station and castle grounds.

It works best with [Yokokan Garden](/asia/japan/honshu/fukui/yokokan_garden), which is just nearby and often sold with a combined ticket.
