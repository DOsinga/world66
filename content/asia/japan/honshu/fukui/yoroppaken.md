Yoroppaken is the classic Fukui sauce katsudon stop, often credited with making the dish famous. The order is simple: pork cutlet over rice, soaked in a sweet-salty sauce rather than covered with egg.

It is close enough to the central sights to work as lunch between the castle area and the rest of the city. Expect a line at busy times.
