Fukushima is a modest Tohoku capital in a broad inland basin, better known to many travellers for the prefecture's name than for the city itself. That is a shame. The city has spring flower hills, old onsen towns, a good art museum, volcanic scenery on its western edge, and orchards that make peaches feel like a civic obsession.

The centre is practical rather than pretty, with the shinkansen station, shops, and restaurants doing their work without much ceremony. The better side of Fukushima starts when you look outward: [Mt. Shinobu](/asia/japan/honshu/fukushima/mt_shinobu_and_iwaya_kannon) rises just north of downtown, [Hanamiyama](/asia/japan/honshu/fukushima/hanamiyama) turns a farming hillside into a famous spring walk, and the old hot-spring settlements of [Tsuchiyu](/asia/japan/honshu/fukushima/tsuchiyu_onsen), Iizaka, and Takayu sit within easy reach.

The city is also a useful base for the Bandai-Azuma Skyline and Azuma-Kofuji, the small volcanic cone west of town. You can make a strong day from flowers, carved Buddhist images, a museum, and a soak, or use Fukushima as a quieter stop between [Sendai](/asia/japan/honshu/sendai), Yamagata, and the deeper mountains of the prefecture.
