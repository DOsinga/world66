Gero Furusato History Memorial Museum covers local archaeology and history from the Jomon and Yayoi periods through more recent centuries. Recreated Jomon-period houses outside the museum help make the early-history material easier to picture.

It is a worthwhile short stop for visitors who want more than the hot-spring side of Gero, especially if they are already visiting Gero Onsen Gassho Village nearby.
