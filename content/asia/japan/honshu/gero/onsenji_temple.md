Onsenji Temple sits above the hot-spring town on the slope of Nakane Mountain. The climb up the stone steps is part of the visit, and the reward is a quiet temple precinct with views over the Hida River and the line of ryokan below.

The temple is tied to Gero's hot-spring story, with a healing Buddha and a setting that makes the town feel more like a valley settlement than a string of bathhouses.
