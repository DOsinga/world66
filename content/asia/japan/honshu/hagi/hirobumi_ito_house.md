Hirobumi Ito House preserves the childhood home of Ito Hirobumi, Japan's first prime minister, alongside the later Tokyo residence moved here after his rise in national politics. Seeing the two houses together gives a clear, physical sense of his path from Hagi student to Meiji statesman.
