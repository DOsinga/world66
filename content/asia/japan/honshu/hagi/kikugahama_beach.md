Kikugahama is the beach that keeps Hagi from feeling like an inland museum town. The sand runs below Mount Shizuki and close to the castle area, so it is easy to fold into a walk through the old quarter.

It is not the reason to cross Japan, but it is a fine place to end the day. In good weather, the sea view softens all the samurai and Meiji history you have been absorbing.
