Kuchiba Residence is one of Hagi's old samurai houses, built between the late 18th and early 19th centuries for a senior retainer of the domain. The house is modest compared with a castle or temple, but its rooms, garden setting, and view toward the Hashimoto River fit neatly into Hagi's samurai-town story.
