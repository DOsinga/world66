Hakone Museum of Art focuses on ancient Japanese ceramics, including earthenware and tea-ceremony objects. The collection is compact, but the setting makes it one of Hakone's more atmospheric museum stops.

The moss garden is the highlight for many visitors, especially in soft light or autumn colour. There is also a tea room, making this a quieter counterpoint to the larger art museums around Gora.
