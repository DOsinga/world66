Geibi-kei Gorge is one of Iwate's classic river landscapes, with sheer rock walls, Buddhist carvings, and flat-bottomed boats poled slowly upstream. The round trip takes about 90 minutes and includes time to step ashore, take in the cliffs, and try tossing lucky stones into a hole in the rock.
