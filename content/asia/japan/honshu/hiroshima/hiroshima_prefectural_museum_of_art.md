The Hiroshima Prefectural Museum of Art stands beside Shukkei-en Garden and has a strong collection of modern European and Japanese art. The permanent galleries include works associated with artists such as Dali and Magritte, while temporary exhibitions range widely.

Its location makes it easy to combine with Shukkei-en and Hiroshima Castle. It is a good choice when you want a quieter art stop away from the Peace Memorial Park circuit.
