---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

Hiroshima sits on the sheltered Seto Inland Sea coast and enjoys a mild climate with hot summers and relatively warm winters compared to other Japanese cities at similar latitude.

**Spring (late March–May)** is the most popular time to visit. Cherry blossom (*sakura*) typically peaks in late March to early April; the Peace Park is lined with flowering trees, and Miyajima's hillsides turn pink. Temperatures are comfortable (15–22°C) and the days are long. Crowds build in April.

**Summer (June–August)** brings the rainy season in June and early July, followed by intense heat and humidity (30–35°C) in August. 6 August is the anniversary of the bombing and draws large crowds for the Peace Memorial Ceremony; if you want to attend, book accommodation well in advance. The city's oyster season is over by this point, but summer festivals and fireworks make evenings lively.

**Autumn (September–November)** is excellent: comfortable temperatures, low humidity, and autumn foliage (*koyo*) on Miyajima and at Shukkei-en from late October into November.

**Winter (December–February)** is mild and rarely snowy in the city. Oyster season is at its peak. This is the quietest season and hotel rates are lower; a good time if the memorials are your main reason to visit and you prefer smaller crowds.
