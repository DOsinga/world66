Iwaki City Sea Dinosaur Centre is a small family-oriented fossil attraction tied to Futabasaurus suzukii, a Late Cretaceous marine reptile discovered near the Ohisa River. It mixes local fossil history with light theme-park elements.

It is not a major museum, but it is a useful stop for families or anyone interested in Fukushima's prehistoric sea life. Pair it with Iwaki's larger museums and coastal sights.
