Spa Resort Hawaiians sounds strange until you know Iwaki's coal story. The resort was created after the decline of the Joban coalfield, turning hot springs and staged Hawaiian dance into a new local economy.

Today it is a big water park, onsen, performance venue, and family resort. It is not subtle, but it is very Iwaki: practical, theatrical, and rooted in a real industrial shift.
