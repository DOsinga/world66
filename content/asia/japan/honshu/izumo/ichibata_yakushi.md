Ichibata Yakushi is a Buddhist temple complex on a hillside above Lake Shinji, traditionally associated with healing, especially ailments of the eyes. The approach and terraces give the visit a sense of ascent, with temple buildings, stone figures, and views across the lake country between Izumo and Matsue.

It is a worthwhile side trip for travelers who want more than Izumo Taisha's Shinto story. The temple has a quieter, more local rhythm, and the lake views help explain why this part of Shimane feels so connected to water, pilgrimage, and old routes between sacred places.
