Enoshima Aquarium is a large seaside aquarium near Enoshima, with dolphin shows, jellyfish displays, and exhibits on the marine life of Sagami Bay. It is an easy add-on to a Kamakura and Enoshima day trip, especially for families or on poor-weather days.
