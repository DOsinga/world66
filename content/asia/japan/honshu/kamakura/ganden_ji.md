Ganden-ji is an old Kannon temple in the green hills east of central Kamakura. Founded in the early 8th century, it is counted as the second temple on the Bandō 33 Kannon pilgrimage.

It is quieter than Kamakura's headline temples and works best for travellers who enjoy filling in the smaller religious sites around the city. The hillside setting is the main pleasure, especially when combined with nearby temple walks.
