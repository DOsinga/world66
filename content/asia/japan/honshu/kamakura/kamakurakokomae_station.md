Kamakurakokomae Station is a small stop on the Enoden line, but its setting has made it unexpectedly famous. Trains pass close to the sea, and the nearby level crossing frames a classic Kamakura view of rails, road, and Sagami Bay.

Fans also know the spot from Japanese pop culture, which means it can get crowded for photographs. Be considerate around traffic and residents, then ride the Enoden onward; the station is best enjoyed as part of the coastal journey, not as a long stop.
