---
title: Things to Do
type: section
---

Kamakura's temples and shrines are distributed across three main areas: the central valley around Tsurugaoka Hachimangū and Kamakura Station, the western valleys where the Great Buddha and Hase-dera sit, and the northern Zen temples of Kita-Kamakura. Between them, the hillside hiking trails offer a way to move through the landscape that the train lines and buses don't.
