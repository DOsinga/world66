---
title: Eating Out
type: section
---

Kanazawa is consistently ranked as one of Japan's finest food cities. The Sea of Japan provides excellent seafood — particularly in winter, when snow crabs, yellowtail, and white shrimp are at their peak. The city also has strong traditions in kaiseki (formal multi-course cuisine) and simpler izakaya eating. Budget travellers will do very well at Omi-cho Market at lunchtime.
