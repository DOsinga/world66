Ikasa Railway Memorial Museum preserves the memory of the small private railway that once linked Kasaoka with inland towns such as Yakage and Ibara. The exhibits are simple: photographs, railway objects, and the story of a local line that mattered before cars took over rural transport.

It is mainly for rail fans or travelers already exploring northern Kasaoka. Pair it with a wider drive rather than making it the sole reason to leave the port area.
