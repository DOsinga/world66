title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Kesennuma's best day combines the port and the coast. Start at the fish-market area and the [Shark Museum](/asia/japan/honshu/kesennuma/shark_museum), then choose between the hilltop [Rias Ark Museum of Art](/asia/japan/honshu/kesennuma/rias_ark_museum_of_art), the preserved tsunami ruins south of town, or a longer trip over the bridge to [Oshima](/asia/japan/honshu/kesennuma/kesennuma_oshima).
