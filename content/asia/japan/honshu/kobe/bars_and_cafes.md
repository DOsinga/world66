---
title: Bars and Cafes
type: section
---

Kobe has a sophisticated bar culture shaped by its international history. The city was one of the first in Japan to import foreign spirits and wine, and a tradition of European-style bars and cafes has been maintained in the Kitano and Motomachi areas since the Meiji era. Sannomiya has the broadest selection of bars, from standing bars (tachinomi) to jazz venues and craft beer pubs.
