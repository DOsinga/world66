The Sawa-no-Tsuru Museum sits in Kobe's Nada sake district, where water, rice, and port access made brewing one of the city's defining industries. The museum occupies a reconstructed brewery space and walks visitors through the tools and seasonal rhythm of traditional sake production.

It pairs naturally with the other Nada sake breweries and museums. Even if you are not a serious sake drinker, the old vats, presses, and brewing implements make the process concrete, and the location helps explain why this side of Kobe developed a culture distinct from the harbourfront.
