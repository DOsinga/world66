Hafuu specialises in hamburger steak (*hambagu*) made from premium Kyoto-raised wagyu beef, cooked to a precise medium and served with a demi-glace sauce, rice, and soup. The restaurant draws a lunchtime queue that regularly extends outside the door -- arrive by 11:30 am to sit without waiting. The interior is unpretentious: counter seating, direct cooking views, and efficient service.

The wagyu used here is reared near Kyoto and notably different from the industrial beef at comparable hamburger restaurants. A second branch operates in Gion.
