The Kyoto National Museum of Modern Art focuses on modern and contemporary art, with a strong emphasis on Kyoto and Kansai artists. Exhibitions change often, mixing well-known names with younger artists and craft-linked work.

It stands in the Okazaki museum district, close to Heian Shrine, making it easy to combine with other east-side sights. Check the exhibition schedule, as galleries may close between shows.
