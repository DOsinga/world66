Pontochō is a single five-block-long alley running parallel to the western bank of the Kamo River between Sanjō and Shijō. It is one of Kyoto's five remaining *hanamachi* (geisha quarters), and from late spring through autumn many of its riverside restaurants extend wooden platforms — *kawayuka* — out over the river bank for open-air dining.

The alley fits exactly two people abreast, and during the cooler hours of evening it gradually fills with diners drifting between *kaiseki* houses, *izakaya*, and tiny bars. Behind discreet doors, the resident *geiko* and *maiko* attend evening engagements. The annual Kamogawa Odori in May is the main public performance by the Pontochō *kaburenjō* (training school). Most restaurants here are reservation-only and pricey; for atmosphere alone, walk the alley end-to-end after dark and back along the river bank.
