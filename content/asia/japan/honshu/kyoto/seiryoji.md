Seiryo-ji is a quieter Arashiyama temple linked with Minamoto no Toru, the nobleman often named as one of the inspirations for Genji in *The Tale of Genji*. It makes a good counterpoint to the busier bamboo grove and Tenryu-ji, especially if you want a more literary thread through western Kyoto.
