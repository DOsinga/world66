Umenomiya Shrine is associated with sake, childbirth, and a cluster of local folk beliefs, including a stone said to aid pregnancy. Its garden is known for seasonal flowers, making it a quiet add-on for visitors moving between Arashiyama and western Kyoto's temple district.
