The Mashiko Museum of Ceramic Art is the best first stop in town. It explains why this rural place became a pottery name, with work by Shoji Hamada and other ceramic artists connected to Mashiko. The museum is part of Ceramic Art Messe Mashiko, so it also works as a practical orientation point before visiting shops and kilns.
