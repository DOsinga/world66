Lafcadio Hearn's Former Residence is the small Matsue house where the writer spent some of his happiest months in Japan. It pairs well with the nearby memorial museum, especially if you want to understand the garden, rooms, and quiet domestic setting behind *Glimpses of Unfamiliar Japan* and parts of *Kwaidan*.
