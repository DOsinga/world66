---
title: "Day Trips"
type: section
---

**Kamikōchi**, the most spectacular high valley in the Japanese Alps, is about two hours away by train to Shin-Shimashima and bus over the Kama Tunnel. The valley is closed to private cars and only open from mid-April to mid-November. It is the obvious day trip if the weather is clear; the walk from Taishō-ike to the Myōjin-bashi bridge takes a couple of hours along the Azusa river under the Hotaka peaks.

**Hotaka and the wasabi farms** (see Daiō above) are 30 minutes north by train. **Norikura Highlands**, with summer wildflowers and one of Japan's highest paved roads, is about an hour by bus. The post towns of the **Nakasendō** — Tsumago and Magome — are an hour or so south, reachable by train to Nagiso and a short connection.

For a longer day, **Kanazawa** is two and a half hours away on the Shinkansen via Nagano.
