Matsumoto Folkcraft Museum is a modest, thoughtful counterweight to the castle and the city's bigger art stops. Its collection focuses on mingei, the beauty of ordinary handmade objects: ceramics, baskets, textiles, lacquerware, and tools shaped for daily use rather than display.

The museum is best for travellers who enjoy quiet rooms and close looking. It helps explain a side of Japanese design that is easy to miss when moving between temples and major museums: practical things made well, with patience and regional character.
