A narrow shopping street running along the south bank of the Metoba river between the castle and Nakamachi. The stalls and shops sell antiques, tea ware, sweets and souvenirs, and the street is studded with frog statues of every size and material. A Shintō shrine dedicated to frogs (Kaeru-daimyōjin) sits in the middle.

It is best visited in the morning when the smaller antique sellers are open. The river beside it is shallow and clean, and locals fish from the embankment.
