Seizanso is associated with Tokugawa Mitsukuni, the second lord of the Mito domain and the historical figure behind the popular Mito Komon legend. The villa and garden sit away from central Mito, which makes the visit feel more like a deliberate pilgrimage into local history than a casual city stop.

The buildings are modest compared with grander samurai residences, but the setting is the draw: wooded paths, water, and a restrained garden atmosphere that suits the story of a retired lord. It is best for travellers already interested in Mito's Tokugawa connections after seeing Kairakuen and Kodokan.
