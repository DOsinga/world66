A collection of short stories by Japan's first Nobel laureate, several of which take place in the Fuji area and along the Izu Peninsula. Kawabata's prose is spare and exact, and Mount Fuji haunts the stories as a presence — symbol of permanence against human transience. A good introduction to the master of Japanese literary minimalism.
