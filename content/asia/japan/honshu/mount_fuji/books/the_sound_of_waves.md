Mishima's most accessible novel — a love story set on a small island off the Japanese coast, far from Fuji but saturated in the same seasonal Japan. It is the ideal novel to read before a trip: short, beautiful, and a perfect gateway into Japanese literary sensibility. Mishima was deeply influenced by Mount Fuji's symbolism and returned to it repeatedly in his work.
