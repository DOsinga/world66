Fuji-Q Highland is the Fuji Five Lakes area's major amusement park, known for large roller coasters and views of Mount Fuji from the rides. It also has themed areas and attractions tied to Japanese pop culture, including Naruto, Boruto, and Laid-Back Camp.

The park is a strong family or theme-park day in its own right, especially for travellers staying around Lake Kawaguchiko. Check ride operations before going, as weather around Mount Fuji can affect the bigger outdoor attractions.
