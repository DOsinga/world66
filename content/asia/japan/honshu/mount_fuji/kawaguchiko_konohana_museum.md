Kawaguchiko Konohana Museum is devoted to Akiko Ikeda's Dayan the Cat stories and the fantasy world of Wachiworld. The exhibits focus on original artwork and scenes from the children's books.

It is a light, family-friendly museum near Lake Kawaguchiko, with a cafe and gift shop that make it an easy rainy-day or between-viewpoints stop.
