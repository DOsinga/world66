Lake Shoji is the smallest of the Fuji Five Lakes, a quiet horseshoe of water with clear views toward Mount Fuji. It is less built-up than Kawaguchiko, so it suits a slower stop for photos, boating, fishing, or simply sitting by the shore when the mountain is out.
