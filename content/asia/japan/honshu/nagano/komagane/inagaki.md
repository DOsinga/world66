Inagaki is a straightforward place to try sauce katsudon, the dish Komagane is proud of. The pork cutlet is dipped in sauce before it lands on rice, making it richer and sweeter than a plain tonkatsu set.

It is close enough to the station to work before or after the bus to the ropeway.
