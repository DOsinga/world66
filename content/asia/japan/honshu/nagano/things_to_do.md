---
title: Things to Do
type: section
---

Nagano's main draw is [Zenkō-ji](/asia/japan/honshu/nagano/zenkoji), and you could spend an entire morning exploring the temple grounds and the Okaidan tunnel beneath the main hall. The [Nagano Prefectural Art Museum](/asia/japan/honshu/nagano/nagano_prefectural_art_museum) in adjacent Jōyama Park makes a good companion to the temple visit.

Outside the city centre, [Togakushi Shrine](/asia/japan/honshu/nagano/togakushi_shrine) is the most rewarding half-day excursion — the cedar-lined approach to the upper shrine is genuinely impressive. The former castle town of [Matsushiro](/asia/japan/honshu/nagano/matsushiro) rewards those interested in samurai-era Japan, and the nearby [Matsushiro Underground Imperial Headquarters](/asia/japan/honshu/nagano/matsushiro_underground_hq) is a sobering piece of wartime history. The [M-Wave Olympic Arena](/asia/japan/honshu/nagano/mwave) and surrounding sports park offer a look at the enduring Olympic legacy from 1998.
