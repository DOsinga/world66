title: "Books"
type: section
---

**Taiko, by Eiji Yoshikawa.** A sweeping novel about Toyotomi Hideyoshi, the peasant-born warlord from a village now part of Nagoya, who rose to unite Japan. Yoshikawa's prose makes the era's politics readable; visiting Nagoya Castle and Kiyosu after reading it is a different experience.

**The Lonesome Bodybuilder, by Yukiko Motoya.** A collection of darkly comic short stories by a Nagoya-born writer. Less about the city than about the strangeness of suburban Japanese life — which is part of Nagoya too.

**Toyota Production System, by Taiichi Ohno.** The slim book that defined modern manufacturing, written by the engineer who built the system at Toyota's Nagoya plants. Essential context for the Toyota Commemorative Museum.
