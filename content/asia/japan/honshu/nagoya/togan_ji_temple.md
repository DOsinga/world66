Togan-ji is a 16th-century Buddhist temple with one of Nagoya's more unusual temple settings. Its grounds include a seated Buddha, links to Benzaiten worship, and a large wooden block that visitors touch as a ritual gesture of leaving old sins behind.

It is a quieter, more local stop than Nittaiji or the big central shrines, but worthwhile if you are exploring eastern Nagoya or want a temple that feels less scripted.
