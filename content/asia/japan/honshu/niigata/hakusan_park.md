Hakusan Park is the green heart of central Niigata, a handsome Meiji-era garden built around a large pond and the Hakusan Shrine. Cherry blossoms make it busy in spring; the rest of the year it is pleasantly quiet. The **Old Prefectural Government Building** (Kyūdai-mon-cho) on the park edge is an 1883 Western-style wooden structure that is now a cultural property, occasionally open for guided tours.

Walk the pond circuit, cross the arched stone bridge to the small island shrine, and visit the flower market stalls that line the entrance on weekend mornings. The park is a five-minute walk from Hakusan Station or fifteen minutes on foot from Niigata Station.
