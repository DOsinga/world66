Minatopia, the Niigata City History Museum, explains the city through its port, river, trade, and civic development. The setting near historic buildings and waterways makes the museum feel connected to the landscape it interprets.

Visit early in a Niigata stay if you want context for the old port area and Shinano River. The museum is manageable in size and combines well with a waterfront walk.
