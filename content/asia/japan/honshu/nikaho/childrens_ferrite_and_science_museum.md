The Children's Ferrite and Science Museum is a small science museum with hands-on exhibits about magnets, sound, ferrite, and related everyday physics. Some displays have English explanations, and the outdoor play area adds a trampoline and jungle gym.

It is not a major museum, but it is a useful family stop around Nikaho, especially in poor weather or when younger travellers need something more active than temples and viewpoints.
