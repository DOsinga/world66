Nanao Art Museum focuses on art from the Noto Peninsula, with special attention to Hasegawa Tohaku, the great Momoyama-period painter born in Nanao. The museum is worth checking if you are travelling through the peninsula and want a cultural stop between the coast and the old market towns.
