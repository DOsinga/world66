The Noto Kiriko festivals are among the most visually striking events in Japan. From July through September, communities across the peninsula carry enormous painted lanterns through their streets in processions accompanied by taiko drumming, flutes, and singing. The lanterns glow from within as darkness falls, illuminating crowds of participants in happi coats pulling on thick ropes.

The festivals vary by town and date; Wajima, Anamizu, and Suzu each have their own. The most impressive are held at night along the waterfront, where the reflected light on the sea adds to the spectacle. Attending one is straightforward — visitors can join the edge of the procession, and accommodation in the area books out well in advance for the major events.
