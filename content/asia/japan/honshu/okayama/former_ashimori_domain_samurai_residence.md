The Former Ashimori Domain Samurai Residence preserves a domestic side of samurai-era history in Okayama's Ashimori district. Rather than castle walls or battlefield stories, the site focuses on residence, garden, social rank, and the everyday setting of a local ruling class.

It is a good excursion for travellers who have already seen Okayama Castle and Korakuen and want a quieter historic district. The distance from central Okayama makes it best as part of a planned Ashimori visit rather than a quick city-centre stop.
