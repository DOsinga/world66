---
title: Getting Around
type: section
---

Okayama has a tram line running from Okayama Station east toward the castle and garden district (Shiroshita stop, about 5 minutes). Most of the main sights are within walking distance of each other once you reach the castle area. The station area has rental bicycles, which are a good option for the longer walk to Yumeji Art Museum or for the full Kibiji route (which starts from Bizen-Ichinomiya Station on the JR Kibi Line, one stop west).

Buses connect the station to outlying areas. The Uno Line local train to Uno port for the Naoshima ferry departs from Okayama Station. The city is compact enough that a combination of the single tram line and walking covers the main sights without difficulty.
