---
title: Getting There
type: section
---

Okayama sits on the Sanyo Shinkansen line between Hiroshima and Osaka. Nozomi shinkansen services reach Osaka in 45 minutes and Hiroshima in 33 minutes; Tokyo is about 3 hours 15 minutes on the fastest services. This central position on the main western Honshu corridor means Okayama is extremely easy to include on a journey between Tokyo and Hiroshima or Kyushu without doubling back.

For the island of Naoshima, the connection is via the Uno ferry terminal: take the JR Uno Line from Okayama Station (about 50 minutes) to Uno port, then the Uno-Naoshima ferry (about 20 minutes). For Kurashiki, the San'yo Line from Okayama takes about 15 minutes. Okayama has a small airport with domestic services but the shinkansen is faster and more practical for most destinations.
