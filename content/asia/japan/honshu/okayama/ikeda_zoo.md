Ikeda Zoo is a compact, family-oriented zoo on the hills above central Okayama. It does not compete with the city's garden and castle for beauty, but it gives children an easy animal-focused outing without leaving town.

Expect a local zoo rather than a major wildlife institution. The hillside setting adds a bit of exercise and greenery, and it can be a useful change of pace after temples, museums, and formal gardens.
