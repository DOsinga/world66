Kibiji Literary Museum presents original works, manuscripts, and transcripts connected to writers from the Okayama area. It is most rewarding if you read Japanese or have a specific interest in modern Japanese literature.

The museum sits north of the city centre, close enough to combine with Okayama's other smaller museums before returning toward the castle and garden.
