RSK Rose Garden is a seasonal flower stop west of central Okayama, known mainly for its rose displays. It is not as essential as Korakuen, but it gives the city another garden option, especially for travellers visiting in late spring or autumn bloom periods.

The garden works best as a relaxed half-day outing for families, couples, or flower enthusiasts with extra time in Okayama. Check the season before going; outside the main bloom windows, its appeal is much quieter.
