The essential Okayama itinerary pairs [Kōraku-en](/asia/japan/honshu/okayama/korakuen) garden with [Okayama Castle](/asia/japan/honshu/okayama/okayama_castle) across the river — both are in the same district east of the station and easily combined in a morning. The castle district also holds the [Okayama Orient Museum](/asia/japan/honshu/okayama/okayama_orient_museum), an unexpectedly strong collection of ancient Middle Eastern art, and the [Yumeji Art Museum](/asia/japan/honshu/okayama/yumeji_art_museum) for the romantic Taishō-era illustrator.

For a full-day excursion, the [Kibiji cycling route](/asia/japan/honshu/okayama/kibiji_cycling_route) west of the city crosses a flat agricultural plain between ancient shrines and burial mounds — one of the more pleasant cycling routes in western Honshu. Day trips to [Kurashiki](/asia/japan/honshu/okayama/kurashiki) and the art island of Naoshima are also easily arranged from Okayama Station.
