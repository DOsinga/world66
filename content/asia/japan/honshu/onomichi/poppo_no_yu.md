Poppo no Yu is a modern neighbourhood bathhouse with indoor baths, an outdoor bath, saunas, and a simple eating area. It is not one of Onomichi's old temples or hilltop views, but it is a useful stop if you want a slow soak after walking the temple loop or cycling the Shimanami Kaido.
