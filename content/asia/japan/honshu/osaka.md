Osaka is Japan's third-largest city and its most extroverted. Where Tokyo is polished and Kyoto is refined, Osaka is loud, friendly, and proud of its food. The local phrase is "kuidaore" — eat until you drop — and the city takes it seriously. [Takoyaki](/asia/japan/honshu/osaka/takoyaki) stalls, okonomiyaki restaurants, and [kushikatsu bars](/asia/japan/honshu/osaka/yoshimoto_infty) are everywhere.

The city is built around two hubs. [Umeda](/asia/japan/honshu/osaka/osaka_station_umeda) in the north is the main business and shopping district, anchored by Osaka Station and the spectacular [Umeda Sky Building](/asia/japan/honshu/osaka/umeda). [Namba](/asia/japan/honshu/osaka/namba) in the south is the entertainment centre, and [Dotonbori](/asia/japan/honshu/osaka/dotonbori) — lined with giant illuminated signs including the famous [Glico Running Man](/asia/japan/honshu/osaka/glico_man) and packed with restaurants and bars — is the visual heart of the city. Between the two, the covered [Shinsaibashi shopping arcade](/asia/japan/honshu/osaka/shinsaibashi) runs for over 600 metres, with the streetwear district of [Amerika-mura](/asia/japan/honshu/osaka/amerikamura) branching off to the west.

[Osaka Castle](/asia/japan/honshu/osaka/osaka_castle) in the east of the city is the most recognisable landmark, surrounded by a large [park](/asia/japan/honshu/osaka/osaka_castle_park) that fills with cherry blossom viewers in April. South of the centre, [Shinsekai](/asia/japan/honshu/osaka/shinsekai) retains the city's pre-war character — narrow lanes, neon, and [Tsutenkaku Tower](/asia/japan/honshu/osaka/tsutenkaku) — while the [Shitennoji Temple](/asia/japan/honshu/osaka/shitennoji), Japan's oldest, anchors the Tennoji district. [Universal Studios Japan](/asia/japan/honshu/osaka/universal_studios_japan) draws visitors with children in the north.

The culture island of [Nakanoshima](/asia/japan/honshu/osaka/nakanoshima) holds the [National Museum of Art](/asia/japan/honshu/osaka/national_museum_art), the [Museum of Oriental Ceramics](/asia/japan/honshu/osaka/museum_oriental_ceramics), and two of Japan's finest civic buildings: the [Central Public Hall](/asia/japan/honshu/osaka/osaka_central_public_hall) and the [Nakanoshima Library](/asia/japan/honshu/osaka/nakanoshima_library). [Kuromon Ichiba](/asia/japan/honshu/osaka/kuromon_ichiba) market in Namba is the best place to eat your way through the city's seafood.

Osaka is the hub for exploring the Kansai region. Kyoto is 15 minutes by shinkansen; Nara is 45 minutes by train; Himeji Castle is 90 minutes west. [Minoh](/asia/japan/honshu/osaka/minoh) to the north offers waterfall hikes and autumn foliage within an hour of the centre. The city's efficient subway and private rail network makes it easy to combine with day trips.
