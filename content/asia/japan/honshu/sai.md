Sai is a small fishing village on the wild west coast of the Shimokita Peninsula, far enough from Aomori city that you feel the map thinning out. The village itself is quiet, but the coastline is one of northern Honshu's odd rewards: forested slopes, fishing hamlets, ferry piers, and the pale rock faces of [Hotoke-ga-ura](/asia/japan/honshu/sai/hotoke_ga_ura) rising straight from blue water.

This is not a place for a casual city break. Buses are slow, English is limited, and a rental car makes the difference between seeing Sai and merely reaching it. Come if you like remote coast roads, small ports, seafood, and places where the best sight is a short boat ride or mountain road away.

[Nuidoishiyama](/asia/japan/honshu/sai/nuidoishiyama) gives the village its landward drama, with a hike above Fukuura and views over the coast and surrounding mountains. Back near the harbour, [Arusasu](/asia/japan/honshu/sai/arusasu) is the practical centre for meals, local information, and boat arrangements. Sai is a spare, weather-marked place, but if you are already exploring Shimokita it is worth the extra road.
