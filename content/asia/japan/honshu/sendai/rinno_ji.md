Rinno-ji is a calm Zen temple north of central Sendai, best known for its garden, pagoda, and unhurried atmosphere. It is not as famous as Zuihoden or the castle site, but it gives a gentler view of the city's temple culture.

The garden is the reason to come, especially when flowers or autumn colour are out. It is a good stop if you are already exploring the northern side of the city around Kita-Sendai and Aoba Shrine, or if you want a quiet hour away from shopping streets and station crowds.
