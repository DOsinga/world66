Shimonoseki Municipal Archaeological Museum looks at the deep human history of the Kanmon Strait
area, from prehistoric settlement to burial sites and excavated objects. It is a local museum, but
the material helps explain why this narrow crossing between Honshu and Kyushu mattered long before
modern bridges and tunnels.

The museum suits travelers who like archaeology or who are already visiting the Chofu side of
Shimonoseki. It is quieter than the waterfront sights, and that calm makes the pottery, tools, and
tomb finds easier to absorb.
