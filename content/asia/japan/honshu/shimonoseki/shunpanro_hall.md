Shunpanro Hall is where the Treaty of Shimonoseki was signed in 1895, ending the First Sino-Japanese War. The small museum has period furniture and treaty-related displays, making it a good companion to nearby Akama Shrine if you want to understand why Shimonoseki matters in East Asian history.
