---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

Shimonoseki is a year-round destination, but the seasons make a real difference.

**Spring (March–May)** is the finest time to visit. Cherry blossoms at Kozanji Temple in Chofu are exceptional — the pale pink flowers against dark cypress-bark rooflines are one of the most striking spring scenes in western Japan. Temperatures are mild (15–20°C) and the waterfront is pleasant for walking.

**Summer (June–August)** is hot and humid, with temperatures often topping 35°C. The Kanmon Strait hosts the Kanmon Sea Festival in August, and Bon Odori dancing takes place across the city. Worth seeing, but the heat can be draining.

**Autumn (September–November)** brings relief from the heat and some fine foliage around Chofu in October. The fugu season opens in October, so this is a good time to eat the city's signature dish at its peak.

**Winter (December–February)** is cool and occasionally wet, but fugu is at its best — the cold water makes the fish leaner and more flavourful. Crowds are minimal and prices are lower.

The Karato Sunday morning market runs year-round, though it is most atmospheric from October through April when fugu is in season.
