Refresh Park Yumura is a family-friendly hot-spring complex near Yumura Onsen. Unlike most traditional onsen, it has swimsuit areas, so mixed groups and families can use the pools together.

The outdoor baths include mountain views, a waterfall, a cave bath, and other novelty pools. Bring swimwear or rent it on site; tattoos are not allowed.
