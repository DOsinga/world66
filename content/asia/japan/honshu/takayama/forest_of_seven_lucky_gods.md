A quiet wooded grove on the western edge of town where seven of Japan's traditional gods of fortune — Daikokuten, Ebisu, Bishamonten, Benzaiten, Fukurokuju, Jurōjin and Hotei — are rendered as huge wooden carvings, each made from a thousand-year-old cedar. The figures are roughly hewn, weathered and surrounded by cedar trees; the atmosphere is unusual, peaceful and faintly mystical.

The site is small and easily missed; it sits a short walk west of the Hida Folk Village and is best combined with that visit.
