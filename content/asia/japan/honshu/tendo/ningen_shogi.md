Ningen Shogi, or human shogi, is Tendo's best-known event. During the cherry-blossom season, performers dressed as warriors and court figures stand in for the pieces on a giant outdoor board, moving at the command of the players.

It is more pageant than match analysis, which is exactly the point. If your timing is right in late April, this is the moment when Tendo's shogi identity becomes memorable rather than merely interesting.
