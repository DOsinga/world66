Inaba Man-yō History Pavilion is a small museum in Kokufu-cho with artifacts from the old Inaba area, especially from the Nara and Heian periods.

It works best as a short stop for travellers already exploring Tottori's historic sites beyond the sand dunes and castle ruins.
