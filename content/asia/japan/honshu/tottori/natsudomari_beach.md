Natsudomari Beach is a quiet coastal stop west of central Tottori, known for the female divers who have worked these waters for centuries. It is not a big resort beach, but it has a local character that makes it more interesting than a simple patch of sand.

Come in good weather for the sea views and the chance, if your timing is lucky, to see the traditional fishing still being practised offshore.
