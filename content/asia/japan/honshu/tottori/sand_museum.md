The Sand Museum turns Tottori's most famous landscape into art. International sculptors build large, detailed scenes from sand, and the exhibition is remade around a new theme each year.

It sounds like a novelty, but the scale and craft are impressive. Pair it with the dunes rather than treating it as a substitute for them.
