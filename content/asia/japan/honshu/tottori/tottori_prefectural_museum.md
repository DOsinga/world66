Tottori Prefectural Museum covers the prefecture's nature, geology, history, festivals, and art. Displays range from regional rocks and minerals to cultural objects and local artworks.

The standouts are the natural history exhibits, including a preserved giant squid and a live giant salamander. It is a useful indoor stop near Tottori Castle Ruins and Jinpukaku.
