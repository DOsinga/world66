Ube Shrine is one of the old shrines of the Inaba area, the eastern part of modern Tottori Prefecture. It is traditionally associated with prayers for fertility, long life, and good fortune, and its quiet grounds make a calm contrast to Tottori's busier castle and dune sights.

The shrine is not a major destination on its own, but it is a good addition for travellers interested in local religious sites around Tottori.
