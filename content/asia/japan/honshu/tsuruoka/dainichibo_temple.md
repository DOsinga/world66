Dainichibo Temple is one of the more unusual stops around Dewa Sanzan, known for displaying the remains of a self-mummified ascetic priest. The visit is intimate and a little strange, with temple treasures shown in a setting where English explanations may be limited but the atmosphere carries much of the story.
