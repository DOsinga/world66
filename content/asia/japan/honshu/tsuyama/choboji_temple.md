Choboji Temple is a quiet Tendai Buddhist temple south of central Tsuyama, with roots in the Heian period. Its main draw is seasonal rather than monumental: thousands of hydrangeas fill the grounds in June, when the temple is at its most photogenic.

It is best for travellers with a car, a bicycle, or a particular interest in temple gardens. Outside hydrangea season, expect a peaceful local temple rather than a major sight.
