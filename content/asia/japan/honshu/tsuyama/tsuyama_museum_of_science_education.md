Tsuyama Museum of Science Education, also called the Tsuyama Wonder Museum, is an old-style science museum near Kakuzan Park. Its displays cover natural history, anatomy, wildlife, and hands-on science, with a slightly eccentric collection that suits a slower stop in town.

It is best for families or rainy weather, and pairs easily with the castle ruins above it.
