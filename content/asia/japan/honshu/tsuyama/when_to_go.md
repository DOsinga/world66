---
title: When to Go
type: section
---

Spring is the showpiece season, when the cherry trees around the castle ruins turn Kakuzan Park into the city's busiest and prettiest place. Autumn is calmer and good for the garden and old streets.

Tsuyama is visitable all year, but winter can feel quiet and some smaller museums or shops keep limited hours. Check opening days before making the trip for one specific site.
