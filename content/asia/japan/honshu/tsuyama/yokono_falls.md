Yokono Falls is a scenic waterfall area north of Tsuyama, with three drops that build from a small upper fall to a larger 16-meter cascade. It is a quiet nature stop and is especially attractive in autumn when the leaves change color.

In summer, the area is also known for nagashi-somen, noodles sent down bamboo chutes beside the falls.
