Mugonkan is a small, serious museum showing work by art students who died during the Second World War. The paintings are not presented as grand national memory, but as unfinished lives: portraits, studies, landscapes, and names that make the loss personal.

It sits outside the centre, so it takes a little effort. That effort is part of the visit, and the quiet setting suits the museum's mood.
