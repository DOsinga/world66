This small museum focuses on Mogami Yoshiaki, the daimyo who expanded Yamagata Castle and raised the domain's status after the Battle of Sekigahara. It is most useful when visited before or after the castle ruins.

Expect a focused local-history stop rather than a blockbuster museum. For travellers interested in samurai politics and how a castle town grew, it fills in the story behind the moats outside.
