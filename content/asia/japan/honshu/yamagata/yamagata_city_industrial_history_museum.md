Yamagata City Industrial History Museum is a small museum about the city's traditional and modern industries. It is especially useful for understanding Yamagata imono, the local metal-casting craft known for high-quality ironware.

It is a brief stop, but worthwhile if you want context for the crafts and products sold around the city.
