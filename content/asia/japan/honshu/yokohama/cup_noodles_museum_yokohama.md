Cup Noodles Museum Yokohama turns instant ramen into a playful design, food, and pop-culture museum. Exhibits explain Momofuku Ando's invention story, while hands-on activities let visitors design packaging or make noodles in a controlled workshop setting.

It is especially good for families and for travellers already exploring Minato Mirai. Reserve or arrive early for popular workshops, because the interactive parts are the difference between a quick look and a memorable visit.
