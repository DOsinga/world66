The Equine Museum of Japan explores horse culture, racing history, and the old Negishi racecourse landscape in Yokohama. It is a specialist museum, but the surrounding park and historic racing context make it more interesting than the theme first suggests.

Visit it with Negishi Forest Park rather than as a standalone cross-city trip. Horse enthusiasts and travellers looking for quieter Yokohama history will get the most from it.
