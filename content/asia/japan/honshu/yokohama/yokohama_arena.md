Yokohama Arena is a major concert and event hall near Shin-Yokohama Station, used for big-name Japanese and international music acts as well as occasional fairs and special events. It is not a sight to detour for on an empty day, but it matters if your trip lines up with a concert or one of the larger events on the calendar.
