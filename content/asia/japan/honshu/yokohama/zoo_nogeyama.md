Zoo Nogeyama is a compact, free zoo on a hill above central Yokohama. It is not the city's flashiest attraction, but it works well for families, budget travelers, or anyone with a spare hour around Sakuragicho and Noge.

The setting is part of the appeal. Paths climb through greenery, views open toward the city, and the scale stays manageable rather than exhausting. Treat it as a neighborhood outing with animals, not a full-day wildlife park.
