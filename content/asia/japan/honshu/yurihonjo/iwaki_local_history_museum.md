Iwaki Local History Museum is the most useful stop for understanding the northern side of Yurihonjo. The complex mixes museum displays with reconstructed buildings, tools, textiles, and local material from the old Kameda and Iwaki districts.

The tower and grounds make it more than a room of cases, though English support is limited. Treat it as a context stop before heading deeper into the former towns that now make up the city.
