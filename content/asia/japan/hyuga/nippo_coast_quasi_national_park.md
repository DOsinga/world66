Nippo Coast Quasi National Park protects a dramatic stretch of ria coastline around Cape Hyuga, where cliffs, coves, and occasional sandy beaches cut into the Pacific shore. The area is best visited for coastal views, short walks, and nearby viewpoints such as Umagase and Kurusu no Umi.
