---
title: Getting Around
type: section
---

Within Morioka, the central area around the castle ruins and old town is walkable. The main JR and Shinkansen station is a fifteen-minute walk from the castle. Local buses cover the wider city and the Iwate Museum of Art out towards the Shizukuishi valley.

For the rest of the prefecture, a rental car is the most practical option. The Tohoku Expressway runs north–south through the western valleys, and roads along the Sanriku coast are scenic if sometimes slow. Distances are deceptive — Hiraizumi is 130 kilometres south of Morioka, and the coast adds another 50–80 kilometres east.

The Sanriku Railway connects coastal towns between Kamaishi in the south and Kuji in the north, with a gap in the middle served by JR's Yamada Line. It's a slow but charming journey with views of sea cliffs and fishing harbours.

From Morioka, local JR services reach Hanamaki (about 30 minutes) and connect through to Ichinoseki for Hiraizumi.
