---
title: Getting There
type: section
---

Morioka is the main gateway to Iwate. It sits on the Tohoku Shinkansen line, two hours and ten minutes from Tokyo on the Hayabusa service — fast, reliable, and remarkably affordable compared to flying. The journey north from Sendai takes around 35 minutes.

For the south of the prefecture, Ichinoseki Station is the stop for Hiraizumi: the town is only a few minutes by local train from Ichinoseki, which is itself on the Tohoku Shinkansen.

The Sanriku coast is harder to reach by public transport. Miyako and Kamaishi have bus connections from Morioka, and the Sanriku Railway runs the length of the coast between Kamaishi and Kuji, rebuilt after the 2011 tsunami. Renting a car in Morioka gives you the most flexibility for exploring the coastal villages.

There is no commercial airport in Iwate; Hanamaki Airport serves mainly charter and seasonal routes. Most visitors fly to Sendai or Tokyo and continue by shinkansen.
