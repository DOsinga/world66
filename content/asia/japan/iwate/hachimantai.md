Hachimantai is less a town than a spread-out mountain district on the Iwate side of a volcanic plateau. The merged municipality has offices and train stations down in the rice fields, but the reason to come is higher up: snow country, hot springs, ski slopes, and open highland walks around Towada-Hachimantai National Park.

The area changes sharply with the season. Late spring brings the short-lived [Hachimantai Dragon Eye](/asia/japan/iwate/hachimantai/hachimantai_dragon_eye), when melting snow turns Kagami-numa pond into a near-perfect ring. Summer and autumn are for walking the plateau and soaking afterward, while winter belongs to [Hachimantai Ski Resort](/asia/japan/iwate/hachimantai/hachimantai_ski_resort) and nearby Appi Kogen.

Hachimantai rewards travellers who have a car or plan bus times carefully. The sights are spread across mountain roads, some close for much of winter, and the best day is built around one strong outdoor plan plus an onsen stop such as [Matsukawa Onsen](/asia/japan/iwate/hachimantai/matsukawa_onsen).
