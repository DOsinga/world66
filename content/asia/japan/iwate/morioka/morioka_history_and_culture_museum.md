This museum sits beside Morioka Castle Ruins Park and is the best place to understand the city before walking the old castle district. The displays cover the Nanbu clan, local festivals, and the shape of Morioka as a castle town.

The festival material is the liveliest part, especially the large floats and introductions to Chagu Chagu Umakko and Morioka Sansa Odori. It is not a vast museum, but it gives the ruins outside useful context.
