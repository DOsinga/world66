Morioka Museum of Great Predecessors presents rotating exhibits on people who shaped Morioka's history, culture, and public life. It is a small local-history museum rather than a broad regional collection.

The museum is best for visitors with extra time in Morioka or a specific interest in the city's writers, thinkers, and civic figures. Pair it with the nearby art museum or central historic sights.
