Oyakuen Garden began in the early Edo period as a medicinal herb garden and later became a garden for the local lord. The layered history gives it more interest than a simple city green space, with museum buildings and old garden design sharing the same grounds.

It is a calm stop in Morioka for anyone interested in gardens, local history, or the quieter traces of the Nanbu domain.
