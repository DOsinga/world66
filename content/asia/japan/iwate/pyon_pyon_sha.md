Pyon Pyon Sha is the restaurant most associated with Morioka Reimen, the cold buckwheat-and-starch noodle dish the city has made its own. The chain was founded in 1983 by a Korean-Japanese family from Morioka, and this branch near the station remains the reference version. The noodles are chewy and translucent, served in a chilled beef bone broth with sliced beef, kimchi, watermelon or Asian pear, cucumber, and a hard-boiled egg.

The dish is a summer staple in Morioka but served year-round here, alongside the restaurant's Korean barbecue menu. The dining room fills quickly on weekend evenings. If you want the full Morioka cold noodle experience alongside the Reimen, the Jajamen — thick wheat noodles with meat miso paste — is equally worth ordering.
