---
title: "Day Trips"
type: section
---

Three good day trips run from Kagoshima:

**Ibusuki**, an hour south by the Ibusuki-no-Tamatebako sightseeing train, is famous for its **sand baths** — you lie buried up to the neck in steaming volcanic sand on the beach, sweating into a towel.

**Chiran**, an hour and a half by bus, is the best-preserved samurai street in Kyushu, with seven small Edo-period stroll gardens still owned by the original families. The town also has a memorial museum to the kamikaze pilots who flew their last sorties from a wartime airfield on its outskirts; the museum is sobering and worth the trip.

**Kirishima**, the mountain range north of the city, has the volcanic crater lake of Onami-no-ike, the old Kirishima-jingu shrine and a string of onsen towns. The toll road over the ridge gives the best mountain views in southern Kyushu.

For the longer trip, the cedar forests of **Yakushima**, three hours by jetfoil from Kagoshima port, deserve at least two nights.
