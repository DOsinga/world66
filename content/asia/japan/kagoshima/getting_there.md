---
title: "Getting There"
type: section
---

Kagoshima sits at the southern tip of the Kyushu Shinkansen. From Hakata (Fukuoka) it is one hour and twenty minutes by the fastest **Mizuho** trains, and three hours forty minutes from Shin-Osaka with a single change at Hakata. The terminal is **Kagoshima-Chuo**, on the western side of the city; the tram and city-loop buses leave from the square outside the station.

**Kagoshima Airport** is in Kirishima, an hour north of the city by limousine bus. Direct flights run to Tokyo (Haneda and Narita), Osaka, Nagoya, Sapporo and Naha, and a handful of short-hop international routes serve Seoul, Shanghai, Hong Kong and Taipei. From the airport, the bus to Kagoshima-Chuo is roughly 1,400 yen one way.

Ferries leave from Kagoshima New Port to Tanegashima, Yakushima and the Tokara and Amami island chains, and the high-speed jetfoil to Yakushima takes under three hours.
