---
title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Kagoshima is small enough to see on foot in a day, and big enough that you should give it two. The unmissable trip is the ferry across the bay to **Sakurajima**, the active volcano that defines the skyline. Back on the city side, **Sengan-en** is the great Shimazu strolling garden, with the volcano as its borrowed backdrop and a UNESCO-listed industrial complex next door. **Mount Shiroyama**, the wooded hill above downtown, is where the Satsuma Rebellion ended; the lookout near the summit gives you the same Sakurajima view the samurai had on their last morning. The **Museum of the Meiji Restoration** is the best introduction to why this remote southern city produced so many of modern Japan's founding fathers, and the **Reimeikan** prefectural museum fills in the wider history. The covered shopping arcades of **Tenmonkan** are the heart of the modern city — eat, drink and wander here after dark.
