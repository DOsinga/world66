Yakushima's main draws are its ancient cedar forests and mountain hiking. [Jōmon Sugi](/asia/japan/kagoshima/yakushima/jomon_sugi) is the headline attraction — a full-day trail through spectacular old-growth forest to one of the world's oldest trees. [Shiratani Unsuikyo Ravine](/asia/japan/kagoshima/yakushima/shiratani_unsuikyo) is more accessible and provided the visual inspiration for Princess Mononoke.

For serious hikers, the summit of [Mount Miyanoura](/asia/japan/kagoshima/yakushima/mount_miyanoura) is the highest point in Kyushu and the centrepiece of multi-day mountain traverses. The coast offers tidal hot springs at [Hirauchi Kaichu Onsen](/asia/japan/kagoshima/yakushima/hirauchi_kaichu_onsen), nesting sea turtles at [Inakahama Beach](/asia/japan/kagoshima/yakushima/inakahama_beach), and [snorkelling and diving](/asia/japan/kagoshima/yakushima/yakushima_snorkelling) in clear subtropical waters.
