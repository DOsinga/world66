Himeji Historical Peace Center is a sober counterpoint to the city’s famous castle, focusing on wartime bombing, civilian life, and the local memory of World War II. It gives Himeji a 20th-century historical layer many visitors otherwise miss.

The museum is modest, and English support may be limited, but its subject is specific and worthwhile. Pairing it with Tegarayama Park or a longer city walk makes it easier to see Himeji as more than a castle stop between trains.
