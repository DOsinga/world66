Hamafukutsuru-Ginjo Brewery and Shop is a working sake brewery in Kobe's Nada district, where visitors can look in on the brewing process through glass viewing areas. It feels more active than a simple display room, which is the point.

The shop is useful if you want to taste or buy local sake after seeing how it is made. It fits well with the other Nada breweries if you are spending part of the day in the district.
