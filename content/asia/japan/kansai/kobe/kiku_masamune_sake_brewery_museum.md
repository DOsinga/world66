Kiku-Masamune Sake Brewery Museum is one of the most accessible stops in Kobe's Nada sake district. It explains traditional brewing with old tools, displays, and enough context to make the surrounding brewery area more than a row of company names.

The museum is compact, and tastings or shop visits are part of the appeal. Pair it with a walk through Nada's other sake sites if you want to understand Kobe through something older and more local than the harbour skyline.
