title: Things to Do
type: section
---

Kobe rewards unhurried exploration. The Kitano hillside, the waterfront at Meriken Park, the sake breweries of Nada, and the mountain resort of Arima Onsen all lie within easy reach of the city centre. For a city its size, Kobe punches above its weight for foreign visitors — its long history of international trade has given it an easy cosmopolitanism that Osaka and Kyoto lack.
