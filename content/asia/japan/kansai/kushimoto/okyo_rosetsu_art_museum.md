Okyo Rosetsu Art Museum is a small, focused collection of ink paintings by Maruyama Ōkyo and his disciple Nagasawa Rosetsu. It is a quiet stop for visitors interested in Japanese painting, especially if you want something cultural to pair with Kushimoto's coastal sights.
