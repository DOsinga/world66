Giōji is a small temple in the quieter northern part of Arashiyama, known less for grand halls than for its moss garden. The temple is linked to *The Tale of the Heike*, where the dancer Gio withdraws from courtly life and devotes herself to Buddhism.

The visit is short, but the setting is lovely: a low thatched hall, maples, and a carpet of bright green moss that looks especially good after rain. It pairs well with Otagi Nenbutsu-ji and the back lanes of Sagano, away from the busiest bamboo-grove crowds.
