Koun-ji is a small temple in eastern Kyoto, known for a garden that uses the surrounding hills as borrowed scenery. It was once associated with Empress Go-Mizunoo, which gives the quiet compound a stronger historical thread than its size suggests.

It is best treated as a calm side stop while exploring the temple districts around Nanzen-ji and the Philosopher's Path. Visitors looking for famous sights should start elsewhere, but those with time in the area may appreciate its quiet setting.
