Kyoto Municipal Museum of Art is part of the Okazaki museum district near Heian Shrine. Its collections focus on Kyoto artists, with temporary exhibitions that range from local work to Japanese and international art.

The museum is easy to combine with nearby east-side sights, especially Heian Shrine, Nanzen-ji, and the Philosopher's Walk area.
