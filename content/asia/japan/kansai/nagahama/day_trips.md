The classic day trip from Nagahama is out onto Lake Biwa itself. Ferries from Nagahama Port cross to [Chikubu-shima](/asia/japan/kansai/nagahama/chikubushima), a tiny sacred island where a shrine and temple both over 1,400 years old cling to a steep wooded slope — a quiet, atmospheric half-day that pairs well with the lakeshore views back in town. [Hikone](/asia/japan/kansai/hikone), with its original castle, lies a short train ride south along the lake and makes an easy add-on if you want a second stop.
