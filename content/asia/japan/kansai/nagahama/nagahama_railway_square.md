Nagahama Railway Square preserves one of Japan's early railway stories through historic station architecture, locomotives, and railway equipment. The site is especially engaging because it ties national modernization to a smaller Lake Biwa town rather than a major metropolis.

Rail enthusiasts will get the most from the rolling stock and technical displays, but casual visitors can still enjoy the old station atmosphere. It pairs well with Nagahama's castle park and traditional shopping streets.
