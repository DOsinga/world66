Ikoma Shrine sits below forested slopes west of Nara and is said to date back to the 5th century. Wooden halls, brass lanterns, and a quiet setting make it a pleasant change from the busier temple circuits. Its annual fire festival is one of the best times to come, if your visit lines up with it.
