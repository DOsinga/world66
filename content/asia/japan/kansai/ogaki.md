Ogaki is a castle and canal city in Gifu Prefecture, west of Nagoya and close to the Sekigahara pass. It is not one of Japan's great old towns, but it has a clear identity: water, lowland castle history, and Matsuo Basho, who ended the journey later published as *The Narrow Road to the Deep North* here.

The centre is easy to handle on foot. Ogaki Castle is a reconstruction, but it anchors the old town, while the Funamachi canal and Oku no Hosomichi memorial give the city a literary reason to matter. In spring, the canal-side cherry blossoms make the Basho connection feel less like a plaque and more like a real place.

Ogaki is best as a half-day or easy day trip from Nagoya, Gifu, or Hikone. It will not replace Kyoto or Takayama, but for travelers interested in Basho, Sekigahara-era history, or quieter castle towns, it clears the bar.
