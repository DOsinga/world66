Moriya Tadashi Art Museum is dedicated to the Ogaki-born nihonga painter Tadashi Moriya. The collection focuses on his traditional Japanese painting, with permanent displays and changing exhibitions that make the museum a quiet cultural stop near the centre of town.

It is a small museum, but a worthwhile addition if you are already visiting Ogaki Castle and the Oku no Hosomichi memorial sites nearby.
