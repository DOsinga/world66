This memorial hall explains why Ogaki matters to Japanese literary travel. Matsuo Basho ended the long journey behind *Oku no Hosomichi*, usually translated as *The Narrow Road to the Deep North*, at Ogaki, and the museum follows the route, the poems, and the people connected with the trip.

The displays are most meaningful if you know even a little about Basho before you arrive. Without that, treat it as a compact introduction and then walk to the canal port where the journey ended.
