Cotton City Museum is a small local museum in a kura storehouse at the registered Ebisui House. Its displays focus on tools used in yarn and cotton production, a useful window into Hirano's older craft and merchant life.

It is best treated as a short stop while exploring the district rather than a major Osaka museum.
