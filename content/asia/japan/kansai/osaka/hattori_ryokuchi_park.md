Hattori Ryokuchi Park is a large green space north of central Osaka with lawns, paths, cherry trees, and bamboo groves. Its standout attraction is the Open-Air Museum of Old Japanese Farmhouses, which preserves traditional rural buildings from different parts of Japan.

The park is a good low-key day trip when you want outdoor space and a cultural stop away from the busiest central districts.
