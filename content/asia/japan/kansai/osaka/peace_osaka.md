Peace Osaka is a war and peace museum near Osaka Castle Park. Its displays focus on the bombing of Osaka during World War II and the effect of war on ordinary people in the city.

The museum is a useful counterweight to the castle and the more upbeat city-history museums nearby. Exhibits include English explanations, so it is accessible even if you do not read Japanese.
