Tennoji Zoo is Osaka's long-running city zoo, set beside Tennoji Park and Shinsekai. Its enclosures include African savanna animals, Asian rainforest species, an aviary, polar bears, penguins, and sea lions.

It is an easy family stop when visiting the Tennoji and Shinsekai area.
