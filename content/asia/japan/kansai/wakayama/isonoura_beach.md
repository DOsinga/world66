Isonoura Beach is Wakayama's main surf beach, with a long sandy shore west of the city and shallow water near the dunes. It is a practical choice for swimming, beginner surfing, and a beach day that still stays close to Wakayama.

The beach is busiest in warm weather, when parking and facilities are easiest to find. Check local surf and swimming conditions before making the trip.
