Yoro Shrine sits on the waterfall route and keeps the old sacred-water story attached to the landscape. It is a modest stop rather than a destination by itself, but it helps the walk feel less like a park circuit and more like a local pilgrimage.

Visit it on the way down from the falls, when the legend and the sound of the water are still fresh.
