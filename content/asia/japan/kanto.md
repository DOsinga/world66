The Kanto region is the heart of modern Japan, the great plain around Tokyo Bay that holds the capital [Tokyo](/asia/japan/kanto/tokyo), the port city of [Yokohama](/asia/japan/kanto/yokohama), and roughly a third of the country's population. Beyond the metropolis lie some of Japan's best day trips: the temples and Great Buddha of [Kamakura](/asia/japan/kanto/kamakura), the shrines of [Nikko](/asia/japan/kanto/nikko), and the hot springs and Mount Fuji views of [Hakone](/asia/japan/kanto/hakone).
