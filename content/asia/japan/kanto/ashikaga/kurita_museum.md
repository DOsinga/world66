Kurita Museum is a spacious porcelain museum near Tomita, close to Ashikaga Flower Park. Its collection focuses on Imari and Nabeshima ware, displayed in carefully designed buildings set among landscaped grounds.

The admission is higher than most small-city museums, so it is best for travellers with a real interest in ceramics. If that is you, it can easily fill a quiet half day.
