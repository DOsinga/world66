Jinmuji Temple is an old hillside temple traditionally dated to 724. It sits among green hills northeast of Kamakura, with walking routes nearby.

The temple is best approached as part of a quiet hike rather than as a single major sight. It suits visitors looking for older temples and wooded paths outside Kamakura's busiest shrine and beach areas.
