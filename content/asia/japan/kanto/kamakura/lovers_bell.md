Lovers Bell is a small Enoshima viewpoint where couples ring the bell and leave padlocks as a promise of lasting affection. The ritual is sweet, but the better reason to climb here is the view over Sagami Bay.

It works best as a short detour while walking around Enoshima, especially in clear weather when the coast opens out below you.
