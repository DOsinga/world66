Kamakura's sights cluster into two main zones: the Zen temple valleys of Kita-Kamakura to the north, and the Hase area with the Great Buddha to the west. The hilltop shrine of Tsurugaoka Hachimangu sits at the centre, with the main shopping street running south from it to the station. Use the filters below to browse by type.
