Kawagoe City Museum explains the history and construction of the city's warehouse district, making it most useful after walking the old streets. Models, artifacts, and local-history displays give context for how Kawagoe developed.

It is next to a small art museum, so the two can be combined into a short cultural stop near Kawagoe Castle Honmaru Palace.
