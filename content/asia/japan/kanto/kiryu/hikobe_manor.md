Hikobe Manor is a restored samurai residence near Kiryu with a history of more than 400 years. It is still associated with descendants of the Hikobe family and is recognized as an important historic site.

The manor is most rewarding for visitors interested in old private houses, samurai-era architecture, and the quieter historic sights around Kiryu.
