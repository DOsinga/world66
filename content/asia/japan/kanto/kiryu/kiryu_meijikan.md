Kiryu Meijikan is an 1878 pseudo-Western building originally used as Gunma Prefecture's public health centre. It now has exhibition rooms and a nostalgic cafe with gramophone music, making it a modest but worthwhile stop for Kiryu's Meiji-era architecture.
