Kurohone History & Folkculture Museum covers local life in the upland Kurohone area north of central Kiryu. The displays focus on everyday customs, regional history, and the silk trade that shaped this part of Gunma.

It is most useful if you are already exploring Kiryu's textile heritage. Look for material on Hoshino Chotaro and Arai Ryoichiro, early Japanese silk-yarn exporters whose factories operated in Kurohone.
