Seki no Magaibutsu is a set of Buddhist figures carved into a boulder near the Fudo Bridge over the Kaburaki River. The Amida Buddha, Kannon Bodhisattva, and Seishi Bodhisattva carvings are protected as a prefectural cultural asset and make a worthwhile short stop for visitors exploring Kiryu's older religious sites.
