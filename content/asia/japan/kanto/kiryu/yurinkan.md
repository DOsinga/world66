Yūrinkan is a group of former warehouses reused as galleries and small cultural spaces in central Kiryu. Its most distinctive draw is the Kiryu Karakuri Ningyo theatre, which preserves the mechanical doll performances once associated with the Tenmangu Shrine Festival.

It fits naturally into a walk through Kiryu's textile and merchant heritage, especially with the nearby Honcho historic district and textile museums.
