Arts Maebashi is the city's contemporary art centre, set in the central shopping district rather than on a museum campus. Its exhibitions change, but the point is consistent: local and contemporary work tied to the city around it.

Check the programme before going. When an exhibition clicks, it gives Maebashi a sharper cultural edge than the city first suggests.
