Boso No Mura is an open-air museum of traditional Japanese culture near Narita. Its reconstructed town street, old houses, samurai residence, and burial mound displays make it more immersive than a standard local museum.

The site is a useful half-day idea if you have extra time around Narita and want something beyond the temple and airport-area stops. Some activities are hands-on, so it also works well for families.
