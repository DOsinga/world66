Taiyuin-byo is the mausoleum of Tokugawa Iemitsu, the third Tokugawa shogun and grandson of Ieyasu. It is smaller and quieter than Toshogu, but richly detailed; visitors can see the main hall and mausoleum from outside, though the tomb itself is not open.
