Toshogu Museum is a small collection of objects connected with Toshogu Shrine and the Tokugawa dynasty. The most memorable exhibit is armor traditionally linked to Tokugawa Ieyasu, shown alongside other shrine treasures and historical material.

It is a compact add-on to the shrine visit rather than a separate half-day museum.
