Aquapark Shinagawa is a popular aquarium near Shinagawa Station, known for combining marine exhibits with light, sound, and dolphin shows. The tunnel tank includes rays and sawfish, including a rare dwarf sawfish display.

It is an easy family stop on the south side of central Tokyo, especially when the weather makes outdoor sightseeing less appealing.
