Asukayama Park is one of Tokyo's classic cherry-blossom spots, with hundreds of sakura trees and a history tied to Tokugawa Yoshimune. It also has several small museums, including the Paper Museum and the Shibusawa Memorial Museum, so it works as more than just a picnic park.
