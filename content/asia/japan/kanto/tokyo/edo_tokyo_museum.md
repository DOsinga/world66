The Edo-Tokyo Museum tells the story of the city from shogunal Edo to modern Tokyo, using large-scale models, reconstructed buildings, and everyday objects rather than dry display cases. The building itself is hard to miss, and the museum pairs well with a Ryogoku sumo outing.
