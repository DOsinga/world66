The Former Iwasaki Family House is a Meiji-era mansion built for the family behind Mitsubishi. Its Western-style main house feels deliberately grand, while the attached Japanese rooms give the visit a quieter domestic side.

The garden is simple, but the house is a useful stop if you are already around Ueno and want a glimpse of elite Tokyo life in the late 19th century.
