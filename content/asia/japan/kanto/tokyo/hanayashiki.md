Hanayashiki is a compact old amusement park beside Senso-ji in Asakusa, with small rides, game booths, and a nostalgic carnival mood. It is not Tokyo's slickest family attraction, but it is the oldest amusement park in Japan and makes an easy add-on if you are already exploring the temple district.
