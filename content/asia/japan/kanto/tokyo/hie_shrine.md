Hie Shrine is a prominent central Tokyo shrine on a small hill near Akasaka. Its grounds are a calm break from the surrounding offices, and the stairway lined with red torii gates is one of its most memorable approaches. The shrine hosts the Sanno Matsuri, counted among Tokyo's major festivals.
