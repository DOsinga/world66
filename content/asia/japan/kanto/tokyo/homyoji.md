Homyoji is an old Buddhist temple associated with Kishimojin, a guardian figure connected with fertility and children. The temple's long history and quiet grounds make it a worthwhile pause in the Ikebukuro area, especially for travellers interested in Tokyo's older religious sites away from the biggest shrine crowds.
