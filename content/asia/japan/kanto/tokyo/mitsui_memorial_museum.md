The Mitsui Memorial Museum is an art museum in a historic 1926 building near Nihonbashi. Its collection is strongest in Japanese tea ceremony objects, ceramics, calligraphy, and East Asian antiques gathered by the Mitsui family.

The museum is a good fit for visitors interested in traditional arts, especially when combined with Tokyo Station, Nihonbashi, or the museum cluster around Marunouchi and the Imperial Palace.
