The Museum of Maritime Science is best known for its ship-shaped building and the preserved icebreaker Soya, Japan's first Antarctic research vessel. The main building has been reduced, but the Soya still gives Odaiba a concrete maritime-history stop rather than another mall or waterfront photo spot.
