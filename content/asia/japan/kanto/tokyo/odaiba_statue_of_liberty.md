Odaiba's Statue of Liberty is a small replica set above Tokyo Bay, with Rainbow Bridge and the Tokyo skyline behind it. It began as a symbol of friendship with France and has become one of the district's easiest photo stops.

Do not expect a major monument. It is worth a few minutes for the view and the odd, very Tokyo collision of New York icon, French symbolism, and bayfront cityscape.
