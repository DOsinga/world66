Tōgō Jinja is a Shinto shrine dedicated to Admiral Tōgō Heihachirō, the naval commander celebrated for Japan's victory at the Battle of Tsushima in 1905. Its calm grounds sit just off Harajuku's busiest streets, making it an easy historical pause between shopping areas and Meiji Shrine.
