The Tokyo Metropolitan Museum of Photography, also known as TOP Museum, is one of Tokyo's best stops for photography. Its galleries rotate through Japanese and international exhibitions, so the focus changes often, but the standard is high.

The museum sits in Ebisu Garden Place, making it easy to pair with Ebisu or nearby Meguro. It is especially worthwhile for visitors interested in contemporary visual culture rather than only the city's larger art museums.
