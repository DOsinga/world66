The Tokyo National Museum of Modern Art, often called MOMAT, is a strong stop for 20th- and 21st-century Japanese art near the Imperial Palace. The permanent collection gives useful context for modern Japanese painting, prints, photography, and design, while temporary exhibitions change regularly. It is compact enough for a focused visit and pairs naturally with Kitanomaru Park or the palace gardens.
