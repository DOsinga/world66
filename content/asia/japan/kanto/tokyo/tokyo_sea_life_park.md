Tokyo Sea Life Park is a large aquarium in Kasai Rinkai Park on Tokyo Bay. Its best-known exhibit is a huge tank where bluefin tuna circle at speed, joined by displays on penguins, seabirds, kelp forests, and marine life from Tokyo Bay and beyond.

The aquarium works well as a family stop, especially when combined with the park, waterfront, and nearby giant Ferris wheel.
