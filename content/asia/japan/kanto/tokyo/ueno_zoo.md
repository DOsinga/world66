Ueno Zoo is Japan's oldest zoo, set inside Ueno Park among Tokyo's densest cluster of museums. It has long been a family stop, and its central location makes it easy to combine with the Tokyo National Museum, the National Museum of Nature and Science, or a walk through the park.

The animal enclosures vary in quality, so it is worth treating the zoo as a convenient family attraction rather than the main reason to visit Ueno.
