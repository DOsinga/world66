Yebisu Brewery Tokyo covers the history of Yebisu beer and the Japan Beer Brewery Company, later part of Sapporo Breweries. The permanent exhibition explains the brewing process and includes a virtual brewery experience, making it a compact stop in Ebisu for beer and industrial-history fans.
