Science Square Tsukuba showcases research from AIST, one of the institutions that gives
Tsukuba its science-city identity. Exhibits introduce robotics, energy, materials,
measurement, and other applied technologies in a compact public-facing setting.

It is best for visitors who want Tsukuba to feel different from a generic suburb or
university town. Pair it with other research-related stops, especially JAXA, to
understand why the city was planned around laboratories.
