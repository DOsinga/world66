Warp Station Edo is an outdoor film backlot used for Japanese historical dramas, including NHK period productions. Visitors can walk through reconstructed Edo-period streets, gates, and buildings that are designed for filming rather than ordinary museum display.

It is best for travellers who enjoy period drama, film sets, or open-air historical reconstructions.
