The Yokohama Anpanman Children’s Museum is a very specific kind of attraction: irresistible if you are travelling with small children who know the character, less essential if you are not. It sits in the Minato Mirai area and mixes character exhibits, play zones, shops, and themed food in a polished, easy-to-manage package.

For families, the practical appeal is obvious. It is indoors, close to other waterfront sights, and designed for children who need hands-on activity rather than another temple or skyline view. Expect crowds on weekends and school holidays, and expect the gift shop to be part of the outing.
