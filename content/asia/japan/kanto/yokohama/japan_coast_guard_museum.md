Japan Coast Guard Museum is a small Minato Mirai museum built around a North Korean spy vessel involved in a 2001 incident in the East China Sea. It is a compact, unusual stop for visitors interested in maritime security, modern history, or the less polished side of Yokohama's waterfront.
