The Kanagawa Museum of Modern Literature sits in the leafy Yamate area, where Yokohama's foreign-settlement history and hilltop parks already make for good wandering. The museum focuses on modern Japanese writers with connections to Kanagawa, using manuscripts, first editions, photographs, and changing exhibitions.

It is a niche stop, best for readers or travelers who like quieter cultural museums. Even if the exhibitions are not fully accessible in English, the setting makes it easy to combine with Harbor View Park, Yamate's old houses, and a slow walk down toward Motomachi.
