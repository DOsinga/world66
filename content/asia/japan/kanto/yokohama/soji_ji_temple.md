Sōji-ji Temple is a temple on the edge of Yokohama, adding a focused stop for travellers who want more than the obvious headline sights. It earns its place here because it gives Yokohama visitors a concrete place to build into a half-day route, whether for culture, scenery, family time, or local history.

It is a calmer counterpoint to the busier commercial districts, and the grounds reward an unhurried visit. Check the official source before you go for current hours, access details, and any booking requirements, then pair it with nearby sights instead of crossing town for a rushed look.
