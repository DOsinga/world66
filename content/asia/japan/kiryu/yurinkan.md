Yūrinkan is a group of former warehouses reused as galleries and performance spaces. Its most distinctive draw is the Kiryu Karakuri Ningyo Theatre, which preserves the city's tradition of elaborate mechanical dolls once performed during Tenmangu Shrine festivals.

The setting suits Kiryu well: old industrial buildings, craft history, and a local art scene that still feels connected to the city's textile-era wealth.
