Rokuroza is the practical counterweight to Arita's display cases. Near Kami-Arita Station, it offers hands-on pottery experiences for visitors who want to try shaping clay rather than only admiring finished porcelain.

It is not the grandest sight in town, but it fits Arita's identity well. If you have children with you, or if museum fatigue sets in, this is the easiest way to turn the porcelain story into something physical.
