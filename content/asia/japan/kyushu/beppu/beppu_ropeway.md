The Beppu Ropeway ascends Mount Tsurumi (1,375 metres) on the western edge of the city, rising from the forested hillside to a summit with panoramic views of Beppu Bay, the city spread below, and the mountainous interior of Kyushu. The journey takes about eight minutes and the views on the way up — over the steaming city and out to sea — are as good as those from the top.

The summit area has walking trails through alpine vegetation and, in late October and November, is one of the best viewpoints in the region for autumn foliage. In winter the summit sometimes has snow while the city below is mild. A restaurant at the top serves Oita Prefecture specialties. The ropeway is on the western edge of the city and requires a bus or taxi from Beppu Station.
