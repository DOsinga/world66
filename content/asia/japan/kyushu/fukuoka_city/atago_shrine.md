Atago Shrine sits on a hill above the Muromi River, looking back over Fukuoka and out toward Hakata Bay. It is considered one of the city's oldest shrines and is tied to Atago worship, but for most visitors the reward is the combination of steps, trees, prayer halls, and broad city views.

This is not as central or as essential as Kushida Shrine, which is why it works best if you are already exploring the western side of town. Go near sunset if the weather is clear. The climb gives enough separation from the city below to make the shrine feel like a small pause rather than another urban stop.
