Fukuoka Asian Art Museum is one of the city's most distinctive museums, focused on modern and contemporary art from across Asia rather than only Japan or the West. That regional lens fits Fukuoka's long role as a gateway city facing the continent.

The museum is a strong central stop, especially if you want an art experience different from the usual national or prefectural collections. Changing exhibitions can be as important as the permanent holdings.
