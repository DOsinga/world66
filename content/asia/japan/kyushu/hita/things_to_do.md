title: Things to Do
type: section
---

Hita rewards a day that mixes old-town wandering with one trip into the hills. Start in [Mameda-machi](#mameda_machi), stop at [Kangien Academy](#kangien_academy) for the town's scholar-merchant history, then go out to [Onta Pottery Village](#onta_pottery_village) if you have a car or are willing to work with rural buses.

[Sapporo Beer Kyushu Hita Brewery](#sapporo_beer_kyushu_hita_brewery) is less old-world, but it fits the town's water story and gives beer drinkers a concrete reason to linger.
