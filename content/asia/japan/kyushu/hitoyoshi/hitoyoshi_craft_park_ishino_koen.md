Hitoyoshi Craft Park Ishino Koen gathers several local craft workshops in a park setting, including cutlery, shochu production, paper, carpentry, ceramics, and tea. Some activities are demonstrations; others are hands-on.

It is a good stop if you want the town's craft and shochu identity to become more concrete than a souvenir shelf.
