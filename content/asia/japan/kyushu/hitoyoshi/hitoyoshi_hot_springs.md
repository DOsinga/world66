Hitoyoshi's hot springs are the easiest reason to linger after sightseeing. The baths are known for simple bicarbonate spring water, and many inns cluster near the station and along the Kuma River.

For a traveller, this is less about a single famous bathhouse and more about ending a river-town day properly.
