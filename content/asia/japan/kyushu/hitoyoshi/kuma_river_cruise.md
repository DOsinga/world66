The Kuma River cruise sends old-style boats downstream from central Hitoyoshi. The ride is mostly relaxed, with commentary in Japanese and enough small rapids to keep it from feeling like a simple ferry.

It is the best way to make the river more than background scenery, especially in clear weather.
