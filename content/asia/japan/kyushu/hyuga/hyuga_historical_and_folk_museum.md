Hyuga Historical and Folk Museum occupies a former wealthy merchant's house in Mimitsu. Its exhibits show local life during the Edo period, and the old house itself is part of the appeal.

English explanations are limited, so it is easiest to enjoy as a historic-house visit paired with a walk through the Mimitsu historic district.
