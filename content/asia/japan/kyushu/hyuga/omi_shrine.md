Omi Shrine is not large, but it has one of Hyuga's best settings. The buildings sit above rough volcanic rocks with the Pacific just beyond, so the visit feels half shrine, half coastal viewpoint.

Look for the boulders and the small cave shrine below the main grounds, but take the steep steps carefully. It is an easy add-on to Isegahama beach and the Cape Hyuga viewpoints.
