Karatsu is a sea-facing city in northern Saga Prefecture, close enough to [Fukuoka](/asia/japan/kyushu/fukuoka_city) for an easy day trip but different in mood. It was once a port looking toward Korea and China, and that old maritime role still shows in the castle, pottery, fishing villages, and seafood culture.

The compact centre has [Karatsu Castle](/asia/japan/kyushu/karatsu/karatsu_castle), a reconstructed keep with a fine view over the bay. East of town, [Niji no Matsubara](/asia/japan/kyushu/karatsu/niji_no_matsubara) runs along the coast in a long belt of black pines. Farther out, [Nanatsugama](/asia/japan/kyushu/karatsu/nanatsugama) cuts sea caves into basalt cliffs, while [Nagoya Castle Ruins](/asia/japan/kyushu/karatsu/nagoya_castle_ruins) recalls the huge military staging ground for Toyotomi Hideyoshi's invasions of Korea.

Karatsu also has a softer side: Karatsu ware, squid in Yobuko, and the riotous Karatsu Kunchi floats every November. It is worth more than a transit stop between Fukuoka and Nagasaki if you like coastal Japan with history in the background.
