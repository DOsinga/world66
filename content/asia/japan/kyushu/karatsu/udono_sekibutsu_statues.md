The Udono Sekibutsu Statues are stone Buddha carvings set in a cave outside Karatsu. Local tradition links the site with Kukai after his return from China, and the quiet setting makes it a worthwhile detour for travellers interested in old religious sites rather than castle-town sightseeing alone.
