---
title: Things to Do
type: section
---

Kashima's best day is compact but distinctive. Give the most time to [Yutoku Inari Shrine](#yutoku_inari_shrine), then walk the preserved sake-brewing streets of [Hizen Hamashuku](#hizen_hamashuku). [Kashima Castle Ruins](#kashima_castle_ruins) add a quiet park stop, while the [Kashima Gatalympics](#kashima_gatalympics) are worth timing a visit for if you are in Saga in late spring.
