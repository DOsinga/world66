Tanga Market (Tanga Ichiba) in central Kokura is one of Kitakyushu's most atmospheric local experiences — a covered market with over 100 stalls selling fresh fish, vegetables, pickles, tofu, prepared foods, and street snacks. The market has been in continuous operation for over a century and retains the density and character of a genuine working market rather than a tourist reconstruction.

The best time to visit is mid-morning when the fishmongers and produce vendors are in full swing. The market's eating stalls — particularly those selling hormone-yaki (grilled offal) and local-style oden — are as good an introduction to Kitakyushu's food culture as any restaurant. The station for the Kitakyushu monorail is immediately above the market entrance, making access straightforward from Kokura Station.
