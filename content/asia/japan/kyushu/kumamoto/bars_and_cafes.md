---
title: Bars and Cafes
type: section
---

Kumamoto's drinking scene runs from polished hotel bars to standing-only sake counters tucked into the alleys off Shimotōri. The local sake — particularly the dry, slightly austere style of the Kuma region — is excellent and the prefecture also makes its own shōchū from rice. Look for the small specialist sake bars (called *kakuuchi*) where you stand at the counter and the master pours from a row of bottles. The Yobukodōri alley between Shimotōri and the river is the densest concentration of small bars in central Kumamoto.
