---
title: Shopping
type: section
---

The covered arcades of [Shimotōri](/asia/japan/kyushu/kumamoto/shimotori_arcade) and Kamitōri are Kumamoto's main shopping spine, with the Tsuruya department store as the city's flagship. For local souvenirs — karashi renkon, basashi sausages, Higo-zōgan damascene metalwork, locally distilled shōchū — the [Sakuranobaba Josaien](/asia/japan/kyushu/kumamoto/sakuranobaba_josaien) complex at the castle is the most convenient one-stop. For Kumamon merchandise of every imaginable kind, head to [Kumamon Square](/asia/japan/kyushu/kumamoto/kumamoto_station_kuro_kamon) inside the Tsuruya.
