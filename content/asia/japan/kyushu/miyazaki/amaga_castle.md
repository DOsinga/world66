Amaga Castle is a reconstructed castle keep with a small museum on local history. The site gives a useful look at the older fortified landscape around Miyazaki, away from the city's better-known shrines and coastal sights.

The castle is best for travellers with a specific interest in regional history or those already exploring the northern side of Miyazaki.
