Miyakoh Botanic Garden Aoshima, also called Aoshima Subtropical Botanical Garden, is a relaxed garden near Aoshima's coast and shrine. The subtropical planting and greenhouse make it an easy add-on to a visit to the island, especially in hot or rainy weather.
