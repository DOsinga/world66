The Miyazaki Prefectural Museum of Nature and History covers the prefecture's landscapes, wildlife, archaeology, and folk culture. It is not flashy, but it helps explain the wider region if your trip is taking you beyond the city.

Behind the museum are relocated traditional houses from elsewhere in Miyazaki Prefecture. They are worth including if you are already visiting Miyazaki-jingu next door.
