Sadowara Castle was expanded by the Ito clan and used as a local residence during the feudal period. One palace building has been reconstructed as the Sadowara History and Folk Museum, giving the site more substance than a simple castle ruin.

The grounds make a worthwhile short detour for visitors interested in regional history around Miyazaki.
