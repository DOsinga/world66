---
title: "Eating Out"
type: section
---

Moji's signature dish is **yaki curry** — Japanese curry rice topped with cheese and egg and baked in a small ceramic dish until the top browns. A dozen restaurants around Mojiko Retro serve it; **Pre Mier** and **Berriini** are reliable standbys. Expect to queue at weekends.

Fresh **fugu (blowfish)**, the celebrated dish of Shimonoseki just across the strait, also appears on local menus in winter — the season runs October to March. **Kaikyo Plaza** at the harbour has several seafood restaurants worth trying.

For a quick lunch, the food court inside Kaikyo Plaza covers udon, ramen, and bento at modest prices.
