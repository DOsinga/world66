---
title: "Eating Out"
type: section
---

Nagasaki's food is unlike anywhere else in Japan. Centuries of trade with China and the Netherlands produced fusion dishes that became local staples: **champon** (a noodle soup of Chinese origin invented here in 1899), **sara udon** (crispy noodles with thick sauce), **Turkish rice** (a single plate with pilaf, spaghetti, and tonkatsu), and **chawanmushi** served as a full meal rather than a side. Castella sponge cake, brought by Portuguese missionaries in the 16th century, is the city's signature sweet.
