Hashima — known as Gunkanjima or 'Battleship Island' for its silhouette — is a concrete husk in the open sea about an hour by boat from Nagasaki. The abandoned coal mining city is the most evocative ruin in Japan: tower blocks crumbling into the ocean, stairways disappearing into sky. A UNESCO World Heritage site since 2015. Tours run from Nagasaki Port; landing requires calm seas and a small fee, and visitors are restricted to a fixed walkway. Several operators offer the trip — book at least a day in advance, more in summer. Even a cruise that only circles the island is striking.
