The hill rising 333 metres above the harbour gives you the city's defining panorama. Tram lines, river bends, and lights climbing the surrounding hills are laid out below you, with the curve of the harbour reaching out toward the East China Sea. The ropeway from Fuchi Shrine carries you to the top in five minutes; the alternative is the winding road by taxi or car. Best at twilight. The summit has a free observation deck, a restaurant, and a coin-operated radar that lets you photograph the city through a tinted lens.
