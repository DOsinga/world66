---

The museum traces the events of 9 August 1945 with directness and restraint. The exhibits start with the city as it was on the morning of the bombing, move through the detonation and immediate aftermath, then expand to the broader story of nuclear weapons and disarmament. The objects are what stay with you: the melted helmet of a thirteen-year-old, the scorched lunchbox with its rice still inside, the steel girders twisted like ribbon. A timed walk-through takes about 90 minutes; allow more if you watch the survivor testimony videos. Combine with the National Peace Memorial Hall next door and the Peace Park across the road.
