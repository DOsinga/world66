After the bombing, Dr Takashi Nagai — a Catholic doctor and radiologist already dying of leukaemia from his medical work — built himself a two-tatami-mat hut and lived there in voluntary poverty until his death in 1951. He wrote a series of bestselling books from this hut, including **The Bells of Nagasaki**, which gave the world its first serious account of the bombing from the ground. The tiny wooden hermitage still stands and a small adjoining museum tells his story. It is a quiet, deeply moving counterpoint to the official memorials a few hundred metres south.
