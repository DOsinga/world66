The oldest standing church in Japan, built in 1864 for the foreign community of the open port and dedicated to the 26 martyrs crucified in 1597. The white-painted, three-spired Gothic Revival exterior sits at the top of a stone staircase just below Glover Garden — a memorable approach. The interior is more austere than the front facade suggests, with imported stained glass that survived the bomb intact, well outside the blast radius. A UNESCO World Heritage site and a National Treasure of Japan.
