The chief Shinto shrine of the city, founded in 1614 partly as an instrument of state suppression of Christianity — local residents were required to register here. The approach climbs 277 stone steps up a wooded slope, with successive torii gates marking the ascent. The shrine sits in a tranquil grove at the top, with views back over the city. Suwa is the focus of the **Kunchi Festival** (7–9 October), Nagasaki's biggest event, when neighbourhood troupes perform dragon dances and Dutch-themed parade floats here over three days.
