Sasebo is a working harbour city on the west coast of Kyushu, shaped by shipyards, the Japanese navy, and a long American base presence. It is less polished than Nagasaki, but it has a real port-town character: steep streets, sea views, jazz bars, burger shops, and ferries moving between the islands.

The reason to come is the coast. [Kujukushima](/asia/japan/kyushu/sasebo/kujukushima_pearl_sea_resort), the "99 islands" scattered off Sasebo, is one of western Kyushu's best seascapes. Cruise boats leave from Pearl Sea Resort, while viewpoints such as [Tenkaiho](/asia/japan/kyushu/sasebo/tenkaiho_observatory) show the islands as a pattern of green humps and blue channels.

Sasebo also has Japan's oddest Dutch day out: [Huis Ten Bosch](/asia/japan/kyushu/sasebo/huis_ten_bosch), a full-scale Netherlands-themed park with canals, brick facades, gardens, and seasonal illuminations. It is artificial, but on its own terms it is impressively built. Combine that with a harbour walk and a Sasebo burger and the city easily justifies a spare day.
