Tenkaiho is the cleanest land view of the Kujukushima islands: a broad lookout where the bay breaks into dozens of wooded islets. It is especially good in late afternoon, and the flower fields below the viewpoint add colour in spring and autumn. If you only have time for one Sasebo viewpoint, make it this one.
