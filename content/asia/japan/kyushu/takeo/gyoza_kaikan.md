Gyoza Kaikan is the kind of local place that makes a small town easier to like: quick, casual, and built around gyoza and ramen rather than ceremony. It is a good lunch stop if you are moving between the station side and the outer sights.

Do not expect polish. The appeal is the everyday energy, low prices, and the sense that you are eating where residents actually eat.
