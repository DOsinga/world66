Mount Miyanoura is the highest summit in Kyushu at 1,936 metres, rising steeply from the forested interior of Yakushima. The ascent is typically combined with neighbouring peaks Nagata-dake and Kuromi-dake on a traverse of the island's high ridge. Most hikers approach from Yodogawa trailhead (reached by bus from Miyanoura port) and complete the circuit in a long day or over two days with a night at the Shintakatsubo refuge hut.

The vegetation changes dramatically with altitude. The lower slopes are dense laurel forest; higher up, the forest opens to dwarf bamboo and scrubby yakusugi cedars twisted by the wind; the summit itself is bare granite, with views to the sea on clear days. Cloud and mist are frequent, and the weather changes rapidly — pack rain gear regardless of the forecast. The traverse route is demanding but well-marked with mountain huts at strategic intervals.
