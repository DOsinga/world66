Yoko Gushiken Memorial Hall is a small museum devoted to Ishigaki's world champion boxer, who held the WBA light-flyweight title from 1976 to 1981. The displays include belts, trophies, boxing gear, and video from his fights, making it a focused stop for visitors interested in local sporting heroes.
