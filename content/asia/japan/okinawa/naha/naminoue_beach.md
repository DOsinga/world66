Naminoue Beach is Naha's central city beach, just below Naminoue Shrine. It is not Okinawa's prettiest stretch of sand, but it is clean, easy to reach and useful if you want a quick swim without leaving the city.

The swimming area can be limited by season and weather, so check the conditions before planning around it.
