Diamond Beach is a small Okinawa Island beach known more for its snorkeling than for its sand. The water has plenty of fish and bright coral, making it a worthwhile stop for confident snorkelers.

Access is by a staircase near the Okashi Goten shop, and the access point closes in the early evening. Go in daylight and check local sea conditions before entering the water.
