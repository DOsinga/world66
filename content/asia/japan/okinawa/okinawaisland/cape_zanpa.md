Cape Zanpa is a rocky headland on the west coast of Okinawa Island, known for its lighthouse, sea cliffs, and wide views over the East China Sea. The cape is close to Yomitan and pairs well with Zakimi Castle Ruins or the beaches along the central coast.

Come for a short coastal walk, sunset views, and a break from the busier resort strips. The lighthouse and open grassy areas make the cape easy to visit even on a relaxed driving day.
