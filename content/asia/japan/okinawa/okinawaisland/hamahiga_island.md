Hamahiga Island lies off Okinawa's east coast, reached by road via the Kaichu-doro Sea Road and bridge links through the surrounding islands. It is small, quiet, and known for coastal rock formations, some weathered into strange mushroom-like shapes.

Come as part of a drive around the Katsuren Peninsula and the east-coast islands. The pleasure is in the sea road, village pace, and short coastal stops rather than a single headline sight.
