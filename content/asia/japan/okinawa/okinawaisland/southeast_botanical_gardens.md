Southeast Botanical Gardens is a large plant garden in central Okinawa, divided between water gardens and tropical botanical displays. It is a gentle stop for seeing island flora without making a full nature excursion.

The gardens also host seasonal events and night illuminations, which can make them more appealing outside the heat of the day. It is most useful for families or travellers based in central Okinawa with a car.
