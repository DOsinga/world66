Yugafukan is a small visitor museum run by the national parks authority on Taketomi Island. Much of the written information is in Japanese, but the short film and audio material on Yaeyama stories and songs still make it worthwhile.

Use it as context before wandering the village or heading to the island's beaches. It helps explain why Taketomi's landscape, music, and old house styles matter.
