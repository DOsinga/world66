Yunnu Rakuen is a subtropical garden with Yoron, Okinawan, and Southeast Asian plants gathered into a small private paradise. The name joins Yunnu, the island's own name for itself, with rakuen, or paradise.

It is a gentle stop rather than a major sight. Come for flowers, shade, and the feeling that the island's warm, humid edge has been collected into one garden.
