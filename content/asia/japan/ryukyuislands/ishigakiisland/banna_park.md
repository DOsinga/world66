Banna Park spreads across the hills north of Ishigaki's town center, with forest paths, rest pavilions, playgrounds, and viewpoints over much of the island. It is an easy place to trade beach time for greenery, birds, and wide views.

The park is especially useful if you have a car, since the lookouts are scattered. Seishika Bridge and the nearby viewing point make a good short stop, with the bridge, lake, and island hills all in one frame.
