---
title: Beaches
type: section
---

Miyako Island has more beach per capita than almost anywhere in Japan. The main ones are well-known; the lesser ones — small coves tucked between limestone outcrops — reward the cyclist willing to explore.

The island's three longest and most beautiful beaches are [Yonaha Maehama](/asia/japan/ryukyuislands/miyakoisland/yonaha_maehama_beach) on the southwest coast, [Sunayama](/asia/japan/ryukyuislands/miyakoisland/sunayama_beach) on the northwest, and [Yoshino](/asia/japan/ryukyuislands/miyakoisland/yoshino_beach) on the east. Each is different: Yonaha Maehama for swimming and lounging, Sunayama for scenery and its dramatic stone arch, Yoshino for snorkelling. On Irabu Island, accessible via the [Irabu Ohashi bridge](/asia/japan/ryukyuislands/miyakoisland/irabu_ohashi_bridge), Toguchi-no-Hama beach is quieter than any of the Miyako beaches and just as beautiful.
