Araha Beach is a long sandy stretch on Chatan's coast, backed by a park with lawns, basketball courts, barbecue areas, and a large pirate-ship playground. It is an easy beach for families, with more space to linger than the small urban coves closer to Naha.

The appeal is simple: swimming, sunset walks, and a relaxed local park atmosphere. Parking is free, but weekends and warm evenings can get busy.
