Busena Marine Park is a small marine attraction on the Busena peninsula, known for its underwater observation tower and glass-bottom boat trips. It gives visitors a way to see reef fish and clear Okinawan water without diving or snorkelling.

Boats normally run frequently in good weather, and a shuttle links the main facilities on the peninsula. It works well as a low-effort family stop on a north Okinawa coast drive.
