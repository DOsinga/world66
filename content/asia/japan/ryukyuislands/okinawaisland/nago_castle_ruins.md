Nago Castle Ruins are more a hilltop park and viewpoint than a complete castle site, but the walk up is pleasant and the views over Nago and the northern Okinawa coast are the reward. The site is also one of Japan's noted cherry blossom spots, with Okinawa's early blooms appearing in winter.

Visit for the setting rather than intact ruins, and combine it with other northern Okinawa stops such as Nakijin Castle Ruins or the Motobu Peninsula.
