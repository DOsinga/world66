Naminoue Shrine sits on a low coral cliff above the sea in the Wakasa district of Naha, a ten-minute walk from the city centre. As the ichinomiya — the ranking shrine — of Okinawa Prefecture, it holds a special significance in the local religious calendar and attracts worshippers throughout the year, particularly around New Year.

The shrine was rebuilt after its destruction in 1945 and the grounds are compact and peaceful despite the urban setting. The cliff edge provides views over the beach below — Naminoue Beach, a popular if small city beach — and out to the East China Sea. The approach through a forested path gives a welcome break from the noise of the city.
