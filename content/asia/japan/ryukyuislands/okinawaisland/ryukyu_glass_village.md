Ryukyu Glass Village is a craft workshop and showroom where visitors can watch Okinawan glass being blown and try simple hands-on projects. The finished pieces lean bright and colourful, with the bubbles and recycled-glass texture associated with the local style.

Book ahead if you want to make your own glassware or accessories. Otherwise, it works as a short stop while exploring southern Okinawa Island.
