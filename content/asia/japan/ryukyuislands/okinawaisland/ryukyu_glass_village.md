Ryukyu Glass Village is a craft center where visitors can watch and try glassmaking, including small items such as dishes, vases, jewelry, and candles.

It is a practical stop in southern Okinawa for travelers interested in local crafts or looking for a hands-on family activity. Some experiences require reservations.
