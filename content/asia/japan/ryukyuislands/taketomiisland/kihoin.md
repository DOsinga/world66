Kihōin is a small temple and local history museum on Taketomi Island, often described as Japan's southernmost temple. Its collection gathers historical objects, island crafts, and cultural material from around Taketomi.

The displays are modest and English explanation is limited, but the stop adds useful context if you are already exploring the village by bicycle. It is most worthwhile for visitors interested in Yaeyama culture beyond the beaches and traditional streets.
