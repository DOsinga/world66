Tokashiki is the largest of the Kerama Islands, a chain of small islands about 30 kilometres west of Naha in Okinawa. The island is known for its clear water, coral reefs, and white-sand beaches — [Aharen Beach](/asia/japan/ryukyuislands/tokashikiisland/aharen_beach) on the south coast and [Tokashiku Beach](/asia/japan/ryukyuislands/tokashikiisland/tokashiku_beach) on the west are the main draws.

In winter (January through March), humpback whales migrate to the warm waters around the Kerama Islands for breeding. [Whale-watching boat trips](/asia/japan/ryukyuislands/tokashikiisland/whale_watching) run from the island and from Naha, and sightings are reliable during peak season. The rest of the year, the main activities are [snorkelling and scuba diving](/asia/japan/ryukyuislands/tokashikiisland/scuba_diving) at some of Japan's finest reefs, and simply sitting on some of the best beaches in Okinawa.

The island is small and quiet, with only a few hundred permanent residents. There is a campsite behind Aharen Beach for budget travellers, and several minshuku (family-run guesthouses) in both Aharen and Tokashiki village.
