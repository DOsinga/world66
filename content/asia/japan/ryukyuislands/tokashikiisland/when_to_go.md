---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

Tokashiki Island is good year-round, but the reason to visit changes by season.

**January to March** is whale-watching season, the single best reason to visit in winter. Water temperatures drop to around 18–20°C (wetsuit recommended for snorkelling), but whale sightings are reliable and the island is quiet.

**April to June** offers excellent diving and snorkelling conditions — water warming, visibility high, crowds absent. Cherry blossoms bloom early in the Ryukyus (late January to February), before the rest of Japan.

**July to September** is peak season. The beaches are at their best, water is warm (28–29°C), and conditions are ideal for everything aquatic. The island fills up with Japanese holiday-makers; book accommodation well ahead.

**October to December** is quieter, with comfortable temperatures (22–25°C) and clear water. Typhoon risk persists through October; check forecasts if visiting early in the period.
