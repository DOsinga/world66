Sanninudai is a rocky coastal viewpoint on Yonaguni Island, known for strange stepped formations and views toward Gunkan-iwa. Some visitors connect the shapes here with Yonaguni's much-debated underwater monument, though the appeal on land is the rugged scenery and odd geology.
