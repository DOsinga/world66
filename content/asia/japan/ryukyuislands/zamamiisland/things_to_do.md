---
title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Zamami's draws are almost entirely natural. [Furuzamami Beach](/asia/japan/ryukyuislands/zamamiisland/furuzamami_beach) on the south side is one of Japan's finest white-sand beaches with excellent snorkelling directly from shore. [Ama Beach](/asia/japan/ryukyuislands/zamamiisland/ama_beach) on the western side is quieter and famous for sea turtle sightings. From January to March, [whale watching](/asia/japan/ryukyuislands/zamamiisland/whale_watching) is the main event — humpback whales breed in the Kerama Straits and boats go out daily from the port. The [Kaminohama Observatory](/asia/japan/ryukyuislands/zamamiisland/kaminohama_observatory) gives panoramic views over the southern coastline and the uninhabited islands of Gahi and Agenashiku. [Diving and snorkelling](/asia/japan/ryukyuislands/zamamiisland/diving_and_snorkelling) in the Kerama National Park waters around the island offer some of the best coral reef access in Japan.
