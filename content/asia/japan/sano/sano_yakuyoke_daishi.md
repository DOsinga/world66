Sano Yakuyoke Daishi is the city's best-known temple, formally Soshuji. It is famous for prayers against misfortune and gets very busy around hatsumode, the first shrine or temple visit of the New Year.

Outside those peak days it is an easy central stop, useful for understanding Sano's local identity before heading into the hills or sitting down to ramen.
