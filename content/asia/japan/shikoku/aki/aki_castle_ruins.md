Aki Castle was once the local power base in eastern Tosa, positioned for conflict toward Awa Province. The keep is gone, but the remaining stonework, moats, and site layout still explain why this spot mattered.

It pairs naturally with the Samurai Quarters nearby. Do both together and the old town makes more sense.
