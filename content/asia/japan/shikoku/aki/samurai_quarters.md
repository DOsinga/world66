The Doi area preserves Aki's old samurai quarter, with earthen walls, clipped hedges, narrow lanes, and one residence open to visitors. Nothing shouts for attention, which is part of the appeal.

Walk slowly and look at the street pattern. This is the kind of small historic district that works because it still feels embedded in an ordinary town.
