Ehime occupies the north-western side of [Shikoku](/asia/japan/shikoku), facing the Seto Inland Sea and climbing south into rugged mountains. It is quieter than the Kansai tourist circuit but deeply rewarding: original castles, old hot springs, citrus orchards, pilgrimage temples, island bridges, and small towns that still feel lived in.

[Matsuyama](/asia/japan/shikoku/matsuyama) is the natural base. Its hilltop castle and [Dogo Onsen](/asia/japan/shikoku/ehime/dogo_onsen) give Ehime two of Shikoku's most memorable sights within one tram ride. South of the capital, [Ozu Castle](/asia/japan/shikoku/ehime/ozu_castle) and [Uchiko](/asia/japan/shikoku/ehime/uchiko_old_town) show the old merchant and castle-town side of Iyo province, while [Uwajima](/asia/japan/shikoku/uwajima) feels more maritime and remote.

The prefecture also has a wilder edge. [Mount Ishizuchi](/asia/japan/shikoku/ehime/mount_ishizuchi), western Japan's highest peak, draws hikers and pilgrims, and the [Shimanami Kaido](/asia/japan/shikoku/ehime/shimanami_kaido) links Ehime's islands to Honshu by one of Japan's great cycling routes. Ehime is not a place to rush; it rewards slow trains, local ferries, and a willingness to stop for mandarins, sea bream, and a hot bath.

## Destinations in Ehime

- [Matsuyama](/asia/japan/shikoku/matsuyama) — castle, Dogo Onsen, museums, and the main transport hub
- [Uwajima](/asia/japan/shikoku/uwajima) — small castle town with a strong southern Ehime identity
- [Shimanami Kaido](/asia/japan/shikoku/ehime/shimanami_kaido) — island-hopping cycling route toward Honshu
- [Uchiko](/asia/japan/shikoku/ehime/uchiko_old_town) — preserved merchant streets and kabuki theatre
- [Ozu](/asia/japan/shikoku/ehime/ozu_castle) — riverside castle town south-west of Matsuyama
