---
title: Getting Around
type: section
---

JR trains link Matsuyama with Imabari, Iyo-Ozu, and Uwajima, though services slow down as you head south. Buses cover smaller towns and mountain areas, but schedules can be thin.

For the Shimanami Kaido, rent a bicycle in Imabari or on the Honshu side at Onomichi. For Mount Ishizuchi or scattered pilgrimage temples, a car gives the most freedom.
