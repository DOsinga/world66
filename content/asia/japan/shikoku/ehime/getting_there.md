---
title: Getting There
type: section
---

Matsuyama Airport has domestic flights from Tokyo, Osaka, Fukuoka, and other Japanese cities. By rail, limited express trains run from [Okayama](/asia/japan/honshu/okayama) across the Seto Ohashi Bridge and along Shikoku's north coast to Matsuyama.

Ferries connect Matsuyama with Hiroshima and other Inland Sea ports. Cyclists often enter or leave Ehime via the Shimanami Kaido between Onomichi and Imabari.
