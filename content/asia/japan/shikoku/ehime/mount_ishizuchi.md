Mount Ishizuchi is the highest mountain in western Japan and Ehime's great inland adventure. It is both a hiking destination and a sacred peak, with shrines, forest trails, and sharp ridges that feel far removed from the citrus coast.

The standard season runs from spring through autumn, with autumn colour especially popular. Weather can be serious near the summit, so carry layers and do not treat the chain sections casually.
