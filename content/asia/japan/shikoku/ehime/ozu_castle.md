Ozu Castle rises above a bend in the Hijikawa River, giving the small city a properly romantic profile. The town below has old merchant houses, riverside walks, and a slower rhythm than Matsuyama.

It is a good day trip if you like compact castle towns. Combine the castle with Garyu Sanso villa and a wander through the old streets.
