Uchiko preserves the merchant-town side of Ehime, with white-walled houses, wax-trade history, and the handsome Uchiko-za theatre. It is smaller and quieter than better-known historic towns, which is exactly why it is pleasant.

Come for a slow walk rather than a checklist. The old streets, craft shops, and theatre visit work well as a half-day from Matsuyama or a stop on the way to Ozu.
