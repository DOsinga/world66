Uwajima Castle is one of Japan's twelve surviving original castle keeps, small but deeply atmospheric. It sits on a wooded hill above [Uwajima](/asia/japan/shikoku/uwajima), reached by a short climb through stone walls and trees.

The keep is modest compared with Matsuyama's, but the authenticity matters. Uwajima itself is far enough south to feel like a different Shikoku, with fishing-port energy, bullfighting traditions, and sea bream on the table.
