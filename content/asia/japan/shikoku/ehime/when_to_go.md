---
title: When to Go
type: section
---

Spring and autumn are the best seasons for Ehime, with mild weather, good cycling conditions, and comfortable castle-town walking. Cherry blossoms around Matsuyama Castle and Ozu are especially handsome.

Summer is hot and humid on the coast but workable if you pace yourself. Mountain hikes around Ishizuchi are cooler, though weather can change quickly. Winter is quiet, with snow possible in the high interior.
