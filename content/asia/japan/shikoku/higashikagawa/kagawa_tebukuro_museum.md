The Kagawa Tebukuro Museum explains why this quiet city became Japan's glove capital. Exhibits cover more than a century of glove production, with old sewing machines, sports gloves, factory tools, and examples from local makers.

It is a niche museum, but a good one: the subject is specific, the craft is still alive, and the displays make Higashikagawa feel less anonymous than it first appears.
