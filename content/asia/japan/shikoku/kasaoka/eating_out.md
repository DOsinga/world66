Kasaoka ramen is the local dish to look for. It usually means a clear chicken-based broth with firm noodles and slices of simmered chicken instead of the pork chashu common elsewhere in Japan. The style grew around a port town with sailors, market workers, and quick meals, and it is still the easiest way to taste something specific to Kasaoka.
