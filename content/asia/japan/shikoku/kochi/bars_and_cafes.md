---
title: "Bars and Cafes"
type: section
---

Kōchi is a drinking city by reputation, and the *izakayas* around the Obiyamachi covered arcade and the streets behind Hirome Market are the place to experience this. The local custom of *bekupai* — a game played at bars that involves forcing the loser to drain their glass — has deep roots here. The local Tosa sake (particularly from Tosaturu and Suigei breweries) is excellent. *Yuzu* — the aromatic Kōchi citrus — flavours sours, *shochu* infusions, and craft beers.
