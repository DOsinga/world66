Kochi People's and Liberty Museum is a small history museum focused on rights, freedom, and social movements in the old Tosa domain and modern Kochi. It gives useful background to the politics and reformist figures that shaped the region beyond the better-known castle and Sakamoto Ryoma sites.

The museum is best for visitors with a specific interest in local history or Japanese political culture. Pair it with Kochi Castle, Hirome Ichiba, or the Ryoma-related sights rather than treating it as a stand-alone half-day stop.
