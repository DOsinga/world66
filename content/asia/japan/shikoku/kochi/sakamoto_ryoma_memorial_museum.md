The Sakamoto Ryoma Memorial Museum stands above Katsurahama and focuses on the Tosa-born activist who helped push Japan from the shogunate toward the Meiji Restoration. Exhibits include letters, documents, and special displays that give useful context before or after visiting Ryoma's statue on the coast below.
