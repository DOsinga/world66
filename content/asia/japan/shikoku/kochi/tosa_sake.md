Kōchi prefecture produces some of Japan's most respected sake, distinguished by its dry, clean style — *karakuchi* rather than the sweeter *amakuchi* of some other regions. The climate and the soft water of the Shikoku mountains suit sake brewing, and the city's culture of serious drinking has always given local brewers a ready audience.

The two names to know are **Suigei** (literally "drunk whale" — a drinking reference) and **Tosaturu** ("Tosa crane"), both available at izakayas throughout the city. At Hirome Market you can try several Kōchi sakes side by side from different vendors. A few liquor shops near the arcade sell bottles to take away, including aged *koshu* varieties and limited-run seasonal releases. The local *yuzu* sake and *yuzu* shochu are also worth seeking out.
