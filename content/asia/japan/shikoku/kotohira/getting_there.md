---
title: "Getting There"
type: section
---

Kotohira is on the JR Dosan line about an hour southwest of [Takamatsu](/asia/japan/shikoku/takamatsu) by Limited Express, slightly longer on the all-stations local. The town has two stations — JR Kotohira and the neighbouring Kotoden Kotohira — sitting side by side a five-minute walk from the foot of the shrine steps. The Kotoden private line runs to Takamatsu via the *kotoden-kotohira line*, a scenic small-train ride with frequent service.

From [Okayama](/asia/japan/honshu/okayama) on the main island, take the Marine Liner across the Seto Ohashi Bridge to Takamatsu (40 minutes) and connect there, or take the direct Limited Express *Shiokaze* from Okayama which stops at Kotohira (about 70 minutes). No bullet train serves Shikoku.
