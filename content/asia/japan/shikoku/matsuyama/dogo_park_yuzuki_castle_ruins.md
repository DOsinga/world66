Dogo Park surrounds the remains of Yuzuki Castle, once the seat of the Kono clan. It is a calm stop near Dogo Onsen, with short paths, reconstructed earthworks, and a small dose of local history. In spring it becomes one of the area's busier cherry-blossom spots.
