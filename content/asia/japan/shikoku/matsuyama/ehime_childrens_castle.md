Ehime Children's Castle is a large family attraction near Tobe Zoo, with play zones spread across a broad site. It includes slides, rides, workshops, and outdoor areas aimed at children.

The scale makes it more of a planned family outing than a quick playground stop, especially for visitors travelling with younger children.
