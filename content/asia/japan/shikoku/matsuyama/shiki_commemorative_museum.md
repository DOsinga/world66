The Shiki Commemorative Museum in the Dogo area is devoted to Masaoka Shiki, the Matsuyama-born poet who helped shape modern haiku. Its collections cover his life, writing, artwork, and the literary culture around him, making it a good pairing with Dogo Onsen and nearby shrines.
