Nakayama Senmaida is a patchwork of small rice terraces in Shodoshima's inland hills. It is not a grand monument, but it adds a rural counterpoint to the island's ports and beaches.

Come when the paddies are flooded or green if you can. The valley is also linked with local kabuki traditions, which helps explain why this small farming area matters more than its size suggests.
