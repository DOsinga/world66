An open-air architectural museum at the foot of Yashima, displaying about thirty traditional buildings collected from across the four prefectures of Shikoku and rebuilt on a wooded hillside site. Farmhouses, a kabuki theatre, a soy sauce brewery, a lighthouse keeper's cottage, a sugar mill — most are accessible inside, all are clearly labelled, and the layout is set up as a long walking loop that takes about ninety minutes.

The English signage is good, and a recently added gallery designed by the architect Tadao Ando shows changing exhibitions of folk art. Closed Wednesdays; substantial entrance fee but excellent value for the time you spend inside.
