---
title: "Festivals"
type: section
---

Tokushima's calendar is dominated by one event so large it eclipses everything else.

The **Awa Odori** runs from 12 to 15 August every year and is the single largest dance festival in Japan, drawing more than a million spectators to a city of 250,000. Some 80,000 dancers in organised troupes called *ren* parade through the central streets each evening from dusk until 22:30, accompanied by shamisen, flutes, and the unmistakable two-beat drum of the *narimono* ensemble. Each ren has its own costume, choreography, and chant — the men in cotton happi coats and headbands, the women in straw hats tilted forward to obscure the face.

Reserved seating in the main paid arenas (Shiyakusho-mae, Aibahama, Minami-Uchimachi, Ryogoku) sells out months in advance; the free outdoor performance areas are first-come, first-served. Accommodation across the city is fully booked by spring, with prices doubling. If you have not booked, day-trip from Takamatsu or Osaka and head home on the last train.

Smaller events worth watching for: the **Indigo Festival** in late July, the **Yoshino River Fireworks** in early August, and the **Hachisuka Cherry Blossom Festival** at the castle ruins in early April.
