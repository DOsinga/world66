Takeo is a small Saga Prefecture city that earns its stop with water, gardens, and one very old tree. It is not a big Kyushu headline, but the Nishi Kyushu Shinkansen has made it easier to fold into a trip between Nagasaki, Saga, and Fukuoka.

The town's core is [Takeo Onsen](/asia/japan/takeo/takeo_onsen), a hot spring with more than a thousand years of history and a handsome old gate near the bathhouses. Nearby, [Takeo Shrine](/asia/japan/takeo/takeo_shrine) shelters a huge camphor tree that feels less like an attraction than a presence.

The reason to linger is [Mifuneyama Rakuen](/asia/japan/takeo/mifuneyama_rakuen), a garden spread below a craggy mountain, especially good in azalea, autumn colour, and evening light events. Add the [Takeo City Library](/asia/japan/takeo/takeo_city_library) and a local gyoza lunch, and Takeo becomes a good slow day rather than just a bath stop.
