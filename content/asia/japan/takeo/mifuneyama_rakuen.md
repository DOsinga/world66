Mifuneyama Rakuen is Takeo's strongest sight. The garden spreads below the bare face of Mount Mifune, using ponds, paths, maples, and flowering shrubs to turn the mountain into scenery.

Spring azaleas are the classic draw, autumn colours are excellent, and the evening light installations have made the garden better known outside Saga. Even without a seasonal peak, it is the place in Takeo most worth building a day around.
