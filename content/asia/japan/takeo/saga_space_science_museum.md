The Saga Prefecture Space and Science Museum is the practical rainy-day stop in Takeo. It is especially useful for families, with hands-on exhibits that break up a trip otherwise built around baths, gardens, and shrines.

It sits outside the most walkable part of town, so treat it as an add-on rather than a default stop. With children, it may be the difference between a quick onsen visit and a full day.
