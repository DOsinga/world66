Takeo Onsen is the town's old heart. The public baths sit close to the station, making this one of the easier traditional hot-spring stops to add to a rail trip across northern Kyushu.

The water is only part of the appeal. The historic gate and bathhouse frontage give the area a sense of occasion before you even step inside.
