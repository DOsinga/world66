Aizu Shinsengumi Memorial Hall is a small museum devoted to the Shinsengumi and their ties to Aizu. Its collection includes documents, artwork, and objects connected with the group and the battles that shaped the end of the shogunate.

It is more specialised than the castle or Iimoriyama, but worthwhile if Aizu's late-Edo history is part of why you came.
