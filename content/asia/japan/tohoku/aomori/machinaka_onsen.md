Machinaka Onsen is a convenient hot-spring bath within an easy walk of Aomori Station. It is a practical choice if you want an onsen soak without taking the trip out to Asamushi Onsen.

The bath is simple and central, with a small outdoor pool that makes it useful after a cold day, a long train ride, or time exploring the waterfront around the station.
