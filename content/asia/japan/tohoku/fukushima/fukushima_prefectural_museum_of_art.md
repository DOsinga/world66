Fukushima Prefectural Museum of Art sits at the foot of Mt. Shinobu, a quiet cultural stop that pairs naturally with the mountain. Its collection is stronger than the building first suggests, with local artists, Japanese modern work, prints, crafts, and Western pieces that include French and American names.

It is not a blockbuster museum, and that is part of its appeal. The galleries are calm, the scale is manageable, and the visit gives Fukushima a more reflective counterweight to blossoms, fruit stands, and hot-spring towns.
