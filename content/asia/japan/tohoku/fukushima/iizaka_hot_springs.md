Iizaka Hot Springs is an easy onsen day trip from Fukushima. The spa town has public bath houses, foot baths and quiet streets that make it simple to sample the hot-spring culture without planning an overnight stay.

Start at the information center near the station for English guidance, bath-house details and walking advice around the town.
