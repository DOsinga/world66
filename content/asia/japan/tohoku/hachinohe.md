Hachinohe is a working port on Aomori's Pacific side, far enough from the usual Tohoku route to feel like its own world. It is known for seafood, morning markets, summer festivals, and a coast where grassy headlands and fishing villages lead into Sanriku Fukko National Park.

The city is at its best when you mix food and shoreline. [Hasshoku Center](/asia/japan/tohoku/hachinohe/hasshoku_center) is a full seafood playground rather than a polite market, while [Tatehana Wharf Morning Market](/asia/japan/tohoku/hachinohe/tatehana_wharf_morning_market) is the big early start. Out by the water, [Kabushima](/asia/japan/tohoku/hachinohe/kabushima) brings a shrine, a breeding colony of black-tailed gulls, and the start of the coast.

The spare day here should go south along the [Tanesashi Coast](/asia/japan/tohoku/hachinohe/tanesashi_coast). It is one of the most accessible pieces of the Sanriku shore, with open lawns, beaches, rock shelves, and small stations on the Hachinohe Line. Hachinohe is not sleek, but it has a strong northern appetite and a coastline that rewards getting out of the centre.
