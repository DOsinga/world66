Tatehana Wharf Morning Market is the early, lively version of Hachinohe: rows of stalls by the harbour selling grilled fish, coffee, vegetables, sweets, and breakfast food to locals and visitors who got up in time.

It usually runs on Sunday mornings from spring into winter, with the best energy early. Treat it as breakfast, not shopping, and leave room to try more than one thing.
