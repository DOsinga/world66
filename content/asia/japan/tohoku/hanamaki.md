Hanamaki is a quiet city in central Iwate best known as the birthplace of Kenji Miyazawa (1896–1933), the poet, children's author, and agricultural teacher whose stories — *Night on the Galactic Railroad* chief among them — are woven deep into Japanese childhood. The city has turned his legacy into a cluster of thoughtful museums and gardens, and it makes an easy and rewarding stop between Morioka and Hiraizumi.

The main draw is the [Kenji Miyazawa Memorial Museum](/asia/japan/tohoku/hanamaki/kenji_miyazawa_memorial_museum) on a hill above town, which traces his short life across science, religion, music, and literature, and the neighbouring [Miyazawa Kenji Dowa-Mura](/asia/japan/tohoku/hanamaki/miyazawa_kenji_dowa_mura), a "fairy-tale village" that stages scenes from his stories. Beyond Miyazawa, Hanamaki is an onsen town: the Hanamaki Onsen district and the older hot-spring hamlets in the hills to the west are popular weekend escapes for travellers coming up from Sendai or down from Morioka.
