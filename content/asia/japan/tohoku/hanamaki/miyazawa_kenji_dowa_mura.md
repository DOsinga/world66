Miyazawa Kenji Dowa-Mura is an interactive museum devoted to the stories of Iwate writer Kenji Miyazawa. Its rooms turn scenes from his children's literature into walk-through settings, making it a useful companion to the nearby Kenji Miyazawa Memorial Museum.
