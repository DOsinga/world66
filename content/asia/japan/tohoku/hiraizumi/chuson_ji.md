Chuson-ji is Hiraizumi's great temple, founded on a wooded hill and famous for the Konjikido, a small hall covered in gold leaf and mother-of-pearl. Unlike Kyoto's Golden Pavilion, the hall is protected inside another building, so the drama is inward: dark lacquer, gilded Buddhist figures, and the mausoleum of the Northern Fujiwara lords.

The approach through the trees is part of the visit. Give yourself time for the smaller halls and museum rather than treating Konjikido as a quick photo stop.
