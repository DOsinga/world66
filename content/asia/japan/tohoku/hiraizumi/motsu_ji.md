Motsu-ji preserves one of Japan's great Pure Land gardens, arranged around a broad pond with stones, islands, and open grass where temple buildings once stood. It is less immediately dazzling than Chuson-ji, but in some ways easier to feel: the whole garden is a map of Buddhist paradise in landscape form.

Walk slowly around the pond. The spaces that look empty now were once part of the design, and the ruins make the garden feel more thoughtful rather than less complete.
