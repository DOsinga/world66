Hirosaki is a castle town in Aomori Prefecture, in the snowy north of [Honshu](/asia/japan). It was the seat of the Tsugaru clan, and it still has a stronger historic atmosphere than many larger cities in Tohoku: castle gates, temple lanes, old houses, and festival traditions survive alongside a modern apple-growing city.

The essential stop is [Hirosaki Castle](/asia/japan/tohoku/hirosaki/hirosaki_castle), especially in cherry blossom season when the park becomes one of northern Japan's great spring scenes. West of the centre, [Zenringai](/asia/japan/tohoku/hirosaki/zenringai) gathers dozens of Zen temples in a defensive line once meant to protect the castle spiritually and practically. [Saisho-in](/asia/japan/tohoku/hirosaki/saisho_in) adds one of the region's most graceful five-storey pagodas.

Hirosaki is also a festival town. Its August Neputa Festival is related to Aomori's better-known Nebuta, but the fan-shaped floats and slightly calmer scale give it its own character. Apples are everywhere too: orchards ring the city, and local menus make full use of them.
