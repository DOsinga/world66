Iwaki City Museum of Folklore and Traditional Housing presents reconstructed houses, household goods, and village scenes from the late Edo period through the early Meiji era. The open-air displays give a practical sense of how people lived in the region before modern housing and industry changed the city.

It pairs well with Iwaki's other history museums, especially for visitors interested in everyday life, rural buildings, and local material culture.
