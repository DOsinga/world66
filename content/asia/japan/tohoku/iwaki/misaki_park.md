Misaki Park occupies a headland on the east side of Onahama Bay, rising about 46 m above sea level. Its main draws are the Shiomidai Lookout and the Marine Tower, both giving broad views over the port, coast, and Pacific.

It is a useful outdoor pairing with Aquamarine Fukushima nearby, especially with children or on a clear day when the bay views are open.
