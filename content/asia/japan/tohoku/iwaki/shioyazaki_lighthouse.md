Shioyazaki Lighthouse stands on a cliff above the Pacific, with broad sea views and a long role in guiding ships along the Fukushima coast. The light has been a local landmark since 1890.

It is a simple but rewarding coastal stop, especially in clear weather when the horizon opens up from the headland.
