Shiramizu Amida-do is the quiet reason to take Iwaki seriously. The temple hall dates from the Heian period and sits in a Pure Land garden of ponds, bridges, and seasonal flowers.

It is Fukushima Prefecture's only building designated as a National Treasure, but the visit is not grand in a loud way. Come for stillness, proportions, and the feeling of an older landscape surviving inside a modern city.
