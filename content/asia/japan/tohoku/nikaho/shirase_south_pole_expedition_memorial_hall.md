The Shirase South Pole Expedition Memorial Hall tells the story of Nobu Shirase, the Nikaho-born explorer who led Japan's first Antarctic expedition. Exhibits cover the expedition, Shirase's life, and the wider history of polar exploration.

It is one of Nikaho's more distinctive museums and pairs well with the coastal sights around Kisakata.
