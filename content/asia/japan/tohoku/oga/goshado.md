Goshado is a set of five shrines above the western side of the Oga Peninsula. The approach from Akagami Shrine climbs the famous 999 stone steps, which turn the visit into a short but memorable walk.

The reward is not a grand building but the whole setting: forest, stone, old worship, and glimpses of the coastline below.
