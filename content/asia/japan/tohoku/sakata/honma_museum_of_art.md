The Honma Museum of Art occupies a former villa linked to the wealthy Honma family, whose fortune helped shape Sakata's merchant culture. The collection includes art and furnishings, but the house and garden are just as important.

On clear days the garden frames Mount Chokai, tying the museum back to the Shonai plain rather than making it feel like a sealed gallery.
