title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Sakata's best sights sit in two clusters. Around the centre, [Sankyo Storehouses](/asia/japan/sakata/sankyo_storehouses), [Somaro](/asia/japan/sakata/somaro), and [Kaiko-ji Temple](/asia/japan/sakata/kaiko_ji_temple) show the old port's rice trade, pleasure-quarter culture, and religious edge.

South of the station, the [Ken Domon Museum of Photography](/asia/japan/sakata/ken_domon_museum_of_photography) and Sakata City Museum of Art make a quieter art circuit. If time is short, choose Sankyo Storehouses first, then add Somaro or Ken Domon depending on whether you want town history or photography.
