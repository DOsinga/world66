Unosu Cliff is a rugged coastal viewpoint on the Sanriku Coast, known for wave-cut rock lines and seabirds, especially cormorants. The name means cormorant's nest.

It is a quieter companion to the better-known Kitayamazaki Cliffs, useful if you are following the coast and want another dramatic stop rather than a town-based attraction.
