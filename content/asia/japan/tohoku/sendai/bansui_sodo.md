Bansui Sōdō is the former residence of Doi Bansui, a Sendai poet and scholar best known in Japan for writing the lyrics to "Kōjō no Tsuki." The house is a quiet literary stop near the city center and is most rewarding for visitors interested in Japanese poetry and local cultural history.
