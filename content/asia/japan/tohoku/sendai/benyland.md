Benyland is Sendai's traditional amusement park, set in the Yagiyama hills near the city zoo. Its rides and family attractions make it a straightforward outing for children, especially when paired with other Yagiyama stops.

This is a local leisure attraction rather than a must-see for every visitor. It earns its place for families staying in Sendai long enough to look beyond temples, shopping arcades, and Matsushima day trips.
