Shirakawa Barrier is the old checkpoint site that marked the symbolic entrance to Tohoku. There is no great gate left, just a shrine, markers, trees, and the quiet of a place that mattered more in memory than in stone.

That memory is the reason to come. Matsuo Basho passed this way on the journey later written as *The Narrow Road to the Deep North*, and older court poets used Shirakawa as shorthand for leaving the known world behind.
