Tendo is a small city just north of [Yamagata](/asia/japan/tohoku/yamagata), famous for one oddly specific craft: shogi pieces. Most of Japan's wooden pieces for Japanese chess are made here, and the town leans into the identity with museums, shopfront displays, giant pieces, and a spring festival where people become the board.

That would be too thin on its own, but Tendo has enough for a good half-day or slow day from Yamagata. [Tendo Shogi Museum](/asia/japan/tohoku/tendo/tendo_shogi_museum) explains the craft, [Hiroshige Museum of Art](/asia/japan/tohoku/tendo/hiroshige_museum_of_art) adds an unexpected ukiyo-e connection, and the onsen area east of the station gives the town a softer edge.

Come for the shogi if you have even a passing interest in games, craft, or small Japanese towns with a single obsession. In late April, [Ningen Shogi](/asia/japan/tohoku/tendo/ningen_shogi) turns that obsession into theatre on the hillside above town.
