Seifu-so is a quiet historic house and garden with roots in a former temple estate. The temple buildings are gone, but the garden survived, and a tea house added in the 1950s gives the place a calm, lived-in elegance.

It is a gentle stop rather than a headline sight, best for travellers who enjoy Japanese gardens, old residential architecture, and a slower corner of Yamagata.
