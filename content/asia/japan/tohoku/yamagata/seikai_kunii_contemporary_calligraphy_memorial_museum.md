This small museum is devoted to Seikai Kunii, the Yamagata-born calligrapher who founded the Seishinsha group in 1917 to study modern calligraphy. The collection includes his own work, pieces he collected, and changing exhibitions connected to Japanese calligraphy.

It is a specialist stop rather than a broad city highlight, but worthwhile if you are interested in brushwork, modern Japanese art, or quieter museums away from the main castle-park circuit.
