Komachi no Yu is a rustic outdoor hot spring bath near Onogawa Onsen. It is simple and open-air, with separate bathing areas and an old-fashioned honour-system feel rather than resort facilities.

The appeal is the plain local bath experience. Bring your own towel and expect a no-frills stop rather than a full spa visit.
