Toko Sake Museum occupies a renovated brewery and shows how sake was made before stainless steel tanks and modern controls took over. The old rooms, tools, and shop make it more atmospheric than a simple tasting counter.

It is an easy walk from the castle area and a good reminder that Yonezawa is not only samurai history. The town's cold winters and clean water have long suited brewing.
