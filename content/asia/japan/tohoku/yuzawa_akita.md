Yuzawa sits in southern Akita, where the Yokote Basin starts to fold into the mountains along the Miyagi and Yamagata borders. The city itself is quiet and workaday, but the wider municipality has a clear identity: deep snow, hot springs, steaming ravines, and the thin, hand-stretched Inaniwa udon that is one of Japan's great regional noodles.

The best day is spent outside the centre. [Oyasukyo Gorge](/asia/japan/tohoku/yuzawa_akita/oyasukyo_gorge) is the main natural sight, with hot water and steam bursting from cracks in a narrow valley. Farther into the mountains, [Kawarage Jigoku and Oyutaki](/asia/japan/tohoku/yuzawa_akita/kawarage_jigoku_and_oyutaki) turn the same geothermal energy into white slopes and a hot-water waterfall.

Yuzawa is not a place to rush through on foot. Attractions are spread out, buses are limited, and winter closes some mountain roads. With a car and a spare day in southern Akita, though, it offers a satisfying mix of volcanic landscape, small-town food culture, and onsen country.
