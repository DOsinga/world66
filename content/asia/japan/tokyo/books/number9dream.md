Mitchell's second novel follows a young man from rural Japan who arrives in Tokyo to find his father, and instead finds himself entangled with yakuza, hackers, and the ghosts of wartime Japan. Tokyo is vividly rendered — the corporate towers of Shinjuku, the underground arcades, the dense anonymity of the city — through the eyes of someone arriving from elsewhere. Mitchell spent several years living in Hiroshima and Osaka, and his Tokyo feels observed rather than invented.
