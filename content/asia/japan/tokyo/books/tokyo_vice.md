Adelstein spent twelve years as a crime reporter for the Yomiuri Shimbun — the first foreigner ever hired by a major Japanese newspaper — covering the Tokyo Metropolitan Police. The result is an insider account of the city's underworld: yakuza, human trafficking, and the blurred lines between organised crime and respectability. A gripping read and a useful corrective to the idea that Tokyo is simply a safe, orderly city.
