A city as monstrous as Tokyo can also create powerful urges to make periodic escapes.

**Kunitachi City**, which is west of [Tokyo](/asia/japan/tokyo) on the JR Chuo Line and takes roughly 30 minutes to reach from [Shinjuku](/asia/japan/tokyo/shinjuku) Station, is a pleasant college town home to **Hitotsubashi University**, a small prestigious national university best known for economics. Many of its buildings are lovely brick buildings from the Meiji era and a stroll through the campus is worthwhile. The broad streets and spacious blocks are a legacy of its postwar period as a US military base. Kunitachi Station is one of the oldest standing and is home in summer to swarms of dive-bombing *tsubame* (swallows). North of the station is residential land; the majority of businesses and the university are to the south, most along *Daigaku-doori* (University Street), which heads due south from the station roundabout. Crowds descend on Kunitachi during the cherry blossom season in late March and early April, when *Daigaku-doori* is lined with cherry blossoms for over a kilometre of its length. Tachikawa, a proper city, is one station further west on the JR Chuo Line.
