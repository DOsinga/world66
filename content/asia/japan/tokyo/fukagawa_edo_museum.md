Fukagawa Edo Museum recreates a small Edo-period neighbourhood with houses, shops, alleys, and everyday objects arranged at walk-through scale. Light and sound effects shift the scene through a day, which makes the museum more immersive than a standard display case collection.

It is a good choice if you want social history rather than temples or big art museums, and it pairs naturally with other low-city sights in eastern Tokyo.
