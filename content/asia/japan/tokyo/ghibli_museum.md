The Ghibli Museum in Mitaka is the essential Tokyo stop for fans of Studio Ghibli and Japanese animation. Exhibits cover animation craft, original short films, playful architectural details, and familiar characters from the films. Tickets are limited and normally need to be reserved well before visiting.
