Gohyakurakanji is a temple known for its hundreds of rakan statues, each carved with a different expression. The statues were made by one monk between 1687 and 1695 and reward slow looking more than a quick glance.

It is a quiet, unusual stop near Meguro, best paired with other small museums and temples in the area.
