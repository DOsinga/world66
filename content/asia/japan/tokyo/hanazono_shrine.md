Hanazono Shrine is a quiet Shinto shrine tucked beside Shinjuku's busiest nightlife streets. The buildings are not Tokyo's grandest, but the contrast with nearby Kabukicho and Golden Gai makes it a memorable pause, especially when the small flea market or one of the shrine festivals is underway.
