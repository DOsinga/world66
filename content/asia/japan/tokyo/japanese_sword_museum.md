The Japanese Sword Museum is a focused museum in Ryogoku devoted to Japanese blades, fittings, and the craft traditions around them. The collection is specialised, but for anyone interested in samurai history, metalwork, or design, it explains why swords were treated as art objects as much as weapons.

Displays are compact and the visit does not need to take long. It combines well with the sumo sights and garden walks around Ryogoku.
