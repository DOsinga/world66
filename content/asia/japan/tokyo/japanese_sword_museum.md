The Japanese Sword Museum displays Japanese art swords, fittings, and related craft objects, including pieces several centuries old. It is a focused museum best suited to visitors interested in metalwork, samurai culture, or traditional Japanese collecting, with views over Kyu Yasuda Garden from the upper floor.
