Jimbōchō is a compact district in Chiyoda where over 170 bookshops line a few blocks of Yasukuni-dori. The shops range from general secondhand stores to dealers specializing in art books, manga, maps, woodblock prints, sporting books, academic texts, and foreign-language literature. It is the densest concentration of secondhand books anywhere in the world.

Even without Japanese, Jimbōchō is worth visiting for the woodblock prints, vintage maps, and illustrated art books that spill onto the pavement outside many shops. Serious collectors come here for out-of-print technical works and rare editions. The neighborhood also has excellent coffee shops and a few good curry restaurants that have been feeding book browsers for decades.
