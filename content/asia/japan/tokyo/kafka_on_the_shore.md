Murakami's novel moves between Tokyo and Takamatsu, but it carries the essential texture of contemporary Japanese urban life: the loneliness, the strange encounters, the shifting boundaries between dream and waking. Reading any Murakami before visiting Tokyo primes you to see the city differently — the late-night bars, the record shops, the unmarked doors. Kafka on the Shore is one of his most accessible and rewarding.
