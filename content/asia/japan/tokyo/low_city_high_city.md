The essential history of Tokyo, tracing the city from its origins as the Edo shogunate capital through the 1923 earthquake that destroyed most of it. Seidensticker was a great translator of Japanese literature and brought the same precision and affection to this history. If you want to understand what the old neighborhoods — Asakusa, Yanaka, Shitamachi — once were, and why their survivors feel different from the rest of the city, this is the book.
