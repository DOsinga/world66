Mohri Garden is a small landscaped garden beside Roppongi Hills, useful as a quiet pause between museums, shops, and nightlife. The pond, trees, and paths give this dense part of Tokyo a pocket of calm.

Its oddest claim to fame is the killifish released into the pond in 2003, descended from fish that had been used in a space-breeding experiment.
