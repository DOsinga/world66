The National Museum of Nature and Science is one of Ueno Park's big museum stops, with galleries on natural history, technology, physics, and life on Earth. The Japan Gallery is the most distinctive part, covering the country's geology, animals, plants, and human history, while the Global Gallery ranges from dinosaurs and insects to space and engineering.

It works well for families and curious adults, especially on a rainy day in Ueno. English labels can be brief, so the audio guide is worth considering if you want more than a quick walk through the highlights.
