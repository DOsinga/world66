Nezu Museum displays the private collection of industrialist Nezu Kaichiro, with Japanese and East Asian art including many Edo-period works. The collection is paired with a well-known garden that makes the visit feel quieter than many central Tokyo museums.

It is a strong stop for art and design visitors around Aoyama, Omotesando or Roppongi.
