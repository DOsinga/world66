Nogi's House and Shrine preserve the memory of General Nogi Maresuke, a Meiji-era military figure who died by ritual suicide after Emperor Meiji's death. The house mixes Japanese and Western architectural details, while the neighbouring shrine gives the site a quiet, reflective character near Akasaka.
