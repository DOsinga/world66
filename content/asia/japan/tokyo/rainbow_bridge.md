Rainbow Bridge is the big sweep of steel linking central Tokyo with Odaiba across Tokyo Bay. By day it frames the harbour, the skyline, and the water buses below; after dark its lighting gives the bay one of its most recognisable modern views.

You can cross by train, road, or on the pedestrian walkway when conditions allow. The walk is the best way to feel the scale of the bridge, with views back toward Tokyo Tower and forward to Odaiba's waterfront.
