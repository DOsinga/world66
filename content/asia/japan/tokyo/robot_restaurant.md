Deliberately, aggressively excessive: a dinner show in Shinjuku's Kabukicho district where giant robots, LED-lit floats, drummers, and bikini-clad performers occupy a basement that cost 100 million dollars to build. The food is secondary; the show is the point. It's expensive, loud, and unambiguously silly — which is exactly what makes it oddly unmissable. Book ahead. Not for everyone, but those who go never forget it.
