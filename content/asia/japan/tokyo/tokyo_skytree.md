Tokyo Skytree is the tallest structure in Japan and one of the clearest symbols of modern Tokyo. The observation decks give a vast view over the low-rise east side, the dense centre, and on clear days Mount Fuji. The tower sits above Tokyo Solamachi, so it is easy to combine the view with shopping or a meal.
