An American journalist's memoir of covering the Tokyo police beat for the Yomiuri Shimbun — the world's largest-circulation newspaper — for over a decade. Adelstein ended up embedded in the world of the yakuza, the vice trade, and the underbelly of a city that presents a very different face in public. Shocking, specific, and often very funny about the peculiarities of Japanese professional life.
