Tsukiji Hongwanji is a Jodo Shinshu Buddhist temple a short walk from Tsukiji Outer Market. Its unusual stone facade, inspired by South Asian Buddhist architecture, looks very different from most temples in Tokyo.

It is easy to add before or after a market breakfast. English-language Buddhist services are sometimes held on Saturday evenings.
