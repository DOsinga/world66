TV Asahi's Roppongi headquarters includes a small public area for fans of Japanese television. The main draw is the mini-museum, with character displays and full-size models from Doraemon and other popular TV Asahi shows. It is a light, family-friendly stop near the museums and galleries of Roppongi.
