Yanaka is old Tokyo, improbably preserved. The neighbourhood escaped the 1923 earthquake, the 1945 bombing, and the post-war bulldozing that transformed most of the city. What remains is a dense warren of wooden shopfronts, local temples, artisan workshops, and the enormous Yanaka Cemetery, which doubles as a park. The main shopping street, Yanaka Ginza, is a gently touristy but genuine shotengai. Come on a weekday morning when it belongs to locals.
