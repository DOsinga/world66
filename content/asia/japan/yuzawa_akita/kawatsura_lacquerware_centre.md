Kawatsura lacquerware has been made in this area since the 17th century. The centre explains the craft on its museum floor, with exhibits on the tools, techniques, and local history behind the lacquer bowls and household goods. The ground floor functions as a showcase and shop, and lacquerware painting classes are sometimes offered with advance booking.
