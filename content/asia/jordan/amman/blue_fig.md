Blue Fig is a well-established Amman restaurant and bar that has been a fixture on the 4th Circle area social scene since the early 2000s — a comfortable, well-designed space that serves international and Lebanese-Jordanian food alongside a decent wine list and cocktails. It is firmly in the west Amman middle-class milieu: not particularly adventurous, but reliable and well-run.

The garden terrace is the place to be on warm evenings. The crowd is mixed — Jordanian professionals, expats, the occasional diplomat — and the atmosphere is relaxed enough for a long evening. The hummus and the grilled items are consistently good; the wine list has some decent Jordanian and Lebanese options.
