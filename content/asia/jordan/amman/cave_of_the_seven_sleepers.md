The Cave of the Seven Sleepers, east of Amman, is linked to the old story of youths who slept in a cave for centuries while fleeing persecution. The legend matters in both Christian and Islamic tradition, which gives the site a broader resonance than its modest size suggests.

The visit is simple: a cave, tomb-like spaces, and a small religious complex rather than a major archaeological park. It is best for travelers interested in Jordan's religious geography and in sites outside the usual Roman-and-Umayyad circuit.
