The Jordan Archaeological Museum sits on the Amman Citadel and displays antiquities from across Jordan. The collection is compact, but it gives useful context to the ruins around it, with objects ranging from prehistoric finds to classical and Islamic-period material.

It works best as part of a Citadel visit rather than as a standalone museum stop.
