---
title: Getting There
type: section
---

Anjara sits on the road between [Jerash](/asia/jordan/jerash) and [Ajlun](/asia/jordan/ajlun), about 70 km north-west of [Amman](/asia/jordan/amman). A shared taxi (service) from Ajlun takes 15 minutes; from Amman, take a bus or service taxi to Ajlun's North Terminal first and switch there.

Most travellers visit as a short stop on a half-day excursion from Amman or Jerash that also takes in Ajlun Castle and Tell Mar Elias. A hired car or arranged driver from Amman makes the loop easy and costs less than a guided tour.
