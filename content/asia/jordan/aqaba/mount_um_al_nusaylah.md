Mount Um al Nusaylah is a short, rough hike above Aqaba with wide views over the town, the Red Sea, and Eilat across the border. The land behind the mountain opens into dry valleys, giving the view more drama than you expect from near the city.

Go early or late in the day, bring water, and treat it as a viewpoint hike rather than a marked urban park.
