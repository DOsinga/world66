---
title: "Eating Out"
type: section
---

Irbid feeds its students cheaply and well. University Street and the surrounding blocks are lined with shawarma counters, falafel joints, and juice bars — a full meal for a dinar or two is easy to find. For sit-down dining, there are a number of Arabic restaurants serving the standard Jordanian spread: mezze, grilled meats, mansaf on Fridays.
