The Irbid Archaeological Museum introduces the deep history of northern Jordan through
finds from the surrounding region. It is a compact stop, but useful in a city that often
serves as a practical base rather than a sightseeing destination.

Visit before heading to Umm Qais, Pella, or other northern sites and the labels and
objects will give those ruins a broader context. The museum is best treated as a focused
historical primer, not a full-day attraction.
