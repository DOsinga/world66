Prophet Nuh Shrine is the reputed tomb of Noah, known as Nuh in Islamic tradition, set on a hill in central Karak.

It is a modest religious site, but it adds a different layer to a visit that is otherwise often focused on Karak Castle and the old town's Crusader and Mamluk history.
