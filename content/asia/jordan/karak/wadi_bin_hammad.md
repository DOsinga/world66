Wadi Bin Hammad is a green canyon near Karak, with warm water, small waterfalls, and narrow passages that make an easy two-hour walk when conditions are dry. It feels much less packaged than Jordan's better-known wadis, but flash-flood risk is real, so avoid it when rain is forecast and arrange transport carefully.
