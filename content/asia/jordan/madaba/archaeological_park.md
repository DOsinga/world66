The Archaeological Park is a covered complex a few minutes' walk south of St George's, built over a cluster of Byzantine ruins to protect them from weather and traffic. Walkways thread above and around mosaic floors so spectacular that any of them would be the centrepiece of a larger museum.

The two highlights are the **Hippolytus Hall**, with its mythological scenes of Aphrodite, Adonis, and the doomed Phaedra and her stepson Hippolytus, and the adjoining **Church of the Virgin**, with a circular floor mosaic showing a geometric medallion surrounded by intricate floral and animal motifs. Many of the smaller fragments come from churches and houses across town and were brought here for preservation.
