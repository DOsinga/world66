---
title: "Getting There"
type: section
---

Madaba sits on the King's Highway about 30 km south of [Amman](/asia/jordan/amman), and is an easy 45-minute drive. The simplest option is a taxi from Amman, which costs around JD 15–20 one way; many drivers will agree to wait three or four hours for a full day trip.

By public minibus you go from Amman's South Bus Station (Mujamma' al-Janub) to Madaba in about an hour for JD 1. Coming from the south, **Petra** and **Wadi Musa** are about three hours by car, and most travellers stop in Madaba on the drive between Petra and Amman. Queen Alia International Airport is only 15 km north — easier to reach Madaba directly from the airport than to go to Amman first.
