---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

**March to May** and **October–November** are the best months: warm enough for the desert sites around Madaba but not blistering. Spring is especially attractive — the high plateau around the city briefly turns green, and the wild flowers in the gorges are spectacular.

**Summer** (June–September) is hot but dry, with daytime temperatures often above 35°C. Mount Nebo can be unpleasant in the middle of the day; aim for an early start. **Winter** (December–February) is cool to cold, with occasional snow on the higher ground, but the churches and Archaeological Park stay open. Christmas in Madaba is genuinely festive — the town has been a major Christian centre for 1500 years and the streets fill with carols and processions on Christmas Eve.
