Petra belongs on the top of any Jordan itinerary. The Nabataeans carved an entire city out of the red sandstone of the Wadi Musa canyon — temples, tombs, dining halls and processional ways, all out of the living rock — and they did it so well that the place still works as architecture two thousand years later.

The visit starts on foot through the [Siq](/asia/jordan/petra/siq), a narrow gorge a kilometre and a half long whose walls rise more than 150 metres above your head. The gorge bends, twists, and finally narrows to a slot. Then it opens. What stands in front of you is [Al-Khazneh](/asia/jordan/petra/al_khazneh) — the Treasury — a 40-metre-high facade so improbably perfect that the moment of arrival has become one of the most photographed in the world. Photographs do not prepare you for it; the scale only registers when you are standing under it.

After the Treasury the canyon opens into a wider valley. Hundreds of tombs are cut into the cliffs, a [Roman theatre](/asia/jordan/petra/roman_theatre) carved straight from the rock seats 8,000 people, and a paved colonnaded street runs past the Nabataean public buildings — the [Great Temple](/asia/jordan/petra/great_temple), the [Temple of the Winged Lions](/asia/jordan/petra/temple_of_the_winged_lions), and the [Qasr al-Bint](/asia/jordan/petra/qasr_al_bint). To see the full extent you need a day, ideally two: the [Monastery (Ed-Deir)](/asia/jordan/petra/ed_deir) is an 800-step climb beyond the main basin, and the [High Place of Sacrifice](/asia/jordan/petra/high_place_of_sacrifice) sits on the ridge above, both rewarding the effort.

Petra is hot and exposed. Bring more water than you think you need, wear real shoes, and start early — by ten o'clock the Treasury area is crowded and the rock has warmed enough to bleach the photographs. The base for visits is the small town of [Wadi Musa](/asia/jordan/petra/wadi_musa) directly above the site, where you will sleep and eat.

Petra sits less than 200 km south of [Amman](/asia/jordan/amman). You can do it as a long day trip on the Desert Highway, but you will miss half of it. Combine it with [Wadi Rum](/asia/jordan/wadirum) to the south, and [Little Petra](/asia/jordan/petra/little_petra) and Beidha on the way north for the fuller picture.
