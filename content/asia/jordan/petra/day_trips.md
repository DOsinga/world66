Most people use Petra as a stop on a longer route, not as a base for day trips — there is more than enough on the archaeological site itself to fill two or three days. The most obvious detour is **[Little Petra](/asia/jordan/petra/little_petra)**, 8 km north, which can be combined with the Neolithic village of Beidha in a half-day.

Further afield, **[Wadi Rum](/asia/jordan/wadirum)** is 90 minutes south and is the natural counterpart — Petra in stone, Wadi Rum in sand. **Shobak Castle**, a 40-minute drive north, is a Crusader fortress on a hilltop with views into the wadi country. **[Aqaba](/asia/jordan/aqaba)** is two hours south for a swim in the Red Sea and a snorkel on the coral reefs.
