Al-Hammam Street is the main artery of Salt's old souq, running through the heart of the old town between the merchants' quarter and the mosque. Unlike the tourist souqs of Amman or Petra, this is a real working market where Salt residents buy their groceries, hardware, and household goods. The architecture above the shop fronts — arched windows, carved limestone lintels, iron balconies — is UNESCO-listed Ottoman heritage.

Walking the length of the street takes about ten minutes at a leisurely pace, but plan to stop: vendors are inclined to conversation, and the alleyways branching off the main route contain small workshops, coffee houses, and the occasional surprise. The steeper lanes above the street lead up toward the residential quarter and the shrines in the hills above.
