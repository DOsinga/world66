As-Salt Secondary School is one of the oldest schools in Jordan and still operates as an active educational institution. The building dates from the early twentieth century and stands as one of the more imposing examples of Ottoman civic architecture in the town. Its history is intimately connected with Salt's former role as the administrative and cultural capital of the Balqa region.

The school is not generally open for tourism, but the exterior is worth seeing from the street. It represents the Ottoman administration's investment in modern education in their provincial cities — Salt was considered important enough to receive a proper secondary school at a time when much of the surrounding region had nothing comparable.
