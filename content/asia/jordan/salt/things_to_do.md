---
title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Salt rewards those who simply walk. The UNESCO-listed old town is compact enough to cover on foot in a morning, with the [Abu Jaber House](/asia/jordan/salt/abu_jaber_house) and [Prophet Jadur Shrine](/asia/jordan/salt/prophet_jadur_shrine) as natural anchors. The [Ottoman architecture](/asia/jordan/salt/salt_ottoman_architecture) along the narrow lanes is the real draw — golden limestone facades with arched windows and wrought-iron balconies that have changed little in a century.

For those interested in history, the [As-Salt Secondary School](/asia/jordan/salt/as_salt_secondary_school) and the [Holy Land Institute for the Deaf](/asia/jordan/salt/holy_land_institute_for_the_deaf) offer glimpses into the town's role as an educational centre during the Ottoman period. A half-day excursion to the Christian village of [Fuheis](/asia/jordan/salt/fuheis) makes a pleasant complement to the Salt old town walk.
