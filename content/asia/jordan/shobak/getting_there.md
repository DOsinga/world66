---
title: Getting There
type: section
---

Shobak lies on the King's Highway, about 230 km south of [Amman](/asia/jordan/amman) and roughly midway between [Karak](/asia/jordan/karak) and [Petra](/asia/jordan/petra). Most visitors come on the way between these two sites, either by private car or hired taxi. There is no reliable public transport to the castle itself — buses on the Ma'an route can drop you on the main highway, but the castle is a further 3 km uphill. From [Petra](/asia/jordan/petra), Shobak is about 50 km north and makes a logical morning detour before heading to Karak.
