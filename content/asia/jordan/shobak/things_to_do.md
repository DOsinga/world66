title: Things to Do
type: section
---

Shobak's single compelling attraction is [Montreal Castle](/asia/jordan/shobak/montreal_castle), the Crusader fortress built by Baldwin I in 1115. It is remarkably atmospheric and rarely crowded — a welcome contrast to the more famous castle at [Karak](/asia/jordan/karak). For those driving the King's Highway, the castle and the views it commands over the surrounding valleys make Shobak an essential stop between Karak and [Petra](/asia/jordan/petra).
