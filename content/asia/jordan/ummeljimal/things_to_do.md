---
title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

The ruins of Umm el Jimal are open for free exploration — there are no fences or ticketing, and you can wander at will through the basalt streets and buildings. The [ruined city centre](/asia/jordan/ummeljimal/ruined_city) gives the best sense of the town's scale and density; the [churches](/asia/jordan/ummeljimal/churches) are among the best-preserved ecclesiastical ruins in Jordan. The [water system](/asia/jordan/ummeljimal/water_system) — reservoirs, channels, and [cisterns](/asia/jordan/ummeljimal/cisterns) cut into the basalt — is extraordinary for a site in such an arid location. The modern village of Umm el Jimal continues to live alongside the ruins, and some local families act as informal guides; engaging one adds considerably to a visit.
