---
title: Bars and Cafes
type: section
---

Almaty has a lively bar and café scene concentrated in the streets between Furmanov and Dostyk avenues. The mix reflects the city's cosmopolitan character: Russian-style beer halls sit alongside craft beer bars, Kazakh tea houses, and international-style café chains. The city earns its local nickname of "club city" — there are more nightclubs per capita than anywhere else in Central Asia.
