---
title: Day Trips
type: section
---

The mountains and steppe around Almaty are extraordinarily accessible. The Ile-Alatau National Park begins at the city's southern edge; the Charyn Canyon is three hours east; Tamgaly's UNESCO-listed petroglyphs are two and a half hours northwest. Most sites are reachable by day without an overnight stay.

Organised day tours operate from the city for most major destinations. Alternatively, shared taxis (marshrutki) serve many routes at very low cost, though schedules can be irregular. A private taxi or rental car gives the most flexibility.
