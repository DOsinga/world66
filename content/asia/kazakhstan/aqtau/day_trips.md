---
title: "Day Trips"
type: section
---

The Mangystau region around Aqtau contains some of the most dramatic and least-visited landscapes in Central Asia. Most visitors come here specifically to explore this desert — Aqtau is just the access point.

**Bozzhyra Valley** is the most spectacular destination: an ancient seabed now exposed as a wide white chalk basin ringed by eroded pillars and pinnacles. It looks like a landscape from another planet and requires a guide and 4WD to reach safely.

**Underground mosques** are scattered across the steppe — medieval structures carved directly into the chalk cliffs, still in use and maintained. Beket-Ata is the most sacred, a four-hour return walk through canyon country; Shakpak-Ata and Shopan-Ata are more accessible. Modest dress required.

**Mangyshlak Peninsula** has its own character: salt flats, pink flamingos, massive hydrocarbon flares burning on the horizon, and then sudden outcrops of red rock. The scale is enormous and disorienting.

All these destinations require a vehicle and, ideally, a local guide. Tour agencies in Aqtau can arrange day or multi-day excursions into the desert. The roads are rough; 4WD is strongly recommended beyond the main paved route.
