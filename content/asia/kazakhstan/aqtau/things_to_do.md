---
title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Aqtau's city attractions are modest, but they are a pleasant surprise given the industrial reputation of the place. The Caspian seafront is a genuine promenade and swimming beach — the lake is calm, saltier than the ocean, and warm in summer. The city is primarily a base for the extraordinary desert landscapes of Mangystau, reachable on day trips.
