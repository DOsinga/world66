The Kazakhstan Central Concert Hall is one of the more sculptural buildings in Astana — a cluster of curved titanium petals enclosing a 3,500-seat auditorium and several smaller halls. The design, by Italian architect Manfredi Nicoletti, opened in 2009 and remains a useful landmark on the eastern half of the left bank.

The programme is broad — symphony concerts, Kazakh folk performances, pop concerts, international touring acts. Acoustic quality is good in the main hall. Even outside performance times the building rewards a walk around the exterior to take in the unusual geometry; lighting at night picks out the curved edges of the petals.
