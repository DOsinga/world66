The Military Historical Museum fills a large cultural building on Respublika Avenue with weapons, uniforms, armour, documents, and patriotic displays tied to Kazakhstan's military past. Its collection reaches beyond the modern army, using older steppe warfare and state-building material to frame the country's defence history.

This is not the first museum to see in Astana, but it is a strong second-tier stop if you are interested in how Kazakhstan presents its national story. The building is formal, the displays are extensive, and the museum gives useful context for a capital that often feels almost entirely new.
