The Museum of the First President occupies the former presidential residence on Astana's older right bank. It focuses on Nursultan Nazarbayev, Kazakhstan's first president, and on the early state-building years after independence.

The tone is official, but the building and exhibits help explain how modern Kazakhstan presents its own recent history. Enter through the security gate on Abay and allow time for the checks.
