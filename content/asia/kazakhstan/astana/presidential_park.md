Presidential Park is a long landscaped strip behind the Aqorda Residence, stretching east toward the Pyramid. It is wide, manicured, and patrolled — flowerbeds, fountains, monuments to national heroes, a small artificial lake at the eastern end. The park functions as the ceremonial garden of the executive complex.

For visitors, it is a useful connector — a walk through the park links the Aqorda area with the Palace of Peace and Reconciliation. The benches are popular in summer evenings and the fountains run from late spring through early autumn. Security is light but visible; the park is open to the public from morning until late evening.
