Shabyt Palace is a glassy, dish-shaped arts building beside the Kazakh Eli monument. It is not Astana's grandest stop, but the shape and setting make it an easy architectural detour when you are already visiting the National Museum, Hazret Sultan Mosque, and the Palace of Peace and Reconciliation.
