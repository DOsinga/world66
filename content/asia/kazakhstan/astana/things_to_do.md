title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Astana's main draws cluster along the central axis of the left bank — a deliberate sequence of monumental architecture built since 1997 to make a capital city out of a Soviet provincial outpost. The walk from [Khan Shatyr](/asia/kazakhstan/astana/khan_shatyr) through the [Bayterek Tower](/asia/kazakhstan/astana/bayterek_tower) and on to the [Aqorda](/asia/kazakhstan/astana/aqorda_residence) and [Palace of Peace and Reconciliation](/asia/kazakhstan/astana/palace_of_peace) is the standard introduction — about three kilometres of glass, golden domes, and Norman Foster geometries.

Beyond the showpieces, the [Hazret Sultan Mosque](/asia/kazakhstan/astana/hazret_sultan_mosque), [National Museum](/asia/kazakhstan/astana/national_museum), and [Astana Opera](/asia/kazakhstan/astana/astana_opera) are worth a half-day each. The right bank, with the older [Nur-Astana Mosque](/asia/kazakhstan/astana/nur_astana_mosque) and the [Central Park](/asia/kazakhstan/astana/central_park), gives a sense of what the city was before the rebuild.

The neighbourhoods to wander are the [Left Bank](/asia/kazakhstan/astana/left_bank) (monumental, planned, slightly unreal), the [Right Bank](/asia/kazakhstan/astana/right_bank) (Soviet-era, more lived-in), and the [Expo District](/asia/kazakhstan/astana/expo_district) around the Nur Alem sphere left over from Expo 2017. The contrast between them is the most interesting thing about Astana as a city.
