---
title: "Getting There"
type: section
---

Kokshetau is connected to the national rail and air network, though neither is frequent.

**By train:** Kokshetau-1 station is on the Trans-Kazakhstan Railway, with direct services to [Astana](/asia/kazakhstan/astana) (about 4 hours), Almaty (overnight), and Petropavl in northern Kazakhstan. The train is comfortable and reliable. The station is east of the city centre.

**By bus/marshrutka:** The central bus terminal handles intercity routes to Astana (frequent, 4–5 hours by road), Petropavl, and Kostanay. This is often the fastest option for Astana.

**By air:** Kokshetau Airport has limited scheduled service, mainly to Almaty. Most visitors arriving by air fly into Astana (Nursultan Nazarbayev International Airport) and continue to Kokshetau by bus or train.

**By car:** The road to Astana passes through Burabay, making a stop there natural on the drive.
