---
title: "Things to Do in Kokshetau"
type: section
---

Kokshetau is a working regional capital rather than a tourist destination, but it has several attractions worth visiting. The [Akmola Regional Museum](/asia/kazakhstan/kokshetau/akmola_museum) is the main cultural institution. The [Lake Kopa promenade](/asia/kazakhstan/kokshetau/lake_kopa) is the city's social center and best walk. [Bukpa Hill](/asia/kazakhstan/kokshetau/bukpa_hill) offers views over the city and the steppe. The real reason to come, however, is as a base for the [Burabay National Park](/asia/kazakhstan/kokshetau/burabay) lake district to the south.
