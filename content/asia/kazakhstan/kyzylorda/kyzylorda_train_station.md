Kyzylorda's main train station has been described by Lonely Planet as the best in Central Asia — a claim that draws curious visitors even when they have no intention of taking a train. The station dates to the Soviet era and is built on a scale that seems disproportionate to the town it serves, which is typical of Soviet infrastructure: every building was an architectural statement.

The interior features ornate decorative elements, high ceilings, and the kind of ceremonial grandeur that Soviet architects were required to produce for public buildings. Whether or not you agree with Lonely Planet's superlative, it's an interesting building worth a look, and the general hubbub of a Central Asian train station — with families camped out on the platform, vendors selling food, and conductors in elaborate uniforms — is a scene in itself.
