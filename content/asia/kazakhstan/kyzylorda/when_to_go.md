---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

Kyzylorda sits in the semidesert of southern Kazakhstan and experiences extreme continental temperatures. Summers are hot and dry, with temperatures regularly exceeding 40°C in July and August — not comfortable for walking around. Winters can bring bitter wind chill, though the city itself rarely drops below -10°C; the steppe to the north is far colder.

The best time to visit is **spring (April and May)**, when the surrounding semidesert briefly blooms. Wild tulips cover the steppe in extraordinary numbers — Central Asia is the native homeland of the tulip, and these spring carpets of red, yellow, and white are one of the genuine natural wonders of the region. Temperatures are mild, the Syr Darya is running full from snowmelt, and the dust and wind are manageable.

**September and October** are also pleasant, with cooling temperatures and clear skies.
