Annunciation Cathedral is Pavlodar's main Orthodox landmark, serving the city's large Russian-speaking community. Its golden domes and white walls make a clear contrast with the modern lines of Mashkhur Jusup Mosque.

Visit quietly and dress with respect. Together, the cathedral and mosque say a great deal about the cultural mix of northern Kazakhstan.
