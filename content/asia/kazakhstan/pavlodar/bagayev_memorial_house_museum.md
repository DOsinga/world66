The D. P. Bagayev Memorial House Museum preserves the home and archive of Dmitry Bagayev, one of Pavlodar's important early photographers. His images are valuable because they caught the Irtysh city and surrounding steppe communities at a moment of rapid change.

The museum is small, but it gives Pavlodar a human-scale historical stop beyond its larger regional museum. Look for the old photographs, domestic rooms, and the sense of how a provincial studio became a record of everyday life.
