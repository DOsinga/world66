Blagoveshchensky Cathedral is one of Pavlodar’s clearest landmarks, its golden domes and pale walls standing out in a city better known for the Irtysh River and Soviet-era planning. The cathedral is modern in its current form, but it draws on familiar Russian Orthodox proportions and iconography.

It is worth a short visit while exploring the centre, especially if you are walking between the riverfront, parks, and civic monuments. The cathedral adds a vertical, ceremonial note to Pavlodar’s otherwise broad and practical urban landscape.
