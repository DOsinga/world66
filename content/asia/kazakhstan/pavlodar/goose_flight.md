Goose Flight is a fossil-bearing natural monument on the eastern bank of the Irtysh. It is not a polished tourist attraction, but it is one of Pavlodar's most unusual sites and a good reminder that the river landscape has a history far older than the city.

Go with realistic expectations: this is a place for travellers interested in geology, fossils, or offbeat local history, not a conventional museum stop.
