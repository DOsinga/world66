The Historical and Regional Studies Museum of Pavlodar Province is the city's clearest museum introduction to the wider Irtysh region. Its collections bring together archaeology, ethnography, natural history, and local urban material, giving travellers context for a part of Kazakhstan that is often seen only in transit.

The museum is most useful near the start of a Pavlodar visit, before walking the riverfront or exploring older streets. It helps connect the modern industrial city with older steppe routes, regional settlement, and the ecology of the Irtysh basin.
