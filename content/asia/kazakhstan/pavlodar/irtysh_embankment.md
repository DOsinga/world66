The Irtysh Embankment is Pavlodar's best everyday walk. Locals come here for air, views, benches, summer evenings, and the simple pleasure of being beside a big Siberian river.

It is not grand in the way of a capital-city promenade, but that is its charm. Start near the centre and walk until the city thins out, watching how the river changes with the season.
