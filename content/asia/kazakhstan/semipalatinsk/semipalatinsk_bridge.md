Semipalatinsk Bridge is one of the city's cleaner modern landmarks, a long suspension bridge spanning the Irtysh River. It is most worth seeing from the embankment, where the lines of the bridge frame the water and city skyline. Come in the evening if you can, when the lighting makes it more than just a river crossing.
