---
title: 'Eating Out'
type: section
---

Shymkent eats well. The city's large Uzbek population means the plov here is widely held to be the best in Kazakhstan, and the bazaars and roadside *ashkana* (cheap canteens) are the place to find it. A plate of plov in a working bazaar canteen at lunchtime costs a fraction of what it would in Almaty, and the rice is usually better.

The other regional specialities to look for are **laghman** (hand-pulled noodles in lamb broth), **manty** (large steamed dumplings), **shashlik** (skewered grilled lamb), and **horse meat dishes** like **kazy** sausage and **beshbarmak** (boiled meat over flat noodles). The latter is closer to a ceremonial dish than an everyday one but most of the larger Kazakh restaurants will serve it on request.
