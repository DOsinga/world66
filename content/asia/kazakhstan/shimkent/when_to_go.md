---
title: 'When to Go'
type: section
---

The shoulder seasons are by far the best. **April to early June** is warm, green and full of wildflowers, with the mountain reserves at their most accessible. **September to October** is dry, golden and similarly comfortable, with daytime temperatures in the low 20s and clear skies.

Summer is brutal — daytime temperatures regularly cross 40 degrees from late June through August and the city becomes uncomfortable to walk in. Winter is cold but rarely extreme by Kazakh standards, with daytime highs around freezing from December to February and occasional snow.
