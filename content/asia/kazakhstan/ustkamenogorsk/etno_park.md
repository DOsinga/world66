Etno Park is a family-oriented cultural park with replicas of traditional homes, a large mosque, flower displays, a zoo, and outdoor exhibits of Soviet-era military equipment. It is a more appealing stop in warm weather, when the park grounds and open-air displays are easier to enjoy.
