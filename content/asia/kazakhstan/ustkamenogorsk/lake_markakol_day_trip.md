[Lake Markakol](/asia/kazakhstan/lakemarkakol) lies about 250 km southeast of Ust-Kamenogorsk in the Altai Mountains, near the Chinese and Russian borders. The lake sits in a high mountain basin at around 1,450 metres and is surrounded by the Markakol Nature Reserve — one of the least-visited and most scenic protected areas in Kazakhstan. Dense Siberian taiga, wild meadows, and the lake itself, which is deep, cold, and strikingly blue, make for an exceptional landscape.

The drive takes five to six hours on roads that vary from decent tarmac to rough tracks approaching the lake; a four-wheel drive is advisable. Possible as a long day trip in summer, though an overnight stay in the small settlement of Urunkhaika allows more time in the reserve. The lake has brown trout and rare lenok; fishing is permitted in designated areas. Expect to share the road with few other tourists.
