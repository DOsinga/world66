The Grand Mosque is the largest mosque in Kuwait, a handsome building in central Kuwait City that offers free guided tours for non-Muslim visitors except during prayer times. The tours, typically led by knowledgeable English-speaking guides, cover Islamic prayer practice, the architectural significance of the mosque's design, and Kuwait's relationship with Islam.

The interior is impressive: the main prayer hall has a gilded chandelier seven metres in diameter, an elaborate geometric ceiling, and walls of Turkish marble. The mosque complex also includes a library and a smaller women's prayer hall. The tour provides a genuine and respectful introduction to Islamic practice rather than a superficial tourist experience.

Visitors should dress modestly — women will need to cover their hair and wear loose clothing; men should have their arms covered. Robes and headscarves are available at the entrance if needed. Tours run most mornings; confirm times at the mosque entrance.
