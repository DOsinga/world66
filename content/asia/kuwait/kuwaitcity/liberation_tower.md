The Liberation Tower is Kuwait's tallest structure, a 372-metre telecommunications tower in central Kuwait City that rivals the Kuwait Towers as the city's visual landmark. An observation deck at 300 metres offers the most comprehensive view of Kuwait City and the Gulf, and on clear days the outline of the Iraqi coast is visible to the north.

A revolving restaurant near the top provides a more leisurely way to take in the view. The tower is less visited than the Kuwait Towers — partly because it is primarily a telecommunications facility rather than a designed attraction — but the observation deck is genuinely worth the visit for the panoramic perspective it gives over the flat, sprawling city and the surrounding desert.

Tickets for the observation deck and restaurant are available at the base. Check opening hours in advance, as they vary with demand.
