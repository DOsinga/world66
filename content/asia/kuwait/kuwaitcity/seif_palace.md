Seif Palace is one of Kuwait City's key royal landmarks, standing near the old commercial heart and the waterfront. Visitors generally view it from outside, but its gates, clock tower, Islamic detailing, and official scale make it a useful stop on a walk through the older part of the capital.

The palace helps connect Kuwait's merchant-town past with its modern state identity. Combine it with the Grand Mosque, Souk Al-Mubarakiya, and the National Museum area for a compact introduction to the city before heading out to the towers and newer districts.
