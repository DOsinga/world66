Salmiya is Kuwait's second city in all but name — a densely populated district about 12 km south of central [Kuwait City](/asia/kuwait/kuwaitcity) that serves as the country's main commercial and entertainment hub. Over 90% of its 150,000 residents are expatriates, giving it a cosmopolitan, multicultural atmosphere unlike anywhere else in the Gulf.

[Salem Al-Mubarak Street](/asia/kuwait/salmiya/salem_al_mubarak_street) is the main shopping drag, lined with malls, restaurants, and a mix of modern stores and older shops that retain something of Kuwait's traditional souk character. The [Marina Crescent](/asia/kuwait/salmiya/marina_crescent) area offers waterfront dining and pleasant evening strolls along the bay.

Salmiya is home to the [Kuwait Scientific Centre](/asia/kuwait/salmiya/kuwait_scientific_centre), one of the best attractions in the country. This waterfront complex houses the largest aquarium in the Middle East, with over 100 species, plus an IMAX cinema and a discovery centre. The [Sheikh Abdullah Al-Salem Cultural Centre](/asia/kuwait/salmiya/sheikh_abdullah_cultural_centre), one of the largest museum complexes in the world, is nearby. On the south shore, the [Al-Hashemi-II](/asia/kuwait/salmiya/al_hashemi_marine_museum) — the world's largest wooden boat — serves as a maritime museum.

Salmiya is walkable in its central area during cooler months, and taxis to Kuwait City take only about 10 minutes during the day. It makes a practical base for exploring the country, with more dining options and a livelier street scene than central Kuwait City.
