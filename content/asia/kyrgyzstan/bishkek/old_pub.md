A British-style pub in central Bishkek, a long-running and reliably busy meeting place. Dark wood, dim lighting, a long list of beers on tap and in bottle, and a kitchen that turns out competent burgers and fish and chips. The Old Pub is where the city's small Anglophone community gathers to watch football, and it remains a useful first-night spot for travellers wanting an easy meal in a familiar setting.

Live music on Friday and Saturday evenings; occasional pub quiz nights advertised on Facebook.
