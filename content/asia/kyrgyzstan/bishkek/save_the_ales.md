A craft-beer bar in central Bishkek, popular with the expat and traveller crowd. Save the Ales pours a rotating list of about 20 beers including the rare local craft brewery output (Steinbrau, Save the Ales' own) and imported Russian and German beers. The food is pub-standard burgers, wings, and a passable pizza.

Live music some weekends; the front terrace fills up on summer evenings.
