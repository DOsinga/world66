A small leafy park near the Old Square, named for the late-19th-century Kyrgyz poet Togolok Moldo. The park is essentially a tree-shaded oblong with benches, a bronze statue of Moldo with his komuz, and a row of pleasant outdoor cafes along its southern edge.

The space is most useful as a green pause-point when walking between the central squares, especially in summer when the Bishkek pavements get unforgiving.
