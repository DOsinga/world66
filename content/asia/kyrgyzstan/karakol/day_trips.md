---
title: "Day Trips"
type: section
---

Karakol is surrounded by some of the most dramatic scenery in Central Asia, and several outstanding destinations lie within a half-day's drive or a full day's walk. The valleys immediately south of town — Karakol Gorge, Altyn-Arashan, and Jeti-Oguz — are the obvious choices. Further afield, the southern shore of Issyk-Kul offers hot springs, canyon walks, and Soviet-era resorts in various states of revival.

Most guesthouses can arrange 4WD transport to the main valley trailheads for around 1,000–2,000 som, depending on destination. Marshrutkas from the bazaar cover Jeti-Oguz and some other western valleys in the morning and return in the afternoon. For the Przhevalsky Museum and mausoleum on the lakeshore, a short taxi ride suffices.
