The Karakol Gorge is the valley that rises immediately south of town, carved by the Karakol River as it descends from the Central Tian Shan. The gorge begins with dense Tian Shan spruce forest — old-growth conifers that thin higher up to reveal open meadows and eventually bare rock and glaciers above 3,000 metres. The river runs cold and fast beside the trail.

As a day trip, the gorge rewards a straightforward walk of four to six hours up into the spruce zone and back, with views opening steadily as you climb. The trail is the start of the Ala-Kul circuit, so you will pass day-trippers heading up and trekkers returning from multi-day routes. Yurt camps operate in the upper valley in summer and serve tea and simple food. The trailhead is accessible by shared taxi from any guesthouse in Karakol; the drive takes about 20 minutes.
