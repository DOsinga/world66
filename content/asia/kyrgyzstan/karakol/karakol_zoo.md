Karakol Zoo is Kyrgyzstan's only zoo, a modest local collection that survived the hard years after the Soviet collapse through community support. Expect a simple place rather than a polished wildlife park, with bears, wolves, deer, camels, Przewalski's horses, and peacocks among the animals.
