Kochkor's animal bazaar is the town at its most alive. On Saturday morning, farmers and herders come in to trade sheep, cattle, horses, tack, fodder, and the everyday goods that keep village life moving.

Go early, dress for dust or mud, and keep your camera polite. It is a good place to understand Kochkor's role as a market town rather than just a booking office for the mountains.
