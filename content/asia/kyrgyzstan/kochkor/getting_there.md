---
title: Getting There
type: section
---

Kochkor sits on the road between Bishkek, Issyk-Kul, and Naryn. Marshrutkas and shared taxis connect it with Bishkek, Balykchy, Karakol, and Naryn, though direct services vary by season and demand.

The bus and taxi stand on the main road is the place to start asking. For remote jailoo trips, book transport through a local office or arrange a shared taxi; schedules are loose once you leave the main highway.
