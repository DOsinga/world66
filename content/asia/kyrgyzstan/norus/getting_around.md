---
title: Getting Around
type: section
---

Norus is a single valley and a small resort — there is nowhere to get around to in the usual sense. The ski area is compact enough to navigate on foot, and the lodge is right at the base of the lifts.

If you want to explore further up the valley to Ysyk-Ata resort or reach the summer hiking trails above the treeline, you will need your own vehicle or a taxi. The valley road continues a few kilometres past Norus, and it is walkable in summer if you are not in a hurry.
