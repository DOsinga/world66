Abshyr-Ata Waterfall is a mountain day trip from Osh, set in a narrow gorge with a waterfall that emerges dramatically from the rock. The site is also treated as sacred, so the visit is as much about local pilgrimage and picnic culture as it is about scenery.

The road and facilities are basic, but the outing gives a quick taste of the mountains west of the Fergana Valley. Go with a driver who knows the route, bring sturdy shoes for wet rock near the falls, and avoid expecting a manicured tourist attraction. Its charm is rough-edged.
