A small white-walled prayer cell perched near the summit of Sulaiman-Too, the Takht-i-Sulaiman (also called the Babur Mosque) is one of the most visited spots in Osh — for its associations with the Mughal emperor and for the panoramic view from the rocky terrace beside it.

The interior is simply a single chamber with a low ceiling, lit by a few windows. Pilgrims pray and leave coins on the ledges. The walk up takes about twenty minutes from the museum, on a stone path that is steep but not difficult.
