Osh's Russian Orthodox church, dedicated to Archangel Michael, is a small but telling landmark in a city better known for Sulaiman-Too, bazaars, and Islamic sites. Its presence reflects the Russian and Soviet layers in southern Kyrgyzstan's history, as well as the mixed communities that have long passed through Osh.

The church is worth a respectful short visit if you are exploring central Osh. Dress modestly, avoid services unless attending appropriately, and treat it as a living religious site. It adds a useful counterpoint to the city's mosques, mausoleums, and mountain shrines.
