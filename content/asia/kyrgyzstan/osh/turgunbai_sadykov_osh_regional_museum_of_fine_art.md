This small regional art museum is a useful counterpoint to Osh's bazaars and sacred mountain. The collection leans toward Soviet realist painting and sculpture, with regional Kyrgyz works that give a quick look at how the south of the country has been represented in modern art.

It is not a blockbuster museum, but it is central, quiet, and worth adding if you have an interest in Soviet-era art or want an indoor stop between the bazaar and Sulaiman-Too.
