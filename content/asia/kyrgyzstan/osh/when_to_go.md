---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

Osh sits in the southern lowlands of Kyrgyzstan and has a continental, semi-arid climate — hot dry summers and cold dry winters, with brief but pleasant spring and autumn seasons in between.

**April to May** and **September to October** are the best times to visit. Daytime temperatures sit between 18 and 28°C, the surrounding countryside is green (in spring) or golden (in autumn), and the bazaar is at its best.

**June to August** are very hot — easily over 35°C in the daytime — and the air can feel still and dusty. Sulaiman-Too is best climbed early in the morning. **November to March** are cold, sometimes snowy, and the surrounding mountain roads close, but the city itself remains accessible and the bazaars still work.
