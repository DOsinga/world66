Tamga is a small village on the quieter south shore of [Lake Issyk-Kul](/asia/kyrgyzstan/lakeissykkul), set just inland from the beach and backed by the dry foothills of the Terskey Ala-Too. It is not polished, and that is its appeal: guesthouses, lake air, dusty lanes, and easy access to some of the south shore's best short excursions.

The village takes its name from [Tamga Tash](/asia/kyrgyzstan/tamga/tamga_tash), a rock bearing a Buddhist mantra in the hills south of town. Nearby, [Skazka Canyon](/asia/kyrgyzstan/tamga/skazka_canyon) turns soft red and yellow sediment into strange little ridges and towers, while [Barskoon Gorge](/asia/kyrgyzstan/tamga/barskoon_gorge) climbs from the lake road into waterfalls, spruce, and high pasture.

Tamga is best for travelers with their own wheels or time to hire a taxi for the day. If you only want cafes and an easy lake resort, the north shore is simpler. If you want a low-key base for the south shore's rock, water, and mountain scenery, Tamga earns its place.
