Tamga's beach is simple: reddish sand and pebbles, wide lake views, and the blue wall of Issyk-Kul stretching toward the northern mountains. It sits about a kilometre from the village, close enough for a swim or evening walk.

Do not expect resort infrastructure. The reward is space, clear water in summer, and a quieter south-shore mood than the busier beaches around Cholpon-Ata.
