title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Uzgen's two draws are its medieval Islamic monuments and its bazaar. The [Uzgen Minaret](/asia/kyrgyzstan/uzgen/uzgen_minaret) and the [Karakhanid Mausoleums](/asia/kyrgyzstan/uzgen/karakhanid_mausoleums) stand together on a low bluff above the Kara Darya river and can be visited in an hour. The [Uzgen Bazaar](/asia/kyrgyzstan/uzgen/uzgen_bazaar) is best on weekdays when Uzbek traders from the surrounding villages fill the stalls.

There is nothing else to detain a traveller — plan on a half-day from Osh or Jalal-Abad and move on.
