---

Huay Xai is primarily a transit point, but the town has a pleasant, unhurried atmosphere and is worth an overnight stay. Most travellers are here for the slow boat to [Luang Prabang](/asia/laos/luangprabang) or for the Gibbon Experience in the Bokeo Nature Reserve.

The temple of Vat Chom Khao Manilat sits on a small hill above town, reached by a flight of steps. It is a working Buddhist temple with fine views over the Mekong and across to Chiang Khong in Thailand. The riverside itself — a strip of guesthouses and restaurants overlooking the wide brown river — is the place to sit in the evenings and watch the sun go down over the Thai bank.
