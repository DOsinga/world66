The Luang Namtha Provincial Museum occupies a modest building on the main road near the centre of town. Its collections cover the province's archaeological history, with artefacts from the prehistoric period through to the colonial era, and a substantial section on the traditional cultures of the region's diverse ethnic communities. Displays include traditional textiles, tools, musical instruments, and ceremonial objects.

The museum is small by international standards but is genuinely informative and well-organised for a provincial institution. English signage is present throughout. It is the right place to start before embarking on any trekking in the area, providing context for what you will see in the villages. Admission is nominal; opening hours are irregular and it is worth checking current times at your guesthouse.
