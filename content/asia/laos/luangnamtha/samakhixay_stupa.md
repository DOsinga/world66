The Samakhixay Stupa stands on a hill on the outskirts of Luang Namtha, accessible by a road that winds up from the valley floor. The stupa itself is a gold-painted Buddhist monument in the standard Lao style; what makes the visit worthwhile is the panoramic view from the hilltop over the Luang Namtha valley, with forested mountains visible in every direction. It is a good orientation point for understanding the landscape before setting out on treks into the surrounding hills.

The walk from town takes 30–45 minutes; the road is motorable. Come in the late afternoon for the best light and cooler temperatures. The hill is quiet at most times and provides a genuine escape from the (modest) noise of the town below.
