Every morning at around 5.30am, hundreds of monks and novices from the city's thirty-plus temples walk in single file through the old town streets, collecting alms from kneeling residents. The procession — tak bat in Lao — is one of the most moving sights in Southeast Asia: silent, unhurried, saffron robes glowing in the early light.

Please observe the ceremony with discretion. Stand well back (at least five metres), do not use flash photography, and avoid making direct eye contact with or touching the monks. A small industry of tourist alms-giving has developed — pre-packaged sticky rice sold to tourists to donate — which the monks and Lao authorities consider disruptive to the ritual. If you want to give alms, buy your own rice from a market stall the evening before and ask your guesthouse how to do it properly.
