Heuan Chan Heritage House is a restored traditional Lao house in central Luang Prabang. It introduces domestic architecture and local crafts in a more intimate setting than the city's temples and royal museum, with displays and hands-on activities that help explain how older houses were used.

It is a good short stop while walking the old town, especially if you want a quieter cultural visit.
