Kuang Si Butterfly Park is a small conservation and education project near the famous waterfalls outside Luang Prabang. It uses planted gardens, netted enclosures, and interpretation to highlight Laos' butterflies and tropical ecology.

It is best visited as part of a Kuang Si Falls trip rather than as a separate excursion. The park adds a quieter natural-history layer before or after the crowds at the falls.
