Ock Pop Tok Living Craft Centre is a fair-trade weaving centre on the Mekong side of Luang Prabang. Visitors can tour the workshops, see silk dyeing and weaving, take short craft classes, or stop at the riverside cafe, making it one of the city's better craft-focused stops.
