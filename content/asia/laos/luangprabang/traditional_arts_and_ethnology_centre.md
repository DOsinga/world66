The Traditional Arts and Ethnology Centre is a small, well-focused museum on the ethnic cultures of Laos. Its exhibits explain textiles, dress, ritual objects, and everyday traditions clearly enough to make the villages, markets, and craft shops around Luang Prabang more meaningful.
