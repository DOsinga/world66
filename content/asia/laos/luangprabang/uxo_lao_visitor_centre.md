The UXO Lao Visitor Centre explains the continuing impact of unexploded ordnance in Laos. Displays include examples of ordnance found in the country, background on clearance work, and a documentary room.

It is a small centre, but it gives important context for travelling in Laos beyond Luang Prabang's temples and scenery. The visit is short and direct, and it makes the scale of the issue much easier to understand.
