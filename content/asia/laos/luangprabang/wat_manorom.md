Wat Manorom, on the southwestern edge of the old town, is one of Luang Prabang's oldest temples — founded in the 14th century. The main building houses a massive bronze seated Buddha, one of the largest in the city, with a somewhat weathered, ancient quality that contrasts with the more polished temples in the centre.

The compound is quieter and less visited than Wat Xieng Thong or the Royal Palace, which gives it a more authentic atmosphere. The resident monks and novices use it as a working monastery rather than a tourist attraction. Worth visiting in the late afternoon when the light is good and the monks may be chanting.
