Dao Heuang Market is Pakse's big everyday market, spreading through a busy trading area near the bridge approach. It is a practical place to see southern Lao produce, coffee, snacks, household goods, and textiles moving through a city that still feels like a regional crossroads.

Go early if you want the market at its most active. It is not curated for visitors, which is the point: expect heat, motorbikes, bargaining, and a jumble of stalls rather than tidy souvenir aisles. Keep valuables close and treat it as a wandering stop before heading to the Bolaven Plateau or Wat Phu.
