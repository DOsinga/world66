Xiengkhoung Provincial Museum is the best indoor introduction to the province around Phonsavan. Exhibits cover local archaeology, ethnic communities, wartime history, and the landscape that leads most travellers onward to the Plain of Jars.

It is worth visiting before the jar sites if you want context rather than just photographs. The museum also helps explain why this quiet-looking plateau carries such a heavy 20th-century history.
