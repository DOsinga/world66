Savannakhet's Dinosaur Museum is a small, slightly unexpected stop built around fossils found in southern Laos. The displays are modest, but the subject is distinctive enough to make it more memorable than many provincial museums.

It is especially good with children or anyone interested in natural history. Do not expect a polished international museum; expect a local collection that broadens Savannakhet beyond colonial streets, temples, and the Mekong.
