Wat Sayaphoum is one of Savannakhet's most important active temples, set close to the Mekong in the older part of town. Its gilded details, shaded compound, and everyday monastic life make it a useful counterpoint to the city's French colonial streets.

Visit quietly and treat it as a living religious site rather than a museum. The best time is early or late in the day, when the light is softer and the temple grounds feel woven into the rhythm of the riverside town.
