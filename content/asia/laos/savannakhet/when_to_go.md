---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

November to February is the coolest and most comfortable period — dry, clear, and around 20-30°C in the day. March-May heats up significantly before the rains arrive. The rainy season (June-October) sees the Mekong rise dramatically and the surrounding countryside turn green. Most travellers pass through in the dry season, but the rainy season is quieter and the riverside atmosphere is different when the river is full.
