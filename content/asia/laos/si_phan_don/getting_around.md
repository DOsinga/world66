---
title: "Getting Around"
type: section
---

Within Si Phan Don, bicycles are the main transport. Guesthouses on Don Det and Don Khone rent them for a few thousand kip per day; the islands are flat and the paths quiet. Cycling around both islands takes a comfortable half day.

Boats are necessary for reaching the dolphin pool at the southern end of Don Khone, the Khon Phapheng Falls, and any of the smaller islands. Boatmen at the southern tip of Don Khone offer fixed-price trips to the dolphin pool; combined boat-bicycle trips taking in all the sights can be arranged through guesthouses.

The ferry between Ban Nakasang and Don Det runs throughout the day and evening. A bridge connecting Don Det and Don Khone (via the French railway bridge) allows walking between the two main islands without a boat.
