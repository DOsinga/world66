---
title: "Getting There"
type: section
---

From [Pakse](/asia/laos/pakse), buses and minivans run south to Ban Nakasang (the ferry landing opposite Don Det) in about 2.5 to 3 hours. Buses depart from Pakse's southern bus station. The minivan service is faster and more comfortable, and guesthouses in Pakse can arrange tickets. A short boat across from Ban Nakasang takes you to Don Det.

From Cambodia, the main crossing is at Nong Nok Khiene/Trapaeng Kriel, the border point connecting Stung Treng in Cambodia with Si Phan Don. Buses from Phnom Penh and Siem Reap go to this border, and onward transport to Don Det is available.

There is no airport at Si Phan Don. Pakse is the nearest airport, with domestic flights to Vientiane and Luang Prabang, and international connections to Bangkok and Vietnam.
