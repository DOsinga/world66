Li Phi Falls on the western side of Don Khone are a series of powerful rapids in a narrow gorge where one of the Mekong's braided channels drops over a rocky ledge. The falls are not high — perhaps 2-3 metres — but the force of the water and the turbulence of the gorge make them impressive. In the wet season the volume is overwhelming; in the dry season the ledge is more visible and the surrounding rocks accessible.

The falls are accessible from the western path on Don Khone, about a 20-minute walk or 10-minute cycle from the French railway bridge. A small entry fee is charged. The setting — a wild gorge in a tropical river — is excellent, and the falls are worth combining with the locomotive at the southern tip and a stop for coffee at one of the simple restaurants near the bridge.
