---
title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Si Phan Don's main activities are things that don't look like activities on paper: watching sunsets from a riverside bungalow, cycling the islands on their quiet paths, taking boats to the waterfalls, and waiting with patience for the dolphins. The Khon Phapheng Falls and the Irrawaddy dolphins are the main specific sights; the islands themselves are the experience.
