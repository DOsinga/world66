---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

The dry season (November to April) is the most comfortable time to visit Si Phan Don. The weather is cooler, the roads and paths accessible, and the islands have their characteristic golden-hour light. The Mekong runs lower, exposing more of the rocky landscape and making some of the smaller rapids navigable by boat.

The wet season (May to October) brings heavy rain, high water, and a different beauty. The waterfalls are at their most powerful, the islands reduced in size as the river rises, and the humidity is intense. Some guesthouses close. The benefit is fewer travellers and the extraordinary sight of the Mekong in full flood.

Avoid late March to May if possible — hot, dry, and the least attractive season.
