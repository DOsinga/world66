---
title: "Eating Out"
type: section
---

Vang Vieng has a surprisingly varied food scene given its size. Lao staples — sticky rice, laap, spicy papaya salad — share the menu with French colonial holdovers: proper baguettes, pâté, and strong coffee. Most restaurants cluster along the main street and the riverbank, with the river-view spots charging a modest premium worth paying at sunset.
