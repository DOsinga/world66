Padeng Cave and Ring Cave are part of the limestone cave landscape that makes Vang Vieng more than a river-tubing stop. They offer a rougher, less packaged outing than the most visited blue lagoons and show how quickly the town gives way to karst, fields, and village paths.

Bring a light, sturdy shoes, and realistic expectations: facilities are limited and conditions can change with rain. The reward is the classic Vang Vieng combination of cave exploration, rural scenery, and sharp limestone ridges above the valley.
