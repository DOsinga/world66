Pha Ngeun is one of the classic Vang Vieng viewpoints, reached by a steep climb through forest and limestone terrain west of town. The reward is the view that explains why people come here: rice fields spread across the valley floor, the Nam Song River curves below, and karst towers rise in every direction.

The trail is short in distance but sweaty and rough, especially after rain. Wear shoes with grip, carry water, and start early or late in the day if you want the light without the worst heat. It is a simple hike, but the panorama is one of Vang Vieng's strongest sights.
