title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Vang Vieng is built around its landscape. The limestone karst formations that ring the valley are riddled with caves, draped in jungle, and reflected in clear blue swimming holes. You can engage with all of this at whatever pace suits you — floating passively through a water cave, climbing a sheer cliff face, or cycling slowly through rice paddies with the mountains as a backdrop.
