---
title: 'Getting Around'
type: section
---

Vientiane is a small capital and most of the central sights can be walked between in under twenty minutes. The main exception is Pha That Luang, which is a long walk or a short tuk-tuk ride from the centre.

**Tuk-tuks** are the default short-distance transport. Drivers cluster around hotels, the markets, and the main monuments. Always agree the fare before you climb in — expect around 20,000-50,000 kip for a hop within the centre and around 80,000-120,000 kip for the Buddha Park run. Tuk-tuks rarely meter; bargaining is normal.

**Bicycles** are an excellent way to see the city. The grid is flat, the traffic is gentler than in Bangkok, and bikes can be hired for around 20,000 kip a day from most guesthouses. Mornings and late afternoons are coolest. A handful of shops also rent motorbikes.

**Ride-hailing.** The Lao app **LOCA** works like a local Uber and is more transparent than haggling for a tuk-tuk. Coverage in the centre is reliable; airport runs are easy to book.

**Taxis** are scarce in the centre and mostly congregate at the airport and hotels. They are useful for trips beyond the city or for early-morning departures to the train station.
