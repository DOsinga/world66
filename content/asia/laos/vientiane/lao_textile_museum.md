The Lao Textile Museum is a semi-open-air museum focused on Lao weaving, clothing, dyeing, and textile traditions. Visitors can see looms and weavers at work, along with examples of traditional dress and dyed thread.

It is a good stop for anyone interested in craft rather than just shopping for finished textiles. The courtyard and small tastings make the visit feel relaxed and personal.
