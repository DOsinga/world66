The Cedars of God — Arz el-Rab — are about 8 km east of Bcharre on the road up toward the Mzaar ski area, at an altitude of around 2,000 m. The surviving grove is small but the individual trees are extraordinary: a few are over a thousand years old, with massive gnarled trunks and broad horizontal canopies.

A short loop trail winds through the forest, and there's a small chapel built among the trees from cedar wood. Go in the morning before the tour buses arrive, and ideally in winter or early spring when there's snow on the branches — it's then that you understand why this image ended up on a national flag. Entrance is a few thousand Lebanese pounds.
