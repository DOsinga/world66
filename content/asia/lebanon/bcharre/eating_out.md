---
title: "Eating Out"
type: section
---

Bcharre is small and the restaurant scene is correspondingly modest, but you'll eat well — this is mountain Lebanon, with mezze, charcoal-grilled meats and trout from the Qadisha. Most places cluster around the main square.

**Makhlouf** is the long-running favourite for kibbeh, sajj manakish and the local arak. **Tiger House** does a generous mixed grill platter and has a terrace looking out over the gorge. For something lighter, the cafes on the main square serve coffee, fresh juices and pastries from the local bakery.

In the valley itself, **Bauhaus** at the Mar Lichaa overlook is worth the drive for the view and a long lunch of mezze and grilled meat. Prices everywhere in town are very reasonable — a full mezze meal for two with drinks rarely tops 30 USD.
