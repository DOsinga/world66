---
title: "Getting There"
type: section
---

Bcharre is about 120 km from [Beirut](/asia/lebanon/beirut) but the drive takes around two and a half hours because the last stretch climbs steadily up into the mountains. Most travellers come by hired car or taxi — the road via Chekka and Amioun is the usual route and is in reasonable shape.

Shared service taxis (vans) run from Beirut's Dora station to Bcharre a few times a day, costing very little but slow. There is no rail service in Lebanon. From [Tripoli](/asia/lebanon/tripoli) the drive is faster — about 50 km, around an hour and a half — and a more common starting point if you've already worked your way north.
