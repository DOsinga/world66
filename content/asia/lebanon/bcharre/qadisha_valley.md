UNESCO recognised the whole landscape as a World Heritage Site in 1998."

The Qadisha (or Kadisha) Valley is a deep limestone gorge that cuts west from Bcharre toward the sea, with sheer cliffs, terraced orchards, and a river running through the bottom. Hidden in its folds are some of the oldest monastic communities in the Christian world — caves, chapels and hermitages, many still in use, some only reachable on foot.

From Bcharre a paved road switchbacks down to the valley floor, where a string of footpaths links the main monasteries. The classic walk takes a full day: descend at Bcharre, follow the river west past Mar Lichaa and Qannoubine, and climb up the far side. Bring proper shoes, water, and a willingness to keep going when the path gets steep. This is the single best thing to do in the region.
