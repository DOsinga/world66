---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

Bcharre's two seasons are completely different trips. **May to October** is hiking weather: warm days, cool nights, the gorge and the cedar grove in full leaf, the road over to the [Bekaa Valley](/asia/lebanon/baalbek) open for traffic. June is probably ideal — the worst summer heat hasn't arrived in the mountains and there are still spring flowers above the tree line.

**December to early April** is the ski season. Bcharre fills up with Lebanese weekenders and there's a festive atmosphere in the cafes. The trade-off is that the higher trails are snowbound and the road through to Baalbek is closed.

**Late March to early May** and **late October to November** are the awkward seasons: skiing is over, hiking is wet and cold, and many guesthouses close. Avoid these months unless you want the town to yourself.
