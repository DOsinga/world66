---
title: "Getting There"
type: section
---

Beiteddine is around 50 kilometres southeast of [Beirut](/asia/lebanon/beirut) and 90 minutes by car. The drive climbs through the Chouf — orchards of olive, fig, and pomegranate, then pine forests as the road approaches the ridge. Buses from the **Cola** terminal in Beirut leave every couple of hours for [Deir el Qamar](/asia/lebanon/deirelqamar) and continue to Beiteddine; service is slow and best avoided in winter. Most visitors come on a half-day tour from Beirut or hire a driver for around US$80 a day.
