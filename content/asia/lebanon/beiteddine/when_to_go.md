---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

At 800 metres above sea level, the Chouf is cooler than the coast — about 10 °C below Beirut on a summer day. **May, June, and September** are the perfect months: warm, dry, clear views across the valley. **July and August** are warm and pleasant for visits but the festival weeks book out the village. **Winter** can be cold and damp, with occasional snow blocking the higher roads; the palace stays open but the festival is silent.
