---
title: "Getting There"
type: section
---

Sidon is 40 km south of [Beirut](/asia/lebanon/beirut) on the coastal highway. Service taxis (shared minibuses) leave frequently from Cola roundabout in Beirut and take about an hour. Private taxis are quick but more expensive — agree the fare before getting in.

There is no passenger railway in Lebanon. From Sidon, the same service taxis continue south to [Tyre](/asia/lebanon/tyre) and east into the mountains. Security conditions in southern Lebanon have varied since 2024; check travel advice before going.
