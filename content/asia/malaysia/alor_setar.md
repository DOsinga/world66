Alor Setar is the capital of Kedah, but it feels less like a hard-edged administrative city than a Malay royal town set in rice country. Paddy fields press close to the outskirts, while the centre gathers mosques, royal buildings, markets, and a tall viewing tower into a walkable cluster.

The heritage core around [Zahir Mosque](/asia/malaysia/alor_setar/zahir_mosque), [Balai Besar](/asia/malaysia/alor_setar/balai_besar), and [Balai Nobat](/asia/malaysia/alor_setar/balai_nobat) is the best place to start. It gives you Kedah's courtly side without making the city feel like an open-air museum.

The other identity is rice. [Paddy Museum](/asia/malaysia/alor_setar/paddy_museum) sits north of town among fields and is more memorable than its plain name suggests. Add [Alor Setar Tower](/asia/malaysia/alor_setar/alor_setar_tower) for orientation and [Pekan Rabu](/asia/malaysia/alor_setar/pekan_rabu) for local shopping, and Alor Setar is easily worth a day between Penang, Langkawi, or the Thai border.
