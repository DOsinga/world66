Pekan Rabu, the Wednesday Market, is more shopping complex than street market now, but it remains a useful stop for local colour. Look for Kedah snacks, textiles, songkok, small crafts, and the everyday trade of a state capital.

It is close to the royal quarter, so it works best as a practical break between sights. Come with modest expectations and an appetite for local sweets or savoury snacks.
