Zahir Mosque is Alor Setar's finest building. White walls, black domes, and slender minarets give it a calm, balanced look, especially from the open ground around the royal quarter.

Visitors should dress modestly and avoid prayer times if hoping to look inside. Even from outside, it is the building that fixes Alor Setar in the memory.
