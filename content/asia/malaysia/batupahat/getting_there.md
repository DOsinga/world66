---
title: Getting There
type: section
---

Batu Pahat is on the **PLUS Expressway** (E2) between Kuala Lumpur and Johor Bahru — exit 235 or 236 — making the drive from JB around 90 minutes and from KL three and a half to four hours. There is no train station: the **ETS** main line runs well inland through Kluang, around an hour away by taxi.

**Long-distance buses** are the standard option for those without a car. Express coaches run hourly to Johor Bahru, Singapore (Larkin Terminal), Kuala Lumpur (TBS), and Melaka from the **Terminal Bas Batu Pahat** in town. The nearest major airport is **Senai International** in JB (90 minutes south).
