Danum Valley is one of Sabah's great rainforest reserves, known for primary forest, canopy walks, birds, gibbons, orangutans, and serious naturalist appeal. It is more expensive and logistically involved than Sepilok or the Kinabatangan, but the forest quality is exceptional.

Most visitors arrange stays through lodges or research-centre-linked operators. It is a place to slow down, listen, and let guides point out what untrained eyes miss.
