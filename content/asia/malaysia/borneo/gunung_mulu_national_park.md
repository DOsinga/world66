Gunung Mulu National Park is Sarawak at its most spectacular: giant limestone caves, rainforest trails, canopy walks, and the jagged Pinnacles of Gunung Api. It is a UNESCO-listed park and one of the strongest reasons to fly into [Miri](/asia/malaysia/miri).

Mulu rewards time. The show caves are accessible, but the Pinnacles trek and deeper cave trips need planning, fitness, and guides.
