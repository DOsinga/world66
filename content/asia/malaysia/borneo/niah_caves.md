The Niah Caves, south-west of Miri, combine immense limestone chambers with deep archaeological importance. Boardwalks lead through forest to the Great Cave, where swiftlet nests, guano, and human history all overlap.

Bring a torch, shoes with grip, and a tolerance for damp cave smells. Niah is less famous than Mulu, but easier to reach by road and deeply atmospheric.
