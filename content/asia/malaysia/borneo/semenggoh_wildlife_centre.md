Semenggoh Wildlife Centre, outside Kuching, offers one of Sarawak's most accessible chances to see orangutans. The animals are semi-wild, moving through the surrounding forest and sometimes appearing at feeding platforms.

It is best visited with humility. Sightings depend on fruiting seasons and animal choice, which is exactly as it should be.
