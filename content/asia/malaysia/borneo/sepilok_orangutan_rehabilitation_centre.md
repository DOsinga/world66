Sepilok, near Sandakan in Sabah, is the best-known orangutan rehabilitation centre in Malaysian Borneo. Visitors usually come for feeding times, when semi-wild orangutans may appear from the forest.

It is an easy wildlife stop, but it should not be treated like a zoo. Keep distance, follow staff instructions, and combine it with the nearby sun bear centre if you have time.
