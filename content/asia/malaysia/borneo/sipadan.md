Sipadan is Malaysia's legendary dive site, off Sabah's east coast near Semporna. Permits are limited, and most divers stay on nearby islands or the mainland rather than on Sipadan itself.

This is not a casual snorkel stop. Go with a reputable operator, book well ahead, and pay attention to current security and sea conditions in eastern Sabah.
