---
title: When to Go
type: section
---

Borneo is hot and humid year-round, with rain possible in any month. The driest general window is often March to October, though local patterns vary between Sabah, Sarawak, mountains, and coasts.

For Mount Kinabalu, clearer weather usually matters more than temperature. For diving around Sipadan and Sabah's east coast, check sea conditions and permit availability well ahead.
