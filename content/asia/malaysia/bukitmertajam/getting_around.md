---
title: "Getting Around"
type: section
---

The old town centre is compact and walkable. Motorbike taxis and Grab are the practical options for reaching the hill trailhead at Cherok Tok Kun, which is about 3 kilometres from the railway station. Local buses connect the town to surrounding villages and to Butterworth. George Town on Penang island is around 30 minutes away by taxi or Grab via the Penang Bridge.
