  Founded in 1918, Jit Sin is one of the few remaining Chinese-medium secondary schools in Malaysia. It is consistently ranked among Malaysia's top secondary schools in national examinations and has produced several prominent Malaysians in science and public life.

Jit Sin National Type Chinese High School sits about 3 kilometres south of the town centre in the Bukit Mertajam district. The school was established in 1918 by the Chinese community of Seberang Perai as a community-funded institution, a common model in the Straits Settlements where Chinese merchant communities financed their own schools rather than relying on colonial provision.

The school buildings are not architecturally notable but the institution itself is a living example of the Chinese educational tradition that survived Malaysia's post-independence education reforms. Visitors with an interest in Malaysian Chinese history may find the campus interesting, and the school hosts occasional public events. The area around the school is a comfortable residential neighbourhood typical of modern Malaysian suburbia, with good hawker stalls nearby.
