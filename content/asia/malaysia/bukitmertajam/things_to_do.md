title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Bukit Mertajam is not a major tourist destination, but it rewards those who take the time to look around. The hill itself is the main draw — the climb through tropical forest is popular with local families at weekends, and the views across the Penang strait are good on clear days. The old town centre has genuine character: Chinese shophouses in varying states of preservation, Tamil temples, and a market culture that remains rooted in the town's working life rather than shaped for visitors.
