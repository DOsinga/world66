The Boh Tea Centre at Sungai Palas combines a working tea plantation with factory tours, a small visitor centre, a shop, and a cafe overlooking the green tea slopes. The narrow road up is part of the effort, but the hilltop views are among the classic Cameron Highlands scenes.
