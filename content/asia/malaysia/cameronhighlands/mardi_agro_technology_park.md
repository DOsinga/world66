MARDI Agro Technology Park is an agricultural research centre that also works as a visitor garden, with roses, strawberries, citrus plots, hydroponic displays, and cafes. It is a practical stop for seeing the cultivated side of the Cameron Highlands, and Trail 5 starts nearby.
