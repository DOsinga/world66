Mossy Forest sits high on Gunung Brinchang, where a boardwalk runs through cloud forest wrapped in moss, ferns, and twisted old trees. The forest can be wet, cool, and misty even when the tea valleys below are clear.

Parts of the moss cover have suffered from heavy visitor traffic, but the short walk and viewpoint still make this one of the Cameron Highlands' most distinctive highland stops. Go early if you want the best chance of mist and thinner crowds.
