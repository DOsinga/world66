Robinson Waterfall is one of the easier natural targets from Tanah Rata, reached by following the Cameron Highlands trail network toward Path 9. The falls are close enough for a short outing, but the surrounding jungle makes the walk feel properly away from town.

Trails in the highlands can be slippery and poorly marked after rain, so wear shoes with grip and avoid starting late in the day.
