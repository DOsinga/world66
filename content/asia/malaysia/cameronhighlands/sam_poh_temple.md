Sam Poh Temple is a large Buddhist temple above Brinchang and one of the easier cultural stops in the Cameron Highlands. Built in the 1970s, it has bright halls, devotional statues, and views over the surrounding highland townscape.

It is a good short visit between hikes, tea estates, and markets, especially if you are staying in or passing through Brinchang.
