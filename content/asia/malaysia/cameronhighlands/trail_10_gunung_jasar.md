Trail 10 climbs through mossy forest to Gunung Jasar, one of the more satisfying day hikes around Tanah Rata. The first half is fairly clear but steep in places, with roots doing the work of steps; beyond the summit the path narrows and can feel overgrown.

Take it seriously as a jungle walk, not a city park trail. Carry water, watch for markers at confusing forks, and allow time for the road walk back after the trail drops near the power station.
