title: Beach Bars
type: poi

Small bars attached to guesthouses or strung along the beach road serve cold Tiger or Carlsberg, basic cocktails, and not much else — which is all you need. Hammocks are strung between the palms. Some bars show movies in the evening on a screen propped against the wall. The pace is unhurried and the conversations tend to run late even when the bar officially closes early. This is where the social life of Cherating actually happens.
