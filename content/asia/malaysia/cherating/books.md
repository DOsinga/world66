**The History of Sumatra** by William Marsden (1783) — the definitive early account of the Malay world by a British scholar who spent years in Sumatra. Dense but authoritative context for understanding the culture, trade networks, and natural history of the region.

**Into the Heart of Borneo** by Redmond O'Hanlon (1984) — a comic and affectionate account of a jungle expedition into Borneo with the poet James Fenton. The best-written adventure travel book about Malaysia; entertaining even if your travels stay on the coast.

**The Great Railway Bazaar** by Paul Theroux (1975) — Theroux's train journey from London to Japan passes through Malaysia, with observations on the east coast and the overnight trains that still run today. The descriptions have aged better than most.

**Tropical Marine Life of the Indo-Pacific** — a field guide to the fish, corals, and invertebrates of the region; useful if you're snorkelling at Cherating or planning dives further up the coast at Pulau Tioman or Pulau Redang.
