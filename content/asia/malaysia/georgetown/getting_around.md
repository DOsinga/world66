---
title: Getting Around
type: section
---

The old town is best explored on foot — the historic centre is small enough to walk across in twenty minutes, and the network of narrow shophouse-lined streets only really works at walking pace. Most of what you came to see is within this compact area.

For longer trips, **Grab** (the Southeast Asian Uber equivalent) is cheap, reliable and the standard way visitors get around. A typical trip within the city costs RM 5-15. Download the app before you arrive and add a payment method.

The **free CAT bus** loops around the old town tourist area roughly every 30 minutes — useful for footsore moments. The wider **Rapid Penang bus** network covers the rest of the island, including the route to Kek Lok Si and Penang Hill (Bus 204 from Komtar). Buses are slow but cheap; pay cash on board.

**Trishaws** (cycle-rickshaws) operate around the old town. They are a tourist experience rather than transport; agree the price before you set off and treat the ride as a sightseeing tour at trishaw speed.

**Bicycles** can be rented at several places along Chulia Street and at Hin Bus Depot. The flat old-town streets are mostly fine for cycling, though traffic on the larger roads is intense and not bike-friendly.

To reach the mainland, drive across the bridges or take the **Penang ferry** from Pengkalan Weld.
