Jade Emperor's Pavilion is a Taoist temple near the foot of Penang Hill, dedicated to one of the central figures of the Taoist pantheon. The present building dates from the early 20th century, though worship on the site goes back further.

The temple is at its liveliest around the Jade Emperor's birthday, on the ninth day of Chinese New Year, when pilgrims arrive in large numbers. At quieter times it makes a worthwhile add-on to Air Itam, especially if you are already visiting Kek Lok Si or heading up Penang Hill.
