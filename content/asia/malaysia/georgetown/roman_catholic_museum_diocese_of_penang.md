The Roman Catholic Museum Diocese of Penang explains the history of Catholicism in Penang and the wider region through church records, artefacts, and religious memorabilia. The same building includes Fr. Joseph Reuten's gallery, with biblical and Catholic paintings interpreted through Asian themes and styles.
