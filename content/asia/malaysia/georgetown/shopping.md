---
title: Shopping
type: section
---

Georgetown's best shopping is in the old town's craft and antique shops rather than the malls. Look for batik (the wax-resist Indonesian/Malay textile), Peranakan beadwork, Chinese pottery and porcelain, and 19th-century colonial-era prints — all sold in restored shophouses along Lebuh Armenian, Lebuh Pantai and Lebuh Acheh.

**Little India** has the city's best selection of saris, Indian fabric, brass and copper kitchenware, jasmine garlands, and spice blends — particularly Penang's distinctive curry powders and chilli pastes. The shops cluster along Lebuh Pasar and Lebuh Pantai.

**Khoo Kongsi** and the **George Town World Heritage Office** both have small shops selling well-curated craft pieces from local makers. The **Hin Bus Depot** Sunday market is the place for contemporary design and independent makers.

For groceries and food souvenirs, the **Chowrasta Market** at the top of Penang Road is a covered indoor market with a famous upstairs section devoted to dried foods — Penang tau sar pneah biscuits, candied fruit, dried squid, herbal teas, traditional medicines. The **Komtar tower** and the **Gurney Plaza / Gurney Paragon** malls are the big modern shopping options if you want air-conditioned chain stores; both are short Grab rides from the centre.
