A four-sided Edwardian clock tower facing the Padang on the edge of Ipoh's Old Town, decorated on each face with a relief frieze of "the four virtues" — wisdom, justice, fortitude and patience — flanked by historical figures including Confucius, Solomon, Hammurabi and Muhammad. Each side is topped by a clock that no longer keeps reliable time.

A short walk from St Michael's Institution and the railway station, and easy to combine with the rest of the colonial Old Town in a one-hour walk. Free, always open.
