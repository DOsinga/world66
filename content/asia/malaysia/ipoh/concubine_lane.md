A narrow, atmospheric alley in the heart of the Old Town — properly called **Lorong Panglima** — running between Jalan Bandar Timah and Jalan Sultan Yussuf. The two-storey shophouses on either side have been cleaned up and turned into small stalls selling everything from local snacks (egg tarts, *pomelo*, *gula apong* ice cream, fresh soya milk) to Instagram-bait fashion and trinkets.

It can get jammed at weekends with day-trippers from KL and Penang, but is at its best on a quiet weekday morning. Cross the street to **Wife Lane** (Market Lane) and **Second Concubine Lane** (Lorong Hale) for a quieter wander.
