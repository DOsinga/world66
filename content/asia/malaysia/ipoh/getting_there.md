---
title: "Getting There"
type: section
---

Ipoh is halfway between [Kuala Lumpur](/asia/malaysia/kualalumpur) and [Penang](/asia/malaysia/georgetown), 200 km north of the former and 160 km south of the latter, on the country's main north–south transport corridor.

The fastest route in is the **ETS (Electric Train Service)** from KL Sentral or Butterworth (Penang) — about **two hours and ten minutes** from KL, very comfortable, several departures a day. The train arrives at the Mughal-Moorish **Ipoh Railway Station**, a five-minute walk from the Old Town.

**Express buses** are cheaper and only marginally slower: three hours from KL, a little more from Penang, half-hourly all day. They arrive at the Aman Jaya bus terminal 10 km north of the city.

**Sultan Azlan Shah Airport** has a few daily flights from KL with Firefly and a handful of seasonal connections — useful only for international travellers in a hurry. Most visitors fly to KL or Penang and take the train.
