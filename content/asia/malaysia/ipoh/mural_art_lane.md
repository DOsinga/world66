A narrow alley off Jalan Masjid in the Old Town, also called **Lorong Panglima Mural** or **Art Lane**, decorated end-to-end with large-format wall murals — paper-aeroplane children, hummingbirds, kopitiam scenes — by the Lithuanian artist **Ernest Zacharevic**, who also painted the famous murals in George Town, Penang. More murals by local artists have since been added on adjacent buildings.

A 15-minute wander does the lane and the surrounding murals. Combine with a coffee at Plan B or Burps & Giggles on the same block.
