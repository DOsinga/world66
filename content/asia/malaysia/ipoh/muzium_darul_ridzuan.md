Muzium Darul Ridzuan is a useful Ipoh history stop, especially if you want context for the tin wealth that shaped the city. The museum occupies a colonial-era house, which is part of the appeal: the building itself hints at the social world created by mining money and British administration.

The displays are modest, but they help connect Ipoh's old town architecture, railway-era growth, and Perak identity. It is best paired with a walk around the padang and nearby heritage streets rather than treated as a destination for a whole afternoon.
