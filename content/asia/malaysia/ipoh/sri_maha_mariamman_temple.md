Sri Maha Mariamman Temple is one of Ipoh's older Tamil Hindu temples. It remains an active place of worship, with community gatherings and Tamil religious instruction alongside its daily religious role.

Visit respectfully and briefly unless a ceremony is under way. The temple adds useful context to Ipoh's Indian heritage beyond the better-known colonial core.
