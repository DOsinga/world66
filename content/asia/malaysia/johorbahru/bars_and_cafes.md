---
title: "Bars and Cafes"
type: section
---

JB's bar scene is modest but lively. The Jalan Stulang Laut strip and the Zone hotel waterfront area are the main concentrations, where a mix of karaoke lounges, casual bars, and waterfront bistros cater to locals and visitors alike. Prices are noticeably lower than Singapore, which draws a steady weekend crowd across the Causeway.
