Johor Bahru's Chinatown is the historic Chinese community area around Tan Hiok Nee Street. The district mixes shophouses, restaurants, galleries, and heritage stops such as the Johor Bahru Chinese Heritage Museum and nearby old Chinese temple.

It is one of the easiest parts of central Johor Bahru to explore on foot, especially for travellers interested in the city's Chinese heritage and older streetscape.
