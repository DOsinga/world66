Johor Bahru Hokkien Association Museum is a small cultural museum focused on the city's Hokkien community. Exhibits cover Fujian heritage, local community history, and events such as Malaya during the Second World War.

It is a useful stop for visitors who want a more local view of Johor Bahru beyond malls, border traffic, and the main colonial-era landmarks.
