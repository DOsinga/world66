Johor Craft Complex showcases Malaysian handicrafts, textiles and small craft demonstrations in Johor Bahru. It is a useful stop if you want something more local than malls and border-city logistics.

The complex is best treated as a short cultural visit or shopping stop for handmade goods. Selection and activity levels can vary, so it works better as a flexible addition than the anchor of a day.
