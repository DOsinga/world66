The Johor Bahru Kwong Siew Heritage Gallery occupies two adjoining shophouses completed in 1907. They once served as the headquarters of the Kwong Siew Clan Association, founded by Cantonese and Hakka migrants who helped shape the Chinese community in Johor Bahru.

The collection is compact but useful: old photographs, documents, clan association material, and everyday objects that explain how the city's early immigrant networks worked. It makes a good companion stop to the Johor Bahru Chinese Heritage Museum and the Old Chinese Temple.
