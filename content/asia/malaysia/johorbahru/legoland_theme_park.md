Legoland Theme Park is the main family attraction in the Iskandar Puteri area west of Johor Bahru. The park is built around Lego-themed rides, shows, building zones, and a Miniland area, with gentler attractions aimed mostly at younger children.

The outdoor sections can be hot, so it is worth arriving early and saving the air-conditioned shows and building areas for the middle of the day.
