Legoland Water Park is the water-park side of the Legoland Malaysia resort in Iskandar Puteri. It has slides, splash areas, and Lego-themed water play, including build-a-raft features aimed at families.

It is most useful as a hot-weather break from the theme park, or as a full family day when paired with the main Legoland attractions.
