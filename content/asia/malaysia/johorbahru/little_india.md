Johor Bahru's Little India centres on Ungku Puan Street, where restaurants, textile shops, spice stores, and religious buildings mark the city's Indian community. It is a compact area rather than a formal attraction, best experienced on foot.

The neighbourhood sits close to several worthwhile stops, including Arulmigu Rajamariamman Devasthanam Temple, India Mosque, Gurdwara Sahib Johor Bahru, and the Arulmigu Sri Rajakaliamman Glass Temple.
