Skyscape is an observation attraction on level 34 of JLand Tower, giving a high view over Johor Bahru and toward the Singapore side of the strait. The glass-floor sections and city views make it a short, easy stop in the city center.

It is best treated as a viewpoint rather than a full afternoon activity, but it works well for families or anyone wanting a quick skyline view.
