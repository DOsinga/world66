Sultan Iskandar Mosque is a modern mosque in Johor Bahru that has been promoted as a visitor-friendly religious site. Built in 2013, it includes the An-Nur Gallery and is set up to receive non-Muslim visitors who want to learn more about Islamic worship and local mosque life.

Dress modestly and check visiting arrangements before going, especially outside regular public programs.
