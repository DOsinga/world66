---
title: Day Trips
type: section
---

**Gunung Lambak** is the closest nature destination — a 510-metre inselberg (an isolated hill rising from flat land) with well-maintained trails, wooden bridges, and picnic areas at the base. The summit takes about two hours at a moderate pace and rewards with views over the surrounding Johor landscape. Long-tailed macaques and dusky leaf monkeys are common. The trailhead is southwest of town.

**Gunung Belumut Recreational Forest** is a longer outing, about 40 km from Kluang. The forest has waterfall trails and is more demanding than Gunung Lambak. Bring food and water.

**Zenxin Organic Park** — a working organic farm with walking tours, a café, and produce for sale. Good for families and a change from the town's urban character.

**Johor Bahru** is about 80 km south on the North-South Expressway — a straightforward day trip to the state capital and its malls, waterfront, and connections to Singapore.
