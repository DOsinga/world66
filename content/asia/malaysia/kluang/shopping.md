---
title: Shopping
type: section
---

Kluang's best shopping is food. The most popular souvenir is Kluang coffee powder — the same strong, smooth blend served at the famous railway station canteen, available in vacuum-sealed packs from the canteen itself and from small shops throughout the town. Tong Huat cookies (pineapple tarts and Chinese New Year-style biscuits) are another local specialty.

**Little India** on Jalan Stesen has saris, spices, and Indian household goods.

**Mansor Toko Batik** — a traditional shop a few minutes' walk from the bus station selling handmade batik cloth, songkoks, and Malay fabric items.

For general shopping, **Kluang Mall** (Jalan Rambutan) is the town's main mall with a hypermarket and chain food outlets. The **Zenxin Organic Park** south of town sells organic produce and dragonfruit wine from the farm.
