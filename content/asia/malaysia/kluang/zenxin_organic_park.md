Zenxin Organic Park is a farm-based attraction outside Kluang, built around organic agriculture, gardens, produce, and family-friendly activities. It gives visitors a rural stop that connects with the area's agricultural landscape rather than only its town centre.

The park is best for families, food-minded travelers, or anyone with a car exploring around Kluang. It is not a major sight on its own, but it adds variety to an itinerary built around Gunung Lambak and local food.
