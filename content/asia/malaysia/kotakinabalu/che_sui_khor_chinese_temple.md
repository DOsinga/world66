Che Sui Khor Chinese Temple is a local religious landmark in Kota Kinabalu, tied to the city's Chinese community and especially Hakka associations. It is smaller and less famous than the city mosque, but it adds another layer to KK's cultural mix.

Visit quietly and respectfully, treating it as an active temple. It works well as a short stop if you are exploring beyond the waterfront and markets.
