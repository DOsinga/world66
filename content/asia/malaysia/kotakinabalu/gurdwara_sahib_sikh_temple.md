Gurdwara Sahib is Kota Kinabalu's Sikh temple, a modest but welcoming religious stop south of the city centre. Visitors should cover their heads before entering; headscarves are usually available at the temple.

It is a short visit, but it adds another layer to Kota Kinabalu's mix of mosques, churches, temples, and migrant communities.
