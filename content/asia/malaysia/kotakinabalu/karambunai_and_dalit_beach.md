Karambunai and Dalit Beach form a long, quieter stretch of coast north of Kota Kinabalu, backed by resorts, mangroves, and views toward the South China Sea. The beaches are not as central as Tanjung Aru, but they feel more spacious and removed from the city.

They are most useful for travellers staying nearby or looking for a calm sunset and beach walk outside the urban waterfront. Access can be shaped by resort properties, so plan the exact entry point before going.
