Monsopiad Cultural Village introduces Kadazan-Dusun traditions in Penampang, about a 30-minute drive from Kota Kinabalu. Its best-known exhibit is the Hall of Skulls, tied to the story of Monsopiad, a warrior said to have defended his village about 300 years ago.
