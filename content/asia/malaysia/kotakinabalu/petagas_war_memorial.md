Petagas War Memorial commemorates members of the local resistance killed during the Japanese occupation of North Borneo. The site is quiet and formal, with memorial stones and landscaped grounds marking one of Sabah’s most painful wartime episodes.

It is a worthwhile stop for travellers trying to understand Kota Kinabalu beyond the waterfront. The memorial is not large, but it gives weight to the city’s Second World War history and pairs naturally with other heritage stops south of the centre.
