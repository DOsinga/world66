Puh Toh Tze Chinese Temple is one of Kota Kinabalu's better-known Chinese Buddhist temples, marked by large seated and guardian figures around its entrance and courtyard. It reflects the city's Chinese community and gives a cultural stop beyond the waterfront markets.

The temple is usually a short visit rather than a half-day attraction. Go for the statues, incense, and colourful temple architecture, and combine it with other sights north of the centre if you have transport.
