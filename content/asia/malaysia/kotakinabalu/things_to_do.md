title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Kota Kinabalu punches well above its weight for outdoor activities. The five islands of [Tunku Abdul Rahman Marine Park](/asia/malaysia/kotakinabalu/tunku_abdul_rahman_park) are reachable by speedboat in 15 minutes for snorkelling and beach time, while [Lok Kawi Wildlife Park](/asia/malaysia/kotakinabalu/lok_kawi_wildlife_park) offers close encounters with pygmy elephants and proboscis monkeys. Serious hikers come from across Asia to climb [Mount Kinabalu](/asia/malaysia/kotakinabalu/mount_kinabalu), with most doing the two-day ascent from Kinabalu Park.

In the city itself, the [Atkinson Clock Tower](/asia/malaysia/kotakinabalu/atkinson_clock_tower) and [Signal Hill Observatory Platform](/asia/malaysia/kotakinabalu/signal_hill_observatory) give historical context and the best views. The [Kota Kinabalu City Mosque](/asia/malaysia/kotakinabalu/kota_kinabalu_city_mosque) and [Sabah State Mosque](/asia/malaysia/kotakinabalu/sabah_state_mosque) are both open to visitors outside prayer times. The [Sabah State Museum](/asia/malaysia/kotakinabalu/sabah_state_museum) and [Sabah Islamic Civilisation Museum](/asia/malaysia/kotakinabalu/sabah_islamic_civilisation_museum) cover the culture and history of the state. For something more theatrical, the [Mari Mari Cultural Village](/asia/malaysia/kotakinabalu/mari_mari_cultural_village) recreates the traditional longhouses and daily life of Sabah's five main indigenous groups.
