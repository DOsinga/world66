Chilling Waterfall is one of the most popular jungle destinations accessible from KKB, requiring an hour's drive followed by a 90-minute trek through primary rainforest that involves crossing the river six times. The waterfall drops into a large, clear pool suitable for swimming, surrounded by boulders and jungle canopy. On weekends it can get crowded; weekday visits are quieter.

The trek itself is half the pleasure — the trail winds through genuine primary forest with tall trees, the sound of cicadas, and occasional wildlife. Wear shoes that can get wet (the river crossings are unavoidable), bring insect repellent, and start early to avoid the afternoon crowds. The forest entrance is at Sungai Chilling, about an hour from KKB town by road through Fraser's Hill direction.
