The Islamic Heritage Park (Taman Tamadun Islam) occupies a large site on Wan Man Island alongside the Crystal Mosque. Its centrepiece is a collection of scale models — approximately one-fifth actual size — of famous mosques and Islamic monuments from around the world: the Dome of the Rock, the Alhambra, the Blue Mosque, Al-Azhar, and many others. The models are constructed with considerable care and are set in landscaped gardens with walkways and water features.

The park is aimed primarily at Malaysian school groups and families, but visitors with an interest in Islamic architecture will find it a useful visual survey of more than twenty significant buildings they are unlikely to see in person. The explanatory panels are multilingual. Entry to the park and the Crystal Mosque is combined, and the two together easily fill a half-day. The island setting, with views of the river and city from the gardens, adds to the appeal. A small boat service runs between the island and the main waterfront if the bridge approach seems too far.
