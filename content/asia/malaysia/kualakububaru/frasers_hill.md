Fraser's Hill (Bukit Fraser) sits at around 1,500 metres in the Titiwangsa range, about an hour's drive up winding mountain roads from Kuala Kubu Baru. The temperature drops noticeably on the plateau — it feels like a different country from the sweaty lowlands below — and the colonial-era bungalows, English cottage gardens, and mock-Tudor architecture give the place a distinctly eccentric, preserved feel.

The real draw for many visitors is birdwatching. Fraser's Hill is considered one of the finest birdwatching sites in Southeast Asia, with over 270 species recorded in the surrounding montane forest. The annual International Bird Race has been held here since the 1980s. Rhododendrons, tree ferns, and mossy oaks line the walking trails through the cloud forest. The gap in the mountains where the single-lane road squeezes through, known as The Gap, is itself a good birding spot at dusk.
