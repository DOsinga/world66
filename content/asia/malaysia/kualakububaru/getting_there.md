---
title: "Getting There"
type: section
---

The KTM Komuter train runs from Kuala Lumpur's Batu Caves station to Kuala Kubu Bharu (KA14) — the journey takes around 90 minutes and costs a few ringgit. This is the easiest option if you don't have a car. By road, KKB is about an hour north of KL via the LATAR highway or the older Route 1 through Rawang and Serendah. From the town centre, you'll need private transport or a hired vehicle to reach the Chiling Waterfall and rafting starting points, as there's no public transport to the forest edge.
