---
title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Kuala Kubu Baru's main draw is the rainforest and rivers immediately surrounding the town. The Chiling Waterfall trek is the standout attraction — a wet jungle walk that ends at a cool natural pool. The Selangor River runs white-water grade two and three rapids through a forested gorge. Fraser's Hill, an hour into the mountains above town, rewards birdwatchers and those seeking cool air away from the lowlands. The shophouse-lined main street and the padang at the town centre are pleasant for an evening stroll.
