The stretch of Jalan Tuanku Abdul Rahman between the old Odeon Cinema area and the Sogo end has one of Kuala Lumpur's better runs of Art Deco and colonial-era commercial buildings. It works best as a short architecture walk while exploring the Masjid India and Chow Kit side of the centre.
