Badan Warisan Malaysia is a heritage-conservation centre best known for Rumah Penghulu Abu Seman, a traditional Malay timber house moved here from Kedah. Guided tours explain the joinery, raised-house design, carved details, and the way a rural house has been reassembled in the middle of Kuala Lumpur.

The garden around the house includes native Malaysian plants, which makes the visit feel less like a static exhibit. It is a useful stop if you want architectural context before or after the city's grander colonial and modern landmarks.
