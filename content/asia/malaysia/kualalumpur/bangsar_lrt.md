Bangsar LRT station (Kelana Jaya line) sits at the bottom of the main Bangsar commercial district and provides a direct link to KL Sentral (4 minutes), KLCC (12 minutes), and the airport rail connection. The station has transformed the neighbourhood's accessibility since opening and made it one of the more practical areas for visitors staying in KL without a car.

Around the station entrance, a clutch of coffee shops, convenience stores, and food stalls has grown up to serve commuters. The walk up Jalan Telawi from here takes about ten minutes and passes through the full range of Bangsar's food and retail. From the station, the walk to KL Sentral via the highway bridge takes about 15 minutes.
