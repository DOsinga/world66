Bangsar Park is a well-maintained green space at the top of Bangsar Baru, popular with the neighbourhood's residents for morning walks, jogging, and picnics. The park has a small lake, benches, and enough shade to make an afternoon here bearable even in KL's heat. It's surrounded by colonial bungalows and quieter streets than the busy commercial strips of Telawi further downhill.

The park is at its best early morning, when residents do their exercise routines before the heat sets in. There are mamak stalls nearby for post-jog teh tarik. It's a short walk from Bangsar LRT station.
