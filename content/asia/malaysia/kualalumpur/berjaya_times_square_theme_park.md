Berjaya Times Square Theme Park is an indoor amusement park on the upper floors of the Berjaya Times Square mall in Bukit Bintang. Its signature feature is a large indoor roller coaster that winds through the building, with separate zones for younger children and bigger rides.

It is most useful as a rainy-day or family stop in central Kuala Lumpur, especially if you are already around Bukit Bintang.
