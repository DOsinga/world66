The classic Bukit Bintang evening walk covers three distinct atmospheres in under a kilometre: starting from Pavilion's luxury mall entrance, walking west along Jalan Bukit Bintang past the department stores and food stalls, then turning north along Jalan Alor (one block north) for the outdoor hawker stalls, and finishing on Changkat Bukit Bintang for the bar street. The whole circuit takes 30 minutes at a stroll or most of an evening if you stop to eat and drink.

Jalan Alor is the pivot: a car-free strip after dark where dozens of outdoor restaurants and hawker stalls cook wok dishes, grilled seafood, and Chinese specialities at pavement tables. It's touristy but good — the food quality holds up, and eating here in the open air while the city hums around you is one of KL's genuine pleasures.
