Bukit Kiara is one of Kuala Lumpur's better-known urban forest areas for hiking and mountain biking. The trails make it a useful escape from the dense city, with enough terrain to interest regular walkers and riders.

Development has reduced the forest over time, but it remains a popular outdoor stop on the Damansara and Mont Kiara side of the city.
