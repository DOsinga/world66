KL is a good base for cooler hill stations and historic colonial towns within a few hours' drive. **Fraser's Hill** and the **Cameron Highlands** are old British retreats high enough to need a sweater at night; **Melaka** (Malacca), the UNESCO-listed Portuguese-Dutch-British colonial port, is two hours south by bus. **Ipoh**, the cave-temple city of the Kinta Valley, is two hours north on the ETS train. **Putrajaya** — Malaysia's planned administrative capital — sits closer in, between KL and the airport, and is worth a half-day for its bridges and pink-domed mosque.
