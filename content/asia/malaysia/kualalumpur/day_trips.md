KL is a good base for cooler hill stations and historic colonial towns within a few hours' drive. [Fraser's Hill](/asia/malaysia/frasers_hill) and the [Cameron Highlands](/asia/malaysia/cameronhighlands) are old British retreats high enough to need a sweater at night. [Melaka](/asia/malaysia/melaka) (Malacca), the UNESCO-listed Portuguese-Dutch-British colonial port, is two hours south by bus, and [Ipoh](/asia/malaysia/ipoh), the cave-temple city of the Kinta Valley, is two hours north on the ETS train. Closer in, [Putrajaya](/asia/malaysia/putrajaya) — Malaysia's planned administrative capital — sits between KL and the airport, worth a half-day for its bridges and pink-domed mosque. For a shorter outing, the [Batu Caves](/asia/malaysia/kualalumpur/batucaves) Hindu shrine is only 13 km north of the centre.
