Galeria Sri Perdana occupies the former official residence of Tun Dr. Mahathir Mohamad, Malaysia's fourth prime minister. The museum displays personal items, gifts, and objects connected with his time in office, making it a focused stop for visitors interested in modern Malaysian political history.
