Guan Di Temple is a 19th-century Chinese temple in Kuala Lumpur's Chinatown, across from Sri Mahamariamman Temple. Built in 1888, it is dedicated to Guan Yu and makes an easy pairing with Petaling Street, Central Market, and the nearby clan houses.
