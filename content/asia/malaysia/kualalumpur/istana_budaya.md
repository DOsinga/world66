Istana Budaya is Malaysia's national theatre and one of Kuala Lumpur's most distinctive modern buildings. Its folded blue roof is inspired by traditional Malay forms, and the venue hosts large-scale theatre, dance, opera, and touring productions.

Even if you are not attending a performance, the building is worth a look for its architecture and its role in the city's performing arts scene. Check the programme before going, as the best reason to visit is still a show.
