Kampung Baru LRT station (Ampang line) is the most convenient public transport access point for the neighbourhood. The station exit opens directly onto the northern edge of Kampung Baru, with food stalls and mamak restaurants immediately visible. From here, Jalan Raja Muda Musa — the main food and market street — is a five-minute walk south into the kampung interior.

The station is also useful as a reference point for the Chow Kit area: the large wet market and the morning food stalls of Chow Kit are a ten-minute walk northwest. The KLCC area is two stops south on the LRT, making Kampung Baru an easy add-on to a KLCC visit.
