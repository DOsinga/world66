A walking tour of Kampung Baru is one of the most interesting things you can do in KL — entirely free, requiring no guide, and giving a completely different perspective on the city than the KLCC-Bukit Bintang tourist circuit. The walk takes about an hour at a gentle pace. Starting from the Saloma Link footbridge, head into the kampung along Jalan Raja Muda Musa, turn into the residential lanes, and spend time in the interior streets where wooden stilt houses, small mosques, and kitchen gardens survive completely intact.

The contrast at every turn is the experience: look right and you see a 1940s house with chickens in the yard; look left and a 50-storey tower fills the sky. On Saturday evenings, the walk combines naturally with the night market along Jalan Raja Muda Musa. Kampung Baru LRT station is the most convenient starting point.
