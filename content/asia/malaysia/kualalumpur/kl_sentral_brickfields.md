KL Sentral is Malaysia's largest transit hub, a purpose-built station complex on the northern edge of Brickfields opened in 2001. From here you can board the KLIA Ekspres or KLIA Transit to the airport (28 minutes), the KTM Komuter for intercity and suburban rail, the LRT (Kelana Jaya line), the MRT (Putrajaya line), the Monorail, and the KL-Singapore ETS express trains. The station is also surrounded by hotels and connected to Nu Sentral mall, making it a self-contained transit and hotel district.

For most visitors, KL Sentral is a functional arrival and departure point rather than a destination. The Express Rail Link to KLIA runs every 15-30 minutes and takes 28 minutes to the main terminal — far faster and more reliable than a taxi. The Brickfields neighbourhood is a five-minute walk south.
