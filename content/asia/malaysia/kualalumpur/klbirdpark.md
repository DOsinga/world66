Set within the Lake Gardens, the KL Bird Park is one of the world's largest free-flight walk-in aviaries, with around 3,000 birds of more than 200 species living in a 8.5-hectare netted enclosure. Hornbills, peacocks, flamingos, and parrots wander past your feet as you walk under the canopy; smaller cages contain owls, raptors, and breeding pairs.

The park is divided into four zones, with feeding shows and bird-of-prey demonstrations through the day — check the schedule at the entrance. Allow two hours. It is hot and humid even by KL standards, so come early. Combined tickets are available with the neighbouring **Butterfly Park** and **Orchid Garden** within the Lake Gardens. Open 9 am to 6 pm daily.
