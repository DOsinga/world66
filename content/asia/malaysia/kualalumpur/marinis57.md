A rooftop bar and Italian restaurant on the 57th floor of Petronas Tower 3 — close enough to the Twin Towers that the photograph of them from the terrace has become an Instagram cliché in its own right. Cocktails run around RM 50-70, the wine list is long, and the small-plates menu (cicchetti) is good if priced for the view rather than the food.

The smart-casual dress code is enforced (no shorts or flip-flops). Reservations strongly recommended for sunset, when the floor-to-ceiling windows fill with the lit-up shafts of the Petronas Towers. Open from late afternoon till late.
