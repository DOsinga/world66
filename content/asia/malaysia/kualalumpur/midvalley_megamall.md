Mid Valley Megamall is one of the largest shopping complexes in Malaysia, a sprawling two-level development on the southern edge of Bangsar anchoring the Mid Valley City mixed-use development. The mall has around 430 shops across a full range — from international brands (Uniqlo, H&M, Marks & Spencer) to local chains, a large food court, a cinema (Golden Screen), and a branch of Metrojaya department store. The adjacent Gardens Mall next door is the upscale sister property, with higher-end brands.

Mid Valley is most useful for practical shopping — pharmacies, electronics, groceries (Cold Storage), and clothing. It's reliably air-conditioned and tends to be less frantic than KLCC or Pavilion. Served by the Mid Valley KTM Komuter station immediately outside the mall's northern entrance.
