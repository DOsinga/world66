The bar scene around Publika and the surrounding streets of Mont Kiara is KL's most consistently international — focused on craft beer and cocktails rather than local Chinese-style kopitiam drinking culture. Several well-regarded craft beer bars operate around Publika Shopping Gallery, including branches of Taps Beer Bar (one of KL's best craft beer selections) and various cocktail bars catering to the neighbourhood's Japanese and Korean expat residents.

The atmosphere on weekend evenings around Publika's main atrium is festive without being loud — more neighbourhood social gathering than full nightclub. Closing times are 1–2 am, late by KL standards. The crowd tends to be older and more international than in Changkat Bukit Bintang. Grab is the only practical transport option at night.
