Muzium Orang Asli introduces the history, material culture, and daily life of Peninsular Malaysia's Indigenous peoples. Its collection of artifacts makes it a useful cultural stop for visitors heading beyond central Kuala Lumpur, especially when combined with nearby Batu Caves.
