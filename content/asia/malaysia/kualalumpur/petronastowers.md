The 452-metre Petronas Twin Towers were the tallest buildings in the world from 1998 until 2003 and remain the tallest twin towers ever built. Designed by Argentine-American architect César Pelli, their stepped silhouette is based on an eight-pointed Islamic star, the cladding is mirror-finished stainless steel, and the joining **Skybridge** on the 41st and 42nd floors is the highest two-storey bridge in the world.

You can book timed tickets — online and well in advance, or in person from a quota released each morning — to visit the Skybridge and the 86th-floor observation deck. The full tour takes about 45 minutes. The towers are spectacular at any time, but the best free view is from the lawn of **KLCC Park** at the base, especially at dusk when the lights come on. The **Suria KLCC** shopping centre fills the six-storey podium underneath.
