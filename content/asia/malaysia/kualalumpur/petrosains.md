Petrosains is a hands-on science museum inside Suria KLCC, focused on energy, technology, and the petroleum industry. It is aimed mainly at families, with interactive exhibits, science shows, and simulation experiences.

It pairs naturally with a visit to the Petronas Twin Towers and KLCC Park, especially on a hot or rainy day when an indoor activity is useful.
