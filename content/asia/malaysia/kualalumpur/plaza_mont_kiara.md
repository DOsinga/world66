Plaza Mont Kiara is a mid-sized lifestyle mall at the base of Mont Kiara's main residential cluster, serving as the neighbourhood's everyday shopping destination. The tenant mix runs to international casual dining (several Asian restaurant chains, a burger joint), a Village Grocer supermarket (KL's best for imported and organic produce), pharmacies, and various wellness businesses. It's a functional neighbourhood mall rather than a destination shopping experience.

Village Grocer is the key draw for visitors: the imported cheese, wine, European staples, and fresh produce selection is among the best in KL, reflecting the neighbourhood's international customer base. If you're self-catering anywhere in the Mont Kiara area, this is the supermarket to use.
