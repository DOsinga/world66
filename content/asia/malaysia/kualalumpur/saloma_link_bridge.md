Saloma Link Bridge is a pedestrian bridge between Kampung Baru and the KLCC side of central Kuala Lumpur. Its steel ribs form a bright, angular canopy that feels functional by day and much more dramatic after dark, when the bridge is lit in changing colours.

Use it as part of a walk between Kampung Baru's food streets and the Petronas Twin Towers. The bridge itself is quick to cross, but the skyline views make it worth slowing down for a few minutes, especially in the evening.
