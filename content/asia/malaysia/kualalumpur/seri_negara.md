Seri Negara is a restored colonial mansion near Kuala Lumpur's Lake Gardens. The estate is tied to Malaysia's independence-era history and has been reopened with galleries and guided visits focused on the country's path to nationhood.

Visits usually require advance reservation for a guided tour. It fits naturally with the nearby museums, gardens, and heritage buildings around the western side of central Kuala Lumpur.
