title: "Shopping"
type: section
---

Shopping in KL splits cleanly between malls and markets. The malls cluster along the **Golden Triangle** of Jalan Sultan Ismail and Jalan Bukit Bintang: **Pavilion KL** and **Suria KLCC** are the upscale anchors, **Lot 10** and **Sungei Wang Plaza** the mid-market workhorses, **Plaza Low Yat** the consumer electronics specialist. On the western edge of town the **Mid Valley Megamall** is bigger still.

For markets and handicrafts, **Petaling Street** in Chinatown runs every evening with stalls of fake watches, fabrics, dried goods, and street food. The restored **Central Market** is a pastel-blue art deco hall full of batik, pewter, and shadow puppets — touristy but pleasantly so. **Kasturi Walk** behind it is a covered pedestrian lane with outdoor stalls. The **Royal Selangor Visitor Centre** in Setapak is the place for pewter, with workshop tours and the chance to hammer your own bowl.

Pasar malams — night markets — rotate through different neighbourhoods on different evenings (Taman Connaught on Wednesdays, Bangsar on Sundays). They are where ordinary KL residents actually shop, and where the food is best.
