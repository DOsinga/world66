Thean Hou Temple is one of the largest and most elaborately decorated Chinese temples in Southeast Asia — a six-storey pagoda-style building on a hilltop in Seputeh (on the southern edge of Bangsar), dedicated to Tian Hou, the Goddess of the Sea. Built by the Hainanese community and opened in 1989, the temple complex includes three main shrines, a hall for community functions, a wedding registry, and panoramic views across southern KL from the upper levels.

The architecture is deliberately spectacular: red lacquer columns, ornate carved panels, colourful roof dragons, and lanterns hung in their thousands. The temple is especially beautiful during Chinese New Year and Mid-Autumn Festival, when additional decorations are added. The view across the city from the terrace is one of the best in KL. A short Grab ride from Bangsar LRT or a 20-minute walk uphill.
