---
title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

KL is best taken in layers. The colonial core at **Merdeka Square** with the Moorish-Victorian Sultan Abdul Samad Building, the National Mosque, and the old Railway Station — all within ten minutes' walk of each other. Then the temples and street food of **Chinatown** around Petaling Street. Then the modern KL of the **Petronas Towers**, the KLCC park and aquarium, the Skybridge views from up high, and the rival viewpoint at the **KL Tower** on Bukit Nanas.

The big half-day excursion is **Batu Caves**, the limestone Hindu shrine 13 km north of the centre. Closer in, the **Lake Gardens** are 92 hectares of parkland with a bird park, butterfly park, orchid garden, and Islamic Arts Museum on its edge. For something quirkier, the rainbow-painted Sky Mirror at Sasaran or the back-alley murals of Bukit Bintang and Kwai Chai Hong reward an hour's wandering.

Wear light clothes and bring water — the equatorial sun and humidity hit hard, and most days end with a short afternoon thunderstorm.
