The Tun Abdul Razak Memorial occupies the former residence of Malaysia's second prime minister, who led the country from 1970 to 1976. The displays cover his personal life and his political career, giving useful context for modern Malaysia beyond the big national museums nearby.

It sits close to the Lake Gardens cluster, so it is easy to combine with the Islamic Arts Museum, the bird park, or a walk through the gardens.
