---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

Kuala Lumpur sits just three degrees north of the equator and the weather varies very little from month to month: daytime highs around 32°C, nights around 24°C, humidity always high, rain possible any day of the year. There is no real dry season but **February to April** are the driest and clearest months, and **November to January** see the heaviest rains, with afternoon thunderstorms most days.

Avoid Chinese New Year (late January or early February) if you want to do business or eat at Chinese restaurants — many shut for a week. **Thaipusam** at Batu Caves (January or February) is a remarkable sight, with hundreds of thousands of devotees climbing the steps with kavadi piercings. **Ramadan** brings excellent food bazaars (pasar Ramadan) every evening; **Hari Raya Aidilfitri** at the end of Ramadan empties the city as Malays return to their kampungs. Late June is the **Malaysia Mega Sale Carnival**, which fills the malls.

The KL haze, drifting in from peat fires in Sumatra, can be bad in August and September; check air quality before booking.
