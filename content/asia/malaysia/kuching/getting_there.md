---
title: "Getting There"
type: section
---

Kuching International Airport is about 10 km south of the city centre. Malaysia Airlines and AirAsia both operate direct flights from Kuala Lumpur; flying time is about 1 hour 40 minutes. Direct connections also serve Singapore and several Indonesian cities including Pontianak. There are no road or sea connections from Peninsular Malaysia — Sarawak is only accessible by air or by sea from Indonesian Kalimantan. The airport to city centre journey takes about 20 minutes by taxi or Grab.
