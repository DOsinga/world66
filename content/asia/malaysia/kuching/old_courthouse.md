The memorial is one of the few statues of a private individual functioning as a hereditary ruler of a foreign territory in Southeast Asia.

The Kuching Old Courthouse, built in 1874 and completed in stages through 1883, is one of the finest examples of colonial architecture in Sarawak. The building now houses a tourism complex, restaurant, and some commercial tenants; the historical structure — the colonnaded façade, the clock tower, the covered walkway — has been carefully maintained.

The Brooke Memorial in front commemorates Charles Brooke, the second White Rajah, and provides a useful orientation point on the waterfront. The courthouse complex sits between the waterfront esplanade and the Sarawak Museum, making it a natural stop on any walk through the historic centre.
