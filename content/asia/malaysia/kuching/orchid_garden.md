Kuching's Orchid Garden is a calm landscaped stop across the Sarawak River from the city center, close to the Astana. It showcases orchids associated with Sarawak, including the Normah Orchid, and gives visitors a softer counterpoint to museums, markets, and riverfront walks.

The garden is compact and best combined with a river crossing or north-bank stroll. It is most rewarding for plant lovers, photographers, or anyone looking for an easy quiet break within the city.
