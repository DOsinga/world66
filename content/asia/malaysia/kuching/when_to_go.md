---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

Kuching is equatorial and has no conventional dry season — rain can fall any month. That said, the period from May to September is generally drier and better for national park visits and outdoor activities. The Rainforest World Music Festival, held at the Sarawak Cultural Village in July or August, is one of the best music events in Southeast Asia and draws international artists and large crowds. The Chinese New Year period (January or February) brings the city's Chinese community onto the streets in force. Bako and Semenggoh Wildlife Centre are rewarding year-round, though animal sightings at Semenggoh are best when forest fruit is scarce (roughly June–September and November–January).
