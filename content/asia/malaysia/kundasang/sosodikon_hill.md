Sosodikon Hill is a short viewpoint walk near the road to Desa Cattle Dairy Farm. The hill is not a serious hike, but it gives a wide look across Kundasang Valley and, when the weather cooperates, a clean view of Mount Kinabalu.

Come early. By late morning the mountain often disappears behind cloud, and the view becomes pleasant rather than memorable.
