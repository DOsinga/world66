Labuan Bird Park sits opposite the Chimney Museum and is made up of three large connected domes. The collection is modest, but it gives families an easy wildlife stop in the same northern Labuan heritage area as the old coal-mining remains.

It works best as a combined visit with the Chimney Museum rather than as a stand-alone destination. The covered aviaries also make it a useful option when the weather is too hot or wet for a longer outdoor walk.
