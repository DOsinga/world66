---
title: "Beaches"
type: section
---

The best beaches run down the west and north coasts. **Pantai Cenang** is the liveliest — a two-kilometre strip of white sand backed by restaurants and bars, good for swimming at low tide. Just north of it, **Pantai Tengah** is quieter and more resort-oriented. Further north, **Tanjung Rhu** on the north-east tip is one of the most beautiful beaches on the island: shallow, clear water, dramatic limestone outcrops, and far fewer people.

The beaches on the east coast (near Kuah) are shallow and less attractive; most visitors don't bother.
