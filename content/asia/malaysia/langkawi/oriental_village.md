Oriental Village is the landscaped complex at the base of Langkawi's SkyCab, mixing souvenir shops, casual food, small attractions, and the boarding point for the cable car up toward the Sky Bridge. It is commercial, but it is also the practical gateway to one of the island's main sights.

Do not come expecting a traditional village. Come because you are riding the cable car, want an easy meal before or after, or need a simple rainy-day wander on the island's western side.
