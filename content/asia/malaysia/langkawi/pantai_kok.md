Pantai Kok is a quieter beach on western Langkawi, north of Pantai Cenang and close to Telaga Harbour, the Langkawi cable car, and the Telaga Tujuh waterfalls. It is a good choice when you want a more relaxed stretch of coast with easy access to the island's inland sights.
