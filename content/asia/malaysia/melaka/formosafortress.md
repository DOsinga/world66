The solitary surviving gateway of the Portuguese fortress built in 1511 after Alfonso de Albuquerque's conquest of Melaka. At its peak, A'Famosa enclosed much of the hilltop and was among the strongest fortifications in Asia. Only this single gate, the Porta de Santiago, survived the systematic British demolition ordered in 1795.

The Dutch added the VOC logo — the East India Company crest — to the gate in 1670 during their occupation. You can still see it carved above the arch today. The site sits at the foot of St. Paul's Hill; the Malacca Sultanate Palace Museum and the Declaration of Independence Memorial are nearby. Despite being just a gate, the scale of what once stood here is worth a moment's reflection.
