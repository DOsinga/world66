The Malay and Islamic World Museum sits close to Melaka's main historic sights and covers Malay history, Islamic culture, and links with the wider region. Displays are spread over several floors, with temporary exhibitions on the ground floor and historical exhibits above.

It is a compact add-on to the Dutch Square and St Paul's Hill area, best for visitors who want more context after seeing the colonial landmarks.
