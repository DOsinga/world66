Melaka Crocodile Farm is a family attraction in Ayer Keroh, north of the historic centre. Crocodiles are the main draw, with feeding sessions and shows on weekends and public holidays, but the site also has reptiles, birds, small mammals, a petting zoo, and a water park. It works best as a half-day break if you are travelling with children.
