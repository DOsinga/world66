Melaka Islamic Museum gives a general introduction to Islam and the wider Islamic world, with particular attention to Malaysia and local religious scholars. It is a modest museum, but it adds useful context in a city where Malay, Chinese, Indian, European, and Islamic histories all overlap.

It works best as a short stop while exploring the historic centre, rather than as a destination on its own.
