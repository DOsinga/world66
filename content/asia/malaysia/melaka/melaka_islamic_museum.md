Melaka Islamic Museum introduces Islam and the wider Islamic world, with sections on Islam in Malaysia and local scholars. Its location near A'Famosa and St. Paul's Hill makes it easy to add to a walk through the historic centre.

The museum is best for travellers who want more context on Melaka's Malay and Muslim history alongside the city's better-known Portuguese, Dutch, and Peranakan sights.
