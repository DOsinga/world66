The Melaka UMNO Museum covers the history of the United Malays National Organisation, the political party that dominated Malaysian government for decades after independence. It is a small, specialised museum rather than one of Melaka's essential stops.

Its value is context. If you are interested in modern Malaysian politics, it adds a useful counterpoint to the colonial buildings and trading history around the old town.
