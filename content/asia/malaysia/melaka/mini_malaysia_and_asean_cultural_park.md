Mini Malaysia and ASEAN Cultural Park is an open-air collection of full-size traditional houses from Malaysia's states and neighbouring ASEAN countries. It is most useful as a quick primer on regional building styles, with cultural performances adding life to what could otherwise feel like a static display.
