Mini Malaysia and ASEAN Cultural Park is an open-air cultural park in Ayer Keroh, on the road between the toll plaza and Melaka town. Its main draw is a set of full-size reconstructions of traditional houses from Malaysia's states and from ASEAN countries.

The park is most worthwhile if you are already visiting the Ayer Keroh attractions or travelling with children. Daily cultural shows add life to the site, but the houses themselves are the reason to stop.
