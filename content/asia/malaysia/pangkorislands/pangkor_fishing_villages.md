Pangkor's east coast is lined with working fishing villages, including Sungai Pinang Kecil, Sungai Pinang Besar, and Kampong Teluk Kecil. They make a low-key but worthwhile stop for a look at the island's everyday maritime life, with jetties, boats, and seafood activity facing the mainland.
