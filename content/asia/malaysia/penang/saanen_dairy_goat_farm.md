Saanen Dairy Goat Farm is a small, family-friendly stop near Balik Pulau where visitors can feed and pet goats in a shaded enclosure. Goat milk, yoghurt drinks and puddings are usually sold on site, and the farm is most useful as a short rural break while exploring western Penang.
