Teluk Bahang Forest Eco Park is a large recreational forest on the northwestern side of Penang, with marked trails leading toward the island's hills, waterfalls, and neighbouring coastal areas. Come for a hot, green break from the beaches, and bring water if you plan to walk beyond the easy paths near the entrance.
