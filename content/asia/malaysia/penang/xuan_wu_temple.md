Xuan Wu Temple is a small Chinese temple in the centre of Balik Pulau, dedicated to the Taoist deity Xuan Wu. Its most distinctive feature is a large bell originally cast in 1895, making it a modest but worthwhile stop while exploring the quieter western side of Penang Island.
