---
title: "Eating Out"
type: section
---

Perak's food is dominated by the **Ipoh kitchen** — Hainanese-style bean sprout chicken, wonton noodles, white coffee, satay celup, and a long list of *kopitiam* breakfasts that draw food pilgrims from across Malaysia. See the [Ipoh page](/asia/malaysia/ipoh) for the city's restaurants. Beyond Ipoh, the state's best eating is rural: **Kuala Kangsar** is famous for its *cendol* and *laksa* stalls; **Taiping** has a strong noodle culture and a popular night market; the small towns of the **Larut plain** are known for grilled river fish and the spicy fermented-rice dish *tempoyak*. On the coast, the fishing village of **Kuala Sepetang** is celebrated for its mangrove-smoked seafood.
