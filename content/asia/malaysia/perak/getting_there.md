---
title: "Getting There"
type: section
---

**By air.** **Sultan Azlan Shah Airport** (IPH) at Ipoh handles a small number of domestic flights (mostly from Kuala Lumpur) and some flights to Singapore. The state's busier air gateways are **Penang International Airport** (PEN, two hours' drive from Ipoh) and **KLIA / KLIA2** (about three hours' drive south).

**By train.** The electrified **ETS** train from Kuala Lumpur reaches Ipoh in about two and a half hours, with continuing services to Butterworth (for Penang) and the Thai border. The line is one of the most pleasant in Southeast Asia — modern, fast, with good views of limestone karst. Kuala Kangsar and Taiping are intermediate stops.

**By road.** The **North–South Expressway** (E1) runs the length of the state, putting Ipoh two hours from KL and Taiping a further hour north. The **East–West Highway** (Route 4) is the only road across to Kelantan and Terengganu, with the spectacular crossing through the Royal Belum and Temengor forests.
