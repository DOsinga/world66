The village of **Sayong**, across the Perak River from Kuala Kangsar, is the country's traditional home of the **labu sayong** — the gourd-shaped earthenware water vessel that is one of Malaysia's most recognisable folk objects. The jars are still made by hand in the village, using river clay turned on a slow wheel, smoothed with a wooden paddle, and fired in low earth kilns that give the surface its characteristic dark sheen.

Several workshops accept casual visitors, and the **Sayong Pottery Demonstration Centre** runs short demonstrations for tour groups. A small **labu sayong** is the easiest and most distinctively Perak souvenir, and a working potter's wheel is one of the few traditional crafts still performed at scale anywhere in the country.
