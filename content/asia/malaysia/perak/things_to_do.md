title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Perak's sights split into three clusters: the **royal town** of Kuala Kangsar, with its golden-domed Ubudiah Mosque and the Sultan's palace; the **Cameron Highlands** in the eastern mountains, with tea plantations and cool walking; and the **Royal Belum rainforest** in the north, one of the oldest forests on earth. The state capital, [Ipoh](/asia/malaysia/ipoh), is covered as its own city; the island of [Pangkor](/asia/malaysia/pangkorislands) and the highland resort of [Cameron Highlands](/asia/malaysia/cameronhighlands) sit nearby in the Malaysia hierarchy.
