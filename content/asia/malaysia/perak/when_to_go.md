---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

Perak has a classic equatorial climate — hot, humid and prone to afternoon thunderstorms year-round. There is no strict dry season but **June–August** and **December–February** are typically the driest months and the best for hiking and outdoor sights. The hill stations (Cameron Highlands, Bukit Larut) are comfortable any time of year, with day-time temperatures around 18–22°C.

The most distinctive seasonal event is the **Royal Belum Rafflesia bloom**, which can happen at any point in the year but is more reliable in **March–May**; trips into the park typically combine the flower with hornbill and elephant viewing. The annual **Sultan of Perak's Birthday** falls on the first Saturday of November and is a public holiday in the state, often marked with cultural events in Kuala Kangsar.
