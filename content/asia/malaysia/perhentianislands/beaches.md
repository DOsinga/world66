---
title: Beaches
type: section
---

The Perhentians have some of the finest beaches in Malaysia — white sand, calm sea, clear water — without the overdevelopment that has transformed much of Langkawi. On Kecil, Long Beach is the busiest but also the most social; Coral Bay is quieter and faces west for sunsets. Besar has several coves and bays accessible only by boat or on foot through the jungle, including the excellent, usually uncrowded stretches of beach on the island's south and northwest sides.

The water colour in the Perhentians is among the most striking in Malaysia: turquoise in the shallows, deepening to blue-green offshore. The sand is pale and fine. The fringing reef starts at the rocky headlands on either end of most bays, which means you can snorkel without going far from the beach towel. Swimming is safe inside the bays; open-water swims between the islands are not recommended due to boat traffic.
