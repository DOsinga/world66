Coral Bay — also called Pasir Panjang — is the calmer alternative to Long Beach on Kecil, facing west and catching the sunset. It's smaller and quieter, with a gentler atmosphere that suits those who want the island without the noise. The bay's name is arguably misleading — most of the reef that gave it the name has been damaged over the years — but the water is still clear and snorkelling around the rocky fringes turns up decent marine life.

Several of the better guesthouses on Kecil are here, and the restaurants along Coral Bay's beachfront have a better reputation for food than most places on Long Beach. It's a five-minute walk across the narrow neck of the island to Long Beach, so you can have the quiet accommodation and walk to the social scene in the evening.
