---
title: Getting There
type: section
---

Ferries to the Perhentians depart from Kuala Besut, a small fishing town on the Terengganu coast. The crossing takes 45 minutes to an hour depending on the boat and sea conditions. Ferries run regularly during the season (roughly 9am–5pm) and cost around RM35–45 each way. Boats drop passengers at the beaches directly — Long Beach and Coral Bay on Kecil, and the main resort areas on Besar.

Kuala Besut is reachable from Kota Bharu airport (45 minutes by taxi or Grab) or from Kuala Lumpur by overnight bus (roughly nine hours to Kuala Besut or the nearby town of Pasir Puteh). There are also buses from Kuala Terengganu. If coming from Thailand, the nearest crossing is at Rantau Panjang or Pengkalan Kubor, both accessible from Kota Bharu.
