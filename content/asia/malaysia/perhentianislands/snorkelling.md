Snorkelling is arguably a better draw than diving at the Perhentians for most visitors — the water is shallow enough over the fringing reefs that a mask and fins get you to the best marine life without a boat ride or a certification. Parrotfish, angelfish, and butterflyfish are everywhere around the rocky headlands; the seagrass beds between the islands attract dugong occasionally as well as turtles more predictably.

The standard half-day boat snorkelling trip leaves from both islands in the morning and calls at four or five sites including Shark Point, Turtle Beach, and a couple of coral gardens. These trips cost around RM60–80 per person and are arranged through any resort or stand-alone dive shop on the beach. If you have your own snorkel gear, free snorkelling off Long Beach and Coral Bay beaches turns up reef fish in the shallows from the first morning.
