title: Things to Do
type: section
---

The Perhentians divide neatly between [Besar](/asia/malaysia/perhentianislands/perhentian_besar) (quieter, more comfortable) and [Kecil](/asia/malaysia/perhentianislands/perhentian_kecil) (cheaper, more social). On Kecil, [Long Beach](/asia/malaysia/perhentianislands/long_beach) is the main strip and [Coral Bay](/asia/malaysia/perhentianislands/coral_bay) offers a calmer sunset-facing alternative. [Scuba diving](/asia/malaysia/perhentianislands/scuba_diving) and [snorkelling](/asia/malaysia/perhentianislands/snorkelling) are the primary activities — the water is clear and the marine life abundant. [Shark Point](/asia/malaysia/perhentianislands/shark_point) reliably delivers blacktip reef sharks; [Tiga Ruang](/asia/malaysia/perhentianislands/tiga_ruang) takes divers through underwater caverns. [Turtle Beach](/asia/malaysia/perhentianislands/turtle_beach) on Besar has resident green turtles both on the beach and in the water offshore.
