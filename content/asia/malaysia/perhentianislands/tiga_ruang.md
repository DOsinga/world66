Tiga Ruang — "Three Rooms" in Malay — is one of the Perhentians' signature dive sites, a series of three connected underwater caverns on the northwestern side of Besar. The caverns are large enough to swim through without equipment difficulties but narrow enough to create an atmospheric sense of enclosure. Light filtering from openings above plays across the rock walls, and the cavern interiors harbour lobster, moray eels, and resting nurse sharks.

The site is suitable for intermediate divers and above — Open Water certification is sufficient but experience with buoyancy control is helpful in the confined sections. Depth ranges from 5 to 20 metres depending on which sections you explore. Most dive operators on both islands offer trips to Tiga Ruang on their standard schedule.
