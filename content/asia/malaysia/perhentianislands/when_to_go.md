---
title: When to Go
type: section
---

The Perhentians are closed during the northeast monsoon from November through February — the sea is too rough for boats and most resorts shut entirely. The season runs from March to October, with March and April being the quietest months as resorts reopen and visitor numbers build gradually.

The best diving and snorkelling conditions fall between April and August, when visibility is at its highest — sometimes exceeding 30 metres — and the sea is calm. July and August are the busiest months, particularly on school holiday weekends when Malaysians and Singaporeans fill Kecil's budget places. Accommodation can be booked out on peak weekends; weekday visits are noticeably quieter.

September and October are pleasant — fewer visitors, reasonable visibility, and the tail-end of turtle nesting season. The sea starts getting choppy again by late October as the monsoon approaches.
