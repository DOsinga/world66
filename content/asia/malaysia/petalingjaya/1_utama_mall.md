---

The biggest mall in PJ and one of the biggest in Malaysia, with over 700 stores spread across two linked buildings. The Old Wing has the more interesting independent shops; the New Wing has the international brands and the food court.

For travellers, the Rainforest atrium is the only piece of architecture worth a detour. For everyone else, this is where the city does its weekend shopping. Connected by free shuttle to The Curve and IKEA five minutes north.
