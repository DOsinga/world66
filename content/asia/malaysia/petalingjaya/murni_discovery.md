The PJ branch of the legendary Murni mamak chain, open until the small hours and packed at every hour with a mix of office workers, students, and post-club eaters. The menu runs to several hundred items — go for the maggi goreng, the roti tisu, and one of the elaborate lasi-glasi mocktails that the kitchen is famous for.

Open-air seating along Jalan SS21/56b. No reservations; turnover is fast.
