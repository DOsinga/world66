Sepilok is not really a town in the travel sense. It is a compact wildlife area west of Sandakan, where three strong conservation stops sit close enough together to make an easy and worthwhile day: orangutans, sun bears, and lowland rainforest.

The [Sepilok Orangutan Rehabilitation Centre](/asia/malaysia/sabah/sepilok/sepilok_orangutan_rehabilitation_centre) is the headline, especially around feeding times, but it should not be treated as a simple animal show. The better visit pairs it with the [Bornean Sun Bear Conservation Centre](/asia/malaysia/sabah/sepilok/bornean_sun_bear_conservation_centre) next door and the [Rainforest Discovery Centre](/asia/malaysia/sabah/sepilok/rainforest_discovery_centre), whose canopy walkway gives you a different view of the forest.

Sepilok is easiest from Sandakan. It fits naturally into a Sabah wildlife route that continues to the Kinabatangan River, but it also stands on its own if you have only one spare day in the area.

## Destinations in Sepilok

- [Sepilok Orangutan Rehabilitation Centre](/asia/malaysia/sabah/sepilok/sepilok_orangutan_rehabilitation_centre) - semi-wild orangutan rehabilitation centre and nursery
- [Bornean Sun Bear Conservation Centre](/asia/malaysia/sabah/sepilok/bornean_sun_bear_conservation_centre) - rescue centre for the world's smallest bear
- [Rainforest Discovery Centre](/asia/malaysia/sabah/sepilok/rainforest_discovery_centre) - canopy walkway, trails, and birdwatching in Kabili-Sepilok forest
