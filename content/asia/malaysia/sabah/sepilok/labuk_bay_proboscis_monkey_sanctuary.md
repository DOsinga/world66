Labuk Bay Proboscis Monkey Sanctuary is a popular add-on to Sepilok's orangutan and sun bear sights. The draw is the chance to see proboscis monkeys at close range from feeding platforms, with mangrove and coastal forest around the site.

Distances inside the sanctuary and the wait for return transport make planning useful. It is easiest with your own car or a driver who will wait.
