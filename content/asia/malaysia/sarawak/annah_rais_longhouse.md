A Bidayuh longhouse community in the hills 90 minutes southeast of Kuching, still inhabited by around 500 people in a single complex of bamboo-walled, longhouse-style structures running along a ridge above the Sungai Saoh. Annah Rais has been open to visitors for around twenty years and has worked out a thoughtful balance — the residents charge a small entry fee, run homestays, and offer guided walks, but the village is still very much a working community.

Half-day visits include a walk through the longhouses, a cup of tuak (rice wine), and an explanation of village governance and the Bidayuh agricultural calendar. Overnight homestays add an evening meal, a music-and-dance demonstration, and the inevitable late-night tuak sessions. Book through any Kuching tour operator.
