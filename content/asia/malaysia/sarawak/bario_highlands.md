A plateau at 1,000 metres in the extreme northeast of Sarawak, near the border with Indonesian Kalimantan, inhabited by the Kelabit — one of the smallest indigenous groups in Borneo, numbering around 6,000 worldwide. The main settlement of Bario is reached by a 50-minute Twin Otter flight from Miri (the only practical access; the overland trek takes a week through trackless jungle), and the highlands' altitude means cool nights, terraced rice paddies, and an absence of mosquitoes.

The plateau is the starting point for jungle treks ranging from a single afternoon to multi-week expeditions across the watershed into Kalimantan. Bario rice — a small, fragrant pearl grain — is grown nowhere else and sells in Kuching's markets for several times the price of lowland rice. Pa Berang and Pa Umor longhouses outside Bario are open for homestays. Book flights well ahead; the planes are 18-seaters and routinely full.
