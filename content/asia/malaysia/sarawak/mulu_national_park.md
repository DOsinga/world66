A UNESCO World Heritage Site in the upper Tutoh River basin in northern Sarawak, accessible only by air or by a multi-day boat-and-trek journey from the coast. Mulu protects one of the most extensive and best-studied cave systems on earth — Deer Cave, the Sarawak Chamber (the largest cave chamber by floor area anywhere), Clearwater Cave (over 100 km of mapped passages), and Lang's Cave with its glittering helictite forest.

Above ground, the park has the Pinnacles of Gunung Api — a forest of 45-metre-tall razor-edged limestone spires reached by a hard three-day trek — and the rainforest canopy walk near park HQ. Most visitors fly in to Mulu Airport (MZV) from Miri or Kota Kinabalu. Stay at the park lodge or the adjacent Mulu Marriott; book caves and treks weeks ahead in high season.
