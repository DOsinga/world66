A rainforest reserve about 20 km south of Kuching, run as an orangutan rehabilitation centre since 1975. Around 25 semi-wild orangutans live in the surrounding forest, and twice a day (around 9 am and 3 pm) the rangers put out a feeding platform of bananas and coconuts to which any orangutans choosing to come down for a free meal will gather.

There is no guarantee of sightings — when fruit is abundant in the forest, the orangutans skip the feedings entirely — but visits during the lean months (typically April–August) routinely produce sightings of half a dozen or more, including mothers with infants. The 90-minute window opens just before feeding and closes shortly after. Small entrance fee.
