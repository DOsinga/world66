Setia EcoHill Park is a tidy neighbourhood park rather than a destination sight, but it is a useful soft landing in Semenyih. The paths, lawns and exercise areas work well for an evening walk if you are staying nearby or travelling with children who are not up for Broga Hill.

Do not cross Kuala Lumpur just for this park. Use it as a low-key stop between meals, or as a backup when rain or heat makes the hill trails unappealing.
