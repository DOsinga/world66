---
title: Getting Around
type: section
---

The central sights are walkable, especially around the waterfront, market, and temple district. For the airport, Bukit Aup, Bukit Lima, or scattered food stops, use taxis or ride-hailing apps.

River boats are transport as much as sightseeing here. If you are heading upriver, check departures early in the day; schedules can shift with demand and river conditions.
