---
title: Getting There
type: section
---

Sibu Airport has domestic flights to Kuala Lumpur, Kuching, Miri, and other Malaysian cities. It sits southeast of town, and taxis or ride-hailing cars are the simplest way in.

The more memorable route is by river. Express boats connect Sibu with Kapit and the upper Rajang settlements, while long-distance buses link the town with Kuching, Bintulu, and Miri along the Pan-Borneo Highway.
