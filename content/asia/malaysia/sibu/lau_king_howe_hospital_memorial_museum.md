The Lau King Howe Hospital Memorial Museum preserves the story of a hospital founded through local philanthropy and long central to Sibu's public life. Medical museums can be dry, but this one is small, specific, and oddly revealing: old equipment, photographs, and ward displays show what modern healthcare meant in a river town.

It is worth pairing with the Heritage Centre if you like local history. You will come away with a better sense of how Sibu grew from trading settlement into regional service town.
