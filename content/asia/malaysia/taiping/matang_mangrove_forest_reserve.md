Matang Mangrove Forest Reserve protects a quiet stretch of coastal forest west of Taiping, where boardwalks and waterways lead through thick mangroves. It is a good change of pace from Taiping's hill and lake sights, especially if you want to see the muddy, tidal side of Perak.

Come for birds, crabs, old charcoal kilns, and the odd glimpse of village life around the reserve edge. The place rewards slow looking more than a rushed stop, so bring mosquito repellent and give yourself time to walk rather than just tick it off.
