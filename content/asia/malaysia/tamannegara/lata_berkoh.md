Lata Berkoh is a series of rapids on the Tahan River, reached by boat from Kuala Tahan in about 45 minutes. The rapids cascade over granite boulders in a stretch of river surrounded by dense forest, forming natural pools for swimming. The water is clear, cool, and refreshing — a relief after the heat and humidity of the trail.

The journey upriver is half the experience: the boat navigates shallows and threads between rocks, sometimes requiring passengers to wade for short stretches. Guides can combine a Lata Berkoh trip with a visit to an Orang Asli village nearby, where you can see traditional blowpipe demonstrations and learn about the Batek people's forest knowledge.
