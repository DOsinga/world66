Juara Turtle Project is a sea turtle conservation project on Tioman, focused on habitat protection, education, and injured turtle care. Short visits introduce the work and explain the threats facing local turtle nesting sites.

What you see depends on the season and current conservation needs: there may be eggs, hatchery work, or recovering turtles. It is best for visitors who want a low-key wildlife stop with a conservation angle.
