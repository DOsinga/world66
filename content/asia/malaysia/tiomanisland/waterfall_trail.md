The Waterfall Trail is a short marked walk from Juara's south beach toward the island's waterfalls. The path starts near the turtle breeding farm and is marked by painted can bottoms nailed to trees.

Allow about 30 minutes each way and wear shoes that can handle mud or wet rock after rain.
