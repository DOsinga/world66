---
title: "Getting There"
type: section
---

Rasdhoo is reached from **Malé**, 56 kilometres east. The most common options:

- **Public ferry**: cheapest, runs Sunday, Tuesday, and Thursday from Malé's Villingili ferry terminal. Three to four hours, around 100 rufiyaa.
- **Speedboat**: 90 minutes, around US$50, with several daily companies. The reliable everyday option.
- **Seaplane**: most resort transfers in Ari Atoll go via seaplane and a couple of operators sell empty seats — fast but expensive.

Rasdhoo has no airport and no resort transfers of its own; check ferry schedules carefully before arrival.
