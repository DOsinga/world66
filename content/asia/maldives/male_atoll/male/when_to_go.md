---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

Malé sits one degree north of the equator and has only two seasons: the dry northeast monsoon from December to April and the wet southwest monsoon from May to November. Temperatures barely move from year-round 30°C daytime / 25°C night; humidity is high throughout.

The dry season is the standard tourist window — calmer seas, more sunshine, less rain — and prices in town rise to match. The wet season brings heavier showers (usually short and intense rather than all-day rain), occasional storms and a quieter, cheaper city. For independent travellers, the shoulder months of November and April hit the best balance: largely settled weather, lower rates, and the spring–neap tides that bring the best surf to the breaks just outside the harbour.
