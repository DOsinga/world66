South Malé Atoll (Kaafu South) sits directly south of [Malé](/asia/maldives/male_atoll) and the main airport atoll, separated by the navigable Vaadhoo Kandu channel. It is quieter than its northern neighbour, with around 30 resorts spread across smaller islands and a string of inhabited local islands at its eastern edge, but it stays within easy speedboat distance of the airport — most transfers run 45 minutes to an hour.

The atoll is best known for its dive sites — wall dives, channels, and underwater pinnacles ("thila") where pelagics gather on the incoming tide. **Kandooma Thila** and the **Embudhu Express** are two of the most-visited drift dives in the Maldives. Sharks, eagle rays, and turtles are commonplace; manta rays come through seasonally.

Snorkelling off the resort house reefs is consistently good. For travellers on a smaller budget, the local island of **Maafushi** at the atoll's eastern edge has the longest-established guesthouse scene in the country and is a useful base for day trips to dive sites, sandbanks, and uninhabited islands.
