---
title: Things to Do
type: section
---

Bolifushi is about the lagoon rather than sightseeing. Snorkel the house reef, book dives in the South Malé channels, take a sunset cruise, or do very little beside water that looks almost unreal when the light is right.

For cultural wandering, markets, or local restaurants, this is the wrong island. Treat Bolifushi as a private-island beach stop and build any wider Maldivian exploring around Malé, Maafushi, or other inhabited islands.
