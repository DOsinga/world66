The drift dive along the eastern edge of Embudhu Kandu, on the channel between Embudhu and Vaadhoo. "Express" is the right word — the incoming current can run at several knots, fast enough that you sometimes have nothing to do but float and watch.

What you see is the standard South Malé spectacle done well: white-tip and grey reef sharks cruising the wall, schools of jacks and barracuda, eagle rays and occasionally mantas in season. The shallow finish over the reef top is a calmer counterpoint, useful for working off the air still in the tank.
