The quietest of the three inhabited islands on the eastern edge of South Malé Atoll, with around 1,000 residents and a small handful of guesthouses. It is the right choice if Maafushi feels too busy and Guraidhoo too remote — a tidy village, a long shallow lagoon, and most days more locals than tourists in the lanes.

The bikini beach is short but the snorkelling directly off it is excellent: turtles, reef sharks, and several patches of healthy coral within a 50-metre swim of shore. The local ferry from Malé runs three times a week.
