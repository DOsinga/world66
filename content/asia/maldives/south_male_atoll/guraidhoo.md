A smaller, quieter local island a short ferry hop south of Maafushi, near the southern tip of the atoll. Around 1,800 people live here and there are a dozen or so guesthouses, mostly run by Maldivian families. The pace is slower than Maafushi — a single lane through the village, a single short bikini beach, a handful of unsigned restaurants.

The dive sites off Guraidhoo are some of the best in the southern atoll. **Guraidhoo Kandu** — the channel between Guraidhoo and the neighbouring island of Kandooma — is the local equivalent of a wildlife corridor, with reef sharks, eagle rays, and barracuda almost guaranteed. Stronger currents than most South Malé sites; experienced divers only.
