A submerged pinnacle in the channel between Kandooma resort island and Guraidhoo. Top of the thila is around 12 metres below the surface; it drops to over 30 metres on the outside. The dive is a drift along the western wall, then up over the top of the pinnacle to finish.

Big fish action is the draw: schooling barracuda, eagle rays in formation, regular white-tips and grey reef sharks, occasional thresher and hammerhead in the deeper water. Strong currents, advanced certification required.
