Maafushi is the busiest of the Maldives' local islands, a 1.3-kilometre strip of coral sand half an hour by speedboat from the capital. The eastern end is conservative Muslim village; the western end is a designated "bikini beach" with bars, dive shops, and around 80 small guesthouses lining the back lanes. The line between the two is taken seriously — modest dress off the bikini beach is the rule.

Most visitors come for the diving and snorkelling. A dozen operators run day trips to nearby sandbanks, manta points (seasonal), and wreck sites, with prices a third of what the resorts charge. Sunset dolphin cruises are the standard evening activity. The food, mostly curry and grilled fish in unfussy beachside restaurants, is good and inexpensive by Maldivian standards.
