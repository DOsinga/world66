  Maafushi prison, which occupies the island along with the residential community, once held high-profile political prisoners during the Maldives' turbulent democratic transition of the 2000s. The island is now one of the most developed local island destinations in the country — it went from almost no tourist infrastructure to dozens of guesthouses in a few years after regulations changed in 2010.
---

Maafushi is the most developed local island in South Malé Atoll — a genuine town where Maldivians live alongside a booming guesthouse industry. Staying here rather than at a resort lets you eat local food, interact with Maldivian families, and pay a fraction of the resort price while still being within reach of excellent diving and snorkelling.

The island has a public beach (bikini beach) designated for tourist use at the southern end, while the rest of the shoreline is for local use — modest dress is expected in the town. Water sports operators line the beach road and offer snorkelling trips, surf excursions (breaks are accessible nearby), and diving. The 45-minute speedboat from the airport is a straightforward transfer that you can book with your guesthouse.
