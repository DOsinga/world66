Snorkelling in South Malé Atoll requires almost no effort — drop off any beach or sandbank and you're on a coral reef. The better snorkelling is in the channels between islands, where currents carry nutrients and the fish life is correspondingly richer. Most guesthouses in Maafushi and Guraidhoo run morning snorkel trips by boat to the best nearby spots.

Biyadhoo reef, around the resort island of the same name, is one of the most accessible good snorkel sites — the shallow reef table is suitable for beginners. For more experienced snorkellers, the channel at Guraidhoo is worth the extra boat ride for the turtle encounters. Water temperature is around 28–30°C year-round; a rash guard is all you need.
