Tsetserleg is the capital of Arkhangai, but it feels more like a mountain town than a provincial centre. The name means "garden", and the town earns it by sitting in a green bowl below Bulgan Mountain, with whitewashed monastery buildings above the market streets and open steppe starting almost as soon as the houses end.

The best reason to stop is [Zayain Khuree](/asia/mongolia/arkhangai/tsetserleg/zayain_khuree), the old monastery complex that now holds the Arkhangai museum. A second active monastery, [Buyandelgeruulekh](/asia/mongolia/arkhangai/tsetserleg/buyandelgeruulekh_monastery), keeps the town's Buddhist life visible rather than purely historical. Above them, [Bulgan Mountain](/asia/mongolia/arkhangai/tsetserleg/bulgan_mountain) gives a short climb and a wide look over the valley.

Tsetserleg also works as a base for the central Mongolian road trip: hot springs, nomad camps, volcanic country, and the curious standing boulder of [Taikhar Rock](/asia/mongolia/arkhangai/tsetserleg/taikhar_rock) are all reachable from town. It is not a polished stop, but after long distances across Mongolia it has the rare combination of a real town, good scenery, and enough culture to justify a full day.
