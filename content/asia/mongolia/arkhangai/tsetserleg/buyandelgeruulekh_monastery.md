Buyandelgeruulekh Monastery is smaller and quieter than Zayain Khuree, but it matters because it is not just a museum piece. Monks still use the complex, and the buildings sit nicely against the hillside above the town.

Come respectfully and do not expect a grand tourist circuit. The reward is the everyday rhythm of a Mongolian monastery in a place most travellers reach only after several long road days.
