The Erdenesiin Khuree Mongolian Calligraphy and Art Center in Kharkhorin is a small but carefully tended gallery dedicated to traditional Mongolian calligraphy and visual art. The collection showcases works in the classical vertical Mongolian script alongside thangka-style paintings and contemporary pieces that draw on the nomadic visual tradition.

For most visitors, the calligraphy alone makes the stop worthwhile. Traditional script was largely replaced by Cyrillic under Soviet influence and remains unfamiliar even to many Mongolians today, which gives the gallery an unexpected political dimension — preservation of a script as an act of cultural continuity. If you are already at Erdene Zuu, the center is only a short walk away and adds useful context to the cultural landscape you have just been walking through.
