Khan Park is the central green space in Ondorkhaan, running from the Museum of Khentii toward the sports complex. It is scattered with Soviet-era monuments, tanks, a Chinggis Khan statue, and a row of Mongolian wrestler figures, which makes it a useful first walk if you want to get a feel for the town.
