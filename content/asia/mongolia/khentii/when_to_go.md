---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

The only practical window for visiting Khentii is June through September. Summer temperatures are warm and pleasant on the steppe and in the river valleys, though the forest areas can be surprisingly cool at night. July and August bring the most daylight and the most stable conditions for rough-road travel, but also the most mosquitoes — bring serious insect repellent for any time near wetlands or rivers.

Spring (April–May) sees the rivers running high with snowmelt, which can make the rough tracks impassable and the river crossings genuinely dangerous. Autumn (September–early October) can be excellent for fishing and birdwatching, with good visibility and cooling temperatures. Winter (November–March) is severe — temperatures drop below −30°C, roads are covered in snow and ice, and most ger camps close.
