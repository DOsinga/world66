---
title: 'When to Go'
type: section
---

July and August are the only really practical months for fieldwork in the Altai — warm days, occasional thunderstorms, snow-free passes. June and September are colder but less crowded. The Golden Eagle Festival in Ölgii in early October draws a small wave of foreign tourists; flights and guides should be booked months ahead. From late October to early May the city is locked in deep cold — January lows of -40 °C are common — and the surrounding country becomes essentially impassable.
