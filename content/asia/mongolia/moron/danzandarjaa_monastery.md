A small, working Buddhist monastery on the western edge of Mörön, rebuilt in 1990 to replace the much larger Möröngiin Khüree complex destroyed in the 1937 purges. A handful of monks live and study here; the main hall holds painted thangkas, a gilded Buddha, and the usual rows of butter lamps. Quiet, weathered, and dignified — the kind of place you sit in for ten minutes and then leave without speaking.

Best visited mid-morning when chanting is going on.
