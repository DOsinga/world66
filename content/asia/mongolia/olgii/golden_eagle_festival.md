The Golden Eagle Festival is the event that puts Ölgii on many travel plans. Kazakh hunters arrive with trained golden eagles, heavy fur clothing, decorated horses, and the quiet confidence of a tradition that still belongs to working families, not only to tourism.

The main festival is usually in early October, when the weather is already sharp. Book early if you are coming then: rooms, vehicles, and good guides disappear quickly.
