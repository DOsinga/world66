The Danzanravjaa Museum introduces the figure behind Sainshand's pilgrimage landscape. Danzanravjaa was a 19th-century lama, writer, composer and artist from the Gobi, and the museum displays religious objects, costumes, paintings and items connected with his work.

It is small, but worth visiting before going to Hamriin Hiid. The context helps turn the monastery trip from a desert excursion into something tied to Mongolian Buddhism and Gobi culture.
