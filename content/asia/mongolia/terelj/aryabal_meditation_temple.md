Aryabal Meditation Temple sits on a forested hillside in Terelj, reached by a stepped path and boardwalk from the valley below. The climb passes Buddhist teaching signs and small ritual details before opening onto wide views over the national park.

The current temple was completed in 2004, but it has become one of Terelj's main stops because it combines an easy hike, a religious site, and a good lookout. It pairs naturally with Turtle Rock on a day trip from Ulaanbaatar.
