The Marshall Zhukov House Museum is a niche but interesting stop for visitors curious about Mongolia's 20th-century history and Soviet connections. It focuses on Georgy Zhukov, whose role in the 1939 battles at Khalkhin Gol made him an important figure in Mongolian memory.

The museum is modest and specialized, so it will not suit every traveller. For those interested in military history or socialist-era Ulaanbaatar, it adds a useful layer beyond the national museums and central monuments.
