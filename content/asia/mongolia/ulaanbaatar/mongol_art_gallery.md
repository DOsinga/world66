Mongol Art Gallery gives Ulaanbaatar visitors a focused look at Mongolian visual art beyond museum history displays. Exhibitions can include painting, sculpture, and contemporary work, making it a useful complement to the Fine Arts Zanabazar Museum.

The gallery is centrally located and manageable, so it fits well into a Sükhbaatar Square or museum day. Check current exhibitions before going if you have a specific interest, since the experience depends heavily on what is on view.
