The Gawdawpalin is the second-largest temple in Bagan, a massive two-storey structure built by King Narapatisithu in the late 12th century and expanded by his successors. The name means "platform for paying royal homage" — the kings of Bagan would hold ceremonies here to receive tribute from vassal states.

The temple was heavily damaged by the 1975 earthquake and subsequently rebuilt, which gives it a newer appearance than many of its contemporaries. Despite this, the interior is worth exploring: the shrines contain large Buddha images, and the upper corridors offer some of the better interior views of any temple in Old Bagan.
