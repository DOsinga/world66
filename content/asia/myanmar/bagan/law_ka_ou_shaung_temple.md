Law Ka Ou Shaung Temple is one of Bagan's smaller temple stops, valued mainly for its views over the plain. It has been used as a sunrise spot because it is quieter than the most famous viewpoints, though access rules in Bagan can change and climbing may be restricted.

Treat it as a short stop while moving between the larger temples nearby, especially around early morning or late afternoon when the brick monuments and tree line catch the low light.
