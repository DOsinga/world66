The Mingalazedi ("Pagoda of Blessing") was completed in 1277, just before Kublai Khan's Mongol forces ended the Bagan empire. It is the last and in many ways the finest of Bagan's great stupas, and its completion was said by astrologers to foretell the city's fall.

The pagoda has a broad base of terraces decorated with glazed terracotta tiles showing Jataka tales — stories of the Buddha's previous lives. Many of the tiles survive in excellent condition, making the Mingalazedi one of the best places in Bagan to study its decorative ceramic work. The stupa stands in the southern part of the plain, away from the main tourist circuit, and is usually quiet.
