Myauk Guni Temple is a smaller Bagan temple north of Taung Guni. It sees fewer visitors than the famous temples nearby, partly because the approach road is rough.

The reward is a quieter terrace with peaceful views across the archaeological plain. It works best as a secondary stop while exploring the central temple zone.
