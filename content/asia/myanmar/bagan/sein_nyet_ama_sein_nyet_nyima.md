Sein Nyet Ama and Sein Nyet Nyima are a paired temple and stupa in the southern part of the Bagan plain. Sein Nyet Ama is a 12th-century square temple with entrances on all four sides, while Sein Nyet Nyima is a nearby stupa guarded by lion figures at its corners.
