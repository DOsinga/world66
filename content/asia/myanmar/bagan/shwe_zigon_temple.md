Shwe Zigon Temple is a major golden pagoda at Nyaung-U, completed in the early 12th century and said to contain relics of the Buddha. Its form influenced later Myanmar pagodas, and the complex is rich in carved and painted details, though the approach is lined with persistent souvenir stalls.
