Upali Thein is a small ordination hall in Bagan, valued above all for its interior mural paintings. Compared with the great temple silhouettes on the plain, it is restrained from the outside, but the surviving painted surfaces make it a rewarding stop for visitors who slow down and look closely.

Because Bagan can become a blur of stupas, Upali Thein is useful for changing scale. It invites attention to monastic function, painted narrative, and the conservation challenges of fragile interiors rather than just sunset views and temple profiles.
