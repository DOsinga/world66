Hinthagon Pagoda stands east of Shwemawdaw Pagoda and is one of Bago's worthwhile temple stops. It is smaller and quieter than the city's headline sights, but its hilltop setting and local worshippers make it a useful addition to a circuit of Bago's pagodas and monasteries.
