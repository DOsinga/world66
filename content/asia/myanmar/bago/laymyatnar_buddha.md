Laymyatnar Buddha is a four-sided Buddha complex, with standing figures facing the four cardinal directions. It is a secondary stop rather than one of Bago's headline monuments, but it is close enough to Shwethalyaung Buddha and Mahazedi Pagoda to include if you are already visiting that part of town.

The appeal is the symmetry of the arrangement and the chance to round out Bago's Buddhist monument circuit without a long detour.
