Sein Thar Lyaung Pagoda is known for its large open-air reclining Buddha image. It is one of Bago's three major reclining Buddha sites, along with the better-known Shwethalyaung Buddha and Mya Thar Lyaung Buddha.

Include it on a circuit of Bago's pagodas if you have time beyond the headline monuments.
