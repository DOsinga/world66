Hpa-Pu Village and Hill offers one of the easier rural viewpoints around Hpa-An. After crossing the river, the walk passes through a small village landscape before climbing to a lookout over fields, water, limestone ridges, and the low city beyond.

It is worthwhile because the journey is part of the attraction. Go early for cooler air, carry water, and allow time for a slow return rather than treating it as a quick photo stop; the appeal is the shift from Hpa-An's streets to the open countryside around them.
