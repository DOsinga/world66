Kaw Ka Taung Cave is a small Buddhist cave south of Hpa-An, lined with Buddha images and tiled passages. The wider stop is part cave shrine and part countryside outing: nearby spring-fed pools, fish, rice fields, and simple restaurants make it a pleasant half-day detour from town.
