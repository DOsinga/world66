The Kayin State Cultural Museum in Hpa-an documents the traditions, textiles, musical instruments, and ceremonial objects of the Karen people and related ethnic groups in Kayin State. The collection includes traditional clothing of S'gaw Karen, Pwo Karen, and Pa-O ethnic groups, alongside agricultural tools, lacquerware, religious objects, and historical photographs.

The museum is small by Myanmar city standards but is well-maintained and provides useful context for understanding Karen culture before or after exploring the surrounding landscape. Kayin State was closed to foreign visitors for many years due to conflict, and the museum helps explain what was happening here during that time. Entry fees are modest and the staff are welcoming to visitors who take time to ask questions.
