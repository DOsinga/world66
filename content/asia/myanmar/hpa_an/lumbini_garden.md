Lumbini Garden sits at the foot of Mount Zwegabin, filled with rows of seated Buddha images that give the place its striking rhythm. The mountain backdrop makes it one of Hpa-An's more memorable religious landscapes, especially if you are heading toward Zwegabin itself.
