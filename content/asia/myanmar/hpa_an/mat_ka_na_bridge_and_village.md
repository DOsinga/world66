Mat Ka Na Bridge, sometimes written Lat Ka Na, is a rural bridge and village stop outside Hpa-an. The bridge is known more for its setting than for the structure itself, with karst hills and fields around the village.

It works best as part of a scooter or cycling loop through the countryside rather than as a stand-alone trip. Go slowly through the village and keep the visit low-key.
