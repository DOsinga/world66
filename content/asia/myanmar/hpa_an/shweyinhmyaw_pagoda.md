Shweyinhmyaw Pagoda is built on a platform over the Thanlwin River at the edge of Hpa-an, its white towers rising from the water like a mirage. It is the most visually distinctive pagoda in the immediate vicinity of town — the floating-on-water effect makes it a favourite subject for photography, especially at sunrise and sunset when the colours are good.

The pagoda is a working place of worship, busy with local people making offerings and taking the air by the river. The riverside location provides good views up and down the Thanlwin. The walkway to the pagoda passes over the water, which adds to the sense of arrival. It is a short walk from Hpa-an's main street and can be combined with a stroll along the riverfront.
