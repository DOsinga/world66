Aung Puppet Show is a small evening performance in Nyaungshwe, the main traveller base for Inle Lake. Burmese marionette theatre is a delicate, highly skilled form, and seeing it in an intimate room makes the strings, gestures, and comic timing easier to appreciate than on a large stage.

The show is short, simple, and best approached as a cultural add-on after a day on the lake. It is not a glossy tourist spectacle; the appeal is the craft, the closeness, and the chance to support a tradition that has fewer regular stages than it deserves.
