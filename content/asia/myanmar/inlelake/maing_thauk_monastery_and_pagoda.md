Maing Thauk Monastery and Pagoda sits above the stilted village of Maing Thauk, reached by a short but warm climb from the lakeside. The reward is not a single famous object, but a quiet religious compound and a wider view over the canals, gardens, and water traffic of Inle Lake.

It is a good low-key stop for travelers already exploring the eastern side of the lake by bicycle or boat. Go early or late in the day, carry water, and keep the visit respectful: this is an active local religious site, not a stage set for photographs.
