The hills around Kengtung are home to a remarkable concentration of ethnic minority groups — Akha, Lahu, Ann, Wa, and several smaller communities — living in villages that have changed little over generations. Day treks to nearby villages and multi-day routes through more remote terrain are the main reason many travellers come to Kengtung at all.

A licensed local guide is legally required and is genuinely necessary for navigation, translation, and managing community relations. Ask at your guesthouse for recommendations — experienced guides know which villages welcome visitors and how to approach them respectfully. The best season for trekking is November to February, when the air is cooler and the mountain paths are not turned to mud by monsoon rains. Bring small, practical gifts such as school notebooks or pencils rather than sweets, and follow your guide's instructions on photography and interaction.
