The central market in Kengtung is one of the most colourful in the Shan State, drawing traders and farmers from the hill tribes of the surrounding mountains. Market days bring the greatest variety of vendors and visitors; it is worth asking locally which days of the week attract the largest gatherings, as these vary by town.

The covered market hall sells fresh produce, dried goods, meat, and household items. Around the periphery, informal stalls set up under parasols and awnings where hill tribe women sell forest products, traditional textiles, and handmade silver jewellery. Prices are local and bargaining is normal. Arrive early — by 9am things are at their most active, and many vendors have packed up by midday.
