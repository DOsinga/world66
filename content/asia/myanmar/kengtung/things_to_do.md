---
title: Things to Do
type: section
---

Kengtung's highlights cluster around the old town centre, with its layered Shan, Tai Khun, and Burmese Buddhist heritage. The Maha Myat Muni Temple is the spiritual heart of the city; the ruined Kengtung Palace tells a darker chapter of its history. Beyond the town, the hills hold hill tribe villages reached on foot with a licensed guide.
