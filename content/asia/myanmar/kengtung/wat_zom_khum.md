Wat Zom Khum is a 15th-century Buddhist temple in the heart of Kengtung's old quarter, and one of the best examples of traditional Tai Khun temple architecture in the Shan State. The main prayer hall features a steeply pitched wooden roof with elaborate carved eaves, and the interior walls retain fading murals depicting Jataka tales — stories from the Buddha's former lives.

The temple is an active monastery and home to a community of resident monks. Visitors are welcome outside of prayer times; look for the head monk if you want to learn more about the monastery's history. The surrounding lanes have some of the finest old wooden shophouses in Kengtung — worth wandering even if you linger only briefly at the temple itself.
