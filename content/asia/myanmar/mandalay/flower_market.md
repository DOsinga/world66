Mandalay's Flower Market is a small but colourful cluster of vendors selling cut flowers, greenery, and garlands. It works best as a quick morning stop, when the stalls are active and the larger wholesale market nearby is also moving fruit, vegetables, fish, and meat through the city.

It is not as essential as the Jade Market, but it gives a lively glimpse of everyday trade away from Mandalay's big monuments.
