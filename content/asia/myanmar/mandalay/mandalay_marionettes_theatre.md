Mandalay Marionettes Theatre keeps one of Myanmar's most distinctive performance traditions visible for travellers: small, precisely controlled puppets accompanied by music and narration. The show is intimate, which makes it easier to notice the skill behind the movements.

It is a good evening choice after a day of monasteries, markets, and palace walls. Confirm performance times locally, and arrive with the expectation of a cultural demonstration rather than a large theatrical production.
