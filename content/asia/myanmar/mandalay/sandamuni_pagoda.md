Sandamuni Pagoda is one of Mandalay's most graceful religious complexes, a field of white stupas arranged around a central golden zedi. It is known for hundreds of inscribed marble slabs, each protected in its own small shrine, giving the place a rhythm of repetition and quiet detail.

Because it sits near Mandalay Hill and Kuthodaw, it fits naturally into a half-day temple circuit. Take your time here: the appeal is not a single object, but the way the white stupas, inscriptions, and hill backdrop build a calm, luminous atmosphere.
