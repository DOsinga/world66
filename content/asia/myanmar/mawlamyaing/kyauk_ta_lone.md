Kyauk Ta Lone is a hill south of Mawlamyaing with a short climb to wide views over the surrounding countryside. The walk takes roughly 20 to 30 minutes and is especially good near sunset.

It is a simple outdoor stop rather than a major monument, but it works well with other sights south of town.
