Maymyo's Chinese Temple reflects the town's Yunnanese community, with a large, colorful complex that stands apart from the colonial hill-station architecture elsewhere in town. It is a modest but worthwhile stop if you are already exploring Pyin Oo Lwin beyond the central market and gardens.

Go for the atmosphere and the mix of cultural influences rather than for a long museum-style visit.
