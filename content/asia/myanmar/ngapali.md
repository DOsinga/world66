Ngapali Beach is one of the most beautiful stretches of coast in [Myanmar](/asia/myanmar) — kilometres of unspoilt white sand fringed with coconut palms and backed by the blue hills of Rakhine State. Unlike the overdeveloped beaches of Southeast Asia, Ngapali remains quiet and low-key, with a handful of small resorts and local fishing villages along the shore.

The beach itself slopes gently into warm water, clear enough to see the bottom. At the southern end, the [Jade Taw fishing village](/asia/myanmar/ngapali/jade_taw_village) has been here longer than any resort, and a morning walk through it gives a very different picture of life on this coast. [Boat trips](/asia/myanmar/ngapali/boat_trips) to the offshore islands are the other main activity — half a day on the water, snorkelling over coral gardens and visiting village harbours.

Ngapali is reached by a short flight from [Yangon](/asia/myanmar/yangon) to Thandwe airport, followed by a 30-minute drive. The season runs November to April; most resorts close during the monsoon.
