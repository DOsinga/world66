Andrew Bay is a quieter coastal area south of Ngapali's main resort beach, with fishing activity, open water views, and a more local rhythm than the hotel strip. It is the kind of place that broadens a Ngapali stay beyond sun loungers and seafood dinners.

Come for a slow excursion rather than facilities. Conditions and access can vary, so use local advice, especially if you are arranging a boat trip or going near the water outside settled beach areas.
