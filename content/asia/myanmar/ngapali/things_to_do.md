---
title: Things to Do
type: section
---

Ngapali's pleasures are simple: the beach itself, boat trips to the fishing villages and offshore islands, and the unhurried pace of a place that has not yet been swallowed by mass tourism. The water is warm and clear, the sand is white and uncrowded, and the only sounds most mornings are the long-tail boats of the local fishermen heading out before dawn.
