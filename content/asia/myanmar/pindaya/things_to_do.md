---
title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

The [Shwe Oo Min Paya cave complex](/asia/myanmar/pindaya/shwe_oo_min_cave) is unmissable — thousands of Buddha images filling a limestone cave above the lake, accumulated over centuries of pilgrimage. The [Shan umbrella workshops](/asia/myanmar/pindaya/shan_umbrella_workshops) along the main street are a pleasant complement, and watching craftspeople work the lacquered paper parasols takes 20 minutes well spent. [Nattamiekan Lake](/asia/myanmar/pindaya/nattamiekan_lake) is beautiful at dawn. If your timing is right, catch the [five-day market](/asia/myanmar/pindaya/pindaya_market) for a window into Shan Hills daily life.
