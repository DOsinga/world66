---
title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Taunggyi's most rewarding experience is its [market](/asia/myanmar/taunggyi/market) — a vast, colourful gathering that draws ethnic groups from across the Shan hills and is one of the most vivid daily markets in Myanmar. The [Shan State Museum](/asia/myanmar/taunggyi/shan_state_museum) provides context for the diversity you see around you. The [Shwe Pone Pwint Pagoda](/asia/myanmar/taunggyi/shwe_pone_pwint_pagoda) on the hilltop above the town offers panoramic views over the plateau.

Taunggyi is most commonly used as a base for visiting [Inle Lake](/asia/myanmar/shan/inlelake), an hour's drive south, but the town itself rewards an overnight stop.
