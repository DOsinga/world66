The Chaukhtatgyi Pagoda houses a massive reclining Buddha — 66 metres long and one of the largest in Myanmar. Unlike the Shwedagon, this is a largely local experience: pilgrims come in large numbers and the atmosphere is devotional rather than touristic.

The enormous figure reclines in a corrugated metal shelter that somehow adds to the experience rather than detracting from it. The face is surprisingly moving at close quarters. Located in Bahan Township, about 3 km north of the Shwedagon. Easily combined with a visit to the Shwedagon Pagoda.
