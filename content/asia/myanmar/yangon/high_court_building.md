Yangon's former High Court is one of the strongest colonial landmarks around Maha Bandula Park, with red brickwork, clock towers, and long arcades that stand out even in a downtown full of old government buildings. The Supreme Court sat here until 2006, when the capital moved to Naypyidaw.

The building is best appreciated from the outside as part of a walk through colonial downtown. It is close to Sule Pagoda, the Strand, and the Secretariat, so it fits naturally into a compact architecture loop.
