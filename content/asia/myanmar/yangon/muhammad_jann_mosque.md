Muhammad Jann Mosque is one of the religious landmarks that reflects Yangon's South Asian Muslim community and the city's layered mercantile past. It adds useful context in a downtown where mosques, churches, Hindu temples, synagogues, and Buddhist pagodas sit close together.

The mosque is generally a respectful exterior stop for most visitors unless access is clearly welcome. It is best included as part of a broader walk through downtown Yangon's religious and colonial-era streets.
