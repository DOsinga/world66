The Taukkyan War Cemetery, maintained by the Commonwealth War Graves Commission, lies about 30 km north of downtown Yangon on the road to Bago. It is one of the most moving sites in Myanmar — 6,374 graves laid out in a large, immaculately kept garden, each stone identical and precisely spaced.

The Memorial to the Missing records over 27,000 names of those who died in the Burma Campaign with no known grave. The scale of loss is almost incomprehensible, and the quietness of the site — well off the tourist trail, rarely visited — intensifies the experience. A short drive from the city; most visitors combine it with a trip to Bago.
