Birgunj Clock Tower is the city's most recognizable central landmark, functioning as both an orientation point and a piece of civic identity. It is not elaborate, but it gives visitors a clear anchor in Nepal's busy gateway city near the Indian border.

The tower is best appreciated while moving through the center rather than as a dedicated excursion. It can help frame a short walk through markets and nearby streets if you are stopping in Birgunj en route.
