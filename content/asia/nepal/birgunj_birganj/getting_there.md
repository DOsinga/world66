---
title: Getting There
type: section
---

The border crossing at Birgunj/Raxaul is the most heavily used overland route between Nepal and India, open to foreign nationals. Immigration offices on both sides of the border handle visas on arrival — bring passport photos and USD cash for the Nepal visa fee. The crossing is open during daylight hours; crossing late in the evening can be problematic as offices close, so plan to arrive with time to spare.

**From Kathmandu by bus:** Tourist buses and local buses run regularly to Birgunj from Kathmandu's Kalanki or Gongabu bus terminals — journey time is around 5–6 hours on the Tribhuvan Highway through the Terai. Night buses are available and commonly used.

**From Raxaul (Indian side):** Raxaul has a railway station with connections to Patna and beyond. Cycle rickshaws ferry people between the Indian and Nepali immigration posts — the actual border gate is a short distance apart. Money changers on both sides offer exchange, though rates vary; Indian rupees are widely accepted in Birgunj itself.

**Onward from Birgunj:** Buses depart from the main bus park north of the border for Kathmandu, Pokhara, and other Nepali destinations. Book tickets and arrange transport before crossing so you are not negotiating logistics while managing luggage and paperwork at the border.
