Parsa National Park, formerly Parsa Wildlife Reserve, protects a large block of Terai forest east of Birgunj. It is less famous than Chitwan, but the landscape is similar in spirit: sal forest, grassland, riverine habitat, and wildlife that can include elephants, deer, wild boar, leopards, and occasionally tiger.

The park works best for travellers who are already using Birgunj as a border or overland stop and want a wilder detour without joining the main Chitwan circuit. Visitor facilities are limited compared with Nepal's better-known parks, so arrange permits, transport, and guiding in advance rather than arriving casually at the gate.
