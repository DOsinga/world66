Butwal's main value for travellers is as a launch point for day trips to the surrounding region. The Siddha Baba gorge to the north is a short ride from the centre, but the major excursion is Lumbini.

**Lumbini** is the birthplace of Siddhartha Gautama, the historical Buddha, and one of the four most sacred sites in Buddhism. The Mayadevi Temple marks the exact spot of his birth; surrounding it is a large archaeological park containing ruins of ancient monasteries, the pillar erected by Emperor Ashoka in the 3rd century BC, and a sacred garden. The UNESCO World Heritage Site is about 22 km west of Butwal, and the road is good — rickshaws, buses, and shared jeeps make the journey straightforward. You need at least half a day to see it properly; a full day is better.

**Palpa (Tansen)** is a hill town about 40 km north of Butwal on the Siddhartha Highway. The old town has a well-preserved bazaar of Newari architecture, the ruins of a Gorkha-era palace, and views over the Terai. The drive takes about 1.5–2 hours and involves some switchbacks as the road climbs into the hills.
