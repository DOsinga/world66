Kali Temple sits on a hill above Dhulikhel and is both a small Hindu shrine and a local viewpoint. The climb is short but steep enough to feel separate from the town, and the reward is a wide look across the surrounding hills.

The temple is busiest with local visitors on weekends. For travellers, it works best as part of a morning walk from Dhulikhel, especially when the air is clear enough for mountain views.
