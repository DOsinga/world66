---
title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Dhulikhel rewards slow exploration. The old Newari town, the sunrise viewpoint, and the ridge walks all deserve unhurried time. The day hike to Namobuddha — one of the most important Buddhist pilgrimage sites in Nepal — is the classic excursion, combining forest trails, hilltop stupas, and monastery courtyards. For a more active day, the mountain-biking loop via Namobuddha and down to Panauti covers serious distance through terraced farmland and traditional villages.
