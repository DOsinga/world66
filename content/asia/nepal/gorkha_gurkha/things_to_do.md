---
title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Gorkha's main draw is its hilltop fortress, but the town and surrounding hills reward further exploration. The walk up to the [Gorkha Durbar](/asia/nepal/gorkha_gurkha/gorkha_durbar) is itself half the experience — the path winds through terraced fields and small shrines with the Himalayan panorama expanding with every step. Within the Durbar complex, the [Gorakhnath Temple](/asia/nepal/gorkha_gurkha/gorakhnath_temple) is the spiritual heart of the site — the tutelary shrine of the Shah dynasty and a place of active pilgrimage. Beyond the Durbar, [Upallokot](/asia/nepal/gorkha_gurkha/upallokot) offers even higher viewpoints for those willing to scramble further.

The [Gorkha Bazaar](/asia/nepal/gorkha_gurkha/gorkha_bazaar) is worth an hour of wandering before or after the Durbar visit — it is an ordinary Nepali hill-town market with none of the tourist veneer of Pokhara or Kathmandu. The [Gorkha Museum](/asia/nepal/gorkha_gurkha/gorkha_museum) near the lower palace adds historical depth, and [Tallo Durbar](/asia/nepal/gorkha_gurkha/tallo_durbar) — the lower palace — is an often-overlooked complement to the fortress above. For trekkers, Gorkha is a starting point for the [Manaslu Circuit](/asia/nepal/gorkha_gurkha/manaslu_circuit), one of Nepal's finest and least crowded long treks.
