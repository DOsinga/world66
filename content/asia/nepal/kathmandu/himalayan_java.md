Himalayan Java is Nepal's most established specialty coffee chain — a local brand that has grown to multiple locations across Kathmandu and other Nepali cities. The Thamel branch, in a bright and spacious building on a main corner, is one of the more comfortable places in the neighbourhood to sit with a laptop and a proper espresso drink.

The coffee is genuinely good — they work with Nepali coffee beans as well as international sources — and the food menu (sandwiches, pastries, breakfast items) is reliable. Wifi is fast and the seating is comfortable, which makes it a popular spot for long-stay travellers working remotely or planning the next leg of a trip.
