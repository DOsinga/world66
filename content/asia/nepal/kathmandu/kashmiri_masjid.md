The Kashmiri Masjid (Pancha Kashmiri Takiya Masjid), near Rani Pokhari, is one of the oldest mosques in Nepal, dating to the 15th century. It serves Kathmandu's small Muslim community — descended mainly from Kashmiri traders who followed the ancient route through the Himalaya — and stands quietly amid the commercial bustle of central Kathmandu as a reminder of the city's religious diversity.

Visitors are welcome outside prayer times; ask at the entrance if in doubt. The mosque is modest in scale and unpretentious in decor, which gives it a genuine feeling of daily use rather than monument status.
