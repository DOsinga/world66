Ranibari Community Forest is a 63-hectare patch of mixed forest in northern Kathmandu, maintained by the local ward community and open to visitors for a small fee. It is the most accessible natural space within the city and a remarkable counterpoint to the built environment a few hundred metres away.

The forest is one of the best birdwatching sites in the valley without leaving Kathmandu — over 100 species have been recorded, including several kingfisher species, owls, woodpeckers, and migrating raptors. Walking the forest paths in the early morning is peaceful and the contrast with the city's standard chaos is striking. Take the ring road north from Thamel and follow the signage for the forest entrance.
