Chobhar Caves are limestone caves near the Chobhar Gorge, with passages said to run for more than a kilometre. The caves sit in a karst landscape close to the Bagmati River.

They are a notable natural sight on the edge of the Kathmandu Valley, but visitors should go with proper local guidance rather than exploring the openings alone.
