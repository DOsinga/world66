---
title: Eating Out
type: section
---

Lumbini is not a foodie destination. The **Tourism Village** has a small cluster of mid-range hotel restaurants serving identikit menus of Indian, Tibetan, and Continental food at inflated prices — passable, never memorable. **Nepali momos** and **dal bhat** at the simpler restaurants near the main gate are usually the best value.

Several monasteries — notably the **Korean** and the **Japanese** — run vegetarian canteens that welcome visitors at lunchtime. The food is simple, monastic, and excellent. A small donation is expected.

The market town of **Bhairahawa**, 20 minutes away, has a wider range of restaurants serving North Indian and Nepali food at local prices.
