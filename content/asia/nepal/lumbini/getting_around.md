---
title: Getting Around
type: section
---

The Lumbini complex itself is roughly **4 kilometres long and 1.5 kilometres wide**. Most visitors hire a **cycle rickshaw** by the half-day; the going rate is modest. **Bicycles** can be rented from most hotels in the Tourism Village.

Walking the whole zone is possible but tiring in the Terai heat. The Sacred Garden is closed to vehicles — you walk in once you reach the south gate.

Around town, **electric rickshaws** run between the Tourism Village, the main gate, and the village of **Lumbini Sanskritik** to the east. **Taxis** for day trips to Tilaurakot are best arranged through your hotel.
