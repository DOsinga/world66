---
title: Getting There
type: section
---

**By air**: the nearest airport is **Gautam Buddha International Airport** at **Bhairahawa** (Siddharthanagar), 22 kilometres east of Lumbini. It now handles a few international flights from Indian cities, Kuala Lumpur, and a regular domestic route from Kathmandu. Taxis from the airport to Lumbini cost a fixed fee; the drive is under 40 minutes.

**By road from Kathmandu**: the journey via the Prithvi Highway and the Siddhartha Highway is around 280 kilometres and takes a hard 8–10 hours by bus or jeep. The road climbs over the Mahabharat range and descends to the Terai. Tourist buses go overnight; jeeps and microvans are faster in daylight.

**By road from India**: the main crossing is at **Sonauli**, a short rickshaw ride from the Indian border post and an hour from Lumbini by bus or taxi. Sonauli connects by overnight bus to Gorakhpur and Varanasi.

**By road from Pokhara**: easier than from Kathmandu, around 5–6 hours by direct tourist bus.
