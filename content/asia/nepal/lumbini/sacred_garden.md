The southernmost of the three planned zones, containing **Lumbini's actual archaeological core** — the Mayadevi Temple, the Ashoka Pillar, the Puskarini Pond, the foundations of devotional stupas built by pilgrims over many centuries, and the **excavated brick mounds** of ancient monasteries.

The Sacred Garden is fenced and quiet, with peacocks and storks wandering the lawns. Entry from the main central canal at the south end; shoes must be removed at the temple. Allow at least 90 minutes to walk the whole garden and read the excavation panels. The mood is genuinely sacred rather than touristic; behave accordingly.
