---
title: When to Go
type: section
---

Lumbini sits in the **Terai lowlands** and shares the climate of the north Indian plain: a sweltering summer, a humid monsoon, and a clear dry winter.

**October to March** is the comfortable season — dry, cool nights, warm days, fields green from the recent monsoon. December and January can be foggy in the early mornings; the fog usually burns off by 10 am. **April and May** are punishingly hot (over 40 °C is normal) and dusty. The **monsoon** from June to September is hot and wet; mosquitoes are bad, and the Sacred Garden floods in places.

**Buddha Jayanti** in May is the major pilgrimage date when the site fills with monks and visitors from across Buddhist Asia. The atmosphere is extraordinary; book accommodation well ahead.
