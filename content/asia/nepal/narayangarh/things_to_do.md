---
title: Things to Do
type: section
---

Most visitors to Narayangarh are passing through on the way to Chitwan National Park. If you have a few hours to spare before your jungle lodges, the Triveni Ghat river confluence is a quiet spot, and the Ganeshthan Temple is worth a brief visit. The town itself is functional rather than charming — a place to change buses, eat a meal, and move on.
