---
title: Things to Do
type: section
---

Panauti is compact and best explored on foot in half a day. The [Indreshwar Mahadev Temple](/asia/nepal/panauti/indreshwar_mahadev_temple) is the centrepiece and reason most visitors come. The [Brahmayani Temple](/asia/nepal/panauti/brahmayani_temple) at the river confluence is the second major religious site. The [Unmatta Bhairav Temple](/asia/nepal/panauti/unmatta_bhairav_temple) and the carved wooden architecture of the old quarters make for good wandering. The town rewards slow walking rather than itinerary-ticking.
