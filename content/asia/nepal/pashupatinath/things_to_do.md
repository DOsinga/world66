title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Pashupatinath is a place of pilgrimage and cremation, not a conventional tourist attraction. Visiting requires a degree of sensitivity — you are walking through an active religious site where Nepali Hindus come to die, mourn, and worship. Dress modestly, do not interrupt cremations or religious ceremonies, and follow the guidance of signs on what is off-limits to non-Hindus.
