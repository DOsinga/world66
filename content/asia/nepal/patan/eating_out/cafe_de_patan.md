Inside the Patan Museum compound, this is one of the better lunch stops in the city — the courtyard setting, with carved wooden columns and a view onto the square, is hard to beat. The menu runs to simple Nepali thali, sandwiches, and cakes. It is primarily a café rather than a full restaurant, but the quality is reliable and the location exceptional. Closes with the museum.
