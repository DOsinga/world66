A small restaurant near Durbar Square focused on traditional Newari cuisine — the food of the valley's indigenous community. Dishes include bara (lentil pancakes), choila (spiced grilled meat), yomari (steamed rice dumplings), and aila (local grain spirit). Newari food is distinct from standard Nepali dal bhat and worth seeking out. The portions are generous and the prices modest. Look for the hand-painted sign on the lane behind the square.
