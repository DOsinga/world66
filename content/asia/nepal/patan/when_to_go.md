---
title: When to Go
type: section
---

The best time to visit Patan — and the Kathmandu Valley generally — is during the dry seasons: October to November and March to April. Skies are clear, temperatures are comfortable (15–25°C), and the mountains are visible from high ground. The monsoon runs from June to September; rain falls heavily in the afternoons, streets flood, and the valley turns intensely green. Winter (December to February) is dry but cold, especially at night.

**Rato Machhindranath Chariot Festival** (April–May) is Patan's most spectacular event. A massive wooden chariot, built fresh each year, is hauled through the city's streets over several weeks by teams of rope-pullers. The festival honours the god of rain and harvest and has been observed here for centuries. The final ceremony — displaying the jewelled vest of the god — only takes place when astrologers declare the time auspicious, and may happen only once every decade or so.

**Janai Purnima** (August full moon) brings large crowds to Kumbheshwar Temple, where pilgrims bathe in the sacred pond. **Krishna Janmashtami** (August–September) is observed at the Krishna Mandir in Durbar Square with all-night prayers and oil lamps illuminating the stone frieze.
