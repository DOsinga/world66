The second of the **Pokhara Valley** lakes, about 15 km east of town. Begnas is bigger than Phewa, surrounded by terraced rice fields rather than the tourist strip, and has fewer than a tenth of the visitors. A few small resorts have appeared along the shore but the place still feels essentially rural.

You can row the lake in a hired wooden boat, swim safely (unusual for Nepal), or walk the ridge between Begnas and the smaller, neighbouring **Rupa Lake**. Visiting in a half-day from Pokhara is easy by taxi or local bus to Begnas village; a night stay in one of the small lakeside lodges is the better way to do it if you have the time.
