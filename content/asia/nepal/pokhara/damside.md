Locally called **Pardi** but known to most travellers as Damside, this is the quieter southern continuation of [Lakeside](/asia/nepal/pokhara/lakeside). It sits beside the small dam at the outflow of Phewa Lake, with a different — and arguably better — view of the Annapurnas and Machhapuchhre than the main Lakeside strip.

Damside has a handful of guesthouses and restaurants, the dam-side walk along the lake, and easy access to [Davis Falls](/asia/nepal/pokhara/davis_falls) and the [Gupteshwor cave](/asia/nepal/pokhara/gupteshwor_cave). Travellers who want the lake without the noise of the main strip often base themselves here. A 15- to 20-minute walk or a short taxi ride to central Lakeside.
