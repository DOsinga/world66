Dipang Lake is one of the quieter lakes east of central Pokhara, near Lekhnath and the better-known Begnas and Rupa lakes. Come for a low-key nature stop: lotus flowers, waterbirds, and a slower rural edge to Pokhara than the busy Lakeside strip.
