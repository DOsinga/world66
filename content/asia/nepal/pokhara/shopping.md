---
title: "Shopping"
type: section
---

Most Lakeside shopping is trekking-oriented: down jackets, hiking poles, knock-off Northface fleeces, sleeping bags, daypacks and boots. Quality is variable — genuine Lowe Alpine and Mountain Hardwear shops exist alongside straightforward fakes, often at a third of the European price for the same gear.

Beyond trekking equipment, you can find Tibetan singing bowls, prayer flags, thangka paintings, pashmina shawls, silver jewellery, and the local **dhaka** weaving (geometric patterns in red, black and white) that is one of the genuine Pokhara crafts. Bargain hard — first prices in Lakeside are typically two to three times the real price. The shops along the central strip have the widest selection; for cheaper, more local prices visit the old bazaar in **Mahendrapul** or **Bagar**.
