The closest of the four Pokhara Tibetan refugee settlements to Lakeside, just down the road from [Davis Falls](/asia/nepal/pokhara/davis_falls). About 700 Tibetans live in Tashiling, working the carpet looms, raising chickens, and running a handful of small handicraft shops along the entry road.

A small monastery sits at the centre of the settlement, with morning and evening prayers. The carpet workshop is open to visitors and the rugs are sold direct from the loom; bargaining is gentler than in Lakeside. The visit fits naturally with a Davis Falls / Gupteshwor cave outing — a good combined half-day from Lakeside.
