title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Pokhara is about the outdoors. Day hikes lead up to hilltop viewpoints — [Sarangkot](/asia/nepal/pokhara/sarangkot) for sunrise on the Annapurnas, the [World Peace Pagoda](/asia/nepal/pokhara/world_peace_pagoda) for an afternoon climb above [Phewa Lake](/asia/nepal/pokhara/phewa_lake) — and the lake itself is the focal point of the city, with **doonga** rowboats, the island shrine of **Tal Barahi**, and an old town a few kilometres north of the new tourist strip. For travellers not heading into the mountains, the [International Mountain Museum](/asia/nepal/pokhara/international_mountain_museum) is the best single place in Nepal to learn about the Himalaya, while the [Gurkha Memorial Museum](/asia/nepal/pokhara/gurkha_memorial_museum) covers the Gurkha regiments' two centuries of service. The **Annapurna Natural History Museum** and the spectacular **Davis Falls** sinkhole round out the half-day options on land. From the sky, Pokhara is the world's tandem-paragliding capital — the launch from Sarangkot and landing on the lakeshore is one of the great accessible adventure activities anywhere.
