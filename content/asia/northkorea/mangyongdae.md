Mangyongdae is a suburb west of [Pyongyang](/asia/northkorea/pyongyang), on the banks of the Taedong River, known as the birthplace of Kim Il-sung. The site has been maintained as a shrine: a simple thatched farmhouse where the founding leader was born in 1912, surrounded by trees and kept immaculately. Visitors are expected to bow.

The surrounding area has been developed into the [Mangyongdae Children's Palace](/asia/northkorea/mangyongdae/mangyongdae_childrens_palace) — a large extracurricular activities centre for selected children — and the [Mangyongdae Funfair](/asia/northkorea/mangyongdae/mangyongdae_funfair) with rides and attractions. The funfair is one of the places in Pyongyang where you can observe families spending leisure time in something like a natural setting.

Mangyongdae is a short drive from central Pyongyang and is included on virtually every tour itinerary.
