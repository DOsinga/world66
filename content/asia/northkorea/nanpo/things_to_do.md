title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Nampo is a working port city; expectations should be set accordingly. The [West Sea Barrage](/asia/northkorea/nanpo/west_sea_barrage) at the mouth of the Taedong River is the single sight that justifies the half-day trip from [Pyongyang](/asia/northkorea/pyongyang). Tour itineraries also sometimes include a visit to a cooperative farm in the rural hinterland, the Nampo Stadium (occasionally used for staged mass events), and the hot springs at nearby Onchon if the guide is in a generous mood.

You will not wander Nampo on your own. The city is shown to foreign visitors only on guided tours, and the routes are tightly choreographed. Bring a notebook and observe.
