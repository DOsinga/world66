This exhibition centre is devoted to the Kimilsungia and Kimjungilia, the hybrid flowers named for Kim Il-sung and Kim Jong-il. Displays fill two floors, and annual flower shows bring formal bouquets from foreign embassies and state organisations.

It is a very Pyongyang sort of stop: part botanical display, part political theatre. Visitors normally see it as part of a guided itinerary rather than independently.
