Kumsusan Palace of the Sun is one of the most formal and politically charged places a visitor can be taken in Pyongyang. The vast building, once Kim Il Sung's official residence, now functions as the mausoleum for Kim Il Sung and Kim Jong Il, with choreographed movement through marble halls, long corridors, and carefully staged memorial spaces.

Independent wandering is not possible, and behaviour is tightly controlled. Dress formally, follow your guides exactly, and understand that the experience is less like visiting a museum than entering a state ritual. For travellers trying to understand North Korea's political theatre, it is among the most revealing stops in the capital.
