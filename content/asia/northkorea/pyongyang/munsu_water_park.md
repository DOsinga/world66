Munsu Water Park is one of Pyongyang's showcase leisure complexes, a large indoor-and-outdoor water park built to demonstrate modern recreation as much as to provide it. For foreign visitors, access depends entirely on the itinerary arranged by guides.

It is not a conventional independent-travel attraction, but in Pyongyang that caveat applies to almost everything. If included, it offers a rare look at state-curated leisure and the city's staged version of everyday fun.
