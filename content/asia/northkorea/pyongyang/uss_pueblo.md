The USS Pueblo is a captured United States Navy intelligence ship kept in Pyongyang as a museum exhibit. North Korea seized the vessel in 1968, and the ship remains one of the more unusual Cold War-era sights shown to foreign visitors.

Visits are normally arranged as part of a guided itinerary. The onboard displays focus on the capture of the ship and its place in North Korea's official version of modern history.
