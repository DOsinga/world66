The Workers Party Monument is one of Pyongyang's clearest pieces of political theatre: three vast hands hold a hammer, sickle, and writing brush to represent workers, farmers, and intellectuals. It was built to mark the party's anniversary and is staged with the same axial confidence as the city's larger ceremonial spaces.

Like most sightseeing in Pyongyang, you see it as part of a guided itinerary, not as a casual wander. Even so, it is worth paying attention to the scale, the relief panels, and the way the monument turns ideology into urban design.
