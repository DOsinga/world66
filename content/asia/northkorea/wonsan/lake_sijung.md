Lake Sijung, on the road between Wonsan and the Kumgang Mountains, is a 2.94-square-kilometre lake that was originally a sea lagoon before a sand barrier formed and enclosed it. The result is an unusual landscape: a calm lake with seven small islands, set immediately beside the Sea of Japan. North Korean tour programmes sometimes include Lake Sijung as part of a Wonsan-Kumgang itinerary.

The lake is used for boating, and the therapeutic mud on the lakebed is one of the more unexpected North Korean tourism attractions. A sanatorium offers treatments to North Korean and, occasionally, foreign guests. The combination of the lake, the offshore coast, and the mountain backdrop makes Sijung one of the most photogenic spots in the Wonsan area.
