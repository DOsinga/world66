The Wonsan-Kalma Coastal Tourist Zone is the most ambitious tourism development in North Korean history — a 2.5-kilometre stretch of beach on the Kalma Peninsula backed by roughly 400 buildings including high-rise hotels, villas, a water park, a concert hall, a department store, and multiple restaurants. The zone was designed to accommodate up to 20,000 guests simultaneously and opened in June 2025.

Its first foreign visitors were Russian tour groups in summer 2025. Whether and when it will open to Western visitors depends entirely on North Korean government policy, which remains unpredictable. The zone represents a significant bet by the North Korean leadership on tourism as an economic strategy — though the scale of the investment and the controlled conditions of any visit make it more a showcase than a conventional tourist destination.
