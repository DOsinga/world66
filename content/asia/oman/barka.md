Barka is a small coastal town on the Batinah plain, about 80km west of Muscat and easily done as a day trip. [Barka Fort](/asia/oman/barka/barka_fort) is well-preserved and sits right on the waterfront, which makes it more photogenic than many of Oman's inland fortifications — and historically significant as the birthplace of the Busaidi dynasty that still rules Oman today. The sea is close enough that you can walk between the fort and the shore in a few minutes, and the [beach](/asia/oman/barka/barka_beach) here has a working-coast character that the more developed stretches near Muscat lack.

Barka is also known for [bullfighting](/asia/oman/barka/barka_bullfighting) — the Omani style, which involves two bulls pushing against each other until one backs down, rather than any matador theatrics. Bouts happen on Friday mornings in season, usually between October and April, and attract local crowds rather than tourist coaches. The [souq](/asia/oman/barka/barka_souq) is a genuine market for the surrounding Batinah agricultural district, with fresh fish, mangoes, dates, and a livestock section.

Barka works well as a half-day excursion from Muscat, or as a stop along the route west toward Rustaq, Nakhal, or Sohar.
