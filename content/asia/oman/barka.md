Barka is a small coastal town on the Batinah plain, about 80km west of Muscat and easily done as a day trip. [Barka Fort](/asia/oman/barka/barka_fort) is well-preserved and sits right on the waterfront, which makes it more photogenic than many of Oman's inland fortifications. The [souq](/asia/oman/barka/barka_souq) beside it sells fresh fish and dates from the Batinah's famous palm gardens.

Barka is also known for [Omani bullfighting](/asia/oman/barka/omani_bullfighting) — two bulls pushing against each other until one backs down, rather than any matador theatrics. Bouts happen on Friday mornings in season, usually between October and April, and attract local crowds rather than tourist coaches.

There isn't much else to keep you in Barka beyond the fort and, if you time it right, the bullfighting. But as a half-day excursion from Muscat, or a stop along the route west toward Sohar, it's a pleasant one.
