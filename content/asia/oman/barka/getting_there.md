---
title: Getting There
type: section
---

Barka is 80 km west of Muscat on the main coastal road (Highway 1, also signed as the Batinah Expressway or old Route 1). The drive from Muscat takes about an hour.

**By Car**: Most visitors come by rental car from Muscat, where international car hire agencies operate at Muscat International Airport and the major hotels. The highway is fast and well-maintained. Barka makes an easy half-day trip from Muscat, or a stop on the way west toward Sohar or the UAE border.

**By Bus**: ONTC buses run along the Batinah coast between Muscat and Sohar, passing through Barka. The service is inexpensive but schedules are not always convenient for a short visit. Check the ONTC website or Muscat bus station for current times.

**By Taxi**: Shared taxis (called baisa taxis) occasionally run the coast route, but private taxis from Muscat to Barka and back will cost significantly more than hiring a car for the day.
