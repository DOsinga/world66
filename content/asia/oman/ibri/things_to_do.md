title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Ibri's attractions divide between the town itself and the remarkable landscape surrounding it. In town, the [Ibri Fort](/asia/oman/ibri/ibri_fort) is a well-restored example of Omani military architecture, and the [old souq](/asia/oman/ibri/ibri_souq) remains a working market rather than a tourist attraction. Outside town, the [Bat Tombs](/asia/oman/ibri/bat_tombs) — a UNESCO World Heritage Site — are among the best-preserved Bronze Age burial sites in the Arabian Peninsula, and the mountain scenery toward [Jebel Shams](/asia/oman/ibri/day_trips) is spectacular.
