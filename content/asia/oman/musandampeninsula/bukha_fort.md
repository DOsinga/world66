Bukha Fort is a restored 17th-century fort on the Musandam coast, south of Khasab. Its rounded, pear-shaped watchtower is the feature to look for, and the setting gives a good sense of how small coastal settlements controlled the approaches between the mountains and the sea.

It is most useful as a stop on a Musandam drive rather than as a stand-alone destination. Combine it with coastal viewpoints and other forts in the peninsula.
