Bassa Beach is a small, accessible beach near Khasab, good for a quick swim or a quiet look at the Musandam coast. It is not the regions most dramatic scenery, but it is convenient and gives travellers a simple shore stop close to town.

Use it as a low-key addition to Khasab Fort, harbour walks, or a dhow cruise day. Bring shade and water, and remember that the best Musandam landscapes are still out in the khors and mountain roads.
