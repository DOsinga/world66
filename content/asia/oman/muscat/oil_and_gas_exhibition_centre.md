The Oil and Gas Exhibition Centre is a small, specialist museum run by Petroleum Development Oman. Its displays explain how oil and natural gas form, how they are found, and how extraction works, which makes it more interesting than the dry name suggests.

It pairs naturally with the nearby PDO Planetarium as a family-friendly science stop. For most visitors it is a short indoor break, but it helps explain the industry that reshaped modern Oman.
