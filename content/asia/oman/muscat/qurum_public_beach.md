Qurum Public Beach is one of Muscat's most accessible city beaches, with a long sweep of sand and views back toward the low hills behind the coast. Facilities are simple, but the beach is clean and popular with local families.

Weekends can be busy, so come on a weekday morning or late afternoon if you want a quieter walk or swim. It pairs well with nearby Qurum and Shatti al-Qurum, where cafes and restaurants are easier to find.
