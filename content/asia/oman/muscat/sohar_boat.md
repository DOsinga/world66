The Sohar is a reconstructed dhow built in Sur for Tim Severin's 1980 voyage from Oman to Guangzhou, an attempt to retrace the legendary voyages associated with Sindbad. The boat is displayed in Muscat as a compact but memorable link between Oman's seafaring history and the Indian Ocean trade routes.
