Trekking Path C38 is a short Muscat hike that quickly leaves the city behind for dry, rugged hills. The reward is a set of views back over the coast and the surrounding mountains.

It is best done early or late in the day, with sturdy shoes and plenty of water. The route works well as an active add-on near Muttrah and Riyam Park.
