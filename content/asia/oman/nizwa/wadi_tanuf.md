Wadi Tanuf starts with the photogenic ruins of Old Tanuf, a mud-brick village damaged during the Jebel Akhdar conflict in the 1950s. Beyond the ruins, a rough 4WD track heads deeper into the wadi toward walking routes through the neighbouring valleys.

It is an easy side trip from Nizwa if you have a car, and a better one if you are set up for rough tracks and dry-country hiking.
