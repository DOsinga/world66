The Footprint of Prophet Saleh's Camel is a small shrine built around a large mark in the bedrock, linked by tradition to the camel in the Quranic story of Prophet Saleh. It is a modest stop, but it gives a glimpse of Salalah's local religious geography beyond the better-known tombs and archaeological sites.

Visitors should remove shoes, and women are expected to cover their hair.
