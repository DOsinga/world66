The tomb of the Prophet Job (Nabi Ayub in Arabic) sits on a hilltop west of Salalah, at an altitude that gives sweeping views over the city and the Dhofar mountains. Job is revered by Muslims, Christians, and Jews alike, and pilgrims come throughout the year to pay their respects at the enclosed tomb structure.

The setting is peaceful and atmospheric, especially in the early morning or late afternoon. The hillside path down from the tomb leads to Ain Ayub, the spring said to have appeared when Job struck the ground. The ruins of old palace structures are scattered around the hilltop. The site is freely accessible; dress modestly and remove shoes to enter the tomb enclosure.
