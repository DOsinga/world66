The Tomb of Nabi Umran is a small shrine in Salalah associated in local tradition with the prophet Imran. Its striking feature is the unusually long covered tomb, set inside a simple compound that contrasts with the city's broader coastal and frankincense landscapes.

The site is a short, respectful visit rather than a major architectural attraction. Dress modestly, keep expectations modest, and treat it as one of several religious and historical stops that help explain Salalah's place in Omani and regional pilgrimage geography.
