Wadi Dawkah Natural Park protects one of Dhofar's key frankincense landscapes, with thousands of frankincense trees spread across a dry wadi. Some trees are more than 200 years old, and frankincense is still harvested here by local Bedu families.

The visit is simple: park by the road and walk among the trees. It is best appreciated as part of a wider Salalah and Dhofar trip focused on frankincense history.
