Wadi Ash Shab is one of Oman's classic wadi walks: a bright canyon with year-round pools, palms and a final swim into a cave waterfall. The trail starts near the Highway 17 overpass and is straightforward, but you should expect heat, uneven ground and some swimming if you want to reach the best part.
