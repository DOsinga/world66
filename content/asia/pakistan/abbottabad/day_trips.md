The standard day trip from Abbottabad is up to the [Galyat hill stations](/asia/pakistan/abbottabad/galyat_hill_stations) — Nathiagali in particular — and back. Allow a full day with a stop for lunch. [Thandiani](/asia/pakistan/abbottabad/thandiani), east of the city, makes a similar half-day outing on a less travelled road.

**Naran and the Kaghan Valley** — the most spectacular day's drive from Abbottabad, but really an overnight rather than a day trip. The road climbs the Kunhar River through pine forest to Naran (180 km, around five hours) and on to Saif-ul-Maluk Lake. The stretch is closed in winter.

[Taxila](/asia/pakistan/taxila), the Buddhist archaeological site 100 km south, can be combined with a return to Islamabad if you are not coming back to Abbottabad. The drive takes about two hours each way; the museum and the three main excavated sites make a full day.

[Murree](/asia/pakistan/murree), the colonial-era hill station on the road to Islamabad, is two hours south and another reasonable day out — though many travellers find it overdeveloped.
