---
title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Abbottabad is a transit town more than a destination, but it has enough to fill a day if you are stopping over. The most rewarding things involve getting out of the city for a few hours.

In the city itself, the British cantonment is the historic core — quiet streets, gardens, and the surviving colonial buildings. [St Luke's Church](/asia/pakistan/abbottabad/st_lukes_church), the [Old Christian Cemetery](/asia/pakistan/abbottabad/old_christian_cemetery), and the [Jalal Baba Auditorium](/asia/pakistan/abbottabad/jalal_baba_auditorium) are all walkable from the centre. [Shimla Hill](/asia/pakistan/abbottabad/shimla_hill) offers the best view of the valley and is reached on foot from the main bazaar.

Out of town, [Thandiani](/asia/pakistan/abbottabad/thandiani), the [Galyat hill stations](/asia/pakistan/abbottabad/galyat_hill_stations), and the [Ilyasi Mosque](/asia/pakistan/abbottabad/ilyasi_mosque) at Nawan Shehr — famous for the cold spring that bubbles up inside it — are the main half-day options.
