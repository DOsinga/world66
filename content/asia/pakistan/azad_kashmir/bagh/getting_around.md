---
title: Getting Around
type: section
---

**Bagh town centre** is small enough to cover on foot. The bazaar, bus stand, and main government offices are within easy walking distance of each other.

**Within the district**, shared wagons (minivans) run between Bagh and the surrounding villages. They depart when full from the main transport point near the bazaar. Fares are low but schedules are informal.

**For Sudhan Gali**, the easiest option is to hire a car or wagon for the day from Bagh town. The road is narrow and winding; the drive takes roughly an hour each way. Motorcycle hire is also available but the mountain roads demand experience.

Mountain roads throughout the district require care — surfaces can be rough, bends are tight, and landslide debris is possible after heavy rain. If you are driving yourself, allow extra time.
