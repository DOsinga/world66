The hills around Banjosa are dotted with small viewpoints, forested ridges, and villages reachable in under an hour by road. [Rawalakot](/asia/pakistan/azad_kashmir/rawala_kot), the nearest town at about 18 km, is the practical base for wider exploration of the Poonch Valley — it has the banks, pharmacies, and restaurants Banjosa lacks, and a bazaar worth a wander for handicrafts, dried fruit, and nuts.

The standout day out is [Toli Pir](/asia/pakistan/azad_kashmir/toli_pir_rawalakot), a hill station at around 3,090 metres reached by a winding two-hour mountain drive. Above the treeline you get some of the widest views in Azad Kashmir, with the Pir Panjal range filling the northern horizon. There is a Sufi shrine at the top and alpine meadows that are exceptional for a picnic in June and July, when the wildflowers are out.

To the south, [Hajira](/asia/pakistan/azad_kashmir/hajira) and [Pallandri](/asia/pakistan/azad_kashmir/pallandri) are each worth a half-day for their frontier markets and pine-and-cedar hill scenery. For something more ambitious, the Neelum Valley — the jewel of Azad Kashmir — lies to the north and is accessible via [Muzaffarabad](/asia/pakistan/azad_kashmir/muzaffar_abad) on a long but rewarding full-day excursion.
