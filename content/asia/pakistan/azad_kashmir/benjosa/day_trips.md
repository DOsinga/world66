---
title: Day Trips
type: section
---

The hills around Banjosa are dotted with small viewpoints, forested ridges, and villages reachable in under an hour by road. [Rawalakot](/asia/pakistan/azad_kashmir/rawala_kot), the nearest town, is 18 km away and makes a practical base for wider exploration of the Poonch Valley. [Hajira](/asia/pakistan/azad_kashmir/hajira) and [Palandri](/asia/pakistan/azad_kashmir/pallandri) to the south are both worth a half-day each for their markets and hill scenery.

For something more ambitious, the Neelum Valley — the jewel of Azad Kashmir — lies to the north and is accessible via Muzaffarabad in a long but rewarding full-day excursion.
