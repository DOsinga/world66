---
title: Eating Out
type: section
---

Eating options at Banjosa are modest and clustered around the lakeside — a handful of small dhabas and stalls catering mainly to day-trippers and weekend visitors. The menus are short and consistent: grilled trout from the lake, karahi chicken, dal, and parathas. Quality is generally good because the ingredients are fresh and the competition is nearby.

Bring snacks or a packed lunch if you are planning to spend most of the day on the trails — the lakeside spots close early in the evening and there is nothing open after dark outside the Tourism Department resthouse.
