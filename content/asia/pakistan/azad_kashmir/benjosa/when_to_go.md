---
title: When to Go
type: section
---

The high season is May to early October, when daytime temperatures sit between 15 and 25 degrees and the forest is at its greenest. Weekends in July and August are busy with families escaping the plains heat — book ahead or arrive midweek.

Winter brings snow from December through February and most of the lakeside facilities close. The road can be cut off after a heavy fall. Spring and autumn are the most rewarding times: cool, clear, and almost empty.
