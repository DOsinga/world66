---
title: "Getting There"
type: section
---

Mong is connected by road to [Rawalakot](/asia/pakistan/azad_kashmir/rawala_kot), the main town of Poonch District, about 25 km away. Shared Suzuki vans and occasional buses run between them throughout the day, though the schedule is informal. From Rawalakot, onward connections reach Muzaffarabad and eventually the rest of Pakistan.

Foreign visitors to Azad Kashmir should verify current permit requirements before travelling — the rules around foreign tourist access to different areas of Azad Kashmir change periodically, and proximity to the Line of Control affects access in parts of Poonch District.
