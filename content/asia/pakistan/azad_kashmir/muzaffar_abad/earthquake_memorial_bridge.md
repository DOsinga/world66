The Earthquake Memorial Bridge spans the Jhelum River in southern Muzaffarabad, a 474-meter cable-stayed structure built to replace infrastructure destroyed in the catastrophic 2005 earthquake. It is also known as the Zulfiqar Ali Bhutto Bridge.

The bridge is an impressive piece of engineering in a difficult mountain environment. Beyond its practical function, it serves as a reminder of the earthquake that reshaped this city — and killed tens of thousands across the region. The surrounding valley gives a sense of the scale of what was lost and what was rebuilt in the years following the disaster.
