Jalalabad Garden is a small botanical-style garden in Muzaffarabad, best when flowers are in season. Tulips are the usual highlight, and the grounds also have birds and shaded paths that make it a gentle break from the city streets.

It is not a major destination by itself, but it is a pleasant short stop if you are already exploring Muzaffarabad.
