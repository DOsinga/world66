---
title: "Things to Do in Rawalakot"
type: section
---

Rawalakot's attractions are all about the natural landscape. The district has mountain viewpoints, a lake resort, river valleys, and hot springs — enough variety to fill several days if you have the transport to reach them. [Banjosa Lake](/asia/pakistan/azad_kashmir/rawala_kot/banjosa) and [Toli Pir](/asia/pakistan/azad_kashmir/rawala_kot/toli_pir) are the two most popular outings, the [Tatta Pani](/asia/pakistan/azad_kashmir/rawala_kot/tatta_pani) hot springs are an unusual half-day trip, and the [Poonch River Valley](/asia/pakistan/azad_kashmir/rawala_kot/poonch_river) is the road to drive if you have time on your hands.
