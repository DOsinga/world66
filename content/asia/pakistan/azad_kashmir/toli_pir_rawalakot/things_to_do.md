Toli Pir's appeal is almost entirely natural — alpine meadows, sweeping valley views, and the quiet of a hill station that sees more domestic Pakistani tourists than foreigners. Rawalakot town below serves as the practical base, with Toli Pir the scenic destination and Banjosa Lake as a secondary stop on the same circuit.
