Fareed Gate is one of the historic entry points into Bahawalpur's old walled city, standing near the heart of the commercial district. The gate itself is a substantial arched structure, and the streets around it retain the dense, layered character of a functioning South Asian bazaar — textile traders, halwa sellers, jewellers, and spice merchants operating in buildings that have seen continuous use for generations.

It is the best place to begin a walking exploration of the old city. The labyrinthine alleys around the gate lead to the Shahi Bazaar, the central library, and the older neighbourhoods where the decorated facades of merchant houses can still be found.
