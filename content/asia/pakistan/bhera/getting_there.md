---
title: "Getting There"
type: section
---

Bhera is conveniently located roughly midway between Lahore and Islamabad, both about two to two-and-a-half hours away by road.

**By road:** The Lahore-Islamabad Motorway (M2) has an interchange near Bhera. From there it is a short drive into town. The old Grand Trunk Road (N-5) also passes close by. Buses and minibuses run along this corridor from both Lahore and Islamabad; ask for Bhera or the Sargodha junction.

**By train:** The nearest railway station is at Khushab or Mianwali; Bhera itself is not on the main line. From those stations you would need onward transport by road.

**By air:** The nearest airports are in Lahore (Allama Iqbal International, about 170km) and Islamabad (Islamabad International, about 140km). Both are well-served by domestic and international flights.
