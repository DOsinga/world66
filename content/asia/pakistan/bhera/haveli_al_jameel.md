The Haveli Al-Jameel, also known locally as Khoti Babu Usman, is a traditional Punjabi mansion that stands as one of the better examples of vernacular architecture in Bhera's old city. The haveli form — a multi-storey courtyard house with elaborately carved wooden balconies, decorative plasterwork, and a rear garden — was the architecture of the prosperous merchant class, and Bhera's position on major trade routes made it wealthy enough to produce several such buildings.

The interior has carved woodwork and decorative plaster elements that reflect the craftsmanship of pre-partition Punjab. The rear garden, while no longer maintained to its original standard, still gives a sense of the private world behind the public facade. The haveli is best seen by asking locally for access — it is not a formal tourist site — but Bhera's residents are generally hospitable to visitors with a genuine interest in the old city.
