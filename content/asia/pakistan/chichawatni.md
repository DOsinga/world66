Chichawatni is a mid-sized agricultural town in Punjab, about 45 km south of Sahiwal on the Grand Trunk Road. It sits roughly 20 km from the ancient Indus Valley site of [Harappa](/asia/pakistan/harappa), making it a plausible base for anyone heading to that archaeological site. Most travellers pass through rather than stop, but the town has a few things worth knowing about.

The local economy runs on wheat, cotton and sugar cane. The area is also known for its buffalo trade — the [Chichawatni Cattle Market](/asia/pakistan/chichawatni/cattle_market) is one of the larger livestock markets in the district, particularly active around Eid al-Adha. On the outskirts, the [Chichawatni Forest](/asia/pakistan/chichawatni/chichawatni_forest) covers around 9,000 acres, making it the second-largest planted forest in Pakistan after Changa Manga. It's a quiet place for a walk or a drive away from the dust of the GT Road.

Crescent Textile's ghee mills here were historically among the first vanaspati (vegetable ghee) factories in Asia. The town also has a connection to Esther John, a Pakistani Christian woman who became a martyr and is now commemorated in Westminster Abbey.
