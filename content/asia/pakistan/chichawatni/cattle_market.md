One of the larger cattle markets in Punjab operates in Chichawatni, with trade in buffalo particularly active around religious holidays — Eid al-Adha turns the market into one of the most animated events in the region, as buyers from across the district come to purchase animals for sacrifice. The market operates year-round but reaches its greatest intensity in the weeks before the festival.

Buffalo are the dominant livestock here; the Sahiwal district breed, prized for its milk yield, is traded widely across Pakistan. The market atmosphere — animals, buyers, sellers, the noise and dust and negotiation — gives a direct window into the agricultural economy that has driven this part of Punjab for centuries. It's not a curated tourist experience in any way, but for anyone interested in rural Pakistan's working life, it is completely genuine.
