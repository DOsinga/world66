---
title: "Getting There"
type: section
---

Chichawatni sits on the Grand Trunk Road about 45 km south of Sahiwal and roughly 180 km from Lahore. The railway station on the Lahore–Karachi main line connects it to both cities with regular services. Buses and wagons run frequently along the GT Road corridor in both directions.

From Chichawatni, shared transport to Harappa (around 20 km) can be arranged at the bus stand; the journey takes 30–40 minutes.
