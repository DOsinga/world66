---
title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Chichawatni's appeal is modest and mostly for those passing through on the Grand Trunk Road or heading to nearby Harappa. The Chichawatni Forest, one of Pakistan's largest planted forests, is the main local attraction — a surprisingly green retreat in this flat agricultural plain. The cattle market operates with particular intensity around religious holidays.

Most visitors use Chichawatni as a transit stop rather than a destination in itself. The town is about 20 km from Harappa, making it a possible base for visiting that Indus Valley site.
