The Kohat Tunnel cuts almost two kilometres under the Khigana mountains, replacing what was previously a slow and dangerous climb up the pass. It opened in 2003 with Japanese funding and is the only modern road tunnel in this part of the country.

For travellers between [Peshawar](/asia/pakistan/peshawar) and [Kohat](/asia/pakistan/kohat), it is essentially the gateway into the Darra Adam Khel area. The southern portal emerges within easy walking distance of the first gun workshops. A sequence of military checkpoints around both portals reflects the long-standing security concerns of the area.
