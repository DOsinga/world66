---

D.G. Khan is predominantly Balochi in culture despite sitting within Punjab province. The major tribes — Mazari, Leghari, Khosa, Rind, Qaisrani, Lakani, and Umrani — are Baloch in origin and maintain strong tribal identities and land-based political structures. Dera (meaning "settlement") in the city's name itself reflects the Baloch pattern of naming places after founding clan leaders.

Punjabi is the most widely spoken everyday language, but Balochi and Saraiki are both common. The area is predominantly Sunni Muslim, with Sufi traditions playing an important role — the shrines of local saints attract large annual gatherings. The population of the wider district is largely rural and agricultural, with the city itself a commercial and administrative hub.
