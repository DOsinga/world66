---
title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

D.G. Khan is not a tourist city, and most visitors are in transit between Punjab and Balochistan or conducting business. The main sights are the city's busy bazaars, the DG Khan Zoo on the edge of the city, and the drive west into the Sulaiman foothills. For anyone with more time, the Taunsa Barrage to the north is worth the trip, and Taunsa Sharif's Sufi shrine draws vast crowds during the annual urs festival.
