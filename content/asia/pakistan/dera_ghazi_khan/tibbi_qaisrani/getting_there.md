---
title: "Getting There"
type: section
---

Tibbi Qaisrani lies on the road network linking [Dera Ghazi Khan](/asia/pakistan/dera_ghazi_khan) (about 80 km south) with Taunsa Sharif and the smaller towns of the western Punjab plain. Most travellers arrive by shared van or local bus from D.G. Khan; private cars and taxis are easy to arrange in either direction.

The nearest railway station and airport are both in Dera Ghazi Khan, with onward connections to Multan, Sukkur and Karachi. There is no organised tourist transport — this is a place you reach because you are already in the region.
