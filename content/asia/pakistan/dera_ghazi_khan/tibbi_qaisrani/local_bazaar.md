The small bazaar in the centre of Tibbi Qaisrani is the heart of village life. Wheat, sugarcane and seasonal vegetables come down from the surrounding farms, and basic household goods, fabric, and agricultural tools are sold from a row of low shopfronts.

It is not a tourist market in any sense, but it is the place to feel the rhythm of a Saraiki-Baloch farming town. Mornings are busiest, and Friday afternoons are quiet around prayer time.
