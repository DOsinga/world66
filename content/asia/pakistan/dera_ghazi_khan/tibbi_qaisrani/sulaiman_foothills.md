A few kilometres west of Tibbi Qaisrani the irrigated farmland ends abruptly and the dry rocky foothills of the Sulaiman Range begin. The transition is striking — green fields one moment, dust and scrub the next.

The hills are tribal land and not a place to wander unannounced, but the view back across the plain at sunset, with the Indus visible in the haze, is the best thing about being in Tibbi Qaisrani. Ask locally before heading more than a short distance off the road.
