title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Tibbi Qaisrani is a farming union council on the western edge of the Indus plain, not a tourist destination. What it offers is the texture of rural southern Punjab: irrigated fields backed by the dry foothills of the Sulaiman Range, weekly cattle markets, and an unhurried daily life shaped by the Qaisrani Baloch tribe whose name the town carries.

For visitors passing through, the highlights are practical rather than monumental — the bazaars, the surrounding countryside, and the route up into the hills. Bring water and modest clothing, and expect to be invited for tea more often than you can accept.
