---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

Southern Punjab is hot. Summers between May and September are brutal — daytime temperatures regularly exceed 45°C, and the monsoon brings sudden heavy storms in July and August. Travel in this period is uncomfortable and roads can flood.

The best months are November to February, when daytime temperatures are pleasant (15-25°C) and the fields are at their greenest after the second cropping. Nights can be surprisingly cold in January. March and October are reasonable shoulder months if you can tolerate some heat.
