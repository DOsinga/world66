Pakistan is one of the great field hockey nations, and Faisalabad has its own dedicated hockey stadium. The sport has deep roots in Punjab, and the stadium hosts national and sometimes international fixtures throughout the year. If you happen to be in the city during a match — check local listings — attending is a lively experience; Pakistani hockey crowds are engaged and the game is fast.

The stadium sits in the eastern part of the city near the Madina Town area. Outside match days it is quieter, but the surrounding streets typically have sporting goods shops and food stalls that give a sense of the neighbourhood.
