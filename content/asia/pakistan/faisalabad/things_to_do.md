---
title: "Things to Do in Faisalabad"
type: section
---

Faisalabad's main draw is its historic bazaar district around the clock tower — a living example of British colonial planning that turned into something distinctly subcontinental. Beyond that, the city has parks, a cricket stadium, and a handful of institutions worth visiting. It is not a major tourist destination, but travellers passing through will find plenty of local life to observe.
