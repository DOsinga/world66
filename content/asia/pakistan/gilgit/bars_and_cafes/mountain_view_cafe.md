One of the more traveller-oriented cafes in Gilgit, with a covered terrace that catches the afternoon light and a reliable supply of green tea, qahwa, and fresh juice. The menu runs to sandwiches and snacks alongside the usual tea options. A useful place to sit between buses or flights, and one of the few spots in town where men and women can sit together without difficulty. The staff speak enough English to navigate an order without gestures.
