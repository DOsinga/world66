One of several restaurants in the bazaar area that serves both standard Pakistani fare and dishes specific to the region. The chapshuro here — thick flatbread stuffed with spiced minced meat — is reliably good and comes straight off the griddle. The daal and rice are solid, the naan excellent. A straightforward place with a local clientele and decent turnover, which is the best indicator of quality in Gilgit's restaurant scene.
