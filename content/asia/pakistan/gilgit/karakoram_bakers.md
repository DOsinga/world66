A bakery and snack shop on the main bazaar strip that has become a default meeting point for travellers passing through Gilgit. Fresh bread, samosas, and pastries come out through the day. The seating area inside is basic but clean, and it is a reasonable place to sit with tea and plan the next leg of a journey. The bakers open early, which makes it useful for travellers catching the morning NATCO bus or the first flight to Islamabad.
