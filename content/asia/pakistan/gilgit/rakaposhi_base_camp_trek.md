  the world: the south face rises approximately 5,800 metres from the Hunza Valley floor

  The base camp trek puts you on the glacier at the foot of this face — close enough that 
  the scale becomes almost incomprehensible.

The trek to Rakaposhi Base Camp is one of the most accessible serious mountain walks from Gilgit. The trailhead is at Minapin village, a short drive north of Gilgit on the Karakoram Highway, and the walk to base camp takes two to three days at a comfortable pace. The route climbs through forest and moraines to a high camp on the glacier with direct views of Rakaposhi's south face.

This is a walk-in trekking route — no technical climbing gear required — though the altitude (base camp sits around 3,700 metres) means acclimatisation days in Gilgit are worthwhile before heading up. Porters are available at Minapin and are a sensible investment: the loads are heavy and local knowledge of conditions is valuable. The trek can be combined with a visit to Hunza Valley, making a logical multi-day loop from Gilgit.
