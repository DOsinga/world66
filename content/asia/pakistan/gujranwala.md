Gujranwala is Pakistan's fifth-largest city, an industrial centre in Punjab with a population of well over a million. It is known across the country as the "City of Wrestlers" — traditional Punjabi [wrestling (pehlwani)](/asia/pakistan/gujranwala/akhara_visit) has been a local passion for generations, and matches at [Sheranwala Bagh](/asia/pakistan/gujranwala/sheranwala_bagh) draw big crowds.

The city's other claim to fame is food. Gujranwala is a serious eating town: tikkas, kebabs, and nan with chai are the daily staples, and the street food stalls lining the main bazaars serve some of the best in Punjab. The [haveli that was Maharaja Ranjit Singh's birthplace](/asia/pakistan/gujranwala/haveli_ranjit_singh) is the main historical sight, with the [Sheranwala Bagh Baradari](/asia/pakistan/gujranwala/sheranwala_baradari) and a handful of Sikh-era and pre-Partition Jain buildings clustered nearby.

The old city is compact enough to cover on foot in half a day. The [bazaars along GT Road](/asia/pakistan/gujranwala/gt_road_bazaar) and around the [Estcourt Clock Tower](/asia/pakistan/gujranwala/clock_tower) are the heart of the working city — cloth, cutlery (Gujranwala has been a metalworking centre for over a century), and food stalls — and well worth wandering, particularly in the late afternoon.
