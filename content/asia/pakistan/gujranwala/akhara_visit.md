Pehlwani — traditional South Asian wrestling, fought in a clay pit called an akhara — is Gujranwala's defining sport. The city's nickname, 'City of Wrestlers', is taken seriously: a generation of national champions has trained here, and the akharas in and around Sheranwala Bagh still see hundreds of young men in training every morning.

Visitors are usually welcome to watch. Mornings (roughly 6–9am) are training; weekend afternoons sometimes have organised matches. Dress modestly, ask before taking photos of wrestlers, and tip the coach if you are invited to stay.
