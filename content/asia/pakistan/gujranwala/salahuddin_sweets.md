A long-standing mithai shop near Sheranwala Bagh, run by the same family for over fifty years. The case is full of the standard Punjabi sweets — barfi, gulab jamun, jalebi, rasgulla — but the rasmalai and the milk-based barfi are the things to try. A box makes a good gift if you are heading on to Lahore or Sialkot.
