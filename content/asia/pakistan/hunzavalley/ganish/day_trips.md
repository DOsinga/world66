---
title: "Day Trips"
type: section
---

Ganish is an excellent base for exploring the central Hunza Valley, with several major sights reachable in under an hour.

**Baltit Fort** sits above Karimabad, a 20–30 minute walk or short drive uphill. The fort is the best-preserved in Hunza and houses exhibits on the Mir's court and valley history. Admission includes a guided tour.

**Altit Fort**, the oldest fort in Hunza at over 900 years, is located about 5 kilometres south of Karimabad. The restored fort perches dramatically above the Hunza River gorge. The nearby Altit village is also worth exploring for its traditional architecture.

**Attabad Lake** is a turquoise glacial lake about 25 kilometres west of Ganish, formed by a massive 2010 landslide that blocked the Hunza River. Boat trips on the lake are a popular outing and the drive along the KKH offers dramatic scenery.

**Eagle's Nest** is a viewpoint above Duikar village, reachable by jeep in about 45 minutes from Ganish. It offers arguably the finest panoramic view of the Hunza Valley, with Rakaposhi, Ultar Sar, Lady Finger, and Bojahagur Duanasir all visible on a clear day.
