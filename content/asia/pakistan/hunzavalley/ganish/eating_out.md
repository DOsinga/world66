---
title: "Eating Out"
type: section
---

Ganish itself has no dedicated restaurants. The village is a short walk or drive from Karimabad, where a good range of cafes and restaurants lines the main bazaar. Many serve local Hunza dishes including chapshuro (meat-filled flatbread), diram phitti, and fresh apricot-based drinks.

For a light meal or tea after touring the village, some guesthouses in the area offer simple food. Otherwise, head up the hill to Karimabad for more options.
