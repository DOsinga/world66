---
title: "Getting Around"
type: section
---

Ganish is a compact village best explored entirely on foot. The historic core of Ganish Khun — the oldest quarter — is a maze of narrow stone lanes that no vehicle can enter. Walking the alleys at your own pace is the only way to appreciate the carved wooden mosques, ancient watchtowers, and traditional houses at close range.

Sturdy footwear is useful as many paths are uneven and steeply stepped. Allow at least two to three hours to explore the village properly, including time to find the main carved mosques and the sacred rocks with pre-Islamic inscriptions on the outskirts.

For the wider Hunza Valley — Altit Fort, Baltit Fort, Eagle's Nest, or the Attabad Lake area — you will need a vehicle. Jeeps with drivers can be hired in Karimabad for half-day and full-day excursions.
