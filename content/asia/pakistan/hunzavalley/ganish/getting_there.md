---
title: "Getting There"
type: section
---

Ganish sits directly on the Karakoram Highway (KKH), roughly 2 kilometres below Karimabad. The village is visible from the main road and most vehicles travelling between Gilgit and Karimabad pass right through it.

From Karimabad, Ganish can be reached on foot in about 20 to 30 minutes by descending the stepped path or road down to the highway. Shared minivans and Suzukis running between Aliabad and Karimabad stop near the village. Private jeep hire is easy to arrange from Karimabad for around 300–500 PKR.

From Gilgit, direct vans and coasters to Karimabad/Hunza leave throughout the morning from the Gilgit bus stand and pass through Ganish. The journey takes around two to three hours depending on road conditions.
