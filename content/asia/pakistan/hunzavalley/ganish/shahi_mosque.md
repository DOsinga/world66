The Shahi Mosque (Royal Mosque) of Ganish is one of the oldest surviving mosques in the Hunza region, with origins believed to date to the 15th or 16th century. The structure is built in the vernacular wooden architecture of the valley — a style that predates the introduction of cement and brick and adapted well to the seismic conditions of the Karakoram.

The interior woodwork — carved columns, decorative brackets, and the mihrab — is exceptionally fine, the product of craftsmen working in a tradition that stretched back centuries. Non-Muslims are generally welcomed to look inside outside prayer times, but it is worth asking locally before entering. The mosque remains an active place of worship for the village community.
