---

Ganish and the immediately surrounding area hold an unusually dense concentration of historical monuments for a place this size. The [Ganish Khun historic quarter](/asia/pakistan/hunzavalley/ganish/ganish_khun) is the starting point — but nearby [Altit Fort](/asia/pakistan/hunzavalley/ganish/altit_fort) and [Baltit Fort](/asia/pakistan/hunzavalley/ganish/baltit_fort) are both within a short walk or drive and are unmissable in the Hunza Valley.
