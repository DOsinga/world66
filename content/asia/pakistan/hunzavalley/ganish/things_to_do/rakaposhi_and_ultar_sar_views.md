---
title: "Rakaposhi and Ultar Sar Views"
type: poi
---

Ganish sits at one of the finest viewpoints in the central Hunza Valley for the two peaks that dominate the skyline: Rakaposhi (7,788m) to the south and Ultar Sar (7,388m) to the northeast above Karimabad. From the upper lanes of the village and from the small open areas near the watchtowers, both summits are visible on clear mornings before the clouds build.

Rakaposhi's south face — one of the most dramatic mountain walls in the Karakoram — rises almost 6,000 metres above the Hunza Valley floor in an unbroken sweep. The mountain is visible from a wide stretch of the KKH but Ganish, slightly elevated above the highway, offers a particularly unobstructed angle. Early morning is best before haze and cloud develop. In spring the snow-covered peak above a foreground of white and pink apricot blossom is one of the classic images of northern Pakistan.

Ultar Sar's jagged ridgeline is visible directly above the hillside where Karimabad sits. Its glaciers feed the irrigation channels that have sustained Hunza agriculture for generations. The juxtaposition of ancient stone village architecture, terraced orchards, and these vast mountain walls gives Ganish its distinctive character and makes even a short visit memorable.
