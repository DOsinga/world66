---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

The best time to visit Ganish is between April and October, when the Karakoram Highway is fully open and the weather is mild. Spring (late March to May) is particularly striking when the apricot orchards surrounding the village burst into blossom — the contrast of white and pink flowers against the snow-dusted mountains is spectacular and attracts photographers from across Pakistan.

Summer (June to August) brings warm days and the clearest mountain views, with long daylight hours ideal for exploring the village and its surroundings. September and October offer golden harvest colours as the apricot and walnut trees ripen, and the crowds thin considerably after the summer peak.

Winter (November to March) is cold and many guesthouses close in Karimabad, but the village itself can be visited if roads are passable. Snowfall sometimes blocks the KKH north of Gilgit.
