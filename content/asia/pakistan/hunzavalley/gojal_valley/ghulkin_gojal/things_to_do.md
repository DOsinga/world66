type: section
---

Ghulkin's main draws are its glacier and the surrounding high-altitude landscapes. The [Ghulkin Glacier](/asia/pakistan/hunzavalley/gojal_valley/ghulkin_gojal/ghulkin_glacier) is walkable in an hour from the village, and experienced trekkers can continue on to [Borith Lake](/asia/pakistan/hunzavalley/gojal_valley/ghulkin_gojal/borith_lake) and across to the Passu Glacier beyond. The village itself — with its circular layout and traditional stone houses — rewards quiet exploration on foot.
