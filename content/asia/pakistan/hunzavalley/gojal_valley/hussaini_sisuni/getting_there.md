---
title: Getting There
type: section
---

Hussaini sits directly on the Karakoram Highway, about 145 km north of Gilgit and 45 km from Karimabad. Any KKH bus between Gilgit and Sost will pass through; flag one down at Karimabad or Aliabad and ask for Hussaini or Passu. Private jeep or shared van is the more reliable option from Karimabad — about an hour and a half each way.

The suspension bridge is a fifteen-minute walk down a track from the highway. Drivers usually wait at the trailhead.
