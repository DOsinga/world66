---
title: When to Go
type: section
---

**Late May through October** is the only practical season — the Khunjerab Pass is officially open 1 May to 30 November, but in practice it can close earlier or later depending on snow. **June to September** is best for travellers, with reliable conditions on both sides of the border.

Even in midsummer, nights at Sost can drop close to freezing. **Winter** is severe: temperatures below –20°C, daily snowfall, and the highway often impassable from Sost north. The local Wakhi population mostly winters further down the valley in warmer places like Gulmit and Karimabad.
