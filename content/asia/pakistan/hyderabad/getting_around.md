---
title: "Getting Around"
type: section
---

Within the city, auto-rickshaws and motorcycle rickshaws are the main option for short distances. The old city and bazaar area is compact enough to explore on foot, though the heat makes walking uncomfortable for much of the year. 

For the Jamshoro campus (Institute of Sindhology), the easiest option is a rickshaw to the bridge, then local transport across. The Tombs of the Talpur Mirs, about 2 km north of the old city, require a rickshaw or taxi.
