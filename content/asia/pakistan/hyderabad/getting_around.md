---
title: Getting Around
type: section
---

Hyderabad's main sights are spread across different parts of the city. Rickshaws and tongas (horse-drawn carriages) cover the old city and the areas around the fort and bazaars. Shared wagons run along the main roads. For the Talpur Mir tombs and Kalhora complex north of the centre, a rickshaw negotiated for a few hours is the most convenient option.

To reach the University of Sindh campus and Institute of Sindhology in Jamshoro, take a miniwagon from the main road toward the Kotri Bridge, cross the bridge, and walk or take another vehicle to the campus (about 1.5 km). The crossing gives good views of the Indus.
