---
title: "Getting There"
type: section
---

Hyderabad is connected to Karachi (164 km south) and the rest of Pakistan by both the Indus Highway and the older route through the Sindhi plains. Karachi is around two hours by road or by train on the Karachi–Lahore main line, with frequent services. Coaches and minibuses from Karachi's Sohrab Goth bus terminal depart regularly.

Hyderabad Junction railway station is a major hub on the Karachi–Lahore line. Trains to Lahore, Quetta, and Peshawar pass through here.
