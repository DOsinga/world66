The Institute of Sindhology at the University of Sindh in Jamshoro, just across the river from Hyderabad, holds the most comprehensive collection of material on Sindhi history, language, music, and cultural heritage in the world. The museum displays include ancient manuscripts in the Sindhi script, traditional musical instruments, textiles, and artefacts tracing the region's history from the Indus Valley Civilisation through the Talpur period to the modern era.

Getting there requires crossing the Indus by bridge and then about 1.5 km of walking or a short rickshaw ride from the Jamshoro road. The university campus is pleasant and the institute's library is one of the most important repositories of Sindhi language material anywhere. It's an academic rather than a tourist attraction, but for those with a genuine interest in the history and culture of Sindh it is hard to match.
