A historic haveli — a traditional courtyard mansion — that has been converted into a museum. The Mukhi House was once the residence of a prominent Hindu merchant family that lived in Hyderabad before Partition and is a fine example of the mercantile architecture that defined the old city's prosperous quarters. The building's interior features decorative plasterwork, carved wooden screens, and a sequence of rooms organised around an internal courtyard that demonstrates how wealthy Sindhi families organised their domestic spaces.

The museum inside displays objects related to Sindhi cultural history, including traditional textiles, household implements, and documents. It is one of the better-preserved examples of pre-Partition domestic architecture in Hyderabad and worth visiting for the building itself as much as the exhibits. Opening hours can be irregular — check locally before visiting.
