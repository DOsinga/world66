The main historic market of Hyderabad, in the heart of the old city. The Shahi Bazaar — "Royal Market" — has been Hyderabad's commercial centre since the Kalhora era and remains the place to buy the goods for which Sindh is famous: **Sindhi ajrak** (the block-printed indigo and red fabric), embroidered shawls, lacquerware, glass bangles, and the locally made rilli patchwork quilts. The bangle market in particular is famous across Pakistan — Hyderabad produces the highest-quality glass bangles in the country, in hundreds of colours and patterns.

The bazaar is best explored on foot in the morning, before the heat and the crowds peak. The surrounding lanes contain small workshops where artisans still produce traditional crafts by hand. It's genuinely practical shopping, not a tourist market: prices are negotiable and local.
