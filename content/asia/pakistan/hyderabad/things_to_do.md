title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Hyderabad rewards explorers who venture into its older quarters. The **Hyderabad Fort**, built in 1766 by the Kalhora rulers, sits above the old city and is the best starting point. The **badgirs** — wind-catching towers that crown the rooftops of old houses — are one of the most distinctive features of the Sindhi urban landscape, and Hyderabad has some of the finest surviving examples. The **Tombs of the Talpur Mirs** north of the city are elaborate tiled mausoleums from the dynasty that ruled Sindh from 1784 until the British conquest of 1843.

The city's main bazaars sell the textiles, lacquerware, and bangles for which Hyderabad is known across Pakistan. The Sindhi ajrak — a block-printed cloth with geometric patterns in deep red and indigo — is made here and is the most recognisable souvenir of the region.
