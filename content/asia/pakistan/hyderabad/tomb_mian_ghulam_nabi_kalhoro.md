The Tomb of Mian Ghulam Nabi Kalhoro is a well-preserved Kalhora-period tomb decorated with Sindhi tilework. The original dome has been lost and replaced by a flat roof, but the structure still gives a clear sense of the dynasty that ruled Sindh from the early 18th century.

It is close to several other Hyderabad historic sights, so it is best visited as part of a short circuit of the Kalhora and Talpur tombs, the old city, and Hyderabad Fort.
