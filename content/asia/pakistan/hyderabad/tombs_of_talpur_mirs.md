A complex of tombs north of Hyderabad city holding the remains of the Talpur Mirs, the dynasty that ruled Sindh from 1784 to 1843. The Talpurs were known as capable administrators and patrons of architecture; these tombs, built in the distinctive Sindhi style, are tiled with intricate geometric and floral patterns in glazed terracotta and coloured kashi tile work that are among the finest surviving examples of this regional decorative tradition.

The main tombs are enclosed within a walled complex and include the mausoleums of several Mir rulers and their family members. The quality of the tilework varies — some panels are exceptionally detailed and well-preserved, while others have been damaged or replaced by mediocre modern restoration. The complex is best visited in the cooler morning hours. A guide from the old city can navigate you here and provide context on the individual rulers.
