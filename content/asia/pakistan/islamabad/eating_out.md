title: Eating Out
type: section
---

Islamabad eats well for a planned city. The traditional cluster is **Melody Food Street** in G-6 and the **Jinnah Super Market** restaurants in F-7, where mid-range Pakistani, Mughlai and Afghan kitchens run side by side. **Kohsar Market** in F-6 and **Saidpur Village** under the Margalla Hills have the higher-end restaurants. **Centaurus Mall** in F-8 is the main international-chain food court.
