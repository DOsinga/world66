F-6 is the diplomatic and old-money sector — wide streets of low villas, mature pines, embassies on every other block. Its commercial centre is the **Kohsar Market**, which has the city's most upmarket coffee shops and the principal source of imported groceries (the Hyperstar supermarket). Lots of leafy walking; the most pleasant residential sector to wander.

Several major embassies are here — Britain, the United States, France — which means more police checkpoints than the rest of the city. Carry your passport.
