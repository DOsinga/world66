F-7 is the most central of the well-off residential sectors — a 2 km square laid out between the diplomatic enclave and the Margalla Hills. Its commercial heart is the **Jinnah Super Market**, which has been the city's main shopping and eating centre since the 1970s. Walk-able streets, mature trees and a long row of mid-range restaurants make it the easiest sector for a visitor to spend time in.

The British and US embassies are immediately to the south in F-6 and the trails into the Margalla Hills start at the north edge. The sector also contains the Gol Market — a small circular plaza with cafes — and a long string of mid-range and upmarket shops.
