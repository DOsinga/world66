The National Art Gallery is Islamabad's main public art museum, housed in a modern building near the Red Zone. Its collection covers hundreds of works by Pakistani artists, from painting and sculpture to contemporary pieces, and gives a useful introduction to the country's art scene.

It is a good cultural stop when you are already visiting central Islamabad's civic sights. The building is air-conditioned and easy to combine with nearby government and monument areas.
