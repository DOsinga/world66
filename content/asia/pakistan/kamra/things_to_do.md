---
title: Things to Do near Kamra
type: section
---

Kamra itself has little to offer the passing traveller beyond roadside dhabas and the logistics of moving between Islamabad and Peshawar. The Pakistan Aeronautical Complex dominates the town and most of the area is functionally closed to casual visitors. The value here is in the surrounding region — Attock Fort and the Indus crossing to the west are genuinely worth a stop.
