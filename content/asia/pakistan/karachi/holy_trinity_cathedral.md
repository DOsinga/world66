Holy Trinity Cathedral is one of Karachi's important colonial-era churches, built for the British garrison and later serving as the cathedral of the Church of Pakistan's Karachi diocese. Its stone tower once had a practical maritime role too, acting as a visible landmark and signal point before the modern city swallowed the old approaches to the harbour.

The cathedral is worth seeking out because it adds another layer to Karachi's religious and architectural map. Around Zainab Market and Fatima Jinnah Road, the city can feel dense and impatient; inside the compound, the old church, memorials, and proportions of the building offer a quieter view of 19th-century Karachi.
