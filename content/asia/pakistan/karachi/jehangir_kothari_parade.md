Jehangir Kothari Parade is a graceful remnant of colonial Karachi, built as a promenade and pavilion above Clifton. Its stone arcade and terrace now sit within the larger Bagh Ibne Qasim area, but they still give the seafront a touch of old civic ceremony.

Come for the architecture and the sense of Karachi looking toward the Arabian Sea. It works well with a Clifton walk, though the experience depends heavily on heat, traffic, and how well the surrounding park is being maintained.
