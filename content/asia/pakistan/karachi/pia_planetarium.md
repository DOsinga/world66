PIA Planetarium is a small science attraction with regular shows about the solar system and other astronomical subjects. English-language shows are usually more detailed, while Urdu shows make up most of the regular programme.

The site also has an aviation angle: guided visits may include a look inside an aircraft, including the cockpit and passenger areas. It is a modest stop, but a useful family option away from Karachi's better-known historic sights.
