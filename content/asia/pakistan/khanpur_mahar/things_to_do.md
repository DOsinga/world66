---

Khanpur Mahar is a small market town and not a tourist destination in any sense. The interest is mainly agricultural — the fields, the sugarcane mills, the cattle markets — and the nearby Indus, which has shaped life in this part of Sindh for thousands of years. Travellers passing through tend to be on their way north to Punjab or south to Sukkur.
