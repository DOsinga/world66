Darra Adam Khel is a small town in the Kohat Pass area, historically part of the tribal Federally Administered Tribal Areas, where the dominant industry for well over a century has been the manufacture of firearms. The craftsmen of Darra — mostly from the Adam Khel Afridi tribe — learned to copy weapons by hand, without factory machinery, producing everything from bolt-action rifles to pistols to shotguns and, at the trade's peak, credible copies of Kalashnikovs, Uzis, and anti-aircraft guns. The main street of the bazaar was once lined with workshops where you could watch a weapon being assembled from scratch and buy it on the spot.

The government cracked down significantly after 2001, and the open trade that made Darra famous is largely over. Access for foreigners is restricted and a permit from the relevant authorities is required — this is not a place you can simply drive to and walk around. Check current conditions and permit requirements very carefully before planning a visit, and do not attempt to enter without the necessary permissions. That said, Darra Adam Khel remains a remarkable place for those who can get there legally: the workshops are still operating, the craft knowledge is still being passed down, and the bazaar retains the atmosphere of a place that has run on its own terms for a very long time.
