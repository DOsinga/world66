---
title: Bars and Cafes
type: section
---

Pakistan is officially a dry country, and Lahore has no public bars in the European sense. Alcohol is served only at international-class hotels (the Pearl Continental, Avari, and a few others) to non-Muslim residents with permits, foreign passport holders, and registered guests. Outside those bars, the city's drinking culture is largely about tea.

The cafe scene is strong. **Pak Tea House** on the Mall has been the city's literary cafe since 1940; **Coffee Tea & Co.** is a more contemporary chain. M.M. Alam Road in Gulberg has the best concentration of modern cafes — espresso bars, dessert spots, late-night shisha lounges (where smoking is legal but increasingly discouraged).
