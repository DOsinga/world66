Gulberg is the upmarket commercial district south of the Mall, laid out in the 1950s and now the city's modern centre of gravity. The main artery is **M.M. Alam Road** (Food Street), a four-kilometre strip of high-end restaurants, cafes, and salons; **Liberty Market**, at its eastern end, is one of Lahore's main shopping areas.

Most of Lahore's better hotels, embassies, and contemporary art galleries cluster here. It is also where most well-off Lahoris live, in walled bungalows behind security gates.
