The Islamic Summit Minar is the obelisk at Charing Cross, built to mark the 1974 Organisation of Islamic Cooperation summit held in Lahore. The monument is easy to pair with a walk along The Mall, and the small museum-library beneath the roundabout gives useful context on the summit and its place in Pakistan's diplomatic history.
