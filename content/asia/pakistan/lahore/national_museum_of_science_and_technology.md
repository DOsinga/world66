Lahore's National Museum of Science and Technology is a practical, slightly old-school museum aimed at making physics, engineering, and natural science tangible. The displays are not glossy, but the best exhibits reward patient visitors who like working models, demonstrations, and the educational side of the city.

It sits near the older academic heart of Lahore, so it works well as a short stop with Government College University, the Mall, or the Lahore Museum. Families get the most from it, but curious adults may enjoy the contrast with Lahore's Mughal and colonial set pieces.
