Shakir Ali Museum preserves the former home and studio world of one of Pakistan's important modern painters. Lahore's great monuments are Mughal and colonial, but this small museum points to the city's twentieth-century art life and the teachers who shaped it.

It is a specialist stop, best for travellers with an interest in art, design, or Lahore beyond the Walled City. The scale is domestic rather than institutional, which suits the subject: you are not just looking at works, but at the memory of a working artist's house.
