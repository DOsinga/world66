Yum is the long-running flagship of M.M. Alam Road's Chinese-Pakistani fusion scene — a large, busy dining room serving a hybrid menu of Chinese standards (chilli chicken, hot-and-sour soup, manchurian) cooked with a definite Pakistani accent. The portions are large, the service is attentive, and the prices are reasonable for what is on the table.

Comfortable for vegetarians, who otherwise struggle in Lahore. Booking is wise on weekend evenings.
