Larkana is the main city of northern Sindh, a hot, dusty, agricultural centre surrounded by rice fields and threaded into both ancient and modern Pakistani history. It is not polished, and it is not an easy sightseeing city, but it has weight: the Indus Valley ruins of [Moenjodaro](/asia/pakistan/moenjodaro) lie to the south, while the Bhutto family mausoleum at Garhi Khuda Bakhsh pulls political pilgrims from across the country.

The city itself was formerly known as Chandka and grew into a regional hub under British rule. Today its streets are busy with bazaars, students, court offices, and traffic heading toward the archaeological mounds and rural shrines around the district. This is a place to visit with patience, local advice, and a clear plan rather than a casual wander.

The strongest day is split between Larkana's own anchors and the surrounding district: [Bhutto Family Mausoleum](/asia/pakistan/larkana/bhutto_family_mausoleum), [Jhukar Jo Daro](/asia/pakistan/larkana/jhukar_jo_daro), and the calmer civic space of [Jinnah Bagh](/asia/pakistan/larkana/jinnah_bagh). Add Moenjodaro if you have not already seen it; that site is the headline reason many travellers come this far into Sindh.
