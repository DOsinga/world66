The Bhutto Family Mausoleum at Garhi Khuda Bakhsh is the most important modern political site near Larkana. Zulfikar Ali Bhutto, Benazir Bhutto, and other members of the family are buried here under a large white-domed structure that feels part shrine, part national memorial.

It draws visitors year-round, with especially large gatherings around death anniversaries and political commemorations. Even if you are not following Pakistani politics closely, the place explains why Larkana carries a national meaning far beyond its size.
