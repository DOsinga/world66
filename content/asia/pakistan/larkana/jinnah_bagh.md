Jinnah Bagh is Larkana's central public park, close to the railway station and main city streets. It is not a formal garden on the scale of Lahore's great parks, but it is useful for understanding the city's daily rhythm.

Come toward evening, when the heat eases and families start to use the paths and lawns. For most travellers it is a short stop, not a destination by itself.
