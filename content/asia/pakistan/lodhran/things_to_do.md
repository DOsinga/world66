title: Things to Do
type: section
---

Lodhran is not a tourist destination in the conventional sense. It's a working agricultural town and district headquarters, and most visitors passing through are here for business or in transit between Multan and Bahawalpur.

The town bazaar is the main point of activity — a busy commercial street where you can watch daily life in a small Pakistani market town unfold. The district is known for producing cotton, wheat, and handicrafts including embroidered textiles, which are sold locally.

The flat Punjab landscape around Lodhran has its own quiet appeal, particularly around the Sutlej River, which flows through the district. Early mornings along the riverbank are peaceful.
