A scholarly but accessible biography of the saint whose shrine dominates the fort mound of Multan and who gave the city its character as a Sufi centre. The book traces Bahauddin Zakariya's training in Baghdad under Shihab-ud-Din Suhrawardi, his decision to settle in Multan in the 1220s, his complicated relationship with the early Delhi sultans, and the institutional Suhrawardiyya tradition that he founded and that survives in the shrine to this day.

It is the most comprehensive English-language account of the man and a useful companion to a serious visit to the shrines.
