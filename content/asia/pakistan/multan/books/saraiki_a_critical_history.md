**Saraiki** is the regional language of southern Punjab and the everyday speech of Multan — distinct from Punjabi proper, with its own poetry tradition built on the mystical *kafi* form perfected by Khwaja Ghulam Farid in the nineteenth century. Christopher Shackle's slim academic study is the best introduction in English, with translations of the major poets and a clear sketch of the cultural geography around Multan, Bahawalpur and Dera Ghazi Khan.

It will not appeal to everyone, but for anyone who finds themselves listening to the qawwali singers at the Shah Shams or Bahauddin Zakariya shrines and wondering what language the audience is responding to, this is the answer.
