**Chowk Bazaar** is the central crossroads of the walled city, where the streets from Bohar Gate, Haram Gate and Delhi Gate meet under the shadow of the **Wali Muhammad Khan Mosque** and the **Phool Hattan Wali Mosque**. It is the densest, oldest and most photogenic part of the bazaar district — a mass of brass shops, sweet shops, fabric merchants, courier stalls and rickshaws, threaded with overhead cables and shaded by improvised awnings.

This is where you buy **Multani khussa** (the city's distinctive curled-toe leather slippers) and the famous blue-and-white **Multani pottery**, both still made by hand in small workshops a few streets back. Allow plenty of time and expect to get lost.
