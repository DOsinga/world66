---
title: "Getting There"
type: section
---

**Multan International Airport** sits about five kilometres west of the city and handles domestic flights from Karachi, Lahore and Islamabad as well as direct international flights to **Dubai**, **Sharjah**, **Doha**, **Jeddah**, **Riyadh** and a handful of other Gulf destinations. Pakistan International Airlines, Air Sial and FlyDubai are the main carriers. A taxi from the airport into the Cantonment costs around Rs 800–1000.

By **rail**, the **Khanewal–Multan line** runs the principal Karachi–Peshawar service via Multan Cantonment and Multan City stations. The most useful train for visitors is the **Tezgam Express** (Karachi–Rawalpindi), which stops at Multan Cantt at convenient times in both directions, and the daytime **Greenline** to Lahore (four hours). By road, the **M-4 motorway** has made Lahore a four-hour drive; **Karachi** is a hard 14-hour run on the M-5. **Daewoo Express coaches** run hourly to most major cities.
