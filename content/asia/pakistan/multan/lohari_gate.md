The **Lohari Gate** ("ironworkers' gate") stands at the north-western corner of the walled city, named after the ironsmiths whose shops once clustered immediately outside it. The gate itself is a plain brick arch repaired many times over the past three centuries, but the lane through it leads directly to the **Shah Yousaf Gardezi Mohallah** and on to the fort mound. The ironsmiths are mostly gone, replaced by retailers of agricultural hardware and machine parts.
