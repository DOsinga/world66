The **Phool Hattan Wali Mosque** ("mosque of the flower-sellers' lane") sits in the heart of Chowk Bazaar, named after the bazaar of flower sellers that once surrounded it. It is small, with a single low dome, a narrow tiled façade and a courtyard barely large enough for fifty worshippers, but the proportions are perfect and the early eighteenth-century blue-and-white **kashi-kari** is some of the finest surviving in the old city.

The mosque is easy to miss — set back behind a row of brassware shops — but it is one of the more atmospheric stops on a walk through the bazaar.
