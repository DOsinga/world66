The **Pak Gate** ("pure gate") is the smallest and southernmost of Multan's six surviving city gates, on Alang Road between Haram Gate and the Bloody Bastion. It was traditionally the route taken by the cattle and milk traders into the walled city, and the lane that runs inward from it still contains a small dairy bazaar and a cluster of old **havelis** with carved wooden balconies.
