title: "Shopping"

---

Multan has been a craft town for a thousand years and you can still buy the things it has always been famous for. The two essential local crafts are **Multani blue pottery** — vases, bowls and tiles in cobalt, turquoise and white, fired in the same kilns that supplied the city's mosque tile-work — and **Multani khussa**: brightly embroidered, sharp-pointed leather slippers that range from the everyday plain to extravagant wedding pairs with mirror-work and gold thread.

Both are made in small workshops in and around **Chowk Bazaar** and **Hussain Agahi**; buy direct from the maker where you can, or from the government **PAK Crafts** showroom on Abdali Road, which has fair prices and reliable quality. **Sohan halwa** in tins makes the standard edible souvenir. For more workaday shopping the **Cantt Bazaar** along Abdali Road is the modern department-store area, and the air-conditioned **Mall of Multan** has the usual international brands.
