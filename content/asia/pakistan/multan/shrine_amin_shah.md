The **Shrine of Muhammad Amin Shah Sani** is a small nineteenth-century tomb within the walled enclosure of the Bahauddin Zakariya shrine complex, on the upper terrace of the fort mound. Amin Shah was a descendant of the great Suhrawardiyya master and is venerated locally as a minor saint. The tomb is a single domed chamber with restrained blue tilework, easy to overlook on the way to the main shrines but worth a glance.
