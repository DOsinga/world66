The bazaar Jain temple stands in Nagarparkar town itself, which makes it the easiest monument to see before heading into the surrounding hills. Its shikhara, carved surfaces, and painted interior give a close look at the style that once made the area a Jain pilgrimage landscape.

The building is no longer used as a living temple, and condition can vary. Go slowly and look for the details: figures, painted ceilings, and the way the temple rises above the everyday lanes around it.
