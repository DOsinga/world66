---
title: "Day Trips"
type: section
---

The most rewarding day trips from Nowshera are to two sites in neighbouring districts. **Takht-i-Bahi**, about an hour's drive northwest near Mardan, is a 1st-century Gandharan Buddhist monastery built across three hilltop terraces — one of the finest surviving Buddhist sites in Pakistan and a UNESCO World Heritage Site since 1980. The cells of the monks, the stupa court, and the assembly hall are all clearly readable on the ground.

**Peshawar**, 40 km west, offers the Qissa Khwani bazaar, the Mahabat Khan mosque, and the Peshawar Museum's outstanding Gandharan collection — easily worth a full day. In the other direction, **Attock Fort** sits 60 km east at the dramatic confluence of the Kabul and Indus rivers, though access is restricted as it is still in military use.
