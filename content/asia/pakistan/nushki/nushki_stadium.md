A modest football stadium near the centre of town, Nushki Stadium hosts matches in the local and provincial leagues and is a useful place to see Nushki on a weekend afternoon. Football has a stronger following in Balochistan than in much of the rest of Pakistan, and the small stands here often fill for fixtures against Quetta or Dalbandin sides.

The pitch is grass when there has been recent rain and dust when there has not. Entry is informal; ask at the gate.
