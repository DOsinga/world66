---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

Nushki has a cold desert climate. **Summer (June–August)** is severe — daytime highs regularly above 40°C, dust storms, and very little shade. **Winter (December–February)** can drop below freezing at night and bring occasional snow on the surrounding hills, though the days are usually clear and bright.

The most comfortable months for travel are **March, April, October, and November**, when daytime temperatures sit around 20–28°C and the desert light is at its best. Pack for a wide diurnal range whatever the season; the air is dry enough that nights cool sharply once the sun is down.
