---
title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Peshawar's history stretches back more than 2,500 years, and much of it is still visible in the old city. The **Qissa Khwani Bazaar** — the Storytellers' Bazaar — is the centre of gravity: a labyrinth of lanes piled with dried fruit, nuts, brass, and carpets, alive with the sounds of Pashto bargaining. Wander it on foot in the cool of the early morning.

The **Peshawar Museum** is the city's unmissable stop — one of the world's great repositories of Gandharan Buddhist sculpture, produced here when this region sat at the crossroads of Greek, Persian, Indian, and Buddhist culture. The **Mahabat Khan Mosque** is the finest Mughal religious building in the city. The **Bala Hisar Fort**, though now a military base closed to visitors, dominates the skyline and is worth seeing from outside.
