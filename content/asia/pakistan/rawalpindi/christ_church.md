Christ Church on the Mall Road is one of Rawalpindi's surviving colonial-era buildings, a Victorian Anglican church built to serve the British garrison when this was the Northern Command headquarters of the British Indian Army. The building is modest but well-maintained, with pointed arches and a small bell tower, sitting somewhat incongruously in its modern surroundings.

The church is still an active place of worship for Rawalpindi's small Christian community. Visitors are generally welcome during daylight hours; it is worth a brief stop if you are walking the Saddar area, both for the architecture and for the reminder that Rawalpindi's religious landscape extends well beyond its mosques.
