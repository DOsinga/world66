---

The flat irrigated countryside around Sargodha is one of the world's largest contiguous citrus-growing regions, devoted overwhelmingly to the kinnow mandarin. Driving through the groves during harvest — December through March — is among the most visually distinctive experiences in Punjab.

Several growers offer farm visits during the season, with tasting and a tour of the packing sheds. The smaller towns of Bhalwal and Bhera to the north are at the heart of the production area.
