The colonial-era central park at the heart of Sargodha, laid out by the British in the early 20th century when the city was established as a planned cantonment town. The park is the social hub of the city — joggers in the morning, families in the evening, a small zoo and a children's playground.

Worth a stroll on a winter afternoon. The surrounding civic buildings — the clock tower, the district courts, the original railway station — give the area its planned colonial character.
