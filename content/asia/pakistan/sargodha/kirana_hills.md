A range of low, rocky hills east of Sargodha, abruptly rising out of the flat plain to a maximum of around 1,000 metres. The exposed black rock is among the oldest in the subcontinent, and the bare slopes give a stark, lunar appearance against the green of the surrounding citrus groves.

The publicly accessible parts of the range have hiking trails and a small picnic area; the eastern and southern flanks contain restricted military zones. Best visited in winter when the heat and haze drop.
