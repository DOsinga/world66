The original early 20th-century railway station, built when the British extended the line from Lahore into the Lower Bari Doab. The buff-stone building with its arched verandah is a typical example of British colonial railway architecture in the Punjab and one of the few intact public buildings from that period in the city.

Trains still operate, though the schedule is thin compared to the main Lahore–Karachi corridor. Worth a quick look for the architecture even if you are not boarding.
