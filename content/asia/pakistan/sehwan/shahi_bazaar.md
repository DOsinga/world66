Shahi Bazaar is the old commercial lane that feeds the shrine area, busy with pilgrims, food stalls, garland sellers, sweets, dry fruit, chadars, and small Sindhi souvenirs. It is not pretty in a restored-old-town way; it is useful, crowded, and alive.

Walk it before or after visiting the shrine. The bazaar makes the devotion around Lal Shahbaz Qalandar feel less like a monument and more like the daily economy of the town.
