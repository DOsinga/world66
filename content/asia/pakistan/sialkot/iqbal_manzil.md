Iqbal Manzil is the birthplace of Muhammad Iqbal, the poet and philosopher regarded as Pakistan's national poet. The house now contains a small museum and library connected with his life and work.

For visitors to Sialkot, it is one of the city's clearest cultural stops and a useful counterpoint to the city's better-known industrial identity.
