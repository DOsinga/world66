The Manthal Buddha Rock sits at the south edge of Skardu valley, near the village of Manthal on the road to Satpara. The carving — about three metres high, in low relief on a single enormous block of granite — depicts a seated Buddha flanked by bodhisattvas, and is the most important visible survival of pre-Islamic Baltistan.

The site is small, easy to find, and worth a fifteen-minute stop. There is no entry fee. Local guides will often be on hand to explain the style and the iconography. Visit alongside the fort and Satpara Lake on a half-day exploration of the valley.
