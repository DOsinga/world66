The Shigar Valley extends northeast from Skardu, a long, fertile river valley lined with apricot and apple orchards and dotted with old Balti villages. It is the historical gateway to the great Karakoram peaks — from the top of the valley, trekkers head into the Baltoro glacier system and ultimately to K2 base camp.

The village of Shigar itself is dominated by the beautifully restored Shigar Fort, a seventeenth-century rajah's palace converted into a heritage hotel by the Aga Khan Trust for Culture. Even if you don't stay, the fort and its small museum are open to the public. The drive from Skardu takes about an hour and a half on a much-improved road.
