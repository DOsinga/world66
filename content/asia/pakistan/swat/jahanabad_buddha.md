The Jahanabad Buddha is a 7-metre-high seated Buddha carved into a granite boulder above the village of Jahanabad in Manglawar tehsil, around 25 km northeast of Mingora. It is one of the largest rock-cut Buddhist sculptures in Asia and dates from the 7th century AD.

Reaching the site involves a short climb up a rocky hillside from the village. The carving sits in a clearing among boulders with views across the valley, and the restoration work (visible up close) is itself part of the story. Bring water; there are no facilities on site.
