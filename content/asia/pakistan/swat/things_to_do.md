title: "Things to Do"

---

Swat's main draws are its natural scenery and Buddhist heritage. Trekking in the upper valley — particularly to [Mahodand Lake](/asia/pakistan/swat/mahodand_lake) and the [Ushu Forest](/asia/pakistan/swat/ushu_forest) — is superb, and the mountains around [Kalam](/asia/pakistan/swat/kalam) offer serious hiking for the more adventurous.

[Malam Jabba](/asia/pakistan/swat/malam_jabba), at 2,652 metres and 39 km northeast of Saidu Sharif, has Pakistan's only ski resort, with a chairlift and runs that are open roughly from December to March. In summer the same area is good for hiking.

The Swat River offers trout fishing — the hatchery at [Madyan](/asia/pakistan/swat/madyan) stocks the river, and the angling season opens in April.
