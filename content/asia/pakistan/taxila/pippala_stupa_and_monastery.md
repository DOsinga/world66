Pippala is one of the Buddhist monastic sites that make Taxila more than a single ruin. The remains sit within the wider UNESCO-listed archaeological landscape, where successive cities, stupas, and monasteries show how important this valley became on the routes between South Asia and Central Asia.

It is a quieter stop than the headline Taxila sites, but that is part of the appeal. Come with a driver or guide who knows the scattered ruins, and use Pippala to understand Taxila as a network of religious and urban sites rather than a museum with one main exhibit.
