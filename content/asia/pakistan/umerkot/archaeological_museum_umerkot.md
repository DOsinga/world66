The Archaeological Museum Umerkot sits within the fort complex and gives the site more than a quick exterior visit. Its collection includes coins, manuscripts, arms, pottery, and objects from the wider Thar and Sindh region.

It is modest, but useful. In a town where history is scattered between legend, empire, and local memory, the museum gives travellers a simple starting point.
