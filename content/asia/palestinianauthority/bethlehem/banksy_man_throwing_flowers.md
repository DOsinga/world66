Banksy's Man Throwing Flowers is one of Bethlehem's best-known pieces of political street art, painted on the separation barrier and widely reproduced as an image of protest, conflict, and fragile hope. Its location matters as much as the stencil itself: the surrounding wall is part of the experience.

Visit with sensitivity to the people who live beside the barrier every day. The mural can be seen quickly, but the stronger travel value comes from understanding it alongside Bethlehem's broader religious, political, and urban landscape.
