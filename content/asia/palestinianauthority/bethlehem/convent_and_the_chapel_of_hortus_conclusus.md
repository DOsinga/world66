The Convent and Chapel of Hortus Conclusus stands south of Bethlehem, near Solomon's Pools. The monastery was built in 1901 and is tied to the Catholic feast of Hortus Conclusus, marked here on the third Sunday of August.

It is a quiet religious stop rather than one of Bethlehem's headline sights, but it fits well into a trip that also visits Solomon's Pools or the countryside around Artas.
