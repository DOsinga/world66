King David's Wells are three old rock-cut cisterns off Manger Square, traditionally linked to the story of David's soldiers breaking through a Philistine garrison to bring him water from Bethlehem. They are a small stop, but a worthwhile one if you are tracing the biblical and old-city sights around central Bethlehem.
