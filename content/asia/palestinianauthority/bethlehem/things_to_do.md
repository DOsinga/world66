title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Bethlehem's main draw is the [Church of the Nativity](/asia/palestinianauthority/bethlehem/church_of_the_nativity) — one of the oldest continuously operating churches in the world — and the surrounding [Manger Square](/asia/palestinianauthority/bethlehem/manger_square). Beyond the pilgrimage circuit, the [Milk Grotto Chapel](/asia/palestinianauthority/bethlehem/milk_grotto_chapel), [Star Street](/asia/palestinianauthority/bethlehem/star_street) in the old city, and [Banksy's Walled Off Hotel](/asia/palestinianauthority/bethlehem/walled_off_hotel) near the separation wall are all worth time. The [Aida Refugee Camp](/asia/palestinianauthority/bethlehem/aida_camp) at the edge of town offers a sobering insight into the long-term reality of displacement.
