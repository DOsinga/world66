The Tomb of Rachel is one of the region's most important Jewish pilgrimage sites, traditionally identified as the burial place of the biblical matriarch Rachel. It sits at the northern edge of Bethlehem, close to the separation barrier, and the setting is more fortified than serene.

Visitors come for prayer, history, and the uneasy sense of how sacred geography and present-day politics overlap here. Access is often simpler from Jerusalem by bus than from central Bethlehem.
