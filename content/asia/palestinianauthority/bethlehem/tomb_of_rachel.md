The Tomb of Rachel is a major Jewish pilgrimage site on the edge of Bethlehem, traditionally identified as the burial place of the biblical matriarch Rachel.

Access and security arrangements can be more complicated than for central Bethlehem sights, and many visitors reach it from Jerusalem. Check current conditions before making a special trip.
