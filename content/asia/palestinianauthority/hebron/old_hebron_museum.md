The Old Hebron Museum occupies the former Palestine Hotel near the entrance to Hebron's old city. The building itself is part of the story: a handsome early-20th-century structure that has moved from hotel to industrial use and back into public life as a heritage space.

Inside, the museum is meant to introduce Hebron's old-town history, architecture, crafts, and everyday culture. It is a useful stop before wandering the souq, especially if you want the surrounding lanes to read as more than a series of checkpoints, shops, and stone facades.
