The old city is the heart of Hebron and its most intense experience. The [Cave of the Patriarchs](/asia/palestinianauthority/hebron/cave_of_the_patriarchs) — sacred to both Muslims and Jews as the burial site of Abraham, Sarah, Isaac, Rebekah, Jacob, and Leah — sits at its centre, divided between a mosque and a synagogue with separate entrances and heavy security. Nearby, the [Hebron Souq](/asia/palestinianauthority/hebron/hebron_souq) is one of the most atmospheric markets in the Palestinian territories, famous for hand-blown glass, pottery, and locally made leather. The [Ibrahimi Mosque](/asia/palestinianauthority/hebron/cave_of_the_patriarchs) and [Tel Rumeida](/asia/palestinianauthority/hebron/tel_rumeida) archaeological site reveal layers going back thousands of years. [Abraham's Oak](/asia/palestinianauthority/hebron/abrahams_oak), a massive ancient terebinth outside town, marks the traditional site where Abraham pitched his tent.
