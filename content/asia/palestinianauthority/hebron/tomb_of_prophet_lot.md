The Tomb of Prophet Lot is a religious site associated with the Biblical figure Lot and his daughters. The surrounding area includes old stone houses and caves, giving the stop a wider historic setting than the tomb alone.

It is a short excursion from Hebron for travellers interested in Biblical and Islamic traditions in the southern West Bank. As with other religious sites in the area, check local access conditions before setting out.
