Tor-Safa Cave is one of the largest caves on the western slopes of the Judaean Mountains. It has narrow passages, larger chambers, and a position beside Al-Safa park, making it a worthwhile natural stop outside Hebron for visitors who are comfortable with rough ground.

Bring a light and sturdy shoes, and check local access conditions before setting out.
