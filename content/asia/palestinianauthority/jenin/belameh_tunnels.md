The Belameh Tunnels are among Jenins more unusual archaeological sights, associated with the ancient settlement of Ibleam. The underground passages and waterworks point to a much older urban story than the modern city first suggests.

Access can depend on local arrangements and conditions, so ask before setting out and do not expect a heavily packaged visitor site. For travellers interested in archaeology, the tunnels add real depth to a Jenin stop.
