---
title: Things to Do
type: section
---

Jenin is a working market town, not a tourist city, and most of what you'll do here is wander — through the souk around the Fatima Khatun Mosque, out to Burqin for the church, into the camp to see the Jenin Horse and the Freedom Theatre. Half a day covers the main sights comfortably; a full day if you also visit one of the olive oil producers in the surrounding villages.
