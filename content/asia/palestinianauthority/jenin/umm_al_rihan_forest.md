Umm Al Rihan Forest is part of the largest woodland area around Jenin, a landscape of dense tree cover unusual for much of the northern West Bank. It is a good change of pace from town sights, with quiet roads and patches of shade.

Go with current local advice and your own transport, since access and conditions can vary. The appeal is the broader wooded landscape rather than a single built monument.
