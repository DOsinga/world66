Herod's Palace ruins sit outside Jericho amid the archaeological landscape of the lower Jordan Valley. The remains are fragmentary, but the setting is powerful: a winter palace complex built for a ruler who understood both luxury and control, placed where desert, water, and routes met.

This is a site for travellers who enjoy reading ruins in context. Combine it with Jericho's other archaeological stops rather than expecting a fully reconstructed monument, and bring sun protection; the exposed setting is part of the experience.
