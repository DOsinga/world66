Naaran Synagogue is an archaeological site near Jericho associated with a Jewish community of the Byzantine period. Its mosaic remains and setting add another layer to an area already dense with tells, monasteries, palaces, and ancient roads.

Access and preservation conditions can vary, so check locally before making it a special trip. For historically minded travellers, it is a worthwhile reminder that Jericho's surroundings were never defined by one era or one community.
