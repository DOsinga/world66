Nabi Musa is a Muslim shrine complex in the desert between Jerusalem and Jericho, traditionally associated with Moses. The site has long been a pilgrimage stop, with courtyards, domed rooms, and a decorated cenotaph inside; the stark landscape around it is part of the experience.
