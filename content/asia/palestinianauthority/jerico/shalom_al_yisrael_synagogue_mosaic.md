The Shalom al Yisrael mosaic preserves the floor of an ancient synagogue in Jericho, with geometric decoration and a Hebrew inscription at its centre. It is a small site, but an important one for understanding the city's Jewish, Byzantine, and early medieval layers.

Because Jericho is often visited for Tell es-Sultan, monasteries, and desert landscapes, this mosaic adds a different kind of stop: quiet, compact, and textural. Check local access before going, as heritage sites in the area can have changing arrangements.
