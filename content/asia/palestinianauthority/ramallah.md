Ramallah is the de facto administrative capital of the Palestinian Authority, perched on a ridge in the central West Bank about fifteen kilometres north of [Jerusalem](/asia/israel/jerusalem). It has grown from a small Christian town into a busy, cosmopolitan city — by West Bank standards — with universities, NGO offices, and the most active nightlife scene in the Palestinian territories.

The city centre around [Al-Manara Square](/asia/palestinianauthority/ramallah/al_manara_square) is noisy and congested, but the streets radiating from it are lined with cafes, restaurants, and bars that fill up on weekend evenings. Ramallah has a young, educated population and a cultural life that includes the [Al-Kasaba Theatre and Cinematheque](/asia/palestinianauthority/ramallah/al_kasaba_theatre), the [Khalil Sakakini Cultural Center](/asia/palestinianauthority/ramallah/khalil_sakakini_cultural_center), and the [Mahmoud Darwish Museum](/asia/palestinianauthority/ramallah/mahmoud_darwish_museum) dedicated to the Palestinian national poet.

Yasser Arafat's tomb and the accompanying museum, the [Muqata'a compound](/asia/palestinianauthority/ramallah/muqataa), are the main visitor attraction. The city is accessible from Jerusalem via Qalandia checkpoint, though crossing times are unpredictable.
