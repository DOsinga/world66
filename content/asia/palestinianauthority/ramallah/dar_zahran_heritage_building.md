Dar Zahran Heritage Building is a restored Ramallah family house that works as a small museum, gallery, and cultural stop. Its stone rooms, domestic details, and old photographs give a more intimate view of the city than the larger political landmarks around town.

The visit is most rewarding if you slow down and look closely at the images of Ramallah's streets, families, and social life. It is a good reminder that the city is not only a modern administrative centre, but a place with layered local memory.
