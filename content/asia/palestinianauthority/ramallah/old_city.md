Ramallah's Old City is not a dense medina, but its older streets still hold churches, mosques, and traditional stone buildings. It is worth a short wander if you want to see the older town behind the modern commercial centre.

Pair it with nearby heritage buildings and cultural stops rather than treating it as a full half-day sight.
