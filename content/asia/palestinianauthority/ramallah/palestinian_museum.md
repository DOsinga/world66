The Palestinian Museum, just outside Ramallah near Birzeit, is one of the most important cultural institutions in the West Bank. The building and gardens are part of the experience, but the real draw is the museum's approach to Palestinian history as lived memory, landscape, art, and diaspora rather than a simple timeline.

Exhibitions change, so check the programme before going. Even when a show is challenging, the museum is worth the trip for travellers who want a deeper understanding of Palestinian identity beyond checkpoints, headlines, and old-city sightseeing.
