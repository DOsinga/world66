---
title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Kavieng's draw is almost entirely in the water. The reefs and channels around the northern tip of New Ireland are some of the most consistently good diving in the Pacific, with very little dive traffic and a strong current that brings in pelagic fish — barracuda, eagle rays, sharks, and the occasional manta. Several wrecks from the Pacific war lie within easy boat range, most still loaded with cargo and largely undived.

On land there is less to do, but the town's waterfront, the Sunday market, and a couple of low-key war memorials are worth a wander. The drive south along the Boluminski Highway — a single road that runs almost the length of the island — passes through coconut plantations and traditional villages and is one of the more interesting overland trips in the country.
