Ela Beach Craft Market is a monthly market run by Ela Murray International School, usually held on the last Saturday of the month. Stalls bring together carvings, woven baskets, handmade crafts, and other work from across Papua New Guinea.

It is one of the easier places in Port Moresby to browse local craft in one place, especially if you want souvenirs with more character than airport-shop stock.
