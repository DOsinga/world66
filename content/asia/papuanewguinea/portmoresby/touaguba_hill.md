Touaguba Hill is one of the better-known high points in Port Moresby, looking over the harbour, the older town, and the spread of suburbs around the coast. The viewpoint helps make sense of a city that can feel fragmented at street level.

Go with local advice about access and timing, as Port Moresby is not a place for careless wandering. With a driver or host, the hill gives a useful orientation stop and some of the city's strongest harbour views.
