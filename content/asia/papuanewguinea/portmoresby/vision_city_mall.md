Vision City in Waigani is Port Moresby's largest shopping mall, holding supermarkets, international brand stores, electronics shops, cafes, and a cinema. It is a practical place to stock up before heading into the provinces, and the food court offers one of the wider ranges of affordable food in the city.

For most visitors, Vision City functions as much as an air-conditioned refuge as a shopping destination. The mall is busy most days and has reliable security, making it one of the more relaxed places to spend time in the city. It is also a useful indicator of the economic stratification of Port Moresby: the expatriate and upper-class PNG community does most of its shopping here.
