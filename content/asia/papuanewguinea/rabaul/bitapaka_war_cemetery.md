Bitapaka War Cemetery is the main Commonwealth war cemetery near Rabaul, holding graves from both world wars in a quiet, carefully maintained setting. It is closely tied to the region's military history, from early Australian operations to the devastating Pacific War years.

The cemetery is a reflective stop rather than a sightseeing spectacle. Pair it with Rabaul's tunnels, volcanic sites, and wartime remains to understand how heavily this landscape was shaped by conflict.
