The New Guinea Club Museum occupies the former New Guinea Club, a 1937 building from Rabaul's colonial period. Its photo displays and local history exhibits give useful context for the town's wartime, volcanic, and colonial stories.

It is a simple museum rather than a polished national institution, but it is central, atmospheric, and worth pairing with Yamamoto's Bunker and the other historic stops around Rabaul.
