The vast majority of travellers passing through Kalibo are headed to **Boracay**, the small island four kilometres off the northwest tip of Panay famous for its long white beach. The standard route is a 90-minute van transfer from Kalibo to Caticlan, a 15-minute pumpboat ride to Boracay, and a tricycle along the island. Boracay can be done as a long day trip but is much better as an overnight (or week).

**Malay**, the mainland municipality opposite Boracay, has its own beaches and a quieter rhythm if you want a beach without the crowds. **Bakhawan Eco-Park** on the coast just outside Kalibo is closer and easier — see the things-to-do listing. For deeper local culture, the towns of **Numancia** (5 km west) and **Lezo** (10 km west) are the centres of the piña weaving industry and are easy half-day visits by tricycle or van.
