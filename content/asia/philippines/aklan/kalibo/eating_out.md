---
title: "Eating Out"
type: section
---

Aklan's traditional dishes are worth seeking out. **Inubaran na manok** — chicken braised in coconut milk with shredded banana pith — is the province's flagship dish, and you will find it on the menu of any restaurant that treats local cooking seriously. **Binakoe**, a coconut-poached fish wrapped in banana leaf, and **adobong puti**, a vinegar-and-garlic adobo without soy sauce, are the other Aklanon classics. Sweet, sticky **inday-inday** rice cakes are sold from carts on Pastrana Park.

The bulk of the restaurants are along the four blocks of Roxas Avenue and the streets immediately around it.
