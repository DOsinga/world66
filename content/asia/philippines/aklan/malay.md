Malay is a municipality on the northwestern tip of Panay Island in Aklan province, and for most travellers it means one thing: the gateway to [Boracay](/asia/philippines/boracay). The [Caticlan Jetty Port](/asia/philippines/aklan/malay/caticlan_jetty_port), where boats depart for the ten-minute crossing to the island, is in Malay's Caticlan barangay.

If your flight arrives at Caticlan Airport (officially [Godofredo P. Ramos Airport](/asia/philippines/aklan/malay/godofredo_p_ramos_airport)), you are already in Malay -- the airport is a short tricycle ride from the jetty. Travellers arriving by bus from Kalibo or other parts of Panay will be dropped at the Caticlan transport terminal.

Beyond being a transit point, Malay has its own quiet appeal. The mainland coast has some undeveloped beaches, and the surrounding countryside is green and hilly — the protected [Northwest Panay Peninsula Natural Park](/asia/philippines/aklan/malay/northwest_panay_peninsula_natural_park) inland still holds endemic warty pigs and hornbills, and the community-run [Motag Living Museum](/asia/philippines/aklan/malay/motag_living_museum) gives an unvarnished look at Aklanon village life. But most visitors pass through quickly, eyes fixed on the white sand across the water.
