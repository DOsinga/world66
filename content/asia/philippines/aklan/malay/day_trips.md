The obvious day trip from anywhere in Malay is **Boracay**, ten minutes across the strait by pumpboat from Caticlan jetty. Most travellers stay on the island, but a day visit from Caticlan is perfectly possible and lets you skip the high room rates.

Beyond Boracay, the **mangrove channels of Buruanga** can be paddled by kayak; the small fishing village of **Tabon** has uncrowded beaches; and the **Nabaoy River**, inland from Caticlan, has clear pools, a swimming spot, and a series of small waterfalls reached by a short hike.
