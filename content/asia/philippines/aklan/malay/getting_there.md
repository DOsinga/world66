---
title: "Getting There"
type: section
---

**By air.** The small **Godofredo P. Ramos (Caticlan) Airport** in Malay is a 5-minute tricycle ride from the Boracay jetty port. Cebu Pacific, AirAsia and Philippine Airlines fly from Manila and Cebu, with most flights operated on smaller aircraft. **Kalibo International Airport**, about an hour to the south by van, takes larger long-haul aircraft and is often cheaper.

**By boat and bus.** Vans and buses run from Kalibo to Caticlan on a tarmacked road, leaving frequently from the airport when flights land. From further afield, **2GO** runs ferries from Manila to Caticlan that take about 12 hours. The crossing to Boracay itself is a 10-minute pumpboat ride from the jetty — pay the boat fare, environmental fee and terminal fee at the three booths before boarding.
