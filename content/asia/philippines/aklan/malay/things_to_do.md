type: section
---

Most visitors only encounter Malay as the gateway to Boracay, but the mainland deserves a brief look in its own right. The **Caticlan Jetty Port** is the boat departure point for the famous island, and the small bayside town of **Caticlan** itself has a handful of family-run restaurants serving fresh seafood and Aklanon dishes.

Further inland, the green hills of mainland Malay are dotted with small barangays, mangrove channels, and the protected forest of the **Northwest Panay Peninsula Natural Park**, a rare patch of lowland rainforest home to the endangered Visayan warty pig and rufous hornbill. **Motag Living Museum** in barangay Motag, just south of Caticlan, gives visitors an honest look at Aklanon rural village life — rice farming, weaving, traditional games — with no theme-park polish.
