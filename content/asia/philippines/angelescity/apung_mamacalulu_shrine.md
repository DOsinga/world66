Apung Mamacalulu Shrine is a stone church known for its image of the Merciful Lord, a figure of Christ that has drawn local devotion since the Spanish colonial period. Pilgrims come to pray before the image, which many devotees regard as miraculous.

It is a quieter stop than Holy Rosary Parish Church, but it adds another layer to Angeles City's old religious centre. Come for the devotional atmosphere rather than grand architecture.
