Bale Herencia is a preserved 19th-century house in Angeles, built in 1860 and known as much for local stories as for its architecture. The building has since held restaurants and small businesses, but its antique facade and old-town setting remain the draw.

It is worth a brief look while walking between Museo ning Angeles, Holy Rosary Parish Church, Pamintuan Mansion, and the other heritage stops in the city centre.
