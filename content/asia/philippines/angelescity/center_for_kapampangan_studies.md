The Center for Kapampangan Studies is a museum and cultural archive at Holy Angel University. Its exhibits introduce the history, language, foodways, and arts of the Kapampangan people, the main ethnolinguistic group of Pampanga.

It is a useful stop for travellers who want more context before visiting Angeles City's churches, old houses, and food landmarks.
