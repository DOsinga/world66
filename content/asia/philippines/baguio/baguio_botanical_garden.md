Baguio Botanical Garden is a broad, pine-cooled city garden on Leonard Wood Road, between Wright Park and Teacher's Camp. It mixes planted beds, sculptures, Cordillera-inspired huts, friendship gardens, and shaded paths in a way that feels very Baguio: civic, cool-weather, and a little theatrical.

It works well as a gentle break between busier sights, especially if you are travelling with family or need a slower hour after the market and cathedral. The garden has been renovated in recent years and can be busier than its name suggests, but it remains one of the easiest places in the city to breathe and walk under trees.
