Bell Church is a Chinese temple near the Baguio-La Trinidad boundary in Barangay Balili. Its gateways, courtyards, dragon details, and hillside setting make it one of the more distinctive religious sites around Baguio.

The temple remains an active place of worship, so visit quietly and avoid interrupting ceremonies.
