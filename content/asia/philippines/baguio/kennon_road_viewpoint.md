Kennon Road Viewpoint gives travellers one of the classic approaches to Baguio: steep
road, mountain air, and a look back at the dramatic terrain that separates the city from
the lowlands. It is a quick stop, but it helps make the climb feel like part of the trip
rather than just transport.

Conditions on Kennon Road can change with weather and maintenance, so check local access
before building plans around it. If the road is open, the viewpoint is best treated as a
scenic pause on the way in or out of Baguio.
