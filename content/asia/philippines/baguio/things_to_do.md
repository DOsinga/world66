---
title: Things to Do
type: section
---

Baguio's main attractions cluster within a few square kilometres of the city centre — Burnham Park, Session Road, the cathedral, Wright Park, and the Mansion all walkable from each other or a cheap jeepney ride apart. The art museums (BenCab and Tam-awan) sit on the outskirts and need a taxi or Grab. Most visitors arrive on weekends; come midweek if you can to avoid the traffic that builds up on the narrow mountain roads.
