The Valley of Colors is a hillside of brightly painted houses on the Baguio-La Trinidad approach, designed to turn an ordinary urban slope into a landmark. Its appeal is immediate and visual: blocks of colour stacked against the mountain-city backdrop.

It is best seen as a quick photo stop rather than a deep cultural site. Go in good light, be careful along the road, and remember that the painted houses are still people's homes, not a theme-park set.
