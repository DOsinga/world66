Baler Hanging Bridge crosses the Tibag-Sabang River near the edge of town, giving a quick look at the river landscape behind the surf beaches. The bridge is simple and narrow, but the sway, water views, and approach through local neighbourhoods make it a memorable short stop.

It is not worth planning a day around, yet it clears the threshold as a small Baler landmark because it is easy to combine with Sabang Beach, the town centre, or nearby heritage stops. Go for a brief walk and photos, not for a major engineered spectacle.
