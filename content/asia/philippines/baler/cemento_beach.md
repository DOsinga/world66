Cemento Beach is Baler's more serious surf break, set away from the beginner-friendly sweep of Sabang. The beach has a rougher, rockier character, and the reef can make conditions powerful, so it is better suited to experienced surfers or visitors who are happy watching from shore.

Come for the coast rather than soft sand. When the swell is working, Cemento shows why Baler has a place in Philippine surf history; when it is quiet, it is still a good excuse to see the less polished side of the bay.
