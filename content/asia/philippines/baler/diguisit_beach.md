Diguisit Beach shows a rougher, more scenic side of Baler than the surf-school strip at Sabang. Rock shelves, pools, and small offshore formations break up the shoreline, making it better for wandering and photography than for a simple long swim.

Go at low tide if you want to explore the rocks safely. The road north of town makes it easy to combine Diguisit with Ermita Hill and other coastal viewpoints, and the beach is most rewarding as part of that short scenic loop.
