Dimadimalangat Rock Formation is one of the rougher, more photogenic pieces of the Baler coast. The rocks sit by the Diguisit shoreline, where waves break hard against dark stone and the Sierra Madre backdrop makes the coast feel less resort-like than Sabang.

It is a stop for views, tide pools, and pictures rather than a place to swim casually. Wear shoes with grip if you plan to clamber around, and time the visit with the tide and weather; the same surf that makes the scene dramatic can make the rocks unpleasant or unsafe.
