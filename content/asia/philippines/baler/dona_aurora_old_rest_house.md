The Doña Aurora Old Rest House is one of Baler's small heritage stops, tied to the memory of Aurora Quezon, wife of President Manuel L. Quezon and the namesake of Aurora province. It gives the town a more personal historical layer beyond surfing and beaches.

The building is modest, and visitors should treat it as a short stop rather than a full museum afternoon. Its interest is strongest when paired with Museo de Baler and the Quezon-related sites around town, which together explain why this relatively small coastal town carries so much provincial symbolism.
