The Millennium Balete Tree near Baler is exactly the kind of roadside landmark that sounds overpraised until you stand beside it. Its buttressed trunk is vast, the roots fold into chambers and ribs, and local guides will cheerfully point out the spaces where people can step inside the living tree.

It is not a wilderness experience; expect souvenir stalls, photo-taking, and a steady flow of visitors. Even so, the tree is impressive and makes an easy inland stop when you want a break from Baler’s beaches, surf schools, and coastal viewpoints.
