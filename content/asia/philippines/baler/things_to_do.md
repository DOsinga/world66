title: Things to Do
type: section
---

Baler is the Philippines' surfing capital, but there is more to do than ride waves. The town sits at the edge of a dense national park, with waterfalls, forest trails, and a dramatic coastline. The history here is also unexpectedly rich — this quiet bay was the site of the last Spanish garrison to surrender during the Philippine Revolution, holding out for 337 days in a church.
