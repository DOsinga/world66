Vincent Gonzales Art Museum is a small, personal art stop in Baler, centered on the work and collection of the local artist. It adds a cultural counterpoint to a town better known for surfing, coastal scenery, and the Museo de Baler.

The museum is best for travelers already spending time in Baler rather than those rushing through. Its appeal is local and intimate, so check current opening arrangements before making a special trip.
