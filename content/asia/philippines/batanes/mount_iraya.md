Mount Iraya is the highest peak in Batanes, a dormant volcano rising above Batan Island. The mountain is part of the islands' daily scenery, and its cloud cover is often read locally as a sign of changing weather.

For hikers, Iraya is a demanding climb through humid forest and volcanic terrain. Go with local guidance and do not treat it as a casual viewpoint walk.
