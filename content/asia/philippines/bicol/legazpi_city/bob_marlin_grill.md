One of the better restaurants at the Embarcadero de Legazpi, Bob Marlin sits over the water on the jetty complex with views across the Albay Gulf. The speciality is fresh seafood from the local fishing boats — grilled fish, squid, crabs and prawns — along with Bicolano classics. It is more tourist-oriented than the restaurants around the central market, but the seafood quality is high and the setting, with Mayon visible on a clear day over the water, is genuinely good.

The Embarcadero as a whole is the most visitor-friendly eating area in the city, with several options clustered together. Prices are higher than the local eateries downtown, but still modest by international standards.
