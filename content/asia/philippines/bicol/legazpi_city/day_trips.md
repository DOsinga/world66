Donsol, about 60 kilometres west of Legazpi, is the main place in the Philippines for whale shark interaction — snorkelling alongside butanding (whale sharks) from January to June, when the animals congregate in the shallow waters offshore. The WWF-run programme is well-managed and focused on non-harassing encounters; this is not an exploitation show. The drive from Legazpi takes about 90 minutes.

Closer in, Camalig and Daraga municipalities offer the Hoyop-Hoyopan and Calabidongan caves, the Cagsawa Ruins, and Sumlang Lake — all within 30 kilometres of the city centre and easily combined into a single day circuit by tricycle or hired vehicle.
