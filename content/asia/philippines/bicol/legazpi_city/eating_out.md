---
title: "Eating Out"
type: section
---

Legazpi is the best place to eat proper Bicolano food — the regional cuisine that the rest of the Philippines knows mainly from Bicol Express. Local cooking uses coconut milk generously and tolerates a heat level that can surprise visitors who associate Philippine food with mild flavours. The Embarcadero area has the most visitor-friendly restaurants; the old Albay district and the central market offer better value and more authentic experience.
