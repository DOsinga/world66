---
title: "Getting There"
type: section
---

**By air**: Legazpi Airport (LGP), just north of the city centre, has flights to Manila operated by Cebu Pacific and Philippine Airlines. The flight takes about an hour. The airport is small and taxis into town are inexpensive.

**By bus**: Several bus companies operate overnight and daytime services between Manila and Legazpi. The journey takes 8–10 hours on the Maharlika Highway. Philtranco and DLTB are the main operators. Buses depart from the Araneta bus terminal area in Cubao, Quezon City.

**By train**: Legazpi station is the southern terminus of the Philippine National Railways South Main Line. Train service from Manila exists but is slow (12+ hours) and infrequent — check current schedules as the line has had intermittent service disruptions.
