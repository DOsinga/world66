The Legazpi Boulevard runs along the waterfront of the Albay Gulf, with Mayon Volcano rising behind the city and the open sea in front — a combination that makes it one of the more dramatically situated waterfront promenades in the Philippines. The boulevard is the social heart of the city in the evenings, when families stroll and vendors set up along the seawall.

At the northern end, the Embarcadero de Legazpi is a small commercial and entertainment complex built out over the water, with restaurants, bars, and a viewing deck that gives unobstructed views across the gulf to Mayon. Boat trips to Cagraray Island and along the coastline depart from the jetty here. The boulevard is at its best just after dawn, when the volcano is clear and the light is clean before the coastal haze builds up.
