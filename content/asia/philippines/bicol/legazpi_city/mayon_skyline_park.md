Mayon Skyline Park is a viewpoint and picnic area at an altitude of about 760 metres on Mayon's lower slopes, accessed by a winding road from Daraga. At this elevation you are above the agricultural lowlands and looking across the upper lava fields toward the summit cone, giving a perspective on the volcano quite different from the views at sea level. On clear days the view extends back across the entire Albay plain to the coast.

The park is run by the government as a day-trip destination and has basic facilities and a series of picnic shelters. The road up passes through banana plantations and coconut groves giving way to secondary forest. Note that access is subject to volcanic alert levels — when Mayon is at Alert Level 2 or above, the upper approach roads close.
