St. Raphael the Archangel Church is the main church of Legazpi's port district. The original 19th-century church was destroyed during the liberation of the city in 1945, and the present building is a more modern replacement.

The church is worth a short stop if you are already around the waterfront or Embarcadero area, especially for its stained glass and its role in Legazpi's older coastal neighbourhood.
