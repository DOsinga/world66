Sumlang Lake is a small volcanic lake at the foot of Mayon, about 12 kilometres north of central Legazpi in the town of Camalig. The lake sits at the edge of the last major lava flow from Mayon and offers one of the closest flat-terrain views of the volcano's cone, reflected in the water on still mornings. Bamboo rafts can be hired for short rides across the lake, and the surrounding area has developed into a minor eco-tourism site with walking paths and lakeside restaurants.

The drive from Legazpi passes through rice fields and coconut plantations in the Mayon volcanic zone, and the changing views of the volcano along the road are as much an attraction as the lake itself. Best visited early morning before the clouds build.
