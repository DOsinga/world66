---
title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Legazpi's headline act is Mayon Volcano — visible from almost everywhere in the city — but the surrounding landscape offers an unusually dense concentration of things to do: coastal ruins partially buried by volcanic eruptions, hill viewpoints, colonial churches, whale shark interactions an hour away at Donsol, and underground cave systems. The city itself rewards a walk through the Albay district with its cathedral and old quarter.
