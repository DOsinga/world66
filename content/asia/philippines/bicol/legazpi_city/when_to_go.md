---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

The best time to visit Legazpi is between November and May, when rainfall is lower and skies clearer — critical for seeing Mayon Volcano. December through February brings the most reliable clear mornings. The rainy season runs from June to October, when typhoons occasionally affect the region and cloud cover can obscure Mayon for days at a time. Legazpi sits on the Pacific typhoon belt and can be directly hit; check forecasts before travelling June–November.

The Ibalong Festival in October celebrates Bicolano mythology and culture with street dancing and parades, making it a lively time to visit despite the weather risks.
