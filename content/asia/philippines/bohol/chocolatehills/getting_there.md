---
title: "Getting There"
type: section
---

The Chocolate Hills are in the municipality of Carmen, about 55 km northeast of [Tagbilaran](/asia/philippines/bohol/tagbilaran), the provincial capital and the point of arrival for most visitors to Bohol.

From Tagbilaran, buses and vans run regularly to Carmen from the Dao Terminal — the journey takes roughly 1.5 to 2 hours. Alternatively, hire a motorbike-with-driver (habal-habal) or join one of the many organised tours that combine the hills with the Tarsier Sanctuary, Baclayon Church, and a Loboc River cruise in a single day. Tours depart from Tagbilaran hotels and from [Alona Beach](/asia/philippines/bohol/pamilacanisland) on Panglao Island.

From Cebu City, fast ferries reach Tagbilaran in about 2 hours. Day trips from Cebu are popular and feasible, though a night or two on Bohol allows a more relaxed visit.
