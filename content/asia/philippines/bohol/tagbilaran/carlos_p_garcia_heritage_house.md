The Carlos P. Garcia Heritage House preserves the Tagbilaran residence of the Bohol-born Philippine president. Declared a heritage house by the National Historical Commission of the Philippines, it gives the city a compact link to national political history.

The visit is modest but worthwhile if you are already in central Tagbilaran. It is less about grand architecture than biography: family rooms, memorabilia, and the story of a provincial base connected to the country's post-war presidency.
