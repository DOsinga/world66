Casa Rocha-Suarez Heritage Center is one of the oldest surviving houses in Tagbilaran. Built in the early 19th century, the former trading villa has been turned into a small museum with family furniture, heirlooms, and personal objects collected over several generations.

The visit gives a compact look at old Tagbilaran domestic life and the merchant families that shaped the city.
