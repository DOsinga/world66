St. Joseph the Worker Cathedral is the main Catholic landmark in central Tagbilaran, standing by Plaza Rizal and the old civic core. It is not as famous as Baclayon Church, but it gives the city itself a historical anchor beyond its role as Bohol's transport hub.

Step inside if you are already walking around the centre. The cathedral is active, so it is best visited quietly outside services, with time to look at the altar, nave, and the everyday rhythm of parish life in the city.
