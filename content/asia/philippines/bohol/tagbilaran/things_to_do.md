---
title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Tagbilaran's own sights are modest, but the surrounding area of Bohol rewards exploration. The [Blood Compact Monument](/asia/philippines/bohol/tagbilaran/blood_compact_monument) in Bool (3 km east) marks the 1565 treaty between Spain and the local chief Sikatuna. A short drive east brings you to the [Baclayon Church](/asia/philippines/bohol/tagbilaran/baclayon_church), the oldest church in Bohol. North of town, [Punta Cruz Watchtower](/asia/philippines/bohol/tagbilaran/punta_cruz_watchtower) and the [Loon Church](/asia/philippines/bohol/tagbilaran/loon_church) make a good half-day circuit. The famous [Chocolate Hills](/asia/philippines/bohol/tagbilaran/chocolate_hills) are about 55 km from town — most visitors go by motorbike or rented scooter.
