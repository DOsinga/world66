Bulabog Beach is Boracay's east-side beach, facing away from the sunset crowds of White Beach. It is windier, narrower, and less polished, which is exactly why kitesurfers and windsurfers use it when conditions line up.

Come in the morning for a walk or during the windy season if you want to watch lessons and sails moving across the water. It is not Boracay's prettiest swimming beach, and conditions can vary, but it shows a more active and less postcard-perfect side of the island.
