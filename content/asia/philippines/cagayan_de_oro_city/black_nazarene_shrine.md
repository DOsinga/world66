The Archdiocesan Shrine of the Black Nazarene is a city-centre shrine dedicated to the Black Nazarene. It holds a replica statue connected with the devotion centred on Quiapo Church in Manila, giving Mindanao devotees a closer pilgrimage site.

It is a meaningful religious stop in central Cagayan de Oro, especially for visitors interested in Filipino Catholic practice.
