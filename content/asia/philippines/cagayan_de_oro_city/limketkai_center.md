Limketkai Center is the dominant mall in CDO and the place where most of the city's better restaurants are concentrated. The complex includes a large shopping mall, a hotel, and an outdoor strip of restaurants and cafes that stays busy through the evening. It sits along CM Recto Avenue, roughly central in the city, and is well-connected by jeepney routes from most parts of CDO.

The restaurant selection covers a reasonable range: Filipino chains, Japanese, Korean, and a handful of local independent spots doing CDO-specific dishes. The outdoor area between the mall and the hotel is the more pleasant section for eating — there's usually a breeze in the evening and the setting is less enclosed than the mall food court. For visitors who've spent a hot day at the market or hiking, Limketkai is the practical choice for a reliable meal with consistent air conditioning. It's not the most interesting option in the city but it's the most dependable, and the grocery inside is good for stocking up on supplies before heading out to Bukidnon or Camiguin.
