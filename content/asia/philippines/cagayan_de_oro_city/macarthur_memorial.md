The MacArthur Memorial Marker stands near the northern waterfront of CDO, close to the bay. It commemorates General Douglas MacArthur's passage through the city during the liberation of the Philippines in 1945. The marker is modest — a stone monument with an inscription — but it sits in a spot with a decent view over Macajalar Bay and is maintained well enough to be worth a brief stop if you're already in the area.

CDO was an important logistics point during the Pacific War. The city's position as the main port and commercial center of Northern Mindanao made it strategically significant, and the surrounding region saw significant fighting. The memorial is part of a broader network of World War II sites across Mindanao, most of which receive little attention compared to the better-known sites in Leyte and Luzon. For visitors interested in the war's Pacific theater, it provides a small but genuine piece of that history.
