Plaza Divisoria sits at the commercial heart of CDO, a few hundred meters from the river and surrounded by the old market district. The park's official name is Golden Friendship Park, a reference to CDO's historical identity as a meeting point between communities from across Mindanao. The plaza has the standard configuration of a Philippine town center — a central gazebo, benches, a fountain, and paths where vendors set up in the late afternoon — but it's busier than most, since the surrounding area is one of the city's main commercial hubs.

The park is most lively in the early morning and early evening. Food stalls set up along the perimeter selling barbecue, fishballs, and cold drinks. On weekends there are often performances or small events. The surrounding streets hold the bulk of the market activity: textile stalls, hardware shops, and the kind of dense informal commerce that makes Philippine city centers worth walking through. It's not a destination in itself but rather a useful orientation point and a place to sit and watch the city move.
