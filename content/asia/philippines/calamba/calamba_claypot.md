A four-metre-high stone-and-mortar claypot, painted brick red, that sits on a platform in the plaza in front of the parish church. The surface is decorated with the names of all Calamba's *barangays* (neighbourhoods). Officially named the *Banga ng Calamba*, it is the city's most photographed landmark and a useful navigation point — most local jeepneys end up routing past it at some point.

A short stop, but a charming one. Easy to combine with the church and the Rizal Shrine, all within two minutes of each other on foot.
