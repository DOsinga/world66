---
title: "Getting There"
type: section
---

Calamba is about 55 kilometres south of Manila and is the southern terminus of the **South Luzon Expressway** (SLEX). By car or grab the drive takes anywhere from an hour to three, depending on Manila traffic. Off-peak it is genuinely quick; rush-hour traffic on EDSA can swallow your morning.

By bus, **DLTB**, **Jam Liner**, and **HM Transport** all run frequent services from Manila terminals (Cubao, Buendia, PITX) to Calamba's central bus terminal. Fares run around PHP 80–120; allow 90 minutes to two hours.

The **Philippine National Railways** South Main Line stops at Calamba station, but service is intermittent — check current schedules before you rely on it. The under-construction North–South Commuter Railway, due to open later this decade, will eventually make rail the obvious choice for the Manila run.
