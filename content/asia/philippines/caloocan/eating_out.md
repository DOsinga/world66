---
title: "Eating Out"
type: section
---

Caloocan is not a destination for fine dining, but it is a good place to eat the everyday food of working-class Metro Manila. **Carinderias** — small open-fronted eateries with steam-tray menus — serve cheap and filling Filipino classics like *kare-kare* (oxtail in peanut sauce), *sinigang* (sour tamarind soup), and rice meals with fried fish or chicken. The streets around Monumento and Caloocan Cathedral are full of them.

For grilled meats, look for an **ihawan** stall in the evenings, where skewered pork barbecue and chicken intestines are cooked over charcoal and served with rice and vinegar. The **Monumento Wet Market** also has a row of food stalls serving regional Filipino specialities at unbeatable prices.
