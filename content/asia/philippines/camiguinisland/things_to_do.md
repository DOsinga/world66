---
title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Camiguin's main draws are outdoor and shaped entirely by its volcanic geology. The [Sunken Cemetery](/asia/philippines/camiguinisland/sunken_cemetery) — a graveyard that sank beneath the sea in 1871 — is the island's most distinctive dive site and one of the most unusual in the Philippines. [White Island](/asia/philippines/camiguinisland/white_island), a crescent sandbar offshore, offers the best swimming with the volcano as backdrop. [Katibawasan Falls](/asia/philippines/camiguinisland/katibawasan_falls) drops 70 metres into a swimmable pool five kilometres from Mambajao.

The two-day ascent of [Mt Hibok-Hibok](/asia/philippines/camiguinisland/hibok_hibok_volcano) is the island's most demanding activity and rewards with panoramic views across the Bohol Sea. [Ardent Hot Springs](/asia/philippines/camiguinisland/ardent_hot_springs) in the hills above Mambajao are volcanic-fed and best visited in the evening. For history, the [Gui-ob Church Ruins](/asia/philippines/camiguinisland/old_volcano) — a colonial campanile half-buried by the same 1871 eruption that created the Sunken Cemetery — are one of the more haunting heritage sites in Mindanao. [Mantigue Island](/asia/philippines/camiguinisland/mantigue_island), 25 minutes offshore, has reliable sea turtle sightings and excellent snorkelling. [Jigdup Shoal](/asia/philippines/camiguinisland/jigdup_shoal) on the south coast is the best dive site for experienced divers.

The island is small enough to circuit by road in two hours but rewarding enough to stay a week.
