The Bonifacio Trial House is a Spanish-era residence remembered for one of the darkest episodes of the Philippine Revolution. Andres Bonifacio and his brother Procopio were tried here in 1897 after the split between Bonifacio's faction and Emilio Aguinaldo's Cavite leadership.

The house now works as a small museum, giving useful context to a political rivalry that still shapes how Filipinos argue about their revolutionary heroes.
