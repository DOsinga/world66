---
title: "Getting There"
type: section
---

Cavite is about 30–45 minutes south of Manila by car or bus, depending on traffic. Jeepneys and buses run from Baclaran (end of the LRT-1 line) to Cavite City and to various towns in the province. Driving south on the Coastal Road or CAVITEX (the Cavite Expressway) is the most direct route. Tagaytay is reached via Aguinaldo Highway from Cavite City or via the Sta. Rosa/Tagaytay Road from SLEX.

There is no major airport in Cavite province; Ninoy Aquino International Airport in Manila is the regional entry point.
