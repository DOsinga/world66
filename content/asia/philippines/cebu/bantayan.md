Bantayan is an island municipality off the northern tip of Cebu, reached by a ferry crossing from Hagnaya Wharf. It is far quieter than the resort strip around Cebu City, and its beaches — particularly the long white-sand stretch at Santa Fe's [Kota Beach](/asia/philippines/cebu/bantayan/kota_beach) — are among the finest in the Visayas. The water is shallow and calm, the crowds are manageable outside Holy Week, and the pace is resolutely unhurried.

Santa Fe is where the ferry docks and where most visitors stay, with a strip of small resorts and guesthouses backing the beach. The main town of Bantayan, about 12 km inland, is the island's commercial centre — the [old colonial church](/asia/philippines/cebu/bantayan/bantayan_church) dating from the 16th century is the most significant sight on the island. [Virgin Island](/asia/philippines/cebu/bantayan/virgin_island), a small uninhabited sandbar a short boat trip from Santa Fe, is the top island-hopping destination.

Getting there involves a bus from Cebu City's North Bus Terminal to Hagnaya Wharf (roughly three hours), followed by a 45-minute ferry crossing to Santa Fe. Bangkas (outrigger boats) run between the island and the mainland; schedules and frequency vary by season.
