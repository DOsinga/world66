---
title: Things to Do
type: section
---

Bantayan Island offers beaches, island-hopping, and a quiet pace that feels genuinely removed from the resort infrastructure of mainland Cebu. The main beach at Santa Fe is the central draw, but the island rewards those who rent a motorbike and explore further — the western coast, the old town of Bantayan with its colonial church, and the outlying islets are all worth the effort.
