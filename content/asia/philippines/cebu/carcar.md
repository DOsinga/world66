Carcar is an old colonial town on Cebu's south coast, about 40 km from Cebu City. It is not a resort and does not try to be one. The draw is the cluster of Spanish-era houses, the big parish church, the town plaza, and the local food stalls that made Carcar a Cebuano stop in its own right.

The heritage core is small enough to walk. Start around [Saint Catherine of Alexandria Church](/asia/philippines/cebu/carcar/saint_catherine_of_alexandria), then look for restored houses such as [Balay na Tisa](/asia/philippines/cebu/carcar/balay_na_tisa) and [Mercado Mansion](/asia/philippines/cebu/carcar/mercado_mansion). The city museum helps pull the pieces together.

Carcar is also known across Cebu for lechon and chicharon. That matters here: the town is best visited as a short heritage-and-food stop on the road south, not as a polished destination.
