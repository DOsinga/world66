---
title: "Bars and Cafes"
type: section
---

**Mango Avenue** in the Uptown district is the main bar strip, with a mix of expat pubs and local clubs that stay lively until late. For something quieter, the cafes around **IT Park** in Lahug serve good coffee and attract a younger professional crowd. The rooftop bars along **Fuente Osmeña** have city views and are popular for evening drinks before dinner.

For nightlife, **The Walk** at Asiatown IT Park has several clubs and live music venues within easy walking distance of each other.
