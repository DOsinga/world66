Cebu City's oldest and largest public market, established in the early American colonial period and named for the charcoal (*carbon* in local dialect) once sold here. Today it sprawls across several blocks near the old port, selling tropical fruit, vegetables, dried fish, cheap clothing, herbs, and flowers. The dried mango and *tablea* (cacao tablets) sections are particularly good for food souvenirs. It's crowded, noisy, and not tourist-polished — which is the point. Go in the morning when produce is freshest. Keep an eye on your valuables.
