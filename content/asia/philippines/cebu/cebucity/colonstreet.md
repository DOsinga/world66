Reputedly the oldest street in the Philippines — laid out by the Spanish in 1565 — Colon runs through the heart of old downtown Cebu, lined with pharmacies, pawnshops, cheap clothing stores, and street food stalls. It's a working-class commercial thoroughfare rather than a polished tourist attraction, and that's exactly what makes it interesting. Walk it to feel the pulse of the city rather than expecting monuments. The street is named after Christopher Columbus (*Cristóbal Colón* in Spanish) and sits near the Parian, the old Chinese quarter.
