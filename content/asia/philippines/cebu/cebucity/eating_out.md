---
title: "Eating Out"
type: section
---

Cebu is famous for its **lechon** — whole roasted pig, considered the best in the Philippines. Most locals will point you to **CNT Lechon** or **Rico's Lechon** for the definitive version, served with liver sauce on the side. For seafood, the **Lantaw Floating Native Restaurant** south of the city serves grilled fish and shellfish over the water.

The city has a strong street food scene around **Carbon Market** and along **Colon Street** — look for *puso* (hanging rice), *ngohiong* (deep-fried spring rolls), and dried mangoes everywhere. SM City Cebu and Ayala Center have food courts that cover everything from Japanese to Filipino.
