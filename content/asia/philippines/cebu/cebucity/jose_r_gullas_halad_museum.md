The Jose R. Gullas Halad Museum is a compact but worthwhile stop for anyone curious about Cebuano and wider Visayan music. Its displays focus on composers, lyricists, performers, and the popular songs that shaped local life before radio and recording changed everything.

It pairs well with the older heritage sights around downtown Cebu because it gives the city a cultural voice rather than another stone facade. The museum is not large, but the photographs, instruments, and listening material make it feel personal, especially if you have heard Visayan ballads elsewhere in the islands.
