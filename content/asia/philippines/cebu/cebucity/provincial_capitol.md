Cebu Provincial Capitol is a major civic landmark in Cebu City, with a neoclassical facade and dome that mark the older government axis of the city. It adds architectural variety to an itinerary often centered on colonial churches and downtown heritage sites.

Most travellers see it from outside rather than touring the building. It is worth a brief stop if you are already exploring along Osmeña Boulevard or tracing Cebu's 20th-century civic architecture.
