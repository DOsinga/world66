The Rizal Memorial Museum is a small cultural museum near Fuente Osmeña, with paintings and sculpture by Cebuano artists alongside antiques donated by old local families.

It is a modest stop rather than one of Cebu City's headline sights, but it adds useful local art and social-history context if you are already exploring the uptown side of the city.
