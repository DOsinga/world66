---
title: "Shopping"
type: section
---

**Carbon Market** near the old port is Cebu City's biggest public market — a sprawling, chaotic place selling dried fish, tropical fruit, vegetables, and cheap goods of every kind. Good for dried mangoes, *otap* biscuits, and *tablea* (cacao tablets for hot chocolate) as souvenirs.

The malls — **SM City Cebu**, **Ayala Center Cebu**, and **Robinsons Galleria** — cover mainstream retail and international brands. **Cebu Wicker Works** and the shops near the Provincial Capitol sell locally made furniture and rattan crafts.
