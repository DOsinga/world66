Sugbu Chinese Heritage Museum is a small museum in the historic Gotiaoco Building, close to Cebu City's oldest colonial sights. Its exhibits focus on the Chinese community's role in Cebu's trade, family life, and urban history.

It is a short stop rather than a major museum, but the location makes it easy to include with Magellan's Cross, the Basilica del Santo Nino, Fort San Pedro, and the old downtown streets.
