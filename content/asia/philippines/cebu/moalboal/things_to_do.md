---
title: Things to Do
type: section
---

Moalboal is built around water rather than sightseeing. The essential day starts at Panagsama for sardines and turtles, continues by boat to Pescador Island if the sea is right, and ends with either White Beach or the bars back near the dive shops. Kawasan Falls is outside Moalboal, but it is close enough and common enough as an excursion to shape how travelers use the town.
