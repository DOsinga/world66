---
title: Getting There
type: section
---

The fast way in is by air. **Francisco B. Reyes Airport** at Busuanga (USU) handles several daily flights from Manila on Cebu Pacific, Philippine Airlines, and AirSwift, and the flight takes about an hour. The airport is 30 km from Coron town — vans meeting each flight run the route in about 45 minutes for a fixed fare.

The slower, cheaper route is the overnight RoRo ferry from Manila Pier 4 — around 14 hours on the 2GO line. There are also ferries between Coron and El Nido (3–4 hours by fast boat) and longer multi-day expedition cruises that work the route between the two via island stops.
