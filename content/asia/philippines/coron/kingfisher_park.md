Kingfisher Park is a quieter side of Coron, away from the standard island-hopping circuit. The appeal is mangrove habitat, birdlife, and the possibility of seeing the area at a slower pace by paddle or guided nature walk.

It is not as dramatic as Kayangan Lake or the wreck dives, but it rounds out Coron well. Come if you want wildlife, night sounds, and still water rather than another crowded boat stop.
