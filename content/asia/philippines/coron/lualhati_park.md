Lualhati Park is the modest seafront promenade on the southern edge of Coron town, with a paved walkway, a few sculptures, and benches looking out over the bay towards Coron Island. In the early evening it fills with locals — families, joggers, teenagers — and small food stalls set up along the path.

It's the easiest place in town to watch the sunset, and a more relaxed alternative to the climb up Mt. Tapyas. The market and several of the more popular cheap eateries are within a couple of blocks.
