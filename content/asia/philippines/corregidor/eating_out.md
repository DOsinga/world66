---
title: "Eating Out"
type: section
---

Eating options on Corregidor are very limited. The Sun Cruises day-trip package includes a buffet lunch at the resort restaurant, which is the practical solution for most visitors. Day-trippers not on a package can eat at the resort restaurant independently. If you want to eat at the gun batteries or the tunnel, bring your own snacks — there are no food vendors at the ruins.
