The restaurant at the Corregidor Hotel — operated by Sun Cruises — is effectively the only place to eat on the island. It serves Filipino and basic international dishes; the day-trip package from Manila typically includes a buffet lunch here. The food is straightforward and the setting is pleasant enough, though the meal is primarily a logistical convenience rather than a dining destination. Independent visitors can also eat here without the package.
