---
title: "Getting There"
type: section
---

Sun Cruises operates the only regular service to Corregidor, running day trips and overnight packages from CCP Harbor on Roxas Boulevard in Manila. The ferry crossing takes roughly an hour each way. Day trips depart in the morning and return in the late afternoon; the guided tramvia tour on the island is included. Overnight packages include accommodation at the Corregidor Hotel.

There is no regular public ferry to Corregidor. Book Sun Cruises in advance, especially on weekends and Philippine holidays when boats fill quickly. The crossing can be rough during the rainy season (June–October) and sailings are occasionally cancelled in bad weather.
