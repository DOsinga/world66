[Alaminos](/asia/philippines/alaminos), about an hour west, is the gateway to the **Hundred Islands National Park** — a cluster of 124 coral-limestone islets dotting Lingayen Gulf. Boat tours from Lucap Wharf are the standard way in.

**Lingayen** itself, 18 km west, has the historic Pangasinan provincial capitol and the long Lingayen Gulf landing beach where the US Sixth Army came ashore in January 1945. **San Fabian** to the east has cleaner stretches of coast for swimming.
