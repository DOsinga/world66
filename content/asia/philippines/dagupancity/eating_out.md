---
title: Eating Out
type: section
---

Dagupan exists for *bangus* and you should plan at least one meal around it. The local preparations are extensive: *sinigang na bangus* (in tamarind broth), *daing na bangus* (split and dried), *rellenong bangus* (deboned, stuffed, and fried), *paksiw na bangus* (in vinegar and ginger), *inihaw na bangus* (grilled whole). Anywhere reasonable in town will do them all well.

Beyond bangus, look for **pigar-pigar** (a Dagupan beef stir-fry with crispy edges), the local *puto Calasiao* rice cakes, and *kakanin* sticky-rice sweets at the Magsaysay market. The seafood at the riverside restaurants is the freshest you will find in the country.
