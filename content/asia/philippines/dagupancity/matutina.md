A long-standing local institution near the centre of town, with a wide veranda and a menu that runs to every bangus preparation in the Pangasinan kitchen. The *rellenong bangus* is the signature; the *pigar-pigar* beef is the standout from outside the seafood. Prices are modest by Manila standards.

Get there before noon at weekends — the dining room fills up quickly, particularly during Bangus Festival.
