---
title: Things to Do
type: section
---

Dagupan is not a sightseeing town in the conventional sense — the sights are the markets, the fish farms, the river, and the food. A half-day on foot covers the cathedral area, the Magsaysay Market, and a walk along the river; an afternoon on a *banca* into the surrounding fishponds gives the better sense of why this place matters.

The big event is the **Bangus Festival** in April. The rest of the year, time your visits to align with the morning fish auctions (around five to seven am) at the river markets.
