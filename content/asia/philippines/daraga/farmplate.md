FarmPlate is a light, family-friendly stop near Daraga that combines gardens, farm animals, food stalls, and views toward Mayon Volcano. It is not one of the region's great historic sights, but it gives travellers with children an easy place to slow down between churches, ruins, and volcano viewpoints.

The best reason to come is the setting. On clear days Mayon does much of the work, turning a simple farm park into a pleasant photo and snack stop. Treat it as a relaxed add-on rather than the centrepiece of a Bicol itinerary.
