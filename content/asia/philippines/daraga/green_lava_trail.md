The Green Lava area near Mayon Volcano gives visitors a closer look at the volcano's lower slopes, lahar fields, and open views back toward the cone. It is part scenic outing and part reminder that Mayon's beauty is inseparable from volcanic risk.

Use a reputable local operator or guide, and do not assume conditions are safe just because the viewpoint is popular. Weather, volcanic advisories, and trail access should shape any visit.
