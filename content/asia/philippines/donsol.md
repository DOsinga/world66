Donsol is a small coastal town in Sorsogon that earned a place on the travel map for one reason: wild whale sharks, or butanding, feeding seasonally in the bay. Unlike the more controversial feeding-based encounters elsewhere in the Philippines, Donsol's model is built around searching for animals that arrive naturally, with trained local guides and rules against touching or feeding.

That ethical difference matters. Sightings are never guaranteed, and that is part of the bargain if you want a wildlife encounter rather than a show. In season, the [Donsol Whale Shark Interaction Center](/asia/philippines/donsol/donsol_whale_shark_interaction_center) is the organising point for boats from Dancalan.

Donsol also has a quieter second act after dark: [Ogod River Firefly Cruise](/asia/philippines/donsol/ogod_river_firefly_cruise), where small boats drift under mangroves and riverside trees flickering with fireflies. The town itself is plain and rural, but the combination of whale sharks, river nights and Bicol countryside makes it worth the detour from Legazpi.
