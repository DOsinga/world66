---
title: "Shopping"
type: section
---

Dumaguete's main shopping destination is Robinsons Place Dumaguete, a mall with supermarkets, clothing stores, a cinema, and a food court. For everyday goods, the Dumaguete Public Market near the port is well-stocked with fresh produce, local seafood, and household goods. There are also small shops selling local handicrafts and weaving in the area around the central plaza.
