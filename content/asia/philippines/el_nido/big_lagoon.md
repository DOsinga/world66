Big Lagoon is the signature Bacuit Bay stop: a long, shallow inlet on Miniloc Island enclosed by high limestone walls. Boats usually stop at the entrance and visitors continue by kayak, which is exactly the right speed for the water, cliffs, and sudden quiet inside.
