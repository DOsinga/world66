General Santos City — known locally as GenSan — is the southernmost city in Mindanao and one of the fastest-growing urban centres in the Philippines. It sits on the shore of Sarangani Bay, a deep natural harbour that has made the city the tuna capital of the Philippines and one of the largest yellowfin tuna processing centres in Southeast Asia. The tuna trade shapes the city's character: the Fisherport Complex in the early morning is a working spectacle, with fishing boats unloading catches that can include individual fish weighing 80 kilos or more.

The city was founded by settlers from the Visayas and has a multi-ethnic population that includes Maranao, B'laan, T'boli, and other indigenous groups from the surrounding highlands, alongside the majority lowland Christian community. The result is a pragmatic, forward-looking city without much colonial architecture — GeneralSantos was established only in 1939 — but with real energy and a strong sense of civic identity.

Sarangani Bay and the nearby Sarangani Province offer the area's best outdoor opportunities: diving on coral reefs, beach resorts at Gumasa in Glan, and mountain trekking to the slopes of Mount Matutum. The city itself is a commercial hub rather than a beach destination, but its position as the gateway to the SOCCSKSARGEN region makes it a useful base for exploring southern Mindanao.
