Gen. Paulino Santos Museum, inside Notre Dame of Dadiangas University, is the city's first formal museum. It covers General Paulino Santos, early settlement in what became General Santos City, and cultural material from South Cotabato and the surrounding region.

It is a modest museum, but useful for visitors who want context before exploring Sarangani Bay or the wider SOCCSKSARGEN area.
