Klaja Ecopark is a karst landscape northeast of General Santos City, with caves, waterfalls, and rugged limestone towers around Barangay Conel.

Nearby Nopol Hills adds wide views over the city, Sarangani Bay, and parts of South Cotabato. It is best treated as an outdoor side trip rather than a quick downtown stop.
