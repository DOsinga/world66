Eagle Ridge is one of the largest and most ambitious golf developments in the Philippines, spreading over 700 hectares in the hills of General Trias. The club has four 18-hole courses designed by different architects, each with a distinct character, and the rolling Cavite terrain gives the layouts elevation changes unusual for this part of Luzon.

For non-golfers, the residential estate and clubhouse area offer a pleasant contrast to the congested coastal strip — the roads are wide, the landscaping is lush, and the views toward Manila Bay on a clear day are considerable. Day access for golf requires prior arrangement; the club is primarily membership-based.
