---
title: Eating Out
type: section
---

Eating on Guimaras revolves around two things: fresh seafood at the beach resorts in the south, and mango. The mango appears in everything — in pies, in shakes, as fresh fruit, dried, candied, in chutney, in jam, in ice cream. Beach resort kitchens are usually small, fish-grill operations; tricycle out and order at the resort that has caught the wind.
