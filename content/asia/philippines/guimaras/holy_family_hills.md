Holy Family Hills is a Catholic pilgrimage site on Guimaras, set on a rise with chapels, devotional statues, and Stations of the Cross spread through the grounds. It is a quiet counterpoint to the island's beaches and mango farms, and it draws both local worshippers and visitors looking for a reflective stop.

The setting is the main reason to go. Paths climb through the hillside complex to viewpoints over the surrounding countryside, making the site work as both a religious visit and a short scenic walk. It is most atmospheric outside the hottest part of the day.
