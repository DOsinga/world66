Nagarao is a small private island off the south-west coast with a long-running German-owned resort, a coral house reef, and dive shop. It is the only place on Guimaras with proper diving infrastructure. Day visitors can come over from Alubihod by arrangement; the snorkel just off the beach is the most rewarding within easy reach of the main island.
