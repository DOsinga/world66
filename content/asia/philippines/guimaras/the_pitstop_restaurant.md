The Pitstop in Jordan is the most famous restaurant on Guimaras, known across the country for its mango pizza. Beyond the gimmick, the kitchen turns out solid Filipino comfort food — sinigang, sisig, grilled tuna — and a long list of mango desserts. The setting is unfussy and the prices reasonable. A practical lunch stop between the wharf and the beaches.
