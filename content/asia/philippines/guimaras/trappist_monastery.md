Our Lady of the Philippines Trappist Abbey is the only Trappist monastery in the country, set in quiet grounds above the Iloilo Strait. The chapel is open to visitors during the day, and the monks' shop sells the famous mango jam, otap, and polvoron made on the premises. The setting is meditative and the products genuinely good; this is one of the most worthwhile stops in central Guimaras.
