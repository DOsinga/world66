Turtle Island is one of the small uninhabited islets on the standard island-hopping circuit from Alubihod and Raymen Beach. It has a tiny white-sand beach, a snorkelling spot in the shallows offshore, and shade from a few coconut palms. Forty-five minutes here is plenty; combine it with Ave Maria and SeaBird on the same boat trip.
