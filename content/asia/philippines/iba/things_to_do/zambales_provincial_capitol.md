The Zambales Provincial Capitol is the seat of the provincial government and the most architecturally composed building in Iba. The structure is set back from the main road on a small landscaped ground, with a formal facade that lends the town centre some measure of civic dignity. It is a working government building rather than a museum, but the grounds are accessible and the building itself is worth a look as you pass through the town centre.
