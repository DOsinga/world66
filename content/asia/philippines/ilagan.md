Ilagan is the capital of Isabela, stretched along the highway through the Cagayan Valley. It is a working provincial city first, not a polished tourist town, but it has enough character to reward a traveller already moving between Cauayan and Tuguegarao.

The main reason to stop is [Ilagan Sanctuary](/asia/philippines/ilagan/ilagan_sanctuary), a nature park inside Fuyot Springs National Park on the lower slopes of the Sierra Madre. It gives the city its green side: caves, springs, short hikes, and Pinzal Falls. Back in town, Ilagan leans into more human stories, from the [Japanese Tunnel](/asia/philippines/ilagan/ilagan_japanese_tunnel) of World War II to the [Giant Butaka](/asia/philippines/ilagan/giant_butaka), a massive version of the reclining chair linked to the city's furniture-making tradition.

Do not expect Ilagan to compete with the Philippines' beach towns or mountain resorts. Its appeal is local and slightly odd: a provincial capital where corn fields, furniture workshops, war memory, and a forest sanctuary sit within the same day's travel.
