The National Shrine of Our Lady of the Visitation of Guibang is one of Isabela's main Catholic pilgrimage sites. It is dedicated to a venerated image of the Virgin Mary, with the feast day marked on July 2.

Outside festival times it is a quiet church visit, but the shrine matters locally and adds a different note to Ilagan's caves, parks, and wartime sights.
