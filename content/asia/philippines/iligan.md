Iligan sits on the north coast of Mindanao, west of [Cagayan de Oro City](/asia/philippines/cagayan_de_oro_city), where the land rises quickly from Iligan Bay into limestone hills and river valleys. The city is industrial in places, but its travel identity is simple and strong: waterfalls.

Iligan calls itself the City of Majestic Waterfalls, and for once the slogan is not empty. [Maria Cristina Falls](/asia/philippines/iligan/maria_cristina_falls) is the famous one, a high Agus River drop tied to Mindanao's hydroelectric power. [Tinago Falls](/asia/philippines/iligan/tinago_falls) is the more beautiful swim, hidden below a long stairway in a green ravine. [Mimbalot Falls](/asia/philippines/iligan/mimbalot_falls) is smaller and easier to reach, while [Timoga Spring Pools](/asia/philippines/iligan/timoga_spring_pools) give you a cold-water break between falls.

This makes Iligan a strong day or overnight stop if you are already travelling through Northern Mindanao. It is not a polished resort city, and access to some falls can change with weather, maintenance, or power-plant rules. But for travelers who like water, short hikes, and local public-transport puzzles, it has enough substance to justify the detour.
