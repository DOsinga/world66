Mimbalot Falls is smaller than Maria Cristina and less dramatic than Tinago, but it is one of the easier waterfalls to work into an Iligan day. The falls slide and drop over a broad rock face in Buru-un, with pools and picnic areas nearby.

Come here when you want a less demanding stop, or use it as part of a waterfall loop with Tinago, Maria Cristina, and Timoga.
