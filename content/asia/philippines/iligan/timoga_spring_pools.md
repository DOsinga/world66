Timoga Spring Pools are a cluster of cold, spring-fed swimming pools in Buru-un. They are social rather than wild: families, food stalls, cottages, and icy clear water flowing down from the hills.

They make the most sense after a hot walk or as a softer stop for travelers who do not want another waterfall stair climb. Go on a weekday if you want the water without the weekend crowd.
