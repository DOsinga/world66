title: Eating Out
type: section
---

Iloilo has one of the most distinctive regional food cultures in the Philippines, anchored by la paz batchoy but extending through a range of dishes that use the local seafood and pork with characteristic Ilonggo seasoning.

**La Paz batchoy** is the dish to know: pork broth noodle soup with offal and chicharron, served at [Ted's La Paz Batchoy](/asia/philippines/iloilo/la_paz_market) at La Paz Market (the original) and at dozens of other spots around the city. Roberto's La Paz Batchoy and Bob's La Paz Batchoy are reliable alternatives with city-centre locations.

**KRI Restaurant** at the SM City Iloilo food court is known for its Ilonggo home cooking, including fresh lumpia and pork dishes. **Tatoy's Manokan and Seafoods** is the most-recommended place for grilled chicken and fresh seafood at moderate prices, with open-air dining near the Mandurriao fish market.

**Breakthrough Restaurant** near the airport road is consistently praised for its seafood at reasonable prices and its fresh catch displays — you select from the day's fish, crab, and shrimp before they're cooked.

For something more upscale, the restaurants in **Smallville Complex** and the **Iloilo Business Park** offer a wider range including Japanese, Korean, and Italian food aimed at the growing professional class.
