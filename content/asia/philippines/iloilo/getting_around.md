---
title: Getting Around
type: section
---

Iloilo City is relatively compact and manageable by the standard Philippine combination of jeepney, tricycle, and taxi.

**Jeepneys** are the main mode of transport and cover most major routes through the city. Fares are fixed and low. The challenge for newcomers is knowing which jeepney to take — routes are written on the vehicle but not always clearly. Asking a local or the driver is the most reliable method.

**Tricycles** (motorcycle with sidecar) serve shorter routes and the narrower streets in residential areas. They operate at fixed fares within a zone but can be chartered for longer trips.

**Taxis** are widely available and metered. The flag-down rate is low by international standards. Grab (the regional ride-hailing app) operates in Iloilo and is generally the most convenient option for visitors.

For visiting **Jaro district** (cathedral, belfry, heritage houses), a tricycle or taxi from the city centre is simplest. **Miagao Church** requires a bus or van from the terminal at Iloilo City Market, about 1.5 hours each way. **Guimaras Island** is reached by pump boat from Parola Wharf.
