Smallville Complex is Iloilo's main entertainment district, a strip of bars, restaurants, and clubs in the Mandurriao area that gets busy on Thursday and Friday evenings and is at its peak on weekends. The area attracts a mix of young professionals, university students, and visitors, and has a range of establishments from casual beer gardens to live music venues.

The character is informal and unpretentious — this is provincial Philippine nightlife rather than anything metropolitan, but the energy is genuine and the prices are substantially lower than in Manila. Several good restaurants in the complex serve Ilonggo food alongside the usual Filipino-Chinese and international options. The area is well-served by tricycles and jeepneys from most parts of the city.
