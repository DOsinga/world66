---
title: Things to Do
type: section
---

Iloilo's sights divide between the colonial heritage of the Jaro district — cathedral, belfry, and a cluster of sugar-wealth mansions — and the more modern attractions along the waterfront and in the business parks. The Miagao Church, forty kilometres west, is a UNESCO World Heritage Site worth the trip. In the evening, the river esplanade and Smallville Complex are the focus of city life.
