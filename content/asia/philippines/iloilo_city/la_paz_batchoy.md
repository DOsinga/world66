La Paz Market in the La Paz district is the original home of la paz batchoy, the noodle soup that put Iloilo on the Filipino food map. The market is a busy public market from early morning, selling fresh produce, meat, fish, and cooked food, but the main reason to visit is Ted's La Paz Batchoy — the original restaurant founded in the 1940s, still at the same stall inside the market.

The batchoy is made with egg noodles in a rich pork stock with pork offal (liver, kidney, heart), chopped fresh pork, and crushed chicharron on top; a raw egg is cracked into the bowl and stirred in by the diner. Eat it in the morning when the stock is freshest. The surrounding market stalls are worth a browse for fresh vegetables and local snacks.
