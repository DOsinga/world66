Lizares Mansion is one of Iloilo's notable heritage houses, a reminder of the wealth that flowed through the city and surrounding sugar country in the early 20th century. Its scale and ornament stand out even in a city with a strong collection of old houses and civic buildings.

The mansion is now associated with institutional use, so access may be limited, but the exterior alone is worth noting if you are tracing Iloilo's architectural history. It pairs well with Jaro's churches, old houses, and the broader story of Iloilo as one of the Philippines' great heritage cities.
