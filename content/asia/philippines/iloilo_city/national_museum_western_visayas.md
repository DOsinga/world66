National Museum Western Visayas gives Iloilo City a strong civic museum stop, with regional archaeology, natural history, and cultural exhibits in a restored landmark building. It is a useful introduction to Panay and the wider Western Visayas before exploring churches, markets, and heritage districts.

The museum is manageable in size and works well with a walk through Iloilo's historic core. Check opening days, as National Museum branches may close on holidays or for installation work.
