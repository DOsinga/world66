San Jose Church stands beside Plaza Libertad, placing it right in the civic and religious heart of old Iloilo. Its stone facade, twin towers, and active parish life make it one of the central stops for understanding the city's Spanish-era layout.

It is easy to combine with a walk through the downtown heritage area. Step inside if the church is open, but even from the plaza it helps anchor the story of Iloilo as a trading city shaped by Catholic institutions, civic squares, and old commercial streets.
