WaterWorld Iloilo is a straightforward family water park on the edge of the city, useful when the heat makes another church or museum a hard sell. Slides, pools, and day-use facilities are the draw rather than any deep cultural story.

It is most relevant for families or travellers with spare time in Iloilo, not as a reason to visit the city by itself. Check current opening days and weather before making the trip out.
