---
title: "Getting There"
type: section
---

**By bus from Manila.** Direct buses (Victory Liner, Coda Lines) run from Cubao to Tabuk overnight, taking 10–12 hours. The mountain section north of Baguio is slow and winding — if you are prone to motion sickness, take medication.

**By plane via Tuguegarao.** Several daily flights from Manila to [Tuguegarao](/asia/philippines/tuguegaraocity) (1 hour). From there, jeepneys and vans run to Tabuk in about 90 minutes through the lowland valley.

**Onward to the mountain villages.** From Tabuk you transfer to local jeepneys or hired vans for the trip up to Tinglayan, Lubuagan, and the trailheads for Buscalan and other terraced villages. Allow several hours; the roads are rough and rain disrupts schedules.

**Note on security.** Travel in Kalinga is generally fine but the New People's Army has been active in remote areas. Stick to organised guides and respect locally posted advice — both the police and the indigenous *bodong* council know exactly which areas are quiet at any given time.
