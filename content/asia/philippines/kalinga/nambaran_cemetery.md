Nambaran Cemetery is an unusual roadside stop near Tabuk, known for the sculptures and personal artworks placed on some of its graves. The result is more local folk art than solemn monument, and passing motorists often stop for a look.

It is worth a short detour if you are travelling through Kalinga by road. Be respectful: this is still an active cemetery, not an outdoor gallery.
