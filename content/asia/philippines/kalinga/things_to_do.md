---
title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Kalinga is a mountain province for travellers willing to make the effort: whitewater on the Chico River, treks into living indigenous villages, and rice terraces less famous (and far less visited) than those at [Banaue](/asia/philippines/banaue) but cut from the same dramatic landscape. Most visits begin in Tabuk, the provincial capital, and push west and north into the highlands from there.
