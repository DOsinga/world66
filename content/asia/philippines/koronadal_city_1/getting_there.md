---
title: "Getting There"
type: section
---

Koronadal is served by General Santos Airport (GES), about 30 kilometres to the south. Several airlines including Cebu Pacific and Philippine Airlines fly the route from Manila; the flight takes about 1 hour 45 minutes. From General Santos airport, vans and buses run to Koronadal in about 45 minutes. There is no airport in Koronadal itself.

Overland connections within Mindanao are good. Buses and vans run frequently from General Santos to Koronadal throughout the day. From Davao, the journey takes about 2.5-3 hours by bus or van via the highway. Ceres and other bus lines connect the city to other major towns in the region. The terminal for most overland services is in or near the city centre.

Security conditions in Mindanao should be checked before travel. South Cotabato, where Koronadal is located, has been generally stable, but the broader region has a complex history of conflict in certain areas. The Philippine government, most embassies, and travel advisories maintain current guidance.
