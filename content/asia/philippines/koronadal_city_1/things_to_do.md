---
title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Koronadal is an administrative centre rather than a destination in itself, and the honest advice is to use it as a base rather than a focus. The city is clean, safe, and orderly by Mindanao standards, but the real reasons to be in this part of the Philippines — the T'boli highlands around Lake Sebu, the waterfalls of Surallah, the coast at General Santos — are all within an hour or two by road.

Within the city, the **Koronadal Cultural Center Museum** has exhibits on the T'boli people, South Cotabato's Indigenous communities, and local history. The **Koronadal Public Market** is the best place to browse local produce and pick up T'boli textiles and beadwork at reasonable prices. The **Lake Sebu road** out of the city climbs into increasingly beautiful country as you head southwest, and the drive itself is worth doing.
