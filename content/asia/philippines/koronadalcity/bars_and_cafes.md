---
title: Bars and Cafes
type: section
---

Koronadal is a provincial city, not a nightlife destination, but it has a handful of cafes worth knowing and a growing cluster of small bars around the commercial centre. The cafe culture here runs on local robusta coffee from Mindanao's highlands and the sweet, milky drinks that dominate Filipino cafe menus — but a good strong brewed cup is always available if you ask. Evenings are relaxed: cold beer with grilled food, or a cafe that stays open late.
