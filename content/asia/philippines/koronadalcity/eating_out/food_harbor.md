Food Harbor sits close to the main bus terminal and is the kind of reliable, no-fuss Filipino eatery that feeds travellers and locals equally well. The menu spans Filipino comfort food — sinigang, kare-kare, grilled liempo — with a few international plates that have been given a local twist. The pig's ear sisig is a local favourite: crispy, vinegar-sharp, and best eaten with a heap of steaming rice.

Prices are modest, portions are generous, and the staff are used to travellers with buses to catch. A good first or last meal in Koronadal before heading on to General Santos or Davao.
