Lantaw Marbel earns its reputation as Koronadal's most scenic dining spot by sitting on a hillside above the city with views across the valley. The food matches the setting — the whole roasted pig (lechon) is the standout dish, crackling-skinned and slow-cooked over coals, and the stuffed milkfish and coconut-braised chicken are consistent draws. The menu leans on Mindanao produce: pineapple from the surrounding farms turns up in marinades and sauces throughout.

The open-air pavilion is best at dusk, when the city lights start to come on below and the air cools. Book ahead on weekends, or expect to wait. Dress comfortably — this is the Philippines, not fine dining, but it does feel like a special occasion.
