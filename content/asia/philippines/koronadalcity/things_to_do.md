---
title: Things to Do
type: section
---

Koronadal's sights are modest by big-city standards, but the city rewards slow exploration. The cathedral and the central plaza form the civic heart, while the Marbel Public Market offers the most honest window into daily life in South Cotabato. For deeper cultural immersion, the tribal villages and crafts centres on the city's edges bring visitors face to face with B'laan traditions that persist despite decades of change.
