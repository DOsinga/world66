Within Las Piñas, the main transport options are jeepney, tricycle, and Grab (the dominant ride-share app in the Philippines). Jeepneys cover the main roads and are the cheapest way to get between areas, but routes can be confusing for first-time visitors. Tricycles (motorcycle sidecars) serve shorter distances and are good for getting from a main road to specific destinations like the church or the wetland park.

For most sights within Las Piñas — the Bamboo Organ and Saint Joseph's Church, the Sarao Jeepney Factory, and the Wetland Park — Grab is the most straightforward option for visitors. Fares within the city are modest.

The LRT Line 1 runs along the eastern edge of the city with a stop at Dr. Santos, useful for reaching the Bamboo Organ area and for connections to central Manila and beyond.
