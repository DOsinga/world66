The Sarao Motors factory in Las Piñas is the most famous producer of the classic Filipino jeepney, the chrome-and-chromatic public transport vehicle that has been a symbol of the Philippines for over 70 years. Visitors can tour the factory floor and see jeepneys in various stages of construction — the handbuilt process involves bodywork fabrication, painting in bright colours and decorative motifs, and installation of the mechanical components.

The factory also functions as a showroom, with completed jeepneys on display that can be examined and photographed. Tours are informal and can usually be arranged by arriving during business hours on weekdays. The jeepney industry faces pressure from the Philippine government's modernisation programme, which is phasing out older vehicles in favour of Euro 4-compliant engines — making a visit to Sarao increasingly poignant as a record of traditional craft manufacturing.
