Mahagnao Volcano National Park protects an inactive volcanic landscape in the interior of Leyte, with crater lakes, forest, and geothermal features that show why the volcano is still classed as potentially active.

The park is a worthwhile nature stop for travellers with time beyond Tacloban and Ormoc. Plan transport carefully, as access is more involved than visiting coastal memorials or city markets, and conditions can change after heavy rain.
