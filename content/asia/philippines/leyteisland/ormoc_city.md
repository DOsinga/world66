Ormoc is a chartered city on the west coast of [Leyte](/asia/philippines/leyteisland), known as the sugar capital of the Eastern Visayas. The surrounding countryside is lush with sugarcane fields and the city serves as a gateway to [Lake Danao National Park](/asia/philippines/leyteisland/ormoc_city/lake_danao_national_park) in the highlands above town.

The city was devastated by Typhoon Haiyan in 2013 but has rebuilt with determination. Today it is a functional, friendly stop for travellers heading between Cebu and the Leyte interior — ferries run regularly from [Ormoc Port](/asia/philippines/leyteisland/ormoc_city/ormoc_port) to Cebu City. The [public market](/asia/philippines/leyteisland/ormoc_city/ormoc_city_public_market) near the harbour is the liveliest part of town in the mornings.
