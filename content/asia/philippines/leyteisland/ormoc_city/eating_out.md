---
title: Eating Out
type: section
tags: [eating_out]
---

Ormoc is not a culinary destination, but the fresh seafood is excellent and the local Filipino cooking is reliable. The waterfront area and the streets near the public market have the most active concentration of eateries. Expect simple, family-run places serving rice plates, grilled fish, sinigang, and adobo.

The SM Center food court offers more variety for those who want airconditioned comfort. For fresh fish at its best, buy from the market in the morning and look for the stalls that will cook what you bring.
