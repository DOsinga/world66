Ormoc's port is both a working ferry terminal and a pleasant waterfront. RORO ferries and fast craft run daily to Cebu City — a journey of between two and five hours depending on the vessel — making Ormoc a natural transit point for travellers crossing between [Leyte](/asia/philippines/leyteisland) and the Cebu island group.

At dusk, the waterfront promenade comes to life with locals taking the evening air. Food stalls set up along the esplanade selling grilled fish, balut, and fresh coconut. It is an easy, informal place to spend an hour before or after the ferry.
