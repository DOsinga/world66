Ormoc sits between the sea and the mountains, and the city's highlights reflect that geography. Lake Danao is the main draw for most visitors — a highland crater lake in a national park about 20 kilometres northeast of the city centre. The drive up is scenic, with views across sugarcane country. At the lake itself you can rent bangka boats and paddle among the floating islands of vegetation.

The city's waterfront has been improved since the devastation of Typhoon Haiyan and the port area is pleasant to walk at dusk. The public market near the harbour is lively in the early morning, full of fresh fish, root vegetables, and the sugar products the region produces.
