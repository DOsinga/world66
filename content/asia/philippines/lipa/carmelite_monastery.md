The Carmelite Monastery of the Most Holy Trinity in central Lipa is the site of the famous 1948 apparitions, when a novice named Teresita Castillo reported visions of the Virgin Mary and rose petals bearing miraculous images fell in the convent grounds. The events drew enormous crowds at the time and remain a subject of active Catholic devotion in the Philippines.

The monastery is an enclosed contemplative community, meaning the nuns have no regular contact with the outside world. A public chapel allows visitors to pray at the site. The grounds include a small shrine to Our Lady of Lipa. Whatever your views on the supernatural, the monastery has an atmosphere of unusual calm, and the story of the apparitions and the decades-long debate about their authenticity is a genuinely interesting piece of Philippine Catholic history.
