San Sebastian Cathedral is the seat of the Archdiocese of Lipa, rebuilt after wartime destruction but still carrying the scale and ceremony of an older provincial cathedral. Inside, the high nave, cupola, and stained glass make it one of the city's most rewarding architectural stops.
