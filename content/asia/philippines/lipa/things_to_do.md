---
title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Lipa's main draws are its religious sites, its heritage houses, and its coffee. The cathedral and the nearby Carmelite Monastery are the spiritual centre of a city with a strong Catholic identity, while the Casa de Segunda gives a glimpse of 19th-century ilustrado life. Coffee farm tours and the highland scenery are reasons to stay longer than a transit stop would suggest.
