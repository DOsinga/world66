The International Rice Research Institute (IRRI), founded in 1960, is the world's leading rice research centre. The campus is adjacent to the UPLB campus and is open to visitors. The main attraction for visitors is the Rice World Museum, which traces the history of rice cultivation from ancient times to the present and displays the scientific breakthroughs that have shaped global food security.

The museum covers rice biology, traditional cultivation techniques, the breeding work that produced high-yield varieties, and the current challenges of climate change and food security. It's informative and well-designed, aimed at both specialists and general visitors. Tours of the seed bank and research facilities can be arranged by appointment. Entry to the Rice World Museum is free.
