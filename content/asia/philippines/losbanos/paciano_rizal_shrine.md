Paciano Rizal Shrine preserves the 1927 house of Paciano Rizal, Jose Rizal's older brother and a general in the Philippine Revolution and the Philippine-American War. The residence remains privately owned by Rizal's heirs, but it contains a small museum with personal effects and material on Paciano's life.

Visits normally require prior arrangement, so it is best for travellers with a specific interest in Philippine revolutionary history or the Rizal family rather than a casual drop-in stop.
