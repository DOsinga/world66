The main draws are [Mount Makiling](/asia/philippines/losbanos/mount_makiling) and the [hot springs](/asia/philippines/losbanos/hot_springs) — the volcano and the thermal waters that it produces. A soak in one of the resort pools, followed by a walk on the [UPLB campus](/asia/philippines/losbanos/uplb_campus) or a visit to [IRRI](/asia/philippines/losbanos/irri), makes a straightforward day.

The [IRRI Rice World Museum](/asia/philippines/losbanos/irri) is genuinely interesting and free — the history of the Green Revolution in Asia told through rice breeding. The [Raid on Los Baños Memorial](/asia/philippines/losbanos/raid_on_los_banos_memorial) marks one of the most celebrated rescue operations of the Pacific War. [Tadlac Lake](/asia/philippines/losbanos/tadlac_lake) is a quiet spot for kayaking and birdwatching. [Laguna de Bay](/asia/philippines/losbanos/laguna_de_bay) is vast and impressive from the shore.

Before leaving, stock up on [buko pie](/asia/philippines/losbanos/buko_pie) — the local coconut custard tart that defines this part of Laguna province.
