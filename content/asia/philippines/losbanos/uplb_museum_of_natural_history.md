The UPLB Museum of Natural History presents biological and zoological collections from the University of the Philippines Los Banos. It is a practical stop for visitors interested in the biodiversity of Laguna, Mount Makiling, and the wider Philippines.

Expect a campus museum rather than a large city institution, and check hours before making a special trip.
