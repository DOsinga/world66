The UPLB Museum of Natural History is a compact science stop on the Los Banos university campus, with biological and zoological collections tied to the forests, farms, and wetlands around Mount Makiling. It works best if you are already exploring the campus or visiting the nearby research institutes, and gives useful context to the area's unusually rich natural life.
