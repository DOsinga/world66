Washington SyCip Park is a pocket of green in Legazpi Village, close to Greenbelt and the office towers that define central Makati. It is small, orderly, and landscaped rather than wild, but the shade and quiet make it a useful reset in one of Metro Manila's densest business districts.

Come for a short morning walk, a coffee break, or a calm pause between galleries, malls, and restaurants. The park is named for businessman Washington SyCip and is managed as a public urban space, so it feels more polished than spontaneous, but that polish is part of its Makati character.
