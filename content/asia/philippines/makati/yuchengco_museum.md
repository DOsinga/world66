Yuchengco Museum adds cultural weight to Makati's office towers and malls. Its exhibitions and collections focus on Philippine art, design, diplomacy, and cultural history, giving business-district visitors a museum option beyond the larger Ayala Museum nearby.

It is a second-tier stop in Makati rather than the first museum to choose, but it is worthwhile when exhibitions align with your interests. Its central location makes it easy to fit between meetings, shopping, or a walk through the Ayala side of the district.
