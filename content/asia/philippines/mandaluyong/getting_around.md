---
title: "Getting Around"
type: section
---

The MRT-3 line runs the length of EDSA and is by far the fastest way to move around Mandaluyong and connect to the rest of Metro Manila. The Boni and Shaw Boulevard stations are the main stops. Jeepneys cover the smaller streets and are cheap but slow; ride-hailing apps (Grab) are the easiest option for distances the MRT doesn't cover. Traffic on EDSA can be severe at peak hours — factor in extra time and use the train whenever possible.
