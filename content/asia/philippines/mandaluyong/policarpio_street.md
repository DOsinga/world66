Policarpio Street is best known in Metro Manila for its Christmas-season house displays, when residents decorate facades with lights, figures, and festive scenes that draw evening visitors. Outside the season it is an ordinary residential street, which is part of why the transformation is memorable.

Its travel value is strongly seasonal. Visit only if you are in Mandaluyong during the holiday display period, go respectfully, and remember that the attraction depends on a lived-in neighbourhood rather than a formal tourist site.
