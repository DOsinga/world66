San Felipe Neri Parish Church is Mandaluyong's historic parish church, built in 1863 and dedicated to the city's patron saint. During the Philippine Revolution, Katipunan revolutionaries used the church area as a relay point for information, giving it a role beyond its religious life.

The church is a worthwhile stop if you are tracing Mandaluyong's older civic and religious core, especially in contrast with the malls and office towers that now define much of the city.
