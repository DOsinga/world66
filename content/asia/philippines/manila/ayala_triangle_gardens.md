A wedge of greenery and old acacia trees in the middle of the Makati business district, Ayala Triangle is where office workers jog and lunch. Its restored 1949 Nielson Tower and ring of restaurants make it a pleasant pause; in December it hosts the city's best Christmas light-and-sound show.
