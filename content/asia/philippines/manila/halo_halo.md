Halo-halo (Tagalog for "mix-mix") is one of the great shaved-ice desserts of Asia, and in Manila's heat it is a genuine necessity. The standard version fills a tall glass with shaved ice over a base of sweetened red beans, garbanzos, nata de coco (coconut jelly), macapuno (soft coconut), kaong (sugar palm fruit), jackfruit, and sweet potato, topped with ube (purple yam) ice cream, a drizzle of condensed milk, and a shard of leche flan. You stir it together before eating — hence the name.

Every fast-food chain and market stall has a version. Razon's of Guagua is generally considered one of the best in Manila, with a simpler version that focuses on the quality of its components. Aristocrat Restaurant on Roxas Boulevard serves a classic version in a historic setting. Order one in the mid-afternoon heat; it will briefly make the city seem manageable.
