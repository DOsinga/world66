Set in a restored colonial house on Cabildo Street, Ilustrado is Intramuros's most refined table — Filipino-Spanish cooking (paella, callos, the signature Ilustrado coffee) served amid old-Manila elegance. A favourite for a long lunch between the walled city's sights.
