Legarda Ancestral Mansion is a heritage house in Manila with period rooms, art, and family collections connected to the city's old elite neighborhoods. The house includes works associated with major Filipino painters such as Juan Luna and Felix Resurreccion Hidalgo.

It is a compact but worthwhile stop for visitors interested in Manila's domestic architecture and cultural history.
