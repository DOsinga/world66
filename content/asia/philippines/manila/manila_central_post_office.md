The Manila Central Post Office Building is one of the grand civic landmarks from the American colonial period. Its long Neoclassical facade, columns, and riverside setting make it one of the most imposing public buildings in central Manila.

The building suffered a major fire in May 2023, so check its current condition before planning around an interior visit. Even from the outside, it helps explain the scale and ambition of early twentieth-century government architecture in Manila.
