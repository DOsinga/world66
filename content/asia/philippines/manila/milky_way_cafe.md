A Makati institution since 1962, Milky Way is the dessert stop that locals send you to for halo-halo — the great Filipino shaved-ice sundae of beans, jellies, leche flan, and ube, done the old-fashioned way. The upstairs restaurant serves solid Filipino mains too.
