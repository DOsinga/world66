The National Museum of Anthropology is part of Manila's National Museum complex, just off Rizal Park. It focuses on the cultures of the Philippines, with galleries of textiles, ritual objects, boats, weapons, musical instruments, and archaeological finds from across the archipelago.

It pairs well with the neighbouring National Museum of Fine Arts and National Museum of Natural History. Come here when you want a clearer sense of how many different peoples and traditions sit inside the idea of the Philippines.
