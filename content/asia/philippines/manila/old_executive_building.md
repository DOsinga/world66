The Old Executive Building is an American colonial government building whose stonework still carries United States emblems from the period. It later served Philippine administrations, including that of Manuel L. Quezon, before becoming part of Manila's museum landscape. It is a useful stop if you are tracing the capital's layered colonial and republican history.
