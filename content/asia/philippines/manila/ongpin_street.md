Ongpin is the spine of Binondo, a narrow, crowded street of Chinese bakeries, herbalists, tea sellers, and gold dealers under a tangle of signs. Walk it slowly, ideally hungry — the hopia shops, dumpling houses, and fruit stalls are the whole point.
