Pasig City Museum occupies a former colonial mansion beside Plaza Rizal and tells the story of Pasig's local history, culture, and civic life. The building itself is part of the appeal, giving the museum more character than a standard municipal display.

It is a modest stop, but worthwhile if you are already in Pasig or want a less obvious history museum away from central Manila's main sights.
