Quinta Market, rebuilt as a bright hall beside the Pasig at the foot of Quezon Bridge, is Quiapo's main market — wet stalls of fish and produce downstairs and cooked-food carinderias where you eat cheaply on plastic stools. This is where Quiapo's street food really lives, with the overflow of fabrics, hardware, and folk remedies spilling into the surrounding streets.
