Santa Cruz Church is one of the main landmarks of Manila's Santa Cruz district. The Jesuits first built and administered a church here in the Spanish period, and the present church anchors the busy streets near Plaza Santa Cruz.

It is an easy stop when moving between Quiapo, Binondo, and the older parts of central Manila.
