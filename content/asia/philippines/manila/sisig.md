Sisig is what Manila eats late at night with cold beer. The original version from Pampanga uses chopped pig's face and ears, seasoned with calamansi (Philippine lime), chillies, and onions, served sizzling on a cast-iron plate. Manila versions often include chicken liver, egg, or other additions; the sizzle as the plate arrives is part of the experience.

The dish is found everywhere in Manila, from basic carinderia (local eateries) to upscale restaurants doing premium versions. Aling Lucing's in Pampanga is credited with inventing the modern sizzling version in the 1970s. In Manila, bars and restaurants in Makati, BGC, and Malate serve reliable versions. Order it with rice and cold San Miguel Pale Pilsen — the standard accompaniment — for the complete local experience. Sisig is also increasingly available in vegetarian and seafood versions, but the pork original is the one worth seeking out.
