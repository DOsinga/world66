The Mind Museum is a major science museum in Bonifacio Global City, with interactive galleries covering subjects such as the universe, life, atoms, technology, and the earth. Its sharp contemporary building is part of the appeal.

It is one of the stronger family stops in Metro Manila, especially on a hot or rainy day. Pair it with the nearby BGC parks, restaurants, or the Manila American Cemetery if you are spending the day in the district.
