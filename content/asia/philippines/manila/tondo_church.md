Tondo Church is one of Manila's most visited churches, with roots going back to 1572. It is best known for its image of the Santo Niño, said to have been brought from Acapulco during the Manila-Acapulco galleon trade.

The church is a meaningful stop if you are exploring the older northern districts of Manila, especially around Divisoria and Binondo.
