The Aga Khan Museum of Islamic Arts, on the Mindanao State University campus, holds the most significant collection of Maranao material culture anywhere — brassware (*gador* containers, betel boxes, the famous tobacco trays), heavily-decorated *kulintang* gong sets, *malong* textiles, wooden architectural fragments salvaged from old *torogan* royal houses, and ceremonial weaponry. There is also an archaeological section covering pre-Islamic Mindanao.

The museum is small (a couple of rooms) but the collection is the real thing rather than a tourist display — university curators have built it from family donations since the 1960s. Modest admission. The campus is the safest part of the city for foreign visitors and the museum is the single most worthwhile stop in Marawi.
