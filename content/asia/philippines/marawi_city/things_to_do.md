title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Marawi's tourist infrastructure was thin before the 2017 siege and is thinner now. The real reasons to come are cultural — Lake Lanao, the Maranao town life around the lakeshore, and the museum collection at Mindanao State University — not a list of sights. Check current travel advisories before you go; the security situation has stabilised but is not entirely settled.
