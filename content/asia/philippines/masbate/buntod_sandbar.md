Buntod Sandbar is a strip of white sand that appears at low tide in the waters south of Masbate City, surrounded by the shallow reefs of Buntod Reef Marine Sanctuary. At its peak, the sandbar stretches for several hundred metres and is narrow enough that you can see water on both sides — the kind of classic tropical beach that Instagram was made for. The reef around it is good for snorkelling, with reasonable coral cover and the fish populations that come with protected waters.

Boats run to the sandbar from the pier in Masbate City in about 20 minutes; several operators offer snorkelling packages with gear. The trip is best timed around low tide, when the sandbar is at its widest. On weekends and holidays it can be busy with local day-trippers; on weekdays you may have it almost to yourself.
