A large devotional statue of Jesus on a hilltop south of the city centre, one of Cagayan de Oro's most visited sites. The Christ of the Divine Mercy stands 50 feet tall on a base that encloses a small chapel and a stairway leading up through the statue itself — an unusual structural conceit that draws both pilgrims and the curious. The hilltop location gives views over the city and Macajalar Bay on clear days.

To reach it, take a jeepney heading south toward Consolacion or hire a tricycle from the city centre (about 30 pesos). The site is open to visitors throughout the day and is free, though donations are accepted. Dress modestly as this is an active place of worship.
