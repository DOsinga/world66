---
title: Shopping
type: section
---

The Divisoria market district is the place for budget shopping — textiles, cheap clothing, dried goods, and local produce fill the stalls around Plaza Divisoria and the surrounding streets. For malls, Limketkai Center and SM CDO Downtown are the main options, carrying international and Philippine chain brands alongside food courts and cinemas.

Locally produced items worth looking for include woven native crafts from nearby Misamis Oriental communities, and dried fish and preserved seafood from the markets near the port.
