Davao Museum is a compact introduction to the cultural mix of Davao and southeastern Mindanao. Its displays focus on local history, indigenous communities, textiles, beadwork, weapons, and everyday objects, giving useful context before you head into the wider region or to the markets and old civic core.

The museum is not a blockbuster, but that is part of its usefulness. It gives names and texture to the Lumad and Moro traditions that are often flattened into generic Mindanao folklore, and it works well as a short indoor stop when the heat or rain makes wandering less appealing.
