La Laguna Viewpoint is a small rocky lookout between Big La Laguna and Small La Laguna. A short path and steps lead up from the shoreline route to a concrete pad on the outcrop.

The stop is brief, but the view over the coves, beaches, and nearby islands makes it a worthwhile pause while walking around Sabang Beach.
