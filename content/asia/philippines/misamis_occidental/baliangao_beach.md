Baliangao, at the northern tip of Misamis Occidental, has the most rewarding stretch of beach in the province: a long bar of white sand on the seaward side of a protected mangrove area, reached by a wooden boardwalk through the trees. The water is clear, the reef is alive, and the village atmosphere is unhurried.

The **Baliangao Wetland Park** is the formal name for the protected area, and the cottages and small homestays here are the standard base for a weekend trip from Oroquieta or Ozamiz. Snorkelling gear is rentable on the beach; boats can be hired for trips to the offshore sandbars at low tide.
