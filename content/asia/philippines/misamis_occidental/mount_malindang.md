Mount Malindang dominates the centre of Misamis Occidental — a deeply weathered volcanic range whose highest peak (Mt. Malindang itself) reaches 2425 metres. The mountain and the surrounding 53,000 hectares of montane forest form the **Mount Malindang Range Natural Park**, one of the most biodiverse protected areas in Mindanao, with Philippine eagles, tarsiers, and a long list of endemic plants.

Climbing the mountain is a serious 3- to 4-day expedition through montane and mossy forest, requiring a guide and permits from the protected-area office in Don Victoriano. Day-trip access is easier from the western side at Lake Duminagat, a small caldera lake at about 1300 metres reached by a long drive plus a half-day walk through the forest.
