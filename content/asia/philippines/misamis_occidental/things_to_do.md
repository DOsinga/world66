Misamis Occidental's draws are spread across the three main cities — [Oroquieta](/asia/philippines/misamis_occidental/oroquieta_city), the provincial capital; [Ozamiz](/asia/philippines/misamis_occidental/ozamiz_city), the largest centre and main commercial port; and [Tangub](/asia/philippines/misamis_occidental/tangub_city), known for its Christmas light displays — together with the inland uplands and the long coastline on Iligan Bay. The [Cotta Fort](/asia/philippines/misamis_occidental/cotta_fort) at Ozamiz is the most photographed historical site; the [Hoyohoy Highland](/asia/philippines/misamis_occidental/hoyohoy_highland) above Tangub gives the best mountain views; and the beaches of [Baliangao](/asia/philippines/misamis_occidental/baliangao_beach) on the northern coast are the most rewarding stretch of sand. A short ferry from Ozamiz reaches Mukas in Lanao del Norte and the road south to Iligan and Cagayan de Oro.
