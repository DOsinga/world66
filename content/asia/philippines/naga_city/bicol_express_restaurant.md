One of Naga's most enduring institutions for Bicolano cooking, this straightforward restaurant on Magsaysay Avenue has been serving the namesake dish for decades. Bicol Express here is the real thing — fatty pork, shrimp paste, coconut milk, and a quantity of fresh chilli that most visitors will find eye-wateringly hot even by Philippine standards. The rest of the menu covers laing, pinangat, and the full roster of Bicol staples at prices that reflect the carinderia tradition rather than a restaurant premium.

The setting is simple and the service is fast. Come at lunchtime when the dishes are freshest and the dining room fills with office workers and students who clearly know what they're doing.
