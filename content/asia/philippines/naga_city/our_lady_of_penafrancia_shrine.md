Our Lady of Peñafrancia Shrine is the older home of the devotion to Ina in Naga, built around the image brought to the Bicol region in the early 18th century. It is especially important during the Peñafrancia festival, when the Traslacion procession begins here before moving toward Naga Metropolitan Cathedral.
