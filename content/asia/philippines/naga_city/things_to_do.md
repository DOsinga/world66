---
title: "Things to Do in Naga City"
type: section
---

Naga City's main draw is its strong Catholic religious heritage, culminating in the extraordinary Peñafrancia Festival each September. Beyond the festival, the city has a compact and walkable downtown with historic churches, a pleasant riverside, and the vibrant energy of a university town. The surrounding Camarines Sur province offers more adventurous options — the Caramoan Peninsula for island-hopping and the slopes of Bulusan Volcano for hiking — making Naga a good base for the Bicol region.
