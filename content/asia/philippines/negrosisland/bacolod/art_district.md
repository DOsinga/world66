Bacolod Art District is a small creative cluster around Lacson Street and Santa Clara Avenue in Barangay Mandalagan. It mixes galleries, murals, studios, cafes, and bars, with a stronger local-artist feel than the city's malls and heritage houses.

The district is best in the late afternoon or evening, when more doors are open and the painted walls have some life around them. It is not a formal museum precinct, so come ready to wander, look for current exhibitions, and treat it as a snapshot of contemporary Bacolod rather than a fixed attraction.
