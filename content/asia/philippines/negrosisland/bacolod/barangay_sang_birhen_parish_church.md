Barangay Sang Birhen Parish Church is notable for its shellwork interior. The centrepiece is Leticia Sia Ledesma's "Barangay Sang Birhen" mosaic, made from tens of thousands of shells, with more shell images used for saints, Stations of the Cross, and altar pieces.
