Buro-Buro and Bocal-Bocal Springs are local swimming springs outside Bacolod, with cool water despite the boiling sound of the Bocal-Bocal name. The site has artificial and natural pools, small waterfalls near the main pool, and huts for rent.

It is a practical family outing rather than a wild nature escape, popular for picnics, barbecues, and reunions.
