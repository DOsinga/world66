Caribbean Water Park is a family water park in Bacolod with a main pool, kiddie pool, jacuzzi, large slides, and a pirate-ship play area. It is aimed mainly at families and groups looking for a half-day break from sightseeing.

The attraction is more about easy recreation than local heritage, but it is one of the clearer family options in the city.
