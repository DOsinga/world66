Negros Forests and Ecological Foundation is a small conservation centre focused on threatened wildlife from Negros Island. Visitors can see rare species such as warty pigs, leopard cats, spotted deer, and bleeding-heart pigeons, and the visit is most rewarding if you are interested in the island's fragile forests rather than a conventional zoo.
