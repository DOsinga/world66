Sacred Heart Church and Seminary is a Gothic Revival-style church in Bacolod, known for its tall spire, high interior, and European-inspired lines. Side chapels are dedicated to the Sacred Heart of Jesus and the Virgin Mary.

It is a short architectural and devotional stop rather than a large sightseeing complex, but it adds variety to a Bacolod itinerary focused on churches, heritage houses, and city landmarks.
