The Ruins is the burned-out skeleton of a grand early 20th-century mansion in Talisay, about 12 kilometres north of Bacolod. The ornate Italianate columns and neoclassical facade survived the wartime fire that gutted the building and have stood for nearly a century in the middle of sugarcane fields — one of the most romantically melancholy sights in the Philippines.

The site is now a popular destination, particularly at sunset when the old stone columns turn golden in the light. The grounds are well-maintained with walking paths through the surrounding gardens. A restaurant and events space operate within the complex. It is the single attraction that most visitors to Bacolod list as unmissable, and the ten-minute drive from the city centre is well worth it. Entry requires a ticket.
