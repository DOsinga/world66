Kabankalan is the second largest city in Negros Occidental, sitting in the southern part of the island about two hours by bus from Bacolod. It's a working agricultural city rather than a tourist hub — the surrounding lowlands are blanketed in sugarcane, and the mountains to the east shelter communities of the Bukidnon people. Most travelers pass through en route to the [Kanlaon Natural Park](/asia/philippines/negrosisland/kabankalan_city/kanlaon_natural_park) or the mountain barangays, but the city itself has a genuine unhurried charm and a useful base of services.

The city center is compact and easy to navigate on foot. The [Cathedral of Saint Francis Xavier](/asia/philippines/negrosisland/kabankalan_city/kabankalan_cathedral) and the plaza around it are the civic heart of town, and [Central Philippines State University](/asia/philippines/negrosisland/kabankalan_city/central_philippines_state_university) adds a student presence that gives the city a livelier cafe scene than its size might suggest. The most compelling reason to stop here is the natural landscape: [Mag-aso Falls](/asia/philippines/negrosisland/kabankalan_city/mag_aso_falls) in the eastern mountains is one of the more accessible waterfalls in the region. Accommodation options are limited but adequate for a night's stop.
