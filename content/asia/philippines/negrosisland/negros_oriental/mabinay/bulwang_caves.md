Bulwang is the usual beginner caving area, with guided visits to Panligawan, Pandalihan, and Crystal Cave. The appeal is not a single vast chamber but the variety: easy passages, white formations, bats, swiftlets, and enough mud and rough rock to make the trip feel like a real outing.
