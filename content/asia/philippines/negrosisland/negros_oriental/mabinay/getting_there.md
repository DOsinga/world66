---
title: Getting There
type: section
---

Mabinay sits on the cross-island road between [Dumaguete](/asia/philippines/negrosisland/negros_oriental) and Bacolod. Buses run between the two cities and pass through Mabinay; from Dumaguete the ride is usually around two to three hours, depending on stops and road conditions.

The road is scenic but winding. If you are self-driving or hiring a motorbike, avoid doing the hill sections after dark.
