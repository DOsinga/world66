Odloman is the serious cave in the Mabinay set, an 8 km-plus system that is often described as one of the longest in the Philippines. It is not for a casual walk-in visit: access can involve swimming across underground water or using ropes, so treat it as a guided expedition rather than a sightseeing stop.
