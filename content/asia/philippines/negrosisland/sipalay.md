Sipalay sits on the southwest coast of Negros, about three to four hours by road from [Bacolod](/asia/philippines/negrosisland/bacolod). It is one of the Philippines' best-kept beach and diving destinations -- still developing, still uncrowded, and all the better for it.

[Sugar Beach](/asia/philippines/negrosisland/sipalay/sugar_beach) is the headline attraction, a strip of white sand lined with small resorts where the pace of life barely registers. [Punta Ballo](/asia/philippines/negrosisland/sipalay/punta_ballo) draws serious divers to its clear waters and healthy reefs, and [Campomanes Bay](/asia/philippines/negrosisland/sipalay/campomanes_bay) is a protected marine reserve with calm water for kayaking, diving, and water sports. Offshore, [Danjugan Island](/asia/philippines/negrosisland/sipalay/danjugan_island) is a protected reserve with lagoons, mangroves, and the endangered white-breasted sea eagle.

The diving here is outstanding. Over 40 dive sites range from shallow house reefs to deep walls, World War II wrecks like the [SS Panay](/asia/philippines/negrosisland/sipalay/ss_panay), and underwater caves. The water is warm, visibility is generally good, and the marine life is abundant. Artistic Diving and Easy Diving are the main operators at Punta Ballo.
