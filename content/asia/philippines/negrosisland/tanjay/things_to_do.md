title: 'Things to do'
type: section
---

Tanjay is a quiet provincial city built around its Spanish colonial church and small central plaza. Most travellers see it in an hour or two on the way north from Dumaguete, but it rewards a short stop. The Santiago Apóstol Church on the main square is one of the oldest Catholic parishes on Negros, and the plaza in front of it is the social heart of the town in the cool of the evening.

The coastline along the Tañon Strait is the other draw — a working fishing town with views across to Cebu and modest local beaches a short tricycle ride from the centre. Inland, the sugar and rice country backs up against the central mountains of Negros, with the Pagahat River cutting down from the highlands.
