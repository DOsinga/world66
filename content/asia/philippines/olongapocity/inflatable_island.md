Inflatable Island is a floating water park off the beach at Subic Bay, with obstacle-course platforms, slides, and oversized inflatable play areas. It is a light, family-oriented stop rather than a cultural sight, but it can be a deliberate half-day choice for travellers staying around Barretto, Baloy Beach, or Olongapo.
