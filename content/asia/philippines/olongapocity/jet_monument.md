A retired US Navy jet fighter mounted on a concrete plinth near the freeport boulevard — a low-key but striking reminder of the era when this entire area was an American military base. The plinth carries a plaque explaining the aircraft's service history and the role of Subic Bay in US Pacific operations.

It is essentially a drive-by sight, but worth a brief stop on the way to or from one of the larger attractions. There are picnic tables in the surrounding park.
