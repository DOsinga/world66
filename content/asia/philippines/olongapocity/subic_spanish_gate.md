---

The Spanish Gate is a heavy arched stone gateway near the Subic Bay waterfront — the only surviving remnant of the Spanish naval station that pre-dated the American base by over a decade. The gate is now a designated National Historical Landmark, set in a small park with interpretation panels.

It is a short stop, more interesting as a marker of the layered colonial history of the bay than as a sight in its own right. It is best combined with a stroll along the nearby boardwalk and the bay-front area.
