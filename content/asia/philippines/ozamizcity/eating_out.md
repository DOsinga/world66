title: Eating Out
type: section
---

Ozamiz is an Ilonggon and Cebuano city, and the food reflects it — fresh seafood from Panguil Bay, grilled meats, and the hearty soups common to northern Mindanao. The main commercial district along Hilarion A. Ramiro Sr. Avenue has the highest concentration of restaurants. The city market is the place to go for cheap, no-nonsense Filipino food at any hour.
