---
title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Pagadian isn't a city of monuments — its draw is the everyday rhythm of provincial Mindanao life and the natural setting around Illana Bay. Start at the waterfront **Plaza Luz** for the bay view, walk the public market, and take a ride on one of the city's famously tilted **tricycles**, modified to handle Pagadian's near-vertical streets.

For something more active, the **Pulacan Falls** in nearby Labangan and a boat trip to **Dao Dao Island** in the bay both make easy half-days out of town. The cathedral on the hill is worth the climb for the bay panorama at sunset.
