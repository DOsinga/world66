Cape Bojeador Lighthouse is a Spanish colonial lighthouse on the high coast of Burgos, first lit in 1892. Its hilltop position makes it one of the classic stops on a northern Ilocos road trip, with views over the sea and the wind-scoured coastline.

The lighthouse is a good pairing with Bangui Wind Farm and the beaches around Pagudpud. It is a short stop rather than a full attraction, but the age, setting, and coastal view make it worth the detour.
