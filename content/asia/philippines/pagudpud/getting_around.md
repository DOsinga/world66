---
title: Getting Around
type: section
---

Tricycles are the usual local transport, and drivers commonly offer circuits covering Saud, the Blue Lagoon side, Kabigan Falls, and the Patapat Viaduct. Agree on the route and price before leaving.

The sights are too spread out for casual walking, and the eastern side is hilly. A hired car or motorbike gives more freedom if you are comfortable with local road conditions.
