The waters around Coron and the Calamian Islands hold around a dozen wrecks of Japanese supply and warships sunk in a single American air raid in September 1944. Most lie in 15–40 metres of water, with several accessible to advanced open-water divers and a few — like the **Lusong Gunboat** — shallow enough to be snorkelled.

Star sites include the **Irako** (a refrigerated supply ship, 28–43m), the **Akitsushima** (a seaplane tender, 25–38m), the **Olympia Maru** (a freighter, 15–25m, the most penetrable of the group) and the **Okikawa Maru** (a tanker, 16–26m). Several Coron dive operators are reputable; the underwater visibility is often around 15 metres but can collapse to half that in poor conditions.
