---
title: "Diving"
type: section
---

Palawan is one of the great diving destinations of Southeast Asia, and a serious traveller can build an entire two-week trip around it.

In **Coron**, the wreck diving is world-class: a dozen Japanese ships sunk in a single afternoon in 1944, most lying at recreational depths in 15–40 metres of water. The **Irako**, **Akitsushima**, **Okikawa Maru** and **Olympia Maru** are the star sites, with the smaller **Lusong Gunboat** accessible to snorkellers.

In **El Nido**, the diving is more about the reef walls of the Bacuit Archipelago and the small offshore atolls — less iconic than Coron's wrecks but with high fish density, healthy soft corals, and very occasional whale sharks.

In the far south, the **Tubbataha Reefs Natural Park** (a UNESCO site in the Sulu Sea, reached only by liveaboard from Puerto Princesa in March–June) is among the best dive destinations in Asia — a remote double atoll with sharks, manta rays, schooling jacks and pristine coral.

Beginners can start in Coron, where most reputable dive shops offer open-water certifications in calm protected bays.
