---
title: "Getting Around"
type: section
---

Within each Palawan town, the main local transport is the **tricycle** — a motorbike with a sidecar — flagged down on the street and bargained for. Short hops within El Nido or Coron town are usually 100–150 pesos; longer trips to nearby beaches a few hundred. Always agree the price before getting in.

Between towns, **shared vans** run on fixed routes (Puerto Princesa–El Nido, Puerto Princesa–Port Barton, etc.) and depart when full. They are quicker and not much more expensive than the buses; they will pick you up from your hotel if you book the day before.

For the island-hopping tours, you buy a **boat seat** rather than transport — the standardised El Nido and Coron tours (A, B, C, D in El Nido; Coron Ultimate, Island Escapade, etc. in Coron) include the boat, lunch, snorkelling gear and entry fees. Private bancas are negotiable for groups.

**Renting a scooter** is straightforward in Puerto Princesa, El Nido and Coron (around 500 pesos a day). The roads on Palawan have improved enormously in the last decade and most are now paved, but they remain winding and shared with chickens, dogs and the occasional water buffalo.
