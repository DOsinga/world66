---
title: "Island Hopping Tours"
type: section
---

The signature Palawan activity is the **island-hopping tour** — a day on a motorised outrigger banca, snorkelling, swimming, and eating fresh-grilled fish on a beach for lunch. In El Nido and Coron the offerings are standardised:

**El Nido Tour A** covers the Bacuit Archipelago's classic lagoons: Big Lagoon, Small Lagoon, Secret Lagoon, Shimizu Island and 7 Commando Beach. **Tour B** is the outer islands and the Matinloc Shrine. **Tour C** is the dramatic Hidden Beach and Helicopter Island circuit. **Tour D** is a quieter mix of Cadlao Island lagoon and Paradise Beach.

**Coron** has fewer official tour names but most operators sell two main loops: the **"Ultimate" tour** of Kayangan Lake, Twin Lagoon, Siete Pecados and a snorkel stop at a wreck; and an **"Island Escapade"** to Malcapuya, Banana Island and Bulog Dos.

Tours leave at about 9 a.m. and return by 4–5 p.m. Bring sunscreen, a hat, water shoes, dry bag and reef-safe products (the reefs need them).
