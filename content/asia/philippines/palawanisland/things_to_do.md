---
title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Palawan is really three destinations sharing one long island, and most travellers visit at least two of them. **El Nido** in the north is the postcard Palawan — limestone karst islets rising out of a turquoise archipelago, reached by motorised *banca* outriggers on a series of standardised "Tour A, B, C, D" island-hopping circuits. **Coron**, four hours by ferry to the north on a separate island group, is the wreck-diving capital of the Philippines and has its own equally beautiful (and quieter) lagoons. **Puerto Princesa** in the south is the capital, the main entry airport, and the base for the UNESCO-listed Underground River, Honda Bay and the central jungle.

Between these are quieter spots: **Port Barton**, halfway up the west coast, is the laid-back backpacker antidote to El Nido. **Sabang** and **San Vicente** have some of the longest empty beaches in the Philippines. **Calauit** in the far north is a strange Cold-War-era safari island stocked with African game.
