Balingasay River winds through mangroves and quiet countryside behind Bolinao. It is a gentler landscape than the beaches: green water, fish pens, river bends, and local boats moving slowly between villages.

River cruises are the easiest way to see it. The appeal is not a single dramatic sight but the change of pace after a day of sun, salt, and road dust.
