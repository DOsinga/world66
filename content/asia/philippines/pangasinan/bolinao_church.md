Bolinao Church, formally Saint James the Great Parish Church, is the old stone heart of town. Its thick walls and weathered facade make a useful reminder that Bolinao was a settlement long before it became a beach base.

It is an easy stop before heading toward Patar or the river. The church square also gives you a quick read on everyday Bolinao, with tricycles, small shops, and the slow pace of a coastal municipality.
