Bolinao Falls is the inland counterpoint to the beach: cool freshwater pools, limestone ledges, and enough shade to make it a useful break from the coastal heat. The falls are not huge, but the swimming is the reason to come.

Go early if you want a quieter visit. By midday the pools can fill with families, music, and picnic tables, which is fun in its own way but less peaceful.
