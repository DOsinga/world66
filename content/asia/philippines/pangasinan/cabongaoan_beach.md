Cabongaoan Beach sits on the Burgos coast, farther from the usual Pangasinan beach circuit and rougher around the edges. The draw is the rocky shoreline, pale sand, and the feeling of being well away from the main resort towns.

Road access takes patience, especially after rain. Bring what you need, keep expectations simple, and treat the rough approach as part of the bargain.
