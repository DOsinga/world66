Cape Bolinao Lighthouse stands on a low, windy hill above Patar, with the South China Sea spread out below it. It is one of those simple stops that justifies the detour: white tower, scrubby headland, hard light, and a broad horizon.

Come late in the afternoon if you can. The lighthouse pairs naturally with [Patar Beach](/asia/philippines/pangasinan/patar_beach), and the road between them is one of the prettiest short drives in western Pangasinan.
