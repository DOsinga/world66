Death Pool, sometimes softened to Depth Pool, is a natural rock pool near Cabongaoan Beach. The name is more theatrical than helpful, but the pool is a memorable bit of wave-cut coast when the sea is calm.

Do not treat it casually. Conditions change quickly on exposed rock, and the spot is only worth visiting when the water is settled and locals say it is safe.
