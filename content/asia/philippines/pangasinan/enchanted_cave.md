Enchanted Cave is a small limestone cave near Bolinao with a clear, cool pool inside. It is more developed than wild, with paths and visitor facilities, but the contrast between hot coastal air and the cave water is hard to argue with.

Treat it as a short stop rather than the centrepiece of a day. It works best combined with [Patar Beach](/asia/philippines/pangasinan/patar_beach), the lighthouse, and other Bolinao sights.
