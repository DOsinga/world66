Lingayen Beach is broad, flat, and local, with a long grey-gold shoreline facing the gulf. It is not Pangasinan's most photogenic beach, but it has history, space, and a lived-in provincial atmosphere.

The beach works well with the nearby capitol area and Lingayen's old civic core. Come for a walk, sunset, or a low-key swim rather than a polished resort day.
