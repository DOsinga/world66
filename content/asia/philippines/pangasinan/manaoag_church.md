Manaoag Church, officially the Minor Basilica of Our Lady of the Rosary of Manaoag, is Pangasinan's great pilgrimage stop. Buses and private cars stream in from across Luzon, especially on weekends and Marian feast days.

The church can be crowded, commercial, and deeply sincere all at once. Visit early, dress respectfully, and give yourself time to watch the rhythm of candles, blessings, and families queuing before the image.
