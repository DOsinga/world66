The Minor Basilica of Saint Dominic in San Carlos adds another strong church stop to central Pangasinan. Its scale reflects San Carlos' old importance as one of the province's major inland towns.

It is not as famous with pilgrims as [Manaoag Church](/asia/philippines/pangasinan/manaoag_church), but that makes it calmer. Stop if you are travelling between Lingayen, Dagupan, and the central towns.
