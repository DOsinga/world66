Nambalan Beach is a low-key Pangasinan beach with grass, coconut shade, and open views of the coast. It is less developed than the better-known beaches around Bolinao, which makes it better for a quiet picnic, a swim, or an overnight camp under the stars.

Bring what you need for the day, including water and sun protection. Facilities are limited, and that is part of the appeal.
