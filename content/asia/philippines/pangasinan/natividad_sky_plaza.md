Natividad Sky Plaza is an eastern Pangasinan viewpoint with a big religious statue, hillside steps, and wide views over the surrounding lowlands. It is a simple local pilgrimage and picnic spot rather than a polished attraction.

The plaza makes sense if you are already crossing the province toward the mountains or Tayug. For most beach-focused travellers it is too far out of the way, but it rounds out the inland side of Pangasinan nicely.
