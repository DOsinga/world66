Santo Rosario Cave System is an adventurous cave outing near Mabini, with tight passages, bats, small pools, and rock formations that reward a bit of scrambling. It is not a polished show cave: expect uneven footing, low entrances, and the possibility of scrapes if you move too quickly.

Go with local guidance and wear clothes that can get dirty. The appeal is the raw, slightly awkward cave experience, not easy boardwalk sightseeing.
