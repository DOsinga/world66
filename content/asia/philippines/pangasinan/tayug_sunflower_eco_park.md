Tayug Sunflower Eco Park is a cheerful inland stop when the flowers are in bloom. It is not an ancient sight or a grand natural wonder, just a bright garden that gives eastern Pangasinan something different from beaches and churches.

Check recent local photos before going because flower displays are seasonal. If the fields are not in bloom, there is little reason to make a special journey.
