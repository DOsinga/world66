Tondol Beach in Anda is known for shallow, pale water that runs a long way from shore. It is a good family beach, less dramatic than Patar but easier for gentle swimming and lazy wading.

The setting is simple, with local resorts and cottages rather than a big resort strip. Check tides before making the trip; the beach changes character a lot between low and high water.
