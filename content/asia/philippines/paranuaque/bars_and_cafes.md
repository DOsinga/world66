---
title: "Bars and Cafes"
type: section
---

The Entertainment City resorts contain their own ecosystems of bars, clubs, and lounges — Okada Manila in particular has a sprawling pool-and-bar complex that is worth visiting even if you don't gamble. For something more local, the streets around BF Homes host a long-established strip of neighbourhood bars and casual hangouts that fill up on Friday and Saturday nights.
