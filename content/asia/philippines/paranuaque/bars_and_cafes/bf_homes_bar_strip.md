The residential streets of BF Homes hold a scatter of neighbourhood bars and live music venues that cater primarily to locals — expats, professionals, and the young-ish crowd who prefer something low-key to the casino megaclubs. The stretch along President's Avenue and the surrounding blocks has the highest concentration, with a mix of sports bars, acoustic music venues, and straightforward inuman (drinking) spots.

Prices are much more reasonable here than at the Entertainment City resorts. A bottle of San Miguel is ₱60–80 at most places; the vibe is relaxed and the crowds are not particularly large. Come on a Friday or Saturday evening.
