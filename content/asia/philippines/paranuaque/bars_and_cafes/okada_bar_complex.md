Cove Manila is Okada's outdoor pool-and-nightlife complex, and it is the most impressive venue of its kind in Metro Manila. On event nights — which happen most weekends — international DJs perform on a floating stage above the pool while the crowd spills across the beach club and cabana areas. The setup is genuinely spectacular, with the bay as a backdrop and the casino towers lit up overhead.

Day passes give access to the pools without any event commitment. The beach club drinks are expensive by Manila standards (expect to pay ₱400–600 for a cocktail on event nights), but the setting justifies it for a special occasion. Book tables or cabanas in advance for major events — they sell out.
