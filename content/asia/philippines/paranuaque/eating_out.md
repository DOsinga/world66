---
title: "Eating Out"
type: section
---

Parañaque's food scene ranges from the chaotic fish markets of Baclaran to polished hotel restaurants inside the Entertainment City resorts. The most local experience is the cluster of seafood eateries along the bay, where you pick your fish live and have it cooked to order. BF Homes and the streets around Festival Mall offer a denser concentration of sit-down restaurants catering to the city's large middle-class population.
