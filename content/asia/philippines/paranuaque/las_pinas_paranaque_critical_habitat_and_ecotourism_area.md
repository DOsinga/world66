The Las Pinas-Paranaque Critical Habitat and Ecotourism Area protects a rare surviving wetland on the edge of Manila Bay. In a heavily urbanised region, its mangroves, lagoons, and birdlife feel both fragile and important.

Birdwatchers will get the most from a visit, especially during migration season, but the site also helps explain the environmental stakes of the bay. Access and conditions can change, so check current visitor information before setting out.
