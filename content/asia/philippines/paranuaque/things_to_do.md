Parañaque rewards visitors who look past the airport traffic. The old town hides one of the oldest churches in the Philippines, the bayfront has been transformed into Southeast Asia's flashiest casino strip, and the Wednesday novena at Baclaran draws one of the largest recurring religious crowds in the world. Between these poles you'll find a city that is simultaneously devout, loud, and quietly proud of its colonial roots.
