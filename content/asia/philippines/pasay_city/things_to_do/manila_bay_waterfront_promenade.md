---
title: "Manila Bay Waterfront Promenade"
type: poi
---

The Manila Bay waterfront promenade runs along the reclaimed land that makes up much of Pasay's western edge, connecting the CCP Complex in the south with the Mall of Asia area further north. The wide pedestrian boulevard faces west across the bay, and on clear evenings it delivers some of the most spectacular sunsets in the Philippines — the sky above the South China Sea turns deep orange and pink, silhouetting the distant Bataan peninsula.

The promenade has been progressively upgraded in recent years. Vendors sell snacks and drinks, and local families come out in large numbers on weekend evenings. The atmosphere is relaxed and genuinely Filipino: food stalls, children on bikes, and impromptu photo sessions against the sunset backdrop. It is entirely free and accessible at any hour, though early morning and late afternoon are the most pleasant times to walk.

From the waterfront you can see the construction activity along Manila Bay — the ongoing reclamation projects are steadily extending the shoreline. The promenade connects several destinations (the CCP, Seaside City, Mall of Asia) that are walkable between each other in the cooler evening hours, making the whole waterfront area a natural circuit for an afternoon and evening visit.
