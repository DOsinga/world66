---
title: "SM Mall of Asia"
type: poi
---

SM Mall of Asia, on the reclaimed waterfront land along Manila Bay, is one of the largest shopping malls in Asia. Opened in 2006, the complex covers around 407,000 square metres of gross floor area and draws visitors for reasons that go beyond shopping: its location on the bay makes it one of the best spots in Metro Manila to watch the sunset, and the outdoor promenade along the water's edge is popular in the evenings with families, couples, and joggers.

The mall's entertainment offerings are substantial. There is an IMAX cinema with one of the largest screens in the Philippines, an ice skating rink, a bowling alley, and a large indoor activity zone. The open-air concert grounds adjacent to the complex host major performances and festivals throughout the year. On weekends the whole precinct fills up, and the ferry terminal nearby connects to Harbour Square and other bayside destinations.

Eating and drinking options inside and around the mall run from fast food and food courts to mid-range Filipino chains and international restaurants. The bayside cluster of restaurants just outside the main building — facing the water — are particularly good for a meal at sunset. The mall is easily reached by LRT-1 (EDSA station, then a short jeepney or tricycle ride) or by Grab.
