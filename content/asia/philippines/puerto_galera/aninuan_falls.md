Aninuan Falls is a small waterfall inland from the Aninuan and White Beach side of Puerto Galera. Reaching it involves a walk through green, hilly terrain with stream crossings, so it feels more secluded than the beach resorts nearby.

The pool below the falls is the main reward, especially on a hot day. It is a good short nature trip for travellers based around White Beach who want a break from the shore without committing to a full-day excursion.
