Talipanan Beach is a quieter strip of sand west of White Beach, with calmer days, swimming, and a few beachfront places to eat. It is a good choice if Puerto Galera's busier beach scene feels too loud.

The beach also pairs well with a short walk inland to Talipanan Falls.
