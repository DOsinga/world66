Talipanan Beach is a quieter alternative to Puerto Galera's busier White Beach, with a softer pace, swimming, and a line of simple beachfront places to eat. It is a good choice when you want the sand without the louder resort strip.

The beach is not isolated, but it feels more relaxed than the main party areas. Come for a slow afternoon, a swim, and seafood or pizza by the water.
