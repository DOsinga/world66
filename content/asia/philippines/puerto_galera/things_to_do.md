---
title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Puerto Galera's main draw is underwater — the Verde Island Passage is widely considered one of the richest marine biodiversity zones on earth, and the dive sites here range from gentle coral gardens to dramatic walls and thrilling drift dives. Above water, the beaches offer an easy counterpoint: Sabang for those who want energy and company, White Beach for a quieter day in the sun.
