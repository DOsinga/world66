The Palawan Heritage Center is a compact museum in central Puerto Princesa, useful for putting the island's landscapes into cultural context. Its guided displays cover local history, Indigenous communities, natural heritage, and the pride Palawan takes in being different from the rest of the Philippines.

It is not a substitute for the Underground River or Honda Bay, but it is a good city stop on a rainy hour or before heading farther across the island. Travelers who usually rush through Puerto Princesa will understand the province better after a visit.
