Bukal Falls is a cool freshwater escape on the boundary of Majayjay and Liliw, close enough to Lucban to work as a nature outing if you have your own transport or local arrangements. The falls are known for clear, cold pools rather than raw height.

Go prepared for a wet walk and changing local access rules, since waterfalls in this part of Laguna and Quezon are often managed by the nearby barangays. It is best as a simple half-day swim and forest break.
