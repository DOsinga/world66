The Mortuary Chapel Ruins are the remains of an old cemetery chapel on a hill above Lucban. Built in the late 1700s, the ruined church keeps its weathered facade and twin belfries, giving it more atmosphere than many small cemetery structures.

It is a brief stop, but worthwhile if you are already exploring Lucban's old religious sites and want a quieter historic corner of town.
