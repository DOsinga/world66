Natalio Enriquez Mansion is a Beaux-Arts heritage house from the Spanish colonial era, built for a former governor of Quezon. Its scale and ornament make it one of Sariaya's more impressive old houses, and it is worth pairing with the town's other ancestral homes if you are exploring beyond Lucena.
