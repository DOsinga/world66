---
title: Things to Do
type: section
---

Lucena's sights cluster around its colonial core and busy waterfront. Saint Ferdinand Cathedral anchors the old city centre, while the Quezon Provincial Capitol grounds make for a pleasant stroll. The Dalahican port district at the southern edge of the city is where life gets gritty and interesting — fishing boats unload, ferries depart for the islands, and the smell of salt and diesel mixes with frying fish from market stalls.
