Iglesia Ni Cristo Templo Central is the main temple of the Iglesia Ni Cristo. The large modern church seats thousands and forms the centrepiece of a wider complex that includes church offices, educational institutions and a museum on the movement's history.

Its scale, architecture and role in a major Philippine religious organization make it one of Quezon City's more distinctive modern landmarks.
