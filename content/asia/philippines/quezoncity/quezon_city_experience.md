Quezon City Experience is a compact museum on the history of Quezon City, set beside the Quezon Memorial Circle. Its exhibits are arranged in a cluster of pod-like buildings, with interactive displays covering the city's foundation, civic life, and role in the Philippine capital region.

It works best as a companion to the memorial itself. You will not need long here, but it adds useful context before or after a walk around the circle.
