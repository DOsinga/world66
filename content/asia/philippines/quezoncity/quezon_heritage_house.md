Quezon Heritage House is a historic house museum focused on the private life of President Manuel L. Quezon and his family. The house was moved from New Manila to Quezon Memorial Circle, where it now complements the memorial and other civic sights.

It is a compact but worthwhile stop for visitors interested in Philippine history beyond the formal presidential exhibits.
