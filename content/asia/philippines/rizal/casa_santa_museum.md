An eccentric private collection of Santa Claus figures from around the world, assembled by one collector over several decades and housed in two storeys of his Antipolo home. There are reputedly around 3,000 Santas — porcelain, wood, fabric, glass — alongside other Christmas memorabilia.

It is the kind of place that should not work but somehow does. Open by appointment; ring ahead. A short walk from the cathedral.
