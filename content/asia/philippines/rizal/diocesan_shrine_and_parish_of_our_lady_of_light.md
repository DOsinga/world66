The Diocesan Shrine and Parish of Our Lady of Light is Cainta's main historic church, with roots in the Spanish colonial period and a much-loved Marian image at its centre. The present building was rebuilt in the 1960s, but it keeps an unusual layout: the entrance sits at the transept because the old Baroque facade now faces the neighbouring school.

Inside, look for the altar painting of Our Lady of Light by Fernando Amorsolo. It is a worthwhile stop if you are tracing Rizal's old churches beyond Antipolo and Morong.
