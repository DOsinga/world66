The Diocesan Shrine and Parish of Saint Clement is Angono's main Catholic church, with a bright modernist interior rather than the heavy stone look common elsewhere. Its real draw is the adjacent ecclesiastical museum, which holds a 1923 miniature painting of the Baptism of Christ by local artist Juan Senson, declared a national treasure.
