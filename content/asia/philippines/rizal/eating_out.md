---
title: "Eating Out"
type: section
---

Rizal's food has two strands: weekend Manila refugees drove the development of a lively restaurant scene in Antipolo, and the artists of Angono fostered a few much-loved village eateries. Local specialities to look for are suman (sticky rice in banana leaf), cashew sweets, kape barako (strong native coffee), and the artist-favourite minaluto seafood platter.
