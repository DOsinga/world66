A small but pleasant waterfall on the road into Antipolo, set in a national park of around three hectares. The Taktak Falls drop about 14 metres into a basin pool surrounded by trees — modest in scale but a real green space close to Metro Manila.

It is at its best after rain; in dry season the flow is thin and the pool can be murky. Steps and walkways are well maintained. A few food stalls cluster near the entrance.
