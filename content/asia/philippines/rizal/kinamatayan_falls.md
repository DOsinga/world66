Kinamatayan Falls is a small waterfall reached by a short hike near Baras. The draw is simple: cool water, forest shade, and a local story about two lovers who are said to have died here rather than be separated.

Go for a low-key nature stop rather than a developed attraction. Paths can be muddy after rain, so wear shoes that can handle a wet trail.
