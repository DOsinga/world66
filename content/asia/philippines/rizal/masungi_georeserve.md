A private nature reserve and conservation project on the Sierra Madre slopes north of Antipolo, around 60 km from Manila. The signature attraction is a guided trail through dramatic limestone karst formations, with steel rope bridges and viewpoints that look out over the jagged peaks and across to Laguna de Bay.

Bookings are essential and timed; the guides walk small groups along the four-hour route. The slightly more demanding Garden Route — a planting tour through the reforested slopes — is the longer alternative. Pricing is steep by local standards, but the proceeds fund the conservation work.
