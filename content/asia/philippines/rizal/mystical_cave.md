Mystical Cave is a guided show cave in the hills around Antipolo, with limestone chambers, stalactites, and stalagmites. It is a straightforward nature stop rather than a wilderness trip, but it works well if you are already visiting Rizal's shrines, galleries, and viewpoints.

Expect narrow passages and uneven footing. A guide helps make sense of the formations and the devotional stories attached to some of them.
