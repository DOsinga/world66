Nuestra Señora dela Annunciata Parish Church, also known as Boso-Boso Church, is an 18th-century mission church in the hills of Rizal. Spanish missionaries built it as an outpost for converting the Dumagat communities who lived in the area.

The church is worth a short detour for its age and setting rather than for lavish decoration. Its stone walls and simple mission form stand apart from the busier pilgrimage churches around Antipolo.
