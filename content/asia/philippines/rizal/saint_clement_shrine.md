The Diocesan Shrine and Parish of Saint Clement is Angono's main Roman Catholic church. Its modernist interior is light and open, and the adjacent ecclesiastical museum displays a 1923 miniature painting of the Baptism of Christ by local artist Juan Senson that has been declared a national treasure.
