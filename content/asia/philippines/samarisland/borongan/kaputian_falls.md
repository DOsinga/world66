Kaputian Falls is one of the inland nature stops that balances Borongan’s beach-heavy image. The waterfall sits away from the main town grid, reached by local roads and a short approach, with a pool and forested surroundings that feel refreshingly different from the coast.

Go with local advice on access and current conditions, especially after heavy rain. It is not a polished attraction, but for travellers who have already seen Sabang and the shoreline, it offers a simple half-day change of scenery.
