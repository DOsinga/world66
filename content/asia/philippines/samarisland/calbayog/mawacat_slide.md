title: Mawacat Slide
type: poi

Located a 35-minute ride from Oquendo District, Calbayog City
proper to Barangay Mawacat and a 25-minute walk thereafter along a foot
trail lined with tall shady trees.

A natural spoon-like formation which inclines
more or less by about 45 degrees and stretching 50 meters long. Water
flows along this giant cistern and from the top, one can slide through
the mossy lane to the cool inviting pool below.
