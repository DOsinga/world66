Iglesia ni Cristo, Lokal ng F. Manalo was the central administrative complex of the Iglesia ni Cristo from 1925 to 1971. Its Art Deco chapel, designed by Juan Nakpil, is the most notable part of the complex.

The buildings are generally viewed from outside rather than toured, so this is a short architecture stop. It fits best with nearby San Juan heritage sites such as Pinaglabanan Shrine and Museo ng Katipunan.
