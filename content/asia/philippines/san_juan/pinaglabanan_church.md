The Archdiocesan Shrine of Saint John the Baptist — known locally as Pinaglabanan Church — is a nineteenth-century Catholic church that stands a short walk from the Pinaglabanan Shrine. The church was built during the Spanish colonial period and is a fine example of the distinctive Filipino-Spanish ecclesiastical architecture found across Luzon.

The facade is well-preserved and the interior retains its colonial-era character, with thick stone walls, a high nave, and ornate altarpieces. It is an active parish and masses are celebrated daily, which means visitors can observe the church as a living institution rather than just a historical artefact.
