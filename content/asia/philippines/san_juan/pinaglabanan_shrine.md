  On 26 August 1896, Andres Bonifacio led several hundred Katipuneros against the Spanish water depot at San Juan del Monte — one of the first armed actions of the Philippine Revolution. The Pinaglabanan Memorial Shrine marks the site of one of the first battles of the Philippine Revolution, fought on 26 August 1896 when Andres Bonifacio and the Katipunan launched their uprising against Spanish rule. The park contains a large bronze monument depicting the Katipunero fighters, surrounded by gardens and a small open-air amphitheatre used for commemorative events.

The shrine is maintained as a national monument and is one of the most historically significant sites in Metro Manila. It is not heavily touristed, which gives a visit a contemplative quality that the more crowded Manila landmarks sometimes lack. The monument is best appreciated with some prior reading about Bonifacio and the Katipunan.
