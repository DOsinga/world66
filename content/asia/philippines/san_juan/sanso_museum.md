Sanso Museum is a focused art museum in San Juan, Metro Manila, dedicated to the work of Juvenal Sanso. The collection introduces a painter whose dreamlike landscapes and forms occupy a distinct place in Philippine modern art.

It is a compact stop, best for travellers who like small artist museums and want to look beyond the biggest Manila institutions. Pair it with other San Juan heritage sights such as Pinaglabanan and the Katipunan museum.
