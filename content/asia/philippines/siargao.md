Siargao is the surfing capital of the Philippines, a teardrop-shaped island in Surigao del Norte at the northeastern edge of the Visayas. The island sits in the Philippine Sea, facing the Pacific, which delivers the consistent swells that have made it one of Asia's most celebrated surf destinations. Cloud 9 — the barrelling reef break off the eastern coast — put Siargao on the international surf map and still draws pros and beginners alike. But the island long ago outgrew its surf-only reputation.

Beyond the waves, Siargao has a different rhythm from the more developed beach destinations elsewhere in the Philippines. The town of [General Luna](/asia/philippines/siargao/general_luna) is the social hub, a loose cluster of restaurants, bars, and surf shops spread along sandy lanes. The nightlife is barefoot and unpretentious. The rest of the island is quieter: lagoons fringed with mangroves, tidal pools that glow at night, rock pools and islets you can reach by boat or motorbike.

The [Sugba Lagoon](/asia/philippines/siargao/sugba_lagoon) in the north is one of the most beautiful places in the Philippines — a vast, shallow turquoise expanse surrounded by forested hills, accessible by a thirty-minute outrigger ride from Del Carmen. The three small islands near General Luna — [Guyam](/asia/philippines/siargao/guyam_island), [Daku](/asia/philippines/siargao/daku_island), and [Naked](/asia/philippines/siargao/naked_island) — make for an easy and rewarding day trip. Inland, [Magpupungko Rock Pools](/asia/philippines/siargao/magpupungko) appear at low tide, revealing crystal-clear natural swimming holes carved into the reef.

Getting to Siargao means flying into Sayak Airport near Dapa from Manila or Cebu, or taking the overnight ferry from Surigao City on the mainland. The island is compact enough to explore on a motorbike, which you can rent cheaply from most guesthouses. The best surf is from August through November, when typhoon swells push through; the dry season from March to June brings calmer seas and easier island-hopping.
