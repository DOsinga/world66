Bay of Plenty is one of the most established bars in General Luna, set right at the junction of the road that leads to Cloud 9. The open-air setup faces the beach and the bar gets a good breeze. Cold beer, rum drinks, and basic cocktails are the staples. It's the kind of place where conversations start easily between strangers — a surf check in the morning can turn into an all-day session here.

The bar really comes alive when there's a good swell. Surfers come in from the water, check who's out, watch the sets, and drink cold San Miguel until the sun goes down. Weekend nights see occasional live music. The food is basic but serviceable: chips, nuts, and bar snacks.
