Cloud 9 is the wave that made Siargao famous. It's a right-hand reef break that barrels over a shallow coral reef just off the east coast of the island, producing the kind of clean, hollow tubes that appear in surf films and Instagram feeds across the world. At its best — a solid swell from the east, light offshore winds — it's among the finest waves in Asia.

The break is watched from a purpose-built wooden boardwalk that extends out over the reef, a classic spectating spot even if you're not surfing. Beginners would be foolish to paddle out at Cloud 9 when it's pumping, but there are gentler breaks nearby — Jacking Horse, Stimpy's, and the channels between them — where you can learn or hone your skills without risking the reef. Local surf instructors set up along the beach and most guesthouses in General Luna can connect you with a lesson.
