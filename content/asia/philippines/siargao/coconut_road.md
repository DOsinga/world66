  The road was planted with coconut palms along its verges decades ago as part of a rural development programme. The palms have grown into a double canopy that turns the road into a cool, shaded corridor. Locals know it simply as the Coconut Road and it has become one of the most photographed spots on the island for visitors who discover it by motorbike.
---

The Coconut Road is a stretch of highway through the interior of Siargao that passes under an unbroken tunnel of mature coconut palms. The palms arch over the road from both sides, their fronds meeting overhead to create a cathedral-like corridor of filtered green light. On a sunny morning, the effect is magical.

The road runs through the agricultural heart of the island, away from the coast and the surf scene. Riding through it is one of those small pleasures that can't be booked or organised — just discovered while exploring on a motorbike. It's on the route between General Luna and the northern villages, so you'll likely pass through it on the way to Magpupungko or Pacifico.
