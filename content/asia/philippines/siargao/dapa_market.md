Dapa is the capital of Surigao del Norte's Siargao component and has a working public market that offers an authentic contrast to the tourist-facing scene in General Luna. The market is liveliest in the early morning, when fishing boats unload their catch and vendors from the island's interior bring vegetables, fruit, and live chickens. Fresh tuna is prominently featured — often enormous, whole fish laid out on bamboo tables.

The market is useful for provisioning if you're staying somewhere with kitchen access, and worthwhile as a window into everyday Siargao life. The town of Dapa is also the departure point for ferries to the mainland. Most visitors pass through Dapa without stopping, which is a mild oversight.
