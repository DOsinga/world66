Sohoton Cove National Park is technically off Siargao island proper, located on Bucas Grande Island to the southwest. It is one of the most dramatic natural environments in Surigao del Norte: a hidden saltwater lagoon accessible only through a narrow cave entrance that you pass through on a boat at low tide. Inside, the cove opens into a cathedral-like space with limestone walls, hanging stalactites, and bright green water.

The park is famous for its stingless jellyfish — harmless, translucent creatures that you can swim through by the thousands in the inner lagoon. The jellyfish numbers fluctuate seasonally; they're most numerous from roughly November to April. Day tours from General Luna leave by speedboat in the early morning and return by afternoon — it's a full day out including travel time. The cove is a protected national park and entry requires permits arranged through the tour operators.
