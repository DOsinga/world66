Near the town of Santa Maria, on the north coast of Siquijor, stands a massive balete (ficus) tree that is one of the island's most distinctive natural landmarks. The tree's aerial roots have merged into a curtain of woody columns around a wide central trunk, and a small freshwater spring pool at its base has been developed into a fish pond where small freshwater fish nibble at the feet of visitors who wade in — a slightly surreal but entertaining experience.

The balete tree has become something of a symbol of Siquijor's reputation for magic and mystery. Balete trees across the Philippines are associated with the spirit world, and this one, given its age and size, carries particular local significance. The pool below is clean and refreshing; a small fee is charged to enter the park area around the tree. The site is on the main road circuit and easy to visit by motorbike.
