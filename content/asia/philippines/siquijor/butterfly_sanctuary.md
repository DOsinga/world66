Siquijor Butterfly Sanctuary is a small, low-key conservation stop in the island's interior, usually visited while looping between waterfalls, old churches, and hill villages. The appeal is quiet rather than spectacular: butterflies, host plants, and a slower look at the island's inland ecology.

It is a good family-friendly pause if you have your own scooter or driver. Do not expect a large zoo-style attraction; the sanctuary is modest, personal, and best appreciated at an unhurried pace.
