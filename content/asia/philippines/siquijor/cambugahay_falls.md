Cambugahay Falls is the most popular natural attraction on Siquijor — a series of three tiered pools fed by a freshwater stream flowing through dense jungle vegetation, with clear, cool water ideal for swimming. The uppermost pool is the largest and deepest; the lower tiers are shallower and accessible to young children. Rattan bridges cross between the levels, and the whole setting has a vivid green quality that comes as a relief in the Philippine heat.

The falls are about 3 kilometres from Lazi town, accessible by tricycle or motorbike on a concrete road through rice fields, then a short walk down steps. Entry requires a small fee. It gets busy on weekends and holidays; arriving early in the morning or on a weekday gives you a better chance of having the place largely to yourself. Bring a change of clothes — the swimming is irresistible.
