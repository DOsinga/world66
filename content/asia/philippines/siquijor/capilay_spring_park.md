Capilay Spring Park is a spring-fed public swimming pool in the centre of San Juan. It is not polished or resort-like, which is exactly why it is useful: a cool freshwater break from the beach circuit and a place where local families gather, especially at weekends.

Bring a towel and keep expectations simple. It works best as a short stop while looping around Siquijor by motorbike.
