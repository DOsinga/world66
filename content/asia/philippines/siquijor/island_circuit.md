The road around Siquijor island is approximately 70 kilometres, entirely paved, and takes 3 to 4 hours to complete without stops — or a full day if you stop at every waterfall, beach, and historic church along the way. Motorbikes are available for rent from guesthouses in San Juan and the capital for around 400–500 pesos per day; a full tank of fuel is sufficient for the circuit.

Going clockwise from San Juan takes you through the capital Siquijor town (with its own beach and old church), north past the balete tree at Santa Maria and the ferry port at Larena, around the eastern tip to Lazi (church, convent, and Cambugahay Falls nearby), and back along the south coast to San Juan. The interior of the island is mountainous and forested; a detour up the Cantabon road adds the cave to the itinerary. The circuit is the best single way to understand Siquijor: small, varied, and unhurried.
