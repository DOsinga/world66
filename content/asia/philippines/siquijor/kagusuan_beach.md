Kagusuan Beach is a scenic beach on Siquijor, known for its rock formations and cliff-backed setting. Access is by a concrete stairway down from above, which helps keep the beach quieter than the island's easier roadside stops.

Come for the seascape rather than facilities. It is best worked into an island circuit with nearby natural stops, and conditions are most rewarding when the tide leaves enough sand exposed below the cliffs.
