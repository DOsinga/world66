Mt. Bandilaan National Park protects the highest part of Siquijor, a cooler forested interior that contrasts sharply with the island's beaches and coastal villages. Trails, springs, religious stations and viewpoints make it one of the best places to understand Siquijor as more than a beach loop.

The park is easiest with a motorbike or driver, and the roads up can be steep in places. On clear days the reward is a broad view over the island and surrounding sea; on cloudier days the forest, quiet and cooler air are reason enough to go.
