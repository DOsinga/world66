San Juan, on the west coast of Siquijor, is the centre of the island's dive industry. Several small operators — most of them run by long-term foreign residents — offer day dives, night dives, PADI courses, and free-diving instruction at competitive rates. The sites range from gentle slopes good for beginners to steep walls and current-swept passages for more experienced divers.

The most popular sites are Siquijor Wall (a long drop-off with good visibility), Coral Garden (shallow reef for snorkellers and beginners), and Mantiguis Channel (good for current dives and big fish). Whale shark sightings are occasional from November to April. Equipment is well-maintained at most operators; prices are lower than Cebu or Bohol for equivalent diving. San Juan also has the best concentration of guesthouses and restaurants on the island, making it the natural base for a diving trip.
