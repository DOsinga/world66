St Francis of Assisi Church is one of the first landmarks many travellers see on Siquijor, close to the port and the islands old religious core. Its white facade and modest scale make it less dramatic than Lazi Church, but it helps set the tone for the island circuit.

Stop here at the beginning or end of a loop around Siquijor, especially if you are interested in the Spanish-era churches scattered across the island. It is an active parish, so keep the visit quiet and brief during services.
