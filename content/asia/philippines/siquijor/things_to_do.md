---
title: Things to Do
type: section
---

Siquijor rewards slow exploration. The island circuit by motorbike is the classic way to cover the highlights in a day; diving and snorkelling around the reefs can occupy several more. The island has a well-earned reputation for traditional healing and sorcery — the healers who gather around Lazi during the Holy Week festival are among the last practitioners of a tradition going back centuries.
