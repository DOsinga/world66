Bontoc Boulevard is the town's simple waterfront promenade, more local breathing space than formal attraction. It is where you go for bay air, snacks, and a quick sense of how the town meets the water.

The best time is late afternoon, when the heat eases and people start drifting toward the shore. Do not expect spectacle; the pleasure is in the ordinary coastal scene.
