Bontoc Port is a small working pier on the town waterfront. It is not a destination in the resort sense, but it gives Bontoc its orientation: boats, tricycles, sea breeze, and the practical business of a coastal municipality.

Stop here if you are already in town or walking the boulevard. It is also a useful landmark for finding local transport and getting directions.
