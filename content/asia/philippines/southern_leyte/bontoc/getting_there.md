---
title: Getting There
type: section
---

Bontoc sits on the main Southern Leyte road between Maasin and Sogod. Buses, vans, and jeepneys connect it with both towns, though schedules are informal and slow travel is part of the bargain.

The nearest practical long-distance gateways are Maasin, Hilongos, and the ferry and bus routes that cross Leyte. If you are coming from Cebu or Bohol, check current ferry schedules before building a tight itinerary.
