---
title: Getting There
type: section
---

Hinunangan is on the eastern coast of Southern Leyte, accessible by road from [Maasin City](/asia/philippines/southern_leyte/maasin_city), the provincial capital, about 90 kilometres to the northwest.

Jeepneys and buses connect Hinunangan to Maasin City and to [Sogod](/asia/philippines/southern_leyte/sogod). The road is paved but winding through mountainous terrain, so journeys take longer than the distance suggests. There is no airport in Hinunangan; the nearest commercial airport is in Tacloban on the island of Leyte, several hours by road and ferry.
