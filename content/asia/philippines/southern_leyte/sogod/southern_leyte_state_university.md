Southern Leyte State University is a public university based in Sogod, the main institution of higher education in the province. The campus is the largest in the town and gives Sogod more of a student-town feel than the purely commercial character of many Philippine provincial municipalities.

The university is not a conventional tourist sight, but the campus is pleasant and open, and the presence of students gives the town some social energy that a pure market town might lack. The university library and facilities serve the surrounding communities. For travellers who find themselves in Sogod for longer than expected, the campus area around the university has some cafes and small restaurants that serve the student population.
