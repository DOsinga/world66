St Bernard sits on the eastern side of [Southern Leyte](/asia/philippines/southern_leyte), between the mountains and the coast road that runs toward Silago. It is a quiet municipal town, but it carries one of the province's heaviest modern memories: the 2006 Guinsaugon landslide, which buried a village after weeks of rain.

That history should not be treated as a sightseeing gimmick. If you visit the [Guinsaugon area](/asia/philippines/southern_leyte/stbernard/guinsaugon_area), go respectfully and understand that for locals it is recent family history, not distant disaster lore. Away from that story, St Bernard has beaches, springs, and waterfall country that mostly serve local visitors rather than outside tourism.

The town is best approached as part of a slow loop through eastern Southern Leyte. Roads are scenic but weather-sensitive, and landslide risk is real in heavy rain. In good conditions, the route gives a rawer view of Leyte than the better-known dive coast around Sogod Bay.
