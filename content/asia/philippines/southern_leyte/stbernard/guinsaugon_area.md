The Guinsaugon area is the reason many outsiders know St Bernard at all. The old village was buried in 2006, and the relocation area around New Guinsaugon is a sober reminder of how vulnerable this part of Leyte can be.

Visit with restraint. This is not a place for dramatic posing or disaster tourism; it is a community landscape where grief, relocation, and daily life sit side by side.
