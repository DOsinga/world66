Himatagon is the practical centre of St Bernard, where the municipal hall, market life, and transport connections cluster. It is not a showpiece, but it is where you get oriented before heading to the coast or upland barangays.

Use it for food, directions, and local transport. In a town like St Bernard, those practical stops matter more than formal attractions.
