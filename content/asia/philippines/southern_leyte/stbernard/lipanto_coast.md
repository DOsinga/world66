Lipanto is one of St Bernard's coastal barangays, listed locally for its beach and marine sanctuary. This is not a polished beach resort strip; expect local facilities, changing conditions, and a coastline that depends heavily on weather and tide.

The coast is best visited with someone who knows the area. If snorkelling or swimming is the plan, ask about currents, boat traffic, and whether the sanctuary is open to visitors.
