---
title: "Getting Around"
type: section
---

The freeport zone is large — about 14,000 hectares — and spread out in a way that makes walking impractical for most purposes. The former naval base layout was designed around private cars.

**Rental cars and motorcycles:** The easiest way to get around. Several rental outfits operate near the main gate and on the waterfront. Motorcycles are popular and cheap to rent for the day; the roads inside the freeport are quiet and well-surfaced.

**Tricycles and jeepneys:** Available just outside the freeport gates, tricycles cover short distances in Olongapo and to Barrio Barretto. Regular jeepney routes connect Olongapo City with the freeport entrance.

**Taxis and apps:** Grab operates in the area and is the most convenient option if you have a Philippine SIM card. Metered taxis are available but less common than in Manila.

**Cycling:** The flat roads inside the freeport are good for cycling; several hotels rent bicycles. The main risk is the occasional speeding truck heading to the port or industrial zone.

The distances between key attractions — Zoobic Safari, Ocean Adventure, the waterfront, and the Barretto strip — range from two to eight kilometres. Plan on using a vehicle or budgeting time for multiple trips.
