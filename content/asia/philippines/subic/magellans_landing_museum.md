Magellan's Landing Museum is a small heritage stop around Subic Bay, using local displays to connect the area with early European contact, maritime movement, and the layered history of the former naval base region. It is modest in scale, but useful for travellers who want something more than beaches, bars, and water activities.

The museum is best treated as a short add-on while exploring Subic and Olongapo rather than a stand-alone reason to travel. Check current opening details before going, as small local museums in the area can keep irregular hours.
