Southeast Asia's first open-water marine theme park, situated at the edge of the bay rather than in tanks. Bottlenose dolphins and California sea lions perform in a sheltered natural cove, and visitors can pay extra for a swim-with-dolphins session. The park also runs a bird show featuring free-flying eagles and a waterfront boat tour. The location is stunning — the backdrop is jungle-covered hills and the open bay — and the shows are polished enough to be genuinely entertaining. The animal welfare standards are closer to a well-run zoo than a roadside circus. Book swim sessions in advance at weekends.
