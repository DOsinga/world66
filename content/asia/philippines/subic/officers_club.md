The yacht club's open-air bar is the most atmospheric place for a drink inside the freeport — a broad terrace over the water where the light on the bay is spectacular at sundown. The crowd is a mix of sailing types, Manila weekenders, and freeport residents. The beer selection is basic (San Miguel, Red Horse) but the cocktails are made properly and the views justify the slightly higher prices. The club welcomes non-members at the bar. Arrive before 6pm to claim a table with a bay view.
