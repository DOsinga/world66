A casual strip of covered food stalls along the bay, best visited in the evenings when the heat drops and the bay turns orange. Stalls serve grilled pork belly (liempo), chicken inasal, pancit noodles, and local rice dishes at street-food prices. It is the cheapest decent meal inside the freeport zone and one of the few places to eat Filipino home cooking rather than the expat-oriented fare that dominates the main restaurants. Bring cash; most stalls are card-free.
