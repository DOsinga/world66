The Spanish Gate is a compact but useful remnant of Subic Bay before the American naval base era. Built in the 1880s as part of the Spanish naval arsenal at Olongapo, it once controlled access to a fortified military zone beside one of Luzon's best natural harbors.

Today the gate is a quick stop rather than a full attraction, but it gives Subic some historical texture beyond beaches, shopping, and former-base infrastructure. Pair it with a walk around the Freeport area if you want to trace how the bay shifted from Spanish naval outpost to American base to civilian leisure zone.
