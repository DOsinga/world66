People's Park in the Sky occupies the highest point on the Tagaytay Ridge, 709 metres above sea level, and on a clear day the panorama extends to Manila Bay in one direction and Mount Banahaw in the other. At the centre of the park sits a strange, never-finished two-storey structure — the beginnings of a private presidential retreat that was abandoned after Ferdinand Marcos was overthrown in 1986.

The park itself is modest — a grassy hilltop with a few terraces, gardens, and the ruined shell of the mansion. The admission fee is low and most visitors come simply for the 360-degree views and to have their photograph taken against the backdrop of Taal Lake. The walk up from the car park is steep; there are also horses for hire if you would rather ride.

The kitsch has a charm of its own. Weekend vendors sell fresh strawberries and sweet corn. The abandoned building has been decorated with murals and garden sculptures over the years, giving it the air of a cheerful ruin.
