Perlas ng Silang is a landscaped flower farm near Tagaytay, with manicured lawns, bright planting beds, and sculpted garden displays. It is a gentle stop rather than a major sight, best for travellers who want fresh air and colour between Tagaytay's busier viewpoints and restaurants.
