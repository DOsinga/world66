Sky Ranch is a theme park and amusement complex on the Tagaytay Ridge, dominated by the Sky Eye — a giant Ferris wheel that at 63 metres high is one of the tallest in the Philippines. The ride lifts you above the ridge with clear views over Taal Lake and, on good days, toward Manila Bay. It is at its most dramatic at dusk.

Beyond the Ferris wheel, Sky Ranch has a collection of rides, carnival games, and food stalls that make it particularly popular with families and younger visitors. The park gets very crowded on weekend afternoons. If you are mainly interested in the Sky Eye, try to arrive early when queues are shorter. The complex also has multiple restaurants and cafes on site.

Sky Ranch is primarily aimed at the Manila weekender market rather than foreign tourists, which gives it an authentic local feel. The combination of views, rides, and food makes it a popular stop for Filipino families, and the energy of a full weekend crowd has its own appeal.
