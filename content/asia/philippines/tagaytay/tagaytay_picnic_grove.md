Tagaytay Picnic Grove is one of the city's old-fashioned crowd-pleasers: a public recreation area on the ridge with views toward Taal Lake and the volcano. It is not polished or quiet, but it is very Tagaytay, especially on weekends when families arrive for picnics, photos, and cool air.

The grove works best as a simple viewpoint and family stop rather than a wilderness experience. Come early if you want breathing room, and combine it with People's Park in the Sky or a meal along the ridge.
