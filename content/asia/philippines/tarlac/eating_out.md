---
title: Eating Out
type: section
---

Tarlac is a crossroads between Kapampangan, Ilocano, and Tagalog kitchens, and you eat well here. The classic local dish is iniruban, a glutinous rice-and-coconut cake that turns up at every fiesta, and tinubong, sticky rice cooked inside bamboo tubes. The Kapampangan-leaning south of the province is sisig country — chopped pork ear and cheek with chillies and calamansi, eaten on rice with a fried egg.

The cheapest food is at the carinderias around the public market and along McArthur Highway. Isdaan Floating Restaurant on the highway south of town is a long-running open-air seafood place built over fish ponds, popular with families and the bus crowd. For more polished cooking, try the restaurants at the SM City Tarlac and Tarlac Hotel.
