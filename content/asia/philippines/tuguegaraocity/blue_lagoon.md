Blue Lagoon is a bright blue swimming spot near Tuguegarao, reached by a short hike down toward the river. The colour is the draw, especially after dry weather when the water is clearer.

Allow time for the walk in and out, wear shoes that can handle rough ground, and ask locally about current access before setting off. Cave exploration may be possible nearby, but conditions change.
