title: "Eating Out"
type: section
---

Tuguegarao's signature dish is Pancit Batil Patung — thick egg noodles stir-fried with minced carabao or beef, bean sprouts, and vegetables, topped with a fried egg and served with a small bowl of egg drop soup called the "batil" used as a dipping broth. It's a meal invented here and eaten throughout Cagayan Valley, but the best versions are in Tuguegarao itself. Gretchen's on Magallanes Street is the most established restaurant for it, with a version considered the standard by the city's annual Pancit Eating Contest. Kainang Pinoy in Barangay Leonarda is another strong choice for local cooking — crispy pata, dinengdeng, and fresh river shrimp from the Cagayan River appear on the menu depending on the season. For breakfast, Crown Bakeshop next to Hotel Roma does good pastries and sandwiches.
