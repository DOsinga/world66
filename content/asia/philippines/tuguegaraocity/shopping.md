---
title: "Shopping"
type: section
---

The Tuguegarao Public Market on the Pan-Philippine Highway is the best place to pick up local produce, dried fish, native snacks, and everyday goods at provincial prices. The central market district downtown has rows of stalls selling clothing, accessories, and household items. For air-conditioned mall shopping, SM Center Tuguegarao Downtown on Luna Street is the most convenient option in the city centre, while Robinsons Tuguegarao to the north has a wider range of shops and a cinema.
