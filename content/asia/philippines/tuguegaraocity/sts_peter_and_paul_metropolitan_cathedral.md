Sts. Peter and Paul Metropolitan Cathedral is the seat of the Archdiocese of Tuguegarao and one of the major Spanish colonial churches in Cagayan Valley. Built by Dominican friars in the 18th century, it was badly damaged during the Second World War and later rebuilt. Its scale, brickwork, and long role in the region's Catholic life make it the central landmark of Tuguegarao.
