The Buntun Bridge carries the Pan-Philippine Highway across the Cagayan River on the western edge of Tuguegarao City, connecting the city to the municipality of Solana. It's one of the longest bridges in the Philippines and a civic landmark. The bridge itself isn't a destination exactly, but crossing it — either by tricycle or on foot — gives you a proper view of the Cagayan River in both directions: wide, powerful, and lined with the flat agricultural plain that defines this part of northern Luzon.
