Camangaan is a barangay of Vigan known for Abel Ilocano weaving. Visitors come for small workshops and shops where local weavers produce traditional textiles, often on handlooms and in family-run settings.

It is most interesting as a craft stop rather than a formal attraction. Go if you want to see or buy local textiles away from the main heritage streets of central Vigan.
