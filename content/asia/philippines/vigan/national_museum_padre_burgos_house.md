The National Museum/Padre Burgos House preserves the former home and memorabilia of Jose Burgos, one of the martyred priests remembered in Philippine history. Behind the house, the former Ilocos Sur Provincial Jail holds exhibits on Ilocano culture, including weapons, kitchen tools, baskets, costumes, jewellery, and other local artifacts.
