Our Lady of Hope Parish Church is Caoayan's main Roman Catholic church, built during the Spanish era in a 19th-century Baroque style. It is a small but useful stop for visitors exploring the heritage towns around Vigan.

The church is close enough to Vigan to pair with the better-known colonial core, especially if you are following Spanish-period architecture in the surrounding municipalities.
