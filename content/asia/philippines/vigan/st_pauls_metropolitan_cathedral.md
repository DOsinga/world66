St. Paul's Metropolitan Cathedral stands at the heart of Vigan's historic centre, facing Plaza Salcedo and anchoring the colonial street grid. It is not separate from the city's UNESCO atmosphere; it is one of the buildings that gives that atmosphere its civic and religious centre.

Visit while walking between Calle Crisologo, the plazas, and the old mansions. The cathedral's broad facade and active parish life are a reminder that Vigan is not only a preserved streetscape but also a working Ilocano city.
