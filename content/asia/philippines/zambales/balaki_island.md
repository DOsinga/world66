Balaki Island is a small private island off the Zambales coast, with calm, shallow water suited to swimming and snorkelling. Access needs to be arranged through the Infanta Tourism Office, but the reward is a quiet day of seagrass beds, simple camping, and clear water away from the busier beach towns.
