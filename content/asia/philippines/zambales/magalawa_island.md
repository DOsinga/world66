Magalawa Island is a small beach island off Palauig, more relaxed than the better-known coves around San Antonio. The appeal is simple: pale sand, shallow water, shade trees, and a pace that suits a picnic or overnight beach stay rather than a packed sightseeing schedule.

It is best treated as a Zambales side trip for travelers already moving along the coast. Boat access is from the Palauig side, and sea conditions can matter, so arrange transfers locally and ask where swimming is safest before heading into the water.
