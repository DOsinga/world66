San Salvador Island lies off Masinloc and is known for white-sand beaches, a marine reserve, and a giant clam sanctuary. It is still a working fishing community, so the appeal is a mix of beach time and a look at local coastal life rather than a polished resort stop.

Go for the water and the marine sanctuary, and treat the island as a day trip that needs a little planning from the mainland.
