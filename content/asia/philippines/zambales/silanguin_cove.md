Silanguin Cove is the farthest of the San Antonio coves, reached by boat along the same Zambales coast as Anawangin and Nagsasa. Its beach has grey volcanic sand, mountain scenery, and a small island at the mouth of the cove, making it a quieter choice for visitors who want the cove landscape with fewer crowds.
