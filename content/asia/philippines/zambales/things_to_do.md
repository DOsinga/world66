---
title: 'Things to Do'
type: section
---

The coastline is Zambales' main draw — dramatic volcanic ash-grey coves in the south, white-sand islands in the north, and all of it far less crowded than the Philippines' famous resort destinations. But inland, the Zambales Mountains offer serious hiking, and the legacy of Mount Pinatubo's 1991 eruption shapes everything from the landscape to the agriculture.
