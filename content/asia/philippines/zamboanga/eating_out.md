---
title: "Eating Out"
type: section
---

Zamboanga's food is a layered Spanish-Filipino-Muslim cuisine you will not find elsewhere in the Philippines. The signatures are seafood (the strait is a rich fishing ground), Chavacano-Spanish-influenced dishes like *paella zamboangueña* and *estofado*, and Muslim-Tausug specialties such as *satti* (skewered grilled meat in a spicy sauce) and *tiyula itum* (a deep black beef soup coloured with burnt coconut).
