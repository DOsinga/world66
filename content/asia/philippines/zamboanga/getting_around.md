---
title: "Getting Around"
type: section
---

The downtown is walkable — Plaza Pershing, the cathedral, the City Hall, Fort Pilar, and Paseo del Mar are all within twenty minutes of each other on foot. For anything further out, the workhorses are **tricycles** (motorcycle sidecars, agree the fare first — ₱30-100 depending on distance) and **jeepneys** (fixed routes, ₱15-20 a ride).

Conventional metered taxis exist but are less common than in Cebu or Manila. Grab is not available; the closest equivalent is Grab-style apps that occasionally have small fleets locally. For the airport, a tricycle from downtown is around ₱150-200; a private hotel transfer is more comfortable but four to five times that. Avoid moving around after dark in the outer barangays.
