---
title: "Getting There"
type: section
---

The easiest arrival is by air. **Zamboanga International Airport (ZAM)** is on the western edge of the city, ten minutes from downtown by taxi. Philippine Airlines, Cebu Pacific, and PAL Express run daily flights from Manila and Cebu — the Manila flight is about 90 minutes.

Boat services from Manila, Cebu, and other Philippine ports use the **Port of Zamboanga** at the foot of downtown. Ferries also run south to Basilan (1 hour) and Jolo (Sulu — security situation permitting). Overland from elsewhere in Mindanao is possible but long: the bus from Pagadian is around 5 hours, from [Davao](/asia/philippines/davao) closer to 15.
