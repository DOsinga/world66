Al Wakrah is a fishing town about 15 km south of central Doha — close enough to reach easily, different enough to feel like somewhere else. For most of Qatar's oil-era growth it was ignored, but the old waterfront quarter has been carefully restored and now has a small, pleasant souq that most Doha tourists miss entirely.

The [Al Wakrah Souq](/asia/qatar/alwakrah/al_wakrah_souq) is smaller and quieter than Souq Waqif in Doha, which makes it more enjoyable in some ways. The restored coral-and-gypsum buildings house craft shops and a few restaurants, and the [dhow harbour](/asia/qatar/alwakrah/al_wakrah_dhow_harbor) behind them still has working boats. It is a good late-afternoon destination when the light is good and the heat is fading. The town also has a [public beach](/asia/qatar/alwakrah/al_wakrah_beach), one of the few genuinely accessible ones near Doha.

Al Wakrah sits on the Red Line of the Doha Metro, making it straightforward to reach without a car.
