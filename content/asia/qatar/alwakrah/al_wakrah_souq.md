The Al Wakrah Souq is one of the better-preserved and better-restored traditional markets in Qatar — smaller and considerably quieter than Souq Waqif in Doha, which gives it a more genuine atmosphere. The coral-and-gypsum buildings have been restored without being over-sanitized, and the ground-floor shops sell handicrafts, incense, spices, and traditional Qatari items rather than tourist trinkets.

Behind the souq, the working harbour still has dhows and fishing boats moored at the quayside. The view from the waterfront — boats in the foreground, the restored façades beyond — is one of the most photogenic in the Doha region. Come in the late afternoon when the light is good and the temperature drops to something manageable.
