Manaratain Mosque is a traditional Al Wakrah mosque built in the 1940s on the site of earlier mosques. Its coral-stone and rock construction, baked-mud roof, and simple form show older Qatari building methods before the modern expansion of the town.

Non-Muslim visitors should not enter, but the exterior is worth a look while exploring Al Wakrah's older coastal area.
