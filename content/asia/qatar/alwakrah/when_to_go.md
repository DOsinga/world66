---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

October through April is the only practical time to visit Al Wakrah — or anywhere outdoors in Qatar. Temperatures are comfortable (18–30°C), the souq is pleasant to walk, and the beach is usable. From May through September, temperatures exceed 40°C and outdoor activity is essentially impossible during daylight hours. Al Wakrah is a half-day trip from Doha and does not need to be planned around any particular season beyond the basic Gulf climate window.
