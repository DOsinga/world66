Al Shaqab is Doha's major equestrian centre, built around Qatar's prestige association with Arabian horses. The complex hosts training, competitions, breeding programmes, and equestrian events on the Education City side of the capital.

It is not a casual drop-in attraction every day, so check the event calendar or tour possibilities before travelling out. When something is on, it gives a distinctive counterpoint to Doha's museums, malls, and waterfront sights.
