Al Zubarah is Qatar's only UNESCO World Heritage Site, lying about 105 km north of Doha on the north-west coast. The site preserves the remains of an 18th-century pearling town — streets, houses, a mosque, wells, and a substantial fort — abandoned after 1811 and untouched since. The setting, on a flat coastal plain with desert on one side and the Gulf on the other, is austere and genuinely remote-feeling.

The fort at Al Zubarah (19th century) has been restored and contains a good exhibition on the site's history and the pearling economy. The adjacent tell gives views over the ruins. The site is best visited in cooler months; it is an exposed, shadeless landscape. The drive along the north-western coast from Doha passes salt flats, flamingo-frequented lagoons, and the ruins of other small settlements.
