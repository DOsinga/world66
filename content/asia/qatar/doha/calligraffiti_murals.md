The Calligraffiti Murals are a series of large wall works painted by French-Tunisian artist eL Seed in 2013. Commissioned by Qatari cultural and public works authorities, the project uses Arabic calligraphy forms at urban scale.

The murals are best treated as a short public-art stop rather than a major museum visit. They are most rewarding for visitors already exploring Doha's western side or heading toward Aspire Zone.
