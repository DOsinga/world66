Fanar Qatar Islamic Cultural Center is easy to spot near Souq Waqif by its spiral minaret. It is aimed at visitors who want a practical introduction to Islam and Qatari culture, with exhibitions, calligraphy, Arabic classes, mosque visits, and cultural talks.
