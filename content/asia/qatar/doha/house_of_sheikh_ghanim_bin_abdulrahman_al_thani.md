The House of Sheikh Ghanim Bin Abdulrahman Al Thani is a restored early-20th-century merchant house in Al Wakra. Built for a local pearl trader, it gives a small but useful glimpse of the domestic architecture that predated Doha's glass-and-steel boom.

The visit is informal, so it works best as a short stop while exploring Al Wakra Old Souq and the waterfront. Ask on site if the house is open before making a special trip.
