The beach at Katara Cultural Village is one of the few stretches of public sandy beach accessible close to central Doha. It is clean, calm, and backed by the cultural village's pale-stone buildings. Swimming is possible most of the year, though the water temperature in winter drops enough to make it brisk.

A section of the beach is reserved for women and families; the remainder is mixed. The beachfront cafés serve good food and are open late in the cooler months. The beach fills on weekends and public holidays; weekday mornings are quieter. It is not a destination beach, but a genuine convenience given how few accessible beaches Doha has close to the centre.
