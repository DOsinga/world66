The Orientalist Museum is connected to Qatar's large collection of Orientalist paintings, watercolours, drawings, and prints. The collection traces European views of the Middle East and North Africa from the 18th century onward, making it an interesting companion to Doha's bigger museum stops.

Access can be irregular, so check current opening arrangements before making a special trip. If it is open, the museum is most rewarding for visitors with a serious interest in art history.
