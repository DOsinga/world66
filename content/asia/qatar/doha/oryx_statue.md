The oversized statue of Orry the Oryx — mascot of the 2006 Asian Games — stands near Aspire Zone in southern Doha. The animal itself, the Arabian Oryx, is Qatar's national symbol: a pale, long-horned antelope of the desert that was hunted to extinction in Qatar in the early 1970s and has since been reintroduced through captive breeding.

The statue is a cheerful piece of public art in a city that tends toward the monumental. It marks the location of the 2006 games infrastructure and is a common stopping point for families visiting the Aspire Zone. The real oryx can be seen at Al Khor Zoo and at the Al Maha Wildlife Sanctuary near the Inland Sea.
