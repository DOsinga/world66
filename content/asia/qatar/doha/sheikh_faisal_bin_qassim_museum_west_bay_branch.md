The West Bay branch of the Sheikh Faisal bin Qassim Museum shows part of the private collection in central Doha, with carpets, furniture, and domestic objects from Qatar, the Gulf, and the wider Middle East. It is smaller than the main Al Samriya museum but easier to reach if you are already in West Bay.
