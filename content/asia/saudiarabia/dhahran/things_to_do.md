---
title: Things to Do
type: section
---

Dhahran's compound is not open to casual visitors, but the surrounding area has two genuinely worthwhile stops. Ithra — the King Abdulaziz Center for World Culture — is Saudi Arabia's most ambitious cultural project in decades and is open to the public. Dammam Well No. 7, on the Aramco grounds, is the spot where commercial oil production in Saudi Arabia began and is sometimes accessible on organized visits.
