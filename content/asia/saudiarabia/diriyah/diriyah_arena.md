The Diriyah Arena, a few minutes east of At-Turaif, is the outdoor venue for the Diriyah Season's big-ticket events — heavyweight boxing matches, tennis exhibitions, large-scale concerts. The Anthony Joshua and Tyson Fury fights of 2019 and 2023 both took place here.

Of interest only if there is an event on. Programming runs from November to February.
