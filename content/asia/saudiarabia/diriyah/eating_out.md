title: Eating Out
type: section
---

Almost all the worthwhile dining in Diriyah is concentrated on or near [Bujairi Terrace](/asia/saudiarabia/diriyah/bujairi_terrace), the recent development overlooking At-Turaif. The mix is heavy on international names — Hakkasan, Long Chim, Brasserie Bouligny, Cipriani — alongside a smaller selection of contemporary Saudi and Levantine kitchens.

- **Najd Village** — the local-favourite for traditional Saudi food: kabsa, mandi, jareesh, served on a cushion in a recreated mud-brick majlis. Several branches; the Diriyah one is the most atmospheric.
- **Suhail at Bujairi** — modern Saudi cooking by chef Mohammed Alkhaldi, the most ambitious local kitchen in the development.
- **Lugmety** — Najdi street food done with care, dates and qahwa coffee taken seriously.
