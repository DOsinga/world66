---
title: "Beaches"
type: section
---

Jeddah sits on the Red Sea but the central Corniche is a paved walkway, not a beach — and the public stretches close to the city are not the place to swim. The proper beach action is at the **private beach resorts** north of the city, around Obhur Creek and out toward Durrat Al Arus.

These resorts charge a day-pass fee (typically 100–300 SAR) and offer clean sand, swimming, watersports, and snorkelling off the resort jetties. Rules on swimwear have eased since 2019 — women can wear bikinis at most private resorts — but conservative dress is still expected at public beaches.

The reefs themselves are the real prize. See the [Red Sea Diving](/asia/saudiarabia/jeddah/red_sea_diving) entry for details on dive operators and sites.
