---
title: "Getting Around"
type: section
---

Jeddah is a sprawling, car-centric city — main areas spread 25 km along the Red Sea coast — and public transport is limited. **Uber and Careem** are the easiest way to get around for foreigners. Both apps work well, fares are cheap (a typical cross-town ride is 30–50 SAR), and drivers know the city better than any map app.

The new **Jeddah Metro** is under construction; the first lines opened in 2024 and the network is gradually extending. For now, expect to use ride-hail for most journeys.

**Renting a car** gives you the most freedom, but Jeddah traffic is chaotic and aggressive — only worth it if you plan day trips up the coast. Women can drive in Saudi Arabia (since 2018) on an international or local licence.

**Walking** works inside Al Balad and along the Corniche; elsewhere distances are too great and pavements are intermittent.
