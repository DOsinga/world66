A 35 km waterfront walk along the Red Sea, running from the old port at the southern end up through the new districts to Obhur Creek in the north. The Corniche is the social heart of the city — by 7 PM in any month except the hottest, families are spread on rugs on the lawns, children play in fountains, and joggers, cyclists, and scooter-riders work the path.

Highlights along its length include the King Fahd Fountain (the tallest in the world), the Al Rahmah floating mosque, dozens of public sculptures, and a string of cafes and seafood restaurants. The southern stretch was recently rebuilt as Jeddah Waterfront and is the most pleasant section to walk in the cooler months.
