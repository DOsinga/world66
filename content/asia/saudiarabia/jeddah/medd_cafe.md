A specialty coffee shop in one of the restored merchant houses in Al Balad — high coral-stone walls, exposed wooden roshan screens, low cushioned seating, and a serious espresso programme. Single-origin beans, pour-overs, and a short menu of pastries and Saudi-Arabic breakfast plates.

The roof terrace is the seat to look for. A useful afternoon stop between Naseef House and Souk Al-Alawi. Closed during prayer times.
