teamLab Borderless Jeddah brings the Japanese collective's immersive digital-art rooms to the edge of Al Balad. Expect darkened spaces, projected flowers, responsive light, mirrored rooms, and installations designed to be walked through rather than viewed from a distance.

It is a contemporary counterpoint to Jeddah's coral-stone old town and Red Sea identity. Book ahead if timed entry is required, and go when you are ready for a sensory, camera-heavy experience rather than a traditional gallery.
