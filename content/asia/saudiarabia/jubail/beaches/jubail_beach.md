The main public beach north of the Jubail Corniche is a long, clean strip of sand with shallow, calm water — the Gulf here is warm year-round and the seabed shelves gently, making it ideal for children. The Royal Commission maintains the beach facilities and the water quality is generally good away from the port zone. Weekends bring local families with grills and tents; weekday mornings are quiet. The beach faces east, so sunrises are spectacular over the water, and the light is better for swimming in the morning before the afternoon heat peaks.
