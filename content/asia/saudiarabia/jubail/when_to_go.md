---
title: When to Go
type: section
---

Jubail has a desert climate moderated slightly by the Gulf. The best time to visit is between November and March, when daytime temperatures are comfortable — typically 18–25°C — and the evenings are cool enough for walking along the corniche. This is also peak season for migratory birds at the wildlife sanctuary and mangrove reserve.

From June to September the heat is intense and humidity from the Gulf makes it oppressive; temperatures regularly exceed 45°C. Most outdoor activity shifts to early morning or after sunset. The industrial facilities operate year-round regardless, but any leisure or sightseeing is genuinely difficult in high summer.

Ramadan is observed strictly in Jubail; restaurants and cafes are closed during daylight hours and public behaviour expectations are more conservative. The city is quieter and some facilities have reduced hours, but it is not an unpleasant time to be there for those who are prepared.
