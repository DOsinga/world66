Elephant Rock is a freestanding sandstone formation near Al-Ula whose shape gives it its name. It is one of the area's most photographed natural landmarks, especially near sunset when the rock and surrounding desert turn warm orange.

Access around the base may be tied to the cafe area, but the formation is still easy to appreciate from the surrounding viewpoints.
