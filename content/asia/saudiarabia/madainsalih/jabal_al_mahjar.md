Jabal al-Mahjar is one of Hegra's Nabataean tomb groups, cut into four parallel sandstone outcrops. The facades are smaller and plainer than the site's showpiece tombs, but the clustered rocks make the necropolis feel broad and lived-in.

It is worth pausing here on a Hegra tour, especially if you want to compare the different tomb areas rather than just see the biggest facades.
