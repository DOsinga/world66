The Old City of Dadan preserves remains from one of the ancient kingdoms of northwest Arabia. Its best-known feature is the Lion Tombs, fragile rock-cut burials whose carved lions are viewed from a distance; visits usually run through the Dadan visitor centre and excavation area.
