Al-Haramain Museum, also known as the Exhibition of the Two Holy Mosques Architecture, preserves objects, models, inscriptions, and architectural pieces connected with the holy mosques in Mecca and Medina. For pilgrims interested in the physical history of the Haram, it adds useful context beyond the rituals themselves.

The museum is outside the immediate mosque precinct, and access arrangements can change, so confirm visiting requirements locally. Like the rest of Mecca, it is relevant only to Muslim visitors, but for them it is one of the city's most meaningful museum stops.
