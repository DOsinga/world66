Masjid al-Khayf is one of the most historically significant mosques in Mecca, located in the valley of Mina near the Jamarat area. According to Islamic tradition, 70 prophets have prayed in this location, and the mosque is specifically mentioned in the Sunnah in connection with prayers during the Hajj period. Pilgrims staying in Mina typically pray at this mosque during the days of the pilgrimage.

The mosque has been enlarged multiple times and can now accommodate tens of thousands of worshippers. Its current form dates largely from Saudi-era reconstructions. It is particularly busy during the Tashreeq days of Hajj, when pilgrims remain in Mina for additional prayers and the stoning rituals.
