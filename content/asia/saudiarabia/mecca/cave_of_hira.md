The Cave of Hira is one of the most significant religious sites around Mecca, set high on Jabal al-Nour. Islamic tradition holds that this is where the Prophet Muhammad received the first revelation of the Quran, giving the small cave a meaning far beyond its physical size.

The climb is steep and can be crowded, especially in hot weather, so it should be approached with patience and respect. For pilgrims who make the ascent, the experience is about devotion, memory, and the view back toward the holy city.
