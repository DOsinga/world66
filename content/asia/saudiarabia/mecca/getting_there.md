---
title: Getting There
type: section
---

**By air:** King Abdulaziz International Airport in Jeddah (JED) is the nearest major airport, about 80 kilometres from Mecca. A dedicated Hajj Terminal handles the millions of pilgrims who arrive during the Hajj season; it is the largest single airline terminal in the world by area. Direct flights serve Jeddah from across the Muslim world, the Middle East, Europe, and North America. During Hajj season, Hajj charter flights operate from dozens of countries.

**By rail:** The Haramain High Speed Railway connects Mecca's Al-Mashaaer Al-Mugaddassah station to Jeddah and Medina. The journey from Jeddah takes about 30 minutes. The line was built specifically to serve the pilgrimage and operates at high frequency during Hajj; service is available year-round for Umrah pilgrims.

**By road:** Mecca is connected to Jeddah by multiple highways. The King Fahd Highway and the road via Taif are the main approaches from the east and south. All road approaches have checkpoints verifying Muslim identity before entry to Mecca.

**Hajj quotas:** The Saudi government allocates Hajj visas by country based on a quota of approximately 1 pilgrim per 1,000 Muslims in each country. For many countries this means waiting lists of years or decades.
