Jabal al-Nour — the Mountain of Light — is a hill about 4 kilometres north of the Grand Mosque, reached by a long stairway of several hundred steps. At its summit is the Cave of Hira, where the Prophet Muhammad is believed to have received the first revelation of the Quran. It is one of the most visited sites in Mecca for pilgrims who want to see the cave associated with the beginning of Islamic revelation.

The climb takes 20–30 minutes and is possible for most pilgrims in reasonable health, though the path can be crowded during peak season. At the summit, the views over Mecca and the surrounding hills are extensive. The cave itself is modest in size and marked with a plaque. Pilgrims typically pray at the cave entrance before descending. A smaller cave below the summit is also visited.
