Mina is the valley east of Mecca that becomes one of the central stages of the Hajj. Its vast tent city, the Jamarat complex, and the movement of pilgrims through prescribed rites make it one of the most logistically extraordinary religious landscapes in the world.

For Muslim travellers, Mina's meaning is inseparable from pilgrimage practice rather than sightseeing. Outside Hajj, the scale is still striking, but the place only truly makes sense when understood through the rituals that bring millions of people here.
