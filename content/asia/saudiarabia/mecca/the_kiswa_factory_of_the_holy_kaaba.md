The Kiswa Factory of the Holy Kaaba is where the embroidered black cloth that covers the Kaaba is traditionally made. For travellers interested in the craft and ritual life around Hajj, it gives context to one of Islam's most recognisable sacred objects.

Access can be limited, so check locally before making a special trip. Even as a brief stop, it helps explain the work behind a ceremony most visitors only see from a distance.
