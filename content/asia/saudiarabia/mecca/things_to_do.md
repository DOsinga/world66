---
title: Things to Do
type: section
---

All the major sites in Mecca are within or immediately adjacent to the Grand Mosque complex. For pilgrims performing Hajj or Umrah, the rituals provide the structure: tawaf around the Kaaba, sa'i between Safa and Marwa, the Mount Arafat vigil, and the stoning of the pillars at Mina. Visitors who are not on pilgrimage can explore the historic areas around the mosque, the Abraj al-Bait tower complex, and the surrounding hill sites with their connections to early Islamic history.
