---
title: When to Go
type: section
---

Mecca can be visited by Muslims for Umrah (the lesser pilgrimage) throughout the year. Hajj, the major pilgrimage that is obligatory once in a lifetime for those who can perform it, takes place during specific days in the Islamic month of Dhul Hijjah and moves earlier in the Gregorian calendar by about 11 days each year.

**Hajj season** is by far the busiest time, with 2–3 million pilgrims in the city simultaneously. The experience is unparalleled in terms of scale and spiritual intensity, but the crowds, heat, and logistical demands are significant. Hajj requires official pilgrimage visas allocated by country; tourists cannot travel to Mecca and simply join the Hajj.

**Umrah** can be performed any time. The period just before and during Ramadan is the most popular for Umrah, with tens of millions performing the pilgrimage in the month surrounding the Islamic holy month. Outside of these peak periods, Mecca is considerably less crowded and the grand mosque is more accessible for prayer and contemplation.

**Climate:** Mecca has a hot desert climate. Summer temperatures regularly exceed 40°C (104°F). The Hajj season falls in summer in some years, making heat management a serious issue for pilgrims. Cooler months (December–February) are more comfortable but still warm by European standards.
