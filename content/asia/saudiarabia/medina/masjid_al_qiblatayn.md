Masjid al-Qiblatayn is one of Medina's important religious sites, associated in Islamic tradition with the change in prayer direction from Jerusalem to Mecca. Its significance makes it more than a neighborhood mosque for many visitors to the city.

Access and expectations depend on pilgrimage schedules and local rules, so non-local visitors should plan respectfully. For those tracing Medina's sacred geography, it is a meaningful stop outside the immediate area of the Prophet's Mosque.
