Masjid Jumua, also written Al Jum'ah Mosque, is a historically important mosque south of central Medina. Tradition associates the site with the Prophet Muhammad's first Friday prayer after the Hijrah, which gives it a particular place in the religious geography between Quba and the Prophet's Mosque.

The current structure is modern, so the visit is about memory and location rather than old fabric. For pilgrims tracing Medina's early Islamic sites, it is a meaningful stop that helps connect the migration story to the present city.
