Mount Uhud rises north of Medina and is inseparable from the Battle of Uhud, one of the defining events in early Islamic history. The mountain itself is stark and reddish, but its meaning comes from the battlefield, cemetery, and stories attached to the site.

For Muslim visitors it is a place of memory and reflection, not just a viewpoint. Go respectfully, avoid turning the cemetery area into a photo stop, and allow time to understand why the landscape matters before moving on.
