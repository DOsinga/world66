---
title: "Day Trips"
type: section
---

**Diriyah** is the most obvious and rewarding day trip — the UNESCO-listed capital of the first Saudi state, about fifteen kilometres from central Riyadh via Wadi Hanifah. Plan at least half a day for the At-Turaif District and the surrounding area.

**Al-Uyayna** is a village about 25 kilometres northwest of Riyadh, birthplace of Muhammad ibn Abd al-Wahhab, the eighteenth-century theologian whose alliance with the Saudi ruling family shaped the religious identity of the kingdom. A small heritage village has been preserved there.

**Thumamah National Park**, about 75 kilometres north of Riyadh, is an accessible patch of desert with sand dunes, desert wildlife, and walking trails — the closest thing to open desert accessible by car from the city without significant off-road driving.

**The Janadriyah National Heritage and Culture Festival**, held annually about 45 kilometres north of Riyadh, is one of the largest traditional festivals in the region: camel racing, traditional crafts, folk music, and food from across the kingdom, organised by the National Guard. The dates vary year to year but typically fall in February or March.
