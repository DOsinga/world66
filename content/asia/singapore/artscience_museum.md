The ArtScience Museum, shaped like a giant white lotus flower with ten finger-like petals, sits at the water's edge of Marina Bay Sands. Moshe Safdie designed it to collect rainwater through its central well and channel it into a reflecting pool, and the building is as much an attraction as its contents.

The permanent draw is Future World, a digital art installation by the Japanese collective teamLab — a series of darkened rooms filled with immersive, interactive light projections that respond to movement and touch. It is consistently one of the most popular paid attractions in the city. The museum also hosts major touring exhibitions, often at the intersection of art, design, and technology that the name promises.
