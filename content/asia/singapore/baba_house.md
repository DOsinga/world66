Baba House is a restored blue shophouse on the edge of Chinatown, kept as a careful portrait of a Peranakan family home. Its rooms show the carved furniture, porcelain, beadwork, and household details of Straits Chinese life in a setting that feels more domestic than museum-like.

Visits are usually by guided tour, so book ahead rather than dropping in. It pairs well with the Peranakan Museum or a walk through the old shophouse streets around Neil Road.
