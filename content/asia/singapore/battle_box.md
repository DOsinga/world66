Battle Box is the former underground command bunker used by British forces in Malaya during World War II. The museum focuses on the days before Singapore's surrender in 1942, with restored rooms, wartime context, and guided interpretation inside the hilltop bunker.

The site is in Fort Canning Park, close to the museum district but uphill from Dhoby Ghaut. It is a strong stop for visitors interested in Singapore's wartime history.
