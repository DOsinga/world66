Singapore Botanic Gardens is the city's great green escape: a UNESCO-listed landscape of palms, tropical trees, lawns, lakes and remnant rainforest just west of Orchard Road. It is large enough for a slow half-day walk, with shady paths, sculpture, picnic spots and themed gardens that make the city feel suddenly less dense.

The National Orchid Garden is the paid highlight, but the free parts of the gardens are worth lingering over too. Come early for cooler weather, or on a Sunday evening when concerts sometimes fill the old bandstand with a very Singaporean mix of families, joggers and music.
