L'Espresso has been serving afternoon tea at the Goodwood Park Hotel on Scotts Road since the 1970s, making it one of Singapore's most established café institutions. The setting is the hotel's ground-floor terrace and indoor lounge, with cakes, scones, sandwiches and pastries made in-house by the hotel kitchen.

The afternoon tea spread changes with the seasons but consistently draws on both classic English and local influences — expect kueh alongside cucumber sandwiches. Weekend high tea is popular enough to require a reservation several days in advance. The hotel itself, a 1900 heritage building with a distinctive tower, is worth a look regardless.

Open daily. Reservations recommended for weekend high tea.
