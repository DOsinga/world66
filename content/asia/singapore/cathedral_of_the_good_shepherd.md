The Cathedral of the Good Shepherd is Singapore's Roman Catholic cathedral and one of the country's oldest Catholic churches. Its restored interior, pipe organ, and quiet compound make it a worthwhile heritage stop near the Civic District.

It is an active church, so visits work best outside services. Pair it with nearby museums, Fort Canning, or the Bras Basah heritage area.
