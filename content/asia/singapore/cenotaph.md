The Cenotaph stands in Esplanade Park as Singapore's main memorial to British subjects from the Straits Settlements who died in the First World War. A second dedication was later added for the Second World War.

It is a short stop rather than a long visit, but it fits naturally into a walk between the riverfront, the Asian Civilisations Museum, and Marina Bay.
