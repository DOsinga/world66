Central Catchment Nature Reserve is Singapore's largest wild green space, a mix of secondary rainforest, remnant primary forest, wetlands, and reservoirs. MacRitchie Reservoir and Upper Peirce Reservoir sit within the reserve, and the trails give a real sense of tropical forest without leaving the city.

Wildlife is part of the appeal: long-tailed macaques are common, and quieter paths can produce birds, reptiles, and forest insects. The reserve is large enough that it works best as a proper half-day walk rather than a quick park stop.
