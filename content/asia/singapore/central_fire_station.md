Singapore's Central Fire Station is the city's oldest surviving fire station still in use, easily recognised by its red-and-white brick facade near the Civic District. The attached Civil Defence Heritage Gallery tells the story of firefighting and emergency services in Singapore, with antique fire engines and equipment on display.

Saturday open-house sessions give a closer look at the working station when places are available. Even outside those times, the building is worth a short stop while walking between Armenian Street, Fort Canning, and the river.
