Changi Boardwalk follows the coast at the eastern edge of Singapore, linking short waterfront sections between Changi Beach Club and Changi Sailing Club. It is an easy place for a sea breeze, sunset views, and a slower look at a part of the island that feels far from the centre.

The walk is simple rather than dramatic, but that is part of the appeal. Combine it with Changi Village or the ferry point for Pulau Ubin if you want a relaxed half day on this side of Singapore.
