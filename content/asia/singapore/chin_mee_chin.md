Chin Mee Chin Confectionery is the most loved of Katong's old coffee shops, a kopitiam on East Coast Road that has been serving charcoal-toasted kaya toast, soft-boiled eggs, and freshly baked buns since 1925. Its marble-topped tables, mosaic floor, and rattan chairs are the genuine article rather than a revival, and for decades it was the neighbourhood's morning institution.

After a brief closure it reopened under new management, keeping the original interior and the signature items: the kaya (coconut jam) toast, the cream horns, and the kopi. It captures the Eurasian-Peranakan breakfast culture of old Katong, and combined with a wander down Koon Seng Road's painted shophouses it makes the best introduction to the eastern neighbourhood.
