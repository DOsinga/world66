The Chinese Garden and Japanese Garden form the most distinctive part of Jurong Lake Gardens. Bridges, pagodas, water features, and landscaped islands make this a good west-side walk when you want greenery without leaving the city.

It is especially atmospheric during lantern and New Year events, but even on an ordinary day the gardens are a calm break from Singapore's denser central sights.
