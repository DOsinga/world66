Singapore's old City Hall is one of the country's most important civic buildings. The Japanese surrender was accepted here in 1945, and Lee Kuan Yew later stood on its steps to announce self-government and independence-era milestones.

Together with the former Supreme Court next door, the building now forms part of the National Gallery Singapore. Even if you do not go inside for the art, pause outside for the long neoclassical facade and the sense of how much modern Singapore passed through this one set of steps.
