East Coast Park is Singapore's long seaside playground, stretching along the island's southeastern shore with beaches, cycling paths, food courts, watersports, and family play areas. The water is not pristine, but the palms, sea breeze, and steady stream of cyclists and skaters give it a relaxed weekend energy.

Come to cycle, walk, picnic, or eat at East Coast Lagoon Food Village after dark. Families should also look for Coastal PlayGrove, while more active visitors can try the skatepark, kayaking, or other watersports when operators are open.
