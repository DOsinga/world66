Esplanade Theatres on the Bay is Singapore's major performing arts centre, instantly recognizable for its spiky twin domes beside Marina Bay. It hosts theatre, dance, opera, concerts, and free outdoor performances on some weekends.

Even without a ticket, it is worth a stop for the waterfront setting and rooftop views over Marina Bay.
