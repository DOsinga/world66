The Former Ford Factory is a World War II museum in the building where British forces surrendered Singapore to Japan in 1942. Its exhibitions cover the Japanese occupation, the surrender, and the experiences of civilians and prisoners of war.

The recreated board room where the surrender took place is the key room. Pair it with Changi Chapel and Museum if you want a fuller view of wartime Singapore.
