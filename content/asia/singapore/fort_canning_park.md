Fort Canning Park is a hilltop green space layered with Singapore history, from ancient Malay royalty to British military use. The park includes Fort Gate, World War II relics, garden paths, city views, and the well-known spiral staircase "tree tunnel."

It is an easy stop between the museum district, Clarke Quay, and Dhoby Ghaut, and it works well as a short heritage walk rather than just a patch of greenery.
