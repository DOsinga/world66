Fuk Tak Chi began as a small temple serving Singapore's Hakka and Cantonese communities. It has since been converted into a museum about the lives of early Chinese immigrants, with objects and displays that connect Chinatown's trading streets to the people who built lives around them.

The museum is small, but it is a useful counterpoint to the larger temples and food centres nearby. Drop in while walking between Telok Ayer, Chinatown, and the Singapore River.
