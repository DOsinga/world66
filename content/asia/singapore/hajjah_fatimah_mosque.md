Hajjah Fatimah Mosque is one of Singapore's most important historic mosques after Sultan Mosque. It was named for the Malay benefactor who funded it, and its design mixes Malay Muslim and colonial British influences.

The leaning minaret is the detail most visitors notice first. Dress modestly when visiting, as at other active mosques.
