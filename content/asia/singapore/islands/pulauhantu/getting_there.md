---
title: Getting There
type: section
---

There is no scheduled ferry service to Pulau Hantu. The only way to reach it is by chartering a bumboat — a traditional wooden motorboat — from either Marina South Pier or West Coast Pier. Operators advertise online and at the piers; expect to pay around S$120–180 for a return trip for a small group, with the boat waiting while you spend a few hours on the island. Weekends see more operators competing for business, which can bring prices down slightly. Arrange your charter in advance, especially during the peak snorkeling season from November to March when boats fill up fast.

The journey takes about 20 to 30 minutes from Marina South Pier and slightly less from West Coast Pier. Pack everything you need for the day: there are no shops, no cafes, and no equipment rental on the island. Bring plenty of water, sunscreen, a packed lunch, and your own snorkeling gear. The only facilities on the larger island are a basic shelter and a toilet block, so manage your expectations accordingly. Most visitors treat this as a self-contained day trip, arriving mid-morning and heading back in the afternoon before the light fades.
