---
title: "Day Trips"
type: section
---

Since Pulau Sekang itself is inaccessible, the southern islands nearby make for good day trips if you are in Singapore and want to see a different side of the city-state.

**[Pulau Hantu](/asia/singapore/islands/pulauhantu)** is the most popular choice for snorkelling and exploring. Charter a bumboat from Marina South Pier or West Coast Pier. There are basic shelters but no food, so bring supplies.

**[Sisters' Islands](/asia/singapore/islands/sisterislands)** comprise Big Sister's Island and Little Sister's Island, a short distance to the east. Big Sister's Island has been developed as a marine park, with guided snorkelling and a focus on coral restoration.

**[Kusu Island](/asia/singapore/islands/kusuisland)** lies to the northeast and has a Taoist shrine and Malay keramat (sacred site) that draw pilgrims during the annual Kusu Island pilgrimage season in October. Ferry service runs from Marina South Pier.

**[Lazarus Island](/asia/singapore/islands/lazarusisland)** is connected to St John's Island by a causeway and has a quiet beach that is one of Singapore's least developed stretches of coastline. Ferry service runs from Marina South Pier.
