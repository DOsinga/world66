---
title: "Getting There"
type: section
---

Pulau Sekang is not accessible to the public. There is no ferry service and no authorised landing point for visitors. The island lies south of Sentosa in the strait between Singapore and the Indonesian Riau Islands, visible from the water but off-limits.

For access to Singapore's southern islands, the nearest practical option is [Pulau Hantu](/asia/singapore/islands/pulauhantu), which can be reached by chartering a bumboat from Marina South Pier or West Coast Pier.
