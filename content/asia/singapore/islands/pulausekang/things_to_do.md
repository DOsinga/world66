Access to Pulau Sekang is restricted and there is no public ferry service. The island is of historical interest primarily to those researching the kampung settlements that once dotted Singapore's southern islands. The nearby [Pulau Hantu](/asia/singapore/islands/pulauhantu) offers similar coastal scenery with coral reefs accessible to snorkellers, and is a practical alternative for visitors interested in the southern island environment.
