---
title: "Getting There"
type: section
---

Sentosa sits 500 metres off Singapore's southern coast. Three routes connect it to the mainland, all quick and straightforward.

The [Sentosa Express](/asia/singapore/islands/sentosa/sentosa_express) monorail departs from VivoCity mall, directly above HarbourFront MRT (Circle and North-East lines). The ride takes about four minutes. The pedestrian [Sentosa Boardwalk](/asia/singapore/islands/sentosa/sentosa_boardwalk) runs beside the road causeway — a flat ten-minute walk with views over the strait, free of charge. The [Singapore Cable Car](/asia/singapore/islands/sentosa/singapore_cable_car) offers the most scenic approach, climbing from HarbourFront Tower 2 across to the island's northern ridge, with sweeping views over Keppel Harbour.

Once on the island, free shuttle buses loop between the main zones, and a beach tram runs the length of the southern coastline. Walking between the resort and the beaches takes about 20 minutes.
