iFly Singapore operates the world's largest indoor skydiving tunnel — a vertical wind chamber 56 feet in diameter that generates winds up to 270 km/h, enough to hold an adult body aloft. It sits near the beach station end of the Sentosa Express route, within walking distance of the cable car station. First-timers go through a briefing and are supervised by instructors throughout; no experience is needed. The experience lasts around two minutes of actual flight time but the session — briefing, gearing up, practice, and two flights — runs about two hours in total. Book in advance; walk-in availability is limited on weekends.
