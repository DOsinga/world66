Palawan Beach sits in the middle of Sentosa's southern coast and claims — with a small suspension bridge to a tiny islet — the title of southernmost point of continental Asia. The islet itself is a minor attraction, but the beach is pleasantly wide and less frenetic than Siloso to the west. A family-friendly zone with a children's playground runs along the promenade. The lagoon is sheltered enough for casual paddling and the beach tram connects to both neighbouring beaches.
