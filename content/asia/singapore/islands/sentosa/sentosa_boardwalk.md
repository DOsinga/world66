The Sentosa Boardwalk is a covered pedestrian and bicycle path running alongside the road causeway from VivoCity mall to the island entrance. The walk takes about ten minutes and is free — the simplest and cheapest way onto the island. Moving walkways assist the uphill section on the mainland side. The boardwalk gives views over the western inlet and the cable car towers overhead. It connects directly to the Sentosa Express station and the resort area, making it a practical alternative to the monorail if you are already at HarbourFront and not carrying heavy bags.
