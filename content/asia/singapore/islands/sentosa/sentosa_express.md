The Sentosa Express is a four-station monorail connecting VivoCity mall at HarbourFront MRT to the island's main zones: Waterfront (Resorts World), Imbiah Lookout, and Beach Station. The journey from VivoCity to Waterfront station takes about four minutes. Trains run every four to eight minutes during operating hours (7am–midnight). A flat fare covers the whole route; the ticket doubles as a ride token on the beach tram at the southern end of the island. It is the most straightforward way to reach Resorts World and Universal Studios from the MRT network.
