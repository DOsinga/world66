Tanjong Beach is the easternmost and quietest of Sentosa's three beaches — fewer facilities than Siloso, less family traffic than Palawan, and a correspondingly more relaxed atmosphere. A single beach club, Tanjong Beach Club, occupies one end and hosts weekend pool parties that draw a mixed crowd of expats and tourists. If you want to actually lie on the sand without navigating around volleyball nets and rental kiosks, this is the one to choose. The beach tram terminates here.
