---
title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Sentosa packs more per square kilometre than almost anywhere in Southeast Asia. The northern end is dominated by the Resorts World complex — Universal Studios, S.E.A. Aquarium, and the casino. The southern coastline has three beaches and Fort Siloso. iFly Singapore, near the cable car station, is the world's largest indoor skydiving tunnel. Weekdays are significantly quieter.
