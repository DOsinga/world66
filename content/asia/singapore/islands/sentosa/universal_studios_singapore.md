Universal Studios Singapore is the anchor of Resorts World Sentosa and the only Universal park in Southeast Asia. Seven themed zones — including Jurassic World, Transformers, and the Battlestar Galactica duelling roller coasters — pack a compact footprint that allows a full run-through in a single day. Arrive at opening time to hit the major rides before queues build. Single-day tickets are sold at the gate and online; online booking lets you skip the ticket counter.

The park suits older children and adults more than toddlers — most high-adrenaline rides have minimum height requirements. The Madagascar and Far Far Away zones cater to younger visitors.
