Jamae Mosque is one of Singapore's oldest mosques, built in the 1830s by Tamil Muslims in the middle of Chinatown. Its stepped minarets and Indian-influenced facade stand out among the surrounding shophouses.

Together with nearby Sri Mariamman Temple and the Buddha Tooth Relic Temple, it shows how tightly Singapore's religious communities have long overlapped in the old city.
