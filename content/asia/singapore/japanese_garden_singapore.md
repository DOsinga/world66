The Japanese Garden is part of the Jurong Lake Gardens area, laid out with bridges, ponds, clipped planting, and Japanese-style landscape details.

It is a calmer west-side alternative to Singapore's central gardens, especially if you are already visiting Jurong or want an easy lakeside walk.
