The Jews of Singapore Museum tells the story of a small community with an outsized place in the city's commercial and civic history. It sits beside Maghain Aboth Synagogue, the oldest surviving synagogue in Southeast Asia, and works best as a focused stop for travellers interested in Singapore's minority histories.
