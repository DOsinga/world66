Kampong Lorong Buangkok is the last surviving traditional village on mainland Singapore. The wooden houses, narrow lanes, and quiet gardens are a sharp contrast with the high-rise city around them, and they give visitors a rare look at the island's older rural life.

This is still a residential place, not an open-air museum. Walk gently, keep to public paths, and do not peer into homes or treat the village as a staged attraction.
