Koon Seng Road is one of Singapore's best-known rows of Peranakan shophouses. The most photographed stretch has brightly painted facades, decorative plasterwork, ceramic tiles, and narrow five-foot ways that show the character of old Joo Chiat.

It is a quick stop, but a rewarding one if you are walking between Katong's food streets and Joo Chiat's heritage lanes. Visit early or late in the day for better light and fewer parked cars.
