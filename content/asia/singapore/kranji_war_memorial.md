Kranji War Memorial is the main Commonwealth war cemetery in Singapore. More than 4,400 service members from the Second World War are buried here, and the memorial walls record the names of over 24,000 people killed across South-East Asia whose bodies were never recovered.

The site is quieter than Changi Chapel, but just as serious. It stands beside Kranji State Cemetery, where Singapore's first two presidents are buried.
