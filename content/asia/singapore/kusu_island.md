Legend has it that a magical turtle turned itself into an island to save two shipwrecked sailors — one Malay, one Chinese. A Malay shrine (keramat) and a Chinese Taoist temple (Tua Pek Kong) now stand on the island as reminders of that legend. Each year in the ninth lunar month, thousands of pilgrims make the crossing for the annual Kusu Island pilgrimage, throwing coins into the temple's Wishing Well.

Outside the pilgrimage season, Kusu is quiet — a sheltered lagoon, a sandy swimming beach, and the temple grounds largely to yourself. Ferries run from Marina South Pier; the crossing takes about 20 minutes.
