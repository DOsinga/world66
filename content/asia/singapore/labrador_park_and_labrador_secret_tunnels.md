Labrador Park is a coastal green space with sea views toward Sentosa and one of Singapore's more tangible World War II landscapes. Bunkers, gun emplacements, and old military remains sit among the paths and trees.

It is a good choice when you want history without spending the whole afternoon indoors. The secret tunnels are not always open, but the park itself is pleasant for a walk and gives the city a rougher, more layered edge than the downtown waterfront.
