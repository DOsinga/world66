The Lim Bo Seng Memorial is a small pagoda-like monument near the Singapore River. It commemorates Lim Bo Seng, a World War II resistance figure who worked with Force 136 against the Japanese occupation and died in prison in 1944 after refusing to betray his comrades.

It is a short, worthwhile pause when walking between the Asian Civilisations Museum, the riverfront, and Esplanade Park.
