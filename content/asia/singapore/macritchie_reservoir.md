MacRitchie Reservoir is one of Singapore's best escapes into real tropical greenery, with forest trails wrapped around the water and long routes that link into the Central Catchment area. The big draw is the HSBC TreeTop Walk, a suspension bridge above the canopy with views over the forest.

The trails range from easy waterside walks to longer, hotter loops toward Bukit Timah. Macaques are common, so keep food packed away, and bring water even for short walks.
