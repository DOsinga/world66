Madame Tussauds Singapore is the Sentosa branch of the wax museum chain, with figures aimed especially at Singaporean and Asian visitors. Displays include local and regional celebrities as well as international names.

It is a light, family-friendly stop rather than a museum for deep sightseeing. Combine it with other Sentosa attractions if you are already spending time on the island.
