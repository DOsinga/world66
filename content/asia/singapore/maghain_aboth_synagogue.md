Maghain Aboth Synagogue is the oldest active synagogue in Southeast Asia, completed in 1878 for Singapore's Baghdadi Jewish community. It is a rare visible reminder of a small community that has been part of the city since the colonial period.

The building is still an Orthodox synagogue, so check visiting arrangements and be respectful if services or community events are taking place. Its presence, close to the city's larger civic and museum district, adds another layer to Singapore's religious map.
