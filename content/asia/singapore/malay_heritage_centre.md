The Malay Heritage Centre occupies the restored Istana Kampong Glam, the old palace of the Malay rulers who once held power here. The building and grounds are the strongest part of the visit, giving Kampong Glam a clear historical anchor behind the fabric shops, cafes, and mosque views.

Inside, the displays explain Malay culture and Singapore's early colonial bargains. The museum is modest, but it adds useful context before wandering the surrounding streets.
