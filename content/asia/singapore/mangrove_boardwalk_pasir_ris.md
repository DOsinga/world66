The mangrove boardwalk at Pasir Ris Park passes through one of Singapore's remaining accessible mangrove areas. The short walk is good for spotting mudskippers, fiddler crabs, butterflies, and wetland birds without leaving the city.

It works best as a low-key nature stop in the east, especially if you are already near Pasir Ris or Changi.
