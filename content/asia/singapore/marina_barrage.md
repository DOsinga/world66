Marina Barrage is both a dam and a public viewpoint at the edge of Marina Bay. Its visitor displays explain Singapore's water management and reservoir system, giving useful context to the city's heavy investment in sustainability.

The rooftop lawn is the main reason to come. It has open views back toward the Marina Bay skyline and is popular with kite flyers when the breeze is good.
