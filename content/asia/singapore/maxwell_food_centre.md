Maxwell Food Centre is one of the most famous hawker centres in Singapore, a covered hall on the edge of Chinatown packed with around a hundred stalls. It draws both office workers from the nearby financial district and food pilgrims, the latter mostly in search of one stall: Tian Tian Hainanese Chicken Rice, whose plates of poached chicken over fragrant rice have collected global attention and a perpetual queue.

Beyond the chicken rice, Maxwell rewards exploration — congee, char kway teow, popiah, and silky tau huay (soybean pudding) all have their devotees here. It is open through the day, busiest at lunch, and close enough to the Buddha Tooth Relic Temple and Sri Mariamman to combine into a single Chinatown morning. Come hungry and prepared to share a table.
