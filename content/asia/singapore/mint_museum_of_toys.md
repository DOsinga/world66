The Mint Museum of Toys holds a huge private collection of vintage toys from Singapore and far beyond, spread over several floors near Bugis. The displays cover more than a century of wind-up toys, character figures, posters, and pop-culture oddities. It is best for adults and older children who enjoy nostalgia; younger children may find the hands-off exhibits frustrating.
