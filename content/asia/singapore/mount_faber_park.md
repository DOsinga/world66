Mount Faber Park is one of Singapore's best urban viewpoints, looking over HarbourFront, Sentosa, and the southern islands. It is also the mainland end of the Singapore Cable Car, so it pairs naturally with a Sentosa visit or a walk along the Southern Ridges.
