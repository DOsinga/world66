National Gallery Singapore fills the former Supreme Court and City Hall buildings with one of the world's strongest public collections of Singaporean and Southeast Asian art. The buildings themselves are part of the visit, with restored colonial interiors joined by modern circulation spaces.

Allow proper time here if you care about art. The collection gives useful context for Singapore beyond shopping malls and skyline views, and the location is close to the Asian Civilisations Museum, the Padang, and the Civic District.
