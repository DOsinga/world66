The National Library of Singapore is a major public library complex near Bugis, with the Central Public Library in the basement and the Lee Kong Chian Reference Library on the upper floors. The building is also worth a look for its public gardens and city views.

Check the event listings if you are nearby, as exhibitions, talks, and small performances often use the complex. The garden levels are a useful quiet pause between Bugis, Bras Basah, and Kampong Glam.
