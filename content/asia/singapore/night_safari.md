Night Safari is a nocturnal wildlife park beside Singapore Zoo, with low-lit walking trails and a guided tram route through larger animal habitats. It was designed for nighttime visits, so the appeal is different from a daytime zoo: the Leopard Trail, Fishing Cat Trail, East Lodge Trail, and Wallaby Trail focus on animals that are more active after dark.

The tram is useful if time is short, while the walking trails are better for seeing smaller exhibits and quieter corners of the park. Flash photography is not allowed.
