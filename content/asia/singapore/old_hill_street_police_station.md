The Old Hill Street Police Station is one of Singapore's most eye-catching colonial-era buildings, with rows of brightly coloured shutters across its long neoclassical facade. It now houses government offices and cultural spaces rather than a police station.

You do not need long here, but it is an easy architectural stop near Clarke Quay, Fort Canning, and the civic district. The exterior is the main draw, especially when the light catches the coloured windows.
