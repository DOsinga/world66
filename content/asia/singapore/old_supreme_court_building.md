The Old Supreme Court Building is one of Singapore's grand colonial civic buildings, with Corinthian columns, a copper-green dome, and a carved allegory of justice above the entrance. Its neoclassical weight contrasts sharply with the modern Supreme Court building behind it.

Together with the old City Hall next door, it now forms part of the National Gallery Singapore. Even if you skip the galleries, the exterior is worth a slow look while walking through the civic district.
