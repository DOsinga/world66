The Pinnacle@Duxton Skybridge is a public viewing deck on the 50th storey of Singapore's tallest public housing project. It gives a wide view over Chinatown, Tanjong Pagar, the port, and the central skyline for a modest fee.

Access rules and payment methods can change, so check the current process before going. It is best as a clear-weather viewpoint rather than a long visit.
