The Rail Corridor follows a former railway line for about 24 kilometres through Singapore, now reused as a walking and cycling route. It cuts through a side of the island that feels less polished than the downtown waterfront: secondary forest, old cuttings, bridges, and neighbourhood edges.

You do not need to walk the whole route. Pick a section near Bukit Timah if you want a green, practical break from the malls and museums, and expect heat, humidity, and muddy patches after rain.
