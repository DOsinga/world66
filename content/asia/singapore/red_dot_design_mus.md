The Red Dot Design Museum displays award-winning product and communication design from around the world, drawn from the prestigious Red Dot Design Awards — one of the largest design competitions globally. Exhibits rotate regularly and cover everything from consumer electronics and furniture to typography and packaging.

The museum moved from its former home in the red-painted heritage building at Maxwell Road to a modern space at 11 Marina Boulevard in 2017, overlooking the Marina Bay waterfront promenade. The new location has more room for temporary exhibitions and a design gift shop worth browsing.

Open Monday to Friday 11am–7pm, Saturday and Sunday 10am–7pm. Admission is free.
