Reflections at Bukit Chandu is a small but well-presented museum about the Malay Regiment's last stand during the Battle of Bukit Chandu in World War II. The building also has exhibits on its earlier use connected with the opium trade, and the nearby Canopy Walk makes this an easy cultural stop to pair with a short Southern Ridges walk.
