Sasanaramsi Burmese Buddhist Temple is a Burmese-style Buddhist temple with a golden spire and richly decorated interiors. The main reason to visit is the temple's collection of Buddha images, including larger statues on the upper floors, which show a different architectural and devotional tradition from Singapore's Chinese and Thai temples.
