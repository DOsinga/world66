Sembawang Hot Spring Park protects Singapore's only natural hot spring on the mainland. It is a modest public bathing spot rather than a spa: visitors come to soak their feet, fill buckets with hot water, and cook eggs in the spring water.

The park is out in northern Singapore, so it fits best if you are already exploring Sembawang or want an odd local stop far from the usual central sights.
