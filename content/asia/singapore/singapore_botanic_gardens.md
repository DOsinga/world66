Singapore Botanic Gardens is the city's UNESCO-listed tropical garden, with broad lawns, rainforest remnants, lakes, and specialist plant collections. It is large enough for a long walk, a picnic, or a focused visit to the National Orchid Garden, and it remains one of Singapore's best green breaks from the dense city.
