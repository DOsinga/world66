Singapore Science Centre is a large hands-on science museum in Jurong, built around hundreds of interactive exhibits. The galleries cover technology, physics, biology, space, and everyday science in a format aimed strongly at families and school-age visitors.

It is a practical rainy-day choice and pairs well with other west-side attractions.
