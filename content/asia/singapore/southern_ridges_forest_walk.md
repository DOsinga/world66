The Southern Ridges Forest Walk is an elevated walkway through the treetops, forming one of the most memorable sections of Singapore's Southern Ridges route. The metal path rises up to about 18 metres and gives a different view of the forest canopy.

It works best as part of a longer walk linking the ridges, parks, and viewpoints west of the city centre.
