Sri Krishnan Temple is a compact Hindu temple dedicated to Krishna, standing just beside the busy Kwan Im Thong Hood Cho temple. That neighbourly setting is part of its charm: worshippers from different traditions pass through the same small stretch of Waterloo Street.

The temple is colourful, active, and easy to combine with Bugis, Bras Basah, or Little India. Visit respectfully, especially during prayer times, and look up at the sculpted figures around the entrance before moving on.
