The Sri Senpaga Vinayagar Temple is the spiritual anchor of Katong, a Hindu temple dedicated to Ganesha that traces its origins to the 1850s, when the deity was found beneath a senpaga (champak) tree by a pond. The current temple, extensively rebuilt and reconsecrated in 2003, is one of the most elaborately decorated in Singapore, with a gilded gopuram and stone carving done by craftsmen brought from India.

The interior is unusually richly finished, with granite pillars, a gold-plated flagpole, and a ceiling inlaid with the names of the deity in many languages. It remains a busy community temple for the area's Tamil community and a striking sight on Ceylon Road, a short walk from the Peranakan shophouses that Katong is better known for.
