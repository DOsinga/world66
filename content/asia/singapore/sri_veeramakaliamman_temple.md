Sri Veeramakaliamman Temple is one of Little India's oldest and busiest Hindu temples, with origins in the 1880s. The present structure dates from the 1980s, but the temple remains deeply woven into the neighbourhood's daily religious life.

It is especially active on Tuesdays, Fridays, and Sundays. Remove your shoes before entering and give worshippers space, as this is a working temple rather than just a monument.
