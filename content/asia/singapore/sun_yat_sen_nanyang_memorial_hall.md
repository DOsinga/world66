Sun Yat Sen Nanyang Memorial Hall occupies Wan Qing Yuan, a late-19th-century villa tied to Sun Yat Sen's revolutionary activity in Southeast Asia. Sun stayed here repeatedly while in exile, and the house became a meeting point for supporters of the 1911 revolution.

The museum is small but worthwhile if you are interested in Singapore's Chinese community and its links to wider Asian politics. It also gives Balestier a historical anchor beyond food streets and shophouses.
