Sungei Buloh Wetland Reserve protects mangroves, mudflats, ponds, and coastal forest in Singapore's far northwest. It is one of the best places in the city-state to remember that Singapore is still tropical, with monitor lizards on the paths and migratory birds feeding in the mud during the northern winter.

Bring binoculars if you have them and go early, before the heat flattens the day. The reserve is less convenient than the central gardens, but much wilder.
