Tan Kim Seng Fountain is a Victorian-style memorial to the merchant and philanthropist who helped fund Singapore's first public waterworks. Its carved figures and busy decoration make it one of the city's more graceful colonial-era fountains.

It is a quick stop rather than a destination by itself, but it fits neatly into a walk between the Singapore River, the Padang, and the museums around City Hall.
