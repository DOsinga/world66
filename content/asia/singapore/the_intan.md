The Intan is a private museum of Peranakan culture housed in a Joo Chiat shophouse — the home and lifetime collection of Alvin Yapp, who opens his house to visitors by appointment. "Intan" means rose-cut diamond in Malay, and the house is packed floor to ceiling with the beadwork, porcelain, furniture, and kebaya of the Straits-Chinese community.

Unlike the city's official museums, a visit here comes with tea, kueh, and the owner's own stories about the objects and the culture that produced them, which makes it the most personal introduction to Peranakan heritage in Singapore. It is small, intimate, and must be booked ahead. For anyone drawn to the painted shophouses of Joo Chiat and Katong, it explains the world that built them.
