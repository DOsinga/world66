Thian Hock Keng is one of Singapore's oldest Chinese temples, founded by the Hokkien community and dating back to the early 19th century. Its ornate roofline, carved brackets, imported tiles, and granite pillars make it one of Chinatown's best architectural stops.

The temple was built without nails, using interlocking timber brackets, and it still carries a plaque presented by China's Guangxu Emperor in 1907.
