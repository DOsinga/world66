Victoria Theatre and Concert Hall is a 19th-century civic arts venue by the Singapore River. Before the Esplanade opened it was Singapore's leading arts centre, and its history includes the 1887 Raffles statue outside and a plaque marking the founding of the People's Action Party in 1954.
