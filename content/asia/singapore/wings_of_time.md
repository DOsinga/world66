Wings of Time is Sentosa's evening outdoor show, using lasers, water effects, fire, music, and fireworks. The performance is short, polished, and aimed especially at families and first-time Sentosa visitors.

It is not a quiet cultural stop, but it can be a convenient way to end a beach or attraction day on the island.
