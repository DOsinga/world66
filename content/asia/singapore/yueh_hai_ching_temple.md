Yueh Hai Ching Temple, also known as Wak Hai Cheng Bio, is the oldest Teochew temple in Singapore. It is dedicated to Mazu, the Taoist goddess of the sea, a fitting patron for a community built around trade and migration.

Look up at the roof and walls: the temple is rich with Teochew carvings and colourful ceramic figures from opera scenes. It also holds an imperial plaque presented by China's Guangxu Emperor in 1899, a rare honour for a Singapore temple.
