Bongjeongsa is a Buddhist temple complex in a steep forested valley in the Sobaek Mountains about 40 km northwest of Andong. It is one of the oldest temple complexes in Korea, founded in 672 CE, and contains a structure — the Geungnakjeon main hall — that is widely considered the oldest surviving wooden building in the country.

The setting is remarkable: the temple is approached through a forested valley, the buildings climbing the hillside in terraces. Several other structures, including a magnificent nine-story stone pagoda and an ancient zelkova tree said to be over a thousand years old, are worth seeing. The temple is active and houses a community of monks. The mountains around it have good hiking trails. A bus from Andong runs to the vicinity; a taxi is easier.
