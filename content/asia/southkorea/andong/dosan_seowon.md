Dosan Seowon is a Confucian academy built in 1574, four years after the death of Yi Hwang (Toegye), the Korean scholar who did more than anyone to adapt Chinese Neo-Confucianism into a distinctive Korean philosophical tradition. The site was a UNESCO World Heritage Site designation in 2019. The academy occupies a hillside above the Nakdong River, now dammed to form a reservoir — the view from the main gate over the water is quietly spectacular.

The complex is modest in scale but carefully arranged: Yi Hwang's personal study, Dosan Seodang, is the oldest structure and the most intimate. The larger lecture halls, dormitories, and shrine buildings were added later. The library holds original printings of Yi Hwang's texts. An outdoor exhibition area explains the scholar's life and philosophy in a way that even visitors unfamiliar with Korean Confucianism will find interesting. The complex is about 25 km north of Andong city centre; buses run from the terminal.
