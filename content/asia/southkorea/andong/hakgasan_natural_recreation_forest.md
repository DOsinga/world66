Hakgasan Natural Recreation Forest is a quiet mountain forest area north of Andong, close to Bongjeongsa Temple. The draw is simple: pine woods, clean air, and walking paths through a less built-up patch of countryside.

It works best as a calm nature stop paired with nearby temple visits rather than as a full-day destination on its own.
