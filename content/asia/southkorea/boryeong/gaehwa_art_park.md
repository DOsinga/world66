Gaehwa Art Park is a large art-and-nature complex near Boryeong, with outdoor sculpture, gardens, and exhibition spaces spread across a former quarry area. It works best as a slow stop rather than a quick photo call, especially if you are already visiting nearby inland sights such as the coal museum or Seongjusa Temple Site.
