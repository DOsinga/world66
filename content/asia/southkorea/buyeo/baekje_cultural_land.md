Baekje Cultural Land is a historical theme park outside Buyeo, built to recreate parts of the old Baekje capital. The complex includes a reconstructed Sabi Palace, temple buildings, tombs, a fortress, and a village area, giving a more visual sense of court life than the archaeological sites in town.
