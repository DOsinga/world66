Baekje Royal Tombs Park preserves a group of royal burial mounds from Buyeo's years as the Baekje capital. The grassy tombs are quiet and understated, but they help make sense of the kingdom whose palaces, temples, and fortresses are scattered across the town.

The small Baekje Gobun Museum explains the tombs and burial customs, which makes the site much easier to read. Visit it together with Jeongnimsa Temple Site and Buyeo National Museum for the clearest picture of Baekje culture.
