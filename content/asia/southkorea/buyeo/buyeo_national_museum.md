Buyeo National Museum is the place where the Baekje capital becomes tangible. Its collection focuses on the Sabi period, with ceramics, Buddhist sculpture, roof tiles, tomb finds, and everyday objects from Buyeo and the surrounding area.

The star is the Gilt-bronze Incense Burner of Baekje, found in 1993 near the royal tombs. Its mountain-shaped lid, tiny figures, animals, and phoenix finial make it one of the great surviving objects of early Korean art.
