Gungnamji Pond is a graceful landscaped pond south of central Buyeo, associated with Baekje royal garden traditions. A pavilion sits on an island reached by a wooden bridge, with lotus beds spreading across the water in summer.

It is not as historically dense as the fortress or museum, but it gives Buyeo a softer rhythm. Come near sunset, when locals walk the paths and the water catches the light.
