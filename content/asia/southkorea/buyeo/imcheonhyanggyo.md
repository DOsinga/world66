Imcheonhyanggyo is a historic Confucian school in Imcheon-myeon, southwest of central Buyeo. The present complex was rebuilt in the late 17th century and is protected as a Chungcheongnam-do monument, making it a worthwhile detour for visitors interested in Korea's local Confucian academies.
