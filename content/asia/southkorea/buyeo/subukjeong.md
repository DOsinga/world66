Subukjeong is a Joseon-era pavilion on the edge of Buyeo, built during the reign of King Gwanghaegun by Kim Heung-guk. The hipped-and-gabled structure is a small stop, but it is protected as a South Chungcheong cultural heritage site and gives a quieter look at traditional architecture away from Buyeo's main Baekje-period sights.
