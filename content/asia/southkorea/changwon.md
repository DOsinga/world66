*Asia > South Korea > Changwon*

Changwon is easy to underestimate because the name now covers several different places: planned Changwon, old port-city Masan, and naval Jinhae. Together they make a worthwhile day or overnight stop between Busan, Daegu, and the south coast.

In spring, Jinhae is the star. [Yeojwacheon Stream](/asia/southkorea/changwon/yeojwacheon_stream) and [Gyeonghwa Station](/asia/southkorea/changwon/gyeonghwa_station_cherry_blossom_street) are among Korea's best-known cherry blossom scenes, and they are busy for good reason. Outside blossom season, the city still has [Junam Reservoir](/asia/southkorea/changwon/junam_reservoir), one of the country's better bird-watching wetlands.

Masan gives Changwon its food-and-port character. [Masan Fish Market](/asia/southkorea/changwon/masan_fish_market) is loud, practical, and more memorable than the newer administrative city centre. Come with a clear plan, because Changwon is spread out and its best pieces are not all near each other.
