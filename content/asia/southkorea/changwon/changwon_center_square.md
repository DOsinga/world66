Changwon Center Square is a broad downtown green space built around a small lake, with lawns, sculptures, and a music fountain that draws people in the evening. Festivals, concerts, and lantern events sometimes use the open space.

It is more a city breathing room than a major sight, but it is a pleasant stop if you are already in central Changwon.
