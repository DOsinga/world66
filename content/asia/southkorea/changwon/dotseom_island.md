Dotseom Island is a small leisure island in Masan Bay, reached by ferry from Changwon's old port side. It has family attractions, sea views, and a slower pace than the city streets around Masan.

Come for a light outing rather than wilderness: the appeal is the ferry ride, the bay setting, and the island's easy-going amusements. It works well with a visit to Masan Fish Market.
