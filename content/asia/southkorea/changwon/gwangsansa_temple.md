Gwangsansa Temple is a Buddhist temple traditionally linked to Wonhyo, the Silla-era monk, and Eunshin, a Chinese monk. It is a quiet religious stop outside Changwon's busier central districts.

Visit if you are already exploring the hills and temple sites around the city, or if you want a calmer contrast to Changwon's markets and waterfront areas.
