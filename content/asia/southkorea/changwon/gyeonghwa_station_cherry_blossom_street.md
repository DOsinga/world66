Gyeonghwa Station is no longer an ordinary railway stop, but the old track has become one of Jinhae's signature cherry blossom walks. In late March or early April, the rails, train displays, and rows of trees create the classic spring photograph.

It is at its best when the blossoms are out. Outside that short season, treat it as a quick curiosity rather than a main reason to cross town.
