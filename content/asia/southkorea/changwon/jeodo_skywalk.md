Jeodo Skywalk is a 170-metre pedestrian bridge with glass sections over the sea. The height is modest, but the clear floor and open water views make it a popular coastal stop.

The bridge is lit at night and is usually closed in bad weather. It is best treated as a short scenic detour rather than a full-day destination.
