Kyungwha Market is a local folk market held on dates ending in 3 or 8 each month. It is close to Gyeonghwa Station, one of Changwon's best-known cherry blossom spots, so it works well as part of a spring visit to the area.

Expect a practical local market rather than a polished attraction, with food, household goods, and seasonal bustle.
