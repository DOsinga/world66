Marine Drama Filming Site is an open-air set on Changwon's coast, built with wooden streets, period buildings, and replica ships for Korean television dramas and films. The setting borrows from the Gaya era, so the visit is part film-location curiosity and part loose historical recreation.

The coastal backdrop is half the appeal. Come in daylight, when you can wander the sets and look out across the Masan shoreline.
