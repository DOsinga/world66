Masan Fish Market is the best place to feel the older port-city side of Changwon. It has sold seafood for generations and still feels more like a working market than a neat tourist display.

Come for the noise, tanks, fish smells, dried goods, and nearby food alleys. It pairs well with a wander through old Masan, especially if the planned-city parts of Changwon feel too orderly.
