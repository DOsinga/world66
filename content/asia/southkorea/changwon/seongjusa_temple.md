Seongjusa Temple is a temple complex in a green setting outside central Changwon. The approach is by paved road, so the visit does not require a hike, though nearby trails make it easy to turn the stop into a longer outing.

Come for the temple buildings, mountain backdrop, and quieter atmosphere compared with the city's urban sights. It is best for visitors with extra time or their own transport.
