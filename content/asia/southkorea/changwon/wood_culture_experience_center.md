The Wood Culture Experience Center is a hands-on museum and activity centre about forests, timber, and traditional wood use in Korea. Exhibits cover trees, wooden houses, and forest culture, while outdoor trails and craft activities make it a practical stop for families who want something quieter than the city centre.
