Yeojwacheon Stream is Changwon's most famous spring scene. Cherry trees arch over the water in Jinhae, with footbridges and walkways turning the stream into one long blossom corridor during the festival period.

It is crowded, sentimental, and heavily photographed. That does not make it weak. Time it for early morning or evening if you can, and accept that the atmosphere is part of the visit.
