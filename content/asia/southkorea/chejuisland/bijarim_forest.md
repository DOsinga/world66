Bijarim Forest is a protected grove of old nutmeg trees in eastern Jeju. Flat gravel paths lead through the trees, making it an easy nature stop even for visitors who are not planning a serious hike.

The forest is quiet, shaded and especially good in warm or wet weather. Its oldest trees are several centuries old, including a large Japanese torreya near the centre of the reserve.
