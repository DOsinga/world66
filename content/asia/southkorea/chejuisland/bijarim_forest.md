Bijarim Forest is a quiet old-growth grove of nutmeg yews on eastern Jeju. Flat gravel paths run beneath dense, glossy trees, and the centrepiece is a Japanese torreya said to be more than 800 years old.

The forest is easy to visit even if you are not a serious hiker: the short route takes about half an hour, while the longer loop gives you more time in the shade. It pairs well with Manjanggul Lava Tube or Seongsan Ilchulbong on an east-coast day.
