Bultapsa Five-Story Stone Pagoda is a reconstructed pagoda at Wondang Temple, a site traditionally linked to the Goryeo king Chungnyeol. The original pagoda was damaged during the Korean War, and the present structure dates from the 1950s.

The pagoda is a small sight rather than a headline attraction, but its twisting corner lines give it a lively shape. It works best as part of a broader Jeju temple or north-coast itinerary.
