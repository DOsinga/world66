Eorimok is one of the gentler Hallasan trailheads, following a forested valley before climbing toward Witse Oreum. The walk is about 4.7 km from the trail start and has a steady grade rather than the harsher feel of some mountain routes.

The trailhead has a rest house and a basic shop. In winter the packed snow can turn parts of the lower route into an informal sledding run for local children, which gives the place a cheerful, very Jeju feel.
