Hallim Park is a large garden and family attraction beside Hyeopjae Beach on Jeju's west coast. It is divided into themed areas, including palm avenues, tropical planting, bonsai, and folk-village displays, so it is more varied than a simple botanical garden.

The park is easy to combine with the beach, especially if the weather is too windy for swimming. It is a gentle stop rather than a wild nature experience, but useful for families and anyone with an interest in plants.
