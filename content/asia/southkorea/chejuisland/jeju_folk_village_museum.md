Jeju Folk Village Museum reconstructs traditional island houses and village scenes across a large outdoor site on Jeju's southeast coast. The displays focus on local architecture, household life, crafts, and customs, making it a practical introduction to Jeju culture.

It takes time to see properly, so it works best as a deliberate half-day stop rather than a quick roadside photo break.
