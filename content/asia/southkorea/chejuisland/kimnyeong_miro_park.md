Kimnyeong Miro Park is a hedge maze near Manjanggul Lava Tube. Opened in 1987, it was Korea's first maze park and uses its layout to nod to Jeju's geography and history.

It is an easy family stop on the island's northeast side, with shaded paths of Leyland cypress and Jeju pine. The resident cats have become part of the park's character.
