Nexon Computer Museum is a good rainy-day stop on Jeju, especially if you grew up with old consoles, early home computers, or arcade machines. The collection covers the history of personal computing and video games, with many working machines rather than glass-case nostalgia.

It is more niche than Jeju's volcanic sights, but that is part of the appeal. Families and anyone interested in game culture can easily spend an hour or two here before heading back to the coast or into Jeju City.
