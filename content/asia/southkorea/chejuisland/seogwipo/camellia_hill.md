Camellia Hill is a large arboretum west of central Seogwipo, planted with thousands of camellia trees and hundreds of varieties. The gardens are laid out for strolling, with seasonal flowers, greenhouse displays, and photo stops spread across the grounds.

Winter and early spring are the main camellia seasons, but the garden has enough planting to remain a pleasant stop through much of the year.
