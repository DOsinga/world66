Gaetkak Jusangjeolli Cliff is a rugged coastal spot near Jungmun, where columnar volcanic rock meets the sea. The access is rough in places, but the payoff is a close look at Jeju's basalt shoreline and open views across the water.

Wear shoes with grip and avoid the rocks in bad weather or heavy surf.
