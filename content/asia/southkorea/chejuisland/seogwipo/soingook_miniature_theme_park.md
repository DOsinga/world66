Soingook Miniature Theme Park lays out small-scale replicas of famous buildings from Korea and around the world, from the Taj Mahal to the Leaning Tower of Pisa. It is light, family-friendly sightseeing rather than a serious cultural stop, but it can be a useful break from beaches and volcanic landscapes.
