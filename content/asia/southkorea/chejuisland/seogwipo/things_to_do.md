---
title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Seogwipo's best sights line up along the south coast. Walk between Jeongbang Waterfall, Lee Jung-seop Street, Cheonjiyeon Waterfall, Saeyeongyo Bridge, and Oedolgae if the weather is kind; use buses or taxis for the longer jump west to Jungmun and Jusangjeolli. The city is one of Jeju's easiest bases for mixing coastal walks with food-market evenings.
