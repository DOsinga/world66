The World Eros Museum is an adults-only museum devoted to erotic art and objects from around the world. It is an offbeat stop rather than a major cultural institution, but it has enough range to interest visitors looking for Jeju's stranger private museums.

Do not plan it as a family stop. For adults, it can be a curious counterpoint to the waterfalls, coast walks, and resort sights around Seogwipo.
