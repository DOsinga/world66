Seongeup Folklore Village is a traditional Jeju village where old stone houses, walls, and local customs are still visible in a lived-in setting. The "folklore" label can undersell it: this is not just a staged display, though some parts are clearly set up for visitors.

A guide helps make sense of the architecture and island culture, but it is still worth a stop for a walk through one of Jeju's best-known historic villages.
