Seopjikoji is a coastal cape on eastern Jeju, close to Seongsan Ilchulbong, where a walking path follows open grass, black volcanic rock, and sea views. It is a popular photo stop, partly because it has appeared in Korean television dramas, but the real draw is the easy cliffside walk.

Come in clear weather if you can, when the ocean views and nearby volcanic cones give the place its shape.
