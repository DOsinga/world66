Suwolbong Peak is a low coastal peak on western Jeju with wide views over the sea and Chagwido Island. Below the pavilion at the summit, a geological trail follows cliffs of layered volcanic deposits and mineral springs.

The peak is especially good near sunset, when the light falls across the western coast and the offshore islands.
