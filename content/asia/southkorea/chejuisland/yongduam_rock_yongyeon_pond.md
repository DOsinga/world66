Yongduam, or Dragon Head Rock, is a volcanic outcrop on Jeju's north coast, said in local legend to be the petrified remains of a water dragon. The shoreline is especially atmospheric after dark, when the rock is lit and the sea breaks around it.

About 100 m east, Yongyeon Pond adds a quieter stop: a small canyon with turquoise water and a pavilion above it. Together they make an easy coastal walk close to Jeju City.
