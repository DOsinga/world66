ARARIO Small City is the surprise in central Cheonan: a cluster of contemporary art, public sculpture, gallery space, shops, and terminal-side urban life where you might expect only buses and department stores. It is not a reason to cross Korea by itself, but it is the best stop in the middle of town.

The gallery has shown internationally known artists, while the plaza works even if you do not go inside. Use it as a reset between bus connections or as a compact art stop before heading out to Cheonan's larger sights.
