Jung-ang Market is one of Cheonan's central food markets, with small shops and stalls selling everyday snacks. Hotteok is a local favourite here, and the mood changes after some daytime shops close and food trucks take over in the evening.

Come for a simple market wander and a snack rather than a polished sightseeing stop. It is most useful if you are already in central Cheonan and want something more local than a station-area cafe.
