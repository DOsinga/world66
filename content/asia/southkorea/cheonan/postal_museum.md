Cheonan's Postal Museum is a small, family-friendly museum about Korea's postal service. It is easy to reach by bus and has enough hands-on, everyday material to work well with younger children.

This is not one of Cheonan's major sights, but it is a useful rainy-day or family stop, especially if you are already in the area and want something lighter than the city's larger history museums.
