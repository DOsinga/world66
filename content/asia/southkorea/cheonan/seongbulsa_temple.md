Seongbulsa Temple is a hillside Buddhist temple outside central Cheonan. Tradition links its origins to the early Goryeo period, though the buildings have been repaired and rebuilt over time.

The temple is a quiet cultural stop, best combined with other sights on the slopes around Taejosan rather than treated as a stand-alone half-day trip.
