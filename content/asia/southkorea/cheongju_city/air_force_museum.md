The Air Force Museum near Cheongju focuses on the history of South Korea's air force and its academy. The displays are most appealing if you are interested in military aviation, modern Korean history, or specialist museums outside the usual city-centre circuit.

It sits away from central Cheongju, so treat it as a planned side stop rather than something to add casually between downtown sights.
