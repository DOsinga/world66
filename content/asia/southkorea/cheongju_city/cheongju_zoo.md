Cheongju Zoo sits beside Cheongju Land and is the city's main animal-focused family attraction. It is known locally for sheltering and rehabilitating injured or sick animals before they can return to more natural conditions.

It is most useful for families or for travelers already heading toward Cheongju Land and the eastern side of the city.
