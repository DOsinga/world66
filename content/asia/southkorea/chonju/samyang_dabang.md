Samyang Dabang is a historic dabang — a traditional Korean coffeehouse that predates the global coffee shop chains by decades. These old-style cafes were once the gathering places of Korean cultural life: dimly lit, furnished with wooden chairs and velvet curtains, serving instant coffee mixed at the counter rather than espresso from a machine.

Samyang Dabang retains much of its original character. The interior is deliberately unchanged: dark wood panelling, retro furniture, and a menu that still features traditional dabang coffee alongside newer offerings. It has become a minor pilgrimage site for those interested in Korean cafe history, and is photographed almost as often as it is drunk in.

It is genuinely worth a visit — not for the coffee itself, which is sweet and mild by contemporary standards, but for the sense of what Korean urban social life looked like before globalisation transformed it.
