Jeonju's greatest concentration of sights is in and around [Hanok Village](#jeonju_hanok_village), the old quarter where hundreds of traditional tile-roofed houses remain in use. Beyond the village, [Jeondong Cathedral](#jeondong_cathedral) and the [Jeonju National Museum](#jeonju_national_museum) are the main city-centre draws, with [Jeonju International Film Festival](#jeonju_international_film_festival) bringing Korea's independent cinema scene here every May.
