General Shin Sung-gyeom Tomb honors the Goryeo-era general who helped King Taejo reunify the Later Three Kingdoms. The site includes the tomb, a tombstone, and monuments marking his service.

It is a quiet historical stop outside central Chuncheon, best suited to visitors already exploring the city's cultural sites or Korean dynastic history.
