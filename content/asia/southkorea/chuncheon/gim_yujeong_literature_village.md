Gim Yujeong Literature Village honours the short-story writer born in Chuncheon in 1908. His work is rooted in rural Korean life, with humour that often has poverty and sadness just under it.

The site includes the restored birth home, exhibition spaces and village details that help place the writing in its landscape. It is a more thoughtful stop than the word "village" might suggest.
