Soyangjeong is a historic pavilion at the foot of Bongui Mountain, above the Soyang River. Its origins are traditionally traced to the Three Kingdoms period, though the pavilion was renamed during the Joseon era.

The stop is small, but it gives a quieter historical counterpoint to the nearby riverside sights and Soyanggang Skywalk.
