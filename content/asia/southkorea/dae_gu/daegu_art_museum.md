Daegu Art Museum is the city's main contemporary art museum, with large exhibition halls and a programme that mixes Korean artists, international shows, and projects tied to the region's art scene. It sits east of the city centre near Suseong, so it is best treated as a planned museum stop rather than a quick downtown detour.
