Daegu Textile Museum covers the history of Korean textiles, clothing, and fashion, with exhibits that connect the city's textile industry to wider changes in design and manufacturing. It is a focused museum rather than a broad city-history stop, but the subject fits Daegu especially well.

Go if you are interested in fabric, fashion, industrial history, or contemporary Korean design.
