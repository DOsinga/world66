Kim Gwangseok-gil Street is a 350-metre mural street near Bangcheon Market dedicated to the Daegu-born singer-songwriter Kim Gwang-seok. Statues, murals, and small music-themed details make it an easy cultural stop while walking between central Daegu sights.
