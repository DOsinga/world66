Suchang Youth Mansion turns a former 1970s residential compound for tobacco-company workers into a small cultural complex. The buildings now hold exhibition rooms, a book cafe, and flexible spaces used for art and community events.

It is a good short stop if you are already exploring central Daegu and want to see how the city reuses its older industrial-era buildings.
