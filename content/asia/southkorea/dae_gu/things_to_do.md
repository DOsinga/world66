---
title: Things to Do
type: section
---

Daegu's main natural draw is [Mount Palgong](/asia/southkorea/dae_gu/mount_palgong) on the northern outskirts, with Buddhist temples on its forested slopes and a cable car to the summit ridge. [Donghwasa Temple](/asia/southkorea/dae_gu/donghwasa_temple), the most famous of Palgong's temples, has a colossal outdoor Buddha visible from afar. In the city, [Seomun Market](/asia/southkorea/dae_gu/seomun_market) is one of Korea's oldest markets, [Dongseongno](/asia/southkorea/dae_gu/dongseongno) is the main pedestrian shopping and café street, and [Gyeongsang-gamyeong Park](/asia/southkorea/dae_gu/gyeongsang_gamyeong_park) preserves a Joseon-era administrative compound in the city centre. [Suseong Lake](/asia/southkorea/dae_gu/suseong_lake) is the favoured evening walk for local families.
