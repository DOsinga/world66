Daedong Sky Park sits above Daedong Mural Village, where painted lanes climb toward a small hilltop park and windmill. The appeal is simple: street art on the way up, then a broad view back over Daejeon.

It is best treated as a short wander rather than a formal sight. Go in clear weather or near sunset, when the village and skyline give the walk more atmosphere.
