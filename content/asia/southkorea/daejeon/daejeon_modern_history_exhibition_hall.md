Daejeon Modern History Exhibition Hall uses a handsome colonial-era building to tell the city's 20th-century story. Exhibits cover Daejeon's growth, railway history, urban planning, architecture, and everyday life, with temporary shows adding broader cultural themes.

It is a useful stop if you want to understand how Daejeon became a modern transport and science city, rather than just passing through it.
