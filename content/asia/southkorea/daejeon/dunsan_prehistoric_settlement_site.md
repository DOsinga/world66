Dunsan Prehistoric Settlement Site preserves and interprets finds uncovered during the construction of Dunsan new town in the early 1990s. Reconstructed huts and displays explain the stone-age tools, farming evidence, and domestic remains found here, turning what looks at first like a grassy park into a compact archaeological stop.
