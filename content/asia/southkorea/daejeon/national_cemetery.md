Daejeon National Cemetery is Korea's main cemetery for war heroes, patriots, and national figures. It is a solemn place rather than a casual park, with formal memorial spaces set in landscaped grounds on the edge of the city.

The surrounding walking trail and quiet setting make it possible to visit at an unhurried pace. Dress and behave respectfully; this is still an active national memorial, not just a historic site.
