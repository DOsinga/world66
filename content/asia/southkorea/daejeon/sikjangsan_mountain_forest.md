Sikjangsan Mountain Forest is one of Daejeon's better outdoor escapes, with walking trails on the slopes southeast of the city and several temple stops along the way. It is open year-round, though access is easiest if you already have local transport sorted.

The appeal is a half-day of forest walking rather than a single monument. Go in clear weather for the mountain atmosphere and views back toward the city.
