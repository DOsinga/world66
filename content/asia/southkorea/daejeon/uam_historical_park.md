Uam Historical Park preserves a quiet scholarly site in a wooded valley on the edge of Daejeon. The grounds include traditional buildings, a pond, and walking paths, with a mountain trail continuing from the rear of the park.

It suits a slower half-day for visitors interested in Confucian scholarship, Korean architecture, and a calm walk away from the city centre.
