Damyang Noodles Street is a row of simple noodle restaurants near the stream, close enough to Juknokwon and Gwanbangjerim Forest to make it an easy lunch stop. The point is not refinement; it is sitting outside, eating something cheap and filling, and letting the town's slow green mood continue at the table.

If you want the more famous local meal, look for tteokgalbi elsewhere in town. If you want a quick break between walks, the noodle street does the job well.
