The Korea Gasa Literature Collection is a small museum devoted to *gasa*, a classical Korean verse form closely tied to the scholar culture of Damyang and the surrounding valleys. Exhibits introduce the writers, manuscripts, and local landscape that shaped this literary tradition.

It is a specialist stop, but a useful one if you are already visiting nearby Soswaewon Garden or exploring Damyang's pavilions. Together, they make the area's old literati culture feel less abstract.
