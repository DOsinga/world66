The Metasequoia-lined Road is the other classic Damyang walk, a long straight avenue where trees planted in the 1970s have grown into a high, photogenic tunnel. It is simple, almost absurdly so, but the scale works: the trunks, the filtered light, and the vanishing point make it feel ceremonial.

Autumn is the prettiest season, but it is worth a look whenever the weather is kind. Pair it with Juknokwon only if you still have an appetite for trees; the experiences are related, but not the same.
