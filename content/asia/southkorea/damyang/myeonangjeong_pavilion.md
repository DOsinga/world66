Myeonangjeong Pavilion is associated with Song Soon, the 16th-century scholar and poet who retired here after leaving government service. The pavilion became a place for teaching and literary discussion, linking it to the wider Confucian and poetic culture of the Joseon period.

The site is a quiet historical stop rather than a large attraction. It pairs best with Damyang's gardens, forest walks, and other heritage sites.
