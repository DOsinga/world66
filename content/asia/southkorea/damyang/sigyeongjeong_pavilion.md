Sigyeongjeong Pavilion is tied to Damyang's classical literary landscape. Built in the Joseon period, it is associated with scholars and poets including Jeong Cheol, whose work helped shape Korean lyric poetry.

The pavilion is best visited with nearby gardens and bamboo sights, as part of a slower look at Damyang's scholar culture and landscape design.
