Bobaljae Viewpoint looks out over the mountains and valleys around Danyang. It is a simple scenic stop, useful for photography and for getting a sense of the layered terrain that makes the area popular for caves, skywalks, and short nature trips.

The viewpoint is best treated as part of a broader Danyang loop rather than a destination on its own. Clear weather matters; haze or low cloud can make the stop much less rewarding.
