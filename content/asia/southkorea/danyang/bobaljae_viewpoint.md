Bobaljae Viewpoint is a simple stop above Danyang for broad views of the surrounding mountains and valleys. It is the kind of place that works best on a clear day, when the ridgelines stack into the distance and the town's dramatic setting becomes obvious.

Treat it as a scenic pause rather than a major excursion. Photographers will get the most out of it in soft morning or late-afternoon light.
