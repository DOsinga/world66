Danuri Aquarium is a freshwater aquarium that gives Danyang's river-and-gorge landscape an indoor counterpart. Displays include local and overseas freshwater species, with recreations of the area's best-known scenic spots, so it works well as a family stop or a wet-weather break between cave and river sights.
