Mancheonha Skywalk is a modern viewpoint on the slopes above the river, with a glass-floored platform that looks down over Danyang and the Namhan valley. It is touristy, but the view is the point, and on clear days it gives you the best quick read of the town's setting.

The complex also has extra-fee activities such as zipline rides, so check what is operating before you go.
