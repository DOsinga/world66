Ondal Tourist Site mixes legend, scenery, and a little theme-park energy. It is tied to the story of Ondal, the low-born court figure who married Princess Pyeonggang and became a general, and includes reconstructed historical buildings, a fortress area, and Ondal Cave.

It is less subtle than Danyang's natural sights, but it helps explain why Ondal imagery appears all over town.
