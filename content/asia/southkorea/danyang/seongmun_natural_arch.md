Seongmun Natural Arch is a stone span above the Namhan River, close to the famous Dodamsambong rocks. It is one of the Danyang Palgyeong, the county's traditional Eight Scenic Views, and a good short stop if you are already following the river sights north of town.

The appeal is simple: rock, water, and a wide view of the valley. Go in clear weather.
