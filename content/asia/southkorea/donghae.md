Donghae is a working port city on South Korea's east coast, where the Taebaek Mountains press close to the sea. It is not polished like a resort town, and that helps: fishing harbours, rail lines, beaches, caves and mountain valleys all sit within the same compact city.

The coastal sights are the easiest hook. [Chuam Chotdaebawi Rock](/asia/southkorea/donghae/chuam_chotdaebawi_rock) is one of Gangwon's classic sunrise views, while [Mukho Lighthouse](/asia/southkorea/donghae/mukho_lighthouse) and the mural lanes below it look over the harbour. Inland, [Mureung Valley](/asia/southkorea/donghae/mureung_valley) gives Donghae a stronger mountain identity than its port frontage suggests.

Come with a loose plan rather than a checklist. Donghae is best when you split the day between sea and valley: sunrise at Chuam, a harbour walk around Mukho, then buses or a taxi into Mureung if the weather is clear.
