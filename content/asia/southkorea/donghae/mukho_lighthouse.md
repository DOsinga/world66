Mukho Lighthouse stands above Mukho Port, high enough to turn a working harbour into a proper coastal viewpoint. The white tower dates from the 1960s and sits in a small park with broad views over the sea and port.

Walk down through Nongoldam Road afterward, where murals and steep lanes lead toward the harbour. The combination is more interesting than the lighthouse alone.
