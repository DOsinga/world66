Coffee Cupper Coffee Museum reflects Gangneung's unusually strong coffee culture. The collection includes coffee roasters, grinders, espresso machines, and other brewing equipment from around the world.

The museum is paired with a coffee farm, so the visit is more hands-on than a simple display of old machines. It makes sense as part of a wider Gangneung coffee outing, especially if you are already exploring beyond the beach strip.
