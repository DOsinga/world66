Daegwallyeong Museum displays a private collection of roughly 2,000 folk and historical items donated to Gangneung. The exhibits range from prehistoric material to more recent everyday objects, arranged by theme rather than as a single chronological story.

It is a modest regional museum, best paired with other Gangneung heritage stops if you are interested in local culture beyond the coast and coffee streets.
