Gangneung Daedohobu Government Office preserves halls from the city's Joseon-era administrative compound. Jeondaecheong Hall held royal tablets used in ritual ceremonies, while Imyeonggwan served as a guest reception hall for officials.

The site gives Gangneung a strong historic stop in the city centre, especially for travellers interested in Korea's regional government architecture.
