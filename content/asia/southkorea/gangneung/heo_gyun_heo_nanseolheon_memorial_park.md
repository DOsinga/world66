This memorial park preserves the former home associated with Heo Nanseolheon, a Joseon-era poet, and Heo Gyun, the writer linked to *The Story of Hong Gildong*. The grounds include a traditional house and a memorial hall, and work well with nearby Gyeongpo-area sights such as Seongyojang, Ojukheon, and Gyeongpodae.
