Jeongdongjin Time Museum is a small themed museum built around a replica steam locomotive and a long train beside the coast. The exhibits play with the idea of time, from early timekeeping to Einstein and modern art.

It is a light, family-friendly stop if you are already in Jeongdongjin for the beach or sunrise views.
