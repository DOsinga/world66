Jeongdongsimgok Badabuchaegil Sea Road is a coastal walking route south of Gangneung, known for exposed marine terraces formed by ancient crustal movement. The cliffs and rock shelves make it more than a simple seaside path, and the route is protected as a Korean natural monument.
