Jumunjin Fish Market is one of the main seafood markets on Korea's east coast. Founded in 1936, it still serves the squid boats and fishing fleet based around Jumunjin.

Come for dried fish, live seafood, and the busy harbour atmosphere rather than polished sightseeing. It is a practical food stop and a good look at Gangneung's fishing side.
