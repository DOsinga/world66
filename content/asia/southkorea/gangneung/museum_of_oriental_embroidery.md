The Museum of Oriental Embroidery is a specialist collection devoted to traditional embroidery from Korea, China, and Japan. It is close to Ojukheon, so it makes sense as part of the same cultural circuit rather than as a standalone trip across town.

The appeal is quiet and detailed: textiles, patterns, and craft rather than big-ticket spectacle.
