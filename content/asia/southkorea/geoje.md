Geoje is South Korea's big island south-west of [Busan](/asia/southkorea/pusan), joined to the mainland by bridges but still shaped by water. Shipyards and apartment blocks sit near quiet fishing coves, pebble beaches, pine-covered headlands, and small offshore islands.

The island's best day is spent on the south-east coast. Boat trips link [Haegeumgang](/asia/southkorea/geoje/haegeumgang) and [Oedo Botania](/asia/southkorea/geoje/oedo_botania), where cliffs, sea caves, and a privately made botanical garden give Geoje its postcard side. Nearby [Windy Hill](/asia/southkorea/geoje/windy_hill) is less wild but gives an easy open view over the same broken coastline.

Geoje also has a sharper historical edge. The [Historic Park of Geoje POW Camp](/asia/southkorea/geoje/historic_park_of_geoje_pow_camp) explains the huge Korean War prisoner camp that once stood here, including the 1952 unrest that made the island known far beyond Korea. Add [Hakdong Mongdol Beach](/asia/southkorea/geoje/hakdong_mongdol_beach) if you want a slower hour by the water.

Geoje works well as a full day from Busan or Tongyeong, but it rewards more time if you have a car. Public buses reach the island, then thin out along the coast.
