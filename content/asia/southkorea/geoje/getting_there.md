---
title: Getting There
type: section
---

Geoje is easiest by road from [Busan](/asia/southkorea/pusan), using the fixed link across Gadeokdo and the Busan-Geoje bridge system. Intercity buses run to Gohyeon and other towns on the island.

From [Tongyeong](/asia/southkorea/tongyeong), buses and roads approach Geoje from the west. Once on the island, a car or taxi saves time because the main sights are spread around the coast.
