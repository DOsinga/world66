Haegeumgang is a cluster of steep rock islets off Geoje's south-east tip, visited by sightseeing boats when the sea allows. The appeal is simple: dark cliffs, narrow channels, and wave-cut caves seen from the water.

It is usually paired with Oedo Botania on the same cruise. Sit outside if you can; the cliffs are the point, not the commentary.
