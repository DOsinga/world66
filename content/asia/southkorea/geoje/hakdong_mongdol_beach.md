Hakdong Mongdol Beach is a dark pebble beach rather than a soft-sand resort strip. The rounded stones make a distinctive sound under the waves, and the bay gives a calmer break between Geoje's cliff and island sights.

Come for a walk or a swim in warm weather, not for seclusion. It is one of Geoje's better-known beaches and can be busy in summer.
