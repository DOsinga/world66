The Historic Park of Geoje POW Camp covers the Korean War camp where tens of thousands of captured North Korean and Chinese soldiers were held. It is a memorial and museum rather than a preserved camp, with exhibits, reconstructions, and military hardware.

The site matters because Geoje was not just a quiet island during the war. In 1952, camp unrest and hostage-taking drew international attention and exposed how bitter the conflict remained even behind the wire.
