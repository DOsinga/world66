Oedo Botania is the polished half of the standard Geoje boat trip: a small offshore island turned into a terraced botanical garden, with palms, camellias, sculpture, and blue-water viewpoints. It can feel staged, but the setting is real enough to carry it.

Most visitors reach it by cruise boat combined with Haegeumgang. Check sailing times before setting out, because poor weather can cancel the trip.
