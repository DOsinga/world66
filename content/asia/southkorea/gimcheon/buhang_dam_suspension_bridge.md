Buhang Dam Suspension Bridge gives Gimcheon a light outdoor adventure stop. The bridge crosses the reservoir near the dam, with open views over the water and the low mountains around it.

It is best treated as part of a wider reservoir visit, especially if you are also interested in the nearby skywalk and zipline area.
