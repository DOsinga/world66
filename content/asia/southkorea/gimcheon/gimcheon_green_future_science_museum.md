Gimcheon Green Future Science Museum is a hands-on science museum focused on climate change, renewable energy, and environmental technology. The exhibits are aimed mostly at families, with interactive displays, a 4D dome theatre, and activity rooms where children can try simple energy and engineering projects.

It is a useful stop if you are travelling with children or want an indoor break between Gimcheon's temples, ponds, and mountain scenery.
