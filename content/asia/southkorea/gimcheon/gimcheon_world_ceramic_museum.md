Gimcheon World Ceramic Museum covers Korean and international ceramics, from celadon and porcelain to folk pottery and contemporary work. The museum also hosts temporary exhibitions and hands-on programs, making it a stronger stop than a simple display room.

It is best for visitors already spending time around Gimcheon, especially those interested in Korean craft traditions or looking for an indoor cultural stop between temples and outdoor sights.
