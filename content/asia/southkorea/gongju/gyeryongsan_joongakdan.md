Gyeryongsan Joongakdan is a Joseon-era ritual building connected with state rites to the mountain spirit of Gyeryongsan. The structure is recognised as a Korean treasure and preserves a formal architectural style associated with court ritual.

It is a focused stop for visitors already exploring Gyeryongsan or Gongju's historic sites, especially those interested in Korea's older religious traditions.
