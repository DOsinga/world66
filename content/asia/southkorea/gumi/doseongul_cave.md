Doseongul Cave is one of the small sights that makes the Geumosan walk feel varied. It sits above Haeunsa Temple on the route toward Daehye Falls, tucked into the rocky slope rather than presented as a big show cave.

The cave is worth the short detour if you are already walking the mountain. Its value is in the sequence: temple, cliff, cave, waterfall, and then the harder trail above.
