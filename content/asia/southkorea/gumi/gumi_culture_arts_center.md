Gumi Culture & Arts Center is the city's main stage for concerts, theatre, dance, musicals, and rotating art exhibitions. It has large and small performance halls, exhibition rooms, and rehearsal spaces, so the experience depends entirely on what is scheduled.

Check the programme before making a special trip. The center is near City Hall and the downtown area, which makes it easy to combine with dinner or coffee before a performance.
