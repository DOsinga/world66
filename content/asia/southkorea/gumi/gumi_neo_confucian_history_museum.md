The Gumi Neo-Confucian History Museum explores the region's Confucian heritage through exhibits on Joseon-era scholarship, local figures, and traditional cultural practices. The grounds include traditional-style buildings, walking paths, and a pond, so it works as both a museum visit and a quiet cultural stop near Geumosan.
