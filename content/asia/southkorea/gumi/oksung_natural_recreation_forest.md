Oksung Natural Recreation Forest is a quiet reservoir-side forest retreat north of Gumi. Walking trails, lakeside decks, and wooded viewpoints make it a gentle nature stop, especially for families or travellers who want fresh air without a hard mountain hike.

The cabins and forest lodges are part of the complex, but the useful travel reason to come is the scenery: pine, water, and easy paths close enough to Gumi for a relaxed half-day.
