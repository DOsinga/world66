President Park Chung Hee Birthplace preserves the traditional house where South Korea's former president was born, now joined by a memorial hall, statue, and garden. It is a charged historical site rather than a neutral shrine, and it is most interesting if you want to understand how modern Korean politics is remembered outside Seoul.
