Lee Sung Dang is a famous Gunsan bakery that traces its roots to the Japanese period and is often described as one of Korea's oldest bakeries. The signature order is danpatppang, a soft bun filled with sweet red bean paste.

Queues can be long, but it is close to the modern-history quarter, so it makes an easy food stop between museums.
