Daein Market is most interesting on Saturday evenings, when the old market turns into a small art-and-food night market. Stalls sell handmade accessories, ceramics, soaps, snacks, and other small goods, and the mix of artists and casual food vendors makes it a lively downtown stop rather than just a shopping errand.
