The Gwangju Biennale is one of Asia's major contemporary art events, held in even-numbered years with exhibitions across the Biennale halls and associated venues. If your dates line up, it can turn Gwangju from a good stop into a destination.

Outside biennale season, the area around Jungoe Park still has museums and cultural facilities, but the energy is different. Check the calendar before planning a trip around it.
