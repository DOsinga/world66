Gwangju Student Independence Movement Memorial Hall commemorates the student uprising against Japanese colonial rule in 1929. The memorial and museum stand in a quiet hilltop park, away from the busier downtown May 18 democracy sites.

Inside, exhibits explain the events and the wider student movement through documents, models, and historical displays. It takes a little effort to reach, often by taxi, but it adds useful depth to Gwangju's strong identity as a city of resistance and civic memory.
