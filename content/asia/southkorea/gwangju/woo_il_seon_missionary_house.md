Woo Il-Seon Missionary House is one of Gwangju's oldest surviving Western-style houses, built in the 1920s for American missionary Robert M. Wilson. Its appeal is quiet but real: a small domestic building that shows how foreign missions, medicine, and education shaped parts of modern Gwangju.

It fits naturally into a walk around Yangnim-dong, where missionary-era buildings sit beside cafes, murals, and older neighbourhood lanes.
