Daebudo Bangameori Food Culture Street is the island's most useful food stop, a cluster of seafood places near the northern end of Daebudo. It is especially known for *bajilack* noodle soup, a clam noodle soup that suits the mudflat coast around here.

Come hungry rather than expecting a polished attraction. The pleasure is in choosing between sliced raw fish, grilled shellfish, soy-sauce marinated crab, and clam-rich stews before or after a drive along the seawall.
