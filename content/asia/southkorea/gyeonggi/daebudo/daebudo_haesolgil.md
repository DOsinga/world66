Daebudo Haesolgil is a set of walking routes around Daebudo, divided into several courses with different coastal and village scenery. The paths are a good way to see the island beyond its roads and seafood restaurants.

Pick a section rather than trying to cover the whole route in one outing, especially if you are relying on public transport.
