Metasequoia Road is a straight, photogenic stretch beside Daebu Bada Hyanggi Theme Park, lined with tall dawn redwoods on both sides. It is a simple place, but good for a walk or bicycle ride when you want something quieter than the island's tidal flats and seafood areas.
