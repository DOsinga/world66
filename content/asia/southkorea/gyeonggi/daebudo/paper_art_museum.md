The Paper Art Museum is a small hands-on museum focused on paper-making and paper craft. It combines exhibits with family-oriented activity spaces, including a children's art museum, traditional play experiences, hanok-style spaces, and craft-making programs.

It works best as part of a broader Daebudo day, especially for families looking for an indoor stop between coastal viewpoints, tidal flats, and the island's other small museums.
