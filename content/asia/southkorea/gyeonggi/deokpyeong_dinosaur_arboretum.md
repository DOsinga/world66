Deokpyeong Dinosaur Arboretum mixes a planted forest garden with dinosaur and insect exhibits, aimed especially at families travelling through Icheon and the wider Gyeonggi area. The setting at the foot of the hills keeps it from feeling like a simple indoor attraction.

Come for an easy half-day rather than a major cultural stop. Children get the most out of the dinosaur theme, while adults will appreciate the walking paths and shade when the weather is kind.
