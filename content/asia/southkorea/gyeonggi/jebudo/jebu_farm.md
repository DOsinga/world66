Jebu Farm is a small family stop on Jebudo, aimed mostly at children. The appeal is simple: animal feeding, short rides, and hands-on farm activities away from the beach and tidal road.

It is seasonal and weather-dependent, so check locally before promising it to children. When open, it adds an easy family break to a day on the island.
