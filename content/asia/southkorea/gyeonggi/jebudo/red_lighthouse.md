The red lighthouse is one of Jebudo's most recognisable landmarks, standing out sharply against the grey-blue West Sea. It is a simple stop, but the colour, sea wall, and open horizon make it a good place for photos and a short waterfront walk.

Come near sunset if the tide and weather cooperate. The lighthouse combines easily with Jebu Beach, the coastal walkway, and the tidal road that links the island to the mainland.
