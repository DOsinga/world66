Baeri Samneung Tombs are three rounded Silla burial mounds in the Namsan area, away from the busiest central tomb parks. Their power is quiet: grass-covered forms, old stone figures nearby, and the feeling that Gyeongju's royal past extends well beyond the postcard circuit.

They are best visited as part of a Namsan outing, where temples, carvings, and tombs sit in a wooded landscape. The site rewards slow looking more than photography, especially if you already know Daereungwon and want a less crowded echo of the same Silla world.
