Daereungwon is an enclosed park in the heart of Gyeongju containing 23 large royal burial mounds of the Silla dynasty, grassy domes rising up to 22 metres high among trees and flower beds. These are the tombs of kings, queens, and nobles who died between roughly the fourth and sixth centuries CE, and their scale gives some sense of the wealth and organisation of the Silla state.

One tomb — Cheonmachong — has been excavated and opened as a small indoor museum, its timber chamber reconstructed so you can walk through and see replicas of the grave goods found here: gold crowns, gilt bronze shoes, earrings, swords, and horse gear. The originals are in the National Museum. Most of the other mounds have not been excavated and remain as they have always been, presiding over the surrounding neighbourhood with the quiet permanence of natural hills. Entry to the park is ticketed; the surrounding neighbourhood outside the park wall also contains smaller unenclosed tumuli worth exploring.
