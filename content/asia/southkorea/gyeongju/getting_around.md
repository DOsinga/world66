---
title: "Getting Around"
type: section
---

The central historic sites — Daereungwon, Cheomseongdae, Banwolseong, and Anapji Pond — are walkable from each other and from Gyeongju Station. Renting a bicycle is an excellent option for exploring the central area; several rental shops operate near the main attractions.

The outlying temple sites (Bulguksa, Seokguram, Girimsa, Golgulsa, Poseokjeong) require buses or taxis. City buses no. 10 and 11 run to Bulguksa; other destinations are served by less frequent services. Taxis are affordable by Korean standards, and many drivers in Gyeongju are accustomed to short trips between historic sites.
