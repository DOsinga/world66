Gyeongju Donggung Garden is a landscaped garden and bird park that offers a softer break from tombs, temples, and royal ruins. It is not ancient Gyeongju, but it works well when you need an easy, colourful stop near the Bomun tourist area.

The appeal is straightforward: greenhouse planting, birds, paths, and photo-friendly corners. Use it as a light family visit rather than a substitute for the city's major Silla sites.
