Namsan, south of Gyeongju, is less a single mountain sight than an outdoor museum spread across ridges and valleys. Trails pass rock-carved Buddhas, pagodas, temple foundations, and quiet forest, making the mountain one of the best ways to feel the old Silla capital beyond its royal tombs.

Choose a route before you go, because the area is large and the rewards are scattered. Even a short hike can be memorable, but a longer walk lets the mountain's rhythm build: stone, pine, shrine, view, and then another unexpected carving in the rock.
