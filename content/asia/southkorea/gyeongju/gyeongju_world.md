Gyeongju World is the city's main amusement park, with rides including a Ferris wheel and roller coasters. The complex also includes smaller themed areas such as California Beach, Kiddy Land, and a children's animal ranch.

It is a family-oriented break from Gyeongju's temples, tombs, and museums, especially if you are staying near the Bomun Lake resort area.
