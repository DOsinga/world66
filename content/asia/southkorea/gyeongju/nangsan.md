Nangsan is a low, wooded mountain in Gyeongju that carries a long Silla-era religious memory. Rather than one single monument, the draw is the cluster of Buddhist sites, old temple traces, and stories tied to the sacred landscape east of the ancient capital.

It rewards slow exploration more than box-ticking. Visitors interested in Gyeongju's archaeology will find it a quieter counterpoint to the royal tombs and museum, with the same Silla history spread through paths and wooded slopes.
