Oreung Tombs Park is one of Gyeongju’s quieter royal burial landscapes, traditionally associated with early Silla rulers and the kingdom’s foundation stories. The grassy mounds are simpler than the heavily visited Daereungwon complex, but the calm is exactly why they are worth considering.

Come when you want Gyeongju’s history without tour-bus density. The park rewards slow walking, reading the context, and letting the low green forms register as part of a city where royal memory is built into the ground itself.
