Samneunggol is one of the richest walking areas on Gyeongju's Namsan, a valley where Silla-period Buddhist carvings, stone figures, temple traces, and royal tombs appear along the paths. It is less about one object than about accumulation: history surfacing every few minutes in the woods.

Wear proper shoes and give it time. The valley is best for travellers who like archaeology in the landscape, where a headless Buddha or weathered relief can feel more powerful because it is still sitting on the mountain that shaped it.
