The largest traditional market in Gyeongju, a covered market near the old train station selling everything from fresh produce and raw fish to clothing and hardware. The food court inside is the best place in the city for inexpensive Korean food — bibimbap, sundae (blood sausage), gimbap, and the Gyeongju speciality ssambap (rice wrapped in assorted leaves with small side dishes).

Gyeongju has its own confectionery traditions: hwangnam bread (hwangnam-ppang), a small pastry filled with red bean paste, has been made here for over a century and is the most popular food souvenir from the city. Several shops in and around the market sell it. The market is busiest in the mornings and early afternoons.
