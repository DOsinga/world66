Sungdeokjeon is a historic shrine south of Oreung, connected with stories of early Silla rulers. The present shrine reflects later rebuilding, but the site still fits into Gyeongju's wider landscape of royal tombs, pavilions, and ritual places.

It is a small stop, best combined with Oreung Tombs Park and the nearby southern edge of central Gyeongju.
