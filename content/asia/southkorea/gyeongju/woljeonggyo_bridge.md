Woljeonggyo Bridge is a reconstructed Silla-style covered bridge near Gyochon Traditional Village and the Gyeongju National Museum. The present bridge is modern, but it gives a good sense of the scale and colour of historic Korean timber architecture.

It is especially popular after dark, when the roofline and red pillars are lit and reflected in the water. Pair it with Gyochon and the nearby palace and tomb sites rather than treating it as a standalone outing.
