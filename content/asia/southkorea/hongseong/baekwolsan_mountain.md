Baekwolsan Mountain is a modest 394-metre hill south-west of Hongseong, with gentle, well-kept trails that suit a short walk rather than a hard mountain day. The summit gives open views over the surrounding fields, low mountains, and the west coast.

It works as an easy outdoor stop if you are already exploring Hongseong's countryside and want a climb with a clear payoff.
