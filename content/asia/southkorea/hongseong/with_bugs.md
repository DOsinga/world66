With Bugs is an insect learning centre near Gwangcheon, aimed mainly at children and families. The appeal is hands-on rather than polished: visitors can see live insects up close and learn about how different species live.

It is not a major museum, but it is a useful family stop if you are travelling through Hongseong with children who need something more active than another temple or viewpoint.
