Wolgwangsa Temple is a quiet Buddhist temple in the Hongseong area, known locally for its moonlit setting and calm atmosphere. It is a low-key stop rather than a major temple complex, but worthwhile for travellers already exploring Hongseong's smaller historic and mountain sights.
