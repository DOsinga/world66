Yongbongsa Temple sits near Yongbongsan Mountain and makes a good cultural stop before or after a walk on the ridge. The Buddhist temple is known for its quiet compound, traditional halls, and the dragon-and-phoenix symbolism in its name.

It is most worthwhile as part of a Yongbongsan outing: come for the mountain scenery, then leave time to walk the temple grounds and take in the architecture at an unhurried pace.
