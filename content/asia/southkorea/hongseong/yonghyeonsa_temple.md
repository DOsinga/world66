Yonghyeonsa Temple is a quiet Buddhist temple in a forest setting near Hongseong. Local stories connect the site with dragons, and the temple is best known as a place for meditation, prayer, and practice close to nature.

It is not a major monument, but the wooded setting makes it a calm stop for travellers interested in smaller Korean temples.
