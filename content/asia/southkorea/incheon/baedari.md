Baedari is an old Incheon neighbourhood known for its secondhand bookstore street, small art spaces, and murals around Geumchang-dong and Songhyeon-dong. Only a handful of the old bookshops remain, but the area still gives a different view of Incheon from the waterfront and Chinatown.

Look for Space Beam, an arts space in a former brewery building, then continue through the lanes toward Changyoung Elementary School for murals showing scenes from local life. It is a short, low-key walk rather than a polished attraction.
