The Creation Museum of Natural History is a small nature-focused museum and garden space in Incheon, with displays aimed at families and school groups. Its grounds are planted with many kinds of flowers and trees, making it more relaxed than a standard indoor museum stop.

It is not one of Incheon's major sights, but it can be useful if you are travelling with children or already exploring this side of the city.
