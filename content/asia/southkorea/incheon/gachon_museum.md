Gachon Museum is a private museum in Incheon focused on Korean medical history and cultural artifacts. Its collection includes traditional medicine materials, old books, and several designated cultural properties.

The museum is most rewarding for visitors with a specific interest in medicine, printing, or Korean cultural history. It pairs naturally with the nearby Incheon Landing Operation memorial area if you are already in this part of the city.
