Ganghwa Hyanggyo is a Joseon-era Confucian school on Ganghwa Island. Like other hyanggyo, it served both as a local academy and as a ritual space honouring Confucian sages.

It is a modest but worthwhile stop if you are already exploring Ganghwa's temples, fortifications, and older settlement sites.
