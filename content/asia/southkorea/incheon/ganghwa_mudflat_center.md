Ganghwa Mudflat Center introduces the wide tidal flats around Ganghwado and Yeongjongdo, among the largest and best-preserved mudflat systems in Korea. The area is important for coastal ecology and birdlife, and the centre helps visitors understand the landscape before exploring the shoreline.
