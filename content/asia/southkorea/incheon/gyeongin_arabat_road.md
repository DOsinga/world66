Gyeongin Arabat Road is an 18-kilometre canal route linking the Han River system with the waters off Incheon. Paths and parkland run along both sides, making it useful for a long bike ride or a quieter waterside walk away from the old port.

The route connects with the Han River bike paths, so stronger cyclists can ride between Seoul and the West Sea. For a shorter visit, choose one access point and treat it as a canal-side park rather than trying to cover the whole length.
