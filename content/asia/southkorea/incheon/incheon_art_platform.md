Incheon Art Platform reuses old-port buildings as studios, galleries, exhibition spaces, and performance rooms. It is one of the better places to see how the city is turning its harbour history into cultural space.

The location makes it especially useful. It sits close to Chinatown and the open-port streets, so you can fold it into a walk rather than treating it as a separate museum trip.
