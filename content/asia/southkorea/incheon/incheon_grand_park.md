Incheon Grand Park is a large inland park with enough variety to justify a stop beyond a quick neighbourhood stroll. It has a botanical garden, arboretum, children's zoo, sledding area, and easy trails around Gwanmosan and Sanga.

The park is especially busy in cherry blossom season, when its trees bloom a little later than many other spots around Incheon.
