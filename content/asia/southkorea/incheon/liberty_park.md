Liberty Park is a historic hilltop park beside Incheon's Chinatown. It is often described as Korea's first modern Western-style park, and it remains a popular local viewpoint over the port, downtown Incheon, and the surrounding hills.

The park is also known for its General MacArthur statue and a memorial to U.S.-South Korean relations. Come in spring for cherry blossoms, or use it as a quiet break while walking between Chinatown and the nearby open-port heritage area.
