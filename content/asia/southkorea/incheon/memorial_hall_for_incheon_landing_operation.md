The Memorial Hall for Incheon Landing Operation explains the 1950 amphibious landing that changed the course of the Korean War. Displays include military equipment, landing craft material, and a memorial statue.

It is a specialist stop, but an important one if you are interested in the war history that shaped modern Korea. It also helps explain why Incheon is more than a port suburb of Seoul.
