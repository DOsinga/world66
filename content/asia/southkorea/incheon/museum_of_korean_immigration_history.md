The Museum of Korean Immigration History sits on Wolmi Island and looks outward from Incheon's port identity. It focuses on Korean emigrants and the lives they built overseas.

It is a good fit with Wolmido rather than a standalone reason to cross town. Visit it when you want Incheon's harbour story to mean more than ferries and seafood.
