The National Museum of Biological Resources introduces Korea's native plants, animals, and ecological diversity through specimen displays and hands-on education spaces. It is useful for families and visitors with an interest in natural history.

The museum sits on Incheon's western edge, so allow for the extra travel time from the city's main sightseeing areas.
