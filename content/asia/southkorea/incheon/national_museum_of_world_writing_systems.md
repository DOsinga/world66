The National Museum of World Writing Systems is a specialist museum in Songdo devoted to scripts and written language. Its white, scroll-like building sits in the park landscape, with exhibits covering dozens of writing systems from around the world.

It is a good stop for families, language lovers, or anyone looking for a quieter museum visit in Incheon's newer waterfront district.
