Oceanscope is a small waterfront viewpoint made from three container-like structures facing the Yellow Sea. It is best used as a sunset stop, with views toward Incheon Bridge and the open water beyond Songdo.

There is not much to do beyond looking and taking photos, so fold it into a broader Songdo walk rather than making a special trip across the city.
