Songdo is Incheon's planned future-city district, built with towers, broad roads, shops, hotels, and a carefully designed Central Park. It can feel staged, but that is partly the interest.

Come here for the contrast with the old port. The lake and park are pleasant, and the skyline shows the version of Incheon that wants to look forward rather than back to the harbour.
