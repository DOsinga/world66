Bakdaljae Natural Recreational Forest is a wooded recreation area below Bakdaljae Pass, with pine forest, mixed woodland, walking trails, pavilions, and viewpoints toward the surrounding Jecheon mountains.

It works best as a half-day nature break rather than a headline sight. Families will find simple facilities and play areas, while walkers can use it as an easy forest-bathing stop before continuing to other Jecheon sights.
