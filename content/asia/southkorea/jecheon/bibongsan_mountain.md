Bibongsan Mountain rises above Cheongpung Lake and is known for views across the water and surrounding ridges. It is a worthwhile nature stop in the Jecheon area, especially when combined with the nearby Cheongpung Lake Cable Car or Cheongpung Cultural Heritage Complex.
