Cheongpung Lake Cable Car is the straightforward way to see why this part of Jecheon draws Korean weekend travellers. The ride climbs above the lake, opening views over the reservoir arms, wooded ridges, and small settlements around Cheongpung.

It is best paired with the nearby heritage complex. One gives the landscape; the other gives the vanished valley some history.
