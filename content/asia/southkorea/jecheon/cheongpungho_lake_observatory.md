Cheongpungho Lake Observatory is a forested lookout above Jecheon's lake country. The short walk up is the point: from the observation deck, Cheongpungho Lake opens out below with the surrounding hills behind it.

It is a simple viewpoint rather than a full attraction, but it pairs naturally with the Cheongpung Cultural Heritage Complex and other lake-side stops.
