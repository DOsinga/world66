Gyodong Folk Painting Village is a mural village in Jecheon with folk-painting themed walls and small hands-on activities. Visitors can walk the lanes, look for painted scenes, and sometimes join workshops in folk painting, food, or pottery.

It is a light, local stop rather than a major museum, but it gives Jecheon a colourful neighbourhood walk.
