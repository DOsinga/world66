The Intellectual Museum is a small Jecheon museum focused on Korean territory and border history, including Dokdo and other places with a disputed or symbolic place in Korean historical memory. Its exhibits are specialised, but they give context to themes that appear often in Korean public history.

This is best for travellers with a particular interest in modern Korean identity, maps, and territorial questions. For a broader first stop in the area, pair it with Jecheon's larger cultural and natural sights.
