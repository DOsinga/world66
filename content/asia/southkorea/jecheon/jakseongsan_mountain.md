Jakseongsan Mountain rises north of Geumseong and is one of Jecheon's quieter hiking areas. It sits near the better-known Cheongpung Lake sights, so it suits travellers who want a mountain walk rather than another reservoir viewpoint.

The appeal is seasonal and outdoorsy: wooded slopes, summer hiking, and views across the surrounding Jecheon countryside. Bring proper shoes and check local transport before setting out, since this is not a casual city-centre stroll.
