Jecheon International Music Film Festival gives the city a cultural season of its own. It combines film screenings, concerts, and music-focused programming, with venues in town and around the lake area.

It is not a reason to come on any random week of the year, but it can turn Jecheon from a scenic detour into a proper stop if your timing lines up.
