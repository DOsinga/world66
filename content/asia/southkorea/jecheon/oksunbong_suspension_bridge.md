Oksunbong Suspension Bridge crosses part of Cheongpung Lake with views toward Oksunbong Peak, one of Jecheon's named scenic views. The bridge is more about the setting than the engineering: water below, wooded slopes around the lake, and the pale rock face of the peak in the distance.

It works best as part of a Cheongpung Lake circuit with the cultural heritage complex or the cable car. Go on a clear day, when the lake and mountain views justify the detour.
