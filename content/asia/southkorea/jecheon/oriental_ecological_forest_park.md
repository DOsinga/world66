Oriental Ecological Forest Park is a landscaped forest park near Biryongdam Reservoir. Lawns, trees, and walking paths make it a gentle outdoor stop, especially in autumn when the leaves change.

It is not a major sight on its own, but it works well as a quiet walk while exploring Jecheon's nearby lakes and forest areas.
