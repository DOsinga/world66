Uirimji Historical Museum explains the reservoir that gives northern Jecheon its strongest historic sight. Exhibits cover the old irrigation works, how the reservoir was built and managed, and the ecology around the water.

It is best paired with a walk around [Uirimji Reservoir](/asia/southkorea/jecheon/uirimji_reservoir), since the museum gives useful context to a place that otherwise looks like a simple lakeside park.
