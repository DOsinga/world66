Arii Hills Skywalk is the quick-hit viewpoint above Jeongseon. The glass-floored platform looks over the Dong River valley and the curved landform of Bamseom below, with mountain ridges closing the horizon.

It is a short visit, but it helps explain the geography of the county. Go on a clear day; in haze or rain, the skywalk loses most of its point.
