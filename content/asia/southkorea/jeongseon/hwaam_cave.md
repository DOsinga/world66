Hwaam Cave combines two stories in one underground visit. Part of the route follows an old gold-mine area, while the natural limestone cave opens into larger chambers with formations and lit walkways.

It is more developed than wild, but the mix of mining history and cave scenery makes it one of Jeongseon's stronger all-weather sights.
