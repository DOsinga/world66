Jeongamsa Temple is a mountain temple associated with the Silla monk Jajang. Its main hall is unusual because it has no central Buddha image; the focus is instead the Sumano Pagoda, which is said to enshrine Buddha relics.

The temple is a quiet stop for travellers exploring the high country around Jeongseon. Come for the old pagoda, the forested setting, and the sense of a working temple set well away from the busier town sights.
