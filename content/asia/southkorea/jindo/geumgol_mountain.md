Geumgol Mountain is a low but distinctive rocky mountain on Jindo, known for caves, cliff formations, and Buddhist remains set into the slope. The middle cave contains a five-storey stone pagoda and a seated figure, reminders of the old Haeonsa Temple that once stood here.

At 193 metres it is not a major climb, but the reward is in the rock shapes and the quiet religious traces. It makes a good short outing if you want something beyond the island's coastal views.
