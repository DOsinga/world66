Jindo Dog Theme Park is dedicated to the island's famous Jindo dog breed. The site includes a promotion centre, activity courses, a medical centre, and family facilities.

Visitors come mainly to see Jindo dog demonstrations and sports displays, which make it one of the more distinctive family stops on the island.
