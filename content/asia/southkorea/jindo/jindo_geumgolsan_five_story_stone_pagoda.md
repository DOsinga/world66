Jindo Geumgolsan Five-story Stone Pagoda is a small historic stop linked to the former Haewolsa Temple site. The pagoda is thought to reflect Baekje-style stonework carried into the region during the Goryeo period, so it rewards travellers interested in Korea's older Buddhist remains rather than big-ticket scenery.
