Jindo Island Jinseong Fortress is a small but old defensive site in Namdong-ri, Imhoe-myeon. Its remains date back to the Three Kingdoms period, giving the southern side of Jindo a historical stop beyond the island's better-known sea parting and sunset viewpoints.
