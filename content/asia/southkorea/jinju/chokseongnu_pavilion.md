Chokseongnu Pavilion stands inside Jinju Fortress on a bluff above the river. It served as a command post in wartime and a civil examination site in quieter periods, which gives it both military and scholarly weight.

The view is the point: tiled roof, cliff edge, water below, and the city beyond. Visit it as part of the fortress loop rather than as a separate stop.
