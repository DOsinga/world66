Jinju National Museum is a compact branch of Korea's national museum system inside the fortress grounds. Its strongest material covers the Imjin War and the long, complicated history of contact and conflict between Korea and Japan.

English labelling is not exhaustive, but there is enough to make the fortress story clearer. Go before or after walking the walls so the names and battles have somewhere to land.
