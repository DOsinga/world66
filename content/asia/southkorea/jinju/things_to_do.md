Jinju is built around one strong historical core. [Jinju Fortress](/asia/southkorea/jinju/jinju_fortress) is the essential stop, with [Chokseongnu Pavilion](/asia/southkorea/jinju/chokseongnu_pavilion) overlooking the Namgang River and the [Jinju National Museum](/asia/southkorea/jinju/jinju_national_museum) explaining the Imjin War context. In October, the [Namgang Yudeung Festival](/asia/southkorea/jinju/namgang_yudeung_festival) turns the same riverfront into the city's signature event.
