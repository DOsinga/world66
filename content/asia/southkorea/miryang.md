*Asia > South Korea > Miryang*

Miryang sits inland from Busan and Ulsan, where the Miryang River runs below wooded hills and old pavilions. It is not a big-city stop. The appeal is a quieter Korean day of views, temples, valleys, and the local version of Arirang, one of the country's best-known folk songs.

The town's classic sight is [Yeongnamnu Pavilion](/asia/southkorea/miryang/yeongnamnu_pavilion), set above the river and often counted among Korea's great pavilions. Outside the centre, the hills become more important: [Eoreumgol](/asia/southkorea/miryang/eoreumgol) is the strange cold valley known as Ice Valley, and [Pyochungsa Temple](/asia/southkorea/miryang/pyochungsa_temple) sits in the Yeongnam Alps with a strong link to monk-soldier history.

Miryang works best with a car, taxi budget, or a patient day on local buses. The sights are spread out, but that is also why the place feels more open than the large southeastern cities nearby.
