The Miryang Ice Valley Cable Car climbs from the Eoreumgol area toward the higher slopes of the Yeongnam Alps. The ride is short, but the reward is the wider mountain view and easier access to high-country scenery.

Check operating hours before building a day around it, especially outside peak season or in poor weather.
