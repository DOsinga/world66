Twin Tunnel turns an abandoned railway tunnel into a light-filled underground walk with photo zones and themed displays. It is not subtle, but it makes good use of the tunnel's steady temperature: cool in summer and sheltered in winter.

Go if you like playful, artificial sights or are travelling with children. If you only have time for one Miryang attraction, choose Yeongnamnu or the mountain valley first.
