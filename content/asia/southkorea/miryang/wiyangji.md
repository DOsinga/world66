Wiyangji is a quiet pond north of central Miryang, best known for its old fringe trees and calm reflections. In spring, when the white blossoms come out, it becomes one of the prettiest small scenes in the area.

It is not a full-day sight, but it is a strong short stop if you are moving between the town and the countryside.
