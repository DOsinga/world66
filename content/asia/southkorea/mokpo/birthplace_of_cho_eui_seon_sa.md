The birthplace of Cho Eui Seon Sa is a small memorial site devoted to the Joseon-era monk closely tied to Korea's traditional tea culture. Pavilions, a garden, and a modest exhibition make it a quiet stop for travelers interested in tea, Buddhism, and local history around Mokpo.
