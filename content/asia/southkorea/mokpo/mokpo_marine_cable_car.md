The Mokpo Marine Cable Car runs from the North Port over Yudalsan and across the water to Gohado. It is an easy way to see the city as a port rather than just a grid of streets: below are fishing boats, rooftops, harbour cranes, and the islands that fill the southwest sea.

Clear weather matters. If the hills are hazy or the wind is up, save your money for the next morning.
