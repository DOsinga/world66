The Mokpo Maritime Museum is run by Korea's national institute for underwater cultural heritage. Its displays focus on shipwrecks, old trade routes, recovered ceramics, and the practical work of excavating objects from the sea.

It is a good fit for Mokpo because the city has always faced the islands. Visit it with Gatbawi and the Natural History Museum nearby rather than treating it as a standalone stop.
