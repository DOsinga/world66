Mokpo Natural History Museum is the broad, family-friendly museum in Gatbawi Culture Town. The collection moves from dinosaur fossils and minerals to birds, insects, marine life, and material from Korea's southwest.

It is not the reason to come to Mokpo, but it rounds out the Gatbawi museum cluster well, especially with children or on a wet day.
