Military Theme Park in Muan is an outdoor military museum and family attraction. Visitors can see aircraft, helicopters, tanks, and armored vehicles up close, with displays that explain military equipment and training.

It is most useful for families or military history enthusiasts travelling around Muan rather than as a general-purpose sightseeing stop.
