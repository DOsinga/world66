Muan Octopus Park is a seaside family stop by the tidal flats of Jogeumnaru Beach, built around Muan's local octopus identity. Its most visible feature is a 14-metre octopus-shaped observatory connected to a slide bridge.

The park also has coastal paths, flower plantings, mudflat experiences, and sunset views over the sea. It works best as a light stop while exploring Muan's coast rather than as a destination on its own.
