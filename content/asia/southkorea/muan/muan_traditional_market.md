Muan Traditional Market is practical and local, with produce, seafood, side dishes, and snacks rather than tourist polish. Look for steamed onion buns and other reminders that Muan is one of Korea's major onion-growing areas.

The five-day market rhythm brings extra life on dates ending in 4 and 9.
