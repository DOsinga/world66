Oh Seung-woo Art Museum focuses on the work of the Korean painter Oh Seung-woo, whose career ranged from temple and folk-life scenes to large mountain landscapes and symbolic later works. The museum gives Muan a worthwhile cultural stop beyond its better-known wetlands and lotus ponds.
