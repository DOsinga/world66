Oseungu Museum of Art is dedicated to Oh Seung-woo, the Muan-born painter whose work moved from temple scenes and folk subjects into bright, symbolic landscapes and abstracted traditional motifs. It is a focused regional art museum, best for visitors who want a quieter cultural stop while exploring Muan or nearby Mokpo.
