Sikyeongjeong Pavilion was built in 1630 by the scholar Hanho Im Yeon as a place for study, writing, and conversation. Its name is often read as a reference to shadows resting for a moment, matching the quiet setting.

The pavilion is a modest historic stop, but its age, scholarly association, and surrounding views make it a useful addition to a slow Muan itinerary.
