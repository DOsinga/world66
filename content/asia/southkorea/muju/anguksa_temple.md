Anguksa Temple sits near the summit area of Jeoksangsan, close to the old royal archive sites it was built to protect. The reward is a quiet temple atmosphere and views across the surrounding mountain ridges.

It works best as part of a Jeoksangsan outing, not as a standalone trip from Muju town.
