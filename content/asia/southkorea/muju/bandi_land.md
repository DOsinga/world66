Bandi Land is Muju's family-friendly nature stop, built around the firefly image that the county uses as a symbol of clean water and air. The complex includes insect exhibits, an observatory, and ecological displays.

It is more educational park than wild nature. Go if you are travelling with children, or if the firefly festival has made you curious about the creature behind Muju's branding.
