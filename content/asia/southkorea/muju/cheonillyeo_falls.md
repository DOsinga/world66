Cheonillyeo Falls is a small waterfall on the wooded slopes of Jeoksangsan, better as a cooling detour than as a destination by itself. The name suggests a fall high enough to touch the sky, but the real pleasure is quieter: shade, mist, and a break from the road up the mountain.

Pair it with Jeoksangsan and the nearby viewpoints rather than making a special trip only for the falls.
