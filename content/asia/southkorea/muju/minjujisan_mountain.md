Minjujisan is a 1,241-metre peak in the Sobaek range, where the borders of Chungcheong, Jeolla, and Gyeongsang meet. The ridge walk gives broad views across inland Korea, with the mountain at its most dramatic when winter snow sharpens the line of the peaks.

It is best treated as a countryside hike from Muju rather than a quick roadside stop. Come prepared for mountain weather and check local trail conditions before setting out.
