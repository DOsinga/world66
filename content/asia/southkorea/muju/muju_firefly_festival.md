The Muju Firefly Festival is the county's most distinctive event. It celebrates fireflies as a sign of clean streams and countryside, with night programs, performances, and guided firefly experiences.

Dates move, usually around late summer or early autumn, so check the official schedule before planning a trip around it. If the timing works, this is the most atmospheric way to see Muju's local identity rather than just its mountains.
