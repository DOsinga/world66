Buljeong Recreational Forest is a mountain forest north of Mungyeong Saejae, with hiking paths, picnic spots, and pine and oak woods around Buljeong Stream. It is a gentle nature stop rather than a hard trek, especially in summer when the stream makes the forest cooler than the town below.
