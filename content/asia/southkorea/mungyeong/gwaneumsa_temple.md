Gwaneumsa Temple sits in the hills around Mungyeongsaejae, close enough to combine with the park but quiet enough to feel separate from the main flow of visitors.

Come for the setting more than architectural spectacle. The temple, stonework, and valley views make a calm side stop before or after the old pass trail.
