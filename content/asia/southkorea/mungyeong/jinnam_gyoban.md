Jinnam Gyoban is one of Mungyeong's prettier landscape stops, with a stone bridge, clear water, cliffs, and pine-covered slopes gathered into a compact valley scene.

It is not a major sight in the formal sense, but it is a good place to break up a day between Mungyeongsaejae and the mining area. Autumn gives it the most colour.
