Mungyeong Rail Bike turns an old railway alignment between Mungyeong and Gaeun into a slow pedal ride through tunnels, bridges, and countryside. The four-seat bikes make it easy for families or couples, and the route gives a gentler view of the city's mining and rail landscape than a museum display alone.

The ride usually takes about 40 minutes one way, with shuttle transport back to the starting point. Reserve ahead on weekends and holidays, when local demand can be high.
