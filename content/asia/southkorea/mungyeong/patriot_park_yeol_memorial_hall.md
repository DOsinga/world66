This memorial hall focuses on Park Yeol, the Korean anarchist and independence activist jailed in Japan during the colonial period. It is more specialised than Mungyeong's landscape sights, but the subject is substantial.

Add it if you are interested in modern Korean history or if you are moving between Jeomchon and the Saejae area with time to spare.
