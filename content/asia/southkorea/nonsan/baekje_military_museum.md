Baekje Military Museum focuses on the military history of the Baekje kingdom and the Nonsan area. Exhibits include artifacts, reconstructed weapons and armour, dioramas, a 3D theatre, and an outdoor reconstructed defence wall.

It is a good supporting stop for travellers already visiting Nonsan's temples, seowon, and historic sites.
