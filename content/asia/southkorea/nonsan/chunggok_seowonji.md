Chunggok Seowonji is a small Confucian academy site near Donam Seowon, built to honour the Sayuksin, the six ministers remembered for their loyalty to the deposed King Danjong. It is a quieter, more specialised stop than Nonsan's headline sights, but useful if you are tracing Korea's seowon tradition beyond the UNESCO-listed names.
