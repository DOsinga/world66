Ganggyeong Cathedral is a registered cultural property in Nonsan, built in 1961 with an unusual peaked-arch interior structure. Its architecture makes it more distinctive than a routine modern parish church.

The cathedral is a short stop for visitors exploring Ganggyeong's historic and religious sites.
