The Ganggyeong Salted Seafood Exhibition Museum explains the history and culture of jeotgal, the salted seafood that made Ganggyeong a regional market town. Displays cover the main varieties, traditional production, and the role of the Geum River in the trade.

It is a specialized museum, but a useful stop if you are already exploring Nonsan's historic and food-related sights.
