The General Gyebaek Historic Site commemorates Gyebaek, the Baekje general remembered for his final stand against Silla forces in the 7th century. The site is a provincial monument and gives historical context to Nonsan's place in the old Baekje heartland.

It is best for travellers with an interest in Korean history, especially if paired with Nonsan's other Baekje-era and Confucian sites rather than treated as a single-purpose excursion.
