Jukrim Seowon is a Confucian academy site near Nonsan, associated with the scholars Jeong Ji-woong and Jeong Yu-gil. Spring and autumn memorial ceremonies are still held here.

It is a quiet historical stop rather than a major museum, but it helps round out Nonsan's older cultural sites.
