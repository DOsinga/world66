This small literature museum is devoted to the Korean novelist Kim Hong-shin, with exhibits on his major works and the literary world around them. It is a focused stop rather than a blockbuster museum, but worthwhile if you are interested in modern Korean writing or want a quieter cultural visit in Nonsan.
