Minae Bridge is a stone bridge in Chaeeun-myeon, designated as a tangible cultural heritage site of Chungcheongnam-do. Its name comes from the nearby Ganggyeongcheon Stream, which is also known locally as Minae.

The bridge is a small but worthwhile stop for travellers tracing Nonsan's older roads, waterways, and rural heritage sites. It works best as part of a short drive through the surrounding countryside rather than as a long standalone visit.
