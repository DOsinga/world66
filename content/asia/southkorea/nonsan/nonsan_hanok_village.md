Nonsan Hanok Village presents traditional Korean residential architecture in a setting adapted for visitors. It is a modest cultural stop rather than a major open-air museum, but it gives a useful look at hanok forms and domestic design for travellers already visiting Nonsan's temples, academies, and lake sights.
