Noseongsanseong is a stone mountain fortress on Noseongsan, designated Historic Site No. 393 of South Korea. The walls follow the ridge in the old *teme* style, using the mountain's shape as part of the defence.

The site is most interesting for travellers who enjoy Korean fortress walks: old stonework, wooded slopes, and views from the higher ground. It works best as a short historical outing from Nonsan rather than as a museum-style visit.
