Songbulam Maitreya is a provincial cultural heritage statue in the hills outside Nonsan. The figure is noted for its shallow carving and U-shaped robe lines, details that make it more rewarding if you take time to look closely.

It is a small heritage stop, best combined with other Nonsan temple and history sights rather than treated as a stand-alone trip.
