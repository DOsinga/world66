Ssanggyesa is a quiet temple outside central Nonsan, best known for the Budo-gun and Junggunbi at its entrance. Both are designated cultural assets of Chungcheongnam-do, giving the temple more weight than its modest first impression suggests.

Come for a calm temple stop rather than a big-ticket monument. It pairs best with other historic sites around Nonsan if you have your own transport.
