Strawberryhyang Rural Theme Park is built around Nonsan's best-known crop. The park has strawberry-themed exhibits, farm experience programs, and family activities that make more sense if you are travelling with children than if you are looking for a conventional museum.

It is most appealing during the strawberry season, when the local produce is part of the point. Outside that period, treat it as a light family stop rather than a major reason to come to Nonsan.
