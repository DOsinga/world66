The Tomb of Seongsammun honours Seong Sam-mun, a 15th-century scholar-official remembered as one of the Six Martyred Ministers of the Joseon dynasty. The site is designated cultural heritage by South Chungcheong Province.

It is a quiet, history-focused stop rather than a showpiece attraction, but it adds useful depth to a Nonsan visit if you are interested in Joseon history.
