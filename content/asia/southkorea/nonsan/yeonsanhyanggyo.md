Yeonsanhyanggyo is a Joseon-era Confucian school complex north of central Nonsan. The site preserves ritual and teaching spaces including Daeseongjeon Hall, Myeongnyundang, and old student quarters, giving a quiet look at the local academy system that shaped education and ceremonies outside Korea's royal capitals.
