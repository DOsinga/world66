40 Steps is a small restored streetscape in central Busan, with old-style lamps and bronze figures showing scenes from the hard years after the Korean War. It is not a large attraction, but it gives a quick sense of the area's postwar memory and is easy to combine with nearby markets and Yongdusan Park.
