Amisan Observatory looks over the Nakdong River estuary, its sand bars, and Dadaepo Beach. It is a strong photography stop on the western side of Busan, especially when the light catches the river channels and tidal flats.

The view is the reason to come. Pair it with Dadaepo or other west Busan sights rather than crossing the city for this alone.
