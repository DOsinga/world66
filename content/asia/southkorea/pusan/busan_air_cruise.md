Busan Air Cruise, also known as the Songdo Marine Cable Car, links Songdo Beach with Amnam Park across the water. The ride gives broad views of the coast, Yeongdo, Namhangdaegyo Bridge, and Busan's southern shoreline.

Standard cabins are fine for the view; the glass-floored Crystal Cabin adds a small thrill. It works well with a visit to Songdo Beach or Amnam Park.
