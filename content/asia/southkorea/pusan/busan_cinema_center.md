Busan Cinema Center is the home of the Busan International Film Festival and one of the city's boldest modern buildings. Its huge cantilevered roof shelters an outdoor cinema and becomes a light show after dark.

It is worth seeing even if you are not catching a film. Come in the evening, when the roof comes alive, and combine it with Centum City or the Haeundae side of Busan.
