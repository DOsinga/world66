Busan Maritime Natural History Museum focuses on marine life, with a large collection of sea-life exhibits that includes giant squid, whale sharks, and oarfish. It is a family-friendly museum and a useful indoor option in northern Busan.

The museum is best for visitors interested in natural history rather than maritime trade or naval history.
