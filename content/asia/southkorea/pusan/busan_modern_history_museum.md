Busan Modern History Museum gives useful context to a city often seen only through its port, beaches, and markets. The building itself carries a heavy history: it was used by Japan's Oriental Development Company during the colonial period and later by the United States Information Service after the Korean War.

The exhibitions cover Japanese occupation, wartime Busan, and Korea's postwar relationship with the United States. It is not a neutral-feeling museum, but that is part of why it is worth visiting; it shows how modern Busan was shaped by occupation, refugees, foreign power, and trade.
