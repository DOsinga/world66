Busan National University Museum is a small university museum focused on archaeology, folk culture, and the history of southeastern Korea. It is not one of Busan's headline sights, but it gives useful local context if you are already in the northern part of the city.

The strongest reason to visit is the regional material: artifacts and displays that connect Busan and the surrounding area to older settlement patterns and everyday culture.
