Dongnae Eupseong History Plaza is an open heritage space built around remains of the old Dongnae fortress. Restored walls, signs, and nearby historic traces explain how this part of Busan was defended during the Joseon period.

It is a good stop if you are exploring northern Busan and want history beyond the beaches and shopping districts. The site is outdoors, so it works best in decent weather.
