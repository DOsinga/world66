Dongnae Hyanggyo is a Confucian academy from the Joseon period. It was once used for classical education and ceremonial rites, and its traditional buildings give a compact look at the role these academies played in Korean local life.

Come for the architecture and the calm setting rather than a large museum experience. It pairs well with other historic stops in Dongnae.
