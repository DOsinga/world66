The Dongnae Traditional Archery Experience gives visitors a hands-on introduction to Korean archery, a practice tied to local martial and Confucian traditions.

It is best treated as a cultural activity rather than a major sight. Pair it with Dongnae's fortress and history stops if you are exploring northern Busan.
