Gadeokdo Fortress is a fragment of late-16th-century military history on Gadeok Island, built in the Japanese style during a period when the approaches to Busan were strategically vital. The surviving stonework is modest, but the setting helps explain why this island mattered.

The site is best for travellers already exploring Gadeokdo, where the reward is as much the island view as the ruin itself.
