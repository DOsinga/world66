Geumgang Botanical Garden sits on the lower slopes of Geumjeongsan and collects Korean and foreign plants in a quieter northern corner of Busan. It is a good fit if you want a softer outdoor stop before or after Beomeosa Temple.

The garden is best treated as a short nature break rather than a major expedition. Spring and early summer bring the most colour.
