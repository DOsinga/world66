Geumjeong Mountain Forest Culture Center introduces the natural environment and forest culture of Geumjeongsan. Exhibits and programs focus on local ecology, conservation, and the mountain landscape north of Busan.

It is most useful as a companion stop before or after walking the nearby trails or visiting Beomeosa Temple.
