Gwangan Grand Bridge sweeps across the water between Haeundae and Suyeong and is one of Busan's most recognisable modern landmarks. It is especially good after dark, when the bridge lights reflect across Gwangalli Beach.

You see it best from the shore rather than from a moving bus or taxi. The beachside view gives the bridge its scale, with the city rising behind it and the bay open in front.
