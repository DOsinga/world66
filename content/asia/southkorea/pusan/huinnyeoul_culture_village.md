Huinnyeoul Culture Village is a cliffside neighborhood on Yeongdo with sea views, small galleries, cafes, and a coastal walking path. Its mix of narrow lanes and open water makes it feel different from Busan's bigger market and beach areas.

Come for a slow walk rather than a single monument. It pairs well with the western Busan sights around Jagalchi, Gukje Market, and Gamcheon Culture Village, but it has a quieter, more coastal feel.
