Hurshimchung Spa is a large public bath and jjimjilbang complex in Busan, with hot, tepid and cold baths, saunas, pools, rest areas, and an outdoor section. It is one of the city's better-known places to experience Korean bathing culture at full scale.

The baths are gender-segregated, while the robe-only resting area is shared. Weekends can be crowded, so go at quieter times if you want a more relaxed visit.
