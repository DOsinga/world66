The Hwamyeong Ecological Park Riverside Trail follows the Nakdong River through wetlands, bird habitat, and open riverside landscapes in northern Busan. It is a good low-key walk when you want green space away from the beaches and downtown markets.

The trail is most rewarding in clear weather or during migration seasons, when the river flats and reed beds have more birdlife.
