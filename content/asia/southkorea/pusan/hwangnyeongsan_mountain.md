Hwangnyeongsan Mountain gives one of Busan's best broad city views, taking in Gwangalli Beach, Gwangan Bridge, the dense urban bowl, and the coast beyond. The summit area has an observation deck and a beacon fire station, with the night view the main draw.

It is a good choice when you want Busan as a panorama rather than a street-level city. Go in clear weather, and bring an extra layer if you are heading up for sunset or after dark.
