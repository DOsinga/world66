Jangsan Mountain rises behind Haeundae and is one of Busan's most convenient half-day hikes. The climb gives wide views over the city, the coast, and on very clear days across the water toward Japan.

The round trip to the summit and back usually takes three to four hours. Trails can be confusing, so start with a good map and pay attention to signs and fences around the military areas near the top.
