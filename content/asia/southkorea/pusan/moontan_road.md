MoonTan Road is a forested coastal walk on Dalmaji Hill above Haeundae. The path gives you sea views, shade, and a slower alternative to the beach and high-rise bustle below.

It is especially pleasant in the evening, when the route is lit and the lookout points open toward the water. Pair it with Haeundae Beach or Haedong Yonggungsa Temple if you are spending a day on Busan's eastern coast.
