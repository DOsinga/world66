The Nakdong River Estuary Migratory Bird Sanctuary protects the broad delta where the Nakdong River reaches the South Sea. Its sandbars, reed beds, and small islands attract migratory birds, making it one of Busan's best nature stops and a contrast to the city's beaches and markets.
