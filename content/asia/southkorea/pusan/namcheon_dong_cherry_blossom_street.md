Namcheon-dong Cherry Blossom Street is one of Busan's better spring walks, when the roadside trees form a pale pink canopy near Gwangalli. It is a simple pleasure rather than a formal attraction, but in bloom it gives the city a softer seasonal scene away from the beach crowds.

Come during cherry blossom season, usually around late March or early April. Outside that short window, treat it as a pleasant neighbourhood street rather than a destination in itself.
