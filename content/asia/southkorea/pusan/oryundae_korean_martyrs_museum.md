Oryundae Korean Martyrs Museum is a small Roman Catholic museum in northern Busan. It focuses on eight Korean Christian martyrs killed in 1868, with displays on local Catholic history and persecution.

It is a specialist stop rather than a headline Busan sight, but worthwhile if you are interested in religious history or already visiting the northern side of the city.
