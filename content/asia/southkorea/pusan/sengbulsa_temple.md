Sengbulsa Temple is a Buddhist temple and monastery on Jangsan above Haeundae. Its hillside position gives it a quieter atmosphere than the beach below, with views back over the dense eastern side of Busan.

Visit as part of a Jangsan walk or when you want a temple stop away from the city's better-known coastal and market sights. The setting is the reason to go: mountain air, city views, and a pause from Haeundae's crowds.
