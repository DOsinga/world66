Seokbulsa Temple is one of Busan's most rewarding overlooked sights, reached by a steep hike from the Geumjeongsan cable car area. The temple is known for large Buddhist figures carved directly into the rock face, with the city spread below.

It takes more effort than Busan's easier temples, but that effort keeps it peaceful. Wear decent shoes and allow time for the climb, especially in warm weather.
