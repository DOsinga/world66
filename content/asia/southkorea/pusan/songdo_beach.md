Songdo Beach is a small, older seaside strip south of central Busan, known as Korea's first official public beach. It is quieter than Haeundae or Gwangalli, with a short sandy shore, low-key cafes and restaurants, and easy access from the Jagalchi area by bus. Come for a calmer waterfront walk rather than a big resort-beach scene.
