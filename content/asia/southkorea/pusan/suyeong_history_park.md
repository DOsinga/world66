Suyeong History Park preserves remains of the former Suyeong-eupseong fortress from the Joseon Dynasty. Informational signs explain the site's defensive role and local history.

It is a modest stop rather than a headline Busan attraction, but useful for visitors already in Suyeong who want a small historic site between the beach, river, and Centum City areas.
