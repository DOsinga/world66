Taejongdae Park is one of Busan's classic sea views, set on the cliffs at the southern end of Yeongdo. Paths lead through pine woods to rocky lookouts, a white lighthouse, and views across the water toward the Oryukdo islets and, on very clear days, Tsushima.

The walk from the entrance to the lighthouse is longer than it first looks, but a small paid trolley loops around the park. Come for sea air, cliff scenery, and a quieter coastal mood than Haeundae or Gwangalli.
