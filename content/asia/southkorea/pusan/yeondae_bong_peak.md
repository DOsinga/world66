Yeondae-bong Peak is the high point of Gadeok Island, reached by a two- to three-hour hike with open views over the sea and the Busan-Geoje Fixed Link. It is a good choice when you want Busan's coastal scenery without staying on the crowded city beaches.

The walk is more about the outlook than any single monument, so pick a clear day and bring water.
