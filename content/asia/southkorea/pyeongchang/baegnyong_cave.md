Baegnyong Cave is a limestone cave in the mountains of Pyeongchang, known for its protected formations and guided underground route. Visits are controlled to protect the cave environment, so check access and reservation rules before making the trip.

It is most worthwhile for travellers with a car who are already exploring Pyeongchang's rural and mountain sights.
