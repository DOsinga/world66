Lee Seung-bok Memorial Hall remembers Lee Seung-bok, a South Korean boy killed by North Korean commandos in 1968. The site was established in 1982 and includes a memorial statue, exhibits, and a reconstruction of the story that became part of South Korea's Cold War memory.

It is a sober stop rather than a broad museum, but it gives useful context for visitors exploring the Daegwallyeong and Pyeongchang area.
