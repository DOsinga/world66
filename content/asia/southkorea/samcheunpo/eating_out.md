---
title: "Eating Out"
type: section
---

The harbour is the food destination. Stalls and small restaurants along the seafront sell the day's catch as raw *hoe* (Korean sashimi), grilled mackerel, spicy fish stews, and *jangeo gui* (grilled eel) for which the south coast is famous. Prices stay reasonable away from the obvious tourist spots — pick the places where the fishermen themselves are eating.
