Baekdamsa is the most remote major temple in Seoraksan, reached via the Naesorak valley from the western side of the park. The approach is through a long, narrow gorge following a river over smooth granite, past emerald pools (the "hundred pools" of the name) and waterfalls. The walk from the Naesorak bus terminal to the temple takes about two hours and is among the most beautiful valley walks in Korea.

The temple itself is large and historic, with several main halls set against the forested mountain walls. Unlike the busier Oesorak side, Baekdamsa sees far fewer visitors and has a quieter, more contemplative atmosphere. A stay at the temple's templestay programme allows visitors to experience monastic routines — early morning ceremonies, meditation, and communal meals. The valley continues above the temple to the base of Daecheongbong peak, Seoraksan's highest summit.
