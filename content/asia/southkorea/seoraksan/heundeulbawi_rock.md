Heundeulbawi is a famous rocking stone on the route toward Ulsanbawi in Seoraksan National Park. The rounded boulder rests on a larger rock and moves slightly when pushed, which has made it one of the park's best-known small sights.

It is not a destination on its own, but it is a memorable stop if you are hiking this side of the park.
