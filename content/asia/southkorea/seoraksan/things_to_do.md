---
title: Things to Do
type: section
---

The dominant activity in Seoraksan is hiking, and the range of routes caters to everyone from casual walkers to serious climbers. [Ulsanbawi](/asia/southkorea/seoraksan/ulsanbawi) is the signature hike — a two-hour climb on iron ladders and stone steps to a dramatic six-peaked granite formation. The [Cheonbuldong valley](/asia/southkorea/seoraksan/biryong_falls) trail is the most popular corridor, passing Biryong Falls and the immense [Towangseong Waterfall](/asia/southkorea/seoraksan/towangseong_waterfall) in a three-hour return walk.

The [Gwongeumseong cable car](/asia/southkorea/seoraksan/gwongeumseong) offers quick access to elevated views and the ancient fortress ruins. [Baekdamsa temple](/asia/southkorea/seoraksan/baekdamsa) in the quieter Naesorak valley is the most rewarding longer excursion. [Gongnyong Ridge](/asia/southkorea/seoraksan/gongnyong_ridge) is the most technically demanding maintained trail, with chains and ladders across a narrow granite arête.

Autumn (October) is peak season for foliage colours and the park is very crowded. For solitude, come in late spring or early summer.
