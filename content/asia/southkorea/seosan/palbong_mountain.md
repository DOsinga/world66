Palbong Mountain is a low but rugged hiking target north of central Seosan. Its name refers to the eight rocky peaks that define the ridge, and the trail uses stairs and ropes where the rockier sections need help.

At 362 metres it is not a long mountain day, but the shape of the ridge and the local legends around its peaks make it more memorable than its height suggests.
