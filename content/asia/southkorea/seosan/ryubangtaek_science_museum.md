Ryubangtaek Science Museum commemorates Ryu Bang-taek, the Seosan-born astronomer associated with a Joseon-era map of the sky.

The museum is a useful stop for visitors interested in Korean science history, astronomy, or local culture. It is also linked with the Ryu Bang-taek Star Festival, usually held in May.
