Yonghyeon Natural Recreation Forest sits in the valleys below Gayasan, with oak woods, clear streams, and walking routes that link into the Seosan Arame-gil trail network. It works well with nearby Gaesimsa Temple and the Rock-carved Buddha Triad if you want a quieter mountain day around Seosan.
