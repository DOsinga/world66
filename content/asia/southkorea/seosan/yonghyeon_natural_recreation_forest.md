Yonghyeon Natural Recreation Forest sits in a valley below the ridges of Gayasan, Seosan's highest mountain area. Clear water, oak forest, hiking paths, and ridge walks make it a good outdoor stop, especially when combined with nearby heritage sites such as Gaesimsa Temple and the Rock-carved Buddha Triad.
