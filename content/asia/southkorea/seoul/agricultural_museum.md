Seoul's Agricultural Museum covers Korean farming, rural tools, and traditional agricultural culture through small exhibitions and historic objects. It is a specialist museum rather than a major city sight, but it gives useful context for how food and rural work shaped daily life.

Its central location makes it easy to combine with Deoksugung, Gyeonghuigung, or Seodaemun Prison History Hall. It is best for travellers who like social history and small museums with a narrow focus.
