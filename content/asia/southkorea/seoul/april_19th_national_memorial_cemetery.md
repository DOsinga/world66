April 19th National Memorial Cemetery commemorates the 1960 April 19 Revolution, a student-led uprising that became a key moment in South Korea's modern democracy movement. More than two hundred people killed during the protests are buried here.

The cemetery is a quiet, solemn stop in northern Seoul. It is most meaningful if you have an interest in modern Korean history or want to understand the city's democracy memorials beyond the central museums.
