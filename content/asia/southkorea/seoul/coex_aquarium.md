COEX Aquarium is a large indoor aquarium inside the COEX complex in Gangnam. It has a broad mix of tanks and themed displays, making it an easy family stop when the weather is poor.

It pairs naturally with Bongeunsa Temple, Starfield Library, and the other COEX-area sights.
