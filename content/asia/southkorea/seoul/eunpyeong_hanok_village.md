Eunpyeong Hanok Village is a modern hanok district on Seoul's northwestern edge, with tiled roofs set below the ridges of Bukhansan. It is quieter and more residential than Bukchon, which is part of the appeal.

Come for a slow walk, mountain views, and the contrast between traditional-looking houses and the high-rise city beyond. Keep the visit respectful: this is a lived-in neighbourhood, not a stage set.
