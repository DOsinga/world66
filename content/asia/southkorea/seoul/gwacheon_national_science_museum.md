Gwacheon National Science Museum is a large, family-friendly science museum beside the Seoul Grand Park area. Opened in 2008, it has hands-on exhibitions, children's galleries, outdoor displays, and a planetarium-style science focus that makes it a good bad-weather stop or an easy add-on to a day around the park and zoo.
