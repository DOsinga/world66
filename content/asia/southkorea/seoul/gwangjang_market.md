Seoul's oldest permanent market, established in 1905, covering several city blocks between Jongno. The food alley is the reason to come: rows of haenyeo grandmothers frying bindaetteok (mung bean pancakes), stalls serving yukhoe (raw beef with sesame and pear), kimbap, and mayak gimbap. The market is also a fabric and textile district — five floors of bolts of silk, linen, and hanbok materials. Best visited at lunch on a weekday when the food stalls are in full swing.
