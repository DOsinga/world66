Jongmyo Shrine was the royal ancestral shrine of the Joseon dynasty, where kings and queens were honoured through formal Confucian rites. Its long, restrained halls and wooded grounds feel quieter and more solemn than Seoul's palace compounds.

It is one of Seoul's essential historic sites, especially for visitors interested in royal ritual, architecture, and the Confucian side of Korean state culture.
