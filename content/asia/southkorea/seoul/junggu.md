title: Jung-gu (Downtown Seoul)
type: neighbourhood
---

Jung-gu is Seoul's historic downtown district, containing the city's oldest commercial streets, the major palaces on its western edge, and the dense shopping and dining concentration of Myeongdong. This is where the city was founded under the Joseon dynasty, and despite intensive redevelopment, it retains a more compact, walkable character than most of Seoul.

The district is bounded roughly by Gyeongbokgung Palace to the northwest and the Cheonggyecheon Stream to the north. Deoksugung Palace sits on its western edge; Namdaemun Market occupies its southern portion. Myeongdong, in the centre of the district, is the most internationally recognisable part of Seoul and its most tourist-oriented shopping street. The older commercial streets around Euljiro are gradually becoming more interesting as independent cafes and workshops move in.
