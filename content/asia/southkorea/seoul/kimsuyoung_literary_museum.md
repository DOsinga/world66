Kimsuyoung Literary Museum honors the Korean poet Kim Soo-young in Dobong-gu, where he lived and worked. Exhibits include manuscripts, poetry collections, translations, a bust, and a reconstructed study. It is a focused stop for visitors interested in modern Korean literature or exploring northern Seoul beyond the main palace districts.
