Konkuk University Museum holds Korean historical artifacts, with the Donggukjeongun as its headline item. This 15th-century set of books explains the pronunciation of Chinese characters and is an important early work in Korean linguistic history. The museum is a focused stop for visitors interested in manuscripts, scholarship, and Korean cultural history.
