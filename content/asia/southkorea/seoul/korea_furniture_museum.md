Korea Furniture Museum displays traditional Korean furniture in a group of preserved hanok houses. The collection includes thousands of pieces, but the setting is just as important: rooms, courtyards, and timber architecture show how furniture was used in daily life.

Visits are by guided tour and usually need to be booked in advance. It is a good choice if you have already seen the main palaces and want a quieter look at Korean domestic design.
