Korean Film Museum traces the history of Korean cinema through a permanent exhibition and rotating themed shows. The permanent galleries move through Korean film chronologically, with displays on major periods, filmmakers, and production culture.

It is a useful specialist stop for film fans, especially if you are already exploring western Seoul. Temporary exhibitions add a reason to check what is on before making the trip.
