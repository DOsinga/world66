The Korean Stone Museum is devoted to Korean stone artifacts, sculpture, and historical objects. Its collection is niche, but it gives stone craft and symbolic forms more attention than they usually receive in Seoul's larger museums.

It is best for visitors with a specific interest in Korean material culture, sculpture, or quieter museums away from the main palace-and-market route.
