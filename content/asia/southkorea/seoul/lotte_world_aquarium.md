Lotte World Aquarium is part of the Lotte World Mall complex in Jamsil, beside Lotte World Tower. The exhibits recreate marine habitats from Korea and the wider world, with large tanks, walk-through viewing areas, and family-friendly displays about ocean life.

It works well paired with the tower or mall on a wet day, and is one of Seoul's easiest major indoor attractions for families.
