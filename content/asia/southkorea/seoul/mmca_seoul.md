MMCA Seoul is the central Seoul branch of the National Museum of Modern and Contemporary Art, Korea, set close to Gyeongbokgung. It occupies a site once used by military institutions and now combines exhibition halls, education spaces, a digital lab, a theatre, and a contemporary building that has won Korean architecture awards.
