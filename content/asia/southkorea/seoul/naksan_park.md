Naksan Park follows the ridge between Dongdaemun and Hyehwa-dong. Its paths connect with stretches of Seoul's old city wall and climb high enough for good views over the dense central districts.

It is especially worthwhile near sunset or after dark, when the city lights make the short climb feel more rewarding.
