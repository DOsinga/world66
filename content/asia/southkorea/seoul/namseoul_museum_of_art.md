Nam-Seoul Museum of Art is a branch of Seoul Museum of Art in a restored Japanese colonial-era building. Exhibitions change regularly and are often free, making it an easy cultural stop if you are in the southern part of the city.

The building gives the museum extra interest: it is not just a white-box gallery, but an adapted piece of Seoul's early-20th-century urban fabric.
