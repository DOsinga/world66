The National Museum of Buddhism is a small museum in central Seoul focused on Korean Buddhist culture. It is most useful for visitors who want context before or after seeing the city's temples and palace district.

The exhibitions change, but the museum usually presents religious art, ritual culture, and education programs tied to Korean Buddhism.
