The wholesale seafood market on the south bank of the Han River, operating around the clock. The auction starts at 3am, when the night's catch comes in and restaurants bid on it wholesale — arriving at 4am to watch is one of the more memorable things to do in Seoul. Pick your seafood from the tanks on the market floor, take it upstairs to one of the restaurants, and they'll prepare it however you like. The sashimi (hoe in Korean) is excellent and cheap by any standard.
