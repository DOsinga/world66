Olympic Park was built for the 1986 Asian Games and the 1988 Seoul Olympics, but it works just as well as a city park. Lakes, lawns, broad plazas, and outdoor sculptures give it room to breathe, and locals come here for walks, photos, concerts, and weekend wandering.
