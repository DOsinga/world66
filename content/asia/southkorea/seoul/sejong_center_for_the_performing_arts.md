Sejong Center for the Performing Arts is a major Seoul cultural venue on Sejong-daero, close to Gwanghwamun Square. Its halls host classical concerts, theatre, dance, Korean traditional performance, and large touring productions.

The building works best as part of a central Seoul day: palaces and civic landmarks outside, then a performance in the evening. Check the programme ahead, since the value of a visit depends almost entirely on what is on.
