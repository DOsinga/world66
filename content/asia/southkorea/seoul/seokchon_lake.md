Seokchon Lake is a pair of lakes wrapped by walking paths below the towers of Jamsil. The setting makes it a useful break from the surrounding malls and skyscrapers, with playgrounds, lawns, and seasonal cherry blossoms along the water.

It pairs naturally with Lotte World Tower or the Lotte World area, especially for a short evening walk.
