Seoul Anglican Cathedral is the central church of the Diocese of Seoul, close to Deoksugung and City Hall. Built from the 1920s, it blends neo-Romanesque church forms with Korean architectural details, giving the quiet compound a different feel from the larger royal and civic sights nearby.
