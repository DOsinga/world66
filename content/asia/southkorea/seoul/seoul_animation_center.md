Seoul Animation Center is a family-friendly comics and animation space near Myeongdong. It has a large comics reading area and paid activity zones where children can meet popular Korean characters such as Pororo and Tayo.

It is most useful for families or animation fans, especially on a rainy day when central Seoul's outdoor sights are less appealing.
