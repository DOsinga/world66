Seoul Baekje Museum tells the story of Hanseong Baekje, the early Baekje kingdom centred around what is now southeastern Seoul. The building's low, sloping form echoes the nearby Mongchontoseong earthworks in Olympic Park.

Inside, the museum uses archaeological finds, models, and reconstructed scenes to explain the Baekje capital, its river trade, and its links with China and Japan. It is a strong stop if you are visiting Olympic Park or Lotte World Tower nearby.
