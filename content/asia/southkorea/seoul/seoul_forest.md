Seoul Forest is the green lung of Seongsu, a former royal hunting ground and water-treatment site reborn as one of the city's largest parks. It rolls down toward the Han River through lawns, wetlands, and groves, with a deer enclosure, a butterfly conservatory, and a forested ecological zone that feels genuinely wild for a park this central.

It is the natural counterweight to Seongsu's warehouse-cafe bustle — families, joggers, and couples spread out across the grass on weekends, and the riverside paths connect through to the wider Han River park system. Come on foot from the Seongsu cafe streets and treat it as the place to slow down after a morning of coffee and concept stores.
