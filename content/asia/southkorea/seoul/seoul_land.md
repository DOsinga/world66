Seoul Land is a family amusement park beside Seoul Grand Park in Gwacheon, south of the city centre. Opened before the 1988 Olympics, it has rides, seasonal decorations, and enough easy entertainment to make sense for families already heading to the zoo or park complex.

It is simple to reach by subway, though it is a day-out destination rather than a quick central Seoul stop.
