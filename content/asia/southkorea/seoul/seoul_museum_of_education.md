The Seoul Museum of Education traces the history of schools, curriculum, textbooks, and education policy in the city. Its displays use photographs and school-related objects to show how formal education changed through modern Korean history.

The museum is small, but its location near Bukchon and Gyeongbokgung makes it an easy specialist stop if you are interested in everyday social history.
