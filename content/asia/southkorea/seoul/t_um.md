T.um is SK Telecom's technology museum in central Seoul, with changing exhibits on communications, digital services, and future technology. It is a polished, hands-on stop for visitors interested in South Korea's tech industry.

Visits usually require advance online reservation, and exhibits change as the technology being shown changes. It is best treated as a planned short museum visit rather than a walk-in attraction.
