The Traditional Crafts Experience Center sits in Bukchon Hanok Village, where the old wooden houses already put you in the right mood for handwork. It runs simple drop-in programs where visitors can try Korean paper crafts, patterned accessories, and other traditional techniques.

This is most useful if you want a hands-on stop between palaces and hanok lanes. The crafts are approachable rather than museum-like, so it works especially well with families or anyone who prefers making something to just looking.
