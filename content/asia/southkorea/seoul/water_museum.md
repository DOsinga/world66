The Water Museum explains the history and technology of Seoul's water supply through exhibitions and hands-on displays. It is a niche museum, but the subject is more engaging than it sounds, especially for families and visitors interested in urban infrastructure.

It sits near Seoul Forest and Seongsu, so it is easiest to visit as part of a wider walk through the area.
