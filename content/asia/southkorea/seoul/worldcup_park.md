World Cup Park is a large west Seoul park complex created from former landfill land near the World Cup Stadium. Haneul Park, the best-known section, is popular for its high grasslands, city views, and silver grass in autumn.

It is spacious and less central than Seoul's palace-and-market sights, but rewarding when you want open air on the Mapo side of the city.
