Yangcheon Archaeological Site preserves the stone remains of a fortress overlooking the Han River. The site was one of the defensive points guarding the river approach to Seoul, and its walls still give a clear sense of why this high ground mattered.

It is more of a quiet history stop than a headline attraction, but it rewards travelers interested in Seoul before the modern city filled the river plain.
