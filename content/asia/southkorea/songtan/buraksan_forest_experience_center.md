Buraksan Forest Experience Center sits at the foot of Buraksan Mountain, with easy access to wooded trails and a quieter side of Pyeongtaek away from the urban blocks around Songtan. It is a modest stop, but useful if you want a short nature break or a simple hike without leaving the area.
