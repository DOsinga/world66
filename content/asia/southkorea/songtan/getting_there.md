---
title: "Getting There"
type: section
---

Songtan is easily reached from [Seoul](/asia/southkorea/seoul) by train on Seoul Subway Line 1 (about 60–75 minutes from Seoul Station to Songtan Station, depending on the service). The station is a short walk from the Shin-Jang district. KTX high-speed trains stop at Pyeongtaek Station nearby, making Songtan a viable stop between Seoul and southern cities.

By car, take the Gyeongbu Expressway south from Seoul — about an hour in normal traffic. From Incheon Airport, a bus or airport limousine service runs to Pyeongtaek, with connections to Songtan.
