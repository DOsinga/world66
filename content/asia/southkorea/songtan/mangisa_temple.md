Mangisa Temple is a quiet Buddhist temple near Songtan, set among wooded paths with a pagoda and traditional temple buildings. It is a calm contrast to the busy shopping streets around the station area.

Come for a short walk, the temple atmosphere, and views of the surrounding greenery. It is best treated as a low-key cultural stop rather than a large temple complex.
