---
title: "Shopping"
type: section
---

The Shin-Jang district is the main shopping area, with a concentration of tailors, leather goods shops, and clothing stores. Custom-made suits, jackets, and leather items are the big draw — turnaround times are fast (suits can often be done in 24–48 hours) and prices are competitive. Quality varies, so look at sample work before committing.

The district also has a range of imported goods, Korean street food stalls, and small restaurants. It has a distinctly international character, reflecting the long-term American military presence at nearby Osan Air Base.
