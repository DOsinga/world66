Suanbo is a small hot-spring town in the mountains of North Chungcheong, close to [Jecheon](/asia/southkorea/jecheon), Chungju, and Woraksan National Park. It is not a big resort city. The mood is quieter: bathhouses, simple restaurants, forested ridges, and buses heading toward trailheads.

The draw is the combination. [Suanbo Hot Springs](/asia/southkorea/suanbo/suanbo_hot_springs) gives the town its identity, with mineral water that has been part of the region's travel culture for centuries. Nearby [Woraksan National Park](/asia/southkorea/suanbo/woraksan_national_park) supplies the hard mountain day, while [Mungyeongsaejae Provincial Park](/asia/southkorea/suanbo/mungyeongsaejae_provincial_park) adds old gates, wooded paths, and a slice of Joseon road history.

Suanbo works best if you like a practical base more than a polished showpiece. Soak in the morning or evening, spend the main part of the day in the hills, and eat simply. It is a good one-day detour from central Korea, especially if you want a softer landing than a full mountain-town expedition.
