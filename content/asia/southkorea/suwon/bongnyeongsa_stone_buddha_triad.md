The Stone Buddha Triad at Bongnyeongsa Temple is a quieter heritage stop than Suwon's fortress walls, but it gives the city a useful Buddhist counterpoint. The carved triad is valued as a cultural property, and the temple setting makes the visit feel devotional rather than merely archaeological.

Come with modest expectations and a respectful manner: this is an active religious place, not an open-air sculpture park. It fits best into a broader Suwon day if you have already seen Hwaseong and want something smaller, older-feeling, and less crowded.
