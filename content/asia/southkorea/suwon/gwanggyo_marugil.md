Gwanggyo Marugil is an easy loop trail around Gwanggyo Reservoir, mixing wooden decks with forest paths. The walk is about 3.4 km and is at its best in cherry blossom season, autumn colour, or fresh winter snow, though unpaved sections can be slippery after rain.
