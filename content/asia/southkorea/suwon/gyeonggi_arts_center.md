Gyeonggi Arts Center is Suwon's main large-scale arts venue, hosting concerts, dance, theatre and touring productions. It gives the city a cultural anchor beyond Hwaseong Fortress and the old royal sites.

For travellers, the centre matters most when a performance lines up with your visit. Check the programme in advance; an evening here can show a contemporary, local side of Suwon that daytime sightseeing does not capture.
