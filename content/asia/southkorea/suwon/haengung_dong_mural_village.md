Haengung-dong Mural Village spreads through the residential lanes near Hwaseong Fortress, where house walls and alley corners have been painted with murals. The project began as a neighbourhood renewal effort, and the result is a low-key walk that pairs well with Hwaseong Haenggung and the fortress circuit.

The appeal is in wandering rather than chasing one single artwork. Go slowly, keep noise down around homes, and treat it as a lived-in neighbourhood with a bright layer of public art.
