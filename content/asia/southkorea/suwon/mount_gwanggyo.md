Mount Gwanggyo is Suwon's main mountain escape, a forested ridge north of the city with trails that local hikers use throughout the year. It gives the city a natural counterpoint to Hwaseong Fortress, museums, and dense apartment districts.

The climb is not technical, but it is a real hike rather than a city stroll. Bring water, expect company on weekends, and use the summit or ridge viewpoints to see how Suwon fits into the wider Seoul-area landscape.
