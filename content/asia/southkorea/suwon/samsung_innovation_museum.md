Samsung Innovation Museum is a polished technology museum in Suwon, the city most closely tied to Samsung's rise. The displays place the company inside a broader history of electricity, telecommunications, semiconductors, screens, and consumer electronics.

It is corporate, but still useful if you are interested in how South Korea became a technology power. Visits may require advance booking or fixed hours, so treat it as a planned stop rather than something to wander into after Hwaseong Fortress.
