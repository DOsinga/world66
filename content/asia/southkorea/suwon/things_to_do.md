---
title: Things to Do
type: section
---

The [Hwaseong Fortress](/asia/southkorea/suwon/hwaseong_fortress) walls are the dominant attraction — a two-hour walk circuits the nearly 6-kilometre perimeter, passing watchtowers, command posts, and the major gates. Start at the Paldalmun (South Gate) near the city centre and walk clockwise. The [Hwaseong Haenggung](/asia/southkorea/suwon/hwaseong_haenggung) palace complex inside the walls adds another hour.

The old market street near the fortress (Paldalmun Market) is one of the better traditional markets in the Seoul metropolitan area — best visited in the morning when the food stalls are in full operation. For those who want to end with something odd, [Mr Toilet House](/asia/southkorea/suwon/mr_toilet_house) is a genuine curiosity about 3 kilometres from the fortress.
