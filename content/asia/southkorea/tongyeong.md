*Asia > South Korea > Tongyeong*

Tongyeong is a handsome south-coast port wrapped around a harbour and scattered islands. It is closely tied to Admiral Yi Sun-sin, whose Joseon naval command was based here during the Japanese invasions of Korea, but it is not just a history stop. The city has steep mural lanes, fish markets, cable-car views, and ferries heading into Hallyeohaesang National Marine Park.

The best day starts around Gangguan Harbour, where fishing boats, the turtle ship replica, [Dongpirang Mural Village](/asia/southkorea/tongyeong/dongpirang_mural_village), and old naval sites sit close together. From there, climb or ride up to [Mireuksan](/asia/southkorea/tongyeong/tongyeong_cable_car) for the classic panorama: islands, bridges, oyster farms, and the broken blue line of the Hallyeo waterway.

Tongyeong is worth more than a transit stop between Busan and Geoje. Come for a full day if you can, eat seafood near the harbour, and leave time for a slow walk through [Yi Sun-sin Park](/asia/southkorea/tongyeong/yi_sun_sin_park), where the city opens out to the sea that made its name.
