Daewangam Songnim is the pine-forest side of Daewangam Park, one of Ulsan's most appealing coastal landscapes. The paths move through wind-shaped pines before opening to sea views, bridges, and the rocky headland associated with Daewangam's local legends.

It is a good antidote to Ulsan's industrial image: accessible, scenic, and easy to combine with the lighthouse and coastal walks around the park. Come outside the hottest part of the day if you want to linger, as the route is more pleasant when the sea breeze is working in your favour.
