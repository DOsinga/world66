Ulsan's position between Gyeongju to the north and Busan to the south makes it an excellent base for day trips. Head south to Busan for Korea's most famous beach at [Haeundae](/asia/southkorea/ulsan/haeundae), big-city nightlife, and the country's largest aquarium. Head north to Gyeongju, the ancient Silla capital, for UNESCO-listed [Bulguksa Temple](/asia/southkorea/ulsan/bulguksa_temple) and [Seokguram Grotto](/asia/southkorea/ulsan/seokguram_grotto), burial mounds, and mountain hiking. [Pohang](/asia/southkorea/pohang), two hours north, has a walkable downtown, the Jukdo fish market, and ferries to Ulleungdo island. West of the city, [Tongdo Aqua Fantasia](/asia/southkorea/ulsan/aqua_fantasia) water park offers family-friendly fun in a mountain setting.
