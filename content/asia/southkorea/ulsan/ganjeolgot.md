Ganjeolgot is a headland on Ulsan's coast where the sunrise arrives early and dramatically over the East Sea. The cape has become a popular New Year's destination, but it is pleasant at quieter times too, with sea air, open paths, and broad horizons.

The appeal is simple and weather-dependent: come before dawn if you want the full ritual, or later for an easier coastal walk. It is one of Ulsan's clearest reminders that the city is more than factories and shipyards.
