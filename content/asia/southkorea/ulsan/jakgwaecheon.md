Jakgwaecheon is a rocky stream shaped by years of water erosion, with carved stone surfaces and Korean inscriptions beside the water. It is a quiet scenic stop near a temple, best suited to visitors who want a short walk and a local landscape outside Ulsan's industrial centre.
