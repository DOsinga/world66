title: 'Things to Do'
type: section
---

Ulsan spreads its activities across widely separated districts, so plan around where you are staying. The [Bangudae Petroglyphs](/asia/southkorea/ulsan/bangudae_petroglyphs) are the city's most significant historical sight — 6,000-year-old Bronze Age rock carvings, among the oldest whale hunting images in the world. The [Ulsan Museum](/asia/southkorea/ulsan/ulsan_museum) covers the full arc from those prehistoric origins to the modern industrial city, and sits beside [Ulsan Grand Park](/asia/southkorea/ulsan/ulsan_grand_park), the largest urban park in Korea, with cycling, a butterfly house, and a petting zoo.

The [Taehwa River Eco Park](/asia/southkorea/ulsan/taehwa_river) bamboo grove is unexpectedly beautiful — thousands of bamboo stalks along the riverbank that attract migratory cranes each winter. [Ulgi and Daewangam Park](/asia/southkorea/ulsan/ulgi_daewangam_park) on the coast combines a clifftop walk with views of the [Hyundai shipyard](/asia/southkorea/ulsan/hyundai_heavy_industries). Sports fans can catch Ulsan Hyundai FC at [Munsu Football Stadium](/asia/southkorea/ulsan/munsu_stadium), one of the better K League atmospheres in the country. The [Ulsan Culture and Arts Center](/asia/southkorea/ulsan/ulsan_culture_arts_center) hosts touring theatre and opera. For hikers, the Yeongnam Alps offer Mt. Gajisan (1,240m), Mt. Sinbulsan, and Paraeso Waterfall, most easily reached by car or taxi.
