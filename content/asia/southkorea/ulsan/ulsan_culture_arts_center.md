The Ulsan Culture and Arts Center is the city's principal venue for performing arts, hosting a programme that includes touring theatre companies, orchestral concerts, opera, and dance. It sits in Dal-dong, easily reached from Samsandong or central Ulsan. The building is a confident piece of civic architecture — rare in a city that tends to prioritise function over form.

The centre has a main hall seating over 1,300 and a smaller theatre for more intimate productions. Programming is largely in Korean, but theatrical and musical performances are worth attending even without language comprehension. Ticket prices are reasonable by Korean standards. Check the schedule at the tourist information office or ask at your accommodation — popular productions book out early.
