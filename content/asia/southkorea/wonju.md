*Asia > South Korea > Wonju*

Wonju is an inland Gangwon city with a practical, workaday centre and a surprisingly strong ring of sights around it. It does not have the instant image of Seoul, Busan, or Gyeongju, but it makes a good one-day escape if you want mountains, architecture, and Korean craft in one trip.

The standout is [Museum SAN](/asia/southkorea/wonju/museum_san), Tadao Ando's mountain art complex in Oak Valley, where concrete, water, stone, and contemporary art do most of the talking. On the natural side, [Sogeumsan Grand Valley](/asia/southkorea/wonju/sogeumsan_grand_valley) gives Wonju its headline bridge and cliff-walk scenery.

Wonju's older identity is tied to Chiaksan and hanji, Korea's traditional handmade paper. [Guryongsa Temple](/asia/southkorea/wonju/guryongsa_temple) sits at the foot of the mountain, while [Wonju Hanji Theme Park](/asia/southkorea/wonju/wonju_hanji_theme_park) gives the city a craft story that is more distinctive than another generic provincial museum.
