Heungbeopsaji is a former Buddhist temple site west of Wonju, now valued more for its history than for standing buildings. The site is tied to important pagodas and stone relics, several of which were removed during the Japanese colonial period and are now held by the National Museum of Korea.

It is a quiet stop for travelers interested in Korean Buddhist heritage and the traces left by vanished temples.
