Toji Literature Park is devoted to Park Kyongni, one of Korea's best-known novelists, and to her major work, *Toji*. The title means "the land", and the park uses the book and the writer's life as a way into modern Korean literature.

It is a quiet cultural stop rather than a blockbuster sight, but it is worthwhile if you read Korean literature or want a slower break from Wonju's busier attractions.
