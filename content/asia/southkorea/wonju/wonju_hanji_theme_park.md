Wonju Hanji Theme Park explains the city's paper-making identity. Exhibits cover the history and uses of hanji, while workshops make it more hands-on than a simple display room.

It is a modest stop compared with Museum SAN, but it gives Wonju local texture. Pair it with lunch in town or use it as a rainy-day counterweight to Sogeumsan and Chiaksan.
