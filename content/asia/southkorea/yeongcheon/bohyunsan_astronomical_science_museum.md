Bohyunsan Astronomical Science Museum sits below one of Korea's important mountain observatory sites. The public museum has astronomy exhibits and a planetarium-style dome, while the nearby research observatory works with Korea's large optical telescope.

Check opening days before climbing this far into the hills. The trip makes most sense if you can pair the museum with clear weather or a public observing event.
