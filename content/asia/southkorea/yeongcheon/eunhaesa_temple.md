Eunhaesa Temple is Yeongcheon's strongest sight. The temple traces its foundation to AD 809 in the Silla period and later moved to its present site during the Joseon dynasty. Its compound includes major halls, small hermitages, Buddhist education buildings, and nationally designated treasures.

The setting matters as much as the checklist. Eunhaesa sits on Palgongsan, so the approach feels like a mountain temple visit rather than a quick urban shrine.
