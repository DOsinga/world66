Manbulsa Temple is less old than Eunhaesa but visually memorable in a very different way. Its name means "ten thousand Buddhas," and the complex leans into repetition, scale, and devotional statuary.

It is a good second temple stop if you have a car and want contrast: Eunhaesa is forested and historic; Manbulsa is more direct and theatrical.
