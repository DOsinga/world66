Yeongcheon's travel value sits outside the dense centre. [Eunhaesa Temple](/asia/southkorea/yeongcheon/eunhaesa_temple) is the main anchor, [Manbulsa Temple](/asia/southkorea/yeongcheon/manbulsa_temple) adds a very different Buddhist landscape, and [Bohyunsan Astronomical Science Museum](/asia/southkorea/yeongcheon/bohyunsan_astronomical_science_museum) gives the mountain country a night-sky angle.
