Dolsan Bridge links central Yeosu with Dolsan Island and gives the city one of its signature night views. The bridge itself is best seen after dark, when the harbour lights, seafood streets, and nearby Dolsan Park make the water feel like the centre of town.

It is not a long stop, but it is a good orientation point for Yeosu's geography: town on one side, island road and hermitage country on the other.
