Odongdo is a small island linked to the mainland by a breakwater, easy to fold into a harbour walk from the Expo area. Paths wind through camellias and coastal trees to cliffs, a lighthouse, and views back across the port.

It is most famous in camellia season, but it is useful year-round: a quiet green counterpoint to the roads and fish restaurants around the waterfront.
