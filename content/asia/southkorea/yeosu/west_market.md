West Market, also known as Seo Market, is one of Yeosu's long-running traditional markets. Stalls sell seafood, meat, clothing, and everyday goods, giving the market a more local feel than the waterfront tourist areas.

It is best visited in the morning or around mealtimes, when the food and seafood stalls are most active.
