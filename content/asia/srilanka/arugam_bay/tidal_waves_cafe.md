A laid-back beach shack that stays busy from early morning until after dark, fuelled by surfers looking for breakfast burritos and strong coffee before paddling out, and by the same people returning for kottu roti in the evening. The fruit smoothies and fresh juices are good and cheap. No pretension, plastic chairs, sand on the floor — exactly right for the place.
