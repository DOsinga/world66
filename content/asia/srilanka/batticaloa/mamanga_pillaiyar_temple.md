Mamanga Pillaiyar Temple is one of Batticaloa's better-known Hindu shrines, dedicated to Ganesha and set a little north of the town centre. It is most interesting during the annual July or August pilgrimage, when the temple becomes a focus for local worship and processions.

Outside festival time it is a quieter stop, worth combining with Batticaloa's fort, lagoon edge, and bridges if you want to see more than the colonial sights.
