Mamanga Pillaiyar Temple is a Hindu shrine in Batticaloa with a strong local following. Its busiest period is the July or August pilgrimage season, when the temple becomes a focus for worship and procession.

Outside festival time it is a quieter stop for visitors interested in Batticaloa's Tamil Hindu culture. Dress modestly and be respectful around active worship.
