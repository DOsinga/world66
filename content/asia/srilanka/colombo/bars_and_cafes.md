---
title: "Bars and Cafes"
type: section
---

Colombo has a lively bar and cafe scene, concentrated in Cinnamon Gardens and the new waterfront developments. The rooftop bar has become a Colombo institution — the elevated views over the Indian Ocean and the city skyline are genuinely spectacular, especially at sunset. Lion Lager is the local beer; arrack (distilled from coconut flower toddy) is the spirit of choice.
