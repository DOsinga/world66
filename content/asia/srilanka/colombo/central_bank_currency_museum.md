The Central Bank Currency Museum traces Sri Lanka's money from ancient coins to modern banknotes. The displays are strongest on local coinage, with examples from early kingdoms, colonial issues, and the country's modern currency.

It is a compact stop in the Fort district, best for travellers with an interest in history, design, or the practical details of how Sri Lanka's economy developed.
