Nelum Pokuna Theatre is Colombo's major modern performing arts venue, named for the lotus pond that inspired its form. Its broad, layered exterior stands near the National Museum and Viharamahadevi Park, making it easy to include on a cultural walk through central Colombo.

The building is worth seeing even without a ticket, but it is better when used for its purpose: dance, music, theatre, and official cultural events. Check schedules locally, as programming varies, and pair an evening performance with nearby Cinnamon Gardens rather than treating it as a daytime museum.
