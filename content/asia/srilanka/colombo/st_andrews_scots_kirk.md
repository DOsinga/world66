St Andrew's Scots Kirk is a colonial-era Presbyterian church in central Colombo, built for the Scottish community that once worked in Ceylon's trade and administration. Its restrained architecture and quiet compound offer a pause from the traffic around Galle Face and the fort district.

The church is a small stop, but it adds texture to Colombo's religious map. It sits alongside Anglican, Catholic, Buddhist, Hindu, and Muslim landmarks that together explain the city's layered port history.
