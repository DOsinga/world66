St Michael and All Angels Anglican Church is a late nineteenth-century landmark in Kollupitiya, dedicated in 1887. Its stone, timber, and mortar construction gives it a different feel from Colombo's larger colonial churches and civic buildings.

It is worth a short look if you are already exploring the central lakeside and Kollupitiya area. The appeal is architectural rather than grand: a quiet church building that still anchors its neighbourhood.
