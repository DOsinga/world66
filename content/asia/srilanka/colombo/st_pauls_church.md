St Paul's Church is a 19th-century Colombo church that adds to the city's scattered collection of colonial-era religious buildings. It is quieter than the headline sights, but useful for visitors tracing how different communities shaped the city.

The church is best treated as a short heritage stop if you are already nearby. Its value comes from context and continuity rather than spectacle, especially when paired with Colombo's older Dutch and British landmarks.
