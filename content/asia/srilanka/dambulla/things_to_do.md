title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Dambulla's reason for existing as a tourist stop is the [Dambulla Cave Temple](/asia/srilanka/dambulla/dambulla_cave_temple), which is one of Sri Lanka's supreme sights. Everything else in town is secondary to the caves, though the [Ibbankatuwa Megalithic Tombs](/asia/srilanka/dambulla/ibbankatuwa_tombs) nearby add a less-visited prehistoric dimension, and the [Rangiri Dambulla Cricket Stadium](/asia/srilanka/dambulla/rangiri_cricket_stadium) is a landmark in its own right — one of the most extraordinary cricket grounds in the world.

The town also functions as the gateway to [Sigiriya](/asia/srilanka/sigiriya) (20 km north) and [Polonnaruwa](/asia/srilanka/polonnaruwa) (65 km northeast), and most visitors use it as a base for one or both.
