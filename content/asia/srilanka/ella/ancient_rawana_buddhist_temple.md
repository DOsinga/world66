The Ancient Rawana Buddhist Temple sits near Ella's Ravana Falls area, where cave shrines, hillside paths, and local legend overlap. The site is often linked in popular storytelling to Ravana, though visitors should treat the mythology as part of the living cultural landscape rather than archaeology.

The temple is a short, atmospheric stop, with painted shrine spaces and views of the surrounding hills. It works best combined with Ravana Falls or a drive through the valley, and it asks for the usual temple courtesies: modest clothing, quiet behavior, and shoes off where required.
