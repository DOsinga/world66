Run by a husband-and-wife team out of what is essentially their front room a block off the main drag. The menu is short — three or four curries, kottu, hoppers in the evening — but the cooking is the best home-style Sri Lankan food in town. Prices are roughly half of the main-street places.

Seats are limited and the place fills up; come early or be prepared to share a table. Cash only.
