Flag Rock is the southernmost projection of the fort ramparts, named for the warning flag Dutch sailors flew here to alert incoming ships to the dangerous rocks just below the surface. It is the most popular sunset spot in Galle: locals, tourists, ice cream sellers and the occasional cliff diver all gather here in the late afternoon.

The cliff divers — young men who plunge into the narrow channel between the rocks for a few rupees — perform mostly for the camera crowd, and the practice has a long local history.
