The post office on Church Street is the oldest functioning post office in Sri Lanka, in continuous operation from the Dutch period through to the present. The single-storey colonial building is unassuming, but the dim interior, with its wooden counters and brass fittings, has barely changed in a century.

You can buy stamps and post a letter as in any post office, or visit the small philatelic museum at the back, which displays historic post boxes, telegraph equipment and stamps from Ceylon and Sri Lanka.
