Hikkaduwa National Park protects the shallow coral reef just offshore from town. On a clear day you can see reef fish, turtles, and coral either by snorkelling or from a glass-bottom boat.

This is fragile, heavily visited reef, so choose operators carefully and do not stand on coral. If visibility is poor, skip the trip rather than forcing a disappointing and damaging outing.
