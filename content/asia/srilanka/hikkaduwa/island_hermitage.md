Island Hermitage on Polgasduwa near Dodanduwa is a Buddhist forest monastery with a long international history. It is not a casual beach attraction, and that is precisely why it is interesting.

Access and visitor expectations should be checked carefully before going. If you do visit, keep the mood quiet and remember that this is a living religious place, not a lakeside photo stop.
