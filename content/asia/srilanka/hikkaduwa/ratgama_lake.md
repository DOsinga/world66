Ratgama Lake lies south of Hikkaduwa, a quieter inland water landscape of mangroves, birds, and small settlements. It is a useful escape when the beach strip starts to feel too loud.

Boat trips and short nature outings are the main draw. Go with a local operator who knows the channels rather than trying to improvise from the roadside.
