The Sea Turtle Hatchery & Rescue Center north of Hikkaduwa gives visitors a close look at Sri Lanka's marine turtles, including olive ridley, leatherback, loggerhead, hawksbill, and green turtles. The centre focuses on hatching eggs and caring for injured turtles before release where possible.

Visit with a critical eye and avoid any place that encourages handling wildlife for photos. At its best, the stop works as a short family-friendly introduction to the turtles seen along this coast.
