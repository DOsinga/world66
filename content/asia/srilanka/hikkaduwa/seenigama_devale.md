Seenigama Devale sits on a tiny offshore islet north of Hikkaduwa. It is a compact, memorable shrine stop, with the sea crossing giving it more character than many roadside temples.

Ask locally about boat access and behave as you would at any active religious site. It combines well with the tsunami memorials north of town.
