Tsunami Honganji Vihara is a roadside memorial north of Hikkaduwa, dominated by a tall Buddha statue facing the coast. It is one of the easiest places to pause and understand how violently the 2004 tsunami struck this area.

The stop is brief but important. Keep the tone respectful; for local families, the disaster is not old history.
