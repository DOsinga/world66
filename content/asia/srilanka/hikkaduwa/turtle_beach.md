Turtle Beach is the informal name for the stretch where sea turtles are often seen close to shore. It can be a wonderful encounter, but it also attracts the usual problems of crowding, feeding, and people getting too close.

Keep distance and do not touch the turtles. The experience is far better when the animals are allowed to move naturally rather than being turned into beach props.
