Nagapoosani Amman Temple is one of the most important Hindu pilgrimage sites in the Jaffna region, standing on Nainativu island off the peninsula's western side. The temple is dedicated to the goddess Nagapoosani Amman and draws worshippers throughout the year, with the annual festival bringing a far larger crowd.

For travellers, the journey is part of the visit: road across the flat islands and causeways, then a short boat crossing to Nainativu. Dress modestly, move with the rhythm of worship rather than treating the temple as a backdrop, and allow time for the ferry logistics. The nearby Buddhist Nagadeepa site adds another layer to the island's religious significance.
