---
title: Things to Do
type: section
---

Jaffna rewards slow exploration. The city itself has enough to fill two or three days — the fort, the temple, the public library, the old Kachcheri ruins — and the peninsula's islands add at least another day if conditions allow. The best approach is to hire a bicycle or a tuk-tuk for the day and work your way through the city before tackling the island ferries. The islands need an early start.
