Kandy War Cemetery is a small, carefully kept Commonwealth cemetery that records the human reach of the Second World War in Sri Lanka. Its order and quiet make a strong contrast with the movement and noise of central Kandy.

The cemetery is not a long visit, but it rewards a respectful stop for travellers interested in wartime history. Reading the headstones gives the place more weight than its modest size suggests.
