Kandy is the natural hub for the central hill country and north-central cultural triangle. The Elephant Orphanage at [Pinnawela](/asia/srilanka/pinnawela) is 40 km northwest and easy by tuk-tuk or organised taxi. The cave temples at [Dambulla](/asia/srilanka/dambulla) — five rock-cut shrine rooms containing 153 Buddha statues — are about 75 km north and a long but manageable day trip. Sigiriya's dramatic rock fortress is 97 km north, best combined with Dambulla.

The scenic train journey east toward [Ella](/asia/srilanka/ella), through tea plantations and nine-arch bridges, is worth doing as an overnight rather than a day trip — get off at Ella or Haputale and return the next day.
