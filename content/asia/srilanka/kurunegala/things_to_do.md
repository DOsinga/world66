---
title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Kurunegala is a crossroads city on the route between Colombo and the Cultural Triangle, and most visitors pass through rather than linger. Those who stop will find the rock outcrops that define the town's skyline worth climbing, a lake good for early-morning walks, and a scattering of temples and ancient sites in the surrounding district that can fill a pleasant day with your own transport.
