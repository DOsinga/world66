---
title: Things to Do
type: section
---

Matara's main sights cluster in and around the old Dutch fort, which occupies a headland between the sea and the Nilwala River. The Star Fort across the river, the Dutch Reformed Church inside the main fort, and the Matara Bodhiya sacred fig tree are the main heritage attractions. Outside town, Dondra Head to the east — the southernmost point of Sri Lanka — is worth the short trip, and the Parevi Duwa island temple is a pleasant walk from the fort area.
