---
title: "Getting There"
type: section
---

Mihintale is 12 kilometres east of [Anuradhapura](/asia/srilanka/anuradhapura), the natural base. **Tuk-tuks** from Anuradhapura town centre run for around 1,500–2,000 LKR each way and can wait for you at the foot of the climb. **Local buses** (route 43) run hourly from Anuradhapura's New Bus Stand and drop you about ten minutes' walk from the entrance.

By car or hired van it is a 20-minute drive on the A12 toward Trincomalee. The whole site is easily seen as a half-day trip from Anuradhapura, leaving you the afternoon free for the ruins of the old capital.
