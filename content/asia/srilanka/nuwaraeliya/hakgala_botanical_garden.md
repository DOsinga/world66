Hakgala Botanical Garden lies southeast of Nuwara Eliya in Sri Lanka's cool hill country, where the altitude makes roses, ferns, and temperate plantings thrive in a way that feels far removed from the lowlands. The garden spreads across slopes beneath Hakgala Rock, with formal beds, forested areas, and misty mountain air.

It is an easy half-day outing from town, especially for travellers who enjoy gardens or need a gentler day between tea estates and hikes. Weather can turn quickly, so carry a light layer even when Nuwara Eliya seems mild. The best visits are slow, with time to notice how colonial garden design met tropical highland ecology.
