---
title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Tissamaharama is primarily a wildlife base, but the town has its own attractions. The [Tissamaharama Raja Maha Vihara](/asia/srilanka/tissamaharama/tissamaharama_vihara) stupa is one of the largest in southern Sri Lanka and reflects the town's long history as a royal capital. [Tissa Wewa](/asia/srilanka/tissamaharama/tissa_wewa), the ancient reservoir beside town, is the best spot for birdwatching and an atmospheric sunset walk. [Yala National Park](/asia/srilanka/tissamaharama/yala_national_park) is the main draw — morning safaris for leopards, elephants and sloth bears. [Bundala National Park](/asia/srilanka/tissamaharama/bundala_national_park) is closer and calmer, excellent for flamingos and migratory waterbirds. The pilgrimage town of [Kataragama](/asia/srilanka/tissamaharama/kataragama), about 15 km north, is one of the most sacred sites in Sri Lanka and worth visiting for its mix of Hindu, Buddhist and Muslim shrines.
