Kanniya Hot Wells are a group of small geothermal wells outside Trincomalee, each with warm mineral water and a layer of local legend attached. The site is simple, but it is one of the area's distinctive inland stops.

Expect a brief, sometimes busy visit rather than a spa experience. It pairs well with other excursions north of town, and respectful behavior matters because the wells have religious and cultural associations.
