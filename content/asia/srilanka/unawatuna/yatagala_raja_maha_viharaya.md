Yatagala Raja Maha Viharaya is a peaceful Buddhist temple inland from Unawatuna, set among large granite boulders and greenery rather than directly on the tourist beach strip. The setting gives it a sheltered, contemplative feel, with shrine spaces and Buddha images tucked into the rockscape.

It is a good short excursion when the coast feels too busy. Dress respectfully, go quietly, and consider combining it with Galle or nearby inland lanes; the temple's value is its atmosphere as much as any single monument.
