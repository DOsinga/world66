Weligama is the softer, more local-feeling neighbour of [Mirissa](/asia/srilanka/mirissa), set around a wide sandy bay on Sri Lanka's south coast. The name means "sandy village", which is unusually literal: the beach is long, shallow, and forgiving, making it one of the best beginner surf spots in the country.

The town itself is busy rather than pretty, with the coastal railway, bus stops, fish stalls, and surf schools all pressed close together. That is part of the appeal. Weligama still feels like a working Sri Lankan town that happens to have good waves, not a resort strip built only for visitors.

The postcard sight is [Taprobane Island](/asia/srilanka/weligama/taprobane_island), a tiny private island just offshore with a villa hidden among palms. Along the road toward Ahangama you may still see [stilt fishermen](/asia/srilanka/weligama/stilt_fishermen), though the practice has become as much performance as livelihood. For a quieter hour, visit the ancient [Kushtarajagala Statue](/asia/srilanka/weligama/kushtarajagala_statue) just inland from the main road.

Weligama works best as a low-key base for the south coast. Mirissa is a short tuk-tuk ride east, Ahangama and Midigama are west, and [Galle](/asia/srilanka/galle) is close enough for a day trip by train.
