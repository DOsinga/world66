Weligama's fish market is a working local stop rather than a polished attraction. Boats come in early, buyers crowd around the catch, and the smell of salt, diesel, and drying nets makes the beach-town postcard feel more honest.

Go in the morning and stay out of the way. It is a quick visit, but it gives useful context to the grilled fish and seafood curries served later in the day along the beach road.
