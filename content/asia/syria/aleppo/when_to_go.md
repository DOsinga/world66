---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

Aleppo has a Mediterranean continental climate: hot, dry summers and mild, wet winters. Spring (March to May) and autumn (October to November) are the most comfortable times to visit, with temperatures between 15 and 25°C and low humidity. Summer is very hot — regularly above 35°C — and the dusty heat can be oppressive in the old city. Winters are cool and occasionally rainy, with some years seeing light snow.

The practical question for 2025 and beyond is less about weather than about conditions on the ground. Aleppo is in a period of significant reconstruction and the situation continues to evolve. Check current travel advisories and connect with organizations working in the region before planning a visit.
