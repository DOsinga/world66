Arwad (also spelled Arwad or Ruad) is a small island just off the coast of [Tartus](/asia/syria/tartus), reachable by boat in about 15 minutes. It is the only permanently inhabited island on the Syrian coast, and one of the oldest continuously occupied islands in the world — Phoenician, Greek, Roman, and Crusader rulers each left their mark.

There are [ruins of a castle](/asia/syria/arwadisland/arwad_castle) near the west coast that you can wander around freely. Locals claim it was built by Alexander the Great, though the Crusaders substantially rebuilt it. The island's [narrow alleys and old sea walls](/asia/syria/arwadisland/sea_walls) and the [small harbour](/asia/syria/arwadisland/arwad_harbour) make for an atmospheric hour or two. You do not need to stay overnight — accommodation on the mainland in Tartus is easier and more practical.
