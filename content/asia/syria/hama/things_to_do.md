Hama's greatest sights line the banks of the Orontes, where the ancient norias still creak and groan through each rotation. Beyond the waterwheels, the city rewards those who linger: an Ottoman palace, medieval mosques layered over Byzantine and Roman foundations, and caravanserais that once lodged merchants on the road between Aleppo and Damascus.
