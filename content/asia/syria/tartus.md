Tartus is Syria's main Mediterranean port after Latakia, a coastal city where the sea gives the country a different mood from the inland caravan cities. It is practical rather than grand, but the old centre has Crusader masonry, narrow lanes, and enough sea air to make it a useful stop on the coast.

The historic name was Tortosa, and the strongest surviving monument is the former [Cathedral of Our Lady of Tortosa](/asia/syria/tartus/cathedral_of_our_lady_of_tortosa), once a Crusader church and later used as a museum. Nearby, the [Citadel of Tartus](/asia/syria/tartus/citadel_of_tartus) is less a single showpiece than a lived-in area of old walls and houses.

Tartus also matters because it is the mainland base for [Arwad Island](/asia/syria/arwadisland). Boats cross from the waterfront when conditions and local circumstances allow. Travellers should check current security and transport conditions carefully before planning any Syrian coastal itinerary.
