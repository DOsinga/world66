Tartus Harbour is a working port, not a leisure marina. That is what makes it interesting: cranes, ferries, fishing craft, and the hard practical edge of Syria's Mediterranean coast all meet here.

For travellers the harbour is mainly useful as the departure area for boats to Arwad Island, when they are running. Ask locally before assuming crossings are possible, and treat port areas with common sense and restraint around photography.
