---
title: "Eating Out"
type: section
---

The village at Alishan station has a cluster of restaurants and food stalls catering to the tourist trade. Staples include braised pork rice, mountain vegetable dishes, and wild boar sausage — a local specialty sold at roadside stalls throughout the area. The tea grown on the slopes around Alishan, particularly high-mountain oolong, is some of the finest in Taiwan and worth buying directly from local farms if you get the chance.

Meal options are limited outside the main village, so it is worth picking up snacks and drinks before heading out on longer forest walks. Prices are higher than in Chiayi city below; this is normal for a popular mountain destination with limited supply routes.
