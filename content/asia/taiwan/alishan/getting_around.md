---
title: "Getting Around"
type: section
---

Within the Alishan National Scenic Area, the internal forest railway network is the main way to get between key points. Small trains connect Alishan Station to Zhushan (the sunrise viewing peak), Shenmu (the giant trees area), and Chaoping. Tickets are sold separately from the main Chiayi–Alishan line; buy them at Alishan Station or from kiosks near the platform.

The pre-dawn Zhushan train — which departs around 90 minutes before sunrise — is the one to book first. It fills up every morning during good weather. Check the departure time on the board at the station the evening before, as it varies with the season.

Most of the forest walking trails are easy to navigate on foot once you are at the relevant station. The paths are well-maintained and clearly signed in both Chinese and English. Walking between Zhushan and Shenmu via the forest trail takes about two hours and is one of the best ways to see the old-growth cypress trees away from the main crowds.
