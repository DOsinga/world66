---
title: "Getting There"
type: section
---

The most straightforward way to reach Alishan from Chiayi city is by bus. Several operators run direct services from Chiayi HSR station and Chiayi city centre; the journey takes around two and a half hours along a winding mountain road. Buses are reliable and reasonably priced, though they can fill up on busy weekends and during cherry blossom season.

The Alishan Forest Railway is the more atmospheric option. The narrow-gauge line climbs from Chiayi Station through three distinct climate zones — tropical forest, subtropical tea plantations, and temperate mountain forest — before arriving at Alishan Station. The journey takes roughly three hours and is a heritage attraction in its own right; the line has been operating since 1912. Demand for seats is high during holidays and the cherry blossom period, so book well in advance through the Taiwan Railways website. Note that sections of the line have been closed for repairs at various times; check current status before planning around it.

Driving is possible via Provincial Highway 18, a winding mountain road that climbs steeply from Chiayi. Allow at least two hours from the city. The road is subject to closure after typhoons and heavy rain.
