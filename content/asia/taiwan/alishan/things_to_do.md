---
title: Things to Do
type: section
---

Alishan's appeal is almost entirely natural — the forests, the clouds, the sunrises, and the train. The small resort village at the top has enough accommodation and food to sustain a night or two, and a night stay is strongly recommended: the sunrise experience at Chushan requires an early start, and the forest walks are at their best in the quiet morning hours before the day-trip crowds arrive.
