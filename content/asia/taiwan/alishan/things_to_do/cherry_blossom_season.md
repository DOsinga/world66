---
title: "Cherry Blossom Season"
type: poi
---

Alishan's cherry blossoms typically peak in late March to early April, turning the area around the main village and the trail between the stations into a corridor of pink and white. The trees are a mix of mountain cherry varieties that bloom later than those at lower elevations — which means Alishan's season extends the blossom window for visitors who have already seen the lowland flowering. At its height, the combination of blossoms, mist, and ancient cypress forest is unlike anywhere else in Taiwan.

The most concentrated display is along the path between Alishan Station and Zhushan, and in the gardens around the main visitor area. The internal forest railway runs through the blossoming sections, and the combination of a flower-lined narrow-gauge track and misty mountain air makes for memorable photographs.

This is also the busiest period of the year by a considerable margin. Accommodation within the scenic area books out weeks in advance, and the sunrise trains at Zhushan are packed every morning. If you plan to visit during cherry blossom season, book accommodation and train tickets as early as possible — or consider a base in Chiayi city and a day trip, arriving early and leaving before the afternoon crowds. Weekdays are significantly less crowded than weekends even during peak season.
