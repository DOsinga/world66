---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

Alishan is a year-round destination, but each season brings a different character. Late March to early April is cherry blossom season, when the sakura trees around the main village burst into pink and white — this is also peak season, so accommodation books out weeks in advance and the sunrise trains fill up before dawn.

Spring and autumn offer the most reliable weather for clear sunrises and forest walks. Summer (June to September) is warm enough to be pleasant at altitude, but typhoon season brings the risk of road closures and landslides; check conditions before setting out and be prepared for disruption. Winter is cold — temperatures drop close to freezing at night — but crowds thin out considerably and the morning mist can produce extraordinary effects over the cloud sea.

Whatever time of year you visit, bring layers. Even in summer the air at 2,200 metres is considerably cooler than on the plains, and it turns chilly quickly once the sun drops. Midweek visits are noticeably more peaceful than weekends, when day-trippers from Chiayi and Tainan arrive in numbers.
