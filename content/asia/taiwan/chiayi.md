Chiayi is easy to treat as the city before Alishan, but that sells it short. It is a compact southern Taiwanese city with Japanese-era timber history, good local food, and a slower rhythm than Taipei or Taichung. The Alishan Forest Railway still gives the place its strongest travel identity: mountain logging, narrow-gauge engineering, and cypress wood all lead back here.

The most useful part of town for visitors sits between the railway station, [Wenhua Road Night Market](/asia/taiwan/chiayi/wenhua_road_night_market), and the old timber district around [Hinoki Village](/asia/taiwan/chiayi/hinoki_village). East of the centre, [Chiayi Park](/asia/taiwan/chiayi/chiayi_park) spreads into a quieter green zone with old shrine remains, a botanical garden, and the Sun-Shooting Tower.

Chiayi is not flashy, and that is part of the appeal. Come for turkey rice, old wood, small museums, and the feeling of a working Taiwanese city that has not been smoothed into a weekend resort.
