The Art Site of Chiayi Railway Warehouse reuses part of the city's old railway infrastructure as a space for exhibitions and local art. It is a modest but worthwhile stop if you are already exploring Chiayi's railway heritage.

The setting is part of the appeal: industrial buildings, tracks, and art spaces sit close to the station area, giving the site a different feel from a standard gallery.
