Chiayi City Historical Relic Museum is a small local-history museum in Chiayi Park. Its displays cover the city's older buildings, civic history, and archaeological finds, with the park setting making it easy to combine with nearby sights.

The museum is modest and English interpretation may be limited, but it is worth a brief stop if you are already exploring Chiayi Park or the city's Japanese-era heritage.
