Hinoki Village is a cluster of restored Japanese wooden dormitories from the Alishan forestry era. The low houses, tiled roofs, and cypress scent make the timber history of Chiayi feel tangible, even now that many buildings hold shops and cafes.

It is polished, but still worth your time. Go early or late for a quieter walk, then pair it with the nearby Alishan Forest Railway Garage Park and Chiayi Municipal Museum.
