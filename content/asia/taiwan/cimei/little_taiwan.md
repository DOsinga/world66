Little Taiwan is a wave-cut platform on Cimei's east coast whose outline looks, from the viewing area above, like a small version of Taiwan. It is one of those simple landscape jokes that works because the coast is already beautiful.

The stop is quick, but it fits well into a scooter loop around the island. Morning or late afternoon light gives the basalt ledges and shallow water more shape.
