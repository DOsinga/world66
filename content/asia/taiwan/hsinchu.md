Hsinchu is one of northern [Taiwan](/asia/taiwan)'s oldest cities, a windy, prosperous place halfway between [Taipei](/asia/taiwan/taipei) and Taichung. It is best known today for the science park that anchors Taiwan's semiconductor industry, but the centre still has older layers: Qing temples, a surviving city gate, Japanese-era civic buildings, and food stalls packed around the City God Temple.

The city is not as immediately seductive as Taipei or Tainan. Its appeal is more local: a compact old core, easy rail access, a good food culture, and several green escapes close enough to reach between meals. [East Gate](/asia/taiwan/hsinchu/east_gate) is the obvious landmark, while [City God Temple](/asia/taiwan/hsinchu/city_god_temple) is where the city feels most alive.

Hsinchu also has a real craft identity. Glassmaking grew here during the Japanese period and later became one of the city's signature industries; the [Craft Glass Museum](/asia/taiwan/hsinchu/craft_glass_museum) gives that history a home. When the weather is clear, [Eighteen Peaks Mountain](/asia/taiwan/hsinchu/eighteen_peaks_mountain) and [Green Grass Lake](/asia/taiwan/hsinchu/green_grass_lake) make the city feel greener than its industrial reputation suggests.
