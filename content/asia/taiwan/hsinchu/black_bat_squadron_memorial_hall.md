The Hsinchu City Black Bat Squadron Memorial Hall commemorates the airmen who served and died during the Cold War-era conflict between Taiwan and Mainland China. Exhibits focus on the squadron's covert missions and the people behind them.

It is a compact history stop that pairs well with Hsinchu's older city sights such as the East Gate and City God Temple.
