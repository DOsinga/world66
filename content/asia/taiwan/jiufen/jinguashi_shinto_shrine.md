Jinguashi Shinto Shrine, also called the Mountain God Shrine, was built in 1933 during the Japanese mining period. Only stone steps, pillars, and foundations remain, but the site still has a strong hillside atmosphere above the old gold-mining settlement.

Come for the mix of history and views. It pairs naturally with the Gold Museum below and the trails around Keelung Mountain.
