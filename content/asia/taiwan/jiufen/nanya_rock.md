Nanya Rock is a stretch of sculpted shoreline at the northern edge of the Northeast and Yilan Coast National Scenic Area. Wind and waves have carved the sandstone into striped, pitted forms, and the pavilions and paths make it an easy stop if you are driving between Jiufen, Jinguashi, and the coast.
