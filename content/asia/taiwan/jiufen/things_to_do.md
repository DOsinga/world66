---
title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Jiufen rewards wandering. The main attractions — the old street, the teahouses, the views — are concentrated in a small area, but the lanes running off the main steps lead to residential neighbourhoods where the tourist density drops sharply. Go up, not just along: the highest lanes of the town, above the main teahouse area, have city and sea views and almost no crowds. The [Gold Museum in Jinguashi](/asia/taiwan/jiufen/gold_museum) is the destination for anyone interested in the mining history that created the town.
