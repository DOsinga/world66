---
title: "Bars and Cafes"
type: section
---

Kaohsiung's drinking scene is centred on three areas: **Yancheng** for craft beer and small bars, **Pier-2** for harbourside drinks, and **Sinkuchan** (around Cijin Park) for late-night clubs.

**Bao Bao Pizza & Beer** in Yancheng pours a long list of Taiwanese craft beers on a converted shophouse balcony. The pizzas are good and the crowd is mostly expats and Kaohsiung 20-somethings.

**Slow Beer Brewery** is the closest thing the city has to a brewpub, on the western edge of Pier-2 with a sea view and a tap list of southern Taiwanese hop-forward beers.

**Brick Yard** in Sinkuchan is a long-running hip-hop and reggae club that draws a young Taiwanese and military crowd; busy from midnight, dead until 11pm.

**Cafe Bar** is the original Pier-2 cafe, in Warehouse C5, with terrace tables overlooking the harbour. Decent coffee and a good place to start an evening before walking down to the Music Center.

For atmosphere over taste, the **rooftop bar of the 85 Sky Tower** has a 360° view; drinks are expensive but the city laid out below makes up for it.
