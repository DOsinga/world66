The long, narrow sandbar that protects Kaohsiung harbour from the open sea, reached by a five-minute ferry from Gushan Pier. Cijin feels like a different world from the city centre — a single main street of seafood restaurants and trinket shops opens onto the **Cijin Beach** on one side, and a short walk past a small temple takes you to the 19th-century **Cijin Lighthouse** and the **Old Fort** with their views over the harbour entrance.

The seafood street offers grilled squid, shrimp, sashimi from the morning's catch and the Cijin specialty of giant black sausage. A short tunnel at the foot of the hill (now the Cijin Tunnel art installation) is the old Japanese-era passage to the lighthouse.

A whole day is easy here if you swim, eat your way along the street and rent a tandem bike to go to the far end of the island. The ferry runs every ten minutes, day and night.
