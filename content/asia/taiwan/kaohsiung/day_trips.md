Kaohsiung's southern position makes it a base for the southern half of Taiwan. **Kenting**, at the island's tropical tip, is about two hours by bus down the coast — beaches, snorkelling and the national park. **Tainan**, Taiwan's old capital, is 40 minutes by HSR or 90 minutes on the slower TRA train and has the strongest concentration of historic temples on the island. **Alishan**, a mountain forest with the famous narrow-gauge railway and sunrise views over a sea of clouds, takes longer — leave very early or stay a night.

For closer options, **Fo Guang Shan** monastery (covered under Things to Do), **Maolin Recreation Area** with its butterfly valleys, and the riverside town of **Meinong** with its Hakka culture and tea houses are all under 90 minutes by bus or car.
