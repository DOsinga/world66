---
title: "Getting There"
type: section
---

**By air**: Kaohsiung International Airport (KHH) sits inside the city limits, only 8 km from the centre — a 15-minute MRT ride on the Red Line. It handles flights from across East and Southeast Asia (mainland China, Japan, Hong Kong, Vietnam, Thailand, Philippines, Singapore) and a few long-haul services.

**By high-speed rail**: HSR trains from [Taipei](/asia/taiwan/taipei) take about 90 minutes to Kaohsiung's Zuoying station. From Zuoying, the MRT Red Line takes 20 minutes to the city centre.

**By conventional train**: TRA services from [Taichung](/asia/taiwan/taichung), [Tainan](/asia/taiwan/tainan) and [Taipei](/asia/taiwan/taipei) reach Kaohsiung Main Station, which is in the centre of town. The journey from Taipei is around 4 hours.

**By bus**: Long-distance buses are cheaper than the HSR and run from a terminal next to Kaohsiung Main Station. Taipei is 4-5 hours by motorway.

**By ferry**: Kaohsiung is a major cruise port but has no scheduled international passenger ferries.
