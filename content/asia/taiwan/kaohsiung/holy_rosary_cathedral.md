A handsome Spanish-built Gothic-revival church in Yancheng, the seat of the Catholic Diocese of Kaohsiung. The exterior is red brick with pale dressings and pointed-arch windows; the interior is light, with a vaulted ceiling and stained-glass windows.

The cathedral is in active use as a parish church, but is open to visitors outside service times. The surrounding lanes are typical Yancheng — narrow, full of cafes and small shops — and easy to combine with a stroll through the neighbourhood. A short walk from Yanchengpu MRT.
