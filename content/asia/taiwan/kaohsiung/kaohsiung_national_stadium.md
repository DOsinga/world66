A 55,000-seat athletics stadium with an unusual curving roof that, seen from above, takes the shape of a dragon. Almost the entire upper surface is covered with solar panels — over a million watts of generating capacity. The interior is just as striking, with the open ribbon-roof seemingly floating overhead.

You can wander into the public side of the stadium during the day even when no event is on, and the local metro authority will sometimes open the roof viewpoint. To see it under lights, time a visit around a Lions FC football match or a concert. Metro Red Line to World Games MRT.
