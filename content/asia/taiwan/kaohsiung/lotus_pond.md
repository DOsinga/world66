A large rectangular lake on the northern edge of Kaohsiung, ringed by an extraordinary collection of Taoist temples, shrines and giant deity figures. The signature view is the **Dragon and Tiger Pagodas** — a pair of seven-storey pagodas connected to the shore by zigzag bridges and entered through the carved mouths of a dragon and a tiger.

Walk clockwise around the pond and you also pass the **Spring and Autumn Pavilions** guarded by a man riding a giant dragon, the **Northern Pavilion** with its huge Xuantian Shangdi statue rising out of the lake, and the **Confucius Temple**, the largest in Taiwan. Each is photogenic enough to merit a stop. The whole circuit takes about two hours. Take metro Red Line to Ecological District, then walk five minutes north.
