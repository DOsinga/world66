Twice the size of Liuhe and where Kaohsiung locals actually go to eat. Ruifeng operates Tuesday, Thursday, Friday, Saturday and Sunday nights and packs hundreds of stalls along narrow alleys near Kaohsiung Arena MRT.

The mix is broader than at Liuhe: skewered meats, fried-rice noodles, fresh-squeezed juices, stinky tofu, bubble tea, plus games and clothing stalls. The market is large enough that you can spend two hours here and not see all of it. Take the Red Line to Kaohsiung Arena and walk five minutes north.
