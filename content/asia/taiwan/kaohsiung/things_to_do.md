---

Kaohsiung's attractions are spread out — the city is large and built around its port, and most of what is worth seeing requires a metro ride or a short taxi hop. The headline sights are the Lotus Pond temples on the northern edge, the converted warehouses of Pier-2 on the waterfront, the British Consulate on Shoushan, and the giant Buddhist complex of Fo Guang Shan inland.

Use the filters below to narrow the list by what you are after — temples, viewpoints, museums, or quirky one-offs like the Dome of Light at Formosa Boulevard station.
