Kaohsiung's Tianhou Temple on Cijin is one of the city's most important old temples,
tied to maritime devotion and the island's fishing history. Dedicated to Mazu, it gives
Cijin's beach-and-seafood day trip a deeper cultural anchor.

Visit while walking between the ferry pier, old streets, and the coast. The temple is
active, so move respectfully, watch the rituals, and take time with the carved and
painted details rather than treating it as a quick backdrop.
