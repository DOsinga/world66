---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

Kaohsiung has a hot tropical climate — much warmer than Taipei, and noticeably drier.

**October to March** is the best time to visit, with daytime temperatures of 22-28°C and little rain. December and January can feel cool to locals but are pleasant for walking.

**April to June** is hot (28-32°C) and humid, with afternoon thunderstorms common from May. Still pleasant if you can deal with the heat.

**July to September** is the height of summer and typhoon season. Daily highs above 33°C, oppressive humidity, and the occasional typhoon shutting things down for a day or two. Avoid if you are not used to heat.

The **Kaohsiung Lantern Festival** on the Love River around Lunar New Year (late January or February) is the city's signature event, with thousands of lanterns floated on the water. The **Spring Scream** music festival down in [Kenting](/asia/taiwan/kenting) draws crowds away in April; the city itself is quieter then.
