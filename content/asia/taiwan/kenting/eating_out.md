---
title: "Eating Out"
type: section
---

Seafood is the thing to eat in Kenting — the fishing boats that work the waters off both coasts bring in fresh catch daily. The resort restaurants on the main strip are expensive by Taiwanese standards; the Kenting Street night market is better value. For the most authentic and cheapest food, make the short trip to Hengchun town, where local restaurants serve the full range of Taiwanese cooking without the resort markup.
