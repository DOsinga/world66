Kenting National Forest Recreation Area protects a lush patch of tropical forest above the resort strip. Trails lead through thick greenery, limestone formations, and cave-like hollows, with the best parts found deeper into the park rather than near the entrance.

It is not the cheapest outing in Kenting, but walkers and plant lovers will get more from it than from another hour on the beach.
