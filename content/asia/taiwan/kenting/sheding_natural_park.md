Sheding Natural Park is a short trail area above Kenting with coral limestone gorges, open grassland and views toward the southern tip of Taiwan. The main loop takes roughly one and a half to two hours on foot, making it an easy nature stop when the beaches feel too busy.

The trail is free, though there may be a small parking fee.
