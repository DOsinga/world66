Beihai Tunnel is a hand-cut military tunnel built in 1968 for amphibious landing craft after the Taiwan Strait crises. Its long rock passage and water-level setting make the Cold War history of the Matsu Islands feel unusually immediate.

Check local access before going, as opening conditions can depend on tides and maintenance.
