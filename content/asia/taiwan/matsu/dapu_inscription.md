The Dapu Inscription is a 1617 stone carving on Jyuguang in the Matsu Islands. It commemorates the local garrison's role in dealing with pirate raids that troubled nearby communities.

It is a small historical stop, but it gives useful context for Matsu's long military and coastal-defense history.
