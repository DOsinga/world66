Qiaozi is a small Beigan village known for temples, fishing lanes, and a quieter mood than Qinbi. It does not have Qinbi's perfect postcard slope, but it feels less arranged for visitors.

Use it as a low-key walking stop while exploring Beigan. The lanes are compact, the harbour is close, and the village gives a good sense of everyday island life.
