Qinbi Tianhou Temple is a small seafarers' temple in Qinbi Village, dedicated to Mazu, the sea goddess worshipped across Taiwan's coast and islands. It fits naturally into a wander through Qinbi's stone houses and lanes, especially if you are interested in the village's maritime roots.
