The War and Peace Memorial Park Exhibition Center explains Matsu's decades under military rule from a former stronghold on Beigan. Displays cover fortifications, island life, and the front-line role the islands played during the Cold War.

It is one of the more useful context stops in Matsu, especially before visiting the old tunnels and coastal military sites.
