Xiyin Triumph Monument is a large hillside landmark on Dongyin, painted directly onto the slope above the coast. Its message is martial and old-fashioned, but the scale and exposed setting make it a memorable stop while exploring the island's military-era sights.

It works best as a quick detour combined with Dongyong Lighthouse and the nearby coastal viewpoints. The monument is more about the island's Cold War atmosphere than fine art.
