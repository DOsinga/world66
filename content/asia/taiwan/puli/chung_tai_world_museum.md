Chung Tai World Museum sits near the monastery and gives useful depth to the visit. Its collection focuses on Buddhist sculpture, ritual objects, and religious art, with enough English context to make a short visit worthwhile.

Pair it with the monastery rather than treating it as a separate half-day. The museum is best for travellers who want to understand the visual language behind the large temple complex next door.
