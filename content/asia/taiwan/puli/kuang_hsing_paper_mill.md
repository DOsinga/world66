Kuang Hsing Paper Mill turns one of Puli's old industries into a hands-on visit. You can see the paper-making process, watch craftspeople work, and usually join a simple workshop rather than just looking at displays.

It is especially good with children, but adults interested in calligraphy, printmaking, or craft production will get something from it too. The visit is short, so combine it with the nearby basin sights.
