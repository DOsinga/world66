Lungnan Natural Lacquerware Museum focuses on Puli's lacquerware tradition, a craft closely tied to the town's modern cultural identity. Displays explain the materials and techniques, and demonstrations may be available by arrangement.

It is a small specialist museum, best paired with Puli's other craft and cultural stops such as the paper mill or Paper Dome.
