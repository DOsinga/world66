The Taiwan Geographic Center Monument marks Puli's claim as the middle of the island. The monument itself is not grand, but the short climb and view over the basin make it a useful orientation stop.

Come early or late in the day if you want softer light over the hills. It is not worth a long detour, but it fits well into a Puli circuit.
