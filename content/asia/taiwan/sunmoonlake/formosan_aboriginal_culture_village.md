Formosan Aboriginal Culture Village is part cultural park, part theme park, and part full-day family outing above Sun Moon Lake. Its strongest reason for inclusion is the Indigenous village area, where architecture, performances, and displays introduce Taiwan's Aboriginal cultures to visitors.

The rides and gardens make it broader and more commercial than an ordinary museum. That mix will not suit everyone, but for families or first-time visitors to central Taiwan it can be an accessible way to combine culture, views, and easy entertainment.
