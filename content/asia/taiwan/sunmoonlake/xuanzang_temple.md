  The monk Xuanzang made a real journey from Tang-dynasty China to India in 629 AD, travelling for seventeen years and covering over 25,000 kilometres to collect Buddhist scriptures. The Xuanzang Temple commemorates the Tang-dynasty Buddhist monk whose journey to India and back inspired the novel Journey to the West. The temple holds what is claimed to be a bone relic of Xuanzang himself, retrieved from Japan after the war and enshrined here in a small inner chamber. Whether the relic is authentic hardly matters — the setting on the hillside above the lake's north shore, reached by a path through dense forest, is genuinely atmospheric.

The approach through the forest gives the Xuanzang Temple a quieter, more meditative feel than the larger Wenwu Temple nearby. It is worth combining the two in a single walk along the northern lakeshore.
