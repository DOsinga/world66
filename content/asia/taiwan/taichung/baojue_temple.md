Baojue Temple is a postwar Buddhist temple in the north of the central city, best known for the enormous gilded Maitreya Buddha that sits on the main roof. The temple itself is unremarkable; the Buddha is the photograph.

The interior holds a hall dedicated to the bones of soldiers who died in the Chinese Civil War, and several minor shrines. The temple is free to enter, with the usual incense and prayer materials available. Open from early morning to early evening.
