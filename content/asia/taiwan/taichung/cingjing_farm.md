Cingjing Farm is a former government livestock farm at 1,700 metres altitude in the central mountains, about three hours from Taichung. The cool climate, alpine views, and sheep grazing on open pasture make it one of the most popular mountain destinations on the island.

The farm has been heavily developed for tourism — bed-and-breakfasts in Swiss-chalet style line the hillside above — but the central pasture and the morning sheepdog show retain their appeal. Combine with the Hehuan Mountain pass at 3,275 metres for serious mountain views. Best as an overnight rather than a single day.
