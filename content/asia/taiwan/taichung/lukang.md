Lukang, an hour south-west of Taichung in Changhua County, is one of the best preserved old towns on the island. It was Taiwan's second-largest port in the late eighteenth century and the original wealth shows in the temples and merchant houses on Bantian Old Street.

The Longshan Temple (a Qing dynasty Buddhist temple often called the most beautiful in Taiwan) and Mazu Temple are the central religious sites. The old street markets, sweet shops, and small artisanal workshops are the everyday draw. Half a day by direct bus from Taichung; longer if you walk both old streets and stay for the evening lights.
