Sun Moon Lake is the largest natural lake in Taiwan, an hour and a half by bus from Taichung. The two halves of the lake — Sun in the east, Moon in the west — meet at the Lalu Island sacred to the Thao indigenous people. The shoreline is encircled by cable car, cycle path, and a popular boat circuit.

A day trip works if you take the early bus and limit yourself to one or two stops; an overnight makes the place easier. The Wenwu Temple, the Xuanguang Pier, and a sunrise from the Ci'en Pagoda are the standard highlights. Reachable from Taichung Gancheng Station on the morning Sun Moon Lake Bus.
