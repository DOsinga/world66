Wanchun Temple is a Qing-era temple in central Taichung, known for its carved details and old couplet traditionally linked to Emperor Guangxu. It is a compact stop, but it adds a useful older layer to a walk through Zhongqu and the streets around Taichung Station.

Visit for the craftsmanship and neighbourhood context rather than for scale. The temple pairs naturally with Taichung Park, Miyahara, and other central sights.
