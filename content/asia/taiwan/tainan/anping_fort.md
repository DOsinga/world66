Anping Fort — the original Dutch colonial stronghold — is the starting point for understanding Tainan's layered history. The Dutch East India Company built Fort Zeelandia here in 1624 on a sandbar off the western coast of Formosa, and for nearly four decades it served as the administrative and commercial heart of Dutch Taiwan. Today the site is partly reconstructed, with original brick remains from the 17th century visible alongside a later Qing-era wall and a lookout tower that gives views across the flat Anping district toward the sea.

The on-site museum traces the Dutch colonial period, the Koxinga era, and subsequent history through maps, artifacts, and models. Outside the fort walls, the Anping neighbourhood's small streets and seafood restaurants make for an easy hour of wandering. The fort pairs naturally with the [Anping Tree House](/asia/taiwan/tainan/anping_treehouse) a few minutes' walk away.
