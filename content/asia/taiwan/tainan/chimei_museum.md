Chimei Museum is one of Tainan's most distinctive museum experiences, a grand private
collection in a deliberately classical setting. The galleries mix Western art, musical
instruments, arms and armor, natural history, and decorative objects in a way that feels
eclectic but carefully staged.

The building, bridge, and grounds are part of the draw, so leave time outside as well as
in the galleries. It sits away from Tainan's old temple core, making it best as a
planned half-day rather than a casual walk-by.
