Sacrificial Rites Martial Temple is a large temple complex in Tainan's old center, blending Taoist and Buddhist elements. The halls include many statues, including the 18 arhats, and the site stays active with worshippers lighting incense and casting divination blocks.

It sits close to Chihkan Tower and Grand Matsu Temple, making it easy to include on a walk through central Tainan's historic temple district.
