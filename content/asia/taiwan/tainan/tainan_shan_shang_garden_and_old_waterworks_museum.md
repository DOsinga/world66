Tainan Shan-Shang Garden and Old Waterworks Museum turns an early 20th-century water treatment site into one of Tainan's more unusual heritage stops. The red-brick machinery halls, filtration ponds, and landscaped grounds show how the city modernised its water supply during the Japanese period.

It sits outside the central sightseeing circuit, so it suits travellers with extra time or a particular interest in architecture and industrial history. The reward is space, greenery, and a side of Tainan that is different from temples, snack streets, and old merchant houses.
