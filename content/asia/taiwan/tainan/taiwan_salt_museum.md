Taiwan Salt Museum explains the island's salt industry on the site of what was once Taiwan's largest salt production area. The displays cover salt-making methods, local industry, and the coastal landscape around Qigu.

It pairs naturally with nearby Qigu Salt Mountain and the old salt fields, giving context to one of the more distinctive day trips from central Tainan.
