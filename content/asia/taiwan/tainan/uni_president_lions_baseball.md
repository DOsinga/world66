Uni-President Lions baseball gives visitors a lively way to experience Tainan beyond temples, food streets, and historic Anping. The team is one of Taiwan's established professional baseball clubs, and home games bring chants, mascots, snacks, and a strongly local crowd atmosphere.

It is not a conventional sight, but it can be a worthwhile evening for travellers interested in Taiwanese popular culture. Check the current Chinese Professional Baseball League schedule and venue before planning around a match.
