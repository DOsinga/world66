Tainan City Zuojhen Fossil Park sits well outside the old city core, but it earns its place for travellers interested in geology, natural history, or a family-friendly day away from temples. The displays focus on fossils found in the region, including prehistoric animals that help explain Taiwan's deep natural past.

Treat it as a short excursion rather than a quick city-centre stop. The museum side is accessible and child-friendly, while the rural setting makes the trip feel different from Anping, Chihkan Tower, and the dense historic streets that dominate most Tainan itineraries.
