Bangka Park is the open plaza directly in front of Longshan Temple, a gathering place that captures the texture of old Wanhua. By day it is full of elderly residents playing chess and chatting; in the evening a long ribbon of blue LED lighting, the 'Bangka Blue', is switched on along the canopy, a small public-art touch that has become a local landmark.

The park sits between the temple, the Longshan Temple metro station, and the lanes of the Herb Alley and Snake Alley night market, making it the natural pivot point for exploring the district.
