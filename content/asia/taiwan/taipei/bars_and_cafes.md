---
title: Bars and Cafes
type: section
---

Taipei has a surprisingly strong cocktail-bar scene — half a dozen Taipei bars regularly appear on Asia's 50 Best Bars list — and a deep cafe culture, particularly in **Da'an District** and around **Yongkang Street**. The most interesting drinking happens in two clusters: the speakeasy and cocktail dens of **Xinyi East District** (Anhe Road, Songren Road, the alleys behind the Eslite bookstore), and the dive-bar and craft-beer strip around the **Red House** in Ximending, with its associated LGBTQ+ scene. For a more traditional experience, the **teahouses** in Maokong on the south side of the city pour Taiwanese high-mountain oolongs in pavilions overlooking the city. Karaoke (**KTV**) is taken extremely seriously in Taipei and is best experienced at one of the giant Cashbox or Holiday chain venues in Zhongshan or Ximen.
