Beitou is Taipei's hot-spring district, half an hour from the city centre on the red MRT line. The Japanese built it up as a spa town in the 1900s — the **Beitou Hot Spring Museum** is housed in the original 1913 public bathhouse, and the **Plum Garden** villa nearby was the writer Yu You-jen's retreat — and the area retains a distinctly Japanese flavour, with shuttered wooden ryokan-style hotels, stone lanterns, and a continuous low smell of sulphur.

The river bed at **Hell Valley** (Geothermal Valley) steams visibly and reaches 90°C; the more attractive **Beitou Park** is a long shady strip of lawns, a stream, and the green-glazed-tile **Beitou Library**, an award-winning piece of modern wooden architecture. Public spas range from cheap segregated baths to expensive private rooms at the heritage hotels.
