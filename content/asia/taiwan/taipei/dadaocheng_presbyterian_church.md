Dadaocheng Presbyterian Church is one of the older Japanese-era Presbyterian churches in Taipei, with a facade that blends Chinese details and late 19th-century Western church design. It is best visited as part of a walk through Datong and Dadaocheng, where its architecture adds another layer to the district's shophouses, temples, and trading history.
