Many of the easiest day trips from Taipei lie within the city's own metropolitan reach. [Tamsui](/asia/taiwan/tamsui), at the end of the red MRT line, is the classic riverside outing — a former foreign trading port with a lively waterfront and a famous sunset over the Taiwan Strait. [Yangmingshan National Park](/asia/taiwan/yangmingshan_national_park) covers the volcanic mountains immediately to the north, with hiking trails, craters, and spring flower meadows, while [Maokong](/asia/taiwan/maokong) in the south is a tea-growing hillside of charcoal-stove teahouses reached by gondola. **Beitou**, also to the north, adds hot-spring bathhouses to the list.

Further afield, the most popular outing runs east to [Jiufen](/asia/taiwan/jiufen), the atmospheric old gold-mining town of stepped lanes and red lanterns, and its near-neighbour **Shifen**, with its old railway line and viaduct waterfall. North along the coast lies [Keelung](/asia/taiwan/keelung), a working seaport famous for its **Miaokou Night Market** and the rock formations of nearby Heping Island, and the green-and-yellow sandstone hoodoos of **Yehliu Geopark**.
