Most of the easiest day trips from Taipei are within the city's own metropolitan boundary — **Tamsui** at the end of the red MRT line, **Yangmingshan** and **Beitou** to the north, **Maokong** in the south. Further afield, the most popular outings run east to the gold-mining village of **Jiufen** and its near-neighbour **Shifen**, with its old railway and viaduct waterfall; the seaside town of **Keelung** with the **Heping Island** rock formations; and the green-and-yellow **Yehliu Geopark** north of Taipei, with its surreal sandstone formations.
