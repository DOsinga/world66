Hu Shih Memorial Hall is a small museum devoted to the scholar, philosopher, and diplomat Hu Shih. It sits on the Academia Sinica campus and is best for visitors with an interest in modern Chinese intellectual history.

The collection includes personal material connected with Hu Shih and exhibits that place his work in context. English audio guidance makes the visit easier than many small specialist museums in Taipei.
