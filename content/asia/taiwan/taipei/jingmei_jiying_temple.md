Jingmei Jiying Temple is a designated historic monument in southern Taipei, honouring Zhang Xun and Xu Yuan from the An Shi rebellion. The Qing-dynasty temple architecture is the reason to come, especially if you are already exploring Jingmei or Wenshan.
