A 90-minute trip out of Taipei to the northeast coast brings you to Jiufen, a former gold-mining town clinging to a steep hillside above the Pacific. The narrow stepped lanes — barely wide enough for two people abreast — are lined with red lanterns, tea-houses, and snack stalls; the views down to the Keelung coast are spectacular, and the **A-Mei Tea House** at the top of the stairs is one of the most-photographed buildings in Taiwan.

The town is at its most atmospheric just before dusk in the rainy season, when mist rolls in off the Pacific and the lanterns light up. It is also extremely popular: weekends are a slow shuffle through dense crowds. Go on a weekday if possible. By the time the last tour buses leave at around 9pm Jiufen becomes much quieter and is at its best. Bus 1062 from Taipei MRT Zhongxiao Fuxing station gets there in 90 minutes.
