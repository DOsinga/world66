Lingnan Fine Arts Museum focuses on Lingnan painting, a southern Chinese school known for bright colour, natural subjects, and a mix of Chinese and Western techniques. The museum is small, but it gives Taipei a specialist art stop beyond the better-known contemporary and national collections.

It is best for visitors with a specific interest in Chinese painting traditions or those already exploring eastern Taipei.
