Millennium Hot Spring is Beitou's public outdoor bath, a cheap and very local way to try the hot springs without booking a private room. It is mixed-gender and swimwear is required, with several pools at different temperatures.

The rules are posted in English and enforced briskly, so read them before getting in. Bring your own swimsuit and towel if you can; buying them on site is possible but not ideal.
