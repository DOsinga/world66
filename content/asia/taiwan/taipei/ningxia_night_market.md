title: Ningxia Night Market
type: poi
---

Ningxia is the smallest and most local-feeling of Taipei's old central night markets — a single block-long line of food stalls set up nightly in the middle of Ningxia Road, with covered seating at the kerbside. Foreign tourists turn up but are a minority; the crowd is mostly residents of the old quarter doing dinner.

The market is particularly strong on traditional snacks: sticky rice with peanut and pork floss, taro ball soup, oyster vermicelli, sweet potato balls. **Lai Ji Tang** at the south end has been serving the same braised pork belly rice bowl for over forty years and rarely has fewer than ten people waiting outside. Open from 5 pm; busiest 7-10 pm.
