Qidong Street Japanese Houses is a short row of restored wooden houses from Taiwan's Japanese colonial period. The low tiled roofs, timber frames, and quiet lanes make it a useful stop for seeing a domestic side of early-20th-century Taipei, especially when paired with nearby Huashan 1914 Creative Park.
