The Shung Ye Museum of Formosan Aborigines sits near the National Palace Museum and gives useful context on Taiwan's Indigenous peoples. Exhibits cover material culture, daily life, ritual, and contemporary identity, with English support good enough for non-Chinese speakers.

It works well as a quieter companion to the National Palace Museum, especially if you want Taiwan's history to include more than imperial Chinese art.
