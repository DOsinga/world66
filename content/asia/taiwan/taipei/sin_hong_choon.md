Sin Hong Choon is a former tea factory and storefront from 1934, built in a mix of Taiwanese, Japanese, and western styles. It now works as a small museum on Taipei's tea trade and the production of Taiwanese tea.

The building fits naturally into a walk around Datong and Dihua Street. Upstairs, the cafe offers introductory tea tastings.
