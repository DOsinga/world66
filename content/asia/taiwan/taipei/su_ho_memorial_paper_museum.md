The Su Ho Memorial Paper Museum is a small, hands-on museum about paper and papermaking, founded in memory of Chen Su-ho, one of Taiwan's last master papermakers. Exhibits explain the craft, but the real draw is the chance to make a sheet of paper yourself.

It works especially well with children or anyone interested in traditional crafts. The museum sits in central Taipei, so it is easy to pair with nearby galleries, cafes, or Huashan 1914 Creative Park.
