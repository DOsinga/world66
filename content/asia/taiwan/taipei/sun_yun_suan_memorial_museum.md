The Sun Yun-suan Memorial Museum is a small museum dedicated to Sun Yun-suan, the former Premier of the Republic of China. Its displays include diaries, manuscripts, photographs, and personal objects that trace his public life and his role in Taiwan's late-20th-century development.

It is a compact stop rather than a major museum, but it fits well with a walk through Dadaocheng and Datong's older streets.
