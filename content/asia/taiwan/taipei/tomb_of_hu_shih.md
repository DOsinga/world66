The Tomb of Hu Shih is a quiet memorial to one of modern China's best-known writers and public thinkers. Hu Shih was a philosopher, essayist and diplomat whose work helped shape the May Fourth Movement and the wider push for a modern written Chinese.

The site is modest, but it gives a useful point of contact with a major intellectual figure in 20th-century Chinese history.
