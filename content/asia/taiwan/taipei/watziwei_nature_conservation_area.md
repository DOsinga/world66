Watziwei Nature Conservation Area protects a small mangrove wetland near the Bali Left Bank. A viewing trail lets visitors get close to the mangroves without entering the protected habitat.

It pairs well with a riverside walk or bike ride along the Tamsui River, especially for birdlife and a quieter look at the estuary landscape.
