---
title: When to Go
type: section
---

Taipei's climate is subtropical and notably wet, with rain or drizzle possible at almost any time of year. The best months for visiting are **October to early December**, when the worst of the summer humidity is past, typhoon season is winding down, and temperatures sit in a pleasant 20-25°C range. **March to May** is the other good window — wet but mild, with the Yangmingshan cherry and calla-lily blossom peaking around late February.

**Summer** (June to September) is hot, humid, and frequently disrupted by **typhoons**, which can shut down the city for a day or two; the rains in June (the *meiyu* season) can last continuously for a week. **Winter** is cold by Taiwanese standards — 12-18°C, rainy and damp — but never freezing. Crowds at the major sights peak around Chinese New Year and the late-October Pride parade; otherwise Taipei is comfortable year-round.
