Xingtian Temple is a busy modern temple dedicated to Guangong, the deified general from the Three Kingdoms period. Worshippers come to pray, leave simple offerings of flowers, fruit and tea, and consult oracle blocks. The passages around Minquan and Songjiang roads are often busy with fortune-tellers drawn by the temple crowds.
