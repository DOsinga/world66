Yangmingshan is a substantial volcanic national park covering the mountains immediately north of Taipei. The landscape is dominated by **Mt. Cising** (1,120 m), a dormant volcano whose summit and crater are reachable by a popular two-hour hike; lower down are fumaroles, sulphur vents, and steaming hot-spring streams. The whole park is criss-crossed by good walking trails.

The park is at its busiest from late February to March, when the **cherry blossom** and later the white **calla lily** of Zhuzihu come into bloom and turn the meadows white and pink for a fortnight. Bus 260 from Taipei Main Station climbs into the park; for the higher trails take a connecting minibus from the visitor centre. Bring a layer — the temperature at the summit can be 10°C below central Taipei.
