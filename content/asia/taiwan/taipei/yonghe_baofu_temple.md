Yonghe Baofu Temple is an old Baosheng Dadi temple just south of central Taipei, built in 1822 for communities with roots in Quanzhou. It is a quieter religious stop than the famous Wanhua temples, but the history helps explain Yonghe's older Chinese migrant ties.
