Zhishanyan Cultural and Ecological Garden covers Zhishan Hill, one of Taiwan's oldest archaeological excavation sites. Trails climb through a small wooded hill south of Tianmu, passing rock layers, old settlement traces, and quiet viewpoints that make it a good add-on to a Shilin or National Palace Museum day.
