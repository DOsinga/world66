The Daxi CKS Residence is a Japanese-era residence later associated with Chiang Kai-shek. Chiang used it as a retreat, and King Hussein of Jordan stayed here during a visit with him.

The house is a modest but useful add-on to Daxi Old Street and Cihu Memorial Park, giving more context to the Chiang Kai-shek sites around Taoyuan. It is best visited as part of a broader Daxi history walk.
