Window on China Theme Park combines miniature landmark models with amusement rides in the Longtan area of Taoyuan. The model zones are the distinctive part, turning famous buildings and landscapes into a walk-through miniature geography lesson.

It is a family-oriented day out rather than a core cultural sight, and it sits well outside central Taoyuan. Travelers with children may find it useful alongside other Longtan and Daxi stops; others can treat it as optional.
