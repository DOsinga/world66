Yingge Rock, or Parrot Rock, is the outcrop that gives Yingge its name. A short trail climbs to the rock from several entrances and gives views over the town below.

The walk is brief, around 20 minutes on the main route, so it is easy to pair with Yingge's ceramics streets and museums. Go in clear weather, when the viewpoint makes the small climb worthwhile.
