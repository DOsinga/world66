Buluowan is a broad terrace above the Liwu River in Taroko Gorge, a useful pause between the sheer marble walls and the higher mountain road. It offers short walks, gorge viewpoints, and displays connected with Truku culture, so it gives more context than a quick roadside photo stop.

The area works well for travellers who want an easier walk than the gorge’s steeper trails. Conditions in Taroko can change after typhoons and earthquakes, so check current access before building a day around it.
