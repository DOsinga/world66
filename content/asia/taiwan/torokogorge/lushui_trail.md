Lushui Trail is one of Taroko Gorge's more accessible short walks, following a historic route above the Liwu River through forest, cliffside sections, and old road cuts. It gives a taste of the gorge's scale without requiring a long hike or a permit.

Trail access in Taroko changes after storms and earthquakes, and the park sometimes opens only short segments. Check the national park status before setting out, then treat whatever section is open as a compact introduction to the gorge's geology and vegetation rather than a major trek.
