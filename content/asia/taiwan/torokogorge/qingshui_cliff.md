Qingshui Cliff is one of Taiwan's most dramatic coastal landscapes, where the mountains drop almost straight into the Pacific. The cliff line runs for more than 20 km, with heights that average around 800 m above the sea.

It is usually visited from viewpoints along the coastal highway north of Taroko Gorge. Go in clear weather if you can, when the blue water and marble cliffs are visible together.
