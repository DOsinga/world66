Sanjhan is a river-tracing spot near Taroko, where clear water, boulders, and narrow channels make a more hands-on outing than the park's regular walking trails. Simple routes can be done with rented gear, while waterfall rappels and harder sections are best left to local guides.

Go only when conditions are safe. Heavy rain can change these streams quickly, and the appeal is the water itself, not a marked viewpoint.
