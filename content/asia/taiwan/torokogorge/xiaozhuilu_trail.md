Xiaozhuilu Trail is a short but sharp introduction to Taroko Gorge terrain, starting near the Shakadang Tunnel by the visitor centre. Its steps, boardwalks, and forested slopes give a quick taste of the gorge's vertical drama before linking naturally with the Shakadang area.

Check current trail status before setting out, as Taroko routes can close after earthquakes, typhoons, or rockfall. When open, it is a useful warm-up walk for travellers who want more than a roadside viewpoint but less than a full-day hike.
