Zhuilu Old Trail is one of Taroko Gorge's classic hikes, climbing from near Swallow Grotto through forest to a narrow cliff section high above the gorge. The exposed ledge gives sweeping views over the marble canyon, but the route is strenuous and usually requires advance permission.
