Da-xi Honeymoon Bay is a coastal stop north of Toucheng, known for surfing and open views toward Guishan Island. It has hosted surfing competitions, but it also works as a simple scenic stop along the coast.

The bay is best in clear weather, when the island is visible offshore and the beach gives a quieter counterpoint to the busier Wushi Harbor and Waiao Beach area.
