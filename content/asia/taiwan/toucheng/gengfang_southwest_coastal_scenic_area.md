Gengfang Southwest Coastal Scenic Area is a quiet stretch of coast near Toucheng with rocky shorelines, walking paths, and open sea views. It is especially good near sunset and works well as a slower stop after Waiao Beach, Wushi Harbor, or Lanyang Museum.
