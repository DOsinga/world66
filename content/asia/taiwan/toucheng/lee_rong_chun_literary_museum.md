The Lee Rong-chun Literary Museum is a small museum dedicated to Toucheng's literary history. It is housed in a heritage-listed Japanese-style building, which is as much a reason to visit as the exhibits themselves.

It pairs well with a walk through Toucheng Old Street. Expect a quiet local museum rather than a major collection, but the building gives a clear feel for the town's Japanese-era layers.
