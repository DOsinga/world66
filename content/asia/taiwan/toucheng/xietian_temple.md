Xietian Temple is a historic Taoist temple in Toucheng dedicated to Guan Yu. Its traditional architecture and active worship make it a calm, atmospheric stop between the old street, Wushi Harbor, and the coast.

Visit respectfully during the day, when you can see the carved details, incense, and daily rituals without needing to plan around a formal tour.
