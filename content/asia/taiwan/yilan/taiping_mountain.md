Taiping Mountain is one of Taiwan's major forest recreation areas, set about 1,950 m above sea level in the mountains of Yilan County. The area is known for cool air, dense forest, mountain views, and old forestry heritage.

It is a strong day trip for travellers who want nature rather than another town stop, though the mountain roads mean it needs more planning than sights in central Yilan.
