Yilan Brick Kiln is a preserved industrial site from the city's brick-making era. It began as the Chen He Cheng Kiln Factory and later operated as Yilan Brick Kiln before production ended in the 1980s.

The appeal is mainly architectural and historical: old kiln structures, brickwork, and the reuse of a small factory landscape. It pairs naturally with Yilan's cluster of museums and restored cultural buildings.
