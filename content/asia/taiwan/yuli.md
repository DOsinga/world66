Yuli is a small township in Taiwan's East Rift Valley, where rice fields sit between the Central Range and the Coastal Range. It is not flashy, and that is part of the appeal: you come here for a slower east-coast day built around cycling, water, and mountain edges.

The easy classic is the [Yufu Bikeway](/asia/taiwan/yuli/yufu_bikeway), a former railway line that crosses the meeting point of the Eurasian and Philippine Sea plates. For a bigger day, head toward Yushan National Park and walk the lower [Walami Trail](/asia/taiwan/yuli/walami_trail), with forest, suspension bridges, and the old Batongguan route into the mountains.

Yuli also has the kind of local reward that makes staying overnight feel sensible rather than dutiful. [Antong Hot Springs](/asia/taiwan/yuli/antong_hot_springs) is close enough for an evening soak, and the town's noodles, pomelos, and quiet station streets make it a good base for the southern Hualien valley.
