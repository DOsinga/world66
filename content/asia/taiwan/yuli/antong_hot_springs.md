Antong Hot Springs sits southeast of Yuli on the Antong River, where clear alkaline water rises hot and faintly sulphurous. It has been known since the Japanese period, and today you can choose between simple public-style bathing and more formal hot-spring inns.

The springs are best as a late-afternoon or evening finish to a Yuli day. After the bikeway or the Walami Trail, the location makes perfect sense.
