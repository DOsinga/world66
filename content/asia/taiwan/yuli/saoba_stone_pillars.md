The Saoba Stone Pillars are two prehistoric standing stones in the East Rift Valley, linked to the Beinan culture and dated to around 3,000 years ago. They are a short, quiet archaeological stop rather than a major site, but the setting makes them worthwhile if you are already travelling through the valley.
