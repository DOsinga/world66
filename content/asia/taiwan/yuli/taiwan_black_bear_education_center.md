The Taiwan Black Bear Education Center is a small volunteer-run centre in Yuli focused on the wildlife of eastern Taiwan, especially the Taiwan black bear. English material is limited, but it can be a worthwhile short stop for visitors interested in local conservation before heading into the surrounding mountains and trails.
