The Yufu Bikeway is the easy reason to stop in Yuli. The 10km route follows an old railway corridor from Yuli toward Dongli, running through open rice-field country and across the visible boundary between the Eurasian Plate and the Philippine Sea Plate.

It is not a hard ride, which is the point. Rent a bike, take it slowly, and let the valley do the work.
