The landscape around Dushanbe rewards exploration. [Hisar Fortress](/asia/tajikistan/dushanbe/hisar_fortress), 30 km west, is the most visited excursion — an 18th-century stronghold now a pleasant ruin with good views. The [Varzob Gorge](/asia/tajikistan/dushanbe/varzob_gorge) north of the city offers cooler temperatures, a Soviet-era health resort, and thermal springs. The [Norak Reservoir](/asia/tajikistan/dushanbe/norak_reservoir), 75 km east, is a striking blue lake in dry mountain scenery created by the former world's tallest dam. Longer trips to [Iskanderkul Lake](/asia/tajikistan/dushanbe/iskanderkul) and the [Fann Mountains](/asia/tajikistan/dushanbe/fann_mountains) require at least two days but are among the finest landscapes in Central Asia.
