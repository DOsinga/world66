---
title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Dushanbe's sights are concentrated but worth your time. The [National Museum](/asia/tajikistan/dushanbe/national_museum) has the finest Buddhist art in Central Asia; the [Gurminj Museum](/asia/tajikistan/dushanbe/gurminj_museum) has a remarkable collection of Central Asian instruments; the [Barakat Bazaar](/asia/tajikistan/dushanbe/barakat_bazaar) is the most vivid place in the city. The [Rokhat Teahouse](/asia/tajikistan/dushanbe/rokhat_teahouse) is an institution. The [Dushanbe Flagpole](/asia/tajikistan/dushanbe/dushanbe_flagpole) and [Ismoil Somoni Monument](/asia/tajikistan/dushanbe/somoni_monument) are the defining symbols of post-independence national identity. The city is compact enough to cover the main sights on foot in a day, leaving time to simply sit in the park or wander the bazaar.
