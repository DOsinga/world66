The Mausoleum of Sheikh Muslihiddin is part of Khujand's old religious core, close to the Friday Mosque and Panjshanbe Bazaar. Come for the quiet courtyard and the sense of how this part of the city still gathers trade, prayer, and local memory in one compact area.
