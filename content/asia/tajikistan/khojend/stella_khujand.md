Stella Khujand is an observation tower shaped after the Tajik crown symbol. Its main value is orientation: the tower is visible from much of central Khujand and gives a clear landmark for reading the city.

Stop by if you are already exploring the centre and want a quick view or photo, especially before heading toward the bazaar and fortress area.
