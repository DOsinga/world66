title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Kurgan-Tyube (now officially Bokhtar) is not a destination in itself but a useful base for two worthwhile excursions. The [Ajina-Tepe Buddhist Monastery](/asia/tajikistan/kurgantyube/ajina_tepe) ruins, 12 km east of the city, are one of the most significant Buddhist archaeological sites in Central Asia and easy to visit in a half-day. The [Tigrovaya Balka Nature Reserve](/asia/tajikistan/kurgantyube/tigrovaya_balka) to the south, one of the few remaining tugai (riverine forest) habitats in Central Asia, requires advance arrangement but rewards serious nature travellers.

The city itself has a modest [bazaar](/asia/tajikistan/kurgantyube/bokhtar_bazaar) worth an hour's browsing, particularly for its dried fruit, nuts, and cotton textiles from the surrounding agricultural plain.
