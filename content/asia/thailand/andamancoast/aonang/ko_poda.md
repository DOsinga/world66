Ko Poda is one of the classic island stops off Ao Nang, with pale sand, clear water, and a limestone stack rising offshore. It is easy to reach by longtail, which means it is rarely empty.

The island is best treated as a half-day swim and picnic stop. Bring water, respect park rules, and do not expect isolation unless you arrive outside peak boat hours.
