Kata is one of [Phuket's](/asia/thailand/andamancoast/phuket) most popular beach areas, sitting on the island's west coast between Karon to the north and Nai Harn to the south. Kata Beach is a crescent of fine sand backed by casuarina trees, with good swimming from November to April when the Andaman Sea is calm. During the monsoon season (May-October) the waves pick up and Kata becomes a decent surf spot.

The beach is divided into two: [Kata Yai (Big Kata)](/asia/thailand/andamancoast/kata/kata_yai_beach) is the main stretch, lined with restaurants and beach vendors, while [Kata Noi (Little Kata)](/asia/thailand/andamancoast/kata/kata_noi_beach) to the south is quieter and more scenic. The [Karon Viewpoint](/asia/thailand/andamancoast/kata/karon_viewpoint) above Kata Noi, looking south over three bays, is one of the most photographed spots on the island.

Kata has a good range of restaurants and bars along the main road (Patak Road) and the side streets. It is less hectic than [Patong](/asia/thailand/andamancoast/patong) but more developed than the southern beaches — a reasonable middle ground for travellers who want convenience without the chaos.
