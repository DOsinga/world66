Sivalai Beach wraps around the sandy point on Ko Muk's east side, close to the arrival pier and the village. It has the classic tropical-island shape: pale sand, shallow water, and longtail boats pulled up near the shore.

It is best for an easy walk, a swim at higher tide, or a first look at the island before heading across to Charlie Beach and the west coast.
