---
title: "Beaches"
type: section
---

Koh Phayam has the kind of white-sand beaches and live coral reefs that southern Thailand used to have before the crowds arrived. The island has no cars — only motorcycles and bicycles on narrow tracks — and no mains electricity in most areas, just small resorts and simple bungalow accommodation.

The two main beaches are [Ao Khao Kwai](/asia/thailand/andamancoast/koh_phayam/ao_khao_kwai) (Buffalo Bay) and [Ao Yai](/asia/thailand/andamancoast/koh_phayam/ao_yai) (Long Beach). Even in high season from November to April, the beaches are uncrowded, and you can usually find a stretch entirely to yourself.
