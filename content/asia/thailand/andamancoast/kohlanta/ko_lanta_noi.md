Ko Lanta Noi is the less touristed northern half of the Lanta pair, linked to Ko Lanta Yai but moving at a different pace. Instead of the long resort beaches that draw most visitors south, you find villages, rubber trees, mangroves, and everyday island life.

It is best explored by scooter or with a local driver, with no pressure to tick off a famous sight. The appeal is contrast: after a few days of beach routines on Ko Lanta Yai, Ko Lanta Noi reminds you that the district is also a lived-in Andaman community.
