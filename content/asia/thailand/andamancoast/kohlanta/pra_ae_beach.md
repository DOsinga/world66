Pra Ae, usually called Long Beach by travellers, is one of Ko Lanta's most useful beaches: broad enough for walking, gentle enough for swimming in season, and lively without feeling as compressed as some resort strips. It is the kind of beach that can carry a whole lazy day.

The mood changes from family swimming in the afternoon to low-key beach bars at sunset. It is not remote, but it is easy, pretty, and central to how many visitors experience Ko Lanta.
