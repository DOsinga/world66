Ngao Mangrove Forest Research Centre protects part of Ranong's tidal forest south of town. The boardwalk lets you step into the mangroves without hiring a boat, with roots, mud, crabs, and dense green growth doing most of the talking.

It is a quiet counterpoint to the hot springs and hills. Check locally before going, as access can change after storms or maintenance closures.
