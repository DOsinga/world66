Ranong Governor's Grave is the Chinese-style tomb of Phraya Rattanasetthi, also known as Kho Su Chiang, Ranong's first governor. The stepped stone setting, granite figures, and hillside position make it a short but interesting stop for the town's Thai-Chinese history.
