---
title: "Bars and Cafes"
type: section
---

Surin Beach is not a late-night destination. The bar scene here is low-key by design — this is one of Phuket's more composed beaches, and the atmosphere after dark reflects that. A few beachside spots serve drinks through the evening, mostly to guests from the nearby hotels and villas, and there are some decent coffee options in the area for morning starts. The main beach road is quiet after 10 pm; anyone wanting a proper night out will need to head south to Patong.
