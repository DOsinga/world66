The Ayutthaya Historical Study Centre is one of the better places to start before heading into the ruined temples. Its exhibits set the old capital in context, covering royal power, daily life, trade, art, and Ayutthaya's links with neighbouring kingdoms and foreign merchants.

The building also serves as a tourism information point, so it is useful as well as interesting. Go early in your visit and the brick stupas and palace foundations around town will make more sense.
