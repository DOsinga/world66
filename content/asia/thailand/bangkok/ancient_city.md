Ancient City, also called Ancient Siam, is an open-air museum with scaled replicas of buildings and monuments from across Thailand. The grounds are large enough that many visitors explore by bicycle, golf cart, or e-bike.

It is a good family stop and a useful overview of Thai architecture, especially if you will not be travelling widely around the country.
