Flerwows — full name Flerwows Smoothie and Flowers — is one of Ari's newer café openings, a florist-and-drinks hybrid behind a flower-covered facade on Soi Ari 1. The idea is simple and charming: order a fruit smoothie and leave with a bunch of sunflowers, surrounded by blooms the whole time.

The menu skews healthy — smoothies and light drinks rather than a full kitchen — and the whole place is built for a relaxed date or a catch-up with friends. Easy to reach near Ari BTS. Open daily into the evening.
