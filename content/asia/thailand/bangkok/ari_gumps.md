Gump's is the photogenic little community mall that captures Ari's creative, youthful energy in one courtyard. Spread over two low-rise levels on Soi Ari 4, it gathers cafés, dessert counters, bubble-tea stands, and bistros around photo-friendly corners — cherry-blossom backdrops, props, and even a sticker-photo booth and camera rental.

The ground floor leans to coffee, drinks, and sweets while restaurants cluster upstairs, and the space regularly hosts craft markets, art shows, and music nights. It's less a shopping destination than a hang-out, and one of the easiest ways to sample several Ari spots — including the soufflé-pancake favourite Fuu — in a single stop.
