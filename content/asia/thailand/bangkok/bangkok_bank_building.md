The Bangkok Bank Building is one of Chinatown's older commercial landmarks, standing opposite the Tang To Kang gold shop. Its exterior mixes early Rattanakosin style with clear European influence, a reminder of the neighbourhood's merchant wealth and international connections.

It is an exterior stop, not a museum, but it adds useful architectural texture to a walk along Yaowarat and the surrounding gold-shop streets.
