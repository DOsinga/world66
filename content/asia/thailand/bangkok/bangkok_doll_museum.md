Tucked down a hard-to-find soi east of Victory Monument, the Bangkok Doll Museum and workshop is the life's work of Khunying Tongkorn Chandavimol, who began making dolls in the 1950s after studying the craft in Japan. The cramped, atmospheric rooms display hundreds of meticulously costumed figures depicting Thai villagers, classical dancers, mythological characters, and historic court scenes.

Visitors can watch artisans at work in the adjoining workshop, where the dolls are still made and sold. It is a small, quirky, and entirely free attraction that delights anyone willing to navigate the maze of back lanes to reach it.
