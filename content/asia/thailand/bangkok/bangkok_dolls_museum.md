Bangkok Dolls Museum is a small craft museum and workshop built around handmade Thai dolls. Its collection includes traditional Thai figures, Khon-inspired dolls, rural scenes, and examples from other countries.

The appeal is the close look at a specialized local craft rather than a large museum experience. It works best as a short stop when you are already around Pratunam or Victory Monument.
