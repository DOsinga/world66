Benjakiti Park gives central Bangkok a welcome stretch of water, trees, wetlands, and skyline views just off Sukhumvit. The lake and old tobacco factory land have been turned into walking and cycling paths, with enough space to feel like a real pause from the traffic around Asok and Queen Sirikit.
