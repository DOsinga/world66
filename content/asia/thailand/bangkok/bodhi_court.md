Bodhi Court is a small but politically charged site at Thammasat University, near the old royal district. The bodhi tree here is linked to Thailand's modern democracy movements, especially the student protests of 1973 and later campus demonstrations against military rule.

It is not a grand monument, but it gives historical weight to a walk between Sanam Luang, the university, and the riverfront. For visitors interested in Thai political history, it helps connect the old city with Bangkok's more recent struggles over democracy.
