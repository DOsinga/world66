A half-Thai, half-American detective investigates murders in Bangkok in a novel that is simultaneously a crime thriller and a guide to the city's social fabric — its corruption, its Buddhism, its sex industry, and its relationship with Western values. Burdett spent years in Bangkok and writes from inside the culture without sanitising it. The first in a six-book series, and the best starting point.
