A novel that moves through several centuries of Bangkok history — from the 19th century through a near-future city half-drowned by rising sea levels. The same buildings recur, occupied by different inhabitants in different eras. An ambitious, lyrical book about a city that has always been in flux, written by a Thai-American author who knows Bangkok from the inside.
