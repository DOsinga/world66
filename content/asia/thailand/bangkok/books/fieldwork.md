A journalist in Thailand investigates the case of an American anthropologist who murdered a missionary in a hill-tribe village in the north. The novel blurs fact and fiction, and the Thailand it describes — its hill tribes, its missionaries, its expat community, and its complicated relationship with outsiders — is rendered with real insight. Won the National Book Award nomination and remains one of the best English-language novels set in Thailand.
