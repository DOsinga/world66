The Channel 7 Boxing Stadium near Victory Monument holds free Muay Thai matches that are broadcast live on Channel 7 television. The fights are genuine competitive bouts rather than tourist shows, featuring national-circuit fighters competing for rankings and prize money. Seats are usually available without advance booking.

The atmosphere is completely different from the paid tourist stadiums elsewhere in Bangkok — the crowd is Thai, the commentary is in Thai, and the betting is serious. The fights typically take place on Sunday afternoon. The stadium is a short walk or motorbike taxi ride from the Ari or Phahonyothin BTS stations. For visitors with any interest in Muay Thai as an actual sport rather than a performance, this is the most authentic and accessible Bangkok venue.
