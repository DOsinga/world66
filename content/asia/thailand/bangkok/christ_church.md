Christ Church is the historic Anglican church serving Bangkok's English-speaking community. Its roots go back to the 19th century, when British residents and missionaries began worshipping in the city, and the present church still feels like a small colonial-era pocket in busy Silom.

It is not a headline sight, but it is a worthwhile pause if you are walking between Silom, Sathorn, and Lumphini Park.
