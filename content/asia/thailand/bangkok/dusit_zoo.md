Dusit Zoo, also called Khao Din, was for nearly eighty years one of Bangkok's most-loved family outings, set in a former royal botanical garden beside the Dusit Palace. Generations of city children fed the elephants and rowed boats on its lake before the zoo closed in 2018 ahead of a planned relocation.

The site is being redeveloped, and while the animals are gone the leafy grounds remain a landmark in the district's royal precinct. Travellers should treat it as a piece of Bangkok history rather than a current attraction, and check whether any replacement facility has reopened nearby.
