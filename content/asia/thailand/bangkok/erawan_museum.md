The Erawan Museum is hard to miss: a giant three-headed elephant rises over the building and sets the tone before you enter. Inside are three levels of antiquities, religious art, and symbolic decoration collected by Lek Viriyapant, the patron behind several of Thailand's most unusual heritage projects.
