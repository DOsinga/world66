Jim Thompson was an American architect who rebuilt the Thai silk industry after World War II and disappeared without trace in Malaysia in 1967. His home — six traditional Thai houses on the edge of a khlong (canal), filled with his collection of Asian art and antiques — is one of the finest house museums in Southeast Asia. The guided tour is required for the interior and lasts about 40 minutes. The garden is peaceful even in the heat; the café serves decent coffee. Near the National Stadium BTS station.
