Kathmandu Photo Gallery is a small photography gallery in a restored Portuguese-style shophouse in Silom. It was founded by photographer Manit Sriwanichpoom and focuses on photography rather than general contemporary art.

The gallery is best treated as a short stop while walking through Silom. Check the current exhibition before going, as the appeal depends heavily on what is on display.
