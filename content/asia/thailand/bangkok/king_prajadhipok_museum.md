King Prajadhipok Museum occupies a 1906 neoclassical building near Wat Saket. Its collection follows Rama VII through a difficult turn in Thai history, from absolute monarchy to constitutional rule, with photographs, documents, personal objects, and exhibits on his abdication and later life in Britain.
