Thonburi's canal network is best experienced by bicycle. Several hire shops near the Wongwian Yai BTS station rent bikes by the half-day, and the routes along Khlong Mon and surrounding canals pass through a Bangkok that has changed little in decades: wooden houses on stilts over the water, orchid farms, small temples without tourists, fresh-water fish farms, and narrow lanes where motorcycles are the only practical transport.

The route circuits are typically 15–25km on flat ground and well within the ability of casual cyclists. A guide is useful for the first visit as the lanes are not well signposted, but several local operators offer guided rides that include lunch at a canal-side restaurant. This is Bangkok's "Venice of the East" experience as it actually exists, not as a tourism product.
