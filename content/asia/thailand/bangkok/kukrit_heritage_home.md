M.R. Kukrit's Heritage Home preserves the former residence of Kukrit Pramoj, the writer, statesman and former Thai prime minister. The compound is made up of five traditional teak houses set around a garden, with the oldest more than a century old. Come for the architecture as much as the biographical displays.
