The Kwong Siew Foundation in Bangkok's Chinatown is known for the Guang Dong Shrine, a 130-year-old Chinese shrine built as a meeting place for overseas Chinese from Guangdong. Its traditional style, imported materials, and Buddha images make it a worthwhile pause while exploring the area's temples and clan associations.
