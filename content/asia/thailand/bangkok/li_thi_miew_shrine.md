Li Thi Miew Shrine is one of Chinatown's larger Taoist shrines, but it still sees far fewer travellers than the temples on the main routes. The roof is crowded with dragon figures and smaller shrine details, while the adjoining hall is dedicated to Kuan Yin, the Goddess of Mercy.

Come while wandering the Chinatown lanes around Yaowarat. It is a working shrine, so the interest is as much in the incense, offerings, and local devotion as in the architecture.
