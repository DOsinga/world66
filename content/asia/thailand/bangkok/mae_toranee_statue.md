The Mae Toranee Statue shows the earth goddess wringing water from her hair, a Buddhist image tied to the story of the Buddha resisting Mara before enlightenment. It is a small sight, but a good pause near Banglamphu and Rattanakosin if you are interested in the symbols that keep appearing in Thai temple art.
