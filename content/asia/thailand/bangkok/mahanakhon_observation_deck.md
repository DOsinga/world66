Mahanakhon Observation Deck sits near the top of one of Bangkok's tallest skyscrapers and gives wide views over Silom, the Chao Phraya River, Lumpini Park, and the central skyline. The visit includes enclosed viewing floors and an open rooftop Skywalk with a glass-floor section high above the street.
