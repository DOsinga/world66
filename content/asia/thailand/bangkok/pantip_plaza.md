For decades Pantip Plaza was the first name in Bangkok electronics, a warren of small booths selling computers, cameras, phones, components, and software across several crowded floors. In its heyday it was as famous for bootleg discs as for legitimate gadgets, a chaotic bazaar of cables and bargaining.

The rise of online shopping has thinned the crowds, and the mall has reinvented itself with more lifestyle and gaming tenants, but it remains a useful stop for repairs, accessories, and second-hand kit. It sits within easy walking distance of the Pratunam markets.
