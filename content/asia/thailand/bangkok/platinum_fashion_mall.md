The Platinum Fashion Mall is the air-conditioned engine room of Pratunam's garment trade, hundreds of small stalls stacked over several floors selling clothing, shoes, bags, and accessories at wholesale prices. Buyers from across Thailand and neighbouring countries come to stock their own shops, and the deals get better the more you buy.

For casual visitors it is a dizzying but efficient place to grab cheap fashion in bulk without the heat and haggling of the open-air market across the road. There is a food court on the upper level when the shopping fatigue sets in.
