Poh Teck Tung Foundation is a Thai-Chinese charitable foundation in Bangkok's Chinatown, but the visitor interest is the lively Da Feng Zu Shrine inside the complex. Worshippers come to make merit, burn paper offerings, and pray for better fortune, giving the shrine a busy local rhythm.

It works best as a short stop while exploring Yaowarat and the lanes around Wat Mangkon Kamalawat. Be respectful around worshippers and funeral-related shops in the surrounding streets.
