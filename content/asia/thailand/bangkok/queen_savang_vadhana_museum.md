The Queen Savang Vadhana Museum occupies part of Sra Pathum Palace, hidden behind Siam Paragon. The palace was the residence of Queen Savang Vadhana and later Princess Srinagarindra.

The appeal is the contrast with the surrounding shopping district: a quiet royal residence and museum in one of Bangkok's busiest commercial areas. Check opening arrangements before going, as access can be limited.
