Rang Nam Road, running south from Victory Monument BTS station, is one of Bangkok's best-established restaurant streets — a 500-metre stretch of mid-range Thai restaurants that has been drawing Bangkok's professional class for decades. The speciality is boat noodles and Thai-Muslim food, with several restaurants serving southern Thai cuisine including massaman curry, roti, and grilled chicken with turmeric (khao mok gai).

The prices are moderate, the quality consistent, and the atmosphere authentically local — these are full-service restaurants with air conditioning, not street stalls. Khao man gai (steamed chicken rice) is available at multiple spots; Kua Kling Pak Sod is well known for southern Thai regional cooking. Rang Nam is busiest at lunch on weekdays and for dinner at weekends.
