Santichaiprakan Park is a small riverside pause on the Chao Phraya, with views toward Rama VIII Bridge and the old Phra Sumen Fort beside it. It is more local hangout than formal sight, but the setting makes it one of Banglamphu's easiest places to sit, watch the river traffic, and cool down between temples and Khao San Road.
