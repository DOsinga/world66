Santiphap Park (Peace Park) provides the one green breathing space in the busy commercial district around Victory Monument. The park is small — a few acres — but contains enough trees, benches, and walkways to attract office workers eating lunch and early morning joggers before the heat sets in.

The park contains a monument to the 1973 democratic uprising and several pieces of public art, giving it a mild cultural dimension beyond its function as green space. Victory Monument BTS is a short walk away; the park is sandwiched between major roads but the interior is quiet enough to provide a genuine pause. Most useful as a place to rest between activities or to eat a takeaway from the surrounding street food stalls.
