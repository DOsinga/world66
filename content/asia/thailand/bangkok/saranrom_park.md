Saranrom Park began as the garden of Saranrom Palace and still feels more composed than many Bangkok parks. Paths loop around a lake, old trees, glasshouses, and wooden pavilions, with locals jogging or playing table tennis after work. It is a useful quiet stop between the Grand Palace area and the river.
