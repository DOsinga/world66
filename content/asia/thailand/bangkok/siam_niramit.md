Siam Niramit is a large-scale theatrical show dedicated to Thai mythology, culture, and history. The 80-minute performance uses a stage said to be the world's largest, elaborate special effects, hundreds of performers, live elephants, and costume design of exceptional complexity to depict three realms of Thai mythology and scenes from traditional Thai life.

The show is unambiguously aimed at tourists rather than Thais, but the production values are high enough to justify the cost for visitors with an interest in Thai performance traditions. A Thai village recreation outside the theatre provides pre-show entertainment. The venue is near Thailand Cultural Centre MRT station, slightly east of the Victory Monument area. Evening shows run nightly; booking in advance is recommended.
