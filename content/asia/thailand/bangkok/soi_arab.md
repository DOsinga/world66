Soi Arab is the small Middle Eastern quarter around Nana, where Arabic signs, halal restaurants, perfume shops and agarwood stalls give Sukhumvit a different rhythm for a few blocks. It is best treated as a short walk rather than a formal sight: come in the evening, eat well, and browse the shops around Sukhumvit Soi 3/1.
