Song Wat Road is one of the best streets in Bangkok's Chinatown for seeing the old trading city behind the neon. A century ago this was rice-merchant territory, and the road still has worn warehouses, shophouses and business facades that feel closer to the river trade than to modern mall Bangkok.

It rewards a slow walk rather than a checklist visit. Come in daylight, wander the side lanes, and pair it with Yaowarat Road and the nearby shrines for a fuller sense of Chinatown's commercial past.
