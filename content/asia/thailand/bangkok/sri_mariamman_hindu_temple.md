Sri Mariamman Hindu Temple is one of Bangkok's most vivid religious sites, built by Tamil immigrants in the 19th century and still busy with worshippers. Its colourful gopuram, incense-filled interior, and mix of Hindu deities and local devotion make it a worthwhile stop while walking through Silom.
