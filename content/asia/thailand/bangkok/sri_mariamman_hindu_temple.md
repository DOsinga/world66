Sri Mariamman Hindu Temple brings a bright South Indian presence to Silom, with a colourful gopuram, carved deities, incense, and a steady flow of worshippers. Founded by Tamil immigrants in the 19th century, it is one of Bangkok's most distinctive small temples and easy to visit while walking the district.
