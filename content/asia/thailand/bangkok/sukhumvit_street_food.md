Sukhumvit's street food scene has shifted significantly in recent years following redevelopment along the main corridor, but the side sois retain some of Bangkok's best casual dining. The area around Thong Lo and Ekkamai BTS stations is currently the most consistent for quality — grilled pork skewers, pad kra pao (basil stir-fry), boat noodles, and som tam (green papaya salad) at stalls and shophouses that serve a mix of Thais and expats.

For the most authentic experience, walk down the numbered sois in the evening when the market stalls set up. Soi 38 once hosted Bangkok's most celebrated night market before the land was sold; its spirit has dispersed into the surrounding neighbourhood and remains very much alive.
