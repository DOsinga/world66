Thai Labour Museum covers the history of Thai workers and labour movements from early forms of forced and wage labour through modern unions and campaigns. It is housed in a former State Railway of Thailand labour union office.

The museum is modest, but it gives a useful view of Bangkok and Thailand from outside the usual palace, temple, and shopping routes. Call ahead before making a special trip, as access can depend on staff being available.
