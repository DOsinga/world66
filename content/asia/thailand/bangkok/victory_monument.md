Victory Monument is one of Bangkok's great transit crossroads, named for the soaring obelisk that rises from a roundabout where a dozen roads and bus routes converge. The monument commemorates a brief 1941 border conflict with French Indochina, and today it functions mostly as a landmark and an orientation point above the swirling traffic.

The streets around it are unpolished but rewarding for food. Minivan stands, cheap eateries, and the famous boat-noodle alley at Soi Ratchawithi draw locals and students from the nearby universities and hospitals. The BTS station overhead makes it an easy place to change direction or grab a quick, authentic meal between sights.

There is little in the way of formal sightseeing here, but for a taste of everyday working Bangkok the area is hard to beat.
