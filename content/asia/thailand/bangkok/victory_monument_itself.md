The Victory Monument is a 50-metre obelisk built in 1941 to commemorate Thai soldiers who died in the Franco-Thai War (1940-41), in which Thailand — under Field Marshal Plaek Phibunsongkhram's military government — annexed territories from French Indochina. The obelisk is surrounded by bronze figures of military personnel representing the different branches of the armed forces.

The monument sits at the centre of a large roundabout that functions as one of Bangkok's principal BTS interchange points. The historical significance is somewhat complicated by the nature of the conflict it commemorates; it is primarily interesting as a piece of mid-century Thai nationalist architecture and as an urban landmark. The BTS station surrounds it with elevated walkways that give good elevated views of the obelisk and the surrounding district.
