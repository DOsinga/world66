The streets surrounding Victory Monument BTS station form one of Bangkok's best street food concentrations, sustained by the large lunchtime and evening crowds of office workers, students, and commuters using the transit hub. The speciality is boat noodles (kuay tiao reua) — a rich, slightly herbal broth with rice noodles, pork or beef, and blood — which you can find at multiple competing stalls on the soi just west of the monument.

Other standouts include grilled pork skewer vendors, fresh coconut ice cream, papaya salad variations, and khao man gai (steamed chicken rice) at several long-running stalls. The activity peaks at lunch (11am–2pm) and again in the early evening (5pm–8pm). This is one of the parts of Bangkok where eating street food in a crowd of Thai people going about their day feels most natural.
