Wat Kanikapon is a late-19th-century temple in Bangkok's Chinatown, a short walk from Wat Mangkon Kamalawat. It is a quieter stop that adds depth to a Chinatown temple walk, especially for visitors exploring beyond the main Yaowarat route.
