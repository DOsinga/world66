Wat Thep Lila is a local Buddhist temple in the Ramkhamhaeng area, founded in the 19th century during the reign of King Rama III. It is not one of Bangkok's grand headline temples, but the active compound gives a quieter look at everyday temple life away from the old city and riverfront routes.
