Wat Thep Lila is a local Buddhist temple in the Ramkhamhaeng area, founded in the 19th century and still active with worshippers. It is not one of Bangkok's showpiece royal temples, but its large compound, Buddha images, and canal-side history make it a worthwhile neighbourhood stop if you are already nearby.
