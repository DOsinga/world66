Wat Thep Thida Ram is a Rama III-era temple that mixes a traditional Thai plan with Chinese details, including porcelain-decorated gables and stone figures around the compound. It sees far fewer visitors than nearby Wat Saket and Wat Suthat, which makes it a calmer stop in this busy part of old Bangkok.

The temple is also linked to Sunthorn Phu, one of Thailand's best-known poets, who lived here as a monk in the 1840s. His former quarters are a small point of interest for Thai literature enthusiasts.
