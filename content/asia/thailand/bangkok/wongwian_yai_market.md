The Wongwian Yai roundabout and the streets surrounding it form one of Bangkok's most authentic local market and food areas, largely untouched by tourism. The rotary itself is dominated by an equestrian statue of King Taksin, the general who reunited Thailand after the fall of Ayutthaya and founded Thonburi as his capital.

The surrounding streets host fresh food markets, cloth vendors, and street food stalls that serve Thonburi residents rather than visitors. The area around Pracha Thipatai Road in the morning is particularly busy with fresh produce. The BTS skytrain terminates here (Wongwian Yai station), making it easily accessible. The area gives a good sense of a Bangkok neighbourhood that functions on its own terms.
