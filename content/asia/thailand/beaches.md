---
title: "Beaches"
type: section
---

Thailand has beaches on two coasts and dozens of islands, and almost every type of beach experience is available somewhere. The Gulf coast runs from Cha Am and Hua Hin south through Bang Saphan to Chumphon, with Koh Samui, Koh Phangan, and Koh Tao offshore. The Andaman coast to the west has Phuket, Krabi, Koh Lanta, and the Similan Islands. Each coast has its own character, its own season, and its own crowd.
