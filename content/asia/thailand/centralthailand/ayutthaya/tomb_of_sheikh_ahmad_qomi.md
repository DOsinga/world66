The Tomb of Sheikh Ahmad Qomi marks the burial place of a Persian Shiite figure who became an important official in the Ayutthaya court under King Songtham. Its mix of Thai and Arabic calligraphy makes it a small but unusual stop for travellers interested in Ayutthaya's international history beyond the main temple ruins.
