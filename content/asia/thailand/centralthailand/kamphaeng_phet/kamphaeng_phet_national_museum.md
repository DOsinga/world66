Kamphaeng Phet National Museum is the practical context stop for the historical park. Its collection includes Buddha heads, ceramics, architectural fragments, and decorative sculpture recovered from the province's ruins.

Visit before the park if you want a clearer sense of the Sukhothai and Ayutthaya layers in the city, or afterwards if you prefer to see the ruins first and then make sense of the fragments.
