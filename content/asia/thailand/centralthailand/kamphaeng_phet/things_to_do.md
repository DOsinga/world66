Kamphaeng Phet is best treated as a cycling or slow-walking ruin day. The [historical park](/asia/thailand/centralthailand/kamphaeng_phet/kamphaeng_phet_historical_park) covers the walled town and forest monastery zones. Within it, [Wat Phra Kaeo](/asia/thailand/centralthailand/kamphaeng_phet/wat_phra_kaeo) is the central royal temple, [Wat Chang Rop](/asia/thailand/centralthailand/kamphaeng_phet/wat_chang_rop) is the great elephant-base chedi on the hill, and the [Kamphaeng Phet National Museum](/asia/thailand/centralthailand/kamphaeng_phet/kamphaeng_phet_national_museum) gives useful context before or after the ruins.
