San Phra Kan is a small but busy shrine near Phra Prang Sam Yot, with old Khmer remains and a steady crowd of macaques around the offerings stalls. It is not as architecturally strong as the three-towered ruin, but it explains why the monkeys dominate this part of town.

Watch your belongings here. The animals are used to people and move quickly when food appears.
