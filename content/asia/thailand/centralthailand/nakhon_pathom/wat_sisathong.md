Wat Sisathong is best known for its giant statue of Phra Rahu, the deity associated with darkness and eclipses. Worshippers often bring black offerings, such as black rice, black jelly, or black chicken, as part of local devotional practice.

The temple is a distinctive stop for visitors interested in living Thai religious traditions beyond the better-known chedis and palace sights around Nakhon Pathom.
