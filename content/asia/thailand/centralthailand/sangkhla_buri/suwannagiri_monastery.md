Suwannagiri Monastery is a Mon Buddhist temple near the Three Pagodas Pass area. It is associated with Luangpho Uttama and is best known for its wooden pillars and interior decoration influenced by Burmese religious art.

The monastery is most relevant for visitors already exploring the Mon cultural sites around Sangkhla Buri and the border, rather than as a stand-alone trip from farther away.
