Sawankhaworanayok National Museum is a small but worthwhile stop for travellers following the Sukhothai and Si Satchanalai temple trail. Its collections include Buddha images, sculpture, and objects once kept at Wat Sawankharam, with pieces ranging from the pre-Sukhothai period into early Ayutthaya.
