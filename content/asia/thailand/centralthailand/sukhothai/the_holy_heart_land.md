The Holy Heart Land is an unusual shrine set on an artificial heart-shaped island inside a heart-shaped water basin. The shape is clearest from above, but even at ground level it is a curious contrast to Sukhothai's older temple ruins.

It is a quick detour rather than a major sight, best for travellers who enjoy odd local landmarks.
