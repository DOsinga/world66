Wat Khao Phra Bat Noi is a small hilltop temple ruin southwest of Wat Saphan Hin. A large bell-shaped chedi anchors the site, with a smaller chedi and the remains of an assembly hall nearby. It is a quieter stop in Sukhothai's western temple zone.
