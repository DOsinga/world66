Wat Tham Hip Bon is a quiet hilltop temple site west of Sukhothai's main ruins. The remains include two chedi flanking a modest viharn, with traces of smaller structures scattered around the site.

It is a lower-key stop than Wat Saphan Hin or the central historical park, but the setting gives it appeal for visitors exploring Sukhothai's outlying temple ruins.
