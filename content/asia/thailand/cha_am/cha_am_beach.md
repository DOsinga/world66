A long, wide beach of grey-brown sand backed by casuarina trees and a road lined with seafood restaurants, deckchair rental stalls, and the occasional jet-ski tout. The water is calm and clean enough for swimming, though not the transparent turquoise of the gulf islands further south.

Weekdays are quiet — often very quiet, out of school holidays — and the beach has a pleasantly unhurried feel. Deckchair hire is cheap, the seafood vendors are good, and the absence of the crowds that descend at weekends makes it a decent base if you want a relaxed few days on the water. Avoid public holidays when Bangkok empties itself onto this stretch of coast.
