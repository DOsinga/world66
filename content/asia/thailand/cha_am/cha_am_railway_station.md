A small, slightly forlorn station on the Southern Line from Bangkok's Hua Lamphong. Not all express trains stop here — check before assuming you can use it. The station itself has a modest period charm; it was built in the 1920s when this stretch of the Southern Railway opened.

For those arriving by train, the beach is a short motorbike taxi or songthaew ride away. Tuk-tuks are available outside but agree on a price before getting in.
