---
title: "Getting Around"
type: section
---

The town is small enough to walk, and the beach is the only area most visitors need to reach. Motorbike taxis and songthaews (shared pickup trucks) cover the main beach road for a few baht. Hiring a motorbike or bicycle is easy and worth it for the 20-kilometre run south to Hua Hin along the coast road, which passes through a mix of beach resorts, royal palaces, and farmland.

For Kaeng Krachan National Park, a rental car or joining a tour from Hua Hin makes more sense than trying to use public transport, which is infrequent and doesn't reach the park entrance.
