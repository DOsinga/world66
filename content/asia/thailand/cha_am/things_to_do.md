---
title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Cha Am is essentially a beach resort with very little else to do — which is a fair description of what most people come for. The beach is the attraction, and the town functions as a quieter, cheaper alternative to Hua Hin. For activities beyond swimming and sunbathing, you'll need to head to Hua Hin or make a day trip to one of the national parks nearby.
