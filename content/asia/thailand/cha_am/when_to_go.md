---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

The best time to visit is November to April, when the Gulf of Thailand coast is dry and sunny with temperatures around 28–32°C. The monsoon runs roughly May to October, bringing rain, cloud, and enough chop to make the beach less appealing — though it never completely shuts down.

The Thai school holidays in October and April bring Bangkok families flooding to the beach; if you want quiet, avoid these periods. Chinese New Year (late January or February) is also busy. The best value weeks are in the heart of the rainy season — you take the risk of rain but often have the beach almost to yourself.
