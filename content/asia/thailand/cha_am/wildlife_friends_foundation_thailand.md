Wildlife Friends Foundation Thailand is a rescue centre inland from Cha Am, known for caring for animals taken from unsuitable captivity or trade. Visits are structured around seeing the sanctuary's work rather than performing with animals, which makes it a more responsible choice than many roadside wildlife stops.

It is a half-day commitment rather than a quick beach diversion, so check visiting arrangements before setting out. Travellers interested in animal welfare will get the most from it; those expecting a zoo-style attraction may find the tone more practical and educational.
