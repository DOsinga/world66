---
title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Chantaburi's main attraction is the riverside old town with its century-old Sino-Vietnamese shophouses and the largest Catholic church in Thailand. Beyond the centre, the surrounding province is full of waterfalls, beaches, and durian orchards. Save Friday to Sunday for the weekend gem market, when traders arrive from Cambodia and beyond to deal in raw rubies and sapphires.
