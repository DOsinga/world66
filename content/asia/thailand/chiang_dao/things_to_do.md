---
title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

The [cave complex](chiang_dao_cave) is the obvious starting point, but the surrounding mountains and jungle offer much more: a [forested temple climb](wat_tham_pha_plong), [hot springs](yang_phu_to_hot_springs), a [weekly hill tribe market](tuesday_market), and some of Thailand's [best birding](birding). [Doi Luang Chiang Dao](doi_luang_chiang_dao) itself is a serious mountain — guides are required for the summit, and many people do it as an overnight trek to catch the sunrise. [Pha Daeng National Park](pha_daeng_national_park) to the north adds waterfalls and Yunnanese villages to the mix.
