Asia Scenic Thai Cooking School has been running since 2007 and offers two distinct experiences: a standard class at its old town location near the north moat, and a farm-based class at a rural property in San Sai district about 20 minutes outside the city. Both formats begin with a market visit. The farm option is particularly praised for its scenery — cooking in an open sala surrounded by vegetable gardens. Evening classes are available at the old town venue. Run by Gayle and family; instruction is patient and unhurried.
