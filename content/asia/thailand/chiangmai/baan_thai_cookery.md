Baan Thai Cookery School occupies a traditional house near Wat Phra Singh, one of the most convenient locations for visitors staying in or near the old city. Morning sessions run 09:30–14:00 and evening sessions 16:00–20:30, both starting with a guided walk through Sompet Market. You choose five dishes from the menu, which covers Thai staples alongside a few northern specialties. Vegetarian and gluten-free options are fully accommodated. Hotel pickup and drop-off is included. One of the better-value mid-range schools in the city, with consistent recent reviews.
