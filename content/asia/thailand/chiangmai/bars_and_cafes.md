---
title: Bars and Cafes
type: section
---

Chiang Mai has a relaxed bar scene — nothing like Bangkok's intensity, but with plenty of good options. The old city has a cluster of backpacker bars near Ratchaphakhinai Road, anchored by [Zoe in Yellow](/asia/thailand/chiangmai/zoe_in_yellow), which fills up every evening with live music and cheap drinks.

The Ping River riverside strip is a more grown-up alternative, with [Good View](/asia/thailand/chiangmai/good_view_bar) drawing a mixed Thai and foreign crowd for live music and cold beers. The Riverside Restaurant nearby is another long-standing favourite.

For coffee, [Wawee Coffee](/asia/thailand/chiangmai/wawee_coffee) uses locally grown northern Thai arabica beans and has several branches across the city. The Nimman Road area has a high density of specialty coffee shops catering to the city's large digital nomad population — many of them in pleasant shaded settings with reliable wifi.
