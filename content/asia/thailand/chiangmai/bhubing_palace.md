Bhubing Palace sits in the cooler hills above Chiang Mai, near the Doi Suthep route. The royal residence itself is not the main point for visitors; the landscaped gardens, mountain air, and seasonal flowers are what make it a worthwhile stop.

Dress respectfully and check opening rules before going, since royal use can affect access. It combines naturally with Wat Phra That Doi Suthep, giving the standard mountain excursion a quieter garden-focused second act.
