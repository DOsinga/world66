Chiang Mai Thai Cookery School has been running since 1993, making it one of the oldest continuously operating schools in Thailand. It offers more flexibility than most — half-day, full-day, and multi-day courses are available, and the curriculum goes deeper into technique than the typical tourist class. The school teaches why a dish works, not just what goes in it: how to balance the four flavors (sweet, sour, salty, spicy), how heat changes coconut milk, why order of addition matters. Longer courses cover regional variations across Thailand. The facilities are comfortable and well-equipped. A good choice if you're serious about learning to cook Thai food at home.
