Chiang Mai is one of the best places in the world to learn Thai cooking. The city has dozens of cooking schools ranging from quick half-day introductions to full-day immersions that begin at a local market and end with you eating the four or five dishes you cooked. Most classes are small groups — typically six to twelve people — so instructors can give individual attention.

The standard format follows a reliable arc: a tuk-tuk or songthaew ride to a local market where your teacher explains ingredients, followed by three to five recipes prepared in a traditional open-air kitchen, followed by eating everything. Common dishes include pad thai, green and red curries, tom kha gai, massaman curry, and mango sticky rice. More specialized courses focus on northern Thai cuisine — khao soi, sai oua sausage, nam prik — which differs considerably from the central Thai food most visitors know.

Half-day courses typically run three to four hours and cover three dishes; full-day courses run five to six hours and include five or more dishes plus a market visit. Prices range from around 800 to 1,500 THB. Most schools provide recipe booklets to take home, and many include a farm visit where you see ingredients growing.

Book a day or two ahead during high season (November–February); walk-ins are often possible in low season.
