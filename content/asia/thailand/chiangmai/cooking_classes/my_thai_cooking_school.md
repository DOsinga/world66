My Thai Cooking School sits just east of the old city moat, making it one of the most conveniently located schools in Chiang Mai. Classes begin with a guided walk through the nearby market where your teacher explains the role of each ingredient — galangal versus ginger, the different basils, how to read a good kaffir lime leaf. Back in the kitchen, you prepare five dishes chosen from a menu that includes both everyday Thai staples and a few northern specialties. The class size rarely exceeds ten. Recipe cards are included, and the teachers are patient with questions. A reliable and consistent choice in the mid-price range.
