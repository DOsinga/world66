The Ping River shaped Chiang Mai's history as its main trade and transport artery, and a cruise aboard one of these traditional flat-bottomed scorpion-tailed boats (their raised sterns curve like a scorpion's tail) is one of the quieter ways to see the city from a different angle. The ninety-minute cruise drifts past riverside temples, colonial-era trading houses, and old wooden buildings whose origins a guide explains along the way. The trip ends at the Scorpion Tailed Boat Village, where mango sticky rice is served and local crafts and herbs are available. Hotel pickup and drop-off is included. The operator is based at Faham, southeast of the Night Bazaar.
