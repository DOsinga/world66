The Doi Pui Nature Trail is one of the higher, greener hiking options near Chiang Mai, taking visitors into the forested slopes above the city. It offers a cooler, more natural counterpoint to temple-hopping and old-city sightseeing.

Conditions, trail clarity, and transport should be checked before setting out, especially in wet weather. For active travelers, the trail helps make Doi Suthep-Pui feel like a mountain landscape rather than only a temple viewpoint.
