Elephant Jungle Sanctuary operates multiple camps in the hills north of Chiang Mai, all within 60 km of the city. The experience is similar across locations: no riding, no hooks, interaction through feeding and walking alongside the elephants in their forested habitat. Morning sessions run 06:30–12:30, afternoon sessions 13:30–18:30. The full-day visit includes two Thai buffet meals. Hotel pickup and elephant food are included in the price. A solid mid-price alternative to Elephant Nature Park for visitors who couldn't secure a reservation at ENP.
