Elephant Nature Park is the best-known and most respected elephant sanctuary in Thailand. Founded in 1995 by Sangduen "Lek" Chailert, the park houses over 100 rescued elephants on a 250-acre property in the Mae Taeng valley, about 60 km north of Chiang Mai. No riding, no shows, no bathing on command — elephants live in family groups and interact with visitors on their own terms.

Several day programmes are available: half-day walks, full-day visits with elephant feeding and walking, and more immersive overnight or week-long volunteer programmes. Prices range from 2,500 THB for a half-day to 6,000 THB for premium full-day programmes. The park books out four or more weeks ahead in high season; reserve early.
