title: Elephant Sanctuary Visits

Northern Thailand has more domesticated elephants than anywhere else in the country, most of them retired from the logging industry that was banned in 1989. Without tourism income, many of these animals would have no means of support. The sanctuaries around Chiang Mai offer visitors a way to contribute directly to elephant welfare while spending time with the animals at close range.

The ethical distinction matters here: look for sanctuaries that prohibit riding, chains, and performance tricks. At responsible sanctuaries, elephants set the terms of the interaction — they approach people on their own, or don't. The experience is more observational and less predictable than a riding camp, and considerably more affecting.

Most sanctuaries are 60–80 km from the city and include hotel pickup in the price. Full-day visits run around 2,500–6,000 THB. Book at least a week ahead in high season (November–February) — Elephant Nature Park in particular sells out weeks in advance.
