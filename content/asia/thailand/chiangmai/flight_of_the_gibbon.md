Flight of the Gibbon operates in the Doi Suthep-Pui National Park forest east of the city in Mae On district. The course runs 5+ km across 33 platforms connected by zip lines (including one 850-metre line), rope bridges, and abseiling sections. The round trip from Chiang Mai takes about 60–90 minutes each way; the full experience including transfers runs around eight hours. Price includes hotel pickup, all safety equipment, and a Thai lunch. The booking website has changed over the years — book through the official site or a reputable agent to avoid outdated listings.
