Gap's School of Thai Culinary Art is run by Jareonporn (Gap) Boontha, who grew up cooking in a traditional northern Thai household. The school is intentionally small and the atmosphere is relaxed. Classes begin with a morning market visit, then move to hands-on preparation of seven or eight dishes — a longer menu than most schools. The real draw is the northern Thai focus: khao soi, sai oua (herbed northern sausage), nam prik ong (tomato and minced pork dip), and other dishes that differ markedly from central Thai cooking. Fresh coconut milk is used throughout; no cartons. Consistently strong recent reviews.
