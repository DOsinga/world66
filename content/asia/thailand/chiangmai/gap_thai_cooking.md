Gap's Thai Culinary Art School is run by Jareonporn (Gap) Boontha, who grew up cooking in a traditional northern Thai household. The school is intentionally small — usually fewer than eight people — and the atmosphere is relaxed and homey. The real draw is the northern Thai menu: khao soi (Chiang Mai's signature curry noodle soup), sai oua (the herbed northern sausage), nam prik ong (tomato and minced pork dip), and other dishes rarely taught at larger schools. If you've eaten your way around Chiang Mai and want to understand what makes the food here distinct from Bangkok or the south, this is the right class to take.
