The mountains within a few hours of Chiang Mai are home to six main hill tribe groups — Karen, Hmong, Akha, Lahu, Lisu, and Yao — each with distinct languages, dress, and traditions. Overnight treks combining hiking with village homestays are one of the classic northern Thailand experiences, and the region around Chiang Dao, Mae Hong Son, and the Pai area offers the most varied terrain.

Two-day, one-night treks typically cost 3,500–5,500 THB and include all meals, guides, transport, and a night in a village guesthouse or homestay. Most tours include bamboo rafting on a section of river and an elephant encounter at a responsible sanctuary. Treks depart daily from Chiang Mai throughout the cool and shoulder seasons; fewer operators run during the hot season (March–May) and the wettest weeks of the rainy season (August–September).

The ethical dimension matters: choose operators who employ hill tribe members as guides and contribute visibly to the communities they visit. Ask specifically where the money goes before booking.
