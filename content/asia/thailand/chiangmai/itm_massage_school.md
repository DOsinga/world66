ITM (International Training Massage) is one of Chiang Mai's most internationally recognised massage schools, with NCBTMB continuing education credits accepted by professional bodies in the US and elsewhere. Courses are taught in English and run at a range of levels: a three-day introduction (5,500 THB), five-day foundation courses (7,000 THB each), specialist modules in foot reflexology and herbal compress, and a full eleven-week professional certification programme (92,000 THB). Located near the north gate of the old city. No prior experience required for introductory courses.
