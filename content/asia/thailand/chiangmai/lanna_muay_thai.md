Lanna Muay Thai, founded by Andy Thompson, is widely considered one of the most established foreigner-friendly Muay Thai gyms in Thailand. Located near the Nimman area west of the old city, it runs group classes Monday to Saturday with morning sessions at 08:00 and afternoon sessions at 16:00. Single drop-in sessions cost 400 THB; a full day (both sessions) is 700 THB; monthly packages with twice-daily training are 8,800 THB. On-site accommodation is available in the adjacent guesthouse, and the gym supports students seeking education visas for extended stays. A women-only class runs on Saturday mornings.
