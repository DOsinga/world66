The Monk's Trail is the forest path from the Chiang Mai University side of town up to Wat Pha Lat, with a steeper continuation toward Wat Phra That Doi Suthep. The first section is the classic walk: shaded, moderately easy, and ending at one of Chiang Mai's most atmospheric hillside temples.
