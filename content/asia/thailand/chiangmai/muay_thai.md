Chiang Mai has a concentration of well-regarded Muay Thai gyms that welcome foreigners at all levels, from complete beginners who want a single session to serious practitioners who train twice a day for weeks or months. The training follows the traditional format: skipping, shadowboxing, bag work, pad work with a trainer, and optional sparring for those who want it.

Single sessions cost around 400 THB; weekly packages run 2,000–4,000 THB depending on session frequency; monthly packages are 6,000–12,000 THB. Most gyms offer accommodation nearby and can arrange education visas for long-stay students. Training typically takes place twice daily — morning around 08:00–10:00 and afternoon around 16:00–18:00, Monday to Saturday.
