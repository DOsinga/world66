The Old Medicine Hospital, also known as Shivagakomarpaj, was established in 1962 and is widely regarded as the origin point of the formalised northern-style Thai massage tradition. Located near the south gate of the old city on Wua Lai Road, it offers courses at multiple levels: a one-day drop-in (2,000 THB, Monday to Wednesday only), two-day foot reflexology (3,500 THB), five-day foundation courses (6,500 THB), and advanced therapeutic courses (7,000 THB). Textbooks and certificates are included. Courses run Monday to Friday, 09:00–16:00. A gold standard for students who want historical credibility in their certification.
