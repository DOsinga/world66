Piroon Nantaya's trekking operation has been running for decades and is one of the more trusted ethical operators in the city. Treks head northwest of Chiang Mai toward the Pai road, passing through Karen, Lahu, and other hill tribe villages. The standard two-day programme includes hotel pickup, all meals, a night in a hill tribe village, bamboo rafting on a section of river, and an elephant encounter at a responsible sanctuary. The company employs hill tribe members directly as guides. Prices vary by group size — private treks run slightly higher than group rates. Longer three- and four-day options are available.
