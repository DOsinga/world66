Santai Muay Thai has been operating since 2002 and has a reputation for teaching solid fundamentals rather than rushing students toward sparring. Training runs twice daily, Monday to Saturday. Weekly packages start around 4,000 THB; monthly packages range from 10,000 to 12,000 THB and include accommodation options at the adjacent Baan Nak Muay guesthouse. The gym welcomes complete beginners and fighters training for competition equally. Known for patient, technically rigorous instruction.
