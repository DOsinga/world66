Thai Akha Kitchen was named the world's best cooking school in 2023 and has maintained exceptional reviews since. The school is rooted in the Akha hill tribe culinary tradition and teaches eleven dishes per session — considerably more than the typical five or six. Morning classes run 09:00–15:00 and include an optional separate market tour at Chiang Mai Gate. Evening classes run 17:00–21:00. Every dish on the menu can be adapted for vegan, vegetarian, or pescatarian diets without compromising on flavour. The school sits a short tuk-tuk ride south of the old city wall in the Hai Ya neighbourhood.
