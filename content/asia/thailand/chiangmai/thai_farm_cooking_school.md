Thai Farm Cooking School is one of Chiang Mai's most distinctive schools, located on a working organic farm about 17 km north of the old city. The experience goes a step further than most: after the market visit, you spend time in the farm's gardens identifying and harvesting the herbs, vegetables, and aromatics you'll use to cook. Classes run five hours and cover five dishes. The rural setting — open-air salas amid paddy fields and bamboo groves — makes the cooking feel grounded in something real. Groups are kept small. Highly recommended for anyone who wants more than just a cooking demo.
