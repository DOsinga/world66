Thai Farm Cooking School operates on a working organic farm about 17 km north of the old city. After a morning market visit, you spend time in the farm's gardens identifying and harvesting the herbs, vegetables, and aromatics you'll use — a step that most city-based schools skip. Classes cover five dishes at individual cooking stations in an open-air kitchen amid rice paddies and bamboo groves. Hotel pickup is included within the service zone. One of the most reviewed cooking schools in Chiang Mai, operating since 2001.
