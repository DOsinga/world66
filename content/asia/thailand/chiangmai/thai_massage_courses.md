Chiang Mai is one of the best places in the world to learn traditional Thai massage. The city has several internationally accredited schools offering structured courses ranging from a one-day introduction to full professional certification programmes. Northern-style Thai massage (Nuad Boran) originated in the Chiang Mai region and differs from Bangkok-style massage in its emphasis on pressure points along energy lines (sen) rather than full-body stretching.

Introductory courses typically run two to three days and cost 3,500–5,500 THB. Foundation-level courses (five days) run around 6,500–7,000 THB. Full professional programmes qualifying students for work abroad can take ten days to several weeks and cost 15,000–90,000 THB. Most schools issue internationally recognised certificates and some offer continuing education credits for practitioners from other countries. No prior experience is needed for introductory courses.
