title: Things to do
type: section
---

Chiang Mai rewards slow visitors. The city is compact enough to explore on foot or by bicycle, but the surrounding mountains, valleys, and villages give it an outward reach that can fill weeks. The old city moat frames the historic core — temples, markets, and narrow lanes — while the Nimman area to the west has the cafes, galleries, and independent shops. Beyond the city, the hills hold elephant sanctuaries, trekking routes, zip-line courses, and the sacred peak of Doi Suthep.

The most popular bookable activities — cooking classes, elephant sanctuary visits, Thai massage courses, and Muay Thai training — all have dedicated operators listed here. For the temples, markets, and natural sights, most are free or very cheap and need no booking.
