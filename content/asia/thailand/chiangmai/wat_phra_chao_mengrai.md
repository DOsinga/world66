Wat Phra Chao Mengrai is a quiet temple in Chiang Mai's old city, close enough to the main temple circuit but usually much calmer. Its two wihan halls have a weathered, gently crumbling look that gives the compound more atmosphere than polish.

One hall holds the Phra Buddha Rupa Phra Chao Mengrai image, the reason to seek the temple out. It is best treated as a short stop while walking between the better-known old-city sights.
