Wat Ram Poeng, formally known as Wat Tapotaram, sits southwest of the old city in a quiet, forested compound and has been one of Thailand's most respected centres for vipassana meditation since the 1970s. It was here that Ajahn Tong Sirimangalo — one of the most influential meditation masters in the Mahasi Sayadaw tradition — established his teaching lineage, which has since spread internationally.

The Northern Insight Meditation Centre (NIMC) attached to the wat offers structured residential retreats open to foreigners with no prior meditation experience. The standard programmes are 10 days and 26 days. Retreats follow a rigorous schedule: 4 am wake-up, alternating sitting and walking meditation throughout the day, a noon meal as the last food, and silence maintained throughout. Instruction is in Thai with translation available in English.

The wat itself is a working monastery with resident monks, traditional Lanna-style buildings, and extensive grounds with tall trees — more serene than the tourist-heavy temples inside the moat. Outside of retreat periods, the grounds are open to visitors. Day visitors should dress modestly and behave respectfully around the monastic community.
