Zabb E Lee is one of the better-value options in Chiang Mai, housed in a traditional wooden Thai house a short walk from the old city moat. The half-day format covers four dishes — typically a soup, a curry, a stir-fry, and a dessert — with instruction that is patient and hands-on rather than demonstration-based. Class sizes are small and the pace is unhurried. There's no market visit included at this price, but the teacher explains ingredient sourcing as part of the lesson. A sensible choice if time or budget is limited, or if you've done the market experience elsewhere and just want focused kitchen time.
