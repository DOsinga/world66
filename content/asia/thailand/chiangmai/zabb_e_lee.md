Zabb E Lee Thai Cooking School operates on a small farm on the northern edge of the city, giving it a greener and quieter setting than most urban schools. Morning classes run from around 09:00 to 14:00 and include hotel pickup. The curriculum covers a standard range of Thai dishes with a relaxed, hands-on approach. Recent reviews praise the instruction and the garden surroundings. Active and well-reviewed through 2026.
