The mountains east of Chiang Mai, particularly around Mae On district, offer some of the best zip-lining in Southeast Asia. Full-day excursions run 60–90 minutes from the city and include multiple lines through forested canopy, suspension bridges, and abseiling sections. Prices typically run 3,500–4,500 THB and include hotel pickup, safety equipment, and a meal. Most operators are suitable for ages 5 and up (weight restrictions apply). Book ahead during peak season — the better-known operators sell out days in advance.
