The Hilltribe Museum and Education Centre introduces the cultures of northern Thailand's hill-tribe communities through displays on houses, tools, clothing, farming, hunting, and daily life. The presentation is modest, but it gives useful context for visitors before taking trips into the surrounding hills.

It is most worthwhile if you want background on the region's communities rather than just a quick photo stop.
