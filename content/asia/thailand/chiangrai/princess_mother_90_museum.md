Princess Mother '90 Museum is a pavilion devoted to Srinagarindra, the mother of King Bhumibol Adulyadej and grandmother of King Vajiralongkorn. The displays mix biography with craft collections, including lacquerware, pottery, weaving equipment, and old folded manuscripts.
