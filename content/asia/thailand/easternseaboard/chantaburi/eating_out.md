---
title: "Eating Out"
type: section
---

Chantaburi eats unusually well for its size. The food is a hybrid of central Thai, Chinese, and Vietnamese, with abundant seafood from the nearby coast and a famous local noodle — Senchan rice noodles — that goes into pad senchan, the signature dish. The waterfront community is full of small cafés and traditional Thai restaurants; the fruit markets in season are an attraction in their own right.
