Wat Phlup is a historic temple north of Chanthaburi's central riverside area, remembered locally for its association with King Taksin during the struggle to recover Siam after Ayutthaya fell. That connection gives the temple more weight than its modest scale might first suggest.

The visit is quiet and local, with old religious buildings, shade, and a slower rhythm than the cathedral-and-waterfront circuit in town. It is most worthwhile for travellers already following Chanthaburi's Taksin story through the city.
