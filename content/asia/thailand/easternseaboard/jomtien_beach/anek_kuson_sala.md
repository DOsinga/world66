Anek Kuson Sala, also known as Wihan Sian, is a Chinese-style art gallery and museum near Wat Yansangwararam. Built in 1987 to mark King Bhumibol's 60th birthday, it displays Chinese antiques, religious images, pottery, paintings, a golden throne, and replicas of Qin dynasty terracotta soldiers. It pairs naturally with the nearby temple complex.
