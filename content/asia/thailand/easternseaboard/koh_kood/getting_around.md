---
title: 'Getting Around'
type: section
---

A single paved road runs roughly north–south along the spine of the island, with short spurs to the beaches and fishing villages. Most visitors get around on a **rented scooter or motorbike** (around 200–300 baht a day), which is the cheapest and most flexible option. The road has plenty of steep, sandy bends — wear closed shoes and ride within your skill.

If you don't want to ride, **songthaews** (shared pickup taxis) run a loose schedule between the main beaches, and most resorts will arrange airport-style transfers to the waterfalls or fishing villages for a small fee. Distances are short — even the southern tip of the island is only about 40 minutes by road from Klong Chao.
