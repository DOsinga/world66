---
title: 'When to Go'
type: section
---

Koh Kood has a strongly seasonal climate. The reliable months are **November through April**: dry, hot, blue skies, and calm seas. December and January are peak — busiest, most expensive, but also the most pleasant weather.

From **May to October** the southwest monsoon brings heavy rain, choppy seas, and rough crossings from the mainland. Many of the smaller resorts close completely from June to mid-October, and the day boats to Koh Rang stop running. The island is much quieter and much cheaper in these months, but a fair number of days will be wet and the sea is often not swimmable. September is the worst month for weather.
