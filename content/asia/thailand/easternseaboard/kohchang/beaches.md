---
title: "Beaches"
type: section
---

Unlike Phuket or Samui, Koh Chang still has a slightly rougher edge — the interior is mountainous jungle and the roads are steep and winding, and the beaches reflect that range.

The west coast has most of the developed beaches. [White Sand Beach](/asia/thailand/easternseaboard/kohchang/white_sand_beach) (Hat Sai Khao) is the longest and busiest, with the widest range of restaurants and bars. Lonely Beach, further south, draws a younger crowd with cheaper accommodation and a relaxed backpacker scene. Klong Prao, in between, is quieter and good for families, with a long stretch of sand broken by a small estuary.

The east coast and southern tip are far less visited. Wai Check Beach and Long Beach are reachable by boat or a rough trail and reward the effort with near-solitude. Bang Bao, at the southern end, is a fishing village built on stilts over the water — the pier is lined with seafood restaurants and dive shops.
