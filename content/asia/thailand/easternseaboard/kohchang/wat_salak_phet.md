Wat Salak Phet is the village temple of Ban Salak Phet, on Koh Chang's quieter southeastern side. The older temple is connected with King Rama V's visits to the island and now works more like a small local museum, while the newer temple nearby keeps the religious life of the village going.

It is not one of Thailand's grand temple sights, but it gives this end of the island a bit of history and local texture. Combine it with the mangroves, fishing villages, and views around Salak Phet Bay.
