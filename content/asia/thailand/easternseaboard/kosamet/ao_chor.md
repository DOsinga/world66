Ao Chor is one of Ko Samet's smaller east-coast beaches, tucked between busier stretches and usually calmer than the island's main nightlife sands. The bay has enough places to eat or rent a chair, but it still feels more relaxed than Sai Kaew.

It suits travellers who want an easy swim and a slower beach day without being completely isolated. Walk the coast path in either direction to compare the neighbouring bays, then return for sunset colour on the water and a quieter dinner by the sand.
