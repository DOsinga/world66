Ao Thian is one of Ko Samet's smaller east-coast coves, sitting south of the better-known Ao Phai and Ao Wong Duean. The beach is broken by rocks and little headlands, which makes it less straightforward for swimming but more interesting if you want a quieter place to sit and watch the water.

It suits travellers who have already seen the main beaches and want to drift farther down the island. Check conditions before swimming, and bring what you need; services are thinner than on the central resort strips.
