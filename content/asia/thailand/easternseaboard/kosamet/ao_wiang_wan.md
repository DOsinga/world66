Ao Wiang Wan is one of the calmer bays on Ko Samet, away from the busiest cluster around Hat Sai Kaew and Ao Phai. The sand-and-rock shoreline is less of a party beach and more of a place to swim, read, and let the island slow down a little.

Facilities are limited compared with the better-known beaches, which is part of the appeal and part of the catch. Bring what you need for a few hours, and do not expect the same choice of food or transport at night that you get on the main east-coast strip.
