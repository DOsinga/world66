Ao Wong Deuan is one of Ko Samet's larger east-coast beaches, a broad crescent of sand south of Ao Chor and north of Ao Thian. It has more shade and more services than the smaller coves nearby, with restaurants, beach chairs, and easy boat access from the mainland.

That access is both its strength and its drawback. The bay is scenic and convenient, but ferries and day-trippers make it less hushed than the island's quieter corners. It works well if you want a proper beach day without feeling cut off from food, boats, and simple comforts.
