Art in Paradise is a 3D trick-art museum, part of a chain that started in South Korea, filled with large-scale illusion paintings designed to be photographed with people posing inside them — a shark bursting through a wall, a tightrope over a canyon, a giant hand reaching into the room. There is no message beyond entertainment; the whole point is the photo.

Working through the galleries and finding the right angle for each scene takes an hour or more. It suits families, groups, and anyone looking for an air-conditioned break from the heat, and it needs less advance planning than most of Pattaya's other attractions.
