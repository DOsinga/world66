Ban Sukhawadee is one of Pattaya's stranger architectural spectacles: a pastel-pink and blue seaside mansion complex built with unabashed confidence. It mixes grand halls, statues, gardens, and decorative excess in a way that is more showpiece than museum.

Do not come expecting subtlety. The pleasure is in the theatrical scale, the sea views, and the oddness of a private dream turned visitor attraction. It pairs well with Pattaya's other eccentric sights, especially if you have had your fill of beaches and want something visually loud.
