Crescent Moon Beach is a small northern Pattaya beach near the Sanctuary of Truth, away from the busiest central seafront. It offers a more contained bay setting, with views back toward headlands and resort buildings.

The beach is useful if you want a quieter coastal pause while visiting the Sanctuary of Truth or exploring Naklua. It is not remote or untouched, but it gives a different Pattaya mood from the long, crowded main beach.
