Million Years Stone Park and Crocodile Farm combines two very different attractions on one site: a landscaped garden of fossilised wood and unusually shaped stones collected over decades, and one of Thailand's larger crocodile farms, with several daily crocodile-wrestling and animal shows.

It is a fuller outing than most Pattaya stops — allow two to three hours to see the gardens and catch a show. As with any animal-performance venue in Thailand, the ethics are worth thinking about before buying a ticket; some visitors choose to see the stone gardens only and skip the shows.
