Wong Phra Chan Beach is a small, quieter strip of sand on the north side of Pattaya Bay. It is not the place for Pattaya's loudest scene; the draw is a calmer shoreline, a short walk, and a bit of space when the central beaches feel too busy.

Come for a low-key break rather than a full beach day. The setting is best in the morning or late afternoon, when the heat and glare ease off.
