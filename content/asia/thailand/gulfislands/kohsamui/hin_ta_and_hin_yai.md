Hin Ta and Hin Yai, also known as Grandfather and Grandmother Rocks, are two famous rock formations at the southern end of Lamai Beach, between Lamai and Hua Thanon. The suggestive shapes have spawned their own local legend, and the formations are a well-known Koh Samui photo stop, with sea views and small stalls nearby.
