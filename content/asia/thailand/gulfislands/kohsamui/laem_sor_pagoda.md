Laem Sor Pagoda stands near the sea at the quiet southern end of Ko Samui. Its yellow-tiled chedi catches the light like gold, and the setting is much calmer than the island's better-known north coast temples.

It is a useful stop when exploring the south coast by scooter or car, especially if you want a temple visit without the crowds around Big Buddha.
