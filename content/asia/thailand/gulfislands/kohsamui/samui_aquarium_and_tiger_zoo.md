Samui Aquarium and Tiger Zoo is a family-oriented attraction on the south coast, with tropical fish, turtles, coral displays, and large cats. It is mainly of interest to visitors looking for an easy indoor-and-outdoor stop away from the beach; check current conditions before going, as animal attractions can change over time.
