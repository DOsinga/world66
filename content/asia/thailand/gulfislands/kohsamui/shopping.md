---
title: "Shopping"
type: section
---

Koh Samui's shopping is spread across its main beach towns rather than concentrated in one place. **Bophut Plaza**, which opens around 6pm at Bophut Beach, is the best general market for souvenirs — fake designer goods, handicrafts, and cheap clothing, all open to bargaining. Chaweng's beach road has a similar strip of stalls, and the fresh market at Lamai is worth a look for local produce and food alongside the usual souvenirs.

For anything more conventional, Chaweng has both a **Lotus's** and a **Big C**, the two big malls on the island, with clothing, electronics, and a scattering of restaurants and a cinema inside. Bophut's old town has the more upmarket end of things — boutiques like **Siddharta** sell beachwear and dresses, and Chaweng's Beach Road has a handful of fashion boutiques as well.

Jewellery shoppers have real choice: **My Jewel International** in Nathon is good value, **Classic Gems** near Chaweng's Centara resort has a large selection and does custom pieces, and **Silvermania** at Lamai specialises in silverware. For handicrafts, **About Arts and Crafts** on Chonwithee Road in Nathon sells handmade ceramics and watercolours from a small shopfront café.
