Wat Khunaram is one of Koh Samui's best-known temples, famous for the mummified body of Luang Pho Daeng, a revered former abbot and meditation master. The monk sits upright in a glass case, with offerings placed nearby by local worshippers.

The visit is short, but memorable. Dress modestly and treat the shrine as an active place of worship, not just a curiosity.
