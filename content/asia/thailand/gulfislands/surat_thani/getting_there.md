---
title: "Getting There"
type: section
---

Surat Thani Airport is served by domestic flights from Bangkok on budget carriers such as Thai AirAsia and Nok Air.

Surat Thani also has a rail station with frequent trains north to Chumphon, Prachuap Khiri Khan, [Hua Hin](/asia/thailand/royalcoast/huahin), and Bangkok. Trains also run south to [Hat Yai](/asia/thailand/deepsouth/hatyai) and onward to Malaysia.

Surat Thani sits on the main north-south highway, with a further road branching off to Krabi and Phuket on the Andaman coast.
