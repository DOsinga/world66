Wat Tham Singkhorn is an old cave temple in the countryside outside Surat Thani, with Buddha images set inside a limestone chamber behind the main temple buildings. It is a quiet stop rather than a major sight, but the mix of shrine, cave, and rural setting makes it worthwhile if you have your own transport.
