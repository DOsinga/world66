Khu Khut Waterfowl Park protects part of the wetland system around Songkhla Lake, a landscape of reeds, lotus, open water, and seasonal birdlife. It is a useful nature break from Hat Yai, especially for travelers interested in wetlands rather than malls and markets.

The experience depends heavily on season, water level, and whether you arrange a boat at the right time of day. Early morning is best for birds and light; midday heat can make the reserve feel flat.
