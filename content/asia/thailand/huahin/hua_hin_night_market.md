The Hua Hin Night Market (Dechanuchit Night Market) is the social heart of the town after dark — a sprawling collection of food stalls, clothing vendors, craft sellers, and occasional live music that fills several blocks near the town centre each evening. It is where locals shop and tourists eat, and the food options are among the best in the region.

Grilled seafood is the main attraction: whole fish, tiger prawns, squid, and crab grilled over charcoal and served with Thai dipping sauces. There are also pad thai stalls, mango sticky rice vendors, fresh fruit stands, and a full range of Thai-Muslim snacks from the south. Prices are lower than in the restaurants and the quality is often higher. The market runs from around 5pm daily and is busiest from 7pm to 10pm.
