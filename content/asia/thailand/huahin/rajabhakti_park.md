Rajabhakti Park is a monumental open space south of Hua Hin, dominated by enormous bronze statues of seven Thai kings. The scale is the point: the figures stand in a formal line against the hills, turning dynastic history into a patriotic landscape.

The park is exposed and best visited outside the harshest midday heat. It is a strong visual stop, especially if you are interested in how modern Thailand presents royal history, but it is more memorial plaza than leafy park.
