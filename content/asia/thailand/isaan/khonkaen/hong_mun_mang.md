Hong Mun Mang is a small city museum near Bueng Kaen Nakhon. Its displays focus on Khon Kaen's local history, Isan identity, and the everyday culture of the province rather than blockbuster artefacts.

It is worth pairing with a walk around the lake. The museum helps connect modern Khon Kaen with the older villages, crafts, and migration stories that shaped the city.
