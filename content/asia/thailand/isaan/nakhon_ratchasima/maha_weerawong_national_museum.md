Maha Weerawong National Museum is small enough to see without museum fatigue. Its collection is strongest on Buddha images and religious objects from the region, with a few oddities connected to royal visits to Korat.

Go here after the Ya Mo shrine, not as a stand-alone reason to cross town. It gives helpful context for the temple art and Khmer traces you will see elsewhere in Nakhon Ratchasima province.
