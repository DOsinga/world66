Luang Por Phra Sai is the revered bronze and gold Buddha image housed at Wat Po Chai in Nong Khai. Local tradition links it to three images cast for the daughters of King Setthathirath of Laos, later moved during the wars between Siam and Laos in the 1820s.

The image is an important local pilgrimage focus and is especially prominent during Songkran on 13 April. For visitors, Wat Po Chai is the right place to understand the story and see the image in its temple setting.
