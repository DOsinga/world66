Roi Et National Museum is a modest but useful stop if you want more than temple photos. Its displays cover archaeological finds, local history, textiles, and everyday culture from the province.

It is best paired with a walk around Bueng Phlan Chai and the nearby civic centre. Roi Et is a quiet city, and the museum helps explain the rice fields, Buddhist traditions, and craft villages around it.
