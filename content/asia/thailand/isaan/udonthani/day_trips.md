The area around Udon Thani rewards a day or two of exploration. [Ban Chiang](/asia/thailand/isaan/udonthani/ban_chiang_national_museum), 56 kilometres to the east, is the obvious first choice — the museum and excavation site make for a half-day trip, with time left over for lunch in the village. Northwest of the city, [Phu Phra Bat Historical Park](/asia/thailand/isaan/udonthani/phu_phra_bat_historical_park) combines strange sandstone formations with ancient Buddhist carvings, and sits close enough to the Nam Som Forest Park to combine both in a single outing. The [Red Lotus Sea](/asia/thailand/isaan/udonthani/red_lotus_lake) at Kumphawapi, 45 km south, is unmissable from December to February. Nong Khai on the Lao border is an hour up the road. None of these destinations is well served by public transport, so hiring a songthaew, motorbike taxi, or car for the day is the practical approach.
