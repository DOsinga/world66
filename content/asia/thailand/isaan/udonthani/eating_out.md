---
title: "Eating Out"
type: section
---

Udon Thani is one of the best places in Thailand to eat Isan cooking — the spicy, fermented, grilled cuisine of the Lao-influenced northeast. The unmissable dishes are **som tam** (papaya salad), **gai yang** (grilled marinated chicken), **larb** (chopped meat salad with herbs), **nam tok** (a similar salad of grilled beef), and **sai krok Isan** (the local fermented pork sausage).

Eat at the night-time stalls around Nong Prachak Lake, in the central market, and along Pho Si Road in the evenings. For sit-down meals, **VT Namnueng** is a famous local Vietnamese restaurant chain that originated in Udon — the do-it-yourself spring rolls of grilled pork sausage and herbs are the signature dish. **Rabieng Patchanee** is the reliable go-to for upmarket Thai cooking with a quieter setting.
