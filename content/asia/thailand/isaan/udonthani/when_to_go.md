---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

Udon Thani has the three classic Isan seasons: **hot** (March to May, with temperatures regularly above 35 C), **wet** (June to October, when afternoon thunderstorms are common but rarely all-day), and **cool** (November to February, when daytime temperatures of 25–30 C and cool evenings make this the comfortable time to visit).

The cool season is also when the **Red Lotus Sea** is in bloom — December to February is the only time to see it. The Buddhist festival of **Asalha Puja** in July and the **Songkran** new year in mid-April are the city's biggest popular events. Pick December for the best combination of weather, lotus flowers, and clear roads.
