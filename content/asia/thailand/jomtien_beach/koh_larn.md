Koh Larn — Coral Island — is the obvious day trip from Jomtien: a small offshore island, 7 km across, with several beaches considerably whiter and clearer than anything on the mainland. **Tien Beach** and **Samae Beach** are the most popular; **Nual Beach** on the south side is quieter.

Speedboats run from Bali Hai Pier in Pattaya and take 15 minutes (around 300 baht round trip); the slower public ferry takes 45 minutes and costs only 30 baht. On the island you can rent a scooter or motorbike-taxi between the beaches, snorkel from the boats, and eat at the open-air seafood restaurants. Day-trippers leave on the late-afternoon boats; if you can, stay a night for the quieter morning beaches.
