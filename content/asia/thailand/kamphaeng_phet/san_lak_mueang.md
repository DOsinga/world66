San Lak Mueang is Kamphaeng Phet's city pillar shrine, a small but meaningful stop near the historic core. Locals regard it as the symbolic navel of the city and the home of its guardian spirits, so it gives a quick glimpse of the beliefs that still sit alongside the old Sukhothai-era ruins.
