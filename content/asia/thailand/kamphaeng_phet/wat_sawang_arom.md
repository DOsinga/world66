Wat Sawang Arom is a small old temple near Kamphaeng Phet with a three-metre Chiang Saen-style Buddha image. The statue is shown in the earth-touching posture, the classic moment of the Buddha calling the earth to witness before enlightenment.

It is quieter than the major ruins in the historical park, but worth adding if you are already temple-hopping around town.
