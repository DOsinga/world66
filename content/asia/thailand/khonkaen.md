Khon Kaen is one of the main cities of Isan, Thailand's northeast: large, educated, practical, and much less arranged around foreign visitors than Chiang Mai or the southern beaches. It is a transport hub, university town, medical centre, and provincial capital all at once.

The city will not overwhelm you with famous sights, but it is a good place to understand modern Isan. Around [Bueng Kaen Nakhon](/asia/thailand/khonkaen/bueng_kaen_nakhon), locals jog, eat, pray, and meet friends in the evening. Nearby [Phra Mahathat Kaen Nakhon](/asia/thailand/khonkaen/phra_mahathat_kaen_nakhon) rises in nine tiers above Wat Nong Waeng, giving Khon Kaen its most recognisable skyline.

Come for regional food, the [Khon Kaen National Museum](/asia/thailand/khonkaen/khon_kaen_national_museum), and access to the wider northeast. It makes a useful day trip from [Mahasarakham](/asia/thailand/mahasarakham), but it also rewards a night or two if you want to eat well and see a Thai city going about its own business.
