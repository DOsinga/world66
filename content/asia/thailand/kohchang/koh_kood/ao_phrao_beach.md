Ao Phrao means "Coconut Bay" and the name is honest — the back of the beach is a working coconut grove. The southernmost of the big west-coast beaches, it is less developed than Klong Chao or Bang Bao and the slower pace shows: a couple of low-key resorts, a few wooden restaurants on stilts at the south end, and a long stretch of mostly empty sand in between.

The water gets deep more quickly here than on Bang Bao, which makes the swimming better. Worth riding down for a long lunch and an afternoon in a hammock.
