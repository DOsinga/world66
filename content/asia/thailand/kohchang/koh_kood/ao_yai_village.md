The southern counterpart to Ao Salad — a stilt village in a sheltered cove on the southeast coast, less visited and quieter. Most of the boardwalks are still residential, but a few houses double as small seafood places and at least one as a homestay.

Worth the longer ride down to the southern end of the island: the road there passes through the most untouched stretch of forest on Koh Kood, and the village itself is one of the few places where the day-to-day life of the island still has nothing to do with tourism.
