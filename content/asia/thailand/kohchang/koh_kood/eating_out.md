---
title: 'Eating Out'
type: section
---

Koh Kood's food scene is split between resort restaurants on the west-coast beaches and the simpler — and usually better — Thai kitchens in the fishing villages on the east side and along the road through the interior.

For the freshest seafood, head to **Ao Salad** in the north or **Ao Yai** in the south: both villages have a handful of family-run wooden restaurants built out over the water, with crab, squid and whatever was landed that morning at fair prices. The west-coast resorts will do you a beach barbecue and a respectable green curry but rarely surprise. Vegetarians and vegans should know that almost any Thai dish on the island can be made without fish sauce if asked, but it is worth asking.
