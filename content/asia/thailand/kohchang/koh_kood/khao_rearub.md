Khao Rearub is a rock formation reached from a hiking route between Klong Chao and Ngamkho Bay on Ko Kood. The formation is locally meaningful as a religious site, and the walk gives a different view of the island away from the beaches.

It is a modest stop, but worthwhile if you want a short inland walk and a bit of local landscape character. Wear shoes with grip, especially after rain.
