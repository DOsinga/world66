Smaller, quieter and arguably more atmospheric than Klong Chao, Khlong Yai Ki is a single-drop fall into a wide, deep emerald pool. The pool is large enough to swim properly, and there are big flat rocks at the edge to lay out on.

The walk in from the road is short — under ten minutes through rubber and tropical hardwood forest. It's a good morning trip from Klong Chao or Bang Bao beach: a 15-minute scooter ride and an hour swimming. Small entry fee shared with Klong Chao.
