The headline beach of Koh Kood and one of the most photographed in eastern Thailand. A wide arc of fine white sand backed by a row of coconut palms and casuarina trees, with the small Klong Chao river emptying into the sea at the southern end of the bay. The water is shallow and exceptionally clear in the dry season.

The beach is partly fronted by resorts but the sand is open to anyone. Kayaks can be rented at the river mouth, and paddling upstream half an hour brings you to the foot of Klong Chao waterfall — the most popular short trip on the island.
