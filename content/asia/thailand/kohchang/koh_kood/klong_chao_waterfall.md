The most visited waterfall on the island, a three-tier cascade dropping into a deep, cool plunge pool wide enough to swim in. King Rama V signed his name on a rock here on a visit in 1911 — the inscription is still legible at the base of the falls.

You can reach it on foot in about 20 minutes from the road, or paddle a kayak up the Klong Chao river from the river mouth at the beach (roughly 30–45 minutes). The pool is best in the rainy season when the falls are at their fullest; by the end of the dry season the flow can drop to a trickle. Small entry fee.
