---
title: 'Things to do'
type: section
---

Koh Kood is small — around 25 km long and 10 km across — and most of what there is to do involves the sea, a beach, or a short trip into the rubber-planted interior. The west coast holds the long sandy beaches that the island is famous for, and the east coast keeps a couple of working fishing villages on stilts. A single paved road runs roughly north–south and a rented scooter or motorbike is by far the easiest way to get around.

The two big freshwater waterfalls (Klong Chao and Khlong Yai Ki) are the standard inland outings, both an easy walk from the road. For divers and snorkellers, the small islands and reefs around Koh Rang to the northwest of Koh Kood are among the best in the eastern Gulf.
