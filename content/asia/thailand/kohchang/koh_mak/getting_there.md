---
title: Getting There
type: section
---

Koh Mak is reached by boat from the mainland or from neighbouring islands. There is no airport — the nearest is at [Trat](/asia/thailand/trat), about 40 km away, with a few daily flights from Bangkok.

**Speedboats** leave from Krom Luang pier in Laem Ngop near Trat. The crossing takes 45–60 minutes and costs around 450 baht. Leelawadee and Boonsiri are the main operators; both have two or three daily departures in season.

**Slow ferries** are cheaper (around 200 baht) but take three hours and run only once or twice a day. They are a relaxed way to arrive if you are not in a hurry.

There are also direct speedboat connections from Koh Chang and [Koh Kood](/asia/thailand/kohchang/koh_kood), useful if you are island-hopping. Most resorts can arrange the transfer as part of your booking.
