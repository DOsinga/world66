The waters south of Koh Mak hold the best dive sites in eastern Thailand. **Hin Rap** and **Hin Luk Bat** are pinnacle dives covered in soft coral, with schooling fusiliers, the occasional whale shark in March and April, and almost always reasonable visibility. The wreck of the **HTMS Chang**, a former US Navy landing ship deliberately sunk in 2012 as an artificial reef, sits in 30 metres of water south-east of the island and is now one of the largest diveable wrecks in the Gulf.

A few small dive shops on Koh Mak — including the long-running BB Divers and Koh Mak Divers — run two-tank trips for around 3,000 baht. Snorkellers can join the same boats for less.
