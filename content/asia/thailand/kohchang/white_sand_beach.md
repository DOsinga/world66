White Sand Beach is Ko Chang's busiest and most developed beach, the first major stretch of sand most visitors reach after arriving on the island. It has the widest choice of restaurants, bars, dive shops, and beach services.

Come here for convenience and a lively scene rather than seclusion. The sand and water are still attractive, but quieter beaches further south are better if you want a slower stay.
