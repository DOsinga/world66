Klong Chak Waterfall is one of Ko Lanta's easier inland adventures, reached by a forest path in the quieter south of the island. The walk passes rubber trees and jungle before ending at a rocky fall that is modest in the dry season and much livelier after rain.

Come for the walk as much as the water. It is a useful change of rhythm after beach days, especially if you combine it with nearby caves or the road down to Mu Ko Lanta National Park. Wear shoes with grip; the path can be muddy and uneven.
