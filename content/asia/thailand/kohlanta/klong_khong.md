Klong Khong is a long beach on Koh Lanta with a small canal and old coconut plantation running behind much of the shore. Local fishing boats are often tied up near the southern end.

The rocky reef makes swimming less reliable than at some nearby beaches, but the open west-facing shore is a good place for sunset.
