Klong Khong is a long west-coast beach backed by an old coconut plantation and a small canal running parallel to much of the sand. The southern end still has a working feel, with local fishing boats tied up near the shore.

The beach is better for slow afternoons, sunsets, and low-key bars than for polished resort scenery. Swimming depends on tide and season, but the atmosphere is one of Ko Lanta's more relaxed stretches.
