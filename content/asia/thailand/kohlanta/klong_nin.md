Klong Nin is a relaxed beach on Ko Lanta, with pale sand, coconut palms, and a quieter feel than the island's busier northern beaches. A small klong divides the beach, and the northern stretch is usually the better choice for swimming because it has fewer rocks.

It is a good beach for travelers who want a full afternoon by the water without being too far from places to eat and stay.
