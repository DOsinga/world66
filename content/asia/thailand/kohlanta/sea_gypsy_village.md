Ko Lanta's Sea Gypsy Village is associated with the Chao Leh, the maritime people who lived from fishing, boats, and seasonal movement long before resort development reached the island. The village gives a different cultural frame to a place many visitors experience only as beaches.

Visit respectfully and without treating the community as a display. The value is in understanding that Ko Lanta has a living coastal culture with its own history, beliefs, and pressures, not just a string of west-coast sunsets.
