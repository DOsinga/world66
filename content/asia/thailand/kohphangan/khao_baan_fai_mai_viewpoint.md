Khao Baan Fai Mai is a short hill walk above the north coast of Koh Phangan, with views back toward Bottle Beach. The southwest approach is the easier route, about 1.8 km with only a modest climb, making it a good viewpoint stop if you are already exploring the Bottle Beach area.
