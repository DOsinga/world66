---
title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Beyond its famous party beaches, Koh Phangan has a surprising amount to offer the curious traveller. The island's mountainous interior conceals waterfalls, jungle viewpoints, and a national park. The north coast is genuinely peaceful. A growing wellness scene — yoga retreats, meditation centres, detox programmes — has settled into the quieter western bays. Give it more than a night.
