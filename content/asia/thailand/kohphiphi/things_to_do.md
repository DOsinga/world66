title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

The main attraction on Phi Phi is the water — [snorkelling and diving](/asia/thailand/kohphiphi/diving), and island-hopping by longtail boat. On land, the hike up to the [Phi Phi Viewpoint](/asia/thailand/kohphiphi/phi_phi_viewpoint) is the one must-do. It is a steep climb, but the panorama over the isthmus and both bays is spectacular. Bring mosquito repellent and go late afternoon for the best light.

Boat trips run to [Phi Phi Leh](/asia/thailand/kohphiphi/maya_bay), where [Viking Cave](/asia/thailand/kohphiphi/viking_cave) shelters the nests of swiftlets harvested for bird's nest soup. [Maya Bay](/asia/thailand/kohphiphi/maya_bay) on the same island — the *Beach* filming location — reopened to visitors with daily limits after years of closure for reef recovery. The enclosed [Pi Leh Lagoon](/asia/thailand/kohphiphi/pi_leh_lagoon) on the same island is one of the most beautiful spots in the Andaman. For a quieter day, [Bamboo Island](/asia/thailand/kohphiphi/bamboo_island) to the north has pristine snorkelling and far fewer visitors. [Long Beach](/asia/thailand/kohphiphi/long_beach) on Phi Phi Don itself is the best choice for a calm beach day without the village crowds.
