Na Mueang Waterfall is one of Ko Samui's better inland sights, reached from the island ring road and set among forested hills. The lower waterfall is the easier stop, while the walk toward Na Mueang 2 gives a more rewarding jungle setting and views of water dropping over purple-toned rock.

It is more satisfying if you treat it as a short nature outing rather than a roadside photo stop. Wear shoes with grip, bring water, and avoid scrambling on wet rock after heavy rain.
