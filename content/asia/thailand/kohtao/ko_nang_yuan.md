Ko Nang Yuan is a privately owned island just off the northwest tip of Koh Tao, reachable by longtail boat in about 10 minutes from Mae Haad. The island's appeal is visual: three small granite outcrops connected by brilliant white sandbars, surrounded by clear turquoise water. There is a small resort on the island, and the owners charge a day-use fee and prohibit plastic bottles (you buy water on-island).

The snorkelling around Ko Nang Yuan is excellent — the Japanese Gardens site lies on the connecting channel between Koh Tao and Ko Nang Yuan. The viewpoint at the top of the largest island is worth the steep climb for a perspective shot looking down over the sandbars and the anchored longtails.
