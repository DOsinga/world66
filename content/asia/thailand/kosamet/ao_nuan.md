Ao Nuan is a small, quieter cove on Ko Samet, tucked between the busier east-coast beaches. It is best for visitors who want a more sheltered place to swim, read, and sit by the water without the full resort-strip feel.

The beach is not large, so it is more of a relaxed stop during a walk along the coast than a whole-day destination. Come for calm water and a slower mood.
