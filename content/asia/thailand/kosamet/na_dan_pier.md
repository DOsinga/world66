Na Dan is the main village on Ko Samet, clustered around the pier where ferries from Ban Phe arrive. It is the only place on the island with anything resembling a town centre: a market, a 7-Eleven, a health clinic, a police post, and several restaurants that cater to locals rather than beach tourists. Prices here are lower than at the beach and the food is better — the village noodle shops and Thai restaurants serve the same dishes at about half what you'd pay on the sand.

The village also has a small temple above the pier with a courtyard overlooking the bay. Worth a short visit in the morning before the day-trippers arrive.
