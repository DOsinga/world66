Ao Nang Landmark Night Market is the easiest evening food stop near the beach area, with grilled seafood, skewers, fruit shakes, sweets, and the usual mix of souvenir stalls. It is not a secret, but it is convenient and lively.

Use it for a casual dinner rather than a culinary pilgrimage. Prices are higher than in Krabi Town, but the choice is broad and the setting works well after sunset on Noppharat Thara.
