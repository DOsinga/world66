Wat Si Chum is one of Lampang's more distinctive temples, reflecting the city's old connections with teak, trade, and Burmese-influenced craft. Look for the carved woodwork, layered roofs, and decorative details that set it apart from the more familiar central Thai temple style.

It is a good stop because Lampang rewards this kind of slow temple-hopping. The town's appeal lies in accumulated detail: horse carts, old teak houses, quiet lanes, and monasteries that show how northern Thailand was shaped by routes running toward Burma as much as Bangkok.
