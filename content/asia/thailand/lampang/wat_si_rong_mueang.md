Wat Si Rong Mueang is one of Lampang's clearest reminders of the teak-trade era, when Burmese merchants and craftsmen shaped parts of the city. The temple was built in 1905 in Burmese style, with a wooden viharn and layered gables that stand apart from the more familiar northern Thai temple forms.

It is not a huge complex, but the woodwork rewards a slow look. Pair it with Wat Si Chum and Wat Phra Kaeo Don Tao if you want to understand why Lampang's temples feel different from Chiang Mai's.
