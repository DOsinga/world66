Ban Vichayen preserves the remains of the 17th-century residence associated with Constantine Phaulkon, the Greek adventurer who became a powerful minister at the court of King Narai. Only the outer walls and fragments of the main buildings survive, but the site adds useful context to Lopburi's Ayutthaya-period royal quarter.
