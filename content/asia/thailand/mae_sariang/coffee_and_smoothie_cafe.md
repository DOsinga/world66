A small cafe a short walk from the morning market does espresso-based coffee, fresh fruit smoothies, and simple breakfasts from mid-morning. A good place to catch up after an early market visit. The coffee is brewed from local northern Thai beans — lighter and less bitter than the robusta blends common in cheaper Thai restaurants.

Wi-Fi is available and the place is popular with backpackers doing long stays in Mae Sariang. Closes mid-afternoon.
