The country around Mae Sariang rewards a full day away from town. The Salawin River border area is the standout excursion; the national parks on either side of the valley are good for those who want wilder terrain.

**Salawin River Boat Trip**

The mighty **Salawin River** rises in the Himalayas, flows through China, and forms part of the Thailand-Burma border. It remains a great natural wonder little known to foreign tourists. If you pass through Mae Sariang, a boat trip down the Salawin should be on your list.

The trip is easily done as a day excursion. Head to [Ban Mae Sam Laep](/asia/thailand/mae_sariang/ban_mae_sam_laep), a Karen and Shan border village on the riverbank where boats depart. You can get there by local songthaew, or by hiring a car or motorbike. From the village, longtail boats are available for hire to cruise downriver through spectacular scenery into [Salawin National Park](/asia/thailand/mae_sariang/salawin_national_park).

**Mae Ngao National Park**

North of Mae Sariang, Mae Ngao National Park covers forested hills along the Yuam River valley. Less visited than Salawin, it has good birdwatching and a few marked trails. Accessible by motorbike in about 45 minutes on the road heading north toward Mae Hong Son.
