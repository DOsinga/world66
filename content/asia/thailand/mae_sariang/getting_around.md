---
title: Getting Around
type: section
---

Mae Sariang town is small enough to walk in half an hour. The temples, riverfront, and market are all within easy strolling distance of the guesthouses.

**Motorbike hire** is the most useful option for getting out of town. Several guesthouses and a few shops on the main road rent motorbikes by the day (around 200–250 THB). The road to Ban Mae Sam Laep and Salawin National Park is paved but winding — it is not suitable for inexperienced riders.

**Songthaews** (shared pickup trucks) run from the market area to outlying villages and to Ban Mae Sam Laep on no fixed schedule — they leave when full. Ask at your guesthouse for departure points and current fares.

**Bicycle hire** is sometimes available from guesthouses. The valley floor around town is flat and pleasant to cycle, but the hill roads are steep.

**Taxis and tuk-tuks** are scarce. Your guesthouse can usually arrange a car and driver for day trips.
