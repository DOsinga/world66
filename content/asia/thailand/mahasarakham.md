Mahasarakham is an Isan college town that few foreign visitors see. With seven institutions of higher learning — led by Mahasarakham University and its 30,000 students — it has the energy and cheap prices that come with a young population, without any of the tourist infrastructure. If you want to see a real central Isan town rather than a polished destination, this is a good place to do it.

The [Museum of Northeastern Isan Culture](/asia/thailand/mahasarakham/northeastern_cultural_centre) on the MSU downtown campus is worth a visit for its overview of the region's history and traditions. The university's [second campus at Khamriang](/asia/thailand/mahasarakham/msu_khamriang_campus), nine kilometres north of town, is striking in itself — modern brick buildings set against rice paddies where farmers still plough with buffalo.

The [fresh food market](/asia/thailand/mahasarakham/fresh_market_clock_tower) near the central clock tower is the best window into daily life — and turns into a busy food street at dusk. The principal city temple [Wat Mahachai](/asia/thailand/mahasarakham/wat_mahachai) holds a regionally important collection of palm-leaf manuscripts. For an outing further afield, the Dvaravati-era chedi at [Phra That Na Dun](/asia/thailand/mahasarakham/phra_that_na_dun) is the closest thing the province has to a heritage destination. The best time to visit is November through January, when the weather is cool and dry.
