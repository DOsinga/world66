---
title: "Eating Out"
type: section
---

This is a college town in Isan, which means cheap, plentiful, and unapologetically regional. You will eat well for 40 to 80 baht a meal almost anywhere; you will rarely eat anything dull. The standard Isan trio of *som tam* (papaya salad), *gai yang* (grilled chicken with sticky rice), and *laap* (a minced-meat salad with herbs, lime, roasted rice powder, and a great deal of chilli) is on every menu and gets better the further you get from the centre.

The food street that opens each evening around the central [fresh market and clock tower](/asia/thailand/mahasarakham/fresh_market_clock_tower) is the best single place to graze. The other obvious zones are the streets running directly outside the downtown MSU gate, where students keep half a dozen noodle-soup, *khao man gai*, and stir-fry stalls in business until late, and the side streets around the Khamriang campus, which run cheaper and rougher. Look for *gaeng om* (a fragrant Isan herb stew), *sai krok Isan* (fermented rice-and-pork sausages), and *neua dad diao* (sun-dried beef, deep-fried until crisp).
