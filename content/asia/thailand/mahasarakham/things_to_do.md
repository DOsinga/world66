---
title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Mahasarakham's attractions are an Isan town's: a couple of working temples in the centre, a low-key cultural museum on the university campus, a fresh market that doubles as the town's living room, and a quiet lake or two on the outskirts where students cycle on Sunday afternoons. None of this is going to wrench you off the Chiang Mai-Bangkok-Phuket loop on its own merits — but if you have ended up here for any reason, there is enough to fill a long, slow day.

The best single thing to do in town is to wander between the [central market](/asia/thailand/mahasarakham/fresh_market_clock_tower), the [Cultural Centre](/asia/thailand/mahasarakham/northeastern_cultural_centre) at the downtown MSU campus, and one of the food streets that run alongside the university gates. The most rewarding short trip out is to the Dvaravati-era [Phra That Na Dun](/asia/thailand/mahasarakham/phra_that_na_dun) chedi an hour to the south.
