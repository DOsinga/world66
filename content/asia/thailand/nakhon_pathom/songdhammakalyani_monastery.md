Songdhammakalyani Monastery is one of the most significant religious sites in Thailand for anyone interested in Buddhist women's practice. Founded by Venerable Voramai Kabilsingh in 1957, it was established specifically to support the ordination and training of bhikkhuni — fully ordained female monks — in the Theravada tradition. The monastery is about 3 kilometres east of the town centre in the Amphoe Mueang district.

The monastery grounds include a main ordination hall, meditation gardens, and accommodation for resident nuns. Visitors are welcome at appropriate times and are expected to dress modestly. The library holds texts related to early Buddhist women's communities. Voramai Kabilsingh died in 2003, but her daughter Dhammananda Bhikkhuni, a former professor at Thammasat University, continues her work and has become an internationally recognised voice for women's ordination in Theravada Buddhism.
