Suchart Subsin Shadow Puppet House is the most personal cultural stop in the city. Suchart Subsin was a major nang talung artist, and his family home now works as a small museum, workshop, and performance space for southern Thailand's shadow-puppet tradition.

The visit is simple but memorable: leather puppets, tools, family stories, and the chance to see how the figures are cut, coloured, and brought to life behind a screen. Call ahead if a performance matters to you.
