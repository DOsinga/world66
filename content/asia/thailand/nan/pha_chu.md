Pha Chu is a cliff viewpoint in Si Nan National Park, south of Nan, with forested hills and a broad sweep of mountain country below. The flagpole on the cliff is famous locally for a lanyard that runs all the way down to the foot of the hill, a small oddity that makes the stop more memorable than a simple roadside view.
