Wat Chimpli Suttahawat is a small temple on the east bank of Ko Kret, about a kilometre south of the ferry landing. Its chapel is the reason to stop: modest, well kept, and a quiet counterpoint to the busier pottery lanes.

It works best as part of a walking or cycling loop around the island rather than as a stand-alone trip.
