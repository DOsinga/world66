Wat Chimpli Suttahawat is a modest temple on Ko Kret's east bank, useful as a quiet turning point if you are walking or cycling part of the island circuit. Its small chapel is the reason to stop: simple, well kept, and much calmer than the busier pottery and food stretches near the ferry.
