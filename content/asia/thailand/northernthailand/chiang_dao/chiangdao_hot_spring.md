Chiangdao Hot Spring is a low-key bathing stop with warm pools, changing rooms, showers, toilets, and small Japanese-style garden areas. A restaurant and bamboo gazebos make it easy to linger after a soak.

The original bathing area nearby is simpler: small concrete basins fed by hot water from a pipe. It is not fancy, but it fits Chiang Dao's quiet mountain rhythm.
