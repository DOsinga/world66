Chiang Mai Historical Centre is a modern introduction to the city's history and northern Thai culture. Multimedia exhibits, models, and guided interpretation explain Chiang Mai's old city, Lanna heritage, and regional traditions.

It is a useful first stop in the Old City before visiting temples and gates, especially for travellers who want context rather than just a list of monuments. The nearby Lanna Folklife Museum makes an easy companion visit.
