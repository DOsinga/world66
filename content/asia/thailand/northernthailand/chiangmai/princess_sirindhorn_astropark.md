Princess Sirindhorn AstroPark is a modern astronomy center north of central Chiang Mai. Its planetarium uses a large full-dome screen, and the site is aimed at visitors who want a science-focused break from temples and markets.

Shows may be in Thai, so check the current program before going. Even so, it is one of the area's better family-friendly indoor attractions.
