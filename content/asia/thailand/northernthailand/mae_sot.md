**Mae Sot** sits in western Thailand, close to the Moei River and the border with Myanmar. Many people pass through on their way to Myawaddy, but the town is more than a crossing point. Its markets, food, languages, and temples all show how long this border has pulled people and goods in both directions.

The town has a strong Burmese and Karen presence, along with Thai, Chinese, and hill-tribe influences from the surrounding mountains. The central market is the easiest place to feel that mix: textiles, jade, dried goods, street food, and everyday groceries all crowd into the same lanes.

Mae Sot is not a pretty resort town, and the border can be politically tense. But it has a distinct identity, and a spare day here is better spent exploring [Wat Thai Wattanaram](/asia/thailand/northernthailand/mae_sot/wat_thai_wattanaram), the [Rim Moei Market](/asia/thailand/northernthailand/mae_sot/rim_moei_market), and the [Thai-Myanmar Friendship Bridge](/asia/thailand/northernthailand/mae_sot/thai_myanmar_friendship_bridge) than treating it as a bus stop.
