The central Burmese market is one of the best places to feel Mae Sot's mix of communities. Stalls sell textiles, dried goods, fresh produce, cooked food, and goods brought across or inspired by the Myanmar side of the border.

Go in the morning, when the market is busiest and most useful. The gem and jade shops are part of the scene, but they are not friendly ground for casual buyers.
