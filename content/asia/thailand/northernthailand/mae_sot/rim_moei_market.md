Rim Moei Market sits near the Moei River and the border crossing, with stalls selling dried foods, textiles, leather goods, small electronics, souvenirs, and imported products from both sides of the border.

It is touristy in parts, but still useful for understanding Mae Sot's trade-town role. Combine it with Wat Thai Wattanaram rather than making a special trip only to shop.
