Wat Mani Herbal Sauna is a simple local steam sauna tucked behind a temple in Mae Sot. The herbal steam rooms are small and separated by gender, and the experience is more local and practical than polished.

Bring your own towel and water, and dress modestly. It is a good stop for travelers who want a low-key public bath experience after walking around town.
