Wat Phaya Wat is one of Nan's older temple sites, known for a rectangular chedi base with Buddha images set around the structure. Its details show a mix of Lanna, Lan Xang, and local Nan styles.

It is quieter than Wat Phumin and the main museum area, but worth adding if you are tracing Nan's temple architecture rather than only seeing the headline sights.
