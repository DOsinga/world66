Wat Phra That Chang Kham Woravihan is one of Nan's most important temples, second only to Wat Phra That Chae Haeng in local status. Its name refers to the elephant figures that support the chedi, a detail that gives the temple its most memorable exterior feature.

Inside the viharn are a large seated Buddha image, faint murals, and Lanna-period religious art. It sits close to Nan National Museum and Wat Phumin, so it is easy to include on a slow walk through the old centre.
