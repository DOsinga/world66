Bo Sib Song, or Well No. 12, is a short nature stop outside Phayao with a walking trail through unusual water-hole formations. The trail leads to a cliff viewpoint over the surrounding countryside.

It is a simple outdoor detour rather than a major sight. Come for a brief walk and the view, and check locally before relying on campsite or restaurant facilities.
