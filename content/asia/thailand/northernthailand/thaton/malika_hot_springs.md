Malika Hot Springs is a small side trip east of Thaton, useful if you are lingering in the valley rather than just passing through. It is not a grand spa destination, but it adds a simple soak to a day built around the temple and river.

Check locally before going, especially outside the main travel season, as facilities and access can be variable.
