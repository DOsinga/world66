Jomtien Beach stretches for about 5km south of Pattaya's main strip, separated from it by Phra Tamnak Hill. The sand is wider and the water calmer than at central Pattaya Beach, and the crowd is smaller and less frantic. It is a genuine swimming beach, and the promenade behind it has restaurants, sun loungers, and a reasonable spread of beach bars.

Water sports — jet skis, parasailing, banana boats — are available along the northern end. The southern end, towards Dongtan Beach, is quieter. Jomtien is a good base for visitors who want beach access without being in the thick of Walking Street.
