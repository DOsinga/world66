Phang Nga Bay is one of the most dramatic seascapes in Southeast Asia: dozens of sheer limestone karst towers rise straight out of calm, shallow emerald water between [Phuket](/asia/thailand/phuket) and [Krabi](/asia/thailand/krabi). The bay is part of a national marine park, and most visitors explore it by longtail boat or sea kayak.

The most famous formation is [Khao Phing Kan](/asia/thailand/phangngabay/khao_phing_kan), better known as James Bond Island after its appearance in "The Man with the Golden Gun." It draws big crowds, but paddle past it and you will find [sea caves and hidden hongs](/asia/thailand/phangngabay/sea_kayaking) — tidal lagoons locked inside the karst towers — and mangrove channels that feel genuinely remote. The fishing village of [Ko Panyi](/asia/thailand/phangngabay/ko_panyi), built entirely on stilts, makes a popular lunch stop. The small provincial capital of Phang Nga town, set among its own limestone crags on the mainland, serves as the usual jumping-off point.
