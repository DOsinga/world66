---
title: "Things to do"
type: section
---

The **Ao Chalong Yacht Club** is the social hub of the bay, welcoming sailors of all experience levels. They run a fun race once a month — open to anyone, with a cheap entry fee that covers prizes for winning crews and a small Thai buffet afterwards. Worth knowing about even if you're not a sailor.

Chalong's main draws are practical as much as scenic. The pier is where almost all boat trips from southern Phuket begin, including dive boats to the Similans, liveaboards, and day snorkelling trips to Coral Island. The bay itself is too shallow for swimming but good for an evening walk along the waterfront.

Nearby Nakkerd Hill — technically accessible from Chalong — is the site of the **Phuket Big Buddha**, visible from most of the southern island. The drive up is steep and dramatic, and the views from the top over Ao Chalong and toward Phi Phi are among the best on the island.

The **Chalong Bay Rum Distillery** on Soi Palai offers daily tours of their small craft operation, where local sugarcane is pressed and distilled on-site. The garden bar at the end of the tour is reason enough to visit even if the process doesn't interest you.
