Wat Chalong is the most important Buddhist temple in Phuket and the most visited. Its official name is Wat Chaiyathararam, but nobody calls it that. The compound sprawls across a large area and contains multiple buildings in Thai temple architecture — gilded roofs, multi-tiered prangs, and a large pagoda that is the focal point of the site. Inside the pagoda is said to be a relic of the Buddha.

The temple is active throughout the day, with monks present, incense burning, and locals making merit alongside the tourist visitors. Despite the crowds and the market stalls outside, the atmosphere inside is genuinely religious rather than purely performative. Come early in the morning for the quietest experience. The temple hosts festivals during Makha Bucha and Visakha Bucha days that attract large numbers of Thai worshippers from across Phuket.
