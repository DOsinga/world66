The Phuket Big Buddha — officially the Great Buddha of Phuket — is a 45-metre seated Maravija Buddha statue on top of the Nakkerd Hills above Karon Beach, visible from much of southern Phuket. The hilltop position at 384 metres elevation gives sweeping views in every direction: Karon Bay to the west, Kata to the south, and Chalong Bay and the wider island to the east.

The site is a functioning Buddhist temple complex and visitors are expected to dress modestly — sarongs are available to borrow at the entrance for those in shorts or sleeveless tops. Several smaller shrines, bells, and Buddha images are arranged around the main statue. The road up from the Kata-Karon area passes through rubber plantations and is steep but manageable; songthaews and tuk-tuks make the trip from Karon for a negotiated price.
