---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

The best time to visit Karon is November to April, during the dry northeast monsoon season. The sea is calm, the sun reliable, and the beach at its most swimmable. December and January are the peak months, with the highest crowds and prices but the best weather.

May to October brings the southwest monsoon — strong winds, heavy rain (though often only in the afternoons), and rough seas with dangerous undertow. Most businesses remain open but the beach itself is less pleasant and red flags are regularly posted prohibiting swimming. Prices drop significantly and the atmosphere is quieter. Some visitors find the off-season Karon perfectly enjoyable; others prefer to visit a less exposed beach on the east coast during these months.
