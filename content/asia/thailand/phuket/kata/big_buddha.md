The Great Buddha of Phuket sits on Nakkerd Hill above Chalong, visible from Kata, Karon, and most of southern Phuket. It is one of the most-visited sights on the island — partly for the statue, partly for the panoramic view from the surrounding platform.

A six-kilometre access road climbs from the village of Karon side. Entry is free; modest dress is required (sarongs are loaned at the entrance). Best at sunset, when the marble glows pink-orange and the surrounding hills go blue. Around 15 minutes by scooter from Kata, longer by car.
