Wat Chalong is the most important Buddhist temple on Phuket — a sprawling complex 15 minutes inland from Kata, with multiple wihans, a 60-metre Phra Mahathat Chedi housing relics, and gilt and lacquer interiors throughout. The chedi is climbable; the top floor has views over the surrounding palm and rubber plantations.

The temple is busiest at weekends and during the Chinese New Year. Entry is free; covered shoulders and knees are required. Combine with the Big Buddha for an easy half-day trip from Kata.
