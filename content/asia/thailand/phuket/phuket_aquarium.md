Phuket Aquarium sits on Cape Panwa and gives a close look at the marine life of the Andaman Sea without needing to dive. The tanks cover reef fish, sea turtles, sharks, and other species found around Phuket, with enough variety to make it a useful rainy-day or family stop.

It is also tied to local marine research, so the visit has a more grounded feel than a simple tourist display. Pair it with the Cape Panwa viewpoints or a quiet southern-island drive.
