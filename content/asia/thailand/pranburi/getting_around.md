---
title: Getting Around
type: section
---

The town, railway station, river mouth, and beach are spread out, so walking is only useful once you are in one area. A bicycle works along the coast road, while a motorbike or local taxi is better for linking town, [Pran Buri Forest Park](/asia/thailand/pranburi/pran_buri_forest_park), and [Khao Kalok](/asia/thailand/pranburi/khao_kalok).
