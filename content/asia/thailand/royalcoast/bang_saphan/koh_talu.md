Koh Talu is a small island a short boat ride off Bang Saphan, home to just one simple resort and a fringing reef that makes for some of the best snorkelling on this stretch of coast. Day trips out from Bang Saphan are easy to arrange and typically include time on the reef plus the island's own quiet beach.

There's no development beyond the single resort, so it stays uncrowded even in high season.
