Cha-am Forest Park is a small coastal nature reserve just inland from the beach. It is an easy place to trade the resort strip for trees, birds, and shaded paths without leaving town.

The park is best for a short walk, birdwatching, or a quiet break with children. Wildlife sightings are never guaranteed, but the mix of woodland and open space makes it one of Cha-am's more pleasant low-key stops.
