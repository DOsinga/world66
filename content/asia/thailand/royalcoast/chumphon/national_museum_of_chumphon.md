The National Museum of Chumphon gives useful context to a province many travelers otherwise treat only as a transit point for the islands. Its galleries cover local archaeology, historic trade routes, natural environment, and the coastal communities of the upper Gulf of Thailand.

It is not a blockbuster museum, but it is a worthwhile stop if you have a spare hour in town before a ferry or train. The displays help turn Chumphon from a timetable problem into a real place with its own history and landscape.
