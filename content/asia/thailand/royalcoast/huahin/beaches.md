---
title: "Beaches"
type: section
---

Hua Hin's beach runs for several kilometres right through the centre of town, easy to reach on foot with no roads to cross. South of town, [Khao Takiab Beach](/asia/thailand/royalcoast/huahin/khao_takiab_beach) is quieter, backed by the hill and golden Buddha of [Khao Takiab](/asia/thailand/royalcoast/huahin/khao_takiab_hill). Beyond that, [Suan Son](/asia/thailand/royalcoast/huahin/suan_son_pradiphat) is lined with casuarina pines and feels almost empty on weekdays. North toward Cha-am the beach widens and turns more resort-oriented but is still pleasant for a long walk.
