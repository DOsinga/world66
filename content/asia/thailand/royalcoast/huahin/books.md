**DK Eyewitness Thailand** covers Hua Hin alongside the rest of the country, with the maps and photography the Eyewitness series is known for — a solid companion for planning a visit before you arrive.

**Thailand: A Short History** by David K. Wyatt is the standard one-volume history of the country and useful background for understanding why Hua Hin became the royal family's preferred retreat from Bangkok.
