Coco@51 sits on the seafront just north of the Hua Hin town centre, with large gardens and outdoor dining looking out over the water. The menu is mostly Thai cuisine and seafood, served at a relaxed pace with a pleasant soundtrack — a good option for an unhurried dinner away from the busier fishing pier restaurants.
