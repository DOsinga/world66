Let's Sea is a well-regarded beachfront restaurant north of the town centre, built directly onto the sand with tables that put you right at the water's edge. The menu runs from Thai favourites to international seafood dishes, and the two attached bars — the Breeze Bar and the Sands Lounge — make it as much a sunset-drinks spot as a dinner destination.
