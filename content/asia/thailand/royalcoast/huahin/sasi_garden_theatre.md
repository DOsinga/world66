Sasi Garden Theatre pairs a two-hour Thai classical dance show — masked Khon dance, sword and martial arts displays, and traditional music — with a set Thai dinner. It bills itself as Hua Hin's first dinner theatre of this kind, and it's performed nightly, making it an easy one-stop introduction to Thai performing arts for visitors who won't get to Bangkok's bigger venues.
