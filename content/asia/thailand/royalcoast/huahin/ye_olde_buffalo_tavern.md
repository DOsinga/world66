An English-style pub near the fishing pier, with flat-screen TVs for sport, a well-stocked bar, and a menu built around British comfort food — fish and chips, steaks, and spare ribs alongside a full English breakfast served from 8am. Free Wi-Fi and takeaway are available, and there are quiz nights and live music some evenings.
