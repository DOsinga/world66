---
title: Getting There
type: section
---

Trains on the southern line stop at [Pran Buri Railway Station](/asia/thailand/royalcoast/pranburi/pran_buri_railway_station), though many travellers arrive by road from [Hua Hin](/asia/thailand/royalcoast/huahin). Minivans and buses along the Bangkok-Hua Hin-Prachuap Khiri Khan corridor can drop you in town, but you will need a songthaew, taxi, or rented motorbike to reach Pak Nam Pran and the beach.
