title: Things to Do
type: section
---

Sakon Nakhon's best sights sit between the town, the lake, and the hills. [Wat Phra That Choeng Chum](/asia/thailand/sakon_nakhon/wat_phra_that_choeng_chum) is the first stop for the city's religious identity, while [Phra That Dum](/asia/thailand/sakon_nakhon/phra_that_dum) gives a quieter look at the older Khmer layer beneath Isan Buddhism.

Leave time for the landscape. [Nong Han Lake](/asia/thailand/sakon_nakhon/nong_han_lake) is central to local life, and [Wat Tham Pha Daen](/asia/thailand/sakon_nakhon/wat_tham_pha_daen) is the strong half-day excursion: a forested hill temple where the view and the carved stonework make the detour feel worthwhile.
