Khao Laem Reservoir is the landscape behind almost everything in Sangkhla Buri: the bridge, the drowned temple, the floating houses, and the wide morning views. It is artificial, but it does not feel tame.

Boat trips are the easiest way to understand the town's geography. Ask around the bridge or your guesthouse, and agree on the route and price before setting out.
