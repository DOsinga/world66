title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

The whole reason to come to Sukhothai is the [Sukhothai Historical Park](/asia/thailand/sukhothai/sukhothai_historical_park) — the moated ruined capital, declared a UNESCO World Heritage Site in 1991 along with its sister cities [Si Satchanalai](/asia/thailand/sukhothai/si_satchanalai_historical_park) and Kamphaeng Phet. Within the central walled enclosure are the most important temples: [Wat Mahathat](/asia/thailand/sukhothai/wat_mahathat), the former royal temple at the heart of the city; [Wat Si Chum](/asia/thailand/sukhothai/wat_si_chum), with its colossal seated Buddha framed by a narrow doorway; the perfectly composed [Wat Sa Si](/asia/thailand/sukhothai/wat_sa_si) on its island in a lotus pond; [Wat Sri Sawai](/asia/thailand/sukhothai/wat_sri_sawai), built on earlier Khmer foundations; the hilltop [Wat Saphan Hin](/asia/thailand/sukhothai/wat_saphan_hin) with its standing Buddha; and [Wat Phra Phai Luang](/asia/thailand/sukhothai/wat_phra_phai_luang) in the northern zone. The [Ramkhamhaeng National Museum](/asia/thailand/sukhothai/ramkhamhaeng_national_museum) by the central gate is the essential starting point. Sixty kilometres north, [Si Satchanalai](/asia/thailand/sukhothai/si_satchanalai_historical_park) is quieter and equally rewarding, with the spectacular [Wat Chang Lom](/asia/thailand/sukhothai/wat_chang_lom). The ancient hydraulic engineering of the [Saritphong Dam](/asia/thailand/sukhothai/saritphong_dam) closes off the valley to the west.
