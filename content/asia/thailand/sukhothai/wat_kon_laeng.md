Wat Kon Laeng is a small temple ruin south of Sukhothai Historical Park's Namo Gate. Its brick remains include a main chedi on a raised base, a vihara, and smaller chedis.

It is not one of Sukhothai's headline monuments, but it works well as a quieter stop while exploring the ruins south of the old city walls.
