Wat Si Phichit Kirati Kanlayaram is one of the quieter temple ruins in the Sukhothai area, useful for travellers who want to move beyond the most photographed monuments. Its remains are more modest than Wat Mahathat or Wat Si Chum, but they still belong to the wider sacred landscape of the old capital.

The site rewards the unhurried temple circuit. After the headline ruins, places like this show how broad Sukhothai's religious geography once was, with smaller monasteries and satellite temples spreading through fields, ponds, and old settlement zones.
