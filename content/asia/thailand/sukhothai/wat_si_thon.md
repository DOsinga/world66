Wat Si Thon is a ruined temple west of Sukhothai's old city, between Wat Tuek and the Or Gate. The remains include a mandapa and a vihara, with traces of the Buddha image that once stood there.

It is a quieter stop for visitors exploring the western temple ruins beyond the main historical park circuit.
