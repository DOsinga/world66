Wat Wihan Thong is a ruined temple southeast of Sukhothai Historical Park's South Gate. Its main feature is a lotus-bud chedi, a form closely tied to the Sukhothai style.

It is quieter than the central park ruins and works best as a short detour for visitors cycling or driving around the southern and eastern temple sites.
