Surat Thani's City Pillar Shrine stands on a site with a rough 20th-century history. The former provincial administration building here was burned during the Japanese landing in December 1941, rebuilt in the 1950s, and destroyed again by a communist rebel bomb in 1982.

The shrine that replaced it is a calm religious landmark in the city centre. Dress modestly and remove your shoes before entering.
