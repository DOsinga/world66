Kuha Cave is a rural cave-temple stop in Surat Thani province, the kind of small limestone sanctuary that makes sense when you are exploring beyond the city or breaking up a drive. The appeal is the mix of shrine, cave chamber, and quiet countryside.

It is not a headline sight for a short Surat Thani stopover, but it is worthwhile for travelers with their own transport and an interest in local temple landscapes. Bring a light and modest clothing, and expect limited facilities.
