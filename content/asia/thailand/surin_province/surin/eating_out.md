---
title: "Eating Out"
type: section
---

Surin's food scene is solidly Isan: fiery, fermented, and cheap. The local style of som tam (green papaya salad) uses pla raa — fermented fish — rather than the milder fish sauce version popular in Bangkok, and the heat level assumes you can handle it.

The [night market near the train station](/asia/thailand/surin_province/surin/surin_night_market) is the undisputed best option for an evening meal. For sit-down restaurants, the streets around the city's central fountain have several mid-range places serving both Thai and Isan food.
