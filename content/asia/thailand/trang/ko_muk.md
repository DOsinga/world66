Ko Muk is the most accessible of Trang's islands, reachable by longtail from Hat Pakmeng in around 45 minutes. The main beach, Hat Farang, is a proper white-sand stretch backed by casuarina trees with a handful of bungalows and seafood restaurants. The island is small enough to walk across in an hour.

The main draw is Tham Morakot (the Emerald Cave), a sea cave on the island's western shore. Access requires swimming or kayaking through a dark tunnel in the cliff — about 80 metres, with the roof close above the water. The hidden beach at the far end is genuinely spectacular: a completely enclosed lagoon with a vertical column of jungle and light above it. Go at low tide, ideally early morning before the tour boats arrive from Krabi.
