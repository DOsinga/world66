The area around Udon Thani rewards a day or two of exploration. **Ban Chiang**, 56 kilometres to the east, is the obvious first choice — the museum and excavation site make for a half-day trip, with time left over for lunch in the village. Northwest of the city, **Phu Phra Bat Historical Park** combines strange sandstone formations with ancient Buddhist carvings, and sits close enough to the Nam Som Forest Park to combine both in a single outing. The **Red Lotus Sea** at Kumphawapi, 45 km south, is unmissable from December to February. **Nong Khai** on the Lao border is an hour up the road. None of these destinations is well served by public transport, so hiring a songthaew, motorbike taxi, or car for the day is the practical approach.
