The Yusuf Hakiki Baba Complex brings together tombs, a mosque, a library, and small exhibition spaces. The main associations are with the Islamic saint Somuncu Baba and the scholar Cemaleddin Aksarayî, making it one of Aksaray's more meaningful religious and historical stops.
