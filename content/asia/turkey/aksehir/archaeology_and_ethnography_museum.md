Aksehir's Archaeology and Ethnography Museum collects local antiquities, household objects, and regional curios. It gives useful context for the town's long history beyond the better-known Nasreddin Hoca sites.

The museum is close to the centre and works best as part of a short circuit with the Western Front Headquarters, Hasan Pasa Imaret Mosque, and the Nasreddin Hoca tomb.
