Alaçatı Pazaryeri Mosque anchors the old market area. The building began life as the Ayios Konstantinos church before becoming a mosque after the population exchange, so it quietly carries the town's Greek and Turkish histories in one place.

It is not a major monument, but it is worth noticing while wandering the centre. The square around it is one of the better places to sit and watch the town go by.
