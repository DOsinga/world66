Alanya Ataturk House Museum preserves the house where Mustafa Kemal Ataturk stayed briefly during his 1935 visit to the town. The building is modest, but it carries the symbolic weight common to Ataturk houses across Turkey, linking a local address to the founding story of the republic.

Inside, exhibits usually mix photographs, documents, personal associations, and period domestic rooms. It is a short visit rather than a major museum, but it sits comfortably in central Alanya and adds historical balance to a town better known for beaches, the castle, and harbour views.
