Sapadere Canyon is one of the better inland breaks from Alanya's beach routine. A wooden walkway follows the cold stream into a narrow gorge, with limestone walls, small cascades, and clear pools that look tempting even when the water is bracing.

It is easy to visit on a tour, though a car gives you more freedom to stop in the surrounding villages. Go early or outside the busiest summer hours if you want the canyon to feel like nature rather than a queue with scenery.
