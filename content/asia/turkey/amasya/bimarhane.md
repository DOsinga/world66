The Bimarhane is one of Amasya's most evocative historic buildings, a medieval medical complex associated with treatment and music therapy. Its stone portal and courtyard architecture fit the city's layered Seljuk and Ottoman landscape, while the subject matter sets it apart from mosques and tombs.

As a museum, it is compact, but the building carries the visit. It pairs well with the riverside mansions and the rock-cut Pontic tombs because it shows Amasya as a centre of learning and care, not only a scenic town in a narrow valley.
