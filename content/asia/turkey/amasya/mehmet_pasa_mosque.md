Mehmet Pasa Mosque is one of the Ottoman religious buildings that gives Amasyas old centre its layered character. It does not dominate the skyline like the royal tombs or the castle, but its proportions and details reward a short detour.

Visit it as part of a riverside walk through Amasya rather than as a separate target. The pleasure of the city is in how mosques, mansions, bridges, and cliffs keep appearing in relation to one another.
