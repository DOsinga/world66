Anamur Beach extends for several kilometres southeast of the town along the coast road toward Mamure Castle. The sand here is a mix of coarse golden grains and fine gravel, the water is a consistent deep blue, and the Taurus Mountains form a backdrop to the north. The beach is rarely crowded — the absence of large hotels keeps visitor numbers low even in July and August.

The stretch closest to the castle end of the beach is the most scenic, with views back toward the fortress walls rising directly from the sea. A few small pansiyons and simple restaurants operate along the road parallel to the beach during summer. The coast road itself carries light traffic, so getting to and from the beach by car or bicycle is straightforward. Dolmuş service from the town centre runs along this road in summer.
