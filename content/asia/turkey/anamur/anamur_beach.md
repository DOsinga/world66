title: Anamur Beach
type: poi
---
Anamur's beach runs for several kilometres along the coast near Turkey's southernmost point — a broad stretch of coarse sand and shingle backed by a low promenade of small hotels and tea gardens. It is never as crowded as the resort beaches to the west, and the water is clean and clear.

The beach is particularly pleasant in the shoulder season — May and September — when the water is warm but the summer crowds have not yet arrived or have dispersed. Simple beach restaurants along the front serve fresh fish and cold beer. The cape itself, visible to the southwest, is the closest point in Turkey to Cyprus, only about 70km away across the water.
