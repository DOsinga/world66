type: poi

The Anamur Archaeological Museum houses finds from the area's two major ancient sites — Anemurium and the hilltop city of Titiopolis — along with smaller finds from survey work across the district. The collection includes coins, ceramics, glass, sculpture fragments, and inscriptions covering the Roman through Byzantine periods.

The museum is small and the displays are not elaborate, but it gives useful context before or after visiting the sites themselves. A visit of 30–45 minutes is usually enough. The museum is centrally located in Anamur town and is easy to combine with a walk along the seafront.
