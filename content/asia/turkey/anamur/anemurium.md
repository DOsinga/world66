Anemurium is one of the most atmospheric ancient sites in Turkey — a complete Roman and Byzantine city on the cape at Turkey's southernmost point, abandoned in the 7th century and largely unexcavated. The ruins spread across a hillside above the sea, covering baths, churches, a theatre, and a harbour, all slowly being reclaimed by scrub vegetation.

The necropolis is the highlight: hundreds of tomb houses arranged like a small city of the dead, some rising to two and three storeys, with mosaic floors and painted plaster still visible inside. The site is a short drive or taxi ride from Anamur town. Wear sturdy shoes and bring water — the terrain is rough and there is little shade.
