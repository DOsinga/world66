Cape Anamur, known in Arabic as Ras al-Khanzir, is the southernmost point of Turkey and of the Anatolian peninsula. The cape juts into the eastern Mediterranean about 4 km west of Anemurium, and the drive or walk out to it passes through scrubby coastal vegetation with views back along the beach to the town. A lighthouse marks the tip. The geography matters: this headland is the point at which you are as far south as Turkey goes, and standing here you are roughly at the same latitude as northern Algeria.

The cape itself is not a developed attraction — there is no car park, café, or viewing platform, just the rocky point and the lighthouse. The sea around it can be rough when winds come from the south, and the water is deep close to shore. Birdwatchers note it as a migration observation point in spring and autumn, when raptors and passerines cross toward or from Cyprus and the African coast.
