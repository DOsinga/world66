---
title: 'Getting There'
type: section
---
Anamur is about 150km east of Alanya along the coastal highway — a spectacular but slow road that winds through the Taurus foothills. The drive takes around three hours. Regular bus services connect Anamur with both Alanya and Antalya to the west and Mersin and Adana to the east; the intercity bus terminal is on the main road through town. There is no train service. The nearest airports are at Antalya (around 220km) and Adana (around 180km).
