The small cluster of fish restaurants near Anamur's harbour operates on a simple model: fish caught that morning by local boats, grilled or fried, served with bread, salad, and rakı if you want it. İskele Balık is representative of these — a covered terrace over the water, plastic chairs, and a glass display case showing what came off the boats. The menu changes with the season and the catch: sea bass, sea bream, red mullet, and swordfish appear in rotation, along with octopus and squid when available.

This is not a restaurant selling an experience — the décor is minimal and the service functional — but the fish is fresh and priced at local rates, which are considerably below what you would pay for comparable quality in Alanya or Antalya. Meze plates of white bean salad, ezme, and stuffed vine leaves come before the main. The restaurant faces west, so the evening light across the water is pleasant. Reservations are not necessary except at weekends in high summer.
