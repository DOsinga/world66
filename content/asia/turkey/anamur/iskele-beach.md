İskele Beach is the most accessible stretch of sand in Anamur, sitting just below the town centre near the fishing harbour. The beach is backed by a modest promenade with a few tea gardens and snack stalls, and the water is clear Mediterranean — calm in summer, cooler and rougher in spring and autumn. It is a proper working-town beach rather than a resort one: local families outnumber tourists on most days.

The eastern end of the beach, closest to the harbour, gets some boat traffic; the western end is quieter. There are no paid lounger setups in the resort style — you bring your own mat or rent from a tea garden. The water depth is gradual, making it reasonable for children. Access is easy from the town centre on foot.
