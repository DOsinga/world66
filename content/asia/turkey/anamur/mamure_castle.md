story: "Mamure Castle is widely regarded as one of the best-preserved medieval castles on the entire Mediterranean coastline. Its history spans over a thousand years: a Roman fort stood here in the 3rd century, it was rebuilt by the Byzantine Empire, captured and expanded by the Armenian Kingdom of Cilicia, then passed to the Crusaders, the Karamanids, and finally the Ottomans, who carried out the last major construction work in the 14th century. The result is a layered accumulation of building phases, all remarkably intact, right at the water's edge."
---
Mamure Castle stands directly on the shore a few kilometres east of Anamur town, its 39 towers and long curtain walls rising from the sea in a state of preservation that is startling. Walking through the main gate into the interior, you find a complete medieval town within the walls: a mosque, a caravanserai, a residential quarter, all slowly being consolidated and restored.

The castle is set between the sea and a small beach, and the combination of the architecture and the setting makes it one of the most impressive fortifications in Turkey. Admission is inexpensive. Allow at least an hour to walk the walls and explore the interior courtyards.
