title: 'Things to Do'
type: section
---
Anamur sits on Turkey's southernmost cape, where the Taurus Mountains drop steeply into the Mediterranean. The town is a working agricultural centre rather than a polished resort, but it sits between two extraordinary ancient sites — the sea fortress of Mamure Castle and the ruined city of Anemurium — and has a long sandy beach that sees a fraction of the crowds found on the resorts to the west. For travellers making the coastal journey between Alanya and Mersin, Anamur is the most compelling stop on the route.
