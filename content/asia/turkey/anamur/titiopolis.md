Titiopolis is a ruined hilltop city set above Anamur in the foothills of the Taurus Mountains, less visited than Anemurium but rewarding for those willing to make the climb. The site preserves the remains of a church, cisterns, and various civic buildings spread across a ridge with commanding views down to the coast and across the Cilician plain.

The city flourished in the Roman and Byzantine periods and appears to have been abandoned around the same time as Anemurium, in the 7th century. Access involves a rough track and some walking; a four-wheel-drive vehicle is useful but not essential in dry conditions. Very few visitors come here, and the solitude — combined with the views — makes it worth the effort for ancient history enthusiasts.
