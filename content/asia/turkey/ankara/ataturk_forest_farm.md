Atatürk Forest Farm was founded under Mustafa Kemal Atatürk as a model agricultural estate and public green space on the edge of Ankara. Over time it has gathered museums, gardens, food shops and recreation areas, making it part park, part national-memory site.

Its meaning is stronger than any single attraction inside it. Visit for a sense of early republican ambition: the idea that the new capital should grow food, teach modern agriculture and give citizens a shared place to breathe.
