Ankara's Chess Museum is a specialist collection with unusual charm, built around chess
sets from many countries and traditions. The appeal is visual as much as strategic:
pieces become miniature sculpture, folk art, political symbol, and travel souvenir.

It is a compact visit in the old Ankara area, best combined with the citadel, small
museums, and nearby lanes. Even non-players can enjoy it if they like design, craft, or
collections with a clear obsession behind them.
