The Monument to Security is the sculptural focus of Güvenpark, one of central Ankara's landmark public spaces. Built in the early Republican period, it reflects the civic and nationalist style that shaped much of the capital's official landscape.

It is best seen as part of a Kızılay and central Ankara walk rather than as a standalone attraction. The monument, park, and surrounding transport hub show the everyday civic centre of the city, where Republican symbolism meets modern urban life.
