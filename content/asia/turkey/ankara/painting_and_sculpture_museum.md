Ankara's Painting and Sculpture Museum is a central stop for Turkish visual art, with collections that trace painting, sculpture, and republican-era cultural identity. The building itself is part of the appeal, tied to the capital's early modern civic architecture.

The museum is a strong addition to an Ankara itinerary built around Anıtkabir, the old citadel, and the Museum of Anatolian Civilizations. It gives the city's modern artistic story a dedicated home rather than treating it as a side note.
