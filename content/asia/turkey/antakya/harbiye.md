Harbiye, ancient Daphne, sits south of Antakya and is tied to the myth of Daphne turning into a laurel tree to escape Apollo. The modern suburb is known for laurel groves, waterfalls, and relaxed meals near the water.

It is a softer stop after Antakya's hard history: less monumental, more about shade, water, and the old stories that still cling to the landscape.
