Hatay Archaeological Museum holds an excellent collection of classical mosaics, coins, sarcophagi, and Bronze and Iron Age artefacts from one of Turkey's richest archaeological regions. It suffered minor damage in 2023, but the museum is being repaired rather than replaced, and remains central to understanding ancient Antioch.
