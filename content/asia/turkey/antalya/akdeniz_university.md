Akdeniz University's main campus sits at the western end of Konyaaltı, with its own beach access and a pleasant park-like setting among pine trees. The university has a botanical garden, several cafes open to the public, and a cultural centre that hosts occasional concerts and exhibitions.

The campus is a good place to escape the tourist circuit on a hot afternoon. The beach section at the university's western end is one of the quieter stretches of Konyaaltı.
