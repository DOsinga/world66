Antalya Zoo is a large municipal zoo and recreation area in Kepez Forest, away from the old town and
beach resorts. The site combines animal enclosures with shaded picnic areas, making it more of a
family day out than a conventional city-centre sight.

It is useful if you are traveling with children or want a low-key break from beaches, ruins, and
shopping streets. Because it sits outside the core visitor area, check transport and opening details
before making the trip.
