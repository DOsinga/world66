---
title: Beaches
type: section
---

Antalya's two main city beaches sit at opposite ends of the urban area. **Konyaaltı Beach** stretches for several kilometres along the western edge of the city — a pebble beach backed by the cliffs on which the city centre sits, lined with beach clubs and cafes. It's popular with locals and easy to reach by tram. **Lara Beach** lies east of the city centre, a long sandy strip that is the favourite of the large package-hotel resorts. It tends to be more crowded and more dominated by hotel guests.

For cleaner water and fewer crowds, head further afield. **Phaselis** (40 km south) has three bays set within the ruins of a Lycian city, and the combination of pine forest, ancient harbour walls, and clear water is hard to beat. **Çıralı** is a more laid-back option, with a long pebbly beach at the foot of the Olimpos canyon, close to the eternal flames of the Chimaera.
