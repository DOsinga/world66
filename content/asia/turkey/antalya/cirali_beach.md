Çıralı is a 2 km stretch of pebbly beach 80 km south of Antalya, at the foot of the Olympos canyon, with the Chimaera flames on the hill above and Olympos ruins at the southern end. It is one of the most unspoiled beach spots on the Turkish Mediterranean — development is limited by the national park designation and by the fact that the beach is a nesting site for loggerhead sea turtles (Caretta caretta).

The pensions and restaurants at Çıralı are small-scale and mostly organic in orientation. The pace is slow. This is the anti-Lara: quiet, car-free in the evening, and beautiful in a way that big resort beaches rarely are. The season runs from May to October.
