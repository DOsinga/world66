Antalya sits in the middle of the most archaeologically dense stretch of the Turkish Mediterranean coast. Half a dozen major Greco-Roman and Lycian sites are within easy reach, along with beach resorts, mountain canyons, and the remarkable Düden Waterfalls just outside the city.
