Demre (ancient Myra) is the town where St Nicholas, the historical bishop who became Santa Claus, lived and was first buried. The Church of St Nicholas has been in continuous religious use in various forms since the 4th century; the current building is largely Byzantine, modified by the Venetians and later by the Russians. It is an important pilgrimage site as well as a tourist destination.

Nearby, the Lycian rock-cut tombs at Myra are among the most impressive in Turkey — an entire cliff face carved with elaborate multi-storey tomb facades. The theatre at the foot of the cliff is also well preserved. Combine with a boat trip to the sunken ruins of Kekova, visible underwater from a glass-bottomed boat.
