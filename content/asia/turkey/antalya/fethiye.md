Fethiye, 200 km west of Antalya, is the largest town on the Lycian coast and the base for exploring some of the best scenery in Turkey. The town has excellent Lycian rock-cut tombs, a fine evening fish market, and access by boat to the famous Blue Lagoon at Ölüdeniz — the most photographed beach in Turkey and genuinely beautiful, though uncomfortably crowded in summer.

The drive from Antalya along the coast road is spectacular but slow. Most visitors fly in and base themselves in Fethiye for several days. For a day trip from Antalya, consider taking the overnight bus or flying. The combination of Fethiye, Ölüdeniz, and the Saklıkent gorge makes an excellent two-to-three day excursion from the city.
