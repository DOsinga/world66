---
title: Getting There
type: section
---

Antalya Airport (AYT) is one of Turkey's busiest international airports, handling direct flights from all over Europe as well as regular connections from Istanbul and Ankara. It sits about 13 km east of the city centre; taxis are widely available and the Havaş airport shuttle runs to the city.

If arriving overland, the bus station (otogar) is on the eastern side of the city. Direct coaches connect Antalya with Istanbul (overnight, around 12 hours), Ankara (8 hours), Konya, Adana, and other major cities. For travel along the coast, smaller dolmuş services connect Antalya with resorts to the east (Alanya, Side, Manavgat) and west (Kemer, Çirali).
