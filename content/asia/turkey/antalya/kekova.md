Kekova is a long island near the town of Kaş, about 200 km west of Antalya. The sheltered waters between the island and the mainland contain the partially submerged ruins of an ancient Lycian city whose coastal buildings sank after an earthquake. Glass-bottomed boats from Kaş and Üçağız glide slowly over the ruins, which are close enough to the surface to be clearly visible without snorkelling.

The surrounding area — Simena castle, the tiny village of Üçağız, the Lycian sarcophagi standing at the water's edge — is some of the most beautiful scenery on the Turkish coast. Most visitors reach it by boat from Kaş; day trips from Antalya are long but possible.
