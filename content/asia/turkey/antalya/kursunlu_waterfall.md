Kurşunlu Waterfall is one of Antalya's easiest nature escapes, a protected pocket of pine shade, pools, and small cascades northeast of the city. The main fall drops into a green-blue basin, and short paths loop through the surrounding ravine, giving you enough walking to make the visit feel like more than a roadside photo stop.

It is less dramatic than a mountain gorge and more polished than a wild swimming hole, but that makes it practical. Come early or outside peak summer afternoons if you want the sound of water to beat the tour-bus chatter, and pair it with nearby Perge or Düden Waterfalls if you have a car.
