Olympos is a ruined Lycian city 90 km south of Antalya, set in a narrow canyon where a stream runs through the ruins to a long beach of fine shingle. The combination of dense vegetation, stone walls, Byzantine towers, and Lycian tombs draped in fig trees is genuinely extraordinary — one of the most atmospheric ancient sites on the Turkish coast.

The beach at the canyon's mouth is beautiful and relatively uncrowded. From Olympos it is a 30-minute walk along the coast to the Chimaera flames at Yanartaş. The drive down from Antalya along the coast road is spectacular in itself. Budget travellers stay at the hostel pensions in the valley; most visitors come on day trips from Antalya.
