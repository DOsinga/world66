Olympos Teleferik carries you by cable car to the 2,365 m summit of Mount Tahtalı, one of the best high viewpoints on the Antalya coast. The ride is short, but the change in scale is dramatic: forest and beach resort country drops away below, with the Mediterranean on one side and the mountains on the other.

The base station is reached from the D400 near Phaselis. From the summit you can walk, hike, or simply stay for the view.
