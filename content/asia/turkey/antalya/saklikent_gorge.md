Saklıkent Gorge, near Fethiye (about 200 km west of Antalya), is a long slot canyon cut through the limestone mountains of the Taurus. A walkway bolted to the canyon wall allows you to penetrate several kilometres into the gorge, wading through cold water where the walkway ends. The gorge is 300 metres deep in places but only 3–4 metres wide.

This is a popular day-excursion destination for those based in Antalya who have already covered the more immediate sites. It requires a full day and is best done on an organized tour or with a car. The restaurant platforms over the stream at the gorge entrance, where you sit with your feet in the water, are a good reward after the walk.
