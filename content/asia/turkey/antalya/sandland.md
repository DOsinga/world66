Sandland is Antalya's large seasonal sand sculpture exhibition near Lara Beach, where artists carve temporary monumental scenes from compacted sand. The themes change, so the experience depends on the year, but the scale is usually the point.

It is best treated as a light evening or family stop after beach time, not as ancient Antalya. Go when the lighting is good and check the current season before making a special trip.
