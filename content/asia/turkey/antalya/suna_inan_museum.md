The Suna & İnan Kıraç Kaleiçi Museum occupies two beautifully restored Ottoman houses in the heart of the old town. The collection focuses on Anatolian and Ottoman domestic life — ceramics, metalwork, textiles, and furniture — displayed in rooms recreated to show how wealthy Antalya families lived in the 18th and 19th centuries.

It is a more intimate and accessible museum than the Archaeological Museum, taking perhaps an hour rather than half a day. The houses themselves are worth seeing — the carved wooden ceilings, the courtyard gardens, and the architectural integration of the two buildings make it one of the best examples of restored Ottoman domestic architecture in the city.
