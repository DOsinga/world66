The Yivli Minaret is Antalya's most recognisable landmark — a tall, elegant 13th-century Seljuk tower whose fluted brick shaft is decorated with geometric tile panels in deep blue. It rises at the top of the old town, visible from the harbour and from much of the city below.

The attached mosque complex (the Yivli Minaret Mosque) is still in use. You can walk around the exterior at any time; entrance to the mosque itself is for worshippers during prayer times. The plaza in front is one of the better vantage points in the old town for taking in the tower's full height.
