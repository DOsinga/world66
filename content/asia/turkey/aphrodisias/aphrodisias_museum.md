Aphrodisias Museum is not an afterthought to the ruins. It holds the reliefs, statues, inscriptions, and architectural pieces that explain why this inland city mattered so much as a centre of marble carving.

Save energy for it rather than rushing through at the end. The Sebasteion reliefs in particular make the open-air remains outside feel more complete, because they restore the human faces, mythological scenes, and imperial theatre that once lined the complex.
