The Sebasteion was a grand temple complex dedicated to Aphrodite and the Julio-Claudian emperors. Its narrow processional court was flanked by multi-storey porticoes filled with carved reliefs, many of them now displayed in the museum.

This is where Aphrodisias' sculptural reputation becomes easiest to grasp. The art is political, religious, and local all at once: imperial Rome presented through the hands of a city that knew exactly what it could do with marble.
