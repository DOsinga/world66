The stadium is the showstopper at Aphrodisias: long, open, and startlingly intact, with rows of seating still wrapping around both ends. UNESCO singles it out as the best preserved example of its amphitheatre-like type in the ancient world.

It was built for athletic contests, but later adapted for other spectacles after earthquake damage changed the use of the theatre. Stand at one end and the scale is obvious in a way that guidebook numbers never quite manage.
