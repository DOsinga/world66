The Tetrapylon is Aphrodisias at its most theatrical: sixteen columns, carved pediments, and a deliberate sense of arrival. It stood east of the Temple of Aphrodite, where processions likely gathered before moving toward the sanctuary.

Much of its power is in the detail. The ornament is rich without feeling heavy, and the reconstruction gives you one of the clearest impressions on the site of how polished the marble city once looked.
