Kazim Karabekir Pasa Museum is the former mansion of General Kazim Karabekir, a key military figure in the Turkish War of Independence. The house museum preserves rooms, personal material, and exhibits connected with his life.

It is a specialist history stop on Istanbul's Asian side.
