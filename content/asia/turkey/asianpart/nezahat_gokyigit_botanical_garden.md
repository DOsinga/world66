Nezahat Gokyigit Botanical Garden is a large botanical garden on Istanbul's Asian side, with themed plant areas, walking paths, and enough space to make it feel like a real break from the surrounding traffic.

The setting is unusual: the garden sits inside the loops of a motorway interchange, so it is not always silent. Even so, it is one of the better green stops in this part of the city.
