Viaport Marina is a large family entertainment complex in Tuzla, on Istanbul's far eastern edge. It brings together an aquarium, a zoo, a water park, amusement rides, shops, and waterfront restaurants in one place.

It is a long trip from central Istanbul, so it works best for families who want a full-day outing rather than a quick stop between the city's historic sights.
