Kucukkoy Town Museum is a small local museum in the village south of Ayvalik. It gives a compact introduction to the area's Greek, Turkish, and refugee history, with displays arranged around a courtyard.

It works best as part of a wander through Kucukkoy rather than as a stand-alone museum trip. The mosque and cafe in the same courtyard make it an easy, low-key stop.
