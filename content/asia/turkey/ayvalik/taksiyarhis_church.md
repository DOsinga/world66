Taksiyarhis Church is a 19th-century Greek Orthodox church on Cunda Island, now used as an automobile museum. The mix is unusual: restored church architecture around a collection tied to the Rahmi M. Koç museums.

It is a worthwhile Cunda stop if you are already walking the old island streets and waterfront.
