Balıkesir is an inland provincial capital between Istanbul, Bursa, Izmir, and the northern Aegean coast. It is mostly modern and workaday, so do not expect the drama of [Bursa](/asia/turkey/bursa) or the seaside charm of [Ayvalik](/asia/turkey/ayvalik). What it does have is a compact centre with a few worthwhile Ottoman and Republican-era sights.

The best short walk links [Zağnos Pasha Mosque](/asia/turkey/balikesir/zagan_pasha_mosque), the [Balıkesir Clock Tower](/asia/turkey/balikesir/balikesir_clock_tower), and the [Kuva-yi Milliye Museum](/asia/turkey/balikesir/kuva_yi_milliye_museum). Together they explain the city's two useful historical layers: its Ottoman foundation and its role in the early nationalist resistance after the occupation of Izmir in 1919.

Balıkesir is a selective stop, not a headline destination. It works best if you are already crossing western Turkey by train or road and want a grounded, untouristy city with enough texture for a half day.
