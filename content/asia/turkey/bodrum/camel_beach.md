Camel Beach, near Ortakent on the Bodrum peninsula, is a sandy, family-friendly beach with shallow water and a more open feel than many small coves around Bodrum. Its tourist nickname comes from the camel rides offered near the shore, which are more novelty than reason to visit.

The better reason is a straightforward beach day: swimming, sunbeds, simple restaurants, and easier water entry than rockier parts of the peninsula. It works well for travellers who want a relaxed coastal stop without leaving the Bodrum area.
