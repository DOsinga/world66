---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

Bodrum's season runs from May to October, with July and August being the absolute peak. In midsummer the town is packed, prices are at their highest, and the temperature can reach 38°C. The sea is at its warmest (26–28°C), the nightlife is in full swing, and every gulet trip and restaurant table needs to be booked ahead.

May, June, and September are significantly better: warm but not extreme, the crowds thin enough to make the bazaar and castle enjoyable, and the sea still perfectly swimmable. October is quiet and pleasant, with some rain possible toward the month's end. Winter (November to April) is off-season — most bars and restaurants close, the ferries run reduced schedules, and the town reverts to its small-city character. Not uninteresting, but not what most people come for.
