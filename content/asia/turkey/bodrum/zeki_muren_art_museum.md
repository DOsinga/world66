The Zeki Muren Art Museum preserves the Bodrum home of one of Turkey's most beloved singers and performers. Costumes, personal objects, and the domestic setting make it a warmer cultural stop than a standard celebrity display.

It is a short visit, but a good one if you want Bodrum to be more than marina views and ancient ruins. Turkish visitors may find the emotional charge stronger, while international travellers get a compact introduction to a major figure in modern Turkish popular culture.
