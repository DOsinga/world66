The Hittite Trail is a network of marked walking and cycling routes around Boğazkale and nearby villages. It is best treated as a way to extend the Hattusa visit into the surrounding countryside rather than as a separate long-distance project.

Conditions and signage can vary, so ask locally before setting out. Even a short section helps you understand why this dry, open landscape made such a strong defensive and ritual setting.
