Golcuk Nature Park is the easy nature break from Bolu, close enough to visit without planning a full excursion. A small lake sits among pine, fir, beech, and hornbeam, with a neat path around the water.

It is more manicured than wild, but that makes it useful. Come for a quiet walk, a picnic, or the postcard view of the wooden lakeside building backed by forest.
