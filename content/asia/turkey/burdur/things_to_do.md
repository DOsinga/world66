---
title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Burdur's best sights are split between the compact centre and the limestone-and-lake country around it. The [Burdur Museum](/asia/turkey/burdur/burdur_museum) is the strongest reason to stop, thanks to its Sagalassos material. Add the [Grand Mosque and Clocktower](/asia/turkey/burdur/grand_mosque_and_clocktower), [İnsuyu Cave](/asia/turkey/burdur/insuyu_cave), and the shore of [Lake Burdur](/asia/turkey/burdur/lake_burdur) if you have your own transport.
