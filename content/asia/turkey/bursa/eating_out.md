---
title: "Eating Out"
type: section
---

Bursa is the birthplace of the [Iskender kebab](/asia/turkey/bursa/iskender_kebab), and no visit is complete without trying one. The original kebabçı is still run by the founding family near the Ulu Cami. The city also has excellent **pide** (Turkish flatbread), **mantı** (dumplings), and **candied chestnuts** — Uludag's chestnut forests have made Bursa the chestnut capital of Turkey.

For **pide**, head to the bakeries around the bazaar district or try Tarihi Cumhuriyet Pide Salonu. Bursa's **chestnut desserts** — candied whole chestnuts and chestnut cream — are sold in pastry shops throughout the centre; Divan Pastanesi on Atatürk Caddesi is a reliable option. For a fuller meal away from the tourist corridor, the **Setbaşı** area along the Gökdere stream has a string of cafes and restaurants popular with locals.
