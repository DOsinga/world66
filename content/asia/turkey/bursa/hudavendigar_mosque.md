Hüdavendigar Mosque is an early Ottoman complex in Bursa, built for Sultan Murad I and set on the
western side of the city. Its unusual two-storey arrangement, combining mosque and medrese
functions, makes it stand out even in a city full of Ottoman religious architecture.

The mosque is a worthwhile stop if you are tracing Bursa's first Ottoman centuries beyond the Grand
Mosque and the central tombs. It is quieter than the main sights and gives a better sense of how
imperial patronage spread across the old capital.
