The Karagöz Museum near the bazaar quarter celebrates the Turkish shadow puppet tradition, which is said to have originated in Bursa in the 14th century. The museum holds a collection of antique Karagöz puppets made from painted camel hide, theatrical sets and accessories, and historical documents tracing the development of the art form across the Ottoman Empire.

Karagöz shadow theatre — in which the irreverent lower-class Karagöz outwits the pompous, educated Hacivat — was the popular entertainment of the Ottoman world for five centuries. The stories are full of wordplay and social satire. The museum organises occasional live performances; check at the entrance for the schedule. The small gift shop sells reproduction puppets made by local craftspeople.
