title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Bursa rewards wandering. The early Ottoman monuments in the city centre are among the best-preserved of their kind anywhere, and the Koza Han district around the Grand Mosque is a place where you can spend hours without noticing. Outside the city, Uludag offers skiing in winter and hiking in summer, accessible by cable car.

The [Green Mosque](/asia/turkey/bursa/green_mosque) and [Green Tomb](/asia/turkey/bursa/green_tomb) are the two buildings most worth your time — their Iznik tilework is as fine as anything in Istanbul. The [Grand Mosque](/asia/turkey/bursa/grand_mosque) with its twenty domes and famous calligraphic pool sits at the heart of the old city. For Ottoman-era bazaar culture, the [Koza Han](/asia/turkey/bursa/koza_han) is a beautifully preserved 15th-century caravanserai still trading in silk and jewellery. The [Irgandi Bazaar Bridge](/asia/turkey/bursa/irgandi_bazaar_bridge) is a rare Ottoman covered bridge with workshops built into the arches.

Uludag rises dramatically behind the city to 2,543 metres. The [Bursa Cable Car](/asia/turkey/bursa/bursa_cable_car) runs from Teferrüç up to the ski resort, with tremendous views. The [Bursa City Museum](/asia/turkey/bursa/bursa_city_museum) in the old municipal building gives context to the city's Ottoman and pre-Ottoman layers.
