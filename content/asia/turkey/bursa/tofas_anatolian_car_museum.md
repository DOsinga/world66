The Tofaş Anatolian Car Museum tells the story of transport in Bursa, moving from horse-drawn carriages to the modern Turkish car industry. It is a good fit for a city whose recent history is closely tied to manufacturing and automotive work.

The collection is especially useful if you want a break from mosques and tombs while still seeing something rooted in Bursa's identity.
