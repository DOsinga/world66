---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

Bursa has a temperate climate with hot summers and cold winters — more so than Istanbul, owing to its position at the foot of Uludag. Spring (April–June) and autumn (September–November) are the best times to visit: pleasant temperatures, few crowds, and the fruit and chestnut harvests in autumn.

**Summer** (July–August) is hot in the city and can be crowded at weekends with day-trippers from Istanbul. **Winter** (December–March) is cold and sometimes snowy in the city, but Uludag is busy with skiers; if you want to ski, book accommodation on the mountain well in advance. The cable car can close during very bad weather.

The **Silk Cocoon Market** at the Koza Han operates in June and July, when Bursa's silk farmers bring their cocoons to sell. It's worth timing a visit around it if you're interested in the silk trade. The **Bursa International Music Festival** runs in June.
