Canakkale's Ceramics Museum is set in an old hammam and focuses on the city's ceramic tradition. Alongside historical pieces, it shows modern ceramic work, some of it for sale, making it a compact but worthwhile stop between the waterfront and the older sights around Cimenlik Castle.
