---
title: "Getting Around"
type: section
---

Central Çanakkale is small enough to walk: the ferry pier, Cumhuriyet Meydanı, the Trojan Horse, and Çimenlik Castle are all within ten minutes of each other along the waterfront.

**To Gallipoli** — the easiest option is the **car ferry across the strait to Eceabat** (around 25 minutes, frequent), which puts you on the European shore where the battlefields are. Tours from Çanakkale and Eceabat include the crossing.

**To Troy** — local **dolmuş minibuses** to Tevfikiye (the village beside the site) leave from the bus station behind the main square; the journey takes around 45 minutes. Returning, the last dolmuş back is in the early evening.

**Within the city** — taxis are cheap, plentiful, and metered. The main bus station for long-distance services is a short taxi ride east of the centre.
