The War History Museum uses old shore fortifications to tell the story of the World War I battles around the Dardanelles. The setting matters as much as the displays: you are looking at the strait that made this area so strategically important.

It pairs well with Cimenlik Castle, Kilitbahir, and the Gallipoli battlefields. Visit before crossing to the peninsula if you want some context for the forts, cemeteries, and memorials.
