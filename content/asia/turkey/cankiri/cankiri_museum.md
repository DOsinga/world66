Çankırı Museum is stronger than you might expect from a small provincial city. Its natural history section displays fossils from the Çorakyerler site, dated to the Late Miocene, alongside archaeology and ethnography from the region.

Start here before walking the old centre. The museum explains why this dry-looking patch of Anatolia has deeper history than the modern streets suggest.
