Chez Galip Museum is one of Avanos's strangest small attractions, linked to the potter Chez Galip and the town's long ceramic tradition. It is best known for its unusual hair museum, but the setting also connects to the workshops that make Avanos a pottery centre.

The museum is not for every visitor, and its eccentricity is the point. If you are already in Avanos to see pottery demonstrations or shop for ceramics, it adds a memorable, offbeat stop that contrasts with Cappadocia's cave churches and valleys.
