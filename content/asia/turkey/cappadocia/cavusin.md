**Çavuşin** is one of the oldest settlements in [Cappadocia](/asia/turkey/cappadocia), a small village on the road between Goreme and Avanos. It is built around a [dramatic cliff face](/asia/turkey/cappadocia/cavusin/old_village) riddled with cave dwellings that were inhabited until rockfalls forced evacuation in the 1960s.

The [Church of St John the Baptist](/asia/turkey/cappadocia/cavusin/john_baptist_church), carved into the cliff at the top of the old village, has some of the oldest frescoes in Cappadocia and offers a panoramic view across the valley. The [Çavuşin Church](/asia/turkey/cappadocia/cavusin/nicephorus_church) (also called the Nicephorus Phocas Church) near the main road has well-preserved 10th-century paintings depicting biblical scenes and Byzantine military saints.

The village is much quieter than nearby [Goreme](/asia/turkey/cappadocia/goreme) and makes a good base for walks into the surrounding valleys — [Pasabag](/asia/turkey/cappadocia/cavusin/pasabag) (Monks Valley) with its fairy chimneys is a short walk north, and the [Red and Rose Valleys](/asia/turkey/cappadocia/cavusin/red_valley) begin at the southern edge of the village.
