Cappadocia is a natural base for deeper explorations of central Anatolia. The [Ihlara Valley](/asia/turkey/cappadocia/ihlaravalley) and [Derinkuyu underground city](/asia/turkey/cappadocia/derinkuyu) are half-day trips easily combined into a full day. [Hacıbektaş](/asia/turkey/cappadocia/hacibektas), the Bektashi pilgrimage town with its handsome Ottoman complex, lies about 45 km northwest and is rarely crowded.

Kayseri, 75 km east, has Seljuk monuments worth a morning visit — the Döner Kümbet tomb and the covered bazaar are both excellent. The route west toward [Denizli](/asia/turkey/denizli) and [Pamukkale](/asia/turkey/denizli/pamukkale) is a classic overland connection, usually done with an overnight stop. [Ephesus](/asia/turkey/izmir/ephesus), near Selçuk on the Aegean coast, is too far for a single day but is the natural next stop on any circuit of western Turkey.
