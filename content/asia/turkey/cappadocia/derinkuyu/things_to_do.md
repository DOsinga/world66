---
title: Things to Do
type: section
---

The underground city is the overwhelming reason to visit Derinkuyu, and it deserves at least two hours. Beyond that, the surrounding landscape rewards a slow afternoon: the soft tuff hills outside town hide smaller, less-visited cave complexes. The [Derinkuyu Underground City](/asia/turkey/cappadocia/derinkuyu/derinkuyu_underground_city) is the main draw and can be combined with a visit to the [Sts. Constantine and Helen Church](/asia/turkey/cappadocia/derinkuyu/sts_constantine_helen_church) in the town centre.
