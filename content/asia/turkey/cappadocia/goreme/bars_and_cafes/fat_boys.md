One of the older and more reliably busy bars in Göreme, popular with backpackers and long-term travellers. The rooftop terrace has views of the surrounding cliffs and fills up after sunset. Cold Efes beer, Turkish wine, and a menu of bar food — pide, gözleme, chips — that will absorb whatever you have been drinking. Live music some nights in summer.
