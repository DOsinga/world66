El Nazar Church is a small rock-cut church near Goreme, carved into the soft volcanic tuff that makes Cappadocia so extraordinary. It is less crowded than the main Goreme Open-Air Museum churches, but still offers the essential experience: a cave-like sacred space with surviving painted decoration.

The church works well as part of a short walk through the valleys around Goreme. Its appeal is not scale but intimacy, and it helps you understand how densely this landscape was once used by monastic and village communities who turned the rock itself into architecture.
