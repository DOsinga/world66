---
title: When to Go
type: section
---

Cappadocia has a harsh continental climate — proper hot summers, cold winters, and frequent wind. The best times to visit are spring (April–May) and autumn (September–October), when temperatures are mild, the landscape is clear, and balloon flights run reliably.

**Spring (April–May)** is arguably the finest season. The valleys are green from winter rains, wildflowers cover the slopes, and the days are warm without being brutal. Balloon flights operate most mornings, though spring wind can cause cancellations — build in an extra day if the balloon is important to you.

**Summer (June–August)** is hot and crowded. Temperatures regularly reach 35°C, walking the valleys becomes uncomfortable by midday, and the town fills with tour groups. The upside: balloon conditions are generally stable, every restaurant and bar is open, and the evenings are balmy. Start any walks at sunrise and be back before noon.

**Autumn (September–October)** mirrors spring in most respects, with the addition of harvest season — local wineries in Ürgüp are pressing grapes in October and some open their doors to visitors.

**Winter (November–March)** is cold, often snowy, and quiet. Many smaller hotels and restaurants close. Balloon flights are frequently cancelled for weeks at a time. But the fairy chimneys under snow are genuinely beautiful, prices drop sharply, and you will have the valleys almost to yourself. Pack seriously warm clothes — nights can fall to −10°C.
