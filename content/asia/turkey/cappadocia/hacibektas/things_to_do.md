---
title: Things to Do
type: section
---

Hacıbektaş is a small town, and its two main attractions — the Hacı Bektaş Veli Complex and the surrounding pilgrimage landscape — are the reason to come. Allow at least half a day; a full day if you want to walk the olive groves to the spring and explore properly. The town itself is quiet and pleasant, with a small bazaar near the museum entrance.
