Ihlara Valley is arguably the most spectacular scenery in [Cappadocia](/asia/turkey/cappadocia). The gorge runs for 16 km, cut by the [Melendiz river](/asia/turkey/cappadocia/ihlaravalley/melendiz_river) through volcanic rock, with steep walls rising up to 150 metres on either side. It lies somewhat apart from the main Cappadocian tourist circuit — getting here takes a bit more effort — but it rewards the trip with an atmosphere that's noticeably quieter than Göreme or Ürgüp.

The valley walls contain around 100 rock-carved Byzantine churches, many decorated with frescoes dating from the 9th to the 13th centuries. The most significant — [Ağaçaltı](/asia/turkey/cappadocia/ihlaravalley/agacalti_church), [Yılanlı](/asia/turkey/cappadocia/ihlaravalley/yilanli_church), [Sümbüllü](/asia/turkey/cappadocia/ihlaravalley/sumbullu_church), [Kokar](/asia/turkey/cappadocia/ihlaravalley/kokar_church) and the 13th-century [Kırkdamaltı](/asia/turkey/cappadocia/ihlaravalley/kirkdamalti_church) — can be seen in a couple of hours, but the real pleasure is following the river path downstream at a relaxed pace, discovering churches on your own, watching kingfishers, and stopping to eat at the riverside restaurants in [Belisirma](/asia/turkey/cappadocia/ihlaravalley/belisirma_village).

The climb down into the gorge at the [main entry staircase](/asia/turkey/cappadocia/ihlaravalley/main_staircase) is steep. The descent is manageable for most people; the climb out is harder. Comfortable shoes and a reasonable level of fitness are recommended. A full day is the right allocation: bring a picnic or eat at one of the riverside restaurants.

The most spectacular finish to the walk is the rock-cut city of [Selime](/asia/turkey/cappadocia/ihlaravalley/selime_monastery) at the northern end — a multi-storey monastic complex carved into the cliff that justifies the trek on its own. The nearby market town of [Güzelyurt](/asia/turkey/cappadocia/ihlaravalley/guzelyurt_town) makes a quieter and far less crowded base than Göreme for travellers who want to stay overnight.
