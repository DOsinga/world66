Uçhisar is the highest point in Cappadocia, dominated by a massive volcanic rock riddled with cave rooms that served as dwellings for centuries — the [castle](/asia/turkey/cappadocia/uchisar/uchisar_castle) visible for miles across the plateau. Climbing to the top takes about 20 minutes and rewards you with the best panorama in the region: a sweep of fairy chimneys, pigeon valleys, and the distant snow-capped cone of Mount Erciyes.

The village below is quieter and more upscale than [Göreme](/asia/turkey/cappadocia/goreme), with some of the best cave hotels in Cappadocia carved directly into the rock. The walk to Göreme through [Pigeon Valley](/asia/turkey/cappadocia/uchisar/pigeon_valley) takes about an hour and passes the distinctive dovecote caves used for centuries to collect pigeon dung as fertiliser. Nearby [Love Valley](/asia/turkey/cappadocia/uchisar/love_valley) and [White Valley](/asia/turkey/cappadocia/uchisar/white_valley) make excellent half-day walks, and the cliffside [sunset point](/asia/turkey/cappadocia/uchisar/sunset_point) is one of the best places in the region for evening light.
