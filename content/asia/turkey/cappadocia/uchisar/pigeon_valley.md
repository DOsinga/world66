Pigeon Valley runs east from Uçhisar to Göreme through a landscape of fairy chimneys, white tuff cliffs and pigeon-cote caves. The hike between the two villages takes about 90 minutes downhill if you go from Uçhisar to Göreme — easier than the other way around — and is among the most spectacular short walks in Cappadocia.

The trail is unmarked but easy to follow; pick it up at the western edge of Uçhisar near the cafés on the rim. Wear sturdy shoes and bring water; there are no facilities along the way. The valley is most beautiful in late afternoon when the cliffs glow apricot. Shared taxis run between the two villages if you want to get back to your starting point.
