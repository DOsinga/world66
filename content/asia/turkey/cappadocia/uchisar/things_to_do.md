---
title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Uçhisar is a small village built around one enormous landmark: the rock castle that rises out of the village like a beehive of cave rooms. The castle and the walks down into the surrounding valleys are the main draws. Most visitors spend a half day in Uçhisar itself, using it as a base for exploring [Göreme](/asia/turkey/cappadocia/goreme), [Zelve](/asia/turkey/cappadocia/zelve), and the valleys in between.
