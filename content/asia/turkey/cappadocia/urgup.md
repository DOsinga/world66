Urgup is one of the main base towns for exploring [Cappadocia](/asia/turkey/cappadocia), along with [Goreme](/asia/turkey/cappadocia/goreme) and Avanos. It has a slightly more upmarket feel than Goreme — the [old stone houses on the hillside](/asia/turkey/cappadocia/urgup/urgup_cave_town) have been converted into boutique hotels carved directly from the volcanic tuff, and the town centre has good restaurants and a lively evening atmosphere anchored by Cappadocia's own [wine bars](/asia/turkey/cappadocia/urgup/urgup_wine).

The fairy chimneys, underground cities, and rock-cut churches that define Cappadocia are all within easy reach. [Göreme Historical National Park](/asia/turkey/cappadocia/urgup/goreme_national_park) is ten kilometres away, [Ortahisar Castle](/asia/turkey/cappadocia/urgup/ortahisar_castle) is five, and the extraordinary Greek stone village of [Mustafapaşa](/asia/turkey/cappadocia/urgup/mustafapasa) lies eight kilometres to the south. Many tour operators run day trips from Urgup covering the main valleys, though renting a car or even walking gives you more freedom. The [hot air balloon flights](/asia/turkey/cappadocia/urgup/hot_air_balloons) that have become synonymous with the region launch at dawn from fields near Göreme.
