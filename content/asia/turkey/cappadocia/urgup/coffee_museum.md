The Coffee Museum near Urgup tells the story of coffee through jars, cups, pots, and other objects tied to coffee culture. The collection leans toward decorative and everyday material rather than a deep technical history of roasting or brewing.

It is a small stop, best paired with Mustafapasa or other nearby Cappadocia sights rather than treated as a main destination.
