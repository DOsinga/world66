The Roman bridges over the Koca Stream are among the quiet pleasures of Aizanoi. They are not fenced-off relics in the abstract; they still help connect the village streets and the archaeological zone, just as bridges did when the stream divided the ancient city.

Pause here between the temple and the other ruins. The bridges make the site feel lived in, and they show how the Roman city fitted around water, roads, and movement.
