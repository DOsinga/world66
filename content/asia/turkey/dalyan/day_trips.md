---
title: Day Trips
type: section
---

Dalyan is a good base for the southwest corner of Turkey. Within an hour or two by road or boat:

- **Köyceğiz Lake and town** — quiet half-day at the head of the lake, with markets on Mondays.
- **Sarıgerme** — long sandy beach 20 minutes west, less protected than Iztuzu so the development is heavier.
- **Dalaman Çayı** — the river that gives the airport town its name has white-water rafting trips in spring.
- **Fethiye and the Lycian coast** — about 90 minutes east, gateway to Ölüdeniz and the Butterfly Valley.
- **Marmaris** — about 2 hours west; a big resort, but useful for ferries to Rhodes.
