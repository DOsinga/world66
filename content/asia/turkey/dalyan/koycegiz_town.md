The town of Köyceğiz sits at the northern end of the lake of the same name, about 25km from Dalyan by road. It's much quieter than Dalyan — locals come for Monday market, Turkish families come for the lakeside promenade, and few foreign visitors stay. The waterfront has a row of cafes under huge plane trees, and the swimming pier at the southern end of town is free.

If Dalyan starts to feel small, a half-day in Köyceğiz makes a good change of pace. Boats also leave from here for the lake and the Sultaniye springs.
