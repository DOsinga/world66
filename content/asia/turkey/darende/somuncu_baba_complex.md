The Somuncu Baba Complex combines a working mosque, tomb, courtyards, fish ponds, and the cliffs of the Tohma valley. It honours the Sufi teacher Hamid-i Veli, better known as Somuncu Baba, and draws pilgrims as well as travellers curious about Anatolia's shrine culture.
