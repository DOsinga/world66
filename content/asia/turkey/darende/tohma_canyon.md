Tohma Canyon is the landscape that gives Darende its punch. Wooden footbridges and paths follow the river below rock walls, with small waterfalls, water wheels, and tea gardens along the way. It is not a wilderness trek; it is an easy canyon promenade with enough drama to justify the stop.
