Demirköy is a small town in Turkish Thrace, backed by the wooded Strandzha mountains and close enough to the Black Sea wetlands at İğneada to make a varied day. The town itself is quiet, but the district has a strong identity: iron, forest, caves, and floodplain lakes.

The best local sight is the [Demirköy Foundry](/asia/turkey/demirkoy/demirkoy_foundry), a ruined Ottoman industrial site where ore from the hills was worked for imperial projects. Farther into the forest, [Dupnisa Cave](/asia/turkey/demirkoy/dupnisa_cave) is the showpiece natural attraction, with wet and dry chambers open to visitors in season.

Demirköy is not a polished resort, and that is part of the point. Use it as a base for the inland forest and the [İğneada Floodplain Forests National Park](/asia/turkey/demirkoy/igneada_floodplain_forests_national_park), rather than expecting a busy town centre.
