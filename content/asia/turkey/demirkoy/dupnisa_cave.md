Dupnisa Cave is the natural highlight of the Demirköy district. The cave system runs for several kilometres through marble, but visitors see a shorter lit route through contrasting wet and dry chambers, with streams, dripstone, and cool air deep in the forest.

The cave is seasonal because bats use parts of the system. Wear shoes with grip, and allow time for the forest road as well as the cave itself.
