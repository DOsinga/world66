---
title: "Day Trips"
type: section
---

Denizli sits at the centre of the Lycos and Maeander valleys, surrounded by Greco-Roman ruins. The main day-trip targets are **Pamukkale and Hierapolis** (just to the north), **Aphrodisias** (100 km west, the most rewarding Roman site in the region), and **Laodicea** (a short drive from town). The lake town of **Salda Gölü**, with its white-sand beaches around a deep crater lake, is around 90 km east on the Burdur road and makes a good summer outing if you have wheels.
