Divriği Castle rises above the old town on a steep hill, with walls that mostly reflect the medieval frontier period. The site looks over the Great Mosque, the town, and the river valley, which explains why so many powers cared about this small place.

Come for the view rather than intact rooms. The climb helps put the UNESCO complex in its setting: not an isolated monument, but part of a fortified mountain town.
