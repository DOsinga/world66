Kız Köprüsü, the Maiden's Bridge, stands north of the old town on the Çaltı River route. The three-arched stone bridge is usually treated as a small side stop, but it adds a useful sense of the old road landscape around Divriği.

It is best combined with the canyon viewpoints rather than visited as a stand-alone destination.
