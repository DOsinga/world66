Seyir Terası is the town's modern viewpoint, a glass-floored balcony on the ridge above Divriği. It looks across the old town, the mosque and hospital, and the Çaltı River canyon.

It is not subtle, but the geography is excellent. Go late in the day if you can, when the light softens on the rock and the town below starts to look less severe.
