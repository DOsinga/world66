Diyarbakir's Ataturk Museum is part of the wider museum landscape inside the old citadel area, where military, administrative, and archaeological layers sit close together. The building is associated with Ataturk's time in the city during the First World War period and was later converted into a museum.

It is not the first stop for every visitor, but it adds useful context if you are already exploring Ickale and Diyarbakir Museum. The interest is as much in the building and the city's role in late Ottoman and early republican history as in the display cases themselves.
