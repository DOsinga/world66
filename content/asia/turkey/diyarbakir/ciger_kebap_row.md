Grilled liver (ciğer) is Diyarbakır's signature street food and Saraçhane Sokak in the old bazaar is where you go to eat it properly. A row of small, identical-looking kebab shops each has a man out front chopping liver into small pieces, threading them onto skewers with tail fat, and grilling over charcoal. The portions are small and cheap — order several. Eat standing or at the basic tables inside. It's busy at lunch; arrive before 1pm or after 2pm to avoid the queue. Wash it down with ayran.
