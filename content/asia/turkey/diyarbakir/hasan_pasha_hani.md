The grandest of Diyarbakır's several hans, Hasan Paşa Hanı sits in the heart of the old bazaar and is the most atmospheric place in the city for a tea or coffee. The double-storey courtyard is built entirely in black and white striped basalt — the same alternating stone style as Ulu Cami — and has been converted into a pleasant open-air cafe. Carpet and souvenir sellers occupy the ground-floor arcades. Even if you're not buying anything, the han is worth walking through.
