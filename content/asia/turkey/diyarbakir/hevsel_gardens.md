The flood plain between the city walls and the Tigris is filled by seven hundred hectares of market gardens that have supplied Diyarbakır with fruit and vegetables since ancient times. Walking down from Mardin Kapı to the riverside gives you a view of the whole sweep of gardens against the dark basalt wall above. The gardens produce the famous Diyarbakır watermelons as well as pomegranates, figs, and a wide range of vegetables. A footpath along the Tigris connects the area to the historic Dicle Bridge.
