The museum occupies the traditional stone house where Ziya Gökalp was born in 1876. Gökalp became the most influential sociologist in late Ottoman and early Republican Turkey; his writings on Turkish identity, nationalism, and modernisation shaped the ideology of the republic. The museum is modest — personal effects, manuscripts, photographs, a reconstruction of his study — but the building itself is a fine example of a traditional Diyarbakır townhouse with its courtyard and basalt construction. Admission is cheap; the visit takes about 30 minutes.
