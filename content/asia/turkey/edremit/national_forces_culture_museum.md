The National Forces Culture Museum occupies an 1840 mansion tied to local resistance during the Greek invasion of 1919 and the Turkish War of Independence. It is a focused history stop rather than a large museum, but the building and local story make it worth seeking out if you are exploring Edremit and Akçay.
