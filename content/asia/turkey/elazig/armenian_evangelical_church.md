The Ermeni Protestan Kilisesi is the most visible survival of the Armenian community that lived in the Elazığ/Harput region until the catastrophe of 1915. The handsome basilica-plan building, with round-arched windows and a small bell tower, was built around 1865 for an Armenian Evangelical congregation that numbered in the thousands.

It is not generally open as a church — services don't take place here any more — but the exterior is visible from Gazi Caddesi and the building's recent restoration has been more sensitive than many in the region.
