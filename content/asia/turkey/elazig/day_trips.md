---
title: "Day Trips"
type: section
---

**Harput** — really part of Elazığ but easy to treat as a day in its own right. Castle, Ulu Cami, Arap Baba, the village lanes.

**Hazar Lake** — 45 minutes south for swimming, fish lunch and a striking volcanic landscape.

**Keban Dam and Pertek** — north of the city to one of Turkey's largest reservoirs and the island-castle of Pertek.

**Malatya** — 100 km west, the modern city is unremarkable but the rock-cut churches and Hittite sites at Aslantepe and Battalgazi are worth a day each.
