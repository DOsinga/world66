---
title: "Eating Out"
type: section
---

Elazığ has a strong regional kitchen. The signature dish is *içli köfte* — bulgur dumplings stuffed with spiced lamb, fried — done particularly well here. Look out also for *gömme*, a bread baked in hot ashes, and *patila*, a sweet pastry from the Harput tradition.

The restaurants along Gazi Caddesi are reliable; for the local specialities, try one of the small Harput-style ocakbaşı grills in the side streets. Fresh fish from Hazar Lake appears on menus through summer. Most restaurants outside the main hotels do not serve alcohol; the city has a substantial conservative population.
