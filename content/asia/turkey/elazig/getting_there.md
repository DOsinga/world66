---
title: "Getting There"
type: section
---

**By air.** Elazığ Airport (EZS) has daily flights from Istanbul (90 minutes) and Ankara, mostly operated by Turkish Airlines and Pegasus. The airport is about 10 km south of the centre.

**By rail.** The eastbound Tatvan Mavi Tren from Ankara stops at Elazığ on its way to Lake Van — slow but scenic, around 20 hours from Ankara. The station is a short walk from the centre.

**By bus.** The otogar (intercity bus station) is well connected. Frequent services run to Malatya (2h), Diyarbakır (3h), Erzurum (5h) and Istanbul (overnight, 15–18 hours).

**By road.** The D300 connects Elazığ east–west across central Anatolia. The route from Malatya via the Karakaya Dam is one of the most scenic drives in the region.
