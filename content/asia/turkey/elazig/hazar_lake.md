Hazar Gölü (also Gölcük) is a long, deep lake in a tectonic basin south of Elazığ, sitting at 1,200 metres altitude. The road from the city climbs through a pass and the lake suddenly appears below — long, narrow, surrounded by bare hills.

The shore is lined with small resorts and tea gardens; the village of Sivrice on the southern end is the usual stop, with restaurants serving grilled fish from the lake. The water is cold even in midsummer but swimmable. About 45 minutes by car from Elazığ.
