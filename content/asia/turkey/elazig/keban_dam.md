The Keban Dam, finished in 1974, is one of the largest hydroelectric projects in Turkey and created a 675-square-kilometre lake on the upper Euphrates. From the right viewpoints — the road north toward Pertek is best — the lake looks like a Norwegian fjord, with steep brown ridges dropping into still blue water.

Boat trips run from a few points along the shore in summer; the easiest are from Çitli, about 50 km north of Elazığ. The crossing to the small ferry town of Pertek, with its medieval Akkoyunlu castle on an island in the lake, is a particularly photogenic day trip.
