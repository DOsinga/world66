Terzibaba Mosque is one of Erzincan's principal religious landmarks, a large modern mosque complex associated with the local spiritual figure Terzi Baba. Its domes and minarets make it a visible anchor in the cityscape.

For visitors, the mosque is a good way to understand Erzincan beyond its mountain setting and transit role. It is a respectful short stop, especially if combined with other central sights or a walk through the surrounding neighbourhood.
