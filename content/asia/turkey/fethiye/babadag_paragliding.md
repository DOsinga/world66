Tandem paragliding from the summit of Babadağ down to Ölüdeniz beach is one of the great adrenaline experiences of the Turkish coast. No experience is required — you are strapped to a licensed pilot who handles everything while you take in the views of the lagoon, the coastline, and the mountains falling away beneath your feet.

Dozens of operators run flights daily from spring through autumn. Prices are competitive and generally include transport from Fethiye or Ölüdeniz to the summit, the flight itself, and GoPro video footage. The flight takes 25-45 minutes depending on thermals. Morning flights tend to have calmer conditions; afternoon flights catch better thermals for a longer ride. Book at any travel agency in Fethiye or Ölüdeniz.
