---
title: "Bars and Cafes"
type: section
---

Fethiye has a lively bar scene concentrated in the old town and along the harbour front. The atmosphere is relaxed rather than raucous — this is not Bodrum or Marmaris. Most bars are open-air, spread along the pedestrian streets of Paspatur, and stay open late into the night during summer. The Car Cemetery Bar and the Ottoman Bar are long-standing favourites. For a quieter evening, the tea gardens along the harbour promenade are a good alternative.
