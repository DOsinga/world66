A long slot canyon cut through the limestone mountains of the Taurus, about 50 km southeast of Fethiye. A walkway bolted to the canyon wall allows you to penetrate several kilometres into the gorge, wading through cold water where the walkway ends. The gorge is 300 metres deep in places but only 3–4 metres wide — one of the deepest canyons in Europe. The restaurant platforms over the stream at the entrance, where you sit with your feet in the water, are a good reward after the walk.
