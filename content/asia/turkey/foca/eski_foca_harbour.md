title: Eski Foça Harbour
type: poi
---

Eski Foça Harbour is the town's front room, with fishing boats, small restaurants, and low stone houses clustered around the bay. It is best in the late afternoon, when the quay fills slowly and the castle headland catches the sun. Use it as the start and end point for exploring the old town.
