type: poi

Boat trips from Foça visit the small islands and coves offshore, including Incir, Fener, Orak, Atatürk, Kartdere, and Metelik. The islands are scrubby and uninhabited, but the cliffs, swimming stops, and chance of seeing seabirds or monk-seal habitat make the trip the town's best half-day outing. The Siren Rocks give the route its mythological hook.
