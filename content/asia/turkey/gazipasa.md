Gazipasa sits east of [Alanya](/asia/turkey/alanya), where Turkey's Mediterranean resort coast starts to loosen its grip. It has an airport, a working town centre, banana groves, and a coastline of coves and ruins that still feels far less packaged than the beaches to the west.

The headline is ancient [Selinus](/asia/turkey/gazipasa/selinus), spread around the hill and river mouth above [Selinus Beach](/asia/turkey/gazipasa/selinus_beach). The Roman emperor Trajan died here in AD 117, which gives the otherwise sleepy ruins a surprisingly imperial footnote. Nearby [Koru Beach](/asia/turkey/gazipasa/koru_beach) and the sea pools around it are the more relaxed swimming option.

Gazipasa is not a place for nightlife or big resort comforts. It is for travellers who have already seen Alanya and want a quieter base, or for anyone flying into [Gazipasa-Alanya Airport](/asia/turkey/gazipasa/gazipasa_alanya_airport) and wondering whether to pause before heading west.

Give it a day if you are following the coast road toward Anamur. The road east is slow and beautiful, with mountains pressing close to the sea.
