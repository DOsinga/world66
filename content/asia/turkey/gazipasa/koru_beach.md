Koru Beach is one of Gazipasa's better swimming spots, known for rocky shelves, small coves, and natural sea pools rather than a single resort-style strip of sand. It is more interesting than polished, which suits Gazipasa well.

Come with water shoes if you have them, and be careful on slippery rock. Families use the calmer pools when the sea is gentle, while stronger swimmers head for the open water. It is best outside the peak heat of the day.
