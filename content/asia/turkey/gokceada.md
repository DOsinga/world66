Gokceada is Turkey's largest island, set in the northern Aegean near the mouth of the Dardanelles. It is not a polished resort island. The roads cross dry hills, the wind is part of daily life, and the best villages sit inland or on ridges where Greek-speaking islanders once kept away from pirates and winter storms.

That older Imbros is the reason to come. [Bademli](/asia/turkey/gokceada/bademli), [Kalekoy](/asia/turkey/gokceada/kalekoy), [Zeytinli](/asia/turkey/gokceada/zeytinli), [Tepekoy](/asia/turkey/gokceada/tepekoy), and [Derekoy](/asia/turkey/gokceada/derekoy) are small, weathered places of stone houses, olive groves, church remains, sea views, and a history that feels close to the surface. Some are lively in summer; others are half-ruined and melancholy.

The coast gives the island its second life. [Kefalos Beach](/asia/turkey/gokceada/kefalos_beach) is the windsurfing beach, with a shallow salt lake behind the dunes. Yildiz Bay and the [Gokceada Marine Park](/asia/turkey/gokceada/gokceada_marine_park) protect one of the island's better stretches of clear water. Gokceada works best with a car or a bicycle, time for slow village stops, and enough flexibility for ferries and wind.
