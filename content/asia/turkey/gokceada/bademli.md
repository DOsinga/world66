Bademli is one of Gokceada's old inland Greek villages, set high enough to catch wide views across the island and north toward Samothrace. The present-day village is quiet and small, but the setting explains why people built here: away from the exposed shore, close to groves and fields, and open to the sea without being at its mercy.
