Gokceada Marine Park covers part of the north coast around Yildiz Bay and Ciftlik Bay. The protected area was set up for seagrass beds and Mediterranean marine life, and the clear coves here are among the island's better places to swim or snorkel when conditions are calm. Facilities and enforcement have been uneven, so treat it as a fragile coast rather than a managed attraction.
