Kalekoy is the island's old castle village, with the older upper settlement on a slope above the sea. The fort itself is little more than fragments, but the village has the right mix of stone lanes, views, and places to eat after sunset. Yildiz Bay, just east of the village, is one of the better coves on the north coast.
