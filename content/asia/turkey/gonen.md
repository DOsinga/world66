Gönen is an inland town in Southern Marmara, about 45 km from the port of Bandırma. It is best known for its geothermal springs, which have drawn visitors since Roman times and still shape the town's low-key spa trade.

This is not a place of grand monuments. Gönen was badly damaged by earthquakes, especially in 1953, and much of the centre is practical postwar building. The appeal is quieter: hot mineral water, a market-town rhythm, rice fields around the plain and easy onward travel to Bandırma, Erdek and the Marmara coast.

If you are crossing the Sea of Marmara by ferry or moving between Bursa and Çanakkale, Gönen makes sense as a restful stop rather than a destination to rush around.
