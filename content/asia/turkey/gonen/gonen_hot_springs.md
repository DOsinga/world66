Gönen's hot springs are the town's main draw, with mineral water that emerges hot and sulphurous before feeding the local baths and spa hotels. The tradition goes back to Roman times, though today's experience is practical rather than glamorous.

Come for a soak, not for miracle cures. The water is relaxing after a long bus ride around the Marmara region, and that is enough.
