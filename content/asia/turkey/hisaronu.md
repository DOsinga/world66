Hisaronu is a small resort village in the hills above Oludeniz, about 8 kilometres from [Fethiye](/asia/turkey/fethiye) in southwestern Turkey. It sits in the pine-covered slopes of the Taurus Mountains, close enough to the famous blue lagoon at [Oludeniz](/asia/turkey/hisaronu/oludeniz_lagoon) to use it as a base without being on the beach itself.

The village has grown considerably from its origins but local building restrictions have kept it from sprawling too aggressively, and newer buildings are generally built in a style that fits the surroundings. It is very popular with British visitors — English is widely spoken, and the range of bars and restaurants is considerable for a place this size.

The main draw is the proximity to the [Oludeniz lagoon](/asia/turkey/hisaronu/oludeniz_lagoon), whose turquoise water and white sand are among the most photographed in Turkey. From Oludeniz you can [paraglide off Babadag mountain](/asia/turkey/hisaronu/babadag_paragliding), join boat trips out to [Gemiler Island](/asia/turkey/hisaronu/gemiler_island) and the rest of the Twelve Islands, walk down through the pine forest to the abandoned Greek town of [Kayaköy](/asia/turkey/hisaronu/kayakoy_ghost_village), or pick up the [Lycian Way](/asia/turkey/hisaronu/lycian_way_walks) for a half-day hike along the coast.
