The Barış Manço Museum occupies the Moda mansion where the Turkish rock musician and television presenter lived from 1984 until his death in 1999. The house, built in 1900 for British businessman William Whittall, now displays stage costumes, instruments, photographs, and rooms connected with Manço's career.

It is a small, specific stop, best suited to visitors already exploring Kadikoy and Moda or anyone interested in Turkish popular culture.
