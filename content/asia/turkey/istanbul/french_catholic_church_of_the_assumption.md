The French Catholic Church of the Assumption is a 19th-century church in Kadikoy, built in 1859 for the district's Catholic community. It is usually easiest to appreciate from the outside unless you are attending mass or a concert.

Its quiet presence is a useful counterpoint to Kadikoy's busier market streets and waterfront.
