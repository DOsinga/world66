The Grand Bazaar is overwhelming and disorienting by design — a labyrinth of 60 covered streets and 3,000-plus shops where jewellery, carpets, ceramics, leather, and endless souvenirs compete for your attention. It is also genuinely impressive as an architectural and commercial achievement: built in 1461, endlessly expanded, it remains a living market after 560 years.

The mistake is buying anything in the first hour. Walk through first, get your bearings, ignore the aggressive calls from doorways. The Bedesten (inner market) at the core of the bazaar is the oldest part and the most atmospheric; it deals in antiques and precious metals. The hans around the perimeter — Büyük Valide Han in particular — are worth finding for their Ottoman courtyard architecture. The bazaar opens Monday to Saturday from around 8:30am to 7pm, closed Sundays.
