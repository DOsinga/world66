İnönü House Museum preserves the island house used by İsmet İnönü, Atatürk's close colleague and later president of Turkey. The rooms are furnished in 1930s style, with domestic details that make the house feel more personal than a formal state museum.

The museum is mainly for visitors already spending time on the Princes' Islands and interested in modern Turkish history. Most signage is in Turkish.
