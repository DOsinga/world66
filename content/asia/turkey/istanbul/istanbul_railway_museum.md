Istanbul Railway Museum is housed in the European-side terminus used by long-distance trains, including the Orient Express. Its exhibits cover Ottoman and modern Turkish railway history.

The museum is an easy add-on around Sirkeci and Eminonu, especially for travellers interested in the old rail-and-ferry routes that once connected Istanbul with Europe and Asia.
