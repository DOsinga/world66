Koca Mustafa Pasha Mosque began as a Byzantine monastery dedicated to Saint Andrew the Apostle, was rebuilt several times, and became a mosque around 1490. The layered history is the point here: a quiet Fatih district building where Byzantine foundations, Ottoman conversion, and neighbourhood religious life overlap.
