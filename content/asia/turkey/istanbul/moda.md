Moda is the leafy residential neighbourhood that occupies the headland south of central Kadıköy, and the part of the Asian side that most rewards an aimless wander. Its tree-lined streets are full of handsome old apartment buildings, independent bookshops, vintage cafés, ice-cream parlours, and small bars, with an easygoing, lived-in character that feels a world away from the tourist districts across the water.

The big draw is the coast: the Moda promenade curves around the shore with grass, benches, and tea gardens, looking back across the Marmara to the Old City skyline and the Princes' Islands — a favourite spot for a sunset stroll, especially when the nostalgic tram trundles past. Combine it with the Kadıköy market and a ferry ride for a perfect, tourist-free half-day on the Asian side.
