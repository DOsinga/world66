The Museum of Innocence was created by Nobel laureate Orhan Pamuk alongside his novel of the same name. Its cabinets tell the fictional love story from the book through everyday objects, while also preserving the texture of Istanbul life in the late 1970s and early 1980s.

It is a small, unusual museum and one of Beyoglu's strongest literary stops.
