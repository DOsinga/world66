The Museum of the History of Science and Technology in Islam displays reconstructions of instruments, clocks, pumps, weapons, and other devices associated with scientific work in Islamic societies. It is in the former Topkapi Palace stables and is most useful as a focused add-on while visiting Gulhane Park or the palace area.
