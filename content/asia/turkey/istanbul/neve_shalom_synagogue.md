Neve Shalom Synagogue is Istanbul's largest synagogue and one of the main religious centers of the city's Jewish community. It is used for major ceremonies such as weddings and bar mitzvahs, and its understated exterior gives way to a more formal interior.

Visitors should check current access rules before going, as security procedures and opening arrangements can change.
