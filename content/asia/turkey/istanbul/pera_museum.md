Pera Museum is a private museum in Beyoglu with collections that range from Orientalist painting to Anatolian weights and measures, Kütahya tiles, and temporary art exhibitions. Its scale is manageable, making it an easy cultural stop near Istiklal Avenue.

The best-known works are in the painting collection, but the smaller historical collections are part of the appeal, especially if you are interested in the material culture of Asia Minor. Check the current temporary exhibition before visiting.
