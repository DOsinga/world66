Rustem Pasha Mosque is a small Sinan mosque near the Spice Bazaar, built in 1564 for Suleiman the Magnificent's grand vizier. Its great draw is the interior tilework: rich Iznik ceramics cover the walls in a way that feels more intimate than Istanbul's imperial mosques.
