Four prisoners in a cell beneath Istanbul's streets tell each other stories of the city above. A formally brilliant novel about political imprisonment, memory, and the city's underground history. Sönmez writes in Turkish but grew up Kurdish, and the novel carries the weight of Turkey's internal tensions without ever becoming a polemic. Spare, haunting, and unlike anything else set in this city.
