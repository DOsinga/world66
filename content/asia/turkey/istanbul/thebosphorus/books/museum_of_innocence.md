Pamuk's richest novel follows a wealthy Istanbul man's obsessive, decades-long love for a distant cousin, played out against the city's social upheavals between 1975 and the early 2000s. The Bosphorus neighbourhoods, the old apartments, the changing fashions of a city modernising while mourning its Ottoman past — all are described with extraordinary precision. Pamuk actually built a museum in Beyoğlu to house the 83 display cases from the novel. Nobel Prize winner, 2006.
