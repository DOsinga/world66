Set in late 16th-century Ottoman Istanbul, a murder mystery among court miniature painters becomes a meditation on art, religion, East and West, and the nature of seeing. The Bosphorus, the bazaars, the coffee houses and palaces of the Ottoman capital are rendered in vivid historical detail. Pamuk's most intricate novel and many readers' favourite.
