Izmir Bird Paradise protects wetlands and saltpans on the Gulf of Izmir, a very
different landscape from the city's seafront promenades and bazaars. Flamingos and other
migratory birds are the headline, but the broader appeal is seeing how close a major
urban area sits to important coastal habitat.

It is best with binoculars, patience, and a plan for transport, since the reserve lies
well outside the central districts. For nature-minded travellers it adds a valuable day-
trip angle to Izmir, especially outside the hottest part of the day.
