İzmir History and Art Museum is a useful archaeological stop inside Kültürpark, complementing the ancient remains at the Agora and the city's larger regional story. Its collections include sculpture, ceramics, coins, and other material from İzmir and the wider Aegean world.

The museum is not as atmospheric as walking through Smyrna's ruins, but it gives context and detail that the open-air sites cannot always provide. It fits naturally into a central İzmir day that includes Kültürpark, the Agora, and Kemeraltı.
