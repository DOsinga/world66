Lefke Gate is the eastern exit from old Iznik, on the road toward Osmaneli. The gate and adjoining wall are well preserved, and just outside are remains of a Roman aqueduct.

It is worth the short walk from the centre because it shows Iznik as a planned walled city, not just a cluster of separate monuments.
