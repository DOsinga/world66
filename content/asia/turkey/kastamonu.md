*Asia > Turkey > Kastamonu*

Kastamonu sits inland from Turkey's Black Sea coast, high enough for cold winters and far enough from the main tourist routes to feel like a provincial capital with its own pace. Its old centre follows the Karaçomak River, where mosques, bridges, caravanserai fragments, and wooden houses break through the modern town.

The city was once the seat of the Candaroğlu beylik, and that history still gives it weight. [Kastamonu Castle](/asia/turkey/kastamonu/kastamonu_castle) looks over the valley, [Nasrullah Mosque](/asia/turkey/kastamonu/nasrullah_mosque) anchors the old commercial quarter, and [Liva Paşa Mansion](/asia/turkey/kastamonu/liva_pasa_mansion) gives a better sense of domestic life than the street scene alone.

The reason to make real time for Kastamonu is [Mahmut Bey Mosque](/asia/turkey/kastamonu/mahmut_bey_mosque) in nearby Kasaba. It is one of Anatolia's great medieval wooden mosques, built without nails and now part of a UNESCO listing. Add the castle walk, a hamam, and a plate of etli ekmek, and Kastamonu becomes a strong inland stop between Safranbolu, Sinop, and the western Black Sea mountains.
