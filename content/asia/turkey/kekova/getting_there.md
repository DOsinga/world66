---
title: Getting There
type: section
---

The main base for Kekova is Kaş, about 35 km away on the D400 coastal road — easily reached from Fethiye (100 km) or Antalya (200 km). Boat tours depart from Kaş harbour daily in season. Alternatively, drive or take a dolmuş to Üçağız village (25 km from Kaş via a minor road), where smaller boats offer more intimate tours. There is no road access to Simena — it is boat only.
