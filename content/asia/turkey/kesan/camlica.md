Çamlıca, about 10 km south of Keşan, was once the Greek village of Grabuna. Some of its stone buildings survive, giving the village more character than the modern town centre.

The distinctive time to visit is Bocuk Gecesi in mid-January, a local winter celebration with costumes, torches, and a ghost-walk mood. Outside the festival, it is a short architectural and village-life stop rather than a full outing.
