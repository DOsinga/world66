Erikli is the largest of the small resort villages on Saros Bay. It has a sandy beach, summer food options, camping, and Tuzla Gölü, the salt lake just behind the coast.

It is a local holiday strip rather than a refined resort. Come for sun, swimming, and an easy beach day from Keşan.
