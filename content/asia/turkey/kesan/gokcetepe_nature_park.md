Gökçetepe Nature Park is one of the strongest coastal stops south of Keşan. It runs along a shingle beach between a cove and pine-clad slopes, with food outlets, camping, and simple cabins.

The sea here is clear and cold, which is part of the appeal on a hot Thracian summer day. Expect crowds on summer weekends, when the park fills with families and campers from the region.
