İbrice Limanı is a small cove and marina on Saros Bay, usually reached through Mecidiye. Its main reason to go is scuba diving: several local dive schools and boats use the cove as a departure point.

Even if you are not diving, the cove is a useful coastal stop between Gökçetepe and the beach villages farther west.
