Atatürk House is a restored house museum in Kırklareli's Yayla quarter, a district of older streets and traditional buildings. The museum is small, but it gives a reason to wander this part of town instead of staying around the main commercial streets.

Visit it as part of a short walk through Yayla, not as a standalone reason to come to Kırklareli.
