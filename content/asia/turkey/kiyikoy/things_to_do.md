---
title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Kıyıköy's appeal is split between the old village and the coast around it. Start with the remains of Kıyıkent Fortress and the Vize Gate, then walk down to St Nicholas Monastery, a rough but memorable Byzantine site cut into the rock above Pabuçdere.

For the outdoors, the most useful trip is south to Kastro, also called Çamlıkoy, where a sandy Black Sea beach backs onto pine forest. You need your own wheels, and currents can be strong, but it gives the village a broader day-trip range than its small centre suggests.
