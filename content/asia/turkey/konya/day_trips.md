---
title: Day Trips
type: section
---

Konya is a useful base for several substantial day trips into the central Anatolian plateau:

**Sille** (8 km) — Greek Orthodox village with its ancient Aya Eleni church. Half a day; reachable by city bus.

**Çatalhöyük** (50 km) — The Neolithic city, one of the great archaeological sites of the world. Allow a full day if you want to combine it with the small museum near the site as well as the Archaeological Museum in central Konya, which holds the best finds.

**Sultanhanı Caravanserai** (110 km) — The largest Seljuk caravanserai in Turkey, halfway to Aksaray, often combined with a continuation toward Cappadocia rather than a same-day return.

**Beyşehir Lake** (90 km west) — The biggest freshwater lake on the Anatolian plateau, ringed by reedy marshes and small villages. The town of Beyşehir has the well-preserved thirteenth-century Eşrefoğlu Mosque, a Seljuk wooden hypostyle building.

**Meke Crater Lake** (110 km east) — A volcanic crater filled with salt water on the road to Cappadocia, dramatic in early morning light.

The drive on east — about three and a half hours — takes you to Cappadocia and its rock-cut towns, including [Göreme](/asia/turkey/cappadocia/goreme), [Ürgüp](/asia/turkey/cappadocia/urgup) and the underground cities at [Derinkuyu](/asia/turkey/cappadocia/derinkuyu); combining Konya with a few days in Cappadocia is one of the classic itineraries through central Turkey.
