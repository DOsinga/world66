The Konya Ethnography Museum sits next to the Archaeological Museum and is normally visited together with it. The collection ranges across the material culture of the Konya region: traditional dress (festival, daily, and bridal), embroidered textiles, kilims and carpets from the surrounding villages, copperware, calligraphic manuscripts, weapons, and the woodworking and metalworking tools of Konya's old guilds.

A reconstructed Ottoman-era village house gives the best feel for how rural Anatolia lived a century ago — sleeping platforms, the central hearth, the food storage cellar. The display is well-labelled in English as well as Turkish. Allow about an hour. Closed Mondays.
