Konya Science Centre is a large modern science museum aimed at families, students, and curious visitors. Its interactive exhibits make a sharp contrast with Konya's Seljuk monuments and Mevlevi heritage sites.

Because it sits away from the historic core, it works best as a planned half-day stop rather than a casual detour. Check exhibition and planetarium schedules if you are visiting with children.
