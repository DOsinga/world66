The Great Mosque of Malatya stands in Battalgazi, the older settlement northeast of the present city centre. It is the key architectural stop in old Malatya and a reminder that the city's historical centre moved over time.

The mosque is most rewarding if you treat Battalgazi as part of the visit, not just a single monument. Combine it with Arslantepe if transport allows, and check current access before setting out.
