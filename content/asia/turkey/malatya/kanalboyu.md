Kanalboyu is the informal name for the green watercourse and median running below Kernek Mosque. It is not a monument, but it is one of the easier places to feel the everyday rhythm of Malatya, especially in the evening.

Come here for a walk, tea, or a low-key meal rather than a major sight. In a city where several formal attractions have been damaged or closed, simple public spaces carry more of the travel experience than they might elsewhere.
