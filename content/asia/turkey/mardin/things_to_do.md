---
title: Things to Do
type: section
---

Mardin rewards wandering without agenda. Start at the [Sultan Isa Medrese](/asia/turkey/mardin/sultan_isa_medrese) — the rooftop terrace gives the best overview of the city and the plain. Walk east along Cumhuriyet Caddesi through the [old city](/asia/turkey/mardin/old_city) to the [Kasımiye Medrese](/asia/turkey/mardin/kasimiye_medrese). The [Church of the Forty Martyrs](/asia/turkey/mardin/forty_martyrs_church) and the [Great Mosque](/asia/turkey/mardin/great_mosque_mardin) are both worth entering. The [Mardin Museum](/asia/turkey/mardin/mardin_museum) provides essential context for the layers of history on show in the streets. The essential excursion is to the [Mor Hananyo Monastery](/asia/turkey/mardin/mor_hananyo_monastery) five kilometres east — one of the oldest functioning Christian monasteries in the world.
