---
title: "Getting There"
type: section
---

**Dalaman Airport** is the closest, ninety minutes by car east of Marmaris, with summer flights from across Europe and direct connections to Istanbul. **Bodrum Airport** to the west is about two and a half hours. Both have airport shuttle buses (Havaş) to Marmaris timed to flights, and taxis for around €50 from Dalaman.

By land, frequent intercity buses run from Istanbul, Ankara, and Izmir to the Marmaris otogar (bus station) on the inland side of town. Local *dolmuş* minibuses connect from there to the seafront and to neighbouring villages.

By sea, daily ferries run in summer from Marmaris to Rhodes — a 50-minute hydrofoil ride to the Greek island just across the strait. The ferries leave from the small port at the western end of the marina; bring your passport and budget for the visa formalities at the Greek end.
