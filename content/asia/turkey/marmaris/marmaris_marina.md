The big marina at the eastern end of the seafront — around 750 berths, one of the largest in the eastern Mediterranean, and the home base for most of the gulets that run Blue Cruises along the Turkish coast. Even if you are not sailing, the boardwalk through the marina is one of the better evening strolls in town: yachts of every size, mid-range restaurants without the tout-pressure of the main strip, and the masts lit up against the dark water.

Most boat trips into the surrounding bays leave from the smaller harbour to the west, in front of the castle. The marina itself is where the longer charters depart from.
