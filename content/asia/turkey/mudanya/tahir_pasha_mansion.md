Tahir Pasha Mansion is a restored 18th-century house a short walk from the waterfront. It was later associated with Tahir Pasha, a 19th-century Ottoman administrator, and now works as a small local museum.

Come for the scale and texture rather than a major collection. The mansion helps explain what old Mudanya looked like before apartment blocks began to crowd the seafront.
