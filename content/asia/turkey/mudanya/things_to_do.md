---
title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Mudanya's strongest sights are historical rather than scenic. Start at the [Mudanya Armistice House](/asia/turkey/mudanya/mudanya_armistice_house), then walk the seafront streets around it for the last pockets of old timber houses.

The [Tahir Pasha Mansion](/asia/turkey/mudanya/tahir_pasha_mansion) gives the town a domestic Ottoman counterpoint, while [Tirilye](/asia/turkey/mudanya/tirilye) is the better place to wander slowly. Go west by car or local bus if you want the older Greek village texture that central Mudanya has partly lost.
