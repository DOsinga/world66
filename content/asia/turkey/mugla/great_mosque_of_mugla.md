The Great Mosque of Muğla, or Ulu Camii, dates to 1344 and the Beys of Menteşe. Much of what you see now reflects later restoration, especially the 19th-century appearance, but the building still anchors the old centre.

It is worth visiting as part of a circuit through the market lanes rather than as a stand-alone monument.
