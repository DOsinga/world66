Kurşunlu Cami was built in 1495, though the front atrium added around 1900 makes it look younger at first glance. Step past that later frontage and the older mosque becomes clearer, especially the leaded dome that gives it its name.

It sits close to the clocktower and caravanserai streets, so it fits naturally into a short old-town walk.
