Muğla Museum holds archaeological and ethnographic material, plus animal and plant fossils found at nearby Kaklıcatepe. Check locally before making a special trip, since Wikivoyage notes it as temporarily closed.

If it is open, it rounds out the old-town walk with some deeper regional context.
