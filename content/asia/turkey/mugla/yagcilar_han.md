Yağcılar Han is a restored 18th-century caravanserai just north of Kurşunlu Cami. It now leans toward crafts and carpets for visitors, but the courtyard form still gives a quick sense of how trade once moved through the town.

It is best seen as part of the old-centre cluster, not as a shopping errand.
