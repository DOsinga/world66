Adamkayalar, the "men of rock", is a set of ancient reliefs carved into the cliff country north of the coast. The figures are weathered but still striking, standing in niches above a rugged valley.

Access can be rough, so this is a better stop for travellers with a car, good shoes, and a taste for less-managed archaeology. It adds a wilder note to the polished cave-and-castle circuit around Narlıkuyu.
