Narlıkuyu Museum is almost comically small: a single protected mosaic from a 4th-century Roman bath complex. The mosaic shows the daughters of Zeus, and the bath was once fed by the same freshwater springs that still make the bay unusually cold.

Do not expect a full museum visit. Treat it as a quick, worthwhile stop that ties the modern fishing village to the Roman spa landscape that once existed here.
