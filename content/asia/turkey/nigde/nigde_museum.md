Niğde Museum gives useful context to a city that sits amid Hittite, Roman, Byzantine, Seljuk, and Ottoman layers. The collection is local rather than blockbuster, which is exactly its value: grave goods, inscriptions, coins, and sculpture explain the country around the town.

It pairs well with the mosques and castle nearby. Give it an hour before heading out to Gümüşler Monastery.
