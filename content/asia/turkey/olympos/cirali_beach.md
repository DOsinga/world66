A 2 km stretch of pebbly beach at the foot of the Olympos canyon, with the Chimaera flames on the hill above and Olympos ruins at the southern end. One of the most unspoiled beaches on the Turkish Mediterranean — development is limited by the national park designation and by the fact that it is a nesting site for loggerhead sea turtles (Caretta caretta). The pensions and restaurants are small-scale. The pace is slow.
