---
title: "Eating Out"
type: section
---

Food in Olympos is straightforward Mediterranean Turkish: grilled fish, mezzes, fresh-baked
gözleme, salads heavy with herbs, and house pomegranate juice. Many treehouse camps cover dinner
in the room rate (variable quality); Çıralı village has independent restaurants worth the bicycle
ride if you want to eat better. There is no haute cuisine here — that's part of the appeal.
