Gelidonya Lighthouse stands on a remote cape south of Antalya, above a rough stretch of Mediterranean coast and a scatter of small islands. The setting is the reason to come: sea, cliffs, pine scrub, and wide views from one of the Lycian Way's memorable corners.

There is no casual road access, so treat it as a hike rather than a quick photo stop. Bring water, sun protection, and enough time for the return walk.
