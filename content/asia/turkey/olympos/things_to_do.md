title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

The double draw of Olympos is the ancient Lycian-Roman city scattered through a wooded valley and
the Chimaera flames burning eternally on the hillside a few kilometres north. Most visitors split
their time between the ruins, the beach, and at least one evening hike up to the fires. The
surrounding Olympos National Park is the gentlest stretch of the Lycian Way coastal trail.
