Ordu is a Black Sea city that works best when you let the landscape lead. The centre sits on the water, the hills rise immediately behind it, and the cable car to [Boztepe](/asia/turkey/ordu/boztepe) turns the whole city into a viewpoint in a few minutes.

This is not one of Turkey's great monument cities. Its appeal is softer: tea gardens above the sea, hazelnut country inland, a long waterfront, old stone houses, and a few small museums and churches that explain the mixed history of the coast. [Paşaoğlu Mansion](/asia/turkey/ordu/pasaoglu_mansion) is the best cultural stop in town.

Ordu clears the one-day test because the city is more than a transfer point. Ride up to Boztepe, walk the waterfront, see the mansion, and if you have a car continue to [Cape Jason](/asia/turkey/ordu/cape_jason), where myth, sea wind, and a small restored church make one of the province's better coastal stops.
