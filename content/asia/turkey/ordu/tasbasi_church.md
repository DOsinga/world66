Taşbaşı Church is one of the clearest reminders of Ordu's older Christian communities. The stone church sits above the city centre and has been used as a cultural venue rather than an active church, but its position and masonry still make it worth seeking out.

See it together with the old houses around Taşbaşı and the climb toward Boztepe. It adds historical texture to a city that is otherwise easy to read only as a modern Black Sea port.
