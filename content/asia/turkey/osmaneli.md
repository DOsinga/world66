Osmaneli sits in the Sakarya River valley between Bilecik and Iznik, a small town that became more interesting because modern traffic passed it by. It was once called Lefke and had a large Greek population; silk, river agriculture, and the old road toward Istanbul shaped the place before rail and highways shifted elsewhere.

The reward is a compact old town rather than a headline monument. Walk west from the main road into Camii Kebir and Camii Cedid for timber-framed Ottoman houses, quiet lanes, and the hollow shell of [St George Church](/asia/turkey/osmaneli/st_george_church), the strongest single sight in town. [Rustem Pasha Mosque](/asia/turkey/osmaneli/rustem_pasha_mosque) adds the Ottoman anchor, while the [Osmaneli old town](/asia/turkey/osmaneli/osmaneli_old_town) gives the visit its texture.

Osmaneli is best as a half-day stop if you are moving between Bursa, Iznik, Bilecik, or Eskisehir. It is not a museum town, and some of the historic fabric is fragile, but the mix of river valley, old houses, and post-exchange silence gives it more bite than a normal service town.
