St George Church is Osmaneli's most striking ruin: a roofless Greek Orthodox church standing in a town where the Greek community is gone. It was built after the great fire of 1874, when much of old Lefke burned, and its rectangular European plan gives it a different feel from the Ottoman houses around it.

The church fell out of use after the 1923 population exchange between Greece and Turkey. What remains is not polished, but it is powerful: high walls, empty windows, and a clear reminder that northwestern Anatolia was once a much more mixed place.
