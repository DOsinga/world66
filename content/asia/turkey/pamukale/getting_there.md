---
title: "Getting There"
type: section
---

Pamukkale is 20 km north of Denizli, the nearest city. Dolmuş minibuses run from Denizli's otogar (bus terminal) to the village of Pamukkale frequently throughout the day, taking about 35 minutes. Denizli itself is well-connected by long-distance bus from İzmir, Antalya, Istanbul, and Kuşadası.

**By train:** Denizli has a train station on the İzmir–Denizli line. From İzmir, trains take about 3.5 hours and run several times daily. From the Denizli station, take a dolmuş or taxi to Pamukkale.

**Day trips from the coast:** Most visitors come on organised day tours from Kuşadası or Selçuk (about 2.5 to 3 hours by road) or from Antalya (about 3.5 hours). This is efficient but leaves limited time on site; staying overnight in the Pamukkale village allows early morning and late afternoon visits that avoid the worst of the tour-bus crowds.

**On site:** The main site entrance is at the lower (south) edge of the terraces, where you pay and deposit your shoes. A second entrance at the top of the plateau (near the museum and theatre) is used by those arriving by road. A path connects the top and bottom entrances via the terraces — most visitors walk down from top to bottom, but any direction works. Allow at least three hours for the terraces and Hierapolis; a full day if you want to walk the necropolis and visit the museum.
