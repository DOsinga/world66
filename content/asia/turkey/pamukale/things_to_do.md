title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

The site is split into two parts that belong together: the natural travertine terraces below, and the ruins of Hierapolis above. Most visitors rush through both in a few hours; you can easily spend a full day if you walk the necropolis and explore the back of the site where the crowds thin out. Bring shoes you can take off quickly — the travertines require bare feet — and swimming clothes if you want the Antique Pool.
