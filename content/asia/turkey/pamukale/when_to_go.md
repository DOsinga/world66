---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

Pamukkale is open year-round, and the site visits well in every season. **Spring (March to May)** is the best time for the terraces: winter rains have replenished the pools, temperatures are mild, and the crowds are smaller than in summer. The white of the limestone is at its most striking when the pools are full.

**July and August** bring heat and large numbers of tour buses from the coast; midday visits become unpleasant. Go early (before 9am, when the light is good and the crowds have not yet arrived from Kuşadası and İzmir) or late afternoon, and the experience is still excellent. **Autumn (September and October)** is nearly as good as spring — hot but not brutal, and the summer rush has mostly passed.

**Winter** can surprise: the plateau receives snow occasionally, and the white terraces against a grey winter sky have their own appeal. The Antique Pool remains open and warm regardless of weather. The site is never closed, though some facilities and food stalls shut in January and February. The bare hillside offers no shade whatsoever — sun protection and water are essential in any warm season.
