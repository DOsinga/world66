---
title: Things to Do
type: section
---

Safranbolu is compact enough to cover on foot over a day or two, but rewards a slower approach. Start at the [Cinci Han](/asia/turkey/safranbolu/cinci_han) in the heart of the [old bazaar](/asia/turkey/safranbolu/old_bazaar) — it has been the centre of commercial life here for 400 years — and work outward from there. The [Cinci Hammam](/asia/turkey/safranbolu/cinci_hammam) next door is the best place in town for a Turkish bath in an authentic setting. The [İzzet Mehmet Pasha Mosque](/asia/turkey/safranbolu/izzet_mehmet_pasha_mosque) anchors the bazaar square. Walk up to the [Kaymakamlar House](/asia/turkey/safranbolu/kaymakamlar_house) museum for the best view of an Ottoman mansion interior. Climb [Hidirlik Hill](/asia/turkey/safranbolu/hidirlik_hill) for the overview of the town. The [İncekaya Aqueduct](/asia/turkey/safranbolu/incekaya_aqueduct) and Tokatlı Canyon, five kilometres out, are worth a half-day trip.
