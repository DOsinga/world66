---
title: Getting There
type: section
---

Selçuk is on the main İzmir-Aydın-Denizli line and has frequent trains and buses both ways. From **İzmir** (Adnan Menderes airport or Basmane station) the local commuter trains take about 90 minutes; from **Denizli/Pamukkale** the train takes about three hours. The Selçuk railway station is in the centre of town, a five-minute walk from the museum and the hotels.

The nearest airport is **İzmir** (60 km north), the main entry. **Bodrum** and **Dalaman** are further away but feasible if you are flying in to combine Ephesus with the south coast.

Most of the Selçuk hotels offer a free pick-up service from either the railway station or the central otogar; check when booking. Cruise ships dock at **Kuşadası** (20 km), and many of the day-trippers to Ephesus come from there.
