---
title: Things to Do
type: section
---

**Ephesus** is the reason almost everyone comes to Selçuk — one of the best-preserved Roman cities in the Mediterranean, with a 25,000-seat theatre, the marble façade of the Library of Celsus, and the painted Terrace Houses still being excavated. Allow a full day.

Beyond Ephesus, the town itself has serious sites within walking distance: the **Basilica of St John** on Ayasuluk Hill, the column of the **Temple of Artemis**, the fourteenth-century **Isa Bey Mosque**, and the small but excellent **Ephesus Archaeological Museum**. **Şirince**, a restored Greek village in the hills above the town, is worth a half-day. **The House of the Virgin Mary** on Bülbül Mountain draws Catholic pilgrims and curious travellers; the **Cave of the Seven Sleepers** on the eastern edge of Ephesus is one of the strangest places in the region.
