---
title: Getting There
type: section
---

Selge is most easily reached by car — about 1.5 hours from Antalya via the Antalya–Isparta road, turning off at Taşağıl into the Köprülü Canyon. The canyon road is surfaced but narrow in places. No public transport goes directly to Selge; organised day trips from Antalya are the easiest option if you are not driving. The trip is typically combined with rafting in the canyon.
