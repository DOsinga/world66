Manavgat's Central Mosque is a modern landmark built in traditional Ottoman-inspired style, with four minarets rising above the town centre. It is most useful as a quick architectural stop if you are already in Manavgat for the market or waterfall.

Visitors should dress modestly and avoid prayer times unless they are there to worship.
