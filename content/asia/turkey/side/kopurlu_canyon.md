story: "The Köprülü Canyon cuts through the western Taurus Mountains about 50km north of Side, and the Köprü River running through it is the main white-water rafting destination in the Antalya region. The canyon walls reach 400 metres in places, and two intact Roman bridges — Oluk Bridge and Bugrum Bridge — span the gorge. The lower section of river is suitable for beginners; the upper section has more challenging rapids. The drive up through the mountains to reach the put-in is worthwhile on its own."
---

Köprülü Canyon National Park is a popular full-day excursion from Side, about 50km north. Organised rafting trips run from various operators in Side and Manavgat, typically including transport, equipment, and a riverside lunch. The canyon scenery is dramatic and the Romans left two stone bridges standing that you rafters pass under. Non-rafters can walk the canyon rim or explore the Selge ancient site nearby.
