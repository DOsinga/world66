The Goksu Delta spreads south of Silifke where the river reaches the Mediterranean. Lagoons, reeds, dunes, and fields make it one of the region's better birding areas, especially during migration.

It is not a manicured park, so bring patience and a sense of direction. The reward is seeing a wilder side of the Cilician coast.
