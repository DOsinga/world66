The Sivas Archaeology Museum is housed in a former School of Industry from the early 20th century. Its collection ranges across prehistoric, Roman, Byzantine, Seljuk, and Ottoman material from the province.

It is not the first sight to see in Sivas, but it rounds out the city's story if the medreses have made you curious about the deeper layers of central Anatolia.
