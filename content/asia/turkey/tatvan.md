*Asia > Turkey > Tatvan*

Tatvan sits on the west shore of Lake Van, where the railway and road meet the long ferry crossing to Van. The town itself is practical and a bit plain, but the setting is not: a huge alkaline lake to one side, the dark mass of Nemrut volcano to the other, and the empty scale of eastern Anatolia all around.

The reason to stop is [Nemrut Crater](/asia/turkey/tatvan/nemrut_crater), not the town centre. The caldera holds lakes, warm springs, bare volcanic slopes, and views back over Lake Van. It is one of the most distinctive landscapes in this corner of Turkey and gives Tatvan a stronger identity than most gateway towns.

Tatvan also works as a base for the western Lake Van shore. The [Lake Van ferry](/asia/turkey/tatvan/lake_van_ferry) is slow but memorable, and the medieval [Ahlat tombs](/asia/turkey/tatvan/ahlat_tombs) are close enough for an easy excursion. It is not polished, but a traveller already in eastern Turkey would be glad of the day.
