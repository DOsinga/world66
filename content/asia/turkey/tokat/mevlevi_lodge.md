Tokat's Mevlevi lodge recalls the order of dervishes founded by followers of Rumi. The Mevlevi were established in Tokat from the 13th century, and the present building presents that culture in a 19th-century mansion setting.

It pairs well with the clock tower and Behzat Mosque nearby, especially if you are tracing Tokat's religious and civic architecture in one walk.
