Tokat Museum is the practical introduction to the city and its valley. The collection covers local archaeology and history, with the surrounding Sulusokak quarter giving the objects a clear setting.

It is worth visiting early in a walk through the old town. After that, the mosques, tombs, and mansions nearby make more sense.
