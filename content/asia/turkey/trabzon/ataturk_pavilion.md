Ataturk Pavilion is one of Trabzon's essential city sights, a graceful early-20th-century mansion set in gardens above town. Mustafa Kemal Ataturk stayed here during visits to Trabzon, and the building now functions as a museum with period rooms and personal associations.

The visit is short but satisfying: woodwork, terraces, garden views, and a quieter hill setting after the traffic of central Trabzon. It combines well with other west-side sights or a broader drive through the city's green suburbs.
