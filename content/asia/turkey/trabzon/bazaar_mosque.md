Bazaar Mosque is a late Baroque mosque built in 1839 by governor Hazinedarzade Osman Pasa in Trabzon's market quarter. It is the oldest surviving mosque in the city.

Its central location makes it an easy stop while walking between the bazaar area, Meydan, and the city's other Ottoman and Byzantine-era sights.
