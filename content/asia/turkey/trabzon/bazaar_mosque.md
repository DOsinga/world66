Bazaar Mosque is the oldest surviving mosque in Trabzon, built in 1839 under governor Hazinedarzade Osman Pasha. Its late Baroque style gives it a different feel from the city's older Byzantine monuments.

The mosque sits in the market quarter, so it works best as part of a wander through the centre rather than as a stand-alone excursion.
