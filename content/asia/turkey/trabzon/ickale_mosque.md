İçkale Mosque stands on Trabzon's old acropolis and is traditionally counted among the first mosques built after the Ottoman conquest of the city. The present building was restored in the nineteenth century, with its minaret added later. It is a modest stop, but it adds a useful layer to Trabzon's Byzantine and Ottoman hillside history.
