Küçük Fatih Mosque is a small former Byzantine church, probably from the 12th or 13th century, later converted into a mosque after the Ottoman conquest of Trabzon. It is a modest stop, but useful for seeing how the city's Byzantine fabric survived inside later Islamic use.
