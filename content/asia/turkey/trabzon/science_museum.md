Trabzon's Science Museum focuses on space, aerospace, and hands-on science displays, with a planetarium as the main draw. It is a useful family stop in the city, especially when the weather is poor or younger visitors need a break from mosques, churches, and viewpoints.

Weekdays can be busy with school groups, so check ahead if the planetarium is the reason for going.
