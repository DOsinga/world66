Sekiz Direkli Hamam is a historic bathhouse west of Trabzon's bazaar quarter. It is a smaller, more local stop than the city's headline monuments, but it gives a useful glimpse of the old public-bath tradition in the Black Sea port. Check current opening arrangements, as men's and women's bathing days are separate.
