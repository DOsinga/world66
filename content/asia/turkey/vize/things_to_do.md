---
title: Things to Do
type: section
---

Vize's sights are clustered around the old hillside north of the centre. Start with the Byzantine church-turned-mosque, climb to the castle remains for the view, then look for the Roman theatre lower down the slope. None of it is grand in the way Istanbul or Edirne are grand, but the layers are rare: Thracian, Roman, Byzantine, Ottoman, and small-town Turkish life all in one short walk.
