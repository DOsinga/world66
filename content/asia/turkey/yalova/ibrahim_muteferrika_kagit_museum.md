The Ibrahim Muteferrika Kagit Museum covers paper-making and early Ottoman printing, anchored by the story of Ibrahim Muteferrika, the Hungarian-born Ottoman scholar who established the empire's first press using movable Arabic type.

It is a small, focused museum rather than a major sight, but it gives Yalova a useful cultural stop beyond the waterfront and hot-spring trips. The subject is unusual enough to reward visitors interested in books, printing, or Ottoman intellectual history.
