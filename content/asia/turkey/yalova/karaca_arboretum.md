Karaca Arboretum is a small planted escape on the road toward Termal, associated with Turkish environmentalist Hayrettin Karaca. It is a selective stop rather than a headline sight, but it fits Yalova's green-weekend character.

Check opening times before going, as access is limited. If it is open, pair it with Termal rather than making a separate trip from the ferry.
