---
title: Things to Do
type: section
---

Yalova's useful sights sit in a loose line from the waterfront into the hills. Start in town with **Yürüyen Köşk**, Ataturk's small seaside mansion, then head southwest toward **Termal** for hot springs and forested slopes.

With a car, continue to **Su Düşen Şelalesi** and the Green-Blue Road. Without one, keep the day simpler: ferry, waterfront, mansion, dolmuş to Termal, and a slow soak before returning to the city.
