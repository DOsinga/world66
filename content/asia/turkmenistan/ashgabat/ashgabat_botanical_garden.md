Ashgabat Botanical Garden is one of the city's quieter counterpoints to marble avenues and monumental squares. The garden collects trees and plants in a dry Central Asian capital where shade itself can feel like a civic luxury.

It is a gentle stop rather than a headline sight. Use it for a slower walk, especially in the cooler parts of the day, and as a reminder that Ashgabat's planned cityscape still needs pockets of green to breathe.
