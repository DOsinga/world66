The Constitution Monument is one of Ashgabat's unmistakable post-independence landmarks, a tall marble-clad statement piece on Archabil Avenue. Built for the twentieth anniversary of Turkmenistan's constitution, it rises to 185 metres and fits the capital's taste for scale, symmetry, and white stone.

For travellers, the monument is useful as part of an architecture circuit rather than as an isolated stop. Seen alongside the Neutrality Monument, Independence Monument, and the city's government avenues, it helps explain Ashgabat's highly choreographed civic landscape.
