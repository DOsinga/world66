Jeitun is an archaeological site linked to some of the earliest settled farming communities in southern Central Asia. For travellers interested in deep history, it broadens Ashgabat beyond marble monuments and modern national museums.

The site is specialized and not as visually immediate as later ruins, so it is best approached with a guide or background reading. Its value lies in context: it helps connect Turkmenistan's desert-edge landscapes with a much older human story.
