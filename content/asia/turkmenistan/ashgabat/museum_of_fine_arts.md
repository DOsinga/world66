Ashgabat's Museum of Fine Arts adds a softer cultural stop to a city often remembered for marble avenues and monumental scale. Its collections focus on Turkmen art, painting, and craft, giving visitors a better sense of visual culture beyond the capital's official architecture.

Opening arrangements can be less predictable than in larger tourist cities, so confirm before crossing town. If it is open, it pairs well with the National Museum and a walk through central Ashgabat's monumental districts.
