The Museum of Turkmen National Values presents traditional Turkmen material culture, including silver jewellery, horse ornaments, and replicas of archaeological finds from Altyn Depe. The displays are useful background for understanding the symbols that appear throughout Ashgabat's monuments and official buildings.

It is a focused museum rather than a large national collection, but worthwhile for visitors interested in Turkmen craft, dress, and identity.
