The Neutrality Monument is one of Ashgabat's unmistakable state monuments, built to celebrate Turkmenistan's official neutrality. Its scale, white stone, and gilded symbolism are pure Ashgabat: grand, controlled, and impossible to confuse with anywhere else.

Whether you admire it or find it overwhelming, the monument is useful for understanding the capital's political theatre. Visit as part of a drive through the white-marble city, where monuments, avenues, and empty space are all part of the message.
