The Turkmenistan Independence Monument is Ashgabat in concentrated form: monumental, highly symbolic, and almost surreal in its scale. The tall column, gold details, fountains, and surrounding park turn independence from the Soviet Union into a carefully staged urban spectacle.

It is worth seeing because it helps explain the capital's personality. Ashgabat's white marble avenues can feel abstract until you stand among monuments like this, where national myth, political theatre, and city planning all occupy the same enormous plaza.
