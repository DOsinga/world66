---
title: Things to Do
type: section
---

Erbent's appeal is elemental: the oasis itself, the Karakum desert stretching in every direction, and the living traditions of Turkmen nomadic culture. The main draws are the famous Tazy hunting dogs, the chance to see carpet weaving and felt-making in traditional workshops, and the sheer, disorienting experience of a settlement that exists in genuine geographical isolation. The village is small enough to walk end to end in twenty minutes, but the surrounding desert invites longer exploration.
