---
title: "Getting There"
type: section
---

Mary sits on the main rail line and road across southern Turkmenistan, between Ashgabat and the Uzbek border. **By air**, daily Turkmenistan Airlines flights from Ashgabat take about an hour. The airport is a few kilometres outside town. **By rail**, the overnight train from Ashgabat is the long but interesting option — about 12 hours, sleeper cars, the standard Soviet-era arrangement. The day train is faster and runs in around six hours.

**By road**, Ashgabat is six hours west on the M37 highway; the drive crosses 350 km of Karakum desert and is straightforward if you have a car and the right paperwork. Independent travel in Turkmenistan generally requires a guide, and most visitors arrive in Mary as part of an arranged itinerary that includes Merv and Gonur Depe.
