The Mary central bazaar is the working market of the city, a covered hall and the surrounding streets full of stalls. The produce is what you would expect: mountains of melon in season — Turkmenistan's melons are famous and August/September is the moment to try them — alongside dried apricots, raisins, and walnuts from the surrounding oasis. The bread section is where to come for hot Turkmen non, stamped and baked on the spot.

This is the most reliable place to grab supplies for a day at Merv or Gonur Depe. The simple food stalls at the back do hot lamb plov, manti, and shashlik for the price of a bottle of mineral water elsewhere.
