Mary Theatre is a local performance venue near the river, with shows that draw plenty of families and children. Performances are mostly in Turkmen, so visitors should come for the atmosphere as much as the plot.

It is not a polished international theatre stop, but it can be a memorable evening if you want to see a little ordinary city culture after visiting the ruins of Merv.
