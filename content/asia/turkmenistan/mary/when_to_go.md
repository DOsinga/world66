---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

**April–May** and **September–October** are the only comfortable months. Spring brings wildflowers and is the best time for Merv and Gonur Depe; autumn is dry and clear. Daytime temperatures sit in the 20s°C, evenings are cool.

**Summer** is punishing — daytime temperatures regularly reach the high 40s°C with no shade on the Merv site. Visiting the ruins in July or August is, in a real sense, dangerous; if you have no choice, start at first light and be off the site by ten. **Winter** is cold, with snow possible, and the surrounding desert can be bitter. Most tour operators run April through October.
