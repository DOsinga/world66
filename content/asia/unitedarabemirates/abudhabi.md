Abu Dhabi is the capital of the United Arab Emirates and the seat of its federal government. It sits on a T-shaped island jutting into the Persian Gulf, connected to the mainland by three bridges. First settled in the 18th century by the Bani Yas tribe, who found fresh water there — Abu Dhabi translates roughly as "land of the gazelle" — the city has transformed beyond recognition since oil was discovered in 1958.

Today it is a global city of gleaming towers, sprawling boulevards, and ambitious cultural institutions. The [Corniche](/asia/unitedarabemirates/abudhabi/corniche), a manicured 8 km waterfront promenade, runs along the western edge of the island and is the social heart of the city. At the other end of the cultural spectrum, the [Sheikh Zayed Grand Mosque](/asia/unitedarabemirates/abudhabi/sheikh_zayed_mosque) is one of the largest mosques in the world and genuinely one of the most beautiful buildings on the Arabian Peninsula.

[Saadiyat Island](/asia/unitedarabemirates/abudhabi/saadiyat_island), just offshore, has become the city's cultural district. The [Louvre Abu Dhabi](/asia/unitedarabemirates/abudhabi/louvre_abu_dhabi) opened in 2017 under a Jean Nouvel-designed dome that scatters patterns of light across the galleries below; the Guggenheim Abu Dhabi is under construction nearby and the [Abrahamic Family House](/asia/unitedarabemirates/abudhabi/abrahamic_family_house) opened in 2023. The [Qasr Al Hosn](/asia/unitedarabemirates/abudhabi/qasr_al_hosn) fort — the oldest stone building in the city — anchors the heritage district on the main island. At the western end of the Corniche, [Emirates Palace](/asia/unitedarabemirates/abudhabi/emirates_palace), the [Etihad Towers](/asia/unitedarabemirates/abudhabi/etihad_towers), and [Qasr Al Watan](/asia/unitedarabemirates/abudhabi/presidential_palace) form a triangle of national set-pieces.

[Yas Island](/asia/unitedarabemirates/abudhabi/yas_island), out by the airport, holds the leisure and motorsport district: [Ferrari World](/asia/unitedarabemirates/abudhabi/ferrari_world), the [Yas Marina F1 Circuit](/asia/unitedarabemirates/abudhabi/yas_marina_circuit), [Warner Bros World](/asia/unitedarabemirates/abudhabi/warner_bros_world), [SeaWorld](/asia/unitedarabemirates/abudhabi/seaworld_yas), and [Yas Waterworld](/asia/unitedarabemirates/abudhabi/yas_waterworld), all close together.

Abu Dhabi is distinctly different from Dubai: less frantic, more planned, more traditional. The pace is slower, the streets less crowded, and the mix of cultures — with a large South Asian and Arab expatriate population alongside Emirati nationals — makes it an interesting place to spend a few days. The deeper Emirati landscape — [Sir Bani Yas](/asia/unitedarabemirates/abudhabi/sir_bani_yas) to the west, the [Liwa](/asia/unitedarabemirates/abudhabi/liwa_desert) dunes to the south, [Jebel Hafit](/asia/unitedarabemirates/abudhabi/jebel_hafit) and [Al Ain Oasis](/asia/unitedarabemirates/abudhabi/al_ain_oasis_link) to the east — repays anyone with a day or two to spare.
