The all-day café in the lobby of Four Seasons Al Maryah — high ceilings, hand-painted murals, a long marble counter with cakes and pastries. Decent flat whites, a respectable shakshuka, and the prevailing meet-for-coffee spot in the financial district.

A reliable wet-day stop and a comfortable place to work for a couple of hours if you need wifi and air conditioning.
