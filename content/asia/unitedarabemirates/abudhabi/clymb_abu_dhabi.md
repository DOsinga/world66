An indoor adventure complex on Yas Island combining the world's largest indoor skydiving wind tunnel (32 m tall) with the world's tallest indoor climbing wall (43 m). Both are walk-up activities — no membership required — and both have a steep learning curve that the in-house instructors are good at flattening.

Useful in summer when outdoor activity is impossible. Booking online ahead of time is cheaper than walking up.
