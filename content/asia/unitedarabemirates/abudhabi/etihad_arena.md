The 18,000-seat indoor arena on Yas Bay, the largest of its kind in the Middle East. Opened in 2021, it hosts the bigger international concert tours that stop in the UAE, UFC fight nights, the WWE shows, and the occasional NBA exhibition game.

Check the calendar — when something is on, the rest of the Yas Bay Waterfront comes alive around it. Otherwise the arena itself is closed between events. Reach it by taxi or the free Yas shuttle.
