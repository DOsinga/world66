A long-running Lebanese institution on Khalifa Bin Shakhbout Street in Khalidiya, packed every night with a mix of Abu Dhabi families and homesick Lebanese. Order the mixed grill, the hummus with pine nuts, the *kibbeh nayyeh* if you trust raw lamb, and a glass of jallab.

The atmosphere is fluorescent-lit and chaotic. The portions are large; the bill is small. Several branches around the city; this is the original.
