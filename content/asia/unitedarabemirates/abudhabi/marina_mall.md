The original big shopping mall on the western Corniche, on the breakwater opposite Emirates Palace. Anchored by Carrefour and the Marks & Spencer department store, with the usual chain stores spread over three levels. Its rotating sky tower restaurant, Tiara, gives a 360-degree view of the city.

Less glamorous than the newer Yas or Galleria malls, but very central and easier to reach without a car. The ice rink on the lower level keeps a constant queue of kids on weekends.
