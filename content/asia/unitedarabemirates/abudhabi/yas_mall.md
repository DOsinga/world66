The largest mall on Yas Island, directly connected to Ferrari World and within shuttle distance of the rest of the leisure cluster. Around 400 shops, the usual mix of international brands, and a 20-screen cinema.

Less ambitious than Yas Marina across the bay but useful as a refuge from the heat and as a place to eat between theme parks. The food court has the better-than-average Emirati Khaleeji Cuisine outlet and a row of Asian counters worth queueing for.
