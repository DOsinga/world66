Ajman is a half-day proposition by UAE standards. The historic core sits between the [Ajman Museum](/asia/unitedarabemirates/ajman/ajman_museum) — the 18th-century fort that used to be the emir's residence and police station — and the working dhow yard on the creek, where wooden boats are still built by hand. The corniche runs north of that to a long public beach.

For a quieter contrast, the [Al Zorah Nature Reserve](/asia/unitedarabemirates/ajman/al_zorah) is one of the better mangrove and flamingo-watching spots in the country, and a sign that Ajman has at least one piece of low-key, well-managed nature on its doorstep.
