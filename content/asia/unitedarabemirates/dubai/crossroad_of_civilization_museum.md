Crossroad of Civilization Museum is a small Bur Dubai museum built around historic books, manuscripts, and artefacts from trade and religious exchange. Highlights noted by Wikivoyage include a Gutenberg Bible and a 16th-century curtain from the door of the Kaaba.

It fits best with a walk through Al Shindagha, Dubai Creek, and the older trading quarters of the city.
