Downtown Dubai is the city's flagship development: a 2.3-square-kilometre district built around the Burj Khalifa, Dubai Mall, and the Dubai Fountain. It is the glossiest and most photographed part of the city, and for most first-time visitors, the default starting point.

Sheikh Mohammed bin Rashid Boulevard curves around the base of the Burj Khalifa, lined with restaurants, cafes, and boutiques in a setting that feels more like a theme park than a city district — by design. The fountains perform every evening, the towers light up after dark, and the whole ensemble is best appreciated from the Burj Khalifa lake promenade at sunset.
