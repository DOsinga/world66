The Dubai Creek Golf and Yacht Club sits on the Deira side of the Creek, its distinctive white peaked roofline (modelled on the sails of a yacht) a recognisable element of the Creek skyline. The 18-hole championship course wraps around a marina and uses the Creek waterway as a natural hazard on several holes.

The club is members-focused but visitors can play with a tee time booking. The clubhouse restaurant has one of the better views of the Creek and is accessible without a golf booking. The course was redesigned by Thomas Bjorn and has hosted European Tour events. It is one of the most scenic courses in the city, though the greens fees reflect this.
