Dubai Dolphinarium is an indoor marine-animal show venue in Creek Park, with dolphin and seal performances aimed mainly at families. It is a straightforward ticketed attraction, useful on a hot day or with children who want something easy and air-conditioned.

Check show times before going, as the visit makes most sense when built around a scheduled performance.
