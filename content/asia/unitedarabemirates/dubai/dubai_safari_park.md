Dubai Safari Park is the city's large wildlife park, with animal zones, educational displays, and safari-style circuits built for families rather than wilderness purists. Lions, elephants, giraffes, bears, and camels are among the headline animals, and the park is laid out as a full half-day outing.

It sits well east of the central tourist districts, so plan transport rather than treating it as a quick add-on. The park is most useful for families who need a break from malls, towers, and beach resorts.
