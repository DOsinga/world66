Etihad Museum tells the story of the formation of the United Arab Emirates, with a focus on the years around independence and federation. It sits beside Union House, where the agreement creating the UAE was signed in 1971.

The building is sleek and polished in the Dubai manner, but the exhibits are at their best when they slow down and show the personalities, documents, photographs, and negotiations behind the union. It is a useful counterweight to the city's malls and skyline.
