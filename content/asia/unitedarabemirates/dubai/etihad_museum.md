Etihad Museum tells the story of the formation of the United Arab Emirates after the British withdrawal from the Gulf. Its exhibits focus on the seven founding rulers, the constitution, and the political steps that led to union.

The complex includes Union House, where the agreement was signed, and the restored Guest Palace. It is one of Dubai's best stops for visitors who want context beyond malls, beaches, and skyline views.
