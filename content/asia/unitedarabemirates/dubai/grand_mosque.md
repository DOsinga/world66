The Grand Mosque is one of Bur Dubai's main religious landmarks, with a tall minaret close to Dubai Creek, the textile souk, and the Al Fahidi historic area. The mosque is best appreciated from the surrounding lanes, where it sits near older Hindu temples and the creekside trading district.
