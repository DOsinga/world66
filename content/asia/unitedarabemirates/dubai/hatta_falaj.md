Hatta Falaj is an old irrigation channel running underground for hundreds of metres near Hatta Heritage Village. It is a compact sight, but it gives a useful glimpse of how water was moved and protected in the mountain settlements before modern infrastructure.

Pair it with the heritage village rather than making a special trip for the falaj alone.
