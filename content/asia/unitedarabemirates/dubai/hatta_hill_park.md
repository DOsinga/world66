Hatta Hill Park sits above Hatta village, with a tower at the top that looks out over the Hajar Mountains and the settlement below. The climb is short, and the view is the reason to make the stop.

The lower park has picnic areas, play space, and walking paths, so it is also a useful break if you are travelling through Hatta with children.
