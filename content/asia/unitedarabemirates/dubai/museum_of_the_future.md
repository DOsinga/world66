The Museum of the Future is as much about the building as the exhibitions inside. Its torus-shaped shell, covered in Arabic calligraphy, is one of the most distinctive pieces of recent architecture in Dubai, and it looks especially sharp after dark. The displays lean more toward immersive technology and design than a traditional museum collection.
