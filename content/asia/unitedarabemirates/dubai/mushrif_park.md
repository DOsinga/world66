Mushrif Park is a large, desert-edge park in eastern Dubai, near Khawaneej. It has more space and a rougher feel than the city's central lawns, with walking areas, family facilities, and patches of native desert landscape. It is most useful if you want an easy outdoor break away from malls and the creek-side districts.
