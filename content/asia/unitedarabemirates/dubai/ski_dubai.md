Ski Dubai is an indoor ski resort inside the Mall of the Emirates, operating year-round at -4°C in a country where outdoor temperatures exceed 45°C in summer. The 22,500-square-metre slope has five runs of varying difficulty, the longest of which is 400 metres with an 85-metre vertical drop. There is also a snow park for non-skiers, a snow bullet ride, and a penguin encounter — the resident colony of king and gentoo penguins is the main draw for most visitors.

Ski Dubai is absurd and genuinely fun. The novelty of passing through glass walls from a desert shopping mall into a blizzard of artificial snow is an experience that captures the city's refusal to accept physical constraints. All ski and snowboard equipment is available to rent, and instructors offer lessons for beginners. It is a convenient afternoon activity during the hot months when outdoor activities are impractical.

story: >
  Ski Dubai uses an advanced snow-making system that operates around the clock. The refrigeration required to maintain sub-zero temperatures in a building exposed to 45°C heat generates enough thermal energy to power a significant portion of the Mall of the Emirates' electricity needs — the waste heat from keeping the ski slope cold is recovered and used to power the rest of the mall.
