The Pointe is an outdoor waterfront dining and leisure development at the tip of Palm Jumeirah, with a semicircular promenade of restaurants and cafes facing the Burj Al Arab across the water. It opened in 2019 and is the most accessible part of the Palm for visitors who are not staying at the Atlantis or one of the villas.

The view from the outdoor seating is excellent at sunset — the Burj Al Arab and the mainland skyline behind it, with the Gulf on either side of the Palm trunk visible from the terrace. The restaurant mix leans toward casual international (Lebanese, Italian, sushi) with a few fast-casual options. It is popular with families in the evening and can get busy at weekends.
