The Women's Museum in Deira looks at the role of Emirati women in the UAE's social, cultural, and political life. It is a modest museum, but it adds useful context in a city where the big-ticket sights often focus on skylines, shopping, and spectacle.

Displays cover daily life, public life, and notable women, including the poet Ousha bint Khalifa Al Suwaidi. It pairs naturally with the nearby souks and Creek-side walking routes.
