title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Fujairah's attractions lean toward nature and heritage rather than theme parks and malls. The coastline offers some of the best diving in the UAE, with [Snoopy Island](/asia/unitedarabemirates/fujairah/snoopy_island) the most accessible spot for snorkelling. The Hajar Mountains are threaded with hiking trails along dry wadis, and [Wadi Wurayah](/asia/unitedarabemirates/fujairah/wadi_wurayah) — the UAE's first mountain national park — shelters freshwater pools and wildlife. [Fujairah Fort](/asia/unitedarabemirates/fujairah/fujairah_fort) and the [Fujairah Museum](/asia/unitedarabemirates/fujairah/fujairah_museum) give a window into pre-oil life in the region, while [Al Bidya Mosque](/asia/unitedarabemirates/fujairah/al_bidya_mosque), 50 kilometres up the coast, is the oldest mosque in the UAE.
