Bait Al Naboodah is a beautifully restored 19th-century Emirati merchant's house in the Heritage Area, giving visitors an unusually intimate view of pre-oil Gulf domestic life. The house was built in 1845 for Ahmed bin Dalmouk, a prominent pearl merchant, and expanded over the following decades as his wealth grew.

The house is built around a central courtyard in the traditional Arab style, with separate quarters for men and women, wind towers for natural ventilation, and a high protective exterior wall. Rooms are furnished to represent different aspects of daily life — reception rooms, sleeping quarters, a kitchen, a room for traditional textile production. Mannequins in period costume help visualize how the spaces were used. It is one of the better house museums in the Gulf and immediately adjacent to Al Hisn Fort.
