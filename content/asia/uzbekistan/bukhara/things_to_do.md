---
title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Bukhara's old city is one of the most concentrated collections of medieval Islamic architecture in the world. Nearly everything worth seeing is walkable from [Lab-i-Hauz](/asia/uzbekistan/bukhara/lab_i_hauz), the teahouse square at the heart of the historic centre.

Start at the [Po-i-Kalyan complex](/asia/uzbekistan/bukhara/poi_kalyan) — the [Kalyan Minaret](/asia/uzbekistan/bukhara/kalyan_minaret), the Kalan Mosque, and the Mir-i-Arab Madrasah make up the most important architectural ensemble in the city. From there walk north to the [Ulugbek and Abdulaziz Khan Madrasahs](/asia/uzbekistan/bukhara/ulugbek_abdulaziz_madrasahs), where you can browse craft workshops inside working medieval buildings. The [domed bazaars](/asia/uzbekistan/bukhara/toqi_sarrofon) connect this district to the Lab-i-Hauz area.

West of the Lab-i-Hauz is [the Ark](/asia/uzbekistan/bukhara/theark), the massive mud-brick citadel, with [Bolo Haouz Mosque](/asia/uzbekistan/bukhara/bolo_haouz_mosque) facing it across the plaza and the [Zindon prison](/asia/uzbekistan/bukhara/zindon_prison) tucked alongside. In the park beyond the Ark you'll find the [Samanid Mausoleum](/asia/uzbekistan/bukhara/samanid_mausoleum) — the oldest surviving building in the city — and the [Chashma-Ayub Mausoleum](/asia/uzbekistan/bukhara/chashma_ayub) with its sacred spring.

For something off the main circuit, the [Saif ed-Din Bokharzi Mausoleums](/asia/uzbekistan/bukhara/saif_ed_din_bokharzi) in the eastern Faroband district are beautiful and rarely crowded. The [Magok-i-Attari Mosque](/asia/uzbekistan/bukhara/magok_i_attari_mosque) — buried several metres below current street level — is the oldest mosque in Bukhara and houses a good textile museum. A taxi ride north of the city brings you to [Sitorai Mokhi-Khosa](/asia/uzbekistan/bukhara/sitorai_mokhi_khosa), the last emir's summer palace, now a fascinating museum of the twilight years of the emirate.
