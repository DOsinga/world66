Akhsykent is the ruined site of an ancient settlement on the Syr Darya, about 25 km west of Namangan. The city was once an important centre of the Ferghana Valley, destroyed during the Mongol invasions, rebuilt under the Timurids, and finally abandoned after a 17th-century earthquake.

The remains are fragmentary, but the site matters for understanding the valley's long urban history before the modern cities took shape. It is most rewarding with a guide or local context, since much of what survives is archaeological rather than monumental.
