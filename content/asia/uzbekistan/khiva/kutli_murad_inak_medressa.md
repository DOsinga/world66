Kutli Murad Inak Medressa is an early 18th-century religious school in Khiva, built around a courtyard with arcaded galleries and corner towers. Its carved wooden door and restrained tilework show the local Khorezm style without the heavy decoration of Khiva's showier monuments.

The medressa once had 81 student cells and was among the wealthier schools in the city. It is best seen as part of a slow walk through the dense historic fabric of Itchan Kala.
