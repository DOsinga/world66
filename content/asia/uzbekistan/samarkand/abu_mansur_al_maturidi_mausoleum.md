The Abu Mansur Al Maturidi Mausoleum honours the influential theologian whose name is central to the Maturidi school of Sunni Islamic thought. In a city crowded with Timurid monuments, the site points to Samarkand's role as a centre of scholarship as well as imperial architecture.

It is a quieter stop than Registan or Shah-i-Zinda, but worthwhile for travellers interested in Islamic intellectual history. The restored complex is best visited as part of a broader circuit through Samarkand's religious and memorial sites.
