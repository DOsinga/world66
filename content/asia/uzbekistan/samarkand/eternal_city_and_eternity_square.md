Eternal City and Eternity Square are part of the modern Silk Road Samarkand development east of the historic core. The area uses Uzbek and Silk Road architectural references to create a walkable cultural quarter of courtyards, domes, shops and performance spaces.

It is not a medieval monument like Registan or Shah-i-Zinda, and travelers should understand it as a contemporary staged environment. Still, it can be an interesting contrast if you have already seen Samarkand's major historic sites and want to see how the city presents heritage today.
