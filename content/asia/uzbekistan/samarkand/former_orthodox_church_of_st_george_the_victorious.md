The former Orthodox Church of St. George the Victorious now houses a Museum of Religious and National Tolerance and an art gallery. The church was built in 1882, then altered heavily in the Soviet period when its domes and tall bell tower were removed.

It is a quiet counterpoint to Samarkand's better-known Islamic monuments. The interest is partly in the building itself and partly in the story it tells about the city's mixed religious past.
