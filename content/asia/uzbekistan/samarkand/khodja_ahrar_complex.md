The Khodja Ahrar Complex sits outside Samarkand's headline cluster of Registan, Shah-i-Zinda, and Gur-e-Amir, but it belongs in the same historical conversation. The complex centres on the memory of Khwaja Ahrar, a powerful 15th-century Sufi figure, with later mosque and madrasa buildings around the shrine.

It is a quieter stop, better for travelers who have time to move beyond the obvious circuit. The restored courtyards and devotional atmosphere make the detour worthwhile if you are interested in Samarkand as a living religious city, not only a museum of Timurid tilework.
