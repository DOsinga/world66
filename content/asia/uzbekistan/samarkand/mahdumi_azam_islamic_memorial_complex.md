The Mahdumi Azam Islamic Memorial Complex is named for the 15th- and 16th-century theologian linked to the Dahbediya Sufi order. The complex includes a 17th-century mosque, old plane trees, and a quieter devotional atmosphere than Samarkand's headline monuments.
