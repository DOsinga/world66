Murad Avliya Shrine and Mosque is a Shia-Sunni shrine in Samarkand. The mausoleum is associated with Khodja Said Musa ibn Said Murtaza, better known as Murad Avliya, and adds a quieter religious stop beyond the city's major monuments.
