Namazgah Grand Mosque sits away from Samarkand's most photographed Timurid monuments, which is exactly why it is useful to include in a longer wander. A namazgah was built for large open-air prayers, and the site points to the religious geography of the city beyond Registan, Shah-i-Zinda, and Bibi-Khanym.

It is a quieter, more specialist stop than Samarkand's headline sights. Travellers interested in Islamic architecture will appreciate the scale and setting, while others may find it best paired with nearby southern districts rather than treated as a standalone excursion.
