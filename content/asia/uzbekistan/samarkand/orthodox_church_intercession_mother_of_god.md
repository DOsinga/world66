The Orthodox Church of the Intercession of the Mother of God is a Russian-style church built in 1903 for the Russian Empire's rifle regiment in Samarkand.

It was closed under Soviet rule and later restored and reopened, making it a useful stop for seeing the city's Russian and Orthodox heritage.
