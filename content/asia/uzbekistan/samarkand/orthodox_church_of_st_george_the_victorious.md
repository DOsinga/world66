The Orthodox Church of St. George the Victorious is the smallest of Samarkand's active Orthodox churches. Its most distinctive feature is the colourful free-standing bell tower at the entrance, with a modest but attractive interior beyond it.

It is a short stop, but it gives a different view of Samarkand's religious landscape from the city's better-known Islamic monuments. Visit while walking through the surrounding central district rather than making it a standalone trip.
