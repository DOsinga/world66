The Roman Catholic Church of St. John the Baptist is Samarkand's only Catholic church. Built in 1916 in a neo-Gothic style by Austro-Hungarian prisoners of war, it is a small but striking reminder of the city's Russian Empire-era layers.

It makes an interesting contrast with Samarkand's mosques, madrasas, and mausoleums. The exterior is the main draw for most visitors, though services and access may vary.
