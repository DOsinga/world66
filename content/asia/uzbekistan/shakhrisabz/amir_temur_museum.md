The Amir Temur Museum gives useful context before or after visiting Shakhrisabz's Timurid monuments. Its displays include a model of Temur's empire and artefacts from earlier Buddhist and Zoroastrian periods.

It is not the city's main reason to visit, but it helps connect the palace, mosque, and mausoleum sites into a clearer historical story.
