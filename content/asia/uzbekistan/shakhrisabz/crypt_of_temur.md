The Crypt of Temur is a stark underground chamber in Shakhrisabz, associated with the funerary landscape of Timur's home city. It is easy to miss if you focus only on the grand remains of Ak-Saray, but it adds a darker, more intimate note to the monumental story.

The chamber is plain compared with the tilework above ground, and that restraint is the point. Shakhrisabz can feel like a place of imperial fragments; the crypt brings the scale down to stone, silence, and the memory of a ruler who planned his legacy carefully.
