The Alisher Navoy Public Museum of Literature is a specialist museum for travelers who want Tashkent to be more than metro stations, monuments, and markets. It is devoted to Alisher Navoi, the great Chagatai poet and statesman, while also presenting broader Uzbek literary culture through manuscripts, books, portraits, and scholarly material.

The museum is most rewarding if you already have some interest in Central Asian literature or want to understand why Navoi's name appears so often across Uzbekistan. Casual visitors may find it niche, but the subject is central to Uzbek identity. Pair it with nearby central Tashkent sights rather than crossing the city for it alone.
