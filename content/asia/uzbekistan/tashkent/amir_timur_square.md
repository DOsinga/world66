A circular park in the centre of new Tashkent, ringed by Stalinist boulevards and dominated by an equestrian bronze of Amir Timur — known in the west as Tamerlane — the fourteenth-century conqueror who built his empire from Samarkand. The plinth carries his motto in four languages: "Strength is in Justice."

The square is the social heart of modern Tashkent. The grand Hotel Uzbekistan sits on one side and the Amir Timur Museum on another. Come at dusk when families gather, kids ride hired electric cars, and the fountains light up.
