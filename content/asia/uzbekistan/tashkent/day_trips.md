The high-speed Afrosiyob train makes Samarkand a feasible — if intense — day trip from Tashkent: two hours each way, a long day on your feet between Registan, Bibi-Khanym, and Shah-i-Zinda, and back to Tashkent for dinner. Most people give it a longer stay, but the day-trip version works if you are short on time.

The **Chimgan mountains**, about 90 minutes east of the city by car, are the easiest natural escape from the heat. In summer there are hiking trails and a chairlift up to a ridge for views of the Chatkal range; in winter the area is Tashkent's modest ski resort, with snow from December to March.

**Charvak Reservoir**, on the route to Chimgan, is a turquoise lake set in the foothills — a popular summer weekend swim and boat destination. Hire a taxi for the day or join a tour.
