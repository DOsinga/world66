---
title: "Getting There"
type: section
---

Tashkent is the main international gateway to Uzbekistan and the busiest air hub in Central Asia. **Tashkent International Airport (TAS)** sits a few kilometres south of the centre, with direct flights to Moscow, Istanbul, Dubai, Seoul, Frankfurt, and the major Central Asian capitals. Uzbekistan Airways is the national carrier.

The **high-speed Afrosiyob train** is the best way to reach Samarkand (around two hours), Bukhara (four hours), and Khiva. Trains depart from Tashkent's main station (Tashkent Vokzal) and book up days in advance — buy through the Uzbekistan Railways website or app, not at the station.

**Overland borders** to Kazakhstan are at Yallama (north) and Chernyaevka (closest to Shymkent). The Kyrgyz border is at Dustlik, a few hours east. Visa requirements have eased for most western nationals — many can enter visa-free for 30 days.
