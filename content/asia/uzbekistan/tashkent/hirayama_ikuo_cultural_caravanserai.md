The Hirayama Ikuo Cultural Caravanserai is a small cultural centre in Tashkent connected with the Japanese painter Hirayama Ikuo, whose work often returned to Silk Road themes. It reflects the cultural bridge between Japan and Uzbekistan as much as the history of caravan routes themselves.

This is a specialist stop, best for travelers already interested in Silk Road art, cultural diplomacy, or quieter museums. It will not replace Tashkent's major Islamic, Soviet, or market sights, but it adds a thoughtful side note to the city's international connections.
