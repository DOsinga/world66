Kukcha Mosque is a large west-side Tashkent mosque rebuilt in the 1990s. The garden behind it contains the mausoleum of Sheikh Zayniddin, a medieval Sufi holy man, making the complex a useful stop for visitors interested in the city's Islamic history away from the central bazaar area.
