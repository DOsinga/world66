A brewpub on the boulevards north of Amir Timur Square, producing reasonably good Bavarian-style lager and a couple of seasonal ales. The food is heavy and beer-friendly — sausages, schnitzels, pretzels — and the place is busiest with Russian-speaking after-work crowds.

A useful break from chaikhana eating. Service is brusque but quick; the small outdoor terrace is the pick in summer.
