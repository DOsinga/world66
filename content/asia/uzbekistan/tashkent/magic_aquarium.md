Magic Aquarium is a walk-through aquarium in Tashkent's Magic City complex, with themed tanks, habitat displays, and the expected shark tunnel. It is aimed mostly at families, but it is one of the city's more polished indoor attractions and works well when the weather makes outdoor sightseeing less appealing.
