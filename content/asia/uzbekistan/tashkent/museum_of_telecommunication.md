The Museum of Telecommunication of Uzbekistan is a specialist Tashkent museum with an extensive collection of working and historic communications equipment. Labels are in English, Russian, and Uzbek, which makes it more accessible than many small technical museums in the region.

It is not a first-stop sight, but it suits travellers interested in Soviet-era technology, telephones, radio, and the infrastructure that connected Uzbekistan before the mobile age.
