Sacred Heart Cathedral is Tashkent's main Roman Catholic church, a Gothic Revival building whose pointed arches and brickwork stand apart from the city's Soviet avenues and Islamic monuments. Its history is tied to Polish and other Catholic communities in Uzbekistan.

The cathedral is most rewarding as an architectural and cultural contrast. It is still an active place of worship, so visit quietly and treat the interior as a living church rather than a museum.
