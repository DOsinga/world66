The Architectural Complex of Al Hakim At-Termizi is one of Termez's key Islamic sites, centred on the mausoleum of the 9th-century scholar and mystic Abu Abdullah Muhammad ibn Ali al-Hakim at-Termizi. The complex grew over centuries near Old Termez, combining shrine, mosque, and later memorial architecture.

It is a contemplative counterweight to the region's Buddhist and archaeological sites. Visit with time to look at the carved details and to understand why Termez matters not just as a frontier city, but as a place of religious scholarship and pilgrimage.
