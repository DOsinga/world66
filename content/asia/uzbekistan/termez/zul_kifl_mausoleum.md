The Zul Kifl Mausoleum stands on an island in the Amu Darya near Termez, associated in local tradition with Dhul-Kifl, a prophet mentioned in the Qur'an. The complex combines pilgrimage, river landscape, and the layered frontier history that makes Termez so unusual.

Getting there requires more planning than the city's central museums and Buddhist ruins, but the setting is memorable. Treat it respectfully as a sacred site, and check local access conditions before setting out, as river routes and permissions can change.
