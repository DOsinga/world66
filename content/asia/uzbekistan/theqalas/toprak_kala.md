Toprak-Kala is one of the most important ruined qalas in the Khorezm oasis, with the remains of a walled town and palace dating from the 1st to 5th centuries CE. The mud-brick site still shows the scale of the ancient settlement, including palace foundations, streets, and defensive walls.

It is a worthwhile stop on a qalas route from Urgench or Khiva, especially for travellers interested in archaeology and the desert history of Karakalpakstan.
