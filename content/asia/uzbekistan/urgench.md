Urgench is a transit city in the Khorezm region of western Uzbekistan, and it is honest about that fact. Most visitors pass through on the way to [Khiva](/asia/uzbekistan/khiva) — 30 km to the southeast — or heading north toward the [Aral Sea](/asia/uzbekistan/moynaq). The surrounding landscape is flat agricultural land, irrigated by a network of canals drawing on the Amu Darya.

The town has a modest collection of cultural monuments worth exploring if you have a few hours. The [Al-Khwarizmi Complex](/asia/uzbekistan/urgench/al_khwarizmi_complex) commemorates the ninth-century mathematician born in this region whose name gave the world both "algorithm" and "algebra." The [Avesta Architectural Complex](/asia/uzbekistan/urgench/avesta_architectural_complex) and [Dashkin Bobo Complex](/asia/uzbekistan/urgench/dashkin_bobo_complex) offer further glimpses of the region's layered history. The [Contemporary Art Museum of Uzbekistan](/asia/uzbekistan/urgench/contemporary_art_museum) has a small branch here showing work by Uzbek artists.

Urgench is the regional transport hub: flights connect it to Tashkent and beyond, and shared taxis scatter in all directions from the bus station. If you are heading to Khiva, there is no real reason to spend a night here — but if circumstances require it, the town is perfectly comfortable and largely untouched by tourism.
