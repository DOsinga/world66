  Muhammad ibn Musa al-Khwarizmi was born in the Khwarazm region around 780 AD and became one of the most influential mathematicians in history. The word "algorithm" derives from the Latin form of his name, and "algebra" comes directly from the title of his treatise. He never lived in this modern city, but the region claims him justly.
---

The Al-Khwarizmi Complex honours Urgench's most famous historical connection: the ninth-century mathematician and scholar whose name gave the world the words "algorithm" and "algebra". The complex centres on a symbolic mausoleum and formal garden, set along the street named after him, and was built to mark the 1200th anniversary of his birth.

The design blends traditional Central Asian architectural motifs with a monumental scale. The garden is well-tended and provides a quiet place to sit away from the busy roads. The monument itself is large enough to feel like a genuine civic statement, and the surrounding plaza is often used for cultural events. If you have an hour to spare between buses, this is where to spend it.
