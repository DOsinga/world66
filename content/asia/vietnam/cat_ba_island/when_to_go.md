---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

The best months are **April to early June** and **September to October**: warm, dry, and stable, with calm seas for boating and clear visibility from the karst summits. July and August are the hottest and busiest, with the Vietnamese domestic crowd arriving at weekends — beaches get packed and accommodation prices spike.

The northern Vietnamese **winter** (November to March) is cool, often grey and drizzly, and seas can be rough enough to suspend ferries. Many guesthouses scale back service and some boat operators close. If you do come in winter, bring a warm layer and plan for a couple of weather days.
