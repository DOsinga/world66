Datanla Falls sits about 5km south of Dalat along the road toward Phan Rang, and it marks the entry point to the most accessible canyoning in the Central Highlands. A steep path descends from the car park to the first cascade, and a funicular is available for the return. Beyond the tourist infrastructure at the top, the falls descend through several more levels of jungle gorge — the domain of the canyoning guides who run abseil and jump sequences through the canyon most mornings. Canyoning here is beginner-friendly and genuinely exciting; operators in central Dalat book the full-day tours that use Datanla as their main venue.
