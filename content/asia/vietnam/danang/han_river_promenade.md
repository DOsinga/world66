The Han River Promenade gives Da Nang a pleasant urban spine between the beach districts and the older city centre. The riverside paths are best in the early morning or evening, when the heat drops and the bridges, boats, and skyline start to define the view.

It is not a single monument, but it is one of the easiest ways to understand modern Da Nang's layout. Walk a stretch around the Dragon Bridge or Han River Bridge, then continue into nearby streets for cafes, seafood, or night-market energy.
