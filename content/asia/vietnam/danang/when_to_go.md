---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

Da Nang has a tropical climate with two distinct seasons. The **dry season** (February–August) is the best time to visit, with hot and sunny weather, temperatures 25–35°C, and calm seas ideal for swimming. March through June sees the hottest, driest weather.

The **wet season** (September–January) brings significant rainfall, particularly October–November when heavy rains and the occasional typhoon can disrupt travel. Da Nang is one of the cities most affected by central Vietnam's autumn flooding — rivers can overflow and streets flood. January is the coolest month (around 20°C), still dry by this point but overcast.

For beach holidays, May and June offer the best combination of hot weather, dry days, and fewer crowds than July and August (peak domestic tourist season).
