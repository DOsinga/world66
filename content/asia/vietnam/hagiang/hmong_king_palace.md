Hmong King Palace, also known as the Vuong family mansion, is one of the most memorable historic stops on the Ha Giang Loop. Built in the early 20th century for Vuong Chinh Duc, a powerful Hmong leader, it mixes Chinese-influenced architecture with the defensive instincts of a mountain stronghold.

The house sits in Sa Phin, among the stone fences and high karst country north of Dong Van. Carved timber, thick walls, courtyards, and displays of old weapons and family objects give it more atmosphere than most small regional museums.
