Tham Luong Waterfall is a remote cascade in the Ha Giang mountains, with two main drops and several smaller falls. One pool is suitable for swimming when conditions are right.

Finding it is part of the effort: the gravel approach can be rough and the path is not always obvious. It is best for travellers already exploring the Dong Van and Ha Giang back roads, not for anyone in a hurry.
