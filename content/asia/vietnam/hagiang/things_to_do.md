title: Things to Do
type: section
---

The Ha Giang Loop is the organizing principle for almost everything here. The 200-kilometre circuit through the [Dong Van Karst Plateau](/asia/vietnam/hagiang/dong_van_karst_plateau) takes visitors past the [Ma Pi Leng Pass](/asia/vietnam/hagiang/ma_pi_leng_pass), through the [Dong Van old quarter](/asia/vietnam/hagiang/dong_van_old_quarter), and down to [Meo Vac](/asia/vietnam/hagiang/meo_vac) — with detours to the [Lung Cu Flag Tower](/asia/vietnam/hagiang/lung_cu_flag_tower) at Vietnam's northern tip and the [Vuong Family Palace](/asia/vietnam/hagiang/vuong_family_palace) near Dong Van. The [Nho Que River](/asia/vietnam/hagiang/nho_que_river) canyon can be seen from above on the pass road and explored by boat from below. In October, the [buckwheat flower fields](/asia/vietnam/hagiang/buckwheat_flower_fields) transform the plateau. To the west, the [Hoang Su Phi terraces](/asia/vietnam/hagiang/hoang_su_phi_terraces) offer a quieter alternative to Sapa's rice paddy scenery.
