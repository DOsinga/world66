Yen Minh is a small district town roughly halfway between Ha Giang city and Dong Van, sitting where the road begins its serious climb into the karst plateau. Most Ha Giang Loop riders stop here for the night — it has several decent guesthouses and a handful of restaurants — and the setting above a small valley is pleasant without being dramatic. The town itself is unremarkable, but the road leading north from Yen Minh through pine forests and the early karst formations is one of the nicer stretches of the Loop: less vertiginous than the Ma Pi Leng section, but with an atmosphere of gradual elevation and thickening landscape.

The Sunday market at Yen Minh brings in minority traders from nearby villages, and the morning fog that often sits in the valley gives the weekly gathering a misty, slightly otherworldly quality. The pine-forested hills immediately around town are genuinely pretty and offer easier walking than the high plateau.
