The main case for an overnight in Haiphong is its position as a less-touristed alternative gateway to Halong Bay and Cát Bà.

**Cát Bà Island**, forty minutes north by hydrofoil, is the best day-trip-or-overnight from the city: hiking in the national park, swimming on the southern beaches, and boat trips into the western edge of Halong Bay. With an overnight stay there is time for everything.

**Halong Bay**, the famous limestone karst seascape, is most often reached via Halong City east of here, but Haiphong-based operators run their own day cruises that go into the same waters from the southern side. They are typically cheaper and less crowded than the Hanoi-based equivalents.

**Đồ Sơn**, twenty kilometres south, offers a beach, a casino, and the bones of the French colonial seaside, all in a few hours.

**Bạch Đằng River**, north toward the border with Quảng Ninh province, is the site of three famous victorious naval battles against China and the Mongols. A small museum tells the story, and the surrounding shrimp farms and pagodas make a half-day loop.

**Hanoi**, the national capital, is only two hours by train from Haiphong — a comfortable enough trip to do as a long day if needed.
