A novel set in contemporary Hanoi, tracing the intersection of three lives: an old pho cook who has fed artists and dissidents for decades, an American woman searching for her father's Vietnamese paintings, and a young tour guide trying to modernise while staying true to his family. Gibb spent time in Vietnam researching the novel, and the Hanoi she depicts — ancient and modernising at once, still haunted by war — feels true. It is one of the gentler approaches to the country, and a good counterpart to the harder-edged war literature.
