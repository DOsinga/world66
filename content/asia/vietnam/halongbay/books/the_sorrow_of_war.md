---

Vietnam's most celebrated novel about the Vietnam War, written from the North Vietnamese perspective by a veteran who was one of only ten survivors from his 500-man brigade. Published in Vietnam in 1990 and later banned there for its unflinching account of trauma, disillusionment, and the lingering wounds of combat. The novel moves back and forth in time between the jungle fighting of the war and the broken peace that followed. Heartbreaking, beautifully written, and unlike anything produced about the war in the West.
