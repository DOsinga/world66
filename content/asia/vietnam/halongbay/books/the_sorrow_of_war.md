A novel told from the North Vietnamese perspective — something almost unheard of in English-language Vietnam War literature. Bao Ninh fought with the Glorious 27th Youth Brigade, and this autobiographical novel follows a young soldier through the jungle campaigns and into an uncertain peacetime. It was originally published in Vietnam in 1991 and was quickly suppressed by the government for its non-heroic, non-ideological tone. It is now recognised as one of the great war novels, and a reminder that the war had two sides, each with its own losses and its own stories.
