Winner of the Pulitzer Prize for Fiction in 2016, *The Sympathizer* is narrated by a Communist spy embedded with a South Vietnamese general fleeing to the United States after the fall of Saigon in 1975. It is a novel about double identity, colonial history, and the ways in which the Vietnam War was told — and mistold — by both sides. Nguyen writes with ferocious intelligence and dark humour. The best novel yet written about Vietnam and its diaspora, and an essential counterweight to the American military narratives that have dominated the story.
