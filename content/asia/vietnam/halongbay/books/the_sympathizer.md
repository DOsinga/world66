Winner of the Pulitzer Prize in 2016, this is the definitive novel of the Vietnamese diaspora — narrated by a communist spy embedded in the South Vietnamese military who flees to America after the fall of Saigon. Nguyen writes with savage wit about colonialism, identity, and the experience of being perpetually between two worlds. The novel's first-person voice is one of the great achievements in recent American fiction. A rare book that makes you see the Vietnam War from a Vietnamese perspective without sentimentality or propaganda.
