---
title: "Eating Out"
type: section
---

Most visitors eat aboard their cruise boat, and the quality varies with the boat's price tier. Fresh seafood — crab, shrimp, squid, and various reef fish pulled from the bay — is the staple, and even budget cruises typically do it well. The fish and squid are usually excellent and very fresh.

Ha Long City's restaurant scene is undistinguished; if you need to eat ashore, the local seafood restaurants near Ben Do wharf serve reasonable crab and prawn. Cat Ba Island's town has a more developed restaurant strip along the harbour front, with Vietnamese seafood restaurants and a few Western-oriented places catering to backpackers. The crab at the waterfront restaurants on Cat Ba is usually excellent.

Vietnamese staples — pho, bun cha, banh mi — are available throughout, though the best versions of these are found in Hanoi rather than at the bay.
