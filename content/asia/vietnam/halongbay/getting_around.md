---
title: "Getting Around"
type: section
---

On the water, most visitors move around Ha Long Bay aboard the boat they arrive on — whether a day-cruise vessel or an overnight junk. Your itinerary determines which islands, caves, and lagoons you visit. Day-trippers have limited flexibility; overnight cruise passengers have more, though the route is generally set by the operator. Kayaking is offered by almost every cruise and is the best way to explore sea caves and narrow passages too shallow for motorised boats.

Ha Long City itself is a working port town with limited tourist infrastructure. Ferries connect Ha Long City to Cat Ba Island, where you can hire motorbikes, bicycles, or cars to explore the national park and the island's quieter beaches. Cat Ba is a better base than Ha Long City if you want to spend more than one night in the area.

Taxis and motorbike taxis (xe om) are available in Ha Long City and on Cat Ba Island. On Cat Ba, renting a bicycle or motorbike is the most practical way to get beyond the main town.
