---
title: "Getting There"
type: section
---

Ha Long Bay is about 170 kilometres east of Hanoi, typically reached in about three to four hours by road. Most visitors join an overnight cruise that handles all transport: tour operators in Hanoi's Old Quarter run departure shuttles directly to Ha Long City's harbour, where the junks depart.

**From Hanoi by road:** Express buses (Hoang Long, Camel Travel, and others) depart from My Dinh bus station and several Old Quarter operators. The journey takes three to four hours depending on traffic, which can be heavy on weekends. Many cruise packages include a minibus pickup from Hanoi hotels.

**Via Cat Ba Island:** A faster route is the Haiphong–Cat Ba hydrofoil (about 45 minutes from Haiphong ferry terminal), reached from Hanoi by express bus to Haiphong (two hours). This puts you on Cat Ba Island rather than Ha Long City, which gives access to the southern bay and the Lan Ha Bay area.

**By seaplane:** Several operators run seaplane flights between Hanoi's Noi Bai Airport and Ha Long Bay, cutting the journey to about 45 minutes and offering an aerial view of the karst landscape on approach. Prices are significantly higher than ground transport but the experience is distinctive.
