---
title: "Getting There"
type: section
---

Ha Long Bay is approximately 165 km (103 miles) east of Hanoi. Most visitors arrive from the capital.

**By bus:** Tourist minibuses and limousine vans run daily from Hanoi's Old Quarter and from several hotels directly to Ha Long City's cruise ports. Journey time is three to four hours depending on traffic and which port you use. Many cruise operators include this transfer in the package price. Public buses from Hanoi's Giap Bat or My Dinh stations are cheaper but slower.

**By private car:** A taxi or hired car from Hanoi takes around three hours on the Hanoi–Ha Long expressway, which has significantly cut travel times since it opened. This is the most flexible option if you are not on a package cruise.

**By seaplane:** Hai Au Aviation operates seaplane flights from Hanoi to Ha Long Bay, landing directly on the water. The flight takes around 45 minutes and the views are remarkable. Expensive, but worth considering for a special occasion.

Most visitors enter the bay aboard cruise boats departing from Ha Long City, Tuan Chau Island, or Hon Gai. Cat Ba Island, accessible by ferry from Ha Long City or by direct ferry from Hai Phong, is an increasingly popular alternative base.
