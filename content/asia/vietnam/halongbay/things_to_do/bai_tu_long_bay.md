The section of the larger Ha Long Bay UNESCO World Heritage zone northeast of the main tourist corridor, receiving a fraction of the cruise traffic despite offering the same limestone karst scenery. The area around Van Don Island and the outer islands is largely undeveloped, with fishing villages and emptier anchorages. A small number of operators specifically target Bai Tu Long Bay for overnight cruises, and the difference in ambience compared to the main zone is considerable — you can anchor in a bay at night with no other boats in sight. Worth seeking out if you have already done the standard Ha Long itinerary or want to avoid the crowds.
