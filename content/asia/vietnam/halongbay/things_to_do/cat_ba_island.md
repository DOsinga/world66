The largest island in Ha Long Bay, Cat Ba has a national park covering the interior, a small town with guesthouses and restaurants, and a bay-facing beach. The national park has hiking trails through tropical forest — the trail to the summit of Ngu Lam Peak takes about two hours and gives views over the southern bay — and is one of the last habitats of the critically endangered Cat Ba langur, a golden-headed monkey found nowhere else on earth. Cat Ba is increasingly used as a base for exploring the southern part of Ha Long Bay (and the adjacent Lan Ha Bay, which many travellers consider more beautiful and much less crowded). Boats and kayaks are available for hire in Cat Ba town.
