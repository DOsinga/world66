The best way to explore Ha Long Bay's hidden geography. Kayaks can pass through sea caves at low tide into enclosed lagoons completely surrounded by limestone walls — floored-caldera formations where the roof has collapsed but the walls remain. Luon Cave, Ba Ham Lake, and the lagoon at Ba Trai Dao are among the finest of these hidden water worlds. Most overnight cruise packages include kayaking as part of the itinerary. Independently renting kayaks from Cat Ba Island gives more freedom, particularly for exploring the less-visited southern sections of the bay. Early morning paddling — before the tour boats arrive — gives you a different experience entirely.
