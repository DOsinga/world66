The best way to experience Ha Long Bay is to sleep on it. Overnight cruises on traditional wooden junk boats — departing from Ha Long City or Cat Ba Island — allow you to wake up anchored among the limestone towers at dawn, before the day-trip boats arrive and the mist has lifted from the water. Two-night cruises push further into the bay and cover more territory; a single night is the minimum worth doing. Most packages include kayaking, cave visits, and meals on board. Quality of boats varies considerably — the mid-range boats (around $100-150 USD per person per night) are generally more comfortable than the cheapest options. Book through a reputable tour agent in Hanoi rather than booking last-minute at the dock.

story: >
  The bay's name translates roughly as "where the dragon descends into the sea." Vietnamese legend holds that a family of dragons sent by the gods to defend Vietnam from invaders spat jewels and jade into the sea, which became the limestone islands that blocked enemy ships. The dragon family liked the bay so much they stayed, and Ha Long was where the mother landed.
