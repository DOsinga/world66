The most impressive of the accessible limestone caves in Ha Long Bay, discovered in 1993 and opened to visitors soon after. A steep staircase climbs from the water's edge to a cavern over 10,000 square metres in area, hung with stalactites and draped in stalagmites, with coloured lighting illuminating the most dramatic formations. The cave shows evidence of human habitation going back thousands of years. It is usually visited as part of a cruise itinerary — the boat anchors offshore and visitors take a tender to the cave entrance. The crowds can be considerable in peak season; arriving in the afternoon, after the main tour groups have left, is more pleasant.
