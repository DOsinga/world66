---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

Ha Long Bay's climate is shaped by its position in northeastern Vietnam, and timing your visit significantly affects the experience.

**October to April** is the best period. Temperatures are cooler (15–25°C / 59–77°F), the water is calm, and visibility in the bay is generally excellent. November to January can be misty, which softens the landscape and makes the karst formations feel even more atmospheric — though it can also affect visibility for photographs. February brings the risk of drizzle and fog.

**May to September** is hot and humid, with occasional typhoons passing through the Gulf of Tonkin between July and September. Cruises are sometimes cancelled or rerouted during severe weather. That said, summer is when Vietnamese domestic tourists flood the bay, and the warm water is good for swimming.

The **Tet holiday** (late January or February) brings the heaviest domestic crowds, with prices and demand at their peak. If your dates overlap with Tet, book well in advance.

For most international visitors, the shoulder months of October–November and March–April offer the best combination of weather, manageable crowds, and price.
