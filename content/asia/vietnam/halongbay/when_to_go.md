---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

Ha Long Bay has a complex climate shaped by the Gulf of Tonkin, and there is no single perfect season.

**March to May** is often cited as the best period. Temperatures are mild (18 to 28 degrees Celsius), rain is minimal, and the bay often has clear visibility and calm water. Mist can be dramatic rather than obstructing.

**June to August** is hot, humid, and the peak tourist season — Vietnamese domestic tourism combined with international visitors makes this the busiest time. Typhoons can affect the bay from June to November, occasionally causing cruises to be cancelled or rerouted with little notice.

**September to November** can be excellent once the typhoon risk decreases — October especially tends to be clear, warm, and less crowded than summer. The water temperature stays comfortable for swimming.

**December to February** brings the cool, misty, drizzly season that produces the atmospheric photographs of junks drifting through low cloud. Temperatures can drop to 10 to 15 degrees Celsius — cold by Vietnamese standards — but the light is often extraordinary and crowds are reduced. Some cruises continue operating; others reduce frequency.

The Chinese New Year / Tet period (late January or February) sees significant domestic tourism and partial closures; book well ahead if travelling then.
