Vietnam has produced and attracted writers of the first order, and Hanoi in particular has been the subject and setting of some of the most powerful fiction and reportage of the 20th century. The novels and memoirs here were chosen because they illuminate something specific about the city and the country — not just the war, though the war is unavoidable, but the texture of Vietnamese life, the complexity of colonial history, and what Vietnam has become in the decades since reunification. Read them before you go, or buy them at one of the Old Quarter's used bookshops and read them while you're here.
