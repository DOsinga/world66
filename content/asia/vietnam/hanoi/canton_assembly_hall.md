Canton Assembly Hall was built for Hanoi's Guangdong Chinese community and later converted to a kindergarten after the 1979 Sino-Vietnamese War. Restored in 2021, it now functions as an exhibition space and gives a glimpse of the Old Quarter's Chinese merchant history.
