Hanoi is the launching pad for most of northern Vietnam. **Halong Bay** and its limestone seascape is the classic trip — realistically an overnight cruise rather than a day trip. **Ninh Binh** (nicknamed "inland Halong") is easier, closer, and can be done in a long day: karst towers rising out of rice paddies, with rowing-boat trips through the caves at Tam Coc or Trang An. **[Ba Vi National Park](/asia/vietnam/ba_vi_national_park)**, an hour west by motorbike or hired car, offers cloud forest, French colonial ruins at the summit, and an escape from the city heat. **Sapa** in the northern mountains takes longer to reach — overnight train to Lao Cai, then a winding road up — but rewards you with trekking and visits to Hmong, Dzao, and Tay villages.
