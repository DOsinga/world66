The Fujian Assembly Hall was built for Hanoi's Chinese community from Fujian province. After being converted to a primary school following the 1979 Sino-Vietnamese War, it was restored in 2015 and again shows the carved woodwork and temple-like details of its earlier life.

It is a compact stop in the Old Quarter, best folded into a walk through the area's markets, clan halls, and old trading streets.
