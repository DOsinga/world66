A narrow café up a flight of stairs on Nguyen Huu Huan Street in the Old Quarter, opened by Nguyen Giang in 1946. Giang created egg coffee (cà phê trứng) during the years when fresh milk was scarce — whipping egg yolk with sugar and condensed milk into a custard foam and pouring it over a strong Vietnamese espresso. The result is dense, sweet, slightly eggy, and completely unlike anything else. The café is still in the family; the recipe is largely unchanged. Sit in the front room by the window.
