Hanoi's Presidential Palace was completed in 1906 as the residence of the French Governor-General of Indochina. Visitors normally view the yellow colonial building from the outside as part of the Ba Dinh historic complex, where its grandeur contrasts with Ho Chi Minh's nearby stilt house.
