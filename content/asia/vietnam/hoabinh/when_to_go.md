---
title: When to Go
type: section
---

October to April is the best time to visit. The weather is cooler and drier, ideal for trekking between villages and taking boat trips on the lake. The rice terraces in the surrounding valleys are at their most dramatic just after harvest, when the paddies turn gold.

May to September brings the monsoon. The upside is that the lake reaches its highest level — important for Thung Nai, where the water must be high enough for boats to weave between the limestone outcrops. Waterfalls in the hills are fuller, and the terraces are a vivid green. The downside is humidity, occasional road closures on mountain routes, and persistent rain.

Hoa Binh city has no major festival that warrants a special trip, though the ethnic communities in the surrounding districts hold traditional celebrations around Tet (late January or February). The White Thai villages of Muong Bi valley come alive around this time with gong performances and communal feasts.
