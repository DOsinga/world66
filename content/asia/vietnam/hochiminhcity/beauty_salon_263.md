Vo Van Tan and the surrounding streets in District 3 form the backbone of the city's more refined restaurant scene — the area that Saigon's middle class and long-term expats frequent rather than the tourist strip in District 1. Independent Vietnamese restaurants, Japanese ramen shops, and specialty coffee roasters cluster in the shaded shophouses along this stretch. It is quieter than the Pham Ngu Lao area, the food quality is consistently higher, and prices remain far below what comparable meals would cost in Bangkok or Kuala Lumpur.
