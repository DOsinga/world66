The FITO Museum is a small, specialised museum on traditional Vietnamese medicine. Its rooms are packed with old tools, jars, books, and ingredients used to prepare herbal remedies, giving the subject more texture than a simple display case could.

The building itself helps: carved wood, courtyards, and narrow staircases make the visit feel more intimate than many city museums. It is a good stop if you have already seen Ho Chi Minh City's headline war and history museums and want something more unusual.
