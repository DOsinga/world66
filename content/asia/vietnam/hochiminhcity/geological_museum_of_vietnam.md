The Geological Museum of Vietnam is a specialist museum for visitors interested in rocks, minerals, fossils, and natural history. The displays are old-fashioned and lightly interpreted, but the collection is unusual enough to reward geology-minded travelers with some extra time in Ho Chi Minh City.
