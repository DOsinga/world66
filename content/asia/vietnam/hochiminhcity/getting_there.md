---
title: "Getting There"
type: section
---

Ho Chi Minh City is served by Tan Son Nhat International Airport (SGN), about 7 kilometres northwest of the city centre. Taxis from the airport take 20–40 minutes depending on traffic; Grab (the regional Uber equivalent) is cheaper and more reliable — book from the arrivals hall using the app. There is no rail connection from the airport to the city.

Domestic flights connect to Hanoi, Da Nang, Hue, Nha Trang, and other Vietnamese cities; several airlines compete and prices are low. The overnight train to Hanoi takes about 30 hours and is a classic journey. The train station (Saigon Station) is in District 3, a short taxi ride from the tourist centre.
