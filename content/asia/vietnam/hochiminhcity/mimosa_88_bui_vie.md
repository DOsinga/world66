Mimosa is a locally-run restaurant on Bui Vien Street serving a mix of Vietnamese and simple Western dishes at very low prices. A breakfast omelette with baguette costs around 25,000 đồng; a full Vietnamese dinner stays under 100,000 đồng. For the budget traveller who wants clean surroundings and an honest kitchen rather than the tourist menus that dominate the nearby backpacker strip, it is a reliable fallback.
