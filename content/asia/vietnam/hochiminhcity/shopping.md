---
title: "Shopping"
type: section
---

Ho Chi Minh City is the shopping capital of Vietnam. The best things to buy are Vietnamese: lacquerware, silk, conical hats, tailored ao dai (traditional dress), coffee equipment, and handicrafts. Bến Thành Market is the most famous shopping destination, though prices require negotiation. Dong Khoi Street has upmarket boutiques and galleries. The Ben Thanh area's night market and the surrounding streets are best for souvenirs after dark.
