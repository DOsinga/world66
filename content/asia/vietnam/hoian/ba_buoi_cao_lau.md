A small family-run stall on Phan Chu Trinh that serves cao lau — the thick wheat noodles with slices of pork, bean sprouts, and herbs that Hoi An claims as its own. The broth is deeper and slightly smokier than the tourist-restaurant versions, and the portions are larger. Plastic chairs, shared tables, low prices. Open for breakfast and lunch only; it closes when the noodles run out, usually by early afternoon.
