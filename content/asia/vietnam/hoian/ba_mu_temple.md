A small but historically significant temple on Nguyen Thi Minh Khai Street, built in the 17th century and dedicated to the three protective goddesses of the Cham people. The red-painted entrance gate and compact courtyard make it easy to miss, but it has been continuously maintained for over 400 years. A good stopping point at the western end of the Old Town circuit.
