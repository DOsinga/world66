---
title: Bars and Cafes
type: section
---

The Old Town's bar scene is concentrated along Nguyen Thai Hoc and the riverside stretch of Bach Dang. Drinks are cheap and the lantern-lit streets make for a pleasant evening out. The town is not a party destination — it closes down earlier than Hanoi or Ho Chi Minh City — but there are good spots for a cold beer or a Vietnamese coffee.
