A smaller, family-run tailor on Phan Boi Chau with a good reputation for women's clothing — particularly silk dresses and ao dai, the traditional Vietnamese tunic. Less expensive than Yaly and more personal in service. The owner speaks good English and is honest about what cuts will and will not work well. If you want one good garment rather than a suitcase full of mediocre ones, this is the kind of place to go.
