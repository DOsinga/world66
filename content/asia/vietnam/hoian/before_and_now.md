A two-storey bar on Le Loi with a rooftop terrace overlooking the tiled rooftops of the Old Town. Good for a sundowner before the night market fills up. The drink menu is straightforward — Vietnamese beer, cocktails, and fresh fruit shakes — and the prices are low. The terrace gets busy from around 6pm; arrive before then if you want a seat with a view. One of the more reliable low-key options in a street full of tourist-focused bars.
