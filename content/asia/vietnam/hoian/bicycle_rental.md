Bicycle rental is available from most guesthouses and dozens of shops around the Old Town. The going rate is low — a few dollars a day for a basic single-speed or slightly more for a bicycle in good condition. The roads to An Bang Beach, Cua Dai Beach, and the villages east of town are flat and light on traffic, making this genuinely one of the better cycling destinations in Vietnam. Electric bicycles are increasingly available for those who don't fancy pedalling in the heat.
