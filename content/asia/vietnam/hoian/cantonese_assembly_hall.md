Built in 1885 by the Cantonese merchant community, this assembly hall on Tran Phu Street is notable for its intricate wood carvings, colourful ceramic mosaics, and a large courtyard with a decorative stone fountain. The main hall is dedicated to Quan Cong and remains an active place of worship. Better preserved than some of the other assembly halls and less crowded than Phuc Kien.
