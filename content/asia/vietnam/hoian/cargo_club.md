A two-storey restaurant and café on the riverside stretch of Nguyen Thai Hoc, with good views of the Thu Bon from the upper terrace. The café does reliable Western breakfasts and Vietnamese food, plus a decent selection of pastries and cakes — useful after a morning of sightseeing. The restaurant side handles dinner well enough. Not cheap by local standards, but the setting earns the premium. Good spot for a cold beer watching boat traffic on the river.
