The Chinese Assembly Hall was built jointly by Hoi An's Cantonese, Teochew, Hokkien, Hainanese, and Hakka communities. It is part of the old town's network of Chinese community halls, temples, and merchant houses.

Visit it as part of a slow walk through the heritage core, where the different assembly halls show how overseas Chinese communities shaped Hoi An's trading history.
