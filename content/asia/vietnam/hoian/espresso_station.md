A small coffee shop on Tran Hung Dao serving properly made espresso drinks alongside Vietnamese coffee — both the iced ca phe sua da and the slower egg coffee variant. The interior is compact and fills quickly in the mornings. A sensible alternative to the chain cafés that have appeared around the Old Town. Good place to sit and plan the day before the sightseeing crowds arrive.
