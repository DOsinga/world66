The smallest and most sombre of Hoi An's Chinese assembly halls, tucked on Tran Phu Street. Built in 1851 to honour 108 Hainanese traders wrongly executed by the Vietnamese emperor, the hall has an elegance that belies its size — the altar screens and decorative roof ridges are among the finest in town. Worth a few minutes if you are doing the assembly hall circuit.
