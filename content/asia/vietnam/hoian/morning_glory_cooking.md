The cooking school attached to Morning Glory restaurant is among the most popular in town, offering morning and afternoon classes in a kitchen above the restaurant. Classes typically cover four dishes from the Hoi An repertoire — white rose dumplings, cao lau, fried wontons, and a soup — with the recipe booklet to take home. The instruction is clear and the class sizes reasonable. No market tour is included, but the central location is convenient if you are based in the Old Town.
