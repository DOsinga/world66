One of the more established restaurants in the Old Town, run by a local chef and cooking school. The menu covers the full range of Hoi An regional dishes — white rose dumplings, cao lau, fried wontons, and grilled river fish — along with broader Vietnamese cooking. The setting is a restored merchant house on Nguyen Thai Hoc. Prices are higher than street stalls but the quality is consistent. Good for a proper sit-down meal without spending a lot.
