The Museum of Sa Huynh Culture focuses on the early culture that occupied the central Vietnamese coast before Hoi An became a major trading port. Its core collection includes pottery and burial urns from the 1st and 2nd centuries. The museum is close to the Japanese Covered Bridge, so it fits easily into an Old Town walk.
