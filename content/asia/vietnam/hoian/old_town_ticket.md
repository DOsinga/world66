Entrance to most of the heritage sites in the Old Town — the assembly halls, old houses, the Museum of Trade Ceramics, and the Japanese Covered Bridge — is covered by a single combined ticket sold at booths around the perimeter. The ticket allows entry to five sites of your choice from the list. You can buy multiple tickets. Buying the ticket before entering the most crowded sites (Phuc Kien Assembly Hall, Tan Ky Old House) avoids having to queue at individual entrances. Ticket booths are on Tran Phu Street and at the main entry points to the car-free zone.
