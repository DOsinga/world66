Phung Hung Old House is a traditional two-storey timber house close to Hoi An's Japanese Covered Bridge. The same family has lived here for generations, and the building shows the mix of Vietnamese, Chinese, and Japanese influences that shaped the old trading port.

Visits are short and usually guided by a family member. It is one of several old houses in Hoi An Ancient Town, so it works best as part of a slow walk through the lanes rather than as a standalone sight.
