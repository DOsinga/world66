A Chinese-style temple on Tran Phu Street, built in 1653 and dedicated to Quan Cong, the Han Dynasty general venerated across southern China and Vietnam as a god of war and protector of merchants. The main hall contains a large gilded statue of Quan Cong flanked by his generals, surrounded by incense coils and elaborate offerings. The temple is covered by the Old Town combined ticket and is one of the better-maintained religious buildings in the Heritage Zone.
