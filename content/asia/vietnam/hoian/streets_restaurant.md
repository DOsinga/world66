A non-profit café and restaurant training programme on Le Loi Street. The food is Vietnamese and well prepared — the spring rolls and pho are particularly good — and eating here directly supports the students being trained. Worth knowing about for anyone who wants their tourist money to do something useful. Breakfast and lunch are the stronger meals; the setting is a quiet courtyard away from the Old Town crowds.
