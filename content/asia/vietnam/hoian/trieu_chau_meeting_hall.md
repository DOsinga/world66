Trieu Chau Meeting Hall is a Teochew community hall built in 1887, close to Hoi An's better-known Fujian Assembly Hall. It reflects the Chinese merchant communities that helped shape the old trading port.

The hall is a worthwhile stop as part of an old-town circuit, especially for visitors comparing Hoi An's assembly halls, temples, and clan buildings rather than only seeing the Japanese Covered Bridge.
