Bánh bao vạc — white rose dumplings — are one of Hoi An's signature dishes: translucent steamed parcels of prawn, shaped to resemble flowers and topped with crispy fried shallots. This family restaurant claims to be the original producer, and most places in town buy their dumplings from here. Ordering them at the source makes a certain kind of sense. The menu also includes cao lau and fried wontons. A reliable, unfussy lunch stop.
