Among the dozens of tailors in the Old Town, Yaly has a reliable reputation for quality finishing and accurate sizing. They handle suits, dresses, shirts, and trousers. Bring a reference garment or clear photos of what you want — communication about cut and detail works better with examples than with words. Allow at least two fittings and 48 hours minimum. Prices are higher than the backstreet tailors but the results tend to be better. The branch on Nguyen Thai Hoc is the main one.
