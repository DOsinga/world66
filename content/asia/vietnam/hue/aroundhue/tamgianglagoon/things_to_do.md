---
title: Things to Do
type: section
---

The lagoon is the attraction — there is no town as such. Hire a boat to explore the channels and oyster farms, eat seafood at a stilt restaurant, or time a visit to catch sunset over the water from one of the floating villages. Most travellers come as a half-day or sunset trip from [Hue](/asia/vietnam/hue), 12 kilometres south.
