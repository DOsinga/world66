---
title: "Getting There"
type: section
---

Thuan An Beach is 15 kilometres east of Hue, an easy drive or motorbike ride along the road that follows the Huong River. The journey takes around 30 minutes.

**By motorbike or bicycle**

The most pleasant way to get there. The road from Hue follows the southern bank of the Huong River, passing temples and rice paddies, and is flat and well-surfaced throughout. Bicycles can make the journey in about an hour.

**By car or taxi**

Taxis from Hue take around 30 minutes. Agree a price before you leave, or use a ride-hailing app. A return trip with waiting time costs a reasonable sum by Western standards.

**By xe om (motorbike taxi)**

Xe om drivers in Hue are familiar with the beach and the trip. Negotiate the price at the start — a return with an hour of waiting time is the standard arrangement.
