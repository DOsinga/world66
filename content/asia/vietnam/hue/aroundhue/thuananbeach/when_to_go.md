---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

The best time to visit Thuan An Beach is from April to September, when the weather is dry and the water calm. The peak period is June to August, when Vietnamese families flock to the beach on weekends.

The central Vietnamese coast receives its heaviest rainfall between October and December, when typhoons can hit the region and swimming is inadvisable. January and February are cool and overcast. March marks the start of the dry season and is a pleasant shoulder-season option with fewer crowds.
