Bach Long Glass Bridge is Moc Chau's most modern attraction, a long glass walkway suspended above the Muong Sang landscape. It is engineered for spectacle: transparent panels underfoot, valley views, and a strong dose of vertigo.

Whether you love it depends on your tolerance for ticketed viewpoints. It is undeniably popular with Vietnamese visitors and easy to combine with Dai Yem Waterfall, but travellers looking for quiet highland atmosphere may prefer the tea roads and villages.
