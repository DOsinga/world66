Hang Doi Cave, often called Bat Cave in English, sits close to Moc Chau town and is one of the easiest sights to fit into a short visit. Steps lead up to a limestone chamber with stalactites, cool air, and views back toward the plateau.

It is not a wild caving experience; it is a convenient town-edge stop. That makes it useful on arrival day, or when weather makes the farther waterfalls and mountain roads less appealing.
