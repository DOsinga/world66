---
title: Eating Out
type: section
---

The seafood along the main strip (Nguyen Dinh Chieu road) ranges from tourist-standard to genuinely good. The restaurants on the inland side of the road are generally cheaper and more likely to have local Vietnamese clientele. Look for sun-dried squid, fish hotpot (lau ca), and thick noodle soup with fishcakes (banh canh cha ca) — these are the regional specialities and far more interesting than the generic Western menus.

**Lam Tong** on the main strip is popular with kiters for its large bowls of noodle soup at low prices — the kind of place that does one thing very well. The **Moon Restaurant** near the beach is known for its pizzas — a surprise in a Vietnamese beach resort, but well-regarded by long-term residents. For fresh seafood by weight at the embankment stalls, the standard approach is to pick your fish, agree a price, and specify how you want it cooked.

The town of Phan Thiet, ten kilometres west, has a better range of genuinely local Vietnamese restaurants than the resort strip. Banh mi stalls here are among the best in the province.
