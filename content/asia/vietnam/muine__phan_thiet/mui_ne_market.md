Mui Ne Market is a colorful local market just off the main road in town. It is most interesting early in the day, when produce, seafood, and everyday household goods give a more local view of Mui Ne than the beach strip.

Pair it with the nearby fishing village or red sand dunes if you want a short break from resort areas and kitesurfing beaches.
