Bai Duong Beach sits north of the Cai River, away from Nha Trang's main tourist beach. It is smaller and quieter, with a more local feel and several seafood restaurants nearby.

The beach is pretty, but not pristine, and drainage channels make it less appealing than the city's best stretches of sand. It works best as a short, low-key coastal stop combined with nearby Hon Chong.
