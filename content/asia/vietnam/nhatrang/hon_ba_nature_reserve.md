Hon Ba Nature Reserve is the mountain counterweight to Nha Trang's beach identity, rising inland into cooler forest, waterfalls, and winding roads. Travel material for the reserve commonly places it about 60 kilometres from Nha Trang and highlights its year-round cool air, lush forest, and the historic house associated with Alexandre Yersin.

The trip is more about the journey and climate shift than a single blockbuster sight. Expect a long ride, changing weather, and a quieter landscape of forest tracks, viewpoints, and streams. It is best in the dry season, when the mountain road is less slippery and the views are more likely to open.
