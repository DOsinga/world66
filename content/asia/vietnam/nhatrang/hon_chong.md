Hon Chong is a rocky headland north of central Nha Trang, where stacked boulders and low surf create a more textured coastal scene than the city's long urban beach. It is a simple place, but the views back along the bay and out toward the islands make it a worthwhile short detour.

Visit in the morning or late afternoon, when the rocks are less harshly lit and the heat is easier to handle. The site is often wrapped in local legends, but its practical appeal is straightforward: sea air, photographs, and a break from resort-front Nha Trang.
