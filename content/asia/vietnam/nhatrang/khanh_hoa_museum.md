Khánh Hòa Museum is a small regional museum near central Nha Trang. Its exhibits cover Cham sculpture, local ethnic minority cultures, and the province's modern history, including a room connected with Ho Chi Minh.

It works best as a short cultural stop between Nha Trang's better-known temples, cathedral, and waterfront sights.
