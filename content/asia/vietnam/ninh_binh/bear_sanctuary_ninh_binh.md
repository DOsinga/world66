Bear Sanctuary Ninh Binh is a rescue and welfare facility for bears formerly connected to bile farming and illegal wildlife trade. It gives visitors a conservation-focused stop in a region better known for karst scenery and temples.

Check visitor arrangements in advance, because sanctuary access is managed around animal welfare rather than casual tourism. When open, it is most meaningful for travellers interested in responsible wildlife experiences.
