Khe Coc Island is part of the wider Trang An landscape around Ninh Binh, where limestone towers, waterways and archaeological traces create one of northern Vietnam's most distinctive settings. The appeal is the same slow combination of water, rock and green that makes the whole complex memorable.

Treat it as one piece of a Trang An day rather than a standalone island outing. Boat routes and visitor arrangements can change, so use current local information when choosing how to see it.
