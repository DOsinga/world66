Trang An is the standout experience in Ninh Binh and one of the best things to do in northern Vietnam. A UNESCO World Heritage site since 2014, the complex covers 6,000 hectares of limestone karst peaks, river valleys, wetlands, and forest. The boat circuit — rowed by local guides, usually one person using their feet on the oars — winds through 47 caves and grottos, across several open valleys, and past Buddhist temples clinging to cliff faces.

The circuit takes two to three hours depending on which route you choose, and the experience is genuinely different from Tam Coc: less crowded, wilder in feel, and covering more ground. The longer circuits take you deeper into the complex. Boats hold four people; guides are assigned at the dock.

Go early morning if possible — before 8am the light is better and the boatmen are fresher. Avoid the weekend afternoons in peak season when the floating market of tour boats can become a traffic jam.
