Van Trinh Grotto is one of the less crowded cave outings in the Ninh Binh area, set in Mo Mountain near waterways connected to the Hoang Long River. Inside, the appeal is the scale of the dry cavern and its limestone formations rather than the famous boat-through-karst scenery of Trang An or Tam Coc.

It suits travellers who already have time for the headline sights and want a quieter cave stop. Expect a rural approach, a climb to the entrance, and limited English interpretation; the reward is a more local-feeling visit in a province where the best-known attractions can feel heavily channelled.
