Phong Nha village is the gateway to the national park — a single main road running alongside the Son River with guesthouses, cafés, restaurants, and tour operators filling the strip. It is small enough to walk end to end in twenty minutes, but it has accumulated a pleasant infrastructure for travellers, and the riverside setting, with karst hills rising behind the opposite bank, is genuinely attractive.

The village is where you organise cave tours, hire bicycles, and book adventure activities. Most operators cluster along the main road, and comparison-shopping takes no more than a morning. The Son River is swimmable at the village itself — the clear water and the view of the hills make an early morning or late afternoon dip one of the better free activities. Boat trips from the village dock head up to Phong Nha Cave, or you can hire a kayak for a more independent trip on the river. A couple of good local restaurants serve central Vietnamese specialities — bún bò Huế, bánh cuốn, and fish dishes from the river.
