Sunworld Hon Thom Water Park is the big theme-park stop at the end of the cable car ride from southern Phu Quoc. It has large water slides, pools, restaurants, and access to a beach where visitors can swim, snorkel, or arrange diving.

It is a packaged resort attraction rather than a quiet island escape, but families and water-park fans may find it a useful full-day outing. It makes the most sense combined with the Sunworld Cable Car.
