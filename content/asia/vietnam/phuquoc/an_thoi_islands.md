The An Thoi archipelago — a scatter of 18 small islands off the southern tip of Phu Quoc — is the best snorkelling and diving destination in the region. The reefs here still support good coral cover and a wide variety of reef fish, sea turtles, and occasional reef sharks. The water is clearest from November to May.

Day tours depart daily from the An Thoi pier in the south of the island or from the main pier in Duong Dong, typically visiting three or four islands with snorkelling stops at each. The cable car from Phu Quoc's south coast to Hon Thom island (one of the An Thoi group) is the world's longest sea-crossing cable car at over 7 km, and offers spectacular views of the archipelago and back toward the island. Most dive operators in Duong Dong also run day trips to the southern reefs for certified divers, and PADI courses are widely available.
