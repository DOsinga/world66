title: Dinh Cau Temple (Rock Temple)
type: poi
---

Dinh Cau temple is a small shrine perched on a rocky promontory at the entrance to Duong Dong harbour, dedicated to the goddess Thien Hau (Mazu) who protects sailors and fishermen. The temple and its lighthouse occupy a dramatic position at the end of a narrow rock outcrop, with views back over the harbour and the fishing fleet moored in the river mouth.

The setting is best at sunset when the fishing boats are heading out or returning and the light turns the harbour golden. The temple itself is modest — a few shrines, incense burners, and a small altar — but it has been an important devotional site for the island's fishing community for well over a century. A short walk around the promontory gives good views over both the harbour and Long Beach stretching south.
