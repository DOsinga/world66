Duong Dong's night market, running along the waterfront near the bridge in the centre of town, is the best place on the island to eat like a local rather than a tourist. The stalls set up from around 5pm and run late into the evening, selling grilled seafood by weight, banh mi, com ga (chicken rice), and the island's best bun quay — a noodle soup with crab and shrimp peculiar to Phu Quoc.

The seafood is extremely fresh — boats unload at the nearby port daily — and the prices are a fraction of what the beachfront restaurants charge. The market is also good for Phu Quoc pepper, dried seafood, and local snacks to take home. It gets crowded in high season but the turnover is fast and there is usually space at the communal plastic tables. Bring cash, negotiate prices if they seem high, and eat early if you want the best selection.
