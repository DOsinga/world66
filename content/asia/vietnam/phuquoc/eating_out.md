---
title: Eating Out
type: section
---

Phu Quoc's local food scene centres on the seafood, the fish sauce, and the island's own variety of black pepper. The [Duong Dong night market](/asia/vietnam/phuquoc/duong_dong_market) is the best and cheapest option for grilled seafood and local noodle dishes. The beachfront restaurants along Long Beach serve acceptable international food at tourist prices; the better local places are in the back streets of Duong Dong town.
