---
title: Getting Around
type: section
---

The island is about 50 km long and 25 km wide at its widest. Most accommodation is concentrated along a 20 km stretch of Long Beach on the west coast, with Duong Dong town roughly in the middle. Renting a motorbike in Duong Dong is the most practical and flexible way to get around — most guesthouses offer them for US$5–10 per day. Taxis and ride-hailing apps (Grab) operate across the island. Bicycle hire is possible for exploring the immediate beach area, but the distances to the national park and southern beaches are too great for comfortable cycling.
