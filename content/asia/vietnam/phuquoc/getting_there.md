---
title: Getting There
type: section
---

Phu Quoc International Airport (PQC) is the main entry point, with multiple daily flights from Ho Chi Minh City (1 hour) and Hanoi (2 hours). There are also direct international connections to a growing number of cities in China, Korea, and Russia. The airport is on the south-central part of the island, convenient for the main beach strip.

Ferries operate from Ha Tien (one hour) and Rach Gia (2.5 hours) on the mainland; both are reachable from Ho Chi Minh City by bus. The ferry from Ha Tien is the more pleasant crossing and connects to Cambodia via a border crossing a few kilometres from town, making a combined Vietnam-Cambodia trip feasible.
