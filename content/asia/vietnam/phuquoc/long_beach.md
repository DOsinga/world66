Long Beach — Bai Truong in Vietnamese — runs south from Duong Dong town for nearly 20 kilometres, making it the longest beach on the island and the main focus of the resort development. The northern stretch near town is backed by guesthouses, restaurants, and bars; the central section is lined with mid-range and international hotels; the southern end, near Ong Lang junction, is the quietest and most pleasant section. The sand is pale and fine, the water warm, and the sunsets from this west-facing coast are reliably spectacular.

The beach is swimmable year-round but at its best from November to April in the dry season; during the southwest monsoon (May–October) the water can be rougher with occasional jellyfish. The development is heavy but the beach is long enough that you can always walk away from the crowds. Motorbike rental is the most practical way to explore the length of it.
