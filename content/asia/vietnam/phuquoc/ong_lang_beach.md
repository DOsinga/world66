Ong Lang is a small, crescent-shaped beach on the island's northwest coast, about 12 km north of Duong Dong, that has retained a relatively relaxed character even as the rest of the island has been developed. A handful of small bungalow resorts and restaurants back the beach, and the surrounding hills are still mostly forested. The water here is clear and calm in the dry season, and the coral in the shallows at the northern end supports decent snorkelling.

The beach faces west-northwest, so the sunset views are excellent. This is a better choice than Long Beach for those who want a quieter holiday — the crowd thins out significantly once you are more than a kilometre or two from the main resort zone. Accommodation here ranges from basic bungalows to a few mid-range boutique properties.
