title: Things to Do
type: section
---

Phu Quoc divides naturally into two kinds of activity: the beaches and water on the west coast, and the national park and cultural sights inland and to the north. The [Phu Quoc National Park](/asia/vietnam/phuquoc/phu_quoc_national_park) is the island's ecological heart and covers most of the northern third. The [Phu Quoc Prison](/asia/vietnam/phuquoc/phu_quoc_prison) museum is the most affecting historical sight, documenting the detention and torture of political prisoners during the Vietnam War.

The fish sauce factories near Duong Dong town offer a fascinating glimpse into a traditional industry unique to the island. Snorkelling and diving in the archipelago's outer islands — particularly the An Thoi group to the south — is among the best in Vietnam for coral and tropical fish.
