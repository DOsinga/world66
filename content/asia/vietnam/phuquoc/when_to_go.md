---
title: When to Go
type: section
---

The dry season runs from November to April, with calm seas, low humidity, and little rain. This is the best time to visit for beach holidays and snorkelling. December to March is peak season, with the highest prices and biggest crowds. From May to October the southwest monsoon brings rain, rough seas on the west coast, and occasional strong winds. The east coast beaches (particularly Sao Beach) remain swimmable during the wet season, and the national park is lush and green. Accommodation prices drop significantly in the wet season.
