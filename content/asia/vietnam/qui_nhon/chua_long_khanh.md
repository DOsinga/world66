Chua Long Khanh is one of the main Buddhist temples in Quy Nhon and in Binh Dinh province. The temple was first founded in the early 18th century, though the current main hall is from the 1950s. Look for the old bronze bell, the lotus pond, and the large bluestone Amitabha statue beside the temple. It is central, free to enter, and easy to combine with the Cham towers and the beach.
