ExploraScience is the public-facing science centre at Qui Nhon's International Centre for Interdisciplinary Science and Education. Its exhibitions focus on astronomy, space exploration, and hands-on science, with enough interactive material to work well for children and adults.

It is a useful change of pace from beaches and Cham ruins, especially for families or anyone interested in Vietnam's growing science and education scene.
