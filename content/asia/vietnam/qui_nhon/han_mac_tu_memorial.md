On the hillside above Qui Nhon, overlooking the bay, stands the grave and memorial to Han Mac Tu, one of the most celebrated Vietnamese poets of the early 20th century. He spent his last years at the Quy Hoa leprosarium below, and his grave has become a pilgrimage site for Vietnamese literature lovers.

The memorial is modest but well-tended — a grave marker, a small altar, and a pavilion with excerpts from his most famous poems on the walls. The path up the hill is short and offers good views back over the bay. The leprosarium itself, which still operates as a hospital, can be visited with permission; a small museum at its entrance tells the history of the institution and of Han Mac Tu's time here.
