---
title: Things to Do
type: section
---

Qui Nhon rewards slow travel. The beaches are the obvious draw — clean, uncrowded, and far longer than those at more famous resorts further up and down the coast. Away from the sand, the surrounding countryside holds Cham towers, a moving poet's memorial, and fishing villages where the daily catch is sorted on the beach at dawn. For a mid-sized Vietnamese city with real beaches, it punches well above its weight.
