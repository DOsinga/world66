---
title: Things to Do
type: section
---

Tay Ninh's central attraction is the Great Cao Dai Temple, which is extraordinary enough to justify the trip from Ho Chi Minh City on its own. The noon ceremony is the most popular time to visit, when hundreds of worshippers file into the nave in coloured robes. The nearby Bà Đen Mountain and the frontier zone near the Cambodian border round out the options for those who want more than a single stop.
