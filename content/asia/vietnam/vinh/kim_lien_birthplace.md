The Kim Lien relic complex, 14 kilometres west of Vinh, preserves the two villages associated with Ho Chi Minh's childhood: Hoang Tru, where he was born in 1890, and Sen Village (also called Kim Lien), where his family later moved and he spent his teenage years. The simple thatched house where he was born has been preserved, along with period furnishings and an extensive museum chronicling his life and the history of the independence movement.

The site is one of the most visited historical sites in Vietnam and is treated with genuine reverence. The complex is well laid out and the museum is substantial; allow at least two hours. Local guides are available and useful for interpreting the significance of objects and the historical context. The site is open daily and admission is modest.
