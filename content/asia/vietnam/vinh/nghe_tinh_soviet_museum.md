The Soviet Nghe Tinh Museum commemorates the 1930-1931 uprising in which peasants and workers across the Nghe An and Ha Tinh provinces briefly established local governing councils, inspired by the recently formed Indochinese Communist Party. The uprising was crushed by French colonial forces, but it is regarded as a precursor to the broader independence movement.

The museum holds documents, photographs, and artefacts from the uprising and its suppression, set in the context of the broader history of Nghe An province — one of the centres of Vietnamese revolutionary activity in the 20th century. It is a somewhat specialist attraction but informative for anyone interested in the history of Vietnamese communism and the independence movement.
