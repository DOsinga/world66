---
title: "Getting There"
type: section
---

Vung Tau is about 120 km south-east of [Ho Chi Minh City](/asia/vietnam/hochiminhcity). The fastest connection is the **hydrofoil/express ferry** from Bạch Đằng pier in central Saigon — roughly 1 hour 30 minutes across the river and bay. Boats run several times a day.

By road, the trip takes 2 to 3 hours by car or taxi via the Long Thành expressway, longer at weekends when half of Ho Chi Minh City heads the same way. Air-conditioned buses run from Miền Đông bus station and arrive at the bus terminal in central Vung Tau. **Vung Tau Airport** on the peninsula handles only helicopter and small-aircraft services to offshore oil platforms — there are no scheduled passenger flights.
