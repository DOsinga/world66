The Robert Taylor Museum of Worldwide Arms is a private collection in Vung Tau with weapons, uniforms, armour, and military objects from different countries and periods. It is one of the city's more distinctive indoor stops, especially useful when the heat or rain makes another beach walk less appealing.

The museum's appeal is breadth rather than a single local story. Expect a collector's eye: dense displays, unusual pieces, and plenty for military-history enthusiasts to inspect at close range.
