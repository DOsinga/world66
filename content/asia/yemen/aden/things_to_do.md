Aden's sights are the legacy of its geography — a port wedged into an extinct volcano — and of layered occupation by Romans, Ottomans, Portuguese, and British. The [Cisterns of Tawila](/asia/yemen/aden/cisterns_tawila) are the oldest and strangest. The Crater district holds the [Aidrus Mosque](/asia/yemen/aden/aidrus_mosque) and the streets of British-era buildings. On the south coast, [Sira Fortress](/asia/yemen/aden/sira_fortress) and the [Al-Maashiq Palace](/asia/yemen/aden/al_maashiq_palace) overlook the harbour. Conflict since 2015 has damaged or closed several monuments. Check on the ground before assuming anything is reachable or safe.
