---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

The best time to visit Socotra is October through April. From June to September, the southwest monsoon makes the island essentially inaccessible: the seas are too rough for boats, flights are suspended, and the wind is relentless. This seasonal isolation has historically protected the island's ecology from over-exploitation.

October to December is the transition period — winds diminish and access resumes. November through March is the peak visiting season, with clear skies, calm seas, and temperatures around 25°C on the coast. April and May are warm and increasingly humid as the monsoon approaches.
