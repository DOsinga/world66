---
title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Thola's attractions are inseparable from its hilltop topography. The fortress, the ancient cisterns, and the mosque of Al-Motahir Bin Imam Sharafidin all sit within a compact area that can be walked in an hour or two — but the point is not speed. The pleasure is in the detail: the way the rock-cut cisterns are still lime-plastered and functional-looking after fifteen centuries, the sight-lines from the castle walls across the ridgelines of the western Yemeni highlands, the carved stonework of the mosque's interior.

This is a town without a tourist industry, which means you will attract curious attention but encounter no ticket offices, no guided tour booklets, and no refreshment stalls. Bring water, wear sensible shoes for the uneven stone paths, and allow the morning light, when the castle catches the eastern sun, for the best visit.

Note: Yemen has been in a state of severe conflict since 2015. Travel to the northern highlands, including Thola, is not possible for most visitors under current conditions. This information is preserved for when the situation changes — and because Thola deserves to be on the record.
