---
title: "Bars and Cafes"
type: section
---

Alega has one bar and it is the right one. Tisa's Barefoot Bar (also listed under Eating Out) operates as the social hub of the village beach — cold drinks, Vailima beer (the Samoan brew brewed in Samoa), and a setting that combines Pacific informality with genuine warmth. There is no nightlife in Alega in any conventional sense; the village observes fa'a Samoa rhythms, which means evenings are quiet and Sundays are silent.
