---
title: "Eating Out"
type: section
---

Eating options in Alega itself are essentially one: Tisa's Barefoot Bar. That is not a complaint. Tisa's is locally sourced, culturally rooted, and the kind of place you remember long after you've forgotten fancier meals. For anything beyond a light meal or snack, Pago Pago is 20–30 minutes west and has a fuller range of restaurants, Chinese buffets, and Korean barbecue joints.
