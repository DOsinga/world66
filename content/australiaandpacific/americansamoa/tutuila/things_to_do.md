title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

The [National Park of American Samoa](/australiaandpacific/americansamoa/tutuila/national_park) on Tutuila's north coast is the main draw for most visitors — a rare piece of protected tropical rainforest that you can walk through without a guide. The trailhead for [Mount 'Alava](/australiaandpacific/americansamoa/tutuila/mount_alava) is at Fagasa Pass, a short drive from Pago Pago.

[Pago Pago Harbor](/australiaandpacific/americansamoa/tutuila/pago_pago_harbor) is one of the finest natural harbours in the Pacific — a deep volcanic caldera that provides almost complete shelter. The [Jean P. Haydon Museum](/australiaandpacific/americansamoa/tutuila/haydon_museum) in Fagatogo covers American Samoan history and traditional culture. [Massacre Bay](/australiaandpacific/americansamoa/tutuila/massacre_bay) commemorates the 1787 death of French sailors from the La Pérouse expedition — an unusual piece of Pacific history.

For those interested in WWII history, the [Blunts Point Battery](/australiaandpacific/americansamoa/tutuila/blunts_point_battery) overlooking the harbour preserves guns installed during the American military build-up in the early 1940s.
