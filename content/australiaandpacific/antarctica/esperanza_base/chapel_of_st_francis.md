The Chapel of St. Francis of Assisi is a modest building with an outsized Antarctic claim: it was the first Roman Catholic chapel on the continent. Baptisms, communions, and weddings have all taken place here, which feels improbable in a place where most visitors arrive wrapped in expedition gear.
