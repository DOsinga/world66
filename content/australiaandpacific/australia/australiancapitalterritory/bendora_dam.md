Bendora Dam is a thin-wall, double-curvature concrete arch dam in the mountains west of Canberra. Opened in 1961, it was the first dam of its type built in Australia and remains part of Canberra's water supply.

The dam is mainly of interest for its engineering and the lookouts around it. It works best as a short stop while exploring the remote roads and forests of the Australian Capital Territory.
