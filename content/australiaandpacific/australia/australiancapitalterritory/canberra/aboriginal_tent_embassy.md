The Aboriginal Tent Embassy stands on the lawn directly opposite Old Parliament House — an informal, continuously-occupied protest installation that has been there since January 1972. It is recognised both as a significant heritage site and as a still-active political action; the people who staff it consider it an embassy because Aboriginal Australians have, in their view, not ceded sovereignty.

Visitors are welcome to walk around and read the displayed material; talking with the residents, when they are available and willing, is the proper way to understand the site. Photography of the installation itself is fine; do not photograph people without permission. The Embassy is one of the few places in central Canberra where the unresolved questions of Australian sovereignty and indigenous dispossession are visible at street level.
