The Canberra Balloon Spectacular is one of the oldest hot-air balloon festivals in the world — held every March in the still autumn mornings, with up to 30 balloons launching from the lawn in front of Old Parliament House between 6 and 7 am over nine days. Many of the balloons are large novelty shapes — animals, cartoon characters, sponsored objects — that make the festival particularly photogenic.

It is free, public, and an excellent excuse to be up before dawn in Canberra. The Old Parliament House lawn is the official viewing area; the Mount Pleasant lookout above the War Memorial gives a wider distant view as the balloons drift north over the city. Confirm launches the night before — the festival is heavily weather-dependent.
