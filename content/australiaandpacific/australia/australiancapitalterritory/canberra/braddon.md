Braddon, immediately north of the city centre, was for decades a sleepy suburb of low-rise light industry and car dealerships. A determined urban renewal effort through the 2010s turned it into Canberra's most lively street-eating-and-shopping district: Lonsdale Street and Mort Street now host a string of small bars, independent coffee roasters, restaurants, design shops, and apartment buildings.

It is the obvious place to come for an evening meal or a Saturday brunch — particularly along Lonsdale Street, where a number of small operators set the city's coffee, ramen and craft-beer standard. Frugii's gelato, Lazy Su, Eightysix and Brodburger are widely recommended; the Hopscotch beer garden and Capital Brewing taproom are easy options for a casual evening.
