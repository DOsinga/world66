Canberra Museum and Gallery is the city's local history and art museum, a useful counterweight to the national institutions around the lake. Its permanent displays explain Canberra's development and regional story, while temporary galleries often focus on local artists and photographers.

The Sydney Nolan Collection is the standout, especially if you want an Australian art stop that feels more compact and local than the National Gallery.
