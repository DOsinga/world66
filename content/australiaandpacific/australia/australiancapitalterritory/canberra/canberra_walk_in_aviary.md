Canberra Walk-in Aviary is a small family attraction at Gold Creek with hundreds of birds from many species in an enclosed walk-through setting. Visitors can feed the birds, which makes it more interactive than a standard aviary.

It is a good short stop for families already visiting Gold Creek, especially in combination with nearby gardens and other north Canberra attractions.
