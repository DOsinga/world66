Civic — the official name is Canberra City, but no one uses it — is the central business district north of the lake. Walter Burley Griffin laid it out around the two octagonal sister squares of City Hill, with the curving Northbourne Avenue running into it from the north. The result is more spacious and less commercially intense than other Australian central business districts.

The Canberra Centre shopping complex, the Canberra Theatre Centre, the central bus interchange and the main government office tower district are all here. Civic is short on charm but well-located; most visitors will pass through it many times. The Glassworks, a contemporary glass-art gallery and working studio in nearby Kingston, is one of the cultural standouts.
