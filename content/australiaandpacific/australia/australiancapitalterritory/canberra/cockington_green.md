Cockington Green, in the northern suburbs of Gold Creek, is a long-running family attraction: a meticulously detailed miniature display of English villages, surrounded by formal gardens. The miniatures are at 1:12 scale and include working miniature railways, fountains and lighting; the international section adds vignettes from a range of other countries.

It is undeniably touristy and not for everyone, but the workmanship is impressive and the gardens are pleasant. The on-site Bavarian-themed cafe leans into the kitsch but does decent coffee and scones. Standard half-day visit for families with younger children. Combine with the Australian Reptile Centre and the National Dinosaur Museum, both nearby in the same Gold Creek tourist precinct.
