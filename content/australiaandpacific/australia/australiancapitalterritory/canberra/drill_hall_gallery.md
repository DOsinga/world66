Drill Hall Gallery is an Australian National University gallery with a strong programme of contemporary Australian and international art. The exhibitions are usually small enough to take in without fatigue, but carefully chosen.

One reason to make the detour is Sidney Nolan's large work *Riverbend*, displayed at the front of the building. It is a good stop if you are already around ANU or the northern side of central Canberra.
