Duntroon Dairy is thought to be the oldest surviving building in the Australian Capital Territory, dating from 1832. It is usually seen from outside rather than toured, but the signage, rural setting, and views toward Lake Burley Griffin make it a worthwhile short stop for travellers interested in Canberra before the capital.
