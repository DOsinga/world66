The diplomatic suburbs of Yarralumla and Forrest hold around 90 embassies and high commissions, and many of them are architecturally remarkable — explicit national set-pieces commissioned by countries proud to make a cultural statement in the Australian capital. The standouts include the Indonesian Embassy (a long Javanese pavilion with a steep blackish-brown roof), the Indian High Commission (Indo-Saracenic style with white domes), the Papua New Guinean High Commission (a haus tambaran ceremonial spirit-house), the Thai Embassy (a pagoda-roofed temple complex), and the bold modernism of the German and Japanese buildings.

The standard route is to drive or cycle a loop through Yarralumla starting at the State Circle roundabout, ending at Westbourne Woods. The Canberra National Capital Authority publishes a free walking-tour map. Photographs of the buildings themselves are usually fine; do not photograph people, guards, or vehicles.
