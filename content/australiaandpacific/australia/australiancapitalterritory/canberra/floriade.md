Floriade is the largest spring flower festival in the southern hemisphere — a six-week installation in Commonwealth Park, on the northern shore of Lake Burley Griffin, that has been running since 1988. About one million tulips, daffodils, hyacinths and other spring bulbs are planted in patterned beds, accompanied by a programme of horticultural talks, food markets, live music and a small theme park with a Ferris wheel.

Floriade runs annually from mid-September to mid-October and is free during the day; the after-dark Floriade Nightfest in the final week is ticketed. Crowds are heavy on warm weekends; come early on a weekday for the best photographs.
