Geoscience Australia is a government science campus with a visitor-facing education centre. The displays cover Australian geology through minerals, rocks, fossils, maps, and historical material, making it most useful for travellers with a real interest in landscapes and earth science.

It sits away from Canberra's main museum cluster, so it is not a casual walk-in from the Parliamentary Triangle. Pair it with Red Hill, Mugga Mugga, or other southern Canberra stops if you are already nearby.
