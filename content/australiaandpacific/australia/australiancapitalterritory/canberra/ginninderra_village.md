Ginninderra Village is a small remnant of the rural district that existed here before Canberra's northern suburbs spread across the landscape. Its best-known survivor is the old schoolhouse, a plain but useful reminder that Belconnen and Gungahlin were once farming country.

It works best as a short heritage stop while visiting the Gold Creek area, rather than as a long outing on its own.
