Gudgenby Homestead is one of Namadgi's better-known historic hut sites, tied to early European grazing in the mountains south of Canberra. The original 1845 hut was dismantled in the 1960s and later reconstructed, so the appeal is less untouched fabric than setting: open valley country, mountain air, and a visible trace of pastoral life before the national park.
