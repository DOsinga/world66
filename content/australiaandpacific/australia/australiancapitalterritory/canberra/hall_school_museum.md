Hall School Museum and Heritage Centre is a small local museum in the former Hall Primary School, north of central Canberra. Its displays focus on early rural schooling in Australia before 1940, with material on bush teachers and settlement-era life around Hall, Belconnen, and Gungahlin.

It is a modest stop, but useful for travellers interested in Canberra's rural edges and the history of its surrounding villages.
