Kambah Pool is a natural swimming spot on the Murrumbidgee River, tucked below the suburbs on Canberra's south-western edge. Big river boulders, bushland, and deep water make it feel much farther from the city than it is.

There is a short walk down from the car park, and the river is at its best in warm weather when the water level is safe. Bring water and shoes you do not mind getting dusty.
