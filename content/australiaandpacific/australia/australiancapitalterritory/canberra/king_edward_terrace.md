Reconciliation Place, the wide ceremonial walkway running between the National Library and Questacon on the south shore of the lake, was opened in 2002 to acknowledge Indigenous Australians in the formal national landscape of Canberra. The walkway is lined with a series of sculptural panels — "slivers" — each commemorating a particular event, person or theme in the history of the relationship between Indigenous and non-Indigenous Australians.

Themes include the Stolen Generations, indigenous land rights, the 1967 referendum on Aboriginal citizenship, and reconciliation itself. The panels read well as a self-guided walk of about 20 minutes; the broader axial view from Old Parliament House down to the lake passes directly across it.
