Kingston, on the southern shore of the lake, is one of Canberra's most popular dining and lifestyle districts — particularly the Kingston Foreshore precinct, an early-2000s waterfront redevelopment with restaurants, bars, and a Sunday morning farmers' market. The Old Bus Depot Markets on Wentworth Avenue are the city's main weekend craft and food market.

Just south of Kingston is Manuka, a slightly more upmarket shopping and eating district laid out around Manuka Oval (the city's main cricket and AFL ground). The two neighbourhoods together account for a substantial share of Canberra's evening restaurant scene. Public transport is patchy at night; take a taxi if you have been to the wine bars.
