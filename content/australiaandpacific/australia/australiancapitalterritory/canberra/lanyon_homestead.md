Lanyon Homestead, in the southern Tuggeranong valley about 30 kilometres south of central Canberra, is a beautifully preserved nineteenth-century pastoral homestead surrounded by working farmland. The main house was built in the 1850s by a Scottish settler and remained in private hands until the 1970s; it has been restored to interpret colonial-era rural life in the Limestone Plains.

The site also includes the Nolan Gallery, a small purpose-built gallery holding a major collection of Sidney Nolan paintings including the second Ned Kelly series. The grounds, with formal English gardens around the homestead and views across to the Brindabella Ranges, are particularly nice on a clear winter morning. There is a good cafe and the property hosts regular outdoor concerts and food markets.
