Lindsay Pryor National Arboretum is a quiet lakeside arboretum near Lake Burley Griffin, named for Australian botanist Lindsay Pryor. It is smaller and less polished than the National Arboretum, but it works well for a calm walk, especially when the trees show autumn colour.

The setting is the main reason to come: open grass, planted tree collections, and easy access to the lake edge.
