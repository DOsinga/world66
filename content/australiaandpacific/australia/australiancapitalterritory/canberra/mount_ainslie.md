Mount Ainslie is the most accessible of the wooded peaks that ring Canberra and the standard place to take in the city in one glance. The summit at 842 metres gives a perfect axial view down Anzac Parade, across Lake Burley Griffin, and up the slope to Parliament House on Capital Hill — the defining ceremonial sight-line of Canberra.

You can drive to the summit on a sealed road, or walk up the Kokoda Trail from the back of the Australian War Memorial — about 45 minutes one way through dry eucalypt forest, often with kangaroos at dusk. The lookout has good interpretation panels and binoculars. Sunset is the obvious time; dawn is even better and considerably quieter.
