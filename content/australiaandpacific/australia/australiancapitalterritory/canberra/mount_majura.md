Mount Majura is the highest hill in northern Canberra and part of the Canberra Nature Park system. Its tracks give you a good bushland escape without leaving the city, with views over the airport side of town, Mount Ainslie, and the Majura Valley.

It is quieter than the more famous Mount Ainslie lookout, which is part of the charm. Bring water, watch for cyclists on shared tracks, and give yourself time to notice the woodland rather than treating it only as a summit walk.
