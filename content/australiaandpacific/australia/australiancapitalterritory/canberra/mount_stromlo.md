Mount Stromlo, on a hill west of Canberra, hosted the Commonwealth Solar Observatory from 1924 and grew into the country's major optical-astronomy facility. The 2003 bushfires devastated the site; the surviving heritage buildings (the original administration building and several domes) have been carefully restored and house a small visitor centre and a planetarium-style theatre.

The Australian National University's Research School of Astronomy and Astrophysics still operates the site as a research base for the Skymapper telescope and as the engineering home of various international instrument programmes. The visitor centre is open weekdays; the cafe is a popular stop on cycling routes out of the city.
