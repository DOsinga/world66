Mount Taylor is one of Canberra's accessible urban hill walks, rising above the southern suburbs with open views to the Brindabellas, Woden, Tuggeranong, and the planned city beyond. It is less iconic than Mount Ainslie, but often quieter.

The climb is straightforward in good weather, with exposed sections that can be hot or windy. Go early or late for better light, and keep an eye out for kangaroos on the slopes.
