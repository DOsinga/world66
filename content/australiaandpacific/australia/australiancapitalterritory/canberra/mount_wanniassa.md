Mount Wanniassa is an 809-metre hill in the Wanniassa Hills Nature Reserve, between the southern Canberra suburbs of Wanniassa, Farrer, Isaacs, and Fadden. Walking tracks climb the east and west slopes.

The reward is the view over Woden, Tuggeranong, and the surrounding ridges, with a quieter feel than Canberra's better-known lookout hills.
