Mulligans Flat Woodland Sanctuary protects box-gum woodland on Canberra's northern edge, with trails for walking, running, and cycling. The reserve is also used for native ecology research, so it offers a more natural and conservation-focused outing than the manicured attractions around Gold Creek.
