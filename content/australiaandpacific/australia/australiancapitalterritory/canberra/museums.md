---
title: "Museums"
type: section
---

Canberra is the museum capital of Australia: as the seat of federal government, it hosts the national institutions that other capitals don't have. Almost all of them are free, all of them are in or near the Parliamentary Triangle, and most are open seven days a week.

The headline institutions are the National Gallery of Australia, the National Portrait Gallery, the National Museum of Australia, the Australian War Memorial, and Questacon. Slightly smaller but still important: the National Library of Australia (with its public exhibition gallery), the National Archives, the Museum of Australian Democracy (in Old Parliament House), and AIATSIS (the Australian Institute of Aboriginal and Torres Strait Islander Studies).

Two or three days are needed to cover the main institutions properly. Most visitors prioritise the War Memorial, the National Gallery and one of the museums (the National Museum for general history; Questacon if travelling with children).
