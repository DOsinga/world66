Namadgi National Park covers 106,000 hectares — almost half of the entire Australian Capital Territory — and protects the northern end of the Australian Alps, the line of mountains that runs through Kosciuszko to the Victorian high country. The peaks include Mount Bimberi (the highest point in the ACT at 1,912 metres) and a substantial alpine plateau that holds snow most winters.

Day hikers can drive in and walk the Yankee Hat Aboriginal rock-art track, the Square Rock walk or the Booroomba Rocks climb; overnight hikers do the Australian Alps Walking Track which starts here and ends 655 kilometres later at Walhalla in Victoria. The visitor centre is on Naas Road, about 50 kilometres south of central Canberra. Snow chains may be needed in winter.
