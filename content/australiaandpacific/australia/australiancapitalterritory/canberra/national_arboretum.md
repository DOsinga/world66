The National Arboretum, on a hill west of the city centre, is one of Canberra's newer attractions: 250 hectares planted up between 2005 and 2013 with around 44,000 trees in 94 separate single-species forests, including many rare and endangered species from across the world. The Wollemi pine forest, the Spanish chestnut forest, and the various Mediterranean oak forests are particular successes.

The visitor centre is dramatic — a large all-timber building shaped like a swooping wave — with very good views down over Lake Burley Griffin and the city. There is a small but excellent National Bonsai Collection on site. The arboretum is freely accessible at all times; a separate sculpture trail, a children's play forest and an outdoor concert venue host events through the warmer months.
