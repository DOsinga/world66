The National Archives, in the East Block of the original 1920s Secretariat buildings beside Old Parliament House, hold the Australian government's records and run a small but unusually interesting public gallery. The permanent display includes the original Federal Constitution document (1900) and the proclamation of federation; rotating shows have covered subjects from World War I service records to Indigenous family history files.

The archives are particularly important for anyone researching military, immigration or government family history — the searchable records are extensive and the reading room provides free access to indexes that you would otherwise pay for. Free entry; the cafe in the courtyard is a quiet lunchtime spot.
