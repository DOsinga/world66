The National Carillon, on Aspen Island in Lake Burley Griffin, was a gift from the British government to mark Canberra's fiftieth anniversary in 1963. The three-tower modernist concrete structure houses 57 bronze bells weighing from 7 kilograms to 6 tonnes — one of the largest carillons in the world — and is played by a small group of specialist carillonists for the public several times a week.

Recital schedules are published on the National Capital Authority website and usually include a Sunday afternoon performance. The bridges to Aspen Island are open at all times, and the lakeshore around the carillon is a popular picnic spot. Tours of the playing chamber and bell room run by arrangement.
