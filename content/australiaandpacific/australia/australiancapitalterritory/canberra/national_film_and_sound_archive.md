The National Film & Sound Archive preserves Australia's audiovisual history in a handsome 1930s Art Deco building in Acton. Exhibitions, talks, screenings, and the Arc Cinema make it more than a research archive, and the building itself is worth a look if you are already exploring central Canberra.
