The National Zoo and Aquarium, on the western edge of Yarralumla near the Scrivener Dam, combines a small but well-presented Australian and international zoo collection with a tropical aquarium and a programme of close-encounter animal experiences. The zoo's signature offering is the "Meet a Cheetah" and "Walk with the Tigers" sessions, where visitors are taken into the keeper-access areas of the big-cat enclosures.

It is a privately operated zoo and smaller than the major capital-city zoos in Sydney or Melbourne, but the cheetah, lion, snow leopard and tiger collections are strong. The aquarium section has freshwater Australian species and a small tropical reef tank. The on-site Jamala Wildlife Lodge offers expensive but memorable accommodation that lets you sleep in rooms looking directly into the big-cat enclosures.
