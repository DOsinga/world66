The Parliamentary Triangle is the formal national core of Canberra: three sides defined by Commonwealth Avenue, Constitution Avenue and Kings Avenue, with Lake Burley Griffin cutting through it. Almost every major national institution stands inside or beside the triangle — Parliament House, the High Court, the National Gallery, the National Library, Questacon, the Portrait Gallery and the original Old Parliament House.

The area is monumental and a little austere — there is not much shopping or eating, and the spaces between the buildings are large — but the concentration of national institutions makes it the standard centre of any Canberra visit. Distances are walkable but not trivial; in summer take water, in winter take a coat. The Captain Cook Memorial Jet and the Reconciliation Place sculptures are between the major buildings.
