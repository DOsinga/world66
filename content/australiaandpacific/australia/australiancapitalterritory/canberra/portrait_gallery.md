The National Portrait Gallery, opened in its current Johnson Pilton Walker building in 2008, sits between the National Gallery and Questacon on the southern lakeshore. The collection focuses on Australian portraiture across all media — paintings, sculpture, photography, video — with subjects including prime ministers, indigenous leaders, sportspeople, artists, scientists and writers.

The building is a clever, understated piece of work: long low-slung galleries arranged around a central courtyard, with constantly changing natural light. The permanent display rotates frequently. Free admission to the permanent collection; ticketed exhibitions; very good cafe and bookshop. Easy to combine with the National Gallery next door.
