Red Hill is one of Canberra's best quick viewpoints, rising just south of the parliamentary triangle. From the top you can read the planned city clearly: Parliament House, Lake Burley Griffin, the avenues, and the Brindabella Range beyond.

The surrounding reserve also has short walking tracks through dry eucalypt woodland, with a good chance of seeing kangaroos near the edges of the day. It is close to the centre but feels usefully spacious, which is very Canberra.
