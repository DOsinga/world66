Royal Military College - Duntroon is the Australian Army's officer training academy, founded in 1911 before Canberra itself was formally established. Much of the campus is restricted, but the grounds have enough history to interest travellers following Australia's military sites.

The most unusual stop is Changi Chapel, built by Australian prisoners of war in Singapore during World War II and later moved to Duntroon. Check current access before going, as this is still an active military college.
