The Shine Dome, on Gordon Street in Acton, is one of the most striking pieces of mid-century architecture in Canberra: a copper-clad concrete dome rising from a circular moat, with sixteen segmented arches around the perimeter. Designed by Roy Grounds in 1958 and opened in 1959, it houses the Australian Academy of Science.

The dome is normally closed to casual visitors but it is open during occasional public tours, lecture nights and Open House Canberra in late October. From the street it photographs beautifully at any time of day, particularly at dusk when the building is uplit. It is a National Heritage Listed building and one of the most internationally significant modernist Australian buildings.
