Stromlo Forest Park is one of Canberra's main outdoor recreation areas, with a network of trails for mountain biking, walking, running, and horse riding. The terrain is open and dry, with views back toward the city and the surrounding ranges.

It is especially useful for travelers who want an active half-day rather than another museum stop. Mountain bikers get the most from it, but there are gentler walking options too.
