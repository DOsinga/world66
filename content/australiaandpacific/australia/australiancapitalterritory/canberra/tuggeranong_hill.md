Tuggeranong Hill rises above the Lanyon Valley on Canberra's southern edge, with open views over the suburbs, the valley floor, and the surrounding ACT hills. It is a simple outdoor stop rather than a built attraction, but it rewards visitors who want a short walk and a wider sense of Canberra's setting.

The hill is sometimes called Mount Tuggeranong, though that name is not official. Go in clear weather and treat it as a viewpoint and nature break on the south side of the city.
