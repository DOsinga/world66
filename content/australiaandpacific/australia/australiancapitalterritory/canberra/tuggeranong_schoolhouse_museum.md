Tuggeranong Schoolhouse Museum preserves a 19th-century schoolhouse from Canberra's rural past. The rooms display furniture, objects, and antiques connected with the area's pastoral period, before the modern capital spread across the valley.

It is a small, specialist stop, best suited to visitors already exploring southern Canberra or the historic homesteads and rural sites around the ACT.
