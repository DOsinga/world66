Yarralumla is the leafy diplomatic and political suburb on the southwestern side of Lake Burley Griffin — home to Government House (the Governor-General's official residence), the Prime Minister's Lodge, and a substantial proportion of the foreign embassies in Canberra. The streets are wide, the gardens are extensive, and the architecture ranges from 1920s mission style through aggressive 1970s modernism to deliberate national-vernacular statements by the embassies of countries proud of their building traditions.

A walk or drive through the embassy belt is one of the underrated pleasures of Canberra — the Indonesian, Indian, Thai and Papua New Guinean embassies in particular are spectacular pieces of cultural display, and the United States embassy compound is the largest American diplomatic facility outside Washington DC. The shopping street at Yarralumla village has good cafes and the long-established Oaks Brasserie.
