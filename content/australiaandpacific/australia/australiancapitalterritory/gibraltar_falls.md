Gibraltar Falls is one of the largest waterfalls in the Canberra region, dropping more than 50 metres through bushland on the edge of Namadgi National Park. It is often visited as its own short outing rather than as part of a longer park trip.

The draw is straightforward: a quick walk, a solid waterfall view, and a wilder feel than you expect this close to Canberra. Take care around the rocks and lookout edges, especially after rain.
