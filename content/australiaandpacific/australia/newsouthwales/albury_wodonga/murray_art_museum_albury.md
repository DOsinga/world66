Murray Art Museum Albury, usually shortened to MAMA, is the city's strongest indoor stop. Its exhibitions change regularly, mixing contemporary work with pieces from the AlburyCity collection, and the building gives central Albury a sharper cultural focus than you might expect from a highway stop.

It pairs well with the nearby LibraryMuseum and a walk down Dean Street. If the weather is hot, smoky, or wet, this is the place to start.
