Armidale Pine Forest is a local recreation area on the edge of town, with tracks through tall plantation pines that are used by walkers, cyclists and horse riders. It is not wilderness, but it gives Armidale an easy outdoor escape without needing to drive out to the national parks.

The forest is best for a leg-stretching walk, a morning ride or a quiet picnic when you want shade and space. Travellers using Armidale as a base for the New England tablelands may find it a useful low-effort outing between bigger trips to waterfalls, gorges and high-country lookouts.
