Dangars Falls is a high waterfall in the Oxley Wild Rivers area, falling about 183 m when water is running. The nearby picnic area has basic facilities, and McDirty's Walking Track gives a longer 6 km option for visitors who want more than the lookout.

The falls are best after rain. Bring water and be prepared to carry out rubbish, as facilities are limited.
