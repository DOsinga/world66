Mount Yarrowyck Nature Reserve protects a granite landscape northwest of Armidale, with an easy 3 km return walk to an Aboriginal rock-art site. The trail is short and mostly level, so it is a good half-day detour for travellers interested in New England's older cultural landscape rather than just its pastoral towns.
