Barraba is a small New England town north of Tamworth, on the road between Manilla and Bingara. It is dry-country New South Wales rather than coast-and-cafe NSW: wide streets, old pubs, grain silos, paddocks, and low ranges on the horizon.

The town has become better known for its public art, especially [Barraba Silo Art](/australiaandpacific/australia/newsouthwales/barraba/barraba_silo_art), but it also works as a base for birdwatching, fossicking, and drives out to dams, lookouts, and national parks. The pace is slow, and that is the point.

You need private transport here. Barraba is useful if you are exploring the New England and North West region in pieces, especially if you like towns that still feel local rather than curated for weekend visitors.
