Barraba Silo Art is the town's clearest visual landmark. Painted by Fintan Magee in 2019, the mural shows a water diviner and references the endangered regent honeyeater, tying the work to the dry landscape around Barraba.

It is an easy roadside stop on the southern edge of town. Like the better silo art in Australia, it gives you a reason to slow down in a place many drivers once passed through.
