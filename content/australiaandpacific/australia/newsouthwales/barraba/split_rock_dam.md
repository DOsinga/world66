Split Rock Dam is a practical outdoor escape southeast of Barraba, with fishing, picnic areas, boating access, and camping. It is not wild country, but it gives travellers a reason to get off the main street for a few hours.

The dam is best with your own car and supplies. In hot weather, shade and water matter more than the distance on the map suggests.
