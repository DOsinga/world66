Eurobodalla Regional Botanic Garden is a calm, useful stop if you want to understand the bush you have been driving through. Its paths and displays focus on plants from the South Coast and nearby ranges rather than imported garden spectacle.

It works especially well after a beach morning, when you want shade, bird calls, and a slower hour away from the traffic on the Princes Highway.
