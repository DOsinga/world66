Mogo Wildlife Park is a compact wildlife park south of Batemans Bay, known for exotic and endangered species rather than a broad native-animal collection. Red pandas, snow leopards, lemurs, and other species make it one of the main family attractions in the area.

It is an easy half-day trip from Batemans Bay. The scale is manageable, so it works well with children or as a relaxed stop while travelling along the coast.
