Oyster Shed on Wray Street is the kind of place Batemans Bay needs: direct, local, and tied to the estuary. The draw is Clyde River oysters, sold from a bright, plain shed across the bridge from town.

Do not come expecting formal service. Come for oysters, a coffee or snack if you need one, and the satisfaction of eating the bay's best-known product beside the water that made it possible.
