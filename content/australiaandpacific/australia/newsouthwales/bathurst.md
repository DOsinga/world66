Bathurst is the oldest inland European settlement in Australia, a Central West city about 200 kilometres west of [Sydney](/australiaandpacific/australia/newsouthwales/sydney). It has the broad streets, cold winters, and solid public buildings of a wealthy nineteenth-century country town, but its modern identity is tied just as strongly to motor racing.

[Mount Panorama](/australiaandpacific/australia/newsouthwales/bathurst/mount_panorama) rises on the edge of town, and for much of the year you can drive the famous circuit at ordinary road speed. At its foot, the [National Motor Racing Museum](/australiaandpacific/australia/newsouthwales/bathurst/national_motor_racing_museum) explains why Bathurst is almost sacred ground for Australian racing fans.

The city is not only about cars. The [Australian Fossil and Mineral Museum](/australiaandpacific/australia/newsouthwales/bathurst/australian_fossil_and_mineral_museum) has one of the country's strongest regional museum collections, while [Abercrombie House](/australiaandpacific/australia/newsouthwales/bathurst/abercrombie_house) and [Ben Chifley's House](/australiaandpacific/australia/newsouthwales/bathurst/ben_chifleys_house) tell two very different stories about inland wealth and political plainness. If you are crossing the Blue Mountains with a spare day, Bathurst has enough substance to be more than a fuel stop.
