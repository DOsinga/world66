Bathurst Observatory Research Facility is an astronomy site outside Bathurst that combines research and education with public telescope tours. Its work includes meteorite study and astrophotography, giving the visit more substance than a simple stargazing stop.

The appeal depends on tour availability and weather, so check ahead before driving out. On a clear night it is a good local option for travellers interested in astronomy.
