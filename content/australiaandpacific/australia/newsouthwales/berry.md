Berry is a small South Coast town that has learned how to be a day trip without losing all of its village bones. Queen Street still has old shopfronts, a handsome museum building, bakeries, cafes, and enough weekend traffic to remind you that Sydney is only a couple of hours away.

The good reason to stay longer is the landscape around it. Behind town, the escarpment rises quickly toward Barren Grounds, with [Drawing Room Rocks](/australiaandpacific/australia/newsouthwales/berry/drawing_room_rocks) giving one of the best short-walk views in the Shoalhaven. East of Berry, [Seven Mile Beach National Park](/australiaandpacific/australia/newsouthwales/berry/seven_mile_beach_national_park) runs along a long surf beach between Gerroa and Shoalhaven Heads.

Berry works best as a gentle base: museum in the morning, lunch in town, then a walk or beach run when the light softens. It is pretty, accessible, and more satisfying if you treat it as town plus countryside rather than just a cafe stop on the highway.
