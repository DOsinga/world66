Berry Museum occupies a former E.S. & A. Bank building on Queen Street, a small but distinctive piece of Victorian Gothic architecture. The displays cover the town, the old Berry Estate, local families, and the broader Shoalhaven district.

It is a useful first stop if you want Berry to feel like more than cafes and homewares shops. Opening hours are limited, so check before building a day around it.
