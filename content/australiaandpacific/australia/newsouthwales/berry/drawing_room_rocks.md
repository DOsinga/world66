Drawing Room Rocks is the short walk that makes Berry more than a pretty village. The track climbs from the Woodhill side of town through forest and heath to a sandstone platform, where weathered rock forms look like natural tables and chairs.

The view reaches across the Shoalhaven, Kangaroo Valley, and the coast on clear days. The walk is not long, but it is steep in sections and the lookout is unfenced, so wear proper shoes and keep well back from the edge.
