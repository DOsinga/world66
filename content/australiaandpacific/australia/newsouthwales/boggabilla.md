Boggabilla is a small border village in northern New South Wales, just south-east of Goondiwindi and the Queensland line. The country around it is flat, hot, and productive, given over to cotton, broadacre crops, sheep, and cattle. Most travellers meet it from the Newell Highway, where it feels like the last New South Wales pause before the border.

The name comes from the Kamilaroi language and is usually translated as "full of creeks." That is a useful clue: this is Goomeroi country, and the most worthwhile stop in town is [Euraba Artists and Papermakers Gallery](/australiaandpacific/australia/newsouthwales/boggabilla/euraba_artists_and_papermakers_gallery), a community art centre known for handmade paper and Aboriginal art.

Boggabilla is not a polished tourist town, and that is part of the point. Use it as a practical stop, then look for the art, the river country, and the quieter roads heading east towards Dthinna Dthinnawan National Park.
