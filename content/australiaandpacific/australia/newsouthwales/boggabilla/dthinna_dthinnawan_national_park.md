Dthinna Dthinnawan National Park sits well out of town, beyond Yetman, and needs a proper vehicle and a little planning. The park protects dry woodland, Aboriginal sites, and quiet picnic country, with horse-riding trails and rougher tracks for confident four-wheel-drivers. Do not treat it as a casual sealed-road detour; check access conditions before you go.
