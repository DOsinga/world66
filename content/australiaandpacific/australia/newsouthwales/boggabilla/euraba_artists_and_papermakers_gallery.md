Euraba is the reason to slow down in Boggabilla. The Goomeroi-owned art centre is known for handmade paper made from cotton off-cuts, turning a local agricultural material into prints, sculptural works, and community art. Visits can depend on staffing and bookings, so check ahead rather than assuming gallery hours.
