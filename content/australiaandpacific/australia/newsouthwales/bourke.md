Bourke is an outback town on the Darling River, far enough from Sydney that "back of Bourke" became Australian shorthand for the middle of nowhere. It is still a service town for a huge dry district, but that remoteness is also the reason to come.

The town was briefly imagined as an inland port, with paddle steamers carrying produce down the Murray-Darling system. The replica [Old Bourke Wharf](/australiaandpacific/australia/newsouthwales/bourke/old_bourke_wharf) and the [Back O' Bourke Exhibition Centre](/australiaandpacific/australia/newsouthwales/bourke/back_o_bourke_exhibition_centre) tell that story well enough to make the river more than scenery.

Bourke is also a base for nearby outback landscapes. [Gundabooka National Park](/australiaandpacific/australia/newsouthwales/bourke/gundabooka_national_park) has Aboriginal rock art and red ranges south of town, while North Bourke adds a small gallery and the old sense of life on the edge of the settled map.
