Back O' Bourke Gallery is a small art stop in North Bourke, with outback-inspired pastel works and a strong focus on the Darling River landscape. It is not a major museum, but it adds a human scale to a town often described only by distance.

Pair it with the river and wharf if you are exploring North Bourke.
