The Crossley Engine is an early oil-fuelled stationary engine, once used for industrial power before eventually being brought to Bourke. It is a niche sight, but a good one if old machinery and outback self-reliance interest you.

Its value is partly symbolic: in places this remote, power, water, and transport were never abstract ideas.
