Braidwood is a small Southern Tablelands town with more substance than its size suggests. Its main street still reads as a nineteenth-century gold-rush service town: broad, low, and lined with old inns, shops, churches, and weathered cottages.

The town is easy to underestimate if you are only driving between [Canberra](/australiaandpacific/australia/australiancapitalterritory/canberra) and the South Coast. Slow down and it becomes a good half-day stop, with the [Braidwood Museum](/australiaandpacific/australia/newsouthwales/braidwood/braidwood_museum), the heritage streetscape, and [Bedervale](/australiaandpacific/australia/newsouthwales/braidwood/bedervale) giving the old town real depth.

Braidwood also works as a base for rougher country to the east. The [Big Hole Walking Track](/australiaandpacific/australia/newsouthwales/braidwood/big_hole_walking_track) is the reason to keep your walking shoes handy rather than treating the place as only coffee and petrol.
