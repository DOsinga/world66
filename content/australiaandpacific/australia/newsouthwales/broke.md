Broke is a tiny village in the heart of the Hunter Valley wine country in New South Wales, sitting among vineyards between Singleton and Cessnock. The area around it is quieter than the more developed parts of the Hunter — fewer tour buses, more open country — which makes it a good base if you prefer your wine tasting unhurried.

The village itself is small, but the surrounding countryside has cellar doors, olive plantations, a macadamia farm, and an alpaca farm scattered along the back roads. The [Broke Fordwich Estate](/australiaandpacific/australia/newsouthwales/broke/broke_fordwich_estate) is a good introduction to the sub-regional style; the [Wollombi Brook](/australiaandpacific/australia/newsouthwales/broke/wollombi_brook) nearby offers easy walking and birdwatching. A coal mine sits at the edge of the village, a reminder that the Hunter Valley runs on mining as much as it does on wine. For wilderness walking, [Yengo National Park](/australiaandpacific/australia/newsouthwales/broke/yengo_national_park) begins less than 20 kilometres south and contains Aboriginal rock engravings in deep sandstone gorges.
