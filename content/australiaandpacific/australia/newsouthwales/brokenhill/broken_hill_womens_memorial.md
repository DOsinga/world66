The Broken Hill Women's Memorial honours the women who held the mining town together during strikes, lockouts, and hard years on the Line of Lode. It is a small but pointed stop in central Broken Hill, especially if you are following the city's labour and mining history.

Pair it with the nearby Trades Hall and Miner's Memorial for a fuller picture of how deeply industrial disputes shaped Broken Hill's civic life.
