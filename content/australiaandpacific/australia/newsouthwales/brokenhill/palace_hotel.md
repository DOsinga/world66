The Palace Hotel is a tall verandahed pub on Argent Street, in the heart of Broken Hill's heritage quarter. From the outside it looks like a fine but unremarkable Federation-era hotel; inside, the walls are covered in a riot of murals that are unlike anything else in Australia — copies of European masters, outback landscapes, religious scenes, all painted directly onto the plaster over decades.

The bar is open to the public and the food is decent pub fare. If you can, walk through the dining rooms and up the stairs to see the full extent of the painting. The rooms above are old-school country pub accommodation.
