The Pro Hart Gallery and Sculpture Park celebrates one of Broken Hill's best-known artists, whose bright outback scenes helped define a popular image of the region. The gallery gives visitors a direct link between the mining city and the desert art culture that grew around it.

It is a personal, approachable stop rather than a large public museum. Come for the paintings, the oddball energy, and the way Hart's work turns dust, miners, insects, and corrugated iron into something unmistakably local.
