Canowindra calls itself Australia's balloon capital, and this is the town's most photogenic modern identity. Flights usually lift off early, when the wind is calm and the Central West landscape is still soft with morning light.

The Canowindra International Balloon Challenge, usually held in autumn, is the best time to see the sky fill with colour even if you are not flying yourself.
