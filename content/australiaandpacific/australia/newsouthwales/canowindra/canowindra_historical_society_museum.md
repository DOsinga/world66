The Historical Society Museum is a classic country museum: domestic objects, farm tools, photographs, documents, and local stories gathered by people who care about the town. It is a useful companion to the fossil museum because it brings Canowindra back from deep time into ordinary rural life.

Opening hours can be limited, so check before building a day around it. If it is open, it is worth an unhurried look.
