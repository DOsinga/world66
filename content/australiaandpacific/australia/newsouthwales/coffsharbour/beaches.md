---
title: "Beaches"
type: section
---

Coffs has roughly thirty kilometres of beaches and the locals split them by character. **Park Beach** is the main town beach, north of the harbour breakwater: long, sandy, surf-club patrolled in the warm months, easy walk from the holiday-park strip. **Jetty Beach**, on the south side of the breakwater, is the calmer family beach, partly sheltered by the breakwater itself.

**Diggers Beach**, immediately north of the Big Banana and accessed by a steep path through coastal scrub, is the local surfer's pick — a clean reef break, fewer crowds, occasional dolphin sightings from the lookout above the car park. Further north, **Sapphire Beach** and **Emerald Beach** are quieter still and run by small holiday-park towns.

The water is warm enough to swim without a wetsuit from October to May, and lifeguard patrols run the same months. Watch for rips on the unpatrolled stretches.
