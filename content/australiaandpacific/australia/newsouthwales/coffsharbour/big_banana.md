The Big Banana is now the front gate of a small amusement park on the highway just north of Coffs Harbour, with a water park, ice-skating rink, mini-golf and toboggan run aimed squarely at families breaking the Sydney–Brisbane drive. The banana itself you can walk through; the inside is a small exhibit on the local industry. The cafe serves chocolate-dipped frozen bananas, which is the right thing to order.

It is unapologetically kitsch and you should not pretend otherwise. Allow ninety minutes if you are travelling with children; thirty if not. Free to enter the site, individual rides cost extra.
