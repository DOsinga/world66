Dolphin Marine Magic is a long-running Coffs Harbour marine attraction with dolphins and seals in a smaller, more informal setting than the big theme-park aquariums. There are scheduled shows, and paid animal encounters are offered separately.

Check session times before going, since the visit works best when timed around a show.
