---
title: "Eating Out"
type: section
---

Coffs is a working harbour and the local food is straightforward seafood, banana-coast cafes and a small but real Italian and Chinese contingent left from waves of post-war migration to the canefields.

The **Coffs Harbour Fishermen's Co-op** sells fresh fish and prawns off the boats at the marina from mid-afternoon — buy a bag of cooked prawns and a lemon and eat them on the harbour wall. Sitting down, the cluster of cafes along **The Jetty strip** (Harbour Drive, between the highway and the marina) is the best concentration: **Fiasco** for Italian, **Latitude 30** for a marina-view bistro, and the various surf-club bistros at Park Beach for cheap dinners with a view.

For breakfast and coffee, the cafes around the **Jetty Village** complex on Harbour Drive are reliable; **Cafe Aqua** at the marina is the picturesque option.
