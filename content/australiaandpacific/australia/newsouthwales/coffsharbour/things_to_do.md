---
title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Coffs Harbour is a beach and outdoor town. The signature short walk is out along the breakwater from the marina to **Muttonbird Island**, where the wedge-tailed shearwaters nest in their thousands from September to April. The summit is the best harbour view in town and the launching point for whale-watching cruises in the southern winter.

In town, the **North Coast Regional Botanic Garden** is a quiet hour, and the **Big Banana** is the obligatory roadside stop — Australia's first Big Thing, opened in 1964, and still a cheerful piece of kitsch. The diving and snorkelling around the **Solitary Islands Marine Park** is the city's hidden quality: tropical and temperate currents meet here, so a single dive can take in tropical fish, grey nurse sharks and (in season) humpback whales.

Inland, the rainforests of **Dorrigo** and the **Waterfall Way** drive deserve a full day each — see Day Trips.
