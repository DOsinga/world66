Collarenebri is a tiny town on the Barwon River in north-west New South Wales, far enough from the coast that the landscape has shifted into dry inland country. It is not a polished tourist town, and that is partly why it is worth a careful stop if you are already travelling between Moree, Walgett and Lightning Ridge.

The town's strongest reason to visit is cultural. [Collarenebri Aboriginal Cemetery](/australiaandpacific/australia/newsouthwales/collarenebri/collarenebri_aboriginal_cemetery) is one of the most distinctive heritage places in the state, with glass-decorated graves that speak to local Gamilaroi mourning practice and community memory. North-east of town, the [Collymongle Carved Trees](/australiaandpacific/australia/newsouthwales/collarenebri/collymongle_carved_trees) are another serious, quiet site tied to older ceremonial life.

There is also a more practical pleasure: the [Collarenebri Bore Bath](/australiaandpacific/australia/newsouthwales/collarenebri/collarenebri_bore_bath), fed by the Great Artesian Basin. That mix of river town, living Aboriginal heritage and hot mineral water is enough to make Collarenebri more than a fuel stop, provided you arrive with respect and do not expect a long list of services.
