Collarenebri Aboriginal Cemetery is the town's most important place to understand. It has been used since the early twentieth century and is listed on the New South Wales State Heritage Register for its distinctive grave decoration, family groupings and continuing importance to the local Aboriginal community.

This is a place for quiet respect. The beauty of the glass-covered graves is real, but they are personal memorials, not curiosities. Stay on obvious paths, do not touch grave decorations, and treat the visit as a chance to understand Collarenebri's living Gamilaroi history.
