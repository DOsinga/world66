The Collymongle Carved Trees are one of the strongest reasons to detour around Collarenebri. The trees are connected with a Gamilaroi ceremonial ground and carry carved designs of deep cultural meaning. Several are displayed near the Collymongle turnoff for visitors to view.

This is not a picnic-photo stop. Take time, read what interpretation is available, and remember that these trees were separated from their original ceremonial setting. Their presence beside the road is convenient for travellers, but the story behind them is much larger and more difficult.
