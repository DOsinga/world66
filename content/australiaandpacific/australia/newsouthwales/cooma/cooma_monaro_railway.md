Cooma-Monaro Railway keeps the old station and local rail story alive. Tourist trains have not been running for years, but the museum still gives railway-minded visitors a reason to look beyond the highway strip.

It is a modest stop rather than a headline attraction. Pair it with the Snowy Hydro centre and the prison museum and Cooma starts to feel like a real Monaro town, not just a road junction.
