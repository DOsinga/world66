The New South Wales Corrective Services Museum is one of Cooma's more memorable stops. It covers prison history from colonial punishment to modern corrections, with objects and stories that are darker than the usual country-town museum fare.

Its setting matters: the museum sits beside Cooma Correctional Centre, and tours have often been led by minimum-security inmates. Check current opening times before building a day around it.
