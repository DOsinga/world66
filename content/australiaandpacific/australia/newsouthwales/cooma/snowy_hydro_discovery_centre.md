The Snowy Hydro Discovery Centre explains the tunnels, dams, power stations, and migration story behind the Snowy Mountains Scheme. It is the best first stop if you want Cooma to make sense as more than a town on the way to the snow.

The exhibits are strongest for travelers with an interest in engineering or modern Australian history. Even if turbines are not your thing, the short film helps show the ambition of the project.
