---
title: "Getting There"
type: section
---

Cooranbong is about 100km north of Sydney and 30km south of Newcastle, making it straightforward to reach by road or rail.

**By train:** Morisset station, about 6km from the town centre, is on the Main Northern line with regular services from Sydney Central (about 90 minutes) and Newcastle (about 30 minutes). From the station, a taxi or rideshare is the easiest option into town.

**By car:** Take the M1 Pacific Motorway north from Sydney and exit at Dora Creek or Morisset. The drive from Sydney takes about an hour in clear traffic.

**By bus:** Limited local bus services connect Morisset station with Cooranbong and the university. Check Transport NSW for current timetables.
