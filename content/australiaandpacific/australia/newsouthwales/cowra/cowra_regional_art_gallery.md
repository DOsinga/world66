Cowra Regional Art Gallery sits in the town's cultural precinct, sharing the area with the library, civic centre, and World Peace Bell. It has a lively program for a town of Cowra's size, mixing touring shows, regional artists, and work from its own collection.

The Calleen Collection and Olive Cotton material give it more substance than a token municipal gallery. It also holds heritage items connected with the POW camp, which makes it a useful stop if you are following Cowra's broader memory trail.
