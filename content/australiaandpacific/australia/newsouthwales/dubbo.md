Dubbo is the Central West city that makes the inland road west of the Blue Mountains feel like it has properly opened out. It is big enough to work as a base, but its best attractions are not generic country-town fillers: the open-range zoo, the preserved gaol, the Royal Flying Doctor visitor centre, and the old homestead west of town each tell a different part of inland New South Wales.

The [Taronga Western Plains Zoo](/australiaandpacific/australia/newsouthwales/dubbo/taronga_western_plains_zoo) is the headline. It spreads animals through large paddocks and asks visitors to move around by bike, cart, car, or long walk, which suits the flat country. In town, [Old Dubbo Gaol](/australiaandpacific/australia/newsouthwales/dubbo/old_dubbo_gaol) gives a sharp, compact look at punishment and policing from 1859 to 1966.

Dubbo also works as a practical stop between Sydney and the outback. The Macquarie River runs through the city, the Tracker Riley Cycleway links the centre with the zoo side of town, and the airport-side [Royal Flying Doctor Visitor Experience](/australiaandpacific/australia/newsouthwales/dubbo/royal_flying_doctor_visitor_experience) explains how remote Australia gets medical care across huge distances.

Give Dubbo a full day if you can. The zoo alone can take most of it, and the city is more rewarding when you let the inland scale, river, and working-town feel sit alongside the visitor sights.
