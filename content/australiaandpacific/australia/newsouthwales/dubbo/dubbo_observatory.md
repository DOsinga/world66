Dubbo Observatory is a small public observatory outside town, set far enough from the centre to make the night sky feel properly dark. Tours focus on the southern sky, with the Milky Way as the main draw on clear nights.

It is a good evening add-on after Dubbo's daytime sights, especially for families or anyone who has not spent much time under inland Australian skies. Check conditions before going; cloud cover matters more here than the calendar.
