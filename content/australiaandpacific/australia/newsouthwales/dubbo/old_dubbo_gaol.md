Old Dubbo Gaol sits in the centre of town, which makes its walls and cells feel more immediate than a ruin on the edge of nowhere. The prison operated from 1859 to 1966 and now works as a hands-on museum of colonial and modern punishment.

The visit is short but memorable: cells, gallows, solitary spaces, and the small domestic details of prison life all sit within a few steps of Macquarie Street.
