The Royal Flying Doctor Visitor Experience is a good reminder that inland Australia is measured in hours and empty kilometres, not just map distance. The exhibits explain how doctors, nurses, pilots, and dispatchers reach patients across remote country where a conventional ambulance makes no sense.

It pairs neatly with the zoo because both sit west and northwest of the city centre. Come here when you want Dubbo's outback role to feel concrete rather than just a word on a highway sign.
