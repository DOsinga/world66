Aslings Beach is Eden's town beach, set on the northern side of Twofold Bay. It is the easiest place for a swim or surf without leaving town, and the rock pool at the southern end is a good fallback when the sea is rough.

The Lake Curalo boardwalk starts near the northern end and gives an easy walk around the lagoon edge. It is not the wildest beach on the Sapphire Coast, but it is the one that makes Eden feel like a coastal town rather than just a harbour.
