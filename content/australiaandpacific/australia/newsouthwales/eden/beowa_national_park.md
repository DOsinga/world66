Beowa National Park, formerly Ben Boyd National Park, wraps around two sections of coast near Eden. It has red-rock cliffs, sheltered inlets, empty beaches, heath, and several of the historic places tied to Twofold Bay's whaling past.

The southern section is the classic day out from Eden, with Boyds Tower, Davidson Whaling Station, and the road toward Green Cape Lighthouse. The Light to Light walk is the longer prize, but even short walks from the car parks give you a strong taste of the coast.
