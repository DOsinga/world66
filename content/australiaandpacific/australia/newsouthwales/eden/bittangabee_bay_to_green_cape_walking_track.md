The Bittangabee Bay to Green Cape walking track is a one-way coastal walk in the southern section of Beowa National Park. It runs for about 7 km through exposed heath, forest and cliff country between the sheltered bay and Green Cape.

Allow two to three hours and treat it as a real bushwalk, not a promenade. It pairs naturally with Green Cape Lighthouse, but you need to plan transport or be ready to walk back the same way.
