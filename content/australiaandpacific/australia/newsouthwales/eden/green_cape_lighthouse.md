Green Cape Lighthouse is the southernmost lighthouse in New South Wales, set on a wind-cut headland about 45 minutes south of Eden. The drive is part of the appeal, pushing through Beowa National Park to a coast that feels remote even by Sapphire Coast standards.

The old concrete lighthouse and keeper buildings give the cape a strong maritime feel, and the views are excellent for spring whale watching. It works best as a slow half-day trip from Eden, paired with Boyds Tower or a section of the Light to Light walk.
