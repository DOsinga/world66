The Haycock Point to Barmouth Beach walking track follows a wild stretch of coast north of Eden. The one-way walk runs from the Pambula River estuary past Haycock Point toward Barmouth Beach, with unspoiled beaches and headland views along the way.

Allow about two hours if you want to walk slowly and take in the coast. The track is a good choice when you want Ben Boyd National Park scenery without committing to a long bushwalk.
