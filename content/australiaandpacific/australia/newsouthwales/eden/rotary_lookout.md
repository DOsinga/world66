Rotary Lookout is the quick orientation stop in Eden. From the headland above town you can look across Twofold Bay, down toward the wharf, and out to the water where migrating whales pass in season.

It is not a long visit, but it belongs early in the day. The view explains the harbour better than a map, and in spring it is one of the easiest land-based whale-watching spots in town.
