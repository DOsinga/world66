Severs Beach is a quiet Sapphire Coast beach north of Eden, inside what is now Beowa National Park. It has the wild, undeveloped feel of this coast, with forest behind the sand and good chances of seeing whales offshore in season.

The beach also has Aboriginal shell middens dated to about 3,500 years ago, so treat it as more than just a swimming stop. Come for a slow walk, keep to established access, and leave the middens undisturbed.
