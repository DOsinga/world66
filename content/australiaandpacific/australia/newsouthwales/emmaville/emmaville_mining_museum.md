Emmaville Mining Museum is the best reason to stop in the village. Its collection includes thousands of mineral and gemstone specimens, mining tools, photographs, and recreated rooms that show how rough life could be on the tin fields.

It is local, volunteer-run, and more interesting than its size suggests. Check opening days before driving out.
