Mystery Face Picnic Area is the starting point for the Mystery Face Walking Track, a short loop through granite country with unusual rock formations, birdlife, and spring wildflowers. The walk is around 2.6 km and takes roughly one to one and a half hours.

Bring water and shoes with grip, especially after rain when the roads and track can be boggy. The picnic tables make it a useful stop on a wider drive through the Emmaville area.
