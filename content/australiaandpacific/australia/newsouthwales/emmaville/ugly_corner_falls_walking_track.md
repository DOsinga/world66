Ugly Corner Falls Walking Track is a short but rough bushwalk north-west of Emmaville. The 3 km return route has steep sections, steps, limited signage, and boggy roads after rain, so it suits walkers who are comfortable with a bit of effort for a quiet waterfall outing.
