---
title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Evans Head is about the coast first. Razorback Lookout is the quick orientation point, especially around sunrise or whale season. Bundjalung National Park is the main reason to linger, with beaches, river paddling, lagoons, and sandy tracks running south from town. The Evans Head Heritage Aviation Museum adds a different note, telling the story of the large World War II RAAF base that once operated here.
