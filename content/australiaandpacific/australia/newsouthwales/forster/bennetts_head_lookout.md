Bennetts Head Lookout is the easy ocean viewpoint in Forster, set above the beaches on Marine Drive. It is close enough to town for a quick stop, but the view is still worth making time for.

Come here to orient yourself before choosing a beach, or to watch for whales during the migration season from May to November.
