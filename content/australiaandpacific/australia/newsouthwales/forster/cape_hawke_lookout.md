Cape Hawke Lookout sits in Booti Booti National Park, reached by a short climb through coastal forest. From the tower you can look back across Forster, Wallis Lake, and the long sweep of beaches to the south.

It is the strongest single viewpoint around town, especially on a clear morning. Bring water and give yourself time for the walk rather than treating it as a quick roadside stop.
