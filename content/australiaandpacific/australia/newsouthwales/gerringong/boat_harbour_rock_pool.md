Boat Harbour Rock Pool is a sheltered ocean pool below the cliffs at Gerringong's old boat harbour. It is small, salty, and scenic, with a sandy shallow end and rock around the edges.

Treat it as a fair-weather swim. The setting is lovely, but it is still an ocean pool, so swell, tide, and slippery rock matter.
