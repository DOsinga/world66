Glen Innes Art Gallery is a local gallery with changing exhibitions of contemporary art, ceramics, jewellery, and sculpture. The focus is regional, but the program also brings in artists from elsewhere in Australia.

It is a low-key cultural stop rather than a large museum. Check the current exhibition if you are in town, especially if you are interested in local craft or want a quieter indoor break.
