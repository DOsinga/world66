Raspberry Lookout is a high country viewpoint over the Bindery-Mann Wilderness Area. The stop is simple, but the view is wide and wild, with forested ridges running away from the road.

Bring binoculars or a long lens if you have one, and do not count on services beyond basic picnic facilities. Weather can change quickly on the range, so carry water and a warm layer even on a clear day.
