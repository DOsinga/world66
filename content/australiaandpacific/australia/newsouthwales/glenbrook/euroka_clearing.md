Euroka Clearing is a wide, grassy opening inside Blue Mountains National Park, south of Glenbrook. It is used as a campground and picnic area, but day visitors come for the quieter bush setting, parrots, cockatoos, and kangaroos feeding around the edges.

It is also a useful base for longer walks and rides in the Glenbrook area. The access road is inside the national park, so check current conditions before committing to it after heavy rain.
