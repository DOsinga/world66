Jellybean Pool is the easy reward near the Glenbrook park entrance: a sandy bend and swimming hole on Glenbrook Creek, reached by a short but steep track. On hot days it can feel more like a local ritual than a wilderness escape.

The pool also sits on longer walking routes toward Red Hands Cave. Wear shoes with grip, take care on the descent, and avoid swimming after heavy rain when the creek is running hard.
