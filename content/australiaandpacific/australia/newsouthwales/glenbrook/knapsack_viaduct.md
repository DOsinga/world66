Knapsack Viaduct is a 19th-century railway viaduct on Lapstone Hill, built in 1867 by engineer John Whitton. The stone arches are an impressive piece of early New South Wales railway engineering.

It is signposted from the Emu Plains motorway exit and works well as a short heritage stop on the lower Blue Mountains edge.
