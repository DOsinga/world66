Nepean Lookout shows a quieter side of the Blue Mountains, looking over the Nepean River rather than the famous upper-mountain cliffs. It is a good stop if you are exploring the Glenbrook end of the national park and want a view without the crowds around Katoomba.

The appeal is simple: bushland, river bends, and the sense of Sydney dropping away behind you.
