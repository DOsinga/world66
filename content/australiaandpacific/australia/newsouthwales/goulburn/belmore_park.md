Belmore Park is Goulburn's central green, set just off Auburn Street and close to the city's main civic buildings. It is the easy pause in the middle of town: lawns, shade, memorials, and enough space for markets and community events.

Travellers will most likely pass it while walking between the station, the court house, and the cathedral. Use it as a breather rather than a headline sight.
