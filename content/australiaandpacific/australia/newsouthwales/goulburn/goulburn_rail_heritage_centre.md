Goulburn Rail Heritage Centre occupies the old roundhouse and locomotive depot, giving the town's railway history a proper industrial setting. Instead of treating trains as detached display pieces, it shows them where they belong: beside turntables, workshops, sidings, and the infrastructure that made Goulburn an important inland railway town.

The collection is best for travellers who like machinery, working museums, and country-town transport history. Check opening days before going, as heritage sites can keep limited hours, but the roundhouse setting makes it more memorable than a simple platform stop at the main station.
