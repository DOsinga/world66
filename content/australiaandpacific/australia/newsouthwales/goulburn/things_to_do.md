---

Goulburn's attractions reflect its history as the first major inland city established in colonial New South Wales. The built heritage is Victorian and Edwardian — churches, a courthouse, public buildings, and the railway station that mark the confidence of a 19th-century regional centre. The Big Merino is the town's most famous landmark, but the real substance is in the streetscapes and historic buildings around the city centre.
