Fig Tree Avenue is one of Grafton's simplest pleasures: a street canopy of huge old figs that makes the town's scale and age feel real. It is not a formal attraction with a ticket booth, just a place to walk slowly and look up.

Pair it with the riverfront and the jacaranda streets. Together they explain why Grafton feels greener and grander than many inland highway towns.
