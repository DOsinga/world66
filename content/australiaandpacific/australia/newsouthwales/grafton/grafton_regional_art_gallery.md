Grafton Regional Art Gallery is the Clarence Valley's main public gallery, with a collection that includes painting, printmaking, sculpture, and work by artists from northern New South Wales. Its changing exhibition program gives the town a stronger cultural stop than its size might suggest.

It is an easy central visit and pairs well with a walk through Grafton's older streets, especially during jacaranda season.
