Susan Island sits in the Clarence River beside Grafton, with a protected western section of lowland subtropical rainforest. It is more important ecologically than its low profile suggests, especially as habitat for flying foxes.

Access and sensitivity matter. Local tourism material often frames it as something to view from the river or bridge rather than trample through, so treat it as part of the Clarence River landscape and respect any restrictions.
