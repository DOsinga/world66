Ben Halls Cave is one of the hideouts associated with the 19th-century bushranger Ben Hall, reached by a walking track in Weddin Mountains National Park. The cave itself is closed for safety, but the walk gives good views and a tangible link to one of the region's best-known bushranger stories.
