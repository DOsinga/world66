The Chrysler Car Museum is a small specialist collection for car enthusiasts, with Chryslers from the 1930s through to more recent models. It is a personal, owner-led museum rather than a large polished attraction, but the depth of knowledge and the unusual focus make it a worthwhile stop if you like motoring history.
