The Grenfell Commodities Silos turn four working grain silos into a broad mural of sheep, cattle, native birds, farmland, and the Weddin Mountains. Painted by Heesco Khosnaran in 2018, they are part of the silo-art habit that has become a real rural Australian road-trip theme.

You do not need long here, but the scale makes it worth pulling over, and the imagery ties the town directly to the country around it.
