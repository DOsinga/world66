The Grenfell Historical Museum fills the former School of Arts building on Camp Street with local material: gold-rush history, household rooms, farm machinery, bushranger stories, and the families who shaped the Weddin district.

It is an old-style country museum, which is part of the charm. Give yourself time to read the labels and follow the town's path from goldfield camp to wheat-belt service centre.
