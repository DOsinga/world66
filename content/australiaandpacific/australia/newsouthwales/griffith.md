Griffith is the Riverina at full scale: irrigation channels, flat farmland, wineries with national labels, and a town centre shaped by Italian migration. It was planned by Walter Burley Griffin and Marion Mahony Griffin, but it feels less like a design exercise than a working food-and-wine city in the middle of dry country.

The best day mixes the human story with the landscape. Start at [Pioneer Park Museum](/australiaandpacific/australia/newsouthwales/griffith/griffith_pioneer_park_museum), walk the stone shelters of [Hermit's Cave](/australiaandpacific/australia/newsouthwales/griffith/hermits_cave), then drive out to cellar doors or the sandstone gullies of [Cocoparra National Park](/australiaandpacific/australia/newsouthwales/griffith/cocoparra_national_park). Griffith's Italian food is not a garnish to the trip; it is one of the things that makes the city itself.

Distances are broad and public transport will not help much with the best stops. Bring or hire a car, and plan around heat in summer.
