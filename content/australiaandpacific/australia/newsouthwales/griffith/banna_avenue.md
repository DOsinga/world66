Banna Avenue is Griffith's main street and the easiest place to feel the city's Italian influence without turning it into a formal attraction. Cafes, old-school restaurants, delis, and ordinary town errands sit side by side.

It is not a postcard strip. Its value is that it feels lived-in, and it makes more sense after a museum stop or a cellar-door drive.
