Cocoparra National Park rises out of the Riverina plains northeast of Griffith, with red sandstone, short gorge walks, lookouts, spring wildflowers, and birdlife. It gives the city a strong nature anchor beyond vineyards and irrigation channels.

Jacks Creek and Falcon Falls are good first walks. Carry water and do not underestimate heat; this is dry country.
