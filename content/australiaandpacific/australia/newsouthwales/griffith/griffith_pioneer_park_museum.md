Griffith Pioneer Park Museum is the clearest introduction to the city. Its buildings and displays cover the irrigation area, early settlement, machinery, wine history, and the Italian community that helped make Griffith feel different from many inland New South Wales towns.

Give it more than a token stop. The museum works best when you let the local story build from several small details.
