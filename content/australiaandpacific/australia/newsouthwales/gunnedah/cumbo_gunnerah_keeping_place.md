Cumbo Gunnerah Keeping Place is one of Gunnedah's most important cultural stops. It holds local Aboriginal artefacts and presents stories from the district through displays, audio-visual material, and arts and crafts.

Opening arrangements can vary, so check ahead before planning the whole morning around it.
