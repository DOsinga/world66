The Dorothea Mackellar Memorial Statue marks Gunnedah's link with one of Australia's most quoted poems. Mackellar's family had a property near town, and the dry inland country around Gunnedah helped shape the landscape behind "My Country."

The statue is a quick stop, but it gives the town a literary identity beyond its usual role as a service centre.
