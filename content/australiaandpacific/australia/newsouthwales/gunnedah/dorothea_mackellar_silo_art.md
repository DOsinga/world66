Dorothea Mackellar Silo Art covers a 29-m-high maize mill with a tribute to the poet and part of her famous poem "My Country." It adds another stop to Gunnedah's Dorothea Mackellar trail, alongside the town's memorial statue.

The mill is private property, so view it from the public walkway around the corner near the bridge linking the Oxley Highway and Warrabungle Street. Do not block the driveway or try to approach the building.
