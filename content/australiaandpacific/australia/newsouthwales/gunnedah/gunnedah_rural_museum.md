Gunnedah Rural Museum is a sprawling local-history collection built around farm machinery, vehicles, engines, household objects, photographs, model railways, and other pieces of rural life.

It is not a polished museum experience, but that is part of the appeal. Visitors who like agricultural history and old machinery can spend a long time wandering through the sheds.
