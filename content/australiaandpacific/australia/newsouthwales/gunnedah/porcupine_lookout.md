Porcupine Lookout is one of the easiest scenic stops around Gunnedah. It looks back across town and out over the Namoi Valley, giving a quick sense of how the settlement sits between low hills and farming land.

It pairs well with Pensioners Hill if you want a short loop rather than a single photo stop.
