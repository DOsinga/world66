Copeton Dam is the big-water escape south-west of Inverell. It is a reservoir rather than a wilderness lake, but the scale is impressive in dry inland country, and it is a well-known local spot for fishing, boating, swimming, and camping.

It works best if you have a car and want to turn Inverell into a full outdoor day. Check water levels and local conditions before building a trip around boating.
