Inverell Pioneer Village gathers the kind of small-town history that is easy to lose: cottages, workshops, farm machinery, household objects, and local memorabilia laid out as a walk-through village.

It is the best place in town to understand how the district worked before sealed highways and big supermarkets. Take your time rather than treating it as a quick photo stop.
