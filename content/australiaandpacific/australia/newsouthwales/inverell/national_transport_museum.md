The National Transport Museum is one of Inverell's most substantial attractions, with a broad collection of classic cars, motorcycles, trucks, model vehicles, and motoring signs.

You do not need to be a devoted car person to enjoy it, but it helps if you like mechanical detail. The collection is strongest as a slow look at Australian road culture, from early touring to the family cars people actually remember.
