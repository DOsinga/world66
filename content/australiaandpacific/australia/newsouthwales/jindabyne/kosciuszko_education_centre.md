Kosciuszko Education Centre explains the wildlife, geology, and Aboriginal heritage of the Australian Alps through hands-on displays and visitor programs. It is a practical stop before heading deeper into Kosciuszko National Park, especially with children or first-time visitors to the high country.

The displays are broader than a standard park noticeboard, covering alpine habitats and the way the landscape changes with snow, fire, and season. There is also an unexpected section on Bhutan's mountain environment.
