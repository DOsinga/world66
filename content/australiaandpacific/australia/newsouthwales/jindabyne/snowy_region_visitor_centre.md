The Snowy Region Visitor Centre is the sensible first stop before heading into Kosciuszko National Park. Staff can help with road conditions, park passes, trail information, and weather warnings, which matter here more than a neat plan on paper.

There are small displays on the region too, but the real value is practical. If you are walking, biking, skiing, or driving into the park, stop here before committing to the day.
