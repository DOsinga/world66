The Thredbo Valley Track is one of the best summer reasons to base yourself in Jindabyne. The route follows the Thredbo River through alpine gums, bridges, and open valley country, with sections that can be split according to fitness and confidence.

It is not a casual town bike path. Check current trail conditions, organise shuttles if needed, and be honest about the lower sections, which are more remote and more demanding than the cruisy marketing photos suggest.
