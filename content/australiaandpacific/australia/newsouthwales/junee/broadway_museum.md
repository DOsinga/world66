Broadway Museum is the town's local history stop, housed in the former Broadway Hotel. Its displays cover Junee's railway years, rural life, and the kinds of small-town stories that explain why the streets here feel bigger than the present population would suggest.

Go after the roundhouse if you still want more context. It is a modest museum, but it helps join the railway, pub, and main-street pieces of Junee together.
