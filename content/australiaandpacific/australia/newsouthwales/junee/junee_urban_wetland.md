Junee Urban Wetland is a small boardwalk reserve close to the town centre. It will not compete with the grand wetlands of inland Australia, but it is a pleasant place to stretch your legs, look for birds, and reset after the railway and factory stops.

Its value is convenience. You can work it into a short town loop without turning the day into a drive.
