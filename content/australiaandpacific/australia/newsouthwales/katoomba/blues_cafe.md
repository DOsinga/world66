Blues Cafe is one of the few places in Katoomba where you can get a decent glass of wine in the evening and sometimes catch live music. It occupies a narrow heritage shopfront on Katoomba Street and has a programme of acoustic and jazz acts on weekend evenings. The crowd is local rather than tourist, which gives it an unpretentious feel that many of the more visitor-facing spots in town lack.
