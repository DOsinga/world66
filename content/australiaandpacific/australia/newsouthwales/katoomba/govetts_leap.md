Govett's Leap is one of the major Blue Mountains lookouts, with broad views over cliffs, forested valleys, and waterfalls near Blackheath. The viewpoint is developed enough for a short stop but also links into longer walking tracks.

Clear mornings are best, before haze and cloud settle into the valley.
