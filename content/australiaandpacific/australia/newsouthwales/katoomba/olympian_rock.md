Olympian Rock is a Blue Mountains lookout near Leura with wide views across the valleys toward Katoomba. It is usually quieter than the best-known viewpoints, which makes it a good stop for travellers who want the scenery without the main crowds.

The approach uses a dirt track, so wear decent shoes and take care after wet weather.
