The Kiama Coast Walk ties the town together better than any road map. The full route runs along the coast between Minnamurra and Gerringong, but visitors can pick off shorter sections from Kiama station, Blowhole Point, Bombo, or the Little Blowhole.

The northern stretch is the most useful if you want drama quickly: Bombo Beach, Bombo Headland, Cathedral Rocks, and changing views back toward the lighthouse. Take water, sun protection, and enough time to stop often, because this is a walk built around interruptions.
