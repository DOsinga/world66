The Little Blowhole sits south of the town centre, tucked into a suburban cliff edge rather than a busy harbour point. It is less grand than the main blowhole, but it often performs more reliably, especially when the swell comes from the north-east.

The viewing area is close to the action, so you hear the rush of water through the rock before the spray appears. It is a good reminder that Kiama's coast is volcanic, fractured, and still being worked on by the sea.
