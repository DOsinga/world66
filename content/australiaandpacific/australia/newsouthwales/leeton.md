Leeton is one of the Riverina's planned irrigation towns, laid out in the early 20th century and still carrying more architectural personality than its size suggests. It is not a major destination, but it passes the spare-day test if you are already moving between Griffith, Wagga Wagga, and the Murrumbidgee.

The town's best argument is its Art Deco core. Start with the [Leeton Art Deco Walk](/australiaandpacific/australia/newsouthwales/leeton/leeton_art_deco_walk), then stop at the red-neon [Roxy Theatre](/australiaandpacific/australia/newsouthwales/leeton/roxy_theatre), a country picture palace that gives the town a real landmark rather than just a main street. The nearby water towers, civic buildings, and old hotel fronts make Leeton feel designed, not accidental.

Nature is close too. [Fivebough Wetlands](/australiaandpacific/australia/newsouthwales/leeton/fivebough_wetlands), just outside town, is the strongest outdoor anchor, with bird hides and open wetland walks. Add the [Yanco Powerhouse Museum](/australiaandpacific/australia/newsouthwales/leeton/yanco_powerhouse_museum) or a tasting at [LillyPilly Estate Winery](/australiaandpacific/australia/newsouthwales/leeton/lillypilly_estate_winery), and Leeton becomes a quiet but coherent Riverina stop.
