LillyPilly Estate Winery is one of the easier cellar-door stops close to Leeton, known for Riverina table wines and sweet dessert styles. It gives the town's irrigation landscape a practical taste: grapes, water, heat, and the kind of family-run winery that fits a slow country afternoon.

It is not the Hunter Valley, and that is part of the point. Come for a relaxed tasting rather than a grand wine-region circuit.
