The Roxy Theatre is Leeton's showpiece, a 1930 country cinema and live theatre with a bold facade, red neon lettering, and the confidence of a much larger town. It was designed in an Art Deco, Art Nouveau, and Spanish Mission mix, and it still anchors the main street visually.

Check the programme if you want to see inside. Even from the street, the Roxy gives Leeton its strongest sense of place and makes the Art Deco walk feel like a real town story rather than a heritage brochure.
