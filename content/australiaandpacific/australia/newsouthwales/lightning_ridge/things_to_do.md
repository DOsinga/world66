---
title: Things to Do
type: section
---

Lightning Ridge is about opal, heat, and odd human persistence. Start with a mine tour so the town's geology makes sense, then look at the galleries and opal shops on Morilla and Opal streets. If you have a car, get the visitor centre map for the car-door routes through the surrounding fields. Finish at the bore baths, preferably when the day has cooled and the steam starts to matter.
