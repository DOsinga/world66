The Lockhart Sculpture and Heritage Trail turns the town's pastoral background into street art. More than twenty sculptures made from recycled farm materials sit around the streets and parks, many linked to the Spirit of the Land Festival and the farming stories of the district.

It is the thing that makes Lockhart more than another small Riverina service town. Pick up a map at the visitor outlet in Green's Gunyah Museum, then walk the trail slowly enough to notice the etched pavers and old verandahs along the way.
