Lockhart's water tower mural is hard to miss: a large-scale piece of public art rising above the low main street and surrounding paddocks. It was painted freehand by Scotty Nagy and Krimsone, whose work appears on other Riverina water towers as well.

The mural is a quick stop, but it gives the town a useful visual anchor. See it as part of the sculpture and heritage walk rather than as a reason to drive here by itself.
