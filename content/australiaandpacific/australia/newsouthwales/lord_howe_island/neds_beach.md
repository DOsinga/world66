Ned's Beach is the island's easiest underwater introduction. Coral and reef fish sit close enough to shore that you can wade in with a mask and be among them within minutes. The beach is also known for its fish feeding, but the better reason to come is the clear water and the chance of seeing turtles and small reef sharks without booking a boat.
