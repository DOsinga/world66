Maitland sits on the Hunter River west of [Newcastle](/australiaandpacific/australia/newsouthwales/newcastle), close enough to be pulled into the city's orbit but different in feel. It is older, flatter, and more river-minded, with a history shaped by trade, floods, farms, and the Lower Hunter's early colonial wealth.

The city has more substance than its suburban edges suggest. [Maitland Gaol](/australiaandpacific/australia/newsouthwales/maitland/maitland_gaol) is the hard-edged headline, [Walka Water Works](/australiaandpacific/australia/newsouthwales/maitland/walka_water_works) is a fine piece of industrial heritage, and [Morpeth](/australiaandpacific/australia/newsouthwales/maitland/morpeth) gives the area its most pleasant historic streetscape.

Maitland is not a rival to the wine country around [Hunter Valley](/australiaandpacific/australia/newsouthwales/hunter_valley), and it does not try to be. It is a good spare-day city for travellers who like old river towns, regional galleries, and places where the past still sits in ordinary streets.
