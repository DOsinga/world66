Maitland Regional Art Gallery is the best indoor stop in the city centre. Its exhibitions change, but the gallery usually balances serious regional art with accessible shows, workshops, and family-friendly programming.

It also helps make High Street worth a slow walk. Pair it with coffee nearby and a look at the older commercial buildings that still give central Maitland its country-city feel.
