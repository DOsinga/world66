Mount Borah is Manilla's headline attraction and the reason the town appears on many pilots' maps. The hill has established launches facing several directions, with open farmland below and thermals that make it one of Australia's serious inland paragliding and hang-gliding sites.

If you do not fly, the place is still worth understanding before you judge Manilla. This is not a casual lookout with a souvenir sign; it is working flying infrastructure, reached by unsealed roads that can close in wet weather. Go with an operator or local advice if you want to do more than watch.
