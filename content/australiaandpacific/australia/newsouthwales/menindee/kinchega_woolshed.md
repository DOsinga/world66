Kinchega Woolshed is the most tangible relic of the old station, a long, weathered building that once handled vast numbers of sheep from the surrounding runs. It is simple, dusty, and much more powerful than a polished museum would be.

The building helps explain how pastoral work reshaped this country after colonisation. Pair it with the old homestead and the river stops, because the story makes more sense when you see the land around it.
