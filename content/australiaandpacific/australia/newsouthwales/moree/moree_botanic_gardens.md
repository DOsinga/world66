Moree Botanic Gardens follow the Mehi River near the visitor information centre, linking a series of small parks and riverside plantings. The walk is easy and gives a quieter look at local trees, birdlife, and the town's river setting.

Pick up the local leaflet if available, then use the gardens as a low-key stroll before or after the artesian baths.
