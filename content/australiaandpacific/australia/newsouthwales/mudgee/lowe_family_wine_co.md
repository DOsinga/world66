Lowe Family Wine Co is one of the Mudgee cellar doors that makes the region feel like more than a casual tasting stop. It is based around a certified organic and biodynamic farm, vineyard, winery, and food gardens on Wiradjuri Country, with tastings, picnics, farm experiences, and the Zin House food operation.

Book ahead if you want more than a quick tasting. The pleasure here is in slowing down: vines, gardens, a glass of wine, and the sense that the food and cellar door belong to the same patch of ground.
