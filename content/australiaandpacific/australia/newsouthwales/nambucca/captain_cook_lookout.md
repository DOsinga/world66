Captain Cook Lookout is a coastal viewpoint above the Nambucca River estuary. It gives a broad view over the river mouth, beaches, and surrounding coastline, so it is a useful first stop for getting your bearings around Nambucca Heads.

The lookout is a simple stop rather than a long activity, but the view is worth the detour on a clear day.
