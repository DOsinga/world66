Waa Gorge is a rugged walking and picnic area in Mount Kaputar National Park. The gorge walk is short in distance but rough underfoot, with steep, unformed sections and many steps, so it suits experienced bushwalkers more than casual strollers.

The picnic area has parking, tables, and toilets. Come prepared for a remote national-park stop and allow a few hours if you plan to walk into the gorge.
