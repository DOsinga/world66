Narrandera sits where big Riverina roads meet the Murrumbidgee River, and it could easily be only a fuel stop. Give it a few hours and it becomes more interesting: river red gums, wild koalas, water recreation, and a streak of country-town eccentricity.

The strongest reason to stop is the country along the river. The [Narrandera Flora and Fauna Reserve](/australiaandpacific/australia/newsouthwales/narrandera/narrandera_flora_and_fauna_reserve) is one of the better low-effort places in New South Wales to look for wild koalas, while [Lake Talbot](/australiaandpacific/australia/newsouthwales/narrandera/lake_talbot) gives the town a summer swimming and picnic focus.

Narrandera also has a good eye for roadside theatre. The [Water Tower Art](/australiaandpacific/australia/newsouthwales/narrandera/narrandera_water_tower_art) turns a utility structure into a local landmark, and the [Big Playable Guitar](/australiaandpacific/australia/newsouthwales/narrandera/big_playable_guitar) is exactly as odd as it sounds.
