The Big Playable Guitar is a classic Australian road-trip oddity, but better than most because it actually connects to Narrandera's country music culture. It was created in 1988 and now sits at the Destination and Discovery Hub.

It will not change your life, but it is fun, quick, and memorable. That is enough for a place made to break up a long Riverina drive.
