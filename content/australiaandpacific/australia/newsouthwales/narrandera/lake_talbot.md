Lake Talbot is Narrandera's warm-weather hangout, close to the Murrumbidgee and the koala reserve. It is the place for a picnic, a swim, or a slow wander when the inland heat makes the main street feel too exposed.

Families often come for the water park, but the broader lake area is useful even outside pool season. It gives the town a proper outdoor focus rather than just a highway strip.
