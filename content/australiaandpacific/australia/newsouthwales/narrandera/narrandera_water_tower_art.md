Narrandera Water Tower Art is a good example of the mural-tower habit that has spread through inland Australia. The tower uses local flora, fauna, landmarks, and river imagery, so it feels tied to the town rather than pasted on.

It is a short stop, but a worthwhile one if you like public art or want a better view of what Narrandera thinks is important about itself.
