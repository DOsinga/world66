Hunter Wetlands Centre protects a restored wetland landscape west of central Newcastle, with boardwalks, ponds, bird hides, and seasonal wildlife. It is a good reminder that the city's industrial and beach identities sit beside a much softer estuarine environment.

The centre is best for birdwatchers, families, and anyone wanting a slower outdoor break from the coast. Bring insect repellent and check conditions after heavy rain; wetlands are at their best when you let the pace drop.
