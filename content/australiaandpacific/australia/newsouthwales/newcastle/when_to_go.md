---
title: When to Go
type: section
---

Newcastle has a warm temperate climate, with hot summers and mild winters. The best time to visit is autumn (March to May) or spring (September to November), when the temperature is pleasant for beach walking and outdoor activities without the summer crowds. Summer (December to February) is peak beach season — the city fills up at weekends, the surf is at its best, and the long evenings are good for the outdoor bars along the foreshore. Expect temperatures in the low to mid 30s Celsius on hot days.

Winter (June to August) is mild and largely dry, with occasional cold snaps when the temperature drops to single figures overnight. The sea remains warm enough for swimming through June. Fewer tourists and good surfing conditions make winter a decent time to visit if you are not beach-dependent.

The Hunter Valley hosts various food and wine festivals throughout the year, with the largest in winter — the cooler temperatures suit the food-and-wine-by-the-fire format, and Sydney visitors make the short journey in numbers.
