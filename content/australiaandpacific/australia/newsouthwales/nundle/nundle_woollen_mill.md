Nundle Woollen Mill is the village's most distinctive stop. It is not just a shop with a heritage label; the draw is the working machinery, the fibre-to-yarn process, and the chance to see a rural industry still made physical.

Tours are the best way to make sense of the place, especially when the machines are operating. Even if you do not knit, the colour, noise, and old equipment make it more memorable than a standard country-town craft shop.
