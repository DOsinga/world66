Oberon sits on the cold western side of the Blue Mountains, where the country opens into pine forest, trout streams, sheep paddocks, and high granite ridges. It is a practical little town rather than a polished resort, but the surrounding landscape gives it a stronger travel case than its size suggests.

The headline sights are outside the town centre. [Mayfield Garden](/australiaandpacific/australia/newsouthwales/oberon/mayfield_garden) is one of Australia's largest cool-climate gardens, [Kanangra-Boyd National Park](/australiaandpacific/australia/newsouthwales/oberon/kanangra_walls) has big Blue Mountains escarpment views without the Katoomba crowds, and [Jenolan Caves](/australiaandpacific/australia/newsouthwales/oberon/jenolan_caves) is one of the country's classic cave systems. Access to Jenolan has been disrupted by road damage and closures, so check conditions before building a trip around it.

Oberon works best with a car and a flexible day. Come for a garden morning, a lookout afternoon, or a slow loop through the plateau when the weather is clear and cold enough to remind you that this part of New South Wales has real seasons.
