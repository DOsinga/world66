The Parkes Radio Telescope is the town's real claim to fame: a 64-metre dish standing in open country north of town. It helped receive television signals from the Apollo 11 moon landing, and the story later became the film *The Dish*.

There is a small visitor centre and cafe, but the strongest moment is simply standing outside and seeing the dish move against the big Central West sky.
