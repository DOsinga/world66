Olsens Lookout is a roadside viewpoint in the Snowy Mountains, with a wide view across the western fall of the Main Range and the ash forests below.

It is a simple stop, but a useful one if you are driving the Alpine Way and want a short break with a stronger sense of the surrounding alpine landscape.
