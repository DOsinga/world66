Perisher Ski Resort is the headline. The appeal is scale: four resort areas, a broad lift network, beginner slopes, long cruising runs, and enough terrain to keep mixed-ability groups busy. It is expensive and very seasonal, but if you are in the Snowy Mountains for snow, this is one of the main places you are here for.
