Port Macquarie sits at the mouth of the Hastings River on the mid-north coast of New South Wales, roughly halfway between Sydney and Brisbane. It began as a penal settlement in 1821 and has grown into a popular holiday town, particularly with families and retirees drawn by the beaches and mild climate.

The town has a string of excellent beaches — [Town Beach](/australiaandpacific/australia/newsouthwales/portmacquarie/town_beach), [Flynns Beach](/australiaandpacific/australia/newsouthwales/portmacquarie/flynns_beach), and [Lighthouse Beach](/australiaandpacific/australia/newsouthwales/portmacquarie/lighthouse_beach) are all within easy reach of the centre. The coastal walk from Town Beach to [Tacking Point Lighthouse](/australiaandpacific/australia/newsouthwales/portmacquarie/tacking_point_lighthouse) is a good way to see them all, with views along the coast and chances to spot dolphins.

The [Koala Hospital](/australiaandpacific/australia/newsouthwales/portmacquarie/koala_hospital) on Lord Street is a unique draw — it cares for sick and injured koalas and is open to visitors daily, with a free guided tour at 3pm. The [Billabong Zoo](/australiaandpacific/australia/newsouthwales/portmacquarie/billabong_zoo) and the [Sea Acres Rainforest Centre](/australiaandpacific/australia/newsouthwales/portmacquarie/sea_acres_rainforest_centre) (a boardwalk through coastal rainforest) are also worth a visit.
