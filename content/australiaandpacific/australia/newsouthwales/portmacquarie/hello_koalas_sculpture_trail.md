Hello Koalas Sculpture Trail turns Port Macquarie's koala identity into a cheerful public-art hunt. Painted koala sculptures appear around town and nearby communities, giving families and casual walkers a reason to explore beyond the waterfront.

It is not a conventional museum or wildlife encounter, but it fits the town well. Pick up a map or use the trail information online, then treat it as a relaxed way to connect the Koala Hospital, town centre, and local parks.
