Port Macquarie's historic graveyard was the main burial ground for early European settlers and remains a quiet place to read the town's colonial history through its headstones. It backs onto Koolonbung Creek Nature Reserve, so a visit can be combined with the boardwalks through the mangroves and wetlands behind the cemetery.
