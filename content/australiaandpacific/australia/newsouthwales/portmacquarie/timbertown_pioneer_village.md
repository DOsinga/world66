Timbertown Pioneer Village, inland from Port Macquarie at Wauchope, recreates the timber settlements that shaped this part of the Mid North Coast. Historic-style buildings, machinery, and demonstrations give context to a region that can otherwise look purely beach-focused to visitors.

It is best approached as a family-friendly heritage stop rather than a major museum. Check current opening and demonstration schedules before making the drive from Port Macquarie, as the live elements matter.
