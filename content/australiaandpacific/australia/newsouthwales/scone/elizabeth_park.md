Elizabeth Park is a small central stop rather than a major sight, but the Mare and Foal sculpture makes it a useful marker of Scone's horse identity. It is the kind of place you pause while walking the main street, not a destination to build a day around.

That modesty is part of the charm. In Scone, the horse culture is not confined to one attraction; it is spread through studs, events, streets, and public symbols like this one.
