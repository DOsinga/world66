Hunter Warbirds is Scone's strongest indoor attraction, an aviation museum at Scone Memorial Airport. The collection focuses on historic military aircraft, with exhibits that work for both aviation enthusiasts and travellers who simply want something more memorable than another country-town museum.

It also helps explain modern Scone. The visitor information centre is here, the airport is active, and the museum gives the town a second identity beyond horses.
