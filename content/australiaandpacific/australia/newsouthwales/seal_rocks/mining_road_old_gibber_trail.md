The Mining Road to Old Gibber Road Cycling Trail is a long bush ride through coastal forest and swamp country inland from Seal Rocks. The full route is about 20 km one way, so it is more of a half-day outing than a quick spin.

The appeal is the quiet landscape rather than a single viewpoint. Bring water, check track conditions after rain, and expect a remote-feeling ride once you leave the beach roads behind.
