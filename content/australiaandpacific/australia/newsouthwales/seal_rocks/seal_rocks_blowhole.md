The blowhole sits near the route between the village and the lighthouse. In calm weather it can be quiet, but during bigger seas the rock shelf throws spray and noise into the air.

Watch it from a sensible distance. The same swell that makes it impressive can make the rocks dangerous, especially when waves arrive in sets.
