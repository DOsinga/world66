The Treachery Headland Walking Track is an easy short walk at the southern end of Seal Rocks. It passes through coastal forest before opening onto grass and views back toward Sugarloaf Point.

It is a good choice when you want more than another beach stop but not a full hike. A side track drops toward Treachery Beach, so bring shoes that can handle sand as well as path.
