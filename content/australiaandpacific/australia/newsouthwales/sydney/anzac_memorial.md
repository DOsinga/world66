The ANZAC Memorial in Hyde Park commemorates people from New South Wales who served in war. The building is both a memorial and a small museum, with displays that give context to Australian military service and remembrance.

It works well as a short, serious stop while walking through central Sydney, especially if you are already visiting Hyde Park or nearby St Mary's Cathedral.
