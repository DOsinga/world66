Balls Head Reserve is a bushy harbour headland west of the Sydney Harbour Bridge, with walking tracks, picnic spots, and open views across the water to the city. The reserve also includes the Coal Loader Centre for Sustainability, built around a former industrial site.

Come for an easy harbour walk away from the main sightseeing crowds. The lookout points are the main draw, especially on clear afternoons.
