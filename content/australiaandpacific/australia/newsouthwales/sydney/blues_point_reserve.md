Blues Point Reserve is a small waterfront lawn on the north side of Sydney Harbour, directly facing the Harbour Bridge and the city skyline. It is simple as parks go, but the view is one of Sydney's cleanest postcard angles.

Come near sunset or for a short break after walking around McMahons Point. On New Year's Eve it becomes one of the popular harbour fireworks viewpoints, so expect crowds and early access controls.
