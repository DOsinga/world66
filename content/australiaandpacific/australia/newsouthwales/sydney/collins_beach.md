Collins Beach is a small, sheltered beach near Manly with shallow water and a pocket of bushland around it. After rain, a little waterfall can run over the rocks, giving the cove a surprisingly secluded feel for metropolitan Sydney.

It is best reached on foot if you are already exploring Manly, as parking nearby can be awkward on busy days.
