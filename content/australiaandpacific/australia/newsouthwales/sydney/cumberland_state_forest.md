Cumberland State Forest is a pocket of eucalypt forest in Sydney's north-west, with short walking trails, picnic areas, a forest information centre, and a native plant nursery. It is not wild bushland, but it is a useful change of pace if you are staying around the Hills District and want an easy walk under the trees.
