Customs House is a historic public building beside Circular Quay, imposing enough to show how important the harbour once was to Sydney's trade. Inside, the large floor model of the city is the detail most visitors remember.

It is an easy stop while moving between the quay, The Rocks, and the Royal Botanic Garden. Step inside rather than only passing the facade; the interior gives the building more value than a quick street view.
