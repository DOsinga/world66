Customs House is a handsome 19th-century building beside Circular Quay, once the official gateway for goods and people entering Sydney by sea. The harbour has moved around it, but the sandstone facade still gives the quay a useful bit of civic weight.

Step inside for the public atrium and the large scale model of central Sydney set into the floor. It is a quick visit, but an easy one to pair with Circular Quay, the Museum of Sydney, and the walk into The Rocks.
