Dalrymple-Hay Nature Reserve protects one of the last patches of blue gum forest that once covered much of northern Sydney. It is a quiet stop for walkers, birdwatchers, and anyone interested in Australian botany rather than a big-ticket attraction.

Look for rainbow lorikeets, king parrots, silvereyes, and the tall forest canopy that makes the reserve feel older and less urban than its suburban setting suggests.
