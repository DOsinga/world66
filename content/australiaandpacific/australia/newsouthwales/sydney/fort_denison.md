Fort Denison sits on a small island in Sydney Harbour, close to the Opera House and Harbour Bridge. It is one of Australia's best-known harbour forts, and its position gives a sharp sense of how Sydney's harbour was defended.

The heritage tour is the reason to go, combining the fort's military history with wide views across the harbour.
