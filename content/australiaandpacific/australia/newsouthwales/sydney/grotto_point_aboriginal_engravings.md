The Grotto Point Aboriginal engraving site preserves rock art on the harbour side of Sydney's northern foreshore. The engravings include human figures, marine life, boomerangs, and land animals, and are a worthwhile stop when walking through the bushland around Dobroyd Head and Middle Harbour.
