Grotto Point Lighthouse is a small early-20th-century lighthouse on Sydney Harbour, reached by a side track near the Manly to Spit Bridge walk. Its squat white towers look almost playful, but the setting is the real draw: open harbour views, bushland around the headland, and a quieter feel than the main waterfront viewpoints.
