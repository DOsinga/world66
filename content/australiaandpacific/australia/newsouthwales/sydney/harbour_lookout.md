Harbour Lookout is one of the North Head viewpoints above Manly, facing back across Sydney Harbour toward the city skyline. On a clear day you can pick out the Harbour Bridge, the Opera House, and the harbour islands.

It is best treated as part of a North Head walk rather than a standalone trip. The views complement the nearby coastal and military lookouts, with the focus here on the sweep of the harbour rather than the open Pacific.
