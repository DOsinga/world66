Kamay Botany Bay National Park protects the headlands around the entrance to Botany Bay, with coastal walks, ocean views, heathland, and important Aboriginal and European history. Around La Perouse, the visitor centre and museum explain the area's links to the Kamay people, Cook's 1770 landing, and French explorer Jean-Francois de Galaup, comte de Laperouse.
