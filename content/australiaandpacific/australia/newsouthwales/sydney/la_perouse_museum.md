La Perouse Museum looks across Botany Bay from a site tied to both Aboriginal history and French exploration. Its collection mixes Aboriginal artefacts with maps, scientific instruments, and relics linked to the French expedition of Jean-François de Galaup, comte de La Pérouse.

The museum pairs well with the short coastal walk toward Endeavour Lighthouse, where the bay views help make sense of why this headland mattered.
