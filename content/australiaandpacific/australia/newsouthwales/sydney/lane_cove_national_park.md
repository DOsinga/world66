Lane Cove National Park protects a green stretch of bushland along the Lane Cove River, close enough to central Sydney to feel surprisingly wild. The Great North Walk passes through the park, and the popular section between Lane Cove Road and Delhi Road has riverside walking tracks, picnic areas, barbecue spots, and places to rent boats for a quiet paddle.
