Luna Park is Sydney's heritage amusement park, set beside the harbour at Milsons Point. Its giant smiling entrance face and old fairground setting make it one of the city's most recognizable family attractions.

The rides suit older children and teenagers better than very young kids, but the location also makes it worthwhile for the harbour views and the ferry or train trip across from the central city.
