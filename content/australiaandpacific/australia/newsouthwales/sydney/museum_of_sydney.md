Museum of Sydney stands on the site of the first Government House, built in 1788 and demolished in 1846. Glass floor panels reveal parts of the excavated foundations, while exhibitions use objects, film, photography, and digital displays to explore colonial Sydney, Aboriginal culture, trade, law, and everyday life.
