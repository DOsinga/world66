The National Art School Gallery sits inside the former Darlinghurst Gaol, a sandstone prison precinct from the 1840s. The setting is a large part of the appeal: exhibitions occupy historic buildings now used by one of Australia's major art schools.

Shows usually focus on contemporary art, student work, and Australian artists. It is a good pairing with the nearby Australian Museum or a walk through Darlinghurst.
