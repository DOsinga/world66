The National Trust Centre occupies one of The Rocks' historic institutional buildings, once used as a military hospital and state school. It now houses the National Trust NSW headquarters and the SH Ervin Gallery, which mounts changing exhibitions.

It is a small stop rather than a headline Sydney sight, but it fits well into a walk through The Rocks, Barangaroo, and Observatory Hill, especially if an exhibition is running.
