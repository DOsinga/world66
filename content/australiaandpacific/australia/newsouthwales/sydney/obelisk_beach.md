Obelisk Beach is a small, rocky cove on the north side of Sydney Harbour, below Middle Head. It is known as a clothing-optional swimming spot, with bush and harbour views giving it a tucked-away feel despite being inside the city.

There are no big beach facilities here. Come for a quiet swim, bring what you need, and be aware that the beach's nude tradition is part of its identity.
