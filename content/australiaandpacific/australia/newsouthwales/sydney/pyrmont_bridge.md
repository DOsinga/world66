Pyrmont Bridge is a historic swing bridge across Darling Harbour, now used by pedestrians rather than road traffic. Its central span can still open for vessels, a reminder of the harbour's working past.

Most visitors cross it simply while moving between the city side of Darling Harbour and Pyrmont. It is worth slowing down for the views over the harbour and for the engineering detail of the old swing span.
