The RAN Heritage Centre is the Royal Australian Navy's main museum, at the northern end of Garden Island. Its collection covers naval history through artefacts, models, photographs, and displays tied to the working base around it.

The museum is modest, but the hill behind it gives a strong view over the naval base and back toward the Sydney CBD.
