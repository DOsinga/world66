Shelly Headland Upper Lookout is a small viewpoint above Shelly Beach near Manly. The view takes in Shelly Beach, Manly Beach, and the headland coastline, making it an easy add-on to a beach walk.

The lookout is reached on foot and is not wheelchair accessible.
