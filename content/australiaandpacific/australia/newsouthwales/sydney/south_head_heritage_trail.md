South Head Heritage Trail is a short loop walk near the entrance to Sydney Harbour. The route passes former lightkeepers' cottages, lighthouse views, and old gun batteries used during the Second World War.

At only a few hundred metres, it is more of a scenic heritage loop than a hike. The reward is the mix of harbour cliffs, military remains, and open views across the water.
