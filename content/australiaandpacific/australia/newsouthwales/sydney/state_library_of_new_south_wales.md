The State Library of New South Wales is both a working reference library and a public cultural stop near the Domain. Visitors can see the historic Mitchell Reading Room, browse changing exhibitions, and use the cafe and bookshop.

The library is most rewarding for its architecture and exhibitions rather than for casual research. It pairs well with the Royal Botanic Garden, the Art Gallery of New South Wales, and a walk through the eastern edge of the CBD.
