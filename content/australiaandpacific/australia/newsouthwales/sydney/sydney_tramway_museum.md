Sydney Tramway Museum at Loftus preserves the city's tram history with restored Sydney cars, interstate trams, and overseas examples. The best part is that this is not just a static shed: heritage trams run on operating track, giving the collection some life and sound.

It is a good stop for transport enthusiasts and families heading toward the Royal National Park or southern Sydney. Check running days before going, as the museum is most rewarding when rides are operating.
