A small harbour village at the eastern tip of the South Head peninsula, reachable by ferry from Circular Quay or by bus. Doyle's on the Beach has been serving fish and chips since 1885 — go for the outdoor tables on the water. The walk along South Head itself gives dramatic views over the Pacific and back to the city, and the lighthouse at the tip dates from 1858. The harbour beach is good for a swim. The ferry ride back through the harbour at sunset is hard to beat.
