Wendy Whiteley's Secret Garden fills a patch of former railway land above Lavender Bay with dense planting, winding paths, and harbour glimpses. Created by Wendy Whiteley, widow of Australian artist Brett Whiteley, it feels informal and personal rather than manicured.
