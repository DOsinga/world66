Clarks Point Reserve gives broad views across Sydney Harbour from the Woolwich side, with the bridge and city skyline in the distance. It is a relaxed local alternative to the better-known inner-harbour viewpoints.

The reserve has picnic space by the water, and Woolwich Lookout above it is worth the short climb for the wider panorama. It is best in clear weather, when the harbour, ferries, and city towers are all visible at once.
