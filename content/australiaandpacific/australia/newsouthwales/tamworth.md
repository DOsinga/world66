Tamworth is the big inland city of northern New South Wales' New England country, and it has one identity that travels far beyond the region: country music. For most of the year it is a practical regional centre on the Peel River. In January, during the Tamworth Country Music Festival, it becomes Australia's main stage for country, folk, buskers, awards nights, and people in wide-brimmed hats.

The country-music side is not just a festival poster. The [Big Golden Guitar](/australiaandpacific/australia/newsouthwales/tamworth/big_golden_guitar) marks the southern approach to town, while the [Australian Country Music Hall of Fame](/australiaandpacific/australia/newsouthwales/tamworth/australian_country_music_hall_of_fame) gives the genre some depth beyond the roadside photo stop.

Tamworth also has a second, quieter claim: in 1888 it became the first town in Australia to light its streets with municipal electric street lighting. The [Tamworth Powerstation Museum](/australiaandpacific/australia/newsouthwales/tamworth/tamworth_powerstation_museum) explains that story. Add the [Marsupial Park](/australiaandpacific/australia/newsouthwales/tamworth/tamworth_marsupial_park) and [Oxley Scenic Lookout](/australiaandpacific/australia/newsouthwales/tamworth/oxley_scenic_lookout), and the city clears the bar for a spare day if you are already travelling inland.
