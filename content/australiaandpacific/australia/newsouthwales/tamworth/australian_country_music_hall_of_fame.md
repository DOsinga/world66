The Australian Country Music Hall of Fame is where Tamworth's big claim gets some substance. The museum follows performers and songs through stage outfits, instruments, handmade objects, photographs, and changing displays about the people who shaped Australian country music.

It is most rewarding if you already know a few names, but even casual visitors will come away understanding why Tamworth's January festival matters. Pair it with the Big Golden Guitar rather than treating the guitar as the whole story.
