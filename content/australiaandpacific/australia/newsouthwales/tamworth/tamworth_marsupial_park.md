Tamworth Marsupial Park is an easy win for families and anyone who wants a break from roads and shops. The 14-hectare park has free entry, native animals, a free-flight aviary, picnic spots, and a large adventure playground.

It sits on the edge of the hill country above town, so it also combines well with Oxley Scenic Lookout and the Kamilaroi Walking Track if you want a little fresh air without leaving Tamworth.
