Tamworth Regional Botanic Garden covers native plantings, ponds, and landscaped garden areas on the edge of town. Paths lead through the grounds and up to a lookout with wide views over the surrounding agricultural country.

It sits close to Tamworth Marsupial Park, so the two make an easy low-key outdoor stop.
