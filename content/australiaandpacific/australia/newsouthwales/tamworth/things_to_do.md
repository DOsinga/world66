type: section
---

Tamworth's essential stops split neatly between music, local history, and views. The country-music trail starts with the [Big Golden Guitar](/australiaandpacific/australia/newsouthwales/tamworth/big_golden_guitar) and continues at the [Australian Country Music Hall of Fame](/australiaandpacific/australia/newsouthwales/tamworth/australian_country_music_hall_of_fame). For something more local and less expected, visit the [Tamworth Powerstation Museum](/australiaandpacific/australia/newsouthwales/tamworth/tamworth_powerstation_museum), then head up to [Oxley Scenic Lookout](/australiaandpacific/australia/newsouthwales/tamworth/oxley_scenic_lookout) or take children to [Tamworth Marsupial Park](/australiaandpacific/australia/newsouthwales/tamworth/tamworth_marsupial_park).
