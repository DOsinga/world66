Kianinny Bay is a small, protected inlet at Tathra's southern edge. It is more tucked away than the main beach, with boat-launch access, rocks, picnic space, and calmer water when the open coast is rough.

It also marks the northern approach to Bournda National Park, so it works well as a quick stop before a coastal walk or a longer explore south of town.
