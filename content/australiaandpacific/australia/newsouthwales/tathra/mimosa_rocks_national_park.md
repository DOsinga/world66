Mimosa Rocks National Park begins near the northern end of Tathra Beach and runs up the coast toward Bermagui. Its appeal is simple: quiet beaches, rocky coves, lagoons, heath, and headlands with sea views.

The park is best with a car, as access points are spread along the Tathra-Bermagui Road. Pick one or two stops rather than trying to cover the whole coast in a rush.
