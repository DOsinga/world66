Tenterfield is a high-country town on the New England Tablelands, close to the Queensland border and surrounded by granite, waterfalls, and bushranger country. It is also tied to Australia's Federation story: Sir Henry Parkes delivered his 1889 Tenterfield Oration here, helping push the colonies toward union.

The strict test is easy here because the town is not relying on one museum. [Bald Rock National Park](/australiaandpacific/australia/newsouthwales/tenterfield/bald_rock_national_park) is the headline, with one of Australia's great granite domes. [Boonoo Boonoo National Park](/australiaandpacific/australia/newsouthwales/tenterfield/boonoo_boonoo_national_park) adds waterfalls, swimming holes, and forest. In town, the [Tenterfield Railway Museum](/australiaandpacific/australia/newsouthwales/tenterfield/tenterfield_railway_museum) keeps the old Great Northern line visible, and the School of Arts gives the place its national-history weight.

Tenterfield is best with a car and a full day. Spend the morning on granite, the afternoon by water or in the railway precinct, and the evening in a town that still feels like a borderland rather than a highway service stop.
