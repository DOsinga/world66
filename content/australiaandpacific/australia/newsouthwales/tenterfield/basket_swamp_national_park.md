Basket Swamp National Park is a quiet upland park east of Tenterfield, with horse-riding trails, picnic spots at Timbarra Falls and Woollool Woolloolni Aboriginal Area, and a walking trail to Timbarra Lookout.

Facilities are limited, so it suits self-sufficient visitors looking for a low-key nature stop. Woollool Woolloolni is culturally significant, so treat the area with care.
