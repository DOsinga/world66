Centenary Cottage Museum is a small local history complex in Tenterfield, built around a preserved workers' cottage and several displays of pioneer-era machinery and domestic life. The site also includes the General Sir Harry Chauvel Gallery and the Petrie Pioneer Cottage.

It works best as a compact stop for context on Tenterfield's rural and colonial history, especially when paired with the railway museum and a walk through the town's older streets.
