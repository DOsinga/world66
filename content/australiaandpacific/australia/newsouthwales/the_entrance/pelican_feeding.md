Every day at 3:30pm, a crowd gathers at Memorial Park on the waterfront at The Entrance — and a squadron of pelicans gathers with them. Volunteers from the local Pelican Feeding Society bring in buckets of fish, and the birds waddle in from the lake with the confidence of regulars who know the routine. It is one of the more endearing wildlife encounters you will find anywhere on the New South Wales coast, and it costs nothing.

Australian pelicans are the largest in the world, with wingspans reaching two and a half metres. Up close they are impressively ungainly on land and almost impossibly graceful in the air. The feeding draws a mix of locals and visitors, and the whole event takes about twenty minutes. Arrive early for a good spot on the foreshore.
