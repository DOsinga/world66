---
title: Beaches
type: section
---

Thirty-seven kilometres of largely undeveloped coast runs south from the Queensland border, ranging from high-energy surf breaks to sheltered family swimming spots. The northern beaches adjoin the Gold Coast's twin-town strip at Coolangatta; south of there the shire's coastline becomes progressively quieter. Kingscliff, Pottsville, and Cabarita are the main coastal townships — all small, all retaining the feel of a working beach town rather than a resort.
