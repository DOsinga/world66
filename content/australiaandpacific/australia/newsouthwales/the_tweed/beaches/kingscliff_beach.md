Kingscliff is the Tweed's most liveable coastal town — relaxed enough to feel genuine, serviced enough to be comfortable. The beach is patrolled year-round, the surf is milder than the northern breaks, and the main street has good cafes and a fresh fish co-op. The town exploded in popularity during the 2020s as Gold Coast residents sought quieter alternatives, but it has so far maintained a reasonable balance. The Cabarita headland a short drive to the south frames a beautiful section of coast and is worth the extra kilometre.
