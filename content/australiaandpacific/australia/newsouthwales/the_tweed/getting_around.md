---
title: Getting Around
type: section
---

**Hire a car.** There is no practical alternative for exploring the Tweed Shire. The coastal towns, the hinterland villages, and the national parks are spread across a large area, and public transport connects only the major coastal settlements. Gold Coast Airport has all major hire car companies, and rates are generally lower there than at Sydney or Brisbane airports.

**Local buses:** NSW TrainLink and local bus services connect Tweed Heads, Kingscliff, and Murwillumbah on limited schedules. The Gold Coast light rail (G:link) reaches Coolangatta — useful for day trips north to Surfers Paradise without a car, but not helpful for accessing the Tweed hinterland.

**Driving times from Tweed Heads:** Kingscliff 15 minutes south; Murwillumbah 35 minutes inland; the Wollumbin–Mount Warning National Park car park 50 minutes; Tyalgum 45 minutes; Byron Bay 45 minutes south.

**Cycling:** The coastal strip between Tweed Heads and Kingscliff is flat and has a reasonable shared path. Hinterland roads are challenging due to hills and narrow lanes, but scenic for experienced cyclists.
