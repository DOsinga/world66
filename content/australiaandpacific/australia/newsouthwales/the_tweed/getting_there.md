---
title: Getting There
type: section
---

**By air:** Gold Coast Airport (OOL) sits two minutes north of the NSW border at Coolangatta. It handles direct flights from Sydney (1 hour), Melbourne (1.5 hours), and several other Australian cities, as well as international services to New Zealand and Asia. The airport is significantly closer to Tweed Heads than to the Gold Coast tourist strip — most visitors underestimate how convenient it is for the Tweed. A taxi or rideshare from the airport to Tweed Heads or Kingscliff takes around 10–15 minutes.

**By road from Brisbane:** The Pacific Motorway (M1) runs directly south through the Gold Coast and crosses into NSW at Tweed Heads. From central Brisbane the drive takes around 90 minutes without traffic; allow longer during Gold Coast school holidays. Byron Bay is a further 40 minutes south on the same highway.

**By road from Sydney:** 770 kilometres north via the Pacific Highway. The drive takes around 9–10 hours; most travellers break it at Coffs Harbour or Port Macquarie. The inland route via the New England Highway adds scenery but similar time.

**By coach:** Greyhound and Firefly run services on the Sydney–Brisbane coastal route, stopping at Tweed Heads. Journey times from Sydney are 12–14 hours. From Brisbane the coach takes around 2 hours to Tweed Heads.
