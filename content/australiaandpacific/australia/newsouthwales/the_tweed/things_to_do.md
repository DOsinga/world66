type: section
---

The Tweed packs an unusual range of experiences into a compact area. At its centre is Wollumbin (Mount Warning), the eroded plug of the world's largest extinct shield volcano, rising dramatically from the valley floor. Along the coast, surf beaches stretch virtually unbroken for 37 kilometres, from Duranbah in the north to beyond Kingscliff in the south. The hinterland rewards anyone who ventures inland: rainforest walks, artists' villages, a world-class regional gallery, and the kind of rural quiet that the Gold Coast stripped away decades ago.
