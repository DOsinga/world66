Cook Island sits just 800 metres offshore from Fingal Head and protects one of the most accessible dive sites on the northern New South Wales coast. The aquatic reserve surrounding it shelters a healthy reef with grey nurse sharks (listed as critically endangered in eastern Australia), loggerhead turtles, manta rays, and large schools of baitfish. The island itself is a nature reserve — landing is restricted — but the surrounding water is open to snorkellers and divers. Local operators run guided dives from Tweed Heads. The best visibility occurs in winter and spring when the water is clearest, though the grey nurse aggregation peaks in late summer.
