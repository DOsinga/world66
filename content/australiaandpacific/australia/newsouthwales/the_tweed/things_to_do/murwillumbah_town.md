The shire's main town sits on the western bank of the Tweed River with Wollumbin framing the skyline to the south-west. The river front and the heritage streetscape of Murwillumbah — art deco civic buildings, wide verandahed shopfronts — give it the unhurried feel of a regional town that has kept its character intact while the coast commercialised around it. The old railway station (the heritage-listed terminus of the Murwillumbah branch line, which closed to passengers in 2004) has been converted into a visitor centre and arts hub. The weekly Murwillumbah Farmers Market draws producers from across the hinterland every Saturday morning.
