The Tweed Range forms the western arc of the ancient caldera rim, its ridgelines linking into the Border Ranges and Nightcap national parks — all listed under the Gondwana Rainforests of Australia World Heritage Area. The walks here are among the finest subtropical rainforest experiences on the east coast. The Pinnacle Lookout on the Tweed Range Scenic Drive delivers views across the entire valley to Wollumbin that justify the drive alone. The rainforest imagery used to promote the wider Northern Rivers region is overwhelmingly photographed in these parks, not Byron Bay. Wildlife watching is excellent: platypus in the creek pools, carpet pythons on warm afternoons, and bowerbirds doing their improbable decorating in the forest understorey.
