The Tweed Regional Gallery is an unexpected cultural landmark in the heart of Murwillumbah. Its permanent collection is strong, but the real draw is the Margaret Olley Art Centre — a wing devoted to the beloved Sydney still-life painter and Tweed locals' favourite. Olley's Sydney studio has been recreated here with extraordinary fidelity: the chaotic stacks of props, the painted doors, the accumulated objects she returned to for decades. It is one of the most intimate artist's studio recreations in Australia. The gallery also administers the Doug Moran National Portrait Prize, one of the country's richest art awards, and the prize exhibition draws visitors from well beyond the shire. Admission to the gallery is free; a small fee applies for the Olley Centre.
