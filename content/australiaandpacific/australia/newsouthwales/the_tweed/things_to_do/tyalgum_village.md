Tyalgum is the prettiest of the Tweed hinterland villages: a handful of timber buildings around a general store, a weekly market, and an overwhelming sense of green. The surrounding valley — enclosed on three sides by the remnant rim of the Tweed caldera — holds small farms, rainforest pockets, and walking tracks into the Nightcap and Border Ranges national parks. The village hosts the Tyalgum Music Festival each July, a chamber music program that brings surprisingly high-calibre performers to this very small place. From Murwillumbah it's a 30-minute drive through sugarcane and subtropical forest.
