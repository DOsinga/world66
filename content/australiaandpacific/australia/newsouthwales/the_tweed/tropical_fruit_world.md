Tropical Fruit World is a working fruit farm and visitor attraction in the Tweed hinterland, growing a large range of tropical and subtropical fruit. Tours focus on tasting, orchard rides, and seeing how the fruit is grown.

It is best treated as a family-friendly stop rather than a serious botanical garden. The giant avocado out front sets the tone: a little kitsch, but memorable if you are exploring the coast and hinterland around the Tweed.
