Tweed Regional Museum in Murwillumbah tells the story of the Tweed through local objects, photographs, and changing exhibitions. Its collection covers Aboriginal history, farming, town life, floods, and the region's relationship with the coast and hinterland.

It is a useful indoor stop before or after exploring the Tweed's villages, beaches, and volcanic landscape.
