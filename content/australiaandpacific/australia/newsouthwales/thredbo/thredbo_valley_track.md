The Thredbo Valley Track is the resort's summer backbone: a mountain-bike and walking route that follows the Thredbo River through Kosciuszko National Park. It can be taken in short family-friendly sections or as a much longer one-way ride with shuttle logistics.

The appeal is not a single viewpoint but the flow of the valley itself: snow gums, granite, river crossings and long downhill stretches away from the lifts. It is one of the few Snowy Mountains activities that feels built for summer rather than borrowed from winter.
