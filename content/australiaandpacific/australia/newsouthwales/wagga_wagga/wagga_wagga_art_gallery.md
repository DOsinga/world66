Wagga Wagga Art Gallery is the city's strongest indoor sight. Its National Art Glass Collection is a nationally significant collection of contemporary Australian glass, shown in a purpose-built gallery beside the main civic centre.

The broader gallery also shows prints, regional work, and visiting exhibitions. Even if glass art is not usually your thing, this is the place in Wagga most likely to surprise you.
