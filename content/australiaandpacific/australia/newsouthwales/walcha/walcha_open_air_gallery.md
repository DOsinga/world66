Walcha's Open Air Gallery is a scattered public-art trail rather than a single fenced attraction. More than 60 works sit around town, many made from local timber or placed where you meet them casually: outside civic buildings, in parks, on road corners.

That informality is its charm. Pick up or download a map, then walk a loose circuit through town before driving out to the waterfalls.
