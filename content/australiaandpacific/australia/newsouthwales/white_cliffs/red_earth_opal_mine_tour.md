An underground opal mine tour is the most direct way to make sense of White Cliffs. Red Earth Opal takes visitors below the surface into the kind of small-scale workings that explain both the town's economy and its dugout culture.

The appeal is not just seeing opal. It is feeling the temperature drop underground and understanding why, in this part of the outback, living and working below the surface can be practical rather than eccentric.
