---
title: Things to Do
type: section
---

White Cliffs is about opal, heat, and adaptation. The best things to do are simple: walk or drive the heritage trail, look across the mine fields, join an underground opal tour if one is running, and visit the old solar power station that once made the town an unlikely renewable-energy experiment.

The wider country matters too. Paroo-Darling National Park is remote and road conditions can change, but its wetlands and birdlife give White Cliffs a second landscape beyond the opal fields.
