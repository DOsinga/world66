The White Cliffs Heritage Trail is the simplest way to understand the town. It links mining sites, dugout country, old town stories, and traces of the improvised life that grew around opal after the field was discovered in the late 19th century.

Do it slowly and with water. The trail is less about a single landmark than the way the town sits between low hills, spoil heaps, shafts, and homes built into the ground.
