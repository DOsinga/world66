The Old Great North Road is the headline sight at Wisemans Ferry. The Devines Hill and Finchs Line loop climbs from near the river into Dharug National Park, following a road cut and walled by convicts in the 1820s and 1830s.

The stonework is still impressive: buttresses, culverts, quarry faces, and retaining walls built in hard country with hand tools and forced labour. The full loop is a serious half-day walk, exposed in hot weather, so carry water and start early in summer.
