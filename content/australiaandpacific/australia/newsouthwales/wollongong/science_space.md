Science Space is Wollongong's hands-on science museum, with interactive exhibits aimed at children and families. The displays cover light, motion, engineering, and the natural world in a format built for touching and trying things out.

Its planetarium is the main reason to plan around a session time. It works best as a half-day family stop, especially in poor beach weather.
