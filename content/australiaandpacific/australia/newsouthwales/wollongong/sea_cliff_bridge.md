The Sea Cliff Bridge is one of Australia's most scenic drives and walks — a 665-metre cantilevered structure that hugs the Pacific coastline between Clifton and Coalcliff, about 20 kilometres north of Wollongong. The bridge was completed in 2005 and replaced a stretch of coastal road that repeatedly succumbed to cliff erosion.

You can drive across it in a couple of minutes or park at either end and walk the dedicated pedestrian path alongside. The views from the path are exceptional: the Tasman Sea below, the escarpment rising to your left, and — if you time it right — humpback whales migrating along the coast. The surrounding area, including the Bald Hill lookout and the Royal National Park just north, makes this a natural half-day trip from Wollongong.
