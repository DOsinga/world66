St Francis Xavier Cathedral is Wollongong's main Catholic church, a Gothic Revival landmark close to the city centre and the waterfront. Its stonework, pointed arches, and calm interior give a different rhythm to a city often experienced through beaches, lookouts, and the escarpment.

The cathedral is worth a short visit if you are walking between central Wollongong and the harbour. It is still an active church, so be respectful of services and quiet times. For architecture-minded travellers, it adds a useful colonial and religious layer to the Illawarra story.
