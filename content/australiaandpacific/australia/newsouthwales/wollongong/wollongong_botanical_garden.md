Wollongong Botanic Garden sits below Mount Keira and gives the city a calm green space away from the beach strip. Its collections include rainforest plantings, open lawns, native gardens, and walking paths that suit a slow hour or two.

It is a practical stop for families, garden-minded visitors, or anyone combining the university side of Wollongong with the escarpment. The garden is at its best when used as a relaxed picnic and walking stop rather than a formal showpiece.
