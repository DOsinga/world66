Young's sights are modest but specific. The [Young Historical Museum](/australiaandpacific/australia/newsouthwales/young/young_historical_museum) and [Lambing Flat Chinese Tribute Gardens](/australiaandpacific/australia/newsouthwales/young/lambing_flat_chinese_tribute_gardens) tell the goldfield and Chinese-Australian story, while orchards and cellar doors such as [Ballinaclash](/australiaandpacific/australia/newsouthwales/young/ballinaclash_fruit_and_wine) explain why the town still feels tied to fruit country.
