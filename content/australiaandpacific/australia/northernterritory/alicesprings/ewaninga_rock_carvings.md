Ewaninga Rock Carvings are a protected group of Aboriginal petroglyphs south of Alice Springs. The carvings sit among low sandstone outcrops and are the main reason to visit Ewaninga Conservation Reserve.

The site is quiet and exposed, with a short walking loop past the carvings. Visit with respect: stay on the marked path, do not touch the rock art, and bring water, sun protection, and your own transport.
