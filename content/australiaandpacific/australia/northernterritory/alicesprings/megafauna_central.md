Megafauna Central is a small fossil museum in central Alice Springs, focused on the giant animals that once lived around the Alcoota fossil beds. Exhibits introduce the extinct marsupials, birds, and reptiles that shaped Central Australia's prehistoric landscape.

It is compact, but it adds useful context before or after visiting the region's desert parks and natural history collections. Allow about an hour.
