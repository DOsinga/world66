The RFDS Alice Springs Tourist Facility explains how the Royal Flying Doctor Service made medical care possible across the vast distances of central Australia. Exhibits cover radio medicine, remote airstrips, and the people who built the service, with a life-size hologram of founder John Flynn.

It is a strong Alice Springs stop because the story belongs to the landscape around the town. After days of driving through the outback, the distances on the maps here feel very real.
