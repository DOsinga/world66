The National Road Transport Hall of Fame is one of Alice Springs' most appropriate museums: a
tribute to the trucks, road trains, mechanics, and drivers that made inland Australia move. Its
collection of vehicles and memorabilia is large, practical, and deeply tied to the distances around
the town.

Even visitors who are not vehicle enthusiasts can come away with a better feel for the outback
logistics that shape Central Australia. The museum helps explain why Alice Springs is a service hub,
not just a stop on the way to Uluru or the MacDonnell Ranges.
