The Residency is a 1920s government house in central Alice Springs, built for the official representative to Central Australia. For decades it was where visiting officials, dignitaries, and local administrators met, so the rooms tell a quieter story of how the town was governed from the desert interior.

It is a modest museum, but a worthwhile one if you are trying to understand Alice Springs beyond its shops and tour departures. Volunteers often add the detail that makes the place come alive.
