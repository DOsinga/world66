The Aviation Heritage Centre is Darwin's main aviation museum, with large aircraft and displays that connect flight to the city's wartime and northern-Australia history. The standout scale of the aircraft makes it more engaging than a small object collection.

It is a worthwhile stop if you are interested in the bombing of Darwin, long-distance aviation, or the logistics of the Top End. The museum is outside the central waterfront, so plan transport rather than leaving it to chance.
