Burnett House is one of Darwin's best small heritage stops, part of the Myilly Point precinct of elevated tropical houses. Built in the 1930s, it shows how architecture worked with heat, storms, shade, and airflow before sealed interiors and air-conditioning changed the city's domestic life.

The house is modest compared with southern Australian mansions, but that is exactly why it matters. Its verandas, louvers, and garden setting tell a specifically Darwin story: practical, exposed, and shaped by climate as much as by style.
