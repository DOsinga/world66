Darwin Entertainment Centre is the city's main performing arts venue, with a playhouse, studio theatre, and exhibition spaces just off Mitchell Street. It hosts touring concerts, theatre, dance, comedy, and local productions, so it is worth checking the programme if you have an evening free in Darwin.

The centre is most useful as a night-time option after the waterfront and outdoor sights have wound down.
