---
title: "Eating Out"
type: section
---

Darwin's food reflects its position: this is the most Asian-influenced city in Australia, and the eating is correspondingly better than you might expect for a city of 150,000. Southeast Asian restaurants — particularly Indonesian, Vietnamese, and Malaysian — are represented alongside Australian pub food and a reasonable Italian scene in Fannie Bay. The Parap Markets on Saturday mornings are the best way to eat cheaply and well.
