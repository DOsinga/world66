The George Brown Darwin Botanic Gardens are a calm, leafy introduction to the tropical north. The collections include palms, cycads, orchids, boabs, mangroves, and monsoon forest plants, with paths that show how different Darwin's climate and vegetation feel from the temperate image many visitors have of Australia.

The gardens are especially useful early or late in the day, when the heat is less punishing and birds are active. They pair naturally with nearby Mindil Beach and the Museum and Art Gallery of the Northern Territory. Bring water, move slowly, and let the shade do some of the work.
