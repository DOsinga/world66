Gunlom Falls is famous for its upper pools and broad views across southern Kakadu, though access has changed over the years and should be checked carefully before planning around it. When open, it is one of the park's classic swimming and sunset-view spots.

The climb to the upper pools is short but steep and exposed. Carry water, wear proper shoes, and remember that seasonal closures in Kakadu are not technicalities; they are part of how the park is managed and protected.
