Cutta Cutta Caves are an easy contrast to the open heat around Katherine. Guided tours descend into limestone chambers with stalactites, stalagmites, bats, and the occasional harmless snake resting out of the sun.

The caves are about 27 km south of town, so they work well on the way in or out. Check tour times before driving down.
