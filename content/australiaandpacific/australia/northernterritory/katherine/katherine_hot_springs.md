Katherine Hot Springs are not wild or remote, but they are very welcome after a long drive or dusty walk. Warm clear water runs through a chain of small pools under palms and tropical shade.

Go early or late if you want a quieter soak. In the wet season the area can close after flooding, so check conditions locally.
