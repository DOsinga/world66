Leliyn, also known as Edith Falls, is the softer western face of Nitmiluk National Park. A large paperbark-fringed pool sits below the falls, and a short rocky loop climbs to upper pools with broad views over the escarpment.

Swimming can close in the wet season or after heavy rain, so check local conditions. When it is open, this is one of the most rewarding half-day trips from Katherine.
