Top Didj Cultural Experience and Art Gallery sits in a bush setting on the road toward Nitmiluk Gorge. It introduces visitors to Aboriginal art and culture through guided activities and a gallery rather than a conventional museum display.

It is most useful for travellers using Katherine as a base for Nitmiluk, especially if you want cultural context alongside the gorge, hot springs, and local history stops.
