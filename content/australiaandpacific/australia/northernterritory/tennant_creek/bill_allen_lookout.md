Bill Allen Lookout gives a clear 360-degree view over Tennant Creek and the surrounding Barkly landscape. It is a short, simple stop rather than a long outing, but the open horizon makes it one of the best places around town to get oriented.

Go near sunset if you can, when the low light gives the plain and town their strongest colours.
