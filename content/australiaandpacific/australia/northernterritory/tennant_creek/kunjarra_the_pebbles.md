Kunjarra, often called The Pebbles, is a smaller granite outcrop north of Tennant Creek. It is sometimes compared with Karlu Karlu, the Devils Marbles, but it has its own meaning as a sacred Warumungu women's place.

Come for the late-afternoon light and keep the visit quiet and respectful. The site is close to town, but it is not just a roadside photo stop.
