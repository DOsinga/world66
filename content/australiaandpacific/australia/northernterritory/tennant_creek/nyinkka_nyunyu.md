Nyinkka Nyunyu is the best place in town to start understanding Warumungu country. The centre uses art, objects, stories, and interpretation to explain local Aboriginal life and the relationship between people and the land around Tennant Creek.

It is worth visiting before driving out to nearby sacred sites. The landscape looks different once you have heard a little of the story behind it.
