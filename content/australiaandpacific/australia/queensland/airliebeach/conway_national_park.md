Conway National Park covers the peninsula behind Airlie Beach — 23,800 hectares of coastal lowland rainforest, mangroves, and eucalypt woodland running down to the Whitsunday Passage. From town, the most accessible walks start at the **Mt Rooper trailhead** near Shute Harbour: a one-hour return climb leads to the Mt Rooper Lookout, with views across the islands.

For a tougher day out, the **Whitsunday Great Walk** is a 30-kilometre multi-day track from Brandy Creek through the rainforest interior. The shorter **Coral Beach Track** (one hour return) leads to a quiet rocky cove, often deserted, where you can see fringing coral at low tide. Birdlife in the park includes white-bellied sea eagles, brush turkeys, and the occasional cassowary.
