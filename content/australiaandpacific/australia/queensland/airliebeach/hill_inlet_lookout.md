At the northern end of Whitehaven Beach on Whitsunday Island, **Hill Inlet** is a tidal estuary where the bright white silica sand and turquoise water mix in shifting patterns as the tide moves in and out. The image you see on every Whitsundays brochure — sand swirled like a marbled cake into the green water — is taken from here.

A short, fairly steep boardwalk climbs from the beach landing at **Tongue Bay** to the **Hill Inlet Lookout**. Day tours from Airlie Beach combine the lookout with a couple of hours of swimming on Whitehaven; the view is best at low tide and in early morning light. Overnight sailing trips anchor in Tongue Bay so passengers can walk up for sunrise.
