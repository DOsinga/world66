---
title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Airlie Beach itself is small — the real attractions are offshore. The town centre is a half-kilometre strip along Shute Harbour Road, with the **Lagoon** at one end providing a stinger-free public swimming pool right on the foreshore. From the marina at **Abel Point**, sailing tours and dive boats leave daily for the Whitsundays — most visitors come for **Whitehaven Beach**, the powder-silica beach on Whitsunday Island that is consistently ranked among the best in the world, and the fringing reefs of the **Great Barrier Reef Marine Park**.

For walks and views, **Conway National Park** climbs into the rainforest above town, and the short **Honeyeater Lookout** track delivers a panoramic outlook over the Coral Sea. The **Bicentennial Boardwalk** runs along the foreshore from the Lagoon to Cannonvale. North of the marina, the **Whitsunday Sailing Club** sits at the edge of the bay, while sunset drinks at one of the foreshore bars look directly out over the islands.
