Beaudesert is a small rural town in the **Scenic Rim**, the arc of mountain country south-west of [Brisbane](/australiaandpacific/australia/queensland/brisbane) that traces the New South Wales border. It sits in the Logan River valley with a working cattle-and-dairy hinterland on one side and the basalt walls of the McPherson Range on the other — a landscape of green pastures, fig-lined creeks and old timber farmhouses.

For travellers it is mainly a base or a turn-off. The town is the obvious gateway to [Lamington National Park](/australiaandpacific/australia/queensland/beaudesert/lamington_national_park) at Binna Burra and O'Reilly's, where ancient Antarctic beech and Gondwanan rainforest cover the rim of an extinct volcano, and to the lakes and waterfalls of [Mount Barney National Park](/australiaandpacific/australia/queensland/beaudesert/mount_barney) further south. The town itself is a single main street with the usual country-town mix of pubs, a [heritage museum](/australiaandpacific/australia/queensland/beaudesert/historical_museum), the Saturday morning [farmers' market](/australiaandpacific/australia/queensland/beaudesert/scenic_rim_farmers_market), and not much else. Stay if you want a quiet base for hiking the Scenic Rim; otherwise drive on up into the hills.
