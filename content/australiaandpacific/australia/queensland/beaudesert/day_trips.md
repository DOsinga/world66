---
title: "Day Trips"
type: section
---

The best of the Scenic Rim is within an hour's drive of Beaudesert. **Lamington National Park** (Binna Burra and O'Reilly's), **Mount Barney**, **Tamborine Mountain**, the **Gondwana Rainforests** at Springbrook and the small wine and food country around **Boonah** all make rewarding day trips. The cool, high road to **Mount Lindesay** is a fine drive in its own right, and the New South Wales border country south of Rathdowney is wild and lightly travelled.
