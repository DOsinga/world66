Bowen's town-centre murals turn local history into an informal walking route. They cover farming, shipping, wartime stories, and the older port town that existed before the Whitsundays became a tourist brand.

The murals are not reason enough to travel to Bowen on their own, but they give texture to a stop between beach visits and make the town feel more lived-in than a resort strip.
