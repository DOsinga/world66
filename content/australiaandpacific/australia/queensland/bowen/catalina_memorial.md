The Catalina Memorial on the foreshore marks Bowen's role as a World War II base for Catalina flying boats. The aircraft flew long night patrols over the Coral Sea, and the memorial gives a sharp piece of wartime context to an otherwise relaxed waterfront.

It is a short stop, but a worthwhile one if you are walking the foreshore or trying to understand why this quiet town has more history than its size suggests.
