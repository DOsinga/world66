Army Museum South Queensland is based around the historic Victoria Barracks, an active military site on the edge of central Brisbane. The museum galleries are not always open in the usual walk-in sense, but guided tours give access to the barracks and selected displays.

It is most worthwhile for travellers with a specific interest in military history or colonial-era Brisbane. Book ahead, as access is controlled and visitor details may be required before the tour.
