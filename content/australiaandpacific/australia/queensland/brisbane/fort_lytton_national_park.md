Fort Lytton National Park protects Brisbane's old coastal defence site near the mouth of the river. Its batteries, tunnels, and military buildings tell a different story from the city's riverfront parks and glass towers.

The fort is best for travellers interested in military history or the strategic geography of Moreton Bay. Opening times can be limited, so it rewards a planned visit rather than a spontaneous detour.
