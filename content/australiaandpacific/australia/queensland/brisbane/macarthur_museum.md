MacArthur Museum covers General Douglas MacArthur's time commanding Allied forces in the South West Pacific from Brisbane during World War II. The museum occupies the city building where his headquarters operated between 1942 and 1944.

The exhibits are most useful for travellers interested in Brisbane's wartime role and the Pacific theatre. It is a focused central-city stop that pairs easily with nearby civic and river sights.
