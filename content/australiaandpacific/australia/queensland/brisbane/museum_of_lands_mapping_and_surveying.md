The Museum of Lands, Mapping and Surveying is a small specialist museum about Queensland's surveying history, land records, and mapmaking. Its collection is most rewarding for visitors interested in old maps, instruments, and the practical work behind settlement, property boundaries, and exploration. It is close enough to central Brisbane sights to fit into a short city history walk.
