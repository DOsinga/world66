New Farm Park is one of Brisbane's loveliest riverside green spaces, famous for its jacarandas, lawns, picnic shade, and big rose gardens. It feels more local than ceremonial, which is part of the charm: families spread out under the trees, cyclists roll past, and the river keeps the edge of the park open and breezy.

It is an easy add-on to a wander through New Farm or an evening around the Powerhouse and the river. Spring is the prettiest time, when the jacarandas turn parts of the park purple.
