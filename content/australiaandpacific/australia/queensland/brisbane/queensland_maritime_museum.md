Queensland Maritime Museum has exhibitions on the state's seafaring history, with historic vessels as the main draw. Visitors can step aboard HMAS Diamantina and see Ella's Pink Lady, the yacht Jessica Watson sailed solo around the world.

The museum sits near South Bank, so it is easy to pair with the riverfront, the parklands, and the cultural precinct.
