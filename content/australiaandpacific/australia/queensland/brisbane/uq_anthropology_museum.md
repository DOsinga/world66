The UQ Anthropology Museum sits on the University of Queensland's St Lucia campus and gives Brisbane a small but serious cultural collection away from South Bank. Its holdings include Aboriginal, Torres Strait Islander, Pacific, and wider world material, usually presented through focused exhibitions.

It is best for travellers already interested in museums, anthropology, or the campus itself. Check opening hours before crossing town, as university museums can keep more limited schedules than big civic institutions.
