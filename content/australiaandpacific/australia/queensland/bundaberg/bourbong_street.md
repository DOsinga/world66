Bourbong Street is the spine of central Bundaberg — a wide colonial-era boulevard lined for several blocks with red-brick and rendered late-nineteenth-century commercial buildings. The standouts are the **Bundaberg Post Office** (1890, Italianate with a clock tower), the former **Commercial Bank**, the **Bundaberg School of Arts** (1888, now a community centre), and **Buss Park**, the small central garden where the **Bundaberg War Memorial** stands.

The street is also notable for its **weeping figs** — a row of mature *Ficus benjamina* planted in the early 1900s, listed as heritage trees in their own right. A self-guided heritage walking trail covers the buildings; pick up a brochure at the visitor information centre on Bourbong itself.
