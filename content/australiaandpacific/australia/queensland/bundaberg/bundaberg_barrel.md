The Bundaberg Barrel is the home of Bundaberg Ginger Beer and the company's brewed soft drinks. It combines a shop and tastings with a small interactive museum about the history and process of brewing soft drinks.

The museum visit takes about an hour and is a good companion to Bundaberg's better-known rum distillery, especially for families or anyone interested in the town's food-and-drink industry.
