The Bundaberg Rum Distillery has been making molasses-based dark rum on the same site by the Burnett River since 1888. It is one of Australia's most iconic industrial sites and the source of the polar-bear-branded bottles in every Australian liquor cabinet.

Guided **distillery tours** run hourly through the day — about 90 minutes including the cooperage, the molasses tanks, the still room, and the rum-vats hall. You finish in the tasting room, where the standard tour comes with two complimentary nips. Several premium tours ("Blend Your Own", "Mighty Bundy") extend the experience. The adjacent **Bundaberg Barrel** — built to resemble a giant bourbon barrel — houses the brand's history museum and gift shop.

Closed-toe shoes required for the production-area tours. Book ahead at peak periods.
