---
title: "Getting There"
type: section
---

**By air.** Bundaberg Airport (BDB) has daily Qantas flights from Brisbane (under an hour). The flights to Lady Elliot Island leave from the same airport.

**By car.** Bundaberg is 360 km north of Brisbane on the Bruce Highway (about 4 hours non-stop). The town is a usual overnight stop between Brisbane and the Whitsundays. From Hervey Bay it is about 90 minutes.

**By train.** **Queensland Rail's** *Tilt Train* runs daily from Brisbane Roma Street to Bundaberg — about 4.5 hours, comfortable, and notably cheaper than flying. The slower *Spirit of Queensland* (Brisbane–Cairns) also stops here.

**By bus.** **Greyhound** and **Premier** both run coaches along the Bruce Highway corridor; the journey from Brisbane is about 6 hours.
