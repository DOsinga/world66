The Hinkler Hall of Aviation, on the edge of the Botanic Gardens, is a serious aviation museum dedicated to Bundaberg's most famous son. The centrepiece is the actual **Avro Avian** Hinkler flew to Australia in 1928, and a full-scale replica of his **Ibis amphibious** prototype. A flight simulator, several other restored aircraft, and a detailed history of his career fill the rest of the building.

Hinkler's small house — reassembled in 1983 from his original Southampton home — sits across the path. Allow two hours. A useful contrast to the rum-and-turtles version of Bundaberg.
