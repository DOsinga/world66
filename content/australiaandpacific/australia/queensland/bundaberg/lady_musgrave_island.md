The two coral cays of **Lady Musgrave** and **Lady Elliot** Islands are the closest pieces of the Great Barrier Reef to Bundaberg and the cheapest way to see it from mainland Queensland.

**Lady Musgrave Island** is a 14-hectare cay inside a 1,200-hectare lagoon, reached by daily high-speed catamaran (about 2 hours each way) from Bundaberg Port Marina. Day trips include snorkelling on the lagoon, a glass-bottom boat tour, and a couple of hours on the island itself.

**Lady Elliot Island** has an airstrip and is reached by 30-minute light aircraft from Bundaberg airport. Day trips and overnight stays at the eco-resort are available. The island sits in deeper water — manta rays are nearly guaranteed in winter, and the wall snorkelling is the best easily accessible reef from mainland Australia.

Either is a long, expensive day; both are worth it. Book well ahead.
