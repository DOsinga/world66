Mon Repos is the most accessible turtle rookery in mainland Australia and the most important breeding site for loggerhead turtles in the South Pacific. From **mid-November through January**, female loggerheads (and occasionally green and flatback turtles) come ashore at night to dig nests and lay eggs. From **mid-January through late March**, the hatchlings emerge and scramble to the sea.

The site is run as a Queensland Parks **night-tour operation only** during the nesting season — groups of 70 are walked to whichever turtle or nest a ranger has identified that evening, with red-light torches and clear protocols to avoid disturbing the animals. The encounter is up close and genuinely moving. **Book online** well in advance — tickets sell out months ahead for the prime December and January slots. Outside the season, the small interpretation centre is open during the day but the beach is otherwise an ordinary stretch of sand.
