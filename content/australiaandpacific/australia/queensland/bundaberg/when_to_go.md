---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

Bundaberg sits just south of the Tropic of Capricorn and has the standard sub-tropical Queensland weather: warm winters, hot humid summers, most of the rain falling between January and March.

**The turtle nesting season at Mon Repos runs from mid-November to late March.** Nesting females come ashore from November to January; hatchlings emerge from January to March. December and January are the busiest tourist months — book Mon Repos tours well ahead.

**Winter (June–August)** is the most comfortable for general sightseeing — temperatures in the low 20s°C, dry, and Lady Elliot Island is at its best for manta rays. The hot, wet **summer** is the time for turtles and swimming but not for inland walks. Cyclones occasionally reach this far south, mostly January through March.
