Cairns is the main departure point for Great Barrier Reef day trips. Dozens of operators run boats to different sections of the outer reef, with options ranging from large catamaran tours with 300 passengers to small group dive charters with six. The reef is genuinely extraordinary — clear water, extraordinary coral and fish diversity, and the scale of the structure itself is humbling.

For first-timers, the introductory snorkel and certified dive packages from the big operators work well and include equipment, lunch, and guided reef walks. More experienced divers should look at smaller live-aboard or day-charter operators who access less trafficked sections of reef. The difference in experience between a crowded pontoon on the outer reef and a quiet spot with a small-group charter is substantial.

Budget travellers should know that reef trips are the single biggest expense in Cairns. A full day on the outer reef will cost AUD 200–280 per person. Fitzroy Island (a short ferry from the marina) offers snorkelling and a National Park beach for a fraction of the price.
