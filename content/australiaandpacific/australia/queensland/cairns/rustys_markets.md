Rusty's Markets runs Friday through Sunday in the Cairns CBD, and it is one of the most enjoyable markets in tropical Australia. The fruit and vegetable section is exceptional — a survey of tropical produce from the Atherton Tablelands and the surrounding region, much of it unfamiliar even to Australians from the south.

Beyond produce, the market has street food stalls with good Asian cooking (laksa, pad thai, Vietnamese rolls), fresh juice vendors, coffee, and the usual mix of clothing and craft stalls. The food section is worth arriving hungry for. Early Saturday morning is the best time: peak freshness, manageable crowds, and the city still cool enough to be comfortable.

The market is covered and runs rain or shine. It's on Grafton Street, within easy walking distance of the Esplanade. Free entry, bring cash for the produce and food vendors.
