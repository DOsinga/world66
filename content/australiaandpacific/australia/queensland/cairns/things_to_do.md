---
title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Cairns is primarily a gateway city — the Great Barrier Reef and the Daintree Rainforest are the main draws — but the city itself has more to offer than most visitors expect. The Esplanade waterfront, the aquarium, and the regional art gallery are all worth your time. Day trips to Kuranda and the Atherton Tablelands round out a busy itinerary.
