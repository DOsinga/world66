Two lighthouses stand side by side at Canberra Terrace, on the headland above Kings Beach. The Old Caloundra Light, built in 1896, is a modest timber-framed structure that is among the oldest remaining lighthouses in Queensland. The newer tower was added in 1968 to handle the growing volume of shipping through the Pumicestone Passage.

The headland setting gives good views over the passage toward Bribie Island and south along the beach. It is a five-minute walk from Kings Beach, and worth the detour for anyone interested in maritime history.
