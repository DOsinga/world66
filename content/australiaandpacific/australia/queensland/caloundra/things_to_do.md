---
title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Caloundra's main activities are beach-based — swimming, surfing, whale watching, and walking the coastal boardwalk. The string of beaches from Bulcock through Kings to Moffat each has a different character and different conditions, making it worth trying more than one. The Queensland Air Museum is a surprisingly good regional aviation museum. Beyond that, Caloundra is a place to slow down rather than tick off sights.
