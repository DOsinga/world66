The Cardwell Forest Drive is a 26 km loop through state forest west of the town, passing several waterfalls and swimming holes accessible by short walks from the road. The road is sealed for most of its length and passable in a conventional vehicle in dry conditions, though the creek crossings require care after rain.

The main stops along the drive are Attie Creek Falls, Murray Falls (the most impressive, dropping into a series of pools in a rocky gorge), and the Spa Pool — a naturally formed rock pool filled by a clear freshwater creek. Murray Falls is also significant as a site of cultural importance to the Girramay people, the traditional owners of this country.

The forest itself is dense and layered tropical rainforest, dark and humid, with buttressed fig trees, giant tree ferns, and the constant sound of water. Platypus have been spotted in the quieter creek sections at dawn and dusk.
