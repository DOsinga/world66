Charleville is a small outback town on the Warrego River, far enough west of Brisbane for the sky to feel larger and the distances to start doing their work. It is a practical service centre, but it has more going on than the usual highway stop: astronomy, bilbies, wartime history, and a few monuments to ideas that did not quite work.

The strongest reason to stop is the [Charleville Cosmos Centre](/australiaandpacific/australia/queensland/charleville/charleville_cosmos_centre), where the dry inland air and dark nights turn into guided stargazing, solar viewing, and planetarium shows. In town, the [Charleville Bilby Experience](/australiaandpacific/australia/queensland/charleville/charleville_bilby_experience) gives a rare close look at one of Australia's oddest threatened marsupials.

Charleville also has a knack for unexpected history. During World War II, thousands of United States Army Air Forces personnel were stationed here, a story told at the [WWII Secret Base](/australiaandpacific/australia/queensland/charleville/wwii_secret_base). Near the visitor centre, the [Vortex Rainmaking Guns](/australiaandpacific/australia/queensland/charleville/vortex_rainmaking_guns) are the wonderful remains of a 1902 attempt to blast drought-breaking rain out of the sky.
