This guided nocturnal-house visit is the easiest way to see bilbies without waiting in the desert at night. The presentation explains why the animal matters, how breeding and release work, and why foxes, cats, and habitat loss have made conservation so urgent.

It is a small attraction, but a distinctive one. If you are travelling with children, this is usually the Charleville stop they remember.
