The Cosmos Centre is Charleville's headline stop. By day it explains astronomy through exhibits, solar viewing, and planetarium sessions; after dark the town's clean inland sky becomes the main attraction.

Book evening sessions ahead, especially in the cooler dry months. Bring a warm layer even if the afternoon was hot: outback nights can drop quickly once the sun is gone.
