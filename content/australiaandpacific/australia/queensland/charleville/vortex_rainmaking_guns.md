These odd cone-shaped guns are the remains of a 1902 attempt to break drought by firing explosives into clouds. The experiment was desperate, expensive, and unsuccessful, which is exactly why the display sticks in the mind.

They do not take long to see, but they add a fine dose of outback eccentricity to a walk around the visitor centre area.
