Charleville's wartime air base brought about 3,500 United States Army Air Forces personnel to the edge of the outback from 1942 to 1946. The museum turns that unlikely chapter into an interactive visit, with material on classified missions, local life, and the base's role in the Pacific war.

The story is strongest if you have already driven across the empty country around town. It makes clear why this remote place mattered.
