Chinchilla sits on the Warrego Highway in the Western Downs, about 300 km northwest of Brisbane. From the road it can look like a fuel stop, but the town has a sharper identity than that: melons, petrified wood, cypress pine country, and one of Queensland's odder agricultural stories.

The local claim to fame is the Chinchilla Red petrified wood found around the district. The [Chinchilla Historical Museum](/australiaandpacific/australia/queensland/chinchilla/chinchilla_historical_museum) is the best first stop, with a large petrified wood collection, restored local buildings, machinery, and timber-industry displays. East of town, [Boonarga Cactoblastis Memorial Hall](/australiaandpacific/australia/queensland/chinchilla/boonarga_cactoblastis_memorial_hall) commemorates the moth that helped defeat the prickly pear plague.

Chinchilla is also Australia's self-declared melon capital. The [Chinchilla Melon Festival](/australiaandpacific/australia/queensland/chinchilla/chinchilla_melon_festival), held every second year, turns that into a messy town-wide party of melon skiing, eating contests, farm tours, markets, and street events. Outside festival time, [Charley's Creek](/australiaandpacific/australia/queensland/chinchilla/charleys_creek) and [Chinchilla Weir](/australiaandpacific/australia/queensland/chinchilla/chinchilla_weir) make the town a pleasant overnight stop rather than just a place to buy petrol.
