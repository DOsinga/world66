Cloncurry is a working outback town on the long road between [Longreach](/australiaandpacific/australia/queensland/longreach) and Mount Isa. Copper put it on the map, but its travel interest comes from a wider north-west Queensland story: mining, aviation, remote medicine, and the stubborn logistics of living a long way from the coast.

The town claims, with some justice, that Qantas was conceived here. More importantly for visitors, the Royal Flying Doctor Service began in Cloncurry, and [John Flynn Place Museum](/australiaandpacific/australia/queensland/cloncurry/john_flynn_place_museum) tells that story well. [Cloncurry Unearthed](/australiaandpacific/australia/queensland/cloncurry/cloncurry_unearthed) adds the mining and mineral side.

Cloncurry is not pretty in the soft sense. It is hot, dry, spread out, and practical. But if you are crossing the north-west, it is a real stop rather than a fuel-and-go place, with enough history and outback landscape to justify a spare day.
