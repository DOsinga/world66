**Lonely Planet Brisbane & the Gold Coast** — The standard practical guide to Brisbane and the surrounding region. Covers transport, accommodation, and the main sights in and around the city, with useful context for exploring the bayside suburbs.

**Moreton Bay and its Islands** by Thomas Welsby — A historical account of Moreton Bay and the communities that grew up around it, including the bayside suburbs north of Brisbane. Welsby was a Queensland lawyer and historian writing in the early twentieth century; his work gives a sense of the bay's place in the region's history.

**Johnno** by David Malouf — One of Queensland's most celebrated novels, set in Brisbane and capturing the city's particular character — its heat, its ordinariness, and the restlessness of its people. Not about Clontarf, but essential reading for anyone wanting to understand what Brisbane is and feels like.

**The Reef: A Passionate History** by Iain McCalman — A cultural and natural history of the Great Barrier Reef and Queensland's coastal waters, including Moreton Bay. Good background reading on the marine environment that shapes this part of the coast.
