The beach and foreshore along Bramble Bay are the heart of Clontarf. The bay waters are calmer than the ocean, making it a good spot for swimming, particularly for families with children. Local anglers fish from the shore and the jetty, and the views across the bay towards Sandgate are pleasant. It gets busy with Brisbane day-trippers on summer weekends, but on weekday mornings it's peaceful and genuinely local in feel.
