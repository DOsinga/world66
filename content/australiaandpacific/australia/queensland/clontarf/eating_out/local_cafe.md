A small number of cafés near the Clontarf foreshore cater to locals, dog walkers, and cyclists doing the bay path. Nothing destination-worthy, but a coffee with views across the bay on a quiet morning is a pleasant enough reason to stop. For a broader choice of breakfast or lunch spots, Sandgate is a short walk or cycle along the foreshore.
