---
title: "Bramble Bay Fishing"
type: poi
latitude: -27.252
longitude: 153.072
---

Bramble Bay is well regarded among local anglers for bream, flathead, and whiting. The jetty at Clontarf is a popular fishing spot requiring no boat, and shore fishing along the foreshore is common. Small tinnies and kayaks also work the shallower sections of the bay. It's a straightforward, unpretentious fishing spot that rewards patience.
