type: poi

The waterfront path connects Clontarf to neighbouring Sandgate and Shorncliffe, making for a pleasant flat walk or cycle of a few kilometres with views across Moreton Bay throughout. The path is well used by locals and is an easy way to explore the broader bayside area on foot or by bike. Sandgate is worth continuing to for a coffee or lunch.
