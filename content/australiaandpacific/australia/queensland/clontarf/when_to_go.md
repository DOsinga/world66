---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

Clontarf works as a day trip from Brisbane year-round. Summer (December to February) is warm and humid, with temperatures regularly above 30°C — the bay is good for swimming but the heat and humidity can make the foreshore walk less comfortable in the middle of the day. Spring (September to November) and autumn (March to May) are the most pleasant seasons for walking the foreshore path. Winter (June to August) is mild — typically 15–20°C — and often clear, making it a fine time to visit for a walk or a morning by the water.

Avoid summer weekends if possible: the beach and foreshore get busy with day-trippers from Brisbane, and parking becomes difficult.
