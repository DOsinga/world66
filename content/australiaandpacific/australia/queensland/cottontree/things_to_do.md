---
title: Things to Do
type: section
---

Cotton Tree is a small beach suburb and there are not many things to do here beyond the things a beach suburb does best: swim, paddle, walk, and eat. The beach is good, the river mouth is calm, and the strip of cafes on the waterfront provides everything needed for a relaxed day. For more extensive activities, Maroochydore and the wider Sunshine Coast are minutes away.
