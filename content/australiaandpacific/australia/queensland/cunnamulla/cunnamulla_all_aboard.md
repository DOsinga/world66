Cunnamulla All Aboard uses the old railway station for a 3D light-and-sound show about rail travel, local characters, and what it meant to live at the end of the line. It is more theatrical than a standard railway display, with a holographic station master leading the story.

Check times before turning up, since the show format depends on scheduled sessions rather than a simple wander-through exhibit.
