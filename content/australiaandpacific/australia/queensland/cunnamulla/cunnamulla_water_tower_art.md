The Cunnamulla water tower was painted by Guido van Helten, whose large-scale rural murals have become a kind of informal trail across inland Australia. This one shows children and links Cunnamulla visually with Charleville, another outback town marked by water-tower art.

It is a quick stop rather than a major sight, but it helps make the town feel less anonymous from the road.
