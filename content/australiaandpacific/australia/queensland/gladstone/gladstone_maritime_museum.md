Gladstone Maritime Museum tells the port city's seafaring story, with displays on civilian maritime history and the former Royal Australian Navy patrol boat HMAS Gladstone.

It is a practical stop for understanding why Gladstone exists where it does: this is a working harbour city, and the museum gives the industrial waterfront some human scale.
