Burleigh Beach is one of the Gold Coast's classic surf beaches, backed by Norfolk pines and close to Burleigh Head National Park. It has patrolled swimming areas, picnic shade, and strong surf appeal, making it a reliable beach stop for both families and experienced surfers.
