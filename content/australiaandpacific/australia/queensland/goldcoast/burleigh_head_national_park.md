Burleigh Head National Park protects the forested headland between Burleigh Heads and Tallebudgera Creek. Short walking tracks pass through rainforest and coastal woodland to lookouts over the Gold Coast beaches, the creek mouth, and the Pacific, making it one of the easiest places in the city to combine a swim with a real nature walk.
