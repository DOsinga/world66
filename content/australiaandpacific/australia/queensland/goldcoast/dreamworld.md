Dreamworld is one of the Gold Coast's major theme parks, with a mix of thrill rides, family rides, water rides, and animal exhibits. It sits in the northern theme-park belt, so it is usually planned as a full-day outing rather than a quick stop from the beach.

The park changed several attractions after a fatal 2016 ride accident, and some older rides have closed since then. Check current rides, opening hours, and safety notices before you go.
