Federation Walk Coastal Reserve is a protected strip of coastal vegetation at Main Beach, north of the busier Surfers Paradise area. Its paths pass through littoral rainforest, native grassland, wetlands, and dunes, with chances to spot birds and other wildlife.

The reserve works well as a low-key walk before or after time on the beach, especially for visitors who want a quieter Gold Coast stop without leaving the coast.
