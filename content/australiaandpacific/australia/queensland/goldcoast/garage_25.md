Garage 25 displays part of the personal vehicle collection of Tony Longhurst, the two-time Bathurst 1000 winner. The collection ranges from early 20th-century cars to motorbikes, mowers, and outboard motors.

It is a specialist stop rather than a broad Gold Coast highlight, but anyone interested in Australian motoring will find enough here to justify the detour north.
