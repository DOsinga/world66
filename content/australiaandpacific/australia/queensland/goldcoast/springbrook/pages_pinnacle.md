Pages Pinnacle is a steep volcanic plug reached by a roughly 6 km return walk in the Gold Coast hinterland. The route is more demanding than the usual rainforest lookout strolls around Springbrook, with scrambling near the top and broad views once you gain the ridge.

It is a good choice for confident hikers who want something rougher and quieter than the main waterfall circuits.
