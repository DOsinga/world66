Pages Pinnacle is a volcanic plug reached by a roughly 6-km return walk in the Gold Coast hinterland. The route is better suited to confident walkers, with some scrambling near the narrow summit ridge.

The reward is a dramatic lookout over the surrounding ridges and valleys, with a wilder feel than the busier Springbrook waterfall walks.
