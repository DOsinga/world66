WhiteWater World is a large water park beside Dreamworld in the northern Gold Coast. It has slides, pools, and family water attractions, making it an easy add-on for visitors already heading to the theme-park cluster around Coomera.

It has fewer rides than Wet'n'Wild, but the location is convenient and the park works well for a full hot-weather break from beaches and sightseeing.
