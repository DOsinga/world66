Goondiwindi is a Queensland border town on the Macintyre River, better as a half-day or overnight stop than as a destination you cross the country to see. It passes the World66 test because its anchors are genuinely local: the old customs point, the river border, cotton country, and the racehorse Gunsynd, still treated here like a civic hero.

The [Customs House Museum](/australiaandpacific/australia/queensland/goondiwindi/customs_house_museum) is the best place to start, because it explains why the border mattered before Federation. From there, walk the [Macintyre River Walk](/australiaandpacific/australia/queensland/goondiwindi/macintyre_river_walk), detour to the [Gunsynd Statue](/australiaandpacific/australia/queensland/goondiwindi/gunsynd_statue), and use the [Botanic Gardens of the Western Woodlands](/australiaandpacific/australia/queensland/goondiwindi/botanic_gardens_of_the_western_woodlands) for a quieter look at the inland landscape.

This is not a big-ticket town. It is a good one-day border stop with enough story to beat another anonymous highway break.
