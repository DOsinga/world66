The Botanic Gardens of the Western Woodlands are Goondiwindi's best quiet stop, with native plantings and walking paths that reflect the drier inland country around town. They are not formal European gardens, and they are better for a slow picnic or birdwatching than for spectacle.

That local focus is the appeal. After the river and museum, the gardens show the landscape beyond the irrigated cotton fields and highway edge.
