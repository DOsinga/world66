Euraba Artists and Papermakers Gallery showcases work by local Indigenous artists, including handmade paper art produced from cotton off-cuts from the region's clothing industry. It is a small, specialist stop for visitors interested in local art and contemporary Aboriginal craft. Visits need to be arranged ahead of time.
