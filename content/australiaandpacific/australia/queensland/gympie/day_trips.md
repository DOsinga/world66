---
title: "Day Trips"
type: section
---

Gympie is a convenient base for the southern end of the **Fraser Coast** — Rainbow Beach, with its multi-coloured sand cliffs and the access point to the ferry across to K'gari (Fraser Island), is about an hour east on a sealed road. The **Cooloola section of Great Sandy National Park** stretches between Rainbow Beach and Noosa and offers good 4WD beach driving and walking.

Closer to town, the Mary Valley villages of **Kandanga**, **Imbil**, and **Amamoor** are pretty rural stops along the Mary Valley Heritage Railway route — Amamoor has a lookout over the valley and is the venue for the Muster country music festival.

The Sunshine Coast hinterland towns of **Maleny** and **Montville**, with their cooler climate and farmers' markets, are about an hour and a quarter south.
