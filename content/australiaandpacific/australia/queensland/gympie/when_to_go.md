---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

Gympie has a humid subtropical climate. Summers (December–February) are hot, humid, and prone to heavy thunderstorms; daytime temperatures sit around 30°C and can be uncomfortable. Winters (June–August) are dry and pleasant, with daytime temperatures around 21°C and cool nights.

The signature local event is the **Gympie Muster**, held over four days in late August at Amamoor Creek State Forest about 30 km south of town. It's one of Australia's biggest country music festivals — over 100 acts, tens of thousands of attendees, and a strong bush-camping atmosphere. Book accommodation and tickets well in advance.

The other date worth knowing is the annual **Gold Rush Festival** in October, which celebrates the town's mining heritage with parades, a re-enactment camp, and street stalls along Mary Street.
