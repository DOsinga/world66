The Torres Strait Heritage Museum is the best introduction to Horn Island, with collections on local island history, pearling, military life, and the World War II bombing raids. It is privately run and stronger than its modest scale suggests.

Allow time to read. The museum gives names, objects, and photographs to a history that can otherwise feel abstract when you are just passing through the airport.
