The Australian Stockman's Hall of Fame is the most ambitious cultural institution in outback Queensland and one of the most engaging museums in the country. It pays tribute to the drovers, shearers, station workers, explorers, and Aboriginal stockmen who shaped the Australian interior over two centuries of European settlement.

The collection covers the full breadth of outback life: stockmen's equipment, station homesteads, droving routes, and the social history of communities that existed at enormous distances from everything. A restored Beechcraft Queen Air from the Royal Flying Doctor Service hangs from the ceiling, a reminder of how remote medicine worked before sealed roads. The 2021 renovation added interactive digital storytelling that draws on personal accounts and oral histories, giving the place a human texture beyond the artefacts.

Allow three to four hours. The café on site is reliable for lunch. The Hall of Fame sits on the edge of town, close to the Qantas Founders Museum, making them natural companions for a full day out.
