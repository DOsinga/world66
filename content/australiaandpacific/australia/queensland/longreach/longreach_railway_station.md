Longreach Railway Station is a Queensland Heritage-listed building that opened as a terminus in 1892 when the Central Western line was extended from Barcaldine. The current timber station was built in 1916 to a design by Henrik Hansen, the same architect behind the stations at Emerald and Archer Park. The platform canopy — a cantilevered shade rather than the curved carriage shades at other stations — is a distinctive detail that distinguishes Longreach's design.

The station is still served by the Spirit of the Outback train, which runs weekly between Brisbane and Longreach. Arriving by train is one of the classic ways to reach the outback, though the journey takes about 24 hours from Brisbane. The station itself is worth walking through even if you are arriving by car — the platform, the old refreshment room, and the surrounding railway infrastructure give a palpable sense of how the outback was connected to the coast in the early 20th century.
