Artspace Mackay is the city's main gallery and the best indoor stop in the centre. Exhibitions change, but the useful thread for travellers is how the gallery connects to Mackay's public art, especially the Fiona Foley installations along the Bluewater Trail.

It is a short visit unless a show grabs you. That is fine: combine it with the riverfront and nearby civic precinct rather than setting aside a full afternoon.
