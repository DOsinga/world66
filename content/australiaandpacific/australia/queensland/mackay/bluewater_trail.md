The Bluewater Trail is Mackay's best urban walk or ride, following the Pioneer River and linking several pieces of the city that otherwise feel spread out. It connects parks, the lagoon, public art, historic architecture, and riverside views in a way that makes Mackay feel more coherent.

Go early or late in the day. The tropical heat can make a midday walk feel much longer than the map suggests.
