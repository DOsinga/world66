**Magnetic Island** — "Maggie" to locals — sits 8 km off the coast of [Townsville](/australiaandpacific/australia/queensland/townsville) in north Queensland. More than half the island is national park, covered in eucalyptus woodland and granite boulders, with 23 km of walking tracks and a resident population of wild koalas.

The island has four main bays with small settlements: Picnic Bay, [Nelly Bay](/australiaandpacific/australia/queensland/magnetic_island/nelly_bay) (where the ferry arrives), [Arcadia](/australiaandpacific/australia/queensland/magnetic_island/arcadia_beach), and [Horseshoe Bay](/australiaandpacific/australia/queensland/magnetic_island/horseshoe_bay). Horseshoe Bay is the liveliest, with a long beach, water sports hire, and a few bars. The [Forts Walk](/australiaandpacific/australia/queensland/magnetic_island/forts_walk), a trail through World War II gun emplacements on the headland between Arcadia and Horseshoe Bay, is the best short hike on the island and a reliable spot for [seeing koalas in the wild](/australiaandpacific/australia/queensland/magnetic_island/koala_spotting). The [Geoffrey Bay snorkel trail](/australiaandpacific/australia/queensland/magnetic_island/geoffrey_bay_snorkel_trail) near Arcadia is among the top ten snorkelling spots in Queensland and is accessible directly from the beach.

Getting there is straightforward — a 20-minute ferry from Townsville's terminal, running frequently throughout the day. A local bus connects the bays, or you can rent a scooter or a classic open-sided Moke car from near the ferry terminal.
