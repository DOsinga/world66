Maroochydore is the commercial centre of Queensland's Sunshine Coast, sitting at the mouth of the [Maroochy River](/australiaandpacific/australia/queensland/maroochydore/maroochy_river) about an hour north of Brisbane. It is less glamorous than [Noosa](/australiaandpacific/australia/queensland/noosaheads) further up the coast, but the beaches are just as good and considerably less crowded.

[Maroochydore Beach](/australiaandpacific/australia/queensland/maroochydore/maroochydore_beach) is a long stretch of clean sand backed by parkland, good for swimming and bodyboarding. [Cotton Tree](/australiaandpacific/australia/queensland/maroochydore/cotton_tree), at the river mouth, is a quieter spot popular with families and stand-up paddleboarders. The [Sunshine Plaza](/australiaandpacific/australia/queensland/maroochydore/sunshine_plaza) shopping centre makes Maroochydore the practical hub for the region, and the restaurant and cafe scene along [Ocean Street](/australiaandpacific/australia/queensland/maroochydore/ocean_street_precinct) has improved considerably in recent years.
