---
title: When to Go
type: section
---

The Sunshine Coast has a subtropical climate and is pleasant year-round, but there are real differences between the seasons. Summer (December to February) is hot and humid with afternoon thunderstorms, and the school holiday crowds make accommodation expensive and the beaches crowded. Winter (June to August) is mild and sunny with low humidity — arguably the best time to visit. Water temperatures stay warm enough for swimming throughout the year.

September and October are particularly good: the school crowds have thinned, the weather is still warm, and prices drop from their summer peak.
