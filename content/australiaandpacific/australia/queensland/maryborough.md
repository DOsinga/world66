Maryborough is one of Queensland's better-preserved heritage towns, set on the Mary River inland from Hervey Bay. It was a busy colonial port before the coast drew most travellers away, and that older role left it with broad streets, civic buildings, warehouses, parks, and a surprisingly strong sense of place.

The town's best sights sit close together around the old port and centre. The [Portside Heritage Precinct](/australiaandpacific/australia/queensland/maryborough/portside_heritage_precinct) gives the town its river history, [Queens Park](/australiaandpacific/australia/queensland/maryborough/queens_park) is one of the finest old municipal parks in regional Queensland, and the [Story Bank](/australiaandpacific/australia/queensland/maryborough/story_bank) turns local author P. L. Travers into more than a statue and photo stop.

Maryborough is not as immediately dramatic as the Fraser Coast beaches, but it is a rewarding day if you like historic towns that still feel lived in. It works especially well as a change of pace from Hervey Bay or as a stop on the inland route north.
