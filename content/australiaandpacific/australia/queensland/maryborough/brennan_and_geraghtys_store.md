Brennan and Geraghty's Store is a preserved 19th-century general store, kept with shelves, packaging, ledgers, and shop fittings that make everyday trade feel unusually close. It is a small museum, but a good one for travellers who like social history rather than only grand buildings.

The appeal is in the detail: tins, labels, counters, accounts, and the sense of a shop shut up and somehow carried forward intact. It rounds out Maryborough's heritage story nicely after the riverfront buildings.
