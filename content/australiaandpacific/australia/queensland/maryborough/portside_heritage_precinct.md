Maryborough's Portside Heritage Precinct is the old riverfront core, where customs, trade, and immigration once made the town one of colonial Queensland's important ports. The warehouses, public buildings, and streetscape still make the river history legible.

This is the right place to start a walk through Maryborough. The Bond Store and Customs House are close by, and the precinct gives the town a stronger identity than the usual regional main street.
