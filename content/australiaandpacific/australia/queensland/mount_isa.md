Mount Isa is a mining city in far north-west Queensland, closer in spirit to the Northern Territory than to the beaches most travellers associate with the state. The mine stacks, dry hills, big roads, and furnace-like summer heat define the place. It is not pretty in the conventional sense, but it is memorable.

The city has enough substance for a real stop. [Outback at Isa](/australiaandpacific/australia/queensland/mount_isa/outback_at_isa) packages the mine tour, Riversleigh fossil story, visitor centre, and regional art in one useful complex. The [Underground Hospital](/australiaandpacific/australia/queensland/mount_isa/underground_hospital) is stranger and more moving: a wartime civilian hospital dug into the hill after the bombing of Darwin. [City Lookout](/australiaandpacific/australia/queensland/mount_isa/city_lookout) gives the best view of mine, town, and outback.

Mount Isa rewards travellers who like places with a hard edge. Come in the cooler months if you can, and time it for the rodeo if you want to see the city at full volume.
