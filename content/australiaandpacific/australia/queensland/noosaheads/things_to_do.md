---
title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Noosa Heads is short on conventional sights and long on outdoor experiences: a coastal headland that doubles as a national park, world-class surf at the point breaks, a sheltered main beach, and a quiet river system perfect for paddling. The town itself is small enough that you can walk between the headland, the beach, and the main strip of Hastings Street in twenty minutes.
