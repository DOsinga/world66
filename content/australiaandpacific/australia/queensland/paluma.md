Paluma is a tiny village in the mountains north of Townsville, set in cool rainforest near the southern edge of the Wet Tropics. It is the opposite of coastal Queensland: misty, green, quiet, and much cooler than the plains below.

Most visitors come for the drive up Mount Spec Road, the stone bridge and swimming holes at [Little Crystal Creek](/australiaandpacific/australia/queensland/paluma/little_crystal_creek), and the forest walks around the village. [Paluma Dam](/australiaandpacific/australia/queensland/paluma/paluma_dam) adds canoeing and camping, while the surrounding bird area is known for rainforest species.

Do not expect much town life. Paluma is a place for a picnic, a swim, a rainforest walk, and a break from Townsville heat.
