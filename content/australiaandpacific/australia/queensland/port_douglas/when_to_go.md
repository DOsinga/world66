---
title: When to Go
type: section
---

Port Douglas has a classic wet-dry tropical climate. The dry season (May to October) is the best time to visit: temperatures are warm but not oppressive (25–30°C), the sea is calm and clear, the reef is at its best, and the jellyfish are largely absent from the beaches. This is peak tourist season and prices are at their highest — book accommodation well in advance for July and August.

The wet season (November to April) brings heavy rain, high humidity, and heat (35°C and above). Cyclones are possible. Marine stingers make beach swimming hazardous outside the stinger enclosures. Some tours continue to run; the rainforest is at its most dramatic and green. If you don't mind the conditions, the wet season is cheaper and much less crowded. The shoulder months of April–May and September–October balance good weather with slightly lower prices.
