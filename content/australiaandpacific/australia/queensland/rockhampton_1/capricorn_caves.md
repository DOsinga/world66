Capricorn Caves is a privately owned limestone cave system about 23 kilometres north of Rockhampton, near the Berserker Range. The caves have been open to tourists since 1882 and contain a series of caverns with notable formations, including the Cathedral Cave — a large open chamber with remarkable acoustics that is used for concerts and has hosted weddings.

Tours range from family-friendly 45-minute walks through the main chambers to adventure caving options in the more confined passages. The Cathedral Cave's most striking feature is a natural light show at the summer solstice, when sunlight shines directly through an opening to illuminate the floor — a predictable enough phenomenon that the cave operators schedule special tours for it each December. The caves are air-conditioned to a constant 16°C, which is welcome in the Queensland summer.
