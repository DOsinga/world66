---
title: Getting There
type: section
---

Rockhampton Airport has regular direct flights from Brisbane (about 1.5 hours) operated by Qantas and Virgin Australia. Flights also connect to Cairns and Sydney. The airport is close to the city centre and easily reached by taxi or rideshare.

By road, Rockhampton is on the Bruce Highway — about 640 kilometres north of Brisbane (6–7 hours). Greyhound coach services run on the Brisbane–Cairns route and stop in Rockhampton. The Queensland Rail Tilt Train from Brisbane also serves Rockhampton, with the journey taking around 7 hours on the faster services. For Great Keppel Island, the jumping-off point is Rosslyn Bay Harbour, about 45 kilometres east of the city, where ferries depart for the island.
