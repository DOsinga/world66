Mount Archer rises directly above Rockhampton and gives the city its best natural lookout. The summit area looks over the Fitzroy River, the grid of town streets, and the dry hills beyond, so it is the easiest way to understand Rockhampton's position between coast and inland Queensland.

The national park has short walks, picnic areas, and sections of dry rainforest and eucalypt woodland. Go early or late for cooler air and better light; midday can be harsh, especially in summer.
