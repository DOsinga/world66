Quay Street runs along the Fitzroy River and contains the densest collection of 19th-century commercial buildings in Queensland outside Brisbane. The streetscape is largely intact: sandstone banks, Victorian offices with iron-lace verandahs, and heritage-listed wool stores that once handled the output of the central Queensland cattle industry.

The Customs House at number 208 and the Rockhampton Club at 166 are among the most photogenic buildings. The Criterion Hotel at 150 Quay Street, built in the 1880s, still operates as a pub and is a good stop for a cold drink. The precinct is best seen on foot — the whole street takes about 30 minutes to walk at a leisurely pace, longer if you stop to read the heritage plaques.
