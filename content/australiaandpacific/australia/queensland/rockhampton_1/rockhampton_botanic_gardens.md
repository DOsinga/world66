Rockhampton Botanic Gardens are one of the city's best-established green spaces, with mature tropical plantings, broad lawns, lagoons, and shaded paths. They date from the colonial era and still feel like a civic garden rather than a decorative patch of parkland.

The gardens pair naturally with the adjacent zoo and make an easy break from the heat of central Rockhampton. They are especially useful for families, birdwatchers, and anyone driving the Bruce Highway who needs a calm stop with room to wander.
