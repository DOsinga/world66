---

Rockhampton is not a place people come to for its own sake, but it rewards a half-day's exploration before moving on. The [Quay Street heritage precinct](/australiaandpacific/australia/queensland/rockhampton_1/quay_street) is one of the best-preserved Victorian streetscapes in Queensland. The [Rockhampton Museum of Art](/australiaandpacific/australia/queensland/rockhampton_1/rockhampton_museum_of_art) is a genuine regional gallery with a solid Australian art collection. North of the city, the [Capricorn Caves](/australiaandpacific/australia/queensland/rockhampton_1/capricorn_caves) are the main natural attraction — limestone cave systems with cathedral-like chambers used for concerts and weddings. [Great Keppel Island](/australiaandpacific/australia/queensland/rockhampton_1/great_keppel_island) is accessible by ferry from Rosslyn Bay and offers beaches and snorkelling an easy day trip away.
