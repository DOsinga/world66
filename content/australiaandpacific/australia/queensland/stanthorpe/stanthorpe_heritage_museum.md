Stanthorpe Heritage Museum is the best place to understand how the Granite Belt became a town of tin miners, orchardists, railway workers, and wine growers. The collection spreads across several historic buildings and is stronger than the usual small-town museum.

It is especially useful early in a visit, before driving through the surrounding farms and old railway settlements. Check opening times before you go, as hours can be limited.
