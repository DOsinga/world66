Stanthorpe Regional Art Gallery gives the town a cultural stop beyond wineries and orchards. It hosts changing exhibitions, workshops, music events, and the Stanthorpe Art Prize, with a collection that mixes regional work and broader Australian art.

The gallery is close to the town centre, so it fits easily into a short walk with a cafe stop or the climb up to Mount Marlay.
