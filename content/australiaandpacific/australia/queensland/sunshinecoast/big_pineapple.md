The Big Pineapple is a 16-metre roadside landmark near Woombye and one of Australia's classic "big things". The complex has shops, food stops, a small train ride, farm displays, rainforest paths, wildlife gardens, and animal encounters, making it an easy family stop in the Sunshine Coast hinterland.
