Buderim Ginger Factory is a ginger-themed family attraction in Yandina, north of the main Sunshine Coast beach towns. It mixes a working ginger brand with light rides, a small train, gardens, shops, and child-friendly displays.

It is most useful for families or anyone looking for an easy inland stop away from the beach. The visit is short and low-key, but the ginger products, gardens, and small rides give it enough substance for a deliberate detour.
