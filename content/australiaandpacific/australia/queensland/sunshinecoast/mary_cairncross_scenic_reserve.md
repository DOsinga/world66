Mary Cairncross Scenic Reserve protects a remnant of subtropical rainforest on the Blackall Range, with walking tracks under dense canopy and a famous lookout toward the Glass House Mountains. It is one of the Sunshine Coast hinterland's easiest nature stops.

The reserve works well for families and casual walkers because the main rainforest loop is short, shaded, and well interpreted. Go on a clear morning for the best mountain views before cloud and haze soften the horizon.
