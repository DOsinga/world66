---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

The Sunshine Coast has a subtropical climate with warm summers and mild winters. The best time to visit is from April to October, when temperatures are pleasant (20-25°C), humidity is lower, and the southeast trade winds provide a cooling breeze. Summer (December to February) is hot and humid, with occasional heavy rain.

The school holiday periods (particularly Christmas and Easter) are extremely busy — accommodation prices spike and beaches become crowded. Outside school holidays, particularly in winter (June-August), the coast is pleasantly quiet. Whale watching season runs from late June to October, when humpback whales migrate north along the coast.
