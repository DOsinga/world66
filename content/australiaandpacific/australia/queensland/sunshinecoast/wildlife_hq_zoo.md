Wildlife HQ Zoo is a compact animal park at the Big Pineapple complex near Woombye. It keeps a mix of Australian wildlife and exotic species, making it an easy family stop if you are moving through the central Sunshine Coast hinterland.

It is not as famous as nearby Australia Zoo, but it is smaller, simpler, and easier to fit into a half day. The zoo is best for families or animal-focused travellers who want a second wildlife stop away from the coast's beaches.
