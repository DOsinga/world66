Thursday Island, or Waiben, is the main settlement and administrative centre of the Torres Strait Islands. It is small, hot, and practical, but it has a sharper identity than many tropical transit points: Kaurareg country, Torres Strait Islander culture, pearling history, and the old colonial fear of invasion all meet on one compact island.

The best visit is simple. Start with [Gab Titui Cultural Centre](/australiaandpacific/australia/queensland/thursday_island/gab_titui_cultural_centre), then go up to [Green Hill Fort](/australiaandpacific/australia/queensland/thursday_island/green_hill_fort) for the view and military history. The [Thursday Island Cemetery](/australiaandpacific/australia/queensland/thursday_island/thursday_island_cemetery) is sobering, with graves that point to the multi-ethnic pearling and trading world that built the island's modern town.

Do not come expecting a resort island. Thursday Island is better as a cultural and historical stop, especially if you are already moving through Cape York or the Torres Strait.
