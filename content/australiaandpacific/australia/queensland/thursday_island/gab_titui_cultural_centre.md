Gab Titui is Thursday Island's essential first stop, a cultural centre and gallery for Torres Strait Islander and Aboriginal art, objects, and stories. It sits on Kaurareg country and gives needed context before you walk the rest of the island.

The exhibitions are small enough to take in without fatigue, but they change the way the island reads: less as a remote outpost, more as the centre of a living sea-country culture.
