Thursday Island Cemetery is one of the island's most telling historical places. Japanese divers, Muslim traders, Pacific Islanders, Torres Strait Islanders, and Europeans are buried here, reflecting the pearling and shipping world that made the town.

It is a quiet walk rather than a formal museum. Read the names and dates, and the island's history becomes much less abstract.
