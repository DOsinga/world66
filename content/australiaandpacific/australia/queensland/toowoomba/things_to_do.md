---

Toowoomba's best stops are its gardens and range-edge views. [Picnic Point](/australiaandpacific/australia/queensland/toowoomba/picnic_point) is the essential lookout over the escarpment, while [Queens Park and Botanic Gardens](/australiaandpacific/australia/queensland/toowoomba/queens_park_and_botanic_gardens) and [Laurel Bank Park](/australiaandpacific/australia/queensland/toowoomba/laurel_bank_park) carry the Garden City identity. [Cobb+Co Museum](/australiaandpacific/australia/queensland/toowoomba/cobb_co_museum) is the best indoor stop, especially if you want the inland Queensland story behind the city.
