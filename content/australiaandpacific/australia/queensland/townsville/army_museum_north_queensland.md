The Army Museum North Queensland occupies the old Kissing Point fortifications, giving the displays a setting with real military weight. Exhibits cover the army's role in North Queensland, wartime defence, uniforms, weapons, photographs, and the region's long connection with garrison life. Townsville is still a major defence city, so the museum explains something current as well as historical.

The location above the Strand adds views and sea air to the visit. It is a good choice if you have already done the waterfront and want a more specific local angle, or if Australian military history interests you. Opening hours can be limited, so check before climbing up.
