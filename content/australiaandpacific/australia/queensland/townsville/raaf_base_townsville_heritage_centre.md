The RAAF Base Townsville Heritage Centre covers the history of the Royal Australian Air Force in Townsville during World War II and after. Displays include indoor exhibits and outdoor aircraft or equipment connected with the base's long military role.

Because it is on an active RAAF base, visitors should check access requirements and opening arrangements before going. For military-history travellers, it adds another angle to Townsville's wartime story.
