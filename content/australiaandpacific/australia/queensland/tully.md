Tully is a small town in Queensland's Wet Tropics, about 140 km south of Cairns, and claims the title of Australia's wettest town — annual rainfall regularly exceeds 4 metres. The surrounding country is almost entirely sugarcane and banana plantations, which is the economic backbone of the area.

For travelers, the main draw is [white-water rafting on the Tully River](/australiaandpacific/australia/queensland/tully/tully_river_rafting), among the best in Queensland, with grade three and four rapids running through rainforest gorges in [Tully Gorge National Park](/australiaandpacific/australia/queensland/tully/tully_gorge_national_park). Day trips run from Cairns. The town itself has little to detain you beyond the [Golden Gumboot](/australiaandpacific/australia/queensland/tully/golden_gumboot) — a seven-metre fibreglass rubber boot celebrating the wettest-town status — but the setting in the rainforest-clad hills is scenic and the drive south toward Mission Beach passes through beautiful country.
