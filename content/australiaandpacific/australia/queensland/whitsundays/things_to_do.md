The Whitsundays are built around boats. Day cruises run to [Whitehaven Beach](/australiaandpacific/australia/queensland/whitsundays/whitehaven_beach), scenic flights loop over the islands and outer reef, and overnight sailing trips spend their time moving between sheltered anchorages on [Whitsunday Island](/australiaandpacific/australia/queensland/whitsundays/whitsunday_island), [Hook Island](/australiaandpacific/australia/queensland/whitsundays/hook_island), and smaller coves.

If you are short on time, choose one strong beach-and-snorkel trip rather than trying to see everything. With two or three days, a sailing trip gives the islands room to breathe: sunrise on deck, a snorkel before the day boats arrive, and a quiet anchorage after sunset.
