Winton is a small Outback Queensland town with a reach far beyond its size. It sits in red, open country west of [Longreach](/australiaandpacific/australia/queensland/longreach), and it has two strong claims on the Australian imagination: the story of "Waltzing Matilda" and some of the country's richest dinosaur finds.

The town itself is compact, with wide streets, pubs, and a dry heat that makes shade feel important. The [Waltzing Matilda Centre](/australiaandpacific/australia/queensland/winton/waltzing_matilda_centre) is the cultural anchor in town, while the [Australian Age of Dinosaurs](/australiaandpacific/australia/queensland/winton/australian_age_of_dinosaurs) sits on a mesa outside it, looking over country where sauropod bones still come out of the ground.

Winton is not a casual detour from the coast. It rewards travellers who are already moving through the outback and want a place with a proper story, a strange landscape, and enough sights to justify stopping for a full day.
