The Dinosaur Stampede National Monument at Lark Quarry protects thousands of dinosaur footprints preserved in a single slab of mudstone. The tracks are often described as the only known evidence of a dinosaur stampede, though the exact story is debated; either way, the sight of so many fossil footprints in one place is extraordinary.

It is a long drive southwest of Winton, so treat it as a half-day trip rather than a quick stop. The road and distance are part of the experience: empty outback, low hills, and then a sheltered building holding a moment from 95 million years ago.
