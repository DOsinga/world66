Wreck Point Scenic Lookout is a small headland stop south of Yeppoon with a wide view across Keppel Bay and the islands. It is easy to reach by road and works well as a quick orientation point before continuing toward Rosslyn Bay, Kemp Beach, or Bluff Point.

The name comes from a shipwreck story, but the view is the reason to stop. On a clear day you can trace the coast north toward Yeppoon and east across the water to Great Keppel Island.
