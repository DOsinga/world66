Yeppoon Foreshore is the town's social strip: beach, palms, lawns, picnic tables, cafes, street art, and the Keppel Kraken water play area. It is polished and family-friendly, but still relaxed enough that it feels like a local waterfront rather than a resort compound.

Come near sunset, when the heat softens and people drift out for a walk along Anzac Parade. The view east over Keppel Bay is the reminder that Yeppoon is more than a beach town; it is the mainland edge of an island coast.
