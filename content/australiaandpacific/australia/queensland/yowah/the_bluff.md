The Bluff is the small rise that proves how flat Yowah's country really is. Drive out toward the communications tower and the town drops behind you, replaced by a wide, dry sweep of mulga, mining tracks, and empty horizon.

It is not a long walk or a big climb. It is a quick stop that gives the opal field its setting.
