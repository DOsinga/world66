Dreamtime Cultural Centre presents Aboriginal and Torres Strait Islander culture on a large site north of Rockhampton. Its displays and guided elements make it one of the region's clearest places for visitors to engage with Indigenous cultural history.

The centre is outside the city core, so plan it as a dedicated stop. It pairs naturally with other Rockhampton day trips, especially if you want more than riverfront architecture and cattle-country imagery.
