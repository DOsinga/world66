Ginger Mule on William Street is one of Rockhampton's better restaurants, serving modern Australian food with an emphasis on local produce. The beef dishes are the obvious focus — the steaks are sourced from local producers and cooked well — but the kitchen also handles fish, chicken, and vegetarian options with care. The room is relaxed and the service is friendly without being fussy.

A good choice for an evening meal when you want something a step above the pub or the chain restaurant.
