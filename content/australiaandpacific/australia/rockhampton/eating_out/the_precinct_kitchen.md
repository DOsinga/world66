The Precinct Kitchen and Bar is located near the Quay Street heritage precinct and serves breakfast, lunch, and dinner in a relaxed setting. The menu covers the full range — eggs and coffee in the morning, burgers and salads at lunch, and more substantial dishes in the evening. The cooking is reliable and the coffee is good.

It is a useful base for exploring the heritage precinct and is one of the better options in that part of the city.
