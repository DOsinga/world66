Mount Archer rises to 607 metres to the northeast of Rockhampton, and the national park that surrounds the summit offers a welcome escape from the flat river valley below. The drive to the summit picnic area takes about 20 minutes from the city centre; from there a short walking track leads to lookouts with views over the Fitzroy River, the coastal plain, and on clear days, the Capricorn Coast.

The mountain's forests are good for birdwatching — koalas are often spotted in the eucalyptus forest on the upper slopes, and rainbow lorikeets and black cockatoos are reliably present. The summit track is well-maintained and accessible to most fitness levels. Early morning and late afternoon visits avoid the worst of the heat and offer the best light.
