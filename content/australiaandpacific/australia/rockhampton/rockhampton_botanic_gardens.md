The Rockhampton Botanic Gardens are a calm and genuinely attractive escape from the heat of the city centre — free, well-maintained, and larger than they initially appear. The gardens cover around 37 hectares and contain a mix of tropical and subtropical plants, a Japanese garden, a rose garden, and a zoo section with Australian wildlife.

The zoo is a highlight if you are travelling with children: it has kangaroos, wallabies, and wombats in accessible enclosures, and the scale is manageable without the exhaustion of a large zoo. The gardens are at their best in the early morning, when the light is good and the temperature is tolerable. The café on the grounds provides morning tea and light lunches.
