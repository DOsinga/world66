Rockhampton Heritage Village is an open-air history museum with around 20 reconstructed and relocated buildings from the 1850s to the 1950s, including a school, hospital, church, and workshops. Allow about an hour for a simple walk-through, longer if you like old machinery, domestic displays, and regional history.
