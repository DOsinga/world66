Rockhampton Zoo sits beside the Botanic Gardens and gives the city a compact, easy wildlife attraction. The collection includes Australian animals alongside selected exotic species, making it especially useful for families who want a low-key animal stop without leaving town.

Its free-entry model and garden setting make it simple to combine with a picnic or a walk through the adjacent grounds. It is not a giant zoo, but it is one of Rockhampton's most practical things-to-do sights.
