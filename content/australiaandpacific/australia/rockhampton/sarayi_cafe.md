Sarayi is one of Rockhampton's better specialty coffee spots, with a focus on quality espresso and a small, carefully considered food menu. The cafe occupies a compact space in the city centre and draws a regular crowd of locals who take their coffee seriously.

The food menu leans toward Middle Eastern-influenced dishes — good for breakfast or a light lunch. The coffee is strong and well-made, using beans that change seasonally. A reliable stop before a day of sightseeing.
