Adelaide sits on a coastal plain between the Mount Lofty Ranges and the sea, the quiet capital of South Australia and — by reputation — the most orderly, least frenetic Australian city. It was founded in 1836 as a free colony (no convicts), laid out to Colonel William Light's rigorous grid plan, and ringed entirely by parkland. That grid and those parks still define the centre: a flat, walkable square mile of wide boulevards, 19th-century sandstone and plane trees, wrapped in green.

What Adelaide lacks in Melbourne's buzz or Sydney's harbour drama, it makes up for in food, wine and festivals. The [Central Market](/australiaandpacific/australia/southaustralia/adelaide/adelaide_central_market) is one of the best produce markets in the country — a whole city block of cheese, charcuterie, oysters and migrant food stalls. Every March the city fills up for the [Adelaide Fringe](/australiaandpacific/australia/southaustralia/adelaide/adelaide_fringe) (the second-largest fringe festival in the world) and [WOMADelaide](/australiaandpacific/australia/southaustralia/adelaide/womadelaide). The nearby wine regions are the main event for many visitors: the [Barossa Valley](/australiaandpacific/australia/southaustralia/adelaide/barossa_valley), [McLaren Vale](/australiaandpacific/australia/southaustralia/adelaide/mclaren_vale) and the Clare Valley are all within an hour.

The seaside suburb of [Glenelg](/australiaandpacific/australia/southaustralia/adelaide/glenelg), reached by the old Bay Tram from [Victoria Square](/australiaandpacific/australia/southaustralia/adelaide/victoria_square), is where Adelaideans go for fish and chips on the beach. North of the city, [Hahndorf](/australiaandpacific/australia/southaustralia/adelaide/hahndorf) in the Adelaide Hills is a well-preserved German settlement with smoky beer halls. [Kangaroo Island](/australiaandpacific/australia/southaustralia/adelaide/kangaroo_island), an hour across the water, is a day or two in itself.

Summer is hot and dry; autumn is the best time for wine country and festival season.
