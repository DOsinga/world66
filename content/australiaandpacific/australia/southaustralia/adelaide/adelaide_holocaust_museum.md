The Adelaide Holocaust Museum and Steiner Education Centre is a small city-centre museum focused on Holocaust history, survivor testimony, and education. Its exhibitions are designed for reflection rather than spectacle.

Check opening times before going, as visits may require advance booking on some weekdays. It is a worthwhile stop for travellers interested in modern history and Adelaide's Jewish community.
