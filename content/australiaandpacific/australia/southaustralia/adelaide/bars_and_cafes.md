---
title: "Bars and Cafes"
type: section
---

Adelaide punches above its weight for small bars, thanks to a licensing reform around 2013 that allowed venues to open without extensive food operations. The result is a laneway bar scene concentrated in the East End and CBD: **Peel Street**, **Leigh Street**, and **Ebenezer Place** are the main strips. Most bars are small, independent, and have good local and imported wine lists.

Adelaide has strong cafe culture, with morning coffee seriousness comparable to Melbourne. The **East End** (Rundle Street east of Frome) and **O'Connell Street** in North Adelaide are the main precincts. Normans coffee on Gouger Street has been roasting locally for decades and is the old benchmark; a wave of newer specialty roasters has raised the bar.

Craft beer arrived slightly later than in the eastern states but has caught up: **Wheaty** (The Wheatsheaf Hotel) in Thebarton is the home of the Adelaide indie beer scene; **Brodburger** on Melbourne Street does good burgers to go with.
