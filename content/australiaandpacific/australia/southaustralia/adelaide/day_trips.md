Adelaide is excellently placed for day trips. The **Barossa Valley** (70 km north) and **McLaren Vale** (40 km south) are both under an hour by car and offer serious wine tasting and good food. **Hahndorf** in the Adelaide Hills is 30 minutes east for German food and a pleasant village atmosphere. **Mount Lofty** (30 minutes) gives views over the city and access to Cleland Wildlife Park, where you can hand-feed kangaroos.

Further afield, **Kangaroo Island** is a two-day trip minimum (ferry from Cape Jervis plus a day or two on the island). The **Flinders Ranges** are 5–6 hours north but reward a longer stay — Wilpena Pound and the Ikara-Flinders Ranges National Park are among the most striking landscapes in Australia.
