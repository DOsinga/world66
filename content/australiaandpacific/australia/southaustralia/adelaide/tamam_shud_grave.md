In December 1948 an unidentified man was found dead on Somerton Beach. He carried no identification, his clothing labels had been removed, and no one came forward to claim him. The case has never been solved. The body is buried in West Terrace Cemetery under a headstone that reads simply "Here lies the unknown man who was found at Somerton Beach."

The grave has become a minor site of pilgrimage for true crime enthusiasts, amateur cryptographers, and the morbidly curious. Recent DNA analysis has identified a possible family connection, but the man's identity remains officially unknown. The cemetery is open during daylight hours; the grave is signposted from the main entrance.
