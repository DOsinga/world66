---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

Adelaide's climate is Mediterranean: hot, dry summers and mild, wetter winters. **March** is the best month to visit — the summer heat has eased (though heatwaves are possible), the **Adelaide Fringe**, the **Adelaide Festival of Arts** (biennial, in even years), and **WOMADelaide** all happen in the same month, making it the most culturally alive the city gets. The Labour Day weekend in March during even-numbered years is particularly intense.

**Autumn (April–May)** is excellent: warm and settled, the festival crowds have dispersed, and the Barossa and McLaren Vale wine regions are in full harvest. **Spring (September–October)** is also pleasant. **Summer** (December–February) can be brutal — Adelaide occasionally records the highest temperature of any capital city in the world; 40°C days are not uncommon, and the city is prone to heatwaves of three or more days.

The **Adelaide Oval** hosts cricket in summer and AFL in winter. The **Clipsal 500** (now the Adelaide 500) street race runs in November or December.
