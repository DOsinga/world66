Barmera is a small Riverland town that earns its page because it is not just another Murray service stop. Its centre faces [Lake Bonney](/australiaandpacific/australia/southaustralia/barmera/lake_bonney), a broad freshwater lake used for swimming, sailing, paddling, fishing, and sunset watching.

The town also has a harder story. The [Loveday Internment Collection](/australiaandpacific/australia/southaustralia/barmera/loveday_internment_collection) explains the large World War II internment and prisoner-of-war camps that operated nearby, while the old camp sites south-west of town remind you that the Riverland's quiet orchards have complicated history under them.

Barmera is best as a relaxed day in the Riverland rather than a major destination on its own. Add [Banrock Station Wetlands](/australiaandpacific/australia/southaustralia/barmera/banrock_station_wetlands) or the irrigation history at [Cobdogla](/australiaandpacific/australia/southaustralia/barmera/cobdogla_irrigation_and_steam_museum), and it becomes a worthwhile stop between Renmark, Berri, and the Murray backroads.
