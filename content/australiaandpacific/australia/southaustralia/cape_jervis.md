Cape Jervis sits at the blunt southern end of the Fleurieu Peninsula, looking across Backstairs Passage to [Kangaroo Island](/australiaandpacific/australia/southaustralia/adelaide/kangaroo_island). For most travellers it is a ferry point rather than a destination, but the setting is better than that sounds: open water, tilted rock shelves, wind, and a feeling of being at the edge of the mainland.

The practical rhythm of the place is set by the ferry. People drive down from [Adelaide](/australiaandpacific/australia/southaustralia/adelaide), queue for Kangaroo Island, and often leave without lingering. If you have time before the crossing, the lighthouse and the rough coast around Fishery Beach give Cape Jervis a small but real reason to pause.

Do not expect much urban life. Cape Jervis is useful, scenic, and exposed, with a tavern, holiday units, and enough services for travellers in transit. Its value is as the mainland threshold to one of South Australia's best wildlife trips.
