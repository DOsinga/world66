Faye's Underground Home is one of the clearest ways to understand Coober Pedy's dugout culture. The house was excavated by hand and kept as a lived-in underground home, complete with rooms that make immediate practical sense once you step out of the desert heat.

The appeal is not polish but intimacy: you can see how mining, climate, and domestic life overlap in a town where going underground was not a gimmick. It pairs well with an opal-mine tour, but it feels more personal than most commercial mine displays.
