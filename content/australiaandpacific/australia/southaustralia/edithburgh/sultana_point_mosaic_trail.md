type: poi

The Mosaic Trail follows the coast from Edithburgh toward Sultana Point, turning a short seaside walk into something more particular to the town. Local artists decorated rocks along the path with nautical mosaics, so the route has small details to notice between the bigger views of Gulf St Vincent and Troubridge Island.

It is an easy walk or cycle rather than a wilderness trail. Start near the jetty and continue south as far as time and weather allow; the coastline becomes quieter beyond the town edge, and Sultana Point gives a good look back toward Edithburgh.
