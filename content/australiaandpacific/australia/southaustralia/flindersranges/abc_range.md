The Heysen Trail is Australia's longest marked walking trail at 1,200 kilometres, running from Cape Jervis on the Fleurieu Peninsula to Parachilna in the northern Flinders. Its most spectacular section cuts through the Flinders Ranges, following the ridges of the ABC Range around the eastern perimeter of Wilpena Pound before continuing north.

The trail can be walked in sections — a few days in the Wilpena Pound area gives a sense of its character: exposed ridge walking with broad views, descent into cool creek gullies, and camps at sites with no facilities but reliable water in good seasons. The ABC Range section above the Pound rewards a full day: the views into the bowl of the Pound from the ridge are among the best in the Flinders.
