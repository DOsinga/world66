Arkaba Station is a former sheep station turned luxury conservation property in the Flinders Ranges, about 20 kilometres north of Hawker. The 63,500-acre property has been returned to native fauna after decades of grazing — fencing out livestock has allowed the native vegetation to recover remarkably quickly, and wildlife numbers have surged.

Guided walks and drives on the property offer the chance to see yellow-footed rock-wallabies, emus, and wedge-tailed eagles in country that is both geologically dramatic and largely undisturbed. Arkaba operates as a small lodge rather than a public park — visitors book accommodation on the property — and the experience is deliberately slow, focused, and quiet.
