Arkaroo Rock is one of the Flinders Ranges' important Aboriginal rock-art sites, with ochre and charcoal images thought to be thousands of years old. The paintings are culturally significant to Adnyamathanha people, and the site is protected.

The rock shelter is reached by a short walking loop through dry ranges country. Go slowly, keep to the track, and treat the art as a living cultural place rather than just a photo stop.
