Paralana Hot Springs is a strange geological stop east of the main sanctuary base, where heated water and gases rise through mineral-rich country. It is interesting rather than pretty, and it fits Arkaroola's identity perfectly: ancient rocks still doing unusual things.

Follow current local advice before visiting. The springs are known for naturally occurring radon gas, so this is a place to look, learn, and move on rather than linger.
