---
title: Things to Do
type: section
---

Arkaroola is about landscape rather than town sights. The best days here are spent on ridges, in dry gorges, or under the night sky.

Most visitors should choose at least one guided activity, especially the Ridgetop Tour or an astronomy session. Self-guided walking is rewarding, but the country is exposed and unforgiving, so start early and carry more water than you think you need.
