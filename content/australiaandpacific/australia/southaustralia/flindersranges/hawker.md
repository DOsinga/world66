Hawker is a small town 365 kilometres north of Adelaide, sitting at the foot of the central Flinders Ranges. It functions as the last proper service point before Wilpena Pound — with a fuel station, motel, caravan park, and a good roadhouse — and most visitors pass through rather than linger. But the surrounding country repays time spent.

The Old Ghan Museum at the edge of town preserves rolling stock and memorabilia from the original Ghan railway that once ran through here. Just outside town, the Yourambulla Caves shelter Aboriginal rock engravings on the cave walls, made by the Adnyamathanha people. Hawker is also a popular launch point for scenic flights over the Flinders in small aircraft.
