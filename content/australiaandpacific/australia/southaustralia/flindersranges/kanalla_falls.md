Kanalla Falls drops over a quartzite ledge on the Edeowie Creek in the heart of the Flinders Ranges, not far from Wilpena. The falls run best in winter and spring after rain; by summer they are often reduced to a trickle or dry altogether, which is worth knowing before making a special trip.

The surrounding terrain is typical of the central Flinders: red gorge walls, native pines clinging to the slopes, and the possibility of yellow-footed rock-wallabies on the creek banks at dawn or dusk. The falls are accessible from the Wilpena Pound resort area via rough tracks.
