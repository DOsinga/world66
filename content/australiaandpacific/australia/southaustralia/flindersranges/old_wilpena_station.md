Old Wilpena Station preserves part of the pastoral history of the Flinders Ranges, with surviving 19th-century station buildings and interpretive signs explaining how European settlement reshaped this country. The site is atmospheric rather than polished, which suits the dry landscape around Wilpena Pound.

It is an easy stop if you are already driving through the central Flinders. Give yourself time to read the signs, since the meaning of the ruins is not obvious at a glance.
