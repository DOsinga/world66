Razorback Lookout is a short stop in the Flinders Ranges with a classic view toward the ridges and dry country around Wilpena Pound. It is mainly a photo stop, but the road, the folding ranges, and the clean line of track into the landscape make it worth pausing for if you are already exploring the gorges nearby.
