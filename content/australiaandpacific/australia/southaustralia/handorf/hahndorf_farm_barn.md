Hahndorf Farm Barn is a family-friendly farm attraction with cows, ponies, deer, ducks, kangaroos, sheep, goats, peacocks, reptiles, and emus. Children can get close to the animals, and the farm also offers simple extras such as pony rides, tractor rides, and picnic areas.
