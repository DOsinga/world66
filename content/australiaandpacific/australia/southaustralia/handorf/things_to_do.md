---
title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Hahndorf is compact enough to see in a day. The main street is the obvious starting point — bakeries, galleries, the Hahndorf Academy heritage centre, and craft shops fill the heritage buildings on both sides. The Cedars, Hans Heysen's studio and garden, is the most interesting stop for those with an interest in Australian art. Outside town, Hahndorf Hill Winery and several neighbouring vineyards offer cellar door tastings, and Beerenberg Farm is the place for strawberry picking in season.
