Innamincka is a speck of a settlement in the far northeast of [South Australia](/australiaandpacific/australia/southaustralia), surrounded by gibber plains, dry channels, and the wide Cooper Creek system. It is not a town in the usual sense so much as a fuel stop, pub, airstrip, and base camp for serious outback travel.

The appeal is the country around it. Waterholes along Cooper Creek break the desert open with coolabahs, birdlife, and campsites, while the tracks out to Coongie Lakes and the Strzelecki are remote enough to make preparation matter. Distances are large, roads can close after rain, and a 4WD, fuel range, water, and local advice are not optional extras.

Innamincka also sits in Burke and Wills country. Their failed 1860-61 expedition is remembered across this region, and the story still gives the landscape a human edge: ambition, bad judgement, endurance, and a long way from help.
