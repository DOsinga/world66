The Elizabeth Symon Nursing Home is a modest building, but in Innamincka modest buildings carry weight. It served an isolated pastoral district where medical help was a long way away, and it is now listed on the South Australian Heritage Register.

It will not take long to see. Pair it with a walk around the settlement and the nearby creek country for a better sense of how small and exposed life here has always been.
