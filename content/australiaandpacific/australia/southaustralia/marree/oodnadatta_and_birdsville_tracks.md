Marree sits at the southern end of the Birdsville Track and on the Oodnadatta Track, which makes the town a practical and symbolic threshold into the South Australian outback. Southbound, the road begins to feel connected to Port Augusta and Adelaide; north and east, the distances become serious.

The junction is not scenic in a postcard way, but it matters. Fuel up, check road conditions, and take the warning signs seriously. These tracks reward prepared travelers and punish casual ones.
