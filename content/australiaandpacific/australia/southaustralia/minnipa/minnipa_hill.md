Minnipa Hill is a small granite rise north of town, more local landmark than showpiece. Its interest is historical: railway-era tanks and catchments here helped supply water before the Tod River pipeline reached the district.

Visit only if you are curious about how these dry-country towns worked. Together with Pildappa and Tcharkuldu, it shows why the granite outcrops around Minnipa were more than scenery.
