The Organ Pipes are a striking volcanic rock formation in Gawler Ranges National Park, where ancient eruptions left columns of stone that resemble the pipes of an organ. The formation is part of the park's broader pattern of rugged hills, dry-country wildlife, and exposed geological history.

It is a deliberate nature stop rather than a roadside quick look. Bring water, check road conditions, and combine it with other Gawler Ranges sights if you are using Minnipa as a base for the area.
