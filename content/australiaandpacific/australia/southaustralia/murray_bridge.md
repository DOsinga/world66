Murray Bridge is the main river town southeast of [Adelaide](/australiaandpacific/australia/southaustralia/adelaide), where the old road and rail crossings pin the city to the Murray. It is not a grand town, but it has a clear river identity and a few anchors that make it more than a fuel stop on the freeway.

The headline is [Monarto Safari Park](/australiaandpacific/australia/southaustralia/murray_bridge/monarto_safari_park), a huge open-range wildlife park just west of town. Back on the river, the historic bridge, wharves, reserves, and low cliffs make Murray Bridge a good place for a slow walk, a paddle, or a short cruise.

The local history is tied to river traffic and the first bridge across the Murray in South Australia. [The Round House](/australiaandpacific/australia/southaustralia/murray_bridge/the_round_house) and [Captain's Cottage Museum](/australiaandpacific/australia/southaustralia/murray_bridge/captains_cottage_museum) keep that story grounded in real buildings rather than plaques.

Come with modest expectations and it works well: a wildlife park, a riverfront, and a bit of Murray history in an easy day from Adelaide.
