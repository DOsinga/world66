Captain's Cottage Museum is a small local-history stop rather than a polished major museum. Its value is in the detail: river trade, agriculture, transport, and the families who shaped the district around the crossing.

Pair it with the Round House if you want the town's history to make sense. Check opening times before going, as visits are often by arrangement or tied to local open days.
