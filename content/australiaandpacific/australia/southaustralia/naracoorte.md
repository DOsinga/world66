Naracoorte is the inland Limestone Coast town that happens to sit beside one of Australia's great fossil sites. The streets themselves are practical and country-town plain, but the surrounding anchors are strong enough to make it more than a service stop.

The obvious reason to come is [Naracoorte Caves National Park](/australiaandpacific/australia/southaustralia/naracoorte/naracoorte_caves_national_park), South Australia's World Heritage fossil site, where cave tours explain extinct megafauna, fossil chambers, bats, and limestone formation. Add [Bool Lagoon](/australiaandpacific/australia/southaustralia/naracoorte/bool_lagoon_game_reserve) after rain or in bird-rich seasons, and the town becomes a useful base for both deep time and wetland life.

Naracoorte also has enough in-town texture to fill the quieter hours: [The Sheep's Back Museum](/australiaandpacific/australia/southaustralia/naracoorte/the_sheeps_back_museum) tells the wool story that shaped the district, while the [Naracoorte Regional Art Gallery](/australiaandpacific/australia/southaustralia/naracoorte/naracoorte_regional_art_gallery) gives the main street a small but real cultural stop.
