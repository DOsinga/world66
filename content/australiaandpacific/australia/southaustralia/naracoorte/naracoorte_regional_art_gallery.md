Naracoorte Regional Art Gallery is a small public gallery in a historic town building, with local and visiting exhibitions and a permanent collection of Australian art. It is not the reason to drive to Naracoorte, but it gives the town centre a worthwhile cultural stop.

Drop in after the caves or Sheep's Back Museum if the doors are open. It is a useful reminder that Naracoorte is not only a base for sites outside town.
