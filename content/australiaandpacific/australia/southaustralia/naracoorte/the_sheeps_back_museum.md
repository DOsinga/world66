The Sheep's Back Museum uses a restored flour mill and adjoining buildings to tell Naracoorte's wool and farming story. It covers shearing, transport, settlement, local trades, and the social history that came with the district's grazing economy.

It is the right museum for this town: practical, regional, and rooted in work rather than nostalgia alone. Pair it with the caves to see both the ancient and modern layers of the Limestone Coast.
