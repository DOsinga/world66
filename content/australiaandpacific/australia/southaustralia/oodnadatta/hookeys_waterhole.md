Hookeys Waterhole is a small but meaningful stop on Neales Creek south of Oodnadatta. In this dry country, water gives a place weight, and the surrounding area is part of Arabana cultural country.

For travellers, it is a quiet reminder that the track is not just empty space between roadhouses. Check local conditions before heading out, especially after rain, and leave the place cleaner than you found it.
