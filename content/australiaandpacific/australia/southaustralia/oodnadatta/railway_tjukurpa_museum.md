The Railway Tjukurpa Museum occupies the old Oodnadatta railway station, the clearest reminder of the period when the Ghan came through town. The building, goods shed, and railway remains make the past feel solid rather than theoretical.

Inside, the displays cover Oodnadatta's railway history and the outback lives tied to it. It is the best first stop in town if you want to understand why this tiny place mattered.
