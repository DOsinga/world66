Port Broughton is a small fishing and holiday town at the northern end of Yorke Peninsula, looking over the sheltered shallows of Spencer Gulf. It is not grand, and that is the point: the pace is slow, the water is tidal, and the town still feels tied to boats, grain, crabs, and summer shacks.

The spare-day case is simple. Walk the [historic jetty](/australiaandpacific/australia/southaustralia/port_broughton/port_broughton_jetty), follow the coastal path toward [Fisherman Bay](/australiaandpacific/australia/southaustralia/port_broughton/fisherman_bay_walk), browse the [Heritage Centre](/australiaandpacific/australia/southaustralia/port_broughton/port_broughton_heritage_centre), then sit with the gulf in front of you. It is a good pause if you are working your way around Yorke Peninsula or cutting between the Copper Coast and Port Augusta.

Port Broughton is less convincing as a long detour from Adelaide. Treat it as a small coastal day rather than a bucket-list destination.
