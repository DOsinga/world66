The walk to Fisherman Bay follows the coast north from Port Broughton Jetty, passing boat ramps, tidal flats, low peninsulas, and the small holiday settlement at the far end.

It is a gentle route rather than a dramatic hike. Take sun protection, water, and tide awareness; the pleasure is in the open gulf light and the slow shift from town to shack country.
