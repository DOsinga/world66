Port Broughton Heritage Centre is a small local museum, the kind that makes most sense once you have seen the jetty and the foreshore. Its displays fill in the story of a town built around farming hinterland, fishing boats, and gulf transport.

Opening hours can be limited, so check before making it the centre of the day.
