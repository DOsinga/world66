Clinton Conservation Park protects a stretch of Samphire Coast north of Port Wakefield, where mangroves, saltmarsh, and shallow tidal water make better habitat than the flat landscape first suggests. Yorke Peninsula tourism promotes the area for birdwatching, with shorebirds feeding along the gulf edge.

It is not dramatic scenery. Bring binoculars and patience, and it becomes a quiet counterpoint to the fast highway just inland.
