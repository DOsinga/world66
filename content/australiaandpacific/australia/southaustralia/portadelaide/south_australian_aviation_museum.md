The South Australian Aviation Museum at Port Adelaide displays aircraft, engines, and equipment tracing the history of aviation in South Australia and Australia more broadly. The collection ranges from early biplanes and World War II aircraft to commercial airliners and modern military jets, all displayed in a large indoor hangar that protects them from the elements.

The museum is volunteer-run and reflects that in its atmosphere — there's genuine enthusiasm here, and knowledgeable guides are often on hand to explain the exhibits. The highlight for many visitors is the collection of complete aircraft that can be viewed at close quarters, including several that are still in airworthy condition. Weekends sometimes feature engine runs and flying demonstrations.
