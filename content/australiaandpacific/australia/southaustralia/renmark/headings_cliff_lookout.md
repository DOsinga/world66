Headings Cliff Lookout gives Renmark its best landscape moment. From the viewing area above the river, the Murray bends below pale limestone cliffs, with red gums and dry Riverland country stretching away behind it.

It is a short drive from Paringa, not a wilderness expedition. That ease is part of the appeal: you can add a strong view and a sense of the Murray's scale to a Renmark day without needing to plan a full regional trip.
