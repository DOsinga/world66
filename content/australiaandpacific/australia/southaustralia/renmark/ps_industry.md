PS Industry is the riverfront anchor that makes Renmark feel like a Murray town rather than just a service centre. The restored paddle steamer is moored by the visitor centre and runs cruises on selected dates, often the first Sunday of the month.

Even when it is not cruising, it is worth stopping here first. The boat puts Renmark's history of river work, irrigation, and houseboat travel into one compact, handsome object.
