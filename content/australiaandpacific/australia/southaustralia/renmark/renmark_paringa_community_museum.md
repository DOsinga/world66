The Renmark Paringa Community Museum is a local history museum covering the river towns of Renmark and Paringa. Its collection leans toward farming, irrigation, river life, and the machinery that shaped settlement along this stretch of the Murray.

It is a modest stop, but useful if you want context before visiting the Paringa Bridge, the PS Industry, or the old homestead sites around Renmark. The appeal is in the practical detail: tools, equipment, and stories from a working river district rather than polished big-city displays.
