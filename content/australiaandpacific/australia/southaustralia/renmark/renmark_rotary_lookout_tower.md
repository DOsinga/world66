Renmark Rotary Lookout Tower stands northwest of town, high enough to give a full sweep over the Riverland's irrigated farms, dry country and Renmark itself. It is a quick stop rather than a destination, but the 360-degree view helps make sense of how the Murray has shaped this corner of South Australia.
