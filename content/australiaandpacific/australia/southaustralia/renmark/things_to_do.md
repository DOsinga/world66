---

Renmark is at its best when you follow the Murray rather than treating the town as a highway stop. Start on the riverfront with the [PS Industry](/australiaandpacific/australia/southaustralia/renmark/ps_industry), then cross or watch the [Paringa Bridge](/australiaandpacific/australia/southaustralia/renmark/paringa_bridge) lift for larger boats.

The town's land story is just as important. [Olivewood Historic Homestead](/australiaandpacific/australia/southaustralia/renmark/olivewood_historic_homestead) explains the Chaffey irrigation colony, and [Headings Cliff Lookout](/australiaandpacific/australia/southaustralia/renmark/headings_cliff_lookout), a short drive beyond Paringa, is the best nearby view of the Murray cutting through the Riverland.
