Little Dip Conservation Park begins just south of Robe and protects a rugged strip of dunes, coastal lakes, heath, and beaches. The lakes range from salty shallows to deeper freshwater, attracting birds and giving the park more variety than a simple beach reserve.

This is the place to stretch Robe into a real day. Tracks vary, some beach access needs care, and the park feels pleasantly rough compared with the town foreshore.
