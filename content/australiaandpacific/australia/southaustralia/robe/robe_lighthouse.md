Robe Lighthouse is a plain modern concrete tower from 1972, built to replace the old Cape Jaffa light. It is not romantic architecture, but it sits in a fine coastal position and helps tie together the headland walk.

The better reason to come is the setting: low cliffs, wind, and open Southern Ocean views. Pair it with the obelisk rather than treating it as a standalone sight.
