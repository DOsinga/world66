title: Getting There
type: section
---

Tumby Bay sits on the Lincoln Highway, about 50 km north of Port Lincoln and roughly 600 km by road from [Adelaide](/australiaandpacific/australia/southaustralia/adelaide). Most travellers arrive by car as part of an Eyre Peninsula road trip.

Regional flights to Port Lincoln put you within an easy drive, but you will still want a car to make sense of the coast.
