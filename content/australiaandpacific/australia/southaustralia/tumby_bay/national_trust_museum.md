Tumby Bay's National Trust Museum is a modest local-history stop, the kind of place that depends on volunteers and shelves of carefully saved objects. It is useful because the town's story is otherwise easy to reduce to beach and boats.

Expect farming, fishing, household, and settlement material rather than a polished regional museum. Check opening times locally before making a special trip.
