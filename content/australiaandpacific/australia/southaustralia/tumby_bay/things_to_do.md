---
title: Things to Do
type: section
---

Tumby Bay is best kept simple: silo art, foreshore, jetty, beach, and a small museum. The town's public art gives you a reason to wander the streets, while the water gives the visit its pace.

Bring sun protection and do not over-plan it. This is an Eyre Peninsula rest day with a few good anchors, not a dense sightseeing city.
