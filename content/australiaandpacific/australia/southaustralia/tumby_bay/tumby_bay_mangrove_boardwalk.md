The mangrove boardwalk is a small change of scene from Tumby Bay's beach and jetty. It runs through the low coastal vegetation south of town, where the tide and birdlife do more of the work than any formal interpretation.

Do it as a short walk rather than a destination in itself. It pairs well with the silo art, since both sit away from the main foreshore and help round out a slow visit.
