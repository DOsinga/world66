The Tumby Bay silo mural is the town's headline sight. Painted by Martin Ron with Matt Gorrick and completed in 2018, it shows local children leaping from the jetty, a subject that fits the town far better than an abstract landmark would.

The silos stand near the entrance to town at Lincoln Highway and Bratten Road, with a viewing area set back from the road. It is a quick stop, but a good one, especially in late afternoon light.
