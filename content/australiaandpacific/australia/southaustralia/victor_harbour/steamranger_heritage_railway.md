SteamRanger is the heritage railway behind Victor Harbor's classic Cockle Train, the coastal run toward Goolwa and the Murray Mouth. It is part transport, part nostalgia: vintage carriages, sea views, and a pace that suits the Fleurieu Peninsula better than a hurried drive.

The railway is especially good for families or anyone building a day around the waterfront. Check the timetable before turning up, because services vary by season and some special steam runs operate separately from the regular heritage trips.
