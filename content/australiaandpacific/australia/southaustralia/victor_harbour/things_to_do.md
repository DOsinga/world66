---
title: Things to Do
type: section
---

Victor Harbor's main attractions are natural and coastal: whale watching in season, the horse-drawn tram crossing to Granite Island, the headland walk to Rosetta Head (The Bluff), and the generally unhurried character of the foreshore. The town has an old-fashioned Australian seaside quality that makes it a popular weekend escape from [Adelaide](/australiaandpacific/australia/southaustralia/adelaide), and it's genuinely pleasant to poke around without an itinerary.
