Woomera is a compact town, so the main stops sit close together. [Woomera Missile Park](/australiaandpacific/australia/southaustralia/woomera/woomera_missile_park), [Woomera Heritage Centre](/australiaandpacific/australia/southaustralia/woomera/woomera_heritage_centre), and [Woomera History Museum](/australiaandpacific/australia/southaustralia/woomera/woomera_history_museum) cover the rocket range story from different angles. [Woomera Baker Observatory](/australiaandpacific/australia/southaustralia/woomera/woomera_baker_observatory) is the bonus if it is open.
