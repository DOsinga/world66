Woomera Baker Observatory makes good use of the town's dark outback sky. It is a small volunteer-run observatory rather than a daily attraction, so check current opening before planning around it.

If it is open, it adds the right kind of night activity to Woomera: quiet, technical, and directly tied to the desert sky above the range.
