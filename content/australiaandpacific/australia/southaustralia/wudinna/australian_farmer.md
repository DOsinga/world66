The Australian Farmer is Wudinna's most visible landmark, standing beside the Eyre Highway in polished local granite. It is big, earnest, and very country-town Australian, but it works because the subject belongs here.

Stop for a few minutes rather than just driving past. The details in the carving point to wheat, sheep, machinery, and family labour, all of which explain the landscape you have been crossing.
