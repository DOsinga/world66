Pildappa Rock is often compared with Wave Rock in Western Australia, and the comparison is fair enough: its curved granite faces fold up from the plain in long, smooth walls. It is less famous, quieter, and all the better for that.

You can walk around the base or climb carefully to the top for broad views over farms and mallee. The access road is unsealed, so slow down and check local conditions after rain.
