Tcharkuldu Rock is smaller in profile than Pildappa but more intricate up close. The granite is scattered with boulders, hollows, and honeycomb-like tafoni weathering, making it a good place to wander slowly rather than simply climb and leave.

The old water-collection works add a human layer to the geology. Early farming settlements had to harvest water wherever the granite allowed it, and the channels and dam here make that problem easy to understand.
