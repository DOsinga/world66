Bicheno Penguin Tours is the town's signature evening activity. The guided visit takes about an hour and is timed for dusk, when little penguins come ashore after feeding at sea.

The value is not just seeing penguins, but seeing them with someone managing lights, distance, and visitor behaviour. Book ahead in busy periods and bring warm clothes; east-coast evenings can turn cold quickly.
