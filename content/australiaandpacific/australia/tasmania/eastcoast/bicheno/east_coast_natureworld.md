East Coast Natureworld sits in coastal bushland and lagoons just north of Bicheno. It is a straightforward wildlife park, but a useful one if you want a close look at Tasmanian devils, wombats, wallabies, and other native animals without relying on luck beside the road.

The setting is relaxed rather than slick. Time your visit for a keeper talk or devil feeding if you can, then use it as a short wildlife stop before heading back to the coast.
