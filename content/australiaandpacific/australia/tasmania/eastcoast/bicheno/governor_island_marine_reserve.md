Governor Island Marine Reserve protects the waters just off Bicheno, including small granite islands and reef habitat. It is best known to divers for kelp, sponges, fish life, and cold-water visibility that can be excellent in settled weather.

This is not a casual beach snorkel for everyone; conditions and water temperature matter. Go with a local operator unless you already know Tasmanian temperate diving well.
