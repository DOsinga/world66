Cape Tourville is the easy Freycinet walk: a short circuit around a lighthouse headland with big views over the Tasman Sea, the Hazards, and the peninsula's outer coast. It is a good choice when you want scenery without committing to the Wineglass Bay climb.

The boardwalk keeps the route simple, and the exposed coast gives it drama in rough weather. Look out for seabirds, passing boats, and, in season, whales offshore.
