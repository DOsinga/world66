Sea kayaking is one of the best ways to feel Coles Bay rather than just look at it. The sheltered water below the Hazards lets you paddle past beaches, moored boats, and granite slopes that change colour through the day.

Conditions still matter, especially outside the inner bay, so use a local operator unless you know the coast well. Sunset trips are popular for good reason.
