---
title: "Eating Out"
type: section
---

St Helens has a small but increasingly serious eating scene, driven by the bike crowd at Blue Derby and the steady summer trade from the coast. The main street has the heaviest concentration; Binalong Bay and Pyengana have a couple of standouts.

**Sea Salt and Soda** on Quail Street has been the local favourite for casual sit-down meals — fresh seafood, brunch, decent coffee. **Captains Catch** on the wharf cooks the day's flathead and scallops; eat on the deck overlooking the boat ramp. For oysters, **Pure Tasmania** on the bay shore sells direct from the lease at off-market prices. At Binalong Bay, the **Bay of Fires Bistro** has the best dinner table on the coast (book ahead in season).
