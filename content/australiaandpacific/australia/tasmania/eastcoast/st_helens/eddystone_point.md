Eddystone Point marks the northern end of the Bay of Fires, in the **Mount William National Park**. The pink-granite lighthouse stands on a low headland of bleached, lichen-stained boulders, with surf beaches running south and north and almost no other development for kilometres in either direction.

The drive from St Helens takes around 90 minutes; the last stretch is unsealed but well-graded. The park has free camping at Stumpys Bay and Picnic Rocks. Bring everything you need — there are no shops, no fuel, often no phone signal.
