---
title: "Getting There"
type: section
---

St Helens is at the end of the **Tasman Highway (A3)** — about 2 hours 30 minutes north of Hobart's airport (via the inland route), or 2 hours 30 from Launceston via Scottsdale and the **Sideling** lookout. Both are scenic drives; the Launceston route is shorter and easier on the eye.

**St Helens Airport** (HLS) takes occasional charter flights but no scheduled passenger services — fly to Launceston (LST) or Hobart (HBA) and drive. Several Tasmanian campervan-hire companies offer one-way pickups.

There is no rail and **no useful public bus** to St Helens — Tassielink's run from Launceston is once a day at best. A car is essential for the Bay of Fires and Blue Derby. Roads are sealed and well-maintained; allow time for the bends.
