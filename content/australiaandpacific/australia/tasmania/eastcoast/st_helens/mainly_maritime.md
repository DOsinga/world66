Mainly Maritime is a small maritime museum in St Helens with a private collection of naval and marine artefacts gathered over decades. The displays range across several centuries, with material connected to warships, seafaring, and marine history.

It is a focused stop rather than a large museum, best paired with the St Helens History Room or a wander around Georges Bay.
