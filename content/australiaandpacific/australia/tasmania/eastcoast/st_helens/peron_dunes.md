The Peron Dunes are the high, pale sand dunes on the St Helens Point side of Georges Bay, close enough to town for an easy outing but wild enough to feel like the coast has suddenly opened up. They are part of the same sandy headland as St Helens Point Conservation Area, but the dunes have their own character: wind-scoured ridges, ocean views, and soft sand that changes shape after bad weather.

Tasmania Parks and Wildlife manages the area as a designated four-wheel-driving site, and drivers need to treat it seriously: soft sand, steep entry points, and tyre deflation are part of the deal. You do not need a vehicle to appreciate the place, though. A walk up the dunes gives a quick, physical sense of the east coast's scale and weather.
