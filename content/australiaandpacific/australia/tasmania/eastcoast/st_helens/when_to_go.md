---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

**December to April** is the busy season — Tasmanian and mainland summer holidays, the warmest sea temperatures (still only 17–19°C — wetsuits help), and the most reliable weather. January is the busiest month; if you can travel in late February or March you'll get similar weather with fewer crowds.

**Autumn (March–May)** is excellent for mountain biking at Blue Derby — cool, dry, no flies — and the rainforest at Pyengana is at its lushest after summer rain.

**Winter (June–August)** is quiet, often crisp and sunny in this rain-shadowed corner of Tasmania, and good for serious bushwalkers and surfers. Many tourist businesses close mid-week.

The **St Helens Game Fishing Classic** is in late February; the **Suncoast Jazz Festival** in late August fills the town for a weekend.
