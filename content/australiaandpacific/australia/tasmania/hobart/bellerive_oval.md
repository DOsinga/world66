Bellerive Oval, commercially known as Blundstone Arena, is Hobart's main cricket ground and one of Tasmania's major sporting venues. Its eastern-shore setting gives it a different perspective on the city, with the Derwent and mountain backdrop close by.

It is most relevant when a cricket match or major event is scheduled, rather than as an empty stadium visit. For sports travellers, a game here can be a strong local experience and an easy reason to cross the river from central Hobart.
