---
title: "Shopping"
type: section
---

The Saturday morning **Salamanca Market** is the shopping event of the week — Tasmanian produce, hardwood crafts, woollens, ceramics, jewellery from local makers, and street food. Outside of Saturdays, Salamanca Place itself stays full of small galleries and design shops. North Hobart's Elizabeth Street has independent bookshops and vintage. For everyday shopping, the Elizabeth Street Mall and adjacent arcades cover the basics in the CBD.
