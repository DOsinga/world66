Hobart's attractions cluster in three places. The waterfront and Salamanca run together along the Derwent, sandstone warehouses turned galleries, restaurants, and the Saturday market. Battery Point sits up the hill behind them, the convict-era seafarers' quarter that has changed less than anywhere else in the city. And twenty minutes north, on a peninsula in the Derwent, sits MONA — the strangest and most important art museum in Australia.
