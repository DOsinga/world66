type: section
---

Launceston is a city worth slowing down in. The Victorian streetscapes and [Cataract Gorge](/australiaandpacific/australia/tasmania/launceston/cataract_gorge) together make for an unusually rewarding day. [City Park](/australiaandpacific/australia/tasmania/launceston/city_park) is the surprising green heart of the CBD, with its colony of Japanese macaques. The [Queen Victoria Museum and Art Gallery](/australiaandpacific/australia/tasmania/launceston/queen_victoria_museum) covers Tasmanian natural history and art across two sites.

The [Old Umbrella Shop](/australiaandpacific/australia/tasmania/launceston/old_umbrella_shop) on George Street is a Victorian time capsule that still sells umbrellas. [Franklin House](/australiaandpacific/australia/tasmania/launceston/franklin_house) south of the city is the finest Georgian house in northern Tasmania. The [National Automobile Museum](/australiaandpacific/australia/tasmania/launceston/national_automobile_museum) has a rotating collection of vintage cars — the exhibits change regularly because all vehicles are privately owned. [Yorktown Square](/australiaandpacific/australia/tasmania/launceston/yorktown_square) is the restored warehouse district good for coffee and browsing.
