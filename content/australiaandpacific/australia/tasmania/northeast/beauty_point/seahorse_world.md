Seahorse World is part aquarium, part working seahorse breeding facility. The guided route moves from display tanks to the production side, where seahorses at different stages of growth make the operation more interesting than a normal small aquarium.

The touch pool and local marine tanks broaden the visit, especially for children. It pairs naturally with Platypus House next door.
