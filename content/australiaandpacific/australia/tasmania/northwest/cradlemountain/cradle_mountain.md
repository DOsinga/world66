Cradle Mountain is the peak everyone comes to see: a serrated dolerite ridge rising above Dove Lake and the surrounding moorland. On a clear morning it looks almost staged, with the lake below and the mountain catching light before the car parks fill.

The summit walk is a serious route, not a casual extension of the lake circuit. It involves steep, exposed, boulder-hopping terrain and should only be attempted in stable weather by walkers with enough time, gear, and confidence to turn back.
