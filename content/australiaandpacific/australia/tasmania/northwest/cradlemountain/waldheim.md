Waldheim is tied to Gustav and Kate Weindorfer, whose early-20th-century home and hospitality helped bring wider attention to Cradle Mountain. The name means "forest home", and the surrounding short walks still feel like a sheltered pocket within the alpine landscape.

It is worth a stop for context rather than spectacle. Cradle Mountain is often sold as pure wilderness, but Waldheim reminds you that the park also has a human history of advocacy, tourism, and changing ideas about what should be protected.
