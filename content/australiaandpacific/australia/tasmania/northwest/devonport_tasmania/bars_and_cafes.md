---
title: "Bars and Cafes"
type: section
---

Devonport is a small city with a modest but solid café and pub scene. The best coffee is on Rooke Street, where a cluster of independent cafes serves as the social heart of the town centre. The waterfront area has a few pubs that cater to ferry passengers and locals alike — unpretentious places with a cold pint and a direct view of the Mersey.
