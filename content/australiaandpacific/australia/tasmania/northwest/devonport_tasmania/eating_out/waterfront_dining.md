The waterfront strip near the Spirit of Tasmania terminal has a handful of restaurants and fish-and-chip shops with views across the Mersey. Convenient for an early meal before an evening ferry departure, or a post-arrival breakfast if you've come in on the overnight crossing. The fish and chips are reliably good — flathead and barramundi are common — and there are a few sit-down options for those wanting something more substantial. Nothing memorable in itself, but practical and well-positioned.
