The Tasmanian Arboretum spreads across a quiet site near Devonport, with collections of Tasmanian and international trees, lakes and walking paths. It is a gentle outdoor stop that suits travelers arriving from or waiting for the Bass Strait ferry.

The arboretum is more about slow wandering than big set-piece displays. Look for birdlife and seasonal color, and allow enough time for the grounds rather than expecting a compact formal garden.
