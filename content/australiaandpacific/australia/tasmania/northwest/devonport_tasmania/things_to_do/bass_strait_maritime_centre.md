The Bass Strait Maritime Centre on the Devonport waterfront covers the seafaring history of one of the world's more treacherous stretches of water: wrecks, lighthouse keepers, the evolution of the Tasmanian ferry trade, and the lives of the fishing and coastal communities that depended on the strait. Well-curated for a regional museum, and well-suited to a rainy morning before hitting the road. The building itself sits close to the water near the ferry terminal precinct.
