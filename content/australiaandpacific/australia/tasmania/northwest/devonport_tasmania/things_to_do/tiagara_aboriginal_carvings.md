Tiagarra is an Aboriginal cultural centre and rock art site on Mersey Bluff, and one of the most accessible places in Tasmania to see ancient engravings. The petroglyphs — including maireener shell engravings and other incised designs — are among the finest examples of Tasmanian Aboriginal rock art and are thought to be thousands of years old. A small on-site museum provides context on Tasmanian Aboriginal history and culture. The site is managed in recognition of its significance to the Palawa people. Worth visiting before or after walking the bluff, as the two are immediately adjacent.
