King Solomons Cave is smaller and drier than Marakoopa, but it packs a lot of limestone decoration into a compact tour. The stalactites, shawls, and calcite formations are the reason to come.

It works well as the second cave of the day. The drive between King Solomons and Marakoopa is short, but allow enough time between guided tours so the caves do not blur into one rushed underground checklist.
