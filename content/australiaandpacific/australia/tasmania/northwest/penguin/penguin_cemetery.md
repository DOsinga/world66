The town cemetery sits on a hilltop just west of Penguin, gazetted in 1897 and still in use. The interest for a visitor is mostly the view — across the rooftops of the town to Bass Strait and the offshore islands — and the headstones of the original settler families, many bearing English Methodist and Scottish surnames from the 1880s onwards.

A quiet stop on the way out of town. Take the back road that climbs up from the western end of the foreshore.
