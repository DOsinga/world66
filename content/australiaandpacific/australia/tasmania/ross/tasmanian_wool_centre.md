The Tasmanian Wool Centre combines a small museum, visitor information desk, and shop in the middle of Ross. The museum is modest, but it gives useful context for the Midlands: sheep runs, shearing, wool classing, and the pastoral wealth that helped shape the village.

It is also the most practical stop in town if you want local information or a Tasmanian wool purchase that is not just another souvenir. Pair it with a walk to the bridge and Female Factory rather than treating it as a destination on its own.
