Scots Uniting Church is a quiet colonial-era church from 1842, designed by James Blackburn and often noted as an unusual Romanesque building in Australia. It is a small sight rather than a destination in itself, but the stonework and simple proportions make it worth a look if you are exploring the Sorell area.

The church is closed, so visits are limited to viewing the exterior from a respectful distance.
