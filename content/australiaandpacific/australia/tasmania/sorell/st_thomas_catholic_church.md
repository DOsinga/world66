St Thomas' Catholic Church is a heritage-listed church in Sorell, designed by the Tasmanian architect Henry Hunter in 1864. Its stonework and simple Gothic Revival form make it one of the town's more appealing historic buildings.

It is a short stop, best combined with Sorell's other colonial-era buildings and nearby coastal drives.
