---
title: When to Go
type: section
---

Sorell is on the relatively dry, sheltered south-east coast of Tasmania, and the climate is milder than the Hobart side of the channel. **Summer** (December–February) is warm, dry, and the best season for the surrounding beaches and the surrounding bushland; daytime temperatures rarely exceed 25°C and evenings cool quickly.

**Spring and autumn** are pleasant, with crisp mornings and long sunny afternoons; spring (September–November) is also the start of the migratory shorebird season at **Orielton Lagoon**, when sandpipers and stints arrive from the northern hemisphere. **Winter** is cool and grey, with occasional frost, but rarely properly cold; the surrounding bushland looks dramatic in winter light.

The **Sorell Fruit Farm** is open from mid-October to early May for picking — strawberries peak in November and December, stone fruit through January, blackberries and tayberries into late summer.
