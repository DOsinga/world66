Devils Kitchen is a deep cleft in the sea cliffs, where waves churn far below the viewing platform. It is less elegant than Tasman Arch and more violent, especially when the swell is up. The short track between the two lookouts gives you one of the best quick tastes of Tasman National Park's cliff scenery.
