Tasman Arch is a natural bridge left behind after the roof of a sea cave partly collapsed. From the lookout you can see the ocean moving through the gap below, with forest still growing across the top of the arch. It is only a short walk from the car park and pairs naturally with nearby Devils Kitchen.
