The Tasman Blowhole is a collapsed sea cave where swell surges into a narrow opening and throws spray into the air. On a calm day it is still a good look at the coast's fractured rock; in rough weather it becomes loud, wet, and much more memorable. The easy paths and nearby food van make it the simplest of the Eaglehawk Neck rock formations to visit.
