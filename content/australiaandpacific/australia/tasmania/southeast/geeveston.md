Geeveston is a small Huon Valley town south of Hobart, with a forestry past that still shows in its visitor centre, timber displays, and carved figures around the streets. It is not grand, but it has a clear identity: this is where the settled valley starts giving way to wet forest and the edge of Tasmania's southwest.

The town itself gives you the [Platypus Walk](/australiaandpacific/australia/tasmania/southeast/geeveston/platypus_walk), Heritage Park, and timber history. The bigger reason to keep going is nearby forest: [Tahune Airwalk](/australiaandpacific/australia/tasmania/southeast/geeveston/tahune_airwalk) for an easy canopy-level day, or [Hartz Mountains National Park](/australiaandpacific/australia/tasmania/southeast/geeveston/hartz_mountains_national_park) for alpine lakes and a much wilder feel.

Geeveston is best as a slow day from Hobart or as a base for the far south. It clears the bar because the town and its nearby forests make one coherent trip, not just a petrol stop on the way to somewhere better.
