Maydena is a tiny forestry village west of Hobart, close to the rainforest and karst country around Mount Field. It is not a polished resort town, and that is part of the point: a few streets, wet forest, mountain air, and enough oddball activity to justify the drive if you like the outdoors.

The strongest reason to come is riding. [Maydena Bike Park](/australiaandpacific/australia/tasmania/southeast/maydena/maydena_bike_park) has turned the surrounding slopes into one of Tasmania's serious downhill mountain-bike stops, with shuttle access and trails that run from mellow to very steep. If that sounds too intense, [Railtrack Riders](/australiaandpacific/australia/tasmania/southeast/maydena/railtrack_riders) offers a gentler kind of rail nostalgia on pedal-powered carts.

The village also sits beside good cave and rainforest country. [Junee Cave State Reserve](/australiaandpacific/australia/tasmania/southeast/maydena/junee_cave_state_reserve) is a short walk through wet forest to the mouth of a cave system tied to some of Australia's deepest underground passages. Maydena works best as a half-day or full-day side trip from Hobart, or as a low-key base if you have brought a bike.
