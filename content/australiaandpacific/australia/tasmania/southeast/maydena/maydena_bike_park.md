Maydena Bike Park is the reason many travellers now know the village. The park uses the steep forested slopes above town for shuttle-served mountain-bike trails, with long descents, technical lines, jump trails, and easier routes for riders still building confidence.

It is not a casual town bike path, so check the trail grades before committing. For experienced riders, though, it is one of Tasmania's most satisfying places to spend a muddy, fast day.
