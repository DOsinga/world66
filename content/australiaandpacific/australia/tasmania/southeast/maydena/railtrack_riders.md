Railtrack Riders runs pedal-powered rail carts along a disused stretch of track from Maydena into the surrounding valley. It is part light exercise, part railway oddity, and part slow look at the bush and old timber-country landscape around town.

The ride is especially good if you want something more memorable than another short roadside stop. Book ahead in quiet periods, since operations are small and weather can change the plan.
