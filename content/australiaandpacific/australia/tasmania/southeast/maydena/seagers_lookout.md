Seagers Lookout is a mountain viewpoint above Lake Fenton in Mount Field National Park, reached from the high country road west of Maydena. The draw is the broad view over the lake, forest, and surrounding peaks rather than a built-up attraction.

It works well as a quick stop if you are already driving into the alpine part of the park, or as the start of a longer walk when the weather is clear.
