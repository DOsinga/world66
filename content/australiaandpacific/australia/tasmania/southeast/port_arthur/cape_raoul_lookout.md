Cape Raoul Lookout is one of the dramatic viewpoints on the Tasman Peninsula, reached by a long return walk through coastal bush. The reward is a wide view over cliffs, sea stacks, and the exposed southern coastline.

The walk is a serious outing rather than a roadside stop, so allow several hours and carry water, weather layers, and enough daylight for the return.
