Remarkable Cave is a wave-cut sea cavern near Maingon Bay, reached by a short return walk from the lookout. The opening is famous for its map-like outline of Tasmania, and the surrounding cliffs give the stop more drama than its easy access suggests.

The track is brief but includes plenty of stairs, so the climb back is the part you feel. Come for the cave, but leave time to look out across the rough Tasman Peninsula coast.
