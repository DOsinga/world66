Remarkable Cave is a sea-carved opening south of Port Arthur, reached by steps from the lookout area. The cave is known for the shape of its seaward gap and for the dramatic meeting of surf, sandstone, and cliff, making it a short but memorable add-on to the Tasman Peninsula's historic sites and coastal walks.
