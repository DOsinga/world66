---
title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Zeehan is strongest when you treat it as a mining-history town rather than a quick fuel stop. Start with the [West Coast Heritage Centre](/australiaandpacific/australia/tasmania/westcoast/zeehan/west_coast_heritage_centre), which is big enough to take a proper chunk of the day. Then cross into the [Gaiety Theatre](/australiaandpacific/australia/tasmania/westcoast/zeehan/gaiety_theatre), walk or drive out to the [Spray Tunnel](/australiaandpacific/australia/tasmania/westcoast/zeehan/spray_tunnel), and leave time for the old graves at [Zeehan Pioneer Cemetery](/australiaandpacific/australia/tasmania/westcoast/zeehan/zeehan_pioneer_cemetery).
