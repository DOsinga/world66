The West Coast Heritage Centre is the reason most travellers should stop in Zeehan. It is not a single-room local museum, but a large complex of old buildings, railway pieces, mining machinery, mineral collections, photographs, and recreated rooms from the west coast's boom years.

The old Zeehan School of Mines and Metallurgy gives the place real weight. This was a mining town that needed technical knowledge, not just luck, and the displays make the labour, money, and risk of the silver years feel close.
