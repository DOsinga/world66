Gum San Chinese Heritage Centre is Ararat's most distinctive museum. It tells the story of Chinese miners who crossed southern Australia during the gold rush and whose discovery at Canton Lead helped give the town its start.

The exhibits are straightforward rather than glossy, but the subject matters. Ararat is rare among Australian towns in putting Chinese goldfields history at the centre of its origin story.
