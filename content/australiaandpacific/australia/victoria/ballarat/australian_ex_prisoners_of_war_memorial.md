The Australian Ex-Prisoners of War Memorial stands beside Lake Wendouree and has the gravity of a national site rather than a local monument. Long granite walls carry the names of Australians held prisoner in conflicts from the Boer War to Korea, making the scale of captivity and survival physically hard to ignore.

Its setting near the Ballarat Botanical Gardens makes it easy to visit, but it deserves more than a passing glance. The design uses water, basalt, and a long processional path to slow you down, which is exactly right for the subject.
