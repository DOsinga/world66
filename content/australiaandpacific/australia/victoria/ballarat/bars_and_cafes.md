---
title: "Bars and Cafes"
type: section
---

Sturt Street and the Lydiard Street block north of the station are the café spine; Bridge Mall and the laneways behind Sturt have the small-bar scene. **Mr Jones**, **Hydrant Food Hall**, and **Forge Pizzeria** (technically a restaurant) are the most-talked-about. **Mitchell Harris Wines** for the cellar door in town; **Eureka Stockade Hotel** for a beer and a pub meal near the rebellion site.
