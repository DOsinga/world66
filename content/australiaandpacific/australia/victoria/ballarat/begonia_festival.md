The Ballarat Begonia Festival is one of the city's signature annual events, centred on the Botanical
Gardens and Lake Wendouree. It builds on Ballarat's cool-climate garden tradition, with massed
begonias, horticultural displays, markets, performances, and family activities.

Because the festival uses places that are already worth visiting, it feels more rooted than a
generic event. If your trip falls over the festival weekend, the gardens and lake become one of the
best ways to see Ballarat acting as a regional city rather than simply a gold-rush heritage
destination.
