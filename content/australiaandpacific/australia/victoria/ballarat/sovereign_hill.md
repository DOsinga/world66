Sovereign Hill is the closest thing Australia has to Colonial Williamsburg — a 25-hectare recreation of an 1850s Ballarat goldfield, with a working mine you can descend into, a panning stream where you can keep what you find, a Main Street of shops staffed by costumed interpreters, a coach and horses, a Chinese village, and a working tent encampment.

Plan a full day. Highlights include the **underground mine tour** (book separately on arrival), **gold pouring** demonstrations several times a day, and the live-actor "Eureka" recreation. The evening show **Aura** (separate ticket) is an hour-long outdoor sound-and-light retelling of the rebellion. Sturdy shoes, hat, sunscreen.
