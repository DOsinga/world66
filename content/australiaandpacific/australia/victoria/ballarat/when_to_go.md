---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

Ballarat sits at 435 metres and is noticeably cooler than Melbourne — summer maxes around 26°C, winter minimums often below freezing with the occasional skiff of snow. The town is at its prettiest in **autumn** (April–May), when the plane trees on Sturt Street and around the lake turn gold and red; and in **late spring** when the botanical gardens are in bloom.

**Begonia Festival** in early March packs the gardens. **Heritage Weekend** in late May opens private buildings on Lydiard Street that you cannot normally see inside. **Winter** in Ballarat is cold but the Sovereign Hill **Winter Wonderlights** show in the evenings has become a serious draw. Mid-summer is the slowest tourist season — and the warmest weather of the year.
