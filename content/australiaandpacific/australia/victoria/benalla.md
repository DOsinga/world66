Benalla sits in north-east Victoria, close enough to the Hume Highway to be easy to dismiss and interesting enough that you should not. It has made art its calling card: murals across town, a respected regional gallery by the lake, and the North East Victoria Silo Art Trail radiating into the surrounding countryside.

The town's best day is a mix of walking and driving. Start with the [Benalla Art Gallery](/australiaandpacific/australia/victoria/benalla/benalla_art_gallery), cross the botanic gardens and lake foreshore, then look for the [Benalla Street Art](/australiaandpacific/australia/victoria/benalla/benalla_street_art) murals around the centre. If you have a car, [Winton Wetlands](/australiaandpacific/australia/victoria/benalla/winton_wetlands) adds open water, dead river red gums, birdlife, and large-scale public art.

Benalla is not as grand as [Bendigo](/australiaandpacific/australia/victoria/bendigo) or as pretty as [Bright](/australiaandpacific/australia/victoria/bright), but it has a clear reason to stop: regional art against a flat, dry, inland Australian backdrop.
