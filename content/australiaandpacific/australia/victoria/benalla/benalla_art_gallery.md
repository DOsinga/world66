Benalla Art Gallery is the town's cultural anchor, set beside Lake Benalla in the botanic gardens. It has the scale and confidence of a good regional gallery: Australian art, touring exhibitions, local work, and enough variety to reward more than a quick look.

The setting helps. You can combine the gallery with a walk along the lake, making it feel less like a standalone museum stop and more like the centre of Benalla's small civic landscape.
