Benalla Costume & Kelly Museum combines local history with displays connected to Ned Kelly. Its collection includes costumes, historical photographs, early newspapers, documents, and Kelly-related objects such as his transportable cell.

It is a useful stop for travellers following the Kelly story through north-east Victoria, and it complements the better-known Kelly sites at Glenrowan.
