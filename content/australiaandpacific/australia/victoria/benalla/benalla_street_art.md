Benalla's street art is not one mural for a brochure. The town centre has dozens of works on laneways, walls, shop backs, and civic buildings, much of it created through the Wall to Wall festival.

Pick up a map or wander slowly through the centre. The best pieces give otherwise ordinary streets a jolt of colour, and the trail is a good way to see the town on foot instead of treating it as a highway break.
