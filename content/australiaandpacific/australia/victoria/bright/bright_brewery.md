Bright Brewery is the best-known drinking establishment in the Ovens Valley and one of the more successful regional craft breweries in Victoria. The brewery occupies a large space on the edge of town with a beer garden looking toward the hills — exactly the right place to end a day's cycling or skiing. The range covers the usual craft spectrum, but the lagers and ales suited to cold weather drinking are the standouts.

The kitchen produces solid pub food — wood-fired pizzas, burgers, and daily specials using local produce. It's family-friendly and typically busy on weekends and during ski season. The brewery also runs tours explaining the brewing process, though you don't need to book a tour to enjoy a pint in the garden.
