---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

**Autumn (March–May)** is Bright's signature season. The deciduous trees planted along the streets and in the parks — oaks, maples, elms, and poplars — put on a spectacular colour display in April. The Bright Autumn Festival runs over two weekends in late April and brings big crowds; book accommodation well ahead.

**Winter (June–September)** is ski season. Bright is the main service town for Mt Hotham and Falls Creek, two of Victoria's largest ski resorts. The town is busy on weekends and during school holidays.

**Summer (November–February)** is hot but the mountains offer relief. Swimming holes in the Ovens River are the local cooling solution. Summer storms can close mountain roads briefly; the Great Alpine Road to Hotham is spectacular but watch for ice in shoulder seasons.

**Spring (September–November)** is the quietest and often most pleasant time — green hills, moderate temperatures, fewer crowds.
