Murrindal Bridge is a rare surviving timber road bridge north of Buchan, built in the 1920s and listed on the National Heritage Register. It is a small stop rather than a long visit, but the unusual wooden structure and rural setting make it worthwhile for travellers exploring the Buchan Caves area by car.
