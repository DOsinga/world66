Camperdown sits in the volcanic country of western Victoria, inland from the Great Ocean Road but shaped by the same slow-travel rhythm. Its main street is lined with elms and old buildings, and a Gothic clock tower rises in the middle like a small-town exclamation mark.

The real reason to stop is the landscape around town. [Mount Leura and Mount Sugarloaf](/australiaandpacific/australia/victoria/camperdown/mount_leura_and_mount_sugarloaf) are part of a volcanic complex on the edge of Camperdown, with short walks and big views over crater lakes, paddocks, and low cones. [Lake Bullen Merri](/australiaandpacific/australia/victoria/camperdown/lake_bullen_merri) is a deep volcanic lake used for fishing, boating, and quiet shore stops.

Back in town, the [Camperdown Clock Tower](/australiaandpacific/australia/victoria/camperdown/camperdown_clock_tower) gives the centre a strong identity, while the [Camperdown Botanic Gardens and Arboretum](/australiaandpacific/australia/victoria/camperdown/camperdown_botanic_gardens) look across the twin lakes of Bullen Merri and Gnotuk. Camperdown is not a blockbuster, but it is a good detour if you like towns whose setting does half the storytelling.
