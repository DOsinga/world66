The Camperdown Botanic Gardens and Arboretum sit on a hill west of town, looking across the crater lakes and volcanic plains. The gardens are small, but the wider arboretum and view make the stop feel generous.

It is a good place to understand how the town, lakes, and cones fit together. Bring a picnic if the wind is kind.
