The Grainger Museum is a small, odd, and rewarding museum on the University of Melbourne campus. It follows the life of Percy Grainger, the Australian-born pianist and composer, through manuscripts, instruments, photographs, costumes, and the personal archive he built around himself.

It is a specialist stop rather than a broad Melbourne highlight, but music lovers will get far more from it than its modest size suggests.
