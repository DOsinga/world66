Castlemaine is a central Victorian goldfields town with a better second act than most. The 1850s rush left it with broad streets, public buildings, old pubs, and a handsome railway station; the last few decades have filled those bones with artists, makers, good cafes, and weekend visitors from [Melbourne](/australiaandpacific/australia/victoria/melbourne).

The town is not as grand as [Bendigo](/australiaandpacific/australia/victoria/bendigo) or [Ballarat](/australiaandpacific/australia/victoria/ballarat), but it is easier to enjoy on foot. [Castlemaine Art Museum](/australiaandpacific/australia/victoria/castlemaine/castlemaine_art_museum) is the cultural anchor, with a serious Australian collection in an Art Deco building. [Buda Historic Home and Garden](/australiaandpacific/australia/victoria/castlemaine/buda_historic_home_and_garden) gives the goldfields domestic story more texture than another mine shaft would.

Castlemaine works best as a slow day: start with art, walk the old centre, sit in the [Botanical Gardens](/australiaandpacific/australia/victoria/castlemaine/castlemaine_botanical_gardens), then nose around The Mill or the market if your timing is right. It is a good stop on the Bendigo line and a fine base for Maldon, Chewton, and the dry goldfields country around Mount Alexander.
