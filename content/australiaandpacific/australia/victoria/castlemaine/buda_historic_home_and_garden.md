Buda is a goldfields villa rather than a grand mansion, and that is its charm. The house was home to the Leviny family for 118 years, and its rooms still hold furniture, artworks, personal objects, and the layered evidence of a creative household living through Castlemaine's boom and quieter years.

The garden is part of the visit, not just scenery around the house. Allow time to walk it slowly, especially in mild weather, when the old trees and formal beds soften the hard goldfields light.
