Garfield Wheel and the Northern Castlemaine Diggings are part of the gold-rush landscape around Castlemaine. The site has remains of mining activity, an abandoned house, a monument, and interpretive signs that explain the diggings.

It is best for visitors already exploring Castlemaine's goldfields history or nearby heritage sites.
