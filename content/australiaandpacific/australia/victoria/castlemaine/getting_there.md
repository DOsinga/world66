title: Getting There
type: section
---

Castlemaine is on the V/Line train between [Melbourne](/australiaandpacific/australia/victoria/melbourne) and [Bendigo](/australiaandpacific/australia/victoria/bendigo). Trains from Southern Cross usually take around 90 minutes to two hours, depending on the service.

By car, take the Calder Freeway northwest from Melbourne and turn off near Elphinstone. The drive is straightforward and takes about 90 minutes in clear traffic.
