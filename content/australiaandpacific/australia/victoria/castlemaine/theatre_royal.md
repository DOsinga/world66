Theatre Royal is one of Castlemaine's best signs that the town is not just preserving its past. The old theatre has lived several lives and still hosts films, gigs, and events in the centre of town.

Check the program before you visit. Even if nothing is on, the building helps make sense of Castlemaine's current identity: a goldfields town where the creative scene feels lived-in rather than staged for weekenders.
