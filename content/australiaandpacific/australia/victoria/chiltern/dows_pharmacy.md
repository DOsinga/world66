Dow's Pharmacy is one of Chiltern's best small interiors. A pharmacy operated on this site from 1859, and the present building dates from the gold rush period. Inside, the old dispensary fittings and pharmaceutical equipment have been kept with very little gloss.

Opening hours are limited, so do not assume you can walk in. Even when closed, the shopfront helps anchor the historic town walk.
