Daylesford is Victoria's classic mineral-springs escape, about 90 minutes northwest of Melbourne. It has the familiar bones of a goldfields town, but its present-day mood is softer: spas, galleries, gardens, lake walks, weekend lunches, and a long-running reputation as one of regional Victoria's most open-minded small towns.

The place is easy to enjoy without a checklist. Walk [Lake Daylesford](/australiaandpacific/australia/victoria/daylesford/lake_daylesford), climb [Wombat Hill Botanic Gardens](/australiaandpacific/australia/victoria/daylesford/wombat_hill_botanic_gardens), then spend time at [The Convent](/australiaandpacific/australia/victoria/daylesford/the_convent), a former 19th-century mansion turned gallery. The wider spa country begins almost immediately at Hepburn Springs.

Daylesford is not built for rushing. It works as a slow day or weekend from Melbourne, especially if you want a country town with food, art, mineral water, and enough landscape to keep the day from becoming only lunch.
