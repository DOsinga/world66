Hepburn Mineral Springs Reserve is just north of Daylesford and explains why the district became Victoria's spa country. You can walk through the reserve, taste mineral water from public pumps, and connect the town's modern wellness business to the springs underneath it.

The water is not to everyone's taste, but the ritual is part of the visit. Bring a bottle, try a little, and keep time for a short walk through the reserve.
