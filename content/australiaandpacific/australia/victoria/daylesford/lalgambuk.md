Lalgambuk is a historically important gold mining site near Daylesford. Its mining landscape is part of the wider Victorian goldfields story and has been on Australia's UNESCO tentative list since 2025.

It is most rewarding for visitors interested in industrial heritage and the gold rush landscape around central Victoria.
