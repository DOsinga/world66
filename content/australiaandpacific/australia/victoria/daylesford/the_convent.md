The Convent is a former 19th-century mansion and convent on the rise near Wombat Hill. Today it works as a multi-level gallery, with changing exhibitions, old rooms, garden corners, and views over Daylesford.

It is polished, but the building keeps enough history to make it more than a retail gallery stop. Pair it with Wombat Hill Botanic Gardens, which sits just nearby.
