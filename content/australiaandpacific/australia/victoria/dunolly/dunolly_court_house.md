Dunolly Court House is one of the clearest reminders that this quiet town once mattered in the Goldfields. The restored building sits close to Broadway, part of a small civic landscape that also includes old commercial facades and public buildings.

It is a quick stop, but it helps the town read as more than a service centre. Walk Broadway rather than treating the court house as a single isolated sight.
