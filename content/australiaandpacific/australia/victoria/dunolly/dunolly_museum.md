Dunolly Museum is the town's best indoor stop, especially if the Welcome Stranger story has brought you here. Its collection covers the gold rush years through photographs, mining equipment, firearms, family records, and replicas of famous nuggets found in the district.

The museum is local rather than slick, which suits Dunolly. It gives the town's old buildings and surrounding diggings enough context to make a short visit worthwhile.
