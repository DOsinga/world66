The historic port precinct is the heart of Echuca's appeal. The [Echuca Wharf](/australiaandpacific/australia/victoria/echuca/echuca_wharf) — the longest wooden inland wharf in Australia — still stands, and you can board the [PS Adelaide](/australiaandpacific/australia/victoria/echuca/ps_adelaide), the oldest wooden-hulled paddlesteamer still operating in the world, for a cruise on the Murray. Beyond the port, the town has a handful of eccentric museums and easy river-country activities that reward a slow couple of days.

The [Port of Echuca Historic Reserve](/australiaandpacific/australia/victoria/echuca/port_of_echuca) covers the wharf, the old bond stores, and the Star Hotel with its escape tunnel. [Sharp's Magic Movie House](/australiaandpacific/australia/victoria/echuca/sharps_magic_movie_house) is an oddity worth an hour — a working collection of Victorian penny arcade machines and hand-cranked film projectors. Out on the water and in the surrounding forest, [kayaking on the Murray](/australiaandpacific/australia/victoria/echuca/murray_river) and [cycling through the red gum flats](/australiaandpacific/australia/victoria/echuca/red_gum_forest_cycling) are the best ways to escape the tourist centre.
