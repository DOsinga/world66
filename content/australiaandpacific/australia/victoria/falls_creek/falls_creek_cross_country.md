The cross-country trails are the quieter side of Falls Creek. They leave from Windy Corner and work out across the Bogong High Plains, where the landscape feels wider and more open than the downhill resort.

Beginners can start with lessons and short groomed loops, while fitter skiers can push deeper into the high country when conditions are good. Treat the area seriously in bad weather: visibility can vanish quickly on the open plains.
