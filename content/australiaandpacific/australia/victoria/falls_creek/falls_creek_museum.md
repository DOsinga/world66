Falls Creek Museum is a small but useful stop when weather closes in or you want the village to feel less anonymous. Its displays cover the ski resort, the Kiewa hydroelectric scheme, old mountain transport, and the people who turned this high basin into a winter village.

It will not take long, but it adds context to the place. Check opening days before making a special trip.
