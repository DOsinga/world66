title: "Day Trips"
type: section
---

Geelong is the main gateway to the [Great Ocean Road](/australiaandpacific/australia/victoria/greatoceanroad), and almost everyone heading to the Twelve Apostles passes through. Closer to home, the Bellarine Peninsula has cool-climate wineries and seafront villages within 20–40 minutes' drive, and the You Yangs ranges to the north offer the best hiking in the immediate area.

- **Bellarine Peninsula** (~30 km) — wineries, Queenscliff's heritage streetscape, ferry to Sorrento.
- **You Yangs Regional Park** (~25 km) — granite peaks with wide views over the bay; trail running and easy walks.
- **Twelve Apostles** (~200 km via the Great Ocean Road) — long day; better as an overnight.
- **Werribee Open Range Zoo & Mansion** (~50 km) — African savannah-style enclosures on the Werribee Park grounds.
