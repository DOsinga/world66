---
title: "Eating Out"
type: section
---

Geelong's eating-out scene has been transformed in the last decade, and the city now punches above its weight for a regional centre. The best independent restaurants and cafes cluster on [Little Malop Street](/australiaandpacific/australia/victoria/geelong/little_malop_street) in the centre and on [Pakington Street](/australiaandpacific/australia/victoria/geelong/pakington_street) in Geelong West — the latter often shortened to "Pako" and considered the city's most reliable strip for breakfast and brunch.

Standouts: the contemporary Australian menu at **Igni** (one-hat, Ryan St) is the most ambitious cooking in the city; **Cartel Coffee Roasters** roasts beans for half the cafes around; **Beach House** on the waterfront does the seafood-and-view formula well; and **18th Amendment Bar** (a few doors off Pako) is a serious cocktail bar with a small plates kitchen.
