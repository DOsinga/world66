The Geelong Library and Heritage Centre is the most striking civic building in central Geelong, with a faceted dome that stands out beside Johnstone Park and the gallery precinct. The public balcony is the reason to step inside: when open in daylight and good weather, it gives an easy view over the city centre.

It is a short stop rather than a major museum, but it fits well with the Geelong Gallery, Little Malop Street, and the National Wool Museum on a walk through the city's cultural core.
