Geelong Maritime Museum tells the city's sea story on a human scale, with models, photographs, objects, and local shipping history rather than blockbuster displays. It helps explain why Geelong's waterfront mattered long before it became a promenade of restaurants and bollards.

The museum works best for travellers already interested in ports, ships, or regional history. Combine it with the waterfront, Cunningham Pier, or the National Wool Museum for a fuller picture of Geelong's working past.
