---

Geelong's modern attractions cluster along the waterfront, where the [Geelong Bollards](/australiaandpacific/australia/victoria/geelong/geelong_bollards) — over 100 painted timber figures by Jan Mitchell — line the promenade between the carousel and the swimming enclosures. Inland, the [Geelong Gallery](/australiaandpacific/australia/victoria/geelong/geelong_gallery) holds one of Australia's better regional art collections and is housed alongside the heritage [Geelong Town Hall](/australiaandpacific/australia/victoria/geelong/geelong_town_hall) on Johnstone Park.

For something more unusual, the [National Wool Museum](/australiaandpacific/australia/victoria/geelong/national_wool_museum) tells the story of Australia's wool industry — central to Victoria's 19th-century wealth — in an 1872 bluestone warehouse, and the [Old Geelong Gaol](/australiaandpacific/australia/victoria/geelong/old_geelong_gaol) up the hill gives a sobering look at colonial-era punishment. Architecture spotters can pick out the Art Deco [T & G Building](/australiaandpacific/australia/victoria/geelong/tg_building) at Moorabool and Ryrie, the Romanesque-revival [St Mary of the Angels Basilica](/australiaandpacific/australia/victoria/geelong/st_mary_basilica) on Yarra Street, and — for sheer oddity — the [Sphinx Hotel](/australiaandpacific/australia/victoria/geelong/sphinx_hotel) on the northern outskirts, complete with full-scale plaster pyramids.
