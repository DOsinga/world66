The heritage precinct covers the key places from the final Kelly conflict: the old railway platform area, the site of Anne Jones's Glenrowan Inn, and the ground where Kelly was captured. Little original fabric survives, so read the signs and bring some imagination.

This is the one Glenrowan sight that matters even if you skip the paid attractions. It puts the legend back into a real, walkable landscape.
