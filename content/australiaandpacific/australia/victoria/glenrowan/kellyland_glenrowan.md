Kellyland is the old-school Glenrowan attraction: an animatronic re-enactment of Ned Kelly's last stand, with sets, sound, and a certain time-capsule charm. The technology shows its age, but that is also part of the appeal.

Go in expecting a short, theatrical roadside attraction rather than a polished museum, and it makes sense.
