This newer interpretive hub gives Glenrowan a clearer, more modern way to understand the siege than the town's older tourist stops. Exhibits cover the Kelly outbreak, the siege, and the people caught up in it, including police, hostages, sympathisers, and local residents.

It is the sensible first stop if you want the story before walking the precinct.
