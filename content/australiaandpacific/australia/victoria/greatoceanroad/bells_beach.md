Bells Beach is one of Australia's most famous surf breaks, with cliffs above the sand and waves that have shaped Victoria's surf identity for decades. Non-surfers still get a strong sense of the place from the lookout.

Swimming is not the reason to come; conditions can be rough and the beach is exposed. Come to watch, photograph, or understand why Torquay became such an important surf town.
