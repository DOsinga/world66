Gibson Steps give you the scale that the main Twelve Apostles platforms can flatten. From the beach, the cliffs loom overhead and the nearby stacks feel massive rather than picturesque.

Access depends on conditions, and the Southern Ocean is not gentle here. Check tide and safety signs before going down, and keep well clear of unstable cliffs and powerful waves.
