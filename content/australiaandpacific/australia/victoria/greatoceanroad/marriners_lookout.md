Marriner's Lookout is a short viewpoint walk above Apollo Bay, reached from Marriner's Lookout Road at the north end of town. The return walk takes about 30 minutes and opens onto a broad view over the bay, the town, and the Great Ocean Road coastline.

It is one of the easiest ways to get above Apollo Bay without committing to a longer Otways hike. Go in clear weather, when the sweep of the coast is the point of the stop.
