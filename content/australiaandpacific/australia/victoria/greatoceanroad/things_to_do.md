title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Drive slowly and get out of the car often. The headline stops are the [Twelve Apostles](/australiaandpacific/australia/victoria/greatoceanroad/twelve_apostles), [Loch Ard Gorge](/australiaandpacific/australia/victoria/greatoceanroad/loch_ard_gorge), [Gibson Steps](/australiaandpacific/australia/victoria/greatoceanroad/gibson_steps), [London Bridge](/australiaandpacific/australia/victoria/greatoceanroad/london_bridge), and the [Bay of Islands](/australiaandpacific/australia/victoria/greatoceanroad/bay_of_islands), but the Otway rainforest breaks up the coast beautifully.

Surfers should allow time around Torquay and [Bells Beach](/australiaandpacific/australia/victoria/greatoceanroad/bells_beach). Walkers can mix short waterfall tracks with beach stairs and cliff-top viewpoints; just keep an eye on tide, wind, and cliff-edge signs.
