---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

Summer brings warmer beach weather and the biggest crowds. Autumn is often the sweet spot: settled light, cooler walking conditions, and fewer people at the famous lookouts.

Winter can be windy and wet, but the Southern Ocean is at its most dramatic and waterfalls in the Otways run strongly. Whatever the season, carry layers; the coast and rainforest can feel like different climates on the same afternoon.
