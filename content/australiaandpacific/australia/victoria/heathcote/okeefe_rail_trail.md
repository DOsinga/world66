The O'Keefe Rail Trail follows the old railway corridor between Heathcote and Bendigo. It is a low-key way to see the dry Goldfields country without committing to a hard bushwalk.

Cyclists get the most from it, but short out-and-back walks from Heathcote also work if you just want an hour away from the road.
