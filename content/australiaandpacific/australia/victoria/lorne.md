Lorne is one of the Great Ocean Road's classic stops, a beach town squeezed between Louttit Bay and the green folds of the Otways. It can be busy and expensive in summer, but the setting earns the attention: surf, a long beach, forested hills, waterfalls, and the coast road curling away in both directions.

The town is easy to enjoy without a plan. Walk the beach, climb or drive to [Teddy's Lookout](/australiaandpacific/australia/victoria/lorne/teddys_lookout), then head inland to [Erskine Falls](/australiaandpacific/australia/victoria/lorne/erskine_falls) when the sun gets hard on the sand.

Lorne is polished now, but it still works as a road-trip pause. Stay long enough to leave the main street, because the best of it is the way rainforest, river, beach, and cliffs crowd into a very small area.
