Teddy's Lookout is the view that sells Lorne properly. From above town, the Great Ocean Road bends along the coast below, the St George River meets the sea, and the Otway hills press in behind the beach.

You can drive close to the lookout or walk up if you want to earn the view. Go early or late for softer light and fewer cars.
