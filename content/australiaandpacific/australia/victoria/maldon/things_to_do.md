---

Maldon works because its sights are close together and tell one story well: gold, steam, and a town that never quite lost its 1850s bones. Start with the [Maldon Historic Streetscape](/australiaandpacific/australia/victoria/maldon/maldon_historic_streetscape), then use the heritage railway and old mine sites to put the buildings in context.

The bush around Mount Tarrengower is part of the appeal. [Beehive Mine and Chimney](/australiaandpacific/australia/victoria/maldon/beehive_mine_and_chimney) and [Carman's Tunnel](/australiaandpacific/australia/victoria/maldon/carmans_tunnel) give the goldfields story physical weight, while [Mount Tarrengower Lookout](/australiaandpacific/australia/victoria/maldon/mount_tarrengower_lookout) opens the view across the dry central Victorian country that fed the rush.
