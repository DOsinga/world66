Abbotsford Convent is a large former convent complex on a bend of the Yarra, now used for studios, galleries, workshops, markets, and community events. The old religious buildings give the site a strong historic frame, but the reason to come is the mix of art, food, gardens, and local activity.

It works well as a slow stop after walking or cycling the river trails. Check what is on before you go, since the place is at its best when exhibitions, classes, or weekend markets are running.
