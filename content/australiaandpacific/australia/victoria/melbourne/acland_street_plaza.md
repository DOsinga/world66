Acland Street Plaza is one of St Kilda's liveliest pedestrian spots, with cafes, shops, street performers, and a constant flow of beach-bound visitors. It is more of a street-life stop than a formal sight.

Use it as the natural hinge between Luna Park, St Kilda Beach, and the neighbourhood's cake shops and bars. It is best in good weather, when the plaza feels properly animated.
