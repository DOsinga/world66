The ANZ Banking Museum sits inside a late 19th-century Gothic Revival bank building in the CBD. The exhibits trace Australian banking from Indigenous exchange systems through gold-rush finance, bank architecture, and modern money.

It is a compact specialist museum rather than a headline stop, but the building gives it extra interest. Drop in if you are already exploring Collins Street's old commercial architecture.
