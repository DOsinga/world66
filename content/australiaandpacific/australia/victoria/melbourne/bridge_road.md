Bridge Road is Richmond's old outlet-shopping strip, lined with fashion stores, cafes, and the steady tram-and-terrace-house rhythm of inner Melbourne. It is less polished than the central malls, which is part of the appeal if you like shopping streets with a bit of local texture.
