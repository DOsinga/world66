The Fire Services Museum holds Australia's largest collection of fire-service material, including uniforms, vehicles, photographs, and other memorabilia. It is a specialist museum, but the old equipment gives the collection more visual appeal than the subject might suggest.

It is best for visitors with an interest in emergency services, working history, or Melbourne's civic past, and it sits close enough to the city centre to be an easy short stop.
