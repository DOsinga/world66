The Jewish Museum of Australia tells the story of Jewish life in Melbourne and across Australia, with exhibits on migration, community history, religion, and ritual objects. It is a thoughtful St Kilda stop, especially for visitors interested in the local congregation and the wider history of Australia's Jewish communities.
