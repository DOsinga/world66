Polly Woodside Maritime Museum is built around the restored 1885 iron-hulled barque Polly Woodside. The ship gives the museum its character, with enough hands-on material and children's activities to make it work for families.

It is a useful add-on to a Southbank or Docklands walk, especially for anyone interested in Melbourne's port history.
