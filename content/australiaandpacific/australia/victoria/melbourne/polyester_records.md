Polyester Records has been a fixture of Brunswick Street since the 1980s and is one of the last of the independent record shops that once defined the strip. The stock runs deep across new and second-hand vinyl, with knowledgeable staff, a strong local and underground selection, and the gig posters and flyers that make a good record shop a kind of community noticeboard.

It is the sort of place where you go in for one record and leave an hour later with three, having been talked into something you had never heard of. For anyone tracking down Australian independent music, or just after the tactile pleasure of flipping through crates, it remains essential.
