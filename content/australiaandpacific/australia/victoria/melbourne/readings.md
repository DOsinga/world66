Readings is Melbourne's best-loved independent bookshop, on Lygon Street since 1969 and a genuine institution of the city's literary life. The Carlton store is the flagship: deep, well-curated stock across fiction, ideas, children's books, and a strong Australian section, with staff recommendations that locals actually trust and a music room of CDs and vinyl that has outlasted the format's obituaries.

It is more than a shop — it runs a packed program of author events, supports a major literary prize, and functions as a kind of community hub for readers in a university suburb that takes books seriously. Even in the age of online retail it remains busy, which says something about how Melbourne feels about it. Allow more time than you planned.
