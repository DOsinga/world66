Scots Church is a Gothic Revival church from 1873 and one of central Melbourne's early Presbyterian landmarks. Its spire and stonework stand out among the busier commercial streets of the CBD.

It is an easy architectural stop while walking between Collins Street, Chinatown, and the Parliament precinct.
