The Night Cat is one of Melbourne's defining live music venues, a big, warm room on Johnston Street with the stage in the round and the band surrounded on all sides by the crowd. It has been running since the early 1990s, and the booking leans toward funk, soul, Latin, jazz, and Afrobeat — music to dance to rather than stand and watch. On a good night the floor is packed and moving.

The layout is the thing: with no clear front or back, the energy circulates instead of pointing one way, and the room feels intimate even when full. Sunday afternoon sessions are a long-standing institution. Check the calendar before you go, as the programming ranges from local acts to touring international names.
