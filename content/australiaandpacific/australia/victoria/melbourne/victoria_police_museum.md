The Victoria Police Museum tells more than 150 years of policing and crime history in Victoria. Displays cover forensic techniques, major investigations, acts of courage, and infamous cases from the state's past.

The collection includes material connected with the Kelly Gang and the Russell Street Police Headquarters bombing. It is a focused stop, best suited to visitors interested in crime history and Melbourne's rougher stories.
