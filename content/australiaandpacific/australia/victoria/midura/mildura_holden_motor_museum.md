Mildura Holden Motor Museum is a specialist collection devoted to Australia's best-known car marque, from early Holdens through to the last locally built models. It is a straightforward, enthusiast-run place, but the range of vehicles gives a clear picture of how deeply Holden sat in Australian suburban life.

You do not need to be a mechanic to enjoy it, though car people will get the most out of the stop. It is an easy add-on while exploring Mildura and the riverfront.
