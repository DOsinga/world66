The meeting of the Murray and Darling rivers at Wentworth is one of the essential short trips from Mildura. It is a simple sight on paper, but standing at the confluence gives you a clear physical sense of Australia's inland river system and the scale of the country behind it.

Use the viewing tower or walk the nearby tracks if conditions are good. The two rivers can look surprisingly different where they meet, and the stop pairs naturally with Wentworth's old port history, paddle-steamer stories, and the dry-country landscapes around Mildura.
