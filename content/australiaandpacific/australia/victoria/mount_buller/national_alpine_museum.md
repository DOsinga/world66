The National Alpine Museum gives Mount Buller a bit of depth beyond the lifts and lodges. Its collection covers Australian skiing, snow gear, resort life, racing, and the people who made the high country into a winter playground.

It is a good bad-weather stop, and also useful in summer when the village can feel quiet. You do not need long here, but it helps explain why places like Buller matter in Australian mountain culture.
