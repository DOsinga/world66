RockWire Via Ferrata is one of the activities that makes Mount Buller useful outside ski season. The fixed-cable route lets non-climbers experience exposed alpine rock with guides and safety gear, adding a more adventurous option to the usual walks and bike trails.

Book ahead and check the operating season, because this is not a casual walk-up attraction. It is best for travellers who want a controlled dose of height and exposure without needing climbing experience.
