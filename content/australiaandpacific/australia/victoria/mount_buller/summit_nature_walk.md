The Summit Nature Walk is a straightforward way to stretch your legs without committing to a full mountain hike. It starts near the upper part of Summit Road and climbs through exposed alpine country toward views over the resort and surrounding ranges.

The path is short, but it still has steps and steeper sections. Wear shoes with grip, and do not underestimate how quickly wind or cloud can change the mood on the ridge.
