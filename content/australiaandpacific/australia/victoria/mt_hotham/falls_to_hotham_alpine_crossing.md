The Falls to Hotham Alpine Crossing is a 37 km multi-day walk through Alpine National Park, linking Falls Creek and Mt Hotham across high ridges, snow gum woodland, and historic hut country. It is one of the strongest reasons to think of Hotham as more than a winter resort.

This is not a casual stroll from the car park. Treat it as a proper alpine walk, with bookings, transport planning, weather checks, and gear to match exposed mountain conditions.
