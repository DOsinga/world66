Noojee is a tiny Gippsland town on the road toward Mount Baw Baw, wrapped in wet forest, fern gullies, and old timber-country history. It is too small to pretend to be a full resort town, and that is part of the appeal.

The reason to come is the landscape close by. The [Noojee Trestle Bridge](/australiaandpacific/australia/victoria/noojee/noojee_trestle_bridge) is the obvious landmark, a tall timber railway bridge in the forest, while [Toorongo Falls](/australiaandpacific/australia/victoria/noojee/toorongo_falls) gives you an easy waterfall walk through damp mountain country.

Noojee works well as a slow day from Melbourne or a break on the way into the high country. Add the [Ada Tree](/australiaandpacific/australia/victoria/noojee/ada_tree) if you want the day to feel properly ancient: one enormous mountain ash can do that.
