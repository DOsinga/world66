Noojee Heritage Centre sits in the old railway-station precinct, close to the start of the trestle bridge rail trail. It is small, but useful before you walk, because it explains why this quiet forest town once needed a railway at all.

Expect local photographs, maps, railway material, and timber-industry history rather than a polished big-city museum.
