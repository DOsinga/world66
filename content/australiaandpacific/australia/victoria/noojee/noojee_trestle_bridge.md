Noojee Trestle Bridge is the town's signature sight: a long timber railway bridge standing high among the trees on the old Warragul-Noojee line.

You can walk around the base and across the bridge, or use it as the start of a short rail-trail outing. It is especially good after rain, when the forest looks lush and the timber feels properly at home.
