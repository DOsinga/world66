Toorongo Falls is a short drive east of Noojee and the best natural walk in the immediate area. The loop track moves through tall, wet forest and links Toorongo Falls with Amphitheatre Falls.

It is not a hard walk, but the place feels more remote than the distance suggests. Wear shoes that can handle damp ground and give yourself time to sit by the river.
