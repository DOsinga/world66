title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Oakleigh is not a destination suburb in the way that Fitzroy or St Kilda are, but it offers something those places cannot: a genuinely functional Greek-Australian neighbourhood that has been shaped by its community rather than by tourism. The streets around Eaton Mall have a lived-in quality — the cafes and pastry shops and souvlaki joints exist because local people want them, not because visitors expect them. That makes the experience of wandering around feel real in a way that curated precincts often do not.

The Eaton Mall strip is compact enough to cover on foot in an afternoon, but the best approach is to arrive in the early evening when the tables outside fill up and the neighbourhood comes to life. Weekend nights in particular bring out the Greek-Australian families and friend groups who treat Eaton Mall as their local village square.
