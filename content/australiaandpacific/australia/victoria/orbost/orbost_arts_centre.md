Orbost Arts Centre is a small community gallery beside Forest Park and the visitor centre. Its exhibitions change, but the place is useful for local craft, timber-country material, and a sense of what East Gippsland makers are doing now.

Drop in if it is open while you are at Forest Park. It is a supplement to the river and rail-trail sights, not the main reason to come.
