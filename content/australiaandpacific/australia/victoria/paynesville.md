Paynesville is a low-key boating town on the Gippsland Lakes in eastern [Victoria](/australiaandpacific/australia/victoria). Its waterfront faces Raymond Island across a narrow channel, so the town's daily rhythm is half mainland marina, half island ferry crossing. Boats nose through the canals, pelicans work the jetties, and almost every useful walk ends up back at the water.

The reason to come is not a single monument. It is the easy combination of lake, ferry, koalas, and quiet cycling. Raymond Island is only a few minutes away and has one of Victoria's most reliable short koala walks. Eagle Point and the Mitchell River Silt Jetties add a strange, narrow landform where the river runs out into Lake King on long banks of deposited silt.

Paynesville is best with time outside: walk the Esplanade, cross to Raymond Island, hire a kayak or small boat if the weather is kind, and finish with fish and chips or a drink by the harbour. It is not a big-ticket destination, but if you are already in East Gippsland, it can fill a very good spare day.
