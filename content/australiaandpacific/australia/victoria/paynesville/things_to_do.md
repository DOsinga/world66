---
title: Things to Do
type: section
---

Paynesville's sights are water-led. The easy win is Raymond Island, reached by ferry from the Esplanade, while the longer outing is the drive or ride to Eagle Point and the Mitchell River Silt Jetties. Around town, the canals and jetties are the point: walk, cycle, paddle, or get out on the lakes if the weather is settled.
