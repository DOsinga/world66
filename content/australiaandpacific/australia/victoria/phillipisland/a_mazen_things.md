A Maze'N Things is a family attraction on Phillip Island with a large maze, illusion rooms, mini-golf, and outdoor adventure activities.

It is aimed mostly at children and groups looking for a light break between beaches, wildlife viewing, and the Penguin Parade. The maze is the main draw, while the indoor illusion exhibits make it useful on a wet or windy island day.
