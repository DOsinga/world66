Churchill Island is a small island off Phillip Island with walking tracks, coastal views, wetlands, and a preserved heritage farm. It is associated with one of Victoria's earliest European agricultural sites, after Lieutenant James Grant planted crops here in 1801.

The island is a quieter counterpoint to Phillip Island's busier wildlife attractions, with enough history and scenery to justify a separate stop.
