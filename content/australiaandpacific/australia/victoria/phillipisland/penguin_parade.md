The Penguin Parade is Phillip Island's best-known wildlife experience, with little penguins coming ashore at sunset and crossing the beach to their burrows. Arrival times shift through the year, from late afternoon in winter to later evening in summer.

Book ahead in busy periods and arrive early for better seats. Viewing areas are outdoors and exposed, and photography is not allowed after dark.
