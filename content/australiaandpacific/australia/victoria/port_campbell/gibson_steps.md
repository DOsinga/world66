Gibson Steps are the best antidote to viewing-platform fatigue. The stairs drop to the sand below the cliffs, where the limestone walls and nearby stacks feel massive rather than distant.

Access depends on tide and conditions. Stay well clear of cliff bases and heavy surf; this is a place to feel the Southern Ocean's force, not test it.
