Port Campbell Bay is the town's gentler face, a small horseshoe beach protected by headlands. On a hot day it is the obvious place to swim after several windy cliff stops.

It also gives Port Campbell a real centre. The beach sits a few steps from the main street, so you can move easily between coffee, fish and chips, the sand, and the jetty.
