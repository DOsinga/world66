The Port Campbell Discovery Walk is the simplest way to get above town without driving. It follows the clifftop near the bay, giving quick views over the village, the inlet, and the rougher water beyond the headlands.

It is not a wilderness walk, but it helps you understand Port Campbell's shape. Do it before dinner or between bigger national-park stops.
