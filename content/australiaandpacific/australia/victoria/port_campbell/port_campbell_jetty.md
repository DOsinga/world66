Port Campbell Jetty is a modest stop, but it gives the town a useful bit of harbour life between the famous lookouts. Walk out for a view back to the beach and cliffs, or linger if locals are fishing.

It is best treated as part of the town, not a destination in itself. Pair it with the bay and the Discovery Walk.
