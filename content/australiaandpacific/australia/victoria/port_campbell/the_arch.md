The Arch is one of the smaller but worthwhile rock formations along the Port Campbell coast. It shows the limestone cliffs in mid-transformation, where sea caves, tunnels, and stacks are slowly being carved out by the Southern Ocean.

It is close to London Bridge, so it makes sense to see both stops together. Come in rough weather if you want to understand how this coast is being made and unmade.
