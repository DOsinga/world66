*Australia and Pacific > Australia > Victoria > Queenscliff*

Queenscliff sits at the narrow entrance to Port Phillip Bay, where Melbourne's shipping channel meets Bass Strait. That geography gives the town its character: forts, lighthouses, pilots, ferries, and old hotels built for visitors who wanted sea air with a little polish.

The town is small enough to walk, and that is the right way to see it. [Fort Queenscliff](/australiaandpacific/australia/victoria/queenscliff/fort_queenscliff) looks over the shipping lanes from Shortland's Bluff, the [Queenscliffe Maritime Museum](/australiaandpacific/australia/victoria/queenscliff/queenscliffe_maritime_museum) explains the port and rescue stories, and the pier-and-harbour area keeps the working-waterfront feeling alive.

Queenscliff also works as a slow Bellarine Peninsula day. Ride the [Bellarine Railway](/australiaandpacific/australia/victoria/queenscliff/bellarine_railway), take the ferry across to Sorrento, or follow the coast to [Point Lonsdale Lighthouse](/australiaandpacific/australia/victoria/queenscliff/point_lonsdale_lighthouse) and watch ships squeeze through the Rip. It has more substance than a resort strip, and enough maritime oddness to justify the detour from [Geelong](/australiaandpacific/australia/victoria/geelong) or [Melbourne](/australiaandpacific/australia/victoria/melbourne).
