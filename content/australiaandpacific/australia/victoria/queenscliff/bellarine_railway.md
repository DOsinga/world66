The Bellarine Railway runs heritage trains from Queenscliff along the old branch line toward Drysdale. The route is gentle rather than spectacular, but the combination of old rolling stock, Swan Bay views, and country paddocks gives it an easy weekend rhythm.

Check the operating calendar before planning around it. Services are not constant every day, and special-event trains can change the feel of the trip.
