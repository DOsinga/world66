---
title: Things to Do
type: section
---

Queenscliff's best sights all point back to the sea. Spend the morning around the fort, harbour, and maritime museum, then use the afternoon for the Bellarine Railway or the lighthouse walk at Point Lonsdale.

The ferry to Sorrento is practical transport, but it can also be the day's best view of the bay entrance. Even if you are not crossing with a car, the harbour is worth a look.
