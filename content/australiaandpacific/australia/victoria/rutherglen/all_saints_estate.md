All Saints Estate, near Wahgunyah, is one of the district's historic names, established in 1864. The estate setting is more polished than the town centre, with old buildings, gardens, cellar-door tastings, and restaurants.

If you only have time for one representative Rutherglen winery, this is a strong candidate.
