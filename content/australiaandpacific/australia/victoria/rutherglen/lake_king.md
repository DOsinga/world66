Lake King is the easiest nature break in town, with a flat short walk, picnic areas, and birdlife around the water. It was formed in the 1860s as a water supply, so even the lake fits Rutherglen's goldfields story.

It is best as a leg-stretch between tastings or a quiet morning walk before the cellar doors open.
