The Albanian Mosque is a distinctive building in a residential suburb of Shepparton — a reminder that the city's food-processing industry drew migrants from across the world, including a substantial Albanian Muslim community that has maintained its own religious traditions here for decades.

The mosque is a functioning place of worship, not a tourist attraction, but its presence is a point of genuine interest for visitors curious about the multicultural threads woven into Australian regional cities. The Albanian community's contribution to the area — in the orchards, at SPC, and in businesses across the city — is part of Shepparton's history that most passing visitors miss entirely.
