Aquamoves is Shepparton's main aquatic and recreation centre — a large indoor-outdoor facility with pools, a gym, group fitness classes, and a waterslide. It's the kind of facility most regional cities would envy.

In summer the outdoor pool is popular with families; the indoor facilities run year-round. If you're staying in Shepparton for a few days, a morning swim here is a good way to start the day. Entry is relatively inexpensive by Australian standards.
