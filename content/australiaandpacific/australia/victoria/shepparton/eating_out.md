Shepparton has improved considerably as a dining destination over the past decade. Fresh produce from the surrounding farms and orchards gives local restaurants a genuine advantage. The city centre has a mix of cafes, Asian restaurants, and pub meals — not a destination dining scene but solid options for a regional city. The multicultural mix of the city shows in the food: you'll find Vietnamese, Lebanese, Indian, and Italian alongside standard Australian fare.
