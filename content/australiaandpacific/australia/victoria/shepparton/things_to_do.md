Shepparton punches above its weight for a regional city. The Shepparton Art Museum is the standout attraction — a striking building that would hold its own in Melbourne. Beyond culture, the city's natural setting on the Goulburn River floodplain offers lakes, wetlands, and cycling trails. The broader region is orchard and farm country, and the harvest season brings its own pleasures.
