Swan Hill Regional Art Gallery sits close to the river and Pioneer Settlement, making it an easy cultural stop rather than a separate expedition. The gallery shows Australian works from its own collection and a changing programme of touring exhibitions.

It is not a reason to cross Victoria by itself, but it adds depth to Swan Hill and gives you a quiet hour away from the heat.
