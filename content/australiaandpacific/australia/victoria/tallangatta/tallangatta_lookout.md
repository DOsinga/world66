Tallangatta Lookout sits above the town and gives the cleanest view over the new settlement, Lake Hume, and the surrounding hills. It is a short drive from the Murray Valley Highway, with picnic tables and a sheltered viewing area.

Come here first if you want to understand the layout of the place. From above, Tallangatta's straight postwar streets and the broad water beside them make the moved-town story much easier to read.
