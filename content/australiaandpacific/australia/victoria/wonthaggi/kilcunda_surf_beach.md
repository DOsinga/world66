Kilcunda Surf Beach is a wide Bass Coast beach backed by sand dunes and open coastal views. It is popular with surfers when conditions are right, and shore fishing is another regular use.

The beach works best as part of a coastal drive around Kilcunda and Wonthaggi rather than as a polished resort stop. Bring your own supplies and pay attention to surf conditions before swimming.
