---
title: Things to Do
type: section
---

Yea is a small country town, and its appeal is in the surrounding landscape rather than the town itself. The [Yea Wetlands](/australiaandpacific/australia/victoria/yea/yea_wetlands) boardwalk is an easy and pleasant stop for birdwatching. The drive through the [Black Spur](/australiaandpacific/australia/victoria/yea/black_spur_drive) towards Marysville is one of the most beautiful in Victoria. The [Yea Flora Fossil Site](/australiaandpacific/australia/victoria/yea/yea_flora_fossil_site) is a minor curiosity for those interested in ancient botany.
