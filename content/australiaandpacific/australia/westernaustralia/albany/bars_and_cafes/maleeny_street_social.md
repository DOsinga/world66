A good daytime cafe with a following among locals who want proper coffee and something more interesting than a standard café menu. The kitchen produces food worth eating — the grain bowls and baked goods have a following — and the space is comfortable for working or lingering. Breakfast and lunch only; closes mid-afternoon.
