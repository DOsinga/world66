One of Albany's most established dinner restaurants, Dylan's occupies a corner on Stirling Terrace and has been feeding the town for long enough to have earned genuine loyalty. The menu centres on local produce — Great Southern lamb, King George Sound fish, seasonal vegetables — prepared with enough ambition to feel like a proper restaurant meal without the pretension. The wine list covers the Great Southern region well. Book ahead for Friday and Saturday evenings.
