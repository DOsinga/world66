A popular all-day café and restaurant on Stirling Terrace that draws a loyal local crowd for its relaxed service and well-sourced ingredients. Breakfast is the main event — eggs on toast done properly, good house-made pastries — but the lunch menu holds up too, with seasonal produce from Great Southern farms. The coffee is reliably good, which matters in a country that takes it seriously.
