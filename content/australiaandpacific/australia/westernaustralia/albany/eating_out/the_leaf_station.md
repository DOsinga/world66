A well-regarded all-day spot on Stirling Terrace that handles breakfast through to late lunch. The focus is on clean, well-sourced produce — eggs from local farms, house-baked bread, seasonal produce from the Great Southern. The coffee programme is taken seriously, and the courtyard seating is worth seeking out on a warm morning. Reliably busy on weekends.
