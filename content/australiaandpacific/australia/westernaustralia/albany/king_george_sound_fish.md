A compact seafood restaurant on the waterfront that takes its brief seriously. The fish comes from King George Sound — the whiting, dhufish, and pink snapper are as fresh as they get this far from a capital city. The kitchen keeps preparations straightforward: grilled, pan-fried, or in a chowder that makes proper use of the local shellfish. Lunch and dinner; book for weekends.
