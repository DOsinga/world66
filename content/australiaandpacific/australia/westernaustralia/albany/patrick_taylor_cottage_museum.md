Patrick Taylor Cottage is one of Albany's oldest surviving houses, a modest colonial cottage now run as a local museum. Its rooms, garden, and domestic objects show a smaller, more intimate side of early European settlement than the town's grander military and maritime sites.

It is a good match for travellers who like historic houses and local texture. The cottage will not take long, but it helps round out Albany's story: port, garrison, whaling town, and ordinary home all existed within a few streets of each other.
