The SSJG Heritage Centre tells the story of the Sisters of St John of God and their work in the Kimberley, especially among Aboriginal communities around Broome. Exhibits cover the order's health, social, and mission work through photographs, objects, and local stories.

It is a modest museum, but it adds important context to Broome's multicultural and colonial history. It fits well with nearby Chinatown, the pearl-lugger history, and the town's other small heritage stops.
