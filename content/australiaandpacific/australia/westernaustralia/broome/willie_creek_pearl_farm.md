Willie Creek Pearl Farm turns Broome's pearling history into a working coastal visit rather than just a museum story. Tours explain how oysters are seeded, grown, harvested, and graded, with the blue-green tidal creek giving the whole place a strong Kimberley setting.

The farm is outside town, so treat it as a planned excursion and check road or transfer options before booking. It pairs naturally with Broome's pearling heritage sights and gives useful context for the pearl shops back in town.
