---
title: "Eating Out"
type: section
---

Victoria Street — known locally as the Cappuccino Strip — is the main strip for eating and drinking. The cafes and restaurants that line it are busy on weekend mornings with locals and visitors making the most of the South West produce that passes through Bunbury on its way to Perth: local wine, fresh fish from the coast, South West beef and lamb.

The Marlston waterfront development on the northern edge of the CBD has a newer cluster of restaurants and bars overlooking the Leschenault Estuary, with a more relaxed outdoor atmosphere and good views in the evenings.
