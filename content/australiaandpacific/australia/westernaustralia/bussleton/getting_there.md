---
title: "Getting There"
type: section
---

Busselton is on the Bussell Highway, about 220 km (a two-and-a-half-hour drive) south of [Perth](/australiaandpacific/australia/westernaustralia/perth). The town does not have a passenger rail line; Transwa runs daily coaches from Perth via Bunbury, taking around three and a half hours.

The **Busselton Margaret River Airport** (BQB), 10 km east of the centre near Yalyalup, takes scheduled flights from Melbourne in peak summer and a handful of regional routes. Most visitors fly into Perth (PER) and drive down.
