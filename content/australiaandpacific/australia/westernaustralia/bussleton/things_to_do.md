---
title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Most visits to Busselton revolve around the jetty and the long shallow beach that runs east and west of it. Beyond the foreshore the town's cultural quarter clusters around ArtGeo on Queen Street, and the surrounding region rewards a day or two: the tall tuart forest north of town, the Vasse-Wonnerup wetlands east, and the surf beaches of Cape Naturaliste a half-hour west.
