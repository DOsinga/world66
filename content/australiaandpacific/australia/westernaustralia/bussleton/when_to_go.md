---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

The classic Busselton holiday is the long Christmas–January summer break, when the foreshore fills up with Perth families and accommodation prices double. The water is warm (around 22°C), the days are dry, and the sea breeze drops in punctually each afternoon. **March and April** are the connoisseurs' months: the school holidays end, the weather stays warm, and the wineries an hour south are crushing fruit.

Winter (June to August) brings rain, wind, and a deeply unfashionable but very pleasant kind of solitude — the kind of weather where the cafes have working fires and the bird counts at Vasse-Wonnerup peak. The **Iron Man Western Australia** triathlon in December and **Cinefest Oz** film festival in late August are the town's two big events.
