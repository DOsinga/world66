Gwoonwardu Mia gives Carnarvon cultural depth beyond the highway stop. The centre focuses on Aboriginal stories, art, and country from the Gascoyne region, where desert, river, and sea meet.

It is the best place in town to slow down and understand whose country you are travelling through. Check opening hours before building a day around it, as regional cultural centres can change schedules.
