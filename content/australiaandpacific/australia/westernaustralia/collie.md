Collie is an old coal town in the jarrah country east of [Bunbury](/australiaandpacific/australia/westernaustralia/bunbury), and it is more interesting than that description sounds. Mining still shapes the place, but the surrounding forest, river pools, reservoirs, and a bold mural trail give travelers several reasons to turn inland.

The headline is [Wellington Dam Lookout](/australiaandpacific/australia/westernaustralia/collie/wellington_dam_lookout), where Guido van Helten's huge Reflections mural covers the dam wall. In town, the [Coalfields Museum](/australiaandpacific/australia/westernaustralia/collie/coalfields_museum) and [Collie Railway Station](/australiaandpacific/australia/westernaustralia/collie/collie_railway_station) keep the coal and rail story grounded. South of town, [Minninup Pool](/australiaandpacific/australia/westernaustralia/collie/minninup_pool) is the easy place to meet the Collie River.

Collie is best by car as a full day from Bunbury or a slower stop on a South West loop. It is not polished wine-country tourism, which is part of the appeal: you get industry, forest, water, and big public art in the same small radius.
