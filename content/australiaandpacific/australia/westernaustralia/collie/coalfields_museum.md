The Coalfields Museum fills the old Road Board buildings on Throssell Street with the things that made Collie: mining gear, timber and farming material, domestic objects, photographs, minerals, and local memorabilia.

It is not a slick museum, but that suits the town. Go here to understand why Collie exists before driving out to the lakes and dam country that now shape its visitor appeal.
