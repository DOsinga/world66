Coolgardie is the older, quieter face of the Western Australian goldfields. In the 1890s it was a roaring gold-rush town with grand ambitions; today the traffic pushes on to Kalgoorlie-Boulder, leaving Coolgardie with wide streets, empty space, and a surprising run of handsome stone and brick buildings.

That contrast is the point. The [Goldfields Exhibition Museum](/australiaandpacific/australia/westernaustralia/coolgardie/goldfields_exhibition_museum) fills the old Warden's Court with mining stories, photographs, and odd survivals from boomtown life. Outside, [Bayley Street](/australiaandpacific/australia/westernaustralia/coolgardie/bayley_street_heritage_walk) still carries the shape of a place that expected to become much bigger than it did.

Coolgardie makes an easy detour from Kalgoorlie or a useful stop on the long road east. It is not polished, and that helps: you can read the boom and bust in the buildings without much interpretation.
