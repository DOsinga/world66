Bayley Street is Coolgardie's open-air history lesson. The town hall, post office, old hotels, and shopfronts sit along a street scaled for a larger town, making the gold-rush optimism almost comically visible.

Walk slowly and read the plaques. The buildings are not just pretty leftovers; they show how fast money, law, drinking, religion, and government arrived once gold was found.
