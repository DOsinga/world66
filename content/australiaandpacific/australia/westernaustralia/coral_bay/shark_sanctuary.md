The Shark Sanctuary near Coral Bay is one of the easier ways to understand Ningaloo as a living reef rather than just a snorkelling backdrop. In the right season and conditions, small reef sharks gather in the shallow nursery waters close enough to watch without getting in the way.

It is not a zoo and sightings are never guaranteed, which is part of the point. Go quietly, keep your distance, and treat the visit as a low-key wildlife stop that pairs naturally with Coral Bay's beaches and reef trips.
