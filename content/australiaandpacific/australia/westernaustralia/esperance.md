Esperance sits on the southern coast of Western Australia, a long way from anywhere — about 700 km southeast of Perth. The reward for getting here is some of the most beautiful coastline in the country. The beaches along the [Great Ocean Drive](/australiaandpacific/australia/westernaustralia/esperance/great_ocean_drive) are startlingly white, the water an intense, almost unreal turquoise, and in the right season you will have long stretches entirely to yourself.

The town itself is a quiet, friendly place that serves as a base for exploring the coast and the national parks nearby. [Cape Le Grand National Park](/australiaandpacific/australia/westernaustralia/esperance/cape_le_grand), about 50km east, has the famous [Lucky Bay](/australiaandpacific/australia/westernaustralia/esperance/lucky_bay) — regularly cited as Australia's whitest beach, where kangaroos lounge on the sand. Nearby Thistle Cove and Hellfire Bay are equally stunning and quieter.

In town, the [Esperance Museum](/australiaandpacific/australia/westernaustralia/esperance/municipal_museum) displays a collection of Skylab debris — the US space station fell over Western Australia in 1979 and Esperance sent NASA a $400 littering fine. The [Esperance Stonehenge](/australiaandpacific/australia/westernaustralia/esperance/stonehenge), a full-scale granite replica built from local stone by a determined local farmer, is the kind of eccentric landmark that only the Australian bush produces.

The drive to Esperance from Perth is long but spectacular. Many visitors combine it with a trip to the pink waters of [Lake Hillier](/australiaandpacific/australia/westernaustralia/esperance/lake_hillier) on Middle Island in the Recherche Archipelago — best seen by scenic flight from Esperance Airport.
