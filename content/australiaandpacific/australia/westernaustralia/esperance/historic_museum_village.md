Historic Museum Village gathers old Esperance buildings around a small heritage precinct, including former police quarters, a school house, a court house, and a tiny 1895 Methodist church. Some buildings now hold cafes, galleries, and shops, so it works best as a short wander rather than a formal museum visit.
