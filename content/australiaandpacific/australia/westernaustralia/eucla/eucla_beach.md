Eucla Beach is reached by walking through the dunes from the telegraph station. It is not a resort beach; it is broad, exposed, and often empty, with old jetty remains to the west and pale dune country behind you.

For travellers crossing the Nullarbor, that emptiness is the appeal. It is one of the few places on the route where you can leave the highway noise behind and feel the coast properly.
