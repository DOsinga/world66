---
title: "Beaches"
type: section
---

The best beaches sit west of town inside or near Cape Range National Park. [Turquoise Bay](/australiaandpacific/australia/westernaustralia/exmouth/turquoise_bay) is the famous one, with clear water and drift snorkelling when conditions are right. [Oyster Stacks](/australiaandpacific/australia/westernaustralia/exmouth/oyster_stacks) is more tide-dependent and less forgiving underfoot, but the coral access can be excellent.

Town Beach is easier for a quick swim or sunset stroll, though it is not the reason to travel this far. Always check local advice on currents, tides, and seasonal wildlife before entering the water.
