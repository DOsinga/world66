---
title: "Getting There"
type: section
---

Exmouth is about 1,100 kilometres north of Perth by road. Learmonth Airport, south of town, is the usual fly-in point, with transfers and car hire serving Exmouth.

A car is strongly recommended. The town, national park, beaches, and gorges are spread out, and public transport is too limited for the kind of flexible reef-and-range days most visitors want.
