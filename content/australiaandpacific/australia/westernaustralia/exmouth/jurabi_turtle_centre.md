Jurabi Turtle Centre explains the turtle nesting that takes place along this stretch of coast. It is a small, practical stop, but useful before you head to beaches where nesting females and hatchlings may be present in season.

Respect the rules around lights, distance, and beach access. Turtle watching is one of Exmouth's privileges, but it is easy to disturb wildlife if you treat it like ordinary beach entertainment.
