The Ningaloo Centre is the sensible first stop in town, especially if you have arrived with a list of beaches but little sense of tides, seasons, or park logistics. It combines visitor information with exhibits on the reef, the range, and Exmouth's unusual defence and space-tracking history.

It will not replace time in the water or the gorges, but it helps you make better decisions before you go. Use it to check current conditions, permits, and tour options.
