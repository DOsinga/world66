---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

April to October is the main season, with more manageable temperatures and the best run of wildlife tours. Whale sharks are usually the headline from autumn into early winter, while humpback activity builds later in the season.

Summer is hot, quiet, and can be punishing. If you come then, plan early starts, carry more water than you think you need, and be realistic about walking in exposed country.
