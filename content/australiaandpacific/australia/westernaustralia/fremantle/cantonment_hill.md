Cantonment Hill is a small rise with one of Fremantle's easiest harbour views. The old signal station at the top is not normally open to visitors, but the hill is worth the short climb for the outlook over the port and the Indian Ocean. The Army Museum of Western Australia sits at the foot of the hill, making the two sights easy to pair.
