---
title: "Getting There"
type: section
---

The Fremantle Line train runs every 15 minutes from Perth station to Fremantle station via the western suburbs, taking around thirty minutes. The station is on Phillimore Street, a two-minute walk from the historic centre and the Shipwreck Galleries. The same ticket covers the city's free CAT (Central Area Transit) buses.

The Swan River ferry from Barrack Street Jetty in Perth is a slower but more picturesque option, around 90 minutes one way. The drive on the Stirling Highway takes about 25 minutes outside peak hours, with paid street parking widely available. Perth Airport is around 30 minutes by car or accessible by train via Perth station.
