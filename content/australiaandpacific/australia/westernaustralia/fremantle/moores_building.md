The Moores Building is a contemporary art gallery in Fremantle with several exhibition spaces and a cafe. It forms part of the city's arts scene and is useful as a short cultural stop near the waterfront and historic West End.

Exhibitions change regularly, so the appeal depends on what is showing, but the building is easy to combine with nearby Fremantle sights.
