The Sail and Anchor, on the corner of South Terrace and Henderson Street opposite the Markets, is the grandest of the old Fremantle pubs. The 1903 Federation building is one of the most photographed in the city — a wide veranda, ornate plasterwork, a corner turret — and it dominates the southern end of the Cappuccino Strip.

The bar inside has changed hands a few times in the last decade but remains a reliable place for a pint with a view of the markets crowd and a respectable kitchen. The rooftop, when it is open, is the best post-shopping perch on a Saturday. The Norfolk Hotel and Little Creatures Brewery, the other two essential drinking stops, are within ten minutes' walk.
