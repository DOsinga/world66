Walyalup Koort — the Noongar name meaning "Walyalup heart," renamed in 2021 — is the central civic square of Fremantle, the patch of ground on which St John's Anglican Church and the old Town Hall both stand. The square was redesigned in 2020 with new paving, plantings, and a children's water-play area.

It is the venue for council ceremonies, community markets, occasional concerts, and the city's annual Christmas tree. The new FOMO retail and food building on the western side, opened in 2022, has changed the square considerably and not entirely without controversy. The trio of square, church, and town hall remains the calmest large open space in the city.
