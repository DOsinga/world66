Geraldton Regional Art Gallery gives the city a compact but serious art stop, with changing exhibitions that often draw on Western Australian artists and regional themes. It is not a blockbuster museum, but it adds a welcome cultural pause to a town many travellers use mainly as a coastal base.

The gallery is easy to work into a central Geraldton walk, especially with the foreshore and heritage buildings nearby. Check what is on before going; a strong temporary show can make it one of the better indoor options in town.
