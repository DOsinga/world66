Wave Rock Wildlife Park is a small bush-style animal park near the entrance to Wave Rock. Expect a modest collection rather than a major zoo: kangaroos, birds, koalas, and other animals in a setting that works best for families.

It is convenient if you are already paying for the Wave Rock area and want a slower stop before or after the granite walks.
