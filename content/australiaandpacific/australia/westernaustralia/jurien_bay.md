Jurien Bay is a fishing and holiday town on Western Australia's Coral Coast, about 220 km north of Perth. It still has a working crayfish industry, but travellers come for the water: pale sand, clear lagoons, sea-lion tours, and the protected reefs of [Jurien Bay Marine Park](/australiaandpacific/australia/westernaustralia/jurien_bay/jurien_bay_marine_park).

The town makes an easy base for a day or two because the good things are varied. You can fish from the [jetty](/australiaandpacific/australia/westernaustralia/jurien_bay/jurien_bay_jetty), snorkel or dive the marine park, drive north to [Sandy Cape](/australiaandpacific/australia/westernaustralia/jurien_bay/sandy_cape), or go inland to [Lesueur National Park](/australiaandpacific/australia/westernaustralia/jurien_bay/lesueur_national_park), one of the state's great wildflower areas.

Jurien Bay is not as famous as the Pinnacles near Cervantes, but it is less of a single-stop attraction. If you are driving Indian Ocean Drive, this is the better place to slow down, get on the water, and let the coast earn its name.
