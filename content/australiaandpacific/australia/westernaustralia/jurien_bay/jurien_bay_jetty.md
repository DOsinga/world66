Jurien Bay Jetty is the easy town walk: a 162-metre pier used for fishing, photos, and a look back at the foreshore. It replaced older jetties damaged by storms, with some old piles still visible nearby.

Come late in the day if you want the softer light. It is also a simple first stop before deciding how much farther up or down the coast to go.
