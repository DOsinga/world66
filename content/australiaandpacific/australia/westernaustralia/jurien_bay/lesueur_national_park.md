Lesueur National Park is one of the strongest wildflower areas north of Perth, with hundreds of native plant species and low heathland that can turn spectacular after winter rain. The sealed scenic drive makes it easy to sample, while walks such as Mount Lesueur give broader views back toward the coast.

Spring is the classic season, but the park is still worthwhile for its quiet, open landscape outside peak bloom.
