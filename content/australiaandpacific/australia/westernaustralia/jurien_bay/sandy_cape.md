Sandy Cape is the prettiest nearby beach escape from Jurien Bay: white sand, turquoise water, low dunes, and a campground tucked close to the coast. In good weather it suits swimming, snorkelling, kayaking, fishing, or simply doing very little.

It is close enough for a half-day, but bring supplies. Services are limited once you leave town.
