Ross Graham Lookout is one of the gentler ways to meet Kalbarri National Park's inland gorge country. The viewpoint opens across the Murchison River, and a short trail drops from the car park toward the river, making it less of a look-and-leave stop than some roadside views.

It is a good choice if you want gorge scenery without committing to a long hot walk. Wildflower season can make the surrounding country especially rewarding, but even in drier months the river access gives the stop more texture than a standard lookout.
