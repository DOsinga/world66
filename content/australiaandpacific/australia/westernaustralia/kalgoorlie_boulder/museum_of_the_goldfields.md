The Museum of the Goldfields is the best place to put Kalgoorlie's wealth and grit into context. Its displays cover prospectors, mining machinery, town life, and the social world that grew around the gold rush.

The museum also holds a strong collection of gold bars, nuggets, and mining artefacts. Visit before or after the Super Pit; the two sights explain each other well.
