---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

The Pilbara has one of the harshest climates in Australia. Summer (November to March) brings extreme heat — temperatures regularly exceed 40 degrees Celsius — combined with high humidity and the risk of tropical cyclones. This is not a good time to be doing anything outdoors.

Winter (May to September) is the season for Karratha. Temperatures are mild to warm (25–35 degrees Celsius), the skies are clear, the seas are calm, and the bushland looks its best. Night temperatures drop to 15–20 degrees. Whale season (June to October) coincides with the best visitor weather. April and October are transitional months — manageable but humid.
