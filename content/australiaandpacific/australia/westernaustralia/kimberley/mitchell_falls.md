Mitchell Falls is a four-tiered waterfall on the Mitchell River in the far northwest of the Kimberley, arguably the most dramatic in a region full of impressive waterfalls. The falls descend about 80 metres through a sequence of rocky pools, with the main drop cascading into a broad plunge pool. Swimming is not permitted in the main pool, but the trail from the trailhead passes several other swimmable rock pools along the way.

Getting there is not straightforward — it requires either a 4WD drive along the Port Warrender Road from the Gibb River Road (the last 60 km is a rough track), or a scenic helicopter flight from Derby, Kununurra, or the Drysdale River Station. The Punamii-unpuu Trail from the Mitchell Falls campsite is about 8.6 km return, taking three to five hours depending on how long you spend at the swimming holes. The plateau setting — low sandstone escarpment, sparse woodland, distant views — is as much the point as the falls themselves.
