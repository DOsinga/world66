Gwalia Museum gives context to the ghost town outside. Its displays cover the Sons of Gwalia mine, the people who worked here, and the machinery that made this isolated patch of country worth fighting heat, dust, and distance.

See the museum before wandering the cottages if you can. The labels make the ruined streets feel less romantic and more human.
