---
title: Eating Out
type: section
---

Mandurah's restaurant scene is mid-range and waterfront-focused. The Mandurah Foreshore and the Marina both have a strip of pubs, cafes, and Asian-fusion places looking onto the water. Most are reliable rather than special, but the views and the easy outdoor seating in summer make up for a lot.

Cicerellos and Han's at the Marina serve fish and chips and grilled local seafood — both reliably good. Flics Kitchen on the marina has a more ambitious menu and is one of the better restaurants in town. The Boundary Island Brewery on the eastern foreshore brews its own beer and turns out competent gastropub food with views of the estuary at sunset. The food trucks at the Saturday Stretch Markets along the foreshore are worth a wander when they are running.
