Cape Naturaliste Lighthouse anchors the northern end of the capes coast, above low heathland and limestone cliffs near Dunsborough. It is smaller and less remote-feeling than Cape Leeuwin, but the surrounding walks and ocean views are excellent, especially when whales are moving along the coast.

The lighthouse is a good first or last stop if you are driving between Busselton, Dunsborough, and Margaret River. Short trails lead through coastal scrub to viewpoints, and the scenery changes quickly with the weather: calm blue water one day, wind-scoured whitecaps the next.
