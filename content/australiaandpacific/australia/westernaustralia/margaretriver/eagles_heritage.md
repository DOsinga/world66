Eagles Heritage is a raptor rehabilitation centre south of Margaret River town, where injured birds of prey are treated and, where possible, released back into the wild. For visitors, the appeal is the chance to see wedge-tailed eagles, owls, falcons, and kites close enough to understand their size and movement.

This is a good rainy-day or family stop, but it is not a zoo in the usual sense. The best reason to go is to support the conservation work and learn a little about the birds that patrol the forests and farmland around the region.
