---
title: Getting There
type: section
---

Margaret River is about 270 km south of Perth. By car, allow three to three and a half hours via Kwinana Freeway, Forrest Highway, Bussell Highway, and then the road through [Busselton](/australiaandpacific/australia/westernaustralia/bussleton). The drive is straightforward, though holiday traffic can slow badly around long weekends.

Coach services connect Perth and Margaret River, usually via Busselton, but they are built for point-to-point travel rather than exploring the region. If you want caves, wineries, and beaches in the same trip, rent a car or join a local tour once you arrive.
