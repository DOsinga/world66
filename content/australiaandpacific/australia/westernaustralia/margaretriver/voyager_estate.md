Voyager Estate is one of Margaret River's polished cellar-door experiences, set among clipped gardens and white Cape Dutch-style buildings south of town. The wines focus on the regional strengths: cabernet sauvignon, chardonnay, and blends that show why this corner of Western Australia is taken seriously by wine people.

It is not the place for a quick splash-and-dash tasting. Book ahead if you want the full seated tasting or restaurant meal, and leave time to walk the gardens. The atmosphere is formal by Margaret River standards, but the setting and attention to detail make it worthwhile.
