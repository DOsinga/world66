Perth Institute of Contemporary Arts, usually shortened to PICA, is the experimental edge of the Perth Cultural Centre. Its exhibitions, performances, and installations lean toward contemporary Australian and international work, often with more risk and less polish than a conventional state gallery.

It is an easy add-on to the Art Gallery of Western Australia or WA Museum Boola Bardip, but it is most rewarding if you are open to work that asks for a bit of patience.
