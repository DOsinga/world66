The State Theatre Centre is Perth's main venue for theatre, dance, and performance, opened in 2011 on the Perth Cultural Centre. The building is striking: a weathered steel facade cut with angular windows, set against the older brick buildings of the Cultural Centre behind it. Inside, two auditoria — the Heath Ledger Theatre (575 seats) and the smaller Studio Underground — handle everything from major touring productions to experimental work.

Black Swan State Theatre Company and Barking Gecko Theatre are the resident companies. The programming is adventurous by Australian regional standards, mixing local productions with touring work from Sydney, Melbourne, and overseas. Check the program before your visit; there is usually something worth seeing. The café-bar on the ground floor is a good spot for pre-show drinks in a well-designed space.
