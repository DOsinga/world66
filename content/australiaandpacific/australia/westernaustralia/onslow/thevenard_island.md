Thevenard Island is the largest of the Mackerel Islands, about 22 km off Onslow. Travellers come for the coral reef, clear water, fishing, snorkelling, and diving rather than for a village or built sight.

Access depends on local operators and conditions, so treat it as a planned outing rather than something to improvise on arrival.
