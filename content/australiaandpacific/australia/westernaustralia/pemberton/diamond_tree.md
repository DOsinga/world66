Diamond Tree is one of the old karri fire-lookout trees near Pemberton. At 51 metres, it is shorter than some of the area's other climbable lookout trees, but it still gives a strong sense of the forest and the lookout tradition.

Treat it as a quick nature stop on a Pemberton forest drive, especially if you are comparing the region's historic tree lookouts.
