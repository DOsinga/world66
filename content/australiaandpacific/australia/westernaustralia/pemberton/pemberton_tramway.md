The Pemberton Tramway runs restored trams through the forest outside town, crossing timber bridges and following river scenery through the karri country. The ride takes about two hours and includes commentary on the area's logging history, railway heritage, and natural features.

It is a gentle way to see the forest if you are not planning a longer hike, and it works well for families or anyone interested in Western Australia's old timber lines.
