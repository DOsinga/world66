---
title: "Bars and Cafes"
type: section
---

Perth has a strong café culture built on excellent coffee and a lifestyle that encourages sitting outside in good weather — which arrives reliably and stays late. The craft beer scene is among the best in Australia, anchored by Fremantle's breweries and a growing number of serious taprooms across the metro area. Northbridge is the nightlife hub, with bars ranging from relaxed craft beer venues to high-volume clubs. For a quieter drink, Subiaco and Leederville deliver the neighbourhood wine bar and café atmosphere that Perth residents use for their actual social lives, as distinct from the places visitors tend to end up.
