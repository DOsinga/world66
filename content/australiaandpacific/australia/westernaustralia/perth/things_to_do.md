---
title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Perth sits in a fortunate position for a traveller: the city itself has a solid concentration of museums, galleries, historic buildings, and urban beaches, while within easy reach are some of Australia's most extraordinary natural and cultural experiences. Rottnest Island with its quokkas and beaches, Fremantle's Victorian port character, the wildflowers of Kings Park, and the craft beer scene at the Fishing Boat Harbour all make Perth more rewarding than its isolation might suggest. The city rewards slow exploration — take a ferry across the river, walk up Jacob's Ladder, spend an afternoon in Northbridge — rather than ticking attractions off a list.
