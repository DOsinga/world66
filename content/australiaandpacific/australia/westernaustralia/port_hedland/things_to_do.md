title: Things to Do
type: section
---

Port Hedland's best stops are honest about where you are: an iron-ore port on a remote tropical coast. Watch the ships from [Marapikurrinya Park](/australiaandpacific/australia/westernaustralia/port_hedland/marapikurrinya_park), time a full-moon evening at [Cooke Point](/australiaandpacific/australia/westernaustralia/port_hedland/cooke_point), and use [Dalgety House Museum](/australiaandpacific/australia/westernaustralia/port_hedland/dalgety_house_museum) to make sense of the town's shift from pastoral outpost to mining export machine. In turtle season, Cemetery Beach adds a natural reason to stay overnight.
