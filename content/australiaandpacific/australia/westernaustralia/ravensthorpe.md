Ravensthorpe is a small town in Western Australia, roughly 550 km southeast of Perth, 290 km east of Albany, and 187 km west of Esperance. It sits among ancient ranges, rocky hills, and rugged river valleys, with bushland rich in geology, native flora, and fauna.

The region is best known for its wildflowers. In spring, visitors from around the world come to see the display — some species grow only in the [Ravensthorpe Range](/australiaandpacific/australia/westernaustralia/ravensthorpe/ravensthorpe_range) area. The [Ravensthorpe Wildflower Show](/australiaandpacific/australia/westernaustralia/ravensthorpe/ravensthorpe_wildflower_show), held each September, is one of the largest community wildflower shows in Australia.

South of town the road runs down to the coast at [Hopetoun](/australiaandpacific/australia/westernaustralia/ravensthorpe/hopetoun), the gateway to [Fitzgerald River National Park](/australiaandpacific/australia/westernaustralia/ravensthorpe/fitzgerald_river_national_park) — a UNESCO biosphere with whale watching, empty beaches, and one of the richest temperate floras in the world.
