A vast coastal wilderness park stretching west and south of Hopetoun, with the Ravensthorpe-side access via the East Mount Barren road. The park covers ancient quartzite peaks ("the Barrens"), white-sand beaches, sea cliffs, and heathland rich in proteas and eucalypts.

Whales pass close inshore between June and October — Point Ann is one of the most reliable land-based southern right whale viewing spots in the country. Bring fuel, water, and a paper map: mobile coverage drops out in the park and the gravel tracks demand attention.
