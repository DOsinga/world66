---
title: "Bars and Cafes in Scarborough"
type: section
---

Scarborough's bar scene clusters around the Esplanade and the foreshore restaurants. **El Grotto** is the year-round favourite for sundowners. **The Lookout** is an upstairs rooftop bar with the best ocean view in the suburb, and **Hash Specialty Coffee** is the locals' morning spot.

For something more old-school, the **Brighton Hotel** has been on Scarborough Beach Road since the 1960s and is still a reliable pub.
