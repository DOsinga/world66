---
title: "Eating Out in Scarborough"
type: section
---

The Scarborough Beach Road strip and the Esplanade have the usual mix of cafes, fish and chip shops and casual restaurants — the kind of beachside food that works after a swim.

**El Grotto** is a beach-shack-style Mexican bar-restaurant on the foreshore, popular for tacos and frozen margaritas. **The Sandbar** and **Hash Specialty Coffee** are reliable for breakfast. **Peach Pit** is a long-running pizza and pasta spot. For fish and chips, the takeaway at **Scarborough Sunset Markets** (Thursday evenings in summer) is a local favourite. The Wednesday and Saturday food-truck nights on the Esplanade are worth checking out if you happen to be in town.
