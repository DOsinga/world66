---
title: "Getting There"
type: section
---

Scarborough is about 14 km northwest of central Perth. By **car**, take Mitchell Freeway north and turn off at Cedric Street or Karrinyup Road — 20 minutes from the city most days. Free and paid parking lines the Esplanade and the back streets.

By **public transport**, take the train to Stirling station on the Joondalup Line and the connecting 990 or 991 bus to **Scarborough Beach bus station** (about 30 minutes door-to-door from Perth station). The 990 also runs direct between Scarborough and Perth via West Coast Highway, scenic but slower.
