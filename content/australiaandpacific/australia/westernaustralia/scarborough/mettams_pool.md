Mettam's Pool is a naturally sheltered lagoon on the coast north of Scarborough. The reef keeps the water calmer than the open surf beaches nearby, making it a popular spot for swimming and snorkeling close to shore.

Bring a mask if conditions are clear; marine life can often be seen without going far from the beach.
