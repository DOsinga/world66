Mettam's Pool is a naturally sheltered lagoon on Perth's northern coast, protected enough for calm swimming when the open beaches are rough. The reef sits close to shore, making it one of the easier snorkelling spots around Scarborough.

Come with a mask on a clear, low-swell day and you can see fish and marine plants without needing a boat or long swim. It is popular with families, so early morning is the quietest time.
