---
title: "Things to do in Scarborough"
type: section
---

Scarborough is built around its beach. The half-day plan is straightforward: swim or surf at [Scarborough Beach](/australiaandpacific/australia/westernaustralia/scarborough/scarborough_beach), lap or laze at the [Beach Pool](/australiaandpacific/australia/westernaustralia/scarborough/scarborough_beach_pool), and walk the [coast path](/australiaandpacific/australia/westernaustralia/scarborough/sunset_coast_walk) north to [Trigg Island](/australiaandpacific/australia/westernaustralia/scarborough/trigg_island) or south to City Beach. End on the [Esplanade](/australiaandpacific/australia/westernaustralia/scarborough/scarborough_esplanade) for sunset and a meal from one of the food trucks.
