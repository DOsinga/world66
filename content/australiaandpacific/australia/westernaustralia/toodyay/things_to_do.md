---
title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Toodyay is small, so the best sights sit close together. Start with the [Newcastle Gaol Museum](/australiaandpacific/australia/westernaustralia/toodyay/newcastle_gaol_museum), cross to [Connor's Mill](/australiaandpacific/australia/westernaustralia/toodyay/connors_mill), then walk Stirling Terrace before heading up to [Pelham Reserve](/australiaandpacific/australia/westernaustralia/toodyay/pelham_reserve) for the view over town and the Avon Valley.
