Canal Rocks is a ragged set of granite outcrops cut by narrow channels, with a footbridge giving close views of the water surging through. It is one of the most dramatic short stops on the coast south of Yallingup.

Stay back in heavy swell. The best experience is not getting close to the edge; it is watching the ocean force its way through the rock slots and feeling how exposed this coast is.
