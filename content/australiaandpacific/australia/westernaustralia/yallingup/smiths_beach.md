Smiths Beach is the longer, more open beach south of Yallingup village. It has the same mixture of beauty and force as the rest of this coast: clean sand, reef edges, surf breaks, and wide Indian Ocean views.

It is a good option when you want a walk rather than a quick viewpoint. As with Yallingup Beach, read the conditions before getting in the water.
