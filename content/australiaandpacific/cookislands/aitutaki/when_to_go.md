---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

May through October is Aitutaki's dry season — lower humidity, clearer skies, and the reliable southeast trade winds that keep the heat manageable. This is the most comfortable time to be here, and the best period for watersports. July and August are peak season: more visitors, higher prices, and the need to book accommodation and the lagoon cruise well in advance. The island is small and popular tour operators fill up fast.

December through March brings the cyclone season. Rain is heavier, heat and humidity rise, and while a direct cyclone hit is rare, the weather is less predictable and some smaller operators close or reduce schedules. The shoulder months — April and November — are often the sweet spot: the dry season's good conditions start arriving (or are still holding on), visitor numbers are lower, and prices soften. If your dates are flexible, aim for May or November and you'll likely have the lagoon looking its best with fewer people on it.
