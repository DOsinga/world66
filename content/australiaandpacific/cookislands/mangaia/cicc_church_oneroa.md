The Cook Islands Christian Church (CICC) at Oneroa is a coral-rendered nineteenth-century church with carved wooden interior beams and the long pews of a Pacific mission building. Sunday services run morning and afternoon; the harmony singing — Mangaia is famous in the Cooks for its choral tradition — is the main reason for visitors to attend. Modest dress expected.
