Ivirua is the small village on the eastern side of Mangaia, one of the six traditional districts of the island. The setting — between the makatea cliffs inland and a fringing reef offshore — is striking, and the village has a small CICC church and a handful of coastal taro patches. Worth a circle of the island ride to see.
