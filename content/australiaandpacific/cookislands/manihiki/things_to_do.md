---

Manihiki's central lagoon is the reason to come. The [black pearl farms](/australiaandpacific/cookislands/manihiki/black_pearl_farms) that line the lagoon floor produce some of the finest cultured Tahitian-style pearls in the Pacific, and visits to working farms are the defining experience of the atoll. The [lagoon](/australiaandpacific/cookislands/manihiki/manihiki_lagoon) itself is stunning — large enough that you can't see across it, gin-clear, and rich with marine life that sees almost no fishing pressure.

The two inhabited islets — [Tukao](/australiaandpacific/cookislands/manihiki/tukao_village) to the north and [Tauhunu](/australiaandpacific/cookislands/manihiki/tauhunu_village) to the south — each have their own church and community life. A walk through either takes half an hour, but the conversations along the way could fill a day.

[Diving and snorkelling](/australiaandpacific/cookislands/manihiki/lagoon_diving) in the lagoon are genuinely exceptional. There is no commercial dive operation — you arrange everything through local contacts — which means the sites are undisturbed. The atoll's outer reef wall also has dramatic drop-offs for those who can get access.
