---
title: "Getting There"
type: section
---

Manuae has no regular transport and no infrastructure. Access is by charter boat from [Aitutaki](/australiaandpacific/cookislands/aitutaki), about 100 km to the northwest, or by private yacht. The crossing takes 6–10 hours depending on conditions, and the reef entrance requires careful navigation — do not attempt it without an experienced skipper who knows the pass.

A **permit from the Cook Islands government** is required before visiting. Contact the Cook Islands Ministry of Marine Resources in Rarotonga well in advance. Permits are granted for genuine scientific, conservation, or low-impact recreational purposes; they are not automatically issued for curiosity visits.

There is no anchorage protected from all weather; conditions in the Cook Islands can change quickly. Visitors typically come on day trips from a boat anchored outside the reef, or stay aboard a well-found yacht. There are no freshwater sources, no food, and no rescue services on or near the atoll.
