---
title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Manuae offers nothing organised — no trails, no guides, no facilities. What it offers is a pristine Pacific atoll: coral reefs, seabirds in enormous numbers, green turtles nesting on the beaches, and a lagoon of extraordinary clarity. The experience requires preparation and self-sufficiency, but for those willing to arrange a charter from Aitutaki, it is genuinely unlike anything else in the Cook Islands.
