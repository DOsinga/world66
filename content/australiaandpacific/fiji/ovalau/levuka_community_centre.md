A small but well-curated local museum on Beach Street covering Levuka's history as the colonial capital, the cession of Fiji to Britain, and the town's commercial history (pearl shell, copra, fish processing). Local photographs and a few good Pacific artefacts.

Twenty minutes does it. The museum is the natural starting point for a self-guided walk through town.
