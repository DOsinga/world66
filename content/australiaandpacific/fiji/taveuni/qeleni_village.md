Qeleni Village is a community-run cultural stop in the lower highlands of Taveuni. Visitors can see dance performances, browse local handicrafts, and get a more direct introduction to traditional Fijian village life than resort areas usually offer.

The rainforest setting is part of the appeal. Treat the visit as a cultural exchange rather than a show, and check locally before heading up.
