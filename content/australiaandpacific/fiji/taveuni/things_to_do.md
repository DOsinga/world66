title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Taveuni divides cleanly between underwater and above ground. The Rainbow Reef in the Somosomo Strait is the main draw for divers — one of the premier soft coral sites in the world. On land, Bouma National Heritage Park takes up a third of the island, with rainforest trails, waterfalls, and a crater lake. The island also has the curiosity of the 180th meridian running through it.
