---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

The best time to visit Vanua Levu is the dry season, from May to October. Temperatures are pleasant (low to mid-20s Celsius), humidity is lower than in summer, and diving visibility is at its best. This is also when yachts from the cruising fleet concentrate in Savusavu Bay, giving the town more life.

The wet season runs from November to April, with higher humidity, more rain, and the possibility of cyclones (though severe cyclones are infrequent). The landscape is strikingly green during the wet season and this is also low season for accommodation pricing. If you are primarily there for diving, the dry season is strongly preferable. If you are on a tight budget and flexible about weather, the shoulder months of May and October offer a reasonable compromise.
