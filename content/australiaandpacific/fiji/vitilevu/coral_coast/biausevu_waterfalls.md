Biausevu Waterfalls are a tiered, roughly 20-metre fall reached by a short rainforest walk inland from the Coral Coast. The path takes about 20 minutes, so it works as an easy half-day nature stop rather than a serious hike.

The falls are strongest after the wet season, which usually ends in April. Bring shoes that can get muddy and expect the pool and track to be quieter outside the main resort excursion times.
