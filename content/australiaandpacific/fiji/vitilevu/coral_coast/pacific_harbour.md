Pacific Harbour sits on the eastern Coral Coast of Viti Levu, about an hour west of [Suva](/australiaandpacific/fiji/vitilevu/suva). It is not the place to come for a quiet village stay. Its identity is adventure tourism: shark dives, jet skis, zip lines, golf, and resort-backed day trips.

The headline reason to come is [Beqa Adventure Divers](/australiaandpacific/fiji/vitilevu/coral_coast/pacific_harbour/beqa_adventure_divers), whose shark dives are among Fiji's best-known underwater experiences. If you want something less intense, [The Pearl golf course](/australiaandpacific/fiji/vitilevu/coral_coast/pacific_harbour/the_pearl_golf_course) and the Arts Village keep the town from being only a dive base.

Pacific Harbour works well as a day or weekend stop from Suva. It is also a useful break in a Viti Levu loop if you want action between the capital and the drier resort coast farther west.
