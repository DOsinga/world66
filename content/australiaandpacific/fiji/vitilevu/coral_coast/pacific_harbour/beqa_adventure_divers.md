Beqa Adventure Divers is the main reason many travellers come to Pacific Harbour. Its shark dives visit a protected reef where bull sharks, reef fish, and coral life are the draw, with conservation work tied to the shark population.

This is a serious paid activity, not a casual beach swim. For certified divers who want one memorable day on Viti Levu, it is the strongest anchor in town.
