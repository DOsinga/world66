Jetski Safari Fiji runs guided jet ski outings from Pacific Harbour. It is the quick-hit version of the town's adventure identity: loud, wet, and built for travellers who want movement rather than another hour on a beach chair.

No previous jet ski experience is normally required, but check conditions and operator rules before booking.
