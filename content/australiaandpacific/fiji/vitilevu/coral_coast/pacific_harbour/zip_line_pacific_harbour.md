Zip Line Pacific Harbour runs canopy rides through the forested country behind the coast. The appeal is simple: a few hours above the river valleys and trees, with better inland views than you get from the resort strip.

It is a useful choice when the sea is rough or when mixed groups want an activity that does not require diving certification.
