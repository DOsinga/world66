A breezy, modern coffee shop on Queens Road between Nadi town and the airport, serving proper espresso, big breakfasts, salads, and burgers. The home-roasted Fijian coffee is the best in town and a relief after a few days of resort instant. It also has reliable Wi-Fi, which makes it a favourite work spot for travellers waiting on a late flight or an island transfer.
