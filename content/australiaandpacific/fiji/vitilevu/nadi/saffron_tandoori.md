A small Indian restaurant near the main bus station, often picked as the best North Indian in town by long-term residents. The menu is short and traditional — biryanis, dal makhani, paneer dishes, plus a good Fiji-style fish curry — and the food is consistent. Air-conditioned, friendly, and a step up in atmosphere from the cheaper Main Street curry houses.
