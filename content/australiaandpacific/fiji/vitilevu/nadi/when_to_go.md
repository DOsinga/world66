---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

The Fijian dry season runs roughly **May to October** — cooler (24–28°C), drier, sunnier, and much less humid. This is the high season for travel and the best time for the islands. The Australian and New Zealand school holidays in late June-July and again in September push prices up and resorts get crowded.

The wet season, **November to April**, brings high humidity, heavy short rain showers (often in the afternoon), and the cyclone risk window (peak January-February). Many people still travel through it — the weather is rarely a complete write-off — but the price you pay for cheaper rates and fewer crowds is the occasional washed-out day. Nadi sits on the dry side of Viti Levu and gets noticeably less rain than the Suva coast in either season.
