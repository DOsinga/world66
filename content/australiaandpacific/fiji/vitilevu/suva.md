Suva is the capital of [Fiji](/australiaandpacific/fiji) and the largest city in the South Pacific outside of Australia and New Zealand. Set on a hilly peninsula on the southeast coast of Viti Levu, it is a more cosmopolitan and urban experience than the resort islands — a working city where you can get a real feel for everyday Fijian life.

The colonial core is concentrated along Victoria Parade, from the white facade of the [Grand Pacific Hotel](/australiaandpacific/fiji/vitilevu/suva/grand_pacific_hotel) and the Art Deco [Government Buildings](/australiaandpacific/fiji/vitilevu/suva/government_buildings) at the southern end, past [Albert Park](/australiaandpacific/fiji/vitilevu/suva/albert_park), through to the busy crossroads at [Sukuna Park](/australiaandpacific/fiji/vitilevu/suva/sukuna_park) and the sandstone [Sacred Heart Cathedral](/australiaandpacific/fiji/vitilevu/suva/sacred_heart_cathedral). The [Fiji Museum](/australiaandpacific/fiji/vitilevu/suva/fiji_museum), set in the lush [Thurston Gardens](/australiaandpacific/fiji/vitilevu/suva/thurston_gardens), is worth a visit for its collection of cannibal-era artefacts, double-hulled canoes, and the rudder of HMS Bounty.

Beyond the colonial set piece, Suva's energy is in its markets and street life. The [Suva Municipal Market](/australiaandpacific/fiji/vitilevu/suva/suva_municipal_market) sells fresh produce, seafood, kava, and handicrafts; the [Handicraft Market](/australiaandpacific/fiji/vitilevu/suva/handicraft_market) on Stinson Parade is the obvious souvenir stop. End the day with a walk along the [seawall](/australiaandpacific/fiji/vitilevu/suva/suva_seawall) at dusk, or a beer at the [Royal Suva Yacht Club](/australiaandpacific/fiji/vitilevu/suva/royal_suva_yacht_club) overlooking the harbour.

Suva is also a practical base for arranging onward travel to the outer islands and the Coral Coast, with ferries leaving from Walu Bay and buses heading west along the Queen's Road.
