The big green rectangle between Victoria Parade and Queen Elizabeth Drive, named after Queen Victoria's consort and at the heart of colonial Suva. Cricket and rugby are played here on Saturdays; school children run laps in the early evening; on big national occasions it is the natural gathering place.

The park is flanked by the [Grand Pacific Hotel](/australiaandpacific/fiji/vitilevu/suva/grand_pacific_hotel) on one side and the [Government Buildings](/australiaandpacific/fiji/vitilevu/suva/government_buildings) on the other, which together give you the entire colonial-era set piece in a single view.
