A long covered hall on the harbour side of Stinson Parade, full of stalls selling wood carvings, woven mats, *masi* (bark cloth), shell jewellery, and the inevitable racks of printed sulus. Quality varies wildly — much of what is sold as "Fijian" was actually made in the Solomons or further afield — but a careful eye finds real things, especially older carved tanoa bowls and turtle-shaped serving platters.

Bargain politely; first prices are typically 50–100 % above what the vendor will accept. The hall is open all week but quieter in the afternoons.
