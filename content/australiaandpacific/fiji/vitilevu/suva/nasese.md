The leafy government quarter, south of the centre along Queen Elizabeth Drive. This is where you will find the [Government Buildings](/australiaandpacific/fiji/vitilevu/suva/government_buildings), the [Parliament of Fiji](/australiaandpacific/fiji/vitilevu/suva/parliament_of_fiji), the [Fiji Museum](/australiaandpacific/fiji/vitilevu/suva/fiji_museum) and [Thurston Gardens](/australiaandpacific/fiji/vitilevu/suva/thurston_gardens), and several of the foreign embassies and high commissions.

The streets are tree-lined and the houses big, slightly faded, with corrugated-iron roofs and wide verandas. The whole quarter is a long walk south from the centre but well worth it on a cooler morning.
