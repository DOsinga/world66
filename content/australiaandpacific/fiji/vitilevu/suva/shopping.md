title: Shopping
type: section
---

Suva is the duty-free shopping centre of Fiji, with prices on electronics, watches, and perfume cheaper than Sydney or Auckland. The shops cluster on Cumming Street, Renwick Road, and Thomson Street — most are run by Indo-Fijian families and most will bargain.

For souvenirs, the [Suva Handicraft Market](/australiaandpacific/fiji/vitilevu/suva/handicraft_market) on Stinson Parade is the obvious stop. For groceries and supermarkets, the big MHCC department store and the Cost-U-Less just outside the centre are the usual choices.
