---
title: "Beaches"
type: section
---

Bora Bora is primarily a lagoon destination rather than a beach destination — the outer motu have the finest white sand, but most of these are attached to private resorts. Public beach access on the main island is limited.

**Matira Beach** at the southern tip of the main island is the best and only true public beach on Bora Bora. About a kilometre of white sand, with calm, shallow water and views across the lagoon toward the outer reef. It gets busy on weekends and in the afternoon; early morning is the best time for swimming and snorkelling. The snorkelling off the northern end of the beach, where the reef comes close to shore, is among the best accessible from the main island.

**Motu Tapu**, the small private islet near the southern end of the lagoon, is the archetypal Bora Bora beach — impossibly white sand, palm trees, turquoise water — and was used in the 1980 film "The Blue Lagoon." It is private but several lagoon tour operators include a stop on the motu as part of their circuit.

The outer motu along the reef — Motu Piti Aau, Motu Tevairoa, Motu Fareone — have the most spectacular beaches and are where the overwater bungalow resorts are concentrated. Day trips to these motu are available from the main island.
