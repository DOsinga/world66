The first of Vaite's Tahitian trilogy follows Materena, a woman who cleans houses for the wealthy and manages the complicated domestic politics of her household in Papeete with warmth, humour, and quiet dignity. Vaite is a Tahitian writer, and this is an insider's portrait of French Polynesian life — not the lagoon-and-overwater-bungalow version, but the version lived by the people who work in the hotels and make the islands run. The trilogy continues with *Frangipani* and *Tiare in Bloom*. Read it and Bora Bora becomes something more than a backdrop.
