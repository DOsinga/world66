The most famous novel of the South Pacific follows the 1789 mutiny against Captain Bligh on HMS Bounty, as told by the fictional narrator Roger Byam. Nordhoff and Hall lived in Tahiti for years — Hall eventually died there — and their Pacific is vivid and specific rather than generic fantasy. The Bounty's crew spent five months in Tahiti before the mutiny, and the book captures why they refused to leave. A page-turner that also happens to be deeply felt, it remains the best introduction to the literature of French Polynesia.
