The classic novelisation of the 1789 mutiny, set largely in Tahiti, where the Bounty spent five months before Fletcher Christian led his uprising against Captain Bligh. Nordhoff and Hall lived in Tahiti themselves (Hall is buried there), and the novel has an authority about Pacific island life that mere research cannot produce. The mutiny itself began — most historians believe — because the crew had spent too long in Tahiti and could not face leaving. You will understand that feeling by the end of your second day on the lagoon.
