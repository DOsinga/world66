---
title: "Getting There"
type: section
---

Bora Bora's Motu Mute Airport sits on a coral islet in the northern lagoon — there is no runway long enough for international jets on the main island. All visitors arriving by air must connect through Papeete's Faa'a International Airport on Tahiti, which handles flights from Los Angeles, Auckland, Tokyo, and Paris (Air Tahiti Nui, Air France, Hawaiian Airlines, and Air New Zealand). The flight from Los Angeles to Papeete takes about eight hours; from Paris, about sixteen.

Air Tahiti operates regular 45-minute propeller flights from Papeete to Bora Bora's Motu Mute Airport. Several flights run daily. From the airport, a shuttle boat ferries passengers across the lagoon to Vaitape quay on the main island or directly to resort motu. The boat ride takes 15 to 20 minutes and is included in most resort transfers.

The Aremiti ferry runs between Papeete and Bora Bora by sea — the crossing takes roughly five hours and operates a few times per week, but is primarily used by residents and cargo rather than tourists.
