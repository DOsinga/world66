---
title: "Getting There"
type: section
---

Bora Bora has its own airport — Motu Mute Airport (BOB) — but it sits on a motu (small islet) on the northern edge of the lagoon, not on the main island. Every arriving and departing passenger takes a boat transfer across the lagoon to reach the island itself. This arrival by water, with Mount Otemanu rising ahead of you, is one of the finest entrances to any destination in the world.

There are no direct long-haul international flights to Bora Bora. All connections go through Papeete's Faa'a International Airport (PPT) on Tahiti. Air Tahiti operates several daily flights between Papeete and Bora Bora — the flight takes about 45 minutes. Most international visitors fly into Papeete from Los Angeles, Paris, Auckland, or Tokyo and connect the same day.

Air Tahiti Nui and Air France serve Papeete from Paris; United, Air Tahiti Nui, and French Bee from Los Angeles. A stop in Papeete is unavoidable, and it is worth building in time to see the city rather than treating it purely as a transit hub.
