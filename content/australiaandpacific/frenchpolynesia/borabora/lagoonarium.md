The Bora Bora Lagoonarium occupies a small motu in the western part of the lagoon and offers structured snorkelling encounters with the marine life that inhabits the shallow water around it. Rays, turtles, reef sharks, and dense schools of tropical fish are all present in calm, very shallow water, making this the ideal introduction to the lagoon for children or less confident swimmers.

Unlike the open-water shark and ray encounters offered by most lagoon tour operators, the Lagoonarium contains the action in a more controlled area — animals have been accustomed to human presence for decades and approach readily. The facility operates morning and afternoon sessions and includes boat transfer from the main island. A good choice for families and a worthwhile half-day stop for any traveller spending several days on the island.
