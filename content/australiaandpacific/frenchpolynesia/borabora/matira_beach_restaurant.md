The most affordable sit-down option near Matira Beach, serving Polynesian and French food at prices that feel reasonable by Bora Bora standards. Poisson cru, grilled fish, and cheeseburgers for lunch; slightly more elaborate seafood and meat dishes in the evening. Tables are set on a terrace with a view of the lagoon and the motu across the water. A solid choice for travellers staying at the Matira end of the island rather than in the resort hotels.
