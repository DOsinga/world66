Bora Bora's outer reef wall offers some of the best diving in French Polynesia. The reef drop-offs reach depths of 30 to 50 metres, with regular sightings of hammerhead sharks, manta rays, Napoleon wrasse, and large grouper. The Anau dive site on the northern reef is known for manta rays, particularly between July and November. The Tapu site on the eastern pass sees regular hammerhead sharks in season. Several PADI-certified dive centres operate from the main island and the major resort motu. Conditions are generally excellent year-round, with water temperatures staying between 26 and 30 degrees Celsius.
