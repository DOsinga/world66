The main island of Bora Bora is small enough to circumnavigate in a few hours by bicycle (the road is about 32 kilometres), scooter, or car. The circuit passes through Vaitape (the main village), the WWII gun emplacements, Matira Beach, the villages of Anau and Faanui, and the fish market at the northern end. A guided jeep tour covers all of this with commentary; several operators run 4WD tours into the interior as well. On your own, a scooter rental from Vaitape is the most practical and enjoyable option for a half-day.
