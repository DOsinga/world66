The signature experience of Bora Bora: a half-day boat tour of the lagoon, stopping to snorkel with blacktip reef sharks and leopard rays in the shallow waters inside the reef, then moving to a coral garden for tropical fish, then usually to a private motu (islet) for a picnic lunch. The sharks and rays are completely habituated to visitors and approach closely — the rays in particular will swim up and press against you for fish scraps. Most operators depart from the main island or the resort motu. A full-day version covers more of the lagoon and is worth the extra time.

story: >
  The blacktip reef sharks in Bora Bora's lagoon have been fed by tour operators for so long that they associate boats with food and arrive within minutes. Despite their size — most are one to two metres — they are genuinely docile in these conditions and have an excellent safety record. The same cannot always be said for the lemon sharks occasionally seen deeper in the lagoon.
