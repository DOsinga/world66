A private marine park on a small islet in the lagoon, offering a more controlled encounter with rays, turtles, lemon sharks, and tropical fish in shallow, enclosed water. Ideal for visitors who want guaranteed close-up access to marine life without committing to an open-water lagoon tour. The fish are accustomed to being hand-fed and approach closely. A good option for families with children or less confident swimmers. The Lagoonarium sits in the southern lagoon and is reached by boat from the main island or the resort motu.
