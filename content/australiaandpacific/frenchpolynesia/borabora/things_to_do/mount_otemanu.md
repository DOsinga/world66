The extinct volcanic peak that dominates Bora Bora's skyline, rising 727 metres from the lagoon. True summit attempts require serious climbing experience and equipment — the final approach involves exposed rock faces — but guided hikes reach high ridgelines with sweeping views over the entire lagoon, the coral reef, and the outer Pacific. The vegetation on the way up shifts from coastal scrub through dense jungle to open ridgeline with flowering plants. Most guided hikes take three to five hours round trip. The adjacent Mount Pahia (661 m) is more accessible and offers similar views.
