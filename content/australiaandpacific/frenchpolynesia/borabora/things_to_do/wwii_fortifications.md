Bora Bora served as a major American supply and refuelling base from 1942, the first US military base established outside North America after Pearl Harbor. The garrison peaked at 6,000 troops on an island of just 3,000 residents. American forces built gun emplacements, fuel tanks, an airstrip (now the island's airport), and a network of fortifications across the hills. The guns at the top of Matira Point and several other sites are accessible on guided jeep tours around the island. The seven-inch coastal defense guns remain in remarkably good condition, still pointing out to sea where Japanese ships never came.
