---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

Bora Bora sits within the tropics and is warm year-round, but the two seasons are distinct.

**Dry season (May to October)** is the best time to visit. Temperatures range from 22 to 28 degrees Celsius, humidity is relatively low, and rainfall is minimal. Trade winds keep the heat comfortable. The lagoon is at its calmest and clearest in the early dry season (May to July). July and August are the busiest months — this is the peak of both the European and American summer holiday seasons — and prices and crowds are at their highest.

**Wet season (November to April)** brings higher humidity, heavier rainfall, and the possibility of cyclones (most likely between December and March). Rain typically comes in heavy afternoon showers rather than continuous overcast, so mornings are often fine. The lagoon is slightly less clear after rain. Prices drop significantly in the wet season, and you will have the island more to yourself.

**Heiva i Bora Bora** (July) is the main annual festival — a month of traditional Polynesian music, dance, outrigger canoe racing, and sporting competitions. The Tahitian dance competitions are particularly impressive and worth timing a visit around.

The manta rays at Anau dive site are most reliably present between July and November.
