---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

Bora Bora lies within the South Pacific tropics and has two distinct seasons.

The **dry season** (May to October) is the best time to visit. Trade winds keep the humidity manageable, rainfall is low, and the lagoon is at its most transparent. Temperatures hover around 27°C (80°F) during the day. July and August see the most visitors, so book well in advance for peak season.

The **wet season** (November to April) brings higher temperatures, humidity, and occasional heavy downpours — though these often pass quickly. Cyclones are possible between December and March, though they are relatively rare in French Polynesia compared to other Pacific regions. The upside: the island is quieter, prices are lower, and the landscape is at its most vibrantly green.

For most visitors, May or June — the early dry season — offers an ideal balance of good weather, manageable crowds, and slightly lower rates than peak summer months.
