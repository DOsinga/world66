---
title: "Getting Around"
type: section
---

The 60-kilometre coast road that loops around Moorea is the only road you need. Most visitors hire transport from the Vaiare ferry terminal on arrival.

**Scooters** are the standard option — agile on the narrow coast road, cheap (around 6,000 CFP a day) and the easiest way to do the full circumnavigation in a relaxed half-day. Helmets are required.

**Cars** are roughly twice the price of scooters but useful if you have luggage, are travelling as a family, or want to keep the dry option. Local hire firms (Avis, Europcar, Albert) have desks at the ferry terminal and the airport.

**Le Truck** — a converted open-sided truck-bus — used to be the local public transport but has been largely replaced by infrequent scheduled buses connecting Vaiare with Pape'ete-bound ferries. Not really viable for getting around the island.

**Bicycles** are available in some of the resort areas and work for shorter circuits, though the road can be hot and tour buses don't always give a generous margin.

**Hitchhiking** is reasonably common locally and generally safe.
