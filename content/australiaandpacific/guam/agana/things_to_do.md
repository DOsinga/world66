---
title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Despite its small size, Hagåtña concentrates most of Guam's historical and cultural sights. The Spanish colonial core around Plaza de España, the Chocolate House, the Spanish bridge, the cathedral, and Fort Santa Agueda can be covered on foot in a couple of hours. The Guam Museum warrants a longer stop. Add Latte Stone Park, the Chief Quipuha statue on the Paseo de Susana, and the Wednesday evening night market at Chamorro Village to round out a full day.
