title: Things to Do
type: section
---

Agat is a small village, and most of its appeal is bound up with the events of July 1944. The War in the Pacific National Historical Park unit at Gaan Point holds the visible relics — a Japanese tank, gun positions, interpretive panels — and the long sweep of the invasion beach is where the US Marines came ashore. Beyond the war sites, the bay is good for snorkelling, and the Mount Alifan ridge above the village offers walking with a long view back over the southwest coast.
