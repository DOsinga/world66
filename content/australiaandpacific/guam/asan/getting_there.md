---
title: Getting There
type: section
---

Asan sits on Marine Corps Drive west of [Hagatna](/australiaandpacific/guam/agana), roughly ten minutes by car from the capital and twenty minutes from Tumon in normal traffic. Guam's public transport is too limited to rely on for these sites, so rent a car or take a taxi if you are not on a tour.

The beach unit is easy to find along the coast road. Asan Bay Overlook is inland on Route 6; the climb is short but puts the battle landscape into much better perspective.
