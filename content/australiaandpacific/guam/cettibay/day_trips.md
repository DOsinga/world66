---
title: Day Trips
type: section
---

The southern coastal road (Route 4) strings together several of Guam's most historically and scenically interesting spots, all within easy driving distance of Cetti Bay.

**Umatac** is the closest village to the south, about 5 km along the coast. It is where Ferdinand Magellan is said to have made his first landfall in the Marianas in 1521, and the village commemorates this with a fort and an annual festival. The bay at Umatac is calm and picturesque, and there are remnants of Spanish-era defences on the hillside above. It takes about 20 minutes to poke around.

**Merizo** lies a few kilometres beyond Umatac and is one of the best-preserved Chamorro villages on Guam. From Merizo, short boat trips run out to **Cocos Island** — a small reef-ringed island with good snorkelling and a calm, shallow lagoon. Cocos Island is one of the few places in the Guam area where you can actually swim in clear water without strong currents.

**Inarajan** on the southeastern coast, reached by continuing on Route 4 or cutting across the island, has a natural swimming pool formed by a reef — popular with families and one of the better swimming spots on the southern coast.

The full loop around southern Guam on Route 4 takes about three hours without stopping; allow a full day if you want to stop at Umatac, Merizo, and Inarajan.
