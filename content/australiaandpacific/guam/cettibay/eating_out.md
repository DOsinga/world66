---
title: Eating Out
type: section
---

There are no restaurants in Cetti Bay itself — this is one of the most remote and undeveloped corners of Guam. The nearest reliable options are in the village of Umatac to the south, which has a small store and occasional food stalls, or in Agat to the north. If you are spending the day hiking or at the overlook, bring your own food and water. The southern coastal road has very limited services along its entire length.
