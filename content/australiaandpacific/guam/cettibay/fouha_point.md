Fouha Point, also known as Creation Point, is a National Natural Landmark on the southwestern coast of Guam near Cetti Bay. According to Chamorro oral tradition, this rocky promontory is the place where the first human beings were created — making it one of the most sacred sites in Chamorro culture. The point juts into the sea at the base of forested cliffs, and the coastal scenery here is raw and dramatic.

Reaching Fouha Point requires hiking down from the road through secondary jungle. The trail is not always well-marked, and you should ask locally before attempting it. The point itself is accessible at low tide; at high tide the rocks are washed over. The combination of the cultural significance, the natural setting, and the general absence of other visitors makes this one of the most memorable stops on the southern coastal road.
