---
title: Getting Around
type: section
---

Once at Cetti Bay, the area is navigated on foot. The Cetti Bay Overlook itself is a short walk from the roadside car park — a few minutes along a concrete path. The Mount Lamlam trail begins near the overlook and takes about 45 minutes to reach the summit; the path is clear but steep in places. Fouha Point requires a longer walk through secondary jungle and is less straightforward — the trail is not always well-marked, and it is worth asking locally before attempting it.

There is no shuttle or organised transport within the area. Distances between the overlook, the Mount Lamlam trailhead, and the Fouha Point access track are small enough that you can drive between them in a few minutes. Wear sturdy shoes, bring more water than you think you need, and start hikes in the morning before the heat builds.
