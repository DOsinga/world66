Dededo is the most populous village in Guam, occupying the northern part of the island above the tourist corridor of Tumon Bay. It is primarily a residential area, home to a large proportion of Guam's population including many of the military families associated with the US bases on the island. Visitors rarely come here to stay, but the northern tip of the island that Dededo opens up is worth the drive.

The [Chamorro Village Night Market](/australiaandpacific/guam/dededo/chamorro_village_night_market) — held each Wednesday evening in Hagåtña but associated with northern Guam's Chamorro community — is the most accessible cultural experience on the island. The real draw further north is [Ritidian Point](/australiaandpacific/guam/dededo/ritidian_point), where a wildlife refuge protects one of Guam's least disturbed beaches and the best accessible reef snorkelling on the island. The forest behind the beach contains ancient Chamorro latte stones and cave drawings.
