Tanguisson Beach is a scenic northern Guam beach below limestone cliffs, with reef-fringed water and distinctive coastal rock formations. It feels more rugged than the hotel-zone beaches and rewards visitors looking for a less polished shoreline stop.

Conditions matter, especially for swimming and reef access, so check surf and currents before entering the water. Even without swimming, the beach is worth a look for its cliffs, trees, and quieter coastal atmosphere.
