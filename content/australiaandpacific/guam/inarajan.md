Inarajan, officially restored as Inalåhan, is one of the most atmospheric villages in southern Guam. It sits on the island's quieter southeast coast, away from the resort strip, with old houses, a tight village centre, and a stronger sense of Chamorro continuity than you find in much of the north.

The usual stop is [Inarajan Natural Pool](/australiaandpacific/guam/inarajan/inarajan_natural_pool), a protected set of rock pools beside the sea. But the village is more than a swim: [Gef Pa'go Chamorro Cultural Village](/australiaandpacific/guam/inarajan/gef_pago) gives context to traditional food, crafts, and houses, while [Gadao's Cave](/australiaandpacific/guam/inarajan/gadaos_cave) and [Bear Rock](/australiaandpacific/guam/inarajan/bear_rock) add the older landscape of legends and coastal forms.

Inarajan works best as part of the southern loop from [Umatac](/australiaandpacific/guam/umatac) and [Merizo](/australiaandpacific/guam/merizo). Bring a swimsuit, but leave time to walk the village slowly.
