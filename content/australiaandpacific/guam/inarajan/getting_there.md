---
title: Getting There
type: section
---

Inarajan is on Guam's southern loop road, about 35 to 45 minutes from Hagåtña by car depending on route and traffic. Most visitors combine it with Umatac, Merizo, and Talofofo Falls in a full-day circuit.

There is no reliable public transport for independent sightseeing here. Roads are generally paved, but village lanes are narrow and parking near the natural pools can be tight on weekends.
