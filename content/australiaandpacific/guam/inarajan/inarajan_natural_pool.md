Inarajan Natural Pool is a set of seawater pools enclosed by volcanic rock on the edge of the village. The pools are sheltered from the open Pacific, which makes them calmer than the surrounding coast and one of the most popular swimming stops on the southern loop.

Conditions still matter. Avoid the pools in rough weather or after storms, and respect any closure signs if platforms or access points are damaged. On a calm weekday morning, though, this is a lovely place to cool off before continuing around the island.
