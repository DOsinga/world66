---
title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Mangilao is a quiet residential village whose draw is the university and the dramatic Pago Bay coastline at its southern edge. The **University of Guam** campus is open and walkable; the **Micronesian Area Research Center** there is the best place in the western Pacific to read about the region's history. South along Route 4, the road opens up to a long view across **Pago Bay** with crashing surf below.
