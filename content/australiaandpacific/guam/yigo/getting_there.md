---
title: Getting There
type: section
---

Yigo is roughly 25 to 35 minutes north of Hagåtña or Tumon by car, depending on traffic. Marine Corps Drive is the main approach, with side roads leading toward the South Pacific Memorial Park, Mount Santa Rosa, and the northern refuge road.

A rental car is effectively required. If you continue to Ritidian Point, check access before you go; the final road can be rough and refuge hours or closures change.
