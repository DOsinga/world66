---
title: When to Go
type: section
---

Abemama lies just north of the equator and has a classic equatorial climate — warm and humid year-round, with temperatures hovering around 28–32°C at all times. There is no true dry season, but the period from June to September is generally drier and the trade winds are steadier, which makes conditions better for sailing and more comfortable for visitors.

The wettest months run from November through February, when heavy squalls are common and the humidity peaks. Cyclones are rare this close to the equator but not impossible — check current conditions before travelling in the southern summer.

The atoll's remoteness means there is no high season in any meaningful sense. You are unlikely to find the island crowded at any time of year.
