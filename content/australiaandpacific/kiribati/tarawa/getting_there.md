---
title: "Getting There"
type: section
---

Getting to Tarawa is the first real travel challenge. **Bonriki International Airport (TRW)** is the only international gateway and is served by a small handful of flights, all of them subject to change at short notice.

**Fiji Airways** flies from **Nadi (Fiji)** via either Funafuti (Tuvalu) or Honiara (Solomon Islands), typically a couple of times per week. **Nauru Airlines** runs a "island hopper" link from **Brisbane** via Nauru, Honiara, and Tarawa. There are also internal flights operated by **Air Kiribati** connecting Tarawa with the outer islands and with Kiritimati (Christmas Island) far to the east.

Schedules are sparse and cancellations or weather-related delays are common. Build a couple of extra days into either end of your itinerary and do not book onward connections too tight. Confirm reservations directly with the airline before you fly — the published schedules are not always current.
