---
title: "Bars and Cafes"
type: section
---

There is no civilian bar or café scene on Kwajalein Atoll. The US military base on Kwajalein Island has a recreation club available to residents and invited guests, but it is not open to independent visitors. Ebeye has no notable bar scene; alcohol availability is limited and the island's social infrastructure reflects its difficult economic circumstances.

If you are a base resident or authorised guest, the recreation facilities on Kwajalein are the extent of the options.
