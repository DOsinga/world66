---
title: Getting There
type: section
---

Reaching Maloelap takes planning. Air Marshall Islands has historically served outer-atoll airstrips when aircraft and schedules permit, but flights can be irregular and should not be treated as guaranteed connections.

The more traditional route is by government or field-trip vessel from [Majuro](/australiaandpacific/marshallislands/majuro). These ships carry passengers and supplies between outer islands, but timing can shift with weather, cargo, maintenance, and local needs. Build in extra days, confirm arrangements locally, and do not plan a tight international connection after returning.
