Maloelap Lagoon is the atoll's great open space, a sheltered sheet of blue water enclosed by thin islets and reef. It is the setting for fishing, small-boat travel, and the slow daily rhythm that makes the outer islands so different from Majuro.

Snorkelling can be excellent where local guides know the passages and coral heads, but this is not a marked beach resort. Bring your own gear, ask locally about currents, and treat the lagoon as working water rather than a managed attraction.
