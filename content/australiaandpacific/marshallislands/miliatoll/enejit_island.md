Enejit is one of the outer islets on Mili Atoll, useful less as a formal attraction than as a reminder of how spread out the atoll really is. Moving between islets changes the scale of the place: the villages fall away, the lagoon opens up, and the reef line starts to dominate everything.

A visit depends entirely on local boat arrangements and conditions. If you are offered the chance to go, treat it as a slow outer-atoll outing rather than a scheduled excursion, and bring water, sun protection, and flexibility.
