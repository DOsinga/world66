---
title: Getting There
type: section
---

Mili is reached from [Majuro](/australiaandpacific/marshallislands/majuro) by irregular domestic air service or by field-trip vessel. The airstrip is on Mili Island, but outer-island aviation in the Marshalls is vulnerable to weather, maintenance, and changing schedules.

Boats are slower but often more realistic for people with time. Ask in Majuro about the latest field-trip ship routing, confirm where the vessel lands, and arrange local accommodation before departure.
