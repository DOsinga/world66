Mili Lagoon is wide, quiet, and barely touched by tourism. The water inside the reef is used for fishing and local transport, and on calm days it has the glassy, enormous feel that makes Marshallese atolls so memorable.

Snorkelling and reef walks depend on weather, tide, and where you are staying. Bring your own equipment, ask before crossing land or reef flats, and take local advice seriously around passes and currents.
