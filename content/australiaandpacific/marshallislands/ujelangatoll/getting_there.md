---
title: Getting There
type: section
---

Ujelang is not a standard travel stop. There are no regular visitor services, and transport depends on government, community, research, or private-vessel arrangements.

Anyone with a legitimate reason to go should start by asking in [Majuro](/australiaandpacific/marshallislands/majuro) and through Marshallese contacts connected to Enewetak and Ujelang. Do not treat the atoll as an open-access yacht stop without checking permission and local expectations.
