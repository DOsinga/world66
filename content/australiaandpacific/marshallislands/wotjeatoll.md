Wotje Atoll sits in the northern Ratak Chain, a long arc of low islets around a large lagoon. It has more of a settled outer-island feel than many remote atolls: there is a village, a school serving northern islands, an airstrip, churches, and the usual Marshallese rhythm of fishing, copra, church days, and boat arrivals.

The atoll's visitor interest is strongest around [Wotje village and its wartime remains](/australiaandpacific/marshallislands/wotjeatoll/wotje_village_and_wwii_remains). Japan fortified Wotje during World War II, and traces of that period still sit among ordinary village life. The [lagoon](/australiaandpacific/marshallislands/wotjeatoll/wotje_lagoon) is the other draw: big, sheltered, and good for slow boat travel when conditions cooperate.

As with the other outer atolls, Wotje takes patience. Services are basic, schedules shift, and you should make arrangements through [Majuro](/australiaandpacific/marshallislands/majuro) before showing up. It is a strong choice if you want the outer-island experience without going quite as far off the map as Ujelang or Enewetak.
