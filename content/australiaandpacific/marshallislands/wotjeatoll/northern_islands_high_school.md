Northern Islands High School is not a tourist sight in the usual sense, and visitors should not wander in uninvited. Its presence matters because it makes Wotje an educational hub for students from scattered northern atolls, giving the village a role beyond its own population.

If you pass nearby with local permission, it helps explain why Wotje feels more active than some outer islands. Schools, churches, and boats tie these remote atolls together as much as airports or government offices do.
