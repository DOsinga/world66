title: Things to Do
type: section
---

Kosrae offers world-class diving, jungle hiking, and the ruins of a pre-contact Micronesian city. Its isolation — no cruise ships, almost no resort tourism — means that what you find here is essentially unmediated: reefs that have barely been touched, a rainforest interior that requires a guide, and a small community whose warmth toward the rare visitor is genuine rather than performed.
