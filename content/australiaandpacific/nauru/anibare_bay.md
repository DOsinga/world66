Anibare Bay is Nauru's best beach stop: a long curve of white sand, palms, clear water, and coral pinnacles on the island's east coast. It is one of the few places on Nauru where swimming feels like the classic Pacific island experience.

Come early for sunrise, when Nauru's eastern position gives the bay especially good light. Anibare Harbour, at the southern end, is also a good place to watch local fishing boats come in.
