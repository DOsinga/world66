Buada Lagoon is Nauru's only freshwater body, a quiet inland basin ringed by palms and dense vegetation. The water is not for swimming, but the lagoon is one of the island's softer landscapes and a good contrast to the mined interior and coastal road.

A sealed road loops around the area, making it an easy short walk or photo stop. It is more a place to understand Nauru's geography than a polished attraction.
