Topside is the mined-out interior of Nauru, where decades of phosphate extraction left a strange field of jagged limestone pinnacles, rusting equipment, and scrubby regrowth. It is not pretty in the usual island sense, but it is one of the most revealing landscapes in the Pacific.

The area explains Nauru better than any viewpoint or beach: the island's brief wealth, environmental damage, and difficult present are all visible here. Go with local guidance, especially around active or restricted areas.
