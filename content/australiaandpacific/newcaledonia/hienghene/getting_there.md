---
title: "Getting There"
type: section
---

Hienghène is on the northeast coast of Grande Terre, around 380 kilometres from Nouméa by road — a full day's drive, even on the (mostly good) RT1. The most rewarding route follows the east coast through Poindimié; the west-coast route via Koné is faster but visually less interesting. There is no air service; a car is essentially required, and rental from Nouméa or Tontouta airport is the standard approach.

Public transport is sparse. The RAÏ buses run from Nouméa to Hienghène a few times a week but the schedule is awkward and you lose all flexibility for stops along the way. Petrol is widely available in the larger east-coast towns; fill up before heading into the more remote stretches.
