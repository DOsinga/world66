Beside the more famous Poule sits the Sphinx — another upthrust limestone formation that, from the right angle, suggests the head and forelegs of an enormous reclining cat. The two together dominate the entrance to Hienghène bay and form one of the most distinctive coastal silhouettes in the South Pacific.

Local Kanak narratives connect both formations to the spirits of the land and they are still considered sacred sites. Visitors view them from the road and the viewpoints rather than approaching directly.
