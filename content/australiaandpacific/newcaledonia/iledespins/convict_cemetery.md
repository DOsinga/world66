The Convict Cemetery is a quiet reminder of Ile des Pins' penal-colony history. Hundreds of convicts are buried here, including Paris Communards who died from harsh conditions or during failed escape attempts.

Most graves are unmarked, but the memorial records many names and gives the site a clear historical weight beyond the island's beaches.
