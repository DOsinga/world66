The ruins of the convict prison are one of Ile des Pins' more sombre historic sites. The old brick walls and broken prison buildings are reminders of the island's use as a French penal colony in the 19th century.

The site is quiet and partly overgrown, which suits it. Visit with the nearby convict cemetery for a fuller sense of this darker chapter in the island's history.
