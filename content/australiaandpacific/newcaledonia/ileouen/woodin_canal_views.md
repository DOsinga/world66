The Woodin Canal separates Île Ouen from Grande Terre and gives the island some of its drama. From the boat, the channel frames both sides of the Grand Sud: red earth, scrubby hills, reef water, and the sense that Nouméa has suddenly fallen a long way behind.

The best views are not necessarily from a single marked lookout. They come as you cross or skirt the canal, especially in clear light when the water shifts from blue to green over the reef shelves.
