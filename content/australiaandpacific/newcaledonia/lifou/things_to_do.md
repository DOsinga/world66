---
title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Lifou's main draws are natural: cliffs, caves, coral, and beaches. The [Jokin Cliffs](/australiaandpacific/newcaledonia/lifou/jokin_cliffs) on the northeast coast are the most dramatic sight on the island — fossilized coral formations rising up to 60 meters above the sea, with views down the coast that are hard to beat. The protected [Jinek Bay Marine Reserve](/australiaandpacific/newcaledonia/lifou/jinek_bay_marine_reserve), on the west coast, is widely regarded as one of the best snorkeling sites in the Pacific.

For something quieter, the [Xodre Cliffs](/australiaandpacific/newcaledonia/lifou/xodre_cliffs) in the south offer the same drama with none of the crowds, and the [Luengoni Caves](/australiaandpacific/newcaledonia/lifou/luengoni_caves) hide freshwater pools in the limestone behind one of Lifou's prettiest beaches. The [Notre Dame de Lourdes Chapel](/australiaandpacific/newcaledonia/lifou/notre_dame_de_lourdes_chapel) above Easo is a short walk and an outstanding viewpoint. To meet the people who make the island work, arrange a tour of one of the family-run [vanilla plantations](/australiaandpacific/newcaledonia/lifou/vanilla_plantations).
