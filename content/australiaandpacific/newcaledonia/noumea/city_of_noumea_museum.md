The City of Noumea Museum occupies the city's first bank building, later used as the town hall for nearly a century. The setting alone gives it character, but the exhibits are useful too, tracing Noumea from the convict era through municipal growth, nickel, war, and modern city life.

It is a practical first museum for understanding the capital rather than only the wider territory. English material and audio guidance make it more accessible than many small local museums in the region.
