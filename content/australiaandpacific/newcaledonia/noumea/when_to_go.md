---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

New Caledonia has a subtropical climate. The best time to visit Nouméa is during the dry season, from June to October — warm rather than hot (22–26°C), low humidity, clear skies, and calm lagoon conditions ideal for diving and snorkelling. July and August are the peak months for Australian visitors and accommodation can be tight.

The wet season runs from December through March, with higher humidity, occasional cyclones, and tropical downpours. The sea remains warm and the reef diving is still excellent, but heat and rain can make extended outdoor sightseeing uncomfortable. April and May are an excellent shoulder period — quieter, still warm, and with good visibility in the lagoon.
