Akaroa Head guards the harbour entrance and gives a wilder edge to a town better known for French street names and dolphin cruises. The historic reserve includes military remains, walking country, and wide views across Banks Peninsula's volcanic harbour landscape.

It is far enough from the village to feel like a proper excursion, so check access and conditions before setting out. The reward is a stronger sense of Akaroa's geography: a sheltered settlement tucked inside a dramatic old crater, with the open Pacific just beyond the headland.
