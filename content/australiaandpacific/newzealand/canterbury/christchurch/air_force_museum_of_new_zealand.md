The Air Force Museum of New Zealand at Wigram tells the story of the country's military aviation through aircraft, archives, personal stories, and restored hangars. It is one of Christchurch's strongest specialist museums.

Allow more time than you might expect if you like aircraft or twentieth-century history. The museum is outside the central city, so plan transport and check for guided-tour or restoration-area options.
