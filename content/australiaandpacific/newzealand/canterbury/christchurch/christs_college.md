Christ's College is a historic boys' school beside Hagley Park, founded in 1850 and built around the traditions of an English public school. Its stone buildings and quadrangles make it one of central Christchurch's more distinctive heritage sites.

Guided tours give access to parts of the campus that casual visitors would otherwise only see from the edge.
