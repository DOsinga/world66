The CTV Memorial Garden marks the site of the Canterbury Television building, where 115 people died in the 2011 Christchurch earthquake. The garden is simple and sombre, with planting that gives the site a quiet dignity amid the rebuilt city centre.

It is a short stop rather than a conventional attraction, but it helps make sense of modern Christchurch. Visit with the same restraint you would bring to any disaster memorial.
