Ferrymead Heritage Park is an open-air museum of Christchurch and Canterbury's transport and everyday history. Historic trams, railway equipment, workshops, cottages, and recreated streets make it especially good for families and anyone who likes practical machinery rather than glass-case displays.

The park sits toward the estuary, away from the central rebuild zone, and needs a little time to do properly. Check operating days for trams and trains, as the liveliest visits are when volunteers have the heritage transport running.
