Hagley Oval is Christchurch's premier cricket ground, tucked into the broad green space of Hagley Park. Its setting is part of the appeal: mature trees, lawns, and nearby gardens make it feel less like a sealed stadium and more like a sporting ground within the city's public landscape.

For cricket fans, a match here is a memorable way to spend a day in Christchurch. Outside fixtures, it is still worth noting while walking between the Botanic Gardens and the park, especially as a symbol of the city's post-earthquake sporting rebuild.
