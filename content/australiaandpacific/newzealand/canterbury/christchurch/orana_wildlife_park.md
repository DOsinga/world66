Orana Wildlife Park is Christchurch's open-range animal park, spread over a large site west of the city. The scale lets many animals live in broad paddocks rather than tight urban enclosures, and the visit feels more spacious than a conventional zoo.

Families will find the easiest appeal, but it is also a good option for travellers with a spare half-day and a car. Native species, big cats, giraffes, and farm-style encounters make it varied enough to justify the trip beyond central Christchurch.
