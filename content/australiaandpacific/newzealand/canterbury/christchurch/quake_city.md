Quake City is a Canterbury Museum exhibition about the earthquakes that reshaped Christchurch in 2010 and 2011. Displays explain the science of the quakes, the damage they caused, and the city's recovery.

It is a strong companion to central Christchurch walks, especially for understanding the empty sites, memorials, new buildings, and preserved ruins around the city centre.
