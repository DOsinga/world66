The Sign of Walkway follows the Port Hills from Sign of the Takahe towards Sign of the Kiwi and Sign of the Bellbird. It is a classic Christchurch hill walk, combining Depression-era rest houses, Victoria Park, Summit Road views, and broad looks back over the city and plains.
