The Teece Museum of Classical Antiquities holds artefacts from Greek, Roman, Egyptian, and Near Eastern cultures. It is a focused museum rather than a large all-purpose collection, making it easy to fit into a central Christchurch walk.

It is most rewarding for visitors interested in ancient history, classical art, or smaller university-linked collections.
