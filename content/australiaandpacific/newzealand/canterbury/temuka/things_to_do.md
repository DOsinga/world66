---
title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Temuka's main attractions are tied to its rural setting and to the legacy of pioneer aviator Richard Pearse. The town centre rewards a slow walk along the main street, with its preserved Edwardian frontages. Beyond town, the Ōpihi and Rangitata rivers are renowned for salmon and trout fishing, and the wider Canterbury countryside opens up to the Mackenzie Basin and the Southern Alps.
