Taupo is one of the world's great skydiving destinations, and on a clear day it is easy to see why: the view from altitude takes in Lake Taupo, the Tongariro volcanic peaks, and on cloudless days extends all the way to the sea in multiple directions. Multiple operators run tandem jumps from the airport south of town, making this one of the most competitive skydiving markets in New Zealand.

You can jump from as low as 9,000 feet (about 25 seconds of freefall) to a maximum of 15,000 feet (roughly 60 seconds). Prices vary accordingly; booking ahead in peak season is advisable. Weather can cancel jumps on short notice, particularly in winter.
