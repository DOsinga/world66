DeBretts is a hot springs resort on the eastern edge of Taupo, drawing water from the same geothermal system that powers the Craters of the Moon. The complex includes a range of pools at different temperatures — from a cool 36°C up to a skin-prickling 44°C — plus a waterslide for families and a quieter section for those who want to soak rather than play.

The outdoor pools have good views over the surrounding landscape. DeBretts is a popular spot for campervan travellers and is considerably less crowded than Rotorua's commercial hot spring complexes. Entry is by the hour and includes access to all pools. Bring a towel.
