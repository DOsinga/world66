Waipahihi Botanical Gardens spread across a hillside above Taupo, with walking and driving tracks through native and exotic plantings. The gardens are especially known for lake views, with Tongariro National Park visible in clear weather.

It is an easy, quiet stop on the edge of town and works well when you want a short walk rather than a full-day outing.
