Awatotara is a privately owned bush block on the western side of Chatham
Island, set aside under a conservation covenant to protect surviving fragments
of native forest and the birds that depend on them. Access is by arrangement
with the landowners and is best done with a local guide who knows the area and
the etiquette around the nesting sites.

This is one of the better places to hear (and with luck see) Chatham Island
warblers, parea (the endemic pigeon), and the occasional Chatham snipe. The
walk through the bush is short but the experience of standing in old kopi
forest with the rain in the canopy is one of the things you came to the
Chathams for.
