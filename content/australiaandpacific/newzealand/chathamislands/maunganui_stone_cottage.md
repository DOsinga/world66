Maunganui Stone Cottage, often called the German Mission House, is a small but
atmospheric reminder of the Chatham Islands' nineteenth-century mission period. Built
from local stone in an isolated setting, it has the plain, weather-tested look of a
building made for survival rather than display.

The appeal is partly the cottage itself and partly the journey across the Chathams'
open, windswept landscape. It is not a polished city museum; it is a quiet heritage stop
that makes the islands' layered Moriori, Maori, missionary, and settler history feel
more tangible.
