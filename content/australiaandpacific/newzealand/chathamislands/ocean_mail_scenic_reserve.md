Ocean Mail Scenic Reserve shows the Chatham Islands at their windswept best: open coastal country, dune systems, low forest, and the feeling of being a long way from the mainland. It is a nature stop rather than a built attraction, and the reward is space, birdlife, and the particular light of the islands.

Access and conditions matter here, so ask locally before setting out and treat tracks with respect. The reserve is best for travellers already committed to the Chathams' slow, outdoorsy rhythm. Bring weatherproof layers; the islands have a way of changing the plan for you.
