---
title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

The Chatham Islands reward slow travel and an interest in birds, history, and
big empty landscapes. Don't miss the
[Moriori dendroglyphs](/australiaandpacific/newzealand/chathamislands/hapupu_dendroglyphs)
at Hapupu, the Moriori cultural centre at
[Kopinga Marae](/australiaandpacific/newzealand/chathamislands/kopinga_marae),
and a walk to the basalt columns of
[Ohira Bay](/australiaandpacific/newzealand/chathamislands/ohira_bay).

For wildlife, visit the
[Awatotara Reserve](/australiaandpacific/newzealand/chathamislands/awatotara_reserve)
or arrange a guided trip to see the recovering populations of black robin and
Chatham petrel. The remote
[Pitt Island](/australiaandpacific/newzealand/chathamislands/pitt_island) is a
day trip in its own right, weather permitting.
