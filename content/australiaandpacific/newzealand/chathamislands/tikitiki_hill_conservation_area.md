Tikitiki Hill Conservation Area gives the Chatham Islands one of their useful high points: open hill country, native vegetation, and the wide, exposed feeling that defines these remote islands. The landscape is not dramatic in a postcard-alpine way. Its power is in weather, space, and the sudden views across farms, coast, and low island horizons.

Go prepared for wind and changeable conditions, even if the day starts gently. The area is best for travellers who want to understand the Chathams beyond settlement and shore: the inland ridges, conservation work, and raw scale of a place sitting far out in the Pacific.
