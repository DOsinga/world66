Waitangi (not to be confused with the Northland town of the same name) is the
largest settlement on the Chatham Islands — a small harbour village of a few
hundred residents that serves as the islands' administrative and commercial
centre. The harbour is a working fishing port and the seasonal home of the
Chathams' famously well-stocked crayfishing fleet.

The Chatham Islands Museum, on the main road through town, traces the history
of the archipelago from Moriori settlement to whaling, farming, and the
modern era. There is a small but useful information centre, a couple of cafes,
and the islands' single fuel pump. Most accommodation is dotted around the
nearby coast rather than in the village itself.
