Coromandel Town is the main settlement on the western side of the northern peninsula, a historic gold- and kauri-timber-era town with a laid-back character that has attracted artists and alternative lifestylers since the 1970s. The main street has good cafes and craft shops, and the town is the jumping-off point for the [Driving Creek Railway](/australiaandpacific/newzealand/coromandelpeninsula/driving_creek_railway) and for the road up to the Coromandel Walkway.

The scenic drive north from Coromandel Town — around the coast to Colville and the northern tip — is spectacular on a fine day, with the Coromandel Range dropping steeply to the sea and views across the Hauraki Gulf to the Coromandel's outlying islands. The Coromandel Harbour on the town's doorstep has good fishing.
