Stargazers uses Kūaotunu's dark skies for small astronomy tours, with telescope viewing of planets, star clusters, galaxies, and the southern Milky Way when conditions cooperate. It is weather-dependent, so treat it as a clear-night prize rather than a fixed appointment.
