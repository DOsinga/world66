Tairua is a beach town on the eastern Coromandel coast with good surf and a more relaxed feel than the busy holiday crowds of Hahei. It sits on a harbour estuary backed by Paku Hill — a volcanic remnant with a walking track to the summit — and faces the barrier island of Pauanui across the water.

The Tairua beach has a decent surf break and is popular with families. The town has good cafes, a fishing harbour, and the kind of low-key atmosphere that makes it appealing outside the peak holiday season. Paku Hill is a short but steep climb rewarded with views over the estuary, the coast, and the Pacific.
