Thames Historical Museum covers the town's early European settlement, the 19th-century gold rush, and later industrial development. Its displays include material from the foundries, logging and timber work, mining, and daily life on the Coromandel Peninsula.

It is a good first stop for understanding why Thames grew so quickly and how its gold-mining past still shapes the town.
