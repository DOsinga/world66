Thames Small Gauge Railway is a volunteer-run miniature railway near the town's mining heritage sites. The short line has a heritage-style station, sidings, a turntable, a tunnel, and small bridges, making it more charming than its size suggests.

It is mainly a family stop or a quick add-on after the Thames School of Mines and Goldmine Experience. Check running days before going, as small heritage railways often operate limited hours.
