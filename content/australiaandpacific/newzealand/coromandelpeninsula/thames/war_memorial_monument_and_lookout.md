The War Memorial Monument above Thames doubles as one of the town's easiest viewpoints. From the top of Waiotahi Creek Road you get a broad look over the township, the Firth of Thames, and the flat land edging the Coromandel hills.

It is a short stop rather than a major sight, but it helps orient you before heading deeper into the peninsula. Go in clear weather, when the view across the water does the work.
