The eastern coast provides the iconic experiences: dig your own geothermal hot pool at [Hot Water Beach](/australiaandpacific/newzealand/coromandelpeninsula/hot_water_beach), walk or kayak to [Cathedral Cove](/australiaandpacific/newzealand/coromandelpeninsula/cathedral_cove), and swim in clear Pacific water at beaches largely uncrowded outside the summer holidays.

The [Driving Creek Railway](/australiaandpacific/newzealand/coromandelpeninsula/driving_creek_railway) at Coromandel Town is obligatory — there is nothing quite like it anywhere in New Zealand. The heritage gold-mining history in [Thames](/australiaandpacific/newzealand/coromandelpeninsula/thames) and surrounds rewards a morning.

Walking tracks in the Coromandel Forest Park range from half-day valley strolls to multi-day ridge traverses. The Pinnacles, accessed from [Kauaeranga Valley](/australiaandpacific/newzealand/coromandelpeninsula/kauaeranga_valley), are the most popular overnight walk.
