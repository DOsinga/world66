Whitianga is the main town on the eastern Coromandel coast and the base for exploring Mercury Bay. It has a population of about 4,500 that swells dramatically over the summer — the town sits on a deep harbour sheltered from the Pacific, and the boating, fishing, and diving are excellent. The harbour ferry (a five-minute ride) connects the main town to the eastern shore, from which the roads run south to Hahei, Cathedral Cove, and Hot Water Beach.

The town has good cafes and restaurants for its size — the influx of affluent Aucklanders over summer has driven up the quality. The Mercury Bay Museum covers the local history including Captain Cook's visit in 1769, when he observed the Transit of Mercury from this coast and named the bay accordingly. Whitianga is also a good base for fishing charters targeting snapper and kingfish in the outer bay.
