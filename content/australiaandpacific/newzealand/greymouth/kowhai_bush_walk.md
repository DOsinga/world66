Kowhai Bush Walk is a short native-bush walk in Greymouth, useful when you want a dose of West Coast forest without committing to a long trail. Expect greenery, damp air, birdsong, and the soft underfoot feel that defines many local walks.

It is best treated as a convenient leg-stretcher around town, especially between longer drives or on a showery day when the forest looks fresh. Pair it with the riverfront or one of the coast tracks for a fuller Greymouth stop.
