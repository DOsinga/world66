On Yer Bike Adventures is a deliberately messy West Coast outing: quad bikes, forest tracks, mud, and enough controlled roughness to make it feel different from another scenic drive. It suits Greymouth's outdoorsy, slightly practical character better than a polished resort activity would.

It is best for travellers who want an active half-day and do not mind getting dirty. Wear the supplied gear properly, listen to the safety briefing, and assume weather is part of the experience rather than a reason the track will stay clean.
