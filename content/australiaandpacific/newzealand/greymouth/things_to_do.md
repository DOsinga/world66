title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Greymouth's main draw for travellers is the [TranzAlpine](/australiaandpacific/newzealand/greymouth/tranzalpine_railway) and the West Coast Wilderness Trail. In town, the floodwall walk along the river is the obvious way to spend an afternoon; the History House Museum and Left Bank Art Gallery cover wet weather.
