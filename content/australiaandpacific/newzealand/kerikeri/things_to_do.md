---
title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Kerikeri punches well above its size for a small town. The main draw is the Kerikeri Basin, a compact historic precinct where the oldest surviving buildings in New Zealand stand within a short walk of each other. Beyond the basin, the Kerikeri River offers walks to a fine waterfall and the hills above town are dotted with orchards and art galleries. The Bay of Islands, a short drive away, adds sailing and dolphin watching to the mix.
