Leigh is a small fishing village north of Auckland, but its pull is bigger than its size. The village sits between quiet bays, surf beaches, and Goat Island, where the Cape Rodney-Okakari Point Marine Reserve protects clear water, rocky reef, and snapper that have learned not to fear snorkellers.

The reserve is the reason to come. On a calm day you can snorkel straight from the shore, take a glass-bottom boat, or visit the [Goat Island Marine Discovery Centre](/australiaandpacific/newzealand/leigh/goat_island_marine_discovery_centre) to understand what you are looking at underwater. It is one of the easiest places near Auckland to see New Zealand marine life without a boat or a big plan.

Leigh itself is simple: a wharf, a general store, a few houses, and the [Sawmill Cafe](/australiaandpacific/newzealand/leigh/leigh_sawmill_cafe), a music venue with more personality than most towns this small can claim. Add [Mathesons Bay](/australiaandpacific/newzealand/leigh/mathesons_bay) and long [Pakiri Beach](/australiaandpacific/newzealand/leigh/pakiri_beach), and Leigh becomes a good spare day from Matakana, Warkworth, or Auckland rather than just a dot on the coast.
