The Leigh Sawmill Cafe is the village's social centre: part cafe, part bar, part live-music venue, set in a former sawmill on the road toward Pakiri. It is the place to go after a swim when you want food, a drink, and a room that actually feels local.

Check the gig calendar if you are staying nearby. For a settlement this small, Leigh gets a surprising amount of music through the door.
