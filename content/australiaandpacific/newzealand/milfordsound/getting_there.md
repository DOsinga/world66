title: Getting There
type: section
---

Milford Sound is at the end of State Highway 94, the Milford Road — one of the most spectacular drives in New Zealand. The road runs 120 km north from [Te Anau](/australiaandpacific/newzealand/teanau) through beech forest, past the Mirror Lakes, through the narrow Homer Tunnel, and down to the fjord. Allow two to three hours each way and stop frequently — the scenery along the road is itself a reason to drive rather than coach.

The Homer Tunnel is a single-lane alpine tunnel and often requires a wait. The road can be closed in winter due to avalanche risk; check conditions before setting out.

Coach services run from [Queenstown](/australiaandpacific/newzealand/south_island/otago/queenstown) and Te Anau, usually as part of day-tour packages that include the fjord cruise. Scenic flights from Queenstown are another option and give a very different perspective on the landscape.

There is no public transport to Milford Sound. A rental car from Queenstown or Te Anau is the independent option.
