Broadgreen House is a restored colonial-era home that gives Nelson a domestic-history counterpart to its beaches, cafes, and art scene. Period rooms, furniture, textiles, and surrounding gardens show how prosperous settler households presented themselves.

The visit is compact and gentle, suited to people interested in architecture, gardens, or local history. It pairs well with a wider Nelson heritage walk rather than demanding a trip on its own.
