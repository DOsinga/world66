The Elliott Street Heritage Precinct preserves one of Nelson's early state-housing areas, with houses from the 1860s through the 1940s spread around Elliott, Trafalgar, and Collingwood Streets. It is not a museum district, but a lived-in streetscape where the value is in the pattern of modest historic homes.

It works best as a short self-guided walk while exploring central Nelson, especially if you are already visiting the market, cathedral area, or Maitai River walkway.
