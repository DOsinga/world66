Elliott Street is a small but unusually complete heritage area, with early Nelson houses and state housing from the 1860s through the 1940s. It is not a blockbuster sight, but it rewards a slow walk if you like domestic architecture and want a glimpse of how the city grew beyond its commercial centre.
