Founders Heritage Park is an open-air museum built around an assembly of relocated colonial-era buildings: a church, a windmill, a print shop, a smithy, an old jail. Some are originals moved from elsewhere in the region; others are accurate reproductions. The result is a small village of working trades that you can walk through at your own pace.

A craft brewery, an organic bakery, and a chocolate maker now occupy some of the buildings, which gives the place an actual reason to come back beyond the heritage interest. The 1840s windmill at the centre is the largest functioning windmill in New Zealand. The park is at Atawhai, a few minutes north of central Nelson.
