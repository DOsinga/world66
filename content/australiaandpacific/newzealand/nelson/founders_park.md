Founders Park is Nelson's living-history village, assembled from historic buildings moved here from around the region. Many of the interiors work as small museums, with displays on local trades, daily life, and settlement history. It is more varied than a standard heritage street, with a weekend railway, craft food stops, and enough detail to reward a slow wander.
