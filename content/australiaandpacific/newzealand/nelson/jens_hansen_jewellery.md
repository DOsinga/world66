story: >
  When Peter Jackson's production designers needed someone to forge the One Ring, they came to this small Nelson workshop. Danish-born goldsmith Jens Hansen made several versions; one was used in the films, others were sold as props or kept. The workshop still produces replicas to order — plain gold bands of varying weights, no engraving, exactly as on screen.
---

The workshop on Trafalgar Street is part jeweller, part minor Lord of the Rings pilgrimage site. You can look at the original prototype rings in a small display, watch jewellers at the bench through a glass partition, and — if you have the budget — order a replica One Ring in your size.

The rest of the studio's output is more conventional contemporary jewellery: silver and gold, often with a Scandinavian-restraint look that suits the founder's heritage. Jens Hansen himself died in 1999, but the workshop continues under family ownership and remains one of Nelson's quirkier small businesses.
