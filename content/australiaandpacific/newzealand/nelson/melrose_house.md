Melrose House is a Victorian-era house set above Nelson's centre, with heritage rooms, gardens, and a popular cafe. It is not a grand mansion, but it does preserve a gentler domestic side of the city's colonial history.

The visit is best folded into a relaxed Nelson day rather than treated as a headline attraction. Come for tea, the garden setting, and a quiet look at period architecture within easy reach of the central streets.
