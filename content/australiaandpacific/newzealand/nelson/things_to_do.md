---
title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Nelson rewards the kind of traveller who plans a long lunch around a museum visit, a gallery crawl, or a hike to a viewpoint and back in time for dinner. The town is small and walkable, the three surrounding national parks are within striking distance, and there is enough indoors — wearable art, the provincial museum, a Lord of the Rings ring-maker — to keep a rainy day interesting.
