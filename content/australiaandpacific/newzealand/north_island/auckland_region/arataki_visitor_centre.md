Arataki Visitor Centre is the main gateway to the Waitakere Ranges, with views over native forest and the west Auckland hills. Inside are displays on local birds, plants, and Maori history; outside, short signed trails introduce the bush before longer walks head deeper into the ranges.

It is a useful first stop if you are heading toward Piha or the west coast beaches, especially if you want advice on track conditions.
