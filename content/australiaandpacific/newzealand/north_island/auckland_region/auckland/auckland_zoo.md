Auckland Zoo is New Zealand's largest zoo, with native species, exotic animals, and broad habitat areas spread across 17 hectares. It is especially good for families, but the native-bird and conservation exhibits make it worthwhile even if you have come to Auckland for the outdoors rather than city attractions.
