The New Zealand Maritime Museum covers the country's seafaring history from Polynesian navigation to modern sailing. Exhibits include working boats, model ships, America's Cup material, and hands-on displays that make it more lively than a static harbor museum.

It sits on the waterfront by Viaduct Harbour, so it is easy to combine with a walk around the marina. Some visits can also be paired with a short harbor cruise on a heritage-style vessel.
