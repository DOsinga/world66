One Tree Hill, or Maungakiekie, is one of Auckland's landmark volcanic cones. The summit rises above Cornwall Park and gives wide views across the city, while the slopes still show terraces from a former Maori pa.

The hill is also tied to early colonial Auckland through the obelisk and grave of Sir John Logan Campbell. Come for the view, but leave time to walk through the surrounding parkland rather than treating it as a drive-up photo stop.
