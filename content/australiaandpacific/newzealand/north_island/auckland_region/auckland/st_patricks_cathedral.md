St Patrick's Cathedral is Auckland's main Catholic cathedral, a Gothic Revival stone church tucked among the towers of the central city. Its site goes back to the first years of colonial Auckland, and the interior gives a quieter view of the city's history than the waterfront and shopping streets nearby.
