St Patrick's Roman Catholic Cathedral is a central Auckland church with roots in the city's early colonial period. The foundation stone of the present stone building was laid in 1884, on land granted by the Crown in 1841.

It remains an active cathedral, so visit outside services if you want a quieter look at the nave and stained glass.
