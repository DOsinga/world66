The Lighthouse / Tū Whenua-a-Kura is a Michael Parekowhai sculpture at the end of the wharf, shaped like a small 1950s house. You cannot enter it, but you can look through the windows to see the southern-skies lighting display inside, with the harbour as the backdrop.
