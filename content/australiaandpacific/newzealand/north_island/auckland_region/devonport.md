Devonport is a charming harbourside village on the North Shore peninsula, easily reached by a 12-minute ferry from downtown [Auckland](/australiaandpacific/newzealand/north_island/auckland_region/auckland). [Victoria Road](/australiaandpacific/newzealand/north_island/auckland_region/devonport/victoria_road) is lined with Victorian buildings housing cafes, bookshops and small galleries, and the whole place has a relaxed, slightly old-fashioned feel that contrasts nicely with the city across the water.

Walk up [Mount Victoria / Takarunga](/australiaandpacific/newzealand/north_island/auckland_region/devonport/mount_victoria) or [North Head / Maungauika](/australiaandpacific/newzealand/north_island/auckland_region/devonport/north_head) for panoramic views of the harbour, Rangitoto Island and the Auckland skyline. North Head is also riddled with historic military tunnels from the colonial era, free to explore. The [Torpedo Bay Navy Museum](/australiaandpacific/newzealand/north_island/auckland_region/devonport/torpedo_bay_navy_museum) on the waterfront covers New Zealand's naval history. It makes for a perfect half-day escape from the city.
