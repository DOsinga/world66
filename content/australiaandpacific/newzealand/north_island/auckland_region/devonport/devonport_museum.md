Devonport Museum is a small local museum, but in Devonport that scale feels right. Its collections cover the peninsula's Maori, naval, ferry, village, and domestic history, helping explain why this pretty seaside suburb has a stronger identity than its size suggests.

Visit it before or after walking Mount Victoria/Takarunga and the waterfront. The museum gives context to the old villas, naval installations, and ferry culture that make Devonport more than just a pleasant Auckland day trip.
