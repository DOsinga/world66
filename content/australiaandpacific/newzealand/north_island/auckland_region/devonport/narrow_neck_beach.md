Narrow Neck Beach is a relaxed Devonport beach north of the village centre, with calm water, harbour views, and a more local feel than some of Auckland's better-known strands. It is a good choice when you want a swim or picnic without turning the day into a long excursion.

The beach pairs easily with North Head, Cheltenham Beach, and the ferry ride from downtown Auckland. It is not dramatic, but it is exactly the sort of simple coastal stop that makes Devonport a useful half-day escape.
