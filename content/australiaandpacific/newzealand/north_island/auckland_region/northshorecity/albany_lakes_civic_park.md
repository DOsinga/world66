A pleasant lakeside park in the heart of the Albany town centre, created as part of the development of Albany as the North Shore's second major hub. The park wraps around a series of linked lakes and wetlands, with a shared walking and cycling path looping the circuit. It is not a destination in itself but makes for an easy half-hour of fresh air if you are in the Albany area for other reasons — visiting Westfield Albany, catching a bus from the station, or attending a match at North Harbour Stadium.

The wetlands attract a variety of birds, including pukeko and grey herons. The path is flat and easy, suitable for young children on bikes. The surrounding parkland includes some grassed areas and a playground.
