Birkenhead occupies a peninsula on the inner Waitemata Harbour, close to central Auckland but with a distinctly local feel. The suburb is hilly, with houses on the slopes looking out over the harbour, and the main commercial strip on Birkenhead Avenue has a cluster of cafes and casual restaurants that attract a mix of locals and people coming over on the ferry.

The ferry service to Downtown Auckland takes around 20 minutes and runs through the day, making Birkenhead a reasonable base if you want to be close to the water with easy city access. Highbury Shops is the main retail area. The harbour views from the waterfront are among the better ones on the North Shore — you look directly across to the CBD and back towards the bridge.
