Devonport sits at the southern tip of the North Shore peninsula, separated from central Auckland by just 12 minutes of harbour crossing but feeling like a different world. The village has a genuine Victorian character — wide verandahed shops, wooden colonial buildings, a naval base that has been here since the 1840s — and a pace that the rest of Auckland has largely lost.

The main street is Victoria Road, lined with cafes, galleries, and independent shops. Two volcanic cones — Mount Victoria (Takarunga) and North Head (Maungauika) — rise above the village and are both worth climbing for the harbour views. North Head is a historic reserve with tunnels and gun emplacements from the First and Second World Wars. Devonport is easily done as a day trip from the city, but it rewards staying longer.
