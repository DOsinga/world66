The East Coast Bays is the collective name for a string of suburbs running along the Hauraki Gulf coast from Mairangi Bay in the south to Long Bay in the north. Each bay has its own beach — Browns Bay, Rothesay Bay, Mairangi Bay, Murrays Bay, Torbay — with calm, sheltered water that is good for swimming and paddleboarding. The coastline faces east across the gulf towards the Coromandel Peninsula.

The area is predominantly residential and suburban, with Browns Bay having the most developed town centre — a good strip of cafes and the busiest beach on summer weekends. Mairangi Bay and Murrays Bay are quieter and popular with families. The **Coastal Walkway** connects several of the bays on foot or by bike, and is the best way to explore the coastline without a car.
