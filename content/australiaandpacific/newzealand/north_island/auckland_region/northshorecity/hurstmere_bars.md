The cluster of bars along Hurstmere Road and the surrounding blocks is the social heart of the North Shore. The crowd skews local — after-work drinks from the nearby offices and apartments are the main trade on weeknights, with livelier weekend evenings when the beach crowd transitions to the bars. The scene is relaxed and mixed-age rather than studenty, which suits the neighbourhood.

Several spots have outdoor tables that catch the afternoon sun, and the proximity to the beach means you can walk down for a post-swim drink without feeling out of place in casual clothes. The bars are not trying to compete with Ponsonby or the city centre — they are neighbourhood places with regulars, which makes them more comfortable than impressive. If you are staying on the North Shore and want a drink without the ferry trip, this is where to go.
