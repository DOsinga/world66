Lake Pupuke is a volcanic crater lake just inland from Takapuna Beach, calm and round where the coastline is open and salty. Parts of the shore are private, but public edges and paths still make it a good change of pace from the beach, with ducks, small boats, and local walkers using the lake through the day.

It is best treated as part of a Takapuna wander rather than a destination on its own. Combine it with the beach, Hurstmere Road, and the coastal walk if you want to see the North Shore away from the city skyline views.
