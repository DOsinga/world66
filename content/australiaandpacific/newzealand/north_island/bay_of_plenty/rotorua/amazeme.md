aMAZEme is a family-oriented hedge maze and garden attraction outside Rotorua. It is deliberately light-hearted, giving children and adults a break from geothermal walks, Maori cultural shows, and adventure activities.

This is a modest stop, but a useful one if you are travelling with kids or want something gentle between bigger Rotorua sights. Allow enough time to enjoy the maze rather than treating it as a photo stop from the car park.
