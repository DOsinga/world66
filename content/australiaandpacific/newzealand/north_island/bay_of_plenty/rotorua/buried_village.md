The Buried Village of Te Wairoa tells one of Rotorua's defining disaster stories: the 1886 eruption of Mount Tarawera, which destroyed settlements and buried the Pink and White Terraces. Excavated remains, displays, and forested grounds make the eruption feel much more immediate than a museum panel in town.

It is south-east of Rotorua, so combine it with the lakes or other Tarawera-area stops. The site is especially worthwhile if you want history to balance the region's geothermal pools and Maori cultural attractions.
