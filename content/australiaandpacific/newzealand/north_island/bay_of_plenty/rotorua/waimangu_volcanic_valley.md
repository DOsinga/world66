Waimangu Volcanic Valley is one of the finest geothermal landscapes in the Rotorua region, created
after the 1886 eruption of Mount Tarawera reshaped this whole area. The walking route follows
steaming cliffs, hot springs, crater lakes, and mineral-stained terraces through a valley that still
feels geologically alive.

The site rewards taking it slowly. Frying Pan Lake, Inferno Crater, and the native bush between them
give a stronger sense of landscape scale than many smaller geothermal parks. It is far enough from
central Rotorua to feel like a proper outing, but close enough to combine with Wai-O-Tapu, Tarawera
history, or a lake-country drive.
