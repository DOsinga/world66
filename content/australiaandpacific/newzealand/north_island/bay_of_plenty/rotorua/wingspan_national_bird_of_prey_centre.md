Wingspan National Bird of Prey Centre focuses on raptor conservation, education, and close-up encounters with New Zealand birds of prey. It is a compact attraction, but the subject is unusual in a Rotorua itinerary usually dominated by geothermal parks, Māori culture, and adventure activities.

The centre is most rewarding when a flying display or guided session is available. It suits families and wildlife-minded travellers, and it adds a conservation angle to a city whose natural attractions are often experienced at much larger scale.
