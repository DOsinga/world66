One of Tauranga's most established seafood restaurants, Harbourside sits on The Strand with harbour views and a menu focused on fresh local fish and shellfish. Snapper, scallops, and green-lipped mussels from the Bay of Plenty appear regularly, prepared simply or with Pacific-influenced sauces.

The waterfront location makes it popular with visitors; booking ahead for dinner is sensible in summer. Lunch is more relaxed and the views across to Mount Maunganui are at their best in the afternoon light.
