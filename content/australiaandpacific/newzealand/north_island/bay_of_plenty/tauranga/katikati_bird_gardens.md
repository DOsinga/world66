Katikati Bird Gardens is a relaxed garden and wildlife stop north of Tauranga, with ponds, planted paths, and birds moving through the grounds. It is smaller and more personal than a formal zoo, which is part of its appeal for families and slow travelers.

The gardens work well as a gentle break between coastal drives or beach towns in the Bay of Plenty. Bring children, binoculars, or simply a willingness to wander without a tight schedule.
