---
title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Tauranga's highlights divide between the city centre and the beach suburb of Mount Maunganui across the harbour. The Mauao (Mount Maunganui) volcanic cone dominates the skyline and offers the best overview of the area from the top. The city itself has a growing arts and cafe scene, while offshore, White Island (Whakaari) draws those who want to experience an active volcanic crater up close.
