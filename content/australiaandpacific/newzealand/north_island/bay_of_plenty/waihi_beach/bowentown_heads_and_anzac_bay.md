Bowentown is the quieter southern end of Waihi Beach, where the coast turns from open Pacific surf toward the sheltered entrance of Tauranga Harbour. Anzac Bay is the gentler swimming spot here, especially when the surf on the main beach is rough.

From the car park, short tracks climb toward Te Ho pa site and the Bowentown headland viewpoints. The reward is a compact coastal panorama: Waihi Beach behind you, Matakana Island and the harbour entrance ahead, and small bays tucked below the headland.
