Kapiti Miniature Railway runs small passenger trains on a dual-gauge circuit in Raumati Beach. Visitors can ride behind miniature locomotives, and some sessions offer the chance to try driving one.

It is a modest but fun family stop, especially if you are already visiting Raumati Marine Gardens or travelling with children who like trains.
