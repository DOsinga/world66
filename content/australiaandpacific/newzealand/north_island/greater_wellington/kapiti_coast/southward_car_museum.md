Southward Car Museum is one of the Kapiti Coast's main indoor attractions, with a large private collection of cars, motorbikes, and motoring memorabilia. The collection covers more than 200 vehicles, from early classics to unusual specialist machines.

It is an easy stop for families and anyone with even a casual interest in transport history. The museum sits inland from Paraparaumu and Raumati, so it works well as a weatherproof break from the coast or as a stop on a drive north from Wellington.
