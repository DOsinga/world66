---
title: Things to Do
type: section
---

Lower Hutt balances its identity as a satellite city of Wellington with a genuine cultural and outdoor life of its own. The Dowse Art Museum is the standout cultural institution; Petone's foreshore and settlers museum anchor the city's history; and the Hutt River and surrounding hills offer easy access to nature without driving far. Wellington is 20 minutes away by train, so it is easy to combine Lower Hutt with the capital.
