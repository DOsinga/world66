Aperitivo is a small Italian bistro just up from the Plimmerton beachfront, popular enough on summer weekends that you should book. The menu sticks to a tight selection of Italian classics — wood-fired pizza, simple pastas, antipasti boards — done well rather than expansively. The wine list is short and Italian-leaning.

The location is the main draw: walk down from the train station, eat lunch on the deck, walk it off along the beach. Open lunch and dinner Thursday through Sunday; reduced hours in winter.
