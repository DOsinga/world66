Battle Hill Farm Forest Park combines an easy rural day out with a serious piece of local history. The park includes the site of an 1846 battle during the Hutt Valley Campaign, along with walking and riding trails, picnic areas, and open farmland.

It is a good choice if you want a break from the coast and harbour sights around Porirua. Families come for the farm animals and eel-feeding, while walkers can use the tracks for wider views over the hills. There is camping, but no dogs, and mobile coverage can be patchy.
