---
title: "Getting There"
type: section
---

Porirua is twenty minutes north of central Wellington by car on State Highway 1 (the Transmission Gully motorway), or 25 minutes by Metlink commuter train on the Kapiti Line — services run every 15–20 minutes through the day. The train is the easier option for a day visit; the station is in the heart of the city centre, two minutes' walk from Pataka.

Coming from the [Kapiti Coast](/australiaandpacific/newzealand/north_island/greater_wellington/kapiti_coast) or further north, Porirua is the first stop on the southern run into Wellington. Plimmerton and other coastal suburbs have their own stations on the same line.
