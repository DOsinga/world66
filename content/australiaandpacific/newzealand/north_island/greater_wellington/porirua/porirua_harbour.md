Porirua Harbour is a deeply indented natural inlet on the south-western coast of the North Island, split into two long arms — the Pauatahanui Inlet to the east and the Onepoto Arm to the west. The city of Porirua wraps around the southern end where the two meet.

The harbour was once severely polluted by stormwater and sewage, and the inner arms still suffer at low tide. The Pauatahanui Wildlife Reserve at the head of the eastern arm — co-managed by Forest & Bird — is the highlight: a saltmarsh and lagoon that is the largest of its kind in the lower North Island, with hides and a short loop track. The reserve is roughly twenty minutes from the city centre via SH 58.
