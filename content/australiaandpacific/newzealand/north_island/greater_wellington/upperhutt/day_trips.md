Upper Hutt is a natural base for exploring the wider Wellington region. The Wairarapa wine country and Martinborough are accessible via the Remutaka Range — a 45-minute drive over the hill. Wellington itself, with its excellent museums, waterfront, and Te Papa, is half an hour south. The Kāpiti Coast beaches lie just over an hour to the northwest.
