The Upper Hutt Blockhouse, also known as the Wallaceville Blockhouse, is a 19th-century timber fortification — one of the few surviving examples of American-style military blockhouses built in New Zealand during the Land Wars era. The structure is compact and severe: heavy timber walls with loopholes for muskets, designed for a garrison to hold a defensive position.

The blockhouse sits in a quiet suburban setting today, which makes the contrast between its martial purpose and its peaceful surroundings all the more striking. It's a Heritage New Zealand-listed building and a tangible reminder of the conflict that shaped the colonial Hutt Valley. Visits are brief — it's worth combining with Barton's Bush nearby.
