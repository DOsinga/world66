The Mount Holdsworth-Jumbo Circuit is a serious Tararua Range tramp rather than a casual town walk. Starting from the Holdsworth road end, the route climbs through forest and onto exposed tops, with hut options and wide views over Wairarapa in settled weather.

It is travel-worthy because it gives Masterton a direct link to classic New Zealand backcountry, but it requires preparation. Weather can change quickly on the tops, so treat the circuit as a mountain route with proper gear, timing, and hut planning rather than a spontaneous day outing.
