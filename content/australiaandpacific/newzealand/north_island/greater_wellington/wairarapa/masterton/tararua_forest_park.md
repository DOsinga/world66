The Tararua Forest Park covers the long mountain range that separates the Wairarapa from the Kapiti Coast — 116,000 hectares of beech and podocarp forest, deep river valleys, and an exposed alpine tops zone above the bushline. It is one of the most accessible serious tramping areas in the country, with a dense network of huts and tracks.

The eastern (Wairarapa) entry points are at Holdsworth and Kiriwhakapapa, about half an hour west of Masterton. Holdsworth has a road-end with a basic campsite, a swimming hole in the Atiwhakatu Stream, and the start of a number of day walks — the Atiwhakatu Hut walk (3 hours return, family-friendly) and the Powell Hut overnight climb (one of the most popular alpine huts in the country) both leave from here. The Tararuas can be dangerous in bad weather; the tops are exposed and the weather changes fast.
