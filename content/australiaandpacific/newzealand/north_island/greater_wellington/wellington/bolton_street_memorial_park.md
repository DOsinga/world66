Bolton Street Memorial Park is a hillside cemetery and green corridor between the Botanic Garden and Thorndon. Its graves include early Wellington settlers, politicians, and families whose epitaphs give the city a more personal history than the big civic monuments do.

The park is best visited on foot after coming down from the Botanic Garden or the cable car. Paths thread between old headstones, native planting, and views back toward the city, making it a quiet historical detour close to Parliament.
