The Cathedral of St Paul is Wellington's modern Anglican cathedral, built in stages between 1954 and 1998. Its reinforced-concrete exterior is plain, but the interior has a strong sense of scale and some fine stained glass.

It is a short walk from Parliament and the Beehive, making it an easy addition to a northern downtown walk. On Saturdays, the car park often has a farmers' market.
