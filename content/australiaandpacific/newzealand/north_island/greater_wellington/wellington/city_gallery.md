City Gallery Wellington is the city's main contemporary art gallery, focused on changing exhibitions rather than a permanent collection. The programme leans experimental, with work by New Zealand and international artists, installations, film, design, and public talks.

Its central location by Civic Square makes it easy to combine with the waterfront, Te Papa, or Cuba Street. Check what is showing before you go, since the experience depends heavily on the current exhibition.
