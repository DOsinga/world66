Katherine Mansfield Birthplace is the restored Victorian house where the writer was born and spent her early childhood. The rooms are furnished to show domestic life in the 1890s, with displays that connect the house to Mansfield's family and writing.

It is a modest but worthwhile stop for readers and anyone interested in Wellington's colonial-era suburbs. Pair it with the Botanic Garden or Parliament area rather than crossing town for it alone.
