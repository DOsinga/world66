Nairn Street Cottage is a preserved 1858 house that stayed in one family for generations before becoming a small museum. Its rooms and garden show domestic Wellington life on a human scale, away from the city's larger national institutions.

The visit is compact, but useful if you want a closer look at early colonial housing and the way one family adapted as Wellington grew around them.
