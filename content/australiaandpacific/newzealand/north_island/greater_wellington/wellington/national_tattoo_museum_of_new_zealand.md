The National Tattoo Museum of New Zealand is a small, specialist museum attached to a tattoo studio near Cuba Street. Its focus is tattooing in New Zealand and the wider Pacific, with particular attention to Maori ta moko traditions.

This is not a major museum on the scale of Te Papa, but it is an unusual stop if you are interested in body art, Pacific identity, or the way tattooing carries family and cultural meaning.
