Pukeahu National War Memorial Park is Wellington's main place of national remembrance. The Art Deco carillon dates from 1932, the Hall of Memories opened in 1964, and the Tomb of the Unknown Warrior was added in 2004.

The wider park includes memorials from several countries, sculptures, and open lawns that make the site feel more like a civic space than a single monument. It is a worthwhile stop between Cuba Street and the waterfront museums.
