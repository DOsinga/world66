---

Wellington's attractions are concentrated in a small area, so you can see a lot on foot in a day or two. The waterfront has [Te Papa](/australiaandpacific/newzealand/north_island/greater_wellington/wellington/te_papa) (the national museum, free) and the [Museum of Wellington City and Sea](/australiaandpacific/newzealand/north_island/greater_wellington/wellington/museum_of_wellington) (also free). The cable car from Lambton Quay climbs to the [Botanic Garden](/australiaandpacific/newzealand/north_island/greater_wellington/wellington/botanic_garden) in Kelburn. [Mount Victoria Lookout](/australiaandpacific/newzealand/north_island/greater_wellington/wellington/mount_victoria) is the best panoramic viewpoint. [Parliament and the Beehive](/australiaandpacific/newzealand/north_island/greater_wellington/wellington/beehive) offers free guided tours. [Zealandia](/australiaandpacific/newzealand/north_island/greater_wellington/wellington/zealandia) in Karori is a fenced wildlife sanctuary with tuatara and kiwi. [Weta Workshop](/australiaandpacific/newzealand/north_island/greater_wellington/wellington/weta_workshop) in Miramar runs public tours of the Lord of the Rings effects studio. The south coast walk to [Red Rocks](/australiaandpacific/newzealand/north_island/greater_wellington/wellington/red_rocks) reaches a seal colony in winter.
