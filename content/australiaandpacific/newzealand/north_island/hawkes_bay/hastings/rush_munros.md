Rush Munro's is a Hastings institution: a family-run ice cream parlour that has been operating since 1926, making it one of the oldest continuously run ice cream businesses in New Zealand. The product is excellent — real dairy ice cream in a range of flavours, served in cones or cups and eaten in the shaded garden behind the shop.

The experience is refreshingly unpretentious. The garden has the relaxed atmosphere of a neighbourhood spot that has never needed to try too hard because people keep coming back. It is a reliable stop for families and a pleasant mid-afternoon break from wine touring. You will find it on Heretaunga Street near the town centre.
