The Faraday Centre is a hands-on technology museum that adds a practical, family-friendly stop to Napier's Art Deco and seaside focus. Its collections cover electricity, machinery, vehicles, communications, and household technology, with many exhibits designed to be operated rather than simply viewed.

It is most rewarding for visitors who like mechanical history or are travelling with children who need something tactile. The museum also quietly reinforces Napier's post-earthquake modernisation story by showing the tools and technologies that shaped 20th-century daily life.
