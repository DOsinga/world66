Pania of the Reef is a bronze statue on Napier's Marine Parade and one of the city's most photographed landmarks. The figure comes from Maori tradition, and the statue has become a local symbol as familiar to Napier as its Art Deco streets.

It is a quick stop, but an easy one to fold into a waterfront walk near MTG Hawke's Bay and the seafront gardens.
