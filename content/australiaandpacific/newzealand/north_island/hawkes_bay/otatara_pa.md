Otatara Pa is one of New Zealand's largest accessible pa sites, set on a hill between Napier and Hastings. The earthworks and terraces are important, and the views stretch across Hawke's Bay toward Cape Kidnappers and out to sea.

It is a short but meaningful stop if you want to understand the region before European settlement. Stay on marked paths and treat the site with respect.
