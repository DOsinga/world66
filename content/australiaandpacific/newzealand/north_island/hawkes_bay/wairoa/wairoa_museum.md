Wairoa Museum is a compact local museum with material on the town, the Wairoa River, Maori history, farming, shipping, and the communities of northern Hawke's Bay. It gives context to a place many travellers otherwise know only as a stop on the road to Lake Waikaremoana or Gisborne.

The museum's strength is local specificity: photographs, family histories, and everyday objects that make the district feel lived-in. It is a good short stop before heading into Te Urewera country or down the coast.
