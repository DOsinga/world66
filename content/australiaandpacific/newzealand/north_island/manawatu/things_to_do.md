The Manawatu's main appeal is the contrast between the flat, intensely farmed plains and the dramatic gorges and ranges that surround them. The [Manawatu Gorge](/australiaandpacific/newzealand/north_island/manawatu/manawatu_gorge) is the most striking natural feature — a deep canyon where the river cuts through the Ruahine Range. Above the plains, the Ruahine and Tararua ranges offer serious tramping on tracks maintained by the Department of Conservation.

In Palmerston North, the [New Zealand Rugby Museum](/australiaandpacific/newzealand/north_island/manawatu/nz_rugby_museum) is a national pilgrimage site for fans of the sport. [Te Manawa Museum](/australiaandpacific/newzealand/north_island/manawatu/te_manawa_museum) combines art, science, and regional history in a purpose-built complex in the city centre.
