---
title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Paihia is a small town, but the Bay of Islands around it is one of New Zealand's great natural playgrounds. The main activities are all water-based: boat tours, dolphin-watching, sea kayaking, diving, and the famous Hole in the Rock cruise. On land, the [Waitangi Treaty Grounds](/australiaandpacific/newzealand/north_island/northland/waitangi) are within walking distance and are the most historically significant site in New Zealand.
