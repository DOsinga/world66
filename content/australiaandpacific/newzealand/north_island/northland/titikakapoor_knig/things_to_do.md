---
title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Almost everything to do in Tutukaka is offshore. Boats leave from the marina in the morning for the Poor Knights Islands, an hour each way through open water, and most visitors are back ashore by mid-afternoon. The harbour itself is small and pleasant, with a beach and a couple of bars overlooking the boats.

On land, the Tutukaka Coast Walks link the harbour to the wide white-sand beach at Matapouri and the famous Mermaid Pools, a sequence of saltwater rock pools tucked into the headland. The whole coast is a string of small beaches, headlands, and coves between the harbour and Whangarei Heads — easy enough to explore over a couple of days.
