Matauri Bay is a broad white-sand beach east of Whangaroa, good for swimming, surfing, and a simple coastal afternoon. It is popular in summer but still feels like a Northland beach rather than a built-up resort strip.

The bay is also a useful waypoint between the Bay of Islands and the Far North. Bring what you need, then let the beach do the work.
