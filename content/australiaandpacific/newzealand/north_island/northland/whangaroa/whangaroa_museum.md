Whangaroa Museum is a small local museum in Kaeo, useful for grounding the harbour's layered history. Expect local material rather than a polished national museum: timber, shipping, families, missions, and the events that shaped the district.

It is a good rainy-hour stop or a sensible first stop before heading out to the harbour and beaches.
