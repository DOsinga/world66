Hawera is a South Taranaki service town that passes the spare-day test mostly because of one excellent anchor: [Tawhiti Museum](/australiaandpacific/newzealand/north_island/taranaki/hawera/tawhiti_museum). The town itself is modest dairy-country New Zealand, but the museum's handmade historical scenes and Traders & Whalers boat ride are strong enough to pull travellers down from New Plymouth or up from Whanganui.

In town, the [Hawera Water Tower](/australiaandpacific/newzealand/north_island/taranaki/hawera/hawera_water_tower) gives Hawera its skyline, even though public access has been closed while safety work is assessed. [Turuturu-Mokai Pa](/australiaandpacific/newzealand/north_island/taranaki/hawera/turuturu_mokai_pa) adds a deeper Maori historical layer, and the small [Elvis Presley Museum](/australiaandpacific/newzealand/north_island/taranaki/hawera/elvis_presley_museum) gives the place an oddball footnote.

Be strict with your time. Hawera is not a substitute for Mount Taranaki or New Plymouth, but it is a worthwhile half-day if Tawhiti Museum is open.
