Zealong Tea Estate sits just outside Hamilton and is New Zealand's most recognisable tea plantation. The rows of tea bushes, tasting room, and guided experiences make it an easy rural counterpoint to Hamilton Gardens and the Waikato River.

The visit is polished and unhurried. Tea enthusiasts will get the most from a tasting or tour, but even casual travellers can enjoy the open setting and the novelty of seeing a working tea estate in New Zealand rather than simply drinking the finished cup.
