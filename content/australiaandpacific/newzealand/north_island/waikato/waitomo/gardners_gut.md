Gardner's Gut is an extensive cave system south of the main Waitomo visitor area, less commercialized than the flagship attractions and accessible only on guided tours for serious cavers. The system connects underground with several other Waitomo cave networks and includes a significant underground river section.

Tours into Gardner's Gut are typically multi-hour expeditions involving crawling, swimming, and scrambling through passages. They're offered by specialist local guide companies and cater to people who want a genuine caving experience rather than a tourist attraction. If you're comfortable in tight, dark, wet places, this is one of the most memorable ways to see what the Waitomo karst looks like beyond the show caves.
