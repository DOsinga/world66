---
title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

New Plymouth punches above its weight for a city of its size. The Govett-Brewster Art Gallery and Len Lye Centre house one of New Zealand's best contemporary art collections. The Coastal Walkway provides thirteen kilometres of waterfront walking. Pukekura Park is a proper botanical garden right in the city centre. And above all this, Mount Taranaki offers everything from short bush walks to serious multi-day alpine routes.
