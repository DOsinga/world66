---
title: When to Go
type: section
---

Wanganui has a mild, temperate climate year-round. Summer (December to February) is warm and dry, with temperatures around 20–25°C; this is the best time for canoe and kayak trips on the Whanganui River. Winter (June to August) is cool and sometimes wet, but the city itself is fully functional and the heritage buildings look particularly atmospheric in the mist.

The Whanganui River festival and the various arts events run primarily in summer. Spring (September to November) brings pleasant walking weather for the river road and the national park.
