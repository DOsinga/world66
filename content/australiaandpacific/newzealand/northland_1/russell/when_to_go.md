---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

The Bay of Islands has a subtropical climate — warm and humid in summer (December to February), mild in winter, and wet year-round. The peak season is December to February, when the bay fills with boats and Russell's few restaurants and accommodation options fill up. Book well ahead for this period.

Shoulder season (March to May, September to November) is often more pleasant — fewer crowds, lower prices, and the bay still beautiful. Winter can be rainy but the days are often fine, and the historical sights are best visited without the summer crowds.
