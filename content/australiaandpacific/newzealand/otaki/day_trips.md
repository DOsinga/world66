The most rewarding escape from Ōtaki is east into the [Tararua Forest Park](/australiaandpacific/newzealand/tararua_forest_park), where bush tracks follow the Ōtaki River from the Ōtaki Forks area about 18 kilometres up the road. Short walks into the foothills make an easy half-day, while the high ranges beyond are serious tramping country best left to well-prepared parties.
