  The outlet shops at Ōtaki have been drawing Wellington shoppers north for decades, offering discounted New Zealand-made clothing, outdoor gear, and goods from brands like Swanndri and various wool and merino producers. It's not glamorous, but if you need good-quality cold-weather gear at prices that reflect a factory outlet rather than a city boutique, this is the place to look.
---

The factory shopping area on the main highway draws a consistent stream of day-trippers from Wellington looking for discounted New Zealand brands. Several shops sell wool products, outdoor clothing, and merino knitwear at better prices than you'll find in the city.

The area is unpretentious and functional. Worth a stop if you're driving through and need practical New Zealand-made clothing — not worth a special trip. SH1 / Ōtaki, adjacent to the motorway exit.
