The Ōtaki Museum is a small but well-organised local history museum that does an honest job of covering both Māori and Pākehā (European) history in the region. The collection includes artefacts, photographs, and documents relating to the Ngāti Raukawa iwi, the early flax trade, and the development of the town.

Free to enter or by koha (donation). Open limited hours — check locally before visiting. Main Street, Ōtaki.
