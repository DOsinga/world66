---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

Ōtaki is worth visiting year-round, but the beach is most enjoyable from December through March when temperatures are warm enough for swimming. The Tararua Ranges are accessible for day walks through most of the year, though the upper tracks can be snow-covered in winter and should not be underestimated regardless of season.

The outlet shopping area on State Highway 1 operates year-round and is busiest on weekends. There is no single major festival or event that makes one season more compelling than another.
