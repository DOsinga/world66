Orca sits by the harbour in central Raglan and works for an easy meal when you want water views without leaving town. The menu is broad rather than fussy, with seafood, mains, and drinks that suit a post-beach evening.

It is popular for the setting as much as the food. Book or arrive early in the busiest summer weeks.
