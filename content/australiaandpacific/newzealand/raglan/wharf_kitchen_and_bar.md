Wharf Kitchen and Bar is down by Raglan Wharf, a good spot when you want a meal away from the busiest part of Bow Street. It is especially useful for lunch, drinks, or a relaxed dinner after walking around the harbour.

The wharf setting is the point: boats, gulls, and a slower edge of town than the beach road.
