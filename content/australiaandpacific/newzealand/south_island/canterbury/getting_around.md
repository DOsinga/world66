---
title: Getting Around Canterbury
type: section
---

Canterbury is a large region and a car is the practical requirement for exploring it beyond the city. The distances are significant: Christchurch to Kaikoura is 2 hours north; Christchurch to Akaroa is 90 minutes east; Christchurch to Lake Tekapo is 3.5 hours southwest; Christchurch to Mount Cook is 4.5 hours. The roads are good and mostly well-signed.

**State Highway 1** runs north-south through Christchurch, connecting Kaikoura and the Marlborough Sounds to the north, and Timaru and Dunedin to the south.

**State Highway 73** (the inland road) heads west from Christchurch through the Waimakariri River gorge to Arthur's Pass and on to Greymouth — this is the road the TranzAlpine train follows.

**State Highway 8** runs south and west from Timaru into the Mackenzie Basin, connecting Fairlie, Tekapo, Twizel, and on to Mount Cook. A circular route combining SH1 south and SH8 back north via the Mackenzie is the standard way to cover the southern highlights in two days.

**Rental cars** are available in Christchurch. **InterCity buses** serve the main routes but frequencies to Tekapo and Mount Cook are limited. The [TranzAlpine](/australiaandpacific/newzealand/south_island/canterbury/tranzalpine) train runs daily Christchurch–Greymouth.
