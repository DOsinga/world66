**The Hooker Valley Track** is the most popular walk in Aoraki/Mount Cook National Park and one of the finest day walks in New Zealand. Starting from Mount Cook Village, the track follows the Hooker River for about 10 kilometres (5km each way) through a glacial valley with the south face of Aoraki/Mount Cook directly ahead for most of the route. Three swing bridges cross the river, and the track ends at Hooker Lake, a glacial lake with icebergs floating in it and the mountain rising behind.

On a clear day, the Hooker Valley Track is simply stunning: the scale of the peaks, the glitter of the glacier on the mountain's face, and the raw alpine environment are as complete and impressive as anything in the Southern Alps. The track is well-maintained and suitable for most fitness levels — allow 3 hours return and dress for all weathers, as conditions change fast at altitude. The car park at the trailhead fills early on summer days; arriving before 8am is advisable. The track is snow-covered in winter but usually manageable with care.
