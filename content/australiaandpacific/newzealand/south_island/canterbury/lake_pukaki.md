**Lake Pukaki** is the largest of the three Mackenzie Basin lakes and the one that offers the most dramatic mountain backdrop. The water is the same vivid glacial turquoise as Lake Tekapo, coloured by rock flour ground off the Southern Alps glaciers, and on still days the reflection of Aoraki/Mount Cook at the lake's far end is perfect. The 55-kilometre State Highway 80 that runs along the western shore, from the junction at Pukaki to Mount Cook Village, is one of the most spectacular drives in New Zealand — nothing but ice-blue water on the left and the Alps on the right, the summit of New Zealand's highest peak growing larger for an hour as you drive toward it.

The salmon farm at the lake's southern end operates a café popular with coach tours, but the lake itself has no development along its western shore beyond the village. Kayaking and fishing are possible. Sunset light on the lake, with Aoraki catching the last orange glow, is genuinely outstanding.
