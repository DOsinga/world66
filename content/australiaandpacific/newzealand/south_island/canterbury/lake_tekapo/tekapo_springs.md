Tekapo Springs is the easy recovery stop after a drive, hike, ski day, or cold night outside. The complex has hot pools, cooler family pools, sauna and spa facilities, and broad views over the lake and surrounding hills.

It is a built attraction rather than a wild hot spring, so do not come expecting wilderness. Come for warm water, big sky, and the pleasure of sitting still after too many kilometres on South Island roads.
