---
title: Things to Do in Canterbury
type: section
---

Canterbury's appeal lies in its dramatic contrasts. The flat coastal plains give way abruptly to the Southern Alps; the ocean is never far east; and the Mackenzie Basin in the southwest is some of the most austere and beautiful high-country landscape in the Pacific.

The region's key experiences: the [TranzAlpine](/australiaandpacific/newzealand/south_island/canterbury/tranzalpine) train journey over the Main Divide, walking in [Arthur's Pass National Park](/australiaandpacific/newzealand/south_island/canterbury/arthurs_pass), whale-watching at [Kaikoura](/australiaandpacific/newzealand/south_island/canterbury/kaikoura), stargazing at [Lake Tekapo](/australiaandpacific/newzealand/south_island/canterbury/lake_tekapo), glaciers and high peaks at [Aoraki/Mount Cook National Park](/australiaandpacific/newzealand/south_island/canterbury/aoraki_mount_cook), and the French-heritage harbour town of [Akaroa](/australiaandpacific/newzealand/south_island/canterbury/akaroa).

The region's main city, Christchurch, has its own [separate page](/australiaandpacific/newzealand/canterbury/christchurch).
