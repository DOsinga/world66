**The Waimakariri River Gorge** is the dramatic passage where the Waimakariri River cuts through the foothills of the Southern Alps en route from the Main Divide to the Canterbury Plains. The TranzAlpine train passes through it and the view from the train carriages — a steep beech-forested gorge with the river racing below — is one of the journey's highlights. For those travelling by road, the gorge is accessible by the road to Arthur's Pass, and the river corridor below the Waimakariri Falls can be explored on foot.

White-water rafting and kayaking on the Waimakariri is popular from Christchurch, with several operators running half-day trips that include the gorge section in season. Jet-boating on the braided reaches below the gorge is another option. The river is also a fine trout fishery, and the shingle beaches along its lower reaches are popular picnic spots on fine days.
