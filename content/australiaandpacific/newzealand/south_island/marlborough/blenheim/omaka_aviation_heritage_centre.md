Omaka Aviation Heritage Centre is the reason Blenheim clears the "worth a day" test even for travellers who are not here for wine. The museum presents rare First and Second World War aircraft in theatrical scenes, with Weta Workshop design and a strong connection to Sir Peter Jackson's private collection.

The result is much more vivid than rows of machines in a hangar. Allow at least two hours, and more if you like aviation, model-making, or military history.
