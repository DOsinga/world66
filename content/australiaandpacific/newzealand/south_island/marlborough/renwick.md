Renwick is a small town in the Wairau Valley, surrounded by the vineyards that made Marlborough famous. Blenheim is bigger and more practical, but Renwick sits closer to many cellar doors and feels more like the wine country itself: low streets, dry hills in the distance, and vines almost at the edge of town.

This is not a town of grand monuments. Its case is simple: hire a bike, taste slowly, and let the day move between family-run wineries, picnic lawns, and one old pub. [Forrest Wines](/australiaandpacific/newzealand/south_island/marlborough/renwick/forrest_wines), [Framingham Wines](/australiaandpacific/newzealand/south_island/marlborough/renwick/framingham_wines), [Fromm Winery](/australiaandpacific/newzealand/south_island/marlborough/renwick/fromm_winery), and [Te Whare Rā](/australiaandpacific/newzealand/south_island/marlborough/renwick/te_whare_ra) are all close enough to make Renwick a strong base for a day in the vines.

Renwick works best for travellers who already want Marlborough wine and prefer a smaller base than Blenheim. If wine is not the point, pass through without guilt.
