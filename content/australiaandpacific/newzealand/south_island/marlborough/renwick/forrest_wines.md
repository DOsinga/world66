Forrest Wines is one of Renwick's easiest cellar-door stops: friendly, unfussy, and set up for lingering rather than rushing a tasting flight. The Forrest family pours a wide range, from classic Marlborough Sauvignon Blanc to less predictable bottles from other New Zealand regions.

The garden setting makes it a good first or last stop on a bike loop. Check current hours before building the whole day around it.
