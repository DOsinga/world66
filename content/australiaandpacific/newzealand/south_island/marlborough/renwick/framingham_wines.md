Framingham Wines gives a useful reminder that Marlborough is not only Sauvignon Blanc. Riesling has long been part of the story here, and the cellar door is a good place to compare styles without leaving Renwick's vineyard belt.

It sits west of the town centre, close enough to combine with Forrest and other Wairau Valley stops.
