Fromm Winery is a good stop when you want a break from the region's bright white-wine stereotype. Its reputation leans toward reds and more structured bottles, especially Pinot Noir and Syrah.

The cellar door is near the eastern side of Renwick, so it fits well into a loop back toward Blenheim.
