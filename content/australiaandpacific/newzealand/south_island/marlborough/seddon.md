Seddon is a small town in Marlborough's Awatere Valley, south of Blenheim on State Highway 1. It sits in dry, open country near the mouths of the Awatere and Blind Rivers, with vineyards now covering much of the valley floor.

The town is practical rather than pretty, but the surrounding landscape has its own spare appeal: pale hills, straight roads, big skies, and rows of vines running toward the coast. Seddon is named after Richard Seddon, New Zealand's long-serving late-19th-century premier.

Most travellers pass through between Blenheim, Picton, and Kaikoura. Stop if you are following the Awatere wine country, riding the Whale Trail, or want to see the old road-rail bridge just north of town.
