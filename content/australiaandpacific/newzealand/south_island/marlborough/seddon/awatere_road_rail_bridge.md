The Awatere Road Rail Bridge was one of New Zealand's unusual double-deck crossings, with trains above and road traffic below. A new road bridge took over the highway traffic in 2008, but the old structure remains part of the local landscape.

The former road deck has been brought back into use for walkers and cyclists as part of the Whale Trail. It is a short, satisfying stop if you like practical engineering with a bit of history attached.
