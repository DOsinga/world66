Ward is a small settlement on State Highway 1 in southern [Marlborough](/australiaandpacific/newzealand/south_island/marlborough), easy to dismiss from the car and more interesting if you turn off the road. The village itself is tiny, but it sits between a strange coastal shelf, the white salt works of Lake Grassmere, and dry limestone country that feels more like inland Canterbury than the lush Marlborough Sounds.

The coast is the reason to stop. [Ward Beach](/australiaandpacific/newzealand/south_island/marlborough/ward/ward_beach) has uplifted rock platforms, seals, boulders and exposed limestone, with the Kaikoura ranges behind you and the Pacific in front. North of the village, [Lake Grassmere](/australiaandpacific/newzealand/south_island/marlborough/ward/lake_grassmere_salt_works) turns pink, white and blue as seawater evaporates in commercial salt ponds.

Ward also makes a good base for short, rough-edged walks. [Sawcut Gorge](/australiaandpacific/newzealand/south_island/marlborough/ward/sawcut_gorge) cuts a narrow slot through pale limestone west of town, while Marfells Beach and Cape Campbell give a wilder coastal day if the tide and weather are kind. Bring a car, water, and some patience; public transport can drop you in Ward, but the good parts are spread out.
