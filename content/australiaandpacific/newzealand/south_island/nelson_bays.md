The Nelson Bays region sits at the top of the [South Island](/australiaandpacific/newzealand/south_island), blessed with the sunniest climate in [New Zealand](/australiaandpacific/newzealand). It is a fruit-growing region backed by forested hills, with long golden-sand beaches along Tasman Bay and a relaxed, outdoorsy character.

[Nelson](/australiaandpacific/newzealand/nelson) is the main town — a compact, artsy city named after the British admiral, with a remarkable concentration of galleries, studios, and the [World of WearableArt museum](/australiaandpacific/newzealand/south_island/nelson_bays/world_of_wearableart). Nearby Richmond is the commercial centre of the Waimea Plains, while [Motueka](/australiaandpacific/newzealand/motueka) to the west is the gateway to [Abel Tasman National Park](/australiaandpacific/newzealand/south_island/nelson_bays/abel_tasman_national_park), one of New Zealand's most popular coastal walks.

Three national parks define the region's outdoor life. [Kahurangi](/australiaandpacific/newzealand/south_island/nelson_bays/kahurangi_national_park) to the west is New Zealand's second-largest park, home to the Heaphy Track. [Nelson Lakes](/australiaandpacific/newzealand/south_island/nelson_bays/nelson_lakes_national_park) to the south is quieter and more alpine, with glacial lakes and excellent birdwatching in its predator-controlled forest sanctuary.
