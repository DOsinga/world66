The Brunner Peninsula Nature Walk is the gentler counterpoint to the bigger Nelson Lakes tramps. It loops from the St Arnaud side of Lake Rotoiti through forest and bays, with views back across the water to Mount Robert and the St Arnaud Range.

It is the right choice when you want a proper taste of the lake without committing to the full circuit or a steep climb.
