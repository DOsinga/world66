The Nelson Lakes Visitor Centre is the practical first stop in St Arnaud. It has current advice on weather, tracks, huts, campsites, and conservation rules, which matters here because the park quickly changes from lakeshore paths to alpine country.

Call in before longer walks or if you are deciding between a short lake stroll and a bigger day on Mount Robert.
