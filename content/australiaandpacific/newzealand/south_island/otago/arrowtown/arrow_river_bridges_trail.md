The Arrow River Bridges Trail is one of the nicest sections of the Queenstown Trail network, following the river downstream from Arrowtown over a series of suspension bridges and boardwalks. It gives you the goldfields landscape at a human pace: schist banks, willow shade, clear water, and open views back to the hills.

Most visitors ride it as part of a longer Queenstown Trail day, continuing toward Gibbston wine country or looping back through the Wakatipu Basin. It also works as an easy walk from town if you do not want to hire a bike.
