The Arrowtown Chinese Settlement preserves the remains of the small village where Chinese miners lived beside Bush Creek during the gold-rush years. The huts are tiny, rough, and deliberately unromantic: stone fireplaces, low doorways, and cramped interiors that make the hardship of the place immediately clear.

It is the most important historic site in Arrowtown because it complicates the town's otherwise handsome gold-rush story. Come here before or after walking Buckingham Street and the contrast is sharp: the main town has verandas and cafes, while this little settlement remembers the people who worked hard claims and were rarely welcomed as equals.
