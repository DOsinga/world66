The Lakes District Museum sits on Buckingham Street and is the best place to put Arrowtown's gold-rush streets into context. It covers Maori trails, European settlement, the rush for gold, and the later farming and tourism history of the Wakatipu Basin.

The museum is small but well judged, with enough local detail to be useful without turning into a regional archive. If you only have an hour in town, see the Chinese Settlement first; if you have longer, the museum fills in the story behind the shopfronts outside.
