---
title: When to Go
type: section
---

Arrowtown is at its best in autumn, especially April, when the deciduous trees along the river and old streets turn yellow and red. The town gets busy during the autumn festival, but the colour is worth the crowds.

Summer is warm and good for cycling; winter is cold, clear, and useful if you are pairing Arrowtown with skiing at Coronet Peak. Spring can be windy but quiet.
