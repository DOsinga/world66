Cargill's Castle is a ruin rather than a restored attraction, but its clifftop position gives it a romantic pull. Built for the Cargill family in the 19th century, the remains now look out over the coast near St Clair, mixing local history with a slightly melancholy seaside atmosphere.

Access and safety conditions can change, so treat it as a view-and-ruins stop rather than a polished heritage visit. It is most worthwhile for travellers exploring Dunedin's coast and interested in the city's old wealth beyond Larnach Castle.
