Dunedin Botanic Garden is one of New Zealand’s oldest botanic gardens and one of Dunedin’s most pleasant slow stops. It climbs from formal beds and a winter garden into hillside plantings, native collections, aviaries, and paths with views back toward the city.

The garden is large enough to reward wandering but close enough to the centre to visit without much planning. It is especially good in changeable weather, when glasshouses, trees, and sheltered corners offer a softer alternative to the Otago Peninsula’s exposed coast.
