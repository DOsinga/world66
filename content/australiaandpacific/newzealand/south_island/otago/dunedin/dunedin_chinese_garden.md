Dunedin Chinese Garden is a traditional scholar's garden built in Shanghai, dismantled, and reassembled in Dunedin. Its courtyards, pavilions, rocks, water, and planting make it a compact but rewarding pause close to the railway station and Toitū Otago Settlers Museum.
