Dunedin’s Gasworks Museum preserves part of the city’s 19th-century industrial infrastructure, the kind of place most cities demolished before anyone thought to explain it. Brick buildings, machinery, boilers, and gas-making equipment tell the story of how Victorian Dunedin lit and heated itself.

It is a niche museum, but a good one if you like industrial history or want to see a less polished side of a city better known for university life and Scottish architecture. Check opening times before going; the site is volunteer-driven and most rewarding when machinery or guided interpretation is available.
