Glenfalloch Woodland Garden covers about 30 acres on the Otago Peninsula, with walking tracks through mature trees, flowers, and sheltered woodland planting. The garden has been recognised as a Garden of National Significance and works well as a slower stop between Dunedin and the peninsula's wildlife sights.
