Holy Trinity Anglican Church in Port Chalmers was built between 1871 and 1874 from local bluestone to designs by Dunedin architect R.A. Lawson. It is a strong example of Early English Gothic Revival church design in New Zealand, with memorial stained glass adding interest inside.
