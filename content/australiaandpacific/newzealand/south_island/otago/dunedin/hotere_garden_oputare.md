Hotere Garden Oputare is a small art-linked garden on Observation Point above Port Chalmers. It marks the former studio site of Ralph Hotere, one of New Zealand's major modern artists, after port expansion changed the headland in the 1990s.

The garden is a quiet stop rather than a formal gallery. Go for the harbour view, the sense of place, and the connection between Hotere's work and the port landscape he watched for decades.
