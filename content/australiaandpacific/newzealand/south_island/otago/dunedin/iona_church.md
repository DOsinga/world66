Iona Church is a stone church in Port Chalmers, built between 1871 and 1883 from local bluestone with Oamaru stone detailing. The interior includes kauri woodwork, a slate roof, and memorial windows.

It is worth a short stop when visiting Port Chalmers or heading out toward the Otago Peninsula. The church's materials and hill-town setting make it one of the more memorable historic buildings around Dunedin's harbour.
