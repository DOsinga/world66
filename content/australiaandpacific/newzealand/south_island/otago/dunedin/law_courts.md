Dunedin's Law Courts are a Gothic civic landmark near the railway station. The 1902 complex was designed by John Campbell and built from dark Port Chalmers breccia with lighter Oamaru stone trim, giving it one of the city's most distinctive public facades.

The building still serves as the city's law courts, so visits are mostly about seeing the exterior and the surrounding historic streetscape rather than touring the interior.
