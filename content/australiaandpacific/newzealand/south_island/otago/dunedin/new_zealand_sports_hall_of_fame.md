The New Zealand Sports Hall of Fame is a small specialist museum inside Dunedin Railway Station, which helps: even if sport is not your main reason for visiting, you are already in one of the city's landmark buildings. The displays honour athletes, teams, and moments from New Zealand's sporting history, from rugby and athletics to less globally obvious disciplines.

It is best for sports-minded travellers or anyone with spare time after admiring the station's tiled halls and exterior. The museum is modest, but it adds a human layer to Dunedin's heritage architecture by filling part of the station with national memory rather than just photographs.
