The Organ Pipes are a set of vertical basalt columns on Mount Cargill, named for their resemblance to a bank of organ pipes. They are one of Dunedin's best short geology walks, with the rock formation giving the climb a clear destination.

The track is steep and can be slippery, so it is more satisfying in dry weather and with proper footwear. On a clear day, combine the rock columns with the wider Mount Cargill views over Dunedin, Otago Harbour, and the peninsula.
