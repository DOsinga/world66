Orokonui EcoSanctuary protects native forest and wildlife inside a large predator-resistant fence north of Dunedin. It is one of the best nearby places to look for rare New Zealand birds and to understand how much work goes into restoring native ecosystems.

The sanctuary works well as a half-day trip from the city. Trails vary in length, and guided visits can help if you are not used to spotting birds in dense bush.
