St Clair is Dunedin's premier beach, a long stretch of dark volcanic sand facing the open Southern Ocean about 5 kilometres south of the city centre. The waves are consistent and powerful — this is a proper surf beach, with a dedicated local surfing community that braves the cold Southern Ocean water year-round. Board rental is available and conditions suit intermediate to experienced surfers.

The famous heated saltwater swimming pool at the end of the Esplanade is one of Dunedin's most distinctive facilities — an outdoor lido filled with sea water warmed to a comfortable temperature, open year-round. On a cold day the contrast between the heated pool and the wild sea just beyond the fence is striking. The Esplanade strip has good cafes and a relaxed atmosphere. It is a popular evening destination for Dunedin locals even in winter.
