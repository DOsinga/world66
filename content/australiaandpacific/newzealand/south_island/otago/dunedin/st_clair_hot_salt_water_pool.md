St Clair Hot Salt Water Pool is a heated outdoor pool on the western headland of St Clair Beach. The water is warmed and salt-based, making it a good option when the surf is too cold or rough for a relaxed swim.

The setting is the main draw: you can swim with the beach and ocean close by, then continue along the St Clair waterfront. Check the season and opening hours before making a special trip.
