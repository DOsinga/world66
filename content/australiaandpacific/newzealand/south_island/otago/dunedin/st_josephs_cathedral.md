St Joseph's Cathedral is a Gothic Revival Catholic cathedral designed by Francis Petre. Construction began in 1878, but the building was completed in a reduced form, leaving it smaller than the original plan with its intended tall spire.

The cathedral is worth a short look for its architecture and its place in Dunedin's strong collection of 19th-century stone buildings.
