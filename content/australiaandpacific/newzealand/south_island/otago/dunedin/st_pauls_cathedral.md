St. Paul's Cathedral is Dunedin's Anglican cathedral, standing beside the Octagon in the centre of the city. Construction began in 1915, but the planned crossing and chancel were never completed, leaving the building with a distinctive mix of grand stone Gothic Revival work and later practical additions.
