---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

Dunedin has a cool temperate climate that can surprise visitors from Australia expecting New Zealand to be warm. Summer (December to February) brings temperatures in the mid-teens to low 20s Celsius, with long evenings and the best conditions for the Otago Peninsula wildlife — albatross chicks and penguin pups are visible from October onward. Winter (June to August) is cold and wet, but the university atmosphere keeps the city lively.

The Otago Farmers Market and most wildlife tours run year-round. Book the Royal Albatross Colony tours well ahead in summer. The Taieri Gorge Railway runs seasonal schedules — check availability before planning your itinerary around it.
