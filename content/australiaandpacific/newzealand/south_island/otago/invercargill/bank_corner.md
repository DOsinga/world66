Bank Corner is a compact piece of old Invercargill, where ornate former bank buildings mark the city's commercial confidence at the turn of the 20th century. The corner is not large, but the facades give central Invercargill one of its most photogenic heritage clusters.

It is worth a look while walking between the city centre's museums, cafes, and Queens Park. Do not expect a formal attraction; the pleasure is in noticing the stonework, proportions, and old financial architecture that still anchors the street grid.
