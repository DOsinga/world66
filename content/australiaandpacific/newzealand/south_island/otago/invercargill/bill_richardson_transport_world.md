Bill Richardson Transport World is a serious museum — perhaps the most impressive of its kind in the Southern Hemisphere. Over 300 vehicles are displayed across a purpose-built facility near the city centre, covering the full sweep of transport history from early-twentieth century steam-powered vehicles to mid-century American trucks to the iconic machines that built New Zealand's road and rail network.

A combined ticket with the adjacent Classic Motorcycle Mecca is the best approach. Together the two collections fill a full day. The presentation is professional throughout — good lighting, detailed interpretation, and vehicles in excellent condition. Don't confuse this with a dusty backyard collection; it is a world-class institution in an unlikely location.
