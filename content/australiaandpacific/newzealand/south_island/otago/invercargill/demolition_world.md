Demolition World is one of Invercargill's stranger pleasures: a rambling village of rescued shopfronts, old signs, machines, mannequins, and bits of Southland life that might otherwise have gone to landfill. It is not polished museum country, which is exactly the point; you wander through a cheerful sprawl of sheds and staged scenes where the joke is often as important as the object.

Come with time to poke around rather than a checklist. Families tend to like the scale and the sense of discovery, while adults get the extra fun of spotting half-remembered brands, tools, and household things from another New Zealand era.
