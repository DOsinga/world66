Oreti Beach stretches for 30 kilometres along the Southland coast west of Invercargill, wild and almost always empty. The sand is firm enough to drive on — locals park here and walk dogs in the evening wind — and the surf is rough Pacific swell, not suitable for swimming but dramatic to watch.

It takes about 10 minutes to reach by car from the city centre. The beach is flat, the sky is enormous, and the Southern Alps are sometimes visible on the horizon to the northwest. In summer the light lingers until nearly 10pm. Bring a jacket — Foveaux Strait wind arrives without warning on this coastline.
