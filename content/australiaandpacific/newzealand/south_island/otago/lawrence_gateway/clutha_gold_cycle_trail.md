The Clutha Gold Cycle Trail is a 71 km trail connecting Lawrence to Roxburgh, following the Clutha River through the gorges of Central Otago. The trail passes through dramatic river gorge scenery — the Clutha Gorge section is particularly impressive, with the river running far below through narrow canyon walls — and visits a series of small historic towns that were gold-rush settlements in the 1860s.

The trail is graded as an easy to moderate ride, accessible to moderately fit cyclists on hybrid or mountain bikes. It takes two to three days end-to-end, with accommodation at Lawrence, Beaumont, and Roxburgh. E-bikes are available to hire and are popular given the occasional climbs. Lawrence is the northern gateway: most cyclists starting from Dunedin take the train or a shuttle to Lawrence, ride south, and return from Roxburgh.
