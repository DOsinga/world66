---
title: Getting There
type: section
---

Lawrence is 90 km west of Dunedin on State Highway 8, a drive of about an hour through rolling Otago farmland. There is no train service, and bus connections are limited — the town is primarily reached by private vehicle or as part of a cycling or touring itinerary.

From Dunedin, head west on the highway through Milton and then south on SH8. The road is good and the drive is straightforward. Lawrence is a natural first overnight stop for people driving or cycling from Dunedin into Central Otago.
