---
title: Things to Do
type: section
---

Lawrence is a quiet heritage town where the main activities are walking, cycling, and absorbing the gold-rush history. Gabriel's Gully, where Gabriel Read made the discovery that triggered the 1861 Otago gold rush, is a short drive south of town. The Clutha Gold Cycle Trail passes through Lawrence, giving cyclists a reason to linger rather than just pass through. The main street has a few heritage buildings worth a look, and the small museum covers the gold-rush era well. For most visitors, Lawrence is a half-day stop between Dunedin and Central Otago rather than a destination in its own right.
