The Oamaru Blue Penguin Colony is the town's headline experience. After dark, little blue penguins come ashore from a day at sea and shuffle back to their nests around the harbour, tiny, quick, and much tougher than they look.

The viewing area is managed as a conservation project as well as a visitor attraction, which makes it easier to watch without crowding the birds. Book the evening viewing if penguins are the reason you stopped in Oamaru.
