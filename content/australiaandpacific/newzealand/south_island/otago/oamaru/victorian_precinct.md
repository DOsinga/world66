Oamaru's Victorian Precinct is the town's old commercial heart, built in the 1870s and 1880s from pale local limestone. The blocks around Harbour and Tyne streets are unusually complete: warehouses, banks, stores, and offices still line the streets close to the harbour.

It is worth more than a quick photo stop. Walk slowly, look up at the stonework, and duck into the galleries and shops that now fill the restored buildings.
