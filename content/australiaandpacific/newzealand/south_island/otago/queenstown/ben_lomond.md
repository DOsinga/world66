Ben Lomond rises to 1,748 m directly behind the town, and the track to the summit is the obvious half-day walk in Queenstown. You can ride the gondola partway up and pick up the trail from there, or walk the whole thing from town through the One Mile Creek track — about 5-6 hours return either way.

The final section is a steep zigzag scramble up a tussock-covered ridge. From the top the view takes in the entire Wakatipu basin, the Remarkables in profile, and on a clear day Mount Aspiring to the north. Take warm layers; the summit can be 20 degrees colder than town.
