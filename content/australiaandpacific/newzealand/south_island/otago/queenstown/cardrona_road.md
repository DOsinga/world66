The road over the Crown Range from Arrowtown to Cardrona and Wanaka is the dramatic shortcut from Queenstown to Wanaka. It climbs steeply out of the Wakatipu basin through switchbacks, crosses a high tussock plateau, and drops down a series of hairpin bends into the Cardrona Valley. Allow 90 minutes including stops at the viewpoints on either side of the summit.

In winter the road sometimes requires chains. In summer it is an exhilarating drive in good weather and a target for cyclists, who climb the south side from Cardrona and reward themselves with a beer at the Cardrona Hotel — the wooden 1863 pub at the foot of the pass.
