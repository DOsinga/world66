The two-storey stone hotel on the corner of Marine Parade and Ballarat Street is one of the few surviving buildings from Queenstown's gold-rush era. It started life in 1871 as a single-storey shanty, was rebuilt in stone in the 1880s, and has been continuously licensed as a hotel since. Today it operates as a boutique hotel; non-guests can stop in at the lakefront bar.

The interiors keep the heavy joinery and dark colours of the period. Sit on the small terrace in the late afternoon — it has the best ground-level view of the lake in central Queenstown — and order a drink before the evening crowd arrives.
