Next door to Fergburger and run by the same outfit. Open round the clock for pies, pastries, sweet bakes, and the famous breakfast bagels. Less famous than its burger sibling but in some ways more useful: you can get a pie at three in the morning here, when nothing else in town is awake.

The mince and cheese pie is the canonical New Zealand bakery item; the venison pie is the more interesting choice.
