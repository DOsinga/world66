Fernhill climbs the western hillside out of town, a residential suburb of houses cut into the slope above Glenorchy Road. The lake views from the upper streets are some of the best in greater Queenstown, which is why a lot of the higher-end short-term rentals are up here.

There is little to do in Fernhill itself, but the One Mile walking track to Ben Lomond starts here, and the trails through the regenerating bush above are popular with mountain bikers. About a fifteen-minute walk down into the town centre.
