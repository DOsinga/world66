Frankton is where the airport, the big supermarkets, and the new commercial developments sit, on the eastern arm of Lake Wakatipu about eight kilometres from central Queenstown. It is functional rather than picturesque, but the lakefront here is genuinely beautiful and underused — a long sandy beach with views back to the town, popular for swimming on hot summer days.

Most visitors pass through on the way to or from the airport without stopping. Worth a detour for the Remarkables Park outlet shopping, the breweries that have opened on the industrial fringe, and the public beach.
