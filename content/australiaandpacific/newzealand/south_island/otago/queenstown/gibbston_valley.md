The Gibbston Valley sits east of Queenstown along the Kawarau Gorge, the southernmost wine region in the world and the start of the Central Otago pinot noir country. A dozen wineries open their cellar doors here — Gibbston Valley, Brennan, Mt Rosa, Peregrine, Chard Farm — most within a short drive of each other.

The valley is rideable as a day trip from Queenstown on the Gibbston River Trail, a 20-kilometre flat ride from the Kawarau Bridge with stops at as many wineries as you can manage. Peregrine's curved-roof winery building is the architectural standout.
