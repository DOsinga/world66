At the far end of Steamer Wharf, with windows on three sides over the lake and a long deck above the water. The menu leans seafood — local fish, oysters, a tower platter for sharing — with a few non-fish dishes for the contrarians. The kitchen is steady rather than fireworks; the location is the draw.

Sit on the deck if the weather is in. The TSS Earnslaw docks immediately behind, which is a feature, not a bug.
