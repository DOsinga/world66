The Kelvin Peninsula sticks out into Lake Wakatipu from the south shore, opposite central Queenstown. The Kelvin Heights neighbourhood occupies the higher ground at its tip — quiet, residential, and home to one of the best lakeside golf courses in the South Island.

Visit for the walking and cycling track that loops the peninsula. It connects to the Queenstown Trail and the cross-lake passenger ferry at the bottom of the hill, which makes it possible to do the whole thing as a half-day loop from town.
