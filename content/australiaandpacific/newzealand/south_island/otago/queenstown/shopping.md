---
title: "Shopping"
type: section
---

Queenstown's shopping is split between practical outdoor gear and gift-shop merino. The Mall and Beach Street have outlets of every major New Zealand outdoor brand — Macpac, Kathmandu, Icebreaker, Mons Royale — useful for filling gaps before a hike or for picking up serious merino layers at New Zealand prices. The art galleries on Marine Parade tend toward landscape paintings of the Wakatipu basin.

For more interesting browsing, walk the lanes off Beach Street and Cow Lane — small jewellers working with greenstone and bone, secondhand record shops, and a handful of clothing boutiques that survive on the seasonal hospitality crowd. Local crafts are better priced in nearby Arrowtown.
