The 22-kilometre dirt road from Coronet Peak into Skippers Canyon is one of New Zealand's most dramatic drives — a narrow shelf cut into the cliffs above the Shotover River, with sheer drops to the river below. Rental insurance does not cover it, so most visitors take a 4WD tour or join a bus.

At the end of the road, the remains of the Skippers gold-rush settlement sit in a sunlit valley with a beautiful suspension bridge over the river. The whole route is a small lesson in the scale and ambition of Otago's gold-rush infrastructure.
