A quiet residential pocket on the lakefront a few kilometres west of town, accessed by the lakeside road that runs from Fernhill toward Glenorchy. Sunshine Bay has a small swimming beach, a public reserve, and one of the few stretches of central Queenstown lakefront that does not feel like a tourist strip.

A good morning walk: head along the One Mile Creek trail or the lakeside path, pick up a coffee at Fernhill on the way back. Most visitors never see this side of town.
