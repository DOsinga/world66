The serrated wall on the east side of Lake Wakatipu is the visual signature of Queenstown — visible from almost everywhere in town, and changing colour through the day as the sun moves over the granite. They rise to 2,343 m at Double Cone and form the boundary of the Wakatipu basin.

In winter they hold one of New Zealand's two main ski fields above Queenstown (the other is Coronet Peak). In summer the Lake Alta walk, accessed from the ski field road, takes you onto a high alpine basin in about 90 minutes. The road in is one of the more dramatic switchback drives on the South Island.
