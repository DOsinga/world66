---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

Queenstown has two genuine high seasons and they are very different. June through September is winter and the busiest period of the year — the ski fields run, the bars are loud, and prices peak with the powder. October to early December is shoulder season: the snow melts, the alpine flowers come out, and accommodation drops to manageable levels.

December through February is summer high season: long warm days, the hiking is at its best, the lake is just about swimmable, and the town fills with adventure travellers. March to May is the quietest, prettiest, and best-value time to visit — autumn colour in Arrowtown is reason enough on its own.
