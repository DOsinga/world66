A grungy rock bar tucked into a laneway, with bare brick walls, sticky floors, and a pool table at the back. The music is loud and guitar-led; the crowd is a mix of locals, seasonal workers, and travellers who have heard the legend. It is one of the oldest still-functioning bars in town and shows it in the best way.

Open late and rarely empty. Order a beer, not a cocktail.
