Puzzling World is an eccentric attraction on the outskirts of Wanaka that has been baffling visitors since 1973. It centres on a large wooden maze that is harder than it looks, but the highlight for most people is the Hologram Hall and the series of illusion rooms — including a room where you appear to shrink and grow as you walk across it, and a chamber where water appears to flow uphill.

It is decidedly family-oriented, but adults find it more entertaining than expected. Allow at least two hours if you are doing the maze and the illusion rooms. It is located about 2 km east of town on the road towards Cromwell.
