That Wānaka Tree is a solitary willow that grows from the shallow edge of Lake Wānaka a short walk from the town centre. It has become one of the most photographed trees in the world — partly because its isolated position in the lake with the mountains behind it makes for an almost geometrically perfect composition, and partly because social media amplified its fame to a point where it now appears on a significant percentage of all photos taken in Wanaka.

Visit at sunrise for the best light and the fewest other photographers. The tree is about 15 minutes' walk from the town centre along the lakefront path.
