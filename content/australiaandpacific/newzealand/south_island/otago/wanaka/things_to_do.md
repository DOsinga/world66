Wanaka's activities divide roughly by season: ski and snowboard in winter, hike and kayak in summer, and stare at autumn foliage in March and April. The town has a relaxed, outdoor-focused culture — the kind of place where the coffee is good, nobody rushes, and the mountains are always visible from the main street.
