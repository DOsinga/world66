Treble Cone, 19 km from Wanaka, is widely considered the best ski field in the South Island for serious skiers. It has the largest vertical drop in New Zealand (700 m), genuinely challenging terrain on the upper mountain, and consistently good snow owing to its southwest-facing aspect. The base is at 1,260 metres and the top at 1,960 metres.

The views from the upper lifts across Lake Wānaka and the Southern Alps are extraordinary. The ski season runs from late June to October. A shuttle bus runs from Wanaka town in the morning and returns in the afternoon; booking ahead in peak season is essential.
