Warbirds & Wheels is a museum near Wanaka Airport with classic cars, vintage motorcycles, and military aircraft. The collection includes aircraft such as an A-4 Skyhawk, Strikemaster, Vampire, and Hurricane, continuing part of the story of the former Fighter Pilots Museum.

It is easy to combine with the nearby aviation and transport attractions around the airport.
