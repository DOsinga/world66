Warbirds Over Wanaka is one of New Zealand's best-known aviation events, staged at Wanaka Airport with the Southern Alps as a dramatic backdrop. The programme centres on vintage military aircraft, aerobatic displays, and ground exhibits that make the airfield feel like a temporary aviation museum.

The catch is timing: this is not an everyday attraction, so it only belongs on an itinerary if your visit overlaps with the event. When it does, it is a strong regional draw and a good fit for travellers who want a break from Wanaka's lake-and-mountain routine without leaving the area.
