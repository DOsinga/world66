---
title: When to Go
type: section
tags: [when_to_go]
---

Wanaka is genuinely a year-round destination. Each season has a distinct character and a distinct crowd.

**Winter (June–September)** brings skiers and snowboarders for Treble Cone and Cardrona. July and August are the busiest months; book accommodation well in advance. The town gets lively in a way it doesn't at other times, with a ski-lodge atmosphere in the bars and restaurants.

**Summer (December–February)** is warm and sunny. The lake is swimmable, hiking conditions are excellent, and the long evening light — sunset at nearly 9.30 pm in December — makes the days feel luxurious. Wanaka is significantly less crowded than Queenstown at this time of year, which is itself a reason to choose it.

**Autumn (March–April)** is the season many locals consider the best. The poplars and willows around the lake and along the roadsides turn gold and amber, the tourist crowds thin, and the weather is still mild. The Wanaka Autumn Festival runs in April.

**Spring (October–November)** is shoulder season — ski fields are closing, summer hasn't arrived, and prices are at their lowest. The mountains still have snow and the valley is green.
