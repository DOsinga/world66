The Gold Guitar Awards are the event behind Gore's claim to be New Zealand's country music capital. Contestants come from across the country for a long weekend of auditions, finals, busking, and country music around town.

If your Southland trip is flexible, the awards are the one time Gore feels less like a service town and more like a destination. They are usually held around King's Birthday weekend.
