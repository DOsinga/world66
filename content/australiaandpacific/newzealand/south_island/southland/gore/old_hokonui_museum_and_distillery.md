The Hokonui museum tells the story Gore likes best: illicit Southland whiskey, hidden stills, local characters, and the long memory of prohibition-era drinking. It is part folklore and part social history, and it gives the town a sharper edge than its tidy main street suggests.

The visitor centre is front-of-house for the museum. Check opening details before going, especially if you are passing through outside the main travel season.
