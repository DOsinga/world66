Tauranga Bay Beach is a broad, sandy, shell-shaped beach near Cape Foulwind on the Buller coast. It is a scenic stop for a coastal walk or a quiet beach break, and the area is sensitive habitat for little blue penguins, so dogs need to be kept under control.
