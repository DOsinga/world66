Tauranga Bay Fur Seal Colony is an easy wildlife stop near Westport, reached by a short track from the car park. Viewing platforms look directly over the rocks where New Zealand fur seals haul out.

The walk takes about 10 minutes and is suitable for wheelchairs, making it one of the simplest seal-viewing stops on the northern West Coast.
