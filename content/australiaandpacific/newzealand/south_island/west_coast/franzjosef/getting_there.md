---
title: "Getting There"
type: section
---

Franz Josef sits on **State Highway 6**, the West Coast road that runs the length of the South Island's western edge. There is no train and no commercial airport — everyone arrives by car or coach.

From [Greymouth](/australiaandpacific/newzealand/south_island/west_coast/greymouth) (and from Christchurch via the Arthur's Pass road) it is around 3 hours' drive south. From Wanaka and Queenstown the route is over the Haast Pass — a spectacular drive of about 3.5–4 hours. The road is sealed and well maintained, but narrow and winding in places; allow more time than the distance suggests.

**InterCity coaches** run daily between Greymouth and Queenstown and call at Franz Josef. The village is small enough to walk end to end in twenty minutes; you do not need a car once you are there, though you will want one for trips to Lake Mapourika and the glacier car park.
