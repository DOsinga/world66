---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

Franz Josef has a temperate-rainforest climate — wet year-round, with around 5,000 mm of rainfall annually. Plan for rain whenever you visit, and consider any clear day a bonus.

**December to February** (Southern Hemisphere summer) is the busiest and warmest period, with temperatures in the high teens to low twenties Celsius and the longest daylight. Heli-hikes and scenic flights are most likely to run; accommodation needs to be booked well in advance.

**March to May** (autumn) and **September to November** (spring) are quieter and often clearer between rain bands. Temperatures drop into single figures at night. **June to August** is winter, with cool days and a slightly higher chance of snow on the ice — visually the glacier is at its best, but heli-flights are more often cancelled by weather.

Whenever you come, build a buffer day into your plans: at least one day of your stay is likely to be lost to weather, and you want flexibility to take the glacier flight on a clear morning.
