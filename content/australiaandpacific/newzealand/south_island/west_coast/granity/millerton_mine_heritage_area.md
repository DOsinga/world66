Millerton sits above Granity in the Papahaua Range, a former coal settlement built around the problem of getting coal down a steep hill to the railway and sea. The old incline, mine remains, and worker-town traces are scattered rather than museum-neat, but the setting makes the engineering easy to understand.

Go in clear weather if you can. The views run back over the rainforest and coast, and the short walks around the old road and mine sites are much better when the cloud lifts.
