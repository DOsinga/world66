Hokitika Gorge is the most dramatic single attraction near Hokitika — a deep, narrow canyon cut by the Hokitika River through grey schist rock, its water a vivid, almost unreal turquoise. The colour comes from suspended glacial silt that scatters light in a way that makes it resemble a swimming pool more than a river. The gorge is about 33 km inland from Hokitika on a good sealed road through farmland and native bush.

The scenic reserve around the gorge has short walking tracks (15–30 minutes each) and a swing bridge that crosses directly over the gorge. The walking is easy and the views from the bridge looking down into the canyon are genuinely spectacular. A longer track follows the river upstream through more bush. It's possible to swim in the gorge in calm conditions, though the water is cold year-round and the currents can be strong after rain. The drive itself passes through some fine West Coast farming country with views of the Southern Alps.
