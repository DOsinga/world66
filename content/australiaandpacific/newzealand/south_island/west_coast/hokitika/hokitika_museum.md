Hokitika Museum occupies the former Carnegie public library, one of the town's most handsome historic buildings. Its collections focus on West Coast history, local industry, and Hokitika's role as a gold-rush and pounamu centre. Check opening status before visiting, as the building has undergone strengthening work.
