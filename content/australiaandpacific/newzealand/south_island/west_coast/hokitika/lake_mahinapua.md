Lake Mahinapua is a calm, dark-water lake just south of Hokitika, surrounded by West Coast forest and wetland. It is a softer landscape than the glaciers and gorges inland, with reflections, birds, and short walks that suit travellers moving slowly along the coast.

The lake is easy to visit by car and works well as a picnic, paddle, or leg-stretch stop. Its history as part of an old inland water route adds a little depth, but the main reason to come is the quiet: still water, dense bush, and the damp green mood of the West Coast.
