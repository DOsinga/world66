Hokitika's main draws are its [pounamu carving workshops](/australiaandpacific/newzealand/south_island/west_coast/hokitika/pounamu_greenstone), [dramatic driftwood beach](/australiaandpacific/newzealand/south_island/west_coast/hokitika/hokitika_beach), and the [Hokitika Gorge](/australiaandpacific/newzealand/south_island/west_coast/hokitika/hokitika_gorge) inland — a short drive rewarded with some of the most photogenic water colour in New Zealand. The annual [Wildfoods Festival](/australiaandpacific/newzealand/south_island/west_coast/hokitika/wildfoods_festival) in March draws a large crowd for an event that's part food challenge, part West Coast party.

It's a good overnight stop between Greymouth and the Fox and Franz Josef Glaciers, with more character than the purely functional glacier-gateway towns further south.
