The West Coast Treetop Walkway lifts visitors into the canopy of the temperate rainforest near Lake
Mahinapua. Instead of looking at the bush only from the track floor, you walk along steel platforms
and bridges among rimu and kamahi crowns, with a tower that opens wider views toward the lake and
Southern Alps foothills.

It is a polished commercial attraction, but it works because the West Coast forest itself is the
subject. The walkway is easiest by car from Hokitika and pairs well with Lake Mahinapua, Hokitika
Gorge, or the town's greenstone workshops, especially for travellers who want a short nature stop
without committing to a long tramp.
