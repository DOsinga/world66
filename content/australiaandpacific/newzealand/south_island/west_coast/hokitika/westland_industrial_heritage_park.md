Westland Industrial Heritage Park collects the heavy machinery that built and worked the Hokitika region: mining gear, sawmilling equipment, tractors, engines, and workshop pieces. It is rough-edged in the best way, more like a working yard of local memory than a polished city museum.

The park is useful because the West Coast's scenery makes more sense when you understand the labour behind it. Rainforest, gold, timber, and remote transport routes all come into focus through the machines left behind.
