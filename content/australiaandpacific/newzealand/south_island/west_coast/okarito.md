Okarito is a tiny coastal settlement north of [Franz Josef](/australiaandpacific/newzealand/south_island/west_coast/franzjosef), reached by a side road through rainforest from State Highway 6. It is one of those West Coast places that feels almost deliberately quiet: a handful of houses, a beach, a lagoon, and the Southern Alps rising behind the bush when the cloud lifts.

The reason to come is the wetland. [Okarito Lagoon](/australiaandpacific/newzealand/south_island/west_coast/westland/okarito_lagoon) is New Zealand's largest unmodified coastal lagoon, a tidal maze of open water, mudflats, and forest edge where white herons feed and the rare rowi kiwi survives in the surrounding sanctuary. Kayaking is the classic daytime trip; guided kiwi walks run after dark when conditions are right.

The village itself is barely a village, which is part of the appeal. Visit [Donovan's Store](/australiaandpacific/newzealand/south_island/west_coast/okarito/donovans_store), climb the [Okarito Trig Walk](/australiaandpacific/newzealand/south_island/west_coast/okarito/okarito_trig_walk) for a view over lagoon, sea, and mountains, then walk the [beach](/australiaandpacific/newzealand/south_island/west_coast/okarito/okarito_beach) until the Tasman weather tells you to turn back.
