Westland packs an extraordinary range of experiences into a narrow coastal strip. The headline acts are the two glaciers — [Fox Glacier](/australiaandpacific/newzealand/south_island/west_coast/franzjosef) and [Franz Josef](/australiaandpacific/newzealand/south_island/west_coast/franzjosef) — but beyond the ice there are mirror lakes, penguin beaches, gold-rush towns, and one of New Zealand's great coastal drives. Wildlife here means kiwi at night, penguins in winter, and the constant sound of birds in rainforest that reaches almost to the tideline.
