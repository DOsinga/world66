Taipa-Mangonui is a chain of small Far North settlements along Doubtless Bay: Taipa, Cable Bay, Coopers Beach, and Mangonui. It is easy to treat the place as a beach strip on the way to Cape Reinga, but Mangonui's old harbour and the pa sites above the coast give it more weight than that.

The best day here moves between water and history. Start with the harbour village and the [Mangonui Heritage Trail](/australiaandpacific/newzealand/taipa_mangonui/mangonui_heritage_trail), climb [Rangikapiti Pa](/australiaandpacific/newzealand/taipa_mangonui/rangikapiti_pa) for the Doubtless Bay view, then spend the warm part of the afternoon on [Coopers Beach](/australiaandpacific/newzealand/taipa_mangonui/coopers_beach) or Taipa's sand.

The strongest single sight is [Butler Point Whaling Museum](/australiaandpacific/newzealand/taipa_mangonui/butler_point_whaling_museum), across the harbour at Hihi. Its 1840s house, gardens, and whaling collection explain why this quiet bay mattered to ships, traders, and local communities long before the holiday homes arrived. Book ahead, because visits are by appointment.
