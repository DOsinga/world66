The Mangonui Heritage Trail is a short self-guided walk through one of the Far North's oldest European settlements. It links the harbourfront, courthouse, hotel, churches, old stores, and viewpoints, with enough interpretation to make the village feel more layered than a lunch stop.

Start near the information centre on Waterfront Drive and give it an hour. The walk is most rewarding when you pause often and look back across the harbour rather than treating it as a checklist.
