Caroline Bay is the kind of beach that rewards a town that didn't expect one. Before the harbour breakwater was built, this stretch of coastline offered no shelter; the beach formed gradually as sediment caught behind the new structure, accumulating over decades into the wide, sandy crescent it is today. It's a sheltered spot by South Island standards, warm enough for swimming in summer and calm enough for families.

The bay is the social heart of Timaru in the warmer months. There's a lagoon, a mini-golf course, a paddling pool, and plenty of flat grass for picnics. In the final days of December and into the new year, the Christmas Carnival takes over — rides, stalls, entertainment — a tradition that has been running since 1912 and still draws large crowds from across Canterbury. Out of carnival season, it's a quiet place: a good spot to stretch your legs after the drive down SH1.
