---
title: "Eating Out"
type: section
---

The Town Basin has the highest density of restaurants and cafes in Whangarei — a string of waterfront places ranging from casual brunch spots to more serious dinner restaurants. Quality is good rather than great; the city does not have a dining destination but several reliable choices.

For something different, head out to Tutukaka or Matapouri on the coast — Schnappa Rock and Vivace at the Tutukaka marina have a more relaxed seaside feel and seafood straight off the boats. Bay of Islands cooking traditions show up here: locally caught snapper, kingfish in season, and a strong cafe culture across the region.
