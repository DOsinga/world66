The Tāoga Niue Museum is Niue's national museum and cultural centre, located in central Alofi. The collection covers the full range of Niuean material culture: traditional carvings, woven mats, tapa cloth, fishing tools, and objects connected to the island's pre-Christian spiritual life. The building also hosts temporary exhibitions on Pacific history and contemporary Niuean art.

The museum is a good starting point for understanding Niue's place in the Pacific — a raised coral island with its own distinct language and traditions, neither Tongan nor Samoan, though sharing elements of both. The staff are welcoming and knowledgeable; conversations here often go well beyond what the labels say. Admission is modest; check opening hours at the tourism office next door.
