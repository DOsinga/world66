---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

The best time to visit Niue is during the dry season, from May to October. Days are warm (around 25°C), humidity is lower, and the ocean conditions are calmer — important if you plan to snorkel or dive. The island receives only a handful of flights a week, so the tourist infrastructure never gets congested regardless of season.

The wet season from November to April brings higher humidity, heavier rainfall, and the risk of tropical cyclones. Niue has been devastated by cyclones in the past — Cyclone Heta in 2004 destroyed much of Alofi — and the cyclone season should inform your timing. That said, between storms the wet season is lush and green, and flights and accommodation are cheaper.

June to August is the best season for swimming with humpback whales, which pass through Niuean waters on their annual migration. August is when the island hosts its annual cultural festival. October is ideal for diving: dry season conditions, warm water, and peak visibility. Spinner dolphins are resident year-round and can often be spotted from shore.

Stargazing is outstanding from April to October, when the sky is clearest. Niue became a UNESCO Dark Sky Territory in 2020 — one of very few whole-island designations in the world. The absence of light pollution anywhere on the island means that on a clear night, the Milky Way is visible with the naked eye from the middle of Alofi.
