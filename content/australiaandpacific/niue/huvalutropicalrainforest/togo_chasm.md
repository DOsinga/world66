Togo Chasm is reached by a track through bush and jagged coral pinnacles on Niue's eastern side. After about 45 minutes on foot, the route drops by ladder into a strange pocket of sand, coconut palms, and rock walls set back from the wild coast.

Wear shoes with grip and bring water. The walk is part of the appeal, but the coral terrain is sharp and the chasm feels much more remote than its distance on the map suggests.
