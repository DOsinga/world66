Norfolk Island's Botanic Garden protects native plants and gives visitors several marked walking routes, from easy upper-level paths to a steeper rainforest walk into the gully. It is one of the simplest places on the island to get a feel for Norfolk's endemic vegetation without committing to a long hike.
