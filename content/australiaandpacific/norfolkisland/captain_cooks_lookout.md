Captain Cook's Lookout has one of Norfolk Island's best coastal views, looking over rock formations, seabird habitat, and the point where Cook came ashore on his second Pacific voyage. The site is easy to visit, with a lookout, boardwalk, and facilities.

It is also the start of the Bridle Track, so you can turn a quick viewpoint stop into a longer coastal walk.
