Fletcher's Mutiny Cyclorama is a 360-degree painted panorama telling the story of the Bounty mutiny, the voyage to Pitcairn Island, and the later Norfolk Island settlement. Local artists painted the work over 16 months, giving it a strong island connection rather than a generic museum feel.

The visit is compact, but it is one of Burnt Pine's more distinctive indoor stops and works well on a rainy or windy day.
