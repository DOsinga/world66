The HMS Sirius Museum displays artifacts recovered from the wreck of HMS Sirius, the flagship of the First Fleet, which was lost off Kingston in 1790. The cannon, anchor, and ballast stones connect Norfolk Island directly to the early colonial history of New South Wales.
