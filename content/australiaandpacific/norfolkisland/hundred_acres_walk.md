Hundred Acres Walk is a coastal nature walk near Rocky Point, known for seabirds nesting and wheeling above the cliffs. The route also passes large Moreton Bay figs, giving the walk a mix of open sea views and island vegetation.

It is a simple outdoor stop rather than a major hike, but it rewards visitors who want a quieter look at Norfolk Island's birdlife and coastal scenery.
