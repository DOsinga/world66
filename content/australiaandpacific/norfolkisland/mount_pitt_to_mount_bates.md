The Mount Pitt to Mount Bates walk follows the ridge from Mount Pitt to Norfolk Island's highest point. The route is only about 1 km and is generally easy, though the final climb to Mount Bates needs a little care.

It is a good short walk for views and a simple way to get a feel for the island's inland high ground.
