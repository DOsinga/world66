The Forbidden Island is a rugged offshore islet and coastal viewpoint on Saipan's eastern side. The landscape is sharper and wilder than the resort beaches, with limestone, surf, and open Pacific exposure.

The hike down toward the coast is the reason to come, but it should be taken seriously: heat, rough footing, and changing surf can make conditions difficult. With good weather and preparation, it is one of Saipan's stronger nature outings.
