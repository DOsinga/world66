---
title: Eating Out
type: section
---

Dining options on Tinian are limited — this is a small, lightly populated island with no tourism infrastructure to speak of. The best-known restaurant is Dynasty Restaurant, which serves Chinese and local food and is popular with the island's residents. Expect simple, generous plates rather than fine dining.

Beyond Dynasty, a few small local eateries and snack shops operate near the main settlement of San Jose. Self-catering visitors will find a small supermarket where provisions can be bought. If you are visiting as a day trip from Saipan, it is worth eating before you come or packing lunch — options can be unpredictable.
