The Japanese Last Command Post is a ruined concrete bunker in the jungle of central Tinian, where the island's Japanese defenders made their final stand during the Battle of Tinian in August 1944. The American assault — a textbook amphibious landing that military historians still cite as a model operation — secured the island in just nine days, but the last Japanese holdouts retreated here before the position was overrun.

The bunker and its surroundings are overgrown but accessible, and the site is marked with interpretive signage. It is a quietly atmospheric spot — the jungle has reclaimed most of it, but the concrete walls still stand, and the history is unmistakable.
