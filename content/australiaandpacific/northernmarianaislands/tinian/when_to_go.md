---
title: When to Go
type: section
---

Tinian has a tropical climate with two broad seasons. The dry season runs from December through June and is the more comfortable time to visit: temperatures hover around 27–30 °C, humidity is lower, and the sea is usually calm enough for diving and snorkelling. July through November is typhoon season — Tinian sits in one of the more active typhoon tracks in the western Pacific, and strong storms can disrupt ferry and flight services from Saipan entirely. If visiting in the wet season, watch forecasts carefully.

Year-round temperatures vary little; even the wet season is warm. There are no major festivals or events that would make one month dramatically better than another. The simplest advice: come between January and May for the best conditions.
