At 242 metres, Mount Ngerchelchuus is the highest point in Palau. The summit sits on the border of Ngardmau and Ngeremlengui states and is reached by a short but steep jungle trail starting from the cross-island road.

The view from the top takes in most of Babeldaob, the reefs of the west coast, and on a clear day the Rock Islands far to the south. Take a guide from one of the surrounding villages — the path is easy to lose and the forest is genuinely thick.
