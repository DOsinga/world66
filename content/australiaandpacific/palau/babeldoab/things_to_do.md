---
title: Things to Do
type: section
---

Babeldaob's attractions are spread out — a car or a guided tour is essential. The east-coast loop takes in the [Badrulchau](/australiaandpacific/palau/babeldoab/badrulchau) stone monoliths in the north, the traditional [Melekeok Bai](/australiaandpacific/palau/babeldoab/melekeok_bai) and the [Capitol of Palau](/australiaandpacific/palau/babeldoab/capitol_of_palau) in the centre, and [Lake Ngardok](/australiaandpacific/palau/babeldoab/lake_ngardok) in the rainforest. The west side has [Taki Falls](/australiaandpacific/palau/babeldoab/taki_falls) and the climb up [Mount Ngerchelchuus](/australiaandpacific/palau/babeldoab/mount_ngerchelchuus).
