The Koror–Babeldaob Bridge — usually just the K-B Bridge — is the only road link between Palau's commercial centre and the country's largest island. For anyone driving north on the Compact Road to explore [Babeldaob](/australiaandpacific/palau/babeldoab), this is the route.

The cable-stayed bridge that stands today, opened in 2002, is worth pausing at for the long view down the channel of small jungle-covered limestone islets that mark the beginning of the Rock Islands. The bridge replaced an earlier concrete bridge whose famous 1996 collapse is still a touchy subject in Palau and a textbook case in civil engineering schools worldwide.
