---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

Palau's climate is tropical and remarkably stable, with temperatures between 27 and 30 degrees year-round. The dry season runs roughly from November to April, with calmer seas, better visibility for diving (often 30 metres or more), and the best weather for boat trips through the Rock Islands.

The wet season, from May to October, brings short heavy downpours and slightly rougher seas, but diving remains good and the islands are noticeably quieter and cheaper. Palau sits below the typhoon belt, so genuine storms are rare. Avoid the peak holiday weeks at New Year and during Japan's Golden Week (late April to early May) if you want quieter dive sites.
