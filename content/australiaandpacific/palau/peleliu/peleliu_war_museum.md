A small museum housed in a former Japanese radio communications building near Kloulklubed, the island's only village. The collection is a hodgepodge of artefacts recovered from the battlefield — uniform fragments, weapons, mess gear, personal letters — and a useful set of period photographs and maps that put the rest of the day's sightseeing in context.

Often not officially open; ask at the village or arrange entry through your guide.
