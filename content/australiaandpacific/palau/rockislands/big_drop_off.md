Big Drop-off is the Rock Islands at their most dramatic underwater: a coral shelf that seems to end abruptly, falling away into blue depth. Divers come for the wall, sea fans, soft corals, clams, and the unnerving pleasure of hovering beside a vertical reef rather than swimming over a flat garden.

It is also a good reminder that Palau's best sights are not always visible from the boat. On calm days snorkellers can enjoy the shallower top of the wall, while divers get the full scale of the drop and the larger fish life moving along it.
