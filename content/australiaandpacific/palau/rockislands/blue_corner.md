Blue Corner is one of Palau's signature dive sites, a reef edge where the current can turn the water into a moving theatre of sharks, jacks, barracuda, and reef fish. It is not a gentle aquarium drift; the draw is the power of the place, with divers often settling in at the corner to watch the action pass in front of them.

Go with a serious operator and listen carefully to the briefing, because conditions can change quickly and this is best for confident, experienced divers. When the current is right, Blue Corner is exactly the kind of dive that justifies building a Palau trip around the Rock Islands.
