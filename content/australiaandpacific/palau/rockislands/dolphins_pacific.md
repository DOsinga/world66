Dolphins Pacific is a marine center in the Rock Islands area of Palau, built around dolphin encounters, education programs, and lagoon-side facilities. Its setting is undeniably beautiful, with Palau's limestone islands and turquoise water doing much of the visual work.

As with any captive marine-animal attraction, travelers should consider their own comfort with the ethics before booking. If you do go, treat it as an educational wildlife encounter rather than a substitute for seeing Palau's reefs and islands in the wild.
