Ngercheu Island lies within Palau's Rock Islands Southern Lagoon, the UNESCO-listed maze of limestone islands, reefs, beaches, and marine lakes south of Koror. It is one of the places where the Rock Islands feel less like a postcard backdrop and more like a real island landscape.

Visits depend on boat operators, permits, weather, and conservation rules. The appeal is the combination of clear water, forested limestone, quiet beaches, and the broader history of Palau's islands rather than a single built attraction.
