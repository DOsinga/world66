The Guadalcanal American Memorial stands on Skyline Ridge, south of the city centre, commemorating the approximately 7,100 American servicemen killed during the six-month Guadalcanal campaign. The memorial is a simple, dignified structure — an open colonnade with inscribed walls listing the major operations and formations involved.

The views from Skyline Ridge are the best in Honiara — out over the town, the harbour, and Iron Bottom Sound beyond. The site is quiet and well maintained. Take a taxi to the ridge; the drive up gives you a sense of the terrain over which the campaign was fought. The nearby hills and valleys still contain unexploded ordnance in places — do not leave marked paths.
