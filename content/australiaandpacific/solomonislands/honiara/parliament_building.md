The Solomon Islands Parliament Building is a pleasant low-rise structure set in gardens south of the town centre. Built in the 1990s with Taiwanese funding, it is the seat of the National Parliament of the Solomon Islands and open to visitors when Parliament is not in session.

The building reflects a broader Pacific architectural style — wooden elements, open verandas, surrounded by tropical planting. The public gallery can be visited when Parliament is sitting, which provides an insight into how democracy functions in a country of 700 islands and dozens of distinct languages. Check locally for sitting times.
