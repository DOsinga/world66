---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

The Solomon Islands sit just south of the equator and are hot and humid year-round. Malaita receives heavy rainfall, particularly on the windward eastern slopes, where annual totals can exceed 5,000mm. The drier and more pleasant months to visit are generally May through October, when the southeast trade winds bring lower humidity and reduced rainfall. This is the most comfortable time to travel by canoe along the coast or on foot into the interior.

The wet season runs from November through April, with the wettest months typically December to March. Tropical cyclones are uncommon in the Solomons compared to other parts of the Pacific but do occur; the main risk period is January to April. Travel during the wet season is not impossible but roads become impassable, coastal travel more difficult, and the combination of heat and humidity more taxing. If your dates are flexible, the May–September window is the obvious choice.
