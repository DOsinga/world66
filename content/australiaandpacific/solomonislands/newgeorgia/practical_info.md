---
title: Practical Information
type: section
---

**Money** — There is no reliable ATM in Munda. Bring enough Solomon Islands dollars in cash from Honiara to cover your entire stay. Some lodges accept credit cards, but do not count on it. USD and AUD can sometimes be exchanged informally at lodges, but at poor rates.

**Health** — Malaria is present in the western Solomons. Start a course of malaria prophylaxis before you leave home and continue it for the recommended period after your return. Use repellent and sleep under a net. The hospital in Munda is basic; for serious medical issues you will need to get to Honiara or further afield. Travel insurance with medical evacuation cover is not optional in this part of the world.

**Communications** — Mobile coverage in Munda is patchy but exists. Our Telekom and bmobile both have some presence in the western islands. Data is slow. Expect long periods without internet access once you leave town. Wi-Fi is available at the main lodges but not always reliable.

**Accommodation** — Options are limited: a few lodges in and around Munda (Agnes Lodge and Zipolo Habu Resort are the best-known), plus village stays arranged through local contacts. Book ahead, especially in the dry season (May to October). Village stays are an excellent way to experience local life but require flexibility and cultural sensitivity — dress modestly, ask before photographing, and accept that schedules are approximate.

**What to bring** — Sunscreen, insect repellent, reef-safe dive/snorkel gear if you have it (rentals are available but selection is limited), a dry bag for boat travel, and enough reading material for rain days. Power is available at lodges but outages occur. Bring a universal adapter.
