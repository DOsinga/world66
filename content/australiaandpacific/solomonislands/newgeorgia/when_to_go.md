---
title: When to Go
type: section
---

New Georgia sits in the tropics and is warm year-round, but there is a meaningful difference between seasons. The dry season runs from May to October — these are the prime months to visit. Seas are calmer, dive visibility is at its best (often 30–40 metres), and the risk of being stuck waiting for a flight or boat in bad weather is lower.

The wet season runs from November to April. Rainfall is heavy, particularly January to March, and the island can feel genuinely remote and cut off during prolonged wet spells. That said, even in the wet season there are clear days, and travellers who don't mind unpredictability will find accommodation cheaper and sites less crowded.

Cyclone season overlaps with the wet season. The western Solomons are not frequently hit directly, but cyclones in the broader region can disrupt transport for days at a time. Travel insurance with evacuation cover is strongly recommended.

There are no major festivals or events in Munda that affect the travel calendar, though the provincial government occasionally organises cultural shows at short notice. Ask locally when you arrive.
