The east side of 'Eua drops in a near-continuous limestone cliff several hundred metres high directly into the open Pacific. There are no roads along this coast and no settlements — the only way in is on foot from the central highland trails.

Several signposted lookout points face out over the ocean: Lakufa'anga, Anokula, and Lokupo are the best known. The cliffs are also the best spot on the island to watch the southerly humpback migration without leaving land. Walk carefully — there are no railings, the limestone edge is undercut in places, and there is nothing but ocean below for thousands of kilometres.
