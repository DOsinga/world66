A natural freshwater pool on the western coast of 'Eua, fed by a spring that emerges from the limestone and runs straight into the sea. The pool is large enough to swim in, clear, and ringed by limestone outcrops and tropical trees.

It is a short walk from the road, signposted from the main north-south route. Locals come here to wash, bathe, and picnic; visitors are welcome but should be discreet. Combine it with a stop at the nearby 'Ovava Tree, a vast banyan that has grown around a sinkhole in the forest.
