Hufangalupe Natural Land Bridge is a dramatic coastal formation where the sea crashes beneath a stone arch and into a blowhole-like sink. It is one of Tongatapu's strongest natural viewpoints, with several places to look down into the crater and out over the surf.

Wear sensible shoes and stay back from exposed edges. The stop is short, but the combination of wave noise, rock, and ocean makes it memorable.
