Mapu a Vaea is a line of natural blowholes on Tongatapu's south-west coast. When the swell hits the reef, seawater is forced through channels in the volcanic rock and shoots upward in white plumes.

The show depends on tide and sea conditions, so it is most impressive at high tide with a decent swell running. The coast here is rugged and open, making the blowholes a worthwhile stop on a loop around the island.
