Mu'a's attractions are all archaeological — the burial mounds of the ancient Tu'i Tonga kings, hidden in plain sight among the coconut palms and modern houses. There are no admission charges and no crowds. You will need a bicycle or car to get between the sites; the area is spread along several kilometres of road on the eastern coast of Tongatapu.
