---
title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Nuku'alofa's sights are modest but distinctive. The Royal Palace, the Royal Tombs at [Mala'ekula](/australiaandpacific/tonga/tongatapu/nukualofa/royaltombs), the unmissable [Basilica of St Anthony](/australiaandpacific/tonga/tongatapu/nukualofa/basilica_st_anthony), and the singing at the [Centenary Chapel](/australiaandpacific/tonga/tongatapu/nukualofa/centenary_chapel) on Sunday morning are the main draws. Walk the seafront, wander Talamahu market, and you have most of a day.
