Princess Margaret Hospital, on the lagoon side of central Fongafale, is the only hospital in Tuvalu. It serves the entire population of the country from a single small facility — basic but functional, staffed by a mix of Tuvaluan and visiting clinicians. Specialised cases are referred overseas, typically to Fiji, on the next available flight.

For visitors with anything more serious than a cut foot or a stomach bug, the practical lesson is to carry good travel insurance with evacuation cover and not to underestimate how isolated the country is. The hospital does its best with what it has, but the limits are real.
