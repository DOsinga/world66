The Tuvalu Sports Ground is the country's national stadium, used for football, rugby, athletics, and the annual independence-day celebrations on 1 October. It is a small, single-stand ground next to the airport, with a grass pitch and an honest, end-of-the-road feel. The Tuvalu national football team plays its home matches here; the country is not a FIFA member but competes regularly in regional competitions.

It is also the venue for the Pacific Games when Tuvalu hosts, and for school events through the year. Match days are a good way to see the whole town turn out — go if there is a fixture on while you are visiting.
