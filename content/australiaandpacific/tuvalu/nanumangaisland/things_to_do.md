---
title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Nanumanga is not a tourist destination — there are no organised sights and no facilities. The interest is in walking the island, sitting in the maneapa at [Tokelau](/australiaandpacific/tuvalu/nanumangaisland/tokelau_village), and exploring the two inland lagoons of [Vaiatoa](/australiaandpacific/tuvalu/nanumangaisland/vaiatoa_lagoon) and [Ha'apai](/australiaandpacific/tuvalu/nanumangaisland/haapai_lagoon).

The most famous feature is offshore: the submerged [Caves of Nanumanga](/australiaandpacific/tuvalu/nanumangaisland/caves_of_nanumanga), where divers in 1986 reported finding what they took to be prehistoric fire pits. Visiting requires permission from the island council and serious diving experience. Even the [reef passage](/australiaandpacific/tuvalu/nanumangaisland/reef_passage) where boats land is worth attention — Nanumanga has no lagoon and the open-ocean transfer is part of the experience.
