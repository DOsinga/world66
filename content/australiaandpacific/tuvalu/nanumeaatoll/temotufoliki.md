Temotufoliki is a small uninhabited islet on the lagoon side of Nanumea, just off the main island near the village. It is one of the closer islets and easy to reach by canoe — a quick paddle from Lolua. The beach is small, the snorkelling around the reef edge is good, and the islet is a quiet picnic spot for villagers on Sundays.

Bring water and shade and ask permission in the village before going — Nanumea's islets are owned and tended by particular families, and the polite thing is to know whose patch you are stepping on.
