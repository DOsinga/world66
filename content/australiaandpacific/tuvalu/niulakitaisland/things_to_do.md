---
title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

There is very little to do on Niulakita in the conventional tourist sense, and that is precisely the point. The island offers reef snorkelling in waters that see almost no boat traffic, birdwatching in dense coconut palm forest, and an experience of extreme isolation that is hard to find anywhere else in the Pacific. If you are here, you are here for the remoteness.
