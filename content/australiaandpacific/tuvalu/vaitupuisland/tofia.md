Tofia is a small settlement on the northern side of Vaitupu, separate from the main Asau cluster and reached by a short walk under the palms. It is little more than a few houses, a small church, and the surrounding pulaka gardens — but the walk to get there from Asau is one of the nicer half-days on the island, shaded under the trees, with glimpses of the lagoon and the ocean alternating on either side.

Bring water; the heat under the palms is humid and unrelenting, even when the sky is overcast.
