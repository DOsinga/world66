Tumaseu is the second of Vaitupu's named village clusters, on the southern side of Asau and effectively continuous with it. The houses, gardens, and pulaka pits stretch in a thin band along the coast under the coconut palms; a short walk takes you from the heart of Asau into Tumaseu without crossing any obvious boundary.

There is nothing in particular to see here, which is rather the point — this is everyday outer-island Tuvalu, dogs in the road, kids on the way to school, washing on lines, the smell of cooking smoke in the early evening. Walk through, accept a coconut if it is offered, and move on.
