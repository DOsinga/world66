---
title: Getting There
type: section
---

**By air:** Air Vanuatu operates light aircraft flights from Port Vila (Efate) to Craig Cove and Ulei airstrips on Ambrym. Schedules change frequently and are subject to weather and demand; book through Air Vanuatu or a local travel agent in Port Vila and confirm before flying. The flight takes about 45 minutes.

**By boat:** Cargo boats connect Ambrym to other islands in Vanuatu, including Santo and Port Vila, but schedules are irregular and conditions can be rough. This is an option for travellers with time and flexibility, not those on a fixed itinerary.

Once on the island, transport between villages is by foot or by local truck along the coastal track. For the volcano trek, guides from the coastal villages will arrange transport to the trailhead.
