---
title: Getting There
type: section
---

Anatom Airport (IATA: AUY) has a short grass airstrip near Anelgauhat Bay. Air Vanuatu operates intermittent services from Port Vila, but schedules change frequently and reliability is limited. Confirm your booking carefully and have a contingency — flights can be cancelled due to weather or demand.

There is no regular ferry service to Anatom. Some travellers arrive on liveaboard dive vessels that include Anatom in their southern Vanuatu itineraries. This is often the most reliable option for divers.

The island has no accommodation in the conventional sense — visitors stay with local families or in very basic guesthouses. Bring all necessary supplies with you, especially any medications, as resupply on the island is extremely limited.
