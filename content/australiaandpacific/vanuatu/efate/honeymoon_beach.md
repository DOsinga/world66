Honeymoon Beach is a pretty beach at the far end of the Pango peninsula, with clear water and a coral reef just offshore. It can be good for snorkelling, though ocean waves make conditions rougher at high tide.

Facilities are basic, with showers, toilets, and changing rooms. Bring reef shoes, food, and water, especially if you plan to stay for more than a quick swim.
