Lelepa is a small island just off the northwest coast of Efate, reached by a short boat trip from Mangaliliu village. The island and its waters form part of Chief Roi Mata's Domain, the country's only World Heritage site.

Guided tours from Mangaliliu cover the rock-shelter where Roi Mata died, the burial site on neighbouring Artok (entered respectfully and reverentially), and the painted cave on Lelepa with its ancient drawings. Book in advance through a community-run guide — the village manages the site itself.
