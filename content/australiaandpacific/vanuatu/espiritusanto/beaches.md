---
title: "Beaches"
type: section
---

Espiritu Santo's beaches are among the finest in the Pacific — powder-white sand, clear turquoise water, and few people. Champagne Beach on the northeast coast is the most celebrated, regularly cited as one of the best beaches in the South Pacific. The beaches here are remote enough that reaching most of them requires a 4WD and some determination, which keeps them uncrowded on most days.
