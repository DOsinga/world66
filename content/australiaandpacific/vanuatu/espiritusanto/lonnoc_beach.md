Lonnoc Beach is one of Espiritu Santo's prettiest north-coast beaches, with clear water, pale sand, and good swimming close to shore. It is a quiet alternative or companion stop to nearby Champagne Beach.

Bring snorkelling gear if the water is calm, and do not count on much beyond simple beach facilities.
