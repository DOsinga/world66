Luganville's open air market is the practical heart of town, where villagers from around Santo bring fruit, vegetables, root crops, and other produce from their gardens. It is a good place to see everyday island trade and pick up fresh food for a simple meal.

The market runs most of the week and is busiest in the morning. Go for produce and local colour rather than souvenirs.
