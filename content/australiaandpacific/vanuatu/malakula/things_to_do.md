---
title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Malakula is the kind of destination where the things to do are not really sights — they are experiences. Visiting villages of the Small and Big Nambas peoples, watching the Rom dance, hiking through the jungle to volcanic crater lakes, snorkelling reefs that almost no one else dives. Everything needs to be arranged through local guides and a sense of pace very different from anywhere else in Vanuatu.
