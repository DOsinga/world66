Bunlap is the southernmost of the major kastom villages on Pentecost's east coast and the spiritual heart of the land-dive tradition. It is reached on foot from the road, a long uphill walk through rainforest from the coast, or by a more rugged track over the central ridge from the western side of the island. There are no vehicles, no shop, no electricity beyond a few solar panels — just the kastom village laid out around a central nasara (dance ground), with the towers built and dismantled each year for the land dive.

Visits are arranged through the village chief, usually via a tour operator in Port Vila or Pangi. Photography requires permission and a fee, dress should be modest by Western standards, and you should be prepared for a long, hot, muddy walk in to get there. The trip is worth every step.
