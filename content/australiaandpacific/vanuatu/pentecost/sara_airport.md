Sara is the northern of Pentecost's two airstrips, on the north coast about halfway between the two ends of the island. It is smaller and less frequently used than Lonorore, but is the practical entry point for the northern villages and the trail across the central ridge.

Flights here are infrequent — typically one or two Air Vanuatu Twin Otter services a week — and operate only when there is enough demand. Always reconfirm the day before. There are no facilities beyond a small shelter, and you will need to have arranged transport in advance with someone in the nearby villages.
