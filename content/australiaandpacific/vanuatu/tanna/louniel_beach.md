Louniel Beach is a black-sand beach on Tanna's northeast coast, backed by palms and open to the island's wilder Pacific side. The dark sand gives the shore a volcanic look that fits Tanna perfectly, especially after visiting Mount Yasur.

It is a quiet beach rather than a resort strip, so come for scenery and space. Conditions can change with the weather and surf, so ask locally before swimming.
