Apia's main market is the easiest place on Upolu to get a feel for everyday Samoa: taro, bananas, coconuts, lavalava cloth, woven baskets, and food stalls under one noisy roof. It is practical as well as atmospheric, especially if you want fruit, snacks, or a cheap lunch.

Morning is best, before the heat builds and the produce stalls thin out. Bring small cash and do not rush; the market is as much a social space as a shopping stop.
