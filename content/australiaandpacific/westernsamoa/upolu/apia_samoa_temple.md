Apia Samoa Temple is a Latter-day Saints temple west of Apia, marked by a tall white spire and carefully kept grounds. The temple interior is reserved for church members, but visitors can still view the exterior and walk the grounds.

It is a short architectural and religious landmark stop for travelers exploring the Apia area.
