Falemata'aga is Samoa's national museum, housed in a colonial-era building that once served as a German school. Its collection is small but useful, with Lapita pottery, stone adze heads, historic photographs, tattoo material, and displays on Samoan birds.

It is a good first stop in Apia if you want context before visiting the cathedral, market, or Robert Louis Stevenson Museum. The building itself adds to the visit, as one of the more tangible reminders of Samoa's German colonial period.
