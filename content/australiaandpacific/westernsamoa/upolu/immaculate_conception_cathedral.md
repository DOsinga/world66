The Immaculate Conception Cathedral is one of Apia's most prominent buildings, standing near the waterfront with twin towers and a richly decorated interior. It is a working church, not just a sightseeing stop, so visit quietly and avoid service times unless you are attending.

The building gives a useful glimpse of how strongly Christianity shapes public life in Samoa. On Sundays the town changes rhythm completely, and the sound of church singing carries much further than the traffic.
