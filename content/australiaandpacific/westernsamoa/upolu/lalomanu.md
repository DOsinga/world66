Lalomanu is the best-known beach area on Upolu's south-eastern coast, with pale sand, clear water, and views toward small offshore islands. It is a strong stop if you are circling the island or linking the south coast with To Sua Ocean Trench.

The appeal is simple: swim, walk the beach, and take in one of Samoa's more photogenic stretches of coast. Facilities vary by beach fale and operator, so bring cash and check locally before settling in for the day.
