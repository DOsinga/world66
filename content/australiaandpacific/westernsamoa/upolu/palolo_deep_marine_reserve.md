Palolo Deep Marine Reserve is Apia's easiest snorkelling escape, a coral-bottomed marine park just east of the harbour. The beach itself is more coral gravel than sand, but the water gets more interesting as you swim farther out, with reef fish and the occasional turtle.

Check the tide before paying to enter. At low tide the coral is sharp and shallow for a long way; an incoming tide makes the swim much easier.
