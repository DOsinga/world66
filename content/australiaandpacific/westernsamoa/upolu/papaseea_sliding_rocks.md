Papase'ea Sliding Rocks are a set of small waterfalls and smooth rock chutes about 6 km southwest of Apia. In good conditions, the rocks can be slid down into the pools below, making this one of Upolu's more playful natural sights.

The falls are best after rain, when there is enough water to keep the slides moving. Check local conditions before going, and take care on the rocks, which can be slippery even where the water looks gentle.
