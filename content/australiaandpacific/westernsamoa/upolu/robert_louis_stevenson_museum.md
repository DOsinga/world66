The Robert Louis Stevenson Museum occupies Vailima, the hillside estate where the Scottish writer spent the last years of his life. The house has wide verandas, polished timber rooms, and enough period detail to show why Stevenson, already ill, chose this tropical ridge over Europe.

Guided visits explain his relationship with Samoa, his family life, and the politics of the islands in the late 19th century. The museum works best paired with the walk up Mount Vaea to his grave.
