---
title: "Getting There"
type: section
---

Ardenica is 18 km south of Lushnjë on the national road that runs down to Fier. The monastery is signposted at the turning, with a short climb up the side road to the hilltop car park. There is no public bus that takes you to the gate; the practical options are a taxi from Lushnjë or Fier, or a self-drive detour off the SH4. From [Tirana](/europe/albania/tirana) it is around 90 minutes by road; from [Berat](/europe/albania/berat), about an hour. Most visitors combine Ardenica with [Apolonia](/europe/albania/apolonia), which is 20 km further south near Fier.
