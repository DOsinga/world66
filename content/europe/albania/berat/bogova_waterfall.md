Bogova Waterfall is a popular nature escape from Berat, reached by a drive through villages and hills before a short walk into a cool limestone pocket. The pool below the falls is cold even in summer, which is precisely why people come when the stone streets of Berat are baking.

It works best as part of a wider day toward Osumi Canyon or Mount Tomorr rather than as a stand-alone tick-box sight. Go early if you want quiet, wear shoes that can handle wet rock, and expect simple local infrastructure rather than a manicured park.
