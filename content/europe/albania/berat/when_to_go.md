---
title: When to Go
type: section
---

Spring (April–June) and autumn (September–October) are the best times to visit Berat. The weather is mild, the light is good for photography, and the town is not crowded. Summer is hot — temperatures regularly reach 35°C — but Berat is in a river valley which captures the heat; the castle is exposed and walking through Mangalem in midday in August is uncomfortable. Mornings and evenings are better in summer.

Albanian summers bring more domestic tourists than international ones; Berat is popular with Albanians from Tirana and the coast, which means restaurants fill on summer weekends. Winter is cold and occasionally wet but very quiet; many guesthouses close in January and February, though the castle and museums remain open. The town's natural light — a warm south-Balkan quality that photographers seek — is at its best in October and early November.
