The Aleksandër Moisiu House-Museum is a rare surviving 19th-century urban house in Durrës, associated with the actor Aleksandër Moisiu. Its displays cover local daily life, folk clothing, household objects, and Moisiu's career, making it a useful small stop near the city's Roman remains.
