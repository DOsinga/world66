Fatih Mosque is one of the oldest mosques in Albania, built in 1503 during the Ottoman period and named for Sultan Mehmed the Conqueror. Earthquakes and wars have damaged it over the centuries, but the restored mosque and minaret remain an important piece of Durrës's Ottoman history.
