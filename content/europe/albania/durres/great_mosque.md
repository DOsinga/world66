The Great Mosque stands near the city centre and is the main Muslim place of worship in Durrës. The current building dates from the post-communist reconstruction period but occupies a site with a long Ottoman-era history. The mosque has a single minaret and a simple interior that reflects the Albanian Ottoman architectural tradition.

Durrës's Ottoman heritage is less visible than in some Albanian cities — the centuries of Roman, Byzantine, and Venetian occupation left more physical traces — but the mosque and the nearby Fatih Mosque together give a sense of the four centuries of Ottoman presence here.
