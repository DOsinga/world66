The Old Doors Trail is a heritage walk through older parts of Durres, focused on historic doors, family houses, courtyards, and the memory of the city before modern redevelopment. It links streets and residences such as Villa Tedeschini with less obvious corners near the centre.

Use it as a slow walk between the city's larger Roman, Byzantine, and Ottoman-era sights. The appeal is in details and street texture rather than a single landmark.
