Holta Springs are warm springs in a narrow canyon east of Elbasan. The main hot pool is a short walk beyond the canyon entrance, with rock walls and clear water giving the place a more natural feel than a built spa.

The springs are best treated as a half-day nature stop from Elbasan. Footwear that can handle wet rock is useful, and water levels can change after rain.
