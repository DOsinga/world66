Llixhat e Elbasanit are the thermal springs southeast of Elbasan, a traditional spa stop where mineral water is the main draw. The facilities are simple by international spa standards, but the springs are part of local travel culture and make an easy excursion from town.

Come for a soak and for the valley setting, not for luxury. It is worth checking current opening arrangements locally, since individual bathhouses and hotels can change hands or schedules.
