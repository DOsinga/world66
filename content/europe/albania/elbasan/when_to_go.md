---
title: When to Go
type: section
---

Elbasan is a year-round stop rather than a destination that demands a particular season. The Shkumbin valley has a continental climate with hot summers and cold winters. July and August can be genuinely hot (35°C or above), which makes the shaded streets of the old town preferable to sitting in the open.

Spring (April–May) and autumn (September–October) are the best times to visit: mild temperatures, the mountains still carrying some colour, and the city going about its normal life without summer heat haze. The Dita e Verës (Summer Day) festival on 14 March is a national celebration with particular significance in Elbasan — it marks the pagan new year and has been observed here longer than anywhere else in Albania.

Winter is cold and sometimes foggy in the valley. There's no particular reason to avoid it if you're passing through, but the old town is at its least inviting in February.
