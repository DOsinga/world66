Fier is a mid-sized city in southwestern Albania, best known to travellers as the nearest town to the ancient ruins of [Apolonia](/europe/albania/apolonia) (about 12 km away) and the [Monastery of Ardenica](/europe/albania/fier/ardenica_monastery). The city itself is industrial and not a destination in its own right, but it is a practical base if you're combining visits to those two sites.

The [ruins of Apolonia](/europe/albania/fier/apolonia_ruins) — an ancient Greek and Roman city that Augustus Caesar used as a base — contain a well-preserved odeum, stoa, and a monastery museum. It is one of Albania's finest archaeological sites and should not be missed if you are passing through.

Buses and furgons connect Fier to Tirana (about 2 hours), [Vlore](/europe/albania/vlore), and [Berat](/europe/albania/berat).
