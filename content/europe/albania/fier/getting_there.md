---
title: "Getting There"
type: section
---

Fier is easily reached from Tirana (about 2 hours by bus or furgon), from Vlora (about 1 hour), and from Berat (about 1.5 hours). Buses and shared minivans (furgons) run frequently on all these routes from early morning. Fier's bus station is in the centre of town.

Fier sits on the main Tirana–Vlora highway (SH4) and is easy to reach by car. It is often used as a lunch stop or overnight break on a drive down the Albanian Riviera.
