---
title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Jalë is a small place and its appeal is simple: the beach, the water, and the surrounding coast. The main activity is swimming and relaxing on the shingle. But a short drive or walk in either direction reveals some of the most dramatic scenery on the Albanian Riviera — Ottoman castles, hidden bays, and villages that cling to steep hillsides above the Ionian Sea.
