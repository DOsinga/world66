Korçë is the largest city in southeastern Albania, set on a plateau at 869 m with a cooler climate than the coast. It has a reputation as one of the more culturally refined cities in Albania — the first Albanian-language school was founded here in 1887, and it was briefly the centre of a French protectorate during World War I.

The [old bazaar](/europe/albania/korce/old_bazaar) (çarshi) has been restored and is pleasant to walk through. The [National Museum of Medieval Art](/europe/albania/korce/national_museum_of_medieval_art) holds over 7,000 icons, artworks, and ecclesiastical objects — the largest collection of its kind in Albania and genuinely impressive. The [Museum of Education](/europe/albania/korce/national_museum_of_education), housed in the 1887 Mësonjëtorja, marks the founding of Albanian-language schooling.

The city is also known for its beer ([Birra Korça](/europe/albania/korce/birra_korca) is Albania's most popular lager, brewed here) and for a lively cultural scene. The [Cathedral of the Resurrection of Christ](/europe/albania/korce/resurrection_cathedral), an imposing neo-Byzantine structure built in the 1990s on the site of a church destroyed under communism, dominates the centre. The 15th-century [Mirahori Mosque](/europe/albania/korce/mirahori_mosque) at the edge of the bazaar is one of the oldest in Albania.

[Voskopojë](/europe/albania/korce/voskopoje) (Voskopoja), about 20 km west, was a major 18th-century cultural and trading centre — at its peak one of the largest towns in the Balkans — and retains several frescoed churches worth visiting. The surrounding mountains offer good hiking.
