A small but historically important museum housed in the original Mësonjëtorja — the first school to teach in the Albanian language, founded in 1887. The building itself is the main exhibit: restored classrooms with period desks, blackboards, and didactic materials.

Albanians treat this as something close to a national shrine; the founding of Mësonjëtorja is taught as a foundational moment in the national awakening (Rilindja). The displays explain the political risk involved and the role Korçë played as the centre of Albanian-language education through the 19th and early 20th centuries.
