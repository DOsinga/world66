---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

Korçë sits at 869 m on a high plateau and has a continental rather than Mediterranean climate. Summers are warm but rarely oppressive — daytime highs around 28°C — and the nights stay cool. Winters are cold by Albanian standards, with snow most years and temperatures dropping below freezing for weeks at a stretch.

The best months are May to early October. The Korça Beer Fest in mid-August is the biggest event of the year and draws crowds from across Albania. February is the worst — cold, sometimes snowy, and many smaller restaurants close for a few weeks.
