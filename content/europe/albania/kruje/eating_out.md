---
title: "Eating Out"
type: section
---

Several small restaurants line the bazaar street and the road up to the castle, and a couple of more interesting spots are tucked into the old town below. The classic Krujë lunch is a plate of *byrek* (filo pie with cheese or spinach), grilled lamb with rice or roasted potatoes, and a glass of *raki* — Albanian fruit brandy, often homemade.

For the best view, the terrace restaurants in the upper castle (notably **Bardhi** and the cafe at the Skanderbeg Museum's foot) overlook the coastal plain to the Adriatic on a clear day.
