---
title: "Getting There"
type: section
---

Most people come from [Tirana](/europe/albania/tirana), 32 km south. **Furgon** (shared minibus) runs hourly from the northern fringes of Tirana (Zogu i Zi) and takes about 45 minutes; expect to pay a few hundred lek. **Taxi** from Tirana is around €25-30 one way. Most organised half-day tours from Tirana combine Krujë with [Durrës](/europe/albania/durres) on the coast.

The closest airport is **Tirana International (TIA)**, 20 km away. From there, you can take a taxi direct or catch the airport shuttle into Tirana and connect with a furgon from there.
