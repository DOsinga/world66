---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

Krujë is pleasant any time of year apart from the deep winter, when sleet and fog can roll up the mountain. **April-June** and **September-October** are the best months — mild, clear, and the surrounding hills are green or red. **Summer** is hot on the coast below but Krujë's 600-metre altitude keeps it cooler. **Winter** can be cold, with occasional snow on the castle walls; some of the smaller museums close.

The local festival of **Skanderbeg Day** on 17 January marks the death of the national hero, and the castle hosts ceremonies. National Day (28 November) and Liberation Day (29 November) are also widely celebrated.
