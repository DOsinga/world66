The Kosovo Exodus Memorial marks Kukës' role during the Kosovo War, when the city received refugees crossing into Albania. Its tower-like form nods to Albanian hospitality and remembrance rather than to triumphal military history.

It is a small stop, but an important one. Without this story, Kukës is just another border town with a good view.
