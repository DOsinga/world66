The two bridges over the Drin give Kukës its easiest view: water below, the rebuilt town above, and mountains closing the horizon. One bridge dates from the communist period, while the newer metal bridge adds a sharper modern line across the reservoir.

Come here before deciding whether to push farther into the hills. The bridges explain the geography of Kukës better than any map.
