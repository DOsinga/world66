Peca Castle is an archaeological ruin north of Kukës, tied to older routes through the White Drin basin. The remains are modest, but the site adds depth to a city that can otherwise feel very modern because the original Kukës lies under the reservoir.

Treat it as a rough historical viewpoint rather than a restored castle. It works best if you already have transport and want to see more than the town centre.
