Vana Gorge cuts between Koritnik Mountain and Gjallica Mountain just outside Kukes. The gorge was carved by erosion from the Lume area and sits within the wider Korab-Koritnik National Park landscape.

It is a good short nature stop from town, especially if you are using Kukes as a base for mountain views rather than just passing through.
