The Ethnographic Museum of Lezhe introduces the city's older domestic culture through traditional clothing, handmade objects, craftwork, and household items. It is a modest stop, but a useful one if you want context before visiting the castle and Skanderbeg Memorial.

The displays are strongest on local costume and everyday craft. Give it a short visit rather than planning a long museum afternoon.
