Kune-Vain-Tale Lagoon is the wetland and coastal reserve southwest of Lezhe and Shengjin. It mixes lagoons, pine woods, low islands, beach, and bird habitat at the mouth of the Drin.

Come when you want a nature break after the castle and memorial. The area is best with your own transport, and it pairs well with a seafood meal on the coast rather than a rushed taxi out and back.
