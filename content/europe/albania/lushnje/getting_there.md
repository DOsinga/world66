---
title: "Getting There"
type: section
---

Lushnjë sits on the main SH4 road between Durrës (about an hour north) and Fier (about 25 minutes south), and is served by frequent intercity buses and shared minibuses (*furgon*) from both. The journey from Tirana takes around an hour and a half by road; the city has no functioning passenger railway.

For Berat, take a furgon east from the Lushnjë bus terminal — the trip is about an hour through the hills.
