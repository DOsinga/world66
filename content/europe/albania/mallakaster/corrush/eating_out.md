---
title: Eating Out
type: section
---

There are no restaurants or food establishments in Corrush. The village is a small rural settlement without any tourism infrastructure. Visitors should bring food and water, or plan to eat in Ballsh, around 15 kilometres to the north, which has a handful of simple Albanian restaurants and cafes serving grilled meat, byrek, and local produce.

Fier, the nearest substantial town, has a wider range of options including restaurants serving traditional Albanian cuisine — tave kosi (baked lamb with yoghurt), fergese (peppers and tomatoes with cottage cheese), and fresh fish from the Adriatic coast nearby.
