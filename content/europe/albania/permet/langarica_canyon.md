Langarica Canyon is a narrow limestone canyon near Përmet, best known around the thermal springs and Ottoman-era bridge at Bënja. The canyon runs for about 5 km, with hot springs, rock walls, and clear water making it one of the area's main nature stops.

In suitable conditions it can be explored by walking, bathing near the springs, or kayaking with local guidance.
