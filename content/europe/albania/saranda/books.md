**Broken April** by Ismail Kadare is the most widely read Albanian novel in translation — a story set in the northern Albanian highlands, but essential context for understanding Albanian culture. Kadare grew up in Gjirokastër, two hours east of Saranda and worth combining.

**The Accursed Mountains** by Robert Carver (1998) is a travel account of Albania in the mid-1990s just after the collapse of communism — chaotic, vivid, and the best English-language account of Albania in that extraordinary transitional period.

**Albania: A Modern History** by Miranda Vickers is the most accessible academic history of the country, covering the Ottoman period through to post-communist reconstruction.
