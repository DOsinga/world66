Drisht Castle is a ruined hilltop fortress above the village of Drisht, northeast of Shkoder. The surviving walls and towers are mostly from the Venetian period, though the site is much older, and the climb gives wide views over the surrounding countryside.

It is best treated as a short rural side trip from Shkoder, often combined with nearby Mes Bridge.
