---
title: "Getting Around"
type: section
---

Shkodër is small enough to walk across in 20 minutes, and the city centre is compact and manageable on foot. For the lake shoreline and Rozafa Castle, a bicycle is the ideal vehicle — the cycling paths are the best in Albania and the terrain is almost entirely flat. Bikes can be rented from several shops in the centre for a few euros per day.

Taxis are available and cheap by Western European standards. Furgons serve surrounding villages throughout the day from the bus station. For the trip to Theth or Valbonë in the mountains, it's easiest to join a shared vehicle from the bus station — these run in summer and cost little. A private car is needed for exploring the less accessible corners of the lake.
