---
title: "Getting There"
type: section
---

Shkodër is served by frequent buses from Tirana (journey time about 2.5 hours) and from Podgorica in Montenegro (about 1.5 hours). The bus station is on the eastern edge of the city centre. There are also minibuses (furgons) running on the same routes throughout the day — they're faster and more frequent than the big buses but depart when full rather than to a timetable.

From Kosovo, furgons run from Prizren and Peja via mountain roads that add scenic value but extend the journey. The road from Theth and Valbonë (the northern mountain villages) is unpaved and requires an SUV or high-clearance vehicle outside summer.

There is no functional passenger rail to Shkodër; the line to Tirana exists on maps but is not reliably operational.
