A 13-arch Ottoman stone bridge spanning the Kir River about 8 kilometres northeast of central Shkodër, near the village of Mes. It is the longest Ottoman bridge in Albania (108 metres) and one of the best-preserved in the southern Balkans, with its hump-backed profile and slender pointed arches all original.

The bridge is open to walkers only; cars use the modern crossing a hundred metres downstream. Visit early morning or late afternoon to avoid the worst of the heat and the small busloads of day-trippers from Shkodër. A taxi from the city is the easiest way to come; allow an hour for the round trip with time on the bridge.
