The Migjeni Museum is a school-museum connected with Albanian poet Millosh Gjergj Nikolla, better known as Migjeni. It focuses on his work and his role in local education in Puka in the 1930s.

It is a modest stop, but worthwhile for travelers interested in Albanian literature and regional history.
