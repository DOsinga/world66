The Museum of Witness and Memory documents political persecution under Albania's communist regime, with a focus on prisoners from Shkoder. Exhibits use photographs, video, and personal testimony to explain the surveillance, imprisonment, and punishment imposed on dissidents.

It is a sober museum, but one of the city's most important stops for understanding 20th-century Albania.
