Shkoder Historical Museum is set in a traditional Shkodran house and covers local history, social life, crafts, and culture. The collection includes paintings, handicrafts, and objects from everyday life in the region.

The building is part of the appeal: its domestic setting gives the museum a more intimate feel than a standard civic collection.
