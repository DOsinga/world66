  The cathedral was converted into a sports hall by the communist regime in 1967 when Albania declared itself the world's first atheist state. Basketball courts were laid over the floor, and for nearly twenty years the building served as a gymnasium. It was reconsecrated in 1990, almost immediately after the collapse of the regime, and has been a working cathedral since.
---

St. Stephen's Cathedral is the main Catholic cathedral of Shkodër and one of the most important Catholic buildings in Albania. The current building dates from the late 19th century and is a handsome neo-Romanesque structure with a confident two-towered facade. Shkodër's Catholic identity is deep-rooted — the city has been a bishopric since the Middle Ages — and the church serves a community that maintained its faith through 45 years of communist repression.

The interior is plain by Italian standards but has been carefully restored since 1990. The adjacent square is a natural meeting point for the neighbourhood. Worth a look alongside the mosques and Orthodox church that sit within a few hundred metres of each other — the coexistence of religious buildings in this compact area says something about Shkodër's history.
