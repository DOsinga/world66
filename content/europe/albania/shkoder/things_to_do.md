---
title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

The single must-see is **Rozafa Castle** on its hill at the confluence of the rivers, with a thousand-year view over Lake Shkodër, the Buna delta, and the Accursed Mountains. After that, the city's character is in its long pedestrianised street, **Rruga Kolë Idromeno**, a stretch of restored Italianate facades that fill with cyclists and evening strollers — Shkodër is unusual in Albania for its bike culture, and a rented bicycle is the right way to move around.

Three buildings round out the historic centre: the **Marubi Museum** (one of the best photography museums in the Balkans), **St Stephen's Catholic Cathedral**, and the **Ebu Beker Great Mosque**. Just outside town are the Ottoman **Mes Bridge** and the lakeside village of **Shiroka**. The **lake itself** — the largest in the Balkans — is the open horizon Shkodër faces.
