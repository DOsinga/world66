Këlcyrë Gorge is a narrow break in the Vjosë valley north of Tepelena, where the river cuts between steep slopes. The road through the gorge gives good views of the water and cliffs, and the Orthodox monastery at the gorge is the main reason to stop rather than simply pass through.
