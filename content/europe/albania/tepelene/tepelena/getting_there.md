---
title: "Getting There"
type: section
---

Tepelena sits on the SH4 highway between Gjirokastra (45 minutes south) and Fier (around 90 minutes north), so most visitors arrive by car. The road through the Vjosa valley is one of the most scenic in Albania.

Furgons (shared minibuses) connect Tepelena with both Gjirokastra and Tirana several times daily, though schedules are informal. The town has no train service and no airport; the nearest international airport is Tirana, about three hours by road.
