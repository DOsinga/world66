---
title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Tepelena is essentially a stop, not a destination — most visitors pause for an hour or two on the road between Gjirokastra and the coast, climb to the castle for the view, and continue on. The castle ruins, the dramatic river setting, and a coffee in the small town square will fill an afternoon comfortably.

The Vjosa river itself is the real attraction for anyone with more time. The wild river park designation in 2023 has put the valley on the map for nature and adventure travel, and several outfitters now run rafting and kayaking trips from upstream villages.
