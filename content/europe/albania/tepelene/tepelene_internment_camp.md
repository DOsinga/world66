Tepelenë Internment Camp was used from 1949 to 1954 by Albania's communist regime to hold families of political opponents. Hundreds of people, including many children, died here.

The remains are in poor condition and are not set up as a formal museum, but the site is a sobering stop for travellers interested in Albania's 20th-century history. It can also be viewed from the northern side of Tepelena Castle.
