The Bektashism Museum sits within the Bektashi World Centre and introduces a religious tradition that is unusually important to Albanias identity. Exhibits explain Bektashi history, ritual life, leaders, and the orders survival through periods of suppression and political upheaval.

It is a useful stop because Tiranas religious landscape is more layered than the headline mix of mosque, church, and communist atheism suggests. Come respectfully dressed and treat the visit as both a museum stop and an active spiritual site.
