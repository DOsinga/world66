A counter-only byrektore — pastry shop — a short walk off Skanderbeg Square, serving paper-thin layered phyllo pies stuffed with white cheese, spinach, leek, tomato, or minced meat. Sold by the slice, eaten standing up or on the small benches outside.

Open from early morning until early evening. The best fast lunch in central Tirana for around 200 lek.
