Behind the National Gallery of Fine Arts, in a small fenced courtyard, the surviving bronzes of Lenin, Stalin, and Enver Hoxha lie roughly stacked against a wall — defaced, missing arms or noses, occasionally vandalised again by new graffiti. It is one of the strangest open-air sculpture parks in Europe, and gets a quick visit on the way into or out of the gallery.

Five minutes. The contrast with what the museum itself displays inside is the whole point.
