A small Ottoman türbe — domed mausoleum — built in 1820 for Kapllan Pasha Toptani, the local strongman of his day. The structure is now stranded between modern apartment blocks and a tram-stop, an unlikely fragment of pre-communist Tirana.

A two-minute stop, but together with the [Tanners' Bridge](/europe/albania/tirana/tanners_bridge) and the [Sulejman Pasha Mosque](/europe/albania/tirana/sulejman_pasha_mosque) it traces a useful Ottoman heritage walk through the modern centre.
