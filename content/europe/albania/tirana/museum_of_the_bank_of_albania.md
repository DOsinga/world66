The Museum of the Bank of Albania is a small museum in central Tirana devoted to money, banking, and economic history. Its numismatic collection traces coinage and currency in the region from antiquity to modern Albania, while the upper level explains the role of the central bank.

It is a focused stop rather than a broad national museum, but the location near Skanderbeg Square makes it easy to add to a walk around the centre. It is most rewarding if you enjoy coins, banknotes, or the material history of trade.
