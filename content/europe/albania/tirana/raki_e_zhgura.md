A tiny shop in the lanes around the New Bazaar selling traditional raki — distilled in small batches from grape, mulberry, and walnut — and Albanian honey, cheese, and dried herbs. The owner is happy to talk you through the differences and let you taste a few before buying. Some of the best take-home edible souvenirs in the city.
