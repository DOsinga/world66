Sali Shijaku's Traditional House is a preserved Tirana house associated with the Albanian painter Sali Shijaku. It combines traditional domestic architecture with displays of art and period atmosphere.

The house is a quieter cultural stop than the city's headline museums, but it adds a useful view of older Tirana away from the main boulevard.
