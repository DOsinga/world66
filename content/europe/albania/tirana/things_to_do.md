---
title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

The city centre is the starting point: [Skanderbeg Square](/europe/albania/tirana/skanderbeg_square), with its [equestrian statue](/europe/albania/tirana/skanderbeg_monument) and the [National History Museum](/europe/albania/tirana/national_history_museum) on one side, and the [Et'hem Bey Mosque](/europe/albania/tirana/ethem_bey_mosque) in the corner — one of the few religious buildings to survive communist-era demolitions. Climb the [Clock Tower](/europe/albania/tirana/clock_tower) beside the mosque for the best low-angle view of the square.

[The Pyramid (Piramida)](/europe/albania/tirana/the_pyramid), originally built as a museum dedicated to Enver Hoxha and now repurposed as a tech education space, is a short walk from the square. Its brutalist form is unmistakable. [Bunk'Art 1](/europe/albania/tirana/bunkart_1) and [Bunk'Art 2](/europe/albania/tirana/bunkart_2), both in former nuclear bunkers, are the most original museums in the city — together with the small but powerful [House of Leaves](/europe/albania/tirana/house_of_leaves), they cover the communist secret police (Sigurimi) and the long dictatorship in detail. The [Martyrs' Cemetery](/europe/albania/tirana/martyrs_cemetery) on the city's eastern edge has good views and holds the graves of those who died in World War II resistance, and the small [Postbllok Memorial](/europe/albania/tirana/postbllok_memorial) at the edge of Blloku is a useful pause on any communist-era tour.

For something less heavy: the [National Museum of Fine Arts](/europe/albania/tirana/national_art_gallery), the new [Resurrection Cathedral](/europe/albania/tirana/resurrection_cathedral), the [Tanners' Bridge](/europe/albania/tirana/tanners_bridge), and a wander through [Blloku](/europe/albania/tirana/blloku) and the [Pazari i Ri](/europe/albania/tirana/new_bazaar) district.

[Mount Dajti](/europe/albania/tirana/mount_dajti) is reached by the [Dajti Express cable car](/europe/albania/tirana/dajti_express) from the city outskirts — a 15-minute ride to 1,600 metres with views over the city and surrounding hills. The park at the top has restaurants and hiking trails.
