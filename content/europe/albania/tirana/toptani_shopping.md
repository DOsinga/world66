The most central modern shopping mall in Tirana, just east of Skanderbeg Square inside the restored fortress walls. International chains, a multiplex cinema, a top-floor food court with a small terrace overlooking the centre, and a supermarket. Useful for travellers needing a quick last-minute purchase or a wet afternoon's escape.
