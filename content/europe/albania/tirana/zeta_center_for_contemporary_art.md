ZETA Center for Contemporary Art is a small independent exhibition space in Blloku, focused on contemporary Albanian and international visual art. Shows change, and the programme can include photography, video, installation, performance and discussion events.

It is not a big museum, but it gives a useful look at Tirana's current art scene beyond the national collections. Check the current exhibition before making a special trip.
