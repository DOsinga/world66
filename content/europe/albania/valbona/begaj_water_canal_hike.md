The Begaj Water Canal hike follows a mountain water channel above the Gashi River, built to carry water to fields near Begaj village. It is a quieter alternative to the main Valbona Valley routes, with the pleasure coming from the unlikely engineering as much as from the valley scenery.

Allow two to three hours each way for the roughly 9 km walk. The canal is usually dry enough to follow outside the June to August irrigation season, but the path can be muddy and exposed in places, so take care on the canal wall.
