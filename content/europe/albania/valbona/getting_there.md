---
title: Getting There
type: section
---

Getting to Valbona requires commitment but the journey is part of the experience.

**From Shkodër:** Take a morning minibus to Koman (about 2.5 hours on a rough road), then the daily ferry across Koman reservoir to Fierza (3 hours), then another minibus up the valley to Valbonë (1 hour). The whole journey takes a full day and must be started early — the ferry departs Koman around 9am and does not wait. Confirm current departure times locally the day before.

**From Kosovo (Prizren or Gjakova):** Cross the border at Morinë and approach via Bajram Curri, from which minibuses run to Fierza and then into the valley. This route is shorter for travellers coming from Kosovo.

**Direct minibus from Shkodër:** In summer, some operators run a combined service that coordinates the Koman–Fierza ferry with onward transport. Ask at Shkodër guesthouses. The minibus returns from Valbonë to the ferry in the morning; you cannot get out and back the same day.

There are no ATMs or card machines in the valley. Bring all cash (Albanian lek) before you leave Shkodër.
