The ferry from Koman to Fierza — or Fierza to Koman, depending on direction — is one of the most spectacular boat journeys in Europe. The route crosses the long, narrow Koman reservoir on the Drin River, threading between sheer limestone cliffs, forested slopes, and waterfalls for about three hours. There are no roads along the valley; the ferry is the only connection.

The boat is a functional local service, not a tourist vessel — it carries villagers, motorcycles, small vehicles, and supplies to isolated communities along the shore. The scenery starts spectacular and stays that way. Most travellers take it as the approach to Valbona: bus from Shkodër to Koman, ferry to Fierza, minibus to Valbonë. The ferry departs once daily in the morning; the exact time shifts seasonally and should be confirmed the day before.
