Theth is the valley at the other end of the Valbona Pass trail, and the combination of the two makes one of the classic Balkan treks. Theth village sits in a long U-shaped valley with a white stone church, a waterfall, a traditional lock-in tower from the blood feud era, and a scattering of guesthouses. It is as remote as Valbona but has been slightly more visited for longer, partly because the road connection (rough but passable by 4WD) provides an alternative to the walk.

The village church dates to 1892, built on the foundations of an earlier wooden structure. The surrounding valley has excellent walks along the river and up into the surrounding peaks. Most hikers arriving from Valbona spend a night or two here before catching the minibus back to Shkodër. The **Grunas Waterfall** — about an hour's walk from the village — is one of the highest in Albania.
