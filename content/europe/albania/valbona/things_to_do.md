---
title: Things to Do
type: section
---

The valley's main attraction is the [Valbona to Theth hike](/europe/albania/valbona/valbona_pass), one of the best mountain walks in the Balkans. For those not doing the full crossing, the [Valbona River](/europe/albania/valbona/valbona_river) walk and shorter routes up into the [Accursed Mountains](/europe/albania/valbona/accursed_mountains) are worthwhile. The approach via the [Koman Ferry](/europe/albania/valbona/koman_ferry) is itself one of the great scenic journeys of Europe.
