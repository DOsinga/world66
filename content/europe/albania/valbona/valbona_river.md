The Valbona River runs the length of the valley, entering it as a clear mountain stream from the heights above and widening to a broad, shallow braid of channels over gravel by the time it reaches the village. The water is permanently cold — snowmelt fed year-round — and an intense turquoise-green where it runs over limestone bedrock. In summer, villagers and hikers swim in the pools beside the road.

The river corridor is the most accessible walking in the valley: a track follows the water for several kilometres from the village upstream, passing through riverside meadows and woodland, before narrowing into gorge terrain higher up. No significant climbing is involved. This walk — flat, shaded, and along running water — is an alternative for those not doing the full pass crossing and makes for an excellent two or three hours.
