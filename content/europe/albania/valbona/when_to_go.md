---
title: When to Go
type: section
---

**June to September** is the main season. Snow closes the Valbona Pass from November to May; in June the pass usually opens but can still have significant snow patches. July and August are the busiest months, particularly on the pass trail, which can have dozens of walkers on a fine weekend. Even in peak season the valley is quiet by Western European standards.

**September** is the best month: the crowds have thinned, the autumn colours on the beech trees are beginning, and the weather is generally stable. The air is clear enough that the views from the pass are at their sharpest.

**Spring and early summer** bring wildflowers across the alpine meadows and full rivers, but the pass may still be snow-covered in late May. Check conditions with guesthouses before attempting the crossing.

The valley is genuinely cold at night even in summer — temperatures can drop below 10°C after dark at the higher guesthouses. Bring a warm layer. The valley floor guesthouses are cooler than they appear.
