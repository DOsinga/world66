Haxhi Ali Cave is a sea cave in the cliffs near the Karaburun Peninsula, large enough to be a memorable boat-trip stop from Vlore. The appeal is the setting as much as the cave itself: clear water, high rock walls, and a sense of leaving the busy waterfront behind.
