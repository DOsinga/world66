Pashaliman Lagoon is a small tidal lagoon south of Vlore, connected to the sea by a narrow channel. Its value is mostly natural: ducks and wintering seabirds use the lagoon as part of their migration route through Albania.

It is not a polished sightseeing stop, but it is worth noting for travellers exploring the coast and wetlands around the Bay of Vlore, especially if birds and quiet landscapes are part of the plan.
