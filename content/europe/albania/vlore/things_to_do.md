Vlorë's historical significance — as the city where Albanian independence was declared in 1912 — gives it a weight beyond its modest size. The combination of history, a long promenade along the bay, the dramatic Kanina hilltop, and the start of the Albanian Riviera to the south makes it one of the more rewarding stops on Albania's Ionian coast.
