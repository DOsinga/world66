The Caldes Project is an outdoor public-art route along the river in the Escaldes-Engordany side of the Andorra la Vella urban area. Javier Balmaseda's bright taps, pipes, and bathtub-like forms play off the district's hot-spring history and the old stone footbridge nearby.

It works best as part of a walk rather than as a single destination. At night the installations are lit, which gives the riverside promenade a more playful feel.
