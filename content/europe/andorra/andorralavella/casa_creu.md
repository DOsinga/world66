Casa Creu is a small architectural stop in Andorra la Vella, useful for noticing the older domestic fabric that survives between shops, roads, and newer mountain-capital development. Its interest lies in detail and context rather than scale.

Fold it into a walk through the historic centre rather than treating it as a destination on its own. It works best when combined with nearby churches, public art, and the old lanes around the capital.
