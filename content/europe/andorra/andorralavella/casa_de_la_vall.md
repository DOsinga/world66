Casa de la Vall is Andorra la Vella's former parliament house, a 16th-century stone building in the old quarter. Guided visits explain how this small fortified house became the seat of the Consell General and a symbol of Andorra's political history.

Tours are short and usually need to be reserved ahead, so it works best as a planned stop while walking through the historic centre.
