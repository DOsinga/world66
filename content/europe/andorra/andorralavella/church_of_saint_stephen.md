The Church of Saint Stephen stands in Andorra la Vella's old quarter, with Romanesque origins and a 20th-century restoration by Modernista architect Josep Puig i Cadafalch. It is small, but the stonework and setting make it one of the easiest ways to see the capital before the shopping streets took over.
