The Church of Sant Andreu is a compact historic church close to the centre of Andorra la Vella. Its modest scale is part of the appeal: this is a mountain-valley parish church rather than a grand urban monument.

Look for it while walking between the commercial centre and the older streets. The church gives a quieter counterpoint to Andorra la Vella's shops and traffic, and it helps explain how much of the country's older identity is tied to small stone churches tucked into the Pyrenean landscape.
