The Escaldes-Engordany Art Centre is a compact cultural stop in a former cinema. Its displays mix sculpture by Josep Viladomat, models of Andorran Romanesque buildings, and temporary exhibitions, so it works best as a short indoor visit while exploring Escaldes.
