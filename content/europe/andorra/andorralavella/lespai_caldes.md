L'Espai Caldes interprets the hot springs that gave Escaldes-Engordany its name and helped shape Andorra's spa identity. It is a small cultural stop, but useful for understanding why thermal water became such a visible part of the urban landscape around Andorra la Vella.

Use it as context rather than a headline attraction. Afterward, the nearby bridges, public water features and Caldea complex read less like isolated landmarks and more like pieces of the same thermal-water story.
