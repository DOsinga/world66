Noblesse du Temps is the Dalí sculpture that has become one of Andorra la Vella's easiest landmarks: a melting-clock bronze set near the river and the commercial heart of town. It is a small stop, but a good one for orienting yourself in the centre.

The sculpture rewards a few minutes rather than a special trip. Use it as part of a walk through the old town and shopping streets, where Andorra's mix of mountain capital, tax-haven commerce, and public art is most visible.
