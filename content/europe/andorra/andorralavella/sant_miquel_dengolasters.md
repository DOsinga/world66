Sant Miquel d'Engolasters is a 12th-century Romanesque church above Andorra la Vella, known for the striking imbalance between its small nave and tall, slender bell tower. Visits usually need to be arranged ahead, but the church is one of the better close-up examples of Andorra's mountain Romanesque style.
