Sant Miquel d'Engolasters is a small Romanesque church from the 12th century, known for the striking contrast between its plain nave and tall bell tower.

The church sits above the central valley and is worth the short trip for Andorra's early medieval architecture. Guided visits may need to be reserved ahead.
