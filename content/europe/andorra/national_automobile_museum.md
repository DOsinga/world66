Museu Nacional de l'Automobil displays cars from the late 19th century through the 1970s, along with a large collection of bicycles. It is a compact but worthwhile stop for visitors interested in transport history.

The museum is in Encamp, making it a good rainy-day or in-between stop when moving through central Andorra rather than a full-day destination.
