Museu Casa d'Areny-Plandolit fills a former noble house with rooms and displays about domestic life in Andorra. The house belonged to one of the country's most influential families, and its interiors give Ordino's small museum cluster a useful social-history anchor.

It sits close to the postal museum, so both can be visited on the same short walk through the village.
