The Museu de la Miniatura is a small museum in Ordino devoted to miniature art and tiny crafted objects. The collection includes matryoshka dolls, decorated eggs, and works associated with the miniaturist Nicolai Siadristy.

It is a short visit, but a worthwhile one if you are already exploring Ordino's museums and old streets.
