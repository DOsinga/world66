Sant Climent de Pal is a 12th-century Romanesque church in the village of Pal, known for its Lombard-style bell tower. It is one of Andorra's better small churches, with the plain mountain setting doing half the work.

Visits may require advance booking or a guided opening, so check locally before driving up. Even from the outside, the proportions and stonework make it worth a stop if you are exploring La Massana parish.
