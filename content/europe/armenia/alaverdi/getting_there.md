---
title: Getting There
type: section
---

Alaverdi is about 170 km north of [Yerevan](/europe/armenia/yerevan) by road, a journey of roughly three hours by marshrutka (shared minivan). Marshrutkas depart from Yerevan's Kilikia bus terminal several times a day and go via Vanadzor before descending into the Debed Gorge. The overnight Yerevan–Tbilisi train also stops at Alaverdi, which is the most convenient option if you are arriving from [Tbilisi](/europe/georgia/tbilisi) — the Georgian capital is about 100 km north.

From Alaverdi, local taxis and occasional marshrutkas reach the nearby villages and monasteries. For Haghpat, a taxi is the easiest option; for Sanahin Monastery, it is a walkable climb from the Sanahin Bridge.
