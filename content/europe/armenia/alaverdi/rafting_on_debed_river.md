Rafting trips on the Debed River run through a mix of calm water and stronger sections in the gorge country around Alaverdi and Akhtala. Conditions are highest and most demanding in May, with easier beginner-friendly trips later as the river drops.

It is a good active break from monastery-hopping in northern Armenia, but check the season and operator details locally before planning around it.
