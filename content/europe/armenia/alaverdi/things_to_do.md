---
title: Things to Do
type: section
---

Alaverdi itself is an industrial town without much to see, but it is the indispensable base for visiting the Debed Gorge monasteries. [Sanahin Monastery](/europe/armenia/sanahin_1) and [Haghpat Monastery](/europe/armenia/haghpat), both UNESCO World Heritage Sites, are within easy reach — Sanahin is practically adjacent to the town, while Haghpat is a short drive or taxi ride away. The Sanahin Bridge, an 11th-century stone arch crossing the Debed, is right in town. The Mikoyan Brothers Museum is a quirky local attraction worth a quick visit.
