The Church of Saint Gregory of the Abughamrents is one of Ani's small masterpieces: a compact, many-sided church set near the western edge of the plateau. Its scale is modest beside Ani's cathedral and city walls, but the building's survival makes it one of the clearest places to study the early medieval Armenian architecture that made Ani famous.

Look for the way the church sits in relation to the ravine and the old inner city. The visit is short, but worthwhile, especially if you are trying to understand Ani as a collection of individual sacred buildings rather than a single ruined complex.
