The Church of the Holy Apostles is one of Ani's more evocative ruins, not because it is complete but because enough survives to show the sophistication of the medieval Armenian city. The gavit and carved stone fragments hint at a building that once stood among Ani's major religious monuments.

Visit it as part of a slow circuit through the archaeological site rather than as a standalone stop. The reward is in comparing its surviving masonry with Ani's larger churches, city walls, and empty plateau setting on the borderland.
