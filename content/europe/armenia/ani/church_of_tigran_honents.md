The Church of Tigran Honents stands near the edge of Ani, where the ruined Armenian capital drops toward the ravine. Its exterior is handsome enough, but the reason to seek it out is inside: unusually vivid medieval frescoes that give the abandoned city a human, devotional presence.

Ani can feel vast and wind-scoured, with monuments scattered across open ground. This church rewards close looking. Bring a torch or use your phone light carefully if the interior is dim, and take your time with the painted saints, donors, and fragments of narrative that survived the city's long abandonment.
