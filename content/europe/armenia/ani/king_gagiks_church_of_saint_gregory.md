King Gagik's Church of Saint Gregory, often called Gagikashen, is one of Ani's great lost ambitions. Built in the early 11th century as a conscious echo of Zvartnots Cathedral, it collapsed early enough that visitors now see foundations, fragments, and the ghost of a much grander plan.

The site is more archaeological than picturesque, but it adds an important layer to Ani. It shows the city's builders competing with Armenia's most celebrated sacred architecture, even when the result proved structurally fragile.
