Kizkale, the Maiden's Castle, is one of Ani's edge places: a ruin zone on a small peninsula-like spur where the medieval city breaks toward ravine and borderland. It is less immediately legible than the great churches, but it adds to Ani's feeling of a city spread across a whole defensive landscape.

Take it slowly and use it to widen your sense of the site. Ani is not just a checklist of Armenian churches; it is walls, ravines, lost quarters, and fragments of power. Kizkale is one of those fragments, best appreciated after the main monuments have given you the basic grammar of the place.
