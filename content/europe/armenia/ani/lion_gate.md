The Lion Gate is the usual way into Ani, and it gives the ruined city a proper threshold. The double line of walls, towers, and carved lion relief make the first few minutes feel ceremonial, even before the plateau opens out toward churches, ravines, and the border.

Do not hurry through it on the way to the cathedral. The gate explains Ani as a defended city, not just a scattered group of churches, and the masonry is a good introduction to the scale of the former capital. Look back after entering; the walls are often more impressive from inside the site.
