The Monastery of the Virgins is one of Ani's more atmospheric outlying ruins, set near the gorge rather than in the main cluster of churches. Its position gives it a different mood: quieter, more exposed, and closely tied to the river landscape that forms Ani's edge.

The ruin rewards travellers who have time to move beyond the headline churches. Paths and access can be rough, so keep an eye on footing and border-area restrictions, and do not underestimate the distance across the archaeological site.
