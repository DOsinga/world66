The Silk Road Bridge below Ani is mostly ruin now, but its position says almost everything. The broken piers stand in the gorge below the medieval city, marking the crossing that once connected Ani to trade routes beyond the Akhurian River.

You usually see it from above rather than visit it directly, and that distance is part of the effect. The bridge helps make sense of Ani as a borderland city of commerce and movement, not just a lonely archaeological site. Look for it when walking the edge of the plateau near the mosque and ravine viewpoints.
