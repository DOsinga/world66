This small house museum is dedicated to Perch Proshyan, the 19th-century Armenian writer who was born here. It is a modest stop, but it adds a literary thread to Ashtarak's cluster of churches and old lanes.

Look for the playful milepost outside, pointing toward famous museums around the world.
