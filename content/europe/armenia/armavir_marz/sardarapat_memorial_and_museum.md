Sardarapat Memorial marks the 1918 battle in which Armenian forces stopped a Turkish invasion and, in Armenian memory, saved the nation from annihilation. The bell tower and winged bulls give the site real force, while the adjacent ethnographic museum is one of the best places in the country to understand Armenian rural life, craft, and identity.
