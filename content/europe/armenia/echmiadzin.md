Echmiadzin — officially Vagharshapat, the country's fourth-largest city — is the spiritual capital of Armenia. The seat of the Supreme Catholicos sits here, and the [Mother See of Holy Etchmiadzin](/europe/armenia/echmiadzin/etchmiadzin_cathedral) has been the headquarters of the Armenian Apostolic Church for more than seventeen centuries.

The main draw is the trio of seventh-century churches included on the UNESCO World Heritage list: the [Etchmiadzin Cathedral](/europe/armenia/echmiadzin/etchmiadzin_cathedral) itself, claimed as the oldest cathedral in the world; the [Saint Hripsime Church](/europe/armenia/echmiadzin/saint_hripsime_church), an architectural masterpiece on the eastern edge of town; and the [Saint Gayane Church](/europe/armenia/echmiadzin/saint_gayane_church), quieter and equally elegant. The cathedral treasury contains an extraordinary collection of relics — including a fragment said to be from Noah's Ark and the Holy Lance.

Echmiadzin is just 30 kilometres from [Yerevan](/europe/armenia/yerevan) and easy to see on a day trip, combined with the [Zvartnots Cathedral ruins](/europe/armenia/echmiadzin/zvartnots_cathedral) nearby. Minibuses leave throughout the day; a taxi from Yerevan is cheap. Allow at least half a day to do the churches justice.
