The Alex and Marie Manoogian Treasury House is a museum within the Mother See of Holy Etchmiadzin. Built in 1982, it displays treasures of the Armenian Apostolic Church, including liturgical objects and works connected to the long history of the cathedral complex.

It is best visited together with Etchmiadzin Cathedral and the other churches nearby, since the collection gives context to the religious centre around it.
