---
title: "Getting There"
type: section
---

Echmiadzin is only twenty minutes by road from Yerevan, which makes it one of the easiest day trips in Armenia.

**Marshrutka** minibuses leave from the Kilikia bus station in Yerevan throughout the day, costing a few hundred dram. A **taxi** from the Yerevan centre is straightforward and inexpensive — agree the fare in advance or use a ride-hailing app like Yandex Go. Many visitors combine Echmiadzin with the ruins of [Zvartnots Cathedral](/europe/armenia/echmiadzin/zvartnots_cathedral), which sit on the same road about four kilometres east of town.

The closest airport, Zvartnots International, is even nearer than Yerevan — handy if you are arriving in the country and want to see the religious capital before pushing on.
