---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

Echmiadzin sits on the open Ararat plain, which makes for very hot summers and cold winters. **April to June** and **September to October** are the easiest months: clear skies, comfortable temperatures, and Mount Ararat usually visible from Zvartnots.

Religious holidays are the most atmospheric (and busiest) times. **Easter** is the great festival of the Armenian Apostolic Church, with all-night services in the cathedral. The **Feast of Vardavar** in July — a Christianised pagan festival — is celebrated with water-throwing in the streets; pilgrims of all ages get drenched. Major ordinations and the consecration of the holy myron (chrism), every seven years, draw enormous crowds.
