The 11th-century footbridge below Garni is a small but evocative medieval structure, often overshadowed by the famous Garni Temple and the basalt gorge. Its appeal is the way it connects the village's monumental sights with older routes through the landscape.

It is worth including if you walk or drive down toward the gorge. The bridge is a short stop, but it adds texture to a Garni visit that might otherwise focus only on the temple platform.
