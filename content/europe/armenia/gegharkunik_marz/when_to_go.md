---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

The lake is at its most beautiful in late spring and early summer when the surrounding mountains are still snow-capped and the meadows are green. July and August are the peak summer months when Armenian families fill the beaches and resort areas around Sevan town — busy and festive, but accommodation fills quickly. Autumn brings golden colours to the hillsides and fewer crowds, and the light over the lake in September and October is superb for photography.

Winter is cold and the higher passes can be snowbound, but the lake itself rarely freezes completely, and the monasteries are accessible year-round. The road from Yerevan to Sevan is ploughed and passable in winter, though the south road around the lake toward Vardenis can become difficult.
