Haghpat pairs naturally with [Sanahin](/europe/armenia/sanahin_1), the sister monastery directly across the Debed canyon. The two were inscribed together on the UNESCO list in 1996 and you can comfortably visit both in a single day from [Alaverdi](/europe/armenia/alaverdi), which is the nearest town with food, transport, and lodging.
