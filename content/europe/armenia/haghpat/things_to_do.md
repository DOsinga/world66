---
title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Haghpat is essentially a one-monastery village. The 10th-century monastery is the reason anyone comes, but a couple of minor sights — the fortress ruins of Kayan Berd, an old khachkar or two, and the views across the Debed canyon to neighbouring [Sanahin](/europe/armenia/sanahin_1) — make it worth lingering for half a day rather than rushing through.
