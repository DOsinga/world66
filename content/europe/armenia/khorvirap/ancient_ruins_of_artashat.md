The ancient ruins of Artashat lie near Khor Virap, spread across roughly the same area as the monastery's walled complex. The visible remains are mostly foundations, with traces of dwellings, other structures, and a bathhouse mosaic.

The site is not polished or heavily interpreted, but it adds real depth to a visit to Khor Virap, connecting the monastery landscape to one of Armenia's old capitals.
