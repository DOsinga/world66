---
title: Things to Do
type: section
---

Hrazdan itself has few tourist attractions — it is an industrial Soviet-era town recovering slowly from economic collapse. But the surrounding area, including the ski resort of Tsaghkadzor just 12 km away, makes the town a practical base with good accommodation options when Tsaghkadzor is full.

The Makravank Monastery, hidden in a ravine within the town limits, is worth finding if you have time. The Kecharis Monastery in Tsaghkadzor is the area's main historical site and easy to reach by taxi.
