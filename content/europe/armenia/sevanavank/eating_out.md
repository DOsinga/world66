---
title: "Eating Out"
type: section
---

Restaurants and shashlik spots line the lake shore near the monastery and along the Sevan highway — this is summer resort territory, and the eating scene reflects that: casual, outdoor, meat-heavy, and mostly shut from October to May. The one thing worth ordering above everything else is ishkhan — Sevan trout, a freshwater species endemic to the lake. It is the local specialty and genuinely good.
