A cooking class at Chalet Gyumri is a useful way to meet the city through food rather than only through churches, museums, and earthquake memory. Dishes such as chanakh and pokhind put local ingredients and household traditions at the centre of the experience.

This is a booked activity, so confirm language, timing, and menu before you count on it. It works best for travellers who want a social, hands-on break and are comfortable letting lunch or dinner become the main event.
