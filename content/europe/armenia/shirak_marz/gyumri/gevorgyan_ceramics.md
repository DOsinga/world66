Gevorgyan Ceramics is a small working studio on the edge of Gyumri where the appeal is the process as much as the finished pieces. Visitors come for wheel work, glazing, and the chance to see how local ceramic motifs are adapted into bowls, tiles, and decorative objects.

It is best treated as a pre-arranged workshop rather than a drop-in sight. For travellers interested in Armenian craft, it adds a useful hands-on counterpoint to Gyumri's architecture and house museums, and it can be combined with the city's art galleries on a slow cultural day.
