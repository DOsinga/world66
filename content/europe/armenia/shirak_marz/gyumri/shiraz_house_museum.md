Hovhannes Shiraz (1914–1984) is considered one of the great Armenian lyric poets of the 20th century, and Gyumri was his city. The house-museum occupies the home where he lived and worked for much of his life, in one of the old tufa-walled streets of the Kumayri historic district. The rooms have been preserved as they were, with his manuscripts, books, and personal effects on display.

For visitors who cannot read Armenian, the poetry itself is inaccessible — but the museum gives a vivid picture of what literary life looked like in a Soviet provincial city, and the building itself is a good example of the old Gyumri domestic architecture that survived the earthquake.
