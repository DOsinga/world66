Harichavank Monastery is a large medieval Armenian monastery complex near the village of Harich. The fortified compound overlooks a river, and one of its most memorable sights is a tiny chapel stranded on a finger of rock that split away after an earthquake.

Inside, narrow stairways lead to small prayer rooms and stone ceilings with varied patterns. The road in can be rough, but the monastery rewards the detour for travellers crossing Shirak between Gyumri and the Yerevan road.
