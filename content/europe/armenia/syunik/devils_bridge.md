Devil's Bridge is a natural stone bridge at the bottom of the Vorotan Gorge, on the road between Halidzor and Tatev. The river has carved a narrow, dramatic crossing here, with mineral springs and small pools tucked into the rock around it.

It is a good stop on the way to Tatev Monastery or the Wings of Tatev cable car, especially if you want to feel the depth of the gorge rather than just see it from above. The paths down can be rough and slippery, so this is better treated as a short nature scramble than a roadside photo stop.
