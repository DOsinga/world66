Harsnadzor Watchtower overlooks the Vorotan Gorge near Tatev. It was part of a warning chain in the Zangezur Mountains, where alarms could be relayed toward Tatev Monastery, and the gorge setting makes the ruined watchtower a scenic historical stop.
