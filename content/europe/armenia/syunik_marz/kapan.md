*Europe > Armenia > Syunik Marz > Kapan*

Kapan is a working southern Armenian city wedged into a narrow valley below Mount Khustup. It is not polished, and the mining industry is part of the view, but the setting is dramatic: steep green and brown slopes rise almost from the streets, with high Syunik history scattered in the hills around town.

The best reason to use Kapan as a base is the combination of mountain and medieval stone. [Mount Khustup](/europe/armenia/syunik_marz/kapan/mount_khustup) dominates the skyline and draws serious hikers, while [Vahanavank](/europe/armenia/syunik_marz/kapan/vahanavank) gives a quieter monastery visit in the forest above the Voghji valley.

Kapan also sits close to old fortress country. [Baghaberd](/europe/armenia/syunik_marz/kapan/baghaberd) is rougher to reach than the famous monastery stops farther north, but that is part of its pull. This is a good page for travelers already committed to Syunik rather than a simple day trip from Yerevan.
