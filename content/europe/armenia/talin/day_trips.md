Talin makes a natural half-day stop on the Aragatsotn circuit west of Yerevan, and the surrounding plain has a couple of worthwhile detours once you've seen the cathedral.

**Dashtadem Fortress** — Five kilometres south of town, the [Dashtadem Fortress](/europe/armenia/talin/dashtadem_fortress) is the obvious add-on: a layered medieval fortification with a 10th-century Arab-period core wrapped in early 19th-century Persian walls, sitting unfenced on the plain with views across to Mount Aragats. There's a 7th-century church in the village as well, making it an easy and rewarding half-hour stop on the way to or from Talin.
