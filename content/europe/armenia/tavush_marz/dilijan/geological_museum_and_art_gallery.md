The Geological Museum and Art Gallery of Dilijan covers the natural and cultural history of the Dilijan region. Its displays include geological specimens, local history material, archaeological finds, and artworks connected with the area.

The museum is most useful as a compact introduction to Dilijan beyond its forest setting and monastery day trips.
