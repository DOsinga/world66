The Museum of Folk Art after V. Sharambeyan is one of Dilijan's best cultural stops, especially if the town's forested setting has you thinking only about walks and monasteries. The museum brings attention back to Armenian craft: carving, textiles, ceramics, and objects tied to domestic life.

Its scale suits Dilijan. You do not need hours, but the visit gives texture to the town and makes a good companion to the preserved old street nearby.
