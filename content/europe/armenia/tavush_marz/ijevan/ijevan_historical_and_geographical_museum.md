The Ijevan Historical and Geographical Museum is a modest regional museum, but it helps make sense of Tavush before you start chasing monasteries through the forests. Its collections cover archaeology, ethnography, nature, and modern local history.

This is not a destination museum. It is the kind of place to visit in the morning, especially if weather is poor or you want a grounded introduction to the region's villages, crafts, and older settlement patterns.
