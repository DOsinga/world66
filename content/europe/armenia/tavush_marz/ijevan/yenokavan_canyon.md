Yenokavan Canyon is a wooded gorge north of Ijevan, known for cliffs, caves, streams, and hiking routes around the village of Yenokavan. It is close to Lastiver Caves and works best as a guided outdoor day trip, with trails leading through forested ravines and past rock shelters.
