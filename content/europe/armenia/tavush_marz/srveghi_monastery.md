Srveghi Monastery is a small red-brick monastic site in the wooded hills of Tavush. It is not as polished as Armenia's famous church complexes, but that is part of the appeal: the setting is quiet, rural, and best suited to travellers already exploring the Ijevan and Berd area by car.
