Tsaghkadzor (also spelled Tzakhadzor) is Armenia's main ski resort, located about 60km northeast of [Yerevan](/europe/armenia/yerevan) in the Kotayk highlands. The name means "valley of flowers" in Armenian — apt for a place that is buried in snow in winter and carpeted in wildflowers by June.

The [ski resort](/europe/armenia/tzakhadzor/tsaghkadzor_ski_resort) climbs Teghenis Mountain from around 1,950m to the 2,819m summit, with a gondola that runs year-round. In winter it pulls in skiers and snowboarders from Yerevan on day trips and weekends; in summer the same gondola delivers hikers and mountain-bikers to the upper slopes, with views across to Lake Sevan on a clear day. The [Tsaghkadzor Olympic Sports Complex](/europe/armenia/tzakhadzor/olympic_sports_complex) on the lower slopes is a reminder that this mountain has been serious training territory since Soviet times.

The medieval [Kecharis Monastery](/europe/armenia/tzakhadzor/kecharis_monastery), a 11th–13th century complex of tufa-stone churches and a gavit, sits at the edge of town and is worth an hour whatever the season. The [Writers' Union Resort](/europe/armenia/tzakhadzor/writers_union_resort), a striking piece of Soviet modernist architecture above the town, offers an unexpected counterpoint: a creative retreat for Soviet-era artists now operating quietly as a hotel, its interiors largely unchanged.

Buses and marshrutkas from Yerevan run regularly and the journey takes about 1.5 hours. Many guesthouses and small hotels operate year-round, with prices rising steeply during the ski season.
