---
title: Bars and Cafes
type: section
---

Apres-ski in Tsaghkadzor is low-key compared to Alpine resorts. The main street has a handful of cafes that serve Armenian coffee, brandy, and mulled wine to skiers coming off the slopes. In the evenings, the larger resort hotels have their own bars which are open to non-residents. The nightlife scene is minimal — Tsaghkadzor is principally a place to sleep well and get back on the mountain, not a party destination.
