---
title: Getting There
type: section
---

Tsaghkadzor is about 60km northeast of [Yerevan](/europe/armenia/yerevan) by road. Marshrutkas (shared minibuses) run from Yerevan's Kilikia bus station (sometimes called Avtokayan) and take around 1.5 hours. Services are more frequent in the ski season and on weekends; they can be infrequent mid-week in summer.

A taxi from Yerevan to Tsaghkadzor typically costs 4,000–6,000 AMD each way and is comfortable for groups of three or four. Several Yerevan-based tour operators run winter ski transfers on weekends. Driving from Yerevan takes about an hour on the M4 highway towards [Hrazdan](/europe/armenia/kotayk_marz), then a short turn-off into the resort.
