Tsaghkadzor City Park is a simple mountain park with a maintained switchback path above town. It is not a headline sight, but it gives you an easy walk when you want fresh air without committing to the ski slopes or a longer hike.

The path climbs toward Panorama Restaurant and is kept usable in winter, which makes it a practical leg-stretcher between monastery visits, skiing, or the ride up the gondola.
