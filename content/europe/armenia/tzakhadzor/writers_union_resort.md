The Writers' Union Resort (Grakanneri Miutyun) occupies a commanding position on the hillside above Tsaghkadzor and remains one of the most architecturally striking buildings in the town. Built during the Soviet era as a sanatorium and creative retreat for Armenian and Soviet writers, it exemplifies the ambitious modernist architecture that flourished in Soviet Armenia between the 1960s and 1980s — all cantilevered concrete, bold geometric forms, and mountain-scale ambition.

The complex is still in operation as a hotel, and even non-guests can walk the grounds and admire the exteriors. The interior public areas retain much of their Soviet-era character: broad corridors, high ceilings, and the particular melancholy of a once-grand institution now quietly aging. The dining room serves Armenian food at moderate prices and is open to outside visitors, which makes for an atmospheric lunch stop combined with a tour of the building. The views across the Tsaghkadzor valley from the upper terraces are excellent at any time of year.
