Vanadzor's reason to exist as a traveller's base is the country around it. Within an hour or two by road you can reach the UNESCO-listed monasteries of [Haghpat](/europe/armenia/haghpat) and [Sanahin](/europe/armenia/sanahin_1) above the Debed Canyon, the great basilica of [Odzun](/europe/armenia/odzun) on its plateau, and the copper-mining town of [Alaverdi](/europe/armenia/alaverdi) at the bottom of the gorge. South into Tavush province, the wooded valleys and resort town of [Dilijan](/europe/armenia/tavush_marz/dilijan) are a contrast in landscape and pace.

If you have a car, the village of Dsegh — Hovhannes Tumanyan's birthplace, with a small house-museum — and the spa town of Stepanavan with its arboretum are both worth half a day each.
