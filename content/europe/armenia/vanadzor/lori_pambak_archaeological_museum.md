The Lori-Pambak Archaeological Museum gives Vanadzor a compact but useful introduction to the wider Lori region. Its collections are strongest as context: prehistoric finds, regional history, and material from the valleys and uplands that most travelers only glimpse from the road.

It is not a polished blockbuster museum, and that is part of its appeal. Visit before heading to Lori's monasteries and gorges, and the landscape starts to feel less like scenery and more like a lived-in corridor with layers of settlement, trade, conflict, and craft behind it.
