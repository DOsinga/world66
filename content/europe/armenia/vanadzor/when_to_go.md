---
title: When to Go
type: section
---

Vanadzor sits at about 1,400 metres in a mountain valley, so it is noticeably cooler than Yerevan in every season. Summers (June to early September) are mild and ideal for hiking — warm days, cool evenings, and far less of the heavy heat that settles on the lowlands. This is the best window for day trips into the Debed Canyon and Tavush forests.

Autumn is short but spectacular, with the surrounding beech and oak forests turning gold and rust through October. Winters are long, snowy, and quiet — the city itself does not shut down, but rural roads can become difficult and many monastery tracks are impassable. Spring is wet and muddy until the second half of May, when the wildflowers come out and the rivers run high.
