A cable car crosses the gorge above the Arpa River, giving views down into the waterfall gorge and across to the forested slopes opposite. The ride itself is short — a few hundred metres — but the perspective is good, and it's a pleasant way to get between the two sides of the gorge without descending and ascending on foot. The car runs in season (spring through autumn); in winter the gorge is often closed due to ice and snow.
