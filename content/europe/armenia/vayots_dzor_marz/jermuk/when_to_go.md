---
title: When to Go
type: section
---

Jermuk at 2,100 metres has a true mountain climate. Summer (June–August) is the main season — days are warm and sunny, the mountain meadows are in bloom, and the spa facilities and cable car are fully operational. The alpine scenery is at its best then.

Spring and autumn are pleasant but can be rainy and cold. Winter brings snow (good if you want the mountain atmosphere; difficult if you are relying on the road from Yeghegnadzor, which can close in heavy snow). The Jermuk ski resort is small and operates when conditions allow.

The town is popular with Armenians for summer holidays and health treatments; foreign visitors are few but welcome.
