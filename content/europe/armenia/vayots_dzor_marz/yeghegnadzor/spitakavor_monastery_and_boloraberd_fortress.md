Spitakavor Monastery and Boloraberd Fortress make one of the stronger active day trips from Yeghegnadzor. The monastery sits in a high, broken landscape north of Vernashen, built of pale stone that gives Spitakavor its name, while the ruined fortress rises nearby on a sharper defensive perch.

The reward is the combination of architecture, mountain air, and isolation rather than a heavily restored monument. Roads can be rough and the fortress section is best treated as a hike, so go with proper footwear, water, and a realistic plan for transport back down.
