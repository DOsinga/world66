A small museum in the former home of the painter Lusik Aguletsi (1946–2018), known for her devotion to traditional Armenian dress, which she wore daily for decades and revived as a contemporary art form. The house is full of her paintings, her costumes, the carved wooden chests she collected, and the carpets and metalwork she gathered from across the Armenian highland.

Her family still runs the museum and often guides visitors through in person. It is small, slightly homemade, and very personal — one of the more unexpected experiences in central Yerevan.
