The Armenian Railways Museum is a small specialist museum by Yerevan's main railway station, focused on the development of rail transport in Armenia and the South Caucasus. Models, photographs, documents, uniforms, and railway equipment tell a story most city itineraries skip.

It is best for transport enthusiasts or travellers already passing through Sasuntsi David station. The museum is modest, but it adds context to a network that connected Yerevan with Tbilisi, Gyumri, and the wider Soviet rail system.
