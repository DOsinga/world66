The Cascade Memorial to the Victims of Soviet Repression stands above the Yerevan Cascade, near the obelisk marking 50 years of Soviet Armenia. Designed by architect Jim Torosyan, it remembers those killed, imprisoned, or deported during Soviet-era repression.

It is easy to miss beside the better-known Cascade and Cafesjian Center, but it adds a sober layer to a walk through one of Yerevan's main public spaces.
