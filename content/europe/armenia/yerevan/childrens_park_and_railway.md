Children's Park is a Soviet-era park in the Hrazdan River gorge, best known for its children's railway. Much of the infrastructure is worn, but that faded atmosphere is part of the appeal, especially if you are interested in Yerevan's Soviet urban landscape.

The park is reached from the centre through the Kond Pedestrian Tunnel and works as a short family stop or an unusual walk below the city.
