The Eduard Isabekyan Gallery is a small art museum dedicated to the Armenian painter Eduard Isabekyan. The collection gives a focused look at one artist rather than trying to tell the whole story of Armenian art.

The round Brutalist building is part of the appeal. It is a worthwhile short stop if you are already walking between central Yerevan's museums and churches.
