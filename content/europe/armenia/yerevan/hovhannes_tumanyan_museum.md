Hovhannes Tumanyan Museum is a Yerevan house-museum devoted to one of Armenia's best-loved writers. Manuscripts, personal rooms, editions, and biographical displays make the museum a literary counterpart to the city's larger national collections.

It is most rewarding if you have some interest in Armenian literature or want to understand cultural figures beyond the genocide and medieval-manuscript narratives. The visit is focused and manageable.
