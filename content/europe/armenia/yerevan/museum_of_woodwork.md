The Museum of Woodwork is a small Yerevan museum focused on Armenian wood carving. Its collection includes carved doors, furniture, decorative pieces, and modern wood-based sculpture.

It is best treated as a short specialist stop, especially for visitors interested in craft traditions and applied art.
