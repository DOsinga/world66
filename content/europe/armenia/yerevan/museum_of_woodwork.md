The Museum of Woodwork is a focused stop for Armenian craft, with carved doors, furniture, decorative objects, and contemporary wooden sculpture. It is not one of Yerevan's headline museums, but it adds a tactile, handmade angle to the city's art scene.

Go if you like traditional materials and quiet collections more than blockbuster displays.
