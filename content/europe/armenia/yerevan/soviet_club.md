Soviet Club is part small museum, part themed restaurant, with Soviet-era cars, appliances, posters, and arcade games gathered into one nostalgic display. It works best as a light, hands-on stop rather than a formal museum: you can look through the objects, play some of the old games, and get a feel for late Soviet domestic culture.
