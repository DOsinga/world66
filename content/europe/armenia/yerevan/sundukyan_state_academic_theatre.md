Sundukyan State Academic Theatre is Armenia's leading drama theatre and one of the best places in Yerevan to catch a serious stage production. The programme is mostly Armenian-language, but the building and the audience are part of the experience: formal, local, and far from the usual tourist circuit.

It is worth checking the schedule if you are interested in Armenian culture beyond churches and museums. Even without perfect language skills, a night here gives you a glimpse of the country's modern theatrical tradition.
