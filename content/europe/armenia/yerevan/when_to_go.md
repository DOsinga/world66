---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

Yerevan has a sharply continental climate: hot, dry summers and cold, dry winters, with very little rain in any season. **May, June, September, and early October** are the best months — warm but not punishing, with clear skies and Mount Ararat usually visible from the city. In **July and August** the daytime temperature regularly tops 35°C and the centre can feel exhausting in the middle of the day; locals flee to Lake Sevan or the cooler highlands.

Winter is cold (down to -15°C in January) and the Caucasus light is short. **December's** Christmas market on Republic Square and the New Year's celebrations are atmospheric but expect snow and slush.

Worth timing a trip around: the **Yerevan Wine Days** in early June, the **Golden Apricot International Film Festival** in mid-July, and the **HIGH FEST** performing-arts festival in October.
