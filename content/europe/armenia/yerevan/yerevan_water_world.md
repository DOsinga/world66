Yerevan Water World is a family-oriented water park on the north side of the city. In summer it has several outdoor pools as well as smaller indoor pools, making it a popular local place to cool off.

The largest pool is used as an ice-skating rink in winter. For visitors, it is most useful as an easy family stop rather than a cultural sight.
