---
title: "Getting There"
type: section
---

St. Anton is on the main Vienna-Innsbruck-Zürich railway line, with direct ICE and Railjet trains from Munich, Vienna, Zürich, and Innsbruck. The journey from Zürich airport takes about two and a half hours and is the most popular access from outside Austria; ski boots and bags travel free on Austrian trains.

By air, the nearest international airports are **Innsbruck** (90 minutes by car or train), **Zürich** (2.5 hours), and **Munich** (3 hours). Resort transfer buses run from all three.

Lech, Zürs, Stuben, and St. Christoph have no train station; the resort village of Langen am Arlberg (one stop west of St. Anton) is the train station for Lech, with a frequent bus connecting up the Flexen road. In winter the road over the Arlberg pass is occasionally closed for avalanche control — the Arlberg rail tunnel underneath stays open.
