  St. Christoph began in 1386 when a shepherd called Heinrich Findelkind founded a hospice on the Arlberg pass to shelter travellers caught in winter storms. The Brotherhood of St. Christoph that grew up around it is one of the oldest charitable institutions in Europe and is still active today; the modern five-star Arlberg-Hospiz Hotel stands on the same spot and incorporates parts of the original building.
---

St. Christoph sits literally on top of the Arlberg pass at 1,800 metres, between St. Anton below and Stuben on the Vorarlberg side. There is little here other than the historic Arlberg-Hospiz Hotel and a handful of other lodgings — the Bundessport- und Freizeitzentrum (the Austrian national ski training centre), and the famous Arlberg ski school, founded by Hannes Schneider in 1921 and still considered one of the best in the world.

The pistes around St. Christoph are wide and high — sunny in the morning, with lift access to the Galzig and from there to the entire Arlberg network. Good for serious skiers who want to be at altitude from the moment they step out of their hotel.
