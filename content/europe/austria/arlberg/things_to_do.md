title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

In winter, the Arlberg is essentially one enormous ski area shared between five resort towns — see [Ski Arlberg](/europe/austria/arlberg/ski_arlberg). In summer, the same terrain opens for hiking, climbing, paragliding, and mountain biking. Several of the resort villages have small museums and historic chapels worth a stop on a non-skiing day.
