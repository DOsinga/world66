The Valluga is the highest peak in the Arlberg ski area at 2,811 metres, reached by a sequence of three lifts from St. Anton: the Galzigbahn to Galzig, the Vallugabahn to the Vallugagrat, and a final tiny cable car to the summit. It is worth doing on a clear day even if you are not skiing — the summit view across the eastern Alps is one of the best in Austria.

The off-piste descent from the Vallugagrat into the Pazieltal (the "Schindlerkar") is one of the most famous in the Alps — long, steep, and demanding. The piste back to St. Anton is a wide, sweeping red that suits good intermediate skiers.
