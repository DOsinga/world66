---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

The Arlberg's reason for being is winter. The ski season runs reliably from **early December** (with most lifts opening 1 December and the resorts in full swing from mid-month) to **late April** for the lower lifts and **early May** for the high terrain around Lech and the Vallugagrat. Snow record is among the best in the Alps thanks to altitude and exposure.

The busiest and most expensive weeks are **Christmas/New Year** and **mid-February to early March**. Best balance of conditions and price is the second half of January or the last two weeks of March — long days, soft snow, and noticeable price drops on accommodation.

**Summer** (mid-June to mid-September) is for hiking and mountain biking, with most lifts running on a reduced timetable. The villages are much quieter and many hotels close in May, October, and November.
