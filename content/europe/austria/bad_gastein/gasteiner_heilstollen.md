The **Gasteiner Heilstollen** is the radon therapy mine at Böckstein, 5 km up the valley from Bad Gastein. Visitors are taken by small train deep into the mountain, where the combination of warm temperatures (around 41°C), high humidity, and mildly radioactive radon gas is used to treat rheumatic and respiratory conditions.

This is not a tourist gimmick — the therapy has a long medical history and is taken seriously by Austrian doctors. The experience involves sitting in the warm, slightly steamy darkness for 40 minutes or so, which many people find deeply relaxing regardless of the medical claims. For those curious about the science or simply looking for an unusual experience, the Heilstollen is genuinely memorable. Sessions should be booked in advance, especially in high season.
