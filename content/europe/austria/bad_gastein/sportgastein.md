Sportgastein is the high, open end of the Gastein valley, where Bad Gastein's spa-town drama gives way to broad Alpine scenery. In winter it is the area's snow-sure skiing sector; in summer the same valley turns into a hiking base with marmot country, waterfalls, and long views into the Hohe Tauern.

It feels wilder and more spacious than the resort centre below. Come prepared for mountain weather, even if the valley floor is warm, and check lift or road access before setting out. For travellers staying in Bad Gastein, Sportgastein is the easiest way to trade Belle Epoque buildings for big, clean Alpine air.
