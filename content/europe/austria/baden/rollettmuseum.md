The Rollettmuseum is Baden's local history museum, set in the former city hall. Its collection moves from prehistoric and Roman material through the medieval town to Baden's 19th-century heyday as a spa resort for the imperial family, artists, and politicians.

It is a good companion to the Beethoven House and the Kurpark. Visit if you want the background story behind Baden's elegant streets rather than just the surface charm of the spa town.
