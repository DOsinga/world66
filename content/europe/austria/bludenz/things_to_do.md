---
title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Bludenz rewards slow exploration. The old town is compact enough to cover on foot in a morning, and the surrounding valleys open up into some of the quietest alpine terrain in Austria. The Großes Walsertal to the north, a UNESCO biosphere reserve, is the standout — genuinely uncrowded, with walking trails that pass through traditional farmsteads and flower-covered meadows. The Brandnertal to the south leads toward the Lüner See, a high-altitude lake worth the effort. Beyond sightseeing, the town's position at the meeting of five valleys means you can pick a different direction each day.
