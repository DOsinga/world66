Walderlebnispfad Marul is a gentle forest experience trail in the Grosses Walsertal area, reachable as an outing from Bludenz. The route is designed to make woodland, streams, and mountain ecology approachable, with stations and short sections that suit families rather than serious hikers. It is a good option when you want the alpine setting without a hard climb.

The trail is best in decent weather, when the forest is dry enough for children to enjoy and the views open between the trees. It belongs in a Bludenz itinerary as a soft outdoor half day, not as the region's main mountain adventure. For that, look higher into the valleys and cable-car routes.
