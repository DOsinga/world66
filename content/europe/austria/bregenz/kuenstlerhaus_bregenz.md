Künstlerhaus Bregenz is a small exhibition house for Vorarlberg artists, separate from the better-known Kunsthaus Bregenz. It is best for travellers who like seeing local contemporary work rather than only the big-name museum circuit.

The scale is modest, so check what is on before making a special trip. It fits easily with a walk through Bregenz's upper town.
