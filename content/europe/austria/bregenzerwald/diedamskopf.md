Diedamskopf is a mountain above the Bregenzerwald with a cable car, hiking trails, skiing in winter, and wide views toward Lake Constance and the surrounding Alps. The summit area has a restaurant and an interactive mountain exhibition, so it can work for both hikers and families.

In summer, choose from easy ridge walks or longer trails; in clear weather the viewpoint is the main reason to go.
