---
title: Things to Do
type: section
---

The Bregenzerwald offers two very different experiences depending on season. In winter, the linked ski resorts of Damüls, Mellau, and Schoppernau provide quiet, snow-sure skiing above tree level. In summer, the valley is excellent walking country with well-marked trails across the alpine meadows and through forests to high viewpoints. The Kanisfluh massif dominates the southern skyline and is the valley's most dramatic landmark.

The valley is also known for its architecture: several contemporary timber buildings by Vorarlberg architects have given the region an unexpected reputation for design quality. The cheese-making tradition is strong — the Bregenzerwald is part of Vorarlberg's dairy culture, and the local mountain cheeses are worth seeking out.
