---
title: When to Go
type: section
---

The Bregenzerwald has two distinct seasons. **Winter (December to March)** is skiing season, centred on Damüls-Mellau; Damüls has one of the most reliable snow records in Austria. The valley is quiet in November and April — most facilities are closed.

**Summer (June to September)** is ideal for walking and cycling. The trails are open from late June, and the high meadows are at their best in July and August when the alpine flowers are out. The Käsestrasse (Cheese Road) is a summer tour of the valley's dairy farms, connecting the villages that produce the local mountain cheeses.

Spring and autumn bring changing light and fewer visitors. The forests are spectacular in October. Summer weekends can be busy; mid-week in July or August is a better time if you want the trails to yourself.
