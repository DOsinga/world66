Europabrucke, often nicknamed the Brucker Harfe, is the modern bow bridge over the Mur near the Mur and Murz confluence. It is not an old monument, but it makes a neat contemporary counterpoint to the medieval centre.

The bridge also gives a useful riverside walk beyond Hauptplatz. Use it to loop the town rather than treating Bruck as only a square and a hill.
