Schlossberg rises directly above Bruck's centre, making it the easiest way to understand the town's position at the meeting of valleys. The walk is short but gives a real change of perspective over the roofs, river, and surrounding hills.

At the top are the Uhrturm clock tower and the remains of Landskron Castle. The ruins are modest, but the view makes the climb worthwhile, especially late in the day.
