Church of St Cornelius is a small Romanesque church in Tosters, with roots in the 11th and 12th centuries. Its scale is modest, but the age of the building and its quiet setting make it a worthwhile detour from Feldkirch's old town.

The cemetery wall is also known for an ancient tree tied to local legends and once believed to have healing powers.
