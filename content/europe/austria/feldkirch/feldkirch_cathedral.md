Feldkirch Cathedral, dedicated to St Nicholas, is the main church of the town and the seat of the diocese. It stands close to the old centre, with a restrained exterior and a calm interior that contrasts with the busier shopping streets outside.

It is worth a short stop even if you are not collecting churches. Feldkirch's medieval scale makes the cathedral feel woven into the town rather than set apart as a monument.
