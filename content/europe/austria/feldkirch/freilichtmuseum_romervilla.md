Freilichtmuseum Römervilla is an open-air archaeological museum preserving the remains of a Roman estate. The ruins date from late antiquity and give a clear sense of rural Roman life in the Rhine valley.

The site is small, but it makes a worthwhile detour for visitors interested in the region's Roman history.
