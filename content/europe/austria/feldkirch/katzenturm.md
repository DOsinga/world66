Katzenturm is a defensive tower from 1507 that once formed part of Feldkirch's town fortifications. It was later converted into a bell tower, and its large bell remains one of the old town's landmarks. It pairs naturally with a walk through Marktgasse and the rest of Feldkirch's historic centre.
