Kunst.Vorarlberg is a small contemporary art venue in a former villa near Feldkirch's old centre. It shows work by regional artists, so the exhibitions give a good sense of the local art scene rather than another standard municipal collection.

Check the current programme before making a special trip. When it is open, it is an easy add-on to a walk through Feldkirch's arcaded streets and castle quarter.
