Tosters is a western district of Feldkirch close to the Liechtenstein border. It is mainly residential, but it gives a sense of how quickly the town spreads from medieval lanes into a cross-border Rhine valley landscape.

Come this way if you are continuing toward Liechtenstein or looking for a quieter walk beyond the old town. It is not a headline sight, but it rounds out Feldkirch's border-town character.
