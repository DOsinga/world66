Wildpark Feldkirch is a small wildlife park above town, focused on animals of the Alps and nearby forests. Expect a modest, family-friendly place rather than a full zoo, with deer, wild pigs, wolves, lynx, and other regional species.

It is worth considering if you have children with you or want an easy green break after Feldkirch's old centre and castle.
