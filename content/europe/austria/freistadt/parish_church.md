Katharinenmünster, Freistadt's parish church, anchors the main square with a broad Gothic interior and a later Baroque tower. The star vaulting in the choir is the detail to look for.

It is worth stepping inside because the church gives scale to the town. Freistadt may be small, but the building reflects the wealth of a medieval market centre.
