Eggenberg Palace is Graz's grand Baroque palace, known for its state rooms, formal symbolism, and broad parkland. The guided tour through the palace is the main reason to come, especially for the painted rooms and the sense of aristocratic theatre built into the architecture.

The surrounding park is pleasant on its own, but the palace interiors are what make the trip west of the centre worthwhile.
