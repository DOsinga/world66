Kesselfall is a short gorge walk north of Graz, where water has cut a tight passage through limestone and wooden walkways carry visitors through the narrowest sections. It is a compact but atmospheric outing, especially in warm weather when the gorge feels cool and enclosed.

The trip suits travellers with a car or those already exploring the countryside beyond Graz. It is best approached as a nature half-day rather than a city sight, with sturdy shoes and attention to seasonal opening conditions.
