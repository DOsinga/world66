A basement wine bar in the old town, with an emphasis on Styrian whites — Welschriesling, Sauvignon Blanc, and the local Schilcher rosé. The room is low-ceilinged and cosy in the way that Graz wine bars tend to be: rough stone walls, wooden tables, no design pretension. The staff know the wine list well enough to make a recommendation worth following.

Suitable for an early evening glass before dinner rather than a long night. Located near the Franziskanerplatz, a short walk from Hauptplatz.
