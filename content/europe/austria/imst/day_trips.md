---
title: "Day Trips"
type: section
---

Imst is one of the best bases in western Tyrol for day trips. The big ones: the **Pitztal** with its glacier and the **Wildspitze** (Austria's second-highest peak); the **Ötztal** with the *Aqua Dome* spa at Längenfeld and the Ötzi-the-Iceman site at the head of the valley; and the **Fernpass** road north to the Bavarian border, with the green-blue lakes of the **Plansee** and **Heiterwanger See** along the way.

For an easier outing, drive the half-hour up to **Kühtai** (2,000 m) for high-Alpine air and a short circular walk around the **Speicher Kühtai** reservoir.
