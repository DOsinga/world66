---
title: "Eating Out"
type: section
---

Tyrolean food is what to expect — *Tiroler Gröstl* (pan-fried potatoes with bacon and a fried egg on top), *Kasspatzln* (cheese spaetzle with crispy onions), *Wiener Schnitzel*, and the local *Knödel* (bread dumplings) in soup or with kraut. Imst is not Vienna; expect honest, generous portions rather than refined cooking.
