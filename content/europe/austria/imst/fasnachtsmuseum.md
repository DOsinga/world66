A small museum dedicated to the **Schemenlaufen**, Imst's quadrennial masked carnival, with rooms of hand-carved wooden masks, embroidered costumes, and old film footage of past processions. The exhibits explain who the various characters are — the bell-ringing Rollers and Schellers, the demonic Spritzer who chase children with squirts of water, the Sackner with their padded suits — and why the procession is only held once every four years.

If you can't time a visit to a Schemenlaufen year (the next is in 2028), this is the next best thing. It also doubles as a workshop where mask-carvers can sometimes be seen at work.
