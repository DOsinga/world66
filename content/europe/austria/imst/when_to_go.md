---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

**Summer (June to early October)** is the obvious time — the gorge walk is open, the gondola runs, and the surrounding valleys are perfect for hiking. July and August are warm and sunny (Imst sits in a notably dry pocket of the Alps) but rarely uncomfortably hot.

**Winter (December to mid-April)** brings reliable snow at Hoch-Imst and easy access to the bigger resorts in the Pitztal and Ötztal. The pre-Lenten **Schemenlaufen** carnival takes place every four years (next: 2028) on the Sunday before Ash Wednesday — an event worth planning a trip around if the year aligns.

Avoid **November and May** when the gondola is closed for service and most restaurants take their seasonal break.
