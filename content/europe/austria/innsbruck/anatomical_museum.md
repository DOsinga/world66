The Anatomical Museum in Innsbruck is a university museum with historic teaching collections, anatomical models, skeletons, skulls, and preserved specimens. It gives a direct look at the history of anatomy and medical education.

The collection includes real human remains, so it is not a casual family stop and may not suit younger children. For visitors interested in medicine or scientific history, it is one of the city's more unusual museums.
