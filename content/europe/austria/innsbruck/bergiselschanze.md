Bergiselschanze is Innsbruck's landmark ski-jump tower on Bergisel, rebuilt in 2001 to a design by Zaha Hadid. The tower combines Olympic history, bold modern architecture, and a high view back over the city and the Inn valley.

You can walk up or take the lift to the top outside sporting events. It pairs naturally with the nearby Tyrol Panorama Museum and the Wilten sights below the hill.
