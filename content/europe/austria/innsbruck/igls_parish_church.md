Igls Parish Church is a historic village church south of Innsbruck. The site was consecrated in 1286, then rebuilt in late Gothic form in the 15th century and again in Baroque style in 1705.

It is most worthwhile if you are already in Igls for the Patscherkofel area or staying outside central Innsbruck. The layered architecture gives the village centre a clear historical anchor.
