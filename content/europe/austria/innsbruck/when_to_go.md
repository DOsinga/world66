---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

Innsbruck has two clear peak seasons: winter (December to March) for skiing and the Christmas markets, and summer (June to September) for hiking, cycling, and outdoor dining. Both are worthwhile.

Winter is the more atmospheric: the Christmas market in the Altstadt is one of Austria's better ones, the Nordkette is snow-covered, and the city has a genuine Alpine-resort energy without the exclusivity of Kitzbühel or the ski villages. February and March are the best months for skiing — stable snow, long days.

Summer is warmer than visitors from the north expect — the valley traps heat and temperatures can reach 30°C in July. This makes the Nordkette a welcome refuge. Spring and autumn are quieter and cheaper, with the mountains still accessible for hiking.
