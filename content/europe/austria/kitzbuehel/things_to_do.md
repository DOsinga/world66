---
title: Things to Do
type: section
---

Kitzbühel is primarily a ski and outdoor resort, but the medieval town centre is worth exploring on its own terms. In winter, the ski area — the "Ski Circus" shared with neighbouring Kirchberg — covers over 220 km of runs serviced by the Hahnenkamm and Hornbahn cable cars. In summer, the same mountains offer hiking and mountain biking, and the Schwarzsee lake provides swimming.

The big draw is the [Hahnenkamm](/europe/austria/kitzbuehel/hahnenkamm) and its legendary [Streif downhill course](/europe/austria/kitzbuehel/streif_course) — in January, the World Cup race week turns the town into a spectacle. [Schwarzsee](/europe/austria/kitzbuehel/schwarzsee) is the swimming lake two kilometres west of town. The [Kitzbüheler Horn](/europe/austria/kitzbuehel/kitzbueheler_horn) opposite the Hahnenkamm is higher and quieter. In town, the painted facades, [St Catherine's Church](/europe/austria/kitzbuehel/st_catherines_church), and the [Museum Kitzbühel](/europe/austria/kitzbuehel/museum_kitzbuehel) fill a few hours.
