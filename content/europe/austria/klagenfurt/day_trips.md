---
title: "Day Trips"
type: section
---

The shores of the **Wörthersee** are a string of resorts within minutes of the centre by S-Bahn or bus: Pörtschach, Velden and the peninsula of Maria Wörth, all with their own beaches and cafes. **Hochosterwitz Castle**, twenty kilometres north-east, is the great picture-book fortress of Carinthia and easily a half-day visit.

Further afield, **Villach** and the dramatic mountains of the Karnische Region are forty minutes' drive west. The hilltop pilgrimage church of **Maria Saal** and the Roman excavations at **Magdalensberg** sit ten and twenty kilometres north respectively, on the broad Zollfeld plain. Ljubljana, Slovenia's capital, is just over an hour away — a satisfying cross-border day trip if you have the time.
