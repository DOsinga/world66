---

A surprising and entirely unexpected sight on the southern edge of Klagenfurt, the Eboardmuseum is a private collection of more than 1,500 electronic keyboards, from early Hammond organs and Wurlitzers to vintage synthesisers, drum machines, and 1980s rarities used by famous bands.

The owner gives the tour himself and is a delight if you have any interest in music; he is happy to demonstrate the instruments. Booking ahead is essential — the museum is run as a one-man show and is not open on a fixed schedule.
