---
title: "Getting There"
type: section
---

**By air.** Klagenfurt Airport, 4 km north-east of the centre, has a modest schedule of flights to Vienna and a couple of low-cost destinations in Germany and the UK. Bus 40 connects the airport to the central station. Most international travellers fly into Vienna or Ljubljana and connect by train.

**By train.** Klagenfurt's Hauptbahnhof is on the southern main line: direct ÖBB services run from Vienna (4 hours), Salzburg, Villach, Ljubljana and Venice. The Wörthersee resort towns are reachable by suburban S-Bahn.

**By road.** The A2 Süd Autobahn from Vienna and Graz, and the A10 Tauern Autobahn from Salzburg, both pass within a few kilometres of the city. From Italy or Slovenia, the road climbs over the Karawanken tunnel and is straightforward.
