This museum occupies the birthplace of Robert Musil, author of The Man Without Qualities, and uses that connection to explore Klagenfurt's literary world. It is a small, specialist stop, but a rewarding one if Austrian modernism means anything to you.

The displays also cover other Carinthian writers, making the museum more than a single-author shrine. Pair it with a walk through central Klagenfurt, where the city's orderly squares contrast nicely with Musil's restless intellectual universe.
