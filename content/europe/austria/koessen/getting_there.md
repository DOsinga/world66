---
title: "Getting There"
type: section
---

Kössen is in the northeast corner of Tyrol, close to the German border. The nearest large city is Rosenheim in Bavaria (about 40 km north). From [Innsbruck](/europe/austria/innsbruck) the drive takes about 90 minutes via the Brenner Autobahn and then east through the Inn Valley.

By public transport, take a train to [Kufstein](/europe/austria/kufstein) and then a bus (line 4042 or 4044) up the valley to Kössen. The journey from Kufstein takes about 40 minutes. From [Kitzbühel](/europe/austria/kitzbuehel) a bus connection runs via St. Johann in Tirol.

Reit im Winkl across the border in Germany is reachable by a short mountain road — follow signs for the Ursprungpass. Munich is about 90 minutes by car.
