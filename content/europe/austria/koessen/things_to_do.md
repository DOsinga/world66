---
title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Kössen is compact enough to explore on foot. Paragliding dominates the outdoor scene — the thermals above the Unterberghorn are among the most reliable in the Alps — but there is plenty here for those who prefer to stay grounded. The baroque parish church anchors the village centre, the Entenloch Gorge offers an easy half-day walk, and the Walchsee lake a few kilometres to the south is the go-to for swimming and cycling.
