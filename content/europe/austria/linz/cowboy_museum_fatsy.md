Cowboy-Museum Fatsy is one of Linz's stranger small museums, founded by entertainer Hans-Jorg Ratzenbock and devoted to the American West. The displays include a replica ranch room, sheriff's office, and gambling room. It is a niche stop, but a memorable one if you like odd private collections.
