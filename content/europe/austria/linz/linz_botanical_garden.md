The Linz Botanical Garden sits on the Freinberg slope, combining planted outdoor collections with greenhouses and views back toward the city. It is a calm, well-kept break from the Danube-side urban core, especially in spring and summer when the beds are at their best.

The garden is compact enough for an easy visit but varied enough to reward plant lovers. It pairs nicely with a walk on the hill above Linz, where the city feels less industrial and more open to the surrounding Upper Austrian landscape.
