Linz Zoo is a modest family zoo on the slopes of Postlingberg, close to one of the city's favourite viewpoints. Its animal collection and hillside setting make it an easy add-on to a trip up the mountain.

Do not expect a major metropolitan zoo; the appeal is scale, convenience, and a relaxed outing with children. Combine it with the Postlingbergbahn and views over Linz for a fuller excursion.
