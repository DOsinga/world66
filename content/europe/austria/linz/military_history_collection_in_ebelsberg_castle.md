The Military History Collection in Ebelsberg Castle adds a martial layer to a district better known for its castle setting south of central Linz. Exhibits focus on regional military history, uniforms, weapons and the conflicts connected with the area.

It is a specialist stop and a little out from the centre, so it suits travellers with a car, military-history interests or time beyond Linz's main museums. Check opening times carefully; smaller castle collections often keep limited hours.
