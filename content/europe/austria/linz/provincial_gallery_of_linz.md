The Provincial Gallery of Linz, traditionally known as the Landesgalerie, is one of the city's core art stops, housed in a historic museum building near the centre. Its collections and exhibitions focus strongly on Upper Austrian and broader Austrian art.

It is a good counterweight to Linz's technology-forward Ars Electronica side. Check the current exhibition programme before going, since the exact experience depends on what is on display.
