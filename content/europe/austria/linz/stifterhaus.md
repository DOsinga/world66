StifterHaus is the former Linz home of the 19th-century Austrian writer Adalbert Stifter. It is now a small literature museum, with manuscripts, multimedia displays, and exhibitions on Austrian writing.

It is a quiet, specialist stop, but a good one if you are interested in the city's literary side or want something more intimate after the larger Danube-side museums.
