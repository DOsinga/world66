Mayrhofen is a Tyrolean resort town at the upper end of the Zillertal, where the valley narrows and the mountains start to feel close on every side. It is best known as a ski base, but it is not only a winter place: the same lifts feed summer hiking, mountain biking, paragliding, and easy high-country views.

The town sits between two mountain systems. [Penken](/europe/austria/mayrhofen/penken) is the big, connected ski and activity side, with access toward Finkenberg, Rastkogel, and Eggalm. [Ahorn](/europe/austria/mayrhofen/ahorn) is simpler and more scenic, rising above the south end of town with broad views and gentler slopes. Add the [Zillertalbahn](/europe/austria/mayrhofen/zillertalbahn), the [Hintertux Glacier](/europe/austria/mayrhofen/hintertux_glacier), and a useful pool-and-sauna complex, and Mayrhofen has enough substance for more than a resort-strip page.

In season it can feel very packaged, with ski schools, rental shops, weekly lodging cycles, and plenty of visitors from abroad. That is the trade-off. The upside is easy logistics: arrive by train, walk around town, and use lifts and buses to reach a lot of mountain terrain without a car.
