---
title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Melk is defined by its abbey, one of the most spectacular Baroque buildings in Europe. The town itself is a pleasant staging point for the Wachau Valley, and several castles and historic sites are within easy reach along the Danube. The combination of the abbey, a riverside cycle ride, and the Wachau landscape makes Melk a satisfying half-day or full-day stop on any itinerary through Lower Austria.
