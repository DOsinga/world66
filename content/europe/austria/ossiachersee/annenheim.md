Annenheim is the western gateway to Ossiacher See and the most practical lakeside stop if you are aiming for the Gerlitzen. It has less old-world charm than Ossiach, but it wins on transport: trains, lake boats, and the cable car all make sense from here.

The village works best as a base for active days. Swim in the morning, ride up the mountain when the sky is clear, and return to the lake for the evening light over the water.
