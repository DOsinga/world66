Bleistätter Moor protects the quieter eastern end of Ossiacher See, where reeds, wet meadows, and shallow water replace bathing lawns. It is a useful corrective to the resort side of the lake: less dramatic, perhaps, but richer in birds and small seasonal changes.

Paths and viewing points make it a gentle walk rather than a wilderness expedition. Go early or late in the day if you care about wildlife; midday is better for stretching your legs between swims than for seeing much movement in the reeds.
