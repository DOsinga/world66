The Gerlitzen Alpe rises above the northwestern shore and gives Ossiacher See its mountain wall. From Annenheim, the Kanzelbahn carries you up toward open slopes, walking paths, and a broad panorama over Carinthia's lakes.

In summer it is a hiking and paragliding mountain; in winter it becomes the local ski hill. Even if you are not planning a long walk, the ride is worthwhile on a clear day because the lake suddenly becomes part of a much larger Alpine map.
