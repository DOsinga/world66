---
title: Getting There
type: section
---

Villach is the easiest gateway. Regional trains and buses serve several lake settlements, including Annenheim and Ossiach, and the drive from Villach usually takes 20 to 30 minutes depending on the shore and season.

Cyclists can follow lake routes from Villach and continue around much of the shore. In summer, boats link the main lakeside villages, which is the most pleasant way to move if you are not in a hurry.
