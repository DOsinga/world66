Lake Ossiach is the reason to come: broad enough for boats and views, narrow enough that the opposite shore always feels close. The water is warm by Alpine standards, and in settled summer weather the public bathing areas fill with families from Villach and Klagenfurt.

The nicest way to understand the lake is to move along it slowly. A bicycle lets you compare the busy resort patches with the quieter reed-fringed sections, while the summer boats give you the mountain backdrop without worrying about traffic or parking.
