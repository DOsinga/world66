Ossiach Abbey is the lake's anchor sight, a former Benedictine monastery whose church and courtyards give the southern shore its most memorable silhouette. The complex sits close to the water, so it is easy to combine a look inside with a swim or a boat stop.

The appeal is not only architectural. In summer the abbey becomes a concert venue, and the combination of music, lake air, and old stone is exactly why Ossiacher See has a more cultured feel than some of Carinthia's purely beach-focused lakes.
