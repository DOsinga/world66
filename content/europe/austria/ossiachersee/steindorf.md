Steindorf sits on the northern side of the lake and is a good place to slow the pace down. It is not a showpiece village, but it gives access to quieter stretches of shore and the eastern end of the lake.

Use it as a practical stop rather than a destination in itself. The appeal is in the combination of lake views, local walking, and easy movement toward the Bleistätter Moor.
