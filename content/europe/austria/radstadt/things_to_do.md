---
title: Things to Do
type: section
---

The medieval old town can be walked end to end in ten minutes, but it rewards going slowly. The walls and three round towers from the late thirteenth century still ring the centre; the parish church, the Capuchin monastery, and the gothic light column are all within a hundred metres of the Stadtplatz. In winter, the surrounding slopes feed the vast Ski Amadé network; in summer the same valley fills with hikers, road cyclists, and mountain bikers.
