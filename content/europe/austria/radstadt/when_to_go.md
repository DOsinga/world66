---
title: When to Go
type: section
---

Radstadt has two distinct seasons, both worth coming for. The ski season runs from early December through mid-April; January and February are the most reliable for snow, and the mountain gets crowded over the Austrian school holidays in mid-February. Aim for early December or mid-March for cheaper rates and quieter slopes.

In summer, July and August are warm and green; the high passes — the Radstädter Tauern — are fully open and most mountain huts are staffed. June and September are excellent for hiking, fewer people, and lower lodging prices. The shoulder seasons of November and late April–May tend to be quiet, wet, and best avoided.
