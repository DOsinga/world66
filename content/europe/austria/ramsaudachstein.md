Ramsau am Dachstein is a mountain village in the Styrian Alps, spread across a sunny plateau at around 1,100 metres with the massive Dachstein glacier looming behind it. The setting is spectacular — green meadows in front, sheer limestone walls behind — and the village has managed to stay genuinely Alpine without tipping into theme-park territory.

In winter, Ramsau is one of Austria's premier [cross-country skiing](/europe/austria/ramsaudachstein/cross_country_trails) destinations, with over 200 kilometres of groomed trails winding through the valley and up toward the glacier. Downhill skiing is available on the [Dachstein glacier](/europe/austria/ramsaudachstein/dachstein_glacier) itself, which offers year-round snow, and at the nearby Planai resort in [Schladming](/europe/austria/schladming). In summer, the area transforms into hiking and climbing country. The [Dachstein Skywalk](/europe/austria/ramsaudachstein/dachstein_skywalk), a glass platform jutting out from the mountain at 2,700 metres, offers vertigo-inducing views across the Alps, and the [ice caves](/europe/austria/ramsaudachstein/dachstein_ice_cave) inside the mountain are worth the cable car ride up.

The village is quiet and traditional, with wooden farmhouses, a couple of inns, and a small church at its center. Ramsau makes a good base for exploring the wider Schladming-Dachstein region, whether you are here for the mountains, the glacier, or simply the clean air and the views.
