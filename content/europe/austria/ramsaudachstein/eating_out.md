---
title: "Eating Out"
type: section
---

Eating in Ramsau is uncomplicated and almost always centres on a wood-panelled Stube serving Styrian classics: pumpkin-seed oil over salads, **Backhendl** (breaded fried chicken), Käsespätzle, beef goulash, and the local schnapps poured at the end of the meal. Trout from local farms shows up on most menus.

The Pehab-Kirchenwirt and the Annaberger Hof, both old village inns, are reliable choices for traditional cooking. Many of the mountain huts above the village serve hearty lunches in summer — Brettljause platters of cured meat, cheese, bread, and pickles are the standard order after a morning of hiking.
