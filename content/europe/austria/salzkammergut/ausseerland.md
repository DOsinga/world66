The Ausseerland is the Styrian portion of the Salzkammergut, centred on the small town of Bad Aussee and three lakes — the Grundlsee, the Toplitzsee, and the Kammersee. It is the most remote and least touristy corner of the Salzkammergut, reached by road over the Pötschenpass from Bad Ischl or by the narrow-gauge Salzkammergut railway, which terminates at Bad Aussee.

The [Grundlsee](/europe/austria/salzkammergut/grundlsee) is the largest of the three lakes, used for swimming and sailing; the Toplitzsee is dramatically set in a gorge at the end of a forest track and has a wartime story attached (Nazi gold was reportedly thrown into it at the war's end — divers have found ammunition boxes but no gold). Bad Aussee itself is a pleasant market town with a traditional Narrenzunft carnival in February that involves elaborate handmade costumes and is one of the oldest folk festivals in Styria.
