Gmunden is the main town on the Traunsee, a city of about 13,000 people with an unusually well-preserved medieval and baroque core. The Schloss Ort — a small castle built on an island in the lake, connected to the shore by a wooden bridge — is the iconic image and has served as the filming location for an Austrian soap opera (*Schlosshotel Orth*) since the 1990s.

The town is also the centre of Austrian ceramic production: Gmunden Keramik, a distinctive style of glazed earthenware decorated with green on cream, has been made here since the 14th century and is sold in the artisan workshops along the old town streets. The Toscana Park on the lake shore has an unusually grand layout for a provincial Austrian town, with formal Italian-style planting and views across the water. Boats run from Gmunden south along the Traunsee toward Traunkirchen and Ebensee.
