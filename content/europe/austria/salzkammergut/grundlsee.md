The Grundlsee is the largest lake in Styria, a long, narrow body of water in the Ausseerland enclosed by forested ridges. It is little visited compared to the Upper Austrian lakes, which makes it an attractive alternative: the water is warm enough for swimming from June to August, the shores are largely undeveloped, and the village of Grundlsee at the western end has a few good guesthouses and a peaceful atmosphere.

A path runs along the northern shore for its entire length, ending at the Toplitzhütte from which a short walk leads to the mysterious Toplitzsee — a deep, dark lake in a narrow gorge, famous for the wartime rumours of sunken Nazi treasure. Beyond the Toplitzsee lies the tiny Kammersee, accessible only on foot. For cyclists, the Ausseerland cycling route connects Grundlsee with Bad Aussee and with the wider Salzkammergut network to the west.
