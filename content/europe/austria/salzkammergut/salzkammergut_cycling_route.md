The main cycling routes through the Salzkammergut follow the river valleys between the lakes, connecting the towns on generally flat terrain with occasional moderate climbs over low passes. The core route runs from Salzburg east along the Salzach and then the Traun valley, passing through Bad Ischl and continuing south to Hallstatt — a distance of about 85 kilometres that can comfortably be cycled in two days with a night in Bad Ischl.

Bikes can be rented at the main railway stations in Bad Ischl and Gmunden, and returned at other stations on the network. The western loop, from Salzburg to the Wolfgangsee and back via Mondsee, makes an excellent one-day ride of about 70 kilometres. The Ausseerland in the east is harder terrain — the roads over the Pötschenpass and through the Enns valley involve real climbs — but the landscapes are proportionally more dramatic. The cycling infrastructure throughout the Salzkammergut is excellent: most main routes are on separated paths or quiet roads.
