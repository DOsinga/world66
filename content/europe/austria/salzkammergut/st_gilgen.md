St. Gilgen sits at the western end of the Wolfgangsee, connected by boat to [St. Wolfgang](/europe/austria/salzkammergut/pilgrimage_church_st_wolfgang) across the lake. It is a quieter and less touristy alternative to its more famous neighbour — a pretty village of 3,500 people with a good beach, decent restaurants, and the Zwölferhorn cable car ascending to 1,520 metres with panoramic views over the lake system.

The village has a water sports centre for windsurfing and sailing; the Wolfgangsee is well suited to both, with reliable afternoon breezes. The Mozarthaus in the town hall commemorates the family connection. For walkers, the path up to the Zwölferhorn plateau and across to the Postalm is a full-day hike with extraordinary views; the Postalm itself is a high alpine pasture with a clutch of farmhouses and is one of the largest alpine pastures in Austria.
