Traunkirchen is a small village on a promontory jutting into the Traunsee, connected to the main road only by a narrow causeway. It was the site of a Benedictine nunnery from the 10th century — the current parish church incorporates the old conventual church — and the village has preserved a medieval layout that the larger Traunsee towns have lost.

The fishing boat pulpit in the church is extraordinary and worth the detour alone. Boats stop here on the Traunsee route from Gmunden; the journey by water across the lake is the best way to appreciate the dramatic position of the village. The Johannesberg Chapel on the hill above Traunkirchen is reached by a steep path and has views over the whole lake.
