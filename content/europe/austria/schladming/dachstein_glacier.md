The Dachstein Glacier sits a short distance north of Schladming, accessible by cable car from the village of Ramsau. It is one of the most visited natural attractions in Austria. In summer you can ski on the glacier when every other mountain in the area is bare, and the glacier touring is excellent for those who want to walk on ice rather than ski on it.

The spectacular **Dachstein Skywalk** is a glass platform cantilevered over a 250-metre drop, giving views across the whole Schladming region. Below the glacier, the **Dachstein Ice Palace** is a series of carved chambers and sculptures in the ice. At 2,700 metres, the Dachstein summit is the highest point in Styria and the views on a clear day extend to a dozen mountain ranges. The World Heritage Site designation covers the whole Dachstein area, including the nearby Hallstatt lake.
