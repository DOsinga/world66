The main mountain above Schladming, rising to 1,894 metres and directly accessible from the town centre by gondola. Planai is the heart of the Ski amadé network, and the mountain that hosts the World Cup Night Slalom each January — one of the most atmospheric races on the alpine circuit, run under floodlights with the town visible in the valley below.

For ordinary skiers, Planai offers wide, well-groomed intermediate runs and excellent snow-making infrastructure. The mountain is connected by lift to Hochwurzen on one side and Hauser Kaibling on the other, allowing a long traverse across the linked area. In summer the gondola runs for hikers and mountain bikers, and the summit views over the Enns Valley and toward the Dachstein are among the best in Styria.
