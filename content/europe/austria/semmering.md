Semmering is a mountain pass, railway landscape, and old resort area between Lower Austria and Styria. It is close enough to Vienna for a long day out, but the mood is already alpine: forested slopes, stone viaducts, old grand hotels, and trains curling across the mountainside.

The reason to come is the [Semmering Railway](/europe/austria/semmering/semmering_railway), the first railway over a high mountain pass and now a UNESCO World Heritage Site. You can ride it as a scenic rail trip, but the better day is to walk part of the railway trail and look back from the [20-Schilling View](/europe/austria/semmering/twenty_schilling_view), where the viaducts line up against the Rax.

Semmering also has a second life as a modest but useful resort. [Hirschenkogel](/europe/austria/semmering/hirschenkogel) brings skiing, night slopes, tobogganing, and summer mountain paths to a place that still feels more like a weekend escape than a polished international resort.

## Destinations around Semmering

- [Semmering Railway](/europe/austria/semmering/semmering_railway) - UNESCO-listed mountain railway and the main reason to come
- [20-Schilling View](/europe/austria/semmering/twenty_schilling_view) - classic lookout over the Kalte Rinne viaduct
- [Hirschenkogel](/europe/austria/semmering/hirschenkogel) - ski hill, toboggan run, and summer walking area
