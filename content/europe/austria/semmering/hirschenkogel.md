Hirschenkogel is the practical resort side of Semmering. In winter the Zauberberg ski area offers a compact set of slopes, night skiing, and a toboggan run; in summer the same hill becomes a base for walking and mountain air.

Do not expect the scale of the Tyrol. The appeal is different: easy access from Vienna and Graz, enough mountain activity for a day, and the railway landscape just below.
