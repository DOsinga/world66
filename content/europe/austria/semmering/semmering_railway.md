The Semmering Railway opened in 1854 and still feels bold: a mainline railway climbing through tunnels, stone bridges, and sweeping viaducts rather than a museum train preserved for tourists. Ride it between Gloggnitz and Murzzuschlag, or use Semmering station as the start of a walk.

The route is most rewarding when you slow down. The railway hiking trail lets you see why the engineering mattered, with repeated views of arches, cuttings, and curves that a train passenger only catches for a few seconds.
