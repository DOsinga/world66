The 20-Schilling View is the classic lookout over the Kalte Rinne viaduct, with the railway, forest, and mountain wall arranged almost too neatly. The name comes from the view's use on Austria's old 20-schilling banknote.

It is not a wilderness hike, but it is the right short walk if you want Semmering's story in one frame: 19th-century engineering, alpine terrain, and a train still using the line.
