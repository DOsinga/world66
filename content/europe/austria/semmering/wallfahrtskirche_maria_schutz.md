Wallfahrtskirche Maria Schutz is a Baroque pilgrimage church below the Semmering pass. The sanctuary grew around a spring whose water was believed to have cured plague, and the present church was rebuilt in the 18th century after earlier damage.

The church is worth a short detour for its setting as much as its interior. It sits in a village landscape of wooded slopes and old pilgrimage routes, with the Semmering mountains close behind.
