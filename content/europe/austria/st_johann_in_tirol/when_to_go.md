---
title: When to Go
type: section
---

St. Johann has two clear seasons. Winter runs from late November to late March, with the best snow conditions usually in January and February. The ski season draws the most visitors, and accommodation prices peak over Christmas, New Year, and Austrian school holidays (usually the last week of January and early February).

Summer — June through September — is the second busy season, favoured by walkers, cyclists, and families. The wildflowers on the Kitzbüheler Horn are at their best in June and July. May and October are quiet shoulder months: cheaper, peaceful, but with lifts limited and some mountain huts closed.
