---
title: "Getting There"
type: section
---

St. Anton am Arlberg sits on the main rail line between Innsbruck and Feldkirch, making it one of the best-connected ski resorts in the Alps. The train from Innsbruck takes around 90 minutes; from Zürich, allow about 2.5 hours changing at Feldkirch. The railway station is in the village centre, within walking distance of most accommodation and the main lifts.

By car, St. Anton is in the Vorarlberg/Tyrol border area, accessible via the Arlberg Road Tunnel from the Vorarlberg side (toll applies) or over the Arlberg Pass when it is open in summer. From Innsbruck, the drive takes about 90 minutes. From Zürich airport, allow 2.5 hours. Parking in the village is limited during peak season; many hotels have their own parking.
