---
title: "Bars and Cafes"
type: section
---

The classic Steyr coffeehouse stop is **Café Werndl** on the Stadtplatz — long wooden tables, newspapers in racks, and a serious cake counter. Several younger cafe-bars run along the same stretch and around the Grünmarkt. Late drinking concentrates in the Wehrgraben district, where former workshops have been turned into bars and small live-music venues.
