---
title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Steyr is essentially a walking museum. Start at the **Stadtplatz**, one of the most beautiful town squares in central Europe, ringed by Gothic, Renaissance, and Baroque facades. The **Bummerlhaus** is the standout — a 15th-century burgher house with an arcaded courtyard and an unbroken survival across six centuries. From the square, climb to **Schloss Lamberg**, the former Styraburg castle, which still towers over the confluence of the Steyr and Enns rivers.

Don't miss the **Stadtpfarrkirche** with its connection to Anton Bruckner and Franz Schubert (Schubert composed his "Trout Quintet" in Steyr), the late-Gothic interior of the **Michaelerkirche** on the other side of the river, and the **Stadtmuseum** in the sgraffitto-covered Innerberger Stadel, which tells the story of the city's iron industry. The **Christkindl pilgrimage church**, a few kilometres outside the centre, runs the famous Christmas post office that hand-stamps mail with the "Christkindl" cancellation.

Active visitors can take the **Steyrtal-Museumsbahn**, Europe's oldest narrow-gauge steam railway, up the Steyr valley in summer, or use Steyr as a base for trips into the **Nationalpark Kalkalpen** to the south.
