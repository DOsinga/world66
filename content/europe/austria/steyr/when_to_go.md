---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

Steyr is at its best in two windows. May to early July gives long days, fresh river-air, the Stadtplatz cafes spilling out into the square, and the Steyrtal-Museumsbahn running on Sundays. Late November to early January turns the city into one of the prettier Christmas-market towns of Austria, with the Christkindl post office running its annual letter-stamping operation a short walk away.

Spring shoulder weeks can be cold and wet; high summer is pleasant but draws Austrian and German visitors in numbers. The Nationalpark Kalkalpen is best from June to October.
