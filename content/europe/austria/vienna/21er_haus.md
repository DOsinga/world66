21er Haus is Vienna's postwar modernist art museum near the Belvedere, with a strong link to Austrian sculptor and architect Fritz Wotruba. The programme mixes permanent holdings with changing contemporary-art exhibitions, making it a good counterpoint to the palaces and old-master collections nearby.
