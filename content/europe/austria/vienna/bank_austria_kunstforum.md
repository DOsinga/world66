Bank Austria Kunstforum is a central Vienna exhibition hall known for polished temporary shows rather than a permanent collection. Its programme leans toward major international names in modern and contemporary art, with past exhibitions devoted to artists such as Schiele, Kokoschka, Cezanne, Picasso, and Van Gogh.

It is best checked by current exhibition rather than treated as an automatic stop. When the show suits your interests, the location near the Freyung makes it easy to fold into a walk through the Innere Stadt.
