Beethoven Wohnung Heiligenstadt is one of the Vienna residences associated with Ludwig van Beethoven. He lived in this part of the city while working through the crisis of his growing deafness and composing major works, including his Second Symphony.

The house is best for travellers who already care about Beethoven or Vienna's musical history. It also fits well with a walk through Heiligenstadt and nearby wine-country edges of the city.
