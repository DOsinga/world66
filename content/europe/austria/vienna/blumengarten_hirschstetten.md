Blumengärten Hirschstetten is a public garden complex run by the City of Vienna's gardening department. It combines themed gardens, display plantings, a preserved farm area, and a small petting zoo, making it especially useful for families or anyone looking beyond the central museums and palaces.

The gardens are in Vienna's outer east, so they take a little effort compared with the Ringstrasse sights, but they offer a quieter look at the city's public garden culture.
