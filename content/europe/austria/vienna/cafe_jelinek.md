A small, gloriously unrenovated coffeehouse on a quiet Mariahilf side street, Café Jelinek is the antithesis of the grand Ringstrasse cafés: shabby, intimate and warmed in winter by a genuine wood-fired tiled stove. The patina is real — worn benches, yellowed walls, stacks of newspapers — and the atmosphere unhurried.

It is the kind of place Viennese regulars guard jealously: come for a melange and a slice of cake, read for an hour, and feel the city slow down. Cash only, no fuss, no wifi to speak of. One of the most atmospheric cafés in the city.
