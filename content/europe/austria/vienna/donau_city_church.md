Donau City Church is a small modern Catholic church in Vienna's high-rise Donau City district, designed by Austrian architect Heinz Tesar. Its dark, compact form and quiet interior make a sharp contrast with the glass office towers around it.

It is most worthwhile if you are already visiting Donauinsel, Donauturm, or the UN district and have an eye for contemporary church architecture.
