The Fälschermuseum is a small Vienna museum devoted to art forgery, copies, and famous frauds. Its collection is strongest when it explains the stories behind the fakes, from skilled counterfeit painters to the forged Hitler diaries, making it a quirky counterpoint to the city's grand art museums.
