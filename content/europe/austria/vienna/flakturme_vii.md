Flakturme VII is a pair of massive Second World War anti-aircraft towers built by the Luftwaffe. Their scale is the point: the concrete blocks are a blunt reminder of wartime Vienna and the difficulty of removing structures built to withstand bombing.

They are best treated as a short historical stop while exploring the surrounding district.
