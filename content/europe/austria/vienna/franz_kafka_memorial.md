This small memorial room marks the building in Kierling where Franz Kafka died of tuberculosis in 1924. Displays include photographs, letters, cards, and some personal material connected with Kafka's final weeks.

It is a focused stop for literature-minded visitors rather than a large museum. Check opening times before making the trip from Vienna or Klosterneuburg.
