Vienna's Gasometers are four huge 19th-century brick gas tanks turned into a complex of apartments, shops, offices, entertainment spaces and a concert hall. The old industrial shells are still the main reason to come: the circular walls, heavy masonry and scale make them feel very different from a normal shopping centre.

The redevelopment brought in architects including Jean Nouvel and Coop Himmelb(l)au, so the pleasure is in seeing how modern Vienna has been fitted inside the remains of its municipal gas works. It is an easy U-Bahn trip from the centre and a good stop for anyone interested in industrial architecture.
