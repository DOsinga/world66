This Academy of Fine Arts gallery is a compact stop for old master painting, with works by Rubens, Bosch, and other Renaissance and medieval artists. It is close to the Secession and Karlsplatz, so it fits well into a museum-heavy walk around the southern edge of the Innere Stadt.
