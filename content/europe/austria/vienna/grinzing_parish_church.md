Grinzing Parish Church is a 15th-century village church that survived repeated damage, including the Ottoman sieges of Vienna and World War II. Its most notable survivor is the organ, which is linked to both Beethoven and Schubert.

It is a short, worthwhile stop while walking through Grinzing, especially before or after visiting the nearby cemetery or one of the traditional Heurigen.
