Hietzinger Cemetery is a quiet historic cemetery near Schönbrunn, known for the graves of major Viennese cultural figures. Franz Grillparzer, Gustav Klimt, Otto Wagner, and Alban Berg are among the playwrights, artists, architects, and composers buried here.
