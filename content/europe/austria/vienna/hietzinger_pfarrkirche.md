Hietzinger Pfarrkirche is the parish church at the center of Hietzing, close to Schonbrunn's western side. Its origins go back to the 15th century, though the present character is largely Baroque after rebuilding following the Turkish war of 1685.

It is a short architectural and local-history stop, best folded into a Hietzing walk rather than treated as a standalone trip across Vienna.
