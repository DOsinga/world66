Hofburgkapelle is the Hofburg's Gothic palace chapel, later given a Baroque interior. It is most rewarding on Sundays and Catholic holidays, when the Court Musicians perform with members of the Vienna Boys' Choir and the Vienna State Opera orchestra and choir.
