Jörgerbad is a historic public bath from the early 20th century, with a 25-metre pool under a retractable glass roof. The building is more atmospheric than most municipal pools, and the complex also has sauna and massage facilities.

Come for a swim or to see a working example of Vienna's old bathhouse culture, especially on a cold or rainy day.
