Jüdischer Friedhof Floridsdorf is a historic Jewish cemetery in Vienna's northern districts, established in 1877 when Floridsdorf was still an independent town. It remained in use until 1978 and preserves part of the Jewish history of an area visitors often miss.

This is a quiet, specialist stop rather than a central Vienna highlight. Opening can be irregular, so it is best for travellers with a particular interest in Jewish heritage or the city's outer districts.
