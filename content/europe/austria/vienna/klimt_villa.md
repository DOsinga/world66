Klimt Villa preserves the house where Gustav Klimt worked from 1911 until his death in 1918. The studio has been reconstructed from old photographs, and the displays explain his late work, models, and clients.

It is a focused visit rather than a large museum, but it is a good add-on for anyone already visiting Hietzing or Schonbrunn and interested in Vienna's art history.
