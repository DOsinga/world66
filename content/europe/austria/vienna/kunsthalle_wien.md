Kunsthalle Wien is Vienna's main venue for contemporary art, with changing exhibitions by Austrian and international artists. It sits in the MuseumsQuartier, so it is easy to pair with the Leopold Museum, Mumok, or a coffee break in the courtyard.

The programme changes often and can be challenging, political, or playful. Check what is on before you go.
