Museum Postsparkasse sits inside Otto Wagner's Postal Savings Bank, one of Vienna's great early modern buildings. The small museum focuses on Wagner's designs, furniture, and plans, while the banking hall itself is the real draw: crisp, practical, and still startlingly fresh.
