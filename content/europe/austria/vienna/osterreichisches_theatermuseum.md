The Österreichisches Theatermuseum fills part of the Baroque Lobkowitz Palace with stage models, costumes, props, and changing exhibitions on theatre and performance. It is a useful stop for visitors interested in Vienna's stage history, especially when paired with the nearby Albertina or Hofburg.
