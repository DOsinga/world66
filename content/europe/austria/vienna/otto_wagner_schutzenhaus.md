The Otto Wagner Schutzenhaus is a restored 1908 building on the Donaukanal, designed by Otto Wagner as part of his wider plans for Vienna's modern waterway. It now houses a restaurant, but the draw is the compact example of Wagner's early 20th-century urban architecture.
