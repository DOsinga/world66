The Phantastenmuseum focuses on the Vienna School of Fantastic Realism, with works by artists such as Ernst Fuchs, Arik Brauer, Rudolf Hausner, Wolfgang Hutter, and Fritz Janschka. It is a niche art stop, but a useful counterpoint to Vienna's larger imperial museums.
