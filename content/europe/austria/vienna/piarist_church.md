The Piarist Church, also known as Maria Treu, is a Baroque church in Josefstadt with an ornate interior and ceiling frescoes by Franz Anton Maulbertsch. Its twin-towered facade and quiet square make it one of the stronger church stops outside Vienna's busiest central sights.

Visit when you are exploring Josefstadt, the Rathaus area, or the streets west of the Ring.
