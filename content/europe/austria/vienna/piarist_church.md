The Piarist Church, also known as Maria Treu, is one of Josefstadt's finest Baroque churches. Its oval interior, side chapels, and ceiling frescoes by Franz Anton Maulbertsch make it a rewarding stop if you are exploring Vienna beyond the Ringstrasse monuments.
