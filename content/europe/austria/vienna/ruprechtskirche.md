Ruprechtskirche is often described as Vienna's oldest church, though archaeologists have complicated that claim by finding older fabric elsewhere. What is clear is that the church was mentioned by 1200, and its Romanesque core feels far older and plainer than most of the Baroque city around it.

The interior is small, quiet, and worth seeking out in the Innere Stadt. It is a good antidote to Vienna's grander churches.
