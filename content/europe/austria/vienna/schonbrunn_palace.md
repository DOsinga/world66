The Habsburg summer residence and one of Europe's finest Baroque palaces, with 1,441 rooms (40 open to the public). The Grand Tour covers the imperial apartments in frescoed detail; the Classic Tour is sufficient for most visitors. The formal gardens behind the palace are free and enormous — walk up to the Gloriette on the ridge for the view of the palace and Vienna behind it. Come in late afternoon when the crowds thin. The palace is a short U-Bahn ride from the centre on the U4 line.
