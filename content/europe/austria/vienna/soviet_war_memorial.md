The Soviet War Memorial near Karlsplatz is an imposing monument to the Red Army's capture of Vienna from Nazi Germany in 1945. Its columns, soldier figure, and Soviet-style form make it one of the city's more striking Second World War memorials.

It is easy to combine with Karlskirche, Belvedere, and the museums around Karlsplatz.
