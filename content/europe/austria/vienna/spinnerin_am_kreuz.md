Spinnerin am Kreuz is a 16-metre Gothic stone column from the late 14th century, standing in Vienna's southern districts. It once marked the southern edge of the city's jurisdiction and later became associated with local legends about the Crusades. The column is a short stop rather than a full outing, but its age and unusual history make it worth noticing if you are exploring beyond the centre.
