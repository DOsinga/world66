Strandbad Gansehaufel is Vienna's great open-air bathing complex on the Old Danube, with lawns, beaches, pools, a wave basin, slides, and plenty of room to spend a hot day outside. It is run by the city and feels more like a democratic summer institution than a private water park.

The 1950s functionalist buildings are part of the charm, as are the different zones for families, serious swimmers, sunbathers, and the large nudist section. Bring a towel, expect crowds on hot weekends, and plan to linger.
