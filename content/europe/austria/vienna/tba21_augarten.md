TBA21 Augarten is a contemporary art venue in the Augarten, formerly known as Augarten Contemporary. It is run by the TBA21 Foundation and presents changing exhibitions by Austrian and international artists.

The building also holds a permanent display of sculpture by Gustinus Ambrosi. It is a useful modern-art stop on the Leopoldstadt side of central Vienna.
