Volkskundemuseum Wien, the Austrian Museum of Folk Life and Folk Art, is housed in the Schonborn Garden Palace. Its exhibitions look at everyday objects, craft, clothing, and folk traditions from Austria and central Europe, with both permanent displays and changing shows.
