WestLicht Center for Photography is a photography museum and gallery in Vienna's Neubau district. Its permanent collection includes unusual cameras, from underwater and toy cameras to spy cameras and a modified Hasselblad linked to the Apollo moon missions.

The centre also hosts temporary exhibitions, including major photography shows. It is a good stop for visitors interested in cameras, photojournalism, or Vienna's smaller specialist museums.
