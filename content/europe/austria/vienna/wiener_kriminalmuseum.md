The Wiener Kriminalmuseum is a crime and justice museum covering Vienna's police, courts, and underworld from the Middle Ages to the 20th century.

Its displays lean into the darker side of city history, with material on famous cases, criminals, and punishment. It is a specialist museum, but a good change of tone if you have already seen Vienna's imperial palaces and art collections.
