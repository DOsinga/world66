The Wiener Schuhmuseum is a niche museum devoted to shoes and shoemaking. Its displays cover historic footwear, tools, and the craft traditions behind Viennese shoe production.

It is not a first-time Vienna essential, but it is a worthwhile detour for design, fashion, or craft-history travellers who want something smaller and more local than the Ringstrasse museums.
