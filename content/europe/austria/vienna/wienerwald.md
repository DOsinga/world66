The Wienerwald is the wooded range of low limestone hills wrapping around the west and north of the city — a UNESCO Biosphere Reserve of around 1,000 square kilometres of beech and oak forest, meadows, and vineyards. It is where the Viennese walk, cycle, and ride at weekends.

From the city, the best gateways are the Kahlenberg and Leopoldsberg in the north (with the steep vineyard slopes of Grinzing and Nussdorf below) and the Lainzer Tiergarten in the west — a former imperial hunting reserve now home to wild boar and red deer, with marked walking trails. Most of the woods can be reached by U-Bahn and a short bus ride.
