The Vienna University of Economics and Business campus is one of the city's strongest modern architecture walks. Opened in 2013, it gathers buildings by several international firms into a compact district beside the Prater.

The headline is Zaha Hadid's Library and Learning Center, all sharp angles and sweeping interior lines. Even if you are not visiting the university, the campus is worth a detour when you want Vienna beyond palaces and Ringstrasse monuments.
