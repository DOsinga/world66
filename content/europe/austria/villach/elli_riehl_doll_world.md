Elli Riehl Doll World is a private museum near Villach devoted to handmade character dolls and miniature scenes. The collection is unusual, personal and strongly tied to Austrian folk-life imagery rather than mass-produced toys.

It is a niche stop, best for families, craft enthusiasts or travelers who enjoy small eccentric museums. Because it sits outside central Villach, confirm opening times before making the detour.
