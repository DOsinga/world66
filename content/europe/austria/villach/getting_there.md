---
title: "Getting There"
type: section
---

Villach is exceptionally well connected for a city of its size — it sits on the intersection of two major European rail corridors and is served by the main motorway routes between Italy, Slovenia, and northern Austria.

**By train** is the most practical option for most visitors. Villach Hauptbahnhof is on the Vienna–Klagenfurt line with regular fast trains from Vienna (about 3.5 hours), and on the corridor connecting Salzburg and Klagenfurt westward to Innsbruck. Direct connections also run south to Udine and Venice (about 2.5 hours), and east to Ljubljana (about 1.5 hours). The station is centrally located and within easy walking distance of the old town.

**By car**, the city is at the junction of the A2 motorway (Vienna–Klagenfurt) and the A10 (Salzburg–Villach). Italy is accessible via the A23 motorway through the Tarvisio pass — the crossing at the border takes minutes and Udine is about 80 kilometres south. Slovenia is even closer: the border at Karawankentunnel is 15 kilometres southeast of the city and Ljubljana is about 90 kilometres from there.

The nearest airports with useful international connections are **Klagenfurt** (30 minutes by train, served mainly by charter and low-cost carriers), **Ljubljana** (about 90 minutes by car), and **Venice/Treviso** (about 2 hours by car). For the widest choice of flights, **Vienna** (about 3.5 hours by train) remains the most practical gateway.
