Relief von Karnten is a large three-dimensional model of Carinthia, useful for understanding the mountain-and-lake geography around Villach before heading out into the region. It turns the area's valleys, passes, and lakes into something you can read at a glance.

This is a short, specialised stop rather than a full museum afternoon, but it adds context for travellers using Villach as a base for Dobratsch, Faaker See, or the wider Alpe-Adria region. It is strongest for map lovers, hikers, and anyone planning day trips.
