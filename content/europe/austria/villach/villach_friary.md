The Villach Friary (Franziskanerkloster Villach) is a Franciscan complex situated just off the main pedestrian area, responsible for the parish of St. Nikolaus. The friary church is less visited than the Stadtpfarrkirche but contains some fine baroque altar work and a calm cloister garden that is accessible during opening hours.

The complex represents a continuous religious presence in this part of the city dating back to the medieval period. For visitors interested in the history of the Reformation in Carinthia — a province that came close to becoming a Protestant stronghold before the Counter-Reformation rolled it back in the early 17th century — the friary is an interesting footnote: the Franciscans remained here throughout, maintaining a Catholic anchor when much of the surrounding region wavered.
