The scheduled boat service between Melk and Krems is operated by DDSG Blue Danube and runs from April to October. The full journey takes about 3.5 hours downstream (Melk to Krems) or 5.5 hours upstream (Krems to Melk) — the current makes a significant difference, and downstream is the more rewarding direction. The boats are comfortable and have outdoor decks from which the scenery — castles, vineyards, vine-terraced bluffs — arrives in sequence.

The service stops at Spitz and Dürnstein, allowing you to get off and re-board a later boat. A good itinerary is to take the early boat from Melk, stop in Dürnstein for a few hours, and then continue to Krems by the afternoon service. Alternatively, cycle one way and return by boat. The boats get crowded in July and August; book ahead or travel on weekdays.
