Gozzoburg is a 13th-century town palace in Krems and one of Austria's most important surviving medieval civic buildings. Restoration work uncovered rare 13th-century indoor frescoes, a major reason to seek it out.

Access to the frescoes may depend on guided tours or exhibition opening times, so check locally before making it the centre of a visit.
