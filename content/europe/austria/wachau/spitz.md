Spitz an der Donau is a wine village in the middle of the Wachau, slightly larger and more lived-in than Dürnstein, with a market square, several wine Heurigen, and a good Gothic parish church with a notable altar. The village sits below the Tausendeimerberg vineyard, one of the most celebrated in the Wachau, whose Rieslings and Grüner Veltliners appear at the top of Austrian wine lists.

The ship museum in Spitz — one of only a handful of such museums in Austria — displays traditional wooden river boats that worked the Danube before motorisation. The Wachau cycling path runs directly through the village; there are bike rentals available. The [Aggstein Castle](/europe/austria/wachau/aggstein_castle) ruins are about 7 kilometres upstream and make a good cycling detour from Spitz.
