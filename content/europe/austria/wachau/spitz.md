Spitz is a wine village in the middle of the [Wachau](/europe/austria/wachau), a little larger and more lived-in than Dürnstein and less dominated by day-trip crowds. The Danube runs below the village, vineyards climb above it, and the broken walls of [Hinterhaus Castle](/europe/austria/wachau/spitz/hinterhaus_castle) watch over the valley from the ridge.

The village sits below the Tausendeimerberg, the "Thousand-Bucket Hill", one of the Wachau's best-known vineyards. Local wine taverns pour Riesling and Grüner Veltliner from the surrounding terraces, and the [Maritime Museum](/europe/austria/wachau/spitz/maritime_museum) adds the river story: wooden boats, Danube trade, and the work that kept the valley connected before motors and highways.

Spitz is easy to visit by bike, boat, train, or car, and it makes a good slower counterpoint to Dürnstein. Climb to the castle ruins, look into the fortified parish church, then settle into a Heuriger rather than rushing straight back onto the road.
