The Wachau cycling route along the Danube is the most rewarding day's cycling in Austria. The EuroVelo 6 long-distance path runs along both banks between Melk and Krems; the north bank has the main road and most of the villages, while the south bank is quieter and passes the [Venus of Willendorf](/europe/austria/wachau/venus_of_willendorf) findspot and [Aggstein Castle](/europe/austria/wachau/aggstein_castle). Most cyclists do the north bank in one direction and return by boat; bikes can be hired in Melk or Krems and returned at the other end, with several rental companies offering one-way hire.

The full route from Melk to Krems is 33 kilometres of almost flat cycling on paved or good-gravel paths. The two or three hours of riding time leaves ample time for stops at [Dürnstein](/europe/austria/wachau/durnstein), [Weissenkirchen](/europe/austria/wachau/weissenkirchen), and [Spitz](/europe/austria/wachau/spitz). The boats that run the river between April and October also carry bikes, so it is possible to cycle one way and float the other. Spring, when the apricot trees are in blossom and the vineyards are just coming into leaf, is the finest time; the autumn wine harvest runs through September and October.
