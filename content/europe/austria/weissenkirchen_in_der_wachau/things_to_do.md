---
title: Things to Do
type: section
---

Weißenkirchen is best treated as a compact Wachau stop: climb through the fortified church complex, look into the old courtyard of the Teisenhoferhof, then walk into the vineyards above town. St. Michael, just west along the river, adds an older fortified church site and makes the village feel like part of a defended medieval landscape rather than just another pretty wine stop.
