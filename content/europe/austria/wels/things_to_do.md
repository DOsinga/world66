The heart of Wels is the long [Stadtplatz](/europe/austria/wels/stadtplatz), anchored at its western end by the [Ledererturm](/europe/austria/wels/lederer), the last surviving medieval gate tower, and at the east by the Gothic [Stadtpfarrkirche](/europe/austria/wels/stadtpfarrkirche). Along the square you will find the town hall, the [Kaiserliche Burg](/europe/austria/wels/kaiserliche_burg) — where Emperor Maximilian I died in 1519, now home to the city museum — and a string of baroque and Renaissance burgher houses. The [Minoritenkloster](/europe/austria/wels/minoritenkloster) just south of the square gives a quieter counterpoint to the busy main street.

A few kilometres north of town, [Schloss Puchberg](/europe/austria/wels/schlopuchberg) is the closest castle to the city. For families, [Zoo Schmiding](/europe/austria/wels/schmiding_zoo) — about 8 km north of Wels — is one of the largest zoos in Austria, with a particularly strong bird collection.
