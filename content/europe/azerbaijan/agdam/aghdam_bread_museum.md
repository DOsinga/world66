The Aghdam Bread Museum is an unusual Soviet-era institution dedicated to the history of bread production and bakery culture in the Agdam region. The collection includes traditional ceramic bread vessels, agricultural implements, historical photographs of farming communities in the area, and exhibits on the history of grain cultivation in Azerbaijan. It is a modest museum by any standard, but its survival through the decades of conflict gives it a poignant quality.

The museum was one of the first cultural facilities to reopen after Azerbaijan recaptured Agdam in 2020. It sits in the Agdam rayon rather than in the ruined city centre itself. For visitors making the trip to Agdam, the Bread Museum provides a counterpoint to the war-ruins narrative — a reminder that this was a functioning agricultural community before the conflict. Hours and access should be confirmed locally before visiting.
