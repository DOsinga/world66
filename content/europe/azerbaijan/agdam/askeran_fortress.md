Askeran Fortress is an 18th-century defensive work built by Panah Ali Khan to guard the approaches to Shusha. Its double walls, entrance gate and towers remain the main reason to stop.

The fortress is outside Agdam rather than in the town centre, but it is one of the more visible historic structures in the area. Check current local access conditions before making a special trip.
