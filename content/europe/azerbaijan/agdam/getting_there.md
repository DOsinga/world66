---
title: Getting There
type: section
---

Agdam is approximately 340 km west of Baku by road. The main route runs via Ganja and then south; the journey takes around 5–6 hours by car. There is bus service from Baku to Agdam, but services are infrequent and the journey is long — a private car or taxi is more practical.

Access to Agdam requires passing through the former conflict zone. As of 2020, Azerbaijan controls the city and surrounding rayon, and the road infrastructure has been gradually restored. However, conditions and access restrictions can change; check current requirements before travelling. Some areas near the former line of contact may still have unexploded ordnance — stay on established roads and paths.
