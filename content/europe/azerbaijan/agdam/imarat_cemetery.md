The Imarat Cemetery — formally the Imarat Garvand cemetery — is a royal burial complex on the outskirts of Agdam, containing the mausoleums and graves of the khans of the Karabakh Khanate and their family members. The complex dates from the 18th and early 19th centuries and represents some of the finest Islamic funerary architecture in Azerbaijan. Ornate carved stone grave markers, octagonal mausoleums, and inscribed tombstones survive in varying states of preservation.

The site is historically significant as the burial place of the rulers of one of the most important Azerbaijani polities of the modern era. Following Azerbaijan's recapture of the region in 2020, restoration work has been carried out on the most damaged structures. The cemetery sits in open ground near the town and is accessible by road. It is worth visiting if you have an interest in Azerbaijani history and Islamic funerary art.
