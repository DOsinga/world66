type: section
---

Agdam is not a conventional travel destination. It is a ruined city, visited primarily by those with an interest in post-conflict landscapes, modern ruins, or the Nagorno-Karabakh conflict. The Juma Mosque and the Bread Museum are the two intact landmarks. Reconstruction work has been underway since 2020, so the character of the site is changing; what you find will depend on when you visit.
