---
title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Aqsu is not a tourist town. What it offers is a small district museum, a couple of modern monuments, and easy access to genuinely interesting things in the surrounding countryside — the ruins of old Shemakha, the Aqsu mountain pass, and the early medieval site at Qaraqoyonlu. A half-day stop on a longer drive through Şamaxı country is about right.
