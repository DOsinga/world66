Baku TV Tower is a 310-metre concrete telecommunications tower and one of the most visible landmarks on the city's skyline. Its height and hilltop setting make it a useful orientation point when exploring the west side of Baku.

The tower has had a rotating restaurant high above the city, so check current access before going specifically for the view.
