---
title: "Day Trips"
type: section
---

The Absheron Peninsula immediately around Baku is the natural focus for day trips. The fire-and-water combination of [Yanar Dag](/europe/azerbaijan/baku/yanar_dag), the [Ateshgah Fire Temple](/europe/azerbaijan/baku/ateshgah), and the working oil field at [Bibi-Heybat](/europe/azerbaijan/baku/bibi_heybat_mosque) makes a single excellent half-day.

A longer trip south takes in the prehistoric rock engravings at [Gobustan](/europe/azerbaijan/baku/gobustan_petroglyphs) and the [mud volcanoes](/europe/azerbaijan/baku/mud_volcanoes) nearby. To the north, the towns of [Quba](/europe/azerbaijan/quba) and the historic Mountain Jewish village of [Krasnaya Sloboda](/europe/azerbaijan/krasnayasloboda) make a longer day trip or an overnight; the Caspian beach resort of [Mardakan](/europe/azerbaijan/mardakan) is a closer escape in summer.
