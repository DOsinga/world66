The International Mugham Center celebrates mugham, Azerbaijan's classical vocal and instrumental tradition. The building itself is part of the appeal, shaped with references to the tar, the long-necked instrument central to the music. Come for a concert if the timing works; otherwise it is an easy cultural stop near Baku Boulevard and the waterfront museums.
