The National Art Museum of Azerbaijan occupies two adjacent historic buildings and holds a large collection of Azerbaijani, European, Russian, and Eastern art. Permanent displays spread across dozens of rooms, from painting and sculpture to miniatures, ceramics, metalwork, and textiles.
