The Stone Museum occupies a former industrial building near Baku's waterfront. Its displays focus on stone carvings, grave markers, and replicas from Gobustan and other sites around Azerbaijan.

It is a small stop, but a useful one if you are interested in the region's rock art and carved-stone traditions. Pair it with the nearby Carpet Museum or Baku Boulevard rather than treating it as a stand-alone half-day sight.
