Teze Bey Hamami is a traditional bathhouse that keeps Baku's hamam culture visible amid the city's modern hotels and restored old-town facades. The appeal is cultural as much as architectural: bathing rituals are part of everyday urban history in the region.

Because it is a working bathhouse, visitors should check arrangements and etiquette rather than treating it like an open museum. It is most worthwhile for travellers interested in living traditions and historic urban services.
