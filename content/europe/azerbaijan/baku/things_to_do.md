title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Baku's sights split between two distinct cities. The first is the walled medieval Old City, [Icherisheher](/europe/azerbaijan/baku/icherisheher), where the [Maiden's Tower](/europe/azerbaijan/baku/maidenstower), the [Palace of the Shirvanshahs](/europe/azerbaijan/baku/palace_of_the_shirvanshahs), and a tangle of caravanserais and mosques fit inside an area you can walk across in fifteen minutes. The second is the oil-boom and post-Soviet city around it — Belle Époque mansions along Istiqlaliyyat Street, the Stalin-era seafront boulevard, and contemporary statements like the [Flame Towers](/europe/azerbaijan/baku/flame_towers) and the Zaha Hadid-designed [Heydar Aliyev Center](/europe/azerbaijan/baku/heydar_aliyev_center).

Allow at least three full days. The Absheron Peninsula day trips to [Yanar Dag](/europe/azerbaijan/baku/yanar_dag) and the [Ateshgah Fire Temple](/europe/azerbaijan/baku/ateshgah) tie the city to its ancient association with petroleum and fire worship, and shouldn't be missed.
