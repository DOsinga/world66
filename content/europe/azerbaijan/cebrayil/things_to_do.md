title: Things to Do
type: section
---

Cebrayil remains largely under reconstruction following the Nagorno-Karabakh conflicts. The district's pre-war heritage is scattered across the landscape: medieval bridges on the Araz river, an ancient tree, and the ruins of watchtowers in the surrounding villages. Access to some sites may be restricted due to ongoing reconstruction and mine clearance operations — check with local authorities before venturing off main roads.
