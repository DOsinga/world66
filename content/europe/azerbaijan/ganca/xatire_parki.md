Xatire Parki is a large memorial park complex in Ganca. Its broad grounds include formal paths, monuments, and ceremonial spaces, making it more of a planned civic landmark than a simple neighborhood park.

The park is best visited as part of a wider look at Ganca's monuments and public spaces.
