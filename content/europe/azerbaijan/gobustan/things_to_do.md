title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Gobustan offers two extraordinary natural spectacles within a few kilometres of each other: 6,000 years of rock art carved into sandstone boulders, and a field of active mud volcanoes that make the landscape look like something from another planet. The Gobustan State Historical and Cultural Reserve is the main site; the mud volcanoes lie about 7 km to the south near the road to Alat. Both can be visited in a half-day from Baku.
