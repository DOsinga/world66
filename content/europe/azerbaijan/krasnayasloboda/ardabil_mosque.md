Ardabil Mosque is a 19th-century mosque in Krasnaya Sloboda, built by people from Ardabil in Iranian Azerbaijan. Its brick form and painted exterior make it one of the more distinctive religious buildings in the settlement, and it is an easy stop while walking between Krasnaya Sloboda and Quba.
