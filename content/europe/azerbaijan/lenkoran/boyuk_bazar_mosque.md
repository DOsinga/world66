Boyuk Bazar Mosque is one of Lenkoran's central historic mosques, standing close to the older commercial core of the city. It is not large by regional standards, but its red-brick massing and market setting make it one of the more atmospheric buildings in town.

Visit respectfully and keep expectations grounded. This is a working religious site rather than a museum, and the reward is in seeing how the old bazaar city still peeks through the modern one.
