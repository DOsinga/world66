Lenkoran's Caspian seafront is low-key, humid, and more local than resort-like. The beaches and promenades here are not the polished face of Azerbaijan, but they are useful for understanding the city's climate and mood.

Come in the evening, when the heat drops and families drift toward the water. The southern Caspian does not have the drama of the mountains inland, but the combination of sea air and tea-country greenness is what makes Lenkoran feel distinct.
