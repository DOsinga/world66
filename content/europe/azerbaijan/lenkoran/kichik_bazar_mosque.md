Kichik Bazar Mosque, the "Small Bazaar Mosque", is another reminder of Lenkoran's old trade quarters. Its scale is modest, but together with Boyuk Bazar Mosque it helps mark the geography of the pre-Soviet town.

The mosque is most interesting as part of a walk rather than as a single destination. Combine it with the central streets and the Caspian side of town to get a sense of how Lenkoran grew between bazaar, fortress, and sea.
