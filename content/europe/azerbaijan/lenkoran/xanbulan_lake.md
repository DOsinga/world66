Xanbulan Lake, also called Khanbulan Reservoir, sits in wooded foothills outside Lenkoran. It is a simple nature stop rather than a formal resort: bring food, walk the quiet edges of the reservoir, and use it as an easy escape from the humid Caspian lowlands.
