The stretch of waterfront along the Kura River through the town centre is Mingechevir's most enjoyable area for an evening walk. Lined with tea houses, small restaurants, and benches overlooking the water, it's where locals gather in the cooler hours after the summer heat. The Kura here is wide and brown, flowing steadily east toward the Caspian.

The promenade connects to the reservoir parkland nearby, and the combination makes for a pleasant hour's walk. Restaurants along the waterfront serve grilled meats and river fish; the shashlik and sturgeon dishes are regional specialties. The views across the water at sunset are genuinely good.
