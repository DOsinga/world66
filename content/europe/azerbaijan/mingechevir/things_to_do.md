---
title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Mingechevir's appeal is tied to its water: the [Mingechevir Reservoir](mingechevir_reservoir), one of the largest artificial lakes in the Caucasus, offers swimming and boating, and the lakeside park is the most pleasant place in town. The [Mingechevir Dam](mingechevir_dam) itself, completed in 1953, is an engineering landmark worth seeing. The town's [Archaeological Museum](archaeological_museum) houses finds from the extraordinarily rich pre-Christian sites that were excavated — and partly submerged — when the reservoir was created. [Mount Bozdağ](mount_bozdag), about ten kilometres south, makes a good half-day excursion with views across the reservoir and valley.
