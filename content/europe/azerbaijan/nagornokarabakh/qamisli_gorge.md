Qamisli Gorge is a steep river gorge in the mountains north of Qamisli village. The appeal is the depth of the cut through the landscape and the remote mountain road that follows it.

It is not a polished visitor site, but it is a worthwhile scenic stop for travellers already moving through this part of Nagorno-Karabakh with suitable transport.
