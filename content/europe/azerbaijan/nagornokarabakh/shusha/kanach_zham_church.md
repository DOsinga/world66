Kanach Zham, often translated as the Green Chapel, is one of Shusha's Armenian church landmarks. Its damaged condition and contested setting make it a sensitive site, but also an important one for understanding the city's religious and cultural layers.

Visitors should approach it with care and current local guidance. As a POI, its value is historical and contextual rather than recreational, especially alongside Shusha's larger cathedral and fortress landscape.
