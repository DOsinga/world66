---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

Khankendi sits at around 850 metres elevation in the Lesser Caucasus mountains, which gives it a continental climate with genuine seasons. Summers are warm and dry, with temperatures in the mid-20s Celsius — the most comfortable time for travel. Spring and autumn are mild and can be very pleasant, with green hillsides in spring and the gold of oak forests in autumn.

Winters are cold, with snowfall likely from December through February. The mountain roads can become difficult in poor weather.

The current political situation affects the practical question of when to go at least as much as the weather does. Verify access conditions before planning a trip, regardless of the season.
