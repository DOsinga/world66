---

Julfa's appeal is almost entirely historical. The [ruins of old Julfa (Cuga)](/europe/azerbaijan/naxcivan/culfa/old_julfa_ruins) and its surroundings contain Armenian churches, a medieval caravanserai, and burial monuments that tell the story of a once-great trading city. North of town, the [Alinjagala fortress](/europe/azerbaijan/naxcivan/culfa/alinjagala_fortress) offers one of the best castle hikes in Nakhchivan. The Julfa Regional Museum on Khagani Street collects archaeological finds from the area.
