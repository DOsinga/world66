---
title: "Eating Out"
type: section
---

Ordubad has a small but authentic local dining scene centred on traditional Azerbaijani food. Expect the kind of cooking found across Nakhchivan — lamb-heavy dishes, flatbreads from a clay oven, soups, and grilled meats — served simply in family-run spots around the town centre. The town's agricultural setting means fresh produce features strongly: pomegranates appear in sauces and salads in season, and the local apricots, figs, and quinces make their way into home cooking. Sit-down restaurants are few; teahouses serve food alongside their main business.
