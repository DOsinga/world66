Shahbuz is a small mountain district in the Nakhchivan Autonomous Republic, bordering Armenia to the north and east. The landscape is the main draw: the high peaks of the Zangazur and Daralayaz ranges rise to over 3,000 metres, the river valleys are cultivated and green, and the area has barely any tourism infrastructure.

The district has been inhabited since at least the 3rd millennium BC. Excavations at Saridere, Agbulag, Kuku, and other sites have yielded coins, pottery, and grave goods spanning the Bronze Age through the medieval period. Several ancient watchtowers remain in the landscape, and the medicinal mineral springs at Badamli, Batabat, Bichenek, and Karvansara draw visitors from across Nakhchivan.
