A low, broad mound on the left bank of the Arpa river just north of the village of Dizə, a few kilometres east of Sharur. Excavations since 2006 have shown that the site was occupied through much of the Chalcolithic and Bronze Age — over five millennia of continuous use, with mud-brick houses, kilns, and one of the earliest copper workshops in the region.

The visible remains are modest — a fenced excavation area and a few interpretive panels — but the site is of real importance to anyone interested in the prehistory of the Caucasus.
