---
title: Getting There
type: section
---

Shusha is reached from Baku by road. The drive is about 350 km — six to seven hours through the lowlands of central Azerbaijan and then up the new highway built since the 2020 war via Fuzuli to Khankendi (Stepanakert) and on into the mountains. There is also a new airport at Fuzuli, an hour's drive away, that handles a handful of flights from Baku.

Access to the Karabakh region remains restricted to organised tour groups and visitors with prior authorisation from the Azerbaijani government. Independent overland travel is not possible. Check with a registered Azerbaijani operator before planning a visit.
