title: Things to Do
type: section
---

Shusha is a compact highland town where almost every building is a monument of some kind — mosques, mansions of nineteenth-century khanate notables, and the great gates of the fortress walls. The Armenian and Azerbaijani communities each invested the place with meaning, and the layers overlap visibly. After the 2020 war the city is being rebuilt by Azerbaijan, and many sites are either restored, ruined, or in scaffolding depending on when you visit.
