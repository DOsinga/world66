---
title: When to Go
type: section
---

Spring (April to June) is the best time to visit the Qax area. The mountain foothills are green, the Ilisu waterfall is running at full volume, and temperatures are pleasant — typically 18–24°C in the valleys. July and August are warm to hot in the lower town but still comfortable in Ilisu and the higher valleys.

Autumn (September to October) is also good: clear skies, cooler air, and the forest above Ilisu turning colour. Winter brings cold and occasional snow, which makes Ilisu atmospheric but also harder to reach if the road ices over.

There are no major festivals in Qax itself that would drive a visit, but if you are in the region during Novruz (late March), you will see the holiday celebrated with the same warmth as anywhere in Azerbaijan.
