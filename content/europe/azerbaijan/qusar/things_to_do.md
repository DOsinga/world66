Qusar's appeal is its landscape rather than its monuments. The [Shahdag Mountain Resort](/europe/azerbaijan/qusar/shahdag_resort) is the main draw for most visitors — year-round, winter for skiing and summer for hiking — but the surrounding highlands hold genuine rewards for those who explore further. The [Khazra Mausoleum](/europe/azerbaijan/qusar/khazra_mausoleum), a rare 15th-century Safavid structure in the village of Khazra, is the most significant historical site near the town. The [Laza Valley](/europe/azerbaijan/qusar/laza_valley) offers waterfalls, alpine meadows, and mountain views, and the [Gusarchai Waterfalls](/europe/azerbaijan/qusar/gusarchai_waterfalls) are accessible on a half-day excursion from town.
