The Albanian Church of Kish is a small but important historic church in the village of Kish, a short trip from Sheki. Local tradition links the site to the apostle Eliseus, and excavations in the early 2000s uncovered earlier layers around the church.

It is one of the clearest day trips from Sheki: compact, old, and set in a village that still feels distinct from the city below.
