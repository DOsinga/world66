Gelersen Goresen is a ruined hilltop fortress north of Kish village, built by a Sheki khan to resist Persian attacks. The remains are rough, but the setting is the reason to go: a steep walk into the foothills, views over the valley, and a picnic-worthy hilltop once you reach the ruins.

Reach Kish first, then continue on foot for the final climb.
