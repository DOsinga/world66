Juma Mosque is one of Sheki's handsome old religious buildings, with a slender minaret rising above the surrounding streets. The mosque dates from the mid-18th century and is known for its geometric decoration.

It is a short, atmospheric stop rather than a large monument. Visit while walking through the old town, and be respectful around prayer times.
