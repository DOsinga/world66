---
title: Getting There
type: section
---

Most travellers approach Yardimli from Masally, following the road west into the Talysh Mountains. The distance is not great, but the road is winding and slower than the map suggests.

Shared taxis and local buses may run from Masally, though departures can be irregular. A private car gives you far more freedom to stop at viewpoints and villages along the way.
