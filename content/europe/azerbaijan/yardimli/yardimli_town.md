Yardimli town is a modest administrative centre rather than a polished destination. Its value is practical: food, transport, a place to ask about road conditions, and a sense of everyday life in the Talysh foothills.

Walk the centre if you are already here, but do not build a whole day around it. The town works best as a base or pause point for the mountain road and the surrounding villages.
