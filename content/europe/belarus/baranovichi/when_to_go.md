---
title: When to Go
type: section
---

Baranovichi sits in the heart of the Belarusian plain, which means cold winters (temperatures regularly dropping below -10°C in January) and warm, sometimes humid summers. Spring and early summer — May through June — are the most pleasant months: mild temperatures, the surrounding forests green, and the days long. Autumn is also agreeable, with the birch and oak forests turning gold in October.

If your reason for visiting is the Belovezhskaya Pushcha forest to the south, note that the forest is open year-round. Winter visits have a certain bare, austere beauty — European bison are easier to spot against the snow — though road conditions can be challenging.
