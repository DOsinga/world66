---
title: "Getting There"
type: section
---

Gomel has a small airport with flights to Minsk and occasional international routes. The main connection is by rail: express trains from Minsk take about 3.5 hours. Buses run from Minsk, Brest, and several Ukrainian cities, though cross-border travel should be checked against current political and visa conditions. The train station is in the city centre, a short walk or taxi ride from the palace park.
