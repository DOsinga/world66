---

The [Kamianiec Tower](/europe/belarus/kamianiec_litosk/kamianiec_tower) is the reason to come, and it is genuinely impressive. The surrounding town is small and quiet; a visit here combines naturally with a day trip to [Belavezhskaya Pushcha](/europe/belarus/kamianiec_litosk/belavezhskaya_pushcha), Europe's last primeval forest, just 20 km to the south.
