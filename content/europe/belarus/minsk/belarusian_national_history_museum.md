The Belarusian National History Museum is one of Minsk’s core cultural stops, with collections that run from archaeology and traditional culture to the complicated modern story of Belarus. It helps put the city’s monumental Soviet avenues into a longer national frame.

Exhibits can be uneven for non-Russian or non-Belarusian speakers, but the objects are strong enough to reward patient visitors. Pair it with the National Art Museum or a walk along Independence Avenue for a fuller sense of how Minsk presents its past.
