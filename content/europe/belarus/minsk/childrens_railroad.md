Minsk's Children's Railroad is a narrow-gauge loop through Chelyuskinites Park, with small stations and trains traditionally operated as a training railway for young rail enthusiasts.

It is most appealing for families or anyone interested in Soviet-era civic curiosities. Pair it with the nearby Botanical Garden or Museum of Stones rather than crossing town for it alone.
