Loshitsa Manor is a 19th-century estate on the southeastern edge of Minsk, surrounded by a large park of old trees along the Loshitsa River. The manor house itself has been restored and now operates as a museum of pre-Revolutionary Belarusian noble life — furniture, portraits, and household objects from the late imperial period that rarely get displayed anywhere because so few such houses survived the upheavals of the 20th century.

The park is the main draw for most Minsk residents: 100 hectares of mature trees, riverbanks, and old orchard land that feel remarkably removed from the urban surroundings. In spring the apple trees blossom and the park fills with Minsk families. In autumn the colours are excellent.

The estate is a 20-minute drive or a longer bus ride south of the city centre. Check opening hours in advance as they vary seasonally. Loshitsa Park, Loshitskaya St.
