The open-air museum at Ozertso gathers wooden houses, farm buildings, churches, tools,
and household objects to show rural Belarusian life outside the capital's Soviet and
modern layers. The setting lets you walk through vernacular architecture at full scale
rather than seeing it compressed into display cases.

It is one of the most useful Minsk excursions for understanding the countryside that
shaped much of Belarusian culture. Allow time for the ride out and back, and visit in
decent weather because the museum is spread outdoors.
