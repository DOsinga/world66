Stalin's Line is an open-air military history complex west of Minsk, built around restored bunkers and field defences from the interwar Soviet fortified line. Tanks, artillery, trenches, and reconstructed positions give it the feel of a large outdoor hardware museum rather than a conventional memorial.

It is a specialised day trip, best for travellers interested in Soviet history, military engineering, or the Second World War landscape around Belarus. The site is outside the city, so plan transport carefully and expect a very different tone from Minsk's urban museums and memorials.
