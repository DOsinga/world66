---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

Minsk has a continental climate with cold winters and warm summers. The city is liveable in most seasons, though the extremes are real.

**Summer (June to August)** is the most comfortable time to visit. Temperatures typically reach 20–25°C, the parks and riverbanks are full of life, outdoor cafe terraces open, and the city puts on its most welcoming face. This is when Minsk is most sociable and most photogenic.

**Spring (April–May)** brings the bloom of the city's many parks and the Loshitsa apple orchards. The light is good and crowds are thin. May is particularly pleasant.

**Autumn (September–October)** is another good window — cooling temperatures, golden foliage in the parks, and the start of the cultural season (theatre, concerts, exhibitions).

**Winter** can be harsh — temperatures drop well below zero and daylight is short. However, Minsk under snow has a certain bleak grandeur, and January and February are when you'll have the monumental avenues largely to yourself. The metro and indoor attractions remain fully operational.

Note that Belarus requires visas for most nationalities. Check visa requirements carefully before booking, and note that the political situation affects travel insurance and official travel advisories for many countries.
