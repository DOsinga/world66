Mogilev Regional Drama Theatre is one of the centre's most characterful secular buildings, a red-brick theatre with decorative white details and a strong late-19th-century presence. It stands near the old heart of town, where a short walk can link the theatre, cathedral, and town hall.

Even if you do not attend a performance, the exterior is worth seeing. It is the kind of public building that gives Mogilev texture: neither grand capital architecture nor village survival, but a confident regional city statement.
