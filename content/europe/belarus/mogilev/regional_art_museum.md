The Mogilev Regional Art Museum occupies an early-20th-century former bank building, which is part of the draw. The architecture mixes late classicism and decorative historicist details, a reminder that Mogilev had a prosperous civic life before Soviet industry came to dominate its image.

The collection focuses on Belarusian and regional art. It is not a blockbuster museum, but it gives a useful counterweight to the city's churches and war memorials.
