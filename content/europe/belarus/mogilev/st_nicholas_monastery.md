St Nicholas Monastery is the strongest Orthodox monument in Mogilev, preserving a 17th-century cathedral, iconostasis, bell tower, walls, and gates. It sits slightly apart from the main civic axis, which gives it a calmer atmosphere than the central square.

The complex is still religious, not just historic. Dress and behave accordingly, and give yourself time to look at the ensemble rather than only the main church.
