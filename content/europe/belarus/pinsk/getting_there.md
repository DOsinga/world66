---
title: Getting There
type: section
---

Pinsk is best reached by train or bus from [Brest](/europe/belarus/brest), [Minsk](/europe/belarus/minsk), or other towns in Brest Region. The railway station is east of the old centre; local buses and taxis connect it with Lenin Square and the riverfront, though the central sights are close enough to walk once you are in town.
