St Alexander Nevsky Church is a small wooden Orthodox church near the river in Vitebsk, close to the older Annunciation Church. Its scale is modest, but the timber construction and bright ecclesiastical details make it a warm contrast to the city's heavier stone and Soviet-era buildings.

It is worth seeing as part of a riverfront walk rather than as a major destination on its own. The church helps show Vitebsk's religious landscape at a smaller scale, and its position near other historic sites makes it easy to include without a detour.
