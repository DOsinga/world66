---
title: Getting There
type: section
---

Aalst is on the main railway between Brussels and Ghent. Trains run every 15–30 minutes and the trip takes about 25 minutes from either city. The station is a 10-minute walk north of the Grote Markt.

By car, Aalst sits on the E40 motorway between Brussels and Ghent; the centre has a low-emission zone, so check whether your vehicle qualifies before driving in.
