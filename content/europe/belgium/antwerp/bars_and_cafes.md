---
title: "Bars and Cafes"
type: section
---

Antwerp's drinking culture runs deep and broad — De Koninck Bolleke is the local everyday beer, but the Trappists and lambic specialists are equally well represented, and the city has a strong cocktail and natural wine scene running alongside.

The classic Antwerp bar experience is sitting on the Grote Markt or Groenplaats with a Bolleke, but the best places sit just off the main squares. [Kulminator](/europe/belgium/antwerp/kulminator) has 700+ Belgian beers with aged Trappist vintages going back decades. [Café Beveren](/europe/belgium/antwerp/cafe_beveren) houses a working 1937 mechanical dance organ that plays for a one-euro coin. [Het Elfde Gebod](/europe/belgium/antwerp/elfde_gebod), behind the Cathedral, is packed wall-to-ceiling with religious statuary. The brewery itself, [De Koninck](/europe/belgium/antwerp/de_koninck_brewery), runs tours and tastings on the south side of town.

For coffee and cake, the small cafés around Vrijdagmarkt and Hendrik Conscience Square are reliable. The fashion-design quarter on Nationalestraat has a younger, more design-forward set of places.
