Carolus Borromeus Church is one of Antwerp's great Baroque interiors, even though its exterior gives little away. The decoration was closely tied to Rubens' studio, and the church's setting on Conscienceplein makes it a good stop between the cathedral, the old centre, and the nearby historic house museums.
