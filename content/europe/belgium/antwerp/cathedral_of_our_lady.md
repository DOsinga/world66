The largest Gothic church in the Low Countries and the dominant feature of Antwerp's skyline — the northern tower reaches 123 metres, and a single stair of 514 steps leads to the top. The southern tower was never completed; the asymmetry is one of the building's distinguishing marks.

Inside, the cathedral holds four of Rubens's greatest paintings: the *Raising of the Cross* and *Descent from the Cross* (1610–14) on the side altars, *Assumption of the Virgin* on the high altar, and *Resurrection of Christ* in the Moretus chapel. The *Descent* in particular is one of the supreme Baroque paintings of any city. The light through the medieval stained glass and the seventeenth-century black-and-white marble floor are both worth time. Climb the tower if it is open — the queue moves slowly but the panoramic view is the best in the city.
