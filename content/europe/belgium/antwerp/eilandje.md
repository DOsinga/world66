The "Little Island" — the area immediately north of the historic centre, around the old Bonaparte Dock and Willem Dock, redeveloped from the 1990s as a residential and cultural quarter. The **MAS** museum is the showpiece building. The **Red Star Line Museum**, in restored docks where two million emigrants boarded ships for America between 1873 and 1934, is the other major institution.

A weekly farmers' market runs at the Bonapartdok, and the area along the quays has a growing concentration of bars, restaurants, and boutique shops. The walk up from the centre takes about 25 minutes through quiet streets; tram 7 from Groenplaats also runs there.
