Extra City Kunsthal is a contemporary visual-arts space in Antwerp, known for experimental exhibitions and projects. It is more suited to visitors who actively seek out current art than to anyone looking for a general city museum.

Check the current programme before going, as the value of a visit depends heavily on the exhibition schedule.
