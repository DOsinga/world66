---
title: "Getting There"
type: section
---

**By train** is the easiest way to reach Antwerp. The city's [Central Station](/europe/belgium/antwerp/central_station) is one of the most beautiful in Europe and a major hub. Frequent trains run to **Brussels** (40 min), **Bruges** (90 min), **Ghent** (50 min), and **Amsterdam** (1 hr 10 min by Thalys). The Eurostar from London and Paris arrives via Brussels Midi with an easy connection.

**By plane**, Brussels Airport is the main option — about 45 minutes by direct train. The smaller Antwerp Airport (ANR) at Deurne handles a few short-haul connections, useful for trips to London City and the Mediterranean. Most international visitors use Brussels.

**By car**, Antwerp sits at the junction of the E19 (Amsterdam–Brussels) and the E17 (Lille–Ghent–Antwerp). Driving into the centre is best avoided — parking is expensive and the streets are restricted. Use the park-and-ride at Linkeroever or one of the perimeter car parks, then take a tram or walk in. The whole inner city is a low-emission zone.
