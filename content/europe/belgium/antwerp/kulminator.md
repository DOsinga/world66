A small, cluttered bar near the Stadspark with what is widely regarded as the deepest Belgian beer list in the country. Around 700 different beers on the printed list, including aged vintages of Trappists and other classics going back decades. The owners ring a bell at the door — tell them you have come to taste beer, and you will be let in.

The interior is famously chaotic — every surface stacked with empty bottles, papers, knick-knacks. The crowd is mostly beer geeks; the service is slow and gruff but knowledgeable. No food beyond cheese plates and small snacks. Cash strongly preferred. Closed Sundays.
