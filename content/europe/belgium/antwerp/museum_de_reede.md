Museum De Reede is a small art museum near the Scheldt devoted to graphic work. Its permanent collection includes pieces by Francisco Goya, Félicien Rops, and Edvard Munch, with temporary exhibitions adding variety.

It is compact enough for a focused visit and pairs easily with Het Steen, the old riverfront, and Antwerp's cathedral quarter.
