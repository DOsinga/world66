Romi Goldmuntz Synagogue is one of Antwerp's major Jewish landmarks, connected to the community that helped shape the city's diamond district. Its scale and history make it more than a neighbourhood prayer house, even if it is not a conventional open-door attraction.

Security and access may be limited, so plan on viewing the exterior unless you have arranged a visit. It is still a meaningful stop for understanding Antwerp beyond the cathedral, port, and fashion quarter.
