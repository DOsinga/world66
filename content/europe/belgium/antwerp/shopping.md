---
title: "Shopping"
type: section
---

The main commercial strip is the **Meir**, the pedestrianised avenue running from Central Station to the Cathedral. Mainstream brands and the larger department stores (Inno, Hema) are here, alongside several restored historic palaces — the Meir Palace was Napoleon's lodging during his 1810 visit.

For Belgian fashion, the **Nationalestraat** and **Kammenstraat** area south of the centre is the only address that matters. The Antwerp Six shops — Dries Van Noten's Modepaleis, Walter Van Beirendonck's Walter, Ann Demeulemeester — are all here, along with younger independent designers.

For diamonds, **Pelikaanstraat** behind the Central Station is lined with retail diamond shops. Negotiate aggressively if you actually want to buy. The DIVA museum has the official certification information.

The **Saturday Vogelenmarkt** at Theaterplein is the city's traditional flea market — antiques, books, vinyl, secondhand clothes — open every Saturday morning. The **Friday market** at Vrijdagmarkt is for plants, household goods, and small antiques.

For chocolate, **Del Rey** on Appelmansstraat is the city's serious chocolatier — better than most of the famous Brussels names.
