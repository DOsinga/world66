Antwerp gives you four overlapping cities to walk through — Rubens's Baroque Catholic city around the [Cathedral](/europe/belgium/antwerp/cathedral_of_our_lady) and the [Grote Markt](/europe/belgium/antwerp/grotemarkt); the merchants' Renaissance city of the [Plantin-Moretus](/europe/belgium/antwerp/plantin_moretus) workshop and the guild houses; the modern docks city around the [MAS](/europe/belgium/antwerp/mas), the [Red Star Line Museum](/europe/belgium/antwerp/red_star_line), and the new [Eilandje](/europe/belgium/antwerp/eilandje) quarter; and the fashion city around [MoMu](/europe/belgium/antwerp/momu) and the Royal Academy.

For a single day, the priority circuit runs: Cathedral, Grote Markt, lunch on Vrijdagmarkt, Plantin-Moretus, walk along the Scheldt to the [MAS](/europe/belgium/antwerp/mas) for the free roof terrace at sunset. For two days, add the [KMSKA](/europe/belgium/antwerp/kmska) and [M HKA](/europe/belgium/antwerp/muhka) in Het Zuid, the [Rubenshuis](/europe/belgium/antwerp/rubenshuis), and an evening in [Zurenborg](/europe/belgium/antwerp/zurenborg). Three days lets you add the [Diamond District](/europe/belgium/antwerp/diamond_district), a tram ride to [Cogels-Osylei](/europe/belgium/antwerp/cogelsosylei), and a beer tour at [De Koninck](/europe/belgium/antwerp/de_koninck_brewery).

The most local way to spend an afternoon is "**een terraske doen**" — sitting on a café terrace on the Grote Markt or Groenplaats with a Bolleke, watching the city pass.
