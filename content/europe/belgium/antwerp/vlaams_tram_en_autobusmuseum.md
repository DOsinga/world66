The Vlaams Tram- en Autobusmuseum fills a former tram depot with historic vehicles from Antwerp and the wider Flemish public transport network. The collection includes early electric trams, an open tram, a steam tram and the rare 1950s Gyrobus.

It is a specialist museum, but a good one if you like industrial heritage or want a different angle on Antwerp beyond the old centre. Check opening times before crossing town, as access is more limited than at the larger city museums.
