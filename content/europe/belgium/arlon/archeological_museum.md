Arlon's Archeological Museum is the town's strongest cultural sight, built around a rich collection of Roman and Gallo-Roman material from the surrounding region. The highlight is the stone sculpture: funerary monuments, carved reliefs, and architectural fragments that show how prosperous this corner of Roman Gaul once was.

It is a compact museum, but a useful one if you are trying to understand why Arlon claims to be among Belgium's oldest towns. Pair it with a walk through the old centre and the remains of the Roman wall rather than treating it as a full-day museum stop.
