Arlon Synagogue is an important Jewish heritage landmark in the old town, notable both for its age and for the rarity of surviving historic synagogues in this part of Belgium. Its restrained exterior fits the street, but the building carries a much broader story of Jewish life in Luxembourg province.

It is a concise stop, usually appreciated from the outside unless access has been arranged, but it gives Arlon a heritage layer beyond Roman remains and churches. Combine it with a walk through the old centre and the hill around Saint-Donat.
