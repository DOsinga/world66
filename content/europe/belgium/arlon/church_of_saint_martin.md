The Church of Saint-Martin is Arlon's most visible landmark, a tall neo-Gothic church that gives the small city a surprisingly strong skyline. Its scale is part of the appeal: in a compact centre, the tower is hard to miss.

Inside, expect a working church rather than a museum. It is still worth a stop for the stained glass, the sense of space, and the way it anchors a walk through one of Belgium's oldest towns.
