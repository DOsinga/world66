Gaspar Museum gives Arlon a compact cultural stop, centred on local art, history, and the kind of civic collection that helps explain a town beyond its main square. It is not a blockbuster museum, but that is part of the point: the visit is manageable, specific, and rooted in the local story.

Allow about an hour and check current opening days before you go. Smaller museums in Belgian towns often reward visitors who slow down, read the labels, and use the collection as context for the streets outside rather than treating it as a quick bad-weather fallback.
