The Jupiter Column is a compact Roman survival in Arlon, a town whose ancient past is easy to overlook if you are only passing through. It points back to Orolaunum, the Roman settlement that once stood here near important routes through the Ardennes.

Treat it as one stop in Arlon's Roman trail rather than a major monument. Its value is cumulative: together with the archaeological museum and scattered remains, it shows that this quiet Belgian town has a much older urban story under the surface.
