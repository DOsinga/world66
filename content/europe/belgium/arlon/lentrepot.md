L'Entrepot is Arlon's main contemporary cultural venue, hosting concerts, theatre, comedy, and
youth-oriented events in a converted warehouse setting. In a small city with a limited list of major
sights, it is one of the better places to catch local cultural life after dark.

Its travel value depends entirely on the programme, so check the calendar before going. When
something is on, it gives Arlon visitors a reason to stay into the evening rather than treating the
town only as a daytime stop near the Luxembourg border.
