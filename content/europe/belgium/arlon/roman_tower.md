The Roman Tower is a small but telling piece of Arlon's claim to be one of Belgium's oldest towns. It is a fragment rather than a set-piece monument, but that is part of the appeal: Roman history appears here inside the modern street plan, not behind a grand archaeological gate.

Stop by while walking between the centre and the archaeological museum. The tower makes most sense as part of Arlon's Roman story, alongside the museum's sculpture and funerary finds, rather than as a standalone reason to come to town.
