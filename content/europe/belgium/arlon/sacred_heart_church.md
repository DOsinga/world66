Sacred Heart Church is a Roman Catholic parish church east of Arlon's centre, built in a Romanesque-Byzantine style at the end of the 19th century. It is less imposing than Saint-Martin, but the rounded forms and brickwork make it a worthwhile detour for architecture-minded walkers.

This is still a working neighbourhood church rather than a museum. Come for a quick look at the exterior and, if the doors are open, the calm interior.
