---
title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Bastogne's principal draw is its World War II history, and the town does it justice. The Bastogne War Museum is one of the best military history museums in Europe, and the Mardasson Memorial rises above the surrounding farmland as a visible reminder of the battle's scale. The town itself is compact and walkable, with the main square serving as a natural starting point for any visit.
