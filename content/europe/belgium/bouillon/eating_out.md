---
title: "Eating Out"
type: section
---

Bouillon has a range of restaurants catering mainly to Belgian and Dutch weekend visitors, so standards are reasonable and portions are generous. Ardennes specialities are the thing to eat: *sanglier* (wild boar) in season, smoked Ardennes ham, trout from the Semois, and *gibier* (game) in autumn. The local *bière artisanale* from the Ardennes breweries is excellent.

**Auberge d'Alsace** near the castle is a reliable choice for traditional Belgian cooking. The restaurant terrace above the river at **Hôtel de la Poste** is the best location for lunch in summer. Most restaurants in Bouillon serve similar menus; quality is generally high because the town depends on visitors returning year after year.

Tip: come on a weekday or book ahead for summer weekends, when the town fills with Belgian families.
