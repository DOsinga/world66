Choco-Story Museum tells the story of chocolate from cacao cultivation to Belgian pralines. The museum is light and family-friendly, with exhibits on trade, production, and chocolate-making techniques.

It is not one of Bruges' grand historic sights, but it makes a useful change of pace between churches, canals, and art museums. Demonstrations and tastings are the main draw for children and anyone curious about Belgium's chocolate culture.
