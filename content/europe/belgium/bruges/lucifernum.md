Lucifernum is a private art space in a former Freemasons' temple in Bruges, with a gothic-tinged garden and small cemetery adding to the atmosphere. It is an offbeat stop for visitors who have already seen the city's main churches, canals, and museums.

Check opening times before going, since access can be more limited than at Bruges' public museums. The draw is the setting as much as the art: old masonry, a theatrical interior, and a garden that feels far removed from the busy centre.
