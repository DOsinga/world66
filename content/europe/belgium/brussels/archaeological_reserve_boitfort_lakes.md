The Archaeological Reserve Boitfort Lakes protects one of the Sonian Forest's oldest known human sites. Finds here date back roughly 6,300 years, making it an unusually early preserved Neolithic landscape near Brussels.

The appeal is quiet rather than monumental. It works best as part of a walk through the forest, where the age of the place gives the woodland an extra layer.
