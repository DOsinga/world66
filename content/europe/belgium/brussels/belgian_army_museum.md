The Belgian Army Museum and Museum of Military History fills the north wing of the Cinquantenaire Palace with arms, uniforms, documents, artillery, tanks, and aircraft. Its collections trace Belgian military history and the conflicts fought on Belgian territory.

The aircraft hall is the standout for many visitors, with planes from the First World War onward. The museum is large and old-fashioned, so choose a section rather than trying to absorb everything in one pass.
