The Centre for Fine Arts — universally known as BOZAR — is a sprawling Art Deco complex designed by Victor Horta in the 1920s, cleverly fitted onto a steep slope so as not to block the view from the royal palace. It is Brussels's principal venue for classical concerts and hosts major touring art exhibitions in its warren of galleries.
