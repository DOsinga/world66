Brussels Beer Project is a small brewery in the Dansaert area that leans into experiments rather than old Belgian brewing ritual. Tours usually explain the project and the brewing process, then finish with a tasting of recent beers.

Book ahead, as tour groups are small. It works well if you want a modern contrast to Brussels' more traditional beer cafes.
