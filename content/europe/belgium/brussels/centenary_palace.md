The Centenary Palace is the grandest survivor of the exhibition halls built for the 1935 Brussels World's Fair. Hall 5 still anchors the Heysel exhibition complex, and its scale and ornament make it worth noticing if you are already visiting the Atomium or the northern fairgrounds.
