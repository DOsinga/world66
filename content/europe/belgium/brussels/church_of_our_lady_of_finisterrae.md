The Church of Our Lady of Finisterrae stands on Rue Neuve, Brussels' busiest shopping street, where its calm interior is a useful contrast to the crowds outside. The church grew from a 15th-century chapel and is now one of the more visible historic churches in the lower city.

Step in if you are walking between the shopping district and the centre; it is a small but worthwhile detour.
