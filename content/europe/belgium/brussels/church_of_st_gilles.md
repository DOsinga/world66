The Church of St. Gilles is a 19th-century parish church mixing Romanesque and Gothic Revival forms. It replaced earlier churches tied to the old village of Saint-Gilles and gives the district a strong architectural landmark just south of central Brussels. It is most useful as a stop while exploring Saint-Gilles, the Marolles, or the Horta Museum area.
