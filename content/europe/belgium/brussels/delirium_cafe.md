A cavernous beer hall down a side alley near the Galeries, Delirium holds a Guinness record for the number of beers it stocks — well over 2,000 at last count. Loud, sprawling, and unabashedly aimed at visitors, but the sheer range, from Trappist classics to obscure lambics, makes it a rite of passage.
