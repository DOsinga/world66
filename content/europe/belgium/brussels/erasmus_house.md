Erasmus House is one of Brussels' oldest Gothic houses and preserves the memory of Desiderius Erasmus, who stayed here in 1521. The museum combines period rooms, early printed works, paintings, sculptures, and a quiet garden, giving a compact introduction to the intellectual world of the Reformation.
