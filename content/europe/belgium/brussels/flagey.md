The hub of southern Ixelles, a streamlined 1930s broadcasting building — nicknamed 'the ocean liner' — now a busy cultural centre and concert hall. The surrounding Place Flagey, with its market and ring of cafés, is one of the best places in the city for an unhurried afternoon.
