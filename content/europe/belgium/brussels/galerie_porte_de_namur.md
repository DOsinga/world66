The covered passage linking Chaussée de Wavre to Chaussée d'Ixelles is the dense commercial core of Matongé — a warren of stalls selling wax-print cloth, wigs and hair products, African groceries, and music. Crowded, colourful, and the best place to feel the neighbourhood's energy.
