Het Zinneke is Tom Frantzen's bronze statue of a peeing dog, installed in 1998 as the canine counterpart to Brussels' better-known Manneken Pis and Jeanneke Pis.

The statue is a quick stop near Sainte-Catherine and the Lower Town, but it is a fun piece of Brussels street character. It was damaged by a car in 2015 and later restored by the artist.
