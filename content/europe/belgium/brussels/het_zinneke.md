Het Zinneke is Brussels' bronze peeing dog, a small street sculpture by Tom Frantzen and the cheekiest companion to Manneken Pis and Jeanneke Pis. It is easy to miss, which is part of the fun: a quick stop on a wander through the lower streets west of the Grand Place.

The name plays with the old Brussels word for mixed-breed dogs and with the Zenne, the buried river that once ran through the city. It is not grand art, but it is very Brussels.
