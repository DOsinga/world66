Jean Massart Botanical Garden is a 5-hectare botanical garden on Brussels' south-eastern edge, near the Red Monastery and Sonian Forest. Managed by ULB, it holds more than 2,000 plant varieties, including medicinal and aromatic collections, orchards and an arboretum. It pairs naturally with a walk in the surrounding forest.
