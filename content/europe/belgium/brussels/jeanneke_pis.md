Jeanneke Pis is the small bronze counterpart to Brussels' better-known Manneken Pis. The sculpture was installed in the 1980s in a narrow lane near the old centre and is now protected behind bars.

It is a quick stop rather than a major sight, but it adds a playful footnote to a walk around the Grand Place, Galeries Saint-Hubert, and the Delirium area.
