The Jewish Museum of Belgium presents Jewish culture, religion, folk art, and community history in Belgium. It is a focused museum rather than one of Brussels' large headline institutions, but it adds useful context to the city's layered religious and cultural history.

Its location near the Sablon makes it easy to combine with Notre-Dame du Sablon, Place du Grand Sablon, and the museums around the upper town.
