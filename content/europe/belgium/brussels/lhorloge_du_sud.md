Part restaurant, part bar, part cultural centre, L'Horloge du Sud is a Matongé institution for pan-African cooking — mafé, yassa, grilled tilapia — with live concerts, talks, and a terrace that fills on warm evenings. A good first stop for the neighbourhood.
