The Marc Sleen Museum focuses on one of Belgium's best-known comic artists, creator of the long-running Nero series. It works well as a companion to the Belgian Centre for Comic Strip Art nearby: smaller, more specific, and useful if you want to understand how deeply comic strips sit in Belgian popular culture.
