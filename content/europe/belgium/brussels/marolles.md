The Marolles is old, working-class Brussels — a tight grid of streets below the Palace of Justice that has kept its salty character despite decades of creeping gentrification. Its heart is the **Place du Jeu de Balle**, home to a flea market every morning since 1873: a sprawl of junk, *brocante*, and the occasional real find, busiest and best at dawn on Sunday. The surrounding **Rue Haute** and **Rue Blaes** are lined with antique dealers, vintage shops, and design stores.

This is the place to come for bric-à-brac, a strong coffee, and the local dialect — the *Marollien* — you may still hear in the cafés. The free public lift beside the Palace of Justice drops you here from the Sablon above, with a fine view over the lower city on the way down.
