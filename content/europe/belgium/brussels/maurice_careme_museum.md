The Maurice Careme Museum preserves the former home of the Walloon poet Maurice Careme, with furniture, artworks, and personal objects left to evoke the house as he knew it. It is a small literary museum, but one with a strong sense of atmosphere.

The visit suits travellers interested in Belgian letters or quiet house museums away from central Brussels. Check opening times before going, as access can be limited.
