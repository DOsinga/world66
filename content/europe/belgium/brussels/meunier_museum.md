The Meunier Museum occupies the former home and studio of Constantin Meunier, the Belgian artist known for his sculptures and paintings of miners, dockers, and industrial workers. The collection gives a compact look at his role in late 19th-century social realism, with the house itself adding atmosphere to the visit.
