This Laeken complex near the Atomium brings together a Japanese tower, a Chinese pavilion, and a museum of Japanese art. The buildings are theatrical, but the collections give them substance: Chinese export porcelain, Japanese decorative arts, and rotating displays tied to the Edo period.

It is best treated as part of a northern Brussels day with the Atomium and nearby parks, rather than as a quick central-museum stop.
