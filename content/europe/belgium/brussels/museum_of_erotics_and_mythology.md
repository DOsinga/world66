The Museum of Erotics and Mythology is a small private museum near the Sablon, housed in an 18th-century townhouse. Its collection covers erotic art and objects from antiquity to modern prints, sculptures, ivory, and curiosities, so it is a niche but distinctive stop for art-minded visitors.
