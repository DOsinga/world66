The Museum of Human Anatomy and Embryology is a specialist medical museum on the French-speaking university's medical campus in Brussels. Its collection includes anatomical preparations, embryos, fetuses, organs preserved in alcohol, wax and plaster models, and displays on pathology and bone disease.

It is a niche museum, but worthwhile for visitors with a serious interest in anatomy, medical history, or teaching collections. Access and guided tours in English or French are usually by request.
