The Museum of Original Figurines, often shortened to MOOF, is a small museum for comic-book fans near the Grand-Place. Its displays turn Belgian comics into three-dimensional scenes, with figurines of familiar local characters alongside French and American figures.

It is more specialised than the larger Belgian Comic Strip Center, but that is part of the appeal. Come for a quick, playful stop if you like comics, model-making, or need a rainy-day museum that works for children as well as adults.
