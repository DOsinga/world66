The Natural Sciences Museum of Belgium is best known for its Bernissart iguanodons, one of Europe's great dinosaur displays. The museum also covers geology, biodiversity, evolution, mammals, and the small animals that live in and around human homes, with enough hands-on material to make it a strong family stop in the European Quarter.
