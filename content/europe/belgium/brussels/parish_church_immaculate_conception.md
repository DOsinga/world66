The Parish Church of the Immaculate Conception stands by Place du Jeu de Balle in the Marolles. Built between 1854 and 1862 for the Capuchins, it has an Italian neo-Romanesque exterior and a 19th-century interior with an oak altar and fittings by Belgian artists and craftsmen.
