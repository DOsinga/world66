Poelaert Square sits beside the huge Palace of Justice, on the edge between upper and lower Brussels. The square itself is broad and plain, but the reason to come is the view: from here the city drops away below you, giving one of central Brussels' best open panoramas.

It is an easy stop when walking between the Sablon and the Marolles.
