Belgium's national art collection fills a vast complex off the Place Royale, spanning the Oldmasters Museum and the Fin-de-Siècle Museum (the separate Magritte Museum shares the campus). The Flemish Primitives and the Bruegels are its heart — Pieter Bruegel the Elder's *Census at Bethlehem* and *Fall of Icarus* among them — backed by Rubens, Jordaens, and a strong 19th-century holding. Allow at least half a day.
