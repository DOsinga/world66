Saint-Cyr House is a narrow Art Nouveau townhouse designed by Gustave Strauven for the painter Georges de Saint-Cyr. The facade is the draw: an ornate, highly vertical composition of ironwork, stone, and glass that rewards a short detour near Square Ambiorix.
