The Salesian Monastery is a quiet religious complex east of central Brussels, tied to an order founded in the early 17th century by Jeanne de Chantal and Francis de Sales. Its interest is mostly architectural and historical, away from the city's better-known churches and museums.

It is a modest detour, not a first-time Brussels essential. Add it if you are already exploring the eastern suburbs or have a particular interest in religious architecture.
