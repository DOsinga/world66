Schaerbeek Beer Museum is a small volunteer-run museum devoted to Belgian brewing culture. Its collection includes thousands of bottles, matching glasses, brewing equipment, copper vessels, trays, signs, and advertising objects, with a tavern area where visitors can try selected beers.

It is not one of Brussels' major museums, but it is a worthwhile specialist stop for beer enthusiasts.
