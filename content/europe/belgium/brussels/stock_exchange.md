The Brussels Stock Exchange is a grand 19th-century market building between the Grand-Place and Sainte-Catherine. Even when the interior is not the focus of a visit, the broad steps, sculpted facade, and busy pedestrian setting make it one of the useful landmarks in the centre.

It works best as a quick architectural stop while walking between the Grand-Place, the Bourse area, and the restaurants around Sainte-Catherine.
