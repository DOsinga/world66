The Town Hall of Schaarbeek is a richly detailed civic landmark in Flemish neo-Renaissance style, built in the 1880s and rebuilt after a major fire in 1911. Its towers, brickwork, stained glass, and decorated interiors say a lot about the confidence of Brussels' fast-growing suburbs at the turn of the 20th century.
