The Van Buuren Museum preserves the 1928 villa of banker and art patron David van Buuren. The house is a polished mix of Amsterdam School architecture, Art Deco interiors, paintings, furniture, and landscaped gardens, making it one of Brussels' better small museums for design lovers.
