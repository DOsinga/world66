---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

Brussels works year-round but has two clear high points. **May to September** is the best weather, with long warm days and a full programme of outdoor events. The city is busiest in July and August, when it hosts the **Ommegang** (a historical pageant on the Grand-Place in July) and the **Flower Carpet** (biennial, August of even years — half a million begonias on the Grand-Place). Summer is also when the terrace culture takes over every square in the city.

**December** is worth considering for the Christmas market, which spreads across the Grand-Place and Place Sainte-Catherine with atmospheric lighting and a better-than-average range of food and drink stalls. It is cold and often rainy, but hotels are cheaper than in summer.

**Spring** (March–May) is the time to see the **Royal Greenhouses of Laeken** — the vast iron-and-glass structures on the royal estate are open to the public for only a few weeks each spring when the exotic plants are in flower. The timing changes annually; check the Royal Palace website ahead of your visit.

Avoid the European Parliament session weeks in autumn if you want reasonable hotel prices — MEPs and lobbyists fill the EU quarter and push rates up sharply across the city.
