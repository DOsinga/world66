title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Charleroi is not a conventional tourist city, but it has genuine substance for those willing to look. The [Bois du Cazier](/europe/belgium/charleroi/bois_du_cazier) in Marcinelle is the city's most important site — the former coal mine where 262 workers died in the 1956 disaster, now a UNESCO World Heritage Site with excellent industrial and glass museums. The city's comic strip heritage is real: the Marcinelle school of Franco-Belgian comics was born here, with Éditions Dupuis publishing Spirou, Lucky Luke, and the Smurfs from this city. Look for Spirou statues in the metro.

The **Belfry** in the upper town is a UNESCO World Heritage Site and the best vantage point in the city. The **Musée de la Photographie** in Mont-sur-Marchienne, housed in a 19th-century Carmelite convent, is one of the finest photography museums in Europe. The **Ville-Haute** (Upper Town) around Place Charles II has the best architecture, including the neoclassical Cathedral of St. Christopher and the elaborate Maison Dorée.
