---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

Charleroi's climate is typically Belgian: mild and damp, with no dramatic seasonal swings. Rain falls throughout the year; summers are warm without being hot (around 22°C in July), winters are cold and grey.

Spring and early summer (May to June) offer the most pleasant weather for walking the Ville-Haute and visiting outdoor sites. The Bois du Cazier is worth visiting any time of year — the museums are enclosed.

The **Charleroi Urban Film Festival** takes place in autumn and screens work in unusual locations around the city, including some of its industrial heritage sites. The festival reflects the city's self-aware relationship with its reputation and is worth checking if you visit in that season.
