---
title: "Eating Out"
type: section
---

De Panne's restaurant scene is shaped by the North Sea and the Belgian coast's love of straightforward, generous cooking. Fresh fish is the main event — sole, shrimp, mussels, and grey North Sea shrimps (*grijze garnalen*) prepared simply, usually with fries and a cold beer. The seafront has tourist-oriented restaurants, but better food is found a street or two back.
