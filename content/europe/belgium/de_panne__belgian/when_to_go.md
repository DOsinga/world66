---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

The Belgian coast has a maritime climate — mild, damp, and windy year-round. July and August are the busiest months, when Belgian and Dutch families pack the seafront; De Panne is less congested than Ostend or Blankenberge, but it still gets crowded in peak summer. Prices for accommodation rise sharply.

Spring and early autumn are the best times for nature in the Westhoek — the light is good, the dunes are less baked, and you can walk for hours without seeing anyone. Plopsaland is open from Easter through October but closed in winter. The Paul Delvaux Museum runs full hours from April to September.

For storm-watching, winter on the North Sea coast can be spectacular — the beach is enormous at low tide and the dunes provide shelter from the worst of the wind.
