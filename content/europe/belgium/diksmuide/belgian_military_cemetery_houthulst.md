Belgian Military Cemetery Houthulst is one of the important First World War burial grounds in the wider Yser and Flanders Fields landscape. Its rows of graves are stark, orderly, and deeply sobering, especially because so many visitors know the war here through trenches and towers before they confront the cemeteries.

It is a meaningful stop from Diksmuide if you are tracing the Belgian front. Give it quiet time; the cemetery's force comes from repetition, names, and the rural stillness around a battlefield that once consumed whole villages.
