---
title: "Eating Out"
type: section
---

The waterfront along the Meuse has a row of restaurants and brasseries serving standard Belgian fare — moules-frites, carbonnade flamande, and waffles. Several offer river views from terraces in summer. For something more specifically local, look out for **flamiche** (a savoury cheese tart from the Dinant area, served warm), **escavèche** (cold fried river fish in a marinade), and **bisque d'écrevisses** (crayfish bisque) on local menus.

Dinant is also known for its **couques** — hard honey biscuits pressed into elaborate decorative moulds — sold in bakeries along Rue Grande. They are meant to be sucked rather than bitten; chewing one is reputed to be how to break a tooth in Dinant.
