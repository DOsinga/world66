The French Military Cemetery at Dinant is a sober reminder that this pretty Meuse town was also a battlefield and a place of civilian tragedy in 1914. Rows of graves on the hillside give the First World War a local scale, away from the larger and better-known cemeteries of Flanders.

It is a quiet, respectful stop rather than a conventional attraction. Combine it with Dinant's citadel and riverfront if you want a fuller sense of how the town's strategic position shaped its history.
