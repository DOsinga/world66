---
title: Eating Out
type: section
---

Durbuy has a remarkably good dining scene for a town of its size. The concentration of restaurants around the main square and along the cobbled lanes caters to the weekend tourist crowd from Brussels and Liège, which means quality is generally high and prices are urban rather than rural. Belgian staples — waffles, grilled meats, local cheeses, and good beer — dominate, but you will also find proper cooking with regional Ardennes ingredients.
