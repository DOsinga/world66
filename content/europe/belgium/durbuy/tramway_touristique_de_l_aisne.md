The Tramway Touristique de l'Aisne runs preserved Vicinal diesel trams on a narrow-gauge line through the Aisne valley north of Durbuy. It is a modest but enjoyable heritage railway, best suited to travellers who like old trams, rural scenery, or slow family outings.
