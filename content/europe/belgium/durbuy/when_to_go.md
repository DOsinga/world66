---
title: When to Go
type: section
---

Summer (June–August) is peak season, when kayaking and hiking are at their best and the town's restaurants and terraces are in full swing. The downside is crowds — Durbuy attracts substantial numbers of day-trippers from Brussels and Liège on summer weekends, and the narrow streets can feel busy.

Spring and early autumn are the better times for a relaxed visit. May and June bring wildflowers to the Ourthe valley; October turns the surrounding forest gold and copper. The weather in the Ardennes is unpredictable — bring waterproofs in any season.

Winter is quiet and the town can be charming in a low-key way, but many smaller restaurants and outfitters operate reduced hours or close entirely between November and March. Christmas weekends see a moderate revival, with markets and lights in the historic centre.
