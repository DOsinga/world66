Henri-Chapelle American Cemetery and Memorial is one of Belgium's major World War II American cemeteries, with thousands of graves from the Battle of the Bulge and the advance into Germany. The long rows of white markers and the memorial chapel make it a solemn stop for anyone tracing the war's route through eastern Belgium.
