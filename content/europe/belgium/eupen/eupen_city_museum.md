Eupen City Museum tells the story of a small border town that was shaped by cloth-making, carnival, language politics, and repeated shifts of sovereignty. The collections are local rather than blockbuster, but that is the point: they explain why Eupen does not feel quite like the rest of Belgium.

The museum sits in the old town on Gospertstrasse, close enough to pair with St. Nicholas Church and IKOB in a single slow morning.
