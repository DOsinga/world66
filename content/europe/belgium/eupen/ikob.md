IKOB is a small contemporary art museum and one of Eupen's strongest arguments for more than a quick lunch stop. It was founded in 1993 and has become the only recognised art museum in Belgium's German-speaking Community.

The programme changes often, so check what is on before you go. Even when the show is modest, the museum gives Eupen a cultural edge that is unusual for a town of this size.
