Moorenhoehe Park is a small hillside viewpoint above Eupen's lower town. It is not a major sight, but the short walk pays off with a clear view over the rooftops, the valley, and the wooded edge of the Hertogenwald.

Use it as a breather between the town centre and the southern side of Eupen. On a clear day it also helps you understand how quickly the city gives way to the High Fens landscape.
