Besloten Hof is a former monastic complex in central Geel, with buildings that trace the town's religious and educational history from the late 17th century onward. The later chapel gives the site its most visible landmark.

It is a quiet stop rather than a headline attraction. Pair it with Sint-Dimpnakerk and the market square to get a fuller picture of Geel's old centre.
