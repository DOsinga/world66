Groot Begijnhof Sint-Elisabeth is one of Ghent's beguinage quarters, a planned religious community once inhabited by beguines: lay women who lived devout lives without taking full monastic vows. Its lanes, houses, and churches preserve a quieter social history than the city's grand towers and guildhalls.

The appeal is atmospheric rather than flashy. Walk slowly, look at the rhythm of small houses and communal spaces, and let it add a more intimate layer to Ghent's medieval and early modern story.
