Ghent's University Museum, formerly the Museum for the History of Sciences, brings together university collections in archaeology, ethnography, morphology, and other fields. It is a useful stop for visitors who like science museums and academic collections.

The museum sits outside the densest medieval core, so it works best when paired with other sights in the museum quarter rather than as part of a quick old-town walk.
