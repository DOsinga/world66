---
title: Getting There
type: section
---

Huy is well served by rail on the Namur–Liège line. Trains run frequently and take about 25 minutes from either city. The station is a short walk from the town centre and the collegiate church. By car, Huy sits on the E42/A15 motorway and is easy to reach from Brussels (about an hour) or Liège (30 minutes). There is no need for a car within town.
