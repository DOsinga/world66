Onze-Lieve-Vrouwehospitaal is Kortrijk's old hospital, founded in the 13th century and still carrying traces of its monastic past. Modern hospital buildings surround parts of the site, but the historic courtyard keeps enough of the older atmosphere to make it worth a short detour while walking between the Grote Markt and the Leie.
