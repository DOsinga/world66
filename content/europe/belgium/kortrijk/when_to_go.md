---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

Kortrijk is a year-round city — the historic centre is compact and walkable whatever the weather. The best time to visit is around July 11, the Flemish national holiday celebrating the Battle of the Golden Spurs, when the city celebrates with processions, outdoor concerts, and events at the Groeninge Monument. In even-numbered years, the Interieur design biennale transforms the Kortrijk Xpo exhibition centre into one of Europe's leading design fairs, attracting visitors from across the continent.

Spring and early summer are pleasant for café terraces and visits to the béguinage garden.
