De Blauwe Schuit (The Blue Barge) is one of the more reliable Belgian brasseries in the city centre, occupying a corner spot near the Grote Markt. The menu runs through Belgian classics — waterzooi (the traditional Flemish stew, available with chicken or fish), steak-frites, croquettes de crevettes — alongside a rotating list of daily specials that make good use of seasonal produce. The room is unpretentious, the portions generous, and the Belgian beer list thoughtfully assembled.

It gets busy in the evenings and at weekend lunchtimes, so it's worth arriving early or booking ahead. Prices are moderate by Belgian standards. Address: Grote Markt area; check the current opening times as these can vary.
