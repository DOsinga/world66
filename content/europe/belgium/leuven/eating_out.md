---
title: "Eating Out"
type: section
---

Leuven's eating scene reflects its character: unpretentious, good value, international enough to keep 25,000 students fed and satisfied. Belgian comfort food anchors the menus — steak-frites, waterzooi, mussels — alongside a range of cuisines brought in by the student population. The streets around the Grote Markt and along Tiensestraat are the main dining areas.
