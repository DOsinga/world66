---
title: "Getting There"
type: section
---

Leuven is one of the most accessible cities in Belgium. Its position between Brussels and Liège on the main east-west rail corridor means it is connected to the rest of the country and to international networks with unusual directness.

**By train** is by far the best way to arrive. From Brussels Centraal or Brussels-Midi, trains to Leuven run every 15 minutes and take around 25 minutes. From Brussels Airport (Zaventem), there is a direct train to Leuven every 30 minutes taking about 15 minutes — shorter than the journey into Brussels itself. From Liège, trains take about 45 minutes. Leuven station is well-positioned, about 10 minutes' walk from the Grote Markt.

**By car**, Leuven sits on the E40 motorway connecting Brussels to Liège and Cologne. The city centre operates a low-emission zone (LEZ) and parking within the centre is limited and expensive. There are several large park-and-ride facilities on the edges of the city; using one of these and walking or cycling in is far more practical than driving to the centre.

**Brussels Airport** is the obvious international gateway, with a direct train connection making the transfer unusually simple. The airport is, in effect, closer to Leuven than to the centre of Brussels.
