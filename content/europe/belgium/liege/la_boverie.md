La Boverie is Liege's main art museum, set in the Boverie Palace in the park of the same name. The permanent collection covers work from the Renaissance to the modern period, while the larger exhibition spaces host temporary shows.

The museum is worth pairing with a walk through Parc de la Boverie and along the Meuse. Even if you only have limited time, it gives Liege a focused art stop beyond the older historic core.
