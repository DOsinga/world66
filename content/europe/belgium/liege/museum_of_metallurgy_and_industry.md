The Museum of Metallurgy and Industry explains the steel and engineering work that shaped Liege. Its standout exhibit is a 17th-century blast furnace with waterwheel-powered bellows, a rare survival from the early industrial world.

The museum is small but unusually specific, and it gives useful context for Liege's identity as a working industrial city rather than only a historic river town.
