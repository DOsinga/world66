The Museum of Public Transport in Wallonia displays historic trams, buses, and other vehicles from Liège, Verviers, Charleroi, and the wider French-speaking part of Belgium. One of the more unusual pieces is the horse-drawn carriage of Liège's last Prince-Bishop.
