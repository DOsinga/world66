Maasmechelen is a municipality in the Belgian province of Limburg, on the Meuse river not far from the Dutch and German borders. The area is best known as a shopping destination — [Maasmechelen Village](/europe/belgium/maasmechelen/maasmechelen_village) is one of Belgium's major outlet shopping centres, drawing visitors from across the region. Beyond retail, the surrounding [Hoge Kempen National Park](/europe/belgium/maasmechelen/hoge_kempen_national_park) offers walking and cycling through heathland and forest, making it a reasonable base for outdoor exploration in Belgian Limburg.

The municipality straddles the point where Belgium borders both the Netherlands and Germany. The Meuse river forms a natural boundary, and a gentle cycling route along the towpath will take you north toward the Drielandenpunt — the famous [three-country point](/europe/netherlands/maastricht) where Belgium, the Netherlands, and Germany meet. Maasmechelen also holds an unlikely footnote in Second World War history: it was here, near the village of Vucht, that a German aircraft made a forced landing in January 1940, placing the secret [invasion plans for Western Europe](/europe/belgium/maasmechelen/mechelen_incident_memorial) in Belgian hands.
