title: Getting There
type: section
---

Maasmechelen is about 80 kilometres east of Brussels and 30 kilometres north of Liège. By car, take the E313 motorway east from [Antwerp](/europe/belgium/antwerp) or the E40/E313 from [Brussels](/europe/belgium/brussels). The nearest major international airport is Brussels Airport, roughly 90 minutes by car; [Maastricht Aachen Airport](/europe/netherlands/maastricht) is closer at under 30 minutes.

By public transport, Genk is the nearest train station, about 10 kilometres away. From Genk, buses run toward Maasmechelen. Maasmechelen Village operates dedicated shuttle bus services from Liège, Hasselt, Maastricht, and Aachen on weekends and shopping days — check their website for current timetables. The shuttle option is particularly useful for visitors coming from Maastricht, which is just across the Dutch border.
