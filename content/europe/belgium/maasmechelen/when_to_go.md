---
title: When to Go
type: section
---

Maasmechelen is a year-round destination given its shopping focus, though the national park is at its best from late spring through early autumn. The heather on Hoge Kempen blooms purple in August — the single most visually striking time to walk the heathlands. Summer weekends bring the largest crowds to the outlet village; weekday mornings are quieter for shopping.

Spring and autumn are good for cycling and walking, with mild temperatures and fewer visitors in the park. Winter is quiet and the village sometimes hosts seasonal events around Christmas. The wettest months are typically July and August, so pack accordingly if hiking is the priority.
