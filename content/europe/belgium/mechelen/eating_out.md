---
title: 'Eating out'
type: section
---

Mechelen has a serious food scene for its size, helped by being close enough to both Brussels and Antwerp to draw chefs and customers from each. The local dish to look for is **Mechelse koekoek** — a heritage breed of chicken, slow-grown and full-flavoured, which appears on most decent menus.

The compact centre means most of the best places are within a short walk of the Grote Markt. The Vismarkt and Haverwerf are the main café-restaurant zones; for fine dining the streets around the Botermarkt are a better bet.
