---
title: 'Getting there'
type: section
---

Mechelen sits on the main rail line between [Brussels](/europe/belgium/brussels) and [Antwerp](/europe/belgium/antwerp). Direct intercity trains take about 15 minutes from Brussels-Central and 12 minutes from Antwerp-Centraal — every few minutes during the day. Brussels Airport is 25 minutes by direct train, which makes Mechelen an unusually easy base for a Belgium trip without a car.

The main station is **Mechelen** at the south edge of the centre; a second station, **Mechelen-Nekkerspoel**, sits on the east side and is sometimes more convenient for the Kazerne Dossin. Both are a 10-15 minute walk to the Grote Markt.
