Auberge sits in the middle of Corbiestraat, one of the streets that sees the most foot traffic in Mol. It is a comfortable, unfussy cafe in the Belgian tradition — good for lingering over a beer or a coffee without anyone rushing you along.

The location makes it a natural meeting point, and the clientele reflects that: a mix of locals from the neighbourhood stopping in at different hours of the day.
