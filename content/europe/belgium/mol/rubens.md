Rubens is the sort of cafe that earns its reputation one scoop at a time. The place is best known for its rich and varied ice cream, which draws people in even when they only meant to pass by. The setting is straightforward — a neighbourhood cafe without pretension — but the ice cream is taken seriously.

It also functions as a regular bar and cafe for the rest of the day, making it a useful stop whether you are after a coffee in the morning or a Trappist beer in the evening.
