---
title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Mol is not a destination that shouts about its attractions, but a couple of them are genuinely worth seeking out. The nuclear research centre north of town is an unexpected draw — one of the most significant scientific facilities in Europe, sitting quietly in the Kempen pinewoods. Beyond that, the lakes and heathland of the surrounding landscape make the area a fine base for cycling and walking.
