---
title: "Getting There"
type: section
---

Mons is well-connected by train. Direct trains run from Brussels (50 minutes), making it an easy day trip from the capital. There are also connections to Lille in France (about 45 minutes) and to Charleroi. The train station is a ten-minute walk from the Grand-Place. By car, Mons sits just off the E19 motorway that links Brussels and Paris.
