Museum François Duesberg is a distinctive decorative-arts museum in Mons, especially known for clocks, gilt bronzes, porcelain, and neoclassical objects. It is the kind of focused private collection that rewards close looking rather than speed.

The museum pairs well with Mons’s Grand Place and civic sights because it shows a more intimate world of taste, craft, and 18th- to 19th-century luxury. It is best for travellers who enjoy objects and interiors as much as architecture.
