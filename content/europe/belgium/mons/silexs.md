Silex's introduces the Neolithic flint mines of Spiennes, one of the most important archaeological landscapes around Mons. The site explains how prehistoric miners extracted flint from underground galleries long before the city's medieval and industrial stories began.

It is a serious archaeology stop rather than a casual town museum. If tours into the mining area are available, book ahead; the underground context is what makes the place much more memorable than a display case of tools.
