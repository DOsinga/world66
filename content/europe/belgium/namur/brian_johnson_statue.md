A modest bronze bust in a side street near Place Saint-Hilaire commemorates AC/DC's first ever gig with **Brian Johnson** as their new singer — a small venue performance at the Namur Casino in March 1980, just weeks before the recording sessions that produced Back in Black. The statue was unveiled in 2019 on the date of that anniversary.

It is a slightly absurd Namurois monument that has nonetheless become a small pilgrimage spot for AC/DC fans passing through Belgium. Combine with a wander through the old town's bars, several of which will happily play AC/DC on request.
