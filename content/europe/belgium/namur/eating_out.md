---
title: "Eating Out"
type: section
---

Walloon cooking is hearty: stewed game, river fish, braised meat, big plates of fries, and beer in everything. Namur is a good place to try it without paying Brussels prices.

Look for **boulets à la liégeoise** (meatballs in a thick sweet-and-sour syrup sauce), **lapin à la kriek** (rabbit in cherry beer), **truite des Ardennes** (Ardennes trout), and **escavèche** — a vinegared fish dish, originally a way of preserving the eel and trout caught in the Meuse. The local **escargots de Namur** are a regional pride.

The best concentration of restaurants is around **Place du Marché aux Légumes** and **Rue des Brasseurs**, both pedestrianised. For a fine-dining splurge, **L'Espièglerie** in the historic Hôtel de Tanneurs is reliable. For a snack and a beer, the brasseries along the Meuse quay are pleasant in summer.
