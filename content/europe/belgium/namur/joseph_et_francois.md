Two seated bronze figures on Place du Marché aux Légumes, just off the cathedral square, are perhaps the most-photographed Namurois after the citadel. **Djoseph** and **Françwès** — Joseph and François — are characters from a long-running cartoon strip in the local newspaper, immortalised here in a bronze argument: a pet snail balanced between them is the cause of the row.

The statue was unveiled in 1989 and quickly absorbed into the city's affection. Locals rub the snail for luck. Have a coffee on the terrace opposite and watch the queue of tourists working out which one is which.
