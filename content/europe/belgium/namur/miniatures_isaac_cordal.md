Look up — but mostly down — as you wander Namur's old town and you may spot a small grey businessman perched on a window ledge, or sheltering in a doorway, or grimly contemplating his reflection in a puddle. The Spanish street sculptor **Isaac Cordal** scattered a series of his trademark miniatures around the city as part of the **KIKK** festival, and many have stayed.

There is no official map; finding them is part of the fun. Likely places include around the Halle al'Chair, near the cathedral, and in the alleys towards the river. The figures are small, drab and easy to miss — which is the point.
