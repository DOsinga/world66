The Museum of Old Namurois Art is a small rainy-day museum focused on the older art and religious heritage of Namur. Its collection includes medieval and Renaissance Christian art, with recorded guiding available in English.

It is not a blockbuster museum, but it adds useful context to Namur's churches, belfry, and old streets.
