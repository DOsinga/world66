---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

Namur has a mild continental climate — cool, wet, and given to grey winters. **May to September** is the best stretch for visiting: long evenings on the riverside terraces, walkable old town, and citadel grounds at their greenest.

The big city festival is the **Fêtes de Wallonie** in mid-September, when the streets fill with concerts, parades and the famous **stilt fights** (the Echasseurs Namurois) — teams of men in seventeenth-century dress duelling on wooden stilts on Place Saint-Aubain. It is unhinged and entirely local; book accommodation in advance.

For a quieter visit, come in **April–June** when the citadel gardens are flowering, or **October**, when the surrounding forests turn. The **Christmas market** along the Sambre quay is small but charming.
