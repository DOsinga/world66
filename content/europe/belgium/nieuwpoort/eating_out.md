---
title: "Eating Out"
type: section
---

Nieuwpoort lives on its grey shrimp. The local *garnalen* — small, sweet, peeled by hand and best eaten cold with a glass of beer — appear on every serious menu in town. The classic dish is *tomaat-garnaal*, a hollowed-out tomato stuffed with shrimp and mayonnaise; the other is *garnaalkroketten*, hot crisp croquettes with a soft shrimp interior. Order both at least once.

Beyond shrimp, the harbour-side restaurants do mussels in season (roughly July to April), grilled sole, and the full range of Belgian classics — *stoofvlees*, *waterzooi*, *vol-au-vent*. Prices are moderate by Belgian standards, with most main courses in the 22–32 euro range. The strip of restaurants along the Kaai facing the marina is the obvious choice for a long, slow lunch.
