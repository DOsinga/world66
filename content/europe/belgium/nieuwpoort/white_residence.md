The White Residence is a modernist landmark on the Nieuwpoort seafront, notable because it cuts through the usual Belgian coast pattern of practical holiday blocks. Its clean lines and pale facade make it one of the more distinctive architectural stops along the promenade.

It is a quick look rather than a destination in itself, best folded into a walk between the beach, harbour, and war memorials. Architecture-minded visitors will appreciate the way it records a different, more ambitious moment in seaside development.
