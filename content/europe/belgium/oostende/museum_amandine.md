Museum Amandine is a former Ostend fishing vessel preserved on the harbourfront as a compact maritime museum. Its story points to the hard North Sea and Icelandic fishing trade that once mattered deeply to the port.

The ship is a short visit, but a good one if you want Ostend to feel like more than promenade and beach. It pairs easily with the Mercator, the fish market area, and a walk along the docks.
