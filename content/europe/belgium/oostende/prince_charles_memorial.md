The Prince Charles Memorial near Ostend preserves a coastal estate and memory site associated with Prince Charles of Belgium, who served as regent after World War II. It adds a more modern royal and wartime layer to a city often visited for beach, promenade, and Ensor connections.

The site is most useful for visitors already exploring the Raversijde area or the Belgian coast's wartime heritage. Check opening times, as access can be more limited than central Ostend attractions.
