Ostend Historical Museum De Plate is a compact local museum for understanding the Belgian coast's biggest city beyond the beach. Its collections touch on fishing, seaside tourism, royal connections, and the everyday life of the port.

It is best on a grey day or as a short cultural pause between waterfront walks. Ostend's identity has always mixed work and leisure, and the museum helps make sense of that combination in a way the promenade alone cannot.
