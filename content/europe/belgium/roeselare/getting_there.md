---
title: "Getting There"
type: section
---

Roeselare is on the railway line between Brugge and Kortrijk, with direct trains running frequently in both directions — about 25 minutes to either city. From Brussels, change at Kortrijk or Brugge; the total journey is about 90 minutes. The station is a short walk north of the Grote Markt. By car, Roeselare is on the E403 motorway between Brugge and Kortrijk, well-connected to the rest of Flanders.
