Roeselare's town hall anchors the Grote Markt, with its oldest section dating from the late 18th century. The attached belfry is the main reason to look up: it belongs to the UNESCO-listed group of belfries across Belgium and northern France, a compact symbol of civic pride in the old Flemish towns.
