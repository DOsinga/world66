Brewery Kerkom is a small rural brewery near Sint-Truiden, best known for its Bink beers. The setting gives it more travel value than a simple bar: visitors come for the brewery cafe, courtyard feel, and a taste of local Haspengouw beer culture.

It is easiest by bike or car as part of a countryside loop through orchards and villages. Opening times matter, so confirm before heading out, especially outside warm-weather weekends.
