Brewery Wilderen is a restored brewery and distillery complex in the village of Wilderen, just outside Sint-Truiden. It combines industrial heritage with a modern tasting room, making it more substantial than a simple cafe stop.

The visit fits the Haspengouw landscape well: orchards, villages, and Belgian beer culture within easy reach of town. It is best for travellers with a car or bike who want a relaxed break between churches, castles, and fruit-country routes.
