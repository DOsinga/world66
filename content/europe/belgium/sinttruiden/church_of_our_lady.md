The Church of Our Lady is Sint-Truiden's main church, standing close to the Grote Markt and the old abbey precinct. The crypt and treasury are the reason to step inside: many of the reliquaries and liturgical objects came from the former abbey, giving the church a stronger historical pull than its plain exterior suggests.
