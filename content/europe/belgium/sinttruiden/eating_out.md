---
title: "Eating Out"
type: section
---

Sint-Truiden is a Flemish town and the food reflects it — solid Belgian cooking, generous portions, and a fondness for local produce. The fruit of Haspengouw shows up in season on most menus, from cherry preparations with game to apple-based desserts and tarts. Several brasseries on and around the Grote Markt serve food throughout the day.
