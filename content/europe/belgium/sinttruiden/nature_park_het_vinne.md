Nature Park Het Vinne protects a large wetland north of Zoutleeuw, west of Sint-Truiden. The reserve covers the site of a former fen and peat-digging area, now restored as open water, reed beds, and walking paths.

It is a quiet stop for birdwatching and an easy walk in the Haspengouw countryside. There is parking by the entrance on Ossenwegstraat.
