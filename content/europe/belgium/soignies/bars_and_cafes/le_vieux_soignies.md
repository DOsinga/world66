A slightly more characterful bar on one of the streets feeding into the Grand Place, with exposed stonework, low ceilings, and an eclectic range of Belgian ales — saisons, abbey beers, strong blondes. The barstaff know their stock and are willing to suggest. Livelier at weekends when there is occasional live music; during the week it functions as a quiet neighbourhood local with a better beer list than it might otherwise warrant.
