A small, traditional Walloon restaurant a short walk from the church, with a menu built around regional staples: waterzooi, lapin à la bière, tarte au riz. The lunch formule — starter, main, and coffee — is good value. The dining room is unpretentious and the cooking is careful without trying to be clever. Popular with local workers at midday; quieter in the evenings.
