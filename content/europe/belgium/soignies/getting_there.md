---
title: "Getting There"
type: section
---

Soignies is well connected by train and straightforward to reach from Brussels or Mons.

**By train** is the easiest option. Direct trains run from Brussels-Midi to Soignies in approximately 45 minutes; trains also connect from Mons in around 15 minutes. The station is a short walk from the Grand Place and the collegiate church. Trains run frequently throughout the day on both routes. Check the SNCB/NMBS website for current timetables.

**By car** from Brussels, take the E19 motorway south towards Mons and exit towards Soignies — around 45 kilometres and under an hour outside of rush hour. Parking is available near the Grand Place.

**By car from Mons**, Soignies is roughly 20 kilometres to the northeast on the N6, a straightforward 20-minute drive.

There is no significant bus connection from outside the immediate region, and no nearby commercial airport — Charleroi Airport is the closest at about 40 kilometres.
