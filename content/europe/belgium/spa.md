Spa is the town that named the world. Long before "spa" became a generic word for any wellness centre with a hot tub, it was this small Walloon town in the Ardennes hills that European royalty visited to drink the mineral-rich spring water and restore their constitutions. The word stuck, and it travelled everywhere — but there is only one original.

The town sits in a green valley about 35 kilometres southeast of [Liège](/europe/belgium/liege), surrounded by forested hills. It is compact and pleasantly worn, with a faded elegance that suits a place whose peak was the 18th century. Tsar Peter the Great came here. So did Victor Hugo. Today you are more likely to share the baths with Formula 1 engineers — the [Circuit de Spa-Francorchamps](/europe/belgium/spa/circuit_de_spa_francorchamps), one of the great motor racing tracks in the world, lies just a few kilometres away.

The spring water is still the point. The [Thermes de Spa](/europe/belgium/spa/thermes_de_spa), built into the hillside above the town centre, offer proper thermal treatments — pools, saunas, jets — with a view over the Ardennes that alone is worth the cable car ride up. The springs themselves, scattered across the surrounding area, can still be tasted free of charge; the [Pouhon Pierre le Grand](/europe/belgium/spa/pouhon_pierre_le_grand) in the town centre is the best-known. The [Casino de Spa](/europe/belgium/spa/casino_de_spa), claiming to be the oldest in the world, faces the same square.

Spa is a good base for walking and cycling in the [Hautes Fagnes](/europe/belgium/spa/hautes_fagnes), the high plateau nature reserve to the east. The landscape up there is bleak and beautiful — open moorland, peat bogs, wooden boardwalks through the mist. For something more dramatic, the ruined [Château de Franchimont](/europe/belgium/spa/franchimont_castle) is 7 kilometres away, with views over the Ardennes valleys and a remarkable story of medieval resistance. The [Lac de Warfaaz](/europe/belgium/spa/lac_de_warfaaz), a quiet lake a short walk from the centre, is a good escape when the town fills up. In July, the [Francofolies de Spa](/europe/belgium/spa/les_francofolies) brings major French-language music acts to town for several days.
