Musée de la ville d'eaux tells the story of Spa as a resort town built around its mineral springs. The exhibits focus on the waters, the visitors they drew, and the town that grew from that reputation.

The museum is housed in a villa built in 1863 for Queen Marie-Henriette of Belgium. It is a useful stop before or after visiting the springs and thermal baths that still define Spa.
