Thuin's North Wall is a short medieval remnant with a wide view over the Sambre Valley. Beside Place du Chapitre, the old cannon known as Le Spantole gives the spot a little extra character.

This is a small stop, but it fits naturally into a walk through the upper town and hanging gardens. Come for the view and the sense of how tightly Thuin once held its hilltop.
