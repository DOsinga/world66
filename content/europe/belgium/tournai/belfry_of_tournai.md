The Belfry of Tournai is the oldest belfry in Belgium and one of the clearest symbols of the city's medieval civic power. Standing near the cathedral and Grand Place, it anchors the old centre with a vertical counterpoint to Tournai's broad Romanesque and Gothic church architecture.

Climbing the tower, when open, is the best way to understand the compact historic core and the Scheldt river setting. Even from ground level, the belfry is essential Tournai: civic pride, stone, bells, and centuries of urban independence made visible.
