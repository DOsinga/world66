Tournai's Folklore Museum is the sort of small regional museum that makes an old city feel lived in rather than merely monumental. Its rooms cover local trades, domestic interiors, costumes, festivals, and everyday objects from Walloon life, with enough specificity to make the past feel close at hand.

It pairs well with the cathedral and belfry because it shifts the scale from civic grandeur to household detail. If you enjoy old shop signs, tools, textiles, and the practical things people used before travel guides cared about them, this is a rewarding slow stop in the centre of Tournai.
