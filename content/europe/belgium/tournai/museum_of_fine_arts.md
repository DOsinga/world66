Tournai's Museum of Fine Arts is worth visiting for both the collection and the building. Victor Horta designed the museum, and its plan gives the galleries a graceful, almost intimate feel compared with Belgium's larger art institutions.

The collection is compact but rewarding, with paintings that make a good cultural counterweight to Tournai's cathedral, belfry, and medieval streets. It is the sort of museum where an hour can feel complete rather than rushed.
