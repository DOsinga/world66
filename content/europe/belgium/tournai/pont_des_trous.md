The Pont des Trous is Tournai's famous fortified water gate, a medieval defensive bridge that once controlled the Scheldt as it passed through the city walls. Its two towers remain among the strongest visual reminders of Tournai's old ramparts.

The bridge has been altered and reconstructed more than once, including controversial recent works to improve navigation, so it is not a perfectly untouched relic. That tension is part of the visit: it is both a landmark of medieval military architecture and a working river monument in a city still negotiating its heritage.
