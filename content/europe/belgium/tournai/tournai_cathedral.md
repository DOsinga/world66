Tournai Cathedral is the city's heavyweight sight, a UNESCO-listed building whose five towers dominate the skyline. It is especially valuable because it shows the transition from Romanesque mass to Gothic height in one enormous church.

Go inside if restoration work allows, and take time with the scale rather than treating it as just another Belgian cathedral. The nave, transept, and choir tell a long architectural story, and the surrounding streets make the building feel rooted in the old city rather than isolated as a monument.
