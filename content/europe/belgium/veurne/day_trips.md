Veurne is well placed for trips around the Westhoek and the Belgian coast. [De Panne](/europe/belgium/de_panne__belgian) and its long Channel beach are six kilometres west; [Diksmuide](/europe/belgium/diksmuide), with the IJzertoren memorial to the First World War dead, lies twenty minutes east; and [Ypres](/europe/belgium/ypres) — Flanders Fields, the Menin Gate, and the in Flanders Fields museum — is a forty-minute drive south. The French border is five kilometres away, with Dunkirk reachable in half an hour.
