---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

Veurne is genuinely pleasant from late spring to early autumn. May through September gives long evenings on the Grote Markt, open churches, and quiet cycling in the surrounding polders. The **Procession of Penitence** on the last Sunday of July is the year's defining event; the **Sint-Walburgakermis** funfair runs alongside it.

Winter visits are very quiet — Veurne is far enough from the Bruges-Ypres tourist circuit to feel almost deserted from November to March, with several restaurants closed.
