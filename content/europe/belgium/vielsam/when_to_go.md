---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

Summer (June–August) is the main walking and cycling season, with long days, warm temperatures, and the full network of trails in good condition. The Ardennes woodland is at its best in late spring and early autumn — May and October in particular have good light and colour without the summer crowds. Winter can be excellent if snow falls, opening the cross-country ski trails, but the Belgian Ardennes gets reliable snow less consistently than the higher German or French mountains to the east; a trip timed for skiing should have a backup plan. Vielsam is quiet enough in all seasons that you are unlikely to have trouble finding a gîte or hotel room even without booking far ahead.
