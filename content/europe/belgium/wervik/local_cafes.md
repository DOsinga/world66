Wervik's cafés have the standard brown-café atmosphere of Flemish small towns: dark wood, low light, Jupiler on tap, Leffe or a regional Flemish ale if you want something with body. The service is unhurried and the regulars are comfortable — a good place to rest cycling legs and let an afternoon stretch out. Some serve basic snacks: cheese, cold cuts, a toasted sandwich. The Grote Markt area has the highest concentration of options and is the natural place to end up after visiting the tobacco museum or returning from the river.
