---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

Cycling season — April through October — is when Wervik sees most of its visitors. Summer is best for the Lys valley routes: long daylight hours, reliable weather, and the river at its most pleasant. The WWI sites nearby are open year-round and are visited in all weathers; autumn and winter visits have a particular atmosphere appropriate to the subject matter. Wervik has no major festivals and no real peak season — it is a quiet town in any season, and that is part of its appeal. The tobacco museum can be visited year-round. If you are combining Wervik with Ypres, allow a full day regardless of season.
