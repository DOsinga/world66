A 19th-century neo-classical country house in Grotenberge, on the eastern edge of Zottegem, set in a large landscaped park with mature trees and ponds. The castle itself is privately used for events and not generally open, but the park is freely accessible and one of the better walking destinations near the town.

The park has marked paths and a long avenue lined with linden trees. A pleasant detour for cyclists heading east towards the Dender valley.
