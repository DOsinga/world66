---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

Late spring and early autumn are the best months for visiting Zottegem and the Flemish Ardennes. Cycling weather is at its best in April–June and September; the lanes are quiet outside the spring classics in March and April.

The Tour of Flanders passes close to Zottegem in early April, and several local cyclo-sportives in May and June use the same hills. Rock Zottegem fills the first weekend of July with festival crowds. Winter is cool, grey, and damp but offers atmospheric quiet walks around the Egmont castle.
