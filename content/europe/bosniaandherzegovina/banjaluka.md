Banja Luka is the second-largest city in Bosnia and Herzegovina and the administrative centre of Republika Srpska, located on the Vrbas River in the north of the country. It is a city that surprises: more relaxed than Sarajevo, greener than expected, and with a riverside character defined by the fast, clear water of the Vrbas flowing through its centre.

The [Kastel Fortress](/europe/bosniaandherzegovina/banjaluka/kastel_fortress) overlooks the Vrbas from a rocky promontory — a fortification built on Roman foundations that is now as much a concert venue as a historical monument. The [Ferhadija Mosque](/europe/bosniaandherzegovina/banjaluka/ferhadija_mosque), a masterpiece of 16th-century Ottoman architecture, was blown up during the 1993 war and rebuilt with painstaking care, reopening in 2016 as both a place of worship and a testament to cultural survival. The [Cathedral of Saint Bonaventure](/europe/bosniaandherzegovina/banjaluka/cathedral_saint_bonaventure) stands nearby, completing an unusual trio of religious landmarks within a few minutes' walk of each other.

The [Vrbas River promenade](/europe/bosniaandherzegovina/banjaluka/vrbas_river_walk) is where the city goes to relax, and upstream the river enters a limestone canyon where [whitewater rafting](/europe/bosniaandherzegovina/banjaluka/vrbas_rafting) is the main outdoor activity. The [pedestrian zone on Gospodska Street](/europe/bosniaandherzegovina/banjaluka/pedestrian_zone) is the social heart of the city, lined with cafes that fill from morning coffee through to late-night rakija. The [Museum of Modern Art](/europe/bosniaandherzegovina/banjaluka/museum_of_modern_art) and the cultural centre at [Banski Dvor](/europe/bosniaandherzegovina/banjaluka/banski_dvor) complete the cultural picture.

Banja Luka tends to get overlooked by travellers rushing between Sarajevo and the Croatian coast. That is their loss — the city is good value, genuinely pleasant, and a useful gateway to the wider Republika Srpska, including the medieval town of [Jajce](/europe/bosniaandherzegovina/jajce) to the south.
