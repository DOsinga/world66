Christ the Saviour Church stands in the centre of Banja Luka, close to Gospodska Street and Banski Dvor. Its tall domes and pale stone make it one of the city's most visible Orthodox landmarks, and it is an easy stop on a walk through the central pedestrian area.

The church is worth seeing for its scale and setting as much as for its interior. It gives the centre of Banja Luka one of its strongest visual anchors.
