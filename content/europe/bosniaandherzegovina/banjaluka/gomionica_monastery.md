Gomionica Monastery sits in the hills west of Banja Luka, far enough from the city to feel like a proper rural detour rather than another urban church stop. The stone church, modest monastic buildings, and wooded setting give it the calm that old Orthodox monasteries often have when they are still living religious places, not museum pieces.

Come for the atmosphere as much as the architecture. The monastery has been tied to the Zmijanje region for centuries, and its treasury and layered history make it a worthwhile half-day excursion if you are already exploring Banja Luka's religious and Austro-Hungarian heritage.
