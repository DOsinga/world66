These small hot spring pools sit beside the river at Srpske Toplice, close enough to Banja Luka to work as an easy local outing. They are simple, public-feeling pools rather than a polished spa, which is part of the appeal.

Bring a towel and expect a low-key stop where the draw is the warm mineral water and the riverside setting.
