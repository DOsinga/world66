Mariastern Abbey, the Trappist monastery north of Banja Luka, is one of the city's quieter historical layers. The complex is linked to a once-flourishing monastic community, local industry, and the cheese-making tradition that still gives the Trapisti name a place in Bosnian food memory.

It is not a big-ticket sight, but it rewards travellers interested in religious history and the edges of the city. Visit respectfully, check current access, and combine it with other stops rather than expecting a full museum-style experience.
