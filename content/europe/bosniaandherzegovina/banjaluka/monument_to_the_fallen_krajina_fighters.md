The Monument to the Fallen Krajina Fighters stands on Banj Brdo above Banja Luka, combining a stark modernist memorial with one of the city's best viewpoints. The monument's scale and hilltop setting make it more than a roadside plaque or local statue.

The walk or drive up is part of the visit, especially in clear weather when the city and Vrbas valley open below. It is a worthwhile stop for travelers interested in Yugoslav memorial landscapes and Banja Luka's wider setting.
