---
title: When to Go
type: section
---

Banja Luka has a continental climate with warm summers and cold winters. The best times to visit are late spring (May–June) and early autumn (September–October): temperatures are comfortable, the Vrbas is running well for rafting, and the city's outdoor cafe culture is in full swing.

July and August are hot but manageable. The river becomes a focus for swimmers and kayakers, and the Demofest music festival (held in late July or early August inside the Kastel Fortress) draws large crowds and is worth timing a visit around if you enjoy outdoor music events.

Winter is cold and sometimes snowy, but the city functions normally and accommodation prices drop significantly. The Christmas market and New Year celebrations are modest but pleasant.
