---
title: Getting There
type: section
---

Bihać is in the far northwest corner of Bosnia, about 90 kilometres from [Zagreb](/europe/croatia/zagreb) and 300 kilometres from [Sarajevo](/europe/bosniaandherzegovina/sarajevo). By car, the most common approach is from Zagreb via the Croatian coastal road, crossing the border at several points; the Bosnian road network in this area is decent. From Sarajevo, the drive is around four hours.

There is a small airport at Bihać Golubić, but regular scheduled services are limited; most visitors fly to Zagreb or Split and drive from there. Buses connect Bihać to Sarajevo and Zagreb, with several services daily. There is no regular passenger rail service.
