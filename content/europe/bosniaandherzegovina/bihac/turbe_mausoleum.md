The Turbe Mausoleum is a small but historically significant Ottoman-era memorial in central Bihać. Built in 1878 by the Austro-Hungarian administration shortly after the occupation of Bosnia, it commemorates the Bosnian defenders who died resisting the Habsburg advance. The structure is a turbe — a domed mausoleum typical of Ottoman funerary architecture — and its construction by the incoming authorities says something about the complex relationship between the new occupiers and the society they were governing.

The mausoleum is a designated national monument of Bosnia and Herzegovina. It stands in the old town area, close to the Captain's Tower, and can be seen in a few minutes as part of a walking tour of Bihać's historic centre.
