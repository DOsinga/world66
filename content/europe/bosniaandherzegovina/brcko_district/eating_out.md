---
title: "Eating Out"
type: section
---

Brcko eats like the rest of Bosnia — simply, cheaply, and well. The staples are **ćevapi** (grilled minced meat links served with flatbread, raw onion, and kajmak cream), **burek** (flaky pastry filled with meat or cheese), **sarma** (stuffed cabbage), and grilled meats. The café culture is comprehensive: coffee stops are everywhere and nobody seems in a hurry. The Sava-side restaurants get more animated in the evenings. Local brandy (rakija, typically šljivovica/slivovitz) is drunk at any hour without apology.
