Hajducka Vrata is a natural stone arch high on Cvrsnica, reached by a demanding hike rather than a casual stroll. It is one of the classic symbols of Herzegovinian mountain walking, with big views across the limestone ridges when the weather is clear.

Go prepared: the route is exposed, weather changes quickly, and some wider mountain areas in Bosnia still require attention to marked trails. For fit hikers, though, this is the anchor that turns Jablanica from a roadside stop into a base.
