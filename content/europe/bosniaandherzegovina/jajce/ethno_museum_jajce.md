The Ethno Museum in Jajce focuses on traditional household objects, clothing, and crafts from the region. It is a small stop, but useful in a town where most visitors move quickly between the waterfall, catacombs, fortress, and old mills.

Its value is context: the exhibits bring everyday Bosnian life into a visit otherwise dominated by rulers, battles, and dramatic scenery. Allow a short visit and treat it as a companion to a slow walk through the old town.
