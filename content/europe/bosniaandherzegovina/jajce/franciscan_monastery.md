The Franciscan Monastery in Jajce is a compact but important historical stop, tied to the final years of the medieval Bosnian kingdom. Its museum preserves religious and local artefacts, and the monastery is traditionally associated with the remains of King Stjepan Tomasevic, the last Bosnian king.

It pairs naturally with Jajce Fortress and the catacombs, filling in the religious and political story behind the town's dramatic setting. The site is quiet, but for a small place like Jajce it carries real historical weight.
