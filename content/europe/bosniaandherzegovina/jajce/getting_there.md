---
title: Getting There
type: section
---

Jajce is about 150 km northwest of [Sarajevo](/europe/bosniaandherzegovina/sarajevo) by road, a journey of roughly two hours on the main highway through the mountains. Buses run several times daily from Sarajevo's main bus station; the journey takes 2.5–3 hours. From Banja Luka (about 90 km north), the road follows the Vrbas River gorge — a spectacular drive in its own right.

There is no train service. Most independent travellers come by private car, which also makes it easier to reach the Pliva Lakes. Day trips from Sarajevo are feasible but leave little time for the site; an overnight stay allows you to see the town at its own pace.
