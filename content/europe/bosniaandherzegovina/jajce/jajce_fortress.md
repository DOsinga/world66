Jajce Fortress crowns the town, pulling together the medieval walls, steep streets, and river valleys below. The climb is part of the visit, because every turn makes Jajce's compact defensive setting clearer.

From the top you can see why the town mattered: water, roads, hills, and fortifications all meeting in one tight place. Go late in the day if you want softer light on the old roofs and surrounding hills.
