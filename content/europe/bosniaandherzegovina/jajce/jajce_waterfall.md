The waterfall at Jajce is one of the most photographed natural sights in Bosnia, and rightly so. The Pliva River drops about 21 metres over a broad limestone face right in the centre of town, creating a thundering cascade that you can see and hear from the old town above. It is an arresting sight — a proper roaring waterfall in the middle of a medieval city, not some modest trickle dressed up by the tourist board.

The best viewing point is from the road below the old town, looking up at the fall against the backdrop of the fortified walls. There is a lower viewing platform and a café nearby. The waterfall is most impressive in spring and after rain; by late summer it can thin considerably. Stairs and paths connect the waterfall viewpoint to the old town above.
