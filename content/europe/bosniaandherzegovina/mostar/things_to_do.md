title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

The [Stari Most](/europe/bosniaandherzegovina/mostar/stari_most) and the surrounding old town are the obvious starting point. The [Kujundžiluk](/europe/bosniaandherzegovina/mostar/kujundziluk) bazaar still rings with coppersmiths, and a small [museum inside the Tara tower](/europe/bosniaandherzegovina/mostar/museum_of_the_old_bridge) at the bridge's western end tells the story of its destruction and rebuilding. Climb the minaret of the [Koski Mehmed Pasha Mosque](/europe/bosniaandherzegovina/mostar/koski_mehmed_pasha_mosque) for the classic photograph.

Beyond the bridge, the larger [Karađoz Bey Mosque](/europe/bosniaandherzegovina/mostar/karadjoz_bey_mosque) and the 18th-century [Muslibegović House](/europe/bosniaandherzegovina/mostar/muslibegovic_house) give a fuller sense of Mostar's Ottoman past. Walk a few minutes upstream to the smaller [Crooked Bridge](/europe/bosniaandherzegovina/mostar/crooked_bridge), or follow the river south to find quieter restaurants on the Radobolja.

The war is still visible. The graffiti-covered [Sniper Tower](/europe/bosniaandherzegovina/mostar/sniper_tower) and the haunting [Partisan Memorial Cemetery](/europe/bosniaandherzegovina/mostar/partisan_memorial_cemetery) above the west bank are essential context. So is the deliberately oversized [Franciscan church tower](/europe/bosniaandherzegovina/mostar/franciscan_church) and the [Holy Trinity Cathedral](/europe/bosniaandherzegovina/mostar/cathedral_holy_trinity) opposite it on the east — both rebuilt after the war and clearly visible from across town. For something lighter, the [Bruce Lee statue](/europe/bosniaandherzegovina/mostar/bishop_statue_bruce_lee) in Zrinjski Park is one of the city's stranger postwar landmarks.
