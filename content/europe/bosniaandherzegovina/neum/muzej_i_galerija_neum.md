Muzej i Galerija Neum is a small museum and gallery near the Church of Our Lady of Health. It gives Neum a modest cultural stop away from the waterfront and beach strip.

The museum is best treated as a short visit while walking through town. It is not a major regional attraction, but it adds useful local context if you are spending more than a quick beach stop in Neum.
