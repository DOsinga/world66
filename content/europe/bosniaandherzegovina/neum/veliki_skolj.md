Veliki Skolj is the larger of the two small uninhabited islets associated with Neum's bay. Like Mali Skolj, it is not a developed island stop; its appeal is geographical, not recreational. It sits in the sheltered waters that make this short coastline feel more like a bay than an open sea resort.

If you take a boat trip or look back from the Klek side, the islet is easy to pick out. Otherwise it is a quiet footnote, but a distinctive one in a country with almost no coast.
