Lukomir is one of Bosnia and Herzegovina's highest and most isolated mountain villages, set above the Rakitnica Canyon in the Bjelašnica highlands. Its stone houses, steep roofs, and summer pastoral life make it a strong stop for travellers interested in traditional Bosnian mountain culture.

The village is usually visited on a hike or guided trip from the canyon area or the Sarajevo highlands. Access can be difficult in winter, when snow often makes the last approach possible only on foot, skis, or snowshoes.
