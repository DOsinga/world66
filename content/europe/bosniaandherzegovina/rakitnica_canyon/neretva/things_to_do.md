---
title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Neretva village is a base for exploring the upper Rakitnica Canyon and the headwaters of the Neretva River. The terrain is wild limestone gorge country — deep narrow canyons, dense forest, and cold clear water. Hiking along the canyon rim or down to the river is the main activity; there are no developed tourist facilities, just the landscape.

Access requires some preparation. The roads are rough, supplies should come with you, and accommodation is limited to what locals can arrange. The reward is one of the quieter corners of the Dinaric Alps, where the river carves through gorges 600–800 metres deep and you are unlikely to encounter another tourist.
