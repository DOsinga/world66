The Cathedral Church of the Nativity of the Theotokos is Sarajevo's main Serbian Orthodox cathedral and one of the largest Orthodox churches in the Balkans. Built in the 19th century, it stands close to the Catholic cathedral and the old Ottoman core, which makes it part of the city's unusually dense religious geography.

The exterior is handsome, with five domes and a pale stone facade, while the interior is worth seeing for its iconostasis and painted decoration. Visit respectfully, as this remains an active church rather than just a monument.
