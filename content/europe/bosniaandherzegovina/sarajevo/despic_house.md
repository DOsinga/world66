Despić House is a historic merchant home now run as part of the Sarajevo City Museum. Its rooms show the life of a wealthy Serb Orthodox family in Ottoman and Austro-Hungarian Sarajevo, and the house is also remembered as a venue for some of the city's earliest theatre performances.
