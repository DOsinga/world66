The Mausoleum of Gavrilo Princip is one of Sarajevo's more complicated memorial sites. Princip's assassination of Archduke Franz Ferdinand in 1914 helped trigger the First World War, and the way he is remembered has shifted sharply depending on politics, period, and community.

Visit with that ambiguity in mind. The mausoleum is not just about one young assassin; it is about how Sarajevo carries layers of imperial, Yugoslav, nationalist, and wartime memory in a city where history is never safely in the past.
