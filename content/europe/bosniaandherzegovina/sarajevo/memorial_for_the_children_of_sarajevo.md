The Memorial for the Children of Sarajevo stands in Veliki Park and remembers the children killed during the Siege of Sarajevo. Its central sculpture is made from stacked green glass, with inscribed names set around the base.

The memorial is a short, sobering stop in central Sarajevo, close to several other war-history sites and museums. It is not large, but its subject gives it a weight that makes it worth seeking out.
