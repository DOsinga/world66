The Museum of Alija Izetbegović occupies space by the Ploča and Širokac gates above Sarajevo's old town. It is dedicated to Bosnia and Herzegovina's first post-independence president, whose political life was shaped by the siege and the difficult years that followed.

The museum is small, with personal material, books, and displays on his wartime role. It is most useful if you are already exploring Vratnik, Kovači cemetery, or the lanes above Baščaršija and want more context for modern Bosnian history.
