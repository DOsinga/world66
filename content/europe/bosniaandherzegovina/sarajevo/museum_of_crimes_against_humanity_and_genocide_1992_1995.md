The Museum of Crimes Against Humanity and Genocide 1992-1995 is a small, direct museum about the Bosnian War and the violence committed during it. Photographs, survivor testimony, and video give the displays a human force that can be difficult but important to face.

It pairs naturally with Gallery 11/07/95, but the two are not the same visit: this museum looks more broadly at the war and its crimes.
