The Museum of Literature & Performing Arts is a small Sarajevo museum focused on local writers, poets, theatre, and the expressive arts. It is most rewarding for visitors with a specific interest in Bosnian literature or cultural history.

Its central location makes it easy to combine with Ferhadija, Markale Market, and the old town museums rather than planning a long separate visit.
