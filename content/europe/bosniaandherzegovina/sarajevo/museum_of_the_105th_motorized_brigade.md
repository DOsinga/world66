This small memorial museum tells the story of the 105th Motorized Brigade, one of the Bosnian Army units that defended Sarajevo during the 1992-95 siege. It is a focused, local stop rather than a broad war museum, useful for visitors trying to understand how the siege was experienced in the city's neighbourhoods.
