Sarajevo Museum 1878-1918 is a small museum near the Latin Bridge, focused on the Austro-Hungarian period from the 1878 occupation to the outbreak of World War I. Exhibits cover daily life, administration, culture, and the political tensions that shaped the city during those four decades.

Its location makes the museum easy to pair with the bridge and the old town. It is most worthwhile if you want more context for Sarajevo's role in 1914 and for the Habsburg layer of the city.
