Sarajevo Zoo, also known as Pionirska Dolina, is a small zoo and recreation park on the edge of the city. It is mainly aimed at families, with animal enclosures, playgrounds, a little train, and other child-friendly attractions.

The zoo is the oldest in Bosnia and Herzegovina and keeps species from several parts of the world. It is a simple outing rather than a major zoo, but useful for visitors travelling with children.
