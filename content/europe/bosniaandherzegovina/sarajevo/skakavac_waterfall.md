Skakavac Waterfall is one of Sarajevo's best nature escapes, dropping through forested hills north of the city. The outing is about the walk as much as the falls: cool woods, mountain air, and a sharp change from the dense streets of the basin.

It is close enough for a day trip but not a casual city-centre stroll. Go with proper footwear, water, and current route information, especially outside summer or after wet weather.
