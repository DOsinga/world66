Svrzo's House is an Ottoman-era residence preserved as part of the Sarajevo City Museum. The rooms, courtyards, and domestic details show how a well-off Muslim family lived in the late Ottoman period, making it a useful counterpoint to Sarajevo's mosques, bazaars, and war museums.
