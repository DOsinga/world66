The Tomb of the Seven Brothers is a small Sarajevo shrine and burial site with a layered local legend. The seven men buried here were not brothers by birth, but are remembered as brothers in faith, with stories linking the site to Sarajevo's early Ottoman period and later episodes in the city's history.

The walled tomb is a quiet stop near the old town. It is best visited as part of a walk through the historic core, where small religious sites often carry as much local memory as the larger monuments.
