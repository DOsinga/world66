Borja is the wooded mountain country west of Teslic. It is not a sharp alpine range; its charm is in forest roads, clear air, and soft ridges that make a relaxed day out from town.

In winter, parts of the mountain are used for recreation around Hajducke vode. In warmer months, it is better as a slow drive and walking area, especially if you fold in Liplje Monastery on the same outing.
