Liplje Monastery is the best cultural excursion from Teslic. It stands in a narrow valley by the Bistrica stream, west of town, with forested slopes around it and Borja mountain above.

The monastery is not large, but the setting gives it force. Combine it with a drive over Borja rather than treating it as a quick photo stop; the road and landscape are half the reason to come.
