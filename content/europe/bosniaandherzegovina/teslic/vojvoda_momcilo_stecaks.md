The Vojvoda Momcilo stecaks are a small group of medieval tombstones a few kilometres southeast of Teslic. They are not a major monument on the scale of Bosnia's best stecak fields, but they add an old, local layer to the town's story.

Visit only if you have a car and a little curiosity for medieval Bosnia. The stop is short, but it pairs well with Banja Vrucica and the quieter roads outside town.
