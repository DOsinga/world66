---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

Travnik is pleasant year-round. **Summer (June–August)** is warm and green; the spring at Plava Voda is particularly attractive, and Mt Vlašić above the town is an escape from the valley heat. **Autumn** brings soft light and fewer visitors. **Winter** is cold and sometimes snowy but the old bazaar has a quiet, atmospheric quality in the off-season. The town is never very crowded — even in summer it doesn't draw mass tourism.
