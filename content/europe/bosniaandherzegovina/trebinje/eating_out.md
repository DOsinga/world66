---
title: "Eating Out"
type: section
---

Trebinje eats well for a small town. Local Herzegovinian cuisine leans heavy on grilled meats, dairy and the simple Mediterranean influences that have travelled up from the coast: lamb on the spit, the local **sušeno meso** (air-dried ham, like an Italian prosciutto), young cheese, fresh figs in season, and the **Vranac** red wine from the Tvrdoš and other regional monasteries. The cafes around the old town do good Bosnian coffee — thick, sweet, served with a Turkish delight.

For a quick lunch, the **ćevapi** (small grilled minced-meat fingers) at any of the kafanas on **Mostarska** street are reliable. For something more substantial, head to a restaurant in the old town or at the river bridge.
