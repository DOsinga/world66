---
title: "Getting There"
type: section
---

Trebinje is just 30km north of [Dubrovnik](/europe/croatia/dubrovnik) and most travellers arrive from there. The drive is spectacular — up the Adriatic karst and over the mountains via the Ivanica or Brgat border crossings — and takes about 45 minutes without queues. Bus services run daily between Dubrovnik and Trebinje (about €10, 90 minutes including the border).

From elsewhere in Bosnia, Trebinje is at the southern end of a long, slow road from [Mostar](/europe/bosniaandherzegovina/mostar) (two hours, gorgeous scenery) or [Sarajevo](/europe/bosniaandherzegovina/sarajevo) (five to six hours via Foča). The closest airport is Dubrovnik (DBV); Mostar (OMO) and Tivat (TIV) in Montenegro are alternatives.
