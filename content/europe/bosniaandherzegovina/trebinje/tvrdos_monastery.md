A working Serbian Orthodox monastery on a low rise above the Trebišnjica river, about 4km west of Trebinje. The community was founded in the 4th century; the present church was built in 1517 over the ruins of an earlier monastery destroyed by the Venetians and Ottomans in turn. The compound combines a small dark church with surviving 16th-century frescoes, the monks' quarters, and a working winery.

The wine cellar can be visited and tastings are offered for a small fee; the monastery sells its **Vranac** red and **Žilavka** white directly. The cellars themselves are vaulted and cool, used continuously since the early 1500s. Free entry to the church grounds; reasonable taxi or rental-car access from Trebinje.
