---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

Trebinje has hot, dry summers and mild winters, with the Mediterranean influence coming up from Dubrovnik. **May to October** is the long warm season; **July and August** can be very hot (35°C+), with the locals retreating to cafes by day. **September and early October** are perhaps the ideal months — warm days, cool evenings, wine harvest at the surrounding monasteries.

**November to March** is the rainy season; the **bura** wind from the north can push the temperature down sharply, and the town is much quieter. The **Trebinje Summer** cultural festival in July fills the squares with music, theatre and poetry readings (in honour of Jovan Dučić, the local poet); it is the best time to visit if you want to catch a sense of the town at full social tilt.
