title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Tuzla's [Pannonica salt lakes](/europe/bosniaandherzegovina/tuzla/panonnica_lake) are the headline, but the city has a compact old town worth exploring on foot. The [Atik Behram Bey Mosque](/europe/bosniaandherzegovina/tuzla/atik_behram_bey_mosque) (the Colourful Mosque) and the [Turalibeg Mosque](/europe/bosniaandherzegovina/tuzla/turalibeg_mosque) anchor the Ottoman core, [Solni Trg](/europe/bosniaandherzegovina/tuzla/solni_trg) documents the ancient salt industry, and the [Kapija Memorial](/europe/bosniaandherzegovina/tuzla/kapija_memorial) is an unavoidable presence on the main pedestrian street.
