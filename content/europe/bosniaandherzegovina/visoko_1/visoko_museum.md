The town museum, occupying a former Austro-Hungarian school building near the centre, covers the medieval Bosnian kingdom, the Ottoman tannery industry that defined the town for four centuries, and the local archaeology — the genuine kind. Useful as a corrective if you have been on the pyramid tour and want to see what professional archaeologists have actually found in the valley.

Small collection, well laid out. An hour is enough.
