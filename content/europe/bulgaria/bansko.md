Bansko is the rare Bulgarian town that does two completely different things well. From December to April it is the country's biggest and most serious ski resort, with the [Bansko Gondola](/europe/bulgaria/bansko/bansko_gondola) lifting skiers from a sleepy stone town into a 75-kilometre marked-piste network on the slopes of [Todorka and Vihren](/europe/bulgaria/bansko/vihren_peak). From June to October it turns into the gateway for [Pirin National Park](/europe/bulgaria/bansko/pirin_national_park) — a UNESCO World Heritage Site full of glacial lakes, granite peaks and old-growth forest, with some of the best mountain hiking anywhere in the Balkans.

The town itself is the other reason to come. The [Varosha old quarter](/europe/bulgaria/bansko/varosha) is a tight grid of high-walled stone houses and cobbled lanes that survived the Communist era largely intact, anchored by the great [Holy Trinity Church](/europe/bulgaria/bansko/holy_trinity_church) and its bell tower. Bansko was a centre of the Bulgarian National Revival in the eighteenth and nineteenth centuries, and the [Bansko Icon Painting School](/europe/bulgaria/bansko/icon_painting_exhibition) was one of the most distinctive in the country. The houses of the painter [Velyan Ognev](/europe/bulgaria/bansko/velyanova_house) and the poet [Nikola Vaptsarov](/europe/bulgaria/bansko/vaptsarov_house_museum) are open as museums and worth an hour each.

The dining is excellent and surprisingly cheap. The traditional *mehana* tavern — long wooden tables, open fire, copper pots — is more or less invented in Bansko, and the local cooking leans heavily on grilled meat, beans, *kapama* (a Pirin specialty of layered meats and sauerkraut), and aged sheep's cheese. [The mehana on Pirin Street](/europe/bulgaria/bansko/mehana_bansko) and a dozen others are easy to find. The town also hosts the [Bansko International Jazz Festival](/europe/bulgaria/bansko/jazz_festival) every August, which fills the cobbled main square with free open-air concerts for a week.

Bansko is roughly two and a half hours from Sofia by car (the motorway makes it shorter than it used to be) and is reached most easily as a day or weekend trip — or longer if you ski or hike. Nearby [Dobrinishte](/europe/bulgaria/bansko/dobrinishte) has thermal baths and a smaller ski area; [Razlog](/europe/bulgaria/bansko/razlog_day_trip) is a short hop with its own slopes and a much quieter town centre.
