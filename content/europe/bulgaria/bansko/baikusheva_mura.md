The Baikusheva Mura — "Baikushev's pine" — is a thirteen-hundred-year-old Macedonian pine standing alone in a clearing in the Banderitsa valley, a few minutes' walk from the road up to the Banderitsa Hut. The trunk is over two metres across at the base, the bark deeply furrowed, and the crown still alive.

It is signposted from the road and reached by a short flat boardwalk. There are usually a few people around in summer; in winter the road is closed and you would have to ski-tour in. It costs nothing and takes ten minutes — easy to combine with a Pirin hike.
