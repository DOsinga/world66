The House-Museum Neofit Rilski preserves the 18th-century home of one of the Bulgarian National Revival's key figures. Neofit Rilski helped lay the groundwork for modern secular education in Bulgaria and is remembered as a teacher, scholar, and early encyclopedist.

The house gives Bansko a cultural stop beyond skiing and mountain scenery. Its rooms and exhibits are modest, but they put the town into the wider story of Bulgaria's 19th-century awakening.
