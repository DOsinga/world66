Koncheto is the narrow, exposed ridge between some of the highest peaks in the Pirin range. The name means "the little horse", a fair warning for the short section where hikers move along a knife-edge crest with steep drops on both sides.

The ridge is one of the classic high-mountain experiences above Bansko, with huge views over marble peaks and glacial valleys. It is for confident, well-equipped hikers in settled weather; in cloud, snow, or high wind, choose an easier Pirin route.
