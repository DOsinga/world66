The House-Museum Neofit Rilski preserves the 18th-century Bansko house of Neofit Rilski, an important figure in the Bulgarian National Revival and a founder of secular education in Bulgaria. The house also shows the town's traditional domestic architecture, with the heavy stone-and-timber construction typical of old Bansko.

It is a useful stop in the Varosha old town area, especially when combined with Holy Trinity Church and the nearby icon exhibition. The visit is short, but it gives Bansko's historic centre more context than a simple walk through the lanes.
