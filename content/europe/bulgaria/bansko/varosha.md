Varosha is Bansko's old quarter — the original town that existed before the gondola, the ski hotels and the apartment blocks. The streets are narrow, cobbled and lined with high stone walls; behind them, two- and three-storey Revival-era houses with overhanging wooden upper floors and stout wooden gates.

The quarter is small enough to wander in a couple of hours. The anchors are [Holy Trinity Church](/europe/bulgaria/bansko/holy_trinity_church) and the main square in front of it; the [Velyanova House](/europe/bulgaria/bansko/velyanova_house) and the [Vaptsarov House Museum](/europe/bulgaria/bansko/vaptsarov_house_museum) are both in the same warren of lanes. Several of the town's best *mehana* taverns are tucked into Varosha courtyards.
