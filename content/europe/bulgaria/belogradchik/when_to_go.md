---
title: When to Go
type: section
---

Late spring (May–June) and early autumn (September–October) are the best times to visit Belogradchik. The weather is comfortable for walking among the rocks, the crowds are manageable, and the light on the formations is excellent in the lower sun angles of those months. Summer (July–August) is warm and the site is busier with Bulgarian domestic tourists.

Winter is cold — northwest Bulgaria can see snow from November through March — but the rocks in snow make for dramatic photographs, and the site is virtually deserted. The fortress and rock park are open year-round.
