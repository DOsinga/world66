Blagoevgrad is a university town about 100 km south of [Sofia](/europe/bulgaria/sofia), home to both the [American University in Bulgaria](/europe/bulgaria/blagoevgrad/things_to_do/aubg) (AUBG) and South-West University. The student population gives the town a livelier atmosphere than you might expect from a mid-sized Bulgarian city.

The old quarter of [Varosha](/europe/bulgaria/blagoevgrad/things_to_do/varosha), with its preserved eighteenth- and nineteenth-century houses and cobbled streets, is the most appealing part of town. The [Church of the Assumption](/europe/bulgaria/blagoevgrad/things_to_do/church_of_the_assumption) sits within the quarter. The town also has a remarkable former synagogue — the [Gotse Delchev Temple](/europe/bulgaria/blagoevgrad/things_to_do/gotse_delchev_temple) — whose Jewish community was protected from Nazi deportation by local citizens during the Second World War.

Blagoevgrad is the closest gateway to the [Rila Monastery](/europe/bulgaria/blagoevgrad/things_to_do/rila_monastery) — the largest and most famous monastery in Bulgaria, a UNESCO World Heritage Site founded in the tenth century by St. Ivan Rilski, about 45 km away. The [Pirin Mountains](/europe/bulgaria/blagoevgrad/things_to_do/pirin_mountains) rise to the south, with Bulgaria's best skiing at Bansko and excellent hiking in summer through Pirin National Park.
