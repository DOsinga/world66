---
title: "Day Trips from Blagoevgrad"
type: section
---

Blagoevgrad's position in the Struma valley makes it an excellent base for day trips. To the northeast, [Rila Monastery](/europe/bulgaria/blagoevgrad/things_to_do/rila_monastery) is the standout excursion — Bulgaria's most important religious site and a UNESCO World Heritage Site, reachable in under an hour by car or on the morning public bus. To the southeast, [Bansko](/europe/bulgaria/bansko) and the Pirin National Park offer hiking in summer and skiing in winter. The [Melnik wine region](/europe/bulgaria/blagoevgrad/day_trips/melnik) lies 80 km south, combining Bulgaria's smallest town with its best red wines and surreal sandstone landscape.

All three day trips can be done in a full day from Blagoevgrad; a car makes them easier, though public transport connects to most.
