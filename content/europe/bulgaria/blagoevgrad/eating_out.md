---
title: "Eating Out in Blagoevgrad"
type: section
---

Blagoevgrad's restaurant scene is driven by the student population from AUBG and South-West University, which means a good range of affordable options around the pedestrian centre. Bulgarian cuisine — grilled meats, shopska salad, banitsa, and dishes from the Pirin mountain tradition — is the default. Prices are among the lowest in Bulgaria.

The pedestrian street (Makedonia Boulevard) and the surrounding streets have the highest concentration of restaurants and cafés. Traditional Bulgarian **mehanas** (taverns) offer grilled meats, village bread, and local wine or rakia in unpretentious settings. The Varosha quarter has a handful of more atmospheric spots for lunch.

Look for **kapama** on menus around here — a slow-cooked Pirin mountain dish of pork, sauerkraut, and sausage baked together in a clay pot with rice and spices, traditionally served at celebrations. It's hearty and regional, and better in Blagoevgrad than most other places in Bulgaria.
