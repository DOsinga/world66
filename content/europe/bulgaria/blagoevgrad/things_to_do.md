---
title: "Things to Do in Blagoevgrad"
type: section
---

Blagoevgrad's compact centre organises itself around two distinct zones: the modern city with its wide pedestrian boulevard and the [Varosha quarter](/europe/bulgaria/blagoevgrad/things_to_do/varosha), the old neighbourhood of cobbled streets and 18th-century houses on the hill above. Allow an hour to walk Varosha properly; the [Church of the Assumption](/europe/bulgaria/blagoevgrad/things_to_do/church_of_the_assumption) at its centre is the main sight.

The real reason to come to Blagoevgrad — and the thing that gives the city more energy than its size would suggest — is [AUBG](/europe/bulgaria/blagoevgrad/things_to_do/aubg), the American University in Bulgaria. The student population keeps bars and cafés busy well into the evening.

The main excursion from Blagoevgrad is to [Rila Monastery](/europe/bulgaria/blagoevgrad/things_to_do/rila_monastery) (45 km northeast), one of the most important religious sites in Bulgaria and a UNESCO World Heritage Site. The [Pirin Mountains](/europe/bulgaria/blagoevgrad/things_to_do/pirin_mountains) rise directly to the south, with Bansko about an hour's drive away.
