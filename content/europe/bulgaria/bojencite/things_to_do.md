---
title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Bojentsi is a preserved architectural village with no mass tourism and virtually no commercial infrastructure. The appeal is simple: a collection of Bulgarian Renaissance-era houses in a mountain setting, untouched by the 20th century. Walking the cobbled lanes, visiting the house museums, and hiking the surrounding hills covers everything the village offers — which is quite enough for a peaceful half-day or a slow overnight.
