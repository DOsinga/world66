The open-air lye and mud baths near Burgas are a rough-and-ready local spa experience beside the salt lakes and the sea. You cover yourself in mineral mud, let it dry in the sun, then rinse off in the paid showers or walk down to the water.

Do not expect luxury. The fun is in the simplicity: salty air, black mud, and a crowd of locals treating the place as part health ritual and part summer outing.
