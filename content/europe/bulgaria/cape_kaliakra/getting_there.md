---
title: "Getting There"
type: section
---

The cape is 12 km southeast of Kavarna and about 60 km from Varna airport. There is no scheduled public transport to the headland itself — the nearest village served by bus is Bulgarevo, 3 km away, from where you walk or hitch the rest. Most visitors arrive in their own car or as part of a day tour from the coastal resorts at Albena, Balchik, or Golden Sands.

The road from Kavarna is paved and signposted; the reserve has a small admission fee paid at the gate. Allow 30 minutes from Kavarna or 90 minutes from Varna. There is a basic restaurant just inside the reserve entrance and a single seafood place near the lighthouse — both close out of season.
