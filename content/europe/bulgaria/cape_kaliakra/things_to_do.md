---
title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

The cape is best treated as a half-day walk: park at the gate, follow the headland out to its tip, drop into the small archaeological museum cut into the cliff, and stop at the lighthouse on the way back. The cliffs and steppe behind them are worth lingering over — dolphins below, raptors above in migration season, and a long sea horizon that swallows the curve of the coast.
