Gabrovo sits in the Yantra valley at the foot of the central Balkan Mountains, a practical industrial city with a surprisingly playful self-image. Bulgarians know it as the capital of humour and satire, a reputation built on jokes about Gabrovo thrift and sharpened by the city's excellent [House of Humour and Satire](/europe/bulgaria/gabrovo/house_of_humour_and_satire).

The city also makes a good base for the northern slopes of the Stara Planina. The [Etar Open-Air Museum](/europe/bulgaria/gabrovo/etar_open_air_museum), just outside town, is the essential stop: water-driven workshops, craft streets, and National Revival buildings arranged as a living museum rather than a dusty display. [Sokolski Monastery](/europe/bulgaria/gabrovo/sokolski_monastery) and the road toward [Shipka Pass](/europe/bulgaria/gabrovo/shipka_pass) push you quickly into mountain history.

Gabrovo is not polished like [Veliko Tarnovo](/europe/bulgaria/velikoturnovo), and that is part of the point. Come for a day if you are circling central Bulgaria by car, or stay overnight if you want museums, mountain air, and a less obvious Bulgarian city with a dry sense of humour.
