Gorna Oryahovitsa is the practical neighbour of [Veliko Tarnovo](/europe/bulgaria/velikoturnovo): a railway junction, market town, and small industrial centre on the north side of the old capital. Most travellers know it because trains stop here before shuttle buses or taxis continue to Tarnovo.

That does not make it useless. If you have time between connections, the [Historical Museum](/europe/bulgaria/gornaoryahovica/historical_museum) gives a concise local history, while the ruins of [Rachovets Fortress](/europe/bulgaria/gornaoryahovica/rachovets_fortress) explain the older road-guarding settlement that gave the town its name. Food-minded travellers should also look for Gornooryahovski sudzhuk, the local dried beef sausage with protected status.

Gorna Oryahovitsa is not a substitute for Veliko Tarnovo. It is a useful add-on: more ordinary, less theatrical, and good for understanding how the modern transport map of north-central Bulgaria works.
