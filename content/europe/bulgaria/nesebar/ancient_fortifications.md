The remains of ancient Mesembria's fortifications greet you near the entrance to the old town. They are not as complete as a castle wall, but they matter because they mark the transition from resort coast to ancient peninsula.

Pause here before plunging into the lanes. Nesebar's charm can look purely medieval at first glance, but the defensive stones point to a much older Greek and Roman town that controlled this small, strategic piece of Black Sea coast.
