---
title: "Getting There"
type: section
---

Pleven lies on the Sofia-Varna rail line, with trains from Sofia and other cities on the route. Trains toward Ruse and Bucharest also stop here.

Buses from Sofia, Ruse, Svishtov, and several northern Bulgarian cities use Pleven as a regular stop. The bus and rail options make it a straightforward break in a north Bulgaria itinerary.
