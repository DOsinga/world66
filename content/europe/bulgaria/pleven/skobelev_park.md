Skobelev Park surrounds the Pleven Panorama and covers ground closely tied to the Siege of Pleven. Monuments, graves, and views across the city make it more than a landscaped approach to the museum.

Walk it after the Panorama, when the battle narrative is fresh. Without that context, the memorials are easier to skim past.
