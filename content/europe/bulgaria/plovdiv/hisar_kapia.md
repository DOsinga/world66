Hisar Kapia is the main surviving gate of the ancient and medieval fortifications of Plovdiv — a massive stone arch that once controlled access to the hilltop citadel. It stands at the entrance to the old town, connecting the modern city below with the National Revival quarter above.

The gate incorporates Roman, Byzantine, and Ottoman stonework in layers that reflect the city's succession of rulers. In its current form it dates primarily from the medieval period, but the base uses Roman masonry and the surrounding walls show repairs from multiple eras. It's one of the most evocative spots in Plovdiv — not because of any single dramatic feature, but because of the sheer accumulation of history compressed into a single archway.
