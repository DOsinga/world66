Sahat Tepe — Clock Hill, also called Danov Hill — is the small wooded rise between the main shopping street and the Roman stadium, five minutes from the centre and almost entirely used by locals. A path of shallow steps winds up through pines to a clearing at the top with the whitewashed Ottoman clock tower in the middle of it.

The view is lower and closer than the one from [Nebet Tepe](/europe/bulgaria/plovdiv/nebet_tepe): rooftops, the pedestrian street below, the other hills across the city. What it has instead is quiet. There is a bench or two, no kiosk, no entrance, and on a weekday afternoon you will share it with a dog walker.

It works best as a twenty-minute detour off the main street rather than a destination. The path up from the western side is the gentler of the two.
