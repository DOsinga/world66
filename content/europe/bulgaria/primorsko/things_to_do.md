---
title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Primorsko's main draws are its beaches and the surrounding Strandzha Nature Park. The town itself is modest — a few streets of restaurants and bars catering to summer visitors — but the natural setting is genuinely attractive. North of town, the Beglik Tash prehistoric rock sanctuary makes an interesting half-day excursion, and the nearby village of Kiten has equally good beaches with slightly more amenities.
