---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

Primorsko is a seasonal resort: essentially everything shuts from October to May. The season runs from June through September, with July and August being the busiest months when Bulgarian families fill the apartments and hotels. The water temperature in the Black Sea peaks in August at around 25°C. The shoulder months of June and September offer warm weather, emptier beaches, and lower prices — a clear better deal than peak season.

The Strandzha Nature Park is best visited in spring (April to May) for wildflowers and birdlife, or in autumn for mushrooms and forest colours — though you'd need your own transport and accommodation in one of the inland villages to do this properly.
