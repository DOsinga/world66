Devil's Bridge is an old stone bridge over the Arda River, set in a scenic part of the eastern Rhodope Mountains. Its arches, river setting, and local legends make it more than a roadside stop, especially if you are exploring the region by car.

The bridge is best visited as part of a wider Rhodope itinerary, with time for the drive and a short walk around the river. Go for the setting as much as the structure itself.
