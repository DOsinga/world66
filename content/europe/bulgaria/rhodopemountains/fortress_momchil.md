Fortress Momchil is a ruined hilltop stronghold in the central Rhodope Mountains. Its first major phase dates to the reign of Justinian I, when the Byzantine Empire fortified routes across the Balkans.

The walls were burned and rebuilt more than once before the Ottoman conquest ended the fortress for good. Today the appeal is the combination of early medieval history, mountain air, and wide views over the Rhodope ridges.
