Fortress Ustra is a ruined 10th-century hill fortress in the eastern Rhodopes, built to guard an important trade route. The surviving walls stand on a ridge with wide views over the surrounding hills.

The approach is part of the appeal: expect a rough uphill walk of about an hour from the nearest road. It is best for travelers who are already exploring the wilder eastern side of the mountains.
