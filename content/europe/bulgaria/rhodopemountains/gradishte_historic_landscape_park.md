Gradishte Historic Landscape Park protects a prehistoric settlement and Thracian sanctuary on a long ridge in the western Rhodope Mountains. The site has rock-cut traces, petroglyphs, and wide mountain views, and is best treated as a short archaeological walk rather than a formal museum visit.
