Karadzhov Stone, or Karadzhov Kamak, is a dramatic rock formation on a high Rhodope plateau near Mostovo. A huge stone is wedged in a cleft between vertical rock walls, reached by rough paths and steps cut into the massif.

The site also has traces of an ancient Thracian sanctuary, including small ritual pits carved into the rock. It is best treated as a hiking and archaeology stop rather than a quick roadside sight.
