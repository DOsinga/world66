The Marvelous Bridges are two huge limestone arches cut by the Erkyupriya River high in the Rhodope Mountains. The larger bridge rises about 45 metres and can be walked beneath, while the smaller arch sits a short distance downstream in the forest.

They are worth the detour if you are exploring the central Rhodopes by car. The appeal is simple: cool mountain air, forest, and a natural stone span that feels too cleanly shaped to be accidental.
