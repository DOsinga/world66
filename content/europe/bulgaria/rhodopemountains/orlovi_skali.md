Orlovi skali, or Eagles' Rocks, is a Thracian cult site near Ardino, where dozens of trapezoid niches were cut high into a rock face above the forest. The purpose of the niches is still debated, but the setting makes the short walk from the parking area feel like a small archaeological excursion rather than a roadside stop.
