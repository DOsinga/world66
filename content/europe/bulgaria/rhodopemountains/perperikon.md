Perperikon is one of Bulgaria's great archaeological sites, a hilltop complex of rock-cut halls, fortifications, sanctuaries, and megalithic remains in the eastern Rhodope Mountains. The site has layers reaching back to the Bronze Age, with later Thracian, Roman, and medieval use.

It takes effort to reach, but it is worth building into an eastern Rhodope route. The appeal is the scale: this is not a single ruin but a whole stone landscape of walls, steps, and chambers spread across the hill.
