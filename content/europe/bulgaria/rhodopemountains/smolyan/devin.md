Devin is a small spa town at the western edge of the Rhodopes, on the River Vacha, known for its mineral springs and as the gateway to the Trigrad and Buynovo gorges. The town itself is modest, but the hot mineral springs that have given it its character attract Bulgarians seeking thermal bathing, and the spa hotels along the river offer relatively affordable hydrotherapy treatments.

The main reason to visit Devin is the landscape surrounding it. The **Trigrad Gorge** is 15 kilometres to the south, and the **Buynovo Gorge** — another dramatic limestone canyon, less visited than Trigrad — is accessible on a marked hiking trail from the town. The drive from Devin into the gorges is one of the finest scenic roads in the Rhodopes, passing through dense forest and along river banks of extraordinary clarity.
