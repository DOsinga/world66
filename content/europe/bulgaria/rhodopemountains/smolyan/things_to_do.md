---
title: Things to Do
type: section
---

The Rhodope Mountains reward slow exploration. Hire a car and drive between the gorges, villages, and ski resorts; the roads are good and the landscape constantly changes. The villages in the central Rhodopes contain some of Bulgaria's finest traditional architecture, and the cave systems here — Devil's Throat, Uhlovitsa — are among the most dramatic in the country. Skiing at Pamporovo in winter, hiking and waterfall-chasing in summer.
