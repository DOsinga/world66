Ruse’s EcoMuseum and Aquarium gives the Danube city a natural-history counterpoint to its grand architecture and National Revival monuments. Exhibits cover river ecosystems, fossils, wildlife, and the environmental story of the region, with aquarium displays that make it useful for families.

It is not the first stop in Ruse, but it rounds out the city well, especially if you are travelling with children or heading onward into the Danube wetlands. Pair it with the Regional Historical Museum to see both the human and natural sides of the lower Danube.
