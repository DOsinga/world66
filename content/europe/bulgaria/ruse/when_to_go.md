---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

Ruse has a continental climate — hot summers and cold winters, with the Danube's moderating influence keeping temperatures slightly milder than inland Bulgaria. Summer (June–August) is the most pleasant time for outdoor sightseeing: the Freedom Square cafes are full in the evenings, and the trip to Ivanovo Rock Monastery is best done in good weather.

Spring and autumn are also excellent. The Danube valley has a particular atmospheric quality in late September and October when the light is soft and the barge traffic on the river is visible through the haze. Winter is cold — January temperatures often fall below freezing — and several smaller museums have reduced hours, but the city doesn't shut down entirely.

The March Music Days festival, held annually in Ruse since 1961, brings classical and chamber music performances throughout March and is one of the oldest music festivals in Bulgaria.
