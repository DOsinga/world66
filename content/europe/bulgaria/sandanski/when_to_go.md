---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

Sandanski has the warmest and mildest climate in Bulgaria, sheltered from cold northern air by the Pirin massif and warmed by proximity to the Mediterranean climate zone. Winters are mild by Bulgarian standards — temperatures rarely drop below -5°C — and the town receives over 2,400 hours of sunshine annually, significantly more than Sofia.

Spring (April–June) and autumn (September–October) are the best seasons for a visit: warm enough to sit in the park and explore on foot, comfortable for hiking in the Pirin foothills, and less crowded than the summer peak. Summer (July–August) is hot and can be uncomfortably so — up to 38–40°C — but the spa hotels and shaded park make it manageable. The town is busy in summer with Bulgarian domestic tourists seeking the mineral water treatments.

For hiking in the Pirin mountains, July and August are the best months for the high peaks, though afternoon thunderstorms are common. Snow can persist on the upper Pirin until June.
