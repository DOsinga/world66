Sofia is a compact, walkable capital with a relaxed atmosphere and a quality-of-life that surprises most first-time visitors. It is one of the cheaper European capitals, has good museums, excellent café culture, and easy access to the [Vitosha mountain](/europe/bulgaria/sofia/vitosha_mountain) that rises directly above the southern suburbs.

The [Alexander Nevsky Cathedral](/europe/bulgaria/sofia/nevskichurch), built in the early 20th century to honor the Russian soldiers who died in the Russo-Turkish War of 1877–78, is the city's most recognizable landmark — one of the largest Eastern Orthodox cathedrals in the world, with a gilded dome and elaborate Byzantine interior. The adjacent crypt houses a large collection of icons. The older [St. Sofia Basilica](/europe/bulgaria/sofia/stsofiachurch) nearby, which gives the city its name, dates from the 6th century and is considerably more austere. The tiny brick [Rotunda of Saint George](/europe/bulgaria/sofia/church_of_st_george), hidden in a courtyard behind the Presidential Palace, is older still and contains three layers of medieval frescoes.

The [National History Museum](/europe/bulgaria/sofia/national_history_museum), in the former communist-era residence on the outskirts of the city, has the best overview of Bulgarian history — Thracian gold, Roman mosaics, medieval art, and the story of the Bulgarian National Revival. The Sofia History Museum in the [old central bathhouse](/europe/bulgaria/sofia/central_mineral_baths) covers the city's own history. The [National Archaeological Museum](/europe/bulgaria/sofia/national_archaeological_museum) is in the former Great Mosque and worth an hour. Don't miss the medieval frescoes of [Boyana Church](/europe/bulgaria/sofia/boyana_church), a UNESCO site on the southern edge of the city.

The area around [Vitosha Boulevard](/europe/bulgaria/sofia/vitosha_boulevard) and the pedestrianised city centre is pleasant for walking and eating. The Lozenets and Studentski Grad (student town) neighborhoods have the most interesting bar and restaurant scenes. The [Women's Market (Zhenski Pazar)](/europe/bulgaria/sofia/zhenski_pazar) is Sofia's main produce market and a good place to eat cheaply among locals; the more elegant [Central Market Hall](/europe/bulgaria/sofia/central_market_hall) near the synagogue is the indoor counterpart.

Vitosha mountain is accessible from the city by tram and gondola lift. The hike to Cherni Vruh (2,290 metres) takes about 3 hours from the gondola top station and gives views over the entire Sofia basin on a clear day.

From Sofia, the [Rila Monastery](/europe/bulgaria/rilamonastery) is an easy day trip (about 2 hours by car). [Plovdiv](/europe/bulgaria/plovdiv) is 2 hours by bus or train.
