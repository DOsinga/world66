---
title: "Getting Around"
type: section
---

Sofia is small enough to walk. The historic core, from Alexander Nevsky to the Palace of Culture, is barely a kilometre across; the central pedestrian streets — Vitosha Boulevard and Graf Ignatiev — are where most visitors spend their time.

**Metro.** Four lines, cheap, clean, and the easiest way to cover longer distances. M4 connects the airport to the centre; M2 runs north–south past the train station; M3 reaches Lozenets and the university. Buy a single-ride ticket or a 24-hour pass from machines at any station.

**Trams and buses** cover everywhere the metro does not. The same tickets work across the network. Validate on board — fines for unvalidated tickets are routinely enforced on tourists.

**Taxis** are cheap by Western European standards but the unregulated airport touts are a long-running scam. Stick to OK Supertrans (yellow cabs, clearly marked), or use Yellow Taxi or Bolt via the app. Confirm the meter is running before you start.

**Vitosha mountain** is reached by the No. 64 or 66 bus to Simeonovo, then the gondola lift up to Aleko. The whole trip from the centre takes about an hour.
