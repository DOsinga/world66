---
title: "Getting There"
type: section
---

**Sofia Airport (SOF)** is 10 km east of the centre with direct flights to most European capitals. The metro M4 line runs from Terminal 2 to the centre in 20 minutes for the price of a normal ticket — by far the easiest way in. Taxis from the official rank at the airport are inexpensive but stick to the regulated companies (OK Supertrans is the standard).

**Trains** arrive at Sofia Central Station north of the centre. The line from Belgrade goes via Niš and crosses the border at Kalotina; the line south to Thessaloniki goes via Kulata. Domestic services connect Sofia with Plovdiv (1.5 hr), Varna (7 hr), and Burgas (6.5 hr).

**Long-distance buses** use the Central Bus Station next to the train station. Service is dense to neighbouring countries: Skopje, Niš, Bucharest, Istanbul, and Thessaloniki all have multiple daily departures. Domestic buses cover everywhere a train does not, often faster.
