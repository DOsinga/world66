The Monument to Vasil Levski marks the place where Bulgaria's great revolutionary leader was hanged by Ottoman authorities in 1873. Levski is one of the central figures in Bulgarian national memory, and the monument is treated with real respect.

It is a quick stop rather than a long visit, but it adds useful context to Sofia's 19th-century history and the national revival that shaped modern Bulgaria.
