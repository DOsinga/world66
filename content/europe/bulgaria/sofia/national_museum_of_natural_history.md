Sofia's National Museum of Natural History has four floors of exhibits covering minerals, fossils, insects, birds, mammals, and other natural-history collections. The displays are traditional but broad, and the central location makes it an easy rainy-day museum.

It is a useful stop for families and for anyone interested in Bulgaria's geology and biodiversity.
