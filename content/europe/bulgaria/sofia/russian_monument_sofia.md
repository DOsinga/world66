The Russian Monument is a compact but prominent marker in Sofia's westward urban fabric, commemorating Russian forces in the Russo-Turkish War. Traffic now swirls around it, which gives the memorial a slightly odd, everyday intensity.

It is not a destination to cross town for on its own, but it is worth noticing if you are walking between central Sofia and the residential districts beyond. The monument adds one more layer to a city where imperial, national, socialist, and modern symbols often sit within a few blocks.
