---
title: "Bars and Cafes"
type: section
---

Sozopol is not a clubbing destination — for that you go to Sunny Beach, about thirty kilometres up the coast, which is exactly the sort of place Sozopol's regulars come here to avoid. The Old Town has a cluster of small bars and cafes along the cliff paths above both beaches; in summer they stay open until two or three in the morning, but quietly.
