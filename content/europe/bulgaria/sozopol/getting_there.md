---
title: "Getting There"
type: section
---

Sozopol is 35 kilometres south of Burgas. The nearest airport is **Burgas (BOJ)**, with seasonal connections from across Europe in summer and a year-round service to Sofia. From the airport, a taxi to Sozopol runs about 40 lev (€20). The number 6 bus runs from Burgas central station to Sozopol roughly every 30 minutes in season; the trip takes about 50 minutes.

By car from Sofia, the drive across the country is around 380 kilometres on the A1 and A6 motorways and takes four and a half hours. The route from Istanbul, around 410 kilometres up the Black Sea coast, takes about five hours including the border crossing.

There is no rail service to Sozopol. From [Burgas](/europe/bulgaria/burgas), buses and minibuses run all day in summer.
