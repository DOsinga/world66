---
title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Stara Zagora's standout attraction is the Neolithic Dwellings Museum — 8,000-year-old ruins in situ in the city centre — but the city has more to offer than this single site. Roman ruins from Augusta Traiana are scattered through the grid streets, and Ayazmoto Park covers a large forested hillside. The State Opera stages productions year-round in a dedicated theatre.
