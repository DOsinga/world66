St Nicholas Church was completed in 1865 and is dedicated to the patron saint of sailors and fishermen. Step inside for the carved wooden work, then wander the nearby Fishermen's Quarter, where old houses and narrow lanes still hint at the river trade that shaped Tutrakan.
