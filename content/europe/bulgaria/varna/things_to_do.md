Varna rewards the curious visitor. The Archaeological Museum alone justifies the trip — it holds one of the most astonishing collections of prehistoric gold in the world. Add a Roman bath complex, a working naval museum on the waterfront, Soviet-era oddities, and several kilometres of park along the Black Sea coast, and you have more than enough to fill a few days.
