The German Underground Hospital is a huge tunnel complex dug during the occupation of Guernsey in the Second World War. It was built to treat casualties from an Allied assault that never came, and its scale still feels unsettling.

The visit is plain rather than polished: long, damp corridors, wartime rooms, and the sense of an island turned into a fortress. Bring a layer even in summer.
