Sausmarez Manor is an old Guernsey estate whose long history shows in its uneven, much-altered house. The bigger draw for most visitors is outside: subtropical gardens and a sculpture trail with contemporary works spread through the grounds.

The manor house is normally seen by guided tour, while the gardens and Artpark are easier to fit into a flexible visit. It makes a good change of pace from St Peter Port's harbour and military history sights.
