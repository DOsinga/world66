---
title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

St Peter Port is a compact town best explored on foot. The harbour is the natural starting point — walk along the quayside, look at the boats, and then climb the narrow lanes that rise steeply behind it toward the upper town. Everything of interest is within walking distance.

[Castle Cornet](/europe/channelislands/guernsey/st_peter_port/castle_cornet) is the dominant landmark, jutting into the harbour on its rocky promontory. [Hauteville House](/europe/channelislands/guernsey/st_peter_port/hauteville_house), Victor Hugo's eccentric former home, is a short climb uphill and one of the most distinctive literary interiors in the British Isles. The [Guernsey Museum](/europe/channelislands/guernsey/st_peter_port/guernsey_museum) sits within the Victorian [Candie Gardens](/europe/channelislands/guernsey/st_peter_port/candie_gardens), which are worth visiting for the harbour views alone.

For something quieter, the [Town Church](/europe/channelislands/guernsey/st_peter_port/town_church) near the waterfront is one of the oldest buildings in Guernsey, and the [La Valette Underground Military Museum](/europe/channelislands/guernsey/st_peter_port/la_valette_museum) — built in German-occupied tunnels during World War II — is one of the best in the Channel Islands on that subject.
