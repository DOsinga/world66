---
title: Getting There
type: section
---

The only way to Herm is by **boat from St Peter Port**, Guernsey. The Travel Trident ferry runs the 20-minute crossing several times a day from Easter to October, less frequently in winter. Tickets are sold at the Guernsey harbour office and aboard.

Sailings depend on the tide — Herm's harbour dries out at low water and the bigger ferries divert to the **Rosaire steps** on the south coast instead, a longer walk into the village. Check the schedule.

There is no airport on Herm and **no cars**. From the harbour you walk everywhere. Day-trippers normally catch the last ferry back around 5 or 6 pm; staying overnight requires booking the small **White House Hotel** or one of the self-catering cottages well in advance for summer.
