A kilometre of beach on the north-east coast of Herm composed almost entirely of **broken shell** rather than sand. Crunchy underfoot, pale white, and edged by shallow turquoise water that genuinely could pass for the Caribbean on a bright day. Children spend whole afternoons picking through the shell-grit for unbroken specimens.

The water is shallow for fifty metres and warm by Channel Islands standards in July and August. A small beach kiosk serves drinks, ice cream, and toasted sandwiches in summer. Twenty-five minutes' walk north of the harbour along the gravel main path.
