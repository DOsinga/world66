---
title: Things to Do
type: section
---

Herm is for walking, swimming, and lying down. The **clifftop coastal path** circles the whole island in three hours at an easy pace, with constantly changing views of Sark, Jethou, and the Guernsey coast. The northern beaches — **Shell Beach** and **Mousonnière** — are the swimming destinations; the southern cliffs are for views. The **village** at the harbour is the only built-up area, with a single pub, a chapel, and a small shop.

Herm gets day-tripper crowds in July and August but they thin out within ten minutes of the harbour. By 5 pm when the last ferry to Guernsey has left, the island reverts to its 60 permanent residents.
