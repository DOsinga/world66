---
title: When to Go
type: section
---

Herm is fully open from **Easter to October**. **June and September** are the best months: long days, warm-enough sea, smaller crowds. **July and August** bring the largest day-trip numbers but the island absorbs them easily — walk a kilometre from the harbour and you have the cliffs to yourself.

**Winter** is bleak, beautiful, and very quiet. The pub and shop stay open with reduced hours for the resident population; the ferries run a much reduced schedule. Not the best season for a first visit, but a stunning one for a return.
