Jersey War Tunnels are a large underground complex built by forced labour during the German occupation of the island. The tunnels were intended as bombproof infrastructure and later adapted as a wartime hospital. Today the site is one of Jersey's strongest occupation-history museums, with exhibits on daily life, resistance, collaboration, and liberation.
