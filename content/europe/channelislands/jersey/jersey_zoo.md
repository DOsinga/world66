Jersey Zoo was founded by conservationist and writer Gerald Durrell and is still run by the Durrell Wildlife Conservation Trust. Its focus is endangered species, with highlights including gorillas, bats, and free-ranging monkeys.

The zoo is one of Jersey's strongest family attractions and also one of its most distinctive cultural stops, because conservation is central to the whole visit rather than just a side theme.
