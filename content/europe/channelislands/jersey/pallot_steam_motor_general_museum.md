Pallot Steam, Motor & General Museum is an eclectic Jersey museum with steam engines, vintage vehicles, machinery, and assorted historic objects. The collection is small but varied, with particular appeal for transport and engineering enthusiasts.

It is outside St Helier, so it is best treated as a deliberate side trip rather than a quick city-centre stop.
