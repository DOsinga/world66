Tucked into the centre of town, 16 New Street is a Georgian merchant's townhouse preserved almost exactly as its inhabitants left it in the mid-1800s. Jersey Heritage has restored it to the period and opened it as a museum of Georgian domestic life. The kitchen, parlour, and bedrooms are arranged as if the family just stepped out, with period furniture, wallpapers, and fittings that have survived almost unaltered.

It is a small museum — you can do it in 45 minutes — but a surprisingly affecting one. The specificity of everyday objects makes the Georgian world feel immediate in a way that conventional museum cases rarely achieve. Entrance is included with a Jersey Museum ticket or Jersey Heritage pass.
