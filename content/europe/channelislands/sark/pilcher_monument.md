The Pilcher Monument is a small obelisk on the southwestern cliffs of Sark, erected in memory of a London merchant who drowned in a boating accident here in 1868. It is worth visiting not so much for the monument itself as for the coastal scenery — the cliffs on this part of the island are among the highest on Sark, and the view south toward Little Sark and out into the Atlantic is excellent.

The path from the village takes about 40 minutes on foot or 20 minutes by bicycle. The cliffs below are a nesting site for seabirds in spring and early summer.
