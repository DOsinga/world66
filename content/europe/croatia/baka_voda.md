**Baška Voda** is a small seaside town on the Makarska Riviera, on the Croatian Adriatic coast south of [Split](/europe/croatia/split). It sits at the foot of [Biokovo Nature Park](/europe/croatia/baka_voda/biokovo_nature_park), with pebble beaches facing the island of Brac across a narrow channel.

The town is quieter and more relaxed than neighbouring [Makarska](/europe/croatia/dalmatia/makarska), with a [pleasant waterfront promenade](/europe/croatia/baka_voda/waterfront_promenade) lined with cafes, a handful of good seafood restaurants, and clear swimming water. The [town beach](/europe/croatia/baka_voda/town_beach) has some of the clearest sea on the riviera, and the pine-shaded coastal path leads north 3 kilometres to the famous beaches at [Brela](/europe/croatia/baka_voda/brela_beach).

Biokovo dominates the landscape inland — the limestone massif rises almost vertically from the shore to 1,700 metres. The Skywalk viewpoint at 1,228 metres offers dramatic views along the coast and out to the islands. The [Sveti Ilija Tunnel](/europe/croatia/baka_voda/sveti_ilija_tunnel) cuts through the mountain and opens up easy access to the extraordinary Imotski lakes in the hinterland. South of town, the small settlement of [Krvavica](/europe/croatia/baka_voda/krvavica_beach) has a quieter beach and fewer tourists.
