---
title: Getting There
type: section
---

The nearest airport is Split, about 60 kilometres north. Ryanair, easyJet, and Croatian Airlines serve Split from a wide range of European cities in summer. From the airport, you can take a bus or shared transfer south along the coast — the journey takes about an hour.

Baška Voda sits on the main coastal road (the Jadranska magistrala, or D8), which runs the length of the Croatian coast from Rijeka to Dubrovnik. Regular bus services connect the town with Split and Makarska; the Split bus takes about 80 minutes. Driving from Split takes 45 to 60 minutes depending on traffic in summer. There is no train connection.
