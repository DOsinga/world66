---
title: Things to Do
type: section
---

Baška Voda punches above its size for a small Dalmatian resort. The pebble beaches, clear Adriatic water, and the dramatic limestone wall of Mount Biokovo rising behind the town make it worth more than a passing stop. Most visitors spend their days between the waterfront and the sea, but the hiking trails up Biokovo and the nearby villages add real depth.
