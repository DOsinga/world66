---
title: When to Go
type: section
---

The Makarska Riviera has a classic Mediterranean climate: hot, dry summers and mild, wet winters. July and August are peak season — the beach is full, prices are high, and accommodation books out months in advance. June and September are significantly more pleasant: the sea is still warm enough to swim, the crowds are smaller, and the light is better.

May is lovely for hiking on Biokovo, when the mountain is still green from winter rain and the endemic flowers are in bloom. October brings the first autumn rains but is still mild enough for swimming into early in the month.

Baška Voda essentially closes down from November to April — most restaurants and many accommodation options shut for the off-season, which is fine if you want the town to yourself but worth knowing before you plan a winter visit.
