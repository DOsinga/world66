Bol is the main resort town on [Brač](/europe/croatia/brac), a stone-built harbour on the island's southern coast that has grown up around its famous beach. [Zlatni Rat](/europe/croatia/bol/zlatni_rat) (Golden Horn), a long triangular spit of white pebble that juts 500 metres into the sea and visibly shifts shape with the wind and currents, is a five-minute walk or water-taxi ride from the harbour and one of the most photographed beaches in Croatia.

The old town behind the promenade is small and easy to wander — stone lanes, a scattering of Renaissance houses, and a wine bar or two tucked into the old quarter. [Vidova Gora](/europe/croatia/brac/vidova_gora), the highest point on any Adriatic island, rises directly above the town and is reachable by a good road or a marked hiking trail. In summer the promenade between the harbour and Zlatni Rat is the social centre of the island after dark.
