---
title: "Bars and Cafes"
type: section
---

The nightlife scene on Brač is modest — this is a beach island, not a party island. Bol has the most bars, with a strip running along the waterfront that fills up in July and August. Supetar is quieter. The real pleasure here is a cold beer or a glass of local prošek (sweet dessert wine) on a terrace after dark, with the sea in front of you.
