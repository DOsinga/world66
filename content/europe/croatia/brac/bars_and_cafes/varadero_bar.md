The most popular beach bar in Bol, right on the promenade between the harbour and Zlatni Rat. Cocktails, cold beer, and a DJ most evenings from June to September. It gets loud after midnight. The terrace looks out over the sea and the view is best around sunset — arrive early for a good seat.
