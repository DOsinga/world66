A small wine bar in Bol's old town, one street back from the main promenade, specialising in Dalmatian wines including bottles from local producers on Brač and nearby Hvar. The prošek (sweet dessert wine) and the Plavac Mali red are both worth trying. The terrace under the stone arch is one of the more pleasant places to sit in the evening — quiet enough to hold a conversation.
