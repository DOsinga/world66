One of the few genuinely sandy beaches on Brač, hidden in a bay on the north coast near Postira. The ruins of a 5th-century early Christian basilica stand just behind the shore — a strange and pleasant combination of swimming and archaeology. Less crowded than the Bol beaches because it requires a short walk or a 4WD track to reach.
