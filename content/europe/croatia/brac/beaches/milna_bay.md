A sheltered inlet on the south-west coast with calm, clear water and several small pebble coves accessible on foot or by boat. The town of Milna itself has a well-preserved Baroque church and a yacht marina; it makes a quieter base than Bol for exploring the western end of the island. The beaches around the bay are never as crowded as Zlatni Rat.
