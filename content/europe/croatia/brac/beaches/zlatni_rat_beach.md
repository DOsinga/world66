Croatia's most famous beach — a 500-metre pebble spit that juts into the sea and visibly changes shape with the current. The water is clear and shallow on both sides of the point. Shaded by pine trees further back, but the tip gets no shade at all. Reach it on foot or by water taxi from Bol harbour (5 minutes).
