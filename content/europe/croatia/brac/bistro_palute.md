On the Supetar waterfront, a good option when you arrive on the ferry and are not yet ready to drive across the island. Fresh fish, grilled calamari, and pasta dishes at reasonable prices. The terrace faces the harbour and catches the breeze on hot days. Better value than the restaurants closer to the beach strip.
