A relaxed café-bar on the Supetar waterfront, open from morning coffee through to late evening drinks. Good spot to wait for the ferry or recover after the crossing. Local people use it as much as tourists do, which keeps the atmosphere pleasant. Serves ice cream and simple snacks as well as drinks.
