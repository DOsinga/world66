Brač's position in central Dalmatia makes it a good base for excursions. [Split](/europe/croatia/split), just 50 minutes away by ferry, is one of the best cities in Croatia — Diocletian's Palace, where the stone for the walls was quarried on this very island, is reason enough. [Hvar](/europe/croatia/hvar), Croatia's most glamorous island, is reached by a short summer boat service from Bol or via Split. [Trogir](/europe/croatia/trogir), a UNESCO-listed medieval town on a small island connected by bridge to the mainland, is an easy day trip from Split that pairs well with a ferry crossing.
