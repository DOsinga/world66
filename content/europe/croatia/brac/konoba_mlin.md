A reliable konoba (traditional Dalmatian tavern) in Bol, a short walk back from the waterfront. The menu follows the season and the catch: grilled fish, octopus salad, and pasta with scampi are all done well. The lamb slow-cooked under the peka (a cast-iron lid with embers heaped on top) needs to be ordered a day in advance but is worth planning for. Outdoor terrace in summer.
