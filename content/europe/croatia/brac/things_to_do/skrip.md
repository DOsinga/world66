The oldest inhabited settlement on Brač, sitting on a hilltop in the island's interior with walls that go back to the Illyrian period. The village is compact and quiet, with medieval houses built from the island's characteristic white limestone. The Brač Museum, housed in the 16th-century Radojković Tower, covers the island's history from prehistoric times through the Roman period to the present day — the Roman mausoleum in the courtyard is the best of it.

Škrip is a 10-minute drive from Supetar and easy to combine with a visit to Splitska, the small harbour town below.
