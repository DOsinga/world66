---
title: "Getting There"
type: section
---

Access to Brijuni is controlled — it is a national park and you cannot simply drive up to a ferry and cross. The standard approach is through Fažana, a small fishing village about eight kilometres from Pula, which is the only departure point for the islands. National park ferries run several times a day in summer and less frequently outside the peak season.

The simplest option is to book one of the organised tours from Fažana, which typically include the ferry crossing, an island buggy tour, the Tito museum and safari park. Independent access is possible — you pay the national park entry fee and are free to explore on foot or by bicycle — but the guided tour gives you the buggy circuit and commentary that covers the main sites efficiently. Pula, the nearest significant city, is about 30 minutes by bus or car from Fažana. Ferries take around fifteen minutes to cross to Veli Brijun.
