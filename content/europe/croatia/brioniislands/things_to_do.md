---
title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Brijuni rewards slow exploration. The main island, Veli Brijun, has no private cars — you move by electric buggy, bicycle, or horse-drawn carriage, which gives the place an unusual atmosphere. The safari park, the Tito museum, and the Roman ruins are the three main draws, but simply cycling through the forested interior is reason enough to come.
