---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

The best time to visit Brijuni is May, June, or September. The island is at its most beautiful in late spring when the meadows are green and the crowds have not yet arrived. July and August bring the peak summer crush — the islands become significantly busier, the tours more hurried, and accommodation scarce if you plan to stay overnight.

The national park operates year-round, but ferry frequency drops significantly from October through April, and some facilities close. A winter visit is possible and has a particular quality — the island stripped of tourists, the ruins quiet, the deer undisturbed — but you need to check the ferry schedule carefully before planning around it.
