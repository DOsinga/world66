title: "Things to Do"

---

Klek is the kind of place where doing very little is the whole point. The sheltered cove, the limestone hills behind, and the long peninsula that gives the village its name make up the natural draw. Most visitors spend their days swimming, walking the coast path, or boating out into the Bay of Mali Ston for fresh oysters and mussels.
