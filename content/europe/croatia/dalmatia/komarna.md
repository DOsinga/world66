Komarna is a small village on the mainland coast of Dalmatia, in the Dubrovnik-Neretva region, directly opposite the Pelješac peninsula across the narrow [Bay of Mali Ston](/europe/croatia/dalmatia/komarna/bay_of_mali_ston). It sits on a hillside above a [small harbour](/europe/croatia/dalmatia/komarna/komarna_harbour), looking across to the oyster beds that made Mali Ston and Ston famous throughout Croatia.

The village is quiet — a handful of houses, a harbour, and not much else — but its position makes it a useful base. The [Pelješac Bridge](/europe/croatia/dalmatia/komarna/peljesac_bridge), completed in 2022, connects the mainland here to the Pelješac peninsula, finally giving Croatia continuous road access to Dubrovnik without passing through Bosnia-Herzegovina.

From Komarna, the oyster towns of **Ston** and [Mali Ston](/europe/croatia/dalmatia/komarna/mali_ston) are minutes away across the water by boat or a short drive via the bridge. The walled town of Ston has the [longest defensive walls](/europe/croatia/dalmatia/komarna/walls_of_ston) in Europe after the Great Wall of China. [Mostar](/europe/bosniaandherzegovina/mostar) and Medjugorje in Bosnia are accessible via the nearby border crossing for those combining Croatia with a Bosnian excursion.
