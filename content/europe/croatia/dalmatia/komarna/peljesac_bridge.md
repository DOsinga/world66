  Opened in July 2022, the 2,440-metre cable-stayed bridge finally gave
  Croatia an uninterrupted road link to Dubrovnik. Until then, every car

The Pelješac Bridge crosses the Bay of Mali Ston from the Komarna shore to the Pelješac peninsula, a graceful pair of pylons supporting a fan of white cables that has rapidly become the new icon of the southern Croatian coast. Komarna sits directly below the mainland abutment.

The best views are from the water — a short kayak or boat trip from Komarna passes underneath — or from the small park at the foot of the pylon on the village side. At night the bridge is lit blue and gold, visible from miles up and down the coast. A handful of viewpoints have appeared along the local roads above the village; the most photographed is the one signposted "Vidikovac" on the road up to Brijesta.
