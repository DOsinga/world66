---
title: "Things To Do"
type: section
---

Komarna itself is a couple of streets, a harbour, and a beach — pleasant, quiet, and not the reason most people come. The bay, the bridge, and the oyster towns are the draw.

A typical day combines a swim from the village harbour, a boat ride to the oyster farms in the Bay of Mali Ston, and a long lunch in Mali Ston. With more time, climb the walls of Ston, drive the wine villages of Pelješac, or head north to the Neretva delta for a flat-bottomed-boat safari.
