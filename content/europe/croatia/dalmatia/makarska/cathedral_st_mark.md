The treasury of the Church of St Mark holds a small but rich collection of liturgical silver, vestments, and panel paintings gathered over centuries from local churches and the Franciscan monastery. Highlights include 17th-century reliquaries and a 15th-century crucifix.

Open in season alongside the church. Modest entry fee. Suitable for a brief stop after visiting the church itself.
