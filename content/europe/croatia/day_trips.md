Croatia's compact geography makes day trips easy: the coast, the islands, and the mountainous interior are rarely more than a couple of hours apart. From the Dalmatian hub cities of Split, Šibenik, and Zadar, the standout outing is [Krka National Park](/europe/croatia/krka_national_park_1), where the Krka River drops over seventeen travertine cascades at Skradinski Buk and a boat carries you upriver to the island monastery of Visovac. Come early in summer, before the boardwalks fill with coach groups.

Near Dubrovnik, the [Trsteno Arboretum](/europe/croatia/trsteno_arboretum) — one of the oldest Renaissance gardens in this part of Europe, founded in 1492 — sits 20 kilometres up the coast and makes an easy half-day escape from the old town.

Crossing into Bosnia and Herzegovina opens up more options. [Medjugorje](/europe/bosniaandherzegovina/medjugorje), the Marian pilgrimage town that draws millions of visitors, is around two and a half hours from Dubrovnik or Split and often paired with a stop in Mostar. Bring your passport and check current border-crossing requirements before you set off.
