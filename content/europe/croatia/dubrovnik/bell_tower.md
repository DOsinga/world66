Dubrovnik's Bell Tower rises over Luža Square at the eastern end of Stradun, one of the Old Town's most familiar landmarks. Its clock is struck by the bronze figures known as the Zelenci, now copies, while the originals are kept in the Sponza Palace.

The tower is an easy landmark to fold into a walk between Sponza Palace, Orlando's Column, and the Church of St. Blaise. It is most useful as a visual anchor in the dense eastern end of the walled city.
