The Elaphiti archipelago consists of 13 islands north of Dubrovnik, of which three are inhabited: Koločep, Lopud, and Šipan. All three are car-free. Lopud has the most visited beach (Šunj, on the far side of the island), reachable by a 20-minute walk through an olive grove. Šipan is the largest and quietest, with vineyards and a summer palace of the Ragusan nobility. Regular ferries run from Dubrovnik's old port (Stari Grad); day trips by boat are popular but do the islands a disservice — they deserve a night.
