Dubrovnik’s Synagogue and Jewish Museum occupy a discreet building in the Old Town, preserving one of Europe’s older Sephardic synagogue spaces. The interior is intimate, with ritual objects and documents that point to the long Jewish presence inside the walled city.

It is a small visit, but a meaningful one. Among Dubrovnik’s stone streets, churches, and palaces, the synagogue adds a quieter layer of history and makes the Old Town feel less like a single-story monument.
