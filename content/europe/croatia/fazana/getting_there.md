---
title: "Getting There"
type: section
---

Fazana is 8 kilometres northwest of Pula, which has an international airport with summer flights from across Europe. From Pula, bus services run to Fazana throughout the day — the journey takes about 20 minutes. By car from Pula, take the coastal road north; there is paid parking near the harbour.

Pula is also connected to Zagreb by train (about 4 hours), and by ferry to various Italian ports (Ancona, Venice) in summer. Fazana itself is reached only by road from Pula.
