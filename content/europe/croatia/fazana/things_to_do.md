---
title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Fazana's main appeal is its simplicity — good swimming, fresh fish, and the boats to Brijuni National Park. The medieval churches scattered through the old town are worth a leisurely hour, and the harbour promenade is pleasant for an evening walk. Most visitors use Fazana as a quiet alternative base to Pula, taking day trips to Brijuni and the Istrian interior.
