Bjelolasica is the main ski resort in Gorski Kotar and the largest ski area in Croatia, topping out at 1534 metres. It is not large by European standards — around 20 km of piste, mostly blue and red runs — but it has reliable snow cover from December to March and is easily accessible from Rijeka (about 70 km) and Zagreb (about 120 km).

In summer, the resort pivots to hiking, mountain biking, and accommodation for walkers exploring the surrounding Velika Kapela mountain range. The terrain around Bjelolasica is significantly wilder and less visited than Risnjak, making it a good choice if you want to spend a night in the mountains without encountering many other hikers.
