---
title: Getting Around
type: section
---

A car is essentially required for getting around Gorski Kotar. The region's attractions are spread across a large forested area with very limited public transport between individual villages and trailheads.

The main town, Delnice, has bus connections to Rijeka and Zagreb, and the A6 motorway passes through the region making it easy to reach by car. From Delnice you can explore much of the region along smaller forest roads, most of which are paved and in reasonable condition.

Cycling is practical on the lake shores and in the valleys; mountain biking is well-developed on trails around Bjelolasica. The hiking trail network is extensive and well-marked — most trailheads can be reached by car, and some by bus from Delnice in summer.
