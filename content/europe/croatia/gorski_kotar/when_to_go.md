---
title: When to Go
type: section
---

Gorski Kotar has two good seasons. Summer (June to September) is when the region comes into its own as a refuge from the coastal heat — temperatures in the mountains are typically 10–15°C cooler than Rijeka or Split, the air is clear after afternoon thunderstorms, and the hiking trails are at their best. July and August are popular with Croatian families on their way to and from the Adriatic.

Winter (December to March) brings skiing at Bjelolasica and Platak, with reliable snow cover above 1000 metres. The forests look remarkable under snow, and the Risnjak trails are passable in good weather with proper equipment.

Spring and autumn are quieter and have their own rewards — wildflowers in May, mushroom season in October, and the forests turning vivid amber and red in late September. These are arguably the best times to visit if your goal is hiking without crowds.

The one caution: Gorski Kotar has some of the highest annual rainfall in Croatia. Afternoon thunderstorms are common in summer, and the region can receive significant snow at any time from October to April. Check conditions before setting out on long mountain routes.
