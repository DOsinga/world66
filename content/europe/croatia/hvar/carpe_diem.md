Carpe Diem is the bar that turned Hvar into a destination for the international clubbing set, sitting on the harbour at the eastern end of the Hvar Town riva. By day it is a sleek lounge with cocktails and tapas; by night it is the most prominent of the town's pre-club bars, where the crowds gather before water taxis ferry them out to the affiliated Carpe Diem Beach Club on Marinkovac in the Pakleni Islands.

Drinks are not cheap (cocktails €15+) and the door can be picky on busy summer nights. Worth a stop for one drink even if clubbing is not your scene — the position is unbeatable.
