---
title: "Eating Out"
type: section
---

Hvar eats well across the island. Hvar Town is the polished, expensive option — slick waterfront restaurants and chef-driven menus, with prices to match the yachts in the harbour. For something earthier, the villages inland and around the eastern bays run *konobas* (rustic taverns) where the menu is whatever was caught or grown that morning: octopus *peka* (cooked under an iron lid covered in embers), grilled fish, *gregada* (Dalmatian fish stew), homemade bread, and local wine in carafes.

Look for the Hvar varietals on wine lists — bogdanuša white and plavac mali red, both grown on the steep terraces of the south coast around Sveta Nedjelja.
