---
title: "Getting There"
type: section
---

**By ferry from Split.** The most common arrival. Jadrolinija and Krilo run multiple daily car ferries and catamarans from Split to Hvar Town (about 1 hour fast, 2 hours slow) and to Stari Grad (about 2 hours, car ferry only). Stari Grad is the port for the car ferry — Hvar Town is for foot passengers.

**By ferry from Drvenik.** Short crossing (about 40 minutes) to Sućuraj at the eastern tip of the island. Useful if you are driving down from Dubrovnik.

**By air.** No airport on Hvar itself. Closest airports are Split (then ferry) and Brač (Bol, then water taxi).

**Bus on the island.** Buses run between Hvar Town, Stari Grad (meeting the car ferries), Jelsa, and Sućuraj, but on a thin timetable. Renting a car or scooter is much easier if you plan to explore beyond Hvar Town.
