Grapčeva Cave is one of Hvar's best inland excursions, hidden in the rocky slopes above the island's south coast. Archaeological finds from the cave point to Neolithic settlement, while the chambers themselves hold stalactites, stalagmites, and the cool stillness that feels very far from the harbour cafés.

Access is usually by guided visit from the abandoned shepherds' village of Humac, and that approach is half the pleasure. Wear shoes with grip and bring water; the path is rough enough to make flip-flops a bad idea. On a hot day, the cave is a welcome reminder that Hvar is more than beaches and nightlife.
