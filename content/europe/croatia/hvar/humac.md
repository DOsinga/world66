Humac is an old stone village inland from Jelsa, largely abandoned but carefully preserved enough to feel like an open-air memory of pastoral Hvar. Dry-stone houses, narrow lanes, and rough limestone walls show a very different island from the harbour towns and beach coves.

It is worth the detour if you have a car or are exploring the island beyond Hvar Town. The setting also makes a good base for walks toward Grapceva Cave and the wilder central ridge of the island.
