Vrboska is the smallest and the prettiest of Hvar's coastal villages — a 16th-century fishing settlement built along both sides of a long, narrow inlet, linked by stone bridges. It is sometimes called "little Venice", which oversells it slightly but captures the canalside feel.

The Fortress-Church of St Mary, on a rise above the village, is a fortified Renaissance church built against Ottoman raids — squat, square, with a single defensive tower. The small Fisherman's Museum on the harbour preserves the implements and boats of a working community that mostly ended in the 1970s. Vrboska is about 25 minutes by road from Hvar Town.
