---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

Hvar's season runs from late May to early October. **June and September** are the best months: warm sea, sunny skies, full ferry timetables, and noticeably fewer people than in peak summer.

**July and August** are hot (often 32–35°C in the shade), crowded, and expensive. Hvar Town is at its most intense — also at its most exhausting. If you want quiet, head inland or to Vrboska and Jelsa.

The **Lavender Festival** in late June draws crowds to Velo Grablje for the harvest weekend. The traditional **Procession of the Cross** at Easter, when the cross is carried through six villages overnight, is on the UNESCO list of intangible heritage and worth timing a visit around if you are in Croatia in spring.

Outside the season the island is half-shuttered: most restaurants close, the catamaran services thin out, and a hard *bura* wind can blow for days. Atmospheric, but not the trip people come for.
