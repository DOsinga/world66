Hvar Town, at the western end of the island, is the glamorous face of Hvar — a Venetian harbour town watched over by the [Fortica fortress](/europe/croatia/hvar_town/fortica_fortress) on the hill behind it, with the [Franciscan Monastery](/europe/croatia/hvar_town/franciscan_monastery) at the edge of the harbour and the grand [Pjaca](/europe/croatia/hvar_town/pjaca) connecting the [Arsenal Theatre](/europe/croatia/hvar_town/arsenal_theatre) to the [Cathedral of St Stephen](/europe/croatia/hvar_town/hvar_cathedral).

The restaurant and bar scene here is the liveliest on the Dalmatian islands in summer, drawing a yacht crowd to waterfront tables and pre-club bars that empty out toward the Pakleni Islands after midnight. By day the pace is gentler: climb to the Fortica for the view, wander the Renaissance lanes behind the Pjaca, or take a water taxi out to the islands for a swim.
