---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

Čiovo is a summer island. The season runs from June to September, with July and August the busiest and hottest months. Okrug Gornji in particular fills with visitors during those two months — the beach gets crowded, prices rise, and accommodation books out weeks in advance.

The shoulder months of late May, June, and September are better for most purposes: the water is warm enough for swimming, the beaches are far less crowded, and the restaurants and boat hire are all open. September is especially good — the sea has had all summer to warm up and the summer crowds have gone.

Outside the summer season, Čiovo quiets down considerably. Most beach bars and tourist facilities close by October and don't reopen until May. The island is pleasant to visit in winter if you want peace and low prices, but there is little open and the catamaran from Split runs on a reduced schedule.

Spring is lovely for walking the coastal path — the garrigue is in bloom, the air is cool, and you will have the coves to yourself.
