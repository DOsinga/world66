Komiža is the prettier of Vis's two towns and the older fishing port. Tightly stacked stone houses spill down a slope to a deep harbour, with the Mardešić tower — a sixteenth-century Venetian fort built into the seafront — anchoring the centre. The town has fewer hotels and a more local feel than Vis town, and most evenings revolve around the cafes along the quay and the seafood restaurants behind them.

The Fishermen's Museum, housed in the seafront tower, covers the falkuša tradition and the broader Adriatic sardine fishery in a small but genuinely interesting display. Komiža is also the port for boat trips to the Blue Cave on Biševo, which leave from the quay starting around 8am.
