Tito's Cave is a wide karst hollow on the slope of Mount Hum that served briefly as the headquarters of the Partisan high command after Tito and his staff retreated to the island in June 1944. The cave is open and signposted, with a small information panel; a path branches off the road from Podšpilje toward the summit. There's nothing dramatic to see other than the cave itself and a great view down the valley, but the historical resonance is real.

The walk in takes about twenty minutes from the parking area and is straightforward in dry weather. The associated complex of Partisan tunnels extends further along the ridge but is less accessible; a guided tour from Komiža covers them properly.
