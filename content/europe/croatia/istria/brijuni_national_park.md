An archipelago of 14 islands off the Istrian coast, accessible only by ferry from [Fažana](/europe/croatia/fazana). The main island, Veliki Brijun, was Tito's private retreat for thirty years and is now the centrepiece of a national park. The combination of Roman ruins, dinosaur footprints, semi-tropical gardens, and exotic wildlife from his diplomatic gifts gives the island a slightly surreal quality.

Day trips run from Fažana — boats take you across, a small train circles Veliki Brijun with stops at the safari park, the Roman villa ruins, and the old Tito photo collection. The visit takes a full day and the ferries book up in summer; reserve in advance. The other islands of the archipelago are either uninhabited or closed to visitors.
