A 6th-century Byzantine basilica in the centre of [Poreč](/europe/croatia/porec), one of the great surviving monuments of the early Christian world and a UNESCO site since 1997. The golden mosaics in the apse — Christ, the Virgin, the bishop Euphrasius holding the church — rival anything in Ravenna and have survived almost untouched for fifteen centuries.

The complex includes the basilica itself, a baptistry, a bell tower, and the bishop's palace, which has been turned into a small museum of liturgical art. Visit early or late to avoid the cruise-ship groups. A modest entry fee covers everything; allow about an hour.
