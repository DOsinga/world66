The oak forests of the Mirna valley, around [Motovun](/europe/croatia/motovun) in inland Istria, hold one of the most productive truffle hunting grounds in Europe. The prized find is the white truffle, *Tuber magnatum pico*, in season from September to January; the less rare black truffle is hunted year-round.

Several local outfits run truffle-hunting walks: typically you head into the forest with a hunter and a pair of trained dogs (usually small breeds, not pigs — pigs eat the truffle) and watch them work the ground. The tours generally end with a tasting of truffle products. Bring stout shoes; the forest floor is uneven and the dogs move quickly. The town of Buzet a little to the north calls itself the truffle capital and hosts a truffle festival every September.
