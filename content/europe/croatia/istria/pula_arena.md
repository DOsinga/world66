A 1st-century Roman amphitheatre at the edge of [Pula](/europe/croatia/pula), one of the six largest surviving in the world and one of the best preserved. The arena could seat about 23,000 people for gladiator games and held the standard underground service galleries where the wild animals were kept.

Today the arena hosts summer concerts (everything from opera to Sting and Massive Attack), the Pula Film Festival in late July, and visits during the day. Climb to the upper tier for the views across the city to the harbour. A small underground museum traces the history of the building and the role of the amphitheatre in Roman provincial life.
