---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

June and September are the sweet spots: warm enough to swim comfortably, quiet enough that you can find a table for dinner without a reservation, and the lavender fields on the island's interior plateau are in bloom through June. July and August are packed — the ferries from Split fill up fast, prices rise steeply, and the coves around town fill with day-trippers from Hvar Town. If you must go in peak season, come midweek and stay long enough to see the evenings, when the day boats leave and the village returns to itself.

The Adriatic reaches its warmest around late August and early September, making autumn visits particularly pleasant for swimming. Winter is very quiet; most restaurants and rooms close from November to March.
