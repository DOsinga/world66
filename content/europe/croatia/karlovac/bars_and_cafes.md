---
title: "Bars and Cafes"
type: section
---

The streets around the cathedral square fill with chairs in the evenings, and the ring of the Zvijezda park provides shady cafés all summer long. Karlovac drinks Karlovačko — the local lager has been brewed in the city since 1854 and is still the default order. River-bank bars set up at Foginovo for the summer.
