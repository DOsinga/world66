---
title: "Day Trips"
type: section
---

Karlovac's position between Zagreb and the coast makes it a convenient base for a long list of day trips. The Plitvice Lakes are just over an hour south; Rastoke, the famous water-mill village on the Korana, is even closer. The kayaking river of the Mrežnica reaches its best near Duga Resa, 12 km away.
