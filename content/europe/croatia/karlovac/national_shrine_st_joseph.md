The shrine of St Joseph sits on the same hill as Dubovac Castle, just above the modern city. The current church was consecrated in 1975 and designated Croatia's national shrine of St Joseph two decades later. The interior is plain, modern and uplifting — the work of mid-century Croatian church-builders making something deliberately uncluttered.

The hilltop setting is the real reward: walk between the shrine and the castle and you have the whole bend of the Kupa and the four-river landscape below you.
