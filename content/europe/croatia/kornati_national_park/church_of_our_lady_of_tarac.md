The Church of Our Lady of Tarac is a small, solitary church on Kornat, set in the stark stone landscape that makes Kornati feel so different from Croatia's greener islands. It is not grand, but its isolation gives it real presence.

The church is best understood as part of the old pastoral and fishing life of the archipelago, with an annual pilgrimage linking the islands back to Murter. Most visitors reach it by boat excursion, often combined with nearby Tureta and the sheltered waters of Tarac bay.
