Magazinova škrila is a striking tilted slab of pale Kornati limestone, set above the bare, island-dry landscape of Kornati National Park. The rock looks almost deliberately placed, like a giant sheet propped into the karst, and it gives wide views over the low islands and hard blue water around it.

Kornati is a place of spare details rather than lush scenery, so features like this matter. Visit as part of a boat trip or guided walk, and take the usual island precautions: sun protection, water, and shoes that can handle sharp limestone are more useful here than beach sandals.
