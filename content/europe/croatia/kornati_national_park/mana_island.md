Mana is one of the Kornati islands that makes the archipelago feel almost lunar: pale stone, dry grass, low walls, and a hard blue sea pressing against the cliffs. It is often noticed for the stone film-set ruins left from the 1950s, which look older than they are and add a strange, theatrical note to the landscape.

Most travellers see Mana from a boat trip through the national park rather than as a long landing. That is enough for the main impression: the island’s south-western cliffs and empty slopes capture the austere beauty that makes Kornati different from greener Croatian islands.
