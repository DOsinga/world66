Tureta is a ruined hilltop fortress on Kornat, the largest island in Kornati National Park. Its stone walls are modest, but the position is superb: the climb gives you a high, dry view over the broken limestone islands and the channels that make Kornati look almost lunar from above.

The site is usually visited by boaters or on organised Kornati excursions rather than independently. Bring water, sun protection, and shoes with some grip; the exposed karst terrain is part of the experience, but it is unforgiving in summer heat.
