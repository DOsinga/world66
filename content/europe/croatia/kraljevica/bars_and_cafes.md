---
title: Bars and Cafes
type: section
---

Drinking and coffee culture in Kraljevica is low-key. A few cafes line the harbour promenade, busy with locals doing their morning kava and evening šetnja (promenade walk). There is little in the way of late-night bars; the town quiets down early by Adriatic resort standards. For a livelier evening, Rijeka is 20 minutes north.
