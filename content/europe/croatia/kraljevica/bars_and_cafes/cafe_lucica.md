A small harbour-front cafe on the Kraljevica riva, popular with locals for morning espresso and afternoon drinks. Sits out on the promenade with views across the marina. Cold beer, rakija, and the usual Croatian cafe snacks. The sort of place you can nurse a drink for an hour while watching the boats.
