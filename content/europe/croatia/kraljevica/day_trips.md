Kraljevica's main use is as a base for the surrounding Kvarner. Rijeka is a 25-minute drive northwest along the coast; Opatija and the strung-out Riviera villages beyond it are a little further. The island of Krk is just south across the bridge, with the airport, the towns of Krk and Baška, and the steep east coast all within an hour.
