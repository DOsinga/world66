---
title: Eating Out
type: section
---

Kraljevica has a small but reliable selection of restaurants, mostly concentrated along the harbour and the waterfront promenade. Seafood dominates — grilled fish, mussels from the bay, and brodet (fish stew) are the local staples. Prices are lower than in nearby Opatija or Rijeka. Most places are informal and family-run, and many close out of season or keep reduced hours in spring and autumn.
