Dva Brata ("Two Brothers") is a straightforward waterfront restaurant in the old town, a short walk from the Zrinski Castle. The menu covers grilled and fried fish, octopus salad, and pasta with seafood. A good choice for a no-fuss lunch after visiting the castles.
