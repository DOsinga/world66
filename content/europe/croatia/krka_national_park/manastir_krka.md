Manastir Krka is a Serbian Orthodox monastery set above the Krka River in the park's less crowded northern reaches. Its white church, monastic buildings, and river setting make it a different kind of stop from the famous waterfall boardwalks.

The monastery is best visited as part of a wider drive through the upper park, especially if you are continuing toward Burnum or the Roski slap area. Dress and behave respectfully, since this is an active religious site as well as a historic landmark.
