Roški slap is the quieter northern counterpoint to Skradinski buk, set where the Krka River spreads into a long sequence of low tufa cascades before dropping through the main waterfall. The park describes the falls as a 650-metre-long barrier, with the delicate first cascades nicknamed the Necklaces, and the setting feels more open and riverine than the dense boardwalk circuit farther downstream.

It is a good choice when Krka's headline sights are crowded or when you want to see more than one face of the national park. Trails, viewpoints, old mill buildings, and seasonal boat connections make it a natural stop on a fuller day through the upper Krka area, especially if you are also visiting Oziđana pećina, Visovac, or the inland archaeological sites.
