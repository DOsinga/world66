---
title: Things to Do
type: section
---

Most visitors head straight for [Skradinski buk](/europe/croatia/krka_national_park/skradinski_buk), the park's showcase cascade and boardwalk circuit, then take the boat out to the monastery island of [Visovac](/europe/croatia/krka_national_park/visovac). With more time, the quieter northern half of the park rewards a longer visit: [Roški slap](/europe/croatia/krka_national_park/roski_slap)'s tufa cascades, the [Manastir Krka](/europe/croatia/krka_national_park/manastir_krka) monastery, the [Oziđana pećina](/europe/croatia/krka_national_park/oziana_pecina) archaeological cave, and the Roman ruins at [Burnum](/europe/croatia/krka_national_park/burnum_archaeological_site) can all be combined into a single day for travelers with a car.
