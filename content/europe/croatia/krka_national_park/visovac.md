Visovac is one of Krka National Park's most memorable sights: a small green island set in the still water of Visovac Lake, with a Franciscan monastery and church at its centre. The setting is almost theatrical, a quiet oval of trees and stone surrounded by the wider karst landscape of the Krka River.

Boat trips to the island add a different rhythm to the park, especially if you have already seen Skradinski buk. The visit is short, but the combination of water, monastery, and long religious history gives Visovac a depth that makes it more than a scenic photo stop.
