Burnum adds Roman military history to a visit otherwise dominated by Krka's waterfalls and river scenery. The site preserves remains associated with a legionary camp, including arches and an amphitheatre, set in the dry karst landscape above the Krka canyon.

It is not as instantly scenic as Skradinski Buk or Visovac, but it gives the park a deeper historical layer. Travellers with a car can combine Burnum with the northern sections of Krka for a more varied day that mixes archaeology, viewpoints, and river landscapes.
