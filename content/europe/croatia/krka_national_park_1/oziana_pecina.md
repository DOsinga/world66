Oziđana pećina is a small but rewarding archaeological cave in Krka National Park, set above the river gorge near the Roški slap area. Finds from the cave connect the landscape to prehistoric settlement, adding a human layer to a park better known for waterfalls, lakes, and travertine.

The cave is best visited as part of a longer walk rather than as a standalone target. Its appeal is in the combination of archaeology, cliffside setting, and views over the Krka landscape, so wear sensible shoes and check which trails are open before setting out.
