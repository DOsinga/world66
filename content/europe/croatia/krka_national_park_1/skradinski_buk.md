Skradinski buk is the classic Krka National Park sight: a broad sequence of waterfalls, pools, reeds, and boardwalks where the river drops through travertine barriers. It is popular for good reason, and it is the place most visitors picture when they think of Krka.

Come early or late if you can, because tour groups can make the boardwalks feel tight. Even with crowds, the mix of water, greenery, old mill buildings, and constantly shifting cascades is worth the effort.
