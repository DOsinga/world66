Osejava Park covers the wooded headland south of Makarska harbour, where paths run through Aleppo pine above cliffs and small rocky coves. It is one of the easiest ways to step out of the resort strip and see the town from a more natural angle.

Come for a morning walk, a swim from the rocks, or sunset views back toward Biokovo and the harbour. The paths are simple but can be uneven, so it is more rewarding in decent shoes than in beach sandals.
