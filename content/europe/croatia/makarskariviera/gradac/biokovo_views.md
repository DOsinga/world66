The Biokovo mountain range rises almost directly behind Gradac, its grey limestone ridges climbing from the coast to over 1700 metres in a series of steep terraces. From the beach, the contrast between the flat blue sea and the craggy mountain wall is one of the most dramatic landscapes on the Dalmatian coast.

The [Biokovo Nature Park](/europe/croatia) is accessible by car from the mountain road above Makarska, about 20 kilometres north of Gradac. Day hikers can reach the summit plateau where the views extend to Italy on a clear day. The park charges an entry fee. For those without transport, the view from the beach itself — particularly in the late afternoon when the rock face catches the western light — requires no effort at all.
