Odysseus Cave is a sea cave on the rocky southern coast of Mljet. A narrow tunnel links the cave to the open water, making it a memorable swim in calm conditions, and the high opening above the cave is used by experienced visitors for rappelling. Around midday, sunlight can turn the water inside a bright blue.
