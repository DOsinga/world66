Motovun is a small hilltop town in central [Istria](/europe/croatia/istria), perched above the Mirna River valley with sweeping views over truffle-rich forests. The old walled town is beautifully preserved — climb through the outer gate and up the stone streets to find a tight cluster of Romanesque and Gothic buildings around a main square with a campanile.

The town is best known for the [Motovun Film Festival](/europe/croatia/motovun/motovun_film_festival), held every summer, which takes over the hilltop with open-air screenings. Outside festival season it is quieter but no less charming — a good base for exploring Istria's interior, sampling local truffles in the [forests of the Mirna valley](/europe/croatia/motovun/truffle_hunting), and driving the hill towns circuit that includes Groznjan and Oprtalj. The [medieval walls and ramparts](/europe/croatia/motovun/town_walls) can be walked in an hour and give views over the whole valley.
