Motovun's pleasures are unhurried: walking the medieval walls, eating truffles, watching the Mirna valley from the ramparts, and exploring the Istrian countryside by car. The Motovun Film Festival in late July briefly transforms the hilltop into a buzzing open-air cinema. The rest of the year it rewards those who simply wander.
