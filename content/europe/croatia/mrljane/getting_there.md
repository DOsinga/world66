---
title: "Getting There"
type: section
---

The most direct route is the car ferry from **Biograd na Moru** on the mainland to **Tkon** at the southern end of Pašman island — a crossing of about twenty minutes, with departures roughly every hour in summer. From Tkon it is a short drive north (around 15 km) along the island's spine to Mrljane.

The island is also connected by road bridge to **Ugljan** at the northern end, so you can come via the Zadar–Preko ferry and drive south. Without a car, infrequent local buses run from Tkon. Most visitors arrive on yachts, mooring in the channel or in one of the small marinas at Sutomišćica and Biograd.
