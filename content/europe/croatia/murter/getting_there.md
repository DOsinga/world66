---
title: Getting There
type: section
---

Murter is connected to the mainland by a small bascule bridge at Tisno, which is the only way on or off the island by road. The bridge lifts daily at fixed times in summer (typically 09:00 and 17:00) to let larger boats through; if you arrive at one of those moments, settle in for a 30-minute wait.

The nearest airports are **Split** (90 km south) and **Zadar** (50 km north), both with budget connections from western Europe. From either, you reach Murter by car or by a combination of bus and local taxi. The closest mainland bus station is at [Šibenik](/europe/croatia/sibenik), about 30 km away, with frequent services to Tisno in season.

Sailors can berth at the ACI marinas at Jezera and at Hramina marina in Murter town, both well-equipped and busy in July and August.
