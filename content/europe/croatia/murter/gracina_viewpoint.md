A short drive east of Tisno on the mainland side, the Gračina viewpoint sits on a low ridge with a wide view back across the Murter channel to the island and out to the Kornati beyond. It is reached by a small marked road from the coast highway and takes only a few minutes from the parking area to the top.

Late afternoon is the right time — the Kornati islands turn deep orange against the water and the village of Tisno lights up as the sun drops.
