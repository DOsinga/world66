A small early-Christian site on the mainland coast east of Tisno, with the ruins of a 6th-century three-aisled basilica and an attached baptistery. The site was excavated in the 1970s; what remains is the wall outline at knee height and a stone tank for the baptismal font.

There is no entry fee and rarely any other visitors. The pine-shaded path that runs along the coast from Pirovac passes the ruins, which makes it a pleasant detour on an evening walk.
