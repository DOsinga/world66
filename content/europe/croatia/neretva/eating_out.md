---
title: "Eating Out"
type: section
---

The Neretva valley has a distinctive local cuisine, quite different from the seafood you get further up the Dalmatian coast. The classic dish is frog and eel paprikash — a heavy stew of river eel and skinned frog legs in onion-and-paprika sauce, traditionally served with polenta. Smoked eel appears on most menus too. The land between the channels grows mandarin oranges in enormous quantities and watermelon in summer; both end up in market stalls along the main road.

The best restaurants are in the small delta villages rather than in Metković itself. Try the konobas around Vid and Opuzen for genuine local cooking, often served at long communal tables in farmhouse settings. The river wine of choice is Plavac Mali, a heavy red from the slopes above the delta — vineyards on the [Pelješac](/europe/croatia/peljesac) peninsula immediately south make some of the best.
