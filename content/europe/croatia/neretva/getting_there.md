---
title: "Getting There"
type: section
---

The Neretva delta is on the main coastal road and rail route between Split and Dubrovnik, and a logical stop on any drive between the two. Metković has a train station with regular services from Ploče and Sarajevo, and the bus station is the main hub for local services around the delta and across the border into Bosnia and Herzegovina.

The nearest airports are at Dubrovnik (about 100 km south) and Split (around 150 km north). Both have car-hire desks, and a hired car is by far the easiest way to explore the delta, the villages, and Počitelj across the Bosnian border. Note that the journey between Dubrovnik and the rest of Croatia historically crossed a short stretch of Bosnia (the Neum corridor), but since 2022 the Pelješac Bridge has removed the need to cross the border for that leg.
