The Neretva Marathon is a race for lađa — narrow wooden boats with shallow draft, originally used for fishing and moving produce through the delta channels. Each lađa carries up to 18 rowers and a helmsman. The course runs 22 kilometres along the river from Metković to Ploče, threading the orchards and reed beds of the lower valley.

The event is held on a Saturday in mid-August and is genuinely festive, with crowds along the banks, music, food stalls, and large quantities of local Plavac Mali wine. The boats themselves are works of regional craftsmanship: hand-built from oak, pine, and chestnut, and decorated for the race in the colours of the village they represent. If you are in southern Dalmatia in August, it is worth detouring for.
