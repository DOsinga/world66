Počitelj is the most photogenic settlement on the lower Neretva — an entire Ottoman village climbing a steep slope above the river, with whitewashed houses, a mosque with a slender minaret, a clock tower, and the round Gavrankapetan tower at the summit. The village sits on the Bosnian side of the border, about 25 km up the river from Metković, and is included on most cultural day trips from the delta.

The climb up to the tower is steep but short, and the view back across the Neretva gorge is the reward. The mosque, the medersa (Islamic school), and several merchant houses are open as small museums. The village was depopulated and damaged in the war of the 1990s but has been substantially restored. Combine the visit with a stop at the famous Kravice waterfalls, 30 km north — both make a comfortable day from the delta.
