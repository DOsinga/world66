---
title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

The Neretva delta combines wetland landscape with a deep historical layer. Most visitors spend their time on the water — in a flat-bottomed boat through the channels and orchards, or paddling a kayak through the reeds — and balance it with a half-day exploring the Roman ruins of Narona or the medieval fortress towers above the river. The town of Metković is the main base; the smaller villages around the delta are quieter and more characterful.
