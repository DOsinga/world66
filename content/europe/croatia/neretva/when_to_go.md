---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

Late spring and early autumn are the best time for the Neretva. From late April through June the wetlands are full of nesting birds and the orchards are in flower; September and October bring the mandarin harvest, when the markets fill with citrus and the delta light is at its softest. Temperatures in those months sit between 18 and 25°C and the mosquitoes are bearable.

July and August are hot — frequently above 30°C — and the delta is at its most insect-heavy. Bring repellent. The mid-August lađa marathon is the year's main event and brings a noticeable crowd, but the rest of the summer is quieter than the coast just north and south. Winter is cold, grey, and quiet, but ideal for birdwatching: tens of thousands of ducks, geese, and waders winter in the protected wetlands of the lower delta.
