A small pebble cove a short walk north of Novigrad's old town, in the direction of Mareda. The beach is more attractive than the concrete platforms in the town centre — fine smooth pebbles, a few patches of grass under the pines, and water that is properly clear.

It is a local-favourite beach rather than a tourist attraction, so it stays relatively quiet even in mid-July. A small beach bar opens in summer. For something larger and more developed, the [Mareda](/europe/croatia/novigrad/mareda) campsite beach is two kilometres further on; for swimming right in town, head for the rocks below the walls.
