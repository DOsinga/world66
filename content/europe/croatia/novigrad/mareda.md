A long stretch of pebble and concrete beach about 2 km north of Novigrad, behind one of the largest camping resorts in the area. The beach itself is open to everyone, and once you walk away from the cluster of pizzerias and beach bars near the campsite entrance it gets much quieter — particularly the section further north towards Dajla.

The setting is open and breezy, with views back to Novigrad. The sea is shallow at the entry — good for children — and there are kayaks and stand-up paddleboards for rent in summer. The walking and cycling path that runs along the coast from Novigrad makes for a pleasant 40-minute approach by foot.
