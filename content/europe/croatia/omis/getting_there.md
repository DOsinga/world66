---
title: "Getting to Omis"
type: section
---

Omis is about 25 kilometres south of Split, reachable by bus or car in roughly 30 to 40 minutes. Buses run regularly between Split and Omis throughout the day; the journey takes around 35 minutes and the service is frequent enough in summer that no booking is needed. The bus stops in the town centre.

By car from Split, the main route follows the coastal road south through Podstrana and Dugi Rat. This road has heavy summer traffic and the last stretch into Omis, where the road squeezes between cliff and coast, can be slow. There is parking near the beach and along the river bank. The town itself is small enough to walk once you have arrived.

Omis has no airport; Split Airport is the nearest, about 35 kilometres away. Taxis from the airport to Omis are straightforward and cost roughly the same as taxis to Split city centre.
