Mirabella Fortress sits on a narrow spike of rock above the old town of Omis, reached by a steep staircase cut into the cliff. The climb takes about 15 minutes from the town centre and is worth it for the view: the Cetina river curving around the base of the cliffs, the Adriatic stretching out to sea, and the canyon opening behind the town. The fortress is small — the walls enclose only a modest tower — but the position is extraordinary.

It was built in the medieval period as part of the Omiš pirate defences and is the most visible landmark of the town from the beach below. There is no entrance fee and the approach path is signposted from the old town. The fortress is best visited in the morning or evening when the light is on the walls.
