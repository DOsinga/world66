Starigrad is the larger of the two fortresses above Omis, sitting on a wide rocky plateau above the canyon. The approach is longer than the climb to Mirabella — allow 30 to 40 minutes from town — and follows a path through the rocky hillside above the canyon. The walls are better preserved than Mirabella and the enclosed area is large enough to explore properly.

The views from Starigrad look down into the canyon at the point where the Cetina river bends, and out over the coast in both directions. On a clear day you can see the islands offshore. The fortress dates to the medieval period and was probably the administrative centre of the Kačić lords who made Omis a pirate state. It is less visited than Mirabella and has a wilder, more abandoned feel.
