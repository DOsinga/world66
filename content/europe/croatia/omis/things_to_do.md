---
title: "Things to Do in Omis"
type: section
---

Omis divides neatly into the old town squeezed between the Cetina river and the cliff, the beaches stretching along the coast, and the canyon inland. The old town is small enough to walk in twenty minutes, with the two ruined fortresses above — Starigrad on the plateau and Mirabella perched on a spike of rock — providing the focal points.

The real draw is the Cetina gorge, which begins immediately behind the town. This is Croatia's best destination for rafting and canyoning, with companies offering half-day and full-day trips through the canyon from spring through early autumn. Zip-lines span the gorge from the cliff edges. Rock climbers find routes on the limestone walls above. In August, the Festival of Dalmatian Klapa fills the streets with traditional a cappella singing.
