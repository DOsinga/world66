Leut is a bar in Volosko that draws a young local crowd alongside the tourists who come for the harbour restaurants. It is small, informal, and stays open late — a different atmosphere from the grand hotel terraces and casino bars of central Opatija. The terrace has harbour views and the selection of Croatian craft beers and wines is well curated. A good option for a drink before or after dinner at one of the Volosko seafood restaurants.
