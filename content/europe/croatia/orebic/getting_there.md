---
title: "Getting There"
type: section
---

Orebić is at the tip of the Pelješac peninsula. By car from Dubrovnik, take the coastal road north then cut across to Ston and along the peninsula — around 2 hours. From Split, the drive is around 3 hours via the Makarska Riviera.

Jadrolinija passenger ferries cross to [Korčula](/europe/croatia/korcula) Town from the quay in the centre of Orebić; the crossing takes 15 minutes. A car-ferry (Dominče to Orebić) also connects the two sides, though it docks slightly east of Korčula Town. In summer there are several crossings per hour. Buses from Dubrovnik and Split serve Orebić in summer via the rest of the peninsula.
