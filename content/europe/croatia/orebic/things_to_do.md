---
title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Orebić is a small town, but it makes good use of its setting. The [Maritime Museum](/europe/croatia/peljesac/orebic/maritime_museum) tells the story of the town's seafaring heyday with unusual depth. The [Franciscan Monastery of Our Lady of the Angels](/europe/croatia/peljesac/orebic/franciscan_monastery) sits high on the hill above the town and rewards the climb with views across to [Korčula](/europe/croatia/korcula). The [beaches](/europe/croatia/peljesac/orebic/beaches) stretching either side of the centre are long, pebbly, and backed by fragrant pine trees. Most people also take the [ferry to Korčula Town](/europe/croatia/korcula), 15 minutes away — one of the best small medieval cities on the Adriatic.
