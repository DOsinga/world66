---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

Osijek has a continental climate with cold winters and hot summers. The best time to visit is spring (April–June) or autumn (September–October): warm days, manageable humidity, and the surrounding countryside at its greenest or most golden. Summer (July–August) is hot and humid — temperatures regularly exceed 35°C, the Drava beach is packed, and Kopački Rit is at its most biodiverse with breeding birds. Winter is cold, foggy, and quiet.

The Osijek Summer Festival (July–August) brings outdoor concerts and theatrical performances to the Tvrđa squares. The city market is liveliest on weekend mornings year-round.
