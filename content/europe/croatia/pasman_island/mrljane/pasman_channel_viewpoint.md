A short walk up the hill behind the village leads to an unmarked but unmistakable lookout: the highest point on this stretch of the island, with a panorama across the Pašman Channel to the mainland coast and the wall of the **Velebit mountains** beyond. On clear days the snow on the peaks is visible well into spring.

The path is informal — follow the small road inland from the harbour, then a footpath up through the olive groves. Twenty minutes brings you to the top. Sunset is the obvious time to come; the channel turns gold and the lights of Biograd na Moru on the mainland begin to pick out across the water.
