Drače's beach is the village's main attraction — a quiet stretch of pebble and shingle in a sheltered bay, with water that grades from pale turquoise in the shallows to deep blue further out. There are no facilities to speak of: no sunbed hire, no bar, no crowds. You bring your own shade, your own food, your own company.

The bay is sheltered from the open Adriatic by the curve of the peninsula, which keeps the water calm most of the year. Underwater visibility can exceed 20 metres on a calm day. There are sea urchins on the rocks — wear water shoes. Swimming is best in July and August when the water temperature reaches 25–27°C, but the bay is swimmable from June through October. A few small boats are moored in the harbour at the northern end; the southern end of the beach is quieter and wilder.
