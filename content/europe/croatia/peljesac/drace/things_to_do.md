title: Things to Do
type: section
tags: [things_to_do]
---

Drače is a place for doing very little on purpose. The beach, the sea, the evening konoba, the wine. There are no museums, no monuments, no queues.

The surrounding Pelješac peninsula offers more structured excursions: the Dingač vineyards a few kilometres west, the medieval walls and oyster beds of Ston to the south, and the ferry from Prapratno to Korčula for a day island-hopping. But the point of Drače is that you don't have to do any of it.
