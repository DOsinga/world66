---
title: "Beaches"
type: section
---

Orebić has several good beaches stretching both east and west of the town centre. Most are pebbly rather than sandy, shaded by mature pines, and face south across the channel toward Korčula. The water is clean and the channel offers some protection from swell, making them generally calm and suitable for children.

The best-known is Trstenica, about a kilometre east of the centre — a long pebble beach with beach bars and sun lounger rental in summer. Punta, west of the ferry terminal, is narrower but often quieter. For more seclusion, walk or drive west along the peninsula to smaller coves between the pines. The naturist beach at Divna, further west, is quieter and more remote.
