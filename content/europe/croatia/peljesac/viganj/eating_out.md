---
title: Eating Out
type: section
---

Viganj is small enough that "eating out" means choosing between a handful of family-run konobas strung along the coast road and the seafront. Grilled fish and meat, the local Plavac Mali red wine, simple pasta — straightforward and good, with the focus on the view over the channel rather than ambitious cooking. Reservations are barely a concept; most places start serving from about 6pm and run late.
