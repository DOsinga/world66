Antika is the windsurfing-crowd favourite on the Viganj seafront — a stone terrace, a short menu of grilled fish, octopus salad, and Dalmatian-style peka lamb (the latter to be ordered several hours in advance). The wine list runs Plavac Mali from the Pelješac vineyards just inland.

It is unfussy and welcoming after a long afternoon in the wind, and the prices are reasonable by Croatian coast standards. Closed November through March.
