---
title: "Getting There"
type: section
---

Plitvice Lakes National Park sits on the D1 highway between Zagreb and Split, which makes it a natural stop on the classic Croatia road trip.

**From Zagreb**: about 130 kilometres (roughly 2 hours) south via the D1 highway. The drive passes through rolling hills and beech forest. Several bus companies operate daily services from Zagreb bus station directly to the park gates; journey time is about 2.5 hours and buses run year-round.

**From Split**: about 240 kilometres (2.5-3 hours) north via the D1. Again, regular buses serve this route and stop at the park.

**From Zadar**: about 140 kilometres (1.5-2 hours) northeast — a good option if combining the park with the Dalmatian coast.

There is no train station near the park. Drivers should note that parking at Entrance 1 fills up quickly in peak season — Entrance 2 has more parking and connects to the Lower Lakes via a panoramic train. A shuttle bus runs between entrances 1 and 2 and is included in the admission price.
