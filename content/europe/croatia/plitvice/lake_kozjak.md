Lake Kozjak is the largest lake in the park, stretching roughly 2.4 kilometres in length and separating the Upper and Lower Lakes sections. It is crossed by a flat-bottomed electric ferry that runs throughout the day — the boat journey takes about 15 minutes and is one of the best ways to appreciate the scale of the park. The lake's banks are forested with beech and maple, and the water is a deep greenish-blue.

The boat runs from a small dock at the northern end of the Lower Lakes section to another dock at the southern end of the Upper Lakes section, with a connecting panoramic train at each end of the park. The ferry is included in the park admission. Paddling on the lake is not permitted, but the boat journey gives excellent views of the surrounding hills and allows you to spot herons and cormorants along the forested shoreline.
