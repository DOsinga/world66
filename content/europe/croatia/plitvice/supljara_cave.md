Supljara Cave is a small cave passage in the Lower Lakes area of Plitvice Lakes National Park, between Gavanovac and Kaluderovac lakes. Stone steps pass through the rock, linking the lakeside boardwalks with higher canyon viewpoints.

It is not a long cave visit, but it adds variety to the standard Lower Lakes walk and gives a different angle on the canyon.
