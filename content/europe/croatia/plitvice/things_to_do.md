---
title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Everything at Plitvice revolves around the lakes themselves. The park has four marked hiking routes (A through F, with the full park taking a full day) that combine boardwalks, forest paths, and boat crossings. The two main zones — Upper Lakes and Lower Lakes — have distinct characters.

Start with the Lower Lakes if you only have a few hours. The Veliki Slap waterfall and the tight canyon section around Lake Novakovića Brod are the park's most dramatic scenery. If you have a full day, continue to the Upper Lakes for their wider, quieter beauty and excellent wildlife spotting — roe deer, otters, and occasionally lynx.
