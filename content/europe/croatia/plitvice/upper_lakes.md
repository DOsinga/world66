The Upper Lakes form a different kind of landscape from the more famous Lower Lakes. Where the lower section is tight and dramatic, the upper section is broader, more forested, and quieter. Twelve lakes extend northward in a chain, flanked by dense beech and fir forest. The lakes here tend to be shallower and more varied in colour — some almost opaque with mineral deposits, others clear enough to see the travertine formations on the bottom.

The Upper Lakes are best accessed via the electric boat from the southern end of Lake Kozjak. Route C and Route H offer excellent coverage of the upper section. Wildlife is more likely here than in the busy Lower Lakes — roe deer are commonly seen in the early morning, and the forest around Ciginovac lake is good for woodland birds. The Prošćansko Lake at the northern end of the upper section is the highest in the park and the source of the entire cascade system.
