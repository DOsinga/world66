---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

Plitvice is spectacular in all four seasons, though the experience varies dramatically by time of year.

**Spring (April–May)** is arguably the best time — the waterfalls are at their most powerful from snowmelt, wildflowers line the trails, and the crowds haven't yet arrived. The air is clear and cool, perfect for walking.

**Summer (June–August)** brings the highest visitor numbers and the warmest water. The park caps daily admissions but the main boardwalk areas can still feel very busy. July and August are peak season — expect queues for the boat, crowded viewpoints, and significantly higher prices for local accommodation. If you must visit in summer, arrive at the gates before 8am.

**Autumn (September–October)** rivals spring for beauty. The beech forest turns golden and amber, the light is warm and golden in the afternoons, and visitor numbers drop sharply after mid-September. This is the best season for photography.

**Winter (November–March)** is transformative if you're lucky with weather. The frozen waterfalls and snow-dusted boardwalks over the still lakes are hauntingly beautiful. Opening hours are reduced and some boat routes don't run, but the park never fully closes and admission prices are lower.
