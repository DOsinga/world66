Podgora is a small Dalmatian coastal town tucked between [Split](/europe/croatia/split) and [Dubrovnik](/europe/croatia/dubrovnik) at the foot of the Biokovo mountain range. Its [pebble beach](/europe/croatia/podgora/podgora_beach) slopes into exceptionally clear water, and the waterfront is marked by the [Seagull's Wings monument](/europe/croatia/podgora/seagulls_wings_monument) — a striking concrete sculpture that has become the town's symbol.

The town makes a good base for exploring the Makarska Riviera. Podgora itself is quiet and unhurried, with a handful of hotels that somehow manage four times as many beds as the town has permanent residents. The [Biokovo Nature Park](/europe/croatia/podgora/biokovo_nature_park) rises directly behind the coast, offering hiking trails with dramatic views over the Adriatic and the islands. Above the modern seafront the abandoned stone [old village](/europe/croatia/podgora/old_podgora_village) is a half-hour walk into the past, and three kilometres up the shore is the long pebble beach at [Tucepi](/europe/croatia/podgora/tucepi).
