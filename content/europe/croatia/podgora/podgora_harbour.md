The small harbour at the southern end of the seafront is where, in September 1942, the Yugoslav Partisan Navy was officially founded — the event commemorated by the Seagull's Wings monument a few hundred metres along. Today it is a working harbour for small fishing boats and the occasional excursion vessel running day trips out to Brac and Hvar.

A handful of seafood restaurants line the harbour wall, with tables right on the water and grilled fish brought in by the boats moored a few metres away. Booking ahead in July and August is wise; the quayside fills up fast as the sun goes down. The ticket kiosk at the harbour sells trips to the offshore islands and panoramic cruises along the riviera; most leave around 9am and return at sunset.
