Podstrana is a small community on the Dalmatian coast just south of [Split](/europe/croatia/split), stretching for about 6 kilometres between the Zrnovnica stream and the Mutogras hill. The seafront is lined with accessible shingle and pebble beaches backed by Mediterranean pine woods and tamarisk trees, making it a popular escape from the city — you can be on the beach within 20 minutes of Split's old town.

The settlement is predominantly residential and tourist-oriented rather than a traditional Dalmatian village, but the quality of the coast and the proximity to Split make it a reasonable base for those who want beaches close at hand while still having easy access to the historic centre. The early Christian [Church of St. Thecla](/europe/croatia/podstrana/sveta_tekla_church), one of the oldest in Dalmatia, is the one genuine historical sight in the village.
