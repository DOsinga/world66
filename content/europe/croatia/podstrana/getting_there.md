---
title: "Getting There"
type: section
---

Podstrana is 10 km south of Split along the coastal road (D8). Local buses run frequently from Split's main bus station and the town centre, with stops along the coastal road through Podstrana. The journey takes 20-30 minutes depending on traffic. By taxi or rideshare from central Split, the journey is about 15 minutes.

The split cycle path connects Split's promenade to Podstrana and continues south — a pleasant 10-km ride along the coast on a dedicated path.
