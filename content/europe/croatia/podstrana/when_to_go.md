---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

The Dalmatian coast season runs June through September, with July and August the hottest and most crowded months. Podstrana's beaches are at their busiest then; rooms are most expensive and advance booking is essential. May, June, and September offer the best balance: warm enough to swim comfortably, quieter beaches, and lower prices.

The proximity to [Split](/europe/croatia/split) means that Podstrana is viable as a base in shoulder season — April and October are warm enough for walking and sightseeing, though swimming is chilly outside the main summer months.
