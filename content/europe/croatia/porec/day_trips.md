Porec makes a comfortable base for exploring northern Istria. Bring or rent a car for the inland hill towns; the coast can be done by bus or boat.

The medieval port of [Rovinj](/europe/croatia/rovinj), 40 minutes south, is the obvious next stop — many travellers prefer its tangled old town to Porec's. [Pula](/europe/croatia/pula) and its enormous Roman amphitheatre is an hour away. Inland, the truffle and wine country around [Motovun](/europe/croatia/motovun) — a hilltop village reached by a long stretch of switchbacks — is the heart of the Istrian interior. The cross-border trip into Slovenia for the Skocjan or Postojna caves is also possible as a long day out.
