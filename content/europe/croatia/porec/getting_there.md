---
title: "Getting There"
type: section
---

Porec sits in northwest Istria, well connected by road and ferry but with no rail link.

**By car**: From Trieste, Slovenia or Zagreb, the Istria Y motorway (A8/A9) brings you to within 5 km of the centre.

**By bus**: Direct coaches run from Trieste, Pula, Rijeka, Zagreb and Ljubljana. The bus station is a short walk from the old town.

**By ferry**: In summer, daily fast-ferry services connect Porec with Venice and Trieste — a scenic three-hour ride across the upper Adriatic, often the most enjoyable way to arrive.

**By air**: The nearest airports are Pula (60 km), Trieste (90 km), and Venice Marco Polo (200 km, via ferry). Pula has the most direct flights to Croatia, while Venice has the widest international connections.
