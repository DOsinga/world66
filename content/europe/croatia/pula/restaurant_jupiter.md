Jupiter is a reliable choice in the old town, a few minutes from the Forum. The menu covers Istrian classics — pasta with truffles, grilled fish, peka (meat or octopus slow-cooked under a bell-shaped lid), and the local sheep's cheese from Pag island. The setting is an old stone building with outdoor tables in summer. Prices are moderate by Croatian standards but fair for the quality. Book ahead in summer if you want a table in the evening.
