---
title: "Getting There"
type: section
---

Rab is reached by ferry. There is no bridge and no airport.

The main connection is from **Jablanac** on the mainland to **Mišnjak** on the southern tip of the island — a crossing of about fifteen minutes. Jablanac is on the coastal highway (the Magistrala, D8) between Senj and Starigrad. Jadrolinija runs this route frequently throughout the day in summer; the ferry takes cars, motorbikes, and foot passengers.

A second route connects **Valbiska** on the island of Krk to **Lopar** at the northern end of Rab. This crossing takes around 45 minutes and is useful if you are island-hopping from Krk. Rapska Plovidba operates this line.

From Split or Rijeka there are seasonal catamaran services to Rab town itself — fast (around two hours from Rijeka) and foot-passengers only.

If you are travelling without a car, the easiest approach is the Jablanac–Mišnjak ferry followed by the island bus service to Rab town. In summer, buses meet the ferries.
