The Rovinj Heritage Museum (Zavičajni muzej) is housed in a Baroque palace on the main town square. Its permanent collection covers the archaeology of the wider Rovinj area — finds from Bronze Age sites including Monkodonja — alongside ethnographic material on traditional fishing, boat-building, and local crafts.

The art collection is the museum's strongest suit and worth the admission alone. It includes work by local painters from the 17th through 20th centuries, with a particular emphasis on the Venetian-influenced Baroque period and the early Modernist artists who formed Rovinj's art colony in the late 19th century. The museum also has a section on the batana, the traditional flat-bottomed fishing boat that is now a symbol of the town's maritime heritage.
