title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Rovinj's main pleasure is wandering — up the steep lanes of the old town, along the harbour, or through the Golden Cape forest park south of the centre. The baroque church of St. Euphemia and the galleries of Grisia Street are the cultural anchors. Day trips to the Lim Fjord and the Roman ruins at Pula add variety.
