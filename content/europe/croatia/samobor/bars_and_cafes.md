---
title: "Bars and Cafés"
type: section
---

Samobor has a pleasant café culture centred on the main square. The town is at its liveliest on Sunday mornings when the outdoor terraces fill with day-trippers and locals doing their weekly promenade. Evenings are quiet — this is not a nightlife destination — but a drink on the square after the day-trippers have gone has its own appeal.
