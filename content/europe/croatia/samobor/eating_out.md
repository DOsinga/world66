---
title: "Eating Out"
type: section
---

Samobor is a foodie day-trip destination for Zagrebčani. The town's most famous export is kremšnita — a thick custard cream slice served at multiple cake shops on and around the main square — but there is more to eat than cake. Traditional Croatian restaurants (konobe) around town serve roast lamb, grilled sausages, and hearty regional stews, and Sunday lunch here is a well-established Zagreb-family ritual.
