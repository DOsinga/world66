---
title: Day Trips
type: section
---

Trogir's position between Split and Šibenik, and its proximity to Split Airport, makes it a natural base for exploring the central Dalmatian coast. Split is 27 km east and easily reached by bus or car. Šibenik, with its UNESCO-listed Cathedral of St James and the nearby Krka National Park, is about 50 km to the north. The Krka waterfalls — an extensive series of travertine cascades and pools — are one of the most popular day trips in Dalmatia and can be reached in under an hour.

The islands of Brač and Hvar are accessible by ferry from Split, making them feasible as long day trips for those based in Trogir. The Bol beach on Brač (Zlatni Rat, one of the most photographed beaches in Croatia) is about two hours from Trogir by bus and ferry combined.
