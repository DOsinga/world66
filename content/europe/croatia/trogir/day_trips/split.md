Split, 27 km east of Trogir, is the largest city on the Dalmatian coast and one of the great historical cities of the Adriatic. The old town is built within the walls of Diocletian's Palace, a 4th-century Roman imperial residence that was later colonised by the local population: medieval streets run through what were once corridors and courtyards, and the mausoleum of Diocletian became the Cathedral of Saint Domnius, one of the oldest cathedrals in the world still in regular use.

Bus 37 from the stop near Trogir's bridge reaches Split in about 30 minutes and runs frequently throughout the day. The ferry terminal in Split is the departure point for services to Brač, Hvar, Vis, and other central Dalmatian islands.
