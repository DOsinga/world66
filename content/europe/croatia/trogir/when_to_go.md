---
title: When to Go
type: section
---

May and June are the best months: warm enough for swimming, the old town is not yet overcrowded, and the restaurants are not running tourist-season menus. July and August are peak season — the town is busy, prices rise sharply, and accommodation needs to be booked months in advance. September is another good option, with warm sea temperatures and crowds thinning out after mid-month.

Trogir is well worth visiting in winter if you are already in Split for another reason. The old town is largely empty of tourists, the cathedral and Kamerlengo Castle are open with minimal queues, and the light on the stone buildings in the late afternoon is remarkable.
