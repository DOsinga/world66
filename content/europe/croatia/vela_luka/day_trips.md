---
title: "Day Trips"
type: section
---

Vela Luka's position at the western tip of the island makes it a useful base for day trips by boat. Mljet National Park, with its saltwater lakes and pine forest, is a popular full-day excursion, as are riviera cruises along the neighbouring coves for swimming and a fish picnic. Wine, walking, and cultural tours through the island's western villages can also be arranged locally, and Mostar and the Neretva delta are reachable for those willing to cross into Bosnia and Herzegovina for the day.
