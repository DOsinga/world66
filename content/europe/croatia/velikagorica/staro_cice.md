Staro Čiče is a small village south of Velika Gorica where traditional Turopolje wooden architecture survives in a reasonably authentic state. The farmhouses follow a regional pattern: long and low, with broad roofs designed to shed the heavy snowfall common in the Posavina lowlands, and wooden galleries along the front facade used for drying corn and storage.

The village is accessible by road from Velika Gorica in about ten minutes. There is no visitor infrastructure — it is a working village rather than a heritage site. Walking through it is pleasant and unobtrusive; the buildings are on private plots but visible from the lanes.
