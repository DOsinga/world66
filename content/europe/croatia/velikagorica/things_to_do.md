title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Velika Gorica's main visitor draw is the [Turopolje Museum](/europe/croatia/velikagorica/turopolje_museum), which documents the history of the Turopolje region — a flat agricultural plain with distinctive wooden architecture that has been largely depopulated by the growth of Zagreb. The [Turopolje Noblemen's Hall](/europe/croatia/velikagorica/turopolje_noblemen_hall) is the town's most historically significant building, a seventeenth-century courthouse that once served the minor nobility of the region.

For those in the area with time before a flight from [Zagreb Airport](/europe/croatia/velikagorica/zagreb_airport), a walk through the town centre and a coffee takes less than an hour. The nearby village of [Staro Čiče](/europe/croatia/velikagorica/staro_cice) preserves traditional Turopolje wooden architecture in a form largely lost elsewhere in the region.
