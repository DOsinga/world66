The Turopolje Museum in Velika Gorica covers the history, ethnology, and archaeology of the Turopolje region, the flat lowland south of Zagreb that the town serves as a centre. The collection includes material from prehistoric settlements on the Sava River plain, Roman-period finds, and a substantial ethnological section documenting the traditional wooden architecture and crafts of the region.

The museum is modest in size but covers its regional subject thoroughly. For visitors interested in Croatian rural history and the distinctive landscape of the Posavina lowlands, it provides context that the larger Zagreb museums do not. Opening hours and admission are standard for a Croatian municipal museum.
