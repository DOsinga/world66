---
title: Eating Out
type: section
---

Vinkovci is deep Slavonian country, and the food reflects it: grilled meats, paprika-heavy stews, freshwater fish, and above all kulen — the cured sausage that defines this region's cuisine. Local wines from the Kutjevo and Ilok appellations are widely available and excellent value.

Look for restaurants serving **čobanac** (a spiced meat stew), **fiš paprikaš** (freshwater fish paprikash, usually carp or catfish), and home-cured meats with pickled vegetables. The market on Trg bana Šokčevića is worth a visit for local produce.
