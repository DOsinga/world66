---
title: Eating Out
type: section
---

Slavonian cuisine is hearty, meat-centred, and heavily influenced by Hungarian cooking from the centuries of Habsburg rule. Paprika — hot and sweet — appears in nearly everything, and pork is the dominant protein. The region's specialities are worth seeking out: kulen sausage, fiš paprikaš (river fish stew), and roštilj (grilled meats) are the dishes that define the Slavonian table. Vinkovci has a range of restaurants and kafići catering to local tastes rather than tourist expectations.
