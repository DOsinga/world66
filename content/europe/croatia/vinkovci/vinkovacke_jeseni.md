Vinkovačke Jeseni — the Vinkovci Autumn festival — is held each year in late September and early October and is the biggest celebration of Slavonian folk culture in Croatia. The festival fills the central square and surrounding streets with performers in traditional embroidered costumes, folk music groups playing tamburica (mandolin-family instruments), dancing ensembles, and food stalls selling Slavonian specialities.

The food is a major draw: kulen, the fiery paprika-spiced sausage that is the region's most famous product, appears in every form imaginable, alongside other Slavonian charcuterie, roast meats, and quantities of local wine and rakija. The atmosphere during the festival's peak weekend is genuinely festive — Slavonians take their folk traditions seriously, and the costumes and music are not performed ironically. If you are in Croatia in late September and want to see something genuinely regional rather than tourist-packaged, Vinkovci during Jeseni is excellent.
