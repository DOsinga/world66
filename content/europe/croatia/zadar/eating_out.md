---
title: "Eating Out"
type: section
---

Zadar eats like the rest of Dalmatia: heavy on grilled fish, octopus, lamb peka (slow-cooked under a metal lid covered with coals), prosciutto and Pag cheese. Italian influence — pasta, risotto, pizza — runs through almost every menu.

The best places are tucked into the old town's small streets rather than on the main squares. Reservations are wise from June to September.
