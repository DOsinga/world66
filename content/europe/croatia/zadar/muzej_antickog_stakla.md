Muzej antičkog stakla covers glassmaking from ancient jewellery and Roman vessels to modern glass art. The collection is especially useful in Zadar, where Roman finds are part of the city's wider archaeological story.

Live glass-blowing demonstrations are often part of the visit, making the museum more engaging than a simple display of cases.
