---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

**May, June and September** are the best months. Daytime temperatures sit in the 22–28°C range, the sea is warm enough for swimming, and the old town is busy but not unbearable.

**July and August** are very hot and very crowded. Restaurants need reservations, the islands fill with day-trippers, and prices climb everywhere.

**October to April** is the off-season. The light is beautiful, the prices drop dramatically, and the locals reclaim the town — but many restaurants and tour operators shut, and the wind (bura) off the mountains can be fierce.

The annual highlight is the **Musical Evenings in St Donatus** festival of early music, running through July and August in the church of the same name.
