Ugrešić, a former Zagreb academic who left Croatia under nationalist pressure in the 1990s, wrote this novel in three loosely linked parts braided around the Slavic folklore figure of Baba Yaga. The opening section is a daughter caring for her ageing mother in Zagreb; the second is a trio of old women at a Czech spa; the third a learned essay on the Baba Yaga myth. Funny, wise, and full of details of the city as it was in the post-war decades.
