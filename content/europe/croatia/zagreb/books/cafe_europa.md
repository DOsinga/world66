Drakulić is Zagreb's most internationally translated essayist, and Café Europa — a series of short pieces written in the late 1990s — captures the strange, half-arrived condition of post-Yugoslav Croatia better than anything else of its period. The book moves between Zagreb cafés, Vienna, Bucharest, and Berlin, watching ordinary people in former communist countries trying to assemble what comes next. Funny, unsentimental, indispensable for anyone trying to understand the country.
