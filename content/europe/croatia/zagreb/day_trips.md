Zagreb is a good base for the inland half of Croatia. The most-visited day trip is to **Plitvice Lakes National Park**, two hours south by car or bus, where wooden walkways weave between sixteen tiered lakes and a series of waterfalls — easily one of Europe's most spectacular natural sites, though heavily touristed in summer.

**Samobor**, a small Habsburg-era town twenty minutes west by bus, is the classic weekend escape for Zagrebans — known for its cream cakes (*samoborska kremšnita*), its small museum, and the wooded hills behind. The region of **Zagorje**, an hour north, holds the great Habsburg hilltop castles of **Trakošćan** and **Veliki Tabor**, plus the Krapina Neanderthal Museum on the site where Croatia's most famous prehistoric finds were made. Slovenia's **Ljubljana** is just over an hour away on a fast train.
