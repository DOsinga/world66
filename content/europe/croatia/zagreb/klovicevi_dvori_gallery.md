Klovićevi Dvori Gallery occupies a former Jesuit monastery in Zagreb's Upper Town and hosts changing exhibitions of Croatian and international art. The programme ranges across periods and styles, so it is worth checking what is on before climbing up to Gradec.

The gallery is easy to combine with Stone Gate, Lotrščak Tower, and the museums around St Mark's Square. Most exhibitions allow photography without flash.
