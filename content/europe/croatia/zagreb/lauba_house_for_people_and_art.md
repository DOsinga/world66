Lauba is a contemporary art space in a former textile factory west of Zagreb's historic center. The industrial shell gives the exhibitions a rawer setting than the city's older museums, and the program tends to focus on modern and contemporary Croatian art.

It is a strong choice if you want Zagreb beyond the cathedral, cafes, and Austro-Hungarian streets. The building itself is part of the appeal: a reminder that the city's cultural life has been expanding into reused industrial spaces.
