---
title: "Shopping"
type: section
---

The main shopping street is **Ilica**, the long east–west spine that runs from Ban Jelačić Square out toward the western suburbs; it carries the international high-street chains and the local department stores. Smaller, more characterful shopping lives along **Radićeva** (climbing toward the Upper Town) and the side streets around **Petar Preradović Square** — clothing boutiques, design shops, vintage stores.

For food, **Dolac Market** is the obvious destination. For Croatian wine, several specialist shops on **Vlaška** and **Frankopanska** stock the small producers from Istria, Dalmatia, and Slavonia. The bigger malls — Avenue Mall, City Center One — sit on the southern ring road and require a tram or car.
