The Technical Museum Nikola Tesla gives Zagreb a different museum register from archaeology, naive art, and Austro-Hungarian streetscapes. Its halls cover machinery, transport, energy, mining, and scientific demonstration, with Tesla's name anchoring the museum's public identity.

It is especially good for families and travellers who like old machines, working models, and industrial history. The museum sits south-west of the centre, but it combines well with the Botanical Garden and the run of cultural institutions around the lower town.
