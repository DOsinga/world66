The Typhlological Museum is a small, specialised museum about blindness, visual impairment, and the institutions created around them. Exhibits cover tactile writing systems, education for blind people, and newer assistive technologies.

Its most memorable section is a dark room where visitors identify objects without sight. The museum is not one of Zagreb's headline stops, but it is thoughtful, unusual, and close enough to the Lower Town museums to combine with a wider walk.
