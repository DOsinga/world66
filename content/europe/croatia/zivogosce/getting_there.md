---
title: "Getting There"
type: section
---

The nearest airport is Split (about an hour and a half by road); Dubrovnik is about three hours south. The Adriatic Highway (D8) runs through the village, and frequent buses connect Živogošće to Makarska (20 minutes north) and on to Split, Dubrovnik, and the inland cities. A car is useful for day trips up onto Biokovo or down the coast.
