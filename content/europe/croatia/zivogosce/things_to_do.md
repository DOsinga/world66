---
title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Živogošće is a beach holiday with a couple of genuinely interesting walks attached. The main draw is the long pebble beach and the unusually clear water; beyond that, the Franciscan monastery in the hills above the bay and the Vruja springs at the south end of the village are worth seeking out, and the Biokovo mountain behind the village gives the most rewarding day-hike on the Makarska Riviera.
