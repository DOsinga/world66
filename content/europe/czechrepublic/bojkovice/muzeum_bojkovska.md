A small local museum housed in a historic building just off Masaryk Square. The collection covers Bojkovice and the surrounding Moravské Kopanice microregion: folk costumes, traditional tools, archaeology from local prehistoric sites, and changing exhibitions on themes from the region's history.

Limited opening hours — typically afternoons only and closed Mondays — so check before you go. Worth thirty minutes if you have any interest in the folk culture that still survives in these hills.
