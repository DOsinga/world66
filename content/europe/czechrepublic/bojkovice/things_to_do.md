---
title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Bojkovice is small enough that a single afternoon covers everything in town. The main draw is the Nový Světlov chateau on the wooded hill at the north edge; from there, walking trails fan out into the White Carpathians. The town square holds a modest local history museum and the parish church of St. Lawrence. Most visitors use the town as a quiet base for hiking and folk-festival outings into the surrounding Moravian Slovakia region.
