The Brno Racing Circuit, widely known as the Masaryk Circuit, is the city's major motorsport venue, set in wooded hills west of the centre. It has hosted international motorcycle and car racing and remains the place to look for track days, race weekends, and high-speed events around Brno.

It is event-dependent: without something scheduled, the circuit is mainly of interest to dedicated motorsport fans. With a race on, it becomes a distinctive Brno day out, pairing forested surroundings with the noise and rhythm of a proper road-racing circuit.
