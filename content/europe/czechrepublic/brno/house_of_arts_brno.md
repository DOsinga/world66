The House of Arts Brno is one of the city's useful cultural stops, sitting close to the centre and focused on changing exhibitions rather than a fixed collection. Its programme often moves between contemporary art, photography, architecture, and design.

It pairs well with Brno's stronger museum and theatre circuit because it shows the city's present tense. Check what is on before you go; when the exhibition suits your interests, it can be one of the better small-gallery visits in town.
