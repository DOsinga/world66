Lužánky Park is Brno's classic urban park, a broad green space with mature trees, lawns, paths, and a long civic history. It is not a monument in the usual sense, but it gives a very usable pause from the city's churches, squares, and cafés.

The park is especially appealing on warm days, when local life fills the lawns and paths. For visitors, it helps Brno feel lived-in rather than only architectural.
