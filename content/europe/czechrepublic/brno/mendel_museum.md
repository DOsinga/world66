The Mendel Museum stands beside the Augustinian abbey where Gregor Mendel carried out the pea-plant experiments that changed biology. For Brno, it is one of the rare sights whose importance reaches far beyond the city.

The museum explains Mendel's life, the monastery setting, and the scientific ideas that grew from his work. Even if genetics is not your usual travel theme, the place is compact, specific, and genuinely tied to the ground beneath your feet.
