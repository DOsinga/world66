---
title: Things to Do
type: section
---

Brno has a compact historic centre that rewards walking. The Cathedral of Ss Peter and Paul and Špilberk Castle dominate the skyline from their respective hills; the Capuchin Crypt and Brno Ossuary are among the more unusual attractions in Central Europe. Architecture enthusiasts should not miss Villa Tugendhat, one of the most important modernist buildings on the continent.
