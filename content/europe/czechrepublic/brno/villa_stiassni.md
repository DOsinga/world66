Villa Stiassni is one of Brno's handsome interwar villas, built in the late 1920s for textile factory owner Alfred Stiassni and his family. Its restrained modern architecture, large garden and preserved interiors show the confident private world of the city's wealthy industrial families before the Second World War.

After the war the villa became a government residence and hosted state guests, including President Edvard Benes and Fidel Castro. It is a good companion to Brno's better-known modernist houses, especially if you like architecture with a political afterlife.
