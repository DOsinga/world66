České Budějovice is the regional capital of South Bohemia, best known internationally as the home of Budvar — Budweiser Budvar — one of the Czech Republic's most famous breweries and the source of a long-running trademark dispute with the American Anheuser-Busch. The [Budvar Brewery](/europe/czechrepublic/ceskebudejovice/budvar_brewery) runs tours and has a good tap room where you can drink unfiltered lager straight from the conditioning tanks.

The old town is well preserved, built around the [main square](/europe/czechrepublic/ceskebudejovice/main_square) — one of the largest in the Czech Republic at 133 metres on each side. The [Black Tower](/europe/czechrepublic/ceskebudejovice/black_tower) (Černá věž) offers good views over the rooftops and south to the Šumava hills. The [Cathedral of St Nicholas](/europe/czechrepublic/ceskebudejovice/cathedral_of_st_nicholas) and the [Dominican Monastery](/europe/czechrepublic/ceskebudejovice/dominican_monastery) — the oldest Gothic building in the city — are both on or just off the square.

České Budějovice makes a useful base for exploring South Bohemia. [Český Krumlov](/europe/czechrepublic/ceskykrumlov) is 25 km to the south — a UNESCO-listed medieval town in a river bend that deserves a full day. The Šumava national park is easily reachable, and several South Bohemian castles are within day-trip range.
