Alš South Bohemian Gallery is one of the region's main art institutions, with collections and exhibitions that connect South Bohemia to wider Czech art history. It is a useful cultural stop for visitors who want more than beer halls and town squares.

Depending on the exhibition, the gallery can be a short visit or a deeper art stop. It works best for travellers already interested in regional museums rather than those trying to cover České Budějovice in a quick walk.
