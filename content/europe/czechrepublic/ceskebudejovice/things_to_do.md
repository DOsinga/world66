title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

České Budějovice is a rewarding town for an afternoon or a full day. The [main square](/europe/czechrepublic/ceskebudejovice/main_square) is one of the largest in the Czech Republic, and the [Black Tower](/europe/czechrepublic/ceskebudejovice/black_tower) next to it gives the best views in the city. The [Budvar Brewery](/europe/czechrepublic/ceskebudejovice/budvar_brewery) runs excellent tours — this is the original Budweiser, and the tap room pours unfiltered lager straight from the conditioning tanks. The [Dominican Monastery](/europe/czechrepublic/ceskebudejovice/dominican_monastery) is the oldest Gothic building in town, while the [South Bohemian Museum](/europe/czechrepublic/ceskebudejovice/south_bohemian_museum) fills a rainy afternoon. The river island of [Střelecký Ostrov](/europe/czechrepublic/ceskebudejovice/strelecky_ostrov) is a quiet green escape at the edge of the old town.
