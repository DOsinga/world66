---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

České Budějovice has a continental climate with warm summers and cold winters. July and August are the warmest months, with temperatures regularly reaching 25–28°C — pleasant for walking the old town. December to February can be cold and grey, though the Christmas market on the main square in late November and December gives the city an appealing atmosphere.

The best time to visit is May to September. Spring is mild and the outdoor cafe tables on the main square come back into use by April. Autumn brings some of the best weather: dry, clear days that are good for cycling the surrounding South Bohemian countryside. The brewery tour runs year-round, so there is no wrong time to visit from that angle.
