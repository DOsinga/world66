Zlata Koruna Monastery is a rewarding excursion from Cesky Krumlov, set in a quiet
village bend of the Vltava rather than inside the tourist crush. The former Cistercian
monastery preserves Gothic architecture, cloister spaces, and a slower monastic
atmosphere.

It is most worthwhile for travellers with an interest in architecture, religious
history, or quieter South Bohemian stops. The trip also gives a useful contrast with
Cesky Krumlov's castle-town spectacle.
