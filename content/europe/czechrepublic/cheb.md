Cheb is a West Bohemian town close to the German border. It is still known by its old German name, Eger, and that borderland history gives the centre a different mood from the grand spa towns nearby. The industrial edges are plain, but the old core is compact, walkable, and worth a real stop.

Start on [King George of Podebrady Square](/europe/czechrepublic/cheb/king_george_square), where merchant houses and the odd little [Spalicek](/europe/czechrepublic/cheb/spalicek) block make the town feel older and stranger than its size suggests. From there, lanes lead toward [Cheb Castle](/europe/czechrepublic/cheb/cheb_castle), with its black tower and Romanesque chapel.

Cheb is not polished like Cesky Krumlov, and that helps. It is a good one-day town for travellers moving between Prague, Karlovy Vary, Marianske Lazne, and Germany: enough history to reward a walk, enough roughness to feel lived-in.
