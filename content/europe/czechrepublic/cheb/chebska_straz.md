Chebska Straz is the easy nature break from Cheb's old centre. The viewpoint sits above the Skalka reservoir, with paths and an educational trail through the woods.

It is not essential if you only have an hour in town, but it rounds out a longer visit nicely, especially after the dense streets around the square.
