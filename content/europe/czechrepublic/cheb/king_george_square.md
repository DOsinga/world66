King George of Podebrady Square is Cheb's main stage. The old merchant houses, galleries, and crooked side lanes give the centre its charm, and the square is small enough that details matter.

Use it as the base for the old town walk. Cheb's industrial outskirts are easy to forget here, where the border-town history is packed into a few tight blocks.
