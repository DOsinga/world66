Spalicek is the strange little block of houses in the middle of Cheb's main square. Eleven originally Gothic buildings stand packed together, giving the square a shape you do not see in most Czech towns.

It is less a museum piece than a visual anchor. Walk around it slowly, then look back from the square edges to see how it breaks the open space into lanes and corners.
