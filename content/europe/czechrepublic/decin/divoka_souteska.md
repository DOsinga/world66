Divoká Soutěska is the upper of the two narrow Kamenice River gorges in Bohemian Switzerland. The route has been used by hikers since the late 19th century and includes a short boat section where cliffs close in around the river. It works well as part of a longer day in the gorges and forest trails north of Děčín.
