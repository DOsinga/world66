Amerika Forest Park is a quiet nature reserve on the edge of Frantiskovy Lazne, centred on ponds and woodland used by breeding and migrating birds. Benches and a small viewing platform make it easy to slow down and watch the water rather than simply pass through.

It is not a grand sight, but it is a good low-key walk if you want a natural pause between the spa town's springs and colonnades.
