František Spring is the old centre of the spa story, the kind of modest pavilion that tells you more about the town than a large monument would. Františkovy Lázně grew around springs like this, with architecture and parkland arranged to make drinking mineral water feel like a social ritual.

Stop here early in a walk through town. The spring gives the surrounding yellow buildings, clipped gardens, and colonnades their reason for being.
