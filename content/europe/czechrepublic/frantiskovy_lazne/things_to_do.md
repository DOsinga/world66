Františkovy Lázně is a town for walking slowly between springs rather than rushing between sights. The [Glauber Springs Hall](/europe/czechrepublic/frantiskovy_lazne/glauber_springs_hall) is the clearest introduction to the drinking-cure side of the spa, while [František Spring](/europe/czechrepublic/frantiskovy_lazne/frantisek_spring) gives the older heart of the resort a simple focus.

Leave time for the [spa parks and colonnades](/europe/czechrepublic/frantiskovy_lazne/spa_parks_and_colonnades). They are not filler between monuments; they are the town's main exhibit.
