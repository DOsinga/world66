Diana Lookout Tower is one of Karlovy Vary's classic viewpoints, standing in the forested hills above the spa centre. You can reach it by funicular or walking paths, making the trip part of the town's long tradition of promenade culture.

The reward is a broad view over the valley, colonnades, and wooded slopes that define Karlovy Vary's setting. It is a strong choice when you want a break from tasting springs and walking the main spa streets.
