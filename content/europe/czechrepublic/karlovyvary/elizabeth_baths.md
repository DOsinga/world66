Elizabeth Baths is a publicly accessible spa complex with an old-school feel, mixing early twentieth-century treatment rooms with plainer later bath facilities. It is not Karlovy Vary's most polished spa stop, but the retro atmosphere and lower prices make it a worthwhile visit for travellers who want to experience the town's bathing culture rather than only admire the colonnades.
