---
title: Things to Do
type: section
---

Kutná Hora's sights divide neatly between the historic town centre and the suburb of Sedlec, about two kilometres away. In the centre, [St. Barbara's Church](/europe/czechrepublic/kutnahora/st_barbaras_church) is the architectural highlight — one of the finest Gothic buildings in Central Europe. The [Italian Court](/europe/czechrepublic/kutnahora/italian_court) traces the town's silver-minting history, and the [Czech Museum of Silver](/europe/czechrepublic/kutnahora/czech_museum_of_silver) offers underground tours of the actual medieval mine shafts. The [Plague Column](/europe/czechrepublic/kutnahora/plague_column) anchors the central square.

In Sedlec, the [Sedlec Ossuary](/europe/czechrepublic/kutnahora/sedlec_ossuary) — the famous bone church — draws most visitors, but the [Sedlec Abbey](/europe/czechrepublic/kutnahora/sedlec_abbey) next door is a masterpiece of Gothic-Baroque architecture that deserves equal attention. Both are a 20-minute walk or short cab ride from the town centre; the Sedlec train stop is convenient if you are coming from Prague.
