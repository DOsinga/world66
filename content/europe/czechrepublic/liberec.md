*Europe > Czech Republic > Liberec*

Liberec lies in northern Bohemia, close to the Polish and German borders, with [Jested Mountain](/europe/czechrepublic/liberec/jested_mountain) rising above the city like a local emblem. It has the solid civic architecture of a former textile city, but the mountains keep it from feeling heavy.

The centre is compact and easy to walk. The neo-Renaissance [City Hall](/europe/czechrepublic/liberec/city_hall) dominates the main square, while Masarykova street leads past museums, galleries, the zoo, and the botanical garden. Liberec also has small surprises: David Cerny's odd [Feast of Giants](/europe/czechrepublic/liberec/feast_of_giants) bus stop, Art Nouveau workers' housing, and old industrial buildings turned to new uses.

Come for a full day if you are already in North Bohemia. Spend the morning in the centre, then go up Jested or use Liberec as a practical base for the Jizera Mountains.
