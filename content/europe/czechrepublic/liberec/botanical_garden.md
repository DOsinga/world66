The Botanical Garden sits next to Liberec Zoo and gives the city an easy indoor-greenhouse stop. It is not the reason to come to Liberec, but it rounds out the museum-and-zoo side of town well.

Keep it for bad weather, families, or anyone who likes plant collections.
