Centrum Babylon is a former textile factory rebuilt as a large indoor entertainment complex, with water-park, science, play, and wellness attractions. It is a very different Liberec from the town hall and mountain views, but it fits the city's habit of reusing industrial space.

It is mainly a family or bad-weather choice. Travellers without children can usually spend their time better in the centre or on Jested.
