Liberec City Hall is the proud face of the old city, a rich neo-Renaissance building from the late 19th century. Its tower, arches, and busy facade make the main square feel far grander than the city's size might suggest.

Start here before wandering the centre. The square around it is the easiest place to read Liberec's German-Czech history and its former textile wealth.
