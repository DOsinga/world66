Feast of Giants is one of Liberec's stranger small sights: a David Cerny sculpture that also functions as a bus stop. The table is set with loaded symbols from Czech and German history, including beer mugs, a carnivorous plant, and the head of Konrad Henlein.

It takes only a few minutes, but it adds bite to a walk around the centre.
