The North Bohemian Museum is the main regional museum in Liberec, set on the museum-and-gallery stretch of Masarykova street. Its collections cover local history, decorative arts, and the craft traditions that grew with the region's industry.

It is a good rainy-day anchor and pairs naturally with the nearby Regional Gallery, zoo, and botanical garden.
