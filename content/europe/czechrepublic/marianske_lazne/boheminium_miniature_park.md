Boheminium displays detailed miniature models of Czech castles, monuments, and historic buildings at 1:25 scale. It is lighter than the spa sights, but it gives families and architecture fans a pleasant break above town.

The walk or short ride up also gets you into the greener edge of Mariánské Lázně.
