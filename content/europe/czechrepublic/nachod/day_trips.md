The [Adršpach-Teplice Rocks](/europe/czechrepublic/adrspach_teplice_rocks), an extraordinary maze of sandstone towers about 40 km west, are the standout excursion from Náchod. Across the border in Poland, [Kudowa-Zdrój](/europe/poland/kudowa_zdroj) offers a faded spa town with mineral colonnades and the [Skull Chapel](/europe/czechrepublic/nachod/skull_chapel) in the Czermna suburb. Further afield, [Hradec Králové](/europe/czechrepublic/hradec_kralove) — the regional capital, about 50 km — has a striking Modernist core by Josef Gočár, and Náchod's writer son Josef Škvorecký made it the literary backdrop of a generation.
