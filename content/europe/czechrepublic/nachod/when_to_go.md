---
title: When to Go
type: section
---

Late spring through early autumn is the best window. The bunkers, the castle, and the surrounding hiking trails are all in full season from May to September; many of the military sites close entirely in winter. July and August are the warmest and busiest months, particularly with Polish day-trippers crossing the border for shopping.

Autumn is beautiful for walks in the wooded hills around Dobrošov, with brilliant beech and birch colour through October. Winter is cold and quiet — the castle stays open with shorter hours, but the rest of the area mostly shuts down.
