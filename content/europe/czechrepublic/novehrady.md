Nové Hrady is a small South Bohemian town close to the Austrian border, south of [České Budějovice](/europe/czechrepublic/ceskebudejovice) and east of [Český Krumlov](/europe/czechrepublic/ceskykrumlov). It is not a big stop, but it has the right ingredients for a quiet Czech detour: a castle, a Baroque monastery, a compact square and wooded hills just outside town.

The town was a frontier stronghold in the Middle Ages, when this part of Bohemia watched the roads into Austria. After 1620 it became closely tied to the Buquoy family, whose presence left both aristocratic buildings and a more settled small-town feel.

Come for a few hours if you are crossing South Bohemia by car or bike. Stay longer if you want easy walking and cycling in the Novohradské Mountains, where forests and small border villages replace the crowds of the better-known Czech towns.
