The Archbishop's Palace is the official seat of the bishops and archbishops of Olomouc. Its political weight is part of the draw: Franz Joseph became emperor here on 2 December 1848, linking the palace to a turning point in Habsburg history.

It is a worthwhile stop for visitors following Olomouc's church architecture and old power centres around the cathedral quarter.
