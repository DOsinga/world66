---
title: "Eating Out"
type: section
---

Olomouc eats well and inexpensively, with traditional Moravian cooking still dominant despite a wave of newer cafés and modern bistros. Specialities to look for include **svíčková** (beef in cream sauce with dumplings), **vepřo-knedlo-zelo** (pork, dumplings and sauerkraut), and **bramboráky** (potato pancakes flavoured with marjoram).

The local cheese, **Olomoucké tvarůžky** — soft, fermented, and famously pungent — is a Hanák regional treasure and an acquired taste. Locals fry them in beer batter, layer them in salads, or serve them on dark rye with onions. **Cukrárna Mahler** is a good old-fashioned café for kava and kremrole pastries; **Moritz** is a brewery-restaurant with reliable Czech food and its own house lager; and **Drápal**, around the corner from Horní náměstí, has been pouring beer to locals since 1936.
