---
title: "Getting There"
type: section
---

**By train.** Olomouc sits on the main Prague–Ostrava–Žilina line, with frequent fast trains. From Prague's main station, **SC Pendolino** and **railjet** services reach Olomouc in about 2 hours; **RegioJet** and **LEO Express** offer cheaper private trains. From Brno, the journey is 90 minutes. From Bratislava and Vienna, expect around 3–4 hours via Brno.

**By bus.** Long-distance coaches by **FlixBus** and **RegioJet** also connect Olomouc to Prague, Brno, Ostrava and across the borders.

**By plane.** There is no commercial airport in Olomouc. The closest are **Brno** (Tuřany, about 90 km), **Ostrava** (Mošnov, about 100 km), and **Vienna** or **Prague** for international long-haul. All three connect by direct train or bus.
