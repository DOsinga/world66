Olomouc Zoological Garden sits on Svaty Kopecek, close to the hilltop basilica. The zoo is a family-friendly stop with animal exhibits, a viewing tower, and wooded paths that make it feel more open than a city-center attraction.

It combines easily with the Basilica Minor on the Holy Hill. Bus 11 runs from central Olomouc to Svaty Kopecek, making the trip straightforward without a car.
