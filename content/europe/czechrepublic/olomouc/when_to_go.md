---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

Olomouc has the central European pattern — warm summers, cold winters, mild shoulder seasons. **May to early October** is the comfortable window: warm enough to sit on the squares with a beer, dry enough to enjoy the surrounding countryside. **July and August** can hit 30°C but Olomouc rarely feels uncomfortably crowded.

**Late November to early January** is the Christmas-market season, when Horní náměstí fills with wooden stalls, mulled wine, and trdelník vendors. **September** brings the **International Organ Festival**, with concerts every weekend in the Church of Saint Maurice. February is bleak; March can be muddy. Snow is possible from December through February.
