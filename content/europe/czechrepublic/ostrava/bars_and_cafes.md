---
title: "Bars and Cafes in Ostrava"
type: section
---

The drinking story in Ostrava begins and largely ends on [Stodolní Street](/europe/czechrepublic/ostrava/stodolni_street), the nightlife strip with 60-plus bars on a single short stretch. Outside Stodolní, the bar scene is more scattered but no less interesting.

**Plan B** is a small live-music venue that doubles as a bar; **Pravda** and **Atlantik Café** are good central spots for a quieter drink. The **Stará Aréna** is a craft beer pub in a former industrial space, with a rotating tap list that often includes Czech micro-breweries you can't find elsewhere. For coffee during the day, **Maryčka** in the Old Town and **Bistro 33** are the standouts.
