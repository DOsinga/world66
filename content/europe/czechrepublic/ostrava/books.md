**Too Loud a Solitude** by Bohumil Hrabal. Not set in Ostrava but written by a Czech who understood industrial labour as well as anyone. A novella about a man who has been compacting waste paper for 35 years; the perfect companion to Lower Vítkovice.

**The Slynx** by Tatyana Tolstaya. An off-beat suggestion: this Russian post-apocalyptic satire shares the same air of post-industrial collapse that Ostrava lives with daily.

**I Served the King of England** by Bohumil Hrabal. A picaresque tour through interwar Czechoslovak society from a waiter's eye view — the same world Ostrava's grand cafes and the Antonín Dvořák Theatre belonged to.

**The Engineer of Human Souls** by Josef Škvorecký. A Czech expatriate novel that swings between Bohemian provincial life and exile in Canada, with the heavy industrial north as a constant reference point.
