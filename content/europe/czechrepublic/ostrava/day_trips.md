---
title: "Day Trips"
type: section
---

Ostrava sits at the eastern end of the Czech Republic and is a useful base for a string of worthwhile day trips. **Štramberk**, a small medieval town 25 km south, has a perfectly preserved old centre and a famous round tower with views over the Beskydy foothills.

The **Beskydy Mountains** themselves are the closest proper hill walking, an hour by car or train from the centre. **Hukvaldy Castle**, a romantic ruin in a deer park, sits a half-hour drive away and is the birthplace of composer Leoš Janáček.

Across the border, **Kraków** is just over two hours by direct train. **Brno** is two hours west.
