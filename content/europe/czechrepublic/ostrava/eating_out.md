---
title: "Eating Out in Ostrava"
type: section
---

Ostrava eats well and cheaply, with a leaning towards heavy, meat-heavy Moravian-Silesian dishes — pork, sauerkraut, dumplings — and the regional speciality of *kalbasa* (smoked sausage) eaten with mustard and slices of dark rye.

**U Zlatého Lva** in the centre is the textbook old-Czech tavern, with goulash and roasted duck and decent beer. **La Petite Conversation** does French-Czech hybrid cooking with a reliably interesting wine list. **Comedor Mexicano**, a long-running spot near Stodolní, is improbably good Mexican food. For lighter fare, **Bistro 33** and **Tovární Caffé** are both contemporary cafe-restaurants worth checking out.

For breakfast, the bakery chain **Pekařství Illík** has shops across the city; expect freshly baked sweet rolls, *koláče* and decent coffee.
