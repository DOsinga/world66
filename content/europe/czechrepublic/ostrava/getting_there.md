---
title: "Getting There"
type: section
---

Ostrava is in the far east of the Czech Republic, close to both the Polish and Slovak borders. **Leoš Janáček Airport** at Mošnov, about 25 km south of the centre, has flights from London, Munich, Warsaw and a handful of seasonal destinations.

By **train**, Ostrava-Svinov is the main station and is a stop on the Prague-Bratislava-Vienna corridor. The fast SC Pendolino and EC trains do Prague in about three hours and Vienna in three and a half. Krakow is just over two hours by direct train.

By **road**, Ostrava sits on the D1 motorway, four hours from Prague or about three hours from Vienna. The bus network is dense — RegioJet, FlixBus and Student Agency all run services from Prague and Brno.
