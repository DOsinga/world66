Stodolní Street is the bar street of Ostrava and arguably the busiest single nightlife strip in the Czech Republic. About sixty bars, clubs and music venues line a few hundred metres of cobbled street, and Friday and Saturday nights bring crowds from all over Moravia.

It is genuinely local — far from the tourist-saturated Prague pub circuit — and the venues run the full range from beer-hall pubs to underground techno cellars to acoustic blues bars. Highlights include **U Sira Frantíka**, a long-running pub with cheap beer; **Marley's**, a reggae bar that has been open since the 1990s; and **Plan B**, a small live-music venue that books touring Czech and Polish acts. Things start late and run very late.
