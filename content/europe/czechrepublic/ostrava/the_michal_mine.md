The Michal Mine is one of Ostrava's strongest industrial-heritage visits, preserving the surface buildings and working routines of a coal mine rather than turning everything into a generic museum shell. Offices, changing rooms, machinery, and mine infrastructure make the labour feel close.

It pairs naturally with Lower Vítkovice and Landek Park, but has its own quieter, more intimate character. Take a guided tour if available, because the story is in the sequence of spaces: how miners arrived, prepared, descended, returned, and became part of the city's hard industrial rhythm.
