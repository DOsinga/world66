---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

**Summer** is when Ostrava comes alive: outdoor seating on every square, the riverfront promenade in use, and a festival calendar that includes [Colours of Ostrava](/europe/czechrepublic/ostrava/colours_of_ostrava) in mid-July — the country's biggest multi-genre music festival — and a string of smaller events at Lower Vítkovice through August.

**Winter** is grey, cold and sometimes smoggy, but the Christmas market on Masaryk Square is one of the better ones in the region and the indoor venues (theatre, opera, Stodolní) keep going regardless. **Spring** and **autumn** can be rainy but the parks are at their best.
