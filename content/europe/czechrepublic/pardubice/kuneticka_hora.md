Kunětická Hora is the hill and castle that rises above the flat country around Pardubice. At 295 m, it is the highest point in the immediate area and gives broad views over the surrounding landscape.

The castle makes the hill more than just a viewpoint, and the trip works well for visitors who have already seen Pardubice's old town and want a short excursion nearby.
