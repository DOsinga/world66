Pardubice Racecourse matters because of the Velka pardubicka, the famously demanding Czech steeplechase run here each autumn. Outside race days it is not the first stop for every visitor, but it explains a large piece of the city's identity.

If your timing matches an event, the racecourse gives Pardubice a sporting edge that most Czech historic towns do not have.
