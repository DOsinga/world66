Kozlovna U Plechandy is a straightforward Czech restaurant near the river, useful because the setting does some of the work. In warm weather, the terrace has one of the better casual views of Písek Stone Bridge.

It is the kind of place to choose for lunch rather than a special dinner: beer, Czech staples, and a good pause between the bridge and the old centre.
