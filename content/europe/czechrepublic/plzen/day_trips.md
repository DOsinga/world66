Western Bohemia is well-served by train and road, making Plzeň a good base for a few day trips. [Karlovy Vary](/europe/czechrepublic/karlovyvary) is 80 km northwest — the famous spa town with its colonnade of mineral springs is around 90 minutes by bus or slow train. [Český Krumlov](/europe/czechrepublic/ceskykrumlov) is 130 km south and takes about two hours by bus, but the fairy-tale castle town is worth the journey.

Closer to Plzeň, the village of **Konstantinovy Lázně** (40 km northwest) is a quieter spa destination. The fortress town of **Horšovský Týn**, 40 km west, has a Renaissance château that sees few foreign visitors. The **Šumava** national park begins about 60 km south — vast forests, peat bogs, and relatively few crowds compared to the better-known Bohemian parks.
