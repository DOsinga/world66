The Pilsner Urquell Brewery is the most important brewing site in the world. Founded in 1842 as the Citizens' Brewery of Plzeň (Měšťanský pivovar), it has operated continuously ever since on the same plot of land southeast of the old town. The brewery complex is large — around 26 hectares — and the guided tour takes you through the full production process, from the malting house to the fermentation cellar.

The highlight of any tour is the descent into the sandstone cellars beneath the brewery, where unfiltered, unpasteurised Pilsner Urquell is poured straight from oak conditioning barrels. This is the beer as it was originally made — cloudy, slightly yeasty, softer than the bottled version — and it is exceptional. Tours run several times daily in Czech and English; book in advance online, especially in summer. After the tour, the Na Spilce restaurant on the brewery grounds serves Czech food alongside tank beer in a cavernous hall that seats hundreds but rarely feels crowded. The brewery museum inside the complex chronicles the full history of the Pilsner style.
