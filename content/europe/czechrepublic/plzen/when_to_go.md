---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

Plzeň has a central European continental climate — cold winters, warm summers, and the most reliable weather in spring and early autumn. July and August are pleasant but can hit 30°C; January and February bring frost and occasional snow, though the city functions normally year-round.

**Spring (April–May)** is the best time to visit: mild weather, long days, and the old town is not yet crowded. **Autumn (September–October)** is equally good, with lower prices than Prague and comfortable temperatures for walking.

The **Plzeň Oktoberfest** (late September) draws crowds to the Pilsner Urquell grounds for a long weekend of unfiltered beer and live music — fun, but book accommodation early. The **Smetanova Plzeň** classical music festival runs each spring. In December, the Republic Square hosts a Christmas market that is quieter and less commercial than Prague's.
