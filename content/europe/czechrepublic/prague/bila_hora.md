Bílá Hora, or White Mountain, is the site of the 1620 Battle of White Mountain, a turning point in Bohemian history and the early Thirty Years' War. The battlefield itself is now a quiet outer-Prague landscape rather than a large museum complex.

Go for the historical context and the memorial setting, not for a full afternoon of exhibits. It is best for visitors who already know the Prague and Habsburg history behind the site.
