Prague Castle is the largest ancient castle complex in the world — a hillside town of palaces, gardens, churches, and galleries covering 70,000 square metres. The focal point is St Vitus Cathedral, begun in 1344 and finished in 1929, with extraordinary stained glass by Alfons Mucha. The Vladislav Hall hosted coronations and jousting tournaments. The Old Royal Palace, Golden Lane (where Franz Kafka briefly lived), and the gardens all require separate tickets. Go in the morning for the view down over the red rooftops of Malá Strana.
