---
title: "Getting There"
type: section
---

Prague is well-connected to the rest of Europe by air, train, and bus.

**By air**

Václav Havel Airport (PRG), 17 km northwest of the centre, handles flights from across Europe and several long-haul routes. Bus 119 connects the airport to Dejvická metro station (Line A) in about 30 minutes; tickets use the standard Prague public transport fare. Taxis and rideshares take around 30–45 minutes to the centre depending on traffic.

**By train**

The main station, Praha Hlavní Nádraží, is on metro Line C and handles international trains from Vienna (4 hours), Berlin (4.5 hours), Munich (5.5 hours), and Warsaw (7 hours). The station has been progressively renovated; the art nouveau upper hall, reopened in 2020, is worth a look. European rail bookings through Austrian Federal Railways (ÖBB) or Deutsche Bahn are usually straightforward.

**By bus**

FlixBus and RegioJet connect Prague with cities across Central Europe at prices well below the trains. The main bus station, Florenc, is on metro Lines B and C, about 10 minutes from the centre. RegioJet also runs comfortable services to Vienna, Bratislava, and Krakow.
