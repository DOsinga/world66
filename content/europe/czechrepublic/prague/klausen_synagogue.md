Klausen Synagogue is the largest synagogue in Prague's former Jewish ghetto and one of Josefov's important Baroque buildings. It stands close to the Old Jewish Cemetery, making it easy to include on a walk through the Jewish Town.

The synagogue is part of the wider Jewish Museum route and adds architectural variety to the district's better-known cemetery and ceremonial hall.
