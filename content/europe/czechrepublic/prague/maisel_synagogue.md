Maisel Synagogue is one of the major monuments of Prague's former Jewish ghetto and forms part of the Jewish Museum circuit. Its exhibitions focus on the history of Jews in Bohemia and Moravia, making it a useful stop for understanding the Jewish Town beyond its famous cemetery.
