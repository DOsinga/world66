Mánes Gallery is a functionalist exhibition building on the Vltava riverbank near the Dancing House. Designed by Otakar Novotný and built between 1928 and 1930, it is one of Prague's notable modernist cultural spaces.

The gallery is run by the Mánes Association of Fine Artists and focuses on modern and contemporary exhibitions. Opening hours and admission depend on the current show, so check the programme before making a special trip.
