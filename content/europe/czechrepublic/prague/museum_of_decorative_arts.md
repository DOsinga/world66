The Museum of Decorative Arts occupies a 17th-century palazzo-style building near Josefov and the Rudolfinum. Its collections focus on applied arts and design, with glass, ceramics, textiles, furniture, metalwork, and contemporary craft shown in a setting that suits the subject.

It is a good stop for visitors interested in Czech glass, design history, or quieter museums around the Old Town and Jewish Quarter.
