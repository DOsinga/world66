The Powder Tower is one of Prague's original city gates, marking the old boundary between the Old Town and New Town. The Gothic tower later served as a gunpowder store, which gave it its present name. It stands beside Municipal House, making the two an easy pair for anyone following the old Royal Route through central Prague.
