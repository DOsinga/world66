Prague City Gallery runs several art spaces around the city, with the House of the Golden Ring near Old Town Square as one of its central venues. The collection focuses on Czech modern and contemporary art, often shown inside historic buildings that are worth seeing in their own right.

It is a good stop when the Old Town crowds get heavy and you want art with a local focus.
