World of Jellyfish is a small aquarium-style exhibition built around illuminated tanks of jellyfish, with sound and visual effects adding to the mood. It is a specialist stop rather than a classic Prague sight, but the displays are unusual and easy to fit into a family day.

Go for the colours, movement, and close-up view of the animals rather than for a long museum visit. Most travellers will not need much more than an hour.
