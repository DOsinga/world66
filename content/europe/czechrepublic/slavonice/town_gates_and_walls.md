Slavonice still has two Renaissance town gates and fragments of its old walls. They are not massive fortifications, but they help define the old town's edges and make the decorated squares feel like part of a complete historic core.

The gates are easy to fold into any walk through town. They also hint at the period when Slavonice was prosperous enough to defend and decorate itself at the same time.
