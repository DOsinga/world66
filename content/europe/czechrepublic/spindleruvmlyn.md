Špindlerův Mlýn is the largest ski resort in the Czech Republic, located in the [Krkonoše National Park](/europe/czechrepublic/spindleruvmlyn/krkonose_national_park) (Giant Mountains) in northeastern Bohemia, close to the Polish border. The area is a national park, and the landscape is rewarding even outside the ski season — excellent hiking in summer, cross-country skiing in winter, and one of Central Europe's finest subalpine environments year-round.

The downhill skiing is modest by Alpine standards — runs are mostly suited to beginners and intermediate skiers — but the resort is well equipped and popular with Czech and German visitors. The ski season typically runs from December through March depending on snowfall.

In summer, the town is a base for hiking in the Krkonoše. [Pančava Waterfall](/europe/czechrepublic/spindleruvmlyn/pancava_waterfall), the highest in the Czech Republic at 148 metres, is reachable on foot from the resort. The highest Czech peak, [Sněžka](/europe/czechrepublic/spindleruvmlyn/snezka) (1,603 m), is reachable by cable car from nearby Pec pod Sněžkou, or on foot via the ridge trail for fit walkers.
