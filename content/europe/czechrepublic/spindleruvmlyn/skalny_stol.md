Skalny Stol is a quieter mountain viewpoint on the edge of the Karkonosze range. The reward is a wide view toward Sniezka and the Jelenia Gora valley, with fewer crowds than the most famous summit routes.

Reaching it takes more effort than the easier viewpoints around Spindleruv Mlyn, so it suits hikers who want a less obvious walk in the national park landscape.
