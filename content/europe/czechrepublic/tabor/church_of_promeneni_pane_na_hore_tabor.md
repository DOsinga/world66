This late-Gothic church anchors Žižka Square, right in the old heart of Tábor. Its tall tower rises above the surrounding rooftops and gives one of the best views over the town and countryside.

Visit it while exploring the square, the Hussite Museum, and the nearby underground tunnels. The church is most rewarding for its setting and tower climb rather than as a long standalone stop.
