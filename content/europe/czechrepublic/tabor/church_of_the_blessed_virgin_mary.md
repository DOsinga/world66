The Church of the Blessed Virgin Mary at Klokoty is a Baroque pilgrimage church just outside Tabor's historic centre. Its white walls, red roofs, and enclosed arcades make it feel quite different from the Hussite old town on the hill.

It is worth the short detour if you have time after the town walls and underground tunnels. The setting is quieter than the centre, and the walk out gives a better sense of Tabor beyond the main square.
