Ústí nad Labem sits on the Elbe in northern Bohemia, about 20 km from the German border. It is an industrial city rather than a picture-postcard Czech stop, but that is part of the point: smokestacks, river bridges, steep wooded slopes and rail lines all crowd into a narrow valley.

The name means roughly "mouth on the Elbe", and the river still defines the city. Trains between Prague and Dresden stop here, making Ústí an easy base for a day or two if you want to explore the Elbe valley without staying in the prettier but busier places.

The most satisfying sight is [Střekov Castle](/europe/czechrepublic/ustinadlabem/strekov_castle), a romantic ruin above the river. Add the zoo, the strange modern Mariánský Bridge and short trips into the surrounding countryside, and Ústí becomes more rewarding than its first impression suggests.
