Aalborg’s Defence and Garrison Museum looks at the city through uniforms, vehicles, weapons, and the long presence of the military in northern Jutland. It is a specialist museum, but the local angle keeps it from feeling like a generic display of hardware.

The strongest material relates to occupation, Cold War defence, and garrison life. It is a good rainy-day stop or a useful addition if you have already seen Lindholm Høje and the central sights. Expect practical exhibits rather than sleek design.
