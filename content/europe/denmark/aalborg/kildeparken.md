Kildeparken is Aalborg's classic central park, close enough to the station and Kunsten to slip naturally into a day of wandering. It has lawns, paths, fountains, and the calm, slightly formal feel of a Danish city park that locals actually use.

Its most distinctive feature is the Singing Trees, planted by visiting musicians and connected with recordings of their songs. It is a small idea, but a charming one, and it gives the park a reason to linger beyond simply sitting in the grass.
