---
title: When to Go
type: section
---

Summer (June–August) is the obvious choice: long days, warm evenings, the terraces of Jomfru Ane Gade full, and the Limfjord at its best for walking the waterfront. Aalborg is livelier than most Danish cities in summer — the student population stays in town and the festival calendar is busy.

Spring and autumn are quieter and still pleasant; the old town looks good in October light. Winter is cold and dark, as it is throughout Jutland, but Aalborg keeps going — the indoor cultural scene (Nordkraft, Kunsten, concerts) is active, and the Christmas market in the city centre is worth a visit. Lindholm Høje is particularly atmospheric in winter fog.
