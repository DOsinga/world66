Aarhus Forestry Botanical Garden is a small arboretum at the northern edge of the Marselisborg forests. Established in 1923, it gathers hundreds of tree and shrub species into a compact five-hectare site.

It is best treated as a quiet add-on to the surrounding forest walks and nearby Marselisborg sights. On windy days, pay attention to warnings about falling branches.
