Aarhus Street Food occupies a converted bus garage near the harbour and is the most practical starting point for casual eating in the city. A large hall of food stalls covers an unusually broad range — Danish open sandwiches, Korean fried chicken, wood-fired pizza, Thai curries, Scandinavian smørrebrød, and several good dessert options. Prices are reasonable, the space is warm and communal in winter and opens onto a courtyard in summer.

It's more interesting than most street food markets of this type because the stalls change regularly and the standards are consistently high. Recommended for lunch or an early dinner, particularly if you're trying to eat well without committing to a full sit-down restaurant.
