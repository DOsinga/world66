Aarhus University Park is a public green space woven through the university campus just north of the city centre. Rolling lawns, mature oaks, and small ponds give it a calmer feel than the surrounding streets.

It is best visited as part of a walk between the university museums and the city centre. The southern section, Vennelystparken, is older and dates from 1824, adding a historic layer to what is already a well-regarded piece of Danish landscape design.
