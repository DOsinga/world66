Aarhus Viking Museum is a small underground museum near the cathedral. It focuses on local life in the Viking Age, with many objects excavated close to where they are displayed.

It is much smaller than Moesgaard Museum, but its central location makes it an easy stop while exploring the old heart of Aarhus.
