Aarhus Volume is a small cultural outfit behind concerts, festivals, and DIY events around the city. Its Volume Village base under Ringgadebroen is part music stage, part workshop, and part social experiment, with the rough-edged feel of Aarhus away from the polished museum circuit.

Check the current programme before going. The space makes most sense when something is on, especially a summer concert or one of the smaller events tied to Institut for (X).
