Badeanstalten Spanien is a renovated 1930s bathhouse near the harbour in central Aarhus. Families come for the pool, while the wellness section adds saunas, steam rooms, and a more grown-up reason to linger.

It is not a museum-piece bathhouse, but the historic building and central location make it one of the more characterful indoor swimming options in the city.
