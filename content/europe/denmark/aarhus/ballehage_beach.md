Ballehage Beach is a clean, sandy stretch south of Aarhus, backed by the green slopes of the Marselisborg Forests. It is a relaxed place for swimming, sunbathing, or a picnic, and it pairs easily with nearby stops such as Marselisborg Deer Park and Den Uendelige Bro.
