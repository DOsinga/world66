Bellevue Beach Park is a relaxed northern beach area in Aarhus, with sand, grass, and open views over the Kattegat. It is less central than Den Permanente but useful when you want more space and a less urban swim.

In summer it works as a simple local escape: bring a towel, check the water temperature, and combine it with a ride or walk along the coast. Outside warm weather, it is still a pleasant place for a brisk seaside break.
