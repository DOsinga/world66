Den Japanske Have is a Japanese garden on the northern edge of Aarhus, laid out in traditional chisen kaiyu style for strolling around ponds, plantings, and composed views. It is a quieter garden visit than the central museums and harbourfront sights.

Opening times can be limited, especially outside the main season, so check before making the trip out from the centre.
