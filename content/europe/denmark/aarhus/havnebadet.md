Havnebadet is Aarhus's harbor bath, a public swimming complex built into the redeveloped waterfront. It gives visitors a direct way to use the harbor rather than just look at it, with pools and decks set among the newer architecture of Aarhus Ø.

It is most appealing in warm weather, but the setting is worth seeing even outside swimming season as part of a waterfront walk.
