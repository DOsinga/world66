Helsingør Theatre is a preserved historic theatre inside Den Gamle By, Aarhus’s open-air town museum. Its value lies in the survival of a small 19th-century playhouse atmosphere, with boxes, stage, and decorative details that make performance history feel tangible.

Because it sits within Den Gamle By, treat it as part of that larger museum visit rather than a standalone reason to cross town. It is especially rewarding for travellers interested in theatre, interiors, or the social life of old Danish towns.
