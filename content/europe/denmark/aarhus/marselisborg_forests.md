Marselisborg Forests give Aarhus an unusually easy escape: beech woodland, rolling paths, and glimpses of the bay just south of the city. The area links naturally with Marselisborg Palace, Mindeparken, and the coast, so it can be a short walk or a longer green loop.

The woods are especially good if you have been moving between museums and cafes in the centre. Paths are well used by locals, and the deer park nearby adds a simple, family-friendly stop without turning the outing into a formal attraction.
