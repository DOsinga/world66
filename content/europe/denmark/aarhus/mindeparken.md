Mindeparken, or Marselisborg Memorial Park, is a broad green space south of central
Aarhus near the royal residence. Its lawns, sea views, and memorial landscape make it
more contemplative than a simple recreation ground.

The park is easy to combine with Marselisborg Palace, the coast, or a longer walk out of
the city centre. It is best in good weather, when the open space and views toward the
bay become the main event.
