The Museum of Ancient Art is run by Aarhus University and focuses on Classical-era objects from Greece, Rome, Egypt, the Near East, and the wider Mediterranean world. The collection includes sculpture, ceramics, small artefacts, and one of Europe's larger collections of ancient Greek coins.

It is a specialist museum, best for visitors with an interest in archaeology, classical art, or university collections rather than a broad city-history stop.
