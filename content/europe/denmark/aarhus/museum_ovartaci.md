Museum Ovartaci focuses on art made by psychiatric patients, with special attention to Ovartaci, an artist who spent much of her life at Aarhus's former psychiatric hospital in Risskov. The collection is unusual, personal, and more intimate than the city's larger art museums.

It is worth considering if you are interested in outsider art, mental-health history, or smaller museums with a strong point of view. Guided visits may need to be arranged ahead.
