Aarhus City Hall is one of Denmark's great modern civic buildings, designed by Arne Jacobsen and Erik Møller with furniture by Hans Wegner. Its marble-clad exterior is restrained, but the building rewards a closer look for its clean lines, clock tower, and carefully designed interior details.
