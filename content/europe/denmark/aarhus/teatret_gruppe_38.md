Teatret Gruppe 38 is a small theatre with a reputation much larger than its room. It is best known for precise, imaginative stage work, often aimed at children and families but made with enough craft to hold adult attention too.

For travellers, the appeal is the scale: this is not grand civic theatre but a close-up performance space where set design, gesture, and timing do the heavy lifting. Check the programme before you go, as language and age range matter more here than at a music or dance show.
