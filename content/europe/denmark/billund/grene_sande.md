Grene Sande is a protected heath and sandy inland landscape near Billund, useful when you want a quieter outdoor break from LEGOLAND, Lalandia, and the town's family attractions. The terrain is open, low, and distinctly Jutlandic, with paths through heath, scrub, and dune-like sand.

It is not a dramatic wilderness, but it gives Billund visitors an easy way to see local nature without a long drive. Go for a short walk, bring wind protection, and treat it as a calm reset between busier attractions.
