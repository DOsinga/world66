WOW Park Billund is an outdoor family adventure park that gives the town a more physical alternative to Lego-branded attractions. The emphasis is on climbing, nets, slides, treehouses, and forest play, with enough scale to keep children moving for hours.

It is best for families who want fresh air and activity rather than another indoor attraction. In a place where many visits revolve around Legoland and Lalandia, WOW Park broadens Billund into a fuller family destination, especially in decent weather.
