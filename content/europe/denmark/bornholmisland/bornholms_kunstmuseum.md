Bornholms Kunstmuseum is the island's main art museum, set above the rocky coast north of Gudhjem. The collection focuses on Bornholm painters and other artists tied to the island, including Oluf Host and Karl Isakson.

The building is part of the draw: a bright, modern museum arranged around sea views, cliffs, and changing light. It is worth combining with the nearby coastal walks if you have a car or bike.
