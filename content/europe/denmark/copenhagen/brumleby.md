Brumleby is a small 1850s workers' housing quarter near Trianglen and Parken Stadium. It was built by the Danish Medical Association after a cholera epidemic as a model for healthier low-cost housing.

The low yellow houses, gardens, and quiet lanes make it a short but worthwhile detour in Osterbro, especially for visitors interested in Copenhagen's social history and well-preserved residential architecture.
