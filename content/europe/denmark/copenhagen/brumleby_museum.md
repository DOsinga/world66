Brumleby Museum is a small local museum in the old cooperative shop and library of Brumleby, Copenhagen's historic workers' housing estate. It is run by residents, so the visit feels personal rather than institutional.

The museum is best for travellers already in Østerbro who are interested in Copenhagen's social history and housing experiments. Opening hours can be limited, so check before making a special trip.
