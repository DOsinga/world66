Bryggebroen is a pedestrian and bicycle bridge across Copenhagen's harbour, linking Islands Brygge with the Vesterbro side near Fisketorvet. Opened in 2006, it was the first new bridge across the harbour in more than 50 years and helped turn the waterfront into a everyday cycling route.

It is most useful as part of a harbour ride or walk, especially when paired with the nearby Cykelslangen cycle bridge.
