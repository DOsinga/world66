Cafe Bopa anchors Bopa Plads, a quiet square in residential Østerbro. The terrace is one of the best in the neighbourhood: a large, shaded outdoor space that fills up on warm afternoons and stays busy into the evening. Inside is casual and unfussy.

The kitchen runs all day — good for a long weekend breakfast or a post-Fælledparken lunch. As the evening wears on it shifts toward a bar crowd, with late closing on weekends. No pretension; a solid neighbourhood staple.
