Charlottenborg Palace is a 17th-century mansion on Kongens Nytorv, best visited today for Kunsthal Charlottenborg, its contemporary art exhibition space. The building is also tied to Copenhagen's art world through the Royal Danish Academy of Fine Arts, making it a worthwhile stop for both architecture and changing exhibitions.
