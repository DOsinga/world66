The canal system of Christianshavn — built in the 17th century on the Dutch model — is one of Copenhagen's most photogenic districts. The main canal, Christianshavns Kanal, runs through the heart of the neighbourhood flanked by yellow and red brick warehouses, houseboats, and the occasional kayak. Walking both sides of the canal and crossing on the small bridges takes about an hour and is one of the best free things to do in Copenhagen.

The canal connects to the wider harbour, and the mixture of historic buildings and working watercraft gives Christianshavn a texture that the older city centre largely lacks. Café Wilder on Wildersgade is a good stop for coffee before or after the walk.
