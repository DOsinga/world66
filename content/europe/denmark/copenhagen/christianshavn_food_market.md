The small covered market on Christianshavn's main square offers fresh produce, cheese, bread, and prepared food from a handful of vendors. It is modest compared to the larger Torvehallerne in the Latin Quarter, but the Christianshavn market has a neighbourhood feel — the customers are mostly local residents, prices are reasonable, and the selection reflects what the neighbourhood actually eats.

Good for picking up lunch ingredients or a prepared meal to eat on the canal banks nearby. Open on weekdays and mornings at weekends.
