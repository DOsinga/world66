Christiania has its own distinctive bicycle culture, including the famous Christiania cargo bike — a three-wheeled cycle with a large wooden box at the front that has become a symbol of Copenhagen cycling and is now manufactured and sold worldwide. You can see these bikes in use throughout the free state, often loaded with children, groceries, or building materials.

Several workshops in Christiania still build and repair these bikes by hand. The car-free lanes of the free state are among the most peaceful cycling environments in Copenhagen, making the community a pleasant place to ride through even if you are not buying anything.
