Copenhill turns the roof of the Amager Bakke waste-to-energy plant into a ski slope, climbing wall, hiking route, and city viewpoint. Even if you do not ski, the building is one of Copenhagen's boldest modern sights and a good excuse to look back over the harbour from above.
