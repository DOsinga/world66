Davids Samling, also known as the David Collection, is one of Copenhagen's best small museums. Its strongest holdings are Islamic art, but the collection also includes European eighteenth-century art and Danish early modern works.

The museum is central, calm, and easy to pair with Rosenborg Castle or the Round Tower. It is especially good if you want a focused art stop that is less crowded than the city's larger museums.
