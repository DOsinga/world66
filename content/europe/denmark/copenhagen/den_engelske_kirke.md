Den Engelske Kirke, also known as St Alban's Church, is an Anglican church beside Kastellet. Completed in 1887, it was backed by Queen Alexandra and stands out for its English Gothic style and flint-stone walls.

It is an easy stop with Kastellet, the Gefion Fountain, and the walk toward the Little Mermaid.
