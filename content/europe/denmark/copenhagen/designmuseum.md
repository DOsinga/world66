Designmuseum Danmark, in an 18th-century former hospital near Amalienborg, has one of Europe's finest collections of Danish and international design and applied arts. The permanent collection covers furniture, fashion, industrial design, textiles, and ceramics from the 18th century to today, with particular strength in 20th-century Scandinavian modernism.

The Danish design room is the heart of the collection: Arne Jacobsen's chairs, Poul Henningsen's PH lamps, Hans Wegner's furniture, and Kaare Klint's safari chair. These are not just museum pieces — many are still in production, and seeing them in context rather than a showroom makes a difference. The building itself is worth the visit: a converted baroque hospital with beautiful courtyards.
