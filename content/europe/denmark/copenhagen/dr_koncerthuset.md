DR Koncerthuset is Copenhagen's main concert hall complex in Orestad, designed by Jean Nouvel for Denmark's public broadcaster. Its blue exterior cube and warm timber auditorium make it one of the city's most important modern cultural buildings.

The best reason to come is for a performance, but architecture-minded visitors may also want to see how the building anchors this newer part of Copenhagen.
