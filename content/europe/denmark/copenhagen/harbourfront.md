Copenhagen's inner harbour has been transformed over the past three decades from an industrial waterway into a swimming and cycling destination. The water is clean enough to swim in — remarkable for a major city — and several floating harbour baths (Havnebadet) are open in summer, including the popular Islands Brygge baths with their pools and diving platforms on the south harbour.

The harbour is crossed by a series of footbridges, including the Cykelslangen (Bicycle Snake) — an elevated bicycle path that curves above the water — and the Inderhavnsbroen pedestrian bridge. Walking or cycling along the waterfront from the Black Diamond library south to Islands Brygge and back gives you the best overview of how the city has evolved. The promenade is lined with former industrial buildings now converted to apartments, offices, and restaurants.
