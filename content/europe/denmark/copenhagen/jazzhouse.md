Jazzhouse on Niels Hemmingsens Gade is Copenhagen's main dedicated jazz club, with live music most nights from around 8pm. The programme ranges from Danish jazz groups to international names visiting for the Copenhagen Jazz Festival or on European tours. The room holds a few hundred people, has good acoustics, and serves a decent selection of beer and cocktails.

The Danish jazz scene is genuinely strong — the country produces an outsized number of respected jazz musicians relative to its size, partly due to the music conservatory system and partly due to venues like Jazzhouse that provide a consistent stage. The Monday night sessions are cheaper and often feature interesting local players. Booking is recommended for name acts; walk-ins are usually possible on weekdays.
