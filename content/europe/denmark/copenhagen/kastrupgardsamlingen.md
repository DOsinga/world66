Kastrupgardsamlingen is an art museum set in an 18th-century country house on Amager. Its collection is strongest in graphic art, including Danish artists and works connected to the Cobra movement, with temporary exhibitions adding variety.

It is a quieter Copenhagen museum, useful when you want something smaller and more local than the central institutions.
