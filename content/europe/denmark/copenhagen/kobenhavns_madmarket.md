The Sunday flea market at Halmtorvet in Vesterbro is one of Copenhagen's better outdoor markets — a mixture of vintage clothing, household goods, and secondhand books spread across the square. It runs on Sunday mornings from spring through autumn, drawing a mix of locals looking for furniture and tourists looking for Scandinavian design at non-museum prices.

The market is smaller than some European equivalents but more curated — genuine vintage rather than junk, with prices to match. The surrounding cafés do a brisk trade in brunch as the market winds down around noon.
