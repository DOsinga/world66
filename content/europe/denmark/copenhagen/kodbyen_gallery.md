Scattered through the Meatpacking District alongside the restaurants and bars are a handful of contemporary art galleries that have set up in former industrial spaces. The scale of the buildings — high ceilings, thick walls, concrete floors — makes them naturally suited to large-scale installation and sculpture. Galleri Bo Bjerggaard is the most established.

The galleries are mostly free to enter and tend to be open during the day when the restaurants are quiet. A good way to spend a morning before the Meatpacking District becomes busy with lunch crowds.
