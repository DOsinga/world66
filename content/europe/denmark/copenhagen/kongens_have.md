Kongens Have, the King's Garden, is Copenhagen's busy green space beside Rosenborg Castle. It is a good place for a walk, a picnic or a pause between the castle, the Botanical Garden and the Statens Museum for Kunst.

In summer, the garden often feels more like a local meeting place than a formal royal park, which is part of its appeal.
