Landbohojskolens Have is the old University Garden at the University of Copenhagen's Frederiksberg campus. It is a small botanical garden with a focus on cultivated plants, laid out as a calm green pocket near the denser parts of Frederiksberg.

It is not a major sight on its own, but it is a pleasant stop if you are already exploring Frederiksberg and want a quieter garden than the better-known palace grounds.
