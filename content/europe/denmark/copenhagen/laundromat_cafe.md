The Laundromat Café on Nørre Allé is Østerbro's branch of a small Copenhagen chain that genuinely combines a working coin laundry with a full café. You can order breakfast, a burger, or a glass of wine while your clothes spin. The interior is comfortably lived-in: mismatched chairs, reliable wifi, an all-day menu broad enough to handle any craving. Long opening hours make it a useful anchor at unusual times of day.

Other branches in Nørrebro, Frederiksberg, and Vesterbro operate on the same format.
