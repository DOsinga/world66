The Kødbyen — Meatpacking District — in Vesterbro is the best cluster of restaurants, bars, and clubs in Copenhagen. The old slaughterhouse buildings from the early 20th century have been converted into a creative quarter where restaurants, design studios, galleries, and food producers share space with the remaining meat traders who never left.

The white sector (Hvidbyen) at the southern end is the culinary heart: Madklubben, Kødbyens Fiskebar (for seafood), Bæst (for wood-fired pizza and natural wine), and a dozen other places of varying ambition. On weekend evenings the outdoor spaces fill up with people eating and drinking, and the area has the relaxed, industrial-cool atmosphere that cities across Europe have been trying to manufacture artificially. This one is the real thing.
