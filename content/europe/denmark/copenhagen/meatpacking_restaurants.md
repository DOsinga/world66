The Meatpacking District (Kødbyen) contains the densest concentration of good restaurants in Copenhagen. In the white sector (Hvidbyen), former cold storage and processing facilities have been converted into ambitious restaurant spaces. Kødbyens Fiskebar specialises in seafood and natural wine; Bæst does wood-fired pizza and charcuterie made in-house; Madklubben is reliably good for Danish bistro cooking.

The outdoor spaces between the buildings are equally important — tables spill out in good weather and the whole area operates as an informal food and drinks quarter from late afternoon onwards. Most restaurants are a step up from casual but not formal, and the atmosphere is more relaxed than the prices suggest. Book ahead for the Fiskebar on weekends.
