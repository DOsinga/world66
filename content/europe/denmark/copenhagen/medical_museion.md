Medical Museion is a museum of medical history in central Copenhagen. Its exhibitions look at medicine, pharmacy, dentistry, bodies, and health through objects, research stories, and public displays.

The museum is a good fit for visitors who want a quieter, more specialized stop near Frederiksstaden, Designmuseum Danmark, and the Marble Church.
