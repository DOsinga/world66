Nordatlantens Brygge is a North Atlantic cultural house in an old Christianshavn dock building. Its changing exhibitions, talks, and performances focus on Greenland, Iceland, the Faroe Islands, and the wider North Atlantic, adding a different cultural angle to the harborfront walk.
