Nørrebro Bryghus is a craft brewery and restaurant that has been making beer in Nørrebro since 2003 — long before Copenhagen's current beer scene developed. The brewery produces a range of ales and lagers, some traditional Danish styles, some more experimental, and the tap room serves them fresh alongside hearty Danish food. The building has high ceilings, exposed brewery equipment, and communal tables.

Good for a long afternoon of drinking and eating in the neighbourhood. The brewery tours (when available) give a sense of the scale of Danish craft brewing and the local ingredients used.
