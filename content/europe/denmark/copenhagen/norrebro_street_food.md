Nørrebrogade and the streets around it have the most diverse and affordable food options in Copenhagen. Pakistani and Lebanese takeaways serve good grilled meats and flatbreads at prices significantly below the rest of the city. Vietnamese and Thai restaurants fill the side streets, often operated by recent immigrants with no concessions to tourist expectations — the menus are in Danish and the food is aimed at regulars.

The stretch between Runddelen and Sankt Hans Torv has the most options, open late and cheap. For anyone eating on a budget in Copenhagen, Nørrebro is the neighbourhood to go to.
