Nørrebro has the best secondhand and vintage clothing scene in Copenhagen, concentrated on Elmegade, Ravnsborggade, and the streets around Blågårds Plads. The shops range from well-curated vintage stores with prices to match to cheaper outlets selling clothes by weight. The turnover is high and the selection reflects a genuinely diverse neighbourhood — you find things here you would not find in the more homogeneous vintage shops of other Copenhagen neighbourhoods.

Ravnsborggade in particular has a cluster of antique and furniture dealers alongside the clothing shops. Weekend mornings are the best time to browse — new stock arrives weekly and the quality varies enough that regular visits are rewarded.
