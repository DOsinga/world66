Nørrebrogade is the main artery of Nørrebro, running from the lakes north through the neighbourhood. It is one of the busiest streets in Copenhagen for cycling — the bike lane can feel like a motorway at rush hour — and one of the most economically diverse streets in the city: shawarma shops, mobile phone repair stalls, and cheap grocery stores alongside craft cocktail bars and specialty coffee shops.

The section between Runddelen and Sankt Hans Torv has the highest concentration of places to eat and drink. Sankt Hans Torv itself is a pleasant square with outdoor café seating that fills up in warm weather. The street represents Nørrebro's character better than any individual sight: dense, diverse, and constantly evolving.
