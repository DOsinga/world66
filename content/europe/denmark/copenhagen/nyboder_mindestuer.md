Nyboder Mindestuer is a small museum inside Copenhagen's historic Nyboder district, the rows of yellow naval housing built from the 17th century onward for Danish sailors. The museum shows everyday life in the neighbourhood from its early years.

It is a useful stop if you are already exploring Kastellet, Frederiksstaden, or the streets around Sankt Pauls Gade, where the scale and colour of Nyboder are still easy to read.
