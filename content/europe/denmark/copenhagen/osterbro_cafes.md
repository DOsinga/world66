Østerbro has a café culture distinct from Nørrebro or Vesterbro — less trendy, more comfortable, more local. The cafés around Trianglen and on Nordre Frihavnsgade tend to be well-established places that have been there for a decade or more, with regulars who come in for the same coffee at the same time each day. There is less emphasis on specialty roasters and more on being a reliable neighbourhood institution.

Café Bang & Jensen on Istedgade (technically on the border with Vesterbro) and Café Livingstone near Trianglen are good examples. The best time to visit is a Saturday or Sunday morning when the neighbourhood's residents use the cafés as an extension of their apartments.
