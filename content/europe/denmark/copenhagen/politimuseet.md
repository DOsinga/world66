Politimuseet is Copenhagen's police museum, housed in an old police station next to a prison. Exhibits cover Danish policing, notable criminal cases, and the tools and evidence used in investigations.

It is a niche but memorable museum, especially if you want something smaller and less polished than the major central collections. The setting gives the displays more atmosphere than a standard crime exhibit.
