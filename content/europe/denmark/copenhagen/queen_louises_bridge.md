Queen Louise's Bridge links central Copenhagen with Nørrebro and is one of the city's busiest cycling crossings. Wide bike lanes, a live bicycle counter, and steady traffic make it a good place to see everyday Copenhagen cycling culture in action.

The bridge also has fine views over the lakes. It is best visited as part of a walk between Torvehallerne, the Botanical Garden, and the Nørrebro lakes.
