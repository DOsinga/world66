The Statens Museum for Kunst in the Østerport area holds the national art collection — paintings by Danish Golden Age masters alongside Matisse, Picasso, and Rubens. The building connects a 19th-century palace with a substantial modern extension, and the junction between them works well. Free on Sundays, which is a good day to go: the park outside (Østre Anlæg) is full of Copenhageners, and the museum café does a reasonable brunch.
