Spejdermuseet is a small museum about the Scout movement in Denmark, set in an old arsenal building in Christianshavn. The displays cover the movement's early years from 1905 onward, with models, uniforms, posters, equipment, and camp material.

It is a niche stop, but a worthwhile one for visitors interested in social history, youth movements, or the quieter museums tucked into Copenhagen's older quarters.
