Vesterbro has the most concentrated bar scene in Copenhagen, centred on the Meatpacking District but extending through the surrounding streets. The bars range from natural wine bars (Pompette, Granola) to classic cocktail bars (Strøm) to neighbourhood beers-and-no-nonsense places. The area around Halmtorvet, just south of the Meatpacking District, has a cluster of bars that fill up on weekend evenings.

What distinguishes the Vesterbro bar scene from other Copenhagen neighbourhoods is that it actually fills up — the area has enough density of bars and restaurants that the streets become lively rather than just individual venues being busy. Go Thursday through Saturday for the best atmosphere.
