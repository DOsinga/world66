Istedgade and the streets running off it have the best cheap and mid-range ethnic food in Copenhagen. Pakistani, Turkish, Lebanese, and Vietnamese restaurants cluster in the blocks west of Central Station — the legacy of the immigrant communities that settled here when the neighbourhood was still working-class. Prices are significantly lower than the new restaurants in the Meatpacking District and quality is often higher.

Nørreport Street's Shawarma spots are the most popular for late-night eating after bars close, but exploring the side streets — particularly Absalonsgade and Saxogade — reveals more interesting options. This is where people who work in Copenhagen's restaurant industry tend to eat on their days off.
