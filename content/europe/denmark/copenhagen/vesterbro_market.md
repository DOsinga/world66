The Saturday food market on Enghave Plads in Vesterbro brings together small producers selling bread, cheese, vegetables, smoked fish, and prepared food. The market is smaller than Torvehallerne but has a strong neighbourhood feel — many stalls are regulars who sell to the same customers each week, and prices are more accessible than the covered market near the Latin Quarter.

A good place for breakfast or lunch on a Saturday morning, eaten from paper bags on the square's benches. The market runs from around 9am until early afternoon.
