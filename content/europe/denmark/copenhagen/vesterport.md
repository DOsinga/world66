One of the small bottle shops and informal bars in the Meatpacking District area that has become a neighbourhood institution. The combination of natural wine retail and informal bar service — bottles opened to drink on-site at retail plus a small corkage — represents the Copenhagen model of low-key wine culture that has spread across the city. Good selection of small producers, knowledgeable staff, and no attitude.

The space is minimal and the atmosphere is entirely determined by whoever is there. On quiet evenings it can be a very good conversation and a very good bottle of wine; on busy nights it can feel crowded. Walk past and see how it looks.
