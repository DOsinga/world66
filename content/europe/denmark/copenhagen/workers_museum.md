type: poi

The Workers' Museum covers Danish everyday and working-class life from the last 150 years. Exhibits use household objects, workplace material, political history, and art to show how industrial workers lived, organized, and spent their free time.

It is a useful counterpoint to Copenhagen's royal and design museums, and it is close enough to Torvehallerne, the Botanical Garden, and Rosenborg Castle to fit into a central Copenhagen walk.
