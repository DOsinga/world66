Ebeltoft is one of Denmark's prettiest small harbour towns, tucked into the Djursland peninsula east of [Aarhus](/europe/denmark/aarhus). Its old centre is all crooked cobbles, half-timbered houses, hollyhocks, and low merchant buildings, with the sea never far away.

The town has more substance than the postcard first suggests. The [Fregatten Jylland](/europe/denmark/ebeltoft/fregatten_jylland) is a full-rigged 19th-century warship hauled up beside the harbour, and the [Glass Museum](/europe/denmark/ebeltoft/glass_museum_ebeltoft) gives Ebeltoft a clear contemporary craft identity. The [Old Town Hall](/europe/denmark/ebeltoft/old_town_hall) anchors the historic core, while the beaches and Mols Bjerge hills make it easy to stretch a visit beyond the lanes.

Ebeltoft works best as a slow day from Aarhus or as a base for Djursland. Come for the old town in the morning, spend a few hours with the frigate and glass, and leave time for the coast before heading back.
