The Glass Museum gives Ebeltoft a sharper edge than many pretty Danish towns. Its collection focuses on contemporary glass from Denmark and abroad, with work that ranges from elegant vessels to strange sculptural pieces that use glass more like light than material.

It sits close to the harbour, so it pairs naturally with Fregatten Jylland. Allow an hour, longer if the temporary exhibition is strong.
