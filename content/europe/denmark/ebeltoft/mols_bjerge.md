Mols Bjerge is the hilly heart of Mols Bjerge National Park, a landscape of open heath, pasture, woodland, and glacial ridges west of Ebeltoft. The area was designated a national park in 2008 and is one of eastern Jutland's best places for walking and wide views.

Trails cross the hills and connect small villages, old lanes, and coastal viewpoints. It is worth setting aside a half day if you want nature alongside Ebeltoft's old town and maritime sights.
