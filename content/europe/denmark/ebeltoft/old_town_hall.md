Ebeltoft's Old Town Hall is a tiny half-timbered building on the old market square, almost toy-like compared with the civic buildings of larger towns. It is the natural centre of a walk through the old streets, where the scale of the town is the real attraction.

The building is often photographed as the symbol of Ebeltoft, but the better reason to come is the setting: cobbled lanes, crooked houses, small shops, and glimpses down toward the harbour.
