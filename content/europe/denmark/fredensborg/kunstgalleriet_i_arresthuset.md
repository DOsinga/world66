Kunstgalleriet i Arresthuset puts changing art exhibitions inside Fredensborg's former courthouse and jailhouse from 1857. The building still keeps much of its old character, so the pleasure is partly the art and partly the unusual setting, with cells and historic details folded into a small gallery visit.
