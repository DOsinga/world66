The Palace Church forms part of the Fredensborg complex and gives the palace a more complete courtly shape than the facade alone suggests. It is a restrained Lutheran chapel rather than a dazzling royal showpiece, but its position beside the palace makes it an essential stop on the garden walk.

Access depends on royal use, services, and tour arrangements, so do not plan a visit around getting inside. From the outside, it helps explain how Fredensborg functioned as a seasonal residence: court, church, garden, and lake all gathered into one small royal world.
