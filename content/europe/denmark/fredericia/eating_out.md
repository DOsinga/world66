---
title: "Eating Out"
type: section
---

Fredericia has a decent if unflashy restaurant scene clustered along Danmarksgade and Gothersgade in the old town. The local specialities are nothing exotic — pickled herring, smørrebrød, pork tenderloin — but the produce is fresh and the standards are high.

**Restaurant Smag** is the most ambitious place in town, serving a modern Danish set menu with a focus on Jutland seafood and game. **Café Vandtaarnet** in the old water tower has a popular brunch menu and good views. For a more casual lunch, **Det Lille Apotek** on the main square does classic open sandwiches and beer. The Saturday market on **Axeltorv** is the place to pick up smoked sausage, cheese and fresh-baked rye bread for a picnic on the ramparts.
