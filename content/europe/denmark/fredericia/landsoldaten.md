The bronze infantryman on the green in front of the Prince's Gate is one of the most famous statues in Denmark. Cast in 1858, the **Land Soldier** commemorates the Danish foot soldiers who broke out of Fredericia's gates on 6 July 1849 and routed a much larger Schleswig-Holstein force besieging the town.

The figure is unusual in being defiantly modest: a private soldier with his foot resting on a captured cannon, helmet pushed back, beard trimmed but not styled. He stands looking outward across the field where the battle was fought. Quick visit, but the centrepiece of the old town.
