The Earth Spirit (*Jordånden*) is a pair of oversized green-painted concrete sculptures by the waterfront in northern Fredericia. One element is an enormous open hand reaching skyward as if pleading; the other, a few steps away, is an equally enormous bare backside also rising from the ground.

The whole thing is meant as a piece of environmental commentary — protect the planet, or it will moon you — but it works just as well as a piece of slightly silly outdoor art. Easy walk or short bike ride from the centre, free, and worth fifteen minutes on a sunny afternoon.
