Møstings Hus is a small exhibition house beside Frederiksberg Gardens and its pond. The building is modest, but the setting is charming and the rotating art shows make it an easy cultural stop while walking through Frederiksberg.

It is best treated as a short add-on rather than a destination for a whole afternoon.
