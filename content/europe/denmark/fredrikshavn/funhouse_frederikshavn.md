FunHouse Frederikshavn is an indoor play and activity centre aimed at families travelling with children. In a ferry and beach town where weather can turn quickly, that practical usefulness matters more than architectural grandeur.

It is a straightforward family stop: climbing, play areas, and a place for children to burn energy between coastal outings. Adults without kids can skip it, but for family travel it clears the threshold as a genuinely useful Frederikshavn POI.
