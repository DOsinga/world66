---
title: Bars and Cafes
type: section
---

Gentofte's cafe culture is low-key and residential. Hellerup has the most options for a coffee or a glass of wine; Charlottenlund has a few places near the beach for a post-swim drink. Bakken itself has several old-fashioned beer halls and snack stalls that are part of the park's character — rough and cheerful in a way that contrasts entirely with the neighbourhood around it.
