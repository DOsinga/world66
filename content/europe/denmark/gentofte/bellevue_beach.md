Bellevue Beach is one of the best-known beaches north of Copenhagen, with a wide strip of sand, grassy areas, and easy access from the coast railway. On hot days it fills quickly with swimmers, volleyball players, and families.

The beach is practical rather than wild: toilets, kiosks, and nearby transport make it an easy half-day escape from the city. Go outside peak afternoon hours if you want space.
