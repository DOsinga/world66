Bellevue Beach is the classic Copenhagen-area beach: broad sand, lawns behind it, bathing piers, ice cream, and easy access from the northern suburbs. On a hot weekend it can feel like half the city has had the same idea, but that is part of the scene.

Come for a swim, beach volleyball, or a lazy afternoon after visiting Dyrehaven or Bakken. It is not secluded, but it is one of the easiest places near Copenhagen to turn a summer day into a proper beach day.
