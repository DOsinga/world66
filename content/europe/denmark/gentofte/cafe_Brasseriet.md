Brasseriet is a straightforward brasserie-style restaurant in Charlottenlund serving Danish and French-influenced dishes to a largely local crowd. The format is familiar — steaks, fish, seasonal specials, a reasonable wine list — and executed with the consistency that comes from feeding the same neighbourhood for years rather than chasing visitors. Lunch is a simpler affair of smørrebrød and salads; dinner is the more serious offering.

The room is comfortable and unhurried, which suits the pace of the area. There is no pressure to leave quickly, and the service reflects the expectation that tables are for the evening. A short walk from Charlottenlund S-tog station.
