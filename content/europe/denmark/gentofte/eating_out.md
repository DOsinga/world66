---
title: Eating Out
type: section
---

Dining in Gentofte skews toward settled, well-heeled local restaurants rather than tourist traps. The best options cluster near Charlottenlund and in Hellerup, where a handful of serious kitchens serve the prosperous residents who live here full-time. Bakken has its own canteen-style food stalls, best treated as park food rather than a dining destination.
