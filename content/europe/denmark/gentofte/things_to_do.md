title: Things to Do
type: section
---

Gentofte's sights cluster in three areas: Klampenborg in the north (Bakken and Dyrehaven), Charlottenlund on the coast (palace, beach, Ordrupgaard), and the inland municipal centre around Bernstorff Park and Gentofte Lake. All are easy to reach by S-tog or by bike along the coastal cycle path.
