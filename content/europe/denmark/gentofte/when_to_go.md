---
title: When to Go
type: section
---

Gentofte is at its best from May to September. The beach at Charlottenlund is swimmable from June through August, and Dyrehaven's beech forest goes through a spectacular greening in May that draws visitors from Copenhagen. Bakken amusement park opens in late March and closes in late August — it does not operate year-round.

July is high season: the coastal path is busy, the beach is packed on warm days, and Ordrupgaard's summer visiting hours extend into the evening. If you prefer calm, visit in late May or early September when the weather holds but the crowds thin. The park grounds around Bernstorff Palace and Dyrehaven are pleasant in autumn colours through October.

Winter is quiet and functional. Ordrupgaard stays open year-round; the deer park is accessible any time but wild and beautiful on a frosty morning. The municipality does not put on significant Christmas markets or winter events — Copenhagen proper is the place for that.
