---
title: "Getting Around"
type: section
---

Haderslev is a compact town and the historic centre is best explored on foot. The main sights — the cathedral, the old market square, and the lake shore — are all within comfortable walking distance of each other and of the railway station.

The Haderslev Dam lake has a well-maintained path running along much of its length, making it easy to walk or cycle between the town centre and the quieter eastern end of the lake. Bicycles can be hired locally and cycle routes extend into the surrounding countryside.

Local bus services connect the town centre with outlying suburbs and with Vojens, which has better rail connections than Haderslev itself. For destinations further afield in South Jutland, a car or the regional bus network is the most practical option.
