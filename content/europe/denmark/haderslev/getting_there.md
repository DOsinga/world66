---
title: "Getting There"
type: section
---

Haderslev sits on the E45 motorway corridor that runs from Copenhagen through Jutland to the German border at Flensburg. By car from Copenhagen it is about two and a half hours; from the border crossing it is roughly 50 kilometres and 40 minutes north.

By train, Haderslev is served by regional services running between Odense and Padborg (the German border station). The journey from Odense takes around an hour and a quarter; from Aarhus around an hour. Change at Vojens for the short branch line into Haderslev itself, or at Kolding if travelling from the north. The station sits a short walk from the old town centre.

There is no commercial airport in Haderslev. The closest airports are Billund (about 60 km north), which has international connections, and Copenhagen Airport for longer-haul flights.
