StreetDome is a modern skate and street-sports facility by Haderslev's harbour, designed as both an activity space and a piece of contemporary urban architecture. Its ramps, bowls, and outdoor areas give the waterfront a youthful, practical edge.

It is most relevant if you skate, travel with active children, or enjoy seeing how smaller Danish towns reuse harbour areas. Even as a quick look, it pairs well with a walk along the water.
