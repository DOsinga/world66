---
title: "Slesvigske Vognsamling"
type: poi
---

The Slesvigske Vognsamling — the Schleswig Carriage Collection — is a specialist museum dedicated to horse-drawn vehicles, housed in a historic coach house in Haderslev. The collection covers the full range of carriages, wagons, and sleighs used in Schleswig-Holstein from the 18th century to the early 20th century, from elegant private landaus and broughams to working farm carts and tradesmen's vehicles. It is one of the more complete collections of its kind in Scandinavia.

The vehicles are displayed with their associated tack, livery, and equipment, giving a sense of the social world they inhabited. The grander carriages speak to the prosperous merchant and aristocratic culture of the Duchy of Schleswig; the working vehicles tell a different story of rural life in the flat Jutland countryside. The period covered by the collection — roughly 1750 to 1920 — spans the entire era of German administration and reunification with Denmark, which gives the objects an added layer of historical interest.

The museum is a short walk from the town centre and can be combined easily with a visit to the cathedral and a walk along the lake. Opening hours are seasonal; check locally before visiting.
