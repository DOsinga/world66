Flynderupgård Museet is a country-house museum near Helsingor, set in an 1830s building with a recreated 1920s rose garden. Exhibits cover local rural life, an old grocery store, farming, and fishing.

The surrounding fields are worked with period tools and animals, making it especially good for families and anyone interested in everyday history outside the castle circuit.
