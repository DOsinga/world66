Helsingør Domkirke, also known as St Olaf's Church, rises above the old centre in red brick. The church dates to the 16th century, with roots going back to the medieval town, and its interior is known for a large 17th-century altarpiece, an ornate pulpit, and carved fittings that reward a short look inside.
