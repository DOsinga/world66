Helsingør Værftsmuseum is a small shipyard museum in the old administration offices by the harbour. Former yard workers helped build the exhibition, so the focus is practical and local: tools, craft, photographs, and the story of shipbuilding in a town better known to visitors for Kronborg.

It pairs naturally with the nearby maritime museum and Culture Yard.
