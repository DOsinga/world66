Oresund Aquarium is a small salt-water aquarium focused on the marine life of the sound between Denmark and Sweden. It is run with an educational bent, with tanks, touch-friendly activities, and feeding sessions that make local sea creatures easier to understand.

It is not a large destination aquarium, but it is a good family stop in Helsingor, especially for children who want something hands-on between the castle, harbour, and old town.
