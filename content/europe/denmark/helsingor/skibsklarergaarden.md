Skibsklarergaarden is a preserved merchant house by Helsingor's harbour. It was used for handling Oresund toll paperwork and supplying foreign ships before they sailed on.

The building dates mainly from 1780, with much of the interior still intact. It gives a good feel for the working harbour town behind Kronborg's grander story.
