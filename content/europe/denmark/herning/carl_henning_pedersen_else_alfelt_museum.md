This artist museum is built around the work of Carl-Henning Pedersen and Else Alfelt, two major Danish modernists whose bright, mythic, and often dreamlike images give the Birk art district much of its colour. The collection is large, focused, and easier to grasp than many contemporary art museums.

It sits close to HEART, so the two museums make sense as a pair. If you only have time for one, choose based on taste: HEART for changing contemporary exhibitions and architecture, this museum for a more concentrated encounter with two artists.
