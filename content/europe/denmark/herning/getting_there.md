---
title: Getting There
type: section
---

Herning has rail links across central Jutland, with Herning Station close to the town centre and Herning Messecenter station serving the MCH venues south of town. Trains are useful if you are coming from Aarhus, Vejle, Silkeborg, or Holstebro, though connections may require a change.

By car, Herning is straightforward and well connected to the Jutland road network. The MCH venues are designed for large event traffic and have extensive parking, but check event-day instructions if you are arriving for a major concert or match.
