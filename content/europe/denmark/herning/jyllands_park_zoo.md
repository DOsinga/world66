Jyllands Park Zoo is a family-oriented zoo southwest of Herning, with animals from several continents, including giraffes, monkeys, ostriches, and seals. It is built for a relaxed half-day visit rather than a formal big-city zoo experience, with feeding sessions and child-friendly spaces carrying much of the appeal.

The zoo is easiest by car from Herning, and is most useful for families looking for an outdoor stop in central Jutland.
