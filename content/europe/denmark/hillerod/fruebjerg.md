Fruebjerg is a 65-metre hill in the forest north of Hillerod, high enough by Danish standards to give broad views over North Zealand. It is also linked to the large Fruebjerg meetings of the late 19th and early 20th centuries, part of the wider folk high school movement inspired by Grundtvig.

It is a modest sight, but a pleasant one if you are walking or cycling through the forests around Hillerod.
