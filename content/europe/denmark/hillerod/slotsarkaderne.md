Slotsarkaderne ("Castle Arcades") is the main indoor shopping centre in Hillerod — a covered three-storey mall with a typical Danish mix of national chains, cafes, a supermarket and a food hall. Useful if it is raining or you need to kill an hour between trains; not exactly atmospheric. The Hillerod station is a five-minute walk away.
