---
title: When to Go
type: section
---

Hillerod is rewarding year-round but at its absolute best in spring and early summer, when the baroque gardens at Frederiksborg are in flower and the long Danish evenings let you spend a full afternoon outside the castle. July and August bring most of the international visitors and the castle interior is at its busiest.

In December a small Christmas market takes over the central market square, with mulled wine, roasted almonds and the castle illuminated behind it. The lake occasionally freezes hard enough for skating in a cold January — when it does, the view from the ice across to the castle towers is unforgettable.
