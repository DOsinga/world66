---
title: "Eating Out"
type: section
---

Horsens has a solid restaurant scene for its size, centred on the pedestrian streets of the old town and the harbour area. Danish and Nordic cooking predominates, with several good fish restaurants along the waterfront. The town also has the usual complement of Italian, Asian, and fast-casual options. During the Medieval Festival, the prison grounds and surrounding streets fill with food stalls serving game, mead, and period-inspired food.
