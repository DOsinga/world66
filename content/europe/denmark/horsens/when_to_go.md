---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

The best reason to time a visit specifically to Horsens is the European Medieval Festival in late August — book accommodation and plan the trip around it if at all possible. Outside that weekend, summer (June–August) is when the fjord and outdoor activities are at their best, with long daylight hours and reliable enough weather for cycling and kayaking.

The rest of the year, Horsens is a pleasant but undemanding stop. The prison museum and industrial museum are open year-round. The concert calendar at Fængslet and Forum Horsens is busy from September through May.
