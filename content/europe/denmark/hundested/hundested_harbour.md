Hundested Harbour is still a proper port, with fishing boats, a ferry link to Rørvig, and enough industrial edges to keep it from feeling staged. Around that working core, the town has built one of North Zealand's better small harbour districts.

Come for a slow wander along the quays. There are glass and craft workshops, casual places to eat fish, seasonal events, and good views across the mouth of the fjord. It is at its best in warm weather, but even off-season the harbour gives Hundested its shape.
