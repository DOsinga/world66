Kikhavn is a small old fishing hamlet near Hundested, known for its half-timbered houses and quiet coastal atmosphere. It is best treated as a short heritage walk rather than a full destination.

Come for a slow wander through the lanes and a look at one of the older settlements on this part of the north Zealand coast.
