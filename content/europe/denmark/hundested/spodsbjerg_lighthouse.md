Spodsbjerg Lighthouse stands on the moraine cliff east of Hundested, close to Knud Rasmussen's House. The lighthouse itself is small and normally seen only from the outside, but the walk is the point: sea, wind, grass, and open views along the coast.

Pair it with the Rasmussen museum and the memorial cairn nearby. Together they make the best short walk in Hundested, especially in clear weather when the Kattegat horizon feels wide and northern.
