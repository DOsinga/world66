The Former Bishop's Home is a late medieval building in Kalundborg's old high town, built in the 15th century for the Bishop of Roskilde. It later served civic uses, including a period as town hall, and its long history makes it a useful stop when tracing the streets around Church of Our Lady.

It is best treated as part of a short architectural walk through the old centre rather than as a standalone attraction.
