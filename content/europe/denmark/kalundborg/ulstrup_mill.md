Ulstrup Mill is a restored Dutch gallery mill west of Kalundborg. Built in 1894 after an earlier mill burned down, it kept working until 1956. Its manually turned cap and tall timber form make it a worthwhile rural heritage stop if you are exploring the Røsnæs peninsula or the countryside around Kalundborg.
