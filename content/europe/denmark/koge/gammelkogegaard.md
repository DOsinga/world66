Gammelkjøgegaard is a historic manor estate on the edge of Køge, valued as much for its parkland and atmosphere as for the buildings themselves. Old trees, water, and estate paths give it a slower rhythm than the town centre.

Access can depend on events and opening arrangements, so check before planning around it. Even a limited visit adds a green, aristocratic layer to Køge's otherwise compact harbour-town character.
