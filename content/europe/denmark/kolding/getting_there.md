---
title: "Getting There"
type: section
---

Kolding is on the main rail line between Copenhagen and Aarhus (Jutland's largest city), and InterCity trains stop here regularly. Copenhagen is about 2 hours away by train; Aarhus is just over an hour. This position on the main Jutland corridor makes Kolding a natural stop rather than a detour.

By car, Kolding sits at the junction of the E45 (Jutland's main north-south motorway) and the E20 (which runs east to the Little Belt Bridge and west toward Esbjerg). The motorway network makes it easy to reach from both [Legoland in Billund](/europe/denmark/billund) (40 minutes northwest) and the Jutland west coast (around an hour).
