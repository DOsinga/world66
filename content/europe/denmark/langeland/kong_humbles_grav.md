Kong Humbles Grav is a roughly 4,000-year-old long barrow on Langeland. Local tradition links it with King Humble, a legendary Danish king mentioned in Saxo Grammaticus' medieval chronicle.

The site is simple, but it is a worthwhile prehistoric stop for visitors tracing Langeland's burial mounds and older landscapes.
