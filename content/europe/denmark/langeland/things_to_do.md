---
title: Things to Do
type: section
---

Langeland's countryside holds most of the island's best experiences. Tranekær Castle and the TICKON land art park in its grounds are in the north; Ristinge Klint's dramatic chalk cliffs are in the southwest; Stoense Skov offers quiet coastal walks near Lohals. Langelandsfort near Bagenkop is one of the Baltic's best-preserved Cold War sites.
