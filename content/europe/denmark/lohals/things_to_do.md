---
title: 'Things to Do'
type: section
---

Lohals is small enough that an afternoon walk covers most of it. The harbour, the beach to the north of the village, and the woodland of Stoense Skov immediately south are the main natural draws. The ferry to Spodsbjerg on Funen leaves from the harbour and is a pleasant short trip in itself.

For anything more substantial, head south down the length of Langeland — about 50 km from end to end — where the island opens out into a mix of farmland, old manor estates, and quiet beaches. Rudkøbing, halfway down, is the island's only proper town.
