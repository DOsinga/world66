Fuglsang Art Museum occupies a 19th-century manor house near Nysted in eastern Lolland, extended by a purpose-built contemporary wing designed by Tony Fretton Architects. The collection focuses on Danish art from the Golden Age to the present day, with particular strength in works associated with the Lolland-Falster region. The setting — manor house, gardens, and coastal landscape — is as much a draw as the art inside.
