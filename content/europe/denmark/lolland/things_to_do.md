---
title: Things to Do
type: section
---

Away from the towns, Lolland rewards unhurried exploration. Nakskov Fjord offers a boat trip to the remote Albuen lighthouse spit. In the east, Aalholm Slot and Fuglsang Art Museum make a good pairing near Nysted. The three manor houses around Sakskøbing — Berritzgaard, Krenkerup, and Ørebygaard — are worth a look for their grounds and architecture.
