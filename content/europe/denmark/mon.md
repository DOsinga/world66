Møn is a small island just south of Zealand, the island [Copenhagen](/europe/denmark/copenhagen) sits on. With superb beaches, gently rolling hills, and idyllic pastoral scenery, it is a perfect escape from the capital — slow-paced and thoroughly relaxing.

The main sight is [Møns Klint](/europe/denmark/mon/mons_klint), a dramatic stretch of chalk cliffs dropping 128 metres into the sea. The cliffs are around 70 million years old and have been a major attraction for Danes and visitors alike for centuries. The nearby Klinteskoven woodland is good for hiking, picnics, and riding, and the romantic landscape park at [Liselund](/europe/denmark/mon/liselund_park) sits a short walk from the cliff edge.

The main town is [Stege](/europe/denmark/mon/stege), a pleasant place to spend an hour browsing the shops on Storegade and sitting in one of its cafes. The 13th-century Stege Kirke has some quirky ceiling frescoes, and the [Møllerporten](/europe/denmark/mon/mollerporten) is one of the best-preserved medieval town gates in Denmark. Several village churches — [Elmelunde](/europe/denmark/mon/elmelunde_church), [Keldby](/europe/denmark/mon/keldby_church) and [Fanefjord](/europe/denmark/mon/fanefjord_church) — hide vivid late-medieval frescoes by an anonymous painter known as the Elmelunde Master.

North of Stege, the [Ulvshale strand](/europe/denmark/mon/ulvshale_beach) is a fine beach with a wild forest nearby; from there a causeway leads onto [Nyord](/europe/denmark/mon/nyord), a tiny island whose marshes are among the best birdwatching sites in Denmark. The Neolithic long barrow at [Grønjægers Høj](/europe/denmark/mon/gronjaegers_hoj) is one of Denmark's most impressive prehistoric monuments.
