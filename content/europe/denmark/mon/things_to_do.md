---
title: Things to Do
type: section
---

Møn's draw is its scenery: the chalk cliffs of Møns Klint, the long beaches at Ulvshale and Råbylille, and the medieval frescoes hidden inside its village churches. The island is small enough to cover in two or three days but rewards a slower pace — a morning walk on the cliffs, an afternoon at a beach, an evening in Stege.
