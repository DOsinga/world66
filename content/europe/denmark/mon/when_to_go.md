---
title: When to Go
type: section
---

The honest answer is June and August. Summer days on Møn are long, the sea is just about warm enough to swim in, and the orchids in Klinteskoven are out. July is busiest — Danish school holidays — and the beaches and Klint car park fill up by mid-morning; aim for early arrivals or an out-of-the-way trail. September has the second-best weather and far fewer people: beech leaves start to turn against the white cliffs, which is a sight in itself.

Winter is genuinely quiet. The Klint is open year-round and dramatic in low sun, but most cafes, smokehouses and the GeoCenter operate on reduced hours or close completely from November to March. Late April through May is reliable for walking and birdwatching without the crowds.
