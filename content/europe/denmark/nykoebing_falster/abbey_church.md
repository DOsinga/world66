The Abbey Church is a late Gothic church from the 15th century and one of the main historic buildings in Nykøbing Falster. Inside, look for the Mecklenburg Ancestral Table, a 17th-century painted family tree tied to the town's ducal history.

It is a modest but worthwhile stop when exploring the centre, especially if you are interested in the religious and courtly history of Falster.
