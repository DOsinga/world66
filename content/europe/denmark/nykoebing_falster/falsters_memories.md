Falster's Memories is Nykobing Falster's local history museum, housed in Czarens Hus, a 17th-century half-timbered building in the town centre. The collection focuses on Falster's past and gives context to a town that many visitors otherwise pass through quickly.

The building is part of the appeal: Czarens Hus is traditionally associated with Peter the Great's stay in Nykobing in 1716. It is a compact stop, best combined with a walk through the old centre and St. Catherine's Church.
