---
title: "Eating Out"
type: section
---

Odense is no Copenhagen, but it has a solid mid-priced food scene focused around Vintapperstræde and the streets behind Brandts. The local touchstones are *smørrebrød* (open rye sandwiches) at lunch and Funen cured ham, the island's gastronomic export. Several restaurants on the edge of the old town have one-Michelin-star ambitions; many more do excellent café-style cooking for a fraction of the price.
