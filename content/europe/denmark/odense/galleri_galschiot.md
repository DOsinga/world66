Galleri Galschiot is sculptor Jens Galschiot's large studio, gallery, and workshop complex on the edge of Odense. The site includes a bronze foundry, sculpture park, gallery shop, art school spaces, and a 400-square-metre exhibition gallery, making it a more hands-on art stop than a conventional museum.
