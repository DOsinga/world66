Odense Å threads south through the city, between the centre and the Funen Village, with a continuous riverside path that runs for nearly 10 km. The middle stretch through Munke Mose is the best — a long, gentle, willow-shaded park where rowing boats can be hired in summer and the river is wide enough for ducks and swans.

The path is the easiest way to walk between the [Hans Christian Andersen Museum](/europe/denmark/odense/hc_andersen_museum) and [The Funen Village](/europe/denmark/odense/thefunenvillage), passing the zoo and several small lakes. In winter, with the cathedral spire visible behind bare trees, it is one of the prettiest walks in any Danish city.
