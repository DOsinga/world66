The Bottle Peter Museum is devoted to one man's improbable life work: Peter Jacobsen, a local mariner who spent four decades building miniature sailing ships inside glass bottles. The collection of over 1,700 ships is housed in the building where he lived and worked, and it is one of the most remarkable collections of folk art in Denmark.

The ships are extraordinarily detailed — some have running rigging as fine as thread, others carry tiny crews. The workshop where Jacobsen did his work is preserved as he left it. The museum is small but utterly absorbing, and represents Ærøskøbing's most distinctive attraction.
