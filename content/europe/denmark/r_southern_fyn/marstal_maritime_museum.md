Marstal, on the eastern end of the island, was a major seafaring town in the age of sail, and its maritime museum reflects that history with unusual depth. The collection covers the period from the late 18th century through the early 20th, including ship models, navigational instruments, charts, and an extensive gallery of marine paintings.

The paintings are the highlight: large canvases showing Marstal ships in storms, in harbours across Europe, and in tropical waters. They were painted for the captains by artists in Antwerp, Rotterdam, and Marseille — records of voyages as much as works of art. The museum also has exhibits on the island's transition to solar and wind energy, an unexpected but satisfying contrast with the sailing era.
