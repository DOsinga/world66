---
title: Getting There
type: section
---

Randers is well-connected by rail. Direct trains run to Aarhus (about 30 minutes) and Aalborg (about 55 minutes), making it easy to visit as a day trip from either city. Trains run frequently throughout the day on the main Jutland spine line. Copenhagen is about 3 hours by direct train.

By road, Randers sits on the E45 motorway (the main north-south route through Jutland), making it easy to include on a road trip. The drive from Aarhus is 35 kilometres north on the E45.

**Randers Airport** exists but handles only private and charter flights — no scheduled commercial services.

For travellers coming from outside Denmark, fly into Aarhus Airport (50 km south) or Billund Airport (100 km southwest), then take the train or drive north to Randers.
