Randers Museum of Art (Randers Kunstmuseum) is housed in a purpose-built building in the town centre and holds a collection spanning Danish art from around 1800 to the contemporary period. The 19th-century Danish Golden Age paintings are a highlight, alongside works from the Skagen school and 20th-century Danish modernism.

The museum runs a changing programme of temporary exhibitions alongside the permanent collection. It is not a destination in itself for most travellers, but for anyone with an interest in Danish art it offers a good afternoon's viewing. Combined with the Kulturhuset across the street, this part of Randers has the feel of an intentional cultural quarter.
