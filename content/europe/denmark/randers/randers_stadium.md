story: Randers FC has a peculiar history of surviving through mergers and financial difficulties to remain in the Danish Superliga, a feat that is disproportionate to the city's size. The club was formed from a 2003 merger of three local clubs, all of which had previously played at higher levels. The city's football tradition goes back to the 1890s — longer than most Danish clubs.
---

Randers FC plays at Cepheus Park, a compact and modern ground south of the city centre. The club competes in the Danish Superliga, the top division of Danish football, and games here offer a good-value alternative to the more expensive and tourist-heavy experiences at Copenhagen or Aarhus clubs.

Attending a Randers FC match is a straightforward affair — the stadium holds around 12,000 and rarely sells out, so tickets are generally available on the day. The surrounding neighbourhood has the usual cluster of fan bars. Check the Randers FC website for the fixture schedule; the Superliga season runs from mid-July to late May with a winter break in December and January.
