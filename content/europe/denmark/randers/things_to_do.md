title: Things to Do
type: section
---

Randers' main attraction is [Randers Regnskov](/europe/denmark/randers/randers_regnskov), the tropical zoo built inside glass domes over the Gudenå river — one of the more unexpected experiences in Jutland and worth a half-day. Beyond that, the town has a pleasant old quarter with [Sankt Mortens Kirke](/europe/denmark/randers/st_martins_church), a significant medieval church where Denmark's Reformation effectively began; the [Randers Museum of Art](/europe/denmark/randers/randers_kunstmuseum), which holds a solid Danish art collection; and the [Kulturhuset](/europe/denmark/randers/kulturhuset), the main venue for concerts and events.

The oddest attraction is [Memphis Mansion](/europe/denmark/randers/memphis_mansion), a full-scale replica of Graceland on the edge of town built by a dedicated local Elvis fan. It is what it sounds like, executed with improbable commitment.

The [Gudenå](/europe/denmark/randers/gudenaa_kayaking), Denmark's longest river, runs through town and is ideal for kayaking downstream toward Randers Fjord.
