---
title: When to Go
type: section
---

Randers has a continental Jutland climate — cold winters, reasonably warm summers, and rain spread fairly evenly through the year. The best months to visit are May to September, when the weather makes walking around the old town and kayaking on the Gudenå genuinely pleasant.

Summer (June to August) is when Randers Regnskov is most popular with Danish families, and prices at accommodation rise accordingly. The contrast between the tropical heat of the domes and the outdoor summer weather is less dramatic than in winter, but the riverfront and fjord are at their best. September is an excellent month: cooler but rarely cold, fewer visitors, and still enough light for outdoor activities.

Winter visits are viable — the Regnskov, museums, and Memphis Mansion are all year-round attractions. The Gudenå sometimes freezes in January and February, which is scenic but rules out kayaking. Check the dates for the Randers Festiwal (mid-August), the city's main music event, if you want or want to avoid a livelier town.
