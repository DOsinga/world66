---
title: "Getting There"
type: section
---

**By train** is the obvious choice. Ribe is on the local Vestbanen line; direct trains run from **Esbjerg** (35 minutes, the nearest main-line hub) and there are good connections through Esbjerg or Bramming from **Copenhagen** (about 4 hours including the change) and **Aarhus** (about 3 hours). The station is a five-minute walk north of the old town.

**By car**, Ribe is just off the E20 motorway via Esbjerg; about 3 hours from Copenhagen via the Storebæltsbroen bridge, 4 hours from Hamburg. There are pay car parks at the edge of the old town; driving in the centre is restricted.

**By air**, the nearest airports are **Billund** (50 minutes by car or bus, well-served by European low-cost carriers) and **Hamburg** (about 2 hours by car).

**By ferry**, the **Esbjerg–Fanø** ferry doesn't help directly, but the **Esbjerg–Harwich** route (now discontinued, alas) used to make Ribe a popular first stop for British visitors arriving in Denmark.
