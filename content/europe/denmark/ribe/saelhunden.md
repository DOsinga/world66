"The Seal," a low half-timbered building right on the Skibbroen quay, is the oldest inn in Ribe still in continuous use and one of the most atmospheric restaurants in Denmark. The bar is wood-panelled and dim, the floors uneven, the ceiling beams so low that tall guests learn to duck. The food is classic Jutland: pickled herring, smoked Wadden Sea shrimp, lammekølle, smørrebrød at lunch, and the inevitable schnapps.

The summer terrace by the river is the prized seat; book ahead. Cash and cards both fine.
