The Wadden Sea Centre introduces the tidal flats, storm surges, bird migration, and coastal landscapes of the Wadden Sea National Park. Exhibits use multimedia and hands-on displays rather than only panels.

It is also a practical base for guided walks into the national park, especially for visitors hoping to understand the landscape before going out onto the flats.
