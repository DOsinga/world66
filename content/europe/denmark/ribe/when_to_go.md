---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

Ribe's tourist season runs from **April to October**, with the peak in **July and August** when Danish families are on holiday and the day-trip coaches from Copenhagen and Hamburg are at their busiest. June and early September are arguably the sweetest months: long daylight, milder weather, fewer crowds.

**Spring (March–April)** and **autumn (September–October)** are the seasons of the **Sort Sol** starling murmurations, the single most impressive natural spectacle on this coast. Many visitors come specifically to time a trip around them.

**Winter (November–February)** is dark, wet and quiet. The cathedral, the museums and the inn restaurants stay open, but most other tourist operations close. Christmas in Ribe is genuinely lovely — a small old-fashioned Christmas market on Torvet, the cathedral lit at night, snow occasional but possible. The town is, if anything, more characterful in winter than in summer.
