---
title: "Getting Around"
type: section
---

Central Rønne is comfortably walkable — almost everything in this guide is within 10 minutes of the harbour. To get out and see the rest of the island, **BAT** runs reliable buses from Rønne to all the main towns (Allinge, Gudhjem, Svaneke, Nexø, Aakirkeby) on a flat fare. Buy a day ticket if you plan more than two trips.

Bornholm is a cyclist's island — there are 235 km of dedicated bike paths, most of them flat and well-signed. Several rental shops sit near the Rønne harbour exit; an electric bike will get you across the island in a couple of hours. A rental car is useful in shoulder seasons when bus frequencies drop.
