---
title: "Getting There"
type: section
---

The standard route is the **Bornholmslinjen ferry from Ystad** in southern Sweden — fast catamarans cross in about 80 minutes and run several times a day. Ystad is reached by direct train from Copenhagen Central, with a combined train-and-ferry through-ticket available from DSB. The whole Copenhagen-to-Rønne journey takes roughly three and a half hours.

A slower overnight ferry runs direct from **Køge** (south of Copenhagen) to Rønne, taking about six hours. **Rønne Airport** is two km south of town with seasonal flights to Copenhagen, Billund and a handful of European cities — DAT runs the year-round service. Ferries arrive at the harbour in central Rønne; the airport is a 10-minute taxi.
