Lakolk is Rømø's best-known beach and one of the widest sandy beaches in Europe. The sand is firm enough for cars in marked areas, which can feel odd at first, but the scale absorbs the traffic better than you would expect. Walk toward the water and the parked cars quickly shrink behind you.

This is the island's classic family beach, with shallow pools left by the tide, space for kite flying, and enough room for windsurfers and beach sailors to keep clear of swimmers. The first weekend in September brings one of Europe's big kite gatherings, when the sky above Lakolk fills with colour.
