The Sea Captain's House, or Kommandørgården, is the island's best cultural stop: an eighteenth-century whaling captain's farm preserved by the National Museum of Denmark. It shows the wealth Rømø's commanders brought home from Arctic whaling, with period interiors, Dutch tiles, farm buildings, and a whale skeleton.

It is a useful corrective to the beach version of Rømø. The island's emptiness today can make it feel like a simple holiday place, but the house tells a sharper story of ships, risk, trade, and families whose fortunes depended on long voyages into cold water.
