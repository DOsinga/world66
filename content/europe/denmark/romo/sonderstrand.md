Sønderstrand is the southern continuation of Rømø's beach world, even broader and more open than Lakolk. This is where the island's wind-powered activities make most sense: blokarts, beach sailing, riding, and long walks where the sea can look kilometres away.

The beach can be up to 3 km wide, depending on tide and weather. That size is the attraction. You do not come here for cosy dunes or a sheltered swim; you come for space, wind, and the slightly unreal feeling of standing on a flat coastal plain that the North Sea has only temporarily given back.
