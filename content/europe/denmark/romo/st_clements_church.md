St Clement's Church is Rømø's old sailors' church, dedicated to the patron saint of seafarers. The oldest parts date from the medieval period, but the building was expanded in the seventeenth and eighteenth centuries, when whaling money gave the island a brief prosperity.

Inside, the details matter more than the architecture: votive ships, low ceilings, name plates on pews, and the practical intimacy of a small island church. It is worth pairing with the Sea Captain's House, because together they show how deeply the sea shaped Rømø's identity.
