---
title: Bars and Cafes
type: section
---

Roskilde has a compact but good café scene concentrated around Stændertorvet and the pedestrian street running from the station toward the cathedral. Most places cater to shoppers and day-trippers, with the emphasis on coffee, open sandwiches, and Danish pastries. A few bars stay open into the evening, though the nightlife is modest — this is a day-trip town rather than a late-night destination.
