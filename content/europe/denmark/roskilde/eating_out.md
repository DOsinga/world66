---
title: Eating Out
type: section
---

Roskilde's restaurant scene is small but decent for a day-trip town. The options cluster around the town square and the cathedral end of the pedestrian street. Most places cater to day-trippers and do straightforward Danish lunch — smørrebrød (open sandwiches), herring plates, and the occasional more ambitious kitchen. The Viking Ship Museum's harbour café is good enough for lunch if you don't want to walk back into town.
