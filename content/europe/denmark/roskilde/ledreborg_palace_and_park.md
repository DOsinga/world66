Ledreborg Palace and Park lies west of Roskilde in the Lejre countryside, giving the cathedral city a refined estate excursion. The palace is privately owned, but the grounds and events make the wider estate a worthwhile stop when access is available.

The appeal is partly architectural and partly landscape: avenues, parkland, and the softer rural mood beyond Roskilde’s historic centre. Check current opening arrangements before setting out, since access can vary by season and event.
