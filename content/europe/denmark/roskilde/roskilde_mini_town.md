Roskilde Mini Town is a large scale model showing how Roskilde may have looked around 1400, when the medieval town was at its height. The model is detailed enough to make the old street plan and church-dominated skyline easy to read.

It is a small stop rather than a major museum, but it pairs well with the Viking Ship Museum and the cathedral area if you are tracing Roskilde's history.
