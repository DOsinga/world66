---
title: When to Go
type: section
---

Roskilde works well year-round as a day trip from Copenhagen, but the peak months are June and July when the Viking Ship Museum's boat trips are running and the Roskilde Festival draws enormous crowds in late June. If you want the town quiet, come in spring or autumn — the cathedral and Viking museum are open all year and the light in September is excellent on the fjord.

Winter is cold and grey, but the Christmas market on the town square in December is genuinely atmospheric, and the cathedral is particularly impressive when it's half-empty.
