Langelands Museum covers the island's history from prehistoric times to the 20th century. It documents Langeland's surprisingly significant maritime past — in 1898 Rudkøbing had 152 ships registered here — and includes archaeological finds from the surrounding farms and coastline. The museum also administers the H.C. Ørsted Exhibition nearby. The collection is compact and well-curated for a small island museum.
