title: Things to Do
type: section
---

Rungstedlund is essentially a single destination: the [Karen Blixen Museum](/europe/denmark/rungstedlund/karen_blixen_museum), which occupies the estate where the author lived and is buried. After the museum, the [Rungsted Bird Sanctuary](/europe/denmark/rungstedlund/rungsted_bird_sanctuary) — the coastal park that Blixen donated as a nature reserve — makes a pleasant walk along the Øresund shore. Nearby Sophienberg, a former royal summer palace just to the north, is worth a look if you have time before catching the train back to Copenhagen.
