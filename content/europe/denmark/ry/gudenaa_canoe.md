The Gudenå river runs right through Ry and connects the lakes of Søhøjlandet in a continuous paddling route. Canoe hire is available in town, and most operators offer multi-day packages with camping equipment and shuttle services to return you to your starting point. The standard route through the lake system takes two to three days at a relaxed pace and passes through quiet forested stretches, open lakes, and the occasional small town.

The river is also suitable for kayaking, and stands-up paddle boards are available in summer. The current is gentle and the route is well-marked, making it accessible to families and beginners. Wild camping is permitted at designated spots along the route, and the lakeside is largely undeveloped — you will see herons, ospreys, and in early summer, nesting grebes.
