---
title: When to Go
type: section
---

Summer (June to August) is peak season when the lakes are warm enough for swimming, canoes are on the water, and the paddle steamer to Himmelbjerget runs daily. The days are long — light until well past 10pm at midsummer — which makes evening walks and lakeside dinners genuinely pleasant. The area gets busy with Danish families in July.

Autumn (September and October) is arguably the best time for walkers. The beech forest turns gold and russet, the crowds thin out, and the light on the lakes in the morning is exceptional. Winter is quiet; cross-country skiing is possible in good snow years, but cannot be relied upon. Spring brings migrating birds to the lakes.
