Museum Samsø is an island museum based in a restored half-timbered farm in Tranebjerg, with exhibits on local history, farming life, and Samsø's prehistoric past. Other museum sites around the island include mills, historic farm buildings, an old post office, and a blacksmith shop.

It is a good stop for understanding the island beyond beaches and cycling routes. Summer brings demonstrations and family-friendly activities such as weaving, spinning, and folk music.
