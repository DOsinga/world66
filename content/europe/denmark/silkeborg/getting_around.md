---
title: Getting Around
type: section
---

The town centre is compact and walkable. The museum, the Hjejlen harbour, and the main shops are all within easy walking distance of the railway station.

Bicycles are the ideal way to explore the lake district. Rental is available in town, and the network of signed cycling routes around the lakes and through the forests is extensive and well-maintained. A day's cycling can take in multiple lakes, the Himmelbjerget, and substantial stretches of forest.

For reaching AQUA and the further lakeshores, a bicycle or car is more practical than walking.
