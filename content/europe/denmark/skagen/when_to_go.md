---
title: When to Go
type: section
---

Skagen is at its best in June and July, when the days are long — at midsummer, the sun barely sets, and the famous Skagen light lasts until 11pm. This is also peak season: the harbour fills with pleasure boats, the museums are busy, and prices are high. Weekends in July in particular can feel crowded.

May and early June are a good alternative — the light is already excellent, the weather is mild, and the crowds haven't fully arrived. September is quieter still and often warmer than its reputation suggests.

Winter in Skagen is stark, with short days and frequent storms off the North Sea. Most tourist facilities close or run reduced hours. But if you want to see the clashing seas at Grenen in a full gale, or have the Sand-Covered Church to yourself, this is the time to come.
