Ungru Manor is a romantic ruin outside Haapsalu, its unfinished walls and empty windows standing in a quiet Baltic landscape. The building looks older than it is, with a deliberately grand, castle-like style that now feels more melancholy than aristocratic.

It is a short detour rather than a long visit, but a memorable one if you like ruins and odd architectural stories. The manor pairs well with Haapsalu's railway station, castle, and old wooden resort streets for a day of faded grandeur.
