Hiiumaa Military Museum tells the island's twentieth-century story through coastal defence, border control, and Soviet-era hardware. The collection includes artillery, communications gear, ammunition, and a reconstructed barracks, with enough hands-on detail to make the history feel close rather than abstract.

The setting helps. Hiiumaa's quiet woods and beaches make the military remains feel slightly unreal, but the museum is a useful reminder that this Baltic island once sat on a tense frontier.
