Kaina Church Ruins are the remains of what was once Hiiumaa's largest church, built around the turn of the 16th century and destroyed during the Second World War. The surviving walls, tombstones, and carved sun cross above the entrance give the site a quiet, weathered dignity.

It is a short stop rather than a major attraction, but it fits Hiiumaa's slow rhythm. Come while driving across the island and take a few minutes to look at the stonework and the open sky where the roof used to be.
