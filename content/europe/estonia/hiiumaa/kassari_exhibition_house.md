The Kassari Exhibition House is the Hiiumaa Museum's branch on Kassari, with displays on island life, maritime history, agriculture, and the manor era. The collection moves from Tsarist and Estonian material into the Soviet period, giving a compact view of how Hiiumaa changed over the last two centuries.

Some of the most memorable objects are outside, including a lifeboat from the MS Estonia, an old school flagpole, and farm and manor equipment. It is a sensible cultural stop while exploring southern Hiiumaa.
