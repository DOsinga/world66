Kopu Lighthouse is one of Hiiumaa's defining landmarks and among the oldest lighthouses in continuous use in the world. Built in the 16th century, it stands inland on the Kopu peninsula rather than directly on the shore, rising above the island's forested landscape.

The lighthouse is worth the detour across western Hiiumaa for both the history and the views. It combines well with Ristna Lighthouse and the wilder coastal stretches at the end of the peninsula.
