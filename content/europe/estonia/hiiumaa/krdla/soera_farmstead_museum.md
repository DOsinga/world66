Soera Farmstead Museum preserves a traditional Hiiumaa farmstead, with a long dwelling-threshing barn, storehouses, an outdoor kitchen, a cellar, and a small smoke sauna arranged around the yard.

It gives a clear, compact look at rural island life before modern farming changed the landscape. The museum is a good stop for visitors using Kärdla as a base for exploring Hiiumaa by car or bicycle.
