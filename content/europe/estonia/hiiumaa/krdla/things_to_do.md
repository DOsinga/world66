---
title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Kärdla is small and honest about it. The town's main draws are the crater park, the museum, and the old cloth factory ruins — all within easy walking distance of the main street. The real reason to base yourself here is as a launching point for the wider island: the Kõpu Lighthouse, the juniper heaths, and the coastal landscapes of Hiiumaa are all within a short drive.
