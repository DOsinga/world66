Kuriste Church is Hiiumaa's surviving Orthodox church, completed in 1873 and dedicated to the Birth of the Mother of God. The modest wooden church preserves icons and liturgical textiles from the late 19th and early 20th centuries.

It is a quiet heritage stop rather than a major sight, but it adds useful context to the island's religious and cultural history.
