Pühalepa Church is Hiiumaa's oldest stone church, with roots in the 14th century and the plain, sturdy feel of a medieval island sanctuary. The present building mostly reflects later rebuilding, but the fortified setting and quiet churchyard make it a worthwhile stop while crossing the eastern side of the island.
