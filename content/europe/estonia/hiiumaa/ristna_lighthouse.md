Ristna Lighthouse stands at the western end of the Kõpu peninsula, where Hiiumaa reaches out toward the Baltic shipping lanes. The iron tower was erected in 1874 from French-made parts, the same year as its better-known sister lighthouse at Tahkuna.

The lighthouse is smaller and less dramatic than Kõpu or Tahkuna, but the setting is good: exposed coast, wind-shaped forest, and a sense of being at the far edge of the island. German ships damaged the tower during World War I, and the concrete belt added in 1920 still shows how the structure was reinforced.
