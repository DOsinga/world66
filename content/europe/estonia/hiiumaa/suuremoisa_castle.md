Suuremõisa Castle is one of Hiiumaa's grandest manor houses, built for Countess Ebba Margaretha Stenbock. The pale Baroque building and its grounds make an easy cultural stop while crossing the island, especially if you want something beyond beaches, lighthouses, and forest roads.
