Kaberneeme is a small coastal village in northern [Estonia](/europe/estonia), about 40 km east of Tallinn on the Kaberneeme peninsula. The village is known for its long, quiet beach — roughly 2 km of sand with pine forest edging right up to the shore. It is a popular summer escape for locals but rarely visited by foreign travellers.

Walking trails wind through the pine forest behind the [Kaberneeme Beach](/europe/estonia/kaberneeme/kaberneeme_beach). The peninsula itself juts out into the Gulf of Finland and has a pleasantly wild, end-of-the-road feel. To the south, the [Kalevi-Liiva dunes](/europe/estonia/kaberneeme/kalevi_liiva_dunes) are an unexpected natural curiosity — a field of glacial sand dunes framed by pine and sea. The surrounding [Ubari Landscape Conservation Area](/europe/estonia/kaberneeme/ubari_landscape_conservation_area) protects the coastal mosaic of forest, meadow, and cove that makes this stretch of the Gulf of Finland so quietly rewarding.
