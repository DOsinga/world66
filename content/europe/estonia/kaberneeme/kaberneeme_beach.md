Kaberneeme Beach is the centrepiece of the village — roughly two kilometres of pale sand backed by a dense wall of Scots pine. The water is shallow and calm, making it safe for swimming in summer when temperatures reach a tolerable 18–20°C. There are no beach bars, no umbrellas for hire, and no lifeguards; you bring your own everything, which suits the visitors who come here perfectly.

The pine forest begins immediately where the sand ends. A series of informal walking paths thread through the trees, with enough shade to make a mid-summer walk genuinely pleasant. Early morning is the best time: the light comes low over the Gulf of Finland, the forest smells of resin, and you are likely to have the whole shore to yourself.
