---
title: 'When to Go'
type: section
---

Kabli is a strongly seasonal place. The beach is busy — relatively speaking — from mid-June through August, when daytime temperatures sit around 20–25°C and the long Baltic evenings keep the sun above the horizon until 10 pm. Swimming is realistic from mid-July through August, when the shallow bay warms enough to be comfortable for more than a quick dip.

For birdwatching, time a visit to late August through early October, when the autumn migration is in full flow and the bird station is most active. Spring migration in May is also rewarding but more compressed. Winter is largely closed — the museum, restaurants, and most facilities shut down, the bird station operates on a reduced schedule, and the only reason to come is for ice fishing or a serious birding visit to look for over-wintering species.
