---
title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Kuressaare's headline attraction is its medieval castle, one of the best-preserved in the entire Baltic region. Beyond that, the town offers spa culture built around local dolomite mud, a pleasant old town of wooden houses and a neoclassical centre, and a strong base for exploring the rest of Saaremaa — an island with craters, windmills, and coastline that rewards a few days of unhurried travel.
