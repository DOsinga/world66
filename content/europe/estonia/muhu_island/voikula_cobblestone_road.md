The Võiküla cobblestone road is a surviving stretch of old stone road on Muhu, best visited as a short there-and-back walk. An information board explains the road's history in Estonian and English.

It is a small sight, but a worthwhile stop if you are already exploring Muhu's villages and rural heritage. Dry weather and shoes with grip make the uneven stones easier.
