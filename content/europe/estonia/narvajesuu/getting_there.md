---
title: "Getting There"
type: section
---

Narva-Jõesuu sits about 15 km north of [Narva](/europe/estonia/narva), Estonia's easternmost city. Narva itself is connected to Tallinn by train (roughly 3 hours) and by bus, with frequent services throughout the day.

From Narva, local buses run to Narva-Jõesuu several times daily; the journey takes about 30 minutes. If you are driving, the road north from Narva follows the Narva River and is straightforward. Tallinn to Narva-Jõesuu by car is approximately 230 km along the E20, taking around 2.5 hours.

There is no rail connection to Narva-Jõesuu itself. Most visitors either arrive by car or take a bus from Narva after arriving by train.
