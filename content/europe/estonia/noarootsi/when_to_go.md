---
title: When to Go
type: section
---

The best time to visit Noarootsi is late spring (May to early June) or early autumn (September). The spring migration brings extraordinary birding, the meadows are at their most colourful, and the days are long. Summer (July–August) is warm and pleasant for cycling and walking, with the coast at its most inviting. Autumn migration is almost as good as spring for birds.

Winter is quiet, dark, and cold — the coast can be windswept and the landscape turns monochrome. Not unpleasant for those who like solitude, but there is little reason to make a special trip.
