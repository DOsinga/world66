Peraküla is a small village beach a few kilometres south of Nõva, with a slightly more developed setup — a seasonal kiosk, a car park, and basic facilities. It is still quiet by any normal measure, but is the social hub of this stretch of coast in summer.

The beach itself is attractive: a long arc of sand with low dunes backed by pine trees, and the Baltic stretching west to the horizon. Sunsets here can be extraordinary, especially in June and early July when the sun sets far in the northwest and the sky turns colours for an hour before it finally drops. A good alternative to the Nõva beach if you want slightly easier access.
