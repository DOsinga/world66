Otepaa's small museum complex covers two local stories: the town's role in Estonian skiing and its connection with the creation of the Estonian flag. The flag-history exhibits explain why Otepaa is an important symbolic stop in the country's national story.

It is a compact visit and fits well with a walk around the town centre.
