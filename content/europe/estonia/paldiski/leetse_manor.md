Leetse Manor is more ruin than mansion now, but the remains still have atmosphere: surviving walls, a decorated gate, the manager's house, and stairs leading down toward the sea.

The approach is part of the interest. On the way you pass relics of Paldiski's Soviet military past, including the sarcophagus-like structures connected with the former submarine training base.
