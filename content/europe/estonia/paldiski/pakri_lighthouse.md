Pakri Lighthouse is one of Paldiski's clearest landmarks, standing near the limestone cliffs and open Baltic coast. The setting is the main draw: wind, sea, cliff edges, and wide views that feel very different from Tallinn's enclosed old town.

It is a strong stop on a Paldiski and Pakri Peninsula outing, especially for travelers interested in coastal landscapes and Cold War-edge towns. Check access and weather, as the cliffs can be exposed.
