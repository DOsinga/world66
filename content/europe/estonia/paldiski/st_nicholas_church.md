St. Nicholas Church is a restored 19th-century church in Paldiski, built in 1842 and later left to decay during the Soviet period. Its plain, blocky form gives it a different look from many older Estonian churches, and its restoration is tied to the town's post-Soviet recovery.

The church is a modest stop, but it helps explain Paldiski's layered history as a port, military town, and reclaimed local community. It is easy to include on a walk through the center before heading to the Pakri cliffs and lighthouse.
