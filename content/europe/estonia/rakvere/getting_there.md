---
title: "Getting There"
type: section
---

**From Tallinn** Rakvere is an easy day trip — 100 km east on the E20. Direct **trains** (Elron) take 75–90 minutes; **buses** are slightly slower but more frequent. Both leave from the central terminals in Tallinn, and the Rakvere station is a 10-minute walk from the old town. By car, it's about an hour and a quarter.

The town is a useful stop on the way to or from **Lahemaa National Park** (40 km north), **Narva** (115 km east) and the Russian border, or **Tartu** (165 km south).
