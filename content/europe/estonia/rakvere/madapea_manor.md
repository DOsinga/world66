Mädapea Manor is a rural estate stop near Rakvere, part of the Baltic-German manor landscape that still shapes much of northern Estonia. It is quieter than Rakvere Castle, but it gives a useful glimpse of the landed world around the town.

Check current access before making a special trip, as manor buildings can have variable opening arrangements. It works best as a short countryside detour for travellers already exploring Lahemaa, Rakvere, and the region's estate architecture.
