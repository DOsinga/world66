Rakvere's Orthodox Church of the Nativity of Theotokos grew from a nineteenth-century parish serving merchants, officials, teachers, soldiers, and later Estonian converts to Orthodoxy. The present church was rebuilt in the early twentieth century in a provincial version of Russian Revival style, which stands out in the Baltic streetscape.

The interior is modest and mostly wooden. Its most unusual feature is the shrine of Sergei Florinski, an Orthodox priest martyred in Rakvere in 1918.
