Founded in 1940 and one of Estonia's two main regional theatres — much-loved, well funded, and impressively prolific for a town of 15,000. The building itself is a sober inter-war affair on the western edge of the old town.

The repertoire is in Estonian, but if you are in town in late August or early September check the **Baltoscandal** international fringe festival, which the theatre hosts and which is mostly performed in English or with surtitles. Otherwise the bar is a pleasant evening stop in its own right.
