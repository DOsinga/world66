Rodewal Manor is a small country estate on the edge of Rakvere. The wooden manor house was built in 1864 and is known for the carved snake decoration on its pediment.

It is a short architectural stop rather than a major museum, best combined with Rakvere's castle, old streets, and other manor houses in the surrounding countryside.
