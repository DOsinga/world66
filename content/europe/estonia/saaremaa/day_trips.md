Kuressaare is the obvious base, but Saaremaa rewards full-day loops. One classic circuit runs north to Angla and Karja, then west to Panga Cliff and back through villages and forest. Another heads south to Sõrve, with beach stops and lighthouse views. The wildest day is west toward Vilsandi National Park and the Harilaid peninsula.
