---
title: Getting Around
type: section
---

Public buses connect the main villages and Kuressaare, but they are not ideal for sightseeing. A car is the practical choice if you want to reach Panga Cliff, Sõrve, Kiipsaare, or the scattered churches and museums in one trip.

Cycling is possible and rewarding in summer, especially around Kuressaare and the quieter village roads, but distances are longer than they look. Wind can be a real factor on exposed coastal stretches.
