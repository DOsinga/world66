---
title: Getting There
type: section
---

Most travellers reach Saaremaa by ferry from Virtsu on the mainland to Kuivastu on Muhu, then continue by road across the causeway to Saaremaa. Buses from Tallinn use the same ferry route and continue to Kuressaare; the whole trip usually takes around four hours.

Kuressaare has a small airport with limited domestic flights. If you are driving, book summer ferry crossings ahead when possible, especially on Fridays and Sundays.
