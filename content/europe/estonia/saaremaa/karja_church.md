Karja Church is one of Saaremaa's most rewarding medieval churches, small from the outside but rich in carved and painted detail. Its scale is rural, yet the interior carries traces of serious craftsmanship: sculpted capitals, old wall paintings, and symbols that have kept art historians busy.

It is worth a detour if you are driving the northern part of the island. The church helps explain why Saaremaa's scattered villages are more interesting than they first appear; history here is often tucked inside plain stone walls.
