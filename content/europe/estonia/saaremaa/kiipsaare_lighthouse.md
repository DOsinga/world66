Kiipsaare Lighthouse is one of Saaremaa's strangest sights, a slender concrete beacon leaning on the Harilaid peninsula in Vilsandi National Park. It looks staged for a photograph, but the tilt is real: coastal erosion and shifting sand have changed the ground around it.

Reaching it takes effort. You normally walk from the Harilaid trailhead through a wind-exposed landscape of scrub, sand, and low shore. That effort is part of the reward. This is not a manicured lighthouse stop; it is a lesson in how mobile a Baltic coastline can be.
