Koigi Bog Observation Tower overlooks one of Saaremaa's wetland landscapes, with open bog, pools, and low forest stretching around the boardwalk area. It is a simple stop, but a worthwhile one for birdwatching, photography, and seeing a quieter inland side of the island.

The tower works best as part of a drive across Saaremaa rather than as a long standalone visit.
