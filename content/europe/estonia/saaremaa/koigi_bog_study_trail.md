Koigi Bog Study Trail crosses Saaremaa's largest bog on a boardwalk route of about 4.8 to 5.7 km. Information boards explain the wetland, and the route has several viewpoints over pools, open bog, and low forest.

Sections can be wet depending on the season, so waterproof footwear is useful. In autumn, cranberries grow in the bog.
