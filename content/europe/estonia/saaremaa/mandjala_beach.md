Mändjala Beach is one of the easiest sandy beaches to reach from Kuressaare, backed by pines and summer cottages on Saaremaa's south coast. The water is shallow and the mood is local rather than resort-polished, which suits the island.

It is a good low-effort stop on a warm afternoon, especially if you are staying in Kuressaare and want sea without a long drive. Facilities vary by season, so bring what you need and treat it as a beach picnic rather than a serviced resort.
