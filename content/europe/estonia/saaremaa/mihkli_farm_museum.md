Mihkli Farm Museum preserves a traditional Saaremaa farmstead in Viki village, with timber buildings, tools, household objects, and the practical layout of island rural life. It is not flashy, but it gives useful context for the fields, stone walls, and low farmhouses you pass elsewhere on the island.

The museum works best as part of a western Saaremaa loop toward Vilsandi or Kiipsaare. It slows the trip down in the right way, reminding you that Saaremaa is not only coast and castles but a long-lived farming landscape.
