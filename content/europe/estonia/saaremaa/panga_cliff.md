Panga Cliff is the most dramatic natural viewpoint on Saaremaa, a limestone escarpment rising above the north coast with a broad view over the Baltic. By mountain standards it is modest; by Saaremaa standards it feels almost theatrical, a sudden edge in an otherwise low island.

There are paths along the top, picnic spots, and enough space to sit and watch the sea. Keep away from unstable edges, especially after bad weather. The cliff is best in clear light or at sunset, when the flatness of the surrounding island makes the drop feel sharper.
