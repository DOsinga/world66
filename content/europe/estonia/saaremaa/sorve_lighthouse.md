Sõrve Lighthouse stands at the narrow southern end of Saaremaa, where the island tapers into a long spit pointing toward Latvia. The drive down the peninsula is part of the experience: pine forest, scattered villages, military traces, and sudden glimpses of sea on both sides.

The lighthouse area gives a strong sense of edge-of-the-map geography. In clear weather, the views stretch over the Gulf of Riga and the Irbe Strait. Check opening hours if you want to climb; even from the ground it is one of Saaremaa's essential coastal stops.
