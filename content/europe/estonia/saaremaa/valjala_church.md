Valjala Church is often cited among the oldest stone churches in Estonia, begun soon after the island's thirteenth-century conquest and Christianisation. Its heavy walls and restrained forms feel closer to a fortified village landmark than to a delicate parish church.

The church is a good first stop after arriving from Muhu, before continuing to Kuressaare or Kaali. It gives Saaremaa's early medieval history a physical presence, and the surrounding village keeps the visit quiet.
