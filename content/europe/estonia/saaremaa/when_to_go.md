---
title: When to Go
type: section
---

Late May through September is the best season for Saaremaa. June and July bring long light, wildflowers, birds, and the fullest ferry and restaurant schedules. August is warmer but busier around Kuressaare.

Spring and autumn are excellent for birdwatching and empty roads, though services thin out. Winter can be beautiful in a severe Baltic way, but many rural attractions keep limited hours and the island feels very quiet.
