The Contemporary Art Museum of Estonia, usually shortened to EKKM, occupies a rough-edged former industrial space near Linnahall. It feels deliberately informal, with exhibitions that lean toward younger artists, experimental work, and the edge between gallery and museum.

Come here after the old town if you want Tallinn to feel less medieval and more alive to the present.
