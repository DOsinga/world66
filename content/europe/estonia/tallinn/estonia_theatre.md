The Estonia Concert Hall and Theatre is the centre of Tallinn's classical music and opera life, an Art Nouveau building completed in 1913 that houses both the Estonian National Opera and the Estonian National Symphony Orchestra. The building on Estonia Avenue is one of the most important pieces of Art Nouveau architecture in Tallinn.

Performances run year-round; the opera and ballet calendar is ambitious for a small country, and ticket prices are very reasonable by Western European standards. The Estonia Theatre's performances are typically in Estonian or their original language with Estonian surtitles, but opera is opera. Check the programme online.
