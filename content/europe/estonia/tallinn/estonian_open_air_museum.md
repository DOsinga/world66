A large open-air museum on the outskirts of Tallinn, holding 74 original historic buildings moved from locations across Estonia: farmhouses, barns, windmills, a chapel, and a schoolhouse spanning five centuries of rural Estonian life. In summer, craftspeople demonstrate traditional skills in some of the buildings, and the café serves traditional Estonian food. The setting — pine forest, meadows, coastline — is as good as the buildings themselves. A half-day trip by bus from the city centre.
