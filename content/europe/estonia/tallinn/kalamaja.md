The neighbourhood of wooden houses northwest of the Old Town, built in the late 19th century for factory workers and now occupied by designers, young families, and good cafés. The Balti Jaam market (behind the train station) is the best daily market in the city for cheap produce, old tools, and secondhand clothes. The Finnish-Estonian ferry terminal is at the north end, and the Lennusadam Seaplane Harbour museum — inside a 1917 concrete hangar at the water's edge — is worth an afternoon.
