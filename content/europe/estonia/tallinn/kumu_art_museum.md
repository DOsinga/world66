The main museum of the Art Museum of Estonia, opened in 2006 in a building by Finnish architect Pekka Vapaavuori embedded into a limestone hillside in Kadriorg Park. The permanent collection covers Estonian art from the 18th century to the Soviet period and beyond — the Soviet-era section is particularly interesting as a record of how artists negotiated the ideological demands of the state. The building's relationship with the hillside and the park around it makes the approach worth the walk from Kadriorg Palace next door.
