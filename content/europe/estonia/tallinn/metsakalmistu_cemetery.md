Metsakalmistu is Tallinn's forest cemetery, laid out among pines rather than formal avenues and stone-heavy monuments. Many of Estonia's major cultural and political figures are buried here, which makes it a quiet map of the country's 20th-century memory.

The cemetery is best approached slowly, as a reflective walk rather than a sightseeing sprint. Graves are often understated, and the woodland setting keeps the mood restrained. It pairs naturally with Pirita or the Tallinn Botanic Garden if you are spending time east of the centre.
