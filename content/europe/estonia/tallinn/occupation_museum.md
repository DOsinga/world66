Vabamu is Tallinn's museum of the Soviet and Nazi occupation periods (1940–1991), located at the edge of Toompea. The permanent exhibition is emotionally serious and carefully researched — it documents the deportations, resistance, collaboration, and survival strategies of the Estonian population under two successive totalitarian occupations.

The museum takes about two hours to do properly. It is not intended to be easy, but it is essential context for understanding modern Estonia and its relationship with Russia. The personal testimonies and artefacts are more affecting than the historical overview panels, and the final section on the restoration of independence is genuinely moving.
