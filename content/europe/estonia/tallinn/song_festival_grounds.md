The Song Festival Grounds are one of Tallinn's most meaningful modern landmarks, built around the huge arched stage used for Estonia's national song festivals. The site is closely tied to the country's choral tradition and to the Singing Revolution, when mass singing became a form of peaceful political resistance.

It is most powerful during a major event, but the empty grounds still communicate the scale of the gatherings they were designed to hold. Combine it with Kadriorg or the seaside promenade, and take a moment to stand back from the stage to understand how many voices the space can gather.
