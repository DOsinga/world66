The main medieval gate through Tallinn's city wall, leading from the new town into the UNESCO-listed Old Town. The two towers that flank the gateway are 15th century; the flower market that fills the lane between them has been here for centuries. Walking through Viru Gate into the medieval street beyond is one of those moments where the transition between eras feels genuinely sharp. The wall beyond is walkable in sections; the towers along it are among the best-preserved in northern Europe.
