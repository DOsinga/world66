The Estonian Sports and Olympic Museum is a compact, interactive museum near Tartu's old town. Its exhibits cover Estonian sport, Olympic history, and winter sports, with enough hands-on material to work for families as well as visitors interested in local culture.

It is not one of Tartu's grand headline sights, but it makes a useful stop when you are already exploring the university quarter and Cathedral Hill area.
