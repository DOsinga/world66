The Old Anatomical Theatre is one of the early buildings of the reopened University of Tartu, built between 1803 and 1805 to designs by university architect J. Krause. Its round form and hilltop setting make it one of the more distinctive university buildings in town.

Inside, the exhibition covers the history of medicine and anatomy. It is a niche stop, but a good one if you are following Tartu's university history rather than just circling the main square.
