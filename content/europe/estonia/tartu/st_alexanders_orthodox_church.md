St. Alexander's Orthodox Church is a two-storey church designed by V. Lunski, with domes inspired by older Russian church architecture. It was deconsecrated for decades, then reconsecrated in 2003, making it a worthwhile stop for visitors tracing Tartu's mix of religious and imperial-era architecture.
