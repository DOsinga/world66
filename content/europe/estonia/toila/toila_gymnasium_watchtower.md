The Toila Gymnasium Watchtower is a simple viewpoint with a broad sweep over the north-east Estonian coast. On a clear day you can pick out Sillamae, Narva-Joesuu, Puhajoe village, the Toila-Ontika limestone cliff, and the Gulf of Finland beyond.
