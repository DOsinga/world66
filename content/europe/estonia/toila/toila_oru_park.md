Toila-Oru Park is a landscaped clifftop park set above the North Estonian Klint — the limestone escarpment that runs along the coast of northeastern Estonia. The park was developed around a grand manor house that no longer exists, but the terraced gardens, ornamental ponds, and winding paths through the cliff-edge trees have been carefully maintained and make for an excellent walk.

The views from the cliff edge look directly out over the Gulf of Finland toward Finland on a clear day. Below the cliff face, a rocky beach stretches in both directions, accessible by a steep path. The park is most impressive in late May when the lilacs and ornamental plantings are in bloom, but it's worth visiting in any season — the bare trees in winter reveal the cliff structure more clearly than summer foliage allows.
