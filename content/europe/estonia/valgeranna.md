**Valgeranna** is a quiet beach village on Estonia's west coast, about 8 km west of Pärnu along the shore of Pärnu Bay. The name means "white beach" in Estonian, and the description is accurate: roughly 500 metres of fine white sand backed by fragrant pine forest, with shallow, calm water that stays warm enough to swim in from June to August.

For most Estonians, Valgeranna is a peaceful alternative to the busier Pärnu beach crowds. The village is small — a handful of summer houses, a campsite, a golf course — but the beach itself is genuinely beautiful, and the surrounding pine-forested coast has a particular Baltic stillness that is hard to find closer to town. The [Audru Polder Nature Reserve](/europe/estonia/valgeranna/audru_polder_nature_reserve) just to the northwest adds another reason to come, drawing birdwatchers and walkers who want something beyond the sand.

Getting here without a car requires a bus from Pärnu, a journey of around 20 minutes. Most visitors come for a day; the campsite and a few guesthouses allow a longer stay if the slower pace suits you.
