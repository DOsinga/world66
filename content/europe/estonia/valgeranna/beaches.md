Valgeranna's beach is the reason most people come. The [Valgeranna Beach](/europe/estonia/valgeranna/valgeranna_beach) stretches about 500 metres along Pärnu Bay, sheltered by pine trees and with water shallow enough for children to wade far out from shore. The sand is genuinely white — the name "Valgeranna" means white beach — and the setting among pines is more attractive than the open municipal beach at Pärnu.
