---
title: Getting There
type: section
---

Valgeranna is about 8 km west of Pärnu along the coastal road. By bus, take a local service from Pärnu bus station — the journey takes around 20 minutes and runs several times a day in summer. By car from Pärnu, take the road toward Audru and follow signs west along the coast; the drive is straightforward and takes about 15 minutes.

Pärnu itself is well-connected: regular express buses run from Tallinn (around 2 hours) and Tartu (around 2.5 hours). There is no direct connection from Tallinn or Tartu to Valgeranna — you change at Pärnu.
