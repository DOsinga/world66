title: Things to Do
type: section
---

Valgeranna is a small place, and the draw is fundamentally the same wherever you look: beach, forest, and the slow rhythm of the Estonian coast. The [Valgeranna Beach](/europe/estonia/valgeranna/valgeranna_beach) is the main event, but the [Audru Polder Nature Reserve](/europe/estonia/valgeranna/audru_polder_nature_reserve) to the northwest is genuinely rewarding for anyone interested in birds or wetland landscapes. The [Valgeranna Golf Club](/europe/estonia/valgeranna/valgeranna_golf_club) is one of the better-established courses in western Estonia for those inclined toward the game.
