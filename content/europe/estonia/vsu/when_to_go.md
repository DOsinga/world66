---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

Võsu is a summer destination. The beach season runs from late June to mid-August, when Baltic water temperatures reach 18–22°C and the long northern evenings make outdoor life genuinely pleasant. July and early August are peak season — the village fills up and advance booking for accommodation is essential.

The forest and bog trails are good from May through October, and early autumn (September) is particularly beautiful: the crowds thin after the school year starts, the forest turns amber and gold, and the bog vegetation goes red and rust. The weather is still mild enough for walking and the light is excellent for photography.

Winter is quiet almost to the point of emptiness. The beach and the bog are deserted, some accommodation closes, and the town lives on a skeleton schedule. But the forest in snow is striking, and if you come on a still winter day the silence in the pines is something you will not forget.
