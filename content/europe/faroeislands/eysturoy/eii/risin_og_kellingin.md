Risin og Kellingin are the two sea stacks standing off the northern coast of Eysturoy, close to Eiði. Risin, the Giant, is the broader stack farther from shore; Kellingin, the Witch, is the sharper one nearer the cliff. They are not huge by Faroese standards, but the setting gives them real force: black rock, white surf, and the open Atlantic behind them.

The easiest views are from the road and lay-bys above Eiði, especially on the route toward Gjogv. Bring a long lens if you want photographs. On windy days the stacks can look almost theatrical, with spray tearing across the water below Eiðiskollur.
