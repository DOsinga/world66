Mykines is the far western edge of the Faroe Islands, a green ridge dropped into the Atlantic beyond [Vágar](/europe/faroeislands/vagar). It is small, exposed, and famous for one thing above all: seabirds. In summer, puffins nest in the grass along the paths, gannets gather around the outer islet, and the island feels like a bird cliff with a village attached.

The only settlement, [Mykines village](/europe/faroeislands/mykines/mykines_village), is a cluster of houses above the landing place. From there the classic walk leads west toward [Mykineshólmur](/europe/faroeislands/mykines/mykinesholmur) and the [lighthouse](/europe/faroeislands/mykines/mykines_lighthouse), crossing a narrow bridge above the sea. It is one of the great Faroese day trips when the weather behaves.

That condition matters. The boat from [Sørvágur](/europe/faroeislands/vagar/srvgur) is seasonal and often cancelled by swell or wind, and helicopter seats are limited. Book ahead, carry food and waterproofs, and leave room in your plans for a forced change. Mykines is unforgettable, but it does not run to anyone's timetable.
