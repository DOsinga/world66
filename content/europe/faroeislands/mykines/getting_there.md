---
title: Getting There
type: section
---

The summer ferry to Mykines leaves from [Sørvágur](/europe/faroeislands/vagar/srvgur) on Vágar and takes about 45 minutes in good conditions. It should be booked in advance, and same-day cancellations are common when the Atlantic swell rises.

Atlantic Airways also operates a limited helicopter service linking Mykines with the rest of the Faroe Islands. It is meant as public transport rather than sightseeing, so seats are few and visitors should not build a tight itinerary around it.
