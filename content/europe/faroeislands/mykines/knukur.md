Knúkur is the highest point on Mykines, rising to 560 metres on the island's main ridge. It is not the standard day-trip walk, but it gives a more solitary sense of the island than the busy route to Mykineshólmur.

Only attempt it in settled visibility. Paths can be faint, the slopes are wet, and fog can remove every landmark at once. If the weather is clear, the views back over the village and west toward the lighthouse are superb.
