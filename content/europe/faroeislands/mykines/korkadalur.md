Korkadalur lies on the northern side of Mykines and is known for its basalt formations, sometimes described as a stone wood. It is a reminder that the Faroes are volcanic islands first and postcard scenery second: layers of lava, softer tuff, and sea erosion have built the cliffs that now hold the birds.

This is a more selective walk than the lighthouse route. Go only with good local advice and settled weather, because the northern slopes are exposed and there is little margin for navigational mistakes in fog.
