Mykineshólmur is the small islet west of Mykines, reached by the classic footpath from the village. The walk crosses open slopes, seabird nesting areas, and a narrow bridge above the channel before continuing toward the lighthouse at the far end.

This is the part of Mykines most visitors picture: puffins close to the path, gannets wheeling offshore, and steep grass falling straight into the Atlantic. Stay on marked routes and obey any seasonal closures. The birds are the reason to come, and they are under pressure from too many careless feet.
