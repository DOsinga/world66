Nólsoy is both an island and its principal settlement, easily reached by ferry from [Tórshavn](/europe/faroeislands/torshavn). The crossing takes about 20 minutes and drops you in a [small village](/europe/faroeislands/nolsoy/nolsoy_village) with a church, a shop, a cafe, and an arch made from the jawbones of a whale.

The island holds one of the world's largest [storm petrel colonies](/europe/faroeislands/nolsoy/storm_petrel_colony), with some 50,000 pairs nesting in burrows on the eastern hillside. Seeing them requires an overnight stay — the birds return to their burrows only after dark, to avoid the skuas that hunt them in daylight. A walk to the southern tip along the ridge trail leads to the [Borðan lighthouse](/europe/faroeislands/nolsoy/bordan_lighthouse) in about five hours return — proper mountain walking, with views that take in most of the southern archipelago. For a shorter visit, head for the viewpoint on the lower hillside above the village.
