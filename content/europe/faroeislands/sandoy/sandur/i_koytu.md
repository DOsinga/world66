Í Koytu is a preserved traditional farmhouse in Sandur that now serves as a house museum illustrating daily life on Sandoy in earlier centuries. The building complex — the main house, outbuildings, and stone-walled yard — has been preserved as an example of the traditional Faroese longhouse farm type, with period furnishings and agricultural tools.

The museum is modest in scale but well-maintained, and the contrast between the preserved interior and the working pastoral landscape visible through the windows gives a vivid sense of how isolated island life once was. In conjunction with the Listasavnið art museum, it provides a more complete picture of Sandoy's culture than either would alone.
