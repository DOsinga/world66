---
title: When to Go
type: section
---

The bird cliff boat trips run from spring through early autumn, with the best birdwatching between May and August when the colonies are at their most active. Puffins arrive in April and leave again in August; guillemots and razorbills linger a little longer.

The Faroe Islands have notoriously changeable weather — fog, rain, and sudden sunshine are all possible in the same afternoon regardless of season. Summer (June–August) brings the mildest temperatures and the longest days, with light lasting until midnight in June. Even then, pack waterproofs and a warm layer for the boat trip into the cliffs.

If you are visiting in winter, the bird cliffs are quiet and the boat trips don't run, but the dramatic landscape is still there — and with far fewer tourists. Road access to Vestmanna from [Torshavn](/europe/faroeislands/torshavn) via the tunnel is year-round.
