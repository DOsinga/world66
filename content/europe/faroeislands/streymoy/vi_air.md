Við Áir preserves part of a former whaling station on the east coast of Streymoy. The open-air museum gives a direct, sometimes uncomfortable look at an industry that shaped Faroese coastal life for much of the 20th century.

It is a short, specific stop rather than a full museum day, but it adds useful context if you are driving between the villages of northern Streymoy.
