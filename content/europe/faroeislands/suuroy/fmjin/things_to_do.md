---
title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Fámjin has three sights, all within walking distance of the village. The small wooden church holds the original Faroese flag and the only runestone in the islands. Behind the village a marked path climbs to the mountain lake of Kirkjuvatn — about an hour up, two or three hours round trip — and the road past the church ends above Prestgjógv, a dramatic sea gorge at the cliffs. Beyond those, the appeal of Fámjin is the village itself: a handful of houses on the only sheltered spot of a wild western coast, the kind of place where a summer cafe is the focal point of social life.
