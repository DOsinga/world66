Porkeri is a small village on the east coast of Suðuroy, the southernmost of the [Faroe Islands](/europe/faroeislands), situated between [Hov](/europe/faroeislands/suuroy/hov) to the north and [Vágur](/europe/faroeislands/suuroy/vgur) to the south. The population is around 330. Like most Faroese villages, it sits at the edge of the sea with steep hillsides rising behind it.

The village has a [wooden church from 1847](/europe/faroeislands/suuroy/porkeri/porkeri_church) with a turf roof — a fine example of the traditional church architecture found across the islands — and a [local museum](/europe/faroeislands/suuroy/porkeri/porkeri_museum) next door. The combination of church and museum makes it a worthwhile stop for anyone travelling the length of Suðuroy. The [ridge above the village](/europe/faroeislands/suuroy/porkeri/ridge_walk) is an easy climb that rewards with views along the entire east coast of the island.
