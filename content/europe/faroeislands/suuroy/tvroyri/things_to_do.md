---
title: Things to Do
type: section
---

Tvøroyri is a base rather than a destination in itself. The town has a grocery shop, a pharmacy, a post office, and a petrol station — the service infrastructure for Suðuroy's scattered communities. The real draws are the island's landscape, which includes some of the most dramatic sea cliffs in the Faroes, accessible by car or on foot from the road that loops around the island. The ferry crossing from Krambatangi to Tórshavn is in itself one of the better sea journeys in the archipelago.
