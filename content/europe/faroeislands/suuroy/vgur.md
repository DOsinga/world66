Vágur is one of the largest towns on Suðuroy, the southernmost island in the Faroes. It sits on the east coast around Vágsfjørður, between the villages of [Porkeri](/europe/faroeislands/suuroy/porkeri) and Lopra. About 1,370 people live here.

The surroundings are dramatic even by Faroese standards. West of the village, [Vágseiði](/europe/faroeislands/suuroy/vgur/vagseii) faces the open Atlantic. To the south, [Eggjarnar](/europe/faroeislands/suuroy/vgur/eggjarnar) drops away in sheer vertical cliffs. Climb [Gjógvaráfjall](/europe/faroeislands/suuroy/vgur/gjogvarafjall) -- the local people call its sea face Skúvanes -- and you're looking straight down into the ocean. North of town at Botni, the Faroes' first [hydroelectric power plant](/europe/faroeislands/suuroy/vgur/botnur_power_plant) was built in 1921 — and it's still running.
