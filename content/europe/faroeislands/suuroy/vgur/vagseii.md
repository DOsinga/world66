Vágseiði is the narrow strip of land that connects Vágur's bay (on the east side of Suðuroy) to the open Atlantic (on the west). Here, the island is only about a kilometre wide. The west side of the eiði is exposed and wild: a wave-cut platform of black rock, with old fishing huts perched above it and a broken concrete pier where the ferry once docked.

It is one of the most atmospheric places on Suðuroy, particularly in stormy weather when the swell smashes against the cliffs and clouds tear past overhead. The walk from Vágur takes about thirty minutes. The road is paved but ends at the eiði.
