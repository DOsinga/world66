Sirkus is the late-night venue of choice in Tórshavn — a small, scruffy bar painted in bright colours, with mismatched furniture and a small dance floor that fills up after midnight on Fridays and Saturdays. The crowd is a mix of locals in their twenties and thirties, plus the occasional visitor who finds their way in.

Closed Sunday through Wednesday in winter. Cash and card both accepted.
