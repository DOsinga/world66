Tinganes is the small peninsula that juts into Tórshavn's harbour and divides it into east and west bays. The black-tarred, red-painted wooden buildings with their grass roofs house the offices of the Faroese government — the Løgmansskrivstovan (Prime Minister's Office) and several ministries are in buildings dating from the 16th to 18th centuries.

You can wander freely between the houses; there are no fences and no signs telling you to keep out. A boulder near the tip carries a runic inscription, and the views back over the harbour towards the modern town are excellent. Visit at the start of any walk around Tórshavn — everything else flows out from here.
