title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

The main draws around Sørvágur are natural and almost all require walking. The lake hike to Sørvágsvatn is the headline act, but the villages of [Bøur](/europe/faroeislands/vagar/bour) and [Gásadalur](/europe/faroeislands/vagar/gasadalur) are worth a day of their own, and a summer morning on the Mykines ferry is one of the defining Faroese experiences.
