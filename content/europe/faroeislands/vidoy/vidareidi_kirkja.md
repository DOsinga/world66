Viðareiði Kirkja is a simple Faroese village church in the far north of Viðoy. Its main interest is the silver, including a salver presented by the British government in thanks for help given to sailors rescued from a 19th-century wreck.

The church is a short stop, but the setting makes it better: Viðareiði sits between sea, mountains, and some of the most dramatic northern-island scenery in the Faroes.
