Bomarsund Fortress was a huge Russian fortification begun in 1830 and left unfinished after British and French forces destroyed it during the Crimean War. The remaining walls, gun positions, and scattered ruins give a clear sense of how ambitious the project was meant to be.

The site is one of Aland's stronger history stops. It pairs well with Kastelholm Castle if you are exploring the eastern side of the islands by car or bicycle.
