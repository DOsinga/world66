Bomarsund Fortress is the great unfinished Russian stronghold of Åland, begun in the 1830s and destroyed during the Crimean War before it could be completed. The ruins are spread across a wide waterfront site, with enough surviving walls and earthworks to make the scale clear.

A small museum near the bridge to Prästö adds the context: maps, pictures, and objects from the fortress explain why this remote island position mattered to the Russian Empire and why Britain and France made a point of demolishing it.
