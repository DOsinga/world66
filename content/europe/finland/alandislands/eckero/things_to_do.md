type: section
---

Start with the [Eckerö Mail and Customs House](eckero_mail_and_customs_house), the best single sight in town and one of Åland's strongest reminders of the Russian period. It is close to the ferry harbour, so it works even on a short stop.

Käringsund is the other useful focus. The [Åland Hunting and Fishing Museum](aland_hunting_and_fishing_museum) and the old fishing harbour sit close together, and [Käringsund Game Safari](karingsund_game_safari) is an easy family stop. In settled summer weather, check whether boat trips are running to [Märket Lighthouse](market_lighthouse), a tiny skerry divided between Finland and Sweden.
