Hoga C is a wooden lookout tower above the Fardsundet strait, with one of the classic postcard views of the Aland Islands. From the top you get the mix that makes the islands so distinctive: forest, low hills, water, and scattered red farm buildings.

Access is through the Uffe pa Berget cafe area. The tower itself is free, but it makes most sense as a short stop while driving or cycling across Fasta Aland.
