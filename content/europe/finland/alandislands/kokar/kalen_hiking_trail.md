The Kalen hiking trail is the best single walk on Kökar. It runs through changing vegetation and over rocky ground, with sea views, prehistoric traces at Otterböte, military remains, and a reconstructed stone labyrinth along the way.

The route is only about seven kilometres, but the ground and weather can make it feel longer. Pick it when you want the island's history and landscape in one outing.
