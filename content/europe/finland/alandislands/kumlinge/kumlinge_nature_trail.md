The Kumlinge Nature Trail loops around Rasnas hill on the northwest side of the main village. The linked loops are short enough for a relaxed island walk, but the terrain is varied and includes rocky viewpoints.

Maps are usually available by the church and guest marina. In warm weather, watch for vipers on the trail.
