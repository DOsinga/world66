Kumlinge Pharmacy is a small, characterful stop in the village centre. Visitors come less for a formal museum experience than for the preserved older fittings and tools, a reminder of how self-contained island communities had to be.

It pairs well with the church because it brings the island's history down to everyday scale. Check local opening times in summer; outside the main season, small Åland sights can be irregular.
