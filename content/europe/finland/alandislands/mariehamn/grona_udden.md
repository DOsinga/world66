Gröna Udden is Mariehamn's convenient beach-and-park area, close enough to town to reach without planning an excursion. It gives visitors a simple Aland summer experience: grass, sand, shallow water, and boats moving through the surrounding channels.

The site is modest, but useful. It works as a family swim stop, a picnic place, or a quiet walk when you want to balance museums and maritime history with the relaxed outdoor life that defines the islands in good weather.
