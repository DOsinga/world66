Lemböte Seafarers' Chapel is a small medieval chapel ruin on a rocky hill south of Mariehamn. The site was known to sailors by the Middle Ages, later fell into ruin, and was partly restored in the 19th century; occasional summer services still keep it from feeling abandoned.
