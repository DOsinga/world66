Lilla Holmen is a little park island close to Mariehamn's eastern harbour. Families come for the small beach, play areas, and resident animals such as peacocks and rabbits.

It is not a major sight, but it is one of the easiest places in town to slow down with children or take a short waterside break between the museums and the harbour.
