Vita Björn served as Åland's prison for about 200 years, until 1975. The small museum preserves four cells and shows how the treatment of prisoners changed from the 18th century into the 20th.

The building also includes the former warden's residence and prison-related objects such as chains and punishment equipment. It is close to Kastelholm Castle and is best treated as part of the same history-focused outing.
