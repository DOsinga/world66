The Espoo archipelago is a chain of small islands off the city's southern shore, reached by summer boats when the weather is good. The islands are simple, outdoorsy escapes rather than resort stops, with rocky shorelines, forest paths, picnic spots, and places for camping. They work best as a slow half-day or full-day trip from Espoo or Helsinki.
