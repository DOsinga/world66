Espoo Central Park is a broad green area south of Espoon keskus, made up mostly of spruce forest, rocky hills, and maintained walking paths. It gives Espoo a wilder central park than the name suggests.

The park is best for a quiet walk, run, or cross-country ski outing rather than a single famous sight. Paths can feel remote in places, so bring a map if you plan to cross the larger forest sections.
