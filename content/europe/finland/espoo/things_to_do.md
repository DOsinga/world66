---
title: 'Things to Do'
type: section
---

Espoo's attractions are spread out — the city is not somewhere you wander on foot from a central square. The five district centres each have their own character. **Tapiola** is the famous garden city and the cultural heart. **Espoo Centre** has the medieval cathedral. **Otaniemi** holds Aalto University and the WeeGee complex. The big draw outside the urban districts is **Nuuksio National Park** on the western edge — old forest, lakes, and a network of marked trails fifteen minutes from the metro.

A day card on the Helsinki regional transport (HSL) network covers Espoo and makes hopping between districts straightforward. Most of the cultural sites cluster around Tapiola and Otaniemi.
