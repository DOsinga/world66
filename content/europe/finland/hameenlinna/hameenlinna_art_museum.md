The Hämeenlinna Art Museum occupies a handsome stone building in the city centre and houses a collection focused on Finnish art from the 19th and 20th centuries. The permanent collection includes paintings from the Finnish Golden Age and works by regional artists who responded to the Häme landscape and lake district.

The museum offers temporary exhibitions throughout the year alongside the permanent collection, which gives reason to visit even for those who have been before. The building's well-proportioned exhibition spaces show the works to good advantage. In a city better known for its castle and natural landscape, the art museum is a less expected pleasure — quiet, well-curated, and free of the crowds that gather at the castle on summer weekends.

Combined with the Sibelius childhood home a short walk away, the museum fits naturally into a half-day exploring the cultural history of the city.
