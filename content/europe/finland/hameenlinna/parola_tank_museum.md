Parola Tank Museum is Finland's main armoured warfare museum, with rows of tanks, assault guns, artillery, and support vehicles from Finnish and foreign service. The collection is especially strong on the Winter War, Continuation War, and Cold War decades, when Finland had to think carefully about defence on difficult terrain.

It sits outside central Hämeenlinna, so it needs a bit of planning, but military-history travellers will find it far more substantial than a small local museum. The outdoor displays make the machines' scale obvious, and the Finnish context keeps the visit from becoming just a hardware catalogue.
