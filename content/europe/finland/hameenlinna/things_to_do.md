title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Hämeenlinna's main sights cluster around the lake shore and the old town. [Häme Castle](#hame_castle) is the centrepiece — one of Finland's finest medieval fortifications, sitting directly on the water. A short distance away, the military museums beside the castle walls cover Finnish artillery history in more detail than most visitors expect. [Aulanko Nature Reserve](#aulanko_nature_reserve) offers the best walking in the region, with a hilltop observation tower overlooking an expanse of lake and forest that stretches to the horizon.

For Sibelius pilgrims, the composer's childhood home on Hallituskatu is a small but well-maintained museum that puts the music in the context of the provincial Finnish city where he grew up.
