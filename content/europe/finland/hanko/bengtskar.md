Bengtskär is a bare skerry in the outer archipelago, crowned by the tallest lighthouse in the Nordic countries. The island sits exposed at the mouth of the Gulf of Finland and feels far more remote than its distance from Hanko suggests.

Summer boat trips run from Hanko and Kasnäs in suitable weather. The lighthouse and sea views are the main draw, with the crossing itself part of the experience.
