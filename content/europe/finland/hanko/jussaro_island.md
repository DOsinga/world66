Jussarö is an island south of Hanko with a strange mix of sea views, mining history, and former military use. Iron ore was once mined here, and the Finnish Defence Forces controlled the island for decades before it reopened to visitors.

In season, boats bring travellers to a small guest marina and basic services. Come for the quiet Baltic setting, but leave time to notice the traces of industry and military life that make the island more than just another skerry.
