---

The 1943 water tower is the highest point in Hanko and the best overview of the town, the harbour, and the surrounding archipelago. An observation deck on the upper floor (reached by a lift) gives a 360-degree panorama; on a clear day Estonia is just visible across the Gulf to the south.

Open daily in summer, weekend afternoons only off-season. Small admission fee. The viewing deck includes some history of the tower itself and Hanko's strange role as the Soviet leasehold base from 1940 to 1941. The base of the tower has a small cafe in season.
