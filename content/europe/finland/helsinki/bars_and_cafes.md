---
title: "Bars and Cafes"
type: section
---

Café culture is strong: Finns consume more coffee per head than any nation, and Helsinki's third-wave roasters can hold their own with Stockholm or Berlin. Beer is the everyday drink, with a thriving craft brewing scene (Sori, Mathildedal, Bryggeri Helsinki). Kallio is the centre of the bar scene; the harbourside terraces in summer are the centre of the cocktail scene. Tip: alcohol in shops above 5.5% ABV must be bought at the state Alko monopoly, closed Sundays.
