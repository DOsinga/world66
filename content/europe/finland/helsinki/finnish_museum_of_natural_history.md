The Finnish Museum of Natural History is run by the University of Helsinki and covers Finnish wildlife, world nature, geology, and prehistoric life. Its displays range from local animals to dinosaur skeletons, making it a useful bad-weather stop and a good choice for families.

It sits west of the city centre, within easy walking distance of Kamppi, the Parliament House, and the National Museum.
