Helsinki's city art museum, on the upper floors of the Tennispalatsi (1937), the surviving functionalist tennis hall built for the cancelled 1940 Olympics. The permanent collection covers twentieth-century Finnish art, with a substantial Tove Jansson section — including the original murals she painted for the City Hall and the original Moomin oil paintings rarely seen elsewhere.

A rotating programme of temporary exhibitions occupies most of the building. The on-site Bio Rex cinema in the same building runs an arthouse programme. Closed Mondays.
