Helsinki Synagogue is the centre of the city's Jewish community, a brick synagogue complex west of Kamppi that also includes community facilities, a school, a kindergarten, and a kosher store.

It is not a casual drop-in sight, so contact the community ahead if you hope to visit inside. Even from the street, it adds a useful piece to Helsinki's religious and cultural map.
