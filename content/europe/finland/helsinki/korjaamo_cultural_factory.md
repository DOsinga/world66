Korjaamo Cultural Factory occupies an old tram depot in Toolo and mixes galleries, performance spaces, a cafe-bar, a shop, and the Tram Museum. It is one of Helsinki's more flexible cultural venues, with a programme that can shift from theatre and concerts to exhibitions and film.

The building itself is part of the appeal, especially if you like reused industrial spaces. Check the schedule before making a special trip; without an event or exhibition, it is a pleasant stop rather than a headline sight.
