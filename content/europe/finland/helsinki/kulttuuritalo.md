Kulttuuritalo, the Helsinki Hall of Culture, is one of Alvar Aalto's major 1950s buildings and still a working concert hall. Its sweeping brick auditorium and copper details show Aalto's softer side: modernist, but warm rather than severe.

The hall has hosted everyone from rock bands to classical performers, so the best visit is for a show. Architecture-minded travellers can still appreciate it from the outside while exploring Kallio and the area around Linnanmaki.
