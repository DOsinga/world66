Malminkartanonhuippu is an artificial hill in northwestern Helsinki and one of the highest viewpoints in the city. The climb is straightforward but long, with hundreds of steps leading to open views across Helsinki, Espoo, and Vantaa.

The hill was built from landfill and construction material, which gives it a strange local backstory as well as a practical use: in winter it becomes one of the city's best sledding hills.
