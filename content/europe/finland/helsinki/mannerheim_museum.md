The Mannerheim Museum occupies the villa where Carl Gustaf Emil Mannerheim lived from 1924 to 1951. The rooms have been kept close to how they looked in his lifetime, with military memorabilia, hunting trophies, Asian travel objects and personal possessions arranged in a surprisingly intimate setting.

It is one of Helsinki's better small museums if you are interested in Finland's 20th-century history. Visits are usually guided, which helps, because the house makes most sense when the objects are tied back to Mannerheim's long and complicated public life.
