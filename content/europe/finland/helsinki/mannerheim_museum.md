The Mannerheim Museum occupies the villa where Carl Gustaf Emil Mannerheim lived from 1924 to 1951. Rooms are preserved with his furniture, hunting trophies, military objects and personal collections.

The museum is strongest for visitors interested in Finnish independence, the Second World War and the public image of Mannerheim. Visits are usually by guided tour.
