The Newspaper Museum is a small central museum devoted to Finnish journalism, printing, and the craft of putting out a daily paper. It is not one of Helsinki's headline museums, but it is an easy stop if you are already walking between the Esplanadi, the Design Museum, and the old commercial streets of the city centre.
