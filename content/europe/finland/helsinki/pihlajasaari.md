Pihlajasaari is a pair of small islands off southern Helsinki, popular with locals in summer but easy for visitors to miss. Ferries run from Merisatama, making the trip a quick change of pace from the city streets.

The islands have sandy beaches, rocky shore, walking paths, and a summer restaurant. Come for a swim, a picnic, or a quiet loop through coastal pine and open sea views.
