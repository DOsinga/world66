An island three kilometres west of the centre, linked to the mainland by a wooden footbridge, given over to an open-air museum of historic Finnish buildings. Farms, log houses, churches, windmills, and a working manor have been relocated here from across the country since 1909. In summer, costumed guides demonstrate weaving, baking, and crofting; in winter the buildings are closed but the island remains a popular walking destination.

The island is also wooded enough to feel rural, with a small beach and tame red squirrels that will accept hazelnuts from your hand. Tram 4 from the centre to the Meilahti stop, then a short walk; or bus 24.
