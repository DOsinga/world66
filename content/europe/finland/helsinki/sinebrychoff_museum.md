The Sinebrychoff Museum is Finland's main museum for older European art, set in a handsome 1842 house commissioned by brewery founder Nikolai Sinebrychoff. Upstairs, the house museum preserves a nineteenth-century domestic setting, with paintings and furnishings from the family collection.
