St. John's Church is Helsinki's largest church and one of the city's strongest Gothic Revival landmarks. Its twin spires rise above the south side of the city, and the interior is worth a short look if the doors are open.

The church is close to the Design Museum and Punavuori, so it fits easily into a walk through southern Helsinki.
