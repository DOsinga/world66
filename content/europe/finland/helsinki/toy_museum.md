The Toy Museum is a small museum in an old house on Suomenlinna, with a dense collection of historic toys, dolls, games, and childhood objects. The appeal is quiet and nostalgic rather than flashy, but the displays give useful context on Finnish and European toy history.

It works best as a short add-on to a Suomenlinna visit, especially for families or anyone interested in everyday design and material culture.
