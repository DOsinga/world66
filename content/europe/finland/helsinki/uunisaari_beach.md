Uunisaari Beach sits on a small island just off Helsinki's southern shore. It is close to Kaivopuisto but feels more tucked away, making it a pleasant summer stop for swimming, sun, and views back toward the city.

Access is by a short seasonal boat ride or winter bridge, depending on the time of year. It is best treated as a relaxed add-on to a walk along the southern waterfront.
