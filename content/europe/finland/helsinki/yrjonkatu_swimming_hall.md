Yrjönkatu Swimming Hall is a historic art deco bath house in central Helsinki, with a pool and several saunas. It is one of the city's classic public bathing experiences as well as an architectural stop.

Men's and women's swimming days are separate. Nude bathing is common, though swimsuits are allowed, so check the current schedule and etiquette before visiting.
