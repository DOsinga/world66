Borgberget Observation Tower stands on a hill near Järvis, giving one of the easier elevated views in Houtskär. In a low island landscape, even a modest tower helps you read the pattern of forest, fields, water, and scattered villages.

It is not a major sight on its own, but it makes a good pause while cycling or driving across the main island.
