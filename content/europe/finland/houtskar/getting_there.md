---
title: Getting There
type: section
---

Houtskär is reached from Turku by following the Archipelago Road through Pargas, Nagu, and Korpo, then taking the ferry from Galtby to Kittuis. Ferries are part of the road system, but timetables still matter, especially if you are continuing toward Mossala, Iniö, or Åland.
