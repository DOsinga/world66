Houtskär Church is a red wooden cross church from the early 1700s, set on a small rise in Näsby. It is plain in the best archipelago way: timber, graveyard, bell tower, and sea air rather than grand architecture.

The church is also the natural starting point for short walks around Näsby. From here you can reach the museum, harbour, and local trails without needing a car.
