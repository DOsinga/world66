Hyppeis is one of Houtskär's handsome harbour villages, with red boathouses lined along the water. It gives the classic Archipelago Sea view without much staging: timber sheds, boats, low shore, and wooded islands beyond.

Stop here for the atmosphere rather than a formal attraction. It is a good reminder that Houtskär's appeal is often in its small harbours and ferry-road pauses.
