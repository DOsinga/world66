Jungfruskär is a small group of islands in the Archipelago National Park, southwest of Houtskär's main villages. Meadows, wooded shore, old fishing settlement traces, and rare plants make it one of the richer nature stops in this part of the Archipelago Sea.

Access is by boat, so it is not a casual detour from the road ferries. If you can arrange the trip, it gives Houtskär its wilder edge.
