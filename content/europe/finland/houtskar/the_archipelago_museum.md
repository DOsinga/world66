The Archipelago Museum in Näsby spreads through a cluster of old buildings near the church. Its boats, tools, models, and workshops explain how people made a living from fishing, transport, small farms, and boatbuilding in this broken-up seascape.

It is the best indoor stop in Houtskär, and a useful correction to the easy summer fantasy of the archipelago. Life here was beautiful, but it was also hard work.
