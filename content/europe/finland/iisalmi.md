Iisalmi is a small Upper Savo town between Kuopio and Kajaani, set among lakes and low forested country. It is not a headline destination, but it has enough local identity to justify a day if you are already moving through eastern Finland.

The best thread is history. [Koljonvirta](/europe/finland/iisalmi/koljonvirta) was the site of an important battle in the Finnish War, and the nearby [Juhani Aho Museum](/europe/finland/iisalmi/juhani_aho_museum) ties the same landscape to one of Finland's major writers. In town, [Gustav Adolf Church](/europe/finland/iisalmi/gustav_adolf_church) gives an older wooden-church anchor.

Iisalmi also has a pleasingly specific modern claim: Olvi, founded in 1878, is Finland's last large independent brewery. The [Olvi Brewery Museum](/europe/finland/iisalmi/olvi_brewery_museum) is not a reason to cross the country, but combined with the battlefield and literary museum it gives the town a clear, local flavour.
