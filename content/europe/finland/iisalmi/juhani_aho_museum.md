The Juhani Aho Museum occupies the writer's childhood home at the old Koljonvirta parsonage. Aho became one of Finland's first professional writers, and the museum keeps the 19th-century setting close to the landscape that shaped him.

The courtyard buildings are part of the visit: granaries, outbuildings, sauna, and other pieces of the old parsonage world. It pairs naturally with the Koljonvirta history path.
