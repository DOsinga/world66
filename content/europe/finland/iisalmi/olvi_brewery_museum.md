The Olvi Brewery Museum tells the story of beer-making in Iisalmi and of Olvi, founded in 1878 and still a major independent Finnish brewery. The collection covers brewing equipment, bottles, labels, advertising, and the small industrial details that usually disappear.

It is a niche museum, but a good one if you like local food-and-drink history. Check opening times carefully, as regular hours can be limited.
