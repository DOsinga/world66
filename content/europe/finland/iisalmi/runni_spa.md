Runni Spa is one of Finland's older spa sites still in use, known for baths and a quiet countryside setting near Iisalmi. It is mainly worthwhile if you want a slow wellness stop or are already travelling through the area.

Check current opening arrangements before going, as spa services and public access can vary more than at ordinary attractions.
