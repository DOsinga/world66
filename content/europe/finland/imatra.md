Imatra is a South Karelian city where the Vuoksi River drops out of Lake Saimaa in a roar of rock, water, and old tourist memory. The town is industrial in parts and close to the Russian border, but its fame comes from [Imatrankoski Rapids](/europe/finland/imatra/imatrankoski_rapids), a sight that drew aristocrats, artists, and ordinary Finnish holidaymakers long before modern tourism had a name.

The rapids no longer run freely all day; hydroelectric works changed that in the 20th century. When the gates open for scheduled shows, the old drama returns, and the surrounding [Kruununpuisto](/europe/finland/imatra/kruununpuisto_park) makes the setting worth seeing even between releases.

Imatra pairs well with [Lappeenranta](/europe/finland/lappeenranta) and the wider [Lakeland](/europe/finland/lakeland). Come for the rapids, but leave time for the castle-like Valtionhotelli, Alvar Aalto's [Church of the Three Crosses](/europe/finland/imatra/church_of_the_three_crosses), and a walk along the Vuoksi.
