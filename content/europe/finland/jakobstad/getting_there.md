---
title: "Getting There"
type: section
---

Jakobstad is on the Gulf of Bothnia coast, about 100 km north of Vaasa and 150 km south of Oulu. The nearest airport is Kokkola-Pietarsaari Airport (KOK), about 25 km south, with connections to Helsinki. By train, the closest main-line station is in Kauhava or Kokkola; a bus or taxi covers the remaining distance. Direct bus services run from Helsinki (around 5 hours) and Vaasa (around 1.5 hours).
