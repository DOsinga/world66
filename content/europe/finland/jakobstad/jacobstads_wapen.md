Jacobstads Wapen is a full-scale replica of an 18th-century sailing vessel, built to honour Jakobstad's maritime history. For a small Ostrobothnian town, that history matters: shipbuilding, trade, and the Gulf of Bothnia shaped local wealth and horizons.

The vessel is most interesting when seen together with the harbour and the town's older wooden quarters. It gives a tangible form to a past that can otherwise feel abstract, especially for visitors arriving by road with little sense of how connected these coastal towns once were by sea.
