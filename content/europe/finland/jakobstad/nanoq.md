Nanoq is an Arctic museum near Jakobstad, built around polar history, northern hunting culture, and expedition stories. Its timber buildings and specialist collections make it more distinctive than the town's size would suggest.

The museum is seasonal, so check opening times carefully. It is most rewarding for travellers interested in Finland's northern connections rather than only its coastal Swedish-speaking towns.
