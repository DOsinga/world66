Jurmo's beaches are not resort beaches. They are low Baltic cobble shores, open to wind and weather, with the most visited stretch between the harbour and Högberget.

They are worth time because they show the island's character so clearly: bare stone, shallow water, and long views across the outer archipelago. In calm weather they are good places to sit; in rough weather they explain why island life here was never easy.
