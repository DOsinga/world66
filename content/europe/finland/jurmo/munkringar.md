The Munkringar, or "monk rings", are four stone circles on the northern shore of eastern Jurmo. No one knows who made them, when they were made, or exactly what they were for.

That uncertainty is the point. They resemble the temporary dwelling foundations known around the Baltic, except those are normally rectangular. The circles are spaced along the shore, giving a walk here the feeling of crossing a half-readable archaeological landscape.
