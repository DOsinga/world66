The Nature Hut stands by the harbour, so it is an easy first stop after the ferry. It explains why Jurmo's bare heath, cobble shores, and low vegetation are so unusual within the Archipelago Sea, with material on rare species and the island's human history.

There is no staff, but the exhibition is useful before heading out on the trails. It also gives context for the nature reserve rules, which matter here more than in a normal village walk.
