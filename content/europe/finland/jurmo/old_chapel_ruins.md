East of the Munkringar are low rectangular stone remains believed to belong to Jurmo's earliest chapel. The identification is not certain, but the site adds another layer to the island's sparse archaeological record.

There is not much structure left. The reward is the setting: a remote shore, old stones, and the sense that religion, seafaring, and survival were once tightly bound together here.
