Oravivuori Arc Point is one of Finland's stations on the UNESCO-listed Struve Geodetic
Arc, the nineteenth-century survey chain that helped measure the shape of the Earth. The
science is the hook, but the visit is also a good excuse for a short forest walk and a
broad view over Finnish lake country.

The site is modest: a marked point, interpretive information, and a lookout rather than
a conventional attraction. That understatement is part of its charm. It suits travelers
who like geography, maps, and places where a small marker connects a quiet hill to a
much larger world.
