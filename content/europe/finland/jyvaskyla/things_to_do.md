title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Jyväskylä's identity is built on two things: Alvar Aalto's architecture and its lakes. The [Alvar Aalto Museum](/europe/finland/jyvaskyla/alvar_aalto_museum) is the essential starting point, but to understand the scale of Aalto's presence here, walk through the [University of Jyväskylä campus](/europe/finland/jyvaskyla/university_of_jyvaskyla), where his buildings span three decades of development. The day trip to [Säynätsalo Town Hall](/europe/finland/jyvaskyla/saynatsalo_town_hall) is essential for anyone serious about architecture.

For a change of pace, [Harju Park](/europe/finland/jyvaskyla/harju_park) offers a forested ridge walk above the city. [Jyväsjärvi](/europe/finland/jyvaskyla/jyvasjarvi) lake sits in the city centre and is excellent for walking and cycling in summer, skating in winter. The [Craft Museum of Finland](/europe/finland/jyvaskyla/craft_museum_of_finland) and [Museum of Central Finland](/europe/finland/jyvaskyla/museum_of_central_finland) provide deeper context on Finnish culture and regional history. [Taulumäki Church](/europe/finland/jyvaskyla/taulamaki_church) is worth visiting for its frescoes and its architectural interest.
