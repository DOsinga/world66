Toivola Old Courtyard preserves a pocket of wooden Jyväskylä, with restored nineteenth-century buildings gathered around a calm yard. Craft shops, cafés, and small displays make it more than a static heritage corner, especially in summer or around seasonal markets.

It is an easy stop in the centre and pairs well with the city's Alvar Aalto sights, because it shows an older, smaller Jyväskylä before modernist brick and campus buildings took over the story. Come for coffee, browsing, and a gentler sense of local history.
