Ysitien Lemmikki Zoo is a small family-oriented animal park in Leppalahti, near Jyvaskyla. The collection is mostly domestic and farm animals, with ponies, llamas, goats, sheep, rabbits, peacocks, reindeer, and similar animals.

It is a simple outing rather than a major zoo, but it can be useful for families looking for an easy stop outside the city.
