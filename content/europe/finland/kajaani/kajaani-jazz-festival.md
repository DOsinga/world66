The Kajaani Jazz Festival has been running since 1971, making it one of the oldest jazz events in Finland. It takes place in spring, typically in late April or early May, and draws performers and audiences from across the country. The venues are mostly indoor — clubs, halls, and a few outdoor stages when the weather permits — and the programming tends toward mainstream and traditional jazz rather than the more experimental end of the spectrum.

For a town of Kajaani's size, the festival punches above its weight in terms of the names it attracts. It has built a reputation over decades and has a loyal following that returns year after year. If your visit coincides with the festival, it is worth checking the programme in advance, as popular events sell out. Outside of festival season the town is quiet in the evenings, so timing a visit to overlap with it gives a very different impression of the place.
