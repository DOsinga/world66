Kaustinen is a small Central Ostrobothnian village with an identity much larger than its population. Its fiddle tradition is not museum folklore but a living local practice, strong enough to be inscribed by UNESCO as intangible cultural heritage.

The village is at its best during the [Kaustinen Folk Music Festival](/europe/finland/kaustinen/kaustinen_folk_music_festival), when the centre fills with concerts, dance, workshops, and musicians from Finland and abroad. Outside festival week, [Folk Arts Centre Kaustinen](/europe/finland/kaustinen/folk_arts_centre_kaustinen) keeps the tradition visible through performances, exhibitions, archives, and the folk music institute.

Kaustinen is not a broad sightseeing town. Its case is narrower and stronger: if you care about music, living tradition, or rural Finnish culture, it is one of the few villages in Finland whose name points to a whole sound.
