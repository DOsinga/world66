Folk Arts Centre Kaustinen is the year-round anchor for the village's music culture. It houses performance spaces, exhibitions, folk music institutions, and festival offices in a modern building close to the festival area.

Even outside July, this is where Kaustinen's identity is easiest to grasp. Check opening times before making a special trip, as the rhythm is quieter outside events.
