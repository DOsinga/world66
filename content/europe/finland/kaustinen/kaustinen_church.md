Kaustinen Church is a plain wooden parish church near the village centre. Its current appearance dates from 1859, and it fits the rural Ostrobothnian landscape better than a showier landmark would.

It is a short stop rather than a destination on its own. Pair it with the churchyard museums if you are walking through the village outside festival week.
