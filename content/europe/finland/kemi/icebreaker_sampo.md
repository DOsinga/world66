Sampo is Kemi's exceptional anchor: a former icebreaker turned tourist vessel that heads onto the frozen Bothnian Bay in winter. The standard trip combines the ship itself, walking on sea ice, and floating in a bright survival suit in a channel cut through the ice.

It is expensive and seasonal, but it is also genuinely distinctive. If Kemi is worth building a journey around, Sampo is the reason.
