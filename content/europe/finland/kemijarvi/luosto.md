Luosto is a small fell resort in Finnish Lapland, about 70 kilometres south of Kemijärvi and right against Pyhä-Luosto National Park. It is a quiet place, more forest and snow than village, with a handful of hotels and cabins gathered below the slopes of Luostotunturi.

The headline attraction is [Lampivaara Amethyst Mine](/europe/finland/kemijarvi/luosto/lampivaara_amethyst_mine), where visitors can dig a small amethyst from ancient bedrock and keep it. Around it are the open fell views and marked routes of [Pyhä-Luosto National Park](/europe/finland/kemijarvi/pyha_luosto_national_park), including the [Ukko-Luosto summit trail](/europe/finland/kemijarvi/luosto/ukko_luosto_summit).

In winter, [Luosto Ski Resort](/europe/finland/kemijarvi/luosto/luosto_ski_resort) is modest but appealing: short queues, reliable snow, reindeer in the forest, and cross-country tracks running between Luosto and Pyhä. It is not a big resort town, and that is the appeal. Come if you want Lapland on a human scale rather than a packed activity hub.
