Suomu is the local fell that gives Kemijarvi a proper winter-sports outlet without the scale or bustle of Finland's biggest resorts. The slopes sit by the Arctic Circle in quiet forest country, and the mountain works best for travellers who want skiing, snow, and Lapland scenery rather than a busy resort village.

The ski area is seasonal, so check lift operations before building a trip around it. Outside ski days, the fell still gives a useful reason to head east of town for snowshoeing, winter views, and a sense of how quickly Kemijarvi opens into wilderness.
