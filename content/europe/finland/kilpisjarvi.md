Kilpisjärvi is Finland's only real alpine village, a tiny settlement in the far northwestern "arm" of Lapland where the country presses against Norway and Sweden. It sits beside a cold clear lake, with the blunt profile of [Saana](/europe/finland/kilpisjarvi/saana) rising straight behind the houses.

The village itself is small, but the setting is big enough to justify the long road north. From here you can climb Saana, walk through [Malla Strict Nature Reserve](/europe/finland/kilpisjarvi/malla_strict_nature_reserve), take a boat-and-hike trip to the [Three-Country Cairn](/europe/finland/kilpisjarvi/three_country_cairn), or follow short trails to waterfalls and fell viewpoints. It feels less like a conventional resort than a borderland base camp.

Kilpisjärvi is at its easiest in summer, when trails are open and the midnight sun keeps the lake bright late into the night. Winter is harsher and more specialised, but also powerful: polar night, deep snow, ski routes, snowmobile tracks, and a good chance of northern lights when the sky clears.
