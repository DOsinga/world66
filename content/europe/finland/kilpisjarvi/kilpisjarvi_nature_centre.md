The Kilpisjärvi Nature Centre is the practical first stop before heading onto the trails. It has maps, route advice, permits, and exhibitions on the local nature, Sámi culture, Saana, and Malla Strict Nature Reserve.

Opening days vary by season, so check before relying on it. When it is open, it is worth using even if you already know where you are walking.
