Saana is the fell that defines Kilpisjärvi. Its steep, sloping shoulder rises over the village and lake, and the climb to the top gives one of the clearest views in Finland: Malla, Sweden, Norway, and the long water of Kilpisjärvi all laid out below.

The standard trail is about 8 km return and takes roughly three to four hours. The path is clear, but the climb is real and the summit can be cold and windy even when the village feels calm. Good footwear matters.
