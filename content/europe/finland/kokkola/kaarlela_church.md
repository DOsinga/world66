Kaarlela Church is the older, quieter counterpoint to Kokkola's wooden-town charm. The grey stone church dates from the medieval period and still sits in its old parish landscape, with thick walls, a steep roof, and an interior that feels far removed from the busier harbour town nearby.

Come for the sense of continuity rather than spectacle. The church is especially worthwhile if you are already exploring Neristan and want to understand how far back the settlement story goes; it shows the rural parish roots that existed before Kokkola became a trading town.
