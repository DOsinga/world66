Korpo, Korppoo in Finnish, is one of the old island municipalities of the Archipelago Sea west of Turku. The main island is big enough for villages, fields and forest, but small enough that the sea keeps appearing between trees, ferry ramps and guest harbours.

This is Swedish-speaking Finland at its most island-minded. Most travellers arrive along the Archipelago Road, crossing by bridges and free road ferries through Pargas and Nagu before the last hop to Korpo. Others come by boat, because the fairways from the Baltic and Aland pass close by.

Korpo is not a single blockbuster sight. Its pull is the combination: the medieval [Korpo Church](/europe/finland/korpo/korpo_church), the marina and exhibitions at [Korpostrom](/europe/finland/korpo/archipelago_centre_korpostrom), the ferry mood at [Galtby Harbour](/europe/finland/korpo/galtby_harbour), and the feeling that the road system is only a polite interruption in a world made mostly of water.
