Archipelago Centre Korpostrom sits by the water south of the main village. It combines a guest marina, exhibitions and summer activities that lean into the biology and history of the Archipelago Sea.

It is one of the best places on Korpo to slow down over lunch, look at boats and remember that this region is an inhabited seascape, not just pretty islands on a map.
