Galtby Harbour is not a polished attraction, but it captures Korpo's geography better than many prettier places. Ferries leave from here toward Houtskar, Norrskata and Aland, while summer traffic gathers around the quay with bicycles, cars and island supplies.

Come for the practical crossing, or just for the sense of how people move through this broken coast.
