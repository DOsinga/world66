Korpo Church is the main historic sight on the island, built in the early 15th century and still looking properly medieval. Inside are old murals, carved wooden figures and an unusual apsidal balcony, a rare survivor in the Nordic countries.

It stands in the parish village, so it is easy to pair with a walk to the small shops and harbour roads nearby.
