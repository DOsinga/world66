Korpo Local History Museum is modest, seasonal and exactly the sort of place that helps a small island make sense. Its collections cover fishing, crafts, household tools and the practical work of living in the archipelago.

Check opening days before going; outside the short summer season, you may only get the exterior and a quiet walk nearby.
