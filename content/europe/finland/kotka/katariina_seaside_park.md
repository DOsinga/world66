Katariina Seaside Park is one of Kotka's strongest outdoor stops, spreading across a coastal point with lawns, paths, sea views, and family-friendly play areas. The park also preserves traces of older fortifications, so it feels more layered than a simple waterfront promenade.

It works well for an unhurried walk in almost any season. For visitors using Kotka as a maritime town rather than just a transit stop, the park gives a clear sense of the city's relationship with the Gulf of Finland.
