title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Kotka's defining attraction is [Langinkoski Imperial Fishing Lodge](/europe/finland/kotka/langinkoski), a Tsarist-era log cabin built for Alexander III on the rapids of the Kymijoki river — a genuinely evocative place to spend an afternoon. In town, the [Maritime Centre Vellamo](/europe/finland/kotka/maritime_centre_vellamo) is the most architecturally striking building on the waterfront, housing three museums under its wave-shaped roof, and [Maretarium](/europe/finland/kotka/maretarium) presents Finnish freshwater fish in naturalistic tanks along the Sapokka bay.

Kotka's waterfront and archipelago are the other main draws: the harbour area makes for good walking, and boat trips out into the island-scattered coastline run in summer. The Kotka-Hamina coastline is one of the wilder parts of the Gulf of Finland archipelago and worth exploring by kayak or on the ferries that run to the outer islands.
