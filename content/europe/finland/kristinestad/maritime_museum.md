The Maritime Museum is a compact collection on Kristinestad's shipbuilding, sailing and coastal trade. It helps explain why this quiet town has so many merchant houses, old storehouses and stories tied to the sea.

Come here after walking the centre rather than before it. The objects are modest, but they give context to the harbour, the bay and the town's Swedish-speaking sailing culture.
