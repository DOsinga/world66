The Great Cemetery was founded in 1867 and is one of Kuopio's older burial grounds. Its best-known grave is that of Minna Canth, the writer and social reformer who remains one of Finland's major literary figures.

The cemetery is a quiet stop for travelers interested in Finnish literature and local history rather than a place to rush through.
