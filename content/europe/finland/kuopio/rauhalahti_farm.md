Rauhalahti Farm is a small animal park on the south side of Kuopio, useful for families who want an easy half-day away from the city centre. The appeal is simple and local: domestic animals, rural buildings, and space for children to move around without the scale or expense of a theme park.

It works best as part of a Rauhalahti outing rather than as a stand-alone reason to visit Kuopio. Pair it with the lakeside paths, swimming areas, or sauna culture nearby, and check opening days before going because the farm is seasonal.
