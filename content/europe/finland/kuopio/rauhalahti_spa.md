Rauhalahti Spa is a lakeside pool and sauna complex south of central Kuopio. It has indoor and outdoor pools, a jacuzzi, an ice-cold pool, a children's pool, a waterslide, saunas, and a steam room, making it useful on cold or wet days and for families who need a break from sightseeing.
