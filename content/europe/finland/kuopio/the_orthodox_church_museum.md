The Orthodox Church Museum in Kuopio is Finland's key collection for Orthodox religious art, vestments, icons and church objects. It reflects the eastern strand of Finnish history, especially the Karelian and borderland traditions that are easy to miss in a Lutheran-majority country.

The museum is calm, focused and more beautiful than its modest size might suggest. It pairs well with Kuopio Museum for travellers who want to understand the cultural mix of eastern Finland rather than only the lake scenery.
