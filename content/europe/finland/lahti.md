Lahti is a city of about 120,000 people in southern Finland, roughly 100 km north of [Helsinki](/europe/finland/helsinki) — an hour by the fast train. It sits at the southern end of a lake system and at the foot of the Salpausselkä ridge, which gives the city its identity as a winter sports hub.

The [Lahti ski jumps](/europe/finland/lahti/ski_jumps) are visible from across the city and host the annual Lahti Ski Games, one of the biggest Nordic skiing events on the calendar. The [Sibelius Hall](/europe/finland/lahti/sibelius_hall), an architecturally striking concert venue built mostly of glass and timber, hosts the Sinfonia Lahti orchestra. The [town hall by Eliel Saarinen](/europe/finland/lahti/lahti_town_hall) and [Alvar Aalto's Church of the Cross](/europe/finland/lahti/ristinkirkko) add a small but well-curated trail of 20th-century architecture. For city breaks, Lahti is less compelling than Tampere or Turku, but as a base for [the lake district](/europe/finland/lahti/vesijarvi) it's practical and easy to reach.
