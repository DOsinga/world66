Ankkuri Beach sits by Lake Vesijärvi in Lahti's harbour-side Ankkuri district, giving the city a simple summer swimming spot close to the Sibelius Hall and waterfront paths. It is a local beach rather than a resort, with the appeal of clean lake water, open sky, and easy access from central Lahti.

Come in warm weather with modest expectations: a towel, a swim, and perhaps a walk along the harbour afterwards. The beach is most useful for travellers already exploring the Vesijärvi waterfront, where Lahti's industrial edge has been softened by marinas, cafés, and cultural buildings.
