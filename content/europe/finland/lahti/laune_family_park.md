Laune Family Park is Lahti's easiest stop with young children. The park has a miniature traffic town with bicycles and scooters, climbing structures, water play, and plenty of grass where adults can take a breather while children burn off energy.

It is not a grand sight, but it is genuinely useful if you are travelling as a family. Bring snacks and treat it as a relaxed break between museums or lakeside walks.
