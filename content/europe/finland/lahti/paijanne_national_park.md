Paijanne National Park protects a lake landscape of islands, ridges, beaches, and clear water south of Finland's second-largest lake. For Lahti travelers, it offers a more expansive nature experience than the city's local parks, especially in summer when boat access opens up the island scenery.

The park takes planning because much of its character is tied to water access. Check routes, ferry or boat options, and seasonal conditions before going; with the right logistics, it is a strong day trip for hiking, paddling, swimming, and seeing Finnish lake country at a quieter scale.
