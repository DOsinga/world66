Alvar Aalto's Church of the Cross is one of the more interesting modern churches in Finland — a white asymmetric building with a sweeping curved roof and an interior dominated by a free-standing concrete cross that appears to hover above the altar.

The church is normally open in daylight hours outside services. There are regular concerts (the organ is excellent) and a small but striking bell tower beside the entrance. It sits across the street from the central market square, an easy stop on any walking route.
