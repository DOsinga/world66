St Michael's Church is Laitila's strongest single building, a medieval stone church completed in the late 15th century. Inside are painted vaults, a 15th-century crucifix, and the plain solidity of a rural Finnish parish church that has outlasted almost everything around it. Check opening times before making a special stop.
