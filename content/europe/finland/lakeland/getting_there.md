---
title: "Getting There"
type: section
---

The Lakeland's cities are well connected by train from Helsinki. Savonlinna is about 4 hours by train; Mikkeli about 2 hours; Lappeenranta about 2.5 hours; Kuopio about 4 hours on direct services. VR (Finnish Railways) operates regular services, and the trains run to time. Driving from Helsinki takes 3–5 hours depending on the destination.

Savonlinna has a small airport with seasonal flights from Helsinki (Finnair operates in summer), though the train is usually more convenient. Tampere, at the western edge of the Lakeland, has good connections from Helsinki by both train and bus. Once in the region, having a car or renting a bicycle significantly expands your options, as the most scenic areas and national parks are not well served by public transport.
