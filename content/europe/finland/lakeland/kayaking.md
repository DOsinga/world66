Kayaking is the best way to experience the Lakeland's maze of islands and waterways, and the flat water of the Saimaa system makes it accessible to beginners. Rental kayaks and canoes are available from several bases around the lake — Savonlinna, Lappeenranta, and the Linnansaari National Park are among the most popular starting points. Multi-day routes allow you to camp on islands and cover substantial distances through archipelago scenery.

The Saimaa system is genuinely vast, and paddlers can choose from routes ranging from a half-day circuit near a town to a two-week wilderness traverse. Route planning should account for the distances between supply points: in some sections, you can paddle for a full day without passing a shop. The national park camping sites on designated islands are free; open fires are permitted in designated hearths. Late June to August is the main paddling season; water temperatures are warm enough for a swim in July, and the long light evenings extend the time on the water.
