Kerimäki Church, about 23 kilometres east of Savonlinna, holds the distinction of being the largest wooden church in the world. Built in 1847 in the Neoclassical style, it has an interior capacity of 5,000 people — remarkable for a wooden structure and extraordinary for its rural location. The pale yellow building stands on a hill above the small village of Kerimäki with an effect that is genuinely surreal: a structure of cathedral proportions in a setting of lake, forest, and scattered farmhouses.

The church is a working Lutheran parish. It is open to visitors during summer months, and services are still held here, though the building is rarely full. The interior is light and plain in the Lutheran manner, with the scale creating an unusual atmosphere of grandeur combined with simplicity. The hilltop location provides good views across the surrounding lakeland.
