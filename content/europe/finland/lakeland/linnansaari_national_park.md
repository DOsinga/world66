Linnansaari National Park protects an archipelago of about 130 islands in Lake Haukivesi, a section of the Saimaa system northeast of Savonlinna. The park was established partly to protect the Saimaa ringed seal, which breeds on the ice of the archipelago in winter, and partly to preserve a representative section of the Saimaa lakeland landscape. The islands range from rocky outcrops to forested larger islands with sandy shores.

The park is accessible only by water: ferries run from the mainland to the main island of Linnansaari in summer, and rental boats and kayaks are available from several points on the shore. Canoeing is the definitive way to explore the archipelago — paddling between islands, camping on designated sites, and watching for osprey and white-tailed eagles overhead. The park has no roads; even its paths are limited, which gives it a genuinely remote feel despite being only 30 kilometres from Savonlinna.
