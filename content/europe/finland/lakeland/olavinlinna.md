Olavinlinna is a medieval castle on a small island in the Saimaa lake system at the edge of Savonlinna. Built in 1475, it is one of the best-preserved medieval castles in Scandinavia and the northernmost medieval castle in the world. Three of the original towers survive, along with substantial sections of the curtain wall, creating an authentic rather than heavily restored atmosphere.

The castle houses a small museum covering its history from Swedish frontier fortress through Russian-era modifications to its current role as a cultural monument. Guided tours are available in multiple languages throughout the summer season. Access is by bridge from the town centre; the castle is visible from across the Saimaa waterways and has been the subject of landscape painters since the 19th century.
