---
title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

The Lakeland rewards those who slow down. The defining activities are water-based: kayaking or canoeing through the archipelago, cruising on the historic lake steamers that still run scheduled routes between towns, swimming from a sauna jetty, and cycling the quiet roads through forested countryside between lakes.

For those wanting cultural anchors, the Savonlinna Opera Festival in July transforms the medieval Olavinlinna Castle into one of Finland's most celebrated cultural events. The Lusto Forest Museum near Punkaharju is the best introduction to the culture of Finnish forests — a subject that is far more central to Finnish identity than it might sound from abroad.
