---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

Summer (June to August) is the classic time for the Lakeland. Days are very long — around the solstice, true darkness never arrives in this latitude — and the combination of warm water, white nights, and a landscape entirely green and reflective is the experience the region is famous for. July is the peak month and coincides with the Savonlinna Opera Festival; book accommodation well ahead for that period.

Winter has its own appeal. The lakes freeze by January, cross-country ski tracks are maintained through the forests, and the smoke saunas and ice swimming culture comes into its own. The scenery is stripped and dramatic — black trees against white ice, the frozen lake stretching to a distant shore. Cross-country skiing conditions are generally good from January through March.

The shoulder seasons (May, September) offer lower prices, fewer crowds, and the particular beauty of the Finnish autumn in September, when the birch leaves turn gold against the dark lake water.
