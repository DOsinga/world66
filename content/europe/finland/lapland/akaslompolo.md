*Europe > Finland > Lapland > Äkäslompolo*

Äkäslompolo is a small Lapland village on the north side of Ylläs, built around a lake and a serious outdoor habit. It is not the place for urban charm. It is the place for ski tracks leaving from the village, fell views across the water, and easy access into Pallas-Yllästunturi National Park.

Winter is the obvious season, when cross-country trails and downhill lifts make the village feel much bigger than its population. Summer and autumn work too, especially if you want hiking, mountain biking, cloudberry marshes, and the ruska colours that briefly set the fells on fire.

The village is quieter than Levi and less polished than a purpose-built alpine resort. That is the point. Äkäslompolo is for travelers who want a practical base with the landscape close at hand.
