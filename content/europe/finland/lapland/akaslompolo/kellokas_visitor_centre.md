Kellokas is the sensible first stop if you are using Äkäslompolo for Pallas-Yllästunturi National Park. It has maps, route advice, exhibitions on local nature and culture, and the practical details that matter before you start walking or skiing.

It is also useful on bad-weather days. The exhibits will not replace a day on the fell, but they make the surrounding landscape easier to read once the clouds lift.
