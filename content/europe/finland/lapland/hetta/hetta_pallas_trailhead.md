The Hetta-Pallas route is one of Finland's classic long-distance hikes, crossing from Hetta into the rounded fells of Pallas-Yllästunturi National Park. The traditional start involves crossing Ounasjärvi from the village side before climbing into open fell country.

Most travellers need several days for the full route, but the trailhead matters even on a short visit. It makes Hetta feel like a threshold: behind you are the village shops and road signs, ahead is a chain of fells where weather, huts, and map-reading decide the day.
