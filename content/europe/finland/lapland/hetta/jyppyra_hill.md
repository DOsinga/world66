Jyppyrä Hill gives Hetta its easiest proper view. The marked loop starts by the Fell Lapland Nature Centre and climbs through birch and pine to an open lookout over Ounasjärvi and the long line of Pallas-Ounas fells.

The walk is short enough for a spare hour, but it feels much wilder than the distance suggests, especially in winter when the trail becomes a snowshoe route. There is a simple shelter near the top; bring matches and something warm if you plan to linger.
