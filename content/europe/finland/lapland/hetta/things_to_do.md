---
title: Things to Do
type: section
---

Hetta's best sights begin at the village edge. Start at the nature centre, climb Jyppyrä for the view, and use the lake and trailheads to understand why this small place has been a northern gateway for hikers, skiers, reindeer herders, and Sámi families.

The long Hetta-Pallas hike needs planning, but you do not need a multi-day trek to enjoy the place. A spare day is enough for the nature centre, a hill walk, and a slow look at Ounasjärvi and the fells beyond it.
