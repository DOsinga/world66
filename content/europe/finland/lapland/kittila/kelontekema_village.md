Kelontekemä is a small backcountry village that escaped the destruction that reached many Lapland settlements during the Second World War. Its appeal is quiet rather than monumental: old rural buildings, forested surroundings, and a sense of how remote Kittilä's villages were before road connections improved.

Come as a short detour if you are exploring the countryside around Kittilä, especially during the village music festival in July.
