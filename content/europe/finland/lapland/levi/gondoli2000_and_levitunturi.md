The Gondoli2000 lift is one of the simplest ways to get above the resort and see why Levi works. From the fell you get the sweep of ski runs, forest, and low Lapland horizons without needing a long hike.

In winter it is part of the ski area; outside the snow season it is useful for views and access to trails when operating. The experience is short, but the sense of place is strong.
