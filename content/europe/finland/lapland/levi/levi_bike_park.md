Levi Bike Park gives the resort a real summer use rather than leaving it as an empty ski village. Lift-served downhill routes and mapped bike trails make the fell accessible when the snow is gone.

It is still a managed resort experience, but it is a good one if you want movement, views, and a reason to stay in Levi outside peak winter.
