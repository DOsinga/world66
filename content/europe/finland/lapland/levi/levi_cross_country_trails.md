Cross-country skiing is the quieter, more Lapland way to use Levi. The maintained track network reaches far beyond the resort core, with forest sections, open fell edges, and enough kilometres to suit both first-timers and strong skiers.

Pick a short loop if you are new to the sport. Distances and cold feel different here, especially when the light is low and the temperature drops fast.
