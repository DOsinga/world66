Muonio sits in western [Finnish Lapland](/europe/finland/lapland), on the Muonio River and the Swedish border. The village is small, but the municipality reaches into serious fell country: Pallas-Yllastunturi National Park, Särkitunturi, old fishing grounds, and lakes that still carry Sami sacred stories.

This is not a town for urban pleasures. Come for long winter darkness, northern lights, cross-country ski tracks, and summer days that barely end. The river valley has been inhabited for thousands of years, and the surrounding forest hides old sieidi stones and saivo lakes that were holy places in Sami belief.

Muonio also has one of Lapland's more quietly strange modern lives. Car makers use the area for winter testing, often behind fences and secrecy, while travelers use the same climate for skiing, snowshoeing, canoeing, and long walks above the tree line.
