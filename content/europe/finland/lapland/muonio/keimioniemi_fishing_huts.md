These old fishing huts stand on Lake Jerisjärvi, where local families used the shore as a fishing base for centuries. The oldest surviving huts date from the 1700s, and the site is protected as a nationally significant built environment.

The appeal is quiet rather than dramatic: weathered timber, lake light, and a clear sense of how people made a living here before tourism and winter testing arrived.
