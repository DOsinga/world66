The Battle of Pelkosenniemi Memorial stands north of the parish village near the rivers that shaped the fighting in December 1939. It is a sober roadside stop rather than a large battlefield park.

The value is in the setting. In winter, with the forest dark and the roads quiet, the story of a Lapland defensive battle becomes much easier to imagine. Pair it with the nearby Soviet soldiers' memorial if you want a fuller sense of the losses on both sides.
