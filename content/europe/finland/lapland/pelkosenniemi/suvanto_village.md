Suvanto is the most atmospheric village stop in Pelkosenniemi. Its old wooden buildings, quiet lanes, and river setting give a rare glimpse of rural Lapland before post-war rebuilding changed the look of the north.

There is not much to "do" here in the usual sense, and that is the reason to go. Walk gently, respect private homes, and let the village work as a piece of living landscape rather than an open-air museum.
