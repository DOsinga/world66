Posio sits in the southeast corner of Finnish Lapland, where the landscape is more lake country and wooded fell than open Arctic tundra. It is a small place, but it has a clear identity: Pentik ceramics, clear-water summer lakes, and two of Lapland's better nature days within reach.

The strongest reason to come is outdoors. [Korouoma Nature Reserve](/europe/finland/lapland/posio/korouoma_nature_reserve) is a deep canyon with cliffs, short day trails, and frozen waterfalls in winter. [Riisitunturi National Park](/europe/finland/lapland/posio/riisitunturi_national_park) is gentler but no less memorable, especially when snow turns the spruce trees into heavy white shapes on the fell slopes.

Posio also has a softer, handmade side. [Pentik-maki Culture Centre](/europe/finland/lapland/posio/pentik_maki_culture_centre) grew from Anu Pentik's ceramics company and gives the town a design stop that feels very different from the usual Lapland souvenir shop. In summer, Lake Livojarvi adds sandy beaches and clear water; in winter, the rhythm shifts to skiing, snowshoeing, and photographing the forests.
