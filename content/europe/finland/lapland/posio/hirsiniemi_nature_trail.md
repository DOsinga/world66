Hirsiniemi Nature Trail is a 16-km loop around Pernunselkä, part of Lake Livojärvi. The route passes sandy beaches, forested shorelines, and several campfire sites.

The trail starts from the Hirsiniemi parking area and crosses near the holiday village Livohka by boat. It is best for visitors with their own transport, as there is no public transport and the access road can be rough.
