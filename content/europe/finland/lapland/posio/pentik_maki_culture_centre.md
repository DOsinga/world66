Pentik-maki is the most distinctive indoor stop in Posio. Pentik began here as a ceramics and interior design company, and the culture centre gathers the shop, exhibitions, and factory-world story into one easy visit.

It is worth a stop even if you are mainly in Posio for nature. The ceramics give the town a specific identity, and the best pieces feel rooted in northern Finland rather than generic Lapland branding.
