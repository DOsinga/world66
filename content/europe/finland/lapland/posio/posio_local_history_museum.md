Posio Local History Museum preserves old buildings and everyday objects from the Posio area. The main building dates from 1895, and the collection is most useful for visitors who want a grounded look at rural life in this part of Lapland.

Visits are modest in scale rather than polished, but guided tours can help make sense of the buildings and household items.
