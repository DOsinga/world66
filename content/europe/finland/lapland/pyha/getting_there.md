---
title: Getting There
type: section
---

Pyhä is reached by road from Kemijärvi, Rovaniemi, and Sodankylä. Seasonal and daily coach links connect the resort with Rovaniemi and Kemijärvi, including airport and railway connections at busy times.

If you come by train from southern Finland, the usual railheads are Rovaniemi and Kemijärvi, followed by bus or car. A car gives more freedom for Lapland side trips, but you can manage Pyhä itself without one once you are at the resort.
