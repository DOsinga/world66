Naava is the practical first stop in Pyhä. It has maps, current trail information, rental-hut keys, and exhibits on the geology, forests, and fell nature of Pyhä-Luosto National Park.

Stop here before walking into Isokuru or climbing higher onto the fells. In winter especially, the staff can save you from making a poor route choice.
