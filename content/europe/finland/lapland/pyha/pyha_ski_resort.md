Pyhä Ski Resort is small compared with the biggest Alpine resorts, but it has a sharper profile than many Lapland ski villages. The slopes sit on Kultakero, with some genuinely steep maintained runs alongside easier pistes.

The bigger reason to choose Pyhä is the mix: downhill skiing, cross-country skiing, snowshoeing, and national-park scenery all sit close together. It suits travellers who want outdoor days more than loud nights.
