Ranua sits in southern Finnish Lapland, far enough from Rovaniemi to feel rural but close enough for a proper day trip. The town is small and plain; the surrounding municipality is the interesting part, a flat world of bogs, low forest, lakes, reindeer country, and deep winter snow.

The anchor is [Ranua Wildlife Park](/europe/finland/lapland/ranua/ranua_wildlife_park), one of the best places in Finland to see Arctic and subarctic animals without pretending you are on a wilderness expedition. Around it are quieter reasons to stay longer: [Lake Simojarvi](/europe/finland/lapland/ranua/lake_simojarvi), the salmon river [Simojoki](/europe/finland/lapland/ranua/simojoki_river), and a local obsession with cloudberries that comes out in summer.

Ranua is not a pretty resort village. It is a practical Lapland base with a strong wildlife identity, good forest-and-water country, and enough character to justify the detour from Rovaniemi or Oulu.
