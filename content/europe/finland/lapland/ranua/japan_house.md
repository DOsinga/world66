Japan House is a small Japan-themed culture centre near Lake Simojarvi. Exhibitions introduce Japanese art and culture in an unexpected Lapland setting, making it a quiet detour for travellers already exploring the lake area.

It is outside Ranua's centre and is not reached by public transport, so plan it only if you have a car.
