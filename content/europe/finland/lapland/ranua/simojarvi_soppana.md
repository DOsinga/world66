Simojärvi-Soppana is a protected lake-and-forest area shared by Ranua and Posio, with sandy shores, islands, small rivers, and old-growth forest. It is useful for easy hikes, kayaking, boating, fishing, and quiet overnight trips using the area's shelters and campfire sites.
