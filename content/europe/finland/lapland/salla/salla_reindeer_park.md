Salla Reindeer Park gives visitors a structured way into the reindeer culture that still defines much of Lapland. Depending on the season, activities include reindeer feeding, sleigh or forest trips, snowshoeing, canoeing, and guided nature outings. It is tourist-facing, but the setting above the Arctic Circle keeps it from feeling like a city petting zoo.

The park is near Sallatunturi, so it pairs naturally with a walk or ski route in Salla National Park. Families will find it easy; independent travelers should still consider stopping because reindeer husbandry is not background scenery here, but part of how the region works.
