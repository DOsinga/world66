Tankavaara Wild occupies the former national park visitor-centre role, with exhibitions, films, and guided nature activities tied to Urho Kekkonen National Park and the surrounding wilderness.

It is not a major museum in its own right, but it helps orient the place. Stop here if you want context before walking the nearby trails, especially if weather or time keeps you from going farther into the park.
