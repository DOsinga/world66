title: Things to Do
type: section
---

Tankavaara is simple: learn the gold story, try panning if the season suits, then walk into the edge of Urho Kekkonen National Park. The village is small enough that you do not need a plan more complicated than that.

The **Gold Prospector Museum** is the serious anchor. The short national park trails and the former visitor-centre area add the landscape that explains why prospecting here was never a comfortable business.
