Ateljee Tapio Autio is the sort of place that gives a small rural destination personality. Tapio Autio, a former policeman, built a private church, studio spaces, sculptures, and hilltop structures with his own hands, turning a quiet spot south of the lake into a personal religious and artistic world.

It is odd, sincere, and local. The outdoor sculptures and tower views over forest and lake are the reason to go; the appeal is less about polished art-world presentation than about seeing what one determined person made in an unlikely place.
