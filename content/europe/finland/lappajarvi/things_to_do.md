title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Lappajärvi is best treated as a slow lake day. Drive or cycle part of the shore, stop for views and swimming, then add the church, the local museum, or Ateljee Tapio Autio if you want something more specific.

The meteorite story is the hook, but the experience is still rural Finland: water, forest, fields, summer cottages, and very little hurry.
