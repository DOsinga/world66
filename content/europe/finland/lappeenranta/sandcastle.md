Lappeenranta's Sandcastle is a seasonal waterfront attraction built from elaborate sand sculptures near the harbor. The theme changes, but the basic appeal is the same: a playful summer installation that makes the lakefront feel festive.

Because it is seasonal, it should not be the only reason to visit. In summer, though, it pairs well with the fortress, harbor, and lake cruises, especially for families.
