---
title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Lappeenranta's attractions cluster around the lakeside fortress area and the harbour. The city is at its most rewarding in summer, when the long evenings invite walks along the water, boat trips into the Saimaa labyrinth, and the sandcastle festival that takes over the harbour beach. Winter brings ice fishing, cross-country skiing, and a quieter, more local character.
