---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

Lappeenranta is fundamentally a summer destination. Between June and August the long days — it barely gets dark at midsummer — fill the harbour, fortress, and lake with activity. The Sandcastle World Championships in July draw visitors from across Finland and give the harbour beach a festive character.

Outside summer, the city is quiet and functional. Winter (December–March) brings reliable snow, which makes the Veluwe forests and lake ice beautiful but reduces the tourist infrastructure to almost nothing — boats stop running, harbour restaurants close, and the fortress cafes shut. Winter is genuinely rewarding for those who like quiet Finnish cities and ice fishing, but requires self-sufficiency.
