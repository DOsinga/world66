Lohja is a lake town west of Helsinki, close enough for a day trip but different in mood from the capital's polished coast. Its identity is built from three things: Lake Lohjanjarvi, limestone, and apples. That sounds modest until you start seeing how neatly they shape the place.

The best day here mixes the underground and the old sacred. [Tytyri Mine Experience](/europe/finland/lohja/tytyri_mine_experience) takes visitors deep into a working limestone landscape, while [St. Lawrence Church](/europe/finland/lohja/st_lawrence_church) is one of Finland's great medieval churches, covered with early 16th-century wall paintings.

In summer, Lohja softens around the water. Aurlahti beach and the Lake Lohjanjarvi shore are easy places to swim, paddle or sit with coffee, and the surrounding municipality has orchards, groves and old villages. It is not a big-city break; it is a good, varied day in western Uusimaa.
