Lohja Museum fills in the everyday history behind the town: farming, local industry, domestic life and the old settlement patterns around western Uusimaa. It is a quieter stop than Tytyri, but useful if you want Lohja to feel like more than a lake and a church.

The museum works best as a short add-on while walking between the centre, the church and the lake shore.
