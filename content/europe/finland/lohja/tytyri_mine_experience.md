Tytyri is the sight that gives Lohja its edge. The guided route descends into an old limestone mine, where industrial history, geology and a surprisingly theatrical light show share the same cold underground space.

It is still tied to a working mining landscape, which keeps the experience from feeling like a theme attraction. Book ahead for English-language guiding, especially outside the main summer season.
