Loviisa Church is the red-brick landmark that anchors the western approach to town. Its tall profile and red tile roof make it one of the easiest buildings to pick out in the centre.

It is a short stop rather than a major sight, but it works well with Old Loviisa, Laivasilta, and the fortress earthworks on a compact walk through town.
