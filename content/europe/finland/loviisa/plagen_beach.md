Plagen Beach is Loviisa's main town beach, close enough to the centre for an easy summer walk. Locals come here to swim, picnic, play games, and sit by the water when the weather is good.

It is a simple seasonal stop rather than a wild stretch of coast, but it adds a pleasant break to a day spent around Old Loviisa, Laivasilta, and the fortress sites.
