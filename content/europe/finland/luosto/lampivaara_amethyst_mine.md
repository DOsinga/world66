Lampivaara Amethyst Mine is Luosto's odd, memorable anchor: a working tourist mine on a fell inside Pyhä-Luosto National Park. Guided visits explain the geology, then hand you tools so you can dig for a small amethyst and keep one stone.

The mine is reached from Luosto by trail, snowcat, or seasonal transport depending on conditions. It is popular with families, but it is not just for children; the combination of ancient rock, open fell views, and a souvenir you actually found yourself gives the visit real charm.
