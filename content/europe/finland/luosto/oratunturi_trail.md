The Oratunturi Trail is a short, rough climb to one of the stronger viewpoints around Luosto. The path is rocky, but the reward is a wide fell-top view over forest, marsh, and low northern hills.

There is a lean-to shelter partway up from the small parking area, which makes it a good target for a slow picnic walk as well as a brisk climb.
