The Astuvansalmi rock paintings are among Finland's most remarkable prehistoric sites,
set on a cliff face above Lake Yovesi. Figures of people, boats, animals, and handprints
survive in a landscape of water, pine forest, and stone, which is exactly the setting
that gives them their force.

Reaching the paintings takes some effort, either by trail or by boat, and that is part
of the reward. They are not museum objects removed from context; they remain in the
lakeside world where they were made. Wear sensible shoes and check access details before
leaving Mikkeli.
