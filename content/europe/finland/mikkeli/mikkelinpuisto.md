Mikkelinpuisto is a landscaped garden park rather than a wild green space, with display gardens, paths, and seasonal plantings that are most rewarding in the Finnish summer. It is a pleasant change of pace in a town better known for wartime headquarters history and lake-country access.

The park works best as a slow stop: a walk, a coffee, and a look at what grows well this far north. Check seasonal opening details, since its appeal is closely tied to weather, flowers, and summer services.
