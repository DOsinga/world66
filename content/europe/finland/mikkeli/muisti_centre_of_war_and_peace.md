Muisti Centre of War and Peace is a modern history museum in central Mikkeli, focused on the human experience of war and the work of peace. It complements the nearby Headquarters Museum, which covers Mikkeli's wartime command role more directly.

The exhibitions use personal stories and interactive displays rather than only military objects, making it one of the city's more accessible museum stops.
