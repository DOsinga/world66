---
title: When to Go
type: section
---

Summer — June through August — is the best time to visit Mikkeli. The lake country is at its most accessible, boat trips run on Lake Saimaa, and the long Finnish days (nearly 24 hours of daylight at midsummer) give the landscape an extraordinary quality. The town's classical music festivals take place in summer, and outdoor activities like cycling, kayaking, and lake swimming are in full swing.

Winter is cold and dark but not without appeal. Snow covers the forests and lakes from December through March, and cross-country skiing on the trails around Naisvuori hill is good. Ice fishing on the frozen lake is a local tradition. If you are coming purely for the war history museums, they operate year-round and winter is a quieter time to visit.

The transition seasons — May and September — are pleasant for walking and for watching the landscape change. Autumn in particular, when the birch trees turn gold against the dark conifers, is genuinely beautiful in the Finnish Lake District.
