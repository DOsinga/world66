Luolalanjärvi is a small wetland nature stop near Naantali, with a birdwatching tower and trail giving views over reeds and open water. It is a modest attraction, but worthwhile for travelers who want a quiet natural counterpoint to the old town and Moomin-themed family sights.

Spring and migration periods are the most rewarding times, though the walk can be pleasant in summer as well. Bring binoculars if birdlife is the main reason for the visit.
