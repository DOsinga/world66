This Bronze Age burial cairn stands on Jättekastet hill above Nagu. It is a small but atmospheric prehistoric site, reached by following the Westerholm Cultural Path from Kyrkbacken.

The final approach is steep, with a rope to help on the climb, so sturdy shoes are useful. The cairn works best as part of the full cultural trail rather than as a quick roadside stop.
