Gullkrona is a small island south of Nagu, known as a summer stop for sailors in the Archipelago Sea. The guest harbour has saunas, a cafe, a nature trail, and a small museum telling the island's pilot and maritime history.

It is the kind of place that makes most sense in summer, when boats are running and the harbour has life. Come for a slow island stop rather than a checklist sight.
