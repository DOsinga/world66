The Westerholm Trail is a short circular walk from Kyrkbacken through woods, old shorelines, and places linked to the Nagu-born painter Victor Westerholm. It is a good way to see the island beyond the marina without committing to a full cycling day.

The path is marked, though the forest sections are uneven and not suited to everyone. Go for the mix of local art history, quiet landscape, and the small Bronze Age traces above the village.
