Fagerö Folkpark is the easy summer face of coastal Närpes. It has a beach, marina, miniature golf, beach volleyball, SUP rental, and a seasonal restaurant, with dances and events on some summer evenings.

It is not a major sight in itself, but it is useful if you are travelling the coast with children or want a relaxed local stop rather than another museum. Pair it with the drive toward the archipelago.
