The church stables are Närpes' signature sight. Long rows of small timber stalls stand near the church, built for the horses of parishioners who came in from the surrounding villages before cars made the journey ordinary.

They are modest buildings, but together they make a rare survival. You get a quick, concrete sense of how spread-out rural Ostrobothnia worked: farms, church, language, and Sunday routines all tied to the same landscape.
