The roadside marker near the Finnish-Norwegian border is the simple, slightly absurd photo stop that gives Nuorgam its place on the map. The true northernmost point lies out in the Teno River, but this is the practical place to stand, take the picture, and feel how far north you have come.

Do not expect drama from the monument itself. The reward is the setting: low Arctic light, the border road, the river, and Norway just beyond.
