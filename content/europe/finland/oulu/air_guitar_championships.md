The unlikely event that put Oulu on the global map: a deadly serious international competition held in late August every year, in which contestants from around the world perform two 60-second rounds of air guitar in front of an audience and a panel of expert judges. Competitors are scored on technical merit, stage presence, and airness.

Spectacle, comedy, and a particular Finnish kind of self-deprecation. Tickets sell out months ahead.
