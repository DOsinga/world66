---
title: "Eating Out"
type: section
---

The local food culture leans on river fish — salmon, perch, whitefish — and game from the surrounding forests. The summer market on the harbour has stalls selling rieska flatbread, smoked fish, and small bowls of leipäjuusto (squeaky cheese) drizzled with cloudberry jam. The centre has the usual range of Finnish casual restaurants and an unusually solid pizza scene for a small Finnish city.
