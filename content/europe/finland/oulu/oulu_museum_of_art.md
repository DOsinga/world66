Oulu Museum of Art is the city's main visual-art museum, with exhibitions that connect northern Finnish artists, contemporary work, and regional cultural identity. Its location near Ainola Park makes it easy to fold into a quiet culture-and-green-space circuit.

The museum is a good indoor anchor in a city where weather can shape the day. It is most rewarding for visitors who want a sense of northern Finland's art scene rather than another generic municipal gallery.
