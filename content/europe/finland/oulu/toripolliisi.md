A bronze statue of a portly policeman standing at parade rest on the market square — the unofficial mascot of Oulu and the meeting point for half the city. Made in 1987 by the local sculptor Kaarlo Mikkonen, it commemorates the market policemen who kept order on the harbour from the 1880s onwards.

Almost unavoidable on any visit. The boots are polished bright from constant touching.
