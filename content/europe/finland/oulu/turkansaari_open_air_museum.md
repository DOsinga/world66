Turkansaari Open Air Museum sits on islands near Oulu, using old wooden buildings and farm environments to show rural life in northern Finland. The setting matters: water, forest, and timber structures make the museum feel rooted in the landscape.

It is best in season, when buildings are open and demonstrations or events bring the site to life. For travellers, it is a good way to see Oulu's hinterland culture rather than only the modern city and waterfront.
