Pori is a mid-sized port city on the Gulf of Bothnia, known across Finland — and to jazz lovers around the world — for its annual [Pori Jazz Festival](/europe/finland/pori/pori_jazz_festival). Every July for nine days, [Kirjurinluoto Island](/europe/finland/pori/kirjurinluoto_island) fills with music, drawing international names alongside Finnish acts to the riverside concert park. The festival is genuinely good, and worth timing a trip around.

Outside festival season, Pori is a pleasant and unhurried coastal city. The nearby [Yyteri Beach](/europe/finland/pori/yyteri_beach), one of the finest sandy stretches in Finland, draws summer visitors, while the [Satakunta Museum](/europe/finland/pori/satakunta_museum) and [Pori Art Museum](/europe/finland/pori/pori_art_museum) give the city a cultural weight that surprises first-time visitors. The [Juselius Mausoleum](/europe/finland/pori/juselius_mausoleum), with its Akseli Gallen-Kallela frescoes, is quietly one of the most remarkable small monuments in the country.

Pori works well as a base for exploring the Satakunta coast, and the summer months — when the archipelago and beaches come alive — are by far the best time to visit.
