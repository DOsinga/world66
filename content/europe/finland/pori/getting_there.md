---
title: "Getting There"
type: section
---

Pori Airport (IATA: POR) is a small regional airport about 3 kilometres south of the centre, with occasional domestic connections to Helsinki. Check current schedules, as services have been intermittent in recent years.

By train, Pori is connected to Tampere (roughly 1.5 hours) and onward to Helsinki (total journey around 3.5 hours). Trains run several times daily. Long-distance buses connect Pori to Turku, Tampere, and Helsinki. Driving from Helsinki takes about three hours on the E18 and Ring Road 2.
