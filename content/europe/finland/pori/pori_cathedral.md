Pori Cathedral is a Gothic-style church beside the Kokemäenjoki river, close to the historic centre. Its name also echoes the old castle site that gave Pori its Swedish name, Björneborg.

It is an easy architectural stop while walking between the riverfront, the old town hall, and Pori's museums.
