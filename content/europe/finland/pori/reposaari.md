Reposaari is an island district at the mouth of Pori's coast, with wooden houses, harbour edges, and a maritime atmosphere distinct from the inland city centre. It feels like a small coastal village attached to a larger industrial region.

Come for a walk, sea air, and a slower look at Pori's relationship with the Gulf of Bothnia. It is especially appealing in summer, when the light lingers and the island's old streets are at their most inviting.
