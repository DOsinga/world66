Pellinki is an island group in the Porvoo archipelago, reached by road and ferry links east of the old town. It is a classic Finnish summer landscape: low wooded islands, boat landings, cottages, and narrow roads that open suddenly onto views of the Gulf of Finland.

The islands are best for a slow excursion rather than a checklist sight. Come for cycling, swimming spots, small harbors, and the change of pace from Porvoo's cobbled center. In summer the area feels lived-in and maritime; outside the season it is quieter, with more of the austere archipelago mood.
