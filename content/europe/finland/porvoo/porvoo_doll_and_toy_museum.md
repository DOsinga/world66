Porvoo Doll and Toy Museum is a small seasonal museum in the old town, with more than a thousand dolls and hundreds of toys dating from the 19th and 20th centuries. It is more intimate than polished, which suits Porvoo's old wooden streets.

Opening is limited, usually in summer, so check before going. When it is open, it is a gentle family stop between the cathedral, the old warehouses and the main lanes of the historic centre.
