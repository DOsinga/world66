Porvoo Hillfort is a wooded historic area beside the Old Town. Iso Linnamaki, the larger hillfort, is one of Finland's biggest ancient fort sites and was used from roughly the 8th to the 14th century. The paths make an easy walk with views back toward old Porvoo.
