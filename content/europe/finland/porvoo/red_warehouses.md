The row of red-painted wooden warehouses lining the south bank of the Porvoonjoki is the most photographed image in Finland outside Helsinki. Built in the 18th and 19th centuries to store goods from the merchant ships that came upriver from the Baltic, they have been converted into shops, galleries, and restaurants while retaining their original structure.

The best view of the warehouses is from the old bridge just upstream, where you can see the entire row reflected in the river when the water is calm. In summer the area comes alive with outdoor cafes and small boats. The warehouses are best visited in morning light when the tourists are thinner and the reflections are clearest.
