The Salpa Line fortifications around Punkaharju are part of the huge defensive system Finland built during the Second World War along its eastern frontier. What you see here is not a single monument but a spread of concrete bunkers, anti-tank obstacles, trenches, and gun positions tucked into the forest.

It works best as a slow outdoor history stop rather than a museum visit. Bring decent shoes, follow the marked paths, and give yourself time to imagine the scale of the project: thousands of men cutting a military line through lakes, ridges, and pine woods that now look peaceful again.
