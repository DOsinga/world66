---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

**Summer** (June to August) is by far the busiest and easiest time: the lakes are warm enough to swim in by Finnish standards, the days are very long, and most of the attractions are open. Mosquitoes can be a nuisance in June; bring repellent.

**Autumn** (September to early October) brings short but spectacular colour through the birches and rowans against the dark pines. Many small businesses close from late September.

**Winter** (December to March) turns the ridge into a quiet ski-trail landscape — cross-country skiing on marked routes is excellent, but you need your own gear and a car. The lakes freeze hard enough to walk on.

Avoid **April and November** — frozen and grey, with limited services and unreliable weather.
