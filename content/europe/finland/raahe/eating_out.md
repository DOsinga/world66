---
title: Eating Out
type: section
---

Choices are limited but solid for a town of this size. **Ravintola Raahen Hovi**, in a 19th-century timber house just off Pekkatori, is the place for a proper sit-down meal — Finnish classics with a Lappish lean, reindeer fillet, perch from the gulf, the obligatory salmon soup.

**Pakkahuoneenkatu** has two or three cafes between the museum and the square; **Café Sofia** is the local pick for buns and cinnamon rolls. **Pizzeria Berlusconi** is the de facto pizza-and-kebab option for a cheap evening meal — every Finnish town has one, and the name is no joke.

In summer, the **Old Raahe** outdoor market by Pekkatori sells local fish, new potatoes, summer berries and pulla.
