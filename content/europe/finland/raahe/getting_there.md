---
title: Getting There
type: section
---

Raahe is on Finland's western coastal route, 75 km south of [Oulu](/europe/finland/oulu) and about 130 km north of [Kokkola](/europe/finland/kokkola). The drive on the E8 takes around an hour from either direction.

There is no longer a passenger railway station in Raahe itself — the line that serves the town is used only for freight to and from the steelworks. The nearest stations with passenger services are Vihanti and Oulainen, both with a connecting bus to Raahe. Long-distance bus services run several times a day from Oulu and Kokkola.

The nearest commercial airport is at Oulu, which has direct flights from Helsinki. Once in town, the old quarter is fully walkable.
