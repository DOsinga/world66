Reksaari is one of Rauma's nearby island escapes, a coastal recreation area used for swimming, walking, picnics, and summer outings. It gives visitors a taste of the Bothnian Sea archipelago without requiring a long expedition or specialist plans.

The island is best in the warmer months, when ferries or local boat access make the trip practical and the sea air is part of the pleasure. It is a low-key nature stop rather than a spectacle. Pair it with Old Rauma for a day that balances wooden-town history with the coast that shaped the city.
