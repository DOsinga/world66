---
title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

The whole of [Old Rauma](/europe/finland/rauma/old_rauma) is the main attraction — a UNESCO-protected neighbourhood of about 600 wooden houses, mostly 18th and 19th century, still lived in. Walking it slowly is the point. Add the [Church of the Holy Cross](/europe/finland/rauma/church_of_the_holy_cross), the [Old Town Hall](/europe/finland/rauma/old_town_hall), and the [Rauma Maritime Museum](/europe/finland/rauma/maritime_museum), and you have a solid day.

If you have a second day, head out to the [Sammallahdenmäki](/europe/finland/rauma/sammallahdenmaki) Bronze Age burial cairns — Rauma's second UNESCO site, half an hour inland by car. Lace fans should time a visit around Lace Week (Pitsiviikko) in late July, when the old town fills with markets, demonstrations, and the famous Night of Black Lace candlelit evening.
