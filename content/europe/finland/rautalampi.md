Rautalampi is a small village in the Finnish Lakeland, west of Kuopio, surrounded by water, forest, and old parish landscapes. It is not a place of big-city sights, but it has a strong local identity: wooden churches, manor country, canoe routes, and easy access to Southern Konnevesi National Park.

The village centre is compact, with [Rautalampi Church](/europe/finland/rautalampi/rautalampi_church) and the [Rautalampi Museum](/europe/finland/rautalampi/rautalampi_museum) almost facing each other. Together they give the cultural side of the place: old Savo parish life, evacuee stories, and the local writers and artists who came out of the district.

The landscape is the better reason to linger. The Rautalampi water route is made for slow travel by boat or canoe, while [Southern Konnevesi National Park](/europe/finland/rautalampi/southern_konnevesi_national_park) protects a mix of clear lake water, islands, cliffs, and forest that feels like the cleanest version of inland Finland.
