Liimattalanharju is the kind of low-key landscape detail that makes the Finnish Lakeland special: a narrow esker road running between lakes, with water and forest appearing on both sides as the route heads toward Kerkonkoski.

It is best by bike or slow car rather than as a single stop. The appeal is the rhythm of the road, the old countryside, and the sense that land here is only barely holding the water apart.
