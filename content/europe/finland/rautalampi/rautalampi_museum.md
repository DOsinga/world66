Rautalampi Museum is a serious local museum for a small village, with a wide collection of objects, photographs, recordings, and changing exhibitions. It is the best place to understand why this quiet parish has a larger cultural footprint than you might expect.

Pair it with the church across the road; together they make the village centre feel coherent rather than just pretty.
