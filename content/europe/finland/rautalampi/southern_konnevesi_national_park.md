Southern Konnevesi National Park is the main natural anchor around Rautalampi. It protects clear lake water, wooded islands, rocky shores, and forest trails west of the village, with canoe routes that make the park feel more like a water landscape than a single hiking area.

You need your own transport or a planned paddling trip to get the most from it. For travelers already crossing the Lakeland, it is the reason Rautalampi clears the bar.
