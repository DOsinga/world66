Riihimäki is a small railway town north of Helsinki that quietly overperforms for museums. Its streets are plain and workaday, but the town has built a clear identity around glass, hunting, local history, and the culture of Finnish civic life.

The strongest stop is the [Finnish Glass Museum](/europe/finland/riihimaki/finnish_glass_museum), housed in a former glassworks and tied to Riihimäki's long industrial design story. Next door, the [Hunting Museum of Finland](/europe/finland/riihimaki/hunting_museum_of_finland) takes a subject that could be narrow and uses it to explain food, forest, wildlife, folklore, and settlement.

In the centre, [Riemu Museums](/europe/finland/riihimaki/riemu_museums) combine the art museum and city museum under one roof. Riihimäki is not a place to build a whole Finland trip around, but it is a good spare-day stop if you are travelling by train between Helsinki, Hämeenlinna, and Tampere.
