The Finnish Glass Museum is Riihimäki's main reason to stop. It occupies a renovated glassworks and covers both the craft of glassmaking and the design history that made Finnish glass internationally recognisable.

The museum works well even if you are not a design specialist. The material is beautiful, the industrial setting gives it weight, and the story ties Riihimäki to a national tradition rather than just a local factory past.
