The Hunting Museum of Finland is more than a room of rifles and trophies. Its exhibitions use hunting to explain how people lived with Finnish forests, game animals, fur, food, beliefs, and later sport.

It pairs naturally with the Glass Museum next door. Together they make Riihimäki feel like a town of national special museums rather than a simple commuter stop.
