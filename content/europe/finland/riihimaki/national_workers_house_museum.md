The National Workers' House Museum preserves a Finnish workers' association house and the social world around it. It is a small, seasonal museum, but it adds a useful political and everyday-life layer to Riihimäki's museum circuit.

Go if you have already committed to a museum day in town. The building itself is part of the exhibit, which makes it more interesting than a generic local-history room.
