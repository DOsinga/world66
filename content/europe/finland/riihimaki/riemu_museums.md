Riemu Museums bring Riihimäki Art Museum and Riihimäki City Museum into one central stop. The art side is built around Finnish works from the Wähäjärvi collection, while the city museum gives context to the railway town, local industry, and everyday life.

It is the easiest museum to reach from the station, and a good counterweight to the more specialised glass and hunting museums west of the centre.
