The Arctic Arboretum is a seven-hectare riverside planting behind the Arktikum Science Centre. It focuses on trees and other plants that can survive Rovaniemi's northern climate, making it an easy outdoor add-on after the museum and a pleasant place to see how Arctic light shapes the landscape.
