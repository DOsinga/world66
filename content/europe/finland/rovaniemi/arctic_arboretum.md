The Arctic Arboretum is a quiet riverside garden behind Arktikum, planted with trees and shrubs that can cope with Rovaniemi's severe climate. In summer it catches the midnight sun along the Ounas River; in winter it is a simple, dark-sky place to watch for aurora.
