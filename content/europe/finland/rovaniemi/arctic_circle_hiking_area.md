The Arctic Circle Hiking Area is the most straightforward way to get a proper Lapland forest walk without mounting an expedition from Rovaniemi. Trails run through pine woods, bog edges, and along the Raudanjoki, with rapids, suspension bridges, and campfire shelters giving the routes a satisfying rhythm.

It works in several levels of ambition, from short family walks to longer day hikes. Bring shoes that can handle wet ground, and remember that even accessible Arctic nature can feel remote once you leave the car park.
