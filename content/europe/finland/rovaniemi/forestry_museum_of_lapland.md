The Forestry Museum of Lapland gives Rovaniemi a grounded local-history stop away from Santa Claus branding and Arctic Circle photo stops. Its open-air buildings, tools, boats, and work camps explain how logging and timber floating shaped life and labour across Lapland.

The museum is seasonal and best visited when the outdoor setting can be appreciated comfortably. It is a good fit for travellers who want to understand the region's forest economy and rural working culture, not just its winter tourism image.
