Harjulampi Bird Watching Tower is a modest but worthwhile nature stop for travellers who want a quiet hour outside central Rovaniemi. The tower overlooks wetland habitat where patient visitors may see waterbirds and seasonal migrants, especially in the lighter months.

This is not a headline attraction, but it fits Rovaniemi's broader outdoor appeal. Bring binoculars if you have them, and combine the stop with nearby trails or river walks rather than making it the sole focus of a day.
