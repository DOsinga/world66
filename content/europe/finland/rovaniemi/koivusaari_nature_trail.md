Koivusaari Nature Trail is a short wetland and riverside walk close to central Rovaniemi, with birdwatching opportunities along the Kemijoki. It is a useful low-effort nature break for visitors who do not have time to reach the larger hiking areas outside town.

The trail is seasonal in feel: birds, water levels and snow conditions change the experience. Bring insect protection in summer and treat it as a quiet walk rather than a major wilderness outing.
