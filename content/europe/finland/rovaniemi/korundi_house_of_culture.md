Korundi House of Culture gives Rovaniemi a serious indoor cultural stop, useful in winter when daylight is short and temperatures discourage idle wandering. The building houses the Rovaniemi Art Museum, with a focus on northern and Finnish contemporary art, and also serves as a concert venue.

The setting has its own appeal: a former postal bus depot, one of the few large buildings in Rovaniemi to survive the destruction of the Second World War. Check the programme before visiting, because an evening concert or temporary exhibition can turn it from a quick museum call into the best cultural night in town.
