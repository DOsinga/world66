Pilke Science Centre explains the forests of northern Finland through hands-on exhibits on timber, forest products, and sustainable use of Arctic woodlands. It pairs well with nearby Arktikum, but its focus is narrower and more practical: how Lapland's forests work and how people use them.

The centre is useful for families and for anyone planning time in Lapland's nature areas, with maps, local information, lockers, and a shop focused on Finnish wood design.
