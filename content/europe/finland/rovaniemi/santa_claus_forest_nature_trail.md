Santa Claus Forest Nature Trail starts near Santa Claus Village and leads through mire, pine and spruce forest. Duckboards cross the wet sections, while wooden stairs help on the steeper parts.

The out-and-back walk is about 1.5 km each way. It is a useful short nature break, but the trail is not suitable for wheelchairs or strollers.
