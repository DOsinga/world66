Santasport Spa is a public pool and spa complex in Ounasvaara, close to Rovaniemi's outdoor trails. The pool area has lap swimming, children's pools, hot and cold pools, a hot tub, and massage jets.

It is most useful as a bad-weather or family stop, or as a warm recovery break after skiing, hiking, or other Ounasvaara activities.
