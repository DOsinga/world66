Somonen Nature Church is an outdoor place of worship built among glacial boulders south-east of Rovaniemi. A wooden cross, simple benches, and a small bell tower turn the rock formation into a quiet open-air chapel.

It is a small stop rather than a headline sight, but worthwhile if you are exploring the forest roads and nature trails around Ounasvaara.
