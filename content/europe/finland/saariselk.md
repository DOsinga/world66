**Saariselka** is a small fell village in Finnish Lapland, well above the Arctic Circle. It is the main gateway to the [Urho Kekkonen National Park](/europe/finland/saariselk/urho_kekkonen_national_park), one of Finland's largest wilderness areas — a vast expanse of fells, forests, and rivers stretching all the way to the Russian border.

In winter, Saariselka offers cross-country skiing on the [Saariselkä Ski Resort](/europe/finland/saariselk/saariselk_ski_resort) trails, [husky safaris](/europe/finland/saariselk/husky_safari), snowmobile tours, and a strong chance to see the [Northern Lights](/europe/finland/saariselk/northern_lights_viewing). In summer, the fells around [Kaunispää](/europe/finland/saariselk/kaunispaa_fell) and [Kiilopää](/europe/finland/saariselk/kiilopaa) are excellent for hiking under the midnight sun. The village itself is purpose-built for tourism — small and functional rather than charming, but the surrounding landscape more than compensates.
