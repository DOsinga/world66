---
title: "Getting There"
type: section
---

The nearest airport is **Ivalo** (IVL), about 25 km north of Saariselkä. Finnair operates daily flights from Helsinki year-round, with extra capacity and direct charters from London, Paris, and other European hubs during the winter season. Several hotels run free shuttle buses from the airport.

By **bus**, regular services on the Helsinki–Rovaniemi–Ivalo–Karasjok route stop in Saariselkä; the journey from Rovaniemi takes about 4 hours. The night train from Helsinki to Rovaniemi continues the trip on the same day by bus.

By **car**, the village sits directly on Route E75, Finland's main north–south artery. From Rovaniemi the drive takes about 3.5 hours; from Helsinki it is a long full day or, more sensibly, two.
