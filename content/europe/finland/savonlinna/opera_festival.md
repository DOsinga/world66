snippet: One of Europe's leading summer opera festivals, staged inside the courtyard of Olavinlinna castle
story: The festival started in 1912 thanks to the Finnish soprano Aino Ackté, who staged five operas inside the castle in a single summer and put the town on the international opera map. After a long interruption, the festival was revived in 1967 and now draws around 60,000 visitors each July — more than the town's regular population.
---

The Savonlinna Opera Festival is the cultural reason most international visitors come to town. For four weeks from early July to early August, the castle courtyard is covered with a temporary roof and turned into a 2,200-seat opera house with extraordinary acoustics. The programme each year includes both standards from the international repertoire and a Finnish production.

Tickets sell out months in advance — bookings open in November. Dress is smart but not formal; many of the audience walk over the bridge to the castle direct from a restaurant. The town fills up completely during the festival, with rates and queues to match. If you can manage even one performance the experience is worth it.
