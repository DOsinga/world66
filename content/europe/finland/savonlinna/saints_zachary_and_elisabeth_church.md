Saints Zachary and Elisabeth Church is a small Orthodox church from 1846 near the centre of Savonlinna.

The building was converted to Lutheran use in 1938 and returned to the Orthodox parish in 2022, making it a quick but worthwhile stop for visitors interested in the city's religious history.
