---
title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Seinäjoki is best approached as an architecture stop with a strong seasonal festival life. The Aalto Centre and Lakeuden Risti are the essentials, and they sit close enough to the railway station for a tight visit.

If you have more time, go south to Törnävä for the old manor setting and the festival grounds. In July, the Tangomarkkinat makes the city feel much larger and stranger than it does on an ordinary weekday.
