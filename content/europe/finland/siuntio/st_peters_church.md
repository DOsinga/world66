St. Peter's Church is Siuntio's medieval stone church, about 560 years old and still the heart of the old church village. Its grey walls and simple proportions are typical of southern Finland's late-medieval churches, but the quiet rural setting makes it especially rewarding.

The church is a good first stop before the manors. It gives Siuntio a historical centre and makes the surrounding lanes feel less like anonymous commuter countryside.
