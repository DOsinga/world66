Suitia Manor is another of Siuntio's old manor sites, with roots in the 15th century and a main building whose oldest parts date from the 1540s. Together with St. Peter's Church, it forms part of a nationally significant built heritage area.

The estate is most useful to travellers when tours are running. Otherwise, treat it as part of a wider Siuntio history loop rather than a guaranteed interior visit.
