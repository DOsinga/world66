One of Tampere's better-known central bars, Hermanni occupies a small street-level space near the Tammerkoski rapids. It is known for a good selection of Finnish craft beers and a relaxed atmosphere that runs from afternoon into the evening. No live music or particular gimmick — just a well-stocked bar with knowledgeable staff in a city that takes its beer seriously.

Useful as a starting point for an evening in central Tampere before moving to a venue with programming. Gets crowded after 9 pm on weekends.
