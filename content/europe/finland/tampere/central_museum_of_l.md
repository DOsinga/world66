The Finnish Labour Museum Werstas (Suomen Työväenliikkeen museo Werstas) is housed in a former cotton and flax mill in the Tampella factory complex. It covers the history of the Finnish labour movement from industrialisation through the 20th century, with particular depth on the 1918 Civil War — fought between the White and Red factions, with Tampere as the central battleground.

The Civil War section is the most compelling part of the museum: Tampere was the Reds' main stronghold and the Battle of Tampere (March–April 1918) was the bloodiest urban battle in Scandinavian history. The museum treats this history without sanitising the violence or the politics on either side. A significant collection of labour movement artefacts, photographs, and personal accounts fills out the broader narrative.
