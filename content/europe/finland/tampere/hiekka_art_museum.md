Hiekka Art Museum is a compact private-collection museum in Tampere, centred on the holdings of goldsmith and collector Kustaa Hiekka. Its rooms mix Finnish painting, sculpture, furniture, and decorative objects in a domestic scale that feels quite different from the city's larger museums.

The museum is best for visitors who like smaller collections with a strong personal thread. It will not take long, but it adds a quieter art stop to a Tampere itinerary that might otherwise focus on industrial heritage, Moomins, or the Finlayson area.
