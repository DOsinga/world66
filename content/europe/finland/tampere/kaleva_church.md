Kaleva Church is one of Tampere's boldest modernist buildings, a tall concrete church whose vertical forms and spare interior mark a sharp break from older Finnish church architecture. It is often compared to a monumental piece of sculpture as much as a parish church.

The church is worth the short detour for architecture-minded visitors. Go for the space, light, and material drama rather than ornate decoration, and be mindful that it remains an active worship space.
