Lielahti Manor is a historic manor house in Tampere's Lielahti district, a reminder that the city's story is not only rapids, factories, and lakeside industry. The building and grounds add an older estate layer to a neighbourhood now better known for everyday urban development.

It is a modest but worthwhile stop if you are exploring western Tampere or following the city's local-history thread. Check current use and opening arrangements, since access can depend on events and municipal programming.
