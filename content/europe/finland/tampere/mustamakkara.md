Mustamakkara is the food that Tampere identifies with most strongly — a thick, dark blood sausage grilled over charcoal or cooked in an oven, served in a split bread roll or on a plate with lingonberry jam. The lingonberry is not optional: the sharpness cuts the richness of the sausage and the combination is genuinely good.

The best place to eat it is at the Tampere Market Hall, where several stalls sell it fresh and cooked throughout the day. The outdoor sellers at Laukontori in summer are a close second. This is not a tourist trap food: Tampere people eat it regularly and take it seriously. If you eat blood products, do not leave without trying it.
