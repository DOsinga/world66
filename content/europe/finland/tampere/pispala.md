story: "Pispala occupies a narrow ridge between Lake Näsijärvi and Lake Pyhäjärvi west of the city centre — a working-class neighbourhood of brightly-painted wooden houses that climbs steeply up both sides of the ridge. It was long the home of factory workers and is now the most bohemian and artistic neighbourhood in Tampere, with a concentration of artists, musicians, and small studios. Väinö Linna, Finland's most celebrated 20th-century novelist, grew up here and set parts of his masterwork 'The Unknown Soldier' in its streets."
---

Pispala is Tampere's most distinctive neighbourhood — a ridge-top collection of colourful wooden houses perched between two lakes, accessible by steep streets and stairs. The views from the ridge road are excellent in both directions: Lake Näsijärvi to the north and Lake Pyhäjärvi to the south, with the city centre visible to the east.

The neighbourhood has preserved its wooden architecture and its working-class identity better than comparable areas in Helsinki, in part because it was only incorporated into Tampere in 1937. Walking the ridge road and exploring the side streets that plunge toward both lakes is the main activity — there are a handful of small cafes and no particular sights, which is exactly the point. Best in summer when the wooden houses glow in the evening light.
