SS Tarjanne is a historic passenger steamship that runs a summer service from Laukontori in Tampere to the town of Virrat, 120 km north, traversing Lake Näsijärvi. The journey takes approximately 7 hours each way and passes through some of the most unspoilt inland waterway scenery in Finland.

The ship has been in continuous operation since 1908 and is maintained in historically accurate condition. The journey is the experience rather than the destination — you can return by bus from Virrat, or take the ship both ways if you want a full day on the water. Departs from Laukontori; runs in summer only. Book well in advance as berths are limited.
