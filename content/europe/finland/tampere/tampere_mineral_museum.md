Tampere Mineral Museum is a compact collection of minerals, gemstones, meteorites, and fossils. Highlights include cut and raw gemstones, a large amethyst specimen from Brazil, and a few unexpected prehistoric curiosities.

It works best as a short indoor stop for visitors who like natural history or geology. The museum is close to other central Tampere sights, so it is easy to combine with the nearby art and workers' housing museums.
