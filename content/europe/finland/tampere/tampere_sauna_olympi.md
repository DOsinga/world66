Public sauna culture is alive in Tampere in a way that feels different from Helsinki, where it has been partially reimagined as a wellness experience. In Tampere, public saunas are neighbourhood institutions — the Rajaportti sauna in Pispala has been operating since 1906 and is the oldest continuously operating public sauna in Finland. It is small, wood-fired, cheap, and genuinely old.

Rauhaniemi on Lake Pyhäjärvi is the most accessible public sauna from the city centre and has year-round lake swimming. For a more social sauna experience, the Kotiharjun sauna in the Kallio district is further out but considered one of the authentic traditional experiences. Allow an evening rather than an hour — the Finnish sauna ritual is not something to rush.
