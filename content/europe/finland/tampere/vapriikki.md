Vapriikki is Finland's most-visited regional museum centre, housed in a former textile factory in the Tampella industrial area. The complex bundles multiple permanent collections under one roof and runs a regular programme of major temporary exhibitions. A single ticket covers all the permanent collections, which means you can move from Ice Hockey history to Moomin illustrations to stuffed Finnish mammals in a single afternoon.

The Moomin Museum within Vapriikki is the main global draw — it holds the original illustrations of Tove Jansson and presents the Moomin world with considerable care. The Hockey Hall of Fame will make sense only if you follow Finnish hockey; the Natural History Museum and the Tampere City Museum are more broadly accessible. Budget at least three hours for the complex.
