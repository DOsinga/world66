Tornio sits on the Finnish side of the Torne River, pressed right against Haparanda in Sweden. The border is the town's identity: you can walk between countries, step across a time zone, and see how Lapland's coast has always looked both east and west.

This is not one of Finland's great city breaks, but it has enough substance for a spare day if you are already in Sea Lapland. [Alatornio Church](/europe/finland/tornio/alatornio_church) links the town to the Struve Geodetic Arc, [Aine Art Museum](/europe/finland/tornio/aine_art_museum) and the [Historical Museum of Tornio River Valley](/europe/finland/tornio/tornio_river_valley_museum) give the centre some indoor weight, and the old border-town oddity is still real.

The best excursion is [Kukkola Rapids](/europe/finland/tornio/kukkola_rapids), north of town, where the Torne River runs through a fishing village known for whitefish and dip-netting traditions. Time Tornio for summer events or for the New Year border celebration and the town becomes more memorable than its low-key centre first suggests.
