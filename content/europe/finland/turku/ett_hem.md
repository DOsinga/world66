Ett Hem preserves the atmosphere of an upper-class Turku home from the late 19th and early 20th centuries. The house museum was created from the bequest of Alfred and Helene Jacobsson, with period interiors, furniture, and an art collection that includes Finnish Golden Age artists.

It is a small, focused museum for visitors interested in domestic interiors, local history, or Finnish art.
