Turku's Healthcare Museum looks at the everyday history of medicine: tuberculosis treatment, polio care, operating-room instruments, and the tools nurses and doctors once used. It is a small specialist museum, but the subject is more immediate than it first sounds.

Go if you like medical history or odd local museums. It is not one of Turku's headline sights, but it adds a human, practical angle to the city's older cultural stops.
