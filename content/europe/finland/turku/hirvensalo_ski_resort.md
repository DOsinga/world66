Hirvensalo Ski Resort gives Turku a convenient winter sports option just outside the centre, on the island of Hirvensalo. Finland's big ski landscapes are far to the north, but this small hill is useful for a few runs, lessons, or family snow time without leaving the city region.

The scale is local, and that is the point. Travellers visiting Turku in winter can use it as a low-effort way to add skiing or snowboarding to a city trip, especially when the archipelago and riverfront are quiet under snow.
