Kakskerta Church is an 18th-century island church south of central Turku. Built between 1765 and 1769 and dedicated in 1770, it has a simple oblong form and an older altarpiece.

It is a worthwhile detour if you are already exploring Kakskerta or the island roads around Turku, but not a first stop for a short city visit.
