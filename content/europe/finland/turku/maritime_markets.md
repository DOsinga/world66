Turku's Maritime Markets bring fish, archipelago produce, handicrafts, and prepared food to the Aura River waterfront. The Archipelago Market is held in April, while the Herring Market takes over the riverside in October.

The draw is the mix of professional fishermen, small food producers, and market stalls in the centre of the city. Come hungry and check the current dates before planning around them.
