Turku's New Potato Festival marks the first harvest from the surrounding archipelago, when stalls along the Aura serve early potatoes with fish, herring, and other local summer food. It is a small seasonal pleasure rather than a blockbuster event, but it gives June visitors a very Finnish reason to linger by the river.
