St Mary's Church in Maaria is one of Turku's quieter medieval rewards. The stone church sits away from the central cathedral circuit, but its age, wall paintings, and village-church scale make it a worthwhile detour for visitors interested in Finland's medieval layers.

The visit is short and contemplative. Pair it with a walk or cycle north of the centre, and check access before going inside; even from outside, the church gives a useful sense of how old parish landscapes survived as modern Turku expanded around them.
