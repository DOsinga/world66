Vepsa Island is one of the easiest ways to turn a Turku stay into an archipelago outing. In summer, boats connect the city with this small island of swimming places, trails, picnic spots, and simple holiday facilities.

It is not a wild island adventure, but that is part of the appeal. Vepsa works well for travellers who want a low-effort taste of the Archipelago Sea without committing to ferries, car logistics, or an overnight stay.
