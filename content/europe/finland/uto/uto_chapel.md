Utö's newer chapel was built in the early 1900s for islanders who could no longer climb to the chapel in the lighthouse. Its plainness suits the place.

Inside, look for the memorial material connected with the 1947 Park Victory wreck, when islanders helped rescue survivors from the American freighter. Utö's sea stories are not decorative; they are part of why the island exists.
