Stenhuset is a small local museum about life on Utö. The building itself is part of the story: it dates from 1753 and is the oldest surviving building on the island.

Do not expect a polished big-city museum. Expect family objects, island memory, and a better sense of how a few dozen people lived with weather, shipping, and isolation.
