Utö Lighthouse is the island's symbol and the reason the place mattered so early. The first lighthouse was built here in the 18th century; the present tower dates from the early 19th century and still marks the route into southwest Finland.

Guided visits are normally a summer matter. Even from outside, the lighthouse gives the island its shape: white tower, bare rock, sea wind, and a sense that every ship is being watched.
