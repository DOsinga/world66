Láŋká Trail is a fell walk from Karigasniemi toward Láŋká, with open views across the far-northern landscape near the Norwegian border. The route is about 7 km one way, with wet ground in places and a rest spot partway along the path.

It is best treated as a proper day walk rather than a casual stroll. Carry a map and compass, and expect exposed conditions even in summer.
