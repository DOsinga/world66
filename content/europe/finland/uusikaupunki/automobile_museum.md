Beside the Valmet Automotive factory, this museum connects Uusikaupunki's modern industrial life with a collection of cars, prototypes, and motoring memorabilia. It is a good stop even if you are only mildly interested in cars, because it explains why this small coastal town matters to Finnish manufacturing.
