The Bonk Museum is a deadpan museum for a fictional global corporation that supposedly pioneered anchovy-powered machines, disinformation systems, and other nonsense technologies. The joke is elaborate enough to become its own little world.

It is small, odd, and very Finnish in its straight-faced absurdity. Check opening arrangements before you go, as visits can require advance booking.
