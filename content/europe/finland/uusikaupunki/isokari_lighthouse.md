Isokari is the classic summer boat trip from Uusikaupunki: an outer-island lighthouse, open sea, low rock, and the feeling that the town's maritime history continues beyond the harbour. Scheduled cruises run in season, often combined with meals or guided walks.

Weather matters. A bright calm day makes the trip; wind and rain can turn the same outing into a very Finnish exercise in endurance.
