Pamprinniemen luontopolku is a 4-km nature trail around the Pamprinniemi peninsula, with sea views, birdlife, old spruce woods, and wind-shaped pines. The area is part of the Natura 2000 network, so it is a good low-key choice for travellers who want a short coastal walk rather than a full-day hike.
