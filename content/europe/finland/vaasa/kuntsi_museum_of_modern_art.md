Kuntsi Museum of Modern Art gives Vaasa a strong contemporary-art stop on the waterfront. The museum is based on the Kuntsi collection and presents modern Finnish and international art through rotating exhibitions in a converted harbor-side setting.

It is a good choice for visitors who want something beyond Vaasa's maritime and regional-history sights. The location near the water also makes it easy to combine with a walk around the inner harbor and nearby cultural venues.
