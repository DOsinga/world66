Replot Bridge links the mainland near Vaasa with Replot, one of the main islands of the Kvarken Archipelago. At just over a kilometre long it is Finland's longest bridge, with high pylons and wide views over the low, island-dotted coast.

It is a good stop if you are heading out into the archipelago by car or bicycle. There is also a public beach near the bridge, useful for a pause before continuing towards the outer islands.
