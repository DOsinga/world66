The Tikanoja Art Museum is housed in the former home of businessman Frithjof Tikanoja, whose private collection became the core of the museum. The collection includes Finnish and international art, with names such as Edgar Degas and Paul Gauguin giving it weight beyond a small local gallery.

It pairs well with Vaasa's other museums, especially if you want a more intimate art stop rather than a large institutional collection.
