Trinity Church is the Neo-Gothic landmark at the heart of central Vaasa. It anchors the surrounding civic blocks, with City Hall, the tourist office, and several school buildings close by.

The church is a simple but useful stop while walking through the centre. Come for the architecture and the setting rather than a long visit; it gives the surrounding streets a clear focal point and helps explain Vaasa's 19th-century planned-city feel.
