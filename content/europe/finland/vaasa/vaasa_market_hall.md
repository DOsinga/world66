The Vaasa Market Hall (Vaasan Kauppahalli) opened in 2021 in the city centre, replacing a long gap in the city's market life. Finnish market halls have a specific character — half grocery, half lunch spot, heavy on local produce and prepared food — and this one fits that tradition well. The emphasis is on Ostrobothnian products: fish from the Gulf of Bothnia, local breads, cheeses, and the kind of lunch food (sausages, open sandwiches, soup) that Finns do efficiently and well.

It's a good stop for lunch before heading to the museums or the harbour. The building also hosts small events and temporary stalls that vary by season.
