---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

Summer (June–August) is the best time to visit. Vaasa sits at 63°N, which means near-midnight daylight in June — the sun barely sets and the harbour and archipelago come completely alive. The Kvarken archipelago is accessible by boat, the outdoor terraces fill every evening, and the crane migration at Söderfjärden peaks in September and October.

Winter is long, cold, and dark. The Gulf of Bothnia often freezes, and when it does, the sea ice creates a strange flat landscape that locals use for walking, skiing, and sometimes driving to the nearest islands. This is a very Finnish experience and worth seeking out, but it requires an acceptance of serious cold and very short days.

Spring (April–May) can be rewarding — the ice breaks up, migrating birds fill the archipelago, and the city emerges from winter without the summer tourist pressure.
