Taipale Canal tells Varkaus's waterway story in one compact place. The original canal opened in 1840 and was an early piece of Finnish inland navigation; a wider later canal now handles modern boat traffic.

It is a quiet, technical sight rather than a dramatic one, but it pairs well with the town's industrial character. Walk it if you are interested in how the Saimaa lake system was made useful for travel and trade.
