type: section
---

Vyborg (Viipuri) packs a remarkable amount of history into a small area. The medieval castle, the old town's winding lanes, Alvar Aalto's modernist library, and the sprawling Monrepos park can all be reached on foot from the market square. Note that as of 2022, the Russia–Finland border is closed, and visiting Vyborg requires travelling from Russia — typically from St Petersburg, 140 km to the east.
