Annecy sits at the northern tip of [Lake Annecy](/europe/france/alpes/annecy/lake_annecy), ringed by mountains that still carry snow well into spring. The [old town](/europe/france/alpes/annecy/vieille_ville) is built around a canal that runs from the lake through cobbled streets lined with pastel-coloured houses — locals call it the Venice of the Alps, and for once the comparison is not entirely absurd.

An 11th-century [castle](/europe/france/alpes/annecy/chateau) built by the Princes of Savoy dominates the town from a rocky spur above the old quarter. Its courtyard and the medieval prisons below are open to visitors. The [Palais de l'Île](/europe/france/alpes/annecy/palais_de_lile), a picturesque stone building straddling the canal, is the most photographed landmark and houses a small history museum. The old town also has the gothic [Église Saint-Maurice](/europe/france/alpes/annecy/eglise_saint_maurice) and the sober [Cathédrale Saint-Pierre](/europe/france/alpes/annecy/cathedral), with the hilltop [Basilique de la Visitation](/europe/france/alpes/annecy/basilique_visitation) above.

The lake is the main draw in summer — remarkably clean water, good swimming beaches at the [Plage de l'Impérial](/europe/france/alpes/annecy/plage_imperial), and every kind of water sport available for hire. The [42-kilometre cycle path](/europe/france/alpes/annecy/voie_verte) around the lake is flat and scenic, and the [Col de la Forclaz](/europe/france/alpes/annecy/col_de_la_forclaz) above the east shore is one of the great paragliding sites in Europe. In winter, the Mont Blanc area and the ski resorts of La Clusaz and Le Grand-Bornand are within easy reach.

Annecy hosts the Old Town Festival in July, a popular Lake Festival in August, and the annual [International Animated Film Festival](/europe/france/alpes/annecy/festival_international_film_animation), one of the most important in the world.
