Lake Annecy stretches 14 kilometres south from the city, framed by the Massif des Bauges to the west and the Bornes-Aravis to the east. Its reputation as the cleanest large lake in Europe is largely justified — the water clarity often reaches 7 or 8 metres, and the colour shifts from emerald to deep blue depending on the angle of the sun.

The 42-kilometre lakeside cycle path (the Voie Verte) loops the entire lake, mostly on dedicated track converted from an old railway. The municipal beaches at the [Plage d'Annecy](/europe/france/alpes/annecy/plage_imperial), [Plage du Pâquier](/europe/france/alpes/annecy/plage_imperial) and a string of village beaches further south offer free or cheap swimming. Sailing, windsurfing, paddleboarding and pedalo rental are available everywhere; even paragliding from the Col de la Forclaz lands on the lakeside fields.
