Annecy is tied closely to animation through its major international festival, and this small museum gives that connection a physical home. Exhibits focus on drawings, storyboards, cut-outs, optical toys, and production material that show how still images become movement.

It is a compact stop rather than a grand museum, best paired with the old town and the castle above it. For film fans it adds a different angle to Annecy, shifting the visit from canals and Alpine views toward the city's contemporary cultural identity.
