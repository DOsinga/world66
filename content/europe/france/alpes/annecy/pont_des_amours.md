The Pont des Amours is a small green iron footbridge thrown across the entrance of the Canal du Vassé, connecting the [Jardins de l'Europe](/europe/france/alpes/annecy/jardins_de_leurope) to the [Pâquier](/europe/france/alpes/annecy/le_paquier). It was built in 1907 to a then-fashionable lattice design and has been called the Lovers' Bridge ever since — local tradition holds that a couple kissing at its midpoint will stay together forever.

Whether or not you bring a partner, walk across it at sunset: the view down the Canal du Vassé framed between the rows of plane trees is one of the classic Annecy photographs.
