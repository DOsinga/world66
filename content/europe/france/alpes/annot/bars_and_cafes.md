---
title: Bars and Cafes
type: section
---

The social life of Annot happens on and around the main square, particularly under the medieval arcades that line one side of it. A couple of cafes put out chairs when the weather is good — which is most of the time from May through September. Drinks are inexpensive by the standards of larger Provençal towns. In the evenings the square is quiet and the light on the boulders above is worth staying out for.
