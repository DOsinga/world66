Ceillac is one of the first villages you reach in the [Queyras](/europe/france/alpes/queyras) regional park, a quiet corner of the southern French Alps near the Italian border. There is a [small ski resort](/europe/france/alpes/ceillac/ceillac_ski_resort), but it has been kept separate from and above the main village, so the settlement retains its mountain character.

The houses still preserve their traditional features, and some bear [painted sundials](/europe/france/alpes/ceillac/village_sundials) — a Queyras speciality found throughout the park but particularly well preserved here. The wildflowers in the surrounding Alpine pastures are fabulous, particularly in June and July. Walking trails lead up to [Lac Miroir](/europe/france/alpes/ceillac/lac_miroir) and [Lac Sainte-Anne](/europe/france/alpes/ceillac/lac_sainte_anne), both spectacularly set beneath rocky peaks. The lakes are the main reason to come in summer, and neither disappoints.
