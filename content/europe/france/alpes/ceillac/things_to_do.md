---
title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Ceillac's appeal is almost entirely outdoors. The surrounding landscape in the Queyras regional park offers some of the finest walking in the southern French Alps, with high-altitude lakes, wildflower meadows and well-marked trails that see far fewer walkers than the more popular Écrins massif. In winter, the small ski resort above the village provides gentle skiing and cross-country trails.
