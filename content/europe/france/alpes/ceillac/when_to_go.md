---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

Summer (mid-June to mid-September) is the main season for walking and outdoor activities. Late June and July are the best for wildflowers, particularly in the meadows above the village and around the lakes. August brings more visitors but the area never feels crowded by the standards of more famous Alpine resorts.

The ski season typically runs from December to March, depending on snow conditions. Snow is usually reliable above 2,000 metres. Spring (April to early June) sees the lakes still frozen and the roads above the village often snowbound — most facilities are closed. Autumn arrives early at altitude, and by October the village is quiet and the days short, but the colours on the larch trees can be extraordinary.
