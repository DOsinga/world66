Chambéry Cathedral, dedicated to Saint François de Sales, is easy to miss if you expect a dramatic French west front. Go inside. The interior is known for trompe-l'oeil decoration that turns walls and vaults into a painted architectural illusion.

It is a good stop between the elephant fountain and the old lanes, and it helps explain Chambéry's mix of modest scale and serious Savoyard history.
