Chamonix eats well for a mountain resort. The Savoyard staples — fondue, raclette, tartiflette — are everywhere, but the better restaurants go further than the cheese-and-potato comfort rotation. There is a cluster of genuinely ambitious kitchens in the town centre, and the après-ski economy means quality standards are kept high by a demanding international clientele.
