Chamonix is essentially one giant outdoor playground, arranged vertically. At the bottom, the town itself has a lively centre, an alpine museum, and a legacy of Olympic history. Above that, cable cars and rack railways lift you into a world of glaciers, aiguilles, and sky-high viewpoints. Whether you come in winter or summer, the question is not what to do — it's which of the many things to do first.
