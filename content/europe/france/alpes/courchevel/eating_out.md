---
title: "Eating Out"
type: section
---

Courchevel has more Michelin stars per resident than most cities. **Le Chabichou** in 1850 (two stars, on the piste) and the legendary **La Bouitte** in the adjacent Belleville valley (three stars) are the headline destinations. Both demand booking weeks ahead in season.

For sun-and-champagne lunches on a vast terrace next to the altiport, **Le Cap Horn** is the institution. Beyond the marquee names, the **Le Praz** village holds a clutch of unpretentious Savoyard places where fondue, raclette, and *tartiflette* are taken seriously and the bill does not require a second mortgage.
