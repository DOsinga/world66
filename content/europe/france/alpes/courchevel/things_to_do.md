---
title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

In winter, the answer is skiing. Courchevel is the western anchor of the **Trois Vallées**, the largest linked ski area in the world. The terrain ranges from gentle blues through the trees above 1550 to the steep Combe de la Saulire and the famous Grand Couloir off the **Saulire** summit. Cross-country trails, snow-shoeing routes, dog-sledding, and ice-driving courses fill in the gaps.

Out of season, the **Vanoise National Park** to the east is the great walking destination, with day hikes from Pralognan and the higher trails to spots like the **Lacs Merlet**. Closer to the resort, the **Olympic ski jumps at Le Praz** can be visited in summer, and the spectacular **Courchevel Altiport** is worth a walk in any season for the views and the take-offs. Rainy days lend themselves to **Aquamotion**, Europe's largest alpine water complex.
