---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

The main season is **December through April**. The resort's altitude (lifts run to 2,738 metres at Saulire and well above 3,000 in the upper Trois Vallées) keeps snow reliable later than at lower French resorts. February school holidays are the most expensive and crowded weeks. **March** typically delivers the best combination of long sunny days and good snow.

**Summer** (mid-June through early September) is a quieter parallel season for hiking, mountain biking, and trail running. A small set of lifts stays open. Late September and October close the resort almost entirely — many hotels and restaurants shut from mid-April until late June, and again from October until the snow returns. Avoid those shoulder weeks unless you specifically want an empty mountain.
