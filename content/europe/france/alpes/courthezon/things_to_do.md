---
title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Courthézon's main appeal is its wine. Two of the appellation's most celebrated estates are within a few minutes' drive, and the cooperative [Cellier des Princes](/europe/france/alpes/courthezon/cellier_des_princes) welcomes visitors without an appointment. The medieval village centre, with well-preserved 12th-century ramparts and three surviving gates, rewards a short walk. [Château Val Seille](/europe/france/alpes/courthezon/chateau_val_seille), the ornate 19th-century town hall set in formal parkland, is open to visitors.
