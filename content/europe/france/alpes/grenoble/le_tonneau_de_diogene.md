A brasserie occupying a vaulted stone room on Place Notre-Dame, a few steps from the cathedral. The menu leans into Dauphiné classics — gratins dauphinois, cheese fondue, and duck confit alongside French bistro standards. The lunch formule is good value and the setting — exposed stone, wooden beams — is genuinely atmospheric. Busy at weekends; book ahead or arrive early.
