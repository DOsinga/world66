The Little Train of La Mure is a scenic heritage railway south of Grenoble, originally built to move coal from the Matheysine plateau and now valued for its mountain views. The route threads above lakes, ravines, and steep slopes, giving passengers an easy way to see Alpine scenery without a strenuous hike.

It is a seasonal attraction and should be planned rather than assumed, especially outside summer. The train works well as a family-friendly day trip from Grenoble or as part of a broader Matheysine excursion. Sit on the view side when possible and allow time around La Mure rather than treating the ride as only transport.
