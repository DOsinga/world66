Cité Internationale stretches along the Rhône beside Parc de la Tête d’Or, a long contemporary complex designed by Renzo Piano around offices, conference spaces, restaurants, and cultural venues. Its red-brick-and-glass rhythm is a sharp change from Lyon’s Renaissance lanes and nineteenth-century boulevards.

It is most useful as part of a walk linking the park, the river, and the Musée d’Art Contemporain. The complex can feel corporate, but the scale, arcades, and park-side setting make it a worthwhile stop for anyone interested in modern Lyon.
