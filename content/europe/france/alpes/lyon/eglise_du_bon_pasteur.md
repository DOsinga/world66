Église du Bon-Pasteur is a 19th-century Romanesque Revival church on the slopes near Croix-Rousse. Its oddity is architectural as much as religious: construction conflicts with the barracks opposite left it without a normal front entrance.

It is a small but memorable stop when walking the lanes and stairways between Croix-Rousse and central Lyon.
