The États-Unis neighbourhood in southeast Lyon was planned in the 1920s by Tony Garnier, the architect behind some of the city's most ambitious early modern projects. Its main draw for visitors is the set of large wall paintings that show Garnier's work and his ideas for an ideal industrial city.

This is not postcard Lyon, and that is part of the appeal. Come if you are interested in urban planning, public housing history, or the way Lyon expanded beyond its old centre.
