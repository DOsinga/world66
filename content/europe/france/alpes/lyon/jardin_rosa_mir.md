Jardin Rosa Mir is a small, highly worked garden in Croix-Rousse, built by Spanish refugee Jules Senis and dedicated to his mother. Its mix of shells, stone, plants, and Gaudi-like detail makes it a focused stop for visitors interested in Lyon's quieter artistic corners.
