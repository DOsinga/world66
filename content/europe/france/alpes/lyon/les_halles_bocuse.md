Les Halles de Lyon Paul Bocuse, on the Cours Lafayette near Part-Dieu, is the city's great covered food market — a temple to Lyonnais gastronomy and the essential first stop for anyone serious about eating in the city. Around sixty stalls sell the products that define the local food culture: **quenelles** (delicate fish dumplings), **rosette de Lyon** (cured pork sausage), **Jésus de Lyon** (large, coarsely textured pork sausage), pink praline tarts, Saint-Marcellin and Saint-Félicien cheeses, fresh truffles in season, and Beaujolais wine.

The atmosphere is extraordinary — professional chefs and home cooks shop alongside tourists, and the vendors are demonstrably proud of what they sell. The market opens Tuesday to Sunday until 7pm, unusual for a French market of this type.
