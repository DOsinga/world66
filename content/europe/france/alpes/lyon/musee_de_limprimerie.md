Musee de l'Imprimerie tells one of Lyon's most important but less obvious stories: the city as a major European centre of printing, publishing, and graphic communication. In the 15th and 16th centuries Lyon stood with places like Venice and Leipzig in the book trade, and the museum uses that history to open out into type, images, presses, and later technologies.

It is a strong choice if you like books, design, or the mechanics behind culture. Check the official status before going, as the museum has had temporary closure periods for works. When open, it gives Presqu'ile a thoughtful indoor stop between churches, squares, and restaurants.
