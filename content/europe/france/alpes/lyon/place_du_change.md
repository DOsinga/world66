Place du Change is one of the main squares of Vieux Lyon. Its west side is marked by the Loge du Change, partly designed by Soufflot and now used as the Temple du Change, while the opposite side has the Gothic facade of Maison Thomassin. It is best seen as part of a walk through the old town and its traboules.
