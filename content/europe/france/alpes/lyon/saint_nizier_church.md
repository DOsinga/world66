Saint-Nizier is one of central Lyon’s most rewarding churches, standing in the Presqu’île between the two rivers. Its asymmetrical towers and Flamboyant Gothic stonework make it distinctive from the outside, while the interior has the layered feel of a parish church that has lived through the city’s commercial and religious history.

It is easy to miss because Lyon has bigger headline monuments, but it belongs on a walk from Place des Terreaux toward the shopping streets of the Presqu’île. Step inside for a quieter, less staged encounter with Lyon’s church architecture before crossing to Vieux Lyon or the river quays.
