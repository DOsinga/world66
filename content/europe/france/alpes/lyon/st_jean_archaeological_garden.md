St Jean Archaeological Garden sits beside Lyon's cathedral and preserves remains of religious buildings that stood here before the present church. The oldest visible traces date to the 4th century.

It is a short stop, but it adds useful context to Vieux Lyon and the long religious history around the cathedral square.
