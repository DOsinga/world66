---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

Lyon is a year-round city. The **Fête des Lumières** in early December — when the entire city is illuminated with light installations in a tradition dating to 1852 — is the most spectacular single event in the Lyon calendar and draws around two million visitors over four evenings. Book accommodation months in advance if you plan to be there. **June and July** bring the Nuits de Fourvière festival, with open-air performances in the Roman theatres. **Spring** (April–May) and early autumn (September–October) are the most pleasant for walking. Summers are warm to hot (25–32°C), with the occasional August heatwave; winters are grey and damp, though mild by northern European standards.
