---
title: "Bars and Cafes"
type: section
---

Méribel's après-ski scene is lively and centred on the Rond-Point des Pistes at the base of the main ski area. Most bars open from around 4pm when the lifts close and run until midnight or later.

**La Folie Douce** at the top of the Pas du Lac gondola is the most famous spot in the entire Trois Vallées — a mountain restaurant and concert venue where DJs and live performers play from mid-afternoon as skiers arrive in ski boots for the party. It is an institution, and a crowded, heaving one: go at least once for the experience.

**Le Rond-Point** at the base of the slopes is a reliable choice for the classic afternoon vin chaud (mulled wine) or a Savoyard beer. The terrace fills up quickly after the lifts close.

**Les Allues Bar** in the original village of Les Allues (the old farming community below the resort) is a more local option — quieter, less expensive, and popular with resort workers.

Mountain cafes on the pistes serve the usual combination of hot chocolate, soupe de chalet, and tartiflette. On a cold day, stopping for a vin chaud on the Roc de Fer terrace at the top of the Saulire is one of the simple pleasures of Méribel skiing.
