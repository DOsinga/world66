The Méribel Ice Palace was built for the 1992 Albertville Winter Olympics, when the resort served as the venue for ice hockey and figure skating competitions. The arena seats around 3,500 spectators and operates as a public ice rink throughout the winter season. Public skating sessions are held most days, with equipment hire available on-site.

The ice rink is a genuine plus for families and non-skiers — an afternoon of skating here is a good alternative to the slopes, and the facility is well-maintained. The arena also hosts ice hockey matches and occasional ice shows during the winter season.

For non-skiers in a party of skiers, the Ice Palace is one of the better options in the resort. The opening hours vary by season and day; check the resort website before going. It is located in the lower village area and easily reached on foot or by the free resort ski bus.
