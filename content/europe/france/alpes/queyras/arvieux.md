Arvieux is a string of small hamlets in the western Queyras, on the road between Guillestre and the rest of the park. It was historically famous for its handmade wooden toys — an industry that revived in the early 20th century to provide winter income, and which still operates in a small workshop in the village today.

The village retains some of the best traditional architecture in the park, particularly the hamlet of La Chalp, with its tall wooden hayloft houses.
