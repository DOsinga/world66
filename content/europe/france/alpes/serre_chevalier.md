Serre-Chevalier is not a single village so much as a long, high Alpine playground in the Guisane valley. It runs from the fortified town of Briancon through Chantemerle and Villeneuve to Le Monetier-les-Bains, with ski lifts climbing the sunny slopes above the valley and the wild edge of the Ecrins close by.

In winter, the reason to come is the [Serre Chevalier ski area](/europe/france/alpes/serre_chevalier/serre_chevalier_ski_area): a big, linked domain of high runs, larch forest pistes, and enough terrain that intermediate skiers can roam for days. In summer the valley turns toward hiking, mountain biking, white-water sports, and road cycling, especially the hard climb to the [Col du Granon](/europe/france/alpes/serre_chevalier/col_du_granon).

The place has a softer side too. [Les Grands Bains du Monetier](/europe/france/alpes/serre_chevalier/grands_bains_du_monetier) gives the upper valley proper thermal-bath character, and Briancon's walls remind you that this has long been a frontier valley, not just a lift system. Serre-Chevalier is best if you want mountain days with a little more local texture than a purpose-built resort.
