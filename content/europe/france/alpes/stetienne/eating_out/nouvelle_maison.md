A well-regarded bistro in the city centre serving market-driven French cooking with strong regional roots — quenelles, charcuterie from the Forez hills, and Loire valley cheeses feature regularly. The menu changes with the seasons and the kitchen takes its produce seriously. Lunch menus are particularly good value: two courses for well under €20. Booking recommended for dinner; the room is small and fills quickly.
