---
title: When to Go
type: section
---

Saint-Étienne sits at around 500 metres elevation, which keeps summers pleasantly cool compared to the Rhône valley below. Spring (April–June) and early autumn (September–October) are the most comfortable times to visit — mild temperatures, light crowds, and the Pilat park at its best for walking.

The Biennale Internationale Design Saint-Étienne is held every two years (even-numbered years) in spring, drawing designers and architecture enthusiasts from across Europe. If your visit coincides with it, book accommodation early.

ASSE home football matches run from August through May, with the biggest games in autumn and spring. The Chaudron atmosphere is best for derby fixtures and cup matches — check the schedule at the club's website before you travel.

Winters are cold and occasionally snowy, but the city stays functional and hotels are cheap. The Pilat park gets good snow cover from December to February, making it popular with cross-country skiers.
