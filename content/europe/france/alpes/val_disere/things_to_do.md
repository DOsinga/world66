Val-d'Isère is built around outdoor days. In winter the lift system is the main event, from easy nursery slopes to Olympic black runs and guided off-piste terrain. In summer the same altitude becomes the appeal: hiking into the Vanoise, riding the bike park, or crossing the Col de l'Iseran when the road is clear.
