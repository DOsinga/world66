Château du Haut-Kœnigsbourg stands on a rocky spur above the Upper Rhine Plain. The medieval castle was ruined in the Thirty Years' War and rebuilt in the late 19th century, so the visit is as much about romantic reconstruction as original stonework, but the scale, setting, and views make it one of Alsace's strongest castle stops.
