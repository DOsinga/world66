Schirmeck is a small town in the Bruche valley of the Vosges mountains, midway between [Strasbourg](/europe/france/alsace/strasbourg) and the [Col du Donon](/europe/france/alsace/schirmeck/col_du_donon). It is an unassuming place, but it sits in beautiful hiking country and carries a weight of history that belies its size.

The [Mémorial d'Alsace-Moselle](/europe/france/alsace/schirmeck/memorial_alsace_moselle) on the hillside above town tells the story of Alsace's painful back-and-forth between France and Germany from 1870 to 1945. A few kilometres south, the former [Natzweiler-Struthof concentration camp](/europe/france/alsace/schirmeck/natzweiler_struthof) — the only Nazi camp on French soil — is preserved as a memorial. Both sites are sobering and well worth visiting.

For lighter pursuits, the [Bruche valley voie verte](/europe/france/alsace/schirmeck/bruche_valley_voie_verte) follows the old railway line through the valley, and the forested ridges above offer well-marked trails through beech and fir forest.
