Museum Tomi Ungerer is devoted to the Strasbourg-born illustrator whose work ranged from children's books to political satire and sharp adult graphic art. The collection gives a different view of Alsatian creativity from the city's cathedrals, timbered lanes, and formal fine-art museums.

It is especially worthwhile for travellers interested in illustration, posters, publishing, or visual satire. The museum is compact enough to combine with the Neustadt, Parc de l'Orangerie, or the museums around the historic centre.
