Stockfeld is an early-20th-century garden city in Strasbourg's Neuhof district, planned with low houses, gardens, and a more open layout than the dense historic centre. It is a quiet architectural detour for visitors interested in urban planning and social housing history.

The district is south-east of the centre and is best treated as a short walk rather than a major attraction. Public transport is the simplest way to reach it.
