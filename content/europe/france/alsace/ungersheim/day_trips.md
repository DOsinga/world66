---
title: "Day Trips"
type: section
---

Ungersheim is well placed for exploring the best of Alsace.

**Colmar** (25km north) is the most obvious next stop — a beautifully preserved medieval town with canals, colourful half-timbered houses, and the Petite Venise quarter reflected in the water. The Unterlinden Museum houses the Isenheim Altarpiece, one of the great works of northern European art.

**Mulhouse** (15km southeast) is an industrial city with unexpectedly excellent museums, including the Cité de l'Automobile — the largest car museum in the world — and the Cité du Train for railway history.

**Route des Vins d'Alsace** begins just west of Ungersheim. The classic Alsace wine trail threads through Riquewihr, Kaysersberg, and Eguisheim, passing vineyards, stork nests, and wine cellars. Allow a full day to drive it properly.

**Eguisheim** (20km north) is consistently rated one of France's most beautiful villages. Its circular medieval centre of pastel half-timbered houses can be walked in under an hour, but few places in Alsace are more photogenic.
