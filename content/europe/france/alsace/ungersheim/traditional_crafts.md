title: Traditional Craft Demonstrations
type: poi

Resident craftspeople at the Écomusée demonstrate the traditional trades that sustained Alsatian villages for centuries — lacemaking, pottery, basket weaving, blacksmithing, and others depending on the season. Unlike a static museum display, these are working demonstrations where you can watch the craft being practised and often ask questions.

The programme varies by season and day; check the Écomusée's schedule when planning your visit.
