A surf club rather than a holiday operation, carrying the French Surfing Federation label and running sessions right through the year rather than just in August — Wednesdays, Fridays, weekends out of season, and daily in the school holidays.

Sessions last an hour and a half to a little over, covering surf, bodyboard and stand-up paddle, at discovery level or as improvement coaching for people who already stand up. Being at Le Moulleau, the south-western corner of Arcachon, puts it closest to the ocean end of town.

Cards, cheques, transfers and Chèques Vacances all accepted.
