---
title: When to Go
type: section
---

Bayonne has a mild Atlantic climate — warm summers, mild winters, and rain year-round. The driest months are June through September. July and August are busy with summer tourists but the town still functions normally, unlike some coastal resorts.

The **Fêtes de Bayonne** in late July or early August is one of the great street festivals of Europe — five days of music, dancing, and bullfighting attended by over a million people. The streets fill with people in white with red scarves, and the festivities run from midday to dawn. Book accommodation months in advance if you plan to attend; it is worth it if you are willing to embrace the chaos. If you prefer a quieter visit, May and June offer good weather and empty streets.
