The Grosse Cloche — "the Big Bell" — is a medieval fortified gate on the Rue Saint-James dating from the 15th century. It is one of the oldest surviving structures in Bordeaux and one of the most photogenic: two squat stone towers flanking a vaulted passage, all worn amber-grey limestone. It was once part of the city's defensive walls and served as the town hall and prison as well as a gate.

The gate is best seen from a little distance, looking along the old pilgrim road that once ran through it toward Santiago de Compostela. The Rue Saint-James is still a lively commercial street, and the combination of medieval gate and busy French street life is very Bordeaux.
