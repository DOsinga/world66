The Halle des Chartrons is a covered market in a renovated 19th-century building in the heart of the Chartrons neighbourhood. Weekend mornings see it at its liveliest, with food producers, artisan bread bakers, cheese vendors, and a central bar serving wine and coffee to the morning crowd.

The market is smaller than the Capucins or Victor Hugo but more atmospheric — the Chartrons crowd is largely local, the produce is well-selected, and the pace is relaxed. The surrounding streets make a natural extension of the market visit.
