The Musée Mer Marine gives Bordeaux’s port history a modern home in the Bassins à flot, the former docklands north of the historic centre. Its exhibitions move between navigation, ocean exploration, ship models, marine art, and environmental questions, which suits a city whose wealth and identity have always been tied to the Garonne and the Atlantic.

It is not as essential as the Musée d’Aquitaine, but it is a strong choice if you are exploring the Bassins à flot, the submarine base, or the Cité du Vin area. The building is spacious, the subject matter is broader than local history, and temporary exhibitions can make it more than a rainy-day fallback.
