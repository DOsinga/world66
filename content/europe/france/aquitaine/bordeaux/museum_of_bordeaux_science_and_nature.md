The Museum of Bordeaux - Science and Nature occupies the edge of the Jardin Public, which makes it easy to pair with a calm walk away from the wine bars and grand stone streets. Its natural history collections are among the strongest in France, with zoology, geology, and paleontology presented in a building that reopened after a long renovation.

This is a good choice for families, rainy afternoons, or anyone who likes a city museum with more than paintings. The setting helps too: after the galleries, the public garden gives you one of central Bordeaux's best places to sit, wander, or let children run around.
