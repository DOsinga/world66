---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

Bordeaux has a mild Atlantic climate — hot dry summers, mild wet winters, with enough rain throughout the year to keep the Garonne flowing. The tourist season peaks in July and August, when the city fills with visitors and the restaurants and wine bars are at their liveliest, though also their most expensive and most crowded.

The wine calendar shapes the best time for enthusiasts. Harvest (vendanges) happens in September and October, when the vineyards around the city are at their most active. The great châteaux in Médoc and Saint-Émilion are particularly atmospheric during this period. June and September are arguably the best months: long days, warm weather, manageable crowds.

Winter in Bordeaux is worth defending. The limestone facades glow even in grey Atlantic light, the restaurants are better value, and the cultural calendar — opera at the Grand Théâtre, exhibitions at the CAPC — is at full speed. The wine bars don't empty out in January.
