Hossegor's surf is world-class and mostly unsuitable for learning — La Gravière in particular is a heavy, shallow barrel that breaks close to shore. The schools here know that, and teach on the gentler beaches north and south of the main peak, or at Seignosse and Capbreton when the swell is too big.

Lessons and board hire run year-round rather than just in summer, which is unusual on this coast and reflects how many people come here specifically to surf.
