Ars-en-Ré rewards slow exploration. The village is small enough to cover entirely on foot, and the pleasures are correspondingly simple: the harbour, the church, the salt marshes, and the cycle paths that thread through the island. It is the kind of place where the best thing to do is not go anywhere in particular, and where sitting at a café table by the water counts as a productive afternoon.

The obvious orientation point is the church steeple — black and white, visible from boats at sea — which rises above the tiled rooftops and salt-marsh grasses. Once you have found that, you have found the village.
