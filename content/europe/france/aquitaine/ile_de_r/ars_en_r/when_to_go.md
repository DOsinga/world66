---
title: When to Go
type: section
tags: [when_to_go]
---

The Île de Ré is one of France's most popular summer destinations, and in July and August Ars-en-Ré fills up with French families on holiday. The village remains charming even then — it is small enough not to feel overwhelmed — but accommodation is expensive and the cycle paths get busy. If you can manage it, late May or June is ideal: the weather is warm, the wildflowers are out in the salt marshes, and the tourist season has not yet fully arrived.

September is also excellent. The crowds thin, the light turns golden, and the oysters — which many locals consider at their best in autumn — are at their richest. The island is windy by nature, with Atlantic weather arriving from the west. Bring a layer even in midsummer.
