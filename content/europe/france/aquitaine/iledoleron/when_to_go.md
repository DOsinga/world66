---
title: When to Go
type: section
---

Île d'Oléron is overwhelmingly a summer destination. **July and August** bring enormous crowds of French holidaymakers — camping sites fill weeks in advance, restaurant queues stretch out the door, and the beach car parks overflow. Prices peak accordingly.

**June and September** are the best months: warm enough for swimming, manageable crowds, and the island retains its working character. The oyster season runs year-round and the fishing ports are active in every season. **Spring** (April–May) is beautiful for cycling through the pine forest and along the coast before the summer rush, though water temperatures are cool.

**Winter** is quiet to the point of closed — many businesses shut from November to March, and the island has an attractive melancholy all its own in the off-season.
