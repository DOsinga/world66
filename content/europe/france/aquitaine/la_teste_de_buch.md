La Teste-de-Buch is a commune on the southern shore of the Bassin d'Arcachon, about 10 km from Arcachon town. It sits at the foot of the [Dune du Pilat](/europe/france/aquitaine/la_teste_de_buch/dune_du_pilat) — the tallest sand dune in Europe at over 100 metres — which draws visitors who climb it for sweeping views across the bay and the Atlantic pine forest. The town itself is a quiet residential base with good transport connections to Bordeaux by train, and immediate access to the oyster farms of the Bassin d'Arcachon and the freshwater [Lac de Cazaux-Sanguinet](/europe/france/aquitaine/la_teste_de_buch/lac_de_cazaux).
