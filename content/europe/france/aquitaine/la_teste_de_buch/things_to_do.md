title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

La Teste-de-Buch's main draw is right on its doorstep: the [Dune du Pilat](/europe/france/aquitaine/la_teste_de_buch/dune_du_pilat), the tallest sand dune in Europe, is a short drive or cycle from town. The Bassin d'Arcachon itself offers oyster trails, boat trips, and the atmospheric Île aux Oiseaux with its wooden cabins on stilts. [Lac de Cazaux](/europe/france/aquitaine/la_teste_de_buch/lac_de_cazaux), one of the largest natural lakes in France, is ideal for swimming and watersports in summer.
