Château Angélus is one of the great names of Saint-Émilion, set west of the town among the rolling vineyard parcels that make this corner of Bordeaux so coveted. Its wines are Merlot-led, powerful, and priced far beyond casual drinking.

For travellers, the point is less to chase prestige than to understand scale. The châteaux around Saint-Émilion are working agricultural estates, luxury brands, and pieces of cultural landscape all at once. Book well ahead if you want a visit or tasting.
