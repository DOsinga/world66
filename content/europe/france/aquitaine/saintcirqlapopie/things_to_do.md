title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Saint-Cirq-Lapopie is mostly about being there — wandering the main street between the river and the castle ruins, ducking into the [church](/europe/france/aquitaine/saintcirqlapopie/eglise_saint_cirq) and the tiny museum, and finding the right terrace for an evening drink. The single unmissable walk is the [Chemin de Halage](/europe/france/aquitaine/saintcirqlapopie/chemin_de_halage), the towpath carved straight into the cliff along the Lot.

Allow a full half day if you want to do the village justice and the towpath both, and try to come outside the midday crowds in summer.
