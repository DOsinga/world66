Eglise Sainte-Colombe is a Gothic church in the Saint-Pierre quarter of Saintes, first mentioned in the 14th century. The present building shows flamboyant Gothic work and preserves part of the city's medieval religious landscape.

It is a worthwhile short detour while walking between the old center's larger Roman and church sights.
