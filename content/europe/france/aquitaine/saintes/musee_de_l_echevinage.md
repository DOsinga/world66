Musée de L'Échevinage is an art museum in the former seat of Saintes' municipal authorities under the Ancien Régime. The building is classed as a historic monument, and that setting is part of the appeal.

The collection focuses mainly on 19th-century French painting. It pairs naturally with Saintes' archaeological museum, cathedral, and Gallo-Roman sights in the old centre.
