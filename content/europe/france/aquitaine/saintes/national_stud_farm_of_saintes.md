The former national stud farm of Saintes is part of the French Haras Nationaux story, when the state managed horse breeding for military, agricultural, and prestige purposes. Its buildings add a different strand to a city better known for Roman and Romanesque monuments.

Access and uses have changed over time, so check current opening arrangements before going. Even from outside, the site helps explain Saintes as more than an ancient town: it also belonged to the practical rural systems of western France.
