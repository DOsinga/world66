title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Sainte-Livrade is a place to settle into, not a place to tick off sights. The compact old centre is built around the medieval church of [Sainte-Marie](/europe/france/aquitaine/stlivrade/eglise_sainte_marie), with the remains of an earlier tower on a square nearby. The pleasure is in wandering the lanes between the church and the river, stopping at the [riverside park](/europe/france/aquitaine/stlivrade/lot_riverside) for a coffee or a swim in summer.

The weekly market on Friday morning is the social event of the town — local produce, cheeses, prepared foods, and the southwestern staples of duck and prunes. Beyond the centre, the [CAFI memorial](/europe/france/aquitaine/stlivrade/cafi_memorial) tells the unexpected story of the Vietnamese community that settled here in the 1950s, and the surrounding orchard country is good for a slow drive towards the bastide towns of Villeneuve-sur-Lot and Pujols.
