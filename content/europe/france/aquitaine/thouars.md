Thouars sits on a rocky bend above the Thouet, in the northern corner of Deux-Sevres where Poitou begins to lean toward the Loire. It is small, but the old town has weight: ramparts, towers, Romanesque churches, steep lanes and a long ducal castle looking across the river valley.

The pleasure here is walking the edge of the old fortress town. The [Chateau des Ducs de La Tremoille](/europe/france/aquitaine/thouars/chateau_des_ducs_de_la_tremoille) is now a school, so you mostly read it from outside, but its scale explains why Thouars mattered. From there, loop through [Saint-Medard Church](/europe/france/aquitaine/thouars/saint_medard_church), the [Prince de Galles Tower](/europe/france/aquitaine/thouars/prince_de_galles_tower), and the [Prevost Gate](/europe/france/aquitaine/thouars/prevost_gate).

Thouars is not a headline stop like Poitiers or La Rochelle. It works best for travellers already moving through western France who want a quieter medieval town with enough substance to fill a day, especially if they like old stone, river views and places that still feel lived in.
