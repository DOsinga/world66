---
title: When to Go
type: section
---

Beauvais sits in the flat farmland of the Oise valley at roughly the same latitude as London, and the climate reflects that: mild summers, cool wet winters, no dramatic season. Spring (April to June) and early autumn (September to October) are the most comfortable times to visit, with reasonable temperatures and less likelihood of the grey drizzle that settles over northern France in winter.

The cathedral looks its best on clear mornings, when sunlight angles through the south transept rose window into the choir. A heavy overcast flattens the interior, so if the sky is cloud-covered when you arrive, consider returning after lunch when Atlantic weather systems sometimes lift.

The Saturday market on Place Jeanne Hachette runs year-round and is liveliest in summer. There is no compelling reason to time a trip around a particular festival — Beauvais is not a city whose calendar defines its character. Come when the light looks good and you have a few hours to spare between flights.
