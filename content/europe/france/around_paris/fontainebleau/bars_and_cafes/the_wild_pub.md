An English-style pub popular with INSEAD students and the international climbing crowd that descends on Fontainebleau at weekends. Good selection of draught beer, a menu of pub food, and a lively atmosphere on Friday and Saturday evenings. Gets busy late; it's one of the few places in town with any nightlife worth the name.
