The restaurant of the Hôtel Napoléon, a few minutes' walk from the palace. The setting is the most formally grand in town — Empire-era décor, white tablecloths, a terrace overlooking the gardens. The cooking is classic French, technically accomplished, and oriented toward the palace-visiting crowd. A good choice for a proper sit-down lunch after a morning in the château.
