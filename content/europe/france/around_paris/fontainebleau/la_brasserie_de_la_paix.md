A traditional brasserie on one of the main streets leading to the palace, with a covered terrace that's pleasant for lunch in good weather. The menu is straightforward French brasserie — omelettes, croque-monsieur, steak frites, daily specials. Popular with local workers at lunchtime and with visitors between palace and forest. Reasonable prices and brisk, friendly service.
