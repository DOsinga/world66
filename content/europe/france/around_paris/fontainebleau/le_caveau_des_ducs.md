A well-established brasserie in a vaulted stone cellar close to the palace, serving classic French dishes — steak, duck confit, crème brûlée — in generous portions. The atmosphere is convivial rather than refined; it fills up at lunch with a mix of day-trippers and locals. Good value for the area and reliable across the menu.
