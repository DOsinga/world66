The Gorges de Franchard is one of the most dramatic sections of the Forest of Fontainebleau — a rocky gorge of pale sandstone formations rising above open heathland, with ancient oaks clinging to the boulders. Walking trails loop through the area on well-signed paths; the full circuit takes about two hours at an easy pace.

The ruined hermitage at the centre of the gorge adds a touch of Romantic atmosphere. The area is less crowded than the bouldering sectors near the road, and in autumn the heath and forest turn extraordinary colours. It is a good destination for families with children who enjoy scrambling on rocks without the intensity of a full climbing session.
