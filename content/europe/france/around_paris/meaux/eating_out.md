---
title: Eating Out
type: section
---

Meaux's restaurant scene is modest but grounded. Local produce — especially the Brie de Meaux — shows up on menus across town, and the Saturday market keeps chefs honest about seasonality. The area around the cathedral and the old town has the highest concentration of places to eat; the streets leading down to the Marne are quieter but worth exploring for simpler, cheaper options.
