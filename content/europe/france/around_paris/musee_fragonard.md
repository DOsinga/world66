Musée Fragonard is a small veterinary and anatomical museum in Maisons-Alfort, best known for preserved animal specimens and the eerie 18th-century anatomical preparations of Honoré Fragonard. It is not a casual family museum, but it is memorable if you like medical history, natural history, and cabinets of curiosities.
