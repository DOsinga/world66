Bois de Saint-Cucufa is the greenest escape in Rueil-Malmaison, a compact forest with a lake, walking paths, a cycling loop, and outdoor fitness trails. It is the kind of place that makes the western Paris suburbs feel less suburban for an hour or two.

It pairs well with the Malmaison sights if you want some fresh air after the chateau circuit. The forest is not a headline attraction, but it is a good low-key walk when you are already in Rueil.
