---
title: Getting There
type: section
---

Clermont-Ferrand Auvergne Airport (CFE) has domestic flights to Paris (several daily) and a limited range of European destinations. The city is also well-served by TGV from Paris Gare de Lyon, with journey times of around three hours. From Lyon, the train takes about 1h30. By car, Clermont-Ferrand is at the junction of the A71 (from Paris via Orléans) and the A75 (south toward Montpellier) — a major motorway intersection that makes the city easily accessible from most of France.

The Clermont-Ferrand train station is a 15-minute walk from Place de Jaude or a short tram ride.
