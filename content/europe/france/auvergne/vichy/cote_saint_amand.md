Côte Saint-Amand, including the Site des Hurlevents above Le Vernet, gives one of the clearer views back toward Vichy and the Allier valley. The orientation table and open natural area make it a simple but worthwhile outing when you want a short climb and a wider look at the landscape around the spa town.
