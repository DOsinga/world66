---
title: Getting There
type: section
---

**By train** is the most practical option. Vichy has its own station on the main Paris–Clermont-Ferrand line, with direct TGV connections to Paris Gare de Lyon taking around three hours. From Clermont-Ferrand (the regional hub, 45 minutes by regional train) there are connections to Lyon and further south. The station is about ten minutes' walk from the Parc des Sources.

**By car**, Vichy is accessible from the A71 autoroute (Paris–Clermont-Ferrand), leaving at the Vichy exit. Driving time from Paris is around three and a half hours. There is adequate car parking near the centre, though the streets around the thermal district can be busy in high season.

**By air**, the nearest airports are **Clermont-Ferrand Auvergne** (about 55 km, with car hire available) and **Lyon-Saint Exupéry** (about 150 km, with good motorway connections). Neither has extensive international routes, so most visitors flying from outside France will connect through Paris.
