---
title: Eating Out
type: section
---

Dijon eats well. The classical Burgundian repertoire — boeuf bourguignon, oeufs en meurette, jambon persillé, escargots, coq au vin — is on every traditional menu, and most places do it well. The city has a Michelin scene to match its food reputation, with several starred restaurants in the historic centre and the suburbs.
