Cannes is glamour on the French Riviera — a city that built its identity on celebrity, film festivals, and designer shopping, but still has the bones of a Provençal fishing town underneath. The annual [Cannes Film Festival](/europe/france/cannes/cannes_film_festival) turns the [Croisette](/europe/france/cannes/promenade_de_la_croisette) into a global stage every May, but outside those two frantic weeks, Cannes is a surprisingly walkable place with good beaches, fine dining, and easy day trips along the coast.

The old quarter, [Le Suquet](/europe/france/cannes/le_suquet), climbs the hill above the [Vieux Port](/europe/france/cannes/vieux_port) with narrow lanes and views over the bay. The [Musée de la Castre](/europe/france/cannes/musee_de_la_castre) inside the medieval castle at the top is worth the climb. Below it, the [Promenade de la Croisette](/europe/france/cannes/promenade_de_la_croisette) stretches along the waterfront past the grand facades of the [Carlton](/europe/france/cannes/carlton_hotel) and [Martinez](/europe/france/cannes/hotel_martinez) hotels. The shopping is high-end — [Dior](/europe/france/cannes/dior), Gucci, Sonia Rykiel — but [Rue d'Antibes](/europe/france/cannes/rue_dantibes) a block inland offers more accessible retail. The [Marché Forville](/europe/france/cannes/marche_forville) morning market is the best place in town for Provençal produce and people-watching.

Offshore, the Lérins Islands make an easy half-day escape: [Île Sainte-Marguerite](/europe/france/cannes/ile_sainte_marguerite) has forests, a fort, the Musée de la Mer, and a prison cell associated with the Man in the Iron Mask; tiny [Île Saint-Honorat](/europe/france/cannes/ile_saint_honorat) is home to Cistercian monks and vineyards. Ferries leave from the [Vieux Port](/europe/france/cannes/vieux_port).

Cannes sits just 16 miles from [Nice](/europe/france/nice), five miles from Mougins, and 11 miles from Grasse — the perfume capital. With the whole Côte d'Azur within day-trip range, the only problem is deciding where to go next.
