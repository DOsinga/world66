One of the most prestigious golf courses on the Côte d'Azur, the Country Club de Cannes-Mougins has operated since 1926 on a 6,312-metre, par-72 course set among pine forests, olive trees, and cypresses, with several water hazards. It has hosted the Cannes Open on the European Tour and attracted a regular membership that ranges from local professionals to visiting celebrities.

Handicap proof is required and reservations are essential. The club has a bar, restaurant, and terrace overlooking the course. Located at 175 Avenue du Golf, Mougins — about 10 minutes by car from central Cannes. Tel: +33 4 93 75 79 13.
