At the eastern end of the Croisette, Port Pierre Canto is the newer of Cannes's two marinas — a forest of masts belonging to sailing yachts, motorboats, and the occasional superyacht. The harbour promenade at this end of the boulevard is quieter than near the Palais, and the views back across the bay toward Le Suquet and the Esterel hills beyond are excellent.

During the Cannes Yachting Festival held each September, Port Pierre Canto becomes the main venue — one of the largest in-water boat shows in the world, with hundreds of yachts open for viewing. Outside festival season it is a working marina where you can hire sailing or motor craft for excursions along the coast. Contact the harbour master's office for current rates and availability.
