---
title: Shopping
type: section
---

Cannes shopping runs from extreme luxury on the Croisette — Dior, Gucci, Cartier, Chanel — to the more approachable retail of Rue d'Antibes and Rue Meynadier. The Marché Forville is the best place for food shopping. The antique market that takes over the Forville space on Mondays is worth browsing for Provençal ceramics, old posters, and vintage clothing.
