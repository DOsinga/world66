Clermont-Ferrand sits on a plateau surrounded by the volcanic peaks of the [Chaîne des Puys](/europe/france/centre/auvergne/clermontferrant/chaine_des_puys), a landscape unlike anything else in France. The city itself is built from dark Volvic lava stone, giving it a striking, almost brooding character — especially the [Gothic cathedral](/europe/france/centre/auvergne/clermontferrant/clermont_cathedral), which rises above the old town in deep black basalt.

The historic centre is compact and walkable, centred on the [Place de Jaude](/europe/france/centre/auvergne/clermontferrant/place_de_jaude) and the narrow streets around the cathedral and the Romanesque [Basilique Notre-Dame du Port](/europe/france/centre/auvergne/clermontferrant/notre_dame_du_port), a UNESCO World Heritage Site. Clermont-Ferrand is a university city with a lively café culture, good restaurants serving [Auvergnat specialities](/europe/france/centre/auvergne/clermontferrant/auvergne_food) like truffade and Saint-Nectaire cheese, and a surprisingly vibrant nightlife for its size.

The real draw, though, is what surrounds it. The [Puy de Dôme](/europe/france/centre/auvergne/clermontferrant/puy_de_dome) — the highest of the volcanic domes — is a short drive away and offers panoramic views across the entire chain. The [Vulcania](/europe/france/centre/auvergne/clermontferrant/vulcania) theme park, the Parc des Volcans d'Auvergne, and dozens of hiking trails are all within easy reach. If you are interested in outdoor pursuits — hiking, paragliding, mountain biking — Clermont-Ferrand is one of the best bases in central France. The city was also the birthplace of [Blaise Pascal](/europe/france/centre/auvergne/clermontferrant/pascal_birthplace) and remains the world headquarters of [Michelin](/europe/france/centre/auvergne/clermontferrant/michelin_headquarters).
