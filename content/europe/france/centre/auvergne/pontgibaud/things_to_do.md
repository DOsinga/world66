---
title: Things to Do
type: section
---

Pontgibaud is a compact medieval village where the main sights can be covered in a half day. The château is the centrepiece, but the village church, the riverside setting, and the nearby Chaîne des Puys volcanic landscape make it worth lingering a little longer. The Puy de Dôme is less than 20 km away, making Pontgibaud a good base or a natural stop on a circuit through the Auvergne volcanoes.
