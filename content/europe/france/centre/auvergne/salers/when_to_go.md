---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

Salers sits at around 950 metres elevation, which means proper mountain weather: cool even in summer, cold and sometimes snowbound from November through March. The village is at its most alive from May to September, when the shops, restaurants, and museum are all open.

July and August are busy — Salers is well-known in France and draws coach tours. If you want to enjoy the streets without crowds, late May, June, or early September are ideal. The autumn colours in the surrounding Cantal countryside are spectacular and the village is much quieter.

In winter the village is largely closed. A few locals remain year-round, but most tourist infrastructure shuts down after the October school holidays.
