Golf de la Foret de Montpensier lies in wooded countryside outside Vichy, giving golfers a quiet alternative to the spa town's promenades and parks. The course setting is the main draw, with fairways cut through forest rather than spread across an exposed resort plain.

It is a specialist stop, but it clears the bar for travelers planning an active stay around Vichy. Arrange transport and tee times in advance, and think of it as a half-day outing rather than a sight for non-golfers.
