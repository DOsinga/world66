The Musée de l'Opéra de Vichy occupies a suite of rooms within the opera building itself and documents the resort's cultural history from the nineteenth century to the present. The collection centres on the performing arts — costumes, set designs, posters, programmes, and photographs from the opera's long history — but also covers the broader story of Vichy's identity as a resort town, including the arrival of celebrity guests, the development of the thermal district, and the disruptions of the two world wars.

The museum is small and specialist, best suited to visitors with a genuine interest in theatre history or in Vichy's Belle Époque past. The physical connection to the opera building adds something: the costumes and props are displayed in the rooms where they were prepared and stored, which gives the collection a tangible connection to the performances it documents. Opening hours are limited; check before visiting.
