---
title: When to Go
type: section
---

Chartres is good year-round. The cathedral is magnificent in any light, but the stained glass is best on a sunny morning or afternoon when the light comes through the windows at full strength. Midsummer weekends bring heavy day-trip crowds from Paris; a weekday visit is quieter. The Chartres en Lumières festival in spring and summer illuminates the cathedral façade and many other monuments after dark, which is worth timing a visit around.
