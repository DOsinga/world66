Musée des Casseaux preserves one of Limoges' great round porcelain kilns, a huge industrial structure from the days when firing ceramics was hot, skilled, and risky work. It complements the national porcelain museum by showing the factory side of the city's famous craft.

Come here if you want to understand how Limoges porcelain was actually made, not just admire the finished pieces in a display case.
