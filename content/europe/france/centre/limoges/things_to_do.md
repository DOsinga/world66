---
title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Limoges rewards a day and a half of careful attention. The cathedral and the bishop's garden are the high points architecturally. The porcelain museum is essential for understanding why the city matters internationally. The old town streets — particularly the Rue de la Boucherie and the area around Saint-Michel-des-Lions — give a feel for the domestic scale of a prosperous provincial city.
