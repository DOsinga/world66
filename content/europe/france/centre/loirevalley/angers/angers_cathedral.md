Angers Cathedral was built mainly in the 12th and 13th centuries and mixes Romanesque mass with Gothic height and detail. Its twin spires and decorated west front make it one of the city's main landmarks.

Inside, look for the medieval stained glass, especially the 12th-century Tree of Jesse window. The cathedral is central enough to combine easily with the old town, the castle, and Angers' museums.
