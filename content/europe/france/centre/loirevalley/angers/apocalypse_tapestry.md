The Apocalypse Tapestry is Angers' great medieval treasure, displayed inside the Château d'Angers complex. Commissioned by Louis I, Duke of Anjou, in the late fourteenth century, it once stretched about 140 metres and told the Book of Revelation in a vast sequence of woven scenes.

The surviving panels are still astonishing in scale and colour. Even if you are not usually drawn to tapestries, this is one of the rare pieces that makes the art form feel monumental.
