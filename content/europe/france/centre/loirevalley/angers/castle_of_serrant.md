Castle of Serrant is one of the more satisfying château visits near Angers because it still feels richly furnished rather than empty and ceremonial. Rooms display tapestries, period furniture, family collections, and an impressive library, giving the estate a domestic texture that many Loire castles lack.

The château is outside the city, so treat it as a half-day excursion by car or organized transport. It suits travelers who have already seen the central Angers castle and want a more intimate aristocratic house rather than another fortress.
