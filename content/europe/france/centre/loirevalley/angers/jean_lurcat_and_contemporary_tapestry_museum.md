This museum fills the 12th-century Saint-Jean hospital with modern textile art, led by Jean Lurcat's vast tapestry cycle "Le Chant du Monde". The setting is half the pleasure: long stone halls, medieval hospital architecture, and bright 20th-century work in conversation with Angers' older tapestry tradition.

Visit it after the Chateau d'Angers if you want to see how the city's famous Apocalypse tapestry inspired later artists rather than stopping at the medieval masterpiece.
