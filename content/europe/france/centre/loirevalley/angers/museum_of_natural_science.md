The Museum of Natural Science in Angers is a traditional natural history museum with zoological, geological, and paleontological collections. It adds a science-focused stop to a city better known for its castle, tapestry, and medieval streets.

The museum is most useful for families, rainy days, and travelers who like regional collections. It pairs easily with the Jardin des Plantes and the surrounding central streets, making it a low-friction addition to an Angers itinerary.
