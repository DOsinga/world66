---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

Late spring and early autumn are the best times to visit. The chateau gardens are at their finest in May and June, and the summer crowds thin out considerably after the French school holidays end in early September. July and August are busy but pleasant; the son et lumière shows only run in summer, so if that is your reason to come, plan accordingly.

The Loire Valley can be hot in July and August, but the chateau's island setting and the shade of the grounds make it more bearable than inland Touraine. Winter is quiet and the town largely closes down — the chateau remains open but many restaurants and shops operate reduced hours.
