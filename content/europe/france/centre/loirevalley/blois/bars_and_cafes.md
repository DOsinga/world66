---
title: "Bars and Cafes"
type: section
---

Blois closes early by French city standards — most of the late-night life clusters on Rue Foulerie, Rue Henri-Drussy, and the streets just north of the river. The terraces around Place du Château fill up in good weather and empty by ten. For a proper drink with locals rather than tourists, walk up into the old town away from the château.
