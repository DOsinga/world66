---
title: "Getting There"
type: section
---

Blois is on the main Paris–Tours–Bordeaux rail line. Direct trains from Paris Austerlitz take about 1h 30m and run hourly; the TER (regional service) is the standard option, with a few faster Intercités runs. From [Tours](/europe/france/centre/loirevalley/tours), trains take 30–40 minutes; from [Orléans](/europe/france/centre/loirevalley/orleans), about 35 minutes.

The Gare de Blois is a flat 10-minute walk down Avenue Jean-Laigret to the château. The nearest major airports are Paris Orly (about 2 hours by car or train+RER) and Tours-Val de Loire (about 50 minutes by car), the latter served by a handful of European low-cost routes.

There is no direct international air access. Most visitors arrive by train from Paris, by car along the A10 autoroute (exit 17), or as part of a wider Loire Valley driving tour.
