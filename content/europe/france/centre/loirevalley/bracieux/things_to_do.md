title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Bracieux itself has only a handful of sights: a 16th-century covered market hall, the small parish church, and the two stone bridges crossing the rivers Beuvron and Bonneure. The real reason most people stay here is the proximity of the great Loire châteaux — Chambord is eight kilometres away, Cheverny ten — and the walking trails through the Sologne forest.
