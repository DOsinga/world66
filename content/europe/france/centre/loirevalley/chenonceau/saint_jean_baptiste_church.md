Saint-Jean-Baptiste Church is the historic parish church of Chenonceaux village, built in the 12th century and remodelled in the 16th. It is listed as a historic monument, though it remains a small village church rather than a major Loire sight.

It is worth a short look if you are walking through the village before or after visiting the chateau.
