---
title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

The fortress is the obvious starting point — a two-hour visit at minimum, longer if you read the museum panels. From there it is a short walk down into the **Vieux Chinon** old town for a wander along Rue Voltaire, with stops at the medieval Maison Rouge, the Église Saint-Étienne, and the riverside Quai Charles VII.

Wine is the other reason to come. The Caves Painctes beneath the fortress are the most atmospheric place to taste; serious enthusiasts visit one of the producers on the outskirts, with Couly-Dutheil and a dozen others within easy reach. Thursday morning brings the Place Jeanne d'Arc market — cheese, charcuterie, wine, and produce from across the surrounding farms.
