---
title: When to Go
type: section
---

The abbey is open year-round, though hours are reduced in winter. Spring (April–May) and early autumn (September–October) are the best times to visit the Loire Valley: the weather is mild, the tourist crowds are manageable, and the vineyards in the surrounding Saumur appellation are either in blossom or harvest. July and August are the busiest months, with tour groups and queues at the abbey.

The Loire Valley experiences a temperate oceanic climate, mild and damp. Rain is possible at any time of year; the summer months are warmest but can also bring thunderstorms.
