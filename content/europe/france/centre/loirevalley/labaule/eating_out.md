---
title: "Eating Out"
type: section
---

La Baule eats fish. The whole coast is on the table: Atlantic sole, sea bass, langoustines from Quiberon, oysters from the Bay of Bourgneuf, mussels from Pénestin. Beyond the obvious seafood, look for **far breton** (a prune flan, the traditional dessert of the region) and **gâteau nantais** (a rum-soaked almond cake from nearby Nantes).

The wine to drink with all this is **Muscadet** — crisp, dry, almost saline white from the Loire estuary just to the east. It is the perfect partner for oysters and the cheapest serious wine in any seafront restaurant.
