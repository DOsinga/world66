---
title: "Getting There"
type: section
---

**By train.** La Baule-Escoublac is on the line from Nantes, with direct TGV services from Paris Montparnasse (around 3 hours). The Nantes-La Baule TER runs roughly every hour and takes about an hour.

**By car.** The N171 from Nantes is a fast dual carriageway — count an hour from Nantes, three to four hours from Paris via the A11.

**By air.** Nantes Atlantique Airport is 75km east, well-connected to most European capitals. From the airport, take the airport shuttle to Nantes station, then the train to La Baule.

**By boat.** There is no scheduled passenger service, but the marina at Port de La Baule and the neighbouring Pornichet harbour welcome visiting yachts.
