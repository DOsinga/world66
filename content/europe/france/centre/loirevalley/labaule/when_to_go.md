---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

The season is firmly summer: **July and August** are the peak — busy, expensive, and warm enough for proper beach days, with the sea temperature usually around 19-21°C. The town is in full swing, the beach clubs are open, and you need to book restaurants.

**June and September** are the sweet spot — warm enough for the beach, the sea still swimmable, fewer crowds, lower prices. Many of the smartest restaurants close in mid-September.

**Easter to May** brings cool, breezy days and a town that is part-shuttered but pleasantly empty. The light is sharp and the coast is beautiful, but you will not be swimming.

**Winter** is quiet. Many shops and most restaurants close. Locals walk dogs on the empty beach in the wind and rain. It has its charm if you want serious solitude.
