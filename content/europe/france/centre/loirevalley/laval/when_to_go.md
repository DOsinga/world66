---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

Laval sits in a mild, slightly damp Atlantic climate. Summers are warm rather than hot, and the riverside parks and quays are at their best from May to September. Spring brings the orchards around the town into bloom; autumn is wet but quiet.

The town's biggest event is the Festival Les 3 Éléphants in late May or early June — a music and arts festival that takes over the riverside parks for a long weekend.
