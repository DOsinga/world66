Loches makes a brilliant base for exploring the southern Loire. [Chenonceau](/europe/france/centre/loirevalley/chenonceau) -- the chateau that arches across the river Cher -- is just 30 km north, half an hour by road. [Amboise](/europe/france/centre/loirevalley/amboise), with its royal chateau on a cliff above the Loire and Leonardo da Vinci's Clos Lucé, is 40 km north. [Azay-le-Rideau](/europe/france/centre/loirevalley/azaylerideau) and [Chinon](/europe/france/centre/loirevalley/chinon) are both within 50 km west.

Closer to home, the village of **Beaulieu-lès-Loches**, just across the river, has the ruins of a substantial 11th-century abbey founded by Foulques Nerra. The forêt de Loches, immediately south of the town, is one of the largest forests of the Touraine, with marked walking and cycling trails.
