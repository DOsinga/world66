---
title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Orléans rewards a day of walking. The [Cathédrale Sainte-Croix](/europe/france/centre/loirevalley/orleans/cathedrale_sainte_croix) and the [Maison de Jeanne d'Arc](/europe/france/centre/loirevalley/orleans/maison_de_jeanne_darc) form the core of any visit, but the old quarter around [Rue de Bourgogne](/europe/france/centre/loirevalley/orleans/rue_de_bourgogne) and the Loire riverfront are worth time on their own terms.

The [Musée des Beaux-Arts](/europe/france/centre/loirevalley/orleans/musee_des_beaux_arts) is strong on French painting and holds a rare collection of 18th-century pastels. The [Orléanais Historical and Archaeological Museum](/europe/france/centre/loirevalley/orleans/musee_historique_et_archeologique) in the Hôtel Cabu has the famous Gallo-Roman Treasure of Neuvy-en-Sullias, including the celebrated dancing horse bronze.

The [Parc Floral de la Source](/europe/france/centre/loirevalley/orleans/parc_floral_de_la_source) is a large botanical garden on the southern edge of the city, built around the spring that marks the source of the Loiret, a short tributary of the Loire. It is particularly worth visiting in spring and early summer when the rose and iris collections are at their best.
