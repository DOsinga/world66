A formal 19th-century park between the train station and the Boulevard Béranger, laid out in the English landscape style by the Bühler brothers in 1872. Mature plane trees, winding paths, a small bandstand, statues of local literary figures, and a swan pond. It is a calm and well-kept park that most visitors never find.

A good place to read or eat lunch from the Marché des Halles if the weather is fine. There are summer concerts in the bandstand on Sunday afternoons. Free; open until dusk.
