title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Tours rewards slow walking. The medieval quarter around [Place Plumereau](/europe/france/centre/loirevalley/tours/place_plumereau) and Place Saint-Pierre-le-Puellier is the most atmospheric part of the city, with half-timbered houses lining narrow lanes that beg to be explored without a plan. The [cathedral](/europe/france/centre/loirevalley/tours/cathedral) and the surviving towers of the lost [Basilica of Saint Martin](/europe/france/centre/loirevalley/tours/basilica_saint_martin) anchor the historic centre, and several good museums — the [Musée des Beaux-Arts](/europe/france/centre/loirevalley/tours/musee_des_beaux_arts), the [Musée du Compagnonnage](/europe/france/centre/loirevalley/tours/musee_du_compagnonnage), and the [CCC-OD](/europe/france/centre/loirevalley/tours/centre_de_creation_contemporaine) — fill the gaps between sightseeing and lunch.
