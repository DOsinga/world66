---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

Late April to early October is the sensible window. May and September are best — warm enough for outdoor terraces, light enough to wander until well after dinner, and the chateaux around the city are not yet overrun. July and August can be very hot in the Loire Valley (mid-30s°C is common) and the main chateaux choke with bus tours.

Tours empties in August when the students leave; the upside is fewer crowds, the downside is that some restaurants close for the summer. Winter is quiet and damp; the city is fine for a weekend, but most chateaux operate on shortened hours.
