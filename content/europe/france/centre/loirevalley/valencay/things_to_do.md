---
title: Things to Do
type: section
---

Valençay's main draw is its château, but the town itself has a few surprises. The SOE memorial, tucked into a small park, commemorates the British agents who parachuted into France during the Second World War — many of whom never came back. The market on the town square operates on a small but genuine scale, and the cheese shops selling the local goat's cheese are worth a stop.
