---
title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

The château and its gardens are the only real reason to come to Villandry, and they are reason enough. The kitchen garden alone is one of the finest gardens in France. Pair the visit with a stop at the petrifying caves a short drive east in Savonnières, or build a longer day with [Ussé](/europe/france/centre/loirevalley/usse) and [Azay-le-Rideau](/europe/france/centre/loirevalley/azaylerideau) along the river road.
