A café on the central place, good for a coffee after walking the grid. The terrace looks out onto the symmetrical square and the two facing market halls — an ideal spot to contemplate the Cardinal's urban ambitions at leisure. The town is quiet; the café terrace, especially on a Saturday market morning, is the main social space in Richelieu.
