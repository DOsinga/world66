---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

**April to October** is the best time to visit if you want to make the most of the park — the gardens are in bloom, the canal is open for rowing, and the chestnut allées are at their most impressive in full leaf. The rose gardens reach their peak in **late June and early July**.

The town itself is worth visiting year-round. The architecture doesn't change with the weather, and Richelieu in winter — empty, slightly austere — has a quality of its own. The Saturday market runs throughout the year, though it's livelier in warmer months.

Spring and early autumn are the sweet spots: good weather, smaller crowds than the main Loire Valley châteaux, and the park looking its best.
