The most consistently recommended restaurant in Hautvillers, Le Caveau serves traditional French regional cooking — terrines, lamb, regional cheeses — in a setting that suits the village: stone walls, wooden beams, and a wine list built around Champagne and still wines from the Marne Valley. The lunch menu is good value. Reservations are advisable at weekends, especially in summer and during the harvest period.
