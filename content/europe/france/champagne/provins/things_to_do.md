---
title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

The whole upper town is the main sight — a fortified medieval citadel listed by UNESCO since 2001 for the completeness of its medieval urban fabric. A single ticket gives access to the main monuments (Tour César, Grange aux Dîmes, Souterrains and the medieval shows), and a day is enough to see everything well. Most attractions are within easy walking distance of each other inside the ramparts.
