---
title: "Day Trips"
type: section
---

Ajaccio sits in the southwest of Corsica and makes a good base for the wilder southern half of the island. Roads are winding and distances take longer to cover than the map suggests — build in extra time.

**Filitosa** (about 1 hour south) is Corsica's most important prehistoric site: standing menhir-statues carved with faces and swords, some dating to around 1500 BC. The site is small but atmospheric, set among ancient olive trees. Open April to October.

**Gulf of Valinco** (1 hour south) has the island's best beaches outside of Porto-Vecchio. The town of Propriano at the head of the gulf is a low-key resort with good seafood restaurants.

**The Cinarca Valley** (45 minutes northeast) holds some of Corsica's most respected vineyards, particularly around Mezzavia and Tiuccia. Several estates are open for tastings without booking.

**Gorges du Prunelli** (about 45 minutes east) — a dramatic canyon inland from Ajaccio, good for an afternoon drive with swimming in the river gorge.

**Corte** (about 1.5 hours north) — the symbolic capital of Corsica's independence movement, with a clifftop citadel, a good regional museum, and the start of serious mountain walking.
