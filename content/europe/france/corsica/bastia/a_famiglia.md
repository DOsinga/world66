A Famiglia is a restaurant in the Terra Vecchia quarter that has made its name serving traditional Corsican home cooking in a properly atmospheric setting — stone walls, bare tables, and a menu written on a blackboard. The cooking is straightforward but uses excellent ingredients: charcuterie boards of Corsican salumi, brocciu-stuffed pasta (cannelloni), slow-cooked veal with maquis herbs, and local wines.

The restaurant fills quickly in the evening and reservations are advisable in summer. Prices are moderate by French standards. The wine list focuses on Corsican appellations — Patrimonio, Ajaccio, and the Muscat du Cap Corse — and the house recommendation is usually the most interesting option. Service is warm in the manner of a place that values regulars over tourists, which is how most of the best restaurants on the island operate.
