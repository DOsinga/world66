---
title: Things to Do in Bastia
type: section
tags: [things_to_do]
---

Bastia rewards a slower pace than most ferry-port cities. The [Vieux Port](vieux_port) and its neighbourhood of [Terra Vecchia](terra_vecchia) are the obvious starting point — lanes climbing from the harbour, baroque churches in unexpected corners, and the smell of coffee drifting from the quayside cafes. From the Vieux Port it's a short walk south to the [Citadelle](citadelle), the Genoese-built upper quarter with the [Musée de Bastia](musee_de_bastia) inside the Palais des Gouverneurs and the best views in town from its ramparts.

The churches deserve some time: the twin-towered [Église Saint-Jean-Baptiste](eglise_saint_jean_baptiste) on the harbour is the largest in Corsica; the [Église Sainte-Croix](eglise_sainte_croix) holds the venerated Cristo Nero; and the [Bastia Cathedral](bastia_cathedral) contains the magnificent processional silver statue of the Assumption. For something stranger, climb to the [Chapelle Notre-Dame de Monserato](chapelle_notre_dame_monserato) with its rare Scala Santa. The [Place Saint-Nicolas](place_saint_nicolas) is where everyone gathers in the evening — wide, shaded, and open to the sea.

For a day trip, the road around [Cap Corse](cap_corse) is one of the most scenic drives in France.
