Saint-Dominique is worth seeking out because Gothic churches are unusual in Corsica. Its lines are heavier and simpler than mainland showpieces, but that restraint suits Bonifacio's defensive old town.

The church sits near the western edge of the citadel, so it pairs well with a rampart walk. Visit it as part of a slow loop through the upper town rather than as a stand-alone stop.
