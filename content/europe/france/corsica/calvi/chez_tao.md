A piano bar inside the citadel that has been a fixture of Calvi nights since the 1930s. The terrace looks out over the rooftops to the sea; the interior is a confusion of mirrors, antique furniture and old photographs.

Drinks are not cheap, and dressing up is encouraged. The crowd is a mix of summer regulars, the Foreign Legion in mufti and curious passers-by.
