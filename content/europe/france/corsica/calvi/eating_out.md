---
title: Eating Out
type: section
---

Calvi has the Corsican usual suspects — wild boar civet, brocciu (the local soft sheep cheese), figatellu sausage, sea urchins in season — and a strong line in fish, especially red mullet, denti, and rockfish caught the same morning. The places along the harbour are aimed at the yacht crowd and priced accordingly; better cooking is in the streets just behind.
