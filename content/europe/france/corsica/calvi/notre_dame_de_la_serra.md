A small 19th-century chapel set high on the rocky ridge above Calvi, reached by a winding road of about four kilometres or a steeper walking path. The views are the point: the whole sweep of the Bay of Calvi, the long beach, the citadel from above, and on a clear day the snow-streaked peaks of the Cinto massif.

Best at sunset. The chapel is usually closed but the terrace is open, and on Pentecost Monday there's a pilgrimage from town up to the chapel for a Mass.
