---
title: When to Go
type: section
---

**May, June and September** are the sweet spots — the sea is warm, the maquis still green in spring, and the town not yet at full bustle. **July and August** are hot, busy and expensive, but the Calvi on the Rocks festival in early July (electronic music) and the Festival du Jazz in late June draw a young, party crowd.

**Winter** is mild but the town empties out — many restaurants close from November to Easter. **Pentecost** brings the pilgrimage up to Notre-Dame de la Serra; the **Rencontres de Chants Polyphoniques** in mid-September fills the citadel churches with traditional Corsican choral singing for a week.
