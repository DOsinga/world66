---
title: When to Go
type: section
---

Late spring (May-June) and early autumn (September-October) are the best times to visit Corte. The weather is warm but not scorching, the gorges are accessible, and the summer crowds have not yet arrived or have gone home. In June the Restonica is still running strong from snowmelt, making the swimming holes exceptional.

July and August are hot and busy. The Restonica car park fills before 9am and the trail to Lac de Melo has queues on the more exposed sections. It is manageable but not ideal. The Tavignano gorge provides a real escape from the crowds at any time of year.

In winter Corte can be cold and wet, and snow is possible. The gorges become more serious undertakings and some sections are closed. However, the town itself has a pleasant off-season quiet — the university is in session, the cafes are full, and you are unlikely to meet another tourist.
