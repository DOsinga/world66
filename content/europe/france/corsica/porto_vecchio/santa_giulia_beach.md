Santa Giulia is a sheltered bay south of Porto-Vecchio, with shallow turquoise water and a long curve of sand. It is easier and more resort-like than some Corsican beaches, which can be a virtue if you want swimming, paddleboards, and a simple half-day by the water.

The bay is heavily used in summer. Treat it as a beautiful but managed beach, not a lonely cove.
