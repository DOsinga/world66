title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Fréjus's main draws are its Roman ruins and the medieval episcopal quarter. The [Arènes de Fréjus](/europe/france/cotedazur/frejus/arenes_de_frejus) is one of the best-preserved Roman amphitheatres in France and still hosts summer events. The [cathedral complex](/europe/france/cotedazur/frejus/frejus_cathedral) with its ancient baptistery and painted cloister is the other essential stop. Further afield, the Missiri Mosque and the Indochina Memorial reward those curious about France's colonial military history.
