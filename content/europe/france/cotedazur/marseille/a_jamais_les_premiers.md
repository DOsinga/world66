On 26 May 1993 in Munich, one goal did it. Basile Boli's header off Abedi Pelé's corner, 43rd minute, and AC Milan were beaten. No French club had won the Champions League before, and none has since. *À Jamais Les Premiers* — Forever the First.

The marker sits under the Stade Vélodrome, a 67,000-seat cauldron in the 8th and one of the loudest grounds in Europe. Raymond Goethals coached that side; Deschamps, Völler and Bokšić all played. In a city with a chip on its shoulder about Paris, 1993 is the one fact nobody can argue away.

Plaque, mosaic or painted wall — the tribute changes, the pride doesn't. On match days half the city's trams seem to funnel here. For Marseillais, 1993 isn't history. It's a fact they renew every time they say the words out loud.
