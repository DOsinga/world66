Rawa-Ruska sits in western Ukraine, near the Polish border. During the war it was a German holding camp — a waystation on the road to the Bełżec extermination camp. Jews deported from Vichy's Southern Zone were funneled east through here from 1942, and almost none survived what came next.

Marseille felt this directly. In January 1943, the SS and Vichy police sealed off the Vieux-Port and Le Panier and rounded up thousands — Jews, foreigners, anyone deemed undesirable. Roughly 1,600 Jewish Marseillais were sent to the death camps.

This plaque turns the numbers back into people. Stand in front of it and the statistics become specific names, specific streets, specific absences.
