The Blue Grotto earns its name mid-morning, when sunlight filters through a submerged entrance and floods the interior with an otherworldly blue glow. Time it right and the effect is unforgettable. It's one of several sea caves cut into the limestone cliffs between Marseille and Cassis.

There's no land approach — you come by boat or sea kayak. Outfitters run guided kayak trips from the Old Port and the beaches near Pointe Rouge, and boat tours from the Vieux-Port pass on their way to the calanques. Experienced paddlers can go solo, but the entrance is impossible in any swell.

Arrive early: the light peaks in the morning, the crowds are thinner, and the mistral shuts down kayak access fast when it blows. And don't be fooled by the calendar — even in July and August, the water stays cold enough to make you gasp.
