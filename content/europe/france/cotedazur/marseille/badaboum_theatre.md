The name tells you everything. *Badaboum* is the sound of a fall, a word French children know before they can read. This theatre announces exactly what it is right on the sign — no translation needed.

Inside it's puppets, clowns, physical comedy, and shows kids can shout back at. None of it leans on text or plot. It works through gesture, rhythm, and surprise — the same tricks that built generations of French street theatre and contemporary circus.

This is a local spot, not a tourist stop. For plenty of Marseille kids, it's the first live show they'll ever see.
