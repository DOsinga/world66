Sugiton is the one most people hit first, and for good reason: 45 minutes on a marked path from the Luminy campus gets you the full show. White limestone dropping into improbable blue-green water, Aleppo pines clinging to the cliffs, the Mediterranean boxed in by rock on three sides. Take Bus 21 from the city, start at the campus car park, climb, then drop to the sea.

Here's the catch: from July through August, on high fire-risk days, the park closes Sugiton entirely — sometimes for weeks during dry summers. This isn't red tape. The garrigue burns fast and there's little infrastructure to stop it out here. Check the park website before you go; the call gets posted the evening before.

The beach at the bottom is small and pebbly, shared by swimmers and a few kayakers coming in off the water. Rocky platforms on either side give you more room to swim. And the water is clear enough that you can read the bottom.
