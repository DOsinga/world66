Château Borély houses Marseille's museum of decorative arts, fashion and ceramics in an 18th-century bastide near the southern parks and beaches. The setting matters as much as the collection: rooms, objects and gardens together show a more elegant domestic side of Marseille.

The museum is a good fit for visitors already heading toward Parc Borély, the Prado beaches or the southern corniche. It offers a quieter counterpoint to MuCEM and the old port, with displays that focus on crafted objects rather than city history.
