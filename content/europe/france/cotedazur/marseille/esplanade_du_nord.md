Head north past the tourist waterfront and you hit a different city. The northern arrondissements are home to a huge chunk of Marseille's population — housing estates, local shopping streets, neighbourhood markets — and the Esplanade du Nord is the kind of space that serves them. Wide, open, designed for people who live here rather than people passing through.

Boules in the afternoon, teenagers on bikes, old men on benches arguing about nothing. No historic monument, no view of the sea. Just a city being a city.

If you want to understand how Marseille actually works — not how it poses for cameras — walk up here. The esplanade won't give you a postcard. It'll give you something more honest.
