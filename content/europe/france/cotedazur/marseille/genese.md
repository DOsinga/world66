Génèse sits quietly near the southeastern edge of the city, close to the coastal path. You often encounter it before you're looking for it — the forms emerge from the landscape rather than demanding attention.

The title means Genesis. In a city founded by Greek sailors from Phocaea around 600 BCE, every stone here has an origin story buried underneath the next one. Port cities are places of beginnings by nature: raw goods arrive, ideas cross with passengers, communities form from colliding languages.

Take a moment to sit with it before walking on toward the calanques. The stillness is the point.
