In 600 BCE, Greek sailors from Phocaea landed here and founded a settlement they called Massalia. When the year 2000 arrived, Marseille had been a city for exactly 2,600 years. The park was the civic response: a new green space in the southern 8th arrondissement, formal gardens and broad lawns designed to last another century.

Jean-Claude Gaudin was mayor from 1995 to 2020 — the longest tenure in modern municipal history — and this was a signature project of his administration. After his death in 2022, the city attached his name to it. The double-barrelled result now marks both the historical occasion and the man.

Families use the lawn, joggers do the perimeter paths. In the late afternoon, the Mediterranean light goes amber and turns even a municipal park into somewhere worth stopping for a moment.
