Jardin Boucle Périer lies in the 7th arrondissement, in or near the Périer neighbourhood — one of the established residential quarters of southern Marseille, situated on the slopes between the Prado boulevard and the sea. The name Périer has deep roots in Marseille's history: Casimir Périer was a nineteenth-century French statesman, and the family name appears across the city's geography.

"Boucle" — meaning loop or bend — may refer to a curve in a nearby road or boundary, a common source of local place names in old French cities. Together, the name describes a garden at the loop or bend in the Périer area.

The 7th arrondissement is one of Marseille's more comfortable residential districts, and its public gardens are generally well-maintained. This jardin serves the local community as a pedestrian green space: a pleasant stop in a neighbourhood that rewards walking.
