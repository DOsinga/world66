"Boucle" means loop or bend — probably a curve in a nearby road that someone decided to name a garden after. It's the kind of place name that makes total sense once you see the street layout. The Périer part is older: the family name runs through Marseille's geography the way old civic families tend to, on streets and squares and public spaces.

The 7th arrondissement sits between the Prado and the sea, which means decent real estate and well-kept streets. The gardens down here get looked after.

Jardin Boucle Périer is a pedestrian green space worth pausing in — particularly if you're walking the residential streets between the Prado and the coast.
