Jardin Carli is a small garden in the dense urban fabric near the centre of Marseille. The Carli surname is found across the western Mediterranean — in Corsica, Sardinia, northern Italy, and Provence — and the name here likely commemorates a local figure, perhaps a former property owner, a municipal councillor, or a family that was once prominent in this part of the city.

Neighbourhood gardens in central Marseille are scarce and valued. The 1st and 2nd arrondissements are densely built, with little space between the old port, the commercial streets, and the residential buildings stacked on the hillsides above. A garden of this kind provides shade, a bench, and a pause — which in this context is genuinely useful.

Jardin Carli is a modest green space in the service of its immediate neighbourhood, more practical than scenic but no less welcome for that.
