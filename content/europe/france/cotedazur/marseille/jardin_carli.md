Central Marseille doesn't have much breathing room. The 1st and 2nd arrondissements are tightly packed — the old port, the commercial streets, and the hillside buildings all pressing against each other. Any garden that survives in this zone is there because someone decided it mattered.

Carli is a surname that turns up across Corsica, Sardinia, and Provence — probably a local figure, maybe a property owner, maybe a councillor. The name stuck; the garden stayed.

It's modest. Shade, a bench, a pause. In the middle of the city centre, that's not nothing.
