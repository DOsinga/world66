Parc Chanot has been Marseille's principal exhibition and events ground since the early twentieth century — the Foire Internationale de Marseille happens here every year. Chanot himself was a nineteenth-century engineer who got things built during the decades when the city's population doubled and its port became one of the world's busiest.

Jardin Chanot is the green space on the complex's edge: a quieter corner away from the exhibition halls, useful to visitors who need a break between stands and to residents of the 8th who just want somewhere to sit.

The 8th arrondissement is one of Marseille's more comfortable districts. The gardens are kept accordingly.
