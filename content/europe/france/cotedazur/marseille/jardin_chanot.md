Jardin Chanot takes its name from the Parc Chanot, the large exhibition and events complex nearby in Marseille's 8th arrondissement. The Chanot in question was a nineteenth-century engineer connected to Marseille's period of rapid industrial and commercial expansion — the decades after 1850 when the city's population doubled and its port became one of the busiest in the world. The Parc Chanot, which hosts the annual Foire Internationale de Marseille among other events, has been the city's principal exhibition ground since the early twentieth century.

The garden occupies a quieter corner of the Chanot complex's surroundings, offering a green counterpoint to the exhibition halls and event infrastructure nearby. The 8th arrondissement is one of Marseille's more affluent districts, and its gardens benefit from that relative prosperity in their upkeep.

Jardin Chanot is a pleasant pause in a busy part of the city, useful to visitors to the exhibition grounds and to residents of the surrounding neighbourhood alike.
