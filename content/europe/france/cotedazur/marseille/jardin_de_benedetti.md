De Benedetti is widely distributed across Italy and Corsica, and the surname's presence on a garden in Marseille's western arrondissements is no coincidence. These neighbourhoods were shaped by dock workers and industrial communities, and a large proportion came — directly or a generation back — from Italy. Nineteenth-century and early-twentieth-century arrivals who worked the port, built the city, and stayed.

The western side of Marseille is rougher around the edges than the southern coast. The gardens here are neighbourhood resources, not scenic amenities — shade from the heat, somewhere to sit that isn't pavement.

Jardin de Benedetti is exactly that: local, modest, and genuine.
