Jardin de Benedetti is a neighbourhood garden in the western arrondissements of Marseille — a part of the city that has long been shaped by working-class communities and successive waves of immigration. The de Benedetti surname is widely distributed across Italy, Corsica, and Provence; in Marseille, Italian family names of this kind often belong to families who arrived in the nineteenth or early twentieth century and put down permanent roots in the city's residential fabric.

The western arrondissements include some of Marseille's older working neighbourhoods, built up around the docks and the industries that grew along the northern shore of the Vieux-Port. Green spaces here are neighbourhood resources first and foremost, valued for shade and for providing a place to gather outside the apartment.

Jardin de Benedetti is exactly that: a local garden serving a local community, modest in scale but genuine in purpose.
