Jardin de la Gare de la Blancarde is a small public garden set immediately beside the Blancarde commuter station in the 10th arrondissement, occupying land along the edge of the rail corridor that bisects this part of eastern Marseille. It is a frankly utilitarian space — the kind of garden that fills a gap between infrastructure and residential streets — but serves a useful purpose in a dense neighbourhood with little open space.

The Blancarde station is a node on the Aix-en-Provence–Marseille-Saint-Charles commuter line, and the area around it is busy with pedestrians moving between the station, the Bd Chave tramway, and the apartment buildings of the surrounding streets. The garden gives commuters a place to slow down, and residents a green counterpoint to the noise of the rail line.

The planting is low-maintenance — hardy trees, shrubs, ground cover — suited to a garden that sits in the shadow of the station infrastructure. It is not a destination in itself, but it is there, open, and free, which in a city with Marseille's summer heat counts for something.
