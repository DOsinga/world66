Right beside the Blancarde station platform, on land squeezed between the rail corridor and the surrounding streets. It's a utilitarian space and doesn't pretend otherwise — hardy trees, low-maintenance shrubs, ground cover that can handle the noise and shadow of the infrastructure next door.

The Blancarde line runs to Aix-en-Provence and to Saint-Charles. The tram and the apartment buildings press in from all sides. In that context, a strip of greenery where you can slow down for five minutes is not nothing.

Not a reason to come to this part of the 10th. But if you're here, it's open, free, and in a Marseille summer that covers the basics.
