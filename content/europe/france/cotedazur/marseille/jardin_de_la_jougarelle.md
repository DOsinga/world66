At the top of the 15th arrondissement, where the city blurs into the surrounding communes — Les Pennes-Mirabeau, Saint-Antoine — the urban fabric loosens and the Jougarelle quarter is what you get: residential streets, detached houses, a pace that has nothing to do with the Vieux-Port.

The garden here is exactly what this kind of neighbourhood needs. Grass, shade trees, benches, a play area. It serves the families in the apartment buildings for whom a public garden is the only alternative to a private yard they don't have.

Few visitors come this far north. But the Jougarelle shows you the residential Marseille that most of the tourist circuit never touches — working-class, functioning, and a different city entirely.
