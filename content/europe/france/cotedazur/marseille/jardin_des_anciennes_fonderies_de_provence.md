The name says it all: the garden of the old Provençal foundries. In the nineteenth and early twentieth centuries, metalworking and manufacturing spread through the inner eastern districts of Marseille. The foundries are gone. The land was cleared and planted.

It's a modest conversion — no dramatic industrial archaeology left standing, no heritage interpretation. What's here is a neighbourhood park on ground that used to produce metal. If you look at the layout closely you might find a retaining wall or an unusual level change that hints at what was here before.

The 10th is still in the long process of changing its economic character — mixed residential, small businesses, rail infrastructure. A garden on reclaimed foundry land fits that picture exactly.
