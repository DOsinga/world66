Jardin des Archives Départementales surrounds the Bouches-du-Rhône departmental archives — the official repository for historical documents covering the whole of the department, from medieval charters to twentieth-century administrative records. The archives building sits in the 3rd arrondissement, one of the historic inner arrondissements north of the Vieux-Port where Marseille keeps much of its civic infrastructure.

The garden is attached to the institution in the way that French public buildings of a certain epoch almost always are: a planted courtyard or perimeter garden that softens the institutional architecture and provides a break from the surrounding streets. It is open to the general public, not only to those consulting the archives, and serves as a small urban pause point in a dense part of the city.

For anyone doing genealogical research or consulting historical records — the archives hold extensive documentation on Marseille's history as a port city and on the families and communities of Provence — the garden is a pleasant place to read notes or collect thoughts between sessions. The 3rd arrondissement is also close to the Palais de Justice and several other administrative buildings.
