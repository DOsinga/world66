Jardin Doria is in the 5th arrondissement, one of the inner eastern districts of Marseille that fills the zone between the Vieux-Port and the university hospital complex at La Timone. The Doria name is an old one in Marseille — the Doria were a prominent Genoese banking and merchant family whose reach extended to Marseille during the medieval and early modern period, when the city had deep commercial ties with Genoa.

Whether this garden commemorates the family specifically, or simply takes its name from a local property or street that already bore it, is uncertain. The garden is a neighbourhood-scale space: a few trees, a bench or two, a patch of open ground in a district where residential buildings are packed close together and private outdoor space is scarce.

The 5th arrondissement has a lively, mixed character — university students, hospital workers, long-established Marseillais families — and small public gardens like Jardin Doria function as the social infrastructure of these streets, places where people briefly encounter each other outside their apartments.
