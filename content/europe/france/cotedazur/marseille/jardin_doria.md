The Doria were a Genoese dynasty — bankers, admirals, merchants — whose reach extended across the medieval Mediterranean, including Marseille. The city had deep commercial ties with Genoa during the period when the Doria were at their peak. The name stuck to a street or property, and eventually to this garden.

Whether the family ever had anything directly to do with this spot in the 5th arrondissement is uncertain. The garden itself is modest: a few trees, a bench or two, a patch of open ground in a district where apartments are packed tight and private outdoor space is scarce.

The neighbourhood has a lively mix — university students from La Timone, hospital workers, long-established local families. A small garden like this is where they cross paths briefly, outside their buildings.
