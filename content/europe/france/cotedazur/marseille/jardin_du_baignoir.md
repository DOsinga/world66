North of the Canebière, the streets close in. This is Belsunce — traditionally the heart of Marseille's North African community, always commercial, always in motion. The Jardin du Baignoir is a small break in that density, in the 1st arrondissement near the Rue d'Aix.

The name is puzzling. *Baignoir* in standard French means a bathtub or a low theatre box. Here it almost certainly refers to a local toponym — a historic building or property on the site. The 1st arrondissement was built up long before anyone thought about planned open space, and this garden is wedged into whatever gap was available.

The market streets, the Gare Saint-Charles, the Arabic-language bookshops — all within a short walk. The garden gives you a place to sit down in a neighbourhood that rarely stops.
