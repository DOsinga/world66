*Bois sacré* — sacred wood. The name invokes the classical *lucus*, the grove set apart from ordinary use, dedicated to something larger. Whether any cult occupied this particular hillside above the Vieux-Port entrance is unlikely. But the Provençal tradition of giving distinguished gardens classical names was real, and this site earns it.

The 7th arrondissement climbs from the Pharo down to Vallon des Auffes and along the southern corniche. Up here, the terrain is rocky garrigue scrub and Mediterranean woodland — pine, holm oak, wild herbs. The paths are rougher than the formal parks of the flat city.

The views across the harbour mouth are what draw people. They've been drawing people since the city was Massalia, 2,600 years ago. The name gets at something true.
