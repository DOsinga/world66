The 11th arrondissement is northeastern Marseille — mid-century housing stock, commercial strips, working-class residential fabric built up fast after 1945. The name "Fédération" probably refers to a local union federation or civic association; it's the kind of name that appeared on postwar public spaces in industrial neighbourhoods across France.

This part of the city doesn't feature in most tourist accounts. But it has a large and active population, and parks here do real work: children's play, older residents on benches, a place to be outside that isn't the street.

The garden is modest and functional. It's part of the systematic network of small municipal parks that Marseille built across the outer arrondissements from the 1970s onward, filling a gap that the older city centre filled organically with its squares and cours.
