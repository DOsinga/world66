Nobody outside the 4th arrondissement ever heard of Francis Chirat. That's kind of the point. Marseille has dozens of these tiny gardens named after people who did something useful — ran the local committee, organised the street fair, lobbied for better rubbish collection — without ever appearing in a history book.

This one sits in the northern residential city, hemmed in by apartment blocks on streets that most tourists never reach. Dense, lived-in, a long way from the Vieux-Port.

The garden is small, maintained by the city's parks service, and used daily by whoever lives in the surrounding blocks. A bench, some shade, a name on a plaque. That's the whole thing — and it's enough.
