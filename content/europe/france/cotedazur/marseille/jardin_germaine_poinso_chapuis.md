In 1947, Germaine Poinso-Chapuis became the first woman to hold a full ministerial post in France. Minister of Public Health and Population, in Robert Schuman's government. France had only given women the vote three years earlier. She was a Marseille lawyer, a Christian democrat, and had been active in the Resistance during the occupation.

After her year in government she came back to Marseille and stayed — regional politics, her legal practice, decades of civic life in the city. The bigger feminist political gains came later and got more attention. Hers was quieter and earlier.

The garden sits in the central-southern arc of the city, close to the 6th arrondissement — busy, well-connected, genuinely used. A better tribute than a plaque on a wall.
