Infossi is a Provençal-inflected family name found in the Marseille region. The garden named after it sits in the far south of the 9th arrondissement, in a part of the city that feels like it's in conversation with the Calanques — the rock and scrub of the national park pressing close against streets of postwar residential blocks.

This area urbanised mostly after the Second World War, housing built to accommodate population growth in what had previously been agricultural and bastide country. There are still older mas and farm properties embedded in the suburb if you know where to look.

The garden is a neighbourhood green space, modest in scale. Out here the Calanques are close but not always walkable from every doorstep — a small park on the street still earns its place.
