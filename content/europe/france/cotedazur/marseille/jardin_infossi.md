Infossi is a Provençal-inflected family name found in the Marseille region; the garden named after it is one of the city's many smaller public spaces that carry local family names without attaching to a widely documented individual. In this part of the city, where the urban fabric transitions gradually towards the rocky coastline of the Calanques, such naming often reflects families who owned land or property in the neighbourhood before it became fully urbanised.

The coordinates place this garden in the southern 9th arrondissement, close to the areas of Marseille where the city begins to thin out into the semi-rural and coastal terrain that eventually reaches the national park. This is a district of relatively modern residential development — housing built to accommodate population growth in the postwar decades — interspersed with older mas and bastide properties that predate the suburb.

The garden is a neighbourhood amenity, well-placed for residents of the surrounding streets and reflecting the municipal policy of distributing small green spaces across the outer arrondissements where open countryside is nearby but not directly accessible from most front doors.
