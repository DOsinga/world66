Chabaud is a Provençal name common in the Marseille region. Joseph Chabaud didn't enter any wider historical record — the garden named after him belongs to that category of local commemoration where the city preserves a name that mattered in a neighbourhood without it ever mattering anywhere else.

What makes this one interesting is where it sits. The far south of the 9th arrondissement, at the point where the city effectively runs out. The Calanques limestone massif presses right up against the residential streets here — drier, rockier, scrubland and pine replacing apartment blocks as you walk further south.

In this setting a small enclosed garden has a particular character. The open landscape of the national park is close, but not every resident can walk there from their front door. This patch of shade and grass still earns its place.
