La Moussière isn't a person's name. It's a place name, the kind that appears in old Provençal land registers: "mousse" means moss, so this was probably low, damp ground — a descriptive label attached to a bastide or agricultural property before the suburb arrived. The name survived the urbanisation. The damp ground presumably did not.

The garden is in the 14th arrondissement, Marseille's far northeast, which is not on any tourist itinerary. Large residential populations, mostly postwar housing estates and commercial zones, the urban fabric gradually thinning as it approaches the Étoile massif to the north. A long way from the Vieux-Port.

Up here, a named public park does real work. There's no waterfront, no grand boulevard — the daily green space for these neighbourhoods is exactly this.
