Stilatti is Italian. That's not incidental — at the peak of Italian immigration to France, Marseille had one of the largest Italian-born populations of any city outside Italy. They came for the port, for construction, for the fishing trade. Their surnames are now woven into the city's street geography, and Stilatti is one of them.

Mathieu Stilatti himself isn't documented nationally, which means he was someone the neighbourhood knew rather than someone history preserved. A local figure, a community face, someone whose name ended up on a fence.

The garden sits in the 4th arrondissement, right in the part of the northern inner city where the old port district bleeds into denser residential streets — exactly the kind of neighbourhood where Italian families settled in the 19th century and stayed.
