Mattei is the name on Cap Corse Mattei, the bitter herbal aperitif that Louis-Napoléon Mattei — a Corsican who settled in Marseille — built into a regional institution in the late 19th century. The Belle Époque advertising poster by Leonetto Cappiello, all swooping lines and Mediterranean colour, is one of the most reproduced images associated with the city's café culture.

Whether this garden commemorates that family specifically or another Corsican bearer of the same surname isn't documented. It doesn't much matter — Corsicans have been part of Marseille's civic and commercial fabric for generations. The city is the mainland gateway to the island, and Corsican families are woven into its life at every level.

The garden is in the 4th arrondissement, northern inner city, modest in scale. A neighbourhood park doing neighbourhood park work.
