Sylvain Bettini doesn't appear in the major reference works. That usually means the person was important at neighbourhood level — a councillor, a resistance figure, a community organiser — someone whose contribution earned a place-name rather than a plaque in a museum.

Saint-André is in the 15th arrondissement, the far northwest of Marseille. Lower density, a mix of housing estates and older residential streets, buses connecting to the Gèze metro stop. This is peripheral Marseille: a long way from the Vieux-Port in every sense.

Most tourists never come here. That's one reason to do it. The Jardin Sylvain Bettini gives you a clear picture of how most Marseillais actually live — far from the waterfront, loyal to their neighbourhood shops, proud of a corner of the city that nobody is trying to rebrand.
