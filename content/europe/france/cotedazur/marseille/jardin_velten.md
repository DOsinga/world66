The Veltens were likely Alsatian or German immigrants who arrived in Marseille during the 19th century, when the city was pulling in people from across France, Italy, Spain, and Central Europe. Merchant families who made good got their names on streets and gardens. The Veltens made good.

The garden sits north of the Palais Longchamp in the upper 4th arrondissement, in the residential zone between the Saint-Charles rail infrastructure to the southwest and the Chartreux hill to the east. Mid-century apartment blocks, local commerce, the metro at Réformés-Canebière. Functional neighbourhood Marseille.

If you're arriving or departing from Saint-Charles and have time to kill, the Jardin Velten is a short walk from the station and considerably calmer than the forecourt. That's probably the most useful thing that can be said about it.
