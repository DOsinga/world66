Mars doesn't have a workshop. That's Vulcan's role in Roman mythology — the divine blacksmith, the maker. Giving Mars an *atelier*, a studio of creative work, is a deliberate paradox: taking the force built for destruction and redirecting it toward something made rather than unmade. The work sits in Marseille's northern arrondissements, where that kind of reframing carries weight.

Public art investment in the northern reaches has historically lagged far behind what gets poured into the Vieux-Port and the waterfront. *L'Atelier de Mars* plants itself in that imbalance — a piece of some ambition in a stretch of city that doesn't expect it, asserting that the creative impulse isn't the exclusive property of tourist-friendly quarters.

This part of Marseille has its own forge, built from centuries of industrial labour, neighbourhood resilience, and economic struggle. What comes out of it is shaped by everything that went in.
