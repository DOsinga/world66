Most theatre companies tour. They arrive, perform, leave. The *maison de théâtre* model is the opposite: a company gets permanent space, uses the same rehearsal room for years, fails in private and tries again. The work that comes out is different — more patient, more local, more shaped by a specific building and its specific neighbourhood.

La Cité works on this residential model. Companies that hold space here can create and perform in the same building. Visiting productions come through too, so the programme mixes that deep familiarity with outside energy.

Marseille has several houses like this, each in a different quarter, each with a different character. They form a network rather than a competition. La Cité contributes from the southern arrondissements, which is to say: away from where the institutions cluster, close to the people who actually live here.
