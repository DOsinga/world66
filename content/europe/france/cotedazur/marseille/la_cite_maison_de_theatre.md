The *maison de théâtre* model addresses a practical problem in the French theatre system: companies that tour cannot develop long-term projects, cannot build a local audience, cannot accumulate the institutional knowledge that comes from working in the same space over many years. A theatre house — a maison — provides the opposite of touring: permanent space, time to rehearse, time to fail, time to start again.

La Cité Maison de Théâtre works on this residential model. Companies that hold space here can rehearse, create, and perform in the same building — a continuity of environment that shapes the work itself. Alongside these resident companies, the venue programmes visiting productions, creating a programme that mixes the deep familiarity of long-term residents with the energy of companies bringing work from elsewhere.

Marseille supports several such houses, which together form the backbone of a local theatre ecosystem: not a series of isolated venues competing for the same audience, but a network of spaces with different characters, different resident companies, and different relationships to the city's neighbourhoods. La Cité contributes to this network from its position in the southern arrondissements, away from the institutional centre.
