A bus is the most overlooked object in a city — unglamorous, shared, invisible through repetition. Stand one on its end and suddenly it's a monument. The destination blind points at the sky. The whole familiar shape becomes strange.

This one is planted in the northern districts, far from the polished waterfront. The years around Marseille-Provence 2013 European Capital of Culture brought a surge of public art to overlooked neighbourhoods, with commissions intended to reactivate streets that the grands projets had not reached. A vertical bus is exactly the right work for that brief — arresting without being precious, legible to anyone who has never set foot in a gallery.

The trip out here is worth it. There's something genuinely disorienting about finding it on an ordinary street, flanked by apartment blocks and parked cars. Good public art makes the neighbourhood stranger and more interesting than it was before. This does that.
