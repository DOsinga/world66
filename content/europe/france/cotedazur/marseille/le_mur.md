Le MUR works on a simple rule: one wall, one artist, one complete takeover. Every few weeks a new artist paints the surface from scratch — usually live over a single weekend — and the previous piece disappears into documentation. Part of a France-wide association running the same model in other cities, but the Cours Julien location gives it a particular setting.

Cours Julien is Marseille's bohemian hillside — painted facades, independent bookshops, café terraces catching the afternoon light. It has been the city's creative neighbourhood since the 1980s, and the wall fits naturally into streetscapes that already treat exterior surfaces as fair game.

Check Le MUR Marseille's social media to catch the changeover weekend. Between editions the wall stands as a finished piece for two to four weeks before the next cycle starts.
