Four names: Colombani Dominique, Lambert Maurice, Mortier Christian, Torresani Louis. Corsican, Savoyard, Norman, Italian surnames — a small cross-section of working-class Marseille who ended up fighting in the same streets in August 1944.

The liberation of Marseille was fast and brutal. Allied forces landed in Provence on 15 August; by the 28th the city was free. In between, French units and local Resistance fighters went house by house against a German garrison that had no intention of going quietly. The port installations had been blown up. The cités of the northern arrondissements contributed large numbers of fighters.

What makes this different from an official monument: the residents put it up themselves. Not the municipality — the neighbours. They decided to name the dead and keep the names visible. Colombani, Lambert, Mortier, Torresani were not famous. Just four men from the estate who did not come back.
