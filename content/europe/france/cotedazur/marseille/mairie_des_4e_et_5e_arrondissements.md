The Mairie des 4e et 5e arrondissements is the administrative hub for two inland arrondissements north and east of the city centre. The 4th arrondissement includes the Chartreux neighbourhood, a relatively middle-class residential area built on the slopes north of the Canebière. The 5th takes in Belle de Mai, a working-class neighbourhood that has undergone significant cultural investment — most notably through La Friche Belle de Mai, a major arts and cultural complex housed in a former tobacco factory.

Like all of Marseille's sectoral mairies, this one handles civil registration and neighbourhood services for residents of the two arrondissements. The sectoral structure dates from a 1982 law that reorganised local governance in Paris, Lyon, and Marseille, creating elected sectoral councils alongside the city-wide municipal council. The intention was to bring local government closer to residents in France's three largest cities.

For visitors, the mairie is primarily of practical interest — a place to handle administrative matters if needed. The surrounding neighbourhoods, particularly Belle de Mai with its arts scene, are more rewarding to explore on foot.
