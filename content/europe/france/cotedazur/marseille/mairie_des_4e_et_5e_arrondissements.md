The 4th arrondissement is solidly residential, built on slopes north of the Canebière. The 5th is Belle de Mai — a working-class neighbourhood that produced César Baldaccini, the sculptor, and more recently La Friche Belle de Mai: a sprawling arts complex carved out of a former tobacco factory, with rooftop skate park, theatre stages, artists' studios, and a year-round programme. One of the most interesting spaces in Marseille.

The mairie covers both arrondissements with standard sectoral functions — civil registration, local services, elected sectoral council. The structure dates from a 1982 law reorganising local governance in Paris, Lyon, and Marseille, intended to bring decision-making closer to residents.

For visitors, the mairie is mostly of practical interest. Belle de Mai with its arts scene is the reason to be in the neighbourhood.
