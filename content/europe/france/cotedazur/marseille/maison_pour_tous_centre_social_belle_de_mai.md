Belle de Mai has two sides. There is La Friche — the converted tobacco factory that became one of France's most celebrated arts centres, written up in every cultural magazine. And there is everything else: one of the most deprived districts in metropolitan France, high unemployment, dense housing, streets that tourists don't walk down. This community centre is from the other side.

Maison Pour Tous — House for Everyone — is a French municipal institution. After-school clubs, language classes, counselling, meeting rooms for neighbourhood groups. It has been here longer than La Friche was famous. It will still be here when the next cultural trend moves on.

Walk past on a weekday morning. The noticeboards outside tell you exactly who uses this neighbourhood and what they need.
