Flowers arrive before dawn on the Cours Belsunce. Roses, gladioli, potted geraniums, seasonal bunches — by the time morning commuters pass, everything is arranged and ready. The stall-holders, or their families, have worked this same stretch for generations.

The street around them has been rebuilt several times. Haussmann, postwar reconstruction, pedestrianisation. What fills it now is North African clothing shops, mobile phone repair stalls, discount goods. The flower market survived all of it. It is the oldest commercial layer that simply never stopped.

Closed Mondays. Come before noon on other days. Come early for the best selection; come late if you want to negotiate on whatever is left.
