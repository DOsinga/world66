Every morning except Monday, the boats come in and the fishermen set up on the Quai des Belges. Not what a supply chain decided to stock — what the Mediterranean gave up last night. Rascasse, grondin, saint-pierre: the bouillabaisse fish, when the fishing was good. Sea urchins and sardines when it wasn't. The restaurants around the port buy here. The connection between catch and kitchen is same-day and direct.

Massalia was founded around 600 BCE and fish were among the original reasons the site worked. The market on the Quai des Belges has operated in some continuous form for most of the port's history. The current arrangements are recent; the underlying activity is not.

Get here before 8am to see the full market. By 10am the best fish is gone and stalls are packing up. It is small — a dozen stalls, no ceremony, no English required. Point at what you want.
