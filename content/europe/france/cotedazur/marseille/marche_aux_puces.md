Plan d'Aou is a large post-war housing estate on a hillside above the autoroute. The flea market here has been running since the early twentieth century — older than the towers that now surround it. Professional dealers share pitches with families clearing out a relative's apartment. The goods are practical, the prices are low, the buyers are neighbours.

This is not the Cours Julien flea market with its curated vintage rails. This is the northern arrondissements — the most economically deprived part of France's most unequal major city — running its own commercial commons. Electrical components at one end. Furniture and household goods in the middle. Clothing at the other.

Take the 35 or L2 bus from the centre. The ride through the northern arrondissements is itself the first half of the trip.
