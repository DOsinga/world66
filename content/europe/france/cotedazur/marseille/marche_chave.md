Boulevard Chavé, Tuesday, Thursday, Saturday mornings. Fish from the overnight boats. Vegetables from the Var plain. Cheese from Alpine producers who have their Marseille rounds. The people shopping here have been shopping here for thirty years. They know which stall has the better tomatoes this week.

The 5th is working-class residential Marseille without the gentrification pressure of the 6th or the tourist foot traffic of the centre. The market matches the neighbourhood: nobody is performing Provence for visitors. It is just a neighbourhood doing its weekly shop.

Arrive before 10am for fish. The stalls wind down from the outside in as stock runs out.
