The côté pair — the even-numbered, eastern side — of the Marché de Castellane mirrors its counterpart across the Avenue du Prado. Together the two halves form a single market that stretches along several blocks of the boulevard; this side tends to hold more textile and household-goods stalls alongside the food vendors, giving the full circuit a variety that neither half alone could offer.

Cross from the impair side at the foot of the block and you pick up the remainder of the market here: clothing, fabric, kitchenware, and seasonal produce that changes with the week. Regulars often work one side on the way down and double back on the other, finishing with whatever they missed on the first pass.
