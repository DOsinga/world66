Cross from the impair side at the foot of the block. This is the pair half — the eastern pavement — and it picks up where the other leaves off. Clothing, fabric, kitchenware alongside seasonal produce. Regulars work one side going down and double back on the other, finishing with whatever they missed on the first pass.

The two halves together make one proper market. Neither is complete without the other.
