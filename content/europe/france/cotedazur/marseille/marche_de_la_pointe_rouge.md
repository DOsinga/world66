The Corniche Kennedy dissolves into the marina car park at Pointe Rouge. The market runs Wednesday and Saturday mornings — vegetables, olives, cheese, charcuterie, a fishmonger who sources locally when he can and from Arnavaux when he cannot. It is small enough that regulars know every trader by name.

Chandlers and yacht-club members come between tides. In summer the beach crowd swells the numbers; by October it is back to neighbourhood rhythm.

The appeal is the position: marina behind you, the Calanques beginning their limestone rise a few kilometres south, a Saturday morning with nothing urgent on.
