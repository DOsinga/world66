The 13th arrondissement's northeastern hills are where Marseille's urban grid quietly gives up. La Rose — named after an old estate — runs on residential time. The metro terminus is here, which means you can get in from the centre in twenty minutes and step out into streets where the pace is entirely different.

The market reflects the neighbourhood. Mostly families, mostly regulars. Summer produce, spice stalls serving the North African community that's settled here over decades. Stallholders who know which customers want the small courgettes and which ones want the big ones.

You won't find anything exotic. That's the point.
