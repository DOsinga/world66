According to medieval legend, Martha of Bethany sailed from the Holy Land after the Crucifixion, landed in Provence, and found a town terrorised by a river monster called the Tarasque. She subdued it with holy water and her own belt as a leash. The people destroyed it. Tarascon built its entire founding myth on the story — they still celebrate the Fête de la Tarasque.

The Sainte-Marthe quarter keeps a quieter version of that memory: a chapel to Martha, a feast day on 29 July. The market itself is standard 14th arrondissement provisioning — vegetables, olives, dried herbs, cheap household goods, the occasional fabric stall.

No dragons. Entirely unpretentious. Worth the detour from the Vieux-Port precisely because of that.
