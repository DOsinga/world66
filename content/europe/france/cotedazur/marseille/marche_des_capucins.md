Italians came first in the 19th century. Then Armenians fleeing the genocide. Then Algerian workers. Then sub-Saharan Africans and Comorians from the 1980s onward. Each wave shaped Noailles and left something behind. The Capucins is the archaeological record of all of it.

The spice merchants stock things no supermarket carries. The halal butchers serve cuts prepared to specific community specifications. The stalls for Comorian, Malian, and Senegalese cooking aren't concessions to diversity — they're just the market, doing what it's always done.

This is the most genuinely multicultural food market in France. It doesn't perform diversity. Arrive with a shopping list.
