The 7th arrondissement is close enough to the calanques that the fish here is actually good — local catches arriving at a covered market that has been in this spot since the 19th century. Also: cheese with proper Corsican and Italian range, charcuterie, flowers. Prices are higher than the Capucins. The clientele knows what it's paying for.

Endoume below the Corniche Kennedy, Bompard above — this is Marseille's most quietly prosperous residential quarter. The market matches it. Unhurried. The same stallholders in the same spots for decades. Conversations about which fish is better today last longer than the transaction itself.

Nothing about this market is designed for visitors. If you're staying in the 7th or walking the Corniche, it's worth fifteen minutes — a version of the city the tourist apparatus hasn't noticed.
