The Poor Clares founded their order in 1212. The sisters here still observe strict enclosure: they live entirely within the walls and have almost no public presence. From the street, it's a high walled compound — easy to walk past without noticing.

There's a small parlour window where monastery-made goods can be collected. That's the transaction. You don't go in.

Communities like this occupy substantial urban plots across France — land walled off from the surrounding city for centuries. This one sits close to animated streets and all the noise of a Mediterranean port. Inside, that might as well not exist.
