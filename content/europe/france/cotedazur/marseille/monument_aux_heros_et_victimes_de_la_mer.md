The Greeks founded Massalia here around 600 BCE. That's 2,600 years of ships leaving this harbour and not always returning. Sailors, fishermen, naval officers, dock workers — the Mediterranean is a beautiful sea with a long memory for the lives it has taken.

This monument stands near the Vieux-Port, close to the point where ships have always departed — for the Levant, for Algeria, for the Americas. It's the right location. You can see the mouth of the harbour from here.

What the monument can't do is count. Storms, wars, the slave trade, colonial campaigns, ordinary shipwreck — the categories blur across centuries. The memorial holds the place of an accounting that can never be made precise. That's exactly why collective monuments like this one exist.
