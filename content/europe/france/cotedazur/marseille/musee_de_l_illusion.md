The Musée de l'Illusion opened in Marseille in 2019 as part of a European franchise of the same name, with locations in Zagreb, Vienna, Paris, and a growing list of other cities. The format is identical across all of them: optical illusions, holograms, mirror mazes, and installations designed to produce disorienting or photogenic effects. There is no Marseille-specific content and no permanent collection in any meaningful sense — the experience is built to be photographed, and repeat visitors would find nothing new.

That said, the museum occupies a well-located building near the Vieux-Port and draws considerable numbers of visitors, particularly families and groups looking for something interactive and accessible. The installations are well-maintained, the staff are multilingual, and the experience is genuinely diverting for an hour or so. Children reliably enjoy it.

If you are in Marseille to understand the city, the nearby MuCEM, Vieille Charité, and Musée d'Histoire de Marseille will serve you better. If you have already seen those and want something light before catching a ferry, or if you are travelling with young children who have run out of patience for medieval harbours, this is a reasonable fallback.
