The name says it all: not the gilded Municipal Opera up the street, but something rawer. This is a performance bar in the tradition of Marseille's great musical mixing — the port has been funnelling North African chaabi, Italian melodrama, and French chanson together for generations, and the venues that matter are the ones that hold all of it at once.

Come expecting charged, slightly unpredictable atmosphere rather than a formal programme. The crowd takes music seriously but wears that seriousness lightly — which, in this city, is the highest compliment you can get.
