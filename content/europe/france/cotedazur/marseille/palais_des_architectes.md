Every building permit signed in France requires an architect registered with the Ordre. The PACA regional council — based here — is one of the busiest in the country. No surprise: from the Vieux-Port's 17th-century grid to Fernand Pouillon's post-war reconstruction to Zaha Hadid's CMA CGM Tower, Marseille has been a testing ground for ambitious building across four centuries.

It's not a public museum. But walk the streets of the southern arrondissements around it and you're looking at exactly the urban fabric this institution regulates. The debates about what Marseille should build next all pass through here eventually.
