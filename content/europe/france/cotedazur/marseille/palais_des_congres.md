This is where the medical congresses, logistics summits, and pharmaceutical conferences land when they choose Marseille. Aix-Marseille Université and its hospital network pull a lot of that traffic; so does the city's position as the natural hub for anything with a North African or Mediterranean dimension.

The setup for delegates is better than most convention venues: beaches ten minutes' walk, Parc Borély nearby, Metro to the Old Port. People don't disappear into a concrete shell for the entire trip.

Not a tourist destination. But if you're wondering why hotel prices spike certain weeks near the Prado, this is why.
