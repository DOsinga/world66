The Palais des Congrès de Marseille is the city's principal venue for large conventions, trade fairs, and professional conferences. Positioned near the Rond-Point du Prado in the southern residential districts, it is close to the Plages du Prado and the Vélodrome stadium, putting it in the part of the city that has absorbed much of Marseille's modern development. The complex includes modular congress halls, exhibition space, and meeting rooms that can be configured for events from a few hundred to several thousand delegates.

Marseille's position as the largest city on the French Mediterranean coast — and as the second port in France — has made it a natural host for events with a maritime, logistics, or Mediterranean focus. Medical and pharmaceutical congresses are among the most frequent users of the venue, reflecting the presence of Aix-Marseille Université and its associated hospital network. The city's connections to North Africa also bring commercial and trade events with a transregional dimension that would be harder to stage anywhere else in France.

For delegates arriving at the Palais des Congrès, the surrounding area offers easy access to the beaches, to the Parc Borély, and to the city centre via the Métro. Marseille's compact geography means that the Old Port, Notre-Dame de la Garde, and the MuCEM are all reachable within twenty minutes, making conference trips here more varied than those to purpose-built convention cities.
