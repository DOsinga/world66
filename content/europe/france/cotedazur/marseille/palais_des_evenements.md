When this car park is full on a Saturday evening, you're looking at a Marseillais celebration: a cousin's wedding, a school-year-end party, a neighbourhood association's fiftieth anniversary dinner. This is where the south and east arrondissements hold the gatherings that matter most.

The venue itself is pure practicality — flexible floor, room for hundreds, catering infrastructure that scales. Nobody photographs it. Nobody reviews it. It just works, which is exactly what these places are for.

The tourist Marseille of the Old Port and the Corniche is real. This is also real.
