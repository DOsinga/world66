The eastern arrondissements are far from the Vieux-Port, far from the tourist trail. This arena is where they put their serious local energy. Basketball and handball run the calendar; when clubs from the 13th and 14th play a derby here, the crowd is as loud and partisan as anything in the city.

The "omnisports" format means the floor shifts between basketball, handball, boxing, gymnastics, and martial arts without any single discipline claiming it permanently — useful flexibility when clubs run on tight budgets.

Concerts fill the gaps in the sporting calendar. Acts that skip more central venues sometimes land here: easier parking, and more convenient for residents who don't want to cross the whole city on a night out.
