When they dug the metro in the 1970s, they needed somewhere to put the rubble. The answer: push it into the sea. That fill became this long coastal strip of grass, paths, and artificial rocky coves running parallel to the Prado beaches.

Near the northern end, where the beach strip begins, there's a full-size copy of Michelangelo's *David* standing by the roundabout. It was presented to the city as a public gift. As landmarks go it's unexpected, but it works.

The park is always open, always free. Summer brings families and joggers in the evenings after the beaches close. Winter empties it almost entirely — but the walk remains good, with views across the bay toward the Frioul islands.
