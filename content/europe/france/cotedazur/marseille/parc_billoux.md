François Billoux spent decades as a senior figure in the French Communist Party. In the mid-1940s he held ministerial posts in de Gaulle's provisional government — a coalition that didn't survive the Cold War. The PCF was pushed out of government in 1947, and Billoux spent the rest of his career as a prominent party figure from the outside.

Marseille had long stretches of left-wing governance and has several such commemorations. Naming a park in the 13th after Billoux is the kind of thing the city does without making a fuss about it.

The park is exactly what the neighbourhood needs: trees, shade, pétanque, a children's area. The 13th is short on green space. On a warm afternoon this fills up fast.
