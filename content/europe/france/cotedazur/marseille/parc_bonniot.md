The centenary oak at the park's southern end was here before Saint-Julien was a suburb. Its canopy alone dominates the space — deep shade over a park that otherwise has cedars, pines, and lindens all planted back when the surrounding streets were still fields. That scale gives Bonniot a weight that newer parks in the 12th lack.

It's a 6,000-square-metre wedge between the boulevard and the Impasse Bonniot — not large, but well-organised. Boules court at one end, children's play area, a small football pitch for teenagers.

This is a neighbourhood park doing its job. The trees make it better than average.
