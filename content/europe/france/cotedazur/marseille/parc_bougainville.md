Louis Antoine de Bougainville sailed around the world for France between 1766 and 1769 — the first French circumnavigation. His expedition's botanist collected a plant in South America that ended up bearing his name. You'll see bougainvillea flowering purple and pink on walls all over Marseille. The climate suits it perfectly.

The park is in the 3rd arrondissement, one of the city's denser northern districts. Plane trees, pétanque courts, a playground. Families from the surrounding tower blocks fill it in the late afternoon.

Working-class, unpretentious, well-used. A district where green space matters, and this delivers it.
