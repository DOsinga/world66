The northern arrondissements aren't the Marseille of the postcards. No harbour, no calanques — just residential streets, plane trees, and people getting on with things. Parc Brégante fits that perfectly.

Plane trees over gravel paths and benches. Retired residents in the morning, parents with young children in the afternoon. Dogs in the early-morning cool. A meeting point, a lunch spot, a shortcut between streets.

If you want to see Marseille unfiltered — unpretentious, working-class, lived-in — this part of the city is where to look. The park is nothing special and that is exactly the point.
