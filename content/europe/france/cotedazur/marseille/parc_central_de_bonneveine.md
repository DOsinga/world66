Bonneveine is a quiet residential quarter halfway between the city centre and the Calanques. The families who moved here came for the Prado beaches nearby and the relative calm. The Prado seafront is less than ten minutes west on foot. This park is where they spend the morning before heading there.

Tall plane trees and pines give it genuine shade — not the pretend shade of young trees, but the deep kind that matters when it's 33°C. The pétanque regulars claim the shadiest corners before noon.

Nothing exceptional here. A well-maintained, well-used neighbourhood park. On a hot summer morning, that shade is the main attraction.
