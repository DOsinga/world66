*Champêtre* means rural or pastoral. This park is in the northeastern outer districts where the city starts to thin out toward the garrigue, and the name still fits: pines, Mediterranean scrub, a looser feel than the manicured parks closer to the centre.

The sound of the city genuinely recedes here. Families spread out on the grass, children run in the shade. The surrounding landscape hints at limestone hills just beyond the city limits.

Not a tourist destination. But if you're in the eastern districts and want a patch of semi-natural Marseille inside the city boundary, this is the place.
