L'Estaque, just down the slope, is where Cézanne started painting in the 1870s and where Braque worked out the foundations of Cubism in 1908. What drew them: ochre rooftops, blue sea, bare white limestone rock, and a quality of light that doesn't exist anywhere else in France. The hills around Parc de Corbière share all of that.

The park sits in the 16th arrondissement, between the industrial port and the village. The name likely comes from the terrain — *corbière* in Provençal points to rocky outcrops or the crows that nest in them.

A neighbourhood park, not a scenic viewpoint. But anyone walking up from L'Estaque toward the hills will pass through it, and the surroundings alone make that walk worthwhile.
