The name tells you what to look for. *Font Obscure* — Dark Spring or Hidden Fountain — points to a water source so overhung with vegetation it acquired an air of mystery. Springs like this were once common in the limestone hills east of Marseille, where water percolates through rock and surfaces in shaded ravines.

The font may no longer flow freely — urban development has altered many of the city's historic springs — but the dense canopy that grew around it remains. Oaks, acacias, Mediterranean pines. The park stays noticeably cooler than the surrounding streets in summer, which is why it fills up on hot afternoons.

For anyone interested in how Marseille's geology shaped its neighbourhoods — the springs, the cisterns, the seasonal streams that once ran through this dry corner of Provence — the name alone is worth a moment's thought.
