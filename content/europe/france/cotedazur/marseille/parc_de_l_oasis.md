Summer in Marseille's northern hills is unforgiving. The 13th arrondissement bakes, and the residential blocks around here offer nowhere to hide. This park earns its name the honest way: shade trees, a bench you can actually use without burning your legs, a few minutes of genuine relief.

It's a neighbourhood park, not a scenic destination. Children play football on the grass. Pensioners claim the shadier spots before noon. Come Saturday morning, joggers loop through. Nobody's here for the view — just for the trees.
