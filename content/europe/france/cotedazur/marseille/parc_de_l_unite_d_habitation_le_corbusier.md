The park isn't an afterthought to the Cité Radieuse — it was designed as part of it. Le Corbusier's team began landscaping this 2.8-hectare site in October 1951, a year before the building opened. The paths follow a diagonal axis so you read the famous pilotis properly as you walk — a designed promenade, not a nice lawn they happened to leave.

Stand beneath those pilotis. The whole 18-storey block hovers above you while the park extends in both directions. That's the idea: housing as a vertical city, freed from the ground. The Cité Radieuse is UNESCO World Heritage. In December 2023, the park earned its own classification as a historic monument — the landscape legally inseparable from the architecture.
