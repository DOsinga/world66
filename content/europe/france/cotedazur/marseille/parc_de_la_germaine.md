Until recently, the dense residential blocks of Saint-Tronc had almost no green space. Then came 14 months of work, three million euros from the Metropolis and the Bouches-du-Rhône department, and 127 new trees planted between a lycée and the Haddad roundabout — a deliberately unglamorous stretch of the Boulevard Urbain Sud.

The name comes from an old local toponym, not a person. The park is honest about what it is: play equipment, benches under young trees, dry public toilets. Give those 127 trees twenty years and this strip will feel transformed. For now it's doing necessary work in a neighbourhood that finally has somewhere to sit outside.
