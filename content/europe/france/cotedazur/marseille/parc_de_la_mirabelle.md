The mirabelle is a small amber plum — fragrant, sweet, grown across Provence for centuries. The park probably occupies what were once orchards before the streets of the 12th arrondissement filled in around them. The name is the last trace.

It's a neighbourhood park in the hilly east of Marseille, where the city rises toward the Massif de l'Étoile. Families from La Valentine use it, the terrain is gently uneven, and on clear days the elevated position offers views back toward the city. Nothing spectacular — just the kind of place that makes a neighbourhood liveable.
