Vieux Moulin — Old Mill. Marseille's limestone hills were once covered with windmills grinding grain for the city, powered by the mistral. By the nineteenth century most had collapsed or been demolished as industrial milling took over. The park marks the spot where one stood. The mill itself is completely gone.

The park is modest — grass and trees in a residential quarter of the eastern arrondissements, serving apartment blocks rather than tourists. But the name does something: it maps a disappeared city onto the existing one, reminding you that these hills were once functional, grinding landscape rather than residential.

If you're interested in Marseille's pre-industrial history, walk up here and look at the ridgeline. Instead of antenna masts, try to picture sails turning in the wind. The mistral still comes through on schedule.
