The Foresta family were Marseille's old landed aristocracy — Marquis de Foresta, estates on the northern hills where the air was cooler and the views extended over the bay. The park incorporates what was once their estate territory. The formal gardens of the gentry are long gone; what's left is a municipal green that the whole neighbourhood uses.

The northern hills feel quite different from the city core: limestone ridges, scattered pines, broader avenues and apartment blocks rather than the tight old-town lanes of the south. The park fits the pace up here — unhurried, local, not on any tourist itinerary.

On clear days after the mistral, the light up here gets very sharp. Worth noting if you make it this far north.
