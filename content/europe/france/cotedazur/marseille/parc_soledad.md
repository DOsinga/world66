The name is the interesting thing: *Soledad*, meaning solitude, sits oddly on a park surrounded by houses and apartment blocks in the 11th arrondissement. At some point this was the quieter edge of the city — suburban in the original sense, before the suburbs filled in. The name stuck long after the solitude didn't.

It's a flat, well-settled neighbourhood park now: morning walks, schoolchildren, the usual. Whatever contemplative quality the name once described has been mostly domesticated. That's fine. Parks do their jobs.

Worth knowing: the southeastern corner of Marseille is close to the Calanques National Park. A short drive south takes you to the trailheads at La Barasse or the coast at Les Goudes, where limestone meets the sea properly. Parc Soledad is a neighbourhood aside — the real draw lies further south.
