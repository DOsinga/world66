Parc Valmer is a terraced garden cut into the hillside above the southern Corniche. From the top — shaded by umbrella pines, facing south — the eye carries past the Malmousque inlet toward the limestone outcrops that mark the beginning of the Calanques. It's an exceptional view, and it costs nothing.

The garden belongs to the Villa Valmer, a nineteenth-century private house that's now a conference centre. The grounds are open to the public. Stone steps and retaining walls hold back the hillside through several levels of planting: agaves, oleanders, Mediterranean scrub. A path descends from the lower terrace to the Malmousque cove, a small inlet popular with local swimmers.

The villas along this ridge were always competing for the same views. Parc Valmer preserves one of the best spots in public hands. A good morning: walk down to the cove, swim, come back up through the garden.
