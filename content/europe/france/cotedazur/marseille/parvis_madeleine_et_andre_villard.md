Cities name their squares after great men. Marseille also names them after the Villards — Madeleine and André, commemorated here for doing the quiet work that holds a neighbourhood together. Nobody outside this quarter knows them. That's the point.

The parvis sits in the northern residential districts, a world away from the Vieux-Port. A small paved forecourt, a bench, maybe a tree. The kind of place where you wait for someone, or let the kids run for five minutes while you catch your breath.

Most cities let ordinary dedication disappear. This one inscribes it in stone, block by block, name by name.
