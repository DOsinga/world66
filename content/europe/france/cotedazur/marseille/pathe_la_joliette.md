Thirty years ago, this was abandoned dockland. Now it's Euroméditerranée — France's largest urban regeneration project — and the Pathé is the leisure anchor of the whole operation. Fourteen screens, modern building, five minutes' walk from the restored nineteenth-century dock warehouses with their cast-iron columns and long arcaded galleries.

The location is genuinely good. MuCEM is down the waterfront. The metro is two minutes away. The esplanade fills up on weekend evenings. It's the easiest cinema to reach from the centre if you're staying near Joliette.

Mainly mainstream French and Hollywood releases, with some original-language screenings thrown in. Go for the film, stay for the docks.
