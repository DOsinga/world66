Pathé La Joliette sits within the vast Euroméditerranée development zone, the largest urban renewal project in southern France, which has steadily transformed a neglected stretch of industrial waterfront north of the Vieux-Port into a working mixed-use district. The cinema occupies a purpose-built building in the Les Docks neighbourhood, a stone's throw from the repurposed nineteenth-century dock warehouses now filled with offices, shops, and restaurants. Pathé, one of France's two dominant commercial cinema chains, anchors the leisure side of the regeneration with a modern fourteen-screen complex that draws audiences from across the city.

The location makes it an easy stop when exploring the new Joliette. The Musée des Civilisations de l'Europe et de la Méditerranée (MuCEM) and the Villa Méditerranée are a short walk south along the waterfront, and the Joliette metro station connects the area to the centre in minutes. Showtimes lean toward mainstream French and Hollywood releases with a handful of original-language screenings each week.

For visitors staying in or near the Joliette quarter, this is the most convenient cinema option. The surrounding esplanade is lively on weekend evenings, and the dock warehouses — with their cast-iron columns and long arcaded galleries — are worth a slow wander before or after a film.
