L'Estaque, just north of here, is where Cézanne came to paint from 1870 onward, and where Braque followed thirty years later and helped invent Cubism. You can still see what drew them: the harbour, the factory chimneys, the flat rooftops stacked against limestone hills, the particular quality of the sea light.

Place aux Fleurs is the neighbourhood square of this area — plane trees, benches, the residents of the surrounding blocks. It has the permanence of a place that has never needed to advertise itself. In the northern arrondissements, where public green space is sparse, reliable shade and somewhere to sit carry real value.

The name — Flower Square — probably came with the aspiration of a newly planted public space. Whether flowers were ever sold here, nobody seems to know. The painted coastline is a short walk in any direction.
