Stand here and watch who's coming off those ferries. The overnight crossings from Algiers, Tunis, and Casablanca — that's the traffic that tells you what Marseille actually is: the northernmost city of North Africa in all but name. The summer queues are a more honest picture of the city than anything in a tourist brochure.

Behind you is the CMA CGM Tower, Zaha Hadid's glass skyscraper for the French container shipping giant. Finished in 2010 at 147 metres, it's the tallest building in the city. The architects made it taper and twist so it reads differently from every angle — next to the ferry terminal's functional concrete, the contrast is jarring in a way that feels very Euroméditerranée.

The whole district got rebuilt from the late 1990s onwards. MuCEM is a ten-minute walk. The Villa Méditerranée is closer. The square itself is more transit node than destination, but it's the right place to understand what this city is actually connected to.
