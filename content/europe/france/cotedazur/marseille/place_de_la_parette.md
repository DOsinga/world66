"Parette" is a Provençal word for a low-growing plant — probably wall-rue fern, the kind that colonises limestone outcrops. Before there were streets here, there were fields and garrigue, and the farmers named every patch of terrain. When the city expanded, it paved over the fields and kept the names.

Eastern Marseille is full of this. The Huveaune valley pushed urban development in one direction, the hills resisted in another, and the street names nailed to posts in between kept the old vocabulary alive. It's a peculiarly French approach to amnesia: erase the landscape, commemorate the vocabulary.

The square serves the apartment blocks around it. Nothing special to see — but the name is a small act of remembrance.
