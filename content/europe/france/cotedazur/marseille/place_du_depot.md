The dépôt was a tram maintenance yard. Marseille ran an extensive tramway network through the early twentieth century, and the yards that serviced it seeded names across the northern neighbourhoods. When the trams were withdrawn in the 1950s, the depots went. The names stayed.

The square is modest — plane trees, benches, buses on routes that trace roughly where the trams once ran. The workshops and repair garages nearby are no coincidence: trades cluster near infrastructure, and when the infrastructure moves, the trades linger.

Marseille brought its trams back in 2007, but this corner of the northern districts sits just beyond the new network's reach.
