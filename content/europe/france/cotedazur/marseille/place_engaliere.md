Endoume is the kind of neighbourhood that doesn't need advertising. Stone buildings, narrow streets cut into the limestone ridge, a settled population that knows every shortcut to the waterfront. Place Engalière is a widening in the local street pattern — a place where the neighbourhood can breathe between the walls.

The name probably comes from an Occitan toponym, preserving a terrain description that official French cartography absorbed without translating. Whatever it once described, the square sits high enough that the Mediterranean is audible before it's visible.

Walkers heading for the Corniche Kennedy pass through without always noticing the square is there.
