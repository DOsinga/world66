The Préfecture des Bouches-du-Rhône is the administrative seat of the most populated department in southern France, and the main building near Place Félix-Baret ran out of room. The Peytral site handles the overflow — permits, residency documentation, the functions that generate the kind of public footfall that fills waiting rooms.

The neighbourhood is one of Marseille's more solidly bourgeois quarters. Wide streets, Haussmann-era stone, an atmosphere that sits closer to Paris than to the Vieux-Port. No reason to come here unless you have paperwork to file — and if you do, check in advance which building your particular service is in.
