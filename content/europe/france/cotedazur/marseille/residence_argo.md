The Argo was Jason's ship — the vessel that carried the Argonauts after the Golden Fleece, one of the great adventure stories of Greek antiquity. Naming an apartment block after it is a quiet piece of civic mythology. Marseille was founded by Greek sailors from Phocaea around 600 BCE, and the sea has run through the city's identity ever since.

Some tellings of the Argonaut myth route Jason through the western Mediterranean, not far from Provençal waters. Whether the developers had that in mind or simply liked the resonance of a famous ship is unknown. Either way, *Argo* appears on buildings across Mediterranean port cities for the same reason — the name carries weight without needing explanation.

The building itself is a postwar residential block, typical of the construction that filled in Marseille's northern districts during the rapid growth decades. Not a landmark to visit — but the name above the entrance carries more history than most building signs in the city.
