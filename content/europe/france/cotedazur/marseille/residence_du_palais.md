The Palais de Justice sits nearby: a neo-classical courthouse with columns and pediment that gives this part of central Marseille unusual gravity. The Résidence du Palais borrows that name, and historically its prestige — an obvious address for lawyers and court officials who needed to be a short walk from work.

The surrounding district is the administrative heart of the city. Wide streets, nineteenth-century stone, the weight of institutional buildings. It has a more Parisian character than most of Marseille — which is either a recommendation or a warning, depending on your preferences.
