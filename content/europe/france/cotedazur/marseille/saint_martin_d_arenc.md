This was the parish of the port workers — the dockers, the warehouse hands, the men who loaded and unloaded ships through the industrial age of Marseille's northern harbour. Saint Martin, the Roman soldier who split his cloak with a beggar, was a fitting patron for a community built on physical labour and mutual reliance.

The Euroméditerranée regeneration project has been eating this neighbourhood since the 1990s. New towers, office blocks, and cultural venues are going up all around Arenc. The church hasn't moved.

That's what neighbourhood churches do in cities like this. They outlast the waves of demolition and renewal, and they mark the fact that before the architects arrived, there were people here with a street life and a Sunday morning.
