Marseille's music credentials are real. The city was running hip-hop and North African cross-pollinations through its port-district clubs before the Paris music industry had noticed. That Mediterranean crossroads position — North African, Italian, Levantine sounds all arriving through the same harbour — gave the scene its character.

Le Moulin fits into that. It's an independent venue in the northern arrondissements, intimate enough that you're close to the stage wherever you stand. Rock, electronic, hip-hop, oud — the programme follows the music, not a marketing brief.

Named after the old mill that once worked this working-class quarter. The neighbourhood's still working-class. Nothing about the place is polished or corporate, which is exactly right.
