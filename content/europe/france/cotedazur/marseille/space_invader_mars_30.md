Space Invader is the anonymous French artist who has been tiling pixelated extraterrestrials — lifted from the 1978 arcade game — onto building corners and façades across the world since the late 1990s. Each city gets its own series code. Marseille is MARS. This is the thirtieth piece placed here.

The tiles are proper ceramic mosaic, which means they survive decades of weather and cleaning campaigns that would dissolve spray paint. Their lo-fi, eight-bit look sits against Haussmann stone, Belle Époque ironwork, or brutalist concrete with cheerful indifference to the architectural context.

The artist maintains a scoring system and an official app called Flash Invaders — you scan each piece to earn points. The Panier and the streets around the Vieux-Port are rich hunting ground. Finding one — craning your neck, suddenly spotting the small alien that has watched this street corner for years while everyone walked past — is a genuine small pleasure.
