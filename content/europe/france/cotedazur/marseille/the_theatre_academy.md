This is the only theatre in Marseille where the plays are in English. The city has been multilingual for most of its 2,600 years — Greek, Arabic, Italian, Armenian — and English fits naturally into that stack, even if it's newer to the stage.

The academy runs both a school and a performance company. Students get training in acting and voice; audiences get productions aimed at Anglophones who want live theatre without wrestling with rapid Marseillais. It sits in the 1st arrondissement, close enough to the tourist routes that you'll walk past it.
