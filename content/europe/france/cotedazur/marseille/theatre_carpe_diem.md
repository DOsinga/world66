The name is from Horace. *Seize the day* — which in a theatre context means live performance, here and now, not recorded or streamed. It's a statement of intent built into the name before the programme even starts.

In practice it's a small independent venue running a mix of amateur, semi-professional, and occasional professional productions. This is how the smaller Marseille stages work — they serve the companies that need a stage, not just the audience that wants one. You develop the next generation of theatre by giving them a room and an audience.

The space keeps everything close and text-driven. No room for spectacle here. Just the play and the people watching it.
