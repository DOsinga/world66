*L'Œuvre* means the work — the creative act, not the product. That framing is deliberate. France has a whole tradition of *théâtre de création*, where the director's vision takes precedence over everything else, including what the audience expects. This theatre sits in that tradition.

It's small and independent, so it doesn't have the subsidies of a *scène nationale*, but the commitment to contemporary creation is the same. The programming mixes Marseille directors with touring companies from the French circuit. What they have in common: the interpretation of the material is the point, not just the plot.

If you want a comfortable revival of a crowd-pleasing classic, this is probably not your night out.
