This was a *gare de marchandises* — a railway goods yard. Big, rough, designed to move bulk freight, not people. When rail freight declined the building sat empty, and the Belle de Mai cultural cluster absorbed it.

*Franche* means free zone — no conditions, no market constraints. The programming takes the space seriously: high ceilings, industrial scale, a floor plan that doesn't dictate where the stage has to be. That suits theatre that thinks architecturally, that treats the room as part of the dramaturgy.

The Belle de Mai area north of central Marseille has become one of the densest concentrations of cultural infrastructure in the city. Gare Franche is one of its live performance anchors.
