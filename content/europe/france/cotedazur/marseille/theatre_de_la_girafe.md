In 1827 a young giraffe named Zarafa stepped off a ship in Marseille — the first giraffe ever seen in France. She was a diplomatic gift from the Ottoman Viceroy of Egypt to King Charles X. Marseille made her a leather coat against the Provençal cold, and the following spring she walked north to Paris, where she became the sensation of the decade.

This small theatre in the port quarter has taken her as its emblem. It programs intimate, experimental work: new French writing, devised pieces, things that wouldn't survive in the subsidised houses. The director probably sold you your ticket.

The giraffe is a fitting mascot. She arrived in an unexpected place and looked at everything from an unusual height.
