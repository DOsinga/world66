Drop down from the Corniche Kennedy via the staircase and you're suddenly in the Vallon des Auffes — a tiny inlet of painted fishing boats and stone boathouses that somehow survived the twentieth century intact. The transition is abrupt enough to feel like a discovery every time.

In summer, there's a stage here at water level, with the sea directly behind it. The audience sits on stepped limestone terracing above. The programme runs to jazz, flamenco, classical music, theatre — things that work outdoors, against ambient sea noise and occasional wind.

Book ahead for July and August. The setting does not need much programming to make an impression.
