The Place de Lenche is believed to sit on or very near the Greek agora of Massalia — the central meeting place of the city founded around 600 BCE. Two and a half thousand years of public use, and now a small theatre where a fish market used to be.

The conversion kept the character of the market building: low ceilings, plain materials, close quarters. That suits the programming — contemporary creation, new writing, work in development. Small-scale productions fit naturally in a room where the relationship between performer and audience is this direct.

Le Panier surrounds it: Marseille's oldest quarter, first landing for successive waves of immigrants, now also home to artists and galleries. The theatre belongs to both versions of the neighbourhood.
