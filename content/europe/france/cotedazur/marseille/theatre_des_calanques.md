The calanques are the white limestone inlets that define Marseille's southern edge — the rock dropping straight into clear water, the national park that begins within city limits. It's the only national park in France that starts inside a major city, which tells you something about how unusual this geography is.

This theatre takes its name from that landscape and sits in the southern arrondissements closest to it. The programme varies by season and company, but the orientation is clear: this is a theatre that knows where it is, positioned relative to the coast rather than the city centre.

In summer, outdoor performances may feature. When they do, the southern Marseille climate makes them genuinely pleasurable.
