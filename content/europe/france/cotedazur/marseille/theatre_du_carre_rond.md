The name is the whole argument. A carré rond — a round square — is a geometric impossibility. That contradiction is exactly the point. This small theatre in the 5th arrondissement builds its entire identity around it: work that shouldn't fit together, forced to share the stage.

The programming leans into the oxymoron. Theatre bleeding into dance. Spoken word crashing into visual art. Comedy with a political edge. The French call it théâtre de recherche — research theatre, formal experiment — but the Carré Rond keeps it grounded enough in Marseillais life to avoid disappearing into abstraction.

And honestly, the name fits the city. Marseille is its own kind of contradiction: ancient and rawly contemporary, beautiful and broken, Mediterranean and stubbornly French. A theatre that makes a virtue of incompatible things is right at home here.
