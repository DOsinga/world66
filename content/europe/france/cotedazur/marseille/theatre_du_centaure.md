In Greek myth, centaurs were mostly trouble — violent, drunk, unruly. The exception was Chiron: the wise one, tutor to Achilles, the centaur who bridged instinct and intellect. This theatre in the southern 8th arrondissement holds both possibilities without resolving them.

Physical theatre, circus arts, performances where the body does the talking — that's the house style. The centaur is the right mascot for it: power coming from two natures that logic says can't combine.

Marseille was founded by Greeks who knew this territory well. Massalia was a colony living between worlds, always at one edge and the beginning of another. That's not a coincidence; it's the whole point.
