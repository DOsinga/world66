The Parc de Font Obscure is a hillside pocket of woodland in the northern quarters, where residents come to escape the heat. Inside it, a natural hollow was turned into an open-air amphitheatre: seating on a gentle slope, stage at the bottom, trees beyond.

Marseille's climate does most of the work here. Late spring through early autumn, evenings are warm and dry, and the park holds something indoor theatres can't manufacture — darkening sky, cicadas in the treeline, the occasional shimmer of an aircraft crossing overhead. The acoustics in the bowl are genuinely good.

Bring a jacket for later in the evening — the park holds cool air once the sun drops. Arrive early for seats on the upper terraces, where you get the full sweep of the stage and the trees behind it.
