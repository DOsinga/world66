The Joliette quarter was port infrastructure for most of its history: warehouses, freight terminals, maritime logistics. Then Euroméditerranée arrived — one of the biggest urban regeneration projects in France — and started replacing industrial uses with offices, housing, cultural venues, and public space.

The Joliette-Minoterie is part of that cultural layer. When a neighbourhood is still being built around you, a working theatre is one of the things that gives it a pulse. It's brought audiences into the Joliette through years when the surrounding streets were being rebuilt.

The programme mixes contemporary creation with touring international work. The Mediterranean position matters: the Joliette has always been a point of exchange, and the programming reflects that geography.
