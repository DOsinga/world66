Le Petit Merlan is a Scène Nationale in the 14th arrondissement, the northernmost and most economically deprived part of the city. Putting a nationally recognised theatre here was a deliberate act. The northern cités — large housing estates with almost no cultural infrastructure — had the same claim to serious programming as any other part of Marseille. The decision said so.

The programming shaped itself to the context. Circus, physical theatre, and contemporary dance sit alongside drama — forms that historically attract audiences without a prior relationship with literary culture. The outreach and youth programme has built a real reputation.

Merlan is the French word for whiting, the fish. An unassuming name for a quarter that rarely appeared on anyone's mental map of the city. The theatre has been working against that invisibility for years.
