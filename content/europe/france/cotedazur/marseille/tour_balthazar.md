The developers gave the towers going up in Euroméditerranée biblical names — Balthazar, one of the three Magi. A quietly grand gesture for buildings rising where cranes and warehouses used to stand.

Balthazar is part of the effort to drag Marseille's economic centre northward, from the Vieux-Port toward Joliette and Arenc. Apartments above, commercial space below — the plan is a real mixed neighbourhood, not another monocultural office district. The streets around it are still finding their footing: fresh plane trees growing alongside construction hoardings.

From Notre-Dame-de-la-Garde or the approaching ferry, Balthazar reads as one piece of a composition still being assembled. Up close, its glass upper floors catch the Mediterranean light and shift colour through the day.
