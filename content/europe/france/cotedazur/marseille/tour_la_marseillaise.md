The anthem was written in Marseille. The tower is named after it. That's the kind of symmetry the Euroméditerranée project goes for.

La Marseillaise rises 135 metres above the former port lands north of the centre — second tallest in the city after the CMA CGM Tower. Jean-Baptiste Lacoudre designed it, completed 2018. The blue-tinted glass curtain wall shifts colour through the day, reflecting the same sky Marseille's workers looked up at when this was still a working dock.

From street level on the esplanade below, the scale is startling. The tower dwarfs the nineteenth-century warehouses still standing nearby. Whether that's a triumph or a loss depends on who you ask — and in Marseille, people ask loudly.
