Marseille sprawls across hills and valleys, which means elevation matters in ways it doesn't in flat cities. Tour Panorama sits in the eastern arrondissements where the ground rises toward the limestone massifs — and from the upper floors you get what the name promises: west across the rooftops to the Frioul islands, east toward the wooded slopes of the Massif de l'Étoile.

It's a residential building, not a landmark tower in the Euroméditerranée mould. Its value is local — a vertical anchor in a neighbourhood where the terrain already does most of the visual work.

If you know a resident, get to the roof. It's one of the less-visited panoramic views in the city, and the less-visited ones are always the best.
