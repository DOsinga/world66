Rue Paradis got its name from the English merchants and consular staff who settled this part of Marseille between roughly 1700 and 1800. They called it Paradise. An honest assessment of the Mediterranean light and the relative quiet of the 8th arrondissement.

Traverse Paradis is one of the short connecting lanes that link the main north-south arteries through the neighbourhood. These traverses run between Rue Paradis, Boulevard Périer, Avenue du Prado — wide enough for foot traffic and occasionally one car, never designed for through-traffic. They appear on maps. They are almost never signed at street level.

Walk one and you get the 8th arrondissement properly: wide late-19th-century apartment buildings, plane trees, a neighbourhood that has stayed prosperous and stable for generations. The traverses are how the people who live here actually move — quick, quiet, away from the avenues. Unremarkable by design.
