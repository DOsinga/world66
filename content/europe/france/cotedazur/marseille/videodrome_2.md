When streaming arrived, Vidéodrome 2 did the opposite of closing. It leaned into what it was: a cultural institution, not a convenience store for films. The shelves hold thousands of titles — VHS, DVD, Blu-ray — with particular depth in world cinema, experimental film, and the cult and genre work that streaming catalogues tend to quietly bury.

It's in Cours Julien, Marseille's bohemian heartland. The library programmes screenings, director talks, events. Membership is affordable. The recommendations from the staff bear no resemblance to an algorithm's suggestions.

One of the last independent video libraries still operating in France. Walk in and browse. You'll find something you didn't know you needed to see.
