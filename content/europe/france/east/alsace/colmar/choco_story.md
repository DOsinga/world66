Choco Story is Colmar's chocolate museum, covering cacao growing, chocolate history, and the way chocolate is made. The visit uses an audio guide and interactive displays, with tastings that make it an easy family stop.

It is not Colmar's headline museum, but it works well as a lighter break between the old town, Unterlinden Museum, and the town's Christmas-season markets.
