---
title: "Getting There"
type: section
---

Mulhouse is served by EuroAirport Basel-Mulhouse-Freiburg, which sits just across the French-Swiss border and is shared by three countries. The airport has good connections to European cities and several international routes. From Paris, the TGV reaches Mulhouse in about two and a half hours. By road, the A35/A36 motorway links Mulhouse to [Colmar](/europe/france/east/alsace/colmar) (40 km), [Strasbourg](/europe/france/east/alsace/strasbourg) (100 km), and Basel (30 km). The central train station, Mulhouse-Ville, is well connected to both French and Swiss rail networks.
