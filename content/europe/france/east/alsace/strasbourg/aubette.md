The Aubette is an 18th-century building on Place Kléber whose main draw is its radical 1920s interior by Theo van Doesburg, Sophie Taeuber-Arp, and Jean Arp. The restored rooms are among the clearest surviving examples of De Stijl design in France, with bold geometric colour schemes that turn the former entertainment complex into a walk-through modernist artwork.
