Parc de l'Orangerie is Strasbourg's best-known city park, a formal green space northeast of the centre near the European institutions. It has broad lawns, tree-lined paths, a lake, playgrounds, and a small free zoo area with birds and other animals.

The park works well as a family stop or as a quiet break from the old town. It is also one of the easier places in Strasbourg to slow down for an hour without leaving the city.
