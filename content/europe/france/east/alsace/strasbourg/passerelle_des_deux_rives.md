Passerelle des Deux Rives is the pedestrian and cycle bridge linking Strasbourg in France with Kehl in Germany. Designed by Marc Mimram and opened in 2004, the cable-stayed bridge is both a practical border crossing and a piece of contemporary public architecture.

It is worth seeing as part of a Rhine-side walk or bike ride. The bridge gives open river views and a clear physical sense of Strasbourg's position on the French-German border.
