The Ponts Couverts are three bridges and four medieval towers at the edge of Strasbourg's Petite France quarter. The bridges lost their roofs in the 18th century, but the name stuck, and the towers still give this bend of the Ill the look of a fortified river gate.

Come for the view as much as the history. From here, the half-timbered houses, canals, Barrage Vauban, and old defensive works line up in one of Strasbourg's classic panoramas.
