Two Shores Garden is a cross-border park linking Strasbourg and Kehl across the Rhine. Its main landmark is the pedestrian bridge over the river, with lawns and paths on both banks.

It works well as an easy walk or bike ride when you want to step out of central Strasbourg and see the Rhine border up close.
