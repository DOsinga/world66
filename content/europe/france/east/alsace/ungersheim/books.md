---
title: "Books"
type: section
---

Reading around Alsace adds depth to a visit — this is a region whose history, architecture, and cuisine have been shaped by centuries of belonging alternately to France and Germany.

- **DK Eyewitness Alsace & Lorraine** — reliable visual guide with good coverage of the Écomusée and the major towns; useful for planning a wider trip through the region.
- **Michelin Green Guide Alsace Lorraine Champagne** — the classic route-based guide with strong detail on heritage sites, wine villages, and the Route des Vins.
- **Alsace: Traditions and Recipes** (various authors) — Alsatian cuisine and folk traditions are inseparable; a good food-culture book illuminates what the Écomusée's craftspeople and kitchens are demonstrating.
- **The Strasbourg Affair** or similar Alsace-set fiction — several thrillers and literary novels use Alsace's Franco-German complexity and border identity as backdrop, giving a feel for the region's layered character that a guidebook cannot.
