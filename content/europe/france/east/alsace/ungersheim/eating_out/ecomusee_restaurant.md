The Écomusée complex includes a traditional Alsatian restaurant and a winstub — the region's characteristic informal wine tavern — serving tarte flambée, choucroute garnie, and other regional dishes. Much of the produce comes from the museum's own farm, which adds an authenticity that matches the setting.

Given the limited options in the village itself, this is the practical choice for lunch or dinner on a museum visit day.
