---
title: "Getting There"
type: section
---

By car, Ungersheim is about 15 minutes from Mulhouse via the A35 and D430, and around 25 minutes from Colmar. By public transport, take a train to Mulhouse then a bus, or use the tram-train T3 toward Bollwiller followed by a taxi for the last stretch. Cycling is feasible via the Rhine cycle route and the flat trails of the Alsace plain.
