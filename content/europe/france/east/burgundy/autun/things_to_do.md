title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Autun's Roman and Romanesque heritage is concentrated and walkable. The [Cathedral of Saint-Lazare](/europe/france/east/burgundy/autun/cathedral_saint_lazare) and its extraordinary Gislebertus sculpture is the centrepiece; the [Musée Rolin](/europe/france/east/burgundy/autun/musee_rolin) next door has the original Eve from the cathedral plus fine Gallo-Roman artefacts. The two surviving Roman gates — [Porte Saint-André](/europe/france/east/burgundy/autun/porte_saint_andre) and [Porte d'Arroux](/europe/france/east/burgundy/autun/porte_darroux) — are a ten-minute walk apart at opposite ends of the old town. The vast [Roman theatre](/europe/france/east/burgundy/autun/roman_theatre) sits on the hillside above, and the [Temple of Janus](/europe/france/east/burgundy/autun/temple_of_janus) stands in a field at the edge of town. For the geologically curious, the [Pierre de Couhard](/europe/france/east/burgundy/autun/pierre_de_couhard) — a massive pyramidal funerary monument on the hillside — is one of the stranger Roman survivals in France.
