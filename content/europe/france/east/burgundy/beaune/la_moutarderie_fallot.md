La Moutarderie Fallot is Beaune's mustard stop: a family mustard house where Burgundy's sharpest condiment gets the kind of attention usually reserved for wine. The site on Rue du Faubourg Bretonnière combines production, a shop, tastings, and visitor routes that explain how mustard seed, vinegar, and local know-how became part of the region's food identity.

It is a useful break from cellars and churches because it stays practical. You see machinery, learn why Burgundy mustard matters, and leave with something easier to pack than a case of Pinot Noir. Book a visit route if you want more than the boutique and tasting counter.
