There is no shortage of places to eat well in Beaune, but Le Bistrot Bourguignon on the Rue Monge earns its place as the practical traveler's choice: reliably good, fairly priced, and deeply Burgundian in everything from the menu to the wine list. This is the kind of place that serves boeuf bourguignon that actually tastes like it was cooked for six hours, and oeufs en meurette (poached eggs in red wine sauce) that remind you why the dish became a classic.

The wine list focuses on Burgundy producers, many of them small négociants and domaines you won't find outside the region. The by-the-glass selection is generous, which matters in a town where you might want to try a village Pommard and a Meursault in the same sitting. Staff are used to travelers and are happy to make recommendations if you explain what you're looking for.

Book ahead for dinner, especially in summer and around the November auction period. Lunch is generally more accessible — the €18-22 formule is good value for the quality. Arrive hungry: portions are not small.
