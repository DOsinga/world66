---
title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Beaune is compact enough to cover on foot, but the surrounding vineyards reward a day or more by bike. The Hospices de Beaune anchors any itinerary; beyond it the town rewards slow wandering — down into cellars, along the ramparts, into the collegiate church. The wine is the reason almost everyone comes, and it is genuinely among the best in the world.
