---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

The most atmospheric time to visit Beaune is **September and October** during the harvest. The vineyards are alive with pickers, the cave entrances smell of fermenting juice, and every domaine is busy. It is not a time when producers welcome casual drop-ins, but the town itself is at its most energetic.

**November** brings the **Hospices de Beaune Wine Auction**, held on the third weekend of the month as part of the *Trois Glorieuses* festival. It is the world's most famous charity wine auction and a three-day celebration that draws buyers and visitors from around the world. Prices during this weekend are predictably eye-watering.

**July and August** are the peak tourist months — the town is busy, producers are doing cave tours, and the evenings are warm and long. The Beaune International Baroque Music Festival takes place in late July.

**Winter** (December to February) is quiet and cold; many smaller domaines close. But Beaune's restaurants are as good as ever, and the absence of tour groups makes it easier to have an actual conversation with a producer.
