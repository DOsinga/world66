---
title: Bars and Cafes
type: section
---

Dijon's nightlife concentrates in two pockets: the bars around Place Émile Zola and Rue Berbisey (student crowd, late hours) and the wine bars and cafés around Place de la Libération and Place François Rude (more sedate, better wine lists, earlier finish). The Burgundy wine bars are the city's signature drinking experience — Côte-d'Or producers by the glass, knowledgeable staff, no pretension.
