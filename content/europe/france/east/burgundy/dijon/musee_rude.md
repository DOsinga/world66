Housed in the disused transept of the 11th-century Église Saint-Étienne, the small Musée Rude displays plaster casts of the major works of François Rude (1784–1855), the Dijon sculptor whose best-known piece is the Départ des volontaires — the "Marseillaise" relief on the Arc de Triomphe in Paris. Casting from the originals at full scale gives a sense of the works that even a visit to Paris doesn't really provide.

The setting in the half-ruined Gothic transept is part of the appeal. Free admission. Allow 30 minutes. Closed Tuesdays.
