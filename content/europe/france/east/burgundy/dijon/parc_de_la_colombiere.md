A formal 17th-century park on the south edge of the old town, laid out in 1671 for the Prince of Condé. The original design — eight long straight allées radiating from a central rondpoint — is intact, with mature horse chestnuts forming a high green vault overhead in summer. The park is a popular weekend walk for Dijon families, with a small zoo for children, a playground, and an aviary.

About 15 minutes' walk south of the Palace of the Dukes, or one stop on the tram. Free; open daily.
