A canalside café next to the lock that opens when the boating season is in full swing, roughly April to October. The terrace catches the afternoon sun and is an excellent place to drink a cold beer and watch the canal boats work through the lock. Coffee and cold drinks in the morning; wine and local craft beer in the afternoon. Closed November through March.
