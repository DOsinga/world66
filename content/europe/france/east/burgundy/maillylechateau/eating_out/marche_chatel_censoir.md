The nearest proper market is at Châtel-Censoir, 7 km south along the canal. It runs on Saturday mornings and draws producers from across the Yonne valley: cheeses, charcuterie, honey, seasonal vegetables, and occasionally a stall selling local wines from the small Coulanges-la-Vineuse appellation. Worth the short drive or canal-boat ride if you are self-catering and want Burgundian produce rather than supermarket provisions.
