Down at the canalside, Le Port caters primarily to the boating crowd but welcomes walkers and cyclists without any fuss. The terrace overlooks the canal lock, and the menu leans towards grilled meats and river fish. The friture de Loire — small fried fish served with tartare sauce — is a regional speciality worth ordering if it is on the board. Open lunchtimes only outside the peak summer months.
