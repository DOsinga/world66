---
title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Mailly-le-Château rewards slow exploration. The upper village on its limestone bluff holds the medieval church, the terrace viewpoint, and the traces of old fortifications. Below, the Canal du Nivernais offers a completely different pace — flat towpaths, moored houseboats, and the quiet rhythm of the river. Walking and cycling are the main activities; there are no queues and no crowds.
