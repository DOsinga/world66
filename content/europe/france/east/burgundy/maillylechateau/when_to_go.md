---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

Mailly-le-Château has a continental climate with warm summers and cold winters. The village itself is open year-round, though most services operate only in the warmer months.

**Spring (April–May)** is the best time to visit. The canal traffic is building, the valley is green, and the limestone cliffs catch the long evening light. Wildflowers cover the hillside paths in May. Accommodation is easier to book and prices are lower than high summer.

**Summer (June–August)** is busy by local standards — the Canal du Nivernais attracts houseboat hire parties throughout the school holidays, and the canal-side café and restaurant fill up. The cliff terrace is spectacular at sunset. Temperatures can reach the low 30s Celsius; the cliff village catches any breeze that is going.

**Autumn (September–October)** brings the grape harvest across the wider Yonne region, and the hillside forests turn gold. There are far fewer visitors after mid-September. The market at Châtel-Censoir is well-stocked with seasonal produce. A very good time to be here.

**Winter (November–March)** is quiet. Most canal businesses close, and the village retreats into itself. The views can be dramatic on cold clear days, and there is an undeniable appeal to having the place entirely to yourself. Come expecting very limited services.

There are no major festivals in Mailly-le-Château itself, but the Auxerre area holds a number of food and wine events in autumn that are within easy reach.
