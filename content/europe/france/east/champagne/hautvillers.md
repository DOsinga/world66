**Hautvillers** is a small village in the Champagne vineyards above the Marne Valley, a few kilometres north of [Epernay](/europe/france/east/champagne/epernay). It was here, in the [Benedictine Abbey of Saint-Pierre](/europe/france/east/champagne/hautvillers/hautvillers_abbey), that the monk Dom Perignon is said to have perfected the techniques for making sparkling wine in the late 17th century. His tomb is in the abbey church and remains a pilgrimage site for wine lovers.

The village is beautiful — stone houses, [elaborate wrought-iron signs](/europe/france/east/champagne/hautvillers/village_signs) depicting the champagne trade, and views across the [vineyard-covered slopes](/europe/france/east/champagne/hautvillers/vineyard_walks) to the valley below. Several small champagne houses offer [direct tastings](/europe/france/east/champagne/hautvillers/champagne_tastings). It is an easy half-day trip from Epernay or [Reims](/europe/france/east/champagne/reims).
