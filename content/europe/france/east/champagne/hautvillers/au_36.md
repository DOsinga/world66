A small restaurant on the main street serving straightforward French lunches: salads, quiches, a plat du jour, and local cheese. Simple, honest, and reasonably priced. More café than restaurant in atmosphere but with hot food at midday. Useful if you want a light bite after visiting the abbey and walking the village, without committing to a full sit-down meal.
