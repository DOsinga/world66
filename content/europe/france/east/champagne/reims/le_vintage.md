Le Vintage is a champagne bar on the Place d'Erlon that specialises in grower-producer champagnes — those made by small independent vignerons rather than the great houses. The selection changes seasonally and covers the full spectrum from blanc de blancs to récoltant-manipulant rosés that you will rarely find outside the region.

It is the kind of place where the staff actually know the wines and will talk you through the differences between villages and grapes. Bar snacks are available, and the terrace on the square is ideal for watching Reims go about its evening. An essential stop for anyone who comes to the city for champagne and wants to go beyond the branded experience.
