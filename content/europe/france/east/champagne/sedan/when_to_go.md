---
title: When to Go
type: section
---

Sedan visits well year-round — the castle is the main attraction and it is open in all seasons, though hours may be reduced in winter. Summer (June–August) brings the best weather for walking the ramparts and exploring Bazeilles. Spring and autumn are quieter and perfectly pleasant in this part of the Ardennes.

The town is close to the Ardennes forests and the Meuse valley, which see some of the best autumn colour in northern France. If you are combining a trip to the Champagne region with the Ardennes, late September to mid-October offers good weather and the harvests are underway in the vineyards to the south.
