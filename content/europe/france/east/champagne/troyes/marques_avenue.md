Marques Avenue is the larger of Troyes' two outlet villages, with around 130 shops spread across two sites (Pont Sainte-Marie and Saint-Julien-les-Villas, a few kilometres apart on the southern edge of the city). French and international brands — Adidas, Calvin Klein, Lacoste, Le Creuset, Longchamp, Sandro — at 30–70% off retail. The selection leans toward French brands, which is the reason to come rather than going to a generic outlet mall. Open daily.
