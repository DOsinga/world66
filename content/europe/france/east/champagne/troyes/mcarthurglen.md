The McArthurGlen outlet in Troyes is one of the group's larger French centres, with around 100 shops in an open-air village format. The mix includes both French labels (Maje, Comptoir des Cotonniers, Repetto) and international ones (Hugo Boss, Tommy Hilfiger, Michael Kors). Discounts are typically 30–50%. Located at Pont Sainte-Marie, adjacent to the Marques Avenue site, which makes it practical to visit both in the same trip. Open daily; parking is free.
