title: "Shopping"

---

Troyes has an unlikely second identity as France's outlet shopping capital. The city was historically a centre of hosiery and textile production, and that legacy has turned into a cluster of large factory outlet villages on its outskirts. The two main centres are [Marques Avenue](/europe/france/east/champagne/troyes/marques_avenue) and the [McArthurGlen Designer Outlet](/europe/france/east/champagne/troyes/mcarthurglen), which together hold well over 200 shops. French and European brands — Lacoste, Longchamp, Sandro, Maje, Le Creuset — at discounts that are genuine rather than theatrical.

This is not a reason on its own to come to Troyes, but if you are visiting anyway and have any interest in French fashion or kitchenware, it is worth half a day. Both villages are about 3 km from the old town; a taxi or the shuttle bus from the city centre is the easiest way to get there.
