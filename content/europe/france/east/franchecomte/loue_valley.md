The Loue valley, in the Département of Doubs, is one of the most beautiful river valleys in France. The [source of the Loue](/europe/france/east/franchecomte/loue_valley/source_de_la_loue) is immensely dramatic — pools and waterfalls in a limestone cirque, where the river emerges in a rush from solid rock — and from there the river winds through a gorge that is a delight at almost every turn.

The village of [Ornans](/europe/france/east/franchecomte/loue_valley/ornans) is worth a visit: it has a museum dedicated to Gustave Courbet, who was born here and painted the valley repeatedly. The [Musée Courbet](/europe/france/east/franchecomte/loue_valley/musee_courbet) is the main cultural attraction in the valley and one of the best small art museums in eastern France. The [Château de Cléron](/europe/france/east/franchecomte/loue_valley/chateau_de_cleron), dramatically sited above the river between Ornans and the source, is one of the finest privately owned castles in the Franche-Comté.

The stretch from the source down to Ornans is the most spectacular, but the lower reaches are well worth continuing through. The Loue is also one of France's great fly-fishing rivers, drawing anglers from across Europe for its trout and grayling.
