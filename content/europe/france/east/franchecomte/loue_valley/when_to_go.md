---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

The Loue valley is most beautiful in spring (April to June), when the river is full, the meadows are green, and the wildflowers are out. Autumn (September and October) is also good, with the forest colours on the gorge walls changing from green to gold and rust. Summer is pleasant but the valley can be crowded in July and August, particularly around Ornans.

Fly fishing on the Loue is best from March to September. The river is one of the finest grayling and trout streams in France, and day permits can be obtained locally.
