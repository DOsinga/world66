The Château de Cons-la-Grandville stands on a rocky promontory above the village and the Chiers valley. Its appeal is the layering: medieval foundations, Renaissance and 18th-century rebuilding, a large barn, gardens, and the neighbouring Benedictine priory all folded into one estate.

It is privately owned and has been restored over recent decades, so access is not as straightforward as at a state-run monument. If you can join a guided visit or catch it during a heritage opening, it is one of the more characterful small château stops in northern Lorraine. Otherwise, the exterior and village setting still justify a short detour from Longwy.
