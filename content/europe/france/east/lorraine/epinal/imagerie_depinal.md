The Imagerie d'Epinal is the working heir to the city's famous print tradition. It is part workshop, part shop, part living archive, and it gives a more tactile sense of the craft than the museum alone.

Visits depend on opening hours and workshop schedules, so check before you cross town. Even a short stop is worthwhile if you like old presses, popular art, or the strange afterlife of images that once hung in kitchens and classrooms across France.
