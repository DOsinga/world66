Nancy is the natural base for anyone visiting Fléville. The city is only 10 km north and easily worth a full day: Place Stanislas, one of the finest 18th-century squares in Europe, is the obvious centrepiece, but the Art Nouveau architecture in the streets around it — Nancy was the home of the École de Nancy — is reason enough to linger.
