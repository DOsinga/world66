---
title: "Bars and Cafes"
type: section
---

The cafes and bars of Metz cluster on a few key squares: **Place Saint-Louis** and **Place de la Comédie** for terraces in the sun, **Place Saint-Jacques** for student crowds and live music, and the narrow streets of the **Outre-Seille** quarter for a quieter, more local feel. Bars typically open from late afternoon and stay busy until midnight; cafes serve through the day.

Lorraine has its own beer tradition (the Saint-Nicolas-de-Port brewery is nearby) and a strong line in cocktails based on local Mirabelle plum eau-de-vie. Most bars also carry good Alsace and Moselle wines.
