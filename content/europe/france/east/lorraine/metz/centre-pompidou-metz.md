A world-class contemporary art museum in Metz, satellite of the iconic Paris Pompidou Centre, housed in a stunning architectural building designed by Shigeru Ban. In summer 2026, it hosts major exhibitions including François Morellet retrospective and Marina Abramović's 'The Abramović Method'.
