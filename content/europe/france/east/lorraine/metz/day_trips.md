---
title: "Day Trips"
type: section
---

Metz is well placed for day trips to neighbouring countries and regions. **Luxembourg City** is just 40 minutes by train, a charming small capital with dramatic fortifications and one of the best modern art museums in Europe (the Mudam). **Nancy**, the other great city of Lorraine, is 40 minutes south by train — its Place Stanislas, a Rococo masterpiece, alone justifies the visit. **Verdun** and the WWI battlefields are about an hour west by car; the cemeteries and memorials of the Western Front make for a sobering day.

Closer to home, the **Fort de Queuleu**, on the eastern edge of Metz, was a Gestapo internment camp during WWII and is now a small museum.
