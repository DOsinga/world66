La Maison de Verlaine à Metz is a small museum devoted to the poet Paul Verlaine and his links with Lorraine, Metz, and Paris. Exhibits cover his life, writing, meeting with Arthur Rimbaud, and later reputation.

It is a focused stop for literary travelers, close enough to combine with Metz Cathedral, Place Saint-Louis, and the city's other central sights.
