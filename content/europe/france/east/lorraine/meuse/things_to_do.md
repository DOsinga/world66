title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Meuse is best understood through its layers of military memory. The battlefields around [Verdun](/europe/france/east/lorraine/verdun) — the **Ossuaire de Douaumont**, the **Fort de Vaux**, the **Mémorial de Verdun** museum — are the defining sites and easily fill a full day. Further south, the village of **Vauquois** is preserved as a shocking First World War mine-warfare site, while at **Saint-Mihiel** the American Monument and the surrounding US battlefield commemorates the 1918 offensive.

Beyond the war sites, the **Château de Hattonchâtel** is a romantic 13th-century hilltop château restored by an American philanthropist in the 1920s. **Lac de Madine**, in the south of the department, is a large reservoir popular for sailing and birdwatching. The **Voie Sacrée**, the road that kept Verdun supplied, runs the length of the department and is itself a route worth driving.
