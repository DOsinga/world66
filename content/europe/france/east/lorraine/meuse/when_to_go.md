---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

Lorraine has a continental climate with hot summers and cold winters. **May to September** are the most reliable months for outdoor visits to the battlefields, Lac de Madine and the country châteaux. **July and August** are the warmest but rarely uncomfortable; the region is far from crowded even in peak season.

**Late autumn** has a particular feel — fog, bare trees, low sun — that suits the war memorials. The two centenaries — 2014–2018 for the First World War battles and 11 November each year for Armistice Day — are the busiest moments at Verdun and Douaumont. **Winter** is cold, often grey, and many smaller sites close from mid-November to Easter; Verdun itself stays open year-round.
