Parc Sainte-Marie is a quieter green space southwest of central Nancy, useful when the more famous Pepiniere feels too busy. It has broad lawns, mature trees, flower beds, and enough shade to make it a good summer pause between Art Nouveau sights.

The park's most distinctive building is an Alsatian-style house, a reminder of the regional borderlands that shaped this part of France. Come for a stroll, a picnic, or a calmer hour away from the grand squares of the centre.
