Salle Poirel is one of Nancy’s useful cultural addresses, a concert and exhibition hall tucked near the station rather than on the showpiece Place Stanislas circuit. Built in the late 19th century, it reflects the city’s appetite for public culture during the same broad period that produced its Art Nouveau reputation.

The building matters most when something is on: chamber music, orchestral concerts, photography, design, or temporary art shows. It is an easy evening option if you are staying in the centre, and it broadens Nancy beyond the usual trio of square, cathedral, and École de Nancy museum.
