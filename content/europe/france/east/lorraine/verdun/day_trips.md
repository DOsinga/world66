---
title: "Day Trips"
type: section
---

**Metz** — 1h east by road or train. The Gothic cathedral has the largest expanse of stained glass of any cathedral in France; the Pompidou-Metz museum is a destination in itself.

**Reims** — 1h 30min west. The coronation cathedral of the kings of France and the cellars of the major Champagne houses.

**Luxembourg City** — 1h 15min. The casemates, the old town, the Mudam contemporary art museum.

**Argonne Forest sites** — west of Verdun, the Argonne was the focus of the American 1918 offensive. Montfaucon, Romagne, Vauquois and the Argonne Forest itself (still cratered) make a strong second battlefield day.

**Meuse TGV station to Paris** — sometimes considered as a day trip in reverse; with a 1h 15min TGV it is feasible to spend a morning in Paris and afternoon in Verdun, or vice versa.
