---
title: "Getting There"
type: section
---

**By rail.** TGV trains from Paris Gare de l'Est run to the Meuse TGV station about 25 km south of Verdun in around 1h 15min; a connecting bus or taxi covers the last leg. Local trains from Metz (1h 30min) and Nancy (2h with change) reach the central Verdun station.

**By road.** The A4 motorway from Paris to Strasbourg passes just south of Verdun and is the fastest road approach — 2h 30min from Paris, 1h 30min from Reims. The N3/D1916 from Bar-le-Duc is the historic Voie Sacrée and a more atmospheric way in.

**By air.** The nearest international airports are Luxembourg (1h 15min by road), Metz-Nancy (45 min), and Paris-Charles de Gaulle (3h). For battlefield touring most visitors arrive by car.
