---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

The battlefield sites are open year-round but several — Fort de Douaumont, Fort de Vaux, the Mémorial — have shorter winter hours.

**April to October** is the standard season. May and September are the sweet spot: long days, mild temperatures, low crowds. July and August are warmest but also busiest, particularly with French and German school groups; the Mémorial can be uncomfortably full.

The **annual commemoration** falls on 21 February (the start of the battle in 1916). The largest events are in centenary years. November is the other peak — for Armistice Day on the 11th, the Ossuary and the cemeteries become focal points for national observance.

Winter is bleak but powerful: bare trees, low light, often a dusting of snow on the shell craters. The Mémorial stays open year-round; many smaller sites close.
