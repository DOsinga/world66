Théâtre André Malraux is Rueil-Malmaison's main performing arts venue, staging theatre, music,
dance, comedy, and family shows. It gives the suburb a cultural anchor beyond its Napoleonic
associations and elegant residential streets.

The theatre is worth considering if you are staying west of Paris or spending an evening in Rueil-
Malmaison after visiting Malmaison-related sights. As with any performance venue, check the
programme first; the building matters most when something good is on.
