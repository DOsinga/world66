---
title: "Getting There"
type: section
---

Alès has a railway station with regional trains to Nimes and Clermont-Ferrand via the Cévennes line. By road, it sits north of Nimes and west of the Rhone valley, making it a practical approach to Anduze, Florac, and the southern Cévennes. Buses fill some gaps, but a car helps for villages and trailheads.
