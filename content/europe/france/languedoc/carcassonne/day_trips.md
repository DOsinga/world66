title: Day Trips
type: section
---

Carcassonne is a good base for the Cathar country. The string of clifftop castles south of the city — Lastours, Termes, Quéribus, Peyrepertuse — were the final strongholds of southern French dualist Christianity in the thirteenth century, and most are within an hour and a half by car. The wine villages of the Corbières and Minervois, the abbeys of Lagrasse and Fontfroide, and the Pyrenean foothills around Limoux all reward an outing.

**Lastours**, the closest, has four ruined towers strung along a single ridge thirty minutes north. **Quéribus** and **Peyrepertuse**, the two most spectacular, are clifftop eagle nests in the southern Corbières, both about ninety minutes' drive. **Toulouse** is an hour west by train and worth a day on its own.
