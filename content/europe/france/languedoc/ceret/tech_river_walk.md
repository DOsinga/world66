The Tech River gives Ceret its lower, greener edge. Paths near the Pont du Diable let you trade the old streets for water, trees, and views back toward the bridge.

It is a modest walk, not a wilderness hike, but it rounds out a visit nicely. After the museum and market, the river makes the town feel less like an art stop and more like a foothill base.
