---
title: Eating Out
type: section
---

Eating out in St-Pons is a low-key affair — the town is small and the restaurant scene is modest, but the ingredients passing through the Saturday market tell you that the raw materials are excellent. A handful of restaurants serve honest Languedoc cooking with a focus on local charcuterie, grilled meats, and the cheeses from the surrounding hills. Book ahead in summer as the better places fill up.
