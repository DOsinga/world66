The Lac de la Raviège, about 20 minutes north of St-Pons on the road toward La Salvetat, is the largest lake in the Haut-Languedoc park and the most accessible swimming destination in the area. A beach area with lifeguards operates in July and August; outside these months you can walk the shoreline through forest and find quiet spots to swim unattended.

Sailing and kayak rental are available from the base nautique at La Salvetat-sur-Agout, the small town at the northern end of the lake. The road around the lake passes through chestnut forest and gives occasional wide views over the water. In autumn the reflections of the turning leaves in the still lake can be very fine.
