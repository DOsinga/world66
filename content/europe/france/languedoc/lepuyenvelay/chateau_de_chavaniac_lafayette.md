The Château de Chavaniac-Lafayette is the birthplace of Gilbert du Motier, the Marquis de Lafayette, and it leans fully into that Franco-American story. The house, rooms, portraits, and displays trace the life of a local aristocrat who became a hero of the American Revolution and a complicated figure in France's own upheavals.

It sits in parkland away from Le Puy-en-Velay, so it is a deliberate excursion rather than a casual town sight. The château works best if you are interested in the 18th century, revolutionary politics, or the odd cultural bridge between rural Auvergne and the United States.
