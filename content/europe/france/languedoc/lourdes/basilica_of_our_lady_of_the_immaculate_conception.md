The Basilica of Our Lady of the Immaculate Conception rises above the grotto at the Sanctuary of Lourdes, its 70 m spire making it one of the site's clearest landmarks. Look for the mosaic of Pope Pius IX above the entrance and listen for the clock, which plays the Ave Maria on the hour.
