The Wax Museum tells the story of Saint Bernadette and the religious history of Lourdes through life-size wax figures. It is old-fashioned, but it can help visitors put the sanctuary's devotional story into a simple visual sequence.

Treat it as a short indoor stop, especially in bad weather or when travelling with children who need a break from churches and processions.
