Montpellier Zoo is one of the city's easiest family escapes, a large zoological park in the northern green belt. The setting is spacious for an urban zoo, with walking routes, animal areas, and a more relaxed pace than the central squares.

It is especially useful because it can fill a half day without feeling like a purely commercial attraction. Check access, opening hours, and any paid sections before heading out from the centre.
