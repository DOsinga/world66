Prades is a small market town in the Têt Valley, about 45 kilometres west of Perpignan in the Pyrénées-Orientales. It has a compact, walkable centre dominated by a magnificent [Baroque church](/europe/france/languedoc/prades/eglise_saint_pierre), a weekly market, and an unhurried village feel that belies its status as the main town of the valley.

Its location makes it a practical base: the ski resorts of Les Angles and Font Romeu are around 50 minutes away, as are the sandy beaches of the Mediterranean coast at Canet Plage and Argelès-sur-Mer. River swimming, a public pool, and a large lake with swimming and dinghy sailing are all within easy reach. [Vernet-les-Bains](/europe/france/languedoc/prades/vernet_les_bains) is a short drive up the valley, the starting point for walks up towards [Canigou](/europe/france/languedoc/prades/canigou_massif).

The [Abbey of Saint-Michel-de-Cuxa](/europe/france/languedoc/prades/abbey_saint_michel_de_cuxa), just outside town, is one of the finest Romanesque buildings in the Pyrenees. Prades is also known for the [Pablo Casals Festival](/europe/france/languedoc/prades/pablo_casals_festival), a chamber music festival founded by the cellist who lived here in exile after fleeing Franco's Spain.
