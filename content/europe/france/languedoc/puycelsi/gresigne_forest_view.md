type: poi

The viewpoints on Puycelsi's western edge look over the Grésigne forest, one of the great wooded landscapes of the Tarn. The forest is the backdrop that makes the village feel remote, even though the drive from Gaillac or Albi is straightforward.

If you have more time, use Puycelsi as a starting point for local walking routes into the surrounding country.
