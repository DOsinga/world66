Domaine Serres-Mazard is one of the easiest local cellar-door stops in Talairan, useful for tasting the robust red wines that define the Corbières. The attraction is simple: you are tasting wine in the landscape that made it.

Check current opening times before turning up, especially outside summer. In this part of the Aude, small producers are worth visiting, but they do not always keep city-shop hours.
