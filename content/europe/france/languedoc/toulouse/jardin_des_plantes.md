Toulouse's Jardin des Plantes is a central green space with old trees, lawns, bridges, and easy links to the museum quarter. It offers a calmer rhythm than the dense streets around the cathedral and Carmes.

The garden is not a botanical destination on the scale of Paris, but it is a useful pause during a day of walking. Families, sketchers, and anyone needing shade in warm weather will get the most from it.
