Les Abattoirs is Toulouse's major modern and contemporary art museum, housed in a former
slaughterhouse on the left bank of the Garonne. The building's reused industrial scale
gives the galleries a strong identity and room for ambitious exhibitions.

It is one of the best cultural stops in Toulouse for visitors who want the city beyond
brick churches, aerospace, and cafes. Pair it with a walk along the river or through
Saint-Cyprien.
