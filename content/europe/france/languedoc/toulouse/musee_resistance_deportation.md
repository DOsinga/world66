The Musee departemental de la Resistance et de la Deportation tells the wartime history of Toulouse
and Haute-Garonne through resistance networks, repression, deportation, and liberation. It is a
serious history museum, grounded in local experience rather than abstract national narrative.

For visitors, it adds necessary context to a city often approached through brick architecture,
aerospace, and southern French street life. Toulouse was also a place of clandestine routes,
occupation pressures, and postwar memory, and the museum makes that history visible through
documents, objects, and testimony.
