---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

**May and June** are the best months — the Loire Valley is green, the weather is warm without being oppressive, and the crowds are manageable. The gardens at Clos Lucé and Chanteloup are at their best.

**July and August** are the peak season. The chateau gets busy by midmorning; arrive at opening (9 am) or after 5 pm when day-trippers from Tours have gone. The Son et Lumière (sound and light show) at the chateau runs on summer evenings — good in warm weather, excellent in a thunderstorm.

**September and October** offer warm days, thinner crowds, and Loire wine harvest. The vendanges (grape harvest) along the Vouvray and Montlouis appellations east and west of Amboise make this a particularly good time for wine enthusiasts.

**Winter (November–March)** is quiet but the chateau and Clos Lucé stay open year-round. Amboise at Christmas has a small market; the candlelit evening tours at the chateau in December are genuinely atmospheric.
