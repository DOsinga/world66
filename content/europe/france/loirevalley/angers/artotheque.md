The Artotheque is Angers' art library, devoted to contemporary work and the lending of original artworks. For visitors, the reason to stop is its changing programme of exhibitions and art-related events.

It is a compact addition to Angers' stronger museum circuit, especially if you are already near the Musee des Beaux-Arts and want to see a more current side of the local art scene.
