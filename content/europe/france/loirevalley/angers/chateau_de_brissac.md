Château de Brissac is often described as the tallest château in France, and its high facade makes an immediate impression after the softer countryside south of Angers. The building mixes medieval bones with later Renaissance and Baroque ambition, the kind of layered aristocratic architecture the Loire does so well.

It is a proper day-trip château rather than an Angers city sight, but it belongs in Angers coverage because the town is the practical base. Interiors, parkland, and family history make the visit more than a photo stop. Wine-country roads nearby turn it into an easy half-day excursion.
