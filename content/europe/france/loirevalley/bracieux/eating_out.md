---
title: "Eating Out"
type: section
---

Bracieux is small but has a long tradition of serious cooking — for decades the village was on the map of French gastronomes thanks to the Michelin-starred Relais de Bracieux, run by Bernard Robin. The Relais has changed hands more than once, but Bracieux's reputation for good Sologne cooking persists, and a handful of restaurants and small hotels keep it alive.
