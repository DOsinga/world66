Montsoreau is a small Loire village with an enviable address: right where the Vienne meets the Loire, between [Saumur](/europe/france/loirevalley/saumur) and [Chinon](/europe/france/loirevalley/chinon). It is one of the Loire's prettiest low-key stops, all pale tufa stone, river light, and lanes that climb gently above the water.

The main reason to come is the [Château de Montsoreau](/europe/france/loirevalley/montsoreau/chateau_de_montsoreau), built unusually close to the river and now used as a contemporary art museum. The juxtaposition works better than it sounds: old defensive stone, wide Loire views, and conceptual art in the same visit.

Montsoreau is best paired with [Fontevraud](/europe/france/loirevalley/fontevraud), only a short drive away, or with a slow day along the Loire à Vélo. You do not need a full day unless you are lingering over wine and river walks, but it is too handsome to treat as just a signpost between bigger châteaux.
