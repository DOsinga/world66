St. Aignan is a small town in the Loir-et-Cher department, sitting on the south bank of the Cher river in the heart of the Loire Valley. The old town climbs the hill above the river in a series of narrow lanes lined with Renaissance and medieval houses, giving it a more authentic feel than the larger chateau towns nearby.

The [Collégiale Saint-Aignan](/europe/france/loirevalley/staignan/collegiale_saint_aignan), partly Romanesque and partly Gothic, dominates the upper town and is worth visiting for its crypt containing some of the finest 11th-century frescoes in the Loire Valley. The [Château de Saint-Aignan](/europe/france/loirevalley/staignan/chateau_de_saint_aignan) above the church is privately occupied and not open to visitors, but the terrace outside its gates offers an excellent view over the Cher valley and the rooftops of the old town.

St. Aignan is a comfortable base for exploring southern Touraine. The [ZooParc de Beauval](/europe/france/loirevalley/staignan/zooparc_de_beauval), one of France's most celebrated zoos and home to giant pandas, is just 4 km south. Vineyards producing Touraine wines surround the town.
