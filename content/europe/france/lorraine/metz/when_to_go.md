---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

Metz has a continental climate, cold and grey in winter, warm and lively in summer. **Late spring through early autumn** (May to September) is the best time to visit, with long evenings on the terraces of Place Saint-Louis and outdoor festivals throughout the summer.

The two events worth timing a visit around are the **Marché de Noël** (late November to late December), one of the best Christmas markets in France, when the centre fills with wooden chalets and lights, and **Mirabelles de Metz** (mid-August to mid-September), a citywide festival celebrating the local plum with parades, tastings, and concerts.
