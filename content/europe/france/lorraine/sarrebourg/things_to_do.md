---
title: Things to Do
type: section
---

The Chagall chapel is the reason most people come, and it deserves the attention it gets. The rest of Sarrebourg rewards a half-day of wandering: the Musée du Pays de Sarrebourg holds good Gallo-Roman archaeological material and regional decorative arts, and the Parcours Chagall links the chapel, the museum, and a garden installation in a coherent walking route through the town centre.

The countryside around Sarrebourg is good cycling territory: the Moselle valley is flat and pleasant, and the route into the Vosges foothills to the east is manageable for most riders. The Canal des Houillères de la Sarre runs through the area and has a towpath suitable for bikes. Saint-Quirin, a pretty village classified as one of the most beautiful in France, is about 20km southeast and worth the drive.
