---
title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

The ceramics museum is the main reason to come, and it is genuinely excellent. Beyond that, the pleasures of Sarreguemines are the quiet kind: walking the riverside paths along the Sarre, crossing the bridge into Germany for a coffee in Kleinblittersdorf, poking through the antique shops for original faience pieces. A half-day or full day is enough for most visitors.
