---
title: "Getting There"
type: section
---

**By car**

The easiest way. Uzès is 25km north of [Nîmes](/europe/france/midi/languedoc/nimes) via the D979, a straightforward 30-minute drive. From [Avignon](/europe/france/provence/avignon) it is about 45 minutes east on the D981. Parking in town is tight in summer; there are pay car parks at the edge of the old centre.

**By bus**

Edgard Transport runs regular buses between Nîmes and Uzès (line C17), with the journey taking around 40–50 minutes. Buses run throughout the day on weekdays, with reduced frequency on weekends and public holidays. The Nîmes bus and train stations are adjacent, making the connection straightforward if you are arriving by rail from elsewhere in France. Check edgard.transports.gard.fr for current timetables.

**By train**

There is no railway station in Uzès — the line was closed decades ago and is not coming back. The nearest mainline station is Nîmes, which has frequent TGV connections to Paris (about 2h 45min), Lyon (1h 10min), and Marseille (30min). From Nîmes, take the bus as above.
