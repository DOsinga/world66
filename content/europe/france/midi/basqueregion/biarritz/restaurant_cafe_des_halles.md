A reliable brasserie adjacent to the market, the kind of place where local traders and fishermen eat alongside tourists without anyone particularly minding. The menu follows Basque coastal logic: grilled fish (usually line-caught), squid in its own ink, mussels from the Arcachon basin, and a decent selection of local wines. Lunch is a better bet than dinner for freshness and atmosphere. Prices are moderate.
