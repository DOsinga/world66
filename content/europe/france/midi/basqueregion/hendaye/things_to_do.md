type: section
---

Hendaye's [beach](/europe/france/midi/basqueregion/hendaye/grande_plage) is the main draw — three kilometres of sand at the mouth of the Bidasoa estuary. For something more unusual, [Château d'Abbadia](/europe/france/midi/basqueregion/hendaye/chateau_dabbadia) sits on the wild headland south of the beach, and [Pheasant Island](/europe/france/midi/basqueregion/hendaye/pheasant_island) in the river is one of the strangest pieces of territory in Europe. The enigmatic [Great Cross of Hendaye](/europe/france/midi/basqueregion/hendaye/great_cross_of_hendaye) in the town square has generated centuries of speculation about its hidden symbolism.
