---
title: "Eating Out"
type: section
---

The food in Saint-Jean-de-Luz is genuinely excellent — fresh Atlantic fish, Basque classics like piperade (eggs with peppers and tomatoes) and axoa (spiced veal with Espelette pepper), and patisseries making gâteau Basque, the almond-cream filled pastry that is the region's signature sweet.

**Chez Pablo** on the harbour quay is one of the best fish restaurants in town — grilled tuna, chipirones (small squid), and merlu koskera (hake in Basque sauce) at honest prices. **Grillerie du Port** is another solid choice for fresh-off-the-boat fish simply cooked. For Basque country cooking in a more formal setting, **Restaurant Ithurria** in nearby Ainhoa (20 minutes by car) is considered one of the finest tables in the Basque country.

For patisserie and gâteau Basque specifically, **Pâtisserie Maison Adam** on Rue Gambetta has been making the cake since 1660 and claims to have invented it — whether or not that's true, it is very good.
