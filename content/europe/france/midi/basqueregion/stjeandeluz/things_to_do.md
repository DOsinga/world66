title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Saint-Jean-de-Luz rewards slow exploration. Start with the [Église Saint-Jean-Baptiste](/europe/france/midi/basqueregion/stjeandeluz/eglise_saint_jean_baptiste) — France's finest Basque church and the venue for the 1660 royal wedding. The [Place Louis XIV](/europe/france/midi/basqueregion/stjeandeluz/place_louis_xiv) is the social heart of town, good for coffee at any hour. Walk the port to watch the fishing boats unload, then find the [Beach of Saint-Jean-de-Luz](/europe/france/midi/basqueregion/stjeandeluz/beach) — one of the best protected beaches on the Atlantic coast. Day trips to [Hondarribia](/europe/france/midi/basqueregion/stjeandeluz/hondarribia_day_trip) across the Spanish border (5 km) or the mountain village of [Sare](/europe/france/midi/basqueregion/stjeandeluz/sare_day_trip) are easy and worthwhile.
