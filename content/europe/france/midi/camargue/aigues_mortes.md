Aigues-Mortes is the great stone exception to the Camargue's watery flatness. Its square medieval walls and towers remain almost completely intact, enclosing a small grid of streets, shops, cafes, and sun-baked squares.

Walk the ramparts if you only do one thing. From above, the view takes in the salt pans, canals, and marshland, and makes clear why this apparently inland town was once a royal Mediterranean port.
