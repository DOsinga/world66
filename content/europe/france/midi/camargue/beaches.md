---
title: "Beaches"
type: section
---

Camargue beaches are long, sandy, and often exposed to wind. Saintes-Maries-de-la-Mer has the easiest town beaches, good for a swim without logistics. Piemanson and Beauduc feel wilder and emptier, but access can change with conservation rules, weather, and road conditions. Bring water, shade, and patience.
