Beauduc is a windswept beach and lagoon area loved by kitesurfers and people who like their coast rough around the edges. It is not the place for polished promenades or beach clubs.

Road conditions and access rules can be awkward, especially after storms. If you make the trip, treat it as a small expedition: fuel, water, sun cover, and a willingness to turn back if the track looks wrong.
