The Camargue is flat, has a network of quiet roads and tracks, and rewards slow travel — which makes it ideal for cycling. Bicycles can be rented in Saintes-Maries-de-la-Mer and Arles. The classic route from Saintes-Maries north to Pont de Gau and on to the Étang de Vaccarès covers the best of the reserve in a day.

The Digue à la Mer, the coastal embankment, is the highlight — a raised path above sea level running east along the coast with the Mediterranean close on one side and the shallow lagoons on the other. Flamingos and avocets feed beside the path, and the light in the morning is extraordinary. The route from Saintes-Maries to the mouth of the Grand Rhône is about 40 kilometres one way — manageable in a day on a reasonably fast bicycle, with time to stop and watch birds. The midsummer wind (the mistral) can be strong; riding with it in the morning and returning in the afternoon is the sensible strategy.
