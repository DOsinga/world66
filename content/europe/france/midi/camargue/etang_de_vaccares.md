The Etang de Vaccares is the broad central lagoon of the Camargue, a sheet of brackish water that gives the delta its open, mirage-like feel. Much of the area is protected, so the experience is less about entering the landscape than watching it from roads, hides, and edges.

Birdlife changes with season and water level. Even when the birds are distant, the lagoon is worth seeing for scale: sky, reeds, salt water, and a horizon so low it feels almost Dutch.
