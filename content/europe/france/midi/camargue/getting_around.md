---
title: "Getting Around"
type: section
---

The Camargue is flat, so cycling is tempting and often excellent, especially on the quieter tracks south of Saintes-Maries-de-la-Mer. Distances, wind, heat, and mosquitoes are the catch. For bird reserves, beaches, and salt pans spread across the delta, a car gives far more flexibility. Do not rely on taxis being available in remote spots.
