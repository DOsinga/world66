---
title: "Getting There"
type: section
---

The Camargue is most easily reached from [Arles](/europe/france/midi/provence/arles), which has a train station with frequent connections to Marseille, Avignon, and Nîmes. From Arles, you need a car, bicycle, or a tour to reach the interior of the reserve — there is no public transport into the park itself.

Saintes-Maries-de-la-Mer, the main town in the southern Camargue, has seasonal bus service from Arles (the line 20 bus runs several times daily in summer, less frequently in winter). By car from Arles, Saintes-Maries is 38 kilometres and takes about 40 minutes on the D570.

The nearest airports are Marseille-Provence (70 km) and Nîmes-Alès-Camargue (55 km). Montpellier airport is also viable at about 75 kilometres.
