---
title: "Getting There"
type: section
---

Arles is the main rail gateway for the Camargue, with trains from Avignon, Marseille, Nimes, and Montpellier. From Arles, buses run to Saintes-Maries-de-la-Mer, but service is limited outside the main season. A car is the easiest way to reach the reserves, salt pans, beaches, and Aigues-Mortes in one trip.
