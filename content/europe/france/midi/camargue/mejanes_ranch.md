The Domaine de Méjanes occupies a large estate on the western shore of the Étang de Vaccarès, accessible by road from Albaron or Arles. It functions as a working ranch with Camargue horses and bulls, an equestrian centre, and a small tourist operation including horse rides, a miniature railway, and occasional bullfighting demonstrations in the arena.

The setting is excellent — the estate fronts directly on the lagoon, and the views across Vaccarès are among the best in the reserve. The miniature railway makes a circuit of the property through marshland and past the water's edge, which is particularly good for families. Evening events during summer often feature Course Camarguaise and traditional Camarguais music. The Méjanes circuit is also the start and end point for several cycling routes into the interior of the reserve.
