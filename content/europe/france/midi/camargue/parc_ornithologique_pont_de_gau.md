The ornithological park at Pont de Gau is the easiest way to see the Camargue's birds at close range. Set on the edge of the lagoons north of Saintes-Maries-de-la-Mer, it covers 60 hectares of marshland with a network of paths and observation hides. The park is not a zoo — the birds are wild, or semi-wild residents of the wetlands, not caged. Flamingos, herons, egrets, purple herons, spoonbills, and dozens of wader species are regularly visible.

The park is particularly good for families and non-specialist visitors who want a guaranteed wildlife experience without the effort of navigating the broader reserve. The paths are wheelchair accessible. Combined with a morning at the Étang de Vaccarès and an afternoon in Saintes-Maries, it makes for a satisfying full day. The best time to visit is early morning or late afternoon, when the light is best and the birds most active.
