Saintes-Maries-de-la-Mer is the Camargue's seaside capital: a low white town of beaches, seafood restaurants, riding stables, and summer crowds. Its fortified church rises above the roofs and gives a fine view across the town, lagoons, and Mediterranean.

The town can feel touristy in high season, but it is still the most practical base in the delta if you want beaches, boat trips, birding, and horse rides without long drives every day.
