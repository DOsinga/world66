---
title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

The Camargue is for slow outdoor travel: birdwatching, cycling, riding, beach walks, and patient drives across flat salt country. Use Saintes-Maries-de-la-Mer or Arles as a base, and build your day around light and wind. Flamingos are easiest at Pont de Gau, wide empty views open around Vaccares and La Gacholle, and Aigues-Mortes gives you the region's cleanest dose of stone history.
