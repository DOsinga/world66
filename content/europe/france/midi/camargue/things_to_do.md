---
title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

The Camargue rewards those who move slowly. The core experience is wildlife watching — flamingos, wild horses, black bulls, and an extraordinary variety of birds. Cycling is the best way to explore; the flat terrain and dedicated bike paths let you reach areas closed to cars. Horseback riding with a local gardian offers a different perspective on the wetlands. The main visitor infrastructure is concentrated around Saintes-Maries-de-la-Mer and Pont de Gau.
