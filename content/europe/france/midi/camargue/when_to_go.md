---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

Spring (April to June) is the best time to visit the Camargue. The flamingos are nesting, migratory birds pass through in large numbers, the vegetation is green, and the temperatures are warm without being brutal. The light is particularly good in May.

Summer is crowded and hot. Saintes-Maries-de-la-Mer fills with beach holidaymakers in July and August, and the mosquitoes — which breed prolifically in the wetlands — are a serious nuisance. If you visit in summer, bring strong repellent and long sleeves for evening.

Autumn (September to November) is quieter and still good for birds, with the added colour of the harvest season — salt in the pans, rice in the paddies. The mistral wind, which blows cold from the northwest, can arrive at any time from autumn onward and makes cycling uncomfortable but clears the air dramatically.

Winter is quiet and austere, with some birds staying on but far fewer species. The Camargue in January has a wild, empty quality that appeals to some travelers, but facilities in Saintes-Maries are limited.
