---
title: "Getting There"
type: section
---

Antibes sits on the main coastal train line, so trains are the easy answer. Nice-Côte d'Azur airport is twenty minutes by train; Nice city centre is half an hour, Cannes ten minutes, Monaco about fifty. Trains run every fifteen to thirty minutes during the day. The station is a five-minute walk from the old town.

By car, the A8 motorway has two exits — Antibes for the town centre and Antibes-Nord for Sophia Antipolis. Parking inside the old walls is impossible in summer; use the underground car parks at Place de Gaulle or Port Vauban and walk. From Nice airport, taxis are about €60 fixed fare.

The local bus network (Envibus) connects Antibes with Juan-les-Pins, Biot, Vallauris and Sophia Antipolis. A one-way ticket is €1; the lines run roughly every fifteen minutes in the daytime.
