Lilian Bonnefoi was the pastry chef at the Eden Roc for two decades and was several times voted the best pastry chef in France. His own shop, on the road around Cap d'Antibes, is a working bakery rather than a dining room — counter, fridge, espresso machine and a queue.

The Tarte Bonnefoi (a multi-layered chocolate slice) is the signature; the lemon tart is exceptional, and the croissants are some of the best on this stretch of coast. Open from breakfast through the afternoon. Go early — many days the best cakes are gone by mid-afternoon.
