---
title: "Bars and Cafes"
type: section
---

The **port** area is the centre of Argelès-sur-Mer's drinking scene, with terrace bars overlooking the boats. In summer, the beachfront bars along **Argelès Plage** stay open late and the whole strip comes alive after dark. The old village is much quieter — a few cafes on the square but little nightlife beyond that.
