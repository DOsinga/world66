---
title: "Eating Out"
type: section
---

Argelès-sur-Mer's food leans Catalan, with plenty of seafood and Mediterranean cooking. The **port** has the best waterfront restaurants; the old village offers quieter spots with more local character; Spanish influence shows up in the tapas, the anchovies and the use of olive oil. The weekly market on Place de la République (Saturdays) is the place to buy local **anchois de Collioure**, mountain hams and Roussillon wines.
