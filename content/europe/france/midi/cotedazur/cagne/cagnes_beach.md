The long pebble beach of Cagnes-sur-Mer, running for several kilometres along the Cros-de-Cagnes seafront and continuing east toward Saint-Laurent-du-Var. The beach is mostly public with a few private concessions near the centre, parts have shower and toilet facilities, and the water is generally clean — sometimes blue-flag rated.

It is not the prettiest beach on the Côte d'Azur — the stones are large and uncomfortable on bare feet, and the Nice airport flight path passes overhead — but it is uncrowded by Riviera standards, easy to reach by bus, and the long promenade is good for an evening walk with a view of the Cap d'Antibes.
