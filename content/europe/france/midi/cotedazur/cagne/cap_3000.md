A large modern shopping mall on the seafront at Saint-Laurent-du-Var, just east of Cagnes — one of the biggest on the French Riviera. **Cap 3000** has the usual French chain stores (Galeries Lafayette, FNAC, Sephora, Zara) plus a large food court and several mid-range restaurants with sea views.

It is useful rather than charming. The reason to mention it: if you need a wet-weather afternoon, a missing piece of luggage, or French pharmacy items at predictable prices, Cap 3000 covers most of it. Easy to reach from Cagnes by bus 200 or by train (Saint-Laurent-du-Var station, then a short walk).
