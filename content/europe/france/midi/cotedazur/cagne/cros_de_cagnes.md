The fishing port and beach district of Cagnes-sur-Mer, between the town centre and the airport. **Cros-de-Cagnes** is a working *port de pêche* — small wooden boats drawn up on the pebble beach, nets hung out to dry, a few weather-beaten huts selling the morning's catch. It is the only fishing village left on this stretch of coast.

Around the port a small cluster of seafood restaurants serves grilled bream, sea bass, and *bouillabaisse* — the cooking is plain and the prices are honest. The long pebble beach stretches west toward Villeneuve-Loubet and east toward Nice; in summer it is busy, in winter it is empty. The bayfront promenade is good for an evening walk.
