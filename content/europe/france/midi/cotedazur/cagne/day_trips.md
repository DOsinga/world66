Cagnes-sur-Mer is the most central base on the western Riviera — every interesting town between Cannes and Monaco is on the local train line. **Nice** is 10 minutes east for the markets of Vieux-Nice and the museums; **Antibes** is 8 minutes west for the harbour, ramparts, and Musée Picasso; **Cannes** is 25 minutes further for the Croisette and the islands of Sainte-Marguerite. Inland, the hilltop villages of **Saint-Paul-de-Vence** (with the Fondation Maeght) and **Vence** (with Matisse's Chapelle du Rosaire) are short bus rides from the Cagnes station.
