---
title: Eating Out
type: section
---

The best dining in Cagnes is up in **Haut-de-Cagnes**, where a handful of small restaurants — often run by chefs who have escaped Nice or Cannes — line the cobbled streets around the château. The cuisine is solid Niçoise: socca, daube, ratatouille, *pissaladière*, the salt-cod *brandade*, and a lot of grilled fish from the local catch landed at the port of Cros-de-Cagnes.

Down on the coast, the **Boulevard de la Plage** has the inevitable strip of beachfront restaurants — variable, mostly aimed at summer holidaymakers, but the seafood places at the Cros-de-Cagnes fishing port end of the beach are reliable.
