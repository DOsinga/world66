---
title: Beach strip restaurants
type: poi
---

The beach strip in the lower town has a row of restaurants and beach clubs serving standard brasserie food, pizza, and grilled fish. Nothing remarkable, but the food is decent and the prices are reasonable by Riviera standards — considerably better value than the smarter beach clubs further west towards Cannes.

Useful for a quick lunch between Nice and Antibes. Sit outside if the weather cooperates.
