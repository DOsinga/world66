The medieval parish church just below the Château Grimaldi in Haut-de-Cagnes. Saint-Pierre is small, dark, and atmospheric — the original 13th-century nave is the oldest piece of religious architecture in town. It is genuinely lived-in: locals stop by for a quiet moment, candles burn for various intentions, and Mass is celebrated weekly.

The carved 17th-century wooden Christ above the altar is a piece of regional craftsmanship worth a quiet look. The view from the small terrace outside the entrance is one of the better panoramas over the surrounding hills. Free, open most daylight hours.
