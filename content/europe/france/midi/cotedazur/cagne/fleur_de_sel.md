A small contemporary French restaurant on a quiet street in Haut-de-Cagnes that has earned a regular following for its short, seasonal menus and serious attention to local produce. The chef changes the menu every six weeks; expect three- or four-course set menus that lean toward modern Niçoise cooking — line-caught fish, vegetables from inland Provence, local olive oil — with the occasional Asian flourish.

The dining room is tiny (a dozen tables) with a small terrace. Reservations essential, especially on weekend evenings. Mid-range by Riviera standards; very reasonable for the quality.
