---
title: Getting There
type: section
---

**Nice Côte d'Azur Airport** is 10 minutes east by car or about 20 minutes by Bus 200 — Cagnes-sur-Mer is essentially in the western suburbs of Nice. The **Cagnes-sur-Mer station** is on the main coastal TER line, with trains every 15 minutes to Nice (10 minutes), Antibes (8 minutes), Cannes (25 minutes), Monaco, and Menton. The high-speed TGV from Paris stops at Nice or Antibes; transfer to the local train.

The **A8 autoroute** runs through the back of the town. From the airport, follow signs to Cagnes-sur-Mer and exit at junction 47 or 48.
