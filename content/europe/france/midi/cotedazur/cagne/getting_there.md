---
title: Getting there
type: section
---

Cagnes-sur-Mer is 15km west of Nice on the A8 motorway and the coastal road. By car from Nice, allow 20–30 minutes depending on traffic; from Antibes, 15 minutes.

By train, the journey is excellent. Frequent services run from Nice (~15 minutes) and Antibes (~10 minutes) to Cagnes-sur-Mer station, which sits in the lower town near the beach. The trains on this line are regular throughout the day. From the station, buses run up towards Haut-de-Cagnes, or it's a moderately steep walk of about 20 minutes.

Nice Côte d'Azur Airport (NCE) is 10km east — roughly 20 minutes by car, or by bus with a change at Nice.
