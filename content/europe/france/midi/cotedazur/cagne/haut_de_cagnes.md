The medieval old town of Cagnes, draped over a steep hill above the modern coast. Haut-de-Cagnes is a tightly-packed warren of cobbled lanes, arched passages, painted shutters, and small fountains, with the Château Grimaldi at its crown and views in every direction.

There are almost no cars (the streets are too narrow), a handful of small galleries and restaurants, and the kind of pace that has mostly been driven out of the more famous Riviera villages. Modigliani, Soutine, Renoir, and a long list of artists lived and worked here in the early 20th century. The walk up from the lower town takes about 20 minutes; a free shuttle bus runs every 30 minutes from Square Bourdet. Park at the bottom — driving up is more trouble than it's worth.
