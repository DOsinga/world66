The seaside racecourse on the western edge of Cagnes-sur-Mer — the second-largest racetrack in France by race-meeting count, after Vincennes in Paris. The course hosts trot, gallop, and steeplechase racing, with the main winter season running from December to March and a separate summer evening meeting in August.

It is the only racetrack on the Côte d'Azur and one of the few places on the Riviera that still feels genuinely Belle Époque — the grandstands date from the 1950s but the social atmosphere has not changed much. Entry is cheap. Children's playgrounds and food stalls make it surprisingly family-friendly. Check the racing calendar before turning up.
