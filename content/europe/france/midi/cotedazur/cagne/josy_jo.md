A long-running grill restaurant in Haut-de-Cagnes that has been in the same family for three generations. **Josy-Jo** is famous for one thing — meat, grilled over a wood fire in the open kitchen — and does it very well. The signature is a thick côte de boeuf grilled to share, but the lamb chops and the chicken are equally good.

The dining room is small, the walls are covered in vintage signed photographs (Picasso, Soutine, and a long roll of less-recognised local figures), and the atmosphere is informal and noisy. Reservations essential. Prices are reasonable for the Riviera; portions are generous.
