A seafood institution on the Cros-de-Cagnes seafront, in business since 1955. **Loulou La Réserve** is the place locals go for grilled whole fish — sea bass, bream, the occasional turbot when the catch is good — and for the classic **bouillabaisse** of the western Riviera. The kitchen is unpretentious and the prices reflect that.

The dining room is plain, the terrace overlooks the beach. Service is brisk and efficient. Reservations advised in summer; closed Mondays. A useful choice for an early evening dinner with a sea view.
