The town's main weekly market, held every Friday morning along **Avenue de Verdun** and the adjacent streets in the lower town. It is the smaller, more local cousin of the famous markets at Nice and Antibes — fewer tourists, lower prices, and the produce of the surrounding hills: olives, *tapenade*, sun-dried tomatoes, lavender honey, sheep's-milk cheeses from the Maritime Alps, *socca*, and the inevitable Provençal fabrics.

Get there early — by 11am the best of the produce is gone. A smaller fish market runs daily on weekdays at the **Halle Garibaldi**. Bring cash.
