The main square of Haut-de-Cagnes, ringed by ancient plane trees and a handful of small restaurants with outdoor tables that fill up at lunchtime and at sunset. The square sits directly in front of the Château Grimaldi and serves as the village's main meeting point.

In summer there are occasional concerts and a small twice-weekly farmer's market. The cafés on the south side have one of the best views in town — south across the lower town to the Mediterranean. A good spot to recover from the walk up.
