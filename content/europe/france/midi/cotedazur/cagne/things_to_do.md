---
title: Things to do
type: section
---

Cagnes-sur-Mer is really two towns. The lower resort is standard Riviera beach strip — adequate for a swim but nothing to seek out. The reward is Haut-de-Cagnes, the medieval hilltop village above it, where the cobbled lanes and arched passages are genuinely unspoilt by Riviera standards.

There are two reasons to make the trip: the Château Grimaldi, with its eccentric Suzy Solidor portrait collection, and Les Collettes, Renoir's house and studio. Both are small, neither is overrun, and together they make for an excellent half-day.
