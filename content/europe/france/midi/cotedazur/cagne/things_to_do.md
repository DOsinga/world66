---
title: Things to Do
type: section
---

Cagnes-sur-Mer has two essentials: the medieval hilltop village of **Haut-de-Cagnes** with its **Château Grimaldi**, and the **Musée Renoir** in the artist's last home and olive grove. Allow half a day for each, more if you want to linger in the village's cafés. Down on the coast, the beach is functional rather than scenic and the seafront promenade is pleasant for an evening walk.
