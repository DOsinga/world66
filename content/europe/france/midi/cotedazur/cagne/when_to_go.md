---
title: When to go
type: section
---

May, June, September, and October are the ideal months: warm enough for the beach, cool enough to walk the village comfortably, crowds at a manageable level, and all the sights open and unstressed.

July and August are hot and busy. The beach strip fills up, the village is more crowded, and the Château and Musée Renoir attract more visitors — though neither is ever overwhelmed the way the larger Riviera sights can be.

Winter is quiet and pleasant for the village. The light is clear, the lanes are largely empty, and the Château and Musée Renoir remain open (hours reduced — check before you go). The beach is pointless in winter but the cultural case for Cagnes holds year-round.
