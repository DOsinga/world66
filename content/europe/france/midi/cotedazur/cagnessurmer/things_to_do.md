Cagnes-sur-Mer divides into two very different experiences: Haut-de-Cagnes, the medieval hilltop village with its château and artists' heritage, and Cros-de-Cagnes, the beach resort below. The Musée Renoir is the most significant cultural draw on the entire stretch between Nice and Antibes.
