---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

The Côte d'Azur has one of the most reliably pleasant climates in Europe: mild winters, long warm springs, hot and dry summers. The best time to visit Cagnes-sur-Mer is April, May, September and October, when the weather is warm, the crowds lighter, and prices more reasonable than high summer.

July and August are the busiest months, with the beach packed and accommodation expensive. If you are coming primarily for the Musée Renoir and Haut-de-Cagnes rather than the beach, the shoulder seasons are distinctly better. The town is also quieter and more charming in these months.
