---
title: "Getting There"
type: section
---

Cannes is on the main coastal train line between Marseille and Menton, making it easy to reach from Nice (30–40 minutes), Antibes (10 minutes), and Monaco (45 minutes). The TGV from Paris takes about five and a half hours with a change in Nice or direct at holiday peaks. The station is centrally located, a short walk from the Croisette.

Nice Côte d'Azur Airport is the nearest airport, about 30 kilometres away. Bus 210 connects the airport directly to Cannes in about 50 minutes; trains from Nice station take 35 minutes. During the Film Festival in May, all routes to Cannes become extremely busy and prices rise sharply — book well in advance if visiting then.
