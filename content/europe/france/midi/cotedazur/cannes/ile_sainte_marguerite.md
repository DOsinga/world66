Île Sainte-Marguerite is the largest of the Lérins Islands, a short ferry ride from Cannes harbour, and a useful escape from the mainland's expense and crowds. The island is mostly wooded and car-free, with a network of walking and cycling paths through umbrella pine forest leading to rocky coves for swimming.

The Fort Royal at the western end of the island is its main sight: a 17th-century sea fortress that was used as a state prison, most famously for the mysterious Man in the Iron Mask. The cell and some exhibits are preserved within the fort, which now also houses the Musée de la Mer with underwater archaeology finds from the surrounding coast. Ferries from the Vieux Port take about 15 minutes; boats run regularly throughout the day.
