The Marché Forville is the covered morning market that supplies Cannes's restaurants and provides the most pleasant shopping in the city. The hall is open every morning except Monday (when there is an antiques market instead) and offers seasonal Provençal produce: tomatoes, courgettes, aubergines, herbs, olives in every variety, local cheeses, charcuterie, and fresh fish from the boats at the nearby port.

The market is at its best and busiest between 7am and 10am. It sits at the foot of Le Suquet, making it a natural starting point for a morning walk up through the old quarter. The street behind the market, Rue Meynadier, is lined with good cheese shops, traiteurs, and boulangeries if you want to build a picnic.
