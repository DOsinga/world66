The Palais des Festivals is the home of the Cannes Film Festival and the other major congresses and trade fairs that fill the Cannes calendar outside the film season. The building is more interesting from the outside than the inside — its distinctive concrete facade and the famous red-carpeted steps facing the Croisette are the defining image of the festival.

Outside major event periods, part of the building operates as a casino and the surrounding area — the Allée des Étoiles du Cinéma, with hand prints of film stars set into the pavement — is accessible on foot. The Palais is at the western end of the Croisette, making it a natural start or end point for a walk along the seafront.
