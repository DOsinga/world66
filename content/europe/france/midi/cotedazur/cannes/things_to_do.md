---
title: "Things to Do in Cannes"
type: section
---

Cannes offers a split personality: the glamour strip of the Croisette and the genuinely pleasant old quarter of Le Suquet. The Palais des Festivals is worth walking past even in the off-season, if only to understand the scale of the Film Festival. The Lérins Islands offshore — Île Sainte-Marguerite and Île Saint-Honorat — are underrated half-day escapes from the mainland bustle. The market at Forville is one of the best on the Côte d'Azur.
