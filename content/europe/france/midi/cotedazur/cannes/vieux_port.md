The Vieux Port — the old harbour — lies at the western end of the Croisette, sheltered by Le Suquet and lined with fishing boats alongside the yachts. It is a useful orientation point for the city and the departure point for ferries to the Lérins Islands. Early mornings see fish being unloaded from small boats at the quai Saint-Pierre — a functioning working harbour scene that is increasingly rare on the Côte d'Azur.

The port's cafe terraces are among the more reasonably priced places to eat in central Cannes. The Allées de la Liberté running alongside the port has plane trees and a flower market in the mornings. It is a pleasant place to sit and watch the boats and the passersby before the day heats up.
