---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

The Côte d'Azur climate means Cannes is pleasant from April through October, with hot dry summers and mild springs and autumns. June and September are arguably the best months: warm enough to swim, without the peak summer crowds of July and August.

May is when the Film Festival takes over the town — hotels triple their prices, access to the Croisette becomes chaotic, and the whole city is calibrated around celebrity and industry. It is worth experiencing once if cinema interests you, but it is an expensive week to be here. The Cannes Lions advertising festival runs in June and has a similar effect on prices, though less dramatically.

Winter is quiet and mild by northern European standards — temperatures rarely drop below 10°C — and the permanent resident Cannes that exists outside the festival season emerges.
