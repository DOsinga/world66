---
title: Things to Do
type: section
---

Digne-les-Bains is a small town with a manageable list of things to see — a fine cathedral, an unusual art museum, botanical gardens, and access to one of the largest geological reserves in Europe. The Train des Pignes to Nice is reason enough to come. For walkers, the Réserve Géologique de Haute-Provence north of town offers some of the best empty mountain country in southern France.
