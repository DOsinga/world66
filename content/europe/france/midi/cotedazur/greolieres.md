Gréolières is a small hilltop village in the Alpes-Maritimes, sitting at around 800 metres and just 40 minutes from Nice airport. Its position between the coast and the mountains gives it a completely different feel from the Riviera below — stone streets, perched houses, and big views across the pre-Alps. The [medieval village](/europe/france/midi/cotedazur/greolieres/medieval_village) centres on the [Église Saint-Pierre](/europe/france/midi/cotedazur/greolieres/eglise_saint_pierre), a Romanesque church with origins in the 12th century, and the ruined castle above is a reminder that this was once a Grimaldi stronghold.

In winter, the ski station [Gréolières-les-Neiges](/europe/france/midi/cotedazur/greolieres/greolieres_les_neiges) above the village is one of the most accessible resorts in France — close enough to Nice that you can be on the snow within an hour of leaving the seafront. The surrounding countryside offers good walking in the warmer months, with the [Gorges du Loup](/europe/france/midi/cotedazur/greolieres/gorges_du_loup) to the southeast and the limestone plateau of Caussols, home to the [Observatoire de la Côte d'Azur](/europe/france/midi/cotedazur/greolieres/observatoire_calern), a short drive south. The neighbouring village of [Cipières](/europe/france/midi/cotedazur/greolieres/cipieres), just east on its own rocky spur, sees even fewer visitors and is worth a detour.
