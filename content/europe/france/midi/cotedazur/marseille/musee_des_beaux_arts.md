The Musée des Beaux-Arts occupies one wing of the grand Palais Longchamp, so the setting is almost as memorable as the collection. Inside are European paintings and sculpture from the 16th to 19th centuries, strongest as a calm cultural stop after taking in Longchamp's fountains and formal architecture.
