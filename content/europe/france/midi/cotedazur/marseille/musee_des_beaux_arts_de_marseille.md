The Musee des beaux-arts de Marseille is the city's fine arts museum, housed in the Palais Longchamp. Its collection includes French, Italian, Spanish, Flemish and Dutch paintings, making it a useful stop for visitors already exploring the Longchamp complex.

The museum pairs well with the palace architecture and nearby gardens, so allow time for the setting as well as the galleries.
