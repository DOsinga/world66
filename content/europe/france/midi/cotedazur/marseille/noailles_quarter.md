Noailles is one of central Marseille's liveliest quarters, just off La Canebiere and close to the old port. Its streets are lined with North African, Middle Eastern, and Asian grocers, spice shops, bakeries, and small stalls, giving the area a bazaar-like feel that is very different from the polished waterfront.

It is best visited as a short wander rather than a single sight. Combine it with the Marche de Noailles and the surrounding shopping streets, especially if you want to see Marseille's immigrant food culture at street level.
