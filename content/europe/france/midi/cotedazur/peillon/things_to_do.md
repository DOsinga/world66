---
title: Things to Do
type: section
---

Peillon is about stone lanes, views, and the short but sharp climb between the station and the old village. The main sights are the pedestrian medieval village and the Chapelle des Pénitents Blancs just below it; the walking routes toward Sainte-Thècle, La Turbie, and the Paillon valley make the visit feel more like a hill-country outing than a quick photo stop.
