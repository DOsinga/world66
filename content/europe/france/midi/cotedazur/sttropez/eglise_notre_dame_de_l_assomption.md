Eglise Notre-Dame-de-l'Assomption de Saint-Tropez is the pastel-toned church that helps define the old town skyline. Built in the early 19th century, it fits closely with the surrounding lanes and harbor-side architecture.

It is an easy stop while walking between the Vieux Port and the old town, and is one of the clearest historic landmarks in central Saint-Tropez.
