The former Saint-Tropez gendarmerie is now a museum about the building, local cinema history, and the town's screen image. Its main claim to fame is its role in the Louis de Funes films Les Gendarmes de Saint-Tropez, but the museum also gives a light, accessible look at how cinema helped shape the modern mythology of Saint-Tropez.
