La Chapelle Sainte-Anne is a small 17th-century chapel on the hill above Saint-Tropez, built in thanks after the town was spared from plague. The chapel is simple, but the walk up is worthwhile for the view back over the port, the red roofs, and the Gulf of Saint-Tropez.
