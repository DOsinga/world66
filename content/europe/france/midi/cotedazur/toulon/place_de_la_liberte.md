Place de la Liberté is the main civic square of Toulon, a broad nineteenth-century space that gives the naval city a more formal face. Its fountain, plane trees, cafes, and surrounding buildings make it a natural orientation point between the station, old town, and harbour.

It is not a destination that needs an hour, but it belongs in the rhythm of a Toulon visit. Stop here for coffee, then continue down toward the port or up toward the museums and shopping streets.
