---
title: "Bars and Cafes"
type: section
---

Bergerac is not a nightlife town, but it has the cafe and aperitif life of a confident provincial centre. The terrace cafes around the Place Pélissière fill up from late afternoon onwards, particularly in the warmer months when the square hosts open-air music. The Maison des Vins runs informal evening tastings in season.

Among the dedicated wine bars, **Le Vinopôle** opposite the Maison des Vins pours the full range of Bergerac and Monbazillac wines by the glass, with cheese and charcuterie boards. **L'Ancre Bar**, near the riverfront, is a more casual locals' bar with a good beer list. After midnight the town quietens fast; for anything later head to the riverbank brasseries, which keep going on summer weekends.
