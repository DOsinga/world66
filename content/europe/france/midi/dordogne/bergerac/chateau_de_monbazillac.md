The Château de Monbazillac sits on a low hill seven kilometres south of Bergerac, with the vineyards of the Monbazillac appellation falling away on every side. The building, finished in 1550, is a transitional work — a fortified manor with corner towers and crenellations, but also Renaissance windows, dormers, and slate roofs.

The château is open to the public and combines a museum (Protestant history, period furniture, a small wine museum) with a tasting room. The view from the terrace runs across the vineyards to Bergerac and the Dordogne valley beyond. The cooperative's wines are sold on site at fair prices, and the cellar restaurant is a decent place for a long Périgord lunch.
