Bergerac is well placed for short trips into the rest of the Périgord. **Sarlat**, an hour east, is the postcard medieval town of the region — the densest concentration of late-medieval architecture in France and the main base for the Dordogne valley chateaux. **Périgueux**, the départemental capital, has a Roman amphitheatre, a Byzantine-influenced cathedral, and an old town worth a half-day.

In the immediate countryside, the Monbazillac vineyards south of town and the bastide villages of **Eymet** and **Issigeac** are easy half-days. Down the river to the east, **La Roque-Gageac** and **Domme** cluster on the cliffs above the Dordogne and can be combined with a *gabarre* river trip. The prehistoric caves of the Vézère valley, around **Les Eyzies**, are an hour and a half away.
