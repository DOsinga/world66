---
title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Bergerac's old town fans out from the river along narrow lanes lined with half-timbered houses, with the [Place Pelissière](/europe/france/midi/dordogne/bergerac/place_pelissiere) and the [Place de la Myrpe](/europe/france/midi/dordogne/bergerac/place_de_la_myrpe) at its heart. A handful of small, well-curated museums — most notably the [Musée du Tabac](/europe/france/midi/dordogne/bergerac/musee_du_tabac) on the cultural history of tobacco — give a half-day of indoor sightseeing when the weather turns. The real point of a stop in Bergerac, though, is to use it as a base for the surrounding [Bergerac](/europe/france/midi/dordogne/bergerac/maison_des_vins) and [Monbazillac](/europe/france/midi/dordogne/bergerac/chateau_de_monbazillac) wine country, both of which start within ten minutes of the town centre.
