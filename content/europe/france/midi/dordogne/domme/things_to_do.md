---
title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Domme is a small medieval bastide, and most of what's worth doing can be covered in half a day. The [Porte des Tours](/europe/france/midi/dordogne/domme/porte_des_tours) is the town's most evocative medieval relic, the [Grotte de Domme](/europe/france/midi/dordogne/domme/grotte_de_domme) is genuinely impressive for its scale, and the [Belvédère viewpoint](/europe/france/midi/dordogne/domme/belvedere) offers what many consider the finest view in the entire Périgord Noir. Day trips to [Château de Castelnaud](/europe/france/midi/dordogne/domme/chateau_de_castelnaud) and [La Roque-Gageac](/europe/france/midi/dordogne/domme/la_roque_gageac) are easy from here.
