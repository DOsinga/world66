---
title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Pujols is small enough that the village itself is the main thing to do — wander the lanes, walk the ramparts, look in both churches, and stop somewhere with a view to eat or drink. The two churches inside the walls are quietly remarkable for a village of three hundred people; the surviving ramparts and the [south gate](/europe/france/midi/dordogne/pujols/porte_nord) frame the views. For a longer day, descend the hill to [Villeneuve-sur-Lot](/europe/france/midi/dordogne/villeneuvesurlot) on foot or by bike — the climb back up is steep but short.
