---
title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Villeneuve-sur-Lot rewards an unhurried half-day or a quiet weekend rather than a sightseeing dash. Start in the arcaded [Place Lafayette](/europe/france/midi/dordogne/villeneuvesurlot/place_lafayette), the geometric centre of the original bastide, and work outward to the [Porte de Paris](/europe/france/midi/dordogne/villeneuvesurlot/porte_de_paris) and [Porte de Pujols](/europe/france/midi/dordogne/villeneuvesurlot/porte_de_pujols) — the two surviving medieval gateways. Cross the [Pont-Vieux](/europe/france/midi/dordogne/villeneuvesurlot/pont_vieux) at sunset for the best view back over the town and stop at the riverside [Chapelle Notre-Dame du Bout-du-Pont](/europe/france/midi/dordogne/villeneuvesurlot/chapelle_du_bout_du_pont).

The Tuesday and Saturday markets are the high point of the local week. For an afternoon excursion, the hilltop village of [Pujols](/europe/france/midi/dordogne/pujols) is twenty minutes by foot up the south-side hill.
