---
title: "Getting There"
type: section
---

Albi has two railway stations: Albi-Ville in the city centre and Albi-Madeleine on the north side of town, both served by TER regional trains. The most useful connection is to [Toulouse](/europe/france/midi/languedoc/toulouse), about 75 kilometres to the southwest, with trains running several times daily and taking around an hour. There are also connections to Castres.

By car, Albi is on the D988/N88 road and about 75 kilometres northeast of Toulouse. The drive from Toulouse takes just over an hour. There is no direct motorway.
