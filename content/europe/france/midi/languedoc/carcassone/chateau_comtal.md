The Château Comtal is the inner castle at the heart of the Cité, a twelfth-century fortress that itself sits within the larger wall circuit. Its own ditch, drawbridge, and ring of towers make it the strongest single point on the site and the place that most repaid a determined siege.

A ticket lets you walk most of the ramparts from up here, including the inner curtain and the long stretch on the north side overlooking the Aude valley. Inside, the museum holds the original Romanesque sculpture stripped from the basilica during the Viollet-le-Duc restoration, plus models of the city at different periods. Two-hour visit minimum.
