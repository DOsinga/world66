---
title: Getting There
type: section
---

**By air.** Carcassonne Airport (CCF), 3 km west of the centre, has cheap Ryanair flights from a number of UK and European cities. Taxis to the centre are 20–25 euros; a shuttle bus connects with the railway station.

**By train.** Carcassonne sits on the main Toulouse–Narbonne line. Trains from Paris run via the high-speed line to Toulouse and then on a slower service east — about five hours total. Toulouse–Carcassonne is 50 minutes; Narbonne–Carcassonne 30 minutes.

**By road.** The A61 motorway from Toulouse to Narbonne passes immediately north of the city; the Cité is signposted from the exits at either end. From Spain (Barcelona), 4 hours via the A9 and A61.
