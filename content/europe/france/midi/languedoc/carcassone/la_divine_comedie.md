A long-established Bastide restaurant with reliable Languedoc cooking at fair prices. The cassoulet, confit of duck, blanquette of lamb, and the regional daube are the things to order; portions are generous and the wine list is short but well chosen.

Closed Sundays and for two to three weeks at Christmas. 29 boulevard Jean-Jaurès, tel. 04 68 72 30 36.
