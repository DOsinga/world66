The Cité is the walled town on the hill east of the Aude — 52 towers, two concentric rings of ramparts, and just over three kilometres of curtain wall enclosing a small inhabited quarter and a separate inner castle. It is the largest medieval fortified town in Europe and the second-most-visited monument in France after the Eiffel Tower.

Entry through the main Porte Narbonnaise is free; the rampart walks and the Château Comtal at the centre are ticketed. Come in the early morning or late afternoon if you want to enjoy the alleys at anything like a walking pace. About a hundred people still live inside the walls, and the night-time view from the lower town, when the walls are floodlit, is the city's most lasting image.
