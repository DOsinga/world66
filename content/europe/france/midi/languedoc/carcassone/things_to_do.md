---
title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Carcassonne is two cities in one. On the hill east of the river is the Cité — a fully walled medieval town, vast and almost overwhelming, the largest of its kind in Europe. Across the Pont Vieux on the left bank is the Bastide Saint-Louis, a thirteenth-century planned grid that became the real working town once the fortifications were no longer needed. You can see both in a day, but two is more comfortable.

Inside the Cité, the unmissable things are the Château Comtal, the Basilica of Saint-Nazaire and Saint-Celse, and the rampart walks. In the Bastide, the cathedral, the central place Carnot, and the Pont Vieux looking back at the walls are the highlights. Beyond the city, the Canal du Midi, the wine villages of the Minervois, and a string of Cathar fortresses are within easy reach.
