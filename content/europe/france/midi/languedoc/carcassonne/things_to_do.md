---
title: "Things to Do in Carcassonne"
type: section
---

Carcassonne's [Cité](/europe/france/midi/languedoc/carcassonne/cite_de_carcassonne) is the main attraction — a medieval fortress city you can walk through freely. The [Château Comtal](/europe/france/midi/languedoc/carcassonne/chateau_comtal) and the [Basilica of Saints Nazarius and Celsus](/europe/france/midi/languedoc/carcassonne/basilica_saints_nazarius) are the highlights inside the walls. Beyond the fortifications, the [Bastide Saint-Louis](/europe/france/midi/languedoc/carcassonne/bastide_saint_louis) is the real working town, and the [Canal du Midi](/europe/france/midi/languedoc/carcassonne/canal_du_midi) passes just north for walking, cycling, or boat trips.
