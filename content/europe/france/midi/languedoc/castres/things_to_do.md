---
title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

A half-day in Castres splits naturally between the old riverside town and the cluster of monuments around the former Bishop's Palace. Walk along the Agout to see the [Maisons sur l'Agout](/europe/france/midi/languedoc/castres/maisons_sur_lagout) — the painted dyers' houses are the town's signature view — and cross the Pont Neuf for the best photograph back. Then loop through the centre to the [Cathédrale Saint-Benoît](/europe/france/midi/languedoc/castres/cathedrale_saint_benoit) and the [Palais de l'Évêché](/europe/france/midi/languedoc/castres/palais_eveche), with its formal garden by Le Nôtre.

The two museums — the [Musée Goya](/europe/france/midi/languedoc/castres/musee_goya) for Spanish painting and the [Musée Jean Jaurès](/europe/france/midi/languedoc/castres/musee_jean_jaures) for the local hero of French socialism — are the cultural reasons to stay longer.
