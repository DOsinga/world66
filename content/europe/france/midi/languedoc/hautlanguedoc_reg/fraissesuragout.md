Fraisse-sur-Agout is a small village in the Haut Languedoc Regional Nature Park, near [St Pons de Thomières](/europe/france/midi/languedoc/hautlanguedoc_reg/st_pons_de_thomieres). It sits at the junction of fine roads into the Monts de Lacaune and the [Monts de l'Espinouse](/europe/france/midi/languedoc/hautlanguedoc_reg/monts_de_lespinouse), surrounded by chestnut forests and granite hillsides.

The village makes a good base for exploring the park on foot or by car. There is an excellent campsite on the river Agout, and the walking trails lead through some of the least-visited countryside in southern France. The main draw is the [Saut de Vésoles](/europe/france/midi/languedoc/hautlanguedoc_reg/fraissesuragout/saut_de_vesoles) waterfall, reached via the dramatic Chemin des 1000 Marches — a steep thousand-step path dropping from the [Lac de Vésoles](/europe/france/midi/languedoc/hautlanguedoc_reg/fraissesuragout/lac_de_vesoles) reservoir above. The plateau surrounding the village also holds several Neolithic standing stones, including the [Menhir de Picarel](/europe/france/midi/languedoc/hautlanguedoc_reg/fraissesuragout/menhir_de_picarel), evidence of prehistoric settlement in these hills.
