---
title: "Getting There"
type: section
---

Fraisse-sur-Agout is a remote village with no public transport connections. You need a car. From [St Pons de Thomières](/europe/france/midi/languedoc/hautlanguedoc_reg/st_pons_de_thomieres), the nearest town with services, take the D14 east through the forest — the drive takes about twenty minutes on winding mountain roads. From Béziers on the coast, allow an hour and a half.
