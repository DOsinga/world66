The terrace of this café on the cathedral square is the natural gathering point in St-Pons on a Saturday morning, when the market fills the square and the smell of coffee and chestnut cake drifts out from the interior. The café itself is simple — good espresso, cold beer in summer, and the occasional homemade tart. It opens early enough to catch the market at its best and is generally the last place to close on a warm evening.
