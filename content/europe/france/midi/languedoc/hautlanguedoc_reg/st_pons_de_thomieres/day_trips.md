St-Pons-de-Thomières is an excellent base for exploring the Haut-Languedoc park and the wider Hérault department. Most of the destinations below are within an hour by car, and several can be combined in a single day.

[Olargues](/europe/france/midi/languedoc/hautlanguedoc_reg/olargues), about 20 kilometres east along the Jaur valley, is generally considered the most beautiful village in the Haut-Languedoc — a Plus Beaux Villages de France member whose medieval lanes, eleventh-century tower, and famous Pont du Diable are well worth an afternoon. The drive on the D908 through the Orb and Jaur gorges is scenic in itself.

[Lac de la Raviège](/europe/france/midi/languedoc/hautlanguedoc_reg/lac_de_la_raviege) lies about 20 minutes north on the road toward La Salvetat-sur-Agout. The largest lake in the park, it offers a supervised summer beach, sailing and kayak rental from the base nautique, and a forested shoreline path that is superb for walking in autumn when the chestnuts turn.

[Béziers](/europe/france/midi/languedoc/beziers) is about 55 kilometres south — roughly an hour by car through the Orb gorge, or a train ride on the scenic Transpyrénéen line. The city sits on a high bluff above the Orb, its silhouette dominated by the Cathédrale Saint-Nazaire and visible from the Canal du Midi below. The nine locks of Fonseranes on the canal just west of the city are a seventeenth-century engineering marvel worth an hour of anyone's time.
