A reliable address on the edge of town, Les Bergeries du Jaur serves traditional Languedoc cooking built around local produce — grilled meats, cassoulet in winter, and a charcuterie board that draws heavily on what comes down from the farms in the Lacaune hills. The dining room is plain and unpretentious, the wine list leans on regional appellations from the Coteaux du Languedoc, and the portions are generous. Lunch is better value than dinner; the plat du jour at midday is usually the best thing on the menu.
