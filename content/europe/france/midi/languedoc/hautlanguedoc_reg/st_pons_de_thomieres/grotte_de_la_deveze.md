The Grotte de la Devèze, just outside St-Pons on the road toward Olargues, is one of the finest show caves in the Languedoc. What distinguishes it from the many other caves in this limestone region is the abundance of aragonite formations — needles, fans, and branching crystals of calcium carbonate that form differently from the more common stalactites. The effect is otherworldly: the guided tour takes you through chambers that look as if they have been decorated by a very patient artist with an unusual aesthetic.

The cave maintains a constant temperature of around 13°C, welcome in summer and potentially chilly at other times. Guided tours run regularly from spring through autumn; check locally for schedules in the off-season. Allow about an hour for the visit.
