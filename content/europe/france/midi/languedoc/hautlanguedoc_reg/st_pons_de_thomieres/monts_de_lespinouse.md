The roads north of St-Pons climb through chestnut forests into the high moorland of the Monts de l'Espinouse — a plateau at around 1,000 metres that forms the backbone of the Parc Naturel Régional du Haut-Languedoc. The landscape up here is open, windy, and surprisingly bleak given that you are just an hour from the Mediterranean coast. In clear conditions the views south across the Hérault and Orb valleys are extraordinary.

The summit area around the Col de l'Espinouse has marked walking routes and good birdwatching — short-toed eagles, buzzards, and red kites are all regularly seen. The chestnut forests on the descent back toward St-Pons are magnificent in autumn, when the colours and the smell of fallen leaves make even the drive worthwhile.
