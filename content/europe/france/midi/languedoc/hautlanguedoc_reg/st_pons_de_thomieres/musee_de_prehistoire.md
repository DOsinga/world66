The Musée de Préhistoire Régionale de Saint-Pons is housed in part of the former bishop's palace beside the cathedral. The collections focus on the prehistoric occupation of the Haut-Languedoc, with well-presented displays of Neolithic and Bronze Age artefacts from excavations in the surrounding hills — polished stone axes, ceramic vessels, bronze ornaments, and skeletal remains from the dolmens that still dot the moorland above the town.

The museum is small but well organised, and the staff are usually willing to discuss the sites in the area. A visit of an hour or so is enough to get a good sense of the prehistoric landscape and to pick up information about visiting the dolmens above St-Pons on foot. Opening hours are reduced outside the summer season; check with the tourist office before making a special journey.
