Olargues, about 20 kilometres east of St-Pons along the Jaur valley, is generally considered the most beautiful village in the Haut-Languedoc. The medieval core climbs a spur above the river, with narrow lanes, stone houses, and an eleventh-century tower that now serves as the church bell tower. The Pont du Diable below the village is one of the oldest surviving medieval bridges in France and makes a fine photograph from the riverside path.

The drive from St-Pons along the D908 through the Orb and Jaur gorges is scenic in itself. Olargues has a couple of good restaurants and a small market on Sundays in summer. Combine it with a stop at the Grotte de la Devèze, which lies roughly halfway between the two towns.
