---
title: "Shopping"
type: section
---

The pedestrianised streets of the Écusson are where to wander — **Rue de la Loge**, **Rue Saint-Guilhem** and the lanes around **Place Saint-Roch** are full of small boutiques, independent designers and second-hand shops. The covered market **Halles Castellane** off Rue de la Loge is a good lunch stop too.

For something more conventional, **Polygone** is a big mall right next to Place de la Comédie. Beyond it, the strange postmodern **Antigone** quarter leads to **Odysseum**, the city's edge-of-town shopping and entertainment district reached by tramline 1. Saturday morning is market morning — the **Marché des Arceaux** under the aqueduct arches is the best of several weekly farmers' markets.
