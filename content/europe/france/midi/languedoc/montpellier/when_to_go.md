---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

Montpellier has the Mediterranean's reliable script: hot dry summers, mild damp winters, and over 300 days of sunshine a year. The shoulder seasons — **April to June** and **September to early October** — are the sweet spot. The Écusson terraces are warm enough to sit out, the beaches are usable, and the city isn't yet packed with summer tourists.

**Summer** (July and August) can hit 35°C and the old town gets crowded, but the cultural calendar is at its peak — the **Festival de Radio France et Montpellier** brings classical music to the city in July, and **Montpellier Danse** is one of Europe's biggest contemporary-dance festivals in June and July. Winters are mild — usually 10–13°C in the day — and almost everything stays open. The tramontane wind can be strong and cold in winter; pack a windproof layer.
