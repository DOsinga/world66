title: "Day Trips"
type: section
---

The Pont du Gard, twenty-five kilometres northeast, is the masterpiece of Roman aqueduct engineering: three tiers of arches striding across the Gardon river, carrying water to Nîmes. It is a UNESCO site with a well-organised visitor centre. Walking trails along the river give the best views.

Several great cities sit within an hour's drive or train ride: [Avignon](/europe/france/midi/provence/avignon) with its papal palace, [Arles](/europe/france/midi/provence/arles) with its Roman amphitheatre and Van Gogh connections, [Montpellier](/europe/france/midi/languedoc/montpellier) with its medieval centre. Closer to home, Uzès is a perfectly preserved small town. South of the city, the wetlands of the Camargue — with bulls, white horses, and flamingos — start about thirty kilometres away.
