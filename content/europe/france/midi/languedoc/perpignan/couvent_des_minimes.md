The Couvent des Minimes is a former 16th-century convent near Saint-Jacques, on the eastern side of Perpignan's old town. Founded in 1573 and later taken over by the army, it now serves as an exhibition space rather than a religious house.

The appeal is the combination of old stone architecture and temporary art shows. It is an easy stop while walking between Saint-Jacques, Hotel Pams, and the cathedral quarter.
