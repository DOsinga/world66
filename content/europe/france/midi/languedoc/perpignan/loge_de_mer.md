The Loge de Mer was Perpignan's medieval stock exchange and commercial chamber, a reminder of the city's trading life when merchants, shipowners, and officials met here to do business. It now houses the tourist office, but the building itself is the reason to pause, especially while walking between Le Castillet, the cathedral, and the old centre.
