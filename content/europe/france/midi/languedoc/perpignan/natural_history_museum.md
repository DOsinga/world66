Perpignan's Natural History Museum covers the minerals, plants, and animals of the Pyrenees, with additional archaeological material from ancient Egypt and an ethnological collection from Oceania.

It is a useful indoor stop near the old center, especially if you want local natural history alongside Perpignan's better-known churches, palaces, and streets.
