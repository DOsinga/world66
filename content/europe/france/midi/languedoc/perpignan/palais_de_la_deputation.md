The Palais de la Députation is a 15th-century civic building in old Perpignan, close to Le Castillet and the cathedral. It once housed Catalan government offices under Aragonese and Majorcan rule, then served a similar role after Perpignan became French.

Today it is used by the city government, so the interest is mostly exterior and historical. As part of a walk through the centre, it helps explain why Perpignan feels as Catalan as it does French.
