The Palais de la Deputation is a 15th-century civic building in Perpignan's old centre. It housed the Catalan government in the period when the city belonged to the Crown of Aragon and later the Kingdom of Majorca.

Its value is mainly as part of a walk around Perpignan's historic core, where Catalan Gothic and French civic architecture sit close together.
