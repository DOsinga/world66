Château de Peyrepertuse is one of the great ruined Cathar castles of the Corbières, stretched along a limestone ridge above the vineyards and scrub. The site feels more like a stone spine than a single castle, with walls, towers, and steep paths stepping across the rock.

Come for the history, but also for the views. The climb is exposed and narrow in places, so wear proper shoes and avoid rushing it in strong wind.
