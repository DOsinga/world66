Aeroscopia is Toulouse's aviation museum, and it makes sense that it sits in Blagnac beside the Airbus world. The hangar is filled with aircraft rather than models alone: Concorde, the Super Guppy, Airbus prototypes, military planes, and civil aircraft that show why this city became one of Europe's great aerospace centres.

It is a strong choice for families and anyone who likes engineering with scale. Combine it with an Airbus-related visit if schedules line up, or come simply to stand under wings that are much larger than they look from an airport window.
