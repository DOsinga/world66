A masterpiece of southern Gothic architecture built by the Dominican order in the 13th century. The nave is divided by a single row of slender columns that branch out into extraordinary palm-tree vaults at the roof — one of the most beautiful interior spaces in France. The cloister garden is a peaceful retreat from the busy city outside. The remains of Thomas Aquinas are kept here. Small admission charge.
