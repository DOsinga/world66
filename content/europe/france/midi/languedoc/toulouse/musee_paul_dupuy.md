Musée Paul-Dupuy is a refined museum of decorative arts, graphic works, clocks, and historic objects housed in a Toulouse mansion. It offers a more intimate counterpoint to the city's larger art and archaeology museums, with collections that reward close looking.

The museum is especially worthwhile for visitors interested in craftsmanship, private collecting, and the material culture of Toulouse. Its central location makes it easy to pair with the cathedral quarter, the old streets south of the Capitole, or Musée des Augustins.
