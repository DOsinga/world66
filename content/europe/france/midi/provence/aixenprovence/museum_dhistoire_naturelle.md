The Museum d'Histoire Naturelle is a small but charming natural history museum, currently housed in the Parc Saint-Mitre after a move from its long-time home in the Hotel Boyer d'Eguilles. The collections cover Provencal geology, paleontology, and zoology, with the dinosaur eggs of Mont Sainte-Victoire as the headline exhibit.

It is the kind of museum where you can spend half an hour or two hours and feel time has been well spent. Excellent for children, with stuffed Provencal wildlife — wild boar, eagle owl, lynx — and a hall of minerals lit up like jewels. Free entry on the first Sunday of every month.
