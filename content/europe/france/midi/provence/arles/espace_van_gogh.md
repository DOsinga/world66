Espace Van Gogh is the former Arles hospital where Vincent van Gogh was treated after his breakdown in 1888. Its courtyard has been restored with the bright arcades and garden that appear in his painting of the hospital, making it a quick but worthwhile stop for visitors following Van Gogh's time in Arles.
