---
title: Eating Out
type: section
---

Cavaillon's restaurants are built around the extraordinary produce that passes through the Monday market. In season, Cavaillon melon appears on almost every menu — as a starter with Serrano ham, as a sorbet, as a main event on its own. The town is well-supplied with bistros and cafes that do simple Provençal cooking well: ratatouille, daube, stuffed vegetables, tapenade.
