The Jean Cocteau Museum holds the largest collection of Cocteau's work in the world, donated to Menton by the American collector Séverin Wunderman. Cocteau — painter, filmmaker, playwright, and all-round provocateur — left a sprawling output, and the museum traces it across media: paintings, drawings, tapestries, ceramics, photographs, and manuscripts.

The building itself is a striking piece of contemporary architecture on the seafront promenade. Even visitors unfamiliar with Cocteau's films or writing will find things to engage with here — the sheer range of his curiosity is compelling, and the biographical rooms explain why he was such a central figure in 20th-century French culture.
