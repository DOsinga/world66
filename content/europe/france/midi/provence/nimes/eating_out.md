---
title: "Eating Out"
type: section
---

Nîmes sits at the edge of several distinct food cultures: Provençal cooking to the east, the more rustic Languedoc tradition to the west, and Camargue produce — bull meat, salt-marsh lamb, rice — close at hand. The city's markets are excellent; the covered Halles de Nîmes is the best place to shop.

Local specialities to look for: *brandade de Nîmes* (salt cod puréed with olive oil and garlic, a dish claimed by Nîmes as its own), bull meat stewed in red wine, and *clafoutis* with local cherries in season.
