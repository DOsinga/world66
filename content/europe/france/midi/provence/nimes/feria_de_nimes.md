The Feria de Nîmes is one of the largest bullfighting festivals in Europe, a five-day event held twice each year: once over Pentecost (late May or early June) and once in September. During the feria, the city transforms utterly — the Arena fills for afternoon *corridas*, the streets run with wine and music until dawn, and a temporary town of *peñas* (music and drinking clubs) springs up in the open areas around the arena.

The bullfighting is the controversial centrepiece: Spanish-style *corridas* with top matadors, advertised weeks in advance and attended by aficionados from across France and Spain. If bullfighting is not for you, the festival atmosphere outside the arena — the flamenco, the Camargue horseback processions, the street food, the sheer human spectacle — is remarkable on its own terms. Book accommodation months in advance if you plan to visit during feria week.
