---
title: "Getting Around"
type: section
---

The old town of Nîmes is compact and almost entirely walkable. The Arena, Maison Carrée, Jardins de la Fontaine, and most of the museums are within 15–20 minutes' walk of each other on flat ground. There is no reason to take a bus or taxi between these sights.

**On foot** is the best option for the historic centre. The main streets are lively and relatively shaded.

**Bicycle:** Nîmes has a municipal bike-share scheme (Velonîmes) with stations across the city, including near the station and main sights. Hire requires a credit card at the terminal; the first 30 minutes are free.

**Bus and tram:** The city has a bus network and a single tram line running roughly north-south. For visiting sights within the old town the tram is of limited use, but it connects the station to the city centre quickly.

**Taxi:** Available from the station and from the main squares; useful for reaching the airport or for evening returns.

**Day trips by car:** To visit the Pont du Gard (23km north), the Camargue, or the garrigue villages north of the city, a car is helpful. Car hire is available at the station and the airport.
