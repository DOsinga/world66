---
title: "Getting There"
type: section
---

**By train:** Nîmes station is well connected. Paris (Montparnasse or Lyon) is 3 hours by TGV. From Montpellier it is 30 minutes; from Marseille around 1 hour 20 minutes; from Avignon 25 minutes. The station is about 1km south of the old town.

**By air:** Nîmes Alès Camargue Cévennes airport handles some Ryanair routes (London Stansted, Birmingham, and others), but it is a small airport with limited services. Montpellier airport (50km) and Marseille Provence airport (90km) offer more options and are easily reached by train from Nîmes.

**By car:** Nîmes sits at the junction of the A9 (east–west along the coast) and the A54 (to Marseille). Parking in the old town is difficult; use one of the underground car parks near the Arena or the Maison Carrée.

**By bus:** Long-distance coach services connect Nîmes to Montpellier, Avignon, and other regional cities; useful if you do not have a rail pass.
