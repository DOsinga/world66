A solid bistro in the old town, close enough to the Arena to be convenient but not a tourist trap. The menu changes with the seasons and leans on regional produce: Camargue bull stew, salt-marsh lamb, river fish, and the local *brandade de Nîmes* done properly — salt cod puréed until silky smooth with olive oil and garlic. The wine list is entirely local, with good Costières de Nîmes at reasonable prices.

Lunch is the best value: the two-course *formule* at around €15–17 is popular with office workers and locals. Evenings are quieter and more formal. Booking is advisable at dinner.
