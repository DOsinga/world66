The Musée de la Romanité opened in 2018 and immediately became one of the best Roman archaeology museums in France. The building itself announces its ambition — a glass facade etched with patterns based on Roman toga drapery, designed by architect Elizabeth de Portzamparc, rising directly opposite the Arena. The effect, especially lit up at night, is theatrical.

Inside, the collection spans three floors and some 5,000 artefacts: mosaics, sculptures, ceramics, weapons, and everyday objects that bring Roman Nîmes to life with unusual clarity. A highlight is the collection of Roman glassware, delicate and improbably intact. The rooftop garden overlooks the Arena and the city — worth the entry on its own. Open Tuesday–Sunday; entry is €8.
