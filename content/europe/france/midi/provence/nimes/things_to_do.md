---
title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Nîmes is an open-air museum of Roman civilization, and most of the key sights are concentrated within easy walking distance of each other in the old town. The Arena, the Maison Carrée, and the Jardins de la Fontaine are the three must-sees; allow a full day if you want to go inside all of them. The Musée de la Romanité, opened in 2018, adds excellent context to everything else you will see.
