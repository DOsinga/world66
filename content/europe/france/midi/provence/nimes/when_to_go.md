---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

Nîmes has a hot, dry Mediterranean climate. Summers are long and very warm, winters mild. The best time to visit for comfortable sightseeing is **spring (April–June)** or **early autumn (September–October)**, when temperatures are pleasant and the light on the Roman monuments is beautiful.

**July and August** are very hot (often 35°C+), and the old town can be crowded. That said, many outdoor events and concerts take place at the Arena in summer — worth checking the programme if you are already there.

**The Ferias:** If you want to experience the city at its most intense, time a visit to coincide with the **Feria de Pentecôte** (late May or early June) or the **Feria des Vendanges** (third weekend of September). Both are five-day events centred on bullfighting but encompassing the whole city in music, dancing, and drinking. Book accommodation well in advance.

**Winter** is quiet and mild — rarely cold, occasionally rainy. The Roman monuments are less crowded and you get them largely to yourself.
