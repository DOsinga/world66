---
title: "Eating Out"
type: section
---

Waterfront restaurants along the main canal serve Provencal and Mediterranean food — fish, bouillabaisse, grilled sea bass, and salads with goat cheese and tapenade. Prices are notably lower than in nearby Saint-Tropez, though still in the holiday-resort range. The restaurants facing the main square and the canal are the best spots; grab a table on a terrace for lunch and watch the boats drift by.
