---
title: Things to Do
type: section
---

Lourdes is primarily a pilgrimage destination, and the sanctuary complex is the reason most people come. The [Grotte de Massabielle](/europe/france/midi/pyrenees/lourdes/grotte_de_massabielle), the grotto where Bernadette reported her apparitions, is open all hours and is the spiritual centre of the site. The three basilicas of the [Sanctuary of Our Lady of Lourdes](/europe/france/midi/pyrenees/lourdes/sanctuary_of_our_lady) — including the vast underground [Basilica of St. Pius X](/europe/france/midi/pyrenees/lourdes/basilica_of_st_pius_x) — and the nightly candlelight processions on the Esplanade make for an extraordinary spectacle whatever your beliefs.

The [Château Fort](/europe/france/midi/pyrenees/lourdes/chateau_fort_de_lourdes) on its rocky outcrop above town houses a good Pyrenean museum. The [Funiculaire du Pic du Jer](/europe/france/midi/pyrenees/lourdes/pic_du_jer) rises to a viewpoint above the town with panoramic mountain views. The [Gave de Pau](/europe/france/midi/pyrenees/lourdes/gave_de_pau) river walk connects the sanctuary sites and is pleasant at any time of day.
