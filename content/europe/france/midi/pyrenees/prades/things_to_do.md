---
title: "Things to Do in Prades"
type: section
---

Prades is a small town, but its central church is exceptional, and the Abbey of Saint-Michel-de-Cuxa just outside town is one of the finest Romanesque buildings in the Pyrenees. The Pablo Casals Festival brings serious musicians and audiences every summer. Beyond the town itself, the valley offers river swimming, mountain walking, and easy access to ski resorts and Mediterranean beaches.
