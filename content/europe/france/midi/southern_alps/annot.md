Annot is a small town in the Southern Alps with more accommodation than you would expect for its size, thanks to its position on the scenic [Train des Pignes](/europe/france/midi/southern_alps/train_des_pignes) line. The mountains above the railway are littered with massive sandstone boulders called [Grès d'Annot](/europe/france/midi/southern_alps/annot/the_gres_boulders) — some the size of houses — and the walk between them is short but thrilling.

The [old town](/europe/france/midi/southern_alps/annot/old_town_annot) above the modern area is fascinating, with medieval lanes and houses built directly against the boulder faces. Any number of walks can be started from Annot, or started from stations higher up the line and finished here. The [Train des Pignes](/europe/france/midi/southern_alps/annot/train_des_pignes_annot) itself is the most scenic way to arrive — the three-hour journey from Nice through gorges and mountain switchbacks is a pleasure in its own right.
