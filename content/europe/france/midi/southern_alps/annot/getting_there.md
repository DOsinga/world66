---
title: Getting There
type: section
---

Annot is on the Train des Pignes narrow-gauge railway between Nice and Digne-les-Bains. Trains run several times a day; the journey from Nice takes about three hours. There is no direct car-free alternative — if you are driving, Annot is reached via the D908 from Entrevaux or from Digne-les-Bains. The village has no direct motorway connection; the roads approaching it are scenic but winding.
