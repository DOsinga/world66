---
title: Walking Routes
type: section
---

Annot is an excellent base for day walks in the Southern Alps. The most popular route is the Grès d'Annot circuit — a 1.5-hour loop through the boulder fields above town, waymarked and accessible for most fitness levels. Longer walks climb into the higher terrain around the Chambre du Roi (a natural boulder shelter used since antiquity), and the GR 4 long-distance path passes through.

Walking between Train des Pignes stations is another good option. From Annot toward Thorame-Haute, the path follows the valley through juniper and oak before climbing to open hillsides with wide views. The terrain is not technical, but walking shoes rather than sandals are advisable. Tourist information is available at the village mairie and there are local walking maps.
