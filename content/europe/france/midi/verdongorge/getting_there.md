---
title: Getting There
type: section
---

The Verdon Gorge sits in the hills inland from the Côte d'Azur, about 80 kilometres north of Nice and 90 kilometres northeast of Aix-en-Provence. The nearest larger towns are Castellane (on the eastern approach) and Moustiers-Sainte-Marie (on the western approach, near the Lac de Sainte-Croix).

There is no public transport to the gorge — a car is essential. The fastest approach from the coast is via the D952 from Castellane. From Aix-en-Provence, the approach via Greoux-les-Bains and Riez to Moustiers is slower but passes through beautiful Haute-Provence landscape.

The rim roads are narrow and winding. Driving the Route des Crêtes and the Corniche Sublime in a single day is perfectly manageable; budget about three hours driving time for both.
