Notre-Dame de Beauvoir is a hill chapel above Moustiers-Sainte-Marie, reached by a steep stone stairway of more than 250 steps. The church has Romanesque origins from the 12th century, with later Gothic and Renaissance details.

The climb is part of the appeal. From the terrace in front of the chapel, the view drops over the tiled roofs of Moustiers and out toward Lac de Sainte-Croix, making it one of the best short walks around the Verdon Gorge villages.
