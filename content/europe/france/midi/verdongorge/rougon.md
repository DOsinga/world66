Rougon is the smallest and highest of the villages at the Verdon Gorge — a hamlet of perhaps 100 permanent residents perched on a cliff edge above the eastern entrance to the canyon. The ruins of its castle occupy the highest point, and the view from there is one of the most dramatic in Provence: the gorge opens below you like a wound in the plateau, the river a thin turquoise thread far below.

The village has a single café and very little else. It's worth stopping for the views and the walk up to the castle ruins, but not for amenities. The nearby Point Sublime viewpoint — accessible by car from the road below — is the most famous viewpoint in the entire gorge.
