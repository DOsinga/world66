The Route des Crêtes follows the north rim of the gorge from La Palud-sur-Verdon eastward, passing a series of dramatic belvederes carved from the limestone. It's a one-way road for much of its length, driven anticlockwise (east to west), and takes 1–2 hours to cover comfortably with stops.

The viewpoints include the Belvédère de l'Escalès (the highest above the gorge floor), the Belvédère du Tilleul (best for the Corniche Sublime views to the south), and several smaller pullouts with benches. At each one the gorge looks different — sometimes the water is visible, sometimes the walls fill the frame, sometimes the scale only becomes apparent when you spot a tiny kayak 600 metres below. Bring binoculars.
