---
title: When to Go
type: section
---

Spring (April–June) and autumn (September–October) are the best times to visit the Verdon Gorge. The weather is warm but not oppressive, the trails are dry, and the crowds are manageable. April brings wildflowers across the plateau; September brings the grape harvest in the surrounding vineyards and the best light for photography.

July and August are very popular — the south of France's main holiday season brings heavy traffic to the rim roads and long queues at the major belvederes. Parking at the Belvédère de l'Escalès and Point Sublime can require waiting in line. Come early in the morning (before 9am) to avoid the worst of it.

Winter is cold and quiet, with occasional snow on the plateau. Most facilities close. The gorge in winter snow is dramatic and almost completely deserted.
