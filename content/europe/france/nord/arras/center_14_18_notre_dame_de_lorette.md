This First World War history centre stands below Notre-Dame-de-Lorette, in the old battlefields north of Arras. Its photographs, films, objects, and animated maps explain the scale of the fighting in Nord-Pas-de-Calais and pair well with a visit to the nearby cemetery and memorials.
