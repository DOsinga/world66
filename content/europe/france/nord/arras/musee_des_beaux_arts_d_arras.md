Musee des Beaux-Arts d'Arras occupies part of the former Saint-Vaast Abbey, a setting grand enough to be part of the visit. Its collections move through paintings, sculpture, decorative arts, and regional history, with the scale of the old abbey giving even smaller rooms a sense of occasion.

It pairs well with the nearby cathedral and the Flemish-Baroque squares that define central Arras. The museum is not as famous as the town's First World War sites, but it gives Arras a deeper cultural footing than the battlefields alone.
