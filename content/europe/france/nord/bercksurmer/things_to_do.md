---
title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Berck-sur-Mer is built around its beach and the activities it enables. The sand here is wide, firm, and extends for kilometres in either direction — ideal for sand sailing, horse riding, and long walks. The town itself has a small museum and a couple of churches, but the real draw is the outdoor life along the Opal Coast. The Authie Bay estuary to the south is a serious bird reserve, and the dunes behind the beach shelter a landscape that feels wilder than the typical French seaside resort.
