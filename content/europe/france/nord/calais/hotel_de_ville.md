Calais' Hôtel de Ville is the city's showpiece civic building, standing beside Rodin's *Burghers of Calais*. Inside, visitors can see the grand staircase and ceremonial rooms when access is available.

The belfry is the real draw: a lift takes you up for a broad view over Calais, the port, and the flat coast around the Channel.
