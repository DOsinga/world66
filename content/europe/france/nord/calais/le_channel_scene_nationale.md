Le Channel, Scène Nationale is Calais' major contemporary performing-arts venue, set in a converted industrial complex rather than a formal theatre palace. That setting gives it a strong local character and makes it one of the city's more interesting cultural stops.

The venue is most useful to travellers when a performance, festival, or exhibition matches their dates. Even then, it is worth noting as part of modern Calais: a working arts space in a city too often reduced to ferries and border logistics.
