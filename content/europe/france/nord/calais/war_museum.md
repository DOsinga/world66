Calais' War Museum is set in a reinforced concrete bunker and focuses on the city during the Second World War. The collection includes period photographs, press clippings, everyday objects, uniforms, weapons, posters, and material from daily life under occupation.

It is a compact but useful counterpoint to Calais' civic monuments and harbour sights, especially for visitors interested in wartime history on the Channel coast.
