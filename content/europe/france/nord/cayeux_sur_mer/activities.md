The reason to book anything in Cayeux is the seal colony at the Pointe du Hourdel — the largest in France, and best seen on a guided walk out over the shingle and sand at low tide, when the animals haul out on the banks. Guides carry telescopes and keep groups at a distance that doesn't disturb the colony, which is both the ethical and the practical choice: seals that get approached simply leave.

Outings are set by the tide table, so the departure time changes every day and the walk is called off in bad weather.
