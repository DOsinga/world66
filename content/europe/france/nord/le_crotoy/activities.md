title: Activities
type: section
---

Almost everything bookable in Le Crotoy is about the bay: boat trips out towards the seal banks and the mouth of the Somme, guided walks across the sand at low tide, and the bird reserve to the north. Trips are tide-dependent rather than clock-dependent, so departure times move day to day and booking ahead is genuinely necessary in summer.

The boats leave from the Quai Courbet in the middle of town; the bird reserve is a ten-minute drive north at Saint-Quentin-en-Tourmont.
