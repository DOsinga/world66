Le Touquet's covered market is one of the resort's better everyday stops, especially on market days when food stalls and local shoppers bring the hall to life. It offers a grounded contrast to the beach, villas, and polished shopping streets.

Come for browsing rather than sightseeing grandeur: cheese, produce, prepared foods, and the social rhythm of a French seaside town. It is a useful stop before a picnic or self-catered meal.
