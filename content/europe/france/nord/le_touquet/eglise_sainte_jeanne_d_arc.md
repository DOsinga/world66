Église Sainte-Jeanne-d'Arc is the main parish church of Le Touquet-Paris-Plage, a resort town better known for dunes, villas, and beach life than for ecclesiastical architecture. Its dedication to Joan of Arc gives the church a distinctly French civic-religious identity, and its brickwork and proportions fit the planned-resort character of the town.

It is not a destination church on the scale of a cathedral, but it is worth a look while walking the centre. The building adds a quieter note to Le Touquet's leisure landscape and helps round out a visit beyond the seafront.
