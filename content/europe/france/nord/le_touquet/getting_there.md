Le Touquet sits on the Opal Coast about 30 km south of [Boulogne](/europe/france/nord/boulogne). By rail, the nearest station is Étaples–Le Touquet, served by TGV and regional trains from Paris Gare du Nord (roughly 2 hours 30 minutes). From the station in Étaples, the town centre is about 4 km by taxi or local bus.

By road from Paris, take the A1 and then the A16 motorway towards Boulogne, turning off at the Le Touquet exit; the drive is around two and a half hours. From the Channel Tunnel terminal at Coquelles, the drive takes under an hour. There is no airport currently serving scheduled passenger flights.
