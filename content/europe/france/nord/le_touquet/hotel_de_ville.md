Le Touquet's Hotel de Ville is one of the resort's strongest civic buildings, an interwar town hall with a belfry that gives the flat seaside town a vertical accent. It fits the resort's taste for theatrical architecture without feeling flimsy.

You will likely pass it while moving between the market streets, villas, and beach. Stop long enough to look at the brickwork and proportions; it tells you how confidently Le Touquet presented itself in its grand resort years.
