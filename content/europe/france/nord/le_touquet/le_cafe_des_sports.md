A more relaxed option than the seafood brasseries along the main strip, Le Café des Sports serves straightforward French cooking at honest prices: steak frites, moules, croque-monsieur, and a rotating daily special chalked on the board. The terrace is pleasant in summer. It draws a local crowd as well as visitors, which is usually a good sign.
