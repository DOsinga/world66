The Musée du Touquet-Paris-Plage gives Le Touquet a cultural counterweight to the beach, villas, and
golf courses. Set in a residential villa, it presents art and exhibitions connected to the resort's
development, with collections that move beyond seaside nostalgia into modern and contemporary work.

It is especially useful on a windy or wet Channel day, when Le Touquet's outdoor pleasures are less
inviting. The museum also helps explain why the town became more than a beach: artists, architects,
sports clubs, and wealthy holidaymakers all shaped the resort's identity.
