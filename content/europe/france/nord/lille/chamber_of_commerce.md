Lille's Chamber of Commerce was built in 1921 and remains one of the most distinctive landmarks around the central squares. The building is now used for offices and co-working, so the main reason to stop is the exterior architecture.

It is an easy short detour while walking between the Opéra, Vieille Bourse, and Grand'Place. The facade is especially striking after dark, when the lighting picks out its tower and ornament.
