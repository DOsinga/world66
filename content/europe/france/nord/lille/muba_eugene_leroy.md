MUba Eugene Leroy is an art museum in Tourcoing, part of the wider Lille metropolitan area. Its collections and exhibitions connect local and regional art history with modern and contemporary work, anchored by the legacy of painter Eugene Leroy.

The museum is best for travellers who have already seen central Lille's headline sights and want to explore the surrounding urban fabric. Tourcoing is close by metro, making MUba a realistic half-day cultural detour rather than a remote excursion.
