The Museum of the Pasteur Institute of Lille tells the story of the institute and the scientists linked to it, including Louis Pasteur, Albert Calmette, and Camille Guerin. Exhibits connect Lille's medical history with the research still carried out by the institute today.

It is a compact museum, best for visitors with an interest in science, public health, or the history of vaccines. Pair it with the Natural History Museum nearby if you want a more rounded museum afternoon.
