---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

Lille works year-round but the most compelling reason to time a trip is the **Braderie de Lille** on the first weekend of September — the largest flea market in Europe, when the entire city centre becomes one vast outdoor sale and every restaurant competes to build the highest mound of moules-frites shells on the pavement outside. Book accommodation months ahead for this weekend.

**Spring and summer** (April–August) are the most pleasant for café terraces and walking Vieux-Lille. The Citadelle park is particularly enjoyable in warm weather. **Winter** is cold and often grey — this is northern France on the edge of Belgium, not the south — but the Christmas market on the Grand'Place is genuinely good and the museums and estaminets are excellent reasons to come regardless of weather.

The **Fêtes de Lille** in June celebrate the city's Flemish heritage with parades, concerts, and folk events centred on the Grand'Place. The **Lille Piano Festival** in October is one of the significant classical music events in northern France.
