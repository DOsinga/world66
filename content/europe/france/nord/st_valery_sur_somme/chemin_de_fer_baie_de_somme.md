The CFBS, the Chemin de Fer de la Baie de Somme, is one of France's best-loved heritage railways. The line runs on metre-gauge tracks for 27 kilometres around the bay, linking St-Valéry to Le Crotoy on the north shore and Cayeux-sur-Mer on the open Channel coast. The locomotives are a working collection of 1880s and early-1900s steam engines, restored and run by volunteers, hauling wooden carriages with brass fittings.

The most popular trip is the run from St-Valéry's lower station across the estuary to Le Crotoy, taking about an hour through marshland and salt meadow. In summer there are several departures a day; in winter the service is much reduced and the timetable is worth checking before you arrive. A round trip with a lunch stop in Le Crotoy makes an easy half day, and children love the open windows and the wind off the bay.
