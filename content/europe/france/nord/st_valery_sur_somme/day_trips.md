---
title: Day Trips
type: section
---

St-Valéry is well placed for a wider exploration of the Baie de Somme and the Picardy coast. The heritage railway is itself a day trip — the run across to **Le Crotoy** on the north shore takes about an hour and lands you in a smaller fishing village with a long beach and a row of seafood cafés.

**Cayeux-sur-Mer**, on the open Channel coast about 15 km south, is famous for its three-kilometre boardwalk lined with painted beach huts. The **Pointe du Hourdel** is a short detour from Cayeux and the best spot for seal watching at low tide.

Inland, **Abbeville** (25 km east) has the surviving facade of the Gothic collegiate church of Saint-Vulfran, badly damaged in 1940 but still worth seeing. Further south, the gardens at the **Château de Bagatelle** are open to visitors in summer. For something more sobering, the World War I battlefields of the **Somme** — Beaumont-Hamel, Thiepval, the Newfoundland Memorial — are a 90-minute drive inland and make a substantial day out.
