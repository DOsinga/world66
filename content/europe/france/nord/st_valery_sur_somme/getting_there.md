---
title: Getting There
type: section
---

St-Valéry has no station of its own on the main French rail network — the heritage steam line that runs into town is for tourism, not transport. The practical access points are:

**By car.** From Paris, take the A16 motorway north to exit 24 (Abbeville), then the D40 west — about two and a half hours total. From Calais or Boulogne the drive is shorter, around an hour and a half down the A16. The town has several car parks on the edge of the centre; do not try to drive into the upper-town lanes.

**By train and bus.** The nearest mainline station is **Noyelles-sur-Mer**, on the Paris-Saint-Lazare to Boulogne line, about 6 km away. Trains from Paris take roughly two hours. From Noyelles, a local bus or taxi covers the last stretch, or you can take the heritage steam railway across the bay on certain summer schedules.

**From the UK.** The Eurotunnel terminal at Calais and the ferry ports at Calais, Dunkerque, and Boulogne are all within easy driving distance. St-Valéry makes a pleasant first stop for travellers heading south into France.
