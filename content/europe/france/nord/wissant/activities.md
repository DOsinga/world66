Wissant's whole economy in summer is wind and water. The bay picks up any north-westerly swell and holds a cross-shore breeze most days, which makes it the main teaching beach on the Opal Coast for surf, kitesurf and wingfoil alike. Lessons run roughly April to mid-November; outside that the water is cold enough that only the wetsuited faithful bother.

Board and wetsuit hire is available from the same schools that teach, and both operate directly off the beach rather than from a shopfront in the village.
