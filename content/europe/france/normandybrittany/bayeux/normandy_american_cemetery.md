The Normandy American Cemetery and Memorial stands on the bluff above Omaha Beach, where the scale of the D-Day landings is reduced to row after row of white marble crosses and Stars of David. More than 9,000 American service members are buried here, most killed during the Normandy campaign, and the site is kept with the formal precision that makes the silence feel even sharper.

It is outside Bayeux, but it belongs naturally on a Bayeux itinerary because the town is the main base for many battlefield visits. Pair it with the beach below, Pointe du Hoc, or the Bayeux war museums, and leave enough time for the visitor center rather than treating the cemetery as a quick photo stop.
