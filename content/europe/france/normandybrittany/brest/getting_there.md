---
title: "Getting There"
type: section
---

Brest has an international airport (Aéroport de Brest Bretagne) about 10 kilometres northeast of the city, with flights to Paris, London, and other European cities. Air France, Ryanair, and Volotea all serve the airport.

By train, Brest station is the western terminus of the TGV line from Paris Montparnasse, with a journey time of about 4 hours. Regional TER trains connect Brest to [Quimper](/europe/france/normandybrittany/quimper), [Rennes](/europe/france/normandybrittany/rennes), and other Breton cities.

By car, the N12 from Paris takes around 5-6 hours. The road crosses the full breadth of Brittany and is mostly fast dual carriageway.
