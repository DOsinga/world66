The whole Côte Fleurie is within easy striking distance. [Trouville](/europe/france/normandybrittany/trouville), across the bridge over the Touques, is essentially the same town with a fishing harbour and rougher edges — and is often the better lunch destination. [Honfleur](/europe/france/normandybrittany/honfleur), 20 minutes east, has the pretty timber-and-slate harbour painted by Boudin and Monet. Further inland, [Lisieux](/europe/france/normandybrittany/lisieux) and the cider and Calvados producers of the Pays d'Auge make for a good driving loop through orchard country.
