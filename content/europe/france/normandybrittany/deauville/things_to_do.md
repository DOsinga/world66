title: Things to Do
type: section
---

Deauville punches above its weight for a small resort town. The horse racing at [Clairefontaine](/europe/france/normandybrittany/deauville/clairefontaine_racecourse) and [La Touques](/europe/france/normandybrittany/deauville/la_touques_racecourse) is genuinely world-class, the [casino](/europe/france/normandybrittany/deauville/casino_barriere) is a spectacle worth walking through even if you don't gamble, and the surrounding park and gardens reward a slower afternoon. The [Promenade des Planches](/europe/france/normandybrittany/deauville/promenade_des_planches) is the obvious walk, and the September [American Film Festival](/europe/france/normandybrittany/deauville/american_film_festival) briefly turns the town glamorous. Active visitors will find golf, sailing, and thalassotherapy all within easy reach.
