---
title: "Getting There"
type: section
---

Dinan is in Brittany's Côtes-d'Armor department, about 50 km west of [Saint-Malo](/europe/france/normandybrittany/saint_malo) and 90 km north of [Rennes](/europe/france/normandybrittany/rennes).

By train, Dinan station has TER Bretagne services to Saint-Brieuc and Dol-de-Bretagne, from where connections run to [Rennes](/europe/france/normandybrittany/rennes), Paris, and the main Breton cities. The station is about 15 minutes' walk from the old town. Note that service is limited — there is only one through-train per day on most routes, so check schedules carefully.

By car, Dinan is straightforward to reach on the N176 from [Saint-Malo](/europe/france/normandybrittany/saint_malo) or the D766 from Rennes via Lamballe. Parking is available on the edge of the old town; the streets inside are too narrow for easy driving.

In summer, boat services run from [Saint-Malo](/europe/france/normandybrittany/saint_malo) down the Rance estuary to the port below the town — a scenic approach.
