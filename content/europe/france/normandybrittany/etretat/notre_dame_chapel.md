A small whitewashed chapel on the eastern cliff dedicated, like many along the Channel coast, to sailors. The current building dates from 1854 and replaces a much older oratory destroyed in the French Revolution. Two stained-glass windows and a few model fishing boats hung from the rafters make up the interior; the location is the appeal.

It is a five-minute uphill walk from the seafront and combines well with the Falaise d'Amont, the aviators' monument, and the topiary gardens — all within a few minutes of each other on the eastern cliff.
