---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

Étretat is at its best **late April to early June and again in September**. The weather is generally fine, the gardens are open, the cliffs are walkable, and the crowds are bearable. **July and August** are the peak — the town fills, parking gets hard, and the beach can disappear under bodies. Restaurants take more advance booking than they would in a city.

**Winter** is wild and atmospheric but limited: many of the smaller shops, museums, and restaurants close from November to March, and the cliff paths are slippery. The chalk and the spray under a winter storm are beautiful, though, and you may have the cliff-tops to yourself.

Whatever season you visit, **check the tide tables** before you go. The arches and the base of the cliffs are only safely accessible around low tide, and the difference here is dramatic — over 8 metres at spring tides. Many of the best photographs are taken at low water.
