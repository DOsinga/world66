---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

Falaise is a year-round destination but best visited from May to September when the castle opens its full range of hours and the surrounding countryside is at its greenest. July and August bring the most visitors, though Falaise never gets crowded by Normandy coast standards — the major D-Day tourist traffic stays closer to the beaches.

Normandy's climate is wet and changeable. Rain is possible any time of year; even summer days can turn grey. Pack a layer. The Saturday market runs all year, but the summer edition has the widest selection of produce and the most atmosphere.
