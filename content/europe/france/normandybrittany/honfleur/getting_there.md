---
title: "Getting There"
type: section
---

Honfleur has no train station of its own — the line was lifted decades ago. The nearest TGV/Intercités hub is Lisieux (about 30 km south), with frequent buses on the Bus Verts du Calvados line 20 from Lisieux station to Honfleur. Coming from Paris, the easier option is the regional bus from Le Havre or Caen, both reached by train from Saint-Lazare in around two hours.

By car, Honfleur is on the A29 motorway, with the spectacular Pont de Normandie crossing the Seine from Le Havre. Parking around the harbour is paid; the Bassin de l'Est car park is the most central. Cruise ships call at the deep-water quay east of the centre on summer days.
