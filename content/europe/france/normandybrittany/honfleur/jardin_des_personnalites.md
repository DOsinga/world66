Le Jardin des Personnalites is a landscaped garden near the Seine estuary, laid out with busts of artists, writers, musicians, and public figures connected to Honfleur. It is not a formal museum but an open-air reminder of how much cultural history has passed through this small harbour town.

The garden is best treated as a slow walk after the Vieux Bassin, especially when the old port is crowded. The paths, lawns, and water views give Honfleur some breathing room, while the sculptures point back to the painters and writers who helped turn the town's harbour light into a subject in its own right.
