A deliberately strange museum in the half-timbered house where Erik Satie spent his childhood. Rather than a conventional biographical display, the visit is a sound-and-image walk: visitors wear headphones that guide them from room to room through floating pears, winged chairs, and self-playing pianos, soundtracked by Satie's music and surrealist narration.

It will divide your travelling companions — some will find it captivating, others tiresome. Allow about an hour. Closed Tuesdays.
