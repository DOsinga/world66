---
title: "Getting Around"
type: section
---

The Perret city centre is compact and best explored on foot — from the Église Saint-Joseph to the MuMa waterfront is about 25 minutes' walk. The grid layout makes orientation straightforward: Avenue Foch points west to the sea, the station is at the eastern end of the centre.

**By bus and tram:** Le Havre has a tram network (line A and B) and an extensive bus network operated by LIA. Trams run east–west along the southern edge of the city centre; tickets are valid across tram and bus. A one-day pass is good value if you plan to visit Graville Abbey or the docks area.

**By funicular:** The Funiculaire du Havre (free) links the lower city to the clifftop plateau in three minutes. Useful for reaching the upper residential neighbourhoods and the views over the estuary.

**By bike:** The city has a reasonable cycling infrastructure and a self-service bike scheme (Le Vélo). The flat terrain of the Perret district is bike-friendly; the climb to the plateau less so.

**By taxi:** Taxis are available at the station and at stands near the ferry terminal. Ride-hailing apps also operate.
