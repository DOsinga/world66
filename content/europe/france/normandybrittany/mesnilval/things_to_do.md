---
title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Mesnil-Val's appeal is almost entirely physical: the beach, the cliffs, and the coastal path. At low tide the rock pools beneath the chalk cliffs are excellent for exploring. The beach itself is pebble with stretches of sand that appear as the tide drops, and the water is cold but swimmable in summer.

The GR21 coastal footpath passes through the village, connecting north towards Criel-sur-Mer and south towards Dieppe. This section of the path, along the cliff tops between Criel and Le Tréport, is one of the quieter stretches — the views are good but the popular sections closer to Dieppe and Étretat draw the crowds. A morning walk in either direction and back is satisfying without needing transport.
