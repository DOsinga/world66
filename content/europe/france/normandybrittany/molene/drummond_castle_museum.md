The Drummond Castle Museum tells the story of the British liner wrecked near Molène in 1896, when more than 240 people died in one of the Iroise Sea's major maritime disasters. It is a small museum, but the subject belongs here: Molène's history has always been tied to shipwrecks, rescue, and difficult waters.

Opening can be seasonal, so check locally before building your whole visit around it.
