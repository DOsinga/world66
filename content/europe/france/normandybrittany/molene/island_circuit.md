The island circuit is Molène's essential walk. The island is only about a kilometre across, so the route is not difficult, but it gives you the full sequence of harbour, low houses, grass, stone walls, beaches, and open sea.

Take your time rather than treating it as a lap to finish. The views shift constantly toward Ouessant, the lighthouses, and the scatter of low islets around Molène.
