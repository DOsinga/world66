Ledenez Vraz is the tidal islet facing Molene's harbour. At low tide, and only when the tide coefficient is high enough, you can walk across from the main island for a close look at the shore and the surrounding archipelago.

Treat it as a tide-dependent walk, not a casual stroll. Ask locally before crossing, and leave plenty of time to return before the water cuts the route off.
