---
title: When to Go
type: section
---

Morlaix and northern Finistère have a mild, damp Atlantic climate. Rain is possible at any time of year, but the summer months (June–August) are the driest and warmest, with temperatures in the low-to-mid twenties. The coast is a few degrees cooler than inland.

Spring (April–May) is a good time: the Parc Botanique is at its best, the crowds are thin, and the light is clear. Summer sees the main tourist season, with markets and outdoor events in the town. Autumn and winter are quiet — some sites reduce hours or close — but the town has a genuine working-town character that makes it pleasant without the seasonal overlay.

The Saturday morning market at Place des Otages is a fixture year-round and is one of the best reasons to time your arrival accordingly.
