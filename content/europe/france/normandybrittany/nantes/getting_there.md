---
title: "Getting There"
type: section
---

Nantes has excellent connections. The TGV from Paris Montparnasse takes about 2 hours and trains run frequently throughout the day. Nantes-Atlantique airport serves destinations across France and Europe. There is regular bus service to and from Paris and other cities. By road, Nantes is about 380 kilometres from Paris and 2 hours from Rennes.
