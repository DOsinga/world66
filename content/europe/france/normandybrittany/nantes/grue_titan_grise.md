The Grue Titan Grise is one of the giant shipyard cranes preserved on the Île de Nantes, a reminder that this now-creative district was once heavy industrial ground. Its scale makes sense of the island's wide quays and open spaces.

See it as part of a walk between Les Machines de l'île, the Loire riverfront, and the old shipyard slipways. Nantes is good at turning industrial remains into public scenery, and this crane is one of the clearest examples.
