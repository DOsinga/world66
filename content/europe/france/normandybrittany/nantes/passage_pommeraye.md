The Passage Pommeraye is a three-level shopping arcade from 1843 that is widely considered one of the most beautiful in Europe. What makes it remarkable is the way it descends three floors through connecting staircases decorated with cast-iron balusters, bronze candelabras, and allegorical figures, all topped by a glass roof that bathes the whole in cool northern light. Walking down through the levels feels like descending through a jewel box.

The arcade runs between the Rue de la Fosse and the Rue Crébillon in the heart of the city centre, and it still functions as a shopping arcade — you can buy clothes, books, and gifts in its boutiques. The best time to visit is on a quiet weekday morning when the light is good and the other visitors are few.
