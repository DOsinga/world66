title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Pontrieux's main attraction is its extraordinary collection of [lavoirs](/europe/france/normandybrittany/pontrieux/lavoirs_de_pontrieux) — private washhouses along the River Trieux. The [Trieux river cruise](/europe/france/normandybrittany/pontrieux/trieux_river_cruise) runs downstream through wooded valleys to the coast from spring to autumn. The weekly market and the medieval tower of Château de la Roche Jagu nearby are worth combining into a day in the area.
