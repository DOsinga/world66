---
title: Eating Out
type: section
---

Quimper takes its food seriously, as you'd expect of a city in one of France's strongest culinary regions. Crêpes and galettes (savoury buckwheat crêpes) are the baseline — but the restaurants here go well beyond the tourist creperie. Look for fresh fish from the nearby ports, Breton lamb raised on salt marshes, and artisan ciders from the Cornouaille orchards.
