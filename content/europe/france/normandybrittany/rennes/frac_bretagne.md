Frac Bretagne is Rennes' contemporary-art collection and exhibition centre, part of France's regional FRAC network. The focus is changing exhibitions rather than a fixed old-master collection, so the experience depends on what is currently installed.

It is a good stop for visitors who want Rennes beyond the medieval streets and parliament building. Check the exhibition programme first; when the show is strong, the centre adds a useful modern counterweight to the historic city.
