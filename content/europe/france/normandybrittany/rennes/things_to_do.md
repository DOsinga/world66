---
title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Rennes is compact enough that the main sights cluster within a twenty-minute walk of each other. The historic core — half-timbered houses around [Place des Lices](/europe/france/normandybrittany/rennes/place_des_lices) and the cobbled streets of [Quartier Saint-Anne](/europe/france/normandybrittany/rennes/quartier_saint_anne) — is the obvious starting point. The [Parlement de Bretagne](/europe/france/normandybrittany/rennes/parlement_de_bretagne) is one of the finest 17th-century buildings in France outside Paris, and the [Musée des Beaux-Arts](/europe/france/normandybrittany/rennes/musee_des_beaux_arts) holds a genuinely strong collection. The [Jardin du Thabor](/europe/france/normandybrittany/rennes/jardin_du_thabor) gives you a broad, formal garden in the English style, and the outdoor [Sculpture by Claudio Parmiggiani](/europe/france/normandybrittany/rennes/fontaine_parmiggiani) in Place du Parlement is worth seeking out for its peculiar history.
