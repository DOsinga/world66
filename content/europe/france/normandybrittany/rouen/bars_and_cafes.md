---
title: "Bars and Cafes"
type: section
---

Rouen's drinking-and-coffee life centres on the Place du Vieux-Marché and the streets around the cathedral. A scattering of small bars in the Saint-Marc neighbourhood, behind Saint-Maclou, caters to a younger crowd. Cider, Calvados, and the Norman aperitif Pommeau all appear on the better lists alongside the more usual wine and beer.
