A long-running brasserie-café on the Place du Vieux-Marché, with a big covered terrace looking across at the modern Joan of Arc church. The food is solid brasserie standard — steak-frites, croque-monsieur, salad — but the location and the people-watching are the main reasons to sit down.

Useful for breakfast and for late drinks; the kitchen runs longer hours than most.
