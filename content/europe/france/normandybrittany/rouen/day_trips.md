---
title: "Day Trips"
type: section
---

Rouen sits in the middle of upper Normandy and makes a good base for the alabaster cliffs of the Côte d'Albâtre to the north (Étretat, Fécamp, Dieppe), the Seine valley to the west (Jumièges Abbey, Honfleur), or Monet's house and garden at Giverny to the east. None is more than 90 minutes away by car.
