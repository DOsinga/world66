Rouen sits in the middle of upper Normandy and makes a good base. To the north the alabaster cliffs of the Côte d'Albâtre run from Dieppe down to [Étretat](/europe/france/normandybrittany/etretat), whose chalk arches and needle are the most painted seascape in the region. West along the Seine valley lie the soaring ruins of Jumièges Abbey and the harbour town of [Honfleur](/europe/france/normandybrittany/honfleur), with its shipwrights' church and yacht-filled basin. To the east, near Vernon, Monet's house and water garden at [Giverny](/europe/france/normandybrittany/giverny) draw a steady stream of visitors from spring through autumn. None is more than 90 minutes away by car.
