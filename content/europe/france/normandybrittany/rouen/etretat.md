The white chalk cliffs and natural arches at Étretat, an hour northwest of Rouen on the Channel coast, are the most photographed seascape in Normandy. The Falaise d'Aval with its slim arch and the further Aiguille needle were painted by Monet, Courbet, and a dozen lesser names; the cliffs above offer a long, exposed walking path with the open sea on one side and grazed pasture on the other.

The small town below is now overwhelmed in summer; come in spring or autumn for the views, and walk along the clifftops away from the crowds.
