Claude Monet's house and gardens at Giverny lie about 70 km southeast of Rouen, in the Seine valley. The pink-walled house is preserved as he furnished it, complete with his collection of Japanese prints; the gardens — the flower garden in front, the water garden with the famous bridge behind — are at their peak from April to September.

Most visitors come from Paris, which makes early morning a particular pleasure if you can manage it; the crowds build through the day. Combine with the Musée des Impressionnismes nearby for a fuller half-day. Drive or take the train from Rouen to Vernon (40 minutes), then a shuttle bus.
