A small modern restaurant a short walk from the cathedral, La Pyramide does a short, modern French menu with strong Norman touches — cream, cider, apples, fish from Dieppe — at fair prices for the standard. The dining room is quiet and the service is friendly without being intrusive.

A useful contrast to the more touristy spots on the Place du Vieux-Marché. Book a day or two ahead.
