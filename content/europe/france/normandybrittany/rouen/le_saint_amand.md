A small wine bar in the Saint-Marc quarter, behind Saint-Maclou, with a thoughtful list of Loire and Burgundy bottles by the glass and a short menu of charcuterie and cheese plates. The crowd is local — Rouennais professionals in their thirties and forties — and the atmosphere is unhurried.

A good place to end the evening after dinner. Opens late afternoon, closes around midnight.
