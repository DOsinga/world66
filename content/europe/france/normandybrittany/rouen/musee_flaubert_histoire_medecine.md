The Musee Flaubert et d'Histoire de la Medecine occupies the former Hotel-Dieu complex where Gustave Flaubert was born in 1821. The setting is unusually apt: his father was a surgeon there, so the museum naturally joins literary biography with the history of medicine.

This is a quieter Rouen stop than the cathedral or Joan of Arc sites, but it adds an intimate layer to the city. Rooms, medical objects, and the birthplace itself make Flaubert feel rooted in a real provincial household rather than floating above Rouen as a name from literature class.
