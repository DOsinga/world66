The Rance Tidal Power Station, about 4km south of Saint-Malo, is the world's first large-scale tidal power plant and still one of the largest. Built between 1961 and 1966, the dam crosses the Rance estuary near the village of La Richardais, and the road across the top serves as the main connection between Saint-Malo and Dinard — cutting what was once a long detour to a two-minute drive.

The dam can be viewed from the road or from a small visitor area at La Richardais. The full visitor experience, when available, includes a tour of the turbine hall deep inside the dam. It is an extraordinary piece of 1960s engineering: 24 turbines, each reversible to generate power on both flood and ebb tides, in a structure that looks more like a piece of infrastructure than a monument — which is exactly what it is.
