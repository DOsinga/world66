---
title: "Getting There"
type: section
---

Trouville shares a station with Deauville — Trouville-Deauville — on the south side of the Touques estuary, a 10-minute walk from Trouville's centre. Direct trains from Paris Saint-Lazare take around two hours, with several services a day. The connection is well-suited to a weekend trip.

By car, Trouville is about two and a half hours from Paris on the A13. Parking near the beach is difficult on summer weekends; park further out and walk in.
