---
title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Trouville's pleasures are simple — the beach, the boardwalk, the fish market, and a couple of small museums. Most of what you came for is along the Touques estuary and the seafront promenade, which can be walked end to end in 20 minutes. The Belle Époque architecture along the boardwalk and the casino are worth the slow look.
