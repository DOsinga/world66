---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

July and August are the busy months, when Parisians fill the town and the seafront is at its liveliest. The weather is variable rather than reliably hot — bring a light jacket even in summer. June and September are calmer and often clearer, with quieter beaches and more available restaurant tables.

Out of season, the town remains charming. October to March is grey and damp but the markets and seafood are excellent year-round. The American Film Festival in early September across the river in Deauville fills both towns to bursting; either time your visit for it deliberately or avoid it.
