---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

May, June, and September are the best months — mild weather, the Norman countryside at its greenest, and a fraction of the summer crowds that descend on Honfleur and Deauville. The cliff paths are at their best in late spring when the hedgerows and orchards are in bloom. July and August are busy on this stretch of coast; Villerville is quieter than its neighbours but still well visited, and accommodation fills up.

Winter is cold and wet, with most of the seasonal businesses closed. The cliff path is fine year-round in good weather, and the village has a certain appeal out of season when it returns to something closer to its normal self — but plan for limited options for food and a short day. The sea light in November and December has its own quality; the Impressionists who worked on this coast painted it in all seasons.
