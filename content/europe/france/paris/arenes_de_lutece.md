The Arènes de Lutèce are the remains of a Roman amphitheatre from ancient Lutetia, tucked into a quiet park in the Latin Quarter. The arena once held thousands of spectators; today you can still make out the seating, stage area, and traces of the oval performance space.

It is a short, worthwhile stop for Roman Paris, especially when paired with the nearby Musée de Cluny and the Left Bank streets around the Panthéon.
