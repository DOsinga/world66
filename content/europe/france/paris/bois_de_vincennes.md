Bois de Vincennes is the great green escape on the eastern edge of Paris, large enough for a half-day of wandering without feeling as if you are still in the city. Its paths run past lakes, wooded lawns, rowing boats, birdlife, and the occasional odd surprise, including a Buddhist temple.

The park works best when you are not in a hurry. Rent a boat on Lac Daumesnil, cross the footbridges to the small islands, or combine the park with the nearby Palais de la Porte Doree for a quieter day away from the central museum crush.
