Hemingway's memoir of life in Paris in the 1920s, written thirty years later and published posthumously. He walks the Left Bank, drinks at the Closerie des Lilas, and keeps company with Fitzgerald, Stein, and Pound. Lyrically written and occasionally dishonest, but essential reading for anyone walking Saint-Germain or the Latin Quarter.
