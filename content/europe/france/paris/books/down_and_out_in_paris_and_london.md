Orwell's account of poverty in 1920s Paris, working as a plongeur in restaurant kitchens and sleeping in doss-houses. One of the most vivid portraits of the city ever written, and still recognisable in parts — the grinding hierarchy of restaurant work, the particular shame of being broke in a city that runs on money.
