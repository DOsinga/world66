Balzac's great novel of ambition and sacrifice in Restoration Paris, centred on a boarding house in the Latin Quarter where a ruined noodle merchant, an ambitious young law student, and a mysterious escaped convict live in uneasy proximity. The social hierarchies of early 19th-century Paris are drawn with the precision of a sociologist. The streets and neighbourhoods Balzac describes are still there.
