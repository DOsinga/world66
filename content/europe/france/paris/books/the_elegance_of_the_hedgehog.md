A French bestseller set in a Haussmann apartment building in the 7th arrondissement, told through the alternating voices of a 54-year-old concierge who secretly reads philosophy and a precocious 12-year-old resident. It is a novel about class, intelligence, and the hidden life of Paris behind grand facades — and a quietly devastating portrait of how the city stratifies its inhabitants.
