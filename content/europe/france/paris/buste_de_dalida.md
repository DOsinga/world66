This small bronze bust remembers Dalida, the Egyptian-born French-Italian singer who became one of Montmartre's best-loved icons. It stands on Place Dalida, near the pretty Rue de l'Abreuvoir, and makes an easy stop while wandering the quieter slopes behind Sacre-Coeur.
