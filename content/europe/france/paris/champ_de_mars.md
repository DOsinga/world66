The formal park stretching from the Eiffel Tower south to the École Militaire. Not a great park by itself — too manicured, too flat — but the position is unbeatable. A picnic on the grass with the Tower overhead is a Parisian rite of passage. After dark, the Tower's light show runs for several minutes on every hour from dusk until 1am (2am in summer). The best view is from here, lying on your back on the grass. Bring wine.
