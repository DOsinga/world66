The Cinema Museum is part of the Cinémathèque Française complex in Bercy, housed in a modern building by Frank Gehry. Exhibits cover film history through cameras, costumes, posters, models, and other objects from French and international cinema.

It is a strong indoor stop for film fans, and the changing exhibitions are often the best reason to check the programme before going.
