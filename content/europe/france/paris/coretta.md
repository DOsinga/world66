Named, like the park it faces, for Coretta Scott King, this is a polished contemporary bistro on the edge of the Clichy-Batignolles development. Big windows look onto the greenery of the Parc Martin Luther King, and the kitchen turns out refined seasonal French cooking — the kind of ambitious neighbourhood restaurant that has followed the young families into the quarter.

It is a good lunch or dinner stop when exploring the newer, northern end of Batignolles, pairing naturally with a walk in the park across the street. Book at weekends, when the dining room and its terrace fill with locals.
