Orwell's account of poverty in 1920s Paris, working as a plongeur in restaurant kitchens and sleeping in doss-houses. One of the most vivid portraits of the city ever written, and still recognisable in parts. The Paris sections describe a world of underground kitchens, petty cruelty, and extraordinary solidarity among the destitute that remains compelling nearly a century on.
