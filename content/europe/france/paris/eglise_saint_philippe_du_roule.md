Église Saint-Philippe-du-Roule is a late 18th-century Neoclassical church near the Champs-Élysées. Its calm, symmetrical interior was inspired by early Christian basilicas, and it is a worthwhile quiet stop while walking between the Arc de Triomphe and central Paris.
