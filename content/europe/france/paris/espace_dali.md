Espace Dali is a small Montmartre museum devoted to Salvador Dali's sculptural work and graphic pieces. Many works are reproductions, so it is not a substitute for a major Dali collection, but it can be a worthwhile short stop for surrealism fans already visiting Sacre-Coeur and Place du Tertre.
