The Guimet Museum is one of Paris's great specialist museums, devoted to Asian art from Afghanistan, India, Southeast Asia, China, Korea, Japan, and the Himalayan world. It is less crowded than the Louvre or Orsay, but the collection has real depth: Khmer sculpture, Buddhist statuary, Japanese prints, Chinese ceramics, and ritual objects that reward slow looking.

The museum suits travelers who want Paris beyond the usual French canon. Its location near the Trocadero makes it easy to combine with the Eiffel Tower area, but give it proper time rather than treating it as filler. The galleries are calm, scholarly, and beautiful without feeling remote.
