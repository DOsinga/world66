La Cité de la Mode et du Design, also known as Les Docks, is hard to miss on the Seine because of the bright green structure wrapped around its riverside facade. The building mixes design exhibitions, event spaces, restaurants, and nightlife.

Come for the architecture and the riverfront rather than expecting a conventional museum. It works well as part of a walk through the newer eastern side of central Paris.
