Maison Europeenne de la Photographie is a major contemporary photography museum in a restored 18th-century hotel particulier in the Marais. It stages several large exhibitions each year and also holds an archive of thousands of photographic works.

It is a strong stop for visitors interested in modern photography, with changing shows, a library, an auditorium, and a cafe.
