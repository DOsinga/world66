The Musée Curie preserves the offices and laboratories where Pierre and Marie Curie worked on radioactivity. Instruments, furniture, documents, and photographs make the science feel close and human rather than abstract.

It is a small museum, but a rewarding one if you are interested in physics, medicine, or the lives behind Paris's intellectual history. Its location near the Panthéon and the Latin Quarter makes it easy to fold into a wider walk.
