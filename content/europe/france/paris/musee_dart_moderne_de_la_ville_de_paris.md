Musee d'Art Moderne de la Ville de Paris occupies the eastern wing of the Palais de Tokyo and holds the city's modern art collection. The works run from the early 20th century onward, making it a strong stop near Trocadero for visitors who want a major museum without defaulting to the Louvre or Orsay.
