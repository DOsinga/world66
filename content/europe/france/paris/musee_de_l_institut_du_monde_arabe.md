The Musee de l'Institut du Monde Arabe is part of the Arab World Institute, on the Seine near the eastern edge of the Latin Quarter. Its exhibitions focus on Arab art, history, and culture.

The building is also known for its freely accessible rooftop, which gives a broad view over the Seine, Notre-Dame, and the northern half of Paris.
