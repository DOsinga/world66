Musee du quai Branly is one of Paris's major museums, focused on arts and cultures from Africa, Asia, Oceania, and the Americas. The collection is large, with tribal art, masks, textiles, sculpture, and multimedia displays.

It sits close to the Eiffel Tower but rewards a slower visit of its own. Allow at least a couple of hours, and more if you want to work through the permanent collection in depth.
