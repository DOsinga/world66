Musée Grévin is Paris's classic wax museum, with hundreds of figures staged in scenes from French history, theatre, sport, politics, and popular culture. The displays are deliberately theatrical rather than academic, making it an easy rainy-day stop for families or anyone curious about a very Parisian form of spectacle.
