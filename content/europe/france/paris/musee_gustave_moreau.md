Musee Gustave Moreau occupies the former home and studio of the Symbolist painter Gustave Moreau. The museum keeps the focus tightly on his life and work, with dense displays that feel close to an artist's private archive.

It is a rewarding stop for visitors interested in Symbolism, 19th-century French art, or smaller Paris museums away from the busiest routes.
