The Musée Marmottan Monet is far enough from the central museum crush to feel like a discovery, even though its collection is anything but minor. Its Monet holdings are the headline, including *Impression, Sunrise*, the painting whose title gave Impressionism its name, along with late water-lily works and views from Giverny.

The setting helps. Instead of the grand procession of the Louvre or Orsay, you move through a former private house near the Bois de Boulogne, where Napoleonic-era rooms and intimate galleries make the art feel close. For Monet in Paris, this is not optional background; it is one of the essential stops.
