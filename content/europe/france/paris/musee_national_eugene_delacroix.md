The Musee national Eugene Delacroix occupies the painter's last home and studio, where he lived from 1857 until his death in 1863. The museum shows works by Delacroix, material connected to his life, and exhibitions on artists from his circle.

It is a quiet, focused museum and works best as a Saint-Germain stop rather than a full afternoon.
