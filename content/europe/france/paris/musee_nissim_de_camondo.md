Musee Nissim de Camondo is an early 20th-century mansion museum beside Parc Monceau, arranged as an 18th-century aristocratic home. Its furniture, porcelain, and decorative arts were left largely intact, making the house a polished time capsule of elite Parisian life before the First World War.
