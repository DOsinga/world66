The Paris Observatory is one of the city's quieter scientific landmarks, built in the 17th century for royal astronomy and aligned with the old Paris meridian. It is not a casual drop-in museum, but the building and its history make it worth noting if you are walking between Montparnasse, the Catacombs, and the Luxembourg Gardens.
