Opera Bastille is Paris's modern opera house, built beside Place de la Bastille and opened in 1989. The glass-fronted building is a major venue for opera and ballet, and it is worth checking the program before a trip if you want to see a performance in one of the city's landmark contemporary cultural buildings.
