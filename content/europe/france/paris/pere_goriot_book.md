Balzac's great novel of ambition and sacrifice in Restoration Paris. The boarding house in the Latin Quarter and the rigidly stratified social world of the period are drawn with the precision of a sociologist. Young Rastignac's arc from provincial student to calculating social climber, played out against the tragic generosity of Old Goriot, is one of the great plots in European fiction. The final scene, in which Rastignac looks out over Paris from Père Lachaise and issues his famous challenge to the city, is unforgettable.
