Place des Victoires is one of Paris's most elegant formal squares, a near-perfect circle framed by 17th-century facades. The equestrian statue of Louis XIV gives it the expected royal theatre, but the real pleasure is the geometry: step in from the surrounding streets and the city suddenly snaps into order.
