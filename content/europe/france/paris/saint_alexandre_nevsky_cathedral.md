Saint-Alexandre-Nevsky Cathedral is a 19th-century Russian Orthodox cathedral near the Arc de Triomphe. Its gilded onion domes and richly decorated interior make it a distinctive stop for visitors interested in Paris's Russian history and religious architecture.
