Saint-Augustin is a striking 19th-century church built during Haussmann's reshaping of Paris. Its design mixes Gothic and Romanesque forms with an iron frame, giving the interior a broad, open feel that differs from older Paris churches. The church is easy to combine with a walk between Saint-Lazare and the Arc de Triomphe area.
