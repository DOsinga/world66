A short, essayistic guide to walking Paris by the American novelist Edmund White, who lived in the city for sixteen years. Wonderfully opinionated and specific about neighbourhoods — the medieval Jewish quarter, the Arab streets, the transvestite bars of the Marais — White writes about Paris as an insider who never lost the curiosity of an outsider. The best companion for anyone who wants to wander the city without a fixed itinerary.
