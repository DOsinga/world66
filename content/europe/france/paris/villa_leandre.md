Villa Léandre is a short residential street in Montmartre with small front gardens and an unusually English feel. Its quiet houses are a contrast to the busier lanes around Sacré-Cœur and the Abbesses area.

It is a brief detour, not a major sight, but it rewards slow wandering through the back streets of Montmartre. Go respectfully, as it is still a residential street.
