The World Heritage Centre is part of UNESCO's Paris headquarters, a postwar international complex that also holds a large collection of modern and contemporary art. Works by major 20th-century artists are spread through the building and grounds, making it more interesting than a normal office visit.

It is not a casual drop-in sight, so check current access before going. When visits are available, the appeal is the mix of architecture, diplomacy, and art, with views toward the Eiffel Tower from parts of the complex.
