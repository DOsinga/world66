---
title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Port Grimaud's main attraction is the village itself — a pedestrian waterfront labyrinth of canals, bridges, and colourful Provencal houses, each with a mooring ring at the door. Rent an electric boat to explore from the water. The Church of Saint-Francois-d'Assise, with its Vasarely stained glass and rooftop terrace, is the architectural highlight.
