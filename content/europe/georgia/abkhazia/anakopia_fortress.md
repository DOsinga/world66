Anakopia Fortress crowns the hill above New Athos, on the site of an early medieval stronghold that once guarded the Abkhaz capital. The ruins include walls, a tower, and remains of an ancient temple.

The paved uphill walk takes about an hour and is rewarded with wide views over the coast and surrounding hills.
