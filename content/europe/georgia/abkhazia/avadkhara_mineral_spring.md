Avadkhara Mineral Spring is a high mountain spring above Lake Ritsa, reached by rough road through the Western Caucasus. The spring is known for its cold mineral water and the alpine setting around it, with a small cafe near the lower spring.

The road is part of the experience and part of the warning: it is rough enough that a high-clearance vehicle is the sensible choice. Combine it with a Lake Ritsa trip rather than treating it as a quick detour from the coast.
