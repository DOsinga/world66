The Beslet Arcaded Bridge is a medieval stone bridge over the Besletka River near Sukhumi. It is traditionally dated to the 12th century, during the reign of Queen Tamar of Georgia.

The bridge is a short, focused stop rather than a large site, but its age and setting make it worthwhile for travellers interested in Abkhazia's medieval Georgian layer.
