The Cathedral of the Annunciation is a neo-Byzantine church in Sukhumi, built between 1909 and 1915 with support from the city's Greek Orthodox community.

Its domes and formal facade make it one of the more visible religious buildings in central Sukhumi. It fits well into a short walk that also takes in the waterfront and botanical garden.
