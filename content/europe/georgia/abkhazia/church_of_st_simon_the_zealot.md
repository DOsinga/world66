The Church of St. Simon the Zealot is a small medieval church in New Athos, traditionally linked to the apostle's death. The building dates to around the 10th century, and traces of 11th-century frescoes were found beneath later plaster.

It is a worthwhile companion stop to New Athos Monastery, especially if you are interested in early Christian sites and older Caucasus church architecture.
