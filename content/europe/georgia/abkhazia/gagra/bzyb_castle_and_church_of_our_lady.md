Bzyb Castle and the Church of Our Lady are ruined medieval remains north-west of Gagra. The church dates from around the 10th or 11th century and still shows the outline of its cross-domed plan, with standing walls, arches, apses, and fragments of ornament.

The site is rougher than Gagra's seaside sights, but that is part of the appeal. It works best for travellers with an interest in Abkhazia's older layers of history and the patience for a short excursion outside town.
