Mamdzyshkha Mountain rises above Gagra to about 1,873 m, giving broad views over the coast, the forested slopes, and the surrounding countryside.

A road climbs toward the summit and passes two observation decks, so you do not have to be a serious hiker to get the main view. Clear weather matters; clouds can swallow the panorama quickly.
