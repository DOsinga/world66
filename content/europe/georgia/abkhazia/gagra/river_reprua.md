The River Reprua is a tiny river near Gagra, often described as one of the shortest rivers in the world. It rises from underground springs close to the coast and runs only a few dozen metres before reaching the Black Sea.

The appeal is the oddity rather than a long visit. Stop if you are already exploring the Gagra coast and want a quick natural curiosity.
