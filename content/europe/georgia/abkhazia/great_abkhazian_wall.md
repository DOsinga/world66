The Great Abkhazian Wall is a long ruined defensive line with the remains of hundreds of towers scattered across the landscape. It is usually linked to early medieval and later Mingrelian-Abkhazian frontier conflicts, and is most interesting as a rough, atmospheric historic landscape rather than as a restored monument.
