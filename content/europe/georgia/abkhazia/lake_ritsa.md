Lake Ritsa is a deep mountain lake in the Western Caucasus at about 900 metres altitude, sitting in a steep wooded basin formed by a landslide some 250 years ago. It is the most-photographed natural site in Abkhazia and the centrepiece of the Ritsa Relict National Park.

The road in from the coast climbs through the Yupshara Canyon, with cliffs rising several hundred metres above the river. The lake itself is encircled by limestone peaks and pine forest, and the air is noticeably colder than at the coast. In Soviet times it was a flagship destination for senior party officials; today it sees Russian rather than international visitors.
