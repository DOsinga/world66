The New Athos Caves are a large karst cave system near New Athos, opened to visitors in the Soviet period. Tours pass through huge underground chambers, and a small train carries visitors into part of the cave complex.

They pair naturally with New Athos Monastery, but allow separate time for the caves; the scale underground is the point of the visit.
