New Athos sits about 20 kilometres up the coast from Sukhumi, on a wooded hillside above the sea. The monastery is a late nineteenth-century complex with a tall striped bell tower and a central cathedral painted in muted ochres and blues, the whole thing visible from far down the coast road.

Below the monastery is the New Athos Cave, a vast natural cavern that the Soviets opened to tourists via a purpose-built underground railway. Both the monastery and the cave still function in their original way, but visiting requires entering Abkhazia, which is closed to most foreign travellers.
