Stalin's dacha near Lake Ritsa is one of several secluded retreats built for him in the Caucasus. The house is tucked into the forest above the lake and preserves the guarded, closed-off atmosphere of a Soviet government residence.

Visitors can usually see the reception rooms, bedrooms, library, and period furniture. It works best as a stop on a Lake Ritsa trip, adding a strange political aftertaste to the mountain scenery.
