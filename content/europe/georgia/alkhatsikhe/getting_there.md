---
title: "Getting There"
type: section
---

Akhaltsikhe is connected by marshrutka (shared minibus) to [Tbilisi](/europe/georgia/tbilisi), with several departures daily from the Didube bus station. The journey takes about 3–4 hours depending on route and traffic. There are also direct marshrutkas to [Borjomi](/europe/georgia/borjomi) (about 1 hour) and Batumi.

For [Vardzia](/europe/georgia/vardzia), marshrutkas run from Akhaltsikhe's local bus station with the last departure typically around 5:30 pm — plan your day accordingly.

There is no passenger rail service to Akhaltsikhe.
