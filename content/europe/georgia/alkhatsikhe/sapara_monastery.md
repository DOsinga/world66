Sapara Monastery sits in the hills southeast of Akhaltsikhe, reached by a road that climbs out of town into forest and open slopes. The monastery complex is medieval, with stone churches, defensive walls, and the kind of quiet mountain setting that makes southern Georgia feel far from the busier tourist routes.

The main church is dedicated to St Saba and contains fresco fragments and carved stone details that reward close attention. Sapara is best visited with your own transport or a hired taxi from Akhaltsikhe, often combined with Rabati Castle in the same day. The contrast between Rabati's restored showpiece and Sapara's more secluded atmosphere is exactly why the trip works.
