Bakuriani is Georgia's main ski resort, sitting at 1,700 metres on the northern slopes of the Trialeti Range, about 30 kilometres from [Borjomi](/europe/georgia/borjomi). It has been a winter destination since Soviet times, and while the infrastructure is dated and the slopes modest by Alpine standards, the resort has a genuine mountain character and draws Georgian families in both winter and summer.

The surrounding landscape is coniferous forest — mostly spruce — and the air is clean and sharp. In winter the place is lively with skiers and snowboarders on the [Kokhta](/europe/georgia/bakuriani/kokhta_ski_area) and [Didveli](/europe/georgia/bakuriani/didveli_ski_area) slopes; in summer the [Alpine Botanical Garden](/europe/georgia/bakuriani/alpine_botanical_garden) and forest trails make it a pleasant escape from the Georgian lowland heat. Getting here is half the adventure: a narrow-gauge railway runs up from Borjomi through the forest, crossing an [Eiffel-designed iron bridge](/europe/georgia/bakuriani/eiffel_railway_bridge) en route — a slow and scenic journey.
