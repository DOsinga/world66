---
title: "Getting There"
type: section
---

Bakuriani is about 30 kilometres from [Borjomi](/europe/georgia/borjomi) and around 200 kilometres from Tbilisi. From Tbilisi, marshrutkas (shared minibuses) run directly to Bakuriani, taking roughly three hours. By car the drive from Tbilisi takes around two and a half hours via the Borjomi valley road.

The most atmospheric way to arrive is on the narrow-gauge railway from Borjomi. The kukushka train runs daily, takes about two hours, and winds through spectacular spruce forest along the Borjomistskali River, crossing the famous Eiffel-designed bridge en route. The train is slower than a marshrutka and runs on Soviet-era rolling stock, but the journey itself is worth the time.
