---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

Bakuriani has two distinct seasons. Winter runs from December to March, when the ski slopes open and the village fills with Georgian families on holiday. Snow is usually reliable from January, though recent winters have been variable — February tends to be the most dependable month for good conditions.

Summer is the other busy period, roughly June to August, when Bakuriani attracts visitors escaping the heat of Tbilisi and the Georgian lowlands. The alpine meadows above the resort are green and wildflower-filled, the botanical garden is at its best, and the temperatures are mild and pleasant. Spring and autumn are quiet and inexpensive, with few crowds, but accommodation choices shrink considerably and some facilities close.
