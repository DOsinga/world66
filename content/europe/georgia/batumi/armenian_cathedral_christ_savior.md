The Armenian Cathedral of Christ the Savior stands in old Batumi, set at an angle to the surrounding street grid. Built in 1885-87, it is a compact but distinctive church with a bell tower and a calm interior.

It is worth a short stop while walking through the old town, especially if you are comparing Batumi's mix of Armenian, Georgian Orthodox, Catholic, Muslim, and Jewish religious buildings.
