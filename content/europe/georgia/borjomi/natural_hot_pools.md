The Natural Hot Pools sit in the forest outside Borjomi and are a popular local picnic and bathing spot.

What was once a single hot pool has been divided into three pools with different temperatures. The setting is simple rather than resort-like, which is part of the appeal after a walk through Borjomi's wooded valley.
