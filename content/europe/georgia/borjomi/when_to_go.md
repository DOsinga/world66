---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

Late spring (May to June) and early autumn (September to October) are the best times to visit Borjomi. The weather is mild, the national park is fully accessible, and the town is busy without being overwhelmed. Summer (July to August) is peak season for Georgian domestic tourists; the town fills up and accommodation prices rise.

Winter sees Borjomi much quieter. The gorge can be beautiful in snow, and Bakuriani's skiing is only 35 kilometres away by the narrow-gauge railway. The national park trails are less accessible but a short walk into the gorge remains possible year-round.
