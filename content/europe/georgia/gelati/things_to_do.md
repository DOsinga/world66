---
title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Gelati Monastery is compact enough to explore thoroughly in a couple of hours, but the quality of what is here rewards a slower pace. The [Church of the Virgin](/europe/georgia/gelati/church_of_the_virgin) — the main cathedral — contains the finest 12th-century mosaics in Georgia: the gold-backed Virgin with Archangels in the central apse has survived nine centuries in remarkable condition. The [Tomb of King David IV](/europe/georgia/gelati/tomb_of_david) is set into the gateway floor so that all visitors walk over it — an act of humility that has been honoured by every pilgrim since the 12th century. The [Gelati Academy ruins](/europe/georgia/gelati/gelati_academy) represent what was once one of the great intellectual centres of the medieval Caucasus.

The nearby [Motsameta Monastery](/europe/georgia/gelati/motsameta_monastery), 4 km away, is smaller and less visited but dramatically positioned on a cliff above a river gorge. It makes an easy combination with Gelati and is the more pilgrimage-charged of the two sites.
