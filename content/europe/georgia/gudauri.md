Gudauri is Georgia's main ski resort, perched at around 2,200 metres on the south-facing slopes of the Greater Caucasus, about two hours' drive north of [Tbilisi](/europe/georgia/tbilisi) along the Georgian Military Highway. The [resort](/europe/georgia/gudauri/gudauri_ski_resort) has runs for all levels — from gentle nursery slopes to serious off-piste terrain — and the snow season typically runs from December to April.

What sets Gudauri apart from better-known European resorts is the price and the emptiness. Lift passes, equipment rental, and accommodation are a fraction of Alpine costs, and even in high season you are unlikely to queue. Heli-skiing is available for those who want untouched powder on the surrounding peaks.

On the drive up from Tbilisi, just before the resort, the [Russia-Georgia Friendship Monument](/europe/georgia/gudauri/russia_georgia_friendship_monument) sits on the edge of the Jvari Pass with panoramic views over the Aragvi gorge — a striking piece of Soviet-era engineering now carrying a more complicated meaning. In summer the area is popular for hiking, paragliding, and as a staging point for trips further up the highway to the [Kazbegi](/europe/georgia/gudauri/kazbegi) region and Mount Kazbek.
