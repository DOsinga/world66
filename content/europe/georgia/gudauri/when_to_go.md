---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

The ski season runs from December through March, with the best snow conditions typically in January and February. March offers good spring skiing with longer daylight hours. April is transition time — the lower runs lose their snow but the top of the mountain holds. May through October, Gudauri is a summer resort: hiking, mountain biking, and paragliding replace skiing. July and August are warm at lower elevations and pleasant on the slopes, but the village is quieter than in winter. Autumn brings spectacular colour to the hills below the resort and is a fine time for hiking with fewer visitors.

The Jvari Pass road can be closed by snowfall in winter. Check conditions before driving up from Tbilisi, and keep weather apps open during your stay.
