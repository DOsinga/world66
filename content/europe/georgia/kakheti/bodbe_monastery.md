Bodbe Monastery sits on a wooded hillside above Sighnaghi, looking out over the Alazani Valley. The monastery is the burial place of St. Nino, the 4th-century woman credited with converting the Georgian royal family to Christianity, and it remains one of the most important pilgrimage sites in the country.

The present basilica mostly dates from the 9th and 11th centuries, though it has been rebuilt and modified many times. The interior frescoes are well preserved, and the tomb of St. Nino lies under a carved stone canopy at the south end of the nave. Below the monastery, a path leads down through terraced gardens to a holy spring where pilgrims collect water. The monastery is active and women must cover their heads to enter. The combination of the religious site and the panoramic valley views makes this the most popular day trip from Sighnaghi.
