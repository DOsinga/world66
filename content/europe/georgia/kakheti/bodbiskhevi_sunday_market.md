Bodbiskhevi Sunday Market is one of Kakheti's bigger traditional markets, with stalls selling produce, meat, cheese, clothes, tools, livestock, and household goods.

It is best treated as a morning stop if you are already around Sighnaghi or Bodbe. The exact market location can be confusing, so ask locally before setting out.
