Khornabuji Fortress is an ancient hilltop stronghold in eastern Kakheti, first mentioned in the 5th century and later damaged during Mongol invasions. The surviving walls cling to a rocky outcrop, so the appeal is both historical and scenic: the climb gives broad views over the surrounding plains.
