Khornabuji Fortress clings to a rocky ridge in eastern Kakheti, with broken walls and towers looking out over the dry plains below. The site is old enough to be mentioned in the 5th century, and its position makes clear why it mattered.

The fortress is more ruin than polished monument, so it suits travellers who like exposed stone, wide views, and a bit of scrambling. Wear shoes with grip and avoid the hottest part of the day.
