Lagodekhi Protected Areas sit at Kakheti's far eastern edge, where vineyards give way to dense forest and the mountains rise toward the borders with Azerbaijan and Dagestan. The reserve is one of Georgia's oldest protected natural areas and offers a very different Kakheti from the wine-cellar circuit around Telavi and Sighnaghi.

Shorter walks lead to waterfalls and forest viewpoints; longer routes climb toward Black Rock Lake and high alpine country. The big hikes are serious undertakings and may require permits or border-zone checks, so ask locally before setting out. For travellers with time, Lagodekhi is one of the best nature reasons to stay in eastern Georgia.
