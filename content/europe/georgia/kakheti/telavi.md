Telavi is the regional capital of Kakheti and the natural base for exploring the wine country. It sits on a plateau above the Alazani Valley with the snow-capped Greater Caucasus forming the northern horizon — a setting that justifies the drive from Tbilisi on its own. The old town clusters around the main square, where a plane tree said to be 900 years old shades much of the central plaza.

The town has a compact core with a busy market, wine shops, and streets that still feel more local than polished. [Batonis Castle](/europe/georgia/kakheti/telavi/batonis_castle) gives Telavi its royal weight: this was the seat of the Kakhetian kings in the eighteenth century. The [Giant Plane Tree](/europe/georgia/kakheti/telavi/giant_plane_tree) is the town's living landmark, old enough to have become part of local folklore.

Telavi is unpretentious and functional, which works in its favour. Come for the small-town Georgian rhythm, then use it as a springboard for [Tsinandali](/europe/georgia/kakheti/tsinandali), Alaverdi, Ikalto, and the wineries of the Alazani Valley.
