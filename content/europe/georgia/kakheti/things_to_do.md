---
title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Kakheti rewards slow travel. The route heading east from Tbilisi along the A302 passes through a string of monasteries, palaces, and ancient churches, each set against the Alazani Valley or the hills above it. A day trip from Tbilisi is possible, but a week does the region justice.

The main base is [Telavi](telavi), the regional capital, from which you can reach [Ikalto Monastery](ikalto_monastery) and the [Shuamta Monasteries](shuamta_monasteries) to the west, [Tsinandali Estate](tsinandali) and [Alaverdi Cathedral](alaverdi_cathedral) to the east, and [Gremi Citadel](gremi) further north. [Sighnaghi](sighnaghi) and [Bodbe Monastery](bodbe_monastery) sit on the Gombori ridge with sweeping valley views. The [David Gareja](david_gareja) complex is a half-day further south, near the Azerbaijan border.

Wine is woven through everything here. Family wineries using ancient [qvevri clay vessels](telavi_wine_tasting) welcome visitors throughout the region. The [Kvareli](kvareli) tunnel winery and the Tsinandali estate offer more formal tastings. In autumn, the entire [Alazani Valley](alazani_valley) turns into a harvest festival.
