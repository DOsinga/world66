Tusheti National Park protects one of Georgia's most dramatic highland landscapes, a world of steep valleys, stone defensive towers, alpine pastures, and villages that are cut off by snow for much of the year. The road in over the Abano Pass is part of the experience and part of the warning: it is spectacular, exposed, and not a drive to improvise.

For walkers, riders, and anyone drawn to the Caucasus beyond the wine-country postcards, Tusheti is a major reason to linger in Kakheti. Guesthouses operate in the short summer season, and routes link villages such as Omalo, Dartlo, and Shenako. Weather changes quickly, and transport should be arranged with drivers who know the road.
