---
title: When to Go
type: section
---

**June to September** is the main hiking season. Temperatures in the valley are pleasant — warm by day, cool at night — and the high routes are clear of snow by mid-June. July and August are the busiest months; the village and the path to Gergeti Trinity Church fill up with tour groups from Tbilisi.

**May and October** are good shoulder months. Fewer crowds, the landscape is either fresh green (May) or golden (October), and prices are lower. Some higher trails may still carry snow in early May.

**Winter (November to March)** transforms Kazbegi. The crowds disappear entirely and the valley is quiet under snow. Gudauri ski resort is only about 30 kilometres south on the Georgian Military Highway, making Kazbegi a logical combination with a ski trip. The road can close temporarily after heavy snowfall, so flexibility is essential.

The Jvari Pass is the most vulnerable point on the highway — check conditions before travelling in any uncertain season.
