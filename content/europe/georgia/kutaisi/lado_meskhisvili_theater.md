The Lado Meskhisvili Theater stands on Kutaisi's central square and gives the city a touch of old civic grandeur. Even if you do not understand Georgian, the building is worth noticing for its presence: a formal theatre facade, broad steps, and a location that puts it in the middle of daily city life.

It is best as a cultural add-on rather than a primary sight. Check the programme if you are spending the night in Kutaisi, especially for music, drama, or festival events. Otherwise, fold it into a walk between the fountain, market streets, and the old centre, where it helps show Kutaisi as more than a base for monasteries and caves.
