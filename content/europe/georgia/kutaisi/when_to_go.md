---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

Kutaisi has a warm, humid climate — significantly wetter and milder than eastern Georgia. Summers are hot (July averages 23°C) and rainy, with frequent afternoon thunderstorms. Winters are mild and damp but rarely cold enough for snow in the city, though the surrounding hills get some.

**Spring (April–May)** and **autumn (September–October)** are the best times to visit. The Caucasus foothills and Colchic forests are at their most vivid in spring, and autumn brings harvest season — a good time to find fresh churchkhela, local wine, and walnuts in the market.

**Summer** is busy with domestic Georgian tourists and backpackers. The sights are accessible year-round but Gelati and Motsameta are at their best in clear weather, when the light on the frescoes and the views from the monastery terraces are at their finest.

**Winter** is quiet and the sights emptier, though some guesthouses close. The Prometheus Caves and Sataplia are open year-round and actually less humid in winter, which is an advantage.
