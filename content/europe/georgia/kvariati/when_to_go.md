---
title: When to Go
type: section
---

Kvariati's beach season runs from mid-June to mid-September, when the Black Sea is warm enough for swimming. July and August are peak summer — the beach gets busy with Georgian domestic tourists, and accommodation is in short supply without advance booking. June and September are the sweet spots: the sea is warm, the crowds are thinner, and the lush subtropical vegetation on the hillsides is at its most vivid.

The Adjara coast is rainy for much of the year. Batumi is one of the wettest cities in the former Soviet Union. Winter in Kvariati is mild but grey, with frequent rain; it's not a winter destination.
