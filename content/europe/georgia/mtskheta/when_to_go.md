---
title: When to Go
type: section
---

Mtskheta is worth visiting year-round, but **spring (April–May)** and **autumn (September–October)** are the most pleasant. The weather is mild, the countryside is green or golden depending on the season, and the main sights are open. Summer brings more visitors — Mtskheta is an easy day trip from Tbilisi and can feel crowded at the cathedral on weekends.

**Religious festivals** draw large crowds of Georgian pilgrims. Mtskhetoba, the feast of the Svetitskhoveli Cathedral, is celebrated in October and is a significant national event. If you visit then, expect crowds and genuine atmosphere in equal measure.

**Winter** is quiet and cold, but the monuments remain open and the lack of tour groups makes for a more reflective visit. Snow on the hillsides and the cathedral is not uncommon in January and February.
