Georgia in Miniatures is a small park with models of more than 50 Georgian landmarks, from churches and towers to civic monuments. It is a light, easy stop rather than a major sight, but useful if you are travelling with children or want a quick visual primer on the country's architecture.

The grounds include a visitor centre, a small amphitheatre, and a cafe. It works best as a short detour while moving along the Guria coast.
