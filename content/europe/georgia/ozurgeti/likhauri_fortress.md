Likhauri Fortress is a ruined medieval stronghold on a hill near the village of Likhauri, south of Ozurgeti. Local tradition links it with Queen Tamar, and the site is commonly paired with nearby Likhauri Church.

The fortress is a small stop, but the hill setting and surviving stonework make it worthwhile for travellers already exploring the historic sites around Ozurgeti.
