Ozurgeti Dramatic Theatre is the town's main performing-arts venue, staging plays, concerts, and local cultural events. It is worth checking if you are overnighting in Ozurgeti rather than just passing through Guria, since performances give a more local evening than the usual restaurant-and-walk routine.

Even when there is no show, the theatre is a useful landmark in the centre of town and pairs naturally with the nearby Ozurgeti History Museum.
