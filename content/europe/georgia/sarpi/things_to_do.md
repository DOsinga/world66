---
title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Sarpi is not a destination you plan a holiday around, but it has one genuinely unmissable sight: the border checkpoint building, a piece of architectural ambition that would look at home in a major European city and sits instead beside a village of fishing boats and tin roofs. Beyond that, the beach here is quieter than anything within reach of Batumi — if you want pebbles and Black Sea without the crowds, this is where to come.
