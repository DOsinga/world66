Hatsvali is the ski resort above Mestia, on the south-facing slopes of the Caucasus at around 2,000-2,400 metres. The current infrastructure — built in 2013 with a gondola from the base and several chairlifts above — replaced a Soviet-era facility that had fallen into disrepair. The skiing is modest: a few kilometres of groomed runs, suited to intermediate and beginner levels. The off-piste terrain above is considerably more interesting for advanced skiers.

The resort is about 10 kilometres from Mestia town by road. The gondola can also be used in summer to access the ridge above for hiking; the views over the Svaneti valley and toward the Caucasus main chain are outstanding. The season runs roughly December to April. Ski rental and instruction are available at the base. The resort has improved significantly since opening but still lacks the amenities of a major European ski area — expect functional rather than comfortable.
