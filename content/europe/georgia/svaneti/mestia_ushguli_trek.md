The Mestia-to-Ushguli hiking route is one of the best multi-day treks in the Caucasus, covering 60 kilometres through the upper Enguri valley over 3-4 days. The path passes through the villages of Zhabeshi, Adishi, and Iprari, crosses two mountain passes, and traverses alpine meadows backed by 4,000-metre peaks. The villages along the route all have simple guesthouses — usually just a family home with spare beds — where you can stay overnight.

The most dramatic section is the crossing from Adishi to Iprari via the Adishi Glacier pass (approximately 2,900 metres), which involves walking across glacier ice and requires careful timing in early summer when meltwater rivers are in flood. July and August are the safest months for this section; earlier in summer the rivers can be impassable. The route can be walked in either direction; Mestia to Ushguli (north to south, mostly uphill) is the standard direction, giving the satisfaction of arriving at the spectacular end goal.
