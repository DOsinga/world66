Alex. Nevskiy Orthodox Church is a Russian Orthodox church in Tbilisi, adding another layer to a city already full of Georgian, Armenian, Catholic, Muslim, and Jewish heritage. Its presence reflects the diverse religious geography of the older city.

It is a modest stop compared with Tbilisi's headline churches, but worthwhile for travelers tracing the city's varied communities and architectural traditions. Visit respectfully and combine it with nearby streets rather than treating it as a standalone destination.
