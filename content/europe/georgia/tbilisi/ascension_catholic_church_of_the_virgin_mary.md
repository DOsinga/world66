The Ascension Catholic Church of the Virgin Mary is a compact historic church in central Tbilisi. Its present form mixes pseudo-Gothic and baroque elements, with a 23 m tower and stained-glass windows brightening the interior.

It is worth a short stop while walking the old streets around Gudiashvili Square, especially for visitors interested in Tbilisi's smaller religious buildings.
