The flea market spread across the Dry Bridge (Metekhi Bridge area) and along the riverbank, open every day from around 10am. Vendors sell Soviet cameras, militaria, Georgian silverwork, antique icons (of uncertain authenticity), old maps, coins, and an enormous amount of strange junk. On weekends the market doubles in size and people come from across the city. Don't expect bargains without negotiation — but prices are low by any European standard. Haggling is expected and friendly.
