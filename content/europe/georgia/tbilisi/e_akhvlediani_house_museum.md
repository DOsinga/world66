The E. Akhvlediani House-Museum is a small branch of the Georgian National Museum devoted to painter Elene Akhvlediani. It is a focused stop rather than a headline museum, but it gives art-minded visitors a closer look at one of Georgia's best-known 20th-century artists and the domestic setting around her work.
