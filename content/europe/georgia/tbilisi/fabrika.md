A Soviet-era sewing factory repurposed as a sprawling creative complex with a hostel, co-working spaces, design studios, food trucks, bars, and a outdoor terrace that stays busy until 3am in summer. The courtyard is the heart of it — shipping containers converted into shops and cafés, a stage for occasional concerts, and a constant crowd of artists, tech workers, and travellers. Around the corner from Chugureti metro station, and one of the best places in the city to sit down with a glass of Georgian wine and a laptop.
