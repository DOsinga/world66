The Rezo Gabriadze Puppet Theatre in the old town is famous both for its performances and for the charmingly eccentric architecture of its building. The theatre was established in 1981 by the Georgian film director and artist Rezo Gabriadze, and specialises in elaborate marionette performances for adults — its most famous production tells the story of the Battle of Stalingrad through the eyes of two young soldiers in love.

Next to the theatre stands the Leaning Tower, a brick clock tower that tilts at a slight angle and features an angel mechanism that performs on the hour. The building's decorated facade, with its mosaics and painted reliefs, is one of the most photographed corners of the old town. Tickets for performances sell out; book ahead if you can.
