title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Tbilisi's sights cluster in several distinct areas. The [Old Town (Kala)](/europe/georgia/tbilisi/old_town) holds the densest concentration: medieval churches including [Sioni Cathedral](/europe/georgia/tbilisi/sionicathedral), [Anchiskhati Basilica](/europe/georgia/tbilisi/anchiskhati), and [Metekhi Church](/europe/georgia/tbilisi/metekhichurch) on its cliff above the river. Above the old town, [Narikala Fortress](/europe/georgia/tbilisi/narikala_fortress) provides the best panorama of the city; reach it on foot through the [Abanotubani](/europe/georgia/tbilisi/abanotubani) sulphur bath district or by cable car from [Rike Park](/europe/georgia/tbilisi/rike_park).

[Rustaveli Avenue](/europe/georgia/tbilisi/rustaveli_avenue) is the grand 19th-century boulevard anchored at one end by [Freedom Square](/europe/georgia/tbilisi/freedom_square) and lined with the [Opera House](/europe/georgia/tbilisi/tbilisi_opera_house), [Parliament](/europe/georgia/tbilisi/parliament_building), [Rustaveli Theatre](/europe/georgia/tbilisi/rustaveli_theatre), and the [National Museum](/europe/georgia/tbilisi/janashimuseumof) (with its extraordinary gold treasury and the [Museum of Soviet Occupation](/europe/georgia/tbilisi/museum_of_soviet_occupation)). The [Art Museum of Georgia](/europe/georgia/tbilisi/art_museum_of_georgia) on nearby Gudiashvili Square holds the main Pirosmani collection.

For something less conventional: the [Dry Bridge Market](/europe/georgia/tbilisi/dry_bridge_market) is the city's best flea market; the [Rezo Gabriadze Puppet Theatre](/europe/georgia/tbilisi/rezo_gabriadze_puppet_theatre) and its tilting clock tower are unmissable; the [Chronicle of Georgia](/europe/georgia/tbilisi/chronicle_of_georgia) monument above the reservoir is one of the most impressive Soviet-era sculptures anywhere; and [Stalin's Underground Printing House](/europe/georgia/tbilisi/stalins_printing_house) is a genuine piece of revolutionary history that few visitors find.

The green escapes: [Mtatsminda Park](/europe/georgia/tbilisi/mtatsminda_park) (take the funicular) for city views; [Vake Park](/europe/georgia/tbilisi/vake_park) and [Turtle Lake](/europe/georgia/tbilisi/turtle_lake) for forest walking; the [National Botanical Garden](/europe/georgia/tbilisi/national_botanical_garden_tbilisi) in the gorge behind Narikala for cool air and a waterfall.
