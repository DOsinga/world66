The U. Japaridze House-Museum is a small branch of the Georgian National Museum dedicated to painter Ucha Japaridze. The house preserves a more personal setting than Tbilisi's larger museums, with works and material connected to the artist's life.

It is a focused stop for visitors interested in Georgian painting or house museums. For most travellers it is best combined with other sights in the surrounding part of the city rather than treated as a stand-alone half-day visit.
