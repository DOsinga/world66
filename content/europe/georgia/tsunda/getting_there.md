---
title: Getting There
type: section
---

Tsunda is on the main road (the S5) between Alkhatsikhe and Vardzia, about 10 km short of the cave city. There is no regular public transport to the village itself; most visitors arrive by marshrutka or shared taxi from Alkhatsikhe and ask to be dropped at the Tsunda turnoff. Private taxis from Alkhatsikhe run to Vardzia and can be asked to stop here. From Tbilisi, the journey is around 4–5 hours.
