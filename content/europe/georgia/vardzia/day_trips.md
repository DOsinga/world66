[Akhaltsikhe](/europe/georgia/alkhatsikhe), 30 km north, is the natural base for visiting Vardzia. The [Rabati fortress](/europe/georgia/alkhatsikhe/rabati_fortress) there is worth a few hours on the same day. The drive between the two passes through the Kura gorge, which is dramatic enough to be an attraction in itself.

[Borjomi](/europe/georgia/borjomi), about 90 km north, is Georgia's famous spa town in a green valley on the edge of the Borjomi-Kharagauli National Park. It makes a reasonable stop on the way to or from Tbilisi if you have your own transport.

[Tsunda](/europe/georgia/tsunda), a tiny village just before Vardzia on the road from Akhaltsikhe, has hot springs and basic accommodation — useful if you want to spend a night in the valley rather than commuting from the city.
