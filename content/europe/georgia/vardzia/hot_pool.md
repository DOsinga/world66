The Hot Pool near Vardzia is a simple public bathing spot fed by warm sulphur water. It is informal rather than polished, but it can be a welcome soak after walking the cave city and the Kura gorge.

Bring clean water for rinsing afterward, since the mineral water can leave a sulphur smell.
