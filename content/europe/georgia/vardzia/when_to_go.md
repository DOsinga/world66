---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

Vardzia is accessible year-round, but the road from Akhaltsikhe can be closed or treacherous in winter when snow settles on the high passes. The best months are May to October. Spring (May–June) brings wildflowers to the gorge and the river runs full and fast. Summer is warm and dry, though the site can get crowded on Georgian public holidays. Autumn (September–October) is excellent: clear skies, cooler temperatures, and the orchards along the Kura turning colour.

In winter, the cave city is atmospheric but cold. The road south from Akhaltsikhe is typically passable, but check conditions before you go. A few overnight guesthouses in the valley stay open through winter.

The cave church hosts services on Sundays and on major Orthodox feast days. If you want to attend — or to avoid the crowds a service brings — check the Georgian Orthodox calendar before your visit.
