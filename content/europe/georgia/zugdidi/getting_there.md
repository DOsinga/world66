---
title: Getting There
type: section
---

Zugdidi sits at a key junction in western Georgia and is well connected to the rest of the country.

**By train** is the most comfortable option from Tbilisi: overnight and day trains run daily, taking around five to six hours. The station is conveniently central. There are also rail connections to Batumi and Kutaisi.

**By marshrutka** minibuses run frequently from Tbilisi (around five hours), Kutaisi (two hours), and Batumi (three hours). They depart from the main bus station adjacent to the train station.

**To Svaneti:** Zugdidi is the main gateway to the Svaneti highlands. Daily marshrutkas run to Mestia (around five hours along a spectacular mountain road) from the bus station, departing in the morning. A small regional airport serves summer flights from Tbilisi to Mestia, though these depart from Natakhtari near Tbilisi rather than Zugdidi itself.
