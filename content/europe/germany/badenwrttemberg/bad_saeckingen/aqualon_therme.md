Aqualon is the practical spa stop in Bad Säckingen: thermal pools, sauna areas, and wellness treatments above the town on Bergseestrasse. It is not the reason to cross Germany, but it fits the town's old cure-resort identity and makes sense after a damp Rhine walk or a Black Forest hike.

Check current hours before going, especially if you are planning around sauna access or treatments rather than a simple swim.
