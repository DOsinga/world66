---
title: Things to Do
type: section
---

Bad Säckingen's main sights sit within an easy walk of each other. Start at St. Fridolin's Minster, cross the old wooden bridge into Switzerland, then return through the riverside park to Schloss Schönau and the Trumpet Museum. If you have more time, the Aqualon thermal baths and Bergsee add the spa-town side of the place.
