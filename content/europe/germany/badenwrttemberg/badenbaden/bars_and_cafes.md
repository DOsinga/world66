---
title: "Bars and Cafes"
type: section
---

The classic move is afternoon coffee and cake at **Café König** on Lichtentalerstraße, a Baden-Baden institution that has been making its own chocolates and pastries since the 1950s. **Café Bandi** is the slightly less formal alternative.

For evening drinks, the **Kurhaus Bernstein-Bar** is the dressed-up choice inside the casino building. **Leo's Bar** on Luisenstrasse is livelier; the wine bars around the Marktplatz favour Baden Spätburgunder and Riesling from just up the road. The town is not a late-night destination — most places shut by midnight.
