---
title: "Eating Out"
type: section
---

Baden-Baden punches above its weight in fine dining. **Le Jardin de France** in the old town holds a Michelin star and serves classic French cooking in a flower-filled courtyard in summer. **Wintergarten** at the Brenners Park-Hotel and **Maltes Hidden Kitchen** (a tiny eight-seat counter) are the other serious destinations; both require booking weeks ahead.

For something cheaper and rooted in the Black Forest, **Rizzi** on the Lichtentaler Allee serves modern Mediterranean and is a town institution. **Weinstube zum Engel** in the old town does proper Baden country cooking — flammkuchen, schnitzel, local Spätburgunder by the glass — at a fraction of the spa-hotel prices.
