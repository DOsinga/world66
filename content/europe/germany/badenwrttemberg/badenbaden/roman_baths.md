Beneath Römerplatz, between the Friedrichsbad and the Caracalla Therme, are the surviving ruins of the **Roman imperial baths** built in the 2nd and 3rd centuries AD. The complex was uncovered during the construction of the Friedrichsbad in the 1840s and has been left in place, accessible by a short underground walk.

What you see is the hypocaust system — the network of brick pillars beneath the floor that channelled hot air to warm the rooms above — and fragments of the bathing pools themselves. A small archaeological display explains the layout. It is a brief visit but it makes a useful introduction before crossing the square to one of the modern baths. The same water still pours up from the same springs.
