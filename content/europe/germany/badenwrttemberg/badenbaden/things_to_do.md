---
title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

The point of Baden-Baden is to take the waters, walk the gardens, see a concert, and eat well. The **Friedrichsbad** is the great spa experience; the **Caracalla Therme** is the easier modern alternative; and the **Roman Bath ruins** between them prove that the tradition is two thousand years old. Above ground, the **Kurhaus** and its casino, the colonnaded **Trinkhalle**, and the elegant **Lichtentaler Allee** are the central walks.

For walks higher up, take the **Merkur Funicular** to its summit, or hike up through the Battert rocks to the impressive **Hohenbaden Castle** ruin. Music is a serious part of the calendar — the **Festspielhaus** hosts top international orchestras, and the **Brahms House** preserves the composer's summer flat. The **Museum Frieder Burda** is the standout among the town's small museums; the **Iffezheim Racecourse** ten kilometres away hosts Germany's most prestigious thoroughbred meetings.
