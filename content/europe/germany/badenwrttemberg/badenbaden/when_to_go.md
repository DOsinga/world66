---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

Baden-Baden works in any season. **Spring** brings the magnolias and the Iffezheim spring meeting. **Summer** is warm and busy with the Festspielhaus's summer programme. **Autumn** is the most atmospheric — Black Forest leaves turning, the late-summer racing meetings, and the harvest in the surrounding vineyards.

**Winter** is the secret good season: the town is quiet, the thermal baths feel even better when there is frost outside, and the Christmas market in the centre is among the prettiest in Germany. The casino, the Festspielhaus, and most museums run year-round.
