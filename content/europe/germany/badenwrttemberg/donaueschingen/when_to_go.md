---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

Donaueschingen sits at around 700 metres in the Schwarzwald-Baar plateau and gets proper continental weather: warm summers, cold winters, and frequent snowfall from December to March.

**May to September** is the best stretch for visiting — the palace grounds are at their prettiest, beer gardens are open, and the Danube cycle path is comfortable. The **Donaueschinger Musiktage**, one of the world's most respected festivals of contemporary classical music, takes place over a long weekend in mid- to late **October**. If avant-garde composition is your thing, that is the time to come; book accommodation well ahead. Otherwise the town is quiet in winter, with little open and shorter daylight hours.
