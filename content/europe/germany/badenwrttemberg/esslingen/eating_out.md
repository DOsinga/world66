Esslingen's old town has a good range of traditional Swabian restaurants within its half-timbered streets. Expect Maultaschen (large pasta parcels with meat and spinach), Spätzle (egg noodles), Zwiebelrostbraten (roast beef with caramelised onions), and a wine list heavy on local Trollinger and Riesling. The Marktplatz and the lanes around it have the highest concentration of options.

**Schwanen** (Rathausgasse 1) is one of the most established traditional restaurants in the old town, serving Swabian cuisine in a half-timbered setting. Maultaschen in brown butter is the dish to order.

During harvest season in September and October, several small Straußwirtschaft wine taverns open up — local vineyard owners granted the temporary right to sell their own wine alongside simple food. Look for the pine-branch bundles hung outside doorways. The Winzerkeller Esslingen cooperative has a tasting room and wine shop, and the castle restaurant at the Burg serves local food and wine in summer on an outdoor terrace with views across the Neckar valley.
