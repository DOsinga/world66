Esslingen's Mittelalter- und Weihnachtsmarkt (Medieval and Christmas Market) is widely regarded as one of the finest Christmas markets in Baden-Württemberg, and its medieval component distinguishes it from the standard German fare. The market runs from late November through 23 December on and around the Marktplatz, with two parallel sections: a traditional Christmas market on the main square and a medieval market in the lanes nearby where traders dress in period costume and sell handmade goods by firelight.

The medieval market features craftspeople working with traditional tools — blacksmiths, potters, glassblowers, leather workers — alongside stalls selling honey wine (mead), smoked meats, and medieval-style pastries. Fire jugglers and musicians in period dress animate the evenings. The backdrop of the Altes Rathaus, the two Gothic churches, and the half-timbered houses makes the setting genuinely appropriate in a way it would not be in a modern city. The market attracts large crowds on weekends; visiting on a weekday evening is more atmospheric.
