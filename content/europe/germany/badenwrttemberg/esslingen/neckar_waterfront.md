The Neckar waterfront in Esslingen is one of the town's most attractive areas, particularly along the Wehrneckarkanal — the medieval canal that branches off the main river and runs through the heart of the lower old town. Lined with willows and backed by the old town walls, the canal has a gentleness that contrasts pleasantly with the grander architecture above.

The waterfront is best explored on foot. A path runs along the north bank of the Neckar proper, with views across to Esslingen's skyline of towers and spires. In summer, the riverside benches and grassy banks fill up with locals; the Esslinger Schwimmbad is also nearby for those who want to swim. The old stone bridges crossing the canal — some dating back several centuries — make the area feel more like a Flemish canal town than a German one.
