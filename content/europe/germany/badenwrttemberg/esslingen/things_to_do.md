title: Things to Do in Esslingen
type: section
tags: [things_to_do]
---

Esslingen's main draw is its intact medieval townscape — most of what's worth seeing is within easy walking distance of the Marktplatz. Allow at least half a day; more if you plan to take in the castle, the vineyards, and a tasting at the Kessler cellars.

The [Marktplatz](marktplatz) is the obvious starting point, with the [Altes Rathaus](altes_rathaus), [Stadtkirche St Dionys](stadtkirche_st_dionys), and [Frauenkirche](frauenkirche_esslingen) all within a few steps of each other. From here, a climb through the [vineyards](esslingen_vineyards) leads up to [Burg Esslingen](burg_esslingen), with progressively better views of the Neckar valley as you ascend. The [medieval town walls](stadtmauer) are worth exploring on foot, and the [Innere Brücke](innere_bruecke) and [Neckar waterfront](neckar_waterfront) make a pleasant afternoon wander.

The [Württembergische Landesbühne](wuerttembergische_landesbuehne) stages drama and opera throughout the season, and the [Stadtmuseum](stadtmuseum) fills any gaps in understanding the town's history. For wine, [Kessler Sekt](kessler_sekt) is the obvious stop for a tour and tasting. In November and December, the [Mittelalter- und Weihnachtsmarkt](mittelaltermarkt) transforms the old town into one of the finest Christmas markets in the region.
