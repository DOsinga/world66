Voltron Nevera is the resort's headline modern coaster, set in the Croatian themed area and built around Nikola Tesla theatre. It is a multi-launch steel coaster with a long, twisting layout, several inversions, and enough airtime to make it the first target for many visitors when the gates open.

If you care about coasters, do this early or late. Midday queues can eat a large part of a visit.
