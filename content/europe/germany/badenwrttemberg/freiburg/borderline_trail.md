The Borderline Trail is Freiburg’s serious mountain-bike descent, running through the wooded slopes east of the city. It is a reminder that Freiburg is not only a pretty old town and university city, but also a gateway to steep Black Forest terrain.

This is a specialist POI rather than a casual walking path. Experienced riders should check current trail rules, conditions, and access before setting out, and visitors without bikes may prefer to treat the area as part of a broader Schauinsland or forest excursion.
