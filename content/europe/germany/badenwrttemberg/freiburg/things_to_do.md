---
title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Freiburg's compact medieval centre rewards exploration on foot. The city's most celebrated landmarks cluster around the [Münsterplatz](/europe/germany/badenwrttemberg/freiburg/munsterplatz), while the old city gates mark the edges of the historic core. Beyond the old town, the wooded Schlossberg offers easy walks straight from the city, the Augustiner Museum holds the best of Upper Rhine art, and the famous Bächle — narrow water channels — run alongside every street.
