---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

Freudenstadt is a year-round destination but the character changes sharply with the seasons. Late spring through early autumn (May–October) is the obvious window for hiking, with the forests at their fullest in June and the deciduous trees colouring through October. July and August can be busy with German spa visitors but the trails empty out quickly outside the most popular routes.

Winter is the second season. Cross-country skiing along the Schwarzwald Hochstrasse and around Kniebis is excellent in good years; downhill skiing is modest. The Marktplatz hosts a small but pretty Christmas market through Advent. November and early March are the dead months — many spa hotels close briefly for maintenance and the weather is reliably grey.
