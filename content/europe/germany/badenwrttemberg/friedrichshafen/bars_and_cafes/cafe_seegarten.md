The Café Seegarten has been a fixture on the promenade for decades. The terrace faces the lake and catches the afternoon sun; it is the kind of place where you order a Melange and a slice of Schwarzwälder Kirschtorte and stay for an hour longer than planned. In the evenings it shifts toward wine and cocktails. The location between the Schlosskirche and the ferry pier means it catches foot traffic from both directions. Arrives before 3pm on weekends in summer if you want a terrace table.
