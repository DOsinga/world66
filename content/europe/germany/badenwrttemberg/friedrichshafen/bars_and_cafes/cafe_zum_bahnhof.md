A reliable town-centre café a short walk from the Stadtbahnhof, Café zum Bahnhof does good breakfasts until midday and strong coffee throughout the day. The atmosphere is local and unhurried — newspapers on a rack, regulars with their morning Milchkaffee, a reliable supply of fresh Brötchen. Good stop if you're waiting for a train or want to escape the lake promenade crowds. Closes early in the evening.
