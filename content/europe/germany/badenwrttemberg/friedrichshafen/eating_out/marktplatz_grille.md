A straightforward Swabian restaurant a few blocks back from the lake, popular with locals at lunch. The menu is short and changes with the week: expect Maultaschen in broth, roast pork with Knödel, and seasonal vegetable dishes. A daily three-course lunch menu costs well under €15. No lake view, but no tourist prices either. Cash preferred; closed Sunday evenings.
