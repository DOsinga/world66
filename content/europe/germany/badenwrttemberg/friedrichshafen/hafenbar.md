A relaxed harbour bar within sight of the ferry landing, the Hafenbar is where locals come to drink cold Bodensee-brewed beer while watching the steamers come and go. It has a casual, unhurried feel that can be hard to find along the more tourist-oriented part of the promenade. Decent selection of regional wines alongside the standard German beers. Open from mid-afternoon; fills up on summer evenings.
