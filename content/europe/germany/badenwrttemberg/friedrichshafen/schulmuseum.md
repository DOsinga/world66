Schulmuseum Friedrichshafen is a specialist museum on the history of schooling, classrooms, and education. Its period rooms, teaching materials, and everyday objects make it easy to see how school life changed across generations.

It is a smaller, more local stop than the Zeppelin and Dornier museums, but it is distinctive and family-friendly. Visitors with children, teachers, or an interest in social history may find it a surprisingly memorable break from the lakefront.
