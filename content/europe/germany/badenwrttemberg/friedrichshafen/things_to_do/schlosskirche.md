The Schlosskirche — palace church — stands directly on the lakefront promenade and is the defining image of Friedrichshafen. The baroque twin towers are visible from the ferry as you approach from Switzerland. Originally a monastery church from the 17th century, it became the royal chapel of the Württemberg dynasty when they made the adjacent palace their summer seat. The interior is all white stucco and gilded altarpieces in the South German baroque manner. The adjacent palace building is now used for offices and not open to visitors, but the church is freely accessible and takes only 20 minutes to see.
