Gersbach is a small highland village in the southern [Black Forest](/europe/germany/badenwrttemberg/blackforest), administratively part of Schopfheim. It sits around 800 metres up, on open slopes with broad views toward the Rhine valley and, on clear days, the Swiss Alps.

This is not the postcard Black Forest of cuckoo clocks and busy spa promenades. Gersbach's older story is glassmaking, charcoal burning, silver mining, and hilltop defensive lines. The village has worked hard to keep that rural character visible, and it won European recognition for village development in the early 2000s.

Come for walking, views, and a quiet mountain base between [Basel](/europe/switzerland/basel) and [Freiburg](/europe/germany/badenwrttemberg/freiburg). Public transport exists from Schopfheim, but a car makes it much easier to combine Gersbach with the rest of the southern Black Forest.
