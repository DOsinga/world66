The Barockschanze is a reconstructed hexagonal earthwork from the defensive lines built in the wars against Louis XIV's France. The wooden tower and raised banks make the military layout easy to read, even if you are not usually interested in fortifications.

Its other virtue is the setting. The site sits in open upland landscape, so the walk out is part of the point.
