The Wald und Glas Zentrum is a small local museum about forest glassmaking in the southern Black Forest. It uses finds, replicas, and process displays to show how glass was made when furnaces moved through the forest in search of fuel.

It is modest, but it gives useful context for Gersbach. The village makes more sense when you understand how closely forest, craft, and fuel were tied together.
