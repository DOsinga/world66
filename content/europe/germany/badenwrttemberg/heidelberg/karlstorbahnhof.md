Karlstorbahnhof is Heidelberg's independent cultural centre, the place to look when the old-town romance starts to feel too polished. After years near the Karlstor, it now operates from Marlene-Dietrich-Platz in the Südstadt, with a programme that moves between concerts, theatre, comedy, cinema, talks, parties, and community events.

It is not a casual daylight sight in the way the castle or Old Bridge is. Karlstorbahnhof matters because it shows the city's contemporary side: student energy, touring musicians, political cabaret, local scenes, and late evenings that are less postcard-friendly but often more alive. Check the calendar and go when something fits your taste.
