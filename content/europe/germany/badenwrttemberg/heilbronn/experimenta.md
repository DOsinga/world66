experimenta is Heilbronn's modern counterweight to the historic market square. The science centre fills a striking building by the Neckar with hands-on exhibits, labs, shows, and activities aimed at curious adults as much as families.

It gives Heilbronn a reason to keep you for more than a quick look at the church. If the weather turns or you are travelling with children, this is the most useful indoor stop in the city.
