The Baden Art Association is one of Germany's oldest art associations, founded in Karlsruhe in 1818. Its programme focuses on contemporary art exhibitions, talks, film screenings, and other temporary projects, so the value of a visit depends on what is showing.

It sits close to the palace and the main museum cluster, making it easy to check alongside the Staatliche Kunsthalle, Schlosspark, and the city centre.
