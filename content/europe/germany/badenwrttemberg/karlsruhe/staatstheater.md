The Baden State Theatre is one of the larger opera and theatre complexes in Germany, with a main hall, studio theatre, and small drama venue sharing a sprawling building in the heart of the city. The theatre has a strong reputation for opera, with a full season running from September to June; the ballet company is one of the more respected in south Germany.

The building, constructed in stages through the 20th century and extensively renovated, is not architecturally distinguished but is central and easy to find. Tickets are available at the box office and online; prices are significantly lower than comparable venues in Munich or Frankfurt. Check the programme before arriving — the theatre runs a busy schedule and a performance at the Staatstheater is one of the better ways to spend an evening in Karlsruhe.
