Christuskirche is one of Mannheim's notable churches, with an original Steinmeyer organ of around 8,000 pipes. The instrument, built in 1911, is among the largest historic organs in Germany.

The church is a good stop if you are interested in sacred architecture or music. Check ahead for concerts, when the building is at its best.
