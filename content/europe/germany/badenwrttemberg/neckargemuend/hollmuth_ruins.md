The Hollmuth is a ruined tower on the hillside directly above Neckargemünd, reachable by a 30-minute walk through the forest from the town centre. The ruins themselves are modest — a partial tower and some wall remnants — but the view from the top over the Neckar confluence and the town below is worth the climb. The footpath continues from here along the ridge and connects to the wider network of forest trails leading toward Dilsberg and Neckarsteinach.
