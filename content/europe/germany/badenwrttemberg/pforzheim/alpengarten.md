The Alpengarten Pforzheim is a commercial alpine plant nursery with a small botanical garden attached, on a hillside south of the city. Around 1,500 species of high-mountain plants grow in beds, rockeries, and a series of greenhouses — gentians, edelweiss, saxifrages, alpine rhododendrons.

The garden is at its best from late April to early June, when most species flower. Open daily in season, weekends only out of season. Free entry; plants for sale at gate prices. A 15-minute drive or 30-minute walk uphill from the city centre.
