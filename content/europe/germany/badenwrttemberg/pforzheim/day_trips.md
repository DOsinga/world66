---
title: "Day Trips"
type: section
---

Pforzheim is on the northern edge of the **Black Forest** (Schwarzwald) and is the natural rail and road gateway into it. The **Enz valley** running south is the closest worthwhile outing — Bad Wildbad, with its forest-canopy walk and thermal baths, is 40 minutes by train. The **Nagold valley** to the south-west reaches the half-timbered towns of **Calw** (birthplace of Hermann Hesse) and **Nagold**.

Further afield, **Karlsruhe** (25 minutes by ICE) has the ZKM media-art centre and one of the most ambitious modern art museums in Germany. **Stuttgart** is the same distance the other way, with its Staatsgalerie, Mercedes Museum, and Porsche Museum. **Baden-Baden** is 50 minutes by car or train, for spa towns and old-money atmosphere.
