---
title: "Eating Out"
type: section
---

Pforzheim's restaurants are mostly solid rather than exciting — a reasonable mix of Swabian, Italian, and Turkish, with a few standout places for serious dining. The river-bank streets between the theatre and the Schmuckmuseum have the densest cluster.

For traditional Swabian, **Kupferdächle** in the rebuilt old town does Maultaschen, Spätzle, and roasts at fair prices. For something more contemporary, **Restaurant Silberburg** has long held a regional reputation. The Wochenmarkt (Marktplatz, Wednesdays and Saturdays) is excellent for picnic provisions — Schwarzwälder ham, local cheeses, fresh bread.
