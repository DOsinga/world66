---
title: "Getting There"
type: section
---

Pforzheim is on the **main rail line** between Karlsruhe and Stuttgart — around 25 minutes from either city by ICE, hourly. The Hauptbahnhof is a five-minute walk from the city centre.

By road, the **A8 motorway** runs along the northern edge of the city. The nearest international airport is **Stuttgart** (STR), 50 km east, served by direct S-Bahn and bus connections. **Frankfurt** is around 200 km north, about 90 minutes by car or ICE plus connection.

The **Enztalradweg** cycle route follows the Enz river from its source in the Black Forest down to the Neckar; it passes through Pforzheim and is well-signposted for self-supported touring.
