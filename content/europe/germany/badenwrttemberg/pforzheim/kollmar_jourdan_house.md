The Kollmar & Jourdan House is one of Pforzheim's more unusual architectural survivors, built for the jewellery firm at the beginning of the 20th century. Its Art Nouveau facade uses glazed brick, pale green wall surfaces, and relief figures representing the continents.

It is a quick stop rather than a long visit, but it fits Pforzheim well: a city where jewellery, industry, and decorative design are tightly linked. Look up at the facade details, which are the point of the detour.
