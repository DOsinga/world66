Maulbronn Monastery is one of the most complete medieval monastic complexes in Germany, preserved with cloisters, church, courtyards, and service buildings that still read as a self-contained world. Its mixture of Romanesque and Gothic architecture makes it rewarding even for travellers who usually skim church sights.

From Pforzheim, Maulbronn works as a strong day trip or half-day excursion. The site is far enough from the city to require planning, but its UNESCO status and unusually intact layout make it much more than a minor nearby church.
