The Stadttheater is Pforzheim's three-stage municipal theatre — opera, drama, dance, and concert hall in a single 1980s complex on the river bank. The resident company is small but ambitious, with a strong opera tradition; the Pforzheim Chamber Orchestra is one of the best-known ensembles of its size in Germany.

The 1990s building is no architectural prize. The programme is what matters. Tickets are cheap by German standards (€15–45). The website has English summaries of productions.
