A 16-hectare municipal wildlife park on the slope above Brötzingen, with around 200 animals — wolves, lynx, bison, fallow deer, wild boar, otters, eagles, owls — many of them species native to the Black Forest. The wolves and the lynx are the obvious draw; the enclosures are generous by zoo standards.

Free entry. Open daily. Combine with a walk up into the surrounding forest, which is part of the Nordschwarzwald nature park.
