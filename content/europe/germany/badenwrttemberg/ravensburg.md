Ravensburg is a well-preserved medieval town in Upper Swabia, about 45 minutes by train south of Ulm and a short hop from [Lake Constance](/europe/germany/badenwrttemberg/lakeconstance). The old centre is remarkably intact, with towers, gateways, and half-timbered houses that make it one of the more picturesque small towns in Baden-Württemberg.

The town is best known as the home of Ravensburger, the board game and puzzle company — a fact the locals embrace with pride. The [Mehlsack](/europe/germany/badenwrttemberg/ravensburg/mehlsack), a distinctive 51-metre white tower, and the [Blaserturm](/europe/germany/badenwrttemberg/ravensburg/blaserturm) with its clock are the most recognisable landmarks. The [Marienplatz](/europe/germany/badenwrttemberg/ravensburg/marienplatz), the main square, is a fine place to sit with a coffee and watch the town go about its business.

A short distance north, [Weingarten Abbey](/europe/germany/badenwrttemberg/ravensburg/weingarten_abbey) is one of the grandest Baroque churches in Germany — and the site of the Blutritt, claimed to be the largest mounted pilgrimage in the world, which sets out every year on the Friday after Ascension.
