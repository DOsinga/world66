---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

Schiltach is at its most photogenic in summer (June to September), when the half-timbered market square is fully lit and the surrounding hiking trails are at their best. Autumn brings mist and colour to the surrounding valleys and is arguably the most atmospheric time to visit, though some attractions have reduced hours.

Winter is quiet but the town is genuinely attractive in snow — the Black Forest gets significant snowfall from December through February. The Schwarzwaldbahn runs year-round and the train journey is particularly striking in winter. Spring arrives late at this altitude, but by May the forests are lush and the hiking season is underway.
