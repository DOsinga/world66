---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

Schönach works in two seasons. Summer (June to September) is the prime hiking season — the trails through the Black Forest are at their best, the air is cool at 900 metres, and the views from the ridge walks are clear. July and August bring the most visitors, though the village is small enough that it never feels overcrowded.

Winter (December to March) is the Nordic skiing season. Schönach has a long history as a cross-country skiing centre, and when there is snow the village becomes a base for skiers. The altitude is reliable enough for good conditions most winters. Spring and autumn are quiet and perfectly pleasant for walking, though some facilities may have reduced hours.
