---
title: When to Go
type: section
---

Schorndorf is a pleasant place to visit year-round. Spring and early summer are the best time for walking in the Rems Valley, when the vineyards are in leaf and the hills are green. Autumn is the wine harvest season and the Besenwirtschaften open in the villages — September and October are the best months for this.

December brings a modest Christmas market to the Marktplatz. Winters can be cold with occasional snow; the museum and the Daimler birth house are open regardless of weather.
