Sinsheim is best treated as a day trip itself, from Heidelberg or Heilbronn. If you are based here, Heidelberg (half an hour by train) is the obvious add-on, and Speyer combines neatly with the Technik Museum's sister site — the Technik Museum Speyer has the Buran shuttle and a walk-through 747.
