Bad Cannstatt is older than central Stuttgart and still feels like a separate town in places. Around the market square, half-timbered houses, narrow lanes, shops, and mineral-spa history give it a more local, less polished character than the city's museum-and-shopping core.

Come for a wander rather than a checklist. The area works well with Wilhelma, the Neckar, or a mineral bath, and it gives Stuttgart a useful second face: not just cars, modern industry, and postwar rebuilding, but an old wine-and-spa town folded into the metropolis.
