The Gottlieb Daimler Memorial occupies the garden house in Bad Cannstatt where Daimler and Wilhelm Maybach worked on early combustion engines and motor vehicles.

It is a compact but meaningful stop for car-history visitors, especially when paired with Stuttgart's larger Mercedes-Benz Museum.
