Haus der Musik im Fruchtkasten displays the Landesmuseum's musical-instrument collection inside the historic granary on Schillerplatz. The collection ranges from older European instruments to pieces from farther afield, with temporary exhibitions and audio material that make it more engaging than a simple case-by-case display.
