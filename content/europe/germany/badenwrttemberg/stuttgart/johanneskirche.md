Johanneskirche is a Gothic Revival church beside Feuersee in Stuttgart-West. Its lakeside setting is the draw: the church and spire reflect in the water, and the view is especially atmospheric in the evening.

The church is an easy detour from central Stuttgart and one of the city's better architectural photo stops.
