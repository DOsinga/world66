Killesbergturm is a light, spiralling observation tower in Stuttgart's Killesberg park area. Its open steel structure makes the climb feel airy, and the reward is a wide view over the city, hills, and surrounding green spaces.

The tower pairs naturally with a walk through Höhenpark Killesberg. It is a simple attraction, but a good one: climb, look across Stuttgart's bowl-like setting, then wander the park rather than rushing back downtown.
