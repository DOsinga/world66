Kurpark Bad Cannstatt is a 19th-century spa park tied to the mineral springs that gave Bad Cannstatt its name. The park, Kursaal, and nearby mineral baths make this part of Stuttgart feel different from the city centre.

Come for a calm walk, or combine it with the MineralBad-Cannstatt complex next door.
