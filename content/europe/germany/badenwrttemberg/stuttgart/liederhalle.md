The Liederhalle is Stuttgart's major concert and congress complex, a postwar cultural building that stands a short walk from the city centre. Its halls host classical music, conferences, and touring performances, but the building itself is also part of Stuttgart's architectural story after the destruction of the Second World War.

It is most rewarding if you catch a concert or have an interest in mid-century civic architecture. Even from outside, the complex helps explain Stuttgart's character: not a museum city frozen in one era, but a place rebuilt with confidence, engineering, and a certain sober elegance.
