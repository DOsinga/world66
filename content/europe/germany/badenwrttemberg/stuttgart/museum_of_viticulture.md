Museum of Viticulture gives Stuttgart's wine culture a concrete home in Uhlbach, one of the vineyard districts east of the centre. Exhibits cover grape growing, cellar work, tools, seasonal labour, and the role wine has played in local identity.

It is best combined with a vineyard walk or a visit to nearby wine villages, since the museum's subject makes most sense in the surrounding landscape. For food-and-wine travelers, it adds depth to Stuttgart beyond cars and museums downtown.
