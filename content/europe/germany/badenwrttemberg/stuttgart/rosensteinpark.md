Rosensteinpark is one of Stuttgart's great green spaces, laid out as an English-style landscape park in the 19th century and now forming part of the city's broad green corridor. Lawns, mature trees, and long paths connect the area around Schloss Rosenstein with museums, Wilhelma, and the Neckar-side districts.

It is a good decompression stop in a city better known for cars, industry, and hills. Families can combine it with Wilhelma or the natural history museums; walkers can use it as part of a longer route through the Green U. In spring and summer, the park makes Stuttgart feel much softer than its traffic-heavy reputation suggests.
