Rotwildpark is Stuttgart at its greenest: a broad forest and nature reserve on the city's western side, historically associated with red and wild boar parkland. It feels far removed from the shopping streets and car museums, even though it is still part of the metropolitan edge.

Come for walking rather than spectacle. Trails, ponds, old trees, and occasional wildlife make it a good choice when the city feels too built-up, and it pairs naturally with other western Stuttgart green spaces for a longer, low-key day outdoors.
