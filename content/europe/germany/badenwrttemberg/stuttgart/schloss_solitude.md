Schloss Solitude sits high on Stuttgart’s western edge, a white Rococo palace built for Duke Carl Eugen as a place of retreat, ceremony, and display. The location is half the point: from the terraces and alleys you feel how deliberately the palace was set apart from the city below.

Inside, guided visits show the polished courtly rooms; outside, the lawns and long axis make it a fine place for a slow walk. It is a useful counterweight to Stuttgart’s car museums and dense centre, especially if you want architecture, landscape, and a bit of royal theatre in one stop.
