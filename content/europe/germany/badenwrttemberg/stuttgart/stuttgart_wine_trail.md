Walking the wine trail is one of the best ways to understand Stuttgart’s geography. Vineyards press surprisingly close to the city, and paths through the slopes reveal a version of Stuttgart that is greener, steeper, and more rural than the car-and-museum image suggests.

Choose a clear day and allow time for views, village edges, and wine taverns if they are open. The trail is a self-guided experience rather than a single attraction, so good shoes and a little route planning matter.
