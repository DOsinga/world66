Wagenhallen is a former railway workshop complex north of Stuttgart's centre that has been turned into an arts and events space. The rough industrial setting gives concerts, exhibitions, markets, and performances a very different feel from the city's polished museums and palaces.

It is worth checking the programme rather than just showing up. With the right event on, Wagenhallen is one of the better places to catch Stuttgart's contemporary creative scene.
