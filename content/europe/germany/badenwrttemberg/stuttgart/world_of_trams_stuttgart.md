World of Trams Stuttgart is a transport museum for people who like cities through their vehicles. Historic tramcars, depot displays, signs, and technical details tell the story of how Stuttgart moved before the modern Stadtbahn took over much of the network.

The museum is especially good if heritage trams are running, turning the visit from static nostalgia into a ride through the city. Check opening days carefully, because specialist transport museums often keep limited hours.
