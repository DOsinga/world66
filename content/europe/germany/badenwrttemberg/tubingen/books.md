**Hyperion, by Friedrich Hölderlin.** Hölderlin's only completed novel — a passionate epistolary book about a young Greek's struggle for freedom — was begun and partly written in Tübingen. Reading it before climbing to the Hölderlinturm makes the visit far more affecting.

**Phenomenology of Spirit, by Georg Wilhelm Friedrich Hegel.** Heavy going, but Hegel began the work in his Tübingen seminary years and the town remains saturated in the German philosophical idealism he, Schelling and Hölderlin shaped together.

**Diaries 1942–54, by Max Frisch.** The Swiss novelist spent extended periods in Tübingen and used it as the setting for parts of his fiction; the diaries give a quietly observed picture of the post-war town.
