The Marquardtei has been a Tübingen institution for decades — equal parts bar, restaurant, music venue and meeting point for the town's broadly left-wing student crowd. The kitchen does honest bistro food (good burgers, vegetarian plates, weekend brunch), the beer list is wide, and the evening atmosphere is a mix of after-work drinks and student arguments.
