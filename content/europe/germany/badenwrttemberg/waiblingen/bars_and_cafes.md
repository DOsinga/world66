---
title: "Bars and Cafes"
type: section
---

The [Cafe Tagblatt](/europe/germany/badenwrttemberg/waiblingen/cafe_tagblatt) on the Marktplatz is the easy daytime stop. For evening drinks in fine weather, the [Biergarten Schwaneninsel](/europe/germany/badenwrttemberg/waiblingen/biergarten_schwaneninsel) on the river is hard to beat. The Postplatz at the lower end of the old town has several wine bars serving Trollinger and Lemberger from the surrounding Remstal vineyards.
