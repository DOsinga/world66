Waiblingen's appeal is the small, almost intact 17th-century old town, reconstructed after the 1634 fire. The compact circuit of the [city wall](/europe/germany/badenwrttemberg/waiblingen/stadtmauer) takes in the [Hochwachtturm](/europe/germany/badenwrttemberg/waiblingen/hochwachtturm), the [Beinsteiner Tor](/europe/germany/badenwrttemberg/waiblingen/beinsteiner_tor), and several smaller towers. Inside the walls, the [Marktplatz](/europe/germany/badenwrttemberg/waiblingen/marktplatz) and the [Michaelskirche](/europe/germany/badenwrttemberg/waiblingen/michaelskirche) are the obvious anchors.

The town's one major modern cultural draw is the [Galerie Stihl](/europe/germany/badenwrttemberg/waiblingen/galerie_stihl) on the Rems — a serious contemporary print and drawing gallery endowed by the local chainsaw firm.
