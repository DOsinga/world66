Waldenbuch is a small town south of [Stuttgart](/europe/germany/badenwrttemberg/stuttgart), sitting at the edge of the Schonbuch forest. It would be easy to dismiss as a suburb, but it has a clear travel hook: this is Ritter Sport's home town, and chocolate, art, and everyday culture are packed into a walkable centre.

The modern [Museum Ritter](/europe/germany/badenwrttemberg/waldenbuch/museum_ritter) shows geometric and abstract art beside the Ritter Sport visitor centre. Across town, [Museum der Alltagskultur](/europe/germany/badenwrttemberg/waldenbuch/museum_der_alltagskultur) fills Schloss Waldenbuch with objects from ordinary German life.

Waldenbuch is best as an easy day from Stuttgart, especially for families or travellers who like small museums more than big-city checklists. Add a wander through the old centre, and the town has enough personality for a relaxed half-day.
