Museum der Alltagskultur turns ordinary things into the main subject: household objects, consumer goods, folk culture, and the habits of daily life. It is housed in Schloss Waldenbuch, a former Wurttemberg hunting lodge.

The museum is stronger than it sounds. It is a good counterweight to Stuttgart's car museums, especially if you are interested in how people lived rather than what engineers built.
