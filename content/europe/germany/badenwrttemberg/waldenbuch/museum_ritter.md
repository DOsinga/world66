Museum Ritter is the grown-up half of the Ritter Sport stop. Its collection, built by Marli Hoppe-Ritter, focuses on geometric and abstract art, especially work that plays with the square.

The building is modern, bright, and easy to combine with the chocolate exhibition next door. It gives Waldenbuch more depth than a factory-shop detour would have on its own.
