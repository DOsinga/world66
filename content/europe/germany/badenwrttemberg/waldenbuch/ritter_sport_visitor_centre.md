The Ritter Sport Visitor Centre is not a factory tour, but it is still the stop most people associate with Waldenbuch. The exhibition covers the company's history, cocoa, and chocolate making, while the shop sells the full range and special editions.

Families should check workshop times in advance. Making a custom chocolate bar is more memorable than simply filling a bag in the shop.
