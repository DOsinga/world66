RömerMuseum in Weissenburg presents finds from the Roman frontier around the Altmuhl Valley. Its highlight is the Weissenburg Roman treasure, buried during the third-century crisis and rediscovered in 1979, with bronze statuettes and other objects from the local Roman settlement and military zone. It pairs naturally with the Roman Limes sites nearby.
