---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

The Altmühl Valley's season runs from May to October. May and June offer pleasant temperatures, wildflowers on the limestone slopes, and fewer crowds. July and August are the peak months for cycling and canoeing — the rivers and trails fill up but the infrastructure (guesthouses, rental shops, transport) works well. September is arguably the best month: warm days, autumn colours starting on the cliffs, harvest festivals in the towns, and noticeably fewer visitors.

The valley is quiet in winter but a few towns (Eichstätt, Weissenburg) remain active year-round. Museums along the route tend to close or reduce hours from November to April.
