---
title: "Getting There"
type: section
---

Ansbach has a direct train connection to [Nuremberg](/europe/germany/bavaria/nuremberg) (about 45 minutes) and is on the regional rail network linking Nuremberg with Crailsheim and Stuttgart. From Nuremberg, trains run every hour or two. From Munich, the journey requires a change in Nuremberg and takes around 2 hours total.

By car, Ansbach sits on the A6 motorway between Nuremberg and Heilbronn. The town is about 40 km southwest of Nuremberg.
