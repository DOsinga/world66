Augsburg's Textilmuseum explores the city's long textile and drapery industry, using machinery, fabrics, and industrial history to show how cloth production shaped the local economy. It is especially strong for visitors interested in technical processes, design, and the social history behind manufacturing.

The museum adds useful context to Augsburg's trade history and makes a good pairing with the city's water-management and merchant-family sites.
