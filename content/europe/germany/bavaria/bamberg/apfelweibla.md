A small brass doorknob on a residential side street in the upper town, easy to walk past if you do not know it is there. Cast in the early nineteenth century in the form of a grimacing apple-seller (Apfelweibla in local dialect), it commemorates a character from E.T.A. Hoffmann's story 'Der Goldne Topf'. The knob is now polished mirror-bright by generations of hands rubbing it for luck. Eisgrube 14, ten minutes' walk from the cathedral.
