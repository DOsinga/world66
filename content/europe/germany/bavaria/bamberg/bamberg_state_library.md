A working research library on the cathedral square, housed in the Neue Residenz, with one of the country's most important early manuscript collections. Rotating displays in the small public exhibition room show off pieces from the holdings — the Bamberg Apocalypse, the Henry II Sacramentary, the famous Bamberg Antiphonary. Not every visit catches the same items, but something always good is on view. Free.
