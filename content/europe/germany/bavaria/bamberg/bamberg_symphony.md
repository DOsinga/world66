A serious symphony orchestra with an unusual founding story — built in 1946 by German musicians expelled from Prague and reassembled around the Czech-Bohemian repertoire they brought with them. The Konzert- und Kongresshalle on the riverbank south of Schillerplatz is their home; the season runs September through June, with a famous Mahler cycle and a Bruckner cycle on regular rotation. International soloists pass through constantly.
