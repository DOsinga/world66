---
title: "Bars and Cafes"
type: section
---

The city has more breweries per capita than almost any other in Germany — eleven within the city limits, fifty-something more in the surrounding villages. Beyond [Schlenkerla](/europe/germany/bavaria/bamberg/schlenkerla), the Rauchbier classic, try **Mahrs Bräu** in the Wunderburg district (the U-Bier, an unfiltered lager, is the local cult), **Greifenklau** by the Altenburg (a hillside garden with views over the city), **Spezial-Keller** for a second take on smoked beer, and the gardens above the city in summer — **Greifenklau-Keller**, **Wilde Rose Keller**, and **Spezial-Keller** on the Stephansberg form a tight circuit. For coffee, **Café Müller** on Austraße is the slow morning option; for late-night cocktails, the bars along [Sandstraße](/europe/germany/bavaria/bamberg/sandstrasse) take over.
