A small, perfectly preserved early-nineteenth-century natural history collection inside the seminary building near the cathedral. The crown jewel is the Bird Hall — a long room with two galleries of wood-and-glass cases unchanged since the 1820s, holding hundreds of bird specimens posed in the dramatic style of their time, with extraordinary survivals of nineteenth-century wax-modelled fruit beneath them. Open limited hours; check before going.
