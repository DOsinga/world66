---
title: "Eating Out"
type: section
---

Bamberg eats Franconian: smoked pork shoulder (Schäuferla), Bratwurst with horseradish, sour-cooked beef (Sauerbraten), and the long-stemmed local onion stuffed with minced pork (Bamberger Zwiebel). [Schlenkerla](/europe/germany/bavaria/bamberg/schlenkerla) is the obvious place to start — communal tables, smoky beer, the classic Schäuferla — and **Spezial-Keller** on the Stephansberg gives you the same food with a hillside beer garden. **Klosterbräu**, the city's oldest brewery, runs a simpler bierstube down by the river. For something more refined, **Eckerts** on Untere Sandstraße does seasonal Franconian with a wine list; **Pfälzer Stube am Dom** on the cathedral square handles Pfalz-style cooking in old vaulted rooms.
