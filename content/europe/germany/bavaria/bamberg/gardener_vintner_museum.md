story: "Bamberg's market gardeners (Gärtner) have grown vegetables on small plots inside the city walls since the Middle Ages. ---

A small, specific museum housed in the Mittelstraße — the original Gärtnerviertel — telling the story of Bamberg's centuries-old tradition of growing vegetables and salad crops on tiny plots inside the city limits. Tools, sepia photographs, seed catalogues, and a working garden plot in the courtyard with the local heritage varieties (Bamberger Süßholz, Bamberger Hörnla potato). Half an hour is enough; the surrounding district is the real museum.
