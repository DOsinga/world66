Originally a Benedictine collegiate church founded by Empress Kunigunde, rebuilt in Baroque form in the early eighteenth century and given to the city's Protestant community in 1807. Inside is a brightly painted three-storey nave with an organ that fills the west wall. From the steps in front, a fine angled view of the cathedral across the rooftops.
