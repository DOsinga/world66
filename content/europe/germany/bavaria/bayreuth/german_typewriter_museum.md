The German Typewriter Museum is one of Bayreuth's more specialised museums, devoted to the machines that turned writing into a mechanical act before computers made the keyboard almost invisible. The collection is strongest if you enjoy industrial design, office history, or the satisfying logic of old mechanisms.

It is a small, focused stop rather than a headline Bayreuth sight, but that is part of its charm. After Wagner, palaces, and baroque gardens, the museum offers a different kind of cultural history: everyday tools, changing technology, and the look of work before the screen.
