The Jean Paul Museum is a literary counterweight to Bayreuth’s Wagner-heavy image. It focuses on the writer Jean Paul, who spent important years in the city and whose humour, digressions, and odd tenderness made him one of German literature’s distinctive voices.

The museum is best for travellers who like small, focused cultural stops. Even if you are not steeped in German Romanticism, it broadens Bayreuth beyond opera and court architecture, showing the city as a place of books as well as music.
