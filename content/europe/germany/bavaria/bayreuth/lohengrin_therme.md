Lohengrin Therme is Bayreuth's thermal bath complex, with warm indoor and outdoor pools, a large sauna area and a spa for massages and treatments. It is a good bad-weather stop or a slow recovery afternoon after a run of palaces, gardens and opera history.

The sauna and spa areas are clothes-free zones, and towels and bathrobes can be rented. Children under six are not admitted, and the spa has a higher minimum age.
