The Museum of Agricultural Tools and Equipment is a specialist stop for travellers interested in the rural world behind Franconia's towns. Its collection of tools and farm equipment points to the labour, seasons, and practical ingenuity that shaped the countryside around Bayreuth.

This is not a headline museum, and casual visitors may find it narrow. It earns its place if you like local history, old machinery, or the quieter stories that sit behind a region better known for opera and palaces.
