The Richard Wagner Museum occupies Haus Wahnfried, the composer's former Bayreuth residence. The museum covers Wagner's life, work, family circle, and the festival culture that made the city a pilgrimage site for opera lovers.

The house and garden are part of the visit. Wagner and his wife Cosima are buried here, and the setting helps explain why Bayreuth became so closely tied to his music.
