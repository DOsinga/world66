---
title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Berchtesgaden's appeal divides into two very different spheres: mountain scenery and Nazi history. Most visitors do both on the same day. The Königssee lake and the Eagle's Nest (Kehlsteinhaus) are the most visited; the Dokumentation Obersalzberg museum provides essential context for the latter. The salt mines are a crowd-pleaser with a theatrical side.
