Burghausen is the Bavarian border town with the absurdly long castle. The [Burghausen Castle](/europe/germany/bavaria/burghausen/burghausen_castle) ridge runs for more than a kilometre above the Salzach river, all towers, walls, courtyards and small houses, with Austria staring back from the opposite bank. It is one of those places that looks theatrical from a distance and becomes stranger the closer you get.

The [old town](/europe/germany/bavaria/burghausen/stadtplatz) lies squeezed between the river and the cliff, its long Stadtplatz lined with pastel facades and arcaded shopfronts. Above it, stairways and lanes climb to the castle; below it, the Salzach promenade is good for a slow walk. On the inland side of the castle ridge, the [Woehrsee](/europe/germany/bavaria/burghausen/woehrsee) gives the town a softer face, with swimming lawns and views back up to the walls.

Burghausen is not a big-city stop, and that is part of its appeal. Come for the castle, stay for the way the town hangs together: river, ridge, lake, and a compact old centre that still feels lived in. It combines easily with [Marktl am Inn](/europe/germany/bavaria/marktl_am_inn) and Altoetting if you are exploring southeastern Bavaria by car or bike.
