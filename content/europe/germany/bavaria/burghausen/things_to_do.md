---
title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

The reason to come is the **Burghausen Castle**, a fortified chain of courtyards that stretches along the ridge for roughly 1,051 metres. Give it time: the best visit is not a single viewpoint but a slow walk from one end to the other, with detours into the **City Museum**, the **State Gallery**, and the small chapels and towers along the way.

Below the ridge, **Stadtplatz** is the town's public room, long and narrow, with the Salzach on one side and the castle cliff on the other. **St. Jakob's Church** anchors the lower town, while the **Woehrsee** on the far side of the ridge is the place to swim, picnic, or photograph the castle reflected in the water.
