type: section
---

Coburg rewards a slow visit. The Veste fortress above the town is one of Germany's finest medieval castles, and the ducal palace in the centre has a history that reaches across the royal families of Europe. Below those headline attractions lies a compact, walkable old town with a handsome market square, a fine church, and a theatre scene that punches above the city's weight.
