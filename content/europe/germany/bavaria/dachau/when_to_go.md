---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

Dachau can be visited year-round. The memorial site is open Tuesday to Sunday, 9am to 5pm, and is closed on Mondays, Christmas Day, and New Year's Day. The castle gardens are open May to September on weekends; the gallery has shorter winter hours. For the best views south to the Alps from the castle hill, choose a clear day after a cold front has passed — typically autumn or spring days with a north wind.

The town is close enough to Munich (25 minutes by S-Bahn) to visit as a day trip at any time of year.
