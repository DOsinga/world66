Aromagarten Erlangen is a small aromatic plant garden run by the university near the clinic quarter. Its paths wind through beds of herbs, spice plants, scented perennials, and a marshy section, making it a quiet change of pace from the town centre.

It is not a grand botanical garden, but it rewards a slow wander if you are already exploring northern Erlangen. The garden works best in the growing season, when the plantings are fragrant and labelled.
