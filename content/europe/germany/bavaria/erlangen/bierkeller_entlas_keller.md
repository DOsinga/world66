The beer cellars at Entlas Keller show how Erlangen stored beer before artificial cooling. Cellars were dug into the hillside and packed with ice to keep barrels cool through the year. Visitors can see part of this underground network, which gives useful context to the city's beer culture and the Bergkirchweih area above it.
