This Reformed church on Bohlenplatz is part of Erlangen's Huguenot and planned-city story, reflecting the Protestant communities that shaped the baroque new town. Its restrained architecture contrasts with more ornate southern German churches.

It is a modest stop, but a useful one for visitors looking at Erlangen beyond the university and palace garden. Treat it as a short architectural and historical marker while walking through the centre.
