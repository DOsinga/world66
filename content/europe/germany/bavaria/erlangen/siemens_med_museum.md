The Siemens Med Museum is a focused industrial and technology museum in a city deeply tied to Siemens and medical engineering. Exhibits trace the development of diagnostic and treatment equipment, including the kind of imaging technology that made Erlangen a serious research and industry town.

It is most rewarding for travellers interested in science, design, or the less romantic side of German urban history. The museum also pairs well with Erlangen's university atmosphere, showing why the city feels more technical and academic than its baroque centre alone suggests.
