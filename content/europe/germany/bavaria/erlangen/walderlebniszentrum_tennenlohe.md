Walderlebniszentrum Tennenlohe is a forest experience centre on the southern edge of Erlangen. It presents the local woodland through hands-on displays, workshops, and family-friendly activities rather than a formal museum visit.

It is most useful for families or anyone already exploring the Tennenlohe forest area. In summer, weekend cafe openings can make it an easy pause during a walk.
