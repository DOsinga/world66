Freising is a small Bavarian city about 35 kilometres north of Munich and just a few kilometres from Munich Airport — which makes it both easy to reach and surprisingly overlooked. It's one of the oldest cities in Bavaria, with a history stretching back to the early medieval period. The bishop's seat here predates Munich itself, and for centuries Freising mattered more than its famous southern neighbour.

The [Domberg](/europe/germany/bavaria/freising/domberg) hill above town is the main reason to visit: a cathedral complex of real beauty, anchored by the [Cathedral of St Mary and St Corbinian](/europe/germany/bavaria/freising/freising_cathedral), whose Romanesque crypt dates from the 11th century. The attached [Diocesan Museum](/europe/germany/bavaria/freising/diocesan_museum) holds an excellent collection of Bavarian religious art. The old streets around the [Marktplatz](/europe/germany/bavaria/freising/marktplatz) below are pleasant without being showy.

Freising's other claim is [Weihenstephan](/europe/germany/bavaria/freising/weihenstephan_brewery), a Benedictine monastery on a hill at the edge of town that has been brewing beer continuously since at least 1040 — making it the oldest operating brewery in the world. The [Bräustüberl](/europe/germany/bavaria/freising/braustuberl_weihenstephan) on-site is the obvious place to sample the results. Weihenstephan is now also home to the Technical University of Munich's brewing faculty, which gives the whole operation a suitably rigorous academic weight. The [Sichtungsgarten](/europe/germany/bavaria/freising/sichtungsgarten_weihenstephan) — a remarkable botanical trial garden on the same hill — is worth an hour of anyone's time.
