---
title: When to Go
type: section
---

Freising is worth visiting any time the weather permits a walk uphill — which in Bavaria means April through October. The Domberg and Weihenstephan are outdoor experiences as much as indoor ones, and both benefit from good weather and long days.

Late spring and early summer (May to June) are the best months: the Sichtungsgarten is in bloom, the beer garden terraces are open, and the alpine views from the Domberg are at their clearest before the summer haze sets in. July and August are warm and busy with Munich day-trippers; still fine, but the Bräustüberl terrace will be crowded at weekends.

Freising itself has no major festivals of note, but the Freising Cathedral stages Christmas concerts in December and the atmospheric Domberg gains a particular quality in winter light.
