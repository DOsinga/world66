Cadolzburg Castle rises above the market town of Cadolzburg west of Fürth, a substantial Hohenzollern fortress restored after wartime damage. Its massing, gate structures, and hilltop position make it feel more complete and purposeful than many small regional castles.

Inside, exhibitions explain medieval rule, courtly display, and the castle's role in Franconian history. It is a strong half-day trip from Fürth or Nuremberg, especially for travellers who want a castle visit that is less crowded than Bavaria's headline sights.
