The Rundfunk Museum is one of Fuerth's more distinctive museums, devoted to radio, television, recording, and broadcasting technology. The collection traces how sound and moving images entered everyday life, with old receivers, studio equipment, domestic televisions, and hands-on displays.

It is especially rewarding if you like industrial design or the social history of technology. Fuerth often sits in Nuremberg's shadow, but this museum gives the city a specialist attraction of its own and makes a good indoor stop on a Franconian city day.
