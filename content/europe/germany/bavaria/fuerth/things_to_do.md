---
title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Fürth rewards slow walking. The Altstadt holds a remarkable concentration of Biedermeier and Jugendstil architecture, most of it intact — the city escaped the worst of the 1945 bombing that flattened its neighbour. The [Jewish Museum Franken](/europe/germany/bavaria/fuerth/jewish_museum_franken) is one of the best Jewish history museums in Germany, and the [Ludwig-Erhard-Zentrum](/europe/germany/bavaria/fuerth/ludwig_erhard_zentrum) puts an unfairly overlooked figure of post-war European history in sharp focus. For a slower afternoon, the [Stadtpark](/europe/germany/bavaria/fuerth/stadtpark) is genuinely beautiful.
