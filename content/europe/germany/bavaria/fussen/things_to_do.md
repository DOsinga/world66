title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

The castles at Hohenschwangau, four kilometres east of town, are the reason most visitors come. [Neuschwanstein](/europe/germany/bavaria/fussen/neuschwanstein) is the postcard one — the Disney prototype, the fairy-tale silhouette on the crag — and [Hohenschwangau](/europe/germany/bavaria/fussen/hohenschwangau) below it is the older, plainer, and arguably more interesting royal family seat where Ludwig II grew up. Tickets must be booked online days in advance; both castles can be combined in a single half-day.

Don't underestimate Füssen itself, which has a handsome Altstadt of painted facades, the baroque [St. Mang's Abbey](/europe/germany/bavaria/fussen/stmangsabbey) on the Lech river, and the [Hohes Schloss](/europe/germany/bavaria/fussen/hohesschloss) (High Castle) with its remarkable trompe-l'oeil painted courtyard. The [Forggensee](/europe/germany/bavaria/fussen/forggensee) lake stretches north and the [Tegelberg](/europe/germany/bavaria/fussen/tegelberg) mountain cable car east — both worth a half-day each for hiking or for the view.
