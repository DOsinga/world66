Fernweh Park is a quirky Hof landmark built around signs from cities and roads around the world. Its name roughly evokes wanderlust, and the appeal is the playful accumulation of place names rather than grand architecture or scenery.

It is a short stop, best for travelers who enjoy odd civic monuments and photo opportunities. Combine it with Theresienstein Park or a walk through Hof rather than making a special journey only for the signs.
