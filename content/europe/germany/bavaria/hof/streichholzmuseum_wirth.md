Streichholzmuseum Wirth is one of those small, eccentric museums that can make a provincial stop memorable. Its focus is matchsticks and matchstick models, a narrow subject handled with the patience and craft that niche collections often reveal.

It will not anchor a trip to Hof, but it is exactly the kind of odd detour World66 likes to keep on the map. Go if you enjoy handmade miniatures, local collections, or museums that clearly exist because one person cared deeply about a very specific thing.
