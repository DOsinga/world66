Maria de Victoria Church is a small Baroque church in Ingolstadt's old town, best known for its enormous ceiling fresco. The exterior is modest, but the interior is rich with stucco, painted perspective, and theatrical detail.

It is worth stepping inside while walking the historic centre, especially if you are interested in southern German Baroque art.
