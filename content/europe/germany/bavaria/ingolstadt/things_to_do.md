Ingolstadt has a concentrated historic centre that rewards a day's exploration: the late-Gothic Münster, the Kreuztor gatehouse, the Deutsches Medizinhistorisches Museum, and the Audi Forum for car enthusiasts. The Frankenstein connection provides a literary side-trip for those who know the novel.
