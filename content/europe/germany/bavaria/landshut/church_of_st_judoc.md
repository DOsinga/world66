The Church of St. Judoc, usually called Jodokskirche locally, is Landshut's second-oldest church after St Martin's. It was built from the late 14th century onward in Gothic style, with a brick tower rising about 77 metres above the surrounding streets.

It is less famous than St Martin's, but that makes it a useful second stop if you want to understand Landshut's medieval scale beyond the main postcard view. The church sits just east of the old centre, an easy walk from the Altstadt.
