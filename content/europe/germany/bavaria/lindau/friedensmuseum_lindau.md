Friedensmuseum Lindau is a small museum devoted to peace, conflict, responsibility, and reconciliation. It offers a reflective change of pace from Lindau's harbor views, island streets, and Lake Constance scenery.

The museum is not a blockbuster attraction, but it has a clear subject and enough cultural purpose to merit consideration for visitors who like thoughtful local museums. Check current hours, as smaller museums can have limited opening times.
