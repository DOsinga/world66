The Lindauer Dahlienschau is a seasonal dahlia display on the edge of Lindau. The flower fields make a gentle short walk, especially when the dahlias are in full colour.

The surrounding apple and pear orchards add to the appeal, and the small hill toward Streitelsfingen gives good views back over Lindau and Lake Constance.
