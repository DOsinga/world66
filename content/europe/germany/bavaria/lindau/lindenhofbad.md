Lindenhofbad is a relaxed Lake Constance swimming spot on Lindau's western shore, away from the densest tourist flow on the island. Lawns, lake access, and views across the water make it a practical summer stop rather than a formal sight.

Its value depends on season and weather, but in warm months it gives travellers a very local way to enjoy the lake. Combine it with a walk along the shore or nearby villas if you want a slower alternative to Lindau harbour and the old town.
