Marktl History Museum gives a compact overview of the town and surrounding region, with rooms covering local life from prehistoric settlement onward. It is a modest stop, but useful if you are already in Marktl for the Benedict XVI birthplace or Saint Oswald's Church and want more context on the town beyond its papal connection.
