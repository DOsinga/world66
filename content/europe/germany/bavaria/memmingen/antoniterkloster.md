The Antoniterkloster is a former monastery of the Hospital Brothers of St Anthony, tucked close to St Martin's. The complex is unusually well preserved, and its arcaded, enclosed feel gives a different texture from the open market square.

Today it holds cultural uses, including local museum space, exhibitions and events. It is worth a look for the building alone, especially if you are following the old-town walking route.
