The market square is the natural place to start in Memmingen. The Renaissance town hall, the Grosszunft guild house and the painted Steuerhaus stand close enough together to make the square feel like an open-air lesson in imperial-city confidence.

Come on a market morning if you can. The stalls soften the formality of the facades and make it easier to imagine the old town as a working place rather than a preserved backdrop.
