The oldest part of the original Munich ducal residence, built around 1253 — the Wittelsbachs lived here before the Residenz was built nearby. The complex of medieval buildings around a courtyard has been heavily modified over the centuries, but the core mediaeval structure is intact. A small museum covers the history of the Holy Roman Empire and its connection to the site.

The Monkey Tower (Affenturm) is the most distinctive feature — look for the carved figures. The Alter Hof is often overlooked in favour of the grander Residenz nearby, but it gives a better sense of Munich's original mediaeval scale. The courtyard is free to enter; museum admission is modest.
