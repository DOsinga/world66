A traditional beer hall attached to the Augustiner brewery in the Neuhausen district, about 2 km west of the Altstadt. Augustiner is Munich's oldest brewery and arguably makes the finest Helles lager in the city — the light, slightly malty style that is the everyday drinking beer of Munich rather than the darker Märzen associated with Oktoberfest.

The Bräustuben is unpretentious and local — the clientele is a mix of brewery workers, neighbourhood residents, and the food is Bavarian and good. The schnitzel with potato salad and the roast pork are the choices to make. No reservations; large tables mean you'll share with strangers, which is the point.
