Bavaria Filmstadt is a visitor attraction at the Bavaria Film studios south of central Munich. The standard visit is a guided tour through working film sets, with a short 4D cinema ride and displays tied to German film and television production.

It is most rewarding for families and film fans, especially if you are curious about what happens behind the camera. Tours are often in German, so check language options before going if that matters to you.
