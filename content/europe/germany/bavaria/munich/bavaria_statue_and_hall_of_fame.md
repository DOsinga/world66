The Bavaria statue and Hall of Fame stand above Theresienwiese, the Oktoberfest grounds, as a 19th-century statement of Bavarian pride. The bronze female figure is 18.5 metres high, backed by a classical hall honouring notable Bavarians.

When it is open, a narrow stairway climbs inside the statue to a tiny viewing platform in the head. The view over the Wiesn is especially memorable around Oktoberfest, though that is also when the site is at its most crowded.
