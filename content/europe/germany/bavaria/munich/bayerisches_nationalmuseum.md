The Bayerisches Nationalmuseum is one of Munich's major museums, with European decorative art and cultural history from the Middle Ages into the early 20th century. The collection ranges across armour, furniture, ceramics, textiles, porcelain, and carved religious art.

It is especially strong if you like objects that show how people lived, worshipped, dressed, and decorated their homes. From autumn to spring, the nativity-scene collection adds another reason to visit.
