Blutenburg Castle is a moated 15th-century hunting lodge on Munich's western edge, built for Duke Albert III of Bavaria and later expanded by Duke Sigismund. It was made for leisure rather than hard defence, with a courtyard, gatehouse, lake, and late Gothic chapel.

The interior is partly used by the International Youth Library, so visits are more about the setting, chapel, courtyard, and occasional cultural events than a full palace tour. It pairs well with a slower day in Munich's west, away from the old-town crowds.
