The BMW brand and customer experience centre adjacent to the BMW Museum and headquarters complex in the Olympic Park area. The building — completed in 2007, designed by the Vienna architectural firm Coop Himmelb(l)au — is one of Munich's more striking pieces of contemporary architecture, with a sweeping glass canopy and open exhibition floors.

Entry is free and no booking is required. The ground floor contains the current BMW lineup and several rotating displays on design and technology. The BMW Museum next door requires a ticket and covers the company's century of engineering history. The factory itself offers guided tours that can be booked through the BMW website; the production line visit is genuinely interesting. All three are in the Olympic Park area, easily combined with Olympiapark on the same visit.
