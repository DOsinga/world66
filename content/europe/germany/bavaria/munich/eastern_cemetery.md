Eastern Cemetery was laid out in 1817 and remains one of Munich's important burial grounds. Its graves include Bavarian nobles, physicians, artists, and public figures, making it a useful stop for visitors interested in the city's 19th- and 20th-century history.

The crematorium also has a darker history: during the Nazi period it was used for victims of the regime, and after the Nuremberg Trials it received the bodies of several executed war criminals. Visit quietly and allow time to read the grave markers.
