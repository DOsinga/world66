At the southern edge of the English Garden, the Eisbach forms a standing river wave that has become one of Munich's most unusual urban sights. Experienced surfers queue on the banks and ride the narrow wave one at a time, often drawing a steady crowd from the nearby park paths.

It is best treated as a viewing stop unless you are already a skilled river surfer. The current is strong and the wave is not suitable for beginners.
