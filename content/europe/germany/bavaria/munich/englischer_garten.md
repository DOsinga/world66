Larger than New York's Central Park, the Englischer Garten runs from the northern edge of the Altstadt to the city's northern suburbs — 3.7 km of landscaped parkland along the Isar river. It was laid out from 1789, partly inspired by English landscape gardens, and has evolved into Munich's great outdoor living room.

On warm days the meadows fill with sunbathers (nude sunbathing on the main meadow is legal and common), the beer gardens fill up, the Kleinhesseloher See lake attracts paddlers, and the Chinese Tower beer garden plays Bavarian folk music. The river channels through the park feed the Eisbach surfing wave at the southern end. The Japanese Tea House on the island in the middle of the lake is a peaceful stop. The park is always free and open.
