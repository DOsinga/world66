The aviation branch of the Deutsches Museum, located at the historic Schleissheim Royal Airfield north of Munich (about 20 km from the centre). The collection covers German aviation history from early biplanes through the Jet Age, with particular strength in Luftwaffe aircraft and early post-war German civil aviation.

The working restoration workshop is visible from the museum floor — aircraft are serviced and restored in view of visitors. The adjacent Schleissheim Palace complex (three palaces, baroque gardens) can be combined with a Flugwerft visit for a full day out of the city. Take the S1 to Oberschleissheim and walk or cycle 2 km to the museum.
