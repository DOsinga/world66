Friedhof Bogenhausen is a small cemetery beside St Georg in one of Munich's older districts. Its appeal is less size than atmosphere: compact paths, an attractive church setting, and graves of well-known German cultural figures.

Film director Rainer Werner Fassbinder, writer Erich Kaestner, author Oskar Maria Graf, and producer Bernd Eichinger are among the notable names buried here.
