Munich's main cultural centre in the Haidhausen district, on the Isar river banks — a large 1985 complex housing multiple concert halls, the Munich Philharmonic's main venue, the municipal library, and several event spaces. The main Philharmonie hall is currently under renovation; classical music events are running at the temporary Gasteig HP8 venue while renovation continues.

The building's location on a hill above the Isar makes the foyer terraces worth visiting even without a concert ticket. The surrounding Haidhausen neighbourhood has Munich's most interesting collection of independent bars and restaurants. Check the Gasteig website for concert schedules; the Munich Philharmonic plays most weekends from September to June.
