The Church of the Holy Spirit, adjacent to the Viktualienmarkt, has a complex history: it began as a hospice chapel in the 13th century, was rebuilt in Gothic style, and then transformed into its current late Baroque form in the early 18th century. The nave is a handsome fusion of Gothic structure and Baroque decoration, with ceiling frescoes by the Asam brothers and ornate side chapels.

The church is free to enter and generally uncrowded. The interior is considerably more interesting than the unremarkable exterior suggests. The combination of Gothic hall-church proportions with exuberant Baroque surface decoration is typical of the Bavarian Baroque style and is handled here with particular skill.
