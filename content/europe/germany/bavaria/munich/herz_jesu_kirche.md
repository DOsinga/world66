Herz Jesu Kirche is a modern Catholic church built after an earlier church on the site burned down. From outside it looks like a large blue glass cube; inside, a warm wooden inner cube gives the space a very different feel.

It is a good stop for modern architecture rather than a traditional Munich church visit, and works best as a short detour for people already exploring the western side of the city.
