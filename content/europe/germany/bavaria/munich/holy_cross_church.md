Holy Cross Church is a Gothic Revival Roman Catholic church in Munich's Au district, built between 1866 and 1886. Its 95-metre bell tower is visible from a distance, and the church survived the Second World War without major bomb damage. It is a worthwhile detour for visitors exploring the east bank of the Isar and Haidhausen.
