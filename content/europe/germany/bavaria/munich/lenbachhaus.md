The city museum of Munich, housed in the villa of portrait painter Franz von Lenbach and an extensive modern extension by Foster + Partners. The permanent collection has two great strengths: the Blue Rider (Blaue Reiter) collection of early German Expressionism — Kandinsky, Klee, Marc, Macke, Münter — and the 20th-century Munich art scene up to the present.

The Blue Rider rooms are among the most important in European Modernist art. Kandinsky's abstractions from 1910 to 1914, when he developed pure abstraction, are here in force. Franz Marc's bright animal paintings are among the most-reproduced German Expressionist works. The museum's contemporary wing hosts rotating exhibitions of high quality. Closed Mondays.
