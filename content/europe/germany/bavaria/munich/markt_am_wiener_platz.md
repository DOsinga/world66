A traditional outdoor market in the heart of Haidhausen, a few streets east of the Isar, running Monday through Saturday. Unlike the more famous Viktualienmarkt, Wiener Platz retains a strongly local character — it's primarily a grocery market for the neighbourhood rather than a tourist destination, though visitors are welcome.

The surrounding neighbourhood has some of Munich's best independent cafes, wine bars, and restaurants — the streets around Kirchenstrasse and Franziskanerstrasse are worth exploring for dinner. The Haidhausen neighbourhood generally has a more relaxed, less showy character than Schwabing, with a younger crowd and lower prices.
