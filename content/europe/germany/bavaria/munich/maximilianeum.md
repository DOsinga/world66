The Maximilianeum closes the grand vista east from Maximilianstrasse, its Renaissance-style facade rising above the Isar. Built in the 19th century as a foundation for gifted students, it now also houses the Bavarian parliament; even from outside, it is one of Haidhausen's defining landmarks.
