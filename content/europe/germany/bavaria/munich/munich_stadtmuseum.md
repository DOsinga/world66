The city museum occupies a former armoury and stable complex near the Viktualienmarkt and covers Munich history from the Middle Ages to the present. The collection is eclectic: medieval arms, 19th-century city models, puppet theatre (one of Europe's best collections), Biedermeier furniture, and the recently added National Socialism exhibition.

The puppet and doll theatre collection in the basement is unexpectedly wonderful — centuries of theatrical puppetry from across Europe and Asia. The photography collection is strong and well-displayed. The café in the courtyard is good for lunch. Closed Mondays.
