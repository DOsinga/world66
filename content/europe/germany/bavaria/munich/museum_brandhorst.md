Museum Brandhorst is part of Munich's Kunstareal museum district and focuses on modern and contemporary art. The permanent collection is especially strong on Andy Warhol and Cy Twombly, with paintings, sculpture, installations, and rotating exhibitions in a bright, purpose-built museum.
