The Museum of Urban and Contemporary Art, usually shortened to MUCA, brings street art and urban art indoors in central Munich. Its exhibitions mix permanent works with changing shows, making it a useful stop for visitors who want a contemporary counterpoint to the old-town churches and royal collections nearby.
