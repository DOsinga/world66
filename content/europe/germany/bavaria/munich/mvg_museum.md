The MVG Museum tells Munich's public transport story inside a former tram repair and assembly hall. Its collection includes historic trams, buses, and works vehicles, with German and English signage and a U-Bahn driving simulator among the hands-on highlights.

It is a strong niche museum for transit fans and families, and a useful change of pace from Munich's art palaces and royal sights.
