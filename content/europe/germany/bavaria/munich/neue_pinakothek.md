The museum dedicated to European art from 1800 to 1900, currently closed for extensive structural renovation. The permanent collection, normally on display here, includes important works by Friedrich, Courbet, Manet, Cézanne, and Van Gogh, as well as the most significant collection of German Romanticism in Europe. Selected works from the collection are displayed in the Alte Pinakothek during the closure.

When the museum reopens, allow two to three hours for the permanent collection. The 19th-century German work — landscape painting particularly — is extraordinary and largely unknown outside Germany.
