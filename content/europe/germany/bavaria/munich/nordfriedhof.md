Nordfriedhof is Munich's large northern cemetery, opened in the late 19th century as the city expanded. It has an impressive funeral hall and many notable burials, including physicist Ernst Mach, historian Oswald Spengler, and singer-actor Johannes Heesters.

The cemetery is a quiet place for a reflective walk, with the main interest in its funerary architecture, old tree-lined paths, and the way it records Munich's growth beyond the older central districts.
