Old South Cemetery was laid out in 1563 after Munich's churchyards ran out of burial space during a plague. It later became the city's main burial ground and holds the graves of figures such as Josef von Fraunhofer, Leo von Klenze, Justus von Liebig, Georg Simon Ohm, and Carl Spitzweg.

The cemetery is no longer active, but its elaborate gravestones, crypts, and quiet paths make it a worthwhile historical stop south of the old town.
