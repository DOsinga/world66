A small island in the Isar river adjacent to the Haidhausen neighbourhood, accessible by footbridge from the Maximilianstrasse end. The island is undeveloped parkland with a few benches and trees, used primarily by locals for walking, picnicking, and wild swimming in the Isar. The river here runs fast and clear — a glacial channel that Munich has gradually rewilded after a century of concrete channelling.

The Isarplan project has restored the gravel banks and natural vegetation along this stretch of the river. Wild swimming is legal in the Isar and well-established in Munich culture; observe the current carefully and always enter upstream of where you want to exit. The footbridge to Praterinsel connects the Haidhausen bank with the Maximilianstrasse/Altstadt side.
