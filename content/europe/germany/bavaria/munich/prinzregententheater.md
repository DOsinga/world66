Prinzregententheater is a handsome Art Nouveau theatre in Haidhausen, modelled on Bayreuth's Wagner-Festspielhaus. It is home to the Bavarian Drama Academy and stages opera, musicals, and theatre.

Even if you are not attending a performance, the building is worth noting for its early 20th-century design and its place in Munich's theatre scene.
