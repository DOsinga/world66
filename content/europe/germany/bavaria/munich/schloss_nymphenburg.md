Schloss Nymphenburg is Munich's great Baroque palace, begun as a 17th-century summer residence and later expanded into a vast royal complex. Visitors come for the ornate state rooms, the palace park, and smaller museums covering porcelain, court carriages, sleighs, and natural history.
