St Benno's is a neo-Romanesque Catholic church in Maxvorstadt, completed in 1895 as the district expanded north of Munich's old centre. It was badly damaged in World War II and rebuilt close to its original form.

The church is worth a short detour for its brick massing, twin towers, and the local details around Saint Benno, an 11th-century bishop of Meissen. Outside, a fish with a key in its mouth recalls the legend of the saint recovering his cathedral keys from a fish.
