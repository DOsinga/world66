St Boniface's Abbey is a Benedictine abbey founded by King Ludwig I in the 19th century. Much of the complex was damaged in World War II and only partly rebuilt, which gives the church a plainer feel than Munich's showier Baroque interiors. It is worth a quiet look while moving between the museum quarter and the old botanical garden area.
