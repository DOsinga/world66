The State Collection of Antiquities on Königsplatz holds Greek, Etruscan, and Roman objects acquired primarily for King Ludwig I in the 1820s through 1840s. The collection is strongest in Attic pottery — black-figure and red-figure vases of exceptional quality — and Etruscan bronze work.

The museum faces the Glyptothek across the square and is often visited in combination with it. Free on Sundays; modest admission on other days. Allow two hours for both museums and the square between them. The new basement display presenting selected objects in Pompeian-red rooms with appropriate lighting is a marked improvement over the previous installation.
