Sugar Mountain turns a former concrete works in south-west Munich into a rough-edged culture and activity space. Depending on the day, you might find bouldering, skating, film screenings, concerts, theatre, exhibitions, or a warehouse party, which makes it useful if you want to see a less polished side of the city.
