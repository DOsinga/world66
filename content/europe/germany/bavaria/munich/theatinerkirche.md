Munich's finest Baroque church, on the Odeonsplatz, built from 1663 for Italian Theatine monks and modelled on Sant'Andrea della Valle in Rome. The exterior — ochre stucco, twin towers, a prominent dome — defines the visual character of the Altstadt. The interior is a dramatic white stucco space with a deep choir and elaborate side chapels.

The royal crypt beneath the church contains 35 members of the Wittelsbach family, including several electors and kings. Entry to the crypt requires a small fee; the church itself is free. The Odeonsplatz in front is one of the city's main public squares; the Hofgarten and Residenz are immediately adjacent. Free organ concerts on some evenings — check the church website.
