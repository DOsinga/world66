The large meadow southwest of the center is best known as the site of Oktoberfest — 14 to 16 days of beer tents, fairground rides, and traditional Bavarian dress, held annually in late September and early October. When the festival is not running, the Wiesn is an open public meadow used for other events, including a smaller spring festival (Frühlingsfest) in April and May.

At the western end of the meadow, the Bavaria statue and the Ruhmeshalle (Hall of Fame) commemorate Bavarian achievements in a pompous but historically interesting way. The statue is hollow and climbable — the head has windows offering views across the city. During Oktoberfest, reserve a table in the tents at least six months in advance; spontaneous visits mean standing at the edges or hoping for no-shows.
