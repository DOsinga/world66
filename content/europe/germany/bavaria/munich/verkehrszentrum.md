Verkehrszentrum is the transport branch of the Deutsches Museum, set in former Munich trade-fair halls. Its exhibits cover urban transport, travel, mobility, and transport technology across several large halls.

The museum is a strong choice for families and transit enthusiasts, and pairs easily with Theresienwiese nearby.
