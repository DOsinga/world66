Westpark was created for the 1983 International Garden Expo and still feels more varied than a routine city park. The western side has the rose garden, lakeside open-air stage and several surviving expo features, including Chinese, Japanese, Nepali and Thai garden structures.

The eastern half is quieter, with paths around Mollsee and a landscape designed to suggest the Alpine foothills south of Munich. It is a good choice when you want green space with more to look at than lawns.
