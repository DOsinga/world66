Deutsches Spielearchiv Nurnberg preserves the history of board games, with original games, reference material, and exhibits on the culture of play. Some games can be tried by visitors, which makes the archive more hands-on than its name suggests.

It is a specialist stop, but a good one for families, game fans, or anyone interested in Nuremberg's long connection with toys and games.
