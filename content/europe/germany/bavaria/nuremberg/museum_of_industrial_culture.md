The Museum of Industrial Culture is set in a former screw factory, the last remnant of the Tafel metalworks complex. Its exhibits trace Nuremberg's industrial life from the Industrial Revolution into the early 20th century.

The museum street shows how housing, work, social life, technology, and local trades changed as the city industrialized. It is a strong stop for visitors who want more than Nuremberg's medieval centre and Nazi-era history.
