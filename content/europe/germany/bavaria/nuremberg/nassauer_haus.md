Nassauer Haus is one of Nuremberg's oldest surviving houses, with Romanesque cellars and a late Gothic upper structure. Its small tower-like form stands out among the old-town buildings near St. Lorenz.

Most visitors see it from the street rather than as a long visit, but it is a worthwhile stop on a walk through Nuremberg's historic center.
