Neues Museum Nurnberg is the city's state museum for modern art and design. It sits inside the Old Town but feels deliberately contemporary, with a glassy modern building that contrasts with Nuremberg's medieval streets.

The collection focuses on art and design from 1945 onward. It is a strong stop if you want a break from imperial history, churches, and Second World War sites, and it pairs well with a walk between the Hauptbahnhof and the old city centre.
