Neunhof Manor is a small moated country estate on Nuremberg's northern edge, once used
by patrician families as a summer retreat outside the city walls. Its appeal is quieter
than the imperial castle: painted interiors, garden setting, and the glimpse it gives
into rural aristocratic life around a powerful trading city.

The manor works best as a secondary Nuremberg stop for visitors who have already seen
the old town and want a more local historic site. Check seasonal openings carefully, as
access is more limited than at the city's central museums.
