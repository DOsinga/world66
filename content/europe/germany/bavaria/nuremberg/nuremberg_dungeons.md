The Nuremberg Dungeons are part of the old tunnel system beneath the city, separate from the better-known art bunker tours nearby. The underground spaces were later adapted during World War II, when parts of the tunnel network were used to protect the city's art treasures.

Visit by guided tour to understand how the medieval underground passages, wartime shelters, and storage rooms fit into the history of the old city.
