St Sebald Church anchors the north side of Nuremberg's old town, with Romanesque beginnings, Gothic height, and later Baroque details layered into one of the city's defining churches. Inside, the carved shrine of Saint Sebald and the post-war reconstruction give the building real weight beyond its handsome exterior.
