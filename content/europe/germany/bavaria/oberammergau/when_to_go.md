---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

Oberammergau is a year-round Alpine village. The decisive question is whether or not you are visiting in a Passion Play year — they fall every decade ending in zero. In a play year the village sees roughly 450,000 visitors between May and October; rooms book out a year ahead and prices double. Outside those years the place is calm.

**Summer (June–September)** is the peak season for walking, cable cars, and beer gardens. Temperatures sit in the low 20s with cool nights — pleasant for Alpine hikes. **Winter (December–March)** brings reliable snow at the cable-car height and quiet skiing at Kolbensattel; the village is at its prettiest under a fresh fall. **Spring and autumn** are the off-season — lifts may be closed for maintenance and some restaurants take long holidays, but you have the painted streets largely to yourself and room rates fall sharply.
