Mariahilf Monastery stands on the hillside above Passau, a pilgrimage complex reached by a long covered stairway from the Inn side of the city. The climb is part of the visit, leading from the river up to a church long associated with devotion to the Virgin Mary.

The monastery rewards the effort with one of Passau's best perspectives, looking back over the rivers, old town, and cathedral skyline. It is a strong complement to the more obvious sights in the center because it turns the city's dramatic geography into a physical experience.
