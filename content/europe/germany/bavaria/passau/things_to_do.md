---
title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Passau's old town is compact and best explored on foot. The main sights cluster around the peninsula between the Danube and Inn, with the hilltop Veste Oberhaus above. Most of the old town can be covered comfortably in a day, though the [Glass Museum](/europe/germany/bavaria/passau/glasmuseum) and a long lunch could easily fill a second.
