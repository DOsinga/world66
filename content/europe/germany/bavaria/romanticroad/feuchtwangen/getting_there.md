---
title: "Getting There"
type: section
---

Feuchtwangen sits on the Romantic Road between Rothenburg ob der Tauber and Dinkelsbühl, about 50 km southwest of Nuremberg and 15 km north of Dinkelsbühl. The town is on the A7 autobahn, with its own exit, so it is easy to reach by car.

The nearest railway stations are at Dombühl (12 km) and Ansbach (30 km), with regional services from Nuremberg and Würzburg. Buses from Dombühl run into Feuchtwangen several times a day; outside school terms the schedule thins out, so check before relying on public transport.

The Europabus along the Romantic Road, a summer-only coach service, stops in Feuchtwangen — useful for travellers without a car.
