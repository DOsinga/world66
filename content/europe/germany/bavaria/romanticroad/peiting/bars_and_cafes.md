---
title: "Bars and Cafes"
type: section
---

Peiting's café and bar scene is modest — this is a working agricultural town rather than a tourist hub. The local Gasthöfe typically serve as both restaurant and local inn, with a Stammtisch corner where regulars gather over Maßkrüge in the evenings. For a livelier atmosphere, Schongau is close enough to reach in a few minutes by car or bike.

During the warmer months, a few places put tables outside; the café culture is unhurried and unpretentious, very much in keeping with the Voralpenland character of the area.
