---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

The best months for Peiting and the Lech Valley are May through September, when the cycling paths are clear, the riverside meadows are in flower, and the Alpine panoramas are at their most dramatic. June and July bring the longest days and the wildflowers for which the Voralpenland is known. August can be warm and occasionally wet — the area sits in the northern rain shadow of the Alps and gets a share of summer thunderstorms.

Spring (April–May) is lovely, with the Lech running high and fast with snowmelt and the deciduous forest along the banks in fresh leaf. Autumn brings mist mornings and good light for photography; the foliage turns in October. Winter is quiet and cold — most of the cycling infrastructure shuts down and the area offers little for visitors unless there is snow for the minor cross-country ski trails nearby.

The Wieskirche, the UNESCO pilgrimage church south of Peiting in Steingaden, attracts large numbers of visitors in high summer; going in the morning or off-peak months gives a much calmer experience.
