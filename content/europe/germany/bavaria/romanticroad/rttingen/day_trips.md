---
title: "Day Trips"
type: section
---

The Tauber valley has two unmissable neighbours within easy reach. [Weikersheim](/europe/germany/bavaria/romanticroad/weikersheim), 15 km north, has one of the finest Renaissance palaces in southern Germany with a baroque garden. [Bad Mergentheim](/europe/germany/bavaria/romanticroad/badmergentheim), a little further on, has the Teutonic Knights' castle and a famous spa park. Both make a half-day trip from Röttingen with a car.
