type: section
---

Röttingen is small enough that an hour's wander covers the highlights. The medieval walls, [Burg Brattenstein](/europe/germany/bavaria/romanticroad/rttingen/burg_brattenstein), the baroque [town hall](/europe/germany/bavaria/romanticroad/rttingen/rathaus), and the famous [sundial trail](/europe/germany/bavaria/romanticroad/rttingen/sundial_trail) are all within a couple of streets of each other. Outside town, the vineyards climb the slope above the Tauber and reward the short walk up with one of the best views of the Romantic Road's opening act.
