Steingaden is a small village at the point where the Romantic Road crosses the Deutsche Alpenstrasse. The village itself is quiet, but two churches make it an essential stop.

The [Wieskirche](/europe/germany/bavaria/romanticroad/steingaden/wieskirche), about 5 km southeast of town, is one of the finest rococo churches in the world and a UNESCO World Heritage Site. Built between 1746 and 1754 by Dominikus Zimmermann, its exterior is plain white — but the interior is an explosion of colour, gilding, and trompe-l'oeil that leaves most visitors genuinely speechless. In Steingaden itself, the 12th-century [Premonstratensian monastery church of St. John the Baptist](/europe/germany/bavaria/romanticroad/steingaden/welfenmunster) is worth a visit for its Romanesque cloister and baroque interior. Summer concerts in both churches draw appreciative audiences.
