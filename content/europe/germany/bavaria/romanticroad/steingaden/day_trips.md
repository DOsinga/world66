title: Day Trips
type: section
---

Steingaden sits at the junction of the Romantic Road and the Alpenstrasse, so a day in either direction opens up a great deal. South-west is [Füssen](/europe/germany/bavaria/romanticroad/fssen) and the Royal Castles of Neuschwanstein and Hohenschwangau, fifty minutes by car. North-east is the Pfaffenwinkel lake country and [Landsberg am Lech](/europe/germany/bavaria/romanticroad/landsbergamlech). For walkers, the Ammergauer Alpen begin immediately to the south, with Oberammergau and its passion-play theatre an hour's drive away.
