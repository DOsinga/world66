Laudenbach Mountain Church stands above the village of Laudenbach, a few kilometres from Weikersheim. Its origins go back to the early 15th century, and the church was consecrated in 1459.

The footpath from the village follows a Stations of the Cross route up to the church, making it as much a short historic walk as an architecture stop.
