---
title: Things to Do
type: section
---

Rosenheim is a pleasant market town rather than a headline destination, but it has a handsome old centre and serves well as a base for day trips into the Bavarian Alps and to the Chiemsee. The **Max-Josefs-Platz** and its surrounding arcaded streets are the heart of the old town. The **Inn-Museum** on the riverbank is the most distinctive local sight, covering the salt trade that made the town wealthy.
