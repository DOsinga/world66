  The site's aviation history predates the museum by decades: Oberschleißheim was the location of the Royal Bavarian Flying Corps base from 1912, making it one of the first military airfields in Germany. The Flugwerft Schleissheim is an aviation museum run as an annex of the Deutsches Museum, housed in a combination of the original 1912 Royal Bavarian Flying Corps hangar and a modern exhibition hall on the same historic airfield. The collection is one of the finest in Germany: more than 50 aircraft spanning a century of aviation, from an 1894 Otto Lilienthal glider replica through World War I fighters, World War II aircraft, Korean War jets, and Cold War-era military planes.

Highlights include a Soviet MiG-21, an American F-4E Phantom, a Messerschmitt 109, and several rare early German aircraft. Helicopters, gliders, and aero-engines fill the spaces between. The scale of the building gives the aircraft room to breathe, and the outdoor area holds larger machines including a Vietnam-era jet that would be too big for the hangar. A visit here makes an excellent pairing with the palaces — very different in character, but the contrast is part of the point.
