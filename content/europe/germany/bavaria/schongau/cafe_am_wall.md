A small café close to the town walls, popular with locals for morning coffee and afternoon cake. The kitchen turns out simple lunch dishes — soups, salads, sandwiches — as well as the usual range of Bavarian baked goods. A good stop before or after walking the wall circuit. Closes in the early evening.
