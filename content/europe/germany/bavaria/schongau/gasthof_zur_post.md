Gasthof zur Post sits on the Marktplatz and is the most central place to eat in Schongau. The menu is straightforwardly Bavarian — roast pork with potato dumplings, Schnitzel, sausage plates, and a soup or two. The dining room is panelled and unpretentious. Portions are generous and prices are reasonable by Bavarian standards. Lunch tends to be quieter than dinner. The attached inn occasionally has rooms available.
