Museum Altes Gymnasium is a local-history museum in a Renaissance former Latin school dating from 1582. The collection covers the city's past through everyday objects, documents, and displays tied to the old school building.

It is a small museum rather than a headline attraction, but the historic building and central location make it a reasonable stop for visitors already exploring Schweinfurt's old town.
