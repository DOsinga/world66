The Museum Georg Schäfer is Schweinfurt's cultural centrepiece and one of the best art museums in Franconia. It occupies a purpose-built contemporary building designed by Volker Staab (completed 2000) and holds a major collection of 19th-century German paintings — Romanticism, Biedermeier, and Realism. The works of Caspar David Friedrich, Carl Spitzweg, Moritz von Schwind, and Adolph Menzel feature prominently.

The building itself is notable — a clean, light-filled structure that makes intelligent use of natural light for the paintings. The permanent collection is complemented by strong temporary exhibitions. It's particularly appealing on a rainy afternoon; allow two hours for a proper visit. The museum café is decent.
