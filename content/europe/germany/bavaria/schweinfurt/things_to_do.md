---
title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Schweinfurt is a compact Franconian town on the Main River with a good art museum, a well-preserved market square, and a few hours of walkable old town. The Georg Schäfer Museum is the main cultural draw. For longer stays, Würzburg (30 km south) and Bamberg (40 km east) are both easy day trips and more substantial destinations, but Schweinfurt rewards a half-day of its own exploration.
