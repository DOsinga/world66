Erzgruben Erlebniswelt turns the Grunten from a scenic backdrop into a worked mountain. Guided visits lead into former ore tunnels and explain the small-scale mining that once shaped this corner of the Allgau.

It is not a huge industrial museum, which is part of the appeal. The visit is compact, physical, and tied directly to the hill above Sonthofen.
