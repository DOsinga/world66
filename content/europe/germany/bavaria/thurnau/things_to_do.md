---
title: Things to Do in Thurnau
type: section
---

Thurnau's two main draws are its castle complex and its pottery tradition. The castle is one of the largest in Franconia and now houses a branch of the University of Bayreuth; the pottery museum is the most complete record of a regional craft that has been continuous here for centuries. The old town centre is compact and pleasant to walk, with a quiet market square lined by half-timbered houses.
