---
title: "Getting There"
type: section
---

Traunstein is on the main railway line between Munich and Salzburg, with direct trains running regularly in both directions. The journey from Munich takes roughly 90 minutes; from Salzburg it's under an hour. The station is a short walk south of the old town. By car, the A8 motorway passes nearby, making it convenient for travellers driving between the two cities.
