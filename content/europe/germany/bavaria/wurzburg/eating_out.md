---
title: Eating Out
type: section
---

Würzburg's food culture is tied closely to its wine. The traditional *Weinstube* — a dark-panelled wine bar that also serves food — is the right place for a full meal: expect hearty Franconian dishes like *Sauerbraten* (marinated roast beef), *Schäufele* (roasted pork shoulder), and *Bratwurst* with sauerkraut. The local bread dumplings (*Klöße*) are excellent. The Juliusspital estate runs a good vinothek. The market square area has the highest concentration of restaurants and cafes.
