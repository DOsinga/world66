Aquarium Berlin is one of Germany's largest aquariums, with thousands of animals displayed across three floors in a historic building beside Berlin Zoo. It can be visited separately from the zoo, which makes it easy to fit into a shorter Tiergarten or City West itinerary.

The aquarium is especially useful on a rainy day or with children, with fish, reptiles, amphibians, and insects shown in a compact indoor setting.
