Art Center Berlin Friedrichstrasse is a small exhibition stop near Friedrichstrasse, with several floors of contemporary art shows. It is not one of Berlin's major museums, but it can be a worthwhile pause if you are already exploring Mitte and want a quieter gallery break.

Check current exhibitions before making a special trip, as the appeal depends heavily on what is being shown.
