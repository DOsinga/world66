Bayerischer Platz is the centre of Schoneberg's Bavarian Quarter, an area badly damaged in the Second World War. The square and surrounding streets are now best known for memorial signs that document Nazi persecution of Jewish residents and gay people.

Albert Einstein once lived in the wider neighbourhood, but the reason to stop is the quiet, street-level memorial work. It turns an ordinary Berlin square into a place for reading the city's history in fragments.
