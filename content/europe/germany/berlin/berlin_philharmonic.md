The Berliner Philharmoniker play in the Philharmonie, Hans Scharoun's 1963 building that looks like a tent thrown over the edge of the Tiergarten. The vineyard-style seating wraps around the stage — there is no bad seat. Standing-room tickets go on sale an hour before each concert for a fraction of the price. Worth it. The Digital Concert Hall streams performances online for those who can't get in.
