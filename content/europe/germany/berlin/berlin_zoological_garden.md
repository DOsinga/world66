Berlin Zoological Garden is Germany's oldest zoo and one of the world's major animal collections, with a central location beside Tiergarten and the Zoo station. Its historic Elephant Gate is one of the most recognizable entrances in Berlin.

The zoo is a strong family stop and combines easily with Aquarium Berlin, which sits next door and can be visited separately.
