The Berliner Ensemble is one of Berlin's best-known theatres, founded after the Second World War by Bertolt Brecht and Helene Weigel. Even if you are not seeing a performance, the theatre is a useful landmark for anyone tracing Berlin's 20th-century cultural history along the Spree.
