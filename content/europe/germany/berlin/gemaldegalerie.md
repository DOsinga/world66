The Gemäldegalerie is one of Berlin's major art museums, with a deep collection of European painting from the 13th to 18th centuries. Highlights include works by Rembrandt, Rubens, Botticelli, Raphael, Titian, Goya, Velázquez, and Watteau, making it a strong stop for visitors focused on old masters.
