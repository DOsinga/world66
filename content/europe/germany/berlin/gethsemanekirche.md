Gethsemanekirche is a Neogothic church in Prenzlauer Berg with a strong place in Berlin's late-20th-century history. It became one of the meeting points for opposition to the East German regime before the fall of the Berlin Wall.

The church is worth a short stop for its architecture and for the way it links a quiet neighborhood setting with the city's political memory.
