Hanfmuseum traces the history, culture, and uses of hemp, with exhibits that range from traditional fibre and food uses to modern debates around cannabis law. The small museum includes live hemp plants grown under permit, making it an unusual stop for visitors interested in social history, agriculture, or drug policy.
