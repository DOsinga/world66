Haus des Rundfunks is a landmark of Berlin broadcasting history, designed by Hans Poelzig and opened in 1931. Its brick expressionist exterior and purpose-built radio studios make it more interesting than a standard office block, though most visitors will see it from outside unless a tour or event is available.
