Jagdschloss Grunewald is Berlin's oldest surviving palace, a 16th-century hunting lodge set beside the Grunewaldsee. The estate combines historic architecture, museum rooms, and easy woodland walks, making it a good destination when you want a quieter counterpoint to central Berlin.
