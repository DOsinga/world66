Kastanienallee is one of Prenzlauer Berg's best streets for a slow wander. Cafes, fashion shops, small theatres, alternative cinemas, and beer gardens sit in and behind the old buildings, giving the street a mix of daily life and low-key nightlife.

It works best as part of a walk through Prenzlauer Berg rather than as a single monument. Come for the street scene, then duck into one of the courtyards or side streets when something catches your eye.
