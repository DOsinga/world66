The Kathe Kollwitz Museum focuses on the drawings, prints, posters, woodcuts, and sculptures of one of Germany's most powerful modern artists. Kollwitz returned again and again to themes of war, poverty, grief, and social injustice, shaped by personal losses in both world wars.

The museum is a serious, compact art stop near the western city centre. It pairs well with a Charlottenburg day that also includes galleries, theatres, or the Kurfurstendamm area.
