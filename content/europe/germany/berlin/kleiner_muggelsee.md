Kleiner Muggelsee is the smaller lake southeast of Berlin's larger Muggelsee.

Its sloping sandy beach, Strandbad Kleiner Muggelsee, is one of the better-known bathing spots in this part of the city and is a good warm-weather escape from central Berlin.
