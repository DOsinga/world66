The Liebermann-Villa am Wannsee is the lakeside summer house of Max Liebermann, one of Germany's leading Impressionist painters. The house museum displays a focused collection of his work, with paintings, prints, and material on his life in Berlin.

The villa's garden and Wannsee setting are part of the appeal. It works best as a calm art stop in southwest Berlin rather than as a quick central museum visit.
