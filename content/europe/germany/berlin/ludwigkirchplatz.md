A leafy square in the well-heeled Fasanenkiez, built around the twin-towered neo-baroque Ludwigkirche and ringed by café and restaurant terraces. It is the social centre of this corner of Wilmersdorf — the kind of place where locals linger over breakfast, walk their dogs, and let children loose on the small playground while the chestnut trees throw shade across the cobbles.

There is nothing to tick off here, just a pleasant, distinctly west-Berlin way to spend an hour. Take a table on the square, order a coffee or a glass of wine, and watch a quieter, more bourgeois side of the city go about its day.
