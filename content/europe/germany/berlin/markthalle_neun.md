A Victorian market hall in Kreuzberg, built in 1891 and saved from demolition by a community campaign. The weekly Street Food Thursday (every Thursday evening) draws a long queue and an excellent crowd — stalls sell everything from Vietnamese bánh mì to Georgian khinkali to wood-fired pizza. Saturday morning is the regular farmers' market, calmer and just as good. Eisenbahnstrasse 42-43.
