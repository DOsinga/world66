Meistersaal is a historic Berlin music hall from the early 1900s, later used as part of the recording studios near the Berlin Wall. David Bowie, Iggy Pop, U2, and other artists recorded in the complex, giving the building a place in the city's modern music history.

It is not a drop-in museum, but occasional guided tours make it a worthwhile stop for visitors interested in Berlin's recording history and the cultural life around Potsdamer Platz.
