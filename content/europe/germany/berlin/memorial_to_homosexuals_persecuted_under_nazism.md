This concrete memorial in the Tiergarten remembers homosexual people persecuted under Nazism. A small window in the block shows a short film, turning a plain exterior into a direct and intimate act of remembrance.

It sits close to the larger Holocaust Memorial and the Brandenburg Gate, so it is easy to visit as part of a walk through Berlin's central memorial landscape.
