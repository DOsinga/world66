Moabit Prison Historical Park preserves the memory of a former prison built around 1850 and later used by the Nazis to imprison, torture, and murder political opponents. The prison itself is gone, but the grounds now function as a public memorial park.

It is a sober, short stop rather than a major museum, worthwhile if you are already exploring the government quarter or Moabit's wartime history.
