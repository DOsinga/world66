Müggelturm is an observation tower in the forested southeast of Berlin, above the Müggelsee area. Climb the stairs for wide views over lakes, woods, and the outer districts of the city, a very different Berlin from the dense centre.

There is no elevator, so the tower is best for visitors comfortable with stairs. A cafe by the tower makes it easy to fold into a longer walk in the surrounding hills and forest.
