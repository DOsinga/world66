The Museum für Kommunikation occupies the former Imperial General Post Office, a grand building close to Leipziger Strasse. Its exhibitions cover postal history, telecommunications, media, and the ways people have sent messages over time, with enough historic objects and hands-on displays to make it more than a specialist stop.
