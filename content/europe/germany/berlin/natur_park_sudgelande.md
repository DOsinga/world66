Natur-Park Sudgelande turns an abandoned rail yard into one of Berlin's more unusual green spaces. Trees and grasses have grown through old tracks, and paths now thread past railway fragments, industrial structures, and public art.

The preserved steam locomotive, turntable, and rail infrastructure make the park more than a simple walk in the woods. It is best for travellers interested in Berlin's habit of reusing old infrastructure rather than hiding it.
