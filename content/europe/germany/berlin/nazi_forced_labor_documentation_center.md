The Nazi Forced Labor Documentation Center preserves part of the Schöneweide forced labour camp, an unusually intact wartime barracks complex in Berlin. Eleven of the original thirteen barracks survive, and the exhibitions explain a part of Nazi history that is easier to miss than the city's more central memorials.
