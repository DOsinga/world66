The centrepiece of Museum Island, housing the Egyptian Museum and Papyrus Collection. The star attraction is the bust of Nefertiti — 3,300 years old, painted limestone, and astonishingly well-preserved — but the building itself is worth the visit. David Chipperfield's restoration after wartime destruction is a masterclass in how to handle a wounded building: the scars are retained and the new work is honest. Book online, especially in summer.
