Neuvenedig is a small waterside area in southeast Berlin, where narrow canals, garden plots, and leafy banks make the city feel unexpectedly loose and quiet. It is not a headline sight, but it is a pleasant detour if you are already out toward Kopenick and want a slower walk by the water.
