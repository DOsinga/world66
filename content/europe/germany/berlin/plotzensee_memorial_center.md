Plotzensee Memorial Center is one of Berlin's starkest Nazi-era memorials. It stands at the site of a former execution chamber where nearly 2,900 people were killed between 1933 and 1945, including many members of the German resistance.

The memorial is quiet and severe rather than monumental. It is a deliberate stop for visitors interested in Berlin's darker 20th-century history.
