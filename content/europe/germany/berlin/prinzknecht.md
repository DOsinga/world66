A large, easygoing gay bar built around a long central counter, Prinzknecht is one of the more relaxed places in the Nollendorfplatz cluster — roomy enough to find a corner, busy enough to feel alive. The crowd is broad in age and type, and the atmosphere is more after-work-drinks than late-night cruise.

Tables spill onto Fuggerstraße in warm weather, and themed evenings and the regular happy hour keep it filling steadily through the week. A solid, unintimidating starting point for a night in the quarter.
