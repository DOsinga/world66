The Ramones Museum is a small Berlin tribute to the New York punk band, with hundreds of original posters, photos, records, shirts, and other memorabilia. It is part museum and part fan clubhouse, with a cafe inside, and works best for visitors already interested in punk history or the band's Berlin connections.
