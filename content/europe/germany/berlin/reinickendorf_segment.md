The Reinickendorf Segment is a small surviving piece of the early Berlin Wall near Schönholz station. It is easy to miss because it resembles an ordinary garden wall, a reminder that the first barriers were often improvised before the familiar concrete panels appeared.

It is a modest stop, but worthwhile for visitors tracing less obvious Berlin Wall remains beyond the best-known memorial sites.
