A sleek, all-white dining room that has been Friedrichshain's grown-up restaurant of choice since long before the district had many of them. The kitchen cooks "Alpine" food — an Austrian-leaning menu of Wiener schnitzel, Kaiserschmarrn and seasonal game alongside lighter modern plates — and takes its cocktails and wine list seriously.

It is dressier and quieter than the brewpubs and burger joints around it, which is exactly the point: a place for a proper dinner in a neighbourhood better known for its nightlife. Tucked away on Simplonstraße near the Boxhagener Kiez; book at weekends.
