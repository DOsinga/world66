Staatsoper Unter den Linden is one of Berlin's major opera houses and a landmark on the historic boulevard through Mitte. The building's royal history and restored interior make it worth noticing even if you are not attending a performance, while opera and orchestral programs give it a clear reason for a planned evening visit.
