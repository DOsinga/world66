Berlin's most improbable park: the runways and aprons of the old Tempelhof Airport, left exactly as they were when the last plane took off in 2008. The result is an enormous, flat, wind-swept commons where Berliners cycle, barbecue, fly kites, and play music on the disused tarmac. There are community gardens in the old terminal outbuildings, and on summer evenings half the city seems to be here. Entry is free. Bring a bike if you can — the runways are long.
