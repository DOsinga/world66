Built on the foundations of the former SS and Gestapo headquarters, this documentation centre lays out the machinery of Nazi terror with unflinching clarity. The outdoor exhibition along Niederkirchnerstrasse, running beside a section of the Berlin Wall, is free and open year-round. Inside, the permanent exhibition on the Nazi rise to power and the mechanisms of occupation across Europe is among the most serious history museums in Germany. No entry charge.
