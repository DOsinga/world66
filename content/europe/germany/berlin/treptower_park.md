Treptower Park is a broad riverside park along the Spree, good for a long walk away from Berlin's tighter central streets. Boats, lawns, and the Insel der Jugend give it an easy weekend feel, especially in warm weather.

The park's most important sight is the Soviet War Memorial, one of Berlin's most imposing memorial landscapes. Its scale and symbolism make Treptower Park more than just a neighbourhood green space.
