Victoriastadt is a compact 19th-century residential quarter in eastern Berlin. It is one of the better-preserved pockets of its period in the city, with rows of brick and stucco buildings that survived with much of the old street pattern intact.

The area is best treated as a short walk, especially if you are already exploring Friedrichshain or Lichtenberg.
