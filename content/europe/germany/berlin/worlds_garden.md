World's Garden is a large landscaped garden park in eastern Berlin, with themed areas including Chinese, Korean, Balinese, Oriental, and Japanese gardens. The different garden styles make it more than a simple city park, especially in spring and summer.

It takes some time to reach from central Berlin, but it rewards visitors who want a slower, greener day away from the main museum circuit.
