title: Things to Do
type: section
---

Bernau's medieval town walls and gates are the main draw — the [Steintor](steintor), [Werdertor](werdertor), and [Mühlentor](muehlentor) survive in unusually good condition and are best seen on a short walking circuit of the old town. The [Heimatmuseum](heimatmuseum) inside the Steintor covers 500 years of local history and is worth an hour.

For architecture enthusiasts, the real prize is the [ADGB Trade Union School](adgb_trade_union_school) about 4km north: a 1928 Bauhaus masterpiece by Hannes Meyer, now a UNESCO World Heritage Site. Combine it with a walk into [Barnim Nature Park](barnim_nature_park) for a full day out of Berlin. The [Pulverturm](pulverturm) — a converted medieval gunpowder tower — hosts the Wolf Kahlen Intermedia Arts Museum, an eccentric and worthwhile stop for anyone interested in media art.
