---
title: When to Go
type: section
---

Bernau is a year-round destination for a day trip from Berlin, though summer (May to September) is the most rewarding time. The Heimatmuseum opens seasonally from May to October, and the Barnim Nature Park and surrounding forests are best enjoyed when the weather allows walking and cycling. The Liepnitzsee is swimmable from June to early September.

Autumn brings the best light for photography of the medieval gates and the birch forests to the north. Winter is quiet — the town is perfectly walkable in cold weather and the brick facades of the Steintor look dramatic against a grey sky, but some of the smaller attractions may be closed.
