Klinger Lake is part of the Lusatian landscape being remade after open-cast lignite mining. What was once industrial extraction is gradually becoming water, shoreline, paths, and habitat, a transformation that is central to understanding the region around Cottbus.

It is not yet a classic resort lake, and that in-between quality is the point. Visit if you are interested in post-industrial landscapes, cycling routes, or the way eastern Germany is turning mined ground into a new lake district.
