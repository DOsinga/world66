Raumflugplanetarium Cottbus is a compact planetarium with astronomy and spaceflight programming close to the city centre. It adds a science-focused stop to a city better known for Branitz Park, Wendish culture, and brick landmarks.

The value is in the show schedule, so check times before making the trip. For families or travellers who enjoy planetariums, it is a useful indoor option in bad weather.
