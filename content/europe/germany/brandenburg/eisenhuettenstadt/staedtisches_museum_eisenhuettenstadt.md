Städtisches Museum Eisenhüttenstadt is the city's municipal museum, covering the area's history from medieval Fürstenberg on the Oder through Eisenhüttenstadt's planned socialist-era development. The collection also includes a gallery of art from the GDR period.

It pairs well with Museum Utopie und Alltag and a walk through the planned city centre. Together they give a clearer picture of why Eisenhüttenstadt is one of Germany's most interesting places for 20th-century urban history.
