---
title: Things to Do
type: section
---

Frankfurt an der Oder is compact enough to explore on foot. Begin around the market square and [Marienkirche](marienkirche), then continue to the [Kleist Museum](kleist_museum) for the city's strongest cultural stop.

The river is the point of the city. Walk the [Oder promenade and Słubice bridge](oder_promenade_and_slubice_bridge) to feel how close Poland is, then add [Museum Viadrina](museum_viadrina) if you want more local history. The city is not pretty in a simple way, but the mix of medieval fragments, postwar rebuilding, university life, and border geography makes it rewarding for a spare day.
