Oranienburg's main draw is its memorial sites, which together form one of the most significant concentrations of 20th-century history anywhere in Germany. The [Sachsenhausen concentration camp](/europe/germany/brandenburg/oranienburg/sachsenhausen_concentration_camp) and the [Museum Sowjetisches Speziallager](/europe/germany/brandenburg/oranienburg/museum_sowjetisches_speziallager) document both the Nazi and Soviet periods of the same grounds. Away from the memorial, [Oranienburg Palace](/europe/germany/brandenburg/oranienburg/oranienburg_palace) and the [Schlosspark](/europe/germany/brandenburg/oranienburg/schlosspark) along the Havel offer a contrast — Baroque architecture and riverside greenery that predate the 20th century by nearly three hundred years.
