The Waage is a well-regarded wine bar in the Dutch Quarter, occupying one of the low-ceilinged 18th-century houses in the neighbourhood's historic core. The wine list covers Germany and Austria with depth, and the staff know their way around it. Small plates — charcuterie, cheese, house-made tarts — accompany the wine. It is the kind of place to end an evening in Potsdam properly, rather than rushing back to Berlin on the last S-Bahn. It fills quickly on weekends; arrive early or accept a wait.
