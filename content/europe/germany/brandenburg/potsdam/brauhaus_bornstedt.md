The Brauhaus at the Bornstedter Gut estate is a brewpub set in the historic buildings of a 19th-century royal farmstead immediately north of Sanssouci Park. It brews its own beers on the premises — a pilsner, a dark lager, and seasonal specialities — and serves them in a large, airy hall with vaulted brick ceilings. The food is hearty central European cooking: pork, game, dumplings, and the kind of generous portions that make sense after a day walking the park.

The location is ideal: the estate is a five-minute walk from the northern entrance to Sanssouci Park, making it a natural endpoint for an afternoon in the park. The large beer garden is popular in summer. The brewpub is a family-friendly establishment and generally less expensive than the restaurant options in the old town proper.
