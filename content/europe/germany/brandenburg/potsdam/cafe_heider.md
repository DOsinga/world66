Café Heider is a Potsdam institution — a grand coffee house in the old town that has been a social fixture for generations of Potsdamers. It serves proper coffee, cakes, and light meals in a room that manages to be both historic and comfortable. It is the kind of place where retired professors read newspapers in the afternoon and where a post-museum coffee feels exactly right. The pastries are made on the premises; the Baumkuchen (tree cake) is particularly good.
