Filmpark Babelsberg turns Potsdam's long film-making history into a visitor attraction, with sets, stunt shows, props, and studio-themed exhibits. Its strongest draw is the connection to Babelsberg, one of Europe's most important film production districts.

This is a family-oriented attraction rather than a conventional museum, and it works best when shows are running. For travelers interested in cinema, it complements the Filmmuseum in central Potsdam by moving from history into the working studio landscape.
