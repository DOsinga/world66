Nikolskoe is a wooded historic spot north of Potsdam, overlooking the Havel. Its Russian-style church and lodge were built in the 19th century and make a quiet detour near the route toward Peacock Island.

The appeal is the combination of forest, river views, and unusual architecture rather than a long list of exhibits. It works best as a short stop while exploring the lakes and palaces around Potsdam.
