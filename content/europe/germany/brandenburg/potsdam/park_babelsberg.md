Park Babelsberg is one of Potsdam's great landscape parks, spreading along the Havel with winding paths, water views, and the Gothic Revival Babelsberg Palace at its centre. It forms part of the UNESCO-listed Potsdam palace landscape and makes an easy walk from Glienicke Bridge, especially if you want a quieter alternative to Sanssouci.
