Restaurant Juliette is a French restaurant in the heart of the Dutch Quarter, occupying a low-ceilinged 18th-century house. The menu is classic French — steak frites, duck confit, bouillabaisse — with good wines and attentive service. It has been one of Potsdam's best restaurants for many years and remains a reliable choice for a proper dinner. Book in advance; the small rooms fill quickly, especially on weekends.
