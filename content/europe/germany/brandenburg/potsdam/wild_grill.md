A long-standing Potsdam favourite near the Dutch Quarter, serving traditional Brandenburg cuisine with a particular emphasis on seasonal game and pork dishes. The interior is homely and the service no-nonsense. It is the kind of unpretentious local restaurant that has largely disappeared from German city centres, offering plates of Eisbein (pork knuckle), Rouladen (beef rolls), and seasonal game stews at prices that feel reasonable compared to the tourist-facing restaurants nearby. Favoured by locals who want a proper German dinner rather than an international menu.
