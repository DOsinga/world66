---
title: "Getting There"
type: section
---

Rheinsberg is about 90 km north of Berlin. By train, take the RE6 from Berlin Hauptbahnhof to Löwenberg, then change to the RB55 to Rheinsberg — the total journey is around 90 minutes. By car, follow the B96 and B122 north from Berlin; the drive takes about an hour in light traffic. There is no direct bus from Berlin.
