The Spreewald is about being on or near water. [Punt boat tours](/europe/germany/brandenburg/spreewald/canal_punt_tours) and [kayaking](/europe/germany/brandenburg/spreewald/kayaking) wind through the same network of 300 channels; the choice is whether you want someone else to do the work or not. The 13 km [walk from Lübben to Lübbenau](/europe/germany/brandenburg/spreewald/lubben_to_lubbenau_walk) along the Hauptspree is the best introduction on foot.

Beyond the water, the [Lehde Open-Air Museum](/europe/germany/brandenburg/spreewald/lehde_open_air_museum) preserves boat-access-only Sorbian farmsteads, and [Sorbian culture](/europe/germany/brandenburg/spreewald/sorbian_culture) — with its distinctive Easter egg tradition — is visible throughout the villages. The [Gurkenmuseum](/europe/germany/brandenburg/spreewald/gurkenmuseum) in Lübbenau makes a convincing case for the pickle as a regional art form.
