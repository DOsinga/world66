Hafenmuseum Speicher XI occupies a former warehouse in Bremen's Überseestadt, the old docklands area that has been steadily remade into a mixed district. The museum focuses on port labour, shipping, cargo and the practical systems that connected Bremen to the wider world.

It is a strong stop if you want to understand Bremen beyond the postcard old town. The surrounding district matters too: cranes, brick warehouses, new apartments and waterside paths show how the port landscape has changed without disappearing completely.
