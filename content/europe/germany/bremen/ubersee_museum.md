The Übersee-Museum stands beside Bremen's main station and reflects the city's old outward-looking merchant life. Its collections combine ethnography, natural history, and trade history, moving between Asia, Oceania, Africa, the Americas, and Bremen's own role in global exchange.

It is a good museum for a rainy day or for anyone trying to understand Bremen beyond the market square and riverfront. The approach is broad, sometimes old-school, but the best galleries make clear how a North German port city connected itself to the wider world.
