Weserburg is Bremen's contemporary art museum, set in former warehouse buildings on a small island in the Weser. The industrial shell and riverside position suit a museum that focuses on postwar and contemporary collections rather than old-master grandeur.

It is a good counterweight to Bremen's medieval square and Hanseatic nostalgia. Even if the current exhibitions vary, the building, river setting, and scale make it one of the city's stronger cultural stops.
