Wilhelm-Wagenfeld-Haus is Bremens design-minded museum, named for the Bauhaus-trained designer best known for clean, functional modern objects. Exhibitions focus on product design, applied arts, and the relationship between useful things and good form.

It is a sharp contrast to the medieval lanes of the Schnoor and the ceremonial market square. Go if you like design, craft, or museums that make ordinary objects worth looking at twice.
