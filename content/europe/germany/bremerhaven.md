Bremerhaven is Bremen's port on the Weser estuary, built for ships too large or too ocean-bound for the old inland city. It is a working harbour first and a pretty town second, which is exactly why it is worth adding to a northern Germany trip.

The best sights sit close together around the Havenwelten waterfront. The [German Emigration Center](/europe/germany/bremerhaven/german_emigration_center) tells the human story of millions leaving Europe by ship. The [German Maritime Museum](/europe/germany/bremerhaven/german_maritime_museum) and the preserved submarine [Wilhelm Bauer](/europe/germany/bremerhaven/wilhelm_bauer) cover the hardware of seafaring, while [Klimahaus Bremerhaven](/europe/germany/bremerhaven/klimahaus_bremerhaven) turns climate science into a full-scale visitor experience.

Bremerhaven is not as handsome as Bremen or Hamburg, and it does not pretend otherwise. Come for migration, ships, docks, polar bears at [Zoo am Meer](/europe/germany/bremerhaven/zoo_am_meer), fish, wind, and the blunt mood of a North Sea port.
