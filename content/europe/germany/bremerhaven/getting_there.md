---
title: "Getting There"
type: section
---

Trains reach Bremerhaven Hauptbahnhof from Bremen, with onward connections across northern Germany. From the station, buses and taxis run to the Havenwelten waterfront and port districts.

By car, Bremerhaven is reached from Bremen via the A27. Hamburg is possible as a longer day trip, but Bremen is the more natural pairing.
