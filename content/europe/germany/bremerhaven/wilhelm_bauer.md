Wilhelm Bauer is a preserved Type XXI submarine, the late-war German design that pointed toward the modern submarine age. Visiting it is cramped, technical, and sobering, especially because the vessel sits in a public harbour rather than behind museum glass.

It pairs well with the German Maritime Museum nearby. Even if you are not especially interested in military history, walking through the narrow interior gives a clear physical sense of what submarine service demanded.
