Zoo am Meer is one of Germany's smallest public zoos, squeezed into the harbourfront near the Weser. Its focus is coastal and polar wildlife: seals, penguins, polar bears, seabirds, and aquarium life connected to northern waters.

The scale makes it easy to visit without losing half a day. It is especially good with children, but adults will still appreciate how tightly it fits Bremerhaven's maritime and North Sea identity.
