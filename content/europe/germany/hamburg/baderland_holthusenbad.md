Bäderland Holthusenbad is a historic public bath in Eppendorf, with swimming pools and warm and cold thermal-style pools included in the basic entry. The sauna area costs extra, but the main reason to come is the chance to use one of Hamburg's more characterful old bathing complexes rather than a plain modern pool.
