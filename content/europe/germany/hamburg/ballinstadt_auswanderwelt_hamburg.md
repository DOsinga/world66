BallinStadt tells the story of the millions of emigrants who passed through Hamburg on their way to new lives overseas. The museum occupies part of the former HAPAG emigration complex built under Albert Ballin, with family-history terminals and displays on the long, uncertain journey from Europe to the Americas.
