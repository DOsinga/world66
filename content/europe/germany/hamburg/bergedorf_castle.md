Bergedorf Castle is the only preserved castle within Hamburg's city limits. It has been mentioned in records since the 14th century and now houses a museum about Bergedorf and the Vierlande region.

The castle is set in a protected park, making it a good eastern Hamburg stop for visitors interested in local history, small museums, and quieter green spaces beyond the central harbour districts.
