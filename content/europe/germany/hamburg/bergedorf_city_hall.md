Bergedorf City Hall is a castle-like villa from 1898-99 that has housed the local city government since 1927. Visitors can also see the 35 m city hall tower.

The building is far from central Hamburg, so it makes sense only if you are already exploring Bergedorf. The small park behind it adds a pleasant short walk.
