Chocoversum is an interactive chocolate museum that follows cocoa from plantation to finished bar. The focus is more hands-on than archival, with tastings and demonstrations built into the visit.

It works well for families or anyone looking for a lighter museum stop near Hamburg's Kontorhausviertel and Speicherstadt.
