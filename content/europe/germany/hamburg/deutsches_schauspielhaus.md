The Deutsches Schauspielhaus is one of Germany's great spoken-theatre stages, close to Hamburg Hauptbahnhof and St. Georg. Its reputation rests on major directors and productions rather than tourist spectacle, but theatre-goers should check the programme if they can follow German or just want to see the grand house in use.
