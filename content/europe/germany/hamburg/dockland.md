Dockland is a glass-and-steel office building on the Elbe in Altona, shaped like the bow of a ship. Its public outdoor stairway climbs 136 steps to the roof, where the reward is a wide view over the harbour and river.

It is a quick architectural stop rather than a museum or tour. Come for the view, especially if you are already walking the waterfront west of the Fischmarkt.
