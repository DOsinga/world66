Hans-Albers-Platz is a small St Pauli square between the Reeperbahn and Herbertstrasse, named for the actor who helped turn the Kiez into a screen legend. It is more atmosphere than monument, best treated as part of a Reeperbahn wander rather than a destination on its own.
