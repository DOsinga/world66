Imam Ali Mosque stands beside the Outer Alster and is one of Hamburg's most important Islamic centres. Built for the city's Iranian community, it is known for its blue-tiled dome and minarets, which make a striking contrast with the lakeside villas around Uhlenhorst. Visit respectfully and check current access before going inside.
