Jenisch Park is Hamburg's oldest landscaped park, spreading across the Elbe slope west of Altona. It is a good place for a quiet walk, with old trees, the Flottbek stream, views toward the river, and two museums on the grounds: Jenisch House and the Ernst Barlach House.
