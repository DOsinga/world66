Mehr!-Theater am Grossmarkt is Hamburg's large musical and events theatre, built into the city's wholesale market complex. Its size makes it a major stop for touring productions, concerts, and one-off stage shows rather than a theatre tied to a single resident musical.

The programme changes often, so it is worth checking only if you are looking for an evening performance while in Hamburg.
