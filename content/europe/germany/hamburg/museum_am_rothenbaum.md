Museum am Rothenbaum, formerly Hamburg's Museum of Ethnology, is one of the city's major museums for world cultures. Its collections range across Africa, Asia, Oceania, and the Americas, with exhibitions that put everyday objects, ritual pieces, textiles, and colonial history into context. It is a good stop if you want a museum beyond Hamburg's maritime and art circuit.
