Riepenburger Muhle is a historic windmill in Hamburg's eastern outskirts. The present mill dates from 1828, though windmills are recorded on the site from the 14th century.

It is a small but worthwhile stop for visitors already exploring the area, with a shop and cafe and local art in a nearby former warehouse.
