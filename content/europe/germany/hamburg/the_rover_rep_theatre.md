The Rover Rep Theatre is an English-language pub theatre below the Irish Rover on Grossneumarkt. It stages professional productions in an intimate basement setting, which makes it a useful address for English-speaking visitors looking for a night out beyond Hamburg's bigger theatres.

Check the programme before going, as the appeal depends entirely on what is playing while you are in town.
