Theater im Hafen Hamburg is a large musical theatre on the south bank of the Elbe, best known for long-running productions such as *The Lion King*. The theatre is part of the harbour experience as much as the show itself: visitors arrive across the river, with views back toward the Landungsbrücken, the Elbphilharmonie, and the old port.
