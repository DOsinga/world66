Tierpark Hagenbeck is Hamburg's long-running zoo, founded from the animal collection of the Hagenbeck family. It is known for early open-enclosure designs and remains one of the city's main family attractions.

Allow enough time for both the animal areas and the aquarium if you are making the trip out from the centre.
