Zeise Hallen is a group of 19th-century industrial halls in Altona, originally built for a company that made ship parts. The brick factory buildings have been reused as a cultural complex, with a cinema, restaurants, and event spaces.

It is not a museum, but the conversion makes a good short stop while exploring Ottensen and Altona. The old industrial shell is the main reason to look in.
