title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Weilburg is a small town that manages to pack in an unusual density of things worth seeing. The Schloss dominates the hilltop above the Lahn loop, the ship tunnel beneath the old town is a genuine curiosity, and the Lahn itself offers one of the best canoe runs in central Germany. It is the kind of place where you expect to spend a couple of hours and end up staying half a day.
