---
title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

The [Johannisburg Palace](/europe/germany/hessen/aschaffenburg/johannisburg_palace) dominates the town with its massive red sandstone bulk above the River Main. Inside, beyond the Dutch and Flemish paintings, is the extraordinary [Cork Model Collection](/europe/germany/hessen/aschaffenburg/cork_model_collection) — miniature reconstructions of ancient Roman monuments made entirely from cork. Across the river, the [Schönbusch Park](/europe/germany/hessen/aschaffenburg/schoenbusch_park) is one of the finest English-style landscape gardens in Bavaria. The [Stiftsbasilika St. Peter und Alexander](/europe/germany/hessen/aschaffenburg/stiftsbasilika) in the old town dates to the 10th century and contains works from Romanesque to Baroque.
