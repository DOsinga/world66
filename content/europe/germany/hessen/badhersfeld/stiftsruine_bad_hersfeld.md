The Stiftsruine is the reason to come to Bad Hersfeld. The former abbey church belonged to the powerful Benedictine Imperial Abbey of Hersfeld, and its roofless walls still show the scale of a Romanesque basilica that once mattered far beyond this small town.

In summer, the Bad Hersfelder Festspiele turns the ruin into an open-air stage for theatre and musicals. Outside festival season it is quieter, and maybe better for first impressions: pale stone, tall arches, grass underfoot, and the strange feeling of standing inside a church that has become both monument and public square.
