Eisenbahnmuseum Darmstadt-Kranichstein is a volunteer-run railway museum on Darmstadt's northern edge. Its collection and special events focus on historic trains, railway technology, and working heritage equipment.

Check the event calendar before going. On special days, historic train rides may link the museum with Darmstadt's central station, which makes the visit much more memorable.
