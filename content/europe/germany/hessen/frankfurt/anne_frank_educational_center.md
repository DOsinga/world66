Anne Frank was born in Frankfurt, and this educational centre uses her biography to talk about Nazism, discrimination, and the responsibilities of memory. It is not a conventional house museum; the focus is on interactive learning and contemporary tolerance work.

The centre makes most sense for visitors already interested in Jewish Frankfurt or 20th-century history. Anne Frank's early family homes were nearby before the family moved to Amsterdam.
