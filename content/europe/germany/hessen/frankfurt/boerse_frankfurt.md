Borse Frankfurt is the Frankfurt Stock Exchange building, one of the city's best-known finance landmarks. The bull and bear statues outside are the main draw for casual visitors and a quick photo stop near the city center.

The trading floor is not normally open to walk-in visitors, so plan on seeing the exterior unless you have arranged a guided visit in advance.
