Frankfurt's Hauptfriedhof is the city's main cemetery, a large and leafy place of old tombstones, mausoleums, and quiet paths. Visitors with an interest in German intellectual history come for the graves of Arthur Schopenhauer and Theodor W. Adorno, but it also works as a calm walk away from the banking towers.
