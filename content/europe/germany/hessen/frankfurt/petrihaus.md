Petrihaus is a small Swiss-style pavilion in a park on Frankfurt's west side. Goethe and Clemens Brentano are among the literary names linked with the house, which gives it more weight than its modest size suggests.

The house is usually seen on a guided tour. Leave time for the surrounding park, with lawns, old trees, and a faded rose garden.
