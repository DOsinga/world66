Plank is a tucked-away cocktail bar in the Bahnhofsviertel, a long narrow room behind an unmarked door on Elbestrasse — a perfect example of the high-end bar culture that has taken over what used to be Frankfurt's red-light district. The bartenders are serious about the craft, the cocktail list rotates seasonally, and the back room serves small plates from one of the best chefs in the city.

The crowd is mid-to-late thirties professionals and Bahnhofsviertel regulars; the music is jazz and downtempo, the lighting low, the prices substantial. Better on weeknights than weekends. Elbestrasse 15.
