Verkehrsmuseum Frankfurt am Main is a small transport museum focused on the city's public transport history. The collection includes old trams, buses, and a steam locomotive, with exhibits that work best for visitors interested in urban rail and local engineering history.

The museum sits beside a former station building from the Frankfurter Waldbahn, the steam-powered tram line that once served this edge of the city.
