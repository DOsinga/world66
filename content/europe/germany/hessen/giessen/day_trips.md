Giessen is well-placed for day trips into central Hesse. Both Marburg and Wetzlar are within 30 minutes by train and offer strong contrasts to Giessen's largely postwar streetscape.

[Marburg](/europe/germany/hessen/marburg) to the north is the most rewarding day trip from Giessen. The old town survived the war intact and climbs a steep hill above the Lahn, crowned by a large Landgrave castle. It is one of the best-preserved medieval cities in Hesse and easy to reach by regional train.

[Wetzlar](/europe/germany/hessen/wetzlar) to the west is another historic town that avoided wartime destruction. It is closely associated with Goethe, who worked there as a young lawyer and drew on his experiences to write The Sorrows of Young Werther. The Dom — an unfinished Gothic cathedral that never received its north tower — is the main landmark.

For those with more time, the Lahn cycle path offers a satisfying way to travel between all three cities on two wheels over a couple of days.
