The Theater Gießen is the main municipal theatre of the city, offering opera, drama, and dance productions in a purpose-built house that serves as a cultural anchor for both the city and the surrounding Middle Hessian region. The theatre has a reputation for adventurous programming relative to its size — it punches above its weight and regularly attracts productions of national interest.

The building sits close to the city centre and is in easy walking distance from the university. The theatre season runs from autumn to early summer, and tickets are reasonably priced compared to larger German cities. For visitors with any interest in German-language theatre or opera, it is worth checking the schedule before arrival — the student population keeps demand for tickets healthy and the programming lively.
