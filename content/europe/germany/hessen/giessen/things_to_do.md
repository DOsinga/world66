title: Things to Do
type: section
---

Giessen is a university city with a compact set of genuine attractions. The [Liebig Museum](/europe/germany/hessen/giessen/liebig_museum) and the [Mathematikum](/europe/germany/hessen/giessen/mathematikum) are both world-class in their way and enough on their own to justify a visit. The [River Lahn](/europe/germany/hessen/giessen/lahn_river) provides the city's outdoor life — cycling, canoeing, and swimming in summer.
