---
title: When to Go
type: section
---

Giessen has a continental climate typical of central Germany: cold winters, warm summers, and rainfall spread fairly evenly through the year. The best time to visit is late spring (May to June) or early autumn (September to October), when temperatures are comfortable and the city's parks and riverside paths are at their most pleasant.

Summer (July to August) brings the highest temperatures and the most activity along the Lahn — this is when canoeing, cycling, and riverside swimming are in full swing. The university is quieter in summer as students leave for the break, which reduces the city's characteristic buzz somewhat. Winter is grey and often damp; there is little reason to time a visit for the colder months unless you are here for other purposes.

The university semester runs October to February and April to July. Visiting during semester makes the city feel most alive — the student-run bars and cafes are busiest, and the theatre and music scene is more active. Giessen hosts a modest market in the weeks before Christmas, centred around the central square (Seltersweg).
