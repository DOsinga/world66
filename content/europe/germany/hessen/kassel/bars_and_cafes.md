---
title: "Bars and Cafes"
type: section
---

The university campus and the streets around the Königsstrasse give Kassel a livelier evening scene than many similarly sized German cities. **Café Nenninger** is the venerable cake-and-coffee institution. **Sandershaus**, in a vast restored industrial building south of the centre, is a creative-quarter hub with a beer garden and live music.

**Wirtshaus zur Klosterkirche** in the old town serves regional beers in a half-timbered building with one of the prettiest courtyards in central Kassel. The **Lolita Bar** on Wilhelmshöher Allee is the place for cocktails. Late-night dancing happens around the Kulturzentrum Schlachthof, in the old slaughterhouse buildings.
