Caricatura is Kassel's gallery for comic art, satire, cartoons, and caricature. It is based in the KulturBahnhof at the main station and presents changing exhibitions tied to German and international comic art.

The gallery is a good stop if you want a lighter counterpoint to Kassel's larger art museums and documenta sites. Visits are usually short, making it easy to combine with other central sights.
