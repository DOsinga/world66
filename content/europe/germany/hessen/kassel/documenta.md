**Documenta** is, for 100 days every five years, the centre of the contemporary art world. The next edition is documenta 16 in 2027. The exhibition spreads across the Fridericianum, the documenta-Halle, several other historic buildings, and a great many sites in the city's parks and streets.

Outside the exhibition years the documenta legacy is still all over Kassel. **Joseph Beuys's 7000 Oaks**, each paired with a basalt column, are still growing throughout the city. **Walter De Maria's Vertical Earth Kilometer** is a brass rod sunk a kilometre into the Friedrichsplatz, marked only by a small square cap. **Jonathan Borofsky's Man Walking to the Sky** still strides upward from the Kulturbahnhof. Even between editions, Kassel functions as a permanent documenta exhibition.
