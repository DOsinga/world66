---
title: "Eating Out"
type: section
---

Kassel's local speciality is **Ahle Worscht**, a dry-cured pork sausage hung in farmhouse attics until it develops a deep flavour distinct from anything in southern Germany. The best place to try it with regional sides like *Weckewerk* is at **Gleis 1** in the Kulturbahnhof or at the long-running **Weinhaus Boos** in the Mittelgasse.

For more ambitious cooking, **Pfeffermühle** in the Markthalle is a reliable seasonal kitchen. **Voit** near the Friedrichsplatz has international ambitions and a Michelin recommendation. Vegetarian and vegan options have multiplied around the university quarter — **El Erni** is the go-to falafel and Middle Eastern stop, **Lebenslust** is the bigger sit-down option. Cheap and good: any of the kebab places along Friedrich-Ebert-Straße.
