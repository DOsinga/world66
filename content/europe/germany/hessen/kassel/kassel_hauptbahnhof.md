The old **Kassel Hauptbahnhof** — once the city's main railway station, now relegated to a regional stop after long-distance trains moved to Wilhelmshöhe — has been reborn as the **Kulturbahnhof**. The handsome 19th-century terminus building now houses a cinema, the Caricatura cartoon museum, a music club, and a series of bars and restaurants in the old waiting rooms.

In front of the building stands Jonathan Borofsky's iconic *Man Walking to the Sky*. The square is a good place for a coffee and a look at one of the city's most famous documenta survivors before catching a tram into the old town.
