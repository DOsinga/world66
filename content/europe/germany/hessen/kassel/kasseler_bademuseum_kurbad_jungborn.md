Kasseler Bademuseum - Kurbad Jungborn tells the story of Kassel's old river baths, using one of the last surviving bath buildings on the Fulda. The baths no longer operate, but the historic riverside setting and small museum make it a quiet stop for visitors interested in everyday urban history.
