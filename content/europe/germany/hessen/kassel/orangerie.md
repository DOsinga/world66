The **Orangerie** is a long, low Baroque pleasure palace stretching along the bank of the Fulda at the edge of the **Karlsaue** park. Built between 1703 and 1711 by Landgrave Karl, it now houses the city's astronomy and physics museum (the Astronomisch-Physikalisches Kabinett) and a planetarium.

The Karlsaue itself is one of the largest Baroque parks in Germany, an island-and-canal landscape of formal flower beds, mature trees, and meandering paths along the river. The setting includes a swan island, an artificial canal system, and during documenta years a whole second exhibition site spreads through the park. Free, always open, perfect for a long evening walk.
