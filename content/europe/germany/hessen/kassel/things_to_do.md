title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

The two essentials are the **Bergpark Wilhelmshöhe** and its **Wasserspiele**, and the contemporary art legacy of the **documenta** — which between editions you experience as the **Fridericianum**, the **7000 Oaks**, the **Vertical Earth Kilometer**, and the **Man Walking to the Sky** at the old Hauptbahnhof. The **Wilhelmshöhe Palace** picture gallery, the **Neue Galerie**, and the **Hessian State Museum** cover the more conventional art-and-history museum needs.

The **Brothers Grimm** are the city's other patron spirits — **GRIMMWELT** is the modern museum, and the city centre is the original setting for much of their fieldwork. For something stranger, the **Museum of Sepulchral Culture** and the **Artists' Necropolis** above the city are unforgettable. Walks along the **Karlsaue** and up the **Bergpark** are the open-air pleasures.
