Traumschiff Tante Olga is one of Kassel’s stranger public artworks, a documenta-era piece with roots in the city’s protest and counterculture history. Like many works left behind by documenta, it is easy to miss if you are moving only between museums and parks.

It is worth a short detour for travellers interested in how Kassel’s art identity spills into ordinary streets. The piece is not conventionally beautiful; its value is that it makes the city’s post-war and activist layers visible in public space.
