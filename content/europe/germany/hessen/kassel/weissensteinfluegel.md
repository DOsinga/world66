The Weissenstein Wing is one of the historic interiors of Wilhelmshoehe Palace, set inside Kassel's UNESCO-listed Bergpark. Its rooms help explain the courtly side of the landscape: this was not just a scenic hillside but a carefully staged princely world.

Visit it together with the palace collections and the Hercules-waterworks route if opening times line up. The wing is especially useful for travellers who want context before walking the park's grand axes, cascades, and viewpoints.
