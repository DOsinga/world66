---
title: 'When to Go'
type: section
---

Limburg is at its best between May and early October, when long evenings light up the cathedral and the cafés on Domplatz spill onto the cobbles. Summers are warm but rarely uncomfortable; winters are grey and wet, though the small Christmas market on the cathedral square is a worthwhile reason to visit in December.

Avoid arriving on a Monday, when most museums close, and try to be in town on a weekday afternoon if you want the old town more or less to yourself — the day-trip coaches from Frankfurt cluster around midday and clear out by 5pm.
