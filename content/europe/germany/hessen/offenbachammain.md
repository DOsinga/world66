Offenbach am Main sits directly across the river from [Frankfurt](/europe/germany/hessen/frankfurt) and is very much its own city despite being swallowed by the same metropolitan sprawl. It has the highest proportion of foreign residents of any large German city — over a third of its population — which gives it a genuinely multicultural character, particularly visible in its food scene and street life.

The city's industrial past centred on leather goods, and the [Deutsches Ledermuseum](/europe/germany/hessen/offenbachammain/deutsches_ledermuseum) (German Leather Museum) remains one of the more unusual specialist museums in Germany. Design has stayed part of the city's identity: the [Klingspor Museum](/europe/germany/hessen/offenbachammain/klingspor_museum) holds an excellent collection of book art and typography. Along the riverfront, old factories are slowly converting into studios and offices, and the [Mainuferpark](/europe/germany/hessen/offenbachammain/mainuferpark) offers a pleasant walk with Frankfurt's skyline across the water.
