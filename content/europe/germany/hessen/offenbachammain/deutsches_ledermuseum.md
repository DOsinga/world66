The Deutsches Ledermuseum (German Leather Museum) is the largest leather museum in the world and one of the more unusual specialist institutions in Germany, holding over 30,000 objects documenting the history, craft, and culture of leather across five millennia. The collection includes footwear from ancient Egypt, Japanese lacquered armour, Moroccan bookbinding, and an extensive shoe collection spanning from medieval Europe to contemporary fashion design.

The museum shares its building with the Shoe Museum and a smaller section on African and pre-Columbian art that reflects the historical breadth of the collection. It's genuinely absorbing for visitors interested in material culture and craft history — and the sheer oddity of a leather museum earns it points for singularity. Open Tuesday–Sunday.
