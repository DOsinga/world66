Kloster Eberbach is one of the Rheingau's great monastery sites, a large Cistercian complex surrounded by vineyards above the Rhine. Its Romanesque and Gothic rooms are handsome in their own right, and film fans may recognise the interiors from *The Name of the Rose*.
