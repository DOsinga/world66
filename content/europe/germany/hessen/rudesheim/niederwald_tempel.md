The Niederwald-Tempel sits above Rüdesheim between the gondola station and the Niederwald Monument, with broad views over the Rhine countryside. Count Ostein built the little classical temple in 1788 as a landscape ornament, and in the Romantic era it drew artists and writers to the hillside.

It is a short stop rather than a major sight, but the combination of view, walk, and Rhine Valley atmosphere makes it worth including on the Niederwald loop.
