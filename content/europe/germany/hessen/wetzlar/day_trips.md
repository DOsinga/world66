Wetzlar makes a good base for the upper Lahn and the rolling country of central Hesse. **Marburg** with its hillside castle and university Altstadt is 35 minutes north by train; **Limburg an der Lahn**, with one of the finest Romanesque cathedrals in Germany, is 40 minutes southwest. **Giessen** is the closest big town (15 minutes), useful for shopping and services rather than sights. **Frankfurt** is under an hour by direct train, which makes the airport easy to reach.

Closer to home, **Schloss Braunfels** stands on a basalt crag 8 km southwest of Wetzlar — one of the most picturesque hilltop castles in Hesse, still owned by the Solms-Braunfels family and visitable on guided tours.
