---
title: 'Eating out'
type: section
---

Wetzlar's restaurant scene is concentrated in the Altstadt — most of the good places are within five minutes' walk of the cathedral. The local Hessian classics are **Handkäs mit Musik** (cheese marinated with onions, vinegar, and cumin), **Grüne Soße** (a seven-herb cold sauce served over potatoes or eggs), and **Frankfurter Würstchen**; the regional drink is **Apfelwein** (cider, often served in the traditional ribbed glass).

For everyday eating, the cafés and brasseries on the **Kornmarkt** have the most outdoor tables and a reliable mix of Hessian and international cooking. The **Eisenmarkt** has a smaller selection but better atmosphere.
