---
title: 'Getting there'
type: section
---

Wetzlar sits on the rail line between Frankfurt and the Ruhr. Direct **regional express trains** from Frankfurt take about 50 minutes; the InterCity service from Cologne or Düsseldorf via Siegen is roughly 2½ hours from Cologne. The station is a 10-minute walk north of the Altstadt across the Lahn.

By car, Wetzlar is on the **A45** ("Sauerlandlinie") between Dortmund and the A3, about 75 km north of Frankfurt. Free parking is available on the Lahninsel directly below the cathedral, which is the most convenient option for a day visit.
