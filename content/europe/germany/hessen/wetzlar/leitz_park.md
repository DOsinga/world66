Leitz-Park is the modern Leica complex on the eastern edge of Wetzlar, opened in 2014. It groups the factory, the corporate headquarters, the Ernst Leitz Museum, a gallery, a hotel, and the company shop on a single site. The **museum** traces the history of the company from 19th-century microscopes through Oskar Barnack's first 35mm prototypes to today's M-series and S-series.

The factory itself can be visited on a guided tour, which goes through the lens-grinding workshops and the camera assembly hall — a serious treat for anyone interested in optics or fine mechanical manufacturing. Book ahead. Cyclists and walkers can reach the park from the Altstadt in 20 minutes along the Lahn.
