St. Johannes Baptist is a modest Gothic-style Catholic church built from Bentheim sandstone in the late seventeenth century. Its oak altars and quiet interior make it a worthwhile stop if you are already walking through town after visiting the castle.

It is not the reason to come to Bad Bentheim, but it adds another layer to the town's religious and architectural history.
