Vital Park is Bad Gandersheim's salt-water bathing and wellness complex. The main draw for visitors is the warm brine pool, with massage and therapy services available separately.

It is a practical rainy-day or recovery stop, especially if you are in town for the abbey, the theatre festival, or walking in the surrounding hills.
