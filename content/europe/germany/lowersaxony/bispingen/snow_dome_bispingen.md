Snow Dome Bispingen is an indoor ski slope with a 300 m run, a snowboard park, a sledging slope, and lessons for beginners. It is more a fun day out than an alpine substitute, but it works well for families or anyone who wants a quick taste of winter sports on the Lüneburg Heath.
