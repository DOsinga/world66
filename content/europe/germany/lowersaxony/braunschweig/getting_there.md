---
title: "Getting There"
type: section
---

**Braunschweig Hauptbahnhof** is on the main ICE line between Hannover and Berlin, with fast trains roughly every half hour. From Hannover it's 35 minutes; from Berlin Hauptbahnhof, around 2 hours. Frankfurt is reachable in under 3 hours with a change at Hannover. Direct trains also run to Hamburg (1 hour 30 minutes).

The city has a small airport (Braunschweig-Wolfsburg Airport) but it handles almost no scheduled passenger flights; all practical air travel uses **Hannover Airport**, 50 km west, from which regional trains complete the journey in about 40 minutes. By car, the A2 autobahn (Hamburg–Berlin) passes north of the city; the A391 connects to the city centre.
