The Literaturzentrum occupies the house where German author Wilhelm Raabe lived from 1901 until his death in 1910. It now serves as a literary museum, research centre, and venue for readings and other literature events.

It is a focused stop for visitors interested in German literature or Braunschweig's cultural history.
