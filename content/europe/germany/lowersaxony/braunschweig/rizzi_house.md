Rizzi-House is Braunschweig's loudest piece of modern architecture: a bright, cartoonish office building designed by American pop artist James Rizzi. Its painted faces, warped windows, and playful colours make a sharp contrast with the more formal buildings around the Schlossarkaden.

You do not need long here, but it is a fun detour while walking through the centre. It is best appreciated from outside, as a piece of public art folded into everyday city life.
