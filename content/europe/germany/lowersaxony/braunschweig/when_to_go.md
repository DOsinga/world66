---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

Braunschweig has a temperate continental climate: cold, occasionally snowy winters (December–February) and warm summers (June–August, typically 18–25°C). The city is pleasant to visit year-round for its indoor museums, but the outdoor medieval squares are best in late spring and summer when café tables spill outside.

**December** is a particularly good time to visit: Braunschweig has one of Lower Saxony's larger and more atmospheric Christmas markets, centred on the Altstadtmarkt and Burgplatz, running from late November through Christmas Eve.

The **asparagus season** (mid-April to June) brings a notable shift in restaurant menus throughout Lower Saxony and is reason enough to time a visit for late spring.
