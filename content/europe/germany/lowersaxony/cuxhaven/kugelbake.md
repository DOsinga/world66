The Kugelbake marks the point where the Elbe ends and the North Sea begins. The wooden navigation mark is simple, but it has become Cuxhaven's signature landmark and appears on the city's coat of arms.

The setting is the point: wide water, passing ships, wind, and the feeling of standing at a real edge. It is worth walking out here even if you normally skip local symbols.
