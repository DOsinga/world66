Schloss Ritzebuettel is a compact castle-like building near the centre of Cuxhaven. It is a useful counterpoint to the harbour sights, a reminder that the town's story is not only fishing boats and beach tourism.

Do not expect a grand castle day out. Treat it as a short architectural stop while moving between the station, shops, and waterfront.
