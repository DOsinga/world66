Windstaerke 10 is Cuxhaven's shipwreck and fishing museum. The subject suits the town well: this coast is about navigation, storms, workboats, and the risks of making a living from the sea.

Go here if you want context for the harbour rather than just a walk along it. The museum gives the waterfront a harder edge and makes the fishing-town identity feel less decorative.
