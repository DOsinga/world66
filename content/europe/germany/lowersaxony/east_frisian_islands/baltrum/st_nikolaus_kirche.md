St. Nikolaus Kirche is a modest church on Baltrum, but on a small East Frisian island modest places matter. Its scale, brickwork, and village setting fit the island's car-free, windblown rhythm better than a grand monument would.

Stop in while walking through the village rather than making it the centre of your day. It combines naturally with the old customs house, dunes, and beach walks that make Baltrum worth visiting.
