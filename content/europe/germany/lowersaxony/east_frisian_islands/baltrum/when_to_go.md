---
title: When to Go
type: section
---

The main season is June to August, when the beach is usable and the full complement of services operates. The island is at its most crowded in July and August. May and September are quieter and often fine. Winter is for the dedicated — the island is largely closed, most guesthouses shut, and the North Sea weather is fierce, but the solitude and the quality of light can be remarkable.
