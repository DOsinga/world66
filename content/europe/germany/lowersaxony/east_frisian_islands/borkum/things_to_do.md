Borkum's appeal is its landscape and its air — the UNESCO-listed Wadden Sea on the sheltered south side, wide North Sea beaches to the north, and the iodine-rich sea air that has made it a health resort since the 19th century. The [New Lighthouse](/europe/germany/lowersaxony/east_frisian_islands/borkum/new_lighthouse) dominates the island's skyline, while the [Old Lighthouse](/europe/germany/lowersaxony/east_frisian_islands/borkum/old_lighthouse) and the [Dykhus Museum](/europe/germany/lowersaxony/east_frisian_islands/borkum/dykhus_museum) tell the island's whaling past. For the best outdoor experience, join a guided [Wadden Sea mudflat walk](/europe/germany/lowersaxony/east_frisian_islands/borkum/wadden_sea_mudflat_walk) — it's unlike anything else in Germany.
