---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

The main season runs from May through September, when the beaches are usable and the ferry schedules are fullest. July and August are the busiest months, with families and a strong German tourist crowd. Spring — particularly May and June — is often better for birding and mudflat walks, when migratory birds pass through in enormous numbers.

Winter is quiet. Many guesthouses and restaurants close from November through March, and the North Sea weather is raw and often stormy. Some visitors actively prefer it for the dramatic skies, empty beaches, and low prices. The island's health resort facilities — thalassotherapy, spa treatments — tend to stay open year-round for that market.
