Island Church Juist is a simple but useful landmark in the village, the kind of place that gives the car-free island a centre of gravity. It is not grand, but its scale suits Juist: modest, community-minded, and closely tied to the rhythm of island life.

Step inside if the doors are open, or use it as a pause while wandering between the ferry area, shops, and beach approaches. On a small island, these everyday buildings often say more about local life than bigger attractions would.
