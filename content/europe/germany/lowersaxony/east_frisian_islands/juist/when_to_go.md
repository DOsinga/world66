---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

Juist's high season is short: late June to early September, when the North Sea is just about warm enough to swim in (16–18°C) and the days are long. July and August are busiest — accommodation must be booked months ahead — but the island is large enough relative to its visitor numbers that the beaches never feel crowded.

May, June, and September are arguably better. The weather is more variable but the air is mild, the migratory birds are at their best (May and September), and prices and crowds drop sharply. In winter the island is almost empty: a handful of hotels stay open, the ferry runs a reduced schedule, and gales off the North Sea make even the village a stark place.
