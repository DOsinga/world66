---
title: When to Go
type: section
---

Summer (July and August) is peak season on Langeoog, when the island fills with German families and the beaches are at their busiest. School holiday periods see maximum crowds and the highest prices.

**Spring (May and June)** is arguably the best time to visit: the weather is mild, the island is less crowded, and the lesser black-backed gulls and other seabirds are nesting in the dunes — prime birdwatching season. The Wadden Sea mudflats are accessible for walks at low tide.

**Autumn (September and October)** offers similar advantages: quieter beaches, milder prices, and dramatic North Sea light. Storms begin rolling in from October onwards, which is part of the appeal for those who like a bracing coast.

**Winter** is bleak but not without its fans. Storms, empty beaches, and a handful of year-round restaurants make it a destination for those seeking solitude.
