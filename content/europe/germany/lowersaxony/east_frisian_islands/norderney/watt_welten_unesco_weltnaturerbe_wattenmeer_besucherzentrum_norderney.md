Watt Welten is the main Norderney visitor centre for understanding the Wadden Sea, the tidal mudflat landscape that defines the East Frisian Islands. Exhibits explain tides, birds, marine life, coastal protection, and the UNESCO World Heritage ecosystem outside the door.

It is an excellent first stop before a mudflat walk or beach day because it makes the flat, shifting landscape much easier to read. Families and nature-minded travelers will get the most from it.
