---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

The main season is July and August, when the island is packed with German families on beach holidays. Book accommodation well in advance for midsummer. The Strandkorb chairs are rented out and the beach is genuinely lively; it can feel crowded.

June and September are quieter and still pleasant for beach visits — the water is cold but swimmable (14–17°C), the dunes are beautiful, and the restaurants have room. The Wadden Sea mudflat walks are good from April through October.

Winter is for the committed: the island gets battered by North Sea storms, the sea is often grey and churning, and most tourist facilities close. But if you like dramatic northern European coastline, a winter weekend on Norderney has its own appeal — the dunes empty out completely and the landscape becomes elemental.
