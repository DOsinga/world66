The Museums-Pferdebahn is one of Spiekeroog's gentlest oddities: a horse-drawn railway using the island's old transport tradition. In season, the open carriage runs from the former station area toward the west end, moving at a pace that suits a car-free East Frisian island.

It is not a major museum in the city sense, but on Spiekeroog it has real charm and local identity. Check the seasonal timetable before counting on it, since weather and operating days matter more here than on a conventional attraction.
