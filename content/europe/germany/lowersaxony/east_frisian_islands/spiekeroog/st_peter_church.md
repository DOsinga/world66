St. Peter Church is Spiekeroog's newer Catholic church, a modest modern building that complements the island's much older Alte Inselkirche. On a small North Sea island, churches are not just architectural stops; they mark the way communities have adapted to isolation, tourism, storms, and seasonal rhythms.

It is worth a brief look while wandering through the village, especially if you are comparing the island's old and newer religious buildings. The church is quiet, simple, and best approached as part of a slow walk rather than a standalone attraction.
