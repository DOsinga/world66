---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

The North Sea climate is bracing in all seasons. Summer (July and August) is the warmest and busiest period — sea temperatures reach 18–20°C, the days are long, and the beach is at its most popular. Book ferries and accommodation well in advance for this period.

Spring and autumn are excellent for birdwatching, when migrating shorebirds and waterfowl pass through in large numbers. The island is quieter, the light is often dramatic, and the Wadden Sea walks are less crowded. September and October see spectacular goose migrations.

Winter is possible for hardy visitors who want the island virtually to themselves — the dunes, empty beach, and grey North Sea have their own stark appeal — but some facilities close out of season. Check before travelling.
