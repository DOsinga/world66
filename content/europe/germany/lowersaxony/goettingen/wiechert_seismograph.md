The Wiechert Earthquake Station, on the wooded Hainberg hill east of the city, was established in 1899 by the physicist Emil Wiechert as the first dedicated seismological observatory in Germany. The original instruments are still in place and still working — the 17-ton vertical-pendulum seismograph is the oldest of its kind anywhere.

The station is run by the university and opens for guided tours on weekends and by appointment; check the institute's website for current times. The walk up from the city centre takes about 40 minutes through the Stadtwald.
