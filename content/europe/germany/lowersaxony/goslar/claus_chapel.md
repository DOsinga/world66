Claus Chapel is a small Romanesque hall church from the 12th century, built into Goslar's old town fortifications. It later served as a miners' hospice chapel, linking it to the mining history that shaped the town. It is a compact stop that pairs naturally with the Kaiserpfalz and Goslar's medieval streets.
