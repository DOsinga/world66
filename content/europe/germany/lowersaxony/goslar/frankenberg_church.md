Frankenberg Church stands in one of Goslar's older quarters, away from the busiest lanes around the market square. Its stonework and setting make it a useful stop for seeing the town as a lived-in medieval mining city rather than only a postcard centre.

The church pairs well with a walk through the Frankenberg district, where half-timbered houses and sloping streets carry the old town beyond its headline monuments. It is a short visit, but a good one for travellers who like urban texture.
