Bueckeburg Palace is a handsome princely residence west of Hanover, within day-trip
reach of Hameln. Its rooms, parkland, and courtly setting offer a more aristocratic
counterpoint to Hameln's half-timbered old town and Pied Piper folklore.

The attached Hofreitschule, or court riding school, adds a distinctive equestrian angle
when performances or demonstrations are running. Check schedules before making the trip,
because the palace and riding school are much better when you can see more than the
exterior.
