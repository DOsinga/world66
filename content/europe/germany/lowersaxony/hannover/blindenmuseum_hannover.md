Blindenmuseum Hannover is a specialist museum with a subject most travelers have rarely seen treated in depth. Its collections explain the history of blind education, Braille, tactile learning, mobility aids, and everyday tools designed for people with visual impairments.

The museum is modest, but the perspective is valuable. It is best for curious visitors who like focused social-history museums rather than broad city surveys.
