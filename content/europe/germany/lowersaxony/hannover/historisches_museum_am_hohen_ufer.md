Historisches Museum am Hohen Ufer is Hanover's city history museum, covering the development of the town from its medieval origins to the present. Exhibits connect Hanover itself with the wider region and the former royal house.

The museum is a good first stop if you want context before walking the old town, the Leine riverfront, and the rebuilt historic core. Expect local history rather than blockbuster art.
