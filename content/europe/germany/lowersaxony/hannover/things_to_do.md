---

Hanover is greener and more walkable than its trade-fair reputation suggests. The compact reconstructed [Altstadt](/europe/germany/lowersaxony/hannover/altstadt) clusters around the [Marktkirche](/europe/germany/lowersaxony/hannover/marktkirche), the [Marktplatz](/europe/germany/lowersaxony/hannover/marktplatz) and the [Old Town Hall](/europe/germany/lowersaxony/hannover/oldtownhall), and from there it is a short stroll to the [Maschsee](/europe/germany/lowersaxony/hannover/maschsee) lake, the [New Town Hall](/europe/germany/lowersaxony/hannover/new_town_hall) with its slanted dome lift, or into the vast [Eilenriede](/europe/germany/lowersaxony/hannover/eilenriede) city forest.

The baroque [Herrenhausen Gardens](/europe/germany/lowersaxony/hannover/herrenhausen_gardens) — split between the formal [Grosser Garten](/europe/germany/lowersaxony/hannover/grosser_garten) and the botanical [Berggarten](/europe/germany/lowersaxony/hannover/berggarten) — are the headline sight. The [Sprengel Museum](/europe/germany/lowersaxony/hannover/sprengel_museum) on the Maschsee shore holds one of Germany's strongest 20th-century collections; the [Niedersächsisches Landesmuseum](/europe/germany/lowersaxony/hannover/niedersaechsisches_landesmuseum) covers everything from prehistoric bog people to old masters; and the [Kestnergesellschaft](/europe/germany/lowersaxony/hannover/kestnergesellschaft) shows ambitious contemporary art.

Architecture buffs should walk the marked Red Thread route between the [Nanas](/europe/germany/lowersaxony/hannover/nanas) on the Leibnizufer, Frank Gehry's [twisting tower](/europe/germany/lowersaxony/hannover/gehry_tower) and the Expressionist [Anzeiger-Hochhaus](/europe/germany/lowersaxony/hannover/anzeiger_hochhaus). Save an evening for the bars of [Lister Meile](/europe/germany/lowersaxony/hannover/lister_meile) and the [List](/europe/germany/lowersaxony/hannover/list) district, or for the alternative scene in [Linden](/europe/germany/lowersaxony/hannover/linden) across the river.
