Brockengarten is an alpine botanical garden near the summit of the Brocken, where the exposed Harz climate creates mountain conditions at a relatively low elevation. It has grown high-altitude plants from around the world since the late 19th century.

Visits are by guided tour, often tied to the summit circular path. It is most interesting if you are already heading up the Brocken and want more than the usual weather-station-and-viewpoint stop.
