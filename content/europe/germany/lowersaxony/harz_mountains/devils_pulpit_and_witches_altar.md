Devil's Pulpit and Witches' Altar are two granite outcrops on the southern side of the Brocken. They are tied to the Harz Mountains' Walpurgis Night folklore, when legend has witches gathering on the mountain.

The rocks lie in a protected area and are not for climbing, but they can be seen from the Gipfelrundweg. They work best as a small folklore-flavoured stop on a Brocken walk rather than as a separate excursion.
