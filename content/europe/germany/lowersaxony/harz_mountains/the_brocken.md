The Brocken is the highest summit in the Harz and the highest mountain in northern Germany, rising to 1,141 metres. You can hike up through forest and open summit moor, or take the narrow-gauge Brockenbahn, which climbs with a satisfying amount of steam and drama.

The mountain has a strange pull: Goethe put witches here in Faust, Cold War East Germany turned the summit into a sealed military zone, and the weather can change from clear to hostile in minutes. On a good day, the views are huge.
