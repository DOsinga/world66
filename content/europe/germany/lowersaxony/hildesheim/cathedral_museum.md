Hildesheim's Cathedral Museum holds a strong collection of church treasure, sculpture, manuscripts, and liturgical art tied to the cathedral's long history. It is the natural follow-up to the cathedral and Bernward Doors, giving more time and context to objects that can feel too dense in a quick church visit.
