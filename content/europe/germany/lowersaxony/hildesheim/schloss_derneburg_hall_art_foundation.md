Schloss Derneburg is a former monastery and castle complex now associated with the Hall Art Foundation. The attraction is the combination of large-scale contemporary art, historic architecture, and a rural estate setting.

It sits outside Hildesheim, so plan it as a deliberate excursion and check opening arrangements before going. For art-minded travellers, it makes a strong contrast to Hildesheim's medieval churches and UNESCO heritage.
