Stadtmuseum Hildesheim tells the city's story from inside one of its most recognisable rebuilt timber-framed buildings, the Knochenhauer-Amtshaus on the market square. The permanent exhibition is best for visitors who want local context after seeing the cathedral, St Michael's, and the restored old town.
