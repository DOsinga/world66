The Old Customs House Museum is the town's main local-history museum, set near the river in a former customs building. It explains Hitzacker's position on the Elbe, the local trading history, and the stories behind the old houses in town.

It is a small museum, but that suits Hitzacker. Pair it with a walk through the old town and up the Weinberg rather than treating it as a standalone destination.
