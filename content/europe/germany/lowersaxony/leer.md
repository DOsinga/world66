Leer sits in East Frisia, close to the Dutch border, where the Leda meets the Ems marsh country. It is small, flat, and quietly handsome, with brick houses, canals, a working harbour, and enough tea culture to remind you that this corner of Germany looks west as much as east.

The old town is the reason to stop. Its lanes run between gabled merchants' houses, the harbour, and little bridges over the water. [Buenting Teemuseum](/europe/germany/lowersaxony/leer/buenting_teemuseum) is the best introduction to the East Frisian habit of drinking strong black tea with rock sugar and cream, while [Leeraner Miniaturland](/europe/germany/lowersaxony/leer/leeraner_miniaturland) gives a family-friendly model version of the region.

Leer is not a place of big sights, and that is part of its appeal. It works well as a gentle stop between [Groningen](/europe/netherlands/groningen), Emden, the East Frisian Islands, and the inland towns of Lower Saxony.
