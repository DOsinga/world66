Buenting Teemuseum is a compact museum devoted to East Frisian tea culture. It explains the local ritual of strong black tea, rock sugar, and cream, and why tea matters so much in a region better known to outsiders for wind, marshes, and fishing towns.

The museum belongs to the Buenting tea company and sits in the old town, so it is easy to fold into a short walk around Leer. Brunnenstrasse 33.
