Leeraner Miniaturland is a detailed miniature version of East Frisia, with model trains, harbours, villages, and island scenes. It is more playful than profound, but it is a good rainy-day stop and works well for families.

The museum is northwest of the centre at Konrad-Zuse-Strasse 1.
