---
title: "Eating Out"
type: section
---

For a small city Lüneburg eats well, with the student population keeping prices moderate and a good range from traditional Northern German to international. The eating areas are the Stintmarkt waterfront in summer, Schröderstraße and Heiligengeiststraße for year-round restaurants, and the streets just north of Am Sande for cafés and lighter food.

For traditional Lower Saxon cooking, **Mälzer Brau- und Tafelhaus** on Heiligengeiststraße is the long-established choice — a brewpub with its own copper kettles, doing decent Schweinehaxe, Labskaus, and house-brewed Pils. **Krone Bier- und Eventhaus** on the corner of Heiligengeiststraße is a similar formula but slightly cheaper and rowdier. **Anno 1517** in a leaning gabled house on Am Sande does proper sit-down regional cooking.

For a treat, **Restaurant Röhms Deli** on Bardowicker Straße does inventive modern food using regional ingredients, with a good wine list. The Stintmarkt has a strip of cheap and cheerful options for warm evenings — the **Pons** and **El Cubo** are reliable, and the **Schallander** brewpub does its own beer overlooking the river.
