Salü Spa is Lüneburg's public thermal bath complex, with indoor and outdoor pools, saunas, massage facilities, and a gym. It works best as a relaxed break after a day of walking the old salt-trading city.

The baths are close enough to the centre to combine with the German Salt Museum or a stroll through Lüneburg's old streets.
