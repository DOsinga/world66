Wildpark Müden is a small wildlife park on the edge of the village, laid out through woods and streamside enclosures. It focuses mostly on European animals and is built for easy wandering rather than spectacle.

For adults without children it is optional, but families will likely be glad of it. It also works as a low-effort outing when the weather is too changeable for a longer heath walk.
