Norden-Norddeich is really two linked moods of East Frisia. Norden is the old inland town, with a broad market square, brick churches, and tea culture taken seriously. Norddeich is the North Sea edge: harbour, sea air, mudflats, seal station, and ferries out to [Norderney](/europe/germany/lowersaxony/east_frisian_islands/norderney) and Juist.

This is not a dramatic place, but it has a strong local flavour. The [Ostfriesisches Teemuseum](/europe/germany/lowersaxony/norden_norddeich/ostfriesisches_teemuseum) is the best introduction to the regional ritual of strong tea, cream, and rock sugar. Nearby, [Ludgeri Church](/europe/germany/lowersaxony/norden_norddeich/ludgeri_church) has one of Germany's great historic organs. In Norddeich, the [Seehundstation Nationalpark-Haus](/europe/germany/lowersaxony/norden_norddeich/seehundstation_nationalpark_haus) makes the Wadden Sea feel less empty and more alive.

Use Norden-Norddeich as more than a ferry platform. Spend half a day in Norden, then go out to the coast for the seal station, a mudflat walk, or a ferry ride if the tides and timetable line up.
