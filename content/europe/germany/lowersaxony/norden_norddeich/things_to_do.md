---
title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Norden-Norddeich is strongest when you split your time between the old town and the coast. In Norden, visit the [Ostfriesisches Teemuseum](/europe/germany/lowersaxony/norden_norddeich/ostfriesisches_teemuseum) and [Ludgeri Church](/europe/germany/lowersaxony/norden_norddeich/ludgeri_church). In Norddeich, the [Seehundstation Nationalpark-Haus](/europe/germany/lowersaxony/norden_norddeich/seehundstation_nationalpark_haus) gives useful context before you head toward the mudflats or ferry terminal.
