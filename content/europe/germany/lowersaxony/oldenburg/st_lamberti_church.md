St Lamberti dominates the market square, but the surprise is inside. The exterior reads like a north German brick church; the interior is a pale, round, neoclassical space rebuilt in the late 18th century, closer to a court chapel than a medieval parish church.

It is a short stop, but a worthwhile one, especially because it sits between the castle, the old town hall, and the pedestrian streets.
