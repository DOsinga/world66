Museum am Scholerberg is Osnabrueck's natural-history and environment museum, with exhibits on regional landscapes, ecology, geology, and biodiversity. A planetarium adds a science angle that makes the museum useful beyond rainy-day backup.

It is strongest for families and travellers who like local nature interpreted clearly. Because it sits near the zoo, the two can be combined into a full, practical day outside the old centre.
