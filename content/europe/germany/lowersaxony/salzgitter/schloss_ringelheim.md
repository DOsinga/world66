Schloss Ringelheim began as a monastery and later took on the form of a palace estate, giving this corner of Salzgitter a more graceful face than the city's industrial reputation suggests. The complex is best appreciated from outside, with the park softening the formal buildings.

It is a quiet stop, not a blockbuster. Come if you are exploring Salzgitter by car or bicycle and want a historic counterpoint to Schloss Salder, the old villages, and the wooded edges of the Harz foreland.
