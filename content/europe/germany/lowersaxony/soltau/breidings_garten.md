Breidings Garten is a quiet public garden on the edge of Soltau, laid out in an English landscape style with ponds, old trees, orchard land, rhododendrons, and a villa setting. It is the softest of Soltau's local sights and a good place to slow down after the more commercial attractions.

Come for a walk, not a spectacle. In spring and early summer the planting gives it more colour; at other times it is simply a pleasant green pocket.
