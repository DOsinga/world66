Heide Park is the reason most travellers know Soltau. It is one of Germany's largest theme parks, with big coasters, family rides, shows, and enough attractions to take a full day.

This is not a quick add-on to a heath walk. Treat it as the day's main plan, check the seasonal opening calendar, and expect crowds on summer weekends and school holidays.
