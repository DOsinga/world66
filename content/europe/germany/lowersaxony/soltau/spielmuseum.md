The Spielmuseum is a good small-town museum, especially with children. Its collection runs from old dolls' houses and model railways to teddy bears, wooden toys, and objects visitors are allowed to handle.

It is not a reason to travel across Germany, but it gives Soltau a real town-centre stop beyond Heide Park. Pair it with a walk through the centre or the Soltau Therme.
