Holdenstedt Castle Museum is a small local-history stop rather than a grand palace visit. Its value is in adding context: rural life, town history, and the older estates around Uelzen.

Go if you have a car or bicycle and want to stretch the visit beyond the station and old centre. Otherwise, keep it as an optional add-on.
