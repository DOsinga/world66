The German Horse Museum is the best place to understand why Verden is so strongly associated with horses. Its collections cover breeding, riding, farm work, sport, and the long cultural history of the animal in Germany.

It is a specialist museum, but not only for specialists. The library is extensive, and the exhibitions include tactile and visual displays that make the subject more approachable than a room full of saddles would be.
