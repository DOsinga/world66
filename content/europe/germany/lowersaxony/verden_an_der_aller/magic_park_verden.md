Magic Park Verden is a local amusement park aimed mainly at younger children. Expect simple rides, fairytale theming, play areas, and a petting zoo rather than big-thrill engineering.

For adults without children it is easy to skip. For families travelling between Bremen, Hannover, or the Lüneburg Heath, it can turn Verden from a cathedral stop into a full day out.
