The MS Stadt Verden runs seasonal boat trips on the Aller, showing the town and its low river landscape from the water. It is a gentle excursion rather than a headline attraction, but the pace suits Verden.

Check times before building a day around it. If there is no sailing, the riverside still makes a pleasant walk after the cathedral and horse museum.
