Sieben Steinhaeuser is a megalithic burial site on the Bergen-Hohne training area near Ostenholz. Despite the name, the site consists of five large stone tombs.

The draw is the age and scale of the prehistoric monuments, which stand out from the heath and forest around them. Access can be limited by military use of the area, so check opening days before making the trip.
