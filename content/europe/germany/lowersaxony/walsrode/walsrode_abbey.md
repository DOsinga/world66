Walsrode Abbey gives the town centre its oldest layer. The convent was first mentioned in 986, and while the complex has changed repeatedly, the quiet precinct still feels distinct from the everyday shopping streets around it.

Visits are mainly about atmosphere and local history rather than architectural spectacle. Pair it with the neighbouring town church and a walk through the centre before heading out to the larger attractions.
