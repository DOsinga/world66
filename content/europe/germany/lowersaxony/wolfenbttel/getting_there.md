---
title: Getting There
type: section
---

Wolfenbüttel is best reached from **Braunschweig**, 15 minutes north by frequent regional train (Erixx and DB Regio services). From Braunschweig there are direct ICE connections to Berlin (90 min), Hannover (30 min), and Frankfurt (3 h).

The station is a 10-minute walk south of the old town. By car, Wolfenbüttel sits on the A395 and B79; parking is easy on the edge of the centre, restricted inside.
