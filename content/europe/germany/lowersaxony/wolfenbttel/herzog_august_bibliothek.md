The Herzog August Bibliothek, founded in 1572, is one of the world's great collections of medieval and early-modern books. Around a million volumes, including 11,000 manuscripts and 350,000 pre-1850 books, fill several connected buildings on the western edge of the old town. The modern reading room is a serious place of research, but the public exhibition rooms display rotating selections of the most spectacular holdings.

The library was a magnet for scholars from across Europe. Leibniz was librarian from 1691 to 1716, Lessing from 1770 to his death in 1781. Their offices are still preserved in the older buildings. Allow at least two hours, more if you have any interest in books or printing.
