The narrow streets along the surviving canals on the eastern edge of the old town are known as Klein Venedig, or Little Venice. Half-timbered houses lean over the water; small wooden bridges cross at every corner; here and there a willow trails into the canal.

It is the most photographed corner of Wolfenbüttel and the easiest place to grasp how the town's network of waterways once functioned. Walk it in the late afternoon, when the low sun catches the timber.
