Schloss Wolfenbüttel was the seat of the Dukes of Brunswick-Lüneburg for two centuries. The building you see today is mostly 17th-century Baroque on a medieval core: a long white facade with an onion-domed tower, sitting across a moat from the old town.

The state apartments on the first floor have been restored as a museum, with rooms of original Baroque furniture, painted ceilings, and family portraits. The cellars hold the local history museum. The combined visit takes about an hour and a half; the courtyard and surrounding gardens are free to enter at any time.
