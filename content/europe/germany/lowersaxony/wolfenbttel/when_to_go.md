---
title: When to Go
type: section
---

Late spring and early summer (May to early July) are the most pleasant times to visit: the Fachwerk houses are at their best in good light, the canals of Klein Venedig are alive with greenery, and the Spargel (asparagus) season is in full swing in the restaurants.

December has its own appeal — the Christmas market on the Stadtmarkt is one of the prettiest in Lower Saxony, set against the half-timbered facades. Otherwise winter and the depths of November are cold and the days are short.
