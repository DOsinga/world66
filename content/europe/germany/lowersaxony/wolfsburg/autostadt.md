The Autostadt is the public face of Volkswagen, a 28-hectare park beside the main plant that is part car museum, part corporate showcase, part theme park. Each Volkswagen Group brand — VW, Audi, SEAT, Škoda, Porsche, Lamborghini, Bentley — has its own pavilion with concept cars, displays, and varying degrees of design pretension.

The standout feature is the pair of glass delivery towers, where new VWs wait to be handed over to their customers via a robotic carousel. The **ZeitHaus** museum is the historical core, with one of the great collections of significant twentieth-century cars from across all manufacturers. Allow most of a day; a multi-day pass exists and is worth it if you have any interest in cars or design.
