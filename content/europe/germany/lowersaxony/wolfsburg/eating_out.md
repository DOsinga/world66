---
title: "Eating Out"
type: section
---

Wolfsburg's eating scene is shaped by its corporate clientele and its workforce, in roughly equal measure. The Autostadt complex has half a dozen restaurants of varying ambition, including the Michelin-starred **Aqua** in the Ritz-Carlton hotel. Outside that bubble, central Wolfsburg has plenty of solid German, Italian, and Turkish places aimed at office lunches and after-shift dinners.

The Porschestraße pedestrian zone, which runs from the station to the Rathaus, has the largest concentration of restaurants in the centre and is a sensible place to start.
