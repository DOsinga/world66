Wolfsburg's attractions divide into two clusters. The **Autostadt**, **AutoMuseum**, and Volkswagen plant tours fill a full day around the canal-side complex north of the centre. South of the railway station, the **Phaeno**, **Kunstmuseum**, and **Alvar Aalto Cultural Centre** make up an unexpectedly serious cluster of postwar architecture and contemporary culture.

A single weekend handles both halves comfortably. Add **Allerpark** in summer for swimming, and **Schloss Wolfsburg** if you want a glimpse of the older landscape on which the planned city was overlaid.
