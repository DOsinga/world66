---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

Wolfsburg has a typical north German continental climate: cool, often grey winters and pleasant but unreliable summers. Most of the major attractions are indoors or under cover, so the time of year matters less than it does in many German cities.

The best months are late May through September. Allerpark and the cycle paths along the canal are at their best in summer, and the Autostadt's outdoor areas are pleasant on a sunny day. If you want to combine Wolfsburg with a VfL Wolfsburg or women's Champions League fixture, plan around the Bundesliga calendar between August and May.
