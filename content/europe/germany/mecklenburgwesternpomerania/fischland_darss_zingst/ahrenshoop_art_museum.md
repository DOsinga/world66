Ahrenshoop Art Museum tells the story of the painters, sculptors, and graphic artists drawn to this Baltic coast village and the surrounding Fischland-Darss-Zingst peninsula. The building itself is striking, and the collection is strongest when it links the local landscape to the artists who came here for its light, dunes, and sea air.
