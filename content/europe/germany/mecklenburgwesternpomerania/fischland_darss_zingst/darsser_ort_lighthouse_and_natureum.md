Darßer Ort Lighthouse stands at the wild northern tip of the Darß peninsula, surrounded by dunes, forest, and shifting Baltic coast. The lighthouse is the landmark, but the setting is the real reason to make the trip.

The attached Natureum, a branch of the German Maritime Museum in Stralsund, explains the coastal landscape, wildlife, and history of this exposed corner of Fischland-Darß-Zingst. Reaching it usually involves walking, cycling, or taking local transport through the protected landscape, which is part of the appeal.
