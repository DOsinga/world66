This small centre occupies the house where Caspar David Friedrich was born in 1774. It is more biographical than blockbuster, tracing the painter's family, workshop background, and Greifswald surroundings. Pair it with the Pomeranian State Museum and Eldena Abbey and the town's Romantic identity starts to make sense.
