Ahlbeck Pier is shorter than the one at Heringsdorf, but older and more atmospheric. Its white pavilion stands right on the beach and gives Ahlbeck a tidy, slightly nostalgic centre.

Come here as part of a promenade walk between the three Kaiserbad districts. The Polish resort city of Świnoujście is close enough that the walk can become an easy cross-border outing.
