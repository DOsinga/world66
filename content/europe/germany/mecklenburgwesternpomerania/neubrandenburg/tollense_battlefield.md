The Tollense battlefield is a Bronze Age archaeological site north of Neubrandenburg. Evidence from the valley points to a large battle in the 13th century BC involving thousands of warriors, an unusually early and large-scale conflict for northern Europe.

The site is mainly of interest for its archaeological story rather than visible ruins, so it pairs best with background reading or museum context.
