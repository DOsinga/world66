Gespensterwald, the "ghost forest" near Nienhagen, is a short coastal excursion from Rostock with a genuinely strange atmosphere. Salt wind from the Baltic has shaped the beeches into leaning, contorted forms, and the trunks look especially dramatic in mist, low sun, or winter light. It is a simple landscape, but a memorable one.

The forest is best treated as a walk rather than a sight with a single viewpoint. Paths lead through the trees and toward the cliffs and beach, so sturdy shoes help if the weather has been wet. It is a good choice when you want sea air and a break from Rostock's brick churches and harbor streets.
