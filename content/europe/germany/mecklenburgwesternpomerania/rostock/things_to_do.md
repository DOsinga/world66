---
title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Rostock's old town rewards a half-day on foot. The brick Gothic churches are the architectural core — St Marien, St Petri, and the Nikolaikirche each have distinct character. The abbey museum and the zoological collection add depth for those with curiosity to spare. The harbour and the Warnemünde beach district broaden the picture considerably.
