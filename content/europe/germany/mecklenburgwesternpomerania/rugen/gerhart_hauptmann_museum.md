The Gerhart-Hauptmann-Museum occupies the house where the Nobel Prize-winning German writer spent his summers with his wife from 1930 to 1943. The rooms are a compact literary museum, with exhibits on Hauptmann's life, work, and long connection to Hiddensee.

It is a quiet stop rather than a large museum, but it adds useful context to the island's long appeal to artists and writers.
