Freilichtmuseum Schwerin-Mueß shifts Schwerin's story from ducal palaces to rural Mecklenburg. Historic farmhouses, barns, gardens, and village buildings show how people lived around the lakes before the capital's castle became the postcard image.

It sits away from the central sights, so it is best for travellers with a car, bike, or extra time. The reward is a slower, more grounded sense of the region, especially in good weather when the open-air setting feels like a village edge rather than a museum campus.
