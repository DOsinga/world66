Internationales Feuerwehrmuseum Schwerin is a specialist museum devoted to firefighting history. The collection ranges from historic fire engines and hand pumps to uniforms, helmets, breathing equipment, models, and other rescue gear.

It is a niche stop, but a substantial one, especially for families or anyone interested in engineering, emergency services, or old vehicles.
