Schwerin's fire brigade museum is a specialist collection, but a surprisingly rich one: historic engines, hand pumps, helmets, breathing masks, uniforms, and scale models fill the halls. The GDR material gives it a local angle, while the international exhibits broaden it beyond a simple town museum. Guided tours are available by advance booking and are the best way to make sense of the collection.
