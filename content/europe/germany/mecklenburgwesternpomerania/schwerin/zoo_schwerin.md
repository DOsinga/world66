Zoo Schwerin is a 25-hectare zoo south of the city centre, established in 1956 and known for roomy animal areas including an Africa exhibit, a rhino enclosure, and the tropical Humboldthaus. It works best as a relaxed family stop after the palace and lakefront sights.
