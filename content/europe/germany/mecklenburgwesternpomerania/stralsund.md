Stralsund is an old Hanseatic town on Germany's Baltic coast, a UNESCO World Heritage Site dating back more than 750 years. It sits on the Strelasund strait, connected to [Rügen](/europe/germany/mecklenburgwesternpomerania/rugen) — Germany's largest island — by a bridge, making it an ideal base for exploring both the island and the surrounding coastline. It is also the departure point for ferries to [Hiddensee](/europe/germany/mecklenburgwesternpomerania/stralsund/hiddensee_ferry), the beautiful car-free island to the west.

The most beautiful view of the old town is from the [harbour](/europe/germany/mecklenburgwesternpomerania/stralsund/harbour). The skyline of the [Town Hall](/europe/germany/mecklenburgwesternpomerania/stralsund/rathaus) and the red-brick Gothic churches — [St. Nikolai](/europe/germany/mecklenburgwesternpomerania/stralsund/st_nikolai), [St. Marien](/europe/germany/mecklenburgwesternpomerania/stralsund/st_marien) — is striking, and the harbour is still the best starting point for a walk through the [Alter Markt](/europe/germany/mecklenburgwesternpomerania/stralsund/alter_markt) and the old town. The [Ozeaneum](/europe/germany/mecklenburgwesternpomerania/stralsund/ozeaneum), a modern aquarium on the harbour, is one of the best in northern Germany. The [German Maritime Museum](/europe/germany/mecklenburgwesternpomerania/stralsund/naval_museum), housed in a beautiful Gothic monastery, is the most visited museum in the state.
