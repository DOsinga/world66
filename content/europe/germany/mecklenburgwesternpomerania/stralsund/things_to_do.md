title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Stralsund's Old Town is an island, almost entirely surrounded by water — the Strelasund to the north and east, three lakes to the south and west. The Hanseatic architecture is exceptional: the Town Hall (Rathaus) with its Gothic tracery facade and the brick churches of St. Nikolai, St. Marien, and St. Jakobi rank among the finest in northern Germany.

The [Ozeaneum](/europe/germany/mecklenburgwesternpomerania/stralsund/ozeaneum) on the harbour is the headline modern attraction — a striking aquarium building with tanks housing North Sea and Baltic Sea ecosystems including enormous shark tanks. The [German Museum of Military History — Naval Section](/europe/germany/mecklenburgwesternpomerania/stralsund/naval_museum) occupies a former historic shipyard and is also worth a visit.

The Altstadt (Old Town) is compact enough to explore thoroughly on foot in a day. From the harbour, ferries and excursion boats head to the islands of Rügen and Hiddensee.
