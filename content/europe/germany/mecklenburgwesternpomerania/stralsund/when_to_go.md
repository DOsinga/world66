---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

The best time to visit Stralsund is from May to September. Summer brings warm Baltic weather (averaging 20°C) and the harbour, beaches, and outdoor spaces are at their best. July and August are the peak months — the town is busier but not overwhelmingly crowded, since most visitors are heading on to Rügen.

June and September are arguably better: fewer people, the same warm weather, and the Baltic light in late summer is extraordinary — long evenings, low sun, clear skies. The Christmas market on the Alter Markt in December is charming and less touristy than those in larger German cities. Winter is quiet and can be grey, but the old town architecture is beautiful in snow.
