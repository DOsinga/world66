---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

Summer — June through August — is the obvious time to visit Warnemünde. The beach is at its best, every restaurant and stall is open, and the town buzzes with both day-trippers from Rostock and cruise-ship passengers. Beach basket (Strandkorb) rentals are available from around May and run through September.

Warnemünde can feel overwhelmingly crowded on days when a large cruise ship is in port, which can bring several thousand visitors at once to a relatively small resort. If you want the beach without the crowds, aim for a weekday in early June or late September, when the weather is still pleasant but the summer rush has eased.

The winter months are quiet, atmospheric, and cold — North Sea winds hit this coast hard. Some fish restaurants along the Alter Strom stay open year-round, and there is a relaxed, local feel to the town out of season.
