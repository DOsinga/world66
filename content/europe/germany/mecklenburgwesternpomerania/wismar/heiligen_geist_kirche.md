Heiligen-Geist-Kirche is a Gothic church in Wismar's old town, with its present form dating mainly from the 15th century. Inside, the main draw is a painted wooden ceiling from 1687 showing scenes from the Old Testament.

It is a worthwhile stop for visitors tracing Wismar's brick Gothic churches beyond the larger landmarks of St. George's and St. Nicholas.
