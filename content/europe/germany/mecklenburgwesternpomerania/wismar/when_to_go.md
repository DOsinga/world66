---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

Wismar is best visited between May and September, when the Baltic coast is at its most hospitable. Summer brings long daylight hours — at this latitude the sky stays light until well past nine o'clock — and the Marktplatz outdoor seating fills with locals and visitors. July and August are the warmest months, with temperatures typically reaching the mid-twenties Celsius, but the town never becomes overwhelmingly crowded in the way that Lübeck or Rostock do.

Spring and early autumn are good alternatives: fewer visitors, lower prices, and a certain quiet quality to the brick churches and harbour that suits the place. Winter is cold and grey, with North Sea weather sweeping in off the Baltic, but Wismar's Christmas market on the Marktplatz is genuinely pleasant if you're dressed for it. The town's UNESCO status and the quality of the preserved buildings make it worth a look in any season — the churches are open year-round and the Marktplatz is always there.
