---
title: "Bars and Cafes"
type: section
---

Aachen's bar scene is shaped by its large student population from RWTH Aachen University, one of Germany's top technical universities, and its proximity to Belgium — Belgian beers are everywhere. The **Pontstrasse** is the main evening street, with a string of bars running north from the old town. The cafes around Am Markt and Münsterplatz are better for daytime coffee and cake.
