---
title: "Eating Out"
type: section
---

Aachen's restaurant scene reflects its position at the edge of three countries: German, Belgian, and Dutch culinary influences mix on most menus. The city's own contribution is *Printen* (gingerbread) and *Reissweck* (a bread roll eaten at New Year), but for full meals you are in the Rhineland tradition — hearty, meat-forward, and good value. The student population keeps prices reasonable and the area around the Pontstrasse has the densest concentration of options.
