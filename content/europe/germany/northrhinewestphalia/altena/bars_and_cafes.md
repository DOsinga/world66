---
title: Bars and Cafes
type: section
---

Altena's cafe and bar scene is modest and geared toward locals rather than tourists. The town centre has a few cafes suitable for coffee and cake in the afternoon, and several straightforward bars where the steelworking heritage of the region still feels close to the surface. Don't expect late nights or craft cocktails — this is a small Sauerland town that winds down early.
