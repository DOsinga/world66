A straightforward town-centre cafe on the market square, good for coffee and a slice of Kuchen in the afternoon. The interior is the sort of comfortable, unfussy place that serves the local population rather than tourists, which keeps the prices reasonable and the atmosphere unhurried. In good weather the tables outside give a view of the small market square and the valley town around it. A useful stop before or after a day at the castle.
