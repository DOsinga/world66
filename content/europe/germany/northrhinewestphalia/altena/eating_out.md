---
title: Eating Out
type: section
---

Altena is a small working town rather than a culinary destination, but there are solid options for a meal. The castle area has a restaurant with views over the valley, and the town centre has the kind of reliable German and international restaurants you would expect in a Sauerland market town. Local specialities lean toward hearty Westphalian cooking — pork, potatoes, rye bread — rather than anything exotic.
