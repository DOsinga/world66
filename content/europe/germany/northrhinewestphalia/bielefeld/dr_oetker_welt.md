Dr. Oetker is one of Bielefeld's best-known companies, and its visitor centre turns the brand's history into a polished tour. It is more corporate experience than museum, but it is also very local: baking powder, pudding, frozen pizza, and a family firm that grew into a household name.

Visits usually need advance booking. It is worth considering if you like everyday industrial history or are travelling with children.
