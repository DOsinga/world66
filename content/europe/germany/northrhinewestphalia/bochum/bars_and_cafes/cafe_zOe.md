Café Zoe is one of the better all-day venues in the Bermuda3Eck: good coffee and breakfast in the morning, a reasonable lunch menu, and a shifting crowd of students and young professionals in the evening when it quietly becomes a bar. The interior is simple and unfussy — wooden tables, decent light — and the service is friendly without being overly attentive.

It is a reliable choice if you want somewhere to sit with a coffee and read, then have a drink later without moving. The outdoor tables on the street are pleasant when the weather allows.
