Bochum Dahlhausen Railway Museum occupies a former railway depot, which is exactly the right setting for locomotives, carriages, turntables, and the heavy machinery of German rail history. It is one of the Ruhr's better specialist museums.

The site is strongest when historic trains or demonstrations are running, but even a standard visit gives a good sense of scale. In a region shaped by coal, steel, and movement, the museum fits naturally into the Ruhr's industrial story.
