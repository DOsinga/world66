A reliable restaurant in the heart of the Bermuda3Eck with a Franco-German menu built around properly sourced meat. The rotisserie chicken is the best thing on the menu, but the daily specials — usually something with seasonal vegetables — give it more range than the name suggests. Service is efficient without being hurried, and the room is pleasant enough to linger in.

Prices are fair for the quality, and you can book ahead for weekend evenings, which is advisable. Good list of Rheinland and French wines by the glass.
