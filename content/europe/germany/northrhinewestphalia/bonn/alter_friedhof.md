Bonn's old cemetery, established in 1715, is one of the most beautiful in the Rhineland — a park-like expanse of ancient trees, ivy-covered chapels, and ornate 19th-century monuments. It lies just west of the city centre between the university quarter and the Altstadt.

The Alter Friedhof is genuinely worth a visit for its atmosphere. Shade trees planted over two centuries ago form a canopy over the graves, and in spring the ground cover of ivy and periwinkle blooms blue beneath them. The cemetery is a popular lunch spot for university staff and local residents. Entry is free and it is open daily during daylight hours.
