Bonn's Jewish Cemetery is a quiet historical layer in a city better known for Beethoven, government, and university life. The gravestones and enclosed setting make the city's Jewish past visible in a direct, unshowy way.

Visit with the same respect you would bring to any active or historic burial ground. It is a short stop, but for travellers interested in local memory and religious history it adds depth to a Bonn walk.
