The Basilica of St. Severin is one of Cologne’s important Romanesque churches, standing in the Severinsviertel south of the main tourist core. It does not compete with the cathedral for spectacle, but it gives a richer picture of Cologne’s older parish and pilgrimage landscape.

The church is worth seeking out if you are tracing the city’s Romanesque architecture or exploring beyond the most crowded streets. Its neighbourhood setting makes the visit feel more local, and the building carries centuries of rebuilding, devotion, and urban change.
