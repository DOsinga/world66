Museum für Angewandte Kunst is Cologne's applied arts and design museum, covering decorative arts, furniture, objects, and changing exhibitions. Its design focus makes it a useful contrast to the city's Roman, religious, and fine-art museums.

Some historical collections may be limited during refurbishment, so check the current programme before going. The museum is central enough to combine with the cathedral and nearby galleries.
