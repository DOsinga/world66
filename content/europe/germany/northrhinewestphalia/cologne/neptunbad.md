Neptunbad is a restored historic bathhouse in Ehrenfeld, now used as a sauna and wellness complex. The old bathing halls have been paired with a Japanese-style sauna area, making it a more atmospheric stop than a standard gym spa.

It is mainly useful for travellers who want a relaxed indoor break, especially in colder weather or after several days of walking Cologne.
