Phantasialand sits in Bruehl, south of Cologne, and is one of Germany's heavyweight theme parks. It packs roller coasters, themed lands, water rides, shows, and elaborate scenery into a surprisingly compact footprint, so the day feels dense rather than sprawling.

It is not Cologne culture in the cathedral-and-museum sense, but it is absolutely travel-worthy if you like theme parks or are travelling with children. Start early, book ahead in busy periods, and treat it as a full-day outing rather than something to squeeze between city sights.
