The ruins of Cologne's Roman city walls and towers are among the easiest ways to see the city's ancient origins in the modern street plan. Cologne was a major Roman colony, and fragments of its fortifications still punctuate the centre.

These remains work best as a walking-route stop between larger sights. They are not as spectacular as the cathedral or the Roman-Germanic Museum collections, but they make the Roman city tangible outdoors, where the old wall line still interrupts the contemporary city.
