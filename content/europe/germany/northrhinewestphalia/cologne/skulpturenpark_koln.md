Skulpturenpark Köln is an outdoor sculpture park north of central Cologne, showing contemporary works in a landscaped setting near the Rhine. The open-air format makes it a low-pressure way to encounter large-scale sculpture without committing to a full museum visit.

Because installations change over time, the park rewards repeat visits and a little curiosity. It pairs well with a walk along the river or a visit to Cologne's northern museum and garden areas.
