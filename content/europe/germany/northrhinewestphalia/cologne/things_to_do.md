---
title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Cologne's greatest concentration of sights sits within walking distance of the cathedral — the museum cluster between the Dom and the Rhine is genuinely one of the best in Germany, and the old town around Alter Markt rewards wandering. Beyond the historic core, Cologne is a city of Kölsch beer halls, a buzzing arts scene, and a Carnival tradition that transforms the city each February into one of Europe's great street parties.
