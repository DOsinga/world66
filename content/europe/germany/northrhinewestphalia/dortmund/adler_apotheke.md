  The Adler Apotheke was first documented in 1322, making it the oldest continuously operating

The Adler Apotheke (Eagle Pharmacy) sits on the Ostenhellweg, the pedestrian street running through the historic centre, and has been dispensing medicines since the fourteenth century. The current neo-Gothic facade is a handsome piece of nineteenth-century commercial architecture, and the interior, still operating as a working pharmacy, retains period fittings and display cases.

It is not a museum, and you cannot simply wander inside for a look — but the exterior and the history are worth noting as you pass along the Hellweg. The pharmacy publishes a brief history of the building that is available inside. For anyone interested in the layers of the city that survived postwar rebuilding, this small continuity is significant: almost nothing else in central Dortmund predates 1945.
