Liebfrauenkirche is an old Dortmund church now used as a quiet columbarium, with urns kept in a respectful public setting. That unusual role gives the building a different atmosphere from the city's busier churches.

Visit briefly and quietly if you are exploring the Kreuzviertel area. The appeal is the calm interior and the way the church has been adapted for remembrance.
