Dortmund's Museum of Natural History is the city's main stop for geology, fossils, animals, and the natural history of the Ruhr region. It gives useful context to a city better known for football and industrial culture, especially through exhibits on local landscapes and the deep time beneath them.

The museum works well for families and for travellers who want an indoor counterpoint to Dortmund's urban sights. It is not as central as the art and culture museums, but the northern location is straightforward to reach and pairs well with a slower day away from the shopping streets.
