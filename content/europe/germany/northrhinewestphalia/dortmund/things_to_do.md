Dortmund's attractions split into two broad categories: the industrial heritage and the football culture. The [Dortmunder U](#dortmunder_u) towers over the city as a symbol of both — a former brewery transformed into an arts centre, its giant golden U still lit up at night. The colliery monuments, the churches, and the memorial museums fill in a picture of a city shaped by coal, steel, war, and an unlikely reinvention.
