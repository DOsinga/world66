DITIB Merkez Mosque is one of Germany's largest mosques, completed in 2008 in a traditional Ottoman style. Beyond the prayer hall, the complex includes spaces for cultural exchange and a library and archive of Islamic documents, giving it a broader role than a neighbourhood mosque.
