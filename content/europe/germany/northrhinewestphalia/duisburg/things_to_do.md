---
title: Things to Do
type: section
---

Duisburg's greatest attraction is Landschaftspark Duisburg-Nord, the former ironworks turned public park that is both jaw-dropping and freely accessible. The Inner Harbour (Innenhafen) provides a second focus: redeveloped waterfront, museums, and restaurants clustered around historic mill and silo buildings. For a Ruhr city, Duisburg punches above its weight for things to do.
