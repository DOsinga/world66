The Jan Wellem Monument stands in front of Düsseldorf's city hall, showing Elector Johann Wilhelm II on horseback. Cast by Gabriel Grupello in 1711, it is one of the city's best pieces of Baroque public art and still anchors the old-town square where Düsseldorf tells its courtly history.
