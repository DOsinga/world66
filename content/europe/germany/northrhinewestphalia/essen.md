Essen was the steel and coal capital of the Ruhr for well over a century, a city built on industry and reshaped by its decline. Today it is one of Germany's more interesting post-industrial cities — [Zollverein](/europe/germany/northrhinewestphalia/essen/zollverein), a UNESCO World Heritage Site, is the most striking example of how the region has repurposed its industrial heritage into culture and tourism. The Bauhaus-style former coal mine now holds the [Ruhr Museum](/europe/germany/northrhinewestphalia/essen/ruhr_museum), a Red Dot Design Museum, and a summer swimming pool, along with Richard Serra's monumental [Slab for the Ruhr](/europe/germany/northrhinewestphalia/essen/slab_for_the_ruhr) atop the adjacent slag heap.

The [Museum Folkwang](/europe/germany/northrhinewestphalia/essen/museum_folkwang) holds one of Germany's finest collections of nineteenth and twentieth-century European art. [Villa Hügel](/europe/germany/northrhinewestphalia/essen/villa_hugel) — the former home of the Krupp dynasty — sits above the [Baldeneysee](/europe/germany/northrhinewestphalia/essen/baldeneysee) in southern Essen, offering a compelling if unsettling window into German industrial history. The medieval [Essen Minster](/europe/germany/northrhinewestphalia/essen/essen_minster) houses the remarkable Cathedral Treasury including the Golden Madonna from around 980 AD — the oldest surviving sculpture of Mary in the Western world. The [Old Synagogue](/europe/germany/northrhinewestphalia/essen/old_synagogue), one of the largest surviving synagogue buildings in Germany, holds a thoughtful memorial and museum on Essen's Jewish history. The [Grugapark](/europe/germany/northrhinewestphalia/essen/grugapark) provides green escape from the dense urban fabric, and the [Aalto Theatre](/europe/germany/northrhinewestphalia/essen/aalto_theatre) — Alvar Aalto's delayed masterpiece — is one of the finest opera houses in western Germany.

Essen is a useful base for exploring the Ruhr, with good transport links to [Dortmund](/europe/germany/northrhinewestphalia/dortmund) and Düsseldorf.
