Hamm's two headline attractions are genuinely unusual. The [Sri Kamadchi Ampal Temple](/europe/germany/northrhinewestphalia/hamm/sri_kamadchi_ampal_temple) is the largest Hindu temple in Europe, its colourful gopuram tower rising incongruously against a North Rhine-Westphalian sky. The [Glaselefant](/europe/germany/northrhinewestphalia/hamm/glaselefant) — a massive glass elephant housing an elevator and restaurant — sits in the [Maximilianpark](/europe/germany/northrhinewestphalia/hamm/maximilianpark), a landscaped former coal mine that is one of the better examples of Ruhr industrial land conversion.

For those interested in the region's industrial history, Hamm was once dominated by coal mining, and the city's transition tells a story common to the whole Ruhr area. The [Gustav Lübcke Museum](/europe/germany/northrhinewestphalia/hamm/gustav_lubcke_museum) covers regional archaeology and history.
