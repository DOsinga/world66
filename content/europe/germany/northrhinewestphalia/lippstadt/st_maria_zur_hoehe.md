St. Maria zur Höhe, also known as the Hohnekirche, dates from around 1220 and is known for its ceiling and wall paintings.

The church is a compact but worthwhile stop in Lippstadt, especially for visitors interested in medieval art and the Byzantine influence visible in its painted ceiling.
