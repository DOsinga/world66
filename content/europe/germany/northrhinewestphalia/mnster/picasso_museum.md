Münster's Picasso Museum holds one of the largest collections of Picasso's graphic work in Germany — prints, lithographs, and drawings spanning his entire career. The focus on graphic art rather than paintings means it can offer depth rather than a greatest-hits selection: the collection shows how Picasso worked through ideas, returned to themes, and experimented with technique across decades.

The museum is housed in a building near the university quarter and is quiet by the standards of major Picasso institutions — you can stand in front of the works without being jostled. The permanent collection is supplemented by temporary exhibitions of 20th-century prints and graphic art. Closed Mondays.
