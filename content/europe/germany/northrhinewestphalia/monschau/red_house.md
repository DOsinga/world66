The Red House was built around 1760 for Johann Heinrich Scheibler, one of Monschau's great cloth merchants. Inside, the rooms still show the confidence of that trade: Rococo and Empire furnishings, textile displays, and a famous carved staircase that turns woollen cloth into a story in wood.
