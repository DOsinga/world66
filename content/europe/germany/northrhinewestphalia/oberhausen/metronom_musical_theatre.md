Metronom Theater is one of the anchors of Oberhausen's Neue Mitte, the leisure district around CentrO and the Gasometer. Its large-scale musical productions give the city an evening attraction that is very different from industrial heritage sightseeing.

The theatre is worth listing because Oberhausen visitors often combine shopping, exhibitions, and entertainment rather than following a classic old-town itinerary. Check the current production before going, since the venue's appeal depends entirely on what is on stage.
