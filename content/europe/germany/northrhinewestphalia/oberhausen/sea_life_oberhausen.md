SEA LIFE Oberhausen is a polished aquarium attraction in the CentrO leisure district, the same broad area that draws visitors for shopping, restaurants, and family entertainment. Its tanks and walk-through displays make it an easy choice when travelling with children or when the Ruhr weather turns grey.

It is not the Ruhr's most distinctive cultural sight, but it clears the threshold as a practical, well-located family stop. Pair it with the Gasometer or other Neue Mitte attractions if you want a day in Oberhausen that mixes industrial heritage with lighter entertainment.
