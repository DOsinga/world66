The Glas Haus is a small art gallery housed in what was formerly a botanical glasshouse — a combination that sounds gimmicky but works well in practice. Natural light floods the space through the iron-and-glass structure, and it lends even modest exhibitions an unusual warmth.

The gallery shows local and regional artists, with a focus on contemporary painting and sculpture. It is not a major institution and the quality varies, but the setting is charming and it is the kind of place that rewards an unscheduled half-hour when you find yourself nearby.
