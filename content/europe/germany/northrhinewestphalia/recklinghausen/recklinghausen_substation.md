The Recklinghausen substation turns electrical infrastructure into industrial heritage, using a former transformer and switching station to explain power generation, grids, and the technology that supported the Ruhr's modern life. It is more specialized than a general city museum.

The museum is best for visitors interested in engineering, industrial landscapes, or Ruhr history. It pairs naturally with other regional industrial sites, where coal, steel, and electricity form a shared story.
