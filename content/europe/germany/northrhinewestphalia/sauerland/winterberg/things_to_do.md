title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Winterberg runs on two seasons: ski season from roughly December to March, and a busy summer season built around mountain biking and hiking. The ski area is the largest in western Germany, and for skiers based in the Ruhr Valley or Cologne, it's the most accessible mountain resort in the country. In summer, the trails that connect the lifts become mountain bike routes, and the Winterberg Bobrun operates year-round.
