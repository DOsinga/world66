---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

Winter (December to March) is the obvious peak season, when the ski area is in operation and the town fills with weekenders from the Rhine and Ruhr cities. Book accommodation well in advance for weekends in January and February; midweek is much quieter.

Summer (June to September) is the second season, driven by mountain biking and hiking. The trails are well-maintained, the Bobbahn runs year-round, and the countryside is green and pleasant. Autumn is arguably the most beautiful time — the beech forests on the Rothaarsteig turn gold in October — but services thin out.

Avoid the shoulder weeks of late November and early April unless you're happy with mud underfoot and no snow on the runs.
