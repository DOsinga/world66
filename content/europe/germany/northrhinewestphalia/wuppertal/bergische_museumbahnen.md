Bergische Museumbahnen preserves historic trams and operates a heritage line through the wooded Kohlfurth area. In a city famous for the Schwebebahn, it adds another layer to Wuppertal's transport story.

It is a strong niche stop for railway and tram enthusiasts, and a pleasant family outing when services are running. Check operating days carefully, since heritage transport is often seasonal or weekend-only.
