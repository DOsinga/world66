---
title: Things to Do
type: section
---

Wuppertal's main draw is its famous [Schwebebahn](schwebebahn) suspended railway, but the city has more to offer than its iconic transport. The [Von der Heydt Museum](von_der_heydt_museum) is one of the better art museums in the Ruhr region, the [Botanischer Garten](botanischer_garten) offers a green escape above the city, and the [Engels-Haus](engels_haus) provides a surprising connection to global history. For something unusual, the [Lego Bridge](lego_bridge) near the main station is a small piece of street art that has outlasted its initial moment of fame.
