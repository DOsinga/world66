---
title: When to Go
type: section
---

Wuppertal is a year-round city with no strong seasonal pull for visitors. Summers are warm and pleasant, which is when the Botanischer Garten is at its best. Spring brings green to the steep hillsides around the valley, and the combination of light and architecture makes walking the city more enjoyable.

Winter is grey and wet, as it is across most of the Ruhr region, but the Schwebebahn runs whatever the weather and the museums and gallery are good reasons to visit in the colder months. There is nothing particularly special about Christmas in Wuppertal compared to Cologne or Düsseldorf, which both have more elaborate Christmas markets if that is the reason for a winter trip.
