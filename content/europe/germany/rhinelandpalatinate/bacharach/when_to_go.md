---
title: When to Go
type: section
---

**Summer (June–August)** is peak season on the Rhine. Rhine cruise ships dock at Bacharach and day-trippers fill the Marktplatz through the middle hours of the day. The town is still worth visiting in summer, but if you want to experience it at its best, stay overnight — by evening the cruise passengers have gone and the place settles back into its own character.

**Spring (April–May)** is the best time to visit. The vineyards are green and the crowds are lighter. The weather is mild and the light on the Rhine is excellent.

**Autumn (September–October)** brings the grape harvest. Riesling grapes are picked late — often well into October — and the harvest season has a festive atmosphere. Wine festivals take place in towns throughout the Mittelrhein in September and October.

**Winter (November–March)** is quiet. Many restaurants and wine taverns reduce hours or close. The Christmas market in December is small but atmospheric against the half-timbered backdrop. The Rhine in winter mist has its own appeal if you don't need sunshine.
