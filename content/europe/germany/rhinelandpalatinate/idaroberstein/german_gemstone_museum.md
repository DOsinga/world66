The German Gemstone Museum explains why this small town became an international name in stones. The collection focuses on gems as craft objects: cut, polished, set, traded, and turned into jewellery.

It is the better first museum if you are interested in the human side of the trade. You see the result of the cutters' work before heading to the mines or the mineral museum, where the raw material takes over.
