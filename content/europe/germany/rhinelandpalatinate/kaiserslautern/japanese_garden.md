Kaiserslautern's Japanese Garden is one of the largest Japanese gardens in Europe, laid out with ponds, bridges, stone lanterns, tea-house views, and carefully planted paths. It is a quiet stop close to the city centre and works well as a break from the busier historic sights nearby.
