Kaiserslautern's Japanese Garden is one of the largest in Europe, laid out with ponds, bridges, stone lanterns, and carefully framed planting. It is a quiet counterpoint to the city centre rather than a quick photo stop, and it works best when you slow down and follow the paths around the water.
