The most photographed spot in the Middle Rhine: the sharp triangular headland where the green Moselle pours into the larger Rhine. The point itself is a vast cobbled platform with a 14-metre equestrian statue of Wilhelm I at the apex, all flagpoles and German tricolours, and it is the natural starting point for any walk in Koblenz.

Climb the plinth for the view across both rivers and up to Ehrenbreitstein. The promenades along both rivers extend in either direction — left along the Moselle to the medieval Baldwin Bridge, right along the Rhine to the Electoral Palace.
