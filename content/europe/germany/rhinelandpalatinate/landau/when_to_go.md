---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

Spring and autumn are the best times to visit. The wine harvest runs from late September to late October and is the most atmospheric time to be in the Palatinate — estates are busy with picking and pressing, Straußwirtschaften open their doors, and the landscape turns gold. May and June bring wildflowers to the Kleine Kalmit and the first outdoor sitting on the Rathausplatz.

Summer is pleasant and warm — the Palatinate is one of Germany's warmest regions — with long evenings good for outdoor dining and cycling. Winter is quiet but the Christmas markets are modest and the Straußwirtschaften mostly close. The Palatinate often stays milder than the rest of Germany in autumn and early winter.
