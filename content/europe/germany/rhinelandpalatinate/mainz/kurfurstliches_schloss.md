The Electoral Palace is one of Mainz's most prominent secular landmarks, standing near the Rhine as a reminder of the city's years under powerful prince-electors. Its red sandstone facade and long river-facing profile make it easy to recognize, even if you only see it from the outside.

The building is best appreciated as part of a walk linking the Rhine promenade, old town, cathedral, and Roman-Germanic Museum area. It adds political and courtly context to a city that visitors often frame only through Roman remains, Gutenberg, and church architecture.
