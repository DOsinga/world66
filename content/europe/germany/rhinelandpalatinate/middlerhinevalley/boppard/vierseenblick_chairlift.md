The Sesselbahn climbs from the west edge of town into the woods and vineyards above the Bopparder Hamm. At the top, the Vierseenblick viewpoint turns the Rhine's huge curve into an optical trick: the river seems to break into four separate lakes. It is the classic Boppard view and the quickest way to understand why this bend in the valley matters.
