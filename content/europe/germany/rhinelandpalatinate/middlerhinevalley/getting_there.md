---
title: "Getting There"
type: section
---

The Middle Rhine Valley has excellent rail access. The left-bank Koblenz-Mainz line (served by regional trains) stops at Boppard, Boppard Hbf, St. Goar, Oberwesel, and Bacharach. The right-bank line serves St. Goarshausen and the towns opposite. Trains run roughly every 30 minutes in each direction. [Koblenz](/europe/germany/rhinelandpalatinate/koblenz) at the northern end and [Mainz](/europe/germany/rhinelandpalatinate/mainz) at the southern end are the main connections to the national network, including high-speed trains from Frankfurt (30 minutes to Mainz).

By car, the B9 runs along the left bank and the B42 along the right bank. Neither road is fast — the valley is narrow and the towns are frequent — which suits the pace of exploring the valley.

Frankfurt am Main airport is the practical entry point: Mainz is 30 minutes by regional train, Koblenz about 75 minutes.
