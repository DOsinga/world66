The cycle path through the Rhine Gorge runs along both banks and is one of the finest riverside cycling routes in Europe. The left-bank path (the B9 and dedicated cycle lane) is flatter and more direct; the right-bank route (Rheinsteig cycle path) is slightly more undulating but often quieter. Either way, the 65-kilometre section between Bingen and Koblenz can be done comfortably in a day at a relaxed pace.

Bicycles can be taken on the regional trains that serve the valley, making it easy to cycle one direction and return by train. Several towns including Boppard and Bacharach have bike rental points. The cycle path passes through or alongside every significant town in the valley — Bacharach, Oberwesel, St. Goar, Boppard — and gives the best low-level views of the castles on the opposing banks. Combined with the river ferry crossings, you can make a flexible loop through the valley over two days.
