The Mittelrhein wine region covers the Middle Rhine Valley and produces Riesling of a distinctive character — high acidity, mineral notes from the slate soils, often with a floral freshness. It is not one of Germany's most famous wine regions (the Mosel and Rhine Hessen dominate export markets) but the wines are excellent and underpriced relative to their quality.

Tastings and direct sales from producers are common in the valley towns. Bacharach, Oberwesel, and the villages of the Bopparder Hamm all have local wineries. The harvest season in September and October brings wine festivals to almost every village; the Bacharacher Weinmarkt and the Bopparder Weinwoche are the largest. Buying directly from a producer and drinking a glass of fresh Riesling while watching the Rhine traffic is one of the simpler pleasures of travelling in the valley.
