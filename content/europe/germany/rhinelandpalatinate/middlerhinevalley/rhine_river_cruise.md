Taking a river boat through the gorge remains one of the best ways to appreciate the Middle Rhine Valley. The Köln-Düsseldorfer shipping company runs regular scheduled passenger services from Cologne to Mainz from April through October; you can board and disembark at any of the river towns. The section between Bingen and Koblenz — the 65-kilometre heart of the valley — takes about 3–4 hours downstream and 5–6 hours upstream.

From the water, the castle-to-town ratio is immediately apparent: barely a hillside lacks a ruin, and the rhythm of vine-covered slope, medieval village, looming fortress, and open river repeats itself with almost theatrical precision. The narrowest passage, where the Lorelei rock constricts the channel, is the most dramatic section. Day-trippers can take the boat one way between Bacharach and Koblenz and return by the frequent rail service.
