---
title: "Eating Out"
type: section
---

Cochem's restaurant scene is focused almost entirely on the Mosel riverfront and the old town lanes radiating from the Marktplatz. The local specialities are straightforward: freshwater fish from the river, local Riesling, and the hearty Moselle dishes — Saumagen (stuffed pig's stomach), smoked meats, and game from the surrounding forests in autumn.
