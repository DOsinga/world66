---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

Cochem is at its most photogenic in spring and autumn. May and June bring flowering vines on the slopes and reliable warm weather without the crush of high summer. September and October are the best months for wine enthusiasts — the harvest is under way, the local Weinfeste open their cellar doors, and the valley takes on amber and gold as the leaves turn.

July and August are the busiest months and the town can feel packed on weekends, though the river and the ridge walks above give you space. December brings an Advent market to the Marktplatz. January through March is quiet to the point of shutdown — many restaurants and wine bars close entirely, and the castle reduces its opening hours. If you come in winter, call ahead.
