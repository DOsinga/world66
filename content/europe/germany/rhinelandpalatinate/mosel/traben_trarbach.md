*Europe > Germany > Rhineland-Palatinate > Mosel > Traben-Trarbach*

Traben-Trarbach sits on both banks of the Mosel, joined by a bridge that feels like the town's front door. It is not as instantly famous as Cochem or Bernkastel-Kues, but it has one of the valley's most distinctive profiles: wine warehouses, Jugendstil villas, a bridge gate, and old cellars dug into the hillside.

The town's odd wealth came from wine. Around 1900, Traben-Trarbach was one of the great wine-trading towns of the Mosel, shipping Riesling across Europe and building in the confident Art Nouveau style of the period. [Brückentor](/europe/germany/rhinelandpalatinate/mosel/traben_trarbach/bruckentor) is the obvious symbol, while the [Mittelmosel Museum](/europe/germany/rhinelandpalatinate/mosel/traben_trarbach/mittelmosel_museum) and riverside villas fill in the quieter details.

There is enough for a full spare day without pretending this is a major city. Walk up to [Grevenburg Castle Ruins](/europe/germany/rhinelandpalatinate/mosel/traben_trarbach/grevenburg_castle_ruins), visit the wine cellars or the [Traben-Trarbach Underworld](/europe/germany/rhinelandpalatinate/mosel/traben_trarbach/traben_trarbach_underworld), and leave time for a slow glass of Riesling by the river. It belongs inside the [Mosel](/europe/germany/rhinelandpalatinate/mosel) page, but it is strong enough to stand as more than a generic wine village.
