The Mittelmosel Museum occupies a handsome old riverside house and gives useful context for the town's wine-trading years. It is the sort of small local museum that works best when you already have the streets outside in your head.

Displays cover Mosel domestic life, wine culture, and the people who passed through Traben-Trarbach. The building itself is part of the appeal: old rooms, river views, and a sense of how comfortably the wine merchants lived.
