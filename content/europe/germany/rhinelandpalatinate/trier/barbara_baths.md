The Barbara Baths are among Trier's major Roman remains, even though they require more imagination than the amphitheatre or Porta Nigra. The surviving foundations mark what was once one of the largest bath complexes in the Roman world north of the Alps.

Walkways and interpretive panels help turn the low stonework into a readable plan of pools, heated rooms, service spaces, and social architecture. Visit them with the Roman Bridge and Imperial Baths in mind, and Trier's scale as a Roman city becomes much clearer.
