Trier’s Old Crane stands on the Moselle as a compact reminder that the city was not only Roman walls and churches, but also a working river port. The stone crane house and timber machinery belong to the age when wine, stone, and goods moved through Trier by boat.

It is a quick stop on a riverside walk, especially if you are already heading toward the Roman Bridge. The mechanism is the point: you can still read how muscle power, wheels, and clever gearing turned the riverbank into infrastructure.
