Trier's Old Jewish Cemetery is a quiet, enclosed place where the city's Jewish history becomes physical. The gravestones are weathered and irregular, and the site feels very different from the grand Roman and ecclesiastical monuments that dominate most Trier itineraries.

It is worth seeking out because it adds a more intimate layer to the city: families, merchants, rabbis, and generations of a community that lived here for centuries. Visit respectfully, and treat it as a historical site rather than a shortcut between bigger attractions.
