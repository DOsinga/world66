St. Matthias' Abbey stands south of Trier's old center and has been a pilgrimage focus for centuries. Its Romanesque church is traditionally associated with the tomb of the Apostle Matthias, making it one of the city's most distinctive religious sites beyond the famous Roman monuments.

The abbey rewards a slower visit: the architecture is restrained, the crypt and pilgrimage associations give it weight, and the walk out from the center shows a quieter side of Trier. It fits best for travelers interested in church history or medieval layers of the city.
