The municipal museum, housed in the surviving 12th-century cloister of the Andreasstift — itself
worth a visit for the Romanesque architecture. Collections cover the Roman settlement of
Borbetomagus (bronze military fittings, glass, mosaics), the medieval imperial city, the
Reformation, and the long Worms wine trade.

The Roman section is particularly strong: Worms was a significant Rhine garrison and the
artefacts on display reflect that. Allow 90 minutes; combine with the adjacent Magnuskirche for a
satisfying southern circuit of the old town.
