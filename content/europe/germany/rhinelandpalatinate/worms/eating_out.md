---
title: "Eating Out"
type: section
---

Worms has solid Pfälzer and modern German cooking in the streets immediately around the cathedral
and the Marktplatz. The cooking leans into the local — Saumagen, Spundekäs, fresh white asparagus
in spring — and the wine lists are good without exception (this is the southern edge of
Rheinhessen, the country's largest wine region by acreage). The Stadtmauer and Kämmerer-Strasse
have the most concentration of decent places.
