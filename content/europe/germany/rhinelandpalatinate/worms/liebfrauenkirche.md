A graceful twin-spired Gothic church standing alone in the middle of vineyards north of the city
centre. Begun in 1276, finished in the 15th century, the church is the spiritual home of one of
the most famous wine names in the world.

The vineyard immediately behind the church — Liebfrauenstift-Kirchenstück — is the original
"Liebfraumilch" plot. The church itself is a quiet, atmospheric stop; a small chapel inside holds
a 15th-century devotional Madonna. Combine with a vineyard walk and a tasting at one of the
Worms wine estates.
