A vast Reformation tableau in bronze and stone, set in a small park north of the cathedral. Martin
Luther stands central, surrounded by his pre-Reformation predecessors (Wycliffe, Hus, Savonarola,
Waldo), the German princes who protected him, and seated female figures representing the great
Protestant cities of Speyer, Augsburg, and Magdeburg.

Inaugurated in 1868 in a wave of pan-German Protestant nationalism. The monument is the largest of
its kind in the world; whatever you think of the cause, the sculptural quality is high. The
inscription around the base — "Hier stehe ich, ich kann nicht anders" — repeats Luther's famous line
from the Diet of Worms.
