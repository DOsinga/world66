---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

Late spring (May–June) and early autumn (September) are the easiest visits — warm days, cool
evenings, and the Rheinhessen vineyards either flowering or harvesting. The biggest event of the
year is the **Nibelungen Festival** in late July and August, an open-air theatre cycle staged on
a giant temporary stage in front of the cathedral. Performances are in German with no surtitles
but the spectacle alone is worth seeing.

The **Backfischfest** in late August/early September is the local wine and fish festival —
twelve days of fairground rides, drinking tents, and parades along the Rhine. Winter is grey,
quiet, and damp but the museums are at their least crowded.
