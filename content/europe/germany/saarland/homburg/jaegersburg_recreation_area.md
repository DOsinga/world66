Jaegersburg is Homburg's easy green escape, set around ponds and woodland north of the centre. The area has walking paths, places to sit by the water, summer pedal boats, and traces of the old Gustavsburg setting.

It is not the main reason to travel to Homburg, but it is a pleasant way to finish the day if you have already seen the caves and Roman museum.
