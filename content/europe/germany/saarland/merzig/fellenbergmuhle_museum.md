The Fellenbergmühle Museum occupies an old mill and focuses on precision engineering, tools, and mechanical craft. It is a specialist museum, but a fitting one in a region where small industry and hand skills mattered as much as coal and steel.

Go if you like workshops, machines, and the quiet ingenuity of old technical trades. Otherwise, treat it as a short companion stop to St Peter's Church nearby.
