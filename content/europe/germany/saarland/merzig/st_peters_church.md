St Peter's Church is Merzig's main historic building, a Romanesque church with twin towers and a calm, weighty presence near the old centre. Its stonework gives the town a deeper time scale than the modern shopping streets suggest.

It is worth stepping inside if the doors are open, then walking around the outside to see how the church sits in the townscape. For a short Merzig visit, start here.
