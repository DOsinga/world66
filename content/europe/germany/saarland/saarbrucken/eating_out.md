---
title: "Eating Out"
type: section
---

Saarbrücken's food scene reflects its Franco-German character: you can eat very well here by German standards, with French influence showing up in wine lists, sauce preparations, and the general seriousness with which food is taken. The St Johanner Markt is the heart of the eating and drinking scene — tables spread across the cobbles in summer and the choice is wide.
