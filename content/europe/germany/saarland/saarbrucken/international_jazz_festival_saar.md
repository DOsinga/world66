The International Jazz Festival Saar brings a mix of established names and newer musicians to Saarbruecken, with the main burst of concerts usually centred on late June. The programme is broader than one weekend, with events appearing around the region through the year.

For visitors, it is worth checking if your dates line up with a concert at the E-Werk or another city venue. Saarbruecken is not a huge nightlife city, so a strong festival booking can change the feel of an evening.
