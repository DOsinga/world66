Johanneskirche is a neo-Gothic Protestant church close to St Johanner Markt in central Saarbrücken.
Its spire, brickwork, and urban position make it one of the more noticeable historic buildings while
walking between the old market area and the modern city centre.

The church is a short stop rather than a major attraction, but it helps fill in Saarbrücken's
architectural texture. Look in if it is open, or simply use it as a landmark while exploring the
streets around St Johann.
