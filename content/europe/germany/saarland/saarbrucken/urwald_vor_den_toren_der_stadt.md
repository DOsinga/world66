Urwald vor den Toren der Stadt is a rewilded forest area just outside Saarbrucken, where parts of the Saarkohlenwald have been left to develop with little intervention. Marked trails make it an easy half-day escape from the city, with a rougher, wilder feel than a normal urban park.
