The Muskau Forest Railway is a narrow-gauge heritage railway running through the woods and former industrial landscape around Bad Muskau and Weisswasser. Steam and diesel trains operate on selected days, turning what could be a short park visit into a more varied day out.

Check the timetable before building plans around it. When trains are running, the ride is especially good for families or anyone with a soft spot for small railways.
