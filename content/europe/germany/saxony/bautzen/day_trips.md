---
title: 'Day Trips'
type: section
---

Bautzen makes a useful base for the surrounding Upper Lusatia region. **Görlitz**, 50 km east on the Polish border, is the more spectacular of the two old towns and is well worth a half-day. **Herrnhut**, the home village of the Moravian Brethren church, is 40 minutes south. The **Zittau Mountains** and the spa town of **Bad Schandau** in Saxon Switzerland are within 90 minutes by car. The **Spreewald**, the network of waterways through the Sorbian heartland, is two hours north.
