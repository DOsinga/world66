---
title: 'Getting There'
type: section
---

**By train**, Bautzen is on the Dresden–Görlitz line, with hourly regional trains from Dresden (about an hour) and Görlitz (30 minutes). The Bautzen station is a 15-minute walk south of the old town.

**By car**, the A4 motorway runs just south of the city, 90 minutes east of Dresden, two and a half hours from Berlin, an hour from Prague via the border at Zinnwald.

**By air**, the nearest airports are Dresden (60 km west) and Prague (260 km south). There are no flights into Bautzen itself.
