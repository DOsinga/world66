---
title: 'Things to Do'
type: section
---

The compact old town can be walked in a long morning. The eleven medieval towers and the bridge approach from the Friedensbrücke give the famous postcard view; up close, the Reichenturm, the Ortenburg, the Cathedral, and the riverside Alte Wasserkunst are the main set-pieces. Add the Sorbian Museum for the cultural context that makes Bautzen unlike anywhere else in Germany.
