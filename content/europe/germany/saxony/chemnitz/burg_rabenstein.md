Burg Rabenstein is a small medieval castle on the western edge of Chemnitz, set above the district that shares its name. It is not a sprawling fortress, but its compact towers and walls give a tangible reminder that this industrial city also has older Saxon layers.

The castle pairs well with the nearby Rabenstein forest and reservoir area, making it useful for a half-day outside the centre. Come for a short historical stop, a walk, and a different view of Chemnitz beyond its modernist and industrial landmarks.
