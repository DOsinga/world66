---
title: "Getting Around"
type: section
---

The city centre is walkable. The Karl Marx Monument, the archaeology museum, the Kunstsammlungen, and the petrified forest site are all within 10 minutes of each other on foot. The **Kaßberg** is a 15-minute walk west of the centre, or a quick tram ride. Chemnitz has a good tram network (the **Straßenbahn**) covering the wider city — the same ticket covers trams and buses. The Saxon Railway Museum and the Parkeisenbahn are in the northeastern Küchwald area, easily reached by tram. A day ticket on public transport is good value if you plan to cover more than two or three stops.
