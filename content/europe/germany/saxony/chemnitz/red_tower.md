The Red Tower is a small but important survivor in central Chemnitz. Built of red brick, it is the last visible remnant of the medieval city wall and has outlasted centuries of rebuilding and wartime damage.

It is a quick stop rather than a full attraction, but it helps anchor the older history of a city better known for industrial and 20th-century landmarks.
