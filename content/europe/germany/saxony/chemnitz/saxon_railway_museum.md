The Saxon Railway Museum occupies a former locomotive depot in the Hilbersdorf district in the northeast of the city, one of the few surviving round-house engine sheds in Saxony. The collection focuses on Saxon and East German railway history, with a particular strength in narrow-gauge material.

Opening times are limited — the museum runs regular event weekends rather than daily opening, so check before you go. On steam days the yard operates as a working shed, with engines under steam and staff who know their locomotives. It's an experience that rewards anyone with more than a passing interest in railways, and is surprisingly engaging even for those who arrive sceptical.
