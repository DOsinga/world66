A compact bar on the edge of the Kaßberg with a loyal neighbourhood clientele. In summer the tables spill onto the pavement and it becomes the kind of place where you end up staying longer than you intended — the combination of cheap beer, relaxed atmosphere, and good people-watching along Weststraße. In winter it retreats inside and gets cosier.

No food beyond bar snacks; no cocktail list to speak of; very good Czech lager on tap. Arrive after 8pm if you want it properly busy.
