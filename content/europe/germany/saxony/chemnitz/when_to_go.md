---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

Chemnitz is a year-round city for museum visits — the indoor attractions are excellent whatever the weather. **Spring and early summer** (May to June) are the best time to appreciate the Kaßberg neighbourhood and the Botanical Garden. **Summer** brings outdoor concerts and events, and the city's parks and squares come into their own. **Winter** is cold but functional; Chemnitz has a traditional Christmas market on the Marktplatz and the usual Saxon Advent atmosphere. 2025 is worth noting specifically: as European Capital of Culture, the city ran a year-long programme of events and exhibitions, some of which have left a lasting mark on the cultural infrastructure.
