Podelwitz Castle sits in the countryside northeast of Colditz, close enough to fold into a short drive or bike ride. It is a Renaissance water castle rather than a major museum stop, so expectations matter.

Come for the setting and exterior more than for a deep visit. It works best if you are already tracing castles and villages along the Mulde valley.
