The Carl Maria von Weber Museum preserves the composer's former summer house in Hosterwitz, giving Dresden a small but meaningful music-history site away from the central opera-and-gallery circuit. It is best for travellers already interested in classical music.

The museum is intimate, and the suburban Elbe-side setting is part of the appeal. Check opening hours carefully, then combine it with nearby Pillnitz or an Elbe excursion.
