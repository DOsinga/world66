Dreikönigskirche is a Neustadt church with one of Dresden's more affordable tower climbs. The view over the city is the main reason to come, especially if you want a broad look at Dresden without paying the higher admission at the more famous Frauenkirche.

It fits well with a walk through the Neustadt and the nearby Kunsthofpassage.
