Dresden Zoo sits beside the Großer Garten, making it easy to combine with the city's largest park. It has the feel of a long-established European zoo, with animal houses, outdoor enclosures, and a compact layout that suits families.

It will not replace Dresden's art museums or rebuilt old town for first-time visitors, but it is a useful half-day option when travelling with children. The park setting is the main advantage, giving the visit room to breathe.
