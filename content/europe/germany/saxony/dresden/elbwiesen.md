The Elbwiesen are Dresden's broad green river meadows, where the city loosens up along the Elbe. On warm evenings people come here to walk, cycle, picnic, play music, or just look back toward the old town skyline.

They are not a single monument, but they are one of the easiest ways to understand Dresden's setting: water, grass, bridges, and baroque towers sharing the same wide valley.
