Glaserne Manufaktur, Volkswagen's Transparent Factory, is a glass-walled production and exhibition site near Dresden's Groser Garten. English tours explain the building and its role in VW's electric-car work, making it a useful stop for visitors interested in modern industry, design, or car manufacturing.
