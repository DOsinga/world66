The Kunsthalle im Lipsius-Bau is a 19th-century exhibition building beside Dresden's old centre, run as part of the Dresden State Art Collections. Its glass-domed architecture is part of the appeal, but the main reason to go is the changing programme of art exhibitions.

It pairs well with the Albertinum and Bruehl Terrace, both just a short walk away.
