Kunsthaus Dresden is a contemporary art venue in the Neustadt, with exhibitions, talks, workshops, concerts, and film screenings. It is smaller and more current than Dresden's grand museum collections, which is exactly the point.

Come if you want a look at the city's present-day art scene after the palaces, churches, and old masters. Check the programme first, as the experience depends on the current exhibition.
