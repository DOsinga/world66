The Kupferstich-Kabinett is Dresden's museum of drawings, prints, and photographs, and one of the oldest collections of its kind in the world. Its holdings range from medieval works on paper to modern photography, with names such as Durer, Rembrandt, and Picasso represented in the collection.

It is part of the Dresden State Art Collections, so it pairs naturally with the Residenzschloss museums nearby.
