The Leonhardi Museum occupies a historic villa in Loschwitz and focuses on changing art exhibitions rather than Dresden's grand old-master circuit. Its quieter setting makes it a useful alternative when the central museums feel crowded or formal.

Pair it with time in Loschwitz, the Elbe slopes, or the funiculars above the river. Check the current exhibition first, because the museum's draw changes with the programme.
