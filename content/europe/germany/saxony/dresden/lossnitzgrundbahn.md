The Lößnitzgrundbahn is a narrow-gauge steam railway running from Radebeul through wooded valleys and small Saxon towns toward Moritzburg. It is a slow, nostalgic ride rather than a fast way to get anywhere, and it pairs well with a visit to Moritzburg Palace.
