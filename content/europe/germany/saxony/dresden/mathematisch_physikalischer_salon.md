The Mathematisch-Physikalischer Salon is Dresden's museum of historic scientific instruments, housed in the Zwinger. Its collection includes 18th-century precision tools, telescopes, clocks, globes, and celestial models that show how Saxon court science measured time, space, and the heavens. It is a rewarding stop for visitors interested in science history or fine mechanical objects.
