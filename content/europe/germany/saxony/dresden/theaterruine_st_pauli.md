Theaterruine St. Pauli turns the roofless remains of St. Pauli Church into an atmospheric open-air performance venue. In a city where reconstruction and memory are constant themes, the site is a vivid example of reuse rather than restoration: the ruin remains a ruin, but it is active again.

It is most worthwhile when there is a performance, concert, or summer event. The venue also gives visitors a reason to spend time in the Neustadt beyond bars and street life, adding a cultural stop that feels very different from the polished museums and monuments across the Elbe.
