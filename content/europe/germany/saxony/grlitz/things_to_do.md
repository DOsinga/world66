title: Things to Do
type: section
---

Görlitz is one of the most rewarding small cities in Germany for walking. With over 4,000 listed buildings spanning nearly every European architectural style from Gothic to Art Nouveau, and the extraordinary accident of having been bypassed by wartime bombing, the entire historic centre is essentially an open-air museum. The town works best as a slow wander — pick a direction and follow it, because almost every street turns up something worth stopping for.
