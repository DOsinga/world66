---
title: When to Go
type: section
---

Görlitz is a year-round destination but is at its best in late spring and early autumn. Summer brings more visitors but the town can absorb them — it is large enough not to feel overwhelmed by crowds the way some smaller German heritage towns do. The light in September and October is particularly good for the elaborate facades of the Untermarkt.

Winter in Görlitz is cold and can be grey, but the Christmas market is one of the best in Saxony and the town looks dramatic in snow. Spring brings the first café terraces open and the crowds have yet to arrive. July and August are the busiest months, coinciding with the school holidays in Germany and Poland.
