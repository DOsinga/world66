Energiefabrik Knappenrode preserves a former briquette factory in the Lusatian mining region near Hoyerswerda. The scale of the machinery and the coal-industry setting make it a strong industrial-heritage stop, especially for travellers interested in how the region was shaped by energy production.

The museum is a better fit as a half-day excursion than a quick town-centre visit. It pairs well with Hoyerswerda's other history stops and gives useful context for the lakes, mines, and post-industrial landscapes around Upper Lusatia.
