Knappensee is one of the lakes that show how dramatically Lusatia has been remade after open-cast mining. Near Hoyerswerda, water, beaches, paths, and restoration work now occupy a landscape once defined by extraction.

Conditions around Lusatian mining lakes can change because of bank stabilisation and safety works, so check local access before making it the centre of a day. When open, it is a useful outdoor counterpoint to Hoyerswerda's museums and the wider Lausitzer Seenland story.
