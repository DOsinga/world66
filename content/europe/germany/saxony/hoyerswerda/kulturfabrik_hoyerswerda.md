Kulturfabrik Hoyerswerda is the town's main independent culture venue, mixing concerts, cinema, theatre, cabaret, and community arts. In a destination shaped by planned housing, industrial history, and regional change, that kind of active cultural space is useful for visitors to know about.

It is most worthwhile when the programme lines up with your stay. Check listings before going, then use it as an evening option or as a way to see a more contemporary side of Hoyerswerda.
