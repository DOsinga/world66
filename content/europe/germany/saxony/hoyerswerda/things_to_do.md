type: section
---

Hoyerswerda is not a conventional tourist destination — it is honest about that. The interest lies in its unusual character: the collision of a medieval Sorbian market town with one of East Germany's largest planned housing developments, now slowly being unmade. The Lausitzer Seenland lake district growing from old open-pit mines to the north and east is the main draw for leisure visitors.
