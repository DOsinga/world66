The Asisi Panometer uses an old Leipzig gasometer as a monumental viewing chamber for Yadegar Asisi's 360-degree panoramas. The industrial shell is part of the appeal: you climb inside a piece of utility architecture and find it repurposed for theatrical scale and image-making.

Exhibitions change, so the subject may be ancient, natural, urban, or historical depending on the year. The constant is the experience of standing on the central platform while the panorama wraps around you. It is a strong Leipzig stop for travellers who like industrial reuse and spectacle with some substance.
