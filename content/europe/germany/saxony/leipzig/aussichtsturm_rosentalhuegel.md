Aussichtsturm Rosentalhügel is a modest observation tower in Leipzig's Rosental, the parkland north-west of the centre. The climb is short, but it gives a useful green view across the city and the zoo-side treetops.

It is best as part of a walk or cycle through the Rosental rather than a standalone expedition. On a clear day, the tower adds a quick change of perspective after the museums, churches, and denser inner-city streets.
