Fockeberg is a wooded hill south of central Leipzig, built from World War II rubble and later turned into a public green space. Its paths lead up to a local viewpoint over the city.

The hill is a low-key stop, but it adds a distinct piece of Leipzig's postwar landscape to a walk through the southern neighborhoods.
