Galerie für Zeitgenössische Kunst, usually shortened to GfZK, is Leipzig's contemporary art gallery, combining a historic villa with modern exhibition spaces. Its programme leans into current art, social questions, and experimental presentation rather than the old-master comfort of a traditional museum.

It is a good fit for travelers who want to understand Leipzig as a living art city, not only a place of Bach, trade fairs, and grand civic buildings. Check the current shows first, because contemporary galleries rise or fall on what is installed at the moment.
