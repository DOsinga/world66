Leipzig's Russian Memorial Church was built to commemorate Russian soldiers who died in
the 1813 Battle of the Nations. Its onion domes and Orthodox forms make it stand apart
from the city's German church architecture, while its history ties directly to the
battlefield landscape south of the centre.

It is a meaningful stop if you are visiting the Monument to the Battle of the Nations or
tracing Leipzig's Napoleonic history. Check access before going inside, but even the
exterior adds a strong visual and historical note.
