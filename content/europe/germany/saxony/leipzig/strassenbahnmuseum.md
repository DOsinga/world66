Leipzig's Straßenbahnmuseum is a treat for anyone who notices the trams that stitch the city together. Historic cars, equipment, route signs, and depot atmosphere show how public transport shaped Leipzig's growth long before today's network became part of daily urban rhythm.

It is a specialist museum, so check opening days carefully; many tram museums run on limited volunteer hours. When open, it is a satisfying detour from the central music and trade-fair sights, especially for families or transport-minded travellers.
