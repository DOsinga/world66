Torhaus Dölitz is one of the most tangible remnants of the old Dölitz manor on Leipzig's southern
edge. The Renaissance-style gatehouse survived when much of the estate disappeared, leaving a
compact historic building that now anchors a specialist museum.

Inside, the exhibitions use tin figures, models, and local-history displays to interpret battles,
civic life, and the changing villages that were absorbed into modern Leipzig. It is a modest sight,
but for visitors already exploring the southern parks, Wildpark Leipzig, or the Markkleeberg lakes,
it adds a grounded historic stop away from the city centre.
