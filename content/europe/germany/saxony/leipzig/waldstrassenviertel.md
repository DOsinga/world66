Waldstrassenviertel is one of Leipzig's best areas for wandering if you like urban fabric more than single monuments. The neighbourhood is packed with late-nineteenth-century apartment buildings, many restored with ornate facades, balconies, and quiet courtyards.

It sits close enough to the centre to fold into a walk toward the Rosental or the zoo. There is no single must-see doorway; the point is the streetscape, which shows Leipzig's bourgeois boom years in a more lived-in way than the big civic buildings do.
