Wildpark Leipzig is a relaxed wildlife park in the southern Auenwald, the floodplain forest that runs through the city. It focuses on mostly European animals in a wooded setting, so it feels more like a forest walk with enclosures than a conventional zoo.

It is a strong family stop and a good excuse to explore Leipzig beyond the centre. Combine it with the nearby river and forest paths if you want a greener day.
