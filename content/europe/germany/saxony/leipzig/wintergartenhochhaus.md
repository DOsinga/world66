Wintergartenhochhaus is a striking high-rise near Leipzig's main station, built in the East German period and still one of the city's most visible modern landmarks. It is not a museum or a romantic ruin, but it helps round out Leipzig's architectural story beyond trade-fair arcades and historic churches.

See it while moving through the station district or walking toward the centre. The building is most interesting as urban context: a reminder that Leipzig's skyline was shaped by socialist-era planning as well as older merchant wealth.
