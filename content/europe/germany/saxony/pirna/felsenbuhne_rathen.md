Felsenbuhne Rathen is an open-air theatre set among the sandstone cliffs below the Bastei. The stage uses the natural rock walls as part of the scenery, giving performances a setting that feels very different from a conventional theatre.

The programme usually mixes adventure plays, family productions, and opera. It is most worthwhile if you are already spending time around Rathen or the Bastei and can time the visit with a performance.
