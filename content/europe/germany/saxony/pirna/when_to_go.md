---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

Pirna and Saxon Switzerland are most visited from May to September, when the weather is reliable and outdoor activities are fully available. The Elbe paddle-steamer services run from late April to October; hiking trails in the national park are accessible all year but at their best in late spring and early autumn.

**Summer (June–August)** is peak season. The Bastei rock formations and Königstein Fortress are crowded on summer weekends, and popular trails can feel like queues. The Elbe is warm enough for riverside walks and the ferry connections are at their most frequent. Evenings in the old town are pleasant.

**Spring and autumn** are the better choices for most visitors. Late April and May bring greenery to the sandstone gorges; September and October bring autumn colour and noticeably thinner crowds. The light on the Elbe valley at these times of year is particularly good.

**Winter** is quiet. Some attractions have reduced hours; the steamers don't run. But the old town looks appealing in frost and the sandstone formations take on a different atmosphere in low mist. Accommodation is cheaper and you have most places to yourself.

The Pirna Christmas market, held in December on the market square, is a modest and relatively un-commercial affair — pleasant if you happen to be in the area, not worth a special trip.
