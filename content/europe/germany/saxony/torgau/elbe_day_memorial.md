The Elbe Day Memorial marks the moment in April 1945 when American and Soviet forces linked up on the Elbe, signalling that Nazi Germany was split in two. The first meeting took place nearby, but Torgau became the symbolic name attached to the event.

It is a small stop, not a grand monument. Its power comes from standing by the river and remembering how suddenly the geography of a quiet town became world history.
