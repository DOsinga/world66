---
title: Eating Out
type: section
---

Dessau's restaurant scene is modest for a city of its size. There is no dining quarter and no strong local cuisine to speak of — East German concrete towns rarely developed one. The clear standout is the Kornhaus, a Bauhaus-designed pavilion on the Elbe that doubles as one of the most atmospheric places to eat in eastern Germany. In the city centre, the choice narrows considerably.
