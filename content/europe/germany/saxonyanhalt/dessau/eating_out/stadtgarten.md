A reliable city-centre restaurant serving German and European food in a straightforward setting with a garden terrace that works well in good weather. The Stadtgarten is the kind of place locals come for a sit-down lunch on a weekday or a relaxed dinner without fanfare — not a destination in itself, but solid and honest. Good for a meal after a morning at the Bauhaus Building before heading out to Wörlitz.
