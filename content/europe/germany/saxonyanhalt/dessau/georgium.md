The Georgium is part of the Dessau-Wörlitz Garden Realm, the UNESCO-listed landscape of parks, palaces, and Enlightenment design around Dessau. Its English-style park, palace, avenues, and water features make it one of the city’s key sights beyond the Bauhaus buildings.

It is best explored slowly on foot or by bike, letting the paths, sightlines, and small architectural moments accumulate. For travellers, the Georgium helps explain why Dessau is not only a modernist pilgrimage stop but also a major landscape-design destination.
