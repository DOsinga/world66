Smoker's Tower is an industrial remnant from a former food-processing plant, now covered in street art. A stairwell leads up to the roof, turning the old structure into an informal lookout over this part of Dessau.

It is rougher and less polished than the city's Bauhaus sights, but that contrast is the appeal. Stop by if you like post-industrial spaces, murals, and odd viewpoints.
