Technikmuseum Hugo Junkers connects Dessau's Bauhaus fame with another side of the city's modernity:
aviation, engineering, and industrial design. Hugo Junkers developed pioneering all-metal aircraft
here, and the museum uses aircraft, engines, and technical exhibits to explain why Dessau mattered
to 20th-century transport and manufacturing.

It is particularly useful if you want a broader Dessau visit than the Bauhaus buildings alone. The
museum shows the same period's faith in materials, mass production, and new forms of movement, but
through machines rather than architecture.
