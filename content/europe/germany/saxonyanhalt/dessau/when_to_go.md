---
title: When to Go
type: section
---

**April to October** is the right window for Dessau. The Wörlitz gardens are the main reason to time your visit: the park is at its best from late April (spring flowers, fresh green) through to October (autumn colour along the water), and the boat rides on the Wörlitzer See only run in the warmer months. Summer weekends in the Garden Kingdom can get busy; a weekday in May or early June is close to ideal.

The Bauhaus buildings can be visited year-round and look good in all seasons — the glass and white concrete of the Bauhaus Building is arguably most striking on a bright winter day when the light is low. The permanent exhibition is open regardless of weather, so a November or February trip is perfectly viable for architecture-focused visitors.

Dessau's summers are warm and pleasant without being oppressive. Winters are cold and quiet — the city empties, the Wörlitz park is closed or limited, and there is less reason to be here unless the Bauhaus is the sole purpose. The city has no particular festival or event calendar worth timing a trip around.
