The Halloren Schokoladen-Museum tells the story of Germany's oldest chocolate factory, whose roots go back to a honey-cake bakery founded in 1804. The museum is a light, family-friendly stop, with displays on chocolate production, the Halloren brand, and the factory's long place in Halle's industrial history.
