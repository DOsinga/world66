The State Museum of Prehistory is the most important reason most archaeology-minded travellers come to Halle. The collections cover central German prehistory from the Palaeolithic onwards, with star pieces including Stone Age burials from the Goseck circle, Bronze Age hoards, and — above all — the Nebra Sky Disc.

The disc has its own darkened room and is well worth the trip on its own. The wider permanent exhibition is large, well-lit, and ambitious; allow at least two hours. Labels include thorough English summaries.
