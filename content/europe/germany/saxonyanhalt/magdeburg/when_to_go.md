---
title: When to Go
type: section
---

Magdeburg has a continental climate with warm summers and cold winters. The best months to visit are **May through September**, when the city is pleasant for walking and the Elbauenpark and river promenades are at their best. Summer temperatures average 22-25°C with occasional hot spells.

The **Christmas market** on the Alter Markt is one of the better-regarded ones in Saxony-Anhalt, running through December. The **Magdeburg Telemann Festival** in spring (every two years) celebrates the composer Georg Philipp Telemann, who was born in the city in 1681 — a significant event in the city's cultural calendar.

Winter is grey and cold but not especially severe. The Gruson-Gewächshäuser (botanical greenhouses) are particularly appreciated in winter for their warm, tropical interiors.
