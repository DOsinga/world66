---
title: "Eating Out"
type: section
---

Quedlinburg is Harz country, so expect hearty regional cooking: roast game, Harzer Roller cheese (the pungent small rounds made just over the hill), and local honey from the Harz foothills. The historic centre has a handful of traditional restaurants clustered around the Marktplatz and the lanes leading up to the Schlossberg.
