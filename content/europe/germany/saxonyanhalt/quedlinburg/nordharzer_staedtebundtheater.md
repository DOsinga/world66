Nordharzer Städtebundtheater gives Quedlinburg a performing-arts life beyond its half-timbered streets and UNESCO scenery. The company serves both Quedlinburg and Halberstadt, with a programme that can include drama, musical theatre, concerts, and family productions.

For visitors, it is most useful when a performance fits an overnight stay. Quedlinburg is often treated as a day-trip museum town; an evening at the theatre is one way to see it behaving like a real regional centre.
