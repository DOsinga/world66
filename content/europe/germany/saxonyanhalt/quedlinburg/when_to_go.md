---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

Late spring and early autumn are the sweet spots — the crowds of summer day-trippers are thinner and the light on the half-timbered facades is softer. Summer weekends draw coachloads from Berlin and beyond. Winter can be atmospheric, especially around the Christmas market, but check that the abbey treasury has normal opening hours, as some attractions reduce their schedules outside the main season.
