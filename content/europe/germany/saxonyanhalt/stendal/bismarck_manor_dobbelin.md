Bismarck's Manor in Doebbelin is a Baroque estate house tied to the wider Bismarck family, though Otto von Bismarck himself did not live here. The house and grounds sit in the countryside west of Stendal and make a gentle stop for travellers interested in regional Prussian history.

The grounds are the easiest part to visit, with a well-kept estate setting and a surviving coach house. It is a modest historic house rather than a major national monument.
