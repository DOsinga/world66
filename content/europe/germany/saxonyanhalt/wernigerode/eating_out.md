---
title: "Eating Out"
type: section
---

Wernigerode has a solid range of restaurants concentrated around the Marktplatz and the pedestrianised streets of the old town. Regional German cooking — Harz dishes, venison, wild boar, local trout — features prominently. Cafe Wien, one of the town's oldest establishments, occupies a building of over 400 years and serves good coffee, cakes, and light meals in a traditional bakery setting.
