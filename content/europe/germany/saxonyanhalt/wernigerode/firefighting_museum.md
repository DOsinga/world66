Wernigerode's Firefighting Museum has a collection of historic firefighting equipment and displays on firefighting in the town and surrounding region.

It is a small, focused museum rather than a headline sight, but it adds useful variety to an old-town visit, especially for families or travellers interested in local history and technical collections.
