Ottofelsen is a jagged granite outcrop in the Harz woods above Wernigerode. Metal ladders and steps help you reach the top, where the view opens over forest, ridges, and, in clear weather, toward the Brocken. It has the satisfying feel of a small adventure without requiring a full mountain day.

The walk from town or from nearby trailheads is part of the appeal, especially if you want to balance Wernigerode's half-timbered streets with a dose of Harz landscape. Wear decent shoes after rain: the rock and forest paths can be slick, and the viewpoint is best enjoyed when you are not rushing.
