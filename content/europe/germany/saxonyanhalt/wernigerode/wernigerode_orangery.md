The Wernigerode Orangery sits in the orbit of the castle and its gardens, a reminder that the town was shaped not only by half-timbered streets but also by courtly display. It is a quieter detail in a place where the castle usually takes the spotlight.

Stop if you are already walking the castle grounds or exploring the slopes above town. The orangery is not a reason to come to Wernigerode by itself, but it rounds out the historic landscape nicely.
