---
title: "Things to Do in Bad Schwartau"
type: section
---

Bad Schwartau's attractions are modest and tied to its twin identities as a spa town and the home of Schwartau jam. The Kurpark and the Sole-Therme are the reasons to stay; [Lübeck](/europe/germany/schleswigholstein/lubeck) is the reason to come to this corner of Schleswig-Holstein in the first place.
