Büsum is a small North Sea town on the Dithmarschen coast, part fishing harbour and part German family resort. It is not a wild place, and in summer it can feel more practical than romantic, but the setting is strong: dikes, shrimp boats, tidal flats, gulls, and the wide horizontal light of the Wadden Sea.

The town's identity rests on **Büsumer Krabben**, the small brown North Sea shrimp landed and processed here, and on the mudflats just beyond the seawall. A good day is simple: walk the [museum harbour](/europe/germany/schleswigholstein/buesum/museumshafen_buesum), eat shrimp, visit the [Museum am Meer](/europe/germany/schleswigholstein/buesum/museum_am_meer), then join a guided walk or boat trip into the [Wadden Sea](/europe/germany/schleswigholstein/buesum/wadden_sea_buesum).

Büsum is still a resort, with beach chairs, family swimming areas, and rainy-day attractions, but it clears the bar because the coast here is not just a backdrop. The tides, fisheries, and dike landscape explain the town.
