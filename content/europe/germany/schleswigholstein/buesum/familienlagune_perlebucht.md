Perlebucht is Büsum's engineered answer to the North Sea's awkward tides: a broad family lagoon with sheltered swimming, sand, play areas, and space for watersports. It is modern, managed, and busy in season, but it works well if you want beach time without waiting for the sea to be in the right mood.

The dike setting keeps it unmistakably North Sea. Even when the beach scene is full of families and Strandkörbe, the wind, salt air, and mudflat horizon are still right there.
