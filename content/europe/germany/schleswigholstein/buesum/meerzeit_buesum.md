Meerzeit Büsum is the town's public swimming and sauna complex, with children's pools, a whirlpool, an adventure pool, a wave pool, and a long slide. It is a useful family stop when the North Sea weather turns rough.

The sauna area and simple bistro make it more than a quick swim stop, especially on cold or rainy days.
