Phänomania is a hands-on science museum built around experiments rather than display cases. It is especially useful with children, or when the North Sea sends rain sideways across the dike and outdoor plans stop being fun.

The exhibits are broad rather than local, so it should not be the main reason to come to Büsum. Treat it as a practical backup that rounds out the town's stronger harbour and Wadden Sea sights.
