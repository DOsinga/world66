---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

The obvious time is June through August, when the beach is at its best, the smokeries are busy, and the harbor is lively. The water in the bay reaches around 20°C in July and August — not warm by Mediterranean standards, but genuinely swimmable for the Baltic. School holiday weeks in July can be busy enough that accommodation fills early, especially on weekends.

May and September offer the same scenery and most of the facilities with fewer people and lower prices. The Windebyer Noor is particularly good for birdwatching in May during the spring migration. In winter, Eckernförde is quiet — many of the seasonal businesses close — but the harbor and old town retain their character, and you can walk the empty promenade in remarkable solitude.
