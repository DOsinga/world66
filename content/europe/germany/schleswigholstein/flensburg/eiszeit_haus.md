Eiszeit-Haus is a small geology museum looking at how the last Ice Age shaped the land around Flensburg. The displays include fossils, rocks, and samples that help explain the fjords, soils, and glacial traces of Schleswig-Holstein.

It is not a big museum, but it works well as a short stop if you are curious about the landscape behind the harbour town.
