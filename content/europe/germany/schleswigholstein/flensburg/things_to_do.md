---
title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Flensburg rewards those who explore beyond the pedestrian zone. The [Nordertor](/europe/germany/schleswigholstein/flensburg/nordertor) is the most photographed landmark and a good starting point for walking north along the old town. The harbour area, with the [Schifffahrtsmuseum](/europe/germany/schleswigholstein/flensburg/schifffahrtsmuseum) and its rum collection, is the best of the city's museums. [Flensburger Brauerei](/europe/germany/schleswigholstein/flensburg/flensburger_brauerei) is known far beyond Germany for its distinctive flip-top bottles.

For something stranger, the [Mumiengrotte](/europe/germany/schleswigholstein/flensburg/mumiengrotte) in a public park contains an early 19th-century stone sarcophagus — one of the city's best-kept secrets. The [Isted Lion](/europe/germany/schleswigholstein/flensburg/isted_lion) tells a story of Danish-German border politics that still resonates in this bilingual city. History enthusiasts should not miss [Mürwik Naval School](/europe/germany/schleswigholstein/flensburg/muerwik_naval_school), briefly the seat of the last Nazi government in May 1945.
