Wehrgeschichtliches Ausbildungszentrum is a naval history museum at the Muerwik Naval Academy. The setting matters: this area was the last seat of the short-lived Doenitz government in May 1945, just before British forces ended it.

The museum is a focused stop for visitors interested in German naval and military history, and pairs naturally with the academy buildings nearby.
