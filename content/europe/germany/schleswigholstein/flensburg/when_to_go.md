---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

Summer (June to August) is the best time to visit Flensburg. The Firth is warm enough for swimming, the harbour terraces are open, and the long northern evenings give the town a pleasant liveliness. Flensburg sits at 54°N, so summer days are noticeably longer than in central Europe.

Winter is cold and grey, with the Firth occasionally freezing over in severe years. The Christmas market is a modest but genuine local event. Spring and autumn are variable — the Baltic coast weather can change quickly — but both seasons have stretches of calm, clear days when the sailing and walking in the surrounding countryside are excellent.
