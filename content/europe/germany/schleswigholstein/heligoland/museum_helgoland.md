Museum Helgoland is a compact stop for making sense of the island before or after the cliff walk. Its displays cover local culture, natural history, fishing, island life, and the repeated political handovers that shaped Heligoland.

It is especially useful if you are staying overnight. Day-trippers often hurry straight to the cliffs, but the museum gives the red rock and bunker history a clearer frame.
