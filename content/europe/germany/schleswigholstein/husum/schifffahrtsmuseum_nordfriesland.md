The Schifffahrtsmuseum Nordfriesland fills in the working side of Husum's harbour. The collections cover shipbuilding, fishing, whaling, navigation, and the smaller trades that connected this coast to the wider North Sea.

It pairs well with the harbour: first look at the boats and basin, then come here for the longer story.
