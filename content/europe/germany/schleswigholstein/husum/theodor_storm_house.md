Theodor Storm is Husum's most famous literary figure, and his phrase "the grey town by the sea" still follows the place around. His former home is now a small museum for readers who want the local context behind the poems and novellas.

It is most rewarding if you know Storm already, but even a short visit helps explain why Husum leans into its weathered, northern character.
