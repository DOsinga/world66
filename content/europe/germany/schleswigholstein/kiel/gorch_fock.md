Gorch Fock is the German Navy's famous sail training ship, a tall white barque closely associated with Kiel's maritime identity. When she is in port, the ship is one of the most graceful sights on the waterfront, all masts, rigging, and naval polish against the working harbour.

Access depends on naval schedules and special events, so most visitors will see her from the quays rather than aboard. Even so, the ship adds a ceremonial note to Kiel's otherwise practical port landscape and is a natural stop for anyone drawn to sailing culture.
