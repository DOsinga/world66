The Schönberger Strand heritage railway runs preserved trains on the old line east of Kiel, with occasional special services connecting through from the city. The rolling stock is mostly drawn from regional railways, so the ride feels more local than polished.

There is also a short heritage tramway at Schönberger Strand, using cars from former Hamburg and Kiel systems. Check operating days before going; this is a seasonal and special-event attraction rather than a daily transport option.
