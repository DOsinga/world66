Stadtgalerie Kiel is the city's municipal art gallery, useful for travellers who want something beyond ships, ferries, and harbour views. Its exhibitions lean toward contemporary art, photography, and regional work, often with a northern German or Baltic context.

It is a compact stop and pairs well with a rainy day in central Kiel. Check the current show first: when the programme is strong, it adds real depth to the city; when it is between exhibitions, the Kunsthalle remains the heavier hitter.
