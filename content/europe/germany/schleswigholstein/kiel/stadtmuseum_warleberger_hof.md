Stadtmuseum Warleberger Hof is valuable partly because so little old Kiel survived the 20th century intact. The museum occupies a former noble residence with vaulted cellars and historic rooms, giving the maritime city a more intimate setting for local history than its busy harbourfront first suggests.

Exhibitions focus on Kiel's urban life, art, and regional culture, often changing enough that repeat visitors may find something different. It is a good rainy-day stop and a useful counterweight to the naval and ferry side of Kiel: here the city is domestic, civic, and older than its modern port image.
