---
title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Kiel's best experiences are tied to water. The [Kieler Förde](/europe/germany/schleswigholstein/kiel/kieler_foerde) — the 17km fjord that forms the city's eastern edge — sets the pace: ferries, sailing yachts, and container ships share the same stretch of water, and the [Kiellinie promenade](/europe/germany/schleswigholstein/kiel/kiellinie) runs alongside it for a pleasant couple of kilometres. Take the short ferry across to Laboe on the eastern shore to visit the [Laboe Naval Memorial and U-995](/europe/germany/schleswigholstein/kiel/laboe_naval_memorial), one of the most striking war monuments in Germany and the only surviving Type VIIC U-boat you can board.

At the northern end of the city, the [Kiel Canal locks at Holtenau](/europe/germany/schleswigholstein/kiel/holtenau_locks) are an unexpectedly absorbing spectacle — ocean-going freighters squeezing through a channel in a quiet suburb, watched from wooden benches on the bank. The [Schifffahrtsmuseum](/europe/germany/schleswigholstein/kiel/schifffahrtsmuseum) on the Dänische Strasse covers the city's maritime history with more depth than the modest entrance fee suggests. For contemporary art, the [Kunsthalle Kiel](/europe/germany/schleswigholstein/kiel/kunsthalle_kiel) holds a solid collection of German painting and expressionist work.

The dominant event in the city calendar is [Kieler Woche](/europe/germany/schleswigholstein/kiel/kieler_woche), the world's largest sailing regatta, which takes over the fjord and the city centre for ten days in late June.
