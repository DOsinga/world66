Kiel’s Zoologisches Museum is the natural-history museum of the university, with collections that range from taxidermy and skeletons to marine specimens from the Baltic and beyond. The building is close to the water and fits well into a day spent around the old harbour side of town.

The large skeletons are the easiest draw, but the quieter value is regional: the museum helps make sense of Kiel as a sea-facing city, not just a ferry port. It is a good rainy-day stop and a compact choice for families or anyone interested in biology.
