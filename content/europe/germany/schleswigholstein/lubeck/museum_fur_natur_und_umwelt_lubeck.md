Museum für Natur und Umwelt is Lübeck's natural history museum, with exhibits on regional nature, ecology, and the Baltic environment. Its most memorable display is a full sperm whale skeleton from a whale that stranded on the coast.

It makes a good change of pace from brick Gothic churches, especially for families or anyone curious about the landscape around the old Hanseatic city.
