---
title: 'When to Go'
type: section
---

Lübeck has a maritime northern climate. Summers are mild — high teens to mid-twenties Celsius — and the long Baltic evenings stretch past 10pm in June. Winters are grey, cold, and often wet; snow falls but rarely lies long.

The classic time to visit is late spring through early autumn (May to September), when the weather is kindest and the harbour cafes are open. The Christmas market on the Markt in front of the town hall is one of the best in northern Germany — late November to 23 December — and turns the Altstadt into something out of a Hanseatic painting. Even in deep winter, the brick churches and marzipan cafes are worth a weekend.
