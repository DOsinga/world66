title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Neumünster's points of interest are modest but genuine. The Museum Tuch + Technik tells the story of the textile industry that shaped the city, the St. Vicelinkirche traces local history to the 12th century, and the Tierpark is a perfectly pleasant zoo in a wooded setting. The city makes sense as a break on a Hamburg–Kiel journey.
