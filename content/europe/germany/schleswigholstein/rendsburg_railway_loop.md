The Rendsburg Railway Loop is an odd and impressive piece of engineering built to lift trains high enough to cross the Kiel Canal in flat Schleswig-Holstein. Instead of climbing a hill, the track curls through a broad loop before reaching the high bridge.

It is mainly a sight for rail and engineering fans, but even casual visitors can appreciate the scale. The best view is from around the bridge and canal, where you can see why such an elaborate solution was needed.
