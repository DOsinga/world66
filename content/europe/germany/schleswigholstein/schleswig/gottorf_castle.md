Gottorf Castle is Schleswig's major cultural landmark, a former ducal seat that now
houses important museums of art, archaeology, and regional history. Its scale and
setting on the Schlei make it the kind of place that can anchor a whole visit to the
town.

Allow time for both the collections and the grounds, especially if you are interested in
northern German and Schleswig-Holstein history. It is more substantial than a quick
castle photo stop.
