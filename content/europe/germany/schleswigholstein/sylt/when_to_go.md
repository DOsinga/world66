---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

The Sylt season is firmly summer. July and August are peak — the beaches are full, the restaurants need bookings made weeks ahead, and prices peak. June and early September are noticeably quieter while the water is still warm enough for swimming and the days are still long.

May and late September are the connoisseur's months: cool, clear light, empty beaches, and prices that drop sharply. Autumn storms bring spectacular surf in October and November but most of the resort infrastructure starts shutting down. Winter on Sylt is cold, grey, and very windy — the island has its devotees who come for the bracing walks and the off-season prices, but most of the kitchens and bars close from November to March.

The water temperature peaks around 18°C in August. Even in summer the wind can be sharp; bring layers and something windproof.
