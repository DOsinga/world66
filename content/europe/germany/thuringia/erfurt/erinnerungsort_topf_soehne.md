Erinnerungsort Topf & Soehne is a hard but important museum on the site of the company that built cremation ovens for Nazi concentration camps. The exhibition focuses on ordinary engineers, managers, and clerks, which makes the place especially unsettling: it shows how industrial skill and office routine became part of mass murder.
