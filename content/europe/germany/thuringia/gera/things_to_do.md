title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Gera rewards visitors willing to look beyond the surface. The [Kunstmuseum](/europe/germany/thuringia/gera/kunstmuseum_gera) and the [Otto-Dix-Haus](/europe/germany/thuringia/gera/otto_dix_haus) together make the city essential for anyone interested in German Expressionism. The [Orangery](/europe/germany/thuringia/gera/orangery), the Jugendstil villas of Untermhaus, and the old castle ruins round out the picture of a city with more depth than its tourist footprint suggests.
