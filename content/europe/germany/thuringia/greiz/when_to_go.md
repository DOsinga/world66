---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

Late spring and early summer are the most rewarding time to visit. The Fürstlich Greizer Park is at its best from May to September, and the Sommerpalais holds occasional concerts in the summer months. Autumn brings the colour of the beech and oak in the park.

Winters are cold and quiet; if there is snow, the town is unusually pretty, but most outdoor attractions close.
