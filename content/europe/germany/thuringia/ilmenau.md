Ilmenau sits where the townscape of [Thuringia](/europe/germany/thuringia) gives way to the Thuringian Forest. It is a university town, a Goethe town, and a practical base for walking up into the wooded hills without having to sleep in a resort village. The centre is small, with a market square, the [St. Jakobus Church](/europe/germany/thuringia/ilmenau/st_jakobus_church), and enough cafes to remind you that the [Technische Universitaet Ilmenau](/europe/germany/thuringia/ilmenau/technische_universitaet_ilmenau) keeps the place young.

The town's real pull is uphill. The [Kickelhahn](/europe/germany/thuringia/ilmenau/kickelhahn), Ilmenau's wooded house mountain, rises south-west of town and carries a lookout tower, forest paths, and the [Goethehaeuschen](/europe/germany/thuringia/ilmenau/goethehaeuschen), where Goethe wrote a few famous lines into the wall of a hunting hut. Nearby, [Jagdhaus Gabelbach](/europe/germany/thuringia/ilmenau/jagdhaus_gabelbach) turns that Goethe connection into a small museum rather than a marketing slogan.

Ilmenau is not grand like [Weimar](/europe/germany/thuringia/weimar) or dramatic like [Eisenach](/europe/germany/thuringia/eisenach), but it has a good balance: forest at the edge of town, a lively student population, and enough literary history to give a day hike some texture.
