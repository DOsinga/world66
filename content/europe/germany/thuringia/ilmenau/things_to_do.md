---
title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Ilmenau is best when you treat it as a town-and-hills destination. Spend a little time around the market square and **St. Jakobus Church**, then follow the Goethe associations uphill toward **Jagdhaus Gabelbach**, the **Goethehaeuschen**, and the **Kickelhahn** lookout tower.

The **Technische Universitaet Ilmenau** gives the town its contemporary life, while the surrounding ridges offer easy access to the Thuringian Forest. The walking is the point: you do not need a long expedition, just a free afternoon and enough energy for the climb.
