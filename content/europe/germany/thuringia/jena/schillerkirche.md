Schillerkirche is a small Protestant church from the 14th and 15th centuries, formally dedicated to Our Lady but best known for its connection to Friedrich Schiller. Schiller and Charlotte von Lengefeld were married here in 1790.

The church is a modest stop, but a worthwhile one for visitors following Jena's literary history.
