The Grossjenaer Bluetengrund is the gentle river-and-vineyard landscape just outside Naumburg, where the Unstrut flows into the Saale. It is a classic Saale-Unstrut scene: water, low cliffs, vines, cycle paths, and small wine stops rather than one single monument.

Go by bike or on foot from Naumburg if the weather is good. The area is especially rewarding for travellers who want to connect the cathedral city with the surrounding wine country, and the Max Klinger vineyard gives the walk a useful cultural anchor as well as a view.
