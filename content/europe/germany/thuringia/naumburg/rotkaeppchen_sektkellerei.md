Rotkaeppchen Sektkellerei is the best-known sparkling-wine house in the Saale-Unstrut wine country, based in Freyburg just outside Naumburg. The cellars have been part of the region's wine story since the nineteenth century, and the guided tours give useful context for why this cool-climate corner of Germany is so serious about bottles and vineyards.

The appeal is as much architectural as alcoholic: brick cellars, production rooms, and a polished visitor route that explains how local grapes become sekt. It makes a natural pairing with Naumburg Cathedral and the surrounding vineyard villages if you want a day that balances culture with a glass of something regional.
