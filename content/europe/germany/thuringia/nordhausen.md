Nordhausen is the urban centre of northern Thuringia, sitting where the Harz begins to rise from the plains. It is not a pretty half-timbered showpiece like [Quedlinburg](/europe/germany/saxonyanhalt/quedlinburg), partly because bombing in 1945 destroyed much of the old centre, but it has enough weight for a serious day.

The hardest and most important visit is [Mittelbau-Dora Memorial](/europe/germany/thuringia/nordhausen/mittelbau_dora_memorial), where concentration camp prisoners were forced to build V-2 rockets in underground tunnels. Nordhausen also has a gentler Harz identity: the [Harz Narrow Gauge Railway](/europe/germany/thuringia/nordhausen/harz_narrow_gauge_railway) starts here for steam journeys into the mountains, and the town's tram network has an unusual connection to the narrow-gauge line.

In the centre, look for the Roland statue, the cathedral area, and the old distilling tradition behind Echter Nordhauser korn. Nordhausen is best treated as a sober, practical base with several distinctive anchors rather than a romantic old town.
