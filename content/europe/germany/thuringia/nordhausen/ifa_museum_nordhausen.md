IFA Museum Nordhausen is a compact technology museum built around engines, tractors, and local industrial machinery. It is most rewarding for visitors who like mechanical detail: cutaway engines, old vehicles, and the practical engineering culture that shaped the town after the war.

It will not convert someone with no interest in machines, but paired with the Harz railway it gives Nordhausen a clear industrial thread.
