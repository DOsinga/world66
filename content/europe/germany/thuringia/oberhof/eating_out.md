---
title: "Eating Out"
type: section
---

Oberhof is a mountain resort town and the food matches the setting: hearty, warming Thuringian cooking built for people who have spent the day in the cold or on the trails. The Thüringer Rostbratwurst — the region's famous grilled sausage — is the local staple, alongside Sauerbraten, potato dumplings, and solid German comfort food. Options are mostly concentrated in the hotel zone and along the main road.
