Oberhof's hotels and standalone restaurants serve hearty Thuringian food suited to the mountain resort setting. Thüringer Rostbratwurst — the region's famous grilled sausage, longer and spicier than most German varieties — is the default comfort food, alongside Sauerbraten (braised marinated beef), potato dumplings, and warming soups. Several options are clustered in the hotel zone along the main road through the resort.
