---
title: "Getting Around"
type: section
---

Central Sonneberg is walkable, and the main toy-related sights are close enough to combine on foot. The observatory and hill walks require more time and a climb, so check buses or take a car if you are short on daylight.

The town's appeal is in a slow, curious circuit rather than a checklist. Give yourself enough time to look at the museum properly and then wander the streets that grew around the toy trade.
