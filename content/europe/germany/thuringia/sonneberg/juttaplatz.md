Juttaplatz is a small square opposite the German Toy Museum and one of the more visible stops on Sonneberg's toy-themed walk. It is not grand, but it works as a compact stage for the town's playful self-image.

Pause here before entering the museum, especially if you are following the Play Mile. The square helps connect the museum to the surrounding streets, so the toy story feels like part of the town rather than a single indoor attraction.
