---
title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

The **German Toy Museum** is the essential stop in Sonneberg, both for families and for anyone interested in how a small town became part of a global export industry. Around it, the **Sonneberg Play Mile** and **Juttaplatz** turn the toy theme into a short town walk rather than leaving it inside a museum case.

For a change of subject, head up toward the **Sonneberg Observatory** and the wooded hills above town. The **Muppberg** near Neustadt bei Coburg is a useful nearby lookout if you have a car or bike and want a broader view of the Franconian-Thuringian borderland.
