Belvedere Palace and Park shows Weimar from its courtly, landscaped side. The baroque summer residence sits above town with gardens, avenues, and outbuildings that make it feel like a designed escape from the intellectual intensity of central Weimar.

The park is as important as the palace. Come for a slow walk, architecture, and the contrast with Goethe-and-Schiller Weimar; it is a reminder that the city's cultural prestige was supported by aristocratic display as much as literary genius.
