---
title: "Bars and Cafes"
type: section
---

Gibraltar's nightlife punches above its weight for a territory of 34,000 people. The main hubs are Casemates Square and the Ocean Village marina.

**Casemates Square** is the liveliest outdoor space, with bars and restaurants spilling onto the square. In summer it stays busy well into the night. **Lord Nelson's** here stays open late, regularly hosts live bands, and is the home of the Gibraltar Rugby Club. **The Star Bar** on Parliament Lane, just off the northern end of Main Street, claims to be Gibraltar's oldest bar — relaxed and unpretentious, with occasional live music.

**O'Reilly's** at Ocean Village is Gibraltar's long-standing Irish pub, with proper Irish draft beer and a traditional Victorian interior — good for a quieter drink away from the Casemates crowd.

The live music scene is active. The Gibraltar International Jazz Festival each October draws well-regarded artists. St Michael's Cave hosts occasional concerts — the acoustics in the cavern are remarkable.
