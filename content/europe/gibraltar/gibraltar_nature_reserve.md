Gibraltar Nature Reserve covers much of the Upper Rock, mixing cliffs, old military works, caves, viewpoints, and the famous Barbary macaques. It is the part of Gibraltar where the territory's odd combination of natural drama and fortress history makes most sense.

You can visit individual sights by road or cable car, but walking between them gives a better feel for the Rock. Keep food packed away around the macaques; they are wild animals, not props for photos.
