---
title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Almost everything worth seeing in Gibraltar is on or inside the Rock itself. The Upper Rock Nature Reserve covers around 40% of the territory and is best explored by Rock Tour taxi or minibus — the cable car is closed for refurbishment until around 2028. Walking up is possible if you have a few hours and the energy for a steep climb.

The main Rock attractions — St Michael's Cave, the Great Siege Tunnels, the Moorish Castle, and the macaque troops — are usually combined in a single Rock Tour (1.5–2 hours by taxi, longer on foot). Book the tour from the border or Cathedral Square and agree on price and inclusions before setting off.

Below the Rock, the Alameda Botanic Gardens at the southern end of Main Street offer a peaceful alternative — free to enter, with views across the bay. The Gibraltar Museum on Bomb House Lane covers the territory's history from Neanderthal finds to the Great Siege; the Moorish baths in the basement are among the best-preserved medieval baths in Europe.

Dolphin watching in the Strait is one of the most rewarding half-day activities. Three dolphin species are resident year-round; orcas pass through seasonally. Several operators run trips from the marina.
