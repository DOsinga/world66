---
title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Aegina rewards a slow pace. The [Temple of Aphaia](/europe/greece/aegina/temple_of_aphaia) is the main ancient site and one of the best-preserved Doric temples in Greece. The abandoned medieval town of [Paliachora](/europe/greece/aegina/paliachora) offers atmospheric wandering among ruined churches. The [harbour town of Aegina](/europe/greece/aegina/aegina_harbour) itself is pleasant for walking, eating, and watching the ferry traffic. Beaches are spread around the island — [Agia Marina](/europe/greece/aegina/agia_marina) on the east coast is the most developed; quieter coves are accessible by scooter or bus. Pistachio groves cover much of the interior, and every shop in town sells the local variety, considered among the finest in the world.
