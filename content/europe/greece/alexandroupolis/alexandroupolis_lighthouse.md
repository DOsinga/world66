The lighthouse is Alexandroupolis's visual anchor: a cylindrical stone tower near the port, built in the late Ottoman period and still standing over the waterfront. It is not a long visit, but it is the place where the city most clearly announces itself.

Come in the evening, when the promenade fills and the lighthouse looks less like an isolated monument and more like part of daily life.
