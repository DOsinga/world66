Tsepel Kagia Hill, also known as Mytero or Monastery Hill, is north of Avantas. The rocky hill has carved stone forms, traces of old structures, and murals linked with constellations, making it a worthwhile detour for travellers exploring the inland sights around Alexandroupolis.
