---
title: "Eating Out"
type: section
---

Ammoudia has a handful of family-run tavernas along the small harbour and on the road leading down to the beach. The cooking is straightforwardly Epirot: grilled river fish, fried whitebait, eel from the Acheron, lamb baked in a clay pot, and feta with watermelon. Most places open from late May to early October.
