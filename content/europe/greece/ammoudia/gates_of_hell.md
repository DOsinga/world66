The Gates of Hell mark the upper entrance to the Acheron Gorge, where the river narrows between steep green slopes before running toward the coast. The name comes from the river's mythic link with the underworld, but the visit is very much alive: cold water, short walks, and summer swimming in one of Epirus' most dramatic river landscapes.
