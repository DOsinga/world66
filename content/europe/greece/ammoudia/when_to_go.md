---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

The Ammoudia season runs from mid-May to early October. June and September are the sweet spots — warm sea, full taverna service, but none of the August crowding. July and August are hot; the wetland mosquitoes are also at their worst then, so bring repellent. The Acheron is at its best for swimming in July and August at Glyki, though the water remains cold all year.

Outside the season the village is largely closed; boats stop running, most tavernas shutter, and accommodation can be hard to find. The migratory bird-watching season at the delta peaks in March–April and September–October.
