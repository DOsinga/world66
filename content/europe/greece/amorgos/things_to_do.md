---
title: Things to Do
type: section
---

Amorgos rewards slow travel. The big draws are the Hozoviotissa monastery, the ancient ruins at Minoa near Katapola, and hiking the trail that runs the length of the island. Swimming is excellent at Agia Anna beach, a short walk down the cliff from Hozoviotissa. There is no nightlife to speak of, which is the point — this is an island for people who want to slow down.
