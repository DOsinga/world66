---
title: When to Go
type: section
---

June and September are the best months for Amorgos. The sea is warm, the crowds are manageable, and the heat is bearable for hiking — July and August can push 35°C, which makes outdoor activity uncomfortable in the middle of the day. Spring (April–May) is lovely for walking but the sea is still cold.

Winter (November–March) sees very few visitors and ferry services thin out dramatically. Some tavernas and accommodation close completely. This is not the time to come unless you specifically want solitude and don't mind limited services.

The island is popular with French visitors — a legacy of *Le Grand Bleu* — and feels noticeably less overrun than Mykonos or Santorini even in peak season, because it takes longer to get here.
