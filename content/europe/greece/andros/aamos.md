A beach bar on the sand at Batsi that manages to be relaxed without being sloppy. Sun loungers by day, cold Mythos and cocktails into the evening. The kitchen turns out light snacks — bruschetta, cold meats, grilled halloumi — well into the afternoon. The best position on the beach for watching the fishing boats come and go.
