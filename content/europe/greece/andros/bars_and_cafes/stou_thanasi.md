A traditional kafeneion on Chora's main plateia that has resisted every attempt to modernise it. The marble-topped tables and mismatched chairs have been here for generations, and the older men who play backgammon in the afternoons act accordingly. Greek coffee comes in a small copper briki, tsipouro in a water glass. The kind of place that makes the afternoon last.
