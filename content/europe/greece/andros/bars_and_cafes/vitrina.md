A small wine bar tucked into a side street off Chora's main pedestrian lane, with a considered list of Greek bottles — Assyrtiko from Santorini, Malagousia from the mainland, and several Cycladic labels you won't find elsewhere. The owner will talk you through the wines if you're willing. Opens at six in the evening and stays busy until midnight in summer. Good charcuterie and cheese boards to accompany.
