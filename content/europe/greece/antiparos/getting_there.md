---
title: "Getting There"
type: section
---

Antiparos is reached by ferry from [Paros](/europe/greece/paros), which in turn has regular connections to Athens (Piraeus) and the other Cyclades. The car ferry between Pounta on Paros's west coast and Antiparos village runs frequently throughout the day — the crossing takes about 10 minutes and runs year-round, though schedules thin out in winter. A second passenger-only ferry runs from Parikia, Paros's main port, several times daily in summer.

From Athens, the usual route is a 2–3 hour ferry from Piraeus to Paros, then the short hop across. In high summer, fast ferries can reduce the Piraeus–Paros journey to around 4 hours. Flying to Paros (from Athens, 45 minutes) and then taking the ferry is the quickest option from Athens but significantly more expensive.
