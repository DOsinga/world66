---
title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Antiparos is small enough to cover thoroughly in two or three days. The Cave of Antiparos is the main attraction and should not be missed; the Venetian kastro in Chora is compact but atmospheric; and the beaches on the south and west coasts are among the cleanest in the Cyclades. Mopeds are the right way to get around — the island's roads are narrow and the distances short.
