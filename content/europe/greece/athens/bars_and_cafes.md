---
title: "Bars and Cafes"
type: section
---

Athens has one of the best night-out scenes in Europe, and the city's bars and cafés are central to its social life. Athenians eat dinner late — 9pm at the earliest — and then drink into the small hours, particularly Thursday through Saturday.

For coffee by day, the cluster of cafés around **Kolonaki Square** and along the streets of **Plaka** are the social hubs. For drinks at sunset, the rooftop bars around **Monastiraki** — particularly **A for Athens** — give head-on views of the Acropolis. For serious nights out, the bar-and-music scene concentrates in **Psiri**, **Exarchia**, and **Gazi**. **Six d.o.g.s.** in Monastiraki is the standout venue for live music in a garden setting.

Greek beer is mostly average lager (Mythos, Alfa); the better choices are the local craft producers, like Septem. Wine by the glass has improved enormously since the 2010s — Greek varieties like Agiorgitiko and Assyrtiko are worth ordering. Ouzo and tsipouro are the classic local spirits, traditionally drunk with small mezedes during long evenings.
