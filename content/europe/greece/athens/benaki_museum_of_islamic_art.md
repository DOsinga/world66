Benaki Museum of Islamic Art is a branch of the Benaki Museum housed in two connected buildings near Kerameikos and Monastiraki. Its collection covers Islamic art across a wide geographic and historical range.

It is a strong stop for visitors who want Athens museums beyond ancient Greek material. The location makes it easy to combine with the Ancient Agora, Kerameikos, or a walk through Monastiraki and Thiseio.
