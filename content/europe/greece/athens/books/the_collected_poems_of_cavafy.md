Cavafy was Alexandrian, not Athenian, but his poems — compressed meditations on Greek history, desire, and the passage of time — are the essential literary companion to any serious engagement with Greece. "Waiting for the Barbarians," "Ithaka," and "The God Abandons Antony" give the ancient world an immediacy no prose history manages. The Daniel Mendelsohn translation is the best available in English.
