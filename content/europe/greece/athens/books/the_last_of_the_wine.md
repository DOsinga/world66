Set in Athens during the Peloponnesian War of the late 5th century BC, this novel follows Alexias, a young nobleman, and his friend Lysis through war, the gymnasia, the drinking parties, and the political catastrophe of Athens's defeat. Renault's Athens is physically vivid and morally complex — she understood the classical city in a way that classicists have repeatedly praised as uncanny. Reading it before climbing the Acropolis gives the ruins a human context that no guidebook can match.
