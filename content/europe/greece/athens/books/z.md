Vassilikos's 1966 novel fictionalises the assassination of the left-wing Greek politician Grigoris Lambrakis in Thessaloniki in 1963, an event that helped trigger the military coup of 1967 and seven years of dictatorship. The book — and Costa-Gavras's famous film adaptation — captures the Athens of political terror and resistance in a way that makes the modern city's democratic confidence feel more hard-won. Essential for understanding 20th-century Greece.
