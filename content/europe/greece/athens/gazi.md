Gazi takes its name from the **Technopolis** gasworks complex at its heart — a complete late-19th-century coal-gas plant converted into a cultural centre with exhibition spaces, performance halls, and a small museum. The brick chimneys and gas-holders are floodlit at night and dominate the surrounding district, which has become the centre of Athens's gay nightlife and a more general club-and-bar scene for younger Athenians.

The area lights up after midnight. By day, look for the gasworks itself, the cluster of independent restaurants along **Persefonis** and **Triptolemou**, and the Kerameikos archaeological site on the northern edge of the district. Connected to central Athens by the Kerameikos metro station.
