Kerameikos was the main cemetery of ancient Athens, lying just outside the city walls at the start of the road to Eleusis. The name comes from *keramos*, the potters' quarter that occupied the same district. It is one of the most atmospheric ancient sites in the city — and one of the least visited.

The site preserves a long stretch of the **Themistoclean Wall** built after the Persian Wars, two great gates (the Dipylon and the Sacred Gate), and several intact funeral monuments along the **Street of the Tombs**. The small **Kerameikos Museum** at the entrance holds the original carved grave stelae and offering vessels found on site. Combined ticket includes the Acropolis and the Ancient Agora.
