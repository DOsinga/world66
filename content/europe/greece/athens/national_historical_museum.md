The National Historical Museum occupies Athens' Old Parliament House and focuses on modern Greek history, especially the Greek War of Independence and the formation of the modern state. The collection includes documents, weapons, furniture, portraits, costumes, and other objects tied to political and military life.

The building is part of the visit: if the old assembly hall is open, it gives the museum a stronger sense of place than a standard display room.
