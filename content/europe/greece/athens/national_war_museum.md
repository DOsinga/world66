The National War Museum covers Greek military history, with displays inside and a more immediately engaging outdoor collection of tanks, artillery, and aircraft. The exhibits are traditional in style, but the museum is useful if you want a focused military-history stop near Kolonaki.

The outdoor equipment is the easiest part to visit casually, while the interior gives more context on the periods of Greek history the museum chooses to emphasize.
