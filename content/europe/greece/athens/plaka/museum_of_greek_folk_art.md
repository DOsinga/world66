The Museum of Greek Folk Art is a strong collection of everyday and ceremonial material culture, often missed by visitors focused only on the ancient sites around Plaka. Displays include tools, folk pottery, interior decoration, costume, embroidery, and other objects that show regional craft traditions.

It is especially rewarding for travellers interested in textiles and domestic culture, and it adds a useful later chapter to an Athens itinerary heavy on classical archaeology.
