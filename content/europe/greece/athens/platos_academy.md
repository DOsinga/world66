Plato's Academy is a low-key archaeological park in the northwest of Athens, marking the district associated with the philosophical school Plato founded in the 4th century BCE. The remains are fragmentary, but the name gives the ground a weight that the stones alone cannot carry.

This is not a substitute for the Acropolis, Agora, or Kerameikos. It is for travellers who like chasing the intellectual map of ancient Athens beyond the headline ruins. The park is open and local in feel, so bring context with you and treat it as a reflective walk.
