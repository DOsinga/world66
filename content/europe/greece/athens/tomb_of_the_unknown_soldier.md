The Tomb of the Unknown Soldier stands in front of the Hellenic Parliament on Syntagma Square. The hourly changing of the guard is the main draw: the Evzones wear ceremonial uniforms and move through a precise, slow ritual that makes the monument one of central Athens' easiest short stop.
