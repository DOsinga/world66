The main municipal market of Athens, operating since 1886 in a neoclassical building on Athinas Street. The interior is divided between the meat hall and the fish market, with vendors shouting prices in a language that seems older than Greek. Butchers sell whole carcasses and every offal imaginable; the fish stalls pack in 300 species at the height of season. Go before noon. The surrounding streets have excellent cheap tavernas serving market workers' lunches of offal soups and grilled meat.
