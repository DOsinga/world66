The Vorres Museum is a private museum in Paiania that combines modern Greek art, folk material, architectural fragments, and gardens. Its mix of contemporary collecting and traditional objects gives a more personal view of Greek culture than the central archaeological museums.

It requires a trip outside the main Athens tourist core, so it suits repeat visitors or art-focused itineraries. The reward is a quieter, layered museum where buildings and courtyards are part of the experience.
