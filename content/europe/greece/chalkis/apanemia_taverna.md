A long-running seafront fish taverna a short walk south of the old bridge, with tables that put you within metres of the water. Order whatever's on the day's chalkboard — grilled barbouni (red mullet), local octopus, sardines.

Lunch is the quieter, more relaxed time. Sundays book out. Service is brisk but warm in the established Greek tavern style.
