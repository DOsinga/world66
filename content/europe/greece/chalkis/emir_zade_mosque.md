A 15th-century Ottoman mosque a short walk inland from the old bridge, restored as a small monument and occasional exhibition space. The minaret is gone, but the prayer hall with its central dome and octagonal drum still gives a clean reading of the original architecture.

A quick visit, fifteen to twenty minutes, useful for a sense of the city's long Venetian-Ottoman-Greek layering. Opening hours are limited — check at the tourist office or the adjacent archaeological museum.
