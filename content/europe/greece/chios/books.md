**The Scene of the Crime** by Victoria Hislop is set partly in Chios and deals with the legacy of the 1822 massacre and its effects on subsequent generations. Hislop writes about Greece with genuine knowledge; this is a useful companion for anyone visiting the ossuary at Nea Moni.

**Massacre at Chios** — the primary historical account of the 1822 events is scattered across various histories of the Greek War of Independence; Philip Mansel's *Levant* (2010) provides good context for the broader Ottoman Aegean world of which Chios was part.

**The Mastic Tree and the Mastichochoria** — various short books in Greek and English are available locally in Chios and cover the mastic villages and their culture in more detail than most travel books reach.
