---
title: When to Go
type: section
---

The best months to visit Chios are May, June, September, and October — warm enough for swimming, not overcrowded, and the light is particularly good. July and August are hot (30–35°C) and the island gets busier, though it never reaches the intensity of Santorini or Mykonos.

The mastic harvest takes place from late August through October, when the trees are scored and the resin crystals collected by hand. Visiting a mastic village during the harvest gives a clearer sense of what the industry actually involves.

Easter is extraordinary if you are willing to deal with crowds — the Rouketopolemos rocket war in Vrontados is one of the most remarkable Easter traditions in Greece. Book accommodation months in advance.

Winter is quiet and some facilities close, but the island is never entirely shut. The weather is mild by Greek standards — winters are short, with occasional rain and temperatures rarely below 10°C.
