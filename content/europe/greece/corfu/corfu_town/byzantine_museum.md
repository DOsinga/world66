The Byzantine Museum is housed in the former Church of Our Lady of Antivouniotissa, a small historic church in Corfu Town. Its collection focuses on Byzantine and post-Byzantine icons, making it a useful stop for visitors interested in Corfu's Orthodox art and church history.
