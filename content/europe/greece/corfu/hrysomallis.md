A long-standing Corfu Town restaurant near the Old Fortress, known for serving traditional Corfiot dishes at honest prices. The menu includes the island's signature dishes: sofrito (veal in white wine and garlic sauce), stifado (braised beef with onions), bourdeto (fish stew with red pepper), and pastitsada (spiced meat with pasta). The waiters are relaxed and friendly, and the food is the real thing rather than tourist approximations.

Address: Nikiforou Theotoki 6, Corfu Town. Tel: +30 26610 30342. Open for lunch and dinner; closed some days in winter.
