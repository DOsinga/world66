The cafe culture around Lake Voulismeni is the social heart of Agios Nikolaos. Tables line the promenade on the lake's east side, and the morning routine of strong Greek coffee and a pastry while watching the fishing boats is one of the pleasures of the town. Evening drinks tend to migrate to the harbour and the marina bars.
