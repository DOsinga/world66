Agios Nikolaos offers the combination of a compact, walkable historic town and easy access to beaches, archaeological sites, and island excursions. The lake, the harbour, the museums, and the surrounding coastline are all accessible without a car, though a hire car opens up the eastern Lassithi plateau and the more remote beaches.
