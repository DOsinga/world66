Arkadi Monastery is one of Crete's most important historic monasteries, remembered for the 1866 uprising against Ottoman rule and the mass sacrifice that made it a symbol of Cretan resistance. The complex includes a church, museum, and courtyards on a plateau southeast of Rethymno.

It is still an active monastery, so dress and behave accordingly. The trip is most rewarding if you leave time for both the museum displays and the quiet setting outside the main buildings.
