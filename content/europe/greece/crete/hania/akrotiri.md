The Akrotiri Peninsula juts northeast from Hania into the Sea of Crete, enclosing the bay on which the city sits. It has several good beaches (Stavros beach is particularly fine, with calm clear water), three historic monasteries, and some excellent fish restaurants. The peninsula is where Hania airport sits, which can make approaching it by road slightly confusing.

The most impressive of the three monasteries is Moni Gouverneto, founded in the 16th century on a remote part of the peninsula, with a Catholic-influenced Venetian facade incongruous in an Orthodox church. Below Gouverneto, a path descends to the abandoned medieval Moni Katholiko — possibly the oldest monastery in Crete — carved into a cave above a dry gorge. The walk from Gouverneto to Katholiko and back takes about two hours on a good marked path.
