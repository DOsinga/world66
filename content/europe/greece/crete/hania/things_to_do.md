title: Things to Do
type: section
---

Hania rewards wandering. The old town is small enough to cover on foot, and much of the pleasure is in the walking itself — through the old [Jewish quarter (Evraiki)](/europe/greece/crete/hania/evraiki), along the [harbour front](/europe/greece/crete/hania/venetian_harbour), and through the Venetian neighbourhood of [Splantzia](/europe/greece/crete/hania/splantzia). Beyond the city, the [Samaria Gorge](/europe/greece/crete/hania/samaria_gorge) is the most famous day trip in all of Crete.

The [Etz Hayyim Synagogue](/europe/greece/crete/hania/etz_hayyim_synagogue) in the Evraiki is one of the most historically significant — and moving — sites in western Crete. The [Agora municipal market](/europe/greece/crete/hania/agora_municipal_market) is the best place to stock up on Cretan produce. For day trips, the [Akrotiri Peninsula](/europe/greece/crete/hania/akrotiri) has good beaches and old monasteries; [Paleochora](/europe/greece/crete/hania/paleochora) on the southwestern coast takes longer but is worth the drive.
