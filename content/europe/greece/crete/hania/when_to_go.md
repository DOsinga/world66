---
title: When to Go
type: section
---

The best months to visit Hania are May, June, and September–October. May and early June are ideal: the Samaria Gorge is freshly open (it opens in May), the wildflowers on the White Mountains are at their peak, the sea is warm enough to swim, and the crowds have not yet arrived. September and October are also excellent — the summer heat has broken, the sea is still warm, and the light is beautiful.

July and August are brutally hot (regularly above 35°C), crowded, and expensive. The Samaria Gorge is at its most congested. If you must go in summer, book accommodation well in advance and plan outdoor activities for early morning.

Winter (November–March) is mild by northern European standards but Hania is quiet — many restaurants and hotels close entirely. The White Mountains hold snow from December through April, creating occasionally spectacular scenery visible from the city. If you want the old town essentially to yourself, February is the off-season extreme.
