Hersonissos's real strength is as a base. Knossos and the archaeological museum in [Iraklion](/europe/greece/crete/iraklion) are 30 minutes west on the highway; the Lasithi plateau and the Dictaean Cave climb up into the mountains an hour south; the Venetian harbour at [Agios Nikolaos](/europe/greece/crete/agios_nikolaos) and the leper-colony island of [Spinalonga](/europe/greece/crete/spinalonga) are an hour east; the Minoan palace at [Malia](/europe/greece/crete/malia) is fifteen minutes along the coast. Almost every hotel can arrange a day-tour bus to any of these, but with a rental car the same loops are cheaper and more flexible. A useful counter-trip is a day inland to the village of Krasi, a short drive south, for its giant 2,000-year-old plane tree and a long lunch under the leaves.
