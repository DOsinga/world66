---
title: 'When to Go'
type: section
---

The resort is essentially a summer machine. The full season runs from late April to late October, with everything open and at full intensity from mid-June to mid-September. July and August are reliably hot (high twenties to mid-thirties Celsius), dry, and crowded; bookings need to be made well ahead, and prices peak. Late May, June and September are the sweet spot — water still warm, crowds thinner, prices lower, and the temperature manageable for walking the archaeological sites at midday.

Between November and March most of the strip closes down, hotels are shuttered, and only a handful of year-round tavernas in Piskopiano and at the harbour stay open for locals. Winters are mild but wet and many roads in the Cretan interior become more interesting; if you want a quiet shoulder-season visit, October is the last reliably warm month.
