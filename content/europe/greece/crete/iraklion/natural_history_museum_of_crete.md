The Natural History Museum of Crete presents the natural environment of Crete, Greece, and the eastern Mediterranean. It is a useful family-friendly museum in Iraklion, especially if you want context on the island's landscapes, wildlife, geology, and changing ecosystems before heading out beyond the city.
