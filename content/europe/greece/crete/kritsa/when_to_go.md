---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

Kritsa is worth visiting year-round, but **April, May, and October** are the best months. Spring brings wildflowers to the gorge and comfortable walking temperatures; October is warm and the summer crowds have thinned out. July and August are very hot — the gorge walk is uncomfortable in midday heat, and the village fills with day-trippers from the coastal resorts.

The church of Panagia Kera is open throughout the year but may close for a midday break in summer. Check current hours before making a special trip.
