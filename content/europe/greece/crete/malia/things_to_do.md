---
title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Malia's essential sight is the **Minoan Palace of Malia**, a large archaeological site east of town and one of Crete's major Minoan palaces. Nearby **Chryssolakkos** adds a smaller but intriguing burial-site stop for archaeology-minded visitors.

For sea and sand, **Potamos Beach** is the better choice if you want a little breathing room near the palace, while **Malia Beach** sits closer to the resort strip and nightlife. The tiny offshore chapel-islet of **Afentis Christos** is a good marker for a waterfront walk, and nearby **Stalida** and **Sisi** offer gentler resort alternatives along the coast.
