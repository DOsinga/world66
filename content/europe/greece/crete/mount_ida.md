Mount Ida, also known as Psiloritis, is the highest mountain on Crete and part of a UNESCO Global Geopark. It is a serious mountain outing rather than a casual stroll, with exposed terrain and unstable footing in places, but the summit rewards prepared hikers with wide views over central Crete.
