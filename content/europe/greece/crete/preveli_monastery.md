Preveli Monastery is one of southern Crete's more rewarding historic stops, with an active upper monastery and the ruins of the older lower monastery nearby. The setting above the coast is half the appeal.

The easier approach is by the wider road from Lefkogia. The narrower mountain road is scenic, but steep, tight, and awkward if you meet oncoming traffic.
