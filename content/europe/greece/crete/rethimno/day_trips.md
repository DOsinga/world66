Rethimno makes an excellent base for exploring western and central Crete. [Chania](/europe/greece/crete/hania) is 55 km west and arguably the most beautiful city in Crete, with its own Venetian harbour and old town that rewards a full day. [Heraklion](/europe/greece/crete/iraklion) to the east has the excellent Heraklion Archaeological Museum and provides access to [Knossos](/europe/greece/crete/knossos), the great Minoan palace. The Samaria Gorge — one of Europe's longest — is accessible from Chania as a day excursion.
