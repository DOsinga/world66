Eleftherna is an ancient city site in the hills south-east of Rethimno, with remains from Greek, Roman, and later periods. The nearby museum helps make sense of the excavations, including finds from the necropolis and public buildings. It is a good inland stop for travellers with a car who want archaeology beyond the better-known coastal towns.
