Rimondi Fountain is one of the small Venetian details that makes Rethimno's old town worth wandering slowly. Built by a local aristocratic family, it has three mask-shaped spouts set into a stone facade, with water still running from them.

It is a quick stop, but a good one if you are already moving between the harbour, Neradje Mosque, and the lanes around the old town.
