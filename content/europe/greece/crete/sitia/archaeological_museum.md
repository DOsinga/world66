The Archaeological Museum of Sitia is small but holds one of the most important regional collections on Crete, focused on the Minoan and post-Minoan finds of eastern Lasithi. The standout is the Palaikastro Kouros, but there are also fine Linear A tablets, Mochlos jewellery, and bronze figurines from Petras.

The building sits on the southern edge of town on the road to Ierapetra. Allow an hour. Closed Tuesdays. The labels are in Greek and English and the exhibits are well lit.
