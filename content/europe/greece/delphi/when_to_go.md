---
title: When to Go
type: section
---

Spring (April–May) and autumn (September–October) are the best times to visit Delphi. The temperatures are comfortable for walking around an outdoor archaeological site, the crowds are thinner than in summer, and the light on the hillside and the valley below is at its most atmospheric.

Summer (July–August) is hot, very crowded with tour groups, and expensive. The archaeological site has almost no shade and is brutal to walk in midday heat. If you must visit in summer, arrive at opening time (8am) and leave before noon. The mountains around Delphi are snowbound in deep winter and some roads can be icy; if you are coming from Athens, check conditions before setting out between December and February.
