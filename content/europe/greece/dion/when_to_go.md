---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

Spring (April–June) is the best time to visit Dion. The weather is mild, wildflowers are out across the plain, and the archaeological site is green rather than the parched ochre it turns in summer. Mount Olympus often retains its snow cap until May or June, which makes for dramatic backdrops.

Summer (July–August) is hot and busy, though Dion is rarely as crowded as the major southern Greek sites. The site offers little shade, so start early in the morning. The Greek cultural organisation OEPO occasionally uses the ancient theatre for summer performances — check local listings.

Autumn is pleasant for walking and the colours on the Olympus slopes are striking from September onwards. Winter visits are quiet but cold, and some facilities may have reduced hours outside the main season.
