The Cave of Maaras is a limestone cave near the Aggitis River, north-west of Drama. Its passages are known for stalactites, clear running water, and the damp green setting around the entrance.

It is a good short detour if you are exploring the caves, gorges, and small mountain villages around Drama. The appeal is natural rather than dramatic: cool air, water, stone, and a quiet break from the plains.
