The Dripstone Cave of Aggitis is a show cave northwest of Drama, near the village of Aggitis. An underground river runs through the cave, leaving visitors with a line of stalactites overhead rather than the usual mix of stalactites and stalagmites.

It works best as part of a day out in the Aggitis area, especially if you are already visiting the gorge or other natural sights around Drama.
