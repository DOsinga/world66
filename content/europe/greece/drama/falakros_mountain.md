Falakros Mountain rises north of Drama and is best known for its ski area, but the high-country views make it worth considering outside winter as well. In summer, the mountain is a scenic drive and walking area rather than just a sports venue.

It is most useful for travellers with a car who want a mountain break from the city and the surrounding plains.
