Aggitis Gorge is one of the strongest nature outings from Drama, where the river cuts between limestone walls that can make even a short trip feel adventurous. Local operators use the gorge for rafting and canoe-kayak routes when conditions are right.

The gorge pairs well with the nearby Aggitis cave area, so it is worth treating it as a full excursion rather than a roadside viewpoint. Check water levels and operator schedules locally, and do not underestimate the heat in the exposed parts of the valley.
