Petroussa Canyon cuts through the countryside north of Drama, offering a wilder outing than the town's central monuments and cafes. The appeal is the combination of rock walls, seasonal water, and a walking landscape that quickly feels removed from the plain.

It is a best-fit stop for travellers with a car or a local hiking plan, not a casual urban sight. Conditions can vary with weather and season, so treat it as a nature excursion: check access locally, bring sturdy footwear, and allow enough time to enjoy the canyon rather than just reach its edge.
