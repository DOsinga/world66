The small museum at the entrance to the archaeological site contains the most important finds from the sanctuary: architectural fragments from the Temple of Asklepios and the tholos, marble reliefs of Asklepios and his family, surgical instruments, and the iamata tablets recording miraculous cures.

The architectural display is particularly valuable for understanding the scale and character of the original sanctuary — the reconstructed pediment sculptures give a sense of what the main temple looked like. The museum is compact and not overwhelming; an hour is enough. Entry is included in the combined site ticket.
