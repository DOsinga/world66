---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

Epirus has a mountain climate: winters are cold and snowy in the highlands, summers are warm but not oppressive at altitude (though hot in the lower valleys). Spring (April–June) is the best time to visit — the wildflowers are extraordinary, the rivers are full, the Vikos Gorge hike is at its best, and the villages are coming back to life after winter.

Autumn (September–October) is almost as good: the beech and oak forests turn gold, the summer crowds are gone, and the weather is stable. Summer brings Greek domestic tourism to the Zagori villages and Ioannina; July and August are busy and some trekking routes get crowded on weekends.

The ski season at Metsovo (just east of Epirus in Thessaly) runs from December to March and attracts winter visitors. The Vikos Gorge trail is not recommended in winter due to ice and snow.
