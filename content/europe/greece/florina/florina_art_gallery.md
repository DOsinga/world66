The Florina Municipal Art Gallery holds a substantial collection of modern Greek painting, with a particular concentration of works by artists connected to the Florina region. The gallery has accumulated work through donations over several decades and presents a genuine survey of 20th-century Greek painting styles — from post-Impressionist landscapes to later figurative and abstract work.

Florina has produced several notable artists, and seeing work by painters who took their subjects from this mountain landscape is a different experience from the standard survey of the Athens art world. The gallery is in the town centre and well signed. Free entry on certain days; check locally for current hours.
