---
title: Things to Do
type: section
---

The Prespa lakes — Mikri Prespa and Megali Prespa — are the major draw and well worth the 45km drive from Florina. Mikri Prespa is the more accessible: the floating footbridge to Agios Achillios island and the ruins of the 10th-century basilica there make for a half-day's excursion. The birdwatching at the lake is excellent year-round — Dalmatian pelicans nest in the reedbeds and can often be seen fishing from the shore.

In Florina itself, the Municipal Art Gallery has a strong collection of modern Greek painting, including works by several Florina-born artists. The old town has a few Ottoman-era structures and a pleasant pedestrian centre around Pavlou Mela Street. The Vigla-Pisoderi ski resort on Mount Verno, 20km from town, operates from December to March with a good set of runs.

The bear sanctuary at Nymfeo village, about 40km northeast, is the only facility of its kind in Greece — rescued bears from illegal captivity are rehabilitated here in large forested enclosures.
