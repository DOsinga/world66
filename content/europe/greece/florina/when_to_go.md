---
title: When to Go
type: section
---

Spring (April–May) and early autumn (September–October) are the best times for the Prespa lakes — birdwatching is excellent during migration, temperatures are pleasant, and the roads through the mountains are reliably clear. The pelicans are at the lakes from early spring through late summer; chick-rearing peaks in May and June.

Summer is warm and Florina fills with domestic visitors escaping the coastal heat. The Prespa area can get busy in August, though it never feels crowded by Aegean island standards. Winter brings reliable snowfall; the Vigla ski resort typically operates from December to March, and the town takes on a quieter, local character. The drive to Prespa can be difficult in heavy snow — check conditions before heading out.
