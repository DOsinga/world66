The Byzantine Tower of Prosforio stands in the centre of Ouranoupoli, near the bus stop for Thessaloniki services. Built by monks from Mount Athos as a defensive tower, it is the town's main historic landmark and has a small museum inside. It is an easy stop before or after the Mount Athos boats.
