Castle of Neposi is a large ruined fortress in Halkidiki, with surviving walls from a fortified settlement used between late antiquity and the medieval period. The remaining circuit gives a sense of the site's former scale.

The ruins are best for visitors who enjoy archaeological landscapes and rough historic sites rather than restored monuments.
