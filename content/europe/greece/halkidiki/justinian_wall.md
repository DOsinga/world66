The Justinian Wall stands near the western end of the Nea Potidaia canal, where remnants of Potidea's old defences can still be seen. The 6th-century wall once stretched for about 1,200 metres and guarded the narrow approach to the Kassandra peninsula.

It is a quick historical stop, best paired with the canal rather than treated as a destination on its own.
