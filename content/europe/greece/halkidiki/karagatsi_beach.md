Karagatsi Beach is a quiet sandy cove on Ammouliani, with clear shallow water and a more natural feel than the busier beaches closer to the village. It works well for a lazy swim, especially if you want somewhere that still feels a little out of the way.

Access is easiest with a car or motorbike, and that extra effort is part of the appeal. Bring what you need for the day, then let the beach do the rest.
