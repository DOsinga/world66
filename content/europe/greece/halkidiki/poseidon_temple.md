The Poseidon Temple near ancient Mende is a fenced archaeological site, so the visit is mostly about seeing the outline of the ruins and understanding the setting. The earliest remains date back to the 11th century BC, and the surrounding coast helps explain why this was a meaningful sanctuary in antiquity.
