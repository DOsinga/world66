---
title: "Getting There"
type: section
---

Hydra has no airport. The only way in is by sea: **fast hydrofoil and catamaran services** run several times daily from Athens' main port, **Piraeus**, taking about 1 hour 50 minutes. The boats are operated by **Hellenic Seaways** and **Blue Star** and tend to be heavily booked in July and August — book ahead online.

There is no car ferry; cars are not allowed on Hydra anyway, so leaving the vehicle at Piraeus or Metochi (a small port on the mainland 5 km opposite Hydra, with a short water-taxi service) is the only option. Once on the island, donkeys and your own feet are the transport; small **water taxis** run to the beaches and to Kamini and Vlychos if you don't fancy the walk.
