The whole Epirus region is a day-trip destination from Ioannina. **Dodona** half an hour south is the easiest single excursion — the oracle of Zeus and its great theatre.

The **Zagori villages** north of Ioannina are the great prize: 46 stone-built villages scattered through high beech and oak forests, connected by old packhorse paths and arched stone bridges. **Megalo** and **Mikro Papigko**, **Monodendri**, **Kapesovo** and **Vradeto** are all small enough to spend a slow day in. The **Vikos Gorge**, between them, is one of the deepest in the world and one of Europe's classic walks.

Further afield, the mountain town of **Metsovo** sits on the pass to Thessaly and is famous for its wine, smoked cheese, and traditional Aromanian (Vlach) architecture. The monasteries of **Meteora** are a two-hour drive across the mountains. South toward the coast, the Roman ruins of **Nicopolis** near Preveza are worth a half-day stop.
