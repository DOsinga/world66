---
title: "Eating Out"
type: section
---

Ioannina's cuisine is Epirote — heavier on dairy, pies, and meat than the Aegean style. Look for **tyropita** and **kotopita** (cheese and chicken pies in flaky pastry), **gyros**, lamb on the spit, and the unusual house version of **pasties** stuffed with leeks. Trout, carp, eels and **batrachopodara** (frog legs) come from Lake Pamvotis itself.

The best fish restaurants are on Ioannina Island — **Pamvotis** and **Sotiris** both serve fresh-caught freshwater fish at lakeside tables. In the new town, **Stoa Louli** is the smarter end of Epirote cooking. For something cheaper, head to the **Mavili promenade** where a string of cafés and grill-houses face the lake. Local cheeses (**feta** and the harder **galotyri** of Epirus) and **tsipouro** (the Greek pomace brandy of choice in the north) are everywhere.
