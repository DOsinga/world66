---
title: "Getting Around"
type: section
---

Ioannina is small and the historic centre is best walked. The old castle district, the Mavili lakefront, and the central commercial streets are all within a 15-minute stroll of each other.

For the **island**, regular boats leave from the Mavili promenade — every half-hour in summer, hourly in winter. **Local buses** cover the new town and the surrounding suburbs, including the Perama Cave (15 minutes north). **Taxis** are plentiful at the central square and around the lake. For day trips into the **Zagori** villages or to **Dodona**, hiring a car is much easier than waiting for the limited rural buses.
