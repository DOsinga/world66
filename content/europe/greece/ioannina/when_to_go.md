---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

Ioannina is one of the wettest cities in Greece — the Pindos mountains catch Atlantic moisture and dump a lot of it here. Winters can be very cold by Greek standards, with snow possible from December to March; summer is hot but cooler than the islands thanks to the altitude (470 m).

**Late spring (May–June)** and **early autumn (September–October)** are the best times: warm, dry, with wildflowers in the Zagori or the first autumn colours. **Summer** is good for hiking in the high country but the city can hit 35°C. **Winter** is bleak but atmospheric — mist over the lake, the mountains snow-covered, the cafés full of locals. Hikers should avoid the high passes between November and May.
