The Odysseas Elytis Open-Air Theatre is a beautiful small amphitheatre between the port of Yialos and Chora, designed in the classical Greek style by architect Peter Haupt. It seats a few hundred people in a natural hollow with the Aegean as backdrop, and hosts concerts, theatrical performances, and cultural events during summer.

The theatre is a surprise on an island better known for its beach bars — evidence that Ios has more cultural ambition than its party reputation suggests. It is open during the day for anyone to walk around when no event is scheduled. The setting alone — carved stone seats, the blue sea, and the silhouette of Chora above — makes it worth a visit.
