---
title: "Eating Out"
type: section
---

Ithaca's food is what you would expect from a quiet Ionian island — simple, fish-led, and reliant on the day's catch. Almost every taverna on the island is family-run, and the menu hardly varies from one to the next: grilled fish, grilled meats, *horiatiki* salad, fried vegetables, and the local *savoro* (fried fish in a vinegar-and-rosemary sauce) when in season.

**Sirines** in Vathy is a long-standing favourite for fish dishes on a quiet side street. **Trehantiri** on the Vathy waterfront does grills and seafood with view of the harbour. **Mythos Restaurant** in Kioni is the established choice on the north coast, with traditional Ithacan cooking and a small garden. In Frikes, **Penelope and Telegonos** is the natural lunch stop.

For something cheaper, the small village of Stavros has a couple of unpretentious tavernas around the square where locals eat. Avoid eating only on the Vathy harbour front — the views are good but the prices reflect that.
