---
title: "Getting Around"
type: section
---

Kalamata is a manageable city on foot or by bicycle for the central areas. The beach strip, old town, and most attractions are within easy walking distance of each other.

**On Foot**

The old town around the castle and the main commercial street, Aristomenous, are compact and very walkable. The seafront promenade runs along the entire beach — a pleasant 2-3 km walk from the port area to the far end of the beach.

**By Taxi**

Taxis are the most convenient way to reach the airport, the port, or destinations outside the centre. They are plentiful and reasonably priced by Western European standards.

**By Local Bus**

KTEL local buses connect the centre with the suburbs and surrounding villages. For most visitor needs, walking and taxis cover everything adequately.

**By Car**

A car is very useful for day trips to the Mani peninsula, Mystras, Olympia, and other Peloponnese sites. Car rental is available at the airport and in the city centre.
