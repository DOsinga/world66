Kalamata's Military Museum covers Greek military history from the War of Independence in 1821 through the modern period. The collection includes uniforms, weapons, documents, and military material.

Some armoured vehicles are displayed in the courtyard, making the museum a compact but useful stop for visitors interested in modern Greek history.
