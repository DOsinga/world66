The Temple of the Visitation of the Saviour is a 19th-century Byzantine-style church in Kalamata. It is dedicated to the city's protector and stands out for its formal church architecture rather than for a long museum-style visit.

It is worth a short stop while walking through central Kalamata, especially for visitors interested in local religious landmarks and the city's 19th-century built heritage.
