The Natural History Museum of Meteora and Mushroom Museum is an odd but useful indoor stop in Kalambaka, especially when weather interrupts monastery visits. Its displays cover local fauna and a surprisingly detailed world of mushrooms, from identification to food culture.

It will not compete with Meteora’s cliffs and monasteries, but that is not the point. The museum adds a natural-history layer to a trip that can otherwise become purely architectural and religious, and it is easy to fit into an afternoon in town.
