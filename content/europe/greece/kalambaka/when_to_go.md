---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

Spring (April–May) and autumn (September–October) are the best times to visit. The weather is mild, the light is soft, and the crowds are manageable. Wildflowers colour the hillsides around the rocks in April, and the monastery opening hours are at their most generous. Summer brings serious heat — July and August temperatures regularly exceed 35°C — and coachloads of day-trippers. The monasteries can feel rushed and overcrowded from late June through August.

Winter (December–February) has its own appeal. Snow falls on the rocks occasionally, producing dramatic photographs, and the monasteries are nearly empty. Some are closed on certain weekdays, so check schedules in advance. The train and bus connections from Thessaloniki and Athens run year-round, making Kalambaka accessible in any season.
