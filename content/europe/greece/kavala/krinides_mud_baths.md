The Krinides mud baths, listed locally as Lasponera or Pilotherapia, are a traditional wellness stop
west of Kavala. Visitors come for the warm clay and mineral-rich mud, a therapeutic practice tied to
the natural deposits around the site rather than a conventional spa interior.

It is a niche attraction, but a memorable one if you are curious about local bathing traditions.
Combine it with Philippi or the Krinides area rather than making a special trip from Kavala only for
the mud baths.
