Kavala's Municipal Tobacco Warehouse is a reminder of the city's tobacco-trading years. Built in the early 20th century by a Turkish tobacco merchant, the building mixes Ottoman and neoclassical details in a style that stands out from the surrounding streets.

The restored warehouse now belongs to the city and is used for exhibitions and events, including maritime history displays. It is a useful stop for understanding why tobacco shaped Kavala's modern growth.
