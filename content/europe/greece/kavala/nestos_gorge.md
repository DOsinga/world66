Nestos Gorge is one of the best nature excursions from Kavala, where the Nestos River cuts a broad, green corridor between Macedonia and Thrace. The landscape combines cliffs, riverside forest, sandy banks, and slow bends that can be explored on foot, by kayak, or on gentle rafting trips depending on season and operator.

It is not a single viewpoint but a small outdoor region, so the best visit depends on how active you want to be. Walkers can follow riverside paths for gorge views, while guided kayak trips give a fuller sense of the water and wildlife. In hot weather, the river makes a welcome break from the coast.
