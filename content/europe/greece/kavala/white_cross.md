The White Cross above Kavala is a simple but rewarding viewpoint, looking down over the amphitheater-like city, its harbor, and the water beyond. It is not a complex monument, but the climb or drive gives a good sense of Kavala's steep geography.

Visit for the view rather than the structure itself. It pairs well with the old town and aqueduct, especially near sunset when the city terraces catch the light.
