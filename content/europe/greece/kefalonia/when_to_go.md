---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

Kefalonia's best months are May, June, September, and October — warm enough to swim, not so hot as to be uncomfortable, and with fewer crowds and lower prices than July and August. May is particularly good: the island is green, the flowers are out, and most tourist facilities are open but not overwhelmed.

July and August are peak season: temperatures reach 35°C or more, accommodation prices double or triple, and the best beaches are busy. If you visit in high summer, arrive early at popular beaches like Myrtos and plan the Melissani Cave visit for before 10am or after 3pm.

November to April is quiet. The island is largely closed to tourism — most tavernas and accommodation in the resort areas shut entirely. The weather is mild by northern European standards but can be wet and grey.
