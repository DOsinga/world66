---
title: When to Go
type: section
---

Lamia has a typical central Greek continental climate: hot, dry summers and cold winters. The surrounding mountains collect significant snowfall between December and February, and the valley can be bitterly cold. Summer temperatures regularly exceed 35°C.

Spring (April–May) and autumn (September–October) are the best times to visit. The heat has not yet set in or has passed, the hills around town are green, and Thermopylae is pleasant to walk around. Avoid high summer if you dislike the heat; the castle hill is fully exposed and there is little shade.

The city celebrates the anniversary of the Battle of Alamana — a local engagement in the Greek War of Independence — on 23 April, with events around the Plateia Diakos. The annual carnival in February is a lively affair by regional standards.
