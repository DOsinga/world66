---
title: "Bars and Cafes"
type: section
---

Larissa has a disproportionately active cafe and bar scene for a city of its size, driven by the large university population. Plateia Laou is surrounded by cafes that operate from breakfast through the early hours. The streets north of the square, toward the river, have a concentration of bars. Greeks in Larissa tend to linger — a coffee here means at least an hour.
