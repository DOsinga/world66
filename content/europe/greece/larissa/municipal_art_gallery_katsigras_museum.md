The Municipal Art Gallery - G I Katsigras Museum holds one of Greece's stronger provincial art collections, focused on modern Greek painting from the 19th and 20th centuries. It is a useful counterpoint to Larissa's archaeological sights, especially for visitors interested in Greek culture beyond antiquity.
