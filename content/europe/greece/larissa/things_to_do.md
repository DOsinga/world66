---
title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Larissa's historic centre concentrates several thousand years of civilization into a short walking radius. The two ancient theatres on Frourio Hill, the Ottoman monuments from four centuries of Turkish rule, and the Diachronic Museum all repay careful attention. The city is also one of the most pleasant places in central Greece to simply wander — its squares and river walk have a genuine local character undiluted by tourism.
