---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

Larissa has a continental climate moderated by proximity to the sea — hot dry summers and mild winters. Spring (April to June) and autumn (September to November) are the most comfortable seasons, with temperatures in the 18–26°C range and reliable sunshine. Summer heat can be intense — the Thessalian plain is one of the hottest parts of Greece in July and August, with temperatures regularly exceeding 38°C. Winter is mild by central European standards but can be grey and wet.

The city is worth avoiding during the August holiday period, when many shops and restaurants close as locals leave for the islands. Spring is ideal: the Thessalian plain is green from winter rains, wildflowers are out on the hillsides, and the city is at full social tempo before the summer heat sets in.
