---
title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Plomari's main attraction is its ouzo culture — visit the [Barbayiannis Distillery and Museum](/europe/greece/lesbos/plomari/barbayiannis_distillery) to learn how the spirit is made and sample the goods. The [old town](/europe/greece/lesbos/plomari/plomari_old_town) of crumbling neoclassical mansions and former tanneries is worth a wander. The [harbour](/europe/greece/lesbos/plomari/plomari_harbour) has fishing boats and seafood tavernas. The beach at [Agios Isidoros](/europe/greece/lesbos/plomari/agios_isidoros), a few kilometres east, is the best swimming spot nearby.
