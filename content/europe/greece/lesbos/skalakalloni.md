Kalloni is a quiet market town about 40 km west of [Mytilini](/europe/greece/lesbos/mytilini), sitting at the intersection of most of the island's bus routes. The beach resort of Skala Kalloni lies about 3 km south of the old town at the head of the [Gulf of Kalloni](/europe/greece/lesbos/skalakalloni/gulf_of_kalloni_birding). The long, gently shelving [beach](/europe/greece/lesbos/skalakalloni/skala_kalloni_beach) is one of the better options on Lesbos for families with young children — the water is shallow and warms quickly in summer.

The gulf and its surrounding wetlands are famous among birdwatchers across Europe: flamingos, spoonbills, waders, and rare migrants gather here in spring numbers that have made Lesbos one of the top birding destinations in the eastern Mediterranean. The main sight in the area is [Limonos Monastery](/europe/greece/lesbos/skalakalloni/limonos_monastery), about 7 km away, a substantial 16th-century complex with a good ecclesiastical museum and a fine old library. Other attractions nearby include the [Temple of Aphrodite at Mesi](/europe/greece/lesbos/skalakalloni/temple_of_aphrodite_mesi), about 14 km east on the southern shore of the gulf.
