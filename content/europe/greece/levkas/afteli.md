Afteli is a small bay beach with clear green water on Lefkada's quieter eastern side. The beach itself is short, so it feels intimate rather than spacious, and it is best for swimming rather than long lazy walks.

Come early if you are driving. Parking is tight and the approach road is narrow enough to make larger cars awkward, but the water is lovely once you are down at the bay.
