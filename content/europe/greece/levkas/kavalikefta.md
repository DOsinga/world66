Kavalikefta is a small pebble beach on Lefkada's west coast, known for clear water and tall rocks rising from the sea. It is not a broad, easy resort beach, but that is part of the appeal: the setting feels raw and compact.

The road down is narrow and awkward, so allow time and do not rush the drive. A small beach bar and basic facilities make it workable for a short stop, especially if you are already exploring the island's western beaches.
