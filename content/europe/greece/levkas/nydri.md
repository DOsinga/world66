The main resort town of the sheltered east coast, sitting opposite a cluster of small wooded islands. Nydri is busy, touristy, and not especially pretty in itself — a strip of tavernas, bars, and boat-trip touts along the seafront — but it is the departure point for the best boat trips on the island.

Day cruises run to **Meganisi**, the long green island just offshore, to **Skorpios** (once owned by Aristotle Onassis and the site of his and his daughter Christina's graves), and to the tiny islet of **Madouri**, where the 19th-century Greek national poet Aristotelis Valaoritis had his estate.
