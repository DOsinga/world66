---
title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Lefkada divides naturally into three: the rebuilt capital with its quirky seismic-proof architecture, the spectacular west coast beaches under towering chalk cliffs, and the green mountainous interior with traditional hill villages. Most visitors come for the beaches, but the island rewards anyone willing to drive the back roads — Lefkada is more wooded and more rural than its better-known neighbours Corfu and Kefalonia.
