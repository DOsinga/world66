title: Things to Do
type: section
---

Marathon's main draws are its ancient battlefield sites and the excellent [Archaeological Museum](/europe/greece/marathon/archaeological_museum_of_marathon). The [Tumulus of Marathon](/europe/greece/marathon/marathon_tumuli) — the burial mound of the fallen Athenians — is the most evocative site, a quiet grassy dome on the coastal plain where the battle was fought. The beach at [Schinias](/europe/greece/marathon/schinias_beach) makes a natural end to a history-focused visit. The area can be covered in a comfortable half-day from Athens, or a full day if you add the ruins at Rhamnus to the north.
