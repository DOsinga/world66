The Gate of Hadrian is a Roman marker in the ancient city, built to honour Hadrian's visit in 124-125 AD. It is a brief stop, but it adds an imperial layer to a site better known for its Greek and Thracian past.

Pair it with the theatre and nearby wall remains rather than treating it as a stand-alone destination.
