The West Wall of ancient Ismara is one of the ruins that makes Maroneia feel like a landscape rather than a single fenced site. The remains sit among olive trees, reached by rough local roads that are part of the experience.

Go only with a sensible vehicle and enough daylight. The reward is not polished interpretation, but the scale of an ancient city whose defensive line once ran from coast to hill.
