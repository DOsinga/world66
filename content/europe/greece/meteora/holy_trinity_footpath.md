The Holy Trinity footpath is one of the best short hikes in Meteora because it lets you approach the monastery from below rather than arriving by road. The path climbs from Kalambaka through the lower slopes and rock shadows toward Agia Triada, with the town dropping behind you and the sandstone towers closing in above.

It is a real uphill walk, not a casual pavement detour, though much of the route is established and easy to follow in dry weather. Start early in summer, carry water, and check monastery opening hours before committing to the climb. Even if you do not enter the monastery, the route gives a far better feel for Meteora's landscape than a quick viewpoint stop.
