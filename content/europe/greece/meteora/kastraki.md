The village of Kastraki sits directly at the base of the Meteora rocks, smaller and quieter than Kalambaka, with the sandstone pillars rising almost from the end of the main street. It is the better base for visitors who want to be close to the monasteries: several of the walking paths between the monasteries start from Kastraki, and the position means you are already partly up the rocks without having taken any transport.

The village has guesthouses, a few tavernas serving standard Greek food, and a pleasant low-key atmosphere. The main reason to stay here rather than Kalambaka is proximity: you can walk to the nearest monasteries, and the view of the rocks from the village itself — particularly at sunset and at dawn, before the tour buses arrive — is remarkable. The cliffs change colour through the day as the light shifts.
