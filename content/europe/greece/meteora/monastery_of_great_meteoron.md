The Monastery of Great Meteoron (Megalo Meteoro) is the largest and highest of the Meteora monasteries, sitting at 613 metres above sea level on the broadest of the sandstone pillars. Founded in the 1340s by the monk Athanasios Meteorites and greatly expanded under the Serbian king Stephen Uroš IV Dušan, it became the spiritual and administrative centre of the entire Meteora monastic community.

The monastery church, the Transfiguration of Christ, dates from the 14th century and contains some of the finest Byzantine frescoes in Greece. The old refectory houses a museum with Byzantine icons, manuscripts, and religious objects from across the Meteora monasteries. The view from the monastery terrace — straight down the sheer cliff face to the Thessalian plain 300 metres below — is the most vertiginous in all of Meteora.
