The Monastery of the Holy Trinity (Agia Triada) stands alone on a pinnacle separate from the main cluster of Meteora rocks, reached by 140 steps cut through a tunnel in the stone. Founded in the 14th or 15th century, the monastery has a small church, an ossuary, and a museum of manuscripts and portable icons. The isolation is total: on three sides the rock drops away to nothing.

The walk up from the road below takes about 20 minutes and is the most dramatic approach of all the Meteora monasteries. The summit view is exceptional — you look across to the other monasteries on their rocks, with the plain of Thessaly spreading in every direction below. Agia Triada is less visited than Great Meteoron or Varlaam, partly because of its position, which makes it one of the more rewarding ones to include in an itinerary.
