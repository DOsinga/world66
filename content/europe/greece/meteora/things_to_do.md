---
title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

The six active monasteries are the heart of any visit to Meteora, but the rock formations themselves — massive sandstone pillars thrown up by tectonic activity and then carved over millions of years by water and wind — are extraordinary in their own right. Walking the road that loops between the monasteries gives you the best sense of scale; the views across the Thessalian plain from the top are memorable.

Dress modestly for monastery visits: covered shoulders and knees are required at all of them, and most lend sarong-style wraps at the entrance. Photography is permitted outside; inside varies by monastery. Opening hours and days differ for each monastery, so check ahead to avoid arriving at a closed gate.
