---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

Spring (April to June) and autumn (September to October) are the best times to visit Meteora. The weather is mild, the light is beautiful, and the tour groups — which arrive in force from mid-June to August — are absent. In spring, wildflowers cover the slopes around the rocks and the air is clear after the winter rains. In autumn the colours in the Thessalian plain below are remarkable and the light takes on a golden quality in the afternoons.

Summer (July and August) brings intense heat and very large crowds, particularly in August. The monasteries fill with tour buses and the road loop becomes congested. If you must visit in summer, arrive early morning or stay for the evening. Winter is quiet and the rocks often emerge through morning mist in a way that makes for extraordinary photographs, but some monasteries reduce their hours or close entirely between November and March.

The monasteries operate on a rotation system — not all are open every day. Generally each is closed one or two days per week, but different monasteries are closed on different days, so you can see two or three on any given day if you plan the circuit. Check current schedules before you arrive.
