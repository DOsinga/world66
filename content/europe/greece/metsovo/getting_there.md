---
title: "Getting There"
type: section
---

The fastest way in is **by car**. Metsovo sits beside the **Egnatia Odos motorway** (A2) about an hour east of Ioannina and an hour west of Kalambaka (for Meteora). The motorway tunnels straight through the Pindos massif; turn off at the Metsovo exit and climb the last few kilometres on the old road. From Thessaloniki the drive is around 2.5 hours; from Athens via Trikala it is about 5.

**By bus**, regular KTEL coaches run from **Ioannina** (about 1 hour) and from **Trikala / Kalambaka** (about 1.5 hours). There are also one or two daily services from Thessaloniki. The bus drops you in the centre, by the square.

There is no train. The nearest airport is **Ioannina** (small, mostly domestic flights from Athens), with **Thessaloniki** and **Preveza** the next nearest options.
