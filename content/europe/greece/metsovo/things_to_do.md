---
title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Metsovo is small enough to walk in an hour, but rewards a slower visit. The two essential stops are the **Averoff Gallery** for nineteenth- and twentieth-century Greek painting, and the **Tossizza Folklore Museum** for a vivid reconstruction of how a well-off Metsovite family lived. The central **Platía** with its plane tree and the surrounding lanes of slate-roofed houses are themselves worth a couple of leisurely passes.

Outside town, the **Katogi Averoff winery**, the small **Karakoli ski resort** and the surrounding **Pindos forest** all repay a half-day excursion. The Katara Pass road up to the watershed, with its view east into Thessaly, is one of the most dramatic short drives in mainland Greece.
