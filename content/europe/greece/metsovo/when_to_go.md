---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

Metsovo has a four-season climate, and each season is very distinct. **Winter (December–March)** is cold, snowy and beautiful: the slate roofs whitened with snow, wood smoke rising from every chimney, Greek skiers up at the small **Karakoli resort** above town. Hotels are full at weekends and over Greek New Year; book ahead.

**Spring (April–June)** is the prettiest season — the wildflowers in the Pindos meadows are extraordinary, the hiking is just opening up, and the town is quiet. **Summer (July–August)** brings most of the international tourists; the temperatures stay pleasantly cool (mid-20s in the daytime), and weekends can be busy, especially over Greek holidays.

**Autumn (September–November)** is excellent: the beech forests on the surrounding hills turn an extraordinary copper, hunting season opens, and the kitchens of the village come back into their own.
