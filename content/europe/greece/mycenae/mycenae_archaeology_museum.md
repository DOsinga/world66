The Mycenae Archaeology Museum sits beside the ancient citadel and helps make sense of the ruins before or after you walk the site. The gold objects are copies of the famous finds now in Athens, but the smaller original artifacts are often more useful for understanding daily life, burial customs, and religion at Mycenae.

Do not skip it as just an add-on to the Lion Gate and palace walls. The museum gives context to a site where many of the most important buildings survive only as foundations.
