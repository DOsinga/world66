One of several bars perched on the waterfront at Little Venice, 180° is named for its panoramic view across the water toward the windmills — the same sunset vista that Mykonos Town is famous for. Cocktails are expensive even by Mykonos standards, but the setting at the right moment, with the light turning the whitewashed walls orange, makes the price feel more justifiable. Arrive 30–45 minutes before sunset to get a seat with a direct sea view; by sunset itself the bar is packed and the sightlines are gone.
