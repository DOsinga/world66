An open-air club perched on a cliff above Paradise Beach, considered one of the best venues in the world for electronic music. Cavo Paradiso hosts internationally known DJs through the summer season, with the dance floor facing the sea and the music carrying across the bay. The setting — a terrace cut into the hillside with views of the water below — is spectacular, and the sound system operates at festival-grade quality.

Events typically start late (midnight or later) and run until well after dawn. The crowd is serious about dancing. Tickets are sold in advance for headline nights and sell out. A covered indoor section runs during bad weather, but the whole point is the outdoor experience.
