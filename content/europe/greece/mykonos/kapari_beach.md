A small, rocky nudist beach on the western side of Mykonos, accessible on foot from Little Venice in about 15 minutes by following the coastal path. The beach is tiny and natural — no facilities, no sun beds — and the water is exceptionally clear. It faces west and catches the last of the afternoon light.

Kapari is a short walk but a world away from the organised beach clubs. The path from town is not signposted but is well-trodden; ask at the waterfront for directions. Bring everything you need and leave no trace.
