The Akronafplia is the older, lower citadel directly behind the old town — the original Nafplio, fortified continuously since Bronze Age times and built over by Byzantines, Franks, Venetians and Ottomans in succession. It is humbler than Palamidi, lower and largely ruined, but it is the historical core of the town and the walls and gatehouses are visible from the streets below.

Most of the headland is accessible on foot via a free lift from the western end of the old town or by following the path from the Arvanitia promenade. The complex is partly occupied by a former hotel (the old Xenia Palace) — long closed and somewhat derelict — but the public viewpoints give the best perspectives over the harbour and the Bourtzi.
