Antica Gelateria di Roma is, by reputation and by repeated taste-test, the best gelato in Greece. The shop has been run by the same Italian family — the Rafos — since 1870 in their original Rome location and from a small premises on the corner of Pharmacopoulou and Komninou in Nafplio since 1995. They make about thirty flavours daily, all from natural ingredients, with the fruit sorbets especially good.

It is small, it gets crowded, the queue moves fast. Take your cup or cone around the corner to the waterfront and eat it walking. Cash preferred.
