Nafplio is the best base in the eastern Peloponnese for day trips, and the three great archaeological sites of the Argolid are all within an hour by car.

**Mycenae** — half an hour north, the Bronze Age palace-fortress that gave its name to the entire pre-Greek Mycenaean civilisation. The Lion Gate, the beehive Treasury of Atreus, the shaft graves where Schliemann found the gold mask he claimed was Agamemnon's. Allow half a day with the site museum.

**Epidaurus** — 40 minutes east, with one of the great surviving ancient theatres anywhere in the world. Acoustically perfect, still used for performances on summer Saturday nights as part of the Athens & Epidaurus Festival.

**Tiryns** — five minutes out of town on the road to Argos. Smaller than Mycenae, with the most impressive cyclopean walls in Greece. An hour is enough — combine with Mycenae on the same trip.

**The Mani** — much further (about two hours south), but Nafplio works as a base for an overnight loop including the tower-house villages and dramatic coast of inner Mani.

**Nemea** — the wine country 30 km north-west, with the small ancient stadium of the Nemean Games and a dozen excellent boutique wineries open for tastings.
