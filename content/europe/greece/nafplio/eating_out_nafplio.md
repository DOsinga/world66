Nafplio eats well. The Argolid plain produces excellent vegetables and citrus, the bay supplies fresh fish, and the *retsina* and *agiorgitiko* from nearby Nemea travel about three minutes from vineyard to glass. The old town has dozens of *tavernas* in the streets and small squares behind Syntagma, and on a summer evening tables spill into the lanes for blocks.

Among the better-known names: Aiolos and Mitato (modern Greek done thoughtfully), Omorfo Tavernaki (a classic family taverna up the steps), Vasilis (cheap, busy, excellent for grills) and Karamanlidika (Anatolian-Greek refugee cuisine). For lunch, the fish tavernas on the waterfront serve straight from the morning catch. Reservations help in summer.
