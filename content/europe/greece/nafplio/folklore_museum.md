The Peloponnesian Folklore Foundation Museum is the best small museum in Nafplio and one of the best ethnographic museums in Greece. Spread across four floors of a restored mansion in the old town, it covers traditional costume, textile, jewellery and household objects from across the Peloponnese, with particular strength in the embroidered wedding costumes of the Argolid and the Mani.

The labels are bilingual Greek and English and the collection is exceptionally well-displayed. Allow an hour. A small shop on the ground floor sells good-quality woven and embroidered souvenirs at sensible prices.
