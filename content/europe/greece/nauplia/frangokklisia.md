Frangokklisia is one of Nafplio's oldest churches, with layers of Catholic and Orthodox history visible in a compact old-town setting. It preserves traces of an earlier Franciscan monastery, making it a useful stop for visitors following the town's Venetian, Ottoman, and Greek religious history through its back streets.
