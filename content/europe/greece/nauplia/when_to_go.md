---
title: When to Go
type: section
---

Spring (April to mid-June) is the perfect time — the wildflowers cling to the Palamidi cliffs, the cafes are open but not packed, and the temperature is right for walking up to the fortress. September and early October are nearly as good, with warm sea and an end-of-summer feeling.

Avoid August unless you book early; the Athenians arrive in force for their own holidays and the harbour cafes can be hard to get a table in. Winter is quiet but charming; some tavernas close but the town keeps living. The Mediterranean Carnival celebrations in February are the loudest event of the year.
