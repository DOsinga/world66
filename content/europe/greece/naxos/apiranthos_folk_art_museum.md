The Apiranthos Folk Art Museum is a small village museum arranged to look like the interior of an 18th- or 19th-century Naxos house. Its three rooms show domestic objects, textiles, and everyday material culture from the mountain villages.

It is a short visit, best combined with a walk through Apiranthos itself. The museum adds useful context to the village's stone lanes and older houses.
