This small Roman Catholic cathedral sits inside the old Kastro quarter, where Naxos still carries traces of its Venetian period. The draw is not scale but detail: family crests, painted capitals, and the odd feeling of a Latin church tucked into a Cycladic island town.

It works best as part of a slow walk through the Kastro, especially if you are already visiting the archaeological museum or the narrow lanes around the castle.
