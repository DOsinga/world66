The Temple of Dionysus at Yria is one of the archaeological sites that shows Naxos beyond beaches and the Portara. The remains mark a sanctuary associated with Dionysus, whose cult had particular importance on the island, and the site helps connect Naxos to the wider religious world of the ancient Cyclades.

Do not expect towering ruins; the appeal is quieter and more contextual. It works best for travellers already interested in archaeology or those exploring inland Naxos, where scattered temples, villages, quarries, and agricultural landscapes give the island a deeper rhythm than the waterfront alone.
