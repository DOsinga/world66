This small museum is dedicated to Iakovos Kambanelis, the Naxos-born playwright, poet, lyricist, and novelist whose work shaped modern Greek theatre. It is a good short stop if you are already walking through Naxos Town and want a cultural counterpoint to the island's archaeological sights.
