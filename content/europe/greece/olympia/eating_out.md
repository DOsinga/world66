---
title: Eating Out
type: section
tags: [eating_out]
---

The village of Ancient Olympia has a strip of tavernas along its main street catering mainly to day-trippers. The food is reliably Greek — grilled meats, salads, local wine — and prices are reasonable given the tourist location. For better cooking, head to the nearby town of Pyrgos or eat at your hotel. Arrive early or late for lunch to avoid the midday rush when tour buses disgorge their passengers.
