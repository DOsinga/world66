---
title: "Beaches"
type: section
---

Paros has beaches for every mood. The southeast coast around Golden Beach is exposed to the meltemi wind that blows hard from the north in summer — a wind that draws windsurfers from across Europe. The north coast near Naoussa has calmer, clearer water. Kolymbithres, with its extraordinary granite rock formations, is unlike anything else in the Cyclades.
