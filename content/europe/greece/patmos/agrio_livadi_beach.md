Agrio Livadi Beach is a relaxed Patmos beach with views toward the small islets off the coast. It is an easy place for a swim and a slow afternoon when you want something less built around sightseeing.

The beach pairs naturally with the nearby Rock of Kalikatsou and the villages around Grikou Bay.
