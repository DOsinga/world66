The Municipal Gallery of Patras is a small but worthwhile art stop in the city center, housed near the Old Town Hall and the municipal library. Its collection focuses on modern Greek painting, including works by artists connected with Patras and western Greece.

It is best treated as a short cultural visit while walking around Georgiou I Square and the old center. The gallery adds useful depth to a Patras itinerary that can otherwise lean heavily on churches, squares, and carnival history.
