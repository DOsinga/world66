---
title: "Beaches"
type: section
---

Paxi has very little sand. What it has is pebble — pale, smooth, often surprisingly clear water — and an east coast crinkled with small coves. Nothing here is a long beach holiday in the usual sense; the swimming spots are pockets you reach by foot, scooter, or dinghy.

The best of them include **Marmari** and **Levrechio** on the east coast between Loggos and Lakka, **Kipiadi** further north, and **Mongonissi** at the southern tip — the only beach with road access, a bar, and parasols. For real sand and turquoise water, you cross to [Antipaxos](/europe/greece/paxi/antipaxos).
