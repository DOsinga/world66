An Italian-leaning restaurant in Lakka, slightly more ambitious than the average island taverna. Hand-rolled pasta, lemon risotto, a short list of mainland Greek standards as a fallback. The wine list is the best on the island, with proper bottles from Cephalonia and northern Greece.

Inside dining room or pavement tables on the harbour. Reservations recommended in season; the place attracts the yacht crowd from Lakka harbour.
