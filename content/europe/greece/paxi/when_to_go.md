---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

May, June, and September are ideal: the sea is warm enough to swim, the days are long, prices are lower, and the cafes have empty tables. July and August are full season — book months ahead, and brace for noon temperatures into the high 30s.

October still works for swimming most days, though the tavernas start closing one by one. Out of season, from November to April, the island goes quiet — most restaurants close, the ferries reduce, and the rain comes in off the Adriatic. A wind-blown stay in February has its own pleasures, but plan ahead.
