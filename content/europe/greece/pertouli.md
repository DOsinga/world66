Pertouli is a small mountain village in the Pindus range of central Greece, about 45 minutes from [Trikala](/europe/greece/trikala). It sits on high grasslands surrounded by fir forest, at around 1,200 metres altitude, and is one of the more accessible ski areas in the country.

The [ski centre](/europe/greece/pertouli/ski_centre) has been operating since 1985 and has runs for beginners and experienced skiers, two chalets, and two lifts. It is small and uncrowded — a good option for a weekend trip from Thessaloniki or Trikala rather than a destination in its own right. In summer the grasslands are popular for [hiking in the Pindus forest](/europe/greece/pertouli/pindus_hiking) and horse riding, and the surrounding forest is home to deer and wild boar.

The nearby villages of Elati and Neraidochori have more accommodation options and make a good base for exploring the area.
