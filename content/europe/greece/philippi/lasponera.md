Lasponera is an open-air healing mud bath near Lydia and Philippi, used for joint and back aches but also interesting as an unusual local bathing stop. Men and women bathe separately in the main mud bath, and old swimwear is sensible.

The outdoor mud bath usually runs in the warmer months. Bring a towel and check the current season before making a special trip.
