Lydia is a small pilgrimage site just outside Philippi, centred on an octagonal church decorated with bright mosaics and paintings. A baptismal font stands beside the stream where tradition links the apostle Paul with the first Christian baptism on European soil, giving the place more meaning than its modest size suggests.
