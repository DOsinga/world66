Agii Saranta Beach is one of the more scenic beaches on the Pilion coast, with clear water, pale sand, and green slopes rising behind it. In summer there are tavernas, bars, and serviced umbrellas.

It is popular with visitors from Volos on summer weekends, when parking near the beach can fill early. Come outside peak hours if you want the setting without the crowd.
