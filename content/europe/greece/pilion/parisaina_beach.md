Parisaina Beach is a wide, undeveloped beach on the eastern side of Pelion, north of Chorefto. It has clear water and a rough, informal atmosphere, with little permanent infrastructure and limited shade before late afternoon.

In summer it draws campers and naturists, so it is best for travellers who want a simple beach day rather than organised facilities. Bring water and sun protection.
