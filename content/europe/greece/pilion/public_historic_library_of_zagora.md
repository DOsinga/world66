The Public Historic Library of Zagora is tied to the education and intellectual life of 18th-century Pelion. It preserves old books, manuscripts, and material connected with the Greek Enlightenment period before the 1821 Revolution. It is a quiet cultural stop for visitors already exploring Zagora and the eastern side of Pelion.
