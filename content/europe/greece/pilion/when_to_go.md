---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

Pelion works in every season. July and August are peak summer — the beaches are at their best but the roads are slow, the villages crowded, and accommodation books out months in advance. June and September are better: warm enough for swimming, emptier, and the light is superb.

Spring (April to May) is excellent for walking the kalderimi paths — the chestnut and apple trees are in bloom, the waterfalls are running, and the villages are quiet. Autumn brings the apple and chestnut harvest; the orchards around Tsagarada and Milies are particularly good in October.

Winter brings snow to the upper villages and attracts Greek skiers to the small ski area near Hania. Portaria and Makrinitsa fill with Athenians on winter weekends; if you want a quiet winter visit, come mid-week.
