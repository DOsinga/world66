Built into the side of Kastella hill, the Veakeio is an open-air amphitheatre with a stunning panoramic backdrop over the Saronic Gulf. In summer it hosts a busy programme of concerts, ballet, and theatre — Greek and international — that runs from June through September.

Buying a ticket is the best excuse to see a Piraeus sunset over the islands. Bring a cushion; the stone benches are unforgiving.
