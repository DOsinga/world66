Aliki Beach is a sandy swimming spot by the lagoon near the Poros channel. The water is shallow, the setting is easygoing, and Bourtzi lies just offshore, giving the beach a more interesting view than many quick-access swimming places.

It is best treated as a relaxed beach break rather than a full-day expedition. Bring what you need, especially outside the busiest season.
