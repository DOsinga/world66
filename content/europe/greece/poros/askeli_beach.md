Askeli Beach is one of Poros's most practical swimming spots, close enough to town to reach easily but spacious enough to feel like a proper beach outing. Clear water, organised sections, and nearby tavernas make it a straightforward choice for a relaxed island day.

It is more convenient than secluded, which is exactly its appeal for many visitors. Come early in high season for the best choice of space, or late in the day when the light softens and the crowds thin.
