Lemonodasos is a green hillside of lemon and orange trees across the water from Poros, near Galatas on the Peloponnese coast. A short boat crossing puts you within walking distance of orchards, scattered tavernas, and views back toward the island.

The place is simple rather than polished: go for the stroll, the scent of citrus, and the panorama from higher up the slope. It is an easy change of pace when Poros town starts to feel busy.
