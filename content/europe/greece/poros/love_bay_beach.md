Love Bay Beach is one of Poros' prettiest small coves, with pine trees close to the water and a sheltered shape that makes the sea feel calm and clear. It is more intimate than the island's broader beaches, which is exactly why people seek it out.

Come outside the busiest hours if you want the cove rather than the crowd. It works best as a swim stop on a slow circuit of the island's western beaches, with enough facilities for comfort but still a distinct little-bay atmosphere.
