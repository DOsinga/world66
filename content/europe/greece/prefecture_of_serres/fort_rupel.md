Fort Rupel is a border fortress north of Serres, built in 1914 and later folded into the Metaxas Line, Greece's interwar defensive system. It became famous in April 1941, when Greek troops resisted the German invasion from these positions.

The site is mainly for travellers interested in military history, but the mountain setting and preserved fortifications make the story easier to grasp than a museum case ever could.
