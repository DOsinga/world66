---
title: Eating Out
type: section
---

Serres food is solidly Macedonian: hearty meat dishes, river fish from the Strymon, and a strong tradition of pies. The local **bougatsa** of Serres is famous all over Greece — a custard-filled phyllo pastry served warm with sugar and cinnamon, traditionally eaten for breakfast. Try it at one of the bougatsa shops near Plateia Eleftherias.

For a proper meal, the tavernas around the old quarter offer grilled lamb, **soutzoukakia** (cumin-spiced meatballs in tomato sauce) and **kavourmas** (a slow-cooked beef stew with bay leaves). Lake Kerkini fish, particularly catfish and carp, appears on menus in season. The covered market sells the local sheep's-milk cheese and the dried pork sausages of Mount Pangaio.
