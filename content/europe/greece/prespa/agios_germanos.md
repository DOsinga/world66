  The Byzantine church in Agios Germanos dates from 1072 AD — making it
  contemporaneous with the Norman Conquest of England and the Great Schism

Agios Germanos is the most accessible of the Prespa villages and the practical base for most visitors. It has a small hotel, a few tavernas, and the Society for the Protection of Prespa's visitor centre, which provides maps and information about the region.

The village's 11th-century Byzantine church is well-preserved and contains original frescoes. The village itself — stone houses, narrow lanes, a central kafeneion — is a good example of traditional Macedonian architecture. From Agios Germanos, the main road descends to the lakes and the pontoon bridge to Agios Achillios island.
