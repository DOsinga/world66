Rodini Park is a shaded green break just outside Rhodes City, with paths following a small creek through cypress and plane trees. It is not a polished formal garden, but the mix of water, stone bridges, peacocks, and old Roman waterworks gives it more character than a routine city park.

Come for a quiet walk when the old town feels hot and crowded. The historical remains are modest, but the setting is a pleasant reminder that Rhodes has layers beyond the medieval walls and harbour.
