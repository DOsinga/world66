The Ancient Mines of Samos are limestone workings linked to the island's ancient building projects, including material used for the Heraion and city buildings. The site has dozens of entrances and multiple levels cut with simple tools.

They are most worthwhile for travelers already exploring the archaeological landscape around Pythagoreio and the Tunnel of Eupalinos.
