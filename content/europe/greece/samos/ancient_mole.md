The ancient mole at Pythagorion is the visible remnant of one of the Mediterranean's earliest major harbour works. The structure once protected the port in deep water, and the western end of today's quay still follows the line of that old engineering project. It is a small sight, but a satisfying one if you are already tracing Samos' ancient history around Pythagorion.
