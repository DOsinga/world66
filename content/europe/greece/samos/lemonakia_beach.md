Lemonakia Beach is one of the appealing beaches near Kokkari on the north coast of Samos. It has clear water, a sheltered cove-like feel, and green hills close behind, making it more scenic than a simple strip of sand.

It is easy to combine with Tsamadou and other nearby beaches, choosing between them according to wind, crowds, and mood. Lemonakia is best for swimming and a relaxed half-day rather than a remote escape.
