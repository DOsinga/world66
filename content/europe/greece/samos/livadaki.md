Livadaki is a small sandy beach on Samos, tucked into a narrow rocky bay north of Vathy. The water is shallow and bright, which makes it easy for swimming rather than just looking from the shore.

It is organised with umbrellas, sun beds, and a beach bar in season. The beach is only about 100 metres long, so arrive early if you want the quieter version of it.
