Panagia Vrontiani is a historic monastery on Samos, founded in 1476 and rebuilt in 1566 on the ruins of an older foundation. Its carved iconostasis, painted icons, and quiet mountain setting make it a worthwhile stop for visitors exploring the island's inland churches and villages.
