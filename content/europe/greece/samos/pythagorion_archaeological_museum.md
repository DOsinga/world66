This small archaeological museum in Pythagorion focuses on finds from the ancient city and its surroundings. The collection is useful before or after visiting the nearby ancient walls, the Tunnel of Eupalinos, and the Heraion, with displays that make the everyday life of ancient Samos easier to picture.
