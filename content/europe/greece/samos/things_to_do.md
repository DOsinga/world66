---
title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Samos packs more variety into one island than any of its East Aegean neighbours. The archaeological sites at the Heraion and Pythagoreio are UNESCO-listed; the Vathy museum holds the giant kouros; and the Kerkis and Ampelos massifs in the interior keep walkers busy for days. Beaches, vineyards, and the strangest 6th-century BC engineering project in Greece round it out.
