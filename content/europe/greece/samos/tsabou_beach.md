Tsabou Beach is a small pebble beach on the north coast of Samos, with clear deep water and a wilder feel than some of the more developed beaches near Kokkari. The setting is scenic, though the beach can get rough when strong northerly winds are blowing.

There are usually sun beds and umbrellas in season, with a taverna near the shore. A smaller, more secluded beach lies just beyond it, making Tsabou a good stop while exploring the island's north-coast beaches.
