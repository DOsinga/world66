Agios Nikolaos Church is a small Greek Orthodox church built into the cliffs near Akrotiri. Its whitewashed walls and three-bell tower are classic Santorini, but the cliffside setting gives it more character than many roadside chapels.

It is a short stop rather than a major sight. Come if you are already exploring Akrotiri and want a quiet place with a bit of local texture.
