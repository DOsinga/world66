Amoudi Bay is the small harbour below Oia, reached by steps or road from the village above. The red cliffs, fishing boats, and caldera views make it one of Santorini's more photogenic waterfront corners.

It is especially popular around sunset, when the narrow waterside path and restaurants can get crowded. Go earlier if you want the bay without the evening rush.
