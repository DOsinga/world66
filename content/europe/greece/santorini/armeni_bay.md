Armeni Bay sits below Oia and takes more effort to reach than the better-known Ammoudi Bay. That extra effort keeps it quieter, with fewer facilities and more of the rough caldera-edge feel that makes northern Santorini so memorable.

Come for a swim, not for a serviced beach day. The setting is the draw: dark rock, clear water, and the cliff town high above.
