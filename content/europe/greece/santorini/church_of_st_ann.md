The Church of St. Ann is one of Mesaria's older landmarks, built in 1827. Its main interest is the carved wooden icon screen, which is decorated with scenes from the Old Testament.

It is a modest stop rather than a major island sight, but it adds useful context if you are exploring Santorini's inland villages away from the caldera crowds.
