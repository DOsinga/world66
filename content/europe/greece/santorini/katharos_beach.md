Katharos Beach is a quieter pebble beach below Oia, with dark volcanic rock and a more tucked-away feel than Santorini's famous caldera viewpoints. It is best treated as a simple swim-and-sit beach, with shoes useful on the stones and nearby food if you want to linger.
