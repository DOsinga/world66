Museum of Prehistoric Thira tells the story of Akrotiri and other Neolithic and Bronze Age settlements on Santorini. Its collection includes pottery, tools, and vivid wall paintings recovered from Akrotiri.

Visit before or after the Akrotiri archaeological site to understand the island's Bronze Age culture and the volcanic eruption that preserved much of it.
