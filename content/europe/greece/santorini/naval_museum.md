Oia's Naval Museum sits in a traditional house and tells the maritime side of Santorini's story. The collection includes ship models, figureheads, old maps, seamen's chests, drawings, and equipment from the island's trading and naval past.

It is a quiet counterpoint to Oia's cliff views and sunset crowds. Drop in if you want to understand how this small volcanic island was tied to the wider Aegean by ships, crews, and trade.
