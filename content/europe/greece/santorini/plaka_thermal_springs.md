Plaka Thermal Springs sit by the caldera coast, where abandoned spa buildings recall an early 20th-century bathing site. The baths operated from 1916 to 1980 before landslide risk ended regular use.

The appeal today is the mix of coastal scenery, geothermal history, and visible ruins rather than a polished spa experience.
