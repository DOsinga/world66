---
title: Getting There
type: section
---

Sikinos is served by ferry from Piraeus (about 9–10 hours) and by smaller ferries connecting it to neighbouring islands including [Ios](/europe/greece/ios), [Folegandros](/europe/greece), and [Santorini](/europe/greece/santorini). The Blue Star Ferries and Seajets routes are the most reliable; check ferry schedules on openseas.gr or ferryhopper.com as routes vary by season.

There is no airport on Sikinos. The nearest airports are on Santorini and [Mykonos](/europe/greece/mykonos). From Santorini, a ferry to Sikinos takes around two hours on the fast boat. The small ferry Skopelitis also connects Sikinos to the smaller Cyclades on a circuit route several times weekly in summer.

All ferries dock at Alopronia harbour, from where a bus runs up to the Hora.
