---
title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Sikinos rewards slow travel. The real pleasures here are walking the old mule paths between the Hora and the coast, sitting in the village square at dusk, and finding your own stretch of rocky shoreline. There are two main archaeological and historical sights — Episkopi and the Kastro of the Hora — and the rest of the island is yours to discover on foot.
