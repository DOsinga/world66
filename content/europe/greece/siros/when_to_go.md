---
title: When to Go
type: section
tags: [when_to_go]
---

Syros has a Mediterranean climate — dry hot summers and mild winters. The island is far enough from the party-island circuit that it never becomes truly overcrowded, but July and August still bring enough visitors to fill the waterfront cafes and make ferry booking advisable.

**May and June** are ideal: the weather is warm and settled, the sea is swimmable from early June, the prices are lower than high season, and Ermoupoli's cafe culture is in full swing. **September and October** are similarly excellent — the crowds thin but the water stays warm from the summer heat.

**July and August** are busy but manageable. The Cyclades summer festival season brings concerts and events to the Apollon Theater, which is worth timing a visit around.

**Winter** (November–March) is quiet, with some hotels and restaurants closed. Ermoupoli is never entirely deserted — it is a real working city, not a resort town — and the winter light on the neoclassical facades is genuinely beautiful. Ferry connections thin out but don't disappear entirely.
