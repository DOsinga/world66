Asselinos Beach sits on the north coast of Skiathos, where the island feels more open and less polished than the busy southern resort strip. The beach has sand, clear water, and a backdrop of green hills, but the main appeal is the relative breathing room.

Conditions can be rougher here when northerly winds blow, so it is worth checking the weather before committing. On calm days, Asselinos is a good choice for travellers who want Skiathos scenery without the full-service beach-club atmosphere.
