A straightforward fish taverna on the edge of the old port, popular with locals rather than the waterfront tourist crowd. The menu changes daily based on what the boats brought in — grilled bream, octopus on the line outside, prawns with ouzo. Order the house salad and whatever whole fish looks freshest. Prices are reasonable for the quality, and the tables fill early, so arrive by 8pm or expect a wait in high season.
