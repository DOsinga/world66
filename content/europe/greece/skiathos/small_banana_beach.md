Small Banana Beach is the quieter cove beside the better-known Banana Beach area on Skiathos' west coast. It has the same clear water and pine-backed setting, but on a smaller scale and with a more tucked-away feeling.

The beach is best treated as part of a west-coast beach day rather than a standalone expedition. Access and atmosphere can change by season, but its appeal is straightforward: a compact sandy cove, good swimming, and a more intimate alternative to the island's busier organized beaches.
