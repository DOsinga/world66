---
title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Skiathos rewards those who venture beyond the beach. The old town has a literary museum worth an hour, the monastery in the hills above town is one of the most historically significant in Greece, and the ruins of Kastro on the island's northern tip are spectacular — a medieval walled settlement abandoned only 200 years ago. Excursion boats from the harbour make it easy to combine a morning at Kastro with an afternoon at Lalaria beach.
