The Monastery of Evangelistria, dedicated to the Annunciation of the Virgin Mary, was founded in 1712 and sits on the wooded slopes of Mount Palouki about 4 km from Skopelos Town. The fortress-like exterior belies an unexpectedly beautiful courtyard inside, planted with flowers and centered on a well. The katholikon (main church) is decorated with fine 18th-century frescoes.

The monastery is active, run by nuns, and open to visitors during daylight hours. Dress modestly — shoulders and knees covered. A small shop sells honey, products, and icons. The drive or hike through the pine forest to reach it is itself worth doing: the air is fragrant with resin, and the forest paths are cool even in summer. From the monastery, views extend over the southern coast of the island.
