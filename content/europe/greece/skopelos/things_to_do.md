---
title: "Things to Do in Skopelos"
type: section
---

Skopelos rewards slow exploration. The island's primary attractions are its extraordinary pine forests, its string of beaches, its monasteries perched on wooded hillsides, and the medieval town itself with its whitewashed lanes and 360 churches. The [Agios Ioannis Chapel](/europe/greece/skopelos/agios_ioannis_chapel) on its dramatic sea-rock is the famous Mamma Mia location. The [Skopelos Town Kastro](/europe/greece/skopelos/kastro) commands views over the whole harbor. For the monasteries, the circuit of [Evangelistria](/europe/greece/skopelos/evangelistria_monastery) and Timios Prodromos takes a half-day.
