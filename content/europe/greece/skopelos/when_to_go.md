---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

Late June through early September is high season: the weather is reliably sunny and hot, the sea is warm and calm, and the island is full. July and August are the busiest months, with prices at their highest and accommodation needing to be booked months in advance.

May, early June, and September are excellent alternatives. The weather is warm, the sea swimmable, the restaurants and boats are running, but the island is much quieter. The pine forests are particularly beautiful in spring, when the undergrowth flowers and the light is soft.

October through April is off-season. Some ferries stop running, many guesthouses close, and the island withdraws into its quieter self. The winter rains are gentle, and for anyone who wants to see Skopelos as a real island rather than a resort, a November or March visit has its own appeal — but check ferry schedules carefully before planning.

The Mamma Mia effect has extended the tourist season somewhat, with visitors arriving year-round specifically for the chapel at Agios Ioannis. This is fine but means even "shoulder" months have a more international tinge than they once did.
