Agios Arthemios Chapel is a small rural chapel on Skyros, the kind of low-key religious stop that rewards travellers who like the island beyond its beaches. Its appeal is in setting, simplicity, and the way chapels mark routes through the countryside.

Do not expect a staffed monument or elaborate interior. Visit respectfully, keep gates and doors as you find them, and fold the stop into a wider drive or walk through Skyros's inland landscapes.
