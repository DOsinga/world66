Agios Fokas Beach is one of the quieter coastal stops on Skyros, a place to aim for when the island's better-known beaches feel too exposed or too busy. Its cove-like setting gives it a more tucked-away character than the long sands around Molos.

Bring what you need and treat it as a simple swim-and-linger beach rather than a full-service resort strip. The reward is the slower Skyros mood: clear water, low hills, and enough distance from the main beach scene to make the island feel larger.
