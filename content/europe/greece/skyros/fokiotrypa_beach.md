Fokiotrypa Beach is a compact cove on Skyros, valued more for its clear water and rocky setting than for broad sand or facilities. It feels tucked away, with the kind of simple Aegean swimming that rewards travellers willing to move beyond the main beaches.

Bring water, shade, and sensible shoes if you are approaching on rough ground. It is best treated as a scenic swim stop during an island drive, not as a fully serviced beach day.
