Kyra Panagia and Kalogria are quieter beach stops on Skyros, away from the busier strips near Chora and Molos. Their value is in the more open coastal setting, where swimming and a slow afternoon matter more than facilities.

They suit travelers with a car or scooter who want to sample the island's less urban coastline. Bring water and check conditions, as these beaches are best treated as simple natural stops rather than organized resorts.
