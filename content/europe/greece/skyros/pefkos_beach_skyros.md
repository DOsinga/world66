Pefkos Beach is a sheltered west-coast spot on Skyros, backed by pines and low hills rather than the busier settlement strip. The name fits the setting: trees, shade, and a softer beach day.

It is a good choice when wind makes other beaches less appealing or when you want a less developed swim. Bring what you need outside high season, when services can be thin.
