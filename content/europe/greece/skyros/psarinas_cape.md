Psarinas Cape is a rugged coastal viewpoint on Skyros, better for landscape and solitude than for facilities. The attraction is the open Aegean edge: rock, wind, sea color, and a sense of the island beyond its main beach settlements.

It is worth considering if you are exploring Skyros by vehicle and want a short scenic detour. Go with realistic expectations, since this is a natural viewpoint rather than a developed attraction.
