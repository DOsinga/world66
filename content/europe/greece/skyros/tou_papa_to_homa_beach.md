Tou Papa to Homa is a small, scenic beach south of Magazia and below the rock of Skyros Town. It is known as a quieter and more secluded swimming spot, with a nudist tradition and a setting that feels tucked under the island's cliff and castle landscape.

Access is less straightforward than at Magazia or Molos, so wear decent footwear and do not expect full beach facilities.
