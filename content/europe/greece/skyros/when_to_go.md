---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

**June to September** is the main season. July and August are hot and dry, with water temperatures in the upper 20s. The island gets busy but never overwhelmed — it has no large hotels and the ferry journey keeps casual crowds away.

**The carnival period (February or early March)** is the most unusual time to visit. The Goat Dance is performed only during Apokries (the Greek pre-Lenten carnival), and Skyros Town fills with locals and visitors who have come specifically to see it. The weather will be cool and possibly wet, but the atmosphere is extraordinary.

**May, June, and September** offer the best balance: warm enough to swim, the island green from winter rains, and tavernas open without the summer crowds.

Avoid the ferry from Kymi on summer weekends — it books out fast. The flight from Athens is a more reliable option in peak season.
