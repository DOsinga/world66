Sparta is mainly worth visiting as a base for places nearby that are better than Sparta itself.

**Mistra** — six kilometres west, the UNESCO-listed Byzantine ghost city on the steep flank of the Taygetos. The fortified medieval town of the Despots of Morea: ruined palaces, intact frescoed churches, a Frankish castle on top, an active convent halfway up. Half a day minimum; ideally a full day.

**Mani Peninsula** — the rocky middle finger of the southern Peloponnese, an hour and a half south by car, with the tower-house villages of the inner Mani, the dramatic west coast and (further down) the spectacular Diros caves. Better as an overnight than a day trip.

**Monemvasia** — a small fortified Byzantine town built into a tied island on the south coast, about an hour and a half east. One of the most striking medieval sites in Greece.

**Gythio** — the small port at the head of the Mani gulf, an hour south. Calm fishing village, decent beaches at Mavrovouni, and the gateway to Mani.

**Langada Pass** — the dramatic mountain road over the Taygetos to [Kalamata](/europe/greece/kalamata), 60 km and ninety minutes one way. A scenic drive in its own right.
