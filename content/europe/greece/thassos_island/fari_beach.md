Fari Beach is a quiet cove on Thassos for travellers who want a low-key swim rather than an organized beach scene. It is reached by a short walk from a small parking area, and the clear water makes it a good snorkelling stop in calm weather.

There are few facilities, so bring water and shade if you plan to stay. The appeal is the quieter setting rather than beach bars or sunbed rows.
