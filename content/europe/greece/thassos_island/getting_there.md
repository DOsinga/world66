---
title: "Getting There"
type: section
---

Thassos is reached entirely by ferry — there is no airport on the island. The two main crossings are from [Kavala](/europe/greece/kavala) (about 90 minutes, carrying passengers and cars) and from Keramoti (about 35 minutes, the faster car-ferry crossing on a dedicated route). Both run frequently in summer; in winter the Keramoti service is more reliable. Tickets are cheap and need no advance booking outside August.

Most visitors fly into **Kavala Alexander the Great Airport (KVA)**, which has direct flights from Athens and seasonal charters from northern Europe. From the airport it is a short taxi ride or bus journey into Kavala and then a ferry to the island. You can also reach Kavala by bus from Thessaloniki (about 2.5 hours), making the island accessible as a day trip or short break from Macedonia's largest city. If you are driving from central Greece, Keramoti is slightly more convenient than Kavala — it sits right at the end of a fast road from the E90 motorway and the crossing is quick enough that you barely have time to drink a coffee on deck.
