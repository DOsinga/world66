Livadi Bay is one of Thassos's more rugged beach settings, with pale water, steep green slopes, and a quieter atmosphere than the island's better-known resort beaches. It appeals most to travellers who are willing to trade facilities for scenery.

Access and conditions can vary, so it is worth checking locally before setting out. On a good day, the bay gives a wilder version of Thassos: marble-bright coast, pine-covered hills, and enough space to slow down.
