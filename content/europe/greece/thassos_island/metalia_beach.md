Metalia Beach sits near Limenaria on the southwest coast of Thassos, with sand, clear water, and the remains of the island's old mining industry close by. That mix gives it more character than a simple sunbed beach.

Come for a swim, then wander toward the ruined industrial buildings and rocks above the shore. It is still a beach first, so summer brings bars, loungers, and crowds, but the setting makes it one of the more memorable stops on this side of the island.
