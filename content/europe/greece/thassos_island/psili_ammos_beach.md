Psili Ammos Beach is one of the better-known beaches on the south coast of Thassos, with soft sand, shallow water, and a busier summer atmosphere than the island's quieter coves. The name means fine sand, and that is very much the selling point.

It suits swimming, families, and travellers who want facilities close at hand. Come early in high season if you want space, or treat it as a social beach rather than a lonely escape. The water is usually the reason people forgive the crowds.
