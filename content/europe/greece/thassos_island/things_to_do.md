Thassos punches above its weight for archaeology. The ancient city of Limenas (Thassos Town) preserves a substantial agora, a hilltop theatre, and stretches of ancient wall — all without the crowds you would encounter at more famous sites. The island's marble quarries at Alyki add a genuinely unusual dimension: industrial archaeology at the water's edge, with Byzantine chapels built from the offcuts.

The interior is less visited but equally rewarding. Pine-forested slopes rise to the summit of Ypsario, mountain villages like Theologos and Kastro preserve a way of life largely unchanged since the Ottoman period, and the Polygnotos Vagis Museum in Potamia holds a remarkable collection in an unexpected setting.
