Tripiti is a beach on the southwest coast of Thasos, a few kilometres from Limenaria on the road toward Skala Maries. It is known for clear water, a broad stretch of sand and pebbles, and the natural opening through the rock that gives the place its name.

It is a straightforward beach stop, but the rock formation makes it more memorable than a generic swimming cove.
