The Vouves mines are old lead-silver workings northeast of Limenaria. Mining here goes back to antiquity, and the open pit still shows tunnels from the early 20th-century operation.

The site is rough rather than polished, but it is an atmospheric stop for travellers interested in industrial history, abandoned places, and unusual inland scenery away from the beaches.
