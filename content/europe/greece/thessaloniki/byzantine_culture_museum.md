The Museum of Byzantine Culture houses the most complete collection of Byzantine art outside Istanbul. The 11 galleries move chronologically from early Christian burial practice through the golden age of Byzantine art to the post-Byzantine period: extraordinary mosaics, frescoes removed from churches, gold jewellery, illuminated manuscripts, and icons of remarkable quality. The building by Kyriakos Krokos won the European Museum of the Year award in 2005. Allow two hours; the context provided makes Thessaloniki's standing churches (the Rotunda, Hagia Sophia) make much more sense afterwards.
