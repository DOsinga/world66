The MOMus Museum of Contemporary Art gives Thessaloniki a modern counterweight to its Byzantine churches, Roman remains, and archaeological museums. Set by the International Fair grounds, it presents Greek and international contemporary work, including pieces connected to the important Costakis collection of Russian avant-garde art.

It is a strong choice for travellers who want the city's cultural life to feel current rather than only ancient. The museum also fits neatly with nearby stops around the waterfront and university district, making it easy to add without pulling away from the centre.
