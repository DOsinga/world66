The Municipal Gallery of Art is worth seeking out as much for its building as for its collection. It occupies Casa Bianca, an early 20th-century villa that survived from the era when Thessaloniki's eastern suburbs were lined with elegant seaside mansions.

The exhibitions focus on Greek art and rotating shows, so the visit varies, but the scale is manageable and the setting is memorable. It is a useful counterpoint to Thessaloniki's Byzantine museums and Roman monuments.
