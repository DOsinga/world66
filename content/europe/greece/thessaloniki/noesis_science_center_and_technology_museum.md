NOESIS, Thessaloniki’s Science Center and Technology Museum, sits east of the city centre and gives the city a modern, family-friendly museum stop beyond its Byzantine and Ottoman layers. Exhibits cover technology, science, and transport, with interactive displays aimed especially at younger visitors.

The planetarium and large-format theatre are part of the appeal, so check the programme before making the trip. It is not as essential as the Archaeological Museum or Byzantine Museum for first-time visitors, but it is a strong option for families or anyone needing a break from ancient stones.
