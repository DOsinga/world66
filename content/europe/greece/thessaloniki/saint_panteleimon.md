Saint Panteleimon is a fourteenth-century Byzantine church near the Rotunda and Arch of Galerius. Only fragments of its original wall paintings remain, but the compact brick architecture makes it a worthwhile stop while exploring Thessaloniki's early Christian and Byzantine sites.
