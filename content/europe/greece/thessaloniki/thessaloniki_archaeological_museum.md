The Thessaloniki Archaeological Museum is one of the city's essential museums, presenting finds from ancient Macedonia and Thessaloniki's wider region. Its collections include sculpture, jewelry, funerary objects, mosaics, and material that connects the city to the Hellenistic and Roman worlds.

It is a high-value stop even for travelers with limited time, especially when paired with the nearby Museum of Byzantine Culture and the waterfront. The museum gives depth to ruins and monuments scattered across the modern city.
