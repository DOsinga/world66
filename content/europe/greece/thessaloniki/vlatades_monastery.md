Vlatades Monastery sits high in Ano Poli, where Thessaloniki's Byzantine past feels closest to the surface. The monastery is part of the city's UNESCO-listed early Christian and Byzantine heritage, but it also works as a practical stop: quiet courtyards, old walls and broad views down toward the gulf.

Come while walking the upper town rather than making a quick taxi dash. The climb through lanes and fortifications gives the monastery its setting, and the view helps explain why this part of Thessaloniki feels different from the busy, gridded city below.
