---
title: When to Go
type: section
---

Thessaloniki has a Mediterranean climate with hot, dry summers and mild, occasionally wet winters. The best times to visit are **spring (April–May)** and **autumn (September–October)**, when temperatures are comfortable for walking and the city is at its most animated without the summer heat.

Summer (July–August) is hot — 35°C is common — and humid. The seafront promenade stays busy into the small hours; the heat forces the city's social life into the evening. Many locals leave for the beaches of Halkidiki at weekends. Winter is mild by northern European standards (5–10°C) and wet but not cold enough to put anyone off exploring: the museums are quiet and the restaurants and bars are at their most local and lively.

The **Thessaloniki International Film Festival** in November is one of the major cultural events in the Balkans, bringing film industry people and good cinema into the city for two weeks. The **International Trade Fair** in September is a large industrial event that fills hotels and raises prices. The city celebrates its patron saint **Aghios Dimitrios** on 26 October with processions and festivities.
