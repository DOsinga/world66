---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

Tolo's high season is **July and August** — warm sea, hot days (often above 32°C), and the beach genuinely busy. Greek families dominate the second half of August. **June and September** are arguably the best months: the sea is plenty warm enough for swimming, the days are slightly cooler, and you can walk straight into any taverna.

**October** still has swimmable water and many places open through to about the 20th. From **November to April** the village goes quiet — most hotels close, a handful of year-round tavernas stay open for locals. The off-season is mild and a perfectly good time to base yourself for the archaeology, though you'll need a car.
