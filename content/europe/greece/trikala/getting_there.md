---
title: "Getting There"
type: section
---

Trikala is served by train on the line from Palaiofarsalos (on the Athens–Thessaloniki mainline) to Kalambaka. This makes it possible to arrive from [Athens](/europe/greece/athens) or [Thessaloniki](/europe/greece/thessaloniki) by rail with a change at Palaiofarsalos, though journey times are long. The more practical option for most visitors is by bus (KTEL) from Athens (about 4.5 hours) or Thessaloniki (about 3 hours). [Larissa](/europe/greece/larissa) is about an hour by bus.

By car, Trikala lies on the E92 road that crosses central Greece. From [Ioannina](/europe/greece/ioannina) it's about 1.5 hours east through the Pindus mountains. [Kalambaka](/europe/greece/kalambaka) and Meteora are 25 kilometres to the northwest, less than 30 minutes by bus or car.
