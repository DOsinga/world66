The Central Museum of Aigai is the modern museum building for Vergina's ancient Macedonian capital, designed to complement the famous royal tombs rather than replace them. Its displays bring together sculpture, architectural fragments, inscriptions, and objects that explain Aigai as a city and royal centre, not just a burial site.

Visit it with the Royal Tombs museum and palace remains if you can. The broader context matters here: Aigai was where Macedonian kings staged power, ritual, and memory. The central museum helps connect the spectacular tomb finds to the urban and political landscape around them.
