The Tsalapata Brickworks Museum preserves a former brick and tile factory that operated through much of the 20th century. The site keeps the kilns, machinery, drying sheds, and production spaces together, making it one of Volos's clearest introductions to the city's industrial past.

Exhibits explain how clay was processed, shaped, dried, and fired, with enough original equipment left in place to make the workflow easy to understand. It is a good stop for visitors interested in industrial heritage, architecture, or the less classical side of Greek history.
