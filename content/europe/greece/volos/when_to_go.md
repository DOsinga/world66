---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

Volos is at its best from April through October. Spring and early autumn are ideal for combining the city with walking on the Pelion peninsula — the kalderimia stone paths are cool enough to hike comfortably, the wildflowers are out, and the beach crowds have not yet arrived. The Pelion's chestnut and plane forests make it particularly beautiful in October.

July and August are hot — temperatures regularly climb above 35°C — and the Aegean beaches on the far side of the Pelion fill with Athenians and tourists on summer weekends. If you are going in high summer, plan beach days for midweek and accept that the coastal villages will be busy. The Pagasitic Gulf side of the peninsula is calmer and the water is warmer, which makes it better for families. The tsipouradika are, if anything, more enjoyable in the heat when a cold glass of tsipouro and a plate of grilled sardines is exactly what you want.

Winter is quiet and the Pelion is largely deserted. The mountain villages see some snow and there is a small ski area near Agriolefkes, but facilities are limited. If you are not interested in skiing or winter landscapes, November through March offers little reason to visit.
