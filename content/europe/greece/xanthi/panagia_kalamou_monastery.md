Panagia Kalamou Monastery sits above Xanthi, close enough to the old town to make a short uphill excursion but far enough to feel removed from the streets below. The site is simple, with the draw coming from its religious role, hillside position, and views over the city.

It is best visited with modest expectations and respectful dress. Combine it with a walk through Xanthi's old quarters, where the town's Ottoman, Balkan, and tobacco-merchant history is easier to read at street level.
