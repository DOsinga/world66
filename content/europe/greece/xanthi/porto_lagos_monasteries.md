The Porto Lagos monasteries sit on small islands in the lagoon east of Xanthi, reached by footbridges over the water. Their setting is the main draw: whitewashed religious buildings, wetland views, and birdlife around Lake Vistonida.

They are worth including on a coastal or lagoon trip from Xanthi, especially for travellers combining monasteries, nature, and village stops rather than staying only in the old town.
