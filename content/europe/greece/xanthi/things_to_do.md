title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

The [old town](/europe/greece/xanthi/old_town) is the essential part of Xanthi — a hillside quarter of tobacco-era mansions in rich colours, overhanging upper floors, and narrow cobbled streets that climb steeply above the modern centre. The [Clock Tower](/europe/greece/xanthi/clock_tower), the sole remnant of a mosque destroyed in a 1941 fire, stands at the edge of the upper town and is visible from throughout the city. The [Folk Museum](/europe/greece/xanthi/folk_museum) in a restored 19th-century mansion gives the best context for the tobacco trade that produced the old town's architecture. The [Saturday bazaar](/europe/greece/xanthi/saturday_bazaar) is the weekly highlight — one of the largest traditional markets in northern Greece, running since the Ottoman period.
