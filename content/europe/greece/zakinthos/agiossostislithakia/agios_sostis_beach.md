Agios Sostis Beach is a short curving strip of fine yellow sand on the southern coast of Zakynthos, sheltered on the seaward side by the small tied islet of [Cameo](/europe/greece/zakinthos/agiossostislithakia/cameo_island). The water is shallow and turquoise — calm enough for small children — and the bay is closed to motorboats, which keeps both the swimming and the underwater visibility good.

The beach is busiest from late morning through mid-afternoon in July and August and reasonably empty either side of that. Sunbeds and a couple of small beach bars line the back; the wooden bridge across to Cameo Island leaves from the far end. Parking on the road behind the beach is free but tight.
