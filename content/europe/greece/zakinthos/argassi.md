Argassi is the easiest beach-resort add-on to [Zakynthos City](/europe/greece/zakinthos/zakynthoscity): close enough for dinner in the capital, but with its own strip of tavernas, bars, boat desks, and sunbeds along the southeast coast. It is not the prettiest village on Zakynthos, and in high summer the main road can feel more functional than charming, but it is convenient, lively, and well placed for short trips around the island.

The beach is narrow rather than spectacular, with shallow water and views back toward Zakynthos Town and the Peloponnese. The small landmark everyone notices is the [Argassi Bridge](/europe/greece/zakinthos/argassi/argassi_bridge), a three-arched stone bridge now standing partly in the sea. It is a modest sight, but it gives the resort a sense of age that many purpose-built beach strips lack.

Argassi works best if you want a base rather than a hideaway. [Zakynthos City](/europe/greece/zakinthos/zakynthoscity) is about 5 km north, the turtle beaches of the [National Marine Park of Zakynthos](/europe/greece/zakinthos/argassi/national_marine_park_of_zakynthos) are a short drive south, and the [Skopos](/europe/greece/zakinthos/argassi/skopos) hillside behind the resort gives the coast a surprisingly wild backdrop.
