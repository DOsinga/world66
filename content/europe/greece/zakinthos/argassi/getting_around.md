---
title: Getting Around
type: section
---

The resort strip is walkable, with most restaurants, bars, supermarkets, and beach access points along or just off the main road. Traffic can be irritating in high season, so walking is often easier than making short hops by car.

For Zakynthos Town, Kalamaki, Vasilikos, or the turtle beaches of Laganas Bay, use a taxi, local bus, scooter, or rental car. Boat trips are sold along the waterfront and usually cover the southern coast, Marathonisi, or the Keri Caves.
