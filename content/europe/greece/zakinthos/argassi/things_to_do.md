---

Argassi is a beach-and-base resort rather than a heavy sightseeing town. The [Argassi Bridge](/europe/greece/zakinthos/argassi/argassi_bridge) is the local landmark, while the beach, boat trips, and short taxi rides to Zakynthos Town fill most itineraries.

For something more natural, head south toward the [National Marine Park of Zakynthos](/europe/greece/zakinthos/argassi/national_marine_park_of_zakynthos) and the turtle-nesting beaches of Laganas Bay, or climb inland toward [Skopos](/europe/greece/zakinthos/argassi/skopos) for views over the southeast coast.
