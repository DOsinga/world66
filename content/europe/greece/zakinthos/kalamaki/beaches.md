---
title: "Beaches"
type: section
---

Kalamaki's own beach is the main draw — a long arc of sand that is calm, safe, and part of the turtle nesting zone. Day trips by hired car or scooter open up the rest of Zakynthos's remarkable coastline, from the white-pebble coves of Navagio on the northwest coast to the quieter beaches around Porto Koukla to the south.
