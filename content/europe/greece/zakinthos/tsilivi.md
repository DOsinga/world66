Tsilivi is a popular family resort on the northeast coast of Zakynthos, about ten minutes from Zakynthos Town. Despite growing as a holiday destination, it retains plenty of traditional Greek tavernas alongside the newer bars and restaurants.

[Tsilivi Beach](/europe/greece/zakinthos/tsilivi/tsilivi_beach) is the main draw: a long, sandy stretch of the sheltered Ionian coast with calm, clear water ideal for families. The resort is compact and unhurried — most visitors are here for sun and sea rather than sightseeing, but there is enough nearby to fill a week with day trips.

From Tsilivi, the [Venetian Castle](/europe/greece/zakinthos/tsilivi/venetian_castle) above Zakynthos Town is 20 minutes by road and one of the best viewpoints on the island. The [Blue Caves](/europe/greece/zakinthos/tsilivi/blue_caves) at Cape Skinari on the northern tip are reached by boat from Agios Nikolaos harbour — typically combined with a stop at Navagio (Shipwreck Beach), one of the most photographed beaches in Greece. The [Solomos Museum](/europe/greece/zakinthos/tsilivi/solomos_museum) in Zakynthos Town covers the poet who wrote the Greek national anthem and is worth an hour on a cooler day.
