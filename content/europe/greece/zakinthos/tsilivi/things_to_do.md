---
title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Tsilivi's main draw is [Tsilivi Beach](/europe/greece/zakinthos/tsilivi/tsilivi_beach) — a long, sandy stretch with calm, clear water that makes it ideal for families and casual swimmers. The beach has sun loungers, water sports, and several bars and cafes directly on the sand. The resort is compact enough to explore on foot.

The surrounding hills offer good walks with views over the Ionian Sea. For excursions, the main island sights — the [Venetian Castle](/europe/greece/zakinthos/tsilivi/venetian_castle) above Zakynthos Town, the Blue Caves on the north coast, and the sea turtle nesting beaches at Laganas — are all reachable on day trips from Tsilivi. Boat trips to Navagio (Shipwreck Beach) are the most popular excursion from the island.
