---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

The best time to visit Tsilivi is May, June, and September. The weather is warm and settled, the sea has reached a comfortable swimming temperature, and the resort is well below its July and August peak. In high summer, Tsilivi and Zakynthos generally are popular and prices rise accordingly.

July and August are hot (often above 35°C) and crowded; the beaches fill by mid-morning and accommodation books up months in advance. If you come in summer, arrive early to the beach and seek shade through the middle of the day. October is peaceful and still warm enough to swim, with clear skies and reduced crowds — arguably the best month for those who want the island without the resort atmosphere.

Sea turtle nesting runs from June to August at Laganas Bay on the south coast. If you visit during this period, heed the protected zone signs — fines are real and enforcement has improved substantially in recent years.
