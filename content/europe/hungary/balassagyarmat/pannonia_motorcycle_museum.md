The Pannonia Motorcycle Museum focuses on Hungary's Pannonia motorcycles and the country's mid-20th-century motorcycling culture. It is a niche museum, but a good stop for visitors interested in design, mechanics, or everyday life under socialist-era industry.

It pairs well with Balassagyarmat's other small museums if you are already spending time in town.
