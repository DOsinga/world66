Bükfürdő sits in an unusually rich corner of western Hungary for day excursions. The medieval walled town of **Kőszeg** is 20 km to the south — its Old Town is one of the best-preserved in Hungary, with baroque architecture and a famous fortress where a handful of Hungarian knights held off the Ottoman army in 1532. **Sopron**, 46 km north and reached in under an hour, has the largest concentration of historic buildings in Hungary outside Budapest, plus good wine from the Fertőmelléki wine region.

**Nagycenk**, 35 km north, has the baroque Széchenyi family palace and the charming Nagycenk Museum Railway — a narrow-gauge line running through the estate, operated partly by children. It pairs naturally with a visit to the nearby **Fertőd Palace** (Esterházy Palace), arguably Hungary's most impressive baroque country seat, where Haydn spent most of his career.
