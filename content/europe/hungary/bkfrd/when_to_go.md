---
title: When to Go
type: section
---

Bükfürdő is a year-round destination because the indoor thermal pools and medical facilities operate regardless of weather. That said, the resort has two distinct seasons.

**Summer (June–August)** is peak season: the outdoor adventure pools and slides are open, families and groups fill the hotels, and the surrounding countryside is green and pleasant for cycling. Prices are at their highest and the complex is crowded on weekends. Book well ahead if visiting in July or August.

**Winter (November–March)** brings quieter conditions and often lower prices. The indoor medicinal pools and sauna world are the main draw — steaming into a hot mineral bath when it is cold outside is a genuine pleasure. The area gets occasional snow, which makes the flat Vas County landscape more attractive than usual.

Spring and autumn are the best compromise: reasonable prices, smaller crowds, and the outdoor pools start reopening in May.
