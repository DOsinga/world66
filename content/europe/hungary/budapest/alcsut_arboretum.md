Alcsut Arboretum is a historic landscaped park west of Budapest, laid out around the
former Habsburg estate at Alcsut. Its paths, old trees, clearings, and seasonal flowers
make it a gentler day trip than the capital's heavy museums and grand boulevards.

The arboretum is especially popular during snowdrop season, but it is pleasant whenever
you want quiet shade and a managed country walk. It is best for travelers with extra
time in Budapest who enjoy gardens, estate landscapes, and small detours.
