Bajor Gizi Actors' Museum covers almost two centuries of Hungarian theatre, from travelling players to the modern stage. The collection is housed in a neo-baroque villa with a garden, giving the museum a more personal feel than a formal national institution.

It is most rewarding for visitors interested in theatre, performance history, or Hungarian cultural life beyond the usual central sights.
