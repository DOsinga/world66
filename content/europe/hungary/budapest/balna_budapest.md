Balna Budapest, also known as CET or the Whale, is a glass-fronted cultural and exhibition building on the Danube near the Great Market Hall. Its riverfront setting and unusual contemporary design make it an easy add-on while walking between the market, Liberty Bridge, and the southern Pest embankment.
