Budakeszi Adventure Park is an outdoor ropes and obstacle-course park in the wooded hills west of Budapest. The courses have several difficulty levels, with bridges, hanging elements, and zip-style obstacles aimed at different ages and confidence levels.

It works best as a family stop or an active half-day outside the city centre, especially when combined with other Buda Hills attractions.
