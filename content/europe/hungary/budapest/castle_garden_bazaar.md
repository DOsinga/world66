Castle Garden Bazaar is the elegant Neo-Renaissance complex below Buda Castle, built in the late 19th century as a formal edge between the palace gardens and the Danube. Its arcades and terraces are worth seeing for the architecture alone, and the restored complex now hosts exhibitions and events.
