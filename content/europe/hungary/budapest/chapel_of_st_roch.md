The Chapel of St Roch is a small plague chapel beside the old hospital complex, with roots in the early 18th century and later additions after plague epidemics in Pest. The facade, statues, relics, and painted interior details make it more than a routine parish stop.

It is a short, focused visit near the edge of the Jewish Quarter and Jozsefvaros, best for travellers interested in Budapest's religious and medical history.
