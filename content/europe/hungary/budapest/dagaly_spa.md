Dagály Spa is a large bath and swimming complex on the Pest side of the Danube. It has year-round lap pools and hot outdoor pools, with more pools and grassy sunbathing areas open in summer.

It is less ornate than Budapest's famous historic baths, but more practical for swimming and a local day out. Choose it if you want pools, space, and a less ceremonial bath experience.
