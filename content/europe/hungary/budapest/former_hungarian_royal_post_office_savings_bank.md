The Former Hungarian Royal Post Office Savings Bank is one of Budapest's showy Art Nouveau facades. Designed by Odon Lechner and completed in 1901, it mixes Hungarian floral motifs, ceramic tiles, majolica, and gilded beehive symbols into a building that rewards a slow look from the street.
