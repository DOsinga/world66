The Foundry Museum occupies part of the old Abraham Ganz industrial complex in Buda. It is a small, specialist museum, but a worthwhile stop for visitors interested in Budapest's industrial history, metalworking, and the factories that helped shape the city's nineteenth- and twentieth-century growth.
