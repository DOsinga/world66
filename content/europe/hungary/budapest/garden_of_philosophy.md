The Garden of Philosophy is a sculpture group on Gellert Hill by Nandor Wagner, arranged around figures from major religious and philosophical traditions. It is a quiet detour from the main Citadella viewpoints, with broad city views nearby and a reflective tone that sets it apart from Budapest's grander monuments.
