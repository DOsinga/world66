The Golden Eagle Pharmacy Museum occupies the site of Buda's first pharmacy after the Ottoman period. Its compact exhibition covers Renaissance and Baroque pharmacy, with old jars, instruments, shop fittings, and the curious overlap between medicine, craft, and belief.

It is a small Castle District stop, best paired with nearby Matthias Church, Fisherman's Bastion, or the Hospital in the Rock.
