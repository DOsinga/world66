Gozsdu Yard Complex is a linked chain of courtyards and early 20th-century buildings in Budapest's Jewish Quarter. The passageways are now busy with cafes, bars, and small shops, but the appeal is the urban form: a dense courtyard complex that shows how this part of Pest was built.
