The Hungarian Museum of Trade and Tourism is a specialist museum in Obuda covering shops, restaurants, hotels, advertising, and travel culture in Hungary. Its collections include old menus, posters, coins, trade documents, and objects from everyday commercial life.

It is a niche but worthwhile stop if you are already exploring Obuda and want a museum away from Budapest's standard circuit.
