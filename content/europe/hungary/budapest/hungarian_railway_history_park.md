Hungarian Railway History Park fills a former MÁV railway workshop north of the city centre with historic locomotives, carriages, and railway equipment. It is more hands-on than a standard transport museum, with some exhibits visitors can board or try during operating days.

The park works best for rail fans and families who want a half-day attraction away from Budapest's usual central sights. Check opening days before going, as the outdoor setting and demonstrations are seasonal.
