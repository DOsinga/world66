The Hungarian Rock Hall of Fame is a niche music museum with instruments, stage equipment, recordings, and memorabilia from Hungarian rock history. Displays include guitars, drum kits, studio equipment, and items connected with well-known local performers.

It is best for visitors with an interest in Hungarian popular music rather than a general museum stop.
