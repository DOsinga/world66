Kazinczy Street Synagogue is the Orthodox synagogue of Budapest's Jewish Quarter, built in 1912-13 in a rich Late Art Nouveau style. Its facade, ironwork, benches, Ark, and interior details make it a strong companion to the larger Great Synagogue nearby, especially if you want to understand the quarter as a living religious district rather than only a memorial landscape.
