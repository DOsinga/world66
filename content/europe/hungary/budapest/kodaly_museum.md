The Kodály Museum preserves the former Budapest apartment of composer and ethnomusicologist Zoltán Kodály. The rooms are furnished with personal objects, folk ceramics, embroidery, photographs, programmes, and other keepsakes from a career that helped shape Hungarian music education and folk-song research.

It is a small, specialist museum, best suited to visitors with an interest in classical music, Hungarian culture, or historic apartments.
