Koller Gallery is a long-running private gallery in Buda's Castle District, founded in 1953. It focuses on contemporary Hungarian art, especially graphic art and engraving.

The gallery is set in a three-storey atelier house. The top floor includes a memorial room for Hungarian artist Amerigo Tot, making it a useful short stop for visitors already exploring the Castle District.
