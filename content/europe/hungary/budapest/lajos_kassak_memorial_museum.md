The Lajos Kassák Memorial Museum preserves the work and archive of Lajos Kassák, a major Hungarian avant-garde writer, editor, and visual artist. It is a focused museum rather than a broad art collection, best for visitors interested in modernism, Hungarian literature, and early 20th-century design.
