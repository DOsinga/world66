The Liszt Museum, formally the Liszt Ferenc Memorial Museum and Research Centre, occupies rooms in the Old Academy of Music where Franz Liszt lived and worked. The collection includes his personal objects, instruments, and period rooms.

It is a focused stop rather than a large museum, but it gives useful context to Budapest's classical music history and pairs naturally with a walk along Andrassy Avenue.
