The Lutheran Church of Budavar is the oldest Lutheran church in Buda, completed and consecrated in 1895. Its Neo-Baroque exterior fits the Castle District streetscape, while the simpler interior centres on a wood altar, a large cross, and a mosaic window above the sanctuary.
