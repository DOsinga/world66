The Margaret Island Water Tower is a 57-metre Art Nouveau landmark from 1911 and one of the island's most distinctive structures. Built in reinforced concrete, it now works as a lookout and exhibition space rather than just a utility building.

Climb for the 360-degree view over Margaret Island, the Danube, and central Budapest. The tower is most interesting when paired with a slow walk through the island's gardens, ruins, and riverside paths.
