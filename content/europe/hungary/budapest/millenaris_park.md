Millenaris Park is a Buda cultural complex built from a former Ganz iron foundry. The mix of exhibition halls, event spaces, lawns, and reused industrial buildings makes it a useful stop when something is on, and a pleasant pause between Széll Kálmán tér and the Castle District.
