The Museum of Hungarian Applied Folk Art presents objects from across Hungary, with exhibits on embroidery, weaving, pottery, carving, and other craft traditions. It is a good small museum for understanding how regional folk styles developed and how practical objects became decorative art.

The collection is most rewarding for visitors already interested in textiles, ceramics, or Hungarian rural culture.
