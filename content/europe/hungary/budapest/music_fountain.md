The Music Fountain on Margaret Island turns a simple waterside stop into a small summer show. Jets rise and fall to classical music and popular songs, with lights added after dark.

It is not a reason to cross Budapest by itself, but it works well as part of a Margaret Island walk. Come in the evening if you want the full effect, then linger by the Danube or continue toward the island's gardens and ruins.
