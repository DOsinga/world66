Nagyteteny Mansion, also known as the Szaraz-Rudnyanszky Mansion, is an 18th-century Baroque mansion in southern Buda. Its rooms are used for a furniture-history exhibition, so the visit is as much about historic interiors and decorative arts as the building itself.
