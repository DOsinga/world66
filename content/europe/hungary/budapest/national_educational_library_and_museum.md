The National Educational Library and Museum is a specialist museum of teaching, science education, and school history. Its displays include 18th-century physics demonstration equipment, celestial instruments, and early classroom tools that show how science was taught before modern laboratories.

It is best for visitors with an interest in education history, old scientific instruments, or the quieter corners of Budapest's museum scene.
