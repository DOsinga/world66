The National Széchényi Library, founded in 1802, is Hungary's national library and a major cultural institution inside Buda Castle. Its public exhibition traces the library's role in collecting and preserving Hungarian books, manuscripts, printed works, and historical material.

The permanent display is most useful for visitors interested in Hungarian cultural history, old books, and the intellectual world behind the country's national collections.
