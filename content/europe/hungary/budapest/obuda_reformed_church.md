Óbuda Reformed Church is an 18th-century church near the old centre of Óbuda. Its plain nave, tower, and spire reflect the district's Reformed community, while remains linked to the medieval royal castle can be seen in the basement.

It is a quiet stop that fits best with a walk through Óbuda's Roman and medieval layers, especially if you are already visiting nearby Aquincum or the district's small museums.
