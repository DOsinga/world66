The Old Town Hall in Buda is a Baroque building in the Castle District that served for generations as the administrative center of Buda and later of the 1st district. Its position near Matthias Church and the main Castle District streets makes it easy to notice while walking the hill.

It is a short architectural stop rather than a major museum, but it helps explain Buda as a working civic town, not only a royal and church precinct.
