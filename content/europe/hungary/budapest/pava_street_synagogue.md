Páva Street Synagogue was built in 1923 by Lipót Baumhorn and now forms part of Budapest's Holocaust Memorial Center. The restrained exterior gives way to a richly decorated interior, while the surrounding complex adds exhibitions and documentation on Hungarian Jewish history and the Holocaust.
