Raday Street Synagogue is the home of the Sim Shalom Progressive Jewish Community, part of the World Union for Progressive Judaism. The listed building was once an inn known as the Two Lions.

It is a modest but worthwhile stop for visitors tracing Budapest's Jewish heritage beyond the best-known synagogues in the old Jewish Quarter.
