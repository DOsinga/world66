This neoclassical Protestant church was built between 1816 and 1830 and remains one of the most important Reformed churches in Budapest. The central tower rises from the bases of two planned towers that were never completed, and the interior has galleries, white-and-gold decoration, and an organ made by the Viennese Deutschmann company in 1831.
