Renée Erdős House is a small local history museum in eastern Budapest, set in an 1895 house once occupied by the writer Renée Erdős. The building gives the museum much of its character, with a domestic scale that contrasts with Budapest's larger central museums.

It is a modest stop, best for visitors already exploring East Pest or looking for smaller neighbourhood museums away from the usual castle-and-boulevard route.
