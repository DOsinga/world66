The Royal Riding Hall is a Neo-Baroque building beside Buda Castle, rebuilt in the late 2010s after its wartime damage and postwar demolition. Its cream facade and formal proportions help restore part of the old palace district's ceremonial setting.

Most visitors will see it from the outside while exploring Castle Hill. It is used for events, so access depends on what is on.
