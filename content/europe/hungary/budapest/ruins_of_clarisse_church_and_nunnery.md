The Ruins of Clarisse Church and Nunnery preserve the basement walls of a medieval convent founded for the Poor Clares in the 14th century. The remains are modest, but they connect Obuda's Roman and medieval layers with the later city around it.

This is a specialist stop rather than a headline sight, best paired with nearby Aquincum or other Obuda history.
