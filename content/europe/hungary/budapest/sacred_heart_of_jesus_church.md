Sacred Heart of Jesus Church is the main Jesuit church in Pest, built between 1888 and 1909 to designs by Jozsef Kauser. Its brick exterior and tall interior make it a worthwhile detour while exploring Jozsefvaros, especially if you like Budapest's turn-of-the-century church architecture.
