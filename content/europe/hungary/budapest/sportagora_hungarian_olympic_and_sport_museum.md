SportAgora is an exhibition space at Budapest's stadium complex. It includes the Ferenc Puskas Stadium Visitor Center, with interactive displays on the old National Stadium and its modern replacement, plus an Olympic history exhibition on the ground floor. It is mainly for visitors with a specific interest in Hungarian sport or stadium history.
