St. Cyril and St. Methodius Bulgarian Orthodox Church is an unusual Byzantine-style church in Budapest, built in 1919 and associated with the city's Bulgarian community. Its design draws on Orthodox models from Bulgaria, including the Alexander Nevsky Cathedral in Sofia.

It is a niche architectural stop, but a worthwhile one for visitors exploring beyond Budapest's standard church circuit.
