St. Teresa of Avila Church stands on busy Nagymező utca, close to Budapest's theatres and music venues. The pale Baroque facade and quiet interior make it a good short pause while walking between the Opera House, Liszt Ferenc Academy of Music, and the Jewish Quarter.
