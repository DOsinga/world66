Szemlo Hill Cave is one of the public caves in the Buda Hills, known for mineral formations that give parts of it the nickname "the underground flower garden." The guided route is short, cool, and easy compared with Budapest's more adventurous cave tours.

It is a good choice if you want to see the limestone world under Buda without crawling or bringing specialist gear. English material is usually available even when the tour itself is not in English.
