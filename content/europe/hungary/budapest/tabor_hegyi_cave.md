Tabor-hegyi Cave is a protected dolomite cave in the Buda Hills, noted for both its geological and archaeological interest. The cave itself is not a casual walk-in attraction, but the hillside setting makes it a worthwhile target if you are exploring Obuda's quieter natural edge.

There is also an observation terrace nearby with a good view back toward Obuda, giving the stop more appeal than the cave name alone suggests.
