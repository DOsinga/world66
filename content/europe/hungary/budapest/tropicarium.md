Tropicarium is a family-oriented aquarium and small zoo in south Buda, best known for its shark tank. The collection also includes rays, tropical fish, reptiles, birds, insects, and a few mammals.

It is not one of Budapest's essential sights, but it works well for families or for a wet-weather break from churches, baths, and grand architecture.
