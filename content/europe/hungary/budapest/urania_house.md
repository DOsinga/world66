Urania House is a protected 1895 building by Henrik Schmahl, with a richly decorated facade on the edge of Budapest's Jewish Quarter. It has shifted from entertainment hall to cinema and theatre school over the years, so it works best as a quick architectural stop while walking between the district's cafes, synagogues, and ruin bars.
