The Vasarely Museum in Obuda is devoted to Victor Vasarely, the Hungarian-French artist closely associated with op art. The collection is full of bold geometric works, optical effects, and pieces that are easy to enjoy even without much art-history background.

It pairs well with nearby Aquincum or a slower visit to northern Budapest, away from the busiest central sights.
