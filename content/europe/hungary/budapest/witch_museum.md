The Witch Museum is a small interactive museum focused on witchcraft stories, objects, and folklore. Visits are guided, with a presenter leading guests through the exhibits and demonstrations.

It is a niche stop rather than a major Budapest museum, but it can work for visitors interested in folklore or unusual small museums away from the main tourist route.
