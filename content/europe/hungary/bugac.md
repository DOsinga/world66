Bugac is a small village on the Great Hungarian Plain (Alföld), sitting within the Kiskunság National Park — the puszta grasslands that stretch across central Hungary. It is best known as the place where you can watch the [famous Hungarian horse shows](/europe/hungary/bugac/bugac_horse_show), where csikós (herdsmen) demonstrate traditional horsemanship including the extraordinary feat of riding five horses at full gallop while standing astride the two rear animals.

The area is genuinely wild: salt lakes, juniper-dotted sand dunes, and open grasslands that feel unchanged for centuries. The [Kiskunság National Park](/europe/hungary/bugac/kiskunsag_national_park) around Bugac is one of Hungary's most important steppe habitats, with great bustards, rollers, and seasonal mirages shimmering across the flat plain. Day trips from [Budapest](/europe/hungary/budapest) or [Kecskemét](/europe/hungary/kecskemet) are easy, but a few hours in Bugac — watching the horse show and eating at the [traditional csárda](/europe/hungary/bugac/bugac_csarda) on the farm afterwards — makes a satisfying excursion from either city.
