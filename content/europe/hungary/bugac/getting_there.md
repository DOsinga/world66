---
title: "Getting There"
type: section
---

The nearest city is Kecskemét, about 35 kilometres to the northeast. From Kecskemét, local buses run to Bugac — the journey takes around an hour on winding country roads. By car the drive is straightforward, following Route 54 south and then minor roads west into the puszta.

From [Budapest](/europe/hungary/budapest), the easiest approach is to take the train to Kecskemét (about 90 minutes) and then change to the local bus, or to hire a car for the day. Several Budapest tour operators offer day trips that include the horse show, lunch at the csárda, and a walk in Kiskunság National Park, which saves the logistics if you are not driving.

There is no direct rail connection to Bugac village itself.
