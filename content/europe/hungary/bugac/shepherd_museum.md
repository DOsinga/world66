The Shepherd Museum is a small museum near Bugac that gives context to the pastoral culture of the Kiskunság plain. It works best as a stop paired with the surrounding puszta landscape, horse shows, and walks in Kiskunság National Park.

Entry is usually tied to the local visitor setup around Karikás Restaurant, so confirm access locally before making a special trip.
