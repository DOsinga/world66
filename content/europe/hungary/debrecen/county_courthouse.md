Debrecen's County Courthouse is one of the central civic buildings that rounds out the city's architectural picture beyond the Great Reformed Church and Kossuth Square. Its official function limits what most travellers will see, but the exterior belongs to the same walk through Debrecen's administrative and commercial core.

Use it as a waypoint rather than a headline sight. Debrecen is best understood by moving between churches, colleges, theatres, public offices, and market streets, seeing how a Calvinist market town grew into eastern Hungary's main city.
