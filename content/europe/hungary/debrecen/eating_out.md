---
title: Eating Out
type: section
---

Debrecen is famous in Hungary for one thing: the Debreceni sausage, a paprika-spiced smoked pork sausage that turns up grilled, sliced into stews, or alongside lecsó. Beyond that, the eating scene is solid traditional Hungarian — goulash, paprikás, stuffed cabbage — with a small but growing set of modern bistros around the city centre.
