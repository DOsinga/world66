Erdőspuszta Exhibition Hall introduces the wooded plain southeast of Debrecen, a landscape that is easy to miss if you only stay around the Great Forest and city centre. Exhibits focus on local nature, forestry, and rural life.

The site is modest, so it works best as part of a wider Erdőspuszta outing rather than a stand-alone museum trip. Travellers with a car will find it more useful than those relying on a tight city itinerary.
