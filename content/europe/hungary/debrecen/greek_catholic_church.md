Debrecen's Greek Catholic Church was built in 1910 in a Neo-Byzantine style, giving it a different mood from the city's Reformed landmarks. The inlaid altar, mosaic ornament, painted decoration, and coloured glass are the main reasons to step inside.

It is a selective stop rather than an essential one, but worthwhile if you are tracing Debrecen's religious architecture beyond the Great Church and the better-known Protestant sites.
