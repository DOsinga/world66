Kápolnás Street Synagogue is an early 20th-century synagogue in Debrecen, built in 1910 in an eclectic style with Byzantine and Moorish details. Its large dome gives it a stronger presence than its side-street setting might suggest.

It is a worthwhile architecture stop for visitors tracing Debrecen's Jewish heritage or walking between the city centre's churches, civic buildings, and museums.
