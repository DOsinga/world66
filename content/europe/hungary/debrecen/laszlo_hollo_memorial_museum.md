The Laszlo Hollo Memorial Museum focuses on the work and studio world of one of Debrecen's notable twentieth-century painters. It is a specialist stop, but it adds texture to the city's cultural scene beyond the Deri Museum and Reformed Great Church.

Go if you have an interest in Hungarian painting or want a quieter museum between the city's larger sights. Because it is small, check opening hours before walking out of your way.
