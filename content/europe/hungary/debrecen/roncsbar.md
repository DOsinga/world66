The Roncsbár ("Wreck Bar") is a sprawling outdoor ruin pub in a former industrial yard east of the centre — cobbled-together furniture, fairy lights, several stages, food trucks, and a beer garden that can swallow a few hundred people. It comes alive in summer with concerts most nights, ranging from local rock to international DJs, and is the obvious answer when someone asks where Debrecen's young people drink.

Open from late afternoon until late. Drinks are cheap; cover charge on bigger gig nights.
