The Zsuzsi Forest Railway is a small narrow-gauge heritage line running from Debrecen toward the wooded outskirts. It began as a working forest railway, but today its appeal is slower and more nostalgic: open countryside, family outings, and a different pace from the city centre.

It is best for railway fans, children, or travellers spending more than a quick stop in Debrecen. Check operating days carefully, as services can be seasonal or limited. When it is running, it pairs naturally with time in the Great Forest.
