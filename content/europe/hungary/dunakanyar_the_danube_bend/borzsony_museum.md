Borzsony Museum introduces the wildlife, archaeology, folk culture, and local history of the Borzsony range on the north side of the Danube Bend.

The collection is small but useful before or after walking in the hills, with displays on Ipoly Valley costume, pottery, peasant furniture, and finds from the surrounding mountains.
