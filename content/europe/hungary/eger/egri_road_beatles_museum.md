The Egri Road Beatles Museum is a guided museum devoted to the band, with films, period newsreels, listening stations, clothing, models, and more than a thousand pieces of Beatles-era memorabilia. It is an unusual stop in Eger, but a worthwhile one for music fans or anyone looking beyond the city's churches, castle, and wine cellars.
