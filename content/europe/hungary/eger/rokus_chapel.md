Rokus Chapel is a small Baroque votive chapel at the edge of the former Roch Cemetery. It was built in the early 18th century after a plague epidemic, a common origin for chapels dedicated to St. Roch.

The interior is plain today, but the chapel adds a quieter historical stop away from Eger's main square and castle.
