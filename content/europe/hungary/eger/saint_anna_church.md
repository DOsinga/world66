Saint Anna Church is a Baroque Roman Catholic church in Eger. It was once connected with the hospital of a Catholic merciful order, and its compact scale makes it a quiet contrast to the city's larger churches and monuments.

It is best visited as part of a walk through central Eger rather than as a standalone destination.
