Saint Rosalia Church is the parish church of Felnémet, just north of Eger. The present Baroque church was built in the 18th century on the site of an older medieval church, with a west-facing tower and sculptural decoration on the main facade.

It is a quiet local sight rather than one of Eger's headline monuments. Stop if you are already in Felnémet or exploring the northern edge of the city, especially if you enjoy small Hungarian churches with layered history.
