The Balint Balassa Museum covers Esztergom's local and regional history from a Baroque building in Vizivaros, the old Watertown below the basilica. The collection is useful context for a city that has been royal seat, church capital, border town, and provincial backwater at different points in its life.

It is not as essential as the basilica or the Christian Museum, but it fills in the story if you are spending more than a quick hour in Esztergom.
