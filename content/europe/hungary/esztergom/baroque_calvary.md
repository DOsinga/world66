Esztergom's Baroque Calvary is a hilltop religious ensemble with stations of the cross, a sculptural crucifixion group, and a Classicist chapel dedicated to Our Lady of Sorrows. The site layers local devotion with the memory of Saint Thomas Becket, whose relic is kept in the chapel.

It is a worthwhile detour for visitors interested in religious art and quieter historic places outside the basilica-focused centre.
