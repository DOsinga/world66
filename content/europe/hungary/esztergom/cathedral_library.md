Esztergom's Cathedral Library is one of Hungary's richest religious libraries, housed in a 19th-century building designed by Jozsef Hild. Its collection includes codices, incunabula, and early Hungarian religious texts, making it a strong specialist stop near the basilica and Christian Museum.
