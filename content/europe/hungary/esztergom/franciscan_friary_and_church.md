The Former Franciscan Friary and Church of the Holy Cross sits in Esztergom's lower historic area, close enough to the basilica and Christian Museum to fold into the same walk. The single-towered church and adjoining friary date mainly from the 18th century.

It is not the reason to come to Esztergom, but it adds another layer to the city's religious architecture. Stop if you are exploring Vizivaros on foot and want more than the headline sights on the hill.
