Old County Hall is a one-storey Baroque building that served as Esztergom's county hall from 1714 to 1805. It now houses the Balassa Bálint Museum, so the stop combines historic architecture with local museum displays.

It sits close to the main sights around Víziváros and the basilica, making it easy to include on a walk through the old centre.
