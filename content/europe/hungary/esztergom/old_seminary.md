The Old Seminary is a long historic building below Esztergom's basilica, shaped by the steep ground between its northern and southern facades. Its southern side faces the basilica in a sober Neoclassical style, while the northern side has a more romantic look.

It is a quick architectural stop rather than a major sight, but it fits naturally into a walk around the castle hill and Víziváros. Look for the statues of archbishops George Szelepcsényi and Nicholas Olah by the entrance.
