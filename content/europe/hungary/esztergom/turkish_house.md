The Turkish House is a small historic building near Esztergom's old county hall. Although its present form is Baroque, part of the structure dates from the Ottoman period, making it one of the city's quieter reminders of that era.

It is a compact stop in Víziváros, close to the Christian Museum, the basilica, and the remains of the royal palace.
