---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

April through October is the most pleasant time to visit. Summer weekends can be crowded, particularly when tour groups come up from Budapest, but the hilltop is large enough that it rarely feels overwhelmed. The Danube Bend scenery is at its best in late spring and autumn, when clear skies and moderate temperatures make the walk around the hill genuinely enjoyable.

The summer boat service from Budapest runs on weekends from May to September — arriving by water is the most dramatic approach and one of the better day excursions in the Budapest region. In winter the boat stops running and the town is very quiet; the basilica and Christian Museum remain open but most riverside restaurants close.
