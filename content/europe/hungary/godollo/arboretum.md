The Godollo Arboretum is a large wooded collection of old trees and planted landscapes on land once tied to the palace estate. It covers hundreds of hectares, giving visitors a quieter, more natural counterpoint to the town's royal palace and pilgrimage church.

Paths run through mixed woodland and open areas, making it useful for a longer walk rather than a quick photo stop. It is best for travellers with extra time in Godollo or a specific interest in gardens and trees.
