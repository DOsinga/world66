King's Hill Pavilion is a small Baroque pavilion from the 1760s near Gödöllő's royal landscape. The hexagonal building once held 54 oil paintings of Hungarian rulers set into the panelled walls; the surviving display is now made up of replicas after most originals were lost or destroyed.

It is a short secondary stop for visitors already seeing the Royal Palace of Gödöllő and its parkland.
