This cemetery near the Mariabesnyo pilgrimage church is best known for the grave of Count Pal Teleki, a twice-serving Hungarian prime minister, geographer, academic, and major figure in Godollo's history. It is a quiet add-on to a visit to the pilgrimage church rather than a standalone half-day sight.
