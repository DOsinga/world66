The Premonstrant Church in Godollo belongs to the town's religious and educational history, connected with the Premonstratensian order that played an important role in local schooling. Its architecture is less famous than the royal palace, but it gives the town another centre of gravity beyond Habsburg nostalgia.

It is worth a short stop while walking through Godollo, especially if you are already visiting the palace and want a fuller sense of the town around it. The church works as a quiet counterpoint to the grander royal story, rooted more in community, teaching and worship.
