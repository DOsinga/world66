Raday Castle in Pecel is a baroque aristocratic house associated with the Raday family, close enough to Godollo to work as a regional heritage detour. It lacks the fame of Godollo's royal palace, but that makes it quieter and more specialised.

The appeal is architectural and literary-historical rather than spectacular. It is best for travellers with a car or a strong interest in Hungarian noble estates, who want to look beyond the obvious Habsburg-era headline sights.
