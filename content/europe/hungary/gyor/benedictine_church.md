The Benedictine Church is one of Győr's strongest Baroque interiors, built in the 17th century and later enriched with painted ceilings, carved furnishings, and a richly worked pulpit. It stands close to the heart of the old town, so it is easy to fold into a walk around Széchenyi Square.

The appeal is inside rather than outside. Look up at the sanctuary and nave paintings, then take time with the details of the altar, pulpit, and organ case.
