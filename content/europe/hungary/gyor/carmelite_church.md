The Carmelite Church is a former Carmelite church in Győr, built between 1713 and 1725. Designed by Márton Witwer, it is one of the old centre's Baroque religious buildings and contains 18th-century altars and sacristy fittings.

It is close to the cathedral and Bishop's Castle, so it fits naturally into a short walk through the historic core.
