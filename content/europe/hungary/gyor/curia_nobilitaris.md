Curia Nobilitaris is a small historic house in Gyor's old centre, with late Renaissance origins from the 16th century and later Baroque alterations. It is the kind of detail that rewards a slow walk through the city rather than a sight to cross town for.

Look for it as part of a circuit around the cathedral, Szechenyi Square, and the surrounding lanes. The appeal is in the layered facade and the way Gyor's older architecture keeps appearing between more obvious monuments.
