Gyor's Diocesan Treasury holds church art and liturgical objects from the city's long Catholic history. The collection ranges from old books and manuscripts to chalices, vestments, and other pieces that were made to impress both the faithful and visiting dignitaries.

It is a compact museum, but worthwhile if you are already visiting the cathedral quarter. The best pieces give a close look at the craftsmanship behind the city's ecclesiastical wealth.
