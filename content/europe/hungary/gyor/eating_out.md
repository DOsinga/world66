---
title: "Eating Out"
type: section
---

Győr has a solid restaurant scene for a city its size, concentrated in the pedestrian streets around Széchenyi Square and along the riverside. Hungarian standards — gulyás, pörkölt, lángos — are well represented, but the city also has Italian, international, and newer bistro-style restaurants that cater to its mix of students, Audi employees, and day-trippers from Vienna and Budapest.
