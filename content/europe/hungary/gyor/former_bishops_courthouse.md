The Former Bishop's Courthouse is a historic Győr building now used for art exhibitions, including a permanent display on sculptor Miklos Borsos. It is not one of the city's grand headline sights, but it fits well into a walk through the old centre if you are interested in Hungarian sculpture and smaller museums.
