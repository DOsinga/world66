The former Camillian Church is one of the smaller Baroque church interiors in Győr's old centre. Its value is in the way it fills out the city's dense church-and-monastery landscape, where religious orders left a compact cluster of facades, towers and courtyards around the historic streets.

It is not a headline sight on the level of the cathedral or Bishop's Castle, but it rewards travelers already exploring the old town on foot. Treat it as a short architectural stop while moving between Széchenyi Square and the riverfront.
