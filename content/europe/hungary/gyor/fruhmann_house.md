Fruhmann House is the former home and workshop of stove-maker Antal Fruhmann. Its exhibition focuses on the tile-stove trade around Gyor, a narrow subject but an unusually concrete one: craft, domestic design, and local industry in one small museum.

It is not an essential stop for every visitor, but it rewards travellers who like specialist collections and the quieter details of old Central European towns.
