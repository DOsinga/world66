Fülesbástya Zoo is a small branch of Xantus János Zoo inside the New Bastion, one of Győr's surviving Ottoman-era fortification remains. The exhibit mixes the old defensive setting with animals and plants from South America, including a large paludarium and a walk past a leaf-cutter ant colony.
