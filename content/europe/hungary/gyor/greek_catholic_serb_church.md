The Greek Catholic Serb Church is a small historic church in Győr, rebuilt in 1727 on the site of an older Turkish-era building. Its simple walled exterior is lifted by the onion-shaped tower cap, a detail that makes it stand apart from the city's larger Baroque churches.

It is best visited as part of a walk through Győr's quieter historic streets, especially if you are interested in the city's Serbian and Greek Catholic heritage.
