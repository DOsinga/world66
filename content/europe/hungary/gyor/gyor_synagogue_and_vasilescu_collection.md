Győr Synagogue is a striking nineteenth-century building that now also serves as a cultural venue and exhibition space. The Vasilescu Collection adds a strong art component, bringing modern Hungarian works into a former sacred space.

The combination of architecture, Jewish heritage, and art makes it more than a minor local gallery. It is a worthwhile stop on a walk through Győr's historic centre if opening hours line up.
