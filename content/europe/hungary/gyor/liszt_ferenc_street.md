Liszt Ferenc Street is a short central Győr street lined with historic houses from the 18th and 19th centuries. The interest is cumulative rather than tied to one building: Baroque, Classicist, and later historicist facades sit close together, showing the city's layered civic architecture.

It is an easy detour while walking between Széchenyi Square, the cathedral area, and Győr's museums. Look especially for the former Jesuit and arts-school buildings and the ornamented corner houses, which give the street its strongest character.
