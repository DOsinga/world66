Napoleon House is a 17th-century Baroque building in Győr's historic centre, named for Napoleon's overnight stay after the Battle of Kismegyer. It now hosts temporary exhibitions of Hungarian and international contemporary graphic art, so it works best as a short stop when an exhibition is open.
