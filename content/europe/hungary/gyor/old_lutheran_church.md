The Old Lutheran Church was built between 1783 and 1785, tucked into a closed courtyard and originally without a tower. Its main interior feature is a late Baroque pulpit altar, with an 1817 red-sandstone baptistery in front.

It is a quiet stop near Gyor's historic centre and pairs well with the nearby synagogue and old streets.
