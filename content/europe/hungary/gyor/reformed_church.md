Győr's Reformed Church is a late historicist, neo-Gothic church from 1905, built with a corner tower and a unified Gothic-inspired interior. South of it stands the converted older Reformed church from 1784, a reminder of the congregation's longer history in the city.

It is a modest but worthwhile architectural stop while walking between Győr's better-known churches and squares.
