Roch and St. Sebastian's Roman Catholic Parish Church is one of Győr's smaller ecclesiastical sights, but it adds texture to a city already rich in Baroque architecture. Churches dedicated to Roch and Sebastian often carry plague-era associations, and this one fits that Central European pattern of faith, illness, and civic memory. Its scale is modest, which makes the details easier to notice.

It is not a reason to visit Győr on its own, but it is a worthwhile stop if you are already walking between the old town's churches, squares, and palaces. Step inside if the doors are open, and treat it as part of the city's layered religious landscape rather than a standalone monument.
