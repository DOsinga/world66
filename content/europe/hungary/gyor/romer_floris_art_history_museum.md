Romer Floris Art and History Museum uses the Esterhazy Palace for exhibitions on local history and art. Its permanent displays include the Radnai Collection, a private collection with Hungarian and European works.

The museum is a good indoor stop in Gyor's old centre, close to the cathedral, Bishop's Castle, and other Baroque streets around the river junction.
