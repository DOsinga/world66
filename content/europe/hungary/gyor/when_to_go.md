---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

Győr is pleasant from spring through to early autumn. **May, June, and September** are the best months: warm enough to enjoy the outdoor café terraces and the river promenades, without the heat of July and August. Summer sees the Győr Summer festival bring outdoor concerts and events to the riverbanks through July and August — a good reason to visit, but also the busiest period.

Winter is cold and occasionally foggy — the river valleys of northwest Hungary get significant mist — but the December Christmas market on Széchenyi Square is a genuine local event rather than a tourist production. The city is close enough to Vienna and Budapest that it makes a comfortable day trip in any season.
