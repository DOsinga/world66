Puszta Animal Park introduces the traditional livestock breeds of the Hortobagy, including grey cattle, racka sheep, water buffalo, and other animals tied to the Great Plain. It helps make the national park's pastoral culture visible at close range.

The park is most useful before or after a wider puszta excursion, when the breeds and herding traditions have more context. It is family-friendly but also genuinely informative for adults interested in rural landscapes.
