title: Things to Do
type: section
---

Kalocsa is a compact town and most of its attractions are within easy walking distance of each other around Szentháromság tér. The [cathedral](/europe/hungary/kalocsa/kalocsa_cathedral) and [Archbishop's Palace](/europe/hungary/kalocsa/archbishops_palace) dominate the square; the Paprika Museum on Szt. István király út and the Folk Art House are a short walk away. You can cover the highlights comfortably in a day trip from Budapest or Kecskemét, though the town rewards a slower pace.

The paprika harvest in September brings the streets alive — strings of drying red peppers hang from every available surface, and the smell of the mills carries across the whole town.
