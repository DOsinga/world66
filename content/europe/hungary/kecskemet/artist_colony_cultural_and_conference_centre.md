The Artist Colony Cultural and Conference Centre occupies an Art Nouveau house designed by Bela Jánszky and Tibor Szévessy in the 1910s. It hosts exhibitions and cultural programmes, but the building itself is the main reason to include it on a walk through Kecskemét's early 20th-century architecture.
