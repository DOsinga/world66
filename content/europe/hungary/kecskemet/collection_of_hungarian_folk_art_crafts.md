The Collection of Hungarian Folk Art Crafts is a modest but useful Kecskemet stop if you care about the handmade side of Hungarian culture. Expect decorated objects, textiles, ceramics, and craft traditions rather than blockbuster displays.

It works best alongside Kecskemet's Art Nouveau architecture and other small museums. The collection gives context to patterns and materials you may see later in markets and rural houses, and it is a good rainy-hour option in the city centre.
