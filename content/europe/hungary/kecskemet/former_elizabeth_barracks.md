The Former Elizabeth Barracks show a sterner side of Kecskemét than its famous Art Nouveau palaces. The complex reflects the city's military and administrative past, with long facades and institutional scale standing apart from the more decorative buildings around Kossuth Square.

It is mainly an architecture-and-history stop, not a polished attraction with a visitor circuit. See it while exploring the wider city if you enjoy built heritage and want Kecskemét to feel less like a single-style postcard.
