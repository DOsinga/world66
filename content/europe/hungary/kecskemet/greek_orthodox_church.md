Kecskemet's Greek Orthodox Church was built in 1829 in neoclassical style. Its simple exterior gives way to a historic interior with 19th-century decoration and an iconostasis dating from the same period.

The church is a short walk from Kossuth Square and fits naturally into a circuit of the city's central religious and civic architecture.
