The House of Nature introduces the habitats of the Danube-Tisza Interfluve, the flat sandy region around Kecskemét and Kiskunság National Park. The permanent exhibition covers local wildlife, landscapes, and traditional rural work, making it a practical first stop before heading into the national park.
