The Hungarian Photography Museum is housed in a former Orthodox synagogue and focuses on the history of photography in Hungary. The setting is part of the appeal: the restored building gives the small museum more character than a standard gallery space.

It is a good stop for visitors already exploring Kecskemet's museums and Art Nouveau centre, especially if you have an interest in photography or visual culture.
