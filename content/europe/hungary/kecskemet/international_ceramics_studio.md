The International Ceramics Studio is a working cultural institution rather than a conventional museum, which makes it a good fit for Kecskemét. The city has a serious applied-arts streak, and the studio brings ceramic artists from Hungary and abroad into that tradition.

Access and exhibitions vary, so check ahead rather than assuming a drop-in gallery visit. If the programme lines up, it offers a more specific view of the city than another handsome square or church: clay, kilns, and contemporary craft in a town that takes design seriously.
