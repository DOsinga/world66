The Museum of Hungarian Naive Artists is housed in Bano House, a Baroque building from around 1730. Its collection focuses on self-taught Hungarian artists, with colorful paintings and objects that often draw on village life, folk memory, and dreamlike scenes.

The museum adds a different note to Kecskemet's center, where most sightseeing revolves around churches and Art Nouveau architecture. It is a good short stop while walking between the city's main squares.
