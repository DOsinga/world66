Nyíri forest is one of Kecskemét's nearby green escapes, a flat woodland area used for walks, cycling and low-key outdoor time. In a city better known for Art Nouveau architecture and fruit brandy, it gives visitors a look at the sandy Great Plain landscape around town.

It is not a dramatic hike, but it can be a pleasant break if you have a car or bike. Go for quiet paths, shade and a sense of the countryside that begins just beyond the city streets.
