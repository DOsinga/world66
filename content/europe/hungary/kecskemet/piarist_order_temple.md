The Piarist Order Temple is an early-18th-century Baroque church in central Kecskemet. Designed by Andras Mayerhoffer and built between 1724 and 1730, it has a single nave, a straight-ended chancel, and a tower set into the southwest facade. It is easy to combine with Kecskemet's other central churches and Art Nouveau landmarks.
