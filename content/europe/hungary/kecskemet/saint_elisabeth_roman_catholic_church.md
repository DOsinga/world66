Saint Elisabeth Roman Catholic Church is a modest Kecskemét religious landmark that broadens the city's architectural walk beyond its better-known Art Nouveau civic buildings. It is a local church first, not a museum.

Include it if you are already exploring nearby streets or comparing Kecskemét's churches. It is a short, respectful stop rather than a primary destination.
