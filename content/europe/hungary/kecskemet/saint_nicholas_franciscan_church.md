Saint Nicholas Franciscan Church stands on Kecskemet's central square, close to the city's better-known Art Nouveau landmarks. The church was completed in the late 18th century, though its fabric mixes older Gothic traces with later Baroque rebuilding.

The detail to look for is the purgatory relief beside the church, a small Calvary remnant from 1792. It is an easy stop while walking between Kossuth Square, City Hall, and Cifrapalota.
