Szorakatenusz Toy Museum and Workshop displays Hungarian peasant toys, including wooden churches, handmade games and other folk objects. It is small, hands-on in spirit and more playful than Kecskemet's formal art collections.

The workshop can sometimes be visited for an additional fee, making it a good family stop near the city centre.
