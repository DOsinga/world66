---
title: "Things to Do in Kecskemét"
type: section
---

Kecskemét's attractions cluster around the linked Kossuth and Szabadság squares in the city centre. The main draw is the architecture — Ödön Lechner's exuberant City Hall, the Cifrapalota Art Nouveau palace, the synagogue and several fine churches all sit within a five-minute walk of each other. Beyond the squares, the Zwack Pálinka distillery, the Bozsó Collection, and the Naïve Art Museum are all worth time.
