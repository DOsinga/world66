The Forestry Museum of Stajer-house sits in the western part of the Kőszeg Protected Landscape Area and explains the woodland, game, and forestry traditions of the Kőszeg Mountains. It is a small specialist museum, best paired with a hike in the surrounding hills rather than treated as a standalone half-day sight.
