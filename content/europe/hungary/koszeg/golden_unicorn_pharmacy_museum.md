The Golden Unicorn Pharmacy Museum preserves an old Kőszeg pharmacy in a building that began as an inn and was later remodelled by the Jesuits. Its cabinets, jars, and fittings make a small but atmospheric stop in the old town.

It works best as part of a short walk around Jurisics Castle, Heroes' Gate, and the neighbouring historic streets.
