Marzipan Tale World is a small, sweet-natured stop near Kőszeg's old centre, where fairy-tale figures and Hungarian scenes are modelled in marzipan. It is not a major museum, but it is a pleasant change of pace if you are travelling with children or have already seen the castle and main square.

The cellar adds an odd extra layer: aerial photographs and 3D images of Hungarian castles, tied loosely to wine regions. Come for a short visit rather than a long one.
