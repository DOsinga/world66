The Post Museum of Kőszeg is a small specialist museum covering postal history in the town and beyond. Exhibits range from Kőszeg's own post history to stamps showing postmen from around the world, old post carriages, and material on letter censorship.

It is best for travellers who enjoy compact local museums and the everyday systems that once tied small towns to the wider world.
