Sgraffito House is one of the small architectural pleasures around Koszeg's old centre. The house dates from around 1560, with later 18th- and 19th-century changes and a Baroque facade layered over its older bones.

It is a quick stop rather than a destination on its own, but it rewards a slow walk between Jurisics ter, the castle, and the town's churches.
