Torkos House is a protected historic building in the centre of Kőszeg. Its origins go back to the Middle Ages, though its present appearance comes largely from an 18th-century Baroque restoration.

It is a quick architectural stop rather than a major museum, but it fits naturally into a walk around Kőszeg's old streets, churches, gates, and castle.
