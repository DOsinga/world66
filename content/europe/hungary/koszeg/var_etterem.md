The restaurant closest to the castle entrance is the natural choice after a morning visiting the museum. The menu is traditional Hungarian — pörkölt, stuffed cabbage, grilled meats, and freshwater fish — with portions sized for people who have just spent the morning walking cobbled streets. The terrace looks toward the castle walls, which is reason enough to eat here on a fine day. Prices are modest; the wine list includes local Kékfrankos.
