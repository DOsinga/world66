Dörgicse is a small north-shore Balaton village with a loose cluster of old churches, temple ruins, a historic bridge, and former granaries tied to the area's wine-growing past. The main draw is the sequence of medieval and baroque religious sites around Alsódörgicse, Felsődörgicse, and Kisdörgicse rather than one single monument.

It works best as a short heritage stop while exploring the quieter countryside behind Balatonfüred and Tihany.
