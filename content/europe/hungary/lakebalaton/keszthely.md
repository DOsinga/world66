Keszthely is the cultured old town at the western end of [Lake Balaton](/europe/hungary/lakebalaton), where the resort mood of the lakeshore meets aristocratic Hungary. It is less flashy than Siófok and less postcard-pretty than Tihany, but it has the strongest single sight on the lake: [Festetics Palace](/europe/hungary/lakebalaton/keszthely/festetics_palace), a grand Baroque residence with a library, park, and museum complex.

The town works well for travellers who want Balaton without surrendering entirely to beach life. You can swim at the municipal strand, walk the small centre, spend a half day at the palace, then take a bus or taxi to [Lake Hévíz](/europe/hungary/lakebalaton/keszthely/lake_heviz), one of Hungary's classic thermal bathing experiences.

Keszthely is also a good western anchor for the north shore. The wine villages, the beaches around [Vonyarcvashegy](/europe/hungary/lakebalaton/keszthely/vonyarcvashegy), and the viewpoint at [Balatongyörök](/europe/hungary/lakebalaton/keszthely/balatongyorok) are close enough for slow day trips by bike, car, or summer bus.
