Amazon House Visitors' Center is a small exhibition space in Keszthely focused on the history of travel by land, water, and air. Its "Travelling Aristocrats" exhibition fits naturally with the town's palace and museum circuit.

Visit it as an add-on while exploring central Keszthely, especially if you are already seeing Festetics Palace or the nearby specialist museums.
