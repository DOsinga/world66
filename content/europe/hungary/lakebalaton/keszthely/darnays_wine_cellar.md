Darnay's Wine Cellar is a small piece of Lake Balaton's older wine country, with a 17th-century timber structure and an engraved main beam dated 1644. It is worth a short look if you are already exploring the Keszthely side of the lake and want something more local than the palace-and-beach circuit.
