Dino and Adventure Park Rezi is a family attraction in the hills north of Keszthely, with life-size moving dinosaur models, play areas, rope courses, climbing walls, trampolines, and small adventure activities. It is aimed squarely at children, and makes most sense as a break from palace visits and lake beaches.

The park is outside the town centre, so plan on driving or arranging local transport from Keszthely or the western Balaton resorts.
