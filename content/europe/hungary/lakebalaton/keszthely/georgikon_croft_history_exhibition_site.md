The Georgikon Croft History Exhibition Site is a farmstead museum tied to Keszthely's long agricultural teaching tradition. Exhibits cover Hungarian agricultural education, corn production, viniculture and rural trades.

The carriage display, wheelwright and blacksmith workshop give the museum more texture than a simple local-history collection, and the working steam-plough is the standout object.
