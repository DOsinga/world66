House of Stoves is a small specialty museum in Keszthely devoted to historic stoves, ovens, cookers, and heating equipment from different regions. The subject is narrow, but the collection gives a hands-on sense of domestic design and craft.

It is best treated as a short add-on near Festetics Palace and the town centre, especially if you like small local museums.
