Libas Beach is a sandy Lake Balaton beach on the north side of Keszthely, with shallow water, lifeguards in season, showers, changing rooms, and a playground. It is a practical stop for families, and rentals for kayaks, canoes, paddle boats, and windsurfing gear add a bit more to do than simply swim.
