Nagymező is a meadow and excursion area in the Keszthely Hills above the northwestern shore of Lake Balaton. It is used for picnics and as a starting point for walks into the hills, including routes toward lookout towers, springs, and caves.

Come for an easy outdoor break from the lakefront towns, especially if you want a short hike rather than another beach afternoon.
