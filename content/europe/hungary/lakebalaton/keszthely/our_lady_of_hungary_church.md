Our Lady of Hungary Church stands on the site of an older medieval church and still carries traces of Keszthely's long religious history. Its Neo-Gothic tower and rose windows come from 19th-century renovation, but the real surprise is inside.

In 1974, 15th-century frescoes were found in the sacristy, adding a medieval layer to a building that first appears more recent from the street.
