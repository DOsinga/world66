The Radio and Television Collection is a small specialist museum in a former Baroque granary. Its collection includes radios, televisions, and broadcast equipment gathered by local collector Jozsef Nadler, with some rare early and mid-20th-century pieces.

It suits visitors who like technical museums or who are already spending time around Keszthely's cluster of smaller collections. Allow a short visit unless you have a particular interest in historic electronics.
