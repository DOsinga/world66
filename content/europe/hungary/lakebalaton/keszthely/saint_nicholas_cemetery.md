Saint Nicholas Cemetery is best known for the Festetics Mausoleum and its cemetery chapel. The neoclassical mausoleum, built in 1925 for Princess Mary Hamilton by Count Taszilo Festetics, has the form of a small classical temple.

It is a quiet companion stop to Festetics Palace, useful for visitors interested in the family history behind Keszthely's grandest landmark.
