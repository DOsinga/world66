Saint Nicholas Cemetery is mainly visited for the Festetics Mausoleum and its cemetery chapel. The mausoleum, built in 1925 for Princess Mary Hamilton, uses the language of a small neoclassical temple and adds another chapter to the Festetics family's presence in Keszthely.
