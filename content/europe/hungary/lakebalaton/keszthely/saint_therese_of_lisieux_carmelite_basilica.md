The Saint Therese of Lisieux Carmelite Basilica is a 20th-century neo-Romanesque church connected with a Carmelite monastery. Its rounded arches, pale stonework, and compact massing make it a noticeable stop while walking through central Keszthely.

It does not compete with Festetics Palace as a headline sight, but it adds another layer to the town's religious and architectural landscape.
