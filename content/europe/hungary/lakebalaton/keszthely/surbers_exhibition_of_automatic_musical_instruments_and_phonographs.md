Surber's Exhibition of Automatic Musical Instruments and Phonographs is a small specialist museum in Keszthely devoted to mechanical music. The collection focuses on automatic musical instruments and phonographs from the era before radio and record players became common.

It is a niche stop, but a good one for visitors interested in old sound technology, mechanical instruments, or unusual private collections.
