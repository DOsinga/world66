Szepkilato is a viewpoint above Lake Balaton with a wide panorama over the water and the surrounding hills. It is a simple stop, but the view makes it worthwhile on a clear day.

There is also a Roman spring nearby, so it works well as a short scenic detour around Vonyarcvashegy and Balatongyorok.
