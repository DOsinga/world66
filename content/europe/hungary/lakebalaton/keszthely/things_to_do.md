---
title: Things to Do
type: section
---

Start with [Festetics Palace](/europe/hungary/lakebalaton/keszthely/festetics_palace), the most complete aristocratic monument around Lake Balaton. The palace and park justify the stop even if you are not staying overnight.

After that, Keszthely becomes more relaxed: wander the centre, visit the [Keszthely Synagogue](/europe/hungary/lakebalaton/keszthely/keszthely_synagogue), swim from the town beach, or make the short trip to [Lake Hévíz](/europe/hungary/lakebalaton/keszthely/lake_heviz). For a broader lake view, head east along the shore to [Vonyarcvashegy](/europe/hungary/lakebalaton/keszthely/vonyarcvashegy) and [Balatongyörök](/europe/hungary/lakebalaton/keszthely/balatongyorok).
