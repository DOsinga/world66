The Toy Exhibition and Tuskevar Waxworks is a small family-oriented museum in Keszthely. Its toy displays include Steiff plush toys, Hungarian tin toys from Gyor, rockets, rocking horses, prams, and other pieces from the 20th century.

It is a light stop, best suited to families or visitors already touring Keszthely's cluster of small museums.
