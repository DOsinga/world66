---
title: Getting Around
type: section
---

Tihany village, the abbey, and the Inner Lake are walkable, but the slopes are real and summer heat can make short distances feel longer. Wear decent shoes if you plan to leave the main streets.

Bikes work well on the lower roads and lakeshore routes, though the climb to the abbey is stiff. If you arrive by car, park outside the tightest village centre and continue on foot.
