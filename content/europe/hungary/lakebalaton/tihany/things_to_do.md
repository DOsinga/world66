---
title: Things to Do
type: section
---

[Tihany Abbey](/europe/hungary/lakebalaton/tihany/tihany_abbey) is the essential stop, both for its history and for the view over Lake Balaton. From there, walk down toward the [Inner Lake](/europe/hungary/lakebalaton/tihany/inner_lake), then loop through the old village and the lavender shops if you can tolerate the summer crowds.

The peninsula is also good for easy walking. The volcanic ridges, reed beds, [lavender fields](/europe/hungary/lakebalaton/tihany/tihany_lavender_fields), and ferry shore make a compact landscape with more variety than its size suggests.
