Lenti Castle is a modest stop, but it gives the town some historical depth. The settlement's medieval importance came from its castle, and the remaining site is a useful reminder that Lenti was not always just a spa and borderland service town.

See it while walking around town rather than making a special trip. The thermal baths and forest railway are stronger reasons to come.
