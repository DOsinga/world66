Lenti Thermal Spa is the town's main reason to linger. It has indoor and outdoor pools, sauna areas, and medicinal thermal water, but the scale is comfortable rather than grand.

It is best treated as half a day rather than a quick dip. Bring a towel and flip-flops, and check the current pool and sauna schedule before committing, especially outside summer.
