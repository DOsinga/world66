This small railway exhibition sits by the narrow-gauge line and makes most sense if you are already riding the Csömödér railway. It covers the forest, timber, and rail history of the area.

Do not expect a major museum. Its value is local colour: a few rooms and objects that help explain why a little town like Lenti has such an appealing forest railway.
