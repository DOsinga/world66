Martonvásár is a small town in Fejér County, about half an hour southwest of [Budapest](/europe/hungary/budapest) by train. Its reason to stop is clear: the Brunszvik estate, where a white neo-Gothic palace stands in a protected park and keeps the memory of Ludwig van Beethoven's friendship with the Brunszvik family.

This is not a town for a long stay, but it makes a neat cultural day trip. The palace complex combines the [Beethoven Memorial Museum](/europe/hungary/martonvasar/beethoven_memorial_museum), the [Agroverzum Science Adventure Center](/europe/hungary/martonvasar/agroverzum_science_adventure_center), and the old palace garden into one easy visit. A short walk away, the Nursery School Museum tells the unusual story of Teréz Brunszvik, who founded the first kindergarten in Hungary.

Martonvásár works best if you like small, focused places: one estate, one strong story, and enough green space to slow the day down.
