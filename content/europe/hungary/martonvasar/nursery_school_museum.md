The Nursery School Museum is a small specialist museum in a Romantic-style building from the 1880s. Its subject is Teréz Brunszvik, the Martonvásár-born educator who founded Hungary's first kindergarten, and the development of nursery education in Hungary.

It is not a headline sight, but it gives the town a second local thread beyond Beethoven. Stop in if you like social history, education, or small museums with a very specific subject.
