Saint Anne Roman Catholic Church is a small Baroque landmark near Martonvásár's main estate sights. Built in 1773, it has frescoes by Cymbal and late-Baroque sculpture inside.

It is not a long visit, but it adds a useful historic stop if you are already in town for the Brunszvik estate, the Beethoven museum, or the palace garden.
