---
title: Things to Do
type: section
---

Most visits revolve around Brunszvik Palace. Start with the [Beethoven Memorial Museum](/europe/hungary/martonvasar/beethoven_memorial_museum), then walk the [Palace Garden](/europe/hungary/martonvasar/palace_garden) and add the [Agroverzum Science Adventure Center](/europe/hungary/martonvasar/agroverzum_science_adventure_center) if you are travelling with children or have an interest in agriculture. The [Nursery School Museum](/europe/hungary/martonvasar/nursery_school_museum) is small, but it gives the town a second story beyond Beethoven.
