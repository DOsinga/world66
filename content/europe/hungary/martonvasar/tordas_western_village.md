Tordas Western Village is a family-oriented activity park near Martonvásár, built around a playful western theme. The mix includes archery, gold washing, escape games, a rodeo bull, playgrounds, craft activities, and seasonal rides, so it works best as an easy outing with children rather than as a historic sight.
