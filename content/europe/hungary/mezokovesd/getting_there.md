---
title: Getting There
type: section
---

Mezőkövesd sits on the main rail and road corridor from [Budapest](/europe/hungary/budapest) to [Miskolc](/europe/hungary/miskolc), making it one of the easier small towns in northern Hungary to reach.

**By train.** Direct services from Budapest Keleti station take around two and a half hours. Trains from Miskolc are 30–40 minutes. The station is on the south side of town, a 15-minute walk from the main square.

**By bus.** Volánbusz operates regular services from Budapest and Eger; the bus station is central. Buses to surrounding villages run from here.

**By road.** Mezőkövesd is just off the M3 motorway between Budapest and Miskolc — about 130 km from Budapest, 1.5 hours by car. Eger is around 35 km west.
