Diosgyori Papermill Museum preserves the history of the local paper industry, with tools, machinery, and displays connected to handmade paper. The workshop area gives the visit more texture than a simple factory display.

Guidance may be Hungarian-only, so it is best for travelers with an interest in industrial heritage or paper-making rather than a general first stop in Miskolc.
