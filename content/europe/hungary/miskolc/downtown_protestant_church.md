Miskolc's Downtown Protestant Church, often called the Rooster Church, is a classicist Calvinist church built between 1786 and 1808. Its 68-metre tower is the tallest church tower in the city, and its rooster-topped spire is one of the centre's easy landmarks.

It is a worthwhile stop while walking through downtown Miskolc, especially if you are comparing the city's varied churches and 19th-century civic streets.
