Feledy House is part of the Miskolc Gallery network and gives the city's art scene a more intimate setting than a large museum. Its association with Gyula Feledy and regional exhibitions makes it a useful stop for visitors interested in Hungarian graphic art and local culture.

It is a secondary sight, but a credible one in central Miskolc. Check current exhibitions before going, since the appeal depends more on the programme than on a fixed blockbuster collection.
