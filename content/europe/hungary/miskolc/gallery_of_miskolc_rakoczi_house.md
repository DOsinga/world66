Rákóczi-House is part of the Gallery of Miskolc network and focuses on contemporary art, temporary exhibitions, and museum education. It is most useful for visitors who want to see the city's current art programme rather than only its churches and historic streets.

The gallery is close to several central Miskolc sights, so it is easy to combine with the Herman Ottó Museum, the Avas area, or a walk through the old centre.
