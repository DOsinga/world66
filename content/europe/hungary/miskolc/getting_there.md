---
title: Getting There
type: section
---

Miskolc is 175 km northeast of Budapest, connected by direct InterCity trains from Keleti station (about 2 hours). Trains are the easiest option — the station is central and services are frequent throughout the day.

By road, the M3 motorway runs between Budapest and the Miskolc area, with the journey taking around 2 hours by car depending on traffic. There is no major airport at Miskolc; the nearest international hub is Budapest Ferihegy, so most visitors arrive by rail.

Intercity buses from Budapest also serve Miskolc, taking a similar time to the train. The bus station is adjacent to the train station.
