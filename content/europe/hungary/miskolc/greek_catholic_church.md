Miskolc's Greek Catholic Church adds a different religious texture to a city better known for its main street and nearby cave baths. The interior is the reason to go, especially the iconostasis and the Eastern Christian atmosphere.

It is a quiet, respectful stop rather than a major blockbuster. Visit if you are already exploring central Miskolc and want to see how varied the city's religious heritage is.
