Lake Hamori is a small artificial lake at Lillafured, surrounded by wooded hills and walking paths. Visitors come for a quiet stroll, boat rental in season, and views of the lake below the resort buildings. It pairs naturally with the nearby caves, palace hotel, and forest walks around Lillafured.
