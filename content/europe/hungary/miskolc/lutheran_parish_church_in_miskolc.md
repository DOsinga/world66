The Lutheran Parish Church in Miskolc is a restrained 19th-century church with an Empire-style pulpit and a later tower. Inside, the main reason to pause is the altarpiece by Bertalan Szekely, one of Hungary's important historical painters.

It is not the city's grandest church, but it adds a quieter layer to a walk through central Miskolc's religious and civic buildings.
