Miskolc Zoo sits in the hills outside the city and began as a wildlife park for animals of the Bukk Mountains. It now mixes local species with exotic animals, including camels, monkeys, parrots, tigers, and leopards.

The zoo is most useful for families or travellers spending longer in Miskolc. Expect a hilltop outing rather than a quick central-city stop.
