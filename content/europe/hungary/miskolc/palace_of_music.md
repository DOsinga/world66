The Palace of Music is a Neo-Baroque concert and education building from the 1920s, used by the Bela Bartok Secondary School and the Bela Bartok Music Institute. Its concert hall is the reason to look inside, with frescoes, gilded stucco, and regular performances.

It is a modest stop rather than a headline sight, but worthwhile if you are interested in Miskolc's musical life or early 20th-century public architecture.
