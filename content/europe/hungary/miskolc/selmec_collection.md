The Library and Collection of Selmec keeps the University of Miskolc's historical material, from mining-themed stamps and miniature books to photographs, certificates, fine art, and technical objects. It is a specialist stop, but a good one if you are interested in the mining schools and industrial history that shaped this part of northern Hungary.
