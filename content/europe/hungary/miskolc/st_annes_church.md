St. Anne's Church, often called the Red Church for its roof, is a Roman Catholic parish church west of central Miskolc. It was built between 1816 and 1823, with late Baroque and Neoclassical elements.

The church is a small but worthwhile addition to a Miskolc architecture walk, especially for visitors already exploring the Avas side of the city.
