The Telecommunications Museum traces more than a century of communication technology in Miskolc, with telephones, switchboards, telegraph equipment, transmission gear, and early mobile phones. It is housed in the city's main post office building.

The museum is a compact specialist stop, most worthwhile for visitors interested in industrial history or old working technology.
