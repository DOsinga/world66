---
title: Things to Do
type: section
---

Miskolc's standout attraction is the thermal [Cave Bath at Miskolctapolca](/europe/hungary/miskolc/cave_bath), but the city rewards a longer visit. [Diósgyőr Castle](/europe/hungary/miskolc/diósgyőr_castle) is the most photogenic historical sight, [Lillafüred](/europe/hungary/miskolc/lillafüred) and the [Bükk National Park](/europe/hungary/miskolc/bukk_national_park) offer good hiking in forest scenery, and the [Avas Hill wine cellars](/europe/hungary/miskolc/avas_lookout_tower) give a glimpse of a much older Miskolc. The [Herman Ottó Museum](/europe/hungary/miskolc/otto_herman_museum) is the best regional museum in northeast Hungary.
