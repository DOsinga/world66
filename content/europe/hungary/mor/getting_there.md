---
title: Getting There
type: section
---

Mór sits on the railway line between Komárom and Székesfehérvár, with several direct trains a day from each. From Budapest, the journey usually requires a change at Székesfehérvár and takes around two hours. The station is on the western edge of town, a fifteen-minute walk to the main square.

By car it is straightforward — about seventy kilometres from Budapest on the M1 motorway, then south on Route 81. Parking is easy and free almost everywhere in town. If you are doing a cellar circuit, a designated driver is the only sensible option; there is no taxi service to speak of after dark.
