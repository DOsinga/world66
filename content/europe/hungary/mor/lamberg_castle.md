The Lamberg Castle is the most prominent building in Mór, a U-shaped baroque palace built between 1762 and 1766 to designs attributed to Jakab Fellner, the same architect who worked at Tata and on parts of the Eszterházy estate at Pápa. Its yellow-washed walls and shallow central courtyard face the parish church across the small main square.

The interior houses the Lamberg Castle Cultural Centre, with occasional exhibitions and concerts in the great hall. The park behind the castle, with old plane trees and a quiet pond, is a pleasant place to sit between cellar visits.
