---
title: Things to Do
type: section
---

Mór is small enough that you can see the main sights in a single afternoon, with the rest of your time given over to the wine cellars on the hillsides. The town's centre clusters around the baroque Lamberg Castle and the parish church, and from there the streets climb out into vineyards. There are no museums of the world-class kind here — the appeal is in the wine, the architecture, and a quietness that has not yet been polished for tourism.
