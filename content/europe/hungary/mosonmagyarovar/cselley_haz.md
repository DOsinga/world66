A handsome late-Baroque townhouse on the main pedestrian street, now used as a small cultural centre with rotating exhibitions of paintings, ceramics, and photography. Worth poking your head in if you are walking past — the courtyard with its arcaded gallery is the architectural draw, with or without an exhibition.
