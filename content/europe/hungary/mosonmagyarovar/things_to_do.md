title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Mosonmagyaróvár is a small town with one star attraction — its thermal baths — and a handful of historic buildings worth seeking out. The Magyaróvár Castle anchors the older half of town, a quiet pedestrian centre runs between the two old settlements that merged to form the modern city, and the Mosoni-Danube branch gives the place a riverside character that few small Hungarian towns can match.
