Nyíregyháza is the capital of **Szabolcs-Szatmár-Bereg** county in the far northeast of Hungary, a flat plains city of around 115,000 people close to the borders with Slovakia, Ukraine and Romania. Its historic centre is a compact set of 19th-century squares dominated by the [Kossuth tér](/europe/hungary/nyiregyhaza/kossuth_ter) and the bulky [Greek Catholic cathedral](/europe/hungary/nyiregyhaza/greek_catholic_cathedral), with a smaller Lutheran church, a Calvinist church, an Orthodox church and the [synagogue](/europe/hungary/nyiregyhaza/nyiregyhaza_synagogue) all within a few minutes' walk — a reminder of the unusual religious mix of this corner of the Great Hungarian Plain.

The main draw for most visitors is **Sóstó**, the wooded lake and spa district 5 km north of the centre, where warm saline waters feed the [Sóstó Thermal Baths](/europe/hungary/nyiregyhaza/sosto_thermal_baths) and the open-air [Sóstó Village Museum](/europe/hungary/nyiregyhaza/sosto_village_museum) — one of the best ethnographic skansen museums in Hungary, with farmhouses, windmills and churches relocated from villages across the eastern plains. The [Nyíregyháza Zoo](/europe/hungary/nyiregyhaza/sosto_zoo) is also unusually good for a regional Hungarian city. The [Jósa András Museum](/europe/hungary/nyiregyhaza/josa_andras_museum) in the city centre covers the archaeology and folk culture of the wider region.
