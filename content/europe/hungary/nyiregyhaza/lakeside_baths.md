The Lakeside Baths at Sóstó are part of Nyíregyháza's main leisure district, north of the city centre. The draw is straightforward: open-air pools, lake surroundings, and a summer holiday atmosphere rather than a formal spa experience.

It is most useful for families or travellers spending more than a quick transit stop in Nyíregyháza. Combine it with the Sóstó village museum, zoo area, or a walk around the lake, and it becomes part of a relaxed day outside the city centre.
