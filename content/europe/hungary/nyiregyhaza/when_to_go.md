---
title: When to Go
type: section
---

Nyíregyháza sits on the open plains of northeastern Hungary and has a continental climate — cold winters, hot summers, without the moderating effect of hills or water. The best time to visit for comfortable sightseeing is **May to September**, when the Sóstó outdoor thermal pools are most enjoyable and the village museum can be explored in warm weather.

**July and August** are the busiest months at Sóstó. If the zoo, baths, and outdoor museums are your main interest, a weekday visit avoids the weekend crowds. The shoulder months of May, June, and September offer pleasant temperatures with fewer visitors.

**Winter** is quiet but not without appeal — the thermal baths are if anything better in cold weather, and the city functions normally year-round. Snow is common but short-lived.
