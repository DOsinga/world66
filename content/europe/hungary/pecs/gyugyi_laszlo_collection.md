The Gyugyi Laszlo Collection is a focused display of Zsolnay ceramics, with pieces from the factory's golden age of historicist and Secession-era design. It is especially useful if you want to understand why Pecs is so closely tied to Zsolnay's glazed architectural ornament and decorative art.

The collection is best treated as a specialist museum stop rather than a quick glance. Guided visits are often the most rewarding way to make sense of the styles, techniques, and changing tastes represented in the cases.
