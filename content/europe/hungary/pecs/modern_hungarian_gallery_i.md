Modern Hungarian Gallery I is one of Pecs's specialist art stops, with works linked to Ferenc Martyn, Amerigo Tot, and other Hungarian modern artists. It is best paired with the city's other museum collections rather than treated as a standalone reason to visit, but art-minded travellers will find a useful local thread through twentieth-century painting, sculpture, and graphic design.
