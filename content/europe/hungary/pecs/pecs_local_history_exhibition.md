The Local History Exhibition is a useful stop for visitors trying to make sense of Pécs beyond its headline monuments. Its displays place the city's Roman, medieval, Ottoman, and civic history into a more connected narrative than you get by visiting individual buildings alone.

This is a supporting sight rather than a must-see museum, but it earns its place in a city with unusually dense history. Go before or after walking the old centre, when maps, objects, and period context can help anchor what you have seen outside.
