The Zsolnay Museum presents the ceramics, designs, documents, and family history behind Pecs' famous Zsolnay factory. The displays focus on the eosin glaze and colourful pyrogranite that made the factory important to Hungarian Art Nouveau.

It is a strong stop for visitors interested in design, decorative arts, or the industrial heritage that shaped modern Pecs. Pair it with the broader Zsolnay Cultural Quarter for more context.
