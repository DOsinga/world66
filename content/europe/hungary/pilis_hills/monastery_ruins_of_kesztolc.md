These Gothic monastery ruins near Kesztolc date from the second half of the 13th century. The remains are modest, but they add a historic stop to walks through the Pilis Hills and help connect the landscape with the medieval religious life of the region.

Come for a short look rather than a long visit, and combine it with other Pilis Hills sights or nearby hiking.
