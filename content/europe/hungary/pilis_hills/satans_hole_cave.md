Satan's Hole Cave is a hydrothermal cave system in the Buda Hills near Solymár, with several kilometres of known passages. It is not a casual walk-in cave; visits are mainly for experienced cavers or travellers arranging a proper guided trip.

The cave is a good fit for the Pilis Hills page because it shows the rougher underground side of the landscape that sits just beyond Budapest's suburbs.
