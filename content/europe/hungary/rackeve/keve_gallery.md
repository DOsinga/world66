Keve Gallery is a small contemporary art space in a Renaissance-style building near the centre of Ráckeve. The exhibitions focus on applied and fine art, so it works best as a short cultural stop rather than a major museum visit.

Pair it with the Serbian Orthodox church, the Danube-side water mill, and a walk through the old centre.
