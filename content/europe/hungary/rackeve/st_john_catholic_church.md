St John Catholic Church stands on Szent István tér in the centre of Ráckeve. It was built between 1791 and 1799, with a high altar from the early 1800s and interior painting by László Patay.

It is not as unusual as the Serbian Orthodox church nearby, but it helps frame the town centre and makes a natural stop while walking between the Danube, the square, and the old civic buildings.
