Fekete-hegy Observation Tower is a modest but satisfying lookout in the wooded hills north of Sátoraljaújhely. It rises above the tree line near the shoulder of Fekete-hegy, opening views across the Hegyköz basin and toward the Milic massif.

This is a walkers' stop, not an urban monument. Use it as part of a hike in the Zemplén hills, with proper shoes and a downloaded route if you are not confident with Hungarian trail signage. In clear weather the view is worth the climb; in mist, the forest does most of the work.
