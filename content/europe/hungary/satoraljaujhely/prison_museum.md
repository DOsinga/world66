The Prison Museum in Satoraljaujhely is an unusual specialist museum devoted to the history of incarceration, punishment, and prison administration in Hungary. It is attached to a town better known for wine-country access and Zemplen hills, which makes the museum a distinctive stop rather than another small-town heritage room.

Displays typically focus on cells, uniforms, restraints, documents, and the changing systems used to police and punish. It is not a cheerful attraction, but it adds a serious historical note to a visit and is most worthwhile for travellers interested in law, social history, or the less-polished institutions behind Central European towns.
