type: section
---

Sátoraljaújhely is not a museum town — it's a working border settlement with an attractive main square and easy access to the Zemplén Hills. The real draw is the landscape: the chairlift up Magas-hegy opens up walks with views into Slovakia, and the wine routes of the Tokaj-Hegyalja wine region begin almost at the town's doorstep.
