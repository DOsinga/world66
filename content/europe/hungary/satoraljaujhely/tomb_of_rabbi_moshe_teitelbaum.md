The tomb of Rabbi Moshe Teitelbaum is a Hasidic pilgrimage site in the old Jewish cemetery of Satoraljaujhely. Teitelbaum, known as the Yismach Moshe, was an important rabbi here in the early 19th century.

The setting is quiet and partly overgrown, so it is best visited with the respect due to an active religious memorial and cemetery.
