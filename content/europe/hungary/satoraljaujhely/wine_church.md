The Wine Church is an unusual 1913 trading house built for the wine business of the Tokaj-Hegyalja region. Despite the name, it is not a church, but its gabled frontage and decorative details give it a ceremonial look.

Look for the coats of arms of the Hegyalja market towns on the facade. It is a quick architectural stop that fits naturally with Sátoraljaújhely's wine cellars and hillside sights.
