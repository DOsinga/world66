The Bánfalva Carmelite Monastery stands on the western side of Sopron, away from the compact old town and closer to the wooded hills that lead toward the Austrian border. Its church and monastic buildings preserve a quieter religious layer of the city, with Baroque details and a retreat-like setting.

It is a good addition if you have already seen Sopron's central squares and towers and want something slower. The monastery's value is partly architectural, partly atmospheric: a reminder that Sopron's history was shaped not only by merchants and border politics, but also by religious communities on the edge of town.
