The Brennbergbánya Mining Memorial House preserves the mining history of the hills southwest of Sopron, where coal mining shaped a community quite different from the wine bars and Baroque streets of the old town. It is a small but specific industrial-heritage stop.

Because it is outside central Sopron, it suits travelers with a car, bicycle or a focused interest in local history. Pair it with a trip into the surrounding hills rather than treating it as an old-town sight.
