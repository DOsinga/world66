The Catholic Collection in Sopron preserves religious art and liturgical objects from the historic
Diocese of Győr-Sopron area. It is a specialist museum rather than a blockbuster sight, but it fits
Sopron's dense old-town fabric of churches, chapels, and ecclesiastical houses.

Visitors interested in sacred art will find the collection useful for understanding the material
culture behind Sopron's churches: vestments, vessels, sculpture, and devotional works that rarely
remain visible in active parish spaces. It is best paired with the Goat Church, St. Michael's, and
the Firewatch Tower area rather than treated as a stand-alone outing.
