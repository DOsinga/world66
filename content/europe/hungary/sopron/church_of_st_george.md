The Church of St. George is one of Sopron's smaller but rewarding historic churches, with architecture that reflects the town's layered medieval and Baroque past. Its appeal lies in the details rather than the scale: altars, vaulting, and the quiet feel of an old urban parish church.

It is best folded into a walk around Sopron's inner town, where churches, courtyards, and fragments of walls sit close together. Visit respectfully, and treat opening hours as variable.
