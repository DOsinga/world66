---
title: "Eating Out"
type: section
---

Sopron's restaurant scene is better than most Hungarian towns of comparable size — the Austrian proximity brings a certain expectation of quality, and the local wine region gives kitchens good raw material to work with. Hungarian standards (goulash, fish soup, pork dishes) appear alongside more Austrian-influenced cooking. The streets around Fő Tér and along the inner ring have the highest concentration of options.
