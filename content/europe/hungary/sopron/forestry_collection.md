Sopron's Forestry Collection is a niche museum, but it fits the city better than it may sound. The surrounding hills and forests have long shaped the region, and the collection looks at timber work, forest management, hunting, tools, and the culture of woodland labour.

This is a stop for curious travellers rather than a universal must-see. If you have already covered the old town's towers and churches, it adds a local subject that most city guides skip: the practical relationship between Sopron and the wooded landscape around it.
