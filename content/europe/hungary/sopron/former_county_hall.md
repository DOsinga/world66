The Former County Hall is one of the dignified civic buildings that gives central Sopron its old administrative weight. Its Baroque facade faces the historic core with the confidence of a town that once mattered deeply to the surrounding county.

It is best treated as part of a slow walk through the old town rather than a standalone expedition. Stop for the architecture, then keep threading the lanes around the Firewatch Tower, churches, and courtyard passages nearby.
