The Former Feherlo Inn is one of Sopron's small architectural stops, a reminder that the old town's appeal is built from many individual facades rather than a single monument. Its value is in the street scene: the layered Baroque and later town-house details that make a walk through central Sopron rewarding.

Do not treat it as a destination on its own. Mark it on a self-guided wander between the Firewatch Tower, churches, and museums, and use it as an excuse to slow down and look up at the buildings rather than simply crossing the centre.
