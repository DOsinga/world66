The Former Szechenyi Mansion is a classical town palace in Sopron's historic centre, associated with Count Ferenc Szechenyi, whose collections helped form the Hungarian National Museum and National Library. The building is not a long visit, but it adds a useful layer to a walk through Sopron's old streets.

Look for it as part of the town's cluster of noble houses, churches, and small museums. Its appeal is strongest for travellers interested in Hungarian history and understated urban architecture.
