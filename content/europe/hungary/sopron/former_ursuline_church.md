The former Ursuline Church and Convent is one of Sopron's notable 19th-century religious buildings, built in a Romantic style with neo-Gothic details. Its scale and decoration make it stand out among the smaller historic buildings near the old town.

It is a worthwhile architectural stop while walking Sopron's center, especially for visitors interested in churches and convent history.
