Gambrinus House is one of the historic burgher houses that make Sopron's old centre rewarding at walking pace. Its details are easy to miss if you rush from the Firewatch Tower to the churches, but the building helps show how dense and layered the medieval town fabric is.

Treat it as part of an architectural trail rather than a stand-alone attraction. Sopron works best when you let small façades, courtyards, plaques, and street corners accumulate into a picture of an old border town shaped by merchants, wine, and shifting empires.
