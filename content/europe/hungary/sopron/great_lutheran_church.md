The Great Lutheran Church was built in 1783 and is one of Hungary's largest Lutheran churches. Its hall interior is divided by eight Tuscan columns, with a wider central nave and galleries over the side aisles, making it a worthwhile stop while walking Sopron's compact historic centre.
