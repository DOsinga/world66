Kolostor utca is one of Sopron's better small streets, lined with town palaces and former aristocratic houses rather than a single blockbuster monument. The pleasure is in the facades: carved gateways, courtyards glimpsed through arches, and the way wealthy families folded baroque taste into a compact medieval street plan.

Come here while wandering between the Firewatch Tower, the churches, and the old city walls. It is not a long visit, but it helps explain why Sopron rewards slow walking: many of its best details sit on ordinary-looking side streets.
