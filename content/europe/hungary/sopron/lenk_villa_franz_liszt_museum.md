Lenk Villa is an eclectic 1890 merchant's villa associated with Sopron's Franz Liszt heritage. The house has a formal entrance with columns, a tympanum, and stone figures, with a small park around it.

The grounds add as much interest as the villa: rare plants, Baroque sculptures, old Lutheran cemetery stones, and the Baker Cross from 1484. It is a compact stop for travellers already exploring Sopron's historic centre.
