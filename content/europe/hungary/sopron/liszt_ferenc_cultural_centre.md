The Liszt Ferenc Cultural Centre occupies the former casino, one of the handsome historic buildings in central Sopron. The five-storey building now hosts concerts, theatre, exhibitions, conferences, and other cultural events, and is named for Franz Liszt, who was born in the former Sopron County.
