Muck Lookout Tower stands in the Sopron Hills, part of the wooded landscape that makes the city feel
more outdoorsy than its compact old town first suggests. The tower is a hiking destination rather
than an urban monument, reached through forest tracks and hill paths.

From the top, the reward is a broad view over the border-country terrain around Sopron, where
Hungary and Austria meet in ridges, vineyards, and woodland. It is best for travellers who want to
balance Sopron's churches and squares with a half-day walk.
