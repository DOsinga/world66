Sopron's Pharmacy Museum is one of those small Central European museums that rewards curiosity. Historic cabinets, ceramic jars, scales, and pharmaceutical tools show the old craft of medicine in a town whose centre is already dense with burgher houses and church towers.

It will not take long, but it fits beautifully into a Sopron old-town wander. Use it as a short, specific stop between larger sights; the pleasure is in the detail, and in seeing how much trade, science, and domestic life once met behind a pharmacy counter.
