Rejpal House is one of Sopron's smaller historic buildings, the kind of burgher-house stop that rewards travellers already paying attention to the old centre's facades. It does not compete with the Firewatch Tower or the main squares, but it adds another layer to the city's built texture.

Visit it as part of a slow walk rather than as a destination in itself. Sopron's appeal comes from accumulated details: courtyards, plaques, medieval fragments, and houses like this that keep the old town from feeling like a single-monument visit.
