Sopron Botanical Garden is a quiet university garden with tree collections and planted paths away from the busier old town. It offers a softer, greener side of Sopron, especially appealing in spring and autumn.

The garden is not a blockbuster attraction, but it is a pleasant pause if you are staying longer than a quick old-town visit. Check access before going, since institutional gardens can have variable opening arrangements.
