Sorhazdomb, or Beerhouse Hill, is a modest lookout on the edge of Sopron. It does not compete with the region's bigger viewpoints, but it gives a pleasant, low-effort walk and a useful angle back toward the town and surrounding hills.

Use it as a breather between heavier historic sights. The climb is short, the setting is green, and the view helps explain why Sopron feels so connected to the wooded borderland around it. It is a small POI, but a worthwhile one if you like orienting yourself on foot.
