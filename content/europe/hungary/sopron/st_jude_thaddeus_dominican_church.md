St. Jude Thaddeus Dominican Church is a Baroque church and former monastery complex just outside Sopron's tight old centre. The church was built in the early 18th century, with its twin towers added later, and the former monastery still gives the block much of its historic shape.

It is best visited as part of a walk through Sopron's churches, courtyards, and museum streets, especially if you are tracing the town's Baroque layer beyond the main square.
