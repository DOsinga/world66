Storno House Museum occupies one of Sopron's notable historic houses on the main square, with interiors and collections linked to the Storno family. The displays mix decorative arts, furniture, and local history in a building that is itself part of the experience.

It is a natural addition to a walk around Fő tér, the Firewatch Tower, and Sopron's old lanes. The museum rewards visitors who enjoy interiors and the layered lives of old town houses.
