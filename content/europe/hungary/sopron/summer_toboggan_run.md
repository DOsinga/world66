The Summer Toboggan Run is a small seasonal family attraction in the wooded hills above Sopron. It gives children and casual visitors a bit of outdoor activity between heavier historic stops in the old town.

It is not a major sight, but it earns a place for families staying more than a quick afternoon. Check operating dates and weather before going, and combine it with a walk or viewpoint in the surrounding Lőverek area.
