Sopron's historic synagogue is an important reminder of the Jewish community that lived inside the medieval town. The building and surviving details help make that history visible in a city better known for walls, towers, and baroque facades.

It is a focused heritage stop rather than a large museum. Visitors interested in Central European Jewish history should include it while walking Sopron's old centre.
