Tomalom Beach is a modest lakeside bathing spot a few kilometres north of Sopron. It has the practical things that make a summer afternoon easy: a children's pool and slide, jetties for sunbathing, showers, drinking water, and rentals for boats, pedal boats, and canoes. It is not a major sight, but it is a useful warm-weather escape if you are staying in town.
