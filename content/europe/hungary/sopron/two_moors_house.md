Two Moors House is one of Sopron's small facade discoveries, known for the sculpted figures that give the building its name. Like many sights in the old town, it rewards attention to doorways, coats of arms, and upper-storey details rather than a ticketed visit.

Use it as part of an architectural walk through central Sopron. It will not compete with the Firewatch Tower or the main museums, but it helps fill in the dense historic texture that makes the town enjoyable on foot.
