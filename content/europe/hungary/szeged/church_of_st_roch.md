The Church of St. Roch is a twin-towered Roman Catholic church built in 1910. Its neo-Gothic design was prepared by Ferenc Raichle, following plans by Sandor Aigner and Karoly Rainer.

It is a quieter stop than Szeged's main cathedral or synagogue, but the sharp spires and early 20th-century church architecture make it worth a short detour while walking the city centre.
