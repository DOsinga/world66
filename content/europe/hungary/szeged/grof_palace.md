Grof Palace is one of the buildings that makes Szeged a pleasure to explore with your eyes up. Standing on Tisza Lajos Boulevard, it is a protected Secession-style landmark, with the decorative confidence and urban scale that followed the city's great rebuilding period.

You usually admire it from the street rather than tour an interior, but that is enough. Combine it with Reok Palace, the New Synagogue, and the central squares for a self-guided Art Nouveau walk through a city that wears its architecture more lightly than Budapest.
