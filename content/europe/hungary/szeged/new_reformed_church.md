The New Reformed Church is a 20th-century Calvinist church in Szeged, planned in 1928 and completed in 1944. Its brick facade and tower make it a useful contrast to the city's better-known Votive Church and Art Nouveau landmarks.

This is a short architectural stop rather than a major sight. It works best while walking the surrounding streets, especially if you are tracing Szeged's churches and post-flood rebuilding history.
