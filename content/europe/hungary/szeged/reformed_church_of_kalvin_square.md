The Reformed Church of Kálvin Square is a useful architectural stop in Szeged, especially because the city is better known for its grand Votive Church and Art Nouveau civic buildings. Its Protestant identity and square setting add another layer to the rebuilt post-flood cityscape.

It is a short stop rather than a destination on its own. Include it while walking between central squares, museums, and the Tisza-side streets.
