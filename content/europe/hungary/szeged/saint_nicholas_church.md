Saint Nicholas Church is a late Baroque Roman Catholic church completed in 1767. Its design is associated with architect Vencel Lechner, and it adds a quieter historic stop to Szeged's centre beyond the city's better-known synagogue and Votive Church.

It is most worth seeking out if you are already walking the old streets north of the Tisza-side core and want to see another layer of Szeged's 18th-century church architecture.
