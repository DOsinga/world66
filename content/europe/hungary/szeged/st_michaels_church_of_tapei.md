St. Michael's Church of Tapei is a Roman Catholic church with roots in the medieval village of Tape, now part of Szeged. The sanctuary preserves Gothic fabric from an earlier church, while the nave and tower show later Baroque and 20th-century rebuilding.

It is a modest but worthwhile stop for visitors interested in Szeged's older religious buildings beyond the city centre.
