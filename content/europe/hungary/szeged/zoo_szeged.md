Zoo Szeged is a substantial, leafy zoo on the western side of the city, laid out with enough space that it feels more like a park than a compact urban menagerie. The collection is broad, with mammals, birds, reptiles, and conservation-focused exhibits spread through shaded paths.

It is most useful for families or anyone staying in Szeged long enough to want a half-day away from the cathedral square and riverfront. Public transport reaches the area, and the slower pace makes it a good warm-weather option when the city centre feels exposed in the afternoon sun.
