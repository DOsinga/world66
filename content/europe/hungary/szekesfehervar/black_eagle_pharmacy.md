The Fekete Sas Patikamúzeum on Fő utca is a beautifully intact 18th-century pharmacy interior, set up as a museum after the working pharmacy closed in 1971. The room is small but extraordinary — carved wooden cabinets crammed with apothecary jars, mortars and pestles laid out as if the pharmacist had just stepped out, and a fine baroque ceiling overhead.

It takes ten minutes to look around and is an essential stop in the old town, particularly if you have any interest in design or the history of medicine. The entry fee is nominal.
