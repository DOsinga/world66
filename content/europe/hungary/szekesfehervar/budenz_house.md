Budenz House is a late Baroque town house that gives a quieter counterpoint to Székesfehérvár's churches and royal ruins. Its main draw is the private art collection of Ervin Ybl, displayed in rooms that still feel more domestic than institutional.

The Scottish Art Nouveau-style study is the highlight for design lovers. It is a small stop, but a good one if you are already exploring the old centre on foot.
