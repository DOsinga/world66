The City Gallery's Deák Collection fills a historic building in the small Esterházy Palace with Hungarian painting from the 20th century. The collection includes work by artists such as József Rippl-Rónai, László Mednyánszky, the Eight, Béla Czóbel, and the Egry circle.

It is a good companion to Székesfehérvár's medieval ruins and civic sights, especially if you want a quieter art stop near the centre.
