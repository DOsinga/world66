Csók István Picture Gallery is a small art stop in Székesfehérvár, best known for Vilmos Aba-Novák's large panel on Hungarian-French historical relations. The work was made for the Hungarian pavilion at the 1937 Paris World's Fair.

The gallery suits travellers who want more than the city's royal ruins and church history. It adds a 20th-century artistic thread to an old town usually read through kings, bishops, and medieval stone.
