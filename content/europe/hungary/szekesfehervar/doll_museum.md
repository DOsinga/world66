The Doll Museum is a small collection of dolls, dollhouses, and toy-room scenes from the 17th to 20th centuries. The displays fill several rooms and include a military-toy scene laid out around a castle.

It is not one of Székesfehérvár's major sights, but it is a pleasant extra stop in the old town, especially for families or anyone interested in historic toys and domestic miniatures.
