Nádasdladány is best known for Nádasdy Palace, a historic estate set in a landscaped park of about 24 hectares. The palace was rebuilt in the 1870s and gives visitors a look at Hungarian aristocratic country-house life outside Székesfehérvár.

The village also has a neo-Gothic Roman Catholic church from the 1880s, but the palace and park are the main reason to make the trip.
