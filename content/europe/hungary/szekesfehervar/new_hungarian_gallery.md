The New Hungarian Gallery presents Hungarian painting from the 19th century onward. The collection is arranged chronologically, which makes it an accessible way to follow the country's major artists and changing styles.

It is a compact art stop in the historic centre of Szekesfehervar, best combined with the nearby churches, palaces, and old-town streets.
