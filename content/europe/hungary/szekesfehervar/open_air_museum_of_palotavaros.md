The Open Air Museum of Palotavaros preserves a small group of thatched houses and a church connected with the Serbian community that once lived around Rac Street. Interiors show domestic life, trades, and local memories, making it a useful stop for understanding Székesfehérvár beyond its royal and baroque monuments.
