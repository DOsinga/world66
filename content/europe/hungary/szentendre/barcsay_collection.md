The Barcsay Collection focuses on Jenő Barcsay, a major 20th-century Hungarian painter, graphic artist, and teacher who spent much of his life in Szentendre. The museum is strongest for visitors interested in Hungarian modern art, especially Barcsay's structured figures, mosaics, and tapestries.
