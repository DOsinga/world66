The Czóbel Museum is dedicated to Béla Czóbel, one of Hungary's important modern painters and a longtime Szentendre resident. Its exhibitions trace his career from early Hungarian work through the stronger French influence that shaped his mature style.

The museum is on Church Hill, close to several of Szentendre's other small museums and churches. It is a focused stop, best for visitors already interested in the town's artists' colony and modern Hungarian painting.
