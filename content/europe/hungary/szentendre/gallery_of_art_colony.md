The Gallery of Art Colony points to the reason Szentendre became more than a pretty Danube-side town. Artists settled and worked here through the twentieth century, helping create the small galleries, studios, and museum culture that still define its centre.

The gallery is a focused stop rather than a blockbuster museum. It works best as part of a broader Szentendre art walk with the Ferenczy and Kovacs Margit collections, giving context to the town's reputation as Hungary's approachable art colony.
