Hubay House is a small museum devoted to vintage Christmas ornaments, with pieces dating from the 19th century through the mid-20th century.

It is a short, light stop in central Szentendre, useful for visitors who enjoy decorative arts, seasonal traditions, or the town's cluster of small specialty museums.
