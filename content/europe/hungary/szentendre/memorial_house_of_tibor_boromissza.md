The Memorial House of Tibor Boromissza preserves the Szentendre home of the painter, who lived here in the 1950s and was part of the town's art colony. It is a small, focused stop rather than a major museum, but it helps explain why Szentendre became such an artists' town.

Visit it as part of a slow walk through the old centre, especially if you are tracing the galleries and studios that give Szentendre its character.
