Szentendre's Music Box Museum is a small museum of mechanical music, set in a historic house close to the centre. The collection includes music boxes, phonographs, barrel organs, player instruments, and musical clocks from the last two centuries.

It is a niche stop, but Szentendre rewards this kind of browsing. Pair it with the town's better-known art museums and churches if you have more than a quick riverside stroll planned.
