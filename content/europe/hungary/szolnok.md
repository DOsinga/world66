Szolnok sits where the Zagyva meets the Tisza, out on the Great Hungarian Plain. It is a county seat and a railway town, but it has more character than that sounds: river walks, an old artists' colony, a former synagogue used as a gallery, and one of Hungary's best aviation museums.

The city does not have the polish of [Eger](/europe/hungary/eger) or the grandeur of [Budapest](/europe/hungary/budapest). Its appeal is flatter and more local. Walk the Tisza embankment, cross the [Tiszavirag Bridge](/europe/hungary/szolnok/tiszavirag_bridge), see the [Szolnok Art Colony](/europe/hungary/szolnok/szolnok_art_colony), then spend proper time at [RepTar Aviation Museum](/europe/hungary/szolnok/reptar_aviation_museum).

Szolnok earns a spare day if you are already crossing eastern Hungary or have an interest in aviation, Hungarian painting, or river towns of the plain. It is not a headline destination, but it is not just a service stop either.
