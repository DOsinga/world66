Szolnok's Reformed Church is a late-19th-century neo-Gothic church completed in 1894 and designed by Otto Sztehlo. Its pointed forms and slender proportions make it one of the more distinctive historic buildings near the city center.

The interior is simple compared with many Catholic churches, but the carved larch pulpit is worth noting if the church is open. Otherwise, it is a short architectural stop while walking through central Szolnok.
