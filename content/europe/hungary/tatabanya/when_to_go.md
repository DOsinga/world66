---
title: When to Go
type: section
---

Late spring and early autumn are the best seasons for Tatabánya, when the Gerecse hills are at their most walkable and the weather is reliably mild. May and June bring fresh green forests and wildflowers on the limestone slopes; September and October produce the deep reds and yellows that the beech and oak forests of central Hungary are known for.

Summers are warm but not extreme; the karst caves stay cool. Winters are cold and overcast, with occasional snow on the Turul and a sometimes bleak look to the industrial parts of town. The mining museum and county museum are good options for poor-weather days.
