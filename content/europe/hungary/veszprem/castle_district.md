The Vár — Castle District — is a single street running along a long ridge above the Séd valley, with palaces, churches, the bishop's residence, and the heroes' gate at the entrance. It is the heart of historic Veszprém and one of the most atmospheric urban ensembles in western Hungary.

Walk in past the Heroes' Gate, pass the County Hall, and follow the street to the cathedral and bishop's palace at the southern end. The viewpoint at the very end of the ridge — the Erzsébet Look-out — gives a sweeping view over the valley below and the Bakony Hills to the north. Allow at least two hours for a slow walk.
