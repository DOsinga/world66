The Church and School of the English Ladies is a neo-Gothic complex in Veszprém, built in the 19th century and also known as Regina Mundi, Queen of the World Church. Its facade includes statues of the Virgin Mary, St Stephen, and St Emeric.

The carved red stone around the entrance and the twin chapels of the Heart of Jesus and Our Lady of Lourdes make it a worthwhile architectural stop while walking the centre.
