---
title: "Getting There"
type: section
---

**By train.** Veszprém is on the rail line between Budapest and the western corner of Hungary at Szombathely. Direct trains from Budapest (Déli pályaudvar) take about two hours; from Győr a little less. The train station is about a kilometre east of the centre.

**By bus.** Buses connect Veszprém to most towns in western Hungary and run more frequently than the trains. The bus station is in the centre, an easy walk to the Castle District.

**By car.** The M7 motorway from Budapest comes within 15 km of Veszprém at the Balatonalmádi exit. About 100 km, an hour and a half. Parking in the lower town is straightforward and free for short stays; on the Castle Hill there is a small car park near the cathedral.
