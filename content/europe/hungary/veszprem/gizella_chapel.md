Gizella Chapel is one of the oldest medieval buildings in Veszprem's Castle District. The small 13th-century Gothic chapel is known for its early frescoes, carved keystones, and unusual dragon capitals.

It is a compact stop, but it fits naturally with St. Michael's Cathedral, the Bishop's Palace, and the religious art collections nearby.
