The Hungarian Construction Industry Museum's Brick Museum, also known as the Tegularium, is a small specialist collection of stamped Hungarian bricks. It is a niche stop, but it fits well into a walk through Veszprém's Castle District for visitors interested in building crafts, local industry, or architectural details.
