The Queen Gizella Museum occupies the baroque Tejfalussy House in Veszprém's Castle District. Its collection focuses on religious art and objects connected with the bishopric, including church treasures, bequests, and donations.

The museum is a natural add-on to St. Michael's Cathedral and the Bishop's Palace. The cellar lapidary is the most distinctive part of the visit for travellers interested in older stonework and church history.
