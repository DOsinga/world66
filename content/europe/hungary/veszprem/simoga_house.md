Simoga House is a late Baroque protected monument near Veszprém's castle district, now used as a House of Arts. The building and its courtyard sit close to the medieval city walls, adding another layer to the old-town walk.

It is a modest but worthwhile stop for visitors already exploring the castle district, especially when exhibitions or cultural programmes are running inside.
