St. Anna Chapel is one of Veszprem's oldest surviving churches in its original form. Built in 1724, the small Baroque chapel is a quiet stop close to the castle district and the old town's steep lanes.

It is worth a short look while walking between Veszprem's historic gates, viewpoints, and churches.
