---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

**May to early October** is the best time to visit. The summer here is warm but not as fierce as on the Hungarian plain — Veszprém sits at 270 metres above sea level — and the long evenings on Óváros tér are one of the city's small pleasures.

**The VeszprémFest** (mid-July) and the **Utcazene Fesztivál** (street music festival, late July) draw crowds and bring extra life to the centre. Hotel prices rise around these events.

**Winter** is cold and grey but the Castle Hill in snow is exceptionally photogenic. Most attractions stay open year-round.
