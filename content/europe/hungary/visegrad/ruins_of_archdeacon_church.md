The Ruins of Archdeacon Church mark one of Visegrad's early medieval religious sites. A parish church stood on the hillside near the old county-seat castle, and the archdeaconry church was built here after 1063, during the time of King Solomon.

The remains are modest, but they add useful context to Visegrad's castle, tower, and royal palace sites.
