The ruins of Pone Navata are the remains of a late Roman military camp on a hill above the Danube. The visible walls outline a five-sided castrum, with later remains from a 10th- or 11th-century governor's castle layered into the site.

It is a modest ruin, but the setting makes it worthwhile if you are already exploring Visegrad's castles and Danube viewpoints.
