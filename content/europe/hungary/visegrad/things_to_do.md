---
title: Things to Do
type: section
---

Visegrad's sights are concentrated and easy to cover: the [Visegrad Citadel](/europe/hungary/visegrad/visegrad_citadel) on the hill, the [Royal Palace](/europe/hungary/visegrad/royal_palace) in the lower town, and the [Salamon Tower](/europe/hungary/visegrad/salamon_tower) by the river take the better part of a day. The setting on the [Danube Bend](/europe/hungary/visegrad/danube_bend) is exceptional — most visitors come as much for the scenery as the medieval monuments. The town is easily combined with [Esztergom](/europe/hungary/esztergom) and [Szentendre](/europe/hungary/szentendre) on a Danube Bend circuit from Budapest.
