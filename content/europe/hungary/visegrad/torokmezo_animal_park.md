Törökmező Animal Park is a small family attraction in the hills above the Danube Bend, with a petting zoo, colorful birds, and animals such as kangaroos, emus, and llamas. It is most useful as an easy add-on to a Visegrád or Danube Bend day out with children.

Do not expect a major zoo; the appeal is a simple animal stop in a scenic area.
