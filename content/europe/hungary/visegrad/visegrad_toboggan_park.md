Visegrad Toboggan Park has two parallel downhill runs near the town's main hilltop attractions. One run operates year-round and the other is seasonal, making it an easy family add-on when visiting the citadel, Salamon Tower, or the Danube Bend viewpoints.

It is a light attraction rather than a cultural sight, but it is well placed for families spending a day around Visegrad.
