---
title: When to Go
type: section
---

Late spring and early autumn are the best times to visit Visegrad. May, June, and September offer warm days without summer's peak crowds, and the wooded hills around the Danube Bend are at their most lush. The view from the Citadel is clear on most days. The Mahart river boats run from spring through autumn and these are among the more pleasant ways to arrive.

July and August bring the largest crowds and the coach parties, particularly on weekends and Hungarian public holidays. The royal palace and citadel are popular school-trip destinations throughout the school year. If you visit in summer, arrive early in the morning before the coaches.

Winter is quiet to the point of sleepiness. Several of the smaller restaurants close for the season, and the Mahart river boats stop running. The Citadel and Royal Palace remain open, however, and the town has a peaceful character in the cold months that its summer self entirely lacks. Snow on the wooded hills above the Danube makes the view from the battlements distinctly atmospheric.
