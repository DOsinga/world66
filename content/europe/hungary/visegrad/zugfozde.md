Zugfozde is a small museum about the period when private palinka distillation was outlawed in Hungary. Short films explain how families protected their stills, hid their equipment, and kept the practice alive despite excise enforcement.

The building itself was once a grocer's store, with an old ice house below. It is a niche stop, but a good one if you are curious about Hungarian food culture beyond castles and river views.
