Bazita Peak TV Tower is the town's easiest panorama. The tower stands southwest of the centre and lifts you over the low hills, fields, and wooded folds that make Zala feel softer than much of western Hungary.

Go in clear weather and treat it as a closing stop rather than the main event. The cafe at the top makes the view linger longer than a quick photo stop.
