Petting Zoo Fenyvespuszta is a small family attraction near Zalakaros, with animals, pony rides, carriage rides, and simple outdoor activities. It works best for families looking for an easy half-day break from the thermal baths.

The zoo is close to the Kis-Balaton nature area, so it can be paired with Kanyavari Island or the buffalo reserve if you have a car.
