Iðnaðarsafnið is Akureyri's industry museum, focused on the workshops, tools, machines, and small-scale production that supported life in northern Iceland. It adds useful local texture beside the town's better-known church, art museum, and harbour views.

The museum is best for travellers who enjoy material history rather than polished national narratives. Its strength is in showing how work, repair, and manufacturing developed in a remote regional centre.
