---
title: Day Trips
type: section
---

Borgarnes is a natural base for the inland sights of Borgarfjörður and the start of the Snæfellsnes peninsula. The classic day from here loops east along Route 50 to **Deildartunguhver**, Europe's highest-flow hot spring, then to **Reykholt** (home of the medieval historian Snorri Sturluson), and on to the **Hraunfossar** and **Barnafoss** waterfalls, where countless small streams flow out of a lava field.

The Langjökull glacier and the ice tunnel at Húsafell are an hour further inland. To the west, the start of the Snæfellsnes peninsula — Eldborg crater, the Gerðuberg basalt columns, and the church at Búðir — is within an easy half-day.
