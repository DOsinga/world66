---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

Summer (June through August) is the prime season: the Great Fish Day festival in August, continuous daylight, and the best conditions for whale watching and island ferry crossings. The Tröllaskagi mountain roads are only fully accessible in summer; the old mountain passes close in winter.

Winter offers a chance to see the northern lights over the fjord, and the area is quieter and more authentically Icelandic in feel. The Grímsey ferry runs year-round, weather permitting.
