Hverfjall is a nearly symmetrical tephra crater on the eastern side of Lake Myvatn, rising starkly above the lava fields near Dimmuborgir. A marked trail climbs to the rim for broad views over the lake country; stay on the path, as access to the crater floor is restricted to limit erosion.
