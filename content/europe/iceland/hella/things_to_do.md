---
title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Hella is not a blockbuster town, but it has enough of its own texture to reward a stop. The sandstone caves at Ægissíða are the standout, Ægissíðufoss gives the village a real riverside walk, and the Rangá valley is good country for horse riding, fishing, and big-sky views toward Hekla.
