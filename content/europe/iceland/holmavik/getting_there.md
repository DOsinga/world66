---
title: "Getting There"
type: section
---

Holmavik is 274 kilometres from Reykjavík by car, most of it via the Hvalfjörður tunnel and then north through the Westfjords. The drive takes roughly four hours in good conditions. There is no direct bus service from Reykjavík; reaching Holmavik without a car requires a combination of services and advance planning.

Hólmavík Airport (IATA: HVK) receives small aircraft from Reykjavík's domestic airport, Reykjavík City Airport. Flights are operated by small carriers serving the Westfjords, but schedules are limited and subject to weather cancellations. Flying is the fastest option; driving gives you access to the Strandir coast road beyond the village.
