---
title: Eating Out
type: section
---

Hveragerði's food scene is small but has a genuine local character rooted in what grows and is produced nearby. Geothermally grown vegetables from the town's greenhouses appear on menus, and the tradition of hot-spring bread — rúgbrauð baked underground in geothermal pots — is taken seriously here. Icelandic lamb from the surrounding uplands is a fixture.
