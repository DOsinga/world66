Exhibition The Quake 2008 is a compact geology stop in Hveragerdi, a town that lives with hot springs, faults, and restless ground. The exhibition focuses on the 2008 South Iceland earthquake and uses the local event to explain what seismic activity means here.

It is not a major museum, but it is useful context before or after walking among the town's geothermal features. In a country where geology often looks spectacular from a distance, this brings it down to floors, walls, instruments, and lived experience.
