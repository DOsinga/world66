Boat tours on the lagoon depart from the northern shore from May to October, operating in two formats. The amphibious duck boats drive down the beach and into the water, giving a gentler experience better suited to families and people without waterproof layers. The Zodiac inflatable boat tours are faster and more adventurous, getting closer to larger icebergs and into the narrower channels. Both tours last about 45 minutes and are led by guides who explain the glacier's formation, the rate of retreat, and the seal and birdlife.

From the boats you see the icebergs at water level — their size is more apparent when you're sitting at the same height. The blue cores of old, densely compressed ice are most visible close up. The seals, which can look small from the road, are actually quite large when a Zodiac pulls alongside their floe. Booking ahead in summer is recommended; tours run frequently but fill fast at peak times.
