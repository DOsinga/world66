---
title: Things to Do
type: section
---

The [lagoon boat tours](/europe/iceland/jokulsarlon/boat_tours) are the main active experience — both Zodiac and duck boat options get you onto the water among the icebergs. [Diamond Beach](/europe/iceland/jokulsarlon/diamond_beach) across the Ring Road bridge gives the famous black sand and ice crystal views. The [Breiðamerkurjökull glacier](/europe/iceland/jokulsarlon/breidamerkurjokull) can be walked on with a guide from the nearby access point. [Breiðárlón](/europe/iceland/jokulsarlon/breidarlón), 10 kilometres west, offers the same glacial lagoon scenery without the crowds. [Harbour seals](/europe/iceland/jokulsarlon/seals) are resident year-round on the ice floes. The wider [Vatnajökull National Park](/europe/iceland/jokulsarlon/vatnajokull_national_park) context is worth understanding for the scale of the landscape. From September to March, [northern lights](/europe/iceland/jokulsarlon/northern_lights) reflect in the lagoon on clear nights.
