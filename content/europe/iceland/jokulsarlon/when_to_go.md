---
title: When to Go
type: section
---

Jökulsárlón is accessible year-round — the Ring Road stays open in winter except during blizzards — and offers different experiences by season. Summer (June to August) brings long daylight hours, accessible glacier hikes, boat tours, Arctic tern nesting, and the highest iceberg activity as warmer temperatures accelerate calving. This is also the busiest period; the car park fills by mid-morning in July.

Spring (April to May) and autumn (September to October) offer the best balance: tours running, fewer visitors, and the possibility of northern lights in the evening hours as darkness returns. October is particularly recommended for photographers: frequent clear nights for northern lights, golden evening light, and the lagoon surface often perfectly still.

Winter visits (November to March) require proper cold-weather gear and are best done in clear weather windows. The lagoon doesn't freeze — it's too salty and the inflow of icebergs keeps the water moving — and in clear winter conditions the combination of ice, black sand, and low sun angles produces extraordinary light. Boat tours are suspended in winter; the lake shore and Diamond Beach are fully accessible on foot.
