Dettifoss is the thunderous heavyweight of north Iceland's waterfalls, a broad wall of grey glacial water dropping into the Jokulsargljufur canyon. The fall is not graceful in the usual postcard sense; it is raw, loud, and best appreciated from the spray-wet viewpoints on either side of the river.

It sits well beyond Lake Myvatn itself, but it is one of the essential trips in the wider Myvatn and Jokulsargljufur area. Bring waterproof layers, watch the wind direction, and give yourself time to see the canyon rather than treating the waterfall as a quick roadside stop.
