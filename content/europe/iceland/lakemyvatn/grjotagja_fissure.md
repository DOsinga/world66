Grjótagjá is a lava fissure near Lake Mývatn with hot geothermal water inside. It used to be known as a bathing cave, but the water can be dangerously hot and the roof has become unstable, so treat it as a quick geological stop rather than a place to swim.

The appeal is the setting: cracked lava, steam, and bright water hidden in the rock. It fits easily into a circuit with Dimmuborgir, Hverfjall, and the other volcanic sights around Mývatn.
