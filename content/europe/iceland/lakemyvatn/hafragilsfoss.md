Hafragilsfoss is a powerful waterfall on the Jokulsa a Fjollum, upstream from Dettifoss and set in a stark canyon landscape. The usual stop is the lookout above the falls, but trails from the Dettifoss area give a closer, rougher approach when conditions are good.
