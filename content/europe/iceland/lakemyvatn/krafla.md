Krafla is one of the strongest volcanic landscapes around Lake Mývatn, shaped by the Krafla Fires eruptions of 1975-1984. Lava fields, steaming ground, solfataras, and dark ridges give the area a raw, newly made look.

The main stop is Víti, a broad explosion crater with a lake inside it, but the wider area rewards a longer walk if the weather is clear. Allow a few hours rather than treating it as a roadside photo stop.
