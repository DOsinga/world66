Námaskarð is the geothermal pass east of Lake Mývatn, known for steaming fumaroles, solfataras and hot mud pools below the low ridge. The landscape is raw, colourful and sulphurous, and it is one of the easiest geothermal stops to add to a circuit of Mývatn and Dimmuborgir.
