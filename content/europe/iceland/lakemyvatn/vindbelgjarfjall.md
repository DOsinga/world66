Vindbelgjarfjall is a 529-metre peak on the western side of Lake Myvatn. The walk starts gently from the road, then climbs steeply over loose rock near the summit, so it feels tougher than the height suggests.

The reward is a wide view over the lake, its lava fields, pseudocraters, and low volcanic hills. Allow roughly 45 minutes to an hour for the climb, and wear shoes with decent grip.
