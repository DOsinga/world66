The Blue Lagoon is Reykjanes' famous stop: a warm, silica-rich bathing complex set in black lava near Svartsengi. It is expensive, heavily managed, and often crowded, but the visual punch is real. Few places announce "Iceland" to first-time visitors quite so efficiently.

Book ahead and check current operating updates, especially during volcanic unrest around Svartsengi and Grindavík. The lagoon may close temporarily when eruptions or gas risk affect the area. If you go, allow time for the full bathing ritual and do not schedule it too tightly before a flight.
