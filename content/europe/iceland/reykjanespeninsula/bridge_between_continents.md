The Bridge Between Continents is a short footbridge over a sandy fissure near Sandvík, presented as a crossing between the Eurasian and North American plates. Geologically, the plate boundary is a broad zone rather than one clean crack, but the stop makes the idea wonderfully tangible.

It is quick, free, and exposed to the wind. Walk down into the fissure after crossing the bridge; the black lava walls and pale sand are more interesting from below than from the car park.
