Brimketill is a small natural rock pool carved into the lava shore west of Grindavík. It is not for swimming; the Atlantic hits this coast hard, and waves can sweep the rocks without much warning.

Visit for the shape of the lava coast and the force of the sea rather than for a long hike. The viewing platform gives the safest angle. On stormy days, stay well back and let the waves do the performing.
