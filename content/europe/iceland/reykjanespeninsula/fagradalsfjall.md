Fagradalsfjall is the volcanic area that brought Reykjanes back into world headlines in 2021, when lava began erupting in the Geldingadalir valley after weeks of earthquakes. The fresh lava fields are stark and fascinating, but access depends on current conditions.

Do not rely on old hiking reports. Trails, parking, gas risk, and closures change with new activity across the peninsula. Check Safetravel and local notices before setting out, and treat any fresh lava as unstable even when it looks solid.
