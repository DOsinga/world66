---
title: Getting Around
type: section
---

A car is the practical way around Reykjanes. Distances are short, roads are generally good, and many sights sit directly beside marked parking areas. Weather is the main complication: wind, winter ice, and fast-moving fog can turn an easy loop into slow driving.

Do not walk onto fresh lava fields, ignore closures, or treat steaming ground as harmless. Before hiking near Fagradalsfjall, Svartsengi, or Grindavík, check Safetravel and the Icelandic Meteorological Office for current access, gas, and eruption warnings.
