---
title: Getting There
type: section
---

Keflavík International Airport sits on the Reykjanes Peninsula, making the region unusually easy to fit into an Iceland trip. Rental cars are available at the airport, and the main road to Reykjavik crosses the peninsula in about 45 minutes.

Public buses connect Keflavík, the airport, and Reykjavik, but they are not useful for most natural sights. To see the peninsula properly, rent a car or book a small-group tour timed around your flight.
