Gunnuhver is a roaring geothermal area near the western tip of Reykjanes, with mud pools, steam vents, and a strong sulphur smell. It feels rawer than many Icelandic geothermal stops because the sea, lava fields, and lighthouse landscape are all close by.

Stay on the boardwalks and watch the wind direction before stepping into steam. The ground here changes, and the warning signs are not decorative. Combined with Reykjanesviti and the nearby sea cliffs, Gunnuhver is one of the peninsula's best short stops.
