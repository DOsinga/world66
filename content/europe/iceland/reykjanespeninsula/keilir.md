Keilir is the neat cone-shaped mountain that rises from the lava fields north of the main Reykjanes road. Its profile is so clean that it works like a compass point for much of the peninsula, visible from surprisingly far away.

The hike is moderate rather than technical, but the approach crosses rough lava and exposed ground. Go only in decent weather and bring proper footwear. The summit view helps make sense of Reykjanes as a broad volcanic plate boundary rather than a string of isolated sights.
