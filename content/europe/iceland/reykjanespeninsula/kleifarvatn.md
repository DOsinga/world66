Kleifarvatn is the large, dark lake east of Seltún, held between bare volcanic slopes. It has a lonely quality that feels very different from the busier airport side of Reykjanes, especially on grey days when the water and hills seem to merge.

The lake is best appreciated as part of the Krýsuvík drive rather than as a destination by itself. Stop at a viewpoint, take in the silence, then continue to the geothermal fields or back toward the coast.
