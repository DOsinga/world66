---
title: When to Go
type: section
---

Reykjanes is visitable year-round, but the experience changes sharply with weather and daylight. Summer brings long light, easier driving, and more tour traffic. Winter is moodier and quieter, with low sun over black lava, but wind and ice can make short walks unpleasant.

Volcanic activity is the variable that matters most. Recent eruption areas may be closed, partly open, or unsafe because of gas and unstable lava. Check official updates close to the day you travel, not weeks in advance.
