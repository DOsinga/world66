Reykjanesviti is the lighthouse near the far western end of the peninsula, standing above lava, geothermal ground, and Atlantic surf. The setting is stronger than the building itself: low cliffs, sea stacks, steam from Gunnuhver, and a sense of land running out.

It is an easy add-on to Gunnuhver and the Bridge Between Continents. In rough weather, the wind can be fierce enough to make even a short walk feel dramatic, so keep a hand on car doors and do not wander close to cliff edges.
