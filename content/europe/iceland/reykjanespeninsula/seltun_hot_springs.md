Seltún is the colourful geothermal field at Krýsuvík, where boardwalks pass steaming vents, yellow clay, red earth, and bubbling mud. It is one of the easiest places on Reykjanes to see active geothermal ground without hiking far.

The area is fragile and hot, so stay on marked paths. Seltún combines well with Kleifarvatn and the eastern Reykjanes roads, especially if you are driving between Reykjavik and the airport with time to spare.
