Sundhnúksgígar is the crater row north of Grindavík associated with the recent eruption episodes that have disrupted the western Reykjanes Peninsula. It is an important place for understanding current Icelandic geology, but it is not a casual sightseeing stop.

Access can be restricted, dangerous, or simply unwise depending on the latest activity. If authorities close roads or lava fields, respect it. The peninsula has plenty of safe geothermal and coastal sights without walking into an active hazard zone.
