title: Things to Do
type: section
---

Reykjanes is built for a self-drive loop. The classic western circuit combines the [Blue Lagoon](/europe/iceland/reykjanespeninsula/blue_lagoon), [Bridge Between Continents](/europe/iceland/reykjanespeninsula/bridge_between_continents), [Gunnuhver](/europe/iceland/reykjanespeninsula/gunnuhver), [Reykjanesviti](/europe/iceland/reykjanespeninsula/reykjanesviti), and the lava shore at [Brimketill](/europe/iceland/reykjanespeninsula/brimketill).

Further east, [Seltún](/europe/iceland/reykjanespeninsula/seltun_hot_springs), [Kleifarvatn](/europe/iceland/reykjanespeninsula/kleifarvatn), and [Keilir](/europe/iceland/reykjanespeninsula/keilir) show a quieter side of the peninsula. Recent eruption areas around [Fagradalsfjall](/europe/iceland/reykjanespeninsula/fagradalsfjall) and Sundhnúksgígar are compelling but require current safety checks before you go.
