Arbaejarlaug is a family-friendly public pool complex on the eastern side of Reykjavik, with indoor and outdoor pools, hot pots, a waterslide, and a steam bath. It is a practical local-style soak after a day in town, and the open views back toward the centre are especially good around sunset.
