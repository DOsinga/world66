---
title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Seyðisfjörður rewards slow exploration on foot. Walk the rainbow street to the [Blue Church](/europe/iceland/seydisfjordur/blue_church), wander along the harbour watching the [Smyril Line ferry](/europe/iceland/seydisfjordur/smyril_line_ferry) manoeuvre through the fjord, and follow the river upstream past the old industrial buildings. The [Technical Museum of East Iceland](/europe/iceland/seydisfjordur/technical_museum_f) is the main indoor attraction, covering the modernisation of the region from the 1880s onwards with hands-on exhibits and a strong sense of the local story. Above the town, the sound sculpture [Tvísöngur](/europe/iceland/seydisfjordur/tvisongur) rewards the fifteen-minute climb with resonating concrete domes and views over the fjord.
