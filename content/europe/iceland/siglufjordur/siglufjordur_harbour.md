Siglufjörður's harbour is still the town's centre of gravity. Fishing boats, old industrial buildings, mountain walls, and the museum buildings sit close together, so even a short walk gives you the shape of the place.

Come before or after the Herring Era Museum. The museum explains the harbour; the harbour proves that the story was not just nostalgia.
