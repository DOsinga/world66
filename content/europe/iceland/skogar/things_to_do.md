title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Skógar has a small number of sites but each one is genuinely extraordinary. The main waterfall, the folk museum, a hidden second waterfall ten minutes' walk away, and access to one of Iceland's great long-distance hiking trails give the settlement a draw well out of proportion to its size — a population of around 25 people and a tourist infrastructure that handles tens of thousands of visitors a year.
