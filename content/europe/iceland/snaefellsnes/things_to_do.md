title: Things to Do
type: section
---

Snæfellsnes rewards a slow circuit of the peninsula over two days. The centrepiece is [Snæfellsjökull](/europe/iceland/snaefellsnes/snaefellsjokull) — the glacier volcano at the western tip — with glacier tours and hiking routes on its slopes. The south coast cliff walk between [Arnarstapi](/europe/iceland/snaefellsnes/arnarstapi) and [Hellnar](/europe/iceland/snaefellsnes/hellnar) is one of Iceland's finest short coastal walks.

The north coast is dominated by [Kirkjufell](/europe/iceland/snaefellsnes/kirkjufell) — the symmetrical mountain that became one of Iceland's most photographed sights. [Búðir](/europe/iceland/snaefellsnes/budir) on the south coast has the famous black church and lava field walking. The [Rauðfeldsgjá ravine](/europe/iceland/snaefellsnes/raudfeldsgja) is a narrow canyon worth exploring near the glacier base. [Lóndrangar](/europe/iceland/snaefellsnes/londrangar) sea stacks and the Vatnshellir lava tube provide more geology. At [Ytri-Tunga beach](/europe/iceland/snaefellsnes/ytri_tunga), grey seals haul out year-round within metres of visitors.

[Stykkishólmur](/europe/iceland/stykkisholmur) at the eastern end is the main town and ferry departure point for the Westfjords.
