Stykkishólmur is a small fishing town on the northern shore of the Snæfellsnes peninsula, with brightly painted houses clustered around a natural harbour. It is the main settlement on the peninsula and the departure point for the [Baldur ferry](/europe/iceland/stykkisholmur/breidafjordur_ferry) across Breiðafjörður to the Westfjords.

The town has a pleasantly end-of-the-road feel. The harbour area is the heart of it — fresh seafood, a handful of cafes, and views across the island-dotted bay. The [Library of Water](/europe/iceland/stykkisholmur/library_of_water), an art installation by Roni Horn in the old library building on the hill, is worth the short walk up. The [Norwegian House](/europe/iceland/stykkisholmur/norwegian_house), Iceland's oldest timber-framed building, sits at the waterfront and houses a local history museum. Just south of town, [Helgafell](/europe/iceland/stykkisholmur/helgafell) — "holy mountain" — is a 73-metre hill that appears in Eyrbyggja saga and offers views across the bay from a short climb. Stykkishólmur makes a good base for exploring Snæfellsnes, with the glacier, lava fields, and dramatic coastline all within an hour's drive.
