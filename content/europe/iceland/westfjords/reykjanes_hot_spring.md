The Reykjanes hot spring in the western Westfjords is fed by geothermal water that flows into a long outdoor pool on a slope above the Ísafjarðardjúp fjord. The pool has been used for bathing since at least the 18th century. A small guesthouse operates here in summer, making Reykjanes a viable overnight stop for those driving the western fjord circuit.

The position is excellent. The pool looks directly out across the fjord, which is one of Iceland's largest. In good weather the views are long. In poor weather — which is common — steam rising from the hot water and low cloud on the mountains creates its own kind of atmosphere. The spring is accessible year-round, though the approach road can be difficult in winter without a suitable vehicle.
