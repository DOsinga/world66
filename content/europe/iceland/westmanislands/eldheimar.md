Eldheimar tells the story of the 1973 eruption on Heimaey, when lava and ash buried parts of the town and forced the island's evacuation. The museum is built around the remains of a house excavated from the ash, making the disaster feel immediate rather than abstract.

It is one of the clearest ways to understand the Westman Islands: a place where people live closely with the volcanic forces that made the islands in the first place.
