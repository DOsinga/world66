The Great Western Greenway is a 42-kilometre off-road walking and cycling path from Westport to Achill Sound, passing through Newport and Mulranny along the shore of Clew Bay. It follows the line of the old railway extension to Achill, which opened in the 1890s and closed in 1937. The full route opened in 2011, and it has been a big success, bringing a steady stream of cyclists to this corner of Mayo.

Because it follows a railway line, the gradients are gentle and the route is easy to follow. Bikes can be hired at Achill Sound and in Westport, and bus 450 runs along the same coast if you only want to ride one way.
