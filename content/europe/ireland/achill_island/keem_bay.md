Keem Bay is a sheltered sandy cove at the very end of the R319, past Dooagh near the western tip of Achill. The road drops down a steep hillside to reach it, with Croaghaun rising behind and Moytoge Head falling sharply into the sea on the south side. The beach holds a Blue Flag, and on a still day the water is a startling turquoise.

From the beach a trail heads up the valley and west for about 1.5 kilometres to Achill Head, the tip of the island, with spectacular sea cliffs on the north side. On Moytoge Head stands an old lookout post built by the British in the First World War and reused by the Irish coast watching service during the Second.
