Kildavnet Castle, also called Carrickkildavnet or Grace O'Malley's Castle, is a small 15th-century tower house on the shore at Cloughmore in the south of Achill. It belonged to the O'Malleys, who ruled these waters, and is associated with their most famous member, Grace O'Malley (Granuaile), the sea captain and pirate who reputedly met Elizabeth I in 1593. You can't go inside, but it's a fine sight on the shore.

A short walk north is Kildavnet's old church and graveyard, where many victims of the Famine are buried, along with a memorial to the ten young islanders who died in the Kirkintilloch fire of 1937.
