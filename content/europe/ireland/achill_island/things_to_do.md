Achill is for being outdoors: walk up to the [Deserted Village](/europe/ireland/achill_island/deserted_village) and on to Slievemore, swim or surf at [Keem Bay](/europe/ireland/achill_island/keem_bay) and Keel, climb [Croaghaun](/europe/ireland/achill_island/croaghaun) for its sea cliffs, and arrive by bike along the [Great Western Greenway](/europe/ireland/achill_island/great_western_greenway).
