title: Things to Do
type: section
---

Adare is a village to walk. Start at the Heritage Centre on Main Street, look into the [Trinitarian Abbey](/europe/ireland/adare/trinitarian_abbey) next door, stroll past the thatched cottages to the river and the [Augustinian Friary](/europe/ireland/adare/augustinian_friary), and book the shuttle to [Desmond Castle](/europe/ireland/adare/desmond_castle) if you're there in summer.
