---
title: When to Go
type: section
---

Inis Meáin is a year-round island for the people who live there, but for visitors the practical window is April through October. Ferries and the island airline operate on reduced schedules in winter and can be cancelled for days at a time when Atlantic weather makes the crossing unsafe. In summer, the days are very long — light until 10pm or later — and the wildflowers on the limestone pavement are extraordinary, particularly in May and June.

The Synge Summer School in late August brings writers and musicians to the island for a weekend of readings and sessions, which adds life without overwhelming the place. The currach racing weekend in late July, when the traditional canvas-and-tar boats race in Galway Bay, is the biggest community event of the year and one of the more genuine spectacles on the Irish west coast calendar.

Be aware that at any time of year, Atlantic weather can close in suddenly. Bring layers regardless of the forecast.
