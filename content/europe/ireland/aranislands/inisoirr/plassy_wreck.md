The *Plassy* is a 1940-built steam trawler, later converted into a cargo ship, that ran aground on Finnis Reef in the great storm of March 1960. The crew were saved; the ship was eventually lifted by a second storm and deposited on the rocks of the eastern shore, where it has been slowly rusting into the landscape for more than sixty years.

The hull is still mostly intact and you can walk right up to it — the upper plates are perforated with rust holes that let the sea wind whistle through, the boilers are still recognisable inside, and the whole thing leans at an improbable angle on a flat shelf of limestone. It has become the unofficial symbol of Inis Oirr, helped enormously by appearing in the opening credits of the Channel 4 sitcom *Father Ted*. Best at low tide, when you can walk all the way around it.
