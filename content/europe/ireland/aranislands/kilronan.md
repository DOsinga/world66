Kilronan (Cill Rónáin) is the main village on Inis Mór, the largest of the [Aran Islands](/europe/ireland/aranislands). The ferry from Rossaveal or Doolin drops you at the pier here, and this is where you will find the island's few pubs, shops, and B&Bs.

The village itself is small — a handful of buildings around the harbour — but it is the starting point for exploring the island. [Dún Aonghasa](/europe/ireland/aranislands/kilronan/dun_aonghasa), the spectacular Iron Age cliff fort on the western edge of Inis Mór, is about 8 km away by road. Most visitors rent a bike in Kilronan and ride out along the stone-walled lanes, stopping at [Dún Dúchathair](/europe/ireland/aranislands/kilronan/dun_duchathair) and the tiny oratory of [Teampall Bheanaín](/europe/ireland/aranislands/kilronan/teampall_bheanaín), before finishing at [Joe Watty's Bar](/europe/ireland/aranislands/kilronan/joe_wattys_bar) for a pint. The [Aran Heritage Centre](/europe/ireland/aranislands/kilronan/aran_heritage_centre) in the village shows Robert Flaherty's classic 1934 film Man of Aran — good context before you set out.
