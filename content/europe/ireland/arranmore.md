Arranmore is a Donegal island close enough for an easy ferry crossing, but far enough offshore to feel like a proper Atlantic escape. It is a Gaeltacht community, with Irish still part of daily life, and the mood is more working island than polished resort.

The island's west and north sides are the reason to come: cliffs, sea stacks, birdlife, and the lighthouse road looking out toward the open ocean. The marked [Slí Árainn Mhóir](/europe/ireland/arranmore/sli_arainn_mhor) loop gives the best shape to a visit, while boat trips and diving make use of the clear, rough-edged coast.

Arranmore works as a long day from Burtonport, but it is better if you stay overnight and let the ferry schedule stop running the day. Bring waterproofs, cash, and a willingness to slow down.
