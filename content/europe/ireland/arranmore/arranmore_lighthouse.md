Arranmore Lighthouse stands at the island's north-west point, where the land breaks toward the Atlantic. The lighthouse is automatic now, but the road out still has the proper edge-of-the-map feeling that makes Donegal's islands worth the effort.

The walk or cycle out is as important as the building itself. Look for seabirds, the old coastguard remains, and the Second World War EIRE marker nearby.
