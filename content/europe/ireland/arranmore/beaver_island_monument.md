The Beaver Island Monument is a modest but telling stop on the road toward the lighthouse. It recalls the Donegal emigrants, many from Arranmore and nearby islands, who settled Beaver Island in Lake Michigan in the 19th century.

It will not detain you long, but it gives Arranmore's isolation a wider story: this small Atlantic island has family lines stretching across another cold, windy island an ocean away.
