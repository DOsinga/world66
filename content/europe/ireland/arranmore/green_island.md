Green Island sits off Arranmore's south-west tip, ringed by cliffs and used by seabirds. You do not land there, but boat trips and sea safaris give a good look at the island, the surrounding stacks, and the rougher side of the Arranmore coast.

It is a weather-dependent outing. In calm conditions it adds a strong wildlife angle to a visit that might otherwise stay on the roads and tracks.
