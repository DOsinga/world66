Slí Árainn Mhóir is the marked walking loop around Arranmore, starting near the ferry side of the island and reaching the wilder west coast. It is the best way to see the island properly: lanes, rough tracks, lake edges, cliffs, and sudden views back through the Rosses islands.

Allow several hours and take the weather seriously. Distances are modest, but wind and rain can make the island feel much larger than it looks on the map.
