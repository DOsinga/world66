The Left Bank Bistro on Fry Place is consistently the best-regarded restaurant in Athlone, with a menu that pivots Irish produce through Mediterranean technique. The fish is particularly good — the kitchen's relationship with local suppliers shows in both freshness and seasonality. The room is warm without being precious, prices are honest for the quality, and booking is advisable at weekends. The lunch menu offers much of the same quality at reduced prices.
