The Barrow Navigation brings the Grand Canal system into Athy before continuing south through Carlow toward the tidal Barrow. For visitors, its towpath is the easiest way to find the town's calmer side.

Walk a short stretch from the bridge, or use Athy as part of a longer cycling or boating route through the Barrow valley. The waterway matters because it turns the town from a road stop into a river stop.
