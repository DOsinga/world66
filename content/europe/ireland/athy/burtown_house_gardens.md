Burtown House is a Georgian villa set in twelve acres of gardens, parkland, and woodland outside Athy. The gardens are the main reason to come: borders, meadowy corners, mature trees, and quiet paths make it a gentle half-day stop if you are exploring south Kildare by car.

House visits are usually by arrangement, so do not treat it like a walk-up museum. Pair it with Athy or the nearby Moone High Cross rather than making a long detour just for the house.
