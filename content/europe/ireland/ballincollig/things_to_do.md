Ballincollig's main attraction is the Royal Gunpowder Mills regional park, which offers one of the most unusual industrial heritage walks in Ireland — a two-kilometre trail along the Lee through the ruins of what was once one of the largest gunpowder factories in the British Isles. The rest of the town is a modern suburb of Cork, but the park and the medieval castle to the south give it real historical depth.
