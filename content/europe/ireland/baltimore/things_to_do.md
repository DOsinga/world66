---
title: Things to Do
type: section
---

In Baltimore you go out on the water: take the ferry to [Sherkin](/europe/ireland/baltimore/sherkin_island) or [Cape Clear](/europe/ireland/baltimore/cape_clear_island), or join a dive, angling or whale-watching trip. On land, climb [Dún na Séad](/europe/ireland/baltimore/dun_na_sead_castle) for the harbour view, walk to the [Beacon](/europe/ireland/baltimore/baltimore_beacon), and visit [Lough Hyne](/europe/ireland/baltimore/lough_hyne).
