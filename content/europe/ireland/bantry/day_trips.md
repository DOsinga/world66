---
title: Day Trips
type: section
---

Bantry is the natural base for exploring the south-west corner of [Ireland](/europe/ireland). The two peninsulas immediately west — Beara and Sheep's Head — are the obvious choices, but [Glengarriff](/europe/ireland/glengarriff) and the gardens of Garinish Island are an easy half-day trip up the bay, and the Mizen Head to the south is reachable as a long loop. None of these are stop-and-go destinations; you want a full day, decent weather, and a tank of petrol.
