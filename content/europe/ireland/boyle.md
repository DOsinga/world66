*Europe > Ireland > Boyle*

Boyle is a small Roscommon town that earns its stop with a good mix of stone, lake, and old landed power. It sits just west of Lough Key, close enough to Sligo and Carrick-on-Shannon to work as an easy detour, but with more substance than a plain road break.

The town's strongest sight is [Boyle Abbey](/europe/ireland/boyle/boyle_abbey), a ruined Cistercian monastery with enough surviving church fabric to give the place weight. A short walk away, [King House](/europe/ireland/boyle/king_house) tells a different story: Georgian landlords, military use, restoration, and local collections inside one of Connacht's more imposing town houses.

Boyle also has the advantage of [Lough Key Forest Park](/europe/ireland/boyle/lough_key_forest_park), just outside town. That makes it more than a heritage stop. You can spend the morning among abbey arches and Georgian rooms, then walk or cycle through lakeside woods in the afternoon.
