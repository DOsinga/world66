Bundoran's main beach runs for about a kilometre in the town's central bay, sheltered enough for swimming and gentle enough for beginners to learn surfing. The sand is pale and the water cold but clean — the Blue Flag designation reflects consistently good water quality. In summer the beach fills with families from Donegal, Leitrim, and Northern Ireland; outside July and August it is mostly the surfers' domain.

Several surf schools operate directly from the beach, offering lessons for all levels. The break here is forgiving compared to The Peak, making it the right place to start if you haven't surfed before. Donegal Adventure Centre is the best-known school, with qualified instruction and equipment hire. On days when the Atlantic is flat and the light falls right, the view from the beach toward the Sligo mountains on the southern horizon is one of the finest on the Irish coast.
