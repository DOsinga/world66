The Roguey Walk is Bundoran's best coastal path — a 2.5-kilometre loop along the cliff edge southwest of the town centre, passing sea arches, blow-holes, and sculpted sandstone formations. The views across Donegal Bay to the Sligo mountains are excellent, and the path is well-maintained and accessible for most walkers.

The blow-holes known as the Fairy Bridges are the walk's signature feature — natural rock arches and perforations in the headland where the sea has broken through the cliff from below. In heavy swell, the spray and noise make them dramatic; in calmer conditions, you can look straight down through the arches to the churning water below. At the end of the walk stands the Carraig na nÉan sculpture, a large perforated sandstone monolith that frames The Peak surf break — a deliberate alignment that makes for a memorable photograph at sunset.
