---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

For surfing, September to March brings the best swells — large, powerful Atlantic groundswells with The Peak firing at its best from October onwards. Conditions can be spectacular but the weather is cold and wet. A decent wetsuit (at least 4mm, with boots and hood) is essential.

Summer (June to August) is warmer and the town is busiest, with families arriving from across Ireland and Northern Ireland. The waves are smaller and the crowds are larger. July and August have the mildest temperatures (around 17–19°C) and the best chance of sunshine, though rain is possible year-round. Spring (April–May) offers a good balance: decent swell, smaller crowds, and improving weather.
