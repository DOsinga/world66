Camden Fort Meagher guarded the entrance to Cork Harbour from the Crosshaven side. The site began as a 16th-century artillery position and was heavily rebuilt in the 19th century, leaving a substantial coastal fort with tunnels, batteries, and harbour views.

It is a strong outing from Carrigaline or Crosshaven for anyone interested in military history, coastal engineering, or the harbour's strategic past.
