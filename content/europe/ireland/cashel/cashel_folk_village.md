Cashel Folk Village is a small heritage museum with reconstructed thatched shops, a forge, and other village buildings. Displays also cover a penal chapel, local history, and the Irish revolutionary period from 1916 to 1923.

It is a compact companion stop to the Rock of Cashel, giving more local texture to the town's history.
