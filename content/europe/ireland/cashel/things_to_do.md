---
title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Cashel is almost entirely defined by the Rock — the dramatic limestone outcrop that rises 60 metres above the Tipperary plain, topped by one of Ireland's most impressive collections of medieval buildings. But the town below rewards a few extra hours: Hore Abbey, the Brú Ború cultural centre, and a couple of good pubs make it worth an overnight stop rather than a rushed day trip.
