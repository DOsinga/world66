Castletownbere sits near the far end of the Beara Peninsula, where a working fishing harbour cuts into one of the most rugged corners of West Cork. It is not a polished seaside town. Nets, trucks, fish boxes and grey industrial corners are part of the picture, but so are dark hills, Atlantic light, and a harbour so well sheltered by [Bere Island](/europe/ireland/castletownbere/bere_island) that it has been strategically important for centuries.

The town is a practical base for the south side of Beara. You can walk out to [Dereenataggart Stone Circle](/europe/ireland/castletownbere/dereenataggart_stone_circle), poke around the broken walls of [Dunboy Castle](/europe/ireland/castletownbere/dunboy_castle), or take the ferry across to Bere Island for military ruins, quiet lanes and sea views. Farther west, the road runs to Allihies, Dursey Sound and some of the wildest driving in Ireland.

Castletownbere is also good for a simple evening. Have seafood, then settle into [MacCarthy's Bar](/europe/ireland/castletownbere/maccarthys_bar), the old pub and shop made famous by Pete McCarthy's travel book. This is a place for travellers who like their scenery with real working-town edges.
