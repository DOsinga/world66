---
title: When to Go
type: section
---

Cavan is a year-round destination for fishing, but for everything else May to September is the practical window. The summer months bring the best walking weather, though the bog and drumlin terrain is waterproof-boot territory whatever the season. July and August see the lakes busiest with anglers and watersports visitors.

The county gets less rainfall than the west of Ireland but more than the east — expect overcast skies as the norm, with clear days arriving as a bonus. Autumn is beautiful, with the birch and oak woods around Lough Oughter turning amber in October.
