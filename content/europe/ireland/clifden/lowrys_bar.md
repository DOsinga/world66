Lowry's is a compact Market Street pub with the ingredients you want in a Connemara evening: stout, whiskey, close-set tables and live traditional music when the night is right. It is popular with visitors, but still feels like a proper town pub rather than a themed show.

Drop in early if you want a seat for music. On wet nights, that can be the difference between a memorable session and listening from the doorway.
