Carey's Castle is a romantic ruin in the woods above the Glenary River, a short drive or longer walk from Clonmel. The building is more atmospheric than monumental, with broken walls, trees, and damp stone giving it the feel of a discovered place.

The walk is the point as much as the ruin. Wear shoes that can handle mud, allow time for the woodland setting, and treat the castle as a quiet local adventure rather than a staffed heritage attraction.
