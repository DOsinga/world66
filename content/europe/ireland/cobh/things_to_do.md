---

Cobh's sights are about the sea and the people who left across it. Start at the [Cobh Heritage Centre](/europe/ireland/cobh/cobh_heritage_centre) and the [Titanic Experience](/europe/ireland/cobh/titanic_experience), climb to [St Colman's Cathedral](/europe/ireland/cobh/st_colmans_cathedral), and give [Spike Island](/europe/ireland/cobh/spike_island) the best part of an afternoon. Most of it is within a short, steep walk of the railway station.
