The Titanic Experience is a guided tour through the White Star Line's old departure hall on Casement Square, following the route taken by the 123 passengers who boarded the Titanic here on 11 April 1912. The ship was too big to dock, so passengers were taken out by the tenders America and Ireland to where she lay at anchor; two hours later she was on her way to New York.

The tour tells the story through the people who left from Queenstown, most of whom did not survive. It's open daily, and adult admission was €13 in 2025. If you want more, the hour-long Titanic Trail walking tour of the town leaves twice a day.
