The Quiet Man Museum is a small, unabashedly nostalgic museum devoted to John Ford's 1952 film, much of which was shot in and around Cong. It recreates the White O'Morn cottage interior and leans into the film's imagined Ireland: costumes, props, village walking tours, and plenty of green-painted sentiment.

If you have no interest in the film, this is skippable. If you do, it is the reason Cong still has a second life on the travel map, and the museum has enough affection and local memory to make the stop worthwhile.
