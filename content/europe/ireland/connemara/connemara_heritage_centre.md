The Connemara Heritage and History Centre near Clifden tells the story of this area's complicated history: the Famine, emigration, land agitation, and the persistence of the Irish language in a region that was hit harder by the 19th century than most of Ireland. The exhibition is housed in a traditional stone building and includes period photographs, artefacts, and personal accounts from the families who lived through the clearances.

The centre also has a small but genuine reproduction of a Connemara farm cottage as it would have appeared before the Famine — rough stone walls, a turf fire, minimal furniture — which gives a physical sense of the poverty that drove so many to leave. The staff are local and knowledgeable. It is a useful counterpoint to the scenic beauty outside.
