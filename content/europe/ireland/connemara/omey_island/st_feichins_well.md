St Feichin's Well is a quiet holy well near the shore, marked by a low ring of stones and small devotional offerings. It is not dramatic, but it adds another layer to Omey's religious landscape and gives the island walk a destination beyond the beach.

Treat it gently. Like many Irish holy wells, its meaning lies as much in local memory and repeated small acts of devotion as in the site itself.
