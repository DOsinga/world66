The Pearse Cultural Centre links Connemara's Irish-speaking culture with Patrick Pearse, the teacher, writer, and Easter Rising leader who spent time in the area before his execution in 1916. The centre is most rewarding if you are interested in the Irish language, local history, or the political roots of modern Ireland.
