title: Things to Do
type: section
---

Connemara's main activities are walking, driving scenic roads, and exploring the coast. The [Twelve Bens](/europe/ireland/connemara/twelve_bens) and [Maumturk Mountains](/europe/ireland/connemara/maumturk_mountains) are for serious hillwalkers; [Diamond Hill](/europe/ireland/connemara/diamond_hill) and the [Connemara National Park](/europe/ireland/connemara/connemara_national_park) trails suit all levels. The historic highlights are [Kylemore Abbey](/europe/ireland/connemara/kylemore_abbey) and the [Alcock and Brown Memorial](/europe/ireland/connemara/alcock_and_brown_memorial) at Derrigimlagh.
