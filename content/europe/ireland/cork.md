Cork is Ireland's second city and the capital of Munster, a compact and hilly place built on islands in the River Lee estuary. The city centre sits between two channels of the river, with the streets rising steeply on both banks. It has a distinct character — more relaxed than Dublin, proudly independent, and genuinely proud of its food culture, its pubs, and its hurling team.

The [English Market](/europe/ireland/cork/english_market), a Victorian covered market near [St Patrick's Street](/europe/ireland/cork/st_patricks_street), is Cork at its best: local producers selling butter, charcuterie, fish, cheese, and breads in a handsome nineteenth-century building that Queen Elizabeth II visited in 2011. The market is working rather than touristic, and worth at least an hour. St Patrick's Street, the main commercial street, was redesigned in 2004 and is the more polished face of the city. Up on the north bank, [Shandon](/europe/ireland/cork/shandon) is the old wool-trading quarter, dominated by [St Anne's Church](/europe/ireland/cork/shandon_bells), whose mismatched sandstone and limestone tower and famously unreliable clock faces earned it the nickname "the four-faced liar." Visitors can climb the tower and ring the bells.

[St Fin Barre's Cathedral](/europe/ireland/cork/st_fin_barres_cathedral), on the southern edge of the centre, is the city's most imposing building — a Gothic Revival structure completed in 1879 with an elaborate west façade. [University College Cork](/europe/ireland/cork/university_college_cork), to the west, has fine Victorian Gothic buildings and the [Lewis Glucksman Gallery](/europe/ireland/cork/lewis_glucksman_gallery), which houses a good collection of Irish art. The city's pub scene is active; the [Franciscan Well Brewery](/europe/ireland/cork/franciscan_well) on North Mall brews its own beers on site, and [Sin É](/europe/ireland/cork/sin_e) on Coburg Street is a reliable spot for traditional music.

Cork is the main hub for County Cork, which includes [Blarney Castle](/europe/ireland/cork/blarney_castle), Kinsale, and the Beara and Sheep's Head peninsulas — all accessible as day trips or short overnight trips.
