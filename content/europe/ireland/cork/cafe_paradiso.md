One of Ireland's best-known vegetarian restaurants, on Lancaster Quay near UCC. Denis Cotter has been cooking serious vegetable-led food here since 1993, drawing produce from Gort-na-Nain Farm in Ballygarvan and turning out dishes that change with the season. The room is small and bright; reservations are essential.
