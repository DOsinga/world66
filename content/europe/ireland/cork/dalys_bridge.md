A small suspension footbridge across the north channel of the Lee, linking the Mardyke and Fitzgerald's Park to Sunday's Well. Corkonians have always called it the Shakey Bridge, because it bounces as you cross. It replaced a ferry; the path leading to it is still called Ferry Walk.
