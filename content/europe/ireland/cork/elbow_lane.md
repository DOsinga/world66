A narrow seventeen-seat restaurant on a lane off Oliver Plunkett Street, with a wood-fired grill at one end of the room and a small brewery at the other. The menu is short — grilled meats and fish, plates of bone marrow, smoked things — and the beers are brewed on the premises. Reservations are almost essential.
