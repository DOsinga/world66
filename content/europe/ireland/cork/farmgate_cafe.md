A small lunchroom and tea-room on the balcony above the English Market, serving food drawn entirely from the stalls below: tripe and drisheen on toast, smoked salmon, soup, plates of cured meats, and a daily roast. Counter on one side, full table service on the other. No reservations; queues at the weekend. Closed evenings.
