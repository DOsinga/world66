Fota Wildlife Park is one of the easiest family day trips from Cork, set on Fota Island east of the city and reachable by road or train. It is more open than a traditional city zoo, with several species kept in large paddocks or free-ranging areas, and enough space to make the visit feel like a walk rather than a queue of cages.

The park works best when you give it a half day and treat the island as part of the outing. It is popular with children, but not only for children: the conservation work, landscaping, and relaxed Cork-harbour setting make it a good break from streets, pubs, and stone monuments.
