A long-running modern Irish bistro on Phoenix Street, run by the Barry sisters since 1982. The menu changes daily according to what the English Market and the Cork fish quays have delivered — squid, hake, hereford beef, Skeaghanore duck — and the wine list is short and clever. Tightly booked at the weekend.
