A contemporary art gallery on the UCC campus, in a 2004 building by O'Donnell + Tuomey that won the European Union Prize for Contemporary Architecture — a cantilevered timber and stone box raised into the canopy of the campus trees. The exhibition programme rotates every few months and leans toward Irish contemporary art. The downstairs café and bookshop are useful. Admission is free.
