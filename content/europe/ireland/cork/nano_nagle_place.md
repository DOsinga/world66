Nano Nagle Place turns an eighteenth-century convent and school site into one of Cork's most thoughtful heritage stops. It tells the story of Nano Nagle and the Presentation Sisters through education, social reform, architecture, gardens, and the changing city around them.

The complex is calm and well scaled: museum rooms, chapel, gardens, cafe, and community spaces sit together rather than feeling like a single static exhibit. It is a strong choice for visitors who want Cork history beyond pubs and shopping streets.
