A large, lively pub on Oliver Plunkett Street with traditional music every night, a beer garden, regular comedy upstairs, and a wide spread of food. It is more polished than Sin É and busier with visitors, but the music is consistently good and the place has avoided turning into a parody of itself. A reliable evening choice when other pubs are quiet.
