Dalkey is a small, upscale village on the coast south of [Dublin](/europe/ireland/dublin), reachable in half an hour on the DART train from Pearse Station. It makes one of the best day trips from the city — scenic, walkable, and blissfully far from Temple Bar.

The centre of the village is compact and pleasant, with good restaurants alongside the usual pubs. A walking tour takes you past several medieval castles: [Dalkey Castle](/europe/ireland/dalkey/dalkey_castle) (also known as Goat's Castle) and [Archbold's Castle](/europe/ireland/dalkey/archbolds_castle) both date from the fifteenth century. Follow the winding streets eastward and you reach the coast, where [Dalkey Island](/europe/ireland/dalkey/dalkey_island) sits just offshore — its ruins of a Napoleonic-era battery and an early medieval church visible from the mainland.

[Sorrento Park](/europe/ireland/dalkey/sorrento_park), nearly hidden on a steep little hill, offers views along the coastline and out to the island, and on a clear day you can see all the way to Wales. For a longer walk, the [Vico Road](/europe/ireland/dalkey/vico_road) south toward Killiney has some of the best coastal scenery in the Dublin area, and the climb up [Killiney Hill](/europe/ireland/dalkey/killiney_hill) or the old [Dalkey Quarry](/europe/ireland/dalkey/dalkey_quarry) caps the visit.
