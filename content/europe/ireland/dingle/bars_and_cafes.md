---
title: "Bars and Cafes"
type: section
---

Dingle is famous for having an absurd number of pubs for a town its size — somewhere between forty and fifty depending on how you count — and most of them serve as informal venues for traditional music every night of the summer. The sessions tend to start late (around nine or nine-thirty), are unamplified, and are the real thing rather than the curated kind you find in the bigger Irish cities.

**Dick Mack's** and **John Benny's** are the two most-talked-about pubs in town and deserve the reputation. **An Droichead Beag** ("the small bridge") on Lower Main Street has nightly trad sessions and tolerates a fair amount of dancing on the flagstones. **Foxy John's** is the wonderful old hardware-shop-and-pub combination where you can buy wellingtons and a pint without leaving the bar.

For coffee, **My Boy Blue** on Holyground does the best espresso in town and **Bean in Dingle** on Green Street is the morning meeting spot for locals. **Murphy's Ice Cream** is essentially a town institution and should be visited at least once.
