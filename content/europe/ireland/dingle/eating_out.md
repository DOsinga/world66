---
title: "Eating Out"
type: section
---

Dingle eats well above its size. The harbour brings in fresh fish most mornings and the kitchens have learned to cook it simply and well. The two best-known seafood places — **Out of the Blue** on the pier and the dining room at **The Boatyard** — sit at opposite ends of the formality scale and both turn out properly good food.

For something quicker, **John Benny's** and a handful of other pubs do reliable chowder and pints. **Idás** on Dykegate Street is the closest the town has to fine dining: ten-table tasting menu, foraged ingredients, booked months ahead in summer. The local **Dingle Bay Hotel** carvery and the chippers on Main Street cover the budget end. Avoid the few visibly tourist-bait places on the main quay in high season; the better food is always one street back.
