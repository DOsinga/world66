Donegal is big and empty, and so is its local writing. Two sources stand out: the county tourism site, which writes about the county in detail, and a local historian on the Inishowen peninsula.

Between them they will find you a coffee at the end of every walk, and a penal-era Mass rock above Tremone Bay that, as the historian says, the tourist never sees.
