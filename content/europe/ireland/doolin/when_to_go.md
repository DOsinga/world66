---
title: When to Go
type: section
---

The west of Ireland's weather is famously variable, but Doolin rewards visits in almost any season. Summer (June to August) is busiest — the pubs fill early, the ferry queues can be long, and the village loses some of its quiet character. The shoulder months of May and September are ideal: better weather than winter, fewer tourists, and the Burren wildflowers at their best in late spring.

Winter is quiet but not unpleasant. The pubs are warm and the sessions more intimate. The cliffs in storm conditions, with Atlantic swells driving spray up the rock face, are a sight that summer visitors rarely see. Wrap up well.
