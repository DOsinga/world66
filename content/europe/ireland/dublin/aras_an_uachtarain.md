Áras an Uachtaráin is the official residence of the President of Ireland, a handsome Georgian mansion set within Phoenix Park. Originally built in 1751 as a park ranger's lodge, it was enlarged and became the Viceregal Lodge before independence, and has been the President's residence since 1938.

Free guided tours run on Saturdays, departing from the Phoenix Park Visitor Centre and covering the principal state rooms with their portraits of former presidents, fine plasterwork, and views over the park. Booking is advisable in summer. The exterior and gardens are not open independently.
