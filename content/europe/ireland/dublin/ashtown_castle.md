Ashtown Castle is a 17th-century tower house that lay hidden inside a later Georgian mansion for over 200 years, only discovered during demolition work in the 1980s. It now stands exposed and restored as the centrepiece of the Phoenix Park Visitor Centre, a remarkable piece of architectural detective work.

The visitor centre itself has good exhibits on the history of Phoenix Park — its origins as a royal deer park, its role in Irish political history, and its ecology. The Ashtown Castle tower can be viewed from the outside, and the centre is free to enter.
