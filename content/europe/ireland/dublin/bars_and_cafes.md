---
title: "Bars and Cafes"
type: section
---

Dublin's pubs are the best argument for the city. Not the bright, themed tourist operations in Temple Bar — the real ones: Victorian mahogany interiors, a coal fire in winter, Guinness pulled correctly, and a conversation that starts with the rugby and ends somewhere unexpected. The best of them have resisted every temptation to modernise.

**Kehoe's** on South Anne Street and **The Stag's Head** off Dame Street are two of the finest Victorian pub interiors in Europe. **Mulligan's** on Poolbeg Street and **Doheny & Nesbitt** on Baggot Street are where locals go when they want a serious drink without atmosphere-by-numbers. **Davy Byrne's** on Duke Street has the Joycean connection. For traditional music, several Temple Bar pubs — the **Palace Bar**, the **Gogarty's** — run sessions that attract real musicians. Avoid the obvious tourist traps and you'll be fine.
