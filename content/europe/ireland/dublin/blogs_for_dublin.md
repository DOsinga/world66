Dublin has an enormous amount written about it, and most of it is the same list. Two local sources do something different. One is a long-running blog by two Dublin historians about the city's pubs, lanes and odd corners. The other is the city's independent paper, which has a series on art hiding in plain sight.

That is the honest count. Several other Dublin blogs turned out to be advertorial, dormant, or written for search engines rather than readers.
