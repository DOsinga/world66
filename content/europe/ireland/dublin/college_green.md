College Green is the triangular public space at the heart of Dublin city, flanked by the front gates of Trinity College and the imposing Palladian façade of the former Parliament House (now Bank of Ireland) on the opposite side. The square has been the city's civic focal point since the 18th century and remains a major meeting point and transport hub.

The Bank of Ireland building, which housed the Irish Parliament until the Act of Union in 1800, can be visited inside during banking hours. The roofed Chamber of the House of Lords, with its original 18th-century tapestries and chandelier, is particularly impressive and free to see.
