Croke Park is the home of Gaelic games and one of the great sporting venues in Ireland. Dublin County GAA plays here, national hurling and Gaelic football finals fill the 82,000-seat stadium, and the museum explains why these games matter so much to Irish identity.

Tickets for major matches can be hard to get, but stadium tours run outside event times and include access to the GAA Museum. The Skyline walk across the roof adds wide views over north Dublin for visitors who want something more than a standard stadium tour.
