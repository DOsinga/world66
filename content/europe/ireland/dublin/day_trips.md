Dublin is well placed for day trips. The [Wicklow Mountains](/europe/ireland/wicklow_mountains) begin barely 20 km south, with the monastic ruins of [Glendalough](/europe/ireland/glendalough) in their glacial valley the standout destination. [Howth](/europe/ireland/howth) headland to the northeast gives you cliff walks and fresh seafood in 30 minutes on the DART. The coastal villages of [Dalkey](/europe/ireland/dalkey), Killiney, and [Dún Laoghaire](/europe/ireland/dun_laoghaire) are all on the DART line and worth a couple of hours each. Closer to home, the castles and great houses of north County Dublin — Malahide Castle and Newbridge House among them — make easy outings, while Powerscourt Estate in Co. Wicklow, with its 47 acres of formal gardens backed by Sugar Loaf Mountain, fills a pleasant half-day by car or tour bus.
