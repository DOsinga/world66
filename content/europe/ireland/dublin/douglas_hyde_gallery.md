The Douglas Hyde Gallery is Trinity College's dedicated contemporary art space, showing challenging and thought-provoking exhibitions by Irish and international artists. Entry is free, the programme is serious, and it operates to the same standard as the better-known Dublin City Gallery (The Hugh Lane) with a fraction of the visitors.

The gallery is named after Douglas Hyde, the first President of Ireland, and occupies a modernist building within the college campus. It's a good destination if you want to see what's genuinely current in Irish and European contemporary art.
