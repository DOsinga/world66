Farmleigh is a grand Victorian house at the north-western edge of Phoenix Park, built in the 1880s and purchased by the Guinness family who extended and lavishly refitted it. The Irish State bought Farmleigh in 1999 to use as an official guest house for visiting heads of state and dignitaries.

The house is open free to visitors most weekends, with guided tours of the principal rooms showing the Guinness family's opulent taste — a ballroom, billiards room, library, and collections of fine art. The demesne gardens and working clock tower estate are attractive in any season.
