The Fry Model Railway at Malahide Castle is a remarkable collection of handmade model Irish trains, assembled by railway engineer Cyril Fry in the 1920s to 1950s. The models are unusual in their subject matter — while most model railways focus on British or European prototypes, Fry meticulously reproduced Irish rolling stock, including the distinctive narrow-gauge railways of the west of Ireland that no longer exist.

The collection runs on a large layout depicting Dublin and surroundings, with the DART, the Liffey, and Malahide Castle itself modelled. It's an unusual and delightful experience — part transport museum, part art object.
