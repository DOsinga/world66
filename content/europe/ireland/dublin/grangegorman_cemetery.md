Grangegorman Military Cemetery is the final resting place of 614 Commonwealth soldiers, many of them from the First World War, laid out in neat white headstones in the Commonwealth War Graves Commission tradition. It is one of several military cemeteries in Dublin, and one of the most affecting — small enough to walk through in its entirety, with headstones for soldiers from across the British Empire who died in Dublin hospitals.

The cemetery is freely accessible and maintained to the high standard typical of CWGC sites. It sits on Blackhorse Avenue at the edge of Phoenix Park, a short walk from the park.
