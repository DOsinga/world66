Jameson whiskey was made on Bow Street from 1780 until production moved to Midleton in County Cork in the 1970s. The old Dublin distillery is now a visitor centre and museum, with guided tours explaining the history, ingredients, and production process behind Ireland's best-known whiskey brand.
