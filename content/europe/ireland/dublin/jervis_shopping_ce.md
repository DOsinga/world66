Jervis Shopping Centre is one of Dublin's largest city-centre malls, occupying a full block off Mary Street in the north inner city. It opened in 1996 and holds around 60 shops including high-street names, a food court, and a multi-screen cinema. Practical for shopping if you are in the area, and handy for the Jervis Luas stop which sits right outside.
