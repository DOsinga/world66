The Knockmaree Dolmen is a megalithic portal tomb dating to around 3000 BC, making it one of the oldest monuments in Dublin. It stands in a quiet corner of Phoenix Park near the Chapelizod Gate, largely unknown to most park visitors. The chamber is small but intact, with a large capstone resting on two upright stones.

Finding it is part of the experience — it's signposted but not prominently, and the walk through the park to reach it passes through one of the quieter sections of the grounds.
