Magazine Fort is an 18th-century military fortification built on a hillock in Phoenix Park to store gunpowder and military supplies for the garrison. Jonathan Swift mockingly proposed it as the ideal location for a magazine fort, not believing it would actually be built there, but it was. The fort is probably best known in modern Irish history for the IRA raid of December 1939, in which almost all of the Irish Army's ammunition was stolen.

The fort is closed to the public but clearly visible from the park paths below the hillock, and makes a dramatic silhouette against the sky.
