The pub on Merrion Row where Ronnie Drew and Luke Kelly first played together in 1962, giving birth to The Dubliners. The sessions continue, most nights from around 9pm, without announcement or reservation — you walk in and if you're lucky a full trad session is underway in the back bar. Cramped, smoky in spirit if not in law, and exactly what it was fifty years ago. Get there early on weekends or you won't fit in the door.
