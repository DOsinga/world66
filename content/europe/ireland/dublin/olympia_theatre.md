The Olympia is Dublin's oldest surviving music hall, operating continuously since 1879. Its ornate Victorian interior — a tiered balcony, cast-iron columns, and elaborate plasterwork — makes it one of the most atmospheric live music venues in Ireland. The intimacy of the room means even a seat at the back feels close to the stage.

The venue hosts a wide range of acts, from rock and folk concerts to comedy and occasional theatre. It's particularly beloved for late-night shows and the annual Christmas pantomime. On concert nights the standing area in front of the stage fills quickly.
