The People's Garden is the only formal garden in Phoenix Park, a Victorian pleasure ground near the Parkgate Street entrance with structured flower beds, a small pond, and a bandstand used for summer concerts. It was laid out in 1864 for the public to enjoy, when most of the park was reserved for the Viceregal Lodge.

In spring it's filled with tulips and daffodils; in summer the rose beds are at their best. It's a gentle introduction to the park and a good place for a picnic near the main entrance.
