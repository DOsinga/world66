At 707 hectares, Phoenix Park is one of the largest enclosed urban parks in the world. The Irish President's residence (Áras an Uachtaráin) is inside, and tours are available on Saturdays. A herd of 600 fallow deer has roamed the park since the 1660s. Dublin Zoo occupies the eastern corner. The Papal Cross marks where John Paul II said mass for 1.25 million people in 1979. It's a 20-minute walk or a short bus from O'Connell Street — worth the trip for a morning walk or a picnic.
