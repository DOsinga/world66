Phoenix Park is one of the largest enclosed public parks in any European capital — 1,750 acres of meadows, woodland, and formal gardens, far bigger than Hyde Park or the Bois de Boulogne. It lies a short bus ride west of the city centre.

The park is home to a herd of around 600 fallow deer, descendants of animals introduced in the 1660s. They are wild but unfazed by visitors and can often be seen grazing near the road. The **Papal Cross** marks where one million people gathered to hear Mass when Pope John Paul II visited in 1979. The **Wellington Testimonial**, a 62-metre obelisk, is the largest obelisk in Europe. The official residences of both the Irish President (Áras an Uachtaráin) and the US Ambassador are within the park grounds.

The **Dublin Zoo** occupies the eastern edge of the park. Cyclists, joggers, and families use the park daily; on weekday mornings it is peaceful enough to feel like genuine countryside inside the city.
