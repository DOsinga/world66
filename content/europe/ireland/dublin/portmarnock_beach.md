Portmarnock Beach is one of the finest Blue Flag beaches in the Dublin area — a long, clean sandy strand on the eastern side of the Portmarnock peninsula, facing across the bay toward Lambay Island. The beach is sheltered enough for swimming in summer and broad enough to walk for a kilometre without reaching the end.

The beach is reached by DART to Portmarnock station, followed by a short walk. On summer weekends it draws families from across north Dublin, but the length of the strand means it rarely feels crowded. The water quality is consistently good.
