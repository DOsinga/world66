The Portmarnock Promenade and coast path runs along the shoreline from the village southward, with wide views across the estuary toward Howth Head and Ireland's Eye. The walk is popular year-round with local residents, and on clear days the Wicklow Mountains are visible across the bay to the south.

The path passes the yacht club and continues along the strand to connect with the beach. At low tide it's possible to walk much of the peninsula's shoreline. The light on this stretch of coast, particularly in the evening, is exceptional.
