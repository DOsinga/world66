Sandymount Strand is a broad tidal beach southeast of central Dublin, backed by a grassy promenade. It is a good place for a long, breezy walk with views across Dublin Bay.

Look for the Martello Tower and the sculpture *Awaiting the Mariner* along the shore. The strand changes character with the tide, so check conditions before walking far out.
