Science Gallery Dublin is a free exhibition space at the Pearse Street entrance to Trinity College, producing some of the most imaginative public programming about the intersection of science and culture in Ireland. Exhibitions have explored topics from sleep to viruses, from memory to sport, all through a lens that combines scientific research with art and design.

The gallery is the flagship of a global network of Science Galleries, and the standard of the exhibitions and their presentation is consistently high. It attracts a younger and more diverse audience than the traditional Dublin museum scene.
