The Shelbourne is Dublin's grand dame hotel, dominating the north side of St Stephen's Green since 1824. Its history is inseparable from that of modern Ireland: the Irish Constitution was drafted in Room 112 in 1922. The hotel has been the gathering point for Dublin society, visiting dignitaries, and political figures for two centuries.

Non-guests can visit for afternoon tea, a meal in the restaurant, or a drink in the legendary Horseshoe Bar — a small, dark, wood-panelled room that has hosted more significant conversations than most parliamentary chambers. The bar is a genuine Dublin institution.
