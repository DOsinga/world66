The Long Room in Trinity College's Old Library is one of the great library interiors of the world — a 65-metre barrel-vaulted hall lined from floor to ceiling with 200,000 of the library's oldest books, the air carrying the particular smell of centuries-old leather and paper. Marble busts of philosophers and writers line the central aisle.

The same building houses the Book of Kells, a 9th-century illuminated gospel manuscript of extraordinary beauty and detail, considered one of the finest works of Western art. The exhibition explaining its creation and context is well done. Book tickets in advance in peak season — the queues can be long.
