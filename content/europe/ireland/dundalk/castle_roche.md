Castle Roche is one of County Louth's most impressive ruins, standing on a rocky rise with wide views over the surrounding countryside. Its broken walls and gatehouse still communicate the strength of an Anglo-Norman frontier castle without needing much reconstruction.

The site is best for travelers with a car and an interest in castles, borderlands, or photography. It is atmospheric rather than heavily interpreted, so bring context with you and leave time to appreciate the setting.
