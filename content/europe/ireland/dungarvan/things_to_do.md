type: section
---

Dungarvan's main draw is outdoors: cycle the [Waterford Greenway](/europe/ireland/dungarvan/waterford_greenway), swim at [Clonea Strand](/europe/ireland/dungarvan/clonea_strand), or walk out on [the Cunnigar](/europe/ireland/dungarvan/the_cunnigar). In town, [Dungarvan Castle](/europe/ireland/dungarvan/dungarvan_castle) and the Waterford County Museum in the Old Town Hall fill a rainy morning.
