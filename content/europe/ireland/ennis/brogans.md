Brogan's is one of the most reliable music pubs in Ennis: traditional sessions seven nights a week through the summer, most nights of the week the rest of the year, and a roster of regular Clare players who give the sessions weight. The pub is also a working bar with food — substantial Irish staples rather than gastropub fare — and the front rooms hold the music while the back is given over to dinner.

Get in by 9 pm if you want a seat for the music; later than that and it is standing only.
