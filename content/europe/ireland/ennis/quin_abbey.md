Quin Abbey is one of County Clare's most atmospheric monastic ruins, a Franciscan friary
built into and around earlier Norman remains. The walls, cloister, tower, and open sky
give it a strong sense of place without the heavy traffic of better-known Irish ruins.

From Ennis it is an easy side trip, especially if you are exploring castles, abbeys, and
village landscapes east of town. The site rewards slow wandering and a little historical
reading before you go.
