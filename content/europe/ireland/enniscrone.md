Enniscrone is a seaside village on the west coast of County Sligo, built around a long strand, dunes, surf schools, and the old habit of soaking in hot seawater and seaweed. It is a resort, but not only a resort strip: the beach is genuinely good, the Atlantic setting is broad, and the seaweed baths give it a local ritual that many prettier beaches lack.

[Enniscrone Beach](/europe/ireland/enniscrone/enniscrone_beach) is the main draw, a long sweep of sand suited to walking, swimming, and beginner surfing. [Kilcullen's Seaweed Baths](/europe/ireland/enniscrone/kilcullens_seaweed_baths) has been operating since 1912 in an Edwardian bathhouse above the shore. Add the old [Cliff Baths](/europe/ireland/enniscrone/cliff_baths), the ruin of [O'Dowd's Castle](/europe/ireland/enniscrone/odowds_castle), and the dunes around the golf course, and Enniscrone has enough for a proper spare day.

The village is easy to reach from Ballina or Sligo by road. It is best in good weather, but even on a grey day the beach walk and a seaweed bath make a strong argument for stopping.
