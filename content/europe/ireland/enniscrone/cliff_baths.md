The Cliff Baths are a small white castle-like building above the shore, built in 1890 and now closed. You cannot use the baths inside, but the tide-filled rock pools below still make the place worth a look on a walk along the village edge.

It is a minor sight, though a useful one: it shows that Enniscrone's sea-bathing culture did not begin with modern wellness language.
