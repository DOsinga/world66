Enniscrone Beach is a long Atlantic strand backed by dunes, with enough space for walking even when summer brings families, surfers, and swimmers. The surf is usually forgiving by west-coast standards, which is why beginners come here.

At low tide the beach feels enormous. Walk west along the sand for open views, or stay near the village end if you want the seaweed baths, cafes, and surf schools close by.
