Barna Woods is not a wilderness, but it is one of Galway's better quick escapes from traffic and shopfronts. The mixed broadleaf woodland lies west of the city near Barna Road, with short paths through damp, leafy ground and access from Cappagh Park or the coastal road.

Its value is in the combination: a woodland stroll, then the option to continue toward Silver Strand and Galway Bay. It suits a low-key afternoon when you want fresh air without committing to Connemara or a full day outside the city.
