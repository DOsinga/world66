Charlie Byrne's is one of Ireland's most beloved independent bookshops, a sprawling, atmospheric secondhand and new bookshop in the Cornstore Mall on Middle Street, two minutes from Shop Street. It stocks everything from current Irish fiction to obscure out-of-print titles, local history, and a particularly good section on Irish language and literature. The shelves are dense and browsing rewarding; you will almost certainly find something you did not know you needed.

The shop is a Galway institution and a good measure of the city's character — the kind of place that survives and thrives in a city that takes reading seriously. Staff recommendations, handwritten and taped to the shelves, are consistently interesting. For anyone travelling onward into Connemara or to the Aran Islands, this is the place to pick up reading material before leaving the city.
