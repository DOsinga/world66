Galway Atlantaquaria is Ireland's largest native-species aquarium, focusing on Atlantic marine life rather than tropical spectacle. Tanks and touch-pool style interpretation help connect Galway Bay, fishing culture, and the wider west-coast ecosystem.

Its Salthill location makes it easy to pair with the promenade. The aquarium is especially useful for families or for visitors who want a weatherproof stop while staying close to the sea.
