---
title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Galway's charm is in its streets and atmosphere as much as any individual sight. The [Spanish Arch](/europe/ireland/galway/spanish_arch), the medieval city walls, and the Long Walk along the harbour are all within a few minutes of each other. The [Galway City Museum](/europe/ireland/galway/galway_city_museum) beside the arch is free and well worth an hour. The great medieval church of [St. Nicholas](/europe/ireland/galway/st_nicholas_church) stands on the corner of Shop Street, and [Galway Cathedral](/europe/ireland/galway/galway_cathedral) rises over the Corrib a few hundred metres west. Beyond the city centre, the [Salthill Promenade](/europe/ireland/galway/salthill_promenade) stretches along the bay and is perfect for a windblown walk.
