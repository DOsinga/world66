Clara Lara Funpark is a family adventure park in the Wicklow woods, close enough to Glendalough to serve as an easy add-on for travellers with children. Its appeal is simple and physical: boats, slides, rope bridges, play areas, and enough outdoor mess to make it feel like a proper day out rather than a polished indoor attraction.

It is seasonal and weather-dependent, so check opening dates before promising it to children. Adults visiting Glendalough for monastic ruins and mountain scenery may not need it, but families often do. It gives younger travellers a release valve after quieter heritage stops.
