Glengarriff is a tiny West Cork village with a setting that does most of the work for it. Bantry Bay lies in front, the Caha Mountains rise behind, and the N71 road squeezes through a glen of oak, birch, and rhododendron before dropping toward the harbour.

The village is best used slowly. Boats run from the pier to [Garinish Island](/europe/ireland/glengarriff/garinish_island), a sheltered garden island in the bay, while the [Glengarriff Nature Reserve](/europe/ireland/glengarriff/glengarriff_nature_reserve) has short forest walks that feel more Atlantic rainforest than tidy parkland. If you only have one walk in you, climb to [Lady Bantry's Lookout](/europe/ireland/glengarriff/lady_bantrys_lookout) for the view back over the harbour.

Glengarriff is also a useful hinge between [Bantry](/europe/ireland/bantry), the Beara Peninsula, and the road to [Kenmare](/europe/ireland/kenmare). It is not a place of big monuments or nightlife. Come for gardens, ferries, weather, and mountain roads, and keep your plans loose enough for rain.
