---
title: Things to Do
type: section
---

Glengarriff's best sights are outdoors and close together: take the boat to [Garinish Island](/europe/ireland/glengarriff/garinish_island), walk the woods of the nature reserve, and save a clear hour for the climb to [Lady Bantry's Lookout](/europe/ireland/glengarriff/lady_bantrys_lookout). With a car, the rougher roads over [Priest's Leap](/europe/ireland/glengarriff/priests_leap) and toward the Caha Mountains give the village its wilder setting.
