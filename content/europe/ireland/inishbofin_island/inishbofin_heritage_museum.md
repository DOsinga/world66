Inishbofin Heritage Museum is a small, useful stop near the old pier. Its collections focus on ordinary island life: tools, household objects, photographs, fishing, farming, and the work that kept a small Atlantic community going.

Visit early in the day if it is open. The museum gives the beaches and ruins more context, and it is close enough to the ferry to fit even a short visit.
