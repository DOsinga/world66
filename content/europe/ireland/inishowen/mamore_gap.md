The Mamore Gap is the pass where the Inishowen 100 leaves the Lough Swilly shore after Dunree and climbs inland between the Urris Hills and Raghtin More. From the top you look out over the small walled fields of Urris to the Atlantic.

Stop at the viewpoint rather than trying to take it all in from the driver's seat. From the gap the route drops towards Leenan Bay and Dunaff, then on to Clonmany and Ballyliffin.
