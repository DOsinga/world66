---
title: "Eating Out"
type: section
---

There are only a handful of places to eat on Inis Oirr, all within a few minutes' walk of the pier. **Tigh Ned's**, the pub next to the pier, does soup, sandwiches, and the classic Irish bar lunch alongside a pint of Guinness. **Tigh Ruairí** (Rory's), a bit further along, has fresh seafood — crab claws, chowder, mackerel — and a beer garden looking out over the bay. **South Aran House** has a more ambitious tasting-menu restaurant focused on island produce, run from the same building as a small B&B; booking essential.

For something quick or self-catering, the small **island shop** stocks bread, cheese, fruit, and basics. The harbour has a kiosk selling teas and cakes in summer. Eating well on Inis Oirr is possible but limited — most visitors plan a single proper sit-down meal and pick up the rest on the move.
