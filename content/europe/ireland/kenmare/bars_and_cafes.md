---
title: "Bars and Cafés"
type: section
---

Kenmare punches above its weight for pubs, partly because so many of them have a back room used for traditional music sessions. The triangle of Henry Street, Main Street, and Shelbourne Street holds most of them. [Crowley's](/europe/ireland/kenmare/crowleys_bar) and [Davitt's](/europe/ireland/kenmare/davitts) are the most reliable for music; the [Atlantic Bar](/europe/ireland/kenmare/atlantic_bar) is the late-night option.

For daytime coffee, the cafés clustered around Henry Street and the new shopping court do solid work. Most close by six, which is when the pubs take over.
