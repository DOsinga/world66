title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Kenmare is small enough to walk in twenty minutes and dense enough to keep you busy for two or three days. The headline sights are within strolling distance of the triangular market square: the [stone circle](/europe/ireland/kenmare/stonecircles) at the end of Market Street, the [Heritage Centre](/europe/ireland/kenmare/heritage_centre) in the old courthouse, [Reenagross Park](/europe/ireland/kenmare/reenagross_park) along the river, and the [pier](/europe/ireland/kenmare/thepier) at the end of the bay road.

A short drive in any direction opens up more — [Cromwell's Bridge](/europe/ireland/kenmare/cromwellsbridge) and the [Old Cemetery](/europe/ireland/kenmare/oldcemetery) on the Bantry road, the romantic ruin of [Dunkerron Castle](/europe/ireland/kenmare/dunkerron_castle) towards Templenoe, and the [waterfalls](/europe/ireland/kenmare/sheen_falls) of the Sheen river just outside town. The [Ring of Kerry](/europe/ireland/ringofkerry) and the Beara Peninsula start at Kenmare and finish at Kenmare, which is why so many people end up basing themselves here.
