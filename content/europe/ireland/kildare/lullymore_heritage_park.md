Lullymore Heritage Park sits on a dry mineral island in the Bog of Allen, which makes it a useful stop if you want to understand the landscape around Kildare rather than just look at it from a road. The park mixes boardwalks, woodland paths, and open bog views with reconstructions that move from early Christian settlement through the 1798 rebellion and the lives of turf cutters.

This is a better visit than the word "heritage park" might suggest. The exhibits are approachable, but the real value is the setting: sphagnum, birch, dark pools, and the strange quiet of a raised bog that once covered much of the midlands. Allow a couple of hours, and bring shoes that can handle damp ground after rain.
