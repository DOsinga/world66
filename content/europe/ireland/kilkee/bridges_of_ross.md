The Bridges of Ross is a natural sea arch on the Atlantic coast of the Loop Head peninsula, near the tiny village of Kilbaha, about 25 kilometres west of Kilkee. There's a car park by the site.

It's an easy stop to combine with the drive out to Loop Head lighthouse at the tip of the peninsula, a few kilometres further west.
