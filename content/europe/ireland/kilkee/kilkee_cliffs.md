West of the town, past the Diamond Rocks, a clifftop path follows the coast with views over sea stacks, coves and the islet of Illaunonearaun. It's a dramatic walk on a wild day, with the Atlantic heaving against the rocks below, and a good alternative to the much busier Cliffs of Moher further north.

Keep well back from the edge, especially in wind. North of town, a separate hike from the golf club leads to Corbally Cliff, Byrne's Cove and George's Head.
