title: Things to Do
type: section
---

Kilkee's pleasures are simple and salty: a swim off the [beach](/europe/ireland/kilkee/kilkee_beach) or in the [Pollock Holes](/europe/ireland/kilkee/pollock_holes), the [cliff walk](/europe/ireland/kilkee/kilkee_cliffs) west of town, and a drive out along the Loop Head peninsula to the [Bridges of Ross](/europe/ireland/kilkee/bridges_of_ross).
