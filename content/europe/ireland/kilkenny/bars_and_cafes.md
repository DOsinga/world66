---
title: "Bars and Cafes"
type: section
---

Kilkenny has a strong pub culture for a city its size. The best pubs are concentrated around John Street, St Kieran's Street, and the bridges over the Nore. [Tynan's Bridge House Bar](/europe/ireland/kilkenny/tynans_bridge_hous) on John's Bridge is the most authentic Victorian pub in the city — a time capsule of original fittings and gas lamps. [Kyteler's Inn](/europe/ireland/kilkenny/kytelersinn) on St Kieran's Street is the oldest and has live traditional music most evenings. [Langton's](/europe/ireland/kilkenny/langtons) on John Street is a Kilkenny institution that pulls in both locals and visitors in equal measure.
