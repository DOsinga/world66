The Watergate Theatre is Kilkenny’s main city theatre, set on Parliament Street within an easy walk of the medieval core. Its programme shifts between drama, comedy, music, community productions, and touring work, so it is worth checking the calendar if you are staying overnight rather than only passing through for the castle.

Kilkenny is often sold as stone streets, pubs, and festivals, but the Watergate shows the city’s everyday cultural side. It is especially useful on a wet evening, when a live performance can feel more local than another round through the same pub circuit.
