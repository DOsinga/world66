---
title: Things to Do
type: section
---

Walk across [Killaloe Bridge](/europe/ireland/killaloe/killaloe_bridge) to Ballina and back, visit St Flannan's Cathedral and the old oratory, then take a cruise onto Lough Derg or head up to [Béal Ború](/europe/ireland/killaloe/brian_borus_fort). Hill walkers can climb [Moylussa](/europe/ireland/killaloe/moylussa).
