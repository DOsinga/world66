Fitzgerald Stadium is the main GAA stadium in County Kerry, with a capacity of around 38,000. It hosts Kerry's home games in the Munster Championship and is one of the larger county grounds in Ireland. If you are in Killarney during the summer and Kerry are playing a championship match, attending a game is an excellent way to experience the passion that Gaelic football inspires in the county.

Tickets for championship matches can be bought through the GAA website or at the gate. The stadium is about a kilometre north of the town centre, a 15-minute walk. Kerry's record in Gaelic football is unmatched in Ireland and the county's supporters are knowledgeable and enthusiastic — even a mundane league match here has atmosphere.
