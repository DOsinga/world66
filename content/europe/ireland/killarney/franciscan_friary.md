Killarneys Franciscan Friary is an active place of worship rather than a preserved ruin, which makes it a useful pause in a town dominated by tours and outdoor excursions. The church has a calm interior and a practical location near the railway station.

It is a short stop, but a worthwhile one if you are walking between the town centre and transport links. The friary adds a quieter civic and religious note to Killarneys otherwise very visitor-driven centre.
