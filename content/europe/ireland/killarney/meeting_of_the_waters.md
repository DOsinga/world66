The Meeting of the Waters is the quiet hinge of the Lakes of Killarney, where the Upper Lake, Muckross Lake, and Lough Leane connect amid woods and old stonework. It is close to Dinis Cottage and the Old Weir Bridge, and the scene is at its best when reached on foot, by bike, or from the water.

This is not a single dramatic lookout so much as a soft, green pause in the national park. Bring time rather than a checklist: the paths, bridge, reflections, and lake traffic make more sense if you let the place unfold slowly.
