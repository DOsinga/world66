A family-run surf school and shop on the promenade at Lahinch, going since 2007. It teaches every level, from first lessons to coaching with video analysis, and rents both soft and hard boards and wetsuits. After your lesson there are free hot showers and private changing rooms on site, which on the west coast of Ireland matters more than it sounds.
