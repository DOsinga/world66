The Connemara Sheep and Wool Centre is part shop, part small museum, and part demonstration space. Its appeal is local rather than grand: sheep are everywhere in this landscape, and the centre gives some context for wool, spinning, weaving, and rural work.

It is a useful rainy-hour stop in Leenane, especially if you want something more rooted than a souvenir shelf. Check opening times outside the main season.
