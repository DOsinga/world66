Macroom Castle is most visible as the gatehouse on the main street. It looks heavily Victorian now, but its bones go back to the 15th century, and the public road still passes through the gate into the old demesne.

Do not expect a full castle visit. The reward is the way the gatehouse frames the town centre, plus the shell of the keep seen from the bridge.
