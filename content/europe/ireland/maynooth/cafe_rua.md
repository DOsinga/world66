A popular daytime café on the main street that caters to both students and visitors. Homemade soups, sandwiches on fresh bread, quiches, and baked goods are the core of the menu. The coffee is good and the room is warm and busy at lunchtime. Closes in the late afternoon, so it is a better choice for breakfast or lunch than for dinner. Cash and card accepted.
