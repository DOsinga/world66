A large, busy bar on the main street that fills up with students on weekend nights. More sports-bar than traditional pub — big screens showing live matches, a long counter, and a younger crowd. Good for watching rugby or football with a pint; not the place for a quiet conversation. The beer garden fills up on summer evenings.
