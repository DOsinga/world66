A student-favourite pub on the edge of the Maynooth University campus, named after the Gothic Revival architect who designed much of St Patrick's College. Dark wood, eclectic décor, and a good selection of Irish craft beers on tap. Busy during university term time, especially on weekday evenings. The name is a reliable conversation-starter if you can work out how to pronounce it.
