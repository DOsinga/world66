A small independent restaurant near the castle end of town that takes its food more seriously than most places in Maynooth. The menu leans toward simple European cooking — good pasta, fresh salads, daily specials built around whatever the market has. Popular with university staff and local professionals at lunchtime. Worth booking in the evening at weekends.
