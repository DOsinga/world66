A reliable pub kitchen on Maynooth's main street serving solid Irish food — beef and Guinness pie, fish and chips, hearty soups. Nothing fancy, but consistent quality and generous portions at reasonable prices. The Sunday carvery draws locals from a wide radius. A good choice if you want a proper sit-down meal without travelling far from the castle.
