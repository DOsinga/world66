---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

Maynooth is a year-round destination given how close it is to Dublin. The campus and castle are accessible in all weathers, though the canal towpath is better in dry conditions. Summer (June–August) brings longer days and reliable enough weather for the walk to Laraghbryan or a bike ride along the canal.

The university term runs from September to May, which means the town is liveliest — and the pubs busiest — during those months. In July and August the student population thins out and the town is quieter, which suits visitors who prefer their heritage tourism without a soundtrack of sports commentary.

The Maynooth Summer Festival, held in July, brings live music and outdoor events to the town centre. The college's formal academic events — conferrings, open days — make the campus worth visiting in late autumn when the Gothic quadrangles are at their most atmospheric.

Avoid planning a visit on days when Maynooth hosts major Gaelic football matches at the university grounds — parking and accommodation become difficult. Check the GAA fixture list if this matters.
