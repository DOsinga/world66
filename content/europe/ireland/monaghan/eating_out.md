---
title: "Eating Out"
type: section
---

Monaghan's food scene is small but better than it has any right to be. **Andy's Bar and Restaurant** on Market Street is the long-running town favourite — solid Irish cooking, big helpings, a properly old-fashioned bar at the front. **The Squealing Pig** does pub food a notch above the usual: good steaks, decent fish, big plates of chips. For coffee and cake, **Coffee Brewz** on Mill Street is the most reliable. For something a bit more ambitious, head out to **Restaurant 19** at Concra Wood Golf Club near Castleblayney, 20 minutes south, which is one of the best restaurants in the border counties.
