Mullingar is the county town of Westmeath, sitting at the heart of Ireland's lakeland midlands — a region of limestone plains, bogland, and dark, fish-rich lakes that attract anglers from across Europe. The town itself is a busy market centre with a working cathedral and an unhurried character that hasn't been touched by tourism. It's not a postcard destination, but it's a decent base for the surrounding countryside.

The main draws are outside the town: Lough Owel and [Lough Ennell](/europe/ireland/mullingar/lough_ennell) offer excellent coarse fishing and sailing, while [Tullynally Castle](/europe/ireland/mullingar/tullynally_castle) — a vast Gothic pile north of the town — opens its gardens to visitors in summer. [Belvedere House](/europe/ireland/mullingar/belvedere_house), a few kilometres south on Lough Ennell, has one of the more unusual features in Ireland: the "Jealous Wall," a fake Gothic ruin built by an 18th-century earl specifically to block the view of his brother's house. Stranger still is the [Hill of Uisneach](/europe/ireland/mullingar/hill_of_uisneach), west of town — the ancient ritual centre of all Ireland, an atmospheric hilltop of earthworks and mythology that sees almost no tourists. In town, the [Cathedral of Christ the King](/europe/ireland/mullingar/cathedral_of_christ_the_king) is worth a look for its scale and its Boris Anrep floor mosaics.
