---
title: When to Go
type: section
---

The Irish Midlands can be visited year-round, but summer (June to August) brings the best weather for lake activities and garden visits. Belvedere House gardens are at their most colourful in June and July; Tullynally's arboretum is spectacular in autumn when the trees turn. Fishing is good from spring through autumn, with coarse fishing permitted year-round on most Westmeath lakes. The Hill of Uisneach's annual Bealtaine fire ceremony draws visitors in early May — book guided tours in advance. Winter is quiet and often rainy, but the landscape around the lakes has an atmospheric stillness in the off-season that rewards those who don't mind grey skies.
