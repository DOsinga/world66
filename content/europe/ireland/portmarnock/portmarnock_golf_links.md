Founded in 1894 on the narrow sandy peninsula between the estuary and the sea, Portmarnock Golf Club is one of the great links courses of Ireland and has hosted the Irish Open many times as well as the 1991 Walker Cup. Its flat, exposed fairways run out along the dunes, fully at the mercy of the wind off the Irish Sea, and the course is regularly ranked among the finest in the world.

It is a private members' club, so casual play requires advance arrangement, but the dramatic coastal setting and its place in golfing history make it a landmark of the Portmarnock peninsula. Keen golfers should enquire well ahead about visitor tee times.
