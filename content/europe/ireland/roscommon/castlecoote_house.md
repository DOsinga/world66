Castlecoote House is a Georgian manor beside the ruins of a 16th-century castle near Roscommon. The house opens for tours in summer, while the grounds have a longer visiting season.

It is a worthwhile countryside stop for visitors interested in Irish country houses, layered estate history, and the remains of older fortified sites.
