---
title: "Eating Out"
type: section
---

For a small village Rosses Point eats well. The two long-running pubs — **Harry's** (also a hotel) and the **Yeats Country Hotel** bar — both serve solid pub food with an emphasis on locally landed fish. **The Driftwood**, near the pier, does seafood platters in a casual setting and is especially popular in summer.

A handful of seasonal cafes and ice-cream kiosks open along the seafront from April to September.
