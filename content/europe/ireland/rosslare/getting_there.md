---
title: Getting There
type: section
---

Rosslare is connected to Dublin and Wexford by the Iarnród Éireann rail service from Rosslare Europort station, with services running several times daily. The journey to Dublin Connolly takes about two and a half to three hours. Wexford town is twenty minutes by rail or road.

By car, the N25 links Rosslare to Wexford and the main road network. Dublin is approximately two hours north on the N11. Bus Éireann runs services to Wexford and further afield, though frequencies are limited compared to the rail service.

For ferry passengers, the port is just south of Rosslare Strand village. Both Stena Line and Irish Ferries operate passenger and car ferries from Rosslare Europort to Wales (Fishguard, Pembroke) and to Cherbourg and Roscoff in France. Booking in advance is strongly recommended in summer.
