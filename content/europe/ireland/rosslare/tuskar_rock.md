Tuskar Rock lies offshore from the Wexford coast, marked by a lighthouse and a long record in Irish maritime history. From Rosslare it is more often a distant sight than a place you visit, but that distance is part of its character.

Watch for it on clear days from the coast or ferry routes. It gives Rosslare's beaches and harbour a wider sea context, reminding you how busy and hazardous this corner of the Irish Sea has always been.
