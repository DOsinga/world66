Ardgillan Castle is an eighteenth-century country house set in wide parkland above the Irish Sea. Guided tours cover the ground floor and kitchens, while the demesne gives you gardens, walking and cycling paths, sea views, and a clear-day look toward the Mourne Mountains.

It is close enough to Skerries to work as a half-day outing, especially if you want a quieter coastal stop than the harbour.
