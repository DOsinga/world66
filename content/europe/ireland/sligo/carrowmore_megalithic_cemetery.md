Carrowmore Megalithic Cemetery is one of the great prehistoric landscapes of western Ireland, with stone circles, dolmens, passage tombs, and views toward Knocknarea. The monuments are low and weathered rather than monumental in the tourist-brochure sense, but their age and density are extraordinary.

The site rewards slow walking and a little imagination. Visit the exhibition first if it is open, then move through the stones with the surrounding hills in mind; Carrowmore makes much more sense as a ritual landscape than as a collection of isolated ruins.
