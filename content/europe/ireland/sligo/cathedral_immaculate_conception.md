The Roman Catholic cathedral of the Diocese of Elphin, an imposing Renaissance-Revival building of 1874 with a richly decorated interior of marble, mosaic, and stained glass. The pinkish facade dominates the southern end of central Sligo.

The interior has been substantially restored and is worth a short visit during the day.
