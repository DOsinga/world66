Creevelea Abbey is a ruined Franciscan friary near Dromahair, close enough to Sligo to work as a quiet side trip. Its broken walls, arches, and riverside setting give it the melancholy charm that Irish monastic ruins do so well.

It is not as famous as Sligo Abbey, but that can make the visit more satisfying. Go with a car or as part of a loop around Lough Gill, Parkes Castle, and the smaller historic sites east of town.
