Glencar Lough sits in the hills north of Sligo and has the soft, moody drama that makes this part of the northwest so good for slow drives. The lake is closely associated with nearby Glencar Waterfall, but the wider setting is the real attraction: steep green slopes, cloud shadows, and quiet water.

It is easy to visit by car from Sligo, especially as part of a loop toward Drumcliffe, Benbulben, or the Leitrim side of the valley. Do not rush it as a waterfall photo stop. The lake is best when you give yourself time to pull over, walk a little, and watch the weather move.
