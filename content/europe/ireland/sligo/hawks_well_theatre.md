Sligo's main theatre, opened in 1982 and named after a 1916 Yeats play. The 340-seat venue programmes a year-round mix of touring drama, music, dance, comedy, and amateur productions, and is the centre of the town's evening cultural life.

Box office and bar are open from late afternoon. Worth checking the listings even on a short visit.
