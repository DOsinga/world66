The Lake Isle of Innisfree is physically small but huge in Sligo's literary imagination. W.B. Yeats turned this little island in Lough Gill into one of the best-known landscapes in Irish poetry, and the shore viewpoint lets you see the modest place behind the famous lines.

Do not expect a big attraction with elaborate interpretation. The value is in the pairing of lake scenery and literature: a quiet stop that makes Yeats Country feel less like branding and more like a real set of hills, water and memory.
