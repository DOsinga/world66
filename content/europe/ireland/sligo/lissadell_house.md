Lissadell House is one of the strongest heritage visits near Sligo, set on the shore of Drumcliff Bay under Ben Bulben. The house is austere Greek Revival rather than cosy country-house picturesque, and that severity gives it a memorable presence against the mountain and Atlantic landscape.

Its associations are unusually rich: childhood home of Constance Markievicz and Eva Gore-Booth, and a place that entered the imagination of W. B. Yeats. The gardens and coastal setting make it more than an indoor tour, so allow time to walk the grounds if they are open.
