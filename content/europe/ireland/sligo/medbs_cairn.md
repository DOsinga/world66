title: Medb's Cairn
type: poi
latitude: 54.2589
longitude: -8.5689
tags: [things_to_do, sight]

The vast unexcavated cairn on the summit of Knocknarea, traditionally identified as the burial of the legendary Connacht queen Maeve. The mound is around ten metres high and 55 metres across at the base, made of loose stones built up over the older Neolithic structure beneath.

Walkers are asked not to climb on the cairn. Combine with the ascent of Knocknarea.
