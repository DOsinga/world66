Parke's Castle stands on the shore of Lough Gill, a compact fortified house that explains a great deal about plantation-era power in this part of Ireland. The restored courtyard, stone walls, and lake setting make it more atmospheric than its modest size suggests.

It is a straightforward excursion from Sligo, especially if you are already tracing Yeats country around Lough Gill. The visit is strongest when combined with the lake scenery: the castle gives the landscape a human story of land, control, and uneasy settlement.
