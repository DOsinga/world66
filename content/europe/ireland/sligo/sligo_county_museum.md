A small free museum in the former manse next to the Yeats Memorial Building, with displays on the archaeology, folklore, and history of County Sligo. The Yeats room is the highlight — manuscripts, letters, photographs, and the poet's Nobel Prize medal.

Worth about an hour. Combine with a visit to the abbey and the Yeats Memorial Building on the same morning.
