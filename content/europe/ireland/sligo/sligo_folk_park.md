Sligo Folk Park is a small open-air museum built around a Victorian farmhouse and rural craft displays. It is aimed partly at children, but the old rooms, tools, and farm buildings give a useful glimpse of everyday life in the west of Ireland before modern comforts arrived.

It is a modest stop rather than a headline sight, best if you are already exploring south County Sligo or travelling with children who need something hands-on.
