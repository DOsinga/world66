Streedagh Beach is a long exposed strand north of Sligo, backed by dunes and framed by views toward Benbulben. It is one of the county's strongest coastal outings, with room for walking, surf-watching, and big Atlantic skies.

The beach also has historic resonance: ships from the Spanish Armada were wrecked off this coast in 1588. Even without that story, Streedagh is worth the short trip from Sligo for its scale, scenery, and sense of open coast.
