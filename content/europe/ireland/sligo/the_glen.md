A narrow, almost invisible glen running along the eastern side of Knocknarea, only known to locals until recently. The walls of the gully rise sheer for some thirty metres, the floor is wet underfoot, and the contrast with the open hill above is total.

The entry is unmarked and the floor can be slippery — best done with someone who knows the way, or follow one of the guided walks that occasionally run from Strandhill. Boots essential.
