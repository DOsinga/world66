title: Things to Do
type: section
---

The Burren rewards walking more than driving. Its headline sights are prehistoric and medieval: [Poulnabrone Dolmen](/europe/ireland/the_burren/poulnabrone_dolmen), [Corcomroe Abbey](/europe/ireland/the_burren/corcomroe_abbey) and the stone forts. [Aillwee Cave](/europe/ireland/the_burren/aillwee_cave) shows you what lies under the limestone, and the waymarked loops in [Burren National Park](/europe/ireland/the_burren/burren_national_park) take you across it.
