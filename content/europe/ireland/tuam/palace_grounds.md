Palace Grounds is the small parkland north of Tuam's centre. It is useful less as a destination than as a breathing space after the churches, with lawns and paths where the town opens out a little.

For a short visit, pair it with the nearby centre rather than treating it as a separate outing. Tuam's interest is history first; this is the easy place to pause while you put that history together.
