Valentia Island lies off the tip of the Iveragh Peninsula in County Kerry, one of the most westerly points in Ireland. It's about 11 kilometres long and 3 wide, green, hilly and quiet, with 658 people living mainly in the little harbour village of Knightstown at its eastern end and in Chapeltown in the middle. The name has nothing to do with Spain: it's an anglicised form of the Irish name for the harbour mouth. In Irish the island is Dairbhre, "oak isle".

This small island has played an outsized part in history. In 1866, after years of failed attempts, a transatlantic telegraph cable was successfully laid from Foilhommerum Bay on Valentia to Heart's Content in Newfoundland. Messages that had taken two weeks by mail boat now crossed in minutes, and cables operated from the island for a hundred years. On the north coast, a Swiss geology student found something far older in 1992: the [Tetrapod Trackway](/europe/ireland/valentia_island/tetrapod_trackway), footprints of one of the first four-legged animals to walk on land, around 385 million years ago.

The best scenery is along the north side. The road climbs [Geokaun Mountain](/europe/ireland/valentia_island/geokaun_mountain) to the 180-metre Fogher Cliffs, and the old [slate quarry](/europe/ireland/valentia_island/valentia_slate_quarry) nearby once roofed the Houses of Parliament in London. At the western end, a path climbs to the signal tower on [Bray Head](/europe/ireland/valentia_island/bray_head) with views out to the Skelligs. Glanleam House in the north-east has subtropical gardens, planted from the 1830s, with tree ferns and plants from South America and New Zealand that thrive in the island's frost-free climate.

The island is joined to the mainland by a bridge at Portmagee, the small fishing village that is the main departure point for boats to [Skellig Michael](/europe/ireland/skelligs/skellig_michael) and the [Skelligs](/europe/ireland/skelligs). The [Skellig Experience](/europe/ireland/valentia_island/skellig_experience) visitor centre is just across that bridge, on the island side. From April to October a small car ferry also runs to Knightstown from Reenard Point near Cahersiveen, a five-minute crossing. Valentia is a short detour off the [Ring of Kerry](/europe/ireland/ringofkerry) road, but it deserves at least a night.
