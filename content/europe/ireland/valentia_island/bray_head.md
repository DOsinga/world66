Bray Head is the windswept south-western tip of Valentia Island, reached by a footpath that starts west of the Skellig Experience centre and climbs to an old signal tower on the headland.

The views from the top reach out to the Skelligs on a clear day and back along the cliffs of the Iveragh Peninsula. There's no shelter on the headland, so dress for Atlantic wind.
