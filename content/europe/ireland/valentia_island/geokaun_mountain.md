Geokaun is the highest mountain on Valentia, and its north face drops to the Fogher Cliffs, 180 metres above the Atlantic. A series of viewpoints on its slopes, including Shepherd's View, Miners View and a viewing deck near the top, look out over Dingle Bay, the mountains of the Iveragh Peninsula and the island itself.

It's the single best view on the island, and on a clear evening it's hard to beat anywhere in Kerry.
