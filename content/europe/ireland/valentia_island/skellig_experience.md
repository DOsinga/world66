The Skellig Experience is a visitor centre on Valentia Island, just across the bridge from Portmagee, with exhibitions on the history, architecture and wildlife of the Skellig islands and their early Christian monastery. For anyone who can't land on Skellig Michael, because of the weather, the waiting lists or the 600 steep steps, it is the next best thing.

The centre also runs cruises around the Skelligs that don't land. It's open daily from March to November, and adult admission was €6 in 2026.
