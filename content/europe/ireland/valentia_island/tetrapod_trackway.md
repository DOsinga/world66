On the rocky north shore of Valentia at Dohilla is one of the oldest records of a vertebrate walking on land: a trail of footprints left in damp sand by a primitive four-legged animal about 385 million years ago, when this part of Ireland lay near the equator in a river basin. The prints were covered by silt and sand and slowly turned to stone.

The longest trackway has some 145 prints in two parallel rows, left and right feet. They aren't dramatic to look at, but the thought of what made them is. Take care on the shore rocks, which are slippery when wet.
