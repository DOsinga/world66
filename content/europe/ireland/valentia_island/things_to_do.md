---
title: Things to Do
type: section
---

Valentia is best explored slowly, by car or bike: drive up [Geokaun Mountain](/europe/ireland/valentia_island/geokaun_mountain), look for the [Tetrapod Trackway](/europe/ireland/valentia_island/tetrapod_trackway) on the north shore, walk out to [Bray Head](/europe/ireland/valentia_island/bray_head), and learn about the Skelligs at the [Skellig Experience](/europe/ireland/valentia_island/skellig_experience) before you take the boat from Portmagee.
