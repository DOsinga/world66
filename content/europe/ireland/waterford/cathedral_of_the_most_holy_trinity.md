The Cathedral of the Most Holy Trinity is Waterford's Catholic cathedral and an important piece of the city's eighteenth-century religious architecture. Its classical interior and central location make it an easy addition to walks through the historic core.

It is quieter than Waterford's Viking and medieval attractions, but it helps round out the city's layered story. Visitors should treat it as an active church and check access around services.
