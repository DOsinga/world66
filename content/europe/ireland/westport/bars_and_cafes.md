---
title: Bars and Cafes
type: section
---

Westport has a good concentration of pubs for its size, and several of them have live traditional music sessions in the evenings. **Matt Molloy's** on Bridge Street is the most famous — it's owned by the Chieftains flute player of the same name and is reliable for trad sessions, though it fills quickly with tourists in summer. For a slightly calmer atmosphere, **The Towers** on the Quay road is popular with locals. **An Bealach** near the Octagon is quieter and more traditional in character.

Coffee culture has arrived in Westport in recent years. A handful of good independent cafes operate in the town centre; the ones along James Street and near the Octagon generally do breakfast and lunch well.
