---
title: When to Go
type: section
---

The west of Ireland is famously wet, and Westport is no exception. Rain is possible at any time of year, and the mountain weather is unpredictable. That said, the summer months from June through August are the driest and brightest, with long evenings and temperatures in the mid-teens to low twenties Celsius.

Reek Sunday — the last Sunday in July — brings enormous crowds of pilgrims to Croagh Patrick and fills the town's accommodation. If you want to experience the pilgrimage, book well in advance. If you want to climb the mountain in peace, avoid that weekend.

Spring and early autumn can be excellent: fewer visitors, lower prices, and the landscape at its most dramatic. The Great Western Greenway cycling trail is best in May or September when the heather is in colour and the midges are less aggressive.

Winter is quiet but the pubs are warm and the scenery in storms is spectacular, if you don't mind being indoors when the Atlantic does its worst.
