Wexford Town sits near the mouth of the River Slaney, where a shallow harbour, narrow medieval lanes, and a surprisingly serious opera tradition meet. It is not as polished as Kilkenny or as busy as Cork, but it has a lived-in southeast character and one of Ireland's best cultural surprises.

The old town still follows a tight street pattern shaped by its Viking and Norman past. [Selskar Abbey](/europe/ireland/wexford_town/selskar_abbey), [St Iberius' Church](/europe/ireland/wexford_town/st_iberius_church), the quay, and the lanes around South Main Street make the best short walk. The waterfront is more workaday than postcard-pretty, but it gives the town air and a sense of its maritime history.

Wexford changes character during the [Wexford Festival Opera](/europe/ireland/wexford_town/wexford_festival_opera), when a quiet county town suddenly fills with opera-goers, fringe performances, and booked-out restaurants. Outside festival time, the [Irish National Heritage Park](/europe/ireland/wexford_town/irish_national_heritage_park) at Ferrycarrig is the strongest reason to linger.

Use Wexford Town as a base for the southeast corner: [Rosslare](/europe/ireland/rosslare) and its ferries are close, Curracloe's beaches are north of town, and the coast toward Hook Head makes a fine loop if you have a car.
