title: Getting There
type: section
---

Wexford railway station is on the Dublin-Rosslare line, with services that are scenic rather than fast. Buses are often more useful, linking Wexford with Dublin, Waterford, Cork, and [Rosslare](/europe/ireland/rosslare). Drivers reach the town from Dublin by the M11/N11 and N25; allow extra time on opera festival weekends.
