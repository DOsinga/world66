The Irish National Heritage Park, west of town at Ferrycarrig, is Wexford's strongest family-friendly sight. It uses reconstructed dwellings, woodland paths, and guided interpretation to walk visitors through thousands of years of Irish settlement.

It is especially good if Irish prehistory normally feels abstract: crannogs, ringforts, early Christian sites, and Viking material become physical and easy to grasp. Allow at least a couple of hours, and more if you join a tour.
