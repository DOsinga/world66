The National Opera House is Wexford's cultural anchor, built on the site of the old Theatre Royal and home to the Wexford Festival Opera. The building is tucked into the town centre rather than standing aloof, which suits Wexford: the festival feels woven into the streets.

Outside festival season, check the programme for concerts, theatre, and touring performances. During the festival, book well ahead and expect the whole town to revolve around curtain times, restaurant sittings, and post-show talk.
