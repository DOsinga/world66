St Iberius' Church stands on North Main Street and is one of the most useful stops for understanding Wexford's layered religious and civic history. Its restrained exterior is easy to miss among the shops, but that is part of its charm.

Step inside if it is open. The church is not a blockbuster sight, but it offers a quiet pause in the centre of town and helps explain how Wexford's old urban fabric survived in fragments rather than grand set pieces.
