type: section
---

Wexford's sights are split between the compact town centre and a few excellent nearby stops. Start with [Selskar Abbey](/europe/ireland/wexford_town/selskar_abbey), [St Iberius' Church](/europe/ireland/wexford_town/st_iberius_church), the quay, and the [National Opera House](/europe/ireland/wexford_town/national_opera_house). If you have a car or taxi budget, add the [Irish National Heritage Park](/europe/ireland/wexford_town/irish_national_heritage_park) and [Johnstown Castle](/europe/ireland/wexford_town/johnstown_castle).
