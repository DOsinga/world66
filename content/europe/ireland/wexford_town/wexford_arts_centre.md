Wexford Arts Centre occupies a handsome building near the town centre and gives Wexford a year-round cultural life beyond the opera festival. Exhibitions, theatre, music, and community arts events rotate through the programme.

It is worth checking what is on, especially if the weather turns or you are in town outside festival season. The centre is small enough to visit casually, but it keeps Wexford from feeling like a one-event town.
