---
title: When to Go
type: section
---

Youghal is best visited from May to September, when the beach season is in full swing and the town is alive with day trippers. July and August are the busiest months, when Irish families descend on Claycastle Beach and the restaurants fill up at weekends. The weather is unpredictable year-round — pack for rain whatever the season — but the summer months offer the best chance of a warm afternoon on the sand.

Spring and autumn are excellent for the historic town itself: the Clock Gate Tower and St Mary's Church are less crowded, and the light on the Blackwater estuary is particularly good in October. Winter is quiet to the point of sleepy, with some businesses closing or cutting hours, but the medieval core has a stark, atmospheric quality in the off-season.
