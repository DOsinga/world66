Youghal Beach stretches below the old town, giving Youghal a second identity beyond walls, gates, and harbour history. The sand is broad and easy to reach, which makes it one of the simplest beach stops on this part of the Cork coast.

It is best in good weather, but even a grey walk has its uses after exploring the town centre. Treat it as a strand for swimming when conditions allow, strolling when they do not, and understanding why Youghal has long been both a historic port and a seaside place.
