Douglas is the capital of the Isle of Man, a self-governing Crown dependency in the Irish Sea between England and Ireland. The town curves around a wide bay, with a Victorian promenade running the length of the seafront and the [horse-drawn trams](/europe/isleofman/douglas/horse_trams) that have been clopping along it since 1876 — the oldest surviving horse-drawn tram service in the world. The [Tower of Refuge](/europe/isleofman/douglas/tower_of_refuge), a small gothic folly on a reef in the bay, is visible from anywhere along the front.

The island is best known for the [TT Motorcycle Races](/europe/isleofman/douglas/tt_motorcycle_races) each June, when Douglas transforms from a quiet offshore town into a petrolhead pilgrimage site. Outside race season, the appeal is quieter — the [Manx Museum](/europe/isleofman/douglas/manx_museum) tells the island's history well, the [Gaiety Theatre](/europe/isleofman/douglas/gaiety_theatre) is one of the finest intact Victorian theatres in Britain, and the island's beautiful glens and coastline are easy to reach. The pace of life here feels a few decades behind the mainland in the best possible way.
