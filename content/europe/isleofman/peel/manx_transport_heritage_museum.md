The Manx Transport Heritage Museum is a small Peel museum covering the island's road, rail, and maritime transport history. Its best-known exhibit is the Peel P50, the tiny 1960s microcar once marketed as the world's smallest road-legal car.

The museum is compact, so it suits a short stop before or after the House of Manannan, Peel Castle, and the harbour.
