The Apennine Botanical Garden near Pescasseroli is a small but useful stop for understanding the park's plant life. It focuses on native mountain species rather than ornamental display, so it works best for curious walkers and families who want context before heading onto trails.

Pair it with the visitor information in Pescasseroli or a gentle walk nearby. It is not the park's headline sight, but it helps put names to the flowers, trees, and habitats you will see outside.
