The park's strongest sights are natural rather than monumental. Start with [Val Fondillo](/europe/italy/abruzzo/abruzzonationalpark/val_fondillo) or [La Camosciara](/europe/italy/abruzzo/abruzzonationalpark/la_camosciara), then add [Lake Barrea](/europe/italy/abruzzo/abruzzonationalpark/lake_barrea), the botanical garden at [Pescasseroli](/europe/italy/abruzzo/pescasseroli), or the higher country around the [Monti della Meta](/europe/italy/abruzzo/abruzzonationalpark/monti_della_meta). Wildlife watching is best with local guides and realistic expectations.
