Val Fondillo, near [Opi](/europe/italy/abruzzo/opi), is one of the park's most rewarding valleys for first-time visitors. The trail follows a clear mountain stream through meadow and beech forest, with the ridges rising steadily on either side.

It is popular because it is beautiful without being punishing. Start early for quiet, carry water, and resist the temptation to treat it like a city park; this is still a protected mountain environment.
