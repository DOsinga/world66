---
title: Things to Do
type: section
---

L'Aquila's historic centre was badly damaged in the 2009 earthquake, but significant parts are now restored or in restoration, and the city's extraordinary heritage — medieval churches, the 16th-century Spanish fortress, the famous 99 fountains — is gradually returning to view. The Gran Sasso massif right on the doorstep gives the city an alpine dimension that few Italian regional capitals can match.

Start in **Piazza del Duomo** for the morning market and the cathedral, then walk five minutes north to the **Forte Spagnolo** for the National Museum of Abruzzo and the bastion walls. The **Basilica di Santa Maria di Collemaggio**, with its pink-and-white stone facade, sits on the southern edge of town and is the city's most important medieval church. Half a day above town on the **Gran Sasso** funivia — and an afternoon at the dramatic ruined castle of **Rocca Calascio**, an hour south — round out a two-day visit.
