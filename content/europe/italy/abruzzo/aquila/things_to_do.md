L'Aquila's historic centre was badly damaged in the 2009 earthquake, but significant parts are now restored or in restoration, and the city's extraordinary heritage — medieval churches, the 16th-century Spanish fortress, the famous 99 fountains — is gradually returning to view. The Gran Sasso massif right on the doorstep gives the city an alpine dimension that few Italian regional capitals can match.
