---

Bominaco's entire reason to visit is its two medieval churches. The [Oratorio di San Pellegrino](/europe/italy/abruzzo/bominaco/oratorio_di_san_pellegrino) contains one of the finest complete fresco cycles in central Italy; [Santa Maria Assunta](/europe/italy/abruzzo/bominaco/santa_maria_assunta) is a Romanesque masterpiece of austere beauty. Both require access through the local custodian — ask at the village or call ahead. The ruined castle above the village is also worth a look for the views over the Abruzzo uplands.
