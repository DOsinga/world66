---
title: Getting There
type: section
---

A car is the easiest way to reach Gran Sasso. From [L'Aquila](/europe/italy/abruzzo/aquila), drive to Fonte Cerreto for the cable car toward Campo Imperatore, or continue by road when open. From [Teramo](/europe/italy/abruzzo/teramo), Prati di Tivo is the practical access point. Public transport exists in pieces but is rarely convenient for mountain days, especially outside summer.
