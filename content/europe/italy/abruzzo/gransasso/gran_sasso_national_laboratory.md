The Gran Sasso National Laboratory is one of the world's major underground physics laboratories, built beneath the massif beside the motorway tunnel. Most travellers will not visit the working labs, but knowing they are there adds a strange modern layer to the mountain.

If public open days or guided visits are available, they are worth considering. Otherwise treat it as a story attached to the landscape rather than a standard attraction.
