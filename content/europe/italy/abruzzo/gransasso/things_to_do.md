Gran Sasso is about high-country walking, views, and mountain roads. The headline route is toward [Corno Grande](/europe/italy/abruzzo/gransasso/corno_grande), usually from [Campo Imperatore](/europe/italy/abruzzo/gransasso/campo_imperatore), while [Prati di Tivo](/europe/italy/abruzzo/gransasso/prati_di_tivo) gives access from the Teramo side. Non-hikers can still enjoy the plateau drive, the botanical garden, and the strange science-and-war stories beneath the mountain.
