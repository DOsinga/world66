Caramanico Terme is one of the classic bases for the northern Majella, a spa town with access to the Orfento valley and several walking routes. It has enough services to be useful without losing its mountain-town character.

Use it as a practical base rather than just a quick stop. The town gives you accommodation, food, park information, and a softer landing after a day in the ravines and forest.
