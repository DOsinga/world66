Montesilvano is the northern extension of the [Pescara](/europe/italy/abruzzo/pescara) beach strip: broad Adriatic sand, long rows of summer lidos, apartment blocks, family hotels, and the flat cycling-friendly coast road. It is not the prettiest town in Abruzzo, but it is practical, sunny, and very easy to use.

The seafront is the reason to come. [Montesilvano Beach](/europe/italy/abruzzo/montesilvano/montesilvano_beach) is wide and shallow, good for families and for anyone who wants the Adriatic without Pescara's busiest central blocks. South of the main resort area, the [Pineta di Santa Filomena](/europe/italy/abruzzo/montesilvano/pineta_di_santa_filomena) gives a welcome strip of shade.

There is a second Montesilvano inland: [Montesilvano Colle](/europe/italy/abruzzo/montesilvano/montesilvano_colle), the older hill settlement above the coast. It is worth the detour at sunset, when the view takes in the beach, the Pescara plain, and the mountains behind Abruzzo.
