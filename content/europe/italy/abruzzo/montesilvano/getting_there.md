---
title: Getting There
type: section
---

Montesilvano is immediately north of Pescara and is connected by local trains, buses, and the coastal road. Pescara airport is the nearest airport, and Pescara Centrale is the main rail hub for longer-distance connections.

If you are staying in Pescara, Montesilvano is close enough for a beach walk, bike ride, or short bus trip. Drivers can use the A14 motorway and the local coastal exits.
