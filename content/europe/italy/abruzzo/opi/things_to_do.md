---
title: Things to Do
type: section
---

Opi is not a place you visit for a long list of sights. It is a place where the setting is the attraction: a stone village on a rocky spur, mountains in every direction, and some of the best hiking in central Italy starting right at your doorstep. The Val Fondillo trail system, the village churches, and a small but worthwhile museum round out a day or two here nicely.
