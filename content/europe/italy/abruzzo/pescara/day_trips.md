Pescara is the best-connected city in Abruzzo and a practical base for the region. The medieval hill town of [Chieti](/europe/italy/abruzzo/chieti) is 15 km inland. [Sulmona](/europe/italy/abruzzo/sulmona), famous for its confetti (sugar-coated almonds) and set in a mountain basin, takes about an hour by road. The [Gran Sasso](/europe/italy/abruzzo/gransasso) massif and [L'Aquila](/europe/italy/abruzzo/aquila), rebuilt after its 2009 earthquake, are roughly an hour by car. [Pescasseroli](/europe/italy/abruzzo/pescasseroli) in the heart of the national park is about 1.5 hours.
