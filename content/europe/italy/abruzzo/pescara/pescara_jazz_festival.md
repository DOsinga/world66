Pescara Jazz is an international jazz festival held every July, typically at the Stadio Adriatico or outdoor venues around the city. It has been running since 1969 and is one of the oldest and most respected jazz festivals in Italy, with a track record of booking major international names alongside Italian artists and young performers.

The festival runs over several nights and is priced accessibly — this is not an elite event. It draws visitors from across Abruzzo and beyond, and July in Pescara coincides with peak beach season, so the atmosphere in the evenings is festive. If your trip coincides with the festival dates, it is worth planning around.
