---
title: Things to Do
type: section
---

Pescasseroli sits inside Abruzzo, Lazio and Molise National Park, and almost everything worth doing here is connected to the natural world. The park authority office in Piazza Sant'Antonio is the right first stop — pick up a trail map, ask about recent bear and wolf sightings, and sign up for a guided excursion if you want the best chance of seeing wildlife. The botanical garden and the park's natural history museum are good for orientation before heading into the backcountry.
