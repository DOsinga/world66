---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

Pineto is a summer destination, busy from mid-June to the end of August when Italian families fill the beach and the town feels alive. July and August are the peak months — crowded, lively, and warm. May, June, and September are pleasant for walking and cycling but the beach infrastructure is only partly open.

Outside summer, Pineto is very quiet. The town is on the Adriatic coast, which means cooler, windier conditions in autumn and winter than the Tyrrhenian side. The Gran Sasso massif is visible from the beach on clear days — a dramatic backdrop.
