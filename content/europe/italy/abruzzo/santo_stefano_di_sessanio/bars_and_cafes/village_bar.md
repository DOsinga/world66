The village bar is small and unhurried -- coffee in the morning, local wine and digestivi in the evening. It is as much a social hub as a business, and the handful of permanent residents tend to drift through at predictable hours. A good place to sit quietly, practise a few words of Italian, and watch village life at its most unperformative pace.
