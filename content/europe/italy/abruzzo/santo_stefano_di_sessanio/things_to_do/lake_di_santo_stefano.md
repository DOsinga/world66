---
title: "Lake di Santo Stefano"
type: poi
latitude: 42.338
longitude: 13.641
---

A small artificial lake sits in the valley below the village, a short walk down from the old centre. It makes for a pleasant evening stroll -- the reflections of the hillside and surrounding ridges are calm and the light is good in the late afternoon. In summer the lake is popular for swimming, and the path around it is easy and flat, a welcome contrast to the steep lanes of the village above.
