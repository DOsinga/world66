---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

May and June are ideal: wildflowers cover the surrounding hillsides, the light is clear, temperatures are comfortable for hiking, and the village is not yet busy. September and October are equally good -- the autumn colours come in on the high slopes, the air sharpens, and Italian summer visitors have gone home.

July and August are warm and pleasant but bring the most visitors, particularly Italian families from the lowland cities escaping the heat. The village handles crowds reasonably well given its size, but accommodation fills up and needs booking ahead.

Winter brings snow to the Gran Sasso and turns the village spectacular -- the pale stone against a white landscape is worth the journey. But it is quieter, some facilities close, and the mountain roads require care. Campo Imperatore becomes a ski area; the village itself is best in winter for those who enjoy deep quiet and the occasional dramatic storm.

The hiking season runs from May through October. The Corno Grande and the higher Gran Sasso routes should not be attempted before the snow clears, usually late May or early June.
