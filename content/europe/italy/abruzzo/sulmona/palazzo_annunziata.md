The Palazzo dell'Annunziata is the architectural showpiece of Sulmona, a long stone facade on the Corso Ovidio whose Gothic windows, Renaissance reliefs and statues form one of the most photographed compositions in Abruzzo. The building has housed a pilgrim hospital, a confraternity, and now hosts the Civic Museum and the Museum of Sacred Art.

The collections inside cover Roman-era finds from Sulmona and its surroundings, including Republican-era sculptures, mosaics, and inscriptions. The adjoining Baroque church of the Santissima Annunziata is open for occasional services and concerts. Opening hours can be limited — check ahead.
