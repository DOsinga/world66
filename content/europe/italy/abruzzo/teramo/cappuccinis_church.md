Cappuccini's Church is one of Teramo's older religious buildings, with origins traditionally traced to the early medieval period and later rebuilding visible in the brick and stone facade. The surviving right aisle and small chapels preserve traces of later medieval and 16th-century work.

Inside, look for the inlaid tabernacle, 18th-century wooden altars by Giovanni Palombieri and his school, and paintings from the 18th century. It is a compact stop for visitors already exploring Teramo's Roman theatre and cathedral area.
