Vasto is a hilltop town on the Adriatic coast of Abruzzo, perched above a long stretch of sandy beach and a small but pretty marina. The old town up top has a medieval [castle](/europe/italy/abruzzo/vasto/castello_caldoresco), a [cathedral](/europe/italy/abruzzo/vasto/vasto_cathedral), and narrow streets with views out over the sea — it feels a world away from the beach resort below.

The main draw is the coastline. Vasto Marina has a broad sandy beach popular with Italian families in summer, while the rocky coves and [trabocchi](/europe/italy/abruzzo/vasto/costa_dei_trabocchi) (traditional wooden fishing platforms on stilts) along the coast south toward San Salvo are worth exploring. The town is also a good base for the wild interior of Abruzzo, with the Majella and Gran Sasso national parks within reach.
