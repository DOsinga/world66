---
title: Getting There
type: section
---

Vasto sits on the A14 motorway, the main Adriatic autostrada from Bologna down to Bari. The exit is **Vasto Sud** for the marina and **Vasto Nord** for the centro storico. Driving times: Pescara 90 minutes north, Rome around three hours via the A24/A14, Naples three hours south via the A14/A16.

The train station, **Vasto–San Salvo**, is on the main Pescara–Foggia line and is served by Frecciabianca and regional trains; it is 8 km south of the town, so plan on a taxi or local bus. Pescara airport (Abruzzo Airport) is the nearest, with low-cost connections to a handful of European cities.
