---
title: When to Go
type: section
---

**Summer (June–September)** is high season — the beach fills up with Italian families, the temperature sits comfortably in the high 20s and low 30s, and the centro storico has its evening passeggiata going every night. August is the busiest month and the most expensive; the marina lidos are full and restaurant reservations are essential.

**Late spring (May, early June)** and **early autumn (September, early October)** are the best windows: warm enough to swim, quiet enough to find a table.

**Winter** is cool, often windy off the Adriatic, and most of the marina is closed; only the centro storico has year-round life.
