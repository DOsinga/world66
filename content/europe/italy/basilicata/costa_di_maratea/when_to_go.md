---
title: When to Go
type: section
---

May, June, and September are the best months — the sea is warm enough for comfortable swimming, the coast is not yet at full capacity, and the macchia is lush and fragrant. July and August are peak season: the roads are busy, the coves crowded by midday, and accommodation is at a premium. The weather is reliably hot and dry.

Outside summer, the coast is strikingly empty and the hiking is superb. Autumn (October–November) brings mild temperatures and occasional storms that make the sea dramatic. Winter is quiet to the point of isolation — most restaurants and hotels close from November to Easter. If you want solitude and the landscape to yourself, spring (April–May) delivers the macchia in full flower, migrating birds on the cliffs, and very few other visitors.
