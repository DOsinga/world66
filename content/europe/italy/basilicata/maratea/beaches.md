---
title: "Beaches"
type: section
---

Maratea's beaches are mostly small, rocky or pebbly coves rather than long stretches of sand — but the water is among the clearest on the Italian mainland, and the coastline is dramatic. The two main beaches are at [Acquafredda](/europe/italy/basilicata/costa_di_maratea/acquafredda), about 10 km north of the centre, and Marina di Maratea, 5 km south along the coastal road. Both have sun-lounger concessions and a handful of beach bars in summer.

Smaller, harder-to-reach coves dot the cliffs between — Cala Jannita with its rare black sand, Spiaggia di Macarro, and the secluded Spiaggia di Filocaio. Many are accessible only by boat or by long stairs down from the road; the best way to see them properly is on a boat trip from Maratea port. The sea is usually swimmable from mid-May through October.
