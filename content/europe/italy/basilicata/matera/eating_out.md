---
title: "Eating Out"
type: section
---

Matera's cooking belongs to the peasant tradition of Basilicata — direct, filling, and built around a few excellent ingredients. Bread is serious here: *pane di Matera*, made from durum wheat semolina and baked in a high dome, has IGP status. Pasta comes as *lagane* (wide flat strips) with chickpeas or with *crusco* peppers — sweet dried red peppers fried in oil until they crisp, a Basilicata speciality worth seeking out. Lamb and pork appear on most menus; the local *pecorino* cheese is sharp and good.

Most restaurants in the Sassi occupy cave rooms and charge accordingly. The quality is generally higher in the Sasso Caveoso; the Sasso Barisano has more tourist traps. Booking is essential in summer.
