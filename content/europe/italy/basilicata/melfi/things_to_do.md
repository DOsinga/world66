title: Things to Do
type: section
---

Melfi is a small medieval town with a disproportionately rich history, dominated by a Norman castle that has survived nearly a thousand years. The main sights are the castle and its museum, the old town below it, and the cathedral — all walkable in a half-day. The broader appeal is the landscape: volcanic Monte Vulture and the Aglianico wine country that surrounds it.
