Metaponto is essentially one big archaeological site. The unmissable pair is the [Parco Archeologico](/europe/italy/basilicata/metaponto/parco_archeologico) (theatre, temples, agora) and the [National Archaeological Museum](/europe/italy/basilicata/metaponto/museo_archeologico) next door — see them together, museum first to put the ruins in context. A few kilometres north, the [Tavole Palatine](/europe/italy/basilicata/metaponto/tavole_palatine) stand alone in a field: fifteen tall Doric columns of a 6th-century temple of Hera, one of the best-preserved Greek monuments in Italy. The beach at [Lido di Metaponto](/europe/italy/basilicata/metaponto/lido_di_metaponto) is the modern resort half of town, useful in summer.
