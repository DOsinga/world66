Montescaglioso sits within easy reach of the most rewarding sights in eastern Basilicata. [Matera](/europe/italy/basilicata/matera) and its sassi are the obvious headline trip. [Metaponto](/europe/italy/basilicata/metaponto) on the Ionian coast preserves the temple ruins and museum of a major Magna Graecia colony. [Miglionico](/europe/italy/basilicata/miglionico) further west has a Norman castle and a famous altarpiece by Cima da Conegliano.
