The Volo dell'Angelo is the famous high-adrenaline link between Pietrapertosa and Castelmezzano. Riders are harnessed to a steel cable and sent across the valley, with the Lucanian Dolomites dropping away below.

It runs seasonally and weather matters, so check before building a day around it. Even if you skip the flight, the walking route between the two villages is worth considering.
