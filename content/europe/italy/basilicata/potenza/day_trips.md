Potenza's real value is as a base for excursions into Basilicata. The cave city of [Matera](/europe/italy/basilicata/matera) (about 100 km east) is the obvious target — UNESCO-listed and one of the most extraordinary places in Italy. The Greek temples at **Metaponto** lie an hour south on the Ionian coast. The hilltop villages of the **Lucan Apennines** — Pietrapertosa and Castelmezzano, joined by a flying-fox zipline, are 40 minutes east. And the wild **Pollino National Park**, Italy's largest, begins an hour south of the city.
