Potenza's food is Basilicata's food — assertive, peasant, pork-and-pasta-driven. Look for **pasta con peperoni cruschi** (crispy dried sweet peppers), **lagane e ceci** (wide noodles with chickpeas), **podolico** beef from the high pastures, and the smoked **canestrato** sheep's cheese. The local salume **lucanica** is the namesake of all modern sausage.

Restaurants are clustered along and just off Via Pretoria. A few that consistently get it right:

- **Antica Osteria Marconi** — modern Basilicatese cooking by chef Pietro Sinisi, refined but rooted. Tasting menu around 60 euros. Closed Sunday.
- **Taverna Oraziana** — traditional, cheaper, hearty. Excellent house-made orecchiette.
- **Bar Renoir** — for an aperitivo with serious cicchetti before dinner.
