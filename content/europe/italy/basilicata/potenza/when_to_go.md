---
title: When to Go
type: section
---

Potenza's altitude — over 800 metres — gives it a climate closer to a small alpine town than to the typical southern Italian city. Winter is genuinely cold; snow is common from December to March and the streets can ice over.

Spring and autumn are mild and lovely; summer is warm (highs in the high 20s) but pleasantly cool at night when the rest of southern Italy is sweltering. The big local event is the **Parata dei Turchi** on 29 May, a colourful pageant commemorating a 16th-century Saracen raid that the city's patron saint reputedly saw off. Worth timing a visit around.
