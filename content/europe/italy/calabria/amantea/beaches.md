---
title: "Beaches"
type: section
---

Amantea has a long sandy beach running south from the centre of town for several kilometres. The closer stretches near the harbour have lidos with sun loungers, parasols, and beach bars for hire; further south, beyond the river mouth, the beach grows wider and more open, mostly free public sand mixed with the occasional pebble.

The water shelves gently — fine for kids — and the colour is the bright clear blue of the Tyrrhenian. North of town, the coast is more dramatic, with rocky coves accessible from the old SS18 highway. The [Coreca Reefs](/europe/italy/calabria/amantea/coreca_reefs) just south are the best snorkelling option in the area. Sea conditions are calmest from June to September; outside the summer season the wind off the Tyrrhenian can be strong.
