---
title: "Eating Out"
type: section
---

Calabrian food is one of the more distinctive cuisines of southern Italy — heavily spiced with peperoncino, big on cured pork, and dependent on the local 'nduja sausage and pecorino crotonese. Seafood dominates along the coast: swordfish, anchovies, sardines, and the local *bianchetto* whitebait fried in olive oil.

In the centro storico, **Mediterraneo** does Calabrian classics with a modern hand — try the swordfish *involtini* and the local Cirò red wine. **Trattoria L'Oste della Pizzata**, a few streets down from the castle, leans into traditional family dishes including hand-rolled fileja pasta with goat ragù. On the seafront, **Lido Azzurro** is the standard pick for fritto misto and grilled prawns straight off the boats, with tables right above the sand.

End the meal with *liquirizia* — Calabria is the world's largest producer of liquorice — or a small glass of the *amaro silano*, a herbal digestif made in the mountains east of town.
