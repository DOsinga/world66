---
title: "Getting There"
type: section
---

Amantea sits on the Tyrrhenian coast of Calabria, on both the SS18 coastal road and the A2 Salerno–Reggio Calabria motorway, which runs inland a few kilometres behind the town. The motorway exit is "Falerna" or "Amantea" depending on which way you are coming from.

The **Amantea railway station** is a couple of kilometres south of the centre, on the Lamezia Terme–Paola line. Local trains stop frequently; from Lamezia (the regional hub) you can connect to fast Frecciarossa services up the Tyrrhenian coast to Rome and Naples, or south to Reggio Calabria. The nearest airport is **Lamezia Terme (SUF)**, about 40 km north, served by easyJet, Ryanair, and ITA with seasonal flights to most of northern Europe. A taxi from the airport takes around 40 minutes; there is also an infrequent local bus.
