---
title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Amantea breaks naturally into the historic centre on the hill — full of stone steps, narrow lanes, and the ruined Byzantine castle — and the lower, newer town along the seafront. Spend a morning climbing through the [centro storico](/europe/italy/calabria/amantea/centro_storico) up to the [castle](/europe/italy/calabria/amantea/amantea_castle), and an afternoon on the beach. The Tyrrhenian sunsets here are among the best on the Calabrian coast.
