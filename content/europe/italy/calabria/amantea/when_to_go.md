---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

The best months for Amantea are May, June, and September — warm enough to swim, light enough on tourists for the beach lidos to feel uncrowded. July and August are hot, busy, and considerably more expensive; book ahead if you plan to come in August.

Out of season the town is sleepy but not closed — the centro storico bars stay open, and the trattorias do shorter hours. Winters are mild but wet and windy along the coast. The big local festival is the **Festa di Sant'Antonio Abate** in mid-January, with bonfires in the streets and roast pig; the **Festa della Madonna del Carmine** in July fills the seafront with processions and fireworks.
