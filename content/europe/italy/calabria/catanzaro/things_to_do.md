type: section
---

Catanzaro rewards patient exploration rather than headline attractions. The [Viadotto Morandi](/europe/italy/calabria/catanzaro/viadotto_morandi) — the massive concrete bridge splitting the city across the Fiumarella gorge — is the defining visual feature of modern Catanzaro. The [funicular](/europe/italy/calabria/catanzaro/catanzaro_funicular) connecting the upper city to the lower marine district is one of Italy's few remaining urban funicular railways. The [Cathedral of the Immaculate](/europe/italy/calabria/catanzaro/cathedral) dominates the historic centre, rebuilt in the 1950s on the site of its destroyed predecessors.
