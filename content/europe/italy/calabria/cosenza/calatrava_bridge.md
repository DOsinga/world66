The Ponte San Francesco di Paola is a footbridge designed by Spanish architect Santiago Calatrava, spanning the Busento River at the edge of Cosenza's historic centre. Completed in 1999, it's one of Calatrava's smaller works but characteristic of his style — a single white parabolic arch carrying cables that support the deck below.

The bridge connects the historic lower town to the modern city and has become one of Cosenza's most photographed structures. It works especially well at dusk when the white concrete takes on a warm glow. The setting — the Busento runs between the old town hill and the modern city — frames it well from either bank.
