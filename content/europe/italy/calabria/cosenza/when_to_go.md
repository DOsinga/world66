---
title: When to Go
type: section
---

Cosenza's valley climate is hot and dry in summer — July and August are genuinely uncomfortable for walking around the steep old town. Spring (April–May) and autumn (September–October) are the best times to visit, with warm temperatures, lower humidity, and the city at its most liveable.

The city doesn't have significant tourist seasons in the usual sense — international tourism is minimal year-round. If you're combining a visit with time in the Sila mountains, note that the plateau has different weather to the valley: cool and often wet in summer, snowbound in winter.
