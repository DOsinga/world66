Palmi sits on a natural terrace above Calabria's Tyrrhenian coast, south of [Gioia Tauro](/europe/italy/calabria/gioia_tauro) and north of [Scilla](/europe/italy/calabria/scilla). It is one of the best viewpoints on the Costa Viola: on clear days the sea opens toward the Aeolian Islands, the Strait of Messina, and Sicily.

The town itself is practical rather than polished, but the setting does the heavy lifting. [Monte Sant'Elia](/europe/italy/calabria/palmi/monte_sant_elia) rises behind the centre, while the coast below drops to coves, rocks, and the famous [Scoglio dell'Ulivo](/europe/italy/calabria/palmi/scoglio_dellulivo) at Tonnara di Palmi.

Palmi also has a strong civic identity. [Palmi Cathedral](/europe/italy/calabria/palmi/palmi_cathedral) anchors the centre, the [Casa della Cultura Leonida Repaci](/europe/italy/calabria/palmi/casa_della_cultura) gathers museums and collections, and the ancient site of [Tauriana](/europe/italy/calabria/palmi/tauriana) reminds you that this stretch of coast has been inhabited and contested for a very long time. If you can time a visit for the Varia di Palmi, do it; otherwise come for the views, beaches, and a quieter Calabrian coastal rhythm.
