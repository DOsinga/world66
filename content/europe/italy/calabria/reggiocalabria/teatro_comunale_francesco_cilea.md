Teatro Comunale Francesco Cilea is Reggio Calabria's main historic theatre, a neoclassical 1,500-seat venue used for concerts, opera, and stage performances.

Even if you are not attending a show, the building is worth noting while walking through the centre between the cathedral, the Aragonese Castle, and the seafront.
