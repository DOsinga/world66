Sant'Andrea Apostolo dello Jonio is small enough that you can see everything on foot in a day — but that is not the point. The pleasure here is in slowing down: a morning swim at the beach below town, a wander through the old stone lanes of the upper borgo, and a long lunch at one of the family trattorias.
