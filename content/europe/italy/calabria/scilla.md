Perhaps Calabria's most picturesque fishing village. If you've read *The Odyssey*, you'll remember what a terrible time Ulysses had getting past Scylla and Charybdis, which flank what is now known as the Straits of Messina. When the atmospheric conditions are right, you can stand in [Marina Grande](/europe/italy/calabria/scilla/marina_grande), gaze out towards Sicily and see the Fata Morgana, an eerie mirage that seems to be the city of Messina reflected (right-side up) in the sea waters.

What definitely is not a mirage is the delightful neighbourhood of [Chianalea](/europe/italy/calabria/scilla/chianalea), where a narrow row of pastel-hued houses are squeezed between the highway and the beach. Scilla also has an elegant 17th-century downtown and [Castello Ruffo](/europe/italy/calabria/scilla/ruffo_castle), whose ramparts offer a great vantage point for La Chianalea and a long view south toward Sicily. The [Scilla Lighthouse](/europe/italy/calabria/scilla/scilla_lighthouse) on the castle headland is one of the pair of lights marking the northern entrance to the Strait of Messina.
