---
title: Getting There
type: section
---

Scilla is 25 km north of Reggio Calabria on the SS18, and the drive along the coast is attractive in its own right. By train, Scilla station is on the Reggio Calabria–Naples line, with frequent regional services from Reggio (about 25 minutes) and connections from Villa San Giovanni and beyond. From the station it is a short walk down to the sea.

From Sicily, take the hydrofoil or ferry from Messina to Villa San Giovanni or Reggio Calabria, then a local train north to Scilla. The hydrofoil crossing takes about 20 minutes. Day trips from Reggio Calabria or Tropea are both feasible.
