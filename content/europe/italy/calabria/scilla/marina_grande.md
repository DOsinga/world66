Marina Grande is Scilla's main beach and waterfront — a long crescent of dark sand at the base of the castle rock, backed by a promenade with bars and restaurants. It is a working beach as much as a holiday one: fishing boats are hauled up on the sand, and the fishermen's cooperative still operates here. The town's swordfish boats are a distinctive shape, long and narrow with a tall crow's nest mast from which a lookout spots the fish.

On calm mornings, this is the best place in Scilla to look out across the Strait of Messina toward Sicily. The Sicilian coastline and the peak of Etna are visible on clear days, and with the right atmospheric conditions — typically early morning in spring or autumn — the Fata Morgana appears: an eerie mirage that seems to lift the city of Messina above the water's surface.
