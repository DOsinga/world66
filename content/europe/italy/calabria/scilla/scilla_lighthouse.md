type: poi

The lighthouse at Scilla sits on the castle headland and is one of the pair of lights that guide ships into the northern entrance of the Strait of Messina — its counterpart, the Capo Peloro Lighthouse, stands on the Sicilian side directly opposite. The strait is one of the busiest and most technically demanding waterways in the Mediterranean, with strong tidal currents running between the Tyrrhenian and Ionian seas.

The lighthouse itself is not normally open to visitors, but the views from the castle platform beside it are among the best in Scilla. The combination of the strait, the Sicilian coastline, and the Calabrian mountains rising behind makes for a genuinely striking panorama.
