title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Soveria Mannelli rewards those who slow down. The old town is compact and walkable, with a handful of churches and a piazza where local life is conducted with the Calabrian unhurriedness that makes these hill towns easy to like. The surrounding forests offer walking through chestnuts and beech trees, and the Sila plateau — one of Calabria's most beautiful natural areas — is a short drive east.
