Sibari (historically Sybaris) was the wealthiest and most powerful city of Magna Graecia, so famously luxurious that "sybarite" became a byword for decadence. The ancient city was destroyed in 510 BC and later buried under river silt — preserved, not lost.

Today the [Archaeological Park of Sybaris](/europe/italy/calabria/sybaris_marine/archaeological_park) preserves excavations from several periods of settlement — the destroyed Greek Sybaris, its successor Thurii, and the Roman Copia on top. The [Museo Nazionale della Sibaritide](/europe/italy/calabria/sybaris_marine/museo_sibaritide) houses the portable finds from the site in a modern building nearby. Sibari Marina, on the Ionian coast, has a long sandy beach and is a popular summer destination for Italian families from the interior of Calabria — considerably less crowded than the Tyrrhenian side.
