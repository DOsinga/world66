---
title: "Getting There"
type: section
---

Sibari lies on the Ionian coast of Calabria, accessible by the SS106 coastal road that runs the length of the Ionian shore. The nearest train station is Sibari on the Reggio Calabria–Taranto line, served by regional trains. By car, Sibari is roughly 80 kilometres from Cosenza (the main inland city) via the A2 motorway and SS534. The nearest airports are Lamezia Terme (130km, the main Calabria airport) and Bari (220km), both served by low-cost carriers from several European cities.
