---
title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Sibari's main draw is archaeological: the ruins of one of the ancient world's wealthiest cities lie preserved in a remarkable open-air park. For visitors who want something less cerebral, the long sandy beach at Sibari Marina stretches south for kilometres and offers the kind of uncrowded Ionian coast summer that has largely disappeared from the more fashionable parts of Italy.
