The Museo Diocesano di Tropea sits beside the cathedral and gives useful depth to a town that many visitors otherwise treat as cliffs, beaches, and sunset photographs. Its collections focus on the religious life of the old diocese: sacred art, liturgical objects, sculpture, and fragments that connect Tropea's churches to the wider history of Calabria.

It is a small museum, best visited before or after the cathedral rather than as a standalone afternoon. That scale is part of the appeal. A short stop here makes the old centre feel less like a pretty stage set and more like a lived religious town with centuries of patronage, craft, and local devotion behind it.
