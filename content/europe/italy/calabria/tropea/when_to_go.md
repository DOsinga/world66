---
title: When to Go
type: section
---

Tropea is best visited from June to September for beach weather. July and August are the peak months — the town fills up with Italian families and international visitors, prices rise sharply, and the narrow streets can feel oppressively crowded on summer afternoons. June and September offer the best compromise: warm enough to swim, far less congested, and with the restaurants still fully open.

May is pleasant for sightseeing but the water is cool. From October to April, many beach facilities close and the town is quiet, though the scenery and the food are as good as ever. The Aeolian Islands, visible on clear days throughout the year, look their best in late spring and autumn when the air is clear.
