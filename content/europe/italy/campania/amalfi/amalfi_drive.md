The Amalfi Drive — the SS163 — is one of the most dramatic roads in Europe, cut into the face of cliffs that drop hundreds of metres straight into the sea. It runs for about 50 km between Vietri sul Mare and Positano, passing through Maiori, Minori, Atrani, Amalfi, Praiano, and Furore. The road is generally wide enough for two vehicles to pass, but buses, delivery trucks, and tourist cars combine to make progress slow and nerves frayed.

The drive is best experienced from a bus (SITA runs regular services along the coast) rather than behind the wheel, which allows you to look at the view rather than at the cliff edge. The hairpin bends, the sea far below, the tunnels cut straight through headlands, and the villages appearing around corners make this one of the great scenic routes. If you do drive, patience is essential — there is no way to hurry and nowhere to overtake. The road is technically a state road but feels like a private adventure.
