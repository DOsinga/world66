The Furore Inn is a luxury resort carved into the clifftop above the fjord, with one of the most dramatic settings on the Amalfi Coast. It is best known for its restaurant, **La Volpe Pescatrice**, which has a terrace with sweeping views over the Gulf of Salerno. The cooking is serious Campanian cuisine: fresh fish, local vegetables, pasta with seafood, and the coast's extraordinary lemons used in both savoury and sweet dishes.

The restaurant is not cheap — expect to pay well above Amalfi town prices — but the combination of food quality and the view is genuinely memorable. Booking ahead is essential in summer. The hotel itself is a discreet, well-run place popular with visitors who want the Amalfi Coast experience without the crowds of Positano. Address: Via dell'Amore 1, Furore. Tel: +39 0898 304711.
