Santa Maria del Bando is a small chapel set high above Amalfi, near the old watchtower of Pontone. The climb is part of the appeal: you trade the crowds at sea level for stone lanes, stairways, and wide views over the coast.

The chapel is modest, but its age, setting, and legend give it character. It is best treated as a scenic walk with a historic reward, not as a standalone church visit.
