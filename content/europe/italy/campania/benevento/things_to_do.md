title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Benevento's compact historic centre packs in a remarkable density of ancient monuments. Start with the [Arch of Trajan](/europe/italy/campania/benevento/arch_of_trajan), the best-preserved triumphal arch in the Roman world, then follow the old town uphill to the [Duomo](/europe/italy/campania/benevento/duomo) and its extraordinary 12th-century bronze door. The [Church of Santa Sofia](/europe/italy/campania/benevento/santa_sofia) and its attached cloister — together housing the [Museo del Sannio](/europe/italy/campania/benevento/museo_del_sannio) — are around the corner. The [Roman Theatre](/europe/italy/campania/benevento/teatro_romano) on the east side of the old town preserves most of its cavea. Near the Arch of Trajan, an [Egyptian Obelisk](/europe/italy/campania/benevento/egyptian_obelisk) from Domitian's Isis sanctuary stands in Piazza Papiniano.

Most of this can be covered on foot in half a day. The city is genuinely off the tourist trail, and you are unlikely to encounter another foreign visitor at any of these sites.
