Contursi Terme is a small spa town about 40 kilometres south of Calitri, reached via the Ofanto valley, and the nearest thermal bath facility to the area. The town sits above the Sele river valley and has been known for its sulphurous springs since Roman times. Several thermal establishments operate here, ranging from spa hotels to day-use facilities.

The waters are sulphurous and mildly radioactive — used therapeutically for respiratory conditions, rheumatism, and skin complaints. The most practical option for day visitors is a day pass to one of the spa hotels, which includes access to indoor and outdoor pools, mud treatments, and sometimes a restaurant.

The surrounding Sele valley is fertile and attractive. Paestum, with its exceptional Greek temples — among the best preserved in the world — is about an hour further south and makes a natural pairing with a thermal bath visit.
