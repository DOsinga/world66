The Natural Arch is one of Capri's most striking geological features: a 12-metre natural limestone arch that appears suddenly at the end of a cliff walk from Capri town, framing a view straight out to sea. It is a 20-minute walk from the Piazzetta through winding lanes that pass some of the island's oldest residential areas.

The arch is accessible without a ticket — just the walk — and for this reason tends to be slightly less crowded than the major sights. The cliff path below it continues to the Grotta di Matermania, a Roman cave used for religious ceremonies. The walk back to Capri can be done via the Belvedere di Tragara and the Faraglioni viewpoint, making a circular route of about 90 minutes from the Piazzetta.
