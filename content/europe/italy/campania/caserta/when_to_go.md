---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

Spring (April to June) and autumn (September to October) are the best times to visit. The park behind the palace stretches for three kilometres, and walking it in summer heat — especially August — is genuinely punishing. Crowds are also thinner outside peak summer months.

The palace itself can be visited year-round, and the interior is welcome shelter on cold or rainy days. If you are planning to walk the full length of the park to the Grande Cascata, aim for a weekday morning in spring or early autumn when the light is soft and the crowds are sparse.
