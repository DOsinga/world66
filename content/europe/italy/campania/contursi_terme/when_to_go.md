---
title: When to Go
type: section
---

The thermal hotels operate year-round, and the cura thermale crowd comes in all seasons — though spring and autumn see the largest numbers of Italian visitors taking scheduled treatment stays. Summer in the Sele valley can be hot, with temperatures in the low-to-mid thirties, which makes the thermal pools a relief rather than a treat.

If you are combining a stay in Contursi with visits to Paestum and the Cilento, late spring (May–June) is ideal: the Greek temples look magnificent in the clear air, the wildflowers are out across the Cilento hills, and the heat has not yet become oppressive.
