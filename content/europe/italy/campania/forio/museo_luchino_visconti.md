Museo Luchino Visconti occupies the villa once used by film director Luchino Visconti. The house and surrounding park are now used for cultural events and displays linked to Visconti's life and work.

It is a good stop for cinema fans and for visitors who want a quieter cultural break from Forio's beaches and gardens.
