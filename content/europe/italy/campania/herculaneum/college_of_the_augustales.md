The College of the Augustales is one of the most interesting indoor spaces at Herculaneum. It belonged to a civic-religious order connected with the cult of Augustus, and its main hall still gives a strong sense of Roman public ceremony. Look for the wall decoration and the way the room feels more formal and official than the site's private houses.
