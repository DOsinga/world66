---
title: Day Trips
type: section
---

Meta's position on the Circumvesuviana railway makes it an excellent base for day trips along the entire Gulf of Naples coastline. Sorrento is 10 minutes by train; Pompeii is 30 minutes; Naples is under an hour. The ferry connection to Capri runs from Sorrento rather than Meta, a short train ride away.

**Sorrento** is the obvious first stop — the town centre, the clifftop gardens, the views over the Faraglioni of Capri. Go early morning before the cruise tours arrive.

**Pompeii** is one of the great historical sites in Europe, and the Circumvesuviana drops you at the entrance. Allow a full day. The neighbouring site of Herculaneum (closer to Naples) is less visited and better preserved in some respects.

**Capri** — ferries from Sorrento take about 20 minutes. The Blue Grotto, the Faraglioni, the Piazzetta. Expensive and crowded in high season; worth it out of season.

**Positano and the Amalfi Coast** are accessible by bus from Sorrento — the SITA bus runs the spectacular coastal road. Positano is the most photogenic; Ravello, further east, is quieter and has the garden of the Villa Cimbrone.
