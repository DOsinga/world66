Chiaia is Naples' most polished neighbourhood, running along the seafront west of Castel dell'Ovo and extending north into the Vomero hill. It has the best shopping, the most sophisticated restaurants, and the highest concentration of wine bars in the city. The streets around Via San Pasquale are dense with small enoteche that fill up from 7pm. The Piazza dei Martiri, with its column of four marble lions and its surrounding boutiques, is the neighbourhood's symbolic centre.

Chiaia is where Naples shows its more composed face. The 19th-century palaces along the waterfront, the Villa Comunale park running along the sea, and the general atmosphere of the neighbourhood — well-maintained, relatively quiet by Neapolitan standards — offer a counterpoint to the chaos of the centro storico. The passeggiata along the seafront from Piazza Vittoria to Castel dell'Ovo is the best evening walk in Naples.
