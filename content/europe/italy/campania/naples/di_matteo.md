Via dei Tribunali, 94. One of the oldest pizzerias on the street and the place that launched pizza fritta — fried pizza — into global consciousness when Bill Clinton stopped here during the 1994 G7 summit in Naples. The photo of Clinton eating a folded pizza at the counter has been on the wall ever since. That moment of presidential authenticity was worth more to the city's tourism economy than any official reception.

The fried pizza (pizza fritta) is a folded, deep-fried half-moon stuffed with ricotta, salami, cicoli (pork cracklings), and provola, eaten standing at the counter. It is not elegant and it is not trying to be. The regular wood-fired pizzas are also good, and the lines are usually shorter than at Sorbillo across the street.
