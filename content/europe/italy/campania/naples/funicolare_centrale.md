Naples has four funicular railways connecting the city below to the Vomero hill above — more than any other city in the world. The Funicolare Centrale, running from Via Toledo to the Vomero, is the busiest. It covers 1,270 metres of track with a rise of 170 metres in about 8 minutes. What would be a stiff climb through the hillside streets is transformed into a comfortable public transit ride; the carriages are large and frequent.

The funiculars are practical transport, not tourist rides, and using them feels like using the city rather than visiting it. The view from the Vomero over the bay and Vesuvius, accessible only once you make the climb, is one of the rewards. The other three funiculars — Chiaia, Montesanto, and Mergellina — serve different flanks of the hill and connect to the Certosa di San Martino and Castel Sant'Elmo.
