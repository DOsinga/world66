Palazzo delle Arti, often shortened to PAN, is Naples' civic contemporary art centre in Chiaia. It hosts temporary exhibitions, cultural events, and parts of the city's public art collections.

The programme changes, so it is worth checking what is on before going. When the exhibitions are strong, it adds a useful modern counterpoint to Naples' archaeological and church-heavy sights.
