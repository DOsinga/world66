Palazzo Mannajuolo is an early 20th-century Art Nouveau apartment building in Chiaia, best known for its marble elliptical staircase. The building is private, so visits depend on respectful access through the entrance hall rather than a formal museum route.

It is a short architectural stop, but a good one if you are already walking between the seafront, Chiaia, and the city's more elegant western streets.
