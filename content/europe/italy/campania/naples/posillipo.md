Posillipo is Naples' coastal district northwest of the centre, long valued for its calmer shoreline and wide views over the bay. Roman ruins, villas, steep lanes, and landmarks such as Palazzo Donn'Anna give it enough substance for a deliberate walk rather than just a scenic detour.
