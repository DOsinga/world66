---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

April, May, and October are the best months: warm enough for the seafront and outdoor eating, not hot enough to be punishing, and less crowded than the summer. Spring also brings the best light for photographs of the bay.

July and August are crowded and very hot — the city fills with Italian tourists and the temperature regularly exceeds 35°C. Many locals leave and some good restaurants close. If you are coming in summer, the heat is part of the experience; compensate by eating late and sleeping through the afternoon.

December and January are quiet and sometimes surprisingly pleasant, though the sea is cold and some outdoor areas close. Christmas is worth timing a visit around: the Christmas market on Via San Gregorio Armeno — where artisans sell hand-carved nativity figures, some traditional and some depicting contemporary celebrities — is one of the best seasonal markets in Italy. The market runs year-round but reaches its peak in December.
