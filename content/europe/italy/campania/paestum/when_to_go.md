---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

April and May are the best months for Paestum. The Campania plain is warm but not yet punishing, the temples look their best in spring light, and you will share the site with far fewer people than in summer. September and October are nearly as good — harvest season, lower temperatures, and the flat landscape takes on a golden quality in the afternoon.

July and August are survivable but uncomfortable. The coastal plain south of Salerno gets genuinely hot, and the site has little shade. The museum is air-conditioned; the temples are not. Morning visits — arriving at opening time and leaving by noon — are the practical solution if you find yourself there in high summer.

The site is open year-round, and winter visits (outside Italian public holidays) can be remarkably peaceful. December and January are cool and sometimes rainy, but the temples look striking in overcast light. The museum tends to be quiet on winter weekday mornings in a way it never is in summer.
