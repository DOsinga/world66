The Shrine of the Virgin of the Rosary is the spiritual centre of modern Pompei, a sharp contrast to the pagan streets and villas of the archaeological site nearby. Its domed basilica, tall campanile and richly decorated interior draw pilgrims from across Italy, especially for Marian feast days.

Even if you came for the ruins, the shrine is worth a detour because it explains why today's city exists as more than a service town for archaeology. Climb or look up toward the bell tower for a sense of how the modern street grid gathers around the sanctuary.
