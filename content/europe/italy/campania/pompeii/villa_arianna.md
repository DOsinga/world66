Villa Arianna is one of the excavated Roman villas of ancient Stabiae, south of Pompeii. It is best known for refined frescoes, mythological scenes, and terraces that once looked across the Bay of Naples. It rewards visitors who want a quieter archaeological stop beyond the main Pompeii ruins.
