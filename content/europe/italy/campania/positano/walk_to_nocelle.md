The walk from Positano up to Nocelle, a small village perched on the ridge above the town, is one of the best hikes on the Amalfi Coast. The path climbs steeply through the town and then becomes the Sentiero degli Dei — the Path of the Gods — a trail that traverses the ridge of the Monti Lattari with panoramic views over the entire coastline.

The walk to Nocelle takes about two hours from the lower town, depending on how often you stop to look out at the sea. From Nocelle you can continue along the ridge to Praiano or descend to Bomerano and catch a bus. Nocelle itself has a small bar and a restaurant with one of the finest views on the coast.
