---
title: When to Go
type: section
---

Positano is best in May, June, and September. July and August are beautiful but intensely crowded — the narrow streets become difficult to move through, the beach fills completely, prices are at their peak, and ferries often sell out. If you go in high summer, arrive early in the morning before the day-trippers.

May and June offer warm temperatures (18–24°C), calm seas, and manageable crowds. September is perhaps the best month: summer heat without the worst of the crowds, warm enough to swim, and the light is softer. October is good for hiking — the Sentiero degli Dei is at its best in autumn colour.

Winter in Positano is quiet to the point of emptiness — many restaurants and hotels close from November to March. The town has a different, more local character in winter, but services are limited.
