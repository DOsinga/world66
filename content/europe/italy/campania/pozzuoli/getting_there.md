---
title: Getting There
type: section
---

Pozzuoli is easily reached from Naples by the Cumana and Metropolitana Circumflegrea rail lines, which run from the Montesanto station in Naples centre to Pozzuoli in about 25 minutes. The Metro Line 2 also serves Pozzuoli with a stop about 15 minutes walk from the town centre. By car, take the Tangenziale di Napoli motorway and exit at Pozzuoli; parking is available but the old town centre is partly ZTL (limited traffic zone).
