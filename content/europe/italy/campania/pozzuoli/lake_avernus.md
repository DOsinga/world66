Lake Avernus is a small, perfectly circular volcanic lake about 4 kilometres west of central Pozzuoli, occupying an ancient crater in the Campi Flegrei. The lake is strikingly beautiful — deep blue-green water surrounded by wooded slopes — and the contrast between its peaceful appearance and its ancient reputation as the gateway to hell is part of its appeal. A walking path circles the lake, taking about an hour at an easy pace.

Near the shore, you can find the remains of what the Romans called the Cave of the Sibyl of Cumae — a long, arched tunnel carved into the volcanic tufa that was associated with oracular tradition. The actual Cave of the Sibyl is at Cumae, several kilometres away, but the atmosphere of tunnels and steam vents around the lake fed the mythology. The ancient tunnel near the lake, sometimes called the Grotta di Cocceio, runs all the way through the hill toward Cumae, though it is not always accessible.
