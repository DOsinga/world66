title: When to Go

Ravello is a year-round destination but has distinct seasons. Spring (April–May) and early autumn (September–October) are the most comfortable: the crowds are manageable, the temperatures pleasant, and the gardens at their best. July and August bring the Ravello Festival and large numbers of visitors; the town is busy but lively. Midsummer heat can make the walk up from Amalfi tiring in the middle of the day. Winter is quiet but mild — most restaurants and hotels stay open, and the views on clear days are exceptional.

**The Ravello Festival** runs from June to September, with the main concerts staged on the terrace of Villa Rufolo. The programme covers classical music, jazz, and dance; the garden stage with the Gulf of Salerno as backdrop is one of the most dramatic concert settings in Europe. Tickets sell in advance and it's worth booking early.
