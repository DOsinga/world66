title: Bars and Cafes
type: section
tags: [bars_and_cafes]
---

Salerno's bar scene centres on the lungomare and the lanes around Via dei Mercanti. The city has a real passeggiata culture — late afternoon and evening, the promenade fills with locals, and the aperitivo hour is taken seriously. Prices are significantly lower than on the Amalfi Coast.
