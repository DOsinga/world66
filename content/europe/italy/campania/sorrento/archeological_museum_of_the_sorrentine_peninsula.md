The Archeological Museum of the Sorrentine Peninsula sits in Villa Fondi at Piano di Sorrento, a short trip from central Sorrento. Its collections cover the Greek, Etruscan, Roman, and local material history of the peninsula, giving context to a coast that is often experienced mainly through views and restaurants.

The setting helps. Villa Fondi looks over the Bay of Naples, and the museum’s scale is manageable enough to visit without sacrificing a whole day. It is a good stop before or after seeing the Roman remains at Bagni della Regina Giovanna, because it gives the ancient coast a wider frame.
