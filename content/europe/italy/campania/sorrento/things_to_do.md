---
title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Sorrento's main pleasures are its position — above the sea, close to everything — and its old town. The sights are modest by Italian standards, but the place is genuinely pleasant to wander. [Piazza Tasso](/europe/italy/campania/sorrento/piazza_tasso) is the hub; from there, the [Cathedral](/europe/italy/campania/sorrento/cattedrale_di_sorrento), the [Chiostro di San Francesco](/europe/italy/campania/sorrento/chiostro_san_francesco), and the [Vallone dei Mulini](/europe/italy/campania/sorrento/vallone_dei_mulini) viewpoint are all within a ten-minute walk. The [Museo Correale](/europe/italy/campania/sorrento/museo_correale) is worth an hour for the intarsia woodwork and the terrace views. The [Bagni della Regina Giovanna](/europe/italy/campania/sorrento/bagni_regina_giovanna) — a natural sea pool in Roman ruins — is the best swimming option near town, a 20-minute walk west along [Via del Capo](/europe/italy/campania/sorrento/via_del_capo). [Marina Grande](/europe/italy/campania/sorrento/marina_grande), the old fishing quarter below the cliffs, is worth the descent for lunch or an evening meal.
