Italy rewards anyone willing to leave the headline cities behind for a day. The most rewarding escape of all is the [Amalfi Coast](/europe/italy/campania/amalfi_coast), a 50-kilometre ribbon of cliffside towns and turquoise coves on the Sorrentine Peninsula — reachable as a long day from Naples or Sorrento, though it well deserves an overnight.
