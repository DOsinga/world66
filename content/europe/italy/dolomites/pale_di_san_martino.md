The Pale di San Martino form the southern massif of the Dolomites — a compact group of towers and walls above the resort town of San Martino di Castrozza in Trentino. The massif is one of the most dramatic in the range: the Cima della Vezzana (3,192m) and the Cimon della Pala (3,184m) rise from the plateau in near-vertical faces above the ski resort, visible from the valley as a wall of pale rock.

The cable car from San Martino reaches the plateau rim in minutes, where the high ground opens into an extraordinary flat landscape of limestone pavement and snow. The ridge walk above the plateau to the Passo Pradidali is one of the best high routes in the Dolomites, with views across the whole southern range. In winter, San Martino di Castrozza is a quieter ski resort than the Sella Ronda circuit — with a fraction of the crowds. The local cheese, smoked meat, and honey from this less-visited corner of the Dolomites are worth seeking out.
