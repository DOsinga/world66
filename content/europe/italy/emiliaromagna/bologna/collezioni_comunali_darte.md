Set inside Palazzo d'Accursio, Bologna's municipal art collection is a quiet counterpoint to the busy square outside. The rooms move through local painting, decorative art, and historic interiors, with works by Bolognese artists such as the Carracci and Giuseppe Maria Crespi.
