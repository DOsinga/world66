Galleria d'Arte Moderna Raccolta Lercaro holds a modern-art collection with Italian and international works, including sculpture by artists such as Manzu, Messina, Rodin, and Giacometti.

It is a focused museum rather than one of Bologna's essential first stops, but it rewards visitors who want more art after the city's larger civic and university collections.
