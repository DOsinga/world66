The Museo delle Cere Anatomiche Luigi Cattaneo is Bologna at its most anatomical: a university collection of 19th-century wax models showing bones, organs, deformities, and medical teaching specimens. It is a small, odd museum, but it fits a city where medicine, scholarship, and a taste for the macabre have long shared the same streets.
