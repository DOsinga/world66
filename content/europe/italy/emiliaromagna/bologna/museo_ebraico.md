Museo Ebraico, Bologna's Jewish Museum, stands in the area of the former ghetto and traces the history of Jewish life in the city and the wider Emilia-Romagna region. Exhibits cover religious traditions, local communities, persecution, and the long links between Bologna and Jewish scholarship.

It is a useful stop while exploring the university quarter and the lanes around Via Zamboni.
