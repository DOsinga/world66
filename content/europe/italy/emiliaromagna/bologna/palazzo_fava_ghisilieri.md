Palazzo Fava Ghisilieri is a 16th-century palace in central Bologna, best known for frescoes by the Carracci family. Its historic rooms are used for temporary exhibitions, so the value of a visit depends partly on what is showing.

Even between major shows, it sits in one of Bologna's richest museum and church clusters. Check the current exhibition before making it a main stop.
