Santa Maria dei Servi sits on Strada Maggiore behind a four-sided portico — a Gothic cloister opening onto the street. The interior is tall, brick, and unusually atmospheric, with the polygonal apse glowing in the late afternoon.

The church holds Cimabue's Madonna Enthroned (the *Maestà*), an early thirteenth-century Byzantine-style panel that is one of the few works securely attributed to the artist outside Florence and Assisi. The arcaded forecourt, used as a market square in the Middle Ages, is one of the few open public spaces along Strada Maggiore — a nice spot to break a walk through the eastern half of the old centre.
