A few doors down from the Finestrella di Via Piella, this small trattoria has built a steady reputation on hand-rolled pasta — tortellini, tagliatelle, lasagne — made in the kitchen each morning. The dining room is small and the tables tight, which is part of the charm.

Cooking is conservative Bolognese: cotoletta alla bolognese (the breaded veal cutlet topped with prosciutto and parmesan), polpette al ragù, baccalà on Fridays. House wines are competent. Bookings recommended for dinner. After the meal, two minutes' walk takes you to the canal window for the obligatory photograph.
