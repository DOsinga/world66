Villa delle Rose is one of Bologna's quieter art spaces, set in a villa near Giardini Margherita and linked to the city's modern and contemporary art museum network. Its exhibitions tend to be temporary, so the appeal depends heavily on what is showing.

The setting helps: after the dense porticoes and food streets of the centre, the villa feels airy and almost residential. Pair it with a walk through the park, especially if you are already heading south of the historic core.
