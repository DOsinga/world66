About 6 km south of Cattolica, in the hills just over the regional border in Le Marche, the medieval fortress of **Gradara** is one of the most complete and dramatic small castles in central Italy. Concentric walls, a moat, a thirty-metre keep and a chapel with a Giovanni Bellini polyptych — and a whole tiny walled village tumbling down the hillside outside the inner curtain.

The castle is most associated with the Malatesta family and with the legend of **Paolo and Francesca**, whose adulterous love and double murder Dante immortalised in the Inferno. It's a 15-minute drive from Cattolica and easily a half-day visit; come early or late to avoid the bus tours.
