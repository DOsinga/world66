The **Lungomare Rasi-Spinelli** runs the full length of the seafront, a wide pedestrian promenade lined with palms, ice-cream kiosks, hotels and the row of private *bagni* (beach concessions) that organise umbrellas, sunbeds and changing rooms by the day or the week. The beach itself is broad, very flat, and made of fine pale sand — easy on bare feet, easy with children, and gently shelving for a long way out.

Most of the beach is privately franchised in the Italian style (you pay the establishment for a parasol and two loungers), but there are free public stretches at either end of town. The whole promenade fills up at *passeggiata* hour, between six and eight in the evening.
