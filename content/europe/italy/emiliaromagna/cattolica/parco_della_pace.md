A pleasant urban park a few blocks back from the seafront, with mature pines, a small lake, a playground, and a sculpture trail that includes a couple of Tonino Guerra installations. It is the largest green space in town, a welcome refuge from the sun in midsummer, and a favourite spot for the local *passeggiata* with strollers and small children.

Open daily, with a small bar on the western edge for coffee and ice-cream.
