---
title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Cattolica is a beach-and-promenade town, and the **beach** itself — broad, flat and three kilometres long — is the obvious main attraction. The seafront **Lungomare Rasi-Spinelli** and its parallel **Piazza I Maggio**, with the dramatic shell-shaped fountain by Tonino Guerra, are the social heart of the resort.

The aquarium, **Le Navi**, is the second-largest in Italy and probably the single best thing to do with children on the entire Romagna coast. For history, **Museo della Regina** gives a good account of the town's Roman and fishing past. And the inland hill towns just south — especially **Gradara**, with its almost cinematic medieval castle — make excellent half-day excursions.
