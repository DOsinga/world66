The former home of Giorgio Bassani, one of the great Italian novelists of the 20th century, has been preserved as a small museum dedicated to his life and work. Bassani was born in Ferrara in 1916 and grew up in the Jewish community he later memorialised in his "Romanzo di Ferrara" cycle of novels — six books set in the city from the 1920s to the post-war period.

The museum includes personal objects, manuscripts, photographs, and editions of his work, and gives a sense of the refined but anxious world of Ferrara's Jewish bourgeoisie that Bassani described. For anyone who has read The Garden of the Finzi-Continis or The Gold-Rimmed Spectacles, a visit adds an extra layer to walking the city's streets and the ghetto neighbourhood.
