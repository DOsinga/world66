---
title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Ferrara's Renaissance core — the whole of which is a UNESCO World Heritage Site — is compact enough to cover in a day on foot or by bicycle. The [Este Castle](/europe/italy/emiliaromagna/ferrara/estecastle), the [Cathedral](/europe/italy/emiliaromagna/ferrara/cathedral), and the frescoed [Palazzo Schifanoia](/europe/italy/emiliaromagna/ferrara/palazzo_schifanoia) are the main draws. The [Palazzo dei Diamanti](/europe/italy/emiliaromagna/ferrara/palazzo_dei_diamanti) hosts major temporary exhibitions. The [Addizione Erculea](/europe/italy/emiliaromagna/ferrara/addizione_erculea) — the Renaissance urban expansion — and the [City Walls](/europe/italy/emiliaromagna/ferrara/city_walls) are best explored by bicycle.
