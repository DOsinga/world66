title: Things to Do
type: section
---

Misano Adriatico's main draws are the [Misano World Circuit](/europe/italy/emiliaromagna/misanoadriatico/misano_world_circuit) for motorsport visitors and the beach for everyone else. The [Porto Verde](/europe/italy/emiliaromagna/misanoadriatico/porto_verde) marina at the southern end of town is the most pleasant part of the waterfront for an evening walk and a meal. Beyond these, the town functions primarily as a beach resort — the pier, the lungomare, and the beach establishments are the daily rhythm of the place from June to September.

For those with a car, Gradara Castle (a perfectly preserved medieval fortress about 10 km south) makes a good half-day outing. [Rimini](/europe/italy/emiliaromagna/rimini) is 18 km north and has an old city worth exploring — the Tempio Malatestiano, the arch of Augustus, and a Roman bridge all within walking distance of each other.
