The Enzo Ferrari Home Museum pairs the house where Ferrari was born with a bright modern gallery shaped like the sweep of a car bonnet. The exhibits use cars, film, and design displays to tell the story of Modena's motor culture and Ferrari's place in it.

It is a natural stop for anyone following the Motor Valley trail, but the museum also works if you are simply curious about how a provincial Italian city became a world name in racing and engineering.
