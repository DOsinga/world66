The Stanguellini Vintage Car Museum is a small but very Modenese stop. The Stanguellini family built and raced cars long before the Motor Valley became a polished tourism brand, and the museum preserves racing cars, engines, photographs, and workshop memories tied to that tradition.

It will not replace the Ferrari museums for scale, but it has a more intimate charm. Come if you like the mechanical side of Modena: hand-built machines, local ambition, and the era when small racing outfits could still make themselves heard.
