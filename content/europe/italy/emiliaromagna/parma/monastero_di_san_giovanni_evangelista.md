The Monastero di San Giovanni Evangelista stands beside Parma Cathedral and adds another layer to the Piazza del Duomo area. The church has a Baroque facade, while the monastery and cloisters keep a Renaissance character.

It is best visited together with the cathedral, baptistery, and Antica Spezieria di San Giovanni. The appeal is quieter than Parma's headline monuments, but the cloisters and church architecture reward a deliberate stop.
