Parma's Orto Botanico is the university botanical garden, a quiet academic green space close to the historic centre. Its plant collections and glasshouse setting offer a different rhythm from the city's churches, theatres, and food-focused sightseeing.

This is a modest POI, but it is useful for travellers who like gardens, scientific collections, or calm breaks between denser cultural stops. Check access before going, since university gardens can keep narrower hours than major museums.
