Palazzetto Eucherio San Vitale is a small Renaissance building in Parma's Ducal Park. Its main interest is artistic: a fresco attributed to Parmigianino and wall paintings by Cosimo da Piazza survive inside.

It is an easy add-on to a walk through the park and the Pilotta area. The palazzetto is not a headline sight, but it rewards travellers who enjoy Parma's quieter Renaissance corners.
