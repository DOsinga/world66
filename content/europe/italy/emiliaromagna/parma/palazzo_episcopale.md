Palazzo Episcopale, the Bishop's Palace, stands beside Parma's cathedral complex. Its Romanesque core dates back to the 11th century, with later work in the 15th century and restorations in the 20th.

It is best seen as part of the cathedral square, where Parma's religious and civic history are packed into a compact set of buildings.
