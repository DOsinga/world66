---
title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

The compact historic centre of Parma is among the richest in northern Italy, with a Romanesque cathedral, a pink-marble baptistery, and the vast Palazzo della Pilotta housing both an opera-era wooden theatre and the city's main picture gallery. Add Verdi's opera house and a handful of one-off museums and curiosities, and a long day or weekend visit fills naturally.
