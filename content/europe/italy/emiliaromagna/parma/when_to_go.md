---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

**Spring (April to June)** is the best all-round time: mild weather, green Po valley countryside, and the run-up to the summer festivals. **September and October** are equally attractive, with the Verdi Festival (October) pulling opera audiences from across Europe.

**Summer (July, August)** is humid and often very hot; many restaurants close for two weeks in August. **Winter** is cold and grey with frequent fog rising off the Po, but it brings the cured-meat and cheese season into its prime — a January meal of new prosciutto and a glass of frizzante Lambrusco is one of the small pleasures of the year.
