The Palazzo Gotico is the medieval city hall of Piacenza, a 13th-century Gothic building in red and white marble and brick that occupies the north side of the Piazza dei Cavalli. The ground floor is an open arcade of pointed arches; the upper floor has large mullioned windows and a crenellated roofline. The building is one of the more elegant medieval civic palaces in northern Italy, particularly in the way it addresses the piazza — the arcade creating a covered walkway that integrates the building with the public space.

The palace now serves as the seat of the provincial council and is occasionally open for exhibitions. The interior has a large Gothic hall on the upper floor. From the piazza, the Palazzo Gotico and the two equestrian statues in front of it make for one of the most photographed views in Piacenza.
