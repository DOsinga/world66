---
title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Start at the [Piazza dei Cavalli](/europe/italy/emiliaromagna/piacenza/piazza_dei_cavalli), the main square anchored by the Gothic [Palazzo Gotico](/europe/italy/emiliaromagna/piacenza/palazzo_gotico) and the two famous equestrian statues. The [Cathedral](/europe/italy/emiliaromagna/piacenza/piacenza_cathedral) a few minutes away has one of the best Romanesque interiors in Emilia and a cupola painted by Guercino. The [Palazzo Farnese](/europe/italy/emiliaromagna/piacenza/palazzo_farnese) museum contains the Etruscan Liver of Piacenza, one of the most unusual objects in Italian archaeology, as well as good art and an impressive carriage museum. For church art, [San Sisto](/europe/italy/emiliaromagna/piacenza/san_sisto) and [Santa Maria di Campagna](/europe/italy/emiliaromagna/piacenza/santa_maria_di_campagna) are both exceptional; the latter's frescoed dome by Pordenone is among the finest Renaissance frescoes in northern Italy. The [Galleria d'Arte Moderna Ricci Oddi](/europe/italy/emiliaromagna/piacenza/galleria_ricci_oddi) has a good collection of Italian art from the 19th and 20th centuries.
