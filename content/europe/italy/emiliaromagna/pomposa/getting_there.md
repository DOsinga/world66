title: Getting There
type: section
---

The abbey lies on the SS309 Romea, the old coast road between Venice and Ravenna, about 50 km north of [Ravenna](/europe/italy/emiliaromagna/ravenna) and 50 km east of [Ferrara](/europe/italy/emiliaromagna/ferrara). It is signed from the main road and has its own car park. Without a car you are out of luck most of the year — local buses from Codigoro and Ferrara serve the abbey infrequently and journeys involve at least one change. Check Tper timetables before relying on them.

Many visitors combine Pomposa with a swim or seafood lunch at [Comacchio](/europe/italy/emiliaromagna/comacchio) or its beaches to the south, or with the medieval town of Mesola and the Po di Volano further north.
