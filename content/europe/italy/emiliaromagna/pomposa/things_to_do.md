type: section
---

Pomposa is essentially its abbey. Start with the [abbey church](/europe/italy/emiliaromagna/pomposa/abbey_church) and its astonishing painted interior, climb your gaze up the [bell tower](/europe/italy/emiliaromagna/pomposa/bell_tower), then make time for the much quieter [chapter house and refectory](/europe/italy/emiliaromagna/pomposa/chapter_house_refectory) and the small [abbey museum](/europe/italy/emiliaromagna/pomposa/abbey_museum). With more time, push on into the surrounding [Po Delta Regional Park](/europe/italy/emiliaromagna/pomposa/po_delta_park) for a cycle ride or a boat trip.
