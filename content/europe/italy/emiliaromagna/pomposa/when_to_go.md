---
title: When to Go
type: section
---

Late spring and early autumn are the best times. April and May bring the Po Delta's bird migration and flowering wetlands, with temperatures in the low twenties and the abbey unusually quiet. September and October are similar — warm enough for the long bike rides that the delta rewards, cool enough that the crowds at the abbey have thinned.

Summer is fine but hot and humid, with mosquitos in the marshes; bring repellent if you plan to cycle. Winter is bleak — the delta light is beautiful but the abbey and its surrounding villages largely close down. The abbey itself is open year-round, but check opening hours in the off season as they shorten in the afternoon.
