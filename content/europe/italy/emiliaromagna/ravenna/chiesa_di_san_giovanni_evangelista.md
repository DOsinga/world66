Chiesa di San Giovanni Evangelista is one of Ravenna's very early churches, traditionally founded in the fifth century by Galla Placidia after surviving a dangerous sea voyage. Most of its original mosaics are gone, but the church still adds context to Ravenna's late Roman and early Christian story.

It is a quieter stop than the city's famous mosaic monuments, best visited as part of a walk between the station and the historic center.
