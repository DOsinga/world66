The Palazzo di Teodorico is a small archaeological stop near Ravenna's station, traditionally linked with Theodoric's palace complex. What you see today is fragmentary: brick ruins, an imposing facade-like remnant, and mosaic floors from excavations that were installed here in the 20th century.

It is not one of Ravenna's great mosaic monuments, but it helps connect the city's late Roman and Ostrogothic story. Stop if you are already tracing Theodoric's Ravenna, especially before or after the Arian Baptistry and Mausoleum of Theodoric.
