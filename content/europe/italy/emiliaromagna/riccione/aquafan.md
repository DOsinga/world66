Aquafan is one of the largest water parks in Italy, occupying a substantial area on the northern edge of Riccione. It has more than 30 water slides and attractions ranging from gentle family rides to serious speed slides, plus wave pools, a lazy river, and dedicated areas for different age groups.

The park is genuinely good at what it does, and on a hot August day it fills to capacity with families and teenagers from across northern Italy. Arriving early is important — the main parking fills by mid-morning in peak season, and the queues for the most popular slides can run to 45 minutes in the afternoon. Weekdays are significantly quieter than weekends.
