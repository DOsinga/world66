A long-running three-floor club in Riccione that exemplifies the Adriatic riviera's industrial-scale nightlife. Each floor runs a different musical mode — commercial dance, house, lounge — and the top-floor cocktail bar holds onto a more grown-up crowd. Busy from midnight to dawn in summer; in winter it opens only Friday and Saturday.

Viale Brindisi 20, Riccione.
