---
title: Eating Out
type: section
---

Rimini sits between the sea and the Marecchia valley, and the food reflects both: piadina flatbreads with squacquerone cheese and prosciutto, fish from the Adriatic, and the rich pasta tradition of Romagna — cappelletti in brodo, tagliatelle al ragù, passatelli. The trick is to avoid the seafront tourist traps and head into the old town or the inland hills.
