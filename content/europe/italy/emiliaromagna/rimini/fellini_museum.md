Opened in 2021 and instantly the most important visitor draw in Rimini, the Fellini Museum spreads across three sites: Castel Sismondo, the Palazzo del Fulgor, and the redesigned Piazza Malatesta between them. Costumes, set pieces, sketches, and immersive video rooms walk you through a career that defined Italian cinema for half a century.

Allow at least two hours. The Cinema Fulgor — the actual cinema where the young Fellini watched his first films, lovingly restored to its 1950s rococo — is part of the visit and still shows movies in the evening.
