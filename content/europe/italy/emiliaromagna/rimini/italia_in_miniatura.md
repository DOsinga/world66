A miniature Italy of nearly three hundred 1:25 scale models of Italian landmarks, set in a thirty-acre park on the northern outskirts of Rimini. Children love it; adults find it endearing rather than tacky, and the larger walk-through scale Venice is genuinely impressive.

Most easily reached by car or the local bus; allow a good half-day with younger kids. Closed in winter except for selected weekends. Via Popilia 239, Viserba.
