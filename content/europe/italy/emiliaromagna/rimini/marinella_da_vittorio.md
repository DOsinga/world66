Family-run trattoria a short walk back from the seafront in the Marina Centro area. The kitchen does a particularly good tagliolini allo scoglio — homemade tagliolini with mussels, clams, and prawns — and other Adriatic seafood standards. Service is friendly and unfussy.

Viale Valturio 39. Closed Mondays.
