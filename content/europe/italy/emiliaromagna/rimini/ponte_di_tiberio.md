A five-arched Roman bridge in white Istrian stone crossing the Marecchia at the northern edge of the old town. The roadway over the bridge was closed to cars in 2015 and is now one of the most pleasant places in Rimini — a wide stone deck looking down at the river and the Borgo San Giuliano on the far bank.

Walk over at sunset for the colour on the limestone. The square at the city end is the natural meeting point of the historic centre; on summer evenings it fills with people taking an aperitivo on the steps.
