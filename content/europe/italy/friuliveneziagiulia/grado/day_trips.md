---
title: "Day Trips"
type: section
---

Grado is well placed for half-day or full-day excursions to nearby coastal and Friulian sites.

- **Aquileia** — 10 km north, the ancient Roman city and the seat of the patriarchate from which Grado later inherited; UNESCO-listed for its basilica with one of the largest early Christian mosaic floors in the world
- **Palmanova** — about 30 km north, a Venetian-built star-shaped fortress town from 1593, perfectly preserved
- **Trieste** — 50 km east, Italy's most idiosyncratic city
- **Lignano Sabbiadoro** — across the lagoon to the west, the Adriatic's largest and most developed beach resort, for contrast
