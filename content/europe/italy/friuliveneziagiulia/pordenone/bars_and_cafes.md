---
title: "Bars and Cafes"
type: section
---

The loggias along the Corso Vittorio Emanuele II shelter an almost continuous row of bars and caffè, many with outdoor tables that are occupied from mid-morning until well into the evening. Pordenone's bar culture is characteristically northeastern Italian — the aperitivo hour (6–8pm) is taken seriously, with spritz and prosecco accompanied by generous plates of cicchetti.
