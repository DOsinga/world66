The Palazzo Ricchieri is a Gothic-Venetian palace on the Corso that now houses Pordenone's civic art museum. The collection is strongest in works by the city's most famous painter, Giovanni Antonio de' Sacchis (Il Pordenone), a Renaissance master whose dramatic, highly individual style placed him somewhere between Giorgione and Titian. His large altarpieces and frescoes can be seen in churches throughout Friuli, but the museum provides the clearest overview of his career.

The museum also holds a good collection of Venetian and Friulian decorative arts, ceramics, and medieval sculpture. Opening hours are restricted — typically Tuesday to Sunday, afternoons only — so check before visiting. Entry is modest.
