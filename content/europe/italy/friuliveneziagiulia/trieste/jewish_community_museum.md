The Museo della Comunita Ebraica di Trieste Carlo e Vera Wagner is a compact museum of the Jewish community of Trieste, with ritual silverware, textiles, documents, and objects from local religious life. It helps explain a community that played an important part in the city's commercial and cultural history.

The museum is close to the centre and works well alongside Trieste's synagogue, San Giusto, and the old streets below the hill.
