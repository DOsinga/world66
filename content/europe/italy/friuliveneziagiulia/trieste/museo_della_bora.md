Museo della Bora is a tiny, eccentric Trieste museum dedicated to the bora, the cold wind that has shaped the city’s habits, jokes, and street life. It is less about grand exhibits than about local obsession made visible.

That specificity is exactly why it works. Trieste is a city of atmosphere, weather, and literary corners, and this small museum gives travellers a playful way to understand a force locals talk about as if it had a personality.
