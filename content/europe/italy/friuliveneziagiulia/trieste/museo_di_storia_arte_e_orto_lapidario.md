The Museo di Storia, Arte e Orto Lapidario brings together archaeology, local history, art, and stone monuments near San Giusto. Its collections range from prehistoric finds and Roman sculpture to Egyptian, Greek, and pre-Roman objects.

It is a good companion to the cathedral and castle above Trieste, especially if you want the city's older layers rather than only its Habsburg streetscape.
