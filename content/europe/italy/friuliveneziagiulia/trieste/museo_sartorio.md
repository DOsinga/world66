Museo Sartorio is an art museum in a 19th-century villa near Trieste's waterfront. Its collection came largely from the Sartorio family and includes paintings, sculpture, drawings, and decorative art from the 19th and 20th centuries.

The highlights include sketches by Giambattista Tiepolo and works that show the taste of Trieste's old merchant elite. It pairs well with nearby Museo Revoltella for visitors interested in the city's art collections.
