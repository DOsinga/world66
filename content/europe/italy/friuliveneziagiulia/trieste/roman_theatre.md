Trieste's Roman Theatre sits in the lower town, where ancient Tergeste spread out toward the sea. The stone seating uses the natural slope of the hill, while the stage and upper parts were probably once timber. It was uncovered in the 1930s, and the statues that decorated it are now kept in the city's museum.
