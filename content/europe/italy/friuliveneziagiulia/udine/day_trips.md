Udine sits at the centre of a small but rewarding region. Forty minutes south brings you to the Adriatic and the lagoons of Marano and Grado; an hour east takes you to Trieste; twenty minutes east is **Cividale del Friuli**, a Lombard town set into a gorge of the Natisone river, with a 8th-century Tempietto Longobardo that is a UNESCO site. **San Daniele**, 30 km north-west, is the home of San Daniele prosciutto and hosts the *Aria di Festa* ham festival each June. The hills above Cividale are the heart of the **Collio** and **Colli Orientali** wine regions — book a tasting at one of the dozens of small wineries.
