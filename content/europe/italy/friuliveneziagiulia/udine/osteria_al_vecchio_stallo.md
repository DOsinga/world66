Address: Via Viola 7. A classic Friulian *osteria* in the historic centre that serves the cuisine of the surrounding countryside without nostalgia or fuss. Expect *frico* (a crisp wheel of fried Montasio cheese and potato), *cjarsons* (sweet-savoury ravioli filled with herbs, raisins and cinnamon), pasta with wild herbs, and game in season.

Wine is poured by the glass from the local cooperative — Friulano, Refosco, Schioppettino — at osteria prices. The place gets full at lunch and dinner; book or arrive early. Cash and cards now both accepted. Closed Wednesday.
