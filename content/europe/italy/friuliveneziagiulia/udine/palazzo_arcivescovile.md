The Patriarchal (now Archbishop's) Palace contains the densest concentration of Tiepolo frescoes outside Venice. The grand staircase, the gallery of guests, and above all the **Sala del Trono** are covered with biblical scenes — the Judgement of Solomon, the Fall of the Rebel Angels — painted between 1726 and 1730 when Giambattista Tiepolo was in his twenties.

The palace also houses the **Diocesan Museum**, with painted wooden sculpture and devotional art from Friuli, and a richly decorated **library**. It is the best single museum visit in Udine. Open daily except Tuesday.
