The Duomo di Udine, dedicated to Santa Maria Annunziata, is a 14th-century Romanesque–Gothic foundation extensively reworked in the 18th century. Inside, the most interesting works are the altar pieces and frescoes by **Giambattista Tiepolo** — his early Udine paintings made his name.

Look up in the side chapels and ceiling cycles to find his bright, airy compositions. Adjacent is the **Oratorio della Purità**, whose Tiepolo *Assumption* ceiling is sometimes opened on request through the cathedral sacristy.
