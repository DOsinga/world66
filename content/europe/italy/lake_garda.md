Lake Garda is the largest lake in Italy and one of the most beautiful in Europe. Straddling three regions — Lombardy, Veneto, and Trentino — it offers something for almost every taste: medieval castles, Roman ruins, lemon groves, windsurfing beaches, and elegant Belle Époque resort towns.

The southern end around [Sirmione](/europe/italy/lombardia/sirmione) is the gentlest and most visited, with the [Scaligero Castle](/europe/italy/lombardia/sirmione/rocca_scaligera) jutting into the water and the ruins of a Roman villa at the [Grotte di Catullo](/europe/italy/lombardia/sirmione/grotte_di_catullo) at the tip of the peninsula. The eastern shore passes through Garda, [Lazise](/europe/italy/veneto/lazise), [Peschiera](/europe/italy/veneto/peschiera), and [Bardolino wine country](/europe/italy/lake_garda/bardolino_wine) — while the western Brescia shore has the most dramatic scenery, with cliffs dropping to the water. [Salò](/europe/italy/lombardia/salo), briefly the seat of Mussolini's puppet republic in 1943, and [Gardone Riviera](/europe/italy/lombardia/gardone) are its main towns; [Gargnano](/europe/italy/lake_garda/villa_bettoni_gargnano) and [Limone sul Garda](/europe/italy/lake_garda/limone_sul_garda) are the prettiest villages further north. The privately owned [Isola del Garda](/europe/italy/lake_garda/isola_del_garda) sits offshore with its neo-Gothic villa and terraced gardens.

At the northern end, [Riva del Garda](/europe/italy/trentinoaltoadige/riva_del_garda) and [Malcesine](/europe/italy/lake_garda/malcesine) see the strong thermal winds that make [windsurfing and kitesurfing](/europe/italy/lake_garda/windsurfing_north_lake) a European-level draw. The [lake ferry network](/europe/italy/lake_garda/lake_ferry) connects the main towns by water — the best way to travel and to see the lake. The [Vittoriale degli Italiani](/europe/italy/lombardia/gardone/vittoriale_degli_italiani) at Gardone Riviera is the most startling single attraction on the shore: the estate-monument of the poet d'Annunzio, equal parts megalomaniac art project and serious museum. The [olive groves](/europe/italy/lake_garda/olive_oil_and_lemon) of the western shore produce one of Italy's finest oils. Verona is only 30 km from the southern shore and makes an easy day trip, especially if you're timing a visit around the opera season in the Roman amphitheatre.
