---
title: When to Go
type: section
---

Anzio works well as a day trip from Rome year-round, but as a beach resort it is best from June through September. July and August bring crowds and heat; late May, June, and September are better — warm enough for the sea, without the summer crush. The war cemeteries and Roman ruins are fine in any season and arguably more contemplative out of summer.

The fish restaurants are open year-round and tend to be better value in the off-season, when they are feeding locals rather than weekend visitors from Rome.
