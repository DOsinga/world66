The Basilica of Santa Cristina is built over the site where, by tradition, the early Christian martyr Cristina was thrown into Lake Bolsena by her father around 300 AD, survived, and was subsequently executed. The church has layers going back to the 11th century, with a Romanesque facade and frescoed interior that have been added to and reworked over the centuries.

Below the main church is the grotto of Santa Cristina, a catacomb-like space carved from tufa rock, where the saint was reportedly imprisoned. The atmosphere down here is genuinely ancient: rough walls, low ceilings, and the smell of stone and candles. The miracle altar — the spot where the Eucharistic miracle occurred in 1263 — is accessible through the grotto.
