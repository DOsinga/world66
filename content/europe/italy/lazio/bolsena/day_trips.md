Bolsena sits in a rich corner of Lazio within easy reach of some of central Italy's best day-trip destinations. Orvieto is 30 minutes north — its cathedral facade and underground tufa caves justify a full day. Civita di Bagnoregio, the "dying city" perched on an eroding tufa outcrop, is about 20km away. Viterbo, with its intact medieval papal quarter, is 30km south.
