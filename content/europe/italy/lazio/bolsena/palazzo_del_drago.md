Palazzo del Drago (also known as Palazzo Netti) is a Renaissance palace on Via Francesco Cozza, one of the better-preserved aristocratic buildings in Bolsena's old town. The facade is relatively restrained, as Italian Renaissance palaces go, but it gives a sense of the town's past importance as a stopping point on the Via Francigena — the medieval pilgrimage road from Canterbury to Rome.

The building is not regularly open for public visits, but it is worth pausing to look at the exterior and the narrow street it sits on, which retains much of its medieval character.
