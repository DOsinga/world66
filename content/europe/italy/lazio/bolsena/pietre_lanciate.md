About 2km south of Bolsena, on the road toward Montefiascone, a field of columnar basalt rises abruptly from the ground. These hexagonal stone prisms — up to 3 metres tall and packed together like a geometric puzzle — are the result of volcanic activity that shaped the whole Viterbo region. They are one of the stranger and less-visited geological sites in central Italy.

The site is freely accessible from the road and takes about 20 minutes to walk around. It is best combined with a visit to Bolsena itself or as a stop on the way to or from Orvieto.
