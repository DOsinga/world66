---
title: Things to Do
type: section
---

The sights cluster in and around the old town, which sits on a tufa plateau cut off on three sides by ravines. The Forte Sangallo and the cathedral are the two headline stops; both are within easy walking distance of each other in the historic centre. For the broader Faliscan story, the Roman ghost-town of Falerii Novi is a short drive west and rewards the effort.
