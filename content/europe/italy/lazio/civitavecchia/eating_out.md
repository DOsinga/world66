---
title: Eating Out
type: section
---

Civitavecchia's restaurants serve solid Lazio cooking with a Tyrrhenian seafood lean: spaghetti alle vongole, fritto misto, grilled fish, and the regional standards (cacio e pepe, amatriciana, carbonara). The waterfront places are touristy and overpriced; the better trattorias are a few blocks inland in the historic centre.
