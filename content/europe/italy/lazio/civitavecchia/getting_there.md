---
title: Getting There
type: section
---

Civitavecchia is 60 km northwest of Rome on the Tyrrhenian coast. **Trains** from Roma Termini run every 20-40 minutes and take 50-80 minutes depending on whether you take an express (Intercity) or a regional. The station is a 15-minute walk south of the cruise terminal. By **car**, the A12 motorway runs from Rome; the exit is signposted from the city centre.

The **cruise port** is one of the busiest in the Mediterranean — most major lines call here as a Rome stop. The terminal building has shuttle buses to the centre of town (free), and a private train (Civitavecchia Express, seasonal) runs direct from the port to Rome. **Ferries** to Sardinia (Olbia, Cagliari, Porto Torres), Sicily (Palermo), Tunisia (Tunis) and Barcelona depart daily from Stazione Marittima.
