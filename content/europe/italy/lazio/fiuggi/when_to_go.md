---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

The spa season runs from May through October. Spring and early autumn are the best times to visit — temperatures are comfortable for walking the hillside, the parks are in good shape, and the springs are busy but not uncomfortably crowded. Late July and August bring real heat, though the altitude of Fiuggi Città (around 750 metres) keeps things more bearable than the Roman plain below.

Winter is quiet to the point of being closed. Most spa facilities shut down between November and April, and the Parco della Fonte loses much of its appeal in the cold. If you find yourself in Lazio in winter, Fiuggi makes a reasonable day trip to see the medieval town, but you will not be taking the waters.
