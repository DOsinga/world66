Saint Francis Church stands high above Gaeta, reached by a broad staircase that makes the approach part of the visit. The church's pale facade and elevated position give it a theatrical presence, especially when seen from the harbour or the lanes below.

Inside, the visit is fairly short, but the setting carries it. Come for the architecture, the climb, and the view back over Gaeta's old town, port, and surrounding coast.
