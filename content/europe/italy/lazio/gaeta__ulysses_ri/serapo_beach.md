Serapo Beach is Gaeta's everyday seaside stage: a long curve of sand just below town, with the headland and the Ulysses Riviera giving it a more dramatic frame than a simple resort beach. It is easy, central, and popular for good reason.

In summer it can feel busy and organised, with paid sections and families filling the shore. Outside the peak, it becomes one of the best places in Gaeta for a walk, a swim, or a lazy hour after visiting the old town and Monte Orlando.
