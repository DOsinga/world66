---
title: When to Go
type: section
---

Gaeta has a classic Mediterranean climate: hot, dry summers and mild, wetter winters. The high season runs from late June through August, when Italian families descend on Serapo beach and accommodation prices spike. The town is at its most animated and most crowded at this time.

**May, June, and September** are the best months to visit. Temperatures are warm enough for swimming — the sea reaches around 24°C in September — but the crowds are manageable and prices are lower. The old town is far more pleasant to explore in the relative cool of late spring or early autumn.

**Winter** (November to March) is quiet, with most beach bars and many restaurants closed. The old town and castle are still worth visiting, and you'll have the alleys largely to yourself. Temperatures rarely drop below 8°C.

**Easter** draws large crowds for the city's Holy Week processions, which are among the most atmospheric in Lazio.
