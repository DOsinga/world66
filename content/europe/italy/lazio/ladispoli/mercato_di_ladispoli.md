Ladispoli's Tuesday market (Mercato Settimanale) sprawls across the central streets and is one of the largest open-air markets in the area north of Rome. Locals come from surrounding towns to buy clothing, household goods, and fresh produce. The food section — vegetables, fish, cheese, and local olive oil — is the most interesting part.

It runs from early morning until early afternoon and is worth combining with a beach day if your visit falls on a Tuesday.
