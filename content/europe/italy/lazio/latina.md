Latina is a planned city about 60 kilometres south of [Rome](/europe/italy/lazio/rome), built in 1932 during the draining of the Pontine Marshes. The rationalist architecture of the [city centre](/europe/italy/lazio/latina/piazza_del_popolo) — all clean lines and wide piazzas — gives it a distinctive look quite unlike the older towns around it.

The city itself is not a major tourist draw, but it sits close to some excellent coastline. The beaches at Sabaudia and Sperlonga are among the best in Lazio, and the [Circeo National Park](/europe/italy/lazio/latina/circeo_national_park), with its forested promontory and coastal lakes, is a worthwhile day trip. Latina also makes a practical base for exploring the southern Lazio coast without Rome's prices or crowds.
