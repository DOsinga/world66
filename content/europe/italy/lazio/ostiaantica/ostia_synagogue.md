The Ostia Synagogue stands in the ancient port of Rome and is one of the oldest known synagogues outside the land of Israel. Its ruins date back to the 1st century, when Ostia was a busy harbour city drawing merchants, sailors, and migrant communities from around the Mediterranean.

It sits away from the main cluster of Ostia Antica ruins, so give yourself a little extra time if you want to seek it out.
