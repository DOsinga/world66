Abbey of the Three Fountains is a quiet monastic complex near EUR with three churches set away from the busy roads around it. The site is linked to the tradition of St. Paul's martyrdom and still has an active religious community.

It makes a calm detour from EUR's modern architecture, with enough history and atmosphere to justify the short trip.
