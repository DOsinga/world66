A collection of short stories set among the working-class Romans of Trastevere, Testaccio, and the outer boroughs in the 1950s. Moravia writes about bus drivers, prostitutes, black marketeers, and domestic servants with precise, unsentimental affection. These are the Romans who lived in the same streets as the tourists' Rome but inhabited a completely different city — and many of them are still here.
