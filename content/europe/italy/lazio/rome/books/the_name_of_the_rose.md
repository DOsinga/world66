A medieval murder mystery set in a Benedictine monastery in northern Italy, but the world it inhabits — papal intrigue, theological argument, the politics of Rome — is essential context for understanding the city's relationship with the Church. Eco was a professor of semiotics at the University of Bologna, and the novel's detective work unfolds through the logic of signs and texts that still shape how Rome presents itself to the world.
