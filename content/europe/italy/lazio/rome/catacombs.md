Rome has some 60 catacombs, most on the southern roads out of the city. The most visited are on the Via Appia Antica: the Catacombs of San Callisto (the official papal burial ground until the 5th century) and the Catacombs of San Sebastiano next door. Guided tours in multiple languages descend into the tufa-carved corridors past loculi — shelf-graves cut into the walls — and early Christian frescoes. Take a bus from Circus Maximus; the road itself is one of the finest walks outside the city centre.
