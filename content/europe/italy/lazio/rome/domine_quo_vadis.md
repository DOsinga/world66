Domine Quo Vadis is a small 17th-century church on the Appian Way, traditionally linked to Saint Peter's vision of the risen Christ while fleeing Rome. Inside is a copy of a stone said to bear Christ's footprints; the original is kept farther along the road at San Sebastiano.
