Fontana delle Tartarughe, the Turtle Fountain, sits in a quieter square between Campo de' Fiori and the Jewish Ghetto. Its bronze turtles were a late fix: the original dolphins were too large for the water pressure, so the smaller turtles became the detail everyone remembers.
