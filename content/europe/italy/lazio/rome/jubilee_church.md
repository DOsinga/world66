Jubilee Church is a striking modern church by Richard Meier, built in 2003 as part of a project to revive the Tor Tre Teste area. Its white concrete sails make it one of Rome's clearest breaks from the city's ancient and Baroque habits, and architecture-minded visitors will get the most from the detour.
