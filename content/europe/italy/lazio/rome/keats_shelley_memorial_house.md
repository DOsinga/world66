The Keats-Shelley Memorial House occupies the rooms beside the Spanish Steps where John Keats died in 1821. It is a small, quiet museum of manuscripts, portraits, letters, and first editions tied to Keats, Shelley, Byron, and the Romantic poets who made Rome part of their story.
