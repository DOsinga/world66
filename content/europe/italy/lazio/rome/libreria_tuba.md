Libreria Tuba defies a single category: part feminist and queer bookshop, part bazaar selling zines, prints, and second-hand objects, part bar open until 01:00. It hosts book launches, political debates, exhibitions, and music events, and is probably the best single indicator of what Pigneto actually stands for beyond the aperitivo bars. Open afternoons Tuesday and Saturday, evenings daily except Monday.
