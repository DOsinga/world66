MACRO is Rome's municipal museum of contemporary art, set in a former industrial complex north of Termini. Its exhibitions change regularly, with a focus on modern Italian and international work rather than Rome's ancient and Baroque collections.

The museum is useful when you want a break from churches, ruins, and old-master galleries. Check the current program before going, since the temporary exhibitions are the main draw.
