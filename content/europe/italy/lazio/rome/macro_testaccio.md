MACRO Testaccio is the contemporary art museum's outpost in Rome's old slaughterhouse complex. The setting is part of the appeal: brick halls, industrial ironwork, and traces of the 19th-century abattoir give the exhibitions a rougher edge than the city's more polished museum spaces.

It works best if you are already exploring Testaccio, where food markets, Roman working-class history, and new cultural spaces sit close together.
