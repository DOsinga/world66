Mezzo is built around vermouth — served neat, on ice, or in well-made cocktails — with a serious list of natural wines and a small menu of plates designed for a long aperitivo. The fitout is stripped-back industrial: bare concrete, low lighting, exposed metal. It opens from 18:00 and suits a pre-dinner or all-evening visit. Consistently recommended as the tightest drinks operation on the Via del Pigneto strip.
