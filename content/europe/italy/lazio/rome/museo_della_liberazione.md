The Museo della Liberazione records Rome under German occupation and the city's liberation during the Second World War. The building itself was used as a prison by the occupying forces, which gives the exhibits a direct and sombre setting.

It is a small museum rather than a broad military-history collection, but it is one of the more focused places in Rome for understanding the occupation years. Accessibility equipment is available, but visitors who need it should call ahead.
