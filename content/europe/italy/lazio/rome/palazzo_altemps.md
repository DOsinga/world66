Palazzo Altemps is a refined Renaissance palace near Piazza Navona and one of the branches of the National Roman Museum. Its galleries focus on ancient sculpture collected and restored by Roman noble families, so the appeal is both the art and the way later collectors reshaped antiquity to suit Renaissance and Baroque taste.
