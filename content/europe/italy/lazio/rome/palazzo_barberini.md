Palazzo Barberini is a major Baroque palace partly designed by Bernini and now home to part of the National Gallery of Ancient Art. The collection includes important works by Filippo Lippi, Caravaggio, and Raphael, including La Fornarina. The building itself is part of the visit, with grand rooms, staircases, and ceiling decoration from Rome's Barberini era.
