The Palazzo di Giustizia is Rome's monumental Palace of Justice, standing beside the Tiber at the edge of Prati. Its heavy late-nineteenth-century architecture has long divided opinion, but the scale, riverside setting, and legal history make it a notable landmark on the walk between Castel Sant'Angelo and central Rome.
