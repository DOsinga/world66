Quarantuno sits directly on the pedestrianised stretch of Via del Pigneto and was one of the bars that established the aperitivo scene here. The format is classic: arrive from 17:30 for wine or a Campari, eat from a Roman and Lazio menu later in the evening, and stay as long as the outdoor chairs stay warm. Craft beers complement the wine list. Closed Mondays.
