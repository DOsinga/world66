The L. Pigorini National Museum of Prehistory and Ethnography holds a large collection covering prehistoric Europe and ethnographic material from outside Europe. Its displays trace human life from the Palaeolithic through the Iron Age.

It is one of the more specialist museums in EUR, useful for visitors interested in archaeology and early cultures.
