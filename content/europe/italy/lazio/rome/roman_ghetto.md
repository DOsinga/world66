The Roman Ghetto lies between the Tiber Island and the Theatre of Marcellus. Established in 1555 and enclosed until the end of the Papal States, it remains one of Rome's most layered historic quarters, with the Great Synagogue, Roman ruins, narrow streets, and long-running Jewish restaurants close together.
