Robert Hughes's Rome (2011) is a comprehensive and passionately argued tour of the city's art and architecture, from ancient Rome through the Baroque period. Hughes brings the same critical intelligence and emotional investment to Bernini, Caravaggio, and Michelangelo that he brought to Australian art and American culture. His account of Bernini's transformation of St Peter's Square, and of Caravaggio's savage, revolutionary paintings in Roman churches, is some of the finest art writing of the past half century. A heavy book to carry, but excellent to read before you arrive.
