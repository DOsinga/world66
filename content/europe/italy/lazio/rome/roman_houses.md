The Roman Houses preserve domestic rooms from the first century, with frescoes, foundations, and small museum displays that show how wealthy Romans lived near the Caelian Hill. The site is quieter than the nearby Colosseum and Forum, and works best for visitors who want a more intimate look at ancient Rome.
