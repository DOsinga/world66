San Francesco a Ripa is a Trastevere church named for St Francis of Assisi, who stayed nearby. Its main draw is Bernini's statue of the Blessed Ludovica Albertoni in the Paluzzi-Albertoni Chapel.

The church is a good stop while walking Trastevere, especially for visitors interested in Baroque sculpture without the crowds of Rome's headline churches.
