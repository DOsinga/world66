Sant'Andrea della Valle is a 17th-century Baroque basilica near Campo de' Fiori, with a grand dome and strong interior art. It is not one of Rome's essential churches, but it is a rewarding stop when walking between Piazza Navona, Largo Argentina and Campo de' Fiori.
