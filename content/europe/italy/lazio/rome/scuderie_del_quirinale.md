Scuderie del Quirinale is the former stables of the Quirinal Palace, now used as a major exhibition venue. Its temporary shows often focus on art, archaeology, and cultural history.

Check the current exhibition before going. The building is near the Trevi Fountain and Trajan's Market, so it fits easily into a central Rome walk.
