The Scuderie del Quirinale occupies the former stables of the Quirinal Palace, now converted into one of Rome's better temporary exhibition spaces. Its shows are usually built around major art, archaeology, or cultural-history themes.

Check the current programme before making a special trip. When the exhibition is strong, the location beside the Quirinal Palace makes it easy to combine with a walk through central Rome.
