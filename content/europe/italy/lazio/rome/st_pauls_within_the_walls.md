St Paul's Within the Walls is Rome's Episcopalian church, begun in 1873 and unusual in a city where most church stops are far older.

The reason to go inside is the art, especially mosaics by Edward Burne-Jones of the Pre-Raphaelite school. It makes a good short detour near Termini and Via Nazionale.
