Set in a northern Italian monastery in 1327, Eco's debut novel is also a meditation on religious authority, the suppression of knowledge, and the culture that produced both the Papacy and its critics. While not set in Rome, it is the essential novel for understanding the medieval Church that built and rebuilt the city. The theological disputes, the power of the Vatican archives, and the relationship between worldly and spiritual authority all illuminate what you are looking at in Rome's churches and papal buildings.
