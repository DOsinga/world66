The Tomb of Priscilla is a small 1st-century Roman tomb on the Via Appia, later absorbed into a pair of medieval farm buildings. It is easy to miss behind its wall, and access is limited, but it makes a worthwhile stop for travellers following the ancient road beyond the city gates.
