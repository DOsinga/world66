Trattoria Pigneto is the unpretentious local choice on the pedestrian strip — porchetta, polpette al sugo, cacio e pepe, and seasonal Roman plates at around €10 a course. No reservations, no fuss. It represents the other side of Pigneto from the cocktail bars: the neighbourhood that still eats like a working-class quarter, a few steps from the aperitivo crowd.
