Villa Doria Pamphili is one of Rome's largest public parks, spread across the grounds of a historic villa west of Trastevere. Romans come here for long walks, jogging, and quiet lawns, while visitors get a break from the dense historic centre and a look at one of the city's great landscaped estates.
