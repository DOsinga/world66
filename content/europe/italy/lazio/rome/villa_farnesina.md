Villa Farnesina is a Renaissance villa in Trastevere, built for the Sienese banker Agostino Chigi and later owned by the Farnese family. Its rooms are decorated with major fresco cycles, including work by Raphael, and offer a quieter art stop than Rome's largest museums.

The Loggia of Cupid and Psyche is the highlight for many visitors. Pair the villa with a walk through Trastevere or across the river toward Campo de' Fiori.
