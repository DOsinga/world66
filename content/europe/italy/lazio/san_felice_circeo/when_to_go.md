---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

**June to September** is beach season, and July and August bring Italian summer crowds — the beaches fill, the harbour is busy with boat trips, and the village restaurants run at capacity. The sea is warm from late June through early October.

**May and June** before the main crowds arrive are arguably the best weeks: the sea is warming up, the national park is green, and the village is not overrun. Wildflowers on Monte Circeo are good in April and May.

**September and October** are excellent for hiking — the summer heat has broken, the trails are quiet, and the sea is still swimmable. October is ideal for birdwatching, with migrating raptors using the Monte Circeo promontory as a stopover on their crossing of the Tyrrhenian.

**Winter** is very quiet. Most restaurants and beach facilities close from November through Easter. The old hilltop village has a certain gloomy charm in winter, but there is not much open.
