---
title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Terracina's big draw is the [Temple of Jupiter Anxur](/europe/italy/lazio/terracina/temple_of_jupiter_anxur) on the hilltop above the old town, with its sweeping views over the Tyrrhenian Sea. Down below, the old town's piazza sits directly on top of the old Roman forum — the ancient stonework is visible right beneath your feet. The beach is long and sandy and the town is a quieter alternative to the Amalfi Coast for a seaside day or overnight stop.
