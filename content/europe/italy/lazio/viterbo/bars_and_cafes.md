---
title: "Bars and Cafes"
type: section
---

Viterbo's bar culture centres on the historic centre, particularly around Piazza del Plebiscito and the streets near the Fontana Grande. The city has a student population from the Università della Tuscia that keeps the cafes busy and the aperitivo circuit alive in the evenings. Gran Caffè Schenardi, operating since 1818, is the most historic establishment in town.
