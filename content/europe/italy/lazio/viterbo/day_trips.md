Viterbo sits at the centre of Tuscia — the old Etruscan heartland — and the surrounding countryside is full of places worth a half-day or full-day excursion.

[Lake Bolsena](/europe/italy/lazio/bolsena), 30 km north, is the largest volcanic lake in Europe, ringed by medieval villages and with remarkably clear water. The town of Bolsena itself is pleasant, with a well-preserved castle and the Basilica of Santa Cristina, site of an important Eucharistic miracle in 1263 that inspired the Feast of Corpus Christi.

[Tarquinia](/europe/italy/lazio/tarquinia) is the greatest Etruscan site in Lazio, about 45 km southwest. The Necropolis of Tarquinia, a UNESCO World Heritage Site, contains painted tomb chambers from the 7th to 2nd centuries BC — some of the finest Etruscan painting to survive anywhere.

[Lake Vico](/europe/italy/lazio/lakevico), immediately south of Viterbo, is the smallest and most intimate of the volcanic lakes in northern Lazio, enclosed within a nature reserve. The crater rim makes for good walking and the surrounding beech and chestnut woods are beautiful in autumn.

[Civitavecchia](/europe/italy/lazio/civitavecchia), an hour west, is the main port for ferries to Sardinia and is worth visiting for its impressive Michelangelo-designed fortress and the Roman imperial baths.
