Villa Lante is one of the great Renaissance garden visits in central Italy, set at Bagnaia just outside Viterbo. The pleasure is not a single grand villa but the disciplined choreography of water, stone, clipped greenery, and terraces descending through the garden.

Come for the fountains and stay for the geometry. The design turns movement into theatre: water runs from upper cascades to pools and basins, while paths frame views with almost mathematical calm. It is an essential stop if you are using Viterbo as a base for the Tuscia region.
