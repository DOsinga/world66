Situated on a wooded plateau in the upper valley of the Bormida di Millesimo, Bardineto is a quiet Ligurian hill town with more history than its size suggests. It became a fief of the Del Carretto of Balestrino in the thirteenth century, and in 1795 was the site of a battle between the Austrians and the French under General Masséna.

Little remains of the polygonal feudal castle — it had sixteen sides — or the medieval fortifications. The [Cappella di San Nicolò](/europe/italy/liguria/bardineto/cappella_san_nicolo), on the hill of the same name, dates from the eleventh century and houses fifteenth-century frescoes. The [parish church](/europe/italy/liguria/bardineto/chiesa_parrocchiale) was built in 1720. The surrounding [chestnut and beech forests](/europe/italy/liguria/bardineto/foreste_di_castagni) make for good walking.
