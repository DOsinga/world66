---
title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Most visitors come to Bocca di Magra to use the boats — to the Cinque Terre, Portovenere, [Punta Corvo](/europe/italy/liguria/bocca_di_magra/punta_corvo), Palmaria, or simply along the coast — and to walk in the [Montemarcello-Magra Natural Park](/europe/italy/liguria/bocca_di_magra/montemarcello_magra_park), which covers the wooded headland on the opposite side of the river. A network of easy trails leads up through pine and holm-oak to the hill village of [Montemarcello](/europe/italy/liguria/bocca_di_magra/montemarcello), with views over the gulf and the Apuan Alps. The [Monastero di Santa Croce](/europe/italy/liguria/bocca_di_magra/monastero_santa_croce), on top of the hill above the village, has a pretty chapel and one of the finest views in the gulf, and the [Orto Botanico di Montemarcello](/europe/italy/liguria/bocca_di_magra/orto_botanico) is a small but well-tended coastal garden. On the water, there are dive schools, small-boat hire, and the usual summer line-up of kayaks, paddle-boards and sailing lessons.
