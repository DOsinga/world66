---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

Bocca di Magra is a summer place — most restaurants, the beach clubs, and the ferries to Punta Corvo and the Cinque Terre run from Easter to October, with the high season in July and August. June and September are the sweet spot: water warm enough to swim, restaurants and ferries operating, and far fewer day-trippers than at the peak.

The natural park behind the village is rewarding year-round and especially good for walking in April and May, when the *macchia* is flowering. Winters are mild but very quiet — most of the seafront closes between November and Easter and the village turns into a sleepy collection of weekend houses.
