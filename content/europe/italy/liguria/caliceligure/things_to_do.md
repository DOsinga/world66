title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Calice Ligure is a quiet agricultural village rather than a destination in itself, but there are a handful of things worth seeing if you are in the area. The [parish church of San Nicolò](/europe/italy/liguria/caliceligure/chiesa_san_nicolo) is the architectural centrepiece — an imposing baroque building with a large dome, built in the eighteenth century over a much older foundation. The [earthworks at Pian dei Corsi](/europe/italy/liguria/caliceligure/pian_dei_corsi) are a remnant of the Napoleonic campaigns through Liguria.

The surrounding countryside is well-suited to walking and cycling. The valley floor is planted with peach orchards and vineyards; the hillsides above carry the olives that produce the local oil. Trails connect the village to the coast at Finale Ligure, about 8 kilometres south, and the hike down makes a good one-way outing.
