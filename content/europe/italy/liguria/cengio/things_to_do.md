type: section
---

Cengio is a small town for walkers and those interested in industrial history. The forested hills above the valley offer marked trails through mixed woodland, and the town's medieval and Spanish-era ruins are worth a look. The Bormida di Millesimo valley was heavily industrialised in the 19th and 20th centuries, and Cengio was the site of a major chemical plant whose legacy still shapes the local environment.
