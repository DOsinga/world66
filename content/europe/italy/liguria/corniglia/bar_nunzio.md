The main café on Largo Taragio, with a handful of tables under sun umbrellas in the square. It is the place locals come for an espresso in the morning and an aperitivo at sunset, and the small kitchen turns out decent panini and bruschette through the day.

Order a glass of Cinque Terre DOC at the counter, walk out into the square, and you have the village's social life around you for about five euros.
