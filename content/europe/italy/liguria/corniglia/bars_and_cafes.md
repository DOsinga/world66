---
title: "Bars and Cafes"
type: section
---

Corniglia is not a nightlife village. By 10pm most of the trattorias have shut and Via Fieschi is quiet. What it does well is the early evening — a glass of Cinque Terre white on the Belvedere or in Largo Taragio, watching the light go out of the cliffs.

The local dessert wine **Sciacchetrà** is the thing to try at least once: amber-coloured, raisin-sweet, and made from grapes dried on wooden racks for two months before pressing. A small glass after dinner is the traditional Cinque Terre nightcap.
