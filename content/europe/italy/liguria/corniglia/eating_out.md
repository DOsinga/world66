---
title: "Eating Out"
type: section
---

Corniglia has a handful of restaurants and bars — fewer than its four neighbours, which is part of its appeal. The best dishes are pesto with trofie pasta, fresh anchovies, and the seafood caught off the cliffs below. Several places also serve simple focaccia and farinata, the Ligurian chickpea flatbread, which make ideal hiking provisions.
