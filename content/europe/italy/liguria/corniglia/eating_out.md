---
title: "Eating Out"
type: section
---

Corniglia has a handful of trattorias along Via Fieschi and a couple of takeaway windows for focaccia, fried anchovies, and seafood cones. Standards are high — the village is small enough that nobody can afford to serve a bad plate of trofie — but tables are limited, so book ahead in summer if you are eating dinner.

Local specialities to look for include **trofie al pesto** (the short twisted pasta of the region with the classic basil sauce), **acciughe ripiene** (stuffed anchovies), and the dessert wine **Sciacchetrà**, made from raisined grapes grown on the terraces above the village.
