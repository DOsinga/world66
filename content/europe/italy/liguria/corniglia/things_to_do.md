---
title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Corniglia is small enough that you can walk every street in an afternoon. The highlights are the medieval **Church of San Pietro** halfway along Via Fieschi, the **Belvedere di Santa Maria** at the village's seaward end (one of the best views on the Italian coast), and the **Largo Taragio** — the village's compact main square. The two trails out of the village, north to Vernazza and south to Manarola along the **Sentiero Azzurro**, are part of the experience even if you only do an hour of either.

There are no real beaches: **Guvano**, the long cove below the cliffs to the north, is hard to reach, and the small rocky landing at the **Marina** is for swimmers happy to scramble. The pleasures of Corniglia are slower — a glass of Sciacchetrà on the terrace, a walk through the vineyards, the early evening light coming up the cliffs.
