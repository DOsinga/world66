title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Diano Marina is not a city of museums or monuments — it is a beach town, and the beach is the main event. Beyond it, the hilltop villages above the resort strip are worth the short drive, especially [Diano Castello](/europe/italy/liguria/dianomarina/diano_castello) and [Cervo](/europe/italy/liguria/dianomarina/cervo). The [Civic Museum](/europe/italy/liguria/dianomarina/museo_civico) in Villa Ardissone offers a compact but worthwhile window into the area's ancient past.
