title: Eating Out
type: section
---

Dolceacqua cooking sits between Liguria and Provence — Genoese pasta and pesto, Niçois socca and stuffed vegetables, plenty of rabbit and snails. The Rossese di Dolceacqua red wine, produced from vines in the surrounding valley, is the local match for almost everything. A few honest trattorias, no luxury places, all in walking distance of the bridge.
