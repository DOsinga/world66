Museum of Modern Art - Villa Croce gives Genoa a focused modern and contemporary art stop in a villa setting above the central waterfront. Its exhibitions and collections move the city's art story beyond palaces, churches, and maritime history.

The museum is best for art-minded visitors who have already seen Genoa's historic core. The villa location also makes the visit feel like a change of pace, with a quieter hillside setting close to the centre.
