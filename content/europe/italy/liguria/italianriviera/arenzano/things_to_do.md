title: Things to Do
type: section
---

Arenzano's main sights cluster in and around the town centre. The [Santuario di Gesù Bambino di Praga](/europe/italy/liguria/italianriviera/arenzano/santuario_gesu_bambino) is a busy pilgrimage church with an impressive permanent nativity scene; the park of [Villa Negrotto Cambiaso](/europe/italy/liguria/italianriviera/arenzano/villa_negrotto_cambiaso) is a free, shaded escape from the heat. The [Lungomare](/europe/italy/liguria/italianriviera/arenzano/lungomare) is the place for an evening walk, and [Punta San Martino](/europe/italy/liguria/italianriviera/arenzano/cape_san_martino) the best spot for sunset.

For a more active day, the [Parco del Beigua](/europe/italy/liguria/italianriviera/arenzano/beigua_park) starts right behind town and climbs into a serious mountain landscape — a UNESCO Geopark with hiking trails for all levels.
