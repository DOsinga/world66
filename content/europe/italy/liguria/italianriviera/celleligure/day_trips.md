---
title: "Day Trips"
type: section
---

**Albisola Marina and Superiore** (5 km east) — neighbouring twin towns famous for their ceramics. The Museo Manlio Trucco shows the local Renaissance majolica tradition, and there are still working ceramic studios you can visit.

**Varazze** (5 km east) — a livelier resort with a long pebble beach, a Romanesque church (Sant'Ambrogio) and a pleasant cycle path along the seafront on the line of the old railway.

**Genoa** (35 km east, 30 minutes by train) — the regional capital. UNESCO-listed Palazzi dei Rolli, the old port redesigned by Renzo Piano, the aquarium, and the great medieval centre. An easy day, returning to Celle for dinner.

**Savona** (8 km west) — an under-rated port city with a 16th-century fortress (Priamàr) and a baroque cathedral. Less polished than Genoa but interesting for the sense of a working Mediterranean port.

**Sacro Monte di Varallo and the entroterra** (1 hour inland) — the wooded valleys behind the coast offer cool relief from summer heat, with chestnut groves and small medieval villages such as Sassello, known for its amaretti.
