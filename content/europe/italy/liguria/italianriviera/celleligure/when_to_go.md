---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

The Italian beach season runs mid-June to mid-September, with July and August at peak temperatures (high 20s to low 30s) and peak crowds. Italian school holidays mean August is busiest and most expensive — beach clubs raise rates, restaurants take no reservations under three people, and the campsite at Capo Olmo fills weeks in advance.

For a quieter visit, come in early June or the second half of September: the water is still warm enough to swim, the beach clubs are open, and you will have far more room. May and early October are pleasant for walking the coastal path but the sea is bracing. The Borgo and its restaurants stay open year-round, though several seafront places close from November to Easter.
