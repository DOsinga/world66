The Chiesa dei Frati Cappuccini is a modest religious stop in Finale Ligure, set apart from the busier rhythm of beach, promenade, and old-town lanes. It is the sort of place that makes sense when you are already walking through town rather than chasing a checklist.

Its value is atmosphere: a quieter corner, a bit of local devotional history, and a different pace from the coast below. Pair it with a wander through Finalborgo or a slow climb above town if you want Finale to feel less like a seaside base and more like a lived-in Ligurian place.
