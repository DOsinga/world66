The cloisters of Santa Caterina are one of Finalborgo's key historic interiors, a restored convent complex folded into the medieval village. The arcaded courtyard, pale stone, and quiet proportions make a good contrast to the busier lanes just outside.

The complex also houses civic museum collections, including material tied to Finale's archaeology and local history. Visit it alongside Finalborgo's walls and San Biagio rather than treating it as a detached museum stop.
