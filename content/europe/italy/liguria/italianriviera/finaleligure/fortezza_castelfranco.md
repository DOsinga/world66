Fortezza di Castelfranco rises above Finale Ligure with the blunt confidence of a coastal fortress. Its position explains its purpose immediately: it watched the town, the shore, and the route inland, and today it gives visitors one of the best architectural viewpoints in Finale.

The fortress is most satisfying when combined with a walk through Finalborgo or the seafront, because it helps connect the beach town with its older defensive landscape. Check access and exhibition details before going; even when interiors are limited, the exterior and views justify the climb.
