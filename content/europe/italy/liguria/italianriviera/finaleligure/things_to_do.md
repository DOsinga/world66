---

Finale Ligure's appeal is unusually varied for a Ligurian resort town. The beach and promenade are obvious, but above town the limestone landscape opens up a world of mountain biking, rock climbing, and hiking through a karst terrain riddled with caves. [Finalborgo](/europe/italy/liguria/italianriviera/finaleligure/finalborgo) — the medieval walled centre 2 km inland — is one of the most attractive villages on the Riviera and worth at least an afternoon. The [Arene Candide cave](/europe/italy/liguria/italianriviera/finaleligure/arene_candide) and the [Civic Museum](/europe/italy/liguria/italianriviera/finaleligure/civic_museum_finale) tell the story of some of Europe's earliest inhabitants.
