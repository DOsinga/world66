---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

The best time to visit Noli is late spring (May-June) or early autumn (September-October), when the weather is warm enough for the beach, the sea is swimmable, and the crowds are manageable. July and August are hot and busy, with parking difficult and the beach packed at weekends, though the evenings are lively and the restaurants are all open.

Winter is mild by Italian standards — frosts are rare — and the town has a quiet, lived-in quality when the tourists have gone. Most restaurants and bars stay open year-round for locals. The spring focaccia and pesto season is a reason in itself to visit: the fresh basil arrives in April and the best places make their pesto fresh daily.
