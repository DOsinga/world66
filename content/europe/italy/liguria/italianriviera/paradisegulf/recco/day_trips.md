Recco's location, on the Genoa side of the Portofino headland, makes it a useful base for the whole gulf. [Camogli](/europe/italy/liguria/italianriviera/paradisegulf/camogli) is one stop south on the railway and a forty-minute walk along the coast path; the rocky harbour and pastel houses are the postcard view of the Riviera. [Portofino](/europe/italy/liguria/italianriviera/paradisegulf/portofino) is twenty minutes by car around the headland, or reachable by ferry from Camogli. [Genoa](/europe/italy/liguria/genoa) is twenty minutes by train in the other direction.
