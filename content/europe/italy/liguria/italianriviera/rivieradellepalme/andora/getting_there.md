---
title: Getting There
type: section
---

Andora is on the main coastal railway line between Genoa and Ventimiglia. The Andora railway station is close to the beach and receives regular services throughout the day; journey time from Genoa is about ninety minutes, from Ventimiglia about forty minutes. The station is walking distance from both the centre and the beach.

By car, Andora is on the A10 autostrada with its own exit. The drive from Genoa takes about an hour; from the French border at Ventimiglia, about thirty minutes. Parking in summer can be tight near the beach, and the marina area fills early. The town has a free parking area a few hundred metres from the waterfront.
