---
title: Things to Do
type: section
---

Andora rewards those who look beyond its beach. The medieval hilltop village of Borgo Castello sits above the modern resort and contains the main historical sights: a Romanesque church, a gate tower, and the ruins of a governor's castle. The Merula River valley offers easy walks through olive groves. The nearby village of Colla Micheri, hidden in the hills a few kilometres inland, is one of the most atmospheric medieval hamlets on this stretch of the coast.
