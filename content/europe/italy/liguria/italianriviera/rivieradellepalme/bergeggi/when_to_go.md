---
title: When to Go
type: section
---

The Ligurian coast has mild winters and hot summers. For beach and diving, June through September is the season, with July and August the busiest months. Even then, Bergeggi stays calmer than the larger resorts nearby — but the small beach does fill up on summer weekends.

May and September are arguably the best months: the water is warm enough to swim and dive in, the crowds are thin, and the light on the island is excellent in the late afternoon. In autumn the coastal path above the beach is pleasant for walking, with views back to the island from the headland.

Winter is quiet. Most bars and restaurants along the beach close from October to April, though the village above carries on its ordinary life.
