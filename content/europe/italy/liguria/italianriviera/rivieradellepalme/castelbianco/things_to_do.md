---
title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Castelbianco is a tiny collection of hamlets in the lower Pennavaire valley — the main reason to come is the restored medieval village of Colletta di Castelbianco, which has become an interesting experiment in rural revival. Walking in the surrounding hills, visiting the old churches, and staying overnight at one of the small local accommodation options are the extent of what's on offer — which is precisely the appeal for those who want the genuine Ligurian backcountry.
