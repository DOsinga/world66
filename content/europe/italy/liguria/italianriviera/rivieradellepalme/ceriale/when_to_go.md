---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

Summer (June to August) is peak season; the beach is busy, accommodation is expensive, and the promenade is lively. May and September are better for walking, sightseeing, and eating — the weather is warm but the crowds are thinner. In winter, Ceriale quiets almost entirely; many restaurants and hotels close. The asparagus season in spring (April–May) is a good time to eat in the surrounding Piana di Albenga.
