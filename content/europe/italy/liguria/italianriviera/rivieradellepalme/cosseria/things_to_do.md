---
title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Cosseria has two real attractions, both linked to its medieval past. The ruins of the **Castrum Crucis Ferrae** stand on the Montecala pass, still showing the original gate, walls, and part of the keep — a quiet site with a wide view across the upper Bormida valleys. The parish **Church of the Immacolata Concezione**, rebuilt in the mid-17th century, has a large rose window and a small but interesting set of period decoration.

Beyond the village, the **Bric Tana–Val Bormida regional park** and walking paths through chestnut forest offer easy, low-traffic hiking. The Napoleonic battlefield where the French defeated the Piedmontese in April 1796 is just south of the modern village; a few markers remain.
