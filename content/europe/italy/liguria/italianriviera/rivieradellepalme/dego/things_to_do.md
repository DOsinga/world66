---
title: 'Things to do'
type: section
---

There isn't a great deal in Dego itself, but the village is a pleasant short stop for travellers crossing the inland Liguria–Piedmont border. The 13th-century castle ruins on the hill north of the village give the best view across the Bormida valley, and the two parish churches in the centre — Sant'Ambrogio and San Rocco — are worth a brief look. The 1796 battle site, a few minutes' walk into the fields east of the village, is marked by a modest commemorative stone.
