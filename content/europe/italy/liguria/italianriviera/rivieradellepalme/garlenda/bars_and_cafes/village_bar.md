The local bar opens early for coffee and pastries. Espresso at the counter — the Italian way, standing up, done in two minutes — is the morning rhythm here. In the early evening the same space shifts to aperitivo: a glass of local white wine or a Campari soda, a few olives, and conversation among people who know each other. The pace and quiet are the point.
