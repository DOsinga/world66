The restaurant at the Castello della Meridiana hotel is a step up from the village trattorie in formality and price. The kitchen works in the Ligurian tradition — fresh pasta, local herbs, good olive oil — and the wine list includes Pigato and Vermentino from nearby producers. A good option for a special dinner without driving far. Booking is advisable.
