---
title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Garlenda is a genuinely quiet inland Ligurian village, and its appeal lies in the combination of things most coastal Riviera resorts lack: a real golf course, good local wine, proper walking country, and a baroque church with a Guercino painting. The pace is slow, the landscape attractive, and the coast is close enough that a beach day is easy without committing to it. For visitors who want Liguria without the summer crowds, this valley delivers.
