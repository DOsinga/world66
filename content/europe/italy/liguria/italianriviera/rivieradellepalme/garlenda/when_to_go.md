---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

Spring (April–June) and early autumn (September–October) are the best times to visit Garlenda. Temperatures are comfortable for walking, the vines and olive trees are in leaf, and the valley is at its most attractive. The coast nearby is busy in summer but Garlenda itself stays noticeably quieter than the beach towns — a practical advantage for anyone who wants to be close to the sea without being in the middle of it.

July and August are hot. Inland Liguria heats up considerably more than the coast and there is less sea breeze at this altitude. If you visit in high summer, walk early in the morning and save afternoons for the coast.

September is worth timing a trip around. The harvest season brings activity to the vineyards and the chestnuts begin to come down in October. The Pigato and Rossese grapes are picked in September, and some producers welcome visitors during harvest.
