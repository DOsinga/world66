---
title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Giusvalla rewards slow exploration on foot. The village is small enough to walk end to end in minutes, but the surrounding hills offer longer hikes through chestnut forests and terraced farmland. The two main points of historic interest — the [medieval castle ruins](/europe/italy/liguria/italianriviera/rivieradellepalme/giusvalla/castle_ruins) and the [church of San Matteo](/europe/italy/liguria/italianriviera/rivieradellepalme/giusvalla/chiesa_san_matteo) — sit within easy reach of the village centre.
