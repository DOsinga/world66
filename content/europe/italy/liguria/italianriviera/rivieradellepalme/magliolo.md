Magliolo is a quiet rural village inland from [Pietra Ligure](/europe/italy/liguria/italianriviera/rivieradellepalme/pietraligure), sitting on the left bank of the stream that shares its name. The economy runs on agriculture and forestry, much as it has for centuries.

The village has medieval roots: it was a possession of the bishops of Albenga in the tenth century before passing to the marchesi Del Carretto of Finale in 1142. The parish [Chiesa di San Lorenzo](/europe/italy/liguria/italianriviera/rivieradellepalme/magliolo/chiesa_san_lorenzo) is the obvious landmark; for active visitors, the limestone cliffs above the village host a well-developed network of [sport-climbing crags](/europe/italy/liguria/italianriviera/rivieradellepalme/magliolo/sport_climbing_crags) that have made Magliolo a small but steady fixture on the European climbing circuit. Otherwise, not much has changed in the essentials since the Middle Ages — this is a place for walking in quiet hills, not sightseeing.
