Magliolo is the kind of place you stay in to escape the crowded coast — but the coast is close enough to be the day's outing. **[Pietra Ligure](/europe/italy/liguria/italianriviera/rivieradellepalme/pietraligure)** and its beach are 15 minutes down the valley. **Finale Ligure**, half an hour east, is the better town: a medieval old quarter, a long pebble beach, and the largest sport-climbing area in Italy. The **Toirano Caves** to the south-west are a striking limestone show-cave system, and the small inland villages of **Calice Ligure** and **Rialto** make for unhurried back-road drives.
