The former abbey of Santa Maria at Fornelli, on the road toward the hamlet of Eremita, is the most significant building in the Mallare valley. Founded in 1179, it predates the town itself as an organised settlement, and its construction in Gothic-Lombard style — a nave with two aisles and heavy tunnel vaulting — reflects the architectural tastes of the Cistercian and Benedictine orders that spread across the Ligurian interior during the medieval period.

Inside, fragments of frescoes survive on the nave walls, faded but still legible in places — saints and devotional scenes from the 14th and 15th centuries. The building fell out of monastic use long ago and is now a listed monument. The exterior is plain stone, well-proportioned, and utterly unchanged by later additions — which makes it feel genuinely old in a way that more restored buildings rarely achieve.
