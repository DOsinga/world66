Two ruined medieval castles guard the valley approaches toward the San Giacomo pass — the road that historically connected Mallare to Finale Ligure and the sea. The Castello del Carruggio and the Castello del Miele face each other across the valley like a pair of broken brackets, both reduced to weathered stone walls and overgrown foundations but still legible enough to understand their original defensive logic.

The castles date to the period of Del Carretto rule in the 12th and 13th centuries, and their paired positioning suggests a gate-and-tower function: anyone travelling the pass would have been in sight of at least one of them at all times. Reaching them requires a short walk uphill from the village; the paths are unmarked but follow the ridgeline clearly enough. The views from both sites extend across the upper Bormida valley.
