---
title: Things to Do
type: section
---

Massimino is not the kind of place you visit for a packed itinerary. The appeal is precisely its smallness: a village of a few dozen souls surrounded by chestnut forests and mountain air, with a medieval hilltop above and a historic pass cutting through the ridgeline. Walkers, history-minded travellers, and those who simply want to sit still somewhere very quiet will find what they are looking for here.
