---
title: When to Go
type: section
---

Spring and early autumn are the best seasons to visit Massimino. In May and June the chestnut woods are green and the mountain air is mild; in September and October the chestnuts ripen and the surrounding forest takes on warm amber tones. Summer is pleasant at this altitude, considerably cooler than the coast below, though the village itself sees little tourist activity even in July and August.

August brings the village's annual festival celebrating local hospitality — a modest but genuine affair typical of the Ligurian interior, with food, communal tables, and the particular warmth of a community that does not see many strangers. Dates shift year to year; ask locally or check the comune of Massimino's notices. Winter closes in early on this north-facing part of the Tanaro valley, with snow a real possibility from December through February.
