---
title: "Things to Do in Murialdo"
type: section
---

Murialdo is a commune of scattered hamlets in the upper Bormida di Millesimo valley. There is no main town to speak of — just a handful of villages connected by narrow forest roads. The appeal is all landscape and local heritage: medieval ruins, an old church, and the chestnut woods that define the hills around Savona. The surrounding countryside belongs to the [Riviera delle Palme](/europe/italy/liguria/italianriviera/rivieradellepalme) hinterland, a short drive from [Millesimo](/europe/italy/liguria/italianriviera/rivieradellepalme/millesimo) and [Cairo Montenotte](/europe/italy/liguria/italianriviera/rivieradellepalme/cairomontenotte).
