---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

Onzo and the upper Arroscia valley are best visited in spring (April to June) and early autumn (September to October), when the walking is comfortable, the olive groves are at their greenest or preparing for harvest, and the Ligurian hills are clear. Summers are warm but manageable at this altitude — around 350 metres — and significantly cooler than the coast.

Avoid August if you prefer quiet: the coastal towns push visitors inland and the roads in the Ligurian hinterland get busier than you might expect. January and February can be cold, with occasional snow on the higher slopes around Monte Peso Grande, though Onzo itself rarely sees heavy snowfall.

The olive harvest runs from late October into November. If you time a visit for this period you will find the small local community engaged in one of the valley's oldest seasonal rituals.
