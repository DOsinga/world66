---
title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

There is not much to "do" in Orco Feglino in the conventional sense — it is a quiet hinterland comune of two old hamlets joined by terraced fields. What it offers is a slice of inland Liguria most coastal visitors never see: medieval stonework on a crag above the village, simple Romanesque churches, and a network of limestone crags above town that have made Finale Ligure one of Europe's premier sport climbing areas. Orco Feglino sits squarely in that climbing belt — the cliffs of Rocca di Perti and Rocca di Corno are within walking distance.
