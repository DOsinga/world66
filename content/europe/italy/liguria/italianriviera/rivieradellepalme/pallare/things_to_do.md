---
title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Pallare is a village to slow down in rather than tick off a list. The two churches — the parish church of San Marco and the older Oratory of the Santissima Annunziata in Biestro — are worth a look, and the wooded hills around the village have pleasant walking. The Val Bormida valley stretches south toward the coast and north into Piedmont, and Pallare sits in the middle of it, surrounded by chestnut and oak forest.
