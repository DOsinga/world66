---
title: When to Go
type: section
---

Piana Crixia is at its best from April through October. Spring brings wildflowers to the hillsides and the chestnuts are in full leaf by May. Summer is warm but the altitude — around 400 metres — keeps temperatures somewhat more moderate than the coast. Autumn is perhaps the finest season: the surrounding chestnut woods turn gold, mushroom season gets underway in the forests, and the grape harvest fills the valley. In winter the village is essentially quiet; some years there is snow.

The Fungo nature reserve is open year-round and accessible in all but the wettest conditions.
