---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

The summer months (June to August) bring Italian beach tourism in full force — the beach fills with lidos, the streets are busy, and prices rise. If you want the beach but not the crowds, late May and early September offer warm water and quieter conditions. Outside the beach season, Pietra Ligure is a peaceful town with good year-round access to the surrounding inland villages and hiking.

The Borgio Verezzi theatre festival runs in summer (typically July–August), when performances take place in the open-air square of the hilltop village just a few kilometres away.
