---
title: Getting There
type: section
---

Stellanello is most easily reached by car from the coast road. [Andora](/europe/italy/liguria/italianriviera/rivieradellepalme/andora) is the nearest town on the Via Aurelia; from there a narrow road climbs through olive groves to the village. There is no regular bus service. The drive up from Andora takes about ten minutes.

Parking is limited in the village; leave the car at the edge and walk in.
