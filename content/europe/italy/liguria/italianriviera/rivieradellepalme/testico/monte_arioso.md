Faint remains of a thirteenth-century castle crown the ridge above Testico. Not much is left — a few sections of wall, foundations softened by centuries of weather — but the site itself is worth the short walk for the panorama over the two valleys.

This was a frontier outpost in its day, controlling movement along the inland ridge route. Once you see the view, the choice of location makes obvious sense.
