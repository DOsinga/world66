---
title: Things to Do
type: section
---

Testico is a place for slow walks and long views. The village core is short on monuments — the [parish church of Santi Pietro e Paolo](/europe/italy/liguria/italianriviera/rivieradellepalme/testico/santi_pietro_e_paolo) and the faint ruins of the [castle of Monte Arioso](/europe/italy/liguria/italianriviera/rivieradellepalme/testico/monte_arioso) above the ridge are the main set-pieces.

The real attraction is the landscape: chestnut woods, pastures, and ridge tracks that open onto views of the Ligurian hinterland and, on clear days, the sea. Bring decent shoes and time.
