Urbe lies on the northern side of the Ligurian Apennines, scattered across the upper valley of the Olba stream. Once a possession of the Abbey of Tiglieto, it was donated to the Bishop of Acqui in the eleventh century and shared the fortunes of Sassello until 1798.

The hamlet of [San Pietro d'Olba](/europe/italy/liguria/italianriviera/rivieradellepalme/urbe/san_pietro_dolba) is a popular mountain resort with restaurants serving typical local cuisine. It draws visitors in summer and is famous for its mushroom picking in the surrounding forests. The seventeenth-century parish church of [San Giacomo](/europe/italy/liguria/italianriviera/rivieradellepalme/urbe/san_giacomo_church), in the locality of Martino d'Olba, contains decorations and a marble altar from the eighteenth century.

The valley sits below some of the best ridge walks in the Ligurian Apennines — [Passo del Faiallo](/europe/italy/liguria/italianriviera/rivieradellepalme/urbe/passo_del_faiallo) has one of the great views of the Ligurian coast, and the peaks of [Bric del Dente](/europe/italy/liguria/italianriviera/rivieradellepalme/urbe/bric_del_dente) and [Monte Reixa](/europe/italy/liguria/italianriviera/rivieradellepalme/urbe/monte_reixa) are both reachable on day hikes from the village.
