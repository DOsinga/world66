---
title: Eating Out
type: section
---

Urbe is mushroom country. In autumn, every trattoria in the valley features porcini in some form — risotto, tagliatelle, polenta, or simply grilled with parsley and garlic. Other staples are wild boar stew, chestnut-flour pasta (testaroli), and ricotta from the small local dairies. Most places are open only at weekends out of season.
