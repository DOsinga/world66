Monte Reixa, on the Beigua Park boundary, rises to 1,183 metres above the upper Olba valley. The summit and its rocky ridges are part of the Alta Via dei Monti Liguri, the long-distance trail that runs along the entire Ligurian ridge.

The walk up from the Passo del Faiallo takes about two hours. In spring the slopes are covered in flowering broom; in autumn they turn copper with the beech leaves.
