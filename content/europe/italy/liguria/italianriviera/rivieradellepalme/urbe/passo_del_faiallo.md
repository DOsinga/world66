At 1,044 metres on the watershed between the Olba and Polcevera valleys, Passo del Faiallo is one of the great viewpoints of the Ligurian Apennines. A small parking area at the top gives onto a sudden, vertiginous view down to Genoa, the coast, and the Mediterranean.

In summer, locals come up to escape the heat of the coast; in winter the pass is sometimes closed by snow but the view is at its best. A small refuge serves wood-fired pizza and farinata.
