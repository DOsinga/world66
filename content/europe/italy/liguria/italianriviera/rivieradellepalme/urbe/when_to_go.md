---
title: When to Go
type: section
---

Autumn is the high point of the year in Urbe — September and October bring the porcini and chestnut harvests, the woods turn copper and gold, and the air is sharp without being cold yet. Summer (June to August) is mild compared with the coast; Genoese families come up to escape the heat.

Winter snowfall closes parts of the higher roads; in early spring the trails become muddy with snowmelt. Late May into June is a fine quiet time for walking — wildflowers everywhere and few other people.
