---
title: When to Go
type: section
---

Varazze follows the standard Italian Riviera seasonal pattern, with most activity concentrated in July and August.

**July and August:** High season. The beach fills with Italian holiday makers, prices peak, and the town is at its most animated. The restaurants and bars stay open late. Accommodation must be booked well in advance.

**June and September:** The shoulder months are arguably the best time to visit. The sea is warm enough for swimming, the crowds are smaller, and the prices are more reasonable. The weather is generally reliable, with clear skies and temperatures in the mid-20s.

**May and October:** Spring and early autumn bring wildflowers in the Beigua mountains above the town and good conditions for hiking. The sea is cooler, and swimming is for the determined, but the light is beautiful and the town is quiet.

**Winter (November–March):** Most beach facilities close, and Varazze reverts to a quiet residential town. The mild Ligurian climate keeps temperatures above freezing except on rare occasions, and the seafront is pleasant for walking. Some restaurants close or reduce hours. It is a perfectly reasonable off-season base for exploring the western Ligurian coast.
