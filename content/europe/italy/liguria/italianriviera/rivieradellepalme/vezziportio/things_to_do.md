Vezzi Portio is a small village whose appeal is mainly its position in the Ligurian hinterland hills, above the coastal bustle. Walking, village atmosphere, and the nearby Borgio Verezzi caves are the main reasons to come. The coastal towns of Spotorno and Noli are 20–30 minutes drive below.
