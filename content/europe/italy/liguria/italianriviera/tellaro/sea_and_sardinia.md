Lawrence wrote much of his early Italian work from the cottage he and Frieda rented in Tellaro in 1913–14, including drafts of *Twilight in Italy*. *Sea and Sardinia* came later, but the same eye is at work: a stranger's irritated, alert account of a southern Europe that was still poor and very strange to an English reader.

Read it on the rocks below the church and the prose makes immediate sense.
