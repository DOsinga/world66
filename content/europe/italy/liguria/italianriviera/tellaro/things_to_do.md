---
title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Tellaro is small enough that the whole village is the attraction. The plan is simple: park in the upper car park, walk down through the caruggi to the seafront piazza, swim off the rocks, and stay for sunset. Above that, the Gulf of La Spezia opens up nearby walks — to the botanical garden at Montemarcello, along the cliff path to Lerici, or down to the river mouth at the Magra estuary.
