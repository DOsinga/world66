---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

May, June, and the first half of September are the best months. The weather is reliably warm, the light is long, the sea is swimmable, and the trail to Vernazza is usually open. Crowds are present but manageable — you can still find space on the beach and a table at a restaurant without too much difficulty.

July and August are extremely crowded and hot. The beach at Fegina fills wall-to-wall by mid-morning, the caruggi are jammed, and the coast trail becomes a slow procession. If you must visit in peak summer, arrive on the early train and leave before afternoon.

Winter is quiet and many restaurants and accommodation options close. The coastal path is often shut for maintenance after the autumn storms, and some of the ferry connections stop running. But the village itself is genuinely peaceful and the light in the off-season can be beautiful. The locals, freed from the seasonal rush, are noticeably more relaxed.
