A young Italian innkeeper on a tiny Ligurian cove encounters a dying American actress in 1962, setting off a story that moves between Italy and Hollywood across five decades. The fictional village of Porto Vergogna sits just beyond the real Cinque Terre, and the Ligurian coast saturates every page. One of the best recent novels set on this stretch of coastline.
