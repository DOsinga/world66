Four women flee grey London to rent a medieval castle on the Ligurian coast for a month. First published in 1922, it remains the definitive novel of what the Italian light and warmth do to the constrained English temperament. The precise location is deliberately vague, but von Arnim spent time on this stretch of coast and knew it well.
