A multigenerational novel about three women running a converted convent hotel in the Cinque Terre village of Vernazza. Unexpected guests arrive, old secrets surface, and the Ligurian landscape — olive groves, lemon trees, the turquoise sea — is described with real affection. Undemanding and pleasurably immersive in its sense of place.
