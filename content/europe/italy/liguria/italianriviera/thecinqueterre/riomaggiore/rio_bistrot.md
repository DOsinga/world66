The most serious kitchen in Riomaggiore, with a terrace overlooking the harbour. Chef Luca Tonelli's menu is built around local seafood and changes frequently to match what's available — expect dishes like spaghetti with pistachio pesto and calamari, or scampi with local herbs. Booking essential in summer. Via San Giacomo 46.
