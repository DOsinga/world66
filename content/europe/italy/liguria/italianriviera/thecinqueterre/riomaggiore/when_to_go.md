---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

**May, June, and September** are the best months for the Cinque Terre. The weather is warm, the paths are open, and the crowds are manageable. In May and early June the terraced hillsides are intensely green and the sea is warming up; in September the light is golden and the water is at its best.

**July and August** bring serious overcrowding. The Cinque Terre National Park imposes crowd-control measures on some trails; the Via dell'Amore requires advance booking. The villages are hot, packed, and hard to enjoy. If you come in August, arrive early in the morning before the tour groups and stay overnight — the evenings are genuinely pleasant when the day-trippers have gone.

**October and November** can be beautiful, but weather becomes unpredictable and some paths close after heavy rain. The terraces are being harvested for grapes and olives — an excellent time to see the working landscape. Some restaurants close by November.

**Winter** is quiet, sometimes cold, and occasionally dramatic with sea storms. Almost no tourists. The village has a genuine local life in winter that summer visitors never see.
