The Cinque Terre rewards slow travel — wandering the lanes of each village, swimming from the rocks, hiking between towns, and eating as locally as possible. The five villages have different characters: Vernazza is the prettiest; Monterosso the most resort-like; Corniglia the quietest; Manarola the most dramatic at sunrise; Riomaggiore the most working-village in feel.
