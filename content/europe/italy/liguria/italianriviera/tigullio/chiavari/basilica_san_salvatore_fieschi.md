The Basilica di San Salvatore dei Fieschi stands in a small village of Cogorno, about 4 km northeast of Chiavari, in a position that has been described as one of the most perfect medieval spaces in Liguria. The Romanesque-Gothic church was commissioned by Pope Innocent IV in the thirteenth century, and the square it faces — still flanked by the original Fieschi palace — is virtually unchanged since it was built.

The interior is sober and beautiful: black and white striped stone typical of Ligurian Gothic, excellent capitals, and a cloister with views across the hills. The basilica is well worth the short detour from Chiavari — it is accessible by car in ten minutes, or by bus from the town centre.
