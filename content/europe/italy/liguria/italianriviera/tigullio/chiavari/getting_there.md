---
title: Getting There
type: section
---

Chiavari is on the main Genoa–La Spezia coastal railway line, with frequent regional trains from Genoa (about 50 minutes) and La Spezia (about 45 minutes). From Milan, change at Genoa; from Florence, change at La Spezia or Pisa. The station is central, a five-minute walk from the old town and lungomare.

By car, Chiavari is on the A12 motorway (Genova–Livorno), exit Chiavari. The drive from Genoa takes about 40 minutes; from Milan about two and a half hours. Parking in the town centre is limited and often metered; the seafront car parks are more convenient. The town is compact and best explored on foot once you arrive.
