The Chiesa di Santo Stefano is Lavagna's main church, originally medieval but substantially rebuilt in the mid-17th century. The current Baroque structure dates to 1650 and was designed by the local architect Gio Batta Ghiso. The interior is modest but contains some worthwhile paintings and decorative stonework. The church sits in the old town, a short walk from the riverfront.

Like most small Ligurian churches, Santo Stefano serves primarily as a parish church and is open for regular services. Visitors are welcome outside of service times. It is not a major sight in its own right, but worth a quick visit if you are already wandering the old town streets.
