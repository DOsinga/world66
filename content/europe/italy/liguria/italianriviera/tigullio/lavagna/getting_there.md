---
title: Getting There
type: section
---

Lavagna has its own train station on the Genoa–La Spezia coastal line, with frequent services in both directions. Trains from Genoa take about 45 minutes; from La Spezia about 1 hour. The station is a few minutes' walk from the town centre and the harbour. Chiavari station, just across the river, has slightly more frequent services on some routes.

By car, Lavagna is on the A12 autostrada (exit Lavagna-Chiavari). Parking near the harbour is available but can be tight in summer. The town is compact enough to explore on foot once you arrive.
