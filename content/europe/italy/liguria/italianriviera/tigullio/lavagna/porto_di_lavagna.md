The Porto di Lavagna is one of the largest marinas on the Ligurian coast, with berths for over a thousand boats. It is a working marina rather than a picture-postcard fishing harbour — functional, well-equipped, and busy with sailing traffic in summer. The harbour front has a promenade with cafés and restaurants, and the overall atmosphere is that of a relaxed sailing town rather than a tourist resort.

In summer, boat excursions leave from the marina to the various coves and beaches along the Tigullio Gulf coast. The harbour is also the departure point for boat trips to [Portofino](/europe/italy/liguria/italianriviera/portofino) and the Cinque Terre further along the coast. Walking the harbour front in the evening — when the boats are lit up and the restaurants are full — is the most pleasant thing to do in Lavagna.
