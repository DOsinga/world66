---
title: Things to Do
type: section
---

Lavagna is a small Ligurian town best experienced at a slow pace: walk the harbour, cross the bridge to [Chiavari](/europe/italy/liguria/italianriviera/tigullio/chiavari), visit the Basilica dei Fieschi in the hills above town, and eat well. The marina is one of the larger ones on the Ligurian Riviera and attracts a sailing crowd in summer. The town does not have major standalone attractions but rewards an unhurried afternoon.
