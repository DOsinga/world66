Rapallo is the largest town on the Tigullio Gulf, with around 30,000 residents and a long history as a resort. Its symbol is the [Castello di Rapallo](/europe/italy/liguria/italianriviera/tigullio/rapallo/castello_di_rapallo) — a small sixteenth-century fort on a rocky islet just off the promenade, once a defence against Saracen pirates, now a venue for temporary exhibitions with its feet in the sea. The [Lungomare Vittorio Veneto](/europe/italy/liguria/italianriviera/tigullio/rapallo/lungomare) curves around the harbour in the classic Ligurian fashion, all palm trees and pastel-painted hotels.

The town's most distinctive excursion is the [cable car to the Sanctuary of Montallegro](/europe/italy/liguria/italianriviera/tigullio/rapallo/funivia_montallegro), rising 612 metres to a hilltop church with sweeping views over the gulf and, on clear days, out to Corsica. The sanctuary dates from the sixteenth century and its walls are covered with a remarkable collection of votive offerings — small painted panels recording disasters narrowly averted by divine intervention.

Rapallo has a minor place in twentieth-century history: in November 1920 it hosted the negotiations that produced the [Treaty of Rapallo](/europe/italy/liguria/italianriviera/tigullio/rapallo/treaty_of_rapallo), settling the border dispute between Italy and Yugoslavia that had simmered since WWI. The literary connection is better known in some circles — Ezra Pound lived here through much of the 1920s and 1930s, and Max Beerbohm spent his final decades on the seafront.

In early July, Rapallo holds its main festival in honour of the Madonna di Montallegro. The [Castello di Rapallo](/europe/italy/liguria/italianriviera/tigullio/rapallo/castello_di_rapallo) is set ablaze with fireworks in one of the most spectacular displays on the Riviera. The town is also a practical base: [Portofino](/europe/italy/liguria/portofino) is 15 minutes by ferry, and the [Cinque Terre](/europe/italy/liguria/italianriviera/thecinqueterre) is reachable by train or summer boat service.
