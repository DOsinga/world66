title: Things to Do
type: section
---

Rapallo's main sights are within easy walking distance of each other along the seafront. The [Castello di Rapallo](/europe/italy/liguria/italianriviera/tigullio/rapallo/castello_di_rapallo) anchors the eastern end of the promenade — a small sixteenth-century fort on a rocky islet that is more photogenic than vast. The [Lungomare Vittorio Veneto](/europe/italy/liguria/italianriviera/tigullio/rapallo/lungomare) curves west from the castle around the harbour, a classic Ligurian seafront of palms, pastel-painted buildings, and café tables.

The town's most striking excursion is the [cable car to Montallegro](/europe/italy/liguria/italianriviera/tigullio/rapallo/funivia_montallegro), rising 612 metres to a hilltop sanctuary with sweeping views over the Tigullio Gulf. The [Basilica of SS. Gervasio e Protasio](/europe/italy/liguria/italianriviera/tigullio/rapallo/basilica_ss_gervasio_protasio) on the main piazza is the town's principal church. History-minded visitors should seek out the [Palazzo Municipale](/europe/italy/liguria/italianriviera/tigullio/rapallo/treaty_of_rapallo), where the 1920 Treaty of Rapallo was signed, ending the post-WWI border dispute between Italy and Yugoslavia.
