---
title: When to Go
type: section
---

Ventimiglia is pleasant year-round by northern European standards — the Riviera climate means mild winters and hot, dry summers, tempered slightly by sea breezes. The Friday market runs every week regardless of season, though summer brings the largest crowds from the French coast.

Spring (April–June) and early autumn (September–October) are the best times to visit. Temperatures are comfortable for walking the old town, the Hanbury Gardens are at their most vivid, and the beaches are usable without the peak-summer crush. July and August bring Italian and French holidaymakers in force; the beach is packed, prices rise, and the weekend traffic jams at the border can be considerable.

Winter is quiet but functional. The Hanbury Gardens are open with reduced hours, the Friday market still draws a crowd of French shoppers, and the old town is essentially empty. The Cuneo–Ventimiglia railway runs its full schedule year-round, making a winter train trip into the Ligurian Alps a genuine option for those who like empty mountain landscapes.
