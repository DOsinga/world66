---
title: When to Go
type: section
---

La Spezia itself is pleasant year-round as a base, but the time you visit matters mainly for the Cinque Terre access. July and August are the most crowded and hottest months; the coastal path between villages can be unpleasantly busy and accommodation in the villages is extremely expensive. Come in May, June, or September for better weather, fewer people, and lower prices.

The Cinque Terre hiking paths are sometimes closed in winter due to landslide risk; check the Parco Nazionale delle Cinque Terre website for current trail status before visiting outside the main season.

Spring (April to June) is arguably the best time — warm enough for swimming from June, wildflowers on the coastal slopes, and the vineyards at their most vivid green before the summer heat bleaches them.
