---
title: When to Go
type: section
---

May and June are the best months: the water is warming up, the crowds haven't arrived, and the light on the gulf is at its finest. September and early October are nearly as good — slightly cooler, with the August peak behind you.

July and August are busy and hot. The harbour fills with boats, the beach at San Terenzo gets crowded, and restaurant tables require booking. Lerici is popular with Italian holidaymakers as well as foreign visitors, which makes summer feel genuinely festive rather than just touristy, but it does mean planning ahead.

Winter is quiet, with many restaurants and boat services reduced or closed. The walking between villages remains excellent year-round — the cliff paths above Tellaro are particularly good on a clear January day when you have them to yourself.
