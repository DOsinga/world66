---
title: "Eating Out"
type: section
---

Manarola eats Ligurian — pesto, anchovies (the *acciughe di Monterosso* are the regional benchmark), focaccia, fritto misto, trofie pasta, and a glass of crisp Vermentino or the sweet Sciacchetrà to finish. The harbourside places lean expensive and touristy; the better kitchens are higher up.
