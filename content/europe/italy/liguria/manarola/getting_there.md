---
title: "Getting There"
type: section
---

**By train.** The only sensible way in. Manarola sits on the **Cinque Terre regional line**, with shuttle trains every 10–20 minutes from La Spezia (12 min, €5 single in summer 2026) and Levanto (15 min). From La Spezia Centrale you can connect to Pisa, Florence, Genoa and Milan. The Manarola station is short — the platform doesn't fit the whole train, so listen for announcements and walk to the correct carriage before your stop.

**By Cinque Terre Card.** If you plan three or more train rides plus a trail walk, the **Cinque Terre Card** covers unlimited trains and most paths for a flat daily fee. Buy it at any of the village stations.

**By car.** Don't. The village has effectively no parking, the access road is closed to non-residents and the only car park is a long stairway above town. Leave the car in La Spezia.

**By boat.** Seasonal ferries run from La Spezia, Portovenere and Monterosso in summer — a slower, scenic alternative.
