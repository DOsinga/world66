---
title: Things to Do
type: section
---

Nasino is a small agricultural village with a handful of genuine highlights — mainly the ancient cave sites in the surrounding Pennavaire valley, the medieval ruins at Vignoletto, and some good hiking into the surrounding mountains.

The **Caves of the Pennavaire Valley** are the most historically significant feature of the area — used by humans from the Epigravettian period (around 9,000 years ago) through the Roman era, they represent one of the longest continuous records of human habitation in Liguria. Finds from these caves are displayed at the Civico Museo Inguano in Albenga.

The **ruins of Vignoletto castle**, above the village, mark the site of a medieval Del Carretto fortification with commanding views over the valley. The **parish church of San Giovanni Battista** and the **shrine of the Madonna di Curagna** are the village's main ecclesiastical buildings. **Monte Galero** (1,708 m) to the north offers the best hiking in the immediate area.
