---
title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Pontinvrea is a quiet hill village rather than a sightseeing destination. A morning is enough to see the parish church, the marchesi palace on the main square, and walk one of the chestnut-forest trails that lead up to the Colle del Giovo. The surrounding Apennines are the real draw: cool air, dense woods, and almost no other visitors.
