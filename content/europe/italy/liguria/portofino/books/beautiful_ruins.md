A young Italian innkeeper on a tiny Ligurian cove encounters a dying American actress in 1962, setting off a story that moves between Italy, Hollywood, and the present day. The fictional village of Porto Vergogna sits just beyond the real Cinque Terre, and the Ligurian coast — its light, its cliffs, its small-scale grandeur — saturates the novel. One of the best recent novels set on this stretch of coast.
