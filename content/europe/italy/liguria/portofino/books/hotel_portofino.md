Set in the summer of 1927, this historical novel follows a British family who open an upscale hotel on the Italian Riviera during the Roaring Twenties. Class, politics, and secrets accumulate over a single season. It became the basis for a television drama and is a smooth, escapist read that conjures the period well — Mussolini's Italy hovering just beyond the terrace.
