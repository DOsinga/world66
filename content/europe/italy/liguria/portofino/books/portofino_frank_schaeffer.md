A semi-autobiographical novel about a ten-year-old American boy whose fundamentalist missionary family takes their summer holidays in Portofino in the 1960s. Calvin Becker explores the village, befriends Italian fishermen, and discovers that the world outside his parents' rigid faith is considerably more interesting. Funny, sharp, and full of affection for the place — it captures Portofino before the superyachts took over.
