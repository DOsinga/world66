Four women escape grey London to rent a medieval castle on the Ligurian coast for a month. First published in 1922, this novel captures exactly the transformation that the Italian light and warmth work on the reserved English temperament. Von Arnim spent time on this stretch of coast and the setting is vividly rendered — sunshine, wisteria, and the slow loosening of social constraints. The castle in the book is generally taken to be in the area around Portofino.
