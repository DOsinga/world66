title: "Day Trips"
type: section
---

The paths of the Monte di Portofino promontory connect several rewarding destinations. [San Fruttuoso Abbey](/europe/italy/liguria/portofino/san_fruttuoso_abbey), a 13th-century Benedictine monastery reachable only by foot (about 2.5 hours each way) or by boat, is one of the finest medieval abbeys on the Ligurian coast — and in the cove below the abbey lies the famous [Christ of the Abyss](/europe/italy/liguria/portofino/christ_of_the_abyss) statue. The church of San Nicolò di Capodimonte on the west side of the promontory offers panoramic views between Camogli and Punta Chiappa. The beach at [Paraggi](/europe/italy/liguria/portofino/paraggi_beach), 3 km along the corniche road, is the closest good swimming spot to Portofino. [Cervara Abbey](/europe/italy/liguria/portofino/cervara_abbey) opens its cliff-edge gardens for guided tours on selected weekends.
