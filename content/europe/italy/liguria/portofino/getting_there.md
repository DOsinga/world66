---
title: "Getting There"
type: section
---

The nearest railway station is **Santa Margherita Ligure**, 6 km up the coast on the Genova–La Spezia line. From there, take the bus (line 82, about 20 minutes) or a taxi to Portofino. In summer, the ferry service from Santa Margherita is the most pleasant option and avoids the road entirely.

**By car** take the A12 motorway (Genova–Livorno) and exit at Rapallo or Recco, then follow the SS227 along the coast. Parking in Portofino is extremely limited and expensive in summer — there is a small pay car park at the village entrance. Driving beyond the car park into the village itself is restricted. In high season you will almost certainly queue.

**By boat** is the ideal way to arrive. Ferries run from Genoa, Rapallo, and Santa Margherita Ligure between April and October; services from Camogli also connect during summer. The ferry dock is right on the Piazzetta.

**By air**, the nearest airport is Genoa Cristoforo Colombo (GOA), about 35 km away. From the airport, take a train to Santa Margherita via Genoa Brignole station.
