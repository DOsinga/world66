---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

**May and June** are the best months: the sea is warming up, the hills are still green, the crowds are manageable, and the light is extraordinary. **September and early October** run a close second — the August masses have gone, the water is at its warmest, and the village regains some of its composure.

**July and August** are hot, crowded, and expensive. The road from Santa Margherita backs up with traffic on summer weekends, the parking lot fills before 9am, and the Piazzetta tables are occupied almost entirely by day-trippers. If you must come in summer, arrive by boat or very early.

**Winter** (November to March) is quiet and sometimes beautiful, but some restaurants and shops close, and the weather can be unpredictable. The paths through the natural park are still walkable and largely empty — a completely different experience.
