---
title: Getting There
type: section
---

Borgio Verezzi lies on the A10 autostrada between Savona and the French border, with an exit at Pietra Ligure (about 3 km away). By car it is roughly an hour from Genoa and just over two hours from Milan.

The nearest train station is Borgio Verezzi, on the coastal Genoa–Ventimiglia line. Trains connect regularly to Savona (about 30 minutes) and to Albenga (15 minutes). From the station, the beach and lower town are a short walk; reaching Verezzi on the hill requires a taxi or a steep 20-minute climb on foot. The Grotte di Valdemino are about 1.5 km from the station.
