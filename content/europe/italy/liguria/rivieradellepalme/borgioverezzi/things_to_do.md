title: Things to Do
type: section
---

Borgio Verezzi packs a surprising amount into a small space. The caves below Borgio are among Liguria's finest, while the hilltop hamlets of Verezzi reward those who make the climb with extraordinary views and a medieval atmosphere that feels genuinely untouched. In summer the town transforms into a cultural destination thanks to one of Italy's most atmospheric open-air theatre festivals.
