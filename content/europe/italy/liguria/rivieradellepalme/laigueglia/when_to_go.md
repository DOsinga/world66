---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

May, June and September are the right months for Laigueglia. The sea is warm enough to swim, the days are long, and the worst of the summer crowds are still arriving or leaving. July and August bring an influx of Italian families on their annual holiday — prices spike, parking becomes a sport, and you should book any restaurant lunch by mid-morning at the latest.

October to April is the off-season. Many of the bathing establishments shut down and a number of restaurants close, but the old town remains open and the weather stays mild — daytime temperatures are 14–18°C, sunny most days. It is a fine time for a longer walking holiday along the coast, with the headlands and inland trails empty.
