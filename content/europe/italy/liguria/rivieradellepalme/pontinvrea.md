An agricultural centre on the northern side of the Ligurian Apennines, surrounded by extensive forests that draw lovers of nature and tranquillity. The ancient settlement of Ponte dei Prati formed part of the commune of Pareto until 1606, when the Gonzaga, rulers of Monferrato, gave it in fief to the d'Invrea of Genoa. In 1730 it passed to Austria.

The [palace of the marchesi](/europe/italy/liguria/rivieradellepalme/pontinvrea/palazzo_dinvrea), now the town hall, is a porticoed building from the seventeenth century. The parish [church of San Lorenzo](/europe/italy/liguria/rivieradellepalme/pontinvrea/chiesa_san_lorenzo) and, on the road to the [Colle del Giovo](/europe/italy/liguria/rivieradellepalme/pontinvrea/colle_del_giovo), the [church of the Madonna del Carmine](/europe/italy/liguria/rivieradellepalme/pontinvrea/madonna_del_carmine) date from the same period.
