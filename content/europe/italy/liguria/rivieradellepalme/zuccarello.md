Zuccarello is a tiny medieval village in the Ligurian Apennines, set in the narrow Neva valley about 30 kilometres inland from Albenga. It was the seat of the Marchesato di Zuccarello for several centuries, and the ruins of the castle on the hill above the village are the clearest reminder of that former importance. The village itself is a single medieval street lined with arched buildings in warm stone, quietly preserved and almost entirely untouristed.

The surrounding valley offers good walking, and the views from the castle ruins take in terracotta rooftops, forested ridges, and on clear days the coast. Zuccarello makes a good half-day excursion from the Riviera delle Palme, or a stop along the inland roads between the coast and the Piedmont border.
