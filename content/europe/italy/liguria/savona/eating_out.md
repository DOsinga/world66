---
title: Eating Out
type: section
---

Savona eats like Liguria with a port-city appetite: focaccia in the morning, farinata from wood-fired ovens, pesto with trofie, anchovies, stuffed vegetables, and simple grilled fish. The useful rule is to look away from the cruise terminal and into the old streets, where bakeries and trattorie are more likely to be serving locals than passengers.
