---
title: Getting There
type: section
---

Savona is on the main coastal railway line between Genoa and Ventimiglia (and the French border beyond). Trains from Genoa take about 40 minutes; from Nice (with a change or direct service) about two hours. The station is about a kilometre from the old centre.

By car, the A10 autostrada connects Savona to Genoa and to the A6 motorway heading north to Turin. The port receives ferries from Grimaldi Lines and others to Corsica (Porto Torres, Bastia) and Sardinia, making it a common embarkation point for travellers heading to those islands.
