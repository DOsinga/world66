---
title: Getting There
type: section
---

Savona is on the Genoa-Ventimiglia railway line, with frequent regional trains to Genoa, Finale Ligure, Albenga, Imperia, and San Remo. The station sits inland from the old centre; local buses and a straightforward walk connect it with the port.

By car, the A10 autostrada follows the coast and the A6 links Savona with Turin. The port handles ferries and cruise traffic, so check access roads carefully on busy embarkation days.
