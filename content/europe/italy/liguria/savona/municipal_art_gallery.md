Savona's Municipal Art Gallery is a compact civic collection that helps the city feel less like a quick port stop. The galleries bring together paintings, sculpture, and decorative arts, with particular value for visitors interested in Ligurian art and the region's ceramic traditions.

It is best approached as part of a slow walk through the old centre rather than as a standalone museum day. Combined with the cathedral area, the fortress, and the ceramic museum, it gives Savona a stronger cultural spine.
