The Scrivia Valley rewards slow exploration. Its pleasures are specific: a narrow-gauge railway that winds through chestnut woods, medieval bridges, a castle perched improbably on a cliff, a cycling heritage that produced two of Italy's greatest champions. The valley sits at the junction of Liguria and Piedmont, and that crossroads character shows in everything — the food, the wine, the landscape.
