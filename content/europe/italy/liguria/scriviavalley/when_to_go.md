---
title: When to Go
type: section
---

Spring and autumn are ideal — the chestnut forests are at their best in October, and mushroom season draws Genoese families into the hills from September onwards. Summer is pleasant at altitude, cooler than coastal Liguria, which is why towns like Torriglia and Casella became traditional retreats for Genoese families seeking relief from the heat.

Winter is quiet. The Giovi Pass can see snow, which closes some minor roads but makes for atmospheric walking.
