title: Eating Out
type: section
---

Sestri Levante's restaurants reflect the Ligurian coast's strengths: fresh fish, good olive oil, and local wines. The town specialises in the seafood of the gulf — branzino, orata, octopus, and anchovies — alongside classic Ligurian preparations: trofie al pesto, focaccia, and farinata (chickpea flatbread). Via Nazionale has the main concentration of restaurants, but the best places tend to be tucked into the old town streets.

[Cantina del Polpo](/europe/italy/liguria/sestri_levante/cantina_del_polpo) on Piazza Cavour is the long-running seafood trattoria worth seeking out: octopus, squid, and cuttlefish prepared simply and well, with good local Ligurian wines. No credit cards, and booking is advisable for dinner.
