---
title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Sestri Levante's pleasures are largely slow ones. The [Baia del Silenzio](/europe/italy/liguria/sestri_levante/baia_del_silenzio) on the northwest side of the peninsula is genuinely peaceful — a curving beach backed by pastel houses with no through traffic. The [Baia delle Favole](/europe/italy/liguria/sestri_levante/baia_delle_favole) on the south side is larger and livelier. The [Marconi Tower](/europe/italy/liguria/sestri_levante/marconi_tower) at the top of the promontory is worth the walk for panoramic views that extend to the Alps on clear days. The 12th-century [Chiesa di San Niccolò](/europe/italy/liguria/sestri_levante/san_nicolo_dell_isola), Roman-Gothic in style, sits near the old castle at the tip of the peninsula. The coastal walk to [Punta Manara](/europe/italy/liguria/sestri_levante/punta_manara), about 45 minutes south, is one of the best short excursions from town.
