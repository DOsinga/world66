---
title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Zuccarello rewards the visitor who slows down. The medieval arcaded streets, the [Del Carretto castle ruins](/europe/italy/liguria/zuccarello/del_carretto_castle), and the [medieval bridge](/europe/italy/liguria/zuccarello/medieval_bridge) over the Neva are the three essential sights. The [parish church of San Bartolomeo](/europe/italy/liguria/zuccarello/san_bartolomeo) with its Romanesque bell tower is easy to miss but worth a look. Nearby [Colletta di Castelbianco](/europe/italy/liguria/castelbianco) — a fully restored medieval hill village three kilometres away — makes a natural extension to the day.
