Torre di Gombito is a 12th-century tower-house in Bergamo's Città Alta. It stands at a historic crossroads and is one of the medieval towers that still give the upper town its vertical skyline.

The tower is a quick architectural stop while walking between Piazza Vecchia and the cathedral area, best appreciated as part of a slow wander through the old town's lanes.
