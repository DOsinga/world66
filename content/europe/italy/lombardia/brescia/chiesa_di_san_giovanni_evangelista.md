Chiesa di San Giovanni Evangelista is one of Brescia's oldest churches, with roots going back to the early fifth century. It is not as famous as the cathedral group or Santa Giulia, but it adds an important early Christian layer to a walk through the old centre.

The church is worth a deliberate detour if you are interested in Brescia's religious history, especially because it sits close to the city's main historic streets.
