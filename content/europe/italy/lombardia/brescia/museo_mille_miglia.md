Museo Mille Miglia celebrates the legendary road race that linked Brescia to Rome and
back, turning cars, route maps, photographs, and race culture into a local story. For
Brescia, the museum matters because the Mille Miglia is not just motorsport nostalgia;
it is part of the city's identity.

Car enthusiasts will get the most from it, but the displays also work as a stylish
introduction to twentieth-century Italy on the road. The museum sits outside the very
centre, so plan it as a targeted stop.
