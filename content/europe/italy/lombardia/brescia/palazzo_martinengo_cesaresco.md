Palazzo Martinengo Cesaresco is a mid-17th-century palace in Brescia's old centre, used by the province and for temporary exhibitions. It is not as essential as Santa Giulia or the Roman forum, but it is worth checking what is on if you are already exploring the streets around Piazza del Foro and want another layer of the city's noble architecture.
