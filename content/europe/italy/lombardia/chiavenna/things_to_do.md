---
title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Chiavenna rewards a day or two of unhurried exploration. The old town is small enough to walk end-to-end in half an hour, but interesting enough to fill an afternoon. The crotti are the defining experience — pack a lunch, find a terrace, and eat in the shadow of the glacial rock. Cyclists heading up or down the Maloja Pass make Chiavenna a regular stop; walkers will find routes into both valleys.
