Il Cambonino Vecchio preserves a side of Cremona that is easy to miss if you only come for violins and the cathedral square. Set in an old farmhouse, the museum documents rural life, tools, domestic spaces, and agricultural work in the Po Valley.

The visit is modest but useful, especially if you want to understand the countryside that fed and shaped Cremona. It gives texture to the flat landscape around the city: cascine, fields, livestock, and the practical rhythms that sat behind the region's more famous musical and urban culture.
