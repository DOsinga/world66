Museo Civico Ala Ponzone is Cremona's main civic art museum, housed in a noble palace and built around local painting, decorative arts, and collected works from northern Italy. It gives the city an art stop to balance the better-known violin and cathedral sights.

The museum is especially useful if you want Cremona to feel like more than music history. Its rooms connect the town to Lombard artistic networks, patronage, and domestic display, all at a pace that suits a smaller city.
