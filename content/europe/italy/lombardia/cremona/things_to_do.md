title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

The [Piazza del Comune](/europe/italy/lombardia/cremona/piazza_del_comune) is the heart of the visit: the [Cathedral](/europe/italy/lombardia/cremona/cremona_cathedral), the octagonal [Baptistery](/europe/italy/lombardia/cremona/cremona_baptistery), the [Torrazzo](/europe/italy/lombardia/cremona/torrazzo) bell tower, and the [Loggia dei Militi](/europe/italy/lombardia/cremona/loggia_dei_militi) all stand here, forming one of the finest medieval civic spaces in Italy. The [Museo del Violino](/europe/italy/lombardia/cremona/museo_del_violino) nearby is essential — a superb museum of luthiery with original Stradivari instruments. The [Teatro Ponchielli](/europe/italy/lombardia/cremona/teatro_ponchielli) is an elegant 19th-century opera house that has a regular season. For those interested in Romanesque architecture, [San Michele](/europe/italy/lombardia/cremona/san_michele) and [San Sigismondo](/europe/italy/lombardia/cremona/san_sigismondo) are worth the short excursion from the centre.
