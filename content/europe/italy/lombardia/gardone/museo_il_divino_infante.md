Museo Il Divino Infante is a small, unusual museum devoted to images of the infant Jesus. Its collection includes statues, carvings, dolls, and devotional objects, giving a focused look at a very specific strand of Catholic popular art.

It is a niche stop rather than a headline sight, but it works well if you are already visiting Gardone Riviera and want something quieter after the Vittoriale or the botanical garden.
