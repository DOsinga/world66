Peschiera del Garda sits at the point where Lake Garda drains into the Mincio river, its old town ringed by star-shaped [Venetian walls](/europe/italy/lombardia/lagodigarda/peschiera/venetian_walls) that are now a UNESCO World Heritage Site. With around 10,000 inhabitants, it is one of the smaller towns on the lake, but its position at the southern tip — just 25 km from Verona — makes it a practical and genuinely pleasant base.

The walls are the main attraction, and they reward a slow walk: the ramparts give views over the lake and the surrounding moraine hills, and the gates and bastions are well preserved. Inside the fortifications, [Piazza Ferdinando di Savoia](/europe/italy/lombardia/lagodigarda/peschiera/piazza_ferdinando) anchors the old centre, flanked by the Palazzo di Comando del Presidio and a small local history museum. The [church of San Martino](/europe/italy/lombardia/lagodigarda/peschiera/san_martino_church) dates from the 18th century and is worth a look inside. The [Porto Canale](/europe/italy/lombardia/lagodigarda/peschiera/porto_canale) running through the centre is the most photogenic stretch of the town, with the lake ferry pier at its mouth.

A short walk west of the walls, the pilgrimage [Sanctuary of the Madonna del Frassino](/europe/italy/lombardia/lagodigarda/peschiera/madonna_del_frassino) and the wetlands of [Lago del Frassino](/europe/italy/lombardia/lagodigarda/peschiera/lago_del_frassino) make a pleasant half-day in the moraine countryside. In the nearby hamlet of Colà you will find a handful of 16th- and 17th-century villas set between the lake and the plain.

If you have a bike, the ride along the Mincio river to Valeggio sul Mincio is one of the better half-days in the area. The village of Borghetto, just below Valeggio, is famous across the region for its tortellini — lunch there makes a fine destination in itself. South of Peschiera, the spectacular [Parco Giardino Sigurtà](/europe/italy/lombardia/lagodigarda/peschiera/parco_giardino_sigurta) draws half a million visitors each spring for the tulip bloom, and [Gardaland](/europe/italy/lombardia/lagodigarda/peschiera/gardaland) — Italy's largest amusement park — is just east of town for those travelling with children.
