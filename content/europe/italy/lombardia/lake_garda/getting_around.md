---
title: "Getting Around"
type: section
---

**By boat** is the most enjoyable option. Navigarda operates ferries connecting all the main towns, with faster hydrofoils on some routes. A day pass allows unlimited on-and-off travel. The car ferry between Maderno (west) and Torri del Benaco (east) saves the long drive around the lake, though queues can be long in summer.

**By car**, the roads around the lake are scenic but often slow. The western Gardesana road, carved through cliffs, is spectacular but narrow and frequently in single-lane tunnel sections. The southern shore road around the base of the lake is dual carriageway and fast but undramatic.

**By bus**, Arriva operates services connecting the major towns, though frequency is limited on rural routes.

**By bicycle**, the lake is manageable but hilly on the west and north shores. The southern lakeshore has a longer flat cycling path. E-bikes are widely available to rent throughout the lake towns.
