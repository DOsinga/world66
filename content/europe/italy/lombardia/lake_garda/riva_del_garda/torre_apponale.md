Torre Apponale is the medieval belfry that anchors the centre of Riva del Garda. The tower dates from the 13th century and rises above the lakefront streets and main square.

Visitors can climb the 165 steps to the top for a compact but rewarding view over the roofs of Riva, the harbour, and the northern end of Lake Garda.
