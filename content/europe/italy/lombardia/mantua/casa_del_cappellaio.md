Casa del Cappellaio is a small Renaissance house in Mantua with frescoes linked to the circle of Andrea Mantegna. It is a modest stop compared with the Gonzaga palaces, but it rewards travellers who enjoy Mantua's quieter historic streets and painted domestic architecture.
