Mantua Cathedral, formally Cattedrale di San Pietro Apostolo, stands on Piazza Sordello near the Palazzo Ducale. Its fabric mixes Romanesque, Renaissance, and Baroque work, making it a worthwhile stop while exploring Mantua's historic core.
