Palazzo d'Arco is a preserved aristocratic house museum in Mantua, with furnished rooms, paintings, library spaces, and domestic collections that show noble life beyond the city's better-known ducal monuments. Its scale feels personal rather than imperial.

The palace is a strong complement to Palazzo Ducale and Palazzo Te. It helps visitors understand Mantua as a lived aristocratic city, not only as a sequence of grand frescoed set pieces.
