Palazzo della Ragione helps frame Mantua's medieval civic centre, standing by Piazza delle Erbe with the clock tower, arcades, and old market buildings around it. It belongs to the city's pre-Gonzaga layer, when commerce and communal government shaped the square.

The palace is less famous than the Ducal Palace or Palazzo Te, but it is exactly the kind of building that makes Mantua rewarding to walk. Look up at the brickwork, the tower, and the way the piazza still narrows around old market routes before you settle in for a coffee nearby.
