title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Mantua is a city of palaces, churches, and paintings. The enormous [Palazzo Ducale](/europe/italy/lombardia/mantua/palazzo_ducale) — once home to the Gonzaga family — sprawls across the northeast corner of the old town with hundreds of rooms, courtyards, and gardens; Andrea Mantegna's Camera degli Sposi, with its illusionistic ceiling, is the highlight. Across town, [Palazzo Te](/europe/italy/lombardia/mantua/palazzo_te) is a Mannerist masterpiece by Giulio Romano, with fantastical frescoed rooms including the jaw-dropping [Sala dei Giganti](/europe/italy/lombardia/mantua/palazzo_te).

The churches are worth seeking out too: the [Basilica di Sant'Andrea](/europe/italy/lombardia/mantua/basilica_santandrea) (designed by Alberti) and the Romanesque [Rotonda di San Lorenzo](/europe/italy/lombardia/mantua/rotonda_san_lorenzo) are architectural landmarks. The [Lovers of Valdaro](/europe/italy/lombardia/mantua/lovers_of_valdaro) — two Neolithic skeletons buried in an embrace 5,000 years ago — are displayed in the Palazzo Ducale museum. On [Piazza Sordello](/europe/italy/lombardia/mantua/piazza_sordello), the medieval [Torre della Gabbia](/europe/italy/lombardia/mantua/torre_della_gabbia) still has its iron cage suspended from the exterior. Several smaller museums and galleries round out a city that rewards slow exploration.
