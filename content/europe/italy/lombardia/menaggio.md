Menaggio is a proper small town on the western shore of Lake Como — not a village, not a resort in the package-tourist sense, but a place where the supermarket is open on Sundays and people actually live. Its lakefront piazza, backed by a grid of streets with bars and restaurants, gives it a different feel from Bellagio's concentrated tourist elegance or Varenna's stepped alleyways.

The town is well positioned for exploring both the central lake and the inland territory. The SS340 road north from Menaggio runs up the Valle Menaggio toward Lake Lugano in Switzerland, passing through small villages in a quiet valley that most visitors to the lake never see. The path up to Monte Grona (1736m) starts from above the town and gives panoramic views across both Como and Lugano. Cyclists use the Menaggio-Porlezza route across the watershed — a popular day ride.

The ferry connections from Menaggio are among the best on the lake: the triangle service to Bellagio and Varenna runs frequently, the car ferry to Varenna carries bikes and bicycles, and longer ferry routes to Como city and to Lecco on the eastern arm stop here. In summer the lakefront fills with families and a more Italian, less international crowd than Bellagio, which makes it a pleasant base for a few days on the water.
