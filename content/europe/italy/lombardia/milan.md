Milan is the economic capital of Italy and one of the major financial and fashion centres of Europe. It is a city of contradictions: brutally efficient in business, yet possessed of some of the country’s finest art and architecture. The [Duomo di Milano](/europe/italy/lombardia/milan/duomo_di_milano) — the Gothic cathedral that took five centuries to complete — dominates the centre and sets the tone: ambitious, elaborate, and absolutely serious.

The historic centre holds the essential sights. [Santa Maria delle Grazie](/europe/italy/lombardia/milan/santa_maria_delle_grazie) houses Leonardo da Vinci’s *Last Supper*, one of the most important paintings in Western art (book months ahead). The [Castello Sforzesco](/europe/italy/lombardia/milan/castello_sforzesco) contains Michelangelo’s last unfinished sculpture. The [Pinacoteca di Brera](/europe/italy/lombardia/milan/pinacoteca_di_brera) has the best Renaissance collection in northern Italy. The [Galleria Vittorio Emanuele II](/europe/italy/lombardia/milan/galleria_vittorio_emanuele), the 19th-century iron-and-glass shopping arcade, connects the Duomo to [Teatro alla Scala](/europe/italy/lombardia/milan/teatro_alla_scala), the world’s most famous opera house.

Beyond the monuments, Milan rewards wandering. The [Brera](/europe/italy/lombardia/milan/brera) neighbourhood has cobbled streets, galleries, and the Pinacoteca. The [Navigli](/europe/italy/lombardia/milan/navigli) canal district comes alive at aperitivo hour. [Via Montenapoleone](/europe/italy/lombardia/milan/via_montenapoleone) and the [Quadrilatero della Moda](/europe/italy/lombardia/milan/quadrilatero) offer the world’s highest concentration of Italian fashion houses. The [Fondazione Prada](/europe/italy/lombardia/milan/fondazione_prada), in a converted distillery south of the centre, is one of the best contemporary art spaces in Europe, in the up-and-coming Porta Romana district beyond the [Arco di Porta Romana](/europe/italy/lombardia/milan/arco_di_porta_romana) gate. To the north, [Porta Nuova](/europe/italy/lombardia/milan/porta_nuova) has given the city a skyline of glass towers and the tree-clad Bosco Verticale.

Day trips from Milan are exceptional: the Certosa di Pavia (a Carthusian monastery of breathtaking elaboration), Bergamo’s medieval upper town, and the Italian Lakes are all within two hours.
