Basilica di Sant'Ambrogio is one of Milan's essential churches, a large Romanesque basilica tied to the city's patron saint. Allied bombing badly damaged the complex in World War II, but enough mosaics, brickwork, and medieval atmosphere remain to make it a strong counterpoint to the Duomo and the Renaissance churches nearby.
