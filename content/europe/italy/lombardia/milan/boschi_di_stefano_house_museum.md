The Boschi Di Stefano House Museum displays a strong collection of 20th-century Italian art in the former home of collectors Antonio Boschi and Marieda Di Stefano. The apartment setting, with design details by Piero Portaluppi, makes the visit feel more personal than a standard gallery.
