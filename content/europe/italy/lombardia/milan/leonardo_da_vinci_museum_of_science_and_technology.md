The Leonardo da Vinci Museum of Science and Technology is Milan's major science museum, housed in the former San Vittore al Corpo monastery. Its collections cover transport, communication, industry, and models inspired by Leonardo's engineering drawings.

It is a strong rainy-day stop and an easy add-on to Santa Maria delle Grazie and the western side of central Milan.
