The Pirelli Tower was Milan's great postwar skyscraper, finished in 1956 for the tyre company and quickly adopted as a symbol of the city's industrial comeback. Its clean, tapered form helped move high-rise architecture away from the heavy towers of the early 20th century.

Today it is partly overshadowed by the glass towers of Porta Nuova, but that contrast is exactly why it is worth noticing. View it from the station area or on a walk between Centrale and Porta Garibaldi.
