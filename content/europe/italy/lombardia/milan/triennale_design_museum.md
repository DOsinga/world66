The Triennale Design Museum is Milan's main stop for Italian design, set in the Palazzo dell'Arte beside Parco Sempione. Its exhibitions cover industrial design, furniture, graphics, architecture, and the objects that helped make Milan one of Europe's design capitals.

The museum works well with a visit to the park, Castello Sforzesco, or Brera. Even if you are not a design specialist, the displays give useful context for the city's modern creative identity.
