Villa Necchi Campiglio is a refined 1930s house designed by Piero Portaluppi for the industrial Necchi Campiglio family. The villa preserves the world of Milan's interwar elite: clean modern lines, polished interiors, art collections, and domestic spaces that still feel lived in.

The garden and pool make the house feel surprisingly quiet despite its central location. It is a good stop for visitors interested in architecture, design, or Milan beyond the cathedral and fashion streets.
