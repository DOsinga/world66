Parco di Monza is one of the city's defining attractions: a vast enclosed park surrounding the Royal Villa and stretching across woods, lawns, villas, farms, and long avenues. It is large enough to feel like a landscape destination rather than a simple urban park.

Visitors use it for walking, cycling, picnics, and access to the famous Autodromo Nazionale Monza. Together with the Royal Villa, it gives Monza a major green-and-heritage sight just outside the dense historic centre.
