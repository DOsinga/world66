---
title: Things to Do
type: section
---

Monza divides naturally into two zones: the historic centre around the cathedral and the Arengario, which can be covered on foot in a morning; and the vast park to the north, which deserves at least an afternoon on its own. The circuit visit is a must for Formula 1 enthusiasts; the cathedral treasury, with the Iron Crown, is the single object in Monza most worth seeing.
