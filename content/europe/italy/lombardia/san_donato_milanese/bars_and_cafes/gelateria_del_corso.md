A family-run gelateria on the main shopping street that has built a loyal local following on the strength of its traditional gelato made on the premises. The flavours follow the Italian orthodoxy — fiordilatte, pistacchio, nocciola, seasonal fruit — and are executed with care. The granita in summer is particularly good. Unlike many suburban gelaterias, they have not gone down the path of neon colours and artificial flavours. A satisfying stop on a warm afternoon.
