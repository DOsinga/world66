A small osteria in the Metanopoli neighbourhood that has been feeding ENI workers and their families for decades. The menu is short, changes daily, and revolves around whatever is fresh at the market — pasta with seasonal ragù, soup in winter, grilled fish on Fridays. The wine list is modest but well-chosen. In good weather, tables spill out onto the pavement in front. The crowd is local and the welcome is warm.
