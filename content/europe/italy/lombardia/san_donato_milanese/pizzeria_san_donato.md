A reliable neighbourhood pizzeria that does what it says: decent Neapolitan-style pizza with a proper charred crust, and a short menu of pasta and salads for those who want alternatives. Unpretentious and good value, it's popular with families in the evening and with anyone wanting a quick, satisfying meal without making a production of it. The house wines are drinkable and inexpensive. Reservations not needed except on Friday and Saturday evenings.
