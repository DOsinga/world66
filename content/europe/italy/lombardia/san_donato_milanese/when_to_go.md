---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

San Donato Milanese shares Milan's climate: cold, foggy winters (December–February), warm and occasionally humid summers (July–August), and pleasant shoulder seasons in spring (April–May) and autumn (September–October). For architectural exploration, spring and autumn are ideal — the light is good, the temperature comfortable, and the fog that characterises the Po Valley in winter has lifted.

There are no major events specific to San Donato that would drive a visit. The town's calendar runs to the standard Italian municipal rhythm: a patron saint's feast (San Donato, 7 August), local markets, and the occasional cultural event organised around ENI's institutional presence. Milan's event calendar — fashion weeks, Salone del Mobile in April, summer festivals — is easily accessible from here by metro and provides the more compelling reason to time a visit.

The Salone del Mobile in April is worth noting specifically: hotel prices in Milan spike dramatically, and San Donato's hotels (several business hotels serve the ENI campus) sometimes offer better availability at more reasonable rates with quick metro access to the fair.
