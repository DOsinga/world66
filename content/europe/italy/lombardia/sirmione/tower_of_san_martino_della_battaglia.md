The Tower of San Martino della Battaglia rises above the southern Lake Garda countryside as a monument to the battles that helped shape Italian unification. Its height and painted interior make the memory of nineteenth-century war unusually physical.

Climb for the view as much as the history. From the top you can read the landscape between vineyards, villages, and the lake, while the museum context nearby explains why this quiet area became part of Italy's national story.
