Villa e Parco Torelli Mylius is one of Varese's quieter villa-and-garden stops, useful when you want the city's green, aristocratic side without committing to a major excursion. The park spreads below Colle dei Miogni with lawns, mature trees, and the romantic landscaping added under the Mylius family.

It is best treated as a local walk with history attached. Pair it with the centre of Varese or other villas, and come for shade, space, and a sense of how much the city identity depends on private estates turned public green rooms.
