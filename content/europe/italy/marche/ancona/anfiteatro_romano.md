Ancona's Roman amphitheatre sits on the hillside near the cathedral, with surviving stonework that hints at the scale of the ancient city. The ruins are not as complete as Italy's famous amphitheatres, but they are an easy historic stop while walking between the old lighthouse area and the upper town.
