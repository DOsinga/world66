---
title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Ancona's sights cluster on and around the cathedral hill and the old town below, with the Conero coast offering beaches, hiking, and white limestone cliffs just a short drive south. Start at the top — San Ciriaco on Monte Guasco — and work your way down through the medieval lanes to the port, then save a day or two for the Conero headland.
