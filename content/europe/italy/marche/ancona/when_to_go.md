---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

May through September is beach season on the Conero, with July and August seeing the headland at its most crowded — parking at Portonovo fills early, and the beaches at Sirolo and Numana get busy. If the Conero coast is the main draw, late May, June, or September offer the best balance of warm water and manageable crowds.

Spring (April–May) and autumn (September–October) are the best times for city sightseeing: mild temperatures, no crowds, and the old town's museums and churches at their most accessible. The Pinacoteca and cathedral are open year-round.

The ferry port operates throughout the year, though crossing frequency to Greece and Croatia drops significantly in winter. Ancona itself is a working city that doesn't close for the off-season.
