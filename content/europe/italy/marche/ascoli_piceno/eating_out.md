---
title: "Eating Out"
type: section
---

Ascoli's food traditions are the Marche at its most particular. Olive all'ascolana — fried, stuffed olives — were invented here and remain the regional aperitivo. Look for fritto misto all'ascolana, anolini in brodo, and the local sweet ciambellone for breakfast. The pizza al taglio places off Corso Mazzini are reliable for a quick lunch.
