Fortezza Pia stands above the upper part of Ascoli Piceno, where older Roman and medieval defences guarded the approaches to town. The surviving fortress is a quiet historic stop rather than a heavily interpreted museum, but the position explains why Ascoli was so defensible.

Come for the walk, the walls, and the view back over the old stone centre. It pairs naturally with the Roman bridge and the medieval towers lower in town.
