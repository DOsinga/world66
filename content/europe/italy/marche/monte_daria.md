Monte d'Aria rises to almost 1,000 metres on the Monte Letegge ridge, with broad views over Camerino, the Sibillini Mountains, and, on clear days, out toward the Adriatic. The summit is marked by transmission masts and wind turbines, so it is not a wilderness peak, but the meadows and pine woods on its slopes make it a good local walking goal in the inland Marche.
