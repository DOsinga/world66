---
title: Eating Out
type: section
---

The cooking in Recanati is traditional Marche cuisine — the standard pasta-and-meat dishes of central Italy, with a strong inflection from the nearby Adriatic. Local specialities to look out for: **vincisgrassi**, a regional version of lasagne layered with chicken livers, sweetbreads and porcini in a rich ragù; **olive all'ascolana**, large green olives stuffed with seasoned meat, breaded and deep-fried; **stoccafisso all'anconetana**, salt cod cooked with potatoes and tomatoes, served further north in Ancona but common on Marche menus.

Recanati is in the heart of **Verdicchio dei Castelli di Jesi** country — a crisp white wine that goes particularly well with the seafood — and you will find good bottles on every list. For reds, look for **Rosso Conero**, made from Montepulciano grapes grown on the limestone of the Conero peninsula not far from here.

Most restaurants are inside the old town walls; reservations are sensible at the weekends. Lunch is normally served from 12:30 to 14:30 and dinner from 19:30 or 20:00.
