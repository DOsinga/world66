---
title: Things to Do
type: section
---

Recanati is a small town, and the best of it can be seen on foot in a leisurely day. The unmissable visit is **Casa Leopardi** with its 20,000-volume library, where the poet read himself almost blind. The hill of the **Colle dell'Infinito** behind the house is the spot that gave him the most famous poem in Italian, *L'Infinito*. From there the old town climbs gently to the **Piazza Giacomo Leopardi** at the centre, with the seventeenth-century Palazzo Comunale, the Torre del Borgo, and a handful of churches worth a quick look. The **Villa Colloredo Mels**, on the western edge of town, holds one of the best small collections of Lorenzo Lotto paintings outside the Marche regional museum in Loreto.
