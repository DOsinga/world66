The Villa Colloredo Mels is a Renaissance villa on the western edge of the old town, set in a large park and looking out across the open country toward the Apennines. The building was the country seat of a noble family from Friuli and was acquired by the comune in the 1980s; it now houses the **Museo Civico**, the town's civic museum.

The highlight is the collection of paintings by **Lorenzo Lotto**, the great Venetian who spent his last years working in the Marche. Four major Lotto altarpieces hang here, including the celebrated *Annunciation* (around 1534), with its famous startled cat fleeing across the floor as the angel arrives. A separate wing holds the **Museo della Citta**, with archaeological finds and a section on Beniamino Gigli, the tenor born in Recanati who rivalled Caruso on the great opera stages of the early twentieth century. Allow about ninety minutes for both museums together.
