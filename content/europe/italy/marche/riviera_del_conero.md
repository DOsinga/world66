The Riviera del Conero is a short stretch of Adriatic coastline south of [Ancona](/europe/italy/marche/riviera_del_conero/ancona) in the [Marche](/europe/italy/marche) region, where [Monte Conero](/europe/italy/marche/riviera_del_conero/monte_conero) drops steeply into the sea. It is one of the most scenic sections of Italy's east coast — a welcome contrast to the flat, developed beaches that line most of the Adriatic.

The main towns are [Sirolo](/europe/italy/marche/riviera_del_conero/sirolo) and [Numana](/europe/italy/marche/riviera_del_conero/numana), both perched on cliffs above the water, with narrow streets and views across the bay. The beaches below are rocky and dramatic, especially the [Spiaggia delle Due Sorelle](/europe/italy/marche/riviera_del_conero/spiaggia_due_sorelle) (Beach of the Two Sisters), named after twin rock stacks that rise from the water. You can only reach it by boat or a steep trail, which keeps it relatively uncrowded. Inland, the hill town of [Castelfidardo](/europe/italy/marche/riviera_del_conero/castelfidardo) is known for its accordion-making tradition.
