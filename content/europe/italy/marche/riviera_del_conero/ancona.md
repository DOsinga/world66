Ancona is the regional capital, an old Greek and Roman port at the north end of the Riviera. Highlights are short and worthwhile: the Arch of Trajan (115 AD) on the old harbour mole, the Romanesque cathedral of San Ciriaco perched on the Guasco hill above the city, and the Pinacoteca Comunale with works by Titian, Lotto and Crivelli.

The waterfront is gritty rather than pretty — this is a working ferry port for Croatia and Greece — but the old centre repays a half day. Easy as a half-day trip from Sirolo or as a stop on the way through.
