Castelfidardo is a small hilltown ten kilometres inland from the Riviera, best known as the world capital of accordion making. The industry started here in 1863 and the town still has dozens of small workshops; the Museo Internazionale della Fisarmonica in the centre traces the history and houses a remarkable collection of historic instruments.

The town was also the site of the 1860 Battle of Castelfidardo, in which Piedmontese troops defeated the Papal States — a step in the unification of Italy. A monument and small museum mark the battlefield outside town. Worth half a day on a quieter beach day.
