[Ancona](/europe/italy/marche/ancona) — the regional capital, with its Roman Arch of Trajan, the hilltop cathedral of San Ciriaco, and a working ferry port — is twenty minutes north and good for a half day.

The hill towns of the Marche interior are all within an hour. [Recanati](/europe/italy/marche/recanati), about 25 kilometres inland, is the birthplace of the poet Giacomo Leopardi and holds three Lorenzo Lotto altarpieces in its Museo Civico. [Loreto](/europe/italy/marche/loreto), fifteen kilometres south, is built around the Holy House and ranks among the most visited Marian shrines in Europe. [Castelfidardo](/europe/italy/marche/castelfidardo) is the world capital of accordion making, with a museum of historic instruments and a Risorgimento battlefield; nearby Osimo is known for its Roman cisterns. Senigallia, half an hour up the coast, has the most elegant seafront in the region.
