title: Day trips
type: section
---

Ancona — port city, Roman arch of Trajan, hilltop cathedral — is twenty minutes north. The hilltowns of the Marche interior are within an hour: Recanati (Leopardi's birthplace, Lorenzo Lotto altarpieces), Loreto (the Holy House and one of the most-visited Marian shrines in Europe), Castelfidardo and Osimo for the accordion museum and Roman cisterns respectively. Senigallia, half an hour up the coast, has the most elegant seafront in the region.
