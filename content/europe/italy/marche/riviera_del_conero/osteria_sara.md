A small, family-run osteria in the historic centre of Sirolo. Short menu of seasonal Marchigiano dishes — pasta with seafood ragù, grilled local fish, vincisgrassi (a Marche lasagna) — at honest prices. The kind of low-key local place you go to when you have had enough of beach restaurants.
