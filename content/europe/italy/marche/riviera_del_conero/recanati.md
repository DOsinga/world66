Recanati, about 25 kilometres inland from the Riviera, is best known as the birthplace of the poet Giacomo Leopardi. His family palace, the Casa Leopardi, contains the library where he spent his teenage years and is open as a small museum. The town also has good Lorenzo Lotto altarpieces in the Museo Civico (Villa Colloredo Mels) — three works including a famous Annunciation.

The historic centre is on a long ridge with panoramic views toward the Adriatic. Half a day, more if you are a Leopardi reader.
