---
title: When to go
type: section
---

June and September are the sweet spot. July and August are hot, crowded with Italian holidaymakers, and the cliff beaches need to be booked or hiked to very early in the day. The shoulder months of May and October are excellent for hiking on Monte Conero but cool for swimming; the macchia is in flower in May.

The park trails are open year-round but the beach boats from Numana run roughly Easter to October.
