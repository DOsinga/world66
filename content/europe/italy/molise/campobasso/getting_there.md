---
title: "Getting There"
type: section
---

**By car**, Campobasso is most easily reached from Naples (2 hours via the A1 and SS87), Rome (3 hours), or Foggia (1.5 hours). The city sits on the SS87 and SS17, neither of which is a fast road. **By train**, Campobasso has a station on the Campobasso–Benevento and Campobasso–Termoli–Pescara lines, but services are slow and infrequent; the journey from Naples requires a change at Benevento and takes over 3 hours. **By bus**, SAIS and Marino operate coaches to and from Rome (about 3 hours), Naples (2.5 hours), and Foggia (1.5 hours). Buses are generally faster and more frequent than trains for connections to major cities.
