---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

Termoli has a textbook Adriatic climate: hot summers, mild winters, and a long shoulder season. **June and September** are the best months — water warm enough to swim, beaches uncrowded, restaurants relaxed. **July and August** see the town packed with Italian holidaymakers, especially the first three weeks of August.

The festival of San Basso (4 August) is the town's biggest event: the silver statue of the patron saint is carried out to sea on a flotilla of fishing boats and blessed before being returned to the cathedral. The Festival del Mare in late July fills the lungomare with food stalls and live music. **Winter** is quiet and many seasonal businesses close, but the borgo is atmospheric in a damp, foggy way and the trains are still frequent.
