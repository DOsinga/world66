---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

Alagna is primarily a winter resort and the ski season (roughly December to April) is when most visitors come. The best snow conditions are typically January and February, when the off-piste terrain is at its finest after fresh snowfall. The cable car to Punta Indren is the key infrastructure; check its status before planning a trip, as it can be closed for wind or maintenance.

Summer (July to September) is excellent for hiking. The lifts run for walkers, the high-altitude routes are snow-free from late June, and the valley has a quiet, working-mountain character that is very different from the winter version. June and early July can still have snow on the upper routes.

Spring (May to June) and autumn (October to November) are shoulder seasons when many restaurants and accommodation options are closed and the village is very quiet. Worth knowing if you want solitude; not ideal if you want services.
