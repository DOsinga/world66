---
title: Eating Out
type: section
---

Asti is a good place to eat Piedmontese food without turning dinner into a performance. Look for agnolotti, tajarin, vitello tonnato, bagna cauda in season, hazelnut desserts, and local Barbera by the glass. The sweeter Moscato d'Asti is best treated as a light dessert wine rather than a generic aperitif.
