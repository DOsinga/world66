Marchesi di Barolo is one of the village's classic names, with cellars close enough to the castle that you can fold it into a short Barolo visit. Tastings here are useful because they connect the grand label to the actual village streets and hills around you.

Book ahead if your timing matters, especially in harvest season and on weekends. This is not the place for a rushed glass between buses; Barolo rewards a slower pace.
