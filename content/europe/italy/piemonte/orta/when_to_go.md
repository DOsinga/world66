---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

Orta is at its best in late spring (May and early June) and early autumn (September), when the weather is warm but not hot, the lake is calm, and the day-trip crowds are present but not overwhelming. July and August are busier and warmer, but the lake provides a constant breeze and the small size of Orta means it never feels as packed as Stresa or Como.

Winter is very quiet. Many restaurants and small hotels close from November to March, and ferry service to the island runs on a reduced schedule, but the empty piazza and bare trees give the town a melancholy beauty of their own. If you do come in winter, check that what you want is open before making the trip.
