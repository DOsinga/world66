Parco della Villa Pallavicino is a gentle change of pace from Stresa's boat trips and lakefront promenades. The estate spreads across lawns, old trees, flowerbeds, and paths with views back toward Lake Maggiore, so it works as both a garden visit and a relaxed family outing.

The park is best known for its animal areas and open spaces rather than a single showpiece monument. It is an easy choice when you want a slower afternoon on land before returning to the Borromean Islands or the lakeside cafes.
