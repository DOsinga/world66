Castello di Rivoli is worth the trip out from Turin for the collision alone: a grand unfinished Savoy residence turned into one of Italy’s most important contemporary art museums. The old palace rooms, long views toward the Alps, and ambitious installations give the museum a scale that central Turin galleries cannot match.

It is not in the historic centre, so plan it as a half-day rather than a casual detour. Travellers interested in architecture get Juvarra’s royal project; art travellers get Arte Povera, international contemporary work, and exhibitions that use the castle’s spaces rather than pretending they are neutral white boxes.
