The Chapel of the Merchants is a small Baroque chapel built from 1680 for Turin's pious congregation of bankers, shopkeepers, and merchants. Its richly decorated interior turns a narrow city chapel into a compact display of sacred art, trade symbolism, and theatrical Baroque detail.

It is a good short stop in the Quadrilatero Romano area, especially for visitors already seeing Santuario della Consolata, Via Garibaldi, and Turin's older commercial streets.
