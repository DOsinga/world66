The countryside around Turin is studded with Savoy palaces, hilltop villages, and the vineyard-covered hills of the Langhe and Monferrato. Several of the royal residences, collectively a UNESCO World Heritage Site, make excellent half-day excursions, while the Po River itself offers a scenic route south through parkland.

For wine country, head into the Langhe (Alba, Barolo, La Morra) or [Monferrato](/europe/italy/piemonte/monferrato), where the rolling hills produce Barbera, Grignolino, and Moscato among UNESCO-listed wine cellars and medieval castles. For mountain air, [Alagna](/europe/italy/piemonte/alagna) at the foot of Monte Rosa offers steep off-piste skiing in winter and high-alpine hiking in summer. For lakes, [Stresa](/europe/italy/piemonte/stresa) on Lago Maggiore gives you the Borromean Islands and Liberty-style grand hotels, while [Orta San Giulio](/europe/italy/piemonte/orta) is quieter and charms almost everyone who makes the effort. Each is comfortably reachable in a day by train or car.
