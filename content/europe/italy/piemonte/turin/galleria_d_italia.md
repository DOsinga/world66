Galleria d'Italia is Intesa Sanpaolo's Turin museum, housed in the Palazzo Turinetti di Pertengo near Piazza San Carlo. The branch opened in 2022 and focuses mainly on photography, with exhibitions drawn from the bank's art holdings and temporary shows.

It is an easy central stop if you are already visiting the Egyptian Museum, Piazza San Carlo, or Palazzo Madama, and it adds a contemporary museum note to Turin's heavily historic centre.
