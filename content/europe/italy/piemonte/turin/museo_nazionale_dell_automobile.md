The Museo Nazionale dell'Automobile is Turin's major car museum, with more than 170 vehicles tracing the history of motoring from early carriages to racing machines and Italian sports cars. The collection fits the city well: Turin was the heart of Italy's car industry, and the museum gives that industrial history a polished, visitor-friendly setting.
