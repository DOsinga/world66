Villa La Tesoriera is a quieter Turin stop, a Baroque villa built in the early 18th century and set inside a public park on Corso Francia. It lacks the imperial scale of the Savoy showpieces, but that makes it useful: you see aristocratic Turin at a more local, lived-in scale.

The villa now houses the Andrea Della Corte civic music library, so most travelers come for the exterior, the park, and the sense of a western neighbourhood with an elegant historical anchor. It is best paired with time in nearby parks or as a break from the denser centre.
