---
title: Eating Out
type: section
---

Pugliese cooking is one of the best arguments for visiting this corner of Italy: orecchiette with cime di rapa, fave e cicoria, burrata so fresh it barely holds together, and grilled lamb seasoned with nothing more than olive oil and herbs. In Alberobello, several restaurants operate inside converted trulli, which adds to the atmosphere even if it sometimes adds to the price.
