title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

The essential Bari is [Bari Vecchia](/europe/italy/puglia/bari/bari_vecchia), the old town on the headland — visit the [Basilica di San Nicola](/europe/italy/puglia/bari/basilica_san_nicola), the [Swabian Castle](/europe/italy/puglia/bari/swabian_castle), and [Strada delle Orecchiette](/europe/italy/puglia/bari/strada_orecchiette) where local women still shape pasta by hand in front of their homes. The [Lungomare](/europe/italy/puglia/bari/lungomare) promenade along the seafront is one of the longest in Italy. For culture, [Teatro Petruzzelli](/europe/italy/puglia/bari/teatro_petruzzelli), Italy's fourth-largest opera house, reopened in 2009 after a notorious arson. Bari is also an excellent base for day trips into [Puglia](/europe/italy/puglia) — [Alberobello](/europe/italy/puglia/alberobello), [Lecce](/europe/italy/puglia/lecce), and [Castellana Grotte](/europe/italy/puglia/castellana_grotte) are all reachable within an hour.
