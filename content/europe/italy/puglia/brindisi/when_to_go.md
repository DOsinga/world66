---
title: When to Go
type: section
---

Brindisi has the hot Mediterranean climate of Puglia's heel, with dry summers and mild winters. July and August are reliably sunny and warm, with temperatures regularly topping 30°C, but the city fills with ferry passengers heading to Greece and the crowds on the waterfront can be significant.

Spring — especially April and May — is the best time to visit. The light is excellent, the sea breezes keep things comfortable, and the city goes about its normal business. October is also pleasant, with warm sea temperatures and quieter streets. December and January are mild by northern European standards but can be grey and occasionally wet.

The Feast of Sant'Teodoro d'Amasea, Brindisi's patron saint, falls in early November and draws processions through the old town.
