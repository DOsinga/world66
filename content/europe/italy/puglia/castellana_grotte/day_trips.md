Castellana sits in easy striking distance of some of Puglia's best-known towns. The trulli of [Alberobello](/europe/italy/puglia/alberobello) are fifteen kilometres south — easily seen in half a day. The coastal town of [Monopoli](/europe/italy/puglia/monopoli) is twenty kilometres east, with its small harbour, old town, and accessible beaches. [Bari](/europe/italy/puglia/bari), the regional capital, is forty kilometres north and reachable by direct train.
