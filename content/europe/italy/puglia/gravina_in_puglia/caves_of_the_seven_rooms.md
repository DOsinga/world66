The Caves of the Seven Rooms are a group of interconnected man-made caves in Gravina's ravine landscape. Early Christian Greek inscriptions and symbols point to the presence of some of the area's first Christian communities. They are best visited as part of a walk through Gravina's cave churches and rock-cut sites.
