The Chapter Museum of Sacred Art displays religious works from Gravina's cathedral chapter, with objects dating from the 11th to the 20th century. The collection includes embroidered vestments, liturgical silver, paintings, and devotional pieces that add useful context to the nearby cathedral and old quarter.
