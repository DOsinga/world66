Gemmis-Pellicciari Palace is an 18th-century noble residence in Gravina's old centre. Built by the Pellicciari family and later associated with the de Gemmis barons, it has an elegant facade, arched details, and two small towers.

It is mainly an architectural stop, best seen as part of a walk through the historic quarter.
