The Rock Church of Madonna della Stella is a cave sanctuary tied to a tradition of a fresco of the Madonna and Child marked by a star. The site may have had earlier pagan use before becoming a Marian shrine in the 16th century.

It is part of Gravina in Puglia's ravine landscape, and is best visited with the nearby bridge, cave churches, and old quarter.
