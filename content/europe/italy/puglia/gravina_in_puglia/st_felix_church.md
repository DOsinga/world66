St. Felix Church is one of the smaller historic churches woven into Gravina in Puglia's old centre, where religious buildings, cave dwellings, and ravine-edge lanes sit close together. Its value is less about monumentality than about how it fits into the town's dense stone landscape.

Visit it while walking between the cathedral area, the ravine viewpoints, and the older quarters. It is a brief stop, but it adds another layer to Gravina's long continuity of churches, rock-cut spaces, and neighbourhood devotion.
