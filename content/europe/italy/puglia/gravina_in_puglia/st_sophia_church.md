St. Sophia Church is a small historic church in Gravina's old quarter, rebuilt in its present form near the end of the 15th century. Its single nave preserves a gilded wooden ceiling with rosettes and a wooden women's gallery, making it a worthwhile stop for visitors already exploring the cathedral area and the ravine-side lanes.
