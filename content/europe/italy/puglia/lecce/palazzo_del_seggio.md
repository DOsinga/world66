Palazzo del Seggio, better known locally as the Sedile, stands on Piazza Sant'Oronzo in the thick of Lecce's old centre. Its mix of Gothic arches and Renaissance detail gives the square one of its stranger architectural notes, especially beside the small Venetian-style church of San Marco.

It is a quick stop rather than a long visit, but it belongs on any walk through central Lecce. The building helps explain why the piazza feels layered rather than simply Baroque.
