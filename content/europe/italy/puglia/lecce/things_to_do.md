---
title: Things to Do
type: section
---

Lecce's Baroque churches and palaces are the main draw. Most of the city's highlights are clustered in the old centre, and the best approach is simply to walk — you will stumble on something remarkable every few minutes.

Start at [Piazza Sant'Oronzo](/europe/italy/puglia/lecce/piazza_santoronzo), where a Roman column and a partly-excavated amphitheatre sit in the open air. Work your way to [Santa Croce](/europe/italy/puglia/lecce/santacroce), the most spectacular Baroque façade in the city, and the [Duomo](/europe/italy/puglia/lecce/duomo) with its enclosed piazza — one of the finest squares in southern Italy. The [Museo Faggiano](/europe/italy/puglia/lecce/museo_faggiano) on Via Ascanio Grandi is worth an hour for its extraordinary layering of history beneath a single house.
