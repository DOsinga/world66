---
title: When to Go
type: section
---

April, May, and September are the best months for Lecce. The heat is manageable, the Adriatic coast nearby is swimmable from May, and the city is busy but not overwhelmed. The Puglia tourist season peaks in July and August, when hotel prices rise steeply and the coast becomes crowded; Lecce itself is less affected than the beach towns since it has no beach of its own.

October and November are pleasant and quiet. The light in autumn is beautiful on the golden stone of the Baroque churches. December can be cold and some restaurants close, but the Christmas market in Piazza Sant'Oronzo is worth knowing about.

Avoid the hottest weeks of July and August for walking around the old town — the stone radiates heat and the streets have limited shade.
