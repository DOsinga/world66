The Church of Madonna della Greca is a small, older-feeling church near the edge of the historic centre. Its stonework and quieter scale make a good contrast with San Giorgio, and it is often the more atmospheric stop.

Do not expect grand spectacle. The appeal is the sense of age and continuity, the way the church seems tucked into the old town rather than placed above it.
