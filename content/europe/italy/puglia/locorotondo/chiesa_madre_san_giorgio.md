The Church of San Giorgio Martire is Locorotondo’s main church, rising in the centre of the old town with a neoclassical facade that stands out against the smaller white houses around it. It gives the town a formal focus without overwhelming its village scale.

Step inside for a quiet break from the lanes and summer glare. The church is most interesting as part of the urban rhythm: square, facade, side streets, and then the old town curling away again in its circular pattern.
