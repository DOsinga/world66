The Ecomuseum of Valle d'Itria is a heritage museum network founded with the municipalities of Alberobello, Cisternino, Fasano, Locorotondo, Martina Franca and Monopoli.

For visitors based in Locorotondo, it is most useful as context for the wider Itria Valley: trulli, dry-stone landscapes, rural culture and the small towns that share the valley's identity.
