---
title: "Bars and Cafes"
type: section
---

The summer evening passeggiata loops through the centro storico and ends at the harbour, where a string of bars set tables out on the quay. Drinks are inexpensive by southern European standards — a spritz is around €5, a glass of Puglian rosato about the same. Aperitivo time stretches long here, and many places will bring small plates of taralli, focaccia, and olives without being asked.
