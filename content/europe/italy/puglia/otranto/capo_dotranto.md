Cape Palascìa, the easternmost tip of mainland Italy, sits about 6 kilometres south of Otranto. From the cliffs you look directly across the Strait of Otranto — only 72 kilometres to Albania — and the colour of the water shifts in bands from turquoise to deep blue.

The Punta Palascia lighthouse, built in 1869 under the post-unification government, has been restored as a small environmental museum. The whole cape is part of the Costa Otranto-Santa Maria di Leuca natural park, and a coastal path links it back along the cliffs to the Baia delle Orte and eventually Otranto itself — a fine walk of two to three hours.
