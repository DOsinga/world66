---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

Otranto is at its best in **May, June, and September**, when the sea is warm enough to swim, the light is sharp, and the old town is not yet packed. July and August are very busy — Italians from across the country book the Salento months in advance, and prices in the historic centre rise sharply.

The town's biggest event is the **Festa dei Santi Martiri** on 13–15 August, marking the anniversary of the 1480 massacre with processions, a re-enactment of the siege, and fireworks over the castle. Outside summer, **April through early May** is quiet but pleasant for walking the coast, and the cathedral and castle are uncrowded.

Winter is mild but most beach restaurants close from November through March; the historic centre stays open year round but with a much shorter list of working trattorias.
