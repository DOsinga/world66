Grotta delle Rondinelle is one of the larger sea caves along Polignano a Mare's rocky coast. It has a wide sea entrance, a small pebble landing inside, and a second opening formed by a collapsed sinkhole above the cave. Boat trips give the easiest view of its double entrance and the limestone walls just west of town.
