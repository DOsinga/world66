---
title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

The old town (Città Vecchia) is the heart of the visit: walk from the [Castello Aragonese](/europe/italy/puglia/taranto/castello_aragonese) at the southern tip up through the narrow streets to the [Cathedral of San Cataldo](/europe/italy/puglia/taranto/taranto_cathedral), with the remains of the [Temple of Poseidon](/europe/italy/puglia/taranto/temple_of_poseidon) standing in Piazza Castello along the way. The [Ponte Girevole](/europe/italy/puglia/taranto/ponte_girevole) connecting the old town to the new city is a remarkable piece of engineering still in active use. Across the bridge in the new town, the [National Archaeological Museum MArTA](/europe/italy/puglia/taranto/marta) contains the finest collection of Magna Graecia goldwork anywhere in the world.
