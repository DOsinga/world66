The Via Marche Necropolis is one of the archaeological traces of ancient Taranto outside the headline museum cases. Tombs and burial finds from areas like this help explain the city's Greek colonial wealth and its later Roman life.

It is a specialist stop, but a useful one if the Museo Archeologico Nazionale has sparked your interest in Taranto's underground layers. Check access locally, as small archaeological areas can have variable opening conditions.
