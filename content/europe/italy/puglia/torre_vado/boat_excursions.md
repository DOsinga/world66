Boat excursions are the easiest way to turn Torre Vado from a beach village into a proper day out. Several operators leave from the small harbour for the sea caves around Santa Maria di Leuca, usually with swimming stops and short routes along the Ionian coast.

In high summer, book ahead and compare routes before paying. Some trips only cover the Ionian caves, while longer ones round the cape toward the Adriatic side.
