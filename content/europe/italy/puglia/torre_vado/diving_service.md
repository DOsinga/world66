Diving Service runs dives and courses from Torre Vado, using the clear water and rocky seabed around the southern Salento coast. The draw is the mix of caves, walls, and wreck sites between Torre Vado and Leuca.

Conditions and schedules depend on weather, so treat it as something to arrange locally rather than a fixed sightseeing stop.
