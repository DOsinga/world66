The Torre Vado watchtower is the village landmark, built in the 16th century when the coast of the Kingdom of Naples needed warning posts against raids from the sea. Its squat cylindrical form, battlements, and sea-facing position explain the village name better than any signboard.

The tower is privately held and not normally open to visitors, so the experience is exterior only. It is still worth seeing while walking the waterfront.
