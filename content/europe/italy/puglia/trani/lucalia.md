A more relaxed harbour-side option on Via Banchina al Porto with tables outside almost on the quay. The kitchen does the classics well — fresh anchovies, grilled octopus, spaghetti alle vongole — at fairer prices than some of the neighbours. Lunch with a view of the cathedral across the water is hard to beat on a clear day. Pace yourself: the antipasti alone can fill a table.
