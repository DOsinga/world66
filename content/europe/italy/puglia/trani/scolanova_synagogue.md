---

One of the oldest synagogues still in use in Europe. The plain stone façade gives little away — a single pointed arched doorway and a few small windows — but inside is a single high-ceilinged room with a women's gallery and a quiet medieval atmosphere. Trani in the Middle Ages had one of the most important Jewish communities in southern Italy, with four synagogues clustered on the streets known as **La Giudecca**. The Scolanova is the only one still functioning as such. Opening hours are limited; check the schedule of the small adjoining museum.
