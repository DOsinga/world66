---
title: When to Go
type: section
---

June and early July are the sweet spot — the sea is warm enough for comfortable swimming, the ferry connections are running their full summer schedule, and the peak-season crowds have not yet arrived. August is peak season: ferries are packed, the beach is full by 10am, and accommodation prices double. Late August and September are excellent again once the Italian summer holiday period ends.

In October the islands quieten dramatically. The sea remains warm into October and the weather is often still fine, but some services reduce or stop. The winter ferry from Termoli runs year-round but is infrequent. Most accommodation closes between November and March.
