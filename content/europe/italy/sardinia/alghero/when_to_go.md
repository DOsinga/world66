---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

Alghero is at its best from May to October. July and August are the peak tourist months, when beaches fill and prices rise sharply; June and September offer good weather with smaller crowds and more reasonable rates. The sea is swimmable from late May through October, and the dive sites around Capo Caccia are calmest in summer.

Winter in Alghero is mild — rarely below 10°C — and the old town is quiet and pleasant for walking. Some restaurants close for parts of November through February. If you are visiting primarily for the architecture and history rather than the beach, autumn or spring offers the best combination of comfortable temperatures and manageable visitor numbers.
