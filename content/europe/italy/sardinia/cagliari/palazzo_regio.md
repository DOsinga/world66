Palazzo Regio was the residence of royal representatives in Cagliari from the medieval period onward. The building was rebuilt and restored over later centuries, leaving a layered civic palace in the Castello district.

It is a natural add-on to Cagliari Cathedral and the surrounding old town streets, especially for visitors interested in Sardinia's royal and administrative history.
