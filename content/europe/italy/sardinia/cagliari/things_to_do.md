---
title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Cagliari's sights divide between the hilltop Castello quarter — the medieval citadel with its Pisan towers, cathedral, and archaeological museum — and the lower city with its early Christian basilica, Roman amphitheatre, and the vast Poetto beach. The Nuragic bronzes in the National Archaeological Museum alone justify the trip for anyone interested in prehistoric cultures. Allow at least two days.
