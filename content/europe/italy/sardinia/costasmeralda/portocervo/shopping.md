---
title: "Shopping"
type: section
---

Porto Cervo's shopping is concentrated in two arcaded spaces designed as part of the original Costa Smeralda plan. The **Piazzetta delle Chiacchiere** and the warren of stairs and alleys called the **Sottopiazza** between it and the marina hold most of the international luxury houses — Hermès, Gucci, Dolce & Gabbana, Cavalli — alongside a few independent boutiques selling Sardinian linen, ceramics, and the local filigree silver jewellery.

Prices are firmly at high-season Italian resort levels. Most shops open only from June to mid-September; outside that window the village is largely shuttered. For Sardinian crafts at sensible prices, look further afield — the markets in **Arzachena** or **Olbia** are a better bet than the boutiques in the resort itself.
