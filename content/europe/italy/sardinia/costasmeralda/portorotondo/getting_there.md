---
title: Getting There
type: section
---

The nearest airport is Olbia Costa Smeralda (OLB), 18 km south. Ryanair, easyJet, and Italian carriers operate seasonal services from most major European cities from April to October. Taxis from the airport cost around €35–45 to Porto Rotondo; car hire is available at the airport and is strongly recommended if you plan to explore the coast and nearby beaches.

By ferry, Olbia is the major port for Sardinia's northeastern coast, with connections from Genova, Livorno, Civitavecchia, and Piombino. Journey times range from 4 to 10 hours depending on origin.

There is no direct public bus service to Porto Rotondo. From Olbia, taxis and hire cars are the practical options.
