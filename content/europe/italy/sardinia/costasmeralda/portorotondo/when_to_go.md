---
title: When to Go
type: section
---

Porto Rotondo is a summer resort and operates almost entirely in June, July, and August. In these months the village is at full capacity: the marina full, the restaurants booked, the beaches crowded, and the piazzetta busy until midnight. Prices are at their highest, especially in the first two weeks of August.

June and September offer a noticeably better experience: the weather is warm (25–28°C), the sea swimmable, the crowds reduced, and the prices lower. In September the light turns softer and the evenings cooler; many consider this the finest time on the north Sardinian coast.

Outside the summer season, most of Porto Rotondo closes completely. Accommodation and restaurants operate on a skeleton schedule from October to May at best, and several months of the year the village is effectively empty. It is not a place for an off-season visit.
