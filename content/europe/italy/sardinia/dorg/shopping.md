---
title: "Shopping"
type: section
---

Dorgali is one of the best towns in Sardinia for traditional crafts. Leather goods — belts, bags, sandals — are made in workshops you can walk into and watch. The town also has weavers producing traditional Sardinian textiles (copertine and tappeti), and ceramics decorated with local motifs. Most workshops sell directly and prices are reasonable. The main concentration is along the town centre streets; no specific market day, just browse.
