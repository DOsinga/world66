Chiesa di Santa Maria Maddalena is the main church of La Maddalena town, with 18th-century roots and a restrained neo-classical frontage. It is a short, worthwhile stop while wandering the centre, especially if you want a quiet interior between ferry arrivals, cafes, and harbour views.
