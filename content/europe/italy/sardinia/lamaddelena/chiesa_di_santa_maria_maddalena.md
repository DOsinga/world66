Chiesa di Santa Maria Maddalena is the main church of La Maddalena town. The church dates from the 18th century, though the current neoclassical facade reflects later rebuilding.

It is a simple but worthwhile stop while walking the old town between the ferry harbour, the waterfront, and the small central squares.
