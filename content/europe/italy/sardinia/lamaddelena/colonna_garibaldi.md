Colonna Garibaldi is one of the first landmarks you see when arriving by ferry in La Maddalena. The granite column is dedicated to Giuseppe Garibaldi, whose name is tied closely to the nearby island of Caprera.

It is a quick stop rather than a destination in itself, but it anchors the waterfront arrival point and makes a simple starting marker for a walk through Maddalena town.
