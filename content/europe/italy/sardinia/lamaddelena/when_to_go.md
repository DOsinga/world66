---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

June and September are the ideal months. The water is warm, the ferries and boat tours are running at full frequency, and the archipelago has not yet reached the July-August peak when prices spike and beaches fill. In September the light is golden, the sea remains warm into late month, and the crowds thin noticeably after mid-August.

July and August are peak season: high prices, queues for ferries, and competition for space on popular beaches like Cala Coticcio. Book accommodation well ahead if visiting in this window. The boat tours are also at their most crowded. That said, the weather is reliably good and the atmosphere is lively.

May is pleasant but the sea is still cool for swimming (around 18–20°C). October onwards brings rain and rough seas; most tourist facilities close by late October and ferry services reduce to winter schedules.
