Teatro Michelucci is an open-air theatre in Olbia designed by the Italian architect Giovanni Michelucci. It is a modest stop, but worthwhile for travellers interested in modern Italian architecture and in the civic spaces beyond Olbia's port and old centre.

See it as part of a short architecture walk rather than as a destination on its own.
