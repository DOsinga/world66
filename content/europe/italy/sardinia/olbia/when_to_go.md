---
title: When to Go
type: section
---

June and September are the best months to visit Olbia and the surrounding coast. The weather is reliably warm and sunny, the sea is swimmable, the tourist infrastructure is fully open, but the crowds and prices of high summer have not yet arrived or have just departed. In July and August the Costa Smeralda operates at maximum intensity — beaches are packed, roads are congested, and accommodation prices roughly double.

October through May is quiet and much cheaper. Many beach-facing businesses close, but Olbia's urban facilities and most restaurants stay open year-round. The shoulder months bring excellent light for photography and pleasant temperatures for walking. Winter weather is mild by northern European standards, though rain and wind from the tramontane can be persistent.

Ferry services from the mainland operate year-round but are most frequent in summer; budget flights from northern Europe typically run April to October only.
