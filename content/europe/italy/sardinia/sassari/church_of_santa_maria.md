The Church of Santa Maria di Betlem is the oldest church in Sassari, founded in 1106 by Benedictine monks. The building's facade is a palimpsest of Sardinian architectural history: Romanesque lower sections, a Gothic rose window, and Spanish Renaissance details added over the centuries. The interior contains notable Gothic carved stonework and some significant medieval frescoes.

The church is closely associated with the *Discesa dei Candelieri* (Descent of the Candles) — a Sassari festival held every August in honour of the Virgin Mary. The guilds of the city carry enormous decorated wooden candles through the streets to the church in a procession that has been held continuously since 1582. The candelabras themselves are housed in the church and are extraordinary works of folk art.
