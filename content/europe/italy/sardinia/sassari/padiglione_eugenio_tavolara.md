The Padiglione Eugenio Tavolara is a small but distinctive 1950s pavilion built for Sardinian crafts and design. Its interest is as much architectural as cultural, with a modernist building by Ubaldo Badas used as an exhibition space for local applied arts.

It is an easy stop while walking central Sassari, especially for visitors interested in mid-century architecture or Sardinian craft traditions.
