---
title: Things to Do
type: section
---

Sassari is a real Sardinian city rather than a tourist resort, which means it offers something rare on the island: everyday Italian life at close quarters. The old town, the cathedral, the Fountain of the Rosello, and the Museo Sanna are the core attractions. The evening passeggiata on Corso Vittorio Emanuele II is one of the best people-watching opportunities in northern Sardinia.
