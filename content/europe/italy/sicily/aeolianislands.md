The Aeolian Islands are a volcanic archipelago just north of [Sicily](/europe/italy/sicily). All seven inhabited islands are of volcanic origin, and on most of them some volcanic activity can still be experienced.

[Lipari](/europe/italy/sicily/lipari) is the largest island with the best tourist infrastructure and some superb black-sand beaches. [Vulcano](/europe/italy/sicily/vulcano) is the southernmost island, famous for its hot springs and sulphurous crater, and is best visited as a day trip from Lipari.

For real adventure, head to [Stromboli](/europe/italy/sicily/stromboli). The volcano is still very active, and a hike to the top of the crater at night to watch the eruptions is an experience not to be missed. Bring warm clothing and a sleeping bag.
