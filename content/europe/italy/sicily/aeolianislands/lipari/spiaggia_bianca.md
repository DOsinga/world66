Spiaggia Bianca lies on the east coast just north of Canneto, accessible by a road and steps or by boat from Canneto port. The sand is volcanic, mixed with pumice dust from the disused mines higher up — distinctly pale by Aeolian standards, and a striking contrast against the dark blue water. The quarry buildings, half-collapsed and partly swallowed by maquis, are part of the scene.

Bring water and a snack — there are limited facilities. The beach is busiest in the mornings; afternoons are calmer once the tour boats move on. The water is clear enough for casual snorkelling around the rocks at either end.
