The Biblioteca Lucchesiana is an ecclesiastical library founded in 1765 near Agrigento's cathedral. Its wood-panelled reading room, old shelving, and collection of manuscripts and early printed books make it a quiet counterpoint to the city's better-known archaeological sights.

Opening can be limited, so check locally before making a special trip. If it is open, it is worth a short visit while exploring the upper old town.
