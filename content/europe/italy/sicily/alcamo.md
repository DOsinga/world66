Alcamo sits inland from the Gulf of Castellammare, between [Palermo](/europe/italy/sicily/palermo) and [Trapani](/europe/italy/sicily/trapani). It is not as polished as the coastal towns, but it has a real western Sicilian centre: Corso VI Aprile, Piazza Ciullo, old churches, a castle, and a civic life built around wine, markets, and evening walks.

The town's best sights are close together in the centre. The [Castle of the Counts of Modica](/europe/italy/sicily/alcamo/castle_of_the_counts_of_modica) gives the place its medieval weight, while the [Basilica di Santa Maria Assunta](/europe/italy/sicily/alcamo/basilica_di_santa_maria_assunta) and smaller museums hold the art and devotional history. Above town, the [Bosco di Alcamo Nature Reserve](/europe/italy/sicily/alcamo/bosco_di_alcamo_nature_reserve) on Mount Bonifato offers a cooler, greener escape with views back toward the coast.

Alcamo works best if you are already exploring western Sicily by car. It is a practical base for nearby beaches and hill towns, but its own charm is slower and more local.
