The Arab Fountain is a small but old piece of Alcamo's water history, built to provide fresh water for people and livestock. It is still working, which gives it more life than many minor monuments.

It is not a headline sight, but it makes a worthwhile stop if you are already tracing Alcamo's older streets and castle area.
