The Arab Watchtower is an early medieval tower associated with Alcamo's Saracen period. Its stone spiral staircase and internal cistern give a compact but vivid look at the town's defensive history.

It is a short stop rather than a long visit, but it pairs well with a walk through Alcamo's older streets and castle area.
