The archaeological site on Mount Bonifato preserves traces of ancient Longuro, a settlement associated with Greek and later Roman occupation. Finds from the area include tomb caves, ceramics, bronze fragments, lamps, and Roman brick stamps. The ruins are fragmentary, but the hilltop setting adds landscape and context to a visit.
