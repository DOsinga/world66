The Church of Saint Anne is a 17th-century church and former Capuchin friary in Alcamo. The church has a single nave, seven chapels, and frescoes by Sicilian artists including Leonardo Mirabile, Giovanni Dato, Eugenio Ligotti, and Salvatore Gagliano.

It is a quiet stop rather than a headline sight, but the interior rewards a look if you are already exploring Alcamo's churches and old centre.
