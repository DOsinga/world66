The Church of Saint Francis of Assisi is one of Alcamo's historic churches, first built in the 13th century and later rebuilt between the early 17th and mid-17th centuries. Its interior preserves a marble altarpiece and sculptures associated with the Gagini workshop tradition.

It is a compact stop rather than a major sight, but it rewards visitors who are already walking Alcamo's old centre and want to understand the town beyond its castle and main basilica.
