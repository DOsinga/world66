The Church of Saints Paul and Bartholomew is one of Alcamo's Baroque churches, built between the 17th century and the late 1600s. Inside, look for the old Madonna del Miele painting, traditionally dated to around 1300.

It is a short stop in the old town rather than a place to plan a day around. Pair it with Alcamo's castle and main churches for a better sense of the town's layered religious architecture.
