The Ex College of Jesuits is a 17th-century complex in Alcamo's old centre. Later additions gave it an arcade, and the attached oratory preserves 18th-century frescoes by Domenico La Bruna.

Today the building houses the Museum of Contemporary Art of Alcamo and the civic library. It pairs well with the nearby Castle of the Counts of Modica on a walk through the historic centre.
