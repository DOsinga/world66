Fossilandia is a small paleontological park and cultural site near Alcamo Marina. It focuses on fossils and the prehistoric landscape of western Sicily, including material linked to the giant Pleistocene tortoise found in the area.

It is a modest but useful stop for families or travellers with a specific interest in fossils.
