Madonna dell'Alto is a sanctuary on Mount Bonifato above Alcamo. Its hilltop setting is the main draw for visitors: the climb or drive up gives wide views over the town, the surrounding countryside, and the coast beyond.

The sanctuary is also tied to local religious celebrations, including processions and dialect poetry recitations. It pairs naturally with the Bosco di Alcamo Nature Reserve on the same mountain.
