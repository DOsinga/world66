This small museum displays musical instruments collected around the world by Alcamo music teacher Fausto Cannone. The collection ranges across folk, ritual, and classical traditions, with instruments that are rarely seen together in a regional Sicilian town.

It pairs well with the Castle of the Counts of Modica and Alcamo's central churches.
