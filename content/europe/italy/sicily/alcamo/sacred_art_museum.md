The Sacred Art Museum in Alcamo displays paintings, sculpture and church treasures from the 13th to the 19th centuries. Many of the works were gathered for safekeeping after the 1968 Belice earthquake.

It is a small, focused museum rather than a broad art gallery, useful if you are already exploring Alcamo's historic centre and churches.
