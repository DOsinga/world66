Saints Cosma and Damiano's Church is a small Baroque church in Alcamo, originally built in 1500 and rebuilt in the early 18th century. Inside are two sculptures attributed to the Serpotta workshop, the main reason to step in.

It is a short stop rather than a destination in itself, best paired with Alcamo's cathedral and castle while walking through the old centre.
