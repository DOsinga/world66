Terme Gorga is a thermal bath complex outside Alcamo, using sulphur-rich waters traditionally associated with respiratory, rheumatic, and skin treatments. Facilities include pools and therapy services rather than a historic spa setting.

It is most useful as a relaxed bathing stop if you are already moving between Alcamo and Castellammare del Golfo. Check current opening times before going, as thermal facilities can change hours outside the main season.
