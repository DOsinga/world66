Calatafimi's Chiesa Madre is the town's main church, with medieval origins and a 16th-century rebuilding that gave it much of its present form. Inside are Roman arches, stucco work, paintings, marble details and carved wooden pieces that reward a slow look.

It is not the reason most travellers come to Calatafimi-Segesta, but it is a good counterpoint to the ancient Greek ruins nearby. Stop in if you are walking through the old centre.
