Badia Church, the former Church of San Lorenzo, is a small historic stop in Caltabellotta. Its main interest is the Gothic portal and a chapel with late sixteenth-century frescoes by Orazio Ferraro da Giuliana.

The building is now used as an auditorium, so it is not a major church visit in the usual sense. It is still worth a look while walking through the old town's steep lanes and viewpoints.
