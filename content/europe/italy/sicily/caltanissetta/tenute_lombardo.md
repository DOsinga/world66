Tenute Lombardo is a family wine estate in the hills south of Caltanissetta, with tastings that lean into the rural setting rather than a glossy tasting-room routine. Local bread and farm produce usually come with the wine.

The views are part of the draw: on clear days the countryside opens out from the Madonie toward the coast near Licata.
