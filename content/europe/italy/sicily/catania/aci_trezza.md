Aci Trezza is a small fishing village about ten kilometres north-east of Catania, famous for the basalt sea stacks just offshore — the Faraglioni dei Ciclopi, said in Homer to be the rocks thrown by the blinded Polyphemus at the fleeing Odysseus.

The village is the setting of Giovanni Verga's I Malavoglia (1881), one of the great Italian novels, and there is a small museum in the house used as a film set for the 1948 Visconti film La terra trema. Beyond literature, the village is simply a pleasant lunch stop with several seafront fish trattorias. Reachable by city bus from central Catania in about half an hour.
