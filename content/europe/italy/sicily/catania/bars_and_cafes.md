---
title: 'Bars and Cafes'
type: section
---

Catania has a strong café and bar culture, anchored by the university and the long, warm evenings. The classic move is a granita and brioche for breakfast (the pistachio or almond granita is the city standard), a coffee mid-morning, an aperitivo before dinner, and a final passeggiata gelato afterwards.

Piazza Università and the small streets just off it are the main aperitivo zone. The waterfront strip around the Ognina marina north-east of the centre is busier and louder, with the city's clubs. San Berillo, the half-demolished quarter east of Via Etnea, has the most interesting independent bars — small, late, and with a strong arts crowd.

**Prestipino**, Via Etnea 30, has been the city's classic café since the nineteenth century — pastries, granita, and the gold-standard cannoli filled to order. **Spinella**, Via Etnea 300, is the other historic café, with notable arancini.

**Vermouth**, Via Gemmellaro 36, in San Berillo, is the best of the independent aperitivo bars — small list, very Sicilian. **Razmataz**, Via Montesano 17, is a long-running music bar in the same quarter.
