San Berillo is a small but disorientingly different quarter east of Via Etnea, between the train station and the centre. The half-demolished streets are lined with battered baroque buildings, tenements with their facades sliced off, and the residue of a sex trade that has not entirely moved on. Over the last decade it has also become one of the most interesting arts neighbourhoods in Sicily, with bookshops, performance spaces, and a strip of small bars built into the empty shops.

The transformation is uneven and far from complete. It is worth a careful afternoon walk, particularly around Via delle Finanze and Via Pistone.
