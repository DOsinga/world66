Santa Teresa is a small Carmelite church a few blocks east of Via Etnea, with a distinctive pink-painted facade and a single bell tower. It is rarely on tourist itineraries but worth a glance if you are walking through.

The interior is restrained, with eighteenth-century altarpieces and a Madonna venerated by the local Carmelite community. Generally open during morning Mass only.
