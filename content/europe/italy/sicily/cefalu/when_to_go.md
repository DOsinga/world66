---
title: When to Go
type: section
---

Cefalù is at its best in May, June, and September. The sea is warm enough for swimming, the beach is manageable, the restaurants are less crowded, and the town feels like itself rather than a summer resort. July and August bring heavy Italian domestic tourism and higher prices; the beach fills up and the narrow streets get congested in the evenings.

Winter is mild — Cefalù's north-facing coast stays relatively warm by mainland Italian standards, and the town remains open and unhurried. The cathedral and the Mandralisca Museum can be visited in peace in November or February, and La Rocca is excellent in cool weather when the climb feels less punishing.
