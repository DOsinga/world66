The Agro-Forestry Museum of San Matteo sits in a traditional rural baglio on Monte Erice. Its small collection looks at the farming, forestry, and mountain work that shaped the countryside around Erice before tourism became the town's main trade.

It is a modest stop rather than a headline sight, but it adds useful context if you are interested in the landscape beyond Erice's stone streets and churches.
