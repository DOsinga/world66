The Norman castle at the southeastern tip of Erice sits on the very edge of the mountain, with sheer drops into the valley below on three sides. Built in the 12th century by the Normans over the site of the ancient temple of Venus Erycina, it occupies the most dramatic position in a village full of dramatic positions.

The castle is open for visits and the gardens around it offer what are arguably the best views in Erice: west across the salt flats to Trapani and the Egadi Islands, north along the coast, and — on exceptionally clear days — as far as Cape Bon in Tunisia. The interior is atmospheric if not extensively furnished. Allow 45 minutes to walk around the perimeter and take in the full panorama.
