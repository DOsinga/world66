The Chiaramontan Aqueduct is a surviving piece of the medieval water system linked to the Chiaramonte family. Its most visible remnant is the historic Difali fountain, a modest but worthwhile stop for visitors tracing Erice's walls, gates, and older infrastructure outside the main hilltop streets.
