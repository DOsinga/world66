The Church of Saint Catherine is a small medieval church in Erice, built in the 14th century and tied to the former palazzo of Giovanni Majorana. It is not one of the town's grand sights, but it adds another layer to a walk through Erice's stone lanes.

Stop by while exploring the old centre rather than making it a stand-alone destination.
