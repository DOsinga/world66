The Church of Saint Francis of Paola is a small single-nave church in Erice, originally linked to the adjacent hospital in the Majorana Soldier Palace. Its history as a hospital chapel gives it a different character from the town's larger churches.

It is a short architectural stop while walking Erice's old centre, best seen together with the Cordici Museum, Chiesa Madre, and the town walls.
