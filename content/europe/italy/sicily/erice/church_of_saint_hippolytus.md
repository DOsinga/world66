The Church of Saint Hippolytus is a small ruined medieval church on the edge of Erice. It preserves fragments of frescoes and incorporates the cave Oratory of Saint Gregory, linking the site to older hermit traditions in the area.

It is a quiet stop rather than a headline sight, but it adds texture to a walk through Erice's churches, walls, and stone lanes.
