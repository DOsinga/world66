The Church of Saint John the Baptist is a Gothic-style church with Erice's largest nave. Inside, look for marble work connected with the Gagini school, whose sculpture shaped many Sicilian churches.

It is best treated as part of a slow walk through Erice's stone lanes and churches rather than as a standalone reason to climb the hill.
