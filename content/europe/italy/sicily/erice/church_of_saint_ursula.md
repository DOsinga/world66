The Church of Saint Ursula sits on the quieter edge of Erice, away from the busiest lanes around the cathedral and castle. Its appeal is modest: old stone, devotional art, and the sense that Erice's religious life was spread through many small churches rather than concentrated in a single monument.

Stop if you are already walking the upper town walls or tracing the back streets toward the Spanish Quarter. It is not a headline sight, but it adds a softer, more local note to a hill town that can otherwise feel like a sequence of viewpoints and souvenir shops.
