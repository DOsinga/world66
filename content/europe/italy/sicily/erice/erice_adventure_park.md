Erice Adventure Park is a wooded activity park with aerial rope courses, zip lines, archery, and picnic areas below the hill town. It is aimed at families and active visitors who want a break from churches, walls, and viewpoints.

Check seasonal opening times before going, especially outside summer. It works best as a half-day outing combined with Erice or Trapani.
