Erice in Miniature displays a 1:100 scale model of the hill town, made with traditional materials and animated with mechanical and lighting effects. It is a small attraction, but the model helps make sense of Erice's compact street plan and landmarks.

It is best treated as a short stop between the town's churches, walls, and viewpoints.
