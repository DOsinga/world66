Laudicina House is an imposing former residence near the Balio Gardens, associated with Domenico Laudicina, a former mayor of Trapani. It brings together two older houses around a courtyard and adds ornate plasterwork with touches of Art Deco.

It is a small architectural stop rather than a major sight, but it fits naturally into a walk between Erice's castle, gardens, and old stone lanes.
