Ottoventi is a modern winery just below Erice, set where the old hill town starts to give way to vineyards and views across western Sicily. It is a better fit for curious travelers than for anyone simply looking for a bar: the draw is tasting local wines in the landscape that produces them.

The place makes sense as part of a slow day around Erice, especially if you have a car or driver and want a break from churches, stone lanes, and castle viewpoints. Book ahead when possible, since winery visits depend on opening hours and tastings rather than drop-in sightseeing.
