Palazzo Burgarella is a late-19th-century palazzo tied to the Burgarella family, whose wealth came from salt, tuna, and wider Mediterranean trading interests. The family developed commercial links from Sicily to North Africa and the Red Sea.

It is a short architectural stop while walking Erice's historic streets, especially for visitors interested in how the town's old merchant families shaped its built fabric.
