Palazzo Platamone is one of Erice's better-preserved aristocratic houses, a 17th-century palace built for a town of narrow lanes and serious family status. Its two facades and upper-level living rooms hint at how the local elite arranged space above the service areas below.

It is a small stop rather than a headline sight, but it adds useful texture to a walk through Erice. Look for it while moving between the churches, walls, and viewpoints.
