---
title: Beaches
type: section
---

Favignana’s beaches are often rocky coves rather than soft sand. Cala Rossa, Cala Azzurra, Bue Marino, Cala Rotonda, and Lido Burrone each have a different mood, but all are best outside the crush of August. Water shoes are useful, and a bike or scooter makes beach-hopping much easier.
