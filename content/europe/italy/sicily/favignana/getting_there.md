---
title: Getting There
type: section
---

Hydrofoils and ferries connect Favignana with Trapani, with seasonal links and excursion traffic from Marsala. Crossings are short, but weather can affect services, especially outside summer.

Trapani is the usual practical gateway, with rail and bus links to Palermo and Marsala. Book return boats ahead in high season; the last departures can sell out when day-trippers all try to leave at once.
