Giardino dell'Impossibile is a botanical garden created inside abandoned calcarenite quarries on Favignana. The stone walls, tunnels, pools, and Mediterranean planting make it feel very different from the island's beach stops.

The garden is seasonal and usually visited with a guide or audio guide, so check opening arrangements before cycling out.
