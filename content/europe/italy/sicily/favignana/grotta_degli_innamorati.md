Grotta degli Innamorati is a sea cave on Favignana's coast, reached by boat or by a rough coastal trail. Sunlight through the water gives the cave blue and green tones, and a small inner bay makes it a scenic swimming spot in calm conditions.

It is best treated as a short coastal adventure rather than a formal sight. Wear suitable shoes if approaching on foot, and avoid the cave when sea conditions are rough.
