Palazzo Florio stands near the port, a refined reminder of the family that reshaped Favignana’s economy through tuna fishing and industry. Its architecture feels almost delicate compared with the heavy working buildings of the tonnara nearby.

See it together with the former factory. The pair tells the story neatly: capital and labour, elegance and production, all concentrated in a small island town.
