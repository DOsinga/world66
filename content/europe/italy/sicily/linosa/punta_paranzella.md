Punta Paranzella is a rocky headland on the northwestern side of Linosa. The draw is the open view of the sea, the island's dark volcanic cliffs, and the seabirds that nest along the slopes.

It is a quiet walking and photography stop rather than a serviced attraction. Go with sturdy shoes and enough time to enjoy the coast without rushing back across the island.
