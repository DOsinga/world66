The Antonio Collisani Civic Museum is housed in a former Franciscan convent in Petralia Sottana. Its displays cover local geology and archaeology, including objects from the Collisani collection.

It is a compact but useful stop when exploring the old towns of the Madonie, especially for context on the area's natural and ancient history.
