Small church in Collesano with a rare local treasure: a 1688 canvas by Gaspare Perdichizzi showing the town before the 1693 earthquake. It is a modest stop, but worthwhile if you are exploring the Madonie villages and want a glimpse of how the area looked before Sicily's great Baroque rebuild.
