Geraci Siculo sits high in the eastern Madonie, a stone village with big views and a serious medieval pedigree. It was one of the Ventimiglia family's strongholds, and the ruined castle above town still explains why this ridge mattered.

The village is quiet, and that is part of its appeal. Come for a slow walk through lanes and viewpoints rather than a checklist of monuments. On a clear day the views across the mountains are worth the climb alone.
