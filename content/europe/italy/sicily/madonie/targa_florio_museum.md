The Targa Florio Museum is devoted to the endurance road race founded by Vincenzo Florio in 1906, one of the oldest motor races in the world. The collection covers the race's cars, drivers, routes, and deep connection with the mountain roads of the Madonie.

It is housed in Collesano's Municipality Palace, a former Dominican convent. Opening hours can be limited, so check locally before making a special trip.
