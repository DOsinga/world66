The Stagnone is a long, shallow lagoon enclosed by the **Isola Lunga**, a low sandbar parallel to the coast. At its widest it is barely two metres deep, and the warm calm water has made it one of the best kite-surfing spots in Europe — there are several schools along the shore.

It is also a working environment: salt pans on the landward side, fish farms in the open water, the island of Motya in the middle. A small protected area, the **Riserva Naturale Orientata dello Stagnone**, covers the whole lagoon and its islands. The road around it is the obvious circuit for a leisurely afternoon by car or bike.
