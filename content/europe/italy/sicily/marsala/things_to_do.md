---
title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Marsala is a slow, walkable town with three distinct things to come for: the Phoenician and Punic remains at the western edge of the city and on the island of [Motya](/europe/italy/sicily/marsala/mozia_island), the **fortified wine** that bears the city's name, and the sunsets over the [salt pans of the Stagnone lagoon](/europe/italy/sicily/marsala/saline_della_laguna). Add the old town's quiet baroque centre and a day fills itself easily.
