---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

Marsala is at its best in late spring and early autumn. April and May give long warm days without the August furnace; September brings the **vendemmia** (grape harvest) and the salt harvest in the lagoon, which is when the pans turn brilliant pink and the windmills are most photogenic.

July and August are reliably hot — well into the mid-thirties — and the town fills with Italian holidaymakers. Winters are mild and grey, with few tourists, and the surrounding wine country is closed up tight; you will have the streets to yourself but a number of restaurants will be shut.
