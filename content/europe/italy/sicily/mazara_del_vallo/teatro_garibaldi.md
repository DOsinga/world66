Teatro Garibaldi is a small 19th-century theatre in Mazara del Vallo, built in 1849 and restored after a long closure. The appeal is the intimate historic interior: painted decoration, tiers of boxes, and the atmosphere of a civic theatre that feels far larger in character than in size.

It is close to the old centre and easy to combine with the Dancing Satyr Museum, the Casbah, and the waterfront.
