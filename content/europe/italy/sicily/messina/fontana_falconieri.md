Fontana Falconieri is one of Messina's historic fountains, a small but worthwhile architectural detail in a city rebuilt many times after earthquakes and war. It is not a headline sight, but it helps fill in the civic texture around the centre.

Visit it as part of a walking route rather than as a standalone destination. The fountain works best when paired with the cathedral, waterfront, and other public monuments that show how Messina presents its history in streets and squares.
