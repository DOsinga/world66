The Madonna della Lettera is one of Messina's defining harbor landmarks, a gilded statue set on a column at the entrance to the port. Its inscription and position make it a civic symbol as much as a religious monument, visible to ferry passengers and waterfront walkers alike.

It is best appreciated as part of the harbor scene, especially when arriving by sea or walking near the strait. The monument helps tie Messina's identity to shipping, devotion, and the narrow waterway facing Calabria.
