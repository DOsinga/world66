Santuario della Madonna di Montalto rises above Messina with one of the city’s best combinations of church architecture and view. From the terrace you can read the harbour, the Strait of Messina, and the dense city below in a way that is hard from street level.

The sanctuary is worth the climb or short ride even if you only stay briefly. It pairs well with the cathedral and central streets, adding a quieter devotional stop and a strong sense of Messina’s setting between Sicily and Calabria.
