---
title: When to Go
type: section
---

May through June and September through October are the best times to visit. The weather is warm enough for swimming and comfortable enough for walking the promontory. The Aeolian boat services run at full frequency from late May, and the summer crowds haven't yet peaked or have just subsided.

July and August bring heat (often above 35°C) and maximum activity at the port — ferries and hydrofoils run constantly, and the town fills with travellers heading to and from the islands. The castle and promontory are best visited early in the morning before the heat becomes oppressive.

Winter is quiet, with ferry services reduced but still running to the main Aeolian islands. The castle is open year-round. If you are using Milazzo purely as a transit point for the islands, the season barely matters — boats run throughout the year.
