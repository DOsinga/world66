Planeta Sciaranuova Estate is a high-altitude vineyard on Etna's northern slopes, where tastings come with views over black lava soil and mountain vines. It is a good stop if you want to understand why Etna wines have such a distinct, mineral edge; olive oil and occasional cultural events add to the visit.
