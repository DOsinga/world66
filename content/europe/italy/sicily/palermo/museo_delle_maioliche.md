Museo delle Maioliche is a private museum devoted to historic maiolica tiles, with pieces from Palermo, Naples, and other Italian ceramic centres. Guided visits explain how the tiles were made, how patterns changed over time, and why this craft mattered in Sicilian interiors.

It is a focused stop rather than a large museum, best for visitors already exploring La Kalsa or interested in decorative arts.
