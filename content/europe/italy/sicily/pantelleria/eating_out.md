---
title: 'Eating Out'
type: section
---

Pantescan cooking is North African as much as Sicilian. The classic dishes are couscous con pesce, *insalata pantesca* (potato, tomato, capers, red onion, oregano), pasta with caper pesto, and rabbit cooked in white wine. Capers — *cucunci* on the bush, *capperi* the buds — and the small black-skinned salty *zibibbo* grapes are island specialities, as is the rich amber dessert wine made from them.
