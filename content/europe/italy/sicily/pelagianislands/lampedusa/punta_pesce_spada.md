Punta Pesce Spada is a coastal point on Lampedusa, useful for visitors who want to explore beyond the island's best-known beaches. The attraction is simple: open sea views, rock, wind, and the feeling of being near Italy's southern edge.

It is best folded into a wider circuit of coves and viewpoints rather than made the centerpiece of a day. Conditions can be exposed, so go prepared for sun and wind.
