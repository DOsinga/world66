Chiesa di Santa Maria dell'Itria is a small but old church in Ragusa's former Jewish quarter. Founded in the 14th century and later linked to the Knights of Malta, it adds a different layer to a city better known for its Baroque rebuild after the 1693 earthquake.

It is not a long visit, but it rewards anyone exploring Ragusa Ibla on foot and paying attention to the older fragments beneath the Baroque surface.
