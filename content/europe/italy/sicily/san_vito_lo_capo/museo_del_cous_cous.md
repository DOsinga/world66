The Museo del Cous Cous is a small museum dedicated to the couscous tradition that links western Sicily with North Africa. Exhibits cover the tools and techniques used to roll and steam couscous, including traditional cuscusiera pots and objects connected to the town's annual Cous Cous Fest.

It is a modest stop rather than a major museum, but it gives useful context to one of San Vito Lo Capo's strongest local identities. Access may need to be arranged through the tourist office.
