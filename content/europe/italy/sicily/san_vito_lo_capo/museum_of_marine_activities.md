The Museum of Marine Activities is a small stop focused on traditional fishing along this part of the Sicilian coast. Housed in a former tuna-processing site, it displays tools, nets, and archival material, making it most useful when paired with Zingaro Nature Reserve or San Vito Lo Capo's coastal sights.
