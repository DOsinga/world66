The Torre della Tonnara di Cofano stands by the old tuna fishery below Monte Cofano, a 16th-century coastal watchtower built to watch for pirate raids. Its star-shaped plan is unusual for Sicily, and the sea-and-mountain setting does half the work before you even look closely at the stone.

It makes a fine stop while walking or driving through the Monte Cofano Nature Reserve. Come for the tower, but linger for the rough coastline and the view back toward the mountain.
