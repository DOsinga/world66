The Basilica Maria Santissima del Soccorso is Sciacca's mother church, standing in the historic centre rather than hiding at the edge of town. It has Norman origins, but the building visitors see today is largely the result of later rebuilding and baroque reshaping.

Inside, look for the scale rather than a single masterpiece. The church matters because it sits at the centre of Sciacca's civic and religious life, especially during local feasts tied to the Madonna del Soccorso.
