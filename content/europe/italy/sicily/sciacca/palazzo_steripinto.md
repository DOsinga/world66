Palazzo Steripinto is one of Sciacca's most distinctive façades, a late medieval palace whose pointed stonework gives it a defensive, almost prickly appearance. It stands out even in a town with plenty of layered architecture.

You do not need much time here, but do stop. The building is a good example of why Sciacca rewards walking slowly: the strongest details are often embedded in ordinary streets rather than framed as major attractions.
