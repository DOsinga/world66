Chiesa di Santa Lucia al Sepolcro stands outside the old walls of Siracusa, on the traditional site of Saint Lucy's martyrdom. The present church is mostly 15th- and 16th-century, though older parts survive in the portal, apses, and lower belfry.

The church is closely tied to the cult of Saint Lucy and to Caravaggio's *Burial of Saint Lucy*, which was painted for this setting. The tomb of the saint is beside the church; the catacombs below are not normally open to visitors.
