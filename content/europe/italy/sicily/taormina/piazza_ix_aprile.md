Piazza IX Aprile is Taormina's main balcony over the Ionian Sea, with wide views toward the coast and Mount Etna. The square sits along Corso Umberto and is framed by cafes, churches, and the Torre dell'Orologio, making it one of the easiest places in town to pause between the hilltop sights.

Come early or near sunset for the best light and a little more space on the terrace.
