---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

**April–June** and **September–October** are the best months. The light is clear, temperatures are pleasant (20–25°C), and the crowds are manageable. The Taormina Film Fest runs in late June and brings glamour and disruption in equal measure.

**July–August** is peak season: extremely hot, very crowded, and prices are at their highest. The Greek Theatre hosts concerts and performances throughout summer, which draws both visitors and traffic jams.

**November–March** is quiet, cool, and often rainy but atmospheric. Most restaurants and some hotels close or reduce hours. Mt Etna may have snow visible. The town is genuinely peaceful and locals reclaim it for a few months.
