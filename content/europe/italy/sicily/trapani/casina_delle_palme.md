Casina delle Palme is a Liberty-style pavilion overlooking the port of Trapani. Attributed to Francesco La Grassa, it adds a graceful early-20th-century note to the waterfront and is sometimes used for cultural or public events.

It is a small stop rather than a major monument, but it fits naturally into a walk between the old town, the harbour, and the nearby baroque facades.
