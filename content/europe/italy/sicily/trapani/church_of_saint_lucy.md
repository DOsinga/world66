The Church of Saint Lucy is one of the small religious stops woven into Trapani's old centre. It is easy to miss if you are moving quickly between the cathedral, harbour, and seafront, but it adds to the layered churchscape of the city.

Visit it as part of a slow walk rather than a special expedition. Trapani rewards that kind of wandering: narrow streets, modest facades, and sudden reminders of the town's long religious and maritime history.
