The Church of the Holy Souls in Purgatory is a Baroque church in Trapani's old centre. It is best known for housing the Misteri di Trapani, the sculptural groups carried through the city during the long Good Friday procession.

Even outside Holy Week, the church is one of the better stops for understanding Trapani's religious art and processional traditions.
