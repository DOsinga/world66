Mulino Maria Stella is a historic windmill in the Trapani salt pans, now used as a small museum about traditional salt harvesting. The exhibits include tools, archival photographs, and devices used to move water and process salt across the shallow pans.

It works well as a focused stop inside the wider salt-pan landscape, especially if you want context before photographing the windmills, basins, and birdlife of the reserve.
