Mura di Tramontana is the seafront promenade along Trapani's historic northern walls. It gives the old town a clean edge against the water, with views toward the Egadi Islands and a small beach below the walls.

Walk it when the light is soft, especially near sunset. It is an easy way to feel Trapani's position between fortress town and working port without needing a formal sight or ticket.
