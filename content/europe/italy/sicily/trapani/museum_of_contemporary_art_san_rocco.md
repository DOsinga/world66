The Museum of Contemporary Art San Rocco is a small contemporary art museum in the restored 17th-century Palazzo San Rocco. Its exhibitions focus on modern cultural, spiritual, and social themes, adding a current art stop to Trapani's mostly historic centre.

It is close to the cathedral and the old-town walking route, so it is worth checking if the current exhibition fits your interests.
