Palazzo D'Alì is one of Trapani's civic landmarks, an elegant municipal palace that helps define the transition between the old town's narrow streets and its broader public spaces. Its façade is the main reason to pause, especially if you are already walking between the port, Corso Vittorio Emanuele, and the cathedral area.

It is a short architectural stop, not a major museum. Trapani rewards slow wandering, and buildings like this are part of the accumulated urban texture rather than headline sights on their own.
