The Post Office Palace is an early 20th-century Liberty-style landmark by Francesco La Grassa. Its curved urban block and decorative façade make it one of the more distinctive civic buildings near Trapani's old town.

It is a short architectural stop rather than a destination on its own. Look for it while walking between the station area, Villa Margherita, and the old town streets.
