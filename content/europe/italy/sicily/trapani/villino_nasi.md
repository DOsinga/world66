Villino Nasi sits near Trapani's western tip, a small Liberty-style villa facing the sea and the Egadi Islands. It is not a grand palace, but its position gives it a romantic quality: architecture, salt air, and a horizon full of ferries and islands.

The villa is easiest to appreciate as part of a walk toward Torre di Ligny. Look at the building from the outside, then keep going for the wider view back along Trapani's narrow peninsula.
