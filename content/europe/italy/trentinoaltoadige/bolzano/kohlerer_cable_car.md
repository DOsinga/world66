The Kohlerer Bahn is a piece of transport history that happens to also offer exceptional views. It opened in 1908 — making it the world's first passenger cable car — and has been running largely continuously ever since. The lower station is a short walk from the old town; the wooden cabin rises steeply through orchards and forest to the village of Kohlern at 1,000m.

At the top, there are several walking routes through the plateau above the city, a panoramic restaurant, and views that put the Dolomites behind Bolzano in proper perspective. The ride itself, in the vintage wooden cabin, is charming. It runs year-round except for maintenance periods.
