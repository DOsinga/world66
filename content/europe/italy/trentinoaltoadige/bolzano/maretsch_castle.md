Maretsch Castle sits surprisingly close to central Bolzano, surrounded by vineyards rather than remote mountain drama. Its towers, frescoed rooms, and compact grounds make it one of the easiest castles to fold into a city walk.

It is often used for events, so interior access can vary. Even from outside, the setting gives a clear sense of Bolzano's blend of Alpine, Tyrolean, and Italian influences.
