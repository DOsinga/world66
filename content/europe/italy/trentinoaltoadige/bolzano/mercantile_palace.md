The Mercantile Palace is one of Bolzano's clearest reminders of its old trading role between the German and Italian worlds. Built in the early 18th century, it has a formal Baroque facade and interiors that once served the city's merchant magistracy.

The building now houses the Mercantile Museum, where the grand staircase and period rooms are as much the attraction as the displays. It is a compact stop, easy to fold into a walk through the old town.
