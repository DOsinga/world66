Bolzano's Mercantile Palace is an early 18th-century Baroque building designed by Francesco Pedrotti of Verona. Its grand stairway and frescoed halls reflect the city's role as a trading center between Italian and German-speaking Europe.

The building houses the Mercantile Museum, making it a good stop for visitors interested in Bolzano's commercial history as well as architecture.
