---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

Folgaria has two distinct seasons, both worth considering. Winter (December to March) is peak ski season: the plateau typically has reliable snow, the lifts run well, and the village fills with Italian families. Book accommodation well in advance for Christmas, New Year, and the February school holidays.

Summer (June to September) is the quieter but arguably more beautiful season. The plateau meadows are covered in wildflowers from late June, the air is cool and clear, and the hiking and cycling trails are uncrowded. May and October are the shoulder months — the lifts don't run and some restaurants may operate reduced hours, but prices drop and you largely have the landscape to yourself.

The alpine botanical garden at Passo Coe is at its best in late June and early July, when the highland flowers are at peak bloom.
