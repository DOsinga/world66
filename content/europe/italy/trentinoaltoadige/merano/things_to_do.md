---
title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

The [Trauttmansdorff Castle Gardens](/europe/italy/trentinoaltoadige/merano/trauttmansdorff_castle) above the town are the main destination — terraced botanical gardens where Empress Sisi once stayed, with views down the valley. Down in town, the [Kurhaus](/europe/italy/trentinoaltoadige/merano/kurhaus) on the Passirio riverside is the symbol of the old spa culture, and the riverside promenades are ideal for a long walk. The [Gothic church of St Nicholas](/europe/italy/trentinoaltoadige/merano/st_nicholas_church) dominates the old town centre; the [Steinerner Steg](/europe/italy/trentinoaltoadige/merano/steinerner_steg) footbridge nearby is the oldest bridge in the city. [Tyrol Castle](/europe/italy/trentinoaltoadige/merano/tyrol_castle) on the ridge above the town was the ancestral seat of the Counts of Tyrol, from whom the whole region takes its name — a short cable car or vigorous walk away.
