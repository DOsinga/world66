Trento is a prosperous city tucked into the Adige valley in the Italian Alps, with a wealth that comes from farming, wine, and high-tech industry. The town is best known for the Council of Trent (1545–1563), the Catholic Church's marathon response to the Reformation, which met in the [Duomo](/europe/italy/trentinoaltoadige/trento/duomo) and the [Castello del Buonconsiglio](/europe/italy/trentinoaltoadige/trento/castello_del_buonconsiglio) over nearly two decades. The castle is now the largest museum in the Trentino region, with medieval art, archaeology, and frescoes — including the spectacular [Cycle of the Months](/europe/italy/trentinoaltoadige/trento/torre_aquila) in the Torre Aquila.

The historic centre is largely pedestrianised and a pleasure to walk around. The [Piazza Duomo](/europe/italy/trentinoaltoadige/trento/piazza_duomo) anchors the old town, and several streets — most notably [Via Belenzani](/europe/italy/trentinoaltoadige/trento/via_belenzani) — still carry outdoor Renaissance frescoes on their facades, a distinctive local tradition. Beneath the modern Piazza Cesare Battisti, the [SASS underground archaeological space](/europe/italy/trentinoaltoadige/trento/sass_underground) preserves a section of Roman Tridentum. At Easter the surrounding orchards are in bloom with apple blossoms, and the valley scenery is beautiful year-round.

The [MUSE](/europe/italy/trentinoaltoadige/trento/muse), Renzo Piano's stepped science museum, is the city's outstanding modern building — south of the centre in the regenerated Le Albere district, next to the Renaissance [Palazzo delle Albere](/europe/italy/trentinoaltoadige/trento/palazzo_delle_albere). The MART (Museum of Modern and Contemporary Art), with over 12,000 works including pieces by de Chirico and Severini, has its main venue in nearby Rovereto but maintains [a gallery space in Trento](/europe/italy/trentinoaltoadige/trento/galleria_civica). The city sits on the main rail line between Verona and the Brenner Pass, making it an easy stop on the route between Italy and Austria.
