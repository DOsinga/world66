The Aeronautical Museum Gianni Caproni preserves one of Italy's important early aviation collections, including aircraft, engines, models, and material connected with the Caproni company. Its hangar setting near Trento's airport gives the displays a practical, workshop-like feel rather than a purely polished museum atmosphere.

It is most rewarding for travellers interested in engineering, military history, or the beginnings of powered flight. Pair it with Trento's science and history museums if you want a broader day of museums beyond the old centre.
