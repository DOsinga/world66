Trento is unusually well positioned for day trips into both the Dolomites and the rest of Trentino-Alto Adige. **[Bolzano](/europe/italy/trentinoaltoadige/bolzano)** is 40 minutes north by train and is the next major city up the Adige valley — bilingual, German-speaking, and home to the Ötzi Iceman.

Closer in, the lakes of **Caldonazzo** and **Levico** to the east are easy half-day swimming destinations. **Rovereto** (15 minutes south by train) is worth a long visit for the **MART**, one of Italy's best contemporary art museums. The valleys of **[Val di Fassa](/europe/italy/trentinoaltoadige/val_di_fassa)** and **Val di Non** open out into the heart of the Dolomites; **[Folgaria](/europe/italy/trentinoaltoadige/folgaria)** is the nearest ski area.
