---
title: "Getting Around"
type: section
---

Trento's old town is compact, pedestrianised, and best covered on foot — almost everything in the centre is within ten minutes' walk of the Piazza Duomo. The MUSE and the Le Albere district are a 15-minute walk south of the cathedral; the Castello del Buonconsiglio is five minutes north.

For trips up Monte Bondone or out to the suburbs, the local **Trentino Trasporti** buses run frequently and a Trentino Guest Card (available from any hotel) gives free travel and museum entry. The **funivia (cable car) to Sardagna** runs every 15–30 minutes from the riverbank just north of the railway station, and bikes can be hired through the city's e-bike sharing system.
