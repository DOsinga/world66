---
title: "Getting There"
type: section
---

Trento sits on the **Brenner railway line**, the main rail route between Italy and Austria, which makes it an unusually well-connected city for its size. Direct trains run hourly to **Verona** (under an hour), **Bolzano** (40 minutes), and **Innsbruck** (about two and a half hours), and several times a day to **Munich**, **Rome**, and **Venice**. The station is a short walk from the old town.

By road, the **A22 Brenner motorway** runs alongside the railway and provides the fastest car connection in either direction. The nearest international airports are **Verona** (about 90 minutes by road) and **Bergamo** (two hours); both have low-cost flights from across Europe.
