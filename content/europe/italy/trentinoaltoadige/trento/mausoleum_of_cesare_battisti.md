The Mausoleum of Cesare Battisti stands on Doss Trento above the city, a severe circular memorial to one of Trentino's most charged historical figures. Battisti was executed by Austria-Hungary during the First World War and later became a symbol of Italian irredentism, which gives the site a political weight beyond its architecture.

The location is part of the experience. From the hill you get wide views over Trento, the Adige valley, and the mountains that explain why this region has always felt like a borderland. Combine the mausoleum with a walk on Doss Trento if you want a quieter, more reflective break from the historic centre.
