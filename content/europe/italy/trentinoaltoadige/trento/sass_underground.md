SASS (Spazio Archeologico Sotterraneo del Sas) is a striking underground museum directly beneath the Piazza Cesare Battisti, in the centre of Trento. The site exposes a substantial section of the Roman city of Tridentum — paved streets, the foundations of houses, a stretch of the eastern city wall and gate, and the remains of a bath complex.

The visit is short (about 45 minutes) but does an unusually good job of conveying the layered city — Roman, medieval, modern — directly above one's head. The lighting design and the glass walkways make it more theatrical than most archaeological displays. A small museum room covers the Roman history of the region with finds from the excavation.
