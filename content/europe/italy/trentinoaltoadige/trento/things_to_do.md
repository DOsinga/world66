---
title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Trento's compact historic centre is largely pedestrianised and easy to cover in a long day, with the [Castello del Buonconsiglio](/europe/italy/trentinoaltoadige/trento/castello_del_buonconsiglio) and the [Duomo](/europe/italy/trentinoaltoadige/trento/duomo) as the two heavyweight sights and a string of frescoed Renaissance palazzi in between. South of the old town, the [MUSE](/europe/italy/trentinoaltoadige/trento/muse) — Renzo Piano's science museum in the former Michelin tyre factory district — is the best modern building in the city and an excellent half-day in itself. From the centre, a short cable-car ride goes up [Monte Bondone](/europe/italy/trentinoaltoadige/trento/monte_bondone) for the alpine setting that defines Trento as much as any of its buildings.
