Torre Vanga is a medieval tower on the edge of Trento's historic center, now used as an exhibition space. The basement has small streams of water emerging through the walls, a reminder of the tower's position near the old watercourses.

You cannot normally visit the top, so the tower is best seen when an exhibition is running or as a short stop on a walk through central Trento.
