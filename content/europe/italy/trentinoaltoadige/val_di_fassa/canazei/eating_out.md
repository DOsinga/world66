---
title: Eating Out
type: section
---

Ladin cuisine is the local speciality — a mountain version of Tyrolean cooking with pasta and dumplings instead of speck and goulash. Look out for canederli (bread dumplings in broth), tortei de patate (potato fritters), and tutres (fried half-moon pastries stuffed with sauerkraut or spinach). Most rifugi on the mountain serve good local food at fair prices.
