---
title: When to Go
type: section
---

Canazei has two clear seasons. The ski season runs from early December to early April; the lifts on the Marmolada glacier may stay open later into spring. Snow is generally reliable above 2,000 metres, and the wider Dolomiti Superski area means you have plenty of options whatever the conditions.

Summer hiking season is late June to mid-September, with the peak in August when half of Italy is on holiday. Late June and early September are quieter, with most lifts running and the meadows full of flowers (June) or in autumn colours (September). May and November are the dead months — most accommodation closes.
