A small wine bar in the centre with a serious cellar of Alto Adige and Trentino whites and reds, plus a tight tapas-style menu of cured meats, cheeses, and warm bites. A good place to land after a long day on the trail, especially if you want something better than the standard hot wine of a refuge.
