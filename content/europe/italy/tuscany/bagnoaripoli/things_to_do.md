---
title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Bagno a Ripoli rewards visitors who want to experience the Florentine countryside without the city crowds. The gentle hills southeast of Florence are dotted with medieval churches, Renaissance villas, and small villages where life moves at a slower pace. Walking or cycling between Antella and Grassina through terraced olive groves is as good an introduction as any.

The municipality is better visited as a half-day excursion from Florence or as a base for exploring the surrounding countryside than as a standalone destination. The appeal is atmospheric rather than monumental.
