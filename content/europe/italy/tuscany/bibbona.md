Bibbona is a small medieval hilltop village in the Livorno province of Tuscany, perched above the coastal plain with views stretching to the Tyrrhenian Sea. The village itself is compact and largely unspoiled — stone streets, a small castle, and the kind of quiet that has mostly been squeezed out of better-known Tuscan hill towns. It sees few tourists, which is part of the appeal.

Below it, a few kilometres down the hill, lies [Marina di Bibbona](/europe/italy/tuscany/bibbona/marina_di_bibbona): a quieter beach resort than its neighbours Cecina and San Vincenzo, with a long sandy beach backed by fragrant pine forest. The combination of a proper medieval village and an accessible beach makes Bibbona a more interesting base than most of the [Etruscan Coast](/europe/italy/tuscany/bibbona/etruscan_coast). It is also well placed for exploring the prestigious wine estates around [Bolgheri](/europe/italy/tuscany/bibbona/bolgheri), home of Sassicaia and Ornellaia, just a short drive to the south.
