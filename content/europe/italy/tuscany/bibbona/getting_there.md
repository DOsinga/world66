---
title: Getting There
type: section
---

Bibbona is best reached by car. The nearest train station is at Bolgheri-Donoratico on the Tirrenica coastal line, about 8 kilometres south; trains run from Livorno (about 40 minutes) and Grosseto. From the station you need a car or taxi. By car from Florence, take the superstrada toward Livorno and then the SS1 (Via Aurelia) south; from Rome, take the A12 and then the SS1 north. Parking is available in the village and at Marina di Bibbona.
