Stone's fictional biography of Michelangelo follows the artist from his apprenticeship in Florence through the ceiling of the Sistine Chapel and back. It is long, vivid, and not always historically precise, but as an imaginative account of what it might have been like to be Michelangelo in Renaissance Tuscany — working in the marble quarries at Carrara, navigating Medici patronage, fighting with popes — it has no rival. The Florentine chapters are particularly strong.
