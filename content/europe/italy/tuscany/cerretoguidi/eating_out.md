---
title: Eating Out
type: section
---

Cerreto Guidi is a small town with modest but genuine local restaurants. The cooking follows the Florentine-Tuscan tradition: thick ribollita, pappardelle with wild boar ragù, grilled meats, and local Chianti Empolese wine. Most establishments cater to locals rather than tourists, which keeps both the prices and the quality honest.
