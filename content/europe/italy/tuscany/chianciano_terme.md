Chianciano Terme is a spa town in the hills of southern [Tuscany](/europe/italy/tuscany), about 90 km southeast of [Florence](/europe/italy/tuscany/florence) and 50 km from [Siena](/europe/italy/tuscany/siena). The town has been known for its thermal waters since Etruscan and Roman times — the springs are said to be particularly good for the liver, which the Italians take seriously.

The old hilltop centre — the [Città Vecchia](/europe/italy/tuscany/chianciano_terme/citta_vecchia) — is pleasant for a stroll, with views over the Val di Chiana and a small archaeology museum with Etruscan votive finds from the springs. But the real draw is the thermal parks: [Terme Sensoriali](/europe/italy/tuscany/chianciano_terme/terme_sensoriali) and the outdoor pools of [Theia](/europe/italy/tuscany/chianciano_terme/theia) offer a mix of therapeutic and leisure bathing. The town makes a practical base for exploring the surrounding hill towns — [Montepulciano](/europe/italy/tuscany/montepulciano), Pienza, and Montalcino are all within easy reach.
