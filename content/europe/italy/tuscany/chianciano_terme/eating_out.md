---
title: "Eating Out"
type: section
---

Chianciano Terme eats in the Tuscan hill-town tradition: hand-rolled pasta, locally pressed olive oil, the Chianina beef that gives the Val di Chiana its name, and wines from Montepulciano and Montalcino within easy reach. The restaurant scene caters heavily to the Italian cure-tourism market — expect full multicourse lunches, early dinners, and very few menus in English.
