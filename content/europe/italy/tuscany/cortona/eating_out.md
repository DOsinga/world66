---
title: "Eating Out"
type: section
---

Cortona's restaurants run from family trattorias tucked into the medieval alleys to a handful of more ambitious places taking advantage of the Val di Chiana's famous beef — *bistecca alla fiorentina* from the local Chianina cattle is a regional specialty.

Other Tuscan staples appear on every menu: *pici* (hand-rolled fat pasta), wild boar ragu, *ribollita* (bean and bread soup), pecorino cheese from nearby Pienza. The local DOC wine, Cortona Rosso, is well-suited to red meats; pair the local Syrah with a steak.
