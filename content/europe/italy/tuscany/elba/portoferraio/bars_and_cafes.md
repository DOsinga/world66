---
title: "Bars and Cafes"
type: section
---

The social life of Portoferraio runs along the harbour and the small piazza behind it. The wide pedestrian quay — the **Calata Mazzini** — is lined with cafés that have set out tables from morning espresso through late-evening *aperitivo*. The view of the boats and the forts above is the obvious draw, and prices reflect it.

Inland by one block, **Piazza della Repubblica** has cheaper and more local options — the morning *cappuccino* and *brioche* crowd here is Portoferraio's own. A handful of small wine bars on the lanes leading up to Forte Falcone serve glasses of local Elba **Procanico** and **Sangioveto**, often with plates of local cheese and salumi. The sweet **Aleatico** and **Moscato** dessert wines are worth trying at the end of a meal.

For a cocktail with a view, head out to **Spiaggia delle Ghiaie** at sunset — the small beach bars open into the evening through the summer. In peak season a few open-air clubs run on the road towards Magazzini, but Portoferraio is fundamentally an early-to-bed town.
