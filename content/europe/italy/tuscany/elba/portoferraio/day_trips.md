Most of Elba is within easy day-trip range from Portoferraio. **Marciana Marina** and the cable car up Monte Capanne from Marciana village are the obvious option — the highest point on the island and one of the best viewpoints in the Tuscan archipelago. **Marina di Campo** on the south coast is the largest beach town on Elba, with a long sweep of sand and a more touristy atmosphere than Portoferraio.

Further afield, the inland town of **Capoliveri** on the eastern hill is the prettiest village on Elba — a hilltop maze of lanes with several good restaurants. **Rio nell'Elba** in the north-east is the centre of the island's long iron-mining tradition and has a small museum. The smaller wine-growing villages along the western and southern coasts are worth driving through for the views even if you do not stop.

For a longer trip, the **Tuscan archipelago islands** of **Capraia**, **Gorgona**, and **Pianosa** are reachable by occasional summer boats from Portoferraio. The closest, Capraia, is a full day's outing with limited boat schedules.
