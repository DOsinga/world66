Forte Falcone sits 80 metres above the harbour, on the highest ground above Portoferraio's old town — the higher and larger of the two Medici forts that anchor the historic centre. It was built by Cosimo I de' Medici from 1548 onwards as the keep of his new ideal city, and the massive bastioned walls have survived almost unchanged. The walls enclose a substantial inner courtyard, the foundations of barracks and a chapel, and a deep cistern.

The fort is reached by a steep cobbled walk up from the **Piazza della Repubblica** in the old town centre. Inside, the parade ground is open to wander and the upper ramparts give views down to the harbour and across to **Forte Stella** to the east. The fort closed for a long restoration in the 2010s and is now partly reopened — check current hours at the tourist office or with a combined Forte Stella / Palazzina dei Mulini ticket. The walls and the views can be enjoyed at any time from the perimeter path.
