---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

Elba's tourist season is unmistakable: **June through early September** is high season, with August solidly booked weeks in advance and prices peaking. Portoferraio in mid-August is a small town overrun by Italian families on their *ferragosto* holidays — fun if you like the atmosphere, frustrating if you wanted a quiet beach.

The shoulder seasons are the better times to visit. **Mid-April to mid-June** has reliable warm weather, the sea warming up by late May, the wildflowers blooming, and the cliff hiking trails (especially around Enfola and Capo Bianco) at their best. **September into early October** is similarly pleasant — the sea is still warm from summer, the crowds thin out from the first day of school, and the prices drop sharply.

**Winter** (November to March) is quiet. Many hotels and restaurants close; the ferry runs less often; rain comes in occasional bands. The forts are still walkable, the old town has the air of an out-of-season Italian harbour town, and you can walk the Ghiaie beach alone. The **Napoleonic anniversaries** in early May (his arrival) and late February (his departure) bring small festivals and re-enactments worth timing a visit around.
