---
title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Empoli's historic centre is compact and easily covered on foot in a morning. The Piazza Farinata degli Uberti is the natural starting point — the Romanesque church of Sant'Andrea and its attached museum hold Renaissance art far above what a town this size would usually have. Nearby, the Palazzo Ghibellino houses a palaeontology museum and the town archive.
