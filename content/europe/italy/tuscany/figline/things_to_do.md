Figline Valdarno is a pleasant small town whose historic centre takes an hour or two to explore on foot. The main draw for most visitors is the surrounding Chianti countryside, which the town makes a good base for. Florence is close enough for a day trip in either direction — Figline works well as a quiet alternative to staying in the city.
