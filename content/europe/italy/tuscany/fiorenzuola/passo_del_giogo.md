Passo del Giogo is one of the Apennine passes linking the Mugello with Firenzuola and the upper valleys beyond. The road is popular with motorcyclists, cyclists, and drivers who enjoy bends more than speed.

For travelers, it is a good scenic crossing and a useful way to understand the area's geography. The pass also carries Second World War associations, so the views are not just pretty; they sit on historically hard ground.
