The Raticosa Pass is another classic road crossing above Firenzuola, linking Tuscany with Emilia-Romagna across open Apennine country. It is less about a single monument than the pleasure of the road, the air, and the view.

Stop if you are driving a loop through the Alto Mugello. The pass gives a clear sense of how remote this corner of Tuscany can feel, despite being within reach of Florence.
