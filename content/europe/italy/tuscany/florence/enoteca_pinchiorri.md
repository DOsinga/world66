Enoteca Pinchiorri is Florence's most celebrated fine dining restaurant, holding three Michelin stars and a reputation that extends across Italy. Giorgio Pinchiorri and Annie Féolde opened it in 1972 in a 15th-century palazzo on Via Ghibellina, building it over the following decades into one of the great Italian restaurants. The wine cellar, with over 150,000 bottles including exceptional depth in Burgundy, Barolo, and Brunello, is the foundation on which everything rests.

The kitchen — currently under chef Riccardo Monco — produces elaborate tasting menus that combine classic Italian technique with contemporary refinement. A meal here is an occasion rather than an everyday option: prices are very high, and booking well in advance is necessary. The courtyard of the palazzo is used for summer dining. For those who want a taste without the full commitment, the wine list is accessible for a glass at the bar. The restaurant is a short walk from Santa Croce, and the neighbourhood setting on Via Ghibellina is considerably quieter than the tourist areas.
