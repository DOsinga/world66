The Tuesday market at Cascine Park stretches for nearly a kilometre through the trees along the Arno, making it the largest open-air market in Florence. Unlike the tourist-oriented stalls around San Lorenzo Basilica, this is firmly a local institution: Florentines come here for discounted clothing, shoes, bags, kitchen goods, and seasonal produce. Prices are negotiable, especially late morning as traders start to pack up.

The market runs every Tuesday from around 8am to 2pm. Cascine Park itself — a long public park following the Arno west of the city centre — is worth exploring after the market closes. A tram line (T1) connects the park to the city centre, or it's a 25-minute walk from the Duomo. The combination of the market and a walk along the river makes for a good Tuesday morning away from the usual tourist circuit.
