Palazzo Magnani Feroni is a 16th-century Renaissance palace on Borgo San Frediano, now operating as a boutique hotel. The rooftop terrace, open to guests and (with a drink purchase) to passing visitors, is one of the best viewpoints in the city — a panorama over the Oltrarno rooftops to the hills beyond, without the crowds of Piazzale Michelangelo and at a lower elevation that gives a more intimate sense of the city's layout.

The palazzo's piano nobile preserves original frescoed ceilings and period furniture, and the common areas of the hotel are accessible to visitors who arrive for the terrace. The Borgo San Frediano neighbourhood around it — with its workshops, wine bars, and relatively unchanged streetscape — is worth exploring before or after. The hotel's ground floor contains a reliable restaurant serving Tuscan food. For the terrace view, late afternoon is the best time: the light is warm, the heat of the day has passed, and the view toward the Duomo and beyond is at its clearest.
