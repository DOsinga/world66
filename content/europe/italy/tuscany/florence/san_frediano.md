San Frediano, the western stretch of the Oltrarno along the south bank of the Arno, is the artisan and nightlife heart of the area, a once-working-class quarter of leather workers, gilders, and furniture restorers whose workshops still line the side streets. In recent years it has become one of the most fashionable corners of the city, regularly cited among Europe's coolest neighbourhoods.

The quarter centres on the Piazza del Carmine, home to the church of Santa Maria del Carmine and its Brancacci Chapel, frescoed by Masaccio in a style that helped launch the Renaissance. By evening the bars and trattorie around Borgo San Frediano fill with a young, local crowd for aperitivo. Cross the Ponte alla Carraia or Ponte Santa Trinita to reach it on foot.
