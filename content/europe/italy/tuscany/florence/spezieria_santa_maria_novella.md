One of the oldest pharmacies in the world, founded by Dominican friars in 1221 and still selling products from its original premises near Santa Maria Novella. The interior — frescoed rooms, stone vaulting, antique cabinets — is as beautiful as any museum in the city. The products include rose water, herbal liqueurs, and perfumes made to formulas that date back centuries.

This is not a budget stop: prices are high and the shopping experience is designed to feel like entering a palace rather than a shop. Worth visiting even if you don't buy anything. The pomegranate potpourri and the acqua di colonia are the most distinctively Florentine souvenirs you can buy here.
