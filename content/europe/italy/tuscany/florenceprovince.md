Florence Province (Città Metropolitana di Firenze) fans out from [Florence](/europe/italy/tuscany/florence) across a varied landscape: the vine-covered Chianti hills to the south, the wooded Apennine valleys of the Mugello to the north, and the industrial Arno valley towns to the west and east.

The [Chianti region](/europe/italy/tuscany/florenceprovince/chiantiregion) is the star attraction outside the city itself — rolling hills between Florence and Siena planted with Sangiovese vines and olive groves, dotted with stone farmhouses and medieval castles. The SS222 road (the Chiantigiana) winds through the heart of it, passing through Greve in Chianti and Gaiole. This is some of Italy's finest wine country; the Chianti Classico appellation produces reds that reward serious attention.

To the north, the [Mugello valley](/europe/italy/tuscany/florenceprovince/mugelloarea) is Medici country: the family came from here, and they commissioned fortresses, villas, and convents across the valley. The [Sieve valley](/europe/italy/tuscany/florenceprovince/sievevalley) and the [Alto Mugello area](/europe/italy/tuscany/florenceprovince/altomugelloarea) are quieter still, with Apennine hiking and small towns that see few tourists. Driving out of Florence into the province's countryside takes only minutes; much of it rewards unhurried exploration by car.
