Greve in Chianti sits in the heart of the Chianti Classico wine country, roughly equidistant from [Florence](/europe/italy/tuscany/florence) (30 km) and [Siena](/europe/italy/tuscany/siena) (35 km). The town's [triangular piazza](/europe/italy/tuscany/florenceprovince/chiantiregion/greveinchianti/piazza_matteotti), lined with arcaded shops and wine bars, is the natural gathering point and a good place to start tasting at the [Enoteca del Chianti Classico](/europe/italy/tuscany/florenceprovince/chiantiregion/greveinchianti/enoteca_del_chianti_classico) or the century-old [Macelleria Falorni](/europe/italy/tuscany/florenceprovince/chiantiregion/greveinchianti/macelleria_falorni).

The weekly market fills Greve's piazza on Saturday mornings, while [Panzano in Chianti](/europe/italy/tuscany/florenceprovince/chiantiregion/greveinchianti/panzano_in_chianti) — a smaller hilltop village in the same commune — has its market on Sundays. Throughout the year the area comes alive with local festivals: the Chianti Classico Festival in the second week of September is the main event, but there are also antique markets at Easter and in October, a flower show in May, Panzano's Festa della Stagion Bona on 25 April, and a chestnut festival in Lucolena in late October.

The surrounding countryside is classic Tuscan postcard: rolling vine-covered hills, cypress-lined roads, stone farmhouses. The wine estates welcome visitors for tastings — the [Castle of Verrazzano](/europe/italy/tuscany/florenceprovince/chiantiregion/greveinchianti/castle_of_verrazzano) is one of the most historically significant — and the walking and cycling between the villages is superb. Just above the town, the perfectly preserved medieval village of [Montefioralle](/europe/italy/tuscany/florenceprovince/chiantiregion/greveinchianti/montefioralle) rewards a short uphill walk with extraordinary views over the Chianti hills. Panzano is particularly known for its butcher, Dario Cecchini, who has turned the art of the bistecca fiorentina into a theatrical experience.
