title: Things to Do
type: section
---

The town's centrepiece is the [Basilica of Santa Maria dell'Impruneta](/europe/italy/tuscany/florenceprovince/chiantiregion/impruneta/basilica) on the broad main square. Beyond the church, Impruneta is about [terracotta](/europe/italy/tuscany/florenceprovince/chiantiregion/impruneta/terracotta_workshops) — the local clay has fired the tiles and pots of Tuscan villas for six centuries, and a few family kilns still open their doors to visitors. In October, the [Fiera di San Luca](/europe/italy/tuscany/florenceprovince/chiantiregion/impruneta/fiera_di_san_luca) takes over the square for one of the oldest agricultural fairs in Tuscany. Nearby, the [Florence American Cemetery](/europe/italy/tuscany/florenceprovince/chiantiregion/impruneta/florence_american_cemetery) is a sober counterpoint to the surrounding vineyards.
