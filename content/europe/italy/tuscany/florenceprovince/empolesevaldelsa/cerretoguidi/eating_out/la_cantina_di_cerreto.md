A local osteria near the central piazza, La Cantina di Cerreto serves straightforward Tuscan food at fair prices. The menu follows the seasons: in autumn you will find porcini mushroom dishes and chestnut preparations; in spring, a lighter hand with fresh vegetables and pecorino. The house wine comes from growers in the surrounding hills. Book ahead for weekend evenings when locals fill the place.
