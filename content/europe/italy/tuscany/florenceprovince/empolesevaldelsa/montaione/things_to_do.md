title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

The main draw in Montaione is [San Vivaldo](/europe/italy/tuscany/florenceprovince/empolesevaldelsa/montaione/san_vivaldo), a remarkable Renaissance sacred complex of eighteen chapels reproducing Jerusalem's holy sites in miniature — one of the most significant pilgrimage sites in Tuscany, yet almost unknown outside the region. In town, the [Palazzo Pretorio Civic Museum](/europe/italy/tuscany/florenceprovince/empolesevaldelsa/montaione/palazzo_pretorio_civic_museum) holds Roman and Etruscan finds from excavations nearby, and the [Roman cistern](/europe/italy/tuscany/florenceprovince/empolesevaldelsa/montaione/roman_cistern) behind the village is a beautifully preserved ancient water reservoir.

The hamlet of [Castelfalfi](/europe/italy/tuscany/florenceprovince/empolesevaldelsa/montaione/castelfalfi) nearby has a medieval castle and a golf course set among Tuscan hills. The surrounding countryside is excellent walking and cycling territory, particularly on quiet gravel roads through vineyards and olive groves.
