---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

Spring (April to June) and autumn (September to October) are the best seasons. The hills are green, the light is soft, the temperature is pleasant for walking, and the roads are quieter than in summer. San Vivaldo in particular has an atmosphere that is heightened on misty autumn mornings.

July and August are hot and dry, with temperatures frequently above 35°C. The Castelfalfi resort fills with Italian and German holiday-makers. The landscape turns golden and dusty. If you come in summer, start early in the morning before the heat builds.

Winter is quiet and cold, with occasional fog rolling in from the Valdelsa. Many restaurants and smaller businesses take a long winter break. The upside: you may have San Vivaldo entirely to yourself.
