Path 14 — locally called the Strada Verde, or Green Road — runs from the center of Vinci up through the olive groves to the hamlet of Anchiano, where Leonardo was born. The walk takes about an hour at a comfortable pace and climbs steadily through terraced groves that look much as they would have in the fifteenth century. The path is well-marked and begins near the Museo Leonardiano.

The route passes through some of the most characteristic landscape of the Montalbano ridge — ancient olives on stone terraces, views across the Arno valley, the occasional stone farmhouse. It is the natural way to connect the two Leonardo sites rather than driving, and for anyone with any interest in the Renaissance it is a genuinely moving walk. The descent is equally good, either returning by the same path or looping back through the lower slopes.
