---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

Spring (April to June) and autumn (September to November) are the best times to visit. The olive harvest runs through November and December, when the mills are working and it's easy to buy oil direct from producers. In summer the hills are hot and dry, though the elevation gives some relief from Florence's urban heat.

Winter is quiet but pleasant for walking — many days are clear and cool, and the bare vines and muted landscape have their own appeal. Avoid August if you want open restaurants and services; many local businesses close for part of the month.
