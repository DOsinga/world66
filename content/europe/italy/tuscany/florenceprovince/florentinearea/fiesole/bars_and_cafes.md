---
title: "Bars and Cafes"
type: section
---

The bar scene in Fiesole is modest and entirely focused on Piazza Mino. A handful of cafes line the square with outdoor tables facing the valley, and on a clear afternoon the terrace views toward Florence rival anything you'll find in the city below. The pace is unhurried — locals linger over coffee and the tourist rush from Florence rarely overwhelms. Come for an aperitivo as the light softens on the hills.
