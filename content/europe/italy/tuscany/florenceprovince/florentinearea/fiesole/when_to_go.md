---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

Spring and autumn are the best times to visit Fiesole. From March to May and September to November the temperatures are mild, the olive trees are at their most photogenic, and the hillside walks to Monte Ceceri or down to San Domenico are a genuine pleasure. The archaeological site is less crowded, and the light on the valley in the morning is exceptional.

Summer brings heat and crowds — not the worst in Tuscany, but noticeable. Fiesole has always been Florence's escape valve from summer; on sweltering August days half the city seems to be on the bus up the hill looking for a breeze. The heat peaks in July and August, but the evenings stay pleasant and the outdoor theatre season at the Roman theatre runs through summer.

Winter is quiet and often cold, but the town empties of day-trippers and the views on clear days — sometimes with snow on the Apennines — are extraordinary. The cathedral and museums stay open year-round.
