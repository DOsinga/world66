One of the older and more relaxed *bagni* on the Forte dei Marmi seafront, Bagno Piero is a good option for visitors who want a day-use sunlounger without the social pressure of the more fashionable clubs. Like all the *bagni* here it offers parasols and sunloungers, a beach bar, and showers. Prices vary significantly between clubs; those at the southern end of the beach, slightly further from the Fortino, tend to be a little cheaper. Book ahead in July and August — the most popular spots sell out well before 10am.
