---
title: "Bars and Cafes"
type: section
---

The bar scene in Forte dei Marmi is woven into the rhythms of the beach resort day: a quick espresso before cycling to the *bagno*, an aperitivo at sunset back from the sea, a late gelato after dinner on the passeggiata. The main concentration of bars is around Piazza Garibaldi and along Via Carducci. Nightlife is understated — Forte dei Marmi is not a club town, and the late-night entertainment is more likely to be a live jazz set at a hotel terrace than a dance floor.
