The best gelateria in town, and one of the better ones on the entire Versilia coast. The gelato is made fresh each morning using local milk and seasonal fruit; the pistachio and the stracciatella are both excellent, and in summer the fresh fig flavour is exceptional. Lines form after dinner but move quickly. A cone here is the standard conclusion to an evening on the passeggiata. Via Carducci, near the central square.
