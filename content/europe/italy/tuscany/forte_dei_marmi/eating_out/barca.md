A long-established trattoria that manages to feel genuinely local despite the resort setting. The menu is Versilian to the core: fish soup (*cacciucco* in its northern Tuscan variant), spaghetti alle vongole, fried anchovies, and the regional pasta *tordelli al ragù*. Portions are generous, prices are more moderate than the town's grander restaurants, and the atmosphere is relaxed. Particularly good for a long lunch after the Wednesday market. Via Carducci; closed Tuesday.
