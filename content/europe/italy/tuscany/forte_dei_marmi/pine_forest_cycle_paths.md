title: Pine Forest and Cycle Paths
type: poi

A broad belt of maritime pine forest — the *pineta* — runs along the whole Versilia coast, separating the beach clubs from the residential streets of the town. Forte dei Marmi's section is threaded with flat, well-maintained cycle paths that are the default form of transport here in summer: the town is famously flat, compact, and extremely well suited to cycling. Bike hire is available from numerous shops on the main streets; most beach clubs also rent them. The forest itself is a pleasure to walk through — cool shade, the scent of resin, the occasional squirrel — and on summer evenings it fills with people drifting between the beach and the town.
