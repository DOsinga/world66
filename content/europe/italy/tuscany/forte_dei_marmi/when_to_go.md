---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

Forte dei Marmi is overwhelmingly a summer resort, and the season runs from mid-June to mid-September. August is the peak: the villas are full, the beach clubs are at capacity, the Wednesday market is at its most spectacular and most chaotic, and the restaurants require bookings weeks in advance. Prices are at their highest. If you want the full experience of the place — the sociality of the beach, the ritual of the passeggiata, the particular atmosphere of Italian high summer — August delivers it completely.

June and September are significantly more relaxed without sacrificing much in the way of weather. The sea is warm enough to swim comfortably by early June; September often has the clearest water and the most settled weather of the year. Many of the grander restaurants and hotels close by the last week of September, and most beach clubs shut down in October.

Outside the summer season, Forte dei Marmi is very quiet. Most shops, restaurants, and even some hotels close for winter. The town is not unpleasant in spring or autumn — the pine forest and the beach are beautiful year-round — but the resort infrastructure that gives the place its character is largely absent.

The Wednesday market runs year-round, though in winter it is a shadow of the summer version.
