Castello di Querceto sits in the hills above Greve in Chianti, where the appeal is the full Tuscan package: stone walls, vines, cypress-framed views, and a cellar built around Chianti Classico rather than quick tasting-room theatre. It is a working wine estate, but the castle setting gives it more atmosphere than a simple shop counter.

Book ahead if you want a proper tour or structured tasting, especially in the busy months when Chianti day-trippers move between estates. It works well with nearby Montefioralle and the Castle of Verrazzano as a slower wine-country day rather than a checklist of labels.
