Livorno is Tuscany's port city, built by the Medici in the late 16th century as a grand commercial harbour and designed from scratch by the architect Bernardo Buontalenti. It has none of the medieval charm of [Florence](/europe/italy/tuscany/florence) or [Siena](/europe/italy/tuscany/siena), but it has character of a different kind — a cosmopolitan, slightly rough-edged trading city with canals, fortresses, and a long history of religious tolerance.

In 1590, Ferdinando I de' Medici declared Livorno a *porto franco* (free port) and enacted the *Leggi Livornine*, laws guaranteeing freedom of religion and trade. The result was a city that attracted merchants, refugees, and adventurers from across the Mediterranean — Jews, Greeks, Armenians, English, Dutch. The old [Venezia Nuova](/europe/italy/tuscany/livorno/venezia_nuova) quarter, laced with navigable canals, still reflects that cosmopolitan past.

The [Old Fortress](/europe/italy/tuscany/livorno/old_fortress) and a New Fortress guard the harbour, and the Medici Port is worth a stroll. The [Monument of the Four Moors](/europe/italy/tuscany/livorno/monument_four_moors) at Piazza Micheli is one of the most compelling — and debated — pieces of public sculpture in Tuscany. Above the city, the [Santuario di Montenero](/europe/italy/tuscany/livorno/santuario_di_montenero) offers sweeping views over the Tyrrhenian Sea and a stunning gallery of ex-voto paintings spanning five centuries. Livorno is also known for its fish cuisine, particularly *cacciucco*, a rich, spicy fish stew that is the city's signature dish — try it at [Trattoria da Galileo](/europe/italy/tuscany/livorno/cacciucco_da_galileo).

For art lovers, the [Museo Civico Giovanni Fattori](/europe/italy/tuscany/livorno/museo_fattori) presents the Macchiaioli painters, Italy's answer to Impressionism, led by Livorno-born Giovanni Fattori. The [New Synagogue](/europe/italy/tuscany/livorno/new_synagogue) stands as testimony to the city's exceptional history of tolerance. Livorno also makes a good base for a ferry to the island of [Elba](/europe/italy/tuscany/elba), Napoleon's first exile, just an hour offshore.
