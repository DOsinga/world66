---
title: Things to Do in Livorno
type: section
---

Livorno is a working port city with a distinct character rooted in its Medici past, its canal district, and its extraordinary history of religious tolerance. Most visitors come through on the way to Elba or Pisa, but the city rewards a day's exploration — the fortress complex, the Venezia Nuova canals, and the Montenero sanctuary all make strong cases for staying longer.
