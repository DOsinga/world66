Puccini's villa at Torre del Lago is the most evocative Puccini excursion from Lucca, set beside the lake where the composer lived and worked. The house museum keeps the visit intimate, focused on rooms, memorabilia, and the landscape that shaped several operas.

It is a short trip toward the coast rather than a central Lucca sight. Opera lovers should also check the summer festival calendar, when Torre del Lago turns Puccini's lakeside world into a performance setting.
