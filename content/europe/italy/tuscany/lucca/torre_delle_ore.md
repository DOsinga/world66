Torre delle Ore is Lucca's old clock tower, standing just off Via Fillungo in the dense medieval centre. The original clock still works, and the climb gives a close view over the rooftops, the line of the shopping street below, and the tree-topped Torre Guinigi nearby.

There is no lift, so come ready for stairs. It is a smaller stop than Torre Guinigi, but a good one if you like towers, city views, and the mechanics of old civic timekeeping.
