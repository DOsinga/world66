---
title: "When to go"
type: section
---

Massa has a mild Mediterranean climate moderated by the sea and the Apuan Alps behind. Summers are warm and sunny, making July and August the peak season for Marina di Massa, when the beach fills with Italian holidaymakers and prices rise. Spring (April–June) is the best time to visit the town itself and the Apuan Alps — the mountains are accessible but not yet bleached by summer heat, and the marble quarries make for spectacular hiking when the light is clear. Autumn brings a pleasant temperature and fewer crowds. Winter is mild by Italian standards but the mountains above can see snow, which adds drama to the landscape.
