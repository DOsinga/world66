Madonna del Soccorso sits near the edge of Montalcino's old centre, where the town begins to open toward the countryside. The sanctuary is a quieter religious stop than the main cathedral and is easy to fold into a loop through the upper streets.

Its position is part of the appeal. Step outside and the stone town gives way quickly to vineyard views, a reminder that Montalcino's walls and wine country are never far apart.
