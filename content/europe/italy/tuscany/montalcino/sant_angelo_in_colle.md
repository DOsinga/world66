Sant'Angelo in Colle is a small village south of Montalcino, surrounded by Brunello country. It is the kind of place that rewards a detour more than a detailed sightseeing plan.

Come for a brief walk, a view, or a meal if timing works. The village helps show the quieter side of Montalcino's wine territory, away from the fortress crowds and tasting-room traffic.
