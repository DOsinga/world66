The Tempio del Brunello is the town's modern interpretive stop for its most famous wine, set within the Sant'Agostino complex. It is designed for visitors who want context before they start tasting: landscape, grape, history, and the pride Montalcino has built around Brunello.

Wine traditionalists may prefer cellars and vineyards, but the museum helps first-time visitors understand why Brunello is treated with such ceremony. Pair it with the civic museum next door if you want a fuller picture of the town.
