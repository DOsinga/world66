Giardino dei Tarocchi is Niki de Saint Phalle's wild sculpture garden in the Maremma, filled with huge mirrored and mosaic figures based on tarot cards. It is strange, colourful, and completely unlike the quiet hill towns around it, which is exactly why it is worth the detour.
