A small fish restaurant on a side street near the cathedral, with a short tasting menu built around whatever came out of the lagoon and the bay that morning. Reservations are essential in summer. Expect raw fish plates, hand-cut pasta with crustaceans, and a slow, attentive style of service that justifies the prices.
