Rocca Aldobrandesca is the fortress above Talamone, standing on the promontory that looks over the village, the harbour, and the coast toward the Maremma. Its walls reflect the long strategic value of this small port on the Tuscan shore.

The interior is not the main draw. Come for the walk up through Talamone, the stonework, and the broad views over the sea and lagoon landscape around Orbetello.
