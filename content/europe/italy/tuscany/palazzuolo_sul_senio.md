Palazzuolo sul Senio is a small village in the Senio valley on the Tuscan-Romagnan border, awarded the title of "Italian Ideal Village" by Airone magazine and the EU. It holds ISO 14001 environmental certification, which says something about the balance between the village and its surroundings.

The setting is lovely — wooded hills, clear streams, and [trails](/europe/italy/tuscany/palazzuolo_sul_senio/senio_valley_trails) for hiking, mountain biking, and horseback riding. The [Palazzo dei Capitani](/europe/italy/tuscany/palazzuolo_sul_senio/palazzo_dei_capitani) in the village centre houses two small museums: one dedicated to traditional mountain life, the other to the archaeology of the upper Mugello valley. The village runs a year-round programme of fairs and festivals, including the Ottobre Palazzuolese (a chestnut and forest products festival on October Sundays) and a medieval re-enactment in July.
