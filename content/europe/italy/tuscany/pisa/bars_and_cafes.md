---
title: "Bars and Cafes"
type: section
---

Pisa's drinking life is the student kind: spritzes at outdoor tables on Piazza delle Vettovaglie, beer at the brick-walled pubs along the river, and a few old-fashioned cafés that have been running for two centuries. The Lungarno on a warm evening, with a glass of wine and the lit-up palazzi reflected in the water, is one of the simple pleasures of the city.
