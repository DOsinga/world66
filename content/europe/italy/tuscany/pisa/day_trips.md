Pisa is one of the best bases in Tuscany for day trips because the train station has frequent direct services to half the region. **Lucca**, half an hour away, is the obvious first choice — a perfectly preserved walled city you can circle on a rented bike along the top of the Renaissance ramparts. **Livorno** is twenty minutes south for the seafood and the canals of the Venezia Nuova quarter.

Further out, **Volterra** (an hour and a half by bus) gives you Etruscan walls and alabaster workshops on a hilltop, while **Viareggio** and the Versilia beaches are a 20-minute train ride north for an afternoon by the sea. Florence itself is an hour by train and easy to do as a day trip in reverse if Pisa is your base — most travellers do it the other way around.
