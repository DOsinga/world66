Palazzo dell'Orologio frames one side of Piazza dei Cavalieri, with an arched passage below and a clock set into the facade above. The present 14th-century building replaced the Torre della Fame, the "tower of hunger" associated with Count Ugolino della Gherardesca.

Dante placed Ugolino's grim death in the *Divine Comedy*, which gives this elegant square a darker edge. It is best seen as part of a walk around Piazza dei Cavalieri rather than as a separate destination.
