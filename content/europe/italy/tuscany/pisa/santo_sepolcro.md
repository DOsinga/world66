Santo Sepolcro is a small Romanesque church in Pisa with an unusual octagonal plan, linked to the medieval pilgrim and crusader routes that shaped the city. It is a short architectural stop away from the Campo dei Miracoli, useful for seeing a quieter side of Pisa's medieval fabric.
