---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

April through June and September to mid-October are the easiest months. The weather is warm but not blistering, the cruise-ship day-trippers from Livorno are still tolerable, and you can climb the tower without queuing in the sun.

July and August are hot, with daytime highs of 32–35°C and crowds that make the Campo dei Miracoli unpleasant from mid-morning to evening. Book the tower for first or last slot of the day. Many restaurants close for two weeks in mid-August.

Winter is quiet and damp, with daytime highs of 8–12°C and occasional Tuscan fog over the river. The Campo dei Miracoli is at its most photogenic in February with empty lawns and low light. The big local festival is the **Luminara di San Ranieri** on 16 June, when the riverbanks are lined with 70,000 candles in glass jars — book a room months ahead.
