---
title: "Getting There"
type: section
---

Pistoia is well connected by train. From Florence, the journey takes around 45 minutes and trains run frequently throughout the day. From Lucca, the train takes about 40 minutes. From Pisa, allow roughly 50-60 minutes with a change at Lucca or Florence. The railway station is on the southern edge of the historic centre, a short walk from the Piazza del Duomo.

By car, Pistoia sits just off the A11 autostrada connecting Florence to the coast. There are car parks outside the historic centre, from which the old town is easily walkable. Driving into the centro storico itself is restricted to residents in most areas.

Pistoia makes a good day trip from Florence and can be combined with Lucca or Prato in a single day for those moving through the region.
