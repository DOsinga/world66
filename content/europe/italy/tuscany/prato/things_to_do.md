---
title: Things to Do
type: section
---

Prato packs a remarkable number of serious sights into a compact medieval centre. The cathedral alone warrants a visit for its Filippo Lippi frescoes. The imperial castle is the most important Hohenstaufen monument in Tuscany. And the Museo di Palazzo Pretorio holds one of the best collections of medieval and Renaissance art in the region — largely overlooked because everyone drives straight through to Florence.
