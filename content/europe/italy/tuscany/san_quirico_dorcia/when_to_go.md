---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

The Val d'Orcia changes dramatically with the seasons, and each has something to recommend it. Spring (April to June) is the classic time: the fields are bright green and covered in wildflowers, the cypresses are freshly dark against the pale sky, and the light has that particular Tuscan clarity that makes the landscape look like a painting. May is the most crowded month — half of Europe seems to converge on this stretch of Tuscany.

Summer is hot and, at the peak, crowded. The landscape turns gold after the wheat harvest in July, which is beautiful but harsh walking weather. September and October bring the harvest season — the smell of grapes and olive pressing fills the villages, and the crowds thin out. Winter is quiet and sometimes foggy, which softens the landscape beautifully, but many restaurants and shops reduce their hours.
