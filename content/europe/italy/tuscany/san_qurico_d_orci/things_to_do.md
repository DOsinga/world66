---
title: Things to Do
type: section
---

San Quirico is a small town and most of what it offers can be covered in half a day. The Collegiata is the main sight, the Horti Leonini the main garden. But San Quirico is also a base — for driving the Val d'Orcia's famous cypress roads, visiting Bagno Vignoni's thermal pool, and exploring the Via Francigena on foot. The countryside around it is as good as anywhere in Tuscany.
