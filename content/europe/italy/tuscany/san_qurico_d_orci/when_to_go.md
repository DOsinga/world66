---
title: When to Go
type: section
---

Spring — April through early June — is the best time to visit. The Val d'Orcia is green from winter rain, the wildflowers are in bloom along the roadsides, and the poppies appear in the wheat fields in May. The light is soft and consistent, ideal for the landscape drives. This is also the least crowded period before summer tourism peaks.

Summer brings heat and dry, golden-brown hills. The scenery is different but still beautiful. July and August see more visitors, particularly at the famous cypress-road viewpoints. Midday heat in August makes outdoor activity uncomfortable; mornings and evenings are fine.

Autumn (October–November) brings cooler temperatures, turning colours, and the harvest — the Val d'Orcia is wine country, and the vendemmia is underway in early October. Winter is quiet and atmospheric; the hills are often misty and the village essentially empty. The Collegiata and Horti Leonini are worth visiting year-round.

The **Radici di San Quirico** flower festival in spring transforms the streets with elaborate floral displays; dates vary by year.
