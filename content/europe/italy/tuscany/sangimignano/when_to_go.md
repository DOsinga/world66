---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

April, May, and September are the sweet spots: the Tuscan light is good, the crowds haven't peaked, and the weather is reliably warm without the August heat. Spring brings wildflowers on the hillsides and the olive groves are green; autumn means harvest season, truffle markets, and the particular golden quality of the afternoon light.

Summer (June through August) is busy, and July and August in particular bring coachloads of visitors through the main gate every morning. The town is small and the streets narrow; on a hot Saturday in August it can feel genuinely overrun. If you're visiting in summer, arrive early and leave by mid-afternoon before the worst of it.

Winter is quiet to the point of being empty. Many restaurants and shops close or reduce their hours from November through March. What's left is almost entirely for locals, which has its own appeal — you'll have the towers and the views to yourself, and the restaurants that stay open are generally the better ones.
