A wood-and-marble grocer's on Via di Città, in business since 1879. The selection runs to local cheeses (mostly pecorino from the Crete Senesi), oils, preserves, panforte and ricciarelli from the city's best producers, and a small but serious wine cellar.

A good place to buy something to carry home, and one of the few historic shops on the street that still feels mostly used by Sienese rather than tourists.
