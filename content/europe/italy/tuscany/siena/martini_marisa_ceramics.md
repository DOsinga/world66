A small workshop and shop in the old town producing hand-painted ceramics with traditional Sienese motifs — the black-and-white balzana of the city's coat of arms, the 17 contrada emblems, and the wolf-and-twins. The work is more careful and less mass-produced than what is on offer in the main shop windows around the Campo.

Prices are moderate to high depending on size. A small tile with a contrada emblem makes a sensible souvenir.
