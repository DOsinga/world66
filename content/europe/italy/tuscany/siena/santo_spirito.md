A small Renaissance church on the eastern edge of the old town, in the Pispini quarter. The Chapel of Saint Catherine of Alexandria off the right aisle holds frescoes by Sodoma — a compact display of one of Siena's finest 16th-century painters in a usually empty church.

A quick stop on the way down through the Pispini gate.
