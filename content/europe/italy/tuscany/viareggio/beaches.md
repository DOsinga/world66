---
title: Beaches
type: section
---

Viareggio's beach is a broad, flat strip of fine sand running the full length of the town — about 3 km from the port canal in the south to the pine forest in the north. Almost all of it is divided into private *stabilimenti balneari* (beach establishments) that rent sun loungers and umbrellas by the day (expect to pay €15–25 per person in high season, including access to showers and changing rooms). A small free public beach (*spiaggia libera*) exists at the northern end near the Pineta di Levante, and another at the southern end near the port. The sea is shallow and calm — suitable for children — and the backdrop of the Apuan Alps visible on clear days makes the setting exceptional.
