One of the more consistent seafood restaurants in the Darsena district, Il Puntodivino is well regarded by locals for fresh catches prepared simply — grilled fish, spaghetti alle vongole, and excellent *cacciucco*, the rich Livornese-style fish stew that is the signature dish of this stretch of coast. The wine list is reasonably priced and focuses on Tuscan labels. Book ahead in summer.
