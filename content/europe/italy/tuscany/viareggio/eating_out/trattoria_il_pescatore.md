A no-frills trattoria near the port canal where the daily menu depends entirely on the morning's catch. The cacciucco here is made with the traditional five varieties of fish and served with toasted bread rubbed with garlic — a more honest version than you'll find on the tourist promenade. Lunchtime is busy with dock workers and boat builders; evenings are quieter. Cash only, no reservations.
