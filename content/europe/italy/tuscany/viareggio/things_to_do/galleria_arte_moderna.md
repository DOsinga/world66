Viareggio's museum of modern and contemporary art occupies a Liberty-era building on Via Garibaldi, a short walk from the seafront. The permanent collection focuses on Tuscan artists from the late nineteenth century onward, with works connected to the *macchiaioli* movement and later figurative painting. Temporary exhibitions bring in contemporary work from across Italy. Admission is modest; check current hours before visiting as they vary seasonally.
