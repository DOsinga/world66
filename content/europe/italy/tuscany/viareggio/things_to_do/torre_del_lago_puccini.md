Torre del Lago, a village 5 km south of Viareggio on the edge of Lake Massaciuccoli, is where Giacomo Puccini lived and worked for most of his creative life — he called it *la mia piccola delizia* ("my little delight"). Villa Puccini, his lakeside home, is now a museum containing the composer's original furnishings, personal effects, and the room where he composed *Tosca*, *Madama Butterfly*, and much of *La Bohème*. Puccini and his wife are buried in the villa chapel. The village hosts the Puccini Festival every July and August, with open-air opera performances in the lakeside theatre. Easily reached by bike from Viareggio along the coastal cycle path.
