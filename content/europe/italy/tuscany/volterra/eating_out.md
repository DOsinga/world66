---
title: "Eating Out"
type: section
---

Volterra eats in the classic inland Tuscan style — wild boar, hare, white beans in pan-flavoured oil, pici with sausage and pecorino, and the famous local sheep's milk cheeses. The town's restaurants run from straightforward family trattorias to a couple of more ambitious modern Tuscan kitchens. Prices are noticeably lower than in nearby Siena or San Gimignano.
