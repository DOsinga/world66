Volterra's municipal picture gallery is housed in the Palazzo Minucci-Solaini, a small 15th-century palace attributed to Antonio da Sangallo the Elder. The collection is modest but contains two undisputed masterpieces: Rosso Fiorentino's *Deposition* of 1521, an extraordinary mannerist work in vibrating reds and pinks, and Luca Signorelli's *Annunciation*. There are also fine 14th-century Sienese altarpieces and a Ghirlandaio.

The Pinacoteca ticket is part of the combined Volterra museum pass. Allow an hour.
