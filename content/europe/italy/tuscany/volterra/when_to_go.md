---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

**May, June, and September** are the best months — warm but not punishing, with long evenings and not too many day-trippers. **July and August** can be hot (often 32–35°C) and the centre fills with tour buses from late morning to mid-afternoon; arrive before 10am or stay overnight to see the town empty out.

**Late September** is the highlight of the year for many: the *Volterra AD 1398* historical festival takes over the town on the last weekend of August with costumed parades, jousts, and medieval markets. **Truffle season** runs October to December — Volterra is in the heart of one of Italy's main white-truffle territories, and several restaurants run set truffle menus.

Winter is quiet and atmospheric. The town's stone buildings hold the cold and the wind whips off the surrounding ridges; bring layers.
