title: "Books"
type: section
---

**The Little Flowers of St. Francis** (Fioretti di San Francesco). The classic 14th-century collection of anecdotes about Francis and his early companions — short, vivid, often very funny tales like the sermon to the birds and the taming of the wolf of Gubbio. The single best book to read before visiting.

**The Life of St. Francis** by St. Bonaventure. The official medieval biography, written by a successor as Minister General of the Franciscans. Drier than the Fioretti but the definitive theological framing of Francis's life.

**Francis: A Life in Songs** by Ann Wroe. A modern poetic biography that uses the medieval sources to create a portrait of Francis that is both rigorous and surprising. Excellent for understanding the man behind the legend.

**Reluctant Saint: The Life of Francis of Assisi** by Donald Spoto. A clear, well-researched popular biography that demystifies Francis without diminishing him. Useful for non-religious readers approaching the saint for the first time.

**A Traveller in Italy** by H. V. Morton. A 1964 classic of English travel writing about Italy; the Assisi chapter remains one of the most evocative descriptions of the town in any language.
