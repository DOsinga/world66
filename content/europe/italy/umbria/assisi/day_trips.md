Assisi sits at the centre of one of the densest concentrations of medieval hill towns in Italy. Any of the following make excellent day trips:

**[Perugia](/europe/italy/umbria/perugia)** — the Umbrian capital, 25 km west, with the splendid Galleria Nazionale dell'Umbria and the warren of medieval streets that climb up to the Rocca Paolina. Trains run every 30–60 minutes.

**[Spoleto](/europe/italy/umbria/spoleto)** — 50 km south, an even older Roman city with the dramatic Ponte delle Torri spanning a wooded gorge. The Festival dei Due Mondi in late June and early July is one of Italy's most important music festivals.

**[Gubbio](/europe/italy/umbria/gubbio)** — 40 km northeast, a pink-grey stone town built up the side of Monte Ingino, with one of Italy's strangest annual festivals (the Corsa dei Ceri) in May.

**[Trevi](/europe/italy/umbria/trevi)** — 35 km south, a smaller and quieter hill town surrounded by the silver olive groves of the Spoleto valley.
