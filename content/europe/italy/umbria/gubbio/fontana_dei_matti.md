  Tradition has it that anyone who walks three times around the fountain while being doused with its water by a true Eugubino — a native of Gubbio — earns the right to be called a *matto*, a madman. The "Fountain of the Madmen" sits in the small Largo del Bargello below the Palazzo del Bargello, halfway up the old town. It is a simple octagonal basin from the 16th century, completely unremarkable in itself — but it is the focus of one of Gubbio's most cherished daft traditions. A patente di matto certificate is even sold by the tourist office for those who go through with the ritual.
