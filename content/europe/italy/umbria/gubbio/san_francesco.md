A large Gothic church begun in 1259 just below the historic centre, on land the Spadalonga family gave the early Franciscan order. The interior is austere — typical of the early Franciscans — but holds important 15th-century fresco cycles by Ottaviano Nelli, Gubbio's most accomplished medieval painter, in the apse. The cloister is also worth a look. The church faces a large open piazza that serves as the modern entry to the old town.
