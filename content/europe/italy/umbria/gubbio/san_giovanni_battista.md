A 13th-century Romanesque and Gothic church just downhill from Piazza Grande, in its own small piazza below the wall of the Palazzo dei Consoli. The bell tower is original; the rest of the building was reworked in the 16th century. The interior is a long single nave with a wooden ceiling and a few decent late-medieval frescoes. A useful five-minute stop between the main piazza and San Francesco.
