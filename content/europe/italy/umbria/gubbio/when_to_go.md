---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

**May** is Gubbio's best month, both for weather and for spectacle: the Corsa dei Ceri runs on 15 May and the surrounding days fill the town with bands, races, and dinners. Book accommodation months ahead. **September and October** are quieter, the colours on Monte Ingino turn copper, and white truffle season starts at the end of October.

**December** brings the world's largest Christmas tree lit up the mountain, which is genuinely worth a trip. **July and August** are the slowest months — Italians head for the coast and Gubbio can feel sleepy, though the heat is more bearable than in nearby cities thanks to the elevation. **Winter** outside Christmas is cold, occasionally snowy, and very atmospheric in the empty stone streets.
