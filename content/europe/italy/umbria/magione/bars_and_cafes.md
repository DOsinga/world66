---
title: Bars and Cafes in Magione
type: section
---

Magione has a modest bar scene centred on the main piazza and the streets immediately around it. Like most Umbrian towns of this size, the bar is a social institution — people come for an espresso standing at the counter in the morning, return for an aperitivo in the early evening, and stop in again after dinner. The atmosphere is local and unhurried. There is nothing designed for tourists here, which is part of the appeal.
