Sitting in the small lakeside hamlet of San Feliciano, this place has direct access to the fishing cooperative next door, which means the perch and tench on the menu were in the lake very recently. The setting by the water is pleasant, especially at lunch. The menu is short and focused: a couple of pasta dishes, grilled or fried lake fish, and simple desserts. It can get busy on summer weekends with day-trippers from Perugia, so arrive early or book ahead.
