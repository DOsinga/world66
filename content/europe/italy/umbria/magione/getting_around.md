---
title: Getting Around
type: section
---

Magione itself is compact and easy to cover on foot — the castle, the churches, and the main piazza are within a few minutes' walk of each other.

For the wider area, a car is the most practical option. The road along the eastern shore of Lake Trasimeno to San Feliciano is short (about 7 km) and scenic. Montecolognola is a 10-minute drive south-east. Parking in Magione is free and easy to find outside the small historic centre.

**By bicycle** is one of the better ways to explore the lake shore. A cycle path connects several points along the eastern and southern shores. Bikes can be hired locally — ask at the tourist office or in San Feliciano. The full circuit of the lake is about 60 km and can be done in a day; for a shorter ride, the section between San Feliciano and Castiglione del Lago on the southern shore is the most complete and scenic.

**Boats** run from San Feliciano to Isola Maggiore (15 minutes) several times a day in the warmer months. The service is reduced in winter. Check current timetables locally, as they change seasonally.

**Local buses** connect Magione to Perugia and to other towns on the lake, but the service is infrequent and primarily useful for commuters rather than visitors.
