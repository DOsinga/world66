---
title: When to Go
type: section
---

**Spring (April–June)** is the best time to visit. The weather is mild, the Umbrian countryside is green, and wildflowers cover the hillsides around the lake. Crowds are thin outside of Italian public holidays. The cycle path around Lake Trasimeno is at its most enjoyable, and boat services to the islands are running.

**Summer (July–August)** is warm to hot, with temperatures regularly reaching 30°C. The lake becomes a weekend escape for people from Perugia and the surrounding area, so San Feliciano and the ferry to Isola Maggiore get noticeably busier. The castle and main sights remain accessible. Early mornings and evenings are the most comfortable time to be outdoors.

**Autumn (September–October)** is another good window. The heat has eased, the lake is calm, and the olive harvest begins in October. Truffle season starts in earnest, which means the local restaurants start cooking with them. The light on the lake in October is particularly good.

**Winter (November–March)** is quiet. Some restaurants and smaller businesses close or reduce hours. The weather is mild by northern European standards — cold but rarely freezing — and the landscape has a stripped-back quality that appeals to some travellers. The castle is typically open on weekends even in winter.
