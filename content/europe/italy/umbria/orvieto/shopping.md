---
title: Shopping
type: section
---

Orvieto has a good concentration of ceramics shops along Corso Cavour — the local style uses warm earth colours and traditional patterns rooted in Etruscan and Renaissance design. Look for work by local potters rather than imported pieces. The town also has several good wine shops specialising in Orvieto Classico DOC and the broader range of Umbrian wines; Cantina Foresi on Piazza del Duomo is reliable.
