---
title: Things to Do
type: section
---

Orvieto is one of Italy's most rewarding small cities: the cathedral alone justifies the trip, but the underground Etruscan tunnels, the Pozzo di San Patrizio, and the tangle of medieval streets all demand time. The town sits on a dramatic volcanic rock (tufa) plateau, visible from the A1 motorway and from the valley below — the setting is half the experience.
