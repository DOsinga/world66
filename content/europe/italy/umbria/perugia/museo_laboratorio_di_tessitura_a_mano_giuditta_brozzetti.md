The Museo-Laboratorio di Tessitura a Mano Giuditta Brozzetti is a hand-weaving workshop and museum set inside a former 13th-century Franciscan monastery. Traditional looms are still used here, which makes the place feel more alive than a static craft display.

Come for the link between Perugia's textile traditions, old machinery, and the unusual setting. It is a small stop, but a distinctive one.
