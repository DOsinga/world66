The Nobile Collegio della Mercanzia is one of the small surprises inside Perugia's Palazzo dei Priori. The merchants' guild settled here in the late 14th century, and the hall still feels like a civic room rather than a museum display: dark timber benches, carved panels, and a sense of compact authority tucked just off the city's grandest public space.

Go after seeing the more famous Nobile Collegio del Cambio next door. The Mercanzia is plainer, older in mood, and less theatrical, but that is part of its charm. Its woodwork is the reason to linger, especially the inlaid panels and the way the room preserves the world of medieval guild power without much need for explanation.
