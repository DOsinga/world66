Trevi is one of Umbria's most striking hilltop villages — a tight cluster of medieval streets and stone buildings stacked on a conical hill above the Valle Umbra, surrounded by olive groves. On the train between [Perugia](/europe/italy/umbria/perugia) and [Spoleto](/europe/italy/umbria/spoleto), you can see it from the window: a perfect silhouette that most guidebooks dismiss as inaccessible and pass over. They are partly right about the access, but wrong to skip it.

Getting up the hill without a car requires some effort. Buses run infrequently and are timed for local schoolchildren rather than visitors; taxis come from Foligno and are not cheap. The walk down with luggage is steep enough to give fit travellers pause. But those who make it find a village that has not yet been overrun — peaceful streets, real residents going about their days, and the kind of quiet that the more famous Umbrian towns have largely lost.

The medieval centre rewards wandering. The [San Francesco Church and Complesso Museale](/europe/italy/umbria/trevi/san_francisco_church) is worth catching if a concert is playing — the acoustics in the nave are extraordinary, the result of ceramic resonators built into the walls. The [Museo della Civiltà dell'Olivo](/europe/italy/umbria/trevi/museo_della_civilta_delolivo) reflects Trevi's centuries-old identity as an olive oil town; the surrounding groves are among the oldest cultivated landscapes in Umbria. At the summit, the [Cattedrale di Sant'Emiliano](/europe/italy/umbria/trevi/san_emiliano_cathedral) commands views down both sides of the hill.

Below the town, two excursions are worth the descent. The [Tempietto del Clitunno](/europe/italy/umbria/trevi/temple_of_clitumnus) — a bafflingly well-preserved early Christian chapel that looks like a Roman temple — stands beside a spring that Virgil and Byron both wrote about. And the Romanesque abbey of [San Pietro in Bovara](/europe/italy/umbria/trevi/san_pietro_in_bovara), in the hamlet at the foot of the hill, is one of the finest and least-visited Romanesque buildings in Umbria.
