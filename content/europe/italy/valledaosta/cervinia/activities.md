---
title: "Skiing"
type: section
---

The Cervinia-Valtournenche-Zermatt ski area has over 360 kilometres of marked pistes and one of the longest ski seasons in the Alps, running reliably from late November to early May. Cervinia's side is heavily weighted toward beginner and intermediate runs — long, wide, well-groomed slopes that go on for miles, perfect for cruising. Top elevation is 3,480 m on the Plateau Rosa, which keeps snow quality high even when lower resorts struggle.

Experts will find the Italian side limited and should consider crossing to Zermatt for steeper, more interesting terrain. The Ventina run — 11 kilometres top to bottom — is the showpiece descent, dropping nearly 1,500 metres of vertical and entirely possible for confident intermediates. Lift queues can be long in peak weeks; midweek skiing is dramatically quieter.
