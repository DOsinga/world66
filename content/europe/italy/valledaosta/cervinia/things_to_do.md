---
title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Cervinia is a winter resort, and the lift-served ski terrain shared with Valtournenche and Zermatt across the Swiss border is the obvious draw — over 360 kilometres of pistes, mostly gentle, all sitting high enough to stay reliable. Summer brings glacier skiing, hiking, and a small but well-kept golf course, and the Matterhorn itself draws climbers and walkers to the surrounding valleys.
