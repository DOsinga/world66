title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

The Basso Veronese's main draw is its chain of castles and fortified towns, most of them built or reinforced by the Scaligeri — the medieval lords of Verona — and their rivals. [Montagnana](montagnana) is the standout, with some of the best-preserved medieval walls in Italy. The smaller towns — [Bevilacqua](bevilacqua_castle), [Sanguinetto](sanguinetto), [Isola della Scala](isola_della_scala) — each have their own fortification to explore, and most see almost no foreign visitors. In September, [Isola della Scala](isola_della_scala) hosts Italy's largest rice fair.
