Venice is just over an hour away by car or by the Aspilus boat service that runs from the Bibione port to San Basilio in central Venice (about two and a half hours each way). Portogruaro, a small canal town with arcaded streets, and Concordia Sagittaria, a sleepy village built over the Roman colony of Iulia Concordia, are 15 km inland and easily combined. To the east, the Roman ruins at Aquileia and the lagoon island of Grado are both inside an hour.
