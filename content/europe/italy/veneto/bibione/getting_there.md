---
title: "Getting There"
type: section
---

**By car** — Bibione is signposted from the A4 Venice–Trieste motorway (exit Latisana-Lignano), about 15 km from the motorway to the resort.

**By train** — the nearest station is **Latisana-Lignano-Bibione** on the Venice–Trieste line. Frequent ATVO buses connect the station to the Bibione bus terminal in about 35 minutes.

**By plane** — Venice Marco Polo (1 hour 15 minutes by car) and Treviso (1 hour) are the closest airports. Trieste is about 1 hour 30 minutes.

**By boat** — In summer the Aspilus catamaran service connects Bibione harbour with Venice (San Basilio) in around 2 hours 30 minutes each way.
