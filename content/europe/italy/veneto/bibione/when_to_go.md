---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

Bibione runs from late April to early October. **June** is the best month: warm sea, long days, beach umbrellas open, prices still moderate, and the **Sand Storm** races to add some noise. **September** is a close second — quieter, with the sea at its warmest of the year.

**July and August** are when most of central and northern Europe descends — the resort is at capacity, with the second half of August being the absolute peak (Italian *Ferragosto*). Book months ahead if you want to come then, and expect to pay double for everything.

Outside the summer season, almost everything closes. The thermal spa stays open in winter, but the beach concessions, most restaurants and many hotels shutter from late October.
