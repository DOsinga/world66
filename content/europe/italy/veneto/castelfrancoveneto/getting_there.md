---
title: Getting There
type: section
---

Castelfranco sits on the main railway line from Venice to Bassano del Grappa and is a major junction for the Venetian plain, with services in all four directions.

**By train:** Direct services from [Venice](/europe/italy/veneto/venice) (about 50 minutes), [Padua](/europe/italy/veneto/padua) (35 minutes), [Treviso](/europe/italy/veneto/treviso) (25 minutes), [Vicenza](/europe/italy/veneto/vicenza) (40 minutes), and Bassano del Grappa (20 minutes). The station is a short walk from the walls.

**By car:** From the A4 motorway (Milan-Venice) exit at Padova Est and follow the SS307 north for about 30 km. From the A27 (Venice-Belluno) exit at Treviso Sud and drive west on the SR53. Parking is plentiful around the perimeter of the walls but mostly metered.

**By bus:** MOM (Mobilità di Marca) connects Castelfranco to surrounding towns, including [Asolo](/europe/italy/veneto/asolo), Montebelluna, and Bassano. Useful for visiting Villa Emo and the smaller Palladian villas in the area.
