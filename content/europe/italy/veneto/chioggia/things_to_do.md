---
title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Chioggia rewards a slow morning. Walk the length of the [Corso del Popolo](/europe/italy/veneto/chioggia/corso_del_popolo) from north to south, ducking into the [Duomo](/europe/italy/veneto/chioggia/duomo) and the side calli, and arrive at the southern tip in time for the [Piazzetta Vigo](/europe/italy/veneto/chioggia/piazzetta_vigo) and a coffee with a view across the lagoon. Time it so you catch the [fish market](/europe/italy/veneto/chioggia/fish_market) before it closes around midday, when the lagoon's haul of the night is spread out on the marble. Afternoons are for the long sandy beach at [Sottomarina](/europe/italy/veneto/chioggia/sottomarina) — a short walk over the bridge and you are in a different town entirely.
