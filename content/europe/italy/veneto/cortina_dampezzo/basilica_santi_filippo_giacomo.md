The parish church of Cortina d'Ampezzo stands at the foot of the Corso Italia with a campanile that has served as a landmark for returning skiers and hikers for centuries. The current building dates from the eighteenth century, though a church has stood on this site since the medieval period. The interior is Baroque in character, with decorated side chapels and a richly painted ceiling. The adjacent bell tower — detached from the main building, as is common in Veneto churches — offers a useful orientation point: it is visible from the Tofana slopes, from the Faloria cable car, and from most of the valley floor. The church holds the status of minor basilica, granted in recognition of its significance to the Ampezzo community.
