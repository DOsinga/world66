Dolomiti d'Ampezzo Natural Park protects the wild northern side of Cortina d'Ampezzo, where pale limestone towers rise above larch woods, scree slopes, and high pastures. It is one of the strongest reasons to base yourself in Cortina: the park holds many of the area's classic walks, viewpoints, and mountain refuges without feeling like a manicured resort zone.

The terrain ranges from accessible valley paths to serious alpine routes, so it works for both scenic day walks and longer mountain outings. Even if you are not hiking far, the park gives Cortina its grand backdrop and its sense of place in the Dolomites, especially around the Tofane and the valleys leading toward the Austrian border.
