The Rinaldo Zardini Paleontological Museum shows a very different side of the Dolomites: ancient reefs, marine fossils, and the geological story behind the dramatic peaks. Its collections are tied to the work of local fossil collector Rinaldo Zardini.

It is a useful bad-weather stop and a good complement to hiking, because it explains what you are looking at in the mountains. Families and geology-minded travellers will get the most from it.
