---
title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Lessinia is for walkers, geologists, and anyone happy to swap crowds for cheese huts and limestone. The highlights are scattered across the plateau and the valleys that cut into it — most are reached by car along the SP6 road north from Verona, with short walks at the end. A day is enough to taste it; a long weekend lets you reach the high pastures and the fossil sites.
