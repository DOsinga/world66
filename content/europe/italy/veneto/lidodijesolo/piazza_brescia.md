Piazza Brescia is the centre of modern Jesolo: the main pedestrian square at the western end of Viale Bafile, surrounded by hotels, the Aqualandia water park, the biggest nightclubs, and the best free beach access. The square itself was redesigned in the 2000s by architect Gonçalo Byrne with a series of curving fountains and palm trees.

It is the most touristy stretch of an already touristy town — head east along Viale Bafile or further out toward Cortellazzo for slightly quieter alternatives.
