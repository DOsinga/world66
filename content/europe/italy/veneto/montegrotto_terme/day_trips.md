The Euganean Hills around Montegrotto reward a day on foot or by car. Within easy reach by short drive or bus: **Abano Terme** has the same thermal waters and larger spa hotels; **Castello del Catajo**, a few minutes south, is a sixteenth-century rural palace with frescoed halls and a deer park; **Villa Barbarigo** at Valsanzibio holds one of northern Italy's finest Baroque gardens, with a hedge maze and water tricks. **Padua** itself is ten minutes by train.
