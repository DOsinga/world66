The Anatomical Theatre inside the Palazzo del Bo — the historic seat of the University of Padova — is one of the world's most remarkable academic spaces. Built in 1594, it is a steeply banked oval of six wooden tiers, each narrower than the one below, designed so that students at the top could see directly down onto the dissecting table in the centre. It holds about 250 people standing and has the atmosphere of a very serious amphitheatre.

The design solved a practical problem: how to let large numbers of students observe a procedure being carried out at the bottom. The solution was so effective that the same design was copied at universities across Europe for the next two centuries. The table in the centre is the original 16th-century slab.

Visits are by guided tour only; tours depart from the Palazzo del Bo reception and are conducted in Italian and English.
