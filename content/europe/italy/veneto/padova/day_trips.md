---
title: Day Trips
type: section
---

Padova sits in the Veneto plain with easy rail and road connections. Venice is 25 minutes by fast train and deserves at least a full day. Verona is 50 minutes and a good contrast — Roman amphitheatre, Shakespeare associations, excellent wine country in the surrounding hills.

Closer to Padova, Abano Terme is just 10 kilometres southwest: a spa resort town known since Roman times for its thermal waters, still functioning as a working thermal destination rather than a museum piece. The Euganean Hills behind Abano Terme make for a pleasant afternoon of walking and wine tasting among small hillside villages. Treviso, 30 minutes north by train, is a smaller and quieter version of a Veneto canal town, often overlooked and well worth the visit.
