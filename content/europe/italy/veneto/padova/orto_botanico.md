The Orto Botanico is the oldest botanical garden in the world that remains in continuous use on its original site, founded in 1545 for the cultivation of medicinal plants by the University of Padova's School of Medicine. The UNESCO designation covers not just the garden itself but its influence on botanical science, medical education, and the development of botanical gardens worldwide.

The garden is circular in layout, the original 16th-century design largely preserved, with more recent additions extending the collection to over 6,000 plant species. The historic centre contains plants that have been in continuous cultivation here since the 16th and 17th centuries. The aquatic section has papyrus and other water plants in Renaissance-era stone tanks. The new greenhouse complex (opened 2014) houses tropical collections and is worth the entrance fee on its own.

Open daily; the best months are April to June when the greatest number of plants are in flower.
