---
title: Things to Do
type: section
---

Padova's attractions are densely concentrated in the historic centre and can be covered in two full days if you are organised. The Scrovegni Chapel requires an advance booking. The Sant'Antonio basilica, Prato della Valle, and the botanical garden are all within easy walking distance of each other in the southern part of the old city; the university, the piazzas, and Caffè Pedrocchi cluster in the centre.
