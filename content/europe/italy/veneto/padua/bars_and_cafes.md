---
title: "Bars and Cafes"
type: section
---

Padua invented the **spritz** — Aperol, Prosecco, and a splash of soda over ice with an orange slice — and the city's bar culture is built around the early-evening aperitivo. The two market squares, **Piazza delle Erbe** and **Piazza della Frutta**, are the centre of the action; from about 6.30 pm every bar terrace fills with students and office workers, and the squares stay busy until midnight.

For something a little more atmospheric: **Caffè Pedrocchi** (1831, the legendary neoclassical café), **Enoteca Da Severino** (a serious natural-wine bar with cicheti), **Osteria al Pero** for traditional cicheti and a quieter crowd, and **Bar dei Osei** for the cheapest spritz and the busiest counter in town. Late nightlife in Padua is centred on the university quarter around Via Belzoni.
