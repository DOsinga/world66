---
title: "Getting Around"
type: section
---

Central Padua is small and almost entirely walkable. From the railway station to Sant'Antonio at the southern edge of the historic centre is around 25 minutes on foot; from Piazza dei Signori to the Prato della Valle is 15 minutes.

A single **tram line** crosses the city north–south, passing the station, the cathedral, the Prato della Valle, and continuing into the southern suburbs. **Buses** cover the wider city and the suburbs. Tickets are sold at tobacconists and station kiosks; validate on board.

**Bicycles** are popular and the streets are flat — bike hire is available at the station and at several outlets in the centre. Padua's medieval streets are mostly pedestrianised; cars need a permit to enter the ZTL zone.
