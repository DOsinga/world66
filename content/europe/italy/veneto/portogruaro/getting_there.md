---
title: Getting There
type: section
---

Portogruaro sits on the main railway line from Venice to Trieste and is one of the easiest places in eastern Veneto to reach.

**By train:** The Portogruaro–Caorle station is a five-minute walk from the old town. Frequent regional trains from [Venice Santa Lucia](/europe/italy/veneto/venice) (about an hour) and Trieste (an hour fifteen). Faster Frecciabianca services stop here too.

**By car:** Portogruaro has its own exit on the A4 (Milan-Venice-Trieste) motorway. Parking is plentiful in lots around the perimeter of the old town. The old centre itself is mostly ZTL (limited traffic zone) and best entered on foot.

**By bus:** ATVO services connect Portogruaro to [Bibione](/europe/italy/veneto/bibione), Caorle, Latisana and other towns of the eastern Veneto coast — useful in summer when the beach resorts get busy.
