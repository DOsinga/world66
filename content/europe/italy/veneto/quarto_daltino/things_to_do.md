---
title: "Things to Do in Quarto d'Altino"
type: section
---

Quarto d'Altino's main draw is the Museo Nazionale di Altino, which houses finds from the ancient Roman city of Altinum. Beyond the museum, the town is primarily of interest as a base for visiting Venice and the Venetian Lagoon — but the Roman history beneath the fields and the museum that reveals it are genuinely worth stopping for.
