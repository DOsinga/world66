title: Things to Do
type: section
---

San Vito di Cadore rewards both winter and summer visitors with dramatic Dolomite scenery and outdoor activity. In winter the ski area connects directly to the Cortina zone; in summer the valley transforms into a hiking and cycling base. The peaks above the village — above all Monte Antelao and the Sorapiss group — define some of the most demanding and spectacular terrain in the eastern Dolomites.
