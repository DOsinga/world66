---
title: When to Go
type: section
---

San Vito di Cadore has two clear seasons. Winter runs from December to March, when the ski area operates and the Dolomites are at their most spectacular under snow. January and February are the coldest months, with reliable snow cover and shorter lift queues than at Cortina. The Christmas and New Year period sees the village at its busiest.

Summer, from late June through September, offers the best hiking weather — warm days, long light, and accessible high trails. July and August are popular with Italian families escaping the heat of the lowlands; the weeks either side avoid the worst crowding while keeping reliable weather. Spring and autumn are quiet and can be beautiful, but many mountain refuges close and some trails are inaccessible until the snow clears.
