A restored 14th-century house used as the city's main exhibition space. It hosted the run of major Asian-themed shows in the 2000s and 2010s — the Silk Road, the Mongols, classical China — that put Treviso briefly on the international exhibition circuit. The programme has thinned out since but the building, with its painted timber ceilings and brick interior, is still worth a look during any temporary show.

Check what is on before visiting; the building closes between exhibitions.
