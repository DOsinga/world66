Venice is 30 minutes away by train and the obvious side trip, though Treviso is small enough that the trip works equally well in reverse. The Prosecco hills around Valdobbiadene and Conegliano lie 30 minutes north by car — UNESCO-listed since 2019 and worth a slow drive with a few cellar stops. Asolo, the small hilltop town 35 km west, was Robert Browning's adopted home.
