---
title: "Eating Out"
type: section
---

Treviso is the home of tiramisu and of radicchio rosso — both invented or perfected here, and both inescapable on local menus. The classic Trevigiana cuisine leans on bitter greens, river fish, and game from the surrounding hills. Eat in the osterie around the centre rather than the tourist-facing places on Piazza dei Signori.
