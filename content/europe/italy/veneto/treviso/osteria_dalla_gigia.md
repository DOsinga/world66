Tiny old-style osteria off the Pescheria, with marble tables, paper place mats, and a handwritten daily menu. The specialty is small bites of tripe and meatballs served with a glass of house red — eaten standing at the counter or perched on one of the few stools. Lunch only, mostly local clientele.

The kind of place that feels unchanged for fifty years, because it has been.
