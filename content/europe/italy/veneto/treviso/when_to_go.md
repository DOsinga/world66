---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

Late spring (May, June) and early autumn (September, October) are best — warm without the suffocating August heat that drives locals to the mountains or coast. Winter is mild but damp, with frequent fog rolling off the Sile. Radicchio rosso is in season from late November through February, and many restaurants build special menus around it. Christmas brings a small but pretty market under the arcades of Piazza dei Signori.
