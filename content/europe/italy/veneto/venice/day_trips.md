The lagoon islands are the obvious half-day excursions — Murano for glassblowing, Burano for colour and seafood, Torcello for its abandoned Byzantine cathedral. All are reached by vaporetto and work well in combination.

On the mainland, the Veneto rewards exploration. Padua is forty minutes by train and has Giotto's Scrovegni Chapel frescoes and a superb indoor food market. Verona — Romeo and Juliet aside — has a remarkable Roman arena still in use for opera, good Renaissance architecture, and a well-preserved old centre. Vicenza is for Palladio: the Teatro Olimpico, the Basilica Palladiana, and a dozen villas in the surrounding hills. Prosecco country begins just north of Treviso and the hill road through Valdobbiadene is pleasant driving.
