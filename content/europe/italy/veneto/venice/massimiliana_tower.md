Massimiliana Tower is a squat 19th-century Austrian fort on Sant'Erasmo, the quiet agricultural island north-east of Venice. Its circular brick form, moat, and low defensive earthworks feel far removed from the palaces of the Grand Canal.

The tower is worth seeking out if you are already using Sant'Erasmo as a slow day away from central Venice, with lagoon views and a sense of how the city defended its outer islands.
