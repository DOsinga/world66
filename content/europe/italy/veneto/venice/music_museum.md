Venice's Music Museum combines a church setting with a collection of musical instruments and Venetian Baroque paintings. It is a compact stop near La Fenice and the Accademia side of the Grand Canal.

The museum is tied to Interpreti Veneziani, who also stage concerts nearby at San Vidal. It is best treated as a short cultural pause rather than a full museum visit.
