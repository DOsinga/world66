Oratorio dei Crociferi is a small oratory in Cannaregio, facing the former Crociferi monastery complex. Its main draw is the painted cycle associated with Palma the Younger and the intimate scale of the interior.

It is a quiet art stop near Chiesa dei Gesuiti and works best as part of a Cannaregio walk, not as a long museum visit.
