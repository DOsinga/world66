Santa Maria Formosa is one of Castello's notable churches, standing on a site associated with a 7th-century foundation legend. The present church was designed by Mauro Codussi in the late 15th century, and it makes a worthwhile stop while walking between San Marco, Castello's lanes, and nearby Palazzo Grimani.
