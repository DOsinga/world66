Santa Maria Formosa is one of Castello's more rewarding church stops, set on a lively campo a short walk from San Marco but with far less crowding. The present church was designed in the early Renaissance by Mauro Codussi, though the site has much older roots and a local legend involving a vision of the Virgin Mary.

Go for the mix of architecture, neighbourhood life, and quiet interior detail rather than a single blockbuster artwork. It fits well into a walk between San Marco, Santa Maria dei Miracoli, and Santi Giovanni e Paolo.
