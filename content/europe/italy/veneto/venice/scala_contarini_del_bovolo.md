Scala Contarini del Bovolo is a small palace tower known for its open spiral staircase, a graceful mix of late Gothic and early Renaissance Venetian design. It sits tucked into the lanes of San Marco, so arriving there feels like finding a hidden architectural prize rather than another grand canal-front monument.

Climb the staircase for close views of the arches and a compact panorama over central Venice. It is best treated as a short, memorable stop between Piazza San Marco, La Fenice, and the Rialto area.
