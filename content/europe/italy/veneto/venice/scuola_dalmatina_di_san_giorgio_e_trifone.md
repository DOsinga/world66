Scuola Dalmatina di San Giorgio e Trifone is a small Venetian confraternity building known for Vittore Carpaccio's painted cycle on the lives of Saints George, Tryphon, and Jerome. It is one of the quieter art stops in Castello, away from the main San Marco flow.

The appeal is focused: come for Carpaccio, the intimate setting, and a glimpse of Venice's historic foreign communities.
