The Scuola Grande di San Giovanni Evangelista is one of Venice's historic confraternity buildings, tucked into San Polo near the Frari. Its showpiece is the monumental Renaissance staircase by Mauro Codussi, leading to ceremonial rooms used for processions, meetings, and religious display.

It is quieter than the nearby Scuola Grande di San Rocco, but worthwhile if you are interested in Venice beyond churches and palaces.
