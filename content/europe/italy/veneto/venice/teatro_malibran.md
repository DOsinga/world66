Teatro Malibran is a historic Venetian theatre dating from 1678 and now operated as part of La Fenice. It hosts opera, ballet, concerts, and symphonic performances in a more intimate setting than Venice's best-known opera house.

It is most rewarding with a ticket, but the building is also a notable stop in the lanes north of Rialto.
