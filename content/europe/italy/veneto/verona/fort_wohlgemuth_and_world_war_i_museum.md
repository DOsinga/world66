Fort Wohlgemuth, also known as Forte Monte, is one of the old Austro-Hungarian fortifications guarding the Adige valley north of Verona. Its military story belongs to the borderlands before Italian unification and to the First World War landscape that still marks the hills around Lake Garda and the Veneto.

The site is a better fit for travellers with a car and an interest in military history than for first-time visitors racing through Verona's Roman and medieval sights. The reward is the combination of stone fort, museum material, and valley views.
