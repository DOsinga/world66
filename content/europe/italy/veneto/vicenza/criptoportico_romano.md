The Criptoportico Romano is one of the few visible remains of Roman Vicenza. The underground passageways once connected villas and sheltered people walking between them from weather and sun.

Its entrance is tucked away near Piazza Duomo, so it is easy to miss. It is best visited as a short archaeological counterpoint to Vicenza's better-known Palladian sights.
