Monte Berico Sanctuary rises above Vicenza as both a pilgrimage church and a viewpoint
over Palladio's city. The basilica's setting is half the experience: arcaded approaches,
hilltop air, and a panorama that explains Vicenza's relationship with the surrounding
plain and hills.

Inside, the sanctuary is an active devotional place, so visit with the same respect you
would bring to any major church. It pairs naturally with Villa Valmarana ai Nani or a
longer walk up from the centre.
