Palazzo Barbaran da Porto is the only Vicenza palace that Palladio both began and completed. Its street front is restrained, but the interior is richer, with frescoed rooms that show how Palladian architecture worked for a powerful urban family.

The palace also houses Palladio-related exhibitions, making it a useful stop if you want to understand the buildings scattered around Vicenza rather than just admire their façades.
