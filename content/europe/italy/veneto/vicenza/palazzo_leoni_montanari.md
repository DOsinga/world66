Palazzo Leoni Montanari is a richly decorated Baroque palace in central Vicenza, now used as an art museum. Its collection is best known for Russian icons, Venetian painting, and works by artists such as Pietro Longhi.

The palace is a good counterpoint to Vicenza's Palladian architecture: smaller than the city's headline sights, but rewarding if you like ornate interiors and quieter art collections.
