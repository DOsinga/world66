Piazzale della Vittoria is the terrace beside Monte Berico Sanctuary, with one of the best broad views over Vicenza. On clear days the city, surrounding plain, and mountain backdrop are all visible from the lookout.

It is an easy add-on to the sanctuary visit and a good sunset stop, especially after walking up from the centre or touring nearby Palladian villas.
