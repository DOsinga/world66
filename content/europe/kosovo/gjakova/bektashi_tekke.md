The Bektashi Tekke is one of Gjakova's important religious sites, tied to the Bektashi Sufi tradition and the city's long mix of Muslim communities. It is a quieter stop than the nearby bazaar and mosques, but it helps explain the religious diversity that shaped Gjakova.

Visitors should treat it as an active spiritual site: dress modestly, keep voices low, and ask before entering private areas.
