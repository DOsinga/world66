Tabak Bridge is a small Ottoman stone bridge whose name points to the tanners' guild, one of the trades that helped shape Gjakova's old urban economy. Its scale is modest, but the arched stonework and guild association give it more character than an ordinary crossing.

It works best as part of a wider walk through Gjakova's bazaar and Ottoman-era sights. Do not go expecting a major monument; go because the bridge is a compact, photogenic piece of the city's craft and trade history.
