Liqenat Lake is a popular mountain hike in the Rugova area above Peja. The climb takes about an hour and rewards you with open views, alpine air, and a quiet lake that works well for a picnic.

Bring food, water, and shoes with grip. The route is short enough for a half-day outing, but the mountain weather can change quickly.
