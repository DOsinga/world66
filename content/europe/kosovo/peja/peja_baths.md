The Peja Baths are thermal and mineral springs outside Peja, set in a small area known for several natural springs. The baths are a traditional local stop for warm mineral water rather than a polished spa resort.

They are best treated as a short side trip if you are already exploring the Peja area, especially if you are interested in local bathing culture or want a gentler outing after hiking around Rugova Gorge.
