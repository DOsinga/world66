Bear Sanctuary Pristina gives former captive restaurant and entertainment bears a far better home in forested enclosures outside the city. The setting is calm, the paths are easy to follow, and the visit tells a larger story about animal welfare in Kosovo after the war years.

It is outside central Pristina, so plan transport rather than assuming a casual walk. The sanctuary is one of the stronger half-day trips from the capital, especially for families or anyone needing a break from urban sightseeing.
