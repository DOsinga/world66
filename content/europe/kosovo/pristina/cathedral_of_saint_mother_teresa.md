The Cathedral of Saint Mother Teresa is one of Pristina's most visible modern landmarks, with a tall bell tower that rises above the central city. The Roman Catholic cathedral was inaugurated in 2010 and is dedicated to Mother Teresa, whose family roots were Albanian. Visitors can stop in to see the interior and, when open, use the audio guides for more context.
