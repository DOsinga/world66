Pristina's Independence Museum occupies a building tied to Kosovo's late-20th-century political history. It served as the headquarters of the Democratic League of Kosovo in the 1990s and was later restored as a museum after being damaged during the war.

The museum is small, but it helps explain the political movement that led toward Kosovo's declaration of independence. It is best combined with nearby central Pristina sights rather than treated as a long standalone visit.
