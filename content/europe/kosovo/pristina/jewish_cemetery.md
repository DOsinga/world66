Pristina's Jewish Cemetery is a small, easily overlooked site on Tauk Bahqe hill, with graves linked to the city's once larger Jewish community. It is not a polished attraction, but it adds an important layer to a city usually read through Ottoman, Yugoslav, and post-war landmarks.

Go respectfully and do not expect extensive interpretation on site. The cemetery is most meaningful for travellers interested in minority histories, urban memory, and the quieter traces of Pristina before the upheavals of the 20th century.
