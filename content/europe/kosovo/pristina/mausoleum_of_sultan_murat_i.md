The Mausoleum of Sultan Murat I stands near the Kosovo Field, where the Ottoman ruler was killed during the Battle of Kosovo in 1389. The türbe marks the place associated with his death and burial of his internal organs, while his body was taken back to Bursa.

For travellers interested in Balkan history, the site is valuable because it sits at the intersection of Ottoman memory, Serbian national history, and Kosovo's layered religious landscape. It is a short trip from Pristina, best paired with other Kosovo Field monuments and approached with awareness that the battle's legacy remains politically and emotionally charged.
