Pristina Ethnographic Museum is one of the strongest heritage stops in the old part of the city. The museum occupies a small complex of 18th- and 19th-century buildings and presents traditional life in Kosovo through themes such as birth, daily life, death, and spiritual heritage.

Guided interpretation is often part of the visit, which helps make sense of the domestic objects, rooms, and customs on display. The restored buildings are as important as the collection, giving a rare look at older urban architecture in Pristina.
