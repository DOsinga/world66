Shadërvani Fountain is a small Ottoman marble fountain between the Carshi Mosque and the Kosovo Museum. It is the last survivor of dozens of public fountains that once supplied Pristina with drinking water and water for ritual ablutions.

The fountain is best seen as part of a walk through the old bazaar area, where the mosque, hammam, clock tower, and museum are all close together.
