Ulpiana Archaeological Park preserves the remains of an important Roman and Byzantine city just southeast of Pristina. The site includes excavated streets, walls, baths, and early Christian basilica remains, showing that Kosovo's central plain was tied into the Roman Balkans long before the medieval Serbian and Ottoman layers most visitors know.

The ruins are open and relatively quiet, so the visit depends on your interest in archaeology rather than spectacle. It combines well with nearby Gračanica Monastery, making a short excursion from Pristina that links the region's classical and medieval histories in one easy loop.
