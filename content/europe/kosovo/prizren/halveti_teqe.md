The Halveti Teqe is a compact Sufi lodge tucked into old Prizren, close enough to the bazaar that it is easy to miss if you are moving too quickly. The complex dates from the Ottoman period and keeps the intimate scale of a working religious house: a courtyard, ritual rooms, carved timber, and objects connected with the Halveti order.

It is worth visiting because it adds a quieter layer to Prizren's better-known mosque and church circuit. Be discreet, dress respectfully, and remember that this is not just a museum piece; ceremonies and community memory still matter here.
