The Prizren Hydroelectric Power Plant Museum preserves an early power plant on the Lumbardhi river, turning a piece of infrastructure into a small industrial-heritage stop. Its machinery and setting help explain how the city modernized beyond its Ottoman-era monuments.

It is not a large museum, but it is distinctive in a city where most visitors focus on mosques, bridges, and hillside views. Combine it with a walk along the river or toward the fortress.
