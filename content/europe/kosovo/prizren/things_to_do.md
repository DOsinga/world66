---
title: Things to Do
type: section
---

Prizren rewards wandering. The old town is compact and largely pedestrianised along the river, with enough mosques, churches, bazaars, and fortifications to fill two or three days. The city also serves as a base for excursions into the Sharr Mountains, which rise steeply from the southern edge of town. Most of the key sights are within easy walking distance of the old bazaar.
