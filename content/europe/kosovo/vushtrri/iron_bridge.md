The Iron Bridge, more often described in heritage sources as Vushtrri's old stone bridge, is one of the town's few sights that immediately explains why this was a route town. Its arches and worn masonry sit close to the centre, giving a tangible link to the older road system across the plain.

It is not a long visit, but it is worth adding to a walk around Vushtrri because the town has a thin POI layer. Pair it with the local museum and mosque area rather than making a special trip just for the bridge.
