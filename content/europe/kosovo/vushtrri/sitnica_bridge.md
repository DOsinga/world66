Sitnica Bridge is a handsome old stone crossing near Vushtrri, valued less for spectacle than for continuity. Its arches and low profile belong to the Ottoman road landscape of Kosovo, when bridges like this linked towns, markets, and river plains.

Stop if you are already exploring Vushtrri or travelling the central Kosovo corridor. The bridge is a short visit, but it gives the town a tangible historic anchor beyond modern traffic and everyday streets.
