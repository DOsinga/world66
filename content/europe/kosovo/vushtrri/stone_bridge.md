The Stone Bridge of Vushtrri, often identified with the Vojinović Bridge, is one of the town's clearest historic landmarks. Its long stone arches recall the old routes that crossed Kosovo before modern roads shifted the traffic elsewhere.

The bridge is a short visit, but it pairs naturally with Vushtrri's old core and museum. Come for the texture of a smaller Kosovo town rather than a polished heritage presentation.
