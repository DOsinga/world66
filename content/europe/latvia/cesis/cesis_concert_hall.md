The Vidzeme Concert Hall in Cesis is a regional cultural centre of some ambition for a town of this size. Opened in 2014, it holds around 800 people and hosts classical music concerts, theatre performances, and the annual Cesis Music Festival in summer — one of the more notable chamber music festivals in Latvia.

The building is contemporary and sits near the castle complex. If you are visiting in summer, check whether there are performances scheduled during your stay.
