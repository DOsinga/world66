**Mark Rothko: A Biography** by James E.B. Breslin (1993) — the definitive life of the painter, covering his Daugavpils origins, immigration to America, and the development of his distinctive abstract style. Essential reading before visiting the art centre.

**The Bradt Guide to Latvia** by Stephen Baister and Chris Patrick — practical and well-researched, with good coverage of Daugavpils and the Latgale region that most guidebooks skip over.

**Riga: A Short History** by Edmund Griffiths — a compact account of Latvia's capital that also illuminates the broader currents of Latvian history, including the Soviet period that shaped Daugavpils's current character.

**Selected Poems** by Aleksandrs Čaks — Latvia's most celebrated 20th-century poet, associated with Riga but essential for understanding the Latvian imagination. His streetwise, melancholy verse captures something of the spirit of Latvian urban life between the wars.
