---
title: "Getting Around"
type: section
---

The city centre is compact and walkable. The main sights — old town churches, the central square, restaurants — are all within easy reach on foot.

The **Daugavpils Fortress** sits about 2km north of the centre, a 15–20 minute walk along a straightforward route, or a short and cheap taxi ride. Local buses also serve the fortress area.

**Taxis** are inexpensive and useful for getting between the fortress and centre, or for reaching accommodation further out. Ride-hailing apps may have limited coverage; asking your hotel to call a local taxi is reliable.

For exploring the **Latgale lake district**, a rental car is the most practical option — public transport reaches some villages but timetables are sparse. Car hire is available in the city.
