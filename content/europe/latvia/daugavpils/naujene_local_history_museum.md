Naujene Local History Museum covers the nature, history and ethnography of the Daugavpils district. Exhibits focus on local settlement, rural life and the landscape around the Daugava River.

It is a modest museum, but useful for travellers exploring the countryside outside Daugavpils or looking for context beyond the city's fortress and churches.
