---
title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

The headline attraction is the **Daugavpils Fortress**, a monumental 19th-century star-shaped fortification that ranks among the largest preserved military complexes in Eastern Europe. Within its walls sits the **Mark Rothko Art Centre**, an unexpectedly excellent museum honouring the abstract expressionist painter who was born in Daugavpils in 1903. The combination of imposing brick ramparts and world-class modern art makes for a memorable afternoon.

The old town rewards a wander, with an unusual concentration of churches representing the city's many religious communities — Orthodox, Catholic, Lutheran, and Old Believer. Beyond the city, the Latgale lake district stretches east, offering easy escapes into nature.
