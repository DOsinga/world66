Daugavpils has a remarkable density of religious buildings packed into its old town, reflecting centuries of overlapping communities. Within a few blocks you'll find Russian Orthodox, Roman Catholic, Lutheran, and Old Believer congregations — the Old Believers being a particularly significant presence in Latgale, having settled here after fleeing persecution in Russia in the 17th century. The **Boris and Gleb Cathedral** is the most striking Orthodox church, with its blue domes visible from much of the centre. The concentration of different faiths in a single neighbourhood is one of the more quietly fascinating things about Daugavpils.
