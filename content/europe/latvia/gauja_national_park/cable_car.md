The Sigulda Cable Car crosses the Gauja Valley on a 1.1 km ride, giving open views over the river, forested slopes, Sigulda Castle, Turaida Castle, Krimulda Manor, and the bobsleigh track. It is one of the easiest ways to grasp the scale of the valley.
