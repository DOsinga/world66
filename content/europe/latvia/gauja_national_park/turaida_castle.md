Turaida Castle is one of the major sights of the Gauja valley. The red-brick castle has been rebuilt and now holds exhibitions on the region's history, but the main draw is the climb up the tower for views over the forested river valley.

The castle is part of the wider Turaida Museum Reserve, so allow time for the grounds as well as the tower. It is a natural stop on a day in the Sigulda and Gauja National Park area.
