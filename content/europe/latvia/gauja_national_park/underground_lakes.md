The Underground Lakes near Straupe are a guided cave visit with water-filled passages beneath the forested Gauja region. The trip is unusual for Latvia and works best as a family-friendly detour rather than a major sight.

Visits need a guide, so check ahead before going. The appeal is the novelty of seeing the hidden lakes and caves up close, especially with children.
