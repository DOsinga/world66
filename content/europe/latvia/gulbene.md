Gulbene is a small town in northeastern Latvia, out in Vidzeme's quieter manor and forest country. It is too far from [Riga](/europe/latvia/riga) to be an accidental stop, but the town has one very good reason to come: the Gulbene-Alūksne narrow-gauge railway.

The [Bānītis](/europe/latvia/gulbene/gulbene_aluksne_banitis) is the only regularly running narrow-gauge passenger railway in the Baltics, and it gives Gulbene a stronger identity than its size suggests. Around it are old railway buildings, quiet streets, and the remains of manor life.

The town itself has [Vecgulbene Manor](/europe/latvia/gulbene/vecgulbene_manor), while [Stāmeriena Palace](/europe/latvia/gulbene/stameriena_palace) makes the best nearby side trip. Gulbene is not polished in the way [Cesis](/europe/latvia/cesis) is, but if old trains, Baltic manor houses, and rural Latvia appeal, it is worth a deliberate day.
