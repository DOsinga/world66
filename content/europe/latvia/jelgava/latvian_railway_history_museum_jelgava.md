This branch of the Latvian Railway History Museum sits by the station, which makes it easy to add before or after a train from Riga. It is small, but the locomotives, photographs, and railway objects tell a useful story in a city shaped by transport routes.

It is a selective stop rather than an essential one. Railway-minded travellers will be happier here than anyone racing through Jelgava in two hours.
