St. Catherine's Church is one of Kuldiga's main historic churches, with medieval origins and a later Baroque interior. Its carved wooden altar and long ties to the old Duchy of Courland make it more than just another parish church.

If the tower is open, climb it for a useful view over the old town roofs and the Venta valley. The church sits close enough to Kuldiga's other central sights to include on a slow walk through town.
