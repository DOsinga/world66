Kuldīga's brick bridge was built in 1874 and still feels made for a slower century. Its seven arches cross the Venta just below the old town, with the rapid spread out upstream and the museum and castle park nearby.

It is the best single viewpoint in town. Cross it once for the view, then go down to the riverbank to see how wide the falls really are.
