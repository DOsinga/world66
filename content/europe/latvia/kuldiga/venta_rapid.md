Venta Rapid is less about height than width: a long, low limestone shelf stretching across the Venta River. From the bridge it looks almost gentle, but down on the bank you understand why locals treat it as the town's outdoor stage.

In spring, fish try to leap the falls on their way upstream. In summer, people wade in and sit under the flow for a cold river massage.
