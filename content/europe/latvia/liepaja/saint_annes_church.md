Saint Anne's Church is the oldest church in Liepaja, first mentioned in 1508. The red-brick building is best known for its tall tower, climbable when access is open, and a large wooden Baroque altar from 1697.

The organ is among the largest in Latvia, giving the church more than just architectural interest. It is an easy stop while walking through the older part of the city.
