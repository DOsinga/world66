St. Joseph's Cathedral is Liepaja's largest Catholic church, a yellow-brick landmark with clustered towers and a strong vertical silhouette. Inside, a ship model recalls the city's fishing and seafaring traditions and the belief that such offerings brought luck to sailors.

It is close enough to the centre to combine with Liepaja Museum, the theatre, and the old streets of Vecliepaja.
