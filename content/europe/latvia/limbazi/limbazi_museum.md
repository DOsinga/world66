Limbaži Museum sits in the old castle manor administrator's building beside the ruins. Its exhibitions cover local history and include material on Baumaņu Kārlis, the composer of Latvia's national anthem.

This is the place that turns the castle hill from a ruin into a story. It is small, but useful if you want to understand why Limbaži mattered in northern Vidzeme.
