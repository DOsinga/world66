The Live Silver Museum is Limbaži's oddest cultural stop. It displays silver jewellery, decorative objects, and larger works by artist Oļegs Auzers, including a large silver palace model that the museum presents as one of the biggest silver artworks in the Baltic region.

It is a specialist collection, so it will not suit everyone. But if you like craft, metalwork, or small museums with a clear personality, it is more memorable than another generic local-history room.
