Limbaži's old fire station is a brick building with a tower that opens to visitors in summer together with the local fire museum. The tower gives a compact view over the town center.

It is a small stop, but a worthwhile one if you are already visiting the castle ruins, Limbaži Museum, or Live Silver Museum nearby.
