All Saints Church is a Latvian-designed Orthodox church from around 1880, built in a neo-Romanesque style rather than the more familiar Russian Orthodox look. It is a modest but worthwhile stop if you are exploring Riga's outer east bank and want to see a different strand of the city's church architecture.
