Art Museum Riga Bourse holds the largest foreign art collection in the Baltic states. The galleries are housed in the former Riga Stock Exchange building in the Old Town, so the architecture is part of the visit.

The collection is strongest for European, Asian, and ancient art, making it a useful counterpoint to Riga's history museums and churches.
