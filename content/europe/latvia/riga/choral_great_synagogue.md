The Choral Great Synagogue is a Holocaust memorial at the ruins of Riga's main synagogue, burned by German forces on July 4, 1941 with hundreds of people locked inside. The site also honors Latvians who helped save Jews during the occupation, making it one of the city's most direct and sobering Jewish heritage stops.
