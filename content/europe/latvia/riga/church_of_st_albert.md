The Church of St. Albert is a Roman Catholic church in Pardaugava with a striking early-20th-century exterior. Parts of its interior were designed in the 1950s, despite the anti-religious pressure of the Soviet period.

It is a worthwhile detour for visitors exploring Riga beyond the Old Town, especially those interested in the city's Catholic and Soviet-era religious history.
