---
title: Getting Around
type: section
---

**Walking** covers most of what visitors want to see. The Old Town and the Art Nouveau district on Alberta iela and Elizabetes iela are about 15 minutes apart on foot; together they make a natural half-day walking circuit.

**Trams and buses** are cheap and frequent. The network is extensive and the stops are signed in Latvian with route numbers. Buy tickets from the driver or validate e-tickets on the card reader inside the vehicle. A single journey costs around €1.15. Tram 11 runs between the Old Town and the Art Nouveau district; tram 1 reaches the Open Air Ethnographic Museum.

**Cycling** is practical in the flat city centre. Several rental companies operate near the Old Town and the Central Market; Sixt and various dockless schemes are available. The Daugava river embankment has a dedicated cycle path.

**Taxis** are metered and generally honest. The main apps are Bolt (formerly Taxify) and Yandex.Taxi; both are cheaper than flagging a cab on the street. Agree the fare before getting in if you use an unmarked taxi.

The Old Town is mostly pedestrianised; driving in the centre is not useful and parking is expensive.
