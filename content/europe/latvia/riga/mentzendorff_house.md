Mentzendorff House is a former wealthy merchant's residence from 1720, now run as a museum of domestic life in Riga during the 17th and 18th centuries. Rooms are furnished to show how the city's prosperous trading families lived.

It is a compact Old Town stop, close to St Peter's Church, the House of the Blackheads, and other central Riga sights.
