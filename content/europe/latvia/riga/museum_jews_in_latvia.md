Museum "Jews in Latvia" is a small museum on Jewish life in Latvia from the 16th century to the present. Exhibits cover community history, the destruction of synagogues during the Holocaust, and Latvians who helped save Jews under Nazi occupation.

Labelling is in Latvian and English, making it a useful, focused stop near Riga's central museum district.
