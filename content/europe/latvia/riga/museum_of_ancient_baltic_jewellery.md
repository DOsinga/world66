The Museum of Ancient Baltic Jewellery is a small Old Town museum founded by Latvian jewellers. It focuses on Baltic ornaments, metalwork, and reconstructed pieces, making it a useful stop for visitors interested in local craft traditions and the material culture of the region before the modern city took shape.
