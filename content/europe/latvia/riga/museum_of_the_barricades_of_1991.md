This small Old Town museum tells the story of the January 1991 barricades, when Soviet troops tried to overturn Latvia's elected government and residents filled central Riga with makeshift defences.

It is a focused stop rather than a broad history museum, strongest if you want to understand the tense final months of Soviet rule in Latvia and the civic resistance that helped shape the country's restored independence.
