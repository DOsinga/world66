The Museum of the Occupation of Latvia explains Latvia's 20th-century occupations under Soviet and Nazi rule, using documents, photographs, personal stories, and stark exhibition design. Its black modernist building stands out sharply beside the reconstructed House of the Blackheads.

This is one of Riga's most important historical museums. It is sobering rather than decorative, and gives essential context for understanding modern Latvia and the meaning of independence here.
