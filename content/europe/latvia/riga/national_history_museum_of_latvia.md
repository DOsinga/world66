The National History Museum of Latvia gives a broad introduction to Latvian history, from early settlement through the country's modern political story. It is a useful stop near the old town if you want context before the churches, guild houses, and Art Nouveau streets.

English coverage can be thinner than in Riga's most tourist-facing museums, but the room summaries and objects still make the bigger timeline clear.
