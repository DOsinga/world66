Riga's Old New Synagogue is a late-19th-century Jewish building that survived the destruction of the Second World War, unlike most of the city's synagogues. It was later altered and used as residential space, so the visit is mainly about the exterior and the history it carries.

It is a modest stop, but worthwhile if you are tracing Riga's Jewish heritage beyond the better-known memorials and museums.
