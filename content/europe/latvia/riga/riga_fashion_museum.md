Riga Fashion Museum is a small museum in Old Town, close to the House of the Blackheads and St Peter's Church. It focuses on fashion history, costume, and changing style rather than Latvian history in the usual civic-museum sense.

The museum is best treated as a short specialist stop while walking through Vecrīga. It is most worthwhile if the current temporary exhibition matches your interests, so check the programme before making it a priority.
