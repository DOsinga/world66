Riga Film Museum covers Latvian cinema through changing exhibitions on film history, actors, and the craft of filmmaking. It is a compact museum, but a useful stop for visitors interested in culture beyond the Old Town's churches and guild houses.

Check the current exhibition before going, as the appeal depends heavily on what is on display.
