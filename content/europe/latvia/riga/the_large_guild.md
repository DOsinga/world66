The Large Guild was the meeting house of Riga's German merchants, a reminder of the city's Hanseatic past and the social order that shaped the Old Town. Today it is home to the Latvian National Symphony Orchestra, so casual visitors usually see it from the outside unless attending a concert. The Gothic Revival facade is worth noting while walking between Livu Square and the House of the Blackheads.
