Riga's Theatre Museum occupies the unusual pre-war house of a former director of the Daile Theatre, one of the major figures in 20th-century Latvian theatre. The setting is part of the appeal, with period rooms and theatre history sharing the same domestic space.

It is a niche but worthwhile stop for visitors interested in Latvian culture, stage design, and Riga's less obvious museums.
