Tukums Old Town is small, but that is part of its charm. The streets around Brivibas Square, Harmonijas iela, and the museum buildings give a quick sense of a Latvian provincial town that has kept its scale.

It is worth wandering without a strict route. The appeal is in low houses, cobbles, small galleries, and the way roses and public planting soften the centre in summer.
