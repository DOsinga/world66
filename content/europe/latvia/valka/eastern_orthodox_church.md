Valka's Eastern Orthodox Church was built in 2003-05 after the Latvia-Estonia border left local Orthodox parishioners separated from the older church in Valga. The building follows Russian Orthodox forms, an unusual sight in this part of Latvia.

It is a short architectural stop for visitors exploring both sides of the Valka-Valga border town.
