The Orthodox Church on the Valga side of the Valka-Valga twin town was built in 1897-98. Its rounded towers give it a Russian Orthodox profile that stands apart from the Lutheran churches and civic buildings around the border town.

The interior is spacious and has two altars, one dedicated to the Mother of God and one to Isidore of Yuryevsky. It is a worthwhile stop if you are using Valka as a short cross-border wander rather than just passing through.
