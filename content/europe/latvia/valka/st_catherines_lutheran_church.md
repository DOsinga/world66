St. Catherine's Evangelical Lutheran Church is one of Valka's main historic landmarks, standing close to the unusual border-town fabric shared with Valga in Estonia. It gives the Latvian side of the town a clear old-centre marker.

The church is a modest stop, but Valka is a modest destination, and that context matters. Pair it with the local history museum and a walk across the border to understand why this divided town is more interesting than its size suggests.
