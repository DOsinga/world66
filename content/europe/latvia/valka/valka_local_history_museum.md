Valka Local History Museum helps explain one of the Baltics' stranger urban stories: a town divided by an international border, with Latvian Valka on one side and Estonian Valga on the other. Exhibits cover local history, education, culture and the border changes that shaped everyday life.

It is a small museum, but useful if you are here for the cross-border curiosity rather than just passing through. Visit before walking the town line and the shared centre; the streets make more sense with a little history in mind.
