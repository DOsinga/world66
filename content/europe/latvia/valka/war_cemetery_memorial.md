Valka's War Cemetery Memorial is a Soviet-era landscape memorial dedicated to soldiers killed in the Second World War. Its granite wall carries hundreds of names, and the design has the heavy, formal character common to Baltic memorial complexes of the late Soviet period.

It is a sober stop rather than a beautiful one. The site is worth including if you are already exploring Valka's border-town history and want to understand how the war is marked here.
