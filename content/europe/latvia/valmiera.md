Valmiera is the main town of northern Vidzeme, set on the Gauja River between Cesis and the Estonian border. It is not as postcard-pretty as Cesis, but it has a stronger working-town feel: students, theatre, river paths, and a compact old centre shaped by medieval Livonian history.

The old core is small. [**St Simon's Church**](/europe/latvia/valmiera/st_simons_church) anchors it with Gothic brickwork, an old organ, and a tower view over the town. Beside it are the [**Valmiera Castle Ruins**](/europe/latvia/valmiera/valmiera_castle_ruins), the remains of a castle built by the Order of the Sword Brothers.

The [**Valmiera Museum**](/europe/latvia/valmiera/valmiera_museum) fills in the local history in buildings around the castle grounds, while the [**Valmiera Drama Theatre**](/europe/latvia/valmiera/valmiera_drama_theatre) gives the town more cultural weight than its size suggests. Add a walk along the Gauja and Valmiera becomes a worthwhile stop, especially if you are already exploring the national park towns of Vidzeme.
