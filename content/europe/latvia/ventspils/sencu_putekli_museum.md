Senču putekļi Museum is a small ethnographic collection connected with the Latvian writer Herberts Dorbe. It preserves local objects and domestic material that give a modest, personal view of Ventspils-area life.

This is a quiet specialist stop rather than a headline attraction, but it can suit visitors looking for local history away from the waterfront.
