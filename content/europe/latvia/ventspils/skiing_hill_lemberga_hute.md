Lemberga hute is Ventspils' playful artificial hill, used for skiing and tubing in winter and as a viewpoint and activity area in warmer months. In a flat coastal city, even a made hill becomes a landmark.

It is best for families or travellers who want an active break from the beach, harbour, and old town. Conditions and activities are seasonal, so check what is operating before you make the trip.
