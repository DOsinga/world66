Sveta Krusta Roman Catholic Church is a modest but worthwhile stop in Ventspils, with a restrained classical look and Corinthian columns rather than the tall, showy tower many visitors expect from Baltic churches.

It is not the city's main landmark, but it adds another layer to a walk between the old town, the castle, and the Venta waterfront.
