Ventspils House of Crafts occupies an old school building from 1763 in the historic centre. Inside are craft displays and workshops covering weaving, ceramics, and other local traditions, plus a recreated 19th-century classroom.

It is a small stop rather than a major museum, but it adds useful texture to a walk through the old town and gives a hands-on look at Latvian craft culture.
