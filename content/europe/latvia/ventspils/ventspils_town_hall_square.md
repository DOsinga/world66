Ventspils Town Hall Square is a compact civic space where old and new Ventspils sit side by side. The early-18th-century Lutheran church anchors one edge, while the modern library, Digital Centre, and Writers' and Translators' House show how the city has reused its historic centre for cultural life rather than leaving it as a museum piece.
