---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

Ventspils is at its most pleasant from **June to August**, when the daylight is long (sunset after 22:00 in mid-June), the Baltic warms enough for swimming, and the outdoor cafe-and-beach life is in full swing. The town's biggest annual event, the **Ventspils City Festival**, takes over the first weekend of August with concerts, parades, and fireworks.

**May and September** are quieter, cool but pleasant, and good months for cycling without the summer crowds. **Winter** is dark and cold (-5 to -15°C from December to February) and most of the beach-related infrastructure shuts down — but the castle, the museums, and the aquapark are open year-round.
