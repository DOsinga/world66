The Sareis chairlift climbs from the upper end of Malbun to the Sareiserjoch at 2,000 metres in about ten minutes. The Bergrestaurant Sareis at the top has a south-facing terrace with a 360-degree view: across to the Austrian Vorarlberg, west into Switzerland, and along the spine of the Rätikon towards Bad Ragaz.

In winter the chairlift serves the Sareis ski slopes — the longest and most rewarding runs in the resort, suited to intermediates and above. In summer it is the starting point for hikes along the Liechtenstein-Austria border ridge — including the popular three-hour walk to the Augstenberg summit.
