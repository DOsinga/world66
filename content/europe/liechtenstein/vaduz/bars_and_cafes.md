---
title: "Bars and Cafes"
type: section
---

Vaduz is small and quiet after dark. The café and bar scene clusters along Städtle and the lanes immediately off it, with a handful of options open until midnight on weekends. The Café Wolf on Städtle is the daytime classic for coffee and cake; for evening drinks, a few of the larger hotels — and Restaurant Engel — keep a bar going. Don't expect nightlife in the urban sense; this is a place to have a glass of local Pinot Noir on a terrace and call it a night.
