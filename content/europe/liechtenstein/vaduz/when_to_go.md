---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

Vaduz is a year-round destination, but each season has its own logic. Late spring and early summer (May to June) are quietest, with mild Alpine weather, long days, and the pedestrian zone in full bloom. July and August bring most of the visitors and the warmest weather (often above 25°C), as well as the highlight of the calendar — the national holiday on 15 August, when the Prince opens the castle gardens for a public reception.

Autumn (September to October) is excellent for walking, with the vineyards turning gold and the alpine pastures of Malbun above town still accessible. Winter brings ski crowds to Malbun and a quieter Vaduz, with the occasional Christmas market on Städtle in December. The Rhine valley gets cold and grey in midwinter; January and February are the months to skip unless you are heading uphill to ski.
