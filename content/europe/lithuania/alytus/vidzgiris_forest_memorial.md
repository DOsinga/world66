The Vidzgiris Forest, just west of Alytus city centre, is the site of one of Lithuania's most significant Holocaust mass graves. Thousands of Jewish residents of Alytus were killed here during the German occupation in 1941. A memorial marks the site, and the Alytus local museum addresses this history directly.

This is not a place to visit lightly, but it is a place worth visiting. Understanding what happened in these forests — repeated across hundreds of Lithuanian towns and villages — is essential context for understanding the 20th-century history of the Baltic states. The forest itself is peaceful; the contrast between the setting and its history is part of what makes the memorial so affecting.
