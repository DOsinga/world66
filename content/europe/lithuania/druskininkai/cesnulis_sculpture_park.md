Česnulis Sculpture Park is a personal, wood-carved world on the edge of Druskininkai, created by Lithuanian sculptor Antanas Česnulis. Figures, folk motifs, towers, and playful details appear among the trees, giving the place a handmade quality that fits the region's forested setting.

It is a good antidote to the town's spa infrastructure. Come for a slow wander rather than a formal gallery visit, and give yourself time to notice the smaller carvings as well as the larger set pieces.
