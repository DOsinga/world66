The M. K. Čiurlionis Memorial Museum is a small museum in the house where the Lithuanian composer and painter spent his childhood summers, offering a window into the life of one of Lithuania's most celebrated artists. Čiurlionis is best known internationally for his "sonata paintings" — abstract, highly atmospheric works with titles like "Sonata of the Sun" and "Sonata of the Sea" — which placed him decades ahead of his time in the development of abstract art.

The museum contains personal effects, photographs, reproductions of his paintings, and recordings of his music, allowing visitors to engage with both sides of his extraordinary dual gift. The house itself is modest, a late 19th-century wooden building typical of the region, and the combination of intimate domestic space and the scale of the artist's ambition makes it a moving visit. Čiurlionis is revered in Lithuania; this is the town where he began to develop his aesthetic ideas, surrounded by the pine forests and the Nemunas river that recur in his paintings.
