Snow Arena is an indoor ski facility a short distance from Druskininkai town centre, offering a 440-metre ski run and snowboarding facilities in a climate-controlled hall. Open year-round, it is particularly popular in the warmer months when skiing is otherwise impossible, and it draws a steady stream of Lithuanian families and beginners who have never skied outdoors.

The main run has a vertical drop of about 45 metres — modest by Alpine standards, but perfectly serviceable for learning or for a couple of hours of runs. The facility rents all necessary equipment, offers lessons for beginners, and has a snowboarding half-pipe. Adjacent to the Snow Arena is the entrance to Aqua Park, and the combination of skiing and water slides makes for a popular full-day family programme in Druskininkai.
