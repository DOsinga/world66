Druskininkai Town Museum is a compact local museum in an early 20th-century neoclassical villa. Its displays cover town life, resort history, and the development of Druskininkai as a spa destination.

It is a modest but useful stop near the centre, especially if you want context before exploring the mineral springs, parks, and older villas around town. The building also hosts occasional concerts and cultural events.
