---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

Druskininkai works in all seasons, which is part of its appeal. Summer (June to August) is the main season for river swimming, forest walks, and outdoor activities — the town is at its busiest and the atmosphere is at its most holiday-like. Spring and autumn are excellent for walking in the Dzūkija National Park, with autumn mushroom season (September to October) being a particularly Lithuanian reason to visit.

Winter is quieter and colder, but the Snow Arena and Aqua Park stay open, the spa facilities are warm, and the town has a peaceful, slightly melancholy character that suits its 19th-century therapeutic origins. Prices in winter are significantly lower than in summer.
