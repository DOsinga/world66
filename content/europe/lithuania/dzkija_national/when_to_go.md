---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

The park is most visited in summer (June–August) for canoeing and hiking. Late spring (May) and early autumn (September) are arguably better — the rivers are higher, the insects less aggressive, and the forest light more interesting. Autumn is the prime mushroom season, when the Dzūkijan tradition of forest foraging is most visible. Winter brings snow to the forest and a stark beauty, but most canoe rentals and visitor facilities close. Spring flooding can make some riverside trails impassable until May.
