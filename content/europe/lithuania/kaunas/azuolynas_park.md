Azuolynas Park is Kaunas at its most relaxed: a broad stand of old oaks, paths, and open lawns east of the centre. It is not a single monument, but it is one of the city's best places to feel how quickly Kaunas opens into green space.

Come for a walk rather than a checklist sight. The park works well with nearby cultural stops and gives families, runners, and tired museum-goers a calmer hour away from the traffic of Laisves aleja.
