One of the better craft beer bars in Kaunas's Old Town, Iodas has a rotating selection of Lithuanian craft ales alongside familiar Baltic and European options. The interior is warm and low-lit, with exposed brick and wooden fittings — the kind of bar that works equally well for an afternoon beer or a late evening. Prices are low by Western European standards.

Lithuanian craft brewing has developed quickly over the last decade and the quality is genuinely interesting, with farmhouse ales and dark lagers getting particular attention from local producers. The staff here can usually recommend something off-the-beaten-track from the current list.
