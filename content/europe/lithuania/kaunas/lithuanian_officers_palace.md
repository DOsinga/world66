The Lithuanian Officers' Palace is one of Kaunas's grand interwar buildings and part of the city's UNESCO-listed modernist architecture. Built in 1937, it was designed as a prestigious military club, with a monumental facade and the granite sculpture *Three Giants* above the entrance.

Inside are ceremonial halls with chandeliers, stained glass, paintings, and period detail. It now works mainly as an events venue, but the exterior alone makes it an easy architectural stop near other central Kaunas sights.
