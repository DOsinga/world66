The Janina Monkutė-Marks Museum is a contemporary art gallery in a converted farmhouse on the edge of Kedainiai. It is dedicated to Janina Monkutė-Marks, a Lithuanian-born artist who spent much of her career in Chicago, and gives the town a small but serious art stop beyond its old town and multicultural heritage sites.
