St Joseph's Church is a handsome wooden church on Radvilų Street, originally tied to a Jesuit mission house. Its history has been less peaceful than its exterior suggests: it was used as a barracks under the Russian Empire and as a store during the Soviet period.

Today it is again a church, and it gives Kėdainiai's old centre a warm timber counterpoint to the brick merchant houses and stone religious buildings nearby.
