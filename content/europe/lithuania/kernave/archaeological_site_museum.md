The Archaeological Site Museum gives Kernave its missing detail. The mounds and valley are impressive outdoors, but the museum explains the long occupation of the site, the medieval craft town, and the finds that turned this quiet corner of Lithuania into a UNESCO World Heritage landscape.

It is a sensible first or last stop. Go before the mounds if you want context; go after if you prefer to walk the site first and then put names and dates to what you have seen.
