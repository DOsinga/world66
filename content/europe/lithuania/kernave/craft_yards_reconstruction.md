The reconstructed craft yards recreate a small fragment of the 13th- and 14th-century town that once stood in the Pajauta valley. They are not ancient buildings, but they are based on archaeological work and help make the empty valley feel inhabited again.

This is especially useful if you are travelling with children or if earthworks alone leave you cold. Seeing fences, work spaces, and simple timber structures gives the site a human scale that the hillforts, by themselves, can hide.
