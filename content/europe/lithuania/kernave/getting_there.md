---
title: Getting There
type: section
---

Kernave is about 35 kilometres northwest of [Vilnius](/europe/lithuania/vilnius). Driving is the easiest option and takes roughly 45-60 minutes, depending on traffic leaving the capital.

Public transport is possible but slower. Buses and minibuses from Vilnius serve the area irregularly, so check the same-day timetable before committing to a car-free trip. The archaeological reserve is walkable once you arrive in the village.
