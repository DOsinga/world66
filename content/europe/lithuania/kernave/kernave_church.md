Kernave Church, formally dedicated to Our Lady of the Scapular, stands in the village above the archaeological reserve. Its present building dates from the early 20th century, long after the medieval town in the valley had disappeared.

It is not the reason to travel to Kernave, but it is worth a short look while moving between the village and the mounds. The churchyard also gives a good sense of how small modern Kernave is compared with the historical weight of the landscape below it.
