The Kernave Mounds are the heart of the archaeological reserve: five grassy hillforts above the Neris valley, arranged close enough that you can understand them as a defensive landscape rather than isolated viewpoints. Their shapes are simple, but the setting is strong, especially when the valley below is full of mist or late-afternoon light.

Take time to climb more than one mound. From above, the site stops feeling like a ruin and starts to feel like a lost town plan, with the river, valley floor, and village all explaining why people settled here for so long.
