The Alksnyne Bunkers are a group of overgrown World War II fortifications on the Curonian Spit, once part of the Schweinsrücken artillery battery. They are among the more substantial military remains in the area.

The site is rough rather than museum-like, so it is best for visitors already exploring the spit and interested in wartime history.
