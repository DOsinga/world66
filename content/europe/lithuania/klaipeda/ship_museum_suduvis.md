Ship Museum Suduvis is a retired minesweeper moored as a museum in Klaipeda. Built for West Germany in the 1950s, the vessel later served Lithuania before being opened to visitors after retirement.

It is a compact maritime stop rather than a large museum, but boarding a real naval vessel gives it a different feel from Klaipeda's land-based history exhibits.
