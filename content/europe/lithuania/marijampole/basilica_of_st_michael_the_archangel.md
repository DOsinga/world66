The Basilica of St Michael the Archangel is the town's main historic anchor. The Marian Fathers were central to the settlement that became Marijampolė, and the church still gives the centre a sense of depth that the newer streets do not.

Inside, look for the shrine of Blessed Jurgis Matulaitis, a bishop and Marian reformer whose remains were reburied here in 1934.
