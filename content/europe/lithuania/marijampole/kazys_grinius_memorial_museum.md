Kazys Grinius lived in this modest house before becoming Lithuania's prime minister and, briefly, president. His six months as president ended in the 1926 coup, which gives the museum more bite than its small size suggests.

It is a good stop if you are trying to understand why Marijampolė mattered in the Lithuanian national revival rather than just passing through another regional centre.
