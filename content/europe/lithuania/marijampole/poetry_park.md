Poetry Park is the green pause in the middle of Marijampolė. It was laid out where the Šešupė River and the Javonis creek meet, with small bridges, fountains, benches, and public sculpture.

It is not a major sight by itself, but it makes the street-art walk more pleasant and gives the city centre some needed breathing room.
