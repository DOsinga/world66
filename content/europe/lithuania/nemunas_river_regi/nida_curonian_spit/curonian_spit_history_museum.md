The Curonian Spit History Museum in Nida helps explain the communities that lived on this narrow sand landscape: fishing, boats, household life, and the practical adaptations needed for life between lagoon and sea. It adds human context to a place often visited mainly for dunes and views.

It is a modest museum, but useful on a slower Curonian Spit itinerary. Pair it with the ethnographic cemetery, Thomas Mann House, or a lagoon-side walk.
