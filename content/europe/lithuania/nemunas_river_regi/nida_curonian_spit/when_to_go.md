---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

July and August are the peak — warm days (20–25°C), long evenings, festival nights, and crowds. Book accommodation weeks ahead. June and September are quieter, cooler, and to many travellers more pleasant: the light is longer in June, the colours warmer in September.

Out of season (October to April), most restaurants and many guesthouses close. The spit is beautiful in winter snow but transport thins out drastically and the cold over the open dunes is severe.
