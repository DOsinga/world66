The Aukstaitija Narrow Gauge Railway, often called Siaurukas, is one of Panevezys's most distinctive heritage attractions. Its narrow tracks and vintage railway culture preserve a transport world that once linked smaller towns and rural areas across northern Lithuania.

Check schedules carefully, because special rides and events matter more than ordinary daily service. Even so, the railway gives Panevezys a stronger travel hook than many visitors expect, especially for families, rail enthusiasts, and anyone curious about Lithuania beyond Vilnius and Kaunas.
