---
title: Eating Out
type: section
---

Panevėžys has a decent provincial restaurant scene without any particular pretension. The staples are Lithuanian: cepelinai (potato dumplings filled with meat or curd), cold cuts and pickles, dark rye bread, and hearty meat mains. A cluster of cafes and small restaurants around Laisvės Square covers most needs; prices are modest and portions are generous.
