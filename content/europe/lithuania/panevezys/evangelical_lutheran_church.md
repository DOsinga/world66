Panevėžys' Evangelical Lutheran Church is a modest wooden church completed in 1885. Its pale Gothic form looks older than it is, and it stands out from the larger Catholic churches that dominate much of the city's religious architecture.

It is a short stop rather than a major sight, but worthwhile for visitors interested in Lithuania's smaller Protestant communities and traditional wooden church design.
