Panevezys Cathedral is the city's main Catholic church and one of its clearest architectural landmarks. Built in the early 20th century, it mixes a red-brick exterior with a tall, confident silhouette that stands out in a city better known for theatres, parks, and regional museums.

The cathedral is worth a short stop while walking between central Panevezys sights. Step inside if services are not in progress, but give the place the respect due to an active parish church rather than treating it as a museum.
