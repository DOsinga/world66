Senvage is Panevezys old river bend park, a central green space where water, paths, sculpture, and city life meet. It gives the city a more relaxed face than its squares and cultural institutions.

Travellers should treat it as a pleasant orientation walk rather than a major attraction. It is especially useful in good weather, when locals are out strolling and the riverfront helps make sense of the city centre.
