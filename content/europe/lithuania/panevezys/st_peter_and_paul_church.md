St Peter and Paul Church is one of Panevėžys' main Catholic landmarks, with twin towers and a calm interior that speaks to the city's religious life beyond its theatres and civic squares. It is not an ornate blockbuster, but it is a visible piece of the local skyline.

The church is best visited as part of a walk through central Panevėžys. Step in quietly if open, especially outside Mass times, and use it as a reference point for exploring the surrounding streets.
