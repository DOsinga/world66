---
title: Street Murals
type: poi
---

Panevėžys has invested in public art more than most Lithuanian cities, and the result is a surprising concentration of large-scale murals across the city centre. The range is broad — figurative portraits, abstract compositions, pieces tied to local history and Lithuanian folklore — and the quality is consistently high. A self-guided walk connecting the main works takes a couple of hours and is the best way to see parts of the centre that visitors might otherwise skip.
