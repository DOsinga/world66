---
title: When to Go
type: section
---

Summer (June–August) is the most pleasant time to visit: outdoor seating opens up, festivals and events use the public squares, and the long northern evenings give the centre a relaxed energy. The weather is warm rather than hot and the flat landscape around the city is green and open.

The theatre season runs September to May, which is the primary reason to plan a visit around a specific date. The Miltinis Drama Theatre's programme is the strongest cultural draw the city has, and visiting during the season means you can attend a performance.

Winters are cold and dark — Panevėžys is far enough north that January days are short — but the city functions normally and prices are lower. The cathedral and indoor eating options remain just as good.
