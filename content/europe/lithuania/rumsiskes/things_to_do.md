title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Rumsiskes' main draw is its [Lithuanian Open-Air Museum](/europe/lithuania/rumsiskes/lithuanian_open_air_museum), one of the largest ethnographic museums in the Baltic states. Plan on at least two to three hours to walk the grounds properly. The [Kaunas Reservoir](/europe/lithuania/rumsiskes/kaunas_reservoir) that defines the landscape is also worth a look — Lithuania's largest artificial lake, with beaches and paths along the shore.
