---
title: "Eating Out"
type: section
---

Šiauliai is not a destination for fine dining, but the centre has a respectable spread of inexpensive Lithuanian restaurants serving the national staples — cold beetroot soup (**šaltibarščiai**), potato dumplings stuffed with meat (**cepelinai**), and the deep-fried bread sticks (**kepta duona**) eaten with garlic. A handful of bistros and pizzerias cover the rest of the city's eating-out needs.
