---
title: "Getting Around"
type: section
---

Central Šiauliai is small and easily walked end to end in twenty minutes. The Vilniaus pedestrian street stitches together the cathedral, the main museums, and Sundial Square. For the Hill of Crosses, the dedicated tourist bus from the central station is the simplest option; otherwise a taxi from the centre costs around 12 to 15 euro one way. The Bolt and Uber apps work in the city.
