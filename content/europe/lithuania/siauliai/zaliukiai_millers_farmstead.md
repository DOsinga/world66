Zaliukiai Miller's Farmstead is a small open-air museum built around a restored windmill and rural homestead on the edge of Siauliai. It gives a practical look at milling, farm work and domestic life in this part of Lithuania, with a scale that feels approachable rather than institutional.

It is a useful companion to Siauliai's bigger urban sights and to the Hill of Crosses outside town. The appeal is in the textures: timber buildings, milling equipment, old household objects and the sense of how closely the city was tied to the surrounding countryside.
