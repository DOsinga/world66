The bear is the old symbol of Samogitia, and Telšiai has turned it into a small civic treasure hunt. Bears appear as bronze figures, plaques, clock details, and street-corner jokes across the centre.

None is worth crossing town for on its own. Together they give a walk through Telšiai a local rhythm, especially for travellers who like places that wear their identity in small public details rather than big monuments.
