Jonušo Museum is an eccentric small museum created by jeweller and artist A. Jonušas. Its Japanese-style pagoda and collection of Asian objects make it an unexpected find in Telšiai.

It is not a major museum, but it is memorable because it is so personal and odd. Pair it with the Samogitian museums nearby rather than making a special trip for it alone.
