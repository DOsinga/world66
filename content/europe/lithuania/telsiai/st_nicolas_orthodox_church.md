St Nicolas Orthodox Church is a small cubist Orthodox church in Telšiai, built after the Orthodox community was removed from the former Church of the Assumption. Its box-like form, capped by a small onion dome, makes it one of the town's more unusual religious buildings.

The church preserves altar furnishings, icons, and other sacred items transferred from the earlier Orthodox church. It is a short stop, but a worthwhile one if you are tracing Telšiai's mix of Catholic, Jewish, and Orthodox heritage.
