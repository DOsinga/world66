Telšiai Cathedral stands on one of the town's central hills, beside the former Bernardine monastery and seminary buildings. The church is not grand in the Vilnius sense, but its raised position, twin-level interior, and diocesan history make it one of the defining places in town.

Come for the setting as much as the architecture. From the cathedral area you can read Telšiai's shape: the old streets falling toward Lake Mastis, the seminary complex around the church, and the compact centre below.
