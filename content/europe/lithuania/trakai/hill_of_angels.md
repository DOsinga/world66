The Hill of Angels is a contemporary open-air devotional landscape near Trakai, filled with carved wooden angels, crosses, and symbolic figures. It is not ancient like the castle, but it speaks clearly to Lithuania's living woodcarving and roadside-cross traditions.

The site works best as a quiet extra stop while exploring the lakes around Trakai. Wander slowly, read the themes attached to the figures, and do not expect polish; its charm is local, handmade, and a little eccentric.
