---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

Trakai is best in late spring through early autumn (May–September), when the lakes are swimmable, the national park is green, and the castle is most animated. July and August bring the most visitors — the castle gets crowded on summer weekends, and the boat rental queues can be long by midday. Come on a weekday or arrive early.

Winter has its own appeal. The castle in snow is particularly photogenic, the crowds are negligible, and the town has a quiet, local character that disappears in peak season. When the lake freezes (usually December through February), ice fishing becomes the local pastime. The cold is real — temperatures regularly drop to -15°C — so dress accordingly.
