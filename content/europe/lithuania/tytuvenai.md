Tytuvėnai is a small town in Samogitia whose scale hides a surprisingly strong travel reason. The centre is the [church and Bernardine monastery ensemble](/europe/lithuania/tytuvenai/tytuvenai_monastery), one of Lithuania's finest provincial Baroque religious complexes, with a courtyard made for slow wandering.

The monastery would be thin on its own, but Tytuvėnai also sits inside a regional park of lakes, forest, wetlands, and low glacial hills. Walk to [Bridvaišis Lake](/europe/lithuania/tytuvenai/bridvaisis_lake) in warm weather, or climb the [observation tower](/europe/lithuania/tytuvenai/tytuvenai_observation_tower) for a quick read of the landscape.

This is not a place to rush across Lithuania for. It is, however, a good spare-day stop from Šiauliai or Kaunas if you want a quiet mix of sacred architecture and countryside.
