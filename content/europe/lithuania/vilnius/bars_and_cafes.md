---
title: Bars and Cafes
type: section
---

Vilnius has a lively bar and cafe scene that has matured considerably since independence. The old town, Gedimino Avenue, and the Užupis neighbourhood have the highest density of options. Lukiškės Prison, converted into a cultural space after closing in 2019, is an unexpectedly atmospheric setting for an evening drink.

Lithuanian beer is worth drinking. The major national brands — **Švyturys** (from Klaipėda) and **Utenos** (from Utena) — are decent lagers; **Gubernija** and **Kalnapilis** are also widely available. The craft beer scene has expanded sharply since 2010: breweries like **Dundulis**, **Sakiškių**, and **Apynys** produce interesting ales and stouts that turn up in specialist bars.

**Midus** (Lithuanian mead) and **gira** (a fermented rye bread drink, similar to kvass) are worth trying as local alternatives. **Starka** is the traditional Lithuanian spirit — a dark rye vodka aged in oak barrels that predates the vodka tradition by centuries.

The [Užupio Kavinė](/europe/lithuania/vilnius/uzupis_cafe) on the Vilnia riverbank is the most atmospheric coffee spot in the city. [Craft Pub Vilnius](/europe/lithuania/vilnius/craft_beer_pub) on Stiklių Street has the best Lithuanian craft beer selection in the old town.
