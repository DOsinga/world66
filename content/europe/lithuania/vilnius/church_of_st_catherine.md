The Church of St Catherine is an 18th-century Baroque church in Vilnius Old Town, built as part of a Benedictine nunnery and dedicated to Saint Catherine of Alexandria. Its facade and stucco decoration make it one of the more graceful churches in the city center.

Many artworks were lost during World War II or moved elsewhere, but the building remains a worthwhile architectural stop while walking between Old Town sights.
