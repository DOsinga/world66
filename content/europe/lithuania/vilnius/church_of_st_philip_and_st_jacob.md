The Church of St Philip and St Jacob is a red-and-white Baroque Dominican church near Lukiskes Square. Completed in the early 18th century, it stands out for its twin towers and for the revered icon of the Virgin Mary Hodegetria inside.

It is a worthwhile stop if you are already exploring New Town or the museums around the square. The church is quieter than Vilnius' Old Town showpieces, which is part of its appeal.
