The Church of St. Theresa stands just north of the Gate of Dawn, close to one of Vilnius' most important pilgrimage sites. Completed in 1650, it is a Roman Catholic church with a richly decorated Baroque interior.

Its location makes it easy to visit while walking the southern edge of the Old Town. The church remains active, so step in respectfully and avoid visiting during services unless you plan to attend.
