The most popular day trip from Vilnius is Trakai, about 30 km west — a medieval island castle sitting in a lake, reachable by frequent bus from the Vilnius bus station in about 45 minutes. Europos Parkas, the open-air sculpture museum at the geographic centre of Europe, is 12 km north of the city and best reached by taxi or car. The Paneriai (Ponary) massacre site, 10 km southwest, is reachable by local train.
