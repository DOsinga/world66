Holy Trinity Church is part of a Ukrainian Greek Catholic monastery in Vilnius Old Town. Most of the present complex dates from the early 17th century, adding another layer to the dense mix of Catholic, Orthodox, Jewish, and Uniate heritage around the Gates of Dawn.

It is a quiet stop while walking the southern part of the Old Town, especially if you are visiting the nearby churches and market streets rather than only the headline sights.
