Markučiai Park stretches into the green eastern edge of Vilnius, linking wooded paths, slopes, and old burial mounds near the Markučiai Manor Museum. It feels more local and less polished than the city's central parks.

Come for a walk if you are already exploring this side of Vilnius or want a soft route toward the Pavilniai greenbelt.
