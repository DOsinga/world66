The Orthodox Church of the Holy Spirit is one of Vilnius Old Town's main Orthodox churches, rebuilt in Baroque and Rococo style in the 18th century. It belongs to the Monastery of the Holy Spirit and remains an active place of worship.

The church is close to the Gates of Dawn, so it is easy to include on an Old Town walk. Step inside for the iconostasis and a different religious layer of Vilnius from the Catholic churches nearby.
