Saulės Cemetery sits in Antakalnis, away from Vilnius's usual old-town circuit, and feels quieter for it. Its history reaches back to the old parish cemetery of St. Peter and St. Paul, with burials associated with Lithuanian and Polish families, scholars, clergy, and civic figures.

This is a place for slow wandering rather than a checklist stop. The family chapels and weathered stones give a layered sense of the city east of the centre, where Vilnius's Catholic, Polish, Lithuanian, and Soviet-era histories overlap without much ceremony.
