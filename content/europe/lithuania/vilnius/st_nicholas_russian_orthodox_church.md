St Nicholas Russian Orthodox Church is a small Old Town church near Užupis. It is easy to confuse with the Roman Catholic Church of St. Nicholas elsewhere in the centre, but this one belongs to Vilnius' Orthodox heritage.

The church is a worthwhile short stop while walking the eastern side of the Old Town, especially for visitors tracing the city's mix of Catholic, Orthodox, Jewish, and secular landmarks.
