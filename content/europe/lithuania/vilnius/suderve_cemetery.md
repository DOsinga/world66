Suderve Cemetery is one of Vilnius's main Jewish cemeteries, a sober place that connects the city's present-day Jewish community with the much larger world that existed here before the Second World War. It is a cemetery rather than a curated memorial, so its importance is quiet and cumulative.

Visitors interested in Jewish Vilnius will find it most meaningful after seeing the old ghetto streets, the Gaon-related sites, and the genocide memorials. Keep the visit respectful and unhurried; the value is in names, dates, and continuity.
