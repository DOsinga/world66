---
title: Things to Do
type: section
---

Vilnius has one of the largest surviving medieval old towns in Europe, a UNESCO World Heritage Site since 1994. Over 40 churches in styles from Gothic to Baroque to Neoclassical fill the skyline with spires. Beyond the old town, the bohemian Užupis district, Jewish history memorials, and several excellent museums reward time spent beyond the obvious itinerary.
