The Vilna Gaon Jewish State Museum is essential for understanding a city once known as the Jerusalem of the North. Its branches and exhibitions cover Jewish religious life, art, daily culture, and the destruction of Lithuanian Jewry during the Holocaust.

Do not treat it as a quick box to tick. Vilnius old town is full of absences as well as monuments, and the museum gives those empty spaces names, lives, and context.
