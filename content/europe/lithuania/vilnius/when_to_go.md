---
title: When to Go
type: section
---

**Spring and summer (May–September)** is the best time to visit. June and July are the peak of summer: average temperatures around 20°C, and the long northern evenings — it stays light until 10pm or later in midsummer — make outdoor life very pleasant. The old town fills with visitors in summer but not unpleasantly so; it's a large old town and absorbs crowds well.

**Autumn (October–November)** brings cooler temperatures and the changing colour of the trees in Vingis Park and the Vilnia valley. October is still pleasant for walking; November turns grey and wet.

**Winter (December–February)** is cold, dark, and occasionally beautiful. The old town looks its best under snow, and the Christmas market in Cathedral Square and Vokiečių Street runs through December. Temperatures can drop to -15°C or below in January. Most tourist sites remain open through winter.

**Events worth timing a trip around:**

- **Kaziukas Fair** (first weekend of March, around St. Casimir's Day, March 4) — a traditional folk crafts market filling the old town streets with amber, linen, woven goods, and food. One of the most charming folk events in the Baltics.
- **Vilnius Festival** (June) — classical music concerts at Cathedral Square, the Congress Concert Hall, and other venues across the city.
- **Vilnius Marathon** (September) — the main annual road race, using a scenic course through the old town and river parks.
- **Užupis Independence Day** (April 1) — the self-declared republic celebrates its independence with street parties and the symbolic border post issuing entry stamps.
