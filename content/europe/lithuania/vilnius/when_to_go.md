---
title: When to Go
type: section
---

May through September is the best time to visit, with June and July the peak of summer. Average temperatures in July are around 20°C, and the long northern evenings (it stays light until 10pm or later) make outdoor life very pleasant. The old town fills with visitors in midsummer but not unpleasantly so.

Early March sees the Kaziukas Fair, a traditional folk crafts market filling the old town streets on the feast of St. Casimir — one of the livelier winter events. The Vilnius Festival in June brings classical music to venues across the city, including Cathedral Square and the Congress Concert Hall. Winter is cold and dark but the old town is beautiful under snow, and Christmas markets run through December.
