Diekirch is a compact town on the Sauer in northern Luxembourg, useful as a transport stop for [Vianden](/europe/luxembourg/vianden) and better than that sounds. It has a walkable centre, a riverside park, a cluster of museums, and enough old-town texture to reward a slow afternoon.

The town's strongest sight is the [National Museum of Military History](/europe/luxembourg/diekirch/national_museum_of_military_history), one of Luxembourg's major Second World War museums. Nearby, the [Museum of the History of Diekirch](/europe/luxembourg/diekirch/museum_of_the_history_of_diekirch) digs further back into local Roman and medieval layers, while [Saint Laurent](/europe/luxembourg/diekirch/saint_laurent) keeps the old centre anchored.

Diekirch also has a lighter side. The town is associated with beer, donkeys, carnival, and summer fairs, which is why the [Donkey Fountain](/europe/luxembourg/diekirch/donkey_fountain) feels less random than it first appears. If you are passing through on the way to Vianden, do not just change buses and leave.

Most travellers will see Diekirch as a half-day stop. Stay longer if you are interested in Battle of the Bulge history or want a quieter base in the Nordstad area.
