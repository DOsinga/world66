Diekirch is one of the Luxembourg names most associated with beer, and the Brewery History Museum leans into that identity. The collection looks at the town's brewing tradition, including advertising and brewery material from more than a century.

This is a small, specialist stop rather than a full beer pilgrimage. Pair it with lunch or a drink in town and it makes more sense than treating it as a standalone reason to travel north.
