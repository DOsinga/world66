---
title: Getting There
type: section
---

Diekirch is connected by train to Ettelbruck, where frequent services continue to [Luxembourg City](/europe/luxembourg/luxembourgcity). The rail branch is short, simple, and useful if you are travelling without a car.

For [Vianden](/europe/luxembourg/vianden), Diekirch is one of the practical transfer points. Buses connect the station with Vianden in about half an hour, making Diekirch a natural pause on the route.
