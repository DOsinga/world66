The National Conservatory for Historic Vehicles is a compact museum for old cars and transport enthusiasts. It shares Diekirch's museum-heavy eastern side with the brewery museum, making the two easy to combine.

The appeal is straightforward: restored vehicles, engineering nostalgia, and a break from castles and war history. It is most rewarding if you already like classic cars, but it adds useful variety to a Diekirch stop.
