Saint Laurent is the main church in Diekirch's old centre. The site is tied to very early Christian and Roman-era remains, and the crypt is linked with the interpretation offered by the town history museum.

It is not a grand cathedral stop, but it matters locally. Visit it while walking between the pedestrian streets and the museums, and use it as a reminder that Diekirch's story did not begin in 1944.
