---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

**May and June** are the classic months: forests in fresh leaf, lakes warming, and — on the Tuesday after Whitsun — the **Dancing Procession (Springprozession)**, a UNESCO-listed pilgrimage that fills the town with tens of thousands of visitors. Book accommodation months ahead if you want to be there for it.

**July and August** are warm and busy, with the Echternach International Music Festival running through the summer in the basilica.

**September and October** are quieter and the Mullerthal forest is at its best — autumn colour through beech and oak, dry trails, and reasonable temperatures.

**Winter** is closed-feeling: many small businesses close, the Mullerthal trails can be icy, but the basilica is open and the town has a quiet appeal.
