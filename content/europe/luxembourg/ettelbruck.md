Ettelbruck is a small town in central Luxembourg, where the Sauer, Wark, and Alzette rivers meet. Its main role for travellers is practical: it is one of the country's useful rail and bus junctions, with easy onward routes to [Diekirch](/europe/luxembourg/diekirch), [Vianden](/europe/luxembourg/vianden), and the northern hills.

The town itself is friendly rather than spectacular. Its strongest story is the Second World War, especially the liberation of Luxembourg by American forces under General George S. Patton's command. The [General Patton Memorial Museum](/europe/luxembourg/ettelbruck/general_patton_memorial_museum) and nearby memorials give Ettelbruck more substance than its modest centre first suggests.

Use Ettelbruck as a base if train connections matter, or as a short stop between Luxembourg City and the north. It will not outshine Vianden or Echternach, but it fits neatly into a northern Luxembourg itinerary.
