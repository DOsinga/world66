The General Patton Memorial Museum is Ettelbruck's main sight. It covers the town's liberation, the Battle of the Bulge context, and the local memory of General George S. Patton through photographs, uniforms, weapons, and wartime objects.

Diekirch has the larger military museum, but this one is useful if you are already in Ettelbruck or following Patton-related sites through Luxembourg.
