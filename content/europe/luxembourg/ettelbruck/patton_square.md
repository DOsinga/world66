Patton Square is the town's central memory point for the liberation of Ettelbruck in 1944. The memorial and statue are simple, but they explain why this otherwise quiet transport town promotes itself so strongly through the Patton story.

It is a short stop, best combined with the museum rather than treated as a destination on its own.
