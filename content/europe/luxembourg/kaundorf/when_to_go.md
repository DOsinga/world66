---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

The Upper Sûre area is best in summer — June through September — when the lake is warm enough for swimming and kayaking, the hiking trails are at their most accessible, and the days are long. July and August bring some Belgian and Dutch holidaymakers, but it never gets genuinely crowded.

Spring is good for walking — the beech forest is vivid in April and May, and the birdlife along the lake is at its most active. Autumn brings softer colours but wetter weather. Winter in the Éislek is quiet and sometimes harsh; the hills can hold snow for weeks, but the area lacks ski infrastructure.
