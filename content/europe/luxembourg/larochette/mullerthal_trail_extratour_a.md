ExtraTour A is Larochette's best walking route, tying the town into the wider Mullerthal Trail network. The loop runs toward Meysemburg Castle, Nommern, and Medernach, with forest paths, pastureland, and the Nommerleeen rock formations along the way.

You do not need to hike the whole route to get value from it. Even a shorter climb out of Larochette gives views back to the castle and valley.
