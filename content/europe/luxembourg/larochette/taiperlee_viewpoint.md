The Taiperlee viewpoint is one of the small rewards on the local L3 hiking trail. It gives the classic view back over Larochette: castle ruins on the ridge, village below, and wooded Mullerthal slopes closing the valley.

It is worth adding if you are already walking beyond the castle, especially because the trail continues toward Ernzen and Meysembourg.
