The anchor venue of the Rives de Clausen complex, in a converted brewery hall. House-brewed beers on tap (the Mansfeld Pils is the staple), a long indoor bar, and a vast covered terrace facing the Alzette. The crowd is mixed in age until late, when the music gets louder and the late-twenties dominate.

Open every night until at least 1am, later at weekends. Reliable food (burgers and brewery dishes) keeps you fed until closing.
