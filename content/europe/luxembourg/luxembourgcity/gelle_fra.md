The 'Golden Lady' on her tall granite obelisk in Place de la Constitution is Luxembourg's national war memorial, unveiled in 1923 to commemorate the volunteers who fought with the Allies in the First World War. Designed by Claus Cito, she stretches a wreath over the figures of two fallen Luxembourgish soldiers at the base.

The terrace around the monument is one of the city's best viewpoints, looking south over the Pétrusse valley toward the Adolphe Bridge and the Gare district.
