The Philharmonie's restaurant is a serious gastro address, especially on concert nights when the kitchen offers a pre-show menu timed precisely to the curtain. Chef Frédéric Eveilleau cooks French with seasonal local sourcing — game in autumn, river fish in summer, vegetables from the city's market gardeners.

Generous lunch menus draw the EU crowd on weekdays. Reservations needed for any concert evening.
