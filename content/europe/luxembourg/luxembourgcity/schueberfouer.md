Schueberfouer is Luxembourg City's big late-summer funfair, held from late August into early September and rooted in a 14th-century market fair. Expect rides, food stalls, beer, and crowds; it is less a quiet cultural stop than a noisy annual ritual that many Luxembourgers grow up with.
