The obvious day trip from Munshausen is [Clervaux](/europe/luxembourg/clervaux), just 6 kilometres north. The town's 12th-century castle houses Edward Steichen's "The Family of Man" — a landmark photography exhibition and UNESCO Memory of the World — as well as a museum on the 1944 Battle of the Bulge and a collection of models of Luxembourg's fortified castles. The Benedictine Abbey on the hillside above the town is still active and open to visitors.
