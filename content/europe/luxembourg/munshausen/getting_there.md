---
title: Getting There
type: section
---

Munshausen has no rail connection and is not served by regular bus. The practical way to get here is by car, and most visitors arrive as part of a drive through the northern Luxembourg Ardennes. From Luxembourg City the drive north takes about an hour, heading up the N7 and then the N18 through Clervaux.

From [Clervaux](/europe/luxembourg/clervaux), which is the nearest town with a rail station, Munshausen is about 6 kilometres south. There is no reliable taxi service, so without a car you are walking or cycling. The road between the two along the Our valley is pleasant on a bike, though it does involve some climbing out of the valley.

Cyclists arriving on the RAVeL network through Belgium can continue into Luxembourg via the Our valley route, which passes close to Munshausen.
