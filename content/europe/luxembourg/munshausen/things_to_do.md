---
title: Things to Do
type: section
---

Munshausen and its surrounding villages offer quiet Ardennes countryside rather than a packed itinerary. The main draw is the Touristic Domain and its working Ardennes draft horses; beyond that, the valley walking trails are the reason most visitors come. The village church of St. Hubert and the hamlets of Drauffelt and Siebenaler reward slow wandering rather than hurried sightseeing.
