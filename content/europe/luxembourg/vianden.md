Vianden is a small town in northeastern [Luxembourg](/europe/luxembourg), set in a narrow valley of the Our river near the German border. It is dominated by [Vianden Castle](/europe/luxembourg/vianden/vianden_castle), one of the largest fortified castles west of the Rhine, perched on a rocky promontory above the town. The castle dates from the 11th to 14th centuries and has been extensively restored — the interior is open to visitors and the views over the valley are excellent. A [chairlift](/europe/luxembourg/vianden/chairlift) from the town centre takes you to the hilltop above for an even wider panorama.

[Victor Hugo](/europe/luxembourg/vianden/victor_hugo_house) lived in Vianden during his exile from France, and his former house on the main street is now a small museum. The [Our Valley walking trails](/europe/luxembourg/vianden/our_valley_walks) in the forested hills above town are among the best in Luxembourg.

The surrounding countryside is some of the most scenic in Luxembourg. A few kilometres north of town, the [Vianden Pumped Storage Plant](/europe/luxembourg/vianden/vianden_pumped_storage_plant) — one of the largest in Europe — can be visited and is surprisingly interesting. Vianden is 40km northeast of [Luxembourg City](/europe/luxembourg/luxembourgcity) and easy to visit as a day trip.
