Wasserbillig sits at the [confluence of the Sûre and Moselle rivers](/europe/luxembourg/wasserbillig/moselle_sure_confluence), right on the German border in eastern Luxembourg. It is a transit town at heart -- many Germans cross the bridge to fill up on cheaper fuel, and most travellers pass through on their way somewhere else. But the setting in the Moselle valley, with vineyard-covered slopes rising on both sides of the river, gives it more charm than its reputation suggests.

The town is a convenient stop on the [Moselle cycling route](/europe/luxembourg/wasserbillig/moselle_cycling_route), which follows the river between Luxembourg City and Trier in Germany. The riding is flat and easy, the path is well maintained, and there are regular spots to stop for a glass of the local Riesling or Elbling wine. The Aquatower, a distinctive modern water tower with a viewing platform, offers panoramic views over the confluence and the surrounding valley.

Wasserbillig itself has a couple of restaurants and cafes near the river. The [Wasserbillig Aquarium](/europe/luxembourg/wasserbillig/wasserbillig_aquarium) on the riverbank is worth a stop, particularly for families — it focuses on native Moselle and Sûre species rather than tropical fish. The Butterfly Garden (Jardin des Papillons) in nearby [Grevenmacher](/europe/luxembourg/grevenmacher), about five kilometres south along the Moselle, makes for a pleasant short trip. The town is well connected by rail, sitting on the Luxembourg City to Trier line, which makes it easy to reach without a car.
