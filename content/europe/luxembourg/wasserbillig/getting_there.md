---
title: "Getting There"
type: section
---

Wasserbillig is well connected by rail. CFL trains run regularly from Luxembourg City, taking about 40 minutes. The station is a short walk from the riverside and from the German border crossing at Oberbillig.

By car, Wasserbillig is just off the A1/E44 motorway between Luxembourg City and Trier. The German border crossing on the Moselle bridge is open around the clock.
