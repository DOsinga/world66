The French Military Cemetery is part of the sombre First World War geography around Bitola, a city that sat close to the Macedonian Front and was repeatedly shelled during the conflict. Rows of graves recall a chapter that many travellers know less well than the Western Front, but which shaped this borderland deeply.

It pairs naturally with Bitola's other war cemeteries and with a walk through the old consular city, where imperial ambitions once overlapped uneasily. The cemetery is quiet and plain; visit for historical context rather than spectacle, and give it the same respect you would any active memorial ground.
