Gazi Haydar Kadi Mosque is a 16th-century Ottoman mosque in Bitola, designed by the celebrated architect Mimar Sinan. Its clean stonework, dome, and restored interior make it one of the better historic stops around the old bazaar area.

The mosque is most rewarding as part of a walk through Bitola's Ottoman core, close to the Clock Tower, Deboj Hamam, and the city's surviving bazaar streets.
