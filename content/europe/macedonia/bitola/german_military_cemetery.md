The German Military Cemetery is a sober reminder that Bitola was not only an Ottoman and Balkan city but also a front-line landscape during the First World War. The graves connect the town to the Macedonian Front, a theatre that rarely gets the attention of the Western Front but left deep marks around Bitola.

It is a quiet, reflective stop rather than a conventional attraction. Pair it with the Jewish Cemetery, the old bazaar, and Heraclea Lyncestis if you want to understand how many layers of history sit around the city.
