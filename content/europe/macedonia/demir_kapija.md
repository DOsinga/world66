Demir Kapija is a small town in southern [Macedonia](/europe/macedonia), where the Vardar River squeezes through the Iron Gate gorge. The town itself is quiet, but the setting is strong: limestone cliffs, caves, old tracks through the canyon, and vineyards that make this one of the country's better wine stops.

It earns its place as more than a highway pause because the attractions stack well. You can walk into the [Demir Kapija Canyon](/europe/macedonia/demir_kapija/demir_kapija_canyon), look for the remains of [Prosek](/europe/macedonia/demir_kapija/prosek), visit [Bela Voda Cave](/europe/macedonia/demir_kapija/bela_voda_cave), then finish with a tasting above town at [Popova Kula](/europe/macedonia/demir_kapija/popova_kula).

Come with a car if you can. Trains and buses exist, but the best part of Demir Kapija is the landscape just outside the streets, not the town grid.
