Bela Voda Cave lies a short way downstream from town in the same limestone system that shapes the Iron Gate. It is one of the longer caves in North Macedonia, and it adds a darker, quieter stop to a canyon day.

Do not treat it like a show cave unless you have current local advice and the right gear. For most travellers it is best folded into a walk through the canyon area.
