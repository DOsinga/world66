Kičevo (Кичево) is a small town of around 30,000 people in the mountainous west of North Macedonia, sitting at the confluence of the [Treska River](/europe/macedonia/kicevo/treska_river_gorge) and Kičevska rivers at about 620 metres above sea level. Surrounded by the [Bistra](/europe/macedonia/kicevo/bistra_mountain), Suva Gora, and Ilinska mountain ranges, it serves as the administrative centre of the Kičevo Municipality and a regional hub for the surrounding villages.

The town has a mixed Albanian and Macedonian population and a quietly distinctive character shaped by that coexistence. Its Ottoman-era past is visible in the [old čaršija (bazaar district)](/europe/macedonia/kicevo/old_bazaar), and the hills above town are dotted with rural monasteries worth a half-day excursion. The nearest significant monastery, [Sveta Bogorodica at Prečista](/europe/macedonia/kicevo/kicevo_monastery), lies just a few kilometres away and is considered one of the more important religious sites in western Macedonia.

Kičevo is not a major tourist destination, but it is a convenient base for hiking in the Mavrovo and Bistra massifs, and the surrounding landscapes — beech forests, river gorges, and high meadows — reward travellers willing to leave the main roads.
