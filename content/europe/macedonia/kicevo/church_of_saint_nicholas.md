The Church of Saint Nicholas stands in Cer, one of the historically important villages around Kicevo. The church dates from the mid-19th century, with an older iconostasis completed in 1839, and the yard includes a bell tower and village cemetery.

It is a small rural sight, but worthwhile if you are already exploring the villages and monasteries around Kicevo. The setting gives a good sense of the area's Orthodox village heritage.
