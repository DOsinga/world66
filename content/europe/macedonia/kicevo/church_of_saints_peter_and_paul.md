The Church of Saints Peter and Paul is the main church in Kičevo. It was built in 1907 on the foundations of an older church, with a prominent bell tower rising from the front facade.

The church is a useful stop while walking around central Kičevo, especially for visitors interested in the town's Orthodox heritage and early 20th-century religious architecture.
