Mavrovo National Park is the big mountain escape west of Kicevo, spreading over high
ridges, deep valleys, forests, and the artificial Mavrovo Lake. It is one of North
Macedonia's strongest nature destinations, with enough scale for hikers, drivers, and
travelers who simply want a cooler, wilder counterpoint to the towns below.

The park works best as a full day trip or an overnight detour. Roads lead toward lake
villages, old monasteries, viewpoints, and trailheads, but weather changes quickly in
the mountains and winter travel can be slow. Go for scenery rather than a checklist: the
pleasure is in the broad Balkan landscape and the sense that the city has fallen away.
