This small history museum covers western Macedonia in the 19th century, the Ilinden Uprising, and the National Liberation War during World War II. It is housed in a late-19th-century building that was used by partisans, which gives the collection more context than a plain display room would.

Expect weapons, flags, documents, and local material tied to Kičevo's wartime history. It is a useful stop if you want to understand the town beyond the bazaar and monastery.
