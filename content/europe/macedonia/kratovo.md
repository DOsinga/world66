Kratovo is a small town with a setting that does half the work for it. It sits in the bowl of an extinct volcano in northeastern [Macedonia](/europe/macedonia), where steep lanes, stone bridges, and old towers step up the slopes.

The town is compact, but it has a real identity. [Kratovo old town](/europe/macedonia/kratovo/kratovo_old_town) is the main sight, with bridges crossing the gullies and surviving towers from the town's mining and trading past. A short drive away, the [Stone Town of Kuklica](/europe/macedonia/kratovo/stone_town_of_kuklica) adds a strange volcanic landscape of earth pillars, while [Lesnovo Monastery](/europe/macedonia/kratovo/lesnovo_monastery) gives the region one of its best medieval church interiors.

Kratovo is not a polished city break. It is a strong one-day detour for travelers who like old stone towns, rough edges, and places where the landscape explains the architecture.
