The Church of Saint Nicholas stands in Krusevo's old bazaar. Built in 1905 after an earlier church burned following the Ilinden Uprising, it serves as the town's cathedral.

The mostly white exterior has a tall central section, while the interior preserves frescoes dating back to 1884.
