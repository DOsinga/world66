Kruševo Cemetery sits above town near the Ilinden Monument and the Toše Proeski Memorial House. It is best known for the graves of Pitu Guli, killed during the Ilinden Uprising, and singer Toše Proeski.

Come as part of a walk through Kruševo's upper historic quarter rather than as a standalone excursion. The hillside setting also gives a quiet sense of the town's scale and mountain surroundings.
