The Nikola Martinovski Gallery fills the birth house of one of North Macedonia's most important modern painters. Martinovski donated dozens of works to Krusevo, and seeing them here adds a personal layer that a larger city museum would not have.

The house itself is part of the pleasure. It is a fine example of Krusevo domestic architecture, so the visit is as much about the town's old interiors as it is about the paintings.
