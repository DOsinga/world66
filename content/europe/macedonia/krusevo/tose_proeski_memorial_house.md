The Toše Proeski Memorial House honours the singer who became a national figure across North Macedonia and the wider Balkans before his death in 2007. The museum is modern and carefully kept, with personal objects, stage material, and a sense of real local affection rather than celebrity packaging.

It sits close to Makedonium, so the two are easy to combine on the same walk above town.
