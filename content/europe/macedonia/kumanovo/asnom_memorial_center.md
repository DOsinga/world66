The ASNOM Memorial Center commemorates the Anti-fascist Assembly for the National Liberation of Macedonia, whose first session in 1944 helped define modern Macedonian statehood and language.

The memorial stands near the Serbian border and is best combined with other sights around Kumanovo and Kokino. Its colorful mosaic facade is the main visual landmark.
