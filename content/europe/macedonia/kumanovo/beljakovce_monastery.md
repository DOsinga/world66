Beljakovce Monastery is a small rural religious site southeast of Kumanovo, centred on a well-preserved church and a spring that locals traditionally regarded as healing. The monastic quarters are mostly ruined, but the church remains the reason to visit.

The site's appeal is quiet and local: oral history links the church to older foundations, later restoration under Ottoman rule, and renewed monastic life in the early 20th century.
