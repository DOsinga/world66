The Church of Saint George is a medieval Orthodox church northeast of Kumanovo, valued for both its architecture and frescoes. First built in 1071 and rebuilt in the early 14th century under Serbian king Stefan Milutin, it is one of the stronger historic church stops in the Kumanovo area.
