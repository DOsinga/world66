Tatar Sinan Beg Mosque, also called the Eski Mosque, is Kumanovo's Ottoman counterpoint to its Orthodox churches. Built in 1532, it has a single dome, one minaret, and old graves in the courtyard.

It is a compact stop, but it matters because Kumanovo's identity is not one-note. The mosque helps show the city's older Ottoman layer.
