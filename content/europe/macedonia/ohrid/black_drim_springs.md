The Black Drim Springs rise beside the Monastery of Saint Naum at the southern end of Lake Ohrid. Clear spring water feeds the Black Drim River here before it runs through the lake and eventually out toward Struga and Albania.

Small boats take visitors around the spring pools and tiny islands. It pairs naturally with the monastery and is worth leaving time for rather than treating Saint Naum as only a church stop.
