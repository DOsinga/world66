The Church of Saint Erasmus is an early Christian site beside the road between Ohrid and Struga. It combines a cave church with the remains of an ancient basilica, with the oldest parts thought to date from around the 8th century.

It is a quiet stop outside the old town, best for visitors already tracing Ohrid's early Christian sites.
