The Church of Saints Constantine and Elena is a small 15th-century church in Ohrid's Old Town, just southeast of Saint Mary Perivleptos. It is modest from the outside, but the single-nave interior is covered with frescoes, making it a worthwhile stop while walking between the old churches above the lake.
