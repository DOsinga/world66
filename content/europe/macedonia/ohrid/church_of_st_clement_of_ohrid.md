The Church of St Clement of Ohrid is a small 14th-century church hidden in the old town near the Bolnicki churches. It is easy to miss, but it matters locally because tradition says the relics of St Clement were hidden here before being moved to Plaosnik.

Visit it as part of a slow walk through Ohrid's dense cluster of medieval churches rather than as a stand-alone sight.
