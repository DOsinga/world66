The Church of St Nicholas is a 19th-century church in the newer part of Ohrid, a short walk north of the lake. Built in 1863 and expanded over time, it offers a quieter contrast to the better-known churches of the old town.

It is a modest stop, best for visitors with extra time in Ohrid or an interest in the town's living parish churches beyond the main heritage circuit.
