---
title: "Getting There"
type: section
---

Ohrid has a small international airport (OHD) about 9 km north of the centre, with seasonal flights from Vienna, Zurich, Basel, and a handful of other European cities — mostly summer-only. Year-round, the easiest air route is to [Skopje](/europe/macedonia/skopje) (170 km, three hours by road) and onward by bus or rental car. The road from Skopje is mostly motorway through Tetovo and on to Kičevo, with a winding mountain stretch over the Bukovo pass at the end.

Cross-border buses connect Ohrid with Tirana (Albania) and with Sofia (Bulgaria) several times a week. Local taxis serve the airport for a fixed flat fare.
