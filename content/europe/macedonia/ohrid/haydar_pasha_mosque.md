Haydar Pasha Mosque is a small early Ottoman mosque on Ohrid's northwestern side, away from the densest run of lakeside churches. Built in the 15th century, it has a plain pitched roof and a single minaret, giving it a quieter presence than the better-known monuments in the old town.

It is worth a short detour if you are tracing Ohrid's Ottoman layer as well as its Byzantine one. The interest is mostly architectural and historical, so it is best combined with nearby streets rather than treated as a long stop.
