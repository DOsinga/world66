---
title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Ohrid's old town is the main attraction. Walk from the harbour up through the lanes to the [Church of St John at Kaneo](/europe/macedonia/ohrid/church_st_john_kaneo), the [Plaošnik complex](/europe/macedonia/ohrid/plaosnik), and [Samuel's Fortress](/europe/macedonia/ohrid/samuels_fortress) at the top of the hill — half a day at an unhurried pace. The [Church of Saint Sophia](/europe/macedonia/ohrid/church_st_sophia) is essential for its 11th-century frescoes. The [Ancient Theatre](/europe/macedonia/ohrid/ancient_theatre) and the [Robevi family house](/europe/macedonia/ohrid/robevi_house) fill in the Hellenistic and Ottoman layers. Out on the lake itself, the [boat trips to Sveti Naum](/europe/macedonia/ohrid/sveti_naum_boat_trip) are the most enjoyable way to see the southern shore.
