Uzunov House is the birthplace of Hristo Uzunov, a local revolutionary figure associated with the Internal Macedonian Revolutionary Organization. The house adds a small, local-history stop to Ohrid's old-town churches and museums.

It is most useful for visitors already exploring the old town on foot and looking for places tied to Ohrid's political and cultural history.
