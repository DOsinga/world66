---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

May, early June, and September are the best months: warm days, cool evenings, the churches and lake fully accessible, no crowds. July and August are hot — daytime highs over 30°C — and the town is busy, with the Ohrid Summer Festival running concerts in the open air through July and the first half of August. Winter is quiet and many lakeside places are closed, but the town has its own beauty in low light and snow on the surrounding mountains, and the churches are at their best without other visitors.
