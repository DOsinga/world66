Zeynel Pasha Tekje is a small Sufi complex near Ohrid's old plane tree square. Dating from the late 16th century, it includes a prayer space and mausoleum connected with the Halveti dervish order, adding an Ottoman and Bektashi layer to the town's better-known Orthodox heritage.
