The ruins of Čarši Mosque mark one of Prilep's older Ottoman-era sites. The mosque was built in 1475 on the site of an earlier religious building, and parts of its walls and minaret still stand.

The remains are unrestored, so this is a quick historical stop rather than a formal museum visit. It is most worthwhile as part of a walk through the older parts of town.
