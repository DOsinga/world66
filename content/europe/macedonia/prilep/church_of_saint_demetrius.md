The Church of Saint Demetrius near Prilep is a medieval Orthodox church whose fabric shows several building phases. Its mixed stonework and long history make it more interesting than a quick glance suggests.

It is best treated as part of a wider Prilep heritage circuit, alongside Marko's Towers, the old bazaar, and nearby monasteries.
