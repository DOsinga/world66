The Church of Saint Nicholas near Prilep is a small, tall medieval church from 1298, notable for its decorated facades and vivid frescoes. Inside, look for scenes such as the Mocking of Christ and Judas's kiss, along with older altar frescoes from an earlier 12th-century church.
