The Memorial Museum of October 11, 1941 covers the events around the start of the National Liberation War in the Prilep area. Its exhibits use documents, photographs, and objects to explain the period before 1940, the beginning of the war, and the events of 11 October 1941.

The museum is small, but it is a useful stop for visitors interested in North Macedonia's Second World War history.
