The Museum of Tobacco reflects Prilep's long role as Macedonia's tobacco capital. Established in 1973, it has a large collection on tobacco growing, processing, smoking and the culture that grew around the crop.

It is a specialized museum rather than a general city introduction, but that is the point: Prilep's identity is closely tied to tobacco, and this collection gives context for an industry that shaped the town and the surrounding Pelagonia plain.
