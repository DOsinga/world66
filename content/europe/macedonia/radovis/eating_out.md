---
title: Eating Out
type: section
---

Radoviš eats well and cheaply. The local cuisine is standard Macedonian — grilled meats, bean dishes, shopska salad, and the slow-cooked village stews that reward anyone who stays long enough to eat where the locals eat. Prices are low even by North Macedonian standards. The town's agricultural hinterland produces good vegetables, and the area is known for its pomegranates and tobacco.
