Konce Monastery is one of the notable historic church sites in the Radovis area. Its Church of St Stefan was built between 1366 and 1371, during the reign of Serbian Emperor Dusan, and still preserves later fresco work from the 18th and 19th centuries.

The complex is well kept, with living quarters, a fountain, and a bell tower around the church.
