---
title: When to Go
type: section
---

Radoviš has a humid subtropical climate — hot, dry summers and cold, snowy winters. Summer temperatures regularly exceed 31°C, and the valley can feel airless in July and August; the surrounding mountains are more comfortable at that time of year. January is the coldest month, averaging just over 1°C, with regular snowfall that turns the Plačkovica foothills into a different landscape entirely.

The best times to visit are **spring (April–May)** and **early autumn (September–October)**. In April and May, the fields around town are green, the pomegranate trees are in flower, and the Plačkovica meadows are at their best — snow still lingers on the upper slopes while the lower trails are clear. Autumn brings the harvest season, which gives the town market a particular energy, and the light on the mountains is at its most vivid.

Radoviš does not have major festivals that would alone justify a visit, but the town's quiet calendar is part of its appeal. You are unlikely to compete with other travellers for anything.
