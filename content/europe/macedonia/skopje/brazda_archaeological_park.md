Brazda Archaeological Park preserves remains from a settlement active around the 5th and 4th centuries BC, when this part of the Skopje basin was linked to Paeonian tribes and wider Balkan trade. The site is modest, but the surviving walls and finds make it a useful stop for travellers interested in the ancient layers beneath modern North Macedonia.

It is outside central Skopje, so it works best as a short detour rather than a casual city-centre sight.
