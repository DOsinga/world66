The Church of the Holy Savior in Dracevo is a 19th-century church with frescoes by Dico Zograf and a quiet courtyard with a bell tower. It is not one of Skopje's headline sights, but it is worthwhile if you are exploring the city's outer neighbourhoods or tracing local Orthodox architecture.
