Cifte Hamam is a 15th-century Ottoman bathhouse in Skopje's Old Bazaar. Its name means "double bath", reflecting the paired men's and women's sections, though the layout differs from the better-known Daut Pasha Baths nearby.

The domed stone rooms now serve as part of the National Gallery, so the visit combines Ottoman architecture with changing art exhibitions. It is a natural stop while walking through the bazaar.
