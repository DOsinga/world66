Dukjandzik Mosque stands on the northern edge of Skopje's Old Bazaar. Built in 1550, it has a main dome, twin domes over the entrance portico, and a minaret that partly survived the 1963 earthquake.

It is a modest but worthwhile stop while walking the bazaar's Ottoman-era lanes, especially in combination with Mustafa Pasha Mosque, the Daut Pasha Baths, and the old market streets.
