Hıfzı Pasha Lodging is a 19th-century residence complex built for Hıfzı Pasha and counted among North Macedonia's notable examples of monumental domestic architecture. The compound includes several buildings, guard towers, stone walls, wall paintings, and carved ceilings.

It is outside the compact centre of Skopje, so it is best for visitors with a specific interest in Ottoman-era architecture and historic residences.
