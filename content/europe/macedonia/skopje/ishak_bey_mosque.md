Ishak Bey Mosque is a 15th-century Ottoman mosque in Skopje's old bazaar area. The building lost much of its original blue tile decoration in the 1689 fire, but its dome, minaret, and surviving six-sided turbe still make it a worthwhile stop while exploring the bazaar.
