Kapan Han is a 15th-century Ottoman inn in Skopje's Old Bazaar, built by Isa Bey Ishakovic. The courtyard and two-storey galleries still give a clear sense of how travellers, merchants, animals, and goods once moved through the bazaar. It now houses restaurants, but visitors can still walk in and look around.
