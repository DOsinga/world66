Kral Kizi Turbe is a small mausoleum associated with the Bosnian princess Catherine. The original structure was destroyed in the 1963 Skopje earthquake and rebuilt in 2014.

It is a modest stop, but it adds useful context to Skopje's Ottoman-era and Balkan heritage sites, especially if you are already exploring the old bazaar area.
