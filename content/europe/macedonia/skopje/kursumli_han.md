Kurşumli Han is the largest of Skopje's surviving Ottoman caravanserais, built in the 16th century around stone arcades and a broad courtyard. It now forms part of the Museum of Macedonia, but the building itself is the reason to come: a solid, atmospheric piece of the old bazaar's trading past.
