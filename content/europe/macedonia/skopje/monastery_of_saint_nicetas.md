The Monastery of Saint Nicetas is a medieval Byzantine-style church north of Skopje, built around 1300 by Serbian King Milutin. Its early-14th-century frescoes and stone-and-brick architecture make it a strong day-trip stop for visitors interested in medieval Macedonia.
