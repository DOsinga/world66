Murat Pasha Mosque stands in Skopje's old bazaar area, where two busy streets meet. Built in 1803, it is an Ottoman Baroque mosque with a single minaret and a courtyard fountain added in the 20th century.

It is a smaller stop than Mustafa Pasha Mosque, but worth a few minutes while walking through the Carsija for its position in the street life of the old quarter.
