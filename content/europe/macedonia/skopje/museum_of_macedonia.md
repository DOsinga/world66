The Museum of Macedonia brings together archaeological, historical, and ethnological collections near Skopje's old bazaar. The ethnological galleries are the strongest part, with traditional costumes from across North Macedonia, including heavy wedding dress, bridal headwear, models of vernacular architecture, and displays on regional customs.

The archaeological section ranges from prehistoric figurines to early medieval material, while the icon gallery covers religious art from the 10th to the 19th centuries. It is a useful stop for visitors who want broader context before travelling farther through the country.
