The Park of the Woman Freedom Fighter sits opposite North Macedonia's parliament, right in the thick of Skopje's monument-heavy centre. It is a small green space, but an interesting stop for the way it gathers modern memorials to fallen soldiers, anti-fascist resistance, and Macedonia's turbulent 20th-century politics.
