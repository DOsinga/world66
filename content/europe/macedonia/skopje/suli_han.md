Suli Han is a 15th-century Ottoman caravansary in Skopje's Old Bazaar, built by Ishak Bey. It is the second-largest of the bazaar's historic hans, with two storeys around an inner courtyard.

The building now houses the Old Bazaar Museum, with exhibits on archaeology, ethnology, fine arts, and local history, as well as university arts spaces. It is a good stop for understanding the bazaar as a trading district rather than only a shopping area.
