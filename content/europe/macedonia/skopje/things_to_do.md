title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Skopje offers two cities in one: the Ottoman old bazaar and the surreal neo-baroque construction project that has colonised the modern centre. The [Čaršija (Old Bazaar)](/europe/macedonia/skopje/carsija) is the essential Skopje experience — a genuine working quarter of craft workshops, mosques, and hans that survived the 1963 earthquake. The new centre is worth seeing for its sheer audacity, particularly [Macedonia Square](/europe/macedonia/skopje/macedonia_square) with its enormous equestrian statue and the triumphal [Porta Macedonia](/europe/macedonia/skopje/porta_macedonia) arch.

The [Skopje Fortress (Kale)](/europe/macedonia/skopje/skopje_fortress) above the bazaar gives the best overview of the city and the Vardar River. The [Museum of the Macedonian Struggle](/europe/macedonia/skopje/museum_of_the_macedonian_struggle) and the [Holocaust Memorial Center](/europe/macedonia/skopje/holocaust_memorial_center) are the most serious museums. The [Memorial House of Mother Teresa](/europe/macedonia/skopje/memorial_house_of_mother_teresa), marking the birthplace of Agnes Gonxha Bojaxhiu, is a pilgrimage point. The [Mustafa Pasha Mosque](/europe/macedonia/skopje/mustafa_pasha_mosque) and the [Daut Pasha Baths](/europe/macedonia/skopje/daut_pasha_baths) are the finest Ottoman structures. The [Skopje Aqueduct](/europe/macedonia/skopje/skopje_aqueduct) north of the city is an unexpectedly impressive ancient ruin.
