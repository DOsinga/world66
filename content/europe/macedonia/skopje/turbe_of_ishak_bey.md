The Turbe of Ishak Bey is a small Ottoman mausoleum near the Clock Tower and Sultan Murat Mosque. It marks the burial place of Ishak Bey, the 15th-century ruler who helped shape Ottoman Skopje, and works best as a short stop while exploring the Old Bazaar area.
