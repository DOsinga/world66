Yahya Pasha Mosque was built in 1504 by Yahya Pasha, an Ottoman commander and son-in-law of Sultan Bayezid II. It stands out for its pyramid-shaped roof and tall minaret, a different profile from the domed mosques found elsewhere in Skopje.
