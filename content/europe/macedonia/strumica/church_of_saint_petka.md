The Church of Saint Petka is the main historic church of Vasilevo Municipality, northeast of Strumica. Built in 1875, it is known for its preserved frescoes and its role as the area's most important local church.

It is best treated as a short detour for visitors already exploring Strumica's monasteries and village churches, rather than as a stand-alone trip.
