---
title: "Getting There"
type: section
---

Strumica is in the far southeast of North Macedonia, an hour from the Bulgarian border at Novo Selo–Zlatarevo and 90 minutes from the Greek border at Doiran. From [Skopje](/europe/macedonia/skopje) it is about 170 km by the M5/A1 highway, around two and a half hours by car. Several bus companies run direct services from Skopje (about 3 hours, departures roughly every hour) and from Sofia in Bulgaria (around 4 hours).

There is no passenger train service. The nearest international airports are Skopje (2.5 hours) and Thessaloniki in Greece (3 hours by car or a longer bus journey via the border). Hire car is the easiest way to reach the surrounding monasteries and villages.
