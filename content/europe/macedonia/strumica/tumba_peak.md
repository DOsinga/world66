Tumba Peak sits high on Belasica Mountain at the meeting point of North Macedonia, Bulgaria, and Greece. At 1,880 metres, it is a border landmark as much as a summit, with wide mountain views and a satisfying sense of standing at a geographic edge.

It is a destination for hikers rather than casual sightseers. Weather can change quickly on the ridge, so treat it as a mountain outing, not a roadside viewpoint.
