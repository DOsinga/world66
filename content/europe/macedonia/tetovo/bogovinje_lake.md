Bogovinje Lake is a high mountain lake in the Šar range above the Tetovo region, reached by a serious hike rather than a roadside viewpoint. The reward is alpine water, open pasture, and the broad mountain country that defines north-western Macedonia.

Go only in suitable weather and with reliable route information, as conditions can be rough and seasonal. It is a strong choice for hikers who want Tetovo's mountains, not just the painted mosque and bazaar.
