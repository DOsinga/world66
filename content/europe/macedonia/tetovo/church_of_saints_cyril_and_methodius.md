The Church of Saints Cyril and Methodius is one of the larger Orthodox churches in the Polog region. Built between 1903 and 1918, it has a three-nave basilica plan, a single dome, white walls, and a red roof.

The interior frescoes were painted by Danilo Nestorovski in 1924. The church also contains a gallery of icons, making it a worthwhile addition if you are already exploring Tetovo beyond the Painted Mosque and Arabati Baba Tekke.
