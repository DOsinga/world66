The Karanikola glacial lakes are a serious mountain outing rather than a casual Tetovo sight. Set high in the Shar Mountains, they reward hikers with cold alpine water, open ridges, and a landscape that feels far removed from the city's mosque, bazaar, and traffic.

Go prepared and treat the route as a mountain hike: weather, navigation, and transport all matter. For fit travelers, the lakes are one of the best ways to understand why Tetovo's backdrop is not just scenery but a real outdoor region.
