This small museum occupies the former Jovanovski family home, where the Communist Party of Macedonia was founded in the 1940s and its first committee was appointed. It is a specialist stop, but it adds a modern political layer to a city better known for Ottoman and Bektashi heritage.

It is most worthwhile if you are already exploring central Tetovo and want more context on North Macedonia's 20th-century history.
