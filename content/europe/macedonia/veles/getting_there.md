---
title: Getting There
type: section
---

Veles is one of North Macedonia's main rail junctions. Trains connect it with [Skopje](/europe/macedonia/skopje), Bitola, Prilep, and the Vardar corridor, though schedules can be thin and should be checked close to travel.

By road, Veles sits on the main north-south route through the country. Buses and shared transport from Skopje are straightforward, and the drive from the capital normally takes under an hour when traffic is kind.
