A small social-history museum housed in a fifteenth-century palazzino on St Margaret's Hill, one of the oldest standing houses in Bormla. The collection ranges across everyday objects from the town's working-class past — dockyard tools, family photographs, religious objects, kitchen ware — and the building itself, restored slowly over many years, is the main exhibit.

Opening hours are limited and dependent on the volunteer curator. Worth checking ahead. Even from outside, the medieval window mouldings and the narrow lane setting give a sense of pre-Knights Bormla that has otherwise almost vanished.
