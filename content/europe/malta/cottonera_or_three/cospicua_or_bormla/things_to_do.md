---
title: Things to Do
type: section
---

Cospicua's pleasures are quieter than its neighbours' — the great baroque Immaculate Conception church, a couple of small museums tracing the dockyard's centuries of work, and long stretches of bastion wall (the Cottonera Lines) that you can walk almost in solitude. The waterfront around the No. 1 Dock has been smartened up in recent years and is the city's most photogenic corner.
