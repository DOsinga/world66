Fort Rinella is a British coastal fort built to defend the Grand Harbour. Its star exhibit is a rare Armstrong 100-ton gun, the kind of massive Victorian artillery that makes Malta's harbour defences feel less abstract.

The fort now operates as a museum with military displays and occasional demonstrations. It sits just outside Vittoriosa, so it fits well with a Three Cities day focused on fortifications and harbour history.
