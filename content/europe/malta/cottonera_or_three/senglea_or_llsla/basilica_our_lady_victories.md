The Basilica of the Nativity of Mary, more commonly the Basilica of Our Lady of Victories, is Senglea's parish church and the largest building on the peninsula. The original was destroyed by aerial bombing in World War II, and the present church is a careful 1950s reconstruction in the Baroque style of the 17th-century original, with a notable octagonal dome.

The most venerated object inside is the statue of Maria Bambina — the Christ Child as a baby — carried in procession each 8 September for the feast of Our Lady of Victories, which celebrates the lifting of both the 1565 Ottoman siege and Italy's surrender in World War II. The church is small but worth a few minutes for the painted ceiling and the marble side chapels.
