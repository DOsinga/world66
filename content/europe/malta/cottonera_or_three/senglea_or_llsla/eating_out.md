---
title: "Eating Out"
type: section
---

Senglea's eating is concentrated along the waterfront on the Cospicua side, where a row of small restaurants and seafood places overlooks the Macina and the yachts in Dockyard Creek. For local Maltese cooking — rabbit stew, *aljotta* fish soup, *pastizzi* — wander up Triq il-Vittorja into the residential streets and look for the small unsigned places that locals favour over the harbourside.
