---
title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Senglea is small — twelve narrow streets on a peninsula barely 400 metres long. The main attractions are the Gardjola Gardens at the tip, the Basilica of Our Lady of Victories with its harbour view, and the walk along Triq il-Vittorja, the spine of the town. It is best combined with [Birgu](/europe/malta/cottonera_or_three/vittoriosa) and Cospicua, which are an easy walk away across the docks.
