---
title: "Bars and Cafes"
type: section
---

Gozo's nightlife is low-key. The liveliest spots are the cluster of bars and restaurants along the [Marsalforn](/europe/malta/gozo/marsalforn) and [Xlendi](/europe/malta/gozo/xlendi_bay) promenades, where tables spill onto the seafront and the evening passes slowly over cold local wine and grilled fish. In [Victoria](/europe/malta/gozo/victoria), a clutch of small bars around It-Tokk and Pjazza San Ġorġ stay open late.

For an aperitif, look for a *pastizzeria* — many double as wine bars in the evening — and try a glass of local Gellewża red or a dry Girgentina white from the small Gozitan vineyards. The local *Cisk* lager is everywhere; for something stronger, ask for a glass of *bajtra*, a sweet liqueur made from prickly pear, which most bars keep behind the counter.
