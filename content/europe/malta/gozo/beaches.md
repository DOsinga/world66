---
title: "Beaches"
type: section
---

Gozo's beaches are small but varied. The two red-sand beaches — [Ramla Bay](/europe/malta/gozo/ramla_bay) and the harder-to-reach [San Blas Bay](/europe/malta/gozo/san_blas_bay) — are the most photographed and the most popular. Both are shallow and family-friendly; San Blas is the quieter of the two thanks to a steep walk down to the sand.

For something different, [Mġarr ix-Xini](/europe/malta/gozo/mgarr_ix_xini) is a narrow pebble cove with crystal water and a single restaurant; **Hondoq ir-Rummien**, just past Qala on the east coast, is a small bay with views over to Comino and another tiny pebble beach. Most of Gozo's swimming, though, is off the rocks — almost any stretch of coast around [Marsalforn](/europe/malta/gozo/marsalforn), [Xlendi](/europe/malta/gozo/xlendi_bay), or [Dwejra](/europe/malta/gozo/dwejra_inland_sea) has a way down to clean, deep water.
