---
title: "Eating Out"
type: section
---

Gozitan cooking is rooted in what the island produces: rabbit braised with garlic and wine is the signature dish, along with *lampuki* (dorado) in season, fresh vegetables from small farms, and *ġbejniet* — small rounds of sheep's milk cheese, either fresh or sun-dried and peppered. The capital [Victoria](/europe/malta/gozo/victoria) has a good range of restaurants, while [Xlendi](/europe/malta/gozo/xlendi_bay) and [Marsalforn](/europe/malta/gozo/marsalforn) have waterfront options serving fresh fish. For a traditional Gozitan home-cooked meal, [Mariblu](/europe/malta/gozo/mariblu_restaurant) in Xewkija is reliable and unfussy.
