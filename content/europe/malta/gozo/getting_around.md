---
title: "Getting Around"
type: section
---

Gozo is small — roughly 14 km by 7 km — and most visitors get around by **rental car**, which is the most flexible option and not expensive. Cars can be rented in [Victoria](/europe/malta/gozo/victoria), at the Mġarr ferry terminal, or delivered to your hotel. Driving is on the left, as in the rest of Malta.

The **public bus network** is cheap and surprisingly comprehensive — almost every village has a service to Victoria, from which you can change to anywhere on the island. Routes can be slow, but Tallinja day passes work across both Malta and Gozo. **Taxis** and **app-based rides** (Bolt, eCabs) operate but coverage thins in rural areas. For shorter distances, the island lends itself well to cycling and walking — the **coastal path** loop around Gozo is a multi-day walking route that strings together most of the major sights.
