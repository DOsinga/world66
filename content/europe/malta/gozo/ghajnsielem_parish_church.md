Ghajnsielem Parish Church is a neo-Gothic landmark above Mgarr Harbour, started in the 1920s but only consecrated decades later. Its tall facade and pointed arches make it stand out among Gozo's mostly Baroque village churches, and it is an easy stop when arriving on the ferry.
