---
title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Gozo rewards slow exploration. The [Ggantija Temples](/europe/malta/gozo/ggantija_temples) near Xagħra are among the oldest freestanding structures in the world, predating the Egyptian pyramids. The walled [Citadel](/europe/malta/gozo/the_citadel) above [Victoria](/europe/malta/gozo/victoria) offers panoramic views across the island. [Ta' Pinu Basilica](/europe/malta/gozo/tapinu_basilica) is a major pilgrimage site with an intimate atmosphere, and [Calypso's Cave](/europe/malta/gozo/calypso_cave) above Ramla Bay connects the island to Homeric legend.

The coast is the other half of the trip: [Dwejra and the Inland Sea](/europe/malta/gozo/dwejra_inland_sea) on the west, the resort bays of [Xlendi](/europe/malta/gozo/xlendi_bay) and [Marsalforn](/europe/malta/gozo/marsalforn), the red-sand beaches of [Ramla Bay](/europe/malta/gozo/ramla_bay) and [San Blas Bay](/europe/malta/gozo/san_blas_bay), and the dramatic clefts of [Mġarr ix-Xini](/europe/malta/gozo/mgarr_ix_xini) and the [Wied il-Mielaħ Window](/europe/malta/gozo/wied_il_mielah). The vast dome of the [Xewkija Rotunda](/europe/malta/gozo/xewkija_rotunda) is visible from almost everywhere on the island.
