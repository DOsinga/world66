---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

Gozo has a classic Mediterranean climate: hot, dry summers and mild, occasionally wet winters. **April to June** and **September to early November** are the best times for most travellers — temperatures in the mid-twenties Celsius, calm seas warm enough for swimming, and a fraction of the high-season crowds.

**July and August** are very hot (often well into the 30s) and the small beaches at [Ramla Bay](/europe/malta/gozo/ramla_bay) and [San Blas Bay](/europe/malta/gozo/san_blas_bay) can fill up at weekends. **Winter** (December to March) brings short rains and cooler weather, but the cliffs and inland walks are at their greenest, and you'll have the island largely to yourself. The biggest events in the calendar are the village *festas* honouring patron saints — most fall between June and September, with fireworks, brass bands, and processions.
