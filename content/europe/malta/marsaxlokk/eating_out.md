---
title: "Eating Out"
type: section
---

Marsaxlokk is one of Malta's best places to eat fish, and that is most of what people come for. A long line of seafood restaurants runs along the waterfront, all serving variations on the same theme — grilled or fried fish, octopus stew, *aljotta* (Maltese fish soup with garlic and rice), and the autumn special **lampuki pie**, a flaky pastry case filled with dorado, capers, olives, and mint.

The places with the most local custom are **Tartarun**, set back a block from the harbour and considered the best fish in the village; **Roots**, with a strong wine list and a more contemporary kitchen; and **Ir-Rizzu** on the waterfront — old-fashioned, busy, and reliable. Most restaurants are closed for a long lunch service after the Sunday market and reopen for dinner. Book ahead for any of them on a Sunday.
