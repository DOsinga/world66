---
title: "Getting There"
type: section
---

Marsaxlokk is in the south-east corner of Malta, about 11 km from [Valletta](/europe/malta/valletta) and 5 km from the airport. The simplest approach is **bus**: routes **81** and **85** run frequently from Valletta to the village; route **119** connects directly from St Julian's and Bugibba in summer. Buses take about 40 minutes from the capital and drop you at the waterfront. A taxi or ride-share from the airport is around 15 minutes.

If you are based in the south of the island, Marsaxlokk pairs naturally with a morning at the **Hypogeum** or **Tarxien temples** and a swim at **St Peter's Pool** on the Delimara peninsula, all within a few kilometres. Driving is straightforward, but parking on the harbour is limited and the Sunday market makes it impossible — come in by bus or park inland and walk.
