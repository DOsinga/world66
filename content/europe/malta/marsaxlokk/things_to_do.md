---
title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

There is not a great deal to *do* in Marsaxlokk — the village itself, the working harbour with its painted *luzzu* boats, and the famous Sunday market are most of the pleasure. Plan a long lunch on the waterfront and a walk on the **Delimara peninsula** out to **St Peter's Pool**, the open-rock swimming spot ten minutes' drive south. Together that makes a full and satisfying day out.

If you want more, the prehistoric **Ġgantija**-era cult site at **Tas-Silġ** on the hill north of the village is open to the public on selected days; the **Għar Dalam** cave at Birżebbuġa, a couple of kilometres west, contains the fossils of dwarf elephants and hippopotami from the last Ice Age.
