---
title: When to Go
type: section
---

Mdina can be visited year-round, but the experience varies considerably by time of day rather than by season. Arrive before 9am or after 4pm and you will have the narrow streets almost to yourself. Between 10am and 3pm the city fills with day-trippers from Malta's coastal resorts and cruise passengers, and the "Silent City" earns its name only in irony.

Spring (March to May) and autumn (September to November) are the most comfortable seasons for Malta generally, with warm weather and lower humidity than the summer months. July and August are hot and crowded across the island. December and January are mild by northern European standards but can be windy.

The most atmospheric time to visit is during the annual medieval festival, held in late autumn, when costumed knights and torchlit processions fill the streets.
