Spinola Palace is a 17th-century palace built by Fra Paolo Rafel Spinola, a knight of the Order of St. John. It is one of the older landmarks around St Julian's and gives some historic depth to an area better known for its waterfront and nightlife.

The palace is best seen as part of a walk around Spinola Bay and Balluta Bay.
