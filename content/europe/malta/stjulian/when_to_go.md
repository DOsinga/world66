---
title: When to Go
type: section
---

Malta has about 300 days of sunshine per year, and St Julian's works as a base year-round. The summer peak (July–August) is hot, 30–35°C, and the beaches are crowded, but the nightlife is at its most intense and the sea is warm for swimming. Spring (April–June) and autumn (September–October) offer the best combination of manageable temperatures, fewer crowds, and a quieter version of the beach and water sports season.

Winter is mild (12–18°C) and genuinely quiet. The nightlife slows considerably between November and March, and most of the outdoor restaurant terraces close or reduce hours. But prices drop sharply and Valletta — a short bus ride away — is at its most uncrowded.
