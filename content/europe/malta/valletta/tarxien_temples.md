The Tarxien Temples are one of Malta's great prehistoric sites, a megalithic temple
complex with carved stone blocks, altars, and traces of ritual architecture older than
the pyramids. Though surrounded by modern streets, the site opens a door into the
island's deepest human history.

It is easy to visit from Valletta and pairs naturally with the Hypogeum, though each
site has a different feel. Go with enough background to appreciate the carvings and the
extraordinary age of the place.
