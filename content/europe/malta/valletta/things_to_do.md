---
title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Valletta packs an extraordinary density of history into its compact grid. The city was purpose-built by the Knights of St John after the Great Siege of 1565, and almost every street holds a palace, church, or fortification from that era. [St John's Co-Cathedral](/europe/malta/valletta/st_johns_co_cathedral) alone is worth a trip to Malta — its gilded interior, Caravaggio painting, and mosaic floor of 400 knights' tombstones are extraordinary. From the [Upper Barrakka Gardens](/europe/malta/valletta/upper_barrakka_gardens) you get the classic view over the Grand Harbour — then take the [ferry](/europe/malta/valletta/grand_harbour_ferry) across to the Three Cities for a perspective back at Valletta's honey-stone bastions.

Walk the [Bastions](/europe/malta/valletta/fortifications_walk) for continuous harbour views and a sense of the city's military engineering. The [Grand Masters' Palace](/europe/malta/valletta/grand_masters_palace) and its [Armoury](/europe/malta/valletta/armoury) on [Republic Street](/europe/malta/valletta/republic_street) tell the story of the Knights' rule over Malta. The [National Museum of Archaeology](/europe/malta/valletta/national_museum_of_archaeology), in the Auberge de Provence, is the best place to understand Malta's prehistoric culture before visiting the temples. The [Lascaris War Rooms](/europe/malta/valletta/lascaris_war_rooms) underground complete the picture with Malta's Second World War story.

Don't miss [Strait Street](/europe/malta/valletta/strait_street) — the former sailors' quarter, recently revived — for the most atmospheric evening walk in the city. [Casa Rocca Piccola](/europe/malta/valletta/casa_rocca_piccola) offers a rare glimpse into a continuously occupied Knights-era palazzo. The [Manoel Theatre](/europe/malta/valletta/manoel_theatre), [Fort St Elmo](/europe/malta/valletta/fort_st_elmo), the [Saluting Battery](/europe/malta/valletta/saluting_battery) noon cannon, and the [Mysterium Fidei](/europe/malta/valletta/mysterium_fidei) underground monastery complete a city that genuinely rewards several days.
