Trabuxu on South Street is a reliable bistro in Valletta's lower grid, popular with both tourists and local workers. The menu covers Maltese and Mediterranean standards — pasta, grilled fish, rabbit dishes — at reasonable prices. The wine list is good for Malta, with a decent selection of Maltese labels alongside Italian wines. The restaurant occupies a traditional Baroque house with vaulted ceilings and wooden beams.

A solid choice for a proper sit-down lunch or early dinner. The location between Republic Street and Strait Street makes it convenient for a meal during or after an afternoon of sightseeing.
