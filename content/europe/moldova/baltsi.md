Baltsi (Bălți) is Moldova's second city, with a population of around 100,000 in the northern part of the country, roughly 120 km from [Chisinau](/europe/moldova/chisinau). It is primarily an industrial and commercial centre rather than a tourist destination, but it serves as a useful base for exploring northern Moldova — including the monasteries of Saharna and Curchi, and the fortress town of [Soroca](/europe/moldova/soroca).

The city has a market, a small historic centre, and a handful of worthwhile stops for travellers who find themselves here. The [Vasile Alecsandri National Theatre](/europe/moldova/baltsi/vasile_alecsandri_theatre) is one of Moldova's three national theatres and worth checking for evening performances. The [Alley of Classics](/europe/moldova/baltsi/alley_of_classics) is a sculptural park that offers a shaded walk among busts of Moldovan literary figures. The [Cathedral of Sts. Constantine and Helen](/europe/moldova/baltsi/sts_constantine_helen_cathedral) anchors the southern part of the city. There is regular bus and marshrutka service to Chisinau.
