---
title: Getting There
type: section
---

Baltsi is connected to [Chisinau](/europe/moldova/chisinau) by regular bus and marshrutka (shared minibus) service. The journey takes about 2.5 to 3 hours. Marshrutkas depart frequently from Chisinau's central bus station throughout the day; the fare is inexpensive. There is also bus service to Soroca, Edineț, and other northern Moldova towns.

There is no regular train service to Baltsi itself — the nearest mainline station is some distance from the city centre.
