Chisinau's Military Museum has a compact collection of Soviet-era military equipment, including armoured vehicles and aircraft. It is most useful for visitors interested in Moldova's 20th-century history and the military legacy left by the Soviet period.

The museum is close enough to the centre to combine with Chisinau's national museums, central market, and memorial sites.
