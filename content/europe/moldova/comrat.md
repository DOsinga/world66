Comrat is the capital of Gagauzia, Moldova's autonomous Turkic-speaking region in the dry southern steppe. The city itself is modest, but the place identity is strong: Gagauz language and culture, Orthodox churches, Soviet-era monuments, and wineries scattered through the red-wine country around town.

Do not come expecting a polished city break. Come because Gagauzia is one of Europe's more unusual cultural corners, and Comrat is the easiest place to start understanding it. The [Comrat Regional History Museum](/europe/moldova/comrat/comrat_regional_history_museum) and the [National Gagauz History and Ethnographic Museum](/europe/moldova/comrat/national_gagauz_history_and_ethnographic_museum) in nearby Besalma give the visit substance.

The central square is anchored by the [Cathedral of Saint John the Baptist](/europe/moldova/comrat/cathedral_of_saint_john_the_baptist), while [Vinuri de Comrat](/europe/moldova/comrat/vinuri_de_comrat) adds the local wine thread. A spare day here is best spent slowly, with a museum, a church, a winery tasting, and a drive through the low, open Gagauz countryside.
