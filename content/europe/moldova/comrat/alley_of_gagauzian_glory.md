The Alley of Gagauzian Glory is a short central walk of monuments and busts connected to Gagauz public memory. It will not hold you for long, but it helps explain how strongly the city presents itself as the capital of a distinct people.

Pair it with the nearby cathedral and regional museum. Together they make Comrat's centre more legible than it first appears.
