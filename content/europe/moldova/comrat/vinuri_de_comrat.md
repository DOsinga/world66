Vinuri de Comrat is the easiest winery to work into a visit to the Gagauz capital. The surrounding region is one of Moldova's southern wine areas, known especially for reds and muscat, and this producer gives that part of Comrat's identity a practical stop.

Check tasting availability before turning up. Even if you only visit the shop, it is a useful reminder that Gagauzia is not just an administrative curiosity but part of Moldova's wider wine map.
