Orhei National Park protects the limestone valleys, villages, monasteries and archaeological landscapes around the Răut River. For travellers based in Orhei, it is the natural frame for Old Orhei and the surrounding countryside: cliffs, cave cells, open fields and river bends rather than a single fenced attraction.

The park rewards slow travel. Hire a driver, cycle if conditions suit, or combine a monastery visit with walks along the ridge lines; the distances are modest, but public transport rarely lines up neatly with the best viewpoints.
