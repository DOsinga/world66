---
title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Orhei itself has a small handful of sights: the 16th-century St Dumitru Church in the centre, the local history museum on the main square, and a tree-lined park along the Răut River. Most travellers use the town as the staging point for the spectacular [Orheiul Vechi](/europe/moldova/orheiul_vechi_2) cave monastery 18 km to the south, which is the real reason to come this way.
