---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

**Late spring (May, June) and early autumn (September)** are by far the best months for Orhei and Orheiul Vechi. The river valley is green, the temperatures are mild, and the paths around the monastery cliffs are dry and easy.

**Summer (July, August)** can be uncomfortably hot in this part of central Moldova — 35°C is not unusual — and the steppe landscape goes brown. **Winter** is cold and grey and not the best time to climb around an exposed clifftop. The cave church remains open year-round, but the bus connection thins out.
