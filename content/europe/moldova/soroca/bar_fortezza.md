A small bar close to the fortress entrance that catches the overflow of day visitors and a regular local crowd in the evenings. The name does the marketing for it — location is everything. Ice-cold Chișinău beer and local wines by the glass are the staples. Simple snacks are available. The outdoor tables have a partial view towards the fortress walls.
