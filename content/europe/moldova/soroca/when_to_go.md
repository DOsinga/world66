---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

The best time to visit Soroca is late spring (May–June) or early autumn (September–October). Summer temperatures can reach 35°C and the limestone bluffs reflect the heat mercilessly; spring brings the landscape to life with wildflowers along the Dniester banks. Winters are cold and grey — temperatures dip below -10°C — but the fortress looks dramatic with a dusting of snow, and the crowds that never were are even fewer. July and August see a trickle of Moldovan domestic tourists.

There are no major festivals in Soroca that drive visitor numbers significantly, but Roma cultural celebrations occasionally animate the Ţigănia neighbourhood in summer. Ask locally about dates, as these are not fixed events on a public calendar.
