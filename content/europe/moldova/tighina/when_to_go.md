---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

Late spring and early autumn are the most comfortable times. May, June and September give long days, mild temperatures and a slow procession of festivals and saints' days along the river. July and August can be very hot — temperatures in the mid-thirties are normal — and the open fortress gives little shade.

Winter is cold, often with snow, and many of the outdoor attractions including the fortress upper walls and the riverside promenade are less inviting. The fortress remains open year-round but its hours shrink in winter; check at the gate or with your guide before arriving.
