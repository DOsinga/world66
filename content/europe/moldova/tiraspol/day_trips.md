The easiest excursion from Tiraspol crosses the Dniester to [Tighina](/europe/moldova/tighina) — known in Russian as Bender — about 15 km west. The town's 16th-century Ottoman fortress, built under Suleiman the Magnificent and still partly used by the military, is the most spectacular historical monument in Transnistria; the drive takes around 20 minutes by marshrutka or shared taxi.

For a quieter half-day, the [Noul Neamț Monastery](/europe/moldova/tiraspol/noul_neamt_monastery) sits in the village of Chitcani, about 15 km south. Founded in 1864 by monks from the Neamț Monastery in Romania, it is an active complex of churches and a tall multi-storey bell tower, and a marshrutka from the city centre reaches it in roughly 30 minutes.
