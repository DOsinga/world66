The Supreme Soviet building is one of the clearest visual statements of Tiraspol's frozen-Soviet atmosphere. The architecture is severe and administrative, but the Lenin statue in front turns it into the postcard image many visitors associate with Transnistria.

You will not spend long here, and access is naturally limited, but the exterior belongs on a walk through central Tiraspol. It helps explain why the city feels politically peculiar as well as architecturally out of time.
