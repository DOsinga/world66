The Yuriy Gagarin Monument is a small but characteristic part of Tiraspol's Soviet-influenced cityscape. It commemorates the first human in space, a figure still prominent in public memory across much of the former Soviet world.

The monument is worth including in a walk focused on Tiraspol's statues, broad avenues, and political symbolism. It is not a destination by itself, but it helps build the picture of a city where Soviet iconography remains unusually visible.
