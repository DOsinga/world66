The Jardin Japonais is a compact Japanese garden beside the Larvotto waterfront and the Grimaldi Forum. Designed by Yasuo Beppu, it fits a stylised mountain, waterfall, brook, beach, and Zen meditation garden into less than a hectare.

It is small, but in dense Monaco that is part of the point: a calm landscaped pause between the seafront, high-rise hotels, and cultural venues nearby.
