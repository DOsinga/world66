Musée de l'automobile de Monaco is the principality's obvious stop for car enthusiasts. The collection runs from old carriages and early motor cars to racing machines, including Formula 1 cars that fit Monaco's grand-prix identity.

It is compact enough to combine with Fontvieille or the old town, but varied enough to interest visitors who normally skip car museums.
