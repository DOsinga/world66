The Musée des Timbres et des Monnaies covers Monaco's postal and monetary history, with Monegasque coins dating back to 1640 and displays of stamps issued by the principality. It is a compact specialty museum in Fontvieille, best for visitors interested in Monaco's statehood and collecting culture.
