The Opera de Monaco, also known as the Salle Garnier, is the ornate opera house inside the Casino de Monte-Carlo complex. Designed by Charles Garnier, architect of the Paris opera house, it is worth noting for its Belle Epoque interior and its central place in Monaco's cultural life.
