The Palais Princier is Monaco's ruling palace, standing above the harbour in Monaco-Ville. Parts of the state apartments are open on self-guided audio tours when the palace is receiving visitors, with frescoed rooms, ceremonial spaces, and views over the port.

The changing of the guard takes place outside the main entrance shortly before noon. Even without touring the rooms, the palace square is one of the best viewpoints over Monaco.
