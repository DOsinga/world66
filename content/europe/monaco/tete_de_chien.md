Tete de Chien is a rocky viewpoint above Monaco with a sweeping look over the principality, the harbour, and the coast. It can be reached by hike or by road, and it is one of the clearest ways to understand Monaco's tight setting between the sea and the cliffs.

On Grand Prix weekend, parts of the Formula 1 circuit are visible from the heights, but the view is worth the trip at any time in clear weather.
