Jadran sits on the southern side of the old town's outer wall, with a terrace facing the open Adriatic and Mogren Beach below. It is one of the better spots for a morning coffee or an afternoon drink without the nightclub atmosphere that takes over the old town in the evenings. The location is hard to beat — the sea glitters below, the old walls are right behind you, and the tourist crush hasn't quite reached this corner.

It gets busy in the evenings during summer but remains calmer than the bars deeper inside the old town. A good option for those who want the atmosphere without the volume.
