Sveti Stefan is the image that sells Montenegro to the world: a small medieval village built on a rocky islet connected to the mainland by a narrow causeway, its terracotta rooftops rising above the Adriatic. It is about 15 km south of Budva and reachable by bus or taxi in around 15–20 minutes.

The island itself is now a luxury hotel complex (Aman Resorts) and access is restricted to guests — you cannot walk through the village unless you're staying there. But the view from the mainland beach and from the road above is excellent and free. The pink-sand beach on the causeway is public and one of the better beaches on this stretch of coast. The village of Sveti Stefan above the beach has a handful of cafes and restaurants with views down to the island. Worth the short trip from Budva even if only to see the famous view.
