---
title: When to Go
type: section
---

May, June, and September are the best months for Budva. The water is warm enough for swimming, the beaches are not yet packed, restaurant prices are lower, and the town retains something of its actual character. July and August are peak season: beaches are crowded from morning, hotel prices double or triple, and the nightclubs run all night. Whether this is appealing or not depends entirely on what you're after.

October is mild and often sunny, though some restaurants and beach operations close after mid-September. Winter is quiet — most tourist businesses shut — but the old town and surrounding coast have a peaceful, local quality that high summer completely erases.
