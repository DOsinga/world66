Režević Monastery is a medieval Serbian Orthodox monastery a few kilometres up the coast from Buljarica toward Petrovac, set in a small valley inland from the shore road. The main church dates to the thirteenth century and contains original frescoes. The setting is quiet and shaded, and the monastery sees far fewer visitors than the more famous sites on the Bay of Kotor. Easily combined with a stop at Gradište Monastery as a half-day excursion from Buljarica.
