This small Roman mosaic near Petrovac is a quiet reminder that the coast was settled long before the beach resorts arrived. The fourth-century floor is usually viewed from a protected gallery rather than walked over, so it is a short stop, but a worthwhile one if you are already exploring Petrovac's old town and monasteries.
