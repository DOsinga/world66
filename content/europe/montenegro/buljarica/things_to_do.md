---
title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Buljarica's centrepiece is [Buljarica Beach](/europe/montenegro/buljarica/buljarica_beach) — at 2.5 km the longest beach in Montenegro, significantly less crowded than the nearby Budva beaches, and backed by olive groves rather than resort hotels. The [Gradište Monastery](/europe/montenegro/buljarica/gradiste_monastery) sits on the hillside above the coast road: a 12th-century Orthodox complex with three churches, fresco-decorated interiors, and a real history of raids, fires, and reconstruction. The [Režević Monastery](/europe/montenegro/buljarica/rezevici_monastery), a few kilometres up the coast toward Petrovac, is another medieval church worth a short detour.
