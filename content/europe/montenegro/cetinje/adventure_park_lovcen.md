Adventure Park Lovcen is a forest rope-course attraction at Ivanova Korita, high in Lovcen National Park above Cetinje. It is a commercial activity, but the mountain setting and family-friendly courses make it more useful to travellers than a generic operator listing.

Come for active time in the trees rather than sightseeing in the usual sense. It works best as part of a Lovcen day with the mausoleum, mountain roads, and fresh air, especially for families who need a break from monasteries and museums.
