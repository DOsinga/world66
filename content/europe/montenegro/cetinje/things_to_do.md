title: Things to Do
type: section
tags: [things_to_do]

Cetinje is compact and its main attractions can be covered in a thorough day — several museums, the monastery, the royal palace, and the preserved diplomatic quarter of 19th-century embassies. The real bonus is Mount Lovćen above the town, with its commanding views and the mausoleum of Montenegro's most famous historical figure.
