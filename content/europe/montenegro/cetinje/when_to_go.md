---
title: When to Go
type: section
tags: [when_to_go]
---

Cetinje sits at 670 metres and has a highland Mediterranean climate — milder than the Montenegrin interior but cooler than the coast, with more rain and occasional snow in winter.

**Spring (April–May)** and **autumn (September–October)** are the best times to visit — comfortable temperatures, fewer tourists than summer, and the Lovćen landscape at its most dramatic. The light in autumn is particularly good.

**Summer (June–August)** is warm and dry, with comfortable temperatures around 25°C. The coast below is packed in July and August, but Cetinje itself never becomes crowded — most coastal tourists make it a half-day excursion rather than a base. The mausoleum road to Lovćen is clear and the views at their clearest.

**Winter (November–March)** brings cold, occasional snow, and short days. Some museums reduce hours. The atmosphere of the empty town with snow on the mountains has a melancholy charm, but check opening hours carefully before visiting.
