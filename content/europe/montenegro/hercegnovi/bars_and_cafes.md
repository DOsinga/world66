---
title: "Bars and Cafes"
type: section
---

Café life happens on Belavista Square in the morning and on the Pet Danica promenade in the evening. The bars that line the seafront below the old town stay open late through summer; in winter the action moves indoors to the cafés around the square.

There is no particular cocktail scene — order a local rakija or a Vranac red and join in.
