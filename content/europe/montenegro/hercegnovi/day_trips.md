---
title: "Day Trips"
type: section
---

Herceg Novi makes a good base for the western Bay of Kotor.

**Kotor** is the obvious one — 30 minutes by the Kamenari ferry, an hour by road. A full day for the old town, the city walls and the fortress climb.

**Perast and the islets** can be combined with Kotor or done separately. The boat trip out to Our Lady of the Rocks is the headline.

**Dubrovnik** is about 90 minutes including the border crossing — feasible as a long day trip but better with an overnight if you can spare it.

**The Luštica peninsula** — Žanjic, the Blue Cave, the abandoned submarine pens at Rose, the village of Rose itself. Easiest as a boat tour from the Herceg Novi promenade.

**Mount Orjen** — hiking from Vrbanj village, the dramatic karst landscape, and the highest peaks in the region.
