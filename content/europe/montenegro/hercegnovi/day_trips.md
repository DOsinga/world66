Herceg Novi makes a good base for the western Bay of Kotor.

[Kotor](/europe/montenegro/kotor) is the obvious one — 30 minutes by the Kamenari ferry, an hour by road. A full day for the old town, the city walls and the fortress climb.

[Perast](/europe/montenegro/perast) and the islets can be combined with Kotor or done separately. The boat trip out to Our Lady of the Rocks is the headline.

[Dubrovnik](/europe/croatia/dubrovnik) is about 90 minutes including the border crossing — feasible as a long day trip but better with an overnight if you can spare it.

The [Luštica peninsula](/europe/montenegro/lustica) — [Žanjic](/europe/montenegro/hercegnovi/zanjic_beach), the [Blue Cave](/europe/montenegro/hercegnovi/blue_cave), the abandoned submarine pens at Rose, the village of Rose itself. Easiest as a boat tour from the Herceg Novi promenade.

[Mount Orjen](/europe/montenegro/hercegnovi/mount_orjen) — hiking from Vrbanj village, the dramatic karst landscape, and the highest peaks in the region.
