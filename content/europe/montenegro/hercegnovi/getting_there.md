---
title: "Getting There"
type: section
---

Herceg Novi sits in the far northwest corner of Montenegro, closer to Dubrovnik than to anywhere in its own country.

**By air.** The nearest airport is Tivat (TIV), about 25 km away across the bay; a taxi costs around €40 or you can take the ferry shortcut across the Verige strait. Dubrovnik (DBV) in Croatia is just over the border, about 40 km away, and often has cheaper flights — buses run to Herceg Novi several times a day, allow extra time for the border crossing. Podgorica airport is much further (around 110 km) and rarely the best option.

**By bus.** There are regular coastal services from Kotor (45 min), Budva (1h 30min), and Podgorica (3h). The bus station is at the western edge of town, with a steep walk down to the centre.

**By road.** The coastal road from the Croatian border at Debeli Brijeg passes straight through Igalo and into Herceg Novi. From Kotor you have two options: drive around the bay (about an hour) or take the Kamenari–Lepetane car ferry, which cuts the journey to 30 minutes and runs around the clock.
