Ivo Andrić's House gives Herceg Novi a literary stop beyond its fortresses and seaside promenades. The Nobel Prize-winning writer lived here with Milica Babić, and the house now functions as a small cultural site tied into the Topla area's museums, churches, and old lanes.

It is best approached as part of a slow walk through Topla rather than as a large stand-alone museum. Andrić's work belongs to the wider history of the former Yugoslavia, but the house also says something about Herceg Novi's appeal as a retreat for artists, diplomats, and people drawn to the Bay of Kotor's softer light.
