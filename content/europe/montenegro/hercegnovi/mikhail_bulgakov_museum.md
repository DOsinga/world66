The Mikhail Bulgakov Museum is a small literary museum in Herceg Novi devoted to the Russian writer's work. Opened in 2020, it displays props, decorative pieces, and other material connected with Bulgakov's stories and stage world.

It is best treated as a short stop while exploring the old town and nearby Forte Mare, especially for readers interested in Bulgakov or Russian literature.
