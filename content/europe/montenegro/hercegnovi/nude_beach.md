Nude Beach is a small pebble beach near the sea caves west of Herceg Novi, used by locals who prefer a more relaxed dress code than on the busier town beaches. The water and rocky setting are the reason to come.

Facilities are minimal: there are toilets at the western end, but little else. Bring what you need, wear water shoes if your feet dislike pebbles, and treat the low-key atmosphere with respect.
