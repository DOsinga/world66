*The Mountain Wreath* (1847) is Montenegro's national epic — a verse drama in decasyllabic lines describing the legendary 18th-century massacre of converts to Islam in Montenegro, sometimes called the *Istraga poturica*. The text is the foundational document of modern Montenegrin national identity, and its language echoes through political speeches, school texts, and casual conversation across the country.

It is also a difficult and morally compromised text — the violence at its centre has been used to justify later violence — and reading it is a way into the more uncomfortable questions about Balkan nationalism. The Vasa D. Mihailovich English verse translation is the standard. The author lies buried at the summit of Lovćen above Kotor.
