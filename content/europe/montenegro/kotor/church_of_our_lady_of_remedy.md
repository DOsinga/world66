The Church of Our Lady of Remedy stands on the steep trail above Kotor's old town, partway up the route toward the fortress. It was built in the 16th century by plague survivors in thanks to the Holy Mother.

The church is small, but the setting is the reason to climb: from here the view opens over Kotor's rooftops, the walls, and the bay below.
