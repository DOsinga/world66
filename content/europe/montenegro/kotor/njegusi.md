Njeguši is the small upland village on the inland side of the Lovćen massif, almost exactly halfway between Kotor and Cetinje. The cold dry air at 900 metres has made it the country's traditional centre for cured meats and cheese — *njeguški pršut* (smoked ham) and *njeguški sir* (a hard sheep's-milk cheese) are sold throughout Montenegro, but always taste better here.

Stop on the drive over Lovćen at one of the village's family-run *konobas* — Kod Pera Na Bukovicu is the long-running benchmark — for a plate of pršut, cheese, and homemade bread, with a glass of medovina (honey wine). The village itself is a couple of streets of stone houses around the small Petrović family church.
