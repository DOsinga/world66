Podgorica is a practical base for day trips into Montenegro's interior and coast. The old capital [Cetinje](/europe/montenegro/cetinje) is 40km south, easily reached by bus or taxi in 40 minutes, and has more historical monuments than Podgorica. Skadar Lake National Park starts just 30km south of the city — the largest lake in the Balkans, fringed by wetlands full of Dalmatian pelicans, cormorants, and herons. Rent a car or join a tour for the lake.

The Morača Canyon, a few kilometres north of the city on the main highway toward Kolašin, is dramatic and easily reached by car or taxi. The 13th-century Morača Monastery sits inside the canyon and is one of the finest examples of Raškan medieval architecture in Montenegro.
