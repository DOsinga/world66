Doclea, also known as Duklja or Dioclea, is the main ancient site near Podgorica: the remains of an Illyrian, Roman, and Byzantine city on the plain north of the modern capital. Walls, foundations, fragments of civic buildings, and early Christian remains survive in a landscape that feels surprisingly open for somewhere so close to town.

The ruins require imagination, but they add historical weight to a city many travellers otherwise treat as a transit point. Go in cooler hours, bring water, and consider reading up before arriving; there is enough on the ground to reward archaeology-minded visitors, but interpretation can be limited.
