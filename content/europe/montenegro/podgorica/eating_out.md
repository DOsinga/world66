---
title: "Eating Out"
type: section
---

Podgorica has a decent restaurant scene for a city of its size, with a strong emphasis on Montenegrin and Balkan food — grilled meats, fish from the Morača and Skadar Lake, and an increasingly confident wine culture based on the local Vranac red grape. The Stara Varoš quarter and the streets around Independence Square have the highest concentration of restaurants.
