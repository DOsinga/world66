Besac Fortress rises just above Virpazar, close enough to visit before or after a boat trip. The stone walls are partly restored, and the main pleasure is the view over the village, railway, channels, and the broader lake basin.

It is a good first stop because it explains the geography. From the ramparts you can see why Virpazar became the obvious lake gateway and why control of these routes mattered.
