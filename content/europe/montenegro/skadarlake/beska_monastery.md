Beska Monastery stands on Beska island in the southern part of Lake Skadar. It is one of the lake's monastic sites connected with the medieval Zeta world, and it gives longer boat trips a strong historical anchor.

This is not usually a casual short-loop stop from Virpazar. Ask specifically about monastery routes if island churches are high on your list, and dress respectfully if landing is part of the trip.
