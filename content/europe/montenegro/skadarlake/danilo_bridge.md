Danilo Bridge is the old stone bridge at Rijeka Crnojevica, the architectural marker that turns the riverside settlement into a postcard. It is small, handsome, and impossible to miss if you arrive in town by road.

The bridge works best as part of the whole Rijeka Crnojevica stop: river, boats, cafes, stone houses, and the slow bend of water heading toward Lake Skadar.
