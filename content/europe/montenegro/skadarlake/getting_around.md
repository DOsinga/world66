---
title: Getting Around
type: section
---

Boat trips from Virpazar are the standard way to see the reed beds, lilies, open water, and island ruins. Kayaks give a quieter experience, especially near the channels and birding areas, but conditions can change with wind.

A car is useful for Pavlova Strana, Rijeka Crnojevica, Godinje, and the wine villages. Drive carefully: the minor roads are narrow, unfenced in places, and often too scenic for impatient traffic.
