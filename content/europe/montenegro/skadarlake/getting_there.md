---
title: Getting There
type: section
---

Virpazar is the easiest gateway to Lake Skadar. It sits on the road and rail line between Podgorica and Bar, so you can reach it by train, bus, taxi, or car from either the capital or the coast.

Rijeka Crnojevica is better reached by car or arranged transfer from Cetinje, Podgorica, or Virpazar. The roads around the northern viewpoints are narrow and slow, which is part of the charm unless you are in a hurry.
