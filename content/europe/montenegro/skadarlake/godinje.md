Godinje is one of the old wine villages above Lake Skadar, a stone-and-vineyard settlement that makes the region feel Mediterranean rather than purely Balkan interior. It is a good detour if you have a car and want something slower than Virpazar's boat-trips-and-restaurants rhythm.

The village is best approached as atmosphere: lanes, old houses, lake views, and local wine rather than a list of monuments. Pair it with Grmozur or a southern lake drive.
