Grmozur is a small fortified islet near Godinje, ruined, atmospheric, and usually visited by boat. Birds now perch where guards once watched, which is a very Lake Skadar kind of transformation.

It is one of the best quick historical stops on a Virpazar boat route. Ask before booking if your trip passes close enough for a good look, because not every standard loop includes it.
