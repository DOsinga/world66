Kom Monastery sits on an island-like rise in the northern lake landscape, reachable by boat when water and routes allow. It is one of the clearest examples of how Lake Skadar combines nature with medieval Orthodox history.

The monastery is small, but the approach gives it weight. Reeds, water, birds, and distant hills make the religious architecture feel inseparable from the lake.
