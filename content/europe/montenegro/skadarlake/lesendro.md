Lesendro is a ruined island fortress near the causeway between Virpazar and Podgorica. It is often seen from boat trips or the road, its low walls sitting in the water like a reminder that Lake Skadar was once a frontier, not just a nature park.

You do not need to land to appreciate it. The best view is often from a boat, where the fortress, reeds, and mountains line up in a way that makes the lake's strategic importance obvious.
