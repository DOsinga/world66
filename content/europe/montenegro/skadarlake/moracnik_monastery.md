Moracnik Monastery occupies an island on the southern side of Lake Skadar. Like Beska, it belongs to the lake's chain of remote Orthodox sites, places that feel protected as much by water and distance as by walls.

It rewards travellers who want more than the standard Virpazar panorama. The lake is at its most interesting when these small islands, ruins, and monasteries interrupt the open water.
