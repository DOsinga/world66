Pavlova Strana is the famous horseshoe viewpoint above the Rijeka Crnojevica river arm. It is one of Montenegro's great roadside views: a green loop of water, steep hills, and the lake country opening beyond.

Go early or late if you can. Midday light flattens the scene, while sunrise and evening make the bends, reeds, and slopes much easier to read.
