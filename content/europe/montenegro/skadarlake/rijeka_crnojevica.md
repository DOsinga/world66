Rijeka Crnojevica is a small riverside town north of Lake Skadar, set where a calm river arm curls toward the lake. It is quieter and more romantic than Virpazar, with stone houses, waterside restaurants, and boat trips that start away from the busiest lake gateway.

The road in is narrow, but the reward is a different mood: less ticket-booth energy, more old Montenegro. It is especially good if you combine it with Pavlova Strana above the river bend.
