Virpazar is the practical gateway to Lake Skadar on the Montenegrin side. The village is small, but it concentrates boat operators, restaurants, guesthouses, information points, and the transport links that make the lake accessible without a complicated plan.

It can feel busy in summer when boat sellers gather around the bridge and square. Stay calm, compare routes, and choose a longer or quieter trip if you want more than the quick lily-and-reed circuit.
