Vranjina is a village and island-hill at the northern edge of Lake Skadar, linked to the mainland by the road and rail crossing. It is not as pretty as the classic lakeside brochures suggest, but it gives a good sense of the lake as working territory rather than pure scenery.

Use it as a pause if you are driving between Podgorica and Virpazar. The views, monastery traces, and water all around make it more interesting than a place this close to the highway has any right to be.
