---
title: When to Go
type: section
---

Spring is the richest season on Lake Skadar: water levels are high, the hills are green, and birdlife is active. Early summer brings water lilies and warm weather, but boat traffic increases around Virpazar.

July and August can be very hot, especially away from the breeze on open water. September and October are excellent for quieter trips, soft light, and combining the lake with the coast.
