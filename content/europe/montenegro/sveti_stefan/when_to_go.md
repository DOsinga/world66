---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

June and September are the best months. The water is warm, the beaches are busy but not overwhelmed, and prices are lower than peak season. July and August are hot, crowded, and expensive — the beaches fill by midday, accommodation books out well in advance, and the coastal road backs up with traffic from Budva.

May and early October are good for those who prefer quiet. The beaches are less busy, the light is beautiful, and you can walk the coastal path from Budva without the summer crush. The island looks magnificent at any time of year; the light in autumn especially flatters the stone architecture.

Winter is very quiet — most beach-facing cafes and restaurants close. The monastery and the viewpoint are worth visiting year-round if you happen to be in Montenegro off-season.
