Donja Lastva is the softer northern edge of Tivat, a shoreline settlement of stone houses, small jetties, and old gardens. It feels far removed from Porto Montenegro even though it is only a short walk or taxi ride away.

There is no single blockbuster sight. The point is the pace: walk the waterfront, look back toward the marina, and notice how quickly the bay becomes domestic again once the superyachts fall out of view.
