---
title: Getting Around
type: section
---

Central Tivat is easy on foot. Porto Montenegro, the Pine Embankment, Kalimanj harbour, and the town centre sit within a compact strip along the water.

For Donja Lastva, Gornja Lastva, the salt pans, and Luštica beaches, use local buses, taxis, or a car. Parking around Porto Montenegro can be tight and expensive in summer; outside the marina zone it is much simpler.
