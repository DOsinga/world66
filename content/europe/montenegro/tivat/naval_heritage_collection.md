The Naval Heritage Collection is the corrective to Porto Montenegro's polish. It preserves a slice of the town's naval history through photographs, equipment, ship models, and the submarine that has become the museum's signature exhibit.

It is small, but it gives the marina district a past. Visit before or after walking the quays so the transformation from arsenal to luxury waterfront feels less abstract.
