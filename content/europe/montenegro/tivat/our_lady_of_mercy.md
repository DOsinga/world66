Our Lady of Mercy is the smallest and most delicate of the Tivat Bay islands, with a monastery church occupying much of the islet. From shore it appears as a compact cluster of stone, cypress, and red roof against the water.

Access depends on local boat arrangements, so treat it as a boat-trip sight rather than a guaranteed stop. It combines well with the Island of Flowers and views toward Luštica.
