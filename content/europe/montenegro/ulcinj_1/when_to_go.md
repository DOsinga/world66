---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

Ulcinj's season runs from June through September. The Albanian community in town celebrates Eid and other Islamic holidays, which fall on different dates each year but add a particular energy to the town that distinguishes it from the rest of the Montenegrin coast. July and August are crowded and hot, with Velika Plaža filling up and accommodation prices rising, though still cheaper than Budva or Kotor.

May and June are excellent for visiting — the weather is warm, the beaches are quiet, and the wildflowers in the dunes south of town are at their best. September extends the season past the main summer rush. Winter is quiet and some restaurants close, but the old town and the beaches have a melancholy beauty in the off-season.
