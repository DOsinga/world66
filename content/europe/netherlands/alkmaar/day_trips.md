Alkmaar sits on a fast train line into Amsterdam and is well positioned for the rest of North Holland. **Amsterdam** itself is 35 minutes away by Intercity and obviously rewards a full day. **Haarlem**, half an hour south, is a quieter and arguably more attractive alternative for an old-town day. **Zaanse Schans**, the open-air windmill village, is best in the morning before tour groups arrive — change at Koog–Zaandijk.

Closer to home, the **dunes and beaches of the North Sea coast** — Egmond aan Zee, Bergen aan Zee, Castricum — are 20 minutes by bus or car. The **Hoorn**–**Enkhuizen** old fishing-town pairing on the Markermeer makes for a longer day east. For something quieter, take the bicycle path out through the polder villages of the Schermer, with their windmills and dykes.
