---
title: "Eating Out"
type: section
---

Alkmaar's restaurant scene is small but reasonable, clustered around the Waagplein, the Mient, and the Verdronkenoord canal. Dutch staples — *bitterballen*, pancakes, herring from the stalls — are on every menu. The traditional thing to eat here is **cheese**, fresh from the market: ask in the dairy shops around the Waagplein for unpasteurised farmhouse Gouda or aged *boerenkaas*, often available in small wedges to take away.

For the local sweet treat, look for **Alkmaarse jongens** — small soft mini-pancakes (poffertjes) served warm with butter and powdered sugar. Several stalls and cafés around the Waagplein serve them, and they go particularly well with a *kopstootje* (small beer and gin chaser) in the late afternoon.
