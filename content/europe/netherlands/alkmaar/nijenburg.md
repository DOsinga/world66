Nijenburg is a historic estate near Alkmaar, associated with the older country-house landscape around Heiloo. Its appeal is less about a single dramatic monument than about the combination of manor, woodland, lanes, and remnants of the defensive and aristocratic geography that once surrounded the city.

It is best for travelers who like quiet heritage walks and small Dutch estates. Pair it with Alkmaar's museums and canals if you want a break from the busy cheese-market core, or use it as a cycling stop through the greener villages south of town.
