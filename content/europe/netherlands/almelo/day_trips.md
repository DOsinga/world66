Almelo sits at the western edge of the Twente region, and its neighbours offer more in the way of museums, nightlife, and urban energy. [Enschede](/europe/netherlands/enschede), 20 minutes by train, is the cultural capital of Twente — home to the Rijksmuseum Twenthe and the open-air Openluchtmuseum Ootmarsum nearby. [Hengelo](/europe/netherlands/hengelo) is a short hop in the other direction and has a lively centre. The German border town of Nordhorn, reachable by bike along the canal, makes an unusual half-day excursion.
