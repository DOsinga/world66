The old weigh house on the main shopping street is one of Almelo's most recognisable historic buildings. Built in the 17th century to weigh goods traded at the market, it has since been converted into a restaurant. Even if you're not eating, the façade is worth a look — a reminder that Almelo was once a genuine trading town before textiles took over.
