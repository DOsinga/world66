The regional archive and history centre for Overijssel holds extensive records and exhibits on the province's history, including the textile industry that made Almelo prosperous in the 19th and 20th centuries. For anyone interested in local genealogy or the industrial heritage of eastern Netherlands, it is an unexpectedly rich resource. The building itself is in the historic centre, within easy walking distance of the Grote Kerk.
