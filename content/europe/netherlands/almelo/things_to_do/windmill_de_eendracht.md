One of the few remaining windmills in Almelo, De Eendracht (meaning "unity") is a working mill that has survived the city's industrial transformation largely intact. It stands as a counterpoint to the textile factories that once dominated the skyline — a reminder of the agricultural economy that preceded them. Open on certain Saturdays when the sails turn.
