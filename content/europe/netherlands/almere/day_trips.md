Almere's best feature as a base is that almost everywhere in the central Netherlands is within an hour by train. **Amsterdam** is 25 minutes away on the direct intercity and makes the obvious day trip — but it is worth getting on a slow stoptrein and exploring the small towns and lakes around the IJsselmeer instead.

**Lelystad**, 15 minutes north, holds the Nieuw Land national park visitor centre, the Bataviawerf shipyard (with the reconstructed 17th-century VOC ship *Batavia*), and the Aviodrome aviation museum. **Marken** and **Volendam**, the much-photographed former Zuiderzee fishing villages, are reached by bus across the Gooimeer; **Naarden-Vesten**, with its perfectly preserved star-shaped fortifications, is half an hour by car.
