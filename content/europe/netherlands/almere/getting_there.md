---
title: "Getting There"
type: section
---

Almere is on the main Amsterdam–Lelystad railway line, with **four intercity stations** (Almere-Poort, Almere-Muziekwijk, Almere-Centrum and Almere-Buiten) and two more local stops. **Trains from Amsterdam Centraal** run every ten minutes and take 20–25 minutes; the fast intercity from Amsterdam-Zuid via the Hanzelijn reaches Almere-Poort in 17 minutes. Direct trains continue to Utrecht, Lelystad, Zwolle and Groningen.

By car the city is on the **A6 motorway** between Amsterdam (30 minutes) and Lelystad (15 minutes), with the **A27** branching off south towards Utrecht. **Schiphol Airport** is 40 minutes by train via Amsterdam-Zuid; **Lelystad Airport** (small regional flights only) is 20 minutes by car. There is no long-distance bus station — Flixbus stops at Amsterdam Sloterdijk instead.
