The **Yanmar Stadion** (still informally known as the Almere City Stadion) is the home ground of **Almere City FC**, the city's professional football club. The club spent two decades grinding through the lower divisions before being promoted to the Eredivisie — the top tier of Dutch football — in 2023, an event the city celebrated with a long week of orange flags.

The 4,500-seat stadium is small but unusually intimate for an Eredivisie ground, and the supporters' end is loud. Tickets are easy to come by; the away clubs that visit are some of the most famous names in Dutch football. Located in Almere-Buiten, a short walk from Almere Buiten railway station.
