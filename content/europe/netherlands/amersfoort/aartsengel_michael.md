This mosaic of the Archangel Michael accompanying two children is one of the less-visited pieces of public art in Amersfoort — sitting on the wall of a residential building in the Soesterkwartier rather than in the tourist centre. Michael is the patron saint of soldiers and protectors, and the image has a quietly devotional quality at odds with its suburban setting.

The mosaic dates from the mid-20th century, when religious imagery in public space was still common in Dutch Catholic neighbourhoods. It is easy to walk past without registering what it is — which makes pausing in front of it feel like a small discovery.
