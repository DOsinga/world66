Placed in 2011 as part of the redevelopment of the station district, this steel sculpture captures two interlocking upward forms — the kind of civic public art commissioned to signal that a regenerated neighbourhood means business. The title is characteristically earnest: ambition and dynamics were exactly what the planners wanted to project about the new Amersfoort Centraal area.

It works better than most of its genre. The forms have genuine tension in them, and they catch the light differently depending on the angle. Worth a second look if you are waiting for a train.
