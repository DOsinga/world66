Explore Amersfoort's remarkably intact medieval streetplan on foot with a local guide. The route takes in the double-arched Koppelpoort, the circle of *muurhuizen* (houses built into the old city wall), and the soaring tower of the Onze-Lieve-Vrouwetoren — all within the compact old centre.

Departures are flexible; groups of two to ten welcome.
