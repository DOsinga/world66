Amersfoort's identity as a city of "kei" (boulder) collectors runs deep. After the Amersfoortse Kei legend made the city famous across the Netherlands for dragging a giant boulder into town, the city embraced the identity and began collecting stones donated by partner cities and regions across Europe. The Belgian stone is one of the earliest in this collection.

There are two Belgian stones in different parts of the city — possibly representing different Belgian partner relationships at different times, or a renegotiation of which Belgian city claimed the pairing. The stones are unassuming objects, but standing next to one you are participating in a civic joke that has been running for 350 years.
