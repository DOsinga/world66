Amersfoort was liberated on 7 May 1945 by the 5th Canadian Armoured Division. The Canadian stone in the Keien collection is one of the few that carries a specific historical weight beyond civic partnership: it marks the end of the German occupation and the arrival of the troops that ended five years of war in this city.

The stone was donated as part of the post-war exchange of friendship gestures between Amersfoort and Canada. The Netherlands has maintained a particularly strong bond with Canada since 1945 — Canadian tulip fields near Ottawa grow from bulbs originally sent as a thank-you by the Dutch royal family — and this stone is a small part of that relationship.
