Chokogin is one of those public sculptures that resists explanation. The name appears to be invented — it has no meaning in Dutch, English, or any obvious other language — and the form is abstract enough to support multiple readings. It was placed in 2017 near the station as part of the ongoing art programme around the Amersfoort Centraal redevelopment zone.

The lack of obvious narrative is part of the point. Not every piece of public art is meant to teach you something or commemorate someone. Sometimes a shape in space is just a shape in space.
