Femkes is a diminutive form used in Dutch dialects and Frisian for young women or girls — affectionate rather than patronising. The three figures here have the quality of a conversation or a greeting, the kind of everyday social moment that street art captures well when it is working properly.

The mural is in the figurative tradition that has dominated Dutch public wall art since the 1970s: representational, human-scale, aiming to bring warmth to a blank wall without demanding that you interpret it. It succeeds on those terms.
