The koperwiek is a fieldfare, a large thrush that passes through the Netherlands in huge flocks every October and November on its way south from Scandinavia. They descend on berry-laden hedgerows and are one of the more striking wildlife events in the Dutch autumn — noisy, gregarious, and present in enormous numbers for a few weeks before moving on.

This bronze statue at the edge of the Vathorst district takes the bird as an emblem for a neighbourhood that did not exist until the early 2000s: new, passing through, establishing itself. The sculpture is unusually naturalistic for a piece of civic public art.
