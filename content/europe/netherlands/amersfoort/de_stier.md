Before it became a park, the land around the Schutterspark area was tied to Amersfoort's role as a regional market town. Cattle markets were a major feature of Dutch provincial life, and a bull is the obvious emblem. This 2007 bronze is a straightforward piece of memorial realism — the kind that simply shows you what was here before.

The park itself occupies land outside the old city walls. The contrast between the historic fortifications visible from here and the placid grazing-scale of the bull gives the spot an accidental poetry.
