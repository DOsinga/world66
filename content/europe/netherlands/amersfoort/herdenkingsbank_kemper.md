Christine de Bosch Kemper (1836–1906) was one of the most significant Dutch social reformers of the 19th century. She worked on poverty relief, prison reform, and the position of women in Dutch society at a time when all three were considered outside the proper sphere of female concern. She co-founded organisations for women's education and was a central figure in the early Dutch women's movement.

The memorial bench is a modest tribute to someone whose work laid foundations that are now so embedded in Dutch social life that most people have forgotten anyone had to argue for them. The bench invites you to sit and think — which is, perhaps, exactly the right monument for someone who spent her life asking people to pay attention to things they preferred to ignore.
