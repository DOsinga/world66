Most of Amersfoort's Keien come from European partner cities abroad, which makes the Hilversumse Kei unusual: Hilversum is 25 kilometres away, across the Gooi — the same sandy ridge landscape that runs west from Amersfoort. Both cities sit on the elevated moraine country above the polder, and they share a geological identity even if they have very different characters (Hilversum is the Dutch media capital; Amersfoort is the military and religious centre of the Gelderse Vallei).

A stone between two Dutch cities that are not technically sister cities is an odd gift, but then the whole Keien tradition is slightly odd, which is the point.
