The Italian stone sits among the European cluster near the Hof. Italy's connection to the Netherlands in the early modern period was substantial: the Habsburg connection brought the Low Countries under Spanish-Italian imperial administration, Dutch merchants traded extensively with Italian city-states, and Dutch painters learned from — and competed with — Italian Renaissance masters.

Amersfoort itself had a small Italian community in the 17th century, merchants and craftsmen who followed the trade routes north. The stone acknowledges a relationship that predates the formal partner-city framework by several centuries.
