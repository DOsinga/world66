Kunsthal KAdE is Amersfoort's exhibition space for modern art, architecture, design, and contemporary culture. It has no fixed collection, so visits depend on the temporary show in place.

The museum is close to the old centre and Koppelpoort, making it a useful indoor stop for travellers who want something more current than Amersfoort's medieval streets and regional history museums.
