"Maak me af, maak me compleet" — finish me, complete me — is the kind of title that works better the longer you sit with it. Is the figure speaking? Is the artist speaking about the work? Is the artwork speaking to the viewer? The title refuses to answer, and that refusal is the point.

The sculpture appears in two closely placed versions (there are two OSM nodes within a few metres of each other), which may reflect a two-part installation, or simply a documentation quirk. Either way, the instruction in the title is a genuine one: what you bring to it is part of what it is.
