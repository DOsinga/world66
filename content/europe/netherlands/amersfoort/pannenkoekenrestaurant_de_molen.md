A traditional Dutch pancake restaurant near the Koppelpoort, serving both savoury and sweet pannekoeken in the time-honoured fashion. Choose from dozens of toppings — smoked bacon and stroop is the classic, but they also do cheese, mushroom, and apple variants. Relaxed and family-friendly, and genuinely good value. A proper Dutch lunch that costs little and fills you up for the afternoon.
