Reebok is the Dutch word for a roe deer (Capreolus capreolus) — the small, solitary deer that lives in woodland edges and agricultural margins across the Netherlands. They were once common in the wooded ridge country east and south of Amersfoort before urban expansion pushed them further out.

The sportswear brand borrowed the name in the 1950s via a misspelling of rhebok, a South African antelope. This bronze, placed near the station, predates that association entirely and refers simply to the animal that used to graze in the fields where the station now stands.
