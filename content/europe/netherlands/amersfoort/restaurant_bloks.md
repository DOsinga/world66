A well-regarded modern Dutch bistro in the city centre, with a menu that changes with the seasons and a preference for local producers. The cooking is confident without being showy — expect dishes built around Dutch vegetables and meat, put together with care. It's the kind of place where the staff know the provenance of every dish. Booking ahead at weekends is advisable.
