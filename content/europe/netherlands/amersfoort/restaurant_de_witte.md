A dependable brasserie on Amersfoort's main square, open for lunch and dinner and good for both. The menu runs through Dutch and French classics — erwtensoep in winter, grilled fish in summer — with a terrace that has prime views of the Hof. The kitchen is consistent and the wine list is sensibly priced. A good choice if you want something a step up from a broodje but don't need a special-occasion restaurant.
