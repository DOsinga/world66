This is the Roman Catholic half of the pair on 't Zand, the church of the parish of Onze Lieve Vrouw van Amersfoort. It belongs to the era when Dutch Catholics, after two centuries of worshipping in hidden churches, could finally build in the open again.

Its best story is a man, not a building. From 1875 to 1892 the chaplain here was Willem van Rootselaar. In his day job he became Amersfoort's very first city archivist and founded the Flehite antiquarian society — the people who collected, catalogued and rescued the town's past. A lot of what this city knows about itself, it knows because a parish priest on 't Zand cared enough to write it down.
