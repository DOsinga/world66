Look carefully on 't Zand and you'll find two Catholic churches almost within sight of each other. This one — the Heilige Georgiuskerk, "Saint George on the Sand" — is not Roman Catholic. It is *Old* Catholic.

The Old Catholics are one of Holland's stranger religious footnotes. Their church broke with Rome back in the 18th century over a bitter theological quarrel, and then, when Rome declared the Pope infallible in 1870, they refused to accept it and dug in for good. They kept the Mass, the bishops and the saints — just not the Pope.

So here, a short walk apart, stand a Roman Catholic and an Old Catholic parish, both dedicated to the same Saint George, both still holding services. A quiet, very Dutch monument to the idea that you can disagree with the head office and still keep the shop open.
