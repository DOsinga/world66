The Sint Pieters en Bloklands Gasthuis has been providing care for the elderly and the poor in Amersfoort since the 13th century, making it one of the oldest continuously functioning charitable institutions in the Netherlands. The name combines two foundations — the Sint Pieters Gasthuis and the Bloklands Gasthuis — that merged over time, and the current building complex on the Westsingel retains elements from various periods of its long history.

"Gasthuis" means guesthouse — these were not hospitals in the modern sense but rather charitable houses where the sick, the elderly, the poor, and travellers could be housed and fed. The religious orders that ran them understood care as a form of prayer; the physical act of looking after the helpless was itself devotion.

The institution still functions as a care facility, which means it has been in continuous operation for over seven hundred years. The buildings have changed; the function has not. That is a remarkable thread of continuity to stand next to.
