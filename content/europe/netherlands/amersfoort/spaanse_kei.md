The Dutch Republic was created by fighting Spain for eighty years (1568–1648). The war began as a revolt against Habsburg tax policy and the Duke of Alba's brutal suppression of religious dissent; it ended with the Peace of Westphalia and formal Spanish recognition of Dutch independence. Amersfoort, as a garrison town, played a military role in that conflict.

A stone from Spain sitting among the Keien collection is therefore the most historically loaded item in the whole set. That two countries can place a boulder in each other's squares 350 years after the war that defined one of them is either a measure of how thoroughly history has been processed, or a reminder of how little anyone thinks about this stuff anymore. Probably both.
