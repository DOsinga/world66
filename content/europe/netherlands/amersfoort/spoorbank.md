Amersfoort is one of the busiest rail junctions in the Netherlands — almost every train between the Randstad and the north or east passes through here. The station has six platforms and sees around 500 train movements per day. For a city of 160,000 people, that is a remarkable amount of rail infrastructure.

The Spoorbank ("railway bench") places you in that context: a seat that is also a statement about waiting, movement, and the particular quality of station life. The bench was placed as part of the station-area public art programme in 2000, when the square outside Amersfoort Centraal was being redesigned.
