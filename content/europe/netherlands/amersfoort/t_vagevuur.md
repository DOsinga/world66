Vagevuur means purgatory — the Catholic concept of a state between death and heaven where sins are purged before the soul can ascend. The building got this name because it served as a kind of civic holding area: debtors and minor offenders awaiting their hearing at the nearby court were held here in a state neither free nor imprisoned, suspended between one life and another.

The house dates from around 1750 and is one of the better-preserved 18th-century domestic buildings near the Hof. The name stuck long after the function disappeared.
