Sweden and the Dutch Republic were allies during the Thirty Years' War (1618–1648). The Peace of Westphalia, which ended that war and recognised Dutch independence, was partly brokered by Swedish military success against the Habsburgs. The two Protestant maritime nations had overlapping interests in limiting Habsburg power.

Swedish granite is among the hardest and most durable stone in Europe — the bedrock that survived multiple ice ages. A Swedish stone in a Dutch city square is an appropriately solid gesture from a country that has been exporting its geology to the rest of Europe since the last glaciation.
