Amstelveen is a prosperous suburb south of [Amsterdam](/europe/netherlands/amsterdam), close to Schiphol Airport. It is not a tourist destination in itself, but it has appeal for visitors staying near the airport or looking for a quieter base than central Amsterdam. The [old village centre](/europe/netherlands/amstelveen/old_village) has been converted largely into restaurants — Argentinian, Indian, French, Italian — and the [Stadshart](/europe/netherlands/amstelveen/stadshart) shopping area is modern and well-stocked.

The town has several pleasant parks, including the [Jac. P. Thijssepark](/europe/netherlands/amstelveen/thijssepark) (a botanical garden arranged by habitat) and access to the vast [Amsterdamse Bos](/europe/netherlands/amstelveen/amsterdamse_bos) forest. The [Cobra Museum of Modern Art](/europe/netherlands/amstelveen/cobra_museum), dedicated to the post-war CoBrA movement, and the pop venue [P60](/europe/netherlands/amstelveen/p60) are the main cultural draws. Amstelveen has a notably large Japanese expat community, reflected in Japanese restaurants and shops.
