The Cobra Museum of Modern Art is the only museum in the world dedicated to the CoBrA movement, the post-war collective whose members included Karel Appel, Corneille, and Constant. The permanent collection spans the work of all the leading figures, from the manic Appel canvases of the early 1950s to the more subdued later work of Constant.

The building, designed by Wim Quist and opened in 1995, is a calm modernist structure of glass and pale brick on the edge of the Stadshart shopping area. Temporary exhibitions cover related figures and broader themes in twentieth-century European art. A small café and museum shop are part of the visit.
