P60 is Amstelveen's main music venue, a mid-sized pop and rock club in the Stadshart with a capacity of around 750. It is part of the small national network of subsidised pop venues and books a mix of touring international acts, Dutch bands, and tribute and cover nights.

Programming runs the full range from indie and metal to hip-hop and electronic. The acoustics are good for a venue of this size, and tickets are reliably cheaper than for equivalent shows in central Amsterdam.
