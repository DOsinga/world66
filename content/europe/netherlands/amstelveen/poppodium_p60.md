Poppodium P60 is Amstelveen's principal live-music venue, hosting concerts, club nights, and cultural events near the Stadshart shopping and transport hub. It gives the suburb a real evening destination of its own rather than pushing every night out toward Amsterdam.

It is only worth planning around if the programme suits you, but that is exactly how to use it: check the calendar, then pair a show with dinner or drinks nearby. For visitors staying in Amstelveen, it can be a convenient, low-effort way to catch Dutch and touring acts outside the capital's busier venues.
