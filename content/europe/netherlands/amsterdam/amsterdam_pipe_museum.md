The Amsterdam Pipe Museum displays a specialist collection of smoking pipes inside a 17th-century canal house near Leidseplein. The collection spans thousands of years of pipe design and is usually seen on a guided visit, so it suits travellers interested in small, unusual museums rather than the city's headline art collections.
