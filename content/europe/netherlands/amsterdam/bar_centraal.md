Bar Centraal is the second venture from the team behind GlouGlou, one of Amsterdam's pioneering natural-wine bars, and it has built a reputation to match — voted Wine Bar of the Year by several Dutch wine-bar guides since opening. The list is all natural and low-intervention wine, paired with a dinner menu that changes weekly and draws on a range of international influences rather than sticking to one cuisine.

It sits near the corner of Ten Katestraat and Bellamystraat, a short walk from Ten Katemarkt — easy to fold into an evening that starts with the market and De Hallen.
