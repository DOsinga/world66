Carré Theatre is a historic Amsterdam theatre opened in 1887 as a permanent circus building. Its riverside location and grand interior make it more interesting than a routine performance venue.

The programme ranges from musicals and cabaret to concerts. Even if you are not seeing a show, it is worth noticing while walking between the Amstel and the Plantage area.
