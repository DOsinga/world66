Amsterdam is an excellent base for the wider Netherlands — the train and bus network puts most of the country within an easy day's reach.

[Haarlem](/europe/netherlands/haarlem) is just 20 minutes by train, with a beautiful old centre and less than a tenth of Amsterdam's tourist traffic. North of the city, the open-air museum of [De Zaanse Schans](/europe/netherlands/dezaanseschans) preserves working windmills half an hour away, while the old Zuiderzee fishing towns of [Volendam](/europe/netherlands/volendam) and [Edam](/europe/netherlands/edam) — the latter famous for its cheese — sit a short bus ride to the northeast. Further north, [Hoorn](/europe/netherlands/hoorn) and [Enkhuizen](/europe/netherlands/enkhuizen) anchor the "Historic Triangle", linked in summer by heritage steam train and vintage boat.

South of Amsterdam lie the Golden Age cities: [Leiden](/europe/netherlands/leiden) with its canals and university, [Delft](/europe/netherlands/delft) of the blue-and-white pottery, the seat of government at [The Hague](/europe/netherlands/thehague), and the canal-laced cathedral city of [Utrecht](/europe/netherlands/utrecht), all under 45 minutes away. In spring, the tulip gardens of [Keukenhof](/europe/netherlands/keukenhof) near Lisse (late March to mid-May) are worth the trip.
