The Electrische Museumtramlijn Amsterdam runs vintage electric trams from the old Haarlemmermeerstation in Amsterdam-Zuid. On operating days, heritage trams follow a museum line toward Amstelveen, with stops along the former railway route.

It is best treated as a moving transport museum rather than a fast way across town. The ride suits visitors interested in Amsterdam's tram history, families, and anyone looking for a slower excursion beyond the canal-ring museums.
