Four floors of photography in a canal house on the Keizersgracht, covering documentary, fashion, fine art, and experimental photography. The programme rotates regularly and tends to be interesting rather than safe: Foam has shown Diane Arbus, Viviane Sassen, and Edward Weston alongside emerging Dutch photographers. The building alone is worth the entrance fee — interconnected 17th-century rooms with original beams overhead. Keizersgracht 609.
