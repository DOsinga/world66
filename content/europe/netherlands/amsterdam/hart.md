H'ART is the museum in the former Hermitage Amsterdam building on the Amstel. After cutting ties with the Hermitage in St Petersburg, it reopened with a new identity and a focus on exhibitions connected to Dutch and international heritage.

The building and riverside setting make it an easy pairing with the Plantage district's other museums.
