Hendrix is an all-day café-bar on De Clercqstraat, one of the streets running perpendicular to Kinkerstraat that have filled with restaurants and bars over the past decade. The menu runs the full span of the day — breakfast and brunch in the morning, a kitchen that stretches to dishes like Korean fried chicken buns and pita rendang by evening, and a full bar later on.

The room is large and the terrace gets the afternoon sun, which makes it one of the more reliable neighbourhood spots to sit for a few hours without committing to a single meal or drink.
