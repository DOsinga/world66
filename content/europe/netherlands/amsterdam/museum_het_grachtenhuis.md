Museum Het Grachtenhuis explains how Amsterdam's Canal District was planned, financed, dug, and filled with the houses that now define the city. The multimedia displays are aimed at visitors who want context before wandering the canals.

It is a good first stop in the Canal Ring. After seeing how deliberate the 17th-century expansion was, the symmetry of the streets, bridges, and merchant houses becomes much easier to read on foot.
