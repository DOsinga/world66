Museum Van Loon is a 17th-century canal house with its rooms restored to the feel of an 18th-century patrician home. The visit is less about a single masterpiece than about seeing how Amsterdam's wealthy canal families lived: portraits, period furniture, painted ceilings, and a garden hidden from the street.
