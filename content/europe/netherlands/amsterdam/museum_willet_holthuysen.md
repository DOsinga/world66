Museum Willet-Holthuysen is a furnished canalside patrician house, open as a museum rather than preserved as a facade. Its rooms show how Amsterdam's wealthy families lived, with silverware, porcelain, books, art, and period interiors from the Dutch Golden Age and later.

It is a good fit if you like house museums: intimate, detailed, and easier to take in than the city's blockbuster collections.
