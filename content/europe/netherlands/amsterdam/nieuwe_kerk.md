Nieuwe Kerk stands beside the Royal Palace on Dam Square and has hosted Dutch royal inaugurations and weddings. It is no longer used as a regular parish church; instead, the Gothic interior serves as a major exhibition space.

The church is most interesting for its royal role, changing exhibitions, and position at the heart of Amsterdam's old civic center.
