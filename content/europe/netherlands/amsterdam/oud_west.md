Oud-West (Old West) lies immediately west of Vondelpark and south of the Jordaan — close enough to both to feel their influence but distinct enough to have its own character. It is one of Amsterdam's most pleasant residential neighbourhoods for daily life: less polished than the canal ring, less touristy than the Jordaan, with a high density of independent shops, good food, and streets where people actually live.

Kinkerstraat and Overtoom are the main commercial arteries. [De Hallen](/europe/netherlands/amsterdam/de_hallen), a converted tram depot on Kinkerstraat, holds a food hall and weekend flea market. [Ten Katemarkt](/europe/netherlands/amsterdam/ten_katemarkt), running Monday to Saturday, is the neighbourhood market — smaller and more local than the Albert Cuypmarkt but consistently good for fresh produce, cheese, and street food. The streets perpendicular to Kinkerstraat — Bellamystraat, Da Costakade, De Clercqstraat — have seen more restaurants and bars open in the last decade than almost anywhere else in the city: [Bar Centraal](/europe/netherlands/amsterdam/bar_centraal), a natural-wine bar near Ten Katemarkt with a weekly-changing menu, and [Hendrix](/europe/netherlands/amsterdam/hendrix), an all-day café-bar on De Clercqstraat, are two of the better anchors.

The neighbourhood was built in the late 19th and early 20th century and has a more architecturally consistent character than some of the more randomly developed inner city districts — the housing blocks are solid, the street widths generous, and the canal crossings frequent. It lacks the architectural drama of the canal ring but compensates with a sense of lived-in normalcy that is harder to find in Amsterdam's more photographed areas.

[Westerpark](/europe/netherlands/amsterdam/westerpark), at the northern edge, is a green space wrapped around the former Westergasfabriek (gas factory), whose ornate brick industrial buildings now house bars, restaurants, cinemas, and cultural spaces. The Westerpark neighbourhood has merged practically with Oud-West — the park is the shared backyard, and the restaurants in the old gasworks are among Amsterdam's most atmospheric.
