Pacific Parc occupies one of the Westergasfabriek's converted industrial buildings, a high-ceilinged room with salvaged furniture and an industrial-nautical feel that's been a Westerpark fixture for years. By day it functions as a relaxed bar and kitchen; in the evenings, especially in summer, the outdoor terrace facing the park fills up and stays loud and busy into the night, sometimes with DJs.

It's one of the more dependable spots in the complex for a drink before or after a film at Het Ketelhuis or a wander through the park.
