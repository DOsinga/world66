The Pianola Museum is a small Jordaan museum devoted to player pianos from the early 1900s. About a dozen instruments are usually on display, with rolls, mechanisms, and musical ephemera showing how these self-playing pianos worked before recorded music became ordinary.

It is a niche stop, but a good one if you like mechanical music, old interiors, or Amsterdam's smaller specialist museums. The museum also holds regular piano concerts.
