Space Expo is a visitor centre and exhibition space near Noordwijk devoted to space flight. Displays cover satellites, astronauts, launch technology, and the work of the European Space Agency.

It is one of the more substantial science stops on the Dutch coast, especially for families or anyone interested in space engineering.
