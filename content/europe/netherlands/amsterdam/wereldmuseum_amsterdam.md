Wereldmuseum Amsterdam is an ethnographic and cultural museum in Oost, with collections from Africa, Asia, the Americas, and Oceania. Its exhibitions focus on art, material culture, and the connections between everyday objects and wider histories.

The museum is a strong choice when you want a major collection outside the busiest museum quarter. It also pairs well with Oosterpark, Dappermarkt, and other stops in Amsterdam-Oost.
