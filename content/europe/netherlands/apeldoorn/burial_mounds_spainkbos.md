The Spainkbos is a modest woodland, but it hides four prehistoric burial mounds from the Neolithic and Bronze Age. They are part of the wider Veluwe landscape, where hundreds of ancient graves survive among the forests and heath.

This is a low-key stop rather than a headline attraction. It is best for travellers who like quiet archaeological sites and want to add a short historic detour to a walk around Apeldoorn.
