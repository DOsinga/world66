*Europe > Netherlands > Arcen*

Arcen is a small Limburg village on the Maas, north of Venlo and close to the German border. Its centre is pretty enough for a wander, but the reason to come is the cluster around it: castle gardens, beer, thermal water, and the Maasduinen landscape.

The [Castle Gardens](/europe/netherlands/arcen/castle_gardens_arcen) are the headline, especially from spring through autumn. [Hertog Jan Brewery](/europe/netherlands/arcen/hertog_jan_brewery) gives the village a second, very Dutch reason to stop, and the riverfront keeps the day from feeling like a set of ticketed attractions.

Arcen is not a must-cross-the-country destination. It is a good Limburg day if you are already near Venlo, the Maas, or the German border and want something gentler than another city centre.
