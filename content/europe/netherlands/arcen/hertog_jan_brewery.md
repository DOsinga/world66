Hertog Jan Brewery gives Arcen a reason to interest travelers who would normally skip a small river village. Tours explain the brewing process and finish with the kind of tasting that makes Limburg's beer culture feel less abstract.

Book ahead, especially on weekends. The brewery sits outside the village centre, so treat it as a planned stop rather than something to drift into after the gardens.
