Airborne at the Bridge is a small information centre overlooking the John Frost Bridge, the key objective in the Battle of Arnhem. Displays, film, and staff explanations set the bridge and river crossing in the wider story of Operation Market Garden.

It is a natural first stop before walking the bridge itself, especially if you do not have time to visit the larger Airborne Museum Hartenstein in Oosterbeek.
