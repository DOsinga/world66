De Gruyterpassage is a short covered pedestrian passage in central Arnhem, built in 1916 in Art Nouveau style. It began as a grocery store, later declined, and was restored and reopened as part of a shopping complex.

It is a brief stop rather than a destination in itself, but the tiled details and old commercial architecture make it worth a look while walking through the centre.
