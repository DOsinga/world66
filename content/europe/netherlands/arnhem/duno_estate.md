Duno Estate is a wooded former summer estate near the Rhine, close to Doorwerth and Oosterbeek. Its oldest feature is the Hunnenschans, a tenth-century circular earthwork that still reads clearly in the landscape.

The estate works best as a quiet walk rather than a single monument. Come for the mix of forest paths, river views, and the sense of older defensive history hidden in the trees.
