Kasteel Rosendael is a country estate just east of Arnhem, with a medieval core, later noble interiors, and parkland that makes it more than a quick photo stop. The castle was damaged during the Second World War and restored afterward, so the visit is partly about survival as well as aristocratic display.

The gardens are the best reason to linger. Their old water features, shell gallery, and wooded paths make Rosendael an easy half-day outing from Arnhem, especially if you want a quieter alternative to the city's larger museums.
