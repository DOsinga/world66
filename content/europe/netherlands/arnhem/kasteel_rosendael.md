Kasteel Rosendael is a historic castle and estate just outside Arnhem, with origins in the medieval Duchy of Guelders. The house and grounds were rebuilt after wartime damage, and the visit combines castle rooms, landscaped gardens, and estate features that make it more than a quick photo stop.
