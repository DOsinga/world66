The Nederlands Wijnmuseum is an unexpectedly deep dive into wine culture in a country better known for beer and jenever. Set around the old Robbers & van den Hoogen wine cellars, it covers importing, bottling, tasting and the Dutch role in the European wine trade.

It is a small, specialist museum, best for travellers who enjoy food history or want a rainy-day stop away from Arnhem's better-known war and open-air museums. Tastings and cellar visits are the reason to come, so check opening arrangements before crossing town.
