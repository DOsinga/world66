Stadsblokken-Meinerswijk is Arnhem's river landscape on the south side of the Rhine, a mix of floodplain nature, grazing areas, paths, and traces of older military and industrial use. It is close to the city but feels open and informal, shaped by water levels rather than by polished park design.

The area is good for a low-key walk or cycle ride, especially if you want to see Arnhem beyond its museums and shopping streets. Expect rougher paths, river views, birds, and a sense of how Dutch cities negotiate space with the water beside them.
