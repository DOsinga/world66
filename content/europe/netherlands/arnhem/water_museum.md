The Water Museum in Arnhem is an interactive museum about water, rivers, dikes, drinking water, and
the Dutch habit of engineering a country around wet ground. It is set near Sonsbeek Park, which
makes the visit feel connected to the landscape rather than sealed inside a classroom.

Families get the most from the hands-on displays, but adults interested in the Netherlands'
relationship with water will find useful context too. Combine it with a walk through Sonsbeek for an
easy green half-day in Arnhem.
