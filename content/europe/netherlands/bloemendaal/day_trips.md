**Haarlem** is the obvious next stop — five minutes by train, a beautifully preserved Dutch town with the Grote Kerk, the Frans Hals Museum, and an excellent restaurant scene. **Zandvoort**, the next station along the line, is the other big beach destination on this coast, with the F1 circuit on its northern edge.

**IJmuiden** to the north has the busy ferry harbour and a long pier worth a walk; the Tata Steel works behind it are an unexpected industrial backdrop. **Amsterdam** is twenty minutes by direct train and needs no introduction.
