A contemporary museum dedicated to graphic design in all its forms -- posters, digital printing, typography, colour theory. It hosts workshops and seminars alongside its permanent and rotating exhibitions. One of the few museums in the world devoted entirely to the discipline.

The permanent collection spans over a century of Dutch and international graphic design, from Art Nouveau posters to contemporary digital work. Breda's status as a design city -- the city hosts the annual Graphic Design Festival -- makes this museum feel at home here rather than random. Worth a visit even if graphic design is not usually your thing; the permanent collection is surprisingly engaging.
