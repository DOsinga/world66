Sint-Antoniuskathedraal is Breda's 19th-century cathedral church, set on a side street close to the old centre. It is less imposing than the Grote Kerk, but its neoclassical form and role as the cathedral of the Roman Catholic Diocese of Breda make it worth a short look while walking between the market square and the city's museums.
