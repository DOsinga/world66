The Fairy Tale Forest is the oldest and most atmospheric part of Efteling, a walk-through woodland of animated scenes, castles, cottages, talking trees, and European storybook characters. It is gentler than the park's roller coasters, but it explains why Efteling feels different from many theme parks: the fantasy begins with folklore rather than speed.

Take it slowly, especially with children. Some scenes are quaint, some odd, and a few have the slightly eerie mood that old fairy tales should have. Even adults racing between headline rides should make time for the forest, because it is the heart of the park's identity.
