---
title: Bars and Cafes
type: section
---

Delft's bar scene is driven by its large student population — around 25,000 students at TU Delft — and centres on the Beestenmarkt and the streets around it. The cafes tend toward the Dutch brown cafe tradition: dark wood, draught beer, and an atmosphere that invites sitting for several hours. The canal-side terraces fill up quickly on sunny days.
