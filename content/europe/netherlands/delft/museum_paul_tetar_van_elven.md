Museum Paul Tetar van Elven is a preserved 19th-century artist's house in central Delft. The rooms still hold period furniture, paintings, and decorative objects, giving a more intimate view of Delft than the larger church and Vermeer stops nearby.

It is a good choice if you like house museums or want a quiet interior after walking the canals. The scale is modest, but that is part of its charm.
