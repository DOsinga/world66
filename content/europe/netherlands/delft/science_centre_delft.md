Science Centre Delft is Delft University's public-facing science museum, set in a handsome building just outside the old centre. Exhibits lean toward engineering, invention, historical machinery, and hands-on activities.

It is especially useful if you are visiting Delft with children, or if you want a break from canals, churches, and Golden Age streets without leaving the city.
