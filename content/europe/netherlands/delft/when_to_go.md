---
title: When to Go
type: section
---

Delft is a year-round destination and the climate is mild enough to visit comfortably in any season. The city's canal beauty is arguably at its best in spring (April–May), when the trees are in leaf and the light is clear, and in autumn (September–October), when crowds thin and the colours turn.

Summer (June–August) is the busiest period, with day-trippers from Amsterdam, The Hague, and Rotterdam adding to the tourist numbers. Even in July, Delft rarely feels overwhelmed — it's small enough to absorb visitors, and the university students who leave in summer take some of the local density with them. Temperatures are typically 18–22°C in summer.

Winter is grey and often wet, but the Christmas market on the Markt in December is pleasant, and the shorter days mean the museums are less crowded. The canal reflections and the old city architecture are striking in winter light. Weekdays are always quieter than weekends, when Dutch day-trippers from the Randstad cities arrive in numbers.
