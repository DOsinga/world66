---
title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Den Bosch rewards slow exploration. Start at [Sint-Janskathedraal](/europe/netherlands/denbosch/sint_janskathedraal) and take your time — the stone figures on the flying buttresses alone are worth an hour. Then head to the [Het Noordbrabants Museum](/europe/netherlands/denbosch/het_noordbrabants_museum) for the Hieronymus Bosch paintings, and don't miss the [Binnendieze](/europe/netherlands/denbosch/binnendieze) boat tour through the city's underground canals. The [Jheronimus Bosch Art Center](/europe/netherlands/denbosch/jheronimus_bosch_art_center) is excellent context for the work you just saw.
