Den Helder's maritime past is its main draw. The [Marinemuseum](/europe/netherlands/denhelder/marinemuseum) at the historic [Willemsoord](/europe/netherlands/denhelder/willemsoord) dockyard is the standout attraction — it traces four centuries of Dutch naval history with ship models, navigational instruments, and full-sized vessels you can board, including the ironclad [HNLMS Schorpioen](/europe/netherlands/denhelder/hnlms_schorpioen) and the celebrated minesweeper [HNLMS Abraham Crijnssen](/europe/netherlands/denhelder/hnlms_abraham_crijnssen). The [Reddingsmuseum Dorus Rijkers](/europe/netherlands/denhelder/reddingsmuseum_dorus_rijkers) is a smaller but worthwhile museum on sea rescue.

The fortifications around the city are equally impressive. [Fort Kijkduin](/europe/netherlands/denhelder/fort_kijkduin) and [Fort Harssens](/europe/netherlands/denhelder/fort_harssens) are part of the broader defence lines protecting the naval base. The [Lange Jaap](/europe/netherlands/denhelder/lange_jaap) lighthouse, the tallest in the Netherlands, rises from the dunes near Huisduinen. Walking or cycling the coastal dike from the city to [Huisduinen Beach](/europe/netherlands/denhelder/huisduinen_beach) and back takes in all of these in a half-day loop.
