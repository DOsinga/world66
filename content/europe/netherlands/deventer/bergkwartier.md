The Bergkwartier — the "mountain quarter" — is the gently raised district east of the Lebuinuskerk, around the twin-towered Bergkerk. It is the most consistently medieval-looking neighbourhood in Deventer and one of the best-preserved late-Gothic urban quarters in the Netherlands: narrow stepped streets, tall gabled merchant houses, small squares, occasional cobbled courtyards.

The whole quarter was earmarked for demolition in the 1960s as part of a post-war urban-renewal plan; a sustained citizen campaign saved it, and the resulting careful restoration won the European Heritage Prize in 1980. The neighbourhood is now full of small antique shops, art galleries, characterful cafes and design studios — it is the obvious place to stroll for an hour after seeing the main sights on the Brink.
