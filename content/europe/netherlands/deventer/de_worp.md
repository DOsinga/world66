The Worp is the broad green floodmeadow on the western bank of the IJssel, directly opposite the old town and reached by the small Pothoofd foot-ferry from Welle. It is technically a park, but it feels more like genuine countryside: open grassland, scattered trees, a long sandy beach for swimming in summer, and an unbeatable panorama of the Deventer skyline.

In summer the Worp is the city's outdoor lounge — picnickers, swimmers, kayakers and people taking the small ferry back and forth like a lazy commute. The Engelse Werk park immediately to the north hosts open-air concerts. The ferry stops in winter when high water makes the crossing unsafe, but the Worp is still good for a brisk walk on a cold day.
