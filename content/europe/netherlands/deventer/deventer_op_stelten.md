Deventer op Stelten ("Deventer on Stilts") is the city's signature summer street-theatre festival, held over a long weekend in early July. It originated in 1991 as a one-off event commemorating the 1591 siege and has grown into one of the most respected outdoor performance festivals in Europe — anchor acts have included Royal de Luxe, Compagnie Carabosse and the Dundu light-puppet ensembles.

Most of the action happens on the Brink, the IJssel quays and the streets of the old town, with around 250 performances over three days from companies based all over Europe. Highlights tend to be the large-scale mechanical sculptures, fire performances after dark, and aerial circus acts strung between historic buildings. The festival is free and gets very crowded; pick your hours.
