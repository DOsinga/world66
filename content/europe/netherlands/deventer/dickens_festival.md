story: The Dickens Festival started in 1991 as an experiment by the Bergkwartier residents' association. It now draws 125,000 visitors to a city of 100,000 — the Bergkwartier shopkeepers say their December sales depend on it almost completely.
---

For one weekend in mid-December, the Bergkwartier becomes a living re-enactment of Victorian London: 950 costumed actors playing characters from Dickens novels and the wider nineteenth century — Scrooge, Mr Pickwick, chimney sweeps, beggars, the Artful Dodger, market women, gentlemen in stove-pipe hats — populate the medieval streets, accompanied by carol singers, brass bands, hot mulled wine and chestnut sellers.

It works extraordinarily well: the Bergkwartier's narrow alleys, gabled houses and absence of cars are a near-perfect approximation of the Victorian London Dickens described. Visitors queue around the block, and admission is now ticketed in slots to manage the crowds. Even with the queuing it is one of the best Christmas events in the Netherlands.
