Museum EICAS is a modern and contemporary art museum in Deventer. Its collection and exhibitions take the Dutch Nul group and the wider international ZERO movement as a starting point, then connect them to later minimalist and conceptual art.

The museum is a focused stop for visitors interested in postwar European art rather than a broad city museum.
