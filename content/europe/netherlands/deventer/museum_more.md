Museum MORE in Gorssel is a modern and contemporary realist art museum a short trip from Deventer. Its collection focuses on figurative and realist work, giving the region a polished art stop beyond Deventer's medieval streets and bookish atmosphere.

Because it is outside Deventer, it works best for travelers with a car, bicycle or a longer stay in the IJssel valley. The museum is substantial enough for a dedicated visit, especially if you are interested in Dutch realism rather than old-master collections.
