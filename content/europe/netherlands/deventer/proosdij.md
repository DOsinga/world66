The Proosdij is usually described as the oldest surviving stone house in the Netherlands, with origins around 1130. It is a small sight, but an important one in Deventer's compact medieval centre.

Look for it while walking between the Lebuinus Church, Walstraat, and the IJssel riverfront. Its age is the draw: this is a rare domestic building from the earliest stone-built phase of Dutch towns.
