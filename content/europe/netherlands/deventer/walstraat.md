The Walstraat is a curving cobbled street one block east of the river, lined with small independent shops — vintage clothing, second-hand books, organic groceries, jewellers, a bakery or two. It is the most browsing-friendly street in Deventer and a deliberate alternative to the chain-store Lange Bisschopstraat that runs parallel to it.

The street's name records that it once ran along the inside of the medieval city wall; the wall itself is mostly gone, but a few stretches and a corner tower survive nearby. The Walstraat is also the closest thing Deventer has to a self-consciously hip street: a few of the cafes do good coffee, the bookshops sell remainders and curiosities, and there is usually some kind of street festival or book market happening on a Saturday in summer.
