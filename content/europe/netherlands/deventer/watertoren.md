Deventer's Watertoren is a modest but handsome water tower, the kind of utility architecture that quietly marks a city's move into the modern age. It is not a destination to cross the Netherlands for, but it adds another layer to a Deventer walk beyond the medieval churches, squares and riverfront.

Look for it as part of a broader wander through the city rather than as a single-purpose visit. The tower works best for travellers who enjoy urban details: brickwork, infrastructure, and the way practical buildings become landmarks once they outlive their original function.
