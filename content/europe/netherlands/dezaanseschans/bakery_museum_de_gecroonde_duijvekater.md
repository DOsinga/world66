The Bakery Museum In De Gecroonde Duijvekater is one of the small craft museums that make Zaanse Schans more than a windmill photo stop. It presents a traditional bakery and shop interior, with displays tied to Dutch breads, sweets, and the local duivekater loaf.

The museum is quick, but that is part of its usefulness in Zaanse Schans: it adds texture between the larger mills and the Zaans Museum. Stop in if you like food history, old shop fittings, or compact museums that explain daily life.
