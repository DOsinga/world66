De Bleeke Dood is a restored flour mill near De Zaanse Schans, part of the working windmill landscape that makes the Zaan region so immediately recognisable. Its name is memorable, but the real value is seeing how an everyday industrial building fits into a landscape of timber houses, water, and wind power.

It is a good stop while walking between the station, the village, and the better-known mills along the river. Treat it as part of the whole Zaanse Schans experience: small on its own, but meaningful in the larger story of Dutch milling and food production.
