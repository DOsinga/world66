De Gekroonde Poelenburg is one of the windmills that keeps Zaanse Schans from becoming only a pretty skyline. It is a paltrok sawmill, a specialised wind-powered timber mill with an open work floor and an odd, broad-shouldered shape that looks different from the cleaner silhouettes of the oil and paint mills nearby.

Look for the pale green body and the frame saws rather than racing through the windmill row. If access is available, the appeal is in the machinery and the industrial story: this was a working landscape of sawn timber, oil, pigment, and trade, not just a cluster of photogenic sails.
