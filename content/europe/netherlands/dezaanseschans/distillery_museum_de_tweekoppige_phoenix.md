Distillery Museum De Tweekoppige Phoenix is a small specialty museum within the Zaanse Schans heritage area. It focuses on traditional Dutch distilling and the tools, bottles, and stories behind a craft that sits alongside the better-known windmills and wooden houses.

It is best treated as a short add-on while exploring Zaanse Schans. The museum gives variety to a visit that can otherwise be dominated by mills and photo stops, especially for travelers interested in food, drink, and small-scale trades.
