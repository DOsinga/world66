The Grocery Museum Albert Heijn is a small shop museum at Zaanse Schans, recreating the kind of grocery from which the Dutch Albert Heijn chain grew. Shelves, packaging, counters, and old retail details make it a compact piece of social history.

It is not the reason to come to Zaanse Schans, but it is a pleasant addition between windmills and workshops. The museum works because it brings the open-air village down to a human scale: what people bought, sold, weighed, and wrapped.
