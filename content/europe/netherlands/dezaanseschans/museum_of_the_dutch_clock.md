The Museum of the Dutch Clock is one of the more distinctive indoor stops at Zaanse Schans. Its displays cover domestic clocks, tower clocks, precision mechanisms, and the craft of Dutch timekeeping in a village better known for windmills and timber houses.

It works well because it broadens Zaanse Schans beyond the postcard view. The museum is compact, but the subject is specific and tangible enough to reward a focused visit, especially on a crowded or wet day.
