De Bonte Hen is the northernmost of the Zaanse Schans windmills, an oil mill whose history reaches back to 1693. Like the better-known De Zoeker nearby, it matters because it shows the Zaan as an industrial landscape, not just a pretty line of sails for photographs.

If the mill is open, go inside for the machinery rather than stopping at the riverbank view. The heavy timber gear, oil cellars, and pressing equipment make clear how much physical work these wind-powered factories once did for the region.
