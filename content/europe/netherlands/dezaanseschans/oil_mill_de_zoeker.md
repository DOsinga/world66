De Zoeker is one of the industrial windmills that makes Zaanse Schans more than a postcard row of sails. Its long life has included work as a polder mill, oil mill, paint mill, and cocoa mill, but today the draw is the old oil-pressing gear, where seeds are still processed in the heavy, rhythmic way that once powered the Zaan economy.

It is worth going inside if the mill is open, because the machinery gives the place its meaning. From outside it is a handsome riverside windmill; from within it becomes a noisy, practical workshop, and a reminder that these mills were factories before they were scenery.
