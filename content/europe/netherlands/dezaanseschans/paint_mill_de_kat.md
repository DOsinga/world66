Paint Mill De Kat is one of the best reasons to take Zaanse Schans seriously rather than treat it only as a windmill backdrop. Inside the working mill, pigments are still ground by wind power, linking the postcard view to the industrial craft that once made the Zaan region wealthy.

Climb inside if it is open. The machinery, colour samples, and creaking timber make the visit far more memorable than another exterior photograph, and the deck gives a good view across the river and neighbouring mills.
