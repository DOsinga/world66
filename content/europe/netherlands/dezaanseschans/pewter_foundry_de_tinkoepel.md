De Tinkoepel adds a useful craft stop to the windmills and wooden houses of Zaanse Schans. The foundry works with pewter in the old way, using moulds, casting benches, and a compact workshop that makes the process easy to understand even if you only stop in briefly.

It is a small attraction, but that is part of the appeal. You can watch a traditional trade at close range, browse pieces made on site, and then step straight back into the open-air village rather than losing half a day indoors.
