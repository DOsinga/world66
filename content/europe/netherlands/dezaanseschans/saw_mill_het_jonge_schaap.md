Saw Mill Het Jonge Schaap is a working wind-powered sawmill at Zaanse Schans, reconstructed from detailed historical drawings. Inside, the moving frame saws show how wind energy was turned into industrial power, cutting timber at a scale that helped fuel Dutch shipbuilding and construction.

It is one of the most satisfying mill visits at Zaanse Schans because the machinery is active and understandable. You can see, hear, and feel the process rather than only admire a picturesque exterior, which makes the broader industrial history of the Zaan region much easier to grasp.
