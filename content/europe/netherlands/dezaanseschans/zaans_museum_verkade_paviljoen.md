Zaans Museum and the Verkade Paviljoen give De Zaanse Schans a deeper historical anchor than the open-air village alone. The museum explains how wind power, trade, milling, shipbuilding, and food production shaped the Zaan region, while the Verkade section focuses on the biscuit and chocolate factories whose packaging became part of Dutch visual culture.

It works well at the beginning or end of a Zaanse Schans visit because it adds context to the mills and timber houses outside. Allow time for the industrial-history displays rather than treating it only as a rainy-day backup.
