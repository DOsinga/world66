OERmuseum West-Drenthe covers the prehistory of the region around Diever, from the Palaeolithic period to the early Middle Ages. The permanent displays include mammoth material, archaeological finds, and local objects from the periods that shaped the Drenthe landscape.

It is a useful stop before visiting nearby hunebedden and other prehistoric sites, since it gives context for the people who lived here long before the villages and farms.
