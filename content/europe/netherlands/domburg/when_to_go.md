---
title: When to Go
type: section
tags: [when_to_go]
---

Domburg is at its best in late spring (May–June) and early autumn (September–October), when the beaches are uncrowded, the light is exceptional, and the dune wildflowers are at their peak. July and August are busy — the Dutch and Belgian summer holiday season fills the village and the beaches with families, and accommodation becomes hard to find and expensive.

The museum is open year-round except in winter, when some facilities close. For walkers and cyclists who want the natural landscape without the crowds, a visit in September or early October combines decent weather with near-solitude on the trails.
