De Biesbosch is one of the Netherlands' great wetland landscapes, a maze of freshwater tidal creeks, willow woods, reed beds, and islands. Its proximity to Dordrecht makes wild-feeling water country surprisingly accessible.

The best way to experience it is by boat, canoe, or a walk from one of the visitor areas. Birdlife, beaver traces, and changing water levels give the park a different rhythm from the country's more manicured urban sights.
