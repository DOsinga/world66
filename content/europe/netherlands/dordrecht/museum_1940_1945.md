Museum 1940-1945 is Dordrecht's small World War II museum, with displays on the occupation, resistance, rationing, weapons, improvised equipment, and daily life in and around the city.

The museum is best for visitors with a specific interest in local wartime history. It adds a more recent layer to a Dordrecht itinerary otherwise dominated by medieval harbours, churches, and merchant houses.
