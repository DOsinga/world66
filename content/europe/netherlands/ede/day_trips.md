The Veluwe puts Ede at the centre of one of the Netherlands' richest day-trip areas. **Arnhem**, ten minutes by train, is the nearest city and has the Airborne Museum Hartenstein — the definitive account of Operation Market Garden — as well as the Open Air Museum and a lively restaurant scene. **Nijmegen**, just south of Arnhem, is one of the oldest cities in the Netherlands and holds the Canadian War Cemetery Groesbeek nearby; it also hosts the famous Four Days Marches each July. **Apeldoorn** to the north is the base for visiting Paleis Het Loo, the former hunting palace of the Dutch royal family, and the northern half of the Veluwe.
