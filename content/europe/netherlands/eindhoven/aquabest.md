Aquabest is a leisure lake north of Eindhoven with beach areas, swimming, and seasonal water-based attractions. It is not a cultural sight, but it is a useful warm-weather escape in a city better known for design, technology, and nightlife.

The place is most relevant in summer, especially for families or groups who want a low-effort day outdoors. Check the current setup before going, as activities and access can vary by season and event calendar.
