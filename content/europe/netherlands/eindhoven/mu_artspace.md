MU Artspace is a compact exhibition venue for contemporary visual culture, with a strong Eindhoven mix of design, pop culture, media, and experimental art. Its shows tend to be more playful and forward-looking than traditional museum displays.

It is a good fit if you are already exploring Eindhoven's design scene or want something smaller and sharper than a large museum. Check the current exhibition before going, since the appeal depends heavily on the programme.
