Natlab began as the Philips physics laboratory, which gives this Eindhoven arts venue more depth than a standard cinema. The building belongs to the Strijp-S story: industrial research, design culture, and the city's reinvention from company town to creative hub.

Today it is a place for films, talks, festivals, and performances, so the best visit is tied to the programme. Even if you only pass through, it helps explain why Eindhoven's modern identity is so closely linked to technology, experimentation, and reused factory space.
