The Oude Toren, or Old Tower, is the surviving tower of a 14th- or 15th-century Gothic church. It is one of the few visibly medieval fragments left in a city better known for Philips, design, and post-war rebuilding.

The tower is a quiet, quick stop rather than a major sight. It is best appreciated as a reminder that Eindhoven had a long life before it became an industrial city.
