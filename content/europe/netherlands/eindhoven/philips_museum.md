title: Philips Museum
type: poi
story: >
  In 1891, Gerard Philips and his father Frederik opened a small factory on Emmasingel to manufacture incandescent light bulbs. Within twenty years Philips was the largest employer in Eindhoven and the company had built housing, schools, sports facilities, and eventually a concert hall for its workers — effectively running a parallel city alongside the official one. The original factory building, now the museum, is where that transformation began.
---

Housed in the original 1891 Philips light bulb factory, this museum traces the company's journey from incandescent lamps to electronics giant. Interactive exhibits cover everything from early radio technology to medical imaging. A must for anyone curious about how one company shaped an entire city. Emmasingel 31.
