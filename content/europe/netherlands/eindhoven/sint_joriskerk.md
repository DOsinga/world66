Sint Joriskerk is Eindhoven's tall Neo-Gothic church, designed by Hendrik Jacobus van Tulder and completed in the late 19th century. Its 91-metre tower makes it the highest church in the city and one of the clearer landmarks on the edge of the centre.

The church is most useful as an architectural stop while walking between central Eindhoven and the museum quarter around the DAF Museum and Van Abbemuseum.
