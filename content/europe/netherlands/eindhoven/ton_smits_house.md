The Ton Smits House is a modest but likeable museum in the former home and studio of Ton Smits, the Eindhoven-born cartoonist and painter whose work appeared in Dutch and international publications. It sits in a quiet residential corner near Genneper Park, so it feels more like stepping into an artist's private world than entering a big municipal museum.

The appeal is in the scale: drawings, cartoons, paintings, and personal material are shown close to where Smits worked. It is not a blockbuster stop, but it gives Eindhoven's design-and-industry story a more intimate artistic footnote, especially if you are already visiting the PreHistorisch Dorp or walking through the parkland south of the centre.
