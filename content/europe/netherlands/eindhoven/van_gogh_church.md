The Van Gogh Church in Nuenen is the small 1824 Reformed church where Vincent van Gogh's father, Theo, preached. Its quiet park setting is part of the appeal, especially if you are following the Van Gogh places around Eindhoven.

The building is modest, but the connection is real: Van Gogh painted this church several times while living in Nuenen. It works best as one stop on a broader Van Gogh walk or cycle ride.
