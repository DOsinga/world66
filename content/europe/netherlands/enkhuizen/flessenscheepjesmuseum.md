Flessenscheepjesmuseum is a small museum devoted to ships in bottles, with around a thousand examples in its collection. About half are usually on display, ranging from simple folk craft to finely detailed miniature vessels.

The museum is housed in the historic Spuihuisje near Enkhuizen's old harbour, so it pairs naturally with a walk through the maritime centre of town.
