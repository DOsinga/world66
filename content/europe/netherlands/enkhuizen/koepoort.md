Koepoort is Enkhuizen's handsome old city gate, a reminder that this quiet IJsselmeer town was once a fortified and prosperous maritime place. The gate's brickwork and balanced shape make it one of the town's most satisfying architectural stops.

It is easy to include on a walk between the station, harbour, and historic centre. Do not rush it: Enkhuizen rewards slow wandering, and Koepoort helps frame the town as more than a museum harbour.
