---
title: Bars and Cafes
type: section
---

The Oude Markt is the social heart of Enschede, with terraces filling every summer evening. The student population keeps a lively bar scene going year-round. For quieter coffee, the area around the Grote Kerk has several traditional Dutch brown cafes (bruine kroegen) with the warm, tobacco-stained atmosphere that the Netherlands does better than anywhere.
