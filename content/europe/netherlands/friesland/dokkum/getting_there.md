---
title: "Getting There"
type: section
---

Dokkum has no train station. The nearest rail connections are at **Leeuwarden** (23 km southwest) and **Groningen** (45 km east).

**By bus:** Arriva operates regular bus services from Leeuwarden to Dokkum; the journey takes about 35 minutes. Services run roughly every hour on weekdays, less frequently at weekends.

**By car:** Dokkum is an easy drive from Leeuwarden on the N361. From Groningen, take the N361 westbound. Parking is available in the town centre.

**By bike:** Friesland has an excellent cycling network, and Dokkum is connected to the regional Fietsroutenetwerk. Cycling from Leeuwarden takes about 90 minutes on flat terrain.
