title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Franeker's main draw is the [Eisinga Planetarium](/europe/netherlands/friesland/franeker/eisinga_planetarium) — a genuine wonder that alone justifies the trip. Beyond that, the town's compact historic centre rewards a half-day's walking, with the sixteenth-century [City Hall](/europe/netherlands/friesland/franeker/city_hall), the [Martenahuis](/europe/netherlands/friesland/franeker/martenahuis) town palace, the [Martinikerk](/europe/netherlands/friesland/franeker/martini_church) with its university tombstones, and the 1657 [Waag](/europe/netherlands/friesland/franeker/waag) (weighing house) all within a few streets of each other.

The town is one of Friesland's eleven historic cities — included in the legendary winter Elfstedentocht skating tour — and is built around a network of canals and small bridges that are themselves part of the appeal.
