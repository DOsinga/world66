A classic Dutch brown café on the Zuiderhaven, panelled in dark wood, with a long bar, a row of small wooden tables, and a clientele drawn from the ship-owners and visiting yachties along the quay. Excellent draught beer and a basic pub-food menu. One of the better places in town for a single quiet drink in winter.
