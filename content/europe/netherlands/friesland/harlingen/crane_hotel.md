---

A spectacular conversion: the working crane that once loaded coal at Harlingen's industrial harbour turned into a three-suite boutique hotel, fully rotatable, with a hot tub on top and a bungee-jump platform. Not cheap and almost permanently booked, but worth a look from the quayside even if you are not staying. The crane lifts and spins on demand for guests; you can sometimes see it move at sunset.
