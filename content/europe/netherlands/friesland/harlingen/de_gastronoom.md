A small, ambitious bistro a couple of blocks back from the Noorderhaven, with a tight changing menu and a strong line in regional ingredients. Frisian lamb, Wadden Sea mussels, salt-marsh herbs. Bookings essential for weekend evenings. Better value than the more obvious harbour-front spots and a useful winter option when the terraces are closed.
