A small white-and-red lighthouse on the harbour mole, built in 1922 to mark the entrance to Harlingen's outer port. After decommissioning at the end of the 20th century it was converted to a single-room luxury hotel — the entire lighthouse rented as one suite, with the old lamp room as the bedroom on the top floor. Even if you are not staying, the lighthouse at the end of its breakwater is one of the iconic images of Harlingen and a five-minute walk from the centre.
