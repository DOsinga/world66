Harlinger Aardewerk Museum is a tiny pottery museum focused on the Frisian ceramic tradition that flourished in the 17th, 18th, and 19th centuries. It is a specialist stop, but a good one if Harlingen's old tiles and maritime merchant houses have already caught your eye.
