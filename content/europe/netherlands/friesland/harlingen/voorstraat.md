The main street of the old town, running north-east from the centre. Cobbled and pedestrian-friendly, lined with 17th- and 18th-century houses, several with the elaborately carved gables that mark Harlingen's commercial heyday. Most of the town's shops and a number of the better cafés are here. Look up: many of the upper façades are more interesting than the modern shopfronts at street level.
