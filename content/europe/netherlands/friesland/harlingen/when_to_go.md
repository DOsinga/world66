---
title: When to Go
type: section
---

Harlingen is at its best in summer, when the harbours fill with traditional ships and the terraces stay open till late. The key events are the **Vlootdagen (Fleet Days)** in late August or early September — three days when the inner harbours fill with historic Frisian sailing ships, the streets are closed to traffic, and there is music and food along the quays. The **Tall Ships Race** stops in Harlingen periodically; check the year in advance.

Winter is bitterly cold and windy off the Wadden, but the ice-covered harbours and the lit-up sailing-ship masts are atmospheric. Cycling along the dike towards Kornwerderzand on a clear winter day is one of the best half-day outings in northern Friesland.
