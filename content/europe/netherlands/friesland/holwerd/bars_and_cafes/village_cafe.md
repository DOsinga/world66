The traditional Frisian kroeg in the village centre is the kind of place where locals outnumber tourists and beer is poured without ceremony. It is unpretentious, unhurried, and exactly what you want after a long walk across the mudflats. Come here for a Grolsch or a jenever, sit at the bar, and have a conversation about the tides.
