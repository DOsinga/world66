Holwerd's obvious day trip is Ameland — that is, after all, why most people are here. But the surrounding Frisian countryside offers several other options within easy reach.

**Ameland** is the main reason to be in Holwerd. The island has wide beaches, a nature reserve, historic captains' houses in Hollum, and a much slower pace of life. A day trip is very doable; staying overnight gives you the island without the day-trip crowds.

**Leeuwarden**, the Frisian capital, is about 45 minutes south by bus or car. It has a strong identity — Frisian language on every sign, the Fries Museum, a handsome historic centre, and a growing food and culture scene. Worth a half-day at least.

**Dokkum** is the closest town, about 15 minutes by car. One of the best-preserved small towns in Friesland, with an intact ring canal, a windmill, and a quiet centre that hasn't been homogenised. Easy to combine with a morning visit to Holwerd.

**Lauwersoog**, about 25 minutes east along the coast, is the other major Wadden ferry port — this one for Schiermonnikoog. The area around the harbour is a seal-watching hotspot; organised boat trips run from the port.
