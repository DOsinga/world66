The small cafe inside the Ameland ferry terminal serves coffee, sandwiches, and basic hot snacks. It is the obvious place to grab something before you board or after you arrive back from the island. Don't expect much — this is functional rather than memorable — but it does the job when you're waiting for a crossing.
