---

Leeuwarden has a small but lively restaurant scene concentrated on the Eewal, the Nieuwestad, and along the Kelders waterfront. Frisian cooking leans on dairy and the sea: try **sûkerbôle** (sweet sugar bread), **fierljepkoek** (almond pastry), **dúmkes** (clove biscuits), and the smoked **paling** (eel) from the lakes south of town.

A few places to know: [Spinoza](/europe/netherlands/friesland/leeuwarden/spinoza) on the Eewal for refined seasonal Dutch cooking; [Eindeloos](/europe/netherlands/friesland/leeuwarden/eindeloos) for adventurous tasting menus; [Proefverlof](/europe/netherlands/friesland/leeuwarden/proefverlof) inside the old Blokhuispoort prison for casual brunch and lunch; and [De Koperen Tuin](/europe/netherlands/friesland/leeuwarden/de_koperen_tuin) in the Prinsentuin park for an elegant lunch with a view.
