title: "Shopping"
type: section
---

Shopping in Leeuwarden clusters in the lanes between the Nieuwestad, Wirdumerdijk, and Voorstreek — small independent boutiques alongside the usual Dutch chain stores. The **Saturday market** on the Wilhelminaplein and Zaailand is the best market in the north of the Netherlands: cheeses, smoked fish from the Wadden, Frisian sausage, fresh flowers.

Bookshop **Van der Velde** inside the [Blokhuispoort](/europe/netherlands/friesland/leeuwarden/blokhuispoort) is exceptional and has a strong Frisian-language section. For Frisian souvenirs that are not embarrassing, **De Friesche Vlag** and the **Fries Museum shop** are the places to look — silver work, ceramics from Makkum and Workum, and Frisian linens.
