---
title: "Getting There"
type: section
---

**By car.** Lemmer sits on the A6 motorway, which links Amsterdam to Friesland over the Houtribdijk causeway across the IJsselmeer. From Amsterdam it is about 90 minutes; from Groningen, 75 minutes via the A7.

**By bus.** Arriva runs a frequent service from Heerenveen (about 35 minutes), which has the nearest railway station. From Sneek, the bus takes about 40 minutes. There is no direct rail service to Lemmer — the line was lifted decades ago.

**By boat.** This is the way most leisure visitors arrive. Lemmer is the southernmost port on the IJsselmeer side of Friesland and the start of the Frisian *staande mast route* (the standing-mast route) for sailing boats heading north into the lakes. Both old and new marinas have transient berths; book in summer.
