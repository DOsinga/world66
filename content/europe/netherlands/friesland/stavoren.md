With only 995 inhabitants Stavoren (Staveren or Starum in Frisian) is small but it is still the oldest city of Friesland. Located on the shores of the IJsselmeer, it received city rights in 1118.

Around the [old harbour](/europe/netherlands/friesland/stavoren/old_harbour) there are a few cute streets where you can wander around and feel like you are back in the seventeenth century. The bronze [Vrouwe van Stavoren](/europe/netherlands/friesland/stavoren/vrouwe_van_stavoren) statue on the harbour pier commemorates the medieval legend that supposedly brought the town's golden age to an end, and a short walk along the dyke brings you to the [Stavoren lighthouse](/europe/netherlands/friesland/stavoren/lighthouse). For something more unusual, the harbour-side [Hotel de Vrouwe van Stavoren](/europe/netherlands/friesland/stavoren/hotel_de_vrouwe_van_stavoren) offers rooms inside converted wine barrels.

Stavoren is connected with [Enkhuizen](/europe/netherlands/enkhuizen) on the other side of the lake by a ferry service.
