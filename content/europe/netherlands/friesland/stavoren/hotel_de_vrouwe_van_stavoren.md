A small hotel on the harbour better known for its sleeping accommodation than its restaurant. Built into the back garden are a row of enormous wooden wine barrels, refitted as compact double rooms — a curtained porthole for a window, two berths, a small bathroom in the adjoining structure. They sleep two at a squeeze and book up months ahead in summer.

Standard hotel rooms in the main building are also available for the less adventurous. The terrace is one of the few places in Stavoren open into the evening and serves the usual Dutch repertoire of croquettes, salads, and small pancakes.
