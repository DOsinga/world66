---
title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Stavoren is small enough to walk around in half an hour, and most of what there is to see clusters around the old harbour and the lakefront. The [Vrouwe van Stavoren](/europe/netherlands/friesland/stavoren/vrouwe_van_stavoren) statue on the harbour wall is the obvious landmark — a bronze figure looking out across the IJsselmeer that pins the town's most famous legend in place.

A short walk along the dyke brings you to the [Stavoren lighthouse](/europe/netherlands/friesland/stavoren/lighthouse) and the lock complex that connects the lake to the inland Friesian waterways. From there it is a few minutes back through narrow streets to the [old harbour](/europe/netherlands/friesland/stavoren/old_harbour) and the cluster of cafés where most visitors end up.
