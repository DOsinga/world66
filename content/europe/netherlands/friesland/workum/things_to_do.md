---
title: Things to Do
type: section
---

Workum's compact historic centre can be covered on foot in an afternoon. The two museums are the main draws — the Jopie Huisman Museum for art, and the Warkums Erfskip for local history — with the Sint-Gertrudiskerk anchoring the market square between them. The IJsselmeer waterfront is a five-minute walk from the centre.
