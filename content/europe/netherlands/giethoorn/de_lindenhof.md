De Lindenhof is the standout restaurant in Giethoorn by an enormous margin. While the rest of the village dining scene is pancakes and tourist lunches, De Lindenhof operates at the level of a serious fine-dining destination: multi-course tasting menus, a wine list that rewards attention, and cooking that reflects the Dutch landscape and the wetland environment the restaurant sits in.

Book well in advance — the restaurant is small, well known, and has a loyal following from Amsterdam and beyond. The setting alongside the canal adds to the experience: arriving by whisper boat for an evening meal is one of those rare occasions when a location genuinely completes the food rather than competing with it. The restaurant is open for dinner and occasional lunches; check the website for current schedules and reservation details.
