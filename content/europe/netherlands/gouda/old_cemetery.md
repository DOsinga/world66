Gouda's old cemetery is a quiet historical counterweight to the market square and cheese-town image. Its paths, stones, and mature trees tell a more local story: civic families, changing funerary styles, and the way a small Dutch city remembered its dead outside the church walls.

It will appeal most to travellers who like cemeteries as open-air archives. Visit respectfully, keep expectations modest, and fold it into a wider walk through Gouda rather than treating it as a headline sight.
