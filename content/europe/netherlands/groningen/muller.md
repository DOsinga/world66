A Michelin-listed restaurant just off the Vismarkt, run by chef Jean-Michel Hengge for more than three decades. The kitchen does French-inspired tasting menus from local ingredients — North Sea fish, game from the Drents-Friese countryside, vegetables from nearby market gardens. The dining room is small and elegant, the service formal without being stiff.

Closed Sunday and Monday. Reserve well ahead, particularly for weekend evenings.
