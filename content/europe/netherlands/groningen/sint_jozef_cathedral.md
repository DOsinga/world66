Sint Jozef Cathedral is the Roman Catholic cathedral for the Groningen-Leeuwarden diocese. Built in 1885-1887, the Neo-Gothic church is known locally for its tower, nicknamed the "drunk's tower" because two clock faces are visible from every direction.
