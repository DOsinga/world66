Groningen's Stadsschouwburg is the city's main theatre, a Neo-Renaissance building from 1883 near the centre. Its programme includes dance, opera, cabaret, and theatre, making it both an architectural landmark and an active performance venue.

Even without a ticket, the building is worth noticing while walking between the Grote Markt and the quieter streets northeast of the centre.
