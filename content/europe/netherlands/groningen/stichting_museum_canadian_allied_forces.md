Stichting Museum Canadian Allied Forces is a specialist museum focused on the Canadian role in the liberation of the Netherlands. For Groningen, where the end of World War II was fought street by street, that subject has real local weight.

Expect a modest, enthusiast-led collection rather than a polished national museum. Uniforms, vehicles, documents, and personal material help connect the city's wartime story to the Allied soldiers who entered the north of the country in 1945.
