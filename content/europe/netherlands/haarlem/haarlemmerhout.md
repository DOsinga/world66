Haarlemmerhout is Haarlem's old city wood, a green southern edge that has been mapped and remade for centuries. It is often described as the oldest public park in the Netherlands, and it still feels more like a piece of urban memory than a simple lawn.

Come here when the museum streets start to feel busy. Paths, mature trees, and long views make it good for a slow walk, while the history of siege, replanting, and landscape design gives the park more depth than its easygoing surface suggests.
