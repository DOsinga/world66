Haarlem's History Museum gives a concise introduction to the city's past, from its Golden Age wealth to everyday local life. It sits near the Frans Hals Museum, making it easy to combine with Haarlem's larger art stop.

This is a small museum, best used for context rather than as the centrepiece of a day. It helps explain why Haarlem mattered as a trading city and how its historic streets grew.
