Haarlem's History Museum is a small local museum across from the Frans Hals Museum. It tells the city's story as a Golden Age trading, textile, and tulip centre, with enough old maps, objects, and city views to make the modern streets easier to read.

It is a compact stop rather than a reason to come to Haarlem by itself. Visit it if you are already in this part of town and want a clearer sense of how Haarlem grew before Amsterdam swallowed most of the attention.
