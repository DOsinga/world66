---
title: Things to Do in Haarlem
type: section
---

Haarlem concentrates a remarkable amount into a compact historic centre. The [Grote Kerk (St. Bavo)](/europe/netherlands/haarlem/stbavo) dominates the Grote Markt with its organ and Golden Age monuments. The [Teylers Museum](/europe/netherlands/haarlem/teylermuseum) is the oldest in the Netherlands, the [Frans Hals Museum](/europe/netherlands/haarlem/frans_hals_museum) is one of the finest Golden Age collections outside Amsterdam, and the [Corrie ten Boom House](/europe/netherlands/haarlem/corrie_ten_boom) is one of the most moving small museums in the country. Beyond the monuments, the city rewards wandering: Haarlem has more [hofjes](/europe/netherlands/haarlem/hofjes) (hidden courtyard almshouses) than anywhere in the Netherlands.
