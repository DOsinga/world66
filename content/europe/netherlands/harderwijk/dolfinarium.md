The Dolfinarium is Harderwijk's most famous attraction, a large marine mammal park near the Veluwemeer waterfront. It has long been the town's big family draw and is easy to reach from the old centre.

It will not suit every traveller, especially if you avoid captive animal shows. But as a local landmark and day-out attraction, it is central to modern Harderwijk.
