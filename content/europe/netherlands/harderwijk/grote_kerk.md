The Grote Kerk anchors Harderwijk's old centre. Built as the town expanded in the medieval period, it is the most obvious reminder that Harderwijk was once a fortified trading place rather than just a lakefront stop.

The church is worth seeing as part of a walk through the centre. Pair it with the Vischpoort and the museum for a quick sense of the old city.
