The main day trip is to the **Wadden Islands** of Vlieland and Terschelling — both reachable from Harlingen by the morning ferry, both worth a full day. Inland, **Franeker** is twenty minutes by train, with the famous Eise Eisinga Planetarium (the oldest working planetarium in the world). **Bolsward**, a similar distance south, is a small Hanseatic town with one of the finest late-Gothic town halls in the Netherlands.
