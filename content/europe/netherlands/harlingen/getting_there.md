---
title: Getting There
type: section
---

**By train.** Direct trains from Leeuwarden run every half-hour, with the line continuing into Harlingen Haven station next to the ferry terminal — useful if you are heading on to Vlieland or Terschelling. From Amsterdam, change at Leeuwarden; total journey around 2 hours 45 minutes.

**By car.** The A31 from Leeuwarden brings you to the southern edge of the town; parking is at the Westerzeedijk lots near the harbour. Driving into the old centre is slow and not recommended.

**Ferries** to the Wadden Islands of Vlieland and Terschelling leave from the Willemshaven, a short walk from both railway stations. Doeksen runs the regular service; the fast catamaran (35 minutes) and the slower car ferry (1h45) are run side by side.
