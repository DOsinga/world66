Heerenveen is a town of about 50,000 in central [Friesland](/europe/netherlands/friesland), best known internationally as the spiritual home of Dutch speed skating. The [Thialf](/europe/netherlands/heerenveen/thialf) arena here is the most famous speed skating venue in the world — World Cup events and Dutch championships are held here regularly, and the atmosphere on race nights is electric.

Beyond ice, Heerenveen is a pleasant if unremarkable Frisian town. The football club SC Heerenveen punches above its weight in the Eredivisie and fills the [Abe Lenstra Stadion](/europe/netherlands/heerenveen/abe_lenstra_stadion) on match days. The historic core has a handful of stately buildings — the seventeenth-century [Crackstate](/europe/netherlands/heerenveen/crackstate), the working [Welgelegen mill](/europe/netherlands/heerenveen/welgelegen_mill), and the small [Museum Heerenveen](/europe/netherlands/heerenveen/museum_heerenveen) — along with the wooded [Oranjewoud estate](/europe/netherlands/heerenveen/oranjewoud) just east of town. The surrounding Frisian countryside of lakes, canals, and wide skies is good cycling territory, and the tiny fortified town of [Sloten](/europe/netherlands/sloten) — one of the smallest cities in the Netherlands — is a short ride away.
