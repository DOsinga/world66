Aambos is a small hilly forest park close to central Heerlen, crossed by paths and the Caumer stream. It is an easy place for a short walk when you want a green break from the town streets.

The park also has a small animal area and paths that continue toward the old oil mill upstream. It is not a major destination, but it works well as part of a relaxed wander through Heerlen.
