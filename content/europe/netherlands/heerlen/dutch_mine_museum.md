The Dutch Mine Museum tells the story of Limburg's coal-mining years from the former shaft building of the Oranje-Nassau I mine. The collection includes mining tools, photographs, and film material that make Heerlen's industrial past easier to picture.

Guided visits can take a couple of hours, so it is worth checking times before you go.
