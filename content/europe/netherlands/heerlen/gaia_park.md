Gaia Park is a large zoo near Heerlen and Kerkrade, laid out around themed habitats from different continents. The design groups animals in settings that suggest their natural environments, which makes it one of the region's stronger family attractions.

Plan more than a quick stop, since the park is spread out and works best at a relaxed pace.
