Sterrenwacht Limburg is a public observatory near Heerlen, offering astronomy evenings, telescope viewing, and educational programmes when skies and schedules allow. It gives the city a small but distinctive science stop beyond Roman baths and mining-era architecture.

The visit depends on timing, weather, and language comfort, so check the programme before going. If there is an open evening, it can be a memorable low-key outing rather than a daytime museum stop.
