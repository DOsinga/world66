Hengelo's best day trips lead straight out into the surrounding countryside. The gently rolling [Twente cycling](/europe/netherlands/twente_cycling) network starts at the city's doorstep, with numbered junction points guiding you through Saxon farmsteads, heath, and woodland — head southeast for the Hoge Boekel estate, or northwest to the village of Delden and its castle.

By train you can be in [Enschede](/europe/netherlands/enschede), the cultural capital of Twente, in ten minutes, or reach [Deventer](/europe/netherlands/deventer) and its Hanseatic old town in under an hour. The German border is close enough that crossing over for an afternoon takes little effort.
