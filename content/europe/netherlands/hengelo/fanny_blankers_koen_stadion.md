  The stadium is named after Fanny Blankers-Koen, the Dutch sprinter who won four gold medals at the 1948 London Olympics at the age of 30 — well past what was then considered prime competitive age for a woman. She became known as the "Flying Housewife" in the press of the era, a label she always found slightly ridiculous.

Hengelo's athletics stadium is a venue for track and field events and is the home ground of a local football club. The city has a genuine athletics tradition — Hengelo hosts the FBK Games each year, one of the top international athletics meetings in Europe, drawing world-class athletes to the 25,000-seat facility.

If you're visiting in late May or early June, check whether the FBK Games are scheduled — it's an affordable way to watch elite athletics. Sportlaan, Hengelo.
