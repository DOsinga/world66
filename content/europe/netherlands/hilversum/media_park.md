The Mediapark, north of the centre, is the operating heart of Dutch broadcasting: nearly all the country's public and many of its commercial radio and TV stations have offices and studios here. The site is a working business park rather than a tourist attraction, but a walk around it is a tour of late-twentieth-century media architecture, with set pieces by MVRDV, Neutelings Riedijk, and others.

Beeld en Geluid sits at one corner; the rest is a fenced campus of studios, towers, and antennae. Hilversum Media Park station drops you right at the gate.
