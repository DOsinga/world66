In front of the Goois Museum on Kerkbrink stands a giant Easter egg covered in colourful ceramic mosaic — an art project by local schoolchildren that ended up being certified as a world record. It is essentially silly and entirely Dutch.

A quick photo stop, but a good one if you are doing the Dudok walking route, which starts nearby.
