---
title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Hilversum's headline attractions are its architecture and its broadcasting heritage. **Dudok's Raadhuis** is the icon — a 1931 masterpiece of brick modernism with a clock tower and reflecting pool — and the **Beeld en Geluid** museum on the Mediapark documents a century of Dutch radio and TV behind a glass facade of coloured panels. The **Sint-Vituskerk** by Pierre Cuypers gives the other side of the story: high Gothic Catholicism at the opposite end of the architectural spectrum.

Beyond the town centre, the **Goois Natuurreservaat** wraps the city in heathland, forest, and Bronze Age burial mounds. **Anna's Hoeve** is a closer, smaller park sculpted out of an old refuse dump. The **Pinetum Blijdenstein** holds the world's most complete collection of gymnosperms in a quiet residential corner. Architecture fans should follow the marked **Dudok Walking Route** through the residential neighbourhoods. For the odd and wonderful, look up the **Wolfsdreuvik** hidden in the Spanderswoud, and the giant ceramic egg in front of the Goois Museum.
