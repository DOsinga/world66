---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

Late August into early September is the best window: the heather across the Goois Natuurreservaat turns deep purple, and the weather is usually dry and mild. May and June give long evenings, green woods, and quieter trails.

Winter is grey but works for indoor sights — Beeld en Geluid is excellent on a rainy afternoon. The Dudok walking route is best appreciated on a clear day when the brick catches the light. Avoid Mondays for the museums, most of which close.
