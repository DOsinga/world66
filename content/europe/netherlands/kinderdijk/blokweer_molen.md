Blokweer Molen is one of Kinderdijk's museum windmills, giving visitors a closer look at how mill families lived and worked in the polder landscape. It complements the famous skyline of mills by turning the exterior view into a more tangible domestic and engineering story.

The mill is especially useful if you want more than photographs from the footpaths. Together with the other museum mill and the pumping station, it helps explain why Kinderdijk is a working water-management landscape rather than just a postcard scene.
