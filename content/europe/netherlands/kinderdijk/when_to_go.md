---
title: When to go
type: section
tags:
- when_to_go
---

The mills are open every day from April to October, with reduced services in winter. The best months are May, June and September — when the light is good, the canals are clear, and the crowds have not yet peaked. July and August can be busy by midday.

For photographers, sunrise and the hour before sunset give the most dramatic light along the row of mills. The site is more or less always accessible — even when the visitor centre is closed in winter, you can still walk the dike.
