---
title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Laren is small and the Singer museum is the obvious draw, but the village rewards a slow afternoon: the medieval Brink at its heart, the neo-Gothic basilica with its centuries-old St John's procession, and the open Westerheide heath beginning a few minutes' walk south. The cafés around the Brink make natural breaks; for a longer outing, cycle out across the heath to the old cemetery at Janskerkhof.
