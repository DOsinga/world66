---
title: "Bars and Cafes"
type: section
---

Leeuwarden's drinking scene clusters along the **Doelesteeg** and the **Ruiterskwartier**, the two short streets that constitute the city's small but enthusiastic nightlife strip. Term-time evenings — the city has around 25,000 students — push the volume up significantly.

For something quieter, the canal-side terraces along the **Nieuwestad** and **Kelders** are the place for a sunny afternoon beer. Order a **Frysk Bier** if you see it; the regional brewery in nearby Bolsward turns out a good range. Wijnbar **De Knipoog** has the best wine list in town.
