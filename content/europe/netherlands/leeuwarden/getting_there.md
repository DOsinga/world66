---
title: "Getting There"
type: section
---

Leeuwarden sits at the end of a railway line from the south. Direct intercity trains run from [Utrecht](/europe/netherlands/utrecht), Amersfoort, Zwolle, and on through Heerenveen — the trip from [Amsterdam](/europe/netherlands/amsterdam) takes around 2 hours 20 minutes with a change at Zwolle, or sometimes direct.

The **Leeuwarden railway station** is a five-minute walk south of the historic centre. By car, the A32 motorway runs in from Zwolle to the south; allow around two hours from Amsterdam. The nearest international airports are **Amsterdam Schiphol** and **Groningen Eelde** (the latter much smaller but only an hour by car).
