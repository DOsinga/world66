A canalside bar and café occupying a long, low waterside terrace below street level on the Hoogstraat, right where the Oude and Nieuwe Rijn meet. On a warm evening it is one of the busiest spots in town, the terrace packed with students and locals, drinks brought out by harried staff weaving past the boats moored alongside.

Sound and atmosphere over food: come for a beer at sunset, not for dinner. Cash and card both accepted, but expect a wait at the bar.
