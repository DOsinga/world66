Leiden's location puts almost all of the western Netherlands within easy day-trip reach. The bulb fields of Lisse and the [Keukenhof](/europe/netherlands/keukenhof) gardens, open mid-March to mid-May, are 20 minutes by bus to the north. The North Sea beach at Katwijk and the dunes around Wassenaar are a 15-minute bike ride or short bus from the centre.

The cities of [The Hague](/europe/netherlands/thehague), [Delft](/europe/netherlands/delft), and [Amsterdam](/europe/netherlands/amsterdam) are all under 40 minutes by train, making any of them an easy half-day if you have already seen Leiden's own centre.
