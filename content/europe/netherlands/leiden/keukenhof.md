The world's most famous bulb garden, Keukenhof lies 15 km north of Leiden in the heart of the Bollenstreek. The 32-hectare park is laid out in a sequence of themed gardens crossed by canals and small ponds, all planted from scratch every autumn for the next year's display.

Buy tickets and a bus ticket online in advance — direct buses from Leiden Centraal Station run frequently during the season. Arrive at opening time to beat the worst of the crowds; the gardens are at their best on a sunny weekday morning.
