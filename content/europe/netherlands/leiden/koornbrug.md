Koornbrug is a covered bridge over the Nieuwe Rijn, right in Leiden's old centre. The bridge dates from 1642 and was covered in the 19th century because it served the corn trade.

It is a small stop rather than a destination by itself, but it fits naturally into a walk between Leiden's canals, markets, and churches.
