Leidse Schouwburg is one of the oldest theatres in the Netherlands, dating from 1705. It is still a working theatre, so the main reason to visit is to see a performance in a historic setting rather than to tour an empty building.

Its central location makes it easy to combine with Leiden's museums and old town streets.
