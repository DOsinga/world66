The Lakenhal is Leiden's city museum: the fine-art collection, the history collection, and a remarkable group of paintings by Leiden masters including Rembrandt, Jan Steen, Lucas van Leyden, and Jan van Goyen. The building itself is a handsome 17th-century classical hall, expanded with a striking modern wing that opened after a full renovation in 2019.

The collection is paced just right — large enough to give a sense of the depth of Leiden's golden age, small enough to absorb in a couple of hours. Specials include silver from the city's medieval guilds and a room dedicated to the textile industry that gave the building its name.
