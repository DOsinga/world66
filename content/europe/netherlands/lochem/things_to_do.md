---
title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Lochem is a town of modest scale and quiet pleasures. A walk around the compact old centre with its medieval church and 17th-century stone houses fills an afternoon; the surrounding country estates, woods and the Twentekanaal supply most of the reason for staying longer. The Lochemse Berg, the wooded ridge south of town, is the only "mountain" in the eastern Netherlands and is laced with walking paths.
