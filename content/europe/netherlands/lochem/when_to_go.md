---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

Late spring to early autumn is the obvious window. May and June are reliably green and the country estates are at their most photogenic. August can be hot for Dutch standards and brings the busiest weekends on the Lochemse Berg.

Winter is quiet but the brown cafes on the Markt come into their own then. The biggest local event is the four-day Lochemse Spektakeltocht walking festival in late June, which fills the town and the surrounding countryside with several thousand walkers.
