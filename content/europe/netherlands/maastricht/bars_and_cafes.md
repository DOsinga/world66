---
title: "Bars and Cafes"
type: section
---

Maastricht has a drinking culture that owes more to Belgium and France than to Amsterdam. The café terraces on the Vrijthof are the obvious place to start — in summer they fill from noon and stay busy until late. Maastricht wine bars are notably good, reflecting the Burgundian tastes of the region, and the local beer scene includes Gulpener and Brand, both brewed nearby in the hills.
