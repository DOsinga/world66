Zeeland is seafood country, and Middelburg's restaurants make the most of it. Zeeland oysters, mussels, and shrimp are the local specialities — the Oosterschelde estuary is one of the best oyster-growing regions in Europe. Beyond seafood, the usual Dutch café and restaurant fare is available across the market square and surrounding streets.
