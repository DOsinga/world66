Miniature Walcheren is an open-air scale model of the entire Walcheren peninsula at 1:20 — large enough to walk through at something like giant scale. The model covers around 4,000 square metres and reproduces the farms, villages, churches, windmills, and coastal defences of Zeeland's westernmost island in extraordinary detail.

Children love it for the obvious reason, but adults will find it genuinely interesting as a record of the 1950s landscape of Zeeland. The model is in a park setting and can be combined with a walk along the old city walls nearby. It is open from April to October.
