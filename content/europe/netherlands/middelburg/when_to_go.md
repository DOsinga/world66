title: When to Go
type: section
tags: [when_to_go]
---

Middelburg has a maritime climate — mild, damp, and windy year-round. Summer (June to August) brings the longest days and the most tourists, though numbers here are modest compared to larger Dutch cities. The market square is at its most lively on Thursday, which is the traditional market day.

Spring and early autumn are the best times to visit: the weather is good enough for the canal walk and cycling excursions, and the historic buildings are uncrowded. Winter is quiet, but many of the outdoor attractions close from November to March (Miniature Walcheren is seasonal, April to October).

Zeeland generally is cold and exposed in winter — the wind off the Westerschelde makes it feel colder than the thermometer suggests. That said, a winter visit to the Zeeuws Museum and the abbey is perfectly pleasant.
