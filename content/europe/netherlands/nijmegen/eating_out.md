---
title: "Eating Out"
type: section
---

Nijmegen eats well for a city its size. The university population keeps prices honest and ensures a good range of options from cheap student-friendly spots to proper sit-down restaurants. The Waalkade and Kwartier district along the river have the highest concentration of places, but the side streets around the Grote Markt and the Benedenstad (lower city) area are worth exploring too.
