Menkemaborg is a handsome brick manor house with moats, gardens, and period rooms that show how Groningen's landed families lived in the 18th century. It is one of the region's clearest examples of a borg, the fortified country houses once scattered across the north.

The interiors are the main reason to go, but the formal gardens and quiet grounds make the visit feel complete. It is a good cultural counterpoint to the wide skies and mudflat landscapes around Noordpolderzijl.
