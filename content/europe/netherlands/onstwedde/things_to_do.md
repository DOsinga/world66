Onstwedde is a quiet Groningen village with two things that repay a short visit: the [Juffertoren](/europe/netherlands/onstwedde/juffertoren), a medieval tower attached to an old church with an unusual baptismal font, and the wider landscape of the Westerwolde area — a corner of Groningen with a character distinct from the more visited north and west of the province.
