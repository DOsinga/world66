Stellingmolen Zeldenrust is a restored 19th-century tower mill in Oss, built in 1860 and brought back into working order in the 1970s. Its raised gallery and brick base make it one of the town's more distinctive old landmarks.

Visitors are usually welcome on Saturday mornings, when you may be able to see the mill machinery and get a closer look at how a Dutch grain mill works. It is a small stop, but worthwhile if you are already exploring central Oss.
