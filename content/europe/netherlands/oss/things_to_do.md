Oss punches above its weight for a town of its size. The Jan Cunen Museum is one of the better regional art museums in Noord-Brabant, the pharmaceutical heritage of Organon gives the place an unexpected layer of industrial history, and the story of Jan de Doot — the 17th-century blacksmith who cut his own bladder stone out — is genuinely one of the more peculiar tales attached to any Dutch city.
