Vorstengraf, the 'princely grave' near Oss, is one of the Netherlands' important prehistoric burial mounds. Its significance lies less in visible drama than in what it revealed: elite Bronze Age burial practices in a landscape that can otherwise feel historically quiet to casual visitors.

The mound is a specialist sight, but a worthwhile one if you are interested in archaeology or deep history. It gives Oss a story older than its town centre and industrial growth, stretching the local timeline back thousands of years.
