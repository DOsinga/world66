---
title: Getting There
type: section
---

Purmerend is one of the easiest day trips from Amsterdam. Direct Sprinter trains run from Amsterdam Centraal to Purmerend station roughly every fifteen minutes throughout the day, and the journey takes about twenty minutes. The station sits a short walk from the historic centre — head straight out of the main entrance and you will reach the Kaasmarkt in under ten minutes on foot. No advance booking is needed; just buy a ticket at the machine or tap in with an OV-chipkaart.

Bus connections run from Amsterdam Noord, on the north bank of the IJ, for those coming from the northern neighbourhoods or arriving via the free ferry from Centraal Station. The buses are slower than the train but useful if you are combining Purmerend with a visit to the Waterland villages along the way. Purmerend also makes a natural starting or finishing point for the Waterlandroute cycling path to Amsterdam — a 20-kilometre ride through the polder landscape that most fit cyclists can manage in under two hours. You can rent a bike in Amsterdam, ride out to Purmerend, and return by train with the bike if your legs give out.
