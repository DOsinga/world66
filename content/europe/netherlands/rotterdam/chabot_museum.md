The Chabot Museum is a compact art museum in a sharp 1930s modernist villa near Museumpark. Its collection focuses on the Dutch expressionist Hendrik Chabot, so it works best as a short, rewarding stop for visitors already moving between Boijmans, Kunsthal, and Het Nieuwe Instituut.
