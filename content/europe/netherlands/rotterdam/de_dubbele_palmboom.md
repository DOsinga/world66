De Dubbele Palmboom is a restored 1825 warehouse in Delfshaven, first built for grain and later used as a distillery. The brick building is one of the neighbourhood's more substantial survivors and helps explain Delfshaven's old working-waterfront character.

Today it houses the Dutch Pinball Museum, a hands-on collection of playable machines from different eras. The building is worth a look while wandering Delfshaven; the museum makes it a stronger stop if you like mechanical games and design.
