De Speelman is a restored 18th-century mill in northern Rotterdam. Built in 1712, it was badly damaged during World War II, then restored and moved to its present location.

It is a modest heritage stop rather than one of Rotterdam's major sights, but it adds a useful older counterpoint to the city's better-known modern architecture.
