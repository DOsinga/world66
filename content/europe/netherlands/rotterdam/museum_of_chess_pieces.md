The Museum of Chess-Pieces is a small specialist collection tucked among Rotterdam's Cube Houses. The displays range from traditional sets to playful modern pieces, with enough variety to make even a quick visit feel more curious than dutiful.

It is best treated as an add-on to the Cube Houses and Oude Haven rather than a destination on its own.
