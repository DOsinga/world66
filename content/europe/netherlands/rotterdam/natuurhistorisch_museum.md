Rotterdam's Natuurhistorisch Museum sits in Museumpark, close to the Kunsthal and Het Nieuwe Instituut. Its collection covers animals, plants, fossils, and the natural world in a compact, approachable way.

The museum is smaller and less polished than Rotterdam's big-name cultural stops, but it has character and changing exhibitions. It is a good choice with children, on a rainy day, or if you are already working your way through Museumpark.
