OorlogsVerzetsMuseum is Rotterdam's war and resistance museum, focused on life in the city and the Netherlands during the Second World War. The story is shaped by occupation, resistance, military events, and the destruction that changed Rotterdam's centre.

It is a smaller museum than the city's headline art and architecture stops, but it gives useful context for understanding modern Rotterdam.
