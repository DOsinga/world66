HH. Laurentius en Elisabethkathedraal is Rotterdam's Roman Catholic cathedral, a solid neo-Romanesque church from 1907. The exterior is heavy, but the interior is better balanced and atmospheric, especially around Sunday mass.

If the doors are open before or after services, it is worth a short look while exploring Delfshaven and the western side of the city.
