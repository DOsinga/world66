The Rotterdam Tram Museum occupies an old tram depot and preserves historic trams, metro cars, and buses from Rotterdam and the surrounding region. The depot setting gives the collection more atmosphere than a simple vehicle hall.

When heritage rides are operating, the museum becomes more than a static display: you can connect the exhibits to the streets and routes they once served. Check opening days before making a special trip, as transport museums often keep limited hours.
