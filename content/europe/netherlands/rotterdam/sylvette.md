Sylvette is a large public sculpture in central Rotterdam based on Pablo Picasso's portraits of Sylvette David. It was installed in 1973 and gives the museum quarter a quick piece of open-air modern art.

It is a short stop rather than a destination on its own, but it fits neatly into a walk between Museum Boijmans Van Beuningen, Kunsthal, and Witte de Withstraat.
