Villa Zebra is a children's museum for modern art, with interactive exhibitions, installations, and workshops designed for younger visitors. It is more hands-on than Rotterdam's larger art museums and works well for families.

The museum sits near the river and Kop van Zuid, so it can be combined with De Hef, the Erasmus Bridge, or other stops on the south bank.
