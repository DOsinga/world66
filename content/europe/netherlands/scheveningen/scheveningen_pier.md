The Pier juts 380 metres out into the North Sea: a long concrete walkway leading to four interconnected glass pavilions and, at the very end, a 40-metre Ferris wheel. From the top capsule on a clear day you can see the coast running south to Hook of Holland and north to Wassenaar.

The pavilions hold a bungee jump, a small zip line over the surf, a few bars and casual restaurants, and a viewing deck. It is more touristy than cool, but the structure itself is striking and the views are genuine. Entry to the pier is free; the Ferris wheel and bungee charge per ride. Lit and open into the evening.
