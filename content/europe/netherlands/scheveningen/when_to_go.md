---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

The high season is July and August, with reliably warm-ish (low 20s) days and water just about swimmable. The boulevard is packed; the strandtenten are in full swing; weekends bring day-trippers from across the Randstad. May and June are pleasanter for walking the beach but the water is still cold and many beach clubs are not yet open.

Out of season Scheveningen is a different place: a quiet, slightly weather-beaten Dutch resort with year-round restaurants in the harbour and an enviable population of windsurfers. The Red Bull Knock Out motocross race on the beach in November is a notable autumn event. December and January storms can be spectacular from the Pier.
