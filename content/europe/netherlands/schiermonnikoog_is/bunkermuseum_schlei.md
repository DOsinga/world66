Bunkermuseum Schlei adds a wartime layer to Schiermonnikoog, an island more often visited for dunes, beaches, birds, and cycling. The museum uses remaining German bunker structures to explain the island's role in the Atlantic Wall.

It is small, but the setting makes the history concrete. Visit when open, then continue into the dunes to see how military remains have been absorbed back into the island landscape.
