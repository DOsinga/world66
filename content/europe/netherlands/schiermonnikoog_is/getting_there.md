---
title: Getting There
type: section
---

Schiermonnikoog is car-free and accessible only by ferry from Lauwersoog, a small harbour on the Groningen coast. The crossing takes about 45 minutes.

If you are coming by train, head to Leeuwarden (direct connections from Amsterdam, Rotterdam, and Groningen) and then take Arriva bus 50 from Leeuwarden station to Lauwersoog — a journey of about 50 minutes. The bus is timed to connect with ferry departures. From Groningen, bus 63 makes the connection directly. If you are driving, leave your car at the Lauwersoog car park and board the ferry on foot.
