Rariteitenkabinet Gribus is a small cabinet of curiosities with island, seafaring, and natural-history oddities. The collection ranges from maritime objects to stranger exhibits such as a mammoth foot and an alleged mermaid foetus.

It is best treated as a short, quirky museum stop while exploring Schiermonnikoog village.
