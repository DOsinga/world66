Schelpenmuseum Paal 14 is a small shell museum on Schiermonnikoog, built around a private collection of more than 1,500 shells and molluscs. The De Boer family runs the museum and can help identify shells found on the island's beaches.

It is a modest but fitting stop on a Wadden island trip, especially for families or anyone interested in beachcombing and marine life.
