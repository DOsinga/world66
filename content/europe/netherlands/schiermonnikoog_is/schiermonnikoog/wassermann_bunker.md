The Wassermann Bunker is a remnant of the Second World War Atlantic Wall on Schiermonnikoog. Its heavy concrete form is stark, but the exposed position also makes it one of the island's practical viewpoints.

It works well as part of a cycling or walking loop across the island. The attraction is less about interior exhibits than about linking wartime history with the open dune landscape around it.
