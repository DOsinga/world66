---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

Summer (July–August) is the peak season: warmest weather, best swimming, and the island at its fullest. It is popular with Dutch families and books out quickly — accommodation is limited and should be reserved months ahead. The ferry runs frequently in summer and the village has a cheerful, relaxed atmosphere.

Spring and early autumn are the best times for birdwatching: migrating waders stop in huge numbers on the tidal flats in April–May and again in August–September. The weather is changeable and the beaches less crowded. Winter on Schiermonnikoog is quiet, cold, and atmospheric: storms roll in from the North Sea, the dunes are swept with horizontal rain, and the village is almost deserted. The island has a fierce, minimal beauty in winter that a certain kind of traveller finds more compelling than the summer version.
