Schiphol is the Netherlands' main airport and, for many travellers, the first Dutch place they actually use. It sits in the Haarlemmermeer polder southwest of [Amsterdam](/europe/netherlands/amsterdam), close enough to the city that the train from [Schiphol Airport Station](/europe/netherlands/schiphol/schiphol_airport_station) reaches Amsterdam Centraal in about twenty minutes.

The airport is busy, efficient, and very Dutch in its love of clear signs and practical design. If you have time between flights, there is more here than duty-free shopping. The small [Rijksmuseum Schiphol](/europe/netherlands/schiphol/rijksmuseum_schiphol) brings real Dutch paintings into the terminal, the [Panorama Terrace](/europe/netherlands/schiphol/panorama_terrace) gives close views of aircraft movements, and the plaza-side [Schiphol Clock](/europe/netherlands/schiphol/schiphol_clock) is one of the airport's better bits of public wit.

Schiphol also works as a surprisingly convenient base for short business trips around the southern edge of Amsterdam. [Amstelveen](/europe/netherlands/amstelveen), Haarlem, Leiden, and The Hague all have direct connections from the airport area, though anyone coming for Amsterdam itself is usually better off sleeping in the city.
