Amsterdamse Bos is a large artificial forest and recreation area east of Schiphol, laid out in the 20th century and still one of the best green escapes on the edge of Amsterdam. It has rowing water, woodland paths, lawns, playgrounds, and long cycling routes.

It is not inside the airport, but it is close enough to matter if you have a half-day and want fresh air rather than another terminal coffee. From Schiphol, buses and taxis reach the Amstelveen side quickly; from central Amsterdam, cycling there is the most enjoyable approach.
