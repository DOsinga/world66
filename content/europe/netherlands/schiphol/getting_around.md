---
title: "Getting Around"
type: section
---

Schiphol is designed around one large terminal complex split into departure halls and piers. Walking is the default, and distances can be longer than they look on a map, especially for non-Schengen gates.

The public side centres on Schiphol Plaza, where the train station, buses, shops, and arrivals halls meet. Airside, follow the yellow signage rather than trying to navigate by terminal number; connections are generally straightforward if you leave enough time for security and passport control.
