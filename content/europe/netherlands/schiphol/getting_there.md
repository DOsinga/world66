---
title: "Getting There"
type: section
---

Schiphol is connected to Amsterdam, Rotterdam, The Hague, Utrecht, and much of the Netherlands by direct trains from the station under the terminal. From central [Amsterdam](/europe/netherlands/amsterdam), trains run every few minutes and usually take 14 to 20 minutes.

Long-distance buses and hotel shuttles stop around Schiphol Plaza. Taxis are easy but rarely necessary unless you have heavy luggage or are travelling very early; the rail link is faster and more predictable for most visitors.
