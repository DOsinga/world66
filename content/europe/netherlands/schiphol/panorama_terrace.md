The Panorama Terrace is Schiphol's open-air viewing deck, reached from the public side of the terminal. It gives a good look across the aprons, taxiways, and parked aircraft, with enough movement to keep children and aviation-minded adults happy.

Security rules and weather can affect access, so check locally before making a special trip. When it is open, it is one of the better free things to do around the airport and a pleasant way to clear your head before a flight.
