Planes@Plaza is an airport gift shop with a better shell than most: sections of a former KLM aircraft have been cut and repurposed into the storefront. It is commercial, of course, but it is also a neat bit of airport theatre.

Stop by if you are already on Schiphol Plaza and want a quick souvenir or a photo that actually says "airport" rather than "mall". The shop is most useful as a short diversion, not a destination in itself.
