Rijksmuseum Schiphol is a compact airside gallery, run by the Amsterdam museum, with a rotating display of Dutch paintings and objects. It is not a substitute for the real [Rijksmuseum](/europe/netherlands/amsterdam/rijksmuseum), but it is a civilised surprise in an environment usually dominated by perfume counters and gate numbers.

The display is free and small enough to visit in ten or fifteen minutes. For a traveller with a long connection, that is exactly the point: a little Dutch art without leaving the terminal.
