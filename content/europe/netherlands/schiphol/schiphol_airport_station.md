Schiphol Airport Station sits directly beneath the terminal and is the reason the airport works so smoothly for independent travellers. You come up by escalator into Schiphol Plaza, with arrivals, departures, ticket machines, and shops all within a short walk.

Trains run constantly to Amsterdam Centraal, Zuid, Utrecht, Leiden, The Hague, Rotterdam, and beyond. If you are landing with a contactless bank card, you can usually tap straight through the gates; just remember to tap out at the other end.
