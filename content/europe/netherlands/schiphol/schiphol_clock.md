The Schiphol Clock, designed by Maarten Baas, is a clever piece of Dutch design humour on Schiphol Plaza. From a distance it looks as if a real worker is standing inside the clock, painting the hands forward minute by minute.

It takes only a moment to see, but it is worth seeking out if you are passing through the plaza. Airports are full of generic objects; this one has a little personality.
