Bastionder is Den Bosch's military history museum and fortification heritage centre, occupying part of the old city walls in a beautifully preserved 17th-century bastion. The museum focuses on the history of the fortified city and the role the walls played in the many sieges Den Bosch endured over its history, most notably the Spanish siege of 1629 when Frederick Henry of Orange finally captured the city after a four-month blockade.

The setting inside a genuine surviving bastion is the real draw here — you can walk along the ramparts and peer into the casements where the garrison once sheltered from artillery. Combined with the underground canal tour nearby, it gives a good picture of how the city's defences worked.
