---
title: "Getting Around"
type: section
---

The historic centre of Den Bosch is compact and walkable — all the main sights are within 15 minutes of the central market square. Bicycles can be rented from shops near the station and are the natural Dutch way to reach the Bolwoningen sphere houses and other outlying points. City buses serve the wider urban area.

One unusual option: a boat tour through the Binnendieze, Den Bosch's underground canal system that runs beneath the old town. The canals date from the medieval period when waterways ran through the city and were partly built over as the city developed. Tours depart from various points in the centre and last about 45 minutes — a genuinely unusual experience and worth doing.
