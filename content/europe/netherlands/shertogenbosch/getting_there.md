---
title: "Getting There"
type: section
---

Den Bosch sits on the main Amsterdam–Eindhoven–Eindhoven–Brussels railway corridor and is well connected. Direct trains run from Amsterdam (about 70 minutes), Utrecht (30 minutes), Eindhoven (25 minutes), and Breda (30 minutes). The station is a 10-minute walk from the old town centre. There is no commercial airport serving the city directly; the nearest are Eindhoven Airport (40 minutes by bus) and Amsterdam Schiphol (about 90 minutes by train).

By car, Den Bosch is at the junction of the A2 (Amsterdam–Eindhoven) and A59 motorways and easy to reach from most of the Netherlands. Parking in the city centre is limited and expensive; the park-and-ride facilities on the edge of the city are cheaper and linked by frequent buses.
