The Jheronimus Bosch Art Center is dedicated to the life and work of Hieronymus Bosch, the city's most famous son, who was born here around 1450 and spent his entire life in Den Bosch. The centre is housed in a 15th-century church — an appropriate setting — and uses high-quality reproductions of all Bosch's known works alongside interactive displays and scholarly commentary to build a picture of what Bosch was painting and why.

The centre opened in its current form in 2016, as part of the city's 500th anniversary commemoration of the painter's death, and has been one of the best art centres in the Netherlands ever since. Even visitors who know Bosch's work from other museums will find new details here. The gift shop sells some genuinely good art prints.
