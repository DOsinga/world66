The Noordbrabants Museum is the regional museum of North Brabant, housed in a handsome neoclassical building in the old town. The collection covers art and history from prehistoric times to the present, with a particular emphasis on the art of the province — which means a good deal of Flemish and Dutch Golden Age painting, and an important collection of work by Vincent van Gogh, who was born in the North Brabant town of Zundert. The museum also holds some original Bosch-related material that the Art Center doesn't have.

The building has been impressively renovated in recent years and the permanent collection is well-displayed. Plan to spend at least 90 minutes here. The museum café is one of the better options for lunch in the area.
