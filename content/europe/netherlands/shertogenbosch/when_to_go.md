---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

Den Bosch is pleasant year-round, with a mild maritime climate. Spring and early autumn are the best times for walking the old town without crowds or rain. Summer is busy with Dutch day-trippers.

The major event is Carnival in February, which transforms the city for three days before Lent. Den Bosch has one of the most celebrated Carnival celebrations in the Netherlands — the city adopts the mock name Oeteldonk and everyone dresses up. The streets are packed and accommodation fills up fast; book well in advance if you want to experience it. If you're not a Carnival fan, this is exactly the wrong time to visit.
