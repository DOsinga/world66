Sneek — or Snits in Frisian — is one of the larger towns in Friesland and the undisputed capital of Dutch watersports. The town sits at the heart of a vast network of lakes and canals, and in summer it fills with sailors, windsurfers, and boat-trippers making the most of the Frisian lakes. The [Sneekermeer](/europe/netherlands/sneek/sneekermeer) just east of town is the centrepiece, and the August **Sneekweek** regatta is the largest inland sailing event in Europe.

The old centre is compact and attractive. The 17th-century [Waterpoort](/europe/netherlands/sneek/waterpoort), a twin-towered water gate straddling a canal, is the town's most photographed sight and a fine piece of Frisian architecture. Beyond it, the centre is pleasant to wander — the late-Gothic [Martinikerk](/europe/netherlands/sneek/martinikerk) and the Rococo [Stadhuis](/europe/netherlands/sneek/stadhuis_sneek) face each other on the Marktstraat, and the [Fries Scheepvaart Museum](/europe/netherlands/sneek/fries_scheepvaart_museum) on the Kleinzand canal covers a thousand years of Frisian shipping. The countryside south of town holds smaller treasures: the canal village of [IJlst](/europe/netherlands/sneek/ijlst), the working sawmill [De Rat](/europe/netherlands/sneek/de_rat), and the 17th-century manor house [Epemastate](/europe/netherlands/sneek/epemastate) at Ysbrechtum.

If you're passing through Friesland, Sneek makes a good base for a day or two on the water. Bike hire is easy to find, and the surrounding countryside is flat and quiet.
