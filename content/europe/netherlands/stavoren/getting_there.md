---
title: "Getting There"
type: section
---

Stavoren is the western terminus of the railway line that runs across southern Friesland from Leeuwarden via Sneek. Trains arrive roughly hourly during the day. The station sits two minutes' walk from the harbour and the centre of the town — small enough to qualify as a request stop in feel if not in fact.

By road, the town is reached on the N359 from the north or via the country lanes that wind south from Workum. The car ferry across the IJsselmeer to [Enkhuizen](/europe/netherlands/enkhuizen) runs several times a day in summer and is the most enjoyable way to arrive — three hours across the lake with a cycle deck if you want to continue on two wheels.
