The [Koffiemolen](/europe/netherlands/terschelling/formerum/koffiemolen) windmill is the obvious stop for coffee and cranberry pie, with its collection of antique coffee mills inside. The [Wrakkenmuseum](/europe/netherlands/terschelling/formerum/wrakkenmuseum) also runs a small café on the museum's terrace.
