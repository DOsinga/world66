---
title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Midsland is a good base for cycling the length of Terschelling. The 40-kilometre "Traces in the Sand" route takes you through dunes, pine forest, salt marshes, and along the wide North Sea beach. The village itself has a church dating from 1881, a scattering of traditional Frisian cottages, and the island's most lively cafe scene outside West-Terschelling.

Every summer the Sint Jan's Draverij horse race is held here — one of the oldest festivals on the island, held on the long grass behind the village. At the start of September the same streets host Rock 'n Roll Street, a weekend of 1950s music, classic cars, and dancing.
