---
title: "Day Trips"
type: section
---

Oudeschild sits within easy cycling range of most of Texel's attractions, making it a natural base for exploring the island.

**Den Burg** (4 km) is Texel's largest town, with the main market, shops, and the Texels Museum covering island history and natural history. The weekly market on Monday mornings is worth catching.

**De Koog** (11 km) is Texel's main beach resort, with a long stretch of North Sea beach backed by dunes. It's busier and more commercial than Oudeschild, but the beach itself is excellent — wide, clean, and open to the Atlantic.

**De Slufter** is a unique tidal inlet in the northwest of the island where the dunes open directly to the sea, allowing salt water to flood in. The resulting landscape — a mix of salt marsh, tidal channels, and dune scrub — is botanically rich and excellent for birds.

**Den Hoorn** (8 km) is arguably Texel's most picturesque village, with a cluster of white-painted houses around a small church and windmill.

**The Hoge Berg** area near the centre of the island offers the closest thing to elevation on a very flat island, with old farmhouses and a landscape that recalls medieval Texel.

For a longer excursion, the ferry back to Den Helder opens up a day trip to **Den Helder** itself, which has the Helders Marinemuseum (Dutch naval museum) and the historic naval dockyards.
