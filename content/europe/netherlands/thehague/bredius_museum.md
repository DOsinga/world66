Bredius Museum presents the private collection of Abraham Bredius, the Dutch art historian and former director of the Mauritshuis. The collection is strongest in Dutch Baroque painting, with drawings, porcelain, silver, and decorative arts adding domestic texture.

Its location near the Binnenhof and Mauritshuis makes it easy to fold into a central Hague museum day. It is quieter and more intimate than the city's better-known galleries.
