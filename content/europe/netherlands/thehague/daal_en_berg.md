Daal en Berg is a 1920s housing development in The Hague designed by Jan Wils. The complex is listed among the Netherlands' top rijksmonumenten and is a useful stop for travellers interested in Dutch modernism and the De Stijl circle beyond the better-known museums.

This is an exterior architecture stop rather than a museum. Walk the surrounding streets and look at how the repeated forms, brickwork, and quiet geometry turn ordinary housing into a carefully composed ensemble.
