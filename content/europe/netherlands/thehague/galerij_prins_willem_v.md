Galerij Prins Willem V is an 18th-century picture gallery connected with the Mauritshuis, hung densely in the old style with paintings from the royal collection. It is a compact but rewarding stop near the Binnenhof and Gevangenpoort, best combined with the Mauritshuis for a fuller look at The Hague's Golden Age art collections.
