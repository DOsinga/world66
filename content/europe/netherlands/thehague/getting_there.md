---
title: Getting There
type: section
---

The Hague does not have its own airport; Schiphol Airport near Amsterdam is the main international hub. From Schiphol, direct trains run to Den Haag Centraal station in about 50 minutes, departing frequently throughout the day. Intercity trains also connect The Hague directly with Amsterdam, Rotterdam, Utrecht, and Leiden, making it easy to combine with other Dutch cities.

By car, The Hague is on the A4 and A12 motorways. Parking in the centre is expensive; the P+R facilities on the outskirts with tram connections are much cheaper. By international coach, FlixBus and Eurolines serve The Hague from several European cities.
