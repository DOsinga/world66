Museon is an interactive science and culture museum near Kunstmuseum Den Haag. Its exhibits are built for hands-on learning, with natural history, technology, sustainability, and global culture presented in a way that works well for younger visitors.

It is especially useful for families or rainy days in The Hague. The museum district location makes it easy to combine with the Kunstmuseum or a tram ride onward to Scheveningen.
