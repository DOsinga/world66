Westbroekpark is one of The Hague's elegant green spaces, set between the city and Scheveningen and best known for its rose garden. In season, the rosarium gives the park a formal, colorful focus that contrasts with the dunes and beach nearby.

The park is easy to combine with Madurodam, Scheveningen or a cycling route through the city's northern green belt. It is a relaxed stop rather than a major sight, but a good one when the roses are out.
