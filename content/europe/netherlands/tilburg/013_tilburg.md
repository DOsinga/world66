013 is one of the best mid-size live music venues in the Netherlands — a former factory building near the city centre that hosts rock, metal, electronic, and indie acts in two halls of different sizes. The programming is eclectic but leans toward alternative and heavy music, and the sound quality is notably good.

Every April, 013 is the hub of the Roadburn Festival, a four-day event that has become one of Europe's most important gatherings for experimental and heavy music. The festival spreads across multiple Tilburg venues but 013 is the main stage. Outside festival season, the venue runs a regular programme of touring acts and local nights most weekends. Worth checking the calendar before you visit — there is often something on worth seeing.
