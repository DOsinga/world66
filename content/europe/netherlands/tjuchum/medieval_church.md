title: Medieval Village Church
type: poi

Tjuchem's small medieval church is typical of the sturdy Romanesque brick churches that dot the Groningen countryside. Dating back several centuries, it sits at the heart of the hamlet and has outlasted most of the community that once surrounded it.

The church is modest in scale but pleasing in its simplicity — thick walls, a modest tower, and the kind of quiet permanence that makes Groningen's rural churches so appealing. Combined with a visit to the Lenin statue just steps away, it makes for an oddly satisfying pairing of the ancient and the recent.
