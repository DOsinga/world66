Tjuchem is a blink-and-you'll-miss-it hamlet in the flat Groningen countryside, but it punches above its weight with one genuinely surprising sight: a nine-metre-tall Lenin statue standing in a private garden. The surrounding landscape of wide skies, canals, and reed beds is classic Groningen — quietly beautiful and rarely crowded.
