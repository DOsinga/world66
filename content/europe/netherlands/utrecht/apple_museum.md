The Apple Museum is a small specialist museum devoted to the history of Apple computers and design. Its appeal is in the vintage machines, rare objects and hands-on displays, especially if you remember the early Mac years or travel with someone who does.

It sits outside the centre of Utrecht, so treat it as a deliberate side trip rather than a quick stop between the old-town sights.
