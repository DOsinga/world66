De Inktpot is a massive brick office building near Utrecht Centraal, famous locally for the small flying saucer sculpture perched on its roof. The UFO was added for the Panorama 2000 art exhibition and has stayed as one of the city's stranger skyline details.

The building is mainly an exterior stop, but it pairs well with the nearby Letters of Utrecht and the station-area sights if you enjoy odd urban landmarks.
