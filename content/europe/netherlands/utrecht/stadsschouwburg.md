Utrecht's Stadsschouwburg is both a working theatre and a Dudok landmark. The 1930s building has the clean brick lines and careful proportions that made Willem Dudok one of the Netherlands' most admired modern architects.

Most theatre performances are in Dutch, but dance, music, and touring productions make the programme broader than that. Even without tickets, the exterior is worth a look if you are walking between the old centre and the eastern side of town.
