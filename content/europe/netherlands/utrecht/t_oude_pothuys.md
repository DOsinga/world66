T' Oude Pothuys is an intimate Utrecht music cellar where jam sessions and small concerts are the point. The setting, tucked by the canals, suits the scale: you come less for a polished venue than for the chance to hear skilled musicians in a close room.

Check the programme before going, since the experience depends on the night. When the music is good, it is a strong way to end a day in Utrecht.
