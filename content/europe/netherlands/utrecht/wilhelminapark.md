Wilhelminapark is one of Utrecht's nicest breathing spaces, especially if you want to see the city away from the canal belt. Its lawns, pond, mature trees, and surrounding villas give Utrecht-Oost a gracious, lived-in calm.

It is not a headline sight, but it is a good local stop on a bike ride or walk east of the centre. Bring coffee, sit by the water, and use it as a reminder that Utrecht's appeal is not only medieval towers and busy canals.
