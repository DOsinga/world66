The Veluwe is the Netherlands at its least urban: a broad sweep of forest, heath, sand drifts, and old estates in Gelderland. It is not wilderness in the dramatic sense -- roads, villages, and cycle paths are never far away -- but in a country this densely settled, the space feels generous.

The best-known pocket is [De Hoge Veluwe National Park](/europe/netherlands/veluwe/de_hoge_veluwe_national_park), where free white bicycles connect woods, dunes, heath, the [Kröller-Müller Museum](/europe/netherlands/veluwe/kroller_muller_museum), and Berlage's [Jachthuis Sint Hubertus](/europe/netherlands/veluwe/jachthuis_sint_hubertus). Further east, [Veluwezoom National Park](/europe/netherlands/veluwe/veluwezoom_national_park) has more relief than most Dutch landscapes, with the [Posbank](/europe/netherlands/veluwe/posbank) giving wide views over heather and forest.

The region is easy to approach from [Apeldoorn](/europe/netherlands/apeldoorn), [Arnhem](/europe/netherlands/arnhem), [Ede](/europe/netherlands/ede), [Heerde](/europe/netherlands/heerde), and [Wageningen](/europe/netherlands/wageningen). Come for cycling, walking, art, and a reminder that the Netherlands is not only canals and flat polder.
