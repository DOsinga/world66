De Hoge Veluwe National Park is the Veluwe's headline attraction and one of the easiest places in the Netherlands to combine nature with serious art. Heath, pine forest, drifting sand, and open grassland sit behind park gates, with a network of paths and free white bicycles connecting the entrances.

The park is managed more like a cultural landscape than untouched wilderness, and that is part of its charm. You can cycle from a quiet stretch of heath to the [Kröller-Müller Museum](/europe/netherlands/veluwe/kroller_muller_museum), stop at the [Museonder](/europe/netherlands/veluwe/museonder), and still have time for a detour to [Jachthuis Sint Hubertus](/europe/netherlands/veluwe/jachthuis_sint_hubertus).
