Elspeetse Heide is one of the quieter heathland areas on the northern Veluwe. It lacks the name recognition of the Posbank or Hoge Veluwe, which is exactly why walkers who prefer space may like it.

The best time is late August, when the heather colours the open ground. Outside that window it is still pleasant, especially as part of a longer cycling day through the villages and forests around Elspeet.
