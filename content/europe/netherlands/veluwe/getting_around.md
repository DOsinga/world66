---
title: "Getting Around"
type: section
---

Cycling is the Veluwe's natural pace. Dedicated cycle paths cross the forests and heath, and many villages rent sturdy Dutch bikes or e-bikes. Inside De Hoge Veluwe National Park, the white bicycles are included with admission and make it easy to link the museum, visitor centre, and heathland.

Public transport works for the main towns and some park entrances, but it is less useful for remote sand drifts or early starts. If you are relying on buses, check the last return before setting out.
