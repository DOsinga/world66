---
title: "Getting There"
type: section
---

The Veluwe has no single gateway. [Apeldoorn](/europe/netherlands/apeldoorn), [Arnhem](/europe/netherlands/arnhem), Ede-Wageningen, and Harderwijk are the most useful rail approaches, with buses continuing to park entrances and villages.

Drivers have the easiest access to scattered trailheads, but a car is not essential for the classic Hoge Veluwe visit: buses connect Ede-Wageningen and Arnhem with Otterlo and Hoenderloo, the main entrances to the national park.
