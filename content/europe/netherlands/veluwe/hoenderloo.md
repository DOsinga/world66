Hoenderloo is another useful gateway to De Hoge Veluwe, sitting among woods on the southern side of the park. It has a quieter feel than Otterlo and works well for walkers and cyclists who want to start close to the forest.

The village itself is simple, with cafes and places to rent or repair bikes. Its value is location: from here you can be on Veluwe paths within minutes.
