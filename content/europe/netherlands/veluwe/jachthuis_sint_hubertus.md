Jachthuis Sint Hubertus is one of the Netherlands' great country houses, designed by H.P. Berlage for Anton and Helene Kröller-Müller. Its brick massing, tower, lake setting, and carefully controlled interiors make it feel more like a secular chapel to early modern design than a hunting lodge.

Visits are usually by guided tour, which is worth taking if you care about architecture. If you do not go inside, the exterior and lake still make a fine cycling stop between the park centre and the heath.
