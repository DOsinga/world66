Jardin d'émail is one of the most recognisable works in the Kröller-Müller sculpture garden: a walk-in, black-and-white enamel landscape by Jean Dubuffet. It looks playful from a distance and stranger when you are standing inside it.

The work is a useful reminder that the Veluwe is not only about quiet nature. In this corner of the park, modern art interrupts the forest with something deliberately artificial, graphic, and odd.
