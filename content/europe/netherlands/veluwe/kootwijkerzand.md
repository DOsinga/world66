Kootwijkerzand is a broad sand-drift landscape west of Kootwijk, often described as the largest active sand drift in Western Europe. The open sand, sparse grasses, and pine edges feel almost coastal, though you are deep inland.

It is best visited on foot, preferably outside the hottest part of a summer day when the exposed sand can become fierce. The watchtower gives a useful overview of how abrupt the transition is between forest and open drift.
