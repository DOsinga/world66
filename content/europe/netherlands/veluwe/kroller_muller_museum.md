The Kröller-Müller Museum is the cultural heart of De Hoge Veluwe. Its Van Gogh collection is exceptional, but the museum is not just a pilgrimage stop for one painter: Seurat, Mondrian, Picasso, and other modern masters give the galleries real depth.

The sculpture garden is just as important as the indoor collection. Works by artists including Jean Dubuffet, Barbara Hepworth, and others sit among lawns, trees, and forest edges, so the museum visit naturally spills back into the landscape.
