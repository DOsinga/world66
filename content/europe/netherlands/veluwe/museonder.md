The Museonder is an underground museum near the visitor centre of De Hoge Veluwe, focused on what happens below the forest floor. Exhibits cover roots, bones, groundwater, and the animals that shape the soil.

It is especially good with children, and it adds useful context before a walk through the park. The point is simple but effective: the Veluwe is not just trees and heath above ground, but a living system underneath your feet.
