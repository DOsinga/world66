Otterlo is a modest village, but it matters because one of the main entrances to De Hoge Veluwe sits on its edge. Buses, bike rentals, cafes, and visitor services make it a practical base for the park.

Do not expect a museum town with grand streets; the attraction is the access. If you want to enter the park early, avoid a rushed day trip, or stay near the Kröller-Müller Museum, Otterlo does the job well.
