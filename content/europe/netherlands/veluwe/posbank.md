The Posbank is the classic Veluwezoom viewpoint, a high heathland ridge by Dutch standards, with views over rolling purple slopes in late summer. It can be busy when the heather blooms, but the view justifies the fuss.

Come early or late in the day if you can. The light is better, the paths are quieter, and the landscape feels less like a weekend outing from the Randstad and more like the open country it really is.
