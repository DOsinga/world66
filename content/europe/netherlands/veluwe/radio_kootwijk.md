Radio Kootwijk is the Veluwe's strangest building: a monumental concrete transmitter hall standing in open heath and forest. Locals call it the cathedral, and the nickname fits; the building has the solemnity of a church and the silhouette of a machine-age temple.

The surrounding area is excellent walking country, with heath, forest, and views towards the Kootwijkerzand. Interiors are not always open, but the exterior alone is worth the detour if you like architecture with a little menace.
