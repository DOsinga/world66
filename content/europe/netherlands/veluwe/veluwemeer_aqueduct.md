The Veluwemeer Aqueduct is a small but memorable piece of Dutch engineering near Harderwijk, where the lake water passes over the road instead of the road bridging the lake. It is more clever than beautiful, but that cleverness is very Dutch.

It is not worth a long detour by itself. If you are already travelling along the Veluwe's western edge, it makes a quick stop and a good reminder that Dutch landscape is often designed as much as discovered.
