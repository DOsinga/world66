Veluwezoom National Park covers the southeastern edge of the Veluwe, close to [Arnhem](/europe/netherlands/arnhem). It has more height and drama than most Dutch landscapes, with rolling wooded ridges, heathland, grazing cattle, and views that open suddenly from the hills.

The park is particularly good for walkers who want a less managed feel than De Hoge Veluwe. The [Posbank](/europe/netherlands/veluwe/posbank) is the famous viewpoint, but the quieter forest paths are the reason to stay longer.
