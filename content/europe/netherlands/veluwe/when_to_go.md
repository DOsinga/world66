---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

Late August and early September are the showpiece weeks, when the heath turns purple and the low light makes the open landscapes glow. Spring is quieter and good for cycling, with fresh leaves in the beech woods.

Summer weekends can be busy around Otterlo, Hoenderloo, and the Posbank. Winter has its own appeal: bare trees, empty paths, and sometimes frost on the sand drifts, though many days are damp and grey.
