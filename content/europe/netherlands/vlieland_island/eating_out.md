---
title: "Eating Out"
type: section
---

Eating out on Vlieland is small-scale and concentrated almost entirely on the Dorpsstraat in Oost-Vlieland. Most places do unfussy bistro food — North Sea fish, mussels in season, local lamb, bread from the village bakery, and the Wadden specialty of *Vlielanderkoek* (a sweet biscuit). Standards are decent and prices are above the mainland but not extortionate.

In summer book ahead — the demand greatly outstrips supply on warm Friday and Saturday evenings. The few beach pavilions on the North Sea side serve lunch and snacks during the day. There is no real fine dining; the island does not pretend to it.
