---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

**May to September** is the main season. The weather is warm enough for the beach (though the North Sea is rarely tepid), the ferries run a full schedule, all the restaurants and bike-hire shops are open, and the Vliehors bombing range is closed for the holidays. **July and August** are when the families come; the village is busy in the evenings but never overwhelmed.

**Late spring (May–June)** has the best of the wildflowers in the dunes and the longest evenings — sunset after 22:00 in June. **September** is quieter, the sea is at its warmest, and the wind drops as the autumn settles in. From **November through February** the island closes down somewhat — fewer ferries, many places shut — but for serious walkers and birdwatchers the winter has its own appeal. The **Oerol** festival of street and landscape theatre runs in June, mostly on Terschelling but with events that spill over to Vlieland.
