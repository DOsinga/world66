Cycling is the primary mode of transport on Schiermonnikoog — cars are banned for visitors, and the island's flat terrain and well-maintained cycle paths make it ideal. You can rent bikes at the harbour on arrival; bring them on the ferry if you have specific ones. The island has about 30 kilometres of marked cycle routes.

The circuit around the eastern end of the island — through the dunes, along the south side to the edge of the salt marshes, and back along the beach access paths — takes about two hours at a relaxed pace. The terrain is almost entirely flat, with the dunes providing the only gradient. Children can manage it easily, and families with young kids make up a significant portion of the island's visitors in summer.
