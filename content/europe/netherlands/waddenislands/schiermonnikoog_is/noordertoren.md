The Noordertoren is Schiermonnikoog's red lighthouse, a working seamark that has watched the island's north side since the nineteenth century. It is the more active of the island's two historic towers and remains one of Schiermonnikoog's clearest visual symbols.

You come for the setting as much as the structure: dunes, wind, North Sea light, and the sense of a small island still oriented toward shipping and weather. Even if you cannot go inside, the lighthouse makes a fine walking target from the village or beach paths.
