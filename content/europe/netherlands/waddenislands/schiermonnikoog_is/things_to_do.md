---
title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Schiermonnikoog is the smallest inhabited Wadden Island and the most stripped-back of the five. There are no cars except for essential services, no tourist traps, and a single small village. The activities are elemental: cycling through the dunes, walking the enormous beaches, and exploring the tidal flats on the south side of the island.

The island is almost entirely national park. The north shore has one of the widest beaches in the Netherlands — several hundred metres of sand at low tide, backed by dune ridges. The south shore's tidal flats are prime birdwatching territory, and a guided mudflat walk (wadlopen) into the Wadden Sea is the most memorable thing you can do here.
