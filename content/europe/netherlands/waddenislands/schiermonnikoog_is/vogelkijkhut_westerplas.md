Vogelkijkhut Westerplas is a birdwatching hide overlooking one of Schiermonnikoog's important freshwater and marsh areas. It is a small stop, but it fits the island perfectly: quiet, windblown, and focused on birds rather than crowds.

Bring binoculars and patience, especially during migration periods. The hide is best combined with a cycling loop across the island, when it becomes one pause in a wider landscape of dunes, polders, and mudflats.
