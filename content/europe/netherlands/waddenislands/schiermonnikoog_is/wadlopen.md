Wadlopen — mudflat walking — is the Wadden Islands' most distinctive activity, and Schiermonnikoog is one of the best places to do it. At low tide, the tidal flats on the island's south side are exposed: vast stretches of grey-brown mud and sand, cut by channels, dotted with lugworm casts, and scattered with cockles, mussels, and razor clams. You sink in to your ankles or your knees depending on where you step.

Guided wadlopen walks depart from the island and cross to the mainland and back, or traverse sections of the flats with a bird guide. The Wadden Society (Waddenvereniging) and local operators run walks throughout the summer season. Book well in advance, wear clothes you don't mind destroying, and expect to be cold, wet, and completely sold on the experience by the end.
