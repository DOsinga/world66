Formerum is a small village about halfway along Terschelling, between [West-Terschelling](/europe/netherlands/waddenislands/terschelling/westterschelling) and Oosterend. The village is dominated by its working windmill, [De Koffiemolen](/europe/netherlands/waddenislands/terschelling/formerum/koffiemolen), where you can stop for coffee and cranberry pie. The eccentric [Wrakkenmuseum](/europe/netherlands/waddenislands/terschelling/formerum/wrakkenmuseum) — a shipwreck museum built up over decades from salvage off the Terschelling coast — is the other reason to stop.

[The beach](/europe/netherlands/waddenislands/terschelling/formerum/beach) is about 2 km north, reached by a pleasant path through forest and dunes. On the way you pass a pony-riding stable popular with children. The village has a small supermarket and several bike rental shops — cycling is the main way to get around Terschelling.
