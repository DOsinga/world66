Midsland is the main village on [Terschelling](/europe/netherlands/waddenislands/terschelling), sitting roughly in the centre of the island. It is where most visitors end up — the village has the island's best concentration of restaurants, cafes, and shops, and several campsites are within cycling distance.

Despite the tourist infrastructure, Midsland keeps its character. The old village centre has traditional Frisian houses and the [Meslaner Church](/europe/netherlands/waddenislands/terschelling/midsland/meslaner_church), a listed 19th-century building, and the wide [Midsland aan Zee beach](/europe/netherlands/waddenislands/terschelling/midsland/midsland_aan_zee_beach) on the north side of the island is just a short bike ride away. In summer the village fills with cyclists, and the annual [Sint Jan's Draverij](/europe/netherlands/waddenislands/terschelling/midsland/sint_jans_draverij) horse race is one of the most popular local events on the Wadden Islands.
