title: "Getting Around"
type: section
---

Midsland is built for cycling. The island has 80 kilometres of dedicated cycle paths, and from Midsland you can reach the beach in ten minutes, [Oosterend](/europe/netherlands/waddenislands/terschelling/oosterend) in about 45 minutes, and [West-Terschelling](/europe/netherlands/waddenislands/terschelling) in half an hour. Bikes can be hired in the village.

The 40-kilometre "Traces in the Sand" circuit is the most popular full-island route, taking you through pine forest, across dune heathland, down to the beach, and back through the polder — a good day out for anyone reasonably fit. Shorter loops from Midsland through [Formerum](/europe/netherlands/waddenislands/terschelling/formerum) are easy and flat.
