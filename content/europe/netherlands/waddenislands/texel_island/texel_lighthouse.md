Texel Lighthouse stands at the northern tip of the island, where the North Sea and Wadden Sea meet. The red tower is one of Texel's clearest landmarks, visible across the dunes and beaches around De Cocksdorp.

Climb the stairs for a wide view over the island, the sea, and nearby Vlieland. Displays inside explain the lighthouse's history, including its wartime damage and the role it still plays for shipping.
