---
title: "Getting Around"
type: section
---

**Cars are not allowed** on Vlieland for visitors — leave yours at the long-stay car park in Harlingen on the mainland. Once on the island, the choices are simple: walk, cycle, or take the village bus.

**Cycling is the default.** Bike rental shops cluster around the ferry terminal and along the Dorpsstraat; expect to pay around €10–15 per day for a regular bike, more for e-bikes and beach tandems. Reservations are sensible in high season. The cycle path network is 26 km, almost entirely flat and well surfaced.

**The village bus** runs the length of the village and out to the beach pavilion in summer — handy when the wind is unmanageable or with small children. The **Vliehors Express** tractor-trailer is the only way to reach the western tip of the island, with several round trips a day from Oost-Vlieland.
