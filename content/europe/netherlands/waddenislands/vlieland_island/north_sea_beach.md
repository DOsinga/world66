Vlieland's North Sea beach runs the whole length of the island — twelve kilometres of fine sand backed by dunes, broken only by a handful of marked beach exits from the cycle path. Even in the height of summer there is space for everyone; you can walk for an hour at the edge of the tide and barely meet anyone once you are a few hundred metres from the access points.

The official "main" beach (Strandpaviljoen) opposite Oost-Vlieland has a beach bar, showers, and a lifeguard in season. The other exits are unmanned — bring your own water and shade. The wind is the constant; even on warm days a windbreak helps. Seal-spotting is common at the western end. Naturism is permitted on a marked stretch of beach south of the village.
