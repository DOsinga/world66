---
title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Vlieland is mostly dune, beach, and sky. The activities are the obvious ones — cycling the network of paths, walking the long North Sea beach, climbing the lighthouse for the view across the Wadden Sea, and sitting on a sunny terrace in Oost-Vlieland. Half the island is uninhabited; the western end is a NATO bombing range that becomes a vast, wind-scoured plain you can cross by guided tractor tour.
