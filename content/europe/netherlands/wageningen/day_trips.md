Wageningen sits at the intersection of two compelling day trip directions. Head east to [Arnhem](/europe/netherlands/arnhem) and the **Airborne Museum at Oosterbeek**, which tells the story of Operation Market Garden and the disastrous Battle of Arnhem in September 1944 — essential context for anyone who has visited Hotel de Wereld. Head north into the [De Hoge Veluwe National Park](/europe/netherlands/dehogeveluwe), the large forested heath reserve that contains the Kröller-Müller Museum with one of the world's most important Van Gogh collections. Both destinations are under 40 minutes by car or bus.
