Het Depot Sculpture Gallery is one of Wageningen's most distinctive cultural stops, focused on contemporary sculpture of the human figure. The collection is shown in refined gallery spaces with garden areas, so the visit feels calm and deliberate rather than crowded.

It suits Wageningen well: thoughtful, compact, and slightly unexpected in a town better known for the university and river landscape. If you enjoy sculpture, it is worth prioritising over a generic local museum stop.
