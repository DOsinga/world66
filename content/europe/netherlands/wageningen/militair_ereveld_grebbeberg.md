Militair Ereveld Grebbeberg is a Dutch military cemetery on the Grebbeberg, where heavy fighting took place during the German invasion in May 1940. Around 800 people are buried here, most of them soldiers killed in the Battle of the Grebbeberg.

The cemetery is a sober stop for visitors interested in the Netherlands' Second World War history.
