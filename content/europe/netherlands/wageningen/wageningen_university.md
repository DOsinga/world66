**Wageningen University & Research** (WUR) is the reason this small Gelderland town has an outsized reputation. Ranked consistently as the world's leading university for agricultural sciences, it draws students and researchers from across the globe — walk the campus and you will hear half a dozen languages before you reach the canteen. Nearly a quarter of Wageningen's population are students.

The campus itself, spread across the wooded western edge of town, is worth a walk. The main Leeuwenborch and Forum buildings are functional modern architecture, but the surrounding gardens and tree-lined paths are genuinely pleasant. The campus bleeds into the town without a sharp boundary — the cafes and bike-choked streets of the centre are full of students. If you want to understand the Dutch dominance of global food science and agri-tech, a campus visit makes it tangible. The university also runs the **World Soil Museum** and various research gardens open to visitors.
