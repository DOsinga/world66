The **Wageningse Berg** is the southernmost finger of the Veluwe, the large forested moraine ridge that runs across the centre of Gelderland. From the crest of the hill, there are clear views south over the Rhine flood plain and west toward Rhenen. The slope is forested with oak, beech, and mixed woodland, crossed by walking and cycling paths that connect to the broader Veluwe network.

It is a 20-minute bike ride from the town centre and makes for an easy half-day outing. The paths are well-marked and mostly flat once you are on the ridge top. In autumn the beech trees turn gold and the forest is particularly attractive. The area is part of a larger green corridor that connects Wageningen to the Hoge Veluwe National Park further north — serious cyclists can string together a full-day loop taking in the Berg, the Rhine bank, and the Rijnstrangen wetlands.
