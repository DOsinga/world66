---
title: "Getting There"
type: section
---

The Weerribben is in northwestern Overijssel, between the towns of Steenwijk and Blokzijl. **By car** is the most practical option: from Steenwijk (itself reachable by direct train from Amsterdam in about 1.5 hours), the park entrances at Ossenzijl and Kalenberg are 12–15 km west on minor roads.

**By train and bus** from Amsterdam: take the intercity train to Steenwijk and then local bus towards Ossenzijl. Services are limited on weekends; check the NS and Connexxion timetables in advance. The journey takes about two hours total.

**Cycling** is excellent in this flat landscape. [Giethoorn](/europe/netherlands/giethoorn), the famous car-free village, is about 15 km southeast of the park and makes a natural base for combining both. Bicycle hire is available in Steenwijk.
