---
title: When to Go
type: section
---

Zandvoort works best on a hot weekday in **June or early September** — long days, warm sand, and a beach that has not yet filled up with the city's weekend crowds. **July and August** weekends are reliably packed: a sunny Saturday on the Zandvoort sand means six-deep queues for the train back to Amsterdam.

The other date worth planning around is the **Dutch Grand Prix**, held in late August or early September on a rotating schedule. Race weekend turns the town orange and books out every bed within thirty kilometres; come a week earlier or later if you want the dunes to yourself. In **winter** Zandvoort is a different town: most pavilions are dismantled, the boulevard is largely empty, and the beach is the domain of dog walkers and storm watchers — which has its own pleasures if you bring the right coat.
