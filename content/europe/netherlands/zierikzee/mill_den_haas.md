Mill Den Haas is a large windmill from 1727 on the edge of Zierikzee's old centre. After restoration, it returned to service as a corn mill in 1987.

It is a worthwhile short detour while walking Zierikzee's harbour gates, towers, and old streets, especially for visitors interested in Dutch milling heritage.
