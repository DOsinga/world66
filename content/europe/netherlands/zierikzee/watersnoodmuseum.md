The Watersnoodmuseum tells the story of the catastrophic North Sea flood of 1953 inside the huge caissons used to close the last breach in the dikes. Exhibits cover the rescue, relief work, reconstruction, machinery, newspaper coverage, and thousands of photographs from the disaster.
