Zwolle is the capital of Overijssel and one of the Netherlands' more rewarding second-tier cities. It has a compact old centre ringed by canals, enough medieval fabric to reward wandering, and a calmer tempo than the big western cities.

The skyline is led by the [Peperbus](/europe/netherlands/zwolle/peperbus), the big tower of the Onze Lieve Vrouwe basilica. Nearby, the [Grote Kerk](/europe/netherlands/zwolle/grote_kerk) anchors the market square, the [Sassenpoort](/europe/netherlands/zwolle/sassenpoort) guards the southern edge of the old town, and the [Museum de Fundatie](/europe/netherlands/zwolle/museum_de_fundatie) adds a very un-medieval oval roof to a classical building.

Zwolle also eats well. [De Librije](/europe/netherlands/zwolle/de_librije) is one of the Netherlands' destination restaurants, but you do not need a Michelin budget to enjoy the city. Cafes, terraces, and shops fill the lanes around the Grote Markt and Diezerstraat, making Zwolle a good day trip from [Heerde](/europe/netherlands/heerde), [Apeldoorn](/europe/netherlands/apeldoorn), or the IJssel towns.
