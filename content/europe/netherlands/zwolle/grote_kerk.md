The Grote Kerk, also known as the Academiehuis, fills one side of Zwolle's central market square. Its size tells you plenty about the old city's wealth, even before you look at the surrounding merchant houses and lanes.

The building now hosts cultural events, exhibitions, and gatherings, so its mood depends on the day. Step inside if it is open; otherwise, use it as the anchor for a slow circuit of the Grote Markt.
