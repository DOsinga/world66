Librije's Zusje is the more accessible sister in the Librije orbit, though "accessible" is relative in this company. It is still a polished, special-occasion restaurant rather than a quick lunch stop.

For travellers planning a food-led night in Zwolle, it is worth checking alongside De Librije. The draw is not only the cooking but the sense that this quiet city has built a serious culinary identity of its own.
