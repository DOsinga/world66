Museum de Fundatie is Zwolle's main art museum and the city's most conspicuous modern intervention. The older building is dignified enough, but the oval rooftop extension gives it the shock value that makes people stop in the street.

Inside, exhibitions range across visual art rather than sticking to one narrow period. It is the best rainy-day choice in Zwolle and pairs neatly with a walk to the [Sassenpoort](/europe/netherlands/zwolle/sassenpoort) or the Grote Markt.
