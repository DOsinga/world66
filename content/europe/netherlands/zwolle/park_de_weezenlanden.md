Park de Weezenlanden is Zwolle's easy green escape, a broad park southeast of the old centre. It is more local than spectacular, with lawns, paths, playground space, and room to sit down after a few hours of brick streets.

Use it as a pause rather than a headline sight. On a sunny day, picking up lunch in the centre and eating it here is a perfectly good Zwolle plan.
