The Vrouwenhuis is a small historic house museum with a specific story: it was established as a home for older women and still preserves rooms, portraits, and traces of domestic life. It is not a blockbuster sight, but it gives Zwolle's history a human scale.

Visits are best for travellers who like interiors and social history. After the larger statements of the Peperbus and Sassenpoort, the Vrouwenhuis feels quieter and more intimate.
