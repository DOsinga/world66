Alta's most striking piece of modern architecture spirals up from the town centre in titanium and glass — a deliberate reference to the aurora borealis that draws visitors to this part of Finnmark. Designed by the architectural firm Reiulf Ramstad Arkitekter and completed in 2013, the Northern Lights Cathedral (*Nordlyskatedralen*) serves as Alta's main church while also functioning as a cultural landmark.

The interior is worth a visit: the main nave rises into a soaring pointed vault, and the stained glass plays with the Arctic light in interesting ways. The viewing platform near the top offers a panorama over the town and the Altafjord. Services are held regularly; check ahead if you want to attend one, or simply visit during opening hours for the architecture.
