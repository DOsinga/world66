Kleivodden is a roadside lookout on Andoya with a wide view over the Arctic Ocean. Bleik lies to one side and the sea cliffs of Bleiksoya rise offshore, making it an easy scenic stop if you are driving the National Tourist Route along the island.

The appeal is simple: open sea, big sky, and a short pause without a hike. It pairs well with the nearby birdwatching and whale-watching stops already used as Andoya day trips from the Vesteralen area.
