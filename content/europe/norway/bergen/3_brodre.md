A relaxed fish restaurant and bar at Bryggen that manages to be unpretentious despite its prime position. The seafood soup is a Bergen classic — thick and cream-based, usually with fish, shrimp, and vegetables. Good for a quick lunch or a longer evening meal. The bar section gets busy on weekend nights.
