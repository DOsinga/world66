Bergen Aquarium sits out on Nordnes and is one of the city’s classic family attractions. Fish tanks, penguins, sea lions, reptiles, and marine displays make it an easy choice in a city where rain is not a rare event but part of the climate contract.

It is strongest for families and for travellers who want a low-key activity after Bryggen, Fløibanen, and the art museums. The walk out to Nordnes is pleasant, and the aquarium combines well with the park and waterfront views at the tip of the peninsula.
