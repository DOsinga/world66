Damsgård Hovedgård is one of Bergen's finest historic houses, a Rococo manor that shows the wealth and taste of the city's 18th-century merchant elite. Its symmetry, painted timber, and garden setting make it feel quite different from Bryggen's tight wharf buildings.

It sits outside the tight tourist core, which helps keep the visit calm. Check seasonal opening times and guided-tour arrangements, then combine it with a wider look at Bergen's less obvious neighbourhoods across the harbour.
