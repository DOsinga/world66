A classic student café-bar in the university district, open from morning coffee through to late-night drinks. Dragefjellet (Dragon's Mountain) sits at the foot of the hill below the university and pulls in a mix of students, academics, and locals who prefer their bar with some intellectual energy. Good for a mid-afternoon coffee break or an evening beer. The terrace is popular in the brief Bergen summer.
