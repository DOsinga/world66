Statsraad Lehmkuhl is a three-masted barque moored in Bergen, built in 1914 and still kept as a working sail training vessel. Even from the quay it adds a strong maritime note to the harbour, and a few times a year it runs short cruises for visitors.

It is most worthwhile if you have an interest in historic ships or catch one of the sailing dates. Otherwise, treat it as a handsome harbour sight while walking between Bryggen, Bergenshus, and Nordnes.
