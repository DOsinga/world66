The Theta Museum preserves the tiny wartime room used by the Theta group, a Bergen resistance network that radioed German fleet movements to London during World War II. The room was reconstructed in the 1980s and displays radio equipment and the group's security system. It is a small museum, but a focused stop for visitors interested in occupation and resistance history.
