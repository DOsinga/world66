Ulriksbanen carries visitors from the city edge up Mount Ulriken, Bergen's highest of the surrounding seven mountains. The ride is short, but the shift in perspective is dramatic: harbour, islands, fjords, and the compact city all open below the summit.

At the top, you can keep the visit simple with the viewpoint and cafe, or continue onto signed mountain trails when weather allows. It is one of Bergen's most efficient ways to turn a city day into a mountain day without committing to a long approach.
