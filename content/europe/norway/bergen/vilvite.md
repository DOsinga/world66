VilVite Bergen Science Centre is a hands-on museum built for touching, testing, and trying things rather than walking past display cases. Exhibits cover energy, weather, the body, technology, and marine science, which makes sense in a city shaped by rain, shipping, and offshore industry.

It is particularly useful for families, or for anyone who has hit Bergen on a third straight wet day and needs an indoor activity with some life in it. The centre is south of the historic core but easy to reach, and it combines well with a wander around the university and waterfront districts.
