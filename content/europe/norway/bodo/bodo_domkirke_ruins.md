Bodin Church, in the Bodin neighbourhood about 5 km east of central Bodø, is a medieval stone church with thirteenth-century origins — far older than the rebuilt city centre around it. The building was expanded in later centuries, but the original stone core survives, giving it a solidity and historicity absent from the post-1940 town. The whitewashed interior is typical of Norwegian Lutheran churches, with carved wooden fittings and a good altarpiece.

Getting here on foot or by bike takes around 30 minutes from the city centre via the coastal path. The church is worth combining with a ride east along the Bodøhalvøya peninsula — the cycling infrastructure is good and the views back toward the city, with the Lofoten Wall behind it, are excellent.
