Svarthammarhola is a limestone cave in the rugged country outside Bodo, better suited to
guided exploration than casual wandering. Its draw is the contrast with the open
Nordland coast: one moment you are in wind and wide light, the next you are underground
in tight rock passages and cold still air.

Go only with a qualified guide and proper gear. The cave is worthwhile for travelers who
want a physical, weatherproof adventure, but it is not a show cave with paved paths and
bright lights. Expect mud, helmets, and a trip that depends on local conditions.
