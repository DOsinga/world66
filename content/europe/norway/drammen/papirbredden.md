Papirbredden — "paper banks" — is the name given to the revitalised stretch of waterfront on the Strømsø side of the Drammenselva, built on the site of a former paper mill. The development, completed in the mid-2000s, includes the Drammen public library (a strikingly designed building open to all, with good cafe seating), a university campus, and several residential and commercial buildings along the river.

The promenade along the water is the most pleasant walking route in central Drammen. In summer, terraces and pop-up food stalls appear along the edge, and the views across to Bragernes are good at any hour. The [Ypsilon Bridge](/europe/norway/drammen/ypsilon_bridge) connects Papirbredden to the Bragernes side, making it easy to walk a loop across both banks. This area shows what Drammen has done right — it was derelict industrial land twenty years ago and is now genuinely enjoyable urban space.
