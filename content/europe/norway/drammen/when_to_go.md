---
title: When to Go
type: section
tags: [when_to_go]
---

Drammen is best visited in summer (June–August), when the riverside terraces are open, the Spiralen viewpoint is clear, and the long northern evenings make the waterfront genuinely pleasant. The city comes alive in a way it simply does not in winter.

That said, winter has its appeal. The Christmas market on Bragernes Torg runs through December, and the Spiralen's hilltop can be snow-covered while the city below is grey and wet — a surreal quality that suits the tunnel's own strangeness. Spring is quiet and fresh; autumn is good for mushroom-foraging in the surrounding forests if you know what you are looking for.

Rainfall is fairly evenly distributed through the year. Drammen sits in a valley that can trap fog in autumn and winter, making it grey even when Oslo is clear. Pack layers in any season.
