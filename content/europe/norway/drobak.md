Drøbak is the small Oslofjord town that makes the short trip south from Oslo feel worthwhile. It has white wooden houses, a busy little harbour, swimming spots, and a slightly eccentric Christmas identity that is more charming than it sounds.

The real anchor is [Oscarsborg Fortress](/europe/norway/drobak/oscarsborg_fortress), on the island just offshore. Its guns sank the German cruiser *Blücher* on 9 April 1940, delaying the occupation of Oslo long enough for Norway's king and government to escape. Back in town, the [old centre](/europe/norway/drobak/drobak_old_centre) is good for a slow wander, and the [Drøbak Aquarium](/europe/norway/drobak/drobak_aquarium) gives a close look at Oslofjord sea life.

This is not a big-city checklist. Drøbak is a ferry ride, a fortress, a harbour lunch, and a few hours of fjord-town air. On a warm day it is one of the easiest small escapes from Oslo.
