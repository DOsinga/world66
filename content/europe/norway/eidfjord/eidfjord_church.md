Eidfjord Church is the village's newer church, close to the centre and fjord. It is not a major architectural monument, but it helps mark the scale of the community behind the tourist traffic.

If you are walking around the village, it is worth a brief look together with the older church nearby. The two buildings say more together than either does alone.
