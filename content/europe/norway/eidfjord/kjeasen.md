Kjeåsen is a mountain farm perched high above the fjord, with the kind of view that makes Norwegian farm history feel almost unbelievable. The setting is spectacular, but also a lesson in how hard people worked to live on narrow shelves of usable land.

The road access is steep and controlled in places, so check local driving rules before setting off. If you make the trip, go for the view and for the sense of old fjord life clinging to impossible terrain.
