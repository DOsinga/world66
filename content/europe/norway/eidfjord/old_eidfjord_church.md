Old Eidfjord Church is a small stone church close to the village, modest in scale but important as a reminder that people lived and worshipped here long before cruise calls and waterfall coaches. Its plainness suits the fjord setting.

It is an easy walk or short stop from the centre. After the drama of Vøringsfossen, the church gives Eidfjord a quieter historical note.
