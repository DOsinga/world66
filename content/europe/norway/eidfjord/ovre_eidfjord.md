Øvre Eidfjord is the small upper-valley settlement between the fjord village and the climb to Vøringsfossen. Most visitors know it because of the Hardangervidda Natursenter, but it also helps break up the journey from sea level to plateau.

Use it as a practical stop for the nature centre, local services, and a look at the valley before the road begins its more dramatic climb.
