---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

June to September is the easiest season, with open mountain roads, long daylight, and the best chance of combining fjord walks with Hardangervidda excursions. May and October can be lovely but less predictable.

Winter changes the place completely. The village remains accessible, but high roads, viewpoints, and plateau routes may be snowbound or limited, so do not plan winter travel as if it were a summer fjord itinerary.
