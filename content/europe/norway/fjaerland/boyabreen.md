Bøyabreen is the easy glacier hit near Fjærland: park by the road and walk a few minutes toward the lake for a direct view of ice dropping from the high Jostedalsbreen plateau. Keep your distance from the ice and slopes; this is a look-at-it place, not a casual scramble.
