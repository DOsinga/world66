The Aurlandsdalen valley hike is one of the classic active day trips from the Flåm and Aurland area. The route follows a dramatic mountain valley shaped by old farm tracks, waterfalls, cliffs, and river scenery, giving a more physical counterpoint to the famous railway and fjord cruises.

It requires planning, proper footwear, and attention to season and transport, but the payoff is high for hikers. Choose a section that matches your fitness and weather window rather than treating the whole valley as a casual stroll.
