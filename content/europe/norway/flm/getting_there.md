---
title: Getting There
type: section
---

The most scenic approach is via the Bergen–Oslo railway, which stops at Myrdal. From Myrdal, the [Flåmsbana](/europe/norway/flm/flamsbana) drops 20 km down to Flåm in about an hour. The Bergen–Oslo route is one of the great train journeys of Europe in its own right; Myrdal sits at altitude and can be snow-covered even in July.

From Bergen, a high-speed express boat also connects directly to Flåm in about 5 hours, passing through the fjord system. Flåm is connected by bus to Aurland and to Gudvangen via the Stegastein road, and from Aurland there is a seasonal bus over the Aurlandsfjellet Snow Road to Lærdal. The village is otherwise road-accessible year-round via the Flåm valley from the main Sognefjord road.
