Undredal Stave Church is one of Norway’s smallest stave churches, tucked into a narrow fjord village a short detour from Flam. Its plain exterior gives little away, but the building preserves the intimate scale and timber construction of Norway’s medieval rural churches.

The church works best as part of a slow fjord day rather than a standalone mission. Pair it with the road or boat trip to Undredal, a look at the village’s goat-cheese tradition, and a pause by the water where the cliffs make the settlement feel almost enclosed.
