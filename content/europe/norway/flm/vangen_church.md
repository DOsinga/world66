Vangen Church stands in Aurland, a short trip from Flam, and is one of the older stone churches in the Sognefjord area. Its simple medieval form is a useful contrast to the dramatic fjord scenery around it: low, solid, and built for a small community rather than spectacle.

The church works well as a cultural stop if you are already exploring Aurland, Stegastein, or the fjord villages beyond Flam. Check access before planning around the interior, but the setting and exterior alone give a quiet pause between viewpoints and boat trips.
