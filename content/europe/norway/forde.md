Førde is a small town in Sunnfjord, Vestland county, serving as a regional hub for the surrounding fjord and mountain landscape of western [Norway](/europe/norway). It is not a destination in itself, but it is a practical base for exploring some of the most dramatic scenery in the country.

The big draw nearby is [Jostedalsbreen](/europe/norway/forde/jostedalsbreen), mainland Europe's largest glacier, accessible via guided walks on the glacier arms at Bøyabreen and Nigardsbreen. The drive from Førde to the glacier takes about two hours through steep-sided valleys and past waterfalls. Closer to town, [Jølstravatnet](/europe/norway/forde/jolstravatnet) lake stretches east towards the mountains and is good for fishing and kayaking in summer — and at its far end, the farmhouse of Norwegian painter Nikolai Astrup is worth a visit.

Førde also hosts the [Førde International Folk Music Festival](/europe/norway/forde/forde_folk_music_festival) each July, Scandinavia's largest folk and world music event, which brings an unexpected burst of energy to this otherwise quiet town. The [Sunnfjord Museum](/europe/norway/forde/sunnfjord_museum) on the outskirts gives a sense of traditional life in western Norway, with old farm buildings assembled beside a quiet lake.
