---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

Førde is accessible year-round, but the reasons to visit are concentrated in summer. July is the best month: the Førde International Folk Music Festival brings unusual energy to the town, Jostedalsbreen glacier walks are in full operation, and the long daylight hours make the drive to Jølstravatnet and the surrounding valleys genuinely enjoyable.

June and August are good alternatives — slightly quieter, with reliable weather and full access to the mountains and glacier. Winter closes most glacier activities and the mountain roads can be treacherous, though the landscape is dramatic in snow. The Sunnfjord Museum is open year-round.
