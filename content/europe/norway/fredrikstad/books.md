**The History of Fredrikstad** by Harald Hals is the standard local history, thorough on the fortification period and the Glomma river trade. In Norwegian only, but worth tracking down for those who read the language.

**Norwegian Fortresses** by Nils Petter Thuesen surveys the major defensive works along the Swedish border, with a substantial chapter on Fredrikstad and Kongsten. Good context for understanding what you're looking at.

**Hunger** by Knut Hamsun — not about Fredrikstad specifically, but set along the Oslofjord coastal communities in the late 19th century and gives a powerful picture of the region's economic conditions at the time the town was transitioning from military to industrial importance.
