Kongsten Fort sits on a hilltop a short walk from Gamlebyen's eastern gate, an irregular star-shaped earthwork with good views over the surrounding countryside and Glomma river valley. It is the outer defensive position of the Fredrikstad fortification system — while Gamlebyen provided the primary ring of walls and moats, Kongsten was designed to hold attackers at a distance before they could reach the town.

The fort is free to enter and open year-round. The earthworks are overgrown with grass and a scattering of old trees, giving it a pastoral character that makes it a pleasant place to walk even if military history isn't your primary interest. The combination of Gamlebyen and Kongsten together makes Fredrikstad's fortification system one of the most complete surviving examples of 17th-century defensive architecture in Scandinavia.
