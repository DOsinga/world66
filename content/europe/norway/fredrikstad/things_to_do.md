title: Things to Do
type: section
---

Fredrikstad's main draw is the Old Town (Gamlebyen) — a 17th-century fortress that ranks among the best-preserved in Scandinavia and still functions as a living neighbourhood. Walking its cobbled streets, crossing the moat on the small passenger ferry, and climbing the earthen ramparts gives you a surprisingly unmediated encounter with Baroque military engineering.

Beyond the fortress, the wider municipality stretches across the Oslofjord shoreline, offering beaches on the island of Kråkerøy and quiet villages on the peninsula of Onsøy. The Glomma river, Norway's longest, splits the city, and following it north or south rewards with industrial heritage and birdlife. The town's arts scene punches above its weight — galleries, crafts studios, and a ceramics tradition that draws visitors in summer.
