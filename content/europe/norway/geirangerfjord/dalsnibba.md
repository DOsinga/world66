Dalsnibba is the high mountain viewpoint above Geiranger, reached by a detour from Road 63. At about 1,500 metres, it gives a wide view over the fjord, the village far below, and the surrounding peaks.

The road is seasonal and exposed, so conditions can change quickly even when Geiranger feels mild. On clear days it is one of the strongest viewpoints in the fjord region.
