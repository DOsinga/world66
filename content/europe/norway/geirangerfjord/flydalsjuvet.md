Flydalsjuvet is one of the classic viewpoints above Geiranger, looking down toward the village, the fjord, and the cruise ships far below. It is closer and more immediate than Dalsnibba, with the cliffs and water filling the view rather than sitting in the distance.

The viewpoint has upper and lower plateaus connected by a gangway, so it is easy to spend a few minutes finding the best angle. Morning or late afternoon light is best for photographs, and the stop fits naturally into a drive on Road 63.
