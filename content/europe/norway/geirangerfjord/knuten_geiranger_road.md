Knuten is a tight 270-degree curve on the old Geiranger road, designed in 1880 to handle the steep climb between Geiranger and the high mountain road toward Grotli. The road was built for horses and carriages, and its hairpins still show how ambitious the route was for its time.

Traffic now uses a newer road nearby, but the old curve remains an interesting stop for travellers following the scenic roads above Geirangerfjord.
