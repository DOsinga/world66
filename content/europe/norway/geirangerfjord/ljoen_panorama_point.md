Ljoen Panorama Point is a high roadside lookout near the hamlet of Ljoen, between the tunnels above the fjord country. The stop gives a broad view toward Geirangerfjord and Sunnylvsfjord.

It is not a long visit, but it is exactly the kind of pause that makes the drive through this part of western Norway memorable.
