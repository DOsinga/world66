Norsk Fjordsenter is the visitor centre for the West Norwegian Fjords UNESCO World Heritage Site. Exhibits explain the landscape, settlement history, avalanches, and the steep farming culture around Geirangerfjord.

It also sits by the upper end of the Waterfall Walk, making it a practical first or last stop when exploring Geiranger on foot.
