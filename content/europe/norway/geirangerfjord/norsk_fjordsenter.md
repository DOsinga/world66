Norsk Fjordsenter is the visitor centre for the West Norwegian Fjords UNESCO World Heritage area, sitting above Geiranger village near the top of the Waterfall Walk. Exhibits explain how the fjord landscape was formed and how people managed to farm, travel, and live on these steep slopes.

It is a useful first stop if you want context before heading out to the viewpoints. Even if you skip the exhibits, the walk between the centre and the village is one of Geiranger's easiest short outings.
