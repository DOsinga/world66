Gjøvik Gård is the old manor property in the middle of town, with museum buildings, gardens, and a calm patch of green close to the lakefront. It gives Gjøvik a small historic core that the newer commercial streets sometimes hide.

Come here for a short, easy stop rather than a major museum visit. It pairs well with the Skibladner pier and the nearby centre if you are moving around town on foot.
