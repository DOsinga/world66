---
title: When to Go
type: section
---

Grimstad's season runs from June to August, when the harbourfront comes alive, the boat services to the islands operate, and the Ibsen Museum is fully staffed with English-language tours. The summer short story festival (*Grimstad Litteraturfestival*) brings writers and readers to town in late June and is a good reason to visit around that time.

Outside summer, Grimstad is quiet. The Ibsen Museum has limited opening hours from September to May, and many of the harbour-based activities don't operate. The town is still pleasant for a day visit in spring or early autumn — the white wooden houses look striking in autumn light — but plan around the reduced hours.
