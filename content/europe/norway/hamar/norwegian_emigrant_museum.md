The Norwegian Emigrant Museum tells the story of Norwegians who left for North America, using exhibitions and a small open-air collection of historic buildings. One of the highlights is a church built in Minnesota in 1896 and later moved back to Norway.

It gives useful context for Hamar and the inland communities that sent many people abroad. The museum is best combined with Hamar's other lakeside and heritage sights.
