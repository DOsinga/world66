The Norwegian Railway Museum is a good fit for Hamar because the town has always been tied to the rail line between Oslo, Lillehammer, and Trondheim. The museum mixes locomotives, rolling stock, railway architecture, and family-friendly exhibits rather than treating railway history as glass-case nostalgia.

It sits west of the centre, close enough to combine with Domkirkeodden on a lakefront walk.
