Skibladner is the world's oldest preserved paddle steamer still in scheduled service, sailing on Lake Mjosa in summer. From Hamar, it turns the lake from scenery into transport history, with slow views of farms, towns, and low wooded shores.

Check the timetable carefully, because this is seasonal and weather-sensitive. When it runs, it is one of the most distinctive ways to understand why the lake matters.
