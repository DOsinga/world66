---
title: "Eating Out"
type: section
---

Hammerfest punches above its weight for food, thanks to the Arctic larder on its doorstep. King crab, reindeer, grouse, and Arctic char all appear on local menus. [Restaurant Havn](/europe/norway/hammerfest/restaurant_havn) on the waterfront is the best option for a proper dinner. Look for restaurants near the harbour that source directly from local fishermen and hunters — the best places change their menus by what came off the boats and the tundra that week.
