title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Hammerfest's main attraction is simply being here — at the edge of the inhabited world. The [Royal and Ancient Polar Bear Society](/europe/norway/hammerfest/royal_polar_bear_society), housed in a small building on the waterfront, issues certificates to visitors who have made it this far north. The [Museum of Reconstruction](/europe/norway/hammerfest/museum_of_reconstruction) for Finnmark and Nord-Troms documents the devastating German scorched-earth retreat of 1944 and the remarkable rebuilding effort that followed — it is one of Norway's most affecting small museums.

In summer, boat trips on the fjord offer [king crab safaris](/europe/norway/hammerfest/king_crab_safari) where you haul the enormous crabs from the water and eat them on the spot. The [Meridian Column](/europe/norway/hammerfest/meridian_column), a UNESCO World Heritage Site, marks the endpoint of the first international scientific survey to determine the shape of the earth. The [Hammerfest Church](/europe/norway/hammerfest/hammerfest_church), built during the postwar reconstruction, is a striking piece of Arctic modernism. For walkers, the hills above town give sweeping views over the Arctic Ocean.
