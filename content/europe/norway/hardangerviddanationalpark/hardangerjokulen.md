Hardangerjøkulen is one of Norway's large glaciers, rising above the Hardangervidda plateau between Road 7 and the Bergen Railway. Its height makes it visible from far away, especially around Finse and the high plateau routes.

The glacier is a serious mountain environment, not a casual walk. See it from marked viewpoints and trails unless you are with qualified guides and have proper glacier equipment.
