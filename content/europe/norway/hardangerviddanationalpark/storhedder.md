Storhedder is a remote archaeological site high in the hills between Bykle and western Norway. At Skutesteinen, figures and runes were carved into soapstone roughly a thousand years ago, leaving a rare trace of people moving through this hard upland landscape.

Getting here is part of the point and should not be treated as a casual roadside stop. For hikers with time, good weather, and the right map, it is a memorable link between Hardangervidda's wild terrain and its older human history.
