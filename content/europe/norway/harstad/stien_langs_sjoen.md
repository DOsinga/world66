Stien langs sjøen is the coastal trail between the Trondenes historic area and Harstad. It is a simple but rewarding walk, with sea views and an easy link between Trondenes Church, the historical centre, and the town.

Use it as the slow route back from Trondenes rather than treating it as a separate expedition.
