Karmsund Civic Museum is the central local-history stop in Haugesund. It covers the town and district rather than one single headline theme, with material on coastal life, urban growth, and the communities around Karmsundet.

Use it as a quieter counterpoint to Avaldsnes and Haraldshaugen. Those places carry the big national story; this museum is better for understanding what everyday Haugesund was built from.
