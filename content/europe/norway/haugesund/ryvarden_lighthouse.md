Ryvarden Lighthouse stands on the coast north of Haugesund, reached by an easy gravel-road walk of about 2 km from Molstrevag. The old lighthouse keeper's setting, sea views, gallery space, and Sleipner shipwreck memorial make it more than a quick photo stop.
