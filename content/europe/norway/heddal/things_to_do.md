---
title: "Things to do in Heddal"
type: section
---

Heddal is about the [Stave Church](/europe/norway/heddal/stave_church) and not much else — and that is enough to justify the detour for any visitor interested in medieval architecture. Combine the church with the adjacent [Open Air Museum](/europe/norway/heddal/open_air_museum) for a half-day visit; the two share a ticket office and stand a short walk apart.
