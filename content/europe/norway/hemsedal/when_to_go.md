---
title: When to Go
type: section
---

The ski season typically runs from December through April, with the best snow conditions in January and February. Hemsedal's high altitude (the ski area tops out above 1,400 metres) and northerly aspect mean snow reliability is better than most Norwegian resorts, and significantly better than most European resorts at comparable altitudes.

The summer season from late June through August brings long days — near-continuous light at the height of summer — and good hiking conditions on the mountain trails. The Hemsila river fills with trout and the alpine meadows are at their most colourful in July. Summer visitor numbers are far lower than winter, which means accommodation is easy to find and the mountains feel genuinely empty.

The shoulder seasons (May and October-November) are quiet to the point of many facilities being closed. Not the time to come unless you specifically want solitude and are self-sufficient.
