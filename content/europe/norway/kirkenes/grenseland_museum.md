The Border Country Museum tells the story of Sør-Varanger: the iron mines that built Kirkenes, the brutal Second World War (the town was flattened by Luftwaffe bombings and Red Army liberation), and the Sami culture of the surrounding tundra.

The WWII section is the strongest — detailed accounts of the bombing campaign, the occupation, and the Red Army liberation of October 1944, supported by good photographic archives. The mining history section covers the Sydvaranger operation from its founding in 1906 to the modern era, with ore samples, equipment, and photographs. The permanent John Savio collection is in a room of its own: woodcuts and prints that are the finest thing in the museum by some distance. The Andersgrotta bunker tour, run by the same institution, is sold as a combined ticket.
