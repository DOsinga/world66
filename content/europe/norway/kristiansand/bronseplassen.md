Bronseplassen is an open-air archaeology and history site at Høvåg, with reconstructed Bronze Age and Viking-era houses. The site also includes activity areas such as a labyrinth, burial setting, and traditional craft elements.

It is most useful for families or travellers interested in early Scandinavian history who are driving between Kristiansand and Lillesand. Check opening times before setting out, as small outdoor heritage sites often run seasonally.
