The main live-music venue in town, on Dronningens gate just off Markens. The programme covers Norwegian rock, indie and singer-songwriter acts, with occasional international touring bands. Bar at the back, small stage at the front, capacity around three hundred; busy on Friday and Saturday nights and not bothered with cocktails or fuss.
