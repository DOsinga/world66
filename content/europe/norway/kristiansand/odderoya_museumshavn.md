Odderoya Museumshavn is a small maritime museum harbour that connects Kristiansand's seafaring history with the old military and quarantine buildings on Odderoya. Historic ochre and red buildings on the hill behind add to the setting.

It works well as part of a walk around Odderoya, especially if you are interested in ships, harbour life, and the city's coastal history.
