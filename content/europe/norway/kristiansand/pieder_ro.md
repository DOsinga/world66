A long-standing pub and bar at the Fiskebrygga, with terrace tables that catch the late sun across the harbour. The crowd is mixed — locals on weekday evenings, summer visitors at weekends — and the beer selection is reasonable. A reliable starting point for an evening in the city; the kitchen does pub food at lunchtime.
