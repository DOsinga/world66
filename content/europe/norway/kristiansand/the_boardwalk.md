Kristiansand's boardwalk follows the central seafront through parks and past several of the city's easy waterfront sights. The route passes Otterdalsparken, the Kilden Performing Arts Centre area, and Christiansholm Fortress, making it a simple way to get oriented on foot.

It is best as an unhurried harbour walk, especially in good weather when the waterfront cafes and public spaces are active.
