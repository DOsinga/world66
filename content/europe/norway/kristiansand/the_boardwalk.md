Kristiansand's boardwalk strings together the harbour, parks, Christiansholm Fortress, Bystranda city beach and the start of the Otra riverside path. It is one of the easiest ways to get a feel for the city, with boats on one side and compact southern Norwegian street life close by.

Use it as a relaxed walk rather than a single sight. The route works well before dinner at Fiskebrygga, or as a longer stroll if you follow the river path away from the centre.
