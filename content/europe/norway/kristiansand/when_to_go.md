---
title: When to Go
type: section
---

Summer is the obvious season — late June to mid-August, when the days are long, the sea is just about swimmable, and the islands fill with Norwegian summer visitors. July is the busiest and the warmest, with average highs around twenty-one and frequent days near twenty-five. September is quieter but still pleasant.

Winter is short, dark and damp rather than cold. Kristiansand sees little of the snow that buries the rest of Norway; the town is mostly closed-feeling from November to March, with most coastal restaurants shut.
