---
title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Kristiansund's main sights cluster on the four islands of the old town centre, with the Klippfisk Museum and Nordmøre Museum anchoring the cultural offer. The city is the gateway to the Atlantic Ocean Road, one of Norway's most photographed drives, and the surrounding coastline rewards anyone who hires a car for a day. Within town, the harbour, the post-war churches, and the old trading quarter on Innlandet are all walkable.
