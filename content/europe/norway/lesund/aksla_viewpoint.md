The hilltop park above the town centre is the single best place to understand Ålesund's geography. Four hundred and eighteen steps lead up from the main street, with benches at intervals for those who need to catch their breath, and at the top a panoramic view takes in the whole town spread across its islands.

The view explains why the Art Nouveau rebuilding is so striking: from above you can see how the buildings cluster on promontories and waterfront positions, and how consistently the rooflines, towers and facades hold together as a unified design. Sunrise and sunset are both rewarding times to make the climb. The park at the top has a small restaurant.
