Apotekergata No. 5 is a bar and music venue in a handsome Art Nouveau building on one of the town's main streets. The interior combines original period details — the building was once a pharmacy — with a modern bar setup and a programme of live music on weekends. It is one of the better-known evening spots in the city and tends to stay lively into the small hours.

The beer selection is good by Norwegian standards, including some local and regional craft ales. The atmosphere is friendly and mixed, drawing both locals and visitors without feeling like a tourist trap.
