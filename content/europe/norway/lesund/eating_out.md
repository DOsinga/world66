---
title: "Eating Out"
type: section
---

Ålesund is a fishing port, and the food reflects it. The clip fish (klippfisk — salt-dried cod) produced here is exported across Europe, and bacalhau in various forms appears on menus around town. Fresh shellfish — crab, langoustines, and prawns — are available year-round, and the Norwegian Food Festival held here every August draws producers from across the country.
