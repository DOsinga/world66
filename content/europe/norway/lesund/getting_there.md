---
title: "Getting There"
type: section
---

**By air:** Ålesund Airport Vigra is 15 km from the city centre, with frequent flights to Oslo, Bergen, and Trondheim, plus direct international connections to Copenhagen. A bus (the Flybussen) connects the airport to the city in about 25 minutes.

**By sea:** The Hurtigruten coastal express calls at Ålesund twice daily — northbound in the afternoon, southbound in the morning — making it reachable as part of a Norwegian coastal voyage. The journey from Bergen takes about 22 hours; from Trondheim about 13 hours.

**By bus:** Long-distance buses connect Ålesund to Trondheim (approximately 5 hours) and other major towns. For Geiranger and the fjord country, Norled and local bus services run in summer.

**By car:** The E39 coastal highway connects Ålesund to Molde and Bergen via a combination of road and ferry crossings. From Oslo, the most direct route is via the E136 through Romsdalen, a spectacular mountain pass in its own right.
