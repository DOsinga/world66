The Sunnmøre Museum is an open-air folk museum on the southern edge of the city, spread across a headland with about 55 historic buildings brought from across the Sunnmøre region. The collection spans from Iron Age buildings to a recreated 19th-century trading post, with interiors furnished and maintained to show how people actually lived.

The maritime section includes replica Viking and medieval boats, and in summer you can watch craftspeople demonstrate traditional boatbuilding techniques. The museum is best visited in good weather when you can walk comfortably between the buildings and take in the water views. It is about 3 km from the centre — reachable by bus or bike.
