Ålesund punches well above its size as a travel destination. The Art Nouveau townscape is the main draw — not a single landmark but an entire city built in one consistent style over four years — and the setting of islands, channels and mountains gives every viewpoint a dramatic backdrop. Add a world-class fjord connection and a strong seafood culture, and you have a town that rewards a stay of two or three days.
