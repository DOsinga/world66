---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

Ålesund is busiest from June to August, when the weather is warmest (though rarely hot — expect 15–20°C) and the Geirangerfjord connection is fully operational. The Norwegian Food Festival in August is worth timing a visit around.

The Art Nouveau architecture looks good in any weather — fog and rain actually suit the melancholy beauty of the Jugendstil facades — and the shoulder months of May and September give you the same townscape with fewer crowds. Runde Island's bird colonies are at their best from late April through July; after August, most seabirds have left the nesting cliffs.

Winter is quiet and cold, but the town is photogenic under snow and the Hurtigruten connection makes it part of a Northern Lights itinerary. Daylight is very short in December and January.
