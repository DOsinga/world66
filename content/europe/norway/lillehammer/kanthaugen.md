Kanthaugen Freestyle Arena lies adjacent to the Lysgårdsbakken ski jump complex on the hillside above Lillehammer. Built for the 1994 Winter Olympics, it hosted the moguls and aerials freestyle skiing events, and has remained in use for training and competition since. The course is more technical than it looks from below — the mogul field on the main slope covers significant vertical drop.

The arena is accessible as part of the Lillehammer Olympiapark complex, which organises guided tours of all the 1994 Olympic venues. In winter, the surrounding area is used for cross-country skiing and snowshoeing, with groomed trails connecting Kanthaugen to the Birkebeineren ski stadium and the wider network around the town.
