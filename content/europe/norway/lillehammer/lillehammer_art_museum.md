The Lillehammer Art Museum occupies a striking building designed by Snøhetta and opened to coincide with the 1994 Winter Olympics. The permanent collection focuses on Norwegian art from the 19th and early 20th centuries, with particular strengths in landscape painting and the national romantic tradition that preceded Norwegian independence.

The collection includes works by many of the defining figures of Norwegian painting: Christian Krohg, Erik Werenskiold, Harriet Backer, and Nikolai Astrup are all represented. The museum also holds a significant collection of 20th-century Norwegian art and runs a changing programme of temporary exhibitions covering both Norwegian and international artists. It is located near the upper end of Storgata, making it an easy combination with a walk along the main street.
