The Norwegian Olympic Museum at Maihaugen covers the history of the Olympic Games from their revival in 1896 to the present, with particular emphasis on Norwegian participation and the 1994 Lillehammer Games. The permanent exhibition includes Olympic torches, medals, sporting equipment, and memorabilia arranged chronologically, with interactive displays that explain the evolution of individual sports.

The 1994 Lillehammer Winter Olympics section is the most comprehensive, drawing on the fact that those Games are still remembered as among the most successful in Olympic history — well-organised, intimate in scale, and marked by moments of genuine sporting drama. The museum is housed in a modern building within the Maihaugen grounds and is included in the combined Maihaugen admission ticket; it can also be visited separately.
