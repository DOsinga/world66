---
title: "Getting Around"
type: section
---

The centre of Lillesand is walkable, and that is how you should see it. The harbour, museum, church, shops, and cafes are all close together.

For Justøy, Brekkestø, Homborsund, and the outer coast, use a car, bicycle, or seasonal boat connections. Distances are short, but the coastline is indented, so routes can take longer than they appear on a simple map.
