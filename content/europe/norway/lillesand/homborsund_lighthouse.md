Homborsund Lighthouse lies east of Lillesand near the coast around Homborsund. It is a quieter lighthouse stop than the obvious harbour sights, with low rocks, open water, and a more exposed feel.

It makes most sense if you are driving or cycling the coast towards Grimstad. Do not rush out just for the building; go for the stretch of shoreline around it.
