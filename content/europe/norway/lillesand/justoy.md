Justøy is an island southwest of Lillesand, reached by road and bridge, with the kind of small coves and summer houses that define the local coast. It is a good choice when you want to leave the town centre without committing to a long excursion.

Bring a bike if you have one. The roads are quiet enough to enjoy slowly, and the best stops are often small places to look at the water rather than named attractions.
