Justøy Chapel is a small local landmark on the island, worth noticing if you are cycling or driving around Justøy. Its scale is modest, but that suits the place.

The chapel works best as part of an island loop: coves, lanes, summer houses, and a few stops where the coast opens towards the skerries.
