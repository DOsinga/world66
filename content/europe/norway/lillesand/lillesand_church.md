Lillesand Church stands just above the centre, a plain white church that fits neatly into the town's coastal palette. It is not a major monument, but its position gives a good sense of the settlement rising from harbour to hillside.

Add it to a short walk through town rather than making a separate outing. The value is in the whole scene: church, wooden houses, gardens, and glimpses back to the water.
