Nørholm is a historic estate northeast of Lillesand, best known for its association with Knut Hamsun. The literary connection is complicated, as Hamsun's reputation is inseparable from both his writing and his wartime politics.

For most travellers it is a contextual stop rather than a must-see. If you are interested in Norwegian literary history, it adds a sharper edge to an otherwise gentle coastal itinerary.
