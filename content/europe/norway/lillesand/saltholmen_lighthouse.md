Saltholmen Lighthouse sits outside Lillesand on the low coastal rocks, part of the practical seamanship that made this coast navigable. It is a small sight, but lighthouses make sense here: the shoreline is beautiful precisely because it is broken into islands and skerries.

The lighthouse is best appreciated from the water or from coastal viewpoints. Treat it as part of the maritime landscape rather than a standalone monument.
