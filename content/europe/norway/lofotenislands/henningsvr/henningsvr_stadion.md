The football pitch at Henningsvær may be the most dramatically situated sporting ground in the world. Wedged onto a narrow islet between two arms of the fjord, it has open Arctic water on three sides and the jagged Lofoten peaks rising behind the goal. From above — and photographs of it circulate constantly online — it looks almost impossibly improbable.

The pitch is fully functional and used by the local club Henningsvær IL. There's no visitor centre, no entry fee, no ticketing — you just walk out and stand on it. The best view is from the rocks above, accessible via a short scramble. Come in the evening light of a Lofoten summer and you'll understand why it has become one of Norway's most photographed spots.
