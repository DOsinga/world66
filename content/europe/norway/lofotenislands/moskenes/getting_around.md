---
title: Getting Around
type: section
---

**By car** is the practical choice for most visitors. The E10 runs the length of the eastern shore from Hamnøy through Reine and Sørvågen to Å. The drive is short — the whole municipality is only about 20 kilometres end to end — but the views make it slow going.

**On foot** between villages is possible in good weather; the road is walkable, though there are few pavements. Reine to Å takes around three hours at a moderate pace.

**By bicycle** the route is manageable in summer and one of the best ways to stop at viewpoints without worrying about parking. Electric bike hire is available in Reine.

**By local ferry** to reach the western beaches. Bunes and Horseid beaches are only accessible by a small passenger ferry from Vindstad, which itself is reached by ferry from Reine. The schedule is seasonal; check [177nordland.no](https://177nordland.no) for current timetables.

**By water taxi** for flexible access to remote coves, the Moskenesstraumen, and the Refsvika cave. Several operators in Reine and Sørvågen offer guided and charter trips.
