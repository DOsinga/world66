type: section
---

Moskenes rewards slow exploration. The most famous view in Norway is here — the red and yellow rorbu cabins of Reine, reflected in still water with jagged peaks rising behind them — but behind that postcard image lies a genuinely wild landscape of difficult hikes, remote beaches, maelstroms, and ancient cave paintings. Most visitors base themselves in a rorbu and spend their days moving between the fishing villages, mountains, and water.
