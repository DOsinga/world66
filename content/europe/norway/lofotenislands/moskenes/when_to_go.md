---
title: When to Go
type: section
---

Moskenes has two distinct seasons, and both are worth experiencing.

**Summer (June–August)** is when the midnight sun means the sky never goes fully dark — photography is spectacular at any hour, and hiking trails are accessible without snow. July and August are the busiest months, and rorbu cabins book out well in advance. The sea is calm enough for boat trips to the Moskenesstraumen and Refsvika cave. Temperatures rarely exceed 18°C even in high summer, so bring warm layers.

**Winter (November–February)** offers the best chance of seeing the Northern Lights. The mountains and villages look completely different under snow. Rorbu rentals are cheaper, the villages are quieter, and on clear nights the aurora can be extraordinary. Storms roll in from the Atlantic without warning and ferries can be cancelled, so build flexibility into your schedule. Daylight is short — only a few hours around midday in December.

Spring and autumn are shoulder seasons with fewer crowds, lower prices, and changeable weather. Migratory seabirds pass through in spring.

The [Lofoten International Art Festival](https://liaf.no) takes place every two years in late summer, with site-specific works across the archipelago.
