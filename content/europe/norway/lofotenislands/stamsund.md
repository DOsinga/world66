Stamsund is a fishing village on the island of Vestvågøy in [Lofoten](/europe/norway/lofotenislands), with about 1,400 inhabitants. It remains an active fishing port — the harbour, the drying racks, and the [rorbu cabins](/europe/norway/lofotenislands/stamsund/rorbu_cabins) along the waterfront give the place its character.

The [Hurtigruten](/europe/norway/lofotenislands/stamsund/hurtigruten_stop) coastal express makes a regular stop at the Stamsund dock, both southbound from Svolvær and northbound from Bodø, making it one of the most scenic arrivals in Norway. Stamsund also has a surprisingly rich cultural life for its size, with two theatre groups and the Nordland Puppet Theatre. The [Stamsund Church](/europe/norway/lofotenislands/stamsund/stamsund_church) on the hillside above town offers good views over the harbour and the Lofoten Wall.
