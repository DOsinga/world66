---
title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Stamsund is a base for experiencing Lofoten life rather than ticking off sights. The harbour, the rorbu cabins along the waterfront, and the fishing operations give the village its character. The Hurtigruten stop is one of the most scenic in the route. Hiking, cycling, and sea fishing are the main activities — the surrounding mountains descend sharply to the water and the trails are good.
