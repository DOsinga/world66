Nordlandshagen Beach shows the softer side of Værøy without losing the island's edge. Sand, grass, low dunes, and mountains sit under the full force of Lofoten weather, so the place can feel peaceful one hour and raw the next.

This is more a walking and photography beach than a swimming beach for most visitors. Bring layers, watch the wind, and pair it with the island's bird cliffs and old fishing settlements for a fuller day on Værøy.
