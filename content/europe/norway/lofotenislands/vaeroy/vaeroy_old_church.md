Vaeroy Old Church is a small wooden church with a larger story than its size suggests. Moved from Kabelvag to Vaeroy in the late 18th century, it connects the island to the older religious and fishing history of Lofoten, when communities shifted buildings and resources around a harsh coast.

The church is a quiet stop between more dramatic island experiences: bird cliffs, abandoned settlements, steep walks, and weather. Its simplicity is the appeal. In a landscape that constantly pulls your eyes outward to sea and mountain, the church brings the scale back to human hands and local memory.
