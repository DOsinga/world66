---
title: "Eating Out"
type: section
---

Vågan's restaurants concentrate in Svolvær and Henningsvær, with one or two notable places in Kabelvåg. The defining local dish is **stockfish** (tørrfisk) — air-dried cod produced on the wooden racks (hjell) you'll see everywhere across the islands from late winter to early summer. **Skrei** (the migratory spring cod) is the seasonal speciality, served fresh and simply with potatoes, carrot, and roe sauce from February to April.

In Svolvær, **Børsen Spiseri**, on the old quay, serves an ambitious modern Norwegian menu in a 19th-century warehouse — good but pricey. **Bacalao Bar** on the waterfront is the more casual option. In Henningsvær, **Klatrekafeen** (the Climbers' Café) is the lively local hangout; the more polished **Lysstøperiet Bakeri & Spiseri** does good seasonal cooking.

Lofoten prices are higher than mainland Norway — expect to pay around 350 NOK for a main course and 150 NOK for a beer.
