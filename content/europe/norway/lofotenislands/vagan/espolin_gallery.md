A dedicated gallery for the work of Kaare Espolin Johnson (1907–1994), one of northern Norway's most beloved artists. Espolin's etchings, lithographs, and paintings draw on the folklore, fishermen, and stark winter light of the Arctic coast — figures in heavy oilskins emerging from luminous fog, the great sea-cliffs of the Vesterålen, the spawning cod of the Lofotfiske.

The gallery is part of the Storvågan museum complex in Kabelvåg, with a combined ticket covering the Lofoten Museum, Espolin Gallery, and Lofoten Aquarium. Allow an hour.
