Kabelvåg, 5 km west of Svolvær, is older, smaller, and quieter — the historic centre of Vågan and arguably the more attractive village. A handful of restored wooden buildings line the harbour; the white wooden Vågan Church (1898) towers behind the village; and the Lofoten Regional Museum, the Lofoten Aquarium, and the Espolin Gallery all sit together in the small Storvågan area at the western end of the village.

Worth a visit for at least a half-day. Easy to combine with Henningsvær on the same outing — both lie southwest of Svolvær on the same road.
