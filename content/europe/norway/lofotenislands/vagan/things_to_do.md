---
title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Vågan is the gateway to Lofoten and where most visitors base themselves for the first night or two. The serious sights are spread across three settlements: **Svolvær** (the practical capital, with the harbour boats and the most accommodation), **Kabelvåg** (older, quieter, with the medieval Lofoten Cathedral and two of the best museums), and **Henningsvær** (the picture-postcard fishing village hung on a causeway over the open sea).

The signature activities are the **Trollfjord boat trip** (a narrow steep-walled fjord 20 km north of Svolvær, accessible only by sea), the **Svolværgeita climb** for experienced mountaineers, and a winter chase of the **Northern Lights** (Vågan sits well above the Arctic Circle). For the rest, the islands are about walking, watching the light on the peaks, and slow days in fishing-village rorbu cabins.
