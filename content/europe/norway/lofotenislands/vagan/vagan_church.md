The huge yellow wooden cross-shaped church above Kabelvåg, locally known as Lofotkatedralen — the Lofoten Cathedral — was consecrated in 1898 to serve the seasonal flood of fishermen during the spring cod season. It is the largest wooden church in northern Norway and one of the most photographed buildings in Lofoten.

The interior is plain but bright. The cemetery around the church has the gravestones of generations of Lofoten fishermen, many lost at sea — a useful reminder of how dangerous the spring fishery was before motorised boats.
