---
title: Getting There
type: section
---

Leknes Airport (IATA: LKN) has daily flights to Bodø operated by Wideroe, with connections to Oslo and other Norwegian cities. The flight from Bodø takes around 25 minutes. Driving from Svolvær, the island capital of Lofoten, takes about an hour on the E10 highway. The Hurtigruten coastal express stops at Stamsund on its daily route between Bergen and Kirkenes — the southbound call is in the late afternoon, northbound in the morning.
