  Haukland's turquoise water and white sand look photoshopped even in person. Haukland is one of Lofoten's finest beaches: a sweep of white sand and turquoise shallows backed by steep mountains, about 15 km west of Leknes. The water is cold by any standard — this is the Arctic Circle — but that doesn't stop people swimming in summer. What strikes most visitors is the improbability of it: subtropical-looking sand in a landscape of bare rock and snow-capped peaks.

The beach connects via a short walk to Vik, another excellent strand immediately to the south. Together they form one of the best coastal walks on the island, easily done in an hour. A small café operates at Haukland in summer; the rest of the year you'll want to bring your own provisions. Parking fills quickly on summer weekends — arrive early or late.
