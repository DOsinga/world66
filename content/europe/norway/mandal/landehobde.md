Landehobde is a World War II coastal fort site west of Mandal, with concrete bunkers, gun positions, and open views over the skerries. It is a good short stop for visitors interested in wartime remains or coastal walks.

The site is mostly outdoors and informal, so wear shoes suitable for uneven ground and treat the old military structures with care.
