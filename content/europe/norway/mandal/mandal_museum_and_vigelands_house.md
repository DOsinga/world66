Mandal Museum occupies a prominent merchant's house in the town centre. Its collection focuses on local history and on artists born in Mandal, including Adolph Tidemand, Amaldus Nielsen, and Gustav Vigeland.

The museum pairs well with a walk through Mandal's old wooden streets nearby.
