Grønli Cave is a lit limestone cave near Mo i Rana, about two kilometres long with guided tours that usually cover the main chambers in under an hour. It is family-friendly by cave standards, but the floor is still uneven and the temperature stays cold year-round.

Bring a warm layer even in summer. The cafe by the cave sells tickets, food, and basic souvenirs.
