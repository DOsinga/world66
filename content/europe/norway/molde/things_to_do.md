Molde's main appeal is its setting rather than its sights. The panoramic view from Varden — 222 mountain peaks visible on a clear day — is the single best thing to do in town. The Romsdal Museum is substantial and worth two hours. The jazz festival in July transforms the town into one of the liveliest places on the Norwegian coast.
