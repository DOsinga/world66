Fru Haugans Hotel is one of the oldest hotels in Norway still in continuous operation, with roots going back to the 1794. The current building, facing the Vefsfjord, has been in business since the mid-19th century and is considered a historic landmark in Mosjøen. It occupies a central position in town and serves as a useful orientation point — the old town of Sjøgata is a short walk away.

The hotel still functions as the main accommodation option in town, and the restaurant is a decent choice for Norwegian classics including locally caught fish from the fjord. Even if you are not staying here, it is worth stepping inside to see the period interiors and the collection of photographs chronicling the town's history.
