---
title: Getting There
type: section
---

Mosjøen sits on both the E6 highway and the Nordland Railway, making it one of the more accessible towns in northern Norway. The railway journey north from Trondheim takes about four and a half hours; south from Bodø takes around three. Trains stop at Mosjøen Station on the northern edge of the old town.

By road, Mosjøen is 275 km north of Trondheim and 185 km south of Mosjøen, and most travellers pass through on the E6. The town is compact and walkable once you arrive — no local transport is needed to reach Sjøgata or the historic centre.
