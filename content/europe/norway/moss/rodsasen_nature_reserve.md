Rødsåsen Nature Reserve sits on the southwest tip of Jeløya and is a good short outing from Moss for views over the Oslofjord. The top of the reserve has the best lookout.

On clear days the view reaches far up the fjord, with Oslo's Holmenkollen ski slope visible in the distance.
