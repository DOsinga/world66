The obvious day-trip target from Narvik is the **Lofoten Islands**, now reachable by road and a short ferry hop via the Hålogaland Bridge and E10 — about three hours' drive each way. **Abisko National Park** in northern Sweden is roughly an hour east on the E10 and one of the most reliable places in Scandinavia for the aurora. The Norwegian fjord town of **Harstad** is also within day-trip range to the north.
