At the head of the Rombaksfjord, about 20 km east of town, the rusted remains of the German Type 1934 destroyer **Z2 Georg Thiele** are still partly visible above the waterline — one of the more haunting wartime relics in northern Europe. She was beached during the second Battle of Narvik in April 1940 and never recovered. Decades of salvage have removed most of the metal, but the bow section and the angled keel are still in place against the cliff.

Sport divers with dry-suit certification can visit her, along with several other wartime wrecks in the surrounding fjords, including the **Erich Giese**, the **Hermann Künne**, and the **Wilhelm Heidkamp**. Several operators in Narvik run boat-based dive trips between May and September. From shore, the most accessible viewing point is at Sildvik, signposted from the E6 just before the Hålogaland Bridge.
