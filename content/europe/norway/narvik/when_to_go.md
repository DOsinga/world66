---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

Narvik has two very distinct travel seasons.

**Summer** (June–August) brings the **midnight sun**: from about 27 May to 17 July the sun never sets, and the light stays bright through the night. Temperatures sit around 15–20°C at sea level, sometimes briefly higher. This is the time for hiking the Rallarvegen along the ore railway, climbing to the cable-car platform at midnight, and fishing the fjords by small boat.

**Winter** (late November–February) brings the **polar night** — from 7 December to 5 January the sun does not rise — and with it three of Narvik's main draws: **alpine skiing** at Narvikfjellet, **killer whale safaris** in the fjords, and excellent chances of seeing the **northern lights** away from the lights of town. February has lengthening days and is often the best month for snow.

**Shoulder seasons.** April and May can be very pretty as snow melts in the lowlands but lingers high up, and September brings short autumn colour on the birches before the snow returns.
