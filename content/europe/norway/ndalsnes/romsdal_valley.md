Romsdal Valley is the dramatic river valley running inland from Andalsnes toward Bjorli. Road E136 follows the Rauma river between waterfalls, salmon pools, and sheer mountain walls, with views of Trollveggen, Romsdalshorn, and the surrounding Romsdal Alps.
