---
title: "Eating Out"
type: section
---

Options in Skarsvåg are limited to what the village offers, which is essentially one thing done very well: fresh king crab from the Barents Sea. The King Crab House serves it simply — boiled or grilled — with a view of the cove. For anything else you will need to drive to Honningsvåg, the nearest town, about 35 kilometres away. The North Cape complex has a café and restaurant if you are up at the plateau.
