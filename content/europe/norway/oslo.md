Oslo is the Nordic capital that most visitors underestimate. Stockholm looks grander, Copenhagen is more charming, Helsinki has the edge in modern design. Oslo is none of those things, but it is the one Nordic capital where you can walk 15 minutes from a high-design museum to a hiking trail into deep forest, and that particular combination of compact city and raw landscape makes it uniquely worth a visit.

The centre fits around the harbour. [Aker Brygge and Tjuvholmen](/europe/norway/oslo/aker_brygge) have been redeveloped into a row of restaurants, galleries, and the Astrup Fearnley Museum. The [Opera House](/europe/norway/oslo/opera_house) — a white marble building rising from the water, designed so you can walk across the roof — is the signature piece of modern Oslo. The adjacent [Munch Museum](/europe/norway/oslo/munchmuseum) and [Deichman Library](/europe/norway/oslo/deichman_library) continue the same waterfront upgrade. The [National Museum](/europe/norway/oslo/national_museum), which opened in 2022, consolidates Norway's art collections including the 1893 "Scream." The **Royal Palace** at the top of Karl Johans gate closes the axis of the city's main street.

Across the fjord, the **Bygdøy peninsula** holds four world-class museums — the Kon-Tiki Museum (Thor Heyerdahl's balsa-wood raft), the [Viking Ship Museum](/europe/norway/oslo/vikingshiphouse) (closed for rebuild, reopening 2027), the [Fram polar expedition ship](/europe/norway/oslo/fram_museum), and the Norwegian Folk Museum's open-air collection of old wooden farmhouses. Further west, [Vigeland Park](/europe/norway/oslo/the_vigeland_park) is Gustav Vigeland's sculpture-park masterwork, open 24 hours and free. [Grünerløkka](/europe/norway/oslo/grunerlokka) is the city's best neighbourhood for cafes and independent culture.

Oslo's natural setting is the other attraction. The [Oslofjord islands](/europe/norway/oslo/oslofjord_islands) are 15–25 minutes away by ferry and popular for swimming in summer. The Oslomarka forest begins within tram reach of the centre; [Holmenkollen](/europe/norway/oslo/holmekollen) ski-jump is 20 minutes by metro. The [Future Library](/europe/norway/oslo/future_library) project — 1,000 trees planted in the Nordmarka to print manuscripts in 2114 — is one of the more unusual things you can visit near any European capital.

Oslo is expensive. A beer is €10, a hotel room rarely under €150. Plan accordingly — or eat at the [Mathallen food hall](/europe/norway/oslo/mathallen_oslo) in Vulkan, and use the tap water everywhere.
