Astrup Fearnley Museet is a contemporary art museum on Tjuvholmen, at the end of Aker Brygge. The private collection opened in 1993 and moved into its Renzo Piano-designed waterfront building in 2012. The building and harbor setting are part of the draw, even before the exhibitions.
