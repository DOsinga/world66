DogA, the Norwegian Centre for Design and Architecture, hosts exhibitions, talks, conferences, family activities, and design-focused events. The programme changes, so the value of a visit depends on what is on during your stay.

It is most worthwhile for travellers with an interest in contemporary architecture, design, urbanism, or Norwegian creative industries.
