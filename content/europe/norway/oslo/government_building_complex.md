Regjeringskvartalet is Oslo's government quarter, a compact area of ministry buildings that shows two centuries of Norwegian public architecture. Visitors can walk through the district to see the surviving modernist and art nouveau buildings, the reconstruction work after the 22 July 2011 bombing, and the July 22 Centre at the heart of the area.
