title: Things to Do
type: section
---

Holmenkollen's main draw is the ski jump and its museum — but the surrounding Nordmarka forest is the real reason Osloers come here in every season. In winter, the cross-country skiing trails start right from the T-bane station. In summer and autumn, the same network of paths is full of hikers and runners. The views over the Oslofjord from the hilltops are the best you'll get without taking a boat.
