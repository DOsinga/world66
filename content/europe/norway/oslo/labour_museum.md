Oslo's Labour Museum tells the story of the workers' district along the Akerselva, where factories, schools, swimming baths, and modest homes shaped the city's early industrial life. The setting is part of the experience: old brick industry and narrow streets still give the area a clear sense of place.

Come here for a grounded look at Oslo beyond royal and national monuments. It pairs well with a walk through Grünerløkka and along the river.
