Middelalderparken preserves the remains of medieval Oslo beside the modern Bjørvika waterfront. Church ruins, fragments of the old royal estate, and open lawns mark the area where the city stood before fire and politics shifted its centre westward.

The park is most rewarding if you have already seen the Opera House and Munch Museum nearby and want to understand what came before the new waterfront. It is quiet, low-key, and best visited in daylight.
