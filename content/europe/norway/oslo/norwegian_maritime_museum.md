The Norwegian Maritime Museum on Bygdøy covers Norway's seafaring history through ship models, boats, paintings, and exhibits on coastal life, exploration, and trade. It pairs well with the Fram Museum and Kon-Tiki Museum nearby, but has its own focus on the broader maritime culture that shaped Norway.
