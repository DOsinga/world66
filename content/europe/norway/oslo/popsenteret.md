Popsenteret is Oslo's popular music museum, with exhibitions and interactive displays about Norwegian pop, rock, and recording culture. It works best as a focused indoor stop in Grünerløkka, especially for visitors interested in the bands, studios, and music scenes behind modern Norwegian culture.
