Stovnertarnet is an accessible lookout tower in Oslo's northeastern suburbs. A long circular ramp winds gently up to the top, so the view is the point of the visit rather than a climb.

From the platform you can look across Groruddalen and, on clear days, toward the Oslo Fjord. It is a good stop if you are already exploring this side of the city or want an easy viewpoint away from the central waterfront.
