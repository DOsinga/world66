Oslo's Tramway Museum covers the city's trams, buses, trolleybuses, and metro, with old vehicles and displays on how public transport shaped the growing city. The wooden tram from the Holmenkollen line is one of the highlights.

It is a specialist museum, best for transport fans or families with children who like old vehicles. For most visitors it works as a pleasant add-on if you are already exploring this side of Oslo.
