The Oslo Tramway Museum preserves the city's tram, bus, trolleybus, and metro history, with restored vehicles including a wooden Holmenkollen Line tram and a trolleybus. It is a good stop for anyone interested in how Oslo grew around public transport, and the older trams give the collection more character than a simple technical display.
