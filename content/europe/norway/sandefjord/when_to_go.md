---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

Sandefjord is at its best in summer, from late June to August, when the coast is warm enough for swimming and the islands and beaches come alive. The harbour fills with leisure boats, and outdoor dining along the waterfront is pleasant in the long Nordic evenings.

Spring and early autumn are good for visiting the Whaling Museum and Gokstad mound without the summer crowds. Winters are cold and grey, and the main reason to visit is Torp airport — the town itself offers little to tempt travellers in December or January.
