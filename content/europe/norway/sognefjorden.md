Sognefjorden is the longest and deepest fjord in Norway — 205 kilometres from the sea to the head of the valley at Skjolden, and over 1,300 metres deep in places. It is the fjord that most Norwegians think of when they think of fjords: enormous, flanked by mountains rising 1,700 metres straight from the water, with farms and villages perched on narrow shelves of flat ground wherever the cliffs relent. The UNESCO World Heritage designation covers the Nærøyfjord arm and the Geirangerfjord together — these are considered the finest examples of fjord scenery anywhere in the world.

The inner branches of the fjord system are what most visitors come for. The [Nærøyfjord](/europe/norway/sognefjorden/naeroyfjord), which branches off the main fjord near Gudvangen, is the narrowest of the world-class Norwegian fjords — barely 250 metres wide in places, with walls rising a kilometre on each side. The [Aurlandsfjord](/europe/norway/sognefjorden/aurlandsfjord) branch leads to the village of Flåm, the arrival point for most visitors to the inner Sognefjorden and the base for the extraordinary [Flåmsbana](/europe/norway/sognefjorden/flamsbana) railway. Together these two arms are the most visited section of the fjord; arrive early or come in May or September if the July crowds are not for you.

Further into the fjord, things get quieter. Sogndal is the main town — a university town of about 7,000 on the Sogndalsfjord branch — and a good base for exploring the eastern reaches. The [Kaupanger Stave Church](/europe/norway/sognefjorden/kaupanger_stave_church), the largest stave church in Vestland county, is nearby. Further east, the valley narrows toward Laerdal, where the world's longest road tunnel (24.5 kilometres) provides the main connection to eastern Norway. [Borgund Stave Church](/europe/norway/sognefjorden/borgund_stave_church), in the valley above Laerdal, is the best-preserved medieval stave church in Norway and one of the finest examples of Viking-era wooden architecture anywhere.

The fjord is best approached by the combination of ferry and railway that has made western Norway so beloved. Arriving by boat from [Bergen](/europe/norway/bergen), or taking the Norway in a Nutshell tour that combines the Flåmsbana railway, the Nærøyfjord ferry, and the Voss railway, gives the fullest picture of what the Sognefjorden landscape is actually like.
