The Aurland Mountain Road climbs from the fjord up to a 1,300-metre pass between Aurland and Laerdal. It was the old road before the Laerdal Tunnel opened in 2000, and is now maintained as one of Norway's national tourist routes.

The reason to drive it is the panorama: fjord views near Aurland, open highland scenery on the plateau, and snow patches that can linger into summer. The road is seasonal and weather-dependent; when it is closed, traffic uses the tunnel below.
