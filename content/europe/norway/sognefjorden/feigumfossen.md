Feigumfossen is a 218-metre waterfall dropping from the mountains above Lustrafjorden, one of Sognefjorden's inner arms. You can see it from the road, but a walking path leads closer to the base for a much better sense of its height and spray.

It is a good stop if you are driving the quieter eastern side of the fjord and want a short walk with a big landscape payoff.
