---
title: Getting Around
type: section
---

The fjord's length — 205 kilometres — means that getting around requires a combination of road, ferry, and rail. The main western section (Flåm, Aurland, Undredal) is best covered on foot or by bicycle; the roads are narrow but quiet and the scale is manageable. The Flåmsbana railway is the most efficient way to reach Myrdal and the plateau above.

Ferry services connect the main settlements along the fjord; the car ferries are essential for crossing the fjord (there are no bridges over the main fjord). Car rental is available in Sogndal and at Bergen Airport; driving allows access to the inland valleys and the mountain roads. The Norway in a Nutshell route packages the key transport connections for the Flåm-Nærøyfjord section into a seamless day trip.
