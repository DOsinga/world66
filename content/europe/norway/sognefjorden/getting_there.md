---
title: Getting There
type: section
---

The main access points to the Sognefjorden are Bergen (2.5 hours from Flåm by bus or express boat) and Oslo (about 4 hours from Sogndal by bus and ferry, or 5 hours from Laerdal by car). The most convenient route from Bergen is the Voss–Myrdal–Flåm train combination; from Oslo, driving via Laerdal and the Lærdal Tunnel is the most practical option.

Sogndal Airport has daily flights from Oslo and Bergen operated by Widerøe, which makes the eastern fjord reachable by air in an hour. Flåm itself has no airport; Bergen Airport is the nearest major hub, about 2.5 hours away by the various fjord transport combinations. The express passenger boat service (Fylkesbåtane) runs between Flåm, Aurland, Leikanger, Hella, and Sogndal on the fjord, connecting the main settlements without a car.
