Kaupanger Stave Church is the largest stave church in Vestland county and one of the largest in Norway. It stands in the village of Kaupanger on the north shore of the Sognefjorden, about 10 kilometres east of Sogndal. The current structure dates from approximately 1135-1170 CE, though the external walls were replaced in the 17th century; the internal nave with its carved wooden columns is original and unusually large for a stave church.

The church is still used for regular worship — services are held on summer Sundays — which gives it a different quality from the museum-piece churches elsewhere in the valley. The De Heibergske Samlinger (Heiberg Collections) folk museum, adjacent to the church, houses buildings from across the Sognefjord region relocated to form an open-air museum. The combination of the stave church and the folk museum makes Kaupanger an excellent half-day stop when driving or cycling along the northern shore of the fjord.
