Norway in a Nutshell is the name given to a classic one-day or two-day touring route combining train, ferry, and bus through the most dramatic section of the Sognefjorden. The standard route runs from Bergen: train to Myrdal, then the Flåmsbana railway down to Flåm, ferry along the Aurlandsfjord and through the Nærøyfjord to Gudvangen, bus to Voss, and train back to Bergen. It takes a full day and covers all the main attractions.

The route can also be done in reverse, or started from Oslo via Myrdal, making it a possible transit between Norway's two main cities. The ferry section through the Nærøyfjord is the highlight. The whole route is pre-packaged and straightforward to book through the Norwegian railway company (Vy) or the fjordnorway.no ticket system; no guide or organised tour is needed. In summer, the route attracts very large numbers; the best experience is outside July, or by booking early morning departures that arrive ahead of the crowds.
