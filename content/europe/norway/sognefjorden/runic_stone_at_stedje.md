The runic stone at Stedje stands near Stedje Church in Sogndal and dates from around 1100. It is a small but worthwhile stop for travelers interested in Norway's medieval church sites and runic inscriptions.

The present church was built in a style inspired by stave churches, and the site is thought to have older church history behind it.
