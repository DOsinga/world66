---
title: Things to Do
type: section
---

The Sognefjorden is primarily about being on or near the water. Fjord cruises, the Flåmsbana railway, hiking above the fjord on the Aurlandsvegen, and visiting the medieval stave churches are the main activities. The Norway in a Nutshell route brings in large numbers of package tourists; independent travellers who slow down and stay a night or two in the inner fjord get considerably more from it.
