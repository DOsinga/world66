Urnes Stave Church is the oldest stave church in Norway, built around 1130 and listed as a UNESCO World Heritage Site. Its carved wooden portals mix Viking-age animal ornament with medieval Christian architecture, which makes the church one of the clearest places to see Norway's religious transition in timber and line.

Visits are by guided tour, and the church can be busy because of its fame. The setting above the Lustrafjord is part of the experience, with water and mountains framing the small dark building.
