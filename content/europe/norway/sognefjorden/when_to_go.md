---
title: When to Go
type: section
---

The Sognefjorden is open for visitors year-round, but the experience varies enormously by season. July is the peak month for the Norway in a Nutshell route — ferries are full, Flåm is crowded, and the Nærøyfjord boats require advance booking. May and June are beautiful: the snow is still on the high peaks, the deciduous trees are at their most intense green, and the waterfalls are at their most powerful from snow-melt.

September is arguably the finest month: the mountains turn gold and rust as the birch trees change colour, the summer crowds have thinned, and the weather is still generally reliable. October brings more rain and mist, which can make the fjords look extraordinarily dramatic if you accept rather than resist the grey.

Winter closes the mountain roads (Aurlandsvegen, Snow Road) and makes ferry schedules less frequent, but the fjords are accessible by road via the Lærdal Tunnel, and a grey winter fjord has its own attraction. The Flåmsbana runs year-round.
