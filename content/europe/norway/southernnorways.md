You might call it Norway's Riviera. The southern coast — traditionally known as Sørlandet, and since a 2020 county merger officially part of Agder — has the mildest climate in the country. It's a run of old fishing villages and white-wooden harbour towns strung along a sunny coastline of islands, coves, and archipelagos. Whether strolling the harbours, sailing among the skerries, or hiking and fishing in the forested interior, Sørlandet is Norway at its most relaxed.

[Kristiansand](/europe/norway/kristiansand) is the regional capital and biggest city, a good base for exploring the coast in either direction. East of it, [Arendal](/europe/norway/arendal) and [Grimstad](/europe/norway/grimstad) share the same narrow streets and pleasure-boat harbours; further along, [Lillesand](/europe/norway/lillesand), [Tvedestrand](/europe/norway/tvedestrand), and [Risør](/europe/norway/risor) get progressively smaller and quieter, each with the same white-painted wooden architecture but its own personality — Risør in particular is one of the prettiest harbour towns in Norway, and fills up every August for its Wooden Boat Festival. [Mandal](/europe/norway/mandal), west of Kristiansand, has Norway's longest sand beach.

Inland, the Setesdal valley runs 210 km north from Kristiansand to Hovden, climbing from gentle farmland into wild mountain country. Along the way is the preserved Setesdalbanen steam railway, the Byglandsfjord (where the wood-burning steamboat Bjoren still runs), the Setesdal Mineral Park, whitewater rafting at Syrtveitfossen, and the silversmiths of Sylvartun. The valley holds Europe's most southerly herd of wild reindeer.

At the head of the valley, Hovden is southern Norway's largest winter sports resort — downhill and cross-country skiing, plus an indoor-outdoor water park — and a good summer hiking base, with marked trails running cabin to cabin through the mountains. Between the coast and the mountains, a broad belt of forested rivers and lakes offers excellent fishing for trout, salmon, sea trout, and arctic char in every season.
