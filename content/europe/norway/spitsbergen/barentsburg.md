Barentsburg is the Russian mining settlement on [Spitsbergen](/europe/norway/spitsbergen), set on the shore of Grønfjorden under Arctic mountains. It is small, strange, and memorable: Soviet-era slogans, coal infrastructure, painted apartment blocks, and a dramatic view across the fjord all packed into a settlement you can cross on foot.

The town exists because of the Svalbard Treaty, which gives treaty countries the right to economic activity on the archipelago. Russia's state-owned Trust Arktikugol has mined here since the 1930s, and Barentsburg remains the main Russian presence on Svalbard.

Most visitors come on a day trip from [Longyearbyen](/europe/norway/spitsbergen/longyearbyen), by boat in summer or snowmobile when conditions allow. Do not expect a polished museum town. The appeal is the uneasy mix of working mine, Arctic outpost, and Cold War time capsule.
