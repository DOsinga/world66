Art Arctic Gallery mixes local history with rotating art displays. The strongest material is often the older Arctic material culture: whaling objects, carved crosses, and fragments connected to early exploration.

It is worth a look if you have finished the short circuit of outdoor sights and want a warmer, slower stop before leaving town.
