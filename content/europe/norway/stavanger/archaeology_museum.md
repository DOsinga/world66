The Museum of Archaeology introduces Rogaland's prehistory, from the Stone Age through the Bronze Age, Viking period, and Middle Ages. The collection is strongest on local finds, so it gives useful context before you head out to the fjords, islands, and old coastal settlements around Stavanger.
