The Norwegian Oil Museum explains the offshore industry that transformed Stavanger and modern Norway after the first North Sea discoveries in 1969. The harbourfront building is striking, and the exhibits mix engineering, economics, daily life offshore, and the risks of drilling at sea.

Displays include submersibles, drilling equipment, a mock oil platform, and audiovisual material, making it one of Stavanger's stronger museums for both adults and families.
