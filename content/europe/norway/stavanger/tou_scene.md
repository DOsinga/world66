Tou Scene is an old brewery on Stavanger's east side turned into a cultural centre, with exhibition spaces, concerts, theatre, and assorted one-off events. The industrial setting gives it a rougher, more local feel than the polished museums around the harbour.

It is best approached as an evening or event stop rather than a guaranteed daytime sight. Check the programme before going; when something good is on, it adds a useful edge to Stavanger's otherwise tidy centre.
