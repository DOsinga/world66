---
title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Stokmarknes is a small place, and the [Hurtigruten Museum](/europe/norway/stokmarknes/hurtigruten_museum) is the main reason to stop. The town also makes a useful base for exploring the wider Vesterålen archipelago — quieter than the Lofoten, with equally dramatic scenery and excellent natural history. [Whale-watching trips](/europe/norway/stokmarknes/whale_watching_vesteralen) depart from Andenes to the north, where sperm whales feed year-round. The [sea bird colony at Bleiksøya](/europe/norway/stokmarknes/vesteralen_birdwatching), also on Andøya, is one of Norway's best puffin sites. Just east of town, [Hadsel Church](/europe/norway/stokmarknes/hadsel_church) is a rare octagonal wooden building from 1824 on a site of worship dating to the 14th century.
