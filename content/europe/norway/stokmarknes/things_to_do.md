---

Stokmarknes is a small place, and the [Hurtigruten Museum](/europe/norway/stokmarknes/hurtigruten_museum) is the main reason to stop. Just east of town, [Hadsel Church](/europe/norway/stokmarknes/hadsel_church) is a rare octagonal wooden building from 1824 on a site of worship dating to the 14th century. For whale-watching and the Bleiksøya seabird colony, see [Andenes](/europe/norway/andenes), about 85 km north.
