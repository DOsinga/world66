---
title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Stokmarknes is a small place, and the [Hurtigruten Museum](/europe/norway/stokmarknes/hurtigruten_museum) is the main reason to stop. The town also makes a useful base for exploring the wider Vesterålen archipelago — quieter than the Lofoten, with equally dramatic scenery and good natural history. [Whale-watching trips](/europe/norway/stokmarknes/whale_watching_vesteralen) depart from Andenes to the north, while the [sea bird colonies at Bleiksøya](/europe/norway/stokmarknes/vesteralen_birdwatching) are among the best in northern Norway. Just east of town, [Hadsel Church](/europe/norway/stokmarknes/hadsel_church) is a striking octagonal building with roots going back to the 14th century.
