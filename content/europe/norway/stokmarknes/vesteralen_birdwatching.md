The Vesterålen islands are quieter and less visited than the Lofoten, which makes them a better choice for birdwatching. The sea bird colonies at Bleiksøya — a small island just off the village of Bleik on Andøya, about 85 km north of Stokmarknes — are among the most accessible in northern Norway. The colony hosts several hundred thousand Atlantic puffins, along with razorbills, guillemots, and northern gannets during summer.

Boat trips from Bleik run from late May through early August, the peak nesting season. The crossing takes only a few minutes and the island's steep cliffs bring you close to the birds without disturbing the colony. Sea eagles are regularly spotted throughout the archipelago, particularly around fish-landing sites and the calmer inlets. The relative lack of tourist infrastructure in Vesterålen means you will often have viewpoints and hiking paths to yourself, which Lofoten visitors rarely get to say.
