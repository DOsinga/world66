Vesterålen is one of the best places in Norway to see sperm whales without travelling to Svalbard. Unlike the humpbacks that come to the nearby Lofoten fjords in winter to feed on herring, sperm whales are present in Vesterålen year-round because the deep submarine canyon off the coast provides the squid they hunt. The most productive departure point is Andenes, about 90 km north of Stokmarknes, where several operators run dedicated whale safari boats with naturalists on board.

Safari season runs roughly from late May to mid-September, when conditions allow reliable sightings. The operators at Andenes are serious about the science — the whale centre there tracks individual sperm whales by their fluke markings and has built up a catalogue of identified individuals over decades. If the whale safari is the reason you are stopping in Vesterålen rather than staying exclusively on Lofoten, Andenes is where to base yourself; but Stokmarknes makes a reasonable stopover on the drive up the archipelago.
