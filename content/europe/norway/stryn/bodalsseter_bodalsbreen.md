The hike from Bodalsseter toward Bodalsbreen is an easy way to get into the glacier valleys around Loen without committing to a hard mountain route. The glacier view depends on conditions and distance, but the walk itself is the point: farms, steep valley walls, meltwater, and the cold open feel of Jostedalsbreen country.
