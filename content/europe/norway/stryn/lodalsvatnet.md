Lodalsvatnet is a mountain lake in the Loen area, enclosed by steep peaks and glacier-shaped scenery. Fine mineral particles in the water can give the lake a green cast, especially in bright weather.

It is a strong scenic stop for visitors using Stryn or Loen as a base, and it pairs well with nearby glacier and valley trips.
