Loen Skylift is the fast way to understand the shape of inner Nordfjord. The cable car climbs from Loen to Mount Hoven in minutes, lifting you from fjord level to a high viewpoint over valleys, lakes, mountains, and glacier country.

It is weather-dependent, so check the cloud before paying for the ride. In clear conditions, it is one of the easiest big-view experiences in western Norway, with walks from the top if you want more than the platform and restaurant.
