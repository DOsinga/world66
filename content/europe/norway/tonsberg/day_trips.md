The Vestfold coast south of Tønsberg offers several good half-day or day-trip options. **Borre** (about 25 km north, near Horten) is home to the largest Viking-age burial ground in Scandinavia — a grassy headland covered in royal tumuli that most people drive past without stopping. It is worth going slowly. **Åsgårdstrand**, 20 km north on the fjord, is the small fishing village where Edvard Munch spent his summers and painted some of his most important works; his house is a small museum. **Sandefjord**, 30 km south on the train line, has a whaling museum of its own and a pleasant waterfront.

The islands of Nøtterøy and Tjøme lie immediately south of Tønsberg, accessible by road. In summer the coastline there — low granite and heath, dotted with bathing spots — is where much of the local population disappears on weekends.
