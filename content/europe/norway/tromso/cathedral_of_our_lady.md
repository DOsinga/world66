The Cathedral of Our Lady is a tiny Catholic church in central Tromso, dating from 1861. Its scale is modest, but its setting is unusual: this is the seat of the world's northernmost Catholic bishop.

It is a quick stop rather than a major sight. Step inside if the doors are open, especially if you are tracing Tromso's 19th-century religious and trading history around the old centre.
