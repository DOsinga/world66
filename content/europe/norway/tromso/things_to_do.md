---
title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

The [Arctic Cathedral](/europe/norway/tromso/arctic_cathedral) across the Tromsø Bridge is the city's most photographed building — its triangular aluminium facade and enormous stained-glass window are unlike anything else in Norway. The [Fjellheisen Cable Car](/europe/norway/tromso/fjellheisen) ascends 420 metres to the summit of Storsteinen in five minutes; from the top, the city, its surrounding islands, and the fjords spread out in every direction.

For museums: the [Tromsø Museum](/europe/norway/tromso/tromso_museum) is the northernmost university museum in the world, strongest on Sami culture and Arctic natural history. The [Polar Museum](/europe/norway/tromso/polar_museum) covers Arctic hunting and exploration in an 1837 blubber warehouse on the waterfront. [Polaria](/europe/norway/tromso/polaria) has the world's northernmost aquarium and a bearded seal pool with underwater viewing. The [Nordnorsk Kunstmuseum](/europe/norway/tromso/nordnorsk_kunstmuseum) is the main art institution for northern Norway, with free permanent-collection access. The [Perspektivet Museum](/europe/norway/tromso/perspektivet) focuses on cultural history and documentary photography.

[Tromsø Cathedral](/europe/norway/tromso/tromso_cathedral) is Norway's only wooden cathedral, built in 1861. The [Arctic-Alpine Botanical Garden](/europe/norway/tromso/tromso_museum) at Breivika is the world's northernmost, and is free and at its best in July.
