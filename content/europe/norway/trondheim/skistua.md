Skistua sits in Bymarka, the forested outdoor area above Trondheim, and is closely tied to the city's ski and hiking culture. For visitors, it is useful as a practical landmark for reaching trails, winter routes, and broad upland scenery outside the compact centre.

The appeal changes with the season: snow brings cross-country skiing, while milder months are better for walks and views. It is not a monument, but it is a strong window into how Trondheim residents use the surrounding hills.
