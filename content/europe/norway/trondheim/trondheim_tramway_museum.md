The Trondheim Tramway Museum in Munkvoll covers the city's tram history, including the Grakallbanen line that still climbs west from the centre. It is a small, specialist museum rather than a full-day sight, but it is a worthwhile stop for visitors interested in public transport history. Opening is seasonal, so check before making the trip.
