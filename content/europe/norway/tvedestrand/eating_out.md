---
title: Eating Out
type: section
---

Tvedestrand's eating scene is small but honest, built around fresh Norwegian seafood and the long summer evenings that draw visitors to waterfront tables. Expect shrimp, crayfish and local catch served simply — this is not the place for elaborate tasting menus, but the quality of ingredients speaks for itself. Most places are seasonal, opening in earnest from late May and winding down by September.
