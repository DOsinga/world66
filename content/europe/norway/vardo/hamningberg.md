Hamningberg is a remote fishing village north of Vardo, reached by a stark coastal road through one of Finnmark's most exposed landscapes. Unlike much of the region, it survived World War II destruction, so its old houses give a rare view of pre-war coastal settlement in far northeastern Norway.

Come for the drive, the Arctic shore, and the sense of reaching the road's end. The village is small and seasonal, so treat it as a scenic heritage stop rather than a place with many services.
