Skjervet Waterfall is a dramatic roadside fall in the narrow country between Voss and Granvin, where water, rock, and engineered road bends all fight for attention. It is a simple stop, but a memorable one if you are exploring the fjord roads by car.

The viewing areas make it easy to appreciate the drop without a long hike. Wet rock and spray are part of the experience, so wear shoes with grip and give yourself time to look into the gorge rather than just photograph the cascade.
