Tvindefossen is a broad, tiered waterfall north of Voss, dropping in a white fan beside the road. Its easy access makes it one of the area's simplest high-impact nature stops, especially for travelers driving between Voss, Flåm, and the fjord country.

The waterfall is quick to visit but visually strong, with spray, rock, and green slopes close at hand. It pairs well with a broader day of valleys, viewpoints, and fjord approaches rather than needing a trip of its own.
