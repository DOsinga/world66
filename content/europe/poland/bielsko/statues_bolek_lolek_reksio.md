Scattered along the route between the train station and the old town are three small bronze statues honouring Bielsko's most famous cultural export: the cartoon brothers Bolek and Lolek, and the friendly dog Reksio. Each was made by local sculptor Bronisław Krzysztof and installed in 2011.

The statues are easy to miss — they sit at ground level, life-sized and unfussy — but they're a nice cue to Bielsko's other identity as a centre of Polish animation. The Studio Filmów Rysunkowych nearby has occasional open days and a small archive; even if you don't visit it, the statues make a small, friendly trail through the modern city centre.
