---
title: "Eating Out"
type: section
---

Eating in Bochnia is straightforward Polish — *pierogi*, *kotlet schabowy* (breaded pork chop), hearty soups and the rest of the small-town menu. Several restaurants ring the Rynek and serve very capable lunches for around 30-50 zlotys.

**Restauracja Murana** on the Rynek is the reliable upmarket choice, with good *pierogi* and grilled meats. For a more casual lunch, **Pizzeria Mamma Mia** does honest Italian, and the *bary mleczne* (milk bars) on the side streets serve filling Polish staples for almost nothing. Stop for a coffee and *szarlotka* (apple cake) at one of the cafés on Sutoris before heading back to Kraków.
