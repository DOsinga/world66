---

The Ceramics Museum in Boleslawiec traces the history of the town's pottery tradition from its origins to the present day. The collection includes historic pieces alongside examples of contemporary production, giving a clear sense of how the craft has developed and how consistent the visual language of Boleslawiec ware has remained over centuries.

The museum is small and takes about an hour to visit properly. It is a useful context-setter if you are planning to visit the factory showrooms — understanding the history and technique makes the shopping more interesting. Located in the old town, a short walk from the market square.
