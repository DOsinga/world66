Brodnica is a town in Kuyavian-Pomeranian Voivodeship in northern Poland, on the [Drwęca river](/europe/poland/brodnica/drweca_river). It has a modest medieval core that makes it worth a stop if you are travelling through this part of the country.

The main sights are the unusually triangle-shaped [old marketplace](/europe/poland/brodnica/market_square) — a rarity in Poland where most market squares are rectangular — and the remains of a [Teutonic Knights castle](/europe/poland/brodnica/brodnica_castle) dating from the 14th century, including a well-preserved tower that can be climbed. The town is not a major tourist destination, but it has a genuine old centre and the surrounding [Brodnica Lakeland](/europe/poland/brodnica/brodnica_lakeland) offers kayaking on the Drwęca river, which is popular with Polish canoeists in summer.
