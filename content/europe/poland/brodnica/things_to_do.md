---
title: 'Things to Do'
type: section
---

Brodnica's old town can be walked in an hour or two: the unusual triangular Market Square, the ruined Teutonic castle on the Drwęca, the brick Gothic parish church, and the Mazurian Gate. Beyond the town itself, the Brodnica Lakeland to the east is the real draw — Drwęca river kayaking, glacial lakes and pine forest.
