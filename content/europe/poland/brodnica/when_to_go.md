---
title: 'When to Go'
type: section
---

Brodnica is at its best between May and September, when the Drwęca is warm enough for kayaking and the Lakeland is fully in leaf. July and August are the busiest months — Polish families on summer holidays — but the crowds rarely overwhelm the town itself. Winters are cold and damp, and most of the outdoor reasons to come disappear under grey skies.
