The Botanic Garden on the northern side of Bydgoszcz is a large green area with varied plantings, streams, rocks, ponds, and quiet paths. It is more of a slow outdoor stop than a formal show garden, but it gives the city a useful nature break.

It can be combined with the nearby forest park, zoo, and family attractions if you are spending time outside the centre.
