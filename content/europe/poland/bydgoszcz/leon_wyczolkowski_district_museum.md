The Leon Wyczółkowski District Museum is not a single-room local collection, but a group of museum buildings around Mill Island covering art, history, and ethnography. The modern art collection is the strongest reason to make time for it.

Its riverside setting also makes it easy to combine with a walk across Mill Island, the old mills, and the footbridges over the Brda. Visitors with limited time should check which branches are open before choosing where to start.
