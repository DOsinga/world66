The Muzeum Wojsk Ladowych is Bydgoszcz's land forces museum, with a strong outdoor display of tanks, artillery, rockets, and other heavy military equipment. Inside, the exhibits trace Polish military history and give context to the weapons and vehicles in the yard.

It is outside the old centre, so it works best as a targeted stop for visitors interested in military history rather than as part of a casual city walk.
