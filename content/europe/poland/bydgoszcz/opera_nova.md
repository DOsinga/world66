Opera Nova sits by the Brda River just beside Mill Island, a rounded modern building that has become one of Bydgoszcz's visual signatures. It hosts opera, ballet, concerts, and festivals, and its position makes it hard to miss even if you are only walking the riverfront.

Check the programme if you are staying overnight. Even without a performance, the building works as part of the riverside circuit through the centre.
