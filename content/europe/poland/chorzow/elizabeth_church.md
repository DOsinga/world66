Elizabeth Church is a Lutheran church from 1844, set within a cemetery in Chorzów. It is tied to Elisabeth Ludovika, the Prussian queen whose delayed conversion to Lutheranism gave the church its name.

The church is a modest but worthwhile historic stop if you are already exploring Chorzów's older industrial and religious sights.
