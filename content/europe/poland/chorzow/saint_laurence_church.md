Saint Laurence Church is a wooden church from 1599, moved to Chorzow from Knurow in the 1930s. Its timber construction makes it stand apart from the heavy industrial landscape usually associated with Upper Silesia.

It is a small, photogenic stop and pairs well with the nearby open-air ethnographic museum and Silesian Park.
