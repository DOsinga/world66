St Hedwig Church is a Neo-Romanesque Roman Catholic church from the 1870s, set a short way south of central Chorzow. Its cemetery lies nearby, making the complex a quiet contrast to the city's heavier industrial sights.

The church is a modest stop rather than a major monument, but it adds another layer to Chorzow's older religious landscape.
