Castle Hill is Cieszyn's essential stop. The medieval castle itself was mostly lost, but the site still holds the round 11th-century chapel of St. Nicholas and St. Wenceslas, one of the oldest Romanesque buildings in Poland, and the 14th-century Piast Tower. Climb the tower for the best view over the Olza, Cieszyn, and Český Těšín.
