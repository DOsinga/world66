The Museum of Cieszyn Silesia gives useful depth to the town's borderland history. Its collections cover archaeology, art, town life, and the wider Cieszyn Silesia region, all housed in the former Larisch palace near the old centre. It is the stop that turns Cieszyn from a pretty border town into a place with a long regional story.
