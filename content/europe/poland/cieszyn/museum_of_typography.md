The Museum of Typography is a small but memorable stop for anyone interested in print, design, or old machinery. Cieszyn once had an important printing trade, and the museum keeps that world tangible with presses, type cases, and demonstrations. It pairs well with Castle Hill because it shows the town's working culture rather than only its monuments.
