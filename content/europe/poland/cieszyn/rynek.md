The Rynek is Cieszyn's main square and the natural place to begin wandering. It is not as grand as Krakow's or Wrocław's, but its scale works in its favour: arcaded houses, the town hall, cafes, and side streets all sit within a few minutes of one another. From here, Głęboka Street leads toward Castle Hill.
