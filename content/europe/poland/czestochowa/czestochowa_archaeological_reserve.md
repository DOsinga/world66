Częstochowa's archaeological reserve is a museum branch focused on early burial and settlement history in the region. It gives the city a deeper time layer beyond the pilgrimage route to Jasna Góra and the 19th-century avenues.

The reserve is a specialist stop, but a worthwhile one for visitors who like archaeology or want to broaden a Częstochowa visit beyond the monastery. Check the local museum schedule, as branch hours can differ from the main museum.
