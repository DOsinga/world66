Czestochowa's Jewish Cemetery is a sobering place, and an important one. The city had a large Jewish community before the Second World War, and the cemetery preserves part of that history in rows of matzevot, fragments, and memorial space.

Visit with respect and enough time to read the site quietly. It is not a casual sightseeing stop, but it gives necessary depth to a city many travellers otherwise know only through Jasna Gora and Catholic pilgrimage.
