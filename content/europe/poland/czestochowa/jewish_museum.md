Czestochowa's Jewish Museum covers the city's once-large Jewish community and the destruction it suffered during the Second World War. Exhibits connect local Jewish history with broader stories of religious life, persecution, and memory.

It is a sober but worthwhile stop for visitors interested in Jewish heritage, especially when combined with the Jewish cemetery and the older streets east of the main pilgrimage route.
