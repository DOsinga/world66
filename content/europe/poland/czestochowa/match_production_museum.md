The Match Production Museum preserves the machinery and process of Częstochowa's match-making industry, which began here in the late 19th century. The factory equipment shows how matches were made in the 20th century, turning a humble everyday object into a compact industrial history lesson.

It is a specialist stop, but a good one if you like working machinery, factories, or smaller museums with a clear local story. It pairs well with the city's more obvious churches and central museums.
