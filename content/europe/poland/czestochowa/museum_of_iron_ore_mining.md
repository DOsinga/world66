The Museum of Iron Ore Mining explains the mining industry that once operated around Czestochowa. The displays focus on the deep seams, tools, and working conditions of local iron ore extraction, with enough industrial detail to make it more than a general history exhibit.

It is a specialist stop, but a useful one if you want to see a side of the city beyond Jasna Gora and the pilgrimage route.
