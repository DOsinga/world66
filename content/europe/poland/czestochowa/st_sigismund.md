St Sigismund Church is one of central Częstochowa's older religious landmarks and a useful counterweight to the much larger Jasna Góra pilgrimage complex. Its parish setting gives a more local view of the city's church life and historic centre.

The church is best visited as part of a walk through central Częstochowa rather than as a destination on its own. It rewards travelers who want to see more of the city's religious architecture after the famous monastery.
