Church Path is a narrow pedestrian passage through Elblag's Old Town, running between brick houses on a route that once linked three churches. It is a small sight, but a pleasant one: the rebuilt historic streets, church towers, and tight brick walls give it a more intimate feel than the broader lanes nearby.

It is worth a short detour while walking between St Nicholas Cathedral, the canal, and the Archaeological and Historical Museum.
