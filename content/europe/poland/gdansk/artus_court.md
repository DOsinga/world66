Artus Court is one of the grand historic interiors on Gdansk's Long Market. Once a meeting place and exchange for wealthy merchants and brotherhoods, it now forms part of the Gdansk History Museum.

The building is worth a deliberate stop for its ornate facade, ceremonial hall, and the way it shows the scale of the city's trading wealth in the 16th and 17th centuries.
