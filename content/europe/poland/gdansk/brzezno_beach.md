Brzeźno Beach is one of Gdańsk's easiest Baltic beaches to reach from the city centre. It sits behind a large coastal park and has the kind of summer facilities that make it practical for a half-day by the water.

It can get crowded in warm weather, but that is part of the local seaside scene. The pier and promenade make it worthwhile even if the Baltic is too cold for swimming.
