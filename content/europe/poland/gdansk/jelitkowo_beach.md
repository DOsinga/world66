Jelitkowo Beach is one of Gdansk's busiest Baltic beaches, close to Oliwa and near the Sopot boundary. It has a more resort-like feel than quieter city beaches, with summer facilities, places to eat fish or ice cream, and easy links to the coastal promenade.
