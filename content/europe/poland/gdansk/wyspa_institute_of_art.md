Wyspa Institute of Art is a contemporary art space on the former Gdansk shipyard grounds. Its exhibitions, performances, concerts, and art bookshop connect the area's industrial history with the city's newer cultural scene.

It is most useful as part of a shipyard-focused visit, especially if you are already going to the European Solidarity Centre nearby. Check the current program before making a special trip.
