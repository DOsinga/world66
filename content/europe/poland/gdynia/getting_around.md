---
title: "Getting Around"
type: section
---

The Tri-City — Gdańsk, Sopot, and Gdynia — runs as one transport network connected by the **SKM** commuter rail line that runs every few minutes from early morning until late evening. The journey end to end takes about 50 minutes; Gdynia central station is on Świętojańska, a five-minute walk from the pier.

Within Gdynia: city buses and **trolleybuses** (Gdynia is one of only three Polish cities with trolleybuses still operating — most of the central routes use them). Tickets are bought at machines on the platforms or in the vehicle. The waterfront and the centre are easily walkable; Kępa Redłowska and Orłowo are quicker by SKM than by bus.

**Cars** are useful for the day trips into Kashubia or to Hel Peninsula but otherwise a nuisance in the centre.
