ORP Błyskawica ("Lightning") is the older of Gdynia's two museum ships — a 114-metre Grom-class destroyer launched in 1936, with a six-year war record under the Polish Navy in exile, and the oldest preserved destroyer anywhere in the world. She has been moored as a museum at the Southern Pier since 1976.

The visit takes you below decks through the engine room, the crew quarters, the captain's cabin, and the operations rooms; many of the gun emplacements and bridge equipment are original. Labels in Polish and English. Allow 90 minutes. Combined tickets are available with the Dar Pomorza next door.
