The Sea Towers, completed in 2009 to a design by the Polish firm APA Wojciechowski, are the city's most visible modern building — twin glass towers (143 and 124 metres) on the harbour edge, holding apartments, offices, and a public observation deck on the 35th floor.

The deck offers the best aerial view of the city: the Southern Pier and museum ships immediately below, the port and shipyards beyond, the wooded hills of Tri-City Landscape Park to the west, and on clear days as far as Hel Peninsula 30 km out into the bay. Modest admission; lift access. The complex's ground floor has a few cafés and a small shopping arcade.
