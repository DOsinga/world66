The Torpedownia is the most photographed ruin on the Polish coast — two boxy concrete platforms standing about 300 m offshore between Babie Doły and Mechelinki, the remains of a 1942 German Kriegsmarine torpedo testing station. The connecting catwalk is gone; the platforms are slowly being undercut by the Baltic.

You cannot get on them — they are unsafe and posted off-limits — but the view from the beach, particularly in storm light, is striking. A short walk along the sand from the **Babie Doły** suburban-train station. Add a half-hour onto a Kępa Redłowska / Orłowo walk if you have come this far.
