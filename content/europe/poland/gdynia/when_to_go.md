---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

Gdynia has a Baltic coastal climate: cool, often grey, with the marine influence keeping the extremes off. Summer (June–August) is high season — long days, warm enough to swim (water around 18–20°C in July), beaches busy, and the city full of weekenders from Warsaw. **September** is the city at its best: warm light, school out, and the **Gdynia Film Festival** in the second week — the most important Polish film event of the year and worth coordinating a visit around if you have any interest.

Winter is cold but not severe, with snow more often dry than deep. The Christmas market on Skwer Kościuszki is small but pleasant. **March and April** are wet and best avoided.
