About 50 km south of Olsztyn lies the gently rolling field where, on 15 July 1410, the combined Polish-Lithuanian army under King Władysław Jagiełło crushed the Teutonic Knights. The battle is one of the foundational events of Polish national identity.

A grim 1960s monument and a small museum mark the spot. The annual reenactment, held on the weekend nearest 15 July, draws tens of thousands of spectators and several thousand armoured re-enactors. There is no public transport — drive, or join a tour from Olsztyn.
