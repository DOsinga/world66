The Forestry Museum in Goluchow spreads its exhibitions through several village buildings. The displays cover forest ecology, forestry technology, and the history of woodland management in Poland.

It is a specialist stop, but a worthwhile one if you are already visiting Goluchow or want a quieter day trip from Kalisz. Pair it with the village park and castle area rather than making it the only reason to travel.
