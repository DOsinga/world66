The Franciscan Monastery in Panewniki is one of Katowice's major religious complexes, centred on a large basilica west of the city centre. Its scale and active parish life make it more than a neighborhood church.

Visitors come for the architecture, the monastery grounds, and the sense of a living Catholic institution in Silesia. Around Christmas it is especially known locally for its nativity display, but even outside that season it is a worthwhile detour for travellers interested in Katowice beyond the modern core.
