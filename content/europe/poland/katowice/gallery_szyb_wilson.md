Gallery Szyb Wilson is exactly the kind of place that makes Katowice interesting: contemporary art inside a former industrial structure. The old mine-shaft setting gives the exhibitions a rougher, more local charge than a neutral white cube.

Exhibitions vary, but the building and district are part of the visit. It is a strong choice if you want to see how Upper Silesia has been reusing its industrial past rather than simply preserving it behind glass.
