The Silesian Freedom and Solidarity Centre tells one of Katowice's most important modern stories: the suppression of the Wujek Mine strike in December 1981 after martial law was declared in Poland. It is a focused museum rather than a general city history collection.

The subject is heavy, but the site matters. Exhibits connect Silesia's mining culture, Solidarity activism, and the violence of the late communist state, giving visitors a sharper understanding of Katowice beyond architecture and post-industrial reinvention.
