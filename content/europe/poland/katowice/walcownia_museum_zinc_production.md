Walcownia Museum of Zinc Production preserves a large industrial rolling mill connected to Upper Silesia's zinc industry. Exhibits explain zinc processing and the region's mining and metallurgical history, with machinery displayed inside a raw industrial hall.

It is best for visitors interested in industrial heritage, especially as a contrast to Katowice's newer cultural quarter and mining-related museums.
