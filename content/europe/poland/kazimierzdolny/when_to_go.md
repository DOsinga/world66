---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

**Late May to early September** is high season, and the town is packed on weekends. Expect full restaurants, busy footpaths in the loess gorges, and a real summer-festival atmosphere on the market square. The annual **Festival of Folk Bands and Singers** in late June is the biggest event of the year — Polish folk musicians from across the country play on outdoor stages around town.

**September and early October** are perhaps the best time: warm enough for walking, autumn colours on the loess hills, and far fewer day-trippers. **Winter** is quiet and very cold but pretty when the river freezes and snow settles on the merchants' houses; some restaurants and galleries close on weekdays.

**April and early May** can be cool and muddy in the gorges; the paths in the landscape park are at their worst.
