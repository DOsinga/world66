Kadzielnia Amphitheatre makes clever use of one of Kielce's strangest urban landscapes: a former limestone quarry turned nature reserve. The stage sits among pale rock walls and greenery, so even a quick look gives you a sense of how dramatically the city has reused its industrial geology.

It is most memorable during concerts and summer events, but the setting is the real attraction. Combine it with a walk through Kadzielnia Reserve, where viewpoints and quarry paths make the amphitheatre feel less like a standalone venue and more like part of the landscape.
