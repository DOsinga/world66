---
title: "Getting There"
type: section
---

Kołobrzeg is on the Baltic coast and reasonably easy to reach from across Poland and central Europe.

**By train** — Kołobrzeg's main station is a 15-minute walk north of the old town. Direct trains run from **Warsaw** (around 8 hours via Poznań), **Poznań** (around 5 hours), and **Szczecin** (around 3 hours). In summer, seasonal direct services run from Kraków, Wrocław, and Berlin.

**By air** — The closest airport is **Szczecin–Goleniów (SZZ)**, around two hours by car, with budget flights to several European cities. **Gdańsk (GDN)** and **Berlin (BER)** are larger options around three to four hours away.

**By car** — The S6 expressway parallels the Baltic coast from Szczecin to Gdańsk. Coming from the south, the S11 connects Poznań to the coast at Koszalin, half an hour east of Kołobrzeg.

**By ferry** — In summer, the catamaran service from Kołobrzeg's marina to **Nexø on Bornholm (Denmark)** runs several times a week — a useful and atypical way of getting to or from Scandinavia.
