title: Archaeological Museum of Krakow
type: poi

Housed in a former Carmelite monastery that later served as a prison, the museum traces Lesser Poland from the Stone Age through the early medieval period. The Egyptian mummies and the ancient Slavic collection are the highlights. The walled monastery garden behind the building is one of the quietest corners in the centre and is free to enter.
