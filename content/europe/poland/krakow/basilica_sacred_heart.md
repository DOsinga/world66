A monumental Jesuit church on Kopernika Street, east of the Planty, designed by Franciszek Maczynski. Its tall tower is visible across the Grzegorzki district, and its interior blends Secession and early modern styles in a way unusual for the city's older Gothic and Baroque churches. It anchors the Jesuit complex that includes the Ignatianum academy next door.
