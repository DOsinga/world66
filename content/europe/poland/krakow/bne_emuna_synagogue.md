Bne Emuna Synagogue is one of Kazimierz's smaller Jewish heritage stops, easy to miss beside the district's better-known synagogues and cemeteries. The building began as a prayer house and now houses the Judaica Foundation's Centre for Jewish Culture.

It is worth a look while wandering Kazimierz, especially if you are interested in how the neighbourhood's religious buildings have been reused since the war. Check ahead for exhibitions or cultural events, as the building is not simply a museum with fixed displays.
