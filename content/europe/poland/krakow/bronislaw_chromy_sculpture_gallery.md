The Bronisław Chromy Sculpture Gallery shows the work of the Krakow sculptor whose public pieces include the Wawel dragon. The collection sits in Decius Park, with sculpture both outdoors and inside the gallery, making it a worthwhile detour for visitors interested in modern Polish sculpture.
