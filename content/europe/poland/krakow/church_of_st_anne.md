Krakow's finest Baroque church, completed in 1703 to a design by Tylman van Gameren, with stuccowork and frescoes by leading Italian artists of the day. Tucked on a narrow street near the Collegium Novum, its bright, harmonious interior is often overlooked by visitors who never leave the Main Square a short walk away.
