Krakow's first fully Baroque church, built for the Jesuits between 1597 and 1619, modelled on the Gesu in Rome. Its dome and broad facade dominate Grodzka Street, the medieval route between the Main Square and Wawel. The crypt holds the tomb of Piotr Skarga, the influential Jesuit preacher, and the acoustics make it a regular venue for chamber-music concerts.
