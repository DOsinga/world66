A branch of the Museum of Krakow on Ghetto Heroes' Square, occupying the actual pharmacy that operated within the ghetto. The reconstructed interior and the testimonies gathered here document daily life and the liquidation of the ghetto from the vantage point of a man who chose to stay. Small and intense, it pairs naturally with a visit to the square outside.
