One of Poland's oldest ethnographic collections, founded in 1902 and housed in the Renaissance town hall on Plac Wolnica. It documents folk costume, craft, ritual and everyday life across Lesser Poland and beyond. The annual competition of Krakow's elaborate Christmas cribs has close ties to the museum.
