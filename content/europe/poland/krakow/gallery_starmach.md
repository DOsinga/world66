Gallery Starmach occupies the former Zucker Synagogue and shows major Polish contemporary artists, including names such as Magdalena Abakanowicz, Tadeusz Kantor, and Jerzy Nowosielski. It is a good specialist stop for visitors already exploring Kazimierz and Podgórze.
