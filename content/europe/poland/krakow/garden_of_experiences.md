The Stanislaw Lem Garden of Experiences is an outdoor science park on Krakow's eastern side, with hands-on installations that demonstrate optics, acoustics, motion, and astronomy. It is aimed at children but works well for curious adults too, especially on a warm day when the open-air exhibits and spherical cinema make a change from the Old Town museums.
