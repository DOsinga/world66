The former Plac Zgody, the main square of the Krakow Ghetto in Podgorze and the assembly point for its deportations. The stark memorial of empty chairs, designed by Piotr Lewicki and Kazimierz Latak, turns the whole square into a monument. The Eagle Pharmacy stands on one corner and Schindler's Factory is a short walk east.
