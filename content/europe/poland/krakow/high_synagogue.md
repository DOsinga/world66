The High Synagogue is a Renaissance synagogue in Kazimierz, named because its prayer room is on the second floor. It is one of the district's historic Jewish sites and is open to the public, sometimes with photo exhibitions.

The building is more modest than Krakow's best-known synagogues, but its unusual layout makes it worth adding to a Kazimierz walk.
