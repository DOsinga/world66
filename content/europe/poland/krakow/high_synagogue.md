The High Synagogue takes its name from the upstairs prayer hall, an unusual arrangement in Poland and a reminder of how tightly built Kazimierz once was. It is less complete than the Old Synagogue, but the Renaissance shell and small exhibitions make it a worthwhile stop on a Jewish Krakow walk.
