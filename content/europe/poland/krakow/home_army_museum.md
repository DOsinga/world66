Set in a converted 19th-century Austrian military warehouse in Grzegorzki, this is the only museum in Poland devoted to the Home Army. Its large, modern displays cover the wartime resistance, the Warsaw Uprising and the persecution of veterans after 1945. It lies a short tram ride east of the Old Town.
