One of the largest of the historic synagogues of Kazimierz, on Kupa Street. Restored fragments of its painted decoration and its tall arched windows survive, and it has served at various times as a house of prayer and an exhibition space documenting the district's Jewish life. It sits within a short walk of the Old Synagogue and the Remuh.
