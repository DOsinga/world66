A legendary literary cafe on Florianska Street, its windowless interior — the name means Michalik's Cave — designed in full Art Nouveau style. It remains a working cafe and a small museum of Krakow's bohemian past; come for coffee and the famous cakes rather than a full meal, and to sit among the original fittings.
