A secular cultural and educational centre on Miodowa Street, hosting Hebrew classes, lectures, a Sunday school, holiday celebrations and a popular cafe. For visitors it offers a window onto contemporary, rather than commemorated, Jewish Krakow, and its volunteers run community meals and events open to all.
