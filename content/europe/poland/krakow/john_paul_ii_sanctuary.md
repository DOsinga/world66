The John Paul II Sanctuary is a modern pilgrimage church near Krakow's Divine Mercy Sanctuary. It was inaugurated in 2013 and includes relics connected with Pope John Paul II.

The sanctuary is mainly of interest to pilgrims and visitors following Krakow's modern Catholic heritage, but the scale of the complex makes it a deliberate stop rather than a passing chapel.
