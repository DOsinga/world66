The Podgorze district holds the remnants of the Krakow Ghetto, the holding place for the city's Jews before their deportation to Belzec and Plaszow. Beyond the fragments of the ghetto wall, the area takes in Ghetto Heroes' Square with its memorial of empty chairs and the Eagle Pharmacy, making the streets south of the river a coherent route through the history of the Holocaust in Krakow.
