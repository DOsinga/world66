Kraków Zoo sits in the wooded hills west of the city centre, making it feel more like a day in the forest than a quick urban attraction. The collection includes roughly 1,500 animals across hundreds of species, with elephants, pygmy hippos, Humboldt penguins, and Przewalski horses among the better-known residents.

It is most useful for families or for travellers spending extra time around Zwierzyniec and the western green belt.
