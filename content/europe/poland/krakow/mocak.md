MOCAK, Krakow's Museum of Contemporary Art, occupies renovated buildings from the former Schindler factory complex. It has permanent and temporary exhibitions of modern art, plus a strong art bookshop and cafe, and pairs naturally with a visit to Schindler's Factory Museum nearby.
