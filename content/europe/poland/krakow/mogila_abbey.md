A medieval Cistercian abbey within the Nowa Huta district, an island of ancient stone in a sea of 20th-century concrete. The Romanesque-Gothic church preserves Renaissance wall paintings and the famous Crucifix of Mogila. The contrast between the monastic complex and the surrounding communist-era estates makes it a rewarding stop on a Nowa Huta tour.
