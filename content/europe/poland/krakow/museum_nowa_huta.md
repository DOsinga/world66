A department of the Museum of Krakow devoted to the planned communist-era district. Through documents, design drawings and everyday objects it explains how the ideal workers' city was laid out around Plac Centralny, and how daily life unfolded there. It is the natural starting point for exploring Nowa Huta's Socialist Realist architecture.
