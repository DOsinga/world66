A museum devoted to the four decades of communist Poland, set in the planned workers' district of Nowa Huta. Its displays cover propaganda, the command economy, daily life under shortage, and the opposition movements that grew here. It anchors a tour of Nowa Huta's Socialist Realist architecture around Plac Centralny.
