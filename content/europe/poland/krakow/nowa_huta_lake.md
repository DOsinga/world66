Nowa Huta Lake is a small reservoir and green space near the centre of Nowa Huta. Locals come for walks, skating, sunbathing, and an easy break from the district's broad streets and socialist-realist blocks.

It is a modest stop, but useful if you are already exploring Nowa Huta and want to see how the neighbourhood relaxes away from its monumental squares and churches.
