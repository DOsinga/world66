Run by the Jagiellonian University's medical college, this museum on Florianska Street holds antique pharmacy fittings, ceramic and glass vessels, old laboratory equipment and a library of pharmacopoeias. The atmospheric room-by-room descent through a historic merchant's house is as much a draw as the exhibits.
