The Walery Rzewuski Museum of Photography occupies a striking renovated building north-east of the centre. Its rotating exhibitions draw on one of the largest photographic collections in Poland, covering both the technical history of the medium and the social history it recorded. A draw for anyone interested in photography or the visual record of old Krakow.
