An opulent Eclectic-style theatre and opera house on Plac Swietego Ducha, part of Krakow's UNESCO-listed historic centre. It stages drama and opera in repertory; even without a ticket the floodlit facade, crowned with a winged genius, is one of the finest sights of the northern Old Town.
