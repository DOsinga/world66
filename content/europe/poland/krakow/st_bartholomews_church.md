St. Bartholomew's Church stands opposite the Cistercian abbey in Mogiła, on the eastern side of Kraków. Built in the 15th century, it is one of Poland's older surviving wooden churches and makes a good pairing with a trip through Nowa Huta's socialist-era urban plan.
