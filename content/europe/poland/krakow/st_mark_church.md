St. Mark Church is a 13th-century church on the northern edge of Krakow's Old Town, founded under Duke Boleslaw the Chaste. Its quiet interior has a rococo pulpit and a striking sculpted Golgotha group on the apse wall, making it a worthwhile short stop away from the Main Market Square crowds.
