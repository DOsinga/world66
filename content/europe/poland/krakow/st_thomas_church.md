St Thomas Church is a small early Baroque church in Krakow's Old Town, built in 1618 for the Carmelites and later used by the nuns of the Holy Ghost hospital. It is a modest stop compared with Krakow's major churches, but worthwhile for visitors exploring the side streets around Stare Miasto.
