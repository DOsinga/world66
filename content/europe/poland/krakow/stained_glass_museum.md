A small specialist museum dedicated to the art of the stained-glass window, set in the historic Zelenski workshop on Aleja Krasinskiego. The collection includes original cartoons by Stanislaw Wyspianski and Jozef Mehoffer, the artists who led Krakow's turn-of-the-century revival of monumental stained glass for the city's churches.
