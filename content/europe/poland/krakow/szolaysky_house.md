Szolaysky House is a branch of the National Museum in Krakow, focused on Polish art and temporary exhibitions. Its best-known material relates to the Young Poland period, including works by Stanislaw Wyspianski. The museum is close to the Main Square, so it is easy to combine with the Old Town's bigger sights.
