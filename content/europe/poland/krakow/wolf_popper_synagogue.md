Wolf Popper Synagogue was once one of Kazimierz's grander synagogues, before being damaged during the Nazi occupation. The building now serves as a cultural centre, with workshops, lectures, film screenings, and other events. It is a worthwhile extra stop while exploring Kazimierz's Jewish heritage sites.
