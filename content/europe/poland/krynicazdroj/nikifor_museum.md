The Muzeum Nikifora is dedicated to Nikifor Krynicki (Epifaniusz Drowniak), the Lemko naive painter who spent most of his life in Krynica and is now regarded as one of the most important self-taught artists of 20th-century Poland. The museum occupies the former spa pavilion known as the Romanówka and holds an extensive collection of Nikifor's small, densely coloured watercolours — mostly townscapes, churches, and imaginary architectural fantasies.

Nikifor's work is immediately recognisable: small format, intricate architectural detail, a palette that shifts between delicate and vivid, and a recurring preoccupation with church buildings and railway stations. The museum tells his story well, including his difficult life as a Lemko minority member in interwar and communist Poland and his gradual discovery by the art world. A worthwhile stop even if you had not heard of him before arriving.
