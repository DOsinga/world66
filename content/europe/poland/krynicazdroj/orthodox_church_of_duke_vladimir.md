The Orthodox Church of Duke Vladimir is a worthwhile architectural stop in Krynica-Zdrój, where spa-town villas, mountain scenery, and Lemko heritage overlap. Its wooden form and Eastern Christian details give the townscape a different texture from the main promenade and resort buildings.

It is best visited as part of a walk through the spa town rather than as a stand-alone sight. For travelers interested in the religious and ethnic layers of the Carpathian region, the church adds useful context.
