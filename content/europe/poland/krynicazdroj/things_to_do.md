---
title: Things to Do
type: section
---

Krynica-Zdrój divides cleanly into two seasons. In summer, the mineral springs, the spa promenades, and the hiking trails in the surrounding Beskid Sądecki mountains are the draws. In winter, the Jaworzyna Krynicka gondola opens access to skiing and snowshoeing terrain. The spa promenade around Park Słotwiński is the social centre in both seasons.
