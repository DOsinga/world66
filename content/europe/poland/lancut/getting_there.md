---
title: "Getting There"
type: section
---

Łańcut is in southeastern Poland, in the Subcarpathian region between Rzeszów and the Ukrainian border. It sits on the main east–west railway line and is easily reached from either direction.

**By train:** Łańcut has its own train station with regular services to and from Rzeszów (about 20 minutes) and Kraków (around 2 hours). Direct trains also connect to Przemyśl to the east. The station is about 1.5 km from the castle — walkable in 20 minutes or easily covered by taxi.

**By car:** From Rzeszów, take the E40 road east; Łańcut is about 17 km. From Kraków, follow the A4 motorway east and exit towards Rzeszów; total journey around 130 km. Ample parking is available near the castle entrance.

**Day trip from Kraków:** Łańcut is commonly visited as a day trip from Kraków, with morning trains allowing several hours at the castle before the return journey. It also combines well with a stop in Rzeszów.
