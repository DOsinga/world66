Lancut's Jewish cemetery dates from the 17th century and is one of the town's quieter historic sites. Its most important grave is that of Naftali Tzvi Horowitz, a Hasidic tzadik whose tomb still draws Jewish visitors with a special interest in the old Galician communities.
