Lancut's Parish Cemetery dates from 1862 and includes graves and memorials connected with soldiers who died in the First and Second World Wars. It is a quiet historical stop away from the castle and synagogue.

Visit respectfully and keep expectations modest: the interest is local memory rather than grand funerary architecture.
