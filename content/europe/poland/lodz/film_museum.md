Film Museum is one of Łódź's most fitting museums, reflecting a city long associated with Polish cinema and the national film school. Housed in a historic palace, it covers film technology, set design, posters, animation, and the culture around moving images.

It is a strong stop even for travelers who are not Polish-film specialists because Łódź's identity is so bound up with industry, images, and reinvention. Pair it with Piotrkowska Street or the city's film-school landmarks for a richer route.
