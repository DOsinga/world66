St. Joseph's Church is the oldest surviving building in Lodz and the only major structure from the city's pre-industrial period. Its modest scale contrasts sharply with the nearby mills, palaces, and Manufaktura complex, which makes it a useful stop for understanding the older town that existed before the textile boom.
