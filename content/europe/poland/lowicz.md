Łowicz is the folk capital of Poland. This small town between Warsaw and Łódź is famous for its colourful [Corpus Christi processions](/europe/poland/lowicz/corpus_christi), where locals dress in elaborate traditional costumes and carpet the streets with flower petals. The tradition has been alive for centuries, and the costumes — bold stripes, intricate embroidery — are among the most recognisable in Polish folk culture.

Outside the festival season, the town is a quiet market centre with a pleasant Baroque [collegiate church](/europe/poland/lowicz/lowicz_cathedral), the [Museum of Łowicz](/europe/poland/lowicz/museum_lowicz) with one of the best folk art collections in the country, and a relaxed rynek (market square) good for a coffee stop. If you are driving between Warsaw and Łódź, Łowicz is worth a detour; if you can time your visit to Corpus Christi (late May or June), it is unmissable. The [Arkadia Romantic Park](/europe/poland/lowicz/arkadia_park), 5 kilometres east, is one of the finest 18th-century landscape gardens in Poland and easily combined with a Łowicz visit.
