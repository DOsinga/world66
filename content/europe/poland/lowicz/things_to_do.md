title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Łowicz is primarily a folk culture destination. The Museum of Łowicz has one of the finest regional folk art collections in Poland, and if you can time your visit to the Corpus Christi procession (late May or June), you will see one of the most colourful and authentic traditional festivals in the country. Outside festival season, the Baroque collegiate church and the market square reward a brief stop.
