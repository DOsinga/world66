The Chachmei Lublin Yeshiva on Lubartowska Street was built in 1930 as the grandest Jewish educational institution in Europe, designed to house 400 students and a library of 22,000 volumes. Its founder, Rabbi Meir Shapiro, also invented the concept of Daf Yomi — a daily page of Talmud study followed by Jews worldwide, so that all study the same page on the same day. Millions of Jews follow the programme today.

The building survived the war and the communist period and has been restored and reopened as a functioning synagogue, cultural centre, and small hotel. The restoration is excellent. Visiting includes the synagogue, exhibition rooms about the yeshiva's history and Rabbi Shapiro's life, and a glimpse of what Jewish Lublin was building toward before it was destroyed.
