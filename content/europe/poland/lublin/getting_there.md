---
title: Getting There
type: section
---

Lublin has a small regional airport (LUZ) with limited connections; most visitors fly into Warsaw Chopin Airport and take the bus. PolskiBus and FlixBus run frequent services from Warsaw (about 2.5 hours); PKS Lublin operates from Warsaw's main bus station. By train from Warsaw Centralna the journey takes about 2 hours 15 minutes on direct services. Lublin is 170 kilometres from Warsaw and sits on the route between Warsaw and Ukraine; the city has bus connections to Kyiv, Lviv, and other Ukrainian cities.
