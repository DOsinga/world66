Our Lady the Victorious Church is a Gothic-Renaissance church tied to King Władysław Jagiełło's victory over the Teutonic Knights at Grunwald in 1410. The king donated the church after the battle, giving it a place in both Lublin's religious landscape and Poland's medieval memory.

It is worth a stop for visitors tracing Lublin's older churches beyond the main Old Town circuit.
