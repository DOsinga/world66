Lublin's Saxon Garden is the city's classic green pause, laid out in the 19th century along Aleje Raclawickie. Its paths, old trees, and English-style plan give travellers a breather between the Old Town, university quarter, and museum stops.

This is not Lublin's grandest sight, but it is a good place to understand the city at walking pace. Use it as a quiet cut-through or a short rest rather than a destination that demands a long detour.
