Dinopark is a family attraction in Malbork with life-size dinosaur models, a small fossil museum, and a 5D cinema. It is aimed squarely at children and makes most sense as a lighter stop after the town's castle-heavy sightseeing.

Food and drink are available inside, so families can treat it as a half-day break rather than a quick look-in.
