Lake Niegocin is one of the larger lakes in the Great Mazurian Lakes chain, connected to the system via Giżycko and the Luczańska Canal. The lake is about 26 square kilometres with a maximum depth of 30 metres — deep enough for good sailing even in light wind. The shores include the Giżycko beach on the northern edge and various smaller bathing spots accessible by boat.

The lake forms the heart of the sailing routes east and west from Giżycko, connecting via the rotating bridge and canal to Lake Kisajno in the east and through a lock system toward the southern lakes. The annual sailing regatta held here in summer attracts hundreds of boats from across Poland and a scattering of foreign competitors. Outside regatta season the lake is busy but not overcrowded.
