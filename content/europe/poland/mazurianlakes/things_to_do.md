---
title: Things to Do
type: section
---

Sailing and kayaking are the primary activities. The [Krutynia River](/europe/poland/mazurianlakes/krutynia_river) offers the classic multi-day kayak route; [Giżycko](/europe/poland/mazurianlakes/gizycko) and [Mikołajki](/europe/poland/mazurianlakes/mikolajki) are the sailing centres. [Wolf's Lair](/europe/poland/mazurianlakes/wolfs_lair) is the most visited historical site; [Łuknajno Reserve](/europe/poland/mazurianlakes/luknajno_reserve) is the best wildlife stop.
