---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

The Masurian summer is short and intense. June through August is the tourist season, when the lakes are warm enough for swimming, the kayak and sailing outfitters are open, and the campsites fill with Polish holidaymakers. July and August are the peak — school holidays bring families from Warsaw, Gdańsk, and Wrocław, and Mragowo's lakeside facilities are at their busiest. August also brings Piknik Country, which doubles the town's population for a weekend and requires booking months in advance.

Outside summer, the region has a different but still worthwhile character. September sees fewer crowds and a warm enough lake for swimming on good days; the forests turn in October with beech and birch colour that can be spectacular. Winter is cold — temperatures regularly below -10°C, lakes sometimes frozen hard enough to walk on — and most tourist infrastructure closes. Spring arrives slowly; by May the lakes are navigable and the kayak season begins. The brief shoulder periods of late May and early September are genuinely the best compromise between good weather, accessible facilities, and manageable crowds.
