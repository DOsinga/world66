Ogrodzieniec is a small town in southern Poland, but the reason to come is just east of it at Podzamcze: the great ruined [Ogrodzieniec Castle](/europe/poland/ogrodzieniec/ogrodzieniec_castle), spread across pale limestone crags on the Eagle Nests Trail. The castle is one of the strongest sights in the Polish Jura, a broken Gothic-Renaissance fortress that looks as if the rock has grown into the walls.

The setting matters as much as the masonry. Around the castle, the Jura rises in dry, grassy hills and strange white outcrops, with walking paths, climbing rocks and views across the upland. [Góra Birów](/europe/poland/ogrodzieniec/gora_birow), a neighbouring limestone hill with a reconstructed wooden stronghold, adds a rougher, older layer to the visit.

Ogrodzieniec is not a polished city break. It is a very good castle-and-landscape day from Katowice, Częstochowa or Kraków, and a natural stop if you are following the Trail of the Eagles' Nests.
