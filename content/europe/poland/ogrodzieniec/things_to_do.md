---
title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Ogrodzieniec is built around one big reason to visit: the castle at Podzamcze and the Jurassic limestone landscape around it. Give the castle enough time to climb the towers and wander the courtyards, then walk out to Góra Birów or along a marked section of the Eagle Nests Trail. Climbers use the outcrops, but even a short walk gives you the mix of ruins, pale rock and open upland that makes this corner of Poland memorable.
