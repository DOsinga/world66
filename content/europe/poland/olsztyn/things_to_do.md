---
title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Olsztyn's old town is small enough to see in a morning, but the city's pleasure is broader than its sights — the eleven lakes within the city limits, the forests on the edge of the centre, and the slow rhythm of life in the regional capital all reward a longer stay. The Gothic castle and the Copernicus connection are the standard headliners; everything else is bonus.
