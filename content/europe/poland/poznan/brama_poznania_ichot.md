Brama Poznania ICHOT is an interactive heritage centre beside Cathedral Island, focused on the early history of Poznan and the beginnings of the Polish state.

The visit uses audioguides and multimedia exhibits rather than a traditional object-heavy museum approach. The rooftop view over Ostrow Tumski is a good bonus before or after visiting Poznan Cathedral.
