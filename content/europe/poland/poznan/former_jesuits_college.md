The former Jesuits' College stands beside the old market area and now serves as Poznan's city office. Visitors can usually step inside to see the public halls and corridors, where the building's history still shows through.

Napoleon stayed here during his 1812 march toward Moscow, and the building later hosted concerts by Frederic Chopin. The goat monument in front links it back to one of Poznan's best-known civic symbols.
