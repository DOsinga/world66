The Holy Blood of Lord Jesus Church is a small historic church near Poznan's Old Market Square. Its history is tied to a local legend about the desecration of the host, and the building remains part of the city's dense old-town religious landscape.

It is not a headline sight on its own, but it is close enough to the main square to include while exploring Poznan's lanes and churches.
