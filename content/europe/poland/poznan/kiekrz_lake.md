Kiekrz Lake is a large open lake on Poznan's northwestern edge. It sits well away from the city center, but still within the city limits, and is best known for sailing, windsurfing, fishing, and longer waterside walks.

Do not expect a polished urban swimming beach. The appeal is the open water and the space to get out of central Poznan for a few hours.
