The Literary Museum of Henryk Sienkiewicz is devoted to the Nobel Prize-winning Polish novelist best known for Quo Vadis. Its collections cover his life, writing, travels, and public reputation, with manuscripts, editions, portraits, and personal material.

It is a focused museum, best for readers and visitors who want more than the headline sights around Poznań's Old Market Square.
