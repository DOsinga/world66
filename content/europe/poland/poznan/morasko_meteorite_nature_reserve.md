Morasko Meteorite Nature Reserve protects a cluster of small meteorite craters and woodland on the northern edge of Poznań. The craters are subtle rather than spectacular, but the combination of geology, forest and walking trails makes the reserve distinctive.

It is best for travelers who enjoy natural history or want a green excursion from the city. Good shoes help after wet weather, and the visit is more satisfying if you read a little about the meteorite fall before walking the trail.
