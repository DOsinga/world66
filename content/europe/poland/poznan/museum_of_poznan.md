The Museum of Poznan is housed in the city's Renaissance Town Hall on the Old Market Square. The setting is part of the appeal: the interiors survived the Second World War better than much of the surrounding old town.

The exhibits cover Poznan's history from the 10th century onward, using documents, objects and the building itself to explain the city's civic story.
