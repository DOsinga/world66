Park Railway Maltanka is a small narrow-gauge railway running along the northern shore of Lake Malta. It links the Śródka end of the lake with the New Zoo, making it both a practical family shortcut and a bit of old-fashioned railway fun.

The ride is short, seasonal, and deliberately light-hearted. It works best as part of a Lake Malta outing: walk or cycle one way, take the little train the other, and leave time for the zoo or lakeside paths if the weather is kind.
