*Europe > Poland > Przemyśl*

Przemyśl sits in south-eastern [Poland](/europe/poland), close to the Ukrainian border, in a bend of the San River. It has the layered look of a frontier city: Polish, Ruthenian, Jewish, Austrian, and military histories all left marks in the churches, cemeteries, steep streets, and ruined forts around the hills.

The old centre is compact and handsome, with a sloping market square, Baroque and Greek Catholic churches, and [Przemyśl Castle](/europe/poland/przemysl/przemysl_castle) above the town. The [Museum of Bells and Pipes](/europe/poland/przemysl/museum_of_bells_and_pipes) is a very Przemyśl stop, set in the old clock tower and tied to local crafts that still shape the city's reputation.

The bigger story is the [Przemyśl Fortress](/europe/poland/przemysl/przemysl_fortress), one of Europe's largest 19th-century defensive systems. Its forts are scattered well beyond the centre, so you need a bike, car, or a deliberate walking plan, but even one ruin gives a strong sense of why this borderland mattered so much in the First World War.

Poland remains under normal travel precautions, but Przemyśl's border position is practical context, not a reason to drift onward blindly. Treat it as a Polish city with active cross-border rail traffic, and check current advice before any onward travel into Ukraine.
