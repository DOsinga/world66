Kopiec Tatarski is the simplest way to get a wide view of Przemyśl. The mound stands on a hill south of the old town, wrapped in local legend and useful for understanding the city's position between river, hills, and border routes.

It is a good late-day walk if the weather is clear. The sight itself is small; the panorama is the reason to go.
