The Museum of Bells and Pipes is exactly the kind of local museum that makes Przemyśl more than a pretty old town. It occupies the late Baroque clock tower and explains two trades closely associated with the city: bell founding and pipe making.

The collection is modest, but the setting is memorable. Climb the tower if it is open; the view back over the old centre is part of the visit.
