---
title: When to Go
type: section
---

Pszczyna is worth visiting year-round, but spring and early autumn are the best times. April and May bring colour to the palace park, and the European bison are active. September and October are pleasant for walking with cooler temperatures and good light. Summer weekends can be crowded with day-trippers from Katowice. Winter visits are quieter and the castle is still open, though the park is less appealing in bare-tree season.
