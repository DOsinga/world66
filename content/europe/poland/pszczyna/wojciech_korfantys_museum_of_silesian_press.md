Wojciech Korfanty's Museum of Silesian Press is a small specialist museum on the history of printing and newspapers in Silesia. It is named for the Silesian politician and publisher Wojciech Korfanty.

It is a niche stop, but a useful one if you are interested in the region's political identity and the role newspapers played in shaping it.
