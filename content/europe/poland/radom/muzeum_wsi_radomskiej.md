Muzeum Wsi Radomskiej is Radom's best attraction, and the one most likely to justify the detour. The open-air museum gathers timber houses, barns, windmills, chapels, and rural workshops from the Radom region, giving shape to the countryside that fed the city around it.

Allow more time than you expect. This is a walking museum, not a room of display cases, and it is strongest when you slow down enough to notice fences, thresholds, tools, and the practical intelligence built into rural architecture.
