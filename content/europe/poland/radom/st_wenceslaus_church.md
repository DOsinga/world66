St Wenceslaus Church is one of Radom's oldest surviving buildings and a useful stop if you are tracing the city's medieval core. It stands away from the busier nineteenth- and twentieth-century streets, in the older part of town where Radom's early shape is easier to imagine.

The church is modest rather than spectacular. Its value is age, continuity, and setting: a reminder that beneath Radom's industrial reputation there is a much older town.
