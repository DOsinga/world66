---
title: "Getting Around"
type: section
---

The Old Town, the castle, the synagogues, and the train station are all within fifteen minutes' walk of one another. For everything in the centre, walking is the simplest option.

The city operates a network of MPK buses with cheap, well-signposted lines covering the outer districts and the airport. Tickets are bought in advance from kiosks or by phone app. Taxis are inexpensive — a centre-to-airport ride is around 50 złoty. The pedestrianised Underground Tourist Route bridge connects the Market Square area to the south bank of the Wisłok for the walk to Olszynki Park.
