The Museum of Bedtime Cartoons is a small Rzeszów museum devoted to Polish children's animated films and the bedtime cartoons known as dobranocki. Its collection focuses on puppets, drawings, memorabilia, and characters familiar to generations of Polish viewers.

It is especially worthwhile for families, animation fans, and visitors curious about everyday popular culture in Poland.
