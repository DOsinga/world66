The Dominican Church and Convent of St. James is one of Sandomierz’s great historic buildings, prized for its Romanesque brickwork and long Dominican presence. Its warm masonry and restrained forms feel very different from later Baroque churches in the region.

It belongs near the top of a Sandomierz walk, alongside the castle, cathedral, and old town viewpoints. The church is substantial enough for architecture lovers, but also quiet enough to feel like a working sacred space rather than a staged attraction.
