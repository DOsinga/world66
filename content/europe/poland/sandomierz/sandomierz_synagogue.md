The Sandomierz Synagogue is a 17th-century building on Żydowska Street (Jewish Street), now functioning as a cultural and exhibition space. The building retains its basic structure from the Baroque period, though much of the original interior was lost during the war.

The synagogue is a quiet and sombre place — particularly meaningful in a town that once had a substantial Jewish quarter and lost it entirely to the Holocaust. The exhibitions inside cover the history of the Jewish community in Sandomierz and the wider region. It is worth a visit both for its architecture and for understanding the town's pre-war character.
