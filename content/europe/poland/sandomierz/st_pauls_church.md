St. Paul's Church stands just outside the densest part of old Sandomierz, close to the gullies and paths that give the town its unusual landscape. Its brick and plaster forms are quieter than the cathedral or castle, but they add another layer to the town's religious architecture.

It is best visited as part of a walk beyond the main square, especially if you are exploring Queen Jadwiga's Ravine or the old approaches into town. The church rewards travellers who enjoy seeing how Sandomierz spreads beyond its postcard core.
