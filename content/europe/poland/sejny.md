Sejny sits in Poland's far northeast, close to Lithuania and not far from the lakes and forests of Suwalszczyzna. It is a small town, but not an ordinary one. Its best reason to stop is the feeling of a borderland: Polish, Lithuanian, Jewish, and Dominican histories all left marks within a few streets.

The clearest walk starts at the [Dominican monastery and basilica](/europe/poland/sejny/dominican_monastery_and_basilica), whose late Renaissance church still dominates the centre. A few minutes away, the [White Synagogue](/europe/poland/sejny/white_synagogue) has become a cultural centre run by the Borderland Foundation, turning a difficult Jewish absence into one of the town's most thoughtful spaces.

Sejny works best as a slow half day while crossing between Suwalki, Wigry, and Lithuania. If you have time, continue out to [Krasnogruda Manor](/europe/poland/sejny/krasnogruda_manor), the Czeslaw Milosz family estate now used for dialogue, literature, and exhibitions near the border.
