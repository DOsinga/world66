---
title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Siedlce does not have a major tourist circuit, but the historic district around the Oginski Palace and the cathedral is compact and pleasant to walk. The [Siedlce Regional Museum](/europe/poland/siedlce/siedlce_regional_museum) is the most informative stop, particularly for the documentation of the town's Jewish history. The [cathedral](/europe/poland/siedlce/cathedral_of_the_immaculate_conception) is worth a look inside, and the [Oginski Palace](/europe/poland/siedlce/oginski_palace) grounds make a quiet place to pause between the train station and the town center.
