The Ecological Education Centre Egzotarium is Sosnowiec's rebuilt botanical and zoological garden. The free area has a cafe and shop, while the ticketed section focuses on tropical plants, animals, and hands-on ecological displays.

It is a modest but useful stop for families, especially on a wet day or as a change of pace from the city's industrial heritage sights.
