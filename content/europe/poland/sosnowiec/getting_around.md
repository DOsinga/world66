---
title: Getting Around
type: section
---

Sosnowiec is well served by trams and buses, which integrate with the wider Silesian metropolitan transport network. The same ticket works across Sosnowiec, [Katowice](/europe/poland/katowice), Będzin, and surrounding towns. Most of the city's sights are reachable on foot or by tram from the central area.

Taxis are inexpensive by Western European standards. The centre — including both the castle and the museum — is compact enough to cover comfortably on foot.
