---
title: Getting There
type: section
---

Sosnowiec is well connected by rail and sits directly within the dense urban network of Upper Silesia. The nearest international airport is [Katowice](/europe/poland/katowice) Airport (Pyrzowice), about 35 km north.

Sosnowiec has two main train stations: Sosnowiec Główny and Sosnowiec Centralna. Fast intercity trains connect to Warsaw (around 2.5 hours), Kraków (about 1 hour), and Wrocław. Regional trains run constantly to Katowice, Będzin, Dąbrowa Górnicza, and other nearby Silesian towns — the region functions as a single interconnected urban area with frequent services.

By road, Sosnowiec sits on the A4 motorway corridor that runs from the German border east through Katowice toward Kraków.
