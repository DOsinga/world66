Sosnowiec-Bory Peat Bog is a protected Natura 2000 area on the eastern side of Sosnowiec. It is best approached as a nature walk rather than a developed attraction.

Go for the wetland landscape, quiet paths, and a look at a different side of the city beyond its industrial and urban sights.
