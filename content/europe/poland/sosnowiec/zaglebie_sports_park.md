The Zagłębie Sports Park is a modern stadium and sports complex in the Środula district, home to Zagłębie Sosnowiec, the city's football club. The stadium holds around 10,000 and has been redeveloped in recent years into a tidy, functional ground that punches above its weight compared to the club's current standing in the Polish football pyramid.

If you happen to be in town on a match day, going to see Zagłębie is an easy and authentic way to spend an afternoon. The surrounding sports park includes tennis courts and other facilities, making it a genuine community sports hub rather than just a stadium.
