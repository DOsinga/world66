Cmentarz Centralny is one of Europe's largest cemeteries, laid out as a broad park of avenues, trees, chapels, monuments, and memorial spaces. In Szczecin it functions not only as a burial ground but also as a major green landscape and a record of the city's German and Polish layers.

It is worth treating as a walk rather than a quick detour. The scale is part of the experience, and the cemetery's memorials help explain how Szczecin remembers war, displacement, civic pride, and ordinary family histories in the same landscape.
