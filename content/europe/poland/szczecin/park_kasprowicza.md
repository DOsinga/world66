Park Kasprowicza is one of Szczecin's major green spaces, stretching around Rusalka Lake and into wooded paths north of the centre. It gives the city an easy outdoor pause between architectural and waterfront sights.

Use it for a walk, a picnic, or a route toward Jasne Blonia and the surrounding cultural venues. It is especially pleasant in warm weather, when the lake and shade make it feel like a real city retreat.
