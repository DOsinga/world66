---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

Szczecin has a temperate maritime climate with cold, grey winters and warm summers. The best time to visit is May through September, when the waterfront comes alive, outdoor terraces open, and boat trips on the Oder are running. July and August are the warmest months, averaging around 22°C.

Winter is not without appeal — the city feels authentically un-touristed in January, Christmas markets appear in December, and the indoor programme at the Philharmonic is at its strongest through the autumn and winter season. But prepare for cold and limited daylight.

Spring (April–May) is particularly pleasant: the parks and gardens in the city's green belt are in bloom, the tourist crowds have not arrived, and the city's many outdoor cafes are just opening for the season.
