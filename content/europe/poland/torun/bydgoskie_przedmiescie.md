Bydgoskie Przedmiescie is a late-19th- and early-20th-century district west of Torun's old town. Its leafy streets have timber-framed houses, villas, a city park, and a zoobotanical garden, making it a good walk for visitors interested in the city's architecture beyond the medieval centre.
