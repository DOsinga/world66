Church of the Assumption of the Blessed Virgin Mary is a Gothic Franciscan church from the 14th century. Its tall nave, lack of a conventional steeple, and chancel turrets give it a different silhouette from Torun's other old-town churches.

It is an easy stop while walking the medieval centre. The church is most rewarding if you are paying attention to Torun's brick Gothic architecture rather than just moving between the best-known Copernicus sights.
