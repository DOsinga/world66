Dwór Artusa stands on the site of Toruń's medieval Artus Court, rebuilt in monumental 19th-century style on the Old Town square. Today it works as a cultural centre, but the reason to stop is the building itself and its place in the civic life of the old merchant city.

It is an easy addition while walking the historic centre, close to the main square's best-known sights.
