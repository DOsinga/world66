Toruń's Ethnographic Museum broadens the city beyond Copernicus, brick Gothic, and gingerbread. Its collections cover rural life, folk architecture, interiors, costumes, tools, and seasonal traditions from the surrounding region.

It is a good second-day museum, especially if the old town has already done its work. The open-air and domestic elements make regional history easier to picture, and the museum helps connect Toruń to the countryside that fed and surrounded it.
