Kepa Bazarowa viewpoint is the classic place to see Torun's old town skyline from across the Vistula. From the left bank, the cathedral towers, old walls, and riverside buildings line up in one of the city's best panoramas.

The viewpoint is on Kepa Bazarowa island and can be reached on foot or by local bus from the area around Plac Rapackiego. Late afternoon and early evening give the best light for photos.
