---
title: Things to Do
type: section
---

Toruń rewards a full day's walking. The medieval old town is compact enough to cover on foot, and almost every street in the UNESCO-listed core contains something worth stopping for. The [Teutonic Knights Castle](/europe/poland/torun/teutonic_castle) ruins on the Vistula bank are a good starting point. The [Copernicus House](/europe/poland/torun/copernicus_house) on Kopernika Street is the birthplace museum for the astronomer — small but well done. The [Old Town Hall](/europe/poland/torun/old_town_hall) houses the District Museum, which covers the city's history from its Teutonic Order origins to the present.

The [Living Museum of Gingerbread](/europe/poland/torun/living_museum_of_gingerbread) runs hands-on baking sessions where visitors make their own pierniki — worth booking ahead in summer. The [Leaning Tower](/europe/poland/torun/leaning_tower) near the city walls is a quirky detour. The [Toruń Cathedral](/europe/poland/torun/torun_cathedral) contains the Tuba Dei, the largest medieval bell in Poland, and the baptismal font in which Copernicus was christened.
