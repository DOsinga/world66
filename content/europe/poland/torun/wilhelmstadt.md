Przedmieście Świętej Katarzyny, also known by its former German name Wilhelmstadt, is the late-19th- and early-20th-century district east of Toruń's Old Town. Its streets mix residential blocks with former military architecture, giving the area a different texture from the medieval core.

The main landmark is St. Catherine's Church, whose tower rises above the district and remains one of Toruń's tallest structures. It is best treated as a short architectural walk after the Old Town rather than a stand-alone museum stop.
