---
title: Getting There
type: section
---

Gdansk Lech Walesa Airport receives direct flights from most major European cities and has good connections onward from Warsaw. The journey into town by train takes about 23 minutes; taxis and rideshares are also available.

Trains from Warsaw take around three hours on the fastest express services. Gdansk is the main hub, with frequent connections to the rest of Poland.

Within Trojmiasto, the SKM commuter rail runs along the coast connecting Gdansk, Gdynia, and Sopot every few minutes during the day. A single ticket covers unlimited transfers within the zone. The train is by far the easiest way to move between the three cities.
