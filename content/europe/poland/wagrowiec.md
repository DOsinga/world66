Wągrowiec is a small town in Greater Poland, northeast of [Poznan](/europe/poland/poznan), where the Wełna River meets Durowskie Lake. It is not one of Poland's big-name stops, but it has enough odd and specific sights for a slow day: Cistercian remains, lakeside paths, and a rare river bifurcation where two waterways cross.

The town's story is tied to the Cistercians, who shaped the surrounding area from the Middle Ages. The former monastery church and museum give Wągrowiec its historic core, while the river crossing and lake make the town feel greener and less ordinary than its size suggests.

Wągrowiec is best for travellers already exploring Greater Poland, or anyone looking for a quiet day trip from Poznan that is not another large city.
