The Regional Museum is the best indoor stop in Wągrowiec. Its collections cover the town, the Cistercian presence, and the surrounding Pałuki region, giving useful background before or after a walk through the old monastery area.

It is a small-town museum, so expect local depth rather than spectacle. That suits Wągrowiec well: the point is to understand why this quiet place has more history than it first appears.
