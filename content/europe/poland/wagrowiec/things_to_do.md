title: Things to Do
type: section
---

Wągrowiec's strongest sights are compact enough for a walking day. Start with the [former Cistercian monastery](/europe/poland/wagrowiec/former_cistercian_monastery), visit the [Regional Museum](/europe/poland/wagrowiec/regional_museum), then follow the river toward the [Wełna and Nielba crossing](/europe/poland/wagrowiec/welna_and_nielba_crossing). Durowskie Lake is the place to slow down at the end of the visit.
