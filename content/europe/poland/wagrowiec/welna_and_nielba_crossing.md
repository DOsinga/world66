The crossing of the Wełna and Nielba rivers is Wągrowiec's oddest sight. The two waterways meet at an angle, and because of the current and channel shape they appear to cross without fully mixing at once.

It is a quick stop, but a memorable one if you like small geographical curiosities. Walk there from the centre rather than driving straight to it; the river path gives the sight a better setting.
