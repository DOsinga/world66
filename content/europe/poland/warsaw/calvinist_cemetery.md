Warsaw's Calvinist Cemetery is a historic burial ground with Halperts' Chapel and the graves of notable Polish artists, industrialists, and public figures, including Wojciech Gerson, Emil Wedel, Tadeusz Stefan Jaroszewski, and Szymon Bogumil Zug. It is a quiet stop for visitors interested in Warsaw's religious and civic history.
