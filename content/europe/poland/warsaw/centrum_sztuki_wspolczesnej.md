Centrum Sztuki Wspolczesnej is Warsaw's major contemporary art centre, housed in the historic Ujazdowski Castle between Park Ujazdowski and Lazienki Park. The setting and exhibitions make it a strong stop for visitors interested in modern Polish and international art.
