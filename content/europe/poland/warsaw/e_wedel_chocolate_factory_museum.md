The E. Wedel Chocolate Factory Museum tells the story of one of Warsaw's best-known confectionery brands and the Wedel family behind it. The focus is on chocolate-making, packaging, advertising, and the growth of a local business that became part of Polish food culture.

It is a good fit for families or anyone interested in everyday industrial history, especially when combined with other stops in Praga.
