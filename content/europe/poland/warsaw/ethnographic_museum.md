Warsaw's Ethnographic Museum uses objects, photographs, and temporary exhibitions to explore the everyday culture of different societies. It is a useful counterpoint to the city's military, royal, and art museums, with more attention to ritual, craft, dress, and domestic life.

The museum is housed in a 19th-century former bank building, so the architecture is part of the visit. It works best for travellers who want cultural context rather than another straight history display.
