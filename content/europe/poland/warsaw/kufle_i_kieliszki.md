Kufle i Kieliszki (Mugs and Glasses) is the best place in central Warsaw to drink Polish craft beer. The bar has around 20 taps rotating through beers from Polish microbreweries, plus an extensive bottle selection. The staff know their beer and the space is relaxed — dark wood, no fuss, reasonable prices. It's on Nowy Świat, which makes it a natural stop on any walk along the Royal Route.

Polish craft beer has developed rapidly since around 2010. The best Polish breweries to look for on the taps: Browar Artezan, Pinta, Ale Browar, and Browarny — producing IPAs, stouts, sours, and excellent Baltic porters.
