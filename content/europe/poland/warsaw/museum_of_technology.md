Warsaw's Museum of Technology occupies part of the Palace of Culture and Science and focuses on machines, vehicles, industrial models, and older technical collections. It is a deliberately old-school museum, but the setting and range of engineering exhibits make it a worthwhile stop for visitors interested in technology.
