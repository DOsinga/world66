Muzeum Techniki is an old-school technology museum inside the Palace of Culture and Science, with planes, cars, industrial models, and machinery laid out in a proudly hands-off style. It feels dated in places, but that is part of the charm; the collection is strongest for visitors who like engineering, industry, and socialist-era museum design.
