Polonia Theatre is one of central Warsaw's better-known private stages, closely associated with actor and director Krystyna Janda. Its productions tend to draw attention well beyond regular theatre circles, so it is worth checking the programme if you can follow Polish or are interested in the city's contemporary stage scene.
