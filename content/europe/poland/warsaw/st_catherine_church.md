St Catherine Church is tied to Warsaw's oldest continuously existing parish, founded in 1238, though the present church is mostly 19th century. Its hilltop position, clean Gothic Revival form, and quiet churchyard make it a worthwhile stop in southern Warsaw.

The churchyard also holds the Monument to the Victims of Communist Terror of 1944-1956.
