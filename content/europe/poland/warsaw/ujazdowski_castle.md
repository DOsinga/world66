Ujazdowski Castle began as a royal residence on the edge of Warsaw's parkland and now houses the Centre for Contemporary Art. Come for changing exhibitions, then use the castle as a stepping stone between Lazienki Park, the embassies, and the quieter southern end of the city centre.
