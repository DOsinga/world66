The Graduation Tower in Wieliczka uses brine from the salt works to create a salty mist around its wooden walls. Visitors walk around the structure for the mineral-rich air, and the upper level gives a view over the town and the salt mine area.

It is a secondary stop after the Wieliczka Salt Mine, but useful if you want a short outdoor break before or after the underground tour.
