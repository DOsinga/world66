This open-air museum gathers rural buildings from western Greater Poland on the edge of Wolsztyn Lake. Cottages, workshops, farm structures, and everyday interiors give the town a regional context that the steam depot cannot provide.

It is an easy add-on to a lake walk. The value is in the small details: tools, room layouts, timber construction, and the way village life looked before industrial Poland arrived.
