A tea and dessert café in the old town, with a long selection of single-estate teas and a renowned cake counter (chocolate fondant, layered torts, fruit pies). The interior is small and slightly theatrical — antique chairs, painted ceiling, mismatched china.

Best as an afternoon stop after museum visits. Cash only, often busy.
