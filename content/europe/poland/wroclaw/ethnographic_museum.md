Wroclaw's Ethnographic Museum fills the former summer palace of the city's bishops with folk art and everyday objects from Lower Silesia. It is a quieter stop than the big central museums, but useful if you want to understand the rural traditions, crafts, and domestic life that shaped the region around the city.
