Jerzy Kalina's Monument to Anonymous Pedestrians is split across both sides of Swidnicka Street, with bronze figures seeming to sink into the pavement on one side and emerge on the other. It is one of Wroclaw's most memorable pieces of public art and an easy stop while walking south from the Old Town.
