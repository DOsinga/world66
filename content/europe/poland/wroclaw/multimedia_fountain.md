A large interactive fountain installed in 2009 as part of the renewal of the Centennial Hall grounds. The fountain has 800 jets, 300 lights, and a flame jet that synchronises with classical music in the warmer months. After dark, projection systems play short films onto a curtain of water — a slightly kitsch but genuinely popular evening attraction.

Best visited at dusk on a summer evening when both the music programme and the visual show are at their best. Free.
