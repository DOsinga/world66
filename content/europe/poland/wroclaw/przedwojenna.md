A self-conscious recreation of a pre-war (przedwojenna) Polish café-restaurant on the Rynek — heavy lace curtains, polished wood, vintage china, a piano in the corner. The menu does Polish classics in a slightly more refined style: rolled-veal roulades, duck breast with cherries, the full range of stuffed cabbages and dumplings.

A reliable choice for a special-occasion lunch or dinner on the square; book ahead for weekend evenings.
