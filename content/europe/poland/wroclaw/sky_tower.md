At 212 metres, Sky Tower was the tallest building in Poland from its 2012 opening until Warsaw's Varso Tower overtook it in 2022. The 49-storey glass tower stands south of the centre, mixing apartments, offices and a shopping mall. The 49th-floor observation deck gives an exceptional view over the whole of Wrocław and on a clear day to the Sudety mountains on the horizon.

A small admission fee for the viewing platform, with an indoor and outdoor section. Easiest to reach by tram from the centre.
