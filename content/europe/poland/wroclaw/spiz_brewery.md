A brewpub on the Rynek itself, with a small in-house brewery visible behind glass and Wrocław's most-drunk beer (an unfiltered local pilsner) on tap alongside seasonal specials. The food is hearty Polish pub fare — sausage platters, pork knuckle, pierogi, and very good fries.

The location below the Town Hall is one of the most atmospheric beer-drinking spots in the centre. Busy on summer evenings; reservations help for weekend dinners.
