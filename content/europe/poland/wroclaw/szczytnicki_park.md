The largest park in central Wrocław, dating from the 18th century and one of the oldest landscape parks in Poland. The park stretches between Centennial Hall and the river and includes the Japanese Garden, a small wooden church relocated from the Bohemian-Silesian border, and a serious arboretum with over 400 species of trees.

Free, open year-round, and ideal for a quiet walk before or after a Centennial Hall visit. In autumn the leaves are spectacular.
