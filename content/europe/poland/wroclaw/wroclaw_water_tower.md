Wroclaw Water Tower is a 63-metre brick utility tower built in 1904-1905, with an observation level partway up the structure. It supplied water until the mid-1980s and was later restored.

The interior has not been open to the public in recent years, so the visit is mainly for the exterior: a distinctive piece of early 20th-century civic engineering south of the centre.
