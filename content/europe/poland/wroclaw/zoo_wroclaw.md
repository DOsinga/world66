Zoo Wroclaw is Poland's oldest zoo, opened in 1865 when the city was Breslau. It remains one of the country's largest animal collections and is a major family attraction in the city.

Allow enough time for a proper visit, especially if you plan to include the zoo's larger themed exhibits.
