The House of Music and Dance is Zabrze's main cultural stage, a big socialist-era performance hall that still anchors the city's concert and event calendar. It is the kind of place where the building, the programme, and the local crowd matter together.

Check the schedule before adding it to your plans; the venue is most useful when there is a concert, cabaret, or dance event on. Architecturally curious travellers may also appreciate it as part of the Upper Silesian civic landscape, a contrast to the mining museums nearby.
