The Military Technology Museum in Zabrze is a specialist collection of vehicles, equipment, and military hardware. It fits the industrial character of the Upper Silesian region, where machinery and twentieth-century history often sit close together.

This is most rewarding for visitors with an interest in engineering, military history, or heavy equipment. Others may prefer Zabrze's mining heritage sites, but the museum adds a different mechanical strand to the city.
