St Anne Church is one of Zabrze's more visible historic churches, a brick Catholic landmark with
twin towers and a strong presence in the surrounding district. It reflects the Upper Silesian mix of
industrial growth, parish life, and confident late-19th and early-20th-century church building.

It is not a headline sight on the scale of Guido Mine, but it is worth a look if you are walking
through Zabrze's older neighbourhoods. The church helps round out the city beyond mines, factories,
and technical museums.
