Zabrze's Water Tower is part of the industrial landscape that gives the city its character, standing apart from the better-known mines and adits. Its brick engineering and height make it a useful visual marker for understanding how utilities, mining, and urban growth shaped Upper Silesian towns.

It is a short architectural stop rather than a full museum visit. See it while moving between Zabrze's industrial heritage sites, especially if you are interested in the infrastructure behind the coal-and-steel story.
