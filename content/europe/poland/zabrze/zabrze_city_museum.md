Zabrze City Museum adds local history and cultural context to a city better known to visitors for industrial heritage sites. Its exhibits help connect everyday urban life with the mining and Silesian history visible elsewhere in town.

It is a secondary stop rather than the main reason to visit Zabrze. Pair it with Guido Mine or other industrial sights if you want a fuller picture of the city.
