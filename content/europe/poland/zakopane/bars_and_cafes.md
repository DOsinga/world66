---
title: "Bars and Cafes"
type: section
---

Zakopane's aprés-ski scene is lively in winter and surprisingly good in summer too. Krupówki Street has cafes at every turn, and the streets immediately behind it have the better bars — livelier, less overpriced, and with a more local crowd.

For coffee and cake, **Café Sabała** (Krupówki 11) is the classic Zakopane institution — on the main street, slightly touristy, but with good coffee and a warm interior of timber and mountain art. **Pijalnia Czekolady** serves excellent hot chocolate, which earns serious points after a wet-weather hike. For beer, the **Watra** bar on Piłsudskiego is where the Polish climbing and skiing crowd tends to congregate, with live folk music on weekends. Żywiec and Tyskie are the most common draught beers; grzane piwo (hot spiced beer) is the local winter drink of choice and served nearly everywhere.
