The Museum of the Zakopane Style - Inspirations focuses on the ideas behind the timber architecture that gives Zakopane much of its character. Exhibits connect highland craft, carved ornament, furniture, and design theory to the villas and public buildings that made the style famous.

It is a specialist stop, but a useful one if you have been admiring steep roofs and carved wooden details around town. Visit before a longer architectural walk and the buildings outside start to read with more purpose.
