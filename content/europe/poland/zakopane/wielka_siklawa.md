Wielka Siklawa is Poland's highest waterfall, dropping about 60 metres from the high Tatra lakes toward the Roztoka Valley. In good light the spray from its two main cascades can throw rainbows across the rock.

It is a hiking objective rather than a roadside stop. The usual approach is from the Morskie Oko side of the Tatras, so check weather and trail conditions before setting out.
