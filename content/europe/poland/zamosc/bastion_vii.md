Bastion VII is the best place to feel Zamość as a fortress rather than just a pretty square. The route runs through firing galleries, casemates, and the old defensive works on the edge of the planned city.

It adds grit to the visit. After the polished facades of the centre, the brick passages make clear that this ideal city was also built to hold a frontier.
