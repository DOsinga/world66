Mosorny Gron is the easier mountain outing above Zawoja. A chairlift serves the slope, with skiing in winter and access to views and mountain-bike routes outside the snow season. The summit area has a lookout tower, so it is useful when you want height without committing to the full Babia Gora hike.

It is a supporting attraction rather than the main event, but it gives Zawoja a second outdoor focus and makes the village work better for mixed groups.
