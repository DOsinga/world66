The Chapel in the Vineyards is a small survival from the countryside that once pressed close around Zielona Góra. It is a modest sight, but it belongs to the same wine landscape that explains the city's identity.

Do not cross town for it alone. It works best as an extra stop if you are already following the wine theme beyond the old centre.
