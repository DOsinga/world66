The Co-Cathedral of St Jadwiga is the main church in the centre of Zielona Góra. Its Gothic origins and later alterations are typical of a city that has shifted between Polish, Silesian, and German histories.

Step inside if the doors are open, but even a quick look from the street helps fill out the old-town walk. The church sits only a few minutes from the market square.
