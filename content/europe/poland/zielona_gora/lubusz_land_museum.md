The Lubusz Land Museum is the city's main museum and the best indoor stop for understanding Zielona Góra's wine culture. Its collections include a dedicated Wine Museum, along with regional art and history.

It is not huge, but it gives the city context. Visit it before or after the Palm House so the vineyard references around town feel less decorative and more rooted.
