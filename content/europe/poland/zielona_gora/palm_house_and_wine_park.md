The Palm House rises above the centre on a hill long tied to Zielona Góra's vineyards. Its glass structure folds tropical plants, city views, a restaurant, and the symbolic remains of the old wine landscape into one stop.

It is the sight that most clearly explains why Zielona Góra feels different from other western Polish towns. Come before sunset if you want the view across the roofs.
