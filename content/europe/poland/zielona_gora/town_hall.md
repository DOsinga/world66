The town hall is the old centre's main landmark, standing in the middle of the market square with a tall clock tower above the surrounding houses. Parts of the building go back to the 15th century, though the look has changed with later rebuilds.

You do not need long here, but the building gives the square its focus and makes a good first stop before wandering the lanes around the old town.
