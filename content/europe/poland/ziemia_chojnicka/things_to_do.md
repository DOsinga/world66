---
title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

The Chojnice Region is small-scale tourism — a market town with a medieval core, a string of glacial lakes, and one of Poland's biggest forest complexes. Most visitors spend their time outdoors: walking in the Bory Tucholskie, sailing on Lake Charzykowskie, paddling the Brda, or cycling between villages on quiet roads. The town of Chojnice itself rewards an afternoon for its gate, square, and parish church.
