---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

Summer is the time. July and August are warm (often 22–27°C), the lakes are swimmable, the sailing club is in full season at Charzykowy, and the forest is at its quiet best. Weekends in those months get busier, but only by Polish lakeside-cottage standards — there are no crowds in the international sense.

Spring and early autumn are excellent for cycling and walking, with cool, clear weather and few other tourists. September is the peak mushroom-foraging month and is a fine time to combine forest walks with eating well. Winter is bleak: short days, frequent snow, lakes frozen solid. Some Poles come for cross-country skiing on the forest tracks, but most of the region effectively closes down between November and March.
