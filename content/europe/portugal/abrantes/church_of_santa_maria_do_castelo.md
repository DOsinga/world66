The Church of Santa Maria do Castelo stands inside the castle enclosure, which gives it a stronger setting than many small-town churches. Its value is partly architectural and partly historical, tied to the families and military orders that shaped Abrantes.

Visit it with the castle rather than as a separate errand. Together they give the hilltop its weight.
