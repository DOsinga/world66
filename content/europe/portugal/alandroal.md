Alandroal is a small Alentejo town east of Évora, close to the Spanish border and the Guadiana. It is not polished, and that is part of the appeal: the streets are whitewashed and slow, the castle sits right in the town, and the surrounding countryside is a dry pattern of olive groves, cork oaks, marble quarries, and reservoir water.

The reason to come is the frontier landscape. [Castle of Alandroal](/europe/portugal/alandroal/castle_of_alandroal) anchors the town, while [Castle of Terena](/europe/portugal/alandroal/castle_of_terena) and the fortified [Boa Nova Sanctuary](/europe/portugal/alandroal/boa_nova_sanctuary) make an easy rural loop to the south. Farther east, [Juromenha Fortress](/europe/portugal/alandroal/juromenha_fortress) looks over the Guadiana toward Spain.

Alandroal is best with a car or bicycle and a willingness to move slowly. This is not a place of long opening hours and dense sightseeing, but the castles, empty roads, and Alentejo light make a good day if you are already in Évora, Vila Viçosa, or Elvas.
