---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

The Algarve has more annual sunshine than anywhere else in Europe, and Faro benefits accordingly. **June through September** is high season — warm enough for beach days, but crowded and expensive. **Late spring (April–May)** and **early autumn (September–October)** offer warm weather, manageable crowds, and better prices; these are arguably the best months. **Winter** is mild by northern European standards and the city is quieter and cheaper, though the ocean is too cold for swimming between late November and early March.

Events worth timing a trip around: **Carnival** in February, with parades and floats. The **Algarve International Music Festival** at the Teatro Lethes runs from late April to early June. **Festival F** brings Portuguese pop, fado and rock acts to the old town over three nights in late August or early September. The **Feira de Santa Iria** is the city's traditional fair in late October, with food stalls, music and a small funfair. The **Ria Formosa Festival** in August and the **Sardine Festival** the same month are both food-and-music affairs centered on the harbour.
