The Reserva Natural de Castro Marim covers the saltmarshes and lagoons just north of Monte Gordo, at the point where the Guadiana River meets the sea. The reserve protects one of the most significant wetland habitats in southern Portugal, with saltpans, reedbeds, and coastal scrub supporting an exceptional range of bird species throughout the year.

Spring (March to May) is the best season, when flamingos are present in large numbers and migratory shorebirds are moving through. Flamingos can be seen as close as a few dozen metres from the road along the reserve boundary. Summer brings breeding herons and egrets; autumn brings another pulse of migration. The reserve has a visitor centre at Castro Marim village and several marked trails through the marshes. You do not need a car to reach the closer viewing areas — a 20-minute walk from Monte Gordo gets you to the reserve edge.
