title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

The Odelouca area is a stretch of inland Algarve that most visitors drive through without stopping: cork oak hills, cistus scrub, and a large reservoir that filled a limestone valley in 2012. It rewards those who do stop, particularly birdwatchers and anyone who wants to swim in a river rather than a beach.

This is not a destination with a town centre or organised facilities. You need a car, patience, and ideally binoculars. What you get in return is a piece of the Algarve that hasn't been rearranged for tourism.
