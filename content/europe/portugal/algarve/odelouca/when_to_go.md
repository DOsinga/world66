---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

Spring — late March through May — is the best time to visit the Odelouca area. The wildflowers are out across the cork oak hills, migrant birds have arrived from Africa, and the weather is warm without being brutal. The river pools are cool but walkable, and the landscape is green rather than the scorched brown of high summer.

Avoid August if you have any sensitivity to heat: temperatures in this inland valley regularly exceed 40°C, and the canyon retains warmth long after the sun drops. For birdwatching, October through April covers the winter visitors; April through June is when the breeding raptors are most active and visible. If you are coming specifically to swim, July and early August are fine — just start early, retreat by midday, and return after 5 pm.
