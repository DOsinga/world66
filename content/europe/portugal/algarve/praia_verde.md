Praia Verde is one of the most beautiful beaches in the Algarve. Located in the eastern Algarve, about one kilometre from [Monte Gordo](/europe/portugal/algarve/monto_gordo) and ten kilometres from [Vila Real](/europe/portugal/algarve/vilareal) on the Spanish border, the [beach](/europe/portugal/algarve/praia_verde/praia_verde_beach) sits inside an environmentally protected area within a forest of umbrella pines.

The sea water is calm and warm in summer, reaching around 22 degrees. The beach is long, wide, and far less crowded than the central Algarve resorts. Behind the pines, the [Reserva Natural do Sapal](/europe/portugal/algarve/praia_verde/reserva_natural_sapal) protects the salt pans and wetlands where flamingos feed year-round. The nearby town of Castro Marim, about 15 minutes away, has a well-preserved [medieval castle](/europe/portugal/algarve/praia_verde/castro_marim_castle) that is worth a half-day visit.
