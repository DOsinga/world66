---
title: Beaches
type: section
---

The beach at Praia Verde is the reason to come here. It is long, wide, and backed by umbrella pines rather than the hotels and apartment blocks that dominate the central Algarve. The eastern Algarve coast generally has calmer, warmer water than the west — the currents from the Mediterranean side of the Guadiana estuary moderate the Atlantic swell, making it safer for families and less experienced swimmers.
