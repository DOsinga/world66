The over 2000-year-old olive tree near Tavira is a small but memorable heritage curiosity, valued for its age and gnarled form rather than for facilities or interpretation. In an Algarve itinerary, it points toward the agricultural history behind the coast's towns and salt flats.

This is a brief stop and should not be oversold. It works best for travellers already exploring the countryside around Tavira, especially those interested in old trees, rural landscapes, and quiet detours away from the beach.
