---
title: When to Go
type: section
---

Tavira is best in spring (April to June) and autumn (September to October). The town is busy in July and August, the beaches are crowded, and prices rise considerably. Outside high season, Tavira is genuinely quiet — some restaurants and cafés reduce hours or close entirely in January and February, but the weather is mild enough for sightseeing and the beaches are often deserted.

The Ria Formosa birdwatching is best in spring (April to May) when migratory species are passing through, and in winter when large numbers of waders and waterfowl are resident in the lagoon. The Easter week processions in central Tavira are atmospheric and draw visitors from across the Algarve.
