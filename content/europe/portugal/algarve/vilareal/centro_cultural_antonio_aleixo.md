Centro Cultural António Aleixo occupies Vila Real de Santo Antonio's former market building. It now hosts changing exhibitions, often focused on painting, sculpture, photography, and local cultural events.

It is a short, central stop rather than a destination for a full afternoon. The building is close to Praca do Marques de Pombal and the riverfront, so it fits naturally into a walk through town.
