The promenade along the Guadiana river is Vila Real's best evening walk. Lined with palms and benches, it runs south from the ferry terminal along the riverbank with Spain — and the white town of Ayamonte — directly across the water. The views across the river are best at sunset when the light catches the Spanish hillside and the fishing boats heading back toward the docks.

The stretch near the ferry terminal gets busy with day-trippers and market stalls on weekends. Walk further south toward the lighthouse for a quieter section. The Jardim do Pombal, a small park along the promenade, is a local gathering point with shaded benches and a view of the river mouth and Atlantic. At certain times of year you can watch flamingos feeding on the shallow mud banks opposite.
