---
title: "Getting There"
type: section
---

Aljezur is not served by train. The nearest rail connection is at [Lagos](/europe/portugal/lagos), about 50 km southeast, which is on the Algarve line from [Faro](/europe/portugal/faro) and [Lisbon](/europe/portugal/lisbon).

From Lagos, the EVA bus company runs a service north to Aljezur several times a day, continuing to Odeceixe. From Lisbon there are direct express buses to the western Algarve. The journey from Lisbon by bus takes roughly three and a half hours.

If you are driving, the IC4/N120 runs west from the main A22 motorway (Via do Infante) to Aljezur. The roads to the beaches are mostly good tarmac. A car is essentially required if you want to explore the coastline independently — public transport within the municipality is minimal.
