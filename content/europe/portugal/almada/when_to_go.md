---
title: When to Go
type: section
---

Almada shares Lisbon's climate: warm, dry summers and mild, sometimes wet winters. The best months for visiting are April through June and September through October — temperatures are comfortable (18–24°C), the beaches are uncrowded, and the ferry ride across the Tagus is pleasant rather than scorching.

July and August are hot (often above 30°C), and Costa da Caparica gets packed with Lisboetas and tourists alike. Cristo Rei can be very busy on summer weekends, particularly Sunday mornings when families come after church. The views from the statue's viewing platform are spectacular in any season, but early morning visits in summer avoid the worst of the crowds and the heat.

Winter is mild and can be rainy, but the old town and the riverside restaurants in Cacilhas are perfectly pleasant on a dry winter's day. The ferry crossing in winter light, with the city skyline and the bridge reflected in the Tagus, has its own appeal.
