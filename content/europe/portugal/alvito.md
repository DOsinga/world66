Alvito is a small Alentejo town between Beja and Evora, quiet even by Alentejo standards. Its draw is not a long list of sights, but a compact group of whitewashed streets, Manueline doorways, and Mudejar details left by the long Moorish presence in the region.

The [Castle of Alvito](/europe/portugal/alvito/castle_of_alvito) is the main reason to stop. It is more fortified palace than battlefield ruin, with Gothic, Manueline, and Islamic-influenced details. Nearby, the [Church of Nossa Senhora da Assuncao](/europe/portugal/alvito/church_of_nossa_senhora_da_assuncao) and [Chapel of Sao Sebastiao](/europe/portugal/alvito/chapel_of_sao_sebastiao) make the town's architectural identity clear.

Alvito is best as a slow half-day from Beja or Evora, especially if you like small towns where the reward is in details: arches, tiles, frescoes, clock towers, and the flat golden countryside around them.
