The Castle of Alvito began in the late fifteenth century and feels more like a noble house built for uncertain times than a hard military fortress. Its towers, battlements, and palace rooms mix Gothic, Manueline, and Islamic-influenced details.

The building has been adapted as a pousada, so access can depend on current use, but the exterior alone gives the town its main silhouette.
