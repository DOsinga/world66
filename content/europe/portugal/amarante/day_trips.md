Amarante sits between two of northern Portugal's biggest draws. **Porto** is 50 minutes west by car or about an hour by bus, perfect for an afternoon raid on the riverside Port lodges. **Peso da Régua** in the heart of the [Douro Valley](/europe/portugal/douro_valley) is 45 minutes east — a good base for a wine-tasting day along the terraced vineyards.

Closer at hand, the Serra do Marão rises south of town, with hiking trails through chestnut forest and a high-mountain road leading to Vila Real. **Travanca** has a notable 12th-century Romanesque church and monastery, twenty minutes by car.
