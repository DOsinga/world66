Parque Aquático de Amarante is a warm-weather family stop on the slopes above the Tâmega valley. Slides, pools, and sun terraces make it a different kind of outing from Amarante's churches, bridge, and riverside old town.

It is worth considering if you are travelling with children or need a low-effort summer afternoon. Check the season and opening hours carefully, as this is a weather-dependent attraction rather than an all-year sight.
