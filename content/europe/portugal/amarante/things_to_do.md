Amarante is small — two hours covers the centre at a slow pace. The town's postcard view is from the south bank of the Tâmega looking across the [São Gonçalo Bridge](/europe/portugal/amarante/sao_goncalo_bridge) toward the church and the row of glassed-in balconies that lean out over the river. From there the [São Gonçalo monastery](/europe/portugal/amarante/igreja_sao_goncalo) and its [art museum](/europe/portugal/amarante/souza_cardoso_museum) are a five-minute walk.
