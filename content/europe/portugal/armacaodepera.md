Armacao de Pera is a small beach resort on the Algarve, in the municipality of Silves. It sits at the eastern end of one of the longest stretches of sand on Portugal's south coast — a wide, pale beach that curves around a sheltered bay. The old village part, around a [small fortress](/europe/portugal/armacaodepera/forte_de_sao_joao) and chapel on a headland, retains some character, though much of the surrounding area has been built over with holiday apartments and hotels.

The main draw is [the beach](/europe/portugal/armacaodepera/praia_de_armacao_de_pera), and Armacao's version is excellent: accessible, lifeguarded in summer, and backed by limestone cliffs that give way to dramatic sea caves a few kilometres east at [Praia do Benagil](/europe/portugal/armacaodepera/praia_do_benagil). The diving and [boat-trip](/europe/portugal/armacaodepera/boat_tours_to_caves) industry centred on these caves is now a major part of the local economy.

Faro Airport is 40km east — about 35 minutes by car. The resort is compact and walkable once you are here, though a rental car is useful for exploring the wider Algarve.
