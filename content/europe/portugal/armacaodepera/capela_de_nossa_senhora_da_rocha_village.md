The whitewashed parish chapel of the old fishing village sits a couple of streets back from the seafront, surrounded by what is left of the original settlement before the resort came up around it.

The chapel itself is modest, with a simple sixteenth-century plan and a tile-dressed interior. The pleasure of the spot is its setting — a small square shaded by a few orange trees, where the resort hotels briefly give way to something older.
