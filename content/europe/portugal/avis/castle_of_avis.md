The Castle of Avis is now more a townscape than a single intact fortress. Three of the original towers remain, along with wall sections folded into houses and streets around the top of the hill.

That piecemeal survival is part of the charm. Walk the upper lanes and you see the castle as Avis residents have lived with it for centuries: not a museum piece, but a defensive shell slowly absorbed into the village.
