---
title: "Getting There"
type: section
---

Batalha has no train station. The closest are [Leiria](/europe/portugal/leiria) (15 km north) and Caxarias (20 km east), both on the Lisbon-Porto line; from either, regional buses or taxis cover the last leg in 20-30 minutes.

By road the town sits on the A19 motorway, an easy spur from the main A1. Driving times are about 1h30 from [Lisbon](/europe/portugal/lisbon), 2h from Porto, 1h from [Coimbra](/europe/portugal/coimbra), and 25 minutes from [Fátima](/europe/portugal/fatima). Long-distance Rede Expressos coaches stop here on the Lisbon-Leiria-Porto route. A small parking lot just behind the monastery handles most arrivals; in high summer it fills by mid-morning.
