Nucleo Museologico da Rua do Sembrano is a compact archaeological site-museum that makes Beja's deep history visible beneath the modern town. The remains include layers from the Roman period and earlier occupation, presented in situ rather than moved into a conventional display case.

It is a short, focused visit, best for travellers who enjoy seeing how a city is built on top of itself. Beja's castle and churches give the skyline; this museum gives the ground underneath, and that makes the town feel older and more layered than a quick walk might suggest.
