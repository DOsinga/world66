Bet Eliahu Synagogue is a modern building, but its importance is historical rather than architectural. It represents the public return of Jewish life in a town where families had hidden belief and ritual for generations.

Access can depend on services and local arrangements, so check before assuming you can enter. Even from outside, it is an important stop in understanding Belmonte.
