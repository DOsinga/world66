The Castle of Belmonte is the town's obvious landmark, a compact medieval fortress above the old streets. Its walls and tower frame wide views over the Beira Interior, and the site is closely tied to the Cabral family.

It is not a huge castle, so do not rush it. Pair it with the old centre and the nearby Jewish heritage sites, where Belmonte's deeper story comes into focus.
