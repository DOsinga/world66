The Arvore da Vida chapel is a small contemporary surprise inside Braga's seminary, built from about 20 tons of plain wood without visible metal fixings. Its quiet, stripped-back interior makes a sharp contrast with Braga's gilded churches and is worth seeking out if you like modern sacred architecture.
