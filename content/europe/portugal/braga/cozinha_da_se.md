A smarter option near the cathedral, with a menu that takes traditional Minho ingredients and applies a careful kitchen to them. The bacalhau à Braga — salt cod baked with potatoes, onion, and olive oil — is as good a version as you will find in the city. The dining room is small and fills up, so booking ahead is worthwhile in the evening. Moderate prices for the quality.
