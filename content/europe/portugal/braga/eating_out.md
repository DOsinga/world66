title: Eating Out
type: section
---

Braga is in the Minho, a region that takes its food seriously. Caldo verde (kale soup with a slice of chouriço), bacalhau (salt cod) in dozens of preparations, and roast veal are the staples. The old centre has a good cluster of restaurants around Largo do Paço and Rua do Souto; the university area north of the centre is cheaper and livelier.

Try [Taberna Belga](/europe/portugal/braga/taberna_belga) for traditional Minho cooking in a casual setting, or [Cozinha da Sé](/europe/portugal/braga/cozinha_da_se) for something smarter near the cathedral.
