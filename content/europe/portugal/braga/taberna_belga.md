A good-value restaurant in the old centre serving straightforward Minho cooking — caldo verde, grilled fish, and slow-cooked meat dishes. The room is simple and the service is brisk, making it a reliable choice for lunch after a morning of churches. House wine by the glass is cheap and drinkable. Gets busy at midday; arrive early or expect a short wait.
