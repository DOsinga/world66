Teatro Circo is Braga's grand early-20th-century theatre, built when the city wanted a proper home for plays, concerts, film nights, and dance. The restored building still works as a cultural venue rather than a frozen monument.

Even if you are not seeing a performance, it is worth noticing while walking the centre. Check the programme if you are staying overnight; it is one of the better ways to see Braga behaving like a living city rather than only a church-and-square day trip.
