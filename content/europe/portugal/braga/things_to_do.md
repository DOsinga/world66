---
title: Things to Do
type: section
---

Braga rewards slow walking. The historic centre is dense with baroque churches and palaces, and everything of importance is within easy reach on foot. Start at the [Sé](/europe/portugal/braga/se) — the oldest cathedral in Portugal — then step outside to the [Garden of Santa Bárbara](/europe/portugal/braga/garden_of_santa_barbara) alongside the medieval walls of the [Archbishop's Palace](/europe/portugal/braga/archbishops_palace). The [Arco da Porta Nova](/europe/portugal/braga/arco_da_porta_nova) marks the western entrance to the pedestrian centre.

For Roman history, the [Dom Diogo de Sousa Museum of Archaeology](/europe/portugal/braga/dom_diogo_de_sousa_museum) and the [Fountain of the Idol](/europe/portugal/braga/fountain_of_the_idol) are the best surviving reminders that Bracara Augusta was once one of the most important cities in Roman Iberia.

The unmissable excursion is [Bom Jesus do Monte](/europe/portugal/braga/bom_jesus_do_monte), a baroque stairway sanctuary perched on a wooded hillside a few kilometres east of the centre. The climb up the zigzagging staircase — or the ride on one of the oldest water-powered funiculars in the world — is as much the point as the church at the top.
