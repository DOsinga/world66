The Castle of Bragança is the centrepiece of the medieval citadel that crowns the hilltop above the modern city. Built in the twelfth and thirteenth centuries, the castle is in excellent condition — the crenellated walls, the keep, and the watchtowers all intact, with views stretching across the plateau toward Spain.

The keep (Torre de Menagem) houses a small military museum with armour, weapons, and documents from various periods of Portuguese history. The castle grounds are pleasant to wander, and the views from the battlements are some of the best in Trás-os-Montes. Entry to the museum is modest; the outer grounds are freely accessible.
