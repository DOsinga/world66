---
title: "Eating Out"
type: section
---

Bragança's cuisine is among the most distinctive in Portugal. The northeast produces excellent smoked meats — particularly the alheira sausage, the chouriço de sangue, and the presunto of the Barroso region — along with feijoada transmontana (a substantial bean and pork stew), roast kid, and wild mushroom dishes in season. Local wines from the Trás-os-Montes are improving rapidly and pair well with the robust food.
