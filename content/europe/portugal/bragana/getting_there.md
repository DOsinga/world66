---
title: "Getting There"
type: section
---

Bragança is remote by design. The nearest city is Porto, about 250 km to the southwest on good roads, roughly a 2.5-hour drive. The A4 motorway connects Porto to Bragança via Chaves and Mirandela.

There is no train line to Bragança — the last rail connection was dismantled decades ago. Rede Expressos operates daily bus services from Porto and Lisbon, with the journey from Porto taking about 3 hours and from Lisbon around 6.5 hours. The bus terminal is in the centre of the city. If you have your own transport, the drive from Porto through the Douro Valley is scenic and worth doing slowly.
