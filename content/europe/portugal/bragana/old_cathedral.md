The Old Cathedral of the Holy Name of Jesus (Antiga Catedral do Santo Nome de Jesus) stands in the lower part of the city and served as the seat of the diocese of Bragança-Miranda from the seventeenth century until 1545, when the diocese was reorganised. The building is a fine example of Manueline and Renaissance architecture, with an ornate portal that shows the transition between the two styles.

The interior has been well-preserved and contains notable gilded woodwork and azulejo tile panels. While the newer cathedral now serves as the main church of the diocese, the old cathedral remains an active religious building and is open to visitors. It is the more architecturally interesting of the two.
