Solar Brigantia is one of the best restaurants in Bragança for traditional transmontana cuisine, offering a menu built around the region's smoked meats, game, and seasonal produce in a comfortable setting. The alheira — the distinctive local sausage originally developed by Sephardic Jews to disguise non-pork ingredients — appears in several forms, and the roast lamb and kid are reliably excellent.

The restaurant is located in the centre of the city and is popular with locals as well as visitors staying nearby. Portions are substantial. Booking on weekends is advisable. Prices are moderate by Portuguese standards.
