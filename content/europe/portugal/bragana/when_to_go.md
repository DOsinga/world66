---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

Bragança has a continental climate — cold winters, warm summers, and a wide temperature range that reflects its inland position far from any maritime influence. Snowfall is common between December and February, and the city can be bitterly cold. Summer (June to August) is warm and dry, with temperatures up to 35°C, and is the most popular time to visit Montesinho.

Spring (April and May) is excellent, with mild temperatures and wildflowers across the park. Autumn brings mushroom season, which is taken very seriously locally, and the forests turn colour through October and November. The winter landscape has its own stark beauty if you don't mind the cold.
