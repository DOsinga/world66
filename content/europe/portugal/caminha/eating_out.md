---
title: "Eating Out"
type: section
---

Caminha sits at the mouth of the Minho, which means river fish on the menu — particularly **lamprey** (*lampreia*) in late winter and **shad** (*sável*) in spring. The local treatment of lamprey is a thick, dark rice dish (*arroz de lampreia*) which is heavy but excellent in the cold months. Outside lamprey season, look for grilled river bass, monkfish rice, and the local *bacalhau à minhota* (salt cod with onions and potatoes).

Restaurants line the riverside and the streets around the main square. Most are family-run and prices are well below those in Porto or Viana do Castelo. The lunch *menu do dia* — usually a soup, a fish or meat plate, a drink, and coffee — is excellent value.
