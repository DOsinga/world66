---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

Spring and early autumn are the best times: clear, mild, with the riverside green and not too many people. Summer (July and August) brings Portuguese holidaymakers and Galician day-trippers, and the place can feel surprisingly busy for its size — though never overwhelming.

Winter is wet. The Minho valley catches the Atlantic weather and rain is frequent from November through February. The compensation is the lamprey season: from mid-January through about the end of March, restaurants put it at the top of their menus and Spanish food enthusiasts cross the river to eat it.

The **Festa de Santa Rita de Cássia** in late May brings processions and music to the streets. The **Festival Vilar de Mouros**, a few kilometres inland, runs over four days in August.
