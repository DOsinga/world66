---
title: "Bars and Cafes"
type: section
---

Cascais empties of day-trippers by sundown and reveals a quieter drinking scene than its location near Lisbon suggests. The marina and the streets behind it have the densest cluster of bars, and the small lanes inland from Praça 5 de Outubro hold the older, more local-feeling spots.

**Bar do Guincho** at the beach end of the coast road is the obvious sundowner — a wooden-decked terrace with cocktails and the Atlantic in your eyes. **Chequers** is a long-running English-Irish pub near the central square popular with expatriates and English-speaking tourists. For an aperitif at the start of an evening, the **Hotel Cidadela** bar inside the fortress walls is hard to beat — sit in the courtyard with a port wine as the sun goes down. **Café Galeria House of Wonders** doubles as a coffee shop by day and a vegetarian bistro and wine bar by evening.
