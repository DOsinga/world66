Cascais sits at the western tip of the Lisbon coast and makes a comfortable base for the surrounding region. **Sintra**, half an hour inland, is the obvious major trip — the cool wooded hills, the Pena Palace, the Moorish castle, and the gardens of Monserrate make a full day. The summer royal court used to move from Cascais up to Sintra in July, and the rail and bus links between the two are easy.

**Lisbon** is 40 minutes east by train, and a Cascais base can pair well with a long day in the capital — particularly Belém and the river-front districts, which the train passes through. The neighbouring resort of **Estoril** is so close it almost merges with Cascais: famous for its grand casino, the long seafront promenade, and several handsome villas built by European royals in exile during the wars of the twentieth century.
