---
title: "Getting There"
type: section
---

The easiest approach is the **suburban train from [Lisbon](/europe/portugal/lisbon)** — the line runs along the coast from Cais do Sodré in central Lisbon to Cascais, with trains every 20 minutes and a journey of about 40 minutes. The route hugs the Tagus estuary and Atlantic coast, and is one of the most pleasant urban rail trips in Europe.

By car, the **A5 motorway** runs west from Lisbon to Cascais in about half an hour. The slower coastal road (Avenida Marginal) takes longer but passes through Estoril and gives you a closer view of the sea. Lisbon airport is half an hour away by motorway. Both directions have a frequent bus service to Sintra (bus 403) running via Cabo da Roca.
