Palácio de Conde de Castro Guimarães is a romantic seafront mansion in Cascais, set beside Parque Marechal Carmona. Its rooms mix aristocratic interiors, decorative arts, books, and views toward the rocky shore.

The palace works well with nearby Casa de Santa Maria and the Paula Rego museum, forming an easy museum-and-garden walk just west of the old town.
