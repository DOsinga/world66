Palácio de Conde de Castro Guimarães is Cascais at its most theatrical: a romantic seaside mansion with turrets, arches, tilework, and rooms filled with the former owner's art and books. It feels closer to a storybook palace than a sober municipal museum.

The setting helps. The palace stands near the water and the Boca do Inferno side of town, so it fits easily into a coastal walk. Inside, the private library and decorative interiors are the main draw.
