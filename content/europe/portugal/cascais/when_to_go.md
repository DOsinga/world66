---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

Cascais has the most reliable weather on the Atlantic coast of Portugal — about 260 days of sun a year, and noticeably cooler than Lisbon in summer thanks to the Atlantic breeze. The town is at its best in **May, June, September and early October**: warm enough to swim (the water hovers around 18–20°C in summer), uncrowded, and bright until late evening.

**July and August** bring the heaviest crowds — many Lisbon residents have summer houses here, and the seafront cafés and beaches fill up. **Winter** is mild (daytime temperatures usually 12–16°C) and rarely freezing, but it does rain. The annual **Cool Jazz Festival** in July is a high point, and the **Cascais Christmas Village** transforms the centre with lights and a market through December.
