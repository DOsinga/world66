The Garden of the Episcopal Palace is Castelo Branco's standout sight, an 18th-century Baroque garden laid out beside the former bishop's winter palace. Its clipped box avenues, granite statues, formal staircases, and ornamental lakes make it one of Portugal's more distinctive historic gardens.

Look for the staircases of the Kings and the Apostles, where sculpture, politics, and religious symbolism are packed into a compact formal design.
