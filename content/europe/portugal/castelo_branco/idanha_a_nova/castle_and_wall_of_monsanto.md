The Castle and Wall of Monsanto crown the hill above the village. The fortress has Templar and medieval roots, and its remaining walls are less polished monument than weathered lookout: granite, wind, and wide country in every direction.

The climb is steep enough to feel like work, especially in summer, but it is the essential Monsanto walk. Go early or late in the day if you want softer light and cooler stone underfoot.
