Lucano's Tower is a small landmark with a good story. Its silver cockerel recalls the 1938 contest that named Monsanto "the most Portuguese village in Portugal", a title the village still wears with pride.

You will pass it naturally while wandering the old lanes. It is worth a pause because it tells you how strongly Monsanto's identity is tied to its granite setting and folk architecture.
