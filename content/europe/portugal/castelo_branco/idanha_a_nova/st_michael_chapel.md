St Michael Chapel is a small Romanesque chapel near the castle approach. It is not a grand church, but its rough stone and exposed position fit Monsanto's hilltop landscape perfectly.

See it as part of the castle walk rather than as a separate detour. The chapel helps make sense of how the old village, the fortress, and the sacred sites all clustered around the same rocky summit.
