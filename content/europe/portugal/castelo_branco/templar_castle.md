The Templar Castle stands above Castelo Branco, with surviving walls and the remains of the former commander's keep. Built by the Knights Templar in the 13th century and later altered under King Dinis, the site is now valued as much for its views as for its ruins. From the top you can see across the city, countryside, and distant mountain ranges.
