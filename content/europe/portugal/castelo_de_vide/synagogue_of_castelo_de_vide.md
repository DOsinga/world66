The synagogue stands in the old Judiaria and is now a small museum about Castelo de Vide's Jewish community. The building is modest, but its survival matters: after the 1496 expulsion order, converted Jews continued to use it as a religious and teaching space.

Visit it together with the surrounding lanes rather than as a standalone attraction. The quarter gives the museum its context.
