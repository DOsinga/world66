---
title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Castelo de Vide is best explored uphill and downhill. Start at the castle for the view and the oldest lanes, then drop into the Judiaria around the synagogue and Fonte da Vila. The town is small enough that wandering is part of the point.

If you have more time, walk or drive up to Nossa Senhora da Penha. The chapel and viewpoint sit above town and make the Serra de Sao Mamede setting much clearer than it feels from the streets below.
