---
title: "Books"
type: section
---

**In the Name of the Rose** by Umberto Eco is not set in Coimbra, but the medieval monastic atmosphere it evokes — labyrinthine libraries, Franciscan debates, the tension between learning and faith — is not far from the world that produced the Joanine Library.

**Night Train to Lisbon** by Pascal Mercier follows a Swiss academic who comes to Lisbon to trace the life of a Portuguese philosopher, but the themes of Portugal's hidden intellectual life, its dictatorships and its silences, illuminate the kind of country Coimbra's university has operated within for centuries.

**The Last Kabbalist of Lisbon** by Richard Zimler is a historical thriller set during the 1506 Lisbon Massacre, which touches on the fate of Portugal's Jewish community — a story also relevant to Coimbra, where the Inquisition was established and many conversos lived. Dark but compelling.
