Museu Portugal Romano em Sico, also known as PO.RO.S, is a modern museum about Roman settlement around Condeixa, Conimbriga, and the Terras da Sico. Its interactive rooms work well before or after Conimbriga, adding context on how the Roman city fitted into the wider region.
