The Cork and Cork Oak Observatory is the one sight in Coruche that feels unlike anywhere else. Designed by Manuel Couceiro, the building is partly covered and lined with cork, turning a local industry into architecture rather than just an exhibition topic.

Inside are spaces for research, training, displays, and events about the cork oak forest and the cork industry. It is most rewarding with a guided visit or when something is scheduled, but even a short stop gives useful context for the montado landscape around town.
