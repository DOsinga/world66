Igreja da Misericórdia is a 16th-century church in Coruche, built in 1580 and later enriched with Mannerist and Rococo details. Inside are murals, canvas paintings, and a 19th-century pipe organ.

It is a compact stop rather than a long visit, but it adds useful depth to a walk around Coruche's historic centre, especially when paired with the nearby municipal museum and castle hill chapel.
