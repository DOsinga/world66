Museu de Lamego fills the former Episcopal Palace with art and religious works tied to the city and the surrounding Douro region. Its strongest pieces include paintings, sculpture, church furnishings, and archaeological finds gathered from local religious houses and private collections.

Pair it with Lamego Cathedral and the Sanctuary of Nossa Senhora dos Remedios for a compact look at the town's religious and artistic history.
