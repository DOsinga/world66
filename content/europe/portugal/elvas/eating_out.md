---
title: Eating Out
type: section
---

Elvas is Alentejo: heavy bread soups, slow-cooked black pork, lamb stews, and a strong tradition of conventual sweets. **Adega Regional** on Rua João Casqueiro is the long-running local choice for *migas* and *carne de porco à alentejana*. **Coelho** does an excellent grilled meats menu with a serious local wine list. For coffee and the city's famous *sericaia* with plums, try the cafés on Praça da República. The local speciality to look for in any shop window is **ameixas de Elvas** — sugared yellow plums, geographically protected and unique to the area.
