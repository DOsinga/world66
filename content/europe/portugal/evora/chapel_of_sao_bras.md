The Chapel of São Brás stands just outside Évora's historic centre and is a good example of late 15th-century Mudéjar-Gothic architecture. Its cylindrical buttresses give the small chapel a fortress-like look. Interior access is limited, but the exterior is worth a short detour for architecture-minded visitors.
