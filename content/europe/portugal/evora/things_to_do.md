---
title: Things to Do
type: section
---

Évora rewards slow walking. The walled centre is compact enough to cover on foot, with Roman ruins, medieval churches, and 18th-century palaces turning up around every corner. The essential circuit runs from the [Praça do Giraldo](/europe/portugal/evora/praca_do_giraldo), the main square, up to the [Roman Temple](/europe/portugal/evora/roman_temple) and [Cathedral](/europe/portugal/evora/cathedral_of_evora), then down to the [Igreja de São Francisco](/europe/portugal/evora/igreja_sao_francisco) and its unsettling [Chapel of Bones](/europe/portugal/evora/capela_dos_ossos).

The [Frei Manuel do Cenáculo National Museum](/europe/portugal/evora/museu_nacional_evora) next to the cathedral has the best collection of Roman and medieval artifacts in the Alentejo. For something further afield, the [Cromeleque dos Almendres](/europe/portugal/evora/cromeleque_dos_almendres) — a neolithic stone circle older than Stonehenge — is a 15km drive west and completely deserves the detour.
