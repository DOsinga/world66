[Coimbra](/europe/portugal/coimbra) is the obvious excursion — 45 minutes by train, with a stunning Joanine Library, Romanesque cathedral, and the energy of one of Europe's oldest universities.

**Montemor-o-Velho** (20km inland) has one of Portugal's most impressive Moorish castles, rising above rice paddies on the Mondego flood plain.

**Praia de Mira** (20km south) is a quieter beach alternative on the edge of the Mata Nacional do Dunas de Mira, a pine forest that runs along the coast.

**Serra da Lousã** (45km inland) offers forested mountain walks, schist villages, and a complete change of landscape from the flat coastal plain.
