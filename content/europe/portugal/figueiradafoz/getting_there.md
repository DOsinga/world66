---
title: Getting There
type: section
---

Train from [Coimbra](/europe/portugal/coimbra) takes about 45 minutes on the CP Urbanos do Mondego commuter line, which runs frequently throughout the day. From Lisbon, the journey is roughly 2.5 hours with a change at Coimbra-B. Regional buses connect from Coimbra and other central Portugal towns.

By car, Figueira da Foz is 45 minutes from Coimbra on the IC8. Coimbra is the nearest significant transport hub — most long-distance connections go through there.

The nearest airports are Francisco Sá Carneiro in Porto (155km) and Lisbon Humberto Delgado (200km).
