Torre do Relogio is the modernist clock tower on Figueira da Foz's waterfront promenade. Built between 1942 and 1947 to a design by Joao Antonio de Aguiar, it gives the beachfront a clear landmark and a useful meeting point near the sand.

It is a short stop rather than a long visit, best combined with the beach, the promenade, or Forte de Santa Catarina.
