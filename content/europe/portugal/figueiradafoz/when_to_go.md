---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

July and August are peak season: warm, busy, and fully operational. The beach fills with Portuguese families, the promenade terraces run late, and the whole resort infrastructure — beach clubs, hire equipment, boat trips — is up and running. It is lively and fun, and you will not be the only person to have thought of it.

June and September offer the best balance. The weather is reliably good, the crowds are noticeably thinner, and prices ease back. These are arguably the best months to visit.

May and October are pleasant and cheap, with mild temperatures and a town that has largely returned to itself after summer. Some beach facilities may be reduced or closed. The pace is very quiet.

Winter is mild by Atlantic European standards but the town empties out. Most tourist facilities close or cut back sharply. Surfers keep coming — the autumn and winter swells at Buarcos are the biggest and most consistent of the year — but for a conventional beach holiday, this is not the season.
