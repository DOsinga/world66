The Church of Saint John the Evangelist stands in the center of Funchal, beside what was once the Jesuit college and is now a public square. Built in the 17th century, the church has a white and black stone façade in the restrained style typical of Jesuit architecture, and an interior decorated with 18th-century azulejo panels and gilded woodwork.

The church is less visited than the cathedral but in many ways more interesting. Its location in a busy square rather than a designated old town makes it feel more embedded in daily Funchal life. It is open most mornings and the interior can be visited freely.
