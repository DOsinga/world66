Quinta do Palheiro Ferreiro is a British-style estate garden above Funchal. The manor itself is private, but the park is open to visitors, with old plane trees, camellias, carefully planted beds, and broad views over the city and coast.

It is less central than Funchal's better-known gardens, which helps keep the atmosphere calm. Come for a slow walk rather than a checklist of sights.
