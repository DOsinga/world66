Museu da Guarda fills part of a former 17th-century seminary beside the old bishop's palace. Its collections cover archaeology, sacred art, local history, and ethnology, giving useful context for a city that is often visited quickly for its cathedral and highland setting.

The building itself is part of the visit, with sober stone spaces that fit Guarda's austere mountain character.
