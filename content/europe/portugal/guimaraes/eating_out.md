---
title: Eating Out
type: section
---

Minho cooking is heavy, green, and unmistakably northern: salt cod, kid roasted on rosemary branches, boiled meats with collard greens, and the regional speciality *rojões* (chunks of pork marinated in vinho verde and fried with chestnuts and tripe). Wash it down with the slightly fizzy local *vinho verde*, which is grown in the valleys just outside town.

In the old town the squares around Oliveira and Santiago fill with terraces; for something less touristy, head down Rua de Santa Maria or out toward Largo do Carmo.
