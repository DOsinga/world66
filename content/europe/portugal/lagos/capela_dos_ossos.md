Capela dos Ossos is the small bone chapel attached to Igreja de Sao Sebastiao. It is one of the Algarve's several ossuaries, a compact and unsettling reminder of the region's older religious culture.

The visit is short, but the church and bell tower make it worthwhile if you are already exploring Lagos' old centre. The tower view over town and the coast is the practical bonus.
