Lagos Municipal Museum is the town's main local-history museum, with archaeology, sacred art and objects tied to the Algarve's maritime past. Its location beside the richly decorated Igreja de Santo António makes the visit feel like part of the old-town circuit rather than a separate museum chore.

The museum is compact but useful for putting Lagos' fortifications, churches and seafaring history into context. Go before wandering the waterfront and old walls if you want a clearer sense of how the town developed.
