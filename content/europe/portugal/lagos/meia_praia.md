Meia Praia is Lagos' big beach, stretching east from town in a long, pale sweep that feels very different from the small coves below the cliffs. It is less theatrical than Ponta da Piedade, but far better if you want space, a long walk, or a straightforward swim.

Because the beach is open and exposed, conditions matter. On calm days it is easy and sociable; when the wind rises, the scale becomes part of the experience. Restaurants and beach bars are scattered along the sand, but there is enough room to escape the busiest patches.
