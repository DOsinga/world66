Praia da Batata is the town beach of Lagos, sitting just by the Forte da Ponta da Bandeira and close enough to the centre that you can be on the sand minutes after leaving the old streets. It is not the wildest beach on the Algarve, but the convenience, cliffs, and sheltered water make it useful and pretty.

At low tide, passages and rock arches connect toward smaller neighbouring coves, and kayak trips often start nearby for the caves and headlands around Ponta da Piedade. It can feel busy in summer, so come early if you want the easy charm before the day fills with towels and tour groups.
