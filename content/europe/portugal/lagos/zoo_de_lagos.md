Zoo de Lagos sits inland from the beaches, near Barão de São João, and makes a relaxed change of pace from the old town and cliffs. It is a compact zoo rather than a grand wildlife park, with birds, small mammals, primates, reptiles, and domestic animals arranged around leafy paths and picnic areas.

It is most useful for families, or for anyone who wants a quiet half day away from the heat and traffic of central Lagos. Go with modest expectations: the appeal is the shade, the easy scale, and the chance to slow down between beach days, not a blockbuster animal collection.
