Located in the centre of Portugal, Leiria is a city with a long history. The town gained prominence in the 12-th century as the southern outpost of the early Portuguese kingdom, in the defence against the Moors. Leiria castle was attacked and destroyed repeatedly.

Sights in town include the [Castle of Leiria](/europe/portugal/leiria/castle_of_leiria), the present version built in 1324 by King Dinis as a fortress and summer palace for himself and his queen, Isabel of Aragon. Below the castle is the small 12th-century Romanesque [Igreja de São Pedro](/europe/portugal/leiria/igreja_de_sao_pedro).

Excursions can be made to Marinha Grande, 12 km to the west, which has a glass factory originally founded by Englishman John Beare in 1748. Another option is the short trip south to Batalha, where the Gothic Abbey is the main attraction.
