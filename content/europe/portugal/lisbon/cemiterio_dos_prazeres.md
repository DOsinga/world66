Cemitério dos Prazeres is one of Lisbon's grand cemeteries, laid out like a small city of mausoleums, family chapels, and tree-lined lanes. It is peaceful rather than gloomy, and the funerary architecture says a lot about old Lisbon's class, faith, and taste.
