Cemitério dos Prazeres is one of Lisbon's most atmospheric cemeteries, laid out like a quiet city of mausoleums, family chapels, and tree-lined lanes. The graves and symbols tell a layered story of Lisbon society, and the calm setting makes it a thoughtful detour from the busier central sights.
