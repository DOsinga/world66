A 19th-century industrial complex in Alcântara repurposed as a creative hub with independent shops, restaurants, a bookshop, print studios, and a Sunday market. The Sunday Mercado is the main draw — vintage clothing, ceramics, artisan food, books — but the complex rewards a weekday visit too when it belongs to the people who work here. The biggest bookshop in Lisbon (Ler Devagar) is inside; the restaurant under the railway tracks (O da Venda) serves good grilled fish.
