Lisbon has many miradouros, but Graça is the one locals use. A terrace beside the Graça church in the hilltop neighbourhood of the same name, with a clear view over the rooftops to the castle, the river, and the April 25th Bridge in the distance. Less crowded than the tourist-dominated viewpoints of Santa Catarina or São Pedro de Alcântara. Evenings are best — a beer from the kiosk, the city turning golden, and no particular urgency to move.
