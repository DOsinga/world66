Monsanto Forest Park is Lisbon's largest green space, spreading across almost 10 square kilometres of wooded hills. Trails, picnic areas, and viewpoints make it useful when you want a break from the dense city centre. Its ecological park has an interpretation centre with permanent and temporary exhibitions.
