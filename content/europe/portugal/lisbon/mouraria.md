The former Moorish quarter below São Jorge Castle, traditionally the poorest and most stigmatised neighbourhood in Lisbon, and now one of the most interesting. Fado was born here — a plaque marks the house where Severa, the first great fado singer, lived in the 1820s. Today Mouraria is one of the most culturally mixed neighbourhoods in the city, with Cape Verdean, Bangladeshi, and Chinese communities alongside old Lisbon families. Walk the steep alleys, eat a custard tart at Largo do Intendente.
