The first and still the most copied food market concept of its type: an old municipal market on the Cais do Sodré waterfront, fitted out with counters from the best kitchens in the city. Tasca do Chico's clams, Marlene Vieira's cod dishes, Solar dos Presuntos for charcuterie, and José Avillez's petiscos counter. It's crowded, it's loud, and the communal tables mean you eat next to strangers. Worth it.
