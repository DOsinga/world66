Companhia dos Engenhos do Norte & Casa do Rum preserves the machinery of a sugarcane and rum works in Porto da Cruz. The steam-powered equipment is the draw: it shows how cane was crushed and processed when the plant was still part of Madeira's working industrial landscape.

The visit is short and self-guided, with a tasting room next door, so it fits well into a trip around the north coast.
