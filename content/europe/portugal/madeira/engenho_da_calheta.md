Engenho da Calheta is a working sugar-cane mill and small museum in Calheta. Some of the machinery is around 200 years old, and the mill still shows the old process of crushing cane and moving the waste bagasse away from the rollers.

The site is compact, with a cafe attached, and works well as a short stop on the south coast of Madeira, especially if you want context for the island's sugar and rum history.
