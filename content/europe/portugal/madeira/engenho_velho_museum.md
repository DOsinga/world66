Engenho Velho Museum is a small industrial museum in Porto da Cruz, set in an old sugar-cane and rum-making works. The self-guided visit leads through dark rooms filled with antique machinery and tools connected to Madeira's cane-processing history.

It is a short stop rather than a major museum, but it adds useful context if you are exploring Madeira's north coast or want to understand the island's rum tradition.
