---
title: "Getting Around"
type: section
---

A hire car is by far the best way to explore Madeira if you intend to leave Funchal. The road network is excellent — the VR1 highway runs along most of the south coast through a series of long tunnels, cutting hours off the old coastal road — and renting a small car is cheap. The mountain roads are narrow, steep, and slow, but the surface is good and the views are extraordinary.

Public buses (Horários do Funchal in the city, SAM and Rodoeste in the rest of the island) cover most settlements but are infrequent away from the south coast. They are practical for one-off trips but frustrating if you want to combine several stops in a day.

Many of the best walks are not easily reached by bus, so a number of local agencies run guided levada walks with transport included — useful for first-timers or those without a car. Taxis are easy to find in Funchal and reasonable for short trips; agree a fixed price in advance for anything longer.
