---
title: "Getting There"
type: section
---

Almost everyone arrives at Cristiano Ronaldo International Airport (Funchal), a few kilometres east of the city. The runway is famously short and partly built on stilts over the sea, which can make for a memorable landing, particularly in strong easterly winds — pilots occasionally divert to the mainland when crosswinds are bad.

Direct flights run from Lisbon, Porto, and most major European cities (London, Manchester, Frankfurt, Paris, Amsterdam, Dublin, Madrid). The flight from Lisbon is about 90 minutes; from London, about four hours. Charter flights from northern Europe fill out the summer schedule and keep prices competitive.

A daily ferry from Caniçal runs to Porto Santo (2 hours) and is the only regular sea connection. Cruise ships dock at Funchal's small port and there is a useful TAP Air Portugal hub connection through Lisbon for visitors from North America.
