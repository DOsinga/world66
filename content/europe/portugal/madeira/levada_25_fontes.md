The Levada das 25 Fontes is the single most popular walking route in Madeira and probably the best introduction to what a levada walk is. It starts at the visitor centre at Rabaçal in the western mountains, drops down a steep concrete path, and follows a level water channel through laurel forest to a small amphitheatre where twenty-five springs feed into the lake.

The walk is about 11 km return and takes 3–4 hours at a relaxed pace. Most of it is genuinely level — the levada needs a constant gentle gradient to keep the water flowing — but the descent from the car park, and the climb back up at the end, will catch you out if you are not expecting it. It is heavily walked in summer; start early or do it on a weekday.
