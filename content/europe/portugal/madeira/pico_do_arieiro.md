Pico do Arieiro is the third-highest peak in Madeira at 1,818 metres, and the only summit you can drive to. A narrow but well-surfaced mountain road from Funchal climbs the central spine of the island and reaches a small car park, café, and viewpoint terrace right on top. On a clear morning the views — over the central mountains, down into the green valleys, and out to the south coast — are some of the best on the island.

It is also the starting point for the classic ridge traverse to Pico Ruivo, the highest peak. Most visitors go up just for the view at sunrise, when the mar de nuvens often fills the valleys below; arrive in the dark, watch the sun lift over the cloud, and drive back down to breakfast in Funchal.
