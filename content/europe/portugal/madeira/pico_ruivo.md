Pico Ruivo is the highest point on Madeira, a rocky summit deep in the central mountains. The walk to it from Achada do Teixeira is the easiest and most popular route — a paved stone path of about 90 minutes return, climbing through low scrub and barren rock to the summit cabin.

The classic Madeira hike, however, is the traverse from Pico do Arieiro to Pico Ruivo: a spectacular 7 km ridge walk along a knife-edge of crumbling lava, through tunnels cut into the rock and across narrow saddles with vertical drops on both sides. Allow 7–8 hours return and start early, before the cloud rolls in from the north coast. Stout boots, water, a head torch for the tunnels, and a head for heights are essential.
