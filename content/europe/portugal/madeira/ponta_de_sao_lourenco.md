Ponta de São Lourenço is the long, narrow peninsula that forms Madeira's eastern tip. Unlike the rest of the island, it is arid and treeless — a place of bare volcanic ridges and exposed sea cliffs falling into deep blue water. The contrast with the lush interior is dramatic.

A well-marked trail (PR8) runs out along the peninsula from the car park at Baía d'Abra to a small house and viewpoint near the tip, about 7 km return and roughly three hours. It is one of the easier proper walks on the island — flat in stretches, with some short steep sections — and the views from the ridges are extraordinary. Take water and sun protection; there is no shade at all.
