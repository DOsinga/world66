Porto Moniz sits at the northwestern tip of Madeira, about ninety minutes from Funchal by car. It is famous for its natural lava pools — a series of seawater swimming pools formed naturally from cooled volcanic rock, with the surf breaking just metres away over the outer walls. There are two sets, one free and one with a small entry fee and concrete-trimmed access, both worth the visit.

The village itself is pleasant — a small harbour, a clutch of seafood restaurants, and the spectacular drive from São Vicente that hugs the cliffs all the way along the north coast. The road through the old tunnels (now bypassed by the modern highway) can still be driven and is unmissable for anyone who likes a slightly hair-raising mountain road.
