---
title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Porto Santo is built around its beach, but the island rewards a little movement. Vila Baleira gives you Columbus history, while the dry interior has short climbs to volcanic viewpoints and odd rock formations.

Distances are small, so a spare day can combine the beach, Casa Colombo, and one hill walk without rushing. With a night on the island, save the viewpoints for early morning or late afternoon when the light is softer and the heat drops.
