Vereda da Penha d'Aguia is a steep hike up the great rock massif above Porto da Cruz. The trail climbs from the village toward a high shoulder with big views over the north coast, the Atlantic and the green folds of eastern Madeira.

It is not a casual promenade: expect a hard climb, uneven ground and exposed sections. Go in clear weather, carry water, and start early if you want the ridge views before cloud builds over the mountains.
