Vereda do Larano is a coastal walking route between Machico and Porto da Cruz, combining levada paths, cliffside track, and broad Atlantic views. The highlight is the exposed stretch around Boca do Risco, where the north coast opens up in a long sweep of sea and rock.

It is a proper one-way hike, not a casual promenade. Start early, carry water, check the weather, and plan your bus or taxi return before setting off.
