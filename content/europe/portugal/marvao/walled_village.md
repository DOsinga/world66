The village of Marvão is not just the approach to the castle. Its lanes, gates, white houses, and wall walks are the reason the castle feels so complete. The ridge is narrow enough that views appear suddenly between houses, then open into wide terraces above the Alentejo.

Give yourself time to wander without a list. The pleasure is in the scale: a small village held inside a huge landscape.
