---
title: "Eating Out"
type: section
---

Minho cuisine is among the most satisfying in Portugal — generous portions, excellent caldo verde (kale soup), bacalhau in various forms, and local pork dishes. Melgaço's restaurants all serve Alvarinho wine produced within the municipality, and pairing it with the local food is one of the best things about being here. The Friday market is a good place to pick up cured meats, cheese, and bread.
