Igreja da Misericordia de Miranda do Douro is a historic church whose construction began in 1578. Its simple single-nave plan and chancel fit the compact old-town setting of Miranda do Douro.

It is a short stop while walking the center, especially if you are already visiting the cathedral and the nearby Museu da Terra de Miranda.
