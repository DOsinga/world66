The Museu da Terra de Miranda is the best place to understand why this corner of Portugal feels different. Its collections cover rural work, local dress, archaeological finds, masks, and the traditions of the Terra de Miranda plateau.

The museum is especially useful for visitors who will not be in town during a festival, when the famous Pauliteiros stick dancers and masked winter traditions are easier to see in real life. It turns those customs into context rather than folklore wallpaper.
