---
title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Miranda do Douro is compact enough to see slowly. Start in the old town at the [co-cathedral](/europe/portugal/miranda_do_douro/concatedral_de_miranda_do_douro), then use the [Museu da Terra de Miranda](/europe/portugal/miranda_do_douro/museu_da_terra_de_miranda) to understand the language, dress, masks, and rural culture of the plateau. Leave time for the Douro cliffs, either from [Miradouro do Castrilhouco](/europe/portugal/miranda_do_douro/miradouro_do_castrilhouco) or from a short environmental cruise below town.
