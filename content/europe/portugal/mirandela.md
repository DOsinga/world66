Mirandela is a small city in Trás-os-Montes, set on a broad bend of the Tua River. It is not a blockbuster stop, but it has a good northern Portuguese rhythm: old granite, hot summers, riverside walks, olive oil, and the famous alheira sausage that carries the town's name across Portugal.

The [Ponte Velha](/europe/portugal/mirandela/ponte_velha) is the reason to start here. The long stone bridge crosses from the older streets to the right bank, and its arches give Mirandela a proper focal point. Around it are river paths, gardens, cafes, and the low-key civic centre where the town feels most itself.

Mirandela's food identity is stronger than its sightseeing list. The [Olive and Olive Oil Museum](/europe/portugal/mirandela/olive_and_olive_oil_museum) explains the local crop in a former mill, while the restaurants serve alheira fried with egg and potatoes. Come if you are already crossing the northeast or looping between [Porto](/europe/portugal/porto), the Douro, and Bragança; don't make it carry a whole trip by itself.
