Monforte is a small Alto Alentejo town between Estremoz and Portalegre, surrounded by olive groves, cattle country and the dry, open landscapes that define this part of eastern Portugal. It is not a headline stop, but it has the slow rhythm and pale stone look that make the Alentejo so appealing.

The town's own sights are modest: castle remains, churches and a Roman bridge. The real prize is just outside town at [Villa of Torre de Palma](/europe/portugal/monforte/villa_of_torre_de_palma), one of the region's important Roman villa sites. It gives Monforte a deeper history than its sleepy streets first suggest.

Come by car. Monforte works best as a rural detour between [Évora](/europe/portugal/evora), Elvas and Portalegre, especially if you like Roman ruins, fortified border towns and landscapes with more space than traffic.
