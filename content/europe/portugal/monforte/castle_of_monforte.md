The Castle of Monforte survives mostly as vestiges rather than a complete fortress, but its position still explains the town's defensive role on the old frontier. The castle guarded routes through Alto Alentejo in a landscape contested by Moors, Portuguese and Castilians over different periods.

Do not expect a polished monument. It is best treated as part of a short walk through the town, paired with the nearby church and the Roman bridge.
