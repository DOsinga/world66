The Roman Bridge over the Ribeira de Monforte is a small but satisfying piece of old infrastructure, the kind of stone crossing that makes sense in an Alentejo town where history is mostly quiet and low to the ground.

It is worth a look while walking around Monforte's centre, especially if you are already visiting the castle remains. The bridge gives the town a practical link to its Roman and medieval past without asking for much time.
