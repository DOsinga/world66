Villa of Torre de Palma is the strongest reason to seek out Monforte. The Roman villa ruins sit in the countryside near Vaiamonte and preserve the outline of a wealthy rural estate, with mosaics and traces of domestic, agricultural and religious spaces.

It is a quiet site, and that suits it. Instead of fighting crowds, you get the Alentejo landscape around the ruins and a good sense of how Roman landowners used this fertile inland region.
