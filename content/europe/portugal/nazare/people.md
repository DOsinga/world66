---
title: "People"
type: section
---

The people of Nazaré have a distinctive identity on the Portuguese coast. The fishing community has its own dress traditions — older women are sometimes seen in the seven skirts (sete saias) that are the traditional costume of Nazaré women, a practice that survives more as cultural expression than everyday dress. The town's character is shaped by centuries of fishing life: close-knit, physically demanding, and oriented around the sea.

The waves have brought a new wave of people in recent decades — professional big-wave surfers, surf photographers, and the crews that follow them around the world. Garrett McNamara, the American surfer who broke the world record here in 2011, settled in Nazaré and has become part of the community. The old fishing town and the international surf circuit coexist in a way that is distinctly Nazaré.
