The Castle of Nisa is more town fabric than isolated monument. Its surviving walls and gates mark the old medieval core, where white houses and narrow streets still follow the defensive shape of the settlement.

It is worth a slow wander rather than a formal visit. Start here to understand Nisa's frontier role before heading out to the stronger castles at Amieira do Tejo and Montalvao.
