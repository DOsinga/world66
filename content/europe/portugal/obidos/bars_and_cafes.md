---
title: Bars and Cafes
type: section
---

Drinking in Óbidos means **ginjinha** — the sour cherry liqueur poured into a tiny chocolate cup, sold for one euro at counters along Rua Direita. Several stands compete for the trade; the original is **Ginjinha de Óbidos** at the western end of the street, but they are all essentially the same drink.

For something quieter, the bars below the castle and on the terraces along the walls do a good line in Portuguese wines and local craft beer.
