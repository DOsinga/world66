Óbidos works best as a day trip from Lisbon, but if you're staying overnight there are several decent outings within half an hour. The dramatic **Lagoa de Óbidos**, a coastal lagoon at Foz do Arelho, has long white-sand beaches and excellent shellfish restaurants. **Peniche**, a working fishing port on the rocky cape further south, is the jumping-off point for the **Berlengas Islands** — a remote nature reserve a 30-minute boat ride offshore.

**Caldas da Rainha**, 6 km north, has a daily fruit and vegetable market in Praça da República, the José Malhoa Museum of Portuguese naturalist painting, and a strong ceramics tradition.
