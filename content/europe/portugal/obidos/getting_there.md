---
title: Getting There
type: section
---

Óbidos is about 85 km north of [Lisbon](/europe/portugal/lisbon), an easy hour by car or a 75-minute ride on the **Rede Expressos** coach (multiple departures daily from Sete Rios station). There is also a regional train but the line is slow and the station is below the town, a stiff uphill walk to the gate.

Drivers should park in the **large car park outside the southern walls** — driving inside the town is not permitted for visitors and the lanes are too narrow anyway.
