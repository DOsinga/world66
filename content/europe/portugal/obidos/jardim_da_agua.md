Jardim da Agua is an outdoor art garden built around water features, tiles, ceramics, and tall sculptural forms. It was designed by Portuguese ceramicist Luis Ferreira da Silva and built in the 1990s.

It is a small, offbeat detour rather than a major monument, but worthwhile for visitors interested in contemporary ceramic art and unusual roadside sights near Obidos.
