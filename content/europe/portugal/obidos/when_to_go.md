---
title: When to Go
type: section
---

Óbidos is best out of peak season. **June to early September** brings heavy day-trip traffic from Lisbon and the cruise terminals — the town is rammed from late morning until about 5pm. Outside those hours, even in summer, it returns to itself.

**April–May** and **October** are ideal: warm weather, manageable crowds, and the surrounding pear orchards full of blossom or fruit. Winter is quiet and atmospheric — and timed right, you can catch the **Mercado Medieval** in July, the international **Chocolate Festival** at Easter, and the magical **Christmas Village** that takes over the inner town in December.
