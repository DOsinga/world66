---
title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Odemira's main appeal is its landscape — the vast, varied territory of Portugal's largest municipality stretches from cork oak plateaus in the east to Atlantic cliffs in the west. The coast, protected within the Parque Natural do Sudoeste Alentejano e Costa Vicentina, draws most visitors. The interior rewards those who take the time to explore: megalithic monuments, Roman bridges, and whitewashed villages are scattered across the hills.
