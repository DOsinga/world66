Estadio do Dragao is FC Porto's home ground and the best place to understand how deeply football runs through the city. On match nights the stadium becomes a loud blue-and-white civic ritual; outside match times, the museum and stadium tours make it accessible even if you cannot catch a game.

It sits on the metro network, so it is easy to reach from central Porto. Football fans should check fixtures before booking the trip, while casual visitors may prefer the quieter museum-and-tour version.
