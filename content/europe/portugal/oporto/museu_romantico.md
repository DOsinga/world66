Museu Romântico occupies the Quinta da Macieirinha, an 18th-century house near the Crystal Palace gardens. The rooms recreate the atmosphere of a 19th-century bourgeois home in Porto, with furniture, decorative arts, and domestic details that slow the city down after the riverfront and wine lodges.

The museum is modest, but that is part of its appeal. It is a good stop if you are already walking through the gardens or want a quieter interior that explains how Porto's comfortable classes lived during the Romantic period. The association with Charles Albert of Sardinia, who spent his final exile nearby, adds a melancholy historical footnote.
