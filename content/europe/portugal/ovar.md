Ovar is a workaday city between Porto and Aveiro with a better visual identity than many larger stops. Its streets and churches show off azulejos with real confidence, from blue-and-white facades in the centre to the almost glowing tiled exterior of Válega's church.

The city makes most sense as a half-day or full-day detour for travellers who like Portuguese tilework. Start with the [Igreja Matriz de São Cristóvão](/europe/portugal/ovar/igreja_matriz_de_sao_cristovao), follow the [Passos de Ovar](/europe/portugal/ovar/passos_de_ovar) through the old centre, then head out to the [Igreja Paroquial de Válega](/europe/portugal/ovar/igreja_paroquial_de_valega), the showpiece.

Ovar also has an Atlantic side. [Praia do Furadouro](/europe/portugal/ovar/praia_do_furadouro) gives the town beach, surf, and seafood energy, so the day does not have to be only chapels and tiles.
