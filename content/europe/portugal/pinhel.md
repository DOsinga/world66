Pinhel is a small hill town in Portugal's Beira Interior, close to the Coa valley and the Spanish borderlands. It calls itself Cidade Falcao, the Falcon City, and the name fits: the castle towers over the historic centre, and the town has leaned into its falcon symbol with a new falconry centre beside the walls.

The old core is compact and best explored on foot. The [Castle of Pinhel](/europe/portugal/pinhel/castle_of_pinhel) and its interpretive centre give the town its medieval shape, the [Municipal Museum](/europe/portugal/pinhel/municipal_museum) fills in the local archaeology and heritage, and the [Falcoaria Real de Pinhel](/europe/portugal/pinhel/falcoaria_real_de_pinhel) makes the town's identity feel less like a slogan. It is not a major city, but it has enough texture for a rewarding half day or quiet overnight if you are travelling through Guarda, the Douro edge, or the fortified towns of the Beira.
