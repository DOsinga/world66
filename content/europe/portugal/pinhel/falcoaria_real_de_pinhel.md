The Falcoaria Real de Pinhel opened beside the castle as part of the town's Cidade Falcao identity. It is designed for the care and training of birds of prey, with outdoor flight areas and a small museum space devoted to falconry.

Check locally before planning around demonstrations, but even as part of a castle walk it helps explain why the falcon appears so often in Pinhel's modern branding.
