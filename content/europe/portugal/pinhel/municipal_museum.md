The Municipal Museum, in the Casa da Cultura, gives Pinhel more depth than the castle alone. Its collections cover local archaeology, religious art, and the material heritage of the surrounding territory, including links to the wider Coa valley story.

It is a good indoor stop after the castle, especially if you are trying to understand why this small town mattered in a frontier landscape.
