---
title: When to Go
type: section
---

The Algarve is warm and dry from April through October, with July and August the hottest months — sea temperatures peak at around 23°C in August. June and September are the best compromise between good weather and manageable crowds. The Festival da Sardinha in August is a week of outdoor concerts, grilled sardines, and general waterfront revelry — a good reason to visit rather than avoid.

Winters are mild by northern European standards: expect 15-17°C in January with some rain. The tourist infrastructure does not close entirely but operates on reduced hours, and prices drop significantly. If you are visiting for the racing circuit, check the motorsport calendar — events are concentrated in spring and autumn.
