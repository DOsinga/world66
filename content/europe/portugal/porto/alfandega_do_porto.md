The Alfândega do Porto — Porto's old customs house — is a massive neo-classical granite building occupying a prominent position on the Douro waterfront west of the Ribeira. The building housed customs operations from 1869 to the late 20th century; today it functions as a convention centre and cultural space, most notably as the home of the Museu do Carro Eléctrico.

The tram museum occupies the ground-floor warehouse space and holds a remarkable collection of historic tramcars, electric streetcars, and the equipment that kept Porto's tram network running for over a century. The trams themselves — restored to their original condition and including examples from the horse-drawn era — are displayed in the original warehouse bays with enough space to walk around them. Porto's historic tram network is still partially operating on the heritage routes to Foz and Massarelos; the museum gives the context that makes riding those routes more interesting.
