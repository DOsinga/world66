Boavista is Porto's modern commercial and cultural district, stretching west from the city centre toward the coast. The Rotunda da Boavista is its anchor, and from there the Avenida da Boavista runs 4 kilometres toward Foz, lined with banks, offices, and the kind of architecture that favoured concrete modernism in the 1970s. The neighbourhood is not conventionally pretty, but it holds two of Porto's most significant modern buildings: the Casa da Música and the Serralves Museum.

The Serralves Foundation's campus — museum, garden, and the art deco Pink Villa — occupies a substantial plot off the Avenida da Boavista and is more easily reached from this direction than from the centre. Between Serralves and the Casa da Música, the Parque da Cidade (Porto's largest urban park) offers a rare stretch of green that runs from the city's western residential areas down to the sea at Foz. For visitors staying in the west of the city, Boavista is the right base for Serralves, the coast, and easy access to the airport.
