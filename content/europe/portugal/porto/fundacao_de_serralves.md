The Casa de Serralves — universally known as the Pink Villa — predates the Serralves Museum by several decades and has a distinct character from the white Siza building beside it. The Villa is a late art deco mansion completed in stages between 1925 and 1944, its pink exterior and curved staircase giving it a glamour that the museum's rigorous modernism deliberately avoids.

The Villa functions as an exhibition space for the Serralves Foundation, showing temporary exhibitions of works from the permanent collection and invited artists. The formal gardens immediately around it — box hedges, reflecting pools, rose terraces — were designed in a Franco-Portuguese style and complement the Villa's architecture. The combination of the Villa's art deco elegance and the garden's formality is best appreciated in the morning, before the day's visitors arrive. The Villa's café terrace is one of the more pleasant outdoor eating spots in Porto.
