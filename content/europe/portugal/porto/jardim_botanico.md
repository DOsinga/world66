The Jardim Botânico do Porto is a compact botanical garden in the Massarelos district, occupying the grounds of an 18th-century aristocratic estate with terraced gardens that descend toward the Douro. The garden holds around 2,000 plant species organized by geographic region and botanical classification, but the setting — formal terraces, old trees, fountains, and the original manor house of the Quinta do Campo Alegre — makes it feel less academic than most botanical gardens of its type.

It is a good place to sit when the city's noise and hills become too much. The garden is rarely crowded, the entry fee is modest, and the tree canopy keeps it cool even in summer. The Museu de História Natural da Universidade do Porto is in the adjacent Faculty of Sciences building; if you have any interest in natural history, the combined visit is worthwhile.
