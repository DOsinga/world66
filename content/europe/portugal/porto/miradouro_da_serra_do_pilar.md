The Mosteiro da Serra do Pilar, atop the hill in Vila Nova de Gaia directly above the Porto-side bridgehead of the Ponte de Dom Luís I, is a UNESCO World Heritage Site and one of the finest viewpoints in northern Portugal. The circular church and its matching circular cloister — built in the 16th and 17th centuries — occupy the highest point of the Gaia hillside, and the terrace around the monastery complex commands a view of Porto, the Douro, and the coast that is unmatched anywhere in the greater city.

Wellington's use of the monastery during the Peninsular War adds a historical dimension to the visit — the explanation boards describe the crossing of the Douro in some detail. The church interior can be visited on a guided tour; the cloister and terrace are accessible independently. The combination of the architectural curiosity (circular monastery), the historical significance, and the magnificent view makes Serra do Pilar one of the stops that rewards planning your route around, especially if you're crossing to the Gaia side for the port wine lodges.
