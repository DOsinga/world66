The Miradouro da Vitória is a small terrace off Rua de São Bento da Vitória, overlooking the descent of the old city toward the Douro. The view takes in the rooftops of the Ribeira, the river, and the Gaia embankment beyond — a classic Porto panorama that is entirely free and considerably less crowded than the more famous viewpoints. It works particularly well at dusk.

The church of São Bento da Vitória behind the viewpoint is one of Porto's more impressive Baroque interiors, though it opens only for services and occasional concerts. The neighbourhood around it — the tangle of lanes between Vitória and the Ribeira — rewards wandering. This is the part of the historic centre where renovation has been slowest, the buildings are most authentically decayed, and the sense of a city that has survived centuries intact is strongest. The street called Escadas do Codeçal, a long staircase dropping from this plateau to the Cais, is one of Porto's great descents.
