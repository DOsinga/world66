The Museu Militar do Porto in the Colégio Militar at the Praça do Marquês de Pombal holds the military collections of the Porto district, covering Portuguese military history from the Age of Discoveries to the 20th century. The Peninsular War section is the strongest: maps of the Douro crossing, weapons and equipment from the period, and the personal effects of officers who fought in the campaign.

The museum also covers the Portuguese campaigns in Africa (Angola, Mozambique, Guinea-Bissau) during the Estado Novo period — a chapter of history that is honestly presented in the current displays, including the political context of the colonial wars and the revolution that ended them. The building itself, a former military college, has impressive courtyards and some fine Baroque architectural details. Entry is free; the museum is rarely crowded and rewards the kind of unhurried visit that the better-known sites rarely allow.
