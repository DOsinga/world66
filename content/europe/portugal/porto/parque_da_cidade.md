Porto's Parque da Cidade is the city's escape valve: 83 hectares of woodland, lakes, meadows, and formal gardens running from the western residential districts down to a beach at the Atlantic coast. It is genuinely large enough to get lost in, and on summer weekends Porto's families bring the entire extended family infrastructure — grills, folding tables, footballs — to the shaded areas around the lakes.

The park has no single main attraction; the pleasure is in the scale and variety. The network of paths winds through sections of woodland, past several lakes where people fish and row, and through open meadows that give the park an unexpectedly rural quality given that the city centre is less than 4 kilometres away. The park's western end meets the beach at Praia de Salgueiros, where the Atlantic surf is strong and the swimming good in summer. The combination of park and beach makes this the best outdoor half-day in Porto.
