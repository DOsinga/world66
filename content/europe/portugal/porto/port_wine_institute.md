The Port Wine Institute has a small exhibition on Port wine and the certification process that protects it. It is useful background before visiting cellars in Vila Nova de Gaia, especially if you want to understand the labels, styles, and official controls behind the drink.

Samples and guided tastings may be available, so treat it as a short specialist stop rather than a large museum.
