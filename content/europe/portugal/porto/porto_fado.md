Fado is a Lisbon art form, and Porto will tell you so readily enough while also insisting that it has its own tradition. Porto fado (fado do Norte) developed independently in the city's working-class neighbourhoods and has a slightly rawer quality than the more codified Coimbra or Lisbon styles. Whether you consider it a distinct genre or a regional variation, the music is worth seeking out in either city, and Porto has venues where you can hear it played seriously.

The best approach is to ask at your accommodation or visit one of the small dedicated fado houses in the Ribeira and Cedofeita — the venues change, and quality varies enormously. Avoid the tourist dinner-plus-fado packages in favour of a bar or small club where the music is the main event. The small clubs in the Barredo area of the Ribeira occasionally host informal fado sessions; locals are the best source of current recommendations. The experience is at its best in an intimate room where the musicians and the audience are genuinely engaged with each other.
