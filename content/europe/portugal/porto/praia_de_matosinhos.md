Praia de Matosinhos is Porto's nearest Atlantic beach, a proper sandy stretch with consistent surf that is genuinely easy to get to from the city centre. The metro (Line A) runs directly from Trindade in the city centre to Matosinhos-Sul in about 20 minutes, which means the beach is practical for a half-day rather than requiring a full-day expedition.

The surf is the main attraction from autumn through spring — Atlantic swells generate good waves for beginners and improvers, and several surf schools operate year-round on the beach. In summer the water is cool (the Atlantic upwelling off the Portuguese coast keeps sea temperatures lower than the latitude suggests) and the beach is busy but not overwhelmingly so by Mediterranean standards. The restaurant strip along the Rua do Bom Sucesso behind the beach — Porto's concentrated fish-eating district — makes the combination of beach and lunch in Matosinhos one of the best half-days the Porto area offers.
