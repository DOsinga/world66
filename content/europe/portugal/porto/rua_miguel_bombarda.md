Rua Miguel Bombarda is the centre of Porto's contemporary art and design scene, a street of galleries, concept stores, and design studios that holds a particularly good monthly market on the first Saturday. The galleries range from serious contemporary art — works by significant Portuguese and international artists, substantial prices — to more accessible design and print shops. The street is short enough to see end-to-end in an afternoon.

The First Saturday Art Walk happens monthly when all the galleries open simultaneously for a combined vernissage — the street fills from around 4pm, the gallery openings are open to everyone, and the adjacent bars fill up later in the evening. The event is unpretentious by art world standards and genuinely well-attended by Porto residents rather than just tourists. The Rua do Rosário, running parallel, has a similar concentration of studios and ateliers that extends the circuit. This part of Cedofeita is the most honest representation of Porto's contemporary creative life.
