Rua de Santa Catarina is Porto's principal pedestrian shopping street, running from the Praça da Batalha in the south to the Mercado do Bolhão in the north. The street itself is unremarkable — the standard mix of international chains and local shops that fills any European pedestrian precinct — but its connections are what make it useful as an orientation point.

At the southern end, the Praça da Batalha anchors the street with the Teatro Nacional de São João and the Cinema Batalha (a beautifully restored 1940s cinema that now serves as a cultural centre). Halfway along, the Café Majestic on a side street is Porto's great historic café. At the northern end, the Mercado do Bolhão is the best food market in the city. The street itself is where you go when you need something — it functions as the city's utility corridor, and understanding where it lies in the city's geography helps navigation considerably.
