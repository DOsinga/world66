She Changes, often nicknamed the anemone, is a large suspended sculpture by Janet Echelman at the edge of Matosinhos. Its red net shifts with wind and light, making the roundabout and seafront feel less like traffic infrastructure and more like a public stage.

It is a quick stop, but a good one if you are already heading to Matosinhos for the beach, seafood, or a coastal walk from Porto. The piece works best from a little distance, where the Atlantic air gives it movement.
