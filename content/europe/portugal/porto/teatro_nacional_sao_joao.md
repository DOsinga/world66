Teatro Nacional São João is Porto's main national theatre and one of the defining buildings on Praça da Batalha. The theatre presents drama, opera, and other stage productions, but it is also worth knowing as a historic cultural landmark in the city centre.

Guided tours sometimes open the stage, rehearsal rooms, dressing rooms, and technical areas when they are not in use. Check the current schedule before building a visit around the tour.
