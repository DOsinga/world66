The riverfront suburb across the Douro from Porto is where all the major port wine lodges are based — Taylor's, Graham's, Sandeman, Ramos Pinto, and dozens more. Each offers tastings and cellar tours of varying quality and price. The best strategy: walk the riverside Cais de Gaia, pick a lodge that interests you, and book a 90-minute tour that includes two or three wines. Taylor's has excellent views from its terrace; Graham's is the most polished; Ferreira is the most historic. Combine with a visit to the cable car up to the Jardim do Morro.
