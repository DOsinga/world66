Praia da Rocha is a beach resort at the mouth of the Arade River, just south of Portimão in the Algarve. The [main beach](/europe/portugal/praiadarocha/praia_da_rocha_beach) is wide and long, backed by eroded sandstone cliffs and sea stacks in characteristic Algarve orange-red. The 16th-century [Fortaleza de Santa Catarina](/europe/portugal/praiadarocha/fortaleza_santa_catarina) stands at the eastern end of the beach, where the river meets the sea.

The resort is one of the older and more established in the Algarve, with a strip of restaurants, bars, and hotels along the clifftop [Avenida Tomás Cabreira](/europe/portugal/praiadarocha/avenida_tomas_cabreira). It gets crowded in July and August but is significantly quieter in May–June and September. To the west, [Praia dos Três Castelos](/europe/portugal/praiadarocha/praia_dos_tres_castelos) and [Praia do Vau](/europe/portugal/praiadarocha/praia_do_vau) are less crowded extensions of the same coastline.

[Portimão](/europe/portugal/praiadarocha/portimao_marina), a short walk or bus ride north, is the nearest town with a market, supermarkets, and the [Museu de Portimão](/europe/portugal/praiadarocha/museu_de_portimao) (housed in a former sardine factory — one of the best regional museums in Portugal). The fishing village of [Ferragudo](/europe/portugal/praiadarocha/ferragudo) sits across the Arade river, and boat trips to the spectacular [Benagil sea caves](/europe/portugal/praiadarocha/benagil_caves) leave daily from the marina.
