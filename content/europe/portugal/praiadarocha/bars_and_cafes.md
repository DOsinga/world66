---
title: Bars and Cafes
type: section
---

The clifftop promenade (Avenida Tomás Cabreira) is lined with bars and restaurants. The resort has a lively nightlife in summer — bars stay open late, and there is live music and clubs along the strip. Prices are reasonable by Western European standards; local beers and Algarve wine are good value. The Casino de Portimão in the marina is the big-night option.
