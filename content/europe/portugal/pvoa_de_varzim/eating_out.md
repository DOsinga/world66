---
title: "Eating Out"
type: section
---

Póvoa has one of the oldest fishing traditions in Portugal, and the kitchen leans heavily on the daily catch. Look for *pescada à poveira* (hake stewed with potatoes), *arroz de marisco* (seafood rice with tomato and coriander), and *lagosta suada* (lobster cooked in its own juices).

The Bairro Sul fishermen's quarter, around the seaport, is the place for traditional seafood — small tavernas where the menu changes with what came off the boats. The casino district and Avenida Vasco da Gama are home to more modern restaurants, including the local specialty *francesinha poveira*, the Póvoa version of Porto's famous sandwich.
