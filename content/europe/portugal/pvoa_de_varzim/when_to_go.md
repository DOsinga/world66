---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

July and August are the busy months, when Portuguese families fill the town. Hotels need booking ahead and the beach bars are at their loudest. June and September offer better value — warm weather, fewer crowds, and the same surf.

The festival of São Pedro, the city's patron saint, takes place in late June with processions, fireworks, and street stalls along the seafront — worth timing a visit around if you can. Out of season, Póvoa is quieter but still pleasant for a weekend trip from Porto.
