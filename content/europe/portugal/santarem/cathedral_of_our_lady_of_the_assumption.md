The Cathedral of Our Lady of the Assumption is Santarem's 17th-century cathedral, raised to that rank when the city became a Catholic diocese in 1975. Its restrained exterior gives way to a rich Baroque interior, making it a worthwhile stop while walking between the historic churches and convents of the old town.
