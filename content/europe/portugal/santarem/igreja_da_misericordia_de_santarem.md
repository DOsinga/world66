Igreja da Misericordia de Santarem is a 16th-century church from the transition between the Renaissance and Mannerist periods. Later Rococo details add to the mix, and the church has been protected as a national monument since 1922.

It is a worthwhile stop while walking between Santarem's other historic churches and viewpoints.
