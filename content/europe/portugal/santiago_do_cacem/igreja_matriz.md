The main church stands beside the castle and is part of the same hilltop group that gives Santiago do Cacém its shape. Its plain exterior fits the Alentejo mood, while the position above town makes it a natural stop when walking the old centre.

Treat it as part of the castle-hill visit rather than a separate expedition: the church, walls, cemetery, and views work best together.
