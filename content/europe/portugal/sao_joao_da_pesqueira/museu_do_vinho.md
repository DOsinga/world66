The Museu do Vinho is the best first stop in town if you are using São João da Pesqueira as a Douro base. Its displays cover local wine culture, the vineyard year, and the work behind the port and table wines that shape the surrounding hills.

It is not a grand museum, but it gives useful context before visiting the quintas or driving down toward the river terraces.
