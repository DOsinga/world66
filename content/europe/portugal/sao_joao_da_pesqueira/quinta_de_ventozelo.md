Quinta de Ventozelo is a big Douro estate near Ervedosa do Douro, spread over terraces that look down toward the river. It is useful for travellers who want more than a tasting room: the estate landscape, old tracks, and vineyard slopes show how hard the Douro has been worked.

Check current visitor options before going, as access can vary by season and booking. Even a short stop helps connect São João da Pesqueira to the wider wine country around it.
