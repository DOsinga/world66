São Salvador do Mundo is one of the most rewarding viewpoints near São João da Pesqueira. From the ridge, the Douro appears below in tight bends, with vineyards and pale rock cutting across the slopes.

It is a simple stop, but it does exactly what a Douro viewpoint should do: it turns the region from a wine label into a landscape you can read.
