---
title: "Bars and Cafes"
type: section
---

Setúbal's nightlife is concentrated along Avenida Luísa Todi, the long riverfront boulevard, and around Praça do Bocage in the centre. Crowds skew local — this is not a tourist resort and the bars don't pretend to be one. The pedestrianised street Rua Antão Girão has a string of small bars that get busy after 11pm on weekends. For a quieter drink, the kiosks (quiosques) along the Doca do Comércio waterfront serve cold beer and wine into the evening with views of the boats.
