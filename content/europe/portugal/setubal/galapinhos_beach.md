Galapinhos Beach is one of the prettiest coves in the Arrabida coast near Setubal, tucked below steep green hills with clear blue-green water. Its small size is part of the beauty, but also the reason it can feel crowded in summer.

Access may involve seasonal traffic controls, shuttle arrangements, or a walk down from the road. Plan ahead, bring water, and go early if you want the beach at its best rather than at its busiest.
