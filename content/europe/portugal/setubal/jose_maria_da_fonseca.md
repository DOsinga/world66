José Maria da Fonseca is one of the classic names of the Setúbal Peninsula, especially for Moscatel de Setúbal, the sweet fortified wine that has long been the region's calling card. The estate in Azeitão combines old cellars, family history, and tastings in a village that makes an easy excursion from Setúbal.

This is not just a bottle-shop stop. The visit helps make sense of the landscape between Setúbal, the Arrábida hills, and Azeitão, where vineyards, cheese, and old estates are part of the local identity. It works well with a slow lunch in Azeitão or a wider day around Arrábida.
