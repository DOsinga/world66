The Roman Ruins of Tróia preserve part of one of the largest fish-salting complexes known from the Roman Empire. The site, occupied until the 6th century, helps explain why this quiet peninsula mattered long before modern resorts arrived.

Walk through the tanks, walls, and excavated structures to get a sense of the scale of the ancient industry across the Sado estuary from Setúbal.
