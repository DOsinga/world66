This small interpretation centre explains the resident bottlenose dolphins of the Sado Estuary, one of the few estuary-dwelling dolphin communities in Europe. It is a useful stop before a boat trip or a walk along Setúbal's waterfront, giving some context for the animals, the estuary, and the pressures on this rare habitat.
