---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

May, June, and September are the best months for Setúbal: warm enough for the beaches in the Arrábida, calmer weather for the dolphin trips, and the heat short of the August broiler. July and August are hot and busy — Portuguese families descend on the Sado coast and parking in the centre becomes a problem.

Winters are mild but wet. December through February can deliver pleasant sunny days as easily as a week of rain. The Festas de São Pedro in late June celebrate the city's patron saint and the fishing industry with processions, concerts, and grilled sardines along the waterfront — it is the loudest and best time to be in town if you can stand the crowds.
