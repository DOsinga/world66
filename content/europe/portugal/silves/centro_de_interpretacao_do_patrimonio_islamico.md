The Centro de Interpretacao do Patrimonio Islamico de Silves helps put the town's Moorish past into focus. Silves was one of the most important Islamic cities in the western Algarve, and the center gives visitors context for the castle, walls, archaeology, and urban layout that still shape the old town.

It is a compact stop rather than a major museum, but useful before climbing to the castle or visiting the municipal archaeology museum. The value is in connecting the visible monuments to the centuries when Silves was a fortified, Arabic-speaking river city tied to trade and scholarship.
