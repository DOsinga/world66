The museum is the best indoor stop in Silves, especially after you have walked the castle walls. It is built around a deep Almohad-era cistern that was discovered during archaeological work, so the building itself tells part of the town's story.

The collection moves from prehistoric material through Roman, Islamic, and medieval Silves. It is compact rather than overwhelming, and it gives useful context for why this quiet inland town mattered so much when it was Moorish Xelb.
