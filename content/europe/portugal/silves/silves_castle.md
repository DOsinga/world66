Silves Castle dominates the town from its hilltop, a red-sandstone fortress that makes the Algarve's Islamic past feel immediate. The walls are largely Moorish in character, with massive ramparts, towers, cisterns, and views over the orange roofs of Silves and the surrounding countryside.

It is the town's essential sight. Walk the walls for the perspective, then leave time for the archaeological museum and cathedral nearby; together they explain why quiet inland Silves once mattered far more than its size today suggests.
