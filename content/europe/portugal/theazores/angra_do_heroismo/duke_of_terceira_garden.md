Duke of Terceira Garden is a manicured green break in the middle of Angra do Heroísmo. Its terraces, paths, and dense planting reflect the Romantic taste of nineteenth-century Portuguese gardens, with enough shade to make it useful as well as pretty. It is an easy stop while walking between the old centre's churches, squares, and viewpoints.
