The Nature Park of Terceira is the island-wide framework that protects many of the landscapes around Angra do Heroísmo: volcanic cones, lava tubes, coastal cliffs, wetlands, and pockets of native laurel forest. It is not a single fenced park, but a network of reserves and geosites that explain why Terceira is more than its handsome UNESCO-listed town.

Use Angra as a base and pick one or two park areas rather than trying to "do" the whole thing. Algar do Carvão, Serra de Santa Bárbara, and the island's walking trails are the obvious starting points for seeing the Azorean mix of pasture, cloud, and volcanic rock.
