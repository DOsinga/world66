Farol da Ponta do Arnel stands on a steep cliff near Nordeste, marking one of Sao Miguel's wilder eastern corners. The lighthouse itself is modest, but the setting is the draw: Atlantic water below, green slopes above, and a road that makes you respect the island's gradients.

Go for the view rather than a long visit. The walk down is steep and the return is harder, so it is best treated as a short scenic detour on a drive around the east of the island.
