A Arruda Pineapple Plantation shows how Sao Miguel's small, intensely sweet pineapples are grown under glass. It is an easy stop from Ponta Delgada, with a short look through the greenhouses and a shop for pineapple liqueur, jam, and other island-made souvenirs.
