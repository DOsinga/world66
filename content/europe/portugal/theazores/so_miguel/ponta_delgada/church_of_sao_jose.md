The Church of Sao Jose stands on Campo de Sao Francisco, one of Ponta Delgada's most useful squares for understanding the old city. Its broad facade and dark basalt detailing are unmistakably Azorean, while the interior holds the gilded woodwork and devotional side chapels typical of major Portuguese island churches.

It is easy to visit while walking between the waterfront, the fort, and the historic centre. The church works best as part of that circuit: step inside for the scale and ornament, then linger outside in the square where religious, military, and civic Ponta Delgada overlap.
