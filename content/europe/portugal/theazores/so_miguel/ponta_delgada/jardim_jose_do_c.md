A formal garden dating from the late 19th century, planted by José do Canto — an Azorean aristocrat and botanist with a passion for exotic species. The garden is modest in size but impressive in variety: tree ferns, camellias, magnolias, and a collection of conifers gathered from around the world. In the centre, a small lake with waterlilies and ducks. It is the calmest corner of the city and a good place to sit in the shade on a warm afternoon.
