Ribeira Grande is the second largest town on [São Miguel](/europe/portugal/theazores/so_miguel), set on the island's north coast about 15 minutes by car from [Ponta Delgada](/europe/portugal/theazores/so_miguel/ponta_delgada). The town has a handsome historic centre with baroque churches and manor houses, centred on a striking [eight-arched bridge](/europe/portugal/theazores/so_miguel/ribeira_grande/ponte_dos_oito_arcos) over the Ribeira Grande river, with the [Igreja Matriz](/europe/portugal/theazores/so_miguel/ribeira_grande/igreja_matriz) and the basalt-arcaded [city hall](/europe/portugal/theazores/so_miguel/ribeira_grande/city_hall) anchoring the small square nearby.

The north coast here is wilder and less touristy than the south. Ribeira Grande is a good base for visiting the [Sete Cidades](/europe/portugal/theazores/so_miguel/sete_cidades) crater lakes to the west, the [tea plantations at Gorreana](/europe/portugal/theazores/so_miguel/ribeira_grande/cha_gorreana) (the only tea produced in Europe), and the [hot springs at Caldeira Velha](/europe/portugal/theazores/so_miguel/ribeira_grande/caldeira_velha). The dark-sand surf beach at [Santa Bárbara](/europe/portugal/theazores/so_miguel/ribeira_grande/santa_barbara_beach) is a couple of kilometres west of town, and the cliff-edge [Miradouro de Santa Iria](/europe/portugal/theazores/so_miguel/ribeira_grande/miradouro_santa_iria) is fifteen minutes east along the coast road.
