The smaller of São Miguel's two surviving tea plantations, in Porto Formoso about twenty minutes east of Ribeira Grande along the north-coast road. Where Gorreana is the larger working factory, Porto Formoso plays the heritage card — restored stone buildings, a short guided tour through the rolling and drying rooms, and a small museum tracing the brief history of Azorean tea.

The free tasting at the end runs through both green and black grades; the green is the better of the two. Combine it with a stop at Chá Gorreana a few minutes further west for a half-day tea run along the north coast, or end the morning at the Miradouro de Santa Iria viewpoint just inland.
