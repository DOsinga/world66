---
title: "Getting There"
type: section
---

There is no airport at Ribeira Grande. Almost everyone arrives by road from **João Paulo II Airport (PDL)** in Ponta Delgada, fifteen kilometres south across the island. Direct flights to PDL run year-round from Lisbon, Porto, and Madeira (TAP and SATA), seasonally from several mainland European cities, and from Boston and the US East Coast on Azores Airlines.

From the airport, a **rental car** is by far the most practical option — the drive over the central ridge to Ribeira Grande takes about twenty-five minutes, and you will want a car for everything outside town anyway. The major rental agencies have desks in the arrivals hall. A **taxi** to Ribeira Grande costs around €25–€30.

If you do not want to drive, the **Linha de Costa Norte** bus run by Auto Viação Micaelense connects Ponta Delgada with Ribeira Grande several times a day (about 45 minutes, a couple of euros). It is fine for getting between the two towns but useless for sights spread along the north coast — assume you will need to combine bus, taxi, and walking, or just rent a car.
