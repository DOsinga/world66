---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

The Azores have mild temperatures year-round — rarely above 25°C in summer, rarely below 14°C in winter — and a lot of rain, especially on the north coast where Ribeira Grande sits. Plan for sun and showers in the same day in any season.

**June to September** is the driest, warmest stretch and the best time for the beach at Santa Bárbara and for hiking. **April and May** are wetter but the island is at its greenest, the hydrangeas are flowering on the roadsides, and visitor numbers are lower. **Winter** brings the big Atlantic swell that draws serious surfers to the north coast, and the rest of the island is quiet and cheap.

The town's main celebration is the **Festa do Senhor Santo Cristo dos Milagres** in Ponta Delgada, a fifteen-minute drive away, on the fifth Sunday after Easter — the biggest religious festival in the Azores and worth timing a visit around if you can.
