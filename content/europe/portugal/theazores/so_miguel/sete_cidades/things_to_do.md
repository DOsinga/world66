---
title: Things to Do
type: section
---

Sete Cidades is a place to walk and look. The twin lakes and the crater rim viewpoints are the reason people come. A half-day is enough to see the lakes, drive the rim, and stop at the main miradouros — but a full day with one of the trails is better. The most popular routes are the Mata do Canário rim hike (down from Vista do Rei) and the lakeside loop around Lagoa Azul. Weather is fickle: the crater traps cloud, and a view that is perfect at sunrise can vanish by mid-morning.
