The Castle of Torres Vedras rises above the old town with a mix of medieval walls, church architecture, and later military use. It was folded into the wider Lines of Torres Vedras, so it links the town's medieval and Napoleonic stories in one compact stop.

Go mainly for context and the view back over the roofs and ridges. It pairs naturally with the Fort of Sao Vicente.
