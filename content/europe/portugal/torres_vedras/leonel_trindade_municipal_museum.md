The Leonel Trindade Municipal Museum gives Torres Vedras its depth. Inside the former Convento da Graca, the collections move from prehistoric archaeology to sacred art and the story of the Lines of Torres Vedras.

It is especially useful before or after Castro do Zambujal, since many finds from the wider municipality are interpreted here rather than out at the archaeological site itself.
