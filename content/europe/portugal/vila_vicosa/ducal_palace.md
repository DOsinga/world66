The Ducal Palace is the reason to come to Vila Viçosa. Built for the House of Braganza from the early 1500s, it stretches across the square in a long marble facade, with state rooms, tiles, frescoes, armory collections, and the heavy atmosphere of a family that eventually became Portugal's royal dynasty.
