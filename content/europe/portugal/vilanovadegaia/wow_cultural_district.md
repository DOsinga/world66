WOW Cultural District is a museum and leisure complex above the Gaia waterfront. Its museums cover wine, chocolate, cork, Porto and the north of Portugal, drinking vessels, and the Pink Palace experience devoted to rose wine.

It is easy to combine with Gaia's port lodges, but it is more polished and modern than the older cellar tours.
