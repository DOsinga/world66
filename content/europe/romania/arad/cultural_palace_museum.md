Arad's Cultural Palace is an imposing early-20th-century complex mixing neoclassical, Gothic, and Renaissance Revival elements. It houses museum collections covering fine arts, natural sciences, and local history.

The building itself is part of the appeal, and it fits well into a walk along Arad's main boulevard of Austro-Hungarian-era architecture.
