Mureș Floodplain Natural Park protects a long ribbon of wetland, gallery forest, islands, and river channels along the Mureș near Arad. It is one of the area's strongest outdoor options, especially for birding, quiet trails, and seeing the river landscape that shaped the city.

The park rewards a slower visit than a simple viewpoint stop. Canoe routes, nature trails, and seasonal wildlife make it a good choice for travelers who want a natural counterweight to Arad's architecture and central boulevards.
