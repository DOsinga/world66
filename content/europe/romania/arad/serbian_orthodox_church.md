The Serbian Orthodox Saint Peter and Paul Church is one of Arad's oldest surviving churches, built around 1700 and still preserving its Baroque form. Its high steeple and long Serbian Orthodox history make it a worthwhile stop on a walk through the older parts of the city.

It is best visited as part of a short circuit linking the theatre, the administrative palace, and the Mures riverside.
