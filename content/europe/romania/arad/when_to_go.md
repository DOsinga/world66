---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

Arad is pleasant from April through October. The city sits in the flat Pannonian plain, so summers are hot (often over 30°C) and winters cold but not severe. Spring and early autumn are the most comfortable seasons for walking around the city centre.

There are no major festivals that should drive timing, though the Timișoara area — an hour away — has a more active cultural calendar. The Minis-Maderat wine region to the east holds harvest events in September, a good excuse for a day trip if you are visiting in autumn.
