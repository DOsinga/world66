A welcoming spot that leans into the Maramureș folk aesthetic — wooden interior, regional artwork, staff who can explain what things are. The food is hearty and local: mămăligă with cheese and sour cream, smoked pork specialities, bean soups. Good value. Busy at weekends with locals, which is the right kind of busy.
