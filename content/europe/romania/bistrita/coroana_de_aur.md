The Coroana de Aur is Bistrița's literary tourist trap, and on its own terms a perfectly decent one. Built in the 1970s on the supposed site of the inn Bram Stoker invented for the opening of Dracula, the hotel restaurant serves Romanian classics and the Stoker-menu trio of robber steak, mămăligă with cheese, and paprika hendl. Prices are reasonable and the dining room is unfussy.
