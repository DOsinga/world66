Restaurants near the castle entrance serve Transylvanian staples: ciorbă de burtă (tripe soup), mititei (grilled minced meat rolls), sarmale (stuffed cabbage leaves), and mămăligă (polenta). Several places have terrace seating with views toward the castle. Quality varies — look for places busy with locals rather than the ones with laminated picture menus right by the gate.
