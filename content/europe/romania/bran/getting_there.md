---
title: "Getting There"
type: section
---

From Brașov, buses leave from Autogara 2 (near the train station) roughly every 30 minutes; the journey takes about 45 minutes and is very cheap. By car, Bran is 28km south of Brașov on the DN73 — a straightforward drive. Taxis and rideshares from Brașov are also practical. Brașov itself is 2.5 hours from Bucharest by direct train, making Bran doable as a day trip from the capital.
