---
title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Bran's main draw is the castle itself, but there is more to do in the village and the surrounding mountains. The castle museum covers both the Dracula legend and the genuine royal history, while the market outside the gates is a good place to pick up local food products and crafts. The Bucegi Mountains and the Piatra Craiului ridge offer serious hiking for those who want to spend more than a day in the area.
