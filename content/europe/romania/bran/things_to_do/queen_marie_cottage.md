A wooden cottage in the village that served as Queen Marie's private retreat, separate from the main castle. Smaller and more intimate than the castle museum, it gives a different perspective on the royal connection to Bran — less the grand residence, more the personal escape. Worth a look if you are interested in the Queen Marie story that runs through so much of this area.
