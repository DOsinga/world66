Bistro de l'Arte has run for years from a small space behind Piata Sfatului and is the steady favourite among Brasov-resident expats and food-minded locals. The cooking sits between French and Romanian — duck rillettes, smoked-trout tartare, beef Wellington — with a short, well-edited wine list heavy on Romanian producers.

The room is small (booking essential) and the service unhurried. Lunch menus are good value; dinner is more of a destination. They host occasional small classical concerts on Sunday evenings.
