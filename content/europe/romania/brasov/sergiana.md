Sergiana, just off Strada Muresenilor, is the place to eat the heavy, smoky, paprika-coloured food of Transylvania. The dining room is in a vaulted cellar with long wooden tables; portions are large; servers wear folk-style aprons. The signature dishes are bulz (smoked-cheese-and-polenta dumpling, usually with sour cream), ciolan (slow-cooked pork knuckle) and various stews served in a bread bowl.

It is the kind of place that locals say is touristy but where you will see plenty of Romanians anyway. Reservations are wise on weekend evenings. Wines from the Recas vineyards are good and not expensive.
