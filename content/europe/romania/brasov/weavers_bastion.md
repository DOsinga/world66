Weavers' Bastion is one of the best-preserved parts of Brasov's old fortifications. The thick-walled courtyard and timber galleries give a clear sense of how the walled Saxon town defended itself.

A small museum inside displays old weapons and a large model of 17th-century Brasov, which helps make sense of the gates, towers, and walls that still shape the edge of the old town.
