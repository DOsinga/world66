Bucharest's Aviation Museum is a specialist museum, but a good one for travellers interested in aircraft and Cold War-era technology. The collection includes military planes, helicopters, engines, uniforms, and exhibits on Romanian aviation history.

It sits outside the most obvious central museum circuit, so it is best treated as a targeted stop. If you are already interested in aviation, the aircraft and technical displays make it far more rewarding than its low-key profile suggests.
