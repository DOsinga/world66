Cotroceni Palace Museum opens the historic wing of one of Bucharest's most important palace complexes. The visit moves through decorated rooms, royal interiors, and architectural layers that connect monastic foundations, royal residence, and modern Romanian state history.

Because the wider Cotroceni complex is also associated with the presidency, access is more structured than at many museums and usually requires following the museum's visiting rules. For travelers interested in Bucharest's royal and political past, it is one of the city's strongest guided interior visits.
