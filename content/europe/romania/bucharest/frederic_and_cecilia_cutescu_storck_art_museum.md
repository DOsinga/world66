The Frederic and Cecilia Cuțescu-Storck Art Museum is one of Bucharest’s rewarding smaller museums, set in a house tied to an important Romanian artist family. It brings together sculpture, painting, domestic space, and the story of Cecilia Cuțescu-Storck, a pioneering woman artist and muralist.

The museum is quieter than Bucharest’s major national collections, which is part of its charm. Visit when you want a more intimate art stop and a glimpse of the city’s early 20th-century creative circles rather than another palace-scale institution.
