The National Geology Museum is one of the more approachable museums around Bucharests Piata Victoriei. Its displays cover minerals, rocks, fossils, and the geological forces behind Romanias mountains, oil fields, salt, and mineral wealth.

It pairs neatly with the nearby natural history and peasant museums, especially for families or science-minded travelers. The presentation can feel old-school, but the specimens are strong and the subject is a good fit for Romania.
