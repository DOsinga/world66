Grigore Antipa Natural History Museum is a natural history museum that adds useful depth to a visit to Bucharest. It is a good stop when you want the local story told through objects, buildings, art, machinery, or archaeology rather than another broad city overview.

Allow enough time to read the displays instead of treating it as a rain-plan filler. The strongest reason to come is context: after visiting, the surrounding streets and monuments in Bucharest make more sense, and smaller details are easier to notice.
