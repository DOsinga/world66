The Museum of Mary and Dr. G. Severeanu is a small historic-house museum with an unusually rich private collection. Its rooms display Greek vases, Tanagra figurines, Roman glass, coins, and other archaeological pieces gathered by one of Bucharest's early 20th-century collectors.
