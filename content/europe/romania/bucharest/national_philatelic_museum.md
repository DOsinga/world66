The National Philatelic Museum is a niche but worthwhile stop for visitors interested in stamps, postal history, and printed ephemera. Its collections include stamp issues, postal boxes, seals, maps, old letters, post-office furniture, and uniforms, giving a compact look at Romania's postal culture.
