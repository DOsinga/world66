Victory Square is one of Bucharest's key civic junctions, where major boulevards meet beside the Romanian government headquarters and several important museums. It is not a square for lingering in the cafe-plaza sense; its interest is urban scale, politics, and orientation.

For travellers it works as a useful hinge between central Bucharest, Calea Victoriei, and the museum quarter around Kiseleff. The space also carries modern political associations, as demonstrations and public gatherings often use it as a focal point.
