Baracca is among the most ambitious restaurants in Cluj — an elegant dining room a short walk from Piața Unirii, with an open kitchen and a menu that takes Transylvanian and Romanian ingredients in a modern direction. Tasting menus and à la carte options both work well.

The chef-driven approach focuses on local ingredients — wild mushrooms, freshwater fish, Transylvanian charcuterie. Wine is taken seriously, with a strong selection of Romanian wines from less-known regions. A reservation is essential, and prices remain modest by European standards for cooking at this level.
