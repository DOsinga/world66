Bob Church was the first Greek-Catholic church built in Cluj while the city was part of the Austrian Empire. Its central location and compact form make it an easy stop on a walk through the old city.

The church is more modest than Cluj's major Gothic and Baroque landmarks, but it adds an important piece to the city's religious and imperial history.
