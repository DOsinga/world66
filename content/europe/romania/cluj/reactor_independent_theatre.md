Reactor Independent Theatre is part of Cluj's contemporary cultural scene rather than a traditional monument. Its programme leans toward independent theatre, new writing, experimental performance, and small-scale productions that show a different side of the city from its squares and churches.

It is most useful for visitors staying more than a night or anyone who wants an evening plan with local creative energy. Check the current programme before going, as the value depends on what is actually playing during your visit.
