---
title: "Day Trips"
type: section
---

Constanța is the natural base for exploring the **Dobruja** region and a stretch of the Black Sea coast.

The most rewarding short trip is to the small monastery of **Histria**, 70 km north, the ruins of the oldest Greek colony in present-day Romania, founded in the 7th century BC on the edge of the Razim lagoon. Equally worth a day is **Murfatlar**, 18 km west, the country's most important wine-growing region — most of the larger estates accept visitors with advance notice.

Other day-trip options include the long quiet beaches of **Vama Veche** and **2 Mai** on the Bulgarian border (south, 60 km — book a car), the cliff-top monastery and bird sanctuary of **Capul Caliacra** just over the border in Bulgaria, and a boat trip to the **Danube Delta** from Tulcea (140 km north, requires an overnight).
