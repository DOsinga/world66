---
title: "Eating Out"
type: section
---

Constanța eats a mix of Romanian, Greek, Turkish and Tatar — the inheritance of a Black Sea port. Fish (sturgeon, *guvizi*, mackerel) and *saramură* (grilled fish with a brine and chilli marinade) appear on most local menus. Tatar *plăcintă* (savoury pastries) and *suberek* (fried meat pies) are sold on every other street corner of the old town. The wines of **Murfatlar**, the celebrated vineyard inland from the city, are widely available and reward exploration.
