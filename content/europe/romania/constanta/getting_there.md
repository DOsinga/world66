---
title: "Getting There"
type: section
---

**By air.** Mihail Kogălniceanu International Airport (CND) sits 25 km north of the city, with seasonal flights from across Europe (Wizz Air, Ryanair, TAROM) plus year-round connections to Bucharest. Buses run from the airport to the city centre in about forty-five minutes; taxis are inexpensive.

**By train.** **Gara Constanța** is on the main line from **Bucharest**, with around eight daily trains. The fastest InterCity services take about two-and-a-half hours. The station is 2 km west of the old town — bus 100 or a short taxi covers the gap.

**By bus and minibus.** Long-distance buses run from Bucharest, Brașov and several Bulgarian cities. The main bus terminal is opposite the railway station.

**By car.** The A2 motorway connects Bucharest to Constanța — about 230 km of mostly-good road, two and a half hours in light traffic, considerably more on summer Friday evenings and Sunday afternoons when half of Bucharest drives down to the coast.
