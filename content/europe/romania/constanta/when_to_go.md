---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

The summer season on the Black Sea coast runs roughly from **mid-June to mid-September** — warm sea (22–25°C), reliable sunshine, busy beaches, prices doubled at Mamaia. The peak is the first three weeks of August, when the Romanian summer holidays concentrate the crowds; if you want the beach but not the crowds, aim for **June** or **September**.

The old town is at its best in **late spring (May–early June)** and **early autumn (September–October)** — warm enough to sit outside, far quieter, and the Casino and Aquarium are open without the long queues. Winters are cold, windy and grey; the city is by no means closed, but you will mostly have it to yourself. The **Limanul** Mamaia music festival in late August and the **George Enescu Festival** events that occasionally come to the Casino are worth timing for.
