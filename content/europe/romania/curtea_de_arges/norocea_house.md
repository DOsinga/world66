Norocea House is a small memorial museum connected with Dumitru Norocea, the painter and restorer associated with the frescoes of Curtea de Arges Monastery. The house gives a quieter, more personal counterpoint to the royal tombs and church architecture that dominate most visits to town.

It is not a blockbuster museum, but it helps explain the craft and conservation behind one of Romania's most important religious monuments. Stop if you are already visiting the monastery area and want a more local layer to the story.
