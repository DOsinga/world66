Galați is a port city on the Danube in eastern [Romania](/europe/romania), close to the border with Moldova and Ukraine. With around 250,000 people it is one of Romania's larger cities, though it rarely features on tourist itineraries. The massive ArcelorMittal steelworks — formerly SIDEX, once the largest in southeastern Europe — still dominates the skyline and the local economy.

The city's position on the lower Danube gives it a rough charm. The waterfront promenade — the [Faleza](/europe/romania/galati/faleza_danube_promenade) — has been modernised, and the old centre along Domnească Street has a scattering of 19th-century buildings, the fortified [Sfânta Precista church](/europe/romania/galati/sfanta_precista_church), the [neo-Byzantine cathedral](/europe/romania/galati/cathedral_st_nicholas), and a surprisingly good [Museum of Visual Arts](/europe/romania/galati/visual_arts_museum). The "Dunărea de Jos" University brings student life to the city. Galați can serve as a base for exploring the Danube Delta, which begins not far downstream — though most travellers head to [Tulcea](/europe/romania/tulcea) for that.
