---
title: "Bars and Cafes"
type: section
---

Café life in Galați happens along the Faleza in summer and on the streets around Domnească Street and the university in winter. The student population keeps prices low and the places lively until late. For a drink with a view, the terraces on the upper Faleza beat anything else in town; for a quieter evening, head to one of the small wine bars opening in restored 19th-century houses near Eminescu Park.
