---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

Galați sits in one of the hotter and drier corners of Romania. Summer (June to August) is the season for the Faleza — long evenings, river breeze, restaurants spilling onto the promenade — but afternoons regularly hit 35°C and humidity off the Danube can be heavy. May and September are kinder: 20–25°C, fewer mosquitoes, the river still lively.

Winters are sharp. Temperatures drop well below freezing, the Danube sometimes ices over near the banks, and a wind off the Russian steppe (the crivăț) bites through anything you wear. There is little reason to visit between November and March unless you have specific business in the city.
