A straightforward bar in the castle precinct area, Cetatii draws a mix of late-afternoon visitors and Hunedoara locals who work in the town centre. Ursus and Timisoreana on tap, simple snacks, and no pretensions. The interior is plain and cool on a hot afternoon; in the evenings it becomes more of a local neighbourhood bar than a tourist stop. If you have time to kill before a bus or train back to Deva, this is a comfortable enough place to sit with a beer.
