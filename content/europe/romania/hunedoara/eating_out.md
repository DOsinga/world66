---
title: Eating Out
type: section
---

The eating options near Corvin Castle are simple and unpretentious — a couple of Romanian restaurants catering mainly to day-trippers. Expect hearty local cooking: pork dishes, sarmale (stuffed cabbage rolls), and ciorba (sour soup). Nothing destination-worthy, but solid and affordable. If you are here for the castle and moving on the same day, both options below will serve you well.
