---
title: When to Go
type: section
---

**Spring and early summer** (April through June) are the best months to visit Hunedoara. The castle grounds are at their most atmospheric with greenery filling in around the rock outcrop, queues are manageable, and the weather is warm enough for the outdoor battlements and towers without the full heat of July. Autumn (September and October) is nearly as good — the crowds thin after the school holidays and the surrounding hills take on colour.

**July and August** see the heaviest tourist traffic, particularly at weekends when day-trippers from Cluj-Napoca and Timișoara fill the castle. Arrive early (at opening) or late in the afternoon to avoid the worst of it. The castle is at its most visually dramatic in summer with clear skies, but the main courtyard can feel crowded.

**Winter** is quieter but the castle is less rewarding — shorter opening hours, some of the towers are closed, and Hunedoara itself offers little else to fill a day. The combination of winter weather and the industrial city around the castle makes it a harder sell.

There are no major festivals timed to the castle, though the municipality occasionally organises medieval-themed events in summer; check the castle website or local listings before visiting if that appeals.
