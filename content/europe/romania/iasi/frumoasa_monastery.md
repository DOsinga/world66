Frumoasa Monastery is a quiet Orthodox monastery in the Nicolina district, south of central Iași. Its metal-roofed church and enclosed grounds make a calm stop away from the better-known churches in the old centre.

It is best visited as a short detour rather than a main outing. The appeal is the contrast between the historic monastery compound and the surrounding communist-era apartment blocks.
