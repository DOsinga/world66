---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

Iași has a continental climate with cold winters and warm summers. Spring (April–May) and early autumn (September–October) are the most pleasant times to visit — mild temperatures, green parks, and the cultural season in full swing at the theatres and opera.

The feast day of Saint Parascheva (14 October) brings massive Orthodox pilgrimages to the city — hundreds of thousands of people arrive, queues to the Metropolitan Cathedral stretch for kilometres, and accommodation books out well in advance. Either plan specifically around this event or avoid the dates entirely.

Summer can be hot, and the university quarter quiets down when students leave, but the museums and major sights remain open. Winter is cold but the city functions normally; Christmas markets and events appear in December.
