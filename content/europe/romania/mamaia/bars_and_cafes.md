---
title: "Bars and Cafes"
type: section
---

Mamaia has one of the liveliest nightlife scenes on the Romanian coast — it is the country's Ibiza equivalent, for better or worse. The promenade comes alive after dark in July and August with outdoor clubs, beach bars, and DJ sets. It is loud, packed, and genuinely fun if that is what you are after. Those seeking something quieter should stick to the lakeside bars.
