---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

Mamaia is a seasonal resort. It is effectively closed from October to April — many restaurants and hotels shut entirely, and the beach is deserted. The season runs from roughly May to September.

**June and September** are the ideal months: warm enough to swim, far less crowded than summer, and prices significantly lower. June sees the Black Sea at about 20°C, warm enough for most swimmers. September is arguably the best month — the sea retains summer warmth (24–26°C), the crowds have thinned after the school year starts, and the light is excellent.

**July and August** are the main season. The beach fills up, prices spike, and accommodation must be booked weeks in advance. The upside is that everything is open and the resort is at full energy. If you are coming for the nightlife, this is the time.

**May** can be cool and the sea is cold (15–18°C), but the resort is very quiet, cheap, and pleasant for walking. Some facilities are still opening up for the season.
