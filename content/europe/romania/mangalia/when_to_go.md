---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

The spa treatments and year-round sulphurous mud baths make Mangalia unusual among Black Sea resorts — it has a genuine off-season visitor base seeking therapeutic cures. The spa clinics operate throughout the year, and some visitors prefer the quieter autumn and spring seasons when prices drop significantly.

For beaches, July and August are the obvious choice: the Black Sea is warm and the coast is animated. Mangalia's beach is less crowded than the resort towns to the north like Mamaia, which is part of its appeal. The spring wildflowers on the coast between Mangalia and the Bulgarian border are worth seeking out in April and May.
