Harghita Băi (Hargitafürdő in Hungarian) is a small spa and ski resort about 30 kilometres west of Miercurea Ciuc, set in the volcanic Harghita Mountains at 1,300 metres altitude. The setting is pine forest and meadow with mineral springs that have been bottled and used in bath treatments since the 19th century.

For visitors, the resort is a low-key day trip — a couple of beginner ski lifts in winter, mountain walking trails in summer, a few small pensions and a single open-air mineral pool. It is more popular with Hungarian-speaking Transylvanian families than with international tourists, which is part of its appeal. The drive from Miercurea Ciuc takes about 45 minutes on partly unpaved roads; rentals are available.
