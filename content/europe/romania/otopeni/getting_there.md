---
title: Getting There
type: section
---

Otopeni is served by Henri Coandă International Airport, Romania's main international gateway. Express trains (Henri Coandă Express) run from the airport to Gara de Nord in central Bucharest every 40 minutes, taking about 22–25 minutes. Tickets cost around 8 lei and are available at airport ticket machines or at the train desk in arrivals.

The 780 airport express bus also runs to central Bucharest (Piața Unirii) throughout the day and night, taking about 40–50 minutes depending on traffic. Taxis from the airport to central Bucharest cost 50–80 lei; use only taxis booked through the official airport taxi desk or ride-hailing apps such as Bolt or Uber to avoid overcharging.
