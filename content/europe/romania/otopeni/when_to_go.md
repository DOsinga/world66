---
title: When to Go
type: section
---

Because Otopeni functions primarily as an airport town, there is no bad time to visit in terms of local conditions — you are likely passing through rather than choosing it as a destination. That said, the broader Bucharest region has a continental climate with warm summers (June to August, often reaching 30°C) and cold winters (December to February, with snow not uncommon). Spring and autumn are mild and pleasant for the short trips to Snagov Lake or Băneasa Forest that make up Otopeni's main visitor appeal.

Summer weekends see Bucharest families out at Snagov Lake in numbers, which can make the lakeshore feel festive but also crowded. If you want a quieter visit to the monastery, a weekday in May, June, or September is ideal. The airport operates year-round without seasonal disruption, and the express train to Bucharest runs regardless of weather.
