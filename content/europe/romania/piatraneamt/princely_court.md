The Curtea Domnească (Princely Court) is a 15th-century royal complex at the heart of Piatra Neamț's old town. The complex includes the Church of Saint John the Baptist, built by Ștefan cel Mare in 1497–1498, and the Princely Tower (Turnul lui Ștefan), a belfry tower from the same period. Both are among the best-preserved examples of Moldavian medieval architecture.

The Church of Saint John the Baptist exemplifies the distinctive Moldavian ecclesiastical style that emerged under Ștefan: a blend of Byzantine plan with Gothic elements in the window tracery and portal sculpture, and an exterior decorated with painted religious scenes that would later be developed, in Bukovina, into the world-famous exterior murals. The interior retains original carved stonework.
