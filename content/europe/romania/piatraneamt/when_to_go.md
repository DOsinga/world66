---
title: When to Go
type: section
---

Piatra Neamț is accessible year-round and has something to offer in every season. The city's position in a mountain amphitheatre means dramatic weather in all directions — sudden mists rolling off the peaks, brilliant autumn colours, deep snow in winter.

Late spring (May–June) and early autumn (September–October) are the most pleasant for visiting: the mountain light is good, the monastery gardens are at their best, and the hiking in the surrounding peaks is excellent without the heat of July and August.

Summer (July–August) is the peak tourist season for the wider Neamț region, particularly for visitors to the painted monasteries of Bukovina in Suceava County. The Durău ski resort opens in winter, usually from December through March, for skiing and snowboarding — a small resort but adequate for a few days on the slopes. The Bicaz Gorge can be icy and dangerous in winter, so check conditions before driving the mountain road.
