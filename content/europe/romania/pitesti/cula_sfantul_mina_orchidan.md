Cula Sfantul Mina - Orchidan is a late-19th-century estate complex with two substantial buildings, including a former wine cellar that preserves the feel of a rural boyar property. It is a niche stop, but its architecture gives a useful contrast to central Pitesti's more modern cityscape.
