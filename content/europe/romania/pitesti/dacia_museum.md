The Dacia car factory is the single largest employer in Pitești and one of the most significant industrial sites in Romania. Founded in 1968 as a Renault licensee, it survived communism, was acquired by Renault in 1999, and now produces the Logan and Sandero models sold across Europe and beyond. The factory is not open to casual visitors, but the brand's story is woven into the fabric of the city.

A small museum celebrating the history of the Dacia brand and its cars — from the communist-era models to the modern lineup — is available to visit with advance arrangement through the company. For anyone interested in industrial history, Eastern European automotive culture, or the story of how a communist state factory was transformed into one of Europe's most competitive budget car manufacturers, it's a fascinating detour.
