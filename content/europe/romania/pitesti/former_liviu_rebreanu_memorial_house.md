This early 20th-century house is associated with Liviu Rebreanu, one of Romania's major novelists. He bought the property in 1930, wrote the novel *Rascoala* here, and died here in 1944.

It is a modest literary stop rather than a major museum, but it adds useful context if you are tracing Romanian cultural history around Pitesti.
