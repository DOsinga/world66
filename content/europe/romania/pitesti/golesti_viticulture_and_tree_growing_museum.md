The Golești museum is one of the more interesting excursions from Pitești because it connects rural architecture with the agricultural life of Argeș county. Traditional houses, wine-making objects, orchard tools, and estate buildings turn what could be a narrow specialist museum into a broader look at Wallachian village life.

Give it more time than a city museum. The appeal is in wandering between buildings, noticing how households, storage spaces, and farm work fitted together. It is especially useful if your Romanian trip is otherwise heavy on monasteries, castles, and city centres.
