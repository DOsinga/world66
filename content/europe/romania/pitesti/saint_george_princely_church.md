Saint George Princely Church is one of Pitesti's oldest churches, built in 1656 by Wallachian prince Constantin Serban and Doamna Balasa. Its age and princely patronage make it a more significant stop than the small exterior first suggests.

The church is most interesting for visitors following old Wallachian religious architecture. Combine it with a walk through the central historic area.
