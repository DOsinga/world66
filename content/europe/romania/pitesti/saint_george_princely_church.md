Saint George Princely Church is the oldest church in Pitesti, built in 1656 under Wallachian prince Constantin Serban and his wife Balasa. Its compact form and old masonry give it a different character from the larger modern churches elsewhere in the city.

The church is a good stop for travellers interested in Wallachian religious architecture. It rewards a slow look more than a long visit, especially if you are already walking through central Pitesti.
