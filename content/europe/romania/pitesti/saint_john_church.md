Saint John Church is a 16th-century church near Pitești, tied to the old Golești family and to the religious landscape around Nămăiești Monastery. It is modest, but it carries more historical weight than its size suggests.

This is a short stop for travellers already exploring Pitești and the nearby Argeș countryside. Come for the age, the local history, and the quiet setting rather than for elaborate decoration.
