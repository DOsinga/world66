Trivale Park is a large protected natural park on the edge of Pitesti, known for its oak forest, stream valleys, and quiet walking atmosphere. It has been protected as a natural monument since 1939, with vegetation and forest habitats that give it more substance than an ordinary city park.

It is a good break from the centre of Pitesti and pairs well with nearby Trivale Monastery.
