Ploiești's Palace of Culture was built in the early 20th century as a courthouse, and it still has the self-confident neoclassical front you would expect from a serious civic building of that era. Columns, stone detailing and a broad facade make it one of the city's better architectural stops.

Today it serves cultural uses rather than legal ones. Even if you do not go inside, it is worth folding into a walk between Ploiești's museums and central market streets.
