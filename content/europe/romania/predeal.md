Predeal is the highest town in Romania, sitting at about 1,100 metres in the Bucegi Mountains on the border between Transylvania and Wallachia. It is a modest ski resort — smaller and quieter than neighbouring [Sinaia](/europe/romania/sinaia) or [Poiana Brașov](/europe/romania/brasov) — with a handful of runs on the [Clăbucet ski area](/europe/romania/predeal/clabucet_ski_area) suited to beginners and intermediates.

In summer, the surrounding forests and mountain trails make it a pleasant base for hiking. The [Piatra Mare](/europe/romania/predeal/piatra_mare) massif to the north is the most rewarding destination, with trails leading to panoramic limestone plateaus at nearly 1,850 metres. The [Predeal Monastery](/europe/romania/predeal/predeal_monastery), tucked into the forest on the southern edge of town, is a quiet Orthodox complex dating to 1774. The town straddles the main road and railway between [Brașov](/europe/romania/brasov) and [Bucharest](/europe/romania/bucharest), so it is easy to reach and makes a practical overnight stop if you are travelling between the two cities.
