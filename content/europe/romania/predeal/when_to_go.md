---
title: When to Go
type: section
---

Winter runs from December to March and is the busy season — the ski runs are at their best in January and February, when snow cover is reliable and temperatures drop well below freezing. Weekends see significant crowds from Bucharest and Brașov; midweek visits are noticeably quieter.

Summer (June to September) is pleasant for hiking, with mild temperatures and the mountain trails free of snow. July and August bring the occasional afternoon thunderstorm, so start hikes early. Spring and autumn are the quietest periods and the forest colours in October are excellent, though many smaller accommodation options close outside peak seasons.
