---
title: Getting There
type: section
---

Săpânța has no train station and no direct bus service worth relying on. The practical options are:

**By car** — the most flexible approach. From [Sighetu Marmației](/europe/romania/sighetulmarmatiei) it is about 18km on a good road (DN19A), taking 25–30 minutes. From Baia Mare, the regional capital, it is around 90km — roughly 1.5 hours — following the Iza Valley road through some of the most scenic countryside in Maramureș. The drive through villages like Bârsana and Ieud is worth the detour.

**By taxi or hired car** — from Sighetu Marmației, a taxi to Săpânța for a half-day visit (cemetery, monastery, and back) costs around €15–25 depending on waiting time. Negotiate the price before leaving.

**By tour** — several operators in Sighetu Marmației and Baia Mare run day trips that combine Săpânța with other Maramureș highlights: the UNESCO wooden churches of the Iza Valley, the Mara Valley, and the village of Bârsana. This makes sense if you are without a car and want to cover the region efficiently.

**By local bus** — there are occasional minibuses from Sighetu Marmației, but schedules are unreliable and return journeys are not guaranteed. Not recommended unless you have time to spare and a backup plan.
