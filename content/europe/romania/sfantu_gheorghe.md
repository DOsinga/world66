Sfântu Gheorghe, Sepsiszentgyörgy in Hungarian, is the capital of Covasna County and one of the main urban centres of Szeklerland. It sits on the Olt River, between the Baraolt and Bodoc Mountains, close enough to [Brașov](/europe/romania/brasov) to work as a day trip but different enough to feel like a separate cultural world.

The city is mainly interesting for its Szekler Hungarian identity. The [Székely National Museum](/europe/romania/sfantu_gheorghe/szekely_national_museum) is the anchor, both for its collections and for its Károly Kós-designed building. Around the centre are older civic buildings, a fortified church, a theatre with deep Hungarian roots and a relaxed central park.

This is not a town of blockbuster sights. Its value is more specific: if you want to understand the Hungarian-speaking communities of eastern Transylvania, Sfântu Gheorghe is one of the best easy bases.
