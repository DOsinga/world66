Tamási Áron Theatre is one of the cultural landmarks of Sfântu Gheorghe, named after the Szekler-Hungarian writer Áron Tamási. The building has served civic and theatrical roles over time, and today it helps keep the city centre visibly Hungarian in language and culture.

Check listings if you speak Hungarian or are curious about local theatre. Otherwise, treat it as part of the central walk and a reminder that Sfântu Gheorghe's identity is not just architectural.
