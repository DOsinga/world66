The old bazaar and city hall area gives Sfântu Gheorghe its small-city centre: 19th-century civic buildings, a clock tower, cafes and the kind of everyday public space that matters more than any single facade.

It is best seen as a short loop rather than a checklist. Walk it after the museum, then continue toward the park or theatre.
