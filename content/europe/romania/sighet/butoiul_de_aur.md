A noisy, friendly, wood-panelled restaurant a couple of streets back from the main square that does grilled meat, mititei (grilled minced-meat rolls), pickles, polenta with cheese, and the local *palinka* (plum brandy). Service is brisk, prices are local, and the kitchen turns out food at speed. Good for a hungry lunch after a morning in the prison museum.
