The standout day trip from Sighet is the **Merry Cemetery** at [Săpânța](/europe/romania/sapinta), 18 km west — a graveyard of painted blue wooden crosses where every headstone carries a comic, sometimes ribald rhymed verse about the life of the person beneath. South of Sighet, the wooden churches and Maramureș villages along the **Iza Valley** make a long day's drive — [Bârsana](/europe/romania/sighet/barsana_monastery), Budești, Ieud, and Poienile Izei all hold churches on the UNESCO World Heritage list. The road continues east into the Mara Valley with more wooden churches at Mara, Hoteni, and Călinești. For a longer drive, the **Rodna Mountains National Park** and the **Pietrosul Rodnei** peak (2,303 m) are within range for a long day.
