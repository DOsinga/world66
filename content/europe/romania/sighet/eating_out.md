---
title: Eating Out
type: section
---

Sighet eats the way Maramureș cooks — generously and with attention to seasonal ingredients. The local classics are *sarmale* (cabbage rolls with mince), *tochitură* (pork stew with polenta and a fried egg on top), *bulz* (polenta stuffed with cheese and grilled), smoked sausages, soft sheep cheese, and trout from the mountain streams. The plum brandy *palinka* (also called *horinca* here) is the everyday digestif. A couple of restaurants on the main square serve regional cooking in a polished setting; the cheaper canteens and pubs in the side streets serve the same food faster and cheaper. Vegetarian options are scarce; the *bulz* and the bean soups are usually safe bets.
