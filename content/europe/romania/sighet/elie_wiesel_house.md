The yellow corner house where Elie Wiesel was born in 1928 and lived until the deportation of Sighet's Jewish community in May 1944 stands as a quiet memorial museum a few streets from the main square. Wiesel — Nobel Peace Prize laureate in 1986 and author of *Night*, one of the defining accounts of the Holocaust — returned several times before his death in 2016 and helped curate the displays. The two ground-floor rooms hold family photographs, items from the deported Jewish community of Sighet (around 14,000 people, of whom only a few hundred returned), and the original manuscripts of some of Wiesel's writing. The first-floor room is preserved as the family's living space.
