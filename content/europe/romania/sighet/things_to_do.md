title: Things to Do
type: section
---

Sighet's central pull is the [Memorial of the Victims of Communism](/europe/romania/sighet/memorial_to_victims_of_communism), housed in the former political prison and one of the most affecting museums in Eastern Europe. The other essential stop is the [Elie Wiesel Memorial House](/europe/romania/sighet/elie_wiesel_house), where the Nobel laureate spent his childhood before being deported to Auschwitz. Beyond the museums, Sighet is a quiet starting point for the wooden churches and traditional villages of [Maramureș](/europe/romania) — the [Merry Cemetery at Săpânța](/europe/romania/sapinta) and the wooden churches of [Bârsana](/europe/romania/sighet/barsana_monastery), Budești, and Ieud are all within reach.
