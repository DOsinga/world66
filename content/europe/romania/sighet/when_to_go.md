---
title: When to Go
type: section
---

Sighet and Maramureș are at their best in **late spring (May–June)** and **early autumn (September–October)** — warm, dry, the meadows green and the trees turning. **High summer (July–August)** is also fine but warmer; the **Hora la Prislop** folk festival is held annually in August on the Prislop Pass, attracting traditional dance and music groups from across Maramureș. **Winter (December–March)** brings deep snow and serious cold, with temperatures regularly below -10°C; the wooden churches are striking under snow, but the country roads can become slow or impassable.

The **harvest** months of September–October are particularly good for visitors interested in rural life — the haymaking, the apple harvest, the autumn fairs, and the plum-brandy distilling are all going on at once.
