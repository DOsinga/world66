---
title: Getting There
type: section
---

**By train**

Sighisoara is on the main Brasov–Cluj-Napoca rail line, making it easy to reach from either city. Journey time from Brasov is around two hours; from Cluj about the same. Several trains run daily in both directions. The railway station is a short walk from the old town.

**By bus**

Minibuses (maxitaxis) connect Sighisoara to Brasov, Sibiu, and Cluj, often faster than the train. They depart from the bus station adjacent to the railway station.

**By car**

Sighisoara sits on the E60 highway between Brasov and Cluj. Parking is available outside the citadel walls. The old town is compact; you will not need a car once you are there.
