Sighisoara Synagogue sits away from the postcard lanes of the citadel, which is part of why it matters. It points to a Jewish layer of the town's history that can be easy to miss when the towers, guild houses, and Dracula associations dominate the visit.

The building is modest compared with Sighisoara's medieval landmarks, but it earns a stop for travellers interested in minority histories and restored heritage. Check access locally, as small synagogues in the region are not always open on predictable hours.
