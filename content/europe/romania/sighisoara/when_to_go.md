---
title: When to Go
type: section
---

Summer (June–August) is the peak season, with warm weather and the annual Medieval Festival in late July. The festival fills the citadel with costumed performers, open-air theatre, and concerts — genuinely festive rather than staged, and worth planning a trip around.

Spring and autumn are excellent times to visit — fewer tourists, reasonable prices, and the surrounding Transylvanian hills in their best colours. Winters are cold and occasionally snowy; the citadel takes on a quiet, slightly eerie atmosphere that suits its medieval character. Some guesthouses close in January and February, but the main sights are open year-round.
