---
title: When to Go
type: section
---

Sinaia has two busy seasons. Winter (December to March) draws skiers to the Bucegi slopes; snow cover is reliable above 1,400 metres, and the resort fills with Bucharest families over weekends and holidays. The ski runs are modest but the atmosphere is lively.

Summer (June to September) is peak season for castle visitors and hikers. July and August can be crowded at the main attractions and the cable car queues can be long on summer weekends. Afternoon thunderstorms are frequent on the plateau — start hikes in the morning.

Spring and autumn are the quietest and in some ways the most rewarding times. In October the beech forests on the lower slopes turn brilliant gold. Temperatures are cool but pleasant, the castle and monastery are less crowded, and accommodation is cheaper. April and May bring wildflowers to the mountain meadows but snow can persist on the upper plateau into May.
