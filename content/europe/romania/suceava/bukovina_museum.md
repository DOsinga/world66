The main history and archaeology museum of Suceava County, covering the history of the Bucovina region from prehistoric times through the medieval Moldavian principality to the Habsburg era. The collection includes medieval weaponry, ceramics, and objects from the princely court, as well as ethnographic displays of traditional Bukovinian costumes and crafts. The section on the painted monasteries explains the iconographic programmes of the frescoes before you visit them in person — worth an hour before heading out to Voroneț or Humor.
