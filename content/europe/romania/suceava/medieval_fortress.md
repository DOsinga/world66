story: >
  Stefan cel Mare (Stephen the Great), who ruled Moldavia for 47 years from this fortress, defeated the Ottoman army here in 1476 — one of the few European rulers to beat them in open battle. He rebuilt the fortress after the Ottomans partially destroyed it, and it remained the seat of Moldavian power until the capital moved to Iași in the 16th century. The Pope called Stefan "Champion of Christ" after his victories.
---

The ruined stronghold of the medieval Moldavian princes crowns a hill above Suceava, with the distinctive outline of its curtain walls still intact against the skyline. Built in the 14th century and expanded by Stefan cel Mare in the 15th, this was the seat of one of the most powerful principalities in medieval Eastern Europe. The ruins are substantial — you can walk the walls and explore the courtyard — and the site has excellent views over the surrounding countryside. Most accessible by taxi or a 20-minute walk uphill from the city centre.
