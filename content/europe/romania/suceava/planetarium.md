Suceava Planetarium is a small science stop on the campus of Stefan cel Mare University, useful when you want a break from monasteries, fortresses, and painted church history. It has the straightforward charm of an older Eastern European planetarium: dome shows, astronomy education, and a working observatory identity.

Check the current program before crossing town, because the value depends on show times and language. For families, rainy days, or travelers with a soft spot for old planetarium projectors, it adds a different note to Suceava's mostly medieval and Bucovina-focused itinerary.
