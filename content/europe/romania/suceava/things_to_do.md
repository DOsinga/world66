---
title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

The main reason to come to Suceava is the **Medieval Seat Fortress** on its hill above the city — the ruined stronghold of the Moldavian princes, including Stefan cel Mare (Stephen the Great), who ruled from here in the 15th century. Down in the town, the **Bukovina Museum** covers the history and archaeology of the region. The **Armenian Monastery of Zamca** on the western edge of town dates from the 15th century and is one of Romania's oldest Armenian religious sites.

Suceava is primarily used as a base for visiting the **painted monasteries of Bucovina** — Voroneț, Humor, Moldovița, Sucevița — within a 50 km radius. These are the real reason most travellers stop here, and day trips are easily arranged.
