---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

Summer (June–August) is the best time to visit: the painted monasteries are open, the countryside is green, and the mountain roads are passable. Winters are cold and snowy — temperatures regularly drop below -10°C — but the snow-covered monastery courtyards can be atmospheric if you don't mind the cold.

Spring and autumn are pleasant but shoulder-season: some smaller monasteries may have reduced hours, and the roads to the more remote ones can be muddy after rain.
