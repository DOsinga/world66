The 27-metre stone watchtower at the heart of the Princely Court complex, built in the mid-15th century and rebuilt in stone after a fire. The name means "Watchtower of the Sundown" — chindie is the Romanian word for the late afternoon call to vespers.

A spiral staircase leads up through four levels of small museum displays to a viewing balcony at the top, with views over Târgoviște's red-tiled roofs to the Carpathians on a clear day. Tight stairs; not for the claustrophobic.
