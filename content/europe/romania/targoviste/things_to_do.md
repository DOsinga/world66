---
title: Things to Do
type: section
---

A small town with one strong reason to come: the medieval Princely Court at its centre, layered over with five centuries of Wallachian and Romanian history. A walking circuit takes in the ruined palace, the church of Vlad the Impaler's father, the Chindia Tower, the art and history museums, and the monastery of Dealu just outside town — comfortably done in a day.
