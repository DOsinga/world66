The Communist Consumer Museum is a basement collection of household objects from Romania's communist years, set below a cafe rather than inside a formal museum building. It is small and informal, but the crowded rooms make everyday life under the old system feel more immediate than a polished display case would.

Come for a quick, odd look at radios, toys, packaging, furniture, and domestic objects that many Romanians grew up with.
