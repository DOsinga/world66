The Corneliu Miklosi Public Transport Museum sits inside an old tram depot in the Fabric district, on the eastern side of the city. The collection is a complete history of public transport in Timisoara: horse-drawn trams from the 1870s, electric trams from 1899 onwards, trolleybuses, buses, snow ploughs and maintenance vehicles.

The depot is huge — a converted brick-built tram hall — and the collection genuinely fills it. Many of the trams are kept in running order and a handful are used on special "heritage line" runs through the city on summer weekends. The displays include original ticket vending machines, depot blueprints, and 19th-century maps of the tram network. The museum is named after the engineer who oversaw the transition to electric traction in 1899.
