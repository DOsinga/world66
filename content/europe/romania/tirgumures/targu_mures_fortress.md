Targu Mures Fortress is the city's old defensive enclosure, built in the 17th century and later adapted by the Habsburgs for military use. Its walls now frame a calm public space used for walks, events, and exhibitions.

The main Calvinist church stands within the fortress, which gives the site both civic and religious weight. It is one of the clearest places in Targu Mures to connect the city's Transylvanian history with its present-day public life.
