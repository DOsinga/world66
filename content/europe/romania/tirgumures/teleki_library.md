Teleki Library is a historic library and museum founded in 1802 by Samuel Teleki, chancellor of Transylvania. Its Baroque building holds a large collection of rare books and manuscripts, making it one of Targu Mures' strongest cultural sights.

The library suits visitors interested in old books, Transylvanian history, and quiet interiors away from the busier streets around the city center.
