The Church of the Holy Archangels in Rogoz is one of the UNESCO-listed wooden churches of Maramures. Built in the region's distinctive timber style, it combines a steep shingled roof, a slender tower, and painted interior decoration that rewards a slow look.

It is a strong stop for travellers following the wooden-church route through Maramures Land. Access and opening times can be local and informal, so allow some flexibility rather than treating it like a large staffed monument.
