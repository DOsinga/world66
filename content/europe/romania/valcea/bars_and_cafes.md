---
title: Bars and Cafes
type: section
---

The café culture in Râmnicu Vâlcea revolves around the central pedestrian zone, Calea lui Traian, where terrace cafés fill up in the evenings. Romanians linger over coffee and cake in the late afternoon; bar life is livelier later. Outside the city, options are limited — the spa towns have hotel bars, and most villages have a local pub that serves beer and spirits to regulars rather than visitors.
