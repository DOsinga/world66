The liveliest bar in Râmnicu Vâlcea's centre, with a mixed crowd of students and young professionals. Draught beer, local spirits, and a reasonable selection of cocktails. The interior is dim and loud at weekends; the small terrace out front is better for conversation. Romanian pop and occasional live music. Stays open until the early hours on Fridays and Saturdays.
