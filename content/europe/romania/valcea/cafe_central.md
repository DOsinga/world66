type: poi

The most popular terrace café on Calea lui Traian, Râmnicu Vâlcea's main pedestrian street. Serves good espresso, fresh pastries, and cold drinks. The terrace is the best place in the city for people-watching on a summer evening, when the whole town seems to come out for a stroll. The ice cream is worth stopping for on its own.
