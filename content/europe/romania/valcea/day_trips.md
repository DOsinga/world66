---
title: Day Trips
type: section
---

Vâlcea is well placed for day trips into the surrounding region. The Drăgășani wine country lies about 50km south, where some of Romania's best white wines are made from native varieties such as Crâmpoșie and Tămâioasă Românească. The wineries are beginning to open for tastings. West across the mountains, the Novaci area gives access to the Transalpina road, one of the highest mountain roads in Romania, with extraordinary views across the Southern Carpathians. Sibiu is about 110km north through the Olt gorge and over the Carpathians — a rewarding drive of around two hours through increasingly spectacular scenery, and one of Romania's finest cities at the far end.

For those interested in spas and thermal waters, the separate resort of Băile Olănești, about 20km north of Râmnicu Vâlcea in a side valley, offers a quieter alternative to the Călimănești complex, with its own mineral springs and a well-established sanatorium. The road there passes through pleasant rolling countryside.
