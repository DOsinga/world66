Sibiu, about 110km north through the Olt gorge and over the Red Tower Pass, is one of Romania's finest cities — a Transylvanian Saxon town with a preserved medieval core of coloured facades, cobbled squares, and Gothic towers. The drive north from Vâlcea is reward enough: the Olt gorge through Cozia, the winding road up through the Red Tower pass, and the sudden opening into the Transylvanian basin at Tălmaciu.

Sibiu's Brukenthal National Museum is the best art museum in Romania outside Bucharest. The main square, Piața Mare, is handsome by any standard. Allow a full day to do the journey and the city justice. The road is well-surfaced and straightforward; the drive takes around two hours in reasonable traffic.
