A guesthouse restaurant in the town of Horezu, within easy walking distance of the monastery, making it the natural lunch stop on a monastery day. The cooking is homestyle Romanian: stuffed peppers, chicken broth, grilled meats, and pies filled with cheese or sweetened pumpkin. The terrace looks out over the wooded hills. Simple, unpretentious, and good value.
