One of the most reliably good restaurants in Râmnicu Vâlcea, Haiducii leans into the rustic Wallachian aesthetic with wooden beams and hunting trophies on the walls. The food is honest Romanian — grilled pork neck, sarmale (stuffed cabbage rolls), and a decent selection of local wines from the Drăgășani region to the south. The portions are generous and prices are reasonable. Book ahead at weekends when the place fills with local families.
