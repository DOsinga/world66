---
title: When to Go
type: section
---

May through October is the best period for visiting Vâlcea. The monastery gardens and the surrounding forests are at their best in late spring and early summer; autumn brings good hiking conditions and the beech forests turn a deep gold in October. The monasteries are open year-round, but winter can bring snow and difficult road conditions in the hills.

The spa towns are busy in summer with Romanian visitors, which is the right season to experience the local culture. Horezu's pottery festival, held in June, is worth timing a visit around.
