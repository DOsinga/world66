Hanul lui Manole is one of Vama Veche's most enduring bar institutions — a terrace venue with a lived-in feel that has been gathering writers, musicians, and regulars since the early days of the village's bohemian reputation. The décor is deliberately rustic: wooden tables, mismatched chairs, fairy lights strung through vines overhead.

It draws a slightly older crowd than some of the beach bars — people who remember the village before the guesthouses arrived. Live music nights happen regularly in summer, usually local folk-rock acts performing sets that run long and finish when everyone is ready to stop.
