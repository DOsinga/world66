  Stufstock began in the early 2000s as a loose gathering on the beach and grew into one of Romania's most beloved alternative music festivals. Stufstock is Vama Veche's signature summer event: a rock and alternative music festival held on the beach, usually in late July or early August. It draws a loyal crowd from Bucharest and beyond — the kind of people who care more about the music and the atmosphere than a wristband upgrade.

The setup is deliberately low-key. Stages are set up close to the water, and audiences sprawl on the sand or stand ankle-deep in the Black Sea listening to Romanian and international acts. No corporate sponsors dominate the banners; no VIP enclosures cut off the view. It's one of those festivals where the gaps between sets are as good as the sets.
