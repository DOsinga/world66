---
title: When to Go
type: section
---

Summer is when Vama Veche is fully alive: July and August bring warm weather, festivals, and the entire beach culture that defines the village. Temperatures average 28–30°C, the sea is calm and swimmable, and the bars run open until dawn. The trade-off is that the village is genuinely crowded — accommodation fills weeks in advance and the streets become shoulder-to-shoulder at weekends.

June and September offer the best balance. The weather is still warm (22–25°C), the sea swimmable, and the crowds noticeably thinner. Festival season tapers off after August, so you lose Stufstock but gain the ability to actually walk around.

Late September is worth knowing about if you can be flexible: the village reverts to something close to its original character — quiet, slightly melancholy, and very beautiful. The Black Sea turns a deeper grey-green, the last guesthouses are still open, and a long weekend here feels genuinely restorative.

Avoid winter unless you're interested in ghost towns. Most establishments close completely from October through May.
