---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

The painted monasteries can be visited year-round, but summer (June to August) gives the best light for seeing the frescoes and the most reliable weather. Spring (May to June) is lovely in the mountain valleys, with wildflowers and good hiking conditions.

Winter brings modest skiing on the slopes around Vatra Dornei — the resort is not in the same class as the Alps, but the infrastructure is functional and the scenery is beautiful. Snow can be expected from December to March. Autumn, particularly September and October, sees warm days and brilliant colour in the Carpathian forests, and fewer tourists than summer.
