The Museum of Artificial Stone is attached to Alexandrov's institute of synthetic crystals, where artificial diamond, sapphire, quartz, and other minerals are produced. The exhibits compare natural and artificial stones and explain the production methods behind synthetic crystals, giving the town an unusual science-focused museum alongside its older historical sights.
