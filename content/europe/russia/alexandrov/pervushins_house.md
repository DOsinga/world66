Pervushin's House recreates the world of a 19th-century Alexandrov merchant family, with staff presenting the rooms through stories, traditions, tea, and local drinks. The interiors are reconstructed rather than original, but the setting feels informal and hands-on, and the mid-19th-century house itself is worth a look for its mixed stone-and-wood classicist facade.
