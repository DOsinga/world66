*Europe > Russia > Barnaul*

Barnaul is the capital of Altai Krai, spread along the Ob River at the point where the Siberian plain begins to feel the pull of the Altai Mountains. It is a working regional city rather than a showpiece, but its old core still carries the story of a place built on 18th-century copper and silver smelting.

The best walk starts around [Demidov Square](/europe/russia/barnaul/demidov_square), where the mining school, old hospital, and Demidov Column recall the factories that made Barnaul important to the Russian Empire. Nearby, the restored [Mountain Pharmacy](/europe/russia/barnaul/mountain_pharmacy) turns an early medical supply house into a small museum and Siberian restaurant, while the [Altai State Museum of Local Lore](/europe/russia/barnaul/altai_state_museum_of_local_lore) gives the wider Altai context.

Barnaul's rough edges are part of the bargain. Soviet blocks and traffic sit close to merchant houses, Orthodox churches, and steep river views from [Nagorny Park](/europe/russia/barnaul/nagorny_park). It is a good spare-day stop if you are crossing southern Siberia or beginning an Altai trip, especially if you like cities whose history is still visible in ordinary streets.
