title: Things to Do
type: section
---

Belgorod's main visitor draw is its World War II history, particularly its connection to the Battle of Kursk — the largest tank battle in history — which was fought partly in the surrounding countryside. The city itself has a compact, manageable centre and a few worthwhile sights, though it was heavily rebuilt in Soviet times and lacks a preserved historic core. Visitors should be aware that as of 2024, the city's proximity to the Ukrainian border has made it the site of security incidents; check current travel advisories before visiting.
