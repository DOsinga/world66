Cherepovets is not a city you visit for its monuments, but it repays attention. The Vereshchagin Estate Museum is the clear highlight — a connection to one of Russia's most powerful painters. The Resurrection Monastery is the reason the city exists. And for industrial tourism, few places in Russia are as frank about what they are as this steel town on the Sheksna River.
