Cheboksary's main attractions cluster around the Cheboksary Bay and the embankment above the Volga. The Chuvash National Museum is the essential stop for understanding the culture of the people who give this republic its identity. The bay itself, carved out in the 1980s, creates an unusually attractive waterfront for a Russian provincial city.
