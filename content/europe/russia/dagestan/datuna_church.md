Datuna Church is a 9th-century Georgian Orthodox church hidden in the mountains of Dagestan, about 300 m upstream in a narrow gorge.

The church was placed in this remote setting to hide it from Mongol armies in the 12th century. It is exceptionally well preserved for its age, but there is no road to the site, so the visit requires a hike.
