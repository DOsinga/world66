The Kropotkin House Museum preserves the house where Peter Kropotkin lived from the Russian Revolution until his death in 1921. It is a small, specific museum, but it gives Dmitrov a story beyond medieval churches and provincial architecture.

Go if you are interested in Russian intellectual history or want a quieter stop after the kremlin. The museum also helps explain why this modest town appears in much wider histories of revolution and exile.
