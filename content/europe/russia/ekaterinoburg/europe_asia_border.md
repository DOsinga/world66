About 25 km west of Yekaterinburg, on the road to Pervouralsk, a stone obelisk marks the geographical boundary between Europe and Asia. The line follows the watershed of the Ural Mountains, here a low forested ridge rather than the dramatic mountains the name might suggest.

The site itself is modest — a paved plaza, a few flagpoles, the obelisk — but the symbolism is irresistible and the drive through the Ural forest is pleasant. Most city tour operators include the border on a half-day excursion.
