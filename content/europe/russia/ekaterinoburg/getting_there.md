---
title: Getting There
type: section
---

**Koltsovo Airport (SVX)** is 16 km southeast of the centre, with direct flights from Moscow, St Petersburg and a number of European, Central Asian, Chinese and Turkish cities. A suburban train and the Aeroexpress bus run from the airport to the main railway station in about half an hour.

The **Trans-Siberian Railway** stops at Yekaterinburg-Passazhirsky station, about 24 hours from Moscow on the fastest trains. The station is one of the busiest on the line and an interesting place in its own right — the original 19th-century building is preserved and used for ticketing and lounges, with a modern concourse behind.

By road, Yekaterinburg sits on the M5 federal highway from Moscow (some 1,800 km) and is well linked to Chelyabinsk, Perm and Tyumen.
