A neoclassical estate on the slope above the Church on Blood, designed in the 1790s for an Old Believer merchant family. The yellow-and-white facade, an imposing portico and a wrought-iron gate make it one of the finest pre-revolutionary residences in the city.

Behind the palace is a large landscaped park with a small artificial pond and pavilion, accessible to the public. The building itself is occupied by the city's House of Pioneers and is not generally open to visitors.
