---
title: Shopping
type: section
---

For Ural specialties — malachite jewellery, Kasli cast iron, gemstone carvings — the best browsing is at **Ural Souvenirs** on Ulitsa Vaynera (a long pedestrian street) and at the gift shops of the Fine Arts Museum and the Yeltsin Center.

**Vaynera Street** itself is the city's main pedestrianised shopping zone, lined with statues of local characters and stretching south from Lenin Avenue. **Greenwich** mall, west of the centre, is a useful all-in-one if the weather is bad.
