The Sverdlovsk Railway Administration Building is one of Yekaterinburg's big institutional landmarks, tied to the rail system that made the Urals feel connected to the rest of Russia. Its long facade and formal massing are a reminder that the city was shaped as much by transport and industry as by churches and merchant houses.

This is an exterior stop rather than a visitor attraction with exhibits. It earns its place if you are interested in Soviet civic architecture or are walking Yekaterinburg as a city of railways, factories, and planned power.
