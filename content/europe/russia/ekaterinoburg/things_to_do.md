title: Things to Do
type: section
---

Yekaterinburg's centre is small enough to walk: the Iset River cuts north–south through it, with the historic centre and the great pond (Gorodskoy Prud) on the east bank, and the Yeltsin Center, City skyscrapers and most museums clustered around it. The Romanov sites lie at the north end of the centre; constructivist apartment blocks survive in the immediate neighbourhoods around them.
