A 7-storey museum and cultural centre devoted to Boris Yeltsin, Russia's first elected president and Yekaterinburg's most famous local. The permanent exhibition walks you through nine "rooms" — from the late Soviet era through to the post-Yeltsin transition — using film, recreated rooms, original documents and the kind of immersive design more familiar from Western museums.

Even visitors with no patience for 1990s Russian politics find the experience engaging. The bookshop, art gallery, café and arthouse cinema make the centre an evening destination in its own right.
