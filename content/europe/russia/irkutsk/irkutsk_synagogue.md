Irkutsk Synagogue is a historic reminder of the city's Jewish community, whose story is tied to Siberian trade, exile, and urban life. It adds another layer to a city often read mainly through Decembrists, wooden houses, and Lake Baikal access.

The synagogue is an active religious site, so access may be limited and respectful behaviour matters. Even a brief exterior stop can be meaningful on a walk through older Irkutsk.
