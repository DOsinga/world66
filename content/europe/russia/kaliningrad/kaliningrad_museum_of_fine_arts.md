Kaliningrad Museum of Fine Arts focuses on regional painting, sculpture, and photography, with rotating exhibitions alongside the permanent collection.

The setting is part of the draw: since 2018 the museum has occupied the former Konigsberg Exchange, a 19th-century building restored after severe wartime damage.
