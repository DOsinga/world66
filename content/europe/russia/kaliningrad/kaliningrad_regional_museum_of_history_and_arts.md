The Kaliningrad Regional Museum of History and Arts is a useful anchor for understanding a city with a complicated identity. Its collections move through archaeology, East Prussian history, war damage, Soviet transformation, nature, and regional art.

Kaliningrad can be disorienting if you only look at surviving German buildings and postwar Soviet avenues. This museum helps connect those fragments, making it a sensible early stop before exploring the city on foot.
