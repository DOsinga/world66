Kaliningrad Zoo occupies a large park in the city centre — one of the best-maintained green spaces in the city — and has grown from its traumatic post-war beginning into a well-regarded institution with over 300 species. The grounds are pleasant to walk in, with mature trees and good landscaping. The zoo has been gradually modernised, with larger and more naturalistic enclosures replacing the original Victorian-era structures.

The zoo is a local institution with real significance in the city's post-war recovery story. Its history is one of reconstruction from almost nothing — the gradual reacquisition of animals and the rebuilding of facilities over decades. Today it functions as both a conventional zoo and a city park, popular with families at weekends. The central location and generous size of the grounds make it worth a visit even if you are not normally a zoo-goer.
