Kaliningrad's river islands and bridges are tied to the famous Seven Bridges of Königsberg problem, the 18th-century puzzle that led Leonhard Euler toward graph theory. The wartime destruction and post-war rebuilding changed the old bridge network, but a walk around Kant Island and the Pregolya bridges is still a good way to connect the modern city with its former street plan.
