The New Kaliningrad Synagogue opened in 2018 on the site of the former New Synagogue, which was built in 1896 and destroyed during Kristallnacht.

It is a meaningful stop for understanding the city's Jewish history and the layers of old Königsberg within modern Kaliningrad.
