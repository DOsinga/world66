---
title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Kaliningrad rewards visitors who engage with its layered, contradictory history. Start at the [Königsberg Cathedral](/europe/russia/kaliningrad/konigsberg_cathedral) on the Pregel River island, where [Immanuel Kant's tomb](/europe/russia/kaliningrad/immanuel_kant_tomb) lies in a portico on the north side. The [Amber Museum](/europe/russia/kaliningrad/amber_museum) in the Dohna Tower is essential — Kaliningrad sits on 90% of the world's amber reserves, and the collection is remarkable. The [Museum of the World Ocean](/europe/russia/kaliningrad/museum_world_ocean) has historic ships to explore, including a submarine.

The [Bunker Museum](/europe/russia/kaliningrad/bunker_museum) gives the most direct account of the 1945 battle that ended Königsberg's German history. [Fort No. 5](/europe/russia/kaliningrad/fort_five) on the northern edge of the city is the most accessible of the extraordinary 19th-century Prussian fortifications. [King's Gate](/europe/russia/kaliningrad/kings_gate) and the surviving medieval gates reward a walking circuit. The [Church of the Holy Family](/europe/russia/kaliningrad/church_holy_family) is one of the finest surviving pre-war buildings. [Kaliningrad Zoo](/europe/russia/kaliningrad/kaliningrad_zoo), whose entire post-war population descended from four survivors, is a pleasantly atmospheric green space.
