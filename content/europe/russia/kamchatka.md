Kamchatka is Russia's wild Pacific peninsula, a long volcanic arc of mountains, tundra, salmon rivers and stormy coast. It is famous for active volcanoes and brown bears, and it feels far from almost everywhere: Moscow is a long flight away, while the next large Russian city is across the Sea of Okhotsk.

[Petropavlovsk-Kamchatsky](/europe/russia/kamchatka/petropavlovsk_kamchatsky) is the main entry point and the only real urban base. From there travellers arrange volcano hikes, boat trips on Avacha Bay, hot-spring excursions and longer expeditions into the interior. Independent travel is possible in and around the city, but the wider peninsula usually needs local guides, permits and serious logistics.
