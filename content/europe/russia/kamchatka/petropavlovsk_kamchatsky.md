Petropavlovsk-Kamchatsky is the capital of [Kamchatka](/europe/russia/kamchatka) and the only practical gateway to one of Russia's wildest regions. The city sits on Avacha Bay, backed by snow-capped volcanoes that make even ordinary streets feel dramatic when the weather clears.

Most travellers fly in, spend a day or two getting organised, and then head out to volcanoes, hot springs, salmon rivers or boat trips. The city itself is rough around the edges, but it has a real frontier energy: port cranes, Soviet apartment blocks, fish markets, military history and mountain views all sharing the same bay.

Petropavlovsk is isolated in a way few cities are. There is no road connection to the rest of Russia; the airport is in nearby Yelizovo, and almost everything beyond the urban area needs either a guide, a sturdy vehicle or a boat. Come for Kamchatka rather than for city comforts, and the place makes sense.
