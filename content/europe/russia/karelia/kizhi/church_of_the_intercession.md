The Church of the Intercession is the smaller winter church beside Kizhi's famous Church of the Transfiguration. Built in the 17th and 18th centuries, it has a more restrained wooden form, with a compact cluster of domes and a quieter interior.

It is best seen as part of the Kizhi Pogost ensemble, where the contrast between the two churches explains how the island's wooden church complex worked through the seasons.
