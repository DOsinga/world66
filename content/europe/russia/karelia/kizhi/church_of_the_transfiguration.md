The Church of the Transfiguration is the great visual climax of Kizhi, a wooden
masterpiece whose stacked onion domes rise above Lake Onega like something carved from
weather and prayer. Built without the usual stone monumentality, it shows the ambition
and skill of northern Russian carpentry at its most theatrical.

Most visitors see it as part of the Kizhi open-air museum, but it deserves more than a
quick photograph. Walk around it slowly and watch how the shingles, domes, and lake
light change from each angle. The church anchors the island's UNESCO status and is the
reason Kizhi feels like a pilgrimage rather than just a ferry outing.
