Kazan's Ekiyat Puppet Theatre is worth noticing before you even check the programme. The building looks deliberately theatrical, with towers, bright detailing, and a storybook quality that makes it one of the city's more playful modern landmarks.

Inside, the theatre stages puppet performances mostly aimed at children, though the craft and staging can interest adults as well. Even if language is a barrier, the exterior and lobby make it a cheerful stop near other central Kazan sights.
