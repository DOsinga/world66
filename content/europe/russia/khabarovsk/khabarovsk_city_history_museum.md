Khabarovsk City History Museum is a small museum focused on the city's own story, from early settlement and imperial expansion through the Soviet period and into modern Khabarovsk. It complements the larger regional museums by narrowing the focus to the city itself.

The displays are most useful for travellers who want context for Khabarovsk's streets, monuments, and role on the Amur. Expect a modest local museum rather than a major institution.
