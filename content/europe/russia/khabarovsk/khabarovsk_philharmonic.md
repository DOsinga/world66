Khabarovsk Philharmonic is the city’s main formal concert venue, a useful stop if you want an evening that is more local than the usual restaurant-and-riverfront circuit. Its programme leans toward classical concerts, choral performances, touring soloists, and regional cultural events.

The building is not the grandest sight in Khabarovsk, but it gives travellers a window into the city’s Far Eastern cultural life. Check the schedule before going, since the appeal depends on what is playing, and expect a largely Russian-language audience and ticketing process.
