The Drama Theatre is Komsomolsk-on-Amur's main stage, with a troupe that produces local work and hosts touring companies from other Russian cities. For most visitors the appeal is as much the building and setting as the performance programme.

If your Russian is limited, check for concerts, dance, or visiting productions where language matters less. The theatre sits in the central part of town, so it is easy to combine with a walk through the Soviet-era avenues.
