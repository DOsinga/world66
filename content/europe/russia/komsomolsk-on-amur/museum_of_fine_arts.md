The Museum of Fine Arts is one of the better indoor stops in Komsomolsk-on-Amur. Its collection mixes local and Soviet-era art with Indigenous material from the lower Amur region, including clothing, ritual objects, and work connected with Nanai and other communities.

It helps give the city a wider context than factories and railway history. Check opening times before setting out, as museum hours in smaller Russian cities can be less predictable than the map suggests.
