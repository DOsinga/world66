Sudostroitel Park, the park of the shipbuilders, is a modest but useful place to see ordinary Komsomolsk-on-Amur at ease. It has rides and amusements rather than grand landscaping, and the name points back to the city's shipbuilding identity.

Come in good weather, when families and teenagers are out. It is not a major sight, but it adds a human counterpoint to the factories, memorials, and big avenues.
