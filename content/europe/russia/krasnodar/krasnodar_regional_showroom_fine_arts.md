The Krasnodar Regional Showroom of Fine Arts is a useful stop if you want a look at the city beyond parks, promenades, and Cossack history. It works more like a civic exhibition hall than a single-collection museum, with changing shows that tend to spotlight painters, graphic artists, and applied-art makers from Krasnodar Krai and the wider Russian south.

The draw is partly the setting: it sits close to the city centre, so it is easy to fold into a walk along Krasnaya Street or a museum afternoon. Check the current programme before you go, because the quality depends on the exhibition calendar, but when a strong regional show is on it gives Krasnodar a cultural texture that a standard history museum cannot.
