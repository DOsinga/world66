Norilsk Golgotha is the city's most important memorial, set on the former burial ground of Norillag prisoners. The exact number of people buried here is not known, which makes the place feel less like a finished monument and more like an open wound.

Several national memorials and an Orthodox chapel stand on the slope. It is not a comfortable stop, but it is central to any honest visit to Norilsk.
