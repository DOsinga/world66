The Norilsk Polar Drama Theatre is one of the city's more unexpected institutions: a professional theatre in a closed Arctic mining city. It opened during the Soviet period and is often described as the northernmost theatre of its kind.

Even if you do not speak Russian, the building and the fact of the theatre say something useful about Norilsk. The city is harsh and industrial, but it is not just a work camp with apartment blocks.
