Makarov Bridge is a light-looking cast-iron pedestrian bridge on a granite base, built in 1913 near Anchor Square. Its riveted structure and naval-engineering feel fit Kronstadt's maritime character.

The bridge is a small stop rather than a destination by itself, but it belongs naturally on a walk between the Naval Cathedral, Anchor Square, and the town's military waterfront.
