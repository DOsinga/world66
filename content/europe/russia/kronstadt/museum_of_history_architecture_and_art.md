Kronstadt's Museum of History, Architecture and Art covers the fortress city's development through models, photographs, documents, and everyday objects. Displays include material on Fort Kronshlot, the early Russian water-supply system, and the siege years.

It is a good indoor companion to the Naval Cathedral, sea gauge, and harbour fortifications.
