title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Kronstadt rewards visitors who come for the naval history and military architecture. The [Kronstadt Naval Cathedral](/europe/russia/kronstadt/kronstadt_naval_cathedral) on Anchor Square is the essential starting point — one of the great neo-Byzantine churches in Russia, recently and magnificently restored. [Fort Alexander](/europe/russia/kronstadt/fort_alexander) (the Plague Fort) on its offshore island is the most atmospheric excursion, accessible by boat in summer. The [Kronstadt Naval Museum](/europe/russia/kronstadt/kronstadt_naval_museum) covers the full sweep of Russian naval history.

[Saint Andrew's Cathedral](/europe/russia/kronstadt/saint_andrews_cathedral) is a place of pilgrimage associated with Father John of Kronstadt, one of the most revered figures in Russian Orthodoxy. The city is also the western terminus of the [Saint Petersburg Flood Barrier](/europe/russia/kronstadt/saint_petersburg_dam), a remarkable piece of engineering that carries the ring road across the Gulf of Finland. Kronstadt makes an excellent day trip from St Petersburg, but history enthusiasts will find enough for a full day or even an overnight stay.
