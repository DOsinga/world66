Pryashnikov Manor is a late-19th-century merchant house with a brick lower floor, wooden upper floors, and decorated window surrounds. It now houses the local history museum, which gives useful context to the churches, paper mill, trade routes, and families that shaped Lalsk.

The building itself is half the reason to go. In a town of religious monuments, it shows the merchant money that helped make those monuments possible.
