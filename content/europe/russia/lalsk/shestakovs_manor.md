Shestakov's Manor is a large wooden merchant house built by the owner of Lalsk's paper factory. It is not as polished as the town's church architecture, but its scale says a lot about the money that industry brought into this remote settlement.

The house is boarded up and best treated as an exterior stop while walking between Lalsk's older civic and religious sights.
