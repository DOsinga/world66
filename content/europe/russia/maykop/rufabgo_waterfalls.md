The Rufabgo Waterfalls are a series of cascades in a narrow limestone gorge about 65 kilometres south of Maykop, near the village of Kamennomostsky. The Rufabgo stream drops through a series of seven main waterfalls over about 4 kilometres of canyon, the largest of which — Shum — falls about 6 metres into a deep pool. The limestone walls of the gorge are up to 70 metres high and heavily forested, making it an impressive destination in its own right rather than just a scenic detour.

The main trail follows the canyon from the entrance near the highway down to the largest falls and takes about 2–3 hours for the full round trip. The path involves some scrambling over rocks and can be slippery after rain. In summer the pools below the falls are cold enough to swim in and local guides offer rope descents into the deeper sections of the canyon.

Kamennomostsky is reached by marshrutka (shared taxi) from Maykop in about an hour. Admission to the waterfall trail is charged; guides are available at the entrance for those who want more than the self-guided route.
