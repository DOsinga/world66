---
title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Maykop is primarily a transit point for the mountains but has one genuinely worthwhile attraction in the [National Museum of the Republic of Adygea](/europe/russia/maykop/national_museum_adygea), which holds an important collection of Bronze Age artefacts from the Maykop culture buried in nearby kurgans. The main reason to linger here, however, is as a base for day trips south: the [Rufabgo Waterfalls](/europe/russia/maykop/rufabgo_waterfalls) near Kamennomostsky are the most accessible and the [Lago-Naki plateau](/europe/russia/maykop/lago_naki_plateau) offers serious mountain scenery an hour's drive into the Caucasus.
