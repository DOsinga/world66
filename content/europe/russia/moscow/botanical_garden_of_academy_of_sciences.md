The Botanical Garden of the Academy of Sciences is one of Moscow's major green collections, with an arboretum, oak forest, rose garden, shade garden, and areas devoted to coastal and flowering plants. It is large enough to feel like a real escape from the city.

Spring and early summer are the best times for flowers, but the old trees and broad paths make it pleasant for a slower walk in most seasons.
