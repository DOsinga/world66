Burganov's House State Museum is a small art museum and sculpture courtyard devoted to the work of Russian sculptor Alexander Burganov. Much of the display is outdoors, with surreal figures, monuments, and portrait sculpture arranged through a compact set of courtyards and galleries.

It is close enough to Arbat and the Pushkin Museum to make an easy extra stop for visitors interested in Russian sculpture or smaller Moscow museums.
