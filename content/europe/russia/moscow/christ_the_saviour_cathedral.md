Christ the Saviour Cathedral is one of Moscow's most prominent churches and among the tallest Orthodox churches in the world. The original cathedral was demolished under Stalin in 1931 and rebuilt after the Soviet period, making it both a major religious site and a symbol of Moscow's turbulent 20th-century history.
