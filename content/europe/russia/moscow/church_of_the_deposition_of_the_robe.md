The Church of the Deposition of the Robe is a small but important church inside the Moscow Kremlin. Built in early Russian style, it served as the private chapel of the Patriarch of Moscow and contains a four-level iconostasis.

Today its gallery also displays Russian wooden sculpture, making it more than a quick architectural stop within the Kremlin complex.
