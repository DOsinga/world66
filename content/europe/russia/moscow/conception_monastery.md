Conception Monastery is an active Russian Orthodox convent in Khamovniki, founded in 1584, closed after the revolution, and reopened in 1995. It is a quieter stop than Moscow's headline monasteries, but worthwhile if you are walking this part of the city and want to see a living religious complex rather than another museum.
