Danilov Monastery is one of Moscow's oldest monasteries, founded in the late 13th century and rebuilt several times after wars, closures, and Soviet use. Since the 1980s it has again been a major religious centre, serving as the headquarters of the Russian Orthodox Church.

The walled complex is calmer than central Moscow's headline sights, with churches, bell towers, and courtyards that reward a slow look if you are interested in Orthodox architecture and living religious institutions.
