The Jewish Museum and Centre of Tolerance is one of Moscow's strongest modern museums, covering Jewish life in the Russian Empire and the Soviet Union through multimedia exhibits, archives, and personal stories.

It is housed in the former Bakhmetievsky Garage, a Constructivist landmark designed by Konstantin Melnikov, so the building itself is part of the visit.
