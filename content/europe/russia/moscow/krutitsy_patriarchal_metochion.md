Krutitsy Patriarchal Metochion is a historic Russian Orthodox estate on the old steep banks east of the Yauza River. The complex includes the Dormition Cathedral, Resurrection Church, Metropolitan's Chamber, tiled gates, and older residential buildings, making it one of Moscow's more atmospheric church compounds.
