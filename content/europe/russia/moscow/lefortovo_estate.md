Lefortovo Estate is a historic palace and park ensemble in eastern Moscow. The grounds include a landscape park laid out from the early 18th century and later garden work from the late 18th and early 19th centuries.

The main landmark is Catherine Palace, also known as Golovin Palace, a Baroque residence linked with Empress Anna. Visit for the surviving palace setting, old garden plan, and a quieter view of Moscow's imperial history away from the Kremlin core.
