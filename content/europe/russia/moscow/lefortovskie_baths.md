Lefortovskie Baths are traditional Russian baths in eastern Moscow, known for steam rooms and venik massage with soaked birch or oak branches. It is a local, old-school bathhouse experience rather than a polished spa.

Go if you want the ritual of heat, cold water, and a vigorous scrub worked into a Moscow day.
