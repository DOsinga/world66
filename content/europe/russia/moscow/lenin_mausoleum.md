Lenin Mausoleum stands on Red Square beside the Kremlin wall and holds the embalmed body of Vladimir Lenin. It is one of Moscow's most politically charged historic sights, drawing visitors as much for the ritual of entering as for the brief viewing itself.

Security and opening times can make the visit feel formal and tightly controlled. It fits naturally with Red Square, Saint Basil's Cathedral, GUM, and the Kremlin.
