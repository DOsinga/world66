The Maly Theater is one of Moscow's old dramatic stages, closely tied to the city's classical theatre tradition. It sits near the Bolshoi, but the mood is different: less imperial ballet glamour, more Russian drama, repertory productions, and a long institutional memory.

Even if you do not speak Russian, the building and setting make it worth noticing while exploring the central theatre district. For a performance, check the programme carefully and expect productions aimed mainly at local audiences.
