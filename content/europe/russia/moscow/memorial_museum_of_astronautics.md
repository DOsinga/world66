The Memorial Museum of Astronautics is Moscow's major museum of space exploration, set beneath the Monument to the Conquerors of Space. Its collection covers early flight, Soviet space technology, astronomy, and space in popular culture, with models, equipment, and exhibits that make it one of the city's strongest science museums.
