The Memorial Museum of Cosmonautics sits beneath the Monument to the Conquerors of Space and tells the story of Soviet and Russian spaceflight. Expect spacecraft models, early satellite exhibits, a Soyuz rocket and a strong sense of how central the space programme became to Moscow's modern identity.
