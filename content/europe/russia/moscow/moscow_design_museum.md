Moscow Design Museum focuses on Russian and Soviet design, from everyday objects to visual culture and industrial design. It is more niche than Moscow's major art museums, but useful if you are interested in how Soviet-era ideas of function, propaganda, and mass production shaped the look of ordinary life.

Check current exhibitions before going, since the museum has often worked through temporary shows rather than a large permanent display.
