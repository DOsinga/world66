The Moscow House of Photography, also known as the Multimedia Art Museum, focuses on Russian and international photography and contemporary visual culture. Its collection includes historic and contemporary Russian photographic work.

The museum also hosts festivals and large temporary projects, so it is worth checking the current programme before visiting.
