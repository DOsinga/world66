The Moscow Museum of Modern Art is spread across several central venues, with the Petrovka branch housed in the former Gubin mansion. Its collections and temporary shows focus on Russian and international art from the 20th century onward.

The museum is useful if you want a break from Moscow's imperial and Soviet set pieces. Check which branch has the current exhibitions before you go, as the name covers more than one site.
