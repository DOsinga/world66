The National Center for Contemporary Art is one of Moscow's main institutions for contemporary Russian art, architecture, and design. Exhibitions change, but the focus is usually on recent work and the place of Russian artists in a wider international scene. It is a useful stop if you want Moscow's art life beyond the big classical collections.
