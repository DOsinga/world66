The Nativity Church at Putinki is one of central Moscow's most distinctive 17th-century churches. Its exterior is crowded with pointed kokoshniks, carved window surrounds, broken cornices, and a lively cluster of tented roofs.

The church is a strong stop for anyone interested in old Moscow architecture, especially because it sits close to the theatre district and can be worked into a walk north of the Bolshoi.
