The Obraztsov Puppet Theatre is Moscow's best-known puppet theatre, with productions aimed mainly at children but staged with enough visual clarity to be enjoyable even without fluent Russian. The building's exterior clock is a small attraction in its own right, with a puppet appearing on the hour.
