Sandunovskye Baths is Moscow's classic banya, famous for its grand interiors as much as for the steam. The halls, marble stairs, and painted ceilings make it feel closer to a palace than a bathhouse.

You can visit for a traditional banya session, and guided tours are sometimes offered for people more interested in the architecture than the ritual. Either way, it is one of the city's more memorable public-bath experiences.
