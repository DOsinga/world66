The State Darwin Museum is Moscow's big museum of evolution and natural history, with large collections spread across several buildings. The displays are strongest on animal life, adaptation, and Darwin's ideas, making it a good choice for families or for anyone who wants a science museum away from the usual Kremlin-and-cathedral circuit.
