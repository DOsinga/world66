The State Historical Museum is the red-brick palace at the northern end of Red Square, opposite St. Basil's Cathedral. Its galleries run from prehistoric finds and Scythian gold to Romanov-era treasures, folk ceramics, manuscripts, weapons, and icons.

It is a dense museum, so do not try to absorb every case. Come for the sweep of Russian history and the building itself, whose painted interiors make the visit feel grand before you have even reached the collections.
