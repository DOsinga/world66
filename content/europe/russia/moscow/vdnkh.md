VDNKh is one of Moscow's major outer-district attractions, a vast Soviet-era exhibition ground that now mixes monumental pavilions, fountains, museums, landscaped avenues, and family amusements. The architecture is the main draw: former republic and industry pavilions line broad walks, with space and science exhibits adding substance beyond the showpiece setting.
