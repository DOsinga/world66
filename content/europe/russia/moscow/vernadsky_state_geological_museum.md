The Vernadsky State Geological Museum is an earth-science museum run by the Russian Academy of Sciences. Its collections cover minerals, fossils, rock formations, geological curiosities, and the mineral resources of Russia.

Highlights include Ural malachite, large crystals, fossil displays, and exhibits explaining plate movement, earthquakes, volcanoes, and the geology around Moscow.
