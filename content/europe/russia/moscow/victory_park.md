Victory Park is Moscow's vast Second World War memorial landscape, built for the 50th anniversary of Victory Day in 1995. Its formal avenues, monuments, fountains, and central obelisk are on a scale that matches the subject.

The park is most worthwhile if you also visit the Museum of the Great Patriotic War, which gives the memorial some needed context. Weekends bring families, veterans, and wedding parties, so come on a weekday morning if you want it quieter.
