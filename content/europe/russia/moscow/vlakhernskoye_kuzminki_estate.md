Vlakhernskoye-Kuzminki Estate is a former Stroganov and Golitsyn estate now folded into Kuzminki-Lyublino Historical Park. The manor complex includes the Blachernae Icon church, estate wings used by the Museum of Moscow, ponds, pavilions, stables, and parkland that still feels like a noble country seat absorbed by the city.
