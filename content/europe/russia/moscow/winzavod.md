Winzavod Contemporary Art Center occupies a group of late-19th-century industrial buildings that once formed part of the Moscow Bavaria brewery and wine works.

Since opening as an arts complex in 2007, it has become a useful stop for contemporary exhibitions, galleries, and a look at how old factory spaces have been folded into modern Moscow.
