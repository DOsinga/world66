The Arctic Research Institute Exposition is a specialist Murmansk stop for travellers interested in polar science rather than naval spectacle. Displays connected to Arctic research help explain why this city became a base for exploration, meteorology, shipping, and northern fieldwork.

Expect a modest institution, not a blockbuster museum. It pairs well with the Lenin icebreaker and regional museum, building a fuller picture of Murmansk as a working Arctic city.
