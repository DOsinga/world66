A small bronze sculpture on a low plinth at the edge of Lake Semyonovskoye depicts a sitting cat with a knotted handkerchief slung over one shoulder, like a traveller's bundle. He is Semyon, the wandering cat — the most photographed monument in Murmansk after Alyosha.

Locals come to rub Semyon's paw for luck, and the bronze there is polished bright by years of use. Wedding couples almost always include a stop at the statue. Children leave him small offerings: bits of fish, milk, and once (according to an attendant) a tinned sardine on a paper plate.
