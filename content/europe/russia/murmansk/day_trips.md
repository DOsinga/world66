---
title: "Day Trips"
type: section
---

The Kola Peninsula stretches east, north, and south from Murmansk, and the most rewarding days out involve getting out of the city. The **Khibiny Mountains** (around Kirovsk and Apatity, three hours south by road) are the highest peaks above the Arctic Circle in European Russia, with hiking in summer and downhill and cross-country skiing in winter. The **Sami village of Lovozero**, about 200 km southeast, is the cultural heart of the Russian Saami and runs reindeer-sledge tours, traditional handicrafts workshops, and a small ethnographic museum.

Closer in, the village of **Teriberka** (130 km northeast) sits on the open Barents Sea coast — the road runs out at a windswept beach of giant wave-rolled boulders. The village starred in the 2014 film *Leviathan* and has become a popular weekend trip. **Polyarny Krug** (Arctic Circle marker) and the **Kola Superdeep Borehole** are both accessible with the right tour operator.
