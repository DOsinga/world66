---
title: "Getting Around"
type: section
---

Central Murmansk is compact enough to walk in twenty minutes end to end. For longer distances, the city has an extensive network of trolleybuses and marshrutka minibuses — frequent, cheap, and warm, which matters when it is -25°C outside. Taxis booked through Yandex are reliable and inexpensive.

For the Alyosha Monument, expect a steep walk up from the trolleybus stop at the base of the hill. The icebreaker *Lenin* and Marine Station are an easy walk from the centre. To reach the Khibiny Mountains or the Sami villages around Lovozero, either rent a car (4WD recommended outside summer) or join a tour from one of the operators around Five Corners — public transport thins out quickly beyond the city.
