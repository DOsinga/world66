The Marine Station (Morskoy Vokzal) is a late-1950s passenger terminal in the working harbour, a low pale-blue building with a clock tower and a spire topped by a Soviet star. From here ferries still run irregularly along the Kola coast to villages without road access, and tour boats depart for short cruises around the bay.

The nuclear icebreaker *Lenin* is permanently moored just outside, and the surrounding quay gives a close-up view of the cranes, container ships, and naval auxiliaries that crowd the eastern shore of Kola Bay.
