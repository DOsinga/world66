The Museum-house of the writer Maxim Gorky preserves one of Nizhny Novgorod’s strongest literary connections. Gorky’s name is woven deeply into the city’s 20th-century identity, and this house museum gives that association a domestic scale rather than a monument’s distance.

Expect period rooms, biographical material, and a quieter mood than at the Kremlin or the big river viewpoints. It is most rewarding if you have some interest in Russian literature, but even casual visitors get a useful glimpse of the city’s intellectual life at the turn of the 20th century.
