The Nizhny Novgorod Povolzhye Peoples' Architecture and Life Museum is an open-air collection of wooden houses, churches, barns, and rural buildings from the Volga region. It preserves the textures of village architecture that can be hard to understand from city museums alone.

The setting is part of the visit: you move between structures, noticing carved details, rooflines, and the practical logic of rural life. It is especially useful if Nizhny Novgorod is your main stop in the region and you will not be travelling deeply into smaller villages.
