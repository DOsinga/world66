Peter the Great's House is a small historic stop tied to the emperor's visits to Nizhny Novgorod. The appeal is the association more than the scale: it is the kind of modest old building that adds texture to a walk through the older city streets.

Drop in if you are already nearby and interested in Nizhny's imperial-era stories. It is best treated as a short detour rather than a main sight.
