The Church of Peter and Paul in Sinichya Gora is one of Veliky Novgorod's older outlying churches, dating to the late 12th century. It stands away from the Kremlin and Yaroslav's Court, which makes it easy to overlook, but that separation is part of the story: medieval Novgorod was a landscape of monasteries, cemeteries, trade routes, and parish churches spread beyond the fortified core.

The church is best for travellers already interested in Novgorodian architecture. Expect a small, austere building rather than a grand cathedral. Its value lies in age, proportions, and survival, especially when compared with the more visited churches inside the central sightseeing circuit.
