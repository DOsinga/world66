The Church of Theodore Stratilates on the Brook is one of Veliky Novgorod's essential medieval churches, built in the 14th century and valued for its architecture and surviving fresco fragments. Its compact form shows the confidence of Novgorod's late medieval church builders.

The church belongs on a walking route through the trading side of the city, especially with the Transfiguration Church and Yaroslav's Court. It is small, but it carries the density of a city where major art and architecture often appear in modest packages.
