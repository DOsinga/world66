The Fyodor Dostoevsky Theatre is Novgorod's main drama venue, housed in a striking late-brutalist building from the Soviet period. The theatre produces a full programme of Russian and international drama, and performances take place regularly throughout the season (September to May). The building itself is an interesting piece of late Soviet architecture — monumental and somewhat at odds with the medieval character of the city around it, which is part of its appeal.

Performances are almost entirely in Russian. If your Russian is up to it, the theatre offers a different perspective on the city beyond the medieval monuments. Even if it is not, the building and foyer are worth a look.
