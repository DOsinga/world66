The Einstein Cafe in Akademgorodok is part of the science city's mythology — a place where physicists, mathematicians, and biologists have been arguing over coffee since the 1960s. The atmosphere is determinedly unpretentious: mismatched furniture, walls covered with equations and portraits, coffee that is strong if not sophisticated.

What you are paying for is the context. Sitting here on a weekday afternoon, surrounded by people who are genuinely thinking hard about the world, feels different from a cafe in the city centre. The Einstein is a living part of Akademgorodok's culture rather than a recreation of it. Evenings sometimes bring small lectures or informal performances. Worth stopping in if you make the trip out to the science city.
