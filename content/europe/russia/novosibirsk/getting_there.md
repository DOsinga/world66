---
title: Getting There
type: section
---

**Tolmachevo Airport** (OVB), 15 km northwest of the centre, is Siberia's main international hub and the busiest airport east of the Urals. Aeroflot and other Russian carriers connect it to Moscow in 3.5 hours; there are also direct flights to European cities, Central Asian capitals, and major Chinese cities. A taxi to the centre takes 30–40 minutes; the cheaper option is the airport express bus to the Rechnoy Vokzal metro station.

By train, Novosibirsk is the major stop on the **Trans-Siberian Railway**. The journey from Moscow takes about 40 hours on the fastest express trains; from Irkutsk (near Lake Baikal) it is about 13 hours. The **main railway station** in the southern part of the centre is a Soviet-era building of some architectural ambition; the metro station beneath it connects to the city's two metro lines.
