The Tara Gates are one of the most visible fragments of the old fortress landscape. Restored in 1991, they stand as a symbolic city gate rather than a defensive work you can explore at length.

They are best seen as part of a walk through the fortress quarter, linking the riverfront, historic buildings, and central streets rather than as a sight to cross town for on their own.
