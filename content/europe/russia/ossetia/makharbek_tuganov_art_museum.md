The Makharbek Tuganov Art Museum is an art museum in Vladikavkaz, housed in an Art Nouveau merchant's house from 1903. Its collection includes paintings and frescoes, with a focus on regional art and the cultural history of North Ossetia.

The building is part of the appeal: its early-20th-century facade gives a useful contrast to the mountain landscapes and religious sites that dominate most visits to Ossetia.
