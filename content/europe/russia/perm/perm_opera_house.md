Perm's opera and ballet theatre is one of the finest in Russia outside Moscow and St Petersburg. The city has cultivated a strong performing arts tradition since the 19th century, and the theatre today continues to stage high-quality productions year-round. Ballet is the main draw — the company has a particularly strong reputation for classical Russian repertoire.

The building itself, a classical-style white and yellow structure in the city centre, is worth seeing from the outside even if you are not attending a performance. Check the schedule on arrival; performances are typically Tuesday through Sunday evenings with some matinees. Tickets are reasonably priced by international standards and often available at the box office on the day.
