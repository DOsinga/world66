---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

Perm has a continental climate with pronounced seasons. Winters are cold and long — January temperatures average around -15°C and snow lies from November through March. Summer is short but warm, with July averaging around 19°C and occasional hot spells reaching 30°C. The city is fully functional year-round, and the Russian winter, far from being a deterrent, is part of the experience.

**Spring (April–May):** The Kama thaws dramatically in April, with ice breaking up on the river in a process called the ice drift (ledokhod) that locals come out to watch. May is pleasant and green.

**Summer (June–August):** The best weather for the Kama embankment, river cruises, and outdoor life. Festival season, including outdoor concerts and cultural events.

**Autumn (September–October):** Short but beautiful. The forests around the city turn gold and the city is at its most photogenic.

**Winter (November–March):** Cold but manageable with appropriate clothing. The opera and ballet season is in full swing. The snow-covered city has a particular atmosphere that is genuine rather than staged.
