title: Things to Do
type: section
---

Pyatigorsk divides naturally between the literary pilgrimage sites associated with Lermontov, the spa architecture of the imperial era, and the mountain scenery.

The [Lermontov House-Museum](/europe/russia/pyatigorsk/lermontov_house_museum) is where the poet lived his last months; the [Lermontov Duel Site](/europe/russia/pyatigorsk/lermontov_duel_site) on Mashuk's slopes marks where he was killed in 1841. The [Diana Grotto](/europe/russia/pyatigorsk/diana_grotto) and the [Aeolian Harp Rotunda](/europe/russia/pyatigorsk/aeolian_harp) are the most picturesque of the 19th-century structures on Mashuk. For the full imperial spa experience, the [Lermontov Baths](/europe/russia/pyatigorsk/lermontov_baths) — a Moorish-style bathhouse complex from the 1880s — are an architectural landmark whether or not you take the waters.

[Mount Mashuk](/europe/russia/pyatigorsk/mount_mashuk) can be reached by cable car or on foot in under two hours; the summit panorama takes in the entire Mineral Waters region and, on clear days, the Greater Caucasus ridge including Elbrus. [Mount Beshtau](/europe/russia/pyatigorsk/mount_beshtau), the five-domed volcanic mountain northwest of the city, is a harder hike but rewards with views across the steppe.
