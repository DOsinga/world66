---
title: When to Go
type: section
---

**May through September** is the resort season. The weather is warm to hot, the mineral springs are at their busiest, and the mountain trails are passable. July and August are peak season and can be crowded, with temperatures regularly above 30°C and high humidity.

**Spring (April–May)** is arguably the best time: mild temperatures, the parkland green and flowering, and far fewer tourists. The mountains are still partially snow-capped, which makes for striking panoramas from Mashuk.

**Autumn (September–October)** is also pleasant — the summer crowds thin, temperatures drop to comfortable levels, and the mountain forests turn. The region has a brief but real autumn colour season.

**Winter** is quiet and cold. Most sanatorium visitors come year-round for treatments, but tourist infrastructure is limited and mountain access is restricted. Not recommended unless you specifically want the spa treatments in a quieter setting.
