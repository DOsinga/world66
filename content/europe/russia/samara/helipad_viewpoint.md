The Helipad Viewpoint sits high above the Volga and gives one of the broadest views in the Samara area. On a clear day you can see the sweep of the Samara Bend, the riverbank, and the long line of the city below.

It is outside the centre, so it works best by car or taxi. Go for the view rather than facilities; the reward is scale, not polish.
