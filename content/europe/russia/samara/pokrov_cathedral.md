Pokrov Cathedral is one of central Samara's more rewarding Orthodox stops, especially for travellers who have already seen the city's Soviet and space-age sights. Its restored blue domes and richly decorated interior bring a different historical layer into view: merchant-town piety, imperial-era church building, Soviet disruption, and post-Soviet revival.

Visit respectfully and outside service if you want time to look around quietly. The cathedral is not Samara's headline attraction, but it balances the bunker, embankment, and broad civic squares with a more intimate sense of local religious life.
