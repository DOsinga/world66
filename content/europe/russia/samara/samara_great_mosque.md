Samara Great Mosque is one of the city's largest religious landmarks, built on a scale that reflects the Volga region's long Muslim presence. Its broad dome and minaret make a strong contrast with Samara's merchant houses, Orthodox churches, and Soviet avenues.

The mosque is away from the tightest tourist core, so it suits travellers interested in the broader cultural geography of the city. Visit respectfully, and use it to understand Samara as part of the multi-ethnic Volga rather than only a Russian river port.
