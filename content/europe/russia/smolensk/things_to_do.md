title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Smolensk's appeal lies almost entirely in its history, and the physical record of that history is extraordinary. Start with the [Smolensk Kremlin Wall](/europe/russia/smolensk/smolensk_kremlin) — walk the surviving sections and climb the towers for the best views of the Dnieper and the city. The [Dormition Cathedral](/europe/russia/smolensk/dormition_cathedral) on Cathedral Hill is the architectural centrepiece, its Baroque facades and golden domes rising above the kremlin wall.

For a deeper understanding of the city's layered religious history, visit the [Church of the Immaculate Conception](/europe/russia/smolensk/immaculate_conception_church) (Catholic, Jesuit, from the Polish period) and the [Choral Synagogue](/europe/russia/smolensk/choral_synagogue) (a surviving trace of the city's Jewish community). The [Church of Saints Peter and Paul](/europe/russia/smolensk/church_peter_paul) is one of the oldest standing buildings in Russia, a 12th-century Romanesque-influenced church on a hill across the Dnieper. The [Smolensk State Museum](/europe/russia/smolensk/smolensk_history_museum) provides essential context for understanding the city's military and cultural history.
