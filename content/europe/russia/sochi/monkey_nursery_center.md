The Monkey Nursery Center is connected with Russia's medical primatology research and with the training of animals for early spaceflight experiments. The grounds hold thousands of primates, mainly macaques, and parts of the centre are open to visitors.

It is an unusual family stop near the Adler side of Greater Sochi, especially for travellers interested in science history as well as animals.
