Park of Southern Cultures is a historic dendrarium in Adler, on the left bank of the Mzymta River. Founded in the early 20th century, it brings together trees and flowering plants from many climates, with ponds, water lilies, lotuses, and shaded paths.

For travelers staying around Adler or the Olympic Park, it is one of the better quiet green spaces in Greater Sochi.
