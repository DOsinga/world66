Sochi Discovery World Aquarium is one of Russia's largest oceanariums, with marine exhibits alongside freshwater species from Russia. It is a straightforward family attraction, but the scale and variety make it one of the stronger rainy-day options in the Sochi-Adler area.

Allow enough time for the main tanks and tunnel displays, especially if visiting with children.
