---
title: "Bars and Cafes"
type: section
---

There is no bar scene in Staraya Ladoga. The village has a permanent population of only a few hundred people and no establishments oriented toward evening drinking. A roadside kiosk sells drinks and snacks and is about as close as it gets. If you want a drink after a day of sightseeing, you will need to head to Volkhov town or save it for Saint Petersburg.
