A small 17th-century stone church on a hill above the village, north of the fortress. The Church of St. John the Baptist (*Ioanna Predtechi*) was built in 1695 — late, by the standards of Staraya Ladoga — but on the site of much older monastery buildings going back to the 13th century. The five-domed Russian baroque form is unusual for the region.

The interior contains a series of 18th-century icons in the original iconostasis. The church functions; services are held on weekends. The walk up the hill is short but steep; the view from the top is one of the best in the village, looking down over the river and the fortress walls.
