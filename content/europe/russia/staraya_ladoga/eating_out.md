---
title: "Eating Out"
type: section
---

Food options in Staraya Ladoga are very limited. This is a small village and not a tourist resort. If you are coming for the day from Saint Petersburg or Volkhov, the practical advice is to bring your own food — a picnic by the river is entirely appropriate to the setting. There is one small café near the fortress that operates in summer, but hours are unpredictable and the menu is simple.
