A small café just outside the fortress gate, open during the summer tourist season. The menu covers basic Russian staples — soup, blini, tea — and the quality is adequate rather than memorable. It fills a genuine need on a warm day after walking around the site. Don't count on it being open outside of June to August, and hours within the season are not always consistent.
