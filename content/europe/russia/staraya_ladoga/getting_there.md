---
title: Getting There
type: section
---

Staraya Ladoga is 125 km east of **Saint Petersburg**, just inside the Volkhov region of Leningrad Oblast. There is no direct rail; the nearest station is **Volkhovstroy-1** in the industrial town of Volkhov, 12 km south, with regular suburban trains (*elektrichka*) from Saint Petersburg's Ladozhsky station — journey time about 2.5 hours. From Volkhovstroy, the local bus 23 runs north to Staraya Ladoga roughly every hour.

Most visitors come by car or on a guided day trip from Saint Petersburg. The drive takes around 2 hours each way; the road is mostly the M18 Murmansk highway. Several Saint Petersburg agencies run combined Staraya Ladoga / Novaya Ladoga day trips on weekends.
