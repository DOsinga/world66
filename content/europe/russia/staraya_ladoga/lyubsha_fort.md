The earthworks of an early medieval fortification on the opposite (east) bank of the Volkhov from Staraya Ladoga, about 2 km downstream. **Lyubsha** is older than the visible Staraya Ladoga fortress and is one of the most important archaeological sites in early Russian history — though it survives only as low grass-covered earth banks rather than walls.

The site is unfenced and open; reaching it requires either a boat across the river or a long drive around via the Volkhov road bridge to the south. There is no museum and very little signage; you need either a guide or some advance reading to make sense of what you are looking at. Most casual visitors skip it.
