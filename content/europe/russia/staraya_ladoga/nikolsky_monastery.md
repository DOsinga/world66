The men's monastery a kilometre south of the fortress, on the same west bank of the Volkhov. The Nikolsky Monastery has a tougher, more austere feel than the Uspensky — the buildings are simpler and the restoration is less complete — but it is the more atmospheric of the two. The main **Cathedral of St. Nicholas** (rebuilt in the 17th century on older foundations) and the freestanding bell tower are open during services.

The monastery is still working as a brotherhood. Visitors are welcome on the grounds outside of liturgical hours; the small museum room near the entrance has icons, vestments, and excavated medieval ceramics. Walk from the village along the river path — about 15 minutes through pine woods.
