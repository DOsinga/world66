A small, white, single-domed church inside the walls of the Staraya Ladoga fortress, built in the late 12th century during the reign of Prince Mstislav. The Church of St. George (*Tserkov Georgiya*) is one of the oldest surviving stone buildings in Russia and a textbook example of the pre-Mongol Novgorodian school of architecture: simple cubic mass, a single drum and dome, minimal decoration on the exterior.

Inside, the surviving fragments of original 12th-century fresco painting are remarkable — among the oldest religious frescoes in Russia. The church is small enough that you'll be in and out in 15 minutes, but the historical weight is substantial. Open only during the warmer months (the building has no heating) and on guided tours from the fortress museum.
