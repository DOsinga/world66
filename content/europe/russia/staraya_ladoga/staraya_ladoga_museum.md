The main museum of the Staraya Ladoga historical preserve, inside the walls of the fortress. The collection covers the entire span of the site — Mesolithic stone tools from the river banks, Varangian-era jewellery and weapons (including Scandinavian-style brooches and Arab silver dirhams from trade with the Caliphate), medieval Russian ceramics, and 17th-century military arms from the Swedish wars.

The display labels are in Russian and (mostly) English. Allow about an hour for the museum proper; combine with the church of St. George and the fortress walls in a single ticket. Open daily except Mondays.
