---
title: Things to Do
type: section
---

There are really three things to see at Staraya Ladoga, and you can do all of them in a single full day: the **fortress** above the Volkhov River, the **Church of St. George** inside it, and the two medieval **monasteries** — Nikolsky to the south and Uspensky to the north of the village. Add the **prehistoric burial mounds** along the river for a half-day extension.
