---
title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

The main draw in Staraya Ladoga is its extraordinary concentration of early medieval history in a small area. The centerpiece is the fortress above the Volkhov, but the two monasteries flanking the village and the ancient burial mounds out in the fields round out what is a genuinely remarkable day. Everything is within easy walking distance of each other, and the setting — river, meadows, and silence — makes the exploration feel unhurried.
