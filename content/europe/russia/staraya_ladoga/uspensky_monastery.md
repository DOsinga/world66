The women's monastery on the northern edge of the village, founded in the early 12th century — making it one of the oldest in Russia. The centrepiece is the **Dormition Cathedral** (*Uspensky Sobor*), a small, beautifully proportioned single-domed church built in the 1150s, contemporary with the Church of St. George inside the fortress. The cathedral has its original 12th-century architecture mostly intact under various restorations, with traces of original fresco painting in the apse.

The monastery is again active — a small community of nuns lives in the surrounding buildings — and is in the middle of an extensive restoration. The grounds are open daily; the church is open during services and occasional visiting hours. Modest dress required; women should cover their heads.
