The riverside path along the west bank of the Volkhov is the connective tissue of any visit to Staraya Ladoga — it runs from the Nikolsky Monastery in the south, past the village and the fortress, up to the Uspensky Monastery and onward to the burial mounds. About 3 km end to end on a generally level path with frequent benches.

The Volkhov itself is wide, slow, and brown, draining Lake Ilmen north into Lake Ladoga. For most of its length there is no industry on either bank — just pine and birch woods, the occasional fisherman, and the spire of the fortress visible above the trees. Walk it in the early morning for the best light.
