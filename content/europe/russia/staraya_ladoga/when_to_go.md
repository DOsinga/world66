---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

The best time to visit Staraya Ladoga is May through September, when the weather is mild, the fortress and museum are reliably open, and the riverbank setting is at its most appealing. Summer weekends — particularly in July and August — can bring tour groups from Saint Petersburg, which adds a little bustle to what is otherwise a very quiet place. If you prefer to have the fortress largely to yourself, a weekday visit or a trip in late May or early September gives you good weather and minimal crowds.

Spring and early autumn are particularly good for photography. The quality of light on the fortress walls and the river in May or September is excellent, and the surrounding fields and trees are either in new leaf or turning colour. Winter is cold and some of the smaller sites reduce their hours or close entirely, and the village can feel desolate. The fortress itself remains visitable but check opening hours before making the journey.
