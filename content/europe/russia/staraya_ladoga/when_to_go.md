---
title: When to Go
type: section
---

**Late spring through early autumn** (May to September) is the only practical window for most visitors. The fortress and the open-air monastic sites are most pleasant in mild weather, and the long northern daylight in June makes for late, atmospheric visits to the churches.

In winter the Volkhov River freezes over and the village receives heavy snow — the view of the white fortress walls above the white river is genuinely beautiful, but the roads can be difficult and several of the smaller museums close. Avoid the late autumn (October–November) and early spring (March–April) when paths are mud.
