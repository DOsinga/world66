Trinity Church is a well-preserved 1676 church near Staraya Russa's central park. Its form is characteristic of 17th-century merchant-class church building, more intimate than a cathedral but still substantial.

It pairs naturally with Staraya Russa's other old churches and Dostoevsky sites, adding another layer to the town's pre-imperial and provincial history.
