Belosselsky-Belozersky Palace is a Neo-Baroque palace on Nevsky Prospekt, close to Anichkov Bridge. The present 19th-century facade is one of the more theatrical noble houses on the avenue, with pink walls, heavy ornament, and a prominent corner position.

It is best treated as part of a Nevsky Prospekt architecture walk. Check current access before planning an interior visit, as use and opening arrangements can vary.
