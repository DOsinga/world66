St Petersburg's Botanical Garden is a good change of pace from palaces and imperial avenues. The grounds include an arboretum, flower gardens, and a botanical museum, with enough space to make it feel like a real walk rather than a quick display garden.

The greenhouse complex is the highlight, though access is usually by guided tour. It is most rewarding if you are already exploring the Petrogradsky side or want a quieter, greener half-day in the city.
