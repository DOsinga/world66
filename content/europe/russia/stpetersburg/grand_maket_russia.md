Grand Maket Russia is a large model-railway and miniature-landscape museum showing scenes from across Russia. Moving trains, lighting effects, and small animated details make it especially popular with families.

It is south of the historic center, so it works best as a planned outing rather than a quick stop between the main imperial sights.
