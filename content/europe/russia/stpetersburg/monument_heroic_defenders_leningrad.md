The Monument to the Heroic Defenders of Leningrad anchors Victory Square with a 48-metre obelisk, sculptural groups, and an underground memorial hall. It commemorates the defence of the city and the breaking of the Siege of Leningrad, making it one of Saint Petersburg's most serious Soviet-era memorials.
