The Museum of Electrical Transport is based in the old Vasileostrovsky tram depot, built in the early 1900s. It focuses on Saint Petersburg's tram and trolleybus history, with vehicles and depot buildings that show how the city's electric transport system developed.

It is a good specialist museum for visitors interested in urban history, engineering, or historic transit.
