The State Museum of the Political History of Russia fills two early 20th-century mansions on the Petrograd Side with exhibits on Russian political life from the imperial period through revolution, Soviet power, and beyond. The setting matters: this part of St Petersburg was close to the upheavals of 1917.

It is a serious history museum rather than a quick palace stop. Go if you want context for the revolutions and the political forces that shaped modern Russia.
