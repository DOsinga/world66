Stroganov Palace is an 18th-century palace on Nevsky Prospekt, designed by Bartolomeo Rastrelli for the wealthy Stroganov family. It is now a branch of the Russian Museum, with restored interiors and exhibitions tied to Russian decorative art and aristocratic life.

The palace is easy to combine with Kazan Cathedral, Singer House, and the central stretch of Nevsky Prospekt.
