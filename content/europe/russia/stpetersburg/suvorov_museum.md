The Suvorov Museum is dedicated to Alexander Suvorov, one of Russia's most celebrated military commanders. Its collection covers military memorabilia, portraits, uniforms, weapons, and material connected with Suvorov's campaigns.

The museum is most rewarding if you have an interest in Russian imperial history. It also gives a different angle on St Petersburg beyond palaces, churches, and art museums.
