The Tovstonogov Bolshoi Drama Theater is one of St Petersburg's heavyweight stages, with a reputation that reaches well beyond the city. Come for serious Russian theatre in a grand old house rather than a light tourist show; even if you do not catch a performance, the building adds another layer to the Fontanka-side theatre district.
