Trinity Bridge is one of St Petersburg's grand Neva bridges, linking the central embankments with the Petrograd Side. Built between 1897 and 1903, it is a bascule bridge with elegant metalwork and broad river views toward the Peter and Paul Fortress and the Summer Garden.
