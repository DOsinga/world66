Trinity Church is an 18th-century Orthodox church in southern St Petersburg, better known by the nickname "Kulich and Paskha." The round church and pyramid-like belfry are said to resemble the Easter bread and cheese dessert served at Orthodox Easter, which makes it one of the city's stranger church silhouettes.
