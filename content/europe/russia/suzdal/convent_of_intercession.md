The Convent of the Intercession is one of Suzdal's classic white-walled religious ensembles, set low by the Kamenka River. Its churches, walls, and quiet courtyards look serene, but the convent also served as a place of forced retirement for noble and royal women.

That mix of beauty and confinement gives the site more bite than a simple postcard view. Walk the grounds slowly and then look back from across the river, where the domes and walls sit perfectly in Suzdal's open, rural skyline.
