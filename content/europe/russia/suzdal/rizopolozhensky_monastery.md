The Rizopolozhensky (Deposition of the Robe) Monastery stands near the centre of Suzdal, its twin-towered gate forming one of the most recognisable silhouettes in the town. Founded in the early 13th century — one of the oldest monastic foundations in Russia — it was substantially rebuilt after the Mongol destruction and expanded further in the 17th century.

The monastery is an active religious community today, and access to the interior is limited. The exterior, however, is freely visible and the gate church with its octagonal towers is striking from the lane outside. The Holy Gate, built in 1688, is an unusually elaborate example of 17th-century Russian ecclesiastical baroque and worth stopping to examine on any walk through the town centre.
