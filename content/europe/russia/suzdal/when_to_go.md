---
title: When to Go
type: section
---

Summer (June–August) is the busiest season, when the meadows are green, the days are long, and the town fills with visitors from Moscow and abroad. Weekends in high summer can be crowded at the major monasteries; arriving on a weekday helps. Late May and September are excellent — cooler and less busy, with good light for photography.

Winter is surprisingly attractive. Snow transforms Suzdal's wooden architecture and golden domes, and the cold keeps crowds away. Several restaurants and guesthouses stay open year-round. The main downside is the short days — in December it gets dark by 4pm.
