---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

Tula is a year-round destination but is most pleasant from May through September, when the parks are green and Yasnaya Polyana is at its best. The birch and oak forests around the estate turn a beautiful gold in October.

Winter is cold (regularly below -10°C) but not prohibitively so — the museums are all indoors, the kremlin looks atmospheric in snow, and Belousov Park becomes a cross-country skiing trail. Summers are warm and occasionally hot, with temperatures reaching 30°C in July and August.

There is no particular festival calendar that should drive your timing. The city is a manageable day trip from Moscow at any time of year.
