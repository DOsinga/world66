Tryokhsvyatskaya Street is Tver's main pedestrian strip, running through the central grid with shops, cafés, and public art. It is not elegant in every stretch, but it is lively and practical, the kind of street where visitors and locals overlap.

Use it to connect the railway-station side of town with the Volga-facing centre. The street is best in the early evening, when the working day has finished and the city comes out for a slow walk.
