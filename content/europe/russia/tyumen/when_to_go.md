---
title: When to Go
type: section
tags: [when_to_go]
---

Tyumen has a continental climate with extreme temperature swings — hot summers and very cold winters.

**Summer (June–August)** is warm to hot, with temperatures frequently reaching 25–30°C. The long days, the green parks along the Tura, and the active street life make this the most pleasant time to visit. July and August can be humid.

**Winter (December–February)** is harsh — temperatures regularly fall below -20°C and occasionally below -40°C. Snow covers the city from November to April. The hot springs at Verkhniy Bor are genuinely magical in deep winter, and the city has an animated indoor life centred around restaurants, theatres, and cultural centres. Pack seriously warm clothing.

**Spring and autumn** are transitional and often grey, with mud in April and rain in September, but prices are lower and the city is quieter. The autumn colours along the Tura embankment in early October can be beautiful.

For most visitors, **June or July** offers the best balance of comfortable temperatures and active city life.
