---
title: When to Go
type: section
---

Ulan-Ude has a brutal continental climate. Winters are dry and very cold (-20°C in January is normal); summers are short, warm and surprisingly sunny.

**Summer (June-August)** is the best time to visit — long, bright days, warm enough for shirt sleeves, and easy travel to surrounding sights and Lake Baikal. July and August are also festival season for both Buryat and Russian Orthodox calendars.

**Winter (November-March)** is a serious test for the visitor but very beautiful. The Lenin head under snow, the smoke rising from chimneys at -25°C, the frozen Selenga — there is a real reward for the right kind of traveller. Bring proper Arctic clothing.

**Spring and autumn** are short and unsettled, with rapid temperature swings.
