**Ulyanovsk** sits on the high western bank of the Volga River, about 700 kilometres east of Moscow, and it is a city defined by one fact: Vladimir Lenin was born here in 1870. The Soviet authorities never let anyone forget it. They renamed the city (it was called Simbirsk until 1924), built a vast memorial complex on the bluffs above the river, and preserved [Lenin's childhood home](/europe/russia/ulyanovsk/lenins_childhood_home) with almost religious devotion. The result is a city unlike any other in Russia — a living museum of Soviet ideology, now slowly making peace with a post-Soviet identity.

Beyond the Lenin cult, Ulyanovsk rewards the curious visitor. The old merchant town of Simbirsk was a prosperous place, and a handful of handsome 19th-century streets survive around the historic centre. The writer Ivan Goncharov, author of *Oblomov*, was also born here, and the city has a good [Goncharov Literary Museum](/europe/russia/ulyanovsk/goncharov_museum) dedicated to him. The Volga itself is ever-present — wide, slow, and imposing — and in summer the riverbanks and the beaches on the far side draw locals out in numbers.

The city of around 600,000 is the capital of Ulyanovsk Oblast, a region that stretches across the Middle Volga forest-steppe. Roughly 73 percent of residents are Russian, with significant Tatar, Chuvash, and Mordvin minorities — a reminder that this part of the Volga has always been a meeting point between Slavic and Turkic worlds.

Ulyanovsk is not on the standard tourist trail, and that is part of its appeal. You can spend time in the [Lenin Memorial Centre](/europe/russia/ulyanovsk/lenin_memorial_centre) without irony or crowds, walk quiet streets where Soviet-era apartment blocks stand next to wooden merchant houses, and get a genuine sense of a Russian provincial city that has not been packaged for visitors. The [Novy Venets Boulevard](/europe/russia/ulyanovsk/novy_venets_boulevard) provides the best overview of both the city and the Volga, while the [Museum of the History of Civil Aviation](/europe/russia/ulyanovsk/aviation_museum) and the [Simbirsk Architecture Museum](/europe/russia/ulyanovsk/simbirsk_architecture_museum) fill out the picture.
