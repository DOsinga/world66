---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

Ulyanovsk has a continental climate with cold winters and warm summers. The Volga freezes in December and thaws in March or April — a dramatic seasonal transition that locals follow closely. Summer (June to August) is the best time to visit: temperatures reach 25–30°C, the riverbanks fill with swimmers and picnickers, and the city's parks are at their most appealing.

Spring and early autumn are pleasant for walking the city, with mild temperatures and fewer crowds than the summer peak. Winter is cold (January averages around -12°C) but not without its own stark appeal — the frozen Volga and the quiet streets of the historic centre have a particular atmosphere that warmer months don't offer. Dress appropriately and the city is perfectly navigable year-round.
