The Mining Plants Headquarters is one of central Yekaterinburg's key historic buildings, tied to the factories, mines, and state administration that created the city. Its classical facade gives a more formal face to a place often remembered for industry and revolution.

Look for it while walking the old centre around the city pond and Plotinka. It is not a long visit, but it helps connect Yekaterinburg's architecture to the Ural mining economy that made it matter.
