Yekaterinburg Circus is immediately recognisable for its unusual dome, a piece of late Soviet civic architecture that stands out even in a city full of strong architectural contrasts. The building is both a landmark and a functioning performance venue.

Even if you do not attend a show, it is worth noticing while moving through central Yekaterinburg. If performances are running, the circus offers a very Russian night out, though travellers should check current schedules and standards before buying tickets.
