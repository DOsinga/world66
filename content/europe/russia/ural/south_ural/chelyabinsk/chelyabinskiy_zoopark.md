Chelyabinskiy Zoopark is the city's main zoo and one of its more straightforward family attractions. It offers animal exhibits and landscaped grounds that provide a softer counterpoint to Chelyabinsk's industrial and Soviet-era sights.

The zoo is most useful for travelers with children or anyone looking for an easy outdoor break within the city. It is not a reason to visit Chelyabinsk by itself, but it clears the threshold as a recognizable local attraction.
