---
title: "Eating Out"
type: section
---

Chelyabinsk has a solid dining scene centred on the Kirovka area and the nearby streets of the city centre. Russian staples — pelmeni, borscht, shashlik — are done well at unpretentious prices. The city also has a range of Georgian, Uzbek, and Japanese restaurants reflecting the diverse population that the Soviet industrial project brought together in the Urals.
