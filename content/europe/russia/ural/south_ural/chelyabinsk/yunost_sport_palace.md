The Yunost (Youth) Sport Palace is an indoor arena in the city centre that serves as the home of junior and training ice hockey in Chelyabinsk, as well as hosting various sporting events and concerts. It sits in a pleasant location near the Miass River embankment and is worth passing on a walk through the central area. The surrounding Yunost Park is a popular spot for locals in summer.
