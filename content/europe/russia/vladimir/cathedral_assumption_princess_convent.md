The Cathedral of the Assumption at the Princess Convent belongs to Vladimir's quieter religious landscape. It does not have the instant fame of the great Assumption Cathedral or St. Demetrius, but the convent setting gives it a more enclosed, contemplative character within the old city.

For travellers following the Golden Ring, this is a worthwhile secondary stop once the headline white-stone monuments are covered. It helps show Vladimir as a lived monastic and pilgrimage city, not only a set of UNESCO-listed facades.
