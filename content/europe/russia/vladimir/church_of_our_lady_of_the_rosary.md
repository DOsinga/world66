The Church of Our Lady of the Rosary is a late-19th-century Catholic church in Vladimir. Its neo-Gothic style is unusual in the region, where Orthodox architecture dominates the historic centre.

The church was built for the Polish and Lithuanian Catholic community in Vladimir and remains active. It is especially atmospheric around Catholic holidays.
