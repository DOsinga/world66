The Old Vladimir Museum recreates everyday life in the city during the late 19th and early 20th centuries. The displays are small and local, but they help fill in the gap between Vladimir's medieval monuments and its later provincial life. The building also has an observation platform, which makes it a useful stop while walking between the Golden Gate and the cathedral area.
