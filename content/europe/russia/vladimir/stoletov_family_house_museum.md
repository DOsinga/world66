This house museum recreates a 19th-century merchant home associated with the Stoletov family. Its exhibits introduce Nikolay Stoletov, a military leader, and Alexander Stoletov, a physicist, while the rooms preserve the scale and domestic feel of a prosperous Vladimir household.
