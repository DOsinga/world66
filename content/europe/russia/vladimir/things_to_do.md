---
title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Vladimir's main attractions are its medieval white-stone churches, among the oldest surviving buildings in Russia. All three major monuments sit along the main east-west avenue and can be visited in a half-day walk.

The [Assumption Cathedral](/europe/russia/vladimir/assumption_cathedral) is the masterpiece — built in 1158 with frescoes by Andrei Rublev inside. The [Cathedral of Saint Demetrius](/europe/russia/vladimir/cathedral_saint_demetrius) is smaller but famous for the extraordinary carved stone relief covering its upper walls. The [Golden Gate](/europe/russia/vladimir/golden_gate) marks the western end of the historic centre with a military history museum inside. For something darker, the [Vladimir Central Prison](/europe/russia/vladimir/vladimir_central_prison) northeast of centre is one of Russia's most notorious penal institutions.
