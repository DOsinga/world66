---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

Vladimir is at its most beautiful in **May to June** and **September to October**, when the weather is mild and the trees around the monuments are in leaf or turning colour. The white limestone of the cathedrals looks particularly striking in autumn light.

Summer (July–August) is warm and busy, with day-trippers from Moscow crowding the main sights. Winter brings heavy snow, which makes the cathedrals' golden domes look spectacular against a white sky, but temperatures regularly fall below -15°C and some outlying sites reduce their hours.

Vladimir can be visited as a day trip from Moscow or combined with Suzdal for a two-day excursion along the Golden Ring.
