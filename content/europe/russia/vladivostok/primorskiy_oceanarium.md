Primorskiy Oceanarium is one of Vladivostok's major Russky Island attractions, combining aquarium exhibits with a broader marine-science setting. Its tanks and displays focus on Pacific marine life, which gives the visit a regional logic rather than the generic feel of many city aquariums.

The distance from central Vladivostok means it works best as part of a Russky Island day rather than a quick downtown stop. Pair it with the island bridges, viewpoints, or university area, and check opening details before going because the trip takes planning.
