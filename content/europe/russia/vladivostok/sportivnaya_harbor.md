Sportivnaya Harbor is Vladivostok's summer waterfront promenade, with a small beach, marina views, food stalls, and family rides along the water. Swimming is officially discouraged, but locals still come here to cool off and wander by the yachts. It is an easy walk from the center and works best as a casual evening stop.
