---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

Voronezh has a continental climate with cold winters and warm summers. The best time to visit is **May to September**, when temperatures are comfortable (20–30°C in July–August) and the reservoir and parks are in full use. Winters are cold and snowy, with temperatures frequently dropping below -10°C, though the city functions normally and has a certain bleak dignity under snow.

Spring (April–May) brings the steppe wildflowers and is particularly pleasant. The Don embankment and the reservoir beaches are at their best in July and August, when the city is lively with outdoor events and festivals. Avoid the shoulder months of November and March, which tend to be grey, muddy, and cold without being picturesque.
