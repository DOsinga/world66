The Parish of St. Seraphim Cathedral is an early-20th-century church by local architect I.A. Charushin, dedicated to Saint Seraphim. Its detailed exterior makes it one of Vyatka's more distinctive religious buildings.

The church also has a tribute to the Romanov royal passion-bearers, which adds a layer of imperial-era memory to the site. It is a worthwhile stop for architecture or church-history interest while walking around Vyatka.
