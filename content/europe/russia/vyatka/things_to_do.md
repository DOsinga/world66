title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Vyatka rewards slow exploration. The historic merchant streets in the center, the clifftop garden with its river views, and the Dymkovo workshops across the water make for a genuinely satisfying day of wandering. The [Assumption Cathedral](/europe/russia/vyatka/assumption_cathedral) is the visual anchor of the city, and the [Vyatka Regional Museum](/europe/russia/vyatka/vyatka_regional_museum) in the former bishop's palace is one of the better provincial history museums in Russia.
