The Vyatka Vasnetsov Art Museum is the city's main art collection, named for the Vasnetsov brothers, who were born in the region. Its holdings cover Russian and European painting, with the Vasnetsovs' own work as the natural highlight.

It is an easy cultural stop in central Vyatka and pairs well with the nearby regional museum if you want a fuller picture of the city's history and artistic life.
