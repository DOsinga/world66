title: "Eating Out"
type: section
---

Yakut cuisine is unlike anything else in Russia. It is built on what survives in this climate — horse meat, reindeer, foal, freshwater fish from the Lena, fermented dairy, and very little in the way of vegetables. The signature dishes are **stroganina** (paper-thin slices of raw frozen fish), **oyogos** (boiled horse ribs), and **kuerchekh** (whipped cream with berries).

The best places to try these dishes are the dedicated Yakut restaurants in central Yakutsk — **Tygyn Darkhan** and **Mahtal** are the long-standing favourites — where the menu lists everything from grilled foal to reindeer dumplings. Russian and pan-Asian food is available too, but you have come a long way to eat a hamburger.
