This university museum is a useful introduction to the peoples of Yakutia, with everyday objects, archaeological finds, and material on Sakha, Even, Evenki, Yukagir, and other northern cultures. It is a good complement to Yakutsk's mammoth and permafrost sights because it puts human history back into the landscape.
