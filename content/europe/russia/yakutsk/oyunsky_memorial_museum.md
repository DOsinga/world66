The Oyunsky Memorial Museum commemorates Platon Oyunsky, the writer and public figure closely associated with modern Sakha literature. In Yakutsk, where museums often focus on permafrost, mammoths, or ethnography, this site adds a specifically literary and cultural angle.

It is a small, specialist stop, but worthwhile for visitors trying to understand Sakha identity beyond the usual extreme-climate narrative. The museum works best with some background reading or a local guide who can explain Oyunsky's role.
