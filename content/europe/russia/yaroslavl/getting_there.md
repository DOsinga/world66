---
title: Getting There
type: section
---

**By train** is the most comfortable way to reach Yaroslavl from Moscow. The journey takes around three hours on fast trains from Yaroslavl station (Komsomolskaya Square, Moscow). Several express and semi-fast services run daily; some continue to Vologda or to the Trans-Siberian route eastward. The main Yaroslavl station is Yaroslavl-Moskovsky, about 3 km south of the historic centre.

**By bus** from Moscow is slower (4–5 hours) and cheaper; buses leave from Shchyolkovskaya bus station. From St Petersburg, there are overnight buses and occasional train connections via Vologda.

**By river** is the most romantic option in summer: several cruise companies run Volga cruises that stop at Yaroslavl as part of the Moscow–St Petersburg route.

No commercial airport serves Yaroslavl for international flights; the nearest hub is Sheremetyevo or Domodedovo in Moscow.
