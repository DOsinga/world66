title: Things to Do
type: section
---

Yaroslavl is one of the easiest Golden Ring cities to explore. The UNESCO-listed historic centre is compact and largely pedestrianised, and the combination of 17th-century churches, the Volga riverbank, and a surprisingly lively student-town atmosphere makes it more rewarding than many visitors expect.

The [Church of Elijah the Prophet](/europe/russia/yaroslavl/church_of_elijah_the_prophet) on Sovetskaya Square is the centrepiece of the historic centre and has the finest frescoes of any building in the city. The [Spaso-Preobrazhensky Monastery](/europe/russia/yaroslavl/spaso_preobrazhensky_monastery) northeast of the centre is where the Igor Tale, one of the masterpieces of medieval Russian literature, was discovered in 1795. The [Volkov Theatre](/europe/russia/yaroslavl/volkov_theatre) on Teatralnaya Square is the oldest drama theatre in Russia. Walk the Volga embankment in the evening for views of the river and the historic church towers reflected in the water.
