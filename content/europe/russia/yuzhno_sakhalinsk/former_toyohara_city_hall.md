Former Toyohara City Hall is a surviving civic building from the Japanese period of Yuzhno-Sakhalinsk, built in the 1920s. It now serves as an office building, so the interest is mainly exterior and historical.

Nearby is a memorial connected with Crown Prince Hirohito's 1925 visit to the city.
