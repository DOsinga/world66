Yuzhno-Sakhalinsk's Railway Museum is an open-air display of old locomotives, railway trucks, Japanese-era railway equipment, and a tunnel boring machine. Schedules can be unreliable, but railway enthusiasts may still find it worth arranging ahead or viewing from outside.
