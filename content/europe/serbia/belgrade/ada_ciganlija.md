Ada Ciganlija is Belgrade’s warm-weather release valve, a Sava River island turned into a lakefront recreation zone west of the centre. Locals call it the city’s sea, and on hot days that makes perfect sense: beaches, paths, cafes, sports courts, and rented bikes all run around the water.

It is not a polished resort, and that is part of the appeal. Come for a swim, a long walk, or a lazy afternoon watching half the city cool off; in winter it is quieter but still useful for a loop around the lake and a coffee away from the traffic.
