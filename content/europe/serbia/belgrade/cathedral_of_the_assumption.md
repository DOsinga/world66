The Cathedral of the Assumption of the Blessed Virgin Mary is Belgrade's principal Roman Catholic cathedral, a quieter counterpoint to the city's better-known Orthodox churches. Its modern lines and active parish life make it feel different from the monumental church architecture most visitors seek out.

It is a short but worthwhile stop for travellers interested in Belgrade's layered religious communities. Combine it with a walk through Vračar or with the much larger Temple of Saint Sava for contrast.
