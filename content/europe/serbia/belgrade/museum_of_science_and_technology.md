Belgrade's Museum of Science and Technology covers machines, industrial design, communications, medicine, and the everyday technologies that shaped modern life in Serbia. Its collections are broad, making it a useful stop for families and mechanically curious travelers.

The museum's Dorćol location places it within reach of the Danube-side neighborhoods and central Belgrade walks. It is less famous than the Nikola Tesla Museum, but it fills a different role by showing technology as a wider social and industrial story.
