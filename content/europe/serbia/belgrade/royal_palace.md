Belgrade's Royal Palace is part of the royal compound in Dedinje and remains tied to Serbia's royal family. Visits are usually by guided tour, often arranged through tourist information offices, and can include the nearby White Palace and its art collection.

It is not a casual walk-in sight, but the tour is worthwhile for visitors interested in royal history, twentieth-century Serbia, and formal palace interiors away from the city center.
