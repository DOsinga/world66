Serbia's constitution still describes Kosovo as the Autonomous Province of Kosovo and Metohija, and Serbia does not recognise the independence Kosovo declared in 2008. For travellers, though, the on-the-ground reality is that Kosovo has its own government, currency, and border controls, and functions as a separate destination — so this guide covers it as one.

**The full Kosovo travel guide, including Pristina, Prizren, Peja, and the rest, lives at [Kosovo](/europe/kosovo).**
