Saint Paul and Saint Peter Church is one of Gjakova's tallest church buildings, visible from several parts of town. It stands close to Saint Anthony's Church and makes a useful stop for visitors tracing the city's Catholic heritage.

The walk from the centre takes about half an hour. The church is most interesting as part of a wider look at Gjakova's mixed religious and cultural history.
