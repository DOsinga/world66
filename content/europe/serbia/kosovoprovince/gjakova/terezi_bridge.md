Terezi Bridge is an 18th-century stone bridge southeast of Gjakova, built with eleven arches across the old road toward Prizren. Its length, setting, and Ottoman-era masonry make it a worthwhile stop by car or taxi, especially when paired with the nearby White Drin Canyon.
