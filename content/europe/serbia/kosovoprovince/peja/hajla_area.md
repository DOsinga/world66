The Hajla area north of Peja is a mountain hiking zone with routes toward Hajla summit and nearby highland villages such as Drelaj, Kuqishte, and Bogë. Trails can be long and markings may be inconsistent, so it is best treated as a full mountain day for prepared hikers rather than a casual town walk.
