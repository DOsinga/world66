The Konak Ethnographic Museum occupies one of Peja's handsome old houses, built in the asymmetrical style typical of the region's Ottoman-era domestic architecture. Its wooden roofline, balanced windows, and enclosed rooms make the building itself part of the visit.

Inside, the museum displays national dress, crafts, and household objects from the Peja area. It is a useful stop before exploring the old bazaar, because it gives context to the domestic culture behind the city's surviving historic streets.
