The Rugova Gorge cuts west from Peja through the Accursed Mountains toward Montenegro and Albania, the Bistrica river tumbling through a slot canyon of pale grey limestone that rises hundreds of metres on both sides. The main road follows the river for 25 kilometres before climbing toward the Çakor Pass — a journey that passes waterfalls, via ferrata routes, rock climbing crags, and the starting points for multi-day treks into the Prokletije range.

For day visitors, the gorge can be driven or cycled; a riverside trail follows the easier lower sections. The most popular trekking routes connect to the Peaks of the Balkans long-distance trail, which links Kosovo, Albania, and Montenegro through some of the most remote mountain scenery in Europe. Local guides can be arranged in Peja for both day walks and multi-day expeditions.
