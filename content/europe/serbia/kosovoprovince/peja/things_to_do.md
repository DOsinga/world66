title: Things to Do
type: section
---

Peja's outstanding attraction is the [Patriarchate of Peć](/europe/serbiaandmontenegro/kosovoprovince/peja/patriarchate_of_pec), a UNESCO-protected monastery complex just outside town at the entrance to the Rugova Gorge — one of the finest examples of medieval Serbian religious architecture and a place of intense historical resonance. The gorge itself, where the Bistrica river cuts through dramatic limestone cliffs, is the main draw for outdoor visitors: hiking, climbing, and canyon walks in some of the most spectacular mountain scenery in the western Balkans.

In town, the old çarshia (bazaar street) still has working craftsmen's shops, and the [Bajrakli Mosque](/europe/serbiaandmontenegro/kosovoprovince/peja/bajrakli_mosque) is one of the finest Ottoman religious buildings in Kosovo. The preserved houses of Albanian feudal lords — [Tahir Bey's Palace](/europe/serbiaandmontenegro/kosovoprovince/peja/tahir_bey_palace) and [Jashar Pasha's House](/europe/serbiaandmontenegro/kosovoprovince/peja/jashar_pasha_house) — give a sense of how the city's elite lived under Ottoman rule. The [Rugova Gorge](/europe/serbiaandmontenegro/kosovoprovince/peja/rugova_gorge) is accessible by road and offers walks ranging from a gentle riverside stroll to multi-day mountain treks into the Prokletije range.
