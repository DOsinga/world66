The Towerhouse of Pasha is an imposing stone kulla in Peja, associated with the League of Peja and the city's late Ottoman-era history. Its thick walls, large windows, and carved details show the local tower-house style that once combined family residence, status symbol, and defensive architecture.

Look for the decorative stonework on the walls, including motifs added by local artisans.
