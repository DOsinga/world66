The White Drin Waterfall near Radavc is one of the easiest natural excursions from Peja, where the river drops through a green limestone setting before continuing across western Kosovo. It is not a remote wilderness stop, but the water, shade, and cliffs make it a satisfying break from the city.

Most visitors combine the waterfall with nearby Radavc Cave or a broader trip toward Rugova Gorge. Go with sturdy shoes if paths are wet, and expect the site to feel more local and relaxed than heavily managed mountain attractions.
