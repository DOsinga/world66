Home is one of Pristina's most consistently recommended restaurants, known for its interpretation of traditional Albanian and Kosovar cooking in a comfortable setting without the tourist-trap feel of some city-centre options. The kitchen takes local ingredients seriously — seasonal vegetables, local dairy, and meat from the surrounding hills — and produces food that is straightforwardly good rather than showy.

The space is warm and unpretentious, popular with both locals and the international aid/diplomatic community that makes up a significant slice of Pristina's non-Albanian population. Reservations are not always necessary but are advisable on weekend evenings.

Dishes to look for include the tavë kosi (baked lamb with yoghurt, the national dish), fresh spinach pite, and grilled trout from the rivers of western Kosovo. Prices are reasonable by Western European standards.
