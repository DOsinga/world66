The National Gallery of Kosovo, situated on the University of Pristina campus, focuses on 20th-century Kosovar and Albanian art. The permanent collection spans painting, sculpture, and graphic arts from the early socialist-realist period through to more recent contemporary work. Temporary exhibitions often feature living artists from Kosovo and the wider Albanian diaspora.

The gallery was established during the Yugoslav period as a vehicle for promoting Albanian cultural identity in art, and the collection reflects that political history. Some of the socialist-realist paintings from the 1950s and 1960s are fascinating documents of that period — heroic workers, partisan landscapes, collectivised farms — while later works show the gradual opening toward Western aesthetic influences.

The campus location puts it near the National Library, so combining both into a single visit makes sense. Opening hours can be erratic; check locally before going, as closures for exhibition changeovers are common.
