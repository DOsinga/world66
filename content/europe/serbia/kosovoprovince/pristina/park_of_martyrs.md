Park of Martyrs is a cemetery and memorial park in Pristina, with the National Martyrs' Monument at its centre. It is a sober place rather than a decorative city park, and it gives a direct sense of how recent history is commemorated in Kosovo's capital.

Visit respectfully and keep expectations simple. The interest is in the memorial landscape and what it says about public memory, not in elaborate museum interpretation.
