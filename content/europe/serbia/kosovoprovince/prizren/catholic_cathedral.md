Prizren's main Catholic church dates from 1870 and has a restrained, Italian-influenced form, with three naves and marble-clad columns inside.

The facade is the detail to look for: different stones give the front more texture than you might expect from the simple outline. Inside, frescoes above the altar add colour to an otherwise calm church interior.
