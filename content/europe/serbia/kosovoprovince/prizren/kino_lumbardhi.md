Kino Lumbardhi is a mid-20th-century cinema near Prizren's old centre, remembered as a gathering place as much as a screening room. It opened in 1952 and later added an outdoor cinema, then became closely tied to DokuFest and the campaign to preserve the building after the war.

It is a worthwhile stop for visitors interested in Prizren's modern cultural life and civic history. Check current access before going, as the cinema has had renovation closures.
