Prizren Hamam is a 16th-century Ottoman bathhouse built by Gazi Mehmet Pasha. Its low brick domes and divided men's and women's sections make it one of the city's most distinctive Ottoman buildings.

The bathhouse no longer functions as a public bath, but it is sometimes used for cultural events and exhibitions. Even from the outside, it is worth adding to a central Prizren walk with Sinan Pasha Mosque, Halveti Teqe, and the old bazaar.
