Suzi Celebi Mosque is one of Prizren's oldest Ottoman mosques, built in 1513. Its small grave enclosure holds the tomb of Suzi Celebi, the poet after whom the mosque is named, making it a compact but worthwhile stop in the city's dense historic centre.
