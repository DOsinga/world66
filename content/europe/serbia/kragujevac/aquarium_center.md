The Aquarium Center is a small public aquarium focused on the freshwater fish of central Serbia — the rivers and lakes of the Šumadija region — alongside a number of tropical tanks. It is not on the scale of the bigger European aquaria, but it works well for families and as a half-hour break from heavier sightseeing.

The centre also runs school programmes and modest research on local river ecology. Located in the northern part of the city centre, easily combined with a walk to the [Šumarice Memorial Park](/europe/serbiaandmontenegro/kragujevac/sumarice_memorial_park) on the same side of town.
