Kragujevac's Botanical Garden has plantings from Asia, Europe, and the Balkans, with information boards introducing notable trees and species. It is a quiet green stop rather than a large formal garden.

It works best as a relaxed addition to a visit around Sumarice Memorial Park and the city's other low-key sights.
