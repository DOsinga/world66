Divostin Monastery sits in the countryside outside Kragujevac, one of the better-known Orthodox monasteries in the Šumadija region. Its history has been broken more than once, including destruction under Ottoman rule and a modern reconstruction in the 1970s, but the quiet setting is still the reason to come.

It works best as a short detour from the city, especially if you are already exploring the memorial sites and old court history around Kragujevac.
