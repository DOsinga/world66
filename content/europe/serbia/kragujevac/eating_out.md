---
title: "Eating Out"
type: section
---

Kragujevac is a Šumadija town and the local food shows it: heavy meat dishes, cheese, kajmak, and the warming sour-cabbage stews of the Serbian interior. The pedestrian strip in the centre has several reliable kafanas serving grilled ćevapi, pljeskavica and karađorđeva šnicla — the local stuffed schnitzel filled with kajmak. Look for places with a wood-burning grill and a paper-tablecloth atmosphere rather than the more polished tourist restaurants.
