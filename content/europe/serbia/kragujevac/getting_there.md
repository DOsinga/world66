---
title: "Getting There"
type: section
---

Kragujevac is about 120 kilometres south of Belgrade by road, roughly a two-hour drive via the A1 motorway and the well-built local roads from the Markovac exit. Regular intercity buses connect Kragujevac with Belgrade, Niš, Novi Sad and most other Serbian cities; the bus station is a short walk from the centre.

Rail service exists but is slow and infrequent — most travellers use buses or drive. There is no commercial airport in Kragujevac; the nearest international airports are Belgrade (Nikola Tesla) and Niš.
