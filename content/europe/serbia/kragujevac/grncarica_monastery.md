Grncarica Monastery is a Serbian Orthodox monastery just outside Kragujevac. Its origins are usually placed in the 13th or 14th century, while the present buildings reflect a 16th-century restoration under the Patriarchate of Pec.

It is a quiet religious and historical stop rather than a busy monument, best suited to travellers who are using Kragujevac as a base for nearby monasteries and memorial sites.
