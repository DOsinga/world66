Ilina Voda Eco Park is a family-oriented park on the edge of Kragujevac, with small lakes, animal enclosures, and walking space. Its odd local landmark is a giant Easter egg sculpture, one of the largest of its kind.

It is not a major sight, but it is useful for families or anyone looking for a relaxed outdoor stop away from the centre.
