---
title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

The single must-see in Kragujevac is the Šumarice Memorial Park, set on a wooded hillside on the western edge of town — moving, sobering, and one of the most important World War II memorial sites in the Balkans. Beyond that, Kragujevac's draw is the cluster of buildings around the old princely court: the Amidža Konak, the Princely Serbian Theatre, and the National Museum, all within easy walking distance. The town is also a working car-manufacturing centre — Zastava and now FCA Serbia — and that industrial heritage runs through its modern identity.
