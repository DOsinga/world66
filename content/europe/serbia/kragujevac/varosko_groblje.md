Varoško Groblje is the older of Kragujevac's town cemeteries and a quietly fascinating place to wander. As well as the usual mix of 19th-century stones and modern marble, several headstones include life-size etched portraits of the deceased — a Serbian Orthodox tradition — and a striking number show the dead alongside their cars.

The cemetery is on the slope above the river a short walk from the centre. As with any cemetery visit, be respectful of mourners and avoid Sunday mornings when families come to tend the graves.
