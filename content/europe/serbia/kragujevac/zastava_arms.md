Zastava Arms is the modern successor of the [Knežev Arsenal](/europe/serbiaandmontenegro/kragujevac/knezev_arsenal) and one of Serbia's best-known industrial brands. The factory produces military and civilian firearms — Kalashnikov derivatives, sniper rifles, pistols — and exports them widely; its M70 assault rifle is in service with armies and insurgent groups across the world.

There is no general public tour, but the company museum can be visited by arrangement and contains an excellent display tracing the development of Serbian small arms from the muzzle-loaders of the 1850s to the present day. Even from outside, the scale of the factory site gives a sense of the role weapons production has played in modern Kragujevac.
