Rusalia Church is a small heritage site near Niš, generally of interest to travelers who are looking beyond the city's fortress, skull tower, and central monuments. Its appeal is quiet and historical rather than visually dramatic.

Because it sits outside the core visitor circuit, it is best approached with specific interest in Serbian religious sites or rural excursions. For most short visits, it ranks below Niš's main sights but still clears the threshold as a contextual side trip.
