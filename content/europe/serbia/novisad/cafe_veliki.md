A Belle Epoque cafe on Dunavska with marble-topped tables, dark wood panelling, and a tile floor that looks original to the 1900s building. The coffee is good, the cake selection deep, and the wait staff treat you to the slow Vojvodina pace of service whether you want it or not.

A good spot for an afternoon coffee and a Dobos or krempita while reading a book.
