The Church of the Great Martyr St. George is the main Serbian Orthodox church in Novi Sad's old centre and the seat of the Eparchy of Backa. Its location, just off the busiest streets of Stari Grad, makes it easy to include on a walk through the historic core.

The church is worth a short stop for its iconostasis, Orthodox atmosphere, and role in the city's Serbian religious life. It pairs naturally with Liberty Square, Dunavska Street, and the small churches scattered through the old town.
