A small modern restaurant in the old centre with a daily-changing chalkboard menu built around freshwater fish from the Danube and Tisa — perch, pikeperch, carp — and local seasonal vegetables. The chef likes Vojvodina classics done seriously, and the wine list is heavy on Fruška Gora producers.

Tables are limited, and locals book a day or two ahead at weekends. Lunch is the better value.
