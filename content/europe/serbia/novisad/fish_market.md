Novi Sad's Fish Market is more than a place to buy fish. Stalls spread out with fruit, vegetables, homemade cheese, pickles, and other everyday foods, giving you a quick look at how the city shops when it is not sitting in cafes on the pedestrian streets.

Go in the morning, when the produce is freshest and the market still has its noise and colour. By early afternoon many vendors have started packing up.
