The Foreign Art Collection is one of Novi Sad's main art stops and part of the Museum of Vojvodina. Its holdings began with a large donation from Dr Branko Ilic, including paintings, sculpture, furniture, and decorative objects by foreign artists.

The collection gives Novi Sad a broader art-historical counterpoint to its local and regional museums. It is close to the old center, so it fits well into a Stari Grad museum walk.
