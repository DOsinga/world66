Palić is a small resort town on a shallow lake just outside Subotica, close to the Hungarian border. Its best feature is not the water itself, which has had a troubled environmental history, but the old resort ensemble along the north shore: park paths, timbered Art Nouveau buildings, villas, and a slow lakeside promenade.

The town came into fashion as a spa and lake retreat in the late 19th and early 20th centuries. That period still gives Palić its look. The [Water Tower](/europe/serbia/palic/palic_water_tower), [Grand Terrace](/europe/serbia/palic/grand_terrace), Music Pavilion, Women's Beach, and lakeside villas make a compact architectural walk.

Palić is best treated as a half-day or easy day trip from Subotica, with extra time if you want the zoo or a winery visit. It clears the bar because the resort architecture, park, lake edge, and [Palić Zoo](/europe/serbia/palic/palic_zoo) form a real identity rather than a loose list of suburban attractions.
