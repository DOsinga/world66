The Palić Water Tower is the symbolic gate to the old resort area. Built in the early 20th century, it combined practical water-tower function with the role of entrance marker and tram stop.

Its carved timber and folk-inspired Hungarian Art Nouveau details set the tone for the lakeside ensemble. Start here, then continue into the Grand Park toward the lake.
