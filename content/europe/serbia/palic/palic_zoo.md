Palić Zoo sits in a landscaped park near the lake and is one of the town's main family stops. It opened after World War II and now mixes animal enclosures with an arboretum-like setting.

The zoo is not the only reason to come to Palić, but it adds substance to a day that might otherwise be only a lakeside stroll.
