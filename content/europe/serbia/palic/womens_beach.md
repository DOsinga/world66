Women's Beach is one of Palić's most distinctive Art Nouveau structures, a timbered lakefront bathing pavilion from the resort era. The name comes from the old division of bathing areas by gender.

Even if you do not swim, it is worth seeing as part of the architectural walk. The building explains Palić better than any brochure: lake leisure, spa manners, and Hungarian-influenced design all in one place.
