Zvonko Bogdan Winery is a polished winery on the edge of Palić, built in a grand style that suits the town's old resort mood. Tours and tastings give visitors a reason to stay beyond the lake promenade.

It is best booked ahead, especially if you want an English-language visit or a fuller tasting rather than a quick look around.
