Gradski Park is the easy green pause in Vršac, sitting between the old centre and the slope that leads toward the castle. It is not a major attraction by itself, but it helps connect the town to the hill landscape behind it.

Use it as a soft start or finish for the castle walk, especially in warm weather when the open streets feel exposed.
