Gudurica Peak is the top of the Vršac Mountains and, at 641 m, the highest point in Vojvodina. That number would not impress in the Balkans proper, but here it feels dramatic because the surrounding Banat plain is so flat.

The peak is best treated as a proper walk rather than a quick city sight. On clear days the view reaches across vineyards, villages, and the Romanian side of the border.
