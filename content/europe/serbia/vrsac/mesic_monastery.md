Mesić Monastery sits near the village of Mesić, southeast of Vršac and close to the vineyards and lower slopes of the hills. It is usually dated to the 15th century, though local tradition reaches further back.

The monastery is a quiet counterpoint to the castle walk: less dramatic, more contemplative, and useful for understanding Vršac as an old borderland town of mixed faiths and communities.
