Gostilje Waterfall is one of Zlatibor's more accessible natural side trips, set in a green valley where water drops over limestone and continues through smaller cascades. It gives the mountain resort a good contrast to viewpoints, caves, and village excursions.

The visit is short but satisfying, especially in spring or after rain when the flow is stronger. Pair it with nearby Zlatibor countryside stops rather than treating it as a full-day destination on its own.
