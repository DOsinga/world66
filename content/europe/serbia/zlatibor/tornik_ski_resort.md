Tornik ski resort is the main downhill skiing facility on Zlatibor, with around 4 km of marked runs and a gondola lift connecting the resort town to the summit. The terrain suits intermediate skiers rather than experts — the runs are not long or steep, but they are well maintained and usually snow-covered from late November through March.

In summer, Tornik operates as a hiking and mountain biking destination. The gondola still runs, and the summit area has walking paths, views, and a restaurant. The peak is the highest point on the plateau and gives the best panoramic view of the surrounding mountains and valleys.
