Serbia and Montenegro was the name of the state that existed from 2003 to 2006, successor to the Federal Republic of Yugoslavia. In June 2006, Montenegro declared independence following a referendum, and the union dissolved. Both countries now have their own pages: [Serbia](/europe/serbia) and [Montenegro](/europe/montenegro).

This directory preserves historical content from the period when the two republics were united. The [Kosovo province](/europe/serbiaandmontenegro/kosovoprovince) content here reflects the situation before Kosovo's declaration of independence in 2008.
