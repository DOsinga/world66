---
title: When to Go
type: section
---

Peja is pleasant from May through October. The summers are warm and dry — ideal for hiking in the Rugova Gorge and the Prokletije mountains — with temperatures in the low 30s Celsius at most. The Peaks of the Balkans trail season runs from June through September. Spring brings waterfalls in full flow and wildflowers on the mountain slopes; autumn turns the gorge foliage gold.

Winter closes the higher mountain passes and makes hiking above 1,500 metres impractical, but Peja itself is accessible year-round and the Patriarchate of Peć is atmospheric in snow. The city celebrates the Albanian national holidays with considerable enthusiasm; Eid is observed by the majority Muslim population. Travel in July and August brings peak prices and the most company on the popular gorge trails.
