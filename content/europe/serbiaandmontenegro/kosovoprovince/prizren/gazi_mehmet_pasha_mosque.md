The Bajrakli Gazi Mehmet Pasha Mosque, built in 1561, is the oldest surviving Islamic monument in Prizren and one of the most important Ottoman buildings in Kosovo. It stands at the heart of the old bazaar quarter, and its modest exterior conceals an elegant interior decorated with calligraphy and geometric tilework.

The mosque remains in active use and is open to non-Muslim visitors outside prayer times. The hexagonal mausoleum in the forecourt, believed to contain the tomb of Mehmet Pasha himself, is a distinctive architectural feature rare in the western Balkans. The surrounding area — a network of cobbled lanes with Ottoman-era workshops and houses — is the most intact part of Prizren's historic centre.
