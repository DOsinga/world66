---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

Sokobanja is a year-round destination given that its thermal baths operate in all seasons. The town is quietest in winter, when the healing properties of the springs are most in demand and the surrounding hills are occasionally snow-covered. Spring and autumn are pleasant for walking. Summer is busy with domestic tourists and the valley can be hot and humid in July and August — the baths provide relief.

The town doesn't have major festivals or events tied to specific dates; it operates on the rhythm of the spa resort rather than the tourist calendar.
