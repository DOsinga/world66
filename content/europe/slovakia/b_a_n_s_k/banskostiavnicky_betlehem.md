Banskoštiavnický betlehem is a large carved wooden Nativity scene with hundreds of figures, many of them moving. It presents religious scenes alongside local mining and town life, making it a compact folk-art summary of Banská Štiavnica.

It is a short, easy stop in the historic center and works well for families or anyone interested in traditional woodcarving.
