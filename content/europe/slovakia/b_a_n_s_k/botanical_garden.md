The Botanical Garden in Banská Štiavnica was laid out in the 19th century for the town's mining and forestry academy. It has mature trees from Slovakia and abroad, arranged across a compact 3.5-hectare slope near the historic centre. It is a calm stop between the town's mining sights and streets, especially for anyone interested in forestry, plant collections, or quieter green spaces.
