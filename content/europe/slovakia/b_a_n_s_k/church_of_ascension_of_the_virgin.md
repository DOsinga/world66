The Church of Ascension of the Virgin began as a Romanesque basilica in the 1230s and has been reshaped by fire, reconstruction, and changing congregations. Dominicans, Protestants, Jesuits, and secular clergy all used it at different points, which makes the building a compact record of Banska Stiavnica's religious history.

Its present look is largely Classical after the 19th-century rebuilding. It is sometimes called the German Church, a reminder of the German-speaking mining community that once helped define the town.
