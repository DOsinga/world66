Svätý Anton Manor is a strong excursion from Banská Štiavnica, combining aristocratic interiors with one of Slovakia's best-known hunting and forestry collections. The manor's rooms preserve the atmosphere of a noble residence, while the museum displays show how land, hunting, and estate culture shaped the surrounding region.

The site works well if you have a car or are already exploring villages around Banská Štiavnica. It adds a different register to the area's mining heritage: less shafts and technical works, more estate life, landscape management, and Central European aristocratic taste.
