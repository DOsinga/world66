The Knocking Tower is a small but distinctive reminder of Banska Stiavnica's mining past. Built in 1681 with Renaissance and Baroque details, it once used a sound apparatus to mark miners' working hours and announce important occasions.

It is close to the old centre, so it works best as one stop in a walk linking the mining museum, churches, and castle sights.
