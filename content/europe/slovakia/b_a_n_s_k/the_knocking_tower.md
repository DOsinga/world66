The Knocking Tower is a small but distinctive reminder of Banska Stiavnica's mining past. Built in the 17th century, it held a sound apparatus used to signal miners when shifts began.

Visit it as part of a walk through the old mining town rather than as a stand-alone destination. Its value is in how it connects the townscape to the daily rhythm of the mines.
