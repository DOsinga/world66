---
title: When to Go
type: section
---

Banská Bystrica is a year-round destination. Summer brings warm weather, outdoor cafes on the main square, and hiking conditions in the surrounding mountains. The city's student population keeps things lively through the academic year, but thins out somewhat in July and August.

Winter is worth considering if you are coming for skiing — the Donovaly ski resort is about 25 km north in the Low Tatras and is one of Slovakia's more accessible ski areas. The city itself is pleasant in snow. The anniversary of the Slovak National Uprising (29 August) brings commemorative events that are worth attending if you are in the region at that time.
