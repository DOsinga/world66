Bardejov is a small medieval town in northeastern Slovakia, near the Polish border, and one of only three UNESCO World Heritage Sites in the country. Its old town is remarkably intact — a [Gothic basilica](/europe/slovakia/bardejov/st_egidius_basilica) with eleven carved altars, a fine late-Gothic town hall, and a [large market square](/europe/slovakia/bardejov/radnicne_namestie) ringed by burghers' houses, all pedestrianised and well-maintained. On a summer evening, the square fills with café tables and has a genuinely pleasant atmosphere that most UNESCO towns have long since traded away for tour groups.

The [medieval fortifications](/europe/slovakia/bardejov/medieval_fortifications) — nearly the complete circuit — are the best preserved in Slovakia. The [Jewish quarter](/europe/slovakia/bardejov/jewish_quarter) with its 18th-century synagogue complex is also worth visiting: one of Central Europe's rare intact examples of Jewish communal architecture. A few kilometres outside town, [Bardejovské Kúpele](/europe/slovakia/bardejov/bardejovske_kupele) is a small spa resort that has been attracting visitors since medieval times, set in a forested valley and including an open-air museum of traditional wooden architecture.
