Ancient Gerulata preserves the remains of a Roman military site in Rusovce, south of central
Bratislava. It was part of the frontier system along the Danube, where Rome watched the edge of its
empire across the river landscapes of Central Europe.

The site is modest in size, but its value is high because it places Bratislava inside a much older
Danube story than medieval castles and Habsburg streets. It is best visited by travellers interested
in archaeology or as part of a southern Bratislava circuit that also includes Rusovce and the Danube
floodplain.
