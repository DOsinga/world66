Slovak literature is not widely translated into English, but a few titles will give context for a visit. Dominik Tatarka's *The Demon of Conformity* (1963) is a key work of Slovak dissident literature — a piercing satire of intellectual compromise under communism that made Tatarka a hero among Czech and Slovak writers. It is out of print in English but occasionally found in second-hand shops.

Michael Zantovsky's *Havel: A Life* (2014) is the most readable biography of Václav Havel, whose career intersected closely with Bratislava's history during the Prague Spring and the Velvet Revolution. Sharon Zukin's *The Naked City* (2010) is not about Bratislava but provides useful frameworks for thinking about how post-socialist cities have been transformed by tourism and gentrification.

For historical background, *The Habsburg Empire* by Martyn Rady (2020) covers the political world in which Bratislava operated for three centuries as the Hungarian capital. Highly readable and thorough.
