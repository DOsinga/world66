The Danubiana Meulensteen Art Museum is one of the most extraordinarily situated art museums in Europe — a collection of contemporary sculpture and painting housed in a purpose-built museum on a small peninsula in the Danube, 30 km south of central Bratislava. The building juts into the river with water on three sides, and the outdoor sculpture park uses the Danube as its backdrop.

The collection focuses on Slovak and international contemporary art from the 1990s to the present, with a strong emphasis on sculpture. The permanent collection includes work by Ivan Csudai, Juraj Meliš, and other leading Slovak contemporary artists. Temporary exhibitions change seasonally.

Getting there requires a car or the summer boat service from Bratislava (June–September). The round trip by car takes about 45 minutes from the city centre. The combination of the unusual setting and a solid art collection makes it one of the more memorable museum visits in the region.
