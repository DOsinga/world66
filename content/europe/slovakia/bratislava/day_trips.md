---
title: Day Trips
type: section
---

Bratislava's location at the meeting point of three countries makes it an unusually good base for day trips. Vienna is 65 km away and takes an hour by train — a full day in Austria's capital and back for dinner in Bratislava is perfectly manageable. Budapest is 2.5 hours and gets you to one of the great cities of Central Europe. Brno in the Czech Republic is 1.5 hours by bus.

Closer to home: [Devín Castle](/europe/slovakia/bratislava/devin_castle) is 12 km west along the Danube, accessible by bike in 30 minutes or by bus in 25. The [Small Carpathians wine route](/europe/slovakia/bratislava/small_carpathians_wine_route) starts at Svätý Jur, 15 km north. Pezinok and Modra are 25–35 km away on the same route.

[Rusovce Mansion](/europe/slovakia/bratislava/rusovce) and the Roman camp at Gerulata are 14 km south. Čachtice, where the "Blood Countess" Elizabeth Báthory lived, is 70 km northeast and makes a longer half-day trip. The Demänovská Ice Cave in the Low Tatras is 3 hours away — a reasonable day trip with an early start.
