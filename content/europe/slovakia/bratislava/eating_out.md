---
title: "Eating Out"
type: section
---

Bratislava's old town is packed with restaurants, ranging from traditional Slovak food (bryndzové halušky — potato dumplings with sheep cheese — is the national dish) to international options. Prices are lower than in nearby Vienna, especially if you step a block or two off the Main Square.

For traditional Slovak cuisine, [Modrá Hviezda](/europe/slovakia/bratislava/tribec_restaurant) below the castle hill is the best option in terms of quality and atmosphere. [Slovak Pub](/europe/slovakia/bratislava/slovak_pub) on Obchodná is reliably filling and cheap. The [Old Market Hall](/europe/slovakia/bratislava/sandrik_vintage_market) on Nám. SNP runs a Saturday farmer's market and occasional street food nights.

[Govinda](/europe/slovakia/bratislava/govinda_restaurant) near the old town is the go-to for cheap vegetarian food. [Zylinder](/europe/slovakia/bratislava/zylinder) on the Main Square has good terrace seating and a reliable kitchen. [Lemon Tree](/europe/slovakia/bratislava/lemon_tree) covers Thai and international dishes competently. The [Bratislava Civic Brewery](/europe/slovakia/bratislava/bratislavsky_mestiansky_pivovar) serves hearty pub food alongside its own beer.
