Hviezdoslav Square (Hviezdoslavovo námestie) is the most gracious public space in central Bratislava — a wide, tree-lined boulevard running east from the old town to the Danube embankment. The square is flanked by the Slovak National Theatre, the Reduta concert hall, and a row of 19th-century hotels and apartments. In the centre stands the statue of the Slovak poet Hviezdoslav, with benches arranged around it.

The square is the venue for outdoor festivals, including a summer jazz festival and the Christmas Market overflow. In the evenings, the terrace of the Grand Hotel Kempinski on one side and the cafe of the Philharmonic on the other fill with people. The atmosphere is pleasant and unhurried — an easy place to sit and watch the city.

At the Danube end of the square, the embankment offers views across to Petržalka and down to the SNP bridge. The riverside path connects east to the Eurovelo cycling route and west to Devín.
