Lemon Tree is a reliable mid-range restaurant in the old town with a menu that covers Thai and international dishes alongside some Slovak staples. The kitchen is competent, the portions are generous, and the prices are fair for the location. The interior is comfortable without being particularly characterful.

It serves as a useful benchmark for old town eating — not the most exciting option in the city, but consistently good and easy to recommend without qualifications.
