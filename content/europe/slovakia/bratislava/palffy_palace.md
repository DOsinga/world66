Pálffy Palace is a classical 19th-century palace tied to Count Ján Pálffy and now used by the Bratislava City Gallery. It is worth stopping for the combination of art collection and old-town architecture rather than as a grand palace tour.

The palace works well as part of a slow walk through central Bratislava, especially if you want a quieter museum stop between the city's bigger landmarks.
