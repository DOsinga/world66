The Old Market Hall (Stará tržnica) on Nám. SNP is Bratislava's best public market and food hall. The 1910 neo-Renaissance building was restored and reopened in 2014 as a mixed-use space: a Saturday farmer's market, regular street food nights, concerts, and community events. The market has become one of the focal points for Bratislava's food scene and a genuine gathering place for the city.

On Saturday mornings the ground floor fills with producers selling vegetables, bread, charcuterie, cheese, and Slovak wine directly to customers. The upper gallery has food stalls and a bar. Street food nights happen once a month and draw a crowd. The building itself is worth seeing even when the market is not operating — a well-restored example of turn-of-the-century civic architecture.

The market is a 10-minute walk north of the Main Square on Nám. SNP.
