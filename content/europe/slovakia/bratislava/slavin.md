Slavín is the Soviet war memorial on Bratislava's highest hill, a short but steep walk north of the old town. The complex includes the graves of 6,845 soldiers who died in the fighting to liberate the city from German occupation in April 1945. A 39-metre obelisk topped with a Soviet soldier towers above the residential neighbourhood, visible from much of the city.

The memorial is well maintained and surrounded by gardens that offer some of the best views in Bratislava — across the Danube, over the rooftops of Staré Mesto, and out to the plains. It is a place worth visiting both for the view and for the sombre scale of the monument, which puts the cost of the war's final months into human terms.

The walk up from the old town takes 20–30 minutes through the pleasant residential streets of Horský Park. The hilltop is peaceful, with few crowds outside of Liberation Day. A small café near the entrance is open in the warmer months.
