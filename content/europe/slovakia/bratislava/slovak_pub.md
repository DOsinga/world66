Slovak Pub on Obchodná Street is a sprawling multi-room pub spread across several floors of a historic building, decorated with folk art and Slovak memorabilia. The beer is cheap and flows freely; the food menu covers Slovak staples — halusky (potato dumplings with sheep cheese), kapustnica (sauerkraut soup), roast pork. It is exactly what the name says.

The pub is popular with budget travellers and Slovak stag parties in equal measure. It can get very loud on weekend evenings. Worth visiting for a beer and a plate of something filling rather than a quiet dinner.
