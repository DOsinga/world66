The Small Carpathians wine region begins just north of Bratislava, making it one of the most accessible wine day trips from any European capital. The wine route runs through a series of small towns — Svätý Jur, Pezinok, Modra, Vinosady — each with its own wine producers, cellars open for tasting, and the quiet charm of a small Slovak town in the process of rediscovering its viniculture.

Pezinok (about 25 km from Bratislava) is the main wine town, with a castle winery and several independent producers. Modra, 10 km further, is known for blue pottery as much as wine and has a pleasant historic centre. Wine tastings at local producers can be arranged without much advance notice during harvest season (September–October); at other times, call ahead.

The journey takes about 30 minutes by car or 45 minutes by regional bus. The route is also popular with cyclists — the hills are gentle and the vineyards are at their best in early autumn.
