---
title: When to Go
type: section
---

Spring and early autumn are the best times to visit. April through June brings mild weather and the city is not yet crowded. September and October are pleasant and the surrounding hills turn colour. July and August can be hot and more tourist-heavy, though the old town is compact enough that crowds rarely feel oppressive.

Winter is worth considering for the Christmas Market on the Main Square, which runs through December and is consistently ranked among the better ones in Central Europe. Temperatures drop to near freezing but the atmosphere is warm.

Summer evenings draw locals out to the embankment and the outdoor terraces of Staré Mesto. The city has a large student population that keeps things lively through the academic year but quieter in July and August.
