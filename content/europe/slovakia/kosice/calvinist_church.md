Košice's Calvinist Church is a plain early 19th-century church with a deliberately simple interior. Its spire has the more memorable detail: a metal rooster made in 1589, older than the church itself.

It is a short stop near the centre rather than a major sight, but it adds another layer to a walk through Košice's religious architecture.
