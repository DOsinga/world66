Cemetery Rozalia is one of Kosice's atmospheric historic cemeteries, set away from the busiest line of Hlavna ulica. Old graves, funerary sculpture, and mature trees make it a reflective place to see the city's social history outside its churches and museums.

It is not a must-see for every visitor, but it rewards travellers who like quiet urban corners. Go respectfully, keep to paths, and treat the cemetery as a cultural landscape rather than a shortcut or park.
