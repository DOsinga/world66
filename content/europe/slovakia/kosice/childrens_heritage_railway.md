The Children's Heritage Railway runs through the wooded Čermeľ Valley north of Košice, using a narrow-gauge line that began as a youth railway in the mid-20th century. The ride is short, scenic and deliberately nostalgic, with small stations and forest stops that make it especially good for families.

It is most useful as a relaxed add-on to Košice Zoo or a walk in the valley rather than as a central-city sight. Check operating days carefully outside summer, since the railway is seasonal and services can be limited.
