Craftsmen's House is a small museum in Košice devoted to the traditional crafts of the region. Displays cover the trades and workshop skills that shaped the city and its hinterland in the Middle Ages.

It is best treated as a short specialist stop while exploring the historic centre. The location near the Executioner's Bastion and Hlavná ulica makes it easy to combine with the main old-town sights.
