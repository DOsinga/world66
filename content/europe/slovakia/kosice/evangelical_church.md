Kosice's Evangelical Church is a Lutheran church from 1816, designed by Viennese court architect Georg Kitzling. Its main draw is the oval cupola, a different note from the Gothic and Baroque buildings that dominate the historic centre.

It is a quick stop rather than a headline sight, but it adds variety to a walk around central Kosice's churches and civic buildings.
