The Immaculata is Košice's Baroque plague column, raised after the plague of 1710-1711. The 14-metre monument stands on a stone base with saints around the column and the Virgin Mary at the top.

It is one of the main historic markers on Hlavná ulica and is easy to include while walking between the State Theatre, St. Urban Tower, and St. Elisabeth Cathedral.
