Kasárne Kulturpark is one of the clearest legacies of Kosice's European Capital of Culture redevelopment: a former barracks complex converted into galleries, performance spaces, workshops, and event venues. It gives the city a contemporary cultural stop outside the historic main street.

The value of a visit depends on what is scheduled, so check exhibitions, concerts, and festivals before going. Even between events, the reused military architecture and courtyard layout make it a useful marker of how Kosice has adapted older civic spaces for new cultural life.
