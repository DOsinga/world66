The summer toboggan track above Kosice is a light, family-friendly outing in the city's northern recreation zone. It gives you speed, trees, and a bit of hillside air without needing a full hiking day in the surrounding country.

It is best treated as part of a broader Bankov or Alpinka outing rather than a standalone sight. Check seasonal operation and weather before going, since small outdoor rides can close quickly in poor conditions.
