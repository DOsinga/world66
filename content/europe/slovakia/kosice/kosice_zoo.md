Košice Zoo sits in a broad forested area north of the city, giving it more space and greenery than many urban zoos. It is a straightforward family outing, with animal enclosures spread across a hilly site.

The zoo works best if you have extra time beyond the old town and want something outdoors. Check transport and opening times before going, as it is not in the compact historic centre.
