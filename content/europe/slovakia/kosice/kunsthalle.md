Košice's Kunsthalle is a contemporary art venue inside a renovated 1930s indoor swimming pool. The building was abandoned for decades before being restored as a cultural centre in 2013.

The exhibitions change, so the value of a visit depends on the current programme. Even so, the reused pool building gives the place architectural interest beyond the art on display.
