Miklus's Prison is a small museum in two linked Gothic houses that later served as a city prison. The exhibition covers Košice history and the building's darker role as a place of confinement, named for one of the city's executioners.

It pairs well with the nearby Executioner's Bastion and the old streets around Hlavna ulica.
