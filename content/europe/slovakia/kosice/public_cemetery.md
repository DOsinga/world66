Kosice's Public Cemetery is the largest cemetery in the city. Part of it is dedicated to the Jewish community and includes a Holocaust memorial whose Star of David once stood on the city's former synagogue, now the House of Arts.

Visit respectfully and treat it as a quiet historical site rather than a park.
