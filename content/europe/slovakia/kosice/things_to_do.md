title: Things to Do

---

Most of what matters in Košice is on or immediately off [Hlavná ulica](/europe/slovakia/kosice/hlavna_ulica), the long pedestrian promenade that runs through the old town. The dominant sight is [St. Elisabeth Cathedral](/europe/slovakia/kosice/st_elisabeth_cathedral), the largest Gothic cathedral in Slovakia and a building of genuine grandeur. Adjacent to it stand the [St. Michael Chapel](/europe/slovakia/kosice/st_michael_chapel) and [St. Urban Tower](/europe/slovakia/kosice/urban_tower), forming a Gothic ensemble at the heart of the street. The [State Theatre Košice](/europe/slovakia/kosice/state_theatre) faces them from across the promenade.

The [East Slovak Museum](/europe/slovakia/kosice/east_slovak_museum) is the most important museum, housing the famous Košice Gold Treasure — one of the largest medieval gold hoards in Europe. The [Executioner's Bastion](/europe/slovakia/kosice/executioners_bastion) preserves a section of the medieval fortifications with a small café inside.

For those who want to venture further from the centre, the Museum of Aviation at Košice airport is worth the trip for aviation enthusiasts, and the Košice Zoo is a good half-day option for families.
