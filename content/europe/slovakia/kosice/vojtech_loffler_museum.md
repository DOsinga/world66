The Vojtech Loffler Museum is a small art museum in the historic centre of Košice, dedicated to the sculptor Vojtech Loffler and to changing exhibitions by regional artists. It is not one of the city's big-ticket sights, but it adds a quieter, more local art stop within a short walk of Hlavná ulica.

Come here if you have already seen the cathedral and theatre and want a compact museum with a stronger Košice connection than a general gallery.
