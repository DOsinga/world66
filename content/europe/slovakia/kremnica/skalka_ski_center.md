Skalka ski center is Kremnica's main mountain playground, used for skiing and winter sports when snow allows and for outdoor recreation in other seasons. It gives the historic mining town a practical link to the surrounding hills.

It is most relevant if you want active time rather than another museum or square. Check conditions before travelling uphill, because the quality of a visit depends heavily on weather and season.
