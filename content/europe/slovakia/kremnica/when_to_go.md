---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

Kremnica works well in any season. Summer (June–September) is pleasant for walking the town and hiking in the surrounding mountains, with warm days and cool nights at 550 m elevation. Autumn brings good colors in the beech forests.

Winter (December–March) is when the ski areas around Krahule come alive, making Kremnica a useful base for a low-key ski holiday. Snow is fairly reliable in the mountains even when it's scarce in the valleys below. Spring is quiet and can be muddy, but the town is uncrowded and the blooming hillsides are attractive.
