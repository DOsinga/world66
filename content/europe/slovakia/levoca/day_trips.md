---
title: "Day Trips"
type: section
---

Levoča sits at the heart of the Spiš region, with several UNESCO and natural sites within easy reach.

**Spiš Castle**, 15 km east, is one of the largest castle ruins in Central Europe — a hilltop fortress whose silhouette dominates the surrounding plain. The castle and Levoča together form a single UNESCO World Heritage Site. The nearby village of **Spišské Podhradie** is worth a stop for the medieval chapter house and the cathedral at Spišská Kapitula.

The **High Tatras** are 40 km west via Poprad — Slovakia's alpine playground, with cable cars up to Lomnický štít and good hiking on well-marked trails. **Slovak Paradise National Park** (Slovenský raj) is 25 km south: a karst plateau full of gorges with built-in ladders and footbridges, a uniquely Slovak way to hike.
