---
title: "Getting There"
type: section
---

The nearest airport with international flights is Košice, about 90 km to the southeast. From Košice, buses to Levoča run several times a day; the trip takes about an hour and a half. Poprad-Tatry airport, 35 km west, has fewer flights but is closer.

By train, the nearest station is Spišská Nová Ves, 13 km south, on the main Bratislava–Košice line. Frequent local buses connect it to Levoča in about 20 minutes. Direct buses also link Levoča to Bratislava (around 6 hours) and Poprad (45 minutes). Driving on the D1 motorway from Bratislava takes about four hours.
