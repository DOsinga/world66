title: Things to Do
type: section
---

Lučenec is a small town and the attractions are modest, but it rewards a few hours of unhurried exploration. The old town centre has a mix of Art Nouveau and neo-Gothic architecture from the late 19th and early 20th centuries, when the town was part of the Kingdom of Hungary and prospering from trade and agriculture.

The [Lučenec Synagogue](/europe/slovakia/luenec/synagogue) is the most architecturally significant building in the town — a large Moorish-revival structure that once served one of the largest Jewish communities in the region. The surrounding volcanic hills of the Novohrad region make for pleasant walking and cycling.
