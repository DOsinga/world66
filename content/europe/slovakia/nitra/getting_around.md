---
title: "Getting Around"
type: section
---

The Old Town and castle area are easily walkable — the main sights cluster within a few hundred metres of each other on and around the castle hill. City buses serve the wider urban area. Dražovce church, the main outlying sight, is about four kilometres north and best reached by car or taxi; the bus serves Dražovce village but the last stretch is a walking path.

Cycling is feasible in the flat areas around the city, and bikes can be rented from a few shops near the centre.
