Ghymes Castle is a hilltop ruin above Jelenec in the Tribec Mountains, originally a Gothic fortress from the 13th century. For visitors based in Nitra, its draw is the combination of forest hike, ruined masonry, and views from a ridge rather than a conventional museum experience.

The castle takes some effort to reach, which helps keep it from feeling like a roadside stop. Wear shoes suitable for uneven paths and treat the ruins with care, as much of the site remains open and unrestored.
