---
title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Nitra rewards the visitor who looks up — at the castle on its hill, at the cathedral towers, at the Romanesque churches scattered through the valley. The sights cluster tightly around the Old Town, with the Dražovce church a short drive north as the main outlier. The Ponitrianske museum and the art gallery fill out a half-day of indoor options.
