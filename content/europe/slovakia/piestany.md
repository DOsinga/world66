Piešťany is Slovakia's classic spa town: genteel, leafy, and built around hot mineral water rather than nightlife. It sits on the Váh River in western Slovakia, with the famous Spa Island tucked between river branches and linked to town by the graceful [Colonnade Bridge](/europe/slovakia/piestany/colonnade_bridge).

The reason to come is the spa culture. The thermal water and sulphurous mud have drawn patients for rheumatism and joint treatment for generations, and even if you are not booking a cure, the parks, colonnades, and old bath architecture make Piešťany a pleasant place to slow down. The town is more health resort than postcard old town, but that is part of its appeal.

Spend your first walk along the river, cross to the spa quarter, then loop back through the centre for the [House of Arts](/europe/slovakia/piestany/house_of_arts) or the [Roman Catholic Church](/europe/slovakia/piestany/roman_catholic_church). Aviation and military-history enthusiasts should make time for the [Military History Museum](/europe/slovakia/piestany/military_history_museum) at the airport.

Piešťany works well as a quiet stop between [Bratislava](/europe/slovakia/bratislava), [Trnava](/europe/slovakia/trnava), and the Váh valley. Come for treatment if you need it; otherwise come for a day of parks, water, and the slightly old-fashioned rhythm of a Central European spa.
