Poprad is the gateway city to the High Tatras — the smallest alpine mountain range in Europe and Slovakia's most dramatic landscape. The city itself sits on a plain at about 670 metres, with the jagged Tatra peaks rising sharply to the north. The [Tatra Electric Railway](/europe/slovakia/poprad/tatras_electric_railway) connects Poprad directly to the Tatra resorts of Štrbské Pleso and Starý Smokovec in under an hour.

The most rewarding part of town is [Spišská Sobota](/europe/slovakia/poprad/spiska_sobota), a beautifully preserved medieval suburb with a main square lined by Renaissance and Baroque houses and a fine Gothic church. It feels like a different world from the Soviet-era blocks that dominate modern Poprad.

In winter the city fills with skiers heading for the Tatra slopes. In summer the same mountains offer excellent hiking, with well-marked trails to alpine lakes and ridgeline routes with views into Poland. [AquaCity Poprad](/europe/slovakia/poprad/aquacity), the geothermal water park on the edge of town, is a reliable fallback on a rainy day — outdoor thermal pools with the Tatras rising above them. The Spiš region to the east — including the spectacular ruined Spiš Castle, a UNESCO site — makes for an easy day trip.
