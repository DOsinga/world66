The Church of St. George stands on the main square of Spisska Sobota, the historic district that gives Poprad much of its old-town charm. Its Gothic bones and later fittings make it more rewarding than the modern city centre might lead you to expect.

Visit it as part of a slow circuit of Spisska Sobota's square, with its burgher houses and quieter pace. For travellers using Poprad as a Tatras transport base, the church is a useful reminder that the town has history of its own.
