---
title: When to Go
type: section
---

Poprad has two clear high seasons: winter (December to March) for skiing in the Tatras, and summer (June to September) for hiking. Both seasons see the Tatra railway running frequently and accommodation in demand. December to February can be bitterly cold — temperatures below -15°C are not unusual at the Tatra resorts — but the combination of snow, clear sky, and mountain views is spectacular.

Spring (April to May) and autumn (October to November) are quieter and cheaper. Spring brings wild flowers to the Tatra valleys and the first bearable hiking temperatures. The mountains can be dramatic in autumn mist, and the beech forests on the lower slopes turn a rich gold. In both shoulder seasons you will have trails and towns largely to yourself.
