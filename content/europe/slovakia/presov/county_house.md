The County House is one of the civic buildings that give Prešov's main street its formal, administrative character. It is not a sight to plan a day around, but it helps you read the city as a regional capital rather than just a pretty provincial town.

Pause for the facade as you move along Hlavná ulica. Prešov rewards this kind of slow architectural browsing: churches, palaces, theatres, and official buildings make more sense together than as isolated stops.
