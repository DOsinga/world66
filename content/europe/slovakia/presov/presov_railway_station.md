Prešov railway station is primarily practical, but it is also the threshold most independent travellers use when arriving in the city. The station sits south of the historic centre, close enough to make arrival straightforward.

There is no need to linger unless you enjoy transport architecture. Its real value for visitors is orientation: from here you can connect Prešov with Košice, Poprad, and the wider eastern Slovak rail network without needing a car.
