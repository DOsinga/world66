Veľký Choč is the classic hiking target in the Choč Mountains, popular because the summit opens wide views across northern Slovakia. It is a proper mountain walk rather than a casual town stroll, but on a clear day the payoff is the kind of all-round panorama that explains why locals keep recommending it.
