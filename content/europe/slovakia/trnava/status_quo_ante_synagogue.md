The Status Quo Ante Synagogue is one of the best-preserved synagogues in Slovakia, a 19th-century building in Moorish Revival style that has been carefully restored and now functions as a gallery and cultural space. The exterior is distinctive — ornate facades in a style quite unlike the surrounding baroque architecture — and the interior retains much of its original decorative scheme.

The synagogue sits in the area that was once the heart of Trnava's Jewish quarter, a short walk from the main square. Exhibitions inside document the history of the Jewish community in Trnava and the broader region. Entry is charged and hours are limited; check locally before visiting. It is worth seeking out as a counterpoint to the overwhelming Catholic atmosphere of the rest of the old town.
