Burian Tower is the bell tower beside Holy Trinity Cathedral in the centre of Žilina. The tower is a compact landmark on the edge of Mariánske námestie and gives the cathedral group much of its skyline.

It can usually be climbed only on arranged visits or tours through the tourist information office, but the view over the old centre makes it worth checking availability.
