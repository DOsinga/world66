The Church of Saint Stephen the King, standing alone in the Závodie district west of the city centre, is the oldest building in Žilina. The Romanesque church was built around the 12th century, making it five or six hundred years older than the Renaissance square in the old town. The structure has been modified over the centuries but retains its Romanesque character in the simple nave, the thick walls, and the small rounded windows.

The church is still in use as a parish church. It sits in a quiet cemetery surrounded by old linden trees, a peaceful contrast to the urban energy of the main square. It is a 20-minute walk or a short bus ride from the city centre. Worth the detour for anyone interested in the early medieval history of the region.
