Rosenfeld Palace is one of Žilina's best Art Nouveau buildings, built in 1907 with the confidence and ornament of a city entering the modern age. The facade is the first reason to stop, but the interior is also shown on guided tours.

It is close to the centre and pairs well with the New Synagogue and Mariánske námestie. Check tour times locally, as access can depend on events and exhibitions.
