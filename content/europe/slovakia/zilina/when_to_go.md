---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

Spring and early autumn are the best times, when the Malá Fatra is at its most beautiful and the old town square is pleasant without summer crowds. Summer is warm and good for hiking. Winter brings snow — the ski area at Vrátna usually opens in December and runs to March, making Žilina a practical and affordable base for skiing. The city itself is quiet in winter but the old town has a certain charm under snow.
