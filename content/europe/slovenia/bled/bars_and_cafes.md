---
title: "Bars and Cafes"
type: section
---

Bled is a quiet, small-town place to drink — the action winds down early most nights. The lakeside hotel terraces dominate the afternoon café trade, while a couple of small pubs in the village handle evening drinkers. The Slovenian craft beer scene is small but interesting; ask for **Reservoir Dogs**, **Bevog** or **Pelicon** if you see them on a menu.
