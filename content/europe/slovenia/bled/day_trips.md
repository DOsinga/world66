Bled is a natural base for the Julian Alps. The most obvious half-day trip is [Lake Bohinj](/europe/slovenia/bohinj), 26 km southwest — bigger, wilder, and far less developed than Bled, with the gateway to Triglav National Park and a cable car up Vogel for high mountain panoramas.

[Kranjska Gora](/europe/slovenia/kranjska_gora), 40 km northwest, is the major ski resort in the Slovenian Alps and the start point for the spectacular Vršič Pass road over to the Soča Valley. The medieval town of [Škofja Loka](/europe/slovenia/skofjaloka), 30 km south, makes a lovely afternoon for anyone interested in well-preserved old towns. And [Ljubljana](/europe/slovenia/ljubljana) itself is only an hour away by direct bus or train if you want a complete change of pace.

For walkers with a full day, the trail from Mala Osojnica round the western end of the lake and down to the village of Bohinjska Bela is a quiet alternative to the crowded lake-shore circuit.
