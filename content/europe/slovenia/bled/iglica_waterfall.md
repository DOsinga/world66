Iglica Waterfall is a small but rewarding natural stop near Bohinjska Bela, a short trip from Bled. The water drops through a narrow rock cleft, and the surrounding wall is known locally for climbing routes as well as the brief gorge walk.

It makes a good low-commitment outing when the lakefront is crowded. The visit is quick, but the cool ravine, mossy rock, and village setting give you a sharper taste of the landscape around Bled than another stroll along the promenade.
