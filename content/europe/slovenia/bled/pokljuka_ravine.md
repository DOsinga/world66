Pokljuka Ravine is a narrow limestone gorge northwest of Bled, cut by water from the Pokljuka glaciers. The walk passes high rock walls, small caves, natural bridges, and short constructed sections where the path threads through the ravine.

It is quieter than Vintgar Gorge and feels more like a forest hike than a managed boardwalk attraction. Good footwear helps, especially after rain.
