title: Straža Hill
type: poi
snippet: Easy 646-metre hill with a chairlift, summer toboggan and lake views
---

A 646-metre forested hill rising directly above the southeast corner of the lake. A chairlift runs from the village to within a short walk of the summit, where you find a small chapel, a beer garden and one of the easier high views of the lake.

The main attraction for families is the summer toboggan — a 520-metre steel chute that twists down the hillside; children can ride with an adult, and tickets are sold individually or as multi-ride packs. In winter the hill becomes a modest beginner's ski slope, useful as an introduction before tackling the bigger resorts in the Julian Alps.
