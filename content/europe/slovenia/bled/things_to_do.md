title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Most of Bled's attractions are concentrated around the lake itself or within a few kilometres of it. A full lake circuit on foot or by bike, an hour at the castle, a pletna ride to the island and a short walk through Vintgar Gorge will fill an excellent two-day visit. For sunrise and sunset photographers, the Mala Osojnica viewpoint is unmissable.
