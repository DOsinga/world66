---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

Bled is busy all year, but in very different ways.

**Summer (June–August)** is peak season. The lake is warm enough to swim in, the long days suit walking around the shore and out to the gorge, and every restaurant terrace fills up. It is also when the tour buses arrive — the island, the castle and Vintgar Gorge can all feel crowded in midday hours. Book accommodation well ahead.

**Late spring (May–early June)** and **early autumn (September–early October)** are arguably the best times to visit. The weather is mild, the crowds noticeably thinner, and the surrounding hills are at their most striking — wild flowers in spring, gold and red foliage in autumn.

**Winter (December–February)** turns Bled into a quieter, snowier resort. The lake occasionally freezes over enough for skating, the surrounding ski areas (Vogel, Krvavec, Straža) open for the season, and the village wears Christmas lights. Days are short and many lake-shore restaurants close, but accommodation prices drop sharply outside Christmas-New Year week.
