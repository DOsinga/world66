Bovec is a small mountain town in the upper Soča valley in northwestern Slovenia, surrounded by the Julian Alps. It is the adventure-sports capital of the country. The [Soča river](/europe/slovenia/bovec/soča_river) — famous for its extraordinary emerald colour — runs through the valley and is one of the best white-water kayaking and rafting rivers in Europe. Canyoning, zip-lining, and paragliding are all well-established here.

In winter, the [Kanin ski resort](/europe/slovenia/bovec/kanin_ski_resort) above Bovec is the highest in Slovenia, with snow-sure slopes and views into Italy. The Vršič Pass, connecting the Soča valley to Kranjska Gora over the Julian Alps, is one of the most spectacular mountain roads in Europe and can be combined with a visit to the source of the Soča at its northern end.

The [Boka Waterfall](/europe/slovenia/bovec/boka_waterfall), the highest in Slovenia, is a short hike from the main road. [Kluže Fortress](/europe/slovenia/bovec/kluzhe_fortress), built by the Austro-Hungarians above a narrow gorge, adds a WWI dimension to the valley — the mountains around Bovec were the Isonzo Front for three years of brutal fighting. The town itself is small and functional — a base for activities rather than a destination in its own right — but the surrounding landscape is genuinely extraordinary.
