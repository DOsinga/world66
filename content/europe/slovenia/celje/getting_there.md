---
title: "Getting There"
type: section
---

Celje is on the main Ljubljana–Maribor–Vienna railway line and is easy to reach by train. The journey from [Ljubljana](/europe/slovenia/ljubljana) takes about an hour; from Maribor about 45 minutes. Trains are frequent, comfortable, and cheap. The station is a five-minute walk from the old town.

By car, Celje is directly on the A1 motorway (Ljubljana–Maribor), with a well-signed exit. Driving from Ljubljana takes about an hour; from Maribor about 45 minutes.

The city is also a natural stopping point on the way to or from Austria via the Karavanke tunnel route.
