---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

Celje has a continental climate with warm summers and cold winters. The best time to visit is May through October, when the castle and outdoor sites are at their most enjoyable. Summer temperatures reach the low 30s Celsius, and the Savinja riverbank is lively with café terraces and evening walkers.

Spring (April to May) brings good walking conditions and the surrounding countryside is green. Autumn is particularly pleasant — the Savinja Valley vineyards turn gold, and the castle hill is covered in changing deciduous forest. Winter is cold (temperatures often below zero at night) but the city functions normally and accommodation prices drop.

The old town's Christmas market in December is modest but pleasant — a good excuse to visit if you are in Slovenia in winter.
