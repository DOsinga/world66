---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

The Slovenian Adriatic is best in **May–June** and **September**, when the sea is warm enough to swim, the air is comfortable (20–28°C), and the crowds are thin. July and August are hot, busy, and considerably more expensive — the town is largely Italian holidaymakers and a fair number of Germans and Austrians during those months.

Winter is quiet but pleasant: temperatures rarely drop below freezing, the bora wind can blow hard for a day or two at a time, and most of the seafront restaurants stay open. April brings the Šape Festival, a local seafood and wine event, and August the Izola Fest of street performance and music. The Izola International Film Festival, mid-June, is small but well-regarded for independent and Balkan cinema.
