Planina pod Golico is a mountain village above Jesenice, known for its position under Golica in the Karawanks. It gives you the alpine side of the municipality after the industrial story of the valley floor.

The village is most appealing in late spring and early summer, when the meadows are bright and the hills feel close. It is a good reminder that Jesenice is both a steel town and a gateway to mountain country.
