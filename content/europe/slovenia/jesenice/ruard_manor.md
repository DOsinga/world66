Ruard Manor stands in the Sava neighbourhood, close to the old industrial quarter. Like Kos Manor, it hints at the wealth and administration behind Jesenice's metalworking past, when local industry needed owners, managers, and social spaces as much as furnaces.

See it as part of the Stara Sava circuit rather than as a standalone attraction. The building helps connect the human and economic sides of the ironworks landscape.
