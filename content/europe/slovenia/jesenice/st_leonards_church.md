St. Leonard's Church is a small historic stop in Jesenice's older townscape. It is not the main reason to come, but it balances the industrial sites with a reminder of local parish life before and during the town's expansion.

Visit if you are already walking through Murova or linking Kos Manor with the station area. Jesenice makes most sense in these layered fragments rather than in one grand set-piece.
