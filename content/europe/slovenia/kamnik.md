Kamnik sits just north of [Ljubljana](/europe/slovenia/ljubljana), close enough for an easy day trip but with a much older mountain-town feel. It was once an important Carniolan trading town, and the old centre still has a tight medieval shape: Šutna Street, Mali Grad hill, church towers, small squares, and the Kamnik-Savinja Alps rising sharply behind the roofs.

The town itself is worth a slow morning. [Mali Grad](/europe/slovenia/kamnik/mali_grad) gives the classic rooftop view, while [Šutna](/europe/slovenia/kamnik/sutna) is the best street for seeing old Kamnik at walking pace. The ruined [Stari Grad](/europe/slovenia/kamnik/stari_grad) castle above town is rougher, but the view makes sense of why Kamnik mattered on the old trade routes.

Kamnik becomes much stronger when you include the valley and plateau above it. [Velika Planina](/europe/slovenia/kamnik/velika_planina), reached by cable car from the Kamniška Bistrica valley or by hiking, is one of Slovenia's most memorable alpine landscapes: a high pasture scattered with oval herdsmen's huts. Farther up the valley, [Kamniška Bistrica](/europe/slovenia/kamnik/kamniska_bistrica) has cold karst springs, forest paths, and trailheads into the high mountains.

This is not a big-city stop. It works because it gives you medieval Slovenia, alpine pasture culture, and real mountain air in a single day.
