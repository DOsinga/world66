Kamniski vrh is a popular local hike above Kamnik. It is a relatively short climb, often done as a half-day outing from the valley, but the route can be confusing enough that a map or offline navigation is useful.

The appeal is the quick access to open mountain views without committing to one of the longer routes deeper into the Kamnik-Savinja Alps.
