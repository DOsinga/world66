Mekinje Ursuline Monastery is a historic religious complex just outside Kamnik's old centre. The site was first mentioned in the 12th century and later became associated with the Ursuline order, leaving a substantial architectural and cultural legacy.

The monastery is best treated as a quiet heritage stop while exploring Kamnik rather than as a large museum. Combine it with Mali Grad, Šutna, and the walk up to Stari Grad.
