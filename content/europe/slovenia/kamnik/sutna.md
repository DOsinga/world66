Šutna is the street where Kamnik feels most like an old Carniolan town rather than a commuter suburb of Ljubljana. It runs through the historic centre below Mali Grad, with old houses, signboards, the parish church of Mary Immaculate, and side views toward the hills.

There is no grand museum moment here. The point is the walk itself, especially if you link it with Mali Grad, Glavni trg, and a coffee stop in the centre.
