Sveti Primož nad Kamnikom is a hilltop church above Črna and Stahovica, reached by a short but steady walk from the valley. The church is known for early 16th-century frescoes, while the open slope around it gives wide views over Kamnik and, in clear weather, toward Ljubljana. It works well as a quieter historic stop paired with Velika Planina or Kamniška Bistrica.
