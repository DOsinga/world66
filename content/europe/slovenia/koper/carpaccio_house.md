Carpaccio House is a 14th-century building traditionally associated with the Venetian painter Vittore Carpaccio. Whether or not the attribution is certain, the house is a useful small stop for understanding Koper's long Venetian layer beyond the main square.

It is close to the old centre, so it fits easily into a walk between Titov Trg, the waterfront, and the cathedral.
