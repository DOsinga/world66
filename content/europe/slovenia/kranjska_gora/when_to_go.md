---
title: When to Go
type: section
---

Winter is ski season, with the most reliable atmosphere from late December through February, though snow conditions vary at lower elevations. March can be excellent for Planica ski-jumping events and longer light.

Summer and early autumn are best for hiking, cycling, Lake Jasna, Zelenci, and the Vršič Pass. The pass road is normally a warm-season route; snow and closures can affect it outside the main driving season.
