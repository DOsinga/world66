[Lake Bohinj](/europe/slovenia/lakebohinj/lake_bohinj) sits in the Julian Alps of northwestern Slovenia, not far from the more famous [Lake Bled](/europe/slovenia/bled) but a world apart in atmosphere. Where Bled caters to coach tours, Bohinj is quieter, wilder, and surrounded by proper mountains — Triglav, Slovenia's highest peak, rises just to the north.

The lake itself is stunning: deep blue water backed by forested slopes and limestone peaks. In summer the area is excellent for hiking, swimming, and mountain biking; in winter the [Vogel ski resort](/europe/slovenia/lakebohinj/vogel_cable_car) offers runs directly above the lake with spectacular views. The [Savica waterfall](/europe/slovenia/lakebohinj/savica_waterfall), a short hike from the western end of the lake, is one of Slovenia's most photogenic natural sites, and the medieval [Church of St John the Baptist](/europe/slovenia/lakebohinj/church_of_st_john_the_baptist) by the lake's eastern bridge is one of the most photographed buildings in the country.

The area has all the beauty of the Austrian or Swiss Alps at a fraction of the price. The small villages around the lake — [Ribcev Laz](/europe/slovenia/lakebohinj/ribcev_laz), [Stara Fuzina](/europe/slovenia/lakebohinj/stara_fuzina), [Ukanc](/europe/slovenia/lakebohinj/ukanc) — have good accommodation and a growing food scene. Most of the lake's surroundings sit inside [Triglav National Park](/europe/slovenia/lakebohinj/triglav_national_park), which keeps development away from the foreshore and the views unspoiled.
