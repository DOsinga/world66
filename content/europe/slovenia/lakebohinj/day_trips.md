Lake Bohinj makes a quiet base for trips into the surrounding Julian Alps. The most obvious is [Lake Bled](/europe/slovenia/bled), 30 minutes east, which is busier and more developed but worth a half-day for the church on the island and the castle. The Bohinj Railway connects through to the Soca Valley on the other side of the mountains — Most na Soci, [Kobarid](/europe/slovenia/kobarid) and [Bovec](/europe/slovenia/bovec) are all reachable in a long day. [Kranjska Gora](/europe/slovenia/kranjskagora), the ski resort on the Italian-Austrian border, is about an hour's drive over the Vrsic pass in summer (closed in winter).
