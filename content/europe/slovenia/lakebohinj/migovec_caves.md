The Migovec Caves form a vast Alpine cave system inside Mount Tolmin Migovec, with tens of kilometres of mapped passages and nearly a kilometre of vertical depth. They are part of the wilder karst landscape above Lake Bohinj rather than an easy roadside show cave.

For most travellers the appeal is in knowing what lies under the mountain while hiking nearby. Serious cave visits require proper local guidance and equipment.
