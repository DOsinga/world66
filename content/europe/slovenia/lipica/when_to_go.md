---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

Lipica is open year-round but the dressage performance schedule varies by season. The main classical performance season runs from April through October, with shows several times a week. In winter the programme is reduced and some training sessions replace public performances — still worth watching but less formal. Check the stud farm's schedule before visiting, as performances can fill up and advance booking is recommended in summer.

Spring and early autumn are ideal: mild temperatures, fewer tour groups than July and August, and the chance to see foals born in spring growing into their coats. The karst landscape is at its most pleasant in May and June when the scrub is in flower.
