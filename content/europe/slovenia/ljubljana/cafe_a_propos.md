One of the better riverfront cafes, occupying a good position on Petkovškovo nabrežje with a large terrace directly on the Ljubljanica embankment. The menu runs to sandwiches, salads, and light meals as well as coffee and drinks. Busy from mid-morning through late evening in summer. This is the kind of place that makes the Ljubljana riverfront so agreeable: no particular distinction, but a good spot to sit with a coffee and watch the river.
