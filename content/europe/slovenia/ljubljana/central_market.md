The covered market running along the right bank of the Ljubljanica between the Triple Bridge and the Dragon Bridge is one of Plečnik's most successful works in Ljubljana. A long colonnaded arcade fronts the river; behind it a roofed market hall sells vegetables, dairy, meat, and a good selection of Slovenian produce. The open-air market on Vodnik Square extends the indoor section on weekday mornings.

This is a working market, not a tourist spectacle. The best time to visit is Saturday morning, when farmers from the surrounding countryside bring their produce. You can buy raw milk, artisan cheese, honey, mushrooms in season, and local wine. The view from the colonnade across the river to the old town is one of the best in the city.
