Kinoteka, the Slovenian Cinematheque, is Ljubljana's home for serious film culture. Its programme leans toward classics, retrospectives, archival screenings, and international cinema rather than standard multiplex releases.

It is an excellent evening option if the schedule lines up, particularly after a day of architecture and cafe-hopping. Check language and subtitle details before booking, since the value of a screening depends on what you can follow.
