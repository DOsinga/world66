Prešeren Square is the social heart of Ljubljana. The broad space in front of the Franciscan Church of the Annunciation is where locals meet, where concerts and events are held, and where the city feels most alive. The pink Franciscan facade closes the northern end; the Triple Bridge and the river lie to the east; Čop Street extends west toward Tivoli Park.

The monument to France Prešeren — Slovenia's national poet — stands at the centre of the square. It is impossible to walk through Ljubljana without crossing this space several times. In summer the square is animated; in December a Christmas market fills it.
