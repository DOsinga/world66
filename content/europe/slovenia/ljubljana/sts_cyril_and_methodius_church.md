Sts. Cyril and Methodius Church is Ljubljana's Serbian Orthodox church, built in the 1930s with five domes topped by gold crosses. Step inside for the frescoed interior, painted in the late 20th century, which feels distinct from the Baroque Catholic churches around the centre.
