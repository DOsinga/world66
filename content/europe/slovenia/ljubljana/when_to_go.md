---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

Ljubljana has a continental climate with warm summers and cold winters. The best months to visit are May through September, when temperatures are comfortable (20–30°C) and the outdoor cafe culture along the river is at its best.

July and August are the warmest and busiest months. The city does not get as crowded as Venice or Prague, but popular restaurants and guesthouses fill up. The Ljubljana Summer Festival runs from late June to mid-August, with classical music, theatre, and opera performances in the courtyard of Ljubljana Castle and at Kongresni trg.

Winter (December–February) is cold and sometimes foggy, but the city is manageable and the Christmas market on Prešeren Square draws local visitors. Hotel prices drop significantly outside summer.
