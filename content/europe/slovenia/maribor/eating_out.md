---
title: "Eating Out"
type: section
---

Maribor's food scene is rooted in Styrian tradition — hearty, meat-heavy, and better than its reputation. The local wine country gives every menu a regional character. Bučno olje (pumpkin seed oil) appears on everything from salads to ice cream. Local dishes worth seeking out include Pohorje roast (poharski zrezek), a pork preparation specific to the region, and štruklji, rolled dumplings that come sweet or savoury.

The best restaurants cluster around the old town and the Lent promenade. The university gives the city a younger eating culture too — there are good cafes and simple restaurants in the streets around the main campus.
