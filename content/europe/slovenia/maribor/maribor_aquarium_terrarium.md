Maribor's Aquarium-Terrarium sits in the City Park and offers a compact collection of fish, reptiles, and amphibians. It is a modest attraction, but its location makes it easy to combine with the park, the Three Ponds area, and a walk north of the old centre.

This is mainly a family-friendly stop rather than a must-see for every visitor. It earns its place because it is central, inexpensive in time, and gives children something concrete to do between Maribor's wine, riverfront, and architectural sights.
