---
title: "Day Trips"
type: section
---

Nova Gorica is well placed for excursions across western Slovenia and into northern Italy. The Vipava Valley unfolds south-east; the Soca Valley begins just north; and Italian Friuli is across the border.

The Soca Valley's emerald rapids around [Bovec](/europe/slovenia/bovec) and the alpine meadows of the Julian Alps are an hour by car. Closer in, the Vipava Valley villages and the wine route through [Goriska Brda](/europe/slovenia/nova_gorica/goriska_brda) make easy half-days. The dramatic Postojna and Skocjan cave systems are about an hour south on the motorway, and [Trieste](/europe/italy/triesteandgrado/trieste) — with its grand seafront cafes and Hapsburg-era architecture — is forty minutes by car or a couple of buses by public transport.
