---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

The Vipava Valley enjoys a near-Mediterranean climate, milder than most of Slovenia. Summers are warm but tempered by the proximity of the mountains; winters are cool and damp rather than freezing, though the bora wind can sweep down from the karst plateau and rattle the windows.

**Spring (April-May)** is lovely — vineyards greening up, cherry blossom in Brda, comfortable hiking on Sabotin and Skabrijel.

**Late summer to early autumn (September-October)** is the wine country sweet spot. Harvest runs through September, the days are warm, the evenings cool, and the leaves in the vineyards turn copper and gold.

**Winter** can be a quiet time to visit, with low prices and the Italian cafes of Gorizia open for unhurried afternoons. The 2025 European Capital of Culture programmes spread events across the calendar — check the official site before you book.
