---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

Novo Mesto is pleasant from April through October. Spring and early summer bring lush green hills and the Krka at its most attractive. July and August are warm (25–30°C) and good for swimming in the river or kayaking. Autumn, when the surrounding vineyards change colour, is particularly atmospheric.

Winter is quiet and cold; most of the tourist infrastructure scales back, but the cathedral and museum remain open. There is no particular reason to avoid the winter unless you want to swim.

The Cviček wine region holds harvest events in September and October, which is a good time to visit if you are interested in the local wine.
