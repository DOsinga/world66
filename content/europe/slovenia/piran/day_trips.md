---
title: Day trips
type: section
---

Piran sits within easy reach of half a dozen worthwhile excursions. **[Secovlje Salt Pans](/europe/slovenia/piran/secovlje_salt_pans)** are the obvious half-day, ten kilometres south on the Croatian border. **Portoroz** next door is the developed beach resort and an evening's pleasant walk away.

**Koper**, twenty minutes up the coast by bus, is the working port city of Slovene Istria with a small Venetian core well worth an afternoon. Further afield, **Trieste** in Italy is an hour by car and offers a complete change of pace — a grand Habsburg seafront and one of the most distinctive coffee cultures in Europe.

To the south, the medieval Croatian hill town of **Motovun** and the truffle country around it are an hour and a half by car; the bigger Croatian coastal town of **Rovinj** is two hours. East of Piran, the Slovene karst region — caves at **Postojna** and the medieval cliffside castle at **Predjama** — is also reachable as a long day trip.
