The Magical World of Shells Museum is a small natural-history museum in Piran with thousands of shells and marine specimens from Slovenia and farther afield. It is run with a collector's enthusiasm rather than a big-museum polish.

It works best as a short stop between walks through the old town, especially with children or on a wet day. Pair it with the aquarium if you want a light marine-themed hour in Piran.
