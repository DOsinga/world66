---
title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Piran's sights are all within easy walking distance of each other. Start at [Tartini Square](/europe/slovenia/piran/tartini_square) and work your way up toward the [cathedral](/europe/slovenia/piran/cathedral_of_st_george), then follow the [city walls](/europe/slovenia/piran/city_walls) along the waterfront. The town is small enough that getting lost is half the fun. For more, the smaller medieval [Trg 1 Maja](/europe/slovenia/piran/trg_1_maja), the [Tartini House](/europe/slovenia/piran/tartini_house), and the rocky tip at [Punta](/europe/slovenia/piran/punta_lighthouse) round out a full day.
