A promenade cafe with tables outside facing the sea and a reliably good espresso — the Italian influence on this coast runs deep enough that the coffee culture is considerably better than the Slovenian average. The name nods to the artistic traditions of the broader region; it is the kind of place that fills with a morning crowd of hotel guests and afternoon visitors from Piran walking along the seafront. In the evening it transitions to an aperitivo bar with local wines and snacks.
