Ojstra Pec Viewpoint is a short woodland walk from Srednja Dobrava, opening suddenly onto views over Radovljica, the Sava valley, and the Karavanke range. The cliff edge sits high enough above the valley floor to make the view feel larger than the modest walk suggests.

If you keep going, another viewpoint at Zjavka has a picnic spot and looks toward Kamna Gorica. It is a good low-key outing when you want countryside without committing to a full mountain hike.
