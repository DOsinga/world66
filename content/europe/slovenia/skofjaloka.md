Škofja Loka is one of Slovenia's loveliest old towns, close enough to [Ljubljana](/europe/slovenia/ljubljana) for an easy day trip but with a different, more enclosed character. The town sits where the Selca and Poljane Sora rivers meet, with a castle above, bridges below, and a compact medieval centre that still feels like a real place rather than a museum set.

Start in the old town squares, then climb to [Škofja Loka Castle](/europe/slovenia/skofjaloka/skofja_loka_castle) for the view and the museum. The [Capuchin Bridge](/europe/slovenia/skofjaloka/capuchin_bridge) is the classic crossing, while the [Capuchin Monastery](/europe/slovenia/skofjaloka/capuchin_monastery) adds a literary and religious thread to the town's story.

Škofja Loka rewards slow wandering more than box-ticking. Walk into [Puštal](/europe/slovenia/skofjaloka/pustal), look back at the castle, and if you have transport continue to [Crngrob Church](/europe/slovenia/skofjaloka/crngrob_church), one of the area's most atmospheric rural churches.

It makes a good counterpoint to [Bled](/europe/slovenia/bled). Bled has the lake and the drama; Škofja Loka has stone lanes, river bends, painted facades, and the quieter confidence of a town that has been handsome for centuries.
