Ajman Castle is a 17th-century manor near Sveti Duh, northeast of Škofja Loka. It is a secondary stop, but it broadens the town's story beyond the obvious medieval core and into the manor landscape of the surrounding villages.

Go only if you are already exploring by car or bicycle. For a first visit, the old town and main castle matter more; Ajman is for travellers who enjoy following the smaller aristocratic and rural traces around a historic town.
