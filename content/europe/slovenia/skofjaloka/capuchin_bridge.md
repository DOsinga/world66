The Capuchin Bridge is the most photogenic crossing in Škofja Loka, a stone bridge connecting the old town with the Capuchin side of the river. It is small, simple, and exactly the kind of structure that makes the town feel intact.

Cross it slowly and look back toward the roofs and castle. The view explains why Škofja Loka is such a satisfying day trip: the pieces of town, river, hill, and church line up without much effort.
