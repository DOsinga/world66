The Capuchin Monastery stands just beyond the bridge and adds a quieter religious note to the old town. Its library is associated with the Škofja Loka Passion Play tradition, one of the cultural threads that makes the town more than a pretty facade.

Opening arrangements can vary, so treat it as a worthwhile stop if access is available rather than a guaranteed museum visit. Even from outside, it helps frame the Capuchin side of town.
