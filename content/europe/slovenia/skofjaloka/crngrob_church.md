Crngrob Church stands north of Škofja Loka in a rural setting, away from the neat medieval core. Its scale and atmosphere make it one of the more rewarding short excursions from town, especially if you like churches that feel rooted in landscape rather than squares.

You will need a car, bicycle, or a longer walk to make the visit comfortable. Combine it with a countryside loop rather than treating it as a quick urban sight.
