Javorca Memorial Church, formally the Memorial Church of the Holy Spirit, was built by Austro-Hungarian soldiers during the First World War for those killed on the Soča Front. Its remote mountain setting and careful wooden interior make it one of the valley's most moving war memorials.

The church is a deliberate side trip rather than a quick roadside stop. Go for the combination of architecture, mountain scenery, and First World War history; check access conditions before setting out, especially outside summer.
