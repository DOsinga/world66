title: Things to Do
type: section
---

The Soča Valley divides naturally into active pursuits on the water and reflective pursuits on the hillsides. [White-water rafting](/europe/slovenia/socavalley/white_water_rafting) is the valley's primary sport — the Soča is one of Europe's best rivers for it, and commercial operators in Bovec run daily trips. Kayaking, canyoning, and paragliding are all well-established.

For history, the [Kobarid Museum](/europe/slovenia/socavalley/kobarid_museum) is unmissable — one of the finest First World War museums anywhere. The [Italian Charnel House](/europe/slovenia/socavalley/italian_charnel_house) above Kobarid and [Kluže Fortress](/europe/slovenia/socavalley/kluzhe_fortress) north of Bovec give physical context to the conflict.

For landscape, the [Soča Trail](/europe/slovenia/socavalley/soca_trail) follows the impossibly blue-green river through the national park. The [Boka Waterfall](/europe/slovenia/socavalley/boka_waterfall) is the highest in Slovenia. Drive or walk the [Vršič Pass](/europe/slovenia/socavalley/vrtic_pass) in clear weather for views over the Julian Alps.
