Tolmin Gorges are the lowest entrance to Triglav National Park and one of the Soca Valley's best short walks. The route follows deep river channels and takes in the confluence of two Alpine streams, Bear's Head rock, a thermal spring, Devil's Bridge, and Dante's Cave.
