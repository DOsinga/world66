Tolmin Museum presents the cultural history of the Soča region, with ethnological and archaeological collections. One of its main exhibits covers the Tolmin peasant uprising of 1713, a key local event in the history of the valley.

It is a good indoor stop for context before exploring the Soča Valley's villages, First World War sites, and river landscapes.
