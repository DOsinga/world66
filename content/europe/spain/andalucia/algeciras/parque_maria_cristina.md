Parque Maria Cristina is the main city park of Algeciras, a shaded green in the upper part of town with mature trees, ornamental planting, and benches that are busy with local residents at all hours. The park has a slightly formal character — fountains, trimmed hedges, paved paths — that contrasts pleasantly with the rough-and-tumble port below.

It is a good place to sit and watch Algeciras life without any tourist pressure. The Hotel Reina Cristina, a historic establishment associated with the 1906 Algeciras Conference, is nearby. The park connects to the Parque de la Conferencia to the south, giving a pleasant stretch of green in the city.
