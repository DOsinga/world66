---
title: "Getting There"
type: section
---

Almería has its own airport, Aeropuerto de Almería, about 9 kilometres east of the city, with flights from several European cities, particularly in summer. The railway station is central, with services to Granada (about 2.5 hours), Madrid (via Antequera), and along the coast. The bus is often faster and more frequent than the train for connections within Andalusia, particularly to Granada, Málaga, and Seville. The port has regular ferry services to Melilla and Morocco.

By car, Almería sits at the end of the A-92 motorway from Granada and the A-7 coastal road. Parking in the city centre is manageable outside peak summer weeks.
