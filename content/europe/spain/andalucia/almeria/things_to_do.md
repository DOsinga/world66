---
title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

The [Alcazaba](/europe/spain/andalucia/almeria/alcazaba_almeria) is the essential sight — one of the largest Moorish fortresses in Spain, free for EU citizens, and considerably less visited than the Alhambra. The [Cathedral](/europe/spain/andalucia/almeria/almeria_cathedral) below it is extraordinary for its fortress-like exterior, designed to withstand pirate raids. The [Civil War Air Raid Shelters](/europe/spain/andalucia/almeria/air_raid_shelters) offer an unforgettable underground tour through tunnels dug in 1937. The [Museum of Almería](/europe/spain/andalucia/almeria/museo_almeria) has strong archaeological collections, particularly its Copper Age material from Los Millares. The [Teatro Cervantes](/europe/spain/andalucia/almeria/teatro_cervantes) is Almería's grandest nineteenth-century building and its main performance venue.
