---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

Almería is the hottest and driest city in Europe — July and August regularly reach 35°C with almost no rain, and the sun is intense. For sightseeing, this makes the middle of summer uncomfortable; the [Alcazaba](/europe/spain/andalucia/almeria/alcazaba_almeria) is largely unshaded and brutal in the midday heat. If you visit in July or August, start early and retreat to the old town by late morning.

Spring (April to June) and autumn (September and October) are the best times to visit: warm enough for the beach and comfortable for walking, with the light in the desert landscape to the north having a quality that photographers particularly prize. The Semana Santa (Holy Week) processions in late March or April are among the most atmospheric in Andalusia.

Winter is mild — rarely below 10°C — and the city is quiet, prices are low, and you will have the [Cathedral](/europe/spain/andalucia/almeria/almeria_cathedral) largely to yourself.
