Golf Club Almerimar is an 18-hole Gary Player design integrated into the resort's original layout, with a second course added later to accommodate the demand from the British and northern European visitors who come specifically to play golf in the Almería sun. The climate — over 320 sunny days per year, mild winters — makes this one of the most playable courses in Spain across the full calendar year.

The courses are not technically demanding by championship standards, but the conditions are consistently good: well-maintained fairways, the desert Almería backdrop, and temperatures that allow comfortable play from September through May even when the rest of northern Europe is in the depths of winter. Tee times can usually be booked through the resort without much advance planning outside of the peak holiday weeks. Green fees are moderate by Spanish golf resort standards.
