---
title: Getting There
type: section
---

Almonaster la Real is in the Sierra de Aracena, about 100 kilometres northwest of [Sevilla](/europe/spain/andalucia/sevilla). By car, take the A-66 motorway north from Sevilla toward Zafra, then the N-435 west toward Huelva and follow local roads to Almonaster — the journey takes about 90 minutes.

There is a railway station at Almonaster-Cerro del Andévalo, on the Huelva–Zafra line, though services are infrequent (a few trains a day at best) and the station is not in the village itself. Most visitors arrive by car. [Aracena](/europe/spain/andalucia/aracena), 18 kilometres east, is the main hub for the sierra and has better bus connections from Huelva and Sevilla.
