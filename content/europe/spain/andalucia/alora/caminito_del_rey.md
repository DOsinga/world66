The Caminito del Rey is a 7.7-kilometre gorge walk through the Desfiladero de los Gaitanes, about 12 kilometres north of Álora. Álora's railway station is one of the main access points: you can walk from the station or take local transport to the southern entrance at El Chorro village. The path is one-way, and most visitors need to arrange a return from the northern exit at Ardales.

Book online well in advance — entry is capped and the walk has become very popular since the restoration. The scenery in the gorge is genuinely exceptional: sheer limestone walls, wooden walkways pinned above the river, and tunnels cut through the rock. Allow about four hours for the walk itself, plus travel time.
