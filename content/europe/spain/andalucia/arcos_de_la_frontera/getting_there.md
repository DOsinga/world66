---
title: Getting There
type: section
---

Arcos lies about 30 km east of Jerez and is one of the natural starts or finishes for a white-village driving route. Jerez has the nearest railway station and airport with useful connections.

**By bus:** Buses link Arcos with Jerez and some nearby towns, but service frequency varies. It works for a simple day trip; it is less flexible for a multi-village loop.

**By car:** The old town streets are narrow and steep. Park in the lower town or signed visitor areas, then walk or take a local shuttle/taxi upward if needed.
