---
title: Things to Do
type: section
---

Arroyo de la Miel's main draws are the cable car up Monte Calamorro and the curious collection of monuments scattered around Benalmádena municipality — an enormous Buddhist stupa, a castle dedicated to Christopher Columbus, and a small but well-presented archaeology museum. The old town centre is worth an hour's wander, and Tivoli World provides a retro amusement park experience for families.
