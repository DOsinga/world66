A small but well-presented municipal museum in Benalmádena Pueblo, focused on the pre-Columbian cultures of the Americas as well as local archaeological finds from the Málaga province. The collection includes ceramics, figurines, and everyday objects from Aztec, Maya, and Inca civilisations alongside Iberian and Roman pieces from the surrounding area. It is a quiet place to spend an hour, and free to enter. Located in the old village of Benalmádena Pueblo, about 3 km from Arroyo de la Miel centre.
