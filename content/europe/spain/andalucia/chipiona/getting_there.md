---
title: Getting There
type: section
---

The nearest airport is Jerez (XRY), about 35 km southeast, with seasonal connections to several northern European cities. Seville's larger airport is around 100 km away and easier from further afield. From either, buses run via Sanlúcar de Barrameda to Chipiona — the Sanlúcar–Chipiona service is frequent and takes 20 minutes. Driving is the simplest option: the A-480 connects to the AP-4 motorway and on to Jerez and Cádiz.

There is no railway station in Chipiona itself; the nearest is in Jerez.
