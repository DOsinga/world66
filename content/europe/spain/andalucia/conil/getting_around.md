---
title: Getting Around
type: section
---

The old town and the main beaches are walkable from each other. Conil is small enough that you rarely need transport within the core area — the centro and Playa de los Bateles are ten minutes apart on foot.

**For the northern beaches**, particularly Cala del Aceite and the coves beyond it, a bicycle or car makes a real difference. Bike rental shops operate along the beach road in summer. The paths north along the coast are mostly flat with a few short climbs at the headlands.

**Local buses** run in summer between the town centre and some of the more distant beaches, including El Palmar, but services are infrequent outside peak season. Check current timetables on arrival.

**Taxis** are available from the rank near the bus stop on Avenida de la Playa. In July and August the waits can be long; it's worth getting the number of a local taxi company.

**A car** is genuinely useful for day trips to [Vejer de la Frontera](/europe/spain/andalucia/vejer), the Cape of Trafalgar, and the inland white towns. Parking in the centre in summer is miserable — most people park on the periphery and walk.
