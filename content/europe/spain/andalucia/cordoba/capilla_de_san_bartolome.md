Capilla de San Bartolome is a small 15th-century Gothic-Mudejar chapel in Cordoba's old Jewish quarter. Its tilework, plaster decoration, and vaulting make it a compact but rewarding stop, especially when combined with the nearby synagogue and lanes of the Juderia.
