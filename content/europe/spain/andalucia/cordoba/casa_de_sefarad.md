Casa de Sefarad is a small museum in a 14th-century house beside Cordoba's historic synagogue. Its rooms cover Sephardic Jewish culture, domestic life, festivals, music, the old Jewish quarter, Maimonides, the Inquisition, and the survival of Jewish traditions among converts. It is compact, but the setting makes it a useful companion to the synagogue and the Juderia.
