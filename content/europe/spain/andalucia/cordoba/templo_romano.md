The Templo Romano is a set of Roman temple ruins a short walk from Cordoba's main square. The temple dates to the 1st century CE, during the reign of Domitian, though the standing columns are reconstructed. It is one of the clearest visible traces of Roman Cordoba, and it is especially atmospheric when lit at night.
