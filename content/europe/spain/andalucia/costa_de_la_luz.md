The Costa de la Luz — the "Coast of Light" — faces the Atlantic across Cádiz and Huelva provinces, and feels a world apart from the built-up Mediterranean costas. Its beaches are broad, wind-blown, and often empty, backed by dunes and pine. [Tarifa](/europe/spain/andalucia/tarifa), at the tip, is Europe's windsurfing and kitesurfing capital and the jumping-off point for Morocco; [Cádiz](/europe/spain/andalucia/cadiz) is one of the oldest cities in the West; and white towns like [Vejer](/europe/spain/andalucia/vejer) and [Conil](/europe/spain/andalucia/conil) sit just inland.
