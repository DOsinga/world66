The Costa del Sol runs along the Mediterranean shore of Málaga province, a near-continuous strip of beaches, promenades, and resort towns backed by the Sierra Blanca. Cosmopolitan [Málaga](/europe/spain/andalucia/malaga) anchors it in the east with its museums and old town; glamorous [Marbella](/europe/spain/andalucia/marbella) and Puerto Banús draw the yacht set in the west; and family resorts like [Torremolinos](/europe/spain/andalucia/torremolinos), [Fuengirola](/europe/spain/andalucia/fuengirola), and [Benalmádena](/europe/spain/andalucia/benalmadena) fill the coast between. [Nerja](/europe/spain/andalucia/nerja), to the east, keeps a more village feel.
