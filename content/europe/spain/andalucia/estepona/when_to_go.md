---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

Estepona enjoys one of the most reliably sunny climates in Europe. Summer (June–September) is dry and hot, with temperatures regularly above 30°C and sea temperatures ideal for swimming. Spring and autumn are the best times for combining beach weather with comfortable sightseeing — April and May in particular have mild temperatures and very little rain. Winter is mild by northern European standards (10–15°C by day) and the town is quiet but not dead. The annual street art festival brings a flurry of activity in late summer.
