Fuengirola is primarily a beach town, and the beach is what most people come for. But the medieval castle, the surprisingly excellent zoo, and the old town quarter give visitors enough to fill a day or two between beach sessions.

The hilltop village of [Mijas](/europe/spain/andalucia/fuengirola/mijas), 15 minutes inland, is the most worthwhile excursion. For more serious day trips, Málaga and Marbella are both under an hour by car or train — Fuengirola sits almost exactly between them.
