A straightforward Andalucian bar on the main square, Bar La Copa is where Gaucín's daily rhythms play out: coffee and tostadas in the morning, cold beer and tapas in the evening. The outdoor tables under the trees fill up during the paseo hours. Nothing fancy — just the reliable backbone of village life, with cheap drinks and a welcoming atmosphere for walkers who've just come down from the hills.
