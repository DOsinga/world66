Centro Jose Guerrero is a small museum devoted to Granada-born abstract expressionist Jose Guerrero. The permanent collection sits on the upper floor, while the lower galleries host changing exhibitions of contemporary art and photography. Its central location makes it easy to pair with the cathedral and Royal Chapel.
