Paseo de los Tristes is one of Granada's loveliest short walks, running above the Darro below the Alhambra walls. The old funeral-route name is gloomy, but the place is not: terraces, stone bridges, river noise, and palace views make it a natural pause between Carrera del Darro, the Albaicin, and the climb toward Sacromonte.
