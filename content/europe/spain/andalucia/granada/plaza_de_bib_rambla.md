Plaza de Bib-Rambla is an old market square near Granada Cathedral, once a busy Moorish and later Christian ceremonial space. Today it is gentler: flowers, a fountain, cafe tables, and a good pause between the cathedral, the Royal Chapel, and the Alcaicería.
