Granada's main attractions divide between the Alhambra complex (requiring a separate timed ticket) and the surrounding historic city. The Nasrid Palaces inside the Alhambra are the non-negotiable priority — book weeks ahead for peak season. The rest of the city rewards slower exploration: the Albaicín neighbourhood, the hammams, the Sacromonte caves, the cathedral quarter.

The filter below covers the whole range: major sights, museums, neighbourhoods, and the more unusual finds like the Corral del Carbón and the forged-relic catacombs at the Sacromonte abbey.
