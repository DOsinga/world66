Guadalteba is an area of outstanding natural beauty tucked into the hills of Málaga province, where a chain of reservoirs lies surrounded by mountains and neat rows of olive trees. The [Embalse del Conde de Guadalhorce](/europe/spain/andalucia/guadalteba/embalse_del_conde_de_guadalhorce) is the most spectacular — a turquoise lake cupped between limestone cliffs — and several restaurants on the hilltops offer views across the whole comarca.

The area has built a reputation as one of the best paragliding destinations in Andalucía. Beyond that, there is no shortage of things to do: climbing, horseriding, canoeing, kayaking, fishing, hiking, and cycling all have established circuits here. [El Caminito del Rey](/europe/spain/andalucia/guadalteba/el_caminito_del_rey), threading through the dramatic Garganta del Chorro gorge on a restored cliff walkway, is one of the most extraordinary walks in Europe and needs to be booked well in advance.

For those with a taste for prehistory, the [Cueva de Ardales](/europe/spain/andalucia/guadalteba/cueva_de_ardales) contains Paleolithic paintings of exceptional antiquity. The [Sierra de Huma](/europe/spain/andalucia/guadalteba/sierra_de_huma) offers rewarding hiking with panoramic views, and the [Castillo de Teba](/europe/spain/andalucia/guadalteba/castillo_de_teba) in the village of Teba holds an unexpected piece of Scottish history.

Guadalteba sits at the heart of Andalucía, making it an ideal base for day trips. [Ronda](/europe/spain/andalucia/ronda) is just 20 minutes away and [Antequera](/europe/spain/andalucia/antequera) half an hour. [Sevilla](/europe/spain/andalucia/sevilla), [Granada](/europe/spain/andalucia/granada), [Córdoba](/europe/spain/andalucia/cordoba), Jerez, and [Málaga](/europe/spain/andalucia/malaga) are all within easy reach by car or train.
