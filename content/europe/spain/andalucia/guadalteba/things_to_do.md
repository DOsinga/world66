title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

The Guadalteba comarca earns its keep on activity, not monuments. The reservoirs form the heart of the experience — four linked lakes (Conde de Guadalhorce, Guadalteba, Teba, and Turón) surrounded by limestone hills and terraced olive groves, with a total shoreline of well over 100 km. You can walk, ride, paddle, or simply sit and stare.
