---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

Spring (March to May) is the finest time to visit Guadalteba. The reservoirs are typically full after winter rains, the wildflowers are in bloom across the sierra, and the temperatures are warm enough for outdoor activity without the brutal heat of high summer. El Caminito del Rey is open year-round but most enjoyable in mild weather.

Summer (June to September) is hot and dry — inland Andalucía routinely hits 38–40°C in July and August. The reservoirs become crowded on weekends with local families seeking the water, but the evenings cool down noticeably and the light at that time of year is extraordinary. The Cueva de Ardales is a natural choice for a cool afternoon.

Autumn brings softer light and comfortable temperatures, and the olive harvest begins in October. Winter is mild by northern European standards but can be wet and overcast for stretches. Frost is uncommon down in the reservoir valleys, though the Sierra de Huma gets cold on its upper slopes.
