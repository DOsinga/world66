Jaén is one of Andalusia's least-visited provincial capitals, and that is precisely its appeal. While tourists crowd Seville and Granada, Jaén goes about its business — pressing olive oil (this province produces more of it than any other on earth), climbing its steep streets, and living in the shadow of a magnificent Arab fortress that towers over everything from the top of Santa Catalina hill.

The castle is the place to start. The [Castillo de Santa Catalina](/europe/spain/andalucia/jaen/castillo_santa_catalina) was built by the Moors and later expanded by the Christians who took the city in 1246 under Fernando III. The views from the battlements are extraordinary — a sea of olive groves rolling to every horizon, interrupted only by the city below and the Sierra Mágina in the distance. The castle is attached to a parador, one of Spain's network of state-run hotels in historic buildings. Even if you are not staying the night, go up for a drink and watch the sun set over the valley.

Back in the old town, the [Cathedral of Jaén](/europe/spain/andalucia/jaen/jaen_cathedral) is a serious piece of Renaissance architecture, begun in 1555 by Andrés de Vandelvira. It is considered one of the finest Renaissance cathedrals in Spain, and it dominates the [Plaza de Santa María](/europe/spain/andalucia/jaen/plaza_santa_maria) with an authority that no photograph quite captures. The old quarter spreads out from here around the churches of [La Magdalena](/europe/spain/andalucia/jaen/iglesia_magdalena) and [San Ildefonso](/europe/spain/andalucia/jaen/basilica_san_ildefonso) — good streets for an aimless afternoon walk. Don't miss the 11th-century [Arab Baths](/europe/spain/andalucia/jaen/banos_arabes), hidden beneath the [Palacio de Villardompardo](/europe/spain/andalucia/jaen/palacio_villardompardo).

Jaén is an easy stop between Granada (about an hour by bus or train) and Úbeda and Baeza, the pair of Renaissance towns to the northeast that are together a UNESCO World Heritage Site. Many travelers pass through all four in a single trip.
