---
title: "Eating Out"
type: section
---

Jaén is the world capital of olive oil — the province produces more of it than the whole of Italy. The local cuisine uses it openly: drizzled on toast for breakfast, slicked over salads, used to fry whole everything.

Two regional dishes worth seeking: **pipirrana** (a finely chopped salad of tomato, pepper, onion, and oil, sometimes with tuna), and **andrajos** (literally "rags" — a hearty stew of rabbit or hare with squares of broken dough). And of course **gachas** (fried-flour porridge) and **migas** in winter.

The city has a real **tapas culture** with the free-tapa-per-drink tradition still going. Stand at the bar, order a beer (cerveza), and a small plate appears next to it. Three drinks roughly equals dinner.
