---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

Jaén is at its best in **spring (March-May)** and **autumn (September-November)**, when temperatures sit between 15 and 25°C and the surrounding olive country is at its most photogenic — flowering wildflowers in spring, mature trees heavy with fruit in autumn.

**Summer (June-August)** is brutal. Temperatures regularly hit 38-40°C; the city empties out at lunch and revives only after dark. Visit if you must, but stick to mornings and evenings.

**Winter** is cool and clear — the castle hill gets the occasional dusting of snow but daytime temperatures are 8-15°C. The **Fiesta de la Virgen de la Capilla** (10-11 June) and **Semana Santa** (Holy Week, the week before Easter) are the two big events worth timing a trip around.

The olive harvest, **November to February**, is when the surrounding countryside is at its busiest — visit a mill in production if you can.
