Jerez's Archaeological Museum traces the city's long history through finds from the surrounding region. The collection reaches back before Roman Asta Regia and helps explain why Jerez mattered well before sherry bodegas and flamenco schools shaped its modern image.

It is a good counterweight to the city's wine and horse sights, especially if you want a quieter stop in the old centre.
