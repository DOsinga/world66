Bertemati Palace is an 18th-century Baroque palace in central Jerez de la Frontera, known for its ironwork balcony and grand domestic architecture. It now houses the Episcopal Diocese of Jerez, so most visitors treat it as an architectural stop while walking between the cathedral, the alcazar, and nearby historic streets.
