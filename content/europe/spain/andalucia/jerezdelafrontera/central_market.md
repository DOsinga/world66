Jerez's Central Market dates from 1885 and still fills its iron, glass, and stone hall with daily food trade. It is a good stop for seeing the older commercial life of the city away from the bodegas and monuments.

Come in the morning, when the stalls are active and the building feels like a market rather than an empty shell. It pairs easily with a walk through the old centre.
