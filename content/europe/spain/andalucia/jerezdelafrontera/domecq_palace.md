Domecq Palace is one of Jerez de la Frontera's aristocratic houses, linked to the families and fortunes behind the sherry trade. Its courtyards, rooms, and decorative detail show the more private side of a city usually introduced through bodegas and horses.

It is best visited as part of the broader sherry-city story: wealth, land, wine, and social status made visible in architecture. Check tour access before going, as historic palaces often depend on guided schedules.
