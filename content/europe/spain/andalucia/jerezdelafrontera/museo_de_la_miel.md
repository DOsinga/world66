Museo de la Miel and the Bees is part of Rancho Cortesano, a countryside honey producer outside Jerez. It explains beekeeping, honey production, and the life of the hive in a hands-on way that works particularly well for families.

This is not central Jerez sightseeing, and you need to plan transport. It is worth considering if you have already seen the sherry bodegas and horse school, or if you want a rural stop that shows another side of the countryside around the city.
