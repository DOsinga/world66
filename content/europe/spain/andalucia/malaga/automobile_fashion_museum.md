The Automobile & Fashion Museum pairs historic vehicles with clothing and design from the same eras. It is housed in Malaga's former tobacco factory, away from the old-town museum cluster, which gives it a different feel from the city's art and archaeology stops.

Come for polished cars, period fashion, and the pleasure of seeing changing taste laid out side by side. It is a niche museum, but a good one if you want something beyond Picasso, the Alcazaba, and the cathedral.
