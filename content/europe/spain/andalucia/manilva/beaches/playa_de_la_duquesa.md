The beach immediately east of Puerto de la Duquesa marina is the most accessible on the Manilva coast — a broad sandy stretch with calm water sheltered partly by the marina breakwater. Sun loungers and umbrellas are available in summer, and the marina restaurants are a short walk away. The beach holds a Blue Flag, which is unusual for this stretch of coast where water quality has historically been variable.
