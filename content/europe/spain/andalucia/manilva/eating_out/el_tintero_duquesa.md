A reliable quayside restaurant on the Puerto de la Duquesa marina serving fried fish, grilled seafood, and Andalusian rice dishes. The house speciality is fritura malagueña — a mixed platter of small fried fish and shellfish typical of the Málaga coast. Terrace tables overlook the boats; book ahead in summer. Prices are moderate by marina standards.
