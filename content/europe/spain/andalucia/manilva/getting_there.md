---
title: Getting There
type: section
---

Manilva is 100 km southwest of Málaga along the AP-7 coastal motorway, and 20 km northeast of Algeciras. **Málaga Airport** (AGP) is the main international gateway; the drive takes about 75 minutes by car. There is no train service. **Buses** run along the coast from Málaga bus station to Algeciras; alight at Sabinillas or Manilva. The service is operated by Alsa and takes about 90 minutes from Málaga. **Driving** is the most practical option if you plan to visit the Roman baths or the hilltop village, as neither is served by local bus.
