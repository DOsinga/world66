The beach at Sabinillas (officially San Luis de Sabinillas) is a long, straight stretch of dark sand fronting the coastal village of the same name. It is quieter than the marina beach and has a more local character — the promenade behind it is lined with neighbourhood bars and small restaurants. Good for a morning swim before exploring the village.
