The Castillo de la Duquesa stands at the edge of the Puerto de la Duquesa marina, a compact 18th-century defensive tower with Roman foundations. The castle is well preserved and periodically open to visitors; its upper terrace gives good views over the marina, the beach, and westward toward Gibraltar on clear days. The surrounding plaza is a pleasant place to sit, and the castle's base is flanked by the restaurant terrace of the marina. Even from outside, the layered history is readable: Roman stonework at the base, later Moorish-era modifications, and the 18th-century Spanish tower on top.
