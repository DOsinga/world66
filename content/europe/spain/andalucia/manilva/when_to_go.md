---
title: When to Go
type: section
---

Manilva has a classic western Costa del Sol climate: hot, dry summers and mild winters. July and August bring temperatures reliably above 30 °C and very little rain; the beaches and marina are busy with Spanish summer holidaymakers. September and October are excellent — the sea is at its warmest, crowds thin out, and the Moscatel grape harvest adds a local event to the calendar (usually late September in the villages above Manilva). Spring (April–May) is the best time for walking and exploring: the hills above town are green, wildflowers are out, and temperatures are comfortable for sightseeing. Winter is quiet but very mild — rarely below 10 °C — and fine for visiting the Roman baths and the village. The area gets its sharpest tramontana winds in winter, which can make the coast blustery.
