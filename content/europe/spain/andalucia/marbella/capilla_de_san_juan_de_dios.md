Capilla de San Juan de Dios is a small 16th-century chapel in Marbella's old town. It has also served as a hospital and a home for foundlings, giving it a deeper social history than its size suggests.

It is an easy add-on while walking between Plaza de los Naranjos, Iglesia de la Encarnacion, and the old town lanes.
