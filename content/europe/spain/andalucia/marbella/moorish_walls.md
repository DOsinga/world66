The Moorish Walls are the remaining fortifications of medieval Marbella, fragments of the old Alcazaba and Medina that once protected the town. They are not extensive ruins, but they add real age and texture to a walk through the old town.

Look for them as part of an unhurried circuit of Marbella's historic centre rather than as a standalone museum-style visit.
