---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

The Costa del Sol lives up to its name: Marbella averages around 320 sunny days a year. The best months for a visit are April, May, June, September, and October — warm enough for the beach, dry, and without the August crush. July and August bring peak crowds, peak prices, and temperatures that push above 35°C. The old town gets particularly oppressive in the afternoon heat.

Winter (November to March) is mild — January averages 17°C — and Marbella works well as a winter sun destination, though most beach bars and water sports operators close down. The mountains behind the city occasionally get snow in January and February, which makes for a striking contrast with the sunlit coast below. The Semana Santa (Holy Week) processions in March or April are elaborate and worth seeing if you're passing through.
