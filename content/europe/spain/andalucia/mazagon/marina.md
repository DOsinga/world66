The small marina at the eastern end of the town shelters a few hundred yachts and fishing boats, and is the focus of evening life in summer when the surrounding bars and restaurants fill up with day-trippers. The view east along the coast at sunset, with the long curve of beach disappearing into the haze, is the best in town.

A fish market operates on the quay in the mornings — anything caught locally arrives here first — and the seafood restaurants on the marina pick from it directly. Sailing and fishing trips can be arranged through the boatmen on the dock.
