---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

Mazagón has an Atlantic climate softened by Andalucian sun: warm and dry in summer, mild and breezy the rest of the year. The town effectively shuts down outside the high season, and visiting in winter — though pleasant — means most chiringuitos and a fair share of restaurants are closed.

**July and August** are hot (30°C+), the water is warmest at around 22°C, and the town is at full tilt with Spanish holidaymakers. Beaches are still wide enough not to feel overcrowded.

**May, June, and September** are the best months: warm days, fewer people, and most businesses still open. The water is cooler but swimmable.

**October to April** is quiet, fresh, and good for walking on the dunes and birdwatching at Doñana — winter is the peak season for migratory birds.
