The Caves of the Old Forge are three small cave rooms showing how older houses in Mijas used the hillside itself for cool storage, stables, and work space. An anvil and simple displays connect the caves to the village's practical past rather than its postcard-white exterior.

This is a small stop, but it fits well into a wander through Mijas Pueblo. It helps explain why the village is built into the slope rather than simply on top of it.
