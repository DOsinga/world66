---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

Mijas is pleasant year-round thanks to its sheltered position on the Costa del Sol. Spring (March to May) and autumn (September to October) are the most comfortable for walking the village and hiking in the sierra — temperatures are warm but not oppressive, and the light is exceptional. Summer (July and August) is hot and busy, with the village crowded with coast-holiday visitors making day trips up the hill; go early in the morning or late afternoon to avoid the worst of it.

Winter is mild and largely crowd-free. Many days between November and March are clear and sunny, and the village has a quieter, more local character than it shows in summer. The annual romería to the Sanctuary of the Virgen de la Peña takes place in September, and summer brings bullfights at the square ring and outdoor concerts.
