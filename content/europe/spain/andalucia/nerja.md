Nerja sits at the foot of the Sierra Almijara on cliffs high above the sea, 50 km east of [Málaga](/europe/spain/andalucia/malaga). It is a pretty and still relatively undeveloped corner of real Spain — quite unlike the high-rise resorts elsewhere on the Costa del Sol. The whitewashed lanes of the [Old Town](/europe/spain/andalucia/nerja/old_town_nerja) climb back from the cliff-top [Balcón de Europa](/europe/spain/andalucia/nerja/balcon_de_europa) and its row of cafés and palm trees.

Below the cliffs is a string of small coves and beaches — [Calahonda](/europe/spain/andalucia/nerja/playa_calahonda) and [El Salón](/europe/spain/andalucia/nerja/playa_el_salon) right in the centre, the larger and livelier [Playa Burriana](/europe/spain/andalucia/nerja/playa_burriana) twenty minutes east, and the protected [Cliffs of Maro-Cerro Gordo](/europe/spain/andalucia/nerja/cliffs_of_maro) beyond. The headline attraction is the great cave system at the [Caves of Nerja](/europe/spain/andalucia/nerja/caves_of_nerja) just east of town; the white village of [Frigiliana](/europe/spain/andalucia/nerja/frigiliana) in the hills above is the standard half-day excursion.
