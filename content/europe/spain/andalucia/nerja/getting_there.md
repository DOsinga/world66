---
title: Getting There
type: section
---

Nerja sits on the N-340 coast road 50 km east of Málaga and 80 km west of Almuñécar.

**By air**, the nearest airport is [Málaga](/europe/spain/andalucia/malaga) (AGP) — about an hour by car or bus. The ALSA bus runs frequent direct services between Málaga airport / city and Nerja's bus station on Avenida de Pescia.

**By car**, take the A-7 motorway east from Málaga and exit onto the N-340 at Torrox; the coastal road runs straight into Nerja. Parking in the centre is difficult in summer — there are several underground car parks just outside the old town.

**By bus**, ALSA connects Nerja to Granada (two hours), Almuñécar, Salobreña and the airport. No rail service.
