Casa de San Juan Bosco is an early 20th-century house in Ronda's old town, entered through a tiled hallway and set around rooms with regional ceramics and 19th-century tapestries. Its main draw is the landscaped terrace, which looks across the gorge toward the Puente Nuevo.
