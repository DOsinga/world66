Casa del Gigante is a restored 14th- and 15th-century house in Ronda's old town, named for the stone figure set into one corner. Its Moorish details include horseshoe arches, carved stucco and decorated wooden ceilings, making it a small but worthwhile stop for travellers interested in the city's Islamic-era architecture.
