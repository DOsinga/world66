Parque Alameda is a shaded public garden on the edge of Ronda's cliffs, a short walk from the bullring and Puente Nuevo. The park itself is simple, but its viewing platforms look across the gorge, the surrounding hills, and the countryside west of town.

It is an easy pause between the old town and the newer streets, especially in hot weather. Come near sunset if you want the softer light over the cliffs.
