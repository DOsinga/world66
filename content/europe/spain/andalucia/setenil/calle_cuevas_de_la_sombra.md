Calle Cuevas de la Sombra — the Street of the Caves of the Shadow — runs parallel to its sunlit sister street on the opposite bank of the Trejo. Where Cuevas del Sol feels open and bright, this street is darker and more enclosed, the rock overhang pressing lower, the light more muted. It is the more dramatic of the two, and many visitors prefer it for exactly that reason.

The houses here are built literally into the rock face, with the gorge wall serving as rear wall and sometimes ceiling. Some have carved small windows directly into the stone. The street is narrower, the atmosphere more intimate, and it tends to be slightly quieter than Cuevas del Sol, making it a better bet for an unhurried walk. A few bars and a small shop occupy the cave spaces at ground level.
