---
title: Getting There
type: section
---

Setenil de las Bodegas is best reached by car. The village sits about 20 km northeast of [Ronda](/europe/spain/andalucia/ronda) on the A-374 road, and the drive through rolling farmland and olive groves takes around 25 minutes. Parking is available at the entrance to the village near the mirador.

A daily bus connects Setenil with Ronda and with Olvera to the north on the Route of the White Villages. Check the Comes or Damas bus timetables for current schedules — services are limited and may not run on Sundays. There is no direct train service.

From the Costa del Sol, Setenil makes a good inland excursion: Ronda is about an hour from Marbella or Málaga by car, adding Setenil extends the trip by another half-hour.
