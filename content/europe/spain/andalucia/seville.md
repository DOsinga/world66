Seville is the capital of Andalucía and the most purely, exuberantly Andalucían city in Spain. Where Barcelona is cosmopolitan and Madrid is national, Seville is intensely local — obsessed with its own traditions, its own rhythms, and its own calendar. Flamenco was born here. The April Fair, the most famous feria in Spain, happens here. Semana Santa transforms the streets every spring into the most theatrical religious event in Europe. If you want to understand what makes southern Spain different from the rest of the country, this is where to come.

The historic centre is compact and almost entirely walkable. The [Cathedral and Giralda](/europe/spain/andalucia/seville/cathedral) anchor one end, surrounded by the medieval lanes of [Barrio Santa Cruz](/europe/spain/andalucia/seville/santa_cruz). The [Real Alcázar](/europe/spain/andalucia/seville/real_alcazar) — the royal palace built by Christian kings using Moorish craftsmen — sits directly to the south. The [General Archive of the Indies](/europe/spain/andalucia/seville/general_archive_indies), housed in a 16th-century merchant exchange, completes the trio of UNESCO World Heritage Sites in a space you can walk across in ten minutes.

Beyond the cathedral quarter, the city opens up. The [Plaza de España](/europe/spain/andalucia/seville/plaza_de_espana) and María Luisa Park, built for the 1929 Ibero-American Exposition, are worth a half-day. Cross the Guadalquivir on the [Puente de Triana](/europe/spain/andalucia/seville/puente_de_triana) and the neighbourhood of [Triana](/europe/spain/andalucia/seville/triana) has its own fierce identity: ceramic workshops, deep flamenco roots, and a riverside strip of bars along Calle Betis. To the north, [Macarena](/europe/spain/andalucia/seville/macarena) is where Sevillians actually live — the Basilica houses the city's most venerated Virgin, and the Alameda de Hércules has good tapas at lower prices. The audacious [Metropol Parasol](/europe/spain/andalucia/seville/metropol_parasol) at La Encarnación, the world's largest wooden structure, is impossible to ignore. Less visited than it deserves, the [Hospital de la Caridad](/europe/spain/andalucia/seville/hospital_de_la_caridad) near the Torre del Oro contains Murillo and Valdés Leal paintings of astonishing quality.

Seville rewards a stay of at least three nights. The high-speed AVE train from Madrid takes two and a half hours; the April Fair and Holy Week both require booking months in advance.
