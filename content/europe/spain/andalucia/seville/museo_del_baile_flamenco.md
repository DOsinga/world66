Museo del Baile Flamenco gives Seville's most famous art form a compact home in the old centre. The museum side introduces flamenco dance, costume, rhythm, and major performers without pretending that panels can replace the real thing.

The stronger reason to come is usually the performance programme. A show here is intimate, easy to arrange, and more focused than the tourist-dinner versions elsewhere; book ahead in busy periods and arrive ready to listen as much as watch.
