The Palacio Arzobispal is the Archbishop's Palace beside Seville Cathedral. Even when interiors are not open, the palace adds to the cathedral quarter's historic fabric, with a formal facade, courtyard glimpses, and a setting among some of the city's most important church buildings.

Treat it as a short architectural stop while moving between the cathedral, the Giralda, and the Archivo de Indias.
