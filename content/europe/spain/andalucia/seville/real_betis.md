A Real Betis match is one of Seville's livelier non-monument experiences. The club plays at Estadio Benito Villamarin in the Heliópolis area, and the green-and-white crowd gives a different football flavour from cross-town Sevilla FC: less polished, very local, and loud when the team is playing well.

For travellers, the draw is the matchday atmosphere rather than the stadium as an architectural sight. Buy tickets from official channels, arrive early enough to navigate the tram or buses, and remember that late kickoffs are common in Spanish football.
