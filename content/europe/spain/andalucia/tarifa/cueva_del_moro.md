Cueva del Moro is a prehistoric rock-shelter site in the hills near Tarifa, known for ancient rock art and its position above the Strait of Gibraltar landscape. The setting matters almost as much as the archaeology: sandstone, wind, scrub, and broad views toward Africa make the site feel tied to deep human movement across this coast.

Access and conservation conditions can vary, so this is not a casual drop-in like a town monument. If you go, treat the site gently and check local guidance first; the value lies in seeing a fragile prehistoric trace in the landscape that produced it.
