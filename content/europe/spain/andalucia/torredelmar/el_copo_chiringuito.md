One of the most established chiringuitos on the Paseo Marítimo, El Copo occupies a prime stretch of the seafront terrace and is particularly popular from late afternoon into the evening. The formula is simple: cold Cruzcampo on tap, cold white wine from the Axarquía, and a rotating selection of tapas and montaditos (small open sandwiches). The sun sets behind the hills to the west and the sea turns orange — this is the quintessential Torre del Mar experience. Open from mid-morning; busiest from 7pm onwards in summer.
