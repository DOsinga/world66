---
title: "Bars and Cafes"
type: section
---

La Carihuela has the best tapas scene — the streets off Calle Quintano Rosado have several good local bars where tapas come free with your drink in the Andalusian tradition. [Bar Central](/europe/spain/andalucia/torremolinos/bar_central) in the town centre is popular with locals and stays busy from mid-morning through the lunch hour. The [El Nogalera](/europe/spain/andalucia/torremolinos/nogalera) neighbourhood is Torremolinos's gay-friendly zone, with bars and clubs around Plaza Nogalera that stay open very late and draw a mixed crowd. For a flamenco show with dinner, the Taberna Pepe Lopez (around €23 per person including a drink) is a reliable tourist option.
