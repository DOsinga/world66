[Málaga](/europe/spain/andalucia/malaga) is 14 km east — 20 minutes on the suburban train or metro — and is an entirely different city from Torremolinos. The Alcazaba fortress, the Picasso Museum, and the old city centre are all excellent reasons to make the trip. [Antequera](/europe/spain/andalucia/antequera), about an hour by bus or car, is worth the effort for its Dolmen de Menga, a Neolithic chamber tomb older than Stonehenge, and for the Laguna de Fuente de Piedra where flamingos gather from January through August.
