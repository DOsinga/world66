---
title: "Eating Out"
type: section
---

The best eating in Torremolinos is in [La Carihuela](/europe/spain/andalucia/torremolinos/la_carihuela), the old fishermen’s quarter on the western side of town. The streets off the seafront promenade are lined with restaurants serving the local speciality — fritura malagueña, a mixed plate of small fried fish that is the definitive dish of this stretch of coast. Gamba blanca (the local white prawn) and dorada a la sal (sea bream baked in a salt crust) are also well worth ordering when available.

El Molino de la Torre, at the bottom of Calle San Miguel near the beach, has a good roof terrace and reasonable prices. For local seafood in La Carihuela, [El Roqueo](/europe/spain/andalucia/torremolinos/el_roqueo) is one of the more reliable choices on the promenade.
