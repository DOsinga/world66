---
title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

The most satisfying thing to do in Torremolinos is to walk [La Carihuela](/europe/spain/andalucia/torremolinos/la_carihuela) early in the morning before the tourists emerge — the old fishermen's quarter has a different rhythm from the main resort. The 14th-century [Torre de Pimentel](/europe/spain/andalucia/torremolinos/torre_de_pimentel), from which the town takes its name, is climbable for free and sits at twelve metres high. [Parque La Batería](/europe/spain/andalucia/torremolinos/parque_la_bateria), on a hill to the west, has fountains, a lake, and good views over the bay from what was once a military lookout. [Cortijo del Tajo](/europe/spain/andalucia/torremolinos/cortijo_del_tajo), on the outskirts, has Roman and pre-Roman archaeological remains.

The [Bajondillo Cave](/europe/spain/andalucia/torremolinos/bajondillo_cave), hidden beneath the resort near Bajondillo beach, is one of the most significant prehistoric sites on the Iberian coast — excavations have found evidence of both Neanderthal and early modern human occupation spanning over 100,000 years. The [Hotel Pez Espada](/europe/spain/andalucia/torremolinos/hotel_pez_espada) on the western promenade opened in 1959 and its pool terrace was where Frank Sinatra, Ava Gardner, and Brigitte Bardot made the Costa del Sol famous.
