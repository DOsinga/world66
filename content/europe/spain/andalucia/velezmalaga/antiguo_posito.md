The Antiguo Posito is an 18th-century civic building in Velez-Malaga, originally tied to the storage and trade of grain and later used as a fish market. Its plain historic fabric makes it a useful stop while walking the old centre.

Today the building is used for art exhibitions and cultural events. Check what is on before making a special visit, but it is easy to include with the nearby churches and fortress.
