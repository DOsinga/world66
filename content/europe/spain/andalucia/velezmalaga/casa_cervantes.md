Casa Cervantes is a historic house in the old centre of Vélez-Málaga, traditionally linked to Miguel de Cervantes during his time as a tax collector. The connection is uncertain, but the building itself is worth a look for its forged balconies, Gothic details, and brick-columned inner patio.

It works best as part of a walk between the town's churches, fortress, and old streets rather than as a long standalone visit.
