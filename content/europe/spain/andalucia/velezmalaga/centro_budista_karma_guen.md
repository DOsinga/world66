Centro Budista Karma Guen is a Buddhist centre in the hills near Velez-Malaga, with a 13-metre Kalachakra Stupa dedicated to the Kagyu lineage. It is a quiet, unusual stop in the Axarquia, best suited to travelers interested in religious sites or broad views away from the coast.
