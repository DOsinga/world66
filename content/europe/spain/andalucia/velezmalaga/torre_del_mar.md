Torre del Mar is the coastal neighbourhood of Vélez-Málaga, about 4 km south of the town centre and connected by local bus and easy cycling routes. It has a long straight beach on the eastern Costa del Sol that is considerably less developed than the resort strip further west toward Torremolinos and Fuengirola — more Spanish, quieter, and with better seafood restaurants on the paseo marítimo.

The beach is wide and gently shelving, good for swimming from June through October. The chiringuitos (beach bars and restaurants) along the seafront serve the full range of Málaga coastal food: fresh fish, prawns, sardines grilled over fire in espetos (traditional bamboo skewers planted in the sand), and the local cold tomato soup ajoblanco. It's a working Spanish beach resort with the rhythms of a place that primarily serves local Spanish families rather than international tourists, which means better food and a more genuine atmosphere. The paseo marítimo is pleasant for a walk, particularly at sunset.
