The Castle of Arcos sits at the highest, most strategic part of town, just off Plaza del Cabildo. It is not generally open as a visitor monument, which can be frustrating, but its mass still shapes the square and the skyline.

Treat it as part of the town's defensive story rather than a standalone attraction. Seen with the walls, cliff, and basilica, the castle explains why Arcos mattered on the frontier.
