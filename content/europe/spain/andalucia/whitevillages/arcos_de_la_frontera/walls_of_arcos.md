The walls of Arcos survive in fragments rather than as a neat circuit, but they are important to how the town reads. The old defences work with the natural ridge, turning the cliff edge into part of the architecture.

Look for the walls as you move through the old town rather than as a single destination. They make most sense in glimpses: stone, drop, lane, and view all at once.
