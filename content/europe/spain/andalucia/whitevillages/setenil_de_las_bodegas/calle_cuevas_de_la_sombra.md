Calle Cuevas de la Sombra is the darker, more enclosed partner to Cuevas del Sol. The overhang is heavier here, and the lane feels compressed beneath the cliff in a way that photographs struggle to exaggerate because the reality is already so odd.

Walk it slowly and look up. The appeal is not a single building but the whole arrangement of street, rock, house, and human stubbornness.
