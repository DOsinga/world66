---
title: Getting There
type: section
---

Setenil is about 20 km north of Ronda and is usually visited by car as part of a white-village route. The roads into town are scenic but narrow in places.

**By car:** Park in signed areas outside the tightest centre and walk in. The streets around the cave quarter were not designed for modern traffic, and trying to drive through them is a poor way to enjoy the town.

**By public transport:** Bus and rail options are limited and can be awkward for a day trip. Check current services from Ronda before relying on them.
