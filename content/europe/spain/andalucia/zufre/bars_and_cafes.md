---
title: Bars and Cafes
type: section
---

Zufre's bar life is simple and local. There is one main bar on the plaza and possibly a second smaller one depending on the season — both cater almost entirely to village residents rather than visitors. Sit outside in the morning with a coffee, or return in the early evening when the locals gather for a drink before dinner. Nobody will rush you and the atmosphere is entirely unpretentious.
