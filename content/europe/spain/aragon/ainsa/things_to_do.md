title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

The old town is the main event in Aínsa. The [Plaza Mayor](/europe/spain/aragon/ainsa/plaza_mayor), the [Castillo de Aínsa](/europe/spain/aragon/ainsa/castillo), and the [Iglesia de Santa María](/europe/spain/aragon/ainsa/iglesia_santa_maria) are all within a few minutes' walk of each other. Beyond the old town, [Ordesa y Monte Perdido National Park](/europe/spain/aragon/ordesa_national_park) is the main draw — the Añisclo and Pineta sectors are both accessible from Aínsa, offering canyon hiking without the summer crowds of the main Ordesa valley.
