---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

Albarracín sits at over 1,000 metres altitude, which keeps summer temperatures manageable when the Spanish lowlands are baking — high 20s in July and August rather than the high 30s of Madrid. Winters can be cold and occasionally snowy, and a dusting of snow on the pink-roofed houses is one of the loveliest sights in Spain.

The town is busiest in **July, August, and at long weekends** (puentes) when Spanish families fill the small hotels. **May, June, and September** are ideal — comfortable temperatures, fewer crowds, and the surrounding sierra at its greenest. The town's main fiesta is in honour of Santa María de Oriente in the first week of September.
