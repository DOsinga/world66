---
title: Things to Do in Cantavieja
type: section
---

The [Plaza de Cristo Rey](/europe/spain/aragon/cantavieja/plaza_de_cristo_rey) and the [Iglesia de la Asunción](/europe/spain/aragon/cantavieja/iglesia_de_la_asuncion) can be seen in an hour. After that, the [Ruta del Contorno](/europe/spain/aragon/cantavieja/ruta_del_contorno) walk below the village reveals the drama of the site in a way the streets above cannot. The surrounding [Maestrazgo landscape](/europe/spain/aragon/cantavieja/maestrazgo_landscape) rewards a half-day or full-day walk if the weather is good.

[Mirambel](/europe/spain/aragon/cantavieja/mirambel), 10 kilometres north, is one of the best-preserved medieval walled villages in Spain and deserves a separate half-day visit. La Iglesuela del Cid, 15 kilometres east, is another Maestrazgo village worth a brief stop for its medieval street pattern and fortified church.
