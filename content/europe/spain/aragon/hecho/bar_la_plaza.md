The bar on the village square is the natural gathering point in Hecho: coffee in the morning before the day's walk, cold beer when you return in the afternoon, and a glass of local wine in the evening while the light fades from the mountains. The terrace faces the sculptures, which gives it an accidental gallery quality.

Tapas in the Aragonese style — cured meats, pickled vegetables, local cheese — come automatically with drinks. In summer it fills with hikers; in winter it is mainly locals, which is equally worth experiencing.
