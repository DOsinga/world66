---
title: Getting Around
type: section
---

The village of Hecho itself is small enough to walk entirely in twenty minutes. The interesting question is how to reach the upper valley.

**By car or bicycle**, the road to the Selva de Oza continues north from the village for about 14 km. It is a good paved road as far as the forest car park, which is also the trailhead for most of the upper valley hikes.

**On foot**, the GR-11 long-distance trail passes through the valley. Day walkers can follow valley trails without needing to carry overnight kit; the Selva de Oza is about a 3–4 hour walk from the village one way.

The village of Siresa, with its Romanesque monastery, is about 3 km north along the valley road — an easy walk or short bike ride.
