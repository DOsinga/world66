---
title: Getting There
type: section
---

**By car** is the practical way. From Jaca, take the A-1601 north through Puente la Reina de Jaca; the drive is about 45 minutes and the road passes through fine mountain scenery. From Pamplona, allow around an hour and a half via Jaca.

**By bus**, a single daily service runs between Jaca and Hecho. The timing has historically been awkward — it arrives in the evening and departs in the morning — which effectively means you are staying overnight. Check current timetables with Hecho's town hall (ayuntamiento) or the bus company, as services change seasonally.

**The nearest airports** are Pamplona (roughly 100 km) and Zaragoza (around 170 km). There is no practical way to reach Hecho by public transport from either airport in a single day without a car.
