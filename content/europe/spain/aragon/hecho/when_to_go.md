---
title: When to Go
type: section
---

**Summer (June–September)** is the main season. July and August bring Spanish hikers in numbers — the Selva de Oza fills up, the village bars stay busy, and guesthouses book out. The weather is warm and mostly dry, though afternoon thunderstorms are common in the mountains.

**Spring (April–June)** and **autumn (September–October)** are quieter and often beautiful — wildflowers in the meadows, turning beech forests in autumn, and the light that comes with fewer people.

**Winter** is quiet. Many businesses close, and the upper valley road may be impassable. The village settles into its own rhythm, which has its own appeal, but facilities are minimal.

The **Fiestas de Hecho** take place in mid-August and include traditional Aragonese folk events and Pyrenean music, with the village square given over to dancing and celebration for several days.
