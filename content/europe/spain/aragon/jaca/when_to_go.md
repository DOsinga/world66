---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

Jaca has two distinct seasons. Winter (December to March) brings skiers for the Astún and Candanchú resorts to the north. The town is lively in a low-key way, accommodation is busy at weekends, and the mountains are the obvious draw.

Summer (June to September) is the hiking and Camino season. The high mountain passes open and the valleys come into their own; July and August see the most visitors and the warmest weather. Spring (April to May) and autumn (October) are good for quieter hiking, though conditions in the high mountains can be unpredictable and early or late season snowfall is possible above 1,500 metres.

The Fiestas de Santa Orosia in late June, Jaca's main annual festival, are worth timing a visit around if you happen to be in the area.
