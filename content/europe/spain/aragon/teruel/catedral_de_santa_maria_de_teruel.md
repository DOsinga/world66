Catedral de Santa María de Teruel is one of the city's essential Mudéjar monuments, combining brickwork, glazed ceramic detail, and a distinctive tower into a style that belongs strongly to Aragon. Along with Teruel's other Mudéjar towers, it forms part of the UNESCO-listed architectural heritage of the city.

The cathedral rewards looking up and slowing down. Its ceiling, tower, and exterior ornament show how Christian and Islamic building traditions blended in medieval Spain, making it much more than a routine provincial cathedral stop.
