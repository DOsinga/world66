Iglesia de San Pedro is one of Teruel's essential historic buildings, combining Mudejar architecture with the city's famous Lovers of Teruel story. Its tower, brickwork, and decorated interior make it a natural companion to the better-known Mudejar landmarks nearby.

The church is especially worthwhile if you visit the mausoleum or interpretation spaces connected with the Lovers tradition. Seen together, they show how Teruel packages architecture, legend, and local identity into a compact old-town route.
