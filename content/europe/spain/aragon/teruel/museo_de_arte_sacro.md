Museo de Arte Sacro is a small religious art museum in a 16th-century palace near Teruel's cathedral. Its collection covers church art from the Romanesque through the Baroque, giving useful context to the city's Mudéjar churches and cathedral.

It is best for visitors who already enjoy ecclesiastical art, carved saints, altarpieces, and local religious history. Pair it with the cathedral rather than crossing town just for the museum.
