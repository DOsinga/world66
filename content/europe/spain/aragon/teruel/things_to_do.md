---
title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Teruel is small and walkable — all the main sights are within a short walk of each other. The four UNESCO Mudéjar towers are the reason most people come, but the [Lovers of Teruel](/europe/spain/aragon/teruel/san_pedro_los_amantes) mausoleum and the [Museo Provincial](/europe/spain/aragon/teruel/museo_provincial_teruel) give the visit real depth. Allow a full day if you want to see everything properly, or a focused half-day for the essential towers and mausoleum.
