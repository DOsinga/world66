The Tower of the Church of San Salvador is a key piece of Teruel's Mudejar architecture, where brickwork, ceramic detail, and Islamic-influenced forms were adapted into Christian urban buildings. It is part of the architectural language that gives Teruel much of its character.

The tower is especially rewarding when seen with the city's other Mudejar towers, because the repetition and variation make the style easier to read. Climb or visit if access is open, but even from the street it is one of the stops that explains why Teruel is more than a convenient pause between larger Spanish cities.
