---
title: Getting There
type: section
---

Torla sits north of Broto in the province of Huesca, at the road entrance to the Ordesa valley. The nearest larger service towns are Aínsa, Sabiñánigo, and Jaca.

**By car:** Driving is the simplest way to reach Torla. From Aínsa, follow the A-138 and then the N-260/A-135 corridor toward Broto and Torla. From Sabiñánigo or Jaca, approach through Biescas and the upper Gállego valley.

**By bus:** Bus services exist but are limited and seasonal patterns matter. Check current timetables carefully, especially if you need to connect onward after a hike.

**By train:** There is no train to Torla. The closest practical rail access is Sabiñánigo, with road or bus connections onward.
