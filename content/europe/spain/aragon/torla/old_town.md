Torla's old town is a compact cluster of stone houses, narrow lanes, and mountain roofs below the Ordesa cliffs. It is not polished in the manner of famous resort villages, and that is a virtue: it still feels like a working Pyrenean base.

Walk it slowly after a day in the park. The details are small - carved lintels, heavy doors, glimpses of peaks between roofs - but they make Torla feel like more than a car park for Ordesa.
