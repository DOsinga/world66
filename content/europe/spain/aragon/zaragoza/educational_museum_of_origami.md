The Educational Museum of Origami in Zaragoza is a specialist museum devoted to paper folding as art, mathematics, and design. Its displays are more intricate than the simple childhood association many visitors bring to origami.

It is a compact but memorable add-on while exploring the old centre. The museum is best for design-minded travellers, families, or anyone looking for a small indoor stop between Zaragoza's larger monuments.
