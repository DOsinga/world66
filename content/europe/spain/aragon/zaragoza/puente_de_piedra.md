Puente de Piedra is Zaragoza's old stone bridge over the Ebro, built in the 15th century and rebuilt several times after flood damage.

The bridge is now mostly pedestrian, with lion sculptures at its ends and one of the best approaches to the basilica and cathedral area across the river.
