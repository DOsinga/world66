Cangas del Narcea is the main town of far western Asturias, set deep in green valleys where the Narcea River cuts through coal country, bear habitat, and steep vineyards. It is not polished coastal Asturias. It feels inland, working, and a little remote, which is exactly the point.

The town has enough substance for a focused detour. Just outside it, [Corias Monastery](/europe/spain/asturias/cangas_del_narcea/corias_monastery) is one of western Asturias's great buildings. Above and around the valley, Cangas is also the centre of Asturias's small wine denomination, explained at the [Cangas Wine Museum](/europe/spain/asturias/cangas_del_narcea/cangas_wine_museum) and in local bodegas.

The wider council gives the place its weight. [Muniellos Nature Reserve](/europe/spain/asturias/cangas_del_narcea/muniellos_nature_reserve) protects one of Spain's finest oak forests, with access limited enough that you need to plan ahead. [Nuestra Senora del Acebo Sanctuary](/europe/spain/asturias/cangas_del_narcea/nuestra_senora_del_acebo_sanctuary) sits high above town, with the kind of mountain view that explains why this corner of Asturias has held onto its own rhythm.
