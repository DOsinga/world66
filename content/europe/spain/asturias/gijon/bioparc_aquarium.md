Bioparc Aquarium sits by Gijon's waterfront and focuses first on the marine life of the Cantabrian Sea before widening out to other aquatic ecosystems. Tanks, tunnels, and interpretive displays make it an easy indoor counterpoint to beaches and cider houses.

It is most useful for families or for travellers interested in the sea that shapes Asturias' coast. Pair it with the Railway Museum or a harbour walk if the weather turns, or use it to give children a break from churches and old-town strolling.
