Llastres' old village is a compact tangle of stone lanes, stairways, and houses squeezed onto the hill above the port. Start high and work down slowly: the views open between rooftops, then the streets settle into the harbour, where fishing boats and seafood restaurants keep the place from feeling like a stage set.
