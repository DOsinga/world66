Santa Maria de Sabada is the parish church above Llastres, built in the 18th century with a later 19th-century bell tower. Its position makes it part of the town's skyline and a useful stop while climbing through the steep old streets.

The church is best combined with the San Roque viewpoint and the old harbor walk rather than treated as a long standalone visit.
