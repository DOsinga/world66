Espalmador is the little island that makes Formentera feel even more stripped back. It sits just north of Pas des Trucadors, with pale sand, shallow turquoise water, and a protected, undeveloped character that is increasingly rare in the Balearics.

You need a boat to visit, and you should bring everything you need because there are no proper services. Treat it gently: the point is not a beach club day, but the feeling of landing on a quiet sand-and-salt edge between Formentera and Ibiza.
