Platja des Canyers is one of Formentera's smaller beaches, a pale strip of sand and clear water close enough to Es Pujols to reach without much planning. It has the easy, low-key character that makes the island's north coast so appealing.

It is not as dramatic as Ses Illetes or as long as Migjorn, but that is part of the point. Come for a swim, a short sun stop, or a quieter alternative when the better-known beaches are busy. As always on Formentera, wind direction matters.
