Platja Llevant runs along the eastern side of the narrow northern spit opposite Ses Illetes. The sand and water are just as Formentera-bright, but the beach often feels more open and less showy, with dunes behind it and long views up the coast.

It is a good choice when Illetes is too crowded or too exposed to the wrong wind. Services are limited compared with bigger resort beaches, so bring water and shade, then walk until you find the stretch of sand that suits the day.
