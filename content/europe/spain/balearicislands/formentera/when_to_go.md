---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

June and September are the best months. The weather is reliably warm and sunny, the sea is swimmable, and the island has not yet reached the density of August. In June, many seasonal restaurants and bars have just opened and are at their freshest; in September, the summer crowds have thinned while the sea retains the heat accumulated over three months. October is still pleasant for walking and cycling, though some beach establishments close after mid-month.

July and August are crowded and expensive. The ferry queues are long, accommodation prices peak, and Ses Illetes in particular can feel uncomfortably busy. The island attracts a moneyed crowd in high summer — there are a lot of day-trippers from Ibiza, superyachts anchored offshore, and rental prices that reflect both. If you must come in August, book everything months in advance and arrive early at the beaches. Winter sees the island nearly deserted; most restaurants and bars close entirely, and the ferry schedule drops to a couple of crossings a day. Some people find the off-season Formentera charming; others find it bleak.
