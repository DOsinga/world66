Baluard de Sant Jaume is part of Ibiza Town's fortified old city. Inside the bastion is a hands-on exhibit about military technology from the 16th to 18th centuries.

The appeal is practical and tactile: visitors can try on armour, handle cannon balls, and see how lines of fire were planned from the walls. It pairs well with a walk through Dalt Vila.
