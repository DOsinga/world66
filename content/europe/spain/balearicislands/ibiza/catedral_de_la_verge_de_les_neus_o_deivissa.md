Ibiza's cathedral crowns the old fortified town, with its pale walls rising above the harbour and the lanes of Dalt Vila. Dedicated to Our Lady of the Snows, it was built in the Catalan Gothic style in the 16th century, then altered over time like most island churches.

The interior is restrained, but the setting makes the visit. Climb up through the old town, step inside for the quiet, then come back out for the views over the port and the Mediterranean.
