Museu Etnografic d'Eivissa - Can Ros is a small museum of Ibizan rural life and material culture. It displays domestic objects, tools, clothing, and craft traditions connected to the island's peasant and artisan households, making it a useful counterpoint to Ibiza's beach and nightlife image.
