---
title: Eating Out
type: section
---

Eating options in San Antonio range from cheap-and-cheerful British-breakfast cafes on the West End strip to decent seafood restaurants along the port. The bay promenade has the better restaurants — look for fresh grilled fish and the local Ibizan staples such as sofrit pagès (a rich meat stew) and bullit de peix (fish broth with rice). Prices are substantially lower than in Ibiza Town.
