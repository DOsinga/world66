Huevo de Colon is the egg-shaped monument in the centre of Sant Antoni, raised in 1992 for the 500th anniversary of Columbus's voyage to the Americas. It is more meeting point than masterpiece, but it is hard to miss and gives the resort town one of its few odd civic landmarks.
