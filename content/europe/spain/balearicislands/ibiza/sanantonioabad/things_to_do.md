title: Things to Do
type: section
---

San Antonio has more going for it than its party reputation suggests. The sunset strip is genuinely one of the most beautiful evening spectacles on the island, and the beaches to the west of town — Cala Bassa, Cala Conta, Cala Salada — rank among Ibiza's finest. If you rent a bicycle or scooter, you can reach quieter coves in twenty minutes.
