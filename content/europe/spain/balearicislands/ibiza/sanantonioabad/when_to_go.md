---
title: When to Go
type: section
---

San Antonio is at its worst and its best in July and August — worst because the resort is overwhelmed with tourists, the roads are gridlocked, and accommodation prices are outrageous; best because the beaches are glorious and every bar is open. June and September are the sweet spot: warm enough for swimming, beaches accessible, prices lower, and the most intense of the crowds absent.

Outside May to October the resort largely closes. Many bars and restaurants shut entirely, and the place has the melancholy air of a fairground in the rain. If you are coming for the music and the nightlife, come in summer. If you are coming for the coves and the countryside, May, June, or late September will give you Ibiza at something close to its best.
