The [Platja de S'Arenal](/europe/spain/balearicislands/mallorca/arenal/platja_de_sarenal) is the focus of everything here — a wide, well-maintained sandy beach running west along the Bay of Palma. It connects seamlessly with the neighbouring [Playa de Palma](/europe/spain/balearicislands/mallorca/playa_de_palma) beach, making the combined strip one of the longest urban beaches in the Balearics. Sun loungers and parasols are available for hire along most of the length; the eastern end, nearest the marina, tends to be a little quieter.

The water is calm, shallow, and clear — well suited to families and non-swimmers. The beach is cleaned daily in summer and holds a Blue Flag designation.
