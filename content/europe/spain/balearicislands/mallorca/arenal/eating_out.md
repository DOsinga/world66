---
title: Eating Out
type: section
---

Eating out in S'Arenal is casual and unpretentious. The seafront and the streets just back from the beach are lined with restaurants serving a mix of Spanish standards, German-inflected menus (schnitzel and pork knuckle are ubiquitous), and generic tourist fare. Look for the daily specials on the chalkboards outside, particularly the *menú del día* at lunchtime, which typically gets you three courses with bread and a drink for around €10–12.

For a taste of the local Mallorcan kitchen, head inland to the Llucmajor-side streets where a handful of quieter local restaurants cater to residents rather than package tourists.
