---
title: When to Go
type: section
---

S'Arenal operates essentially as a summer resort: the hotels, bars, and most of the restaurants are open from May to October, with the peak season running July through August. In those months the beach is crowded, prices are at their highest, and the Ballermann strip is in full swing. If you want the beach without the crush, late May, June, or early September are significantly more pleasant.

Outside high summer, temperatures are mild — daytime highs around 18–20°C in spring and autumn. Many businesses close from November through March and the town is quiet to the point of being slightly deserted. July and August temperatures regularly reach 30°C; the sea breeze off the bay makes it bearable.
