---
title: Getting There
type: section
---

Palma de Mallorca Airport (PMI) is about 12 km from Magaluf. Taxis take about 20 minutes and are the most convenient option. A cheaper alternative is to take the bus from the airport to Palma and then change to the local TIB bus service to Magaluf, but this takes considerably longer with luggage.

From [Palma](/europe/spain/balearicislands/mallorca/palmademallorca), the TIB bus route 103 connects the city to Magaluf. The journey takes around 30 minutes from the city centre. Car hire is widely available at the airport and makes it much easier to explore the surrounding coastline — the road through Calvià and along to Andratx passes some excellent scenery.
