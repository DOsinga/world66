---
title: Things to Do
type: section
---

Magaluf is not a cultural destination, and it does not pretend to be one. The beach is the main event, followed by the waterpark and, for those seeking it, the strip of bars and clubs. For something different, the short walk to Cap de Cala Figuera or the boat trip to Palma rewards anyone willing to go slightly off the resort itinerary.
