The Museu Etnològic dels Calderers is set in an 18th-century Mallorcan mansion near Sant Joan. Rooms, tools, farm buildings, and household objects are arranged to show rural island life before mass tourism changed Mallorca's economy.

It is a good inland stop if you want a break from beaches and resort towns. Tastings of local produce are often part of the visit.
