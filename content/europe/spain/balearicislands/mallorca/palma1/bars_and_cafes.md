---
title: Bars and Cafes
type: section
---

The La Lonja neighbourhood around the Llotja is the epicentre of Palma's bar scene, with tables spreading out from bars along the Passeig de Sagrera in summer. The old town has good cafe options around the Passeig des Born. For a local coffee, look for the small neighbourhood cafes rather than the tourist-facing terraces; Palma has a strong cafe culture and the Mallorcans take their breakfast seriously — a pa amb oli (bread rubbed with tomato and olive oil) with coffee is the standard morning combination.
