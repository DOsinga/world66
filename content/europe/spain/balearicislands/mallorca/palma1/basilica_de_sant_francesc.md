Basilica de Sant Francesc is a 13th-century church and cloister in Palma's old town, close in spirit to the cathedral but usually much calmer. The Gothic interior is plain in the best way, while the cloister rewards slow looking with slender columns, worn stone, and a little breathing room away from the city's busier sights.
