Fundacion Juan March Palma is a modern art museum in a 17th-century mansion in central Palma. It is a branch of the Madrid foundation established by financier Juan March Ordinas.

The collection includes work by major Spanish artists such as Picasso, Miro, and Dali, making it a useful stop when you want an indoor cultural break close to the old town streets.
