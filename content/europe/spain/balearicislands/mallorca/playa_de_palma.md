Playa de Palma is a 3 km stretch of sandy beach running between the former fishing villages of Ca'n Pastilla and S'Arenal, a few kilometres east of Palma. Since the 1960s it has become one of the busiest resort strips in the Balearics, drawing a predominantly German-speaking crowd and earning a reputation for raucous nightlife. The beach itself is wide, clean, and well-equipped.

The seafront promenade — the Paseo Marítimo — is lined with bars, restaurants, and the odd surviving reminder of the fishing village origins. The water is calm and shallow, making it good for families with young children. At the Ca'n Pastilla end, the [Palma Aquarium](/europe/spain/balearicislands/mallorca/playa_de_palma/palma_aquarium) is worth an afternoon. The [Club Nàutic S'Arenal](/europe/spain/balearicislands/mallorca/playa_de_palma/club_nautic_sarenal) at the southern end offers sailing and water sports for those wanting more than sunbathing.

If you are after solitude or culture, this is not the place; if you want easy beach access with reliable sun and cold beer within minutes of the airport, it delivers.
