The Club Nàutic S'Arenal is the sailing club at the southern end of the Playa de Palma strip, with a marina that shelters several hundred boats of all sizes. Founded in the 1960s alongside the resort's early development, it offers sailing courses, yacht charters, and equipment rental for windsurfing and kayaking.

The bay here is well-suited to sailing — the southerly breeze picks up reliably in the afternoon, and the calm water close to shore makes it good for learners. Casual visitors can walk the marina to look at the boats and grab lunch at one of the terrace restaurants overlooking the water. The club also hosts regattas through the summer season that are worth watching if you happen to be around.
