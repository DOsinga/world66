title: Things to Do
type: section
---

Playa de Palma is first and foremost a beach destination, but there are a few worthwhile stops beyond the sand. The [Palma Aquarium](/europe/spain/balearicislands/mallorca/playa_de_palma/palma_aquarium) at the Ca'n Pastilla end of the strip is genuinely impressive. The [Club Nàutic S'Arenal](/europe/spain/balearicislands/mallorca/playa_de_palma/club_nautic_sarenal) offers sailing and water sports for those who want something more active than sunbathing.
