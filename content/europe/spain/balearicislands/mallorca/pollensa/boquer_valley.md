The Bóquer Valley is a celebrated short walk for birdwatchers and casual hikers, starting at the north end of Port de Pollença's beach and crossing a low pass to the rocky cove of Cala Bóquer. The walk is about two hours round trip, mostly easy underfoot.

It is one of the best places in the Balearics for autumn and spring bird migration — chats, warblers, raptors, and the resident blue rock thrush all turn up in the rocky valley. The end of the walk is a steep pebbly cove with crystal water, good for a swim before turning back.
