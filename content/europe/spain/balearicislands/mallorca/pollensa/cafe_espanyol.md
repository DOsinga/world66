A traditional Pollença café on Plaça Major, with tables under the plane trees and a long wooden bar inside. Good for an early-morning *cafè amb llet* and an *ensaïmada* before walking up the Calvari, and for an early-evening *cerveza* afterwards.

It is one of the best vantage points for watching the Sunday market come together at first light. The food menu is straightforward — *tapas*, *bocadillos*, occasional daily plates.
