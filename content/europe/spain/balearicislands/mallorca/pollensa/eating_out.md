---
title: "Eating Out"
type: section
---

Pollença has a stronger restaurant scene than its size suggests — a mix of solid Mallorcan home cooking, a couple of high-end places that have collected serious reviews, and the usual cluster of tourist-trade options around the main square. Specialities worth seeking out are *sobrasada* (the spreadable cured sausage), *tumbet* (the local ratatouille), *pa amb oli* (bread with oil and tomato, the Balearic staple), and the breakfast pastry *ensaïmada*.
