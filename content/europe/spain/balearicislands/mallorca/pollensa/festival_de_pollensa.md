Pollença's summer classical music festival, founded in 1962, draws serious orchestras and soloists to perform in the cloister of Sant Domingo — an open-air venue with extraordinary acoustics. Concerts run through July and August.

Tickets sell out for the marquee names but are usually obtainable for the less famous evenings. The atmosphere is intimate and informal by classical-festival standards; locals turn out in summer linens and the cloister fills with about 600 people.
