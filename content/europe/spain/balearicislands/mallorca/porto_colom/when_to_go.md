---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

Porto Colom is a summer place but more bearable than most of Mallorca's coast at the peak. May through June and September into early October are the best months: warm, sea swimmable, restaurants open, no crowds.

July and August fill up — the village population multiplies several times over with Spanish and German summer-house owners, and parking near the harbour becomes effectively impossible. Hotel and rental prices peak in the first three weeks of August.

November to March is genuinely quiet. The weather is mild rather than warm, many waterfront restaurants close, and the village becomes more of a place where locals live than a destination. Days can still be sunny; the sea is too cold for most swimmers but excellent for walks along the headland.
