---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

July and August are peak season: the beach is full, the restaurants are open, and the whole resort hums with activity. Prices are at their highest and accommodation books up well in advance. The weather is hot and reliably dry — temperatures regularly reach 32°C, and the sea is warm from June through to October. If you want the classic beach holiday experience and don't mind sharing it with everyone else, this is when to come.

May, June, and September are better for almost everything except guaranteed warmth. The weather is still excellent, the crowds are smaller, and you can get a room without booking months ahead. October is a shoulder month — many restaurants and beach bars close up by mid-October, but the weather often remains fine, and the light has a quality that July simply doesn't have. Serious birdwatchers should note that S'Albufera is at its best during spring and autumn migration, when species turn up that wouldn't be found in summer.

Winter is quiet to the point of somnolence. Most of the resort closes down, though Alcudia town itself remains a functioning community. If you want Mallorca without the tourism machine, January is yours. But don't expect much to be open along the waterfront.
