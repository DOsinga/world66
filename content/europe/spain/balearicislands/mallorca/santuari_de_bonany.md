Santuari de la Mare de Deu de Bonany is a hilltop hermitage in central Mallorca, with a Baroque chapel and broad views across the island's plains. The setting is the main reason to come, especially in clear weather.

It is a simple, quiet stop rather than a large monument, but it works well as a detour while exploring the inland villages around Petra and Sineu.
