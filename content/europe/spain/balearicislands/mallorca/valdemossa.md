Valldemossa is a small mountain village tucked into the Serra de Tramuntana, easily reached by bus from [Palma](/europe/spain/balearicislands/mallorca/palma1) or from Sóller. It looks down over the rugged north-west coast of Mallorca with views that stop you in your tracks.

The village is forever linked to Frédéric Chopin and George Sand, who spent the winter of 1838–39 in a converted cell at the [Real Cartuja](/europe/spain/balearicislands/mallorca/valdemossa/real_cartuja) — the old Carthusian monastery at the heart of the village. The visit didn't do Chopin's tuberculosis any favours — and getting a piano delivered to a remote Mallorcan village in winter was its own ordeal — but the setting was extraordinary. Just beside the monastery is the [Palau del Rei Sanxo](/europe/spain/balearicislands/mallorca/valdemossa/palau_del_rei_sanxo), a medieval royal palace where Chopin recitals are performed on the hour, even if you happen to be the only person there.

Beyond the monastery circuit, the [village centre](/europe/spain/balearicislands/mallorca/valdemossa/village_centre) rewards unhurried wandering — stone lanes, flower-draped courtyards, and a good pa amb oli bar or two. Valldemossa also serves as an excellent base for [walking in the Serra de Tramuntana](/europe/spain/balearicislands/mallorca/valdemossa/tramuntana_walks), with the GR-221 long-distance route passing through the village.
