Cala en Porter is a sheltered south-coast beach set at the end of a narrow bay below pale cliffs. The sand, shallow water, and easy facilities make it one of Menorca's more family-friendly coves.

It is more developed than the island's remote beaches, but that is part of the appeal if you want a simple swim stop with services close at hand.
