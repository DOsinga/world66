Cala Macarella and the smaller Cala Macarelleta are two of Menorca's best-known coves, with clear blue water, pale cliffs, and pine-backed sand. Macarella is the easier beach to reach, while Macarelleta takes a short walk over the headland and is usually quieter. Arrive early in summer, when both fill quickly.
