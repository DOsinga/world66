Caló Blanc is a tiny cove on Menorca, more a pocket of pale rock and clear water than a full beach. Its small size is the point: it feels sheltered, simple, and very different from the island's broader sandy beaches.

There is little room here, so come early and do not expect much infrastructure. It is best for a swim and a short stop while exploring the coast around Sant Lluís.
