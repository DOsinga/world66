Castel Sant Felip was built in the 16th century to guard the entrance to Maó harbour, then expanded by the British in the 1760s as Menorca changed hands. Much of it was demolished after later fighting, but the remains still show why this harbour mattered so much.

The site is most interesting if you care about Menorca's military history or want context before visiting nearby Fort Marlborough. The surviving tunnels and walls make the harbour feel less like scenery and more like a contested prize.
