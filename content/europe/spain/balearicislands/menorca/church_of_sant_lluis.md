The Church of Sant Lluis is a neoclassical parish church designed by French architect Antoine d'Allemand. Its 32-metre bell tower is the most visible landmark in Sant Lluis, and the facade carries a French royal coat of arms above a three-arched portico.

It is a short architectural stop if you are exploring the southeast of Menorca or passing through Sant Lluis on the way to the coast.
