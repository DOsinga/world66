The Pedreres de s'Hostal is a former sandstone quarry on the edge of Ciutadella that has been transformed into one of Menorca's most unusual cultural spaces. The quarry provided much of the marès — the local honey-coloured sandstone — used to build Ciutadella over the centuries, and the vertical walls of extracted stone create a natural amphitheatre of considerable beauty. The quarry closed in the 1990s and was subsequently acquired by a conservation foundation.

Today the site has two distinct areas: an upper part with sculpture gardens, where contemporary works are set among the quarry terraces and seasonal wildflowers, and a lower excavated section where the scale of the operation becomes clear — chambers and corridors cut deep into the soft rock, with a cathedral-like quality of light. Events and exhibitions are held in the quarry throughout the year, taking advantage of the natural acoustics. It is open for visits most days in summer; hours are reduced in winter.
