The Plaça des Born is the main square of Ciutadella and one of the finest in the Balearic Islands. It occupies a natural terrace overlooking the harbour, with views down to the water through a gap in the old town walls. The square has the unhurried formality of a place that was once the centre of political power: the 19th-century town hall faces the former governor's palace across the flagstones, and the facades of noble houses frame the surrounding streets.

The square is most animated in the evening, when the whole town seems to pass through. Cafes with outdoor tables line the western side, and the harbour below fills with the lights of the moored boats. During the Fiesta de Sant Joan in June, the square is the stage for the Jocs des Pla, the traditional horseback events that are the centrepiece of Menorca's most important festival: local noblemen dressed in 16th-century costume ride black horses through crowds that press against them.
