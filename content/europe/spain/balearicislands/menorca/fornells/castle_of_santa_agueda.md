story: "Santa Àgueda was the last Moorish stronghold on Menorca, holding out against Aragonese forces until 1287 — the final year of the island's Islamic period. The castle itself is ruined, but the Roman-era road that climbs to it — paved with flat stones still in place after nearly two millennia — is one of the most atmospheric walks on the island. The summit is Menorca's third-highest point and commands views from coast to coast on a clear day."
---

A ruined Moorish hilltop castle in the municipality of Ferreries, about 15 kilometres south of Fornells. The approach follows a well-preserved Roman road through pine forest; the walk from the car park at Santa Cecília farmstead takes around an hour each way. The ruins at the top are skeletal but the views justify the effort.
