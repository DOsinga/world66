It is not the island's most spectacular beach — those require a drive to the wilder north coast — but its proximity to the village and the gentle conditions make it the most practical for a casual swim between lunch and exploring the waterfront."
---

A small, calm beach on the sheltered inner shore of the bay, within walking distance of the village centre. The water is shallow and clear, with sea-grass beds visible beneath the surface. No facilities beyond a few summer sun loungers; best visited in the morning before the afternoon boats stir up the bay.
