story: "The British built this cylindrical watchtower in 1802 during their third occupation of Menorca, intended to guard the entrance to the bay against French naval incursion. It was never seriously tested in battle. The tower has been restored and is open to visitors; the views from the roof take in the full sweep of the bay and the village below."
---

A British-built coastal watchtower on the southern edge of Fornells, dating from the early 19th century. The climb to the top is short and the panorama over the bay is worth the effort. Entry is cheap and the site rarely crowded, even in summer.
