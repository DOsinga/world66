Windsurf Fornells is the main rental and instruction centre, operating on the western shore of the bay. It also offers kayak, paddleboard, and catamaran hire for those who prefer a slower pace on the water."

The main water sports centre on the Bay of Fornells, offering windsurfing, kayak, paddleboard, and sailing instruction and rental. Equipment quality is consistently good and the instructors speak English. Beginners and families are well catered for; the sheltered bay is genuinely forgiving for first-timers.
