Illa de l'Aire is a small islet just south of Punta Prima, known for its lighthouse, black lizards, and protected waters. The surrounding marine reserve has richer fish life than much of the nearby Mediterranean, making the island a good focus for boat trips and snorkelling around Menorca's southeast coast.
