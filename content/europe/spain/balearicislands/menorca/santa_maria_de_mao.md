Santa Maria de Maó is an 18th-century Roman Catholic church in the centre of Maó. The interior is neoclassical, but the main reason to step inside is the large organ completed in 1810.

It is an easy stop while exploring Maó's old town and harbour streets. The church is most rewarding if you can hear the organ or visit when the interior is open.
