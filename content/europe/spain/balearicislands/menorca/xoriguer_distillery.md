Xoriguer Distillery is a historic gin distillery in Mao, continuing a Menorcan tradition that grew from the island's British period. Visitors can see the old copper stills and taste the distillery's gin and liqueurs.

The visit is short, but it is a useful stop near the harbour if you are interested in local food and drink traditions. The shop is open year-round.
