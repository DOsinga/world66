Azkuna Zentroa is a former wine warehouse turned cultural centre, redesigned with striking interiors by Philippe Starck and Thibaut Mathieu. The complex includes exhibition spaces, a library, cinemas, restaurants, and a roof swimming pool with a glass floor.
