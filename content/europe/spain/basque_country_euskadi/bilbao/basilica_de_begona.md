Basílica de Begoña stands above Bilbao’s old town, reached by stairs or a short climb that immediately changes the feel of the city. The church is dedicated to the Virgin of Begoña, the city’s patron, and remains a living devotional place rather than just a historic monument.

The basilica pairs naturally with Casco Viejo: climb up for the church, the quieter hilltop atmosphere, and a different angle on Bilbao’s tight valley geography. Inside, keep the visit respectful; outside, take a moment to look back toward the city.
