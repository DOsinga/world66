---
title: "Getting Around"
type: section
---

The city centre is compact and the Casco Viejo, Guggenheim, and Ensanche are all within comfortable walking distance of each other — 20 minutes on foot from one end to the other. For longer journeys or wet weather, Metro Bilbao (Lines 1 and 2) covers the city efficiently. The metro is a sight in itself: Norman Foster's glass entrance canopies (*fosteritos*) have become city icons.

The tram (Euskotren) runs through the centre along the Nervión riverbank, connecting the Casco Viejo with the Guggenheim and the conference centre area. A BilbaoCard covers metro, tram, and bus services and pays for itself quickly if you use public transport regularly.
