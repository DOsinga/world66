An old-fashioned village bar that has been the social hub of Laguardia proper — meaning the people who actually live here — for generations. The interior has the comfortable shabbiness of somewhere nobody has felt the need to renovate because everything works perfectly well already. Locals come for the afternoon coffee and the evening wine; visitors are welcome but will feel they've wandered into real village life rather than a tourist bar. Wine is local and cheap. Calle Mayor.
