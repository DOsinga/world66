A wine-focused bar that takes the local product seriously, with a selection of Rioja Alavesa wines by the glass spanning everything from young, fruit-forward joven to aged reservas and gran reservas from smaller producers. Good for comparing styles side by side before deciding what to buy. The pintxo selection is better than average and the staff know the wines well enough to guide you. A natural stop after a bodega tour when you want to continue exploring what the region produces. Calle Mayor, Laguardia.
