One of Laguardia's most respected tables, Amelibia has built its reputation on straightforward but excellent Riojan cooking: roasted lamb, grilled vegetables from the local kitchen garden, and the classic potato and pepper stew (patatas a la riojana) done with real care. The dining room is set in a stone-vaulted cellar beneath the old town, which provides natural atmosphere. The wine list is extensive and focused on Rioja Alavesa producers, including some smaller bodegas not widely distributed. Tasting menus are available at weekends. Calle Mayor, Laguardia.
