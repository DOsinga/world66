---
title: Getting Around
type: section
---

Laguardia's old town is tiny and entirely walkable — end to end in less than ten minutes. The streets inside the walls are pedestrianised and flat enough for most visitors, though there are some cobbled slopes and uneven surfaces typical of medieval towns.

The wineries and sites outside the old town require transport. Most are 5–15km from the village across open countryside with no footpaths, so a car or bicycle is needed. The Rioja Alavesa wine route connects bodegas across the valley; pick up a map at the tourist office.

Cycling is a popular way to explore the valley. The terrain is mostly gently rolling vineyard country and the distances between bodegas are manageable. Bike hire is available in Laguardia; ask at the tourist office for current operators. Some bodegas specifically cater to cyclists with secure storage and routes.

Taxis are available in Laguardia for trips to wineries; the tourist office can arrange these. There is no public transport between the village and the wineries.
