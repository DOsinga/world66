---
title: "Getting There"
type: section
---

Portugalete is on Bilbao Metro Line 2 (Bilbao–Santurtzi), about 20 minutes from central Bilbao. The Portugalete station is a few minutes' walk from the Vizcaya Bridge. Trains run frequently throughout the day. By car from Bilbao, follow the A-8 motorway west and take the Portugalete exit; parking can be tight on weekends.
