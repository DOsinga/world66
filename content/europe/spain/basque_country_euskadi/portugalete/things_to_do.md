---

Portugalete rewards a half-day visit. The [Vizcaya Bridge](/europe/spain/basque_country_euskadi/portugalete/vizcaya_bridge) is unmissable — ride the gondola across or walk the upper walkway for estuary views. From the bridge, the old town climbs steeply uphill to the [Basilica de Santa María](/europe/spain/basque_country_euskadi/portugalete/basilica_de_santa_maria), with narrow lanes and pintxo bars along the way. The [Nervión Promenade](/europe/spain/basque_country_euskadi/portugalete/nervion_promenade) runs along the waterfront below the bridge.
