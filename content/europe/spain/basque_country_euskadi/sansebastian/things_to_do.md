San Sebastián packs an extraordinary range of experiences into a small city. The [Playa de la Concha](/europe/spain/basque_country_euskadi/sansebastian/playa_de_la_concha) is one of the finest urban beaches in Europe; [Monte Urgull](/europe/spain/basque_country_euskadi/sansebastian/monte_urgull) with its castle and giant Christ offers the classic panorama over the bay; and the [San Telmo Museum](/europe/spain/basque_country_euskadi/sansebastian/san_telmo_museum) is the best place to understand Basque culture. But the real reason most people come is the food — see the Eating Out and Bars and Cafes sections for the pintxo bars and Michelin-starred restaurants that make this the food capital of Europe.
