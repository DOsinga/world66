The home of Saski Baskonia, Vitoria's basketball club and one of the founding teams of the EuroLeague. The 15,000-seat arena, opened in 2008, also hosts concerts, conferences, and the city's New Year's Eve celebration.

Saski Baskonia has won three Spanish league titles and consistently competes at European level. Catching a game on a winter evening — preferably the local rivalry against Real Madrid or Barcelona — is one of the more authentic Vitoria experiences.
