[Laguardia](/europe/spain/basque_country_euskadi/laguardia), a walled medieval town in the heart of Rioja Alavesa wine country, is 45 minutes south and the obvious wine-tasting day out. The villages of the Cantabrian range south of the city — Salinas de Añana with its terraced salt pans, Quejana with its painted Gothic chapel — make a different kind of day. The painted cliffs and prehistoric caves of the Valle de Valdegovía are an hour west. Bigger trips reach [Bilbao](/europe/spain/basque_country_euskadi/bilbao) and the Guggenheim or [San Sebastián](/europe/spain/basque_country_euskadi/sansebastian) for La Concha beach.
