A small square tucked at the foot of the San Miguel church, on the upper of the two levels of the Los Arquillos arcade. The medieval iron grille protecting the niche of the ceremonial sword (machete) is still in place on the back of the church.

A fine spot for a quiet sit-down with a coffee, between the noise of Plaza de la Virgen Blanca below and the busier streets of the old town above.
