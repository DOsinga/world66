Saladrigas House is a Catalan Noucentista house built between 1918 and 1920 by architect Isidre Puig i Boada. Its main appeal is the facade detail, especially the wrought iron work on the balconies.

It is a short architectural stop rather than a major attraction, but it fits naturally into a walk through central Blanes before heading toward the harbour, Sa Palomera, or the Marimurtra Botanical Garden.
