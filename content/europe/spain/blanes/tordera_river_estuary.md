The Tordera River Estuary marks the southern edge of Blanes, where river, beach and wetland habitats meet. It is a modest but worthwhile nature stop, especially for birdwatchers and walkers who want a break from the resort seafront.

The landscape changes with water levels, storms and migration seasons, so expectations should stay flexible. Combine it with a beach walk or the botanical gardens rather than treating it as a standalone destination.
