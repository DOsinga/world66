---
title: "Getting There"
type: section
---

Cáceres is well connected by train and reasonably accessible by road. The high-speed AVE line connects Cáceres to Madrid (journey time around 2.5 hours) and Badajoz, with connections to Lisbon. Trains arrive at the station about 2 km west of the city centre.

By road, Cáceres sits on the A-66 (Via de la Plata motorway) running north-south through Extremadura, and the A-58 running west to Portugal. The nearest major airports are Madrid Barajas (295 km) and Lisbon (280 km); Badajoz has a small regional airport 90 km away. Long-distance buses connect Cáceres to Madrid, Seville, Salamanca, and cross-border services to Lisbon.
