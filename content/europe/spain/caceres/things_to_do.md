title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

The Ciudad Monumental is reason enough to come to Cáceres. The walled old town is among the most intact medieval and Renaissance urban environments in Europe, with dozens of noble palaces, towers, and churches packed into a small area. Walk it on foot — no cars penetrate the inner streets — and take your time with the details: carved escutcheons above doorways, storks nesting on tower tops, Roman foundations visible at street level.

Outside the walls, the Plaza Mayor is the social heart of the city, with the old town gates forming a dramatic backdrop. The contemporary art museum is a serious institution worth half a day. The Cave of Maltravieso, a short walk from the centre, contains hand stencils dated at over 66,000 years old — older than any modern human known to have been in Europe, which raises uncomfortable questions about who made them.
