---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

Spring (March–May) and autumn (September–November) are the best times to visit. Temperatures are pleasant, the light on the old stone is beautiful, and crowds are manageable. Spring brings storks back to the tower tops — their nests are a feature of the skyline.

Summer is hot (regularly above 40°C in July–August) and the old town can feel airless. Many locals leave; some restaurants close in August. Winter is mild by Spanish standards but cool, with occasional frost. The WOMAD festival, held in Cáceres each May, is one of the best world music festivals in Europe and brings the city briefly to international attention.
