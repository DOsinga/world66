The Faro de Orchilla (Orchilla Lighthouse) stands at the far southwest tip of El Hierro, occupying what was for centuries the western edge of the known world. For over a thousand years, the meridian passing through this point served as the zero longitude — the line from which European cartographers measured east and west across the globe.

The drive out to the lighthouse crosses arid volcanic landscape, the terrain becoming progressively more stark and windswept as you approach the cape. The lighthouse itself is a working station and not accessible inside, but the setting — sheer cliffs, black rock, and the open Atlantic — is powerful. On a clear day the drop from the cliffs to the ocean feels vertiginous. The road is rough in places and a 4WD is useful.
