Villa Winter stands above Cofete like the beginning of a spy novel: isolated, severe, and surrounded by rumours that are often more dramatic than the evidence. Built in the 20th century on the Jandía peninsula, the house is linked in popular imagination to wartime intrigue, secret passages, and German connections, though travellers should keep a firm line between documented history and local legend.

Even without the stories, it is a memorable stop because of the setting. The road to Cofete is rough, the mountains are bare, and the Atlantic below looks huge and indifferent. Villa Winter works best as part of a wider Cofete outing, where the building, the beach, and the harsh landscape all reinforce each other.
