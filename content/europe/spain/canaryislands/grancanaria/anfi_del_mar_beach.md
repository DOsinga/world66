Anfi del Mar Beach is a sheltered resort beach on Gran Canaria's south coast, known for pale imported sand that looks more Caribbean than Canarian. The water is usually calm, which makes it an easy choice for families and less confident swimmers.

Do not come expecting wilderness. The beach is built into a resort complex, with facilities close by and plenty of people in season. Come for comfort, swimming, and a very managed version of the island's sunny south.
