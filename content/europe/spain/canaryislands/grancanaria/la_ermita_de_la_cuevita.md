La Ermita de la Cuevita is a small cave chapel in the mountains of central Gran Canaria, dedicated to the Virgen de la Cuevita. The setting is the point: a devotional space cut into the rock, tied to island traditions rather than the resort coast most visitors first see.

The chapel is especially important in late August, when a procession and cycling events honour the Virgin, patron of cyclists and folk musicians. At other times it is a quiet detour if you are exploring the interior around Artenara.
