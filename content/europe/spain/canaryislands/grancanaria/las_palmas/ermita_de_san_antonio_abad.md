Ermita de San Antonio Abad is a small former church in Vegueta, built in 1757 on the site of an older chapel. Local tradition links the spot with Columbus before his Atlantic crossing, and today the building is used as an exhibition space connected with the Centro Atlantico de Arte Moderno.
