Poema del Mar is Las Palmas' modern aquarium, opened in 2018 near the port and arranged around jungle, reef, beach, and deep-ocean habitats. The large viewing windows, rays, sharks, and family-friendly layout make it a good bad-weather option or a change of pace from Las Canteras.
