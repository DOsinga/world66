Pueblo Canario is a compact 1930s interpretation of a traditional Canarian village, set beside Doramas Park. It was designed by artist Nestor Martin-Fernandez de la Torre and completed by his brother Miguel, with whitewashed walls, timber balconies, and a staged village-square feel.

Come for the architecture, the craft displays, or one of the folk performances. It is deliberately theatrical, but it gives Las Palmas a handy place to see Canarian design in one short stop.
