Los Azulejos is a roadside stretch of strangely coloured volcanic rock in western Gran Canaria, striped in green, ochre, and blue-grey mineral tones. It is a quick stop rather than a destination, but a good one if you are driving the mountain roads and want a clear look at the island's raw geology.
