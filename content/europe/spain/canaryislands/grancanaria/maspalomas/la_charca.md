La Charca is the brackish lagoon between the Maspalomas dunes and the lighthouse, where water birds gather in the middle of the resort zone. It is not pristine wilderness, but the contrast between dunes, palms, water, and nearby hotels makes it one of Maspalomas's most distinctive natural corners.

Birdwatchers linger here, especially around the edges of the lagoon. Expect occasional smells from the still water.
