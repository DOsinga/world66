Aqualand Maspalomas is the largest water park in the south of Gran Canaria and a reliable choice for families travelling with children. The park has a wide range of slides and pools covering everything from gentle toddler-friendly splash areas to high-speed tube slides and free-fall drops for thrill-seekers.

Admission includes unlimited use of all attractions, and there are enough places to eat and drink inside to make a full day of it without leaving the park. It can get busy in peak season, so arriving early is advisable to secure sun loungers and beat the queues on the most popular rides.
