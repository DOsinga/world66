---
title: 'Bars and Cafes'
type: section
---
Nightlife in Playa del Inglés is dominated by the Yumbo Centre, a large open-air complex that transforms after dark into one of Europe's most famous LGBTQ+ entertainment hubs. Bars, clubs, and karaoke venues fill its four floors, and the party tends to get going well past midnight. Outside the Yumbo, the hotel strips have plenty of more conventional bars serving cheap beer and cocktails to a crowd in search of uncomplicated fun.
