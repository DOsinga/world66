---
title: "Bars and Cafes"
type: section
---

Playa del Inglés has more bars than most towns ten times its permanent population. The main concentration is at the Yumbo Centrum, which operates as a conventional shopping mall during the day and transforms into the centre of LGBTQ+ nightlife after dark — hundreds of bars, clubs, and drag venues spread across four floors. The Yumbo is explicitly gay-friendly but not exclusively so; the crowd is mixed and the atmosphere generally welcoming.

Beyond the Yumbo, the Cita shopping centre has a bar and restaurant concentration with a broader crowd mix. The beach promenade has scattered beach bars open through the day. The resort's rhythm is predictable: things are quiet until mid-morning, beach bars fill through the afternoon, and the nightlife proper does not start until late — midnight is not an early start here. If you want something calmer, the Meloneras end of the resort, closer to the lighthouse, has a more sedate bar scene aimed at slightly older visitors.
