The Maspalomas lighthouse stands at the far western tip of the beach, where the dunes meet a small lagoon and the coast turns north. Built in 1890, the 55-metre white tower is one of Gran Canaria's most recognisable landmarks and marks the southernmost point of the island.

The walk to the lighthouse from Playa del Inglés along the beach and through the dunes takes about an hour and is one of the best ways to experience both the coastline and the dune reserve. The lagoon just behind the lighthouse attracts wading birds and is a pleasant spot to rest before heading back.
