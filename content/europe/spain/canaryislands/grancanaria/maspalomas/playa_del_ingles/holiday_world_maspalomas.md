Holiday World Maspalomas is a family amusement complex with rides, bowling, restaurants, pubs, and evening entertainment. The amusement park uses a points card system for rides rather than a simple admission ticket.

It is most useful for families or groups looking for something to do after the beach, especially in the evening.
