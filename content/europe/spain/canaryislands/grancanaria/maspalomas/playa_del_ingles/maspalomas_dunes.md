The Maspalomas dunes are the most dramatic natural feature on Gran Canaria's southern coast — 400 hectares of shifting Saharan-looking sand that feel utterly out of place next to a major tourist resort. Walking into the dune field, the hotels and beach bars disappear behind the ridgelines and you could be anywhere in the Sahara.

The dunes are protected as a Special Nature Reserve and are crossed by marked paths. The western edge meets the Maspalomas lagoon and lighthouse, making a walk from Playa del Inglés beach through the dunes to the lighthouse one of the most enjoyable excursions in the south of Gran Canaria.
