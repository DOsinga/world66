Parque Botanico is a modest green pocket in Maspalomas, useful when the resort streets and beach promenade start to feel too built-up. Paths wind past palms, succulents, flowering shrubs, and shaded corners that show off the dry-climate plants that suit southern Gran Canaria.

Do not expect a major botanical institution. Treat it as an easy hour outdoors, especially if you are staying in Playa del Ingles and want a low-key break from the dunes, shopping centres, and beach crowds.
