Paseo Costa Canaria is the promenade that makes Playa del Inglés easier to enjoy on foot. It runs along the seafront and low cliffs, with views over the beach, the Atlantic, and toward the Maspalomas dunes.

Use it early or late in the day, when the resort crowds thin and the light is kinder. It is less about one landmark than about stitching together beaches, viewpoints, cafes, and the long sandy edge of southern Gran Canaria.
