Playa de Maspalomas sits on the far side of the dune reserve from Playa del Inglés proper, separated by the full width of the protected area. This means you either walk through the dunes to reach it — around 30 minutes from the eastern end of the main beach — or approach from the Maspalomas village side near the lighthouse. The result is a beach that sees far fewer visitors than the main strip, even in high season.

The beach itself is wide and the sand is similar to the main beach, though the shoreline curves more dramatically here as it follows the edge of the dune field. The Charca de Maspalomas lagoon sits just inland, visible from the western end of the beach, and wading birds work the shallows around its margins. There are sun loungers and a couple of beach bars at the Maspalomas end, but the further east you walk toward the dunes the facilities thin out quickly. This is also where the naturist section of the beach begins.
