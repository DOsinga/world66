The beach at Playa del Inglés is the reason most people come here — several kilometres of golden sand backed by a promenade of hotels, restaurants, and beach bars. The Atlantic keeps the water fresh even in summer, and the steady trade wind makes it popular with surfers and windsurfers as well as sunbathers.

The beach merges seamlessly to the west with the Maspalomas dunes, making it possible to walk from the hotel zone into a landscape that feels remarkably remote. Sun loungers and parasols are available for hire along most of the beach, and the water is generally calm and safe for swimming.
