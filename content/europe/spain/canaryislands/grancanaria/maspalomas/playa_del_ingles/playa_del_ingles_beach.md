The beach at Playa del Inglés runs about three kilometres from roughly San Agustín in the east to where the sand begins its transition into the Maspalomas dune system in the west. The sand is a warm gold colour, the water stays between 19 and 23 degrees Celsius through most of the year, and the gentle Atlantic swell makes it comfortable for swimming most days. Sun loungers and parasols are available for hire along the full length.

The beach is wide enough that it never feels completely overcrowded even in peak season, though the stretch directly below the main promenade fills up quickly in the mornings. The further west you walk toward the dunes, the quieter it gets and the fewer facilities you will find. Lifeguard towers are staffed during peak hours; watch for the flag system — a yellow flag means swim with caution, red means stay out of the water. Public showers and foot-washing stations are at regular intervals along the promenade above the beach.
