---
title: 'Things to Do'
type: section
---
Playa del Inglés is the beating heart of Gran Canaria's southern tourist strip — a purpose-built resort of wide sandy beaches, towering hotel blocks, and relentless sunshine. The beach itself is magnificent, stretching south into the extraordinary dunes of Maspalomas. Beyond the sunbathing, there are water parks, boat trips, and a nightlife scene centred on the famous Yumbo Centre that runs until well after dawn.
