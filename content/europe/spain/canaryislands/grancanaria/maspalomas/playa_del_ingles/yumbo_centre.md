The Yumbo Centre is a large, open-air shopping and entertainment complex that doubles as the undisputed centre of Playa del Inglés nightlife. By day it operates as an ordinary shopping centre with supermarkets, pharmacies, and tourist shops. By night — especially from around midnight onwards — it becomes one of the best-known LGBTQ+ entertainment venues in Europe.

Four floors of bars, clubs, drag shows, and karaoke venues surround a central atrium that stays lively until sunrise. The atmosphere is inclusive and unpretentious. Even if late-night clubbing isn't your thing, an early-evening drink here while watching the crowd gather is worth experiencing.
