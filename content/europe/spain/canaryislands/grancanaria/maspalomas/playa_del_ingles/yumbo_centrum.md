The Yumbo Centrum sits on Avenida de Estados Unidos and operates on two completely different schedules. During daylight hours it functions as a standard resort shopping centre — a few supermarkets, souvenir shops, travel agents, and a handful of cafés serving full English breakfasts. After around 10pm the atmosphere changes entirely: the bars and clubs on the upper floors open their shutters, the lighting shifts to neon, and the place fills up. The LGBTQ+ scene here is the most established on the island, and the crowd tends to be mixed in age and nationality.

The complex has around 200 units spread across four open-air levels arranged around a central courtyard. Not every bar is worth your time, but the upper floors have a few places with good sightlines over the whole structure that work well for an early drink before moving on. Entrance to the complex is free; you pay at the bars. If you are not interested in nightlife, the daytime version is entirely ordinary.
