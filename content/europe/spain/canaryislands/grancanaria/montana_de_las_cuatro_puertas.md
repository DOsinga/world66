Montaña de las Cuatro Puertas is an archaeological site on a hill near Telde, used by the ancient Canarians as a place of worship. The caves, carved openings, and views over eastern Gran Canaria make it a worthwhile stop for visitors interested in the island before the Spanish conquest.
