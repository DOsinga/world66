Montana de Tauro is a mountain massif in Gran Canaria's interior, known for high walls, open views, and hiking routes across the top. It is a strong choice for visitors looking beyond the island's beaches.

Routes can feel exposed and remote, so check conditions and carry water. The reward is a broad look at the dry, rugged landscape of central Gran Canaria.
