The parish church of San Juan Bautista in Telde is one of Gran Canaria's most rewarding historic churches. Building began around 1520 in dark and pale volcanic stone, leaving a late Gothic facade that later Baroque changes did not erase.

Inside and out, the church feels older than much of the island's resort coast. It is worth a stop if you are crossing the east side of Gran Canaria and want a piece of the island's pre-tourism town life.
