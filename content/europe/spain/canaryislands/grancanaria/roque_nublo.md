Roque Nublo is one of Gran Canaria's most recognizable natural landmarks, a volcanic rock monolith high in the island's central mountains. The walk from the Ayacata area is a classic short hike, with broad views over the valleys and, in clear weather, across to Tenerife.

Bring water and sun protection, and allow enough time to enjoy the viewpoints rather than treating it as a quick roadside photo stop.
