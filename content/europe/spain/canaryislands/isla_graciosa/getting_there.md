---
title: "Getting There"
type: section
---

The only way to reach La Graciosa is by ferry from Órzola on the northern tip of [Lanzarote](/europe/spain/canaryislands/lanzarote). The crossing takes about 30 minutes. Líneas Romero operates several departures daily, more in summer. Day trippers fill the morning boats; if you are staying overnight, the afternoon ferries are calmer and cheaper.

There are no cars on La Graciosa — officially it is the largest car-free inhabited island in Spain. Bicycles and walking are the only options once you arrive. You can bring a bicycle on the ferry.
