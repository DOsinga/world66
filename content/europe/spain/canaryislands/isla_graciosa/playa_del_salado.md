Playa del Salado is one of the easiest beaches to reach from Caleta de Sebo, which makes it a practical first swim or last pause on La Graciosa. The sand, low dunes, and views back toward Lanzarote give it the simple, open feel that makes the island so appealing.

It is not as dramatic as Playa Francesa, but convenience has its own value here. Bring shade and water, because La Graciosa offers little cover, and remember that the island's low-key charm depends on visitors leaving the beach as clean as they found it.
