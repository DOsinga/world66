Alto de Garajonay is the highest point on La Gomera, rising to 1,487 metres inside Garajonay National Park. On a clear day the summit gives a full 360-degree view across the island and out toward Tenerife, El Hierro, and La Palma.

Cloud and mist can close in quickly, so it is worth timing the walk for a clear spell. Even a short visit gives a strong sense of the island's volcanic shape and highland forest.
