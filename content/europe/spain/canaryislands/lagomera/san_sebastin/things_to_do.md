title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

San Sebastián de La Gomera is compact enough to explore on foot in a single morning, but the three sites connected to Columbus and the town's founding make it genuinely worthwhile. The [Torre del Conde](/europe/spain/canaryislands/lagomera/san_sebastin/torre_del_conde) dominates the waterfront; the [Casa de Colón](/europe/spain/canaryislands/lagomera/san_sebastin/casa_de_colon) tells the Columbus story well; and the [Iglesia de la Asunción](/europe/spain/canaryislands/lagomera/san_sebastin/iglesia_de_la_asuncion) is where the admiral is said to have prayed before his first departure. El Pozo de la Aguada, the colonial well from which Columbus drew fresh water, is a short walk from the plaza.
