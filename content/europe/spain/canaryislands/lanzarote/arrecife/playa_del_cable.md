Playa del Cable is a local beach on the edge of Arrecife, less polished than Playa del Reducto and more useful if you are staying nearby or walking the coast toward Playa Honda. The sand, low buildings, and open Atlantic light make it a pleasant pause without much ceremony.

It is not one of Lanzarote's great beaches, but it clears the bar as an easy city-coast stop. Come for a swim, a walk, or a quiet hour away from the harbour streets. For a full beach day, you may still prefer Reducto or the island's wilder southern coves.
