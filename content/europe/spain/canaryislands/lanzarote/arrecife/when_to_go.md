---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

Lanzarote has one of the most agreeable year-round climates in Europe: warm in winter, hot but rarely punishing in summer, with very little rain. December through March is the most popular time for European visitors looking for sun; June through September is hotter but still ventilated by the trade winds.

The Carnival of Arrecife in February or March is the city's biggest celebration, with parades, costumes, and a stretched-out week of street parties. Worth timing a visit around, though hotels book up fast.
