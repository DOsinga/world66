Casa Monumento al Campesino is Cesar Manrique's tribute to Lanzarote's rural workers, set near the island's geographic centre. The complex combines whitewashed architecture, a monument, craft workshops, and displays tied to traditional island life.

It is less a conventional museum than a cultural stop, useful for understanding how Manrique turned Lanzarote's farming landscape and vernacular buildings into part of the island's modern identity.
