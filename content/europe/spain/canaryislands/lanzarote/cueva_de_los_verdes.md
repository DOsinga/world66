Cueva de los Verdes is Lanzarote's show cave, part of an 8 km lava-tube system formed by the La Corona eruption about 4,000 years ago. The guided route is well lit and theatrical without feeling fake, and the strange colours and hidden drops make it one of the island's best inland sights.
