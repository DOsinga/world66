---
title: Getting Around
type: section
---

Playa Blanca is compact enough to walk end to end in 20 minutes, so most of the town itself is navigable on foot. For the Papagayo beaches you need transport — either a car, a taxi, or the shuttle buses that run from the main promenade in season.

Hiring a car from one of the agencies in town gives you the most flexibility, particularly if you want to make day trips to [Timanfaya National Park](/europe/spain/canaryislands/lanzarote/timanfaya) or the north of the island. Bicycles and electric scooters are available for rent from several points along the promenade.
