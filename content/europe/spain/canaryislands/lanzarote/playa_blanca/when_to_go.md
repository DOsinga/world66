---
title: When to Go
type: section
---

Lanzarote has one of the most consistent climates in Europe — warm and sunny year-round, with temperatures rarely falling below 18°C even in January. This makes Playa Blanca genuinely viable at any time of year, and it draws a significant winter sun crowd from northern Europe between November and March.

High summer (July–August) is the hottest and busiest period, with temperatures around 27–30°C and the Papagayo beaches at their most crowded. Spring (March–May) and autumn (October–November) offer warm weather with fewer visitors and lower prices. July and August occasionally bring hot dry winds from the Sahara (the calima), which push temperatures above 35°C and fill the air with fine dust — not pleasant for beach days. The calima can arrive without much warning.
