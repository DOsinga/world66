---
title: Bars and Cafes
type: section
---

Puerto del Carmen has a nightlife scene that runs the full length of the Avenida de las Playas — the eastern stretch known locally as **The Strip** (between the old harbour and roughly opposite the Hotel San Antonio) is the busiest, with a row of sports bars, karaoke pubs and small clubs that stay open until 3 or 4am in summer. It is loud, drinks are cheap, and the crowd is mostly British, Irish and German package holidaymakers.

For something more pleasant, try the harbourside cafes at **Varadero** in the late afternoon for an early sundowner, or the terrace bars at the Centro Comercial Atlántico opposite Playa Grande. The Canarian rum-based drinks — **ron miel** (honey rum), **mojito canario** with local rum — are worth ordering over the usual San Miguel beer. Many of the resort's restaurants also work as bars in the late evening.
