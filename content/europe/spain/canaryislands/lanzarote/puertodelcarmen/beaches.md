---
title: Beaches
type: section
---

Puerto del Carmen has three main beaches strung along its six-kilometre seafront. **Playa Grande** in the centre is the busiest, with the calmest water and the most facilities — sunbeds, restaurants, lifeguards. **Playa de los Pocillos** to the west is longer and breezier, popular with windsurfers. **Playa Matagorda**, further west still and right under the airport flight path, is the quietest of the three.

All three are golden imported sand and gently shelving, with water that holds 19–24°C year-round. For a more dramatic Lanzarote beach experience, drive 35 minutes south to the wild dunes and turquoise water of **Playa de Papagayo** near Playa Blanca, or 25 minutes north to **Playa Famara** for the long surf beach below the Famara cliffs.
