Casa Museo Jose Saramago preserves the Lanzarote home where the Portuguese Nobel laureate spent his later years. The rooms, library, garden, and writing spaces make the visit feel personal, especially if you know his novels or want a quieter cultural break from the resort coast.

The house is in Tias, just inland from Puerto del Carmen, so it is best reached by taxi, bus, or a short drive. Book or check tour arrangements before going, as small literary house museums often work on set visiting hours rather than continuous flow.
