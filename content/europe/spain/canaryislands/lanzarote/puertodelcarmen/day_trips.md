Puerto del Carmen is well-placed for exploring the rest of Lanzarote. The single must-do day is **Timanfaya National Park** — the surreal, still-warm volcanic landscape created by the 1730s eruptions — best combined with the **La Geria** wine country en route and lunch at the bodega-restaurant El Chupadero or César Manrique's own El Diablo. To the north, **Jameos del Agua** and **Cueva de los Verdes** (both Manrique landmarks built inside lava tunnels) and the [Mirador del Río](/europe/spain/canaryislands/lanzarote/miradordelrio) viewpoint can be done as a single long day.

The capital [Arrecife](/europe/spain/canaryislands/lanzarote/arrecife) is a 15-minute drive and worth half a day for the Castillo de San José (now César Manrique's modern art museum), the seafront and the small old town. The Sunday market at [Teguise](/europe/spain/canaryislands/lanzarote/teguise), the old capital, is the biggest weekly market in the Canaries and worth timing a visit around. For an island hop, ferries run several times daily from [Playa Blanca](/europe/spain/canaryislands/lanzarote/playa_blanca) (40 minutes south) to **Fuerteventura**'s Corralejo.
