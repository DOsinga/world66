Playa Matagorda is the westernmost of Puerto del Carmen's three main beaches and the closest to the airport — a 700-metre stretch of sand with the lava-rock low cliffs of Lanzarote behind, and the runway end (with planes lifting off directly overhead) to the west. It is by far the quietest of the three beaches, with no hotels lining the promenade and a much smaller crowd, but the trade-off is that the wind is also strongest here.

Surfers and bodyboarders favour the eastern end, which has more reliable breaks; the western end is calmer for swimming. A small string of cafés along the promenade serves coffee and basic meals. Combine with a walk along the seafront back into central Puerto del Carmen — the full beach-to-beach promenade is one of the more pleasant flat walks on the island.
