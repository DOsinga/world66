Astronomia Lanzarote takes advantage of one of the island's quieter assets: dark skies above a volcanic landscape with relatively little humidity. A guided stargazing session can turn an evening in Teguise or the interior into something more memorable than another restaurant terrace.

The experience depends on weather, moon phase, and guide quality, so book with those details in mind. Bring warm layers even on a warm island; night air in exposed inland locations can feel surprisingly sharp.
