---
title: When to Go
type: section
---

Lanzarote is a year-round destination, with daytime temperatures rarely much below 18°C in winter or much above 30°C in summer. **Teguise** sits inland at modest elevation, so it is a degree or two cooler than the coastal resorts — pleasant in summer, sometimes breezy and chilly on a winter morning.

**Sunday** is the day everyone visits, for the weekly market. If you want to see the village at its busiest and most colourful, come on a Sunday morning. If you want to wander the streets in peace, almost any other day works. **Avoid August**, when the heat and tourist crowds peak together; the spring months of March, April and May are the best of the year.

The big local festival is the **Procession of Nuestra Señora de Guadalupe**, the patron-saint celebration in early September, when the village fills with islanders for several days of music, processions, and folk dancing. It coincides with what is locally called the *Bajada de la Virgen*. Even a single day during the fiesta is memorable.
