Lanzarote's best sights are inseparable from its volcanic landscape. The island rewards a car and an early start: distances are short, but the headline places are spread from the lava fields of [Timanfaya](/europe/spain/canaryislands/lanzarote/timanfaya) to the cliffs around [Mirador del Rio](/europe/spain/canaryislands/lanzarote/miradordelrio).
