Archaeological Museum Benahoritas is one of La Palma's key cultural museums, focused on the island's Indigenous Benahoarite past. Exhibits explain settlement, ceramics, tools, burial practices, rock art, and the volcanic landscape that shaped life before the Spanish conquest.

It is especially useful because many of La Palma's most famous attractions are natural rather than historical. A visit here gives context before exploring caves, ravines, and archaeological sites elsewhere on the island.
