---
title: Getting There
type: section
---

Tenerife South Airport is the main gateway, about 20 minutes away by taxi in normal traffic. TITSA buses connect the airport with Las Américas, Los Cristianos, Costa Adeje, and other south-coast resorts.

The resort is also easy to reach from Los Cristianos on foot, by taxi, or by local bus. If you plan to explore Teide, Anaga, or the north coast, hiring a car for a day or two is more useful than keeping one for the whole stay.
