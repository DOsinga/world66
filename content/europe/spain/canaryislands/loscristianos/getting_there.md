---
title: Getting There
type: section
---

Tenerife South Airport is about 20 minutes away by taxi, and airport buses stop in or near Los Cristianos. Local buses link the town with Las Américas, Costa Adeje, Playa San Juan, Los Gigantes, and Santa Cruz.

The ferry terminal is central. Fred Olsen and Naviera Armas run services from Los Cristianos to La Gomera, and there are also connections to other western Canary Islands depending on the route and season.
