Auditorio de Tenerife is Santiago Calatrava's wave-like concert hall on the Santa Cruz waterfront. Completed in 2003, it has become one of Tenerife's strongest modern landmarks: even without attending a performance, it is worth seeing for the sculptural roofline, harbour setting, and views back toward the city.
