Barranco de Badajoz is a ravine above Güímar with a mix of natural drama, Guanche archaeology, and abandoned industrial remains. The dry riverbed cuts into the hillside, with old mine shafts, aqueduct traces, and ruined structures scattered along the route.

The barranco also has a local reputation for eerie stories, from lost people to supernatural sightings. Treat those as folklore, but go for the landscape and history; access can change after landslides, and the old mine shafts should not be entered.
