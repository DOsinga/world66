The Canarian Military History Museum is housed in Fort Almeyda, a 19th-century fortress built to strengthen the northern side of Santa Cruz de Tenerife after earlier attacks on the coast. Its setting gives the collection more interest than a standard military display.

Inside, the museum covers the island's coastal defences, artillery, uniforms, and the military history of the Canaries. It is most worthwhile for visitors already spending time in Santa Cruz or anyone interested in the city's relationship with the sea.
