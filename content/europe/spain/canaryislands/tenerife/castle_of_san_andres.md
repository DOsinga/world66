The Castle of San Andres is a partly ruined coastal fort built between 1697 and 1706 to defend Tenerife from pirate raids. Its surviving tower stands near the shore at San Andres and is tied to the 1797 Battle of Santa Cruz, when its guns helped damage Admiral Nelson's ship.
