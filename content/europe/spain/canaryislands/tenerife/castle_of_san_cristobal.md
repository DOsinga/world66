The Castle of San Cristobal was Santa Cruz de Tenerife's first major fortification, built in the late 16th century to guard the bay against pirates. Most of the castle was demolished in 1929, but excavated remains are now visible beneath Plaza de Espana through a short underground visit with a small interpretation centre.
