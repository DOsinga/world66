Chinyero is a small volcanic cone on the western slopes of Teide. Its 1909 eruption was the last on Tenerife, leaving lava and ash fields that now form one of the island's more accessible volcanic landscapes.

The cone is modest beside Teide, but it is a worthwhile stop for travellers interested in the island's geology. Walking paths in the area give close views of young lava flows, pine forest recovery, and the rugged terrain shaped by the eruption.
