The Church of Our Lady of the Snows is the old parish church of Taganana, one of the first settlements founded after the Spanish conquest of Tenerife. Its thick masonry walls, red volcanic stone corners, and three-nave interior feel far removed from the resort side of the island.

Come while exploring the Anaga Mountains. The church makes most sense as part of a wander through Taganana's steep lanes and rugged north-coast scenery.
