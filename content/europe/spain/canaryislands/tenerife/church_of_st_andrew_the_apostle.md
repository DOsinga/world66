The Church of St. Andrew the Apostle is one of Tenerife's oldest churches, begun soon after the Spanish conquest of the island. Its modest scale fits San Andres, but the long history gives the village a deeper anchor than the beach alone.

Inside are religious artworks including a nineteenth-century statue of Christ. The bell tower is also part of the local story: the smaller bell is said to be linked to Admiral Nelson's failed attack on Tenerife in 1797.
