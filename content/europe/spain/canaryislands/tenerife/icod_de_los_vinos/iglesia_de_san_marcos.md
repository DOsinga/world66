Iglesia de San Marcos anchors the old centre beside the dragon tree. It began on the site of an earlier chapel and keeps the kind of details that reward a quick look inside: a Renaissance portal, carved wooden ceiling, and a silver-worked Baroque altar.

The church also gives the area around Plaza de San Marcos its shape. Pair it with the dragon tree rather than treating it as a separate outing.
