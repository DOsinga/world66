Museo de Malvasia is a small wine museum in Icod de los Vinos, focused on the sweet Malvasia wines that once made Tenerife famous with European drinkers. The visit is simple: a little local wine history, a garden setting, and the chance to taste what is still made on the island.

It is a pleasant short stop after Parque del Drago or the old centre of Icod, especially if you want a local product that is tied closely to the town's name and history.
