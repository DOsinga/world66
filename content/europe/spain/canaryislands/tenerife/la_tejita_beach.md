La Tejita Beach is one of southern Tenerife's better open beaches, backed by the low volcanic bulk of Montaña Roja. It feels less hemmed in than the resort beaches nearby, with a long sweep of sand and steady Atlantic wind.

That wind makes it popular with windsurfers and kitesurfers, and it hosted world championship competition in 2015. Swimmers should pay attention to conditions, but for space, views, and a wilder edge, it is a strong choice.
