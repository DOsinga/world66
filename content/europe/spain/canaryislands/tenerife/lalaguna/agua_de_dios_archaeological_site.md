The Agua de Dios ravine holds archaeological remains linked to Tenerife's Guanche culture, including caves that were important long before the Spanish conquest. It is not a polished museum-style sight, but it gives a useful glimpse of the island's older history beyond La Laguna's colonial streets.

The site works best for travellers already exploring the Tegueste and La Laguna area. Wear decent shoes and treat the ravine as a fragile archaeological place, not just another viewpoint.
