IES Canarias Cabrera Pinto is a working school in a former convent, a short walk from La Laguna's historic centre. The draw for visitors is the old cloister and the small exhibition rooms, which usually show temporary work by Canarian artists.

It is not a full museum, but it adds a quiet, lived-in stop to a walk through La Laguna's old religious and academic quarter.
