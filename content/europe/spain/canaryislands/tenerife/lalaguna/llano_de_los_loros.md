Llano de los Loros is a small viewpoint in the Anaga highlands above La Laguna. The platform looks south across the Anaga valleys toward Santa Cruz de Tenerife and its port.

It is best treated as a short scenic stop while driving or hiking through Parque Rural de Anaga.
