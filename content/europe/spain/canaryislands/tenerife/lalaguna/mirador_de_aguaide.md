Mirador de Aguaide is a viewpoint in the Anaga side of La Laguna municipality, looking over the broken north coast of Tenerife. The scene is all cliffs, ocean, steep green slopes, and scattered villages, very different from the island's resort south.

It is a strong stop if you are driving or hiking through Anaga from La Laguna. Weather changes quickly here, so cloud can either hide the view or make it more dramatic as it moves through the ravines.
