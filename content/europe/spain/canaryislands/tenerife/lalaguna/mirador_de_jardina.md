Mirador de Jardina is a roadside viewpoint above La Laguna, looking back across the city and toward the ridges of Anaga. On a clear day it gives one of the easiest broad views of the historic town's setting between mountains and the north coast of Tenerife.

The stop is brief, but it is worthwhile if you are driving into Anaga Rural Park or looping through the hills above La Laguna. Weather changes quickly here, so cloud can turn the view into a short atmospheric pause rather than a panorama.
