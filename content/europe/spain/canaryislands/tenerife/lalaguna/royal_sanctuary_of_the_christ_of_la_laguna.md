The Royal Sanctuary of the Christ of La Laguna is one of the city's most important religious sites, home to the Cristo de La Laguna, a deeply venerated image of Christ and a symbol of Tenerife religious life.

Inside, the sanctuary also keeps an exact replica of the Shroud of Turin, one of only two such copies in Spain. It is a quiet but meaningful stop while walking La Laguna's historic centre, especially if you are interested in the island's devotional traditions.
