St. Juan Bautista Cemetery is tied to one of La Laguna's darkest episodes: the 1581 bubonic plague outbreak, when more than 9,000 victims were buried in an improvised cemetery.

It is a sombre, specialist stop, but worthwhile for visitors interested in the city's early history beyond the main churches and squares.
