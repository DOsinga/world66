Mirador de Humboldt looks across the Orotava Valley toward Puerto de la Cruz, with Teide visible on clear days. The viewpoint is named for Alexander von Humboldt, who admired the valley during his 1799 journey through Tenerife.

There is some uncertainty about whether this exact spot is the one Humboldt visited, but the view earns the name. Look inland as well: the slopes above include the Cueva de Bencomo, linked in local tradition with the Guanche king Bencomo.
