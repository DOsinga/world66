Parque Marítimo César Manrique is a saltwater pool complex on the Santa Cruz waterfront, designed in the volcanic-landscape style associated with Lanzarote artist and architect César Manrique. Pools, palms, black rock, waterfalls, and planted terraces soften what could otherwise feel like a plain municipal leisure complex.

It is most useful on a warm day in Santa Cruz, especially for families or anyone wanting a swim without leaving the city. Check current opening before going, as facilities and pool access can vary.
