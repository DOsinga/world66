---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

Puerto de la Cruz has one of the most reliably pleasant climates in Europe year-round, which is exactly why the British have been coming here since the Victorian era. The north coast is greener and slightly cooler than the south — temperatures sit between 18°C and 25°C through most of the year, with very little rain outside of December and January.

**Winter** (December–February) is warm and sunny by northern European standards — typically 18–20°C — and peak season. The town is busy with European visitors escaping the cold. Christmas and New Year are celebrated with processions and fireworks.

**Spring** (March–May) is excellent: warm, uncrowded, and with the Orotava valley at its greenest. The Festival of Corpus Christi (usually June) sees the streets of La Orotava covered in elaborate carpets of flowers and volcanic sand — worth timing a visit around.

**Summer** (June–August) is the hottest period — 24–27°C — and attracts Spanish mainland visitors. The nights are warm and the terraces are lively.

**Autumn** (September–November) is warm and quieter, with calmer seas for boat trips and whale watching. October and November occasionally bring Atlantic storms and heavy rain.
