Red Mountain Natural Reserve protects the red volcanic cone, dunes, beaches, and wetland habitats beside El Medano. It is one of the south coast's better easy nature walks, with open views over the Atlantic and a landscape that feels much wilder than the nearby resort strips.

The reserve is good for a short hike, birdwatching, or a quiet beach stop at La Tejita. It is exposed and dry, so bring water and sun protection even for a quick loop.
