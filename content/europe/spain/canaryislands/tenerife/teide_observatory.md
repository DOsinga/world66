Teide Observatory sits high on Mount Teide at about 2,390 metres, where clear skies and thin air have made Tenerife one of Europe's most important astronomy bases. The complex is primarily a working research site, but guided visits open parts of it to travellers interested in astronomy and the island's scientific landscape.
