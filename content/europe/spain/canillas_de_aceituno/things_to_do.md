---
title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Canillas de Aceituno is primarily a base for walking in the **Sierra de Tejeda Natural Park**. The village sits at the foot of La Maroma, the highest peak in Málaga province at 2,069 metres, and trails of varying difficulty fan out from the village edge into the limestone mountains above. The path to the summit is demanding but rewards on clear days with views that extend across the coast to North Africa.

Below the peak, the sierra is carved by the **Tejeda Gorge** (Barranco de los Cazadores), a dramatic limestone canyon where the trail follows the stream through dense vegetation and occasional scrambling sections. The gorge walk is one of the best in the Axarquía.

In the village itself, the **Iglesia de San Antonio de Padua** is worth a look — a whitewashed church with a simple Mudejar-influenced tower that anchors the main square. The surrounding network of white villages — **Sedella**, **Salares**, and **Arenas** — are each within a short drive and make for pleasant afternoon detours.
