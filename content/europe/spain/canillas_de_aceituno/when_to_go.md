---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

Spring (March to May) is the best time to visit Canillas de Aceituno. The almond trees blossom from February, the wildflowers are at their peak, and the streams in the gorge are flowing. Temperatures are mild and comfortable for walking — typically 15–20°C in the village, cooler on the sierra.

Summer is hot and dry, with temperatures in the thirties. The gorge walk is still feasible early in the morning but the summit trail is punishing in the midday heat. Autumn brings cooler temperatures and occasional rain, which refreshes the landscape. Winter is quiet — the village largely empties of visitors — but clear winter days are spectacular, and La Maroma summit may be covered in snow.

The Christmas and Easter periods see more activity, with Spanish visitors from the coast coming up to the hills.
