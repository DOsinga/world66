title: Things to Do
type: section
---

Comillas punches well above its weight architecturally. Three major monuments — El Capricho, the Pontifical University, and the Sobrellano Palace — sit within easy walking distance and together form one of the most remarkable concentrations of late 19th-century architecture anywhere in Spain. Beyond the grand buildings, the town has a beautiful beach and a compact old centre worth wandering.
