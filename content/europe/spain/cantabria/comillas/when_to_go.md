---
title: When to Go
type: section
---

The Cantabrian coast is greener and wetter than the Mediterranean — this is Atlantic Spain, and rain can arrive any time of year. Summer (July–August) is the driest and warmest period, with temperatures reaching the mid-20s Celsius, but the town fills with Spanish holidaymakers and prices rise accordingly. The beach can get crowded on good-weather weekends.

Spring and early autumn are ideal: mild temperatures, fewer crowds, and the landscape at its most vivid green. The architectural monuments are open year-round (El Capricho has reduced hours in winter). May and September are particularly good months for combining architecture, walking, and the coast without the summer crush.
