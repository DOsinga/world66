---
title: "Bars and Cafes"
type: section
---

The bar scene in Santander is dense and lively, particularly in the Cañadío neighbourhood and along the Paseo de Pereda by the waterfront. The local tradition of an evening pintxos crawl — stopping at several bars in succession, each for one small bite and a glass of wine or cider — makes it easy to cover a lot of ground without settling into one place.
