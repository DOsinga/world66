Benicàssim is a small coastal town on the Costa del Azahar — the Coast of Orange Blossoms — between [Valencia](/europe/spain/valencia) and Castellón. Unlike the more heavily developed costas further south, this stretch of coast remains predominantly Spanish in character, with far fewer foreign tourists and a more authentic atmosphere.

The town has [good beaches](/europe/spain/castellon/benicssim/beaches) and sits at the foot of the [Desert de les Palmes](/europe/spain/castellon/benicssim/desert_de_les_palmes), a natural park of forested hills that offers excellent walking with views over the coast. Each July, Benicàssim hosts the [FIB festival](/europe/spain/castellon/benicssim/festival_internacional), one of Spain's biggest music events, which briefly transforms this quiet resort into an international party. The surrounding area rewards exploration: [Peñíscola](/europe/spain/castellon/peniscola) to the north has a dramatic clifftop castle, [Morella](/europe/spain/castellon/morella) is a striking medieval walled town with a UNESCO-listed monastery, and [Valencia](/europe/spain/valencia) is about an hour south by train.
