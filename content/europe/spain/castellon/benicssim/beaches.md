---
title: Beaches
type: section
---

Benicàssim's beaches run for about six kilometres along the coast, divided into four named stretches that are all easy to walk between along a paved promenade. They are sandy, gently shelving, and were among the first beaches in Castelló province to be awarded a Blue Flag for water quality.

**Playa de Voramar** in the north is the smaller, prettier beach, with a nineteenth-century bathing hotel still in operation behind it and old villas of the Belle Époque hidden among the pines. **Playa Almadraba** is a quieter family beach with shallow water. **Playa Heliópolis** is the longest and broadest, with the most amenities. **Playa de Torre San Vicente** in the south is named for the 16th-century watchtower that still stands on the sand, a relic of the days when the coast was raided by Barbary pirates.
