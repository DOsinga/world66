The most rewarding day trips from Benicàssim run north and inland. **Peñíscola**, half an hour up the coast, has a spectacular medieval castle rising on a rocky promontory above a whitewashed old town, with a small but excellent beach below. **Morella**, an hour and a half inland, is one of Spain's finest preserved walled hill towns and the seat of an extraordinary clifftop castle. Closer to home, the regional capital **Castelló de la Plana** is 15 minutes by train and worth half a day. South, **Valencia** is around 90 minutes away and an easy day out by train.
