---
title: Getting There
type: section
---

Benicàssim is on the **AP-7** coastal motorway and the **N-340**, with frequent exits to the town. The **Renfe** suburban line (C-6) links it to Castelló de la Plana in 15 minutes and Valencia in around 90; long-distance trains do not stop here, so for Madrid or Barcelona the easiest option is to connect at Castelló.

The nearest international airport is **Valencia (VLC)** about 90 minutes south by car or train. **Castelló airport (CDT)** is closer but has very few scheduled flights. **Barcelona-El Prat (BCN)** is around three hours by motorway.
