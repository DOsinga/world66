type: section
---

Benicàssim's two great draws are very different: a six-kilometre run of sandy beaches with good Mediterranean swimming, and the steep, scrubby hills of the **Desert de les Palmes** behind town, with monasteries, hermitages, and walking trails through pine forest. In summer the FIB festival turns the town into one of Spain's biggest pop-music venues for a week.
