Fuente de los Baños is the main reason to visit Montanejos. The spring water holds around 25C through the year and runs into a clear bathing area framed by limestone walls, with fish in the shallows and deeper pockets near the rock.

It can be peaceful outside peak season and busy in summer, when access controls may apply. Come early, travel light, and treat it as a natural river site rather than a swimming pool.
