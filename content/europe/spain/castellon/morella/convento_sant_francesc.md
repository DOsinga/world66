The Convent of Sant Francesc was founded in the late 13th century and is the largest medieval complex in Morella after the castle. The Gothic cloister — with its elegant arches and carved capitals — is the highlight, and functions as an event space and exhibition venue. The church is used for concerts during the Sexenni festival.

The convent is less visited than the basilica and the castle, which makes it more pleasurable. The cloister garden in summer, shaded by the Gothic arches, is one of the most peaceful spots in the town.
