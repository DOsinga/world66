---
title: When to Go
type: section
---

Peñíscola has a classic Mediterranean climate — hot, dry summers and mild winters. The old town and castle are worth visiting year-round, but the experience varies enormously by season.

**July and August** are extremely busy. The north beach fills up, the old town gets crowded by mid-morning, and prices rise sharply. If you are coming primarily for the beach and don't mind the crowds, this is fine. If you want to explore the castle and old town without queuing and jostling, avoid it.

**June and September** are the sweet spots. The sea is warm, the weather reliable, and the crowds manageable. Most facilities are open and prices are lower.

**April, May, and October** are good for sightseeing. The old town is quiet, the light is excellent for photography, and you can walk the walls in peace. The sea is cool but swimmable by May for anyone not fussy about water temperature.

**Winter** (November to March) is quiet to the point of deserted. Many restaurants and shops close or reduce hours. The castle and old town are open and worth visiting on a clear day — there's something striking about having the whole rock to yourself.
