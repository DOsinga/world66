Castile and León is Spain's largest region, a high tableland of golden-stone cities, Gothic cathedrals, and Romanesque churches strung along the Camino de Santiago. The university city of [Salamanca](/europe/spain/castile_and_leon/salamanca), the Roman aqueduct of [Segovia](/europe/spain/castile_and_leon/segovia), and the cathedrals of [Burgos](/europe/spain/castile_and_leon/burgos) and [León](/europe/spain/castile_and_leon/leon) are among the finest historic sights in the country.
