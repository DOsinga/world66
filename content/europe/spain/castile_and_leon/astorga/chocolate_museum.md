Astorga has a surprisingly deep chocolate tradition, and this small museum explains how cacao, trade, religious orders, and local workshops made the city a confectionery centre. Displays cover molds, labels, machinery, advertising, and the domestic culture around drinking chocolate.

It is a light but worthwhile stop between Astorga's cathedral, Roman remains, and Gaudí palace. The museum works best for visitors interested in food history or looking for a distinctively local indoor attraction.
