---

El Bierzo cooking belongs to its own world, distinct from both Castilian and Galician. The signature dish is **botillo**, a smoked sausage of pork ribs, tail and tongue, boiled slowly and served with cabbage, chickpeas and potatoes. It is heavy winter food but eaten across the seasons; ask for it at any traditional restaurant in Villafranca or Cacabelos.

The other regional specialities are **pimientos del Bierzo** — broad, sweet roasted peppers protected by DO — and river trout (trucha al Bierzo, cooked with smoked bacon). Mencía is the wine. **La Moncloa de San Lázaro** in Cacabelos and **Mesón Don Nacho** in Ponferrada both keep traditional menus; book ahead at weekends.
