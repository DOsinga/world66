---
title: Eating Out
type: section
---

Ponferrada's food scene is built around the Bierzo's distinctive regional ingredients: Mencía wine, botillo del Bierzo (a cured meat stew that is the local speciality), pimientos asados (roasted peppers), and chestnuts in autumn. The old quarter has a cluster of restaurants and bars that cater to both pilgrims passing through and locals who eat late and well.
