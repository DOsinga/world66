Santa María de Vizbayo is a small church with a large claim: it is often described as one of the oldest Romanesque churches in El Bierzo. Its scale is modest, but the mix of early forms, stonework, and hillside setting makes it a rewarding detour from Ponferrada's Templar-heavy centre.

Come for quiet architecture rather than spectacle. The church works best for travellers already interested in Romanesque buildings, Camino landscapes, or the older villages around Ponferrada.
