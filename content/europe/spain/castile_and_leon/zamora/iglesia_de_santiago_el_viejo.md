Iglesia de Santiago el Viejo is a small Romanesque church outside Zamora's old walls, traditionally linked to the legend of El Cid being knighted at its altar. The church is modest, but its age, simple stonework, and position near the historic core make it an easy addition to a walk through Zamora's medieval sights.
