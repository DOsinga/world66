---
title: "Eating Out"
type: section
---

Albacete eats like the agricultural heart of La Mancha: heavy on lamb, game, manchego, saffron and the slow stews of the high plain. The signature dishes are **atascaburras** (a salt-cod and potato purée with garlic and walnuts), **gazpacho manchego** (a hot game stew thickened with torn flatbread), and **moje** (a chopped salad with tuna, anchovies and pomegranate). The cheap-and-cheerful **tapas crawl** in the streets around Calle Tesifonte Gallego is the local way to dinner.
