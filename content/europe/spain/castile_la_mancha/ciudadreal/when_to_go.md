---
title: When to Go
type: section
---

La Mancha is one of the most extreme corners of Spain weather-wise — fierce summers, sharp winters, very little rain. The best months for a visit are **April to early June** and **September to early November**, when the days are warm rather than scalding and the plains are at their greenest in spring or their gold-and-red harvest colours in autumn.

July and August bring temperatures regularly above 35°C and the city feels emptied out, though hotels are cheap. Winter is cold, with frosts at night, but bright skies and almost no crowds — and the perfect time to eat a heavy game stew.

**Semana Santa** (Holy Week) sees solemn processions through the old town, and the **Pandorga de Ciudad Real** in late July is the main local festival, in honour of the city's patron the Virgen del Prado.
