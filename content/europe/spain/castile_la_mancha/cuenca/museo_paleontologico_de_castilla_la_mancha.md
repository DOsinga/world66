Museo Paleontologico de Castilla-La-Mancha, often shortened to MUPA, is a strong modern museum devoted to fossils and prehistoric life from the region. Dinosaurs are the crowd-pleaser, but the museum also helps explain the landscapes and geology around Cuenca.

Its hilltop setting adds good views back toward the city, so it is more than a rainy-day fallback. It is especially worthwhile for families or anyone who wants a change from Cuenca's medieval streets and hanging houses.
