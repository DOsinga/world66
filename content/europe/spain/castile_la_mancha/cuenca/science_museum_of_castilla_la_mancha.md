The Science Museum of Castilla-la Mancha is a family-friendly museum in Cuenca with hands-on science displays and a planetarium. It makes a useful change of pace from the old town's churches, bridges, and hanging houses.

It is most appealing for families, but the planetarium and regional science exhibits can also work well on a rainy or very hot day.
