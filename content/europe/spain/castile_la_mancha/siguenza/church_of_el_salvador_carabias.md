The Church of El Salvador in Carabias is a small Romanesque church in the countryside west of Sigüenza. Its open arcaded porch is the feature to look for, a graceful rural version of the stone galleries found across parts of Castile.

It is a quiet detour rather than a major sight, but it suits Sigüenza well. The pleasure here is in linking the town to the villages around it, where medieval churches and castles still sit in landscapes that tourism has barely altered.
