---
title: 'Eating Out'
type: section
---

Tarancón's location on the Madrid–Valencia highway has supported a long tradition of *casas de comidas* and roadhouse-style restaurants — usually serving good versions of inland Castilian classics: roast lamb (*cordero asado*), *gachas manchegas*, *morteruelo*, *pisto*, *queso manchego* with Tarancón's local wine. The town does not have a celebrated dining scene, but several of the places on the main street are reliable for a hearty Castilian lunch.
