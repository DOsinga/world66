Iglesia de los Jesuitas stands near the highest point of Toledo, and the climb up its twin towers is the main reason to come. From the top you get a clear view over the dense old city, the cathedral, and the countryside beyond the Tagus.

The church itself is worth a look, but it is the panorama that makes it stand out among Toledo's many religious buildings.
