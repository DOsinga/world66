Argentona is a small town in the Maresme comarca, 33 km northeast of [Barcelona](/europe/spain/catalonia/barcelona) and just 4 km inland from the coastal town of Mataró. It is a quiet, leafy place that makes a good escape from the city — close enough for a day trip, but with the unhurried feel of a Catalan country town.

The town has a notable collection of Modernista architecture, including the [Casa Garriga i Nogués](/europe/spain/catalonia/argentona/casa_garriga_nogues) and several other early 20th-century summer villas by Josep Puig i Cadafalch. The [Font Picant](/europe/spain/catalonia/argentona/font_picant) mineral spring has drawn visitors since the 19th century, and the [Museu del Càntir](/europe/spain/catalonia/argentona/museu_del_cantir) — a whole museum given over to the Catalan ceramic water jug — is the unmissable curiosity. The surrounding [Serralada Litoral hills](/europe/spain/catalonia/argentona/serra_marina) offer pleasant walks through pine forest and Mediterranean scrub, with views down to the coast.
