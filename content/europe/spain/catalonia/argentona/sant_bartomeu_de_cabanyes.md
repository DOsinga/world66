Sant Bartomeu de Cabanyes is a simple Romanesque church in the hills west of Argentona. The small 11th- to 13th-century building has a barrel-vaulted nave and a semicircular apse with a narrow alabaster window.

It is a modest but worthwhile historic stop if you are walking or driving through the Serralada Litoral.
