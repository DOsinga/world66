---
title: When to Go
type: section
---

Banyoles has a mild Catalan climate, warm and dry in summer, cool in winter. Spring and autumn are the best times to visit for lake walks and outdoor activities — temperatures are comfortable and the light on the water in the early morning is at its best.

Summer (July–August) brings more visitors and more water activity on the lake, though Banyoles is never overcrowded by Catalan standards. The rowing clubs are at their most active in spring and early summer.

The town is an easy day trip from [Girona](/europe/spain/catalonia/girona) year-round, and the drive or cycle through the Pla de l'Estany countryside is pleasant in any season.
