The Archaeology Museum of Catalonia on Montjuïc covers Catalonia's ancient past through finds from prehistory, the Iberian period, Greek and Roman settlement, and the early medieval period.

It is a good counterpoint to Montjuïc's art museums and works well with nearby stops such as the Fundació Joan Miró, the Magic Fountain area, and the hill's gardens.
