Robert Hughes wrote this cultural history of Barcelona in 1992, the year of the Olympics, and it remains the definitive account of the city's architecture, art, politics, and identity. Hughes was an exceptional art critic and his account of Gaudí, Modernisme, and Catalan nationalism is both scholarly and compulsively readable. His chapters on the Civil War and on the meaning of Picasso's time in Barcelona are excellent. A long book, but one that rewards the investment: you will see the city differently after reading it.
