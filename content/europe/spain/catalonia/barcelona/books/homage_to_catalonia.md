Orwell volunteered for the Republican militia in 1936 and was wounded at the Aragon front. His memoir of those months — the chaos of revolutionary Barcelona, the street fighting between rival left-wing factions on Las Ramblas, the sense of a city briefly transformed — is one of the great pieces of political writing in English. Walking the Ramblas after reading this gives the promenade an entirely different weight.
