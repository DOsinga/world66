Orwell's account of fighting on the Republican side in the Spanish Civil War, including the street fighting in Barcelona in May 1937 when Communist and Anarchist factions turned on each other. He was shot through the throat by a sniper at the front and returned to Barcelona to find the political situation had collapsed into terror. One of the most honest and disillusioned war books ever written, and essential for understanding the city's complicated relationship with its own history.
