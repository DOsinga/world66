Published in 1944 and winner of the inaugural Premio Nadal, this short novel follows a young woman arriving in postwar Barcelona to study at the university, moving into her grandmother's decaying apartment on Calle de Aribau. The city she finds is grey, hungry, and claustrophobic — a portrait of Barcelona under Franco that remains startlingly vivid. Laforet was 23 when she wrote it.
