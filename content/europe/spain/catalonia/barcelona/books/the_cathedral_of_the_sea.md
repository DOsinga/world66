A sweeping historical novel set in 14th-century Barcelona, centred on the building of the Santa Maria del Mar church in the Born neighbourhood. The church is still there — a beautiful Gothic building of unusual austerity — and the novel makes its construction feel immediate: the slave labour, the guild politics, the social hierarchies of medieval Catalonia. A long book, worth it.
