A sweeping historical novel set in 14th-century Barcelona, centred on the building of the Santa Maria del Mar church by the people of the Ribera district — ordinary citizens who carried the stone themselves, block by block, from a quarry outside the city. It is a rich portrait of medieval life in what is now the Born neighbourhood, with its guilds, inquisitors, and the grinding hierarchies of feudal Catalonia.
