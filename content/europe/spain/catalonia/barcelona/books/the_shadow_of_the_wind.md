Set in Barcelona in the years after the Spanish Civil War, this novel follows a young boy who discovers a mysterious book in the Cemetery of Forgotten Books, a secret library hidden in the labyrinth of the old city. The Gothic Quarter comes alive in these pages — its winding streets, hidden courtyards, and layers of history both architectural and political. It is the essential Barcelona novel, the book that gives the old city its atmosphere before you have seen it in person.
