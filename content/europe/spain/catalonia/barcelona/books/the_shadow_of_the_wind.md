The essential Barcelona novel, and one of the best-selling Spanish novels of the twentieth century. Set in the years after the Spanish Civil War, it follows a young boy who discovers a mysterious book in the Cemetery of Forgotten Books — a secret library hidden in the Gothic Quarter. The novel weaves through the city's winding streets, from the Raval to the Eixample, with Barcelona's postwar grief and beauty present on every page. If you read one book before visiting, make it this one.
