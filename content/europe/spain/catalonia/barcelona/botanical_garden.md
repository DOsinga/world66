The Botanical Garden of Barcelona spreads across Montjuïc with Mediterranean-climate plants from Catalonia, California, Chile, South Africa, Australia, and the Mediterranean basin. The current garden opened in 1999, though Barcelona had an earlier botanical garden from the late 19th century.

It is a quiet stop for plant lovers and a good change of pace while exploring Montjuïc's museums, Olympic sites, and viewpoints.
