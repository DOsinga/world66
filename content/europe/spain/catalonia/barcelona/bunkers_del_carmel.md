The ruins of Civil War anti-aircraft batteries on the hill above El Carmel, with an unobstructed 360-degree view of the city, the sea, and the mountains behind. Not on most tourist maps, which is precisely why it's popular with locals. At sunset, half of Barcelona seems to come up here with wine and a blanket. No facilities, no entry charge, and a steep walk from the nearest bus stop — all of which keeps it honest.
