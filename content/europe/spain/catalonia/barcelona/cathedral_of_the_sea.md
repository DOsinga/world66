A sweeping historical novel set in 14th-century Barcelona, centred on the building of the Santa Maria del Mar church in the Born quarter. Falcones's novel follows Arnau Estanyol from slave porter to wealthy merchant against a backdrop of plague, Inquisition, and civic upheaval. The descriptions of medieval Barcelona — its guilds, its markets, the division between patricians and workmen — bring the city's oldest layers to life in ways that a history book cannot. The church itself still stands and is one of the most beautiful Gothic buildings in Spain.
