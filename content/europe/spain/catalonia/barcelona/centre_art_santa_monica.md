Centre d'Art Santa Mònica is a contemporary art centre at the lower end of La Rambla. It hosts temporary exhibitions, installations, talks, and other cultural programming by Catalan, Spanish, and international artists.

Because the programme changes often, it is worth checking what is on before making a special trip. The location makes it an easy cultural stop while moving between the Gothic Quarter, the waterfront, and Montjuïc.
