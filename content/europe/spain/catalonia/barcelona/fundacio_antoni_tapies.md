Fundació Antoni Tàpies is devoted to the Catalan artist Antoni Tàpies and to modern and contemporary art more broadly. The museum sits in a striking modernista building by Lluís Domènech i Montaner, topped by Tàpies' own wiry rooftop sculpture.

It is a strong stop if you want Barcelona's art scene beyond Picasso, Miró, and Gaudí.
