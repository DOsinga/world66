The Hash Marijuana & Hemp Museum occupies the modernist Palau Mornau near the Gothic Quarter. Its permanent exhibition looks at the cannabis plant as an industrial, medicinal, ritual, and recreational material, making it one of Barcelona's more unusual small museums.
