Orwell came to Barcelona in December 1936 to report on the Civil War and ended up fighting for six months on the Aragon front. His account of the revolutionary atmosphere of 1930s Barcelona — the workers' militias, the anarchist committees, the posters exhorting everyone to treat one another as equals — is still the most vivid description of a city in the grip of genuine social transformation. The second half, dealing with the communist suppression of the POUM and Orwell's narrow escape, is among the finest political writing in English.
