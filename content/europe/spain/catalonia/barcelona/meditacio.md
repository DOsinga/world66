Noucentisme was the cultural movement that dominated Catalan intellectual and artistic life from roughly 1906 to 1923, consciously positioning itself against the decorative excess of Modernisme and in favour of classical order, Mediterranean rootedness, and what its theorists called *seny* — a kind of rational, measured good sense that they considered characteristically Catalan. Meditation — not religious, not ecstatic, but composed and clear-eyed — was central to the Noucentista ideal of how a cultivated person should inhabit the world.

A sculpture called *Meditació* is therefore making a philosophical argument as much as an artistic one. The seated, inward-focused figure was a favourite form for Noucentista sculptors: it demonstrated that repose could be as powerful as action, that the life of the mind was worth representing in public space alongside the life of commerce and politics.

The area near the Barri Gòtic where this piece stands was substantially rebuilt in the twentieth century — the "Gothic" Quarter contains as much 1930s reconstruction as genuine medieval fabric. The meditation the figure offers is perhaps one of the more useful activities available in this context: pausing to look carefully at what is actually old and what has been made to appear old.
