Miramar Gardens sit on the lower slopes of Montjuic, close to the harbour cable car station. They are at their best in spring, when the roses and fountains soften the stone terraces, but the real reason to come is the view. From the Mirador Miramar you look across the port, the old city, and the sweep of Barcelona below.
