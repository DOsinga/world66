The Natural Sciences Museum of Barcelona explores the history of the Earth, the evolution of life, and the relationship between people and the natural world. Its permanent exhibitions make it one of the stronger family-friendly museum stops in the Sant Marti area.

It is especially useful if you are already visiting the Forum waterfront or want a quieter science-focused break from the city's art and architecture circuit.
