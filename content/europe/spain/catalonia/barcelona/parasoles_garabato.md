The Forum district was created from scratch on reclaimed industrial and sewage-treatment land for the Universal Forum of Cultures in 2004, a large-scale international event that Barcelona organised partly to demonstrate it could still pull off something as ambitious as the 1992 Olympics.

*Parasoles garabato* — scribble parasols — were installed as part of the public art programme that accompanied the Forum, intended to give the new district some identity before the buildings around it had acquired any history. The irregular, hand-drawn forms were meant to contrast with the strict geometry of the Forum's main esplanade.

Whether the Forum district has achieved genuine urban life since 2004 is a question Barcelonans debate. The parasols remain, marking an area that is still in the process of becoming something.
