The Parc de Diagonal Mar was designed by Enric Miralles and Benedetta Tagliabue and opened in 2002, two years after Miralles's death. The park covers approximately 14 hectares at the point where the Avinguda Diagonal meets the sea — the easternmost extension of the Diagonal, which was extended to the coast in 2004 as part of the Diagonal Mar development.

The park design rejected the conventional approach of a manicured green space. Instead, Miralles designed an elevated walkway system — concrete paths elevated 2-3 metres above ground level, supported on organic-form columns — that winds through the park above planted zones and water features. The columns have folded, branch-like forms; the elevated paths create a different relationship to the ground below them. The design was controversial: critics found the elevated paths alienating and the maintenance costs excessive.

The park occupies land that was, until the late 20th century, the industrial outskirts of Poblenou — factories, warehouses, and port facilities. The entire Diagonal Mar development — the shopping centre, the apartment towers, the park — was built on this industrial land as a post-industrial replacement. The area was also adjacent to the Camp de la Bota execution ground, a fact that was not fully acknowledged in the development's promotional materials. The park sits at the intersection of Barcelona's darkest 20th-century history and its most ambitious early 21st-century urban development.
