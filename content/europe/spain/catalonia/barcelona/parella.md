*Parella* — Couple — is a large bronze sculpture by the Catalan artist Alfons Serrahima, installed on the Plaça de la Barceloneta in 1992 as part of the waterfront regeneration that accompanied the Olympic Games. The placement at the edge of the old fishermen's neighbourhood, looking out toward the sea, was deliberate.

The Barceloneta waterfront had been closed to the public since the industrial port expansion of the nineteenth century. The 1992 Olympics gave the city the political and financial occasion to tear down the factories and warehouses that blocked access, create a kilometre of public beach, and build the Olympic Village on reclaimed industrial land to the north.

*Parella* stands at the point where Barceloneta — the oldest continuously inhabited working waterfront district in the city — meets the new Olympic beach. It marks a boundary between an old Barcelona that faced away from the sea it lived from, and a new one that turned back toward it.
