Plaça Sant Felip Neri is a small, enclosed square in the Gothic Quarter with a Baroque church, a fountain, and a quiet atmosphere that feels far removed from the busier lanes nearby. Its beauty is tied to a darker history: the square stands over an old cemetery and still carries scars from the Spanish Civil War.

The square is reached through narrow streets near the cathedral cloister. It is best visited slowly and respectfully, as both a historic site and one of the old city's most atmospheric corners.
