The Sagrada Família is the most visited monument in Spain and one of the most extraordinary buildings in the world — a Gothic-Modernist basilica designed by Antoni Gaudí that has been under continuous construction since 1882. Gaudí took over the project in 1883 and worked on it until his death in 1926, leaving it roughly a quarter complete. Construction continues today and is expected to finish sometime in the 2030s.

The exterior is staggering. The Nativity facade (the older, original side) is encrusted with stone figures and organic forms; the Passion facade, more angular and austere, was finished later. Inside, the forest of branching stone columns and the kaleidoscopic light through the stained glass create an effect unlike any other building you are likely to enter.

Book tickets well in advance — this is the most visited site in Spain and same-day entry is rarely available. The tower lifts are worth the extra cost for views across the city.
