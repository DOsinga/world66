The essential Barcelona novel. Set in the years after the Spanish Civil War, it follows a young boy who discovers a mysterious book in the Cemetery of Forgotten Books, a secret library hidden in the heart of the old city. The Gothic Quarter comes alive in these pages, its winding streets and hidden courtyards rendered with an affection that is impossible to fake. Ruiz Zafón spent years living in Los Angeles, and his nostalgia for Barcelona gives the book a particular emotional intensity. If you read one novel before visiting, make it this one.
