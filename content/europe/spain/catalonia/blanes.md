Blanes marks the southern gateway to the Costa Brava, where the rocky coastline and pine-backed coves begin in earnest. It is a working fishing town first and a resort second, which gives it a rougher, more authentic feel than the manicured spots further north.

The old town climbs a hill above the [harbour](/europe/spain/catalonia/blanes/harbour), where fishing boats still land each afternoon and the day's catch goes straight to the restaurants along the front. The [Marimurtra Botanical Garden](/europe/spain/catalonia/blanes/marimurtra), perched on a cliff above the sea, is one of the finest on the Mediterranean coast — 4,000 plant species, clifftop paths, and views straight out to sea. Above the garden, the ruins of [Sant Joan Castle](/europe/spain/catalonia/blanes/sant_joan_castle) occupy the highest point, with panoramas both north and south along the coast.

In late July the town hosts an [international fireworks competition](/europe/spain/catalonia/blanes/fireworks_competition) that draws crowds from across Catalonia. Blanes is also the southern terminus of the GR-92 coastal path, which runs north through the Costa Brava all the way to the French border.
