Sa Forcanera Cove is a small, wild cove below the cliffs near Blanes. It has clear water, pebble beach, old dock remains, and no facilities, which keeps it much quieter than the town beaches.

Access is part of the appeal and the warning: the old path from Blanes is not usable, so visitors normally see it from Marimurtra Botanical Garden or reach it by kayak, swimming, or careful rock-hopping from nearby Santa Anna.
