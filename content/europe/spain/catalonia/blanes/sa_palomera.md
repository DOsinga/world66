Sa Palomera is the rocky outcrop that divides Blanes' waterfront and gives the town its symbolic role as the gateway to the Costa Brava. A short stairway leads up the rock, with views back over the harbour, the long beach of S'Abanell, and the mouth of the Tordera.

It is a quick visit, but it belongs near the top of a Blanes walk because it is so tied to the town's identity. Go early for empty views or at dusk, when the promenade fills and the rock becomes a natural balcony over the bay.
