Santa Clotilde Gardens are formal cliffside gardens laid out in the style of Italian Renaissance gardens. Terraces, clipped greenery, stairways, and sea views make them one of the more polished coastal sights near Blanes.

Come for a short walk rather than a full-day outing. The best reason to visit is the combination of garden design and open views over the Mediterranean.
