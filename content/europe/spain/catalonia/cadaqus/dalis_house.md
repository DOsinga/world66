Dali's House in Portlligat was Salvador Dali's longtime home near Cadaques and is now a museum. The house grew from a group of fishermen's huts into a strange, intimate set of rooms that reflects the artist's life and work on the Costa Brava.

Visits are by timed entry and should be booked in advance, as only small groups are admitted at a time.
