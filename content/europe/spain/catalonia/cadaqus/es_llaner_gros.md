Es Llaner Gros is a small beach area near Cadaqués, close enough to fit into an easy coastal wander from the village. It offers a quieter swimming stop than the busiest central waterfront, with the same rocky Costa Brava character.

It is best appreciated as part of a slow Cadaqués day: walk, swim, dry off, and return toward the whitewashed lanes. Do not expect a broad sandy resort beach; the appeal is compact, local, and scenic.
