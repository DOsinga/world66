Es Llaner Petit is a small beach close to Cadaques, less dramatic than the Cap de Creus coves but much easier to reach from town. It gives you the essentials of the local waterfront: pale stones, clear water, fishing boats, and white houses rising behind the bay.

The beach is best for a quick swim or a quieter pause during a walk around the village edge. It complements nearby Es Llaner Gros rather than replacing the bigger landscape experiences north and east of Cadaques.
