title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Cadaqués is small enough that wandering it is itself the main activity. The whitewashed warren of streets above the bay, the curve of the waterfront with its moored boats, the light on the water in the late afternoon — this is what people come for, and it takes no organisation to enjoy. The Dalí connection gives you a concrete itinerary around the wider peninsula.
