Aiguamolls de l'Emporda is one of Catalonia's best wetland reserves, a flat landscape of lagoons, reed beds, meadows and bird hides between Castello d'Empuries and the sea. It is a welcome change of pace after stone streets and summer traffic.

The reserve is especially rewarding in migration seasons, when storks, herons, ducks and waders pass through. Even outside peak birding months, the walking paths are easy and atmospheric, with views toward the Pyrenees on clear days. Bring binoculars if you have them, and insect repellent if you visit around dusk.
