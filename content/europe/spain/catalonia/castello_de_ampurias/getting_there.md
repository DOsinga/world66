---
title: Getting There
type: section
---

Castello d'Empuries is easiest by car, about 15 minutes from [Figueres](/europe/spain/catalonia/figueres) and a little over an hour from Girona. Buses connect it with Figueres and Roses, but frequencies are not ideal for a rushed day trip.

The nearest useful rail station is Figueres. From there, take a bus or taxi, or combine Castello with Empuriabrava and the Aiguamolls by car.
