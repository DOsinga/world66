Museu del Joguet is Figueres' toy museum, with thousands of toys from different periods and several pieces linked to well-known Spanish figures, including Salvador Dali. It is a lighter museum stop in a city dominated by Dali, and works well for families or anyone interested in everyday design and childhood history.
