La Llar is a reliable, mid-range Catalan restaurant in the old town, popular with locals for its menú del día at lunch. The food is traditional Empordà cooking — fish stews, grilled meat, roast vegetables with romesco — at prices that reflect its local customer base rather than its tourist-adjacent location.

Good place to eat before or after the Dalí museum, and considerably more affordable than the tourist-oriented places clustered near the museum entrance.
