---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

Figueres is busiest in summer, when the Dalí Theatre-Museum is packed and advance booking is essential. If you can visit in spring (April–May) or autumn (September–October), you will find shorter queues, more comfortable temperatures, and a cleaner sight of the Pyrenees to the north. The museum opens year-round, though hours are shorter in winter.

The town's main market is held on Thursday mornings and is a good reason to time your visit — fresh produce, local cheese, and Empordà wines are all worth buying.
