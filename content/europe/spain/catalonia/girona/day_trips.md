Girona sits at the heart of the Empordà and the buses and regional trains make it an easy base for a day out. The most rewarding short trip is to [Besalú](/europe/spain/catalonia/besal), a small medieval town reached by bus in under an hour. You can do a round trip if you check the timetables carefully. Besalú is built around a wonderful fortified Romanesque bridge over the Fluvià, and its compact old centre and former Jewish quarter reward an unhurried wander.

For something more surreal, the [Gala Dalí Castle](/europe/spain/catalonia/girona/gala_dali_castle) at Púbol makes a fine half-day excursion into the Empordà countryside, part of the trio of Dalí sites alongside Figueres and Portlligat.
