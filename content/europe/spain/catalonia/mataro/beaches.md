---
title: Beaches
type: section
---

Mataró's beaches run for several kilometres along the seafront, wide and sandy with calmer crowds than Barcelona's packed strands. The main [Platja de Mataró](/europe/spain/catalonia/mataro/platja_de_mataro) stretches south from the port, backed by a promenade of palm trees and a string of chiringuitos. The water is Mediterranean-calm for most of the summer.

The beaches are Blue Flag certified and well maintained, with showers, lifeguards in season, and sports areas. Unlike Barcelona, you can actually find a patch of sand without arriving at dawn.
