---
title: "Bars and Cafes"
type: section
---

The Passeig d'en Blay, the wide tree-lined boulevard through the centre of Olot, is where the town gathers at the end of the day. Café tables spill out under the plane trees from spring to autumn. For something quieter, the cafés around the Plaça Major and the side streets off Carrer Mulleras are more local.
