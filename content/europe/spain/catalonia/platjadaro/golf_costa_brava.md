Golf Costa Brava is an established course in Santa Cristina d'Aro, a short drive inland from Platja d'Aro's beaches. The setting gives it a different rhythm from the seafront: pine, fairways, and low hills instead of promenades and summer crowds.

It is mainly for golfers, but it can be a useful break if you are staying on the central Costa Brava and want a half day away from the beach. Check tee times and transport before committing, as it is not a casual walk-up attraction in the resort center.
