title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

The beach is the primary draw: [Platja Gran](/europe/spain/catalonia/platjadaro/platja_gran) is the long central sandy beach with all the services, while a string of smaller coves — [Sa Conca](/europe/spain/catalonia/platjadaro/sa_conca), Cala Rovira — offer quieter alternatives a short walk or drive from the resort. Away from the beach, the [Camí de Ronda](/europe/spain/catalonia/platjadaro/cami_de_ronda) coastal walking path connects Platja d'Aro to neighbouring coves and beaches with fine views. Inland, the medieval village of [Castell d'Aro](/europe/spain/catalonia/platjadaro/castell_daro) with its ruined castle is 3 km from the shore and worth the trip.
