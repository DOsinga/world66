---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

Platja d'Aro is primarily a summer resort and functions at its fullest between late June and mid-September. August is the peak month — the beach is packed, restaurants are busy, and prices are at their highest. July and the first half of September are almost as busy but somewhat more comfortable.

Outside the summer peak, the resort is much quieter. May and early June are pleasant for walking — the [Camí de Ronda](/europe/spain/catalonia/platjadaro/cami_de_ronda) is at its best in spring, with wildflowers on the headlands and cool temperatures for hiking. October is mild and the sea remains warm enough for a swim through the month. November through April many businesses close, and the town returns to the local Catalan residents.

The Wine and Cava Festival in May brings Costa Brava producers to the town centre with tastings. The summer beach games run July and August. The October Beer Festival attracts visitors from across the region.
