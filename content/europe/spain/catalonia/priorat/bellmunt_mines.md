Bellmunt del Priorat gives a different side of the comarca: mining rather than wine. The village's old lead mines have been turned into a museum, with underground sections that show how tough and industrial this rural landscape once was.

It is a useful counterweight to the romance of vineyard terraces. Priorat's beauty is obvious, but the mines remind you that people have worked these hills hard in more ways than one. Check opening times before you go, as hours can be seasonal.
