The Cartoixa d'Escaladei is the ruined Carthusian monastery that gave Priorat its name and much of its historical identity. Set below the Montsant cliffs, it has the melancholy grandeur of a place that was once powerful and then abandoned to weather and politics.

Restored sections help you imagine the scale of monastic life here, but the real pleasure is the setting: stone walls, open sky, vineyards and the pale cliffs beyond. It is one of the region's essential stops, especially if you want Priorat to be more than a wine label.
