---
title: Getting There
type: section
---

Priorat is easiest by car from [Reus](/europe/spain/catalonia/reus), Tarragona or Barcelona. Reus is the nearest natural gateway, and Falset is the region's main town for services.

There are rail services to Falset-Marça on the Barcelona-Tarragona-Zaragoza line, but public transport is limited once you are inside the comarca. If you want wineries, villages and viewpoints in one day, rent a car or arrange a driver.
