---
title: When to Go
type: section
---

Spring and autumn are the best seasons for Priorat. Spring brings green vines and wildflowers; autumn brings harvest, cooler walks and the richest colours in the vineyards.

Summer can be very hot in the valleys, especially if you plan to hike or taste wine in several villages. Winter is quiet and atmospheric, though some visitor hours shrink and the hills can feel stark.
