---
title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Reus rewards those who explore its old centre on foot. The [Gaudí Centre](/europe/spain/catalonia/reus/gaudi_centre) is the logical starting point, covering the life and work of the town's most famous son. From there, the [Modernista Route](/europe/spain/catalonia/reus/modernista_route) leads through a remarkable concentration of Art Nouveau buildings, including [Casa Navàs](/europe/spain/catalonia/reus/casa_navas) and the [Institut Pere Mata](/europe/spain/catalonia/reus/institut_pere_mata). The [Plaça del Mercadal](/europe/spain/catalonia/reus/placa_del_mercadal) is the best place to sit and take in the old town's atmosphere, with its arcaded buildings and busy cafe terraces. Reus also has a straight shot to the Roman ruins at [Tarragona](/europe/spain/catalonia/tarragona) and makes a practical base for the [Priorat](/europe/spain/catalonia/priorat) wine country.
