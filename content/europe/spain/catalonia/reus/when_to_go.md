---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

Reus has a Mediterranean climate — hot dry summers and mild, wet winters. Spring (April to June) and autumn (September to October) are the most comfortable times to visit, with temperatures in the 18–25°C range and the old town at its most pleasant for walking.

The biggest annual event is the **Fira de Sant Jordi** on 23 April, when the streets fill with book and rose stalls in the Catalan tradition. The town's Modernista Route is best walked in the cooler hours of the morning. If you are combining Reus with a beach stay on the Costa Daurada, summer is unavoidable but manageable — the town itself is a few degrees cooler than the coast and far less crowded.

The Thursday and Saturday markets in the Plaça del Mercadal run year-round and are worth timing a visit around, as is the weekly market at the Mercat Central. The Priorat wine harvest runs from mid-September to mid-October, making autumn the ideal time for wine-country visits around the region.
