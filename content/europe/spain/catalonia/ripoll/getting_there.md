---
title: "Getting There"
type: section
---

Ripoll is straightforwardly reached by train from [Barcelona](/europe/spain/catalonia/barcelona). The R3 Rodalies line runs from Barcelona Plaça de Catalunya to Ripoll in about an hour and 45 minutes, with trains running several times daily. The station is a short walk from the monastery.

By car, Ripoll is about 100 km north of Barcelona via the C-17 motorway, following the Llobregat and Ter valleys into the Pyrenean foothills. Parking is available near the monastery. From Ripoll, the C-17 continues north to Ribes de Freser and then the French border at Puigcerdà.
