Canyelles Petites is one of the easier beach escapes from the centre of Roses. It is more intimate than the long town beach, with clear water, a curve of sand, and rocky edges that make it good for a swim when the sea is calm.

This is not wilderness; apartments and summer services sit close behind the beach. That convenience is part of the appeal, especially for families or anyone who wants a half-day by the water without driving deep into Cap de Creus. Arrive early in high season, when parking and space disappear quickly.
