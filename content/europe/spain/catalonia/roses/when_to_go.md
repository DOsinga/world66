---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

Roses is a summer resort and is busiest in July and August, when temperatures sit around 28–32°C and the beaches fill up. For warm enough swimming with fewer crowds, June and September are the sweet spot — water still around 22°C, hotel prices well below peak, and most restaurants open.

May and October are pleasant for walking the Cap de Creus and visiting the citadel but on the cool side for swimming. The town quietens substantially from November to March, with many seafront restaurants closed and the tramontana wind regularly howling down off the Pyrenees.
