---
title: "Eating Out"
type: section
---

Sabadell has a good range of local restaurants, most concentrated around the Plaça de Sant Roc and the side streets running south from it. Prices are noticeably lower than in [Barcelona](/europe/spain/catalonia/barcelona) for comparable quality. The Catalan cooking here is unfussy — grilled meats, salt cod in various preparations, good bread with tomato — rather than the showier modern cuisine the city sometimes imports.

The Mercat Central is a starting point for understanding what local ingredients look like. Several small restaurants near the market serve a lunch menu (menú del día) that offers three courses with wine for a fixed price — still the best-value way to eat well in Catalonia.
