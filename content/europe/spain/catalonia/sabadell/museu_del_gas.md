Museu del Gas occupies the former Gas Natural headquarters in central Sabadell. Its collection explains the history of gas supply in Spain, with company archives, technical objects, and displays on how gas changed homes, factories, and city infrastructure.

It is a specialist museum, but a useful one in Sabadell, where industrial history is a big part of the city's identity. Pair it with the nearby history museum or central market.
