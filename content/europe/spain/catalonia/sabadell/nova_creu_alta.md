The Estadi de la Nova Creu Alta is home to CE Sabadell FC, the city's professional football club. The stadium is on the northern edge of the city and is a functional lower-division ground rather than a showpiece — which gives it an authenticity that larger stadiums sometimes lack. Watching a match here puts you in the company of genuine local supporters rather than tourists.

Match schedules vary by season and division. Check the club's website for fixtures if you want to attend a game. The surrounding neighbourhood has the kind of bars and cafes that fill before and after matches.
