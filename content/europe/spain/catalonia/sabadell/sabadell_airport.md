Sabadell Airport is a small general aviation airport on the southern edge of the city, about 3 km from the centre. It handles no commercial passenger flights — it is used by flight schools, private aircraft, aeroclub members, and the regional aerial emergency services. The airport also houses some aviation maintenance operations.

For aviation enthusiasts, the airport is worth a visit: small planes land and take off frequently, and the perimeter fence is accessible for watching operations. The aeroclubs based here sometimes run open days. There is no public terminal to visit as such, but the airfield environment is pleasant and low-key.
