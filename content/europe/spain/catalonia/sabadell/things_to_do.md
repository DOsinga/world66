Sabadell's industrial heritage is the reason to come. The [Vapor Buxeda](/europe/spain/catalonia/sabadell/vapor_buxeda) is the most evocative surviving textile factory, and the [Sabadell Art Museum](/europe/spain/catalonia/sabadell/sabadell_art_museum) and [History Museum](/europe/spain/catalonia/sabadell/sabadell_history_museum) together tell the story of how the wool trade turned this town into one of Catalonia's wealthiest cities. The [Central Market](/europe/spain/catalonia/sabadell/central_market) is worth a visit for the building as much as the produce.

For architecture enthusiasts, [Torre Millenium](/europe/spain/catalonia/sabadell/torre_millenium) marks the city's more recent ambitions, and the [ESDi School of Design](/europe/spain/catalonia/sabadell/esdi_school_of_design) represents its continuing investment in creative industries. Football fans should note that [Nova Creu Alta](/europe/spain/catalonia/sabadell/nova_creu_alta) is home to CE Sabadell FC, one of Catalonia's older clubs.
