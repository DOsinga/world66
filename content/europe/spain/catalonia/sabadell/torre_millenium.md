The Torre Millenium is a contemporary high-rise tower in the western part of Sabadell, visible from much of the city. It houses apartments and offices and does not have a public observation deck or visitor facilities. Its interest is architectural — as the most prominent feature of Sabadell's modern skyline and as evidence of the city's development since the decline of its textile industry.

The surrounding area, known as Can Roqueta, has a mix of modern residential construction and repurposed industrial land. It is not a visitor destination in itself but gives a sense of how the city is developing on the edges of its historic core.
