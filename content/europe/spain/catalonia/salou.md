Salou is the Costa Daurada's biggest beach resort, a purpose-built holiday town on the Catalan coast about 100 km south of [Barcelona](/europe/spain/catalonia/barcelona). It draws a largely British and Northern European crowd with its nine sandy beaches, warm shallow water, and [PortAventura World](/europe/spain/catalonia/salou/portaventura_world) — one of Europe's largest theme park complexes — just outside town.

It is not a place for those seeking authentic Catalan culture — Salou is unabashedly a mass-tourism destination, with seafront hotels, English-language pubs, and neon-lit nightlife strips. But if you want reliable sunshine, clean beaches, and easy entertainment for families or groups of friends, it does its job well. The [Llevant Beach promenade](/europe/spain/catalonia/salou/llevant_beach) is pleasant for an evening stroll, and the Illuminated Fountain puts on a light-and-music show at its end. The [Camí de Ronda](/europe/spain/catalonia/salou/cami_de_ronda) coastal path connects the rocky southern coves for those who want some exercise.

For a change of pace, Tarragona is only 10 km north, with its Roman amphitheatre and well-preserved old quarter. [Barcelona](/europe/spain/catalonia/barcelona) is reachable in under three hours by train, and the mountain monastery of Montserrat makes an excellent day trip.
