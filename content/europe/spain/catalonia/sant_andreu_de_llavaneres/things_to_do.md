---
title: Things to Do in Sant Andreu de Llavaneres
type: section
---

Sant Andreu de Llavaneres is a quiet town built for relaxation. The beach is the main draw in summer, and the forested hillside trails above the town give good views over the coast. The Archive Museum in Can Caralt is the one genuine cultural attraction — a well-presented local history collection in a handsome 18th-century farmhouse.
