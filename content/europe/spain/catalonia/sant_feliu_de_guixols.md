Sant Feliu de Guíxols is a handsome old port town on the Costa Brava, midway between Barcelona and the French border. It has a more lived-in feel than some of its glitzier neighbours — the economy was built on cork and fishing before tourism arrived, and the old town still has working-harbour character.

The [seafront promenade](/europe/spain/catalonia/sant_feliu_de_guixols/passeig_del_mar) is pleasant, the Romanesque [monastery of Sant Feliu](/europe/spain/catalonia/sant_feliu_de_guixols/monestir_sant_feliu) anchors the town centre, and the [coastal path to Tossa de Mar](/europe/spain/catalonia/sant_feliu_de_guixols/cami_de_ronda) is one of the best walks on the Costa Brava. The neighbouring headland of [S'Agaró](/europe/spain/catalonia/sant_feliu_de_guixols/sagaro) has a fine beach and some striking early-20th-century architecture.
