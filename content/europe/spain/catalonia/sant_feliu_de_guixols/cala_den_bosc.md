Cala d'en Bosc is one of the small coves that give the coast around Sant Feliu de Guixols its rugged appeal. It is not a broad resort beach, but a more intimate rocky inlet where the water and pine-covered slopes feel close together.

The cove works best as part of the Cami de Ronda experience rather than as an isolated beach destination. Walk the coast, stop for a swim if conditions are good, and continue toward the next headland or viewpoint.
