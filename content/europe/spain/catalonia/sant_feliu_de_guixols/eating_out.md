---
title: "Eating Out"
type: section
---

Sant Feliu's restaurants concentrate around the Plaça del Mercat and along the seafront promenade. Local specialities lean on the sea — the Costa Brava is famous for its **suquet de peix** (fish stew), **arroz negro** (rice with squid ink), and the regional sausage-and-rice combination **arròs a la cassola**. Catalan favourites — pa amb tomàquet, escalivada, calçots in season — are everywhere.

For an upscale meal try **Sa Marinada** by the marina (modern Catalan, good wine list); for a casual lunch with a view, the seafood places along the Passeig del Mar; for tapas, the small bars around Plaça Espanya. The covered Mercat Municipal on the inland edge of the old town has good fresh produce and is a useful place to assemble a picnic for the Camí de Ronda.
