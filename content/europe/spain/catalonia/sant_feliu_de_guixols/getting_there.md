---
title: "Getting There"
type: section
---

There is no train to Sant Feliu. Coming from Barcelona, the easiest options are the **Sarfa/Moventis bus** from the Estació del Nord (around 1h45, runs every 1–2 hours) or driving (roughly the same time on the AP-7 to Tossa exit and then the coastal road).

The closest train station is **Caldes de Malavella** on the Barcelona–Girona line, with a connecting bus into Sant Feliu (around 30 minutes). For air arrivals, **Girona-Costa Brava Airport** is 25 minutes by car (or 50 minutes by bus); **Barcelona-El Prat** is 90 minutes.

Once in town, everything central is on foot. Buses run regularly up and down the coast — line 21 to Tossa de Mar and beyond — and a small fleet of taxis is on the rank by the Plaça del Mercat.
