Mirador dels Frares is a small but rewarding viewpoint on the rocky edge of Sant Feliu de Guixols. It gives you the Costa Brava in miniature: blue water, hard stone, pines, and a town that drops quickly into coves and headlands.

Use it as part of a coastal walk rather than a destination by itself. The viewpoint pairs well with the Ermita de Sant Elm and the paths around the southern edge of town. Go early or late in hot weather, when the light is better and the exposed sections are less punishing.
