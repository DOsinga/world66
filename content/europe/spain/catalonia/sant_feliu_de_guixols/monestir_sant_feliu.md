The Benedictine monastery of Sant Feliu, founded in the 10th century, anchors the centre of town. The most distinctive part is the **Porta Ferrada** — a three-arched stone narthex from around 998 AD, embedded in the later monastery wall. Behind the portal lies the Romanesque-Gothic church (now used for occasional concerts) and the cloister, parts of which are open to visitors as part of the town's History Museum.

The monastery courtyard hosts the **Festival de Porta Ferrada** each summer, one of Catalonia's oldest music festivals, with concerts in the open-air square in front of the Porta. Even outside festival nights the square is the heart of evening strolling in town.
