A wide arc of golden sand at the head of S'Agaró bay, just over a kilometre north of central Sant Feliu — a much prettier beach than the town beach and a short walk along the seafront. The sand shelves gently into shallow water; the bay is mostly protected from the prevailing easterly winds.

There are a couple of beach restaurants, a couple of small chiringuitos in summer, and chair-and-umbrella rental. The northern end of the beach connects directly to the S'Agaró promenade and Camí de Ronda.
