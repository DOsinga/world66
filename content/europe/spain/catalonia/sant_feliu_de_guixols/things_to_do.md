---
title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Sant Feliu's main appeals are walking, swimming, and the small but excellent local museums. A morning is enough to cover the monastery, the History Museum, and the seafront. Allow an afternoon for the famous **Camí de Ronda** coastal path to Tossa de Mar — twelve kilometres of cliff path, hidden coves, and pine forest, one of the best walks on the Costa Brava. The smaller Camí de Ronda south from Sant Feliu to S'Agaró is gentler and can be done in an hour each way.
