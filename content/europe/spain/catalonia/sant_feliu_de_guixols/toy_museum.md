A small private toy museum just off the seafront with a charming collection of pre-war tin toys, rocking horses, dolls, and Catalan caganers (the traditional nativity-scene figurines that are squatting and… well, doing what their name suggests — a Catalan folk custom). Aimed primarily at families with younger children but quite enjoyable for the rest of us as well.

Open afternoons, small entrance fee. The shop sells reproductions of vintage toys and traditional Catalan toys you can't easily find elsewhere.
