Via Ferrada Cala del Moli is one of the Costa Brava's most memorable active outings: a protected climbing route fixed into sea cliffs just outside Sant Feliu de Guixols. The route uses cables, rungs, and bridges with the Mediterranean directly below.

It is not a casual coastal walk. You need the right equipment, a head for exposure, and either prior via ferrata experience or a qualified guide; with that in place, it is a sharp, salty change from beach-and-promenade tourism.
