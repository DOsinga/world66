---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

The Costa Brava season runs from late April to early October. **May, June, and September** are the sweet spots — warm enough to swim, not too crowded, the Camí de Ronda walkable without melting. **July and August** are hot, busy, expensive, and the small beaches fill up by 10 am.

Time it around the **Festival de Porta Ferrada** (late July to mid-August) for evening concerts in the monastery courtyard — a long-established summer festival drawing jazz, world music, and classical performers, plus occasional theatre.

**Winter** is mild, quiet, and gloriously empty. Many seafront restaurants close from November to March; the town museums stay open.
