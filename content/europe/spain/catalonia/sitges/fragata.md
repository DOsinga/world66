La Fragata occupies a prime corner on the seafront directly below the Sant Bartomeu church, with a glassed-in terrace looking out over Platja de la Ribera. It is one of the longest-established serious restaurants in town and the place for the full Catalan seafront treatment: rice dishes, fideuà, fresh fish from the catch, and a wine list weighted toward Penedès and Priorat.

It is not a bargain — beach-front Sitges never is — but the cooking is consistent and the view from a sunset terrace table is among the best on the coast. Reserve.
