A two-block pedestrian street officially called Carrer 1er de Maig and universally known as "Sin Street", this is where Sitges does its serious drinking. The narrow lane is wall-to-wall bars and clubs, particularly busy with the LGBTQ+ crowd, and on summer weekends it becomes one big open-air party from midnight until dawn.

The neighbouring Carrer del Marquès de Montroig is its slightly more relaxed continuation, with terraced cafe-bars that fill the early evening before things move down toward Sin Street proper. Pace yourself and bring a sense of humour.
