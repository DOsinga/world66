Tarragona's flagship modern restaurant, in a beautifully converted 18th-century townhouse on a quiet street directly behind the cathedral. Chef Quintín Quinsac (the **Q** in AQ; his partner Anna Comprubí is the **A**) cooks ambitious modern Catalan menus — house-cured anchovies, a celebrated dish of **fideuà** with shellfish, smoked aubergine with langoustines, suckling pig with local figs. The wine list runs deep into Catalan and Tarragona DO producers.

The dining room is exposed-stone-and-warm-lighting, the service relaxed and informed, and the kitchen produces some of the best food in southern Catalonia. Bookings essential, often a week ahead. Excellent value compared to similar restaurants in Barcelona.
