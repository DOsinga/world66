story: >
  The phrase "tocar ferro" — to touch iron — is a Catalan expression for warding off bad luck, equivalent to the English "touch wood." At the Balcó, the iron railing at the cliff edge has become the place to do it: local tradition holds that touching the railings here brings good luck, and the iron is worn smooth by a century of palms.
---

The end of the Rambla Nova ends in a small balcony perched on the edge of the limestone cliff, 25 metres directly above the Mediterranean. The view takes in the Roman amphitheatre below, the long sweep of **Platja del Miracle** beach to the east, the port of Tarragona, and — on clear days — the silhouette of the Costa Daurada coast running south towards Salou.

A short, simple, essential stop. Come at sunset when the iron railing fills with locals doing their evening *paseo*, runners, and lovers. The brasseries along the surrounding plaça are a serviceable place for a glass of cava with the view.
