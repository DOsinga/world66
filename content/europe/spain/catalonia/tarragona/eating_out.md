---
title: "Eating Out"
type: section
---

Tarragona eats well — better, on the whole, than Barcelona for the price. The Costa Daurada produces some of the best **fish and shellfish** in Spain, and the city's restaurants take advantage: the daily auction at the port supplies the better kitchens before the market opens. The local speciality is **calçots**, sweet long Catalan spring onions grilled over fire and dipped in **romesco** sauce — a 19th-century Tarragona invention. The season runs January to April.

The old town has the highest concentration of restaurants; the **El Serrallo** fishing quarter on the harbour is the place for the freshest fish.
