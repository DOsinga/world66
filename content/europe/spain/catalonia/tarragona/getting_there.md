---
title: "Getting There"
type: section
---

**Tarragona** is on the high-speed rail line: from Barcelona Sants the AVE takes 30 minutes (to Camp de Tarragona station, 10 km outside the city); regional trains take 90 minutes and arrive at the much more convenient central station on the seafront. From Madrid the AVE is 2.5 hours.

The nearest international airport is **Reus** (15 km west, mostly low-cost European flights). **Barcelona-El Prat** is 90 minutes by road and is the better option for most long-haul arrivals.

By car, Tarragona sits on the AP-7 toll road and the parallel free N-340 between Barcelona and Valencia. Free parking is easy on the western edge of the old town; do not drive into the old town itself.
