Museu del Port explains Tarragona's maritime life, from fishing and dock work to the modern commercial port. Exhibits cover the people, boats, cargo, and industries that shaped the waterfront.

It is a compact museum, best paired with a walk through El Serrallo or along the port rather than treated as a full half-day stop.
