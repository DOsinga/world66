---
title: Getting There
type: section
---

The easiest way to reach Terrassa is on the FGC (Ferrocarrils de la Generalitat de Catalunya) suburban train line S1 from Plaça Catalunya in central [Barcelona](/europe/spain/catalonia/barcelona). Trains run every 10 minutes or so during the day, take about 50 minutes, and arrive at Terrassa Rambla station in the heart of the city. A second route via Sabadell is operated by Rodalies (line R4) and uses Estació del Nord; it is slightly less frequent.

By car, Terrassa is just off the C-58 motorway, about 30 minutes from Barcelona depending on traffic. Parking in the city centre is restricted; use one of the underground car parks. The closest airport is Barcelona-El Prat, about an hour by car or train via Plaça Catalunya.
