---
title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Start in the Vila Vella, where the old walls and towers frame the town's best views. The walk up to the lighthouse is short but steep enough to feel earned, and the lanes inside the walls are better taken slowly than treated as a checklist.

After that, choose your version of Tossa. Platja Gran is the easy town beach, Es Codolar is smaller and more enclosed below the walls, and the coastal paths lead out to quieter coves and cliff views. If the weather turns, the Roman villa gives you a quick, worthwhile dose of history near the centre.
