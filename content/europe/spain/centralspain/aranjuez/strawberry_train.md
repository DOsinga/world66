The Strawberry Train (Tren de la Fresa) is a heritage railway service that runs from Madrid's Atocha Station to Aranjuez on weekends from May to June, using a vintage steam locomotive and restored 19th-century carriages. It's a tourist service rather than a practical transport option, but it's a genuinely enjoyable way to arrive in Aranjuez and adds a layer of historical charm to a day trip.

The journey takes about 50 minutes and includes a brief stop at a station along the way where strawberries — Aranjuez's famous local product — are served to passengers. The service is popular with families and tends to sell out on spring weekends. Book in advance through Renfe's heritage railway programme. The return journey back to Madrid is included in the ticket.
