Ciudad Real is the capital of the province of the same name, set in the flat plains of La Mancha in central Spain — the landscape that inspired Cervantes' Don Quixote. It's a quiet regional city with a well-preserved old centre, surrounded by vineyards that produce some of Spain's most prolific wine output.

The [Cathedral of Santa María del Prado](/europe/spain/centralspain/ciudadreal/cathedral) and the [Puerta de Toledo](/europe/spain/centralspain/ciudadreal/puerta_de_toledo), a 14th-century fortified gate, are the main historical landmarks. The old centre is compact and walkable, anchored by the arcaded [Plaza Mayor](/europe/spain/centralspain/ciudadreal/plaza_mayor). Outside the city, the [windmills of Consuegra](/europe/spain/centralspain/ciudadreal/consuegra_windmills) and Campo de Criptana are the region's most photographed sights, an easy day trip that puts you firmly in Quixote country.

Ciudad Real is also a gateway to the [Tablas de Daimiel National Park](/europe/spain/centralspain/ciudadreal/tablas_de_daimiel), a wetland reserve about 30 km north, where migrating birds stop in large numbers in spring and autumn.
