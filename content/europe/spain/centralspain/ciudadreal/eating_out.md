---
title: Eating Out
type: section
---

La Mancha is straightforward eating country — game, lamb, strong cheese, hearty stews, all washed down with the local Valdepeñas red. Look for **pisto manchego** (a thick ratatouille often topped with a fried egg), **migas** (fried breadcrumbs with chorizo), **gachas** (a pork-and-pea stew thickened with flour) and the famous **Manchego cheese**, ideally aged.

Tapa culture is strong: order a drink in many bars on Calle Palma or around Plaza Mayor and you get a small free plate to go with it.
