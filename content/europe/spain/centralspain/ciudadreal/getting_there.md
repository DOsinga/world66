---
title: Getting There
type: section
---

Ciudad Real sits on the **AVE high-speed line** between Madrid and Seville — the journey from Madrid Atocha takes under an hour, which makes a day trip from the capital genuinely feasible. There are roughly twenty AVE trains a day in each direction.

By road, the A-4 motorway from Madrid takes about two and a half hours by car or by frequent ALSA bus. The city's airport (the famously failed **Ciudad Real Central**) reopened to limited cargo traffic but remains effectively closed to passenger flights. The nearest international airports are Madrid Barajas and Valencia.
