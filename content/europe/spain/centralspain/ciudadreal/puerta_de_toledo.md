The Puerta de Toledo is the most distinctive landmark in Ciudad Real, a horseshoe-arched gate set in a square brick tower in the Mudéjar manner, with two octagonal turrets flanking the entrance. It once stood guard over the road north toward Toledo and Madrid; today it stands island-like in the middle of a paved plaza with traffic flowing around it.

Walk up close and the alternating bands of brick and stone show clearly. The gate is best seen at dusk when the warm lights pick out the surfaces.
