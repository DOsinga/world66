Ciudad Rodrigo is a compact walled city in western Castile, about 90 km southwest of [Salamanca](/europe/spain/castillaylon/salamanca) and close to the Portuguese border. It makes an excellent half-day or day trip from Salamanca, offering genuine medieval atmosphere without the tourist crowds.

The [Cathedral](/europe/spain/centralspain/ciudadrodrigo/ciudad_rodrigo_cathedral), just inside the walls from the bus stop, has beautiful carved column bases in the cloisters and a quiet interior worth spending time in. The real pleasure of the town, though, is simply wandering — counting the stone coats of arms above doorways, following the narrow streets between well-preserved buildings, and sitting in the [Plaza Mayor](/europe/spain/centralspain/ciudadrodrigo/plaza_mayor) with a coffee. The square is not in Salamanca's league, but it has its own unhurried charm.

The well-preserved circuit of [medieval walls](/europe/spain/centralspain/ciudadrodrigo/city_walls) can be walked in under an hour and gives good views over the surrounding countryside and down into the streets below. At the western end, the 14th-century [Castillo de Enrique II](/europe/spain/centralspain/ciudadrodrigo/castillo_enrique_ii) now operates as a parador, its towers rising above the ramparts in a particularly photogenic way.
