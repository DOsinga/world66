A popular bar at the foot of the old city, named after the Torre de Mangana, the watchtower that overlooks the square. Cold beer, cheap wine, and a free tapa with each drink — typical Castilian bar culture. It fills with locals from 7pm onwards and has a genuine neighbourhood feel. Good starting point for a bar crawl through the lower streets of the old town.
