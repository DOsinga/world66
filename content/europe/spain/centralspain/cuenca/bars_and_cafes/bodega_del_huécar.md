A wine bar in the old city specialising in wines from Castilla-La Mancha — particularly from the Manchuela and Ribera del Júcar denominations, both within the province. The owner is knowledgeable and will guide you through local producers if you're interested. Good selection of cured meats and local cheeses. Compact space; best visited outside peak hours when the atmosphere is more relaxed.
