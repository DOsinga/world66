A small café on the Plaza Mayor ideal for watching the square and the cathedral façade over a coffee or a morning tostada. Stone walls, simple tables outside, and a mix of locals and visitors make it the most comfortable place for an extended sit in the old city. The café con leche is good; the pastries are local and come fresh in the morning. Opens early, closes mid-evening.
