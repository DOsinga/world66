A long-established restaurant in the old city serving classic Cuenca cooking. The morteruelo here is reliably good — dense and deeply flavoured, served warm with toasted bread — and the roast lamb comes from local farms. The dining room occupies a stone-vaulted space with views towards the gorge. Booking is advisable at weekends. Mid-range to expensive.
