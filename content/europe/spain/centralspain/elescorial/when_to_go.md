---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

El Escorial is worth visiting year-round but has a clear peak season. Spring (April–June) and autumn (September–October) offer the best combination of moderate temperatures and manageable crowds. The town sits at 1,000 metres elevation, so it is noticeably cooler than Madrid in summer — a welcome escape on hot July and August days, though that also means it draws more visitors.

The monastery is packed on summer weekends; arriving early in the morning, or visiting on a weekday, makes a significant difference. In winter the surrounding Sierra de Guadarrama is sometimes snow-covered, which looks spectacular but can make hiking impractical.

The monastery is closed on Mondays.
