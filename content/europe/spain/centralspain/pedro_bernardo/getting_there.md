---
title: "Getting There"
type: section
---

Pedro Bernardo is about 120 km west of Madrid by road. The most direct route is the A-5 motorway towards Extremadura, then north via the Tiétar valley. The drive takes around 90 minutes from Madrid. There is no regular public transport to the village itself; a car is effectively essential. The nearest town with bus connections from Madrid is Arenas de San Pedro, about 20 km to the west.
