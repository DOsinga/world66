---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

Spring (April–June) and autumn (September–October) are the best times for walking: mild temperatures, good visibility, and the forest in its best colours. The chestnut groves turn golden in late October. Summer brings warm weather and the natural pools are at their most appealing, but July and August see the most visitor traffic and the lower hillsides can be uncomfortably hot by midday.

Winter is quiet and cold, with occasional snow on the upper slopes above the village. The sierra road can be slippery; check conditions before driving up from the valley. The village is at its most peaceful between November and March, when it largely returns to its own rhythm.
