---
title: "Getting There"
type: section
---

**By train:** Direct Alvia trains run from Madrid Chamartín to Salamanca in around 2 hours 15 minutes to 2 hours 30 minutes, several times a day. There are also regular services from Valladolid (about 1 hour) and Ávila. Note that Salamanca does not yet have AVE high-speed rail — services use the medium-speed Alvia trains on a conventional line.

**By bus:** ALSA runs frequent coaches from Madrid (Estación Sur) to Salamanca in around 2.5 hours; also services from Valladolid and other Castilian cities. The bus is often cheaper than the train and drops you at the bus station just west of the old town.

**By car:** From Madrid, take the A-6 towards Valladolid and then the A-50 west to Salamanca — about 2.5 hours in normal traffic. Parking in the historic centre is limited; there are underground car parks near the Gran Vía.
