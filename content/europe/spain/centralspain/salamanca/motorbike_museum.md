Salamanca's Motorbike Museum is a specialist collection that gives a different angle on a city dominated by sandstone university buildings and churches. Historic motorcycles, design details, and mechanical history make it worthwhile for visitors with even a modest interest in transport culture.

It is not a first-tier Salamanca sight, but it can be a good change of pace after the monumental centre. The museum is most useful on a longer stay or in poor weather.
