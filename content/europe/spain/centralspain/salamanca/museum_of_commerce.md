The Museum of Commerce gives Salamanca a different kind of history from its university and cathedral circuit. It focuses on shops, trades, packaging, advertising, and the everyday mechanics of buying and selling in the city.

That makes it a useful small stop if you have already seen the monumental core. The displays are modest, but they broaden the story of Salamanca beyond stone facades and student life, especially for anyone interested in social history or old retail design.
