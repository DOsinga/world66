---
title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Salamanca's compact historic centre packs an extraordinary amount into a short walk: the finest baroque square in Spain, one of Europe's oldest universities, two attached cathedrals, and a nobleman's palace studded with scallop shells. Allow at least a full day to take in the main sights — and stay for sunset, when the Villamayor sandstone glows gold.
