Talavera once had three lines of walls, and the surviving stretches of the first enclosure are among the most atmospheric parts of the city. The square towers and projecting albarrana towers recall the town's Islamic and medieval frontier history.

Walk the preserved sections around the old centre rather than expecting one continuous circuit. The walls are best treated as a series of fragments: a tower here, a stretch of masonry there, and sudden reminders that Talavera was once a fortified place on a strategic river crossing.
