The Monastery of San Joaquin and Santa Ana is a restrained neoclassical building in central Valladolid, designed with the sober discipline associated with Francesco Sabatini. Its church and museum do not shout for attention, which is part of their character.

Inside, the interest is sacred art and the calm geometry of the architecture rather than spectacle. It makes a worthwhile short stop if you are already exploring Valladolid's churches, sculpture museum, and historic core.
