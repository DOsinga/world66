The Oriental Museum in Valladolid holds a wide collection of Asian art and religious objects inside the Convento de los Agustinos Filipinos. The setting reflects Spain's historic links with the Philippines and the wider Pacific.

It is a specialist museum rather than a headline sight, but worthwhile for visitors interested in Asian art, missionary history, or something quieter than the city's main churches and squares.
