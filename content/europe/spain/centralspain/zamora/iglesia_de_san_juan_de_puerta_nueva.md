Iglesia de San Juan de Puerta Nueva stands right on Zamora's Plaza Mayor, so it is one of the easiest Romanesque churches in the city to fold into a walk. Its south rose window and compact stone proportions are the details to slow down for, especially if you are already comparing Zamora's extraordinary run of medieval churches.

It is not as essential as the cathedral or La Magdalena, but its location makes it more than filler. Step inside if it is open; otherwise the exterior still rewards a pause between the square, the old streets, and the route toward the river.
