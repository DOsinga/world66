San Pedro de la Nave is one of the great early medieval church visits around Zamora. The building is spare and compact, but the carved capitals and Visigothic stonework make it much richer than its size suggests.

It stands outside the city, so it is easiest with a car or as part of a wider route through the Zamora countryside. For travellers with any interest in architecture, it is worth the detour because it shows a layer of Spanish history that is easy to miss between Roman bridges and later cathedrals.
