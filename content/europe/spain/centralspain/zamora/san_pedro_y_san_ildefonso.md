Iglesia de San Pedro y San Ildefonso is one of Zamora's older and more characterful churches, standing close to the historic core rather than competing with the cathedral for drama. Its value is in the layers: Romanesque origins, later Gothic and Baroque changes, and a long association with Saint Ildefonso, one of the city's most important religious figures.

It is worth adding to a Zamora walk because the church shows how dense the city's medieval fabric is. You can move from the cathedral and castle area into smaller streets and still keep finding serious stonework, carved portals, and quiet corners of ecclesiastical history. The visit is short, but it helps fill in the city beyond the headline monuments.
