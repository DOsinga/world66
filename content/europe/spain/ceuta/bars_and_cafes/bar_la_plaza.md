A well-worn tapas bar on the Plaza de África, popular with locals from the early morning coffee crowd through to the late-evening beer drinkers. The bar serves a rotating selection of free tapas with drinks — a tradition that has largely disappeared in mainland Spain but persists here. The jamón and the tortilla are reliable. Standing room at the bar is the point.
