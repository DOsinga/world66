The terrace café on the Paseo del Revellín is where the city comes to watch itself go by. Coffee in the morning, cold beer and a plate of olives from mid-afternoon, a glass of Fino while waiting for dinner. The promenade here is Ceuta at its most Spanish — European café life with North Africa audible and visible across the fence. Cheap, cheerful, and central.
