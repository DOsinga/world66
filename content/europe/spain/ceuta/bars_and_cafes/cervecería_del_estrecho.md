A straightforward cervecería near the ferry terminal that has been the first (or last) stop for travellers crossing the strait for many years. Cold draft beer, good fried fish, and a no-frills atmosphere that suits the transit nature of the place. Open from early morning — useful if you have a ferry to catch and need something more substantial than a vending machine breakfast.
