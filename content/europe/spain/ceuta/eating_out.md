---
title: "Eating Out"
type: section
---

Ceuta's food scene reflects its crossroads position. Andalusian cooking — fresh fish, fried seafood, gazpacho — is the dominant register, but Moroccan spices, harissa, and slow-cooked tagine-style dishes appear on many menus. The fish market supplies restaurants with whatever came in that morning. Eating well here is easy and inexpensive by mainland Spanish standards.
