Situated near the Royal Walls, this is one of the better places in Ceuta for Spanish-Moroccan fusion cooking. Merguez alongside patatas bravas, harissa-spiked aioli, slow-cooked lamb with cumin next to tortilla española. The cooking navigates the two traditions without forcing them. The terrace has views towards the fortifications and is popular for evening meals in the warmer months.
