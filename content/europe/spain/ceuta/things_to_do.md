title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Ceuta packs a remarkable amount of history into a very small space. The Royal Walls dominate the old town, but there is also a well-preserved fortress on Monte Hacho, a striking cathedral, and a seafront leisure complex that is unlike anything else on the North African coast. The city's strategic position — guarding the strait between Europe and Africa — explains almost everything you see here.
