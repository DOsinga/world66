Agost Pottery Museum is the town's main reason to visit. It occupies a former early-20th-century ceramics factory and keeps the old production spaces legible: wheels, drying areas, kilns, and everyday vessels that once made Agost's reputation.

It is not a large museum, but it explains the town better than a walk through the streets alone. Start here before looking for the remaining workshops.
