Carrer de l'Alfareria is the street where Agost's pottery identity is easiest to see outside the museum. The name itself means potters' street, and the chapel, old workshops, and ceramic details keep the craft visible in the townscape.

It is a short wander, not a set-piece attraction. Pair it with the museum and the chapel rather than treating it as a standalone sight.
