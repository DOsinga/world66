type: poi

This small 19th-century chapel is dedicated to Santa Justa and Santa Rufina, the patron saints of potters. Its glazed tiles and ceramic details make it a neat local expression of Agost's craft tradition.

The chapel is tiny, but it is one of the places where the town's identity feels specific rather than generic.
