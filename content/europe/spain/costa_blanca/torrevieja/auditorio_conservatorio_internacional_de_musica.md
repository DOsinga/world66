The Auditorio Conservatorio Internacional de Música gives Torrevieja a serious indoor performance venue beyond its beaches and seafront promenades. The modern complex hosts concerts, conservatory activity, and cultural events, with music at the centre of its identity.

It is most relevant when something is on. Check the programme before you go, especially if you are interested in Torrevieja’s strong choral and habanera traditions, and treat the auditorium as an evening plan rather than a daytime landmark.
