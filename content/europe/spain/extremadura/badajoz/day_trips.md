Badajoz makes a good base for exploring Extremadura and the western edge of Portugal. **Mérida**, less than an hour east, has some of the finest Roman ruins in Spain — the theatre, the amphitheatre and the aqueduct of Los Milagros. **Cáceres**, a UNESCO World Heritage city, is about an hour and a half north and rewards a slow wander through its almost untouched medieval centre.

Across the Portuguese border, **Elvas** is just 15 km away — another UNESCO site, with the largest dry-moat bastioned fortress in the world and the spectacular 16th-century Amoreira Aqueduct. The whole town is walkable from the bus station; bring your passport.

For nature, the **Cornalvo Natural Park** east of the city has Roman-era reservoirs, dehesa oak woodland and good birdlife. Further afield, the **dolmens of Valencia de Alcántara** and the megalithic landscape around **La Serena** give a sense of the deep prehistory of the region.
