---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

Badajoz sits in one of the hottest parts of Spain. Summer afternoons regularly hit 40 °C and the city largely empties between two and six in the afternoon — even by Spanish standards, the siesta is taken seriously here. Outside the high summer, the climate is mild and dry.

**Spring (March-May)** is the best time. Days are warm but not punishing, the countryside is green, and the spring wildflowers across Extremadura are remarkable. **Carnaval** in February or early March is one of the largest in Spain — Badajoz takes its carnival very seriously, with a week of parades and elaborate competing comparsas.

**Autumn (September-November)** is also pleasant, with mild temperatures and the matanza pig-slaughter season getting underway across the surrounding villages — a good time for trying fresh chorizo and morcilla.

**Summer (June-August)** is hot enough to be an issue for sightseeing. Visit early morning or after 7pm; many shops close 2-6 in the afternoon. **Winter (December-February)** is mild and quiet, with cool dry days and crisp nights.

Two festivals worth timing for: the **Festival Ibérico de Música** in early June brings Spanish and Portuguese classical and folk musicians to the Alcazaba. The **San Juan** festival in late June fills the streets for nearly a week.
