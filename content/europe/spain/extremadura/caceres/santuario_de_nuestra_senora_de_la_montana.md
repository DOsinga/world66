The Santuario de Nuestra Señora de la Montaña sits on the Sierra de la Mosca above Cáceres, looking back across the plain to the old city. It is dedicated to the city's patron saint and remains a living pilgrimage site rather than just a viewpoint.

The climb or drive up is worthwhile for the panorama alone: Cáceres' towers, walls, and ochre roofs read beautifully from this distance. Inside, the sanctuary is modest, but the combination of local devotion and landscape gives it a stronger sense of place than many grander churches.
