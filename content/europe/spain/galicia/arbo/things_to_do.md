---
title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Arbo's attractions are modest but genuine. The single most distinctive thing here is the river itself — the line of medieval stone fish traps known as the pesqueiras, still used in late winter and spring to catch migrating lamprey. The Museo do Viño introduces the wine of the Condado de Tea sub-zone, the Os Muiños da Rocha mill park demonstrates the corn-grinding tradition along the Deva, and a short hike up Mount San Fins gives a view across the entire valley. Time a visit for the Festa da Lamprea in April or May if you want to see what makes the place tick.
