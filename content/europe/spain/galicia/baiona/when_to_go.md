---
title: When to Go
type: section
---

Galicia has a maritime climate: cool, green, and wet for much of the year. Summer (July and August) is the warmest and driest time, with temperatures in the low to mid-20s Celsius, though Atlantic rain can arrive at any time. August brings the most visitors; accommodation fills quickly in the old town.

Spring and early autumn are good alternatives. The light is softer, the crowds are thinner, and the coast is still walkable. In October the barnacle fishing season is at its peak and the seafood restaurants are at their best.

Winter is quiet and often stormy, but the coast is dramatic in rough weather and the town functions year-round for the Galician visitors who come to eat seafood and walk the fortress walls.
