title: "Getting There"
type: section
---

**By air**

The nearest airport is **Vigo–Peinador (VGO)**, about 40km south. It handles flights from Madrid, Barcelona, and several European cities, including Ryanair routes from London, Dublin, and Brussels. The alternative is **Santiago de Compostela (SCQ)**, roughly 55km north, which has a wider range of connections including transatlantic flights via Madrid. Neither airport has a direct bus connection to Cambados — you'll need to rent a car or take a taxi onward.

**By car**

A car is the most practical way to reach Cambados and explore the surrounding Salnés valley and Rías Baixas wine country. From Vigo, take the AP-9 motorway north and exit at the O Grove/Cambados junction onto the PO-548. From Santiago, head south on the AP-9 and follow signs for Vilagarcía de Arousa, then take the coast road south to Cambados. The drive from either city takes around 45–50 minutes in normal traffic.

**By bus**

MONBUS operates regional coach services from Vigo, Pontevedra, and Vilagarcía de Arousa to Cambados. Journey times vary: roughly 30 minutes from Vilagarcía, about an hour from Pontevedra, and around 90 minutes from Vigo. Services are infrequent — check schedules carefully and note that Sunday services are sparse. The bus stop is a short walk from the town centre.
