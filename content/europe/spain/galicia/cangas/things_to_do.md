title: Things to Do
type: section
---

Cangas offers an unusually varied set of pleasures for a small Galician town: wild Atlantic beaches, a weathered old town, good seafood, and the best views of [Vigo](/europe/spain/galicia/vigo) from across the ría. The municipality has almost 40 beaches, ranging from tidy resort strands to car-free Atlantic coves that take a pine-forest walk to reach.
