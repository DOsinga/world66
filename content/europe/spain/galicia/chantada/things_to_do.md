---
title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Chantada's main draws are its wine country landscape and the Roman and medieval history embedded in the countryside around it. The town centre is a starting point rather than a destination in itself.

The big set-pieces are the [Embalse de Belesar](/europe/spain/galicia/chantada/embalse_de_belesar) — the reservoir that defines the southern landscape and the Ribeira Sacra vineyards above it — and the surviving stretches of [Roman road](/europe/spain/galicia/chantada/roman_road) running south toward Monforte de Lemos. The riverside village of [Belesar](/europe/spain/galicia/chantada/belesar) is worth a stroll, and on a clear day the hilltop [Ermita do Faro](/europe/spain/galicia/chantada/ermita_do_faro) opens up wide views over the valley.
