---
title: "Getting There"
type: section
---

Combarro is 8 km west of [Pontevedra](/europe/spain/galicia/pontevedra) city and most easily reached from there. Local buses connect the two; check the schedule with Monbus, the main operator. By car it's a 15-minute drive. From [Vigo](/europe/spain/galicia/vigo_city), the drive takes about 30 minutes via the A-9 motorway. There is limited parking near the old quarter, especially in summer — arriving by bus or taxi is more practical on busy weekends.
