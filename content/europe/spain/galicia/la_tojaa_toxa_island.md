La Toja (A Toxa in Galician) is a small island off the coast of [Galicia](/europe/spain/galicia), connected to the O Grove peninsula by a bridge built in the early 20th century. Together with the Island of Arousa, it is one of the great island spaces in the Rías Baixas.

The island developed around its thermal spa, and today it is a genteel resort with hotels and leisure facilities. The most distinctive sight is the [Ermida de San Caralampio](/europe/spain/galicia/la_tojaa_toxa_island/ermida_san_caralampio), whose walls are entirely covered in scallop shells — a striking and peculiar piece of Galician architecture. The [Gran Hotel La Toja](/europe/spain/galicia/la_tojaa_toxa_island/gran_hotel_la_toja), built in 1907, anchors the southern end of the island, and [pine-shaded walking paths](/europe/spain/galicia/la_tojaa_toxa_island/island_walking_paths) circle the wooded shoreline.
