A Toxa is a small island, and a full tour of it on foot takes less than two hours. The main draws are the remarkable shell-covered chapel, a slow walk through the pine forest with sea views in every direction, and the curious spectacle of the grand spa hotel sitting in the middle of a wooded island. It makes an ideal afternoon stop when visiting the O Grove peninsula.
