Castro de Elvina is an Iron Age hillfort on the edge of A Coruna, a reminder that the
city's history reaches well beyond its Roman lighthouse and maritime core. The excavated
stone structures and hillside setting show the pre-Roman castro culture of Galicia in a
form close to the city.

It is best for travellers interested in archaeology or anyone with a car or enough time
to look beyond the waterfront. The site is open-air, so weather and footwear matter more
than at a museum.
