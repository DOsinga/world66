The Emilia Pardo Bazan Museum is tucked into the old city, inside the house associated with one of Galicia's most important writers. It is a small, literary stop: rooms, manuscripts, portraits, and period detail rather than spectacle.

For visitors who like cultural context, it adds a sharper edge to A Coruna than another waterfront walk can. Pardo Bazan's life connects the city to 19th-century debates about class, gender, realism, and modern Spain, and the house makes that history feel close and domestic.
