A Coruña's Fine Arts Museum is a strong rainy-day stop in a city where the sea often steals the attention. The collection includes Galician and Spanish painting, prints, decorative arts, and European works, displayed in a building that combines old convent fabric with modern museum spaces.

It is not overwhelming, which is part of its charm. Visit between walks along the waterfront or through the old town, and use it to see how Galicia's cultural world fits into the wider Spanish and Atlantic picture.
