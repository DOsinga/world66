Maria Pita House stands on the site associated with the local heroine who helped defend A Coruña during the English assault of 1589. The small museum tells the story of the siege and Maria Pita's place in the city's memory.

It is close to the old town and Plaza de María Pita, so it works best as part of a walk through the historic center. Expect a compact museum, with signage mainly in Galician.
