A Coruna's Military Museum sits near the old harbour and focuses on the city's long relationship with coastal defence. The collection includes weapons, uniforms, models, maps, and material connected to Galicia's military history and Spain's Atlantic-facing fortifications.

It is a specialist stop, but a useful one if you have already seen the Tower of Hercules and Castelo de San Anton. Together they make the city's strategic position easier to understand: this coast was not just scenic, it was watched, fortified, and fought over.
