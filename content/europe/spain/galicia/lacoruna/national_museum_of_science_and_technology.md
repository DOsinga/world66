The National Museum of Science and Technology, usually shortened to MUNCYT, gives A Coruña a modern museum stop beyond the old town and seafront. Its collections and interactive exhibits cover machinery, transport, communications, and the everyday technologies that shaped modern Spain.

It is particularly good with children, but adults who enjoy design, engineering, or aviation will not feel short-changed. The museum pairs well with a walk along the western waterfront, where the city’s Atlantic light and contemporary buildings change the mood from medieval lanes to open coast.
