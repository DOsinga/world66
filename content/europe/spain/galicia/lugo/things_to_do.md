---
title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Lugo's old town is compact and walkable, and virtually everything worth seeing is either on
or inside the Roman wall. The [wall](/europe/spain/galicia/lugo/roman_wall) itself is the centrepiece: walk the full two-kilometre
circuit on the sentry path for views over the city and the Miño valley. The [cathedral](/europe/spain/galicia/lugo/cathedral) and
the [Provincial Museum](/europe/spain/galicia/lugo/provincial_museum) are both free to enter and within easy walking distance of each other.
The [Domus do Mitreo](/europe/spain/galicia/lugo/domus_do_mitreo) museum and [Praza do Campo](/europe/spain/galicia/lugo/praza_do_campo) round out a full day inside the walls,
while the [Roman bridge](/europe/spain/galicia/lugo/roman_bridge) makes a worthwhile short walk down to the Miño.
