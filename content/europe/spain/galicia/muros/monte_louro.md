Monte Louro is the dramatic coastal landmark west of Muros, a twin-peaked mountain rising beside beach, dunes, and lagoon. It is the kind of place that makes the Galician coast feel bigger and wilder than the fishing-town streets suggest.

Come for a walk, a beach stop, or simply the view back toward the ría. The area is exposed, so weather matters; on a clear day it is one of the best natural excursions from Muros.
