---
title: Getting There
type: section
---

Noia is 35 km southwest of [Santiago de Compostela](/europe/spain/galicia/santiagodecompostela) and is connected by regular buses (Monbus) throughout the day — the journey takes about 45 minutes. The nearest airport is Santiago de Compostela, with flights to Madrid, Barcelona, and London.

By car, take the AG-56 motorway from Santiago, which makes the trip quick and easy. The town centre has parking near the waterfront. There is no train station in Noia; the nearest rail connection is at Padrón, 25 km east.
