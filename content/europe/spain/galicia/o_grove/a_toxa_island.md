A small pine-covered island connected to O Grove by a road bridge — you can walk across in ten minutes. The whole island is privately developed but freely accessible: the grand 1907 spa hotel **Gran Hotel La Toja** anchors the southern end, a 9-hole golf course occupies the middle, and the **Shell Church** (Ermida de San Caralampio) sits in pine woods on the western side.

The island is at its best for a slow afternoon walk: pine-shaded paths, sea views in every direction, occasional benches with views back to the O Grove waterfront. Several small art galleries and souvenir shops cluster near the bridge end; the spa hotel's terrace café is open to visitors for a coffee or a glass of cava.
