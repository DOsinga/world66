---
title: "Eating Out"
type: section
---

You come to O Grove to eat shellfish. The town has dozens of restaurants — most of them clustered along the harbourside Avenida do Beiramar — and a serious one almost everywhere will serve **percebes** (goose barnacles, the most expensive and most prized), **navajas** (razor clams), **mejillones** (mussels — the local ones are world class), **vieiras** (scallops), **pulpo á feira** (octopus the Galician way, on a wooden plate with paprika and olive oil), **camarones** (small shrimp), and **lubrigantes** (Atlantic lobster).

Pair with cool, salt-sharp **Albariño** wine from the surrounding **D.O. Rías Baixas** — the wine region that surrounds O Grove on three sides.
