---
title: Bars and Cafes
type: section
---

The wine bars of Ribadavia are the social infrastructure of the town. In the old quarter you can move between them on foot, tasting Ribeiro whites from different producers, and spend an entire afternoon going nowhere in particular. The tradition of drinking wine from ceramic cups (cuncas) is alive here — ask for "un cunca de Ribeiro" and you will get a small earthenware bowl of local white for a euro or two.
