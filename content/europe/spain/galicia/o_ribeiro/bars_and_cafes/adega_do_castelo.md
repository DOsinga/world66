Located near the castle, this wine bar is one of the better places to taste Ribeiro whites side by side without committing to a full bottle. The owner pours from a rotating selection of small producers in the comarca — you might encounter a Treixadura from the Avia valley one visit and a Torrontés blend the next. Simple cheese and charcuterie boards are available. The stone interior is cool in summer and worth the short walk uphill from the main square.
