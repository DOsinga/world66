More wine bar than restaurant but with a kitchen that takes its food seriously. The menu focuses on the kind of small dishes that make sense alongside wine: empanada de bacallau (salt-cod pastry), queixo de tetilla (the soft local cheese), and jamón. The Ribeiro selection is broad for a place this size — they stock whites from several small producers in the Avia valley — and the staff can talk about them with some knowledge. Popular in the evenings.
