---
title: Getting There
type: section
---

Ribadavia is 25 km southwest of Ourense and 65 km east of Vigo. Both cities have train connections to the rest of Spain, making either a sensible base if you are arriving by rail.

**By train:** There is a local train line (Renfe) running between Ourense and Vigo that stops at Ribadavia. The journey from Ourense takes around 30 minutes. From Vigo it is about an hour. Trains run several times a day but check the schedule — frequency drops on weekends.

**By car:** The AP-53 motorway connects Santiago de Compostela and Ourense; from Ourense, the N-120 road runs west through the comarca, passing through or near most of the main villages. A car is strongly recommended for exploring the vineyards and smaller municipalities away from Ribadavia.

**By bus:** There are bus connections from Ourense to Ribadavia, operated by regional bus companies. Frequency is limited and a car gives much more flexibility for the surrounding region.
