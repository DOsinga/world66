A reliable choice near the Praza Maior, O Xantar serves traditional Galician cooking without fuss: pulpo á feira (octopus with paprika and olive oil on wooden boards), lacón con grelos (cured pork with turnip greens), and whatever fish the market has produced that morning. The house Ribeiro white comes in ceramic cups, as is the custom in the region, and costs almost nothing. Lunch menus are good value and heavily patronised by locals — a reliable sign in a small town.
