Ribadavia's main square is the natural centre of town life. The sixteenth-century town hall anchors one side; arcaded stone buildings frame the others. In the evenings the square fills with people, and during the Feira do Viño do Ribeiro — held annually in late April or May — it becomes the focal point of the festival, packed with wine producers pouring glasses of Ribeiro white to anyone who has bought a tasting cup.

On an ordinary day it is a pleasant square to sit in, not grand but human in scale, with cafés at the edges and the old quarter's lanes leading off in several directions.
