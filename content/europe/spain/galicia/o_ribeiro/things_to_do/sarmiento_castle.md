The castle of the Counts of Ribadavia rises above the Campo da Feira at the edge of the old town — a fifteenth-century ruin that still holds its interior structure, corner towers, and curtain walls. The Sarmiento family were among the most powerful nobles in medieval Galicia, and this was their seat. Walking inside the remains you get a clear sense of the scale of it: this was built to impress, not just defend.

The castle was declared a Historic-Artistic monument and is free to enter. The towers offer good views over the rooftops and the river valleys beyond.
