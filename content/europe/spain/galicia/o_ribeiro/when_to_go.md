---
title: When to Go
type: section
---

O Ribeiro has a transitional climate — warmer and drier than coastal Galicia, with some Mediterranean influence filtered through the mountain ranges to the north and west. Summers are hot in the river valleys, reaching 30°C or above in July and August, though the evenings cool quickly. Winter is mild but wet.

**Spring (April–May)** is the ideal time to visit. The vines are shooting, the countryside is bright green, and the Feira do Viño do Ribeiro takes place in Ribadavia — usually in late April or early May — when dozens of producers bring their wines to the Praza Maior and surrounding streets. The festival is friendly and unpretentious, with a strong local attendance.

**Summer (June–August)** is warm and busy on weekends, when day-trippers arrive from Ourense and Vigo. The heat in the valleys is noticeable, but mornings are pleasant for walking.

**Harvest season (September–October)** is another excellent time. Picking is done largely by hand on the steep slopes, and the smell of fermenting must hangs over the wineries. Some producers welcome visitors during harvest — it is worth calling ahead.

**Winter** is quiet and a little bleak, but the town remains open and the wine bars continue to function. If you want Ribadavia without any other tourists, January or February will deliver it.
