Termas do Muino da Veiga is one of the open-air bathing areas that make Ourense feel different from other Galician cities. The pools sit by the Mino River, away from the old centre, where hot mineral water and river air turn a simple soak into the city's most distinctive ritual.

Check the current opening status before you go, as Ourense's public thermal areas can close or change access rules because of river levels, weather, or health regulations. If it is open, bring swimwear, a towel, and a relaxed schedule rather than expecting a spa with polished service.
