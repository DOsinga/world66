Termas Outariz e Canedo is part of Ourense's riverside hot-spring route, with warm mineral pools beside the Mino west of the city centre. Outariz is the upper group of pools and Burgas de Canedo the lower group.

Bring swimwear, a towel, and flip-flops, and check current opening status before setting out. Pools in Ourense's thermal areas can close temporarily because of river conditions, maintenance, or local rules.
