The Ruta do Viño Rías Baixas threads together more than 50 wineries open to
the public across the five sub-zones of the appellation. The densest cluster
is in Val do Salnés around Cambados, where you can comfortably visit three or
four bodegas in a day on foot or by bicycle. Pazo Baión, Granbazán, and Bodegas
Martín Códax are among the easiest to visit — most ask for an appointment but
some take walk-ins.

The wine itself is the reason to come: pale gold, intensely aromatic, naturally
low in alcohol, and the perfect companion to the seafood served at every
village restaurant. Visit during the September harvest if you can — the whole
region smells of fermenting grapes for two weeks.
