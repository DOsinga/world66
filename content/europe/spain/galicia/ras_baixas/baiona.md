Baiona, on the southern shore of the Ría de Vigo, is one of the most
attractive small towns in the Rías Baixas. The old town is a tangle of granite
arcades, fish restaurants, and small squares; above it stands the medieval
fortress of Monterreal, now converted into a parador hotel, with a long
fortified perimeter you can walk for the views.

The town's claim to fame is its role in the Columbus story: the Pinta docked
here in 1493 with the first news of America, and a moored replica still recalls
the moment. Beyond the history, Baiona is a working port with a strong sailing
culture and excellent seafood — order percebes (gooseneck barnacles) and
Albariño, sit on the harbour wall, and enjoy.
