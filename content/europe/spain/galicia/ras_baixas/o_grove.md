O Grove sits at the tip of a near-island peninsula at the mouth of the Ría de
Arousa and is one of the best places in Galicia to eat shellfish. The annual
Festa do Marisco in early October — declared of National Tourist Interest —
draws tens of thousands of visitors for a fortnight of mussels, oysters,
percebes, and razor clams.

Beyond the festival, O Grove is a working port with a long causeway connecting
to the island of A Toxa, a small spa enclave with a famously photogenic
church covered entirely in scallop shells. Boat trips from the harbour visit
the mussel rafts that float in their thousands across the ria.
