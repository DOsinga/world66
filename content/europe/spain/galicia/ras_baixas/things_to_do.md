---
title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

The defining experience of the Rías Baixas is to combine wine and sea — drive
the small coastal roads between the four rias, stop at a fishing village for
mussels and Albariño, and visit the
[Albariño wine bodegas](/europe/spain/galicia/ras_baixas/albarino_wine_route)
that line the route.

Don't miss [Combarro](/europe/spain/galicia/ras_baixas/combarro), the
photogenic granary-village of stone hórreos right on the water, or
[Cambados](/europe/spain/galicia/ras_baixas/cambados), the unofficial capital
of Albariño country. The Cíes Islands —
[Islas Cíes](/europe/spain/galicia/ras_baixas/islas_cies) — are one of the
best beach destinations in Europe.

For history, see the medieval pilgrim town of
[Baiona](/europe/spain/galicia/ras_baixas/baiona) and the riverside fortress
of [A Guarda](/europe/spain/galicia/ras_baixas/a_guarda) at the mouth of the
Miño.
