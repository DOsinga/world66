title: Things to Do
type: section
---

Redondela punches above its weight for a town of its size. The twin railway viaducts are the obvious starting point, but the Isla de San Simón, the historic cemetery, and the old town around the *colexiata* all reward a few hours of wandering. The Camino Portugués passes straight through, bringing pilgrims year-round and keeping the cafes lively.
