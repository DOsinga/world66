The Ría de Vigo offers several distinct experiences: the protected beaches and cliffs of the Cíes Islands, the historic port at Baiona where news of Columbus's first voyage first arrived in Europe, and the working city of Vigo with its fish market, castle, and contemporary art museum. The mussel and oyster bateas scattered across the water are a constant and characteristic feature of the landscape.
