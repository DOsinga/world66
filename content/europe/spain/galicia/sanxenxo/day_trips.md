Sanxenxo is well placed for short trips into the rest of the Rías Baixas. **Pontevedra**, 25 km east, is the obvious one — a beautifully preserved old town with stone arcades and pilgrim history. **Santiago de Compostela** is a long day or, better, an overnight: the cathedral city is the natural climax of any Galician trip. **Cambados**, north across the peninsula, is the capital of Albariño wine and the right place for a winery visit and a slow lunch.

A shorter trip is the **island of A Toxa** in the bay near O Grove, with its strange 12th-century chapel covered entirely in scallop shells, and the **Ons Islands** — a national park, reachable by ferry from Sanxenxo or Portonovo in summer, with cliffs, beaches, and almost no buildings.
