---
title: "Eating Out"
type: section
---

This is the Rías Baixas — seafood country. The local *mariscos* are some of the best in Spain: spider crab (*centolla*), goose barnacles (*percebes*), razor clams, oysters from the rafts in the ría, and the celebrated Galician octopus (*pulpo a feira*) boiled in copper pots and dressed with paprika and olive oil. Most restaurants worth visiting in Sanxenxo and Portonovo lean into the seafood tradition.

Wash everything down with the local **Albariño**. The vines on the slopes around the Ría de Pontevedra produce one of Spain's finest white wines — crisp, aromatic, faintly saline, perfect with shellfish. Drink it cold, drink it young, and drink it with food rather than as an aperitif.
