RC Celta de Vigo is more than a football club — in a city that identifies strongly with Galicia rather than Spain, supporting Celta is an expression of local identity. The team plays in La Liga, Spain's top division, at the Abanca Balaídos stadium in the Balaídos neighbourhood south of the city centre.

Attending a home match is one of the best ways to experience the city's culture. The atmosphere, particularly for games against Spanish clubs from Castile or Madrid, is electric. Tickets can be bought through the club website or at the stadium on match days. The ground holds just under 29,000 spectators. Check the fixture list in advance — the season runs August to May.
