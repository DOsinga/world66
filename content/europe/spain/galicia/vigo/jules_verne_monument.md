One of the most distinctive public sculptures in Spain stands at the edge of Vigo's harbour: a bronze Jules Verne locked in combat with a giant octopus. The work was created by sculptor José Molares for the centenary of Verne's death, unveiled in 2012, and references the author's famous association with Vigo Bay.

In *Twenty Thousand Leagues Under the Sea*, Verne has Captain Nemo and the crew of the Nautilus dive in the bay to recover treasure from a fleet of Spanish galleons sunk during the 1702 Battle of Rande. The fictional treasure has inspired numerous real salvage attempts over the centuries. The monument sits near the harbour entrance, a short walk from the Mercado da Pedra and the old town steps — easy to add to any waterfront walk.
