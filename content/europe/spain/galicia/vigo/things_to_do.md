---
title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Vigo rewards exploration on foot. The [Casco Vello](/europe/spain/galicia/vigo/casco_vello) climbs steeply from the waterfront through narrow granite streets to the hilltop Parque do Castro. The [Islas Cíes](/europe/spain/galicia/vigo/islas_cies) — accessible by ferry from the port — hold some of the most beautiful beaches in Spain. Back in the city, the [Mercado da Pedra](/europe/spain/galicia/vigo/mercado_da_pedra) and the old fishing district are worth a morning, and the [Museo do Mar de Galicia](/europe/spain/galicia/vigo/museo_do_mar) on the waterfront tells the story of the sea that built this city.

The monument to [Jules Verne and the Giant Octopus](/europe/spain/galicia/vigo/jules_verne_monument) at the harbour is one of the city's most distinctive landmarks, referencing Verne's novel *Twenty Thousand Leagues Under the Sea*, which is partly set near Vigo Bay.
