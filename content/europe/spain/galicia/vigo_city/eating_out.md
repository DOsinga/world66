---
title: "Eating Out"
type: section
---

Galician cooking is the reason to eat in Vigo: raw oysters, mussels in white wine, pulpo á feira (octopus dusted with smoked paprika and served on wooden boards), and the empanada gallega, a thick savoury pie filled with tuna, bacalao, or pork. The Rías Baixas wines — especially Albariño — are the natural partners for all of it. Restaurants cluster around the Berbés quarter and the Casco Vello, with prices that remain modest by Spanish standards.
