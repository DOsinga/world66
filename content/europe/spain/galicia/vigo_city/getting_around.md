---
title: "Getting Around"
type: section
---

The city centre is walkable, though it's built on hills and some streets are steep. The old Berbés quarter, Porta do Sol, and O Castro hill can all be reached on foot from the waterfront in under 30 minutes. Castrelos Park and the Municipal Museum are further out — a bus or taxi makes more sense.

The municipal bus network covers the whole city and is cheap. The most useful line for visitors connects the port area with the city centre and the university. Taxis are plentiful and the fares are reasonable by Spanish standards.

Ferries to the Cíes Islands depart from the terminal at the central harbour (Estación Marítima de Ría). Services run from roughly Easter to September; frequency increases in July and August. Buy tickets in advance online or at the terminal — the daily visitor quota for the islands fills up fast in high summer.
