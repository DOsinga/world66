---
title: "Getting There"
type: section
---

Vigo has its own airport at Peinador, about 15 km east of the city centre, with domestic flights to Madrid, Barcelona, and other Spanish cities, plus some seasonal European connections. The airport is small and the flight options limited; most international visitors arrive via Porto (Portugal), which is only 120 km south and connected to Vigo by bus and train.

The train station, Vigo-Guixar, sits on the central waterfront. Renfe runs regular services to Santiago de Compostela (1 hour), A Coruña (2 hours), Porto (2.5 hours), and Madrid (about 5 hours). A second station, Vigo-Urzáiz, is higher up in the city and serves some of the same routes; check which station your train uses. The two stations are about 20 minutes apart on foot.

Long-distance buses are cheap and connect Vigo to Porto and most Galician cities. ALSA operates the main routes; the bus station is near the waterfront. If you're coming from Porto, the Rede Expressos service is reliable and runs several times a day.
