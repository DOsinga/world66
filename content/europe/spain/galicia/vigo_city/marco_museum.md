MARCO, Vigo's Museum of Contemporary Art, occupies a 19th-century building that began life as a courthouse and prison. The conversion gives the museum a strong architectural frame: galleries arranged around the old institutional bones, now used for temporary exhibitions rather than a permanent collection.

It is the best central stop for contemporary culture in Vigo, especially on a wet Atlantic afternoon. Since exhibitions change, check what is on before going; even then, the building and its location on Príncipe make it easy to combine with a walk through the city centre.
