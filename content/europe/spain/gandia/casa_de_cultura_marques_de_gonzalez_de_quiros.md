Casa de Cultura Marques de Gonzalez de Quiros is a 19th-century palace used as Gandia's cultural centre. It hosts temporary art exhibitions, concerts, and civic events in a historic setting close to the old centre.

It is a useful secondary stop after the Ducal Palace and the collegiate church, especially when an exhibition is running. Even outside event times, the building adds another layer to Gandia's small but rewarding historic core.
