---
title: "Getting There"
type: section
---

Albacete sits squarely on the **Madrid–Alicante AVE high-speed line**, with frequent trains making **Madrid Atocha in 1 hour 30 minutes** and **Alicante in 50 minutes**. The station is on the southern edge of the centre, an easy walk to most hotels.

The city is on the **A-31 motorway** (Ocaña–Alicante), with the bus station a few hundred metres from the train station — ALSA and Avanza buses serve [Cuenca](/europe/spain/la_mancha/cuenca), [Toledo](/europe/spain/la_mancha/toledo), Valencia and the smaller La Mancha towns. The nearest commercial airports are **Alicante** and **Valencia**, both about two hours away by car or train.
