The provincial museum is genuinely worth the trip to Albacete on its own. Set in a low modern building inside Parque Abelardo Sánchez, it holds two outstanding collections — a Roman section with a complete and exquisite set of **bone dolls** found in a Roman girl's grave at Ontur (the only intact such set in Spain), and a remarkable Iberian section with the **Cerrillo Blanco bulls and sphinxes**, 4th-century BC stone sculptures from a local necropolis.

The fine-arts wing on the upper floor is more uneven but includes a few paintings by the local 20th-century artist Benjamín Palencia. Allow an hour and a half. Free for EU citizens; closed Mondays.
