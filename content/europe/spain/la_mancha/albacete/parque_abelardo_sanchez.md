The biggest urban park in Castilla-La Mancha and the green lung of Albacete, the **Parque Abelardo Sánchez** stretches across 12 hectares of mature trees, ponds, palms and rose beds a short walk south of the cathedral. Locals call it simply *el parque*; it holds a duck-filled pond, a small open-air theatre, a children's traffic-education circuit and the Provincial Museum.

Pleasant for a quiet hour, especially in summer when the rest of the city bakes in the sun. The northern end has a couple of kiosks selling cold beer and *granizado* (slush ice).
