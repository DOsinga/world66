---
title: "Day Trips"
type: section
---

Consuegra is well placed for a longer windmill-and-castle circuit. **Campo de Criptana**, 45 km south, has another spectacular row of mills overlooking the plain — and a stronger Cervantes pedigree. **Almagro**, 80 km south, has a beautifully preserved 17th-century *corral de comedias* (open-air theatre). The walled city of **Toledo**, 65 km north, makes an obvious extension if you have a second day.

For a quieter detour, **Tembleque** (24 km north) has one of the most photogenic Plaza Mayors in Castile, with three-tier wooden balconies running around all four sides.
