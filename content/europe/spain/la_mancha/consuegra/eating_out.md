---
title: "Eating Out"
type: section
---

La Mancha eats simply and well — *pisto manchego* (a slow-cooked pepper-and-tomato stew with eggs), *migas* (toasted breadcrumbs with chorizo and grapes), *manchego cheese* eaten with quince paste, and *atascaburras* (a salt-cod and potato purée drizzled with olive oil). Saffron features in many of the rice and chicken dishes; ask for *arroz a la manchega*.
