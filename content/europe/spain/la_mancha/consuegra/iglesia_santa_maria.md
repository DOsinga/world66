The main parish church of Consuegra sits on Plaza de España in the heart of the old town. The current building is a 16th-century Mudéjar-influenced reconstruction of a much older Romanesque church, with a sober brick exterior and a tall single-naved interior that holds a fine 17th-century main retable.

Worth a brief look on your way through the old town. Open mainly during mass times; outside those, ask at the tourist office in one of the windmills for arranged access.
