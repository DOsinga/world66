The town's small archaeological and ethnographic museum, **Los Corredores**, is housed in a 17th-century granary on Plaza de España — a tall, arcaded building that is itself worth a look. Inside are finds from the Roman colony of *Consaburum*, including mosaics and inscriptions; a section on the great 1891 flood; and a recreated saffron worker's room with bunches of flowers laid out for stripping.

Quick to see (45 minutes) but well done, and gives useful context for a visit to the Roman dam and the windmills. Small entry fee.
