---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

La Mancha has fierce extremes — **summer** brings 38–40°C heat with no shade on the windmill ridge; **winter** sees sharp frosts and the occasional dusting of snow. The shoulder seasons are easily best.

**April–May** is when the plain is briefly green and the wildflowers bloom around the Roman dam. **September–November** is the most rewarding time, particularly the **last weekend of October**, when the Saffron Festival fills the town with food stalls and folk dancing. If you only have one day to spare for La Mancha, this is the day.
