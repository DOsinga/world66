The Panteón de la Duquesa de Sevillano is one of Guadalajara's more unusual buildings — an ornate neo-classical mausoleum built in the late 19th century by the Duchess of Sevillano, one of the richest women in 19th-century Spain. The duchess commissioned a structure that would serve as both a family tomb and a statement of piety and wealth, and the result is a lavish domed chapel with frescoed interiors, white marble surfaces, and elaborate bronze fittings.

It sits slightly outside the main cluster of monuments, near the San Francisco park, and is easy to miss. The building is occasionally open for visits; check locally for times. Even from outside, the dome and ornate entrance are worth seeing.
