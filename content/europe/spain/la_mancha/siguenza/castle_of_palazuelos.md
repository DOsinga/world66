The Castle of Palazuelos is a ruined medieval castle set on a hilltop about five kilometres north of Sigüenza. It controlled the road north from the city toward the mountain passes leading into Aragón, and the remains of its walls and towers are still substantial enough to give a sense of its original scale. The setting is striking, with wide views over the dry agricultural landscape of the Guadalajara highlands.

The castle dates from the 13th to 15th centuries and passed through several noble owners. Like many castles in this part of Spain, it was abandoned after the end of the frontier conflicts and fell gradually into ruin. The site is not formally managed as a tourist attraction but is accessible; it makes a good short excursion for those with a car and an interest in medieval military architecture. The surrounding countryside of the Serranía de Guadalajara is excellent walking territory.
