---
title: Getting There
type: section
---

Sigüenza is on the Madrid-Zaragoza railway line, with regional trains from Madrid-Chamartín taking roughly two hours. The station is below the old town; the climb to the cathedral is short but steady.

By car, it is about 90 minutes from Madrid via the A-2 and CM-1101, depending on traffic around the capital. A car is useful if you want to visit Palazuelos, Pelegrina, or the surrounding villages, but not necessary for the town itself.
