---
title: "Getting There"
type: section
---

Sigüenza is on the main Madrid-Zaragoza railway line and has regular direct trains from Madrid Chamartín station. The journey takes about two hours. This is easily the most convenient way to arrive; the station is at the bottom of the hill leading up to the historic centre, a short walk away.

By car, Sigüenza is about 135 kilometres northeast of Madrid on the A-2 motorway, then the CM-110 regional road. The drive takes around 90 minutes from central Madrid in normal traffic. There is parking on the outskirts of the old quarter; parking inside the historic centre is restricted.

There are no regular bus services from Madrid that are convenient for a day trip; the train is by far the better option. From Guadalajara city there are local bus connections.
