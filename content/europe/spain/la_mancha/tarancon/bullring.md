Tarancón's bullring, built at the end of the nineteenth century, is a circular brick arena of modest size on the south side of town. It hosts only a handful of corridas a year — typically during the town's August fiesta — but the building is open for visits at other times and is in regular use for concerts, regional festivals and the *encierros* (running of the bulls) in summer.

Whether or not you have any interest in the bullfight itself, the architecture is a respectable provincial example of the type, with the characteristic horseshoe-arched façade and the wooden barrera around the ring. It is worth a quick look on a walk down through the town's southern streets.
