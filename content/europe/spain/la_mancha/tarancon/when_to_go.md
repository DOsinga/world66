---
title: 'When to Go'
type: section
---

Tarancón sits on the inland Castilian *meseta* and has a continental climate: bitterly cold winters with occasional snow, and hot, dry summers when daytime temperatures regularly cross 35°C. Spring and autumn are by far the best times to visit — mild days, low rainfall, and the surrounding fields green or harvested.

The town's main *fiestas* fall in early September, around the feast of the *Virgen de Riánsares*, with processions, *encierros* and concerts in the bullring. A quieter visit is possible in May or October, when the cafés around the Plaza Mayor are full but the heat and the crowds are not.
