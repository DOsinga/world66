---
title: "Getting There"
type: section
---

Valdepeñas is well connected to Madrid by both rail and road, making it an easy day trip or overnight stop.

The quickest option is the AVE high-speed train from Madrid Puerta de Atocha to Ciudad Real (about 50 minutes), followed by a regional RENFE service the remaining 30 km to Valdepeñas — the full journey takes around 90 minutes with a good connection. Alternatively, direct regional trains on the Madrid–Cádiz line stop at Valdepeñas station, with a journey time of around two hours. The station is about a ten-minute walk from the town centre.

By car, Valdepeñas is directly on the A-4 motorway (the old road to Cádiz and Andalusia), 215 km south of Madrid. The drive takes around two hours depending on traffic leaving the capital.

From [Toledo](/europe/spain/la_mancha/toledo), the drive south on the CM-42 takes about an hour. There is no direct train connection between Toledo and Valdepeñas.
