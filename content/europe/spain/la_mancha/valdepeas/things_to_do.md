title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Valdepeñas is, above all, a wine town — and the best way to understand it is to visit the bodegas that have shaped the place for centuries. But there is more here than wine: a handsome Gothic church, a lively central square, windmills straight out of Cervantes, and a fine art museum.

The [Museo del Vino](/europe/spain/la_mancha/valdepeas/museo_del_vino) on Calle Princesa is the natural starting point. From there, the old centre clusters around the [Plaza de España](/europe/spain/la_mancha/valdepeas/plaza_de_espana) and the [Iglesia de la Asunción](/europe/spain/la_mancha/valdepeas/iglesia_de_la_asuncion), both within easy walking distance.

For wine, the [bodega visits](/europe/spain/la_mancha/valdepeas/bodega_visits) around the edge of town are the main event — underground cellars carved into limestone, some centuries old, with tastings included. The [Molinos de Gregorio Prieto](/europe/spain/la_mancha/valdepeas/molinos_gregorio_prieto) windmill park just outside town places you squarely in Don Quixote country. The [Fundación Gregorio Prieto](/europe/spain/la_mancha/valdepeas/fundacion_gregorio_prieto) rounds out the cultural picture with work by Valdepeñas' most celebrated painter.
