La Rioja is Spain's most famous wine region, a small province where vineyards carpet the valley of the Ebro beneath the Sierra de Cantabria. Beyond the bodegas and the tapas bars of [Logroño](/europe/spain/la_rioja/logrono), it offers monasteries, dinosaur trackways, and striking modern winery architecture.
