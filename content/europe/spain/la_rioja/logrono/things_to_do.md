---
title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

The [Co-Cathedral](/europe/spain/la_rioja/logrono/cocatedral_logrono) with its twin Baroque towers is the dominant landmark of the old town. The [Church of San Bartolomé](/europe/spain/la_rioja/logrono/church_san_bartolome) has the finest Romanesque portal in the city, dating from the twelfth century. The [Museum of La Rioja](/europe/spain/la_rioja/logrono/museo_la_rioja) covers regional history in an elegant Baroque palace. For wine, the bodega visits around the city and in the villages of the Rioja Alta are the main draw — ask at the tourist office for current opening times, as arrangements change frequently.
