Arco de la Victoria is a large triumphal arch at Moncloa, built between 1950 and 1956 to commemorate the Nationalist victory in the Battle of Ciudad Universitaria during the Spanish Civil War. Its scale and setting make it one of the more visible Franco-era monuments in Madrid, worth a short stop for visitors tracing the city's 20th-century history.
