The neighbourhood west of the city centre, anchored by the **Parque del Oeste** and the university quarter of **Moncloa**. Argüelles is the traditional student neighbourhood of Madrid — Complutense University is nearby, and the streets around **Calle de la Princesa** fill with students and young locals. The area has a relaxed, unglamorous energy compared to Malasaña or Chueca, and is better for an afternoon beer than a late-night bar crawl.

The key draw is the green corridor along the western edge: **Parque del Oeste** runs from Argüelles to the **Debod Temple**, an authentic Egyptian temple gifted to Spain in 1968 and reassembled overlooking the Manzanares valley. The **Teleférico** cable car departs from the park, crossing above the Casa de Campo. **Calle de Isaac Peral** and **Calle del Pintor Rosales** have the best terrace bars, with views over the park.
