Basílica La Milagrosa is an early 20th-century church in Chamberí, built in a mix of Neo-Mudejar and Gothic Revival styles. The brickwork and pointed forms make it a worthwhile architectural stop if you are walking between Museo Sorolla and the quieter streets north of central Madrid.
