The most concentrated tapas street in Madrid — a single sloping street of about 300 metres in La Latina with twenty-odd tapas bars, tabernas, and restaurants opening their doors more or less continuously from midday until 2 am. The clientele is a mix of Madrileños doing a proper tapas crawl, serious food tourists, and the Sunday lunchtime crowd spilling over from El Rastro.

Standout addresses on the street include **Txirimiri** (pintxos), **El Almendro 13** (tostas and vermouth), and **Casa Lucas** (creative tapas). The street runs from Plaza Puerta Cerrada down to Plaza de la Cebada. The best approach is to go without a plan — walk the whole street, pick somewhere with an open table, and work your way up or down. Metro La Latina.
