Faunia is a zoological park in Madrid's southeastern suburbs, with themed areas for animals from regions such as Africa and Antarctica. It is designed as a family day out, with indoor and outdoor habitats rather than a quick city-centre sight.

Plan it as a half-day trip, especially with children or if you want a quieter alternative to Madrid's central museums and monuments.
