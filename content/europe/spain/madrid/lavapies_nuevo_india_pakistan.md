title: Indian and South Asian Restaurants of Lavapiés
type: poi
---

Lavapiés has the best concentration of South Asian restaurants in Madrid — not tourist-oriented, but cooking for the working-class South Asian community that settled here from the 1990s onward. The greatest density is on and around Calle de Mesón de Paredes and Calle Lavapiés itself: Bangladeshi, Pakistani, and Indian restaurants serving biryanis, karahi dishes, and daal at prices that feel remarkable in central Madrid.

These are not fancy places — formica tables, minimal decoration — but the cooking is substantially better than the tourist-facing Indian restaurants around the rest of the city center. The halal meat shops and South Asian grocery stalls on the surrounding streets are also worth exploring. Metro Lavapiés.
