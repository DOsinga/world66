The central square and social hub of Madrid's most ethnically diverse neighbourhood, Plaza de Lavapiés is surrounded by bars, grocery stalls selling international produce, and the constant movement of residents from Spain, South Asia, North Africa, sub-Saharan Africa, and China — a mix that makes it unlike anywhere else in central Madrid.

It is not a conventionally beautiful square — flat, largely treeless, with functional buildings on all sides — but the atmosphere on a summer evening, with tables spilling out from every bar and half the neighbourhood apparently having the same idea, is difficult to match. The neighbourhood has seen considerable gentrification pressure and some conflict over its future, but the street life remains genuinely mixed. Metro Lavapiés.
