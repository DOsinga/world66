The most photographed café in Malasaña and a good argument that self-conscious vintage aesthetic and excellent breakfast can coexist. Lolina has mismatched 1970s furniture, old magazines on the tables, and a menu built around weekend brunch staples — eggs, avocado toast, granola, fresh juice, strong coffee. Lines form on weekend mornings.

The area around Calle del Espíritu Santo where it sits is the densest concentration of cafés and brunch spots in the neighbourhood. Lolina is worth the queue if you arrive early; otherwise there are a dozen credible alternatives within half a block. Closed Mondays. Metro Noviciado or Tribunal.
