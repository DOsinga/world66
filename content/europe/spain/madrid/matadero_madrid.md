Matadero Madrid is a former municipal slaughterhouse in Arganzuela that has been turned into a major arts and cultural centre. The early 20th-century brick buildings now hold exhibitions, performances, design spaces, film events, and public courtyards, making it one of Madrid's strongest examples of industrial architecture reused for contemporary culture.
