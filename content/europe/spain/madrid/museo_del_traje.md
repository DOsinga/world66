Museo del Traje is Madrid's costume museum, with collections that trace clothing and fashion in Spain from medieval garments to contemporary design. The displays are a good change of pace from the city's bigger art museums.

The 1970s building has its own architectural interest, and the surrounding lawns and fountains make the museum feel more relaxed than many central Madrid sights.
