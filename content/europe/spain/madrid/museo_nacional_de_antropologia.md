The Museo Nacional de Antropología is a small museum near Atocha with collections on cultures from Asia, Africa, and the Americas. Most displays are in Spanish, but leaflet translations help make the exhibits accessible to visitors who want a quieter museum stop near Retiro and the Prado corridor.
