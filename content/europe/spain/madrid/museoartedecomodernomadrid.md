The string of terrace bars and restaurants along Calle de Serrano and Calle Ortega y Gasset that constitutes Salamanca's most visible eating and drinking scene. This is where the neighbourhood comes for long weekend lunches: white tablecloths, good wine lists, and traditional Spanish cooking done carefully — cocido madrileño, roast suckling pig, grilled fish.

**El Paraguas** on Calle Jorge Juan and **José Luis** on Calle Serrano are the most enduring of the traditional restaurants. For tapas, the streets around Calle Claudio Coello have a cluster of good bar-restaurants with outside tables. The Salamanca eating scene runs to a different pace from the rest of Madrid — later starts, longer meals, higher prices. Metro Serrano or Velázquez.
