Palacio Real de El Pardo began as a royal hunting lodge and later served as Francisco Franco's residence. It is still used as an official guest house for visiting dignitaries, but opens to the public when not in official use.

The interiors are known for their tapestries, including designs linked to Francisco Goya. Check access before travelling, since official use can affect visiting hours.
