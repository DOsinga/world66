Parque de El Capricho is one of Madrid's loveliest parks, laid out from the late eighteenth century with a taste for Romantic gardens. Lakes, fountains, small palaces, shaded paths, and ornamental corners make it feel more composed than the city's larger everyday parks. It sits away from the centre, but the detour pays off if you like gardens.
