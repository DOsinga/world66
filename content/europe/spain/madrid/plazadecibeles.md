The grandest of Madrid's roundabouts, with the white marble fountain of the goddess Cybele drawn in her lion-pulled chariot at the centre (a 1782 work by Ventura Rodríguez). The four buildings surrounding it form one of the great urban tableaux in Europe: the **Palacio de Cibeles** (the early-20th-century neo-Gothic former post office, now the Madrid town hall and a cultural centre with a roof viewpoint), the **Bank of Spain**, the **Palacio de Buenavista** (army headquarters), and the **Palacio de Linares** (Casa de América).

The fountain doubles as the celebration spot for Real Madrid football and the Spanish national team — when either wins anything important, the players climb the fountain and tie a scarf around the goddess. The roof of the Palacio de Cibeles is open to the public (small fee) and gives a 360-degree view down both halves of the Paseo del Prado-Recoletos axis.
