title: National Library (Biblioteca Nacional)
type: poi
---

One of the largest libraries in the world and the main national archive for Spanish literature, housed in a massive neoclassical building on the Paseo de Recoletos dating from 1896. The library itself is not open for general browsing without an accreditation card, but the **Museo del Libro** on the ground floor is free and open to the public, displaying rare manuscripts, maps, and printed books from the collection.

The building's reading rooms are occasionally opened for special visits; the permanent exhibition covers the history of writing and book production in Spain from the medieval period onward. The attached Museo Arqueológico Nacional at the same address has a separate entrance and charges admission. Metro Colón.
