The big square at the western end of Gran Vía, reopened in 2021 after a five-year redesign that buried the traffic, opened up the green space, and gave the square back to pedestrians. The two anchor buildings are the **Edificio España** (a 1953 mixed-use tower) and the **Torre de Madrid** (1957 — at one time the tallest building in Spain).

At the centre of the square is the **monument to Miguel de Cervantes**, with bronze statues of Don Quixote and Sancho Panza in front of the writer's seated figure. Trees, a long shallow reflecting pool, and the green slope up to the Templo de Debod give the redesigned square a generous, well-loved feel. Use it as a hinge between the Palacio Real (south) and the Templo de Debod / Casa de Campo (west).
