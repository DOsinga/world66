---
title: "Shopping"
type: section
---

Madrid's shopping splits between three distinct zones. The grand bourgeois shopping district is the **Salamanca neighbourhood**, with the international luxury brands lining Calle Serrano and the side streets — Loewe, Hermès, the Spanish jewellers Suarez, and the Madrid flagships of every major European fashion house. The grand-old-Spain department store **El Corte Inglés** has half a dozen branches around the city; the flagship is on Calle Preciados just off Puerta del Sol.

For something more characterful, the **El Rastro** flea market every Sunday morning in La Latina is the great Madrid shopping experience — antique books, vinyl, vintage clothing, hardware, and a fair amount of junk spread across a maze of streets south of Plaza de Cascorro. The **Mercado de San Miguel** by Plaza Mayor is restored, beautiful, and aimed at tourists; the neighbourhood markets of **San Antón** in Chueca and **Antón Martín** in Lavapiés are cheaper and more local.

For specialty: **Casa de Diego** (fans) at Puerta del Sol since 1858, **Antigua Casa Talavera** (ceramics from across Spain) on Calle Isabel La Católica, **El Flamenco Vive** for flamenco gear, and the Sunday morning **stamp and coin market** in Plaza Mayor.
