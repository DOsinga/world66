The Community of Madrid surrounds the Spanish capital (the city of [Madrid](/europe/spain/madrid) is listed directly under Spain). The region beyond the city is rich in royal and historic day trips: the palace and gardens of [Aranjuez](/europe/spain/madrid_region/aranjuez), the university town of [Alcalá de Henares](/europe/spain/madrid_region/alcaladehenares), birthplace of Cervantes, and the vast monastery-palace of [El Escorial](/europe/spain/madrid_region/elescorial) in the Sierra de Guadarrama.
