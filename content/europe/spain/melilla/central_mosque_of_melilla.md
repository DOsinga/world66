type: poi

The Central Mosque of Melilla is one of the clearest reminders that the city is both Spanish and North African. Its architecture and urban presence sit alongside churches, synagogues, military buildings, and modernist facades in a compact, layered cityscape.

Non-Muslim visitors should treat it primarily as an exterior architectural stop unless access is clearly permitted. It fits naturally into a walk about Melilla's religious diversity and border-city identity.
