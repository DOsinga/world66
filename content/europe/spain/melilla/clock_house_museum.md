The Clock House, or Torre de la Vela, is an old watchtower and barracks within Melilla's fortified historic core. Its clock tower is one of the city's familiar landmarks, tied to Melilla's military history and its position above the Mediterranean.

The site is worth including with Melilla la Vieja and the nearby monumental complex, especially for the views over the old town and coast.
