Horcas Coloradas Beach is one of Melilla's better-known beaches, named for the reddish rock formations around the shore. The sand, calm water, and open views make it a popular place for swimming and an easy coastal break from the city centre.

It works best as a relaxed beach stop rather than a formal sight. Bring what you need for the beach and combine it with a walk along Melilla's seafront.
