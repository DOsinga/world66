King Felipe VI Farm School Park is a small educational park on the edge of Melilla, with farm animals, play areas, green space, and short walking paths. It is aimed mostly at families, especially younger children who need a break from streets and museums.

Do not expect a major sight, but it is a useful low-key stop if you are travelling with kids or want a quieter corner of the city.
