The Plaza de Armas monumental complex forms part of Melilla la Vieja, the fortified old city above
the harbour. Walls, gates, batteries, and restored spaces here tell the story of a Spanish enclave
shaped by the sea, Morocco, and centuries of military pressure.

It is one of the better places to understand Melilla beyond its modernista streets. Go slowly
through the ramparts and viewpoints, because the value is in the way the fortress, Mediterranean
light, and layered frontier history sit together.
