The Port of Cartagena is not just scenery. It is the reason the city exists, a deep natural harbour guarded by hills and used for trade, naval power, fishing, and now cruise ships. Even a short waterfront walk makes Cartagena's geography obvious.

The most pleasant stretch for visitors is around the inner harbour, where museums, promenades, and cafes face the water. Look beyond the leisure boats and you can still see the working port and naval infrastructure that keep Cartagena from becoming merely picturesque.
