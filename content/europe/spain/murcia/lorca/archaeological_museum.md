The Museo Arqueológico de Lorca occupies the Casa de los Salazar, a beautifully
restored early 17th-century mansion built around a central courtyard. The
collection traces the long human occupation of the Guadalentín valley, with
material from Bronze Age Argaric settlements, Iberian and Roman sites, and the
medieval Islamic period.

The highlights include painted Argaric ceramics, fine Islamic glazed pottery, and
a small but engaging set of Roman mosaics and inscriptions. The museum is good
value, almost always uncrowded, and easily combined with the nearby churches and
the climb to the castle.
