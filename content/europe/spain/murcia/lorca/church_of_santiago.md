The Iglesia de Santiago is a 16th- and 17th-century parish church a short walk
from the Plaza de España. The plain stone exterior gives little hint of the
elaborate interior — coffered wooden ceilings, gilded retables, and a series of
side chapels endowed by the noble families of the city.

The church suffered serious damage in the 2011 Lorca earthquakes — the dome
collapsed and parts of the upper walls had to be rebuilt — but it has now been
fully restored. Worth combining with a visit to the nearby Plaza de la Concordia
and the small museums of the brotherhoods that organise the Semana Santa
processions.
