The Colegiata de San Patricio is the most important church in Lorca and an
architectural anthology of the Spanish Renaissance and Baroque. Construction
began in 1533 and continued for nearly two centuries, so the building blends a
Plateresque main portal, a Renaissance interior, and a heavily Baroque facade
on the western front.

The interior is unusually bright and well-proportioned, with a high vaulted nave
and a remarkable set of polychrome wooden retables. The 2011 earthquake damaged
the building badly and the restoration has been long, but most of the major
spaces are now back in use. Sits right on the Plaza de España.
