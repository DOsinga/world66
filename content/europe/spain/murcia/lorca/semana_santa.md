Lorca's Semana Santa, declared of International Tourist Interest, is unlike any
other Holy Week in Spain. The processions are organised by two rival
brotherhoods — the Paso Blanco (whites) and the Paso Azul (blues) — and instead
of staying within the conventional liturgical framework they have evolved into
elaborate biblical pageants featuring Roman cavalry, chariots, costumed Old
Testament characters, and floats embroidered with real gold and silver thread.

The processions wind through the streets of the old quarter on each evening of
Holy Week and culminate in a long, electrifying parade on Good Friday. Book
accommodation months in advance. Outside of festival time, the museums of each
brotherhood (Museo de Bordados del Paso Azul and Museo del Paso Blanco) display
the costumes and embroideries year-round.
