---
title: "Getting There"
type: section
---

Lorca lies on the A-7 motorway between Murcia and Almería and is easily reached
by car — Murcia is about 50 minutes away, Cartagena about an hour, and Granada
roughly two and a half hours. The nearest airports are Murcia-Corvera (45
minutes) and Almería (an hour and a half).

Trains run from Murcia several times a day to Lorca-Sutullena station, just south
of the historic centre. Buses connect Lorca to the major cities of the region
and to Madrid (about five hours).
