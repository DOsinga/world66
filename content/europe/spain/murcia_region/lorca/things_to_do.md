---
title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

The [Castillo de Lorca](/europe/spain/murcia_region/lorca/castle_of_lorca) is the obvious
starting point — both for the views over the city and for the well-presented
exhibitions on Lorca's Jewish, Muslim, and Christian past. From there, walk down
into the centro histórico to see the
[Colegiata de San Patricio](/europe/spain/murcia_region/lorca/college_of_saint_patrick),
the spectacularly tall [Church of Santiago](/europe/spain/murcia_region/lorca/church_of_santiago),
and the elegant Plaza de España.

The [Archaeological Museum](/europe/spain/murcia_region/lorca/archaeological_museum) is
worth an hour, and during Holy Week the
[Semana Santa](/europe/spain/murcia_region/lorca/semana_santa) processions are arguably
the most spectacular in Spain.
