Centro Municipal de Arte y Exposiciones gives Aviles a smaller, more local counterpoint to the headline Niemeyer Centre. Its temporary exhibitions usually lean toward contemporary art, photography, and cultural programming, making it worth checking if you are already exploring the old town and riverfront.

The building is not a blockbuster attraction, and the value depends on what is showing. Still, Aviles rewards travellers who look past the single famous landmark, and this gallery is one of the places where the city's everyday cultural life comes through.
