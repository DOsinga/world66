title: Things to Do
type: section
---

Cangas del Narcea is best treated as a mountain-and-wine base rather than a quick photo stop. [Corias Monastery](#corias_monastery) is the main architectural sight, while the [Cangas Wine Museum](#cangas_wine_museum) gives context to the steep vineyards that make this the only Asturian wine country with its own protected identity.

With a car and planning, the day opens out into the Fuentes del Narcea mountains. [Muniellos Nature Reserve](#muniellos_nature_reserve) needs advance permission for the core forest, but even its visitor areas explain the landscape well. [Nuestra Senora del Acebo Sanctuary](#nuestra_senora_del_acebo_sanctuary) is easier to reach and gives a big view over the valleys.
