The Nicanor Pinole Museum is a focused art museum devoted to one of Gijon's own painters, with works that move between portraiture, local scenes, and the cultural life of Asturias. It is small enough to visit without fatigue, but specific enough to feel rooted in the city.

It is most rewarding if you want a quieter art stop after the waterfront and old quarter. The museum adds a local voice to Gijon's cultural circuit, especially alongside the Juan Barjola Museum and the city's industrial-era streets.
