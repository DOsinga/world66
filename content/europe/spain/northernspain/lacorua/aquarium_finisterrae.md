Aquarium Finisterrae sits on the waterfront between the Torre de Hércules and the Domus museum. Its focus is the marine life of the Galician coast and the Atlantic — the richest fishing waters in Spain — with particular attention to the ecosystems of the local rias (the drowned valleys that give Galicia its convoluted coastline).

The aquarium is well-designed and thoughtfully presented. The tank sequence moves from intertidal rock pools through estuarine environments to open Atlantic species; the displays cover both the biology and the human relationships with the sea. A large central tank allows viewing from multiple levels.

Part of the same municipal science complex as the Domus museum next door. The combined ticket is good value; both are manageable in a half-day. Good for families; also worth the visit for adults interested in marine ecology.
