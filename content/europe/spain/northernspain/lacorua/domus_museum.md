The Domus (Casa del Hombre — House of Man) is a science museum dedicated to the human body and human identity. It occupies Arata Isozaki's striking slate-and-concrete building on the waterfront, which looks out over the Paseo Marítimo and the Atlantic.

The permanent collection covers human biology and perception — how bodies function, how senses work, how humans have understood themselves across history and cultures. The interactive exhibits are particularly well-developed for a European science museum of this era; many are still engaging. Temporary exhibitions cover themes from psychology and neuroscience to social history.

Part of the same municipal complex as Aquarium Finisterrae. The building is worth seeing from the outside even if you don't go in — it is one of the more interesting pieces of contemporary architecture in Galicia.
