---
title: "Eating Out"
type: section
---

La Coruña is one of the best seafood cities in Spain. Galicia's rias produce extraordinary shellfish — percebes (goose barnacles), navajas (razor clams), berberechos (cockles), centollos (spider crabs), and vieiras (scallops) are all found on local menus. The tapas culture here means you can work your way through the streets around Plaza de María Pita sampling pulpo a feira (octopus with paprika and olive oil), empanada (stuffed pastry), and glasses of Albariño white wine for relatively little money.
