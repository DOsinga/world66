---
title: "Getting Around"
type: section
---

The La Coruña peninsula is compact and walkable. The Torre de Hércules at the northern tip to the old town at the base is about 3 km, easily covered on foot along the Paseo Marítimo. City buses cover the wider city and the beach neighbourhoods. Taxis and rideshares are available.

Cycling is promoted through a city bike-share system. The Paseo Marítimo has a dedicated cycle lane running its full length, making it the best way to cover the waterfront quickly. For day trips to the wider province — to Finisterre, the Costa da Morte, or the Rias Altas — a car is necessary as public transport coverage is limited.
