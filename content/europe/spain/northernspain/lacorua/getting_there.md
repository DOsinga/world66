---
title: "Getting There"
type: section
---

La Coruña has its own airport, A Coruña Airport (LCG), with connections to Madrid, Barcelona, and several other Spanish cities, as well as some European destinations. It is smaller than Santiago de Compostela's airport (SCQ), which is 60 km south and has wider international connections, particularly to the UK and Germany.

By rail, La Coruña is connected to Madrid by high-speed AVE service (about 4 hours via Santiago de Compostela) and to Santiago de Compostela by frequent regional services (about 30 minutes). The railway station is on the southern harbour side of the city.

By bus, La Coruña is linked to Madrid, Santiago, Vigo, and other Galician cities. The bus station is near the city centre.
