The Paseo Marítimo is one of the great waterfront walks of Atlantic Europe. It runs 13 kilometres around the entire La Coruña peninsula, alternating between sheltered harbour views and exposed Atlantic coast depending on which side of the headland you are on.

The most dramatic section faces the open ocean: broad paving, low sea walls, and the spray of Atlantic swells on rough days. The characteristic glass-fronted houses (galerías) that line the landward side of the promenade give La Coruña a distinctive skyline — the galería was developed here as a way of capturing winter light and heat while keeping out the rain and Atlantic wind.

The promenade is well-used by locals year-round — joggers, cyclists, dog walkers, families. In summer it extends into an outdoor social circuit in the evening. The stretch from the Aquarium to the Torre de Hércules is the most rewarding for visitors.
