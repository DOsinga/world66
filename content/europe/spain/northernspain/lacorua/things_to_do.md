---
title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

La Coruña's headline attraction is the Torre de Hércules, the Roman lighthouse that has guided ships into this Atlantic harbour for nearly 2,000 years. Beyond it, the city rewards exploration: the old town's Romanesque churches, the elegant glass-fronted houses (galerías) on the seafront, the 13-kilometre Paseo Marítimo, and a science museum designed by Arata Isozaki that gives the waterfront a 21st-century counterpoint to the Roman tower. The city is also one of the best places in Galicia to eat — the seafood here is exceptional.
