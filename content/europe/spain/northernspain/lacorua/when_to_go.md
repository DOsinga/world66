---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

La Coruña's climate is Atlantic — mild and damp year-round, but rarely extreme. Summer (July–August) is the warmest and driest period, with temperatures typically reaching 22–24°C. This is peak season; the city is at its liveliest and the beaches are popular.

Spring and autumn are pleasant and less crowded. Rain is possible at any time of year, and the Atlantic can be stormy in winter, but the city is built for rain — the galerías and arcades mean much of the social life happens under cover. Winter temperatures rarely drop below 8°C.

The Semana de las Letras Gallegas in May celebrates Galician literature. The city's main festival, the Festas de María Pita, runs through August with concerts, fireworks, and a medieval fair in the plaza.
