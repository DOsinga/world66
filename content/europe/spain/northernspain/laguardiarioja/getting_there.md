---
title: Getting There
type: section
---

Laguardia has no train station. The nearest large cities are Logroño (15 km south, across the regional border in La Rioja) and Vitoria-Gasteiz (60 km north, the Basque capital). Both are well connected by rail to Madrid and the rest of Spain, and both have car hire options.

By car from Logroño, follow the A-12 west and then north toward Elciego and Laguardia — the drive takes about twenty minutes. From Bilbao, take the AP-68 motorway south toward Logroño and exit toward Laguardia; allow an hour. There is no regular public bus service directly to Laguardia from the main train stations, so a hire car or taxi from Logroño or Vitoria is essentially required for those without their own transport.

Parking outside the walls is free; the town centre is pedestrian-only, so you will need to leave the car at the perimeter.
