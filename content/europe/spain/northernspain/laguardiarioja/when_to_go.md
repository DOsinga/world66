---
title: When to Go
type: section
---

The Rioja Alavesa harvest — la vendimia — runs through September and into October, and is the most atmospheric time to be in Laguardia. The bodega tours are busiest, the vineyards are being worked, and there is a particular sense of purpose in the air. Spring (April to June) is quieter, the landscape is green, and the wildflowers in the vineyards are in bloom.

Summer weekends in July and August bring day-trippers from Bilbao and San Sebastián and can make the town feel crowded. Midweek visits are far more pleasant. Winter is cold and often foggy, and several restaurants and bodegas close or reduce hours, though the covered colonnades of the old town provide some shelter and the lack of crowds has its own appeal.
