The Jurassic Museum of Asturias, usually shortened to MUJA, is the coast's main indoor sight. Its exhibitions explain the dinosaur footprints and fossil record of eastern Asturias, with full-scale models outside and a low, distinctive building set above the sea. It is especially good with children, but the local geology gives it more weight than a simple dinosaur display.
