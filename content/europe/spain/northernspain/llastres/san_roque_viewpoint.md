type: poi

The small San Roque chapel sits above Llastres and gives the village its postcard angle. From here you can see the harbour, the beaches, the Sueve mountains, and on clear days the deeper line of the Picos de Europa. It is a short climb, and it is the first place to go if you want to understand the shape of the village.
