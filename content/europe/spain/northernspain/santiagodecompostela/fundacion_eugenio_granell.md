Fundacion Eugenio Granell is a small but serious art museum in Santiago de Compostela, centred on the Galician surrealist Eugenio Granell. It occupies the Pazo de Bendaña on Praza do Toural, a convenient stop just off the old-town pedestrian routes.

The collection gives Santiago a modern, experimental counterweight to its medieval and religious identity. Expect works by Granell, documents from exile and avant-garde networks, and temporary exhibitions that make the foundation more than a single-artist memorial.
