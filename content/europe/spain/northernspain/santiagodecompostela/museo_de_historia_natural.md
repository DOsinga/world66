Santiago's Museo de Historia Natural is run by the university and gives a useful change of pace from churches, cloisters, and pilgrim streets. Its collections cover minerals, fossils, animals, plants, and the natural landscapes of Galicia.

It is especially good on a wet day, which Santiago supplies often. The museum helps connect the city to the forests, coast, and granite country around it rather than treating Santiago as only a religious destination.
