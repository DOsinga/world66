The Two Marías is a bright, much-photographed sculpture in Alameda Park, showing sisters Maruxa and Coralia Fandiño. The figures are cheerful at first glance, but their story is tied to poverty, political repression, and the way a city remembers its eccentrics.

It is a small stop, but a good one because it adds local feeling to Santiago's grander pilgrim architecture. Find it while walking the Alameda for views back toward the cathedral towers.
