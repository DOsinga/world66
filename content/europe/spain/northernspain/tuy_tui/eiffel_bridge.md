The Puente Internacional crosses the Miño river between Tui and Valença, Portugal, a distinctive iron lattice structure built by the Eiffel company in 1884. Walking across it gives fine views up and down the river valley, and the short walk (about 800 metres) deposits you in the old walled town of Valença on the Portuguese side. The crossing is free and open to pedestrians, cyclists, and vehicles.

Valença's walls are exceptionally well preserved — a 17th-century Vauban-style fortress with star-shaped bastions, ramparts, and gates still intact. The town inside the walls is tourist-oriented but interesting, with a view back over the bridge to Tui from the walls' highest point. This combination of two historic border towns facing each other across a river makes the Tui-Valença crossing one of the most visually satisfying land borders in Europe.
