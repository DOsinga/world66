---
title: Getting There
type: section
---

Tui is on the main Vigo-Porto rail corridor — Guillarei station (3 kilometres from town) has regular trains to Vigo (20 minutes) and connections to Porto on the Portuguese side. Vigo is the nearest major city, about 30 kilometres north on the AP-9 motorway. There are regular buses from Vigo to Tui. From Santiago de Compostela, Tui is about 90 minutes by road.

Crossing to Portugal is simple: walk or drive across the bridge to Valença, where Portuguese trains connect south toward Porto and Lisbon. The Camino Portugués passes through Tui for pilgrims walking to Santiago.
