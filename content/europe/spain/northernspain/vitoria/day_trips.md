The Rioja Alavesa wine region lies just 30–40 kilometres south of Vitoria-Gasteiz over the Sierra de Cantabria, and the contrast is dramatic: the green Basque highlands drop suddenly into the dry Mediterranean landscape of the Rioja, with terraced vineyards covering the slopes. The villages of Laguardia, Elciego (home to the Frank Gehry-designed Marqués de Riscal hotel), and Labastida all make good stops. Several wineries offer visits and tastings, and the medieval walls of Laguardia are worth a walk in themselves.

[Bilbao](/europe/spain/northernspain/bilbao) and [San Sebastián](/europe/spain/northernspain/sansebastian) are both under an hour by train, making easy day trips for anyone based in Vitoria.
