The Fournier Museum of Playing Cards, housed in a neo-Gothic palace in the old town, displays a remarkable collection assembled over more than a century by the Vitoria-based Fournier playing card company. The collection spans five centuries and covers decks from across the world — not just Spanish and European cards but Asian, American, and African examples, military decks, propaganda cards, tarot sets, and cards made for specific games that no longer exist.

The museum is smaller than its reputation might suggest, but the collection itself is genuinely fascinating, touching on graphic design, social history, and global trade. The 15th-century Spanish decks are the highlights.
