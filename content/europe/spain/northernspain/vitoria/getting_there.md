---
title: Getting There
type: section
---

Vitoria-Gasteiz has a small airport with connections to Barcelona and other Spanish cities, but most visitors arrive by train or car. The train station is on the southern edge of the centre, a 10-minute walk to the old town. There are regular services from [Bilbao](/europe/spain/northernspain/bilbao) (45 minutes) and [San Sebastián](/europe/spain/northernspain/sansebastian) (1 hour). By car from Madrid it is about 4 hours via the A-1.
