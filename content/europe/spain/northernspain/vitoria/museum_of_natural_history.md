Vitoria-Gasteiz's Museum of Natural History occupies the Torre de Doña Otxanda, a medieval defensive tower that gives the collections more character than a standard modern gallery would. Inside are fossils, minerals, and displays on the natural environment of Álava and the Basque Country.

It is a compact stop, best used as a change of pace while exploring the old town. The building itself is part of the attraction: you get a little science, a little local landscape context, and a reminder that Vitoria's medieval core still has its defensive bones showing.
