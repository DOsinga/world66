---
title: When to Go
type: section
---

Vitoria-Gasteiz is good year-round. The Fiestas de la Virgen Blanca in August (4–9 August) are the city's main event, centred on the Plaza de la Virgen Blanca and involving fireworks, street processions, and the ceremonial descent of Celedón — a papier-mâché figure with an umbrella — from the church tower. The Vitoria-Gasteiz Jazz Festival in July draws serious music fans. Spring and autumn are the most comfortable seasons for walking.
