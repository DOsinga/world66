Ontinyent is a small city of about 36,000 people in the Valencia region, tucked into the hills of the Vall d'Albaida — a wide inland valley an hour's drive from the coast. It has a compact old quarter rising to a [Moorish-era castle](/europe/spain/ontinyent/castell_ontinyent) with views over the surrounding hills, and a pleasant central square that comes alive in the evenings.

The highlight of the year is the [Moros i Cristians](/europe/spain/ontinyent/moros_i_cristians) festival in late August, when the town re-enacts the medieval battles between Moorish and Christian forces with elaborate costumes, competing bands, and nocturnal parades. It is one of the most elaborate celebrations of this tradition in the Valencia region and well worth timing a visit around. Outside the festival season, the natural pools at [Pou Clar](/europe/spain/ontinyent/pou_clar) — cold, clear rock pools on the river just outside town — are the main draw.
