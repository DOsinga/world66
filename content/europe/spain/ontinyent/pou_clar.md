Pou Clar is a series of natural rock pools on the Clariano River about 2 km from the town centre, formed where the river drops over limestone ledges to create pools deep enough for swimming. The water is cold (16–18°C year-round) and very clear, and the pools are fringed by vegetation in a narrow gorge. Local teenagers use the higher ledges for cliff jumping.

The site is free to access and popular with locals from spring through autumn. There are basic facilities nearby. The walk from town along the river path takes about 30 minutes. It is one of the more attractive natural swimming spots in the interior Valencia region and entirely uncommercialized.
