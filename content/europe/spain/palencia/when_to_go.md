---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

Palencia has a classic Castilian continental climate: cold winters, hot dry summers, and pleasant spring and autumn months. Late spring (May–June) and September–October are the best times to visit — comfortable temperatures for walking and driving the province. July and August are hot and the city partially closes down as residents leave for the coast or mountains. The Fiestas de San Antolín in early September are the main annual celebration, with processions, concerts, and street activity centred on the cathedral.
