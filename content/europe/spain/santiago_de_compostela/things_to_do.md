---
title: Things to Do
type: section
---

The old town is one of the most complete Romanesque ensembles in Europe, and you could easily spend a full day just wandering its granite arcades without a plan. The Cathedral is the obvious starting point, but the squares around it — Praza das Praterías, Praza da Quintana, and the vast Praza do Obradoiro — each reward unhurried attention. The Monastery of San Martiño Pinario, a hulking Benedictine complex immediately north, is often overlooked by pilgrims rushing to the main event.

For the Botafumeiro, check Mass times in advance — the giant incense burner is only swung on feast days and at certain pilgrim Masses, not every service.
