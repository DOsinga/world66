Museu d'Aigues d'Alacant explains how water has shaped Alicante, from old wells and cisterns to the modern supply network. It stands beside the Pozos de Garrigos, a group of historic wells used from the Moorish period into the 19th century.

The museum is small but useful if you are already exploring the old town below Santa Barbara Castle. Its exhibits add context to a dry coastal city where water management has always mattered.
