Benalmádena is a resort town on the Costa del Sol between [Torremolinos](/europe/spain/spanishcostas/torremolinos) and [Fuengirola](/europe/spain/spanishcostas/fuengirola), about 20 minutes west of [Málaga](/europe/spain/andalucia/malaga). It divides into three distinct parts: the [pueblo](/europe/spain/spanishcostas/benalmadena/pueblo) (old village) up in the hills, Arroyo de la Miel (the main residential and commercial centre), and the costa (the beachfront strip with hotels, bars, and the marina).

[Puerto Marina](/europe/spain/spanishcostas/benalmadena/puerto_marina) is the focal point — a large marina complex with restaurants, bars, and shops built in a vaguely Moorish style. The [beaches](/europe/spain/spanishcostas/benalmadena/playa_bonita) are decent if not spectacular, with fine grey sand and warm water. The [Tivoli World](/europe/spain/spanishcostas/benalmadena/tivoli_world) amusement park, the [Sea Life aquarium](/europe/spain/spanishcostas/benalmadena/sea_life), and the [Selwo Marina](/europe/spain/spanishcostas/benalmadena/selwo_marina) are all aimed squarely at families. The [Teleférico](/europe/spain/spanishcostas/benalmadena/teleferico) connects Arroyo de la Miel to the top of Monte Calamorro, with good views along the coast.

For a break from the resort strip, the [Castillo de Colomares](/europe/spain/spanishcostas/benalmadena/castillo_colomares) above Arroyo de la Miel is a bizarre and oddly charming monument to Christopher Columbus, built in the 1980s and 90s and mixing Byzantine, Romanesque, Gothic, and Mudéjar styles. Higher up, the [Benalmádena Stupa](/europe/spain/spanishcostas/benalmadena/benalmadena_stupa) is the largest Buddhist stupa in the western world.
