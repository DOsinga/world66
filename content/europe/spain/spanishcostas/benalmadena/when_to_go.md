---
title: When to Go
type: section
---

The Costa del Sol claims more than 300 days of sunshine a year, and Benalmádena is no exception. May, June, September, and October are the best months: warm, dry, and not yet (or no longer) overrun. July and August are very hot — easily into the high 30s Celsius — and the beaches, restaurants, and roads are packed with Spanish and European holidaymakers.

Winter is mild but variable: most days are sunny and in the high teens, but you do get the occasional grey week of rain. The Mediterranean is cold for swimming from December to April. Easter (Semana Santa) brings spectacular processions in the pueblo and Málaga, but you should book accommodation well in advance.
