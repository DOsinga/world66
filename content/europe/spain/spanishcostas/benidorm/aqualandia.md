Aqualandia is Benidorm's big water park, set inland from Levante Beach and built for a full day of slides, wave pools, and family noise. It is a very different experience from the old town or the Serra Gelada cliffs, but it is a major part of Benidorm's holiday machinery and a practical choice when the heat is high.

The park suits families and groups more than solitude seekers. Arrive early in peak summer, expect queues for the headline slides, and budget for a commercial day out. If you are travelling with children, it can be one of the easiest ways to break up beach time without leaving the resort area.
