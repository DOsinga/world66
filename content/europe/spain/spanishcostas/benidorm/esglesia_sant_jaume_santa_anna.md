The Església de Sant Jaume i Santa Anna is the white-and-blue parish church at the top of Benidorm's old town, close to the Balcón del Mediterráneo. Its tiled domes and simple Mediterranean lines are a reminder that Benidorm was a fishing town before the towers and package holidays arrived.

Step inside if the doors are open, then linger in the lanes and viewpoints around it. The church is not large, but its position makes it part of the essential old-town circuit. It gives a quieter pause between the beach promenades and the viewpoints over Levante and Poniente.
