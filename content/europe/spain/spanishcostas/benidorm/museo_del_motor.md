Museo del Motor is a classic vehicle museum near Benidorm, displaying cars, motorcycles, and motoring memorabilia. It gives the resort a different kind of indoor attraction for days when beach, bars, and theme parks are not the plan.

The museum is mainly for enthusiasts and families who like old machines. It is not central to understanding Benidorm, but it clears the threshold as a specialist attraction in a destination built around leisure.
