San Luis Park is a green pocket at the western edge of Buñol, where the town starts to open into the river landscape. The park has a spring, a fountain, a small chapel, and an open-air auditorium.

It is worth including on a Buñol walk because it links the old centre with the water route toward Cueva Turche. On a hot day, the shade and running water are more useful than another monument.
