---
title: "Eating Out"
type: section
---

Calpe's fish market auctions the day's catch each afternoon at the port, and the best restaurants in town are built around what comes off the boats. The old town above the port and the streets around the harbour have the most interesting options — the beach strips are dominated by tourist menus and all-day breakfasts aimed at the British expat community.

**El Bodegon** — a reliable choice for fresh grilled fish and rice dishes near the port. Order the arroz a banda, the local rice cooked in fish stock, which is the signature dish of the Costa Blanca.

**La Cantina del Puerto** — on the port itself, with tables outside overlooking the fishing boats. Simple fish and seafood at fair prices; popular with locals at lunchtime.
