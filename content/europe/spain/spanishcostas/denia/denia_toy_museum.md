The Dénia Toy Museum is a compact, appealing museum housed in the former railway station. Its collections focus on the town's once-important toy industry, especially wooden toys and early 20th-century manufacturing.

It is an easy add-on near the centre and a good rainy-day or family stop. The museum also gives Dénia a story beyond the castle and marina, showing how a coastal town tied itself to craft production as well as tourism.
