Ermita de Santa Llucia is a small chapel rather than a major monument, but its hillside setting gives it more travel value than its size suggests. The whitewashed building sits away from Denia's busier waterfront and old-town lanes, with views toward the Montgo landscape.

It is best folded into a walk or short drive, not treated as a stand-alone excursion. Go for quiet, a little local religious history, and the pleasure of stepping out of the resort rhythm for half an hour.
