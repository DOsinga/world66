---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

Jávea has around 300 days of sunshine per year and a mild climate by any measure — even January averages 13°C. The best months for visiting are May, June, and September, when the beaches are accessible but the summer crowds have not yet arrived or have already left. July and August bring hot, dry weather and significantly more tourists; accommodation prices spike and the coves fill by 10am.

The Montgó hiking is best in spring and autumn when temperatures are comfortable and the wildflowers are at their most varied. Swimming is feasible from May to October; the sea temperature peaks in August at around 27°C.
