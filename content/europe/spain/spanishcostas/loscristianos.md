Los Cristianos began as a fishing village and still has a little more everyday texture than neighbouring Playa de las Américas, though the two now run together along the south Tenerife coast. It is a resort, certainly, but one with a working [port](/europe/spain/spanishcostas/loscristianos/port_of_los_cristianos), ferry passengers, older streets, and a proper town centre behind the beach.

The main appeal is convenience with a slightly gentler edge. [Playa de Los Cristianos](/europe/spain/spanishcostas/loscristianos/playa_de_los_cristianos) is sheltered by the harbour, [Playa de Las Vistas](/europe/spain/spanishcostas/loscristianos/playa_de_las_vistas) is broader and sandier, and [Montaña Chayofita](/europe/spain/spanishcostas/loscristianos/montana_chayofita) gives a quick volcanic viewpoint between the resorts.

Los Cristianos is also the practical gateway to La Gomera, El Hierro, and La Palma by ferry. That makes it a good base if you want south-coast sun but plan to escape Tenerife's busiest resort strip for at least a day.
