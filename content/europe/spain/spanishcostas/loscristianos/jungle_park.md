Jungle Park is a landscaped animal and adventure park above Los Cristianos, built around winding paths, bird displays, big-cat enclosures, and family rides. It is more controlled attraction than wild nature, but the hillside setting gives it views and a break from the resort strip.

It is best treated as a half-day family outing, especially if children need a change from beaches and pools. Check transport and show times in advance, because the park sits inland from the waterfront and works better with a little planning.
