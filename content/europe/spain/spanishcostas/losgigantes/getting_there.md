---
title: Getting There
type: section
---

Los Gigantes is on Tenerife's west coast, about 40 minutes by car from Tenerife South Airport in normal traffic. The road is straightforward until the final approach, where the resort's steep streets can be awkward for parking.

TITSA buses link Los Gigantes with Playa San Juan, Costa Adeje, Las Américas, and Los Cristianos, but services are slower than driving. A car is useful if you want to visit Masca, Teno, or Teide independently.
