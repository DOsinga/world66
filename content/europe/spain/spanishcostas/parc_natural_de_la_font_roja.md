Parc Natural de la Font Roja protects a cool, forested mountain ridge inland from the Costa Blanca. It is a good antidote to the coast: shaded trails, limestone slopes, viewpoints, and a feeling of being properly above the heat and sprawl below.

Routes range from easy walks to longer hikes, but the mountain weather can change quickly. Bring water, check the park information before setting out, and do not treat it as a beach-day stroll.
