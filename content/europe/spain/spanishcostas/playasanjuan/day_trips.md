---
title: "Day Trips"
type: section
---

Playa San Juan works best as a quiet base for exploring the southwest coast and the mountain interior.

[Los Gigantes](/europe/spain/spanishcostas/losgigantes), twenty minutes north, has the most dramatic cliffs on the island — a vertical wall of basalt 600 metres high running into the Atlantic. The Masca valley winds inland from there, a narrow ravine with a small village at the head; the hike down to the sea is one of the classic Tenerife walks.

[Teide National Park](/europe/spain/canaryislands/tenerife/teide), about an hour by road, is unmissable — a vast volcanic caldera with the snow-capped peak of Teide in its centre. You can drive a loop around the crater, take the cable car most of the way up the mountain, or walk a stretch of the lava-field trails.

South along the coast, [Los Cristianos](/europe/spain/spanishcostas/loscristianos) and [Playa de las Américas](/europe/spain/spanishcostas/lasamericas) are the big resorts, useful mainly for the ferry to La Gomera and for nightlife if you want a noisy evening out.
