---
title: "Eating Out"
type: section
---

The seafood along the harbour is the obvious draw — the restaurants buy direct from the fishing boats, so what you eat is what was caught that morning. **Tasca El Puerto**, on the harbourside, does plain grilled local fish (vieja, sama, cherne) with mojo verde and *papas arrugadas*. **El Bote** is a step up in price and ambition, with a daily menu based on the catch.

Away from the harbour, the village restaurants serve the standard Canarian repertoire — rabbit in salmorejo, *ropa vieja*, *carne fiesta*. Look for places with a chalkboard menu and locals at the bar; the tourist-trap places further south have not yet colonised San Juan.
