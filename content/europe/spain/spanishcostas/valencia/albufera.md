The Albufera is a freshwater lagoon 11 km south of Valencia, separated from the Mediterranean by a strip of pine forest and rice paddies. It is the largest lake in Spain, a critical stopover for migratory birds (herons, egrets, flamingos, ospreys), and the cradle of Valencian rice cultivation. The boat ride from the village of El Palmar at sunset is a Valencian classic.

Make a day of it: drive to El Palmar, eat paella at lunch in one of the village's many rice restaurants (Casa Salvadora, Nou Racó), take the flat-bottomed boat out among the reeds in late afternoon, watch the sun go down behind the western shore. Buses 25 and 24 run from Valencia city centre.
