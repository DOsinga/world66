---
title: "Day Trips"
type: section
---

The Valencian coast and hinterland offer easy escapes. **El Saler** and the **Albufera natural park**, twenty minutes south, are the closest and most distinctive — boat trips on the lagoon, paella in the rice-growing village of El Palmar, dunes along the sea. **Sagunto**, half an hour north, has one of the best-preserved Roman theatres in Spain and a Moorish-Iberian-Roman hilltop castle that piles up two thousand years of fortifications on a single rock.

**Xàtiva**, an hour south by train, is a handsome medieval-Moorish hill town with a magnificent two-castle fortress and a long history as the rival of Valencia in the 13th century — the Borgia popes were born here. Further south, **Cullera** and **Gandía** have long sandy beaches if you want a low-key alternative to the city ones.

For a longer day, the Roman aqueduct at **Peñíscola** and the dramatically perched old town make a worthwhile two-hour drive north up the coast.
