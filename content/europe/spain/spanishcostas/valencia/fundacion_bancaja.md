Fundacion Bancaja is one of Valencia's dependable central art stops, set near Plaza Tetuan and used for rotating exhibitions, talks, workshops, and cultural programmes. Its shows often lean toward Spanish modern art and Valencian painters, with Sorolla appearing naturally in the institution's orbit.

Because the programme changes, this is a place to check rather than blindly schedule. When the exhibition is strong, it gives the historic centre a useful indoor counterweight to churches, markets, and tiled palaces. It is also easy to combine with the nearby old town and riverbed gardens.
