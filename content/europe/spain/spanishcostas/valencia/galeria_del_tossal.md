Galeria del Tossal is a small underground exhibition space in Valencia's old centre, built around remains of the city's Moorish wall. The setting is the main draw: contemporary art shows sit beside stone arches and medieval masonry.

It is not a major museum, but it makes a good short detour if you are already exploring El Carmen or walking between La Lonja and the cathedral quarter. Check current exhibitions before making a special trip.
