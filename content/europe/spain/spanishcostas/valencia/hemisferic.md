L'Hemisferic is the eye-shaped planetarium and IMAX cinema in Valencia's City of Arts and Sciences, one of Santiago Calatrava's signature buildings in the complex. The films are the practical reason to go inside, but the building itself is the draw: a white concrete shell reflected in the surrounding pools and best appreciated as part of a slow walk through the whole Turia garden complex.
