The most famous beach restaurant in Valencia, on the Paseo Neptuno facing the Malvarrosa, in continuous operation since 1898. The standard order is **paella valenciana** (chicken, rabbit, snails, green beans) or **paella de marisco** (seafood) at lunch, cooked over wood, served in the pan with a wooden spoon.

It is large, busy, touristy in the most obvious sense, and still — when the paella arrives — undeniably good. Other beach restaurants along the same promenade (La Marcelina, La Rosa) are at least as authentic and often quieter. Reserve for weekend lunch.
