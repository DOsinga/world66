Museo del Patriarca is the small museum of the Corpus Christi Seminary, with a compact collection of 16th- and 17th-century paintings. Highlights include works connected with Juan de Juanes, El Greco, and early Flemish masters.

Visits are by guided tour, so check the schedule and reserve ahead if you need a tour in a specific language.
