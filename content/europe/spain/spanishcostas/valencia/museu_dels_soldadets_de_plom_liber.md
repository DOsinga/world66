Museu dels Soldadets de Plom L'Iber is a museum of tin soldiers housed in an old Gothic palace in Valencia. Its private collection is vast, with figures arranged to show episodes from Valencian, Spanish, and world history.

The museum is especially good for visitors who enjoy military history, miniature craft, or unusual collections. Its location near the old city sights makes it easy to combine with a walk through El Carmen and the area around La Lonja.
