Platja de l'Arbre del Gos is a more natural stretch of beach south of Valencia, near the Albufera side of the coast. It is less polished than the city's main urban beaches and includes a nudist section.

Facilities are simple but useful, with showers, drinking fountains, and picnic benches. It works best as a quieter beach stop rather than a full-service resort day.
