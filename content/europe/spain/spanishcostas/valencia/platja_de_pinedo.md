Platja de Pinedo is one of the closest beaches to Valencia, with a more urban feel than the wilder sands farther south toward Albufera. Cafes, snack vendors, showers, and accessible facilities make it practical for an easy half day by the sea.

It is not the quietest beach on this coast, but convenience is its strength. The northern end allows dogs, so choose your stretch of sand according to the kind of beach day you want.
