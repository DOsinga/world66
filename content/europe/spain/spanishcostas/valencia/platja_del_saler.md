Platja d'El Saler is a broad beach south of Valencia, backed by dunes and pine woods on the edge of Albufera Natural Park. It is one of the area's better-developed natural beaches, with showers, drinking fountains, picnic tables, and seasonal loungers while still feeling less urban than the city beaches.
