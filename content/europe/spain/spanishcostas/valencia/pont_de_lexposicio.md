Pont de l'Exposició is one of Santiago Calatrava's crisp white contributions to Valencia, spanning the former Turia riverbed near the Alameda. Its ribbed arch and clean lines make it instantly recognisable, especially against the green strip of the Turia gardens below.

It is a quick architectural stop rather than a destination on its own. Cross it as part of a walk through the Jardines del Turia, where Valencia's habit of turning infrastructure into public space is at its most convincing.
