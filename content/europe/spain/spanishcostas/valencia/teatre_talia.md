Teatre Talia is a historic theatre in Valencia's old city, built in 1928 and still used for local plays, dance, and occasional flamenco performances. Its location near El Carmen, La Lonja, and the cathedral makes it easy to fold into an evening in the centre.

Check the programme before going; the building is most worthwhile when you can see a performance rather than just passing the facade.
