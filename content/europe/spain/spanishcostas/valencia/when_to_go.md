---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

Valencia is comfortably visitable in any month — winter lows rarely drop below 6°C, summer highs are usually around 30°C, and the city sees 300 sunny days a year. The shoulder seasons, **April–June** and **September–October**, are the sweet spots: warm enough for the beach, not yet boiling, and not so packed with tourists.

The unmissable date is **Las Fallas**, 15–19 March. The city builds 700 enormous papier-mâché figures (the *fallas*) for a year, displays them for a week, sets off the daily Mascletà pyrotechnic show at 2 pm in the Plaza del Ayuntamiento, and on the night of 19 March burns every figure to the ground in a single fiery night called La Cremà. It is louder, more chaotic, and more cathartic than almost anything else in Europe. Book accommodation months ahead.

**August** is hot, humid, and half the city is on holiday — many of the best restaurants close for weeks. Avoid unless you want lazy beach days. The **July Feria** and the **October feria** (the city's regional holiday) are smaller but still festive.
