Torre del Castellet is the remnant of a 16th-century watchtower built to watch for Barbary pirate raids. Little more than foundations, the tower base, and rock-cut cisterns remain, but the hilltop setting gives wide views toward the Penyal d'Ifac and the Calpe coast.
