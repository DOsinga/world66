The Costa Blanca stretches down the coast of Alicante province, named for its bright light and pale sand. [Benidorm](/europe/spain/valencia/benidorm), a wall of high-rise hotels above two huge curving beaches, is its brash heart and one of Europe's biggest package-holiday resorts. Quieter pleasures lie nearby: the towering Peñón d'Ifach at [Calpe](/europe/spain/valencia/calpe), the coves and old town of [Xàbia (Jávea)](/europe/spain/valencia/javea), and the harbour town of [Dénia](/europe/spain/valencia/denia).
