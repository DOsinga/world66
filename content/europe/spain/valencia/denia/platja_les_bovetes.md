Platja Les Bovetes is one of Dénia's broad northern beaches, with sand, shallow water, and a more relaxed feel than the town-centre waterfront. It is the kind of beach that works well for families or anyone who wants a straightforward swim without hunting for a rocky cove.

In summer it has the usual Costa Blanca rhythm of umbrellas, beach bars, and parking pressure, so arrive early if you care about space. Outside peak season, the long open shore is just as good for a walk with Montgó sitting behind the town.
