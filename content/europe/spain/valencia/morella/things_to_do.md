---
title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Morella's compact size means you can cover most of the major sights on foot in a day — but it rewards a slower pace. The [castle](/europe/spain/castellon/morella/castle) is the obvious centrepiece, accessed via a steep path from the old town. The [Basilica de Santa María la Mayor](/europe/spain/castellon/morella/basilica_santa_maria) is the architectural highlight, with its extraordinary spiral stone staircase. [Walking the medieval walls](/europe/spain/castellon/morella/town_walls) is one of the best free things to do in the region.

The Els Ports Natural Park surrounding Morella offers hiking through limestone gorges, waterfalls, and dense oak forest — good for a half-day if you have a car. The area around Morella also has several historic villages within 30km that make worthwhile short excursions: Forcall and Olocau del Rey are both well-preserved examples of the same highland medieval landscape.
