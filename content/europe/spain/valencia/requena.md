Requena sits inland from Valencia, where the coastal plain gives way to Bobal vineyards and a tougher Castilian edge. Its old quarter, La Villa, is the reason to stop: a medieval knot of lanes, churches, walls, and caves cut into the soft limestone under the streets.

The [Cuevas de la Villa](/europe/spain/valencia/requena/cuevas_de_la_villa) are the town's strongest anchor. They were used as cellars, silos, shelters, and ossuaries, and they make Requena feel less like another pretty hill town and more like a place with a second city underneath it. Above ground, the [Torre del Homenaje](/europe/spain/valencia/requena/torre_del_homenaje) and [Iglesia del Salvador](/europe/spain/valencia/requena/iglesia_del_salvador) keep the medieval frame intact.

Wine gives Requena its present-day identity. The [Palacio del Cid wine museum](/europe/spain/valencia/requena/palacio_del_cid_wine_museum) is a useful introduction before you move on to the wider Utiel-Requena wine country. Requena is not a major destination on its own, but it is a good one-day detour from Valencia if caves, old stone, and local wine sound better than another city afternoon.
