Iglesia del Salvador is the most rewarding of La Villa's churches, especially for its late Gothic stonework. It was begun in the 14th century and finished in the early 16th, which fits the old quarter's layered border-town feel.

You do not need long here. Treat it as part of a slow walk through La Villa, where the church fronts, stone lanes, and cave entrances work together.
