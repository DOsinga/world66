The Valencian Community stretches down Spain's eastern Mediterranean coast, from the orange groves and rice paddies around [Valencia](/europe/spain/valencian_community/valencia) — the region's vibrant capital and birthplace of paella — to the beach resorts of the Costa Blanca in the south. It is a region of sun, sea, and festivals, with the fiery Fallas of Valencia and long sandy coastlines drawing visitors year-round.
