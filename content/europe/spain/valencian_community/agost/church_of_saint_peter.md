The parish church of Saint Peter sits in the old centre and shows the town's more formal side, with Valencian baroque details and a bell tower above the tight streets. It is the natural counterpoint to the pottery museum and potters' chapel.

Step in if it is open, but even from the square it helps anchor Agost as more than a craft stop.
