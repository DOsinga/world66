Fundacion Eberhard Schlotter is a small art foundation dedicated to the German painter Eberhard Schlotter, who lived and worked in Altea for many years. It is housed in a 17th-century residence, which gives the collection a more intimate setting than a standard gallery.

Come for a focused cultural stop in the old town rather than a large museum. The permanent collection and temporary shows are most rewarding if you already have an interest in local artists or Altea's long appeal to painters.
