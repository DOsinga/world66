Torre de Bellaguarda is a reconstructed 16th-century watchtower in Altea. It is reached from the street by a short staircase and gives a quick look at the coastal defence system that once watched over this part of the Costa Blanca.

It works well as a short stop while walking between Altea's old town lanes, viewpoints, and church square.
