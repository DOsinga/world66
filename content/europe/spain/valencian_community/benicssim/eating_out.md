---
title: Eating Out
type: section
---

Benicàssim's restaurants split sharply between the chiringuitos and beach paellerías along the seafront, where the speciality is wood-fired rice — **arroz a banda**, **arroz negro**, the local fideuá and of course paella — and the more traditional tapas bars and old-style restaurants in the village centre. As elsewhere in the Castelló province, the **citrus fruit** is excellent and the local sparkling wine, **cava del Mediterráneo**, is worth trying. For a full Valencian seafood meal of rice and fish, a long lunch at one of the beach restaurants is the local way.
