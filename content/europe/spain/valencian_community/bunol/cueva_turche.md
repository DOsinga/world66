Cueva Turche is the natural counterweight to Buñol's castle: a rock amphitheatre, a pool, and a seasonal waterfall just outside town. It is a good warm-weather stop, especially if you are walking the local water route.

The setting is small and can feel crowded in summer. Go early, take your litter back with you, and do not expect a managed resort-style swimming spot.
