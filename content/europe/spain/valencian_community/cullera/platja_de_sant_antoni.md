Platja de Sant Antoni is Cullera's main town beach: long, sandy, and backed by the services you expect from a Spanish family resort. It is not secluded, but it is easy.

Come here when you want the practical version of the Mediterranean: a swim, lunch nearby, and a short walk back into town instead of a complicated beach expedition.
