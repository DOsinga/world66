Torre de Santa Ana, also called Torre de la Reina Mora, is a 13th-century tower that once formed part of the Moorish castle's defensive perimeter. It was later altered to help defend the coast against Barbary pirate raids.

The restored tower now includes a small exhibit on the history of the tower and castle, and pairs naturally with Castell de Cullera above town.
