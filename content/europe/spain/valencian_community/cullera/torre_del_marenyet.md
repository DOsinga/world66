The Torre del Marenyet was built in 1577 as part of the watchtower network that guarded this coast from Barbary pirates. It is plain, sturdy, and easy to understand: a lookout post from a time when a sail on the horizon could mean trouble.

It works best as a small stop after the castle or Dragut cave, especially if you are following the Júcar south of town.
