The Ermita de Sant Joan is a small hermitage on the slopes leading toward the Montgo massif. It is modest, but its rural setting and simple architecture make it a good low-key stop for travellers exploring Denia beyond the castle, marina, and beaches.

Visit it as part of a walk or drive through the scattered countryside around Montgo. The hermitage gives a glimpse of older religious life in the area, where small chapels marked routes, fields, and seasonal gatherings rather than grand urban ceremony.
