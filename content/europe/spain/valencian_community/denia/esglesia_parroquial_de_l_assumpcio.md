Església Parroquial de l'Assumpció is Dénia's 18th-century parish church, close to the old town and castle. Its main interest is inside, where Baroque altarpieces add a richer note to an otherwise compact historic centre.

It is not a destination on the scale of Dénia Castle, but it is worth a look while wandering the streets below the fortress, especially if the doors are open.
