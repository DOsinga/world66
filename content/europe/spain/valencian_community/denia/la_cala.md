La Cala is one of Denia's small southern coves, a very different proposition from the long sandy beaches north of town. The appeal is clear water, rocks, and a more tucked-away feeling along the coast toward Les Rotes.

It is best for confident swimmers and people who want a short coastal stop rather than a full beach day. Bring water shoes, check sea conditions, and combine it with the Les Rotes promenade or a drive toward Montgo if you want Denia's rocky side rather than its resort front.
