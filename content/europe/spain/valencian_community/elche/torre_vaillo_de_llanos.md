Torre Vaillo de Llanos is a restored watchtower from the late 15th or early 16th century, built as part of Elche's defensive network. The city now uses it for temporary exhibitions.

Visitors can climb the tower for a view over the surrounding palm groves, making it a compact historic stop with a clear sense of place.
