Platja de L'Ahuir is the quieter, more natural end of Gandia's beach line, with dunes and vegetation instead of the dense resort feel of Platja del Nord. It is the beach to choose when you want more space and a less managed stretch of sand.

Part of the beach is used by nudists, and the dune system gives it a wilder feel than the main urban waterfront.
