Cap de la Nau is a steep rocky headland east of Jávea, marked by a lighthouse and wide views over the Mediterranean. In clear weather the view can reach toward Ibiza, and the cliffs shelter sea caves below, including the Cova dels Orgues, which is reached by boat.
