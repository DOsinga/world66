Creu del Portitxol is a cliff-top cross across the bay from Illa de Portitxol. The spot has sea views and a small archaeological footnote: Roman amphoras and other finds from the 3rd and 4th centuries have been discovered nearby.

It is a short, scenic stop for travellers exploring Javea's coves and headlands.
