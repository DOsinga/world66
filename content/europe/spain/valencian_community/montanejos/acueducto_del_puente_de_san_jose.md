The Acueducto del Puente de San José is a picturesque stone bridge over the Montán River. Built in 1803 and restored in 2008, it is one of the small historic structures that rewards a slow walk around Montanejos.

It works best as part of a wider stroll through the village and riverside area rather than as a standalone destination.
