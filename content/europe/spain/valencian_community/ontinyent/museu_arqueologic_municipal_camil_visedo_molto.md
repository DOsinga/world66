The Museu Arqueologic Municipal Camil Visedo Molto is housed in a historic building with Gothic and Renaissance elements. Its collection focuses on local archaeology, with a strong group of Iberian ceramics.

It is a modest museum, but useful if you want to understand Ontinyent and the surrounding Valencian interior as more than a pretty old town.
