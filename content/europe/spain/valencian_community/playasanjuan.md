Playa San Juan is a small fishing village on the southwest coast of Tenerife, between [Los Gigantes](/europe/spain/canaryislands/losgigantes) and [Playa de las Américas](/europe/spain/canaryislands/lasamericas). It has managed to keep much of its local character while the rest of the coast was swallowed by resort development. The [harbour](/europe/spain/valencian_community/playasanjuan/harbour) still has working fishing boats, and the seafood restaurants along the waterfront serve what came in that morning.

The [beach](/europe/spain/valencian_community/playasanjuan/playa_san_juan_beach) is modest — a strip of dark volcanic sand sheltered by a breakwater — but the village is a pleasant base for anyone who wants a quieter alternative to the big tourist centres. The [promenade](/europe/spain/valencian_community/playasanjuan/promenade) is good for an evening walk, and boats leave from the harbour for [whale and dolphin watching](/europe/spain/valencian_community/playasanjuan/whale_watching) trips along the coast. Los Gigantes and the Masca valley are an easy drive north.
