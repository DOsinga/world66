This municipal wine museum occupies the Palacio del Cid, a historic house in La Villa. It is a modest stop, but useful if you want Requena to read as a wine town rather than just a medieval one.

The displays cover local vineyard work, cellar tools, and the culture around Utiel-Requena wines. Visit before a bodega tasting and the surrounding landscape makes more sense.
