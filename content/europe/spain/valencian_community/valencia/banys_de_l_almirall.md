Banys de l'Almirall is a rare surviving medieval bathhouse in Valencia, built in 1313 after the Christian reconquest but using Arabic-style bath architecture. It remained in use as a bathhouse into the 20th century, which makes it one of the city's more unusual historic interiors.

It is a compact stop near the cathedral area and pairs well with Valencia's older archaeological and Moorish sites.
