Centre Cultural la Beneficencia occupies a restored 19th-century former hospice in Valencia's old centre. The complex now works as a cultural centre with two museums and a changing programme of temporary exhibitions.

It is a useful stop in El Carmen, especially when paired with IVAM, the street art nearby, and the older sights around Torres de Quart.
