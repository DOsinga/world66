The Museo Histórico Militar de Valencia is a specialist museum, but a useful one if you want a different angle on the city from markets, ceramics, and modern architecture. Its collections cover uniforms, flags, weapons, models, and documents linked to Spanish military history and Valencia's role as a garrison city.

The displays are old-fashioned rather than immersive, so it works best for travellers with a real interest in military history. It is close enough to the central districts to fold into a broader walk, especially on a day when you want a quieter museum away from the busiest sights.
