Mirador Valencia is a rooftop viewpoint in the old centre, set above a private social club. The draw is simple: a 360-degree look across the compact historic core, with the cathedral, market roofs, and surrounding streets laid out below.

It is a short stop rather than a major sight, but useful if you want a quick orientation point near the Silk Exchange and Mercado Central. Check access before going, as rooftop viewpoints can change hours or entry rules.
