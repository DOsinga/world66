MuVIM, the Valencian Museum of Enlightenment and Modernity, focuses on culture, ideas, and visual communication. Its temporary exhibitions often cover subjects such as graphic design, photography, cinema, and civic life.

The building and garden, designed by Guillermo Vázquez Consuegra, add to the visit. It is a useful central museum stop near Valencia's old town markets and civic squares.
