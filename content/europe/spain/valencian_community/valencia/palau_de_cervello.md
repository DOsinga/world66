Palau de Cervello is an 18th-century noble residence that became Valencia's royal guest house after the old royal palace was demolished. Today it houses the Municipal Archive and a small museum, useful for understanding the city's Bourbon-era politics as well as for seeing one of the old centre's more restrained palace interiors.
