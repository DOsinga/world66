Pont de la Trinitat is Valencia's oldest bridge, with the current stone structure dating from the early 16th century. Two 17th-century sculptures of Valencian saints stand on the bridge after being moved here from another medieval crossing.

It is a small but worthwhile detail on a walk along the Turia gardens or between the old town and the Museu de Belles Arts.
