Pont de l'Assut de l'Or is a cable-stayed bridge by Santiago Calatrava in Valencia's City of Arts and Sciences district. Its leaning white pylon rises to 125 m, making it one of the most visible modern structures in the city.

It works best as part of a walk through the Turia gardens and the surrounding Calatrava buildings.
