Abisko and Björkliden sit on the Kiruna-Narvik railway in far northern [Sweden](/europe/sweden), above the Arctic Circle and beside the long cold sweep of Lake Torneträsk. It is a small settlement pair, but the setting is huge: birch forest, open fell, the U-shaped notch of Lapporten, and some of the clearest winter skies in Swedish Lapland.

[Abisko National Park](/europe/sweden/abisko_and_bjorkliden/abisko_national_park) is the anchor. The park begins near Abisko Turiststation, where the [Kungsleden](/europe/sweden/abisko_and_bjorkliden/kungsleden_trailhead) starts its long southward route through the mountains. In summer, even short walks reach canyon, lake, and fell views; in winter, snow and polar night turn the same area into one of Scandinavia's best aurora bases.

The [Aurora Sky Station](/europe/sweden/abisko_and_bjorkliden/aurora_sky_station) above Mount Nuolja gives Abisko its winter reputation, while [Björkliden](/europe/sweden/abisko_and_bjorkliden/bjorkliden) adds skiing and one of the classic views over Torneträsk toward Lapporten. If you are already in Kiruna or Narvik and have one spare day, this is the mountain detour to choose.
