Abisko National Park is one of Sweden's most accessible Arctic landscapes. It runs from the shore of Torneträsk up into fell country near the Norwegian border, with trails starting close to the railway.

The park is strong in every season. Summer brings canyon walks, Kungsleden departures, and midnight sun; winter brings snowshoeing, skiing, and unusually reliable chances of clear sky for the northern lights.
