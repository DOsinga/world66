Aurora Sky Station sits high on Mount Nuolja, reached by chairlift from Abisko Turiststation. Its appeal is practical as much as romantic: the elevation, dark sky, and Abisko's dry microclimate give visitors a better chance of seeing the northern lights than most places in the region.

Book ahead in winter and dress as if you will be standing still in the Arctic, because you will be. Clear weather matters more than any brochure promise.
