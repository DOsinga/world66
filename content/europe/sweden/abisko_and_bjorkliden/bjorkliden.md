Björkliden is the quieter mountain-resort half of this area, set above Torneträsk with wide views toward Lapporten. In winter it has downhill skiing and easy access to snowy fell country; in summer it works as a base for walks and viewpoints.

It is not large, which is part of the appeal. Come for the mountain setting rather than nightlife or urban services.
