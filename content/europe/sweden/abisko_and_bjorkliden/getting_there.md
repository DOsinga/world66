---
title: Getting There
type: section
---

The railway between [Kiruna](/europe/sweden/kiruna) and Narvik is the simplest way in, with stops at Abisko Östra, Abisko Turiststation, and Björkliden. Long-distance trains connect from Stockholm and other Swedish cities, while regional trains link the Lapland towns.

By road, the E10 follows the same corridor between Kiruna and Narvik. Winter driving is serious here; use a properly equipped car and watch the forecast.
