Kungsleden, the King's Trail, starts at Abisko Turiststation and runs south through some of Sweden's finest mountain country. Serious walkers continue for days or weeks, but the first kilometres already give a taste of birch forest, river, and open fell.

Even a short out-and-back makes sense if you are not equipped for a hut-to-hut trek. Check season, snow, and river conditions before planning anything longer.
