Lapporten is the great visual signature of the area: a broad U-shaped valley cutting the skyline south of Torneträsk. You see it from many places around Abisko and Björkliden, but the views from Björkliden are especially clean.

It is more viewpoint than attraction, and that is fine. In clear weather, the shape of the valley gives the whole landscape its sense of scale.
