---
title: "Getting There"
type: section
---

**Arvidsjaur Airport** (AJR), 13 km south of town, has scheduled flights to Stockholm Arlanda — usually two or three daily in season. It also handles a substantial volume of winter charter flights from Germany and elsewhere, brought in by the car-testing industry.

By road, the **E45** (Inlandsvägen) runs through town from south to north. The drive from Stockholm is about 11 hours; from Östersund 5 hours; from the Norwegian border (Mo i Rana) around 4 hours.

In summer, the **Inlandsbanan railway** stops at Arvidsjaur on its long run between Östersund and Gällivare — a slow but classic journey through Lapland forest.
