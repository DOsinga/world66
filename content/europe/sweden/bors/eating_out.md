---
title: "Eating Out"
type: section
---

Borås's eating scene punches above its weight thanks to the textile-design crowd and the student population — there is more good cooking here than in most cities its size. The best concentration is along Allégatan and around Stora Torget in the centre, and a small cluster has grown up around the Textile Museum in Simonsland.

**Restaurang Vävaren**, inside the Simonsland complex near the Textile Museum, does a confident modern Swedish menu — venison, char, root vegetables, lingonberry — in a high-ceilinged former mill hall. **Tapasbaren Gnista** on Sandgärdsgatan is a long-standing favourite for Spanish small plates and a long wine list. **Kashmir** is the pick of the city's Indian restaurants, and **Pinchos** has a busy branch on Stora Torget if you want a casual quick dinner.

For coffee and cakes, **Nordstans Konditori** is the institution — a 1920s café on Allégatan with marble tables and the city's best almond pastry.
