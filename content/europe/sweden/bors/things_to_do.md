Borås is small enough to walk in a morning and large enough to keep you busy for a long weekend if you have an interest in art, design, or industrial history. The big draws are the trio of museums in repurposed textile mills along the Viskan river, the open-air sculpture trail (No Limit Street Art Borås) that scatters murals through the centre, and a zoo on the north edge of town that is among the largest in Scandinavia.
