---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

Borås is best from **late May to early September**, when the parks are in leaf, the river paths are usable, and the city's outdoor café terraces are open. Summer days this far north stretch on past 10pm; the city stays awake with them.

The **Borås International Sculpture Biennial** runs every two summers and is a good excuse to time a visit — the temporary installations are scattered across the city centre alongside the permanent collection. The **No Limit street-art festival** typically takes place in early September and is when new murals go up; if you want to watch them being painted, that is the week to be in town.

Winters are dark, damp, and cold rather than dramatically snowy — Borås is too close to the Atlantic to get much of the deep frozen weather of inland Sweden. The museums and indoor sights make a visit perfectly viable in any season; just expect short days and grey light from November through February.
