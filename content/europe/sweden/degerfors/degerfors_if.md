Degerfors IF is the town's football club and, for many residents, its primary source of civic pride. Founded in 1907, the club has spent most of its history in the lower tiers of Swedish football but has punched above its weight repeatedly — reaching the top division (Allsvenskan) multiple times and maintaining a passionate local following in a town of fewer than eight thousand people.

Home games at Stora Valla stadium draw a high proportion of the local population. If you are passing through Degerfors on a match day, attending a game — especially if Degerfors IF is in Allsvenskan that season — gives you a sense of what Swedish small-town football culture looks like: intimate, knowledgeable, and genuinely invested. Stora Valla holds around 8,000 spectators and sits right in the town.
