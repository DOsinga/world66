The Engelsberg–Norberg Railway is a heritage line that follows the route once used to carry iron ore through the Bergslagen mining country. Run by enthusiasts of the Engelsbergs Norberg Järnvägshistoriska Förening (ENJ), the railway offers occasional steam and diesel-hauled excursions, typically in summer.

For visitors who want to understand the full picture of how the Bergslagen iron industry was connected — from the forests where charcoal was burned, through the ironworks, to the markets beyond — a ride on this heritage line adds another dimension to the Engelsberg experience. Check the ENJ website for current timetables, as services are not daily.
