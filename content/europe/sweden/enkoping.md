Enköping is a small city west of [Uppsala](/europe/sweden/uppsala), close enough to Stockholm and Västerås to be easy by train, but with a quieter Uppland character of parks, lake-country estates, and old trade routes. It calls itself Sweden's nearest city, which is marketing, but the geography helps: roads, rail, and Lake Mälaren all meet here.

The city is best when treated as a gentle day out rather than a blockbuster. Start with the parks and local museum in the centre, then use a car or summer boat to reach the countryside. The strong anchors are outside the main streets: [Boglösa's rock carvings](/europe/sweden/enkoping/boglosa_rock_carvings), [Grönsö Manor](/europe/sweden/enkoping/gronso_manor), and [Ekolsund Castle](/europe/sweden/enkoping/ekolsund_castle) give Enköping a much older and grander story than its modern size suggests.

This is not a place to rush through a checklist. Come if you like Swedish small towns, gardens, Bronze Age remains, and manor-house landscapes around Lake Mälaren. If you are already in Uppsala, Västerås, or Stockholm and want a lower-key excursion, Enköping clears that bar.
