Enköping Museum is the sensible first stop if you want the town to make sense before heading into the countryside. It covers the city's medieval trade role, local industry, military history, and the surrounding Uppland landscape.

It is a compact museum, not a destination by itself, but it helps turn Enköping from "pleasant small town" into a place with context. Combine it with a walk through the central parks.
