Kvekgården is an open-air farm museum showing rural life in 19th-century Uppland. The preserved farm buildings give a compact look at traditional construction, domestic life, and farm work in the countryside outside Enköping.

Visits are usually by booked tour, so check ahead rather than treating it as a drop-in museum.
