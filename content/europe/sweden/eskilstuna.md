Eskilstuna is an old industrial city on the river between Lake Hjälmaren and Lake Mälaren. It began as a religious centre around Saint Eskil, then grew into one of Sweden's great metalworking towns, known as Stålstaden, the Steel City.

That history is still easy to see. The [Rademacher Forges](/europe/sweden/eskilstuna/rademacher_forges) preserve a 17th-century smithing quarter in red wooden buildings, while the [Munktell Museum](/europe/sweden/eskilstuna/munktell_museum) turns old factories and heavy machines into the city's strongest indoor attraction. Families often add [Parken Zoo](/europe/sweden/eskilstuna/parken_zoo), which mixes animals, rides, and pools in one big day out.

Eskilstuna is not Sweden at its prettiest, and that is part of the appeal. It is a working city with enough history, museums, and riverside walking to make a spare day from Stockholm or Västerås feel well spent.
