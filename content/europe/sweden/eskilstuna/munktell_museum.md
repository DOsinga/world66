The Munktell Museum is a hands-on industrial museum in the old factory district, built around the machines and engineering history that made Eskilstuna a Swedish manufacturing name. The collection includes tractors, engines, construction machines, and early mechanical oddities tied to Theofron Munktell and later Volvo CE.

It is a strong stop even if you are not normally a machinery person. The scale of the vehicles, the old industrial buildings, and the child-friendly exhibits make the story easy to grasp.
