Munktellbadet is Eskilstuna's modern public bath complex, opened in 2016. It has swimming pools, a family bathing area, a relaxation area, and a gym.

It is a practical rainy-day or family stop rather than a historic sight, but it is one of the city's main visitor-facing indoor recreation venues.
