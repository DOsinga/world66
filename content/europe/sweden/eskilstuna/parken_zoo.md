Parken Zoo is Eskilstuna's big family attraction, west of the centre. It combines a zoo, amusement rides, and summer pools, so it can absorb most of a day if you are travelling with children.

The animal collection is modest by international standards but varied for Sweden, and the extra rides make it more than a quick zoo visit. Check the season before going, because the full offer is summer-weighted.
