---
title: "Getting There"
type: section
---

Falkenberg sits on the E6 motorway and the West Coast Line railway between [Gothenburg](/europe/sweden/gteborg) and Malmö. By car it is about an hour south of Gothenburg, two hours north of Malmö, two and a half hours north of Copenhagen.

The fastest trains stop at the modern Falkenberg railway station (Falkenberg Stn), which is actually 5 km out of town at Skogstorp. A regular shuttle bus links the station to the centre. Older slower trains stop at the old town station closer to the centre but are less frequent. The nearest airport is **Gothenburg Landvetter (GOT)**, about 90 minutes' drive north.
