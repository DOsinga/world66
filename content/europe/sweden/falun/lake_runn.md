Lake Runn gives Falun its easy outdoor edge: a broad Dalarna lake with islands, boating, swimming, and long views beyond the town. In summer it is a place for kayaks, docks, and lazy shoreline time.

Winter changes the personality completely. When conditions are right, ploughed skating routes turn the frozen lake into one of the region's best cold-weather playgrounds, making Runn useful in a Falun itinerary well beyond the mining heritage.
