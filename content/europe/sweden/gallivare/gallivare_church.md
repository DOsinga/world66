---

The current wooden church dates from 1882, replacing an earlier chapel from 1747. The interior is plain — a single nave, wooden pews, a painted altarpiece — but it is one of the older Christian sites in this part of Lapland, and a quiet place to step into away from the bustle of the town centre.

In the churchyard you can see a couple of Sami grave markers as well as the standard Lutheran headstones.
