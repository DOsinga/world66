Delsjon Canoeing opens up one of Gothenburg's best near-city landscapes: wooded lakes, rocky shores, and calm water close enough for a half-day escape. It is a reminder that the city is not only trams, seafood, and brick streets, but also quick access to Swedish outdoor life.

Pick a settled day and give yourself time to paddle without rushing. The outing works especially well in summer, when a swim, picnic, or walk in the reserve can turn a rental into an easy afternoon outside the centre.
