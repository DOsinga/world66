Gothenburg Mosque is a visible modern religious landmark on Hisingen, set near the river rather than hidden in the city centre. Its dome and minaret mark the growth of Gothenburg's contemporary Muslim community and add another layer to the city's port-side identity.

It is an active mosque, so visitors should be respectful and check access rather than assuming tourist hours. The site is most relevant for travellers interested in modern religious architecture and multicultural Gothenburg.
