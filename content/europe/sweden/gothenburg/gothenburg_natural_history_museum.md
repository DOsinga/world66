Gothenburg Natural History Museum is a classic natural-history museum next to Slottsskogen, known for animal specimens, geology, and the kind of old-school displays that still fascinate children and adults. It adds an indoor educational stop to the city's big park district.

Combine it with Slottsskogen, the botanical garden, or nearby Linne. The museum is especially useful on wet days, when Gothenburg's outdoor plans need a backup.
