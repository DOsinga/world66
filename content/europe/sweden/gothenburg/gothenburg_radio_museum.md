Gothenburg Radio Museum is a specialist collection of radio and broadcasting equipment, from early crystal sets and vacuum tubes to police radios, televisions, and technical manuals. It is the sort of museum that rewards curiosity about how communication technology used to work.

It is not a broad city highlight, but for visitors interested in electronics, engineering, or broadcasting history it is a worthwhile detour near the riverfront.
