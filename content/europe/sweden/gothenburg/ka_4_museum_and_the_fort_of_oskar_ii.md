The KA 4 Museum and Fort of Oskar II sit on Gothenburg's western defensive edge, where the city meets the skerries and shipping channels. The site tells the story of coastal artillery and the military planning that once guarded Sweden's second city from the sea.

This is a specialist but worthwhile trip if forts, military history, or harbour geography interest you. It also gives a different reason to head west from central Gothenburg, beyond the usual archipelago ferries and seafood stops.
