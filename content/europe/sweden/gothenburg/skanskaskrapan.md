Skanskaskrapan is one of Gothenburg's most recognisable modern landmarks, a red-and-white tower by the river that locals nicknamed "Läppstiftet" - the Lipstick. It is not subtle, but it gives the old port city a useful vertical marker near the business district and harbour approaches.

The tower matters most as part of the waterfront skyline. If public access to the upper levels is available when you visit, the view helps you understand Gothenburg's spread along the Göta älv; otherwise, treat it as an architectural waypoint while walking between the station, riverfront, and newer harbour developments.
