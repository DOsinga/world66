Trädgårdsföreningen is one of central Gothenburg's easiest pleasures: a 19th-century garden park close to the station, the canal, and the main shopping streets. Its lawns, rose garden, cafes, and glass Palm House make it more structured than an ordinary city park without feeling formal or remote.

The park works in almost any Gothenburg itinerary because it is central and free to wander. In warm weather it is a place to slow down between museums and neighbourhood walks; in colder months the Palm House gives the visit a sheltered focus.
