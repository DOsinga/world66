Galgberget — literally Gallows Hill — is a limestone promontory just outside the northern city wall with views over the sea and back towards Visby. It is a nature reserve with characteristic Gotland alvar: flat limestone pavement colonised by orchids, sedums, and other plants adapted to the thin, dry soil. The walking paths through the reserve are easy.

The hill is a good 30-minute walk from the city centre through the northern gate and along the coast. The combination of the historical associations, the alvar landscape, and the coastal views makes it worth the detour — and it gives a sense of the open limestone countryside that covers much of the island outside Visby.
