Lickershamn has a small rauk field, but its standout is Jungfrun, the largest rauk on Gotland. The limestone sea stack stands about 500 metres from the village and gives this stretch of coast a sharper profile than the surrounding shore.

It is a good stop if you are tracing Gotland's raukar beyond the better-known fields, or if you want a short coastal walk with a clear natural landmark.
