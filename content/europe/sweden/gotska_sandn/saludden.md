Säludden is the seal-watching corner of Gotska Sandön, a remote national-park island that already feels far from ordinary Sweden. A viewing hide just inland lets visitors watch seals hauled out near the shore without crowding them or spoiling the reason they come here.

The pleasure is in patience. Bring binoculars, keep quiet, and accept that the island sets the pace. The walk, the sand, the wind-bent pines, and the chance of seeing seals together make Säludden one of Gotska Sandön's clearest rewards.
