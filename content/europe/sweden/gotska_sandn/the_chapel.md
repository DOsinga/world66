The chapel on Gotska Sandön is a small marker of human presence on an island better known for sand, pine forest, seals, and isolation. In such a remote national park setting, even a modest wooden chapel takes on weight: it speaks of lighthouse keepers, seasonal workers, shipwrecks, and the need for community far from the mainland.

Visit it as part of a wider walk rather than as a standalone monument. The pleasure is in the contrast between the simple building and the wild Baltic island around it, where nature is clearly the stronger force.
