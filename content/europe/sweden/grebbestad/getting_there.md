---
title: Getting There
type: section
---

Grebbestad is about 120 km north of Gothenburg on the Bohuslan coast. By car, take the E6 motorway north to Tanumshede and then follow smaller roads west to the coast — the drive takes about 90 minutes from the city. There is no direct public transport; the nearest train station is at Tanumshede, about 10 km away, with connections to Gothenburg and Oslo. From Tanumshede, taxis or local buses cover the final stretch in summer.
