---
title: Things to Do
type: section
---

Grebbestad's pleasures are outdoor and maritime. Oyster safaris, sea kayaking among the granite skerries, swimming from smooth rock shelves, and walking the coastal path are the main activities. The Iron Age grave field at Greby is the main historical sight within easy reach. In summer the harbour fills with boats and the handful of restaurants do brisk business — this is a place Swedes go to eat oysters and decompress.
