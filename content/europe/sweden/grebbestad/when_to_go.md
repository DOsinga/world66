---
title: When to Go
type: section
---

Grebbestad is primarily a summer destination. June through August is when the village is fully alive — restaurants open, oyster safaris run daily, the harbour fills with boats, and the sea is warm enough for swimming. July is peak season and accommodation books up well in advance; if you can come in June or early September, you will find the same landscape with fewer crowds.

Outside summer, Grebbestad is very quiet. Most restaurants close entirely from October to April. The coastline in autumn and winter has a stark beauty, and the Greby grave field is worth visiting year-round, but the village itself has little to offer the visitor once the season ends.
