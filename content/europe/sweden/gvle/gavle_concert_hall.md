Gävle Concert Hall is the city’s main serious music venue and the home stage of the Gävle Symphony Orchestra. The building sits by the Gavleån river and is worth noticing even if you are not attending a performance: its glassy, late-20th-century form gives the waterfront a sharper cultural edge than the old town alone.

For travellers, the best reason to come is an evening concert. Programming ranges from symphonic work to touring artists, and ticket prices are often gentler than in the big Scandinavian capitals. If you have a free night in Gävle, checking the calendar here is more rewarding than treating the city as only a stopover.
