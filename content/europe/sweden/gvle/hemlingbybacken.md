Hemlingbybacken is the ski slope in Gävle's Hemlingby outdoor area, a practical local hill rather than a grand mountain resort. In winter it gives the city an easy place for downhill practice, snow play, and short sessions close to home.

Outside the ski season, the wider Hemlingby area is useful for walking, running, and low-key forest recreation. It is best understood as a local outdoors hub: modest, accessible, and very Swedish in its everyday relationship with nature.
