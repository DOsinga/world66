The Regional Art and History Museum is Gavle's county museum and art gallery. Its collections cover regional history and art, with a building financed in 1933 by a tobacco and shipping magnate.

It is a good indoor counterpoint to Gamla Gefle and the railway museum, especially for travellers who want more context on the city and surrounding county.
