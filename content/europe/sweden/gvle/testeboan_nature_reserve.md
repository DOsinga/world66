Testeboån Nature Reserve protects a stretch of river landscape north of central Gävle, with forest, rapids, wetlands, and walking paths close to the city. It is a good reminder that northern Swedish nature does not require a remote trip to feel present.

The reserve suits a gentle walk, birdwatching, or a quiet break from town. Conditions vary with season and water level, so expect a natural area rather than a manicured park.
