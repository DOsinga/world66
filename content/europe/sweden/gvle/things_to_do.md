Gävle punches above its size for attractions. The [Swedish Railway Museum](/europe/sweden/gvle/swedish_railway_museum) is the best of its kind in Scandinavia; the [Gävle Goat](/europe/sweden/gvle/gavle_goat) is a piece of absurdist Swedish folk tradition with a devoted international following; [Gamla Gefle](/europe/sweden/gvle/gamla_gefle) is one of the few intact wooden town districts left in Sweden; and the [Prison Museum](/europe/sweden/gvle/prison_museum) is a genuinely worthwhile stop. Add a hockey game at the [Brynäs arena](/europe/sweden/gvle/brynas_arena) if the season is right, and you have a full day or two.

Furuvik, an amusement park and zoo 8km south of the city, is particularly good for families — the combination of a working amusement park with a proper zoo (including great apes) in a beech forest is unusual anywhere in Scandinavia.
