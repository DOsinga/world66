Bothnian Bay National Park protects a scattered group of low islands in the northern Gulf of Bothnia. The landscape is open and spare, with birdlife, seals, and views across brackish northern sea.

Visits depend on boat access and weather, so it suits travelers who plan ahead rather than a casual town stroll. In summer it is a strong nature outing from the Haparanda and Tornio area.
