The café inside Dunkers Kulturhus has the best outdoor table situation in Helsingborg: a long terrace right on the harbour edge with unobstructed views across to Denmark. On a clear day you can watch the ForSea ferries arriving and departing every few minutes while drinking a coffee. The kitchen does a decent lunch of soups, open sandwiches, and daily specials. Worth stopping in even if you are not visiting the museum.
