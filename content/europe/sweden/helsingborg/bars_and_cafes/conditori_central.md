A proper Swedish konditori a block from Stortorget, Conditori Central does the fika ritual correctly: good coffee, cinnamon buns that smell right, and a calm room with enough space that you are not jostling for a seat. The afternoon cake counter is worth arriving early for. A straightforward, reliable place that does not try to be a café-bar hybrid.
