Ramlösa Brunnspark is the historic spa park south of Helsingborg, associated with the mineral water that made the Ramlösa name known across Sweden. The old spring environment, mature trees, and surviving spa buildings give the park a different tone from the city's waterfront sights.

It is a gentle place for a walk rather than a high-drama attraction. The interest comes from the layered spa history and the contrast between the quiet parkland and the industrial and suburban areas around it.
