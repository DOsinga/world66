A straightforward French bistro on a side street off Stortorget, La Petite has been feeding Helsingborg for decades with honest steak frites, moules marinières, and a short wine list that does not try to be more than it is. The room is small and noisy when full, and the staff are seasoned enough not to rush you. Lunch specials are good value.
