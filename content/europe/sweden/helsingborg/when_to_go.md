---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

The best time to visit Helsingborg is late May to early June, when the rhododendrons at Sofiero Palace are at their peak. The garden is at its most spectacular in this narrow window, and the days are long enough (18+ hours of daylight in June) to make the most of the city and the coastline.

Summer (June to August) is the busiest season: the waterfront fills with outdoor cafes, the ferry crossing to Denmark is popular with day-trippers, and the beaches north and south of the city are in use. The weather is mild rather than hot — temperatures of 20–25°C are typical, with cool evenings. Book accommodation ahead for July.

Spring and autumn are quieter and often pleasant: the light on the strait can be beautiful, and the city feels more like itself without the summer crowds. Winters are grey and cold, with short days, but Helsingborg has enough indoor cultural life — the symphony orchestra, the theatres, the museums — to hold a visit together.

The H+ festival in late summer, focused on urban culture and music, attracts visitors from across Scania.
