The Stadspark (Town Park) runs along the lakeshore south of the harbour. It was laid out in the spa era of the late 19th century, when Hjo briefly competed with the larger Swedish resorts for summer guests. The grounds keep the gentle layout of that period: paths winding under tall trees, a pavilion, and views across the water to Vättern's far shore.

The wooden Stora Hotellet (now restored and used for events) and the bandstand are the surviving spa-era set pieces. The park is the obvious place for a picnic, a swim from the small beach, or simply a slow walk to look at Vättern. In summer there are still occasional outdoor concerts.
