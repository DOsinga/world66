---
title: When to Go
type: section
---

Hjo is a summer town. The steamer Trafik runs from late May to early September, the harbour cafes are open, and the lake is just about warm enough to swim from mid-July onward. Midsummer (around 21 June) and the annual harbour week in early July are the busy peaks; book accommodation well ahead if you intend to come then.

May and September are quieter and prettier: the wooden town looks its best in low autumn light, and the long northern evenings linger well into the night. Winter is cold and dark — the town closes down for most practical purposes from November to March, though there is occasional skating on the bay when conditions allow.
