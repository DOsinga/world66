A reliable waterfront cafe near the Match Museum, Grännäs offers the classic Swedish fika experience: strong coffee, freshly baked cinnamon rolls, and open sandwiches (smörgås) with gravlax, smoked salmon, or shrimp. The terrace has views over the lake and fills up on sunny afternoons.

The menu is uncomplicated and well executed. Lunch specials follow the Swedish husmanskost tradition — beef stew, meatballs with lingonberry, fish of the day. Good value by local standards, and relaxed enough that you can sit for an hour over a coffee without feeling pressured to leave.
