Rosenlunds Rosarium is a seasonal rose garden east of central Jönköping, close to the lakefront and the Elmia area. In bloom it is a gentle, colourful stop rather than a major sight.

It works best as part of a walk or bike ride along Lake Vättern. Come for a pause, a bench, and a softer view of Jönköping than the traffic corridors sometimes give.
