---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

Summer (June to August) is the time to come. The lake is the focus of outdoor life, the long promenade fills up in the evenings, and daylight lasts until 10pm. Lake Vättern is cold even in summer — around 15°C — but brave swimmers do use the beaches.

The city hosts Elmia, a major trade fair and exhibition centre, which brings periodic influxes of visitors and can affect hotel availability. Spring and autumn are mild and uncrowded. Winters are cold but the city is liveable; the lake rarely freezes because of its depth.
