---
title: When to Go
type: section
---

Summer (June–August) is peak season: Öland's meadows and beaches are at their best, the castle runs its outdoor events programme, and the days are long. The island gets busy in July, especially around Midsummer. If you are coming primarily for Öland, late June and early August are less crowded than the high peak.

Spring and autumn are good for the castle and old town without the summer crowds. The Öland landscape is beautiful in late May when the alvar flowers, and in early October when the birdwatching at Ottenby is at its peak.

Winter is quiet. The town functions normally and the castle operates reduced hours, but Öland loses most of its appeal. If you are interested in the Glass District, winter works well — the glassworks are open year-round and are less visited out of season.
