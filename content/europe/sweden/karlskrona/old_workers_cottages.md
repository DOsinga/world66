The west side of Trosso has several streets of small wooden cottages once used by workers from Karlskrona's naval yard. They make a quiet, human-scale contrast to the grand military and church architecture elsewhere in town, and are best seen as part of a slow walk through Bjorkholmen and the old harbour districts.
