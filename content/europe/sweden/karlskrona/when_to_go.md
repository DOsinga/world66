---
title: When to Go
type: section
---

Karlskrona has one of the sunniest climates in Sweden — the Blekinge coast regularly records more sunshine hours than most of the country. Summers are warm and pleasant, with long evenings and temperatures in the mid-20s Celsius. June through August is the high season, when the city fills with Swedish visitors and the free archipelago ferries run at their most frequent.

Spring and early autumn are good times to visit if you want the sights without the crowds. May is particularly pleasant — the city is preparing for summer, the days are long, and the archipelago is still quiet. September brings clear light and cooler temperatures. October through December is grey and sometimes bleak, but the city remains perfectly functional and you will have the museums to yourself.

The one festival worth knowing about is Karlskrona's summer market in July, when the harbour fills with boats and the streets around Stortorget come alive. The Marinmuseum runs special events during the summer months, including sailing days on the bay.
