Alsters herrgård is a short trip east of Karlstad, overlooking Lake Vänern. The manor is best known as the birthplace of Gustaf Fröding, the Värmland poet whose work is still woven into Swedish literary memory.

Come for the setting as much as the house: parkland, lake views, walking paths, a cafe, and exhibitions make it a gentle half-day rather than a formal museum visit.
