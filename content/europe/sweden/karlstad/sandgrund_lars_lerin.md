Sandgrund Lars Lerin is Karlstad's standout cultural stop. The old dance restaurant on Sandgrundsudden now holds permanent exhibitions by Lars Lerin, one of Sweden's best-known contemporary watercolour painters.

The appeal is not just local fame. Lerin's work is quiet, exact, and often melancholy, with landscapes and interiors that reward slow looking. Pair it with Värmlands Museum next door and Karlstad suddenly feels like more than a convenient place between Stockholm and Oslo.
