---
title: Getting There
type: section
---

Kramfors has a small regional airport at **Höga Kusten Airport**, twenty kilometres south of town, with daily flights to Stockholm Arlanda. Trains run from Stockholm to **Kramfors station** on the Ådalsbanan line, taking around 6 hours.

By car, the E4 motorway runs along the coast a few kilometres east of town. From the south it is about 500 km from Stockholm; from the north, about 300 km from Umeå.
