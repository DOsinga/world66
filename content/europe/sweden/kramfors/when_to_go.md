---
title: When to Go
type: section
---

Summer — late June through August — is the only time the small museums and the visitor facilities at the High Coast are reliably open. This is also when the long, slow northern light makes the river valley spectacular; in late June the sun barely sets.

Winter is dark, cold, and quiet, with reliable snow from December onwards. It can be beautiful but most attractions close down completely. Spring (May) is unpredictable: still cold, but the days are already long.
