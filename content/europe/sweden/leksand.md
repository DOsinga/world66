*Europe > Sweden > Leksand*

Leksand sits where Lake Siljan drains into the Österdalälven, in the part of Dalarna that Sweden often uses to picture itself: red timber houses, folk dress, church boats, crispbread, and a very serious Midsummer. It is small, but it has a stronger identity than many larger towns.

The town centre is easy to handle on foot. Around the church you can visit [Hildasholm](/europe/sweden/leksand/hildasholm), [Leksands Hembygdsgårdar](/europe/sweden/leksand/leksands_hembygdsgardar), and the lakefront before heading north toward Siljansnäs for the [Naturum Dalarna](/europe/sweden/leksand/naturum_dalarna) tower. In summer, lake cruises and Leksand Sommarland pull families in; around Midsummer the place becomes one of Sweden's great folk gatherings.

Leksand is not a city-break destination in the urban sense. Its value is Dalarna in concentrated form: lake light, rural architecture, a huge parish church, and traditions that still fill a natural amphitheatre with tens of thousands of people once a year.
