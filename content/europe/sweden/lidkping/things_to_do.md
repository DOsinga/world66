Lidköping's main draw is what surrounds it: Läckö Castle to the north, Kinnekulle plateau to the east, and the broad expanse of Lake Vänern on its doorstep. The town itself has the Rörstrand porcelain heritage to explore and a neat lakeside centre worth an afternoon.
