Linkopings Slotts- & Domkyrkomuseum sits inside the old castle beside the cathedral and explains why this compact corner of Linkoping mattered for centuries. The exhibits focus on bishops, church power, and the town's role as an ecclesiastical centre from the Middle Ages onward.

It pairs naturally with the cathedral and castle exterior. On its own it is a specialist museum, but together they make the old centre much easier to understand.
