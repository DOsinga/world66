Fagelsjo Gammelgard, also known as Bortom aa, is the most remote of Ljusdal's UNESCO farmhouses. The distance is part of the appeal: by the time you reach Fagelsjo, the farm feels tied to forest roads, self-sufficient households, and the older economy of inland Halsingland.

Inside are decorated rooms, preserved furniture, and household objects that make the place feel less like a museum reconstruction than a farm whose owners stepped away and left the doors shut.
