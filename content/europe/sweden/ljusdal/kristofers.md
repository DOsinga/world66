Kristofers is the Jarsvo entry in the UNESCO Decorated Farmhouses of Halsingland group. Its appeal is more focused than the larger farmstead complexes nearby: this is a place to look closely at painted interiors, timber craft, and the way rural wealth expressed itself indoors.

Pair it with Stenegard or Järvzoo if you are based in Jarsvo. The farmhouse usually needs a tour or seasonal opening, so check times before building a day around it.
