Ludvika is an industrial town in southern Dalarna, in the heart of Bergslagen — the central Swedish mining district that drove Swedish industry for several centuries. The town is surrounded by forests and lakes, and the area still has wolves and bears, though you are unlikely to see either.

The nearby [Brunnsvik Folk High School](/europe/sweden/ludvika/brunnsvik_folk_school) has a notable history as a centre for the Swedish labour movement. The town itself has a small but well-kept [open-air museum and mining museum](/europe/sweden/ludvika/ludvika_gammelgard) that documents the Bergslagen iron industry. For visitors, the main appeal is the surrounding countryside: good walking, cycling, swimming in [Lake Väsman](/europe/sweden/ludvika/lake_vasman), and fishing in the quiet Bergslagen landscape.
