The Ludvika town hall is a clean Functionalist building completed in 1938 by architect Hakon Ahlberg, who designed many of Sweden's interwar civic buildings. Its restrained brick exterior and tall clock tower mark the centre of the modern town. Inside, the council chamber retains its original wood panelling and 1930s detailing.

It's not a destination in itself but worth a glance if you are walking through the centre — a small but typical example of Swedish modernism applied to a provincial town hall.
