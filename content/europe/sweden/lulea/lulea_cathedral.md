Luleå Cathedral stands on Storgatan in the heart of the city, a confident red-brick neo-Gothic building that has served the Diocese of Luleå since 1893. The exterior makes the most of its prominent position: the tower rises to 59 metres and can be seen from far out on the bay. Inside, the cathedral is less severe than many neo-Gothic churches — the warm brick, clear glazing, and well-proportioned nave create a welcoming space.

The cathedral is the seat of the Bishop of Luleå and one of the more northerly cathedrals in the world. Regular concerts are held here, particularly during the summer festival season, and the acoustics are excellent. It is open to visitors daily and makes a natural orientation point when exploring the city centre.
