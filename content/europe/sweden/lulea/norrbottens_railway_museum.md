Norrbottens Railway Museum explains Lulea's role at the southern end of the Iron Ore Line and the wider rail network that opened northern Sweden to mining and industry. The collection includes railway equipment and exhibits tied to the region's heavy transport history.

It is most appealing if you are interested in railways, mining history, or the infrastructure that shaped Norrbotten.
