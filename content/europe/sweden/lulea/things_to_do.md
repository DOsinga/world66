---
title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Luleå rewards visitors willing to engage with its Arctic character rather than resist it. The UNESCO-listed [Gammelstad Church Town](/europe/sweden/lulea/gammelstad) is the headline attraction, a perfectly preserved medieval church village ten kilometres from the centre. In the city itself, [Norrbottens Museum](/europe/sweden/lulea/norrbottens_museum) gives the best overview of the region's history and Sami culture. The [Norrbottensteatern](/europe/sweden/lulea/norrbottensteatern) presents serious theatre with a northern sensibility.

Winter activities centre on the frozen bay: the [Luleå Archipelago](/europe/sweden/lulea/lulea_archipelago) becomes accessible on foot and by car across the ice. In summer, the same archipelago is kayaking and island-hopping territory. The [Flygmuseet F21](/europe/sweden/lulea/flygmuseet_f21) south of the city has a fine collection of Swedish military aircraft. [Teknikens Hus](/europe/sweden/lulea/teknikens_hus) on the university campus is excellent for children and the scientifically curious.
