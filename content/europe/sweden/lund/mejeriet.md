Mejeriet is Lund's long-running cultural venue, set in a former dairy and used for concerts, clubs, film, theatre, and community events. In a university city where the best evenings often depend on term-time energy, it is one of the places to check first.

There is no reason to visit without an event, but with the right booking it gives a good look at local student-town nightlife beyond pubs and cafes. Check the programme in advance, since the calendar shifts between live music, stage work, and one-off festivals.
