Vikingatider is an open-air archaeological museum north-west of Lund, with reconstructed Viking houses, farm settings, workshops, and seasonal re-enactments. It is most rewarding when guided tours or craft events are running, because the appeal is in seeing how the settlement worked rather than just looking at buildings.
