title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Lövstabruk is an open-air heritage village as much as a tourist attraction. Most of what there is to see is visible just by walking through the remarkably intact ironworks settlement — the mansion, the church, the workers' houses, and the industrial core are all within easy walking distance of each other. The site is managed as a heritage area and some buildings can be visited on guided tours arranged through the local association.
