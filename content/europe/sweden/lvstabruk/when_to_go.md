---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

Lövstabruk is best visited in summer (June to August) when the mansion and some of the ironworks buildings are open for guided tours and the grounds are at their most attractive. The church may be open for services and occasional organ concerts throughout the warmer months.

In winter, the village is quiet and most interiors are closed, but the setting — a formal 18th-century estate in the snow-covered Uppland landscape — has its own appeal for those willing to explore from the outside. Spring and early autumn are pleasant for the grounds without the summer crowds.

The area around Lövstabruk is a good destination for cyclists — the flat Uppland countryside and the network of small roads between Uppsala and the coast make for scenic riding. The village itself is about 45 kilometres north of Uppsala.
