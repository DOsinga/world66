---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

The season is short and intense. From mid-June to mid-August Lysekil is a busy Swedish summer resort: full restaurants, all the boat tours running, weekend crowds from Gothenburg and Oslo. The weather is the best you will find on the Bohuslän coast, with long evenings and water just about warm enough to swim.

Outside the summer window the town quietens dramatically. Late May and early September are good compromises — most things still open, half the visitors. October to April it is a small fishing-port-with-spa: many restaurants close, the bathing pavilion runs winter sauna sessions for the brave, and storms blow in off the Skagerrak.
