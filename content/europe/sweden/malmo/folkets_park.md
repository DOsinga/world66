Folkets Park is a central Malmo park and entertainment ground, with lawns, playgrounds, food and drink, family activities, and a terrarium. In warm weather it feels more like a social backyard than a formal city park.

It is best treated as an easy local stop: bring children, meet friends, or pause here while exploring Mollevangen and the streets south of the old centre.
