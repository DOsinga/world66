Malmo Stadsbibliotek is worth a look for its architecture as much as its books. The older library building is joined to a bright modern glass wing, creating one of the city's better public interiors.

It is a calm stop between the old town and Kungsparken. Even if you are not browsing the shelves, step inside for the light, the reading rooms, and the view back toward the park.
