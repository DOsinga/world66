Slottsträdgården is an organic garden between Kungsparken and Malmöhus Castle, open through the year. Its themed beds, kitchen garden, seasonal flowers, and small volunteer-run cafe make it a gentle stop while walking the castle and park area.

It is not a major sight on its own, but it adds a pleasant, local-feeling pause to central Malmö's museum and waterfront loop.
