Teknikens och sjofartens hus is the technology and maritime branch of Malmo Museums, just west of Malmohus Castle. The collection is strongest on transport, aviation, and shipping, with cutaway models and a Swedish U3 submarine from 1943 that visitors can enter. Labels may be mostly in Swedish, but the machinery and ship displays are easy to follow.
