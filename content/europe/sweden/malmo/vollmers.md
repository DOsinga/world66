Malmö's top fine dining restaurant, holder of two Michelin stars, serving a tasting menu focused on Scandinavian ingredients interpreted with creativity and precision. The kitchen works closely with local farms and foragers, and the menu changes with the seasons — expect dishes built around things like fermented grains, preserved summer berries, and coastal shellfish from the Öresund.

The setting is a restored 19th-century townhouse on Tegelgårdsgatan, intimate rather than grand. Reservations are essential and should be made well in advance. The wine pairing is serious and well-chosen. This is destination dining — not for a quick meal but for an evening that rewards attention.
