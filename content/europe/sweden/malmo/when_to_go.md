---
title: When to Go
type: section
---

Malmö is a year-round city, but the best time to visit is May through September, when long Scandinavian summer days mean light until 10 or 11 in the evening, the beaches at Ribersborg fill up, and the outdoor terraces on Lilla Torg are in full swing.

July and August are the warmest months, with temperatures typically reaching 20–25°C. The city quiets down slightly in August when Swedes go on holiday, which can actually make it a pleasant time to visit without the crowds. June is often the best month overall — long days, warm weather, and the city fully awake after winter.

Winter in Malmö is dark and often cold, with temperatures around 0–5°C from December through February. But the city doesn't shut down: the Christmas market on Lilla Torg and Stortorget runs through December, the cultural calendar stays busy, and the proximity to Copenhagen means there is always something on. Storm-watching on the Öresund in January has its own bleak appeal.

Spring arrives early compared to the rest of Sweden — Malmö's coastal position keeps temperatures moderate, and flowers appear in the parks in March and April. Autumn (September–October) is also excellent, with warm temperatures, lower tourist numbers, and the food and wine scene in full form.
