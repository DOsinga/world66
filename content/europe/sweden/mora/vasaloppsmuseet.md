Vasaloppsmuséet in Mora tells the story of Sweden's great long-distance ski race, from heroic finishes to the odd details that build up over almost a century of competition. Exhibits cover famous skiers, race traditions, and the blueberry soup that has become part of the event's identity.

It is a natural add-on to the Vasaloppet finish area and the Zorn Museum, especially if you are visiting Mora outside race week.
