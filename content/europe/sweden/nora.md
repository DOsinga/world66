Nora is a small, well-preserved town in Örebro county, in the old Swedish mining district of Bergslagen. Its centre is a grid of cobblestoned streets lined with wooden houses from the eighteenth and nineteenth centuries, painted in the traditional deep reds and yellows. It has the feel of a place that has changed very little.

The [historic railway](/europe/sweden/nora/museum_railway) between Nora and Örebro, built in 1856 for iron ore transport and claimed to be the oldest railway in Sweden still in use, now operates as a museum line running steam and vintage diesel trains. It is a popular outing from Örebro, especially in summer. The old ironworks village of [Pershyttan](/europe/sweden/nora/pershyttan), just south of town, gives the clearest picture of what Bergslagen looked like at its industrial peak.
