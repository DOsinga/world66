title: Things to Do
type: section
---

Nora is a town for walking slowly and looking at things. The preserved wooden townscape is the experience: eighteenth-century merchant houses, painted in the deep Falun red and ochre yellows of the Swedish tradition, lining cobblestoned streets that haven't changed their layout since the town was founded in 1643. The [museum railway](/europe/sweden/nora/museum_railway) is the headline attraction for many visitors, running steam trains on Sweden's oldest surviving rail line. The surrounding landscape of [Bergslagen](/europe/sweden/nora/pershyttan) — the old iron-mining heartland — adds context to everything else.
