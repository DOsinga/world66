Arbetets Museum is Norrköping's museum of work and labour history, set in the striking Strykjärnet building in the old industrial landscape. Its exhibitions look at working life, social change, and everyday history rather than only machines or factory architecture.

The museum fits naturally with a walk through Industrilandskapet, where former mills, the river, waterfalls, museums, and cultural venues make one of the city's strongest visitor areas.
