---
title: Eating Out
type: section
---

Norrköping has a good restaurant scene for a city of its size, with the best options clustered in the Industrilandskapet and the old town. Predictably, there is strong Swedish-Nordic cooking, but the city's diverse immigrant population — significant numbers of Somali, Iraqi, and Syrian families settled here in recent decades — means decent non-European food is easier to find than in many comparable Swedish cities.
