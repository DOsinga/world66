Barnkulturcentrum is Nykoping's children's culture centre, with hands-on activities and workshops aimed at younger visitors. It is most useful for families looking for a break from castles, churches, and conventional museums.

The centre also includes Gripes modellteatermuseum, a small toy theatre museum. That makes it more distinctive than a standard playroom, especially for visitors interested in puppetry, stage models, or children's theatre.
