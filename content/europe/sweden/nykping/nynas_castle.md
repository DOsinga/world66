Nynäs Castle is a country estate on the coastal road north-east of Nyköping. In summer, visitors can tour the castle and see interiors that show how a Swedish manor house changed over several centuries.

The grounds add to the visit, with an orangery, gardens, and a cafe. It is a good day-trip stop for travellers with a car who are exploring the coast and countryside around Nyköping.
