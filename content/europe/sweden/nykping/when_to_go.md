---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

Nyköping is at its best in summer, from June to August. The waterfront comes alive, the castle grounds are pleasant to walk, and the archipelago south of town is accessible by boat. Days are long — at midsummer it barely gets dark — and the wooden old town looks its best in clear light.

Winter is cold and dark but not extreme. The town is quiet and the museum is a better reason to visit than the frozen riverbank. Spring arrives slowly: wildflower season at Labro Meadows peaks in late May and early June, which is a good time to visit if you want to avoid summer crowds.

There are no major festivals that would specifically draw a visitor to Nyköping, though the town celebrates midsummer in the usual Swedish fashion, with dancing around a maypole and general conviviality.
