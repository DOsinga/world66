---
title: "Getting Around"
type: section
---

The best way to see Öland is by **bicycle or car**. The island is 137 km long and relatively flat, which makes cycling genuinely practical — many visitors bring bikes from the mainland or hire them in Borgholm. The main road (Route 136) runs the full length from south to north, and quieter roads crisscross the interior. A full bicycle circuit of the island takes two to three days; day riders can cover one region comfortably.

**By car**, the island is easy to navigate. There is one main road and side tracks to most sights. Parking is free and plentiful at all the major attractions. A car gives you the flexibility to stop at the windmill rows and limestone plains that cyclists can reach but a bus would bypass.

**Local buses** connect Borgholm to other towns on the island, but service is infrequent and does not reach most of the archaeological sites or nature reserves. If you don't have a car or bicycle, base yourself in Borgholm and arrange day trips from there.
