The Öland Bridge is 6,072 metres long and connects the island to [Kalmar](/europe/sweden/kalmar) on the Swedish mainland. When it opened in 1972, it was the longest bridge in Europe — a record it held for several years. The crossing takes about 10 minutes by car and there is no toll.

From the bridge, particularly in clear weather, the views across the Kalmar Strait are expansive — the flat profile of Öland ahead, the mainland behind. The bridge has become something of a symbol of the island and is frequently photographed, especially at sunset when the strait turns golden. On summer Friday afternoons it can be slow with holiday traffic, but outside peak season the crossing is effortless.
