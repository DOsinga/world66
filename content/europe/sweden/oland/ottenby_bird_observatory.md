Ottenby Bird Observatory sits within Ottenby Nature Reserve at the southern tip of Oland. The area is one of the island's best birdwatching sites, with coastal meadows, migration routes, and the nearby Lange Jan lighthouse.

It is a good stop for anyone driving the length of Oland, especially in migration season when the reserve is at its most active.
