Vida Museum och Konsthall is a modern art museum and gallery on Öland, best known for glass, painting, and contemporary Swedish art. Works by Bertil and Ulrica Hydman Vallien are among the regular draws, alongside changing exhibitions.

The museum is a good bad-weather stop and a useful change of pace from Öland's beaches, windmills, and archaeological sites. Allow an hour or two, more if the temporary exhibitions are strong.
