---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

Öland is a summer island. The peak season is **June through August**, when the Swedish school holidays send families across the bridge in large numbers. The island is lively but not unpleasantly crowded during this period, and the wildflowers on the alvar are at their best in June and July.

**Spring (May)** is a beautiful time to visit — fewer people, wildflowers beginning to bloom, and the birdwatching excellent as migrants pour through on their way north. **Autumn** is quieter still and the light on the alvar is particularly good; the ancient oaks in Halltorps Hage turn spectacular colours in October.

**Winter** is for locals only. Many tourist facilities close from October to April, and the island's isolation is more pronounced. The bridge is occasionally closed during severe storms, though this is uncommon.
