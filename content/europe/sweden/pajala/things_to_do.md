---
title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Pajala's attractions are few but specific: the Laestadius museum, the Torne River, and the landscape of the Arctic Tornedalen. For visitors with time and interest, the town is also a starting point for exploring the unique bilingual culture of the Swedish-Finnish border region. Most travellers pass through on their way further north or as part of a Swedish Lapland circuit.
