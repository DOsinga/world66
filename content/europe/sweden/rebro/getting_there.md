---
title: Getting There
type: section
---

Örebro is well-connected by rail. Direct trains run from Stockholm (about 2 hours) and Gothenburg (about 2.5 hours) on the main west coast line, with frequent daily services. The train station is a short walk from the castle and city centre.

By car, Örebro sits at the junction of the E18 and E20 motorways, making it easily reachable from Stockholm (about 2 hours), Gothenburg (about 2.5 hours), and Oslo (about 3.5 hours via E18). There is a regional airport (Örebro Airport) with limited domestic and charter services; most air travellers use Stockholm Arlanda.
