---
title: Getting There
type: section
---

Örebro is well-connected by rail. Direct trains run from [Stockholm](/europe/sweden/stockholm) in about 80–90 minutes and from [Gothenburg](/europe/sweden/gothenburg) in about two hours. The train station is a short walk north of the city centre. The journey from Stockholm passes through flat central Swedish countryside and is a comfortable way to make the trip.

By car, Örebro sits on the E18/E20 motorway between Stockholm and Karlstad and is roughly 200 km west of Stockholm. Parking in the city centre is metered and available.

Örebro Airport (ORB) handles a small number of domestic routes but is not a primary gateway — most visitors arrive by train.
