The Oset and Rynningeviken Nature Reserve sits where the River Svartån flows into Lake Hjälmaren on Örebro's eastern edge — a wetland complex of reedbeds, shallow bays, and managed meadows that is one of Sweden's premier birdwatching sites. The area holds a well-developed network of hides, boardwalks, and marked trails through the marshes.

Species visible depending on season include great bittern, marsh harrier, bearded reedling, and during migration, enormous numbers of waders, ducks, and geese using Lake Hjälmaren as a staging ground. The reserve is closest to the city in spring and autumn migration periods, when unusual species appear with regularity. Non-birders will still find the wetland landscape pleasant — flat, open, and very different from the manicured urban parks around the castle. The reserve is about 3 km east of the city centre; reachable by bicycle or on foot along the river path.
