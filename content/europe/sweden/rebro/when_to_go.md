---
title: When to Go
type: section
---

Summer (June–August) is the best time to visit Örebro. The castle tours, Svampen restaurant, and Wadköping outdoor events all operate at full capacity, and the city's parks and riverbanks are at their best. The long Scandinavian summer days mean you have plenty of light.

Spring migration at Oset and Rynningeviken peaks in April–May, which is an excellent time for birdwatchers. Autumn brings smaller numbers but often rarer species. Winter in Örebro is cold and dark — not a compelling time for most visitors — though the city takes on a quiet, Nordic charm when snow covers the castle grounds.
