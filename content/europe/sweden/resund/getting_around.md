---
title: "Getting Around"
type: section
---

The Öresund Region has one of the best integrated cross-border public transport systems in Europe. The Öresundståg train service runs every 20 minutes between Malmö and Copenhagen Airport (CPH), taking about 35 minutes, and between Helsingborg and Copenhagen via the bridge. A single Öresund ticket covers the entire regional network on both sides of the border.

Malmö is the main Swedish hub and has good connections to Helsingborg (45 minutes by train) and to Gothenburg and Stockholm. Copenhagen is easily reached in 35-40 minutes from Malmö Central. The Helsingborg–Helsingør ferry takes 20 minutes and runs every 15-20 minutes throughout the day. Cycling is well-supported throughout the region, with extensive dedicated paths on both sides of the water.
