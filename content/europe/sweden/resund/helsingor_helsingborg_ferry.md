The two castles facing each other across the water — Kronborg on the Danish side and Kärnan on the Swedish — were built partly to extract tolls from the constant ship traffic that once made this strait one of the most profitable waterways in northern Europe."
---

The 20-minute ferry between Helsingborg and Helsingør is one of the most historically loaded crossings in northern Europe. For centuries this narrow point controlled trade between the Baltic and the North Sea, and the fortifications on both shores reflect that. The ferry still runs today, operated by ForSea, with departures every 15-20 minutes. Kronborg Castle — Shakespeare's Elsinore — is a 10-minute walk from the Helsingør ferry terminal, making the crossing a practical and scenic way to visit it.
