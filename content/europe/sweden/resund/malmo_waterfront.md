story: "The Turning Torso, designed by Santiago Calatrava and completed in 2005, is a 190-metre residential tower in the Västra Hamnen district of Malmö, rotating 90 degrees from base to top through nine stacked pentagonal sections. It was built on the site of a former shipyard that had been the industrial heart of Malmö, and its construction was part of a deliberate effort to transform the city's image from declining heavy industry to design and knowledge economy. It remains the tallest building in Scandinavia. The waterfront around it has walking and cycling paths along the strait with views of the bridge and the Danish coast."
---

The Turning Torso anchors the Västra Hamnen waterfront district, which has been transformed from a shipyard into a mixed residential and commercial area with some of the best urban waterfront walking in southern Sweden. The building itself is striking from any angle — a white helix that changes shape as you move around it. The waterfront promenade gives a clear view of the Öresund Bridge and on clear days the outline of Copenhagen across the strait.
