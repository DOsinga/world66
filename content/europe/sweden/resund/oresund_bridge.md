story: "The Öresund Bridge is 7.8km long and carries both a four-lane motorway and a double-track railway. Midway across, it meets an artificial island — Peberholm — where the combined crossing becomes an undersea tunnel for the final 4km to the Danish coast, allowing shipping to pass unobstructed. The bridge opened in July 2000 and has been crossed over 100 million times since. It is lit at night, visible from the shores on both sides, and became internationally famous as the setting for the opening sequence of the Scandinavian crime series Broen/Broen, known in English as The Bridge."
---

The Öresund Bridge links Malmö to Copenhagen across the strait that has divided and connected Scandinavia from the continent for centuries. The train crossing takes 15 minutes from Malmö Central; the view from the rail carriages as the bridge rises over the water — Sweden behind you, Denmark ahead — is one of the better experiences available from a commuter train. The bridge is toll-based for cars; the train is covered by standard regional rail tickets.
