---
title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

The Öresund Region is less a single destination than a network of connected places made accessible by excellent rail and ferry links. The Öresund Bridge is the starting point: cross it, and Copenhagen is fifteen minutes from Malmö by train. The Danish side offers Kronborg Castle in Helsingør, the Viking Ship Museum in Roskilde within day-trip range, and Copenhagen's museums and waterfront. The Swedish side has Malmö's Turning Torso and Moderna Museet, Helsingborg's medieval tower, and the open farmland and beaches of Skåne. The region works best as a base for exploring both countries simultaneously.
