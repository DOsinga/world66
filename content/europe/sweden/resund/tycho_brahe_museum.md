The Tycho Brahe Museum on Ven tells the story of the astronomer's island observatory, Uraniborg, built in 1576 and later abandoned after Brahe left Denmark. The site is one of the earliest examples of a purpose-built scientific research centre, and the museum explains how Brahe's precise observations helped change European astronomy.
