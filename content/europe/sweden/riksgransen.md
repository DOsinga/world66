Riksgränsen is Sweden's far-north border resort, wedged against Norway on the railway between Kiruna and Narvik. It is small, seasonal, and very much a ski place, but it has a stronger identity than an ordinary resort strip: this is one of the northernmost lift-served ski areas in the world, with a season that runs toward the midnight sun.

The appeal is snow, not town life. In late winter and spring, skiers come for off-piste terrain, high-latitude light, and the odd pleasure of riding lifts when most European ski areas are already thinking about summer. The [Riksgränsen ski area](/europe/sweden/riksgransen/riksgransen_ski_area) is the anchor, while the railway station and nearby [Katterjåkk](/europe/sweden/riksgransen/katterjakk) make it unusually easy to reach for somewhere this remote.

Outside ski season, treat Riksgränsen as a compact mountain base rather than a full destination. The broader [Abisko and Björkliden](/europe/sweden/abisko_and_bjorkliden) area has the deeper year-round range, but if you are already crossing between Kiruna and Narvik, Riksgränsen gives the border mountains a sharp, memorable stop.
