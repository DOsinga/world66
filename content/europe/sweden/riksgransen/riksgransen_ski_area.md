Riksgränsen Ski Area is the reason the settlement is on the map. The lift system is not huge, but the northern latitude, heavy snow, and open terrain give it a reputation among skiers who care more about conditions than village gloss.

The classic time is spring, when the days stretch out and the snowpack is still deep. Beginners can ski here, but the place is most interesting for confident skiers looking for off-piste lines and the strange light of the Arctic shoulder season.
