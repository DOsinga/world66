---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

Södertälje is a year-round city with good train connections from Stockholm. Summer (June–August) is the nicest time to visit: the canal is busy with boats, the open-air museum at Torekällberget has all its activities running, and you can sit outside by the waterfront. Winter is cold and dark but perfectly manageable — the indoor attractions (Tom Tits Experiment, Marcus Wallenberg-hallen) are open throughout the year.

The canal lock is worth seeing at any time of year, and it operates continuously to let boats between Lake Mälaren and the Baltic.
