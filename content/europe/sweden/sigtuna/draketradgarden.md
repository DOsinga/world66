Draketradgarden is a preserved historic garden in Sigtuna, connected with the Drake family and the town's later bourgeois houses. It gives a small but useful glimpse of how abandoned medieval plots were reused as homes and gardens.

It is a short, quiet stop while walking Sigtuna's old streets.
