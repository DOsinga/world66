Mora Church is a red-painted stone church dating back to the 13th century, with a tall tower and a richly carved wooden altarpiece inside. Its lakeside position and long local history make it one of the main landmarks in Mora.

The church also has a strong link with the Vasaloppet ski race, which starts ceremonially here before the long route through Dalarna.
