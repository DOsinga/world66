The Orsa Grindstone Museum tells the story of the local sandstone once used for sharpening stones and millstones. It is a small, specialised museum, but the subject is closely tied to Orsa's geology and working history.

It is best for travellers already exploring the Siljan area who enjoy local craft and industrial heritage. The museum helps explain a trade that shaped this part of Dalarna without leaving grand monuments behind.
