Tomteland, also known as Santaworld, is a family theme park near Gesunda south of Mora. Instead of big rides, it is built around wooden houses, costumed actors, and a Santa-themed village in the forest.

It is liveliest in summer and before Christmas. Outside peak periods there may be fewer actors and a quieter atmosphere, so check the seasonal programme before planning around it.
