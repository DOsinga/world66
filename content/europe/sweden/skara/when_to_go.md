---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

Skara is at its best from May to early September, when the cathedral square is open-air, the museum's open-air village is in full swing, and Skara Sommarland is open. Midsummer week in late June is a good time for festivities, though most things close for the holiday itself.

Winters are dark, cold, and quiet — pretty if there is snow on the cathedral roofs, but not much happens.
