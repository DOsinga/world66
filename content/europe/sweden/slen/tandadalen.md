A purpose-built resort 15 kilometres west of Sälen village, connected to neighbouring Hundfjället by lift. Tandådalen is shaped around a single valley with broad, gentle slopes — popular with families and beginners. Two large snow parks attract freestyle skiers.

The base area is a self-contained mini-town with hotels, apartments, a swimming pool and an ice-skating rink. SkiStar's "Experium" indoor activity centre is here.
