Aschanska gården preserves the domestic side of old Eksjö: rooms, furnishings, and courtyard life from a 19th-century middle-class home. It is a small sight, but it sharpens the old town from pretty streets into a lived-in place.

Access is usually by guided tour, especially outside the main summer season, so check locally before counting on it.
