Nybro is a working Småland town between Kalmar and Växjö, best understood as one of the practical gateways to Glasriket, the Kingdom of Crystal. Glass, wood, and paper shaped the local economy, and [Pukeberg Glassworks](/europe/sweden/smaaland/nybro/pukeberg_glassworks) keeps that identity visible close to town.

This is not the most romantic stop in Småland, but it has enough personality for a spare day: old glassworks, forest recreation at Svartbäcksmåla, a moose park west of town, and the deeply unlikely [007 Museum](/europe/sweden/smaaland/nybro/007_museum).

Use Nybro as a low-key base if you are moving through [Småland](/europe/sweden/smaaland) by train or car. The bigger Glasriket story belongs to the wider region, but Nybro gives it a useful town centre and a few good detours of its own.
