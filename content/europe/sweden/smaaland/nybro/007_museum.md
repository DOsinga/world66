The 007 Museum is one of those small-town collections that makes a trip more memorable precisely because it is so unlikely. It is a private James Bond museum inside an auto-parts business, built around decades of collecting.

Do not expect a polished national museum. Expect enthusiasm, props, posters, vehicles, and a collector's logic that has somehow made Nybro part of James Bond geography.
