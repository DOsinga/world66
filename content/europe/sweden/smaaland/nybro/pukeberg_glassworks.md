Pukeberg Glassworks is the clearest expression of Nybro's glass identity. The old industrial site now mixes glass heritage, workshops, design activity, and reused brick buildings on the edge of town.

It is most rewarding when something is active: a shop, studio, exhibition, or demonstration. Even when quiet, the site helps connect Nybro to the wider Glasriket story.
