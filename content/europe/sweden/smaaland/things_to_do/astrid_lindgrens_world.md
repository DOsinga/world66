Vimmerby, a small town in the northern part of Småland, was the birthplace of Astrid Lindgren, the author of Pippi Longstocking, Emil of Lönneberga, Ronja Rövardotter, and dozens of other beloved children's books. The town has leaned fully into this heritage: Astrid Lindgrens Värld is a large theme park where the settings from her books have been faithfully recreated as you can walk through. Villa Villekulla — Pippi's house — stands there along with Emil's farm at Katthult, the village of Bullerbyn, and the island of Saltkråkan.

The park is aimed primarily at families with younger children, and the atmosphere is gentler and more literary than typical theme parks. Actors in costume play the characters, there are live shows throughout the day, and the craftsmanship of the sets is high. The park is open from late May through August, with extended hours during the Swedish school summer holidays in July.

Separate from the theme park, the town centre has the Astrid Lindgren birthplace museum, where you can visit the actual house she grew up in and follow her life story. Vimmerby's town square and surroundings retain much of the small-town Swedish character she described in her books, and a walk through town with one of her stories in mind gives the place a particular resonance.
