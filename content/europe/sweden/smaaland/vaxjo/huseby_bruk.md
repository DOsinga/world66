Huseby Bruk is a preserved ironworks estate south of Vaxjo, with old factory buildings, a manor, and village-like grounds. The industrial history reaches back to the 17th century, and the site remained in use into the 20th century.

It is strongest as a half-day heritage stop, especially if you are interested in old workshops and estate landscapes.
