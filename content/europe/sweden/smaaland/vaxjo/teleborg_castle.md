Teleborg Castle looks much older than it is. Built in 1900, it borrows the language of medieval towers and romantic stonework rather than the plain style you might expect from a modern Swedish building.

It sits south of the centre near the university and lake, making it a good target for a walk or bike ride rather than a full excursion.
