Växjö Cathedral is the city's landmark, a medieval church made easy to spot by its two tall spires. The building has been altered over centuries, but it still gives the centre its strongest silhouette.

Inside, the most Småland detail is the glass. Works from Kosta Boda and other regional makers connect the cathedral to the glass country that begins in the forests east of the city.
