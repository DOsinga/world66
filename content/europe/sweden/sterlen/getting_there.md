---
title: "Getting There"
type: section
---

Österlen is in the southeast corner of Skåne, easy to reach from Copenhagen and Malmö but a long drive from Stockholm. Most international visitors fly to **Copenhagen** (CPH) and take a train across the Öresund bridge — Malmö Central is 30 minutes from the airport, and Ystad another hour on the regional service.

**By train.** Pågatåg regional services run hourly between Malmö and Ystad, and from Ystad on to Simrishamn. Both lines are scenic and good for car-free trips.

**By ferry.** Ystad has direct ferries to Bornholm (Denmark) and Świnoujście (Poland), making the area a useful stop on the way in or out of Sweden.

**By car.** From Copenhagen, the drive takes about two hours via the Öresund bridge and the E22. A car is by far the easiest way to explore the smaller villages and the coast once you arrive.
