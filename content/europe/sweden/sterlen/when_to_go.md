---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

The high season is short: roughly mid-June to mid-August. Swedes from Stockholm and Malmö come down for the warm weather and the long evenings, and the small towns fill up considerably — though the area never feels crowded by Mediterranean standards.

**May and early June** are arguably the best time to visit. The apple orchards around Kivik blossom in late May; days are long and bright, the air is crisp, and most restaurants and museums are open. Water is too cold for swimming.

**Late June through August** is summer proper. Daylight runs from 4am to 10pm, the sea is just warm enough for a swim, and the local food and cider farms are at full tilt. Book accommodation ahead.

**September** offers the apple harvest, mist over the fields in the early morning, and far fewer visitors. Many places shut from mid-October until Easter.
