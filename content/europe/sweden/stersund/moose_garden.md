Moose Garden is a small wildlife attraction outside Östersund built around Sweden's most iconic large animal. Visitors come for close encounters, photography and interpretation about moose biology, behaviour and the animal's place in northern Swedish culture.

It is a controlled visitor experience rather than a wilderness safari, but that makes it accessible for families and travellers unlikely to see moose safely from the road. Confirm opening times before setting out, especially outside the main summer season.
