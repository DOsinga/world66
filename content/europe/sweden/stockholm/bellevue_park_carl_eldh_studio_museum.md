Bellevue Park looks over Brunnsviken and gives a quieter northern edge to a Stockholm walk. Its best reason to stop is the Carl Eldh Studio Museum, where early 20th-century sculpture is shown in the artist's former studio.

The combination of parkland, water views, and a small artist's museum makes this a good detour if you are exploring Hagastaden or the parks north of the centre.
