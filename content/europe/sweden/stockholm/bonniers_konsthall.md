Bonniers konsthall is a contemporary art hall backed by the Bonnier publishing family, with changing exhibitions by Swedish and international artists. It is a good fit if you want Stockholm's current art scene rather than another royal or national collection, and the building is close enough to central Norrmalm to visit without a long detour.
