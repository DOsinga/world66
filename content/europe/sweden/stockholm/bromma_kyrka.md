Bromma kyrka is one of Stockholm's oldest surviving buildings, with a round church core from the 12th century and later medieval additions. Inside are medieval wall paintings and centuries of parish history, making it a strong stop for travellers interested in older Stockholm beyond the central islands.

The church sits west of the city centre, so it is best visited as part of a trip through Bromma rather than as a quick detour from Gamla Stan.
