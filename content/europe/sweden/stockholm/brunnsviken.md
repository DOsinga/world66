Brunnsviken is a brackish bay beside Stockholm's National City Park. Its shoreline has several beaches and green stretches, particularly on the eastern side near the university.

It is a good outdoor break from central Stockholm, suited to walking, swimming in season, or combining with nearby gardens and park sights around northern Djurgarden and Frescati.
