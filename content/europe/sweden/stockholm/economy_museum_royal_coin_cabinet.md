The Economy Museum, built around the Royal Coin Cabinet collection, tells the story of money, wealth, and financial life in Sweden. Coins and banknotes are the anchor, but the displays are really about how people have measured value and managed exchange.

It is a focused museum rather than one of Stockholm's headline stops, useful if you like social history or want something quieter near the Djurgarden and Ostermalm museum cluster.
