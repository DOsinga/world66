Eric Ericsonhallen is a former naval church on Skeppsholmen, now used mainly as a concert hall. Choir music is its natural strength, and the simple church interior gives performances a clear, intimate acoustic.

It is most worthwhile when something is on the programme, but the building also fits neatly into a walk around the island's museums and waterfront paths.
