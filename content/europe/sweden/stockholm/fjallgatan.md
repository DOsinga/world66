Fjällgatan is a picturesque street on Södermalm with one of Stockholm's classic views. From the ridge, you look back across the water toward Gamla Stan and the central waterfront.

It is best visited as part of a walk east from Slussen or combined with Fotografiska and the streets above Stadsgarden. The view is especially good in clear weather, when the old town and harbour line up below the ridge.
