Hagaparken is a large historic royal park in Solna, just north of central Stockholm. It is good for a long walk or picnic, with lakeside paths, pavilions, and views toward Brunnsviken. Haga Palace is the residence of Sweden's Crown Princess, so the palace itself is not a normal visitor sight, but the surrounding park is open to the public.
