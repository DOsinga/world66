Hamburger Börs is a historic Stockholm stage restaurant, with roots going back about two centuries. Its reputation comes from dinner shows, concerts, and cabaret-style nights, and the guest list over the years has ranged from Swedish performers to international names such as Frank Sinatra and Liza Minnelli.

It is worth checking the programme if you want an evening that feels more Stockholm than a generic bar crawl. The draw is the performance and the old entertainment-room atmosphere, not just the meal.
