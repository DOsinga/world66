Kulturhuset Stadsteatern is Stockholm's central house of culture, facing Sergels torg in the heart of Norrmalm. It brings together theatre, exhibitions, a library, cafes, and public spaces, so it is useful both as a rainy-day cultural stop and as a good place to read the mood of modern Stockholm.
