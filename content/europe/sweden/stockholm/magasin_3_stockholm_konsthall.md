Magasin 3 Stockholm Konsthall began in 1987 in a former warehouse in the old free port district. Its large industrial spaces are used for contemporary art exhibitions, often with international artists and newly produced work.

It sits a little away from Stockholm's main museum circuit, so check what is showing before making the trip. When the exhibition is strong, the scale of the space is part of the draw.
