Stockholm's Maritime Museum tells Sweden's seafaring story through ship models, naval history, and surviving pieces of historic vessels. One highlight is material from Amphion, an 18th-century royal yacht.

The museum sits close to Djurgården's larger museum cluster, so it works best for travellers with a particular interest in ships, naval history, or Sweden's relationship with the Baltic.
