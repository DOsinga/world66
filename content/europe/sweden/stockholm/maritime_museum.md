Stockholm's Maritime Museum tells Sweden's seafaring story through ship models, naval history, and surviving pieces from royal and working vessels. The collection includes remnants of the 18th-century royal yacht Amphion, but the broader pleasure is seeing how deeply the Baltic and long-distance trade shaped Swedish identity.

It sits a little away from the city's museum cluster on Djurgården, so it is best for travellers with a real interest in ships, naval history, or Sweden's relationship with the sea.
