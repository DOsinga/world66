Stockholm's Museum of Science and Technology tells Sweden's engineering story through machines, inventions, industrial design, and digital culture. It is the kind of museum where the national habit of building things well becomes visible, from older mechanical objects to computers and communications.

The museum is especially good with children, but it is not only for them. Exhibits on computing, transport, and technology history make it a useful counterweight to Djurgården's art and royal museums, and it sits close enough to combine with several other stops on the island.
