Stockholm's National Museum of Fine Arts is Sweden's main museum for European and Swedish art. The collection includes works by Rembrandt, Rubens, Goya, Renoir, Degas, Gauguin, and major Swedish artists, shown in a renovated 19th-century museum building by the waterfront.
