The Northern Jewish Cemetery is part of the large Northern Cemetery in Solna. Its Moorish-style chapel opened in 1857, and the cemetery is tied to several layers of Stockholm's Jewish history.

One especially moving part of the site is its connection to Holocaust survivors who reached Sweden after the war and died soon after arrival. It is a quiet, reflective stop rather than a conventional museum.
