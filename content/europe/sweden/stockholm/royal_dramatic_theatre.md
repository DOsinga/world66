The Royal Dramatic Theatre is Sweden's national stage for spoken drama and one of Stockholm's most elegant public buildings. Its marble facade, sculptural decoration, and waterfront position make it worth a look even if you are not attending a performance.

Guided tours sometimes run in English and are the best way to see the interiors. Otherwise, combine it with a walk along Nybroviken and nearby Östermalm.
