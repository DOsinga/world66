The Royal Mews keeps the ceremonial vehicles, stables, and working transport traditions of the Swedish Royal Court. Visits are by guided tour, group visit, or special event, so it is a place to plan rather than casually wander into.

For travellers interested in royal ceremony, horses, or historic carriages and cars, it adds a quieter stop near Östermalm's larger museums.
