Skogskyrkogården is Stockholm's UNESCO-listed woodland cemetery, designed by Gunnar Asplund and Sigurd Lewerentz. Its chapels, landscape design, and quiet forest setting make it a major work of modern architecture as well as the burial place of Greta Garbo.
