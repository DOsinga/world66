SoFo — "South of Folkungagatan" — is the hip, independent-minded quarter of Södermalm, Stockholm's largest inner-city island and traditionally the working-class district. The neighbourhood is dense with vintage clothing shops, independent bookstores, design studios, and small restaurants, mostly in the blocks between Skånegatan and Ringvägen. It has been fashionable for long enough that it is no longer edgy exactly, but it remains genuinely local — the antithesis of the tourist-heavy Gamla Stan.

On the last Friday of each month, SoFo Night runs late with shops staying open and events in the streets. The area rewards aimless wandering more than planning — the best finds tend to be on side streets. Medborgarplatsen, the main square at the northern edge, has good bars and is a natural place to start or end an afternoon.
