The Spirits Museum on Djurgarden tells the story of alcohol in Sweden, from drinking customs to state control, taxation, and advertising. Its best-known collection is the Absolut Art Collection, with works created for Absolut Vodka campaigns by artists including Andy Warhol and Keith Haring.

It is a small, focused museum and works best as part of a Djurgarden museum day rather than as the main reason to cross town.
