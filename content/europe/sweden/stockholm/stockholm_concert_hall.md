Stockholm Concert Hall is the blue Swedish Grace landmark on Hötorget and the home of the Royal Stockholm Philharmonic Orchestra. It opened in 1926 and is best known internationally as the setting for the Nobel Prize award ceremony, apart from the Peace Prize in Oslo.

The building is worth a look even if you are not attending a concert. Its clean classical lines, broad steps, and busy market-square setting make it one of central Norrmalm's more distinctive cultural buildings.
