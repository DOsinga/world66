Stockholm Concert Hall is a Swedish Grace landmark from 1926 and the home of the Royal Stockholm Philharmonic Orchestra. It is also the venue for the annual Nobel Prize ceremony, except for the Peace Prize, which is awarded in Oslo.

Even without attending a concert, the blue facade and ceremonial setting make it a worthwhile architecture stop around Hotorget and central Norrmalm.
