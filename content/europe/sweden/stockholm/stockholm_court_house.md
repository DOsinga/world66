Stockholm Court House is one of Kungsholmen's strongest examples of Swedish National Romantic architecture. The early 20th-century courthouse has a massive tower, restored interiors, and a public weekday entrance with security screening. It works well as a stop on a walk between City Hall and the western side of central Stockholm.
