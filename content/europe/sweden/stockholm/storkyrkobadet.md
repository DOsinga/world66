Storkyrkobadet is a small historic bath hidden beneath Gamla Stan, in vaulted rooms that were once part of an old wine cellar. The setting is the draw: a quiet underground pool and sauna in the middle of Stockholm's busiest old-town streets.

It is more intimate than a modern spa and has separate bathing times for men and women. Check current opening hours before going, as access can be limited.
