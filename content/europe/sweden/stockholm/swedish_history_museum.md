The Swedish History Museum traces the country from the Ice Age to the present, with especially strong material on the Viking Age and the medieval period. The Gold Room displays major gold and silver finds from the Bronze Age through the 16th century.

It is one of Stockholm's essential museums for understanding the older layers of Swedish history. Allow enough time for the Viking and medieval galleries rather than treating it as a quick stop between Djurgården museums.
