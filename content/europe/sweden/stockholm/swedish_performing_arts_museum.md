The Swedish Performing Arts Museum covers theatre, dance, and music in Sweden and beyond, with exhibits that make stage history more hands-on than a traditional costume-and-programme display. It also includes material on Swedish pop music.

Its location in central Stockholm makes it easy to combine with the Army Museum, Hallwyl Museum, or a walk through Ostermalm.
