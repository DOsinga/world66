Tensta konsthall is a contemporary art space in the western suburb of Tensta. Since opening in 1998, it has built a reputation for exhibitions that connect art with migration, urban life, education, and the politics of the surrounding neighbourhood.

It is a useful stop for travellers who want Stockholm's art scene beyond the central museums. Check the current exhibition before making the trip, as the appeal depends strongly on what is showing.
