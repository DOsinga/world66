Waldemarsudde was the home of Prince Eugen, a painter and collector whose villa at the edge of Djurgården is now one of Stockholm's most rewarding art museums. The collection focuses on Nordic painting from the late 19th and early 20th centuries, with the prince's own work shown beside pieces he collected.

The setting is part of the appeal. The villa, gallery wings, gardens, and waterfront views make it an easy museum to combine with a longer walk around Djurgården.
