The forested island just east of Sundsvall, reached by the Alnöbron road bridge, Alnön is the city's summer escape — beaches on the eastern shore, fishing villages, the medieval Old Church, and a scattering of cafés. The 40 km circuit drive around the island takes a slow afternoon.

The geology is unusual: a single ancient volcanic intrusion that has yielded several minerals not found elsewhere in Scandinavia. The hiking paths are flat and well-marked.
