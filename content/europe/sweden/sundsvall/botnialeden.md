Botnialeden is a marked coastal trail running south from Sundsvall toward Galtstrom, with forest, river crossings, sea views, and historic stops along the Bothnian coast. At about 48 km, it can be tackled in sections or used for a longer walk if you have the time and weather.

For most visitors, the easiest taste is a day hike from the Sundsvall end near Sodra Berget. The trail is new, but it already gives the city a stronger outdoor link to its coastline and surrounding woods.
