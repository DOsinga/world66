Bureplatsen is a piece of contemporary land art on the western edge of the city. Built over the remains of an old waste landfill, the site combines re-shaped grass terraces with stainless steel sculptural elements by the artists Mats Bigert and Lars Bergström. The piece takes its name from Harry Martinson's epic poem Aniara about a spaceship lost in deep space.

It's a quirky walk if you have a spare hour and a car, or an easy detour off the E14 heading west out of town.
