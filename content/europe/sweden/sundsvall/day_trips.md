---
title: Day Trips
type: section
---

The [High Coast](/europe/sweden/the_high_coast) (Höga Kusten), a UNESCO World Heritage site of dramatic uplift coastline, begins about 80 km north of Sundsvall and makes the most worthwhile day trip — drive the coast road past Härnösand and Nordingrå, walk the cliffs at Skuleberget, and you can be back by evening. Closer to home, the island of [Alnön](/europe/sweden/sundsvall/alnon) directly east is a short drive with beaches, the medieval Old Church, and a quiet circuit road. Inland, a hour west on the E14 brings you to Liden with its old wooden church and the start of the Storsjön lake country toward Östersund.
