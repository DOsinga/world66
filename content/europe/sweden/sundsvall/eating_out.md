---
title: Eating Out
type: section
---

Sundsvall's restaurant scene is concentrated around Storgatan and Esplanaden in Stenstan. For a long, considered meal, Skonertgatan and Invito are the long-standing fine-dining options. Casual choices include Tant Anci & Fröken Sara for upmarket Swedish home cooking, Saigon Pho for Vietnamese, and Bishop's Arms for a pub-style menu and a serious beer list. The Sundsvalls Museum café and Kafferepet on Storgatan are reliable lunch and coffee stops. The Norrlandish habit of strong coffee and dense cinnamon buns is observed everywhere.
