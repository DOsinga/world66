The post-fire parish church of Sundsvall — a neo-Gothic red brick building completed in 1894, with twin spires that dominate the western end of the city. The interior is plainly decorated, with high vaulted ceilings and good acoustics; choir and organ recitals are frequent.

Open most days. The church gives the best sense of how confidently the late-19th-century city wanted to be seen.
