Norra Berget Open-Air Museum is part heritage village, part hilltop park, and part viewpoint over Sundsvall. Historic buildings, local-history displays, animals, and walking paths make it one of the city's most rounded visitor stops.

The setting matters as much as the exhibits. From the hill you can read Sundsvall's position between forested slopes, harbour, and the stone city below, then spend time among the older rural buildings that explain the region beyond the centre.
