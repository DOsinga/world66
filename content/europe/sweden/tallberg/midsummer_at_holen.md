Tällberg's midsummer celebration is the village at its most distinctive. The maypole raising, music, ring dances, folk dress, and family crowds are all familiar Swedish midsummer ingredients, but Dalarna gives the ritual extra weight. This is one of the regions where visitors come specifically to see the tradition done properly.

The main public programme is tied to Holens Gammelgård and the village around it. Dates shift with midsummer, so check the current schedule before planning a trip around it.
