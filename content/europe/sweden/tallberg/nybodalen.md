Nybodalen is the quieter of Tällberg's nearby nature reserves, a pocket of wooded paths and small-scale ravine scenery east of the village. It is not wild country, but it gives a good contrast to the hotel terraces and craft shops around the centre.

Use it for an hour outdoors, especially if you are travelling without a car and want a walk that starts from the village itself.
