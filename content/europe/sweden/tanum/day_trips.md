The Bohuslän coast around Tanum is rich in fishing villages, granite skerries, and excellent seafood. The closest worthwhile outing is **Grebbestad**, 10 km west on the coast, famous for its oyster beds and shellfish. Boats from Grebbestad and neighbouring villages run summer trips out to the Väderöarna ('Weather Islands') — a windswept granite archipelago at the open sea, with seal colonies and an isolated guesthouse.

A little further south, **Fjällbacka** is a postcard fishing village with a steep granite cleft (Kungsklyftan) cutting through the cliff above the harbour — used as a location in several films. North of Tanum, the **Kosterhavet National Park** off Strömstad protects Sweden's only marine national park, accessible by ferry from Strömstad to the Koster Islands.
