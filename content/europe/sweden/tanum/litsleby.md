---

Litsleby is best known for its single giant figure, painted red against the granite. The surrounding rocks have boats, ploughmen, and animals, but it's the spear-god who stops people in their tracks. Archaeologists think he may represent a sky god or a chieftain elevated to mythic status.

The site is a five-minute drive from Vitlycke, with a small car park and a short woodland path. No entry fee. Combine with Aspeberget, the next site along.
