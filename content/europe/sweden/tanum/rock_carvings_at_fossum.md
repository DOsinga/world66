The rock carvings at Fossum are part of Tanum's remarkable Bronze Age landscape, where ships, people, animals, and symbols were pecked into the granite. Fossum is especially useful because it shows the carvings as a landscape experience, not just as a museum label.

Visit it alongside Vitlycke if you can. The figures are easier to understand after seeing the museum context, but Fossum gives you the pleasure of standing in the open air and imagining the old shoreline, rituals, and movement through this part of Bohuslän.
