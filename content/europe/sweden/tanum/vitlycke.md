Vitlycke is the central site of the UNESCO-listed Tanum rock carvings. The panel here is one of the largest in northern Europe, a sloping rock face covered with hundreds of figures — long-boats, sun symbols, hunters, the famous 'bridal couple' embracing, and a tall warrior with a raised axe.

The accompanying **Vitlycke Museum** sits at the foot of the rock and gives the carvings their archaeological context, with a reconstructed Bronze Age farm in the grounds. The museum is open daily April to October; the rocks themselves are accessible year-round. Allow two hours for the panel and the museum together.
