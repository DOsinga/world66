---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

The rock carvings can be visited year-round, but the practical season is **May to September**. The Vitlycke Museum is closed in winter, days are short, and the paths over the rocks can be slippery with ice or wet leaves. In summer the days are long enough to see all four main sites in a single afternoon.

**Midsummer** (around 21 June) is busy with Swedish holidaymakers along the Bohuslän coast — accommodation in nearby Grebbestad and Fjällbacka books out months in advance. **August** is slightly quieter and the sea is warm enough for swimming.

Outside summer, **September and early October** are the best months: cool, clear, golden light on the granite, and almost no other visitors at the carving sites.
