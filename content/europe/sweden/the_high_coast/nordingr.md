Nordingrå is a church village at the heart of the [High Coast](/europe/sweden/the_high_coast), the UNESCO-listed stretch of Sweden's Bothnian coastline where post-glacial land uplift has created a dramatic landscape of steep granite hills, sheltered inlets, and old fishing harbours.

The surroundings are famous for their beauty — the [Rotsidan](/europe/sweden/the_high_coast/nordingr/rotsidan) red granite cliffs, the lighthouse on the island of [Högbonden](/europe/sweden/the_high_coast/nordingr/hogbonden), and the quiet coves where you can swim in summer make this one of the most scenic areas in northern Sweden. The fishing harbour at [Bönhamn](/europe/sweden/the_high_coast/nordingr/bonhamn) and the eccentric outdoor museum [Mannaminne](/europe/sweden/the_high_coast/nordingr/mannaminne) are essential stops. The pace of life here is slow and deliberate, which is exactly the point.
