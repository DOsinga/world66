---
title: Getting There
type: section
---

Nordingrå sits halfway between Stockholm and Umeå and is most easily reached by car: the E4 runs along the coast a few kilometres inland, and the turn-off at Docksta drops you into the heart of the area in about ten minutes.

The nearest railway station is **Härnösand** to the south (about 60 km) or **Örnsköldsvik** to the north (about 45 km), both on the Botniabanan line from Stockholm. From either you will need a bus or taxi for the last stretch; in summer there are also occasional shuttle buses to the main High Coast attractions. **Höga Kusten Airport** at Kramfors-Sollefteå handles a few flights from Stockholm.

Driving from Stockholm takes about six hours; from Umeå around two and a half.
