In a small outdoor art park near the village, a complete unit of the legendary Stockholm metro train known as Silverpilen sits incongruously in the woods. The bare aluminium-bodied train, retired from service in 1996, was the basis of one of Sweden's most enduring urban legends — that on certain nights an empty silver train would appear on Stockholm's metro and take its passengers somewhere very dark indeed.

The installation, run by the artist Mikael Genberg, is open to walk through whenever the gate is open. It is free, photogenic in a melancholic way, and unexpected.
