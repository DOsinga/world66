title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Trollhättan's two main visitor draws are the waterfalls and the Saab museum. The **Trollhättan Falls** run only during the annual "waterfall days" (usually in July); during the rest of the year the gorge is dry and the water diverted through the hydroelectric plant. Even without water, the gorge is dramatic and the walk around the old lock system and power station ruins is worthwhile.

The [Saab Car Museum](/europe/sweden/trollhttan/saab_car_museum) traces seventy years of automotive history from the prototype of 1947 to the end of production in 2011. The [Innovatum Science Center](/europe/sweden/trollhttan/innovatum_science_center) next door covers the industrial and engineering history of the region. Together they make a full half-day.

Trollhättan is also worth visiting for film history enthusiasts: the Film i Väst studios have produced a remarkable number of significant Scandinavian films here since 1992, and the town's locations appear frequently in Nordic cinema.
