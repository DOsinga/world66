Strandpromenaden is Uddevalla's scenic boardwalk along the southern shore of Byfjorden. The route links the harbour area with waterfront views, bathing spots, and the old resort setting around Gustafsberg.

It is an easy way to get a feel for the fjord without leaving town. Start near the harbour and continue as far as time and weather allow.
