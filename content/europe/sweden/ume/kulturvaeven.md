Väven (Kulturväven) is Umeå's main cultural centre, opened on the riverfront in 2014 to coincide with the European Capital of Culture year. The building houses the city library, a city hotel, conference facilities, and various cultural organisations, making it a genuine hub for both locals and visitors. The library is excellent — large, well-designed, and stocked with materials in multiple languages including English.

The location directly on the Ume River is its greatest asset. The ground floor opens toward the water, and the terrace is a popular gathering point in summer. Even if you have no specific reason to go inside, the building itself is interesting architecture — a substantial glass and steel structure with a warm interior that manages to feel welcoming rather than institutional.
