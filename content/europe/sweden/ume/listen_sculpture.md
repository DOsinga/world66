"Listen" is a public sculpture in central Umeå depicting a cat with its ear pressed to the ground, a pose that looks curious and alert. The work was commissioned as part of the city's cultural investment and became controversial almost immediately: critics argued that a cat listening to the ground was an inadequate symbol for a MeToo monument, while supporters defended the work's ambiguity as precisely the point. It has since become one of the more talked-about pieces of public art in northern Sweden.

The sculpture sits close to the main pedestrian area and is easy to find. Whatever your view on the merits of the artistic choice, the controversy itself is illuminating — it reflects the genuine engagement that Umeå has with culture and public life, and the willingness of the city to debate its own decisions. The cat has become something of a landmark despite, or because of, its disputed status.
