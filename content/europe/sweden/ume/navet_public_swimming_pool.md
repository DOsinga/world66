Navet is Umeå's central public swimming complex, aimed as much at families as at lap swimmers. The indoor pools include water slides, a wave machine, a climbing wall, a current channel, children's warm pools, a jacuzzi, and steam baths.

It is a practical rainy-day stop and a good option for families who need a break from museums and city walking.
