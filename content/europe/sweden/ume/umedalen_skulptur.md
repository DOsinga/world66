Umedalen Skulptur turns a former hospital district into one of Umeas best art walks. Large contemporary sculptures sit among lawns, paths, and institutional buildings, so the experience feels more like discovering art in a neighborhood than entering a formal museum.

It is free, open-air, and especially good in long northern summer light. Bring a map or follow the signed works, because the pleasure is in letting the pieces appear gradually as you move through the area.
