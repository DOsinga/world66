Biotopia is Uppsala's small natural-history museum, with dioramas and displays focused on Swedish animals and habitats. It is especially good for families, giving children an accessible introduction to Nordic nature without leaving the city.

The museum is compact and close to other central Uppsala sights, so it works well as a short indoor stop. Pair it with the botanical garden, Carolina Rediviva or a walk toward the river.
