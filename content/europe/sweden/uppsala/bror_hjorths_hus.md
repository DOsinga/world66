Bror Hjorths Hus preserves the home and studio of Swedish artist Bror Hjorth, with sculpture, paintings, and personal working spaces. It is one of Uppsala's more intimate art stops, very different from the cathedral and university museums.

The museum rewards visitors who like artist houses and twentieth-century Nordic art. It is small, so check opening hours and combine it with nearby city walks or other cultural stops.
