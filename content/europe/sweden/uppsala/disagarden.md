Disagarden is an open-air museum made up of preserved farm buildings from the Uppland countryside. The interiors and guided tours show what rural life in the region looked like in the 19th century.

It is an easy add-on to Old Uppsala, especially for families or visitors who want a more everyday view of Swedish history after seeing the burial mounds.
