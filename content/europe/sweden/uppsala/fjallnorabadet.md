Fjällnorabadet is where Uppsala turns into lake country. The swimming area sits east of the city among woods and water, with beaches, lawns, trails, and simple facilities that make it a practical summer escape rather than just a dot on the map.

It is best treated as a half-day outdoors stop: swim if the weather is kind, walk the nearby paths, or bring food and linger by the water. The place is local in atmosphere, but for travellers spending more than a quick museum day in Uppsala it shows an easy piece of Uppland countryside.
