Fyrishov is Uppsala's big swimming and recreation complex, useful on a cold or rainy day and especially easy with children. It has indoor pools, a tropical-style water park, children's pools, and outdoor pools in summer.

It is more practical fun than cultural sight, but it is a solid family stop if you need a break from churches, museums, and university history.
