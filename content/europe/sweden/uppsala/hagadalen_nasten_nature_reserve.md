Hagadalen-Nasten Nature Reserve gives Uppsala a generous wild edge, with forest paths, open meadows, and trails that start surprisingly close to the city. It is a strong choice when you want a Swedish outdoor break without planning a major excursion.

The reserve is good in all seasons, though footwear matters after rain and snow. Bring a picnic, watch for cyclists and skiers depending on the route, and treat it as a half-day counterpart to Uppsala Cathedral, museums, and university sights.
