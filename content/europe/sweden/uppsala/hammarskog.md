Hammarskog is a relaxed lakeside escape southwest of Uppsala, centred on an old manor house and the surrounding recreation area. It is the sort of place locals use for picnics, gentle walks, swimming in summer, and crisp outdoor time when the weather turns cold.

For visitors, Hammarskog is useful when you want a green half-day rather than another museum. The manor gives the area a focal point, but the real pleasure is moving between meadows, woods, and lake edges. It is best with a bicycle, car, or a clear plan for public transport, since it sits outside the city centre.
