Lennakatten is Uppsala's heritage railway, running vintage trains from Uppsala Central Station east into the countryside. The line covers about 33 km and turns a simple rail ride into a half-day excursion with old carriages, rural stations, and a slower pace than modern travel.

Check operating days before planning around it, as heritage services are seasonal rather than daily commuter trains.
