Mora Stenar is associated with the medieval election and acclamation of Swedish kings, giving a small rural site an importance that is larger than its physical remains. The stones and memorial setting point to a time when kingship was performed in public before later state institutions hardened into place.

It is a worthwhile detour for travellers interested in Swedish history, especially if Old Uppsala has already raised questions about power, ritual, and landscape. The site is understated, so read a little context before going.
