Stadsskogen is Uppsalas everyday wilderness, a broad forest reserve close enough to reach from the centre but large enough to feel properly wooded. Trails pass through pine, spruce, and mixed woodland, with small clearings and winter routes that locals use for skiing when conditions allow.

It is not a manicured showpiece like Stadstradgarden; that is the point. Come here when you want a quiet walk, a run, or a look at how quickly Swedish city life gives way to forest.
