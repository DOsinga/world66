Varberg is a west-coast town in Halland, south of [Gothenburg](/europe/sweden/gothenburg), with enough history and sea air to feel like more than a beach resort. Its shoreline mixes sandy bays, cold baths, summer restaurants, and old spa culture, while the centre is watched over by a fortress that has seen the long tug-of-war between Denmark and Sweden.

The obvious first stop is [Varberg Fortress](/europe/sweden/varberg/varberg_fortress), both for the ramparts and for the Halland Museum inside, where the medieval Bocksten Man is the unforgettable exhibit. A few kilometres away, [Getterön Nature Reserve](/europe/sweden/varberg/getteron_nature_reserve) gives birders and walkers a very different version of the coast.

Varberg also has one world-class oddity: [Grimeton Radio Station](/europe/sweden/varberg/grimeton_radio_station), a UNESCO-listed longwave transmitter site from the 1920s. Add [Kallbadhuset](/europe/sweden/varberg/kallbadhuset) and the beaches around Apelviken, and Varberg makes an easy, varied day from Gothenburg or a slower seaside base in its own right.
