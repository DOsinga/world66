Vendelsöarna is a protected group of low islands off the Halland coast, shaped by centuries of grazing into an open coastal landscape. The reserve is best for quiet walks, sea views, and birdwatching rather than developed attractions.

The islands are a worthwhile stop for travellers exploring the Varberg coast who want a wilder counterpoint to the town, fortress, and beaches.
