Värnamo is a small Småland town with more personality than its size suggests. It sits just off the E4 in southern Sweden, close to forests, lakes, and the wide wetland of [Store Mosse National Park](/europe/sweden/varnamo/store_mosse_national_park).

The town's best reason to stop is design. [Vandalorum](/europe/sweden/varnamo/vandalorum) brings contemporary art and craft into a building based on Renzo Piano's barn-like concept, while the [Bruno Mathsson Center](/europe/sweden/varnamo/bruno_mathsson_center) keeps the work of one of Sweden's great modernist furniture designers close to the house and workshop where he grew up.

Värnamo is not a big-city destination, and that is part of the appeal. Give it a day if you are crossing Småland: walk [Apladalen](/europe/sweden/varnamo/apladalen), look at chairs and glass walls, then head into the bog country where boardwalks and bird towers make southern Sweden feel suddenly remote.
