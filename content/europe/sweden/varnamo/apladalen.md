Apladalen is Värnamo's town park and homestead museum, a green pocket of old cottages, paths, lawns, and small exhibits near the centre. It is not a blockbuster sight, but it gives the town a soft landing: somewhere to walk after a train ride or lunch.

The preserved 18th- and 19th-century buildings show small-town craft and rural life in Småland. Families come for the playground and animals; travellers without children come for the shade, old oaks, and calm.
