The Bruno Mathsson Center is devoted to Värnamo's most famous designer, whose chairs and glass-house experiments helped define Swedish modernism. It includes his parents' home, the old furniture workshop, and a glass building connected to his ideas about light and living.

This is a quiet, specialist stop, but a rewarding one. If you care about Scandinavian furniture, it gives you the rare chance to see Mathsson's work in the town and domestic setting that shaped it.
