Pizzeria Chaplin is the kind of local place that makes a small town more memorable than expected. It opened in 1979 and became known in Värnamo not only for pizza, but for an interior and garden built by founder Zivan Tomic.

The useful reason to go is simple: it is central, informal, and tied to a real local story. If you want one unfussy meal in town after Vandalorum or Apladalen, this is a good candidate.
