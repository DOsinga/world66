Vandalorum is Värnamo's sharpest cultural stop, showing contemporary art, craft, and design in a cluster of red buildings just outside the centre. The exhibitions change, but the focus stays close to material, form, and Nordic design culture.

It is worth visiting even if you only have mild museum energy. The building, fields, shop, and cafe make it feel less like a formal art institution and more like a useful pause on a road trip through Småland.
