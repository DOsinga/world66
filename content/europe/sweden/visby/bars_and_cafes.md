---
title: "Bars and Cafes"
type: section
---

Visby's social life in summer revolves around its outdoor cafe terraces, particularly on Stora Torget and along the harbour. Fika — Swedish coffee-and-cake culture — is taken seriously here, and the bakeries are excellent.

For evening drinks, the harbour bars get the sunset, while the streets behind the cathedral host more grown-up bars in vaulted cellars. Try **Munkkallaren** (Monk's Cellar), in a 13th-century vaulted basement just off Stora Torget — long beer list, good for a late-night stop. **Skafferiet** is a popular wine bar in a converted merchant's house. In summer, the pop-up bars in Almedalen Park and along the waterfront stay lively until late.
