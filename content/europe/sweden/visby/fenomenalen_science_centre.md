Fenomenalen Science Centre gives Visby a family-friendly indoor stop beyond medieval walls and ruins. Its hands-on exhibits are aimed at children, with experiments and activities that make science playful rather than formal.

It is best for families, especially on a rainy day or when younger travellers need a break from heritage sightseeing. Check current opening hours, as smaller science centres can vary by season.
