---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

Gotland is overwhelmingly a summer destination, and Visby's character changes radically with the season.

**July and August** are the peak: hotels and restaurants are open, the ferries run frequently, the long Baltic evenings turn the limestone gold, and Medieval Week (early August) is in full theatrical swing. The downside is the crowds — the population of Gotland doubles in July — and the prices, which double with it.

**May, June and September** are the sweet spot for many visitors. The botanical garden is at its peak in May; the lilacs bloom in June; the September light along the wall is the prettiest of the year. You will have the ruins largely to yourself and most restaurants will still be open.

**Winter** is when Visby returns to being a quiet provincial town. Many places close, ferries are reduced, but the empty cobblestone streets in low slanting light are atmospheric in their own way, and the locals say December roses really do bloom here.
