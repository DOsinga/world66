---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

Västerås follows the standard Swedish seasonal pattern. Summer — June through August — is the obvious choice: long days, warm temperatures, the lake swimming beaches in full use, and the harbour district at its most lively. Days can easily stretch to 18 hours of light in June.

Spring and autumn are underrated. The trees around Anundshög and along the Svartån are attractive in autumn colour, and the city functions perfectly well without the summer crowds. Winter is cold and often grey, but the cathedral and museums are excellent reasons to visit, and the Christmas markets in December add some warmth to the dark months.
