Bornholm is a Danish island in the middle of the Baltic, three hours by Stena Line ferry from Ystad harbour. It has a distinct character within Denmark: round medieval churches (used for defence as much as worship), a strong smoked herring tradition centred on the village of Hasle, excellent ceramics and glass studios, and a landscape of rolling farmland, granite cliffs, and sandy coves in the south.

A day trip is feasible but tight. Most visitors find an overnight stay — or two or three — better suits the island's pace. Bicycle hire is available in Rønne and is the natural way to explore.
