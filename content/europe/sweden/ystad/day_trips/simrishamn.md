Simrishamn is a small fishing port 40 kilometres northeast of Ystad along the Österlen coast — the southeast corner of Skåne known for its farmland, apple orchards, and artists' studios. The town has a good preserved medieval church (St Nicholas), a quiet harbour, and the kind of low-key fish restaurants that a working port naturally produces.

Combining Simrishamn with Ales Stenar and the Kåseberga coast makes a full day of Österlen. The drive along the coast road is part of the point — rolling countryside, glimpses of the Baltic, the occasional mediaeval church visible from the road.
