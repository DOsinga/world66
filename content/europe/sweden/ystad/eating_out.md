---
title: 'Eating Out'
type: section
---

Ystad's restaurant scene is modest but solid. Hotel Continental's dining room is the traditional fine-dining option; the Lottas Krog on Stora Östergatan is the local favourite for Skåne cooking. Café and bakery stops are easy — Fridolfs Konditori on Lilla Norregatan has been making cakes since the 1920s. Outside town, the Kåseberga smokehouse is the obvious lunch when visiting Ales Stenar.
