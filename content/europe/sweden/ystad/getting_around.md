---
title: 'Getting Around'
type: section
---

Ystad's old town is small enough to walk entirely. The main sights — Stortorget, St Mary's Church, the Greyfriars Abbey, Per Helsa's Court, the Bio Saga cinema — are within 10 minutes of each other on foot. The harbour is a 15-minute walk from Stortorget via Hamngatan.

**Bicycle**: The town has a flat layout and marked cycling paths along the coast. Bikes can be rented from a couple of shops near the station. The coastal path east to Ystad Saltsjöbad beach is a pleasant 20-minute ride.

**By car**: Essential for reaching Ales Stenar (20 km east at Kåseberga) and Sandhammaren beach (25 km east). The coast road via Kåseberga is easy driving with good parking at both sites.

**Regional bus**: Skånetrafiken buses serve the villages east and west along the coast. The line to Kåseberga runs a few times a day in summer, less often in winter — check timetables before relying on it.

**Taxi**: A few local firms operate in Ystad. Journey times are short given the distances involved. Useful for returning from the harbour ferry terminal late at night.
