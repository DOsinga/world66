St Blaise Chapel is a small historic chapel near Saint-Triphon, south of Aigle. It is the kind of minor Swiss sight that works best when you are already exploring the villages and vineyards around the valley.

The chapel's value is in its setting and survival rather than spectacle. Pair it with Saint-Triphon and the Aigle vineyards for a slow, local afternoon away from the better-known Lake Geneva resorts.
