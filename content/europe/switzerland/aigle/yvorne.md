Yvorne is the wine village just north of Aigle, tucked into the Chablais vineyards. It is not a place of big monuments; its appeal is the lanes, cellars, and the sense that the valley is more agricultural than the fast railway line suggests.

Come here if you want to turn Aigle's castle-and-museum visit into a proper wine-country walk. The village is close enough for a short outing, and the vineyard slopes give broad views across the Rhône plain.
