The Furka Steam Railway runs over the old mountain route made redundant by the Furka Base Tunnel. The line is one of Europe's highest heritage railways, with steam trains, high alpine scenery, and a seasonal operation that still depends on major volunteer work to remove and reinstall equipment before and after winter.
