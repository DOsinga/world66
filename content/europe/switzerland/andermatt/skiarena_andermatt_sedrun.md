SkiArena Andermatt-Sedrun is the main winter draw in Andermatt, linking slopes above the Urseren Valley with terrain toward Sedrun. The area offers a mix of high mountain runs, pass scenery, and rail-linked villages, which makes it more varied than a single-resort ski hill.

In good snow conditions it is one of central Switzerland's stronger ski bases, especially for travellers who like big views and a less polished feel than the country's most famous resorts. Outside peak winter, check lift operations carefully before planning around it.
