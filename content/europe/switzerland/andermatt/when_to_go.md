---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

Andermatt has two distinct seasons. Winter (December to April) is for skiing: the Gemsstock above the village holds snow late into spring, and the linked Andermatt-Sedrun-Disentis ski area offers extensive terrain across three valleys. The north-facing Gemsstock runs are among the most reliably snowy in the Swiss Alps.

Summer (June to October) opens the alpine passes and the hiking and mountain biking trails. The Furka Pass is typically clear of snow from late June; the Oberalp and Gotthard from earlier in the season. Autumn is quiet and beautiful — the valley meadows turn gold and the passes are at their most dramatic, with early snow on the peaks. May and November are the shoulder months when lifts and many businesses close.
