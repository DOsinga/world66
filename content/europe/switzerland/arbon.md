Arbon is a small town on the southern shore of Lake Constance in the canton of Thurgau. Its old town sits on a promontory jutting into the lake, with the medieval [Schloss Arbon](/europe/switzerland/arbon/schloss_arbon) and a cluster of half-timbered houses around it. The [lakeside promenade](/europe/switzerland/arbon/lakefront_promenade) is one of the prettiest on the Swiss side of the Bodensee.

The town has a long industrial history — the Saurer truck and textile machinery factory was based here for over a century, and the old factory buildings are gradually being converted into cultural spaces at the [Saurer WerkZwei](/europe/switzerland/arbon/saurer_werkzwei). The lakefront park, the views across to the German shore, and the relaxed pace make Arbon a pleasant stop between St. Gallen and Konstanz.
