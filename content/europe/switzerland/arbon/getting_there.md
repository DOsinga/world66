---
title: "Getting There"
type: section
---

Arbon is on the lakeside railway line between Rorschach and Konstanz, with regular services to both. From St. Gallen, change at Rorschach — the total journey takes around thirty minutes. Arbon also has a ferry connection across the lake to Friedrichshafen in Germany in summer. By car it sits on the main Bodensee road, a scenic alternative to the motorway.
