Ascona is Ticino at its most polished: a small town on Lake Maggiore with pastel houses, palm trees, café terraces, and a waterfront that feels closer to Italy than to Zurich. It is quieter and more graceful than neighbouring [Locarno](/europe/switzerland/locarno), which is exactly the point.

The old lanes run down to Piazza Giuseppe Motta, the lakeside promenade where most visits begin and end. Above town, [Monte Verità](/europe/switzerland/ascona/monte_verita) adds a stranger, more interesting layer: an early 20th-century colony of artists, reformers, vegetarians, dancers, and free spirits who made this hill a European laboratory for alternative living.

Ascona works well as a base for slow days on Lake Maggiore. Boats run to the [Brissago Islands](/europe/switzerland/tessin/brissago_islands), bikes roll easily towards Locarno, and summer brings [JazzAscona](/europe/switzerland/ascona/jazzascona), when the lakefront turns into an open-air music venue.
