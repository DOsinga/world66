---
title: Getting There
type: section
---

**By train and bus.** Take the train to [Locarno](/europe/switzerland/locarno), then continue by local bus or taxi to Ascona. The ride is short, but in summer the road along the lake can be busy.

**By boat.** Seasonal boats on Lake Maggiore connect Ascona with Locarno, Brissago, and the Brissago Islands. They are slower than the bus and much more pleasant in good weather.

**By car.** From the Gotthard route, follow signs for Locarno and Ascona. Parking near the waterfront is limited; use signed car parks and walk into the old town.
