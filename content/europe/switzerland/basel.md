Basel, the second largest city of Switzerland, sits at the point where the Rhine bends north and three countries — Switzerland, France, and Germany — share a border. It is known as a pharmaceutical and financial centre, but that tells only part of the story. This is an intensely cultured city, and its long patronage of the arts has produced a remarkable concentration of museums and galleries for a place of under 200,000 people.

The old town divides along the Rhine. On the left bank, Grossbasel clusters around the [Basler Münster](/europe/switzerland/basel/mnstercathedral) — the red sandstone cathedral where Erasmus is buried — and the [Rathaus](/europe/switzerland/basel/rathaus), whose startlingly red facade dominates the Marktplatz. Cross to Kleinbasel via one of the current-powered [Rhine ferries](/europe/switzerland/basel/rhine_ferry) and the city feels more modern and less tourist-oriented.

The [Kunstmuseum](/europe/switzerland/basel/kunstmuseum) is Switzerland's most important public art collection, and a short walk brings you to the [Museum der Kulturen](/europe/switzerland/basel/museum_der_kulturen), the [Historical Museum](/europe/switzerland/basel/historical_museum), and the [Natural History Museum](/europe/switzerland/basel/natural_history_museum). Out in Riehen, the [Fondation Beyeler](/europe/switzerland/basel/fondation_beyeler) is one of Europe's best private collections. The [Museum Tinguely](/europe/switzerland/basel/tinguely_museum) on the Rhine bank showcases the kinetic sculptures of Jean Tinguely, whose playful [fountain](/europe/switzerland/basel/tinguely_fountain) also enlivens the Theatre square in the old town.

Basel's most visited annual event is Art Basel in June, one of the world's most important contemporary art fairs. The [Basler Fasnacht](/europe/switzerland/basel/when_to_go) carnival — which begins precisely at 4 am on the Monday after Ash Wednesday — is wilder and more atmospheric. In summer, locals float down the Rhine in waterproof bags called Wickelfisch; watching this from the bank is free entertainment.

A good day trip is to walk or cycle north to the [Dreiländereck](/europe/switzerland/basel/dreilaendereck), the point where Switzerland, France, and Germany meet in the Rhine.
