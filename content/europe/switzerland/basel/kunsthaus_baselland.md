Kunsthaus Baselland is a contemporary art venue focused on changing exhibitions, with particular attention to artists from the Basel region and the tri-border area where Switzerland, France, and Germany meet. Its programme is more experimental than the city's major museums, so it rewards visitors who want to see the local art scene rather than only the canonical collections.

Check the exhibition calendar before making the trip from central Basel.
