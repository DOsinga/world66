Schaulager is a distinctive Basel-area art institution created for storing, studying and occasionally exhibiting contemporary art. The Herzog & de Meuron building is part of the draw, with a heavy, sculptural presence that suits its hybrid role.

It is not always open like a normal museum, so check exhibitions and access before going. When open, it is a strong stop for serious contemporary-art visitors who have already seen Basel's central museums.
