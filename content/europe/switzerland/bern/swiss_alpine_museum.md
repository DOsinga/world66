The Swiss Alpine Museum looks at the Alps as landscape, workplace, cultural symbol, and sporting arena. Exhibitions cover subjects such as geology, glaciers, weather, farming, settlement, alpinism, and winter sports.

It is a good Bern stop if your trip includes the mountains but you want context beyond postcard scenery. The collection also includes Alpine art, with works connected to the way Switzerland has imagined and promoted its high country.
