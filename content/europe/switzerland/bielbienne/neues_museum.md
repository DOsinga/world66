Neues Museum Biel spreads across the Schwab and Neuhaus buildings and brings together art, archaeology, local history, film, and watchmaking. That mix fits Biel/Bienne well: the city is industrial, bilingual, and more varied than it first looks.

It is a good indoor stop if you want context for the region rather than another lake view or old-town stroll.
