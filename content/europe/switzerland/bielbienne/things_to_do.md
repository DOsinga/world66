---
title: Things to Do
type: section
---

Biel/Bienne sits between the Jura mountains and Lake Biel, and the things worth doing reflect both: the medieval old town with its Gothic church and ring square, the lake and its boat connections to St. Peter's Island, and the Jura hills above the town with their funiculars and gorge walks. The watchmaking industry has left its mark in museums and in the architecture of the company headquarters that ring the city centre.
