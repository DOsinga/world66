---
title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Breil/Brigels offers mountain walking in summer, uncrowded skiing in winter, and a clutch of remarkable things for a village of its size: a Robert Maillart bridge in the valley below, several ruined medieval castles on the ridges, an alpine lake, and access to the Rhine gorge. The Romansh language and culture give the place a character quite distinct from the German-Swiss ski resorts nearby.
