Brig is easy to dismiss as a rail junction, because trains to Zermatt, Milan, Andermatt, and the Glacier Express all pass through or near here. Stay a little longer and the town explains why routes mattered so much: it sits below the Simplon, where Alpine crossing, trade, military roads, and rail tunnels all meet.

The centre is compact and walkable, with [Stockalper Palace](/europe/switzerland/brig/stockalper_palace) giving the town far more architectural weight than most transit stops. Across the river in Naters, the [Swiss Guard Museum](/europe/switzerland/brig/swiss_guard_museum) adds an unusual angle, while the [Railway, Nature and Art Trail](/europe/switzerland/brig/railway_nature_and_art_trail) turns the railway landscape into a proper walk.

Brig is not as beautiful as nearby mountain resorts, but it is useful, historic, and more interesting than its timetable-board reputation. It works well as a half-day stop in Valais or as a practical base before heading over the Simplon.
