The Railway, Nature and Art Trail starts near Brig station and turns the local rail landscape into a walk rather than background noise. Over about 12 km it passes railway buildings, river stretches, public art, and views toward the north portal of the Simplon Tunnel.

It is a good choice if you have a long stopover and would rather move than sit with your luggage.
