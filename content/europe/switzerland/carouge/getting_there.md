---
title: "Getting There"
type: section
---

Carouge is a 15-minute tram ride from central Geneva. Tram 12 (direction Carouge-Rondeau) and Tram 15 (direction Palettes) both serve the neighbourhood, stopping at Place du Marché and Ancienne-Douane. Buy a ticket before boarding — Geneva's public transport covers Carouge within the standard zone.

By bike, Carouge is an easy 20-minute ride from the old city via the Arve riverbank. There are several Genèveroule bike-share docking stations in the neighbourhood.
