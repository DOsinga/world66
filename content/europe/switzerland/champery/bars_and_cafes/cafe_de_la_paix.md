A straightforward village café on the main street, open for coffee and breakfast from early morning and staying open through the afternoon. It serves the local community as much as tourists, and has the slightly worn, comfortable atmosphere of a place that has been here a long time. In the afternoons it draws a mix of older residents, off-duty ski instructors, and walkers recovering from the hill. Croissants and simple pastries in the morning; coffee and cold drinks through the day.
