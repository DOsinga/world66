The liveliest après-ski spot in the village, Le Farinet draws skiers off the mountain from mid-afternoon as the lifts wind down. It sits at the base of the village with a terrace that catches the last of the afternoon sun, and the atmosphere in peak winter weeks is reliably cheerful. The drinks list runs to beers on tap, vin chaud, and a short selection of spirits. In summer it is quieter but still open, making it a reasonable option for an evening drink before dinner.
