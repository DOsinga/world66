A small restaurant on the main street with a menu that blends Swiss staples with more eclectic influences — an unusual combination in a village this size. The fondue is reliably good, but the kitchen also turns out pasta dishes and salads suited to warm summer evenings. The space is informal with wooden walls and low lighting, making it comfortable for a long dinner after a day on the trails or the pistes.
