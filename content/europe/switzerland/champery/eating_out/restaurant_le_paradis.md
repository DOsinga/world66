A mountain restaurant on the Planachaux plateau, Le Paradis sits in a sun-trap position with a wide terrace facing the Dents du Midi. It functions primarily as a lunch stop for skiers in winter and hikers in summer. The menu is classic Alpine: croûte au fromage, rösti, soups, and grilled meats. The terrace fills up fast on good-weather days and the view from a sunny lunch table here — snowy peaks in winter, flower-covered meadows in summer — is one of the simple pleasures of Champéry.
