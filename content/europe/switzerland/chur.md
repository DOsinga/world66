Chur claims the title of oldest city in Switzerland, with evidence of settlement stretching back more than 5,000 years. Tucked into the Rhine valley at the foot of the Alps, it is the capital of Graubünden, the country's largest and most sparsely populated canton. The [old town](/europe/switzerland/chur/chur_altstadt) is small enough to walk in an hour, but dense with crooked lanes, painted facades, and quiet squares that reward a slower pace.

The city works as a gateway to some of Switzerland's most spectacular mountain scenery. The Glacier Express and Bernina Express both depart from Chur, threading through valleys and over passes that justify Switzerland's reputation for dramatic train journeys. In winter, the ski resorts of Arosa and Lenzerheide are less than an hour away. In summer, the hiking trails that fan out into the surrounding mountains are world-class.

[Chur Cathedral](/europe/switzerland/chur/chur_cathedral), dating from the 12th century, anchors the upper old town, and the [Bündner Kunstmuseum](/europe/switzerland/chur/bundner_kunstmuseum) — with its outstanding Giacometti collection — is a short walk away. In the lanes between them, you will find the [Gigerplatz fountain](/europe/switzerland/chur/gigerplatz_fountain), quietly celebrating H.R. Giger, Chur's most famous modern son. For a wider view, walk or cycle north along the Rhine to [Haldenstein Castle](/europe/switzerland/chur/haldenstein_castle), perched above the valley with panoramic views back toward the city.
