The Casino Crans-Montana occupies a central position on the main street in Crans and serves as the resort's primary evening venue for those who want something beyond a hotel bar. The gaming floor has the standard table games — roulette, blackjack, and poker — along with slot machines. But the casino draws as much for its restaurant and concert programme as for the gambling.

The bar is open to non-gamblers and fills up on winter evenings with a well-dressed crowd. Live music performances and DJs are scheduled throughout the winter season, typically from Thursday to Saturday. Entry to the gaming areas requires a valid passport or national ID card, and the dress code, while not strict, discourages ski gear.
