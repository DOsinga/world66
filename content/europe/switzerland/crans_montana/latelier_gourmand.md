L'Atelier Gourmand is the most serious restaurant in Crans itself, a modern Swiss kitchen running tight, market-driven menus that change with the season. The cooking is technically accomplished without being fussy — local produce from the Rhône Valley and the mountains above it, treated with care rather than theatrical excess. The wine list gives proper coverage to Valais appellations alongside broader Swiss and French options.

The room is sleek and the service attentive but not stiff. Prices are at the upper end of the resort range, and you are paying for cooking that is genuinely good rather than merely expensive. Reservations are recommended in high season; the restaurant also caters to non-guests of the hotel it is associated with.
