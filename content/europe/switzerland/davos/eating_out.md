---
title: "Eating Out"
type: section
---

Davos has the full range of Swiss mountain resort eating — fondue and raclette in wooden-beamed restaurants, international hotel dining, and a decent smattering of Asian and Italian options for those who've had enough cheese. Prices are resort-level throughout. The Promenade (Davos Platz's main strip) has the densest concentration of restaurants. Most of the better eating is in hotels.
