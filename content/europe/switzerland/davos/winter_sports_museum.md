The Winter Sports Museum is a compact Davos collection devoted to the equipment, images, and stories of alpine winter sport. Skis, skates, sleds, posters, and photographs trace how the resort became a laboratory for cold-weather leisure and competition.

It is a niche museum, but a fitting one in a town whose identity is built around snow. Visit on a poor-weather afternoon or before heading onto the slopes, and Davos' sporting past becomes more visible in the modern resort around you.
