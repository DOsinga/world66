Bogn Engiadina is Scuol's modern expression of an old spa tradition. The complex uses the town's mineral waters in indoor and outdoor pools, saunas, and Roman-Irish bathing rooms, with mountain views from parts of the bath area.

It is not a wild Alpine experience, but it is exactly the right thing after a day on the slopes or the trails. Scuol makes more sense once you have soaked here.
