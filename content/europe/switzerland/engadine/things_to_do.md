type: section
---

The Engadine offers skiing in winter (centred on [Corviglia](/europe/switzerland/engadine/st_moritz) and Corvatsch), walking and mountaineering in summer, and the [Bernina Express](/europe/switzerland/engadine/bernina_express) year-round. The [Morteratsch Glacier](/europe/switzerland/engadine/morteratsch_glacier) walk, [Sils Maria](/europe/switzerland/engadine/sils_maria), and the [Julier Pass](/europe/switzerland/engadine/julier_pass) with its Roman columns are the standout excursions.
