---
title: When to Go
type: section
---

The Engadine has two main seasons with a shoulder period between.

**Winter (December to March)** is the classic period for St. Moritz and the ski resorts. The snow is reliable, the light on the lake is extraordinary, and the valley runs at full capacity — which means full prices and full restaurants. The lake freezes thick enough for events from January. The Cresta Run operates November through February.

**Summer (late June to September)** brings hikers, cyclists, and the Bernina Express crowd. The glacier walks are accessible, the valley is warm by day (18–22°C) but cool at night, and the wildflowers on the high passes bloom in July. Sils Maria and the Fex Valley are best in summer. **July and August** are the peak months with corresponding pressure on accommodation.

**Shoulder periods** (April–May and October–November) are quiet. Some facilities close between seasons. The passes may still have snow in May; October brings autumn colour to the larch forests. Hotel rates drop significantly.

The **Engadin Ski Marathon** in March is one of the largest cross-country ski races in Europe, with 42,000 participants on the Engadine lakes — if you are here, plan around it.
