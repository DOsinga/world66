Titlis is the headline excursion from Engelberg: a 3,238-metre mountain with glacier scenery, cable-car access and a panorama that can reach deep across the Swiss Alps. The cliff walk near the top is short, exposed and memorable, especially on a clear day.

Weather matters here. If clouds are sitting on the summit, wait if you can; the whole point is the height and the view.
