Talmuseum Engelberg is the valley museum, housed in the protected Wappenhaus near the centre of the village. Its collection covers local life, religious objects, and the history of Engelberg and its surrounding valley.

It is a useful indoor stop when you want context for the abbey, the village, and the mountain culture beyond the ski lifts.
