---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

Flims has two distinct high seasons. In winter (December–March) it is a ski resort: the Flims-Laax-Falera area has over 220 kilometres of pistes and serious snowboard parks. Summer (June–September) brings hikers, mountain bikers, and swimmers. The shoulder months of May and October are quiet and inexpensive but can be cold. July and August are the best months for the Caumasee and the Rhine Gorge walks.
