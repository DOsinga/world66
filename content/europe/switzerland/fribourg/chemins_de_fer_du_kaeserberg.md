Chemins de fer du Kaeserberg is a miniature railway museum north of Fribourg, with a large model network running through an imagined Swiss landscape. The display has more than two kilometres of track at HO scale, arranged across several levels.

Visits are usually timed as a show rather than a casual drop-in, so check the schedule and reservation rules before going. It is best for railway enthusiasts and families with patient children.
