Musée de la Bière Cardinal is a small beer museum in former storage cellars connected with Fribourg's brewing history. Exhibits explain the old Cardinal brewery and traditional brewing methods.

It is a niche stop, but worthwhile if you are interested in Swiss beer culture or want a short indoor visit outside the usual churches and old-town viewpoints.
