La Perle du Lac is one of Geneva's loveliest lakeside parks, set on the right bank with lawns, mature trees, and open views across the water. It is refined rather than wild, very much in keeping with Geneva's polished lakefront character.

Use it as part of a walk along the northern shore of Lake Geneva, especially in clear weather when the mountains sharpen the horizon. The park is also a good place to slow down between museums, international institutions, and the busier quays.
