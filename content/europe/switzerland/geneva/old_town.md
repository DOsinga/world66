Geneva's Old Town is the compact historic core around St Pierre Cathedral. Its lanes, small squares, city hall, cannons, and old houses make it the easiest place in the city to understand Geneva before its banking and international-institution era.

Allow an hour or two to wander, with Maison Tavel, the Reformation Wall, and the cathedral as natural anchors for the walk.
