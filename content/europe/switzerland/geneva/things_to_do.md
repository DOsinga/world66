title: Things to Do
type: section
---

Geneva sits at the southwest tip of Lake Geneva (Lac Léman), with the Alps visible on clear days. The city is small, walkable, and packed with sights for its size — a function of being the global headquarters of dozens of international organisations and a centre of Protestant Reformation history.

The old city (Vieille Ville) rises above the lake on the left bank, centred on the medieval [Cathédrale Saint-Pierre](/europe/switzerland/geneva/cathedrale_saint_pierre) where John Calvin preached. The narrow streets around the cathedral, leading down to Place du Bourg-de-Four, are the prettiest in the city. The [Reformation Wall](/europe/switzerland/geneva/reformation_wall) in the [Parc des Bastions](/europe/switzerland/geneva/parc_des_bastions) is the city's most distinctive monument, and the [Maison Tavel](/europe/switzerland/geneva/maison_tavel) nearby is the oldest house in the city.

The right bank holds the UN district — the [Palais des Nations](/europe/switzerland/geneva/palais_des_nations) and the powerful [International Red Cross Museum](/europe/switzerland/geneva/red_cross_museum) — plus the train station and the [Pâquis](/europe/switzerland/geneva/paquis) quarter. On the lake itself, the [Jet d'Eau](/europe/switzerland/geneva/jetdeau) — a 140-metre water jet — has become Geneva's symbol; the [Flower Clock](/europe/switzerland/geneva/flower_clock) sits a short walk away in the Jardin Anglais.

For art, the [Musée d'Art et d'Histoire](/europe/switzerland/geneva/musee_art_histoire) is the largest in Switzerland and free. The [MAMCO](/europe/switzerland/geneva/mamco) houses contemporary art in a converted factory in [Plainpalais](/europe/switzerland/geneva/plainpalais). The [Barbier-Mueller](/europe/switzerland/geneva/barbier_mueller) has outstanding tribal art, the [Patek Philippe Museum](/europe/switzerland/geneva/patek_philippe) is the world's finest watch collection, and the [Musée d'Histoire des Sciences](/europe/switzerland/geneva/musee_histoire_sciences) is set in a free neoclassical villa. Outside the centre, the [CERN](/europe/switzerland/geneva/cern) particle physics laboratory offers free public tours by booking, and the [Carouge](/europe/switzerland/geneva/carouge) district south of the centre has a Sardinian-influenced character all of its own.
