---
title: When to Go
type: section
---

Summer is the obvious time to visit Gerzensee — the lake is swimmable, the cycling routes across the plateau are dry, and Alpine views to the south are at their clearest. June and September are often pleasanter than August when the plateau can be humid.

Spring brings wildflowers to the Belpberg slopes. Winter is quiet; the plateau is often under cloud while the Alps above are in sunshine, so days can be grey. Bern is only 20 minutes away and accessible year-round.
