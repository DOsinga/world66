First (2,168 m) is the main summer mountain destination above Grindelwald, reached by an 30-minute gondola from the village. The plateau gives access to a network of hiking trails including the classic First to Schynige Platte traverse — a five- to six-hour ridge walk with uninterrupted views of the Eiger, Mönch, and Jungfrau.

The area has been developed into an adventure zone with the First Flyer zip line, the First Glider paragliding launch, a cliff walk, and mountain carts for the descent. The Bachalpsee, a high lake reflecting the Schreckhorn and Finsteraarhorn, is a 45-minute walk from the top station and one of the most photographed views in Switzerland. Even without any activities, the gondola ride up and the views across the valley make First worth the trip.
