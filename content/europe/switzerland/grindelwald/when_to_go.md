---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

Grindelwald has two clear seasons. **Summer (June to September)** is for hiking — the trails are clear of snow, the wildflowers are out, and the days are long. July and August are the busiest and most expensive months; June and September offer the same hiking with smaller crowds and lower prices.

**Winter (December to March)** is ski season. Grindelwald sits in the Jungfrau ski area, which connects with Wengen and Mürren for a combined 213 km of pistes. The Jungfraujoch excursion can be done year-round but the light and visibility are best in summer.

April, May, and October/November are shoulder months: many lifts are closed, some trails are still icy, and the village is quiet. Not the best time unless you have a specific reason to be there.
