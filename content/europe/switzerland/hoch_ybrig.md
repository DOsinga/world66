Hoch-Ybrig is a family-friendly ski resort in the Schwyz Pre-Alps, about 90 minutes from [Zurich](/europe/switzerland/zurich) by public transport. You take the train to [Einsiedeln](/europe/switzerland/einsiedeln) and then a bus up into the mountains. With around 50 km of pistes — mostly blue and red runs — it draws families and intermediate skiers rather than thrill-seekers, and the slopes rarely feel crowded. A handy combined ticket covering train, bus, and lift pass can be bought at the station, which takes the hassle out of getting there.

In winter, the [ski area](/europe/switzerland/hoch_ybrig/ski_area) runs from the base station at Weglosen (1,035 m) up to the Sternen area at 1,856 m. The [NBC Snowpark](/europe/switzerland/hoch_ybrig/ski_area) adds kickers and rails for those who want them, and a long [toboggan run](/europe/switzerland/hoch_ybrig/toboggan_run) descends from the mountain to the valley — with night sessions on select evenings.

In summer the ski runs give way to hiking trails. The [Panoramawanderweg](/europe/switzerland/hoch_ybrig/panoramawanderweg) between the Sternen and [Spirstock](/europe/switzerland/hoch_ybrig/spirstock) summits is the standout route — flat enough for all fitness levels, with wide views across the Alps of Schwyz, Uri, and Glarus. The lifts run in summer too, so you can reach the high terrain without the climb. It is a small resort, not a destination you would plan a whole trip around, but as a day or weekend escape from Zurich it works very well.
