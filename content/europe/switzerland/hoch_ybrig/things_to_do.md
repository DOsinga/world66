Hoch-Ybrig's appeal is simple: in winter it is a small, uncrowded ski area well suited to families and intermediate skiers; in summer it becomes a hiking destination with panoramic views across the pre-Alpine landscape of canton Schwyz. The lifts run in both seasons, so you can get up to the higher terrain without the climb.
