Klosters is a small, unhurried alpine village in the [Prättigau valley](/europe/switzerland/klosters/prättigau_valley), 20 minutes from Davos but a world apart in character. While Davos has grown into a conference city, Klosters has stayed resolutely itself: a scatter of wooden chalets, church steeples, and flower-hung balconies backed by high peaks. The British royal family has been coming here for decades — [Chalet Eugenia](/europe/switzerland/klosters/chalet_eugenia) became famous through decades of royal visits — which says something about the place: quiet, tasteful, and thoroughly alpine.

The skiing connects via the [Gotschnagrat](/europe/switzerland/klosters/gotschnagrat) into the [Parsenn ski area](/europe/switzerland/klosters/parsenn_ski_area), giving access to one of Switzerland's largest ski networks without the Davos crowds. The [Madrisa](/europe/switzerland/klosters/madrisa) area on the other side of the valley is quieter and good for families. In summer, the valley is excellent walking country, with trails through the [Schlappin Valley](/europe/switzerland/klosters/schlappin_valley) and up onto high pastures. The [Sunniberg Bridge](/europe/switzerland/klosters/sunniberg_bridge) spans the valley just west of the village — one of the most elegant highway bridges in the Alps — and the [Schwarzsee](/europe/switzerland/klosters/schwarzsee) lake at Laret makes a fine detour toward Davos.
