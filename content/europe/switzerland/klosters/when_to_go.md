---
title: When to Go
type: section
---

Klosters has two distinct high seasons with a quiet shoulder in between.

**Winter (December–April)** is the primary season for most visitors. The ski area opens in late November or early December depending on snowfall, and the Parsenn runs stay open into April, sometimes later at altitude. The resort is busiest over Christmas and New Year, then again in February during Swiss school holidays. The royal family's annual visits tend to fall in late January or early February, bringing a brief burst of photographers to the village.

**Summer (late June–September)** is quieter but increasingly popular, with excellent hiking from the cable car stations and a peaceful atmosphere in the village. The Gotschna and Madrisa gondolas operate for walkers and cyclists, and temperatures on the valley floor are warm but rarely oppressive. July is the peak summer month.

May and June, and October and November, are slow: lifts are closed, some hotels and restaurants shut for the shoulder season, and the village is at its quietest.
