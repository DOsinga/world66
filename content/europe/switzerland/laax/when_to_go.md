---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

Laax is a year-round resort with two clear peaks. The ski season runs from late November to late April, with the best snow typically in January and February. The Vorab glacier extends the season at the top, and in good snow years runs remain open into May. The resort's altitude and north-facing aspects give it reliable snow cover, though it is not immune to warm spells in mid-winter.

Summer (June to September) is increasingly popular for mountain biking and hiking, and July and August are warm enough to swim in the Caumasee. The shoulder months — May and October — are quiet, with some lifts closed and the village subdued. The freestyle snowpark draws its own crowd in early season (December) when the pipe and park team is building features for the winter ahead.
