Lausanne sits at the centre of a stretch of Lake Geneva shore that offers some of Switzerland's best day trips. The rail connections are excellent, and most destinations are under an hour away.

[Montreux](/europe/switzerland/montreux) is 25 minutes by train, known for its jazz festival in July and for the Château de Chillon just beyond the town — a well-preserved medieval castle on a rocky islet in the lake. [Vevey](/europe/switzerland/vevey) is 15 minutes by train, a quieter and more local town with a large Saturday market and a Charlie Chaplin museum. The Lavaux vineyard walk between the two towns can be done in an afternoon.
