Fondation de l'Hermitage occupies a handsome nineteenth-century house above Lausanne, with gardens and views that are nearly as much a part of the visit as the art. The foundation is built around temporary exhibitions, often drawing from strong European collections rather than relying on a fixed permanent display.

Check the current show before climbing up the hill, because the character of a visit changes with each exhibition. When the programme suits you, it is one of Lausanne's most pleasant museum experiences: quiet rooms, good scale, and a graceful setting away from the lakeside crowds.
