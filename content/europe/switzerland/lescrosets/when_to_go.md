---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

The ski season runs roughly from mid-December to mid-April. The best snow on the upper slopes is usually from late January to March; February is the busiest month thanks to school holidays in Switzerland, France, and the UK.

Summer brings a second short season from late June to early September, when most of the lifts reopen for walkers and mountain bikers. The high pastures are at their best in July, with wildflowers across the slopes. April–May and October–November are shoulder months — the lifts are closed and most restaurants in the village shut down.
