Tobogganing Park Leysin turns a snowy slope into a set of groomed tubing runs, with banked curves and enough speed to feel playful without needing ski skills. It is a good choice for families, mixed-ability groups, or anyone who wants a winter activity that is simpler than committing to a full ski day.

The park is seasonal and weather-dependent, so check conditions before building a day around it. When it is running well, it adds a cheerful, low-barrier option to Leysin's mountain activities.
