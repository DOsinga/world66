Falconeria Locarno is a small bird-of-prey centre a short walk from Locarno's old town, built around live falconry demonstrations rather than static cages. Hawks, owls, vultures, and eagles fly low over the audience, which makes the show more engaging than a standard animal display.

It is an easy half-day add-on if you are travelling with children or want a break from lakefront strolling and mountain viewpoints. Check show times before going, since the visit makes most sense when you can see the birds in flight rather than only walking the grounds.
