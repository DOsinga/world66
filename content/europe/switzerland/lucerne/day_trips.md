Lucerne is built for excursions. The lake's boats and a dense network of mountain railways put a whole region of peaks, valleys, and lakeside towns within an easy day's reach of the old town.

The obvious trip is [Rigi](/europe/switzerland/rigi), the mountain massif rising straight out of the lake to the east. Take a boat across to Vitznau and ride Europe's oldest rack railway to the summit ridge for one of the great central-Swiss panoramas, then come down a different way to make a loop of the whole mountain. Mount [Titlis](/europe/switzerland/lucerne/mount_titlis), reached by train to Engelberg and a sequence of cable cars onto a glacier, is the other big mountain day — snow and altitude without any technical effort.

Closer to hand, the [Felsenweg on the Bürgenstock](/europe/switzerland/lucerne/felsenweg_buergerstock) is a short cliffside walk above the water with wide views over the lake and the surrounding peaks. The lake boats themselves are worth a day, calling at Brunnen and Flüelen and at the meadow of Rütli, the mythic spot where Switzerland was said to have been founded.
