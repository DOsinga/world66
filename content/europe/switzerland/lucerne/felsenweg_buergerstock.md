The Felsenweg on the Buergenstock is one of the classic short walks above Lake Lucerne, tracing the cliff line with wide views over the lake, Lucerne, Rigi, Pilatus, and the central Swiss Alps. The route is engineered rather than wild, with railings, tunnels, and lookout points that make the drama accessible without requiring a serious mountain day.

It works best as part of a lake-and-funicular excursion from Lucerne. Weather matters: on a clear day the walk feels spectacular, while low cloud can reduce it to a damp path through trees. Combine it with the Hammetschwand lift when open for the most memorable version of the outing.
