The Lucerne Culture and Congress Centre, usually shortened to KKL Luzern, is the city's major modern architectural statement. Designed by Jean Nouvel, it sits beside the lake and station with a broad projecting roof, reflecting pools, concert halls, and exhibition spaces.

Even without a ticket, the building is worth a look while walking between the station, lakefront, and old town. With a performance, it becomes one of Lucerne's best cultural experiences, especially during the city's classical music calendar.
