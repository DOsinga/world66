Mineralbad & Spa Rigi Kaltbad is a modern mountain spa designed by Mario Botta. The main draw is the heated outdoor mineral pool, where the view opens across Lake Lucerne and the surrounding peaks; steam baths and quieter spa areas add to the visit.
