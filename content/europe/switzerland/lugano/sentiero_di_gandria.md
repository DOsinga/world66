Sentiero di Gandria is the lakeside walking path from the end of Lugano's promenade toward the village of Gandria. Also called the Olive Trail, it follows the water with views across Lake Lugano and interpretive panels about the olive trees that once shaped this shore.

It is one of Lugano's easiest rewarding walks: start from Castagnola, take your time along the lake, then continue into Gandria for stone lanes, terraces and the option of returning by boat.
