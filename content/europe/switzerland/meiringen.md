Meiringen is a mountain village in the Haslital, the upper Aare valley east of [Interlaken](/europe/switzerland/interlaken). Several Alpine routes meet here, which once made it a market stop and now makes it a useful base for walks, trains, buses, and passes through the Bernese Oberland.

The town is best known for [Reichenbach Falls](/europe/switzerland/meiringen/reichenbach_falls), where Arthur Conan Doyle sent Sherlock Holmes and Professor Moriarty over the edge in "The Final Problem". Meiringen has leaned into the connection with a small [Sherlock Holmes Museum](/europe/switzerland/meiringen/sherlock_holmes_museum), but the landscape would be worth visiting without the literary murder attempt. The [Aare Gorge](/europe/switzerland/meiringen/aare_gorge), just east of town, cuts through limestone in a narrow walkway of water, cliffs, and shadow.

In winter, Meiringen-Hasliberg is a modest ski area rather than a high-altitude powerhouse. In summer, it is better: lifts, valley trains, walking paths, the gorge, and the high roads toward Grimsel and Susten passes all make the village a practical, unpretentious base.
