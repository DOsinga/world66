This small model railway recreates the metre-gauge Albula line in Graubunden. The layout was first built in Interlaken, dismantled in 2010, and rebuilt in Meiringen in 2015.

It is a modest stop, but railway fans and families can pair it easily with Meiringen's Sherlock Holmes sights and the nearby mountain railways.
