The Swiss Museum of Games occupies a lakeside castle setting in La Tour-de-Peilz, just
along the shore from Montreux. Its subject is playful but broad, covering board games,
strategy, chance, and the social worlds that gather around games.

The museum is a pleasant change of pace from vineyards, jazz heritage, and Belle Epoque
lakefront scenery. It works especially well for families or on a rainy day, and the
lakeside location makes it easy to combine with a walk toward Vevey or Montreux.
