---
title: "Getting There"
type: section
---

Morgins is about 100 kilometres from Geneva Airport, the most convenient international entry point. The drive takes around ninety minutes in summer; allow extra time in winter for mountain road conditions and possible chains requirement on the final climb into the village.

By public transport, take the train from Geneva to Monthey (direct or via Lausanne), then a bus or taxi up the valley. The PostBus runs to Morgins but service is infrequent, particularly outside ski season. Most visitors arrive by car, especially those with ski equipment.

The village is also accessible from the French side via Châtel, which connects through the Pas de Morgins on a minor mountain road (closed in winter). In the ski season, the most practical French approach is to drive to Châtel or Avoriaz and ski across the border.
