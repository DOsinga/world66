---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

**Winter (December to April)** is the primary season. The ski lifts typically open in mid-December and close in mid-April, depending on snow. January and February are the coldest and snowiest months. The Portes du Soleil is a mid-altitude domain (the village sits at 1,350m), which means in poor snow years the lower slopes can be thin — the best skiing is often accessed by going higher, toward Avoriaz on the French side.

**Summer (July to September)** is the second season, when hikers and mountain bikers replace skiers. July and August are warm but not hot — a welcome contrast to the valleys below. The lifts run from late June through early September.

**Spring and autumn** are quiet and most infrastructure closes. The passes above the village can hold snow into May, and the lifts are rarely running before late June.
