---
title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Mürren's activities divide naturally between the peaks above and the trails along the cliff edge. The Schilthorn cable car is the headline attraction, but the Allmendhubel funicular and the Northface Trail give even casual walkers access to the drama. In winter, the piste map is modest compared to Grindelwald or Wengen, but that is part of the appeal — shorter queues and a village that feels like skiing actually used to feel.
