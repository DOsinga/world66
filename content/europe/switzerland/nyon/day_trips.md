Nyon sits in a prime position on Lac Léman with the Jura foothills rising behind it, so there is no shortage of day trip options. Cross the lake to the medieval village of [Yvoire](/europe/france/alpes/yvoire) by CGN boat in twenty minutes, or walk [Le Sentier des Toblerones](/europe/switzerland/le_sentier_des_toblerones) — a seventeen-kilometre trail following WWII anti-tank barriers from the lakeshore up into the hills above Nyon. Closer to town, the [Château de Prangins](/europe/switzerland/nyon/prangins_castle) is a short walk along the lakeshore.
