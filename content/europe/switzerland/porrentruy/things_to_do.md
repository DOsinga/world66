---
title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Porrentruy is a compact old town that rewards a half-day's walking. The [Château de Porrentruy](/europe/switzerland/porrentruy/chateau_de_porrentruy) dominates from its promontory and is the most visible landmark. Below it, the old town holds an eighteenth-century fountain square, the [Jesuit Church of St-Pierre](/europe/switzerland/porrentruy/eglise_st_pierre), and handsome stone townhouses.

The area's most distinctive draw is its paleontology: the Ajoie plain yielded one of Europe's richest Late Jurassic fossil sites at Courtedoux, and the [JURASSICA Museum](/europe/switzerland/porrentruy/jurassica_museum) in town is the best place to see the results. For those wanting to walk, the [Gorges du Doubs](/europe/switzerland/porrentruy/gorges_du_doubs) to the north offer dramatic river scenery.
