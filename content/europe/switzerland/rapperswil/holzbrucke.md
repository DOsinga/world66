The Holzbrücke is a wooden footbridge linking Rapperswil with the village of Hurden on the opposite shore of the lake. At 841 metres it is one of the longer wooden bridges in Europe, and the walk across it — with the lake on both sides and the Alps visible on clear days — is one of the most satisfying short walks in the Zurich region.

The bridge follows the line of an ancient lake crossing that has been in use since prehistoric times. Archaeology has documented pile dwellings on both shores and in the shallows dating to the Bronze Age; the medieval bridge that stood here was an important route between Zurich and the eastern cantons. The small chapel of Heilig Hüsli stands at the Rapperswil end, an 18th-century successor to a medieval sanctuary that marked the start of the pilgrim route to Einsiedeln.
