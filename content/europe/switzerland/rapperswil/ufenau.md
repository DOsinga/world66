Ufenau is a small island in Lake Zurich between Pfäffikon and Rapperswil. Visitors come for the easy scenic walk around the island, lake views, and the quiet change of pace from the busy lakeside towns.

There is a restaurant on the island, and in summer it is also a popular setting for weddings and boat excursions.
