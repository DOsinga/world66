Museum im Lagerhaus is the quieter counterpoint to Saint Gallen's big-ticket abbey and textile sights. Its focus is outsider art, naive art, and art brut: work made outside the usual academy-and-gallery circuit, often by self-taught artists whose lives matter as much as their technique.

The museum suits travellers who have already seen the abbey precinct and want something less polished but more personal. Expect compact exhibitions rather than a grand collection march; the reward is a look at Swiss and international art that rarely fits neatly into the standard art-history timeline.
