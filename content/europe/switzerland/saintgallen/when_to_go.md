---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

Saint Gallen works well year-round. The abbey and library are open throughout the year, though the library has a winter and summer display rotation and may be briefly closed between exhibitions. Summer is pleasant; the Drei Weieren swimming ponds above the city are a local institution in July and August.

The city is not primarily a tourist destination, which means it never feels overwhelmed by visitors. May to October are the most comfortable months for walking the old town; winter can be grey and damp, though the Christmas market is one of the better ones in eastern Switzerland. The Olma agricultural fair in October draws visitors from across the region.
