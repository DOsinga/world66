---
title: 'Getting There'
type: section
---

Schiers is on the Rhätische Bahn (RhB) Klosters line. Hourly trains run from Landquart (a major junction reached from Zurich Airport in about 90 minutes) up the Prättigau, stopping in Schiers in about 20 minutes. From Schiers the line continues to [Klosters](/europe/switzerland/klosters) (20 minutes) and on to [Davos](/europe/switzerland/davos). The Swiss Travel Pass is valid on the entire RhB network.
