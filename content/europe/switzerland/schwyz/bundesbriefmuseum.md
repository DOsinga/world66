The Museum of the Swiss Charters of Confederation is Schwyz's essential stop. It presents the documents, seals, and political memory of the early Confederation in the town most closely associated with Switzerland's name.

This is not a flashy museum, and that suits the material. Give it time if you are interested in how a mountain alliance became a country; skip quickly if you only want scenery.
