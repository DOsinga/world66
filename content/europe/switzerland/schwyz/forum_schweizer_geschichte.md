Forum Schweizer Geschichte Schwyz is a history museum in an 18th-century building near the centre of town. Its permanent exhibition looks at Swiss history through everyday objects, daily life, and changing social habits.

Pair it with the nearby Museum of the Swiss Charters of Confederation. Together they give Schwyz more depth than its pretty streets first suggest.
