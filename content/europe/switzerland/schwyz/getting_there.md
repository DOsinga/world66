---
title: Getting There
type: section
---

**By train.** Schwyz station sits below the town centre on the Gotthard line, with regular trains from Zurich, Zug, Arth-Goldau, and Lucerne. Buses connect the station with the old town, Ibach, and the valley villages.

**By car.** The A4 motorway passes nearby, making Schwyz straightforward from Zurich and Lucerne. In good weather the roads up to the Mythen and Stoos areas are busy; use public transport if you plan a linear walk.

**For Hoch-Ybrig.** Travellers heading to Hoch-Ybrig usually change here or at Einsiedeln for buses into the valley, which is why Schwyz appears in many mountain-route directions.
