Rotenfluh is the easier mountain option above Schwyz, reached by lift from Rickenbach and set close to the Mythen. It gives big views without demanding the sharper summit climb of Grosser Mythen.

Use it for a gentler walk, a meal with a view, or a family-friendly mountain day. In clear weather the panorama over Lake Lucerne and the Central Swiss ridges is more than enough reward.
