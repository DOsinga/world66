The Stoosbahn climbs from the valley south of Schwyz to the car-free mountain village of Stoos. Its steep gradient and unusual cabins make the ride feel like a piece of Swiss engineering theatre.

Once at the top, continue to the ridge walks above Stoos if the weather is good. The railway is fun on its own, but the views towards Lake Lucerne are the real reason to go.
