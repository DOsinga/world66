Simmental is the long alpine valley that runs southwest from Thun toward [Zweisimmen](/europe/switzerland/simmental/zweisimmen_village) and the Saanenland, cutting through the pre-Alps of the Bernese Oberland. It is the ancestral home of the [Simmental cattle breed](/europe/switzerland/simmental/simmental_cattle) — the large red-and-white cows grazing on every steep slope have been exported to farms across the world, though they look most at home here against a backdrop of dark wooden chalets and mountain pasture.

The valley is a transit corridor as much as a destination in itself. Travelers heading from the Swiss Mittelland to Gstaad or Lenk pass through Zweisimmen, the main town at the valley's southern end. A few stop for longer, drawn by the relative quiet and authenticity compared to the resort towns beyond the Saaner ridge. The [Simme river](/europe/switzerland/simmental/simme_river_walk) runs clear through the whole length, and the walking and cycling along its banks is straightforward and genuinely pretty.

Historically, the valley's prosperity came from cattle trading and cheese — the local Hobelkäse, a hard aged cheese shaved into paper-thin curls, is still made by valley dairies. The older villages like [Boltigen](/europe/switzerland/simmental/boltigen_village) have a low-key, unhurried feel that the higher-altitude resorts have long since traded away. The [Jaunpass](/europe/switzerland/simmental/jaun_pass) road at the valley's western edge leads into French-speaking Fribourg canton — one of the more interesting short drives in the region when it is open. In winter, [Lenk ski resort](/europe/switzerland/simmental/lenk_ski_resort) is the valley's main draw, and the alpine cirque at [Iffigenalp](/europe/switzerland/simmental/iffigenalp) above it is one of the better summer walking destinations in the Bernese Oberland. The [Seebergsee](/europe/switzerland/simmental/seebergsee) above Zweisimmen and the [Weissenburg Castle ruins](/europe/switzerland/simmental/weissenburg_castle) in the lower valley round out the sights.
