The 11i Uhr is a modern clock that plays with Solothurn's fondness for the number eleven. It has only eleven hours on its face, and several times a day it plays the Solothurn Song.

It is a quick stop rather than a major sight, but it fits the city's small eccentric traditions nicely. Look for it while walking the old centre, especially if you enjoy local oddities that would make no sense anywhere else.
