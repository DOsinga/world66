Zeitglockenturm is Solothurn's old clock tower, standing over the main square. It is the oldest building in the city and carries an astronomical clock with figures and inscriptions that echo the better-known Zytglogge in Bern.

The tower is an easy landmark for orienting yourself in the old town, and one of the best quick stops for visitors interested in Solothurn's medieval fabric.
