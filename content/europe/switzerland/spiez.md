Spiez is the gentle side of the Bernese Oberland: a small town on a sheltered bay of Lake Thun, with vineyards on the slope, a castle above the water, and the pyramid of the [Niesen](/europe/switzerland/spiez/niesen) rising behind. It is prettier than its role as a railway junction suggests.

The classic visit is simple: walk down from the station to [Spiez Castle](/europe/switzerland/spiez/spiez_castle), wander the lakeside, then follow the vineyard paths above the bay. Boats link Spiez with [Thun](/europe/switzerland/thun), Oberhofen, and [Interlaken](/europe/switzerland/interlaken), so it fits easily into a Lake Thun day.

Spiez is not a place for nightlife or big-city museums. Its strength is slower: a swim in summer, a glass of local wine, a boat across the lake, and mountain views that arrive without the crowds of Interlaken.
