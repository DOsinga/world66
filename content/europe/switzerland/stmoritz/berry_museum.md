The Berry Museum is housed in Villa Arona in the centre of St. Moritz and focuses on Peter Robert Berry, a local spa physician and painter. The collection includes oil paintings, pastels, and drawings made over several decades.

It is a compact art stop that adds a quieter cultural angle to St. Moritz, especially for visitors interested in Engadine landscapes and local history.
