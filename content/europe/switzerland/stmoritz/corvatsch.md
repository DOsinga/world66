Corvatsch is one of the big mountain names around St. Moritz, reached from Surlej and rising into the Upper Engadine’s high, open scenery. In winter it brings long ski runs and a serious alpine feel; in summer the same lifts and trails give walkers wide views toward the Bernina range and the lakes below.

It pairs well with Corviglia rather than replacing it. Corviglia is the classic St. Moritz ski hill, while Corvatsch feels a little wilder and higher, especially when the weather is clear and the valley floor drops away beneath you.
