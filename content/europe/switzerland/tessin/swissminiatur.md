Swissminiatur in Melide, on the shore of Lake Lugano, collects 123 scale models of Switzerland's most significant buildings and landscapes across a lakeside park. The parliament building in Bern, the Matterhorn, the Gotthard railway, the old town of Berne — all rendered at 1:25 scale in remarkable detail, with miniature trains running between them.

It sounds kitsch and it is, cheerfully so, but the park is genuinely well done. Children love the trains; adults find themselves unexpectedly absorbed in the miniature civic infrastructure. The setting is attractive — the park runs along the lake shore, with views across to Morcote. On warm days, the combination of Swissminiatur and a swim in the lake makes for a lazy afternoon. The park is open from late March to November.
