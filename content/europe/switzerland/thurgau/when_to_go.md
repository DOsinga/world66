---
title: When to Go
type: section
---

Late April brings the apple-blossom — a brief, dense pink-and-white covering of the canton's southern slopes — and is the prettiest time to visit. Summer (June–August) is when the lake activities reach full capacity, with the ferries running long timetables and the cycling and swimming season at its peak; daytime temperatures around 25–28°C, evenings pleasantly cool.

September and October are the harvest months — apple presses running, Heuriger-style farm taverns serving the year's new cider — and the most distinctive time to visit if you are interested in the food and drink side of Thurgau. Winter is quiet and grey; the lake-shore towns slow noticeably and many of the smaller museums close December through March.
