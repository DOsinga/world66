---
title: "Eating Out"
type: section
---

Verbier eats like the international ski resort it is — French and Italian alpine cooking dominates, with London- and Geneva-priced fine dining at the high end and an above-average crop of mountain restaurants on the pistes. Local Valais specialities are everywhere: fondue, raclette, viande séchée (dried beef), Cholera (potato and apple pie), and Fendant white wine from the Rhône Valley.
