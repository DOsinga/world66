A classic Swiss cafe on the corner of the Grande Place, useful at almost any hour. Morning coffee and croissant, a quick lunch plate, an afternoon beer watching the square fill up for the market. The outside tables give a direct view across the huge open space. Nothing distinctive about the food or the decor — it is a reliable neighbourhood cafe of the kind that Swiss towns do consistently well.
