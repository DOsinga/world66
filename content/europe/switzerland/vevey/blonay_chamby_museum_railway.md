The Blonay-Chamby Museum Railway runs vintage trains through the hills above Lake Geneva and maintains a substantial collection of historic rail vehicles. It is one of the region's best specialist outings for rail enthusiasts and families.

The line is seasonal and schedule-based, so the experience depends on operating days. When trains are running, it pairs well with a Vevey or Montreux stay, adding mountain views and transport history to the lakeside itinerary.
