---
title: Eating Out
type: section
---

Vevey has the dense restaurant lineup of a small Swiss town that knows it is on the tourist trail — quality is generally high and you can pick almost anything along the lakefront or in the old town with reasonable confidence.

For traditional fondue and Vaudoise specialities try **Le Mazot** behind the rue du Lac, a small old-town room that has been doing the same Alpine repertoire for decades. **Denis Martin** in the centre is a serious gastronomic destination — molecular cooking with a Vaudoise base, one Michelin star, and a tasting menu that runs three to four hours. For lake fish — perch, pike, fera — the lakefront brasseries opposite the Alimentarium do simple, well-prepared plates. The Saturday market is a meal in itself if you want to assemble a picnic on the lake.
