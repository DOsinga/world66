A small bar on the rue des Deux-Marchés that is livelier than most of central Vevey after 10pm. Cocktails, local beers, and a soundtrack that gets louder as the evening advances. The crowd is mixed — students from the nearby hotel management schools, regulars from the neighbourhood, and occasional late-night visitors who have found everything else closed. Probably the closest thing the town has to a proper bar rather than a cafe that serves wine.
