Wengen's Lutheran Church stands on an outcrop at the north end of the village, where its white clock tower is one of the local landmarks. The interior is plain, but the churchyard setting gives broad views over the Lauterbrunnen valley in clear weather.

It is a short, easy stop while walking through Wengen rather than a destination on its own. Go for the view and the village landmark, not for a rich church interior.
