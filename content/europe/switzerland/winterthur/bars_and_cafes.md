---
title: "Bars and Cafes"
type: section
---

Winterthur has a student population from the ZHAW university and a lively arts community that keeps the bar scene honest. Most of the action is in the Altstadt and the Sulzerareal — the former Sulzer industrial site that has been converted into a mixed-use cultural and nightlife district. Weekends draw crowds from Zurich, 20 minutes away by train.
