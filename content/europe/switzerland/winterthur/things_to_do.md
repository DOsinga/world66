---
title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Winterthur punches well above its weight as a cultural destination. The city has more museums per capita than almost anywhere in Switzerland, anchored by a remarkable concentration of art — from old masters at Am Römerholz to photography at the Fotomuseum. The pedestrianised old town makes for easy walking, and the twice-weekly market on the Graben is a pleasant morning outing.
