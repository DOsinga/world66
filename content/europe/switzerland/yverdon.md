Yverdon-les-Bains sits at the southern tip of Lake Neuchâtel, a small city that has been a spa town since Roman times. The thermal baths are still the main draw — the [Centre Thermal](/europe/switzerland/yverdon/centre_thermal) is fed by sulphurous springs at 29°C — but Yverdon has more to offer than hot water.

The 13th-century [Savoy castle](/europe/switzerland/yverdon/yverdon_castle) on [Place Pestalozzi](/europe/switzerland/yverdon/place_pestalozzi) now houses the town museum, and the old town around it is pleasant for a stroll. The real surprise is the [Maison d'Ailleurs](/europe/switzerland/yverdon/maison_dailleurs), a museum of science fiction and utopia that is unique in Europe, with a collection ranging from Jules Verne first editions to contemporary speculative art. North of the centre, the [Menhirs de Clendy](/europe/switzerland/yverdon/menhirs_de_clendy) — a 6,500-year-old neolithic alignment — stand on the [lakeshore](/europe/switzerland/yverdon/lake_neuchatel_shore), where the calm waters of Lake Neuchâtel are good for swimming in summer. Yverdon makes a relaxed base for exploring the Jura foothills and the northern Vaud countryside.
