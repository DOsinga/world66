Villette Park is a landscaped lakeside park in Cham, just west of Zug, built around lawns, mature trees, and views over Lake Zug. It has the calm, well-kept feel of a Swiss civic garden, with the water never far away.

The park is best for a relaxed walk, a picnic, or a pause on a cycling route around the lake. It is not a major sight, but it adds an easy green outing to Zug's old town and waterfront.
