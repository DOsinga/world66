Zurich's China Garden is a small walled garden on the lakeside, given by Kunming as a gesture of thanks for Zurich's technical help with the Chinese city's water supply. Its pavilions, ponds, walls, and planting make a calm contrast to the open lakefront promenades nearby.

It is easy to combine with a walk along the lake or a visit to the Pavillon Le Corbusier, and it is best enjoyed as a short, quiet stop rather than a major sight.
