The Eidgenössische Technische Hochschule (ETH) Zurich is one of the world's leading technical universities, and its main building — the Hauptgebäude, on the Polyterrasse above the old town — is one of the grandest 19th-century buildings in the city. Built by Gottfried Semper in 1864 in a confident Italian Renaissance style, the building is open to visit during the day and the **rooftop terrace** behind it gives one of the finest panoramic views in central Zurich, free and very rarely crowded.

The lobby and main staircase under the entrance dome are worth a quick visit; the ETH **library** sometimes hosts small exhibitions of rare books or scientific instruments from its collection. The Polyterrasse café — a sunny outdoor terrace — is a quieter alternative to the lakeside bars for an evening drink. Reach it on the Polybahn funicular from Central in two minutes.
