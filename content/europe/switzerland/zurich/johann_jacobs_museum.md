The Johann Jacobs Museum is a small Zurich museum with an unusually specific focus: the global histories of trade, commodities, and cultural exchange, especially around coffee. That makes it a useful counterpoint to the city's art museums and financial landmarks.

Its exhibitions can be more thoughtful than obvious, so it suits travellers who like compact, idea-driven museums. Check current opening and exhibition details before going, since small museums often change rhythm.
