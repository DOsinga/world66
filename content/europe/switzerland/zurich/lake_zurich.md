Lake Zürich (Zürichsee) is a long, narrow, crescent-shaped lake — 40 kilometres end-to-end, two kilometres wide at most — stretching south-east from the city into the foothills of the Alps. The city sits at the northern end where the River Limmat drains the lake; from the lake promenades you look the length of the water towards Rapperswil and, on a clear day, the snow-covered Glarus Alps beyond.

In summer the lakefront is busy: people swim from the public lake baths (**Strandbad Mythenquai**, **Strandbad Tiefenbrunnen**), rent pedalos and kayaks, and picnic on the grass strip at **Bürkliplatz**. Scheduled lake boats (ZSG) run from Bürkliplatz to small towns up both shores — the round-trip to **Rapperswil** at the far end takes four hours and is one of the great relaxed days out in Switzerland. Winter swims (with a heated changing tent at Mythenquai) attract a hardy local following.
