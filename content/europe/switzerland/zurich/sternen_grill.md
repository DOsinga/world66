The Sternen Grill, on the corner of Theaterstrasse at Bellevue, is the most famous **Wurst** stand in Zurich. Locals queue up at all hours for the unbeatable combination of grilled **St. Galler Bratwurst** with a small *Bürli* roll, a smear of mustard and (optionally) a glass of beer or a coffee, served at standing counters by the door.

The grill has been on this corner since 1963 — Old Zurich; the sausages are made to its own recipe by a single regional supplier; the white-tiled bar has the time-warp look of an unselfconsciously functional Swiss diner. Cheaper, faster, and arguably more characteristically Zurich than the smart restaurants nearby. Open until late evening; open Sundays. There is a small sit-down section if the standing counter is full.
