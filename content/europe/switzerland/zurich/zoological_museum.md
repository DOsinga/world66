The Zoological Museum displays the University of Zurich's animal collections in an approachable format. The upper floor focuses on Swiss and regional fauna, while the lower floor ranges more widely across animals from around the world.

It is a good family-friendly museum and a useful change of pace from Zurich's art museums, churches, and lakefront walks.
