Kruti Skhody Art Gallery focuses on Ukrainian folk and decorative art, with ceramics, carving, glass painting, embroidery, weaving, jewellery, and other handmade work. It is a small cultural stop rather than a major museum, but useful for visitors interested in local craft traditions.
