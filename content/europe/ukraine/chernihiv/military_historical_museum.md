Chernihiv's Military and Historical Museum focuses on the city's experience of the Second World War and the wider military history of the region. The displays are local in scope, but they add useful context to a city whose churches and historic core carry many layers of conflict.

It is a short stop rather than a major museum, best combined with the older sights around the historic centre.
