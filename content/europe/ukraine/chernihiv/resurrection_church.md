Resurrection Church is an 18th-century Baroque church in Chernihiv, built between 1772 and 1775. Its brick and stucco form is more modest than the city's great cathedral landmarks, but it adds another layer to Chernihiv's rich church architecture.

It is a short stop for travellers tracing the city's historic religious buildings.
