The Regional Museum of Folk Architecture and Life is an open-air museum of traditional wooden buildings from Bukovina, with houses and rural structures from the 17th to 20th centuries.

It gives Chernivtsi visitors a useful look at the countryside and folk culture around the city, not just the university and Habsburg-era centre.
