Saint Cross Cathedral is a Catholic church in central Chernivtsi, built between the late 18th and early 19th centuries and later reconstructed. It is one of the city's notable surviving churches from the Habsburg period.

The appeal is the largely preserved interior and the way the building reflects Chernivtsi's layered Central European religious history.
