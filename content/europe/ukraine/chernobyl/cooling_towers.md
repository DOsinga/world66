The unfinished cooling towers south-east of the Chernobyl reactors are one of the starkest reminders of the plant's abrupt halt after the 1986 disaster. They were built for two additional reactors that were never fuelled, and the exposed concrete and rebar still make the scale of the abandoned expansion easy to grasp.
