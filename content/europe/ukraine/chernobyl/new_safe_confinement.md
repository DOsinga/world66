The New Safe Confinement is the huge steel arch built over the destroyed Reactor 4 at the Chernobyl Nuclear Power Plant. Visitors cannot approach it closely, but it is visible from a distance and is one of the defining sights of the exclusion zone.

Its appeal is not scenic beauty but scale and historical weight: the structure shows how the disaster is still being contained decades later.
