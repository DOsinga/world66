Vehicle Cemetery Buryakivka is one of the more unsettling industrial remains of the Chernobyl disaster. After the cleanup, highly contaminated vehicles were gathered on concrete pads in the forest. Access depends on current Exclusion Zone rules and guided-route permissions.
