title: Orta Cami Mosque
---

A small mid-17th-century mosque in the heart of Bahchisaray's old quarter, one of the handful of pre-modern Crimean Tatar religious buildings that survived the Soviet-era demolitions. The building is unassuming — a simple square hall with a slim minaret — and unusually intact for the region.

Restored after 1991 and now used again by the local Crimean Tatar community for prayers. Visitors are welcome outside prayer times; modest dress required. The mosque sits in a tangle of narrow streets that gives the best surviving sense of how Bahchisaray must have looked under the khans.
