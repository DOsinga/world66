Count's Landing Stage is the ceremonial sea gate of Sevastopol, with broad granite steps leading down to the harbour. Its position on the waterfront makes it one of the city's classic arrival views.

Come for the setting as much as the structure itself: the landing stage sits close to the central waterfront monuments and gives a clear sense of why Sevastopol's sheltered bay shaped the city's history.
