Sevastopol Dolphinarium is a summer family attraction in Artillery Bay, with scheduled dolphin and seal performances. Its central location makes it easy to combine with a waterfront walk or the nearby art museum.

Check the current season and schedule before going, since the shows have historically moved or changed outside summer.
