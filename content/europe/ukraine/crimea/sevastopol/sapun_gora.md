Sapun Gora is one of Sevastopol's most important war-memory landscapes, a ridge associated with the fierce fighting for the city during the Second World War. The site combines views, memorials, military hardware, and a diorama tradition that fits Sevastopol's habit of telling its history through sieges.

The tone is solemn and patriotic rather than neutral. Even so, the location helps explain why Sevastopol's identity is so bound up with military endurance. Give it time, and read the site as both battlefield and civic shrine.
