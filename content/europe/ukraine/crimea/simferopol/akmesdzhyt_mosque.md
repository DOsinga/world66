A large new central mosque under construction on the eastern edge of the city, built to a contemporary Ottoman-revival design with a tall central dome and four slim minarets. When complete it will be the largest mosque in Crimea and a long-overdue main place of worship for the surviving Crimean Tatar community.

Even as a building site the mosque is impressive in scale, and the surrounding plaza already gets used for community events. Whether you can enter depends on the current stage of construction; ask at the gate, dress modestly.
