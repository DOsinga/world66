Simferopol is the natural base for exploring the rest of Crimea. The most rewarding day trip is to [Bahchisaray](/europe/ukraine/crimea/bahchisaray), 30km west, with its remarkable Tatar khan's palace and the cliff monastery at Chufut-Kale. The trolleybus and bus routes south reach [Yalta](/europe/ukraine/crimea/yalta) and the rest of the south coast, including the imperial Livadia Palace. [Sevastopol](/europe/ukraine/crimea/sevastopol) and the ancient ruins of Chersonesus are about an hour and a half by bus.
