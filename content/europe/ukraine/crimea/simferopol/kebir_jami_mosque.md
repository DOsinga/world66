The oldest building in Simferopol and the spiritual heart of its surviving Crimean Tatar community. Founded in 1508 under the Crimean Khanate, the mosque is a low, stout structure of pale local limestone with a single tall minaret, set in a quiet walled courtyard a short walk south-east of the old town centre.

The interior is small and plain — whitewashed walls, an arched mihrab, woven carpets — and most striking for its quiet after the busy streets outside. The mosque is in active use; visitors are welcome outside prayer times, with shoes off and shoulders and knees covered.
