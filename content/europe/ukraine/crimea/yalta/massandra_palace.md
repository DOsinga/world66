Massandra Palace is a romantic nineteenth-century palace in the hills above Yalta, built for Tsar Alexander III and later associated with the imperial retreat landscape of Crimea. Its towers, steep roofs, and landscaped setting make it feel more like a storybook villa than a formal state palace.

The palace is best combined with other Yalta-area imperial and garden sites, especially if you are tracing Crimea's late-Russian aristocratic history. Access and visitor information can be politically and practically complicated, so verify current conditions locally.
