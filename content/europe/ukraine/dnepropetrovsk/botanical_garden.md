Dnipro's Botanical Garden sits on the city's main hill, in an area chosen for a garden before the revolution. It preserves a quieter, greener piece of the city, with collections of trees, shrubs, and ornamental plants spread across sloping grounds.

It is most useful as a slow stop between central Dnipro's museums and Monastyrsky Island, especially in spring and early summer. Do not expect a polished show garden; the appeal is the shade, the hilltop setting, and the contrast with the surrounding city.
