---
title: "Getting There"
type: section
---

Dnipro International Airport receives domestic flights from Kyiv and, before the 2022 invasion, served European routes. The airport's international operations have been suspended since the start of Russia's full-scale war — the current advice is to check current conditions carefully before planning any trip. When overland travel is possible, the city is connected to Kyiv by regular rail services (about six hours by overnight train) and intercity express buses. The train station is centrally located, within walking distance of the main boulevard.

Given the ongoing conflict, travellers should consult current foreign government advisories and verify transport links before visiting.
