---
title: Bars and Cafes
type: section
---

Donetsk had a lively cafe culture concentrated along Artyoma Street and the streets around Teatralna Square. By the early 2010s the city had adopted the Central European cafe habit — outdoor seating in summer, pastries and espresso year-round — alongside a bar scene that reflected its mix of students, miners, and the business class that had grown up around the coal and steel industries.
