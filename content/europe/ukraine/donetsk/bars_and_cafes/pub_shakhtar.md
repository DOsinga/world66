A football-themed bar in the city centre, heavy on Shakhtar Donetsk memorabilia and always busy on match days. The walls were covered in club colours — orange and black — photographs of famous players, and framed shirts. The crowd was partisan and good-humoured. Good for watching away games on television or simply drinking among people who took their football seriously. Served Ukrainian lager and imported beers at prices that made it accessible to working-class supporters.
