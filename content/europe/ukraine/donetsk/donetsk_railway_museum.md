Donetsk Railway Museum covers the region's railway history through documents, uniforms, equipment, photographs, and other railway objects. It is a specialist museum, but useful for visitors interested in industrial history and the role railways played in the Donbas.

It is best paired with other central Donetsk sights rather than treated as a stand-alone reason to visit the city.
