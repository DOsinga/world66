A popular mid-range restaurant in the city centre that combined a beer-hall format with solid Ukrainian cooking. The long wooden tables, the smell of grilled meats, and the noise of a busy Friday evening made it feel like a place actually used by the city, rather than dressed up for visitors. Grilled pork, pickled vegetables, dark bread, and a good selection of Ukrainian and Czech beers. Reservations advisable at weekends.
