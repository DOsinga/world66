Forged Figures Park is a small central Donetsk park known for decorative metal sculptures created by blacksmiths. Arches, figures, benches, and playful forged details turn the space into an open-air craft display.

The park is an easy urban stop and one of the city’s more distinctive public spaces. Current travel conditions in Donetsk are a separate matter, but as a point of interest it reflects local metalworking pride in a form you can walk through.
