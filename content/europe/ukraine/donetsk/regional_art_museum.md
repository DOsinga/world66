Donetsk Regional Art Museum holds a large collection of paintings, graphics, sculpture, decorative art, and antiquities, with works spanning European and Ukrainian art from the early modern period through the 20th century. It is the city's main fine-arts collection and a useful indoor stop for understanding Donetsk beyond its industrial image.

Opening conditions may change, so check locally before planning a visit.
