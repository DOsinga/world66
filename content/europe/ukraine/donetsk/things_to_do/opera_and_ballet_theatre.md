The Donetsk State Academic Opera and Ballet Theatre was one of Ukraine's major regional opera companies, performing in a neo-classical building that dominates Teatralna Square. Founded in 1941, the company built a reputation for ballet in particular, attracting audiences from across the Donbas region. The building's colonnaded facade was one of the most photographed in the city.
