The main civic square of central Donetsk, Teatralna Square was the heart of the city's public life before 2014. Flanked by the Opera and Ballet Theatre and the Musical Drama Theatre, it was the place Donetsk residents came for concerts, celebrations, and summer evenings. Fountains, rose gardens, and broad paving gave it an unexpectedly pleasant character for a city best known for steel and coal.
