Tsar Pushka is a decorative cannon monument in central Donetsk, a small landmark rather than a major museum or historic site. Its value is mostly as part of the city's public-space texture, especially when walking between central squares, parks, and theatres.

Current travel conditions in Donetsk can be extremely complicated, so this entry should be treated as contextual rather than an active recommendation. Anyone in the city should rely on current security information and local guidance.
