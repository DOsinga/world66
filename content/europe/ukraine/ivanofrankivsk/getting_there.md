---
title: "Getting There"
type: section
---

Since Russia's invasion **Ukrainian airspace is closed** to civilian aviation, so the practical routes are overland.

**From Poland.** The nearest entry points are by **train** or **bus** via Lviv. The overnight train from Przemyśl (the Polish border town) reaches Lviv in around five hours, then a connecting train to Ivano-Frankivsk takes another two and a half hours. By bus, FlixBus and a number of Ukrainian operators run direct from Warsaw and Kraków to Lviv and onward to Ivano-Frankivsk.

**Within Ukraine.** Regular Intercity trains link Kyiv, Lviv, Chernivtsi and Odesa with Ivano-Frankivsk; the Lviv connection is the most useful for foreign travellers. The Ivano-Frankivsk railway station sits on the eastern edge of the centre — a 15-minute walk or short marshrutka ride to the Rynok.
