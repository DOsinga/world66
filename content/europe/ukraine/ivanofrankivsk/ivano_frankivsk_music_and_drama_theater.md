Ivano-Frankivsk Music and Drama Theater is the city's principal stage, named for Ivan Franko and central to local cultural life. The building and its performances give travellers a way to see the city as a living Ukrainian regional capital rather than just a pretty old centre.

Check current programming if you understand Ukrainian or simply want the experience of a local theatre night. Even from outside, the venue belongs on a central walk because it anchors one of the city's important public spaces.
