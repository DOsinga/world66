Krylos is a historic village near Halych, reachable as a day trip from Ivano-Frankivsk, and is closely tied to the medieval Galician principality. Archaeological remains, church sites, and museum displays make it more substantial than a simple rural detour.

It is best for travelers interested in western Ukraine's deeper history, especially the period before Lviv became the dominant regional city. Pair it with other Halych-area sights if transport allows.
