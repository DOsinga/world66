Ivano-Frankivsk's Literary Museum traces the region's writing and literary culture from early texts to the modern period. The collection is large for a local museum, with manuscripts, books, and exhibits tied to Prykarpattia's cultural life.

It is a specialist stop, but a useful one if you want to understand the city beyond its central architecture.
