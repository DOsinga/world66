Kamianets-Podilskyi is one of the most dramatic small cities in Ukraine — an old town set on a rocky island formed by a horseshoe bend of the Smotrych River, connected to the mainland by a single narrow bridge, and dominated by a formidable medieval castle at the island's western tip. The combination of the canyon landscape, the castle, and the densely packed old town inside the loop of the river makes it visually extraordinary and very unlike anywhere else in central Europe.

The [castle](/europe/ukraine/kamianetstpodilskyi/kamianetstpodilskyi_castle) is the principal attraction — a complex of towers and bastions that was fought over by the Kingdom of Poland, the Ottoman Empire, the Lithuanian Grand Duchy, and eventually Russia over five centuries of contested history. The old town it defended retains its medieval street plan and a remarkable density of religious buildings: [Sts. Peter and Paul Cathedral](/europe/ukraine/kamianetstpodilskyi/sts_peter_and_paul_cathedral) was built as a Catholic church, briefly converted to a mosque during the Ottoman occupation, and converted back to a Catholic cathedral. The [Holy Trinity Church](/europe/ukraine/kamianetstpodilskyi/holy_trinity_church) and the remains of the Armenian trading quarter reflect the town's medieval multiculturalism — it was home to Polish, Armenian, and Ukrainian communities among others.

The [Smotrych Canyon](/europe/ukraine/kamianetstpodilskyi/smotrych_canyon) that protects the old town is itself a striking natural feature — the river has cut through the local limestone to create sheer walls up to 20 metres high, and walking along the canyon rim gives views of the castle and old town that explain why this location was so strategically prized. The New Bridge that spans the canyon to the north is a favourite viewing point.

Kamianets-Podilskyi is about 100 kilometres from Chernivtsi and makes an excellent stop on any journey through western Ukraine. Note that the city, like all of Ukraine, should be visited with awareness of the ongoing conflict that began in 2022; check current conditions before travelling.
