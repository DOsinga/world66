The Church of St. Panteleimon is a late 19th-century church in Kharkiv, built in a Russian-Byzantine style. Its decorative facade, belfry, and restored details make it one of the city's secondary religious architecture stops.

The church suffered alterations during the Soviet period, including the loss of parts of its tower and cupola, before restoration work was completed in the late 20th century. It pairs well with other central Kharkiv churches and civic buildings.
