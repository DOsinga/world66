Kharkiv's House with Chimeras is an early-20th-century architectural oddity, mixing modernist lines with English Gothic details. Look up at the facade for the sculpted creatures that give the building its name: chimeras, salamanders, wolves, and heraldic figures worked into the stone.

The building now belongs to the theatre faculty of the Kharkiv arts institute, so it is mainly an exterior stop. It is still worth a pause if you are walking through this part of the city and like architecture with a bit of mischief in it.
