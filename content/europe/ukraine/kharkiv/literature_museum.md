Literature Museum adds a focused museum stop to Kharkiv, useful when you want a break from the main squares and headline monuments. Allow enough time to read the displays rather than treating it as a photo stop. Check current opening days before going, because smaller museums can keep limited hours, and pair it with nearby streets or parks so the visit fits naturally into a half-day route.
