The Kharkiv National Opera and Ballet Theatre, named after Mykola Lysenko, is one of the principal venues for classical performance in Ukraine. The current building dates from 1991 (replacing an earlier theatre destroyed in the war) and has a functional brutalist exterior that conceals a well-equipped interior with good sightlines and acoustics.

The company has a long history and a serious reputation — the ballet troupe in particular has produced dancers who went on to careers in major European companies. The repertoire covers standard opera and ballet classics alongside Ukrainian works, and ticket prices remain extremely affordable by Western European standards. The theatre faces Freedom Square, making it easy to combine a performance with an evening stroll around the illuminated square.
