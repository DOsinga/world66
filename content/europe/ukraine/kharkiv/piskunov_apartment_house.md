The Piskunov Apartment House is a central Kharkiv architectural stop, notable for the decorative confidence of the city's pre-revolutionary apartment-building boom. It adds texture to a walk that otherwise tends to focus on squares, museums, and Soviet-era scale.

This is a brief look rather than a destination that needs a long visit. It makes sense for travellers already tracing Kharkiv's architecture on foot and looking for smaller historic details between the larger landmarks.
