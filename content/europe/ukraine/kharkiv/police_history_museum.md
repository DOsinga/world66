Kharkiv's Police History Museum is a small specialist collection inside the police cultural complex. The displays cover police service through photographs, archival documents, newspaper clippings, uniforms, medals, weapons, reports, and wartime material.

It is not one of Kharkiv's essential sights, but it can be worthwhile if you are interested in civic history or Soviet and post-Soviet institutions. Pair it with the nearby Kharkiv Art Museum or the central streets around the Mirror Stream.
