Kharkiv's Shevchenko Drama Theatre is one of the city's serious cultural institutions, tied to the long history of Ukrainian-language theatre in the east of the country. The repertoire and performance calendar matter most, but the building also anchors a central slice of Kharkiv's theatrical life.

Check the current situation carefully before planning around a performance; Kharkiv has been directly affected by the war in Ukraine, and schedules, access, and safety conditions can change. In calmer times, this is the sort of venue that helps visitors see the city as more than Soviet avenues and university buildings.
