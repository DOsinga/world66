Kharkiv's signature sight is [Freedom Square](/europe/ukraine/kharkiv/freedom_square), one of the largest city squares in Europe, ringed by extraordinary Constructivist and Stalinist architecture. The [Annunciation Cathedral](/europe/ukraine/kharkiv/annunciation_cathedral) provides gilded counterpoint to the Soviet grandeur, and the [Kharkiv Art Museum](/europe/ukraine/kharkiv/kharkiv_art_museum) holds a collection that ranges from Ukrainian icons to 20th-century avant-garde work. The city's universities give it an intellectual energy distinctive from Kyiv, and exploring the academic quarter around [Karazin University](/europe/ukraine/kharkiv/karazin_university) reveals some of the finest Neoclassical buildings in Ukraine.

Note: Kharkiv has been severely affected by the conflict in Ukraine that began in 2022. Verify current safety conditions before planning any visit.
