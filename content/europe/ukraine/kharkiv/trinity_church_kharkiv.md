Trinity Church is one of central Kharkiv's historic Orthodox churches, less visually dominant than Annunciation or Assumption Cathedral but still part of the city's old sacred map. Its value is in the quieter scale and continuity of worship.

Because Kharkiv has been affected by war, conditions around historic buildings can change. Check local advice before visiting and treat the church as an active religious place rather than a resilient backdrop.
