---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

Kharkiv has a continental climate with cold winters (frequently below -10°C) and warm summers (around 25°C). Spring (April–May) and early autumn (September–October) are the most comfortable times to visit for sightseeing — mild temperatures, manageable crowds, and the universities in session, which gives the city its characteristic energy.

Summer is the peak season for outdoor events and the city's parks are lively. Winter is cold but the city functions normally; the metro keeps everyone moving regardless of the weather. Kharkiv's festival calendar traditionally included significant jazz, theatre, and film events, though the situation has changed substantially since 2022.

Travel to Kharkiv requires careful monitoring of the security situation. The city has been a front-line zone in the conflict that began in 2022 and has experienced significant shelling and population displacement. Check current government travel advisories before planning any visit.
