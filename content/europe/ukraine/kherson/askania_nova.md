  The reserve was founded in 1828 by Friedrich Falz-Fein, a German landowner who set aside part of his estate as a sanctuary for the steppe wildlife being rapidly displaced by agricultural expansion. His grandson expanded it dramatically in the early 20th century, importing exotic animals from around the world — zebras, bison, and ostriches still roam the grounds alongside native steppe species.
---

Askania-Nova is one of the most important nature reserves in Europe — a 11,000-hectare island of virgin feather-grass steppe that has never been ploughed, the largest such area remaining on the continent. The reserve protects a complete steppe ecosystem that once stretched from Hungary to Mongolia, now reduced to fragments. Over 600 plant species and dozens of bird species have been recorded here.

The reserve includes an arboretum of over 200 hectares planted in the 19th century, and a zoological park that holds animals from steppe and savanna ecosystems worldwide. In spring, the steppe wildflower display is extraordinary. Access from Kherson is about 180 km; in peacetime, day trips were possible by car or organised tour. The reserve's current status should be verified given its location in a conflict-affected region.
