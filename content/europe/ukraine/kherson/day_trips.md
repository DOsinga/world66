The main excursion from Kherson in peacetime was to [Askania-Nova](/europe/ukraine/askania_nova), a biosphere reserve about 180 km northeast — one of the last areas of virgin steppe grassland in Europe, with an arboretum and a zoo of steppe and savanna animals. Access and conditions should be verified carefully given the current security situation.
