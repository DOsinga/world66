Kolomyia is a western Ukrainian city on the Prut River, halfway in spirit between Pokuttia and the Hutsul villages of the Carpathian foothills. It is not as polished as [Lviv](/europe/ukraine/lviv), but it has something more specific: museums, markets, and old streets tied to mountain craft traditions that are still part of local life.

The sight everyone remembers is the [Pysanka Museum](/europe/ukraine/kolomyia/pysanka_museum), a giant painted Easter egg in the middle of town. More rewarding, if you have time, is the [National Museum of Hutsulshchyna and Pokuttia Folk Art](/europe/ukraine/kolomyia/hutsulshchyna_and_pokuttia_museum), whose textiles, woodcarving, ceramics, and ritual objects explain why Kolomyia matters culturally.

Travel in Ukraine remains shaped by Russia's full-scale invasion. Kolomyia is in the west, far from the main front lines, but missile alerts and infrastructure disruption can still affect plans. Check current security advice before travelling, and treat railway timetables as something to verify close to departure.
