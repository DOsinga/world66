The Church of the Annunciation is one of Kolomyia's oldest and most valuable buildings, a wooden church with a separate belfry. It shows the local religious architecture that once defined towns and villages across this part of western Ukraine.

Its appeal is quiet rather than grand. Visit it as a counterpoint to the museums: the same world of wood, pattern, and ritual, but expressed in architecture rather than display cases.
