---
title: Getting There
type: section
---

**By train.** Kolomyia is on regional rail routes between Ivano-Frankivsk, Chernivtsi, and the Carpathian foothills. In wartime, schedules can change; check Ukrainian Railways close to departure.

**By bus or marshrutka.** Buses and minibuses connect Kolomyia with Ivano-Frankivsk, Chernivtsi, Kosiv, and nearby Hutsul villages. They are useful for local travel but can be crowded and slow.

**By car.** Roads through the region are manageable in good weather, though winter and wartime fuel or checkpoint delays can complicate plans.
