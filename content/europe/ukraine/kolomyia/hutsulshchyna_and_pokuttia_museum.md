The National Museum of Hutsulshchyna and Pokuttia Folk Art is the deeper reason to visit Kolomyia. Its collection covers the craft and domestic culture of the Hutsul and Pokuttia regions: carved wood, embroidered shirts, woven textiles, ceramics, metalwork, and painted ritual objects.

Give it more time than the Pysanka Museum. The displays explain how the Carpathian foothills developed such a strong visual identity, and why Kolomyia became one of the best places to understand it.
