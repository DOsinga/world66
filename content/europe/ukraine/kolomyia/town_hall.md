Kolomyia Town Hall stands at an angle to the main square, a small architectural quirk that makes it more memorable than many provincial civic buildings. Built in the late 19th century, it belongs to the Austrian layer of the city's history.

The square around it is a useful place to start walking. From here you can reach the museums, churches, and older commercial streets without needing a formal route.
