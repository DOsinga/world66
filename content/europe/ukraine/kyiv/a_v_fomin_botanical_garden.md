A.V. Fomin Botanical Garden is the botanical garden of Kyiv's St. Volodymyr University. It covers about 22.5 hectares and holds thousands of plant species.

The garden is especially known for its greenhouse collection, including old palm trees that made the greenhouse one of the notable plant houses of its time. It is an easy green stop near central Kyiv.
