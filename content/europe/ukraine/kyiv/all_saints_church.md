All Saints Church is a Ukrainian Baroque church near the Kyiv Pechersk Lavra, founded at the end of the 17th century. Its facades are richly decorated, and the interior preserves a carved multi-tiered wooden iconostasis from the early 18th century.

It is a natural short stop while exploring the Lavra and the Pechersk ridge. Look for the painted interior walls, added by local artists in the early 20th century, and the way the church fits into Kyiv's dense sacred landscape.
