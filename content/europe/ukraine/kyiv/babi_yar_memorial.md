Babyn Yar (Babi Yar) is a ravine in the Syrets district of north-western Kyiv where some 100,000 people — primarily Jews, but also Roma, Soviet prisoners of war, and Ukrainian nationalists — were killed by the Nazis between 1941 and 1943. The ravine itself was largely filled in during the postwar redevelopment of the area.

A scattered collection of memorials now occupies the site: the Soviet bronze of 1976; the Menorah of 1991; the contemporary glass-and-steel synagogue of 2021 by Manuel Herz; and the still-evolving Babyn Yar Holocaust Memorial Center. The grounds are open and walkable.
