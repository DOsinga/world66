A long-running electronic music venue, gallery, and bar in a former ribbon factory in Podil. Closer was at the centre of the new Ukrainian club scene in the 2010s, hosting techno parties, art shows, and lectures across its courtyard and several rooms. It is the place most often credited with putting Kyiv on the European underground music map.

It still operates intermittently — events depend on conditions in the war. Check the schedule before going.
