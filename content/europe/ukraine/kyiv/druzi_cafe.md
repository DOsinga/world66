A small chain of coffee shops with branches around central Kyiv — Bessarabska Square, Podil, Pechersk — doing competent espresso, simple breakfasts, and weekday lunches. The interiors are clean and design-led without being precious; the menu is short and reliable.

A good first-coffee-of-the-morning option when you do not want a hotel breakfast. Wi-fi is free and works.
