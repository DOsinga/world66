The Golden Gate was the main ceremonial entrance to medieval Kyiv. Yaroslav the Wise built it in the 1030s as part of the city walls, with a church dedicated to the Annunciation on its upper level. After centuries as a ruin it was reconstructed in 1982 for the city's 1500th anniversary, on the original foundations.

A small museum inside the reconstructed gate covers the history of medieval Kyiv. The park around it, with its statue of Yaroslav on a bench, is a pleasant place to sit and is one of the obvious starting points for a walk through the Upper Town.
