A small, modish restaurant on a side street off Khreshchatyk, doing a contemporary mid-range menu of Ukrainian and European dishes — duck breast with cherry sauce, lamb shoulder, beetroot tartare. The interior is clean Scandinavian with exposed brick and good lighting.

Reservations recommended in the evenings. The wine list is short but well-chosen, with a strong selection of Ukrainian producers.
