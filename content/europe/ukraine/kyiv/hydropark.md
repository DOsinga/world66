Hydropark is Kyiv's big summer playground on islands in the Dnieper, created in the Soviet period as a landscape of beaches, boat rentals, sports grounds, and open-air leisure. The metro stop drops you directly into a different city mood from the formal centre.

It is at its best in warm weather, when locals come for swimming, sun, exercise, and cheap riverside food. Travellers should go with realistic expectations: this is everyday Kyiv recreation, sometimes scruffy, often lively, and more revealing than another polished monument.
