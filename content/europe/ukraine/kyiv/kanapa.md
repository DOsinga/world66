A serious modern Ukrainian restaurant on Andriyivskyy Descent, in a converted historic house with a small garden. Chef Yevhen Klopotenko, who became nationally famous for the campaign to register borshch as a Ukrainian heritage dish with UNESCO, designs the menu — modern interpretations of pre-Soviet Ukrainian cuisine using sourced ingredients from small Ukrainian producers.

Booking essential at the weekend. The tasting menu is worth the splash; à la carte is more affordable.
