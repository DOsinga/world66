A no-nonsense Georgian dumpling restaurant in Podil — Georgian food is Kyiv's second cuisine and the chain-style khinkalnayas serve it cheaply and well. Order khinkali (giant soup dumplings, eaten by hand), khachapuri (a cheese-stuffed bread boat), lobio (bean stew), and a glass of Georgian wine.

Several outlets across central Kyiv. The Podil branch is the most relaxed. Cash and cards.
