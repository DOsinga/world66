A small wine bar and bakery in Podil run by the same team as several of the city's better restaurants. The wine list focuses on small Ukrainian and Eastern European producers; the bread is properly fermented and baked on the premises.

A pleasant midday or early-evening stop. Cheese and charcuterie plates are the main accompaniment to wine.
