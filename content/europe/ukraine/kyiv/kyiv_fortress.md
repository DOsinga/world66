Kyiv Fortress, also known as the Pechersk Fortress, is a large 19th-century defensive complex spread across the hills above central Kyiv. It was built by the Russian Empire as part of a wider system of fortifications, and its walls, gates, barracks, and earthworks still show how the city was treated as a strategic military position.

The site is not a single polished castle but a layered military landscape, with museum displays on the fortress and on political imprisonment in the complex. It is most rewarding if you are interested in Kyiv beyond churches and boulevards, because it reveals the city as a place repeatedly shaped by empire, defence, and state power.
