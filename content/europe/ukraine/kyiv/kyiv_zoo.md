Kyiv Zoo is one of the city's classic family outings, spread across a leafy site west of the centre near the university district. It has the usual mix of big-cat enclosures, primate houses, birds, reptiles, and smaller mammals, so it works best when you want a slower half day rather than another church or museum.

The zoo is not Kyiv's most essential sight, but it is a practical choice with children and a chance to see a different, residential side of the city. Check opening details before crossing town, especially in winter or during periods when city services are disrupted.
