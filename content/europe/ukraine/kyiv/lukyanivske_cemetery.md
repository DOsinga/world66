Lukyanivske Cemetery is one of Kyiv's notable historic cemeteries, with graves and monuments tied to many periods of the city's public life. A small museum room near the administration building gives background on the cemetery and some of the people buried here.

It is close enough to Babyn Yar and Saint Cyril's Church to combine with other north-west Kyiv history stops.
