Mamaeva Sloboda is an open-air museum in Kyiv that recreates a Cossack-era village landscape with wooden buildings, craft settings, church architecture, and folk-culture programming. It offers a more immersive vernacular-history experience than a conventional indoor museum.

It is especially useful for visitors interested in Ukrainian rural traditions, Cossack imagery, and hands-on cultural displays. Check current opening conditions and events, since programming can shape the visit.
