The Museum of Magdeburg Law is a small Kyiv history museum focused on the city's tradition of self-government. Its permanent exhibition explains how Magdeburg rights shaped Kyiv's civic life, courts, trade, and local administration over several centuries.

It is a specialist stop rather than a first museum for Kyiv, but it adds useful context if you are already exploring Podil and the riverfront monuments.
