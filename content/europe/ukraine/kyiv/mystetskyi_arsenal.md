A vast 18th-century arsenal building near the Lavra, converted into one of Eastern Europe's largest contemporary art and cultural centres. The exterior is plain industrial; the inside is a sequence of brick-vaulted halls that host temporary exhibitions, book fairs, design weeks, and the annual Book Arsenal — Ukraine's most important literary festival.

There is no permanent collection; what's on depends entirely on the calendar. Check the schedule before going.
