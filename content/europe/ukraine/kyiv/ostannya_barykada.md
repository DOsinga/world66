A speakeasy-style restaurant hidden behind an unmarked door inside the Globus shopping centre under Maidan Nezalezhnosti. The menu is contemporary Ukrainian — modernised versions of traditional dishes, local produce, Ukrainian wines and craft beers. The interior is heavy on revolutionary memorabilia, photographs from the Maidan, and a generally patriotic atmosphere.

Reserve ahead and look up the password — without it you cannot get in. Worth the gimmick; the food is genuinely good.
