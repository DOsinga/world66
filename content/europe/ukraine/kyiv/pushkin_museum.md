Kyiv's Pushkin Museum holds books, manuscripts, photographs, and period material connected with Alexander Pushkin and the literary world of the early 19th century. The collection includes signed editions and documents from people around Pushkin's circle.

It is a specialist museum, best suited to visitors with an interest in literature or Kyiv's older cultural institutions. Check current access before making a dedicated trip.
