The Theatre and Cinematographic Arts Museum documents the development of Ukrainian drama, theatre, film, and performance culture. Exhibits include documentary material, folk musical instruments, and displays on Ukrainian literature and ethnology.

It is a specialist museum, but a worthwhile addition if you are already visiting the Lavra area and want cultural context beyond churches and war memorials.
