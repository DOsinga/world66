Lugansk's local history museum covers the history of the Luhansk region from early settlement through industrial growth and modern conflict. Exhibits include regional archaeology, natural history, and military objects outside the museum building.

It is one of the city's more useful context stops for understanding Donbas history beyond the main squares and monuments.
