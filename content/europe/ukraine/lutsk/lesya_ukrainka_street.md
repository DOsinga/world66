Lesya Ukrainka Street is Lutsk's central pedestrian street, lined with shops, restaurants, small squares, and several landmark buildings.

It is less a single monument than a good walking spine through the city centre. Use it to connect the old market area, civic buildings, and the everyday street life around central Lutsk.
