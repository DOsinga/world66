Lutsk Art Museum has a collection of European art from the 17th century to the early 20th century. The holdings include works tied to Spanish, Italian, Flemish, French, Austrian, German, English, and Polish schools.

The museum is close to Lubart's Castle, so it works best as a compact cultural stop while walking through Lutsk's old centre.
