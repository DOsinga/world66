Sviato-Feodosiivskyi Hram is the Church of St. Theodosius, consecrated in 1906. The appeal is mostly inside, where murals and icons give the church more character than its simple exterior suggests.

It is a short stop for travellers already exploring Lutsk's religious and historic sights.
