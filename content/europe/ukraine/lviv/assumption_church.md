Lviv's Assumption Church is part of a Renaissance ensemble that also includes the Chapel of the Three Holy Bishops and the Korniakt Tower. The complex is one of the old city's most rewarding church stops, especially if you are following Lviv's mix of Ukrainian, Polish and wider eastern European influences.
