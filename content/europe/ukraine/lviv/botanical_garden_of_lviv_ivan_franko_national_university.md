The Botanical Garden of Lviv Ivan Franko National University is a working academic garden with a large collection of trees, shrubs, herbaceous plants, and tropical species. It is a quiet stop for visitors interested in urban botany rather than a polished show garden.

The garden sits east of central Lviv, close enough to combine with Lychakiv Cemetery or the open-air folk architecture museum. Come for a calm green break and a look at one of the city's scientific collections.
