The Center for Urban History of East Central Europe is a research and public-history institution in
Lviv, with exhibitions, talks, archives, and walking projects focused on cities, memory, and the
region's layered past. It is not a conventional tourist museum, but it can be one of the city's most
intellectually rewarding stops.

For visitors who already know Lviv's churches, squares, and cafes, the centre offers a deeper way to
think about how the city has changed under different empires, wars, languages, and communities.
Check the current exhibition or event schedule before going.
