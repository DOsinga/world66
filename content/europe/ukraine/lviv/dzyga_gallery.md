Dzyga Gallery is a small contemporary art space in Lviv's historic centre, close to the Dominican Church and the Pharmacy Museum. It shows visual art, multimedia work, and other projects connected to the city's independent cultural scene.

It is not a major museum, but it is a useful stop if you want to see something current rather than another formal historic interior. Check what is on before making a special trip.
