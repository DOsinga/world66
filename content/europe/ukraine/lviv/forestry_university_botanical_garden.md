The Botanical Garden of the Lviv Forestry University is a low-key green space attached to one of the city's specialist universities. It is not as polished as a formal palace garden, but its mature trees, plant collections, and campus setting make it a worthwhile detour for visitors who enjoy urban botany.

Use it as a quiet break from Lviv's dense historic centre rather than as a headline attraction. The garden pairs well with a walk through the city's southern parks and shows another side of Lviv's long academic life.
