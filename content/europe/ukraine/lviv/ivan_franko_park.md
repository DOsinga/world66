Ivan Franko Park is one of central Lviv's classic green spaces, set beside the university and close to the old town's western edge. Its paths, benches, and mature trees make it a useful place to slow down between churches, coffee houses, and Habsburg facades.

The park is not a headline sight, but it belongs to the rhythm of the city. Students cut through it, locals sit out in good weather, and the surrounding streets show Lviv's 19th-century layer particularly well. It is best folded into a walk rather than treated as a standalone outing.
