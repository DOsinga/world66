Krekhiv Monastery is a strong day trip from Lviv for travellers interested in religious architecture and the quieter countryside north of the city. The Basilian monastery has fortified walls, churches, and a setting below wooded hills that makes it feel removed from urban western Ukraine.

The visit is as much about atmosphere as architecture. Pilgrimage paths, springs, and forested slopes around the monastery give it a landscape setting, while the complex itself shows the layered Greek Catholic history of the region.
