The Lviv Art Gallery is one of the city's main art institutions, with its central collection displayed in the Potocki Palace. The focus is European art, especially Renaissance and Baroque works, while related branches cover books, religious art, and sculpture.

It is a strong indoor stop for visitors who want Lviv beyond churches, cafes, and streetscapes. The palace setting adds to the visit, and the collection gives useful context for the city's old Polish, Habsburg, and Ukrainian cultural layers.
