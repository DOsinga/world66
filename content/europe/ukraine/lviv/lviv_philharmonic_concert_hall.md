Lviv Philharmonic Concert Hall is one of the city's main classical music venues, set in a handsome old hall just southwest of the historic centre. Lviv has a deep musical tradition, and this is the place to look for symphonic concerts, chamber programmes, and festival performances.

Even if you do not plan your trip around a concert, check the schedule once you are in town. An evening here gives a less touristic view of Lviv than another cafe or cellar bar, and the building itself fits neatly into the city's Central European architectural mood.
