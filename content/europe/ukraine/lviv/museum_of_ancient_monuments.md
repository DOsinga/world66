The Museum of Ancient Monuments of Lviv occupies the former Church of St John the Baptist, one of the sites linked with the city's earliest medieval layers. Its collections are modest, but the setting helps connect Lviv's polished central streets with the older Ruthenian and Armenian worlds that shaped the city.

This is a specialist stop, best for travellers who already know the main churches and museums and want to dig deeper. The building and its immediate surroundings are as important as the displays, so take the visit slowly.
