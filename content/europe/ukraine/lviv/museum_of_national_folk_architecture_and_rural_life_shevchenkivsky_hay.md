Shevchenkivsky Hay is Lviv's open-air museum of rural Ukrainian architecture, spread across wooded ground on the eastern edge of the city. Wooden churches, farmhouses, barns, mills, and workshops have been moved here from different regions, giving the place the feel of several small villages rather than a conventional museum. It is one of the best ways in Lviv to understand the vernacular world behind the city's polished Habsburg facades.

Give it time. The paths climb and dip through trees, and the pleasure is partly in wandering from one homestead to the next, looking at joinery, icons, ovens, tools, and household details. Folk festivals and craft demonstrations add life when they are scheduled, but even on a quiet day the museum makes a strong half-day break from central Lviv.
