Lviv's Museum of Natural History holds a large collection of zoological, botanical, geological, and paleontological material. It occupies an 18th-century palace that was later rebuilt in classical style, adding another layer of interest to a visit.

The museum is a useful indoor stop near the old centre, especially for families or anyone interested in regional natural history.
