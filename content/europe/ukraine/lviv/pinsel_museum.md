The Pinsel Museum is devoted to the fierce, twisting sacred sculpture of Johann Georg Pinsel, one of the most distinctive artists associated with 18th-century Galicia. His figures do not sit calmly in niches; they bend, flare, and almost shout, which makes the museum feel unusually alive for a small collection.

It is a strong indoor stop for anyone interested in church art, Lviv's Baroque heritage, or the borderland culture of the old Polish-Lithuanian and Habsburg worlds. Even if you normally skip sculpture museums, this one has enough personality to justify the detour.
