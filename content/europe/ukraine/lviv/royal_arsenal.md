The Royal Arsenal is a 17th-century defensive building tucked into Lviv's old city fabric. Built under Polish King Wladyslaw IV, it once formed part of the city's fortified military infrastructure.

The stone facade, baroque details, and surviving portals make it a worthwhile stop while walking between the Dominican Church, the old walls, and the museums around the historic center.
