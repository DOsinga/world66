Saint Anna Temple stands on a site with a long church history: a wooden church was first built here in 1507, restored in 1599, and later replaced by a stone church in the 1760s.

It is a quieter stop than Lviv's major cathedrals, but worthwhile for visitors tracing the city's layered religious architecture west of the old centre.
