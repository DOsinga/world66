Seasons House is a late-classical townhouse on Virmenska Street, worth a pause for the reliefs on its facade. Chronos appears between the upper floors, while scenes of daily life represent the seasons and a frieze of zodiac signs runs above them.

It is a small architectural stop rather than a major sight, but it fits neatly into a walk through Lviv's Armenian quarter.
