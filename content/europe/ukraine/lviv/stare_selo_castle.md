Stare Selo Castle is a large ruined fortress southeast of Lviv, with surviving walls and towers that still show its former strength. The setting is rough rather than restored, which gives the site a more exploratory feel than a polished museum castle.

It is best approached as a day-trip stop for travellers who like ruins and regional history. Check current conditions and access before going; when feasible, the castle adds a wilder defensive counterpoint to Lviv’s refined old-town architecture.
