Stryiskyi Park is one of Lviv's most elegant green spaces, laid out in the late 19th century with curving paths, mature trees, ponds, and open lawns. It feels more refined than a simple neighbourhood park, especially in the quieter upper sections.

The park is a good southward walk from the centre when you want Lviv beyond churches, cafes, and Habsburg facades. It also gives a glimpse of the city's old exhibition grounds and its habit of folding greenery into urban life.
