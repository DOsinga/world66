The Gallery of Ukrainian Military Uniforms is a small specialist exhibition in central Lviv. Its focus is Ukrainian military dress from the early 20th century, giving a compact visual introduction to the armies and movements that shaped the period.

It is a niche stop, but worthwhile if you are interested in military history or want a short museum visit near the centre.
