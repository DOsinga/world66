---
title: Things to Do in Lviv
type: section
---

Lviv's old town is one of the best-preserved city centres in Eastern Europe, compact enough to explore on foot but rich enough to keep you busy for days. The UNESCO-listed core is dense with churches, palaces, courtyards, and curious museums. Beyond the centre, the cemeteries, parks, and Habsburg-era neighbourhoods reward the visitor who wanders off the obvious routes.
