Univ Monastery is one of the more meaningful religious day trips from Lviv, a fortified monastic complex with deep roots in Ukrainian Greek Catholic tradition. Its walls, church, and rural setting give the visit a very different mood from Lviv's urban churches.

The monastery is active, so the best visits are quiet and respectful rather than tour-bus hurried. It suits travelers interested in religious history, pilgrimage places, or the countryside around Lviv, and it pairs well with a broader exploration of villages east of the city.
