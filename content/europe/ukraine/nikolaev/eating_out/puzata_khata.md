The Mykolaiv branch of this well-known Ukrainian canteen chain is a reliable and unpretentious option in the city centre. The format is self-service: you walk past the counter, choose from borscht, varenyky, holubtsi, deruny, and the day's hot mains, pay by weight, and sit down. The food is straightforward Ukrainian home cooking done consistently well. It's not the most memorable meal in the city, but it's filling, affordable, and honest — useful if you want something quick and local without navigating a menu in Ukrainian.
