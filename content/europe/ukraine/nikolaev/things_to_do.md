title: Things to Do
type: section
---

Mykolaiv's sights reflect its unusual identity: a naval-industrial city with a surprisingly rich cultural and scientific heritage. The Museum of Shipbuilding anchors the old city centre, the astronomical observatory occupies its own quiet quarter, and beneath the parks the Bronze Age city of Dykyi Sad waits to be explored. The zoo, meanwhile, is genuinely world-class by any measure.
