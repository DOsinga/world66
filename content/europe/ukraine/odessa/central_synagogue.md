Odesa's Central Synagogue is a working religious centre and a reminder of the city's large Jewish community. The building dates from the mid-19th century and was restored in the 1990s after decades of Soviet-era interruption.

Inside, the bimah, mikvah, library, and community offices make it more than a historic shell. It is most rewarding for travellers interested in Odesa's Jewish history, especially alongside the city's literary and multicultural landmarks.
