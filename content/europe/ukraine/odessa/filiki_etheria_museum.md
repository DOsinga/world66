The Filiki Etheria Museum occupies buildings once used by members of Odessa's Greek community. In 1814 they became a meeting place for Filiki Eteria, the secret society that helped prepare the Greek War of Independence.

The museum is a small but worthwhile stop for visitors interested in Odessa's Black Sea trading history and the city's Greek connections. It pairs naturally with a walk around the old centre and nearby Prymorsky Boulevard.
