---
title: "Getting Around"
type: section
---

The city centre is compact and best covered on foot. Trams run the length of the main streets and are slow but charming; the route from the train station to Arcadia beach via the city centre is one of the longest tram routes still operating in Ukraine. Taxis and ride-hailing apps are widely available. The cable car provides a scenic (if unreliable) descent from the clifftop promenade to Otrada beach.
