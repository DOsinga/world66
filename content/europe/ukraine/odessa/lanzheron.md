Lanzheron is the closest beach area to central Odessa, below Shevchenko Park and reachable by public transport followed by a short walk. It is a practical choice for a quick Black Sea stop without going farther out to Arcadia, and the nearby cable car and dolphinarium make the area busier than a simple strip of sand.
