Malibu is a beach in the Luzanovka neighbourhood, northeast of central Odesa. It is less famous than Arcadia, but it is easy to reach by bus and is known locally for cleaner sand and a more serviced beach setup than many stretches of the city shore.

Come for a straightforward Black Sea beach stop rather than architecture or nightlife. The trip makes most sense in warm weather, when the long ride out from the centre is part of a relaxed beach day.
