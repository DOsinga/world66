The Museum of Western and Eastern Art occupies the former Pototsky residence, a late 19th-century mansion close to Odessa's central sights. Its galleries range from icons to European painting and Asian decorative art, with works by well-known artists such as Aivazovsky giving the collection more weight than its modest size suggests.
