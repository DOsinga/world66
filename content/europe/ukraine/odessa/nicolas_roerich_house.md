Nicolas Roerich House is a small Odessa museum devoted to the Russian painter, traveller, and philosopher. Founded in 2000, it has several rooms with reproductions, albums, and portraits connected with Roerich and his artistic circle. It is a niche stop, best for visitors already interested in Roerich or Symbolist-era art.
