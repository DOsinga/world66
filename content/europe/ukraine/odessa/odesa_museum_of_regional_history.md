The Odesa Museum of Regional History fills a 19th-century mansion with exhibits on the city and its wider region, from medieval steppe history through the port's imperial and modern eras. It is a useful stop if you want more context than the seafront monuments can give.

The setting is part of the appeal: rooms of an old city mansion framing maps, documents, weapons, household objects, and changing exhibitions tied to Odesa's complicated past.
