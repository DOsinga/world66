Poltava's aircraft museum preserves a striking open-air collection of Soviet long-range and strategic aviation, including large bombers and support aircraft connected with the city's military airfield history. The scale of the aircraft is the reason to come.

Opening conditions can be affected by the wider security situation in Ukraine, so verify locally before planning around it. When accessible, it is one of Poltava's more distinctive specialist museums.
