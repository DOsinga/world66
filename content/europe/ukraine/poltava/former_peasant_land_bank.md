The former Peasant Land Bank is one of Poltava's most eye-catching buildings, a piece of early 20th-century architecture whose colour and ornament make it stand apart from the city's more classical monuments. Built for a financial institution serving agricultural borrowers, it now reads less as a bank than as a civic landmark.

Look for the tiled surfaces, decorative gables, and almost storybook quality of the facade. It is a good stop while walking central Poltava because it broadens the city's architectural range: not only imperial squares and war monuments, but also the confident regional style of a city tied to land, credit, and Ukrainian identity.
