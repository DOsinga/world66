Assumption Church is one of the older church buildings in central Uman, a restrained classicist reminder of the city's Catholic past. It is a small sight, but useful if you are trying to read the city beyond Sofiyivka Park.

Add it to a central walk rather than planning around it. Uman's best travel day works by layering these smaller stops around the park and pilgrimage quarter.
