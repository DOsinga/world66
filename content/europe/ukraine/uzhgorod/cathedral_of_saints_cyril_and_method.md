The Cathedral of Saints Cyril and Method is a large Orthodox church in Uzhhorod, built on a scale that makes it hard to miss. Its broad footprint and tall central mass give it a different presence from the city's older, smaller churches.

Services have been held here since 2000, and the cathedral remains an active place of worship. For visitors, it is mainly an architectural stop and a useful marker of the city's more recent religious life.
