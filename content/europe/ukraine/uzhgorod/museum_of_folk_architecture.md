The Museum of Folk Architecture and Life is an open-air museum on the hill next to Uzhhorod Castle, containing over 30 traditional structures relocated from villages across Zakarpattia Oblast. It is one of the best such museums in eastern Europe: the buildings are not replicas but the real thing, moved here to save them from abandonment or demolition.

The collection includes Rusyn, Ukrainian, Romanian, and Hungarian vernacular buildings — farmhouses, barns, windmills, and churches — each representing a different community that has lived in the Carpathian borderlands over the last four centuries. The 18th-century wooden Church of St Michael, with its three shingled onion domes, is the centrepiece. Allow a couple of hours to walk through the full site at a comfortable pace; it is easy to spend longer.
