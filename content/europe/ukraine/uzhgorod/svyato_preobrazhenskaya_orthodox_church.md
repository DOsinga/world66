Svyato-Preobrazhenskaya Orthodox Church is one of Uzhhorods active religious landmarks, set within a city better known for its castle, synagogue, and river embankment. It broadens the picture of a borderland city where Central European, Orthodox, Greek Catholic, and Jewish histories sit close together.

The church is worth a short stop if you are already exploring beyond the main square and castle. Go quietly, dress respectfully, and treat it as a working place of worship rather than an architectural museum.
