The Roshen Fountain sits on the Southern Bug River in central Vinnytsia and is one of the largest floating fountain complexes in Europe. The structure consists of three floating platforms holding 307 water jets, 3,500 LED lights, and laser projectors. On summer evenings from May through September, it stages light-and-music shows synchronised to classical and popular music, drawing crowds to the riverside promenade.

The shows run at dusk and last roughly 20 minutes, with multiple performances on weekend evenings. The fountain is best viewed from the embankment on the city-centre side of the river, where benches, food stalls, and the promenade infrastructure have been developed around the shows. Even outside show times, the fountain structure itself is a distinctive sight on the water. The surrounding embankment has been substantially upgraded and is now Vinnytsia's main public riverside space.
