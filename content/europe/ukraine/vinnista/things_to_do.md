---
title: Things to Do
type: section
---

Vinnytsia's main sights are centred on the Southern Bug riverfront and the compact old town along Soborna Street. The city is walkable at its core; most of the key attractions can be reached on foot from the centre.

The **Roshen Fountain** on the river is the city's most famous sight — a floating fountain complex that puts on light-and-music shows on summer evenings, drawing large crowds to the embankment promenade. The **Pirogov National Museum** to the south of the centre preserves the estate and embalmed body of the celebrated 19th-century military surgeon Nikolai Pirogov, a genuinely unusual and memorable visit.

The **Transfiguration Cathedral**, a large Orthodox church on the riverbank, and **Saint Nicholas Church** are the main historic religious buildings. The Soviet-era **Vinnytsia Water Tower** is a landmark of the city skyline. Outside the city, the ruins of Hitler's **Werwolf** bunker at Strizhavka make for an eerie excursion.
