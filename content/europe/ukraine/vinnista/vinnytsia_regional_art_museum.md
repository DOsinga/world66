Vinnytsia Regional Art Museum holds paintings, decorative art, porcelain, and Italian majolica in a collection that spans several centuries. It is a useful stop for seeing regional cultural history beyond the city's better-known churches and monuments.

The museum is near the centre, so it pairs easily with the Transfiguration Cathedral, the water tower, and the other compact sights around central Vinnytsia.
