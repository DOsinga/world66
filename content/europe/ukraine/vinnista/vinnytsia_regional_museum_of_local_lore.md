Vinnytsia Regional Museum of Local Lore covers the archaeology, natural history, and regional culture of Vinnytsia oblast. Highlights include Sarmatian ornaments, Bronze Age burial finds, Cossack-era material, and the mammoth skeleton nicknamed Winnie.
