The Vinnytsia Water Tower is a distinctive early 20th-century structure in the city centre, built in 1912 during the period when Vinnytsia was part of the Russian Empire. The tower's unusual appearance — tall masonry shaft with horizontal bracing elements — makes it one of the more photogenic industrial buildings in the city.

The tower served as a working water supply installation for decades and is now a recognised architectural landmark of Vinnytsia. It sits near the city's central streets and is visible from several points in the centre. The surrounding area has been modestly landscaped and the tower is occasionally used as a backdrop for events. It is not open to the interior for visitors but the exterior is worth a look while walking through the centre.
