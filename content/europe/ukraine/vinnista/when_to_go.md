---
title: When to Go
type: section
---

Vinnytsia is at its most appealing in **summer (June–August)**, when the Roshen Fountain operates its evening shows, the riverside promenade is lively, and the weather is warm (typically 22–28°C). This is when the city is most tourist-friendly.

**Spring (April–May)** and **autumn (September–October)** are quieter and pleasant — comfortable temperatures for walking, without the summer crowds. The Podillya Dendropark is especially good in spring blossom and autumn colour.

**Winter (November–March)** is cold, with temperatures often below freezing and snow from December onward. The fountain is inactive from October until May. The city continues to function normally in winter and there are no particular advantages or disadvantages for visiting, but most outdoor attractions are less appealing.

Note: visitors from outside Ukraine should check current travel advisories before planning a trip given the ongoing security situation since 2022.
