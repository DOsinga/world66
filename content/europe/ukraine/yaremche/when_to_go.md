---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

Yaremche has two distinct seasons.

**Summer (June through September)** is the hiking season. Daytime temperatures sit between 18 and 25 °C at town level, cooler on the ridges. Trails dry out, the alpine meadows fill with wildflowers, and the long evenings make for easy long walks. July and August are the busiest months, with school holidays bringing Ukrainian families up from the cities; book ahead. Sudden afternoon thunderstorms are common above 1,500 metres — start early and watch the sky.

**Winter (December through March)** is for snow. Temperatures sit around freezing at town level, well below at altitude, and snow lies through most of the period. Bukovel, 30 kilometres south, runs Ukraine's largest ski operation; smaller cross-country tracks and sleighing options are based out of Yaremche itself. April and October are mud seasons — trails are wet, lower forests bare, and many guesthouses close.

A working note: Ukraine has been at war since 2022 and the security situation can shift quickly. Yaremche is in the far west and has remained safe, but check current advisories and have travel insurance that covers the country before booking.
