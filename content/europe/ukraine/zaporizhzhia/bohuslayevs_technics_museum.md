Bohuslayev's Technics Museum, also known as the Motor Sich Museum, displays Soviet-era aircraft, motorcycles, engines, and military equipment. The collection is strongest on aviation and industrial technology, reflecting Zaporizhzhia's long role as a manufacturing city.

It is a useful stop for visitors interested in aircraft, engineering, or Soviet industrial history, and pairs well with the city's larger industrial and Dnipro River sights.
