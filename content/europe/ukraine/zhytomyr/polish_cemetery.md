Zhytomyr's Polish Cemetery was founded in 1789 and reflects the city's long Polish and Catholic history. It is no longer an active cemetery, but its old graves, wartime associations, and local stories about catacombs make it a worthwhile stop for visitors tracing the city's layered past.
