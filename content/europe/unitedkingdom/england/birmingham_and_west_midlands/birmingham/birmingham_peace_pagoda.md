Birmingham Peace Pagoda, formally the Dhamma Talaka Pagoda, rises above Edgbaston Reservoir in a place where you do not quite expect Burmese Buddhist architecture. Its white-and-gold stupa, monastery buildings, and small grounds make a peaceful contrast with the traffic and brick streets around Ladywood and Edgbaston.

Come with the right expectations: this is an active religious site, not a museum. The reward is the atmosphere, the unusual silhouette against the reservoir, and the reminder that Birmingham’s spiritual map is much wider than its industrial image suggests. Dress and behave respectfully, and combine it with a walk around the water.
