Lickey Hills Country Park covers more than 200 hectares of heathland, conifer plantation and deciduous woodland south-west of Birmingham. It is best in spring for bluebells and in autumn for colour and bilberries, with trails, viewpoints and a visitor centre making it an easy countryside outing from the city.
