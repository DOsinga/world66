Woodgate Valley Country Park gives Birmingham a surprisingly rural escape inside the city boundary. The park follows the Bourn Brook through meadows, hedgerows, woodland, and wetland, with paths that are easy enough for a casual stroll but long enough to feel like a proper break from traffic. It is a good reminder that Birmingham's edges are greener than its industrial reputation suggests.

This is a local's park, not a headline attraction, so come for fresh air rather than spectacle. It works well if you are staying in the west of the city or want a low-cost walk with open space and birdlife. After rain, some paths can be muddy, which is part of the country-park bargain.
