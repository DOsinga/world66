---
title: When to Go
type: section
---

Church Stretton is a year-round destination, but summer and autumn are the most popular seasons for walking. July and August bring the greatest crowds to Carding Mill Valley on weekends; if you want the Long Mynd to yourself, come midweek or in the shoulder months. The heather on the plateau blooms purple in August, which is the one time the crowds are worth tolerating.

Spring is excellent for walking — the batches run with clear water from winter rain, the valley sides are vivid green, and the paths are largely empty. April and May are particularly good, with long daylight hours and the hills coming back to life.

Winter walking has its appeal too. Snow on the Long Mynd is common and transforms the plateau into something genuinely remote-feeling. Frost on Caer Caradoc on a clear December morning, with views stretching to Snowdonia, is hard to beat. Pack for cold, wet conditions whenever you go — the weather comes in quickly from Wales.

The town hosts a small literary festival in October and various walking events through the year, but none so big as to overwhelm the place.
