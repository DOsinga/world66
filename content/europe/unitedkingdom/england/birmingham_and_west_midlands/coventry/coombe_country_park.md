Coombe Country Park gives Coventry a generous green escape, with woodland trails, formal gardens, open lawns, and a lake around the former Coombe Abbey estate. It is especially useful if you are travelling with children or need a break from the city centre.

The park is not just a picnic field. Bird hides, wetland edges, and long paths make it good for a half-day outdoors, while the old estate buildings add a bit of historic atmosphere to the walk.
