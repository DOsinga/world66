The Coventry Canal Art Trail turns a working industrial corridor into a slow, linear city walk. Public artworks, bridges, towpath views, and old canal infrastructure make the route more than a shortcut; it is a reminder that Coventry's history was tied to transport and manufacturing as much as to its cathedral ruins.

It is best approached as an hour or two outdoors rather than a single sight. Walk a manageable section from the basin, watch for sculpture and details along the water, and use it to connect the centre with the city's less polished industrial edges.
