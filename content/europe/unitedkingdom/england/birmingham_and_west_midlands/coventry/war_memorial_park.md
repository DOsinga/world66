War Memorial Park is Coventry's main green lung, laid out after the First World War as a civic memorial and recreation ground. Its formal memorial area gives way to broad lawns, paths, sports fields, and tree-lined spaces that locals use year-round.

For visitors it is a good way to see everyday Coventry beyond the cathedral quarter. Go for a walk, a picnic, or a pause after the city-centre museums, and keep the memorial purpose in mind rather than treating it as just another park.
