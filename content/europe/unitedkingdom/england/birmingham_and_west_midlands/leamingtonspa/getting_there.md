---
title: "Getting There"
type: section
---

Leamington Spa railway station on The Parade has regular direct trains to London Marylebone (about 90 minutes) and Birmingham Snow Hill (about 30 minutes). The station also has connections to Coventry, Oxford, and Stratford-upon-Avon. National Express coaches serve the town from Birmingham and London. By car, Leamington is just off the M40 motorway, making it easily accessible from London and the South East.
