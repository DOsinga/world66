One of the most consistently praised Indian restaurants in Leamington, Mango occupies a smart dining room just off The Parade. The cooking covers the North Indian and Bangladeshi repertoire competently, with good lamb dishes and a selection of well-spiced vegetarian options that go beyond the usual.

Prices are reasonable for the quality. The service is attentive without being overbearing. Worth booking ahead at weekends — the student crowd from Warwick University keeps the restaurant busy most evenings.
