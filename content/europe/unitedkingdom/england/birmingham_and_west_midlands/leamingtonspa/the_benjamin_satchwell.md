Named after the man who co-discovered Leamington's medicinal springs in 1784, the Benjamin Satchwell is a Wetherspoons pub occupying one of the grandest buildings on The Parade. The setting is better than the brand would suggest — high ceilings, tall windows, and the kind of Victorian pub interior that the chain has done a good job preserving rather than modernising.

Like all Wetherspoons, it is cheap and reliable. The value for money is hard to beat in the town centre, and the building itself is worth stepping into regardless of what you drink. Open from morning for breakfast through to late evening.
