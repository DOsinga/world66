---
title: "Bars and Cafes"
type: section
---

Herefordshire is cider country and Ledbury takes the drinking of it seriously. Almost every pub will pour you a flight of three or four local ciders alongside the standard beer; the bar of **The Feathers Hotel** is the most atmospheric place to do it. The smaller **Prince of Wales** on Church Lane is a proper old-fashioned drinkers' pub with timber-framed walls leaning inwards.

The town also has a strong cafe culture, helped by the literary visitors. **The Mousetrap Cheese Shop** on Church Lane combines a counter and a small lunch room. **Three Counties Coffee** on the High Street roasts its own beans. The **Market Theatre** cafe is the quirky option for a coffee with cake among amateur dramatic society posters.

For evening drinks with a view, head out to the foot of the Malvern Hills — the **Wellington Inn** at Wellington Heath or the **Royal Oak** at Ledbury Hill both have good gardens and a long late-summer evening.
