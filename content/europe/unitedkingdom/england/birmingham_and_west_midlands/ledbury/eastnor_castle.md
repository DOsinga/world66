Eastnor Castle sits two miles east of Ledbury in a deer park at the foot of the Malverns. It is open to the public on most weekends from Easter to October, and the visit is a complete period set-piece: the Great Hall with its medieval armoury, the long Gothic drawing room, the Pugin-decorated state apartments, and a small lake immediately below the castle. The wider estate has marked walking trails, an adventure playground and the long-stay deer herd.

The castle is also a regular wedding and film location — most recently it doubled for the British country house in *Bridgerton*. Check the open-day calendar before driving out; on closed days only the grounds are accessible, on a separate ticket.
