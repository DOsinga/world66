---
title: "Eating Out"
type: section
---

Ledbury has been on the food map since chef Mark Stinchcombe took over **Eckington Manor** nearby and Shaun Hill's old protégés started running its kitchens, but the town itself has remained sensibly unpretentious about it. Two pubs and a handful of cafes do all the work.

**The Feathers Hotel** on the High Street is the right choice for a proper lunch — bar food and local Herefordshire beef under 450-year-old oak beams. **The Talbot Hotel** on New Street is the other historic coaching inn, with a quieter dining room. For coffee and lunch, **Three Counties Coffee Roastery** on the High Street and the **Mousetrap Cheese Shop** lunch counter on Church Lane are the two reliable stops; the latter does the best cheese-and-ham toastie in the county.

For a longer meal, the **Three Tuns** at nearby Bromsberrow and the **Butchers Arms** at Woolhope are both worth the short drive. Local specialities to watch for: Hereford beef, cider-glazed pork, perry pears, and the indispensable **Hereford Hop**, a cheese rolled in toasted hops.
