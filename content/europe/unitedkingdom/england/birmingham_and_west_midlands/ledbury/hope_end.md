The house she knew was demolished and replaced in 1867; only the eccentric Moorish stable-block survives."

Hope End is a small private estate three miles north of Ledbury in the foothills of the Malverns. The main house has been rebuilt twice since the Brownings sold it, but the Moorish stable block — a Regency oddity of horseshoe arches and minarets — survives and is now part of a country-house hotel. The wider estate is criss-crossed by public footpaths through old plantations and orchards, and the views back across the valley to the Malverns are open and quiet.

There is no formal museum or visitor centre — Hope End is a walking destination rather than a tourist site. The Browning Trail runs from Ledbury through Hope End to Eastnor and back; the full circuit is about 12 miles, but you can easily pick up a shorter segment from Wellington Heath.
