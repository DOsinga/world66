The Market House sits squarely in the middle of Ledbury High Street, a black-and-white timber-framed hall propped above the cobbles on sixteen chestnut posts. It is the town's emblem and the most photographed building in Herefordshire. The ground floor remains open: a Saturday morning produce market still runs underneath, with locals chatting between the cars that have to slow to a crawl around the pillars.

The upper hall is occasionally open for community events and craft fairs; entry is free when it is. Otherwise the building is best appreciated from outside, ideally from the bottom of the High Street looking up. Pair the visit with the climb up Church Lane immediately behind.
