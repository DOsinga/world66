St Michael and All Angels is the parish church at the top of Church Lane, with origins in the 12th century and a separate eight-storey bell-tower across the churchyard. The combination is unusual: most English churches have their spires attached. The interior is unexpectedly large, with a long nave, a fine collection of medieval and Tudor tombs, and the rare survival of medieval misericords in the choir.

The tower is occasionally open for tower climbs in the summer — when it is, take the offer; the view from the top stretches to the Malvern Hills. Entry to the church itself is free; donations support the upkeep.
