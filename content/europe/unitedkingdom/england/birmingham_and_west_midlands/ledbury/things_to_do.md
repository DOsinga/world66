---
title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Ledbury is a place to walk, not a place to tick off sights. The High Street is the set-piece — a wide cobbled rake of black-and-white timber-framed buildings running down to the **Market House**, the 17th-century oak-framed hall raised on chestnut pillars in the middle of the road. From the Market House, narrow **Church Lane** climbs to **St Michael and All Angels**, with a separate detached bell-tower that has rung over the town for eight hundred years.

The other deliberate stops are the **Painted Room** — a 16th-century townhouse whose Tudor wall-paintings were rediscovered behind plaster in the 1980s — and the **Master's House**, the town's restored medieval almshouse, now the library and tourist information centre. After that, head for the **Malvern Hills** five miles to the east, the **Eastnor Castle** estate next door, or one of the **cider farms** that ring the town.
