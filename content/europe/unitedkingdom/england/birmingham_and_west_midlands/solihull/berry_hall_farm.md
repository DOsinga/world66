A medieval moated farmhouse on Ravenshaw Lane, about two kilometres east of Solihull town centre. It is privately owned and not open to the public inside, but the exterior — approached via a footpath — is clearly visible and worth seeing. The moat is intact and the half-timbering is well preserved.

For visitors interested in medieval English rural architecture, Berry Hall is an unusual survival: an unrestored, unmodified example of a type of structure that has usually been either demolished or heavily adapted. The footpath that passes it forms part of a pleasant walk through the eastern edge of Solihull and along the River Blythe.
