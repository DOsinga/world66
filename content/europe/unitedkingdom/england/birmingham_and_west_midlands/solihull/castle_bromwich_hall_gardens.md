Castle Bromwich Hall Gardens preserve a formal garden landscape on the edge of
Birmingham and Solihull, with restored period layouts, walls, orchards, and seasonal
planting. The appeal is quieter and more historical than a modern public park.

It is a good excursion for garden lovers or anyone based in Solihull who wants a
heritage stop close to town. Check seasonal hours and events, since gardens like this
change significantly through the year.
