The National Memorial Arboretum is a large woodland landscape of military and civic memorials near Alrewas. More than 350 memorials stand among the young trees, from regimental monuments to quiet corners for emergency services, civilian groups, and conflicts that are easy to overlook.

The Armed Forces Memorial is the centrepiece, but the arboretum is best taken slowly. Paths loop through the grounds, and the tone is reflective rather than grandiose, making it one of Staffordshire's most moving stops.
