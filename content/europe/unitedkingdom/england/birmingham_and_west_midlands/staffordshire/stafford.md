Stafford is the county town of Staffordshire, a working Midlands market town that looks more interesting once you leave the through roads. Its centre still has a tight old street pattern, a proper market-square feel, and enough timber framing and red brick to remind you that this was once a much more important administrative stop.

The best reason to pause is the [Ancient High House](/europe/unitedkingdom/england/birmingham_and_west_midlands/staffordshire/stafford/ancient_high_house), an enormous Elizabethan town house on Greengate Street that seems almost too tall and top-heavy for the modern high street around it. Close by are [St Mary's Church](/europe/unitedkingdom/england/birmingham_and_west_midlands/staffordshire/stafford/st_marys_church), [St Chad's Church](/europe/unitedkingdom/england/birmingham_and_west_midlands/staffordshire/stafford/st_chads_church), and the old legal quarter around [Shire Hall](/europe/unitedkingdom/england/birmingham_and_west_midlands/staffordshire/stafford/shire_hall).

Stafford is not a glamorous destination, but it is a useful one. Trains on the West Coast Main Line make it an easy base for [Staffordshire](/europe/unitedkingdom/england/birmingham_and_west_midlands/staffordshire), and the town works well as a low-key stop between the Potteries, Cannock Chase, and the western Midlands.
