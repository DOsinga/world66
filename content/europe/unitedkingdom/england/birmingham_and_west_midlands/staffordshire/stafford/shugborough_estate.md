Shugborough Estate is a Georgian mansion and country estate near Stafford, now run by the National Trust. The house is surrounded by formal gardens, parkland, a model farm, and estate buildings that show how a grand 18th-century property worked.

It is one of the stronger day-out sights around Stafford, especially for visitors interested in historic houses, garden walks, and the landscape of the Trent Valley.
