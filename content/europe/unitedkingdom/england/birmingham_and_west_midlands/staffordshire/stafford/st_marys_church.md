St Mary's is the strongest church in Stafford, a Grade I listed parish church with a long medieval history and a confident position just off the commercial streets. It rewards more than a quick glance, especially if you enjoy English church architecture that mixes Norman, Early English, and later Gothic details.

The interior is quiet and spacious, with carved stone, old monuments, and enough architectural layers to make it feel like a small lesson in Stafford's history. It is a good counterweight to the busier high street: step inside for ten minutes and the town feels older and calmer.
