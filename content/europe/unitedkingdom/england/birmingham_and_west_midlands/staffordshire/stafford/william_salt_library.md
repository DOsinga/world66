The William Salt Library is a specialist local-history library devoted to Staffordshire. It is not a casual museum in the usual sense, but travellers with a taste for county history, genealogy, maps, or old prints may find it more rewarding than another high-street wander.

The collection grew from the papers and books of William Salt, a 19th-century banker and antiquarian. Check access before going, as research libraries keep different hours from tourist attractions, but keep it on your list if Stafford is more than a passing stop.
