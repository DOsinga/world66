Bilston, now part of the metropolitan borough of Wolverhampton but historically its own town, sits a few miles south-east of the city centre and has a long industrial past in ironworking, japanning, and enamel production. It is not a pretty place, but for anyone interested in the Black Country's craft heritage, it has genuine historical significance.

Bilston Market, one of the older outdoor markets in the West Midlands, still runs weekly and is worth a look for food, second-hand goods, and an authentic slice of Black Country commercial life. The Bilston Craft Gallery was an important venue for contemporary craft, though its status changes; check locally before visiting. The area around Bilston is also useful context for understanding what the broader Wolverhampton conurbation looks and feels like beyond the city centre.
