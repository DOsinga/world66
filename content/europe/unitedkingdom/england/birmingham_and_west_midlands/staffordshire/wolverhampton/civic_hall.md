The Wolverhampton Civic Hall is a 3,000-capacity mid-twentieth century venue that has been at the heart of British live music for decades. Now operated as University of Wolverhampton at The Halls, it continues to host major touring acts across rock, indie, and electronic music. The programme sits above most regional venues in scope and frequency.

The building itself is a solid piece of civic architecture — not beautiful, but functional in the way that great music venues often are. The main room has decent sight lines and a straightforward standing/seating arrangement that works well for most genres. The booking history alone — from Led Zeppelin to the Cure — gives it a status that few equivalent venues in the Midlands can match.
