The Grand Theatre is one of Frank Matcham's finest surviving regional theatres — a beautifully preserved Victorian venue on Lichfield Street, just along from the art gallery. The auditorium is a gem: gilded, intimate, and with excellent sight lines from every seat. It hosts touring West End productions, ballet, opera, and what is generally regarded as one of the best pantomimes in the Midlands.

The theatre is worth visiting even outside of a performance: the exterior and the public areas of the building reflect the ambition Victorian cities had for their cultural institutions. Check the programme before visiting Wolverhampton — catching a show here is one of the better reasons to come.
