Molineux is one of England's historic football grounds and home to Wolverhampton Wanderers — Wolves — a club with deep roots in the city and a following that is passionate even by English football standards. The stadium sits just north of the city centre, its golden facade visible from a distance, and holds around 32,000 fans on matchday.

If you can get to a home game, the atmosphere is worth experiencing: this is proper Midlands football, loud and committed. Matchday tickets vary in availability depending on the opponent, but Championship and Premier League seasons typically have tickets available for many fixtures. The club has a small museum and offers stadium tours for non-matchday visitors. The statue of legendary manager Stan Cullis stands outside — a fitting tribute to the man who built one of postwar English football's great teams.
