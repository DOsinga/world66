West Park is Wolverhampton's classic Victorian park, laid out with lawns, paths, a lake, planting, and ornamental park buildings close to the city centre. It gives the city a calm green counterweight to its shopping streets, university blocks, and industrial heritage.

The park is most appealing as part of a slow local walk rather than a destination that justifies a long detour. For visitors already in Wolverhampton, though, it is one of the easiest places to understand the civic confidence of the late-19th-century town and to take a break from traffic.
