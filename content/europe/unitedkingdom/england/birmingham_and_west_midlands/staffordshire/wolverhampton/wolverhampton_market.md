Wolverhampton has run a street market in the city centre for centuries, and what remains today reflects the city's population more honestly than most indoor shopping centres. The outdoor market stalls in and around the Piazza Wolverhampton (the broad pedestrianised square off Market Street) sell a mix of fresh produce, fabrics, household goods, and Caribbean and South Asian food.

The food stalls are the main reason to visit: jerk chicken, West Indian patties, South Asian sweets, and fresh vegetables not often seen in supermarkets. Come on a Saturday for the fullest selection. The market has moved and contracted over the years, but the core of street trading around the city centre pedestrian areas is still lively on busy days.
