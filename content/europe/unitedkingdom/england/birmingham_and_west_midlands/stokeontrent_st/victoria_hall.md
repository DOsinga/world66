Victoria Hall is Stoke-on-Trent's main concert-hall venue, standing in Hanley beside the city's civic and theatre core. It broadens the local visitor picture beyond pottery museums, gardens, and football by giving the city a regular live-performance anchor.

The building is most relevant when there is a concert, comedy night, or touring production on. Check the programme before planning around it; without an event, it is mainly an exterior landmark.
