Hall's Croft is the Jacobean house associated with Shakespeare's daughter Susanna and her husband, Dr John Hall. The timber-framed building is one of the main Shakespeare family sites in Stratford-upon-Avon and fits easily into a walking route between the town centre and Holy Trinity Church.

Exhibits focus on the household and on medical practice in Hall's time. The period rooms and garden make it more than a quick plaque stop, especially for visitors already following Stratford's Shakespeare trail.
