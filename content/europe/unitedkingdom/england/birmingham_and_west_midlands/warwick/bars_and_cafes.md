---
title: "Bars and Cafes"
type: section
---

Warwick's pubs are the heart of its evening life. Most are traditional and centuries old — the Tilted Wig on Market Place, the Rose & Crown across the way, the Old Fourpenny Shop near the train station — and they pour a serviceable selection of Midlands ales. For coffee during the day, several independent cafes line Swan Street and the area around the Lord Leycester Hospital.
