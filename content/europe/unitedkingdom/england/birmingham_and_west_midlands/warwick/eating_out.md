---
title: "Eating Out"
type: section
---

Warwick has a small but solid selection of restaurants concentrated along Smith Street, Jury Street, and around the Market Place. The town benefits from being close enough to Leamington Spa for the wider Warwickshire food scene to reach it, and the steady tourist traffic supports a couple of more ambitious kitchens.

Pub food is widely available and the standard is good — Warwickshire has more than its share of well-kept country pubs, and several within the town itself.
