The Shire Hall on Northgate Street served for centuries as the seat of Warwickshire's county courts. The Palladian facade of 1758 is one of the grandest 18th-century public buildings in the Midlands. Behind it lies the Old Gaol — a network of stone-vaulted cells that operated from the 1680s through the 1860s and now houses a small museum on crime and punishment in the county.

A more sobering counterpart to the castle's pageantry. Check opening hours, which are seasonal.
