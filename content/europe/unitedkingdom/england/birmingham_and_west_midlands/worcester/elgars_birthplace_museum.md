Elgar's Birthplace Museum preserves the cottage where composer Edward Elgar was born, just outside Worcester. The site combines the modest family house with displays on his life, music, and local roots.

The garden and countryside setting are part of the appeal, giving the museum a quieter feel than the city-centre sights. It is a good stop for anyone interested in British music or Elgar's connection to Worcestershire.
