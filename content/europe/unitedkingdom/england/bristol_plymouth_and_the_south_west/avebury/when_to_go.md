---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

Avebury is worth visiting year-round, but the stone circle has a particular atmosphere in autumn and early spring when crowds are thin and mist sometimes fills the ditch around the henge. Summer brings the most visitors, particularly around the solstices, when people gather at the stones for informal dawn celebrations — similar to Stonehenge but without the barriers. If you visit in midsummer, arrive early to have the stones to yourself before the tour coaches appear.

Winter days are cold but often clear, and the low sun angle makes the stones cast long shadows across the turf. The walk to West Kennet Long Barrow and Silbury Hill is muddy after rain; wear boots if planning the full circuit.
