Saltford Brass Mill is a compact industrial-history stop between Bath and Bristol, occupying a riverside mill that once helped turn copper and zinc into brass goods for a wide market. The appeal is in the survival of the place itself: water power, furnaces, workshops, and a village setting that show another side of the Georgian Bath region.

It is not a grand museum, so plan around its open days and combine it with a walk or cycle along the Avon. For travellers who like machinery, local industry, or small volunteer-run sites, it adds useful texture to a Bath itinerary that can otherwise lean heavily on crescents and Roman stone.
