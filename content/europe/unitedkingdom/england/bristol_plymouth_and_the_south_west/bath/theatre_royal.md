Bath's Theatre Royal opened in 1805 and remains one of the city's main cultural venues. The historic theatre presents drama, opera, music, comedy, pantomime, and touring productions in a handsome building close to the centre.

It is most rewarding if you book a performance, but the theatre also adds another layer to Bath's Georgian and Regency streetscape.
