---
title: Bars and Cafes
type: section
---

Blandford Forum has strong local drinking credentials thanks to Hall & Woodhouse, the Dorset brewer responsible for the Badger range of ales. The brewery's presence means well-kept local beer is easy to find in the town's pubs. The market place and surrounding streets have a handful of cafes well suited to a mid-morning stop or a cream tea after exploring the Georgian town centre.
