A traditional tearoom on the market place, well placed for a cream tea or a coffee after walking the Georgian town centre. The setting — in a period building overlooking the square — is the main draw. Dorset cream teas are served in the proper fashion, and the homemade cakes are good. Best visited mid-morning or after a walk along the Stour; it fills up on market days.
