A traditional Georgian coaching inn on West Street, in keeping with the town's architectural character. The Crown serves reliable pub-style food using local Dorset ingredients — game from the surrounding estates, lamb from the chalk downlands — alongside the usual grills and bar snacks. The dining room retains some period character. As the most prominent hotel in the town centre, it also functions as a social hub and a reasonable place to take stock over a meal after walking the Georgian streets.
