A well-regarded restaurant and bar on Salisbury Street, popular with locals for more relaxed lunches and dinners. The menu runs from sandwiches and lighter lunches to more substantial evening dishes, with a focus on fresh local produce from Dorset. The wine list is decent and the Dorset ales are well kept. A reliable choice for a proper sit-down meal in a town that is not overrun with dining options.
