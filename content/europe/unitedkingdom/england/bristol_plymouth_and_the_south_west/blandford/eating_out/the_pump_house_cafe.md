A cafe housed in the old Georgian pump house just off the market place — the very building erected after the 1731 fire to supply the town with water and prevent a repeat disaster. The Pump House itself is a listed Georgian structure, and eating or drinking here gives a direct connection to the post-fire rebuilding story. Serves breakfasts, lunches, and cream teas. A pleasant spot on a market day.
