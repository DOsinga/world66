The Georgian parish church at the centre of the rebuilt town, completed around 1739 by John and William Bastard after the great fire destroyed the earlier medieval church. It is one of the finest Georgian churches in Dorset — a broad, light-filled building in red brick with Portland stone dressings, a tower of restrained classical design, and an interior that retains much of its original 18th-century character including box pews and a three-decker pulpit. The building stands on the market place and acts as the visual anchor for the whole Georgian ensemble.
