The heart of Blandford Forum and the showpiece of the Bastard brothers' rebuilding. The market place and its surrounding streets form one of the most intact and coherent Georgian townscapes in rural England — a sequence of two- and three-storey brick buildings with consistent proportions, regular fenestration, and stone details that give the whole ensemble a calm dignity. The church, the town hall, and the former grammar school all open onto the same space. A market is still held here on Thursdays and Saturdays, and the stalls fill the square much as they have since the medieval period.
