Located at Blandford Camp, the military base about 3 miles northeast of the town, this is one of the best regimental museums in the British Army. It tells the story of military communications from the Crimean War to the present day, with hands-on exhibits, Cold War artefacts, and extensive displays on codebreaking, radio, and the development of battlefield communications technology. Free entry and easy parking make it worth the short drive from the town centre, particularly for anyone with an interest in military history or technology.
