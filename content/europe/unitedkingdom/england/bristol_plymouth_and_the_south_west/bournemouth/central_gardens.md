Central Gardens are the green thread through Bournemouth's middle, carrying you away from the shops and toward the sea by way of lawns, mature trees, paths, and planted borders. They are less dramatic than the beach, but they explain why the resort feels unusually leafy for a busy seaside town.

Use the gardens as a walking route rather than a destination to tick off. They connect with the Lower Gardens and The Square, making an easy traffic-free approach to the pier and seafront. In summer they are good for a pause in the shade; outside peak season they offer a quieter view of Bournemouth's old resort planning.
