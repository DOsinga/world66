Bournemouth makes an excellent base for exploring Dorset and the Jurassic Coast. [Swanage](/europe/unitedkingdom/england/bristol_plymouth_and_the_south_west/swanage), with its sandy bay, Victorian pier, and walks out to Old Harry Rocks, is a 40-minute drive or a 90-minute trip via the chain ferry from Sandbanks. The clifftop walk from Studland to Old Harry is one of the great short coastal walks in England.

Inland, [Salisbury](/europe/unitedkingdom/england/bristol_plymouth_and_the_south_west/salisbury) with its great medieval cathedral is about an hour by road or train, and combines well with Stonehenge a few miles further on. The New Forest -- ancient royal hunting ground, half an hour to the east -- is full of free-roaming ponies and good walking; Lyndhurst is the main village.
