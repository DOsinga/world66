Aerospace Bristol tells the story of the city's long aviation industry on the historic Filton site, where aircraft design and manufacturing shaped twentieth-century Bristol. Its star exhibit is Concorde Alpha Foxtrot, the last Concorde built and the last to fly.

The museum is a strong choice even if you are not an aviation obsessive, because it connects engineering, local labour, wartime production, and the glamour of supersonic travel in one place. Allow enough time for the Concorde experience rather than treating it as a quick photo stop.
