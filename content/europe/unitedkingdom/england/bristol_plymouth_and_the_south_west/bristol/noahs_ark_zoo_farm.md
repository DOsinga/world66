Noah's Ark Zoo Farm is a large family zoo west of Bristol, with more than a hundred animal species and one of northern Europe's larger elephant habitats. The site mixes traditional zoo enclosures with farm animals, play areas, and open countryside, so it works best as a half-day family outing.

The zoo has attracted controversy for some of its interpretive material, but as a visitor stop its main draw is the scale of the animal collection and the easy access from Bristol and the coast.
