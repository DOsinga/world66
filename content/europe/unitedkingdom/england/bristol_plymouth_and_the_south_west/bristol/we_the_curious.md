We The Curious is Bristol's hands-on science centre on the Harbourside, built around experiments, engineering, life sciences, making, and open-ended play rather than glass-case displays. It is aimed strongly at families, but the best exhibits are good enough to keep adults from simply waiting by the exit.

The location makes it easy to combine with M Shed, Bristol Cathedral, Pero's Bridge, or a general wander along the floating harbour. It is especially useful on wet days, when Bristol's outdoor street art and waterfront walks lose some of their appeal.
