Caerhays Castle and Gardens sits above the south Cornwall coast, with a Regency Gothic castle and a large woodland garden known for magnolias, camellias, and rhododendrons. Much of the planting grew out of early 20th-century expeditions to China, and the estate became important in breeding the Williamsii camellias now common in British gardens.

The garden is the real draw. Come in spring if you can, when the magnolias and camellias are at their best, and leave time to wander the paths rather than treating it as a quick castle stop.
