Gyllyngvase Beach is Falmouth's main sandy beach, close enough to town to reach on foot and large enough to feel like a proper seaside stop rather than a small cove. The beach has lifeguard patrols in summer, public toilets, showers, and places for coffee or snacks behind the sand.

The reef at the southwest end is popular with snorkellers, while the open bay draws swimmers, surfers, and paddleboarders. It gets busy in summer, so come early if you want parking or a quieter swim.
