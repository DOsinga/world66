---
title: Day Trips
type: section
---

Newquay is a good base for the north Cornish coast and inland.

**Padstow** (40 minutes north) for harbour-front seafood and Rick Stein restaurants. **St Ives** (an hour southwest) for the Tate, the Barbara Hepworth Museum and one of the loveliest small towns in England. **Tintagel** (an hour and ten minutes north) for the cliff-top ruins of Arthurian myth. **The Eden Project** (45 minutes southeast) is the famous botanical biodome built into a former china clay pit. The **Lost Gardens of Heligan** are nearby. Bodmin Moor — wide, empty, dotted with prehistoric remains — starts a half-hour drive inland.
