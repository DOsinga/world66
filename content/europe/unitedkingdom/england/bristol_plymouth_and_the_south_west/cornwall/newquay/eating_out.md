---
title: Eating Out
type: section
---

Newquay is a holiday town and most of its eating reflects that — fish and chips, pasties, burgers, and a long lineup of cheerful seafront pubs. There is also a small but real foodie scene, especially out at Watergate Bay.

**The Beach Hut at Watergate Bay** does excellent burgers and fish and chips with sand outside the door. **Senor Dick's** is a long-standing Mexican on the headland with a sea view. **The Stable** on Fore Street is the best of the pizza chains. **The Boathouse** at the harbour serves crab, lobster and other catch landed just below. Cornish pasties are everywhere; **The Cornish Bakery** and **Warrens** are the chains, but smaller independents on Bank Street often do better.

For an upmarket evening, **Lewinnick Lodge** on the Pentire headland combines a cliff-edge view with good modern British cooking. Booking advised in summer.
