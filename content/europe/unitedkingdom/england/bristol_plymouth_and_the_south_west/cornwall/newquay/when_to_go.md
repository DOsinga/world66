---
title: When to Go
type: section
---

Cornwall's weather is mild and changeable year-round.

**Summer (June-August)** is the high season — long evenings, warm sea, packed beaches, and the loudest nightlife. Prices peak in July and August and accommodation can be hard to find without booking ahead.

**Spring (April-May)** and **autumn (September-October)** are the best times to visit. The town is calmer, accommodation is cheaper, and the Atlantic swell builds in September into the prime surf months. Water temperatures are warmest in September.

**Winter (November-March)** is wet and windy. Most attractions are closed and the beaches are empty, but for storm-watching and serious surfing it is the most rewarding time. Bring waterproofs and a good wetsuit.
