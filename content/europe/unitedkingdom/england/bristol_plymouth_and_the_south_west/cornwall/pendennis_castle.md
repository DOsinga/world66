Pendennis Castle is one of England's best-preserved coastal fortresses, guarding the entrance to the Carrick Roads at Falmouth. Its defences show several centuries of military history, from Tudor artillery works through later coastal batteries and Second World War installations.

The headland setting is a major part of the visit. Allow time for the sea views as well as the gun rooms, walls, and military exhibits.
