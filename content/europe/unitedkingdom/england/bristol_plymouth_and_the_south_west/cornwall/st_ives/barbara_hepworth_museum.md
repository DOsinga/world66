The Barbara Hepworth Sculpture Garden preserves the studio in Trewyn where Hepworth lived and worked from 1949 until her death in 1975. Administered by Tate St Ives, it is one of the most moving artistic spaces in Britain.

The garden is small — just a quarter of an acre — but densely planted and filled with Hepworth's bronze and stone sculptures arranged as she wanted them, in dialogue with the plants, the changing light, and each other. Inside the studio, her tools lie where she left them, her overalls hang on the door, and the workbench carries the marks of decades of carving. The combination of intimate domestic space and large abstract sculpture is unlike anything else. Book tickets in advance in summer — it is popular and visitor numbers are restricted.
