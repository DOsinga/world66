---
title: Getting There
type: section
---

**By bus**: The First Kernow 1/1A service runs from Penzance to St Just (30 minutes) several times daily, and continues to Land's End. Penzance is the main transport hub for the far west, with good rail connections to the rest of England.

**By train**: The nearest station is Penzance, at the end of the Great Western Railway line from London Paddington (about 5 hours direct). The Night Riviera sleeper from London Paddington runs several nights a week, arriving at Penzance in the morning.

**By car**: St Just is 8 miles west of Penzance on the A3071. Parking in the town square is metered and can be busy in summer. Roadside parking is available on the approach roads for longer stays. Driving the B3306 coastal road between St Ives and St Just is spectacular but the lanes are narrow; allow extra time.

**Land's End Airport**: Served by Skybus flights from Newquay, Exeter, and the Isles of Scilly. This is primarily used for the Isles of Scilly link, but the airport is 2 miles from St Just.
