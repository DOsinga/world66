---
title: When to Go
type: section
---

May to September offers the longest days and the best weather for coastal walking — the evenings are long and the light exceptional. July and August bring the most visitors, though St Just sees far fewer tourists than Penzance or St Ives, and accommodation pressure is manageable.

April and October have the best light for photography and the fewest crowds. The moors and coast are accessible and quieter, but the weather can turn quickly and some accommodation and cafes operate reduced hours.

Winter in the far west is wild: gales, dramatic sea conditions, and very short days. The coastal path is exposed and can be hazardous in high winds. The town functions normally year-round and has a year-round community feel that the tourist villages lack; the pubs are reliable and warm.

For the Levant beam engine: it runs under steam on specific announced days, typically weekends from Easter through September. Check the National Trust website before visiting if the engine is your main purpose.
