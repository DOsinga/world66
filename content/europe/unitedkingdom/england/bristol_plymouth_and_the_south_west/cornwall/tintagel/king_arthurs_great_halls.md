King Arthur's Great Halls is a historic building in Tintagel with an interior shaped around the Arthurian legend. It was created as a ceremonial hall and contains paintings by William Hatherell and stained glass windows showing scenes from the stories.

It is most interesting as part of Tintagel's wider Arthurian landscape, alongside Tintagel Castle, Merlin's Cave, and the village's other legend-linked sights.
