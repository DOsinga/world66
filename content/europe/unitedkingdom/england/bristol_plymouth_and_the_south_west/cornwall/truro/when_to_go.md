---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

Truro is a year-round city and works in most seasons. Summer (June to August) brings the warmest weather and the longest days — ideal if you are using the city as a base for coastal day trips. The annual Truro City of Lights lantern parade takes place in November and turns the city centre into a genuinely spectacular event, worth building a trip around.

The farmers' market runs on Wednesday and Saturday mornings and is well worth catching whatever the season. August can be very busy with tourist traffic through Cornwall generally, which means parking and accommodation need booking well ahead.

Winter is quiet, cold (by Cornish standards — rarely below freezing), and off-season, but the city carries on and the cathedral looks particularly good in grey winter light.
