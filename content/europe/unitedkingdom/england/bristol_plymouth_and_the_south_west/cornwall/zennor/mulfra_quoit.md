Mulfra Quoit is a small Neolithic chambered tomb on the moorland above west Cornwall. Its capstone has slipped and now leans against the remaining supports, giving the monument a rougher, more exposed feel than better-known quoits nearby.

It is a short, atmospheric prehistoric stop for walkers crossing the high ground between Zennor, Madron, and the Penwith moors.
