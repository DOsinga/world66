---
title: "Things to Do in Ashburton"
type: section
---

Ashburton punches above its weight for a town of its size. The medieval stannary history shows in the architecture, the independent shops are better than most market towns manage, and the surrounding Dartmoor landscape — tors, rivers, and moorland — is immediately accessible from the high street.

The [River Ashburn](/europe/unitedkingdom/england/bristol_plymouth_and_the_south_west/devon/ashburton/river_ashburn) runs through the lower part of town and down to join the Dart. [Buckland Beacon](/europe/unitedkingdom/england/bristol_plymouth_and_the_south_west/devon/ashburton/buckland_beacon) is a short drive and an easy walk, with views across the whole of southern Dartmoor. [Buckfast Abbey](/europe/unitedkingdom/england/bristol_plymouth_and_the_south_west/devon/ashburton/buckfast_abbey), two miles north, is one of the genuinely impressive monastic buildings in Devon. [St Andrew's Church](/europe/unitedkingdom/england/bristol_plymouth_and_the_south_west/devon/ashburton/st_andrews_church) at the bottom of the main street is the town's medieval centrepiece.

[Pridhamsleigh Cavern](/europe/unitedkingdom/england/bristol_plymouth_and_the_south_west/devon/ashburton/pridhamsleigh_cavern) is an accessible wild cave on the outskirts of town — a Devon curiosity that most visitors don't know about. [Holne Bridge](/europe/unitedkingdom/england/bristol_plymouth_and_the_south_west/devon/ashburton/holne_bridge) is a medieval crossing point over the Dart and a good starting point for walking in the Dart Valley.
