---
title: Things to Do
type: section
---

Chagford is a walker's base above all else. The surrounding moorland holds some of the finest Bronze Age archaeology in Britain, and the lanes and paths that radiate from the town square will take you to stone rows, stone circles, and granite tors within an easy half-day. The nearby attractions — Castle Drogo, Gidleigh Castle, Scorhill Stone Circle — make worthwhile short excursions.
