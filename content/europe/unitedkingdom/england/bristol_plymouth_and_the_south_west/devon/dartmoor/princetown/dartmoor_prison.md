Dartmoor Prison is one of England's most famous — and most forbidding — places of incarceration. Still in operation as a Category C men's prison, it dominates the skyline of Princetown with its granite walls and watchtowers, a bleak silhouette entirely in keeping with the surrounding moorland. The prison is not open to visitors, but it can be clearly seen from the road.

The adjacent **Dartmoor Prison Museum** (in the former gatehouse building) tells the story of the prison from its Napoleonic origins through the 20th century. It holds original artefacts, prisoner records, and a reconstructed cell. The museum is small but genuinely absorbing and gives a good sense of what life inside was actually like. Opening hours are limited — check before visiting.
