---
title: When to Go
type: section
---

Widecombe is a year-round destination, though the experience varies considerably by season. Summer (June–August) brings the most visitors and the most reliable weather, with the moorland looking its best in long daylight. September brings the fair, the best single reason to time a visit if you can. The village is relatively quiet in spring and autumn, the weather more unpredictable, and the moor has a different, more dramatic character under low cloud and mist.

Winter on Dartmoor is not for everyone — the high moor can be genuinely wild, with fog, driving rain, and occasional snow — but on clear winter days the light across the moorland is extraordinary and the village is almost entirely free of tourists. Wellies or proper walking boots are advisable at any time of year.
