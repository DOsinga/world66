Wind Tor is one of the accessible granite tors above Widecombe, a short climb into the open moor with the village quickly dropping away behind you. The rocks are not Dartmoor's biggest, but the setting gives you the classic mix of grass, granite, sheep, and wide sky.

It is a good choice when you want a real moorland walk without committing to a long route. Weather changes fast here, so carry a layer and take navigation seriously even on a short outing.
