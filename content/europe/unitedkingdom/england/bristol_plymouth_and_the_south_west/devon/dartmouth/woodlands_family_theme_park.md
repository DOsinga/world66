Woodlands Family Theme Park is the big all-weather family attraction outside Dartmouth,
with rides, indoor play, animals, and activity zones spread across a rural site. It is
built for children and mixed-age groups rather than thrill-ride purists.

For visitors using Dartmouth as a South Devon base, it is a practical rainy-day or high-
energy alternative to boats, beaches, and historic houses. Check seasonal opening, since
the experience changes with weather and school holidays.
