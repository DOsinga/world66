Rougemont Castle preserves Norman remains on the edge of central Exeter. The grounds are open to the public, while the central castle buildings have been reused for events and holiday lets. It is a short, useful stop near the city walls and the Royal Albert Memorial Museum, especially for visitors following Exeter's medieval history.
