Saltram House is Plymouth's major country-house escape, a National Trust property with Georgian rooms, portraits, landscaped parkland, and gardens close enough to the city to feel almost suburban. The house shows the polished side of Devon's landed history, far removed from Plymouth's naval docks and post-war centre.

The parkland makes the visit flexible. You can tour the interiors if they are open, walk the estate paths, or use Saltram as a green half-day with children. It is particularly good for travellers who want a historic house without committing to a long drive deep into the countryside.
