Plymouth's sights cluster around two areas: [the Barbican](/europe/unitedkingdom/england/bristol_plymouth_and_the_south_west/devon/plymouth/the_barbican), where the Elizabethan waterfront has survived intact, and [Plymouth Hoe](/europe/unitedkingdom/england/bristol_plymouth_and_the_south_west/devon/plymouth/plymouth_hoe), the grassy headland above it. The rebuilt city centre has less to offer, but Drake Circus holds the city museum. Allow a full day to cover the waterfront properly.

The [City Museum and Art Gallery](/europe/unitedkingdom/england/bristol_plymouth_and_the_south_west/devon/plymouth/city_museum_and_art_gallery) on Drake Circus covers Plymouth's history from its Tudor heyday through the Blitz and beyond. [Prysten House](/europe/unitedkingdom/england/bristol_plymouth_and_the_south_west/devon/plymouth/prysten_house) next to St Andrew's Church is Plymouth's oldest domestic building, a 15th-century merchant's courtyard rarely visited but worth seeking out.
