title: Bars and Cafes
type: section
---

Brendon's only pub is the Staghunters Inn, which doubles as a hotel. Oare has no facilities at all. For more options, you need to drive to [Lynton and Lynmouth](/europe/unitedkingdom/england/bristol_plymouth_and_the_south_west/exmoor/lynton_and_lynmouth), about four miles away.
