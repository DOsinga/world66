---
title: When to Go
type: section
---

Brendon and Oare are accessible and pleasant from late spring through early autumn. May and June are the best months: the valley is green and flowered, the rivers are running well, and the lanes are not yet clogged with summer holiday traffic. July and August bring more visitors; the roads and the Staghunters Inn fill up.

Autumn is underrated — the oak woods along the East Lyn turn gold in October, and the moorland above has a different character when the bracken dies back and the views open up. Avoid the lanes in high summer if you can: the single-track roads between Brendon and Oare are genuinely difficult to navigate when streams of holiday cars are also trying to use them.
