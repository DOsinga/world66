Combe Martin sits on Exmoor's far western coastal fringe, tucked into one of Devon's longest villages stretching along a narrow valley down to the sea. The coastal scenery is excellent, with dramatic cliffs and a sheltered sandy beach that makes for good swimming at low tide.

The village is best known for the [Pack o' Cards](/europe/unitedkingdom/england/bristol_plymouth_and_the_south_west/exmoor/combe_martin/pack_o_cards) inn, built at the beginning of the 18th century with winnings from gambling. It has 52 windows across four floors and 13 doors — the builder clearly had a theme in mind. Beyond the pub quiz trivia, Combe Martin makes a good base for walking the South West Coast Path, with the spectacular headland of the [Great Hangman](/europe/unitedkingdom/england/bristol_plymouth_and_the_south_west/exmoor/combe_martin/great_hangman) nearby.
