---
title: "Getting There"
type: section
---

Dulverton has no railway station — the nearest is Tiverton Parkway, around 12 miles south on the main Bristol-to-Exeter line, with hourly trains from London Paddington (around two hours fifteen minutes). From Tiverton Parkway, a pre-booked taxi reaches Dulverton in about 25 minutes; bus services exist but are sparse.

By road, Dulverton sits on the A396 from Tiverton (M5 junction 27) to Minehead. Most visitors arrive by car. The town has a small free car park and on-street parking is limited and easily filled in summer.
