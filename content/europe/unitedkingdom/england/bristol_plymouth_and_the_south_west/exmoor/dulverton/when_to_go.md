---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

Exmoor has a temperate but damp climate — be prepared for rain in any month. May and September are arguably the best times to visit: long days, dry-ish weather, and far fewer visitors than peak summer. The bluebells in the woods around Tarr Steps are spectacular in late April and early May. Heather flowers across the high moor in August and into early September, turning the slopes purple.

Autumn (October) is the rut, when red deer stags can be heard roaring across the moor at dusk — a memorable experience if you walk quietly out of the village in the evening. Winter is quiet, often beautiful, but several pubs and tea rooms close or run reduced hours.
