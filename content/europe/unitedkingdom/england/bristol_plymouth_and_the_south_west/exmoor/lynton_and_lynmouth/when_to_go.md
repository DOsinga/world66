---
title: When to Go
type: section
---

Lynton and Lynmouth are a year-round destination, but the best conditions are from late spring through early autumn. July and August are peak season — the cliff railway is busiest, the harbour fills with day-trippers, and parking in both villages becomes a genuine problem. Come in May, June, or September and you'll have the coast paths largely to yourself.

Winter brings raw Atlantic weather but also a particular bleakness that suits the moorland very well. The Valley of Rocks in low cloud is properly dramatic. Many of the cafes and some accommodation close between November and Easter, so check ahead before visiting out of season.

The Lynmouth flood happened on the night of 15–16 August 1952, following an extreme rainfall event over Exmoor. If you visit in mid-August it's worth pausing at the flood memorial.
