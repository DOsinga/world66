---
title: "Eating Out"
type: section
---

For a small village, Porlock and its surrounds eat well. The two pubs at Porlock Weir — the **Ship Inn** and the **Café**/**Bottom Ship** — are the obvious lunch stops, both serving local crab, mackerel, and Exmoor lamb in summer; the Ship in particular has a lovely terrace overlooking the harbour. In Porlock itself, the **Ship Inn** (a separate establishment dating to the thirteenth century, sometimes called the Top Ship) is the village's main pub, with reasonable bar food and Exmoor ale.

For something more substantial, **The Café at The Big Cheese** in the centre of Porlock makes excellent ploughman's lunches with local cheeses, and **Miller's at the Anchor** in Porlock Weir does ambitious seafood-focused evening menus. Cream teas are everywhere in the summer; the tea garden at Selworthy is the prettiest setting if you happen to be passing.
