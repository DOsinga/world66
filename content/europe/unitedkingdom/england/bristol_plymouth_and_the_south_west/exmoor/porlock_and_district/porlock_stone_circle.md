A ring of small standing stones on the open moorland of Porlock Common, the Porlock Stone Circle is one of the lesser-known prehistoric monuments of Exmoor. Of the original twenty or so stones, only ten or so still stand visibly above the grass; the rest have toppled or been buried by peat.

The circle is reached on foot across the open moor from a lay-by on the Porlock-to-Exford road. It is small (about 24 metres in diameter), atmospheric, and almost always deserted — and combines well with a walk to nearby Whit Stones and Alderman's Barrow.
