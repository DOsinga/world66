---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

Porlock is at its best in **late spring (May)** and **early autumn (September–October)**. May brings bluebells in Hawkcombe Wood and Horner Wood, the cliffside above Culbone is thick with wildflowers, and the long days mean coastal walks can run on until 9pm. Autumn brings turning oak leaves in the combes and the heather flowering on the open moor.

Summer (July–August) is the busiest season, when the South West Coast Path and the Porlock Weir pubs both fill up. Winter is quiet, often wet, and many of the smaller pubs and cafés cut back their hours — but a sharp clear winter day on Dunkery Beacon, with snow on the high moor and a view across the Bristol Channel, is one of the great Exmoor experiences.
