Winsford is widely considered the prettiest village on [Exmoor](/europe/unitedkingdom/england/bristol_plymouth_and_the_south_west/exmoor), set in a hollow where the River Exe meets the Winn Brook. Thatched cottages cluster around a ford, the [Royal Oak Inn](/europe/unitedkingdom/england/bristol_plymouth_and_the_south_west/exmoor/winsford/royal_oak) sits at its heart, and the village feels remarkably unvisited despite being within easy reach of the moor's main roads.

Two well-known Exmoor attractions are close by: [Dunkery Beacon](/europe/unitedkingdom/england/bristol_plymouth_and_the_south_west/exmoor/winsford/dunkery_beacon), the highest point on the moor at 519 metres, with wide views on clear days toward Wales and the Bristol Channel; and [Tarr Steps](/europe/unitedkingdom/england/bristol_plymouth_and_the_south_west/exmoor/winsford/tarr_steps), a medieval clapper bridge of flat stones spanning the River Barle, about four miles to the southwest. The open ridge of [Winsford Hill](/europe/unitedkingdom/england/bristol_plymouth_and_the_south_west/exmoor/winsford/winsford_hill) rises immediately above the village, with the curious [Caratacus Stone](/europe/unitedkingdom/england/bristol_plymouth_and_the_south_west/exmoor/winsford/caratacus_stone) on its flank.
