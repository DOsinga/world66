---
title: 'Getting There'
type: section
---

Glastonbury lost its railway station in 1966 and has not had one since, which makes getting there slower than you would expect for somewhere so famous.

**By train.** The nearest station is Castle Cary, about 12 miles east on the line between London Paddington and Penzance. From there, a taxi to Glastonbury runs about £20-25. Bath Spa station, with frequent trains from London, Bristol, and the south coast, is connected to Glastonbury by Mendip Xplorer and First Bus services taking about 90 minutes.

**By bus.** First Bus 376 runs from Bristol bus station through Wells to Glastonbury in around 90 minutes, every 15-30 minutes during the day. The 29 connects Glastonbury with Taunton via Street. The bus station is just off Magdalene Street, a few minutes' walk from the Abbey.

**By car.** Glastonbury is about 130 miles west of London, 30 miles south of Bristol, and 30 miles south-west of Bath. Pay-and-display car parks are scattered around the town centre; the Abbey, Tor, and Chalice Well each have their own. The Tor itself can only be reached on foot from one of two parking-and-walking points.

**Festival week.** All conventional transport groans under the strain. Many festival-goers arrive by coach direct to the Worthy Farm site; if you are visiting the town itself, expect heavy traffic on roads in and out.
