---
title: 'Things to Do'
type: section
---

Glastonbury packs an unusual concentration of myth, ruin, and ritual into a town of about 9,000 people. The Tor and the Abbey are the obvious draws; the Chalice Well, the White Spring, and the lesser-known thorn trees give the place its other-worldly quality. A weekend is more than enough, an afternoon will do for the highlights.
