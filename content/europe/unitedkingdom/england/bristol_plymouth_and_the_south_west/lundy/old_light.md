Old Light is the most satisfying building on Lundy, partly because it is beautiful and partly because it is a lesson in how sensible plans fail at sea. It stands high in the middle of the island, where the views are tremendous on a clear day.

The climb is steep and narrow, but worth it if it is open. From the top, Lundy becomes simple: a long back of rock, green fields, hard edges, and the Atlantic pressing in from the west.
