---
title: Beaches
type: section
---

Minehead's beach is a long, gently shelving stretch of fine sand and shingle that runs east from the harbour for about three miles to Blue Anchor. At low tide it is enormous; at high tide the sea covers most of it and laps the seawall. The water quality has been consistently rated good in recent years and there is a designated swimming zone with seasonal lifeguards in front of the resort.

The walk east along the promenade to **Blue Anchor Bay** is flat, paved, and good for families. For something wilder, head west on the South West Coast Path: within ten minutes you are above the town on North Hill, with the small pebble cove of **Greenaleigh Beach** down a steep path below.
