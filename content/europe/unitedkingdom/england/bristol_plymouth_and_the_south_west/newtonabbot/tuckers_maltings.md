Tucker's Maltings is the landmark industrial building most associated with Newton Abbot's brewing heritage. The long brick malthouse, kiln, and working history make it a rare survival from the days when malt production was a visible part of town life.

The site has changed uses over time, so check current public access before planning a visit around it. Even from the outside, though, it is one of Newton Abbot's more distinctive historic buildings and a useful stop for travellers interested in beer, industry, or the practical trades behind Devon market towns.
