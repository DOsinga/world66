A reliable independent restaurant in the city centre, popular with locals for lunch and early dinner. The menu runs to salads, sandwiches, and more substantial hot dishes at lunch — the soups are particularly good. Straightforward cooking from decent local ingredients, without the tourist pricing that affects some of the closer-in places. Busy on market days; worth booking for dinner at weekends.
