A small, atmospheric pub on Minster Street with a credible claim to being one of the oldest in Salisbury — parts of the building date to the fifteenth century. The interior is genuinely old rather than artificially so: low beams, uneven floors, and a series of tiny interconnected rooms that make it feel more like a medieval house than a pub. Serves real ales including local Wiltshire beers, and pub food at lunch. Gets busy on market days and summer weekends.
