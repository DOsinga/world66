The Stonehenge Cursus is a vast Neolithic earthwork north of the stone circle, easy to overlook because it is not as visually obvious as the famous stones. Archaeologically, though, it is central to the wider ritual landscape around Stonehenge.

You need imagination here: low banks and long lines in the grass rather than a dramatic monument. That quietness is part of the appeal, especially if you want to understand Stonehenge as a landscape of linked sites rather than a single fenced icon.
