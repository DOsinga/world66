The Avenue is the ancient ceremonial approach to Stonehenge, linking the stone circle with the River Avon. Its banks and ditches are still visible in places, giving a clearer sense of how the monument sat within a wider ritual landscape.

Visitors can follow parts of the route toward King Barrows Ridge. It is most rewarding for people who want to understand Stonehenge beyond the stones themselves.
