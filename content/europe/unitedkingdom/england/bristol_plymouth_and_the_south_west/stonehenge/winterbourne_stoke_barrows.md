The Winterbourne Stoke Barrows are part of the wider prehistoric landscape around Stonehenge, a group of burial mounds that help explain why this stretch of Wiltshire mattered long before modern roads and visitor centres. They are not as instantly legible as Stonehenge itself, but they make the landscape feel older and larger.

Come with a map and realistic expectations: these are earthworks, not reconstructed monuments. Their reward is context, especially for travellers interested in the ceremonial and burial landscape surrounding the famous stones.
