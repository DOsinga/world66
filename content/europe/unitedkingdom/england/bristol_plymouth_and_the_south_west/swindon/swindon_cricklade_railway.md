The Swindon & Cricklade Railway keeps a short stretch of the old Midland and South Western Junction Railway alive north of town. Its main base is Blunsdon Station, where volunteers run heritage trains, maintain rolling stock, and recreate the feel of a small country railway rather than a grand main-line museum.

It is best treated as a half-day outing, especially if steam or diesel services are running. Check the timetable before going, because the charm depends on catching an operating day rather than simply looking at closed platforms and sidings. For railway-minded travellers, it pairs naturally with Swindon's larger STEAM Museum.
