The Beehive is a reliable pub in Swindon's Old Town — the original settlement that predates the railway town by centuries. It stocks a good range of real ales and has a beer garden that gets busy on summer evenings. The Old Town area around Cricklade Street and Wood Street has the best concentration of independent pubs in Swindon, and the Beehive is a good anchor for a walk between them.
