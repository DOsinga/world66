Hestercombe House and Gardens is one of the strongest garden visits near Taunton, with formal, landscape and Victorian garden layers around a historic house. The restored Lutyens and Jekyll garden is the highlight for many visitors, combining architecture, planting and long views.

The estate needs a few hours if you want to walk the grounds properly rather than just tour the house. It is outside Taunton, so plan transport and treat it as a half-day excursion.
