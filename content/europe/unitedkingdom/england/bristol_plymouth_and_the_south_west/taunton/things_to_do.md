---
title: "Things to do in Taunton"
type: section
---

Taunton's compact centre can be walked in a morning. The castle and museum are the main attractions, with the Minster church and a clutch of timbered streets close by. The town's real value, though, is as a base for the surrounding country — the Quantocks, the Blackdowns, the Levels, and Exmoor are all within easy reach.
