The Tudor Tavern at No 15 Fore Street is a Grade I listed timber-framed merchant's house built in the late fifteenth century — the finest surviving medieval domestic building in Taunton. The two upper storeys jetty out over the pavement in classic fashion, the close-studded timbers carved with quatrefoils and grotesque heads.

Saved from demolition by a sympathetic restoration in the 1980s, it now houses a restaurant on the ground floor — you can sit inside and look up at the original blackened beams of the medieval roof. The exterior alone is worth a stop; the elaborate timber detail does not photograph well in flat light but rewards a careful look.
