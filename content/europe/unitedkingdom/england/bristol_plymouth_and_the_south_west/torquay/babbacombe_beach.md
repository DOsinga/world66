The next cove east of Oddicombe, reached by a single-track road that drops down through the woods from Babbacombe Downs. Smaller and quieter than its neighbour, with a row of small fishing huts, a couple of cafes, and a few moored boats. The road is narrow enough that drivers are warned to use the upper car park and walk down.

Good for a quiet swim and a fish lunch at the Cary Arms below the cliff.
