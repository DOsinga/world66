A short Edwardian funicular that drops roughly 240 feet down the red cliff from the top of Babbacombe Downs to Oddicombe Beach. The ride takes about a minute and saves you a long scramble down the path. The views over Lyme Bay from the upper station are some of the best in town — you can see all the way to Portland Bill on a clear day.

Cliff Road, Babbacombe. Runs from spring to autumn; check seasonal hours.
