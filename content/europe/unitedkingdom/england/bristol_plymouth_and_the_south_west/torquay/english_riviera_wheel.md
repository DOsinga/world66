The English Riviera Wheel is a large observation wheel on Torquay's seafront. The enclosed gondolas give a quick, easy view over the harbour, marina, and sweep of Tor Bay.

It is a simple family attraction rather than a deep cultural stop, but the view makes it a useful add-on while walking the waterfront.
