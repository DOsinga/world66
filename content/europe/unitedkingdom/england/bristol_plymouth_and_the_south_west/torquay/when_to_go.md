---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

Torquay sits in the warmest pocket of mainland Britain, with average summer maxima around 21°C and very mild winters thanks to the Gulf Stream. Palm trees and bananas grow happily on the seafront and proper frost is rare. **July and August** are the busiest months — hotels fill, beaches get crowded, and prices are highest. The town is at its prettiest in **late May and June**, when the weather is reliably good but the school-holiday rush has not started.

**September** is the quiet sweet spot: the sea is at its warmest, the cafes are still open, and the seafront empties out. **Winter** is mild but most attractions close — fine for a coastal walk and a pub lunch, less so for a beach holiday. The annual **Torbay Royal Regatta** in late August is the biggest event, with fireworks over the bay and a Red Arrows display.
