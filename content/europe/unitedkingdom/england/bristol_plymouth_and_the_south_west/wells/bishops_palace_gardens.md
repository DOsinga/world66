The Bishop's Palace Gardens are one of Wells' loveliest places to linger, wrapped around the medieval palace and its moat just off the market place. The setting feels almost theatrical: gatehouse, water, lawns, old walls, and the cathedral close only a short walk away.

The gardens are more than a pretty frame for the palace. They give Wells its quiet, enclosed side, with enough paths and corners to make the visit feel unhurried. Go after seeing the cathedral, when the contrast between public grandeur and sheltered greenery is at its best.
