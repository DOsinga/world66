Radipole Lake is the rare nature reserve that begins almost in the middle of town. Reeds, lagoons, boardwalks, and hides sit just behind Weymouth's streets, creating a quick shift from seaside bustle to wetland quiet. Birdwatchers may see warblers, ducks, gulls, kingfishers, and seasonal migrants, while non-specialists still get an easy green walk.

It is a useful counterweight to Weymouth's beach and harbor. Drop in for an hour, or linger longer if the birdlife is active and the weather is kind. The reserve is flat and accessible by local standards, making it one of the easiest wildlife stops on this part of the Dorset coast.
