Tudor House Museum is a small historic-house visit that suits Weymouth's old harbour setting. The building preserves domestic details from the town's early-modern past, when Weymouth was a working port rather than a seaside resort.

It is not a long visit, but it adds texture to a walk through the harbour streets. Pair it with the quayside and nearby lanes for a compact look at Weymouth before beach time takes over.
