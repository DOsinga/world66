The Merry Maidens is one of Cornwall's best-preserved Bronze Age stone circles, nineteen granite stones standing in a near-perfect circle in a field near St Buryan, on the Penwith peninsula beyond Penzance. Unlike many prehistoric sites that need real archaeological imagination to appreciate, this one is legible at a glance — a complete, deliberate ring, roughly 24 metres across, that has stood here for around 4,000 years.

It sits free and unfenced right by the road, an easy stop on the way toward Land's End, and pairs naturally with the wider concentration of prehistoric remains on Penwith — standing stones, burial chambers, and Iron Age villages scattered across the same stretch of moor and farmland.
