type: section
---

[Kenilworth Castle](/europe/unitedkingdom/england/east_midlands/kenilworth/kenilworth_castle) is the reason to come — one of England's largest castle ruins, with red sandstone walls stretching across a hilltop and a reconstructed Elizabethan garden that commemorates the extraordinary 1575 royal visit where Robert Dudley spent a fortune entertaining Queen Elizabeth I. A young William Shakespeare almost certainly witnessed the spectacle from nearby Stratford. [Abbey Fields](/europe/unitedkingdom/england/east_midlands/kenilworth/abbey_fields) in the town centre holds the remains of a twelfth-century Augustinian priory in pleasant open parkland, with a 1930s lido still operating in summer.
