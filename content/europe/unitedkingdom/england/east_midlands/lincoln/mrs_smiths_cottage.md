Mrs Smith's Cottage is a preserved mid-19th-century redbrick home in Bracebridge Heath. It tells the story of Hilda Smith, who lived here until 1995 and kept much of the house in old-fashioned working order, giving visitors a compact look at everyday village life near Lincoln.
