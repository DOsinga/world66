The Museum of Lincolnshire Life fills a former Victorian barracks with exhibits on the county's agricultural, industrial, domestic, and military history. The collection is broad, from farm machinery and trades to household objects and local regimental material.

It is close to Lincoln Castle and the Cathedral Quarter, so it works well as a quieter addition to the main uphill sights.
