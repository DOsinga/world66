Melton Mowbray is a Leicestershire market town with one claim that is not trying to be fashionable: this is pork pie and Stilton country. The town calls itself the Rural Capital of Food, and for once the label has some weight. Its best day out is a mix of food shops, a handsome parish church, a small museum that takes local trades seriously, and countryside close enough to reach without much planning.

Start in the compact centre, where [Ye Olde Pork Pie Shoppe](/europe/unitedkingdom/england/east_midlands/melton_mowbray/ye_olde_pork_pie_shoppe) sells the hand-raised pies that made the town famous. [St Mary's Church](/europe/unitedkingdom/england/east_midlands/melton_mowbray/st_marys_church) gives Melton its best building: a large medieval church with a tower whose earliest stonework reaches back to the Norman period. A few streets away, [Melton Carnegie Museum](/europe/unitedkingdom/england/east_midlands/melton_mowbray/melton_carnegie_museum) ties the food story to fox hunting, farming, markets, and the old Great North Road.

The town is not as rich in sights as [Leicester](/europe/unitedkingdom/england/east_midlands/leicester) or Lincoln, but it has a strong flavour of its own. [Burrough Hill](/europe/unitedkingdom/england/east_midlands/melton_mowbray/burrough_hill) is the best nearby open-air stop, with Iron Age earthworks and big views across rolling Leicestershire. If you are nearby with a spare day and an appetite, Melton is worth the detour.
