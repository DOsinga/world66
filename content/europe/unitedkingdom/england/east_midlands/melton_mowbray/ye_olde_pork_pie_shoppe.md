Ye Olde Pork Pie Shoppe is the obvious first stop in Melton Mowbray. Dickinson & Morris has been tied to the town's pork pie trade since the nineteenth century, and the shop on Nottingham Street is still where visitors come to buy the dense, hand-raised pies that made Melton famous.

This is more shop than museum, but that is part of the charm. Come hungry, buy a pie, and treat it as the town's edible monument.
