The Haunted Museum is a small Nottingham museum devoted to ghost stories, witchcraft, haunted objects, and horror pop culture. It is a niche stop rather than a broad city highlight, but it gives the Lace Market area a different kind of indoor attraction.

It is close to the National Justice Museum, City of Caves, and Nottingham Contemporary, so it is easiest to visit as part of a wider walk through the historic centre.
