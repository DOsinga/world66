Chatsworth House is the Peak District's grand stately home, a vast 17th-century seat of the Dukes of Devonshire set in parkland beside the River Derwent. The house, art collection, formal gardens, fountains, and wooded walks make it one of the strongest reasons to linger on the eastern side of the national park.
