Eyam Museum tells the story of the Peak District village that isolated itself during the 1665-66 plague outbreak. Displays explain the epidemic, the village quarantine, and the people whose decisions made Eyam a lasting part of English plague history.

The museum is a good starting point before walking through the village and out to the related plague sites in the surrounding fields.
