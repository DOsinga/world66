Castleton does most of the work here, and it earns it. Climb Mam Tor and you get the Hope Valley on one side and Edale on the other, with the crumbling old road below as a reminder that this hill has been quietly falling apart for decades. Winnats Pass delivers the same limestone drama at road level — a narrow gorge with crags pressing in from both sides — while the short, steep pull up to Peveril Castle's ruined keep trades altitude for a neat panorama over Cave Dale and the cave-riddled hills beyond.

Kinder Scout is the odd one out geographically, up on the Dark Peak's high moorland rather than around Castleton, but it belongs on this list twice over: for the views across the plateau and Kinder Downfall, and because the 1932 mass trespass that happened up here helped write the right to roam into English law. Between the two halves of the park, these five spots cover the White Peak's limestone drama and the Dark Peak's open moor without much overlap.
