Bateman's Brewery Visitors Centre is at Wainfleet, a short trip from Skegness, and gives the resort area a stronger inland stop than another seafront amusement. The brewery has long local roots, and tours connect the working site with Lincolnshire pub culture.

It is best for travellers with an interest in beer, industrial heritage, or a wet-weather alternative to the beach. Opening and tour times matter, so plan around the brewery schedule rather than treating it as a guaranteed drop-in attraction.
