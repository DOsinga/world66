**By train**, Worksop is on the Retford to Sheffield line, with regular services connecting to Sheffield (around 35 minutes) and Nottingham (around 55 minutes via Retford). East Midlands Railway operates most services. The station is a short walk from the town centre.

**By car**, Worksop sits just off the A57 between Sheffield and Lincoln. The M1 motorway is about 12 miles to the west, making it straightforward to reach from the wider motorway network. Clumber Park is around 6 miles southeast of the town centre via the A614.

**By bus**, connections to Nottingham, Sheffield, and surrounding villages run from the bus station in the town centre, though services are infrequent for some destinations. For Clumber Park, a car or bicycle is the most practical option — the main entrance off the A614 has a large car park, and National Trust members enter free.
