title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Aldeburgh is best seen as a walk: the beach and fishing huts, [Scallop](/europe/unitedkingdom/england/suffolk/aldeburgh/scallop) to the north, the [Moot Hall](/europe/unitedkingdom/england/suffolk/aldeburgh/moot_hall) in the old centre, and the [Martello Tower](/europe/unitedkingdom/england/suffolk/aldeburgh/martello_tower) on the shingle spit to the south. Add [The Red House](/europe/unitedkingdom/england/suffolk/aldeburgh/the_red_house) if you care about Britten, Pears, or 20th-century music.
