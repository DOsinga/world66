Bury St Edmunds Guildhall is a rare survivor of medieval civic life, later adapted and reused for centuries. Its long history is the attraction: this was not built as a tidy heritage site, but as a working building for a town that mattered.

The Guildhall is also linked to the Second World War, when rooms were used by the Royal Observer Corps. Check opening times, because access can be more limited than at the abbey or cathedral, but include it if you want the town's civic story as well as its religious one.
