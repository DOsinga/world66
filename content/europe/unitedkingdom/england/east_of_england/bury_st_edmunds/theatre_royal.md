The Theatre Royal is a rare surviving Regency playhouse and one of Bury St Edmunds' most distinctive cultural stops. The National Trust has restored the building, and guided tours let you see how a compact early-19th-century theatre worked before modern auditoriums changed the shape of performance.

It is still a working theatre, so check the programme if you want to pair the architecture with a show.
