St Andrew's in Chesterton is a parish church north of central Cambridge, worth seeking out for its medieval Doom painting around the chancel arch. The faded scene of the Last Judgement gives a rare glimpse of the vivid religious art that once filled many English churches.

It is a quiet stop rather than a major museum-style attraction, but the painting makes it more than a routine church visit.
