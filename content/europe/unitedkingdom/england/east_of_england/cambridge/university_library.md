Cambridge University Library is mainly a working research library, but its public exhibitions make it useful for visitors. The displays draw on manuscripts, rare books, maps, and other material from one of the world's great academic collections.

Exhibitions change through the year, so check the current programme before walking over from the college area. It pairs well with the nearby Backs and the university museums west of the centre.
