Beth Chatto's Gardens are a serious pilgrimage stop for gardeners, especially anyone interested in matching plants to difficult conditions. The famous gravel garden was developed without routine irrigation, turning poor, dry soil into a lesson in texture, drought tolerance, and restraint.

The gardens sit outside Colchester and are best visited deliberately rather than squeezed into a city walk. They are at their best for travellers who notice planting, design, and the quiet intelligence of a garden that looks natural because it is carefully edited.
