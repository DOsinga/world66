Dereham is a central Norfolk market town, less polished than the coast and less obvious than [Norwich](/europe/unitedkingdom/england/east_of_england/norwich). Its appeal is practical and historical: railways, farm life, a windmill, and a small museum in one of the town's oldest buildings.

The [Mid-Norfolk Railway](/europe/unitedkingdom/england/east_of_england/dereham/mid_norfolk_railway) is the strongest draw, running heritage trains south toward Wymondham on selected days. Just outside town, [Gressenhall Farm and Workhouse](/europe/unitedkingdom/england/east_of_england/dereham/gressenhall_farm_and_workhouse) gives a much fuller look at rural Norfolk, with workhouse buildings, farm displays, and open grounds.

In the town itself, [Bishop Bonner's Cottage Museum](/europe/unitedkingdom/england/east_of_england/dereham/bishop_bonners_cottage_museum) is a small local-history stop in a thatched building, and [Dereham Windmill](/europe/unitedkingdom/england/east_of_england/dereham/dereham_windmill) adds another easy landmark. Dereham is not a grand destination, but it rewards a spare day if you are already exploring inland Norfolk.
