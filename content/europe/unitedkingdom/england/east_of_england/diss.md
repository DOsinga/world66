Diss is a South Norfolk market town close to the Suffolk border, with its old centre gathered around [Diss Mere](/europe/unitedkingdom/england/norfolk/diss/diss_mere). The lake is the thing that makes the town itself memorable: a deep, still pool beside the shops, with ducks, benches, and a sense that the town grew around water before it grew around roads.

The town works best as a quiet base for the Waveney Valley and the Norfolk-Suffolk border country. The [Boudicca Way](/europe/unitedkingdom/england/norfolk/diss/boudicca_way) starts or ends here, and rural lanes make the area good for cycling if you are happy with maps and slow roads. Diss is also on the London-Norwich rail line, which makes it easier to reach than many East Anglian market towns.

Most of the stronger sights sit just outside town rather than on the high street. [Bressingham Steam and Gardens](/europe/unitedkingdom/england/norfolk/diss/bressingham_steam_and_gardens) is the family-friendly one; the [100th Bomb Group Memorial Museum](/europe/unitedkingdom/england/norfolk/diss/100th_bomb_group_memorial_museum) gives the surrounding countryside a sharp Second World War story. Diss is not a place to rush across England for, but if you are nearby it has enough texture for a good day.
