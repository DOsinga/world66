Bressingham mixes steam railways, traction engines, gardens, and a little old-fashioned family entertainment just west of Diss. It is strongest if you like working heritage machinery, but the gardens and short train rides make it an easy multi-generation outing.

Opening days vary by season, so check before building a day around it. If the trains are running, give it more time than you think.
