title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Framlingham is compact. The castle is the main event, and the best visit is a simple loop: wall walk, castle grounds, church, Market Hill, and the paths around the mere.

If you have a car or bicycle, add [Saxtead Green Post Mill](/europe/unitedkingdom/england/suffolk/framlingham/saxtead_green_post_mill), a working-style post mill in open countryside. It rounds out the town with the rural Suffolk that Framlingham still belongs to.
