Ipswich Art School is an exhibition space for local and regional art. The gallery is useful if you want to see contemporary work from Suffolk artists, especially alongside nearby stops such as Ipswich Museum and Christchurch Mansion.

Programming changes, so it is worth checking what is on before making a special trip.
