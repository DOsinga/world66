Ipswich Blackfriars is a modest but evocative survival from the town's medieval religious life. The Dominican friars arrived in Ipswich in the thirteenth century, and the surviving fragments now sit folded into streets and later buildings rather than presented as a grand set-piece ruin.

Come for the sense of layers rather than spectacle. It is the kind of stop that makes Ipswich's age easier to read: a few walls, names, and alignments hinting at a much larger friary that once stood here. Pair it with St Peter's, Cardinal Wolsey's Gate, and the waterfront for a short walk through the older town.
