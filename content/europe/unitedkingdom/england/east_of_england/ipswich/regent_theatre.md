The Regent Theatre is Ipswich’s main large-scale performance venue, a restored cinema-era house that now hosts touring musicals, comedy, concerts, dance, and community shows. It gives the town an evening option beyond pubs and restaurants.

Its value is schedule-dependent, so look at listings before building a night around it. Architecturally and socially, though, it is a useful reminder that Ipswich has a civic entertainment life larger than its medieval lanes and waterfront.
