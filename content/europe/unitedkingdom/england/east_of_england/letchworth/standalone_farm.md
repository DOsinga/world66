Standalone Farm is a working show farm on the edge of Letchworth Garden City, set in Hertfordshire countryside. Children can meet farm animals such as lambs, calves, ducks, pigs, goats, cows, and horses.

It is aimed mainly at families, with indoor and outdoor play areas, picnic space, and farm displays that make it a useful wet-weather or half-day stop.
