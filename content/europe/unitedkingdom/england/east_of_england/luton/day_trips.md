---
title: "Day Trips"
type: section
---

**St Albans** — 20 minutes by train, St Albans has Roman ruins (Verulamium), a Norman cathedral, and one of the best-preserved medieval city centres in southeast England. An easy and rewarding half-day from Luton.

**Woburn Abbey and Safari Park** — about 20 minutes by car, Woburn Abbey is a stately home with fine art collections, and the adjacent safari park is one of the better ones in England. Good for families.

**Bedford** — 30 minutes by train, Bedford has a pleasant riverside, good museums, and strong connections to John Bunyan (author of *The Pilgrim's Progress*), who was born nearby. The town is more attractive than Luton and worth an afternoon.

**London** — 35–40 minutes by Thameslink from Luton Airport Parkway to St Pancras. The obvious day trip, and one of the fastest connections from any English regional town to central London.
