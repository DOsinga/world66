---
title: "Getting There"
type: section
---

Luton Airport Parkway station connects to London St Pancras International in around 22 minutes on Thameslink. Direct trains also run to Brighton, Bedford, and other southeast cities, making it a convenient stopping point on several rail corridors. The A1(M) and M1 motorways pass nearby, giving good road access from the north and south. From the station, a dedicated shuttle bus runs to the airport terminal — allow around 10 minutes for the connection.
