Catton Park is a gentle green stop north of Norwich, with open grass, mature trees, and traces of designed landscape history. It is not as essential as the cathedral or castle, but it gives the city’s northern side a pleasant, low-key walking option.

Use it when you want fresh air without leaving Norwich for the Broads or the wider Norfolk countryside. It suits a slow stroll, a picnic, or a pause between more formal sights.
