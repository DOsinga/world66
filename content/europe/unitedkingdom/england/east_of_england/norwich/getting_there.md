---
title: "Getting There"
type: section
---

Norwich is well connected by train from London Liverpool Street — the journey takes around 1 hour 50 minutes. Trains run roughly every 30 minutes during the day. Greater Anglia operates the route.

By road, the A11 connects Norwich to the M11 motorway and London. The city is about 190 km from central London.

Norwich International Airport handles flights to a handful of European destinations and some domestic routes, but most visitors arrive by train.
