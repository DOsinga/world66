Peterborough is easy to underestimate. From the train it can look like logistics sheds, ring roads, and post-war growth, but the city has one of England's finest cathedrals at its core and a much older story stretching into the fenland east of town.

Start with [Peterborough Cathedral](/europe/unitedkingdom/england/east_of_england/peterborough/peterborough_cathedral), whose great west front is spectacular even by English cathedral standards. Around it are the old precinct, [Peterborough Guildhall](/europe/unitedkingdom/england/east_of_england/peterborough/peterborough_guildhall), [St John the Baptist Church](/europe/unitedkingdom/england/east_of_england/peterborough/st_john_the_baptist_church), and [Peterborough Museum and Art Gallery](/europe/unitedkingdom/england/east_of_england/peterborough/peterborough_museum_and_art_gallery).

The real surprise is how quickly the city opens into water, rail heritage, and archaeology. [Railworld Wildlife Haven](/europe/unitedkingdom/england/east_of_england/peterborough/railworld_wildlife_haven) and the [Nene Valley Railway](/europe/unitedkingdom/england/east_of_england/peterborough/nene_valley_railway) sit close to the centre, while [Flag Fen](/europe/unitedkingdom/england/east_of_england/peterborough/flag_fen) preserves a Bronze Age timber causeway in the low, wet landscape that shaped the region. Peterborough is not pretty all the way through, but it is much more rewarding than its reputation.
