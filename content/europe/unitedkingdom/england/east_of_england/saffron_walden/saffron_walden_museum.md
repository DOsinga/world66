Saffron Walden Museum is an old-fashioned local museum in the best sense: archaeology, natural history, domestic objects, fossils and town history gathered under one roof. It helps explain why this small place mattered, from saffron cultivation to market-town wealth.

It pairs well with a wander through the older streets around the castle ruins and market square. Do not expect blockbuster displays; come for context and local texture.
