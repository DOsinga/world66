Belfairs Woodland Centre is a visitor centre for the ancient woodlands around Leigh-on-Sea and Southend. It introduces the area's habitats and wildlife through family-friendly displays and activities.

Use it as a starting point for a woodland walk rather than as a standalone museum-style visit.
