St. Clements Church is the historic parish church of old Leigh, with parts dating to the 14th century. The north aisle is often noted for its boat-like form, a fitting detail in a fishing town.

It is a quiet heritage stop above Leigh-on-Sea, best combined with a walk through the old town, the waterfront, and the nearby estuary views.
