Stevenage is rarely anyone's picture-postcard Hertfordshire stop, and it is better to be honest about that. It was Britain's first post-war new town, planned for growth after 1946, and much of its centre still has the practical, concrete, pedestrianised confidence of that era.

Look a little closer and the town becomes more interesting. The [Six Hills](/europe/unitedkingdom/england/east_of_england/stevenage/six_hills) beside the old Great North Road are Roman burial mounds hiding in plain sight, while [St Nicholas' Church](/europe/unitedkingdom/england/east_of_england/stevenage/st_nicholas_church) and the Old Town keep the pre-new-town settlement visible. The [Stevenage Museum](/europe/unitedkingdom/england/east_of_england/stevenage/stevenage_museum) is useful for making sense of the abrupt leap from village to planned commuter town.

Stevenage works best as a short, curious stop between [Hitchin](/europe/unitedkingdom/england/east_of_england/hitchin), [Letchworth Garden City](/europe/unitedkingdom/england/east_of_england/letchworth), and London. Add [Fairlands Valley Park](/europe/unitedkingdom/england/east_of_england/stevenage/fairlands_valley_park) if you need open space, or the [Gordon Craig Theatre](/europe/unitedkingdom/england/east_of_england/stevenage/gordon_craig_theatre) if you are staying locally in the evening.
