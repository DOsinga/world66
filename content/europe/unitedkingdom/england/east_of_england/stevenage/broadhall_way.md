Broadhall Way is the home ground of Stevenage F.C., south of the town centre. It will not mean much to non-football travellers, but match days give Stevenage a local pulse that the shopping precinct cannot provide.

If you enjoy lower-league English football, check fixtures before visiting. The ground is compact, practical, and very much part of the town's modern identity rather than a heritage attraction.
