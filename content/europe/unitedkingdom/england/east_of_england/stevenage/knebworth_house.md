Knebworth House is a flamboyant Gothic country house on an estate that has belonged to the Lytton family for centuries. The house, gardens, deer park, church, and family-friendly grounds make it the standout excursion from Stevenage, with a side history of writers, film shoots, and huge open-air concerts.
