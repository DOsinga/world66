Shaw's Corner was the country home of George Bernard Shaw and his wife Charlotte from 1906. The house is kept much as they left it, with Shaw's clothes, writing spaces, and domestic details still in place.

The garden is half the pleasure. Look for the small writing hut where Shaw worked on plays and could rotate the structure to follow the sun.
