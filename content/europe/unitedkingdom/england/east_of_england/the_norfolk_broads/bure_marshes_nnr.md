Bure Marshes National Nature Reserve protects a broad slice of the wetland landscape that makes the Norfolk Broads special: reed beds, dykes, fen, wet woodland, and shallow broads formed from old peat workings. It is not a manicured park; much of the reserve is deliberately restricted to protect fragile habitats and birdlife.

For visitors, the practical reward is the boardwalk through the marshes and around Cockshoot Broad, where you can get into the landscape without damaging it. Come with binoculars and patience rather than a checklist. The reserve works best as a slow Broads stop, especially if you are already moving between Horning, Woodbastwick, and Ranworth.
