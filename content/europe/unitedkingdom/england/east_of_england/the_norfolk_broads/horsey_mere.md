Horsey Mere is one of the wilder-feeling corners of the Norfolk Broads, a broad sheet of water edged by reeds, grazing marsh, and big East Anglian sky. The restored Horsey Windpump gives the scene its landmark, but the pleasure is mostly in walking slowly and watching the light move across the water.

This is a good place for birdlife and for understanding the Broads away from the busier cruiser routes. Combine the mere with the paths toward Horsey Gap, where winter seal-watching on the coast is a very different but equally memorable Norfolk experience.
