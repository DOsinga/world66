Martham Broad is a quieter National Nature Reserve on the River Thurne, north of Great Yarmouth and close to West Somerton. Its two shallow broads, marshes, reed beds, and fenland make it one of the Broads' better wildlife stops, especially for visitors who want somewhere less busy than the marquee boat-hire villages.

The reserve is best treated as part of a slow nature day rather than a quick photo stop. Dragonflies, marsh birds, otters, and deer are the appeal, and the open, watery landscape changes sharply with weather and season. Paths and access can be wet or limited, so check current reserve guidance before setting out.
