The Norfolk Wherry Trust keeps alive one of the Broads' most distinctive sights: the black-sailed trading wherry. Its vessel, Albion, is a restored cargo boat from the late 19th century, the kind that once carried reed, grain, coal, and marsh produce through this flat watery landscape.

Seeing Albion under sail is far more evocative than reading another panel about the Broads. Trips and open days vary, so check ahead, but the trust is a strong choice if you want local boat history rather than just a hired cruiser for the afternoon.
