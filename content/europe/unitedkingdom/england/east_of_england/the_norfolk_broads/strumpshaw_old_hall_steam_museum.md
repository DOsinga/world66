Strumpshaw Old Hall Steam Museum has a large private collection of stationary and mobile steam engines. On running days, the engines are in steam, which makes the visit much more interesting than a static display.

Opening is limited and often seasonal, so check before travelling. It is a practical side trip for visitors exploring the eastern side of the Norfolk Broads.
