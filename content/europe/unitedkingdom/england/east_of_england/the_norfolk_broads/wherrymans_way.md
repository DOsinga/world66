Wherryman's Way is a 35-mile walking route following the River Yare between Norwich and Great Yarmouth. It passes through broadland villages, marshes, river views, and stretches of quiet countryside that show how the Broads were shaped by water transport.

The full route is a multi-day walk, but short sections make useful day walks for visitors based in the Broads. Public transport at the ends and at several intermediate points makes it possible to sample one stretch rather than commit to the whole trail.
