Wroxham Boat Hire is one of the straightforward ways to get onto the Norfolk Broads without committing to a multi-day cruiser. From Wroxham, a day boat puts you immediately onto a network of slow rivers, reed beds, waterside cottages, and open broads that are difficult to appreciate properly from the road.

This is an activity rather than a conventional sight, but it is central to how the Broads are experienced. Book ahead in busy school-holiday periods, arrive early enough for the handover, and keep the route modest if you want time to stop for lunch or watch birdlife along the banks.
