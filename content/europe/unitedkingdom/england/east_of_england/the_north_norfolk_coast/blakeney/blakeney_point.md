Blakeney Point is the headline. The long shingle spit shelters marshes and tidal water, and its seal colony is one of the strongest wildlife experiences on the English coast.

Most visitors see it by boat, which is usually the right choice. Walking from Cley is possible, but the shingle is tiring and the weather can turn a pleasant idea into a slog. Keep well away from seals on land, especially during pupping season.
