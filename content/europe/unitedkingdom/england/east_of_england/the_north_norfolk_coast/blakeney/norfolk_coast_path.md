The Norfolk Coast Path passes close to Blakeney and gives the village its best low-key activity: walking beside creeks, marsh, and big East Anglian sky. Short stretches toward Cley or Morston are enough for most visitors.

The path is easy to follow, but it is exposed. Bring a layer even when the village feels mild.
