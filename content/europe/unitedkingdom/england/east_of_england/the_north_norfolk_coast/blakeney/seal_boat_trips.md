Seal boat trips are the easiest way to experience Blakeney Point. Boats usually leave from Morston or Blakeney, run with the tide, and keep visitors at a sensible distance from the hauled-out seals.

Book ahead in busy months and be flexible about timing. The sea here is ruled by tides, not by your lunch plans.
