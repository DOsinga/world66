Blakeney Point is a long shingle spit and National Trust nature reserve west of Cley Beach. It is famous for grey seals, with pups in winter, and for nesting terns in summer.

You can walk from Cley Beach, but this is exposed coastal walking rather than a casual village stroll. Check tide, weather, and seasonal dog restrictions before setting out. Boat trips from Morston are the easier way to see the seals, but the walk gives you the rawer sense of the coast.
