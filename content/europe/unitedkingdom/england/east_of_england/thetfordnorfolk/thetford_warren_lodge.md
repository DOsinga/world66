Thetford Warren Lodge is a small, tough medieval building with an unusually specific purpose: it housed the warrener who guarded the rabbit warrens on Thetford Heath. Rabbits were valuable for meat and fur, and the lodge's thick walls and elevated position make more sense when you imagine it as a practical watch post rather than a romantic ruin.

It is a short stop, but a memorable one if you are walking or cycling around the heath. The appeal lies in the oddity of the place and the open Breckland landscape around it. Combine it with Thetford Forest, Grime's Graves, or the town's priory ruins for a day focused on the area's older, quieter history.
