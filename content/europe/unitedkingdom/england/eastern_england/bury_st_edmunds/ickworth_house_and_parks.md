Ickworth House and Parks is a National Trust estate at Horringer, just outside Bury St Edmunds. The grand house was the former seat of the Marquess of Bristol, and its unusual rotunda makes it one of Suffolk's more distinctive country houses.

The visit is not only indoors: the estate has formal gardens, parkland, lakes, walking routes, play areas, and deer, so it works well as a half-day trip from Bury St Edmunds.
