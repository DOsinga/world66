Moyse's Hall is a small museum in one of Bury St Edmunds' most distinctive medieval buildings, facing the market area. The flint-and-stone exterior gives it immediate presence, and the collection ranges through local archaeology, crime, clocks, and town history.

It is the kind of museum that works best when you let it be odd. Do not expect a single grand narrative; expect a building with deep roots and a collection that catches the stranger corners of Suffolk life.
