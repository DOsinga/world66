All Saints, Jesus Lane is a preserved 19th-century church and one of Cambridge's best Arts and Crafts interiors. The exterior is fairly restrained, but inside are rich wall decorations, stained glass, and work associated with G. F. Bodley and William Morris.

It is no longer used for regular worship, which makes it easy to visit as a quiet architectural stop near the colleges.
