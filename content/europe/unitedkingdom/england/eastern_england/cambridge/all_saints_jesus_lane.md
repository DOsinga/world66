All Saints, Jesus Lane is a preserved 19th-century church and one of Cambridge's best small Arts and Crafts interiors. It is no longer used for regular worship, which makes the richly painted walls, Bodley fittings, and stained glass easier to appreciate as a complete decorative scheme.

It is close to Jesus College and the Round Church, so it fits naturally into a central Cambridge walk.
