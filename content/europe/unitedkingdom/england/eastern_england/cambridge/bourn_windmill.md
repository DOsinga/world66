Bourn Windmill is a small but appealing post mill in the countryside west of Cambridge. Its timber form and rural setting give a quick glimpse of the agricultural landscape that surrounds the university city.

It is best visited on open days or as part of a car or cycling loop through the villages. Do not treat it like a central Cambridge sight; its value is in the quiet detour and the survival of a fragile old working structure.
