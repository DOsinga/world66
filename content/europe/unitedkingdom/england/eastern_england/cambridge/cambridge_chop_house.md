The Cambridge Chop House on King's Parade has the best position of any restaurant in the city — a bay window table looks directly onto King's College Chapel. The menu is traditional British: steaks, slow-cooked lamb, chops, pies, and Sunday roasts. The quality is solid and reliable rather than exciting. Prices are mid-range to upper-mid-range. Booking is advisable on weekends. For a meal in view of the chapel that will not require a second mortgage, this is the practical choice.
