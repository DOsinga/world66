Cambridge Science Centre is a hands-on science museum aimed especially at children and families. The exhibits focus on experiments, problem-solving, and the kind of practical science that makes a rainy Cambridge afternoon easier with kids.

It is not one of the city's great historic sights, but it is a useful change of pace from colleges and chapels. Check current opening arrangements before going, as the centre's public exhibitions and outreach programmes can move between venues.
