Corpus Christi College was founded by Cambridge townspeople rather than by a monarch or bishop, which gives it an unusual place in the university's history. Old Court, just inside the main entrance behind St Bene't's Church, dates from the 1350s and is the oldest surviving college courtyard in Cambridge.
