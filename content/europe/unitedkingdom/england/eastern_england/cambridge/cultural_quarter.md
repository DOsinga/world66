The West Road Concert Hall is the main classical music venue in Cambridge, a purpose-built concert hall on the western edge of the university district that hosts the Cambridge Philharmonic Orchestra, chamber concerts, and visiting ensembles year-round. The acoustic was designed specifically for chamber and orchestral music and is among the better modern concert halls in the region. Visiting performers of international quality appear regularly. Check the programme at west-road.co.uk.
