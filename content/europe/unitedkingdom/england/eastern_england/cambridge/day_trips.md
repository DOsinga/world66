Cambridge sits in the flat heart of East Anglia with several worthwhile destinations within easy reach. Ely Cathedral, 20 minutes by train, is one of the finest medieval buildings in England and set in a quietly charming small town. Colchester, an hour by train, is Britain's oldest recorded town with a Norman castle and strong Roman remains.
