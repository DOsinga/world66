The Museum of Cambridge is a social history museum in a former coaching inn near Castle Hill. Its collections focus on everyday life in Cambridge and the surrounding South Cambridgeshire villages, away from the better-known university story.

Displays draw on thousands of local objects, from domestic life to trades and village customs. Opening can depend on volunteer staffing, so check current hours before making a special trip.
