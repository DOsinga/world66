Cambridge Museum of Technology occupies a former sewage pumping station, turning Victorian engineering into the exhibit rather than just the container. Steam engines, boilers, printing equipment, and local industrial material show a different Cambridge from the colleges.

It is especially good for families and visitors interested in machinery or urban infrastructure. The museum also broadens the city's story by showing the practical systems that kept scholarly Cambridge functioning.
