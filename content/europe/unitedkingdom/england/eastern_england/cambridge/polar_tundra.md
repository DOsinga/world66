The entrance passage to the Scott Polar Research Institute on Lensfield Road features a series of dramatic murals depicting the landscapes and figures of polar exploration. The institute's public-facing galleries contain not just historic equipment and documents but also contemporary art and photography from ongoing Arctic and Antarctic research expeditions. The combination of Victorian heroism and modern climate science gives the place an unusual resonance. Free entry; open Tuesday to Saturday.
