The Punter on Pound Hill, near Kettle's Yard, is a neighbourhood pub that serves as a useful non-tourist alternative to the more famous pubs in the centre. Good selection of cask ales, a food menu that changes with the seasons, and a quiet garden that fills up on warm evenings with locals from the Castle Hill and Newnham areas. Worth knowing about if you are visiting Kettle's Yard or the Round Church and want somewhere nearby for a drink.
