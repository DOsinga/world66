St Radegund's on King Street is a small, unpretentious restaurant that has built a good reputation for honest cooking at reasonable prices — a rarity in central Cambridge. The menu changes frequently and skews toward seasonal British and European dishes. No molecular gastronomy, no tasting menus, just well-prepared food in an informal room. Booking recommended on weekends; prices are well below the tourist-facing restaurants on King's Parade.
