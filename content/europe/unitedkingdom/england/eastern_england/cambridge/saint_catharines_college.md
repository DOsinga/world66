Saint Catharine's College is one of the central Cambridge colleges, founded in 1473 and rebuilt around a compact 17th-century court. Its three-sided brick Main Court makes it a worthwhile short stop while walking along Trumpington Street.

The college is close to Queens' College, King's Parade, and the Mathematical Bridge, so it fits naturally into a walk through the older college core.
