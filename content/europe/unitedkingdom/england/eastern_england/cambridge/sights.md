---
title: Sights
type: section
---

Most of Cambridge's historic buildings are on or very close to King's Parade and the college Backs. A few excellent sites — Kettle's Yard, the Round Church, the Scott Polar Museum — lie a short walk from the tourist centre and are far less visited than they deserve.

Note that many colleges charge for entry (usually £5–10), and some close entirely during exam periods in May and June. Check porter's lodge hours before planning a visit.
