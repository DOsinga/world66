St. Benet's Church is a small 11th-century church tucked into Cambridge's central lanes. Its main architectural feature is a Saxon arch in the nave, a rare survival in a city better known for later college chapels.

The church is also one of Cambridge's bellringing churches. It is worth a quiet stop while walking between King's Parade, the market, and the museums.
