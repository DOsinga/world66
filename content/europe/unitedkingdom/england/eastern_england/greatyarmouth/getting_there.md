---
title: "Getting There"
type: section
---

Great Yarmouth is 20 miles east of Norwich. The train from Norwich takes about 30 minutes and runs regularly throughout the day; Great Yarmouth station is at the western edge of the town centre. By car, the A47 from Norwich is the main approach, and there is ample parking near the seafront. There is no direct rail link from London — you must change at Norwich.
