SEA LIFE Great Yarmouth is a straightforward seafront aquarium, good for families and for the sort of weather that sends everyone indoors from the beach. Expect tanks of sharks, rays, jellyfish, and smaller marine life, with the usual hands-on and conservation messaging aimed at children.

It is not a reason by itself to visit Great Yarmouth, but it fits the town's classic resort rhythm: beach, pier, amusements, and something sheltered when the North Sea breeze wins. Book ahead in school holidays, when it can become one of the busier family attractions on the front.
