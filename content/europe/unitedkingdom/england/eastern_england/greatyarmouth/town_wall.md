Great Yarmouth's medieval town wall survives in impressive fragments around the old borough, with towers and long stretches that hint at the scale of the fortified port. It is one of the strongest historic layers in a town better known for seaside entertainment.

The wall is best appreciated as a walk between surviving sections rather than a single stop. It gives useful context to the Rows, the minster, and the town's long maritime wealth.
