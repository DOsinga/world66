---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

Great Yarmouth is a summer resort and most of its attractions operate only between late May and September. The Hippodrome, Britannia Pier's theatre, and the beach attractions all shut down for winter. July and August are the busiest months; the town fills with British families on holiday and accommodation prices reflect this.

May, June, and September offer a quieter, more pleasant version of the same town. The beach is still good, the main attractions are open, and you will not be fighting for chips. The winter months are quiet to the point of bleakness, though the old town's history makes sense year-round.
