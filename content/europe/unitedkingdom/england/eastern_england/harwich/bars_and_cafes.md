---
title: "Bars and Cafes"
type: section
---

Harwich's drinking is concentrated in the old-town pubs — the **Alma Inn** on King's Head Street and the **Hanover Inn** on Church Street are the two most reliable, both serving local East Anglian ales and a regular flow of locals. The seafront has a couple of older pubs with terraces overlooking the harbour, useful for a beer on a long summer evening.

For coffee and cake, the small cafés around Ha'penny Pier and the visitor centre do the job; nothing in Harwich is far from anything else, so wander until you find a window seat you like.
