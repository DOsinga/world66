---
title: "Day Trips"
type: section
---

Harwich is a useful base for the southern Suffolk and Essex coast. The **Harwich Harbour ferry** runs across the estuary in summer to Felixstowe and Shotley, on the Suffolk side — a small adventure in itself. **Felixstowe** offers a long Victorian seafront and the country's biggest container port, which is a strangely compelling sight from the seafront pubs. The pretty Stour estuary villages on the Essex shore — **Manningtree**, **Mistley** — are a half-hour drive away.

For real ambition, **Colchester**, Britain's oldest recorded town with a Norman castle and Roman remains, is forty-five minutes inland. **Constable Country** — the watery flat landscape of the Stour valley that John Constable painted endlessly in the early nineteenth century — starts twenty minutes west of Harwich and rewards a slow day with a car.
