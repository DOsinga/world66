A small but cheerful museum, set in the old railway buildings beside Harwich Town station, telling the story of the Mayflower's 1620 voyage from the town's perspective rather than Plymouth's. Christopher Jones, the ship's captain, was born here and lived in Harwich until the year before the famous crossing; many of the ship's crew were Harwich men. The exhibits cover the ship, the voyage, the lives of the Pilgrims, and the Mayflower's long-term significance — and include a children's section with dressing-up clothes and a small replica deck.

Outside, the ship project itself is the main attraction: a continuing community build of a working replica Mayflower, open for inspection on tour days. Combine with a walk to Christopher Jones's house and St Nicholas Church for a complete Mayflower-themed afternoon.
