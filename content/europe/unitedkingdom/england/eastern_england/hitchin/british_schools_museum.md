A wonderful small museum housed in the original Victorian and Edwardian schoolrooms of the Hitchin British Schools, just off Queen Street. The buildings preserve a complete sequence of 19th-century educational architecture — a Lancasterian schoolroom of 1837 with stepped tiered galleries, a Victorian classroom of the 1850s, and an Edwardian classroom — all open to walk through.

Guided tours led by retired teachers run on weekend afternoons; dressing-up boxes and pencil drills make it a hit with children. Modest admission. Open weekend afternoons only outside school holidays; check before going.
