---
title: "Getting There"
type: section
---

Hitchin sits on the **East Coast Main Line** and is reached from **London King's Cross in around 30 minutes** by Thameslink and LNER services — fast enough that the town has become a serious commuter destination. The railway station is a 10-minute walk east of the centre on Walsworth Road.

The town is one junction off the **A1(M)** motorway (junction 8); London is about 35 miles south, Cambridge about 30 miles north-east. There is no direct intercity bus to compare with the train.
