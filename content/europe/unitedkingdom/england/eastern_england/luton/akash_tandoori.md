A long-running Bangladeshi restaurant on Dunstable Road in Bury Park, the kind of place that defines Luton's reputation for good, cheap curries. The menu runs the standard British-Bangladeshi length — biriyanis, baltis, jalfrezis, tandoori — but the kitchen is more careful and the spicing more interesting than the chain equivalents in central London.

The dining room is plain, the service is brisk, and the prices are about half what you'd pay in zone one. BYO licence with corkage. Open late. A useful refuge after a delayed flight at the airport.
