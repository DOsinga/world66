The Bury Park area, west of the town centre, has a dense cluster of Bangladeshi, Pakistani, and Indian restaurants, sweet shops, and grocery stores. It is widely regarded as one of the best South Asian food streets outside London — genuinely good and unpretentious, with a wide range of regional cuisines represented. Worth seeking out if you have any time in the town.
