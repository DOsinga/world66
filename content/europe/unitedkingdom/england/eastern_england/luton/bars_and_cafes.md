---
title: Bars and Cafes
type: section
---

Luton has a perfectly respectable selection of traditional pubs — the older terraced-house corner pubs in High Town and George Street are the best of the type — and a smaller assortment of newer bars in the centre. The town's large Muslim community means several neighbourhoods (notably Bury Park) have no licensed venues at all; the centre, by contrast, has a robust Friday-and-Saturday night scene aimed at the local student and young-professional population.

For coffee, the centre has the usual chains; for something more independent, try one of the small specialty cafés in the area around the Mall and Bute Street.
