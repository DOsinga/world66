**Notes from a Small Island** by Bill Bryson — Bryson's affectionate and often funny journey around Britain includes stops in towns like Luton, and captures something of the texture of English urban life in the 1990s.

**The Bedfordshire and Hertfordshire Regiment** and related local histories — various volumes on Bedfordshire cover the county's history, industries, and communities in detail. The Luton Museum service has published pamphlets on lace-making history that are worth seeking out.

**Twice Migrants: East African Sikh Settlers in Britain** by Parminder Bhachu — a sociological study of South Asian communities in Britain relevant to understanding towns like Luton with large East African Asian populations.

**Discovering the Chilterns and the Vale of Aylesbury** — a useful guide to the broader region around Luton, covering walking routes, historic houses, and villages within easy reach.
