The most ethnically distinctive neighbourhood in Luton, immediately west of the town centre. **Bury Park** has been the focal point of the town's South Asian community since the 1960s — the Pakistani-Kashmiri community is the largest, with significant Bangladeshi and Indian populations also.

The area is most interesting along **Dunstable Road**: a long strip of South Asian sweet shops, fabric merchants, jewellery counters specialising in 22-carat gold, Halal butchers, and probably the densest concentration of South Asian restaurants in eastern England. The food is very good and very cheap. The neighbourhood is also home to the **Luton Central Mosque** and **Kenilworth Road football stadium**. Best visited in daylight hours; the area is lively but the street layout takes some getting used to.
