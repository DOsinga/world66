Dunstable Downs is the high chalk edge of the Chiltern Hills, rising to the highest point in Bedfordshire. On a clear day the views run far across the Vale of Aylesbury, and the open slopes are good for a breezy walk. It is also a classic spot for watching gliders and kites work the wind.
