The northern end of the **Chiltern Hills** Area of Outstanding Natural Beauty, 5 miles west of Luton. The Downs are a long, steep chalk escarpment dropping westward into the Aylesbury Vale, with panoramic views over Bedfordshire and Buckinghamshire. The National Trust runs a visitor centre at the top with a café, an exhibition on the geology and ecology, and good wheelchair-accessible paths.

The grasslands on top are some of the best-surviving chalk grassland in eastern England — an unusual ecosystem with orchids, harebells, and rare butterflies in summer. The **Icknield Way**, a Bronze Age trackway, runs along the ridge. Combine with a visit to **Dunstable** town below or a longer walk along the escarpment to **Whipsnade**.
