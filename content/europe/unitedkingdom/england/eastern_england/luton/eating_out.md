---
title: Eating Out
type: section
---

Luton's food scene is genuinely diverse — a reflection of one of the most ethnically mixed populations in the UK outside London. **Bury Park** has a long stretch of South Asian restaurants and sweet shops along Dunstable Road serving Pakistani, Kashmiri, and Indian food that compares well with anywhere in the country at a fraction of the London price. The Bangladeshi community concentrates around Park Town. The Caribbean community runs several jerk specialists in the centre.

For more conventional dining, the town centre has the usual range of pub food and chains; the nicer restaurants tend to be in the surrounding villages and at country-house hotels like **Luton Hoo**.
