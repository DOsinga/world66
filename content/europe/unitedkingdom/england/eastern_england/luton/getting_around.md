---
title: "Getting Around"
type: section
---

The town centre is walkable, though the main sights are spread out. Wardown Park Museum is about 15 minutes on foot north of the centre. Stockwood Discovery Centre is further south and is best reached by local bus or taxi. A local bus network connects the main sights. Cycling is possible along the River Lea corridor, which provides a pleasant route north from the centre towards Wardown Park.
