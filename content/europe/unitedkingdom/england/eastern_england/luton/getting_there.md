title: Getting There
type: section
---

**London Luton Airport** (LTN) is the main reason most people know the name — one of the UK's busiest airports, with budget carriers like EasyJet, Ryanair, and Wizz Air running short-haul European routes. The airport is about 3 km south-east of the town centre and connected to **Luton Airport Parkway** railway station by the DART automated shuttle (3 minutes); from Luton Airport Parkway, **Thameslink** trains run direct to London St Pancras (around 25 minutes) and on to Brighton, plus services north to Bedford.

The main **Luton** railway station in the town centre has the same Thameslink line and East Midlands Railway services to Nottingham and Sheffield. The **M1** motorway passes the town with junctions 10, 10a, and 11.
