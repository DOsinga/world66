A live-music, theatre, and arts venue in a converted Victorian hat factory in the centre of town, on Bute Street. The Hat Factory hosts a busy programme of stand-up comedy, small-scale theatre, contemporary dance, and gigs by touring bands — generally at the cheaper end of the live music spectrum, and often a useful spot to catch comedians on their way to or from the Edinburgh Fringe.

The building itself is one of the few visible reminders of Luton's hat-making past, with the original brickwork, wide internal staircases, and warehouse spaces converted to dual use. The ground-floor bar is open through the day even when there is no show on. Check the listings before turning up.
