The home ground of **Luton Town F.C.**, on Maple Road in the Bury Park neighbourhood. Kenilworth Road has been Luton's stadium since 1905 and is built into a tight residential terrace — a remarkable survival of pre-war English football architecture in an era when most clubs have moved to out-of-town bowls. Capacity is just over 11,000.

The Hatters have had a remarkable recent run, dropping out of the football league entirely in 2009 and climbing back through five divisions to the Premier League by 2023. The stadium hosts match-day tours when the team is away; check the club's website. Genuine football fans should try to see a game here before the planned move to **Power Court** is built.
