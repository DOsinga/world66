The town's biggest annual event: a Caribbean-style street carnival held on the late May bank holiday Monday, with a parade through the town centre and a festival site in Wardown Park. **Luton Carnival** has been running since 1976 and is one of the largest events of its kind outside London's Notting Hill.

The parade features costumed dance groups, steel bands, sound systems, and floats from local community organisations. The festival in Wardown has multiple stages, food stalls, and craft markets. Free; family-friendly during the day; the after-parties carry on into the evening. If you are in the area on that weekend, it is one of the most distinctive cultural events anywhere in the East of England.
