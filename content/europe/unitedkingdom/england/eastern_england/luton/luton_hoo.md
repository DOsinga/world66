A Robert Adam-designed Palladian country house on a Capability Brown estate, two miles south-east of Luton. **Luton Hoo** was rebuilt and remodelled by successive owners — most extensively by the diamond magnate Sir Julius Wernher around 1900 — and the result is a remarkable layered piece of architecture, combining Adam's 1760s plan with later French rococo interiors, a sweeping Edwardian saloon, and an Italianate chapel.

The house is now a luxury hotel (the Luton Hoo Hotel, Golf and Spa) — not open to the general public except by booking for afternoon tea, lunch, or a stay. The 1,000-acre **grounds** are also private but stretches of the public footpath cross the estate and the lake is visible from the south side. The walled garden is occasionally open for special events. For most visitors, the experience is photographic from the estate boundary or via dinner at the hotel.
