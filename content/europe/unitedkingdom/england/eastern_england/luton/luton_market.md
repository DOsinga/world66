The covered market in the centre of town, off Bute Street. The market has been a Luton institution for over a century — the current building dates from the 1970s — and reflects the town's demography in its mix of stalls: South Asian fabric and gold, Caribbean groceries, Polish delis, English fish and meat, vintage clothes, and a row of plain café counters.

It is open Monday to Saturday and is at its busiest on Saturday mornings. The prices are some of the lowest in the southeast for fresh produce, halal meat, and South Asian groceries. The food court is a useful lunch stop. Easier to enjoy when you have a specific shopping mission in mind.
