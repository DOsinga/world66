A traditional pub on the corner of Pomfret Avenue and Hitchin Road in the High Town area, north of the station. **The Painters Arms** is the kind of unpretentious Victorian street-corner local that has mostly disappeared from the south of England — a horseshoe-shaped bar, a real fire in winter, several well-kept cask ales, and a clientele that is mostly regulars.

There is no food (or a basic crisps-and-pies menu at most), no music venue billing, and no theme — just a pub. Walking distance from the station and a useful first or last stop in town. Cash and card both work.
