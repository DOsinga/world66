A free, sprawling museum complex on the southern edge of Luton, set in 60 acres of period gardens. **Stockwood** combines the **Mossman Collection** of historic horse-drawn carriages (one of the largest in the UK), a series of social-history galleries on Luton's industrial past, a Victorian period garden, a sensory garden, a sculpture garden by the artist Ian Hamilton Finlay, and a café with reasonable food.

The carriage halls are unexpectedly engrossing — Roman chariots through Victorian state coaches — and the gardens are a serious horticultural undertaking, with each section recreating a specific period (medieval physic, Tudor knot, 17th-century, Victorian Italianate). Free; closed Mondays in winter. Allow 2–3 hours.
