---
title: Things to Do
type: section
---

Most travellers' encounter with Luton is the airport, and most never leave it. If you do, the highlights are the **Wardown Park Museum** (local history and lace-making) in a Victorian villa by the River Lea, the **Stockwood Discovery Centre** (gardens, carriages, social history) south of town, and a walk on the chalk grasslands of the **Dunstable Downs**. **Luton Hoo** to the southeast is a country house and gardens by Robert Adam, with grounds laid out by Capability Brown — the best single site in the borough.
