Set in a Victorian villa beside the River Lea, Wardown Park Museum has an unexpectedly good collection covering Luton's local history, including an extensive lace-making display — the Bedfordshire lace-making tradition was once a major cottage industry in the region. Entry is free, and the riverside setting in Wardown Park makes it a pleasant stop even if you only wander the grounds.
