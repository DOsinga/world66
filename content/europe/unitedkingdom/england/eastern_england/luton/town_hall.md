A handsome neo-Georgian municipal building of Portland stone, with a tall clock tower and a colonnaded entrance facing **George Square**. Luton Town Hall is the town's main architectural set-piece — a Grade II-listed example of the inter-war public building boom — and the front steps are the site of the annual Remembrance Sunday gathering.

Not normally open to casual visitors except during occasional public events. Worth looking at from the outside and walking through George Square, which has been pedestrianised and is the centre of the town's regular markets and outdoor events.
