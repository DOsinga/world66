A 50-acre Victorian park on the western bank of the River Lea, just north of the town centre. The park combines formal Victorian planting, a serpentine boating lake, mature trees, a bandstand, tennis courts, a bowling green, and the much-restored Wardown villa now housing the museum.

It is the closest thing Luton has to a major urban park and is the centrepiece of the town's annual **Luton Carnival** in May — one of the largest one-day carnival events in Europe. On a sunny weekend the park is full of families; on weekdays the lakeside paths are quiet and pleasant. The walk from the town centre takes about 20 minutes through the High Town neighbourhood.
