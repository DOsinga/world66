Whipsnade Zoo is one of Europe's largest wildlife parks, spread across open countryside near the Dunstable Downs. Its size gives the animal areas more room than a typical city zoo, and the visit can easily take most of a day.

The draw is the mix of big animals, conservation work, and a setting that feels more spacious than London Zoo.
