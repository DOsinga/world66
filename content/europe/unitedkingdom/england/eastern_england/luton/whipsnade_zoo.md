The Zoological Society of London's larger sister site to London Zoo, 6 miles west of Luton on the chalk downland. Whipsnade is one of the biggest zoos in Europe and has the space to keep large herd animals — elephants, rhinos, giraffes, bison, wolves — in genuinely sizable enclosures. The site is so big that a steam train (the **Jumbo Express**) runs around part of it.

Allow a full day; arrive at opening to see the elephants and big cats before the crowds. The Zoological Society runs a serious conservation breeding programme here. Cars are allowed to drive through certain sections; for non-drivers a free internal bus loops the site. Pricey but the experience is more like a wildlife park than a conventional zoo.
