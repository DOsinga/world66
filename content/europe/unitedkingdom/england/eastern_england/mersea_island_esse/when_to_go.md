---
title: When to Go
type: section
---

Mersea Island is at its best from late spring through early autumn. Summer weekends are busy at West Mersea — the Company Shed will have a queue and parking is tight — but the atmosphere is good and the island wears its popularity lightly. September and October are arguably the best months: quieter, and the start of native oyster season (September through April).

Avoid winter if you want the beach experience, though the island has a bleak, atmospheric quality in the off-season that suits the estuarine landscape. Whatever the season, check tide times: the Strood causeway floods at high tide, typically covering the road for an hour or two twice a day.
