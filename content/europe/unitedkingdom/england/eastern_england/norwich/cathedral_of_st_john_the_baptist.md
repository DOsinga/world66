The Cathedral of St John the Baptist is the other great cathedral experience in Norwich, distinct from the older Anglican cathedral near the river. Built in Gothic Revival style, it rises with a confidence that makes it one of the city’s most impressive Victorian-era buildings.

Inside, the scale and stonework reward a slow look rather than a quick photograph from the street. It is close enough to the centre to fold into a Norwich walk, especially if you are comparing the city’s medieval, nonconformist, and Catholic layers.
