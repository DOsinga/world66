The City of Norwich Aviation Museum sits by Norwich Airport and preserves aircraft, cockpit sections and local aviation history. It is strongest on Cold War and regional aviation, with outdoor aircraft displays that make the visit more tactile than a panel-only museum.

It is outside the medieval city centre, so plan transport rather than trying to fold it into a cathedral-and-market walk. Aviation enthusiasts will get the most from it, but families can also use it as a practical half-day stop.
