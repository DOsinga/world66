Norwich Market is one of the largest open-air markets in England and one of the most genuine. Around 200 stalls spread across the slope below the castle in a grid of striped canvas awnings, selling fresh produce, meat, fish, hot food, clothing, hardware, and a miscellany of other things. It is a working market, not a tourist attraction — the prices are low, the range is practical, and the quality of fresh food is good.

The hot food stalls at the centre are particularly worth exploring at lunchtime: traders sell everything from curries to salt beef bagels to crêpes. A few stalls have been there for decades, run by the same families. The market is open Monday through Saturday; it is liveliest on Wednesday and Saturday.

The market sits at the heart of the old city, with the castle on the mound above it and the Guildhall on its flank — a medieval civic arrangement that has survived remarkably intact.
