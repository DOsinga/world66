The Eight Bells is the sort of pub Saffron Walden does well: old beams, a central location and enough food to make it useful between the garden, museum and market streets. The building dates back to the 16th century, which gives even a simple lunch some local atmosphere.

It is a pub-restaurant rather than a destination meal. That is fine; the point here is a pint or plate in a building that belongs to the town.
