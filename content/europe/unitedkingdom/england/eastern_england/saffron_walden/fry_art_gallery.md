The Fry Art Gallery is small, but its focus is sharp: 20th-century artists connected with northwest Essex, including Eric Ravilious and Edward Bawden. That gives the collection a local grain rather than the usual small-town miscellany.

Opening hours are limited and seasonal, so check before going. If it is open, it adds a thoughtful hour to a Saffron Walden day.
