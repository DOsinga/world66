The Cliffs Pavilion is Southend-on-Sea's main large performance venue, hosting touring theatre, concerts, comedy, and seasonal shows above the seafront. It matters most when the programme aligns with your visit.

Use it as an evening option after the pier, cliffs, or waterfront. Book ahead for popular touring shows, and check start times against train plans if returning to London or elsewhere the same night.
