The Palace Theatre gives Southend-on-Sea a more traditional cultural stop away from the pier, amusement rides, and seafront lights. It is an intimate Edwardian theatre with a programme that can include touring drama, comedy, music, and community productions.

It is worth checking the listings if you are staying overnight or want a quieter evening than the waterfront offers. The building's scale is part of the charm: small enough to feel local, established enough to make a night out feel like more than filler.
