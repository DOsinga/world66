---

The defining experience of Southend is the walk (or the [pier railway ride](/europe/unitedkingdom/england/eastern_england/southendonsea/southend_pier_railway)) out along [Southend Pier](/europe/unitedkingdom/england/eastern_england/southendonsea/southend_pier), at 1.34 miles the longest pleasure pier in the world. The seafront is anchored by [Adventure Island](/europe/unitedkingdom/england/eastern_england/southendonsea/adventure_island), a busy free-entry amusement park, and the [Sea Life Adventure](/europe/unitedkingdom/england/eastern_england/southendonsea/sea_life_adventure) aquarium. The unusual [Southend Cliff Railway](/europe/unitedkingdom/england/eastern_england/southendonsea/southend_cliff_railway) lifts you up from the seafront to the cliff-top gardens. Inland, the [Beecroft Art Gallery](/europe/unitedkingdom/england/eastern_england/southendonsea/beecroft_art_gallery), the small [Southend Central Museum](/europe/unitedkingdom/england/eastern_england/southendonsea/southend_central_museum) and the wonderfully odd [Cranfield's Curiosity Cabinet](/europe/unitedkingdom/england/eastern_england/southendonsea/cranfields_curiosity_cabinet) make up a small but interesting indoor circuit. The [Kursaal](/europe/unitedkingdom/england/eastern_england/southendonsea/the_kursaal), an Edwardian entertainment dome at the east end of the seafront, remains an architectural landmark. Out at the muddy mouth of the Thames sits the unsettling [Graveyard of Lost Species](/europe/unitedkingdom/england/eastern_england/southendonsea/graveyard_of_lost_species), a slowly sinking boat carved with the names of vanished Estuary fish and birds.
