Westcliff Gardens run along the higher ground above Southend's seafront, offering a calmer alternative to the pier and amusement arcades. Paths, planting, and benches look out across the Thames Estuary, where the horizon is wide and low.

The gardens are best for a gentle walk, especially if you want sea air without the full bustle of the main promenade. They also make a pleasant route between Westcliff and central Southend.
