The Gordon Craig Theatre is Stevenage's main performance venue, attached to the town's arts and leisure centre near the station. It is a practical civic theatre rather than a grand historic auditorium, which fits Stevenage's new-town character.

Check the programme if you are staying locally: touring theatre, comedy, music, pantomime, and community productions all pass through. The venue is especially convenient if you are arriving by train, as it sits only a short walk from the platforms.
