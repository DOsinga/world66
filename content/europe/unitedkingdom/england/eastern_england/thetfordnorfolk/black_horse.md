One of Thetford's oldest pubs, the Black Horse occupies a timber-framed building on King Street that dates back to at least the seventeenth century. It is a proper town pub — real ales, a straightforward menu at lunchtime, and the sort of interior that has resisted renovation. The exposed beams and low ceilings are genuine rather than decorative.

A convenient stop after visiting the nearby Thomas Paine statue or the Ancient House Museum, which are both within a few minutes' walk.
