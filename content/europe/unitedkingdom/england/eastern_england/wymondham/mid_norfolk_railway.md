The Mid-Norfolk Railway runs heritage services from Wymondham Abbey station toward Dereham. The southern terminus is a pleasant little stop in its own right, with gardens and a booking office, but the point is the slow ride through rural Norfolk. Check operating days before building a trip around it; this is a heritage railway, not an everyday commuter service.
