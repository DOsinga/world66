Wymondham Heritage Museum tells the town's story in the old Bridewell, a former prison close to the centre. It is best for visitors who want local texture rather than big-ticket spectacle: the fire, the market, rural trades, and the town's reformers and rebels all get their turn. Pair it with the abbey and Market Cross for a rounded visit.
