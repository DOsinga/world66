Holst Victorian House, formerly known as the Holst Birthplace Museum, preserves the Cheltenham house where Gustav Holst was born in 1874. The museum combines composer biography with a broader view of Victorian middle-class domestic life, from the music room to working household spaces.

It is a compact museum, but it has a clear reason to exist: Holst's connection to Cheltenham gives the town a cultural story beyond Regency terraces and festivals. Music lovers will get the most from it, while others can treat it as a quieter counterpoint to Montpellier and the Pump Room.
