St John the Baptist is a big, complicated Cotswold parish church that grew over centuries rather than arriving as one neat design. The result is a rich interior of chapels, tombs, stained glass, carved stone, and small historical surprises.

It sits at the bottom of town near the River Windrush, away from the busiest part of the High Street. Give it time; it is the place in Burford most likely to outlast the postcard view.
