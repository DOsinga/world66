Hidcote Manor Garden is one of the great Arts and Crafts gardens of the Cotswolds. It was designed by the American horticulturalist Lawrence Johnston as a sequence of outdoor "rooms", with hedges, gates, long views, clipped forms, and contrasting planting styles.

The garden rewards slow wandering more than a quick circuit. Look for the formal rooms near the house, the wilder edges, and the views out into the surrounding countryside. It is run by the National Trust and is one of the strongest garden visits in the region.
