Dean Heritage Centre tells the story of the Forest of Dean from an old watermill site near Soudley. The exhibits are strongest on forest trades: charcoal-burning, timber work, and the free-mining tradition that shaped villages across the forest.

It is a useful stop if you want context before walking the woods or visiting the old industrial sites. The setting is part of the appeal, with mill buildings, outdoor displays, and forest paths close by.
