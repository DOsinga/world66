The Romano-Celtic temple at Lydney Park is a quiet archaeological site on the edge of the Forest of Dean. Its excavated remains give a clear sense of a late Roman sanctuary, with enough surviving structure to make the visit more than a line on a map.

The site has an unusual literary footnote too: the 1920s excavation was led by Mortimer Wheeler, with J.R.R. Tolkien involved in studying the inscriptions. Come for the Roman history, and stay for the views across the Severn.
