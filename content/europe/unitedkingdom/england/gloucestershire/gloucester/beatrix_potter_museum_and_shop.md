The Beatrix Potter Museum and Shop occupies the small tailor's house that inspired *The Tailor of Gloucester*. It sits down a narrow lane close to Gloucester Cathedral, so it is an easy add-on while exploring the old centre.

The place is small, but that suits the story. It is best for Potter readers, families, and anyone who likes literary sites that still feel tied to a real street rather than a large museum display.
