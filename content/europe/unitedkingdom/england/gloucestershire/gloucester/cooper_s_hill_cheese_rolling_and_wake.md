The Cooper's Hill Cheese-Rolling and Wake is one of England's great pieces of organised foolishness. Competitors fling themselves down the steep grass slope at Cooper's Hill after a round of Double Gloucester cheese, usually with more tumbling than running.

It is a short event rather than a conventional attraction, so timing matters. If you are in Gloucester around the late May bank holiday, it is worth the detour for the crowd, the absurdity, and the very local pride wrapped around the spectacle.
