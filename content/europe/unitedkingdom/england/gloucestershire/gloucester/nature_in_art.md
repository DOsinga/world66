Nature in Art is a specialist museum north of Gloucester that focuses on art inspired by nature, wildlife and landscape. Set in Wallsworth Hall, it mixes changing exhibitions with a permanent collection that is broader than a standard local gallery.

The museum is outside the city centre, so it suits travelers with a car or those already exploring the villages north of Gloucester. It is a quiet counterpoint to the cathedral and docks, especially for visitors interested in wildlife art or botanical subjects.
