Batsford Arboretum is a 55-acre collection of trees and shrubs near Moreton-in-Marsh, with especially good Japanese maples, magnolias, and pines. It is one of the better garden stops in the north Cotswolds, particularly in spring blossom and autumn colour.

Paths wind through the slope rather than following stiff formal lines, so the visit feels like a woodland walk with rare trees tucked into it. Allow time for a slow circuit rather than treating it as a quick roadside stop.
