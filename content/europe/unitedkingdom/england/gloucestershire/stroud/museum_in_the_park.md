Set in a 17th-century wool merchant's house in Stratford Park, this free museum tells the local story through mill history, landscape, art, everyday objects, and a few oddities. The walled garden is a quiet bonus in good weather.

It is not a grand museum, but it gives Stroud useful depth. Visit before exploring the valleys and the old textile settlements around town.
