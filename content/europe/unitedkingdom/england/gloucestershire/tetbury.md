Tetbury is a compact Cotswold market town with steep lanes, antique shops, and more serious sights nearby than its size suggests. Its streets have the usual warm limestone and old coaching-town feel, but the town is not just pretty stonework. Highgrove, Chavenage House, Westonbirt Arboretum, and the Market House give it a real day-trip spine.

[Market House](/europe/unitedkingdom/england/gloucestershire/tetbury/market_house) anchors the centre, with narrow streets dropping away toward the old wool town lanes. [Highgrove Gardens](/europe/unitedkingdom/england/gloucestershire/tetbury/highgrove_gardens), the private gardens of King Charles III, sit just southwest of town and open for pre-booked tours on selected dates. [Chavenage House](/europe/unitedkingdom/england/gloucestershire/tetbury/chavenage_house) adds Elizabethan architecture and guided tours with a human touch.

Tetbury also works well with [Westonbirt Arboretum](/europe/unitedkingdom/england/gloucestershire/tetbury/westonbirt_arboretum), one of Britain's great tree collections, about three miles away. If you only have one day in the southern Cotswolds, Tetbury is a sound choice: town walk, garden or house tour, then an arboretum ramble.
