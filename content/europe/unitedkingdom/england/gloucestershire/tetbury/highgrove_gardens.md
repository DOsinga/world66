Highgrove Gardens surround the private Gloucestershire home of King Charles III and Queen Camilla. The gardens have been shaped since 1980 around organic methods, formal garden rooms, wildflower planting, and the very particular tastes of their owner.

Visits are by pre-booked guided tour on selected dates, with security checks and strict arrival rules. It is worth planning around if gardens interest you; casual drop-ins are not possible.
