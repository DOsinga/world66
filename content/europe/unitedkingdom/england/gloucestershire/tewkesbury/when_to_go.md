---
title: When to Go
type: section
---

Tewkesbury is a year-round destination but the weather is most reliable from May to September. The town's annual Medieval Festival, held each July, is one of the largest outdoor medieval events in England, with re-enactments of the Battle of Tewkesbury drawing thousands of visitors. Accommodation books up well in advance for the festival weekend.

Winter and early spring are the seasons when the water meadows flood, which can be dramatic and atmospheric — the Severn Ham under water with the abbey tower rising above the mist is one of those English landscape views that stays with you. The town is quieter and less crowded outside the summer season.
