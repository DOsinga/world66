Rydal Mount was William Wordsworth's family home for the last decades of his life. The house is modest by grand-house standards, but the gardens and views give the visit its force: terraces, trees, and glimpses of the surrounding fells.

It sits just outside Ambleside on the way to Grasmere, and works best as part of a walk through Rydal rather than a quick drive-by. Literary pilgrims will care most, but even sceptics usually concede that Wordsworth chose his view well.
