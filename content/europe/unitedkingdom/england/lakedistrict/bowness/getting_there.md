---
title: 'Getting There'
type: section
---
The easiest approach by public transport is to take a train to Windermere station, which sits on a branch line from Oxenholme on the West Coast Main Line. Oxenholme has direct connections to London Euston, Manchester, and Glasgow. From Windermere station it is about a mile and a half downhill to Bowness-on-Windermere — walkable in 20–25 minutes, or there is a frequent bus. By car, the M6 is the main route from the south; exit at junction 36 and follow the A590 and A591.
