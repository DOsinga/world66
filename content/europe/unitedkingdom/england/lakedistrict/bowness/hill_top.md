Hill Top is the farmhouse at Near Sawrey on the western shore of Windermere that Beatrix Potter bought in 1905 and where she wrote and illustrated many of her most famous books. Now owned by the National Trust, it is one of the most visited houses in the Lake District — and rightly so, because the National Trust has preserved it almost exactly as Potter left it.

The easiest approach from Bowness is by ferry across the lake to Bowness Nab, followed by a short walk through the village. The house is small and visits are often timed in peak season to manage numbers. The garden, the farmyard, and the views across the fields to Esthwaite Water beyond are all as Potter painted them, and the experience rewards the effort of getting there.
