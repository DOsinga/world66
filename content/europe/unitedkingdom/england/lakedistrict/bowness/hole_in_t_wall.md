The Hole in t' Wall claims to be the oldest pub in Bowness, with parts of the building dating to 1612. It sits just back from the lake on Lowside, a short walk from the main pier, and its low ceilings and flagstone floors give it the character that the town's more tourist-facing establishments often lack.

Real ales from local Cumbrian breweries are the main draw, and the pub gets busy on summer evenings — arrive early if you want a seat inside. It is also said to be one of the inspirations for the Lakeland pubs in Wordsworth's writing, though that claim is made of a fair few local establishments.
