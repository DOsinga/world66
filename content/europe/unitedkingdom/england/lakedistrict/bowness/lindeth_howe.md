Lindeth Howe sits on a wooded hillside on the southern edge of Bowness, reached by a short drive or a pleasant walk up from the lake. The building was bought by Beatrix Potter in 1912 as a home for her mother, and she visited regularly until her mother's death in 1932. It is now a hotel, and the restaurant is open to non-residents for dinner — worth booking ahead as the dining room is not large.

The cooking is modern British with an emphasis on Cumbrian ingredients: local lamb, Herdwick mutton, and game in season feature regularly. The room looks out over the hotel's gardens and the lake beyond, which makes the setting among the better ones in the area. The cooking is more reliable than some of the town-centre options that trade heavily on location. A good choice for a sit-down dinner after a day on the fells.
