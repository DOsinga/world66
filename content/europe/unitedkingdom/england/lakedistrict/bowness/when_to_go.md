---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

Bowness is at its best in May, June, and September. The lake is navigable, the fells are green, the crowds are present but manageable, and the weather is more likely to cooperate than in July and August — when the town gets genuinely packed and the car parks around the promenade are full by 10am on sunny days.

July and August are the peak school-holiday period and bring the highest visitor numbers. The town still functions fine — the lake cruises run constantly, the ice cream queues move quickly — but expect competition for everything. If you prefer quieter walking and some sense of elbow room, the peak summer weeks are the wrong time.

October and November have a quality of their own: the fells turn gold and red, the tourists thin out, and the lake takes on a grey, moody beauty that the postcards do not show. Winter is cold and wet, with short days and limited opening hours at smaller attractions, but for walkers who do not mind the conditions, the landscape is at its most dramatic. The cruise boats run a reduced winter timetable.
