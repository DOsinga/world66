Opened in 2019 on the lakefront just north of the Bowness pier, the Windermere Jetty Museum houses a remarkable collection of historic boats spanning over 150 years of lake craft. The building itself, designed by Carmody Groarke, is striking — a long low structure clad in cedar with a cantilevered roof that reaches out over the water.

Inside, the boats range from Victorian steam launches and Edwardian motor yachts to the record-breaking Hydroplane Bluebird K3. Interactive displays cover the engineering and social history of the lake, and the boathouse section allows visitors to see the vessels up close in their conservation environment. A good wet-weather option and genuinely interesting even for those without a particular interest in boats.
