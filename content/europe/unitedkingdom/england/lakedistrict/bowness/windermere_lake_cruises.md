Windermere Lake Cruises operates a fleet of boats from the pier in Bowness, running north to Waterhead at Ambleside and south to Lakeside at the foot of the lake. The boats run year-round and are one of the most enjoyable ways to take in the scale of England's largest natural lake and the surrounding fells.

The northern route passes Belle Isle and gives good views of the wooded western shore. At Lakeside you can connect with the Lakeside and Haverthwaite Railway steam trains. Tickets can be bought for single journeys or as hop-on-hop-off passes covering the whole lake, making it practical to use the boats as transport between villages as well as for sightseeing.
