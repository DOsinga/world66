Hardknott Roman Fort is a remote ruin on the old Roman road over Hardknott Pass, built between about 120 and 138 CE. The stone outlines are stark rather than elaborate, but the setting is magnificent: high, exposed, and windswept, with the fells making the Roman choice of route feel both bold and brutal.
