title: Books
type: section
---

**The Prelude** by William Wordsworth — The autobiographical poem with whole books devoted to his Hawkshead childhood. Reading the school passages in the village where they happened is the obvious thing to do.

**The Tale of Samuel Whiskers** by Beatrix Potter — Set in Hill Top Farm itself; you can see the staircase in the kitchen and the cheese in the larder appear in the illustrations.

**Beatrix Potter: A Life in Nature** by Linda Lear — The standard modern biography, particularly good on her transformation from picture-book artist into Lake District sheep farmer and National Trust benefactor.

**A Pair of Blue Eyes** by Thomas Hardy — Set on the Cornish coast rather than the Lakes, but the only major Victorian novel by a writer who knew Hawkshead well enough to use it as inspiration for his characters' rural ways.
