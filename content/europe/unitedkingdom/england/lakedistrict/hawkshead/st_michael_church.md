The parish church stands on a small rise above the village and is one of the prettiest spots in Hawkshead. Mostly fifteenth-century with later additions, it has a low square tower, a churchyard full of weathered slate gravestones, and views across the rooftops to the surrounding fells.

The interior is more interesting than the modest exterior suggests, with a barrel-vaulted ceiling, a set of seventeenth-century painted texts decorating the walls, and several memorial tablets to old Hawkshead families. Open daily in daylight hours, no admission charge.
