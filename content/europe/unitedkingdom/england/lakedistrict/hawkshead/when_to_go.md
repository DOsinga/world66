---
title: When to Go
type: section
---

The Lake District has a deserved reputation for rain — Hawkshead gets around 1700 mm a year — but it is also strikingly beautiful in all weathers, and a wet day in the village can be every bit as evocative as a sunny one.

**Spring (April–May)** is the prettiest season, with daffodils, lambs in the fields, and the trees coming into leaf. The crowds have not yet arrived. **Summer (June–August)** is warmest and busiest, with school holidays packing the car parks and the Potter Gallery selling out hours in advance. Come early or stay late in the day. **Autumn (September–October)** brings the leaves and arguably the best walking weather of the year. **Winter** is quiet, atmospheric, and cold; many pubs stay open but the smaller cafés and attractions close.
