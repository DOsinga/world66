The ambitious one: a small hotel and restaurant up on the Whinlatter Pass, about ten minutes' drive from Keswick, with a tasting-menu kitchen run by Ben Wilkinson that holds a Michelin star. Five or seven courses, regional sourcing, a focused wine list. The view down through the trees toward Bassenthwaite is part of the price.

Worth the drive. Book well ahead.
