The oldest pub in town, in a 17th-century coaching inn just off the market square. Low beams, dark panelling, a wide range of ales and a fire in winter. The food side is straightforward pub fare — pies, fish and chips, Sunday roasts.

A reliable refuge on a wet afternoon, and convenient to almost everything in the town centre.
