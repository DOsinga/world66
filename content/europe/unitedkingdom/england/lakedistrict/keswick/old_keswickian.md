The fish and chip shop on the market square, busy from late morning until late evening. Haddock and cod, mushy peas, decent chips fried in beef dripping. Queue spills out the door at six in summer.

Eat in or take a paper-wrapped portion across the road to a bench and watch the climbers come off the fells.
