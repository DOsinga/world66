title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Ravenglass is the starting point for the Ravenglass and Eskdale Railway, a narrow-gauge steam line that runs seven miles up the beautiful Eskdale valley. The village also sits beside the most substantial Roman ruin in the north of England — the four-metre walls of the bath house at Glannoventa — and within a short walk or drive of Muncaster Castle, Muncaster Mill, and the triple-river estuary of the Esk, Mite, and Irt.

The village itself is a single street running down to the shingle foreshore; the whole place can be walked in ten minutes. Plan a half day for the railway and Muncaster Castle, or a longer day if you intend to ride to Dalegarth and walk in Eskdale.
