---
title: "Bars and Cafes"
type: section
---

For drinking, **The Hole in t' Wall** in Bowness is the historic pub of choice — 17th-century low beams, real ale and a stone-flagged courtyard. **The Royal Oak** in Bowness is the bigger, busier alternative with a wide beer selection and food until late. Up in Windermere town, **The Crafty Baa** is a craft-beer specialist with a long rotating tap list and decent small plates.

For coffee and cake, **Lazy Daisy's** on Crescent Road in Windermere does proper espresso and homemade lemon drizzle; **Poppi Red** in Bowness has a more pink-and-floral feel and is much-loved by locals. The patisserie at **Linthwaite House** does an excellent afternoon tea if you want to make an occasion of it, with views over the lake from the terrace.
