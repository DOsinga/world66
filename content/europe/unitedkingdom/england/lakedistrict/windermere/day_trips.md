Windermere is well placed as a base for exploring the central Lake District. **Hawkshead** and Beatrix Potter's house at Hill Top are an easy half-day west of the lake; **Grasmere**, with Wordsworth's Dove Cottage, is a short bus ride north up the A591. **Coniston**, with its smaller lake and the Ruskin Museum, is twenty minutes by car or a longer trip by bus over the hill from Ambleside.

For more strenuous hill walking, **Helvellyn** and the Striding Edge ridge are within reach for a long day, as is **Scafell Pike** — England's highest mountain — though both work better from Patterdale or Wasdale respectively. **Keswick**, at the northern end of the National Park, is the alternative base for that part of the Lakes and is an hour by bus or car.
