---
title: "Eating Out"
type: section
---

The eating in Windermere splits roughly into three: the family-pub-and-fish-and-chips circuit in Bowness, a quieter set of more ambitious restaurants up the hill in Windermere town, and a handful of country-house and gastro options spread out around the edge of the lake.

In Bowness, **The Angel Inn** does a good upmarket pub menu with proper Cumbrian sourcing; **Lucy's on a Plate** in Ambleside (sister restaurant) is reliable for breakfast and lunch. In Windermere, **Hooked** is the best fish restaurant in the area, and **Francine's** does straightforward bistro cooking that has been keeping return visitors happy for years. For a special meal, **Holbeck Ghyll** above Windermere has a Michelin-starred restaurant in a country-house hotel with stunning lake views, and **Linthwaite House** offers similar in a more contemporary setting.

For something quick, the bakeries on Crescent Road in Windermere town make decent sandwiches; **Poppi Red** in Bowness is a much-loved café for cake and a pot of tea.
