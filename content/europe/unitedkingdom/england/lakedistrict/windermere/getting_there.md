---
title: "Getting There"
type: section
---

**By train.** Windermere station is the end of a short branch line from **Oxenholme**, which itself sits on the West Coast Main Line. Direct trains run from Manchester Airport (1h 40m) and Manchester Piccadilly (1h 30m); connections from London Euston via Oxenholme take around three hours. The branch line is a single-track relic that has been threatened with closure for decades but keeps running.

**By car.** Junction 36 of the M6 motorway feeds the A591, which is the main road into the Lake District. From Manchester or Liverpool allow about two hours; from London, five hours including a break. Parking in Windermere and especially Bowness is expensive and sometimes impossible on summer weekends — the park-and-ride at Brockhole is often the easiest option.

**By bus.** National Express runs direct coaches from Manchester, Birmingham and London Victoria. Local Stagecoach services connect Windermere and Bowness to Ambleside, Grasmere, Kendal and Keswick at high frequency in season; the open-top 599 is a sightseeing route in itself.
