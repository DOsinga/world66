The Blackpool Illuminations are the town's great autumn ritual: miles of lights along the promenade, switched on after the main summer season would normally fade. They are not subtle, and subtle would be wrong here.

Walk part of the route, take a tram, or drive if you have patience for slow traffic. The lights are best understood as civic theatre rather than decoration, a statement that Blackpool refuses to go quietly into the dark.
