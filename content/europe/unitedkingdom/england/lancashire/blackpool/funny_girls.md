Funny Girls is a Blackpool institution: a cabaret and drag venue in a former cinema on the North Shore side of town. It is polished, knowingly over the top, and far more memorable than a generic night in a chain bar.

Book ahead for busy weekends and arrive ready for a show rather than a quiet drink. It fits Blackpool perfectly because it treats spectacle as a public service.
