Blackpool Grand Theatre is one of the town's architectural treasures, a late-Victorian theatre by Frank Matcham tucked close to the main shopping streets. Its interior is richly decorated without feeling pompous.

Check the programme before you go: touring drama, comedy, dance, and family shows all pass through. Even if the show is not your priority, the building itself deserves attention.
