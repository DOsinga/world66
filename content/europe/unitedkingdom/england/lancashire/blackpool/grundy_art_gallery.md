Grundy Art Gallery is Blackpool's public art gallery, close to the North Pier end of town. It opened in 1911 and mixes changing exhibitions with works from the council collection.

It is a good quiet counterpoint to the seafront attractions: small enough for a short visit, but serious enough to reward anyone interested in contemporary art or Blackpool's cultural history.
