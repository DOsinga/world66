---
title: "Eating Out"
type: section
---

Beverley eats well above the standard of most market towns its size. There are two or three serious independent restaurants, a strong pub-food scene anchored by the historic inns around the market squares, and a small but improving cafe scene on the side streets.

**Whites Restaurant**, on North Bar Without, is the town's destination address — small, modern British tasting menus, good wine list, regularly written up in the national press. Book ahead. **The Westwood**, in the old Sessions House at New Walk, does a more relaxed but still ambitious modern menu in handsome surroundings.

For traditional pub food, the **Sun Inn** on Flemingate, the **Cornerhouse** on Norwood, and the **King's Head** on Saturday Market are all reliable, with locally brewed beers and changing daily specials. For lunch and brunch, look along Toll Gavel and Ladygate for a row of independent cafes.
