---
title: "Getting There"
type: section
---

Beverley is easy to reach from Hull, York, and the wider East Riding, and reasonably easy from further afield with a single change.

**By train** — Beverley is on the line from **Hull** (around 15 minutes) and **Scarborough** (a little over an hour) via Bridlington. Trains from York connect via Hull and take roughly an hour and a half. The station is a short walk east of the town centre.

**By car** — The town sits on the A1079 between Hull and York and is signposted from the M62 (around 30 minutes from junction 38). Several pay-and-display car parks circle the town centre; the historic core is best walked.

**By air** — The closest airports are **Humberside (HUY)**, about 35 minutes by car, and **Leeds Bradford (LBA)**, around an hour and a quarter.
