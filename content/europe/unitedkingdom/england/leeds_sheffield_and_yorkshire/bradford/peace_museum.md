The Peace Museum looks at the people and movements that have worked against war, violence, inequality, and extremism. It is a compact museum, but the subject gives it a different tone from Bradford's larger industrial and media collections.

Expect stories of campaigners, conscientious objectors, peace movements, and social justice work rather than military history. It is a worthwhile short stop in the city centre, especially if you are already visiting the Wool Exchange or the National Science and Media Museum.
