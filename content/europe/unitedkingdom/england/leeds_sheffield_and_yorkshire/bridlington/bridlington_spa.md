Bridlington Spa is the town's seafront entertainment complex, rebuilt more than once after fires, floods, and other mishaps while keeping much of its period character. Its main spaces include the large Royal Hall, the Edwardian Spa Theatre, and smaller event rooms.

It is still a working venue for concerts, theatre, and conferences. Even without a ticket, it is worth noticing as part of Bridlington's old resort architecture and promenade life.
