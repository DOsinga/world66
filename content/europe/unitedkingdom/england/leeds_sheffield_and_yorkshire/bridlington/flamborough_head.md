Flamborough Head is the hard edge of the Bridlington coast. Chalk cliffs, sea caves, coves, and a lighthouse mark the headland, with paths giving wide views back toward Bridlington Bay and north toward Bempton.

It is best treated as a walking stop, not just a viewpoint. Weather changes quickly on the exposed headland, and cliff edges deserve respect even on calm days.
