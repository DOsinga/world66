Brodsworth Hall and Gardens is one of the strongest day-out sights near Doncaster: a Victorian country house preserved with the atmosphere of a family seat that slowly aged rather than being polished into perfection. The interiors show the changing fortunes of the estate, while the formal gardens have been carefully restored.

It is a good choice if Doncaster itself feels short on classic visitor sights. Allow time for the gardens as well as the house, especially in spring and summer when the terraces, lawns, and flower borders carry much of the appeal.
