The Minster of St George — often called Doncaster Minster — is a large medieval-footprint church in the centre of the town that was comprehensively rebuilt by George Gilbert Scott in the 1850s in the Decorated Gothic style. The result is one of the finest Victorian churches in England: spacious, well-proportioned, with good Victorian glass and an impressive organ.

It became a Minster in 2004, recognising its historical and architectural importance. The building is free to enter and there are usually volunteers on hand to explain the history. The surrounding streets, particularly around the Corn Exchange and the market, retain a sense of the old market town character.
