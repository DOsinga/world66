---
title: "Getting There"
type: section
---

Doncaster is on the East Coast Main Line, with frequent direct trains to London King's Cross (about 85 minutes), Leeds (25 minutes), and Edinburgh. The station is a ten-minute walk from the town centre. By road, the M18 and A1(M) converge nearby. Sheffield is 30 minutes by train; York about 40 minutes.
