The South Yorkshire Aircraft Museum sits on the former RAF Doncaster site and keeps the town's aviation story visible. Its collection includes aircraft, cockpits, engines, uniforms and local wartime material, with a strong emphasis on hands-on interpretation rather than polished glass cases.

It is particularly good for families and aviation enthusiasts. Allow enough time to talk to the volunteers if they are available; much of the charm is in the local knowledge around Doncaster's airfield history and the individual machines on display.
