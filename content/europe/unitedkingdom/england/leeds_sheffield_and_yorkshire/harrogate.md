Harrogate is a handsome Victorian spa town in North Yorkshire, one of England's most elegant and self-assured small cities. The town grew rich in the 18th and 19th centuries on the strength of its mineral springs — visitors came from across Europe to "take the waters" — and the legacy shows everywhere: in the broad tree-lined avenues, the immaculate public gardens, the confident stone architecture, and a civic pride that keeps everything polished and well-planted.

The mineral springs that made Harrogate famous are still here. The Royal Pump Room on Crown Place was the busiest spa in Europe for much of the 19th century, dispensing sulphurous water to tens of thousands of visitors a year. Today it's a museum, and you can still taste the water — though most visitors decline after one sip. The Royal Baths complex nearby, a vast Edwardian confection of terracotta and tile, gives a sense of the scale of the spa industry at its height.

The 200-acre Stray — an expanse of protected common grassland — wraps around the southern edge of town. It was secured by Act of Parliament in 1770 to ensure that residents would always have access to the mineral wells, and it remains one of Harrogate's great amenities: no buildings, no roads cutting across it, just open green space within walking distance of the centre. Valley Gardens, to the west of the town, is a more formal park with a Sun Pavilion, a Grade II listed glasshouse, and a famous display of spring flowers.

Betty's Tea Rooms on Parliament Street has been a Harrogate institution since 1919. The queues outside on a Saturday morning are testament to its enduring appeal — the Fat Rascal (a large scone-like pastry studded with cherries and peel) is the thing to order. Harrogate punches well above its weight for restaurants generally, partly because it hosts major national conferences and exhibitions, which keeps standards high and the scene varied. The Turkish Baths, still operating in their original Victorian premises off Parliament Street, are a genuine experience rather than a tourist attraction.

Day trips from Harrogate are excellent. The [Yorkshire Dales](/europe/unitedkingdom/england/yorkshiredales) are a short drive west, [Ripon](/europe/unitedkingdom/england/leeds_sheffield_and_yorkshire/ripon) with its cathedral and Fountains Abbey is 12 miles north, and [York](/europe/unitedkingdom/england/leeds_sheffield_and_yorkshire/york) is an easy half-hour by train.
