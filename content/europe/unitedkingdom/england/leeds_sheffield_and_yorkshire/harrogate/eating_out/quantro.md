Quantro on Cheltenham Crescent is one of Harrogate's most reliable independent restaurants, with a menu that moves between European and North African influences — lamb, chickpeas, harissa, roasted vegetables alongside more conventional grills and pasta. The room is small and the atmosphere unpretentious. Good wine list by Yorkshire standards. Book ahead at weekends; the tables fill quickly.
