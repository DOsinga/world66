Tap Social is a social enterprise café and restaurant in the town centre that trains people with criminal records or addiction backgrounds in hospitality work. The food is straightforward and well-made — good sandwiches and salads at lunch, a broader menu in the evenings — and the coffee is excellent. The atmosphere is relaxed and the prices are fair. Worth supporting.
