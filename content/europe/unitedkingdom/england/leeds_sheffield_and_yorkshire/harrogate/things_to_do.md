---
title: Things to Do
type: section
---

Harrogate's spa heritage dominates its sightseeing, but the town offers more than nostalgia. The Royal Pump Room and Royal Baths anchor the Victorian story, while the Mercer Art Gallery, Valley Gardens, and the broad open Stray give the town its distinctive unhurried character. The Turkish Baths are one of the few Victorian spa facilities in England that still function, and they are well worth an afternoon.
