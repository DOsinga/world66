---
title: Eating Out
type: section
---

Hawes has a good selection of cafes, tea rooms, and pubs for its size. Walkers doing the Pennine Way or day trips in the dale keep several establishments busy. The White Hart and the Board Hotel are the main pubs; most visitors eat at lunchtime since the town quiets significantly in the evenings. Wensleydale cheese features on most menus in one form or another.
