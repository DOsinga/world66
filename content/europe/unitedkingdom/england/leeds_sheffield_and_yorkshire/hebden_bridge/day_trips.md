The Calder Valley is easy to explore by train. [Todmorden](/europe/unitedkingdom/england/leeds_sheffield_and_yorkshire/todmorden) is the natural neighbour to the west, while Halifax and Bradford add industrial museums, Piece Hall scale, and the Bronte edge of West Yorkshire.
