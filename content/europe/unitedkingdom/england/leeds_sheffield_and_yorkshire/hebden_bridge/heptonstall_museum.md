Heptonstall Museum is a small local museum in a former school building above Hebden Bridge. Its best-known display covers the Yorkshire Coiners, the 18th-century gang of coin clippers whose story is tied closely to the Calder Valley.

It works best as part of a walk around Heptonstall, adding local context to the village's old lanes, churches, and hillside setting.
