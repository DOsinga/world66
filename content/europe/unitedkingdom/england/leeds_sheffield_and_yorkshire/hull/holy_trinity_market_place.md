Market Place is the original medieval marketplace of Hull, lying immediately south of Hull Minster and dominated by the gilded equestrian statue of William III. Markets were held here from the Middle Ages until the 19th century, when trade moved indoors to the Trinity Market hall beside the church. The cobbled space still hosts occasional events — Christmas markets, City of Culture spillover, and the Freedom Festival.

The surrounding buildings include some of Hull's finest 18th- and 19th-century commercial frontages, with banks and merchants' houses clustering around the square. The Old Custom House, the Lion and the Key pub, and the Three Tuns hotel are all worth a look in passing.
