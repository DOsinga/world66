Princes Quay opened in 1991 and is unmissable: a four-storey glass shopping centre on steel piles standing in the water of Princes Dock. The setting was ahead of its time, the architecture has not aged especially gracefully, and the retail mix has been hit hard by the long decline of mid-tier centres. The cinema on the top floor remains popular, and the views from the upper levels across the Marina and into the Old Town are worth the lift up.

The dock itself is full of mooring rings and bollards from its working life. Walk around the outside on the wharfside path between Whitefriargate and the Fruit Market for a view of how the centre sits in the water — far more interesting than the shops.
