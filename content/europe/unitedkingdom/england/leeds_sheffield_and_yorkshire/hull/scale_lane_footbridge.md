  Scale Lane Bridge swings open about ten times a week to let larger vessels
  pass up the River Hull. Uniquely, pedestrians are allowed to stay on the
  bridge while it swings — it carries them out into the middle of the river

  and has become one of Hull's quietly memorable city-walking experiences.

The Scale Lane Footbridge connects the Old Town to the regenerated east bank of the River Hull. It is a curving steel structure that pivots on a single base near the eastern shore. The walking surface is decked in hardwood and ends in a small viewing platform.

The bridge is open round the clock when not swinging. The east bank, formerly working wharves, is being gradually redeveloped — the Arctic Corsair will eventually be moored here in a new museum site, and a new pedestrian quarter is taking shape. The view back to the Old Town from the bridge is one of the best in the city.
