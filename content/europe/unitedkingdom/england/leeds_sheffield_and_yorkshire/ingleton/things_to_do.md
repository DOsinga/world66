Ingleton's appeal is almost entirely based on its landscape. The [Ingleton Waterfalls Trail](/europe/unitedkingdom/england/leeds_sheffield_and_yorkshire/ingleton/ingleton_waterfalls_trail) follows the two rivers that meet at the village through a sequence of cascades and gorges — probably the best waterfalls walk in the Yorkshire Dales. [White Scar Cave](/europe/unitedkingdom/england/leeds_sheffield_and_yorkshire/ingleton/white_scar_cave) on the road toward Chapel-le-Dale is the longest show cave in Britain and a worthwhile hour underground.

Above the village, [Ingleborough](/europe/unitedkingdom/england/leeds_sheffield_and_yorkshire/ingleton/ingleborough) is one of the Yorkshire Three Peaks — a long but straightforward climb rewarded with an Iron Age hillfort at the summit and views across the Dales. Between the waterfalls gorge and the summit, the [Twistleton Scars](/europe/unitedkingdom/england/leeds_sheffield_and_yorkshire/ingleton/twistleton_scars) offer a very different experience: stark limestone pavement, largely unvisited, with a lunar quality quite different from the greener Dales valleys.
