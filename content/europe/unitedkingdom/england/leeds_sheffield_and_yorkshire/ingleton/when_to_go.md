---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

The waterfalls trail is best in late autumn and winter when the rivers are in spate — the falls are at their most dramatic after several days of rain. The downside is wet and slippery paths; waterproof boots are essential. Summer brings drier conditions and longer days, which makes for more comfortable walking but less spectacular waterfalls.

Ingleborough is best climbed between April and October. Winter ascents are possible but require experience and the right equipment; the summit plateau can be brutal in bad weather. The cave is open year-round but occasionally closes after heavy rain.

Bank holidays and summer weekends bring visitor numbers that the waterfalls trail can absorb but that fill the car park and the village. A midweek visit in spring or autumn gives the best balance of good conditions and manageable crowds.
