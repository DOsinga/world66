The Museum of Rail Travel at Ingrow, operated by the Vintage Carriages Trust, sits beside Ingrow (West) station on the Keighley and Worth Valley Railway. The collection focuses on historic passenger coaches from the Victorian and Edwardian eras, with several restored to full working order. The carriages range from early six-wheelers to Pullman coaches, all displayed in a purpose-built museum building.

It's a specialist collection that rewards anyone with an interest in railway history — the quality of restoration work is high, and the indoor setting allows close inspection of the vehicles. Combined with a ride on the KWVR itself, Ingrow makes a natural stop on a day out from Keighley.
