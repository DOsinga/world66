Leeds Industrial Museum at Armley Mills tells the story of the city's role in the Industrial Revolution. The setting helps: the museum occupies a former mill, so the machinery, textiles, printing displays, and engineering history sit in the kind of building that shaped modern Leeds.

It is a strong stop if you want to understand why Leeds grew so quickly, and why industry still sits close to the city's identity.
