Middleton Railway runs steam and diesel trains on a short heritage line south of central Leeds. Its history is the real draw: it began in 1758 carrying coal by horse-drawn wagons on wooden rails, making it one of the oldest public railways still in operation.

The ride is only about a mile, so come for the industrial history rather than a grand rail journey. It is an easy family outing and a useful reminder that Leeds was shaped as much by coal and engineering as by shopping arcades and civic buildings.
