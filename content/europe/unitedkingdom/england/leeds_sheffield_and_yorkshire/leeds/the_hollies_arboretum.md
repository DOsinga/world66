The Hollies Arboretum is a wooded botanical garden in north Leeds. It is a quiet place for a walk, with mature trees, planted collections, and a lush setting that feels far from the city centre.

It is most rewarding if you enjoy gardens for their atmosphere rather than formal bedding displays.
