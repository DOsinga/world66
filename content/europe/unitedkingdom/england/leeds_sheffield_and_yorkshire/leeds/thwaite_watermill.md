Thwaite Watermill sits on an island between the River Aire and the Leeds-Liverpool Canal, a reminder of how much of south Leeds was shaped by water power, mills, and controlled waterways. The mill dates from 1820 and has had several industrial lives, which makes it more interesting than a single-purpose heritage display.

It is a good stop if you want to see Leeds beyond the shopping arcades and civic centre. The canal-side setting also makes it easy to fold into a longer walk through the city's industrial landscape.
