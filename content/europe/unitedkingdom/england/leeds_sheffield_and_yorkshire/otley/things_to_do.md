title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Otley's pleasures are mostly outdoor and unhurried. The main draw is [The Chevin](/europe/unitedkingdom/england/leeds_sheffield_and_yorkshire/otley/the_chevin) — the forested ridge that walls the town to the south, offering wide views over Wharfedale and good walking through mixed woodland. Below it, [Wharfemeadows Park](/europe/unitedkingdom/england/leeds_sheffield_and_yorkshire/otley/wharfemeadows_park) runs along the River Wharfe and is as pleasant a stretch of town park as you'll find in Yorkshire.

In the town centre, [All Saints' Church](/europe/unitedkingdom/england/leeds_sheffield_and_yorkshire/otley/all_saints_church) is worth a visit — a substantial medieval church with some good carving and a peaceful churchyard. The [Otley Museum](/europe/unitedkingdom/england/leeds_sheffield_and_yorkshire/otley/otley_museum) is small but informative about the town's history, particularly its role as a printing and market centre.

The market runs on Fridays and Saturdays in the town square, and Otley has an above-average number of independent shops for a town its size. The Buttercross in the market square is a stone market cross that has marked the heart of the town for centuries.

Otley has a bafflingly high density of pubs for its population — around 20 at last count. This is a genuine tradition rather than a tourist gimmick.
