---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

Ripon and its surroundings are accessible year round, but the experience changes significantly with the seasons. Fountains Abbey and Studley Royal are spectacular in autumn when the trees around the water gardens turn colour; the ruins are also at their most atmospheric in low winter light, with few visitors and mist rising from the Skell. Spring brings daffodils along the river walk.

Summer weekends at Brimham Rocks and Fountains Abbey can be crowded. Midweek in May or September is the ideal time: quieter, cheaper, and often warmer than the peak of summer. The nine o'clock horn-blowing in the market square can be seen on any evening of the year.
