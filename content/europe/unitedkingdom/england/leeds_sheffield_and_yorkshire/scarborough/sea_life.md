SEA LIFE Scarborough is a family-focused aquarium on North Bay, with marine tanks, touch-pool-style encounters, and conservation messaging built around sea creatures from Britain and farther afield. It is a commercial attraction, but it has enough local relevance to work in a seaside town where weather can quickly change outdoor plans.

The aquarium is best for families, especially with younger children, or for visitors who want a gentle indoor break from the beach and castle. Adults without children may find it a lower priority than Scarborough's headland, old town, and cliff walks. Book ahead in busy school-holiday periods.
