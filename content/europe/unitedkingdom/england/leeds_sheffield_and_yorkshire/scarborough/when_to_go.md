---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

Scarborough is best visited from May to September, when the weather is most likely to cooperate and the beaches and outdoor activities are fully accessible. July and August are the busiest months, when the town fills with British seaside holidaymakers and accommodation gets both scarcer and pricier. A visit in June or September offers much of the summer experience with fewer crowds.

The town has a quieter, more local character in the off-season. The Stephen Joseph Theatre runs year-round, and the castle and galleries are open through the winter. The North Sea in winter has a dramatic, blustery quality that some visitors find more interesting than the summer holiday scene.
