Sheffield is a city built on steel — and on hills. England's fourth-largest city sits where the River Don meets the Sheaf, at the edge of the Peak District, and the combination of industrial grit and wild moorland is part of what makes it distinctive. The steelworks that once filled the Don Valley are mostly gone, but their legacy runs through the city's museums, its architecture, and its character.

The city centre has been substantially rebuilt since the 1990s, with the [Winter Garden](/europe/unitedkingdom/england/leeds_sheffield_and_yorkshire/sheffield/winter_garden), the [Millennium Gallery](/europe/unitedkingdom/england/leeds_sheffield_and_yorkshire/sheffield/millennium_gallery), and the redeveloped Sheaf Square around the station. The [Crucible Theatre](/europe/unitedkingdom/england/leeds_sheffield_and_yorkshire/sheffield/crucible_theatre) hosts the World Snooker Championship every spring, and the [Peace Gardens](/europe/unitedkingdom/england/leeds_sheffield_and_yorkshire/sheffield/peace_gardens) beside the [Town Hall](/europe/unitedkingdom/england/leeds_sheffield_and_yorkshire/sheffield/sheffield_town_hall) are the focal point of the centre's social life.

[Kelham Island](/europe/unitedkingdom/england/leeds_sheffield_and_yorkshire/sheffield/kelham_island), a former industrial quarter along the Don, has become one of the most interesting neighbourhoods — old workshops turned into craft breweries, independent restaurants, and the excellent [Kelham Island Museum](/europe/unitedkingdom/england/leeds_sheffield_and_yorkshire/sheffield/kelham_island_museum), where a massive River Don Engine still runs on demonstration days. Drink at the [Kelham Island Tavern](/europe/unitedkingdom/england/leeds_sheffield_and_yorkshire/sheffield/kelham_island_tavern), eat at [Cutlery Works](/europe/unitedkingdom/england/leeds_sheffield_and_yorkshire/sheffield/cutlery_works) or [The Milestone](/europe/unitedkingdom/england/leeds_sheffield_and_yorkshire/sheffield/the_milestone), and end the night at [The Leadmill](/europe/unitedkingdom/england/leeds_sheffield_and_yorkshire/sheffield/the_leadmill).

Sheffield has two universities and a large student population, which keeps the nightlife lively and the food scene diverse. The real bonus, though, is the location: the Peak District National Park begins at the city's western edge, and you can walk from the suburbs onto open moorland in under an hour. Stanage Edge, one of the finest gritstone edges in England, is a short drive away.
