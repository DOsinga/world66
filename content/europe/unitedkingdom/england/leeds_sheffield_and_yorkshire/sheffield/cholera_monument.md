The Cholera Monument stands above Sheffield as a stark reminder of the 1832 epidemic that killed hundreds in the growing industrial city. The memorial and surrounding grounds are quiet today, with paths, trees, and views back toward the centre.

It is not a long visit, but it adds a human and public-health chapter to Sheffield's steel and music stories. The walk up from the station or city centre is worthwhile if you like urban history with a view.
