---
title: "Getting There"
type: section
---

**By Train** — Sheffield Station sits on the Midland Main Line, with regular fast services from London St Pancras (just over 2 hours), Birmingham, Manchester, and Leeds. CrossCountry trains run to Edinburgh, Bristol, and the south coast. The station is a 10-minute walk from the city centre.

**By Air** — Sheffield has no airport of its own; the nearest are Doncaster Sheffield (closed 2022 but periodically rumoured to reopen), Manchester (about an hour by car or train), and Leeds Bradford. East Midlands Airport is also within reach.

**By Road** — The M1 motorway runs east of the city centre; Junction 33 or Junction 34 are the standard exits. Parking in the centre is expensive but plentiful. Long-distance coach services run to Pond Street bus interchange in the city centre.
