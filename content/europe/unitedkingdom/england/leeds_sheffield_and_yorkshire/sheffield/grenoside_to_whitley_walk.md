The Grenoside to Whitley walk is an easy four-mile loop through woodland, small valleys, and villages on Sheffield's northern edge. It is the kind of walk that shows how quickly the city gives way to South Yorkshire countryside.

The route is gentle enough for a half-day outing and works especially well if you want fresh air without committing to a full Peak District trip. Its pub start and finish is a practical bonus.
