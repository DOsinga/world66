The Lyceum Theatre is Sheffield's grandest traditional theatre, a restored Edwardian house with a richly decorated auditorium and a strong central location near Tudor Square. Its programme brings in touring drama, musicals, comedy, opera, ballet, and family shows, so it is one of the best ways to turn a Sheffield evening into more than a pub crawl.

Even if you are not seeing a performance, the building helps explain Sheffield's civic confidence at the turn of the 20th century. For visitors, the practical advice is simple: check the schedule early, because the value of the Lyceum depends entirely on what is playing. It pairs naturally with the Crucible and the city's compact cultural quarter.
