Shepherd Wheel Workshop is a restored water-powered cutlery workshop on Porter Brook, showing the small-scale industry that helped make Sheffield famous for metalwork. The Grade II-listed site keeps the wheel and workshop machinery in place, giving a clear picture of how grinders and cutlers worked before large factories took over.

It is a compact museum, best paired with a walk in the Porter Valley.
