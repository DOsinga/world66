The Steelworker is a piece of public art that speaks directly to Sheffield's industrial identity. It is not a museum display or a nostalgic set-piece, but a street-level reminder that steelmaking shaped the city's labour, politics, and pride.

See it as part of a walk through central Sheffield rather than as a standalone pilgrimage. The sculpture works best when paired with the city's surviving industrial architecture, its cutlery heritage, and newer public spaces that show how Sheffield has rebuilt around that history.
