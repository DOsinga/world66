The Turner Museum of Glass is a specialist collection at the University of Sheffield, focused on glass as art, craft, and material experiment. It is a small museum, but the subject fits Sheffield's broader story of making and industrial skill.

Go when you want a precise, quiet collection rather than a blockbuster attraction. The interest lies in colour, form, technique, and the way a city known for metalwork also has room for this more fragile kind of material culture.
