Wyming Brook Nature Reserve is a wooded gorge on Sheffield's western edge, with rocky paths, stream crossings, and routes that can be extended toward Redmires Reservoirs and Stanage Edge. It is one of the best quick escapes from the city into the Peak District landscape.

Wear shoes with grip, especially after rain, and allow more time if you continue toward Hathersage.
