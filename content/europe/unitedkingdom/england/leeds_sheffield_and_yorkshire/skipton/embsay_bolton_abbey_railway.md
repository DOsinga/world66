The Embsay & Bolton Abbey Railway is a preserved line just outside Skipton, running through a short but handsome stretch of Yorkshire Dales countryside. Trains usually operate between Embsay and Bolton Abbey, with restored stations, vintage carriages, and the slow rhythm that makes heritage railways appealing even to people who are not train obsessives.

It pairs naturally with Bolton Abbey or a day around Skipton, and it is one of the area's easiest family outings. Check the timetable before you go, since services vary by season and special events can change the feel of the trip. When steam is running, the journey has enough character to justify the detour from the town centre.
