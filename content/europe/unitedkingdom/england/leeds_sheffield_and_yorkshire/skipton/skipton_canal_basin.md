Skipton Canal Basin is the town's gentlest counterweight to the castle and market street. Narrowboats gather here on the Leeds and Liverpool Canal, with short cruises, towpath walks, waterside pubs, and a slower rhythm that suits Skipton's role as a gateway to the Dales.

It is not grand, but it is very usable. Come for a walk after the castle, or use the basin as the start of a longer towpath stretch if you want countryside without immediately committing to hill walking. On a busy day, the boats and stone warehouses give the town a lived-in canal character rather than a museum-piece feel.
