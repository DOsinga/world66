The South Pennines is a landscape where the ruins of the textile industry — cotton around Todmorden, wool everywhere else — dissolve into steep moorland valleys and mill-side streams. Spanning the municipal boroughs of Bradford, Calderdale and Kirklees in West Yorkshire, it feels like a place caught between eras: handsome stone towns, derelict mill towers, and some of the best walking in northern England.

[Hebden Bridge](/europe/unitedkingdom/england/leeds_sheffield_and_yorkshire/hebden_bridge) is the most visited town in the area and worth the attention. The valley at [Hardcastle Crags](/europe/unitedkingdom/england/leeds_sheffield_and_yorkshire/south_pennines_yo/hardcastle_crags) just above it — where two rivers meet in woodland owned by the National Trust — offers excellent walking without much effort. [Gibson Mill](/europe/unitedkingdom/england/leeds_sheffield_and_yorkshire/south_pennines_yo/gibson_mill) at its heart was one of the first mills to switch from water to steam power, and has been restored as a visitor centre that runs entirely off-grid.

[Haworth](/europe/unitedkingdom/england/leeds_sheffield_and_yorkshire/bradford) is Brontë country: the parsonage where the sisters lived is now a museum and still draws literary pilgrims despite the gift-shop overlay around it. [Todmorden](/europe/unitedkingdom/england/leeds_sheffield_and_yorkshire/todmorden) and Ripponden are quieter and more workaday, but both have mill ruins worth poking around. The [Rochdale Canal](/europe/unitedkingdom/england/leeds_sheffield_and_yorkshire/south_pennines_yo/rochdale_canal) runs through the region and makes for good towpath walking along the valley floors, and the [Pennine Way](/europe/unitedkingdom/england/leeds_sheffield_and_yorkshire/south_pennines_yo/pennine_way) climbs out of the Calder Valley toward [Stoodley Pike](/europe/unitedkingdom/england/leeds_sheffield_and_yorkshire/south_pennines_yo/stoodley_pike), the gritstone obelisk that dominates the southern skyline. For cyclists, the long [Cragg Vale](/europe/unitedkingdom/england/leeds_sheffield_and_yorkshire/south_pennines_yo/cragg_vale) climb out of Mytholmroyd is one of the most-ridden ascents in northern England.

The old Industrial Museum in Halifax, just outside the area's boundary, is one of the best museums of its kind in England.
