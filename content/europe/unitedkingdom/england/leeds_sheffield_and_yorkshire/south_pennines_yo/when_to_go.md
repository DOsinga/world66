---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

**Late spring (May to June)** is the loveliest moment. The hawthorns are out, bluebells fill the woodland at Hardcastle Crags, and the moorland heather is still green; days are long and the weather is generally drier than later in the summer.

**Summer (July to August)** is the warmest stretch and the heather purples on the tops in August, but it can be busy on weekends with day-trippers from the cities.

**Autumn (October to early November)** brings dramatic light, copper-coloured woods along the canal towpaths, and quiet trails. The moors take on a sombre brown.

**Winter** is bleak, often wet, and the days are short — but the bare landscape has its own austere appeal, and the pubs in Hebden Bridge and Todmorden are at their cosiest. Pennine Way walking in January is for serious walkers only; lower walking along the canals is fine in most conditions.
