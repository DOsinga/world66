---
title: "Eating Out"
type: section
---

Wakefield's restaurant scene is small but improving, with most of the better places clustered on Westgate and around the Hepworth. The pub food is generally reliable; for a more substantial meal a number of independent restaurants now do good modern British cooking using produce from the surrounding farms (including, in season, the celebrated forced rhubarb).

The cafe at the [Hepworth Wakefield](/europe/unitedkingdom/england/leeds_sheffield_and_yorkshire/wakefield/hepworth_wakefield) is one of the best lunch options in town. For Sunday roasts, several of the village pubs around Sandal and Crigglestone are worth the short drive.
