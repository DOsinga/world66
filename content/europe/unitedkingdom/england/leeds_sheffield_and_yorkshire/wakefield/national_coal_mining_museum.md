The National Coal Mining Museum for England, about ten miles west of Wakefield at the former Caphouse Colliery, offers what is probably the best mine tour in Britain: a real descent of one hundred and forty metres into a working pit, led by ex-miners with first-hand experience of underground work. The tour lasts about an hour underground and gives a vivid sense of what coal mining was actually like, from medieval pillar-and-stall workings to modern long-wall faces.

Above ground the museum continues with exhibits on the social history of coalfield communities, the pit-village world that built much of West Yorkshire, and the long aftermath of the 1984–85 strike. Admission is free; the underground tour requires booking and warm clothing.
