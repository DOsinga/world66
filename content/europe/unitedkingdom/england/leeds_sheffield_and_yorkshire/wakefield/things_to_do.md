Wakefield's two principal attractions are both contemporary galleries set in older landscapes. [The Hepworth Wakefield](/europe/unitedkingdom/england/leeds_sheffield_and_yorkshire/wakefield/hepworth_wakefield) on the River Calder houses the most substantial public collection of Barbara Hepworth's work anywhere, in a David Chipperfield building that is itself a sculpture. The [Yorkshire Sculpture Park](/europe/unitedkingdom/england/leeds_sheffield_and_yorkshire/wakefield/yorkshire_sculpture_park) just outside town displays large-scale outdoor work — Henry Moore bronzes, Andy Goldsworthy installations, rotating shows — across five hundred acres of parkland.

Wakefield is also one of the oldest market towns in West Yorkshire and rewards a slow walk: the medieval [Cathedral](/europe/unitedkingdom/england/leeds_sheffield_and_yorkshire/wakefield/wakefield_cathedral), the rare [Chantry Chapel](/europe/unitedkingdom/england/leeds_sheffield_and_yorkshire/wakefield/chantry_chapel) on the bridge over the Calder, and the earthwork remains of [Sandal Castle](/europe/unitedkingdom/england/leeds_sheffield_and_yorkshire/wakefield/sandal_castle) — site of one of the famous battles of the Wars of the Roses — are all easy to combine with a gallery visit.
