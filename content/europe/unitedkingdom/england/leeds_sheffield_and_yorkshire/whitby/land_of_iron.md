Land of Iron tells the story of the Cleveland ironstone industry, with guided tours into a former drift mine at Skinningrove. The underground section is the reason to come: it makes the heavy, dirty work behind Teesside's iron and steel history much easier to understand.

Allow about 90 minutes for the tour. From Whitby it works best as a focused industrial-history stop on a wider coast trip toward Saltburn.
