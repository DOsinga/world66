---

The cliff above the east side is the obvious starting point: climb the [199 steps](/europe/unitedkingdom/england/leeds_sheffield_and_yorkshire/whitby/199_steps) to [St Mary's Church](/europe/unitedkingdom/england/leeds_sheffield_and_yorkshire/whitby/st_marys_church) and the ruins of [Whitby Abbey](/europe/unitedkingdom/england/leeds_sheffield_and_yorkshire/whitby/whitby_abbey) beyond. The graveyard, the abbey, and the view back over the harbour are exactly the scenes that Bram Stoker used for Dracula's arrival in England.

Down in the old town, the [Captain Cook Memorial Museum](/europe/unitedkingdom/england/leeds_sheffield_and_yorkshire/whitby/captain_cook_museum) is where the young Cook lodged as an apprentice. The eccentric [Whitby Museum](/europe/unitedkingdom/england/leeds_sheffield_and_yorkshire/whitby/whitby_museum) in Pannett Park keeps fossils, ship models, and the famous Hand of Glory. The town is also the historic centre of the [Whitby jet](/europe/unitedkingdom/england/leeds_sheffield_and_yorkshire/whitby/whitby_jet) industry — most of the small jewellery shops on Church Street and Sandgate sell the real thing.

Walk the [piers and lighthouses](/europe/unitedkingdom/england/leeds_sheffield_and_yorkshire/whitby/west_pier_lighthouse) out into the North Sea, watch the [Swing Bridge](/europe/unitedkingdom/england/leeds_sheffield_and_yorkshire/whitby/whitby_swing_bridge) open for fishing boats, and find the [whalebone arch](/europe/unitedkingdom/england/leeds_sheffield_and_yorkshire/whitby/whalebone_arch) on the west cliff, framing the abbey from across the harbour. Twice a year the [Whitby Goth Weekend](/europe/unitedkingdom/england/leeds_sheffield_and_yorkshire/whitby/whitby_goth_weekend) turns the streets into a Victorian costume parade.
