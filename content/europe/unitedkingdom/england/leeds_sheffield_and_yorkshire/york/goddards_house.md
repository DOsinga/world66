Goddards House is the former family home of Noel Goddard Terry, part of the Terry's chocolate family. The 1920s Arts and Crafts house has rooms open to visitors and gardens that can be explored in full.

It is outside York's old city core, so it is most useful for visitors who have already seen the headline sights or who are interested in domestic architecture, gardens, and the city's chocolate-making families.
