St Martin-le-Grand is a small but rewarding church on Coney Street, with a 15th-century core, a restored west window and a handsome 17th-century clock on the facade. The building was badly damaged in a 1942 air raid, then carefully repaired rather than erased from York's busy shopping street.

It is not a major York sight, but it is exactly the kind of detail that rewards slow walking in the city centre. Step in if it is open, or at least pause outside for the clock and surviving medieval fabric.
