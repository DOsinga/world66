The Maltings near Lendal Bridge is one of those pubs that serious drinkers of cask ale seek out by reputation. The selection of Yorkshire ales on handpump rotates continuously, drawn from breweries across the county — Timothy Taylor, Black Sheep, Roosters, and smaller independents all appear regularly. The pub is unpretentious to the point of austerity: no food beyond bar snacks, no background music trying too hard, no décor that has been carefully distressed to look old. It is simply a good pub.

The clientele mixes locals, students, and visitors who have done their research. The bar staff know the beers well and are helpful about what is currently on. The location near the river and Lendal Bridge makes it a natural first or last stop on a city-centre walk. It is reliably busy on weekend evenings but rarely unpleasant.
