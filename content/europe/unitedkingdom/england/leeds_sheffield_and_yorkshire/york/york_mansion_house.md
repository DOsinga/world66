York Mansion House is the official residence of the Lord Mayor of York, built in 1732 and still one of the city's grand civic interiors. The rooms show off furniture, ceramics, glass, art, and an important collection of civic gold and silver.

The restored 18th-century kitchen is the surprise. Cooking demonstrations make the house feel more lived-in than many formal Georgian interiors.
