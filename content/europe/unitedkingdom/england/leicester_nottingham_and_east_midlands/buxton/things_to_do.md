---
title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Buxton's appeal divides neatly into two categories: the Georgian and Victorian spa town within the town itself, and the Peak District wilderness that begins at its edges. The Crescent, the Opera House, and the Devonshire Dome are the architectural highlights; Poole's Cavern, Solomon's Temple, and the trails into the national park are where you go when the weather cooperates.
