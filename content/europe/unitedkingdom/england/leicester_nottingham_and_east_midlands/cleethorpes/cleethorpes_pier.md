Cleethorpes Pier is modest now, but it still gives the town centre its seaside focus. When it opened in 1873 it stretched much farther into the Humber; wartime shortening and later rebuilding left the compact pier you see today.

It is most useful as a marker for a walk: north toward the station and Grimsby, or south toward the better beach, boating lake, and miniature railway.
