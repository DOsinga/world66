Meridian Park marks the point where the Greenwich Meridian reaches the coast. It is a small, quick stop rather than a major sight, but there is something satisfying about standing on zero degrees longitude with the Humber and North Sea air in front of you.

The marker works best as part of a longer walk or cycle down the promenade from the pier toward the south end of Cleethorpes.
