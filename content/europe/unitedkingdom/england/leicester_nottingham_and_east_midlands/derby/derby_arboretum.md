Derby Arboretum is an important park in British urban history, commonly cited as England's first public arboretum. Designed in the nineteenth century, it reflects the era's belief that landscaped green space could improve city life.

Today it works as both neighborhood park and heritage landscape. Visitors interested in public gardens, urban planning, or Derby's Victorian history will find more meaning here than in an ordinary local green.
