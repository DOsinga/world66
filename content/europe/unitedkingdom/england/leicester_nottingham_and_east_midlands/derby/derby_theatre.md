Derby Theatre is the city's main producing theatre and an active cultural venue rather than a static sight. Its value for visitors depends on the programme, but it is a legitimate stop for anyone looking for evening culture in Derby.

Check what is on before making a special trip. For theatre-minded travellers, it can round out a Derby visit that otherwise leans toward industrial heritage, the cathedral quarter, and nearby Peak District excursions.
