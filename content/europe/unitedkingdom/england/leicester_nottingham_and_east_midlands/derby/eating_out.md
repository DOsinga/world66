---
title: Eating Out
type: section
---

Derby's restaurant scene punches above its weight for a city of its size. The Cathedral Quarter and Sadler Gate in the old town centre concentrate most of the independent restaurants, while the Intu Derby shopping centre brings chain dining for those who want it. The city has a strong South Asian community, and some of the best value eating is in the curry houses around the Normanton Road corridor to the south.
