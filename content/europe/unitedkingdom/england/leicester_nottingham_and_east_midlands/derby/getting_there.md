---
title: Getting There
type: section
---

Derby sits on the Midland Main Line, with fast trains to London St Pancras in around 90 minutes and regular services to Sheffield, Leeds, and Nottingham. East Midlands Parkway station, a few miles south, connects to East Midlands Airport with a short taxi or bus ride. The city is also well positioned on the M1 motorway — Junction 25 is the main exit — making it easy to reach by car from most of England.

National Express coaches serve Derby from London Victoria and other major cities, arriving at Derby Bus Station adjacent to the train station.
