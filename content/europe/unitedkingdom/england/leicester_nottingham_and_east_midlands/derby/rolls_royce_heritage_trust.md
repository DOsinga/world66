Rolls-Royce has been making aero engines in Derby since 1908, and the Rolls-Royce Heritage Trust operates a museum on the Sinfin site that traces that history from the first Silver Ghost to the Trent engines that power modern wide-bodied jets. The collection includes complete engines, workshop equipment, archival photographs, and personal items from engineers who worked through the great eras of aviation.

Access is by pre-booked guided tour only — the museum is on an active industrial site and unaccompanied visits are not permitted. Tours run on specific days and book up well in advance. The experience is worth the planning: you see the actual engines rather than scale models, and the guides are typically retired engineers who worked on the machines they are describing. Check the Heritage Trust website for current tour availability.
