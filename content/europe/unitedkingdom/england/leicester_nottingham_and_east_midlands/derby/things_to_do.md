title: Things to Do
type: section
---

Derby's industrial heritage is its greatest draw. The Derwent Valley Mills corridor, running north from the city to Cromford, was inscribed as a UNESCO World Heritage Site in 2001 — the stretch of river where water-powered textile machinery transformed how goods were made and the world was organised. Within the city, the cathedral quarter rewards an afternoon of wandering, and the Derby Museum holds a collection that would be the envy of far larger cities.
