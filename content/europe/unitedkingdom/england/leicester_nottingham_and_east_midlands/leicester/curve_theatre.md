Curve Theatre is Leicester's main contemporary performance venue, set in the Cultural Quarter rather than hidden inside the older civic core. It stages musicals, drama, dance, comedy, and touring productions, and its open, modern building is part of the point.

Check the programme before you go, because the value of the visit depends heavily on what is on. Even without tickets, it helps anchor a wander through Leicester's regenerated cultural streets east of the centre.
