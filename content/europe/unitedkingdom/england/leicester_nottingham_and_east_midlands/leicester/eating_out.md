---
title: "Eating Out"
type: section
---

Leicester's food scene is defined by its South Asian community, particularly the Gujarati vegetarian restaurants along Belgrave Road. This is some of the most authentic Indian vegetarian cooking in Britain, with mithai shops, chaat counters, and full-service restaurants offering thali, dosas, and sweets unavailable elsewhere in England. Beyond the Golden Mile, the city centre has a decent spread of cafes and independent restaurants.
