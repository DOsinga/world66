---
title: "Getting Around"
type: section
---

Lincoln's centre is compact and walkable, but it is *vertical* — Steep Hill is genuinely steep, and travellers with reduced mobility will find it hard going. Most visitors plan a single Uphill loop and a separate Downhill loop, rather than constantly bouncing between the two.

**Walking.** From the railway station in Downhill to the cathedral square is about 15 minutes if you take the climb in one go, with several stop-and-catch-your-breath spots along the way.

**Bus.** The Walk and Ride service (route 1) loops between the railway station, Castle Square at the top of the hill, and back down — useful if the climb is not feasible. Tickets are cheap; buy from the driver.

**Bike.** Lincoln has expanded its bike-share scheme around the central campus area; bikes are useful for the riverside Witham trail but a struggle on the cliff.

**Taxi.** Black cabs and licensed minicabs are plentiful, especially at the railway station and Castle Square ranks.
