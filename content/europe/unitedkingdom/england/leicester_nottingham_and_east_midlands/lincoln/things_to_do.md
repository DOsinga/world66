---
title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Lincoln divides into two halves separated by the dramatic limestone ridge that gives the city its shape. The **Uphill** quarter, on top of the cliff, is where most of the famous sights cluster — the cathedral, the castle, Bailgate, the Roman gates, the Bishop's Palace, the city's best museums. The **Downhill** quarter spreads along the River Witham and Brayford Pool — High Street shops, the medieval Stonebow, the riverside restaurants, the railway station.

Between the two, Steep Hill drops 60 metres in a quarter of a mile, lined the whole way with small shops, tea rooms, and timber-framed houses. The hill is the city's great signature walk and, by some accounts, the steepest historic street in England. Allow a leisurely full day for the Uphill quarter, half a day for Downhill and the river.
