---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

**Late spring to early autumn (May–September)** is the easiest time to enjoy Lincoln. Long days, mostly settled weather, the riverside terraces in use, and the open-air cathedral concerts running through July and August.

**Christmas market (first weekend of December)** is the city's biggest event of the year — one of the largest and oldest Christmas markets in Britain, drawing several hundred thousand visitors over a long weekend with stalls running through the cathedral close, Castle Square, and Steep Hill. The atmosphere is genuinely magical; book accommodation months ahead, and expect significant crowds.

**Winter outside the market season (January–February)** is quiet and atmospheric — fog often rolls up the cliff, the cathedral towers float above it, the pubs are warm. A good time for a low-key cathedral visit.

**Autumn (October–November)** has decent weather, smaller crowds, and the city's smaller festivals (the Steampunk weekend in late August has become a fixture).
