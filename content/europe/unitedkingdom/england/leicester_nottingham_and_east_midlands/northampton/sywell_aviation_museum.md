Sywell Aviation Museum is a volunteer-run collection at Sywell Aerodrome, housed in wartime Nissen huts and focused mainly on military aviation. The displays cover aircraft, uniforms, equipment, photographs, and local airfield history.

It is a modest museum rather than a large national collection, but it is worth the detour for aviation history enthusiasts, especially if you are already exploring Northamptonshire by car.
