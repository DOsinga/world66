Lawrence grew up in Eastwood, a mining village ten miles north-west of Nottingham, and his 1913 autobiographical novel is set in and around the coalfields of the city's hinterland. The book follows Paul Morel's coming of age in a Nottinghamshire pit village, his complicated relationship with his mother, and his attempts to escape both. The landscape — the slag heaps, the woods, the trains running into Nottingham — is rendered with extraordinary specificity.
