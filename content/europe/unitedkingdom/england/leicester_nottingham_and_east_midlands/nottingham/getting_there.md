---
title: "Getting There"
type: section
---

Nottingham sits on the Midland Main Line between London St Pancras and Sheffield; **East Midlands Railway** trains from St Pancras take just under two hours. CrossCountry services connect Nottingham with Birmingham (about 75 minutes), the North East and Edinburgh, and the South West via Birmingham.

**By road** the city is at the junction of the M1 (junctions 24, 25 and 26) and A52, well placed for the East Midlands and the southern Peak District. **East Midlands Airport** is 15 miles south-west with a regular Skylink bus into the city; it handles mostly low-cost European routes. Birmingham is the nearest airport for long-haul.

Nottingham Station is a 15-minute walk south of the centre or two stops on the **NET tram**, which is the easiest way to reach Old Market Square.
