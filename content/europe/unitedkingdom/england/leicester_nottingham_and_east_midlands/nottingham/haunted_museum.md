Nottingham's Haunted Museum is a compact, theatrical collection of witchcraft, haunted objects, and pop-culture horror. It is more oddball than essential, but it fits the city well if you like your museums strange and a little macabre.

Pair it with the nearby caves and Lace Market sights rather than making a special trip across town.
