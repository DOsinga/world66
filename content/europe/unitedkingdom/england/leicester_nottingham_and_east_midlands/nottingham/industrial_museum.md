Nottingham Industrial Museum tells the county's industrial story through engines, machines, transport, telecommunications, and local manufacturing exhibits. Its location in the Wollaton Hall stable block makes it easy to combine with one of Nottingham's best-known park-and-museum sites.

The museum is especially good for visitors interested in working machinery and local industry rather than polished art displays. Check opening times carefully, as specialist museums like this can have limited hours.
