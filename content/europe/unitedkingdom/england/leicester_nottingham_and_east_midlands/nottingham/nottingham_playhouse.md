Nottingham Playhouse is one of the city's main theatres and a strong piece of 1960s modernist architecture. The resident company has a long national reputation, but the building is worth a look even if you are not seeing a show.

Outside the entrance is Sky Mirror, Anish Kapoor's polished steel dish that tilts the sky down into the square. It gives the theatre forecourt a public-art stop as well as a performance venue.
