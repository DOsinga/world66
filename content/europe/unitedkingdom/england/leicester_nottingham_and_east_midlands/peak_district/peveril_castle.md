Peveril Castle is the ruined Norman castle above Castleton, with a surviving 12th-century keep and a steep approach from the village. The reward is the view over Cave Dale, Peak Cavern, and the surrounding limestone hills.

The site is managed by English Heritage. The path is paved but climbs hard, so allow time for the ascent.
