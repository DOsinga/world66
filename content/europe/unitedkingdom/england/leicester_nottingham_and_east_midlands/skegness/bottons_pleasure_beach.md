Bottons Pleasure Beach is Skegness's seafront funfair, with rides, arcades, and family amusements beside the beach. Entry to the park is free, with rides paid for by tokens or wristbands.

It is not a heritage sight, but it is one of the classic Skegness family stops and gives the resort its old-fashioned seaside energy.
