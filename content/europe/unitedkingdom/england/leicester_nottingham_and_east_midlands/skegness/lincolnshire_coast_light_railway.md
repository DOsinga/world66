A 2 ft narrow-gauge heritage line running along part of the Skegness Water Leisure Park at Walls Lane, a few kilometres north of the town. The line is short — barely a third of a mile of track — but the locomotives are genuine pieces of industrial history: ex-military diesels and small steam engines that once worked on construction sites, peat bogs, and military depots around Britain.

Trains run on weekends and bank holidays through the summer, with extra services during school holidays. Tickets are cheap and unlimited for the day. The volunteer crew are happy to talk about the engines if you show any interest, and the workshop is usually open so you can see locomotives under restoration. A useful diversion for a rainy hour if you have children, or for any railway enthusiast.
