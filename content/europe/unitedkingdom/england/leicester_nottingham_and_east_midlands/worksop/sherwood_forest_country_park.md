Sherwood Forest Country Park protects the most famous part of the old Sherwood Forest, with walking routes through ancient oaks and displays on the forest's history and ecology. The Robin Hood associations draw the crowds, but the veteran trees are the real reason to come.

The visitor facilities make it an easy day trip from Worksop, though weekends and school holidays can be busy.
