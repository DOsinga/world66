The 7 July Memorial in Hyde Park commemorates the 52 people killed in the London bombings of 7 July 2005. It is made from 52 stainless steel columns, grouped to reflect the four attack sites.

It is a quiet, sober stop on the edge of the park, close enough to combine with other central London sights.
