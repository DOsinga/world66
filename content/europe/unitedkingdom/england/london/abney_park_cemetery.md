Abney Park Cemetery is one of London's Victorian garden cemeteries, now softened into a leafy woodland of leaning monuments, tangled paths, and quiet corners. It is less polished than Highgate or Brompton, which is part of the appeal: the place feels half cemetery, half urban nature reserve.

The old chapel, carved memorials, and free guided walks make it worth seeking out if you are exploring north-east London. It is also a good break from the traffic and noise around Stoke Newington.
