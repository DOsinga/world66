Alexandra Palace is a grand Victorian landmark on high ground in north London, best known as the site of the BBC's early regular television broadcasts. The building is used for exhibitions and events, but the wider draw is the hilltop setting: Alexandra Park has a boating lake, open lawns, an ice rink, and broad views across the city.
