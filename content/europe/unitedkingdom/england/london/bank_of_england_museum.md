The Bank of England Museum tells the story of the bank from its founding in 1694 to the present day. Displays cover banknotes, gold, financial crises, and the role of the Bank in the City of London.

One of the memorable exhibits lets visitors handle a real gold bar, making this a compact but worthwhile stop for anyone interested in money, trade, or the Square Mile.
