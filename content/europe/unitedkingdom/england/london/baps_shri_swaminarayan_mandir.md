BAPS Shri Swaminarayan Mandir in Neasden is one of London's most striking modern religious buildings. Built as a traditional Hindu stone temple, it was the first of its kind in Europe and is still a major visitor sight as well as an active place of worship.

Go for the carved stonework, the calm interior, and the chance to see a major Hindu temple far from the usual central London circuit.
