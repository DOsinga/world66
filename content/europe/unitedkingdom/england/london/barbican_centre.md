The Barbican Centre is a major arts complex in the City of London, with concert halls, theatres, cinemas, galleries, and a conservatory set within a large Brutalist estate. It is worth visiting for performances, exhibitions, or simply to explore one of London's most distinctive postwar architectural landscapes.
