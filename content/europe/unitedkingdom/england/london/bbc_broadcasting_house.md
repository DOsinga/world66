BBC Broadcasting House is the corporation's historic London headquarters, with the 1930s Old Broadcasting House at its core. Look for the Prospero and Ariel sculpture above the entrance and the Art Deco details around the Portland Place facade.

It is mainly an exterior stop rather than a full visitor attraction, but it is a worthwhile detour for anyone interested in British broadcasting, architecture, or media history while walking around Oxford Circus and Fitzrovia.
