Benjamin Franklin House is the only surviving home where Franklin lived, tucked just off Trafalgar Square. He stayed here for many years before the American Revolution, and the museum uses the rooms to tell the story of his London life, his science, and his politics.

It is a compact visit rather than a grand house museum, but unusually good for anyone interested in the Atlantic world of the 18th century.
