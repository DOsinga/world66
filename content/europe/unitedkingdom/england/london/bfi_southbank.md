BFI Southbank is the British Film Institute's riverside home under Waterloo Bridge. Its screens focus on classic, international, silent, and less mainstream films, with a film bookshop and spaces tied to the BFI archive.

The free Mediatheque is the extra reason to stop even if you are not seeing a film, letting visitors explore parts of the national film and television collection.
