Amis's 1989 novel is set in a London of pubs, snooker halls, and a gathering sense of apocalypse — somewhere in a decaying Ladbroke Grove. The city it describes, with its layered class anxieties and moral exhaustion, is both a period document and still uncomfortably recognisable. Amis was writing at the height of Thatcherism, and London's inequalities are embedded in every page.
