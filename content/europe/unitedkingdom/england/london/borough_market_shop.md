Borough Market is London's most famous food market and one of the best in Europe — a covered market under Victorian railway arches at London Bridge, selling produce, bread, cheese, meat, fish, olives, spices, and prepared foods from independent traders and artisan producers. It has operated on this site in various forms since the 13th century.

Saturday is the main market day when the stalls are all open and the atmosphere is best. Come hungry: the hot food traders (salt beef sandwiches, venison burgers, Spanish tortilla, Ethiopian injera) make for an excellent lunch eaten standing up. The permanent traders include Neal's Yard Dairy (the best cheese shop in London) and Brindisa (Spanish produce).
