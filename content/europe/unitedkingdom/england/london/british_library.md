The British Library is the United Kingdom's national library and one of the great research libraries of the world. For visitors, the main draw is the free Treasures Gallery, where displays can include medieval manuscripts, early printed books, maps, music, and literary documents.

The building is next to St Pancras and King's Cross, so it works well as a short cultural stop before or after a train journey.
