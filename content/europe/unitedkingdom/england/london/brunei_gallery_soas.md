The Brunei Gallery at SOAS hosts changing exhibitions on cultures and art from Asia, Africa and the Middle East. It is a public gallery as well as a university resource, with a programme that can range from historical material to contemporary work.

Its location near the British Museum makes it an easy addition for visitors already in Bloomsbury.
