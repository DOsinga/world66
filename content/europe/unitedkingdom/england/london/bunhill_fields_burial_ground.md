Bunhill Fields is a small nonconformist burial ground on the edge of the City, best known for the graves of William Blake, Daniel Defoe, and John Bunyan.

It is a quiet, atmospheric pause between Shoreditch, Clerkenwell, and the City, with old tombstones set close among the paths and trees.
