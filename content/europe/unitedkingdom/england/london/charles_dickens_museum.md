The Charles Dickens Museum occupies the writer's former Bloomsbury home, where he lived in the late 1830s while writing early novels such as *Oliver Twist*. The rooms hold manuscripts, paintings, furniture, personal objects, and displays that connect Dickens's domestic life with the London he turned into fiction.
