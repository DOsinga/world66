Chelsea Old Church stands by the Thames and has roots going back to the 12th century. It is closely linked with Thomas More, whose statue stands outside, and it is known for the chained books kept inside the church.

The church was badly damaged during World War II and carefully rebuilt, so it combines deep local history with a visible story of wartime loss and repair. It makes a good quiet stop on a walk through Chelsea or along the river.
