The underground complex beneath King Charles Street where Winston Churchill and the War Cabinet directed the Allied war effort from 1939 to 1945. The Cabinet War Room itself has been preserved as it was on the day the war ended: maps still pinned to the walls, phones in their cradles, the transatlantic phone disguised as a toilet cubicle. Upstairs, a separate Churchill Museum traces his life. Busy at weekends; go on a weekday morning for room to breathe.
