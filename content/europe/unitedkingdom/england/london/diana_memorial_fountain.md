The Diana Memorial Fountain is less a traditional fountain than a shallow oval stream flowing through Hyde Park. Its low granite channel was designed to be touched and crossed, which gives it a very different mood from London's more formal royal memorials.

On warm days children paddle in the water and families settle on the grass around it. It is a small stop, but a good one if you are already crossing the park between Kensington and Knightsbridge.
