Fenton House is a 17th-century National Trust house in Hampstead. It is known for its collection of early keyboard instruments, along with art, porcelain, and domestic rooms that show the scale of an old Hampstead merchant's house.

The garden and frequent recitals give it more life than a simple period interior. It pairs well with a walk on Hampstead Heath or through Hampstead village.
