Gray's Inn is one of London's four Inns of Court, the old legal societies that still shape the barristers' world. Its courts, hall, chapel, and gardens sit behind the traffic of Holborn, and the best visit is a quiet wander when the gates are open. It is less famous than Temple, but the sense of a self-contained legal quarter is just as strong.
