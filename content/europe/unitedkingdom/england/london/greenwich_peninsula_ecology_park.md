Greenwich Peninsula Ecology Park is a small wetland reserve tucked into the modern development around the peninsula. Ponds, reedbeds, boardwalks, and quiet paths make it a good place to look for birds, dragonflies, and other urban wildlife. It is a short, low-key nature stop near the Thames Path and the O2.
