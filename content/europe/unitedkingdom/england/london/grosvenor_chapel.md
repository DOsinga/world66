Grosvenor Chapel is a small Georgian church in Mayfair, built in 1739. Its plain classical proportions became a model for several churches in the northeastern United States.

It is a quiet stop rather than a major London sight, but worth noticing if you are walking between Bond Street, Hyde Park, and the older corners of Mayfair.
