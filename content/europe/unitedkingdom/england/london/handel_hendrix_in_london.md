Handel & Hendrix in London links two very different music stories in neighbouring Mayfair houses. George Frideric Handel lived and worked here in the 18th century, while Jimi Hendrix later lived next door in the late 1960s.

The museum is a strong stop for music fans, with period rooms, exhibitions, and a close look at how both musicians used London as a creative base.
