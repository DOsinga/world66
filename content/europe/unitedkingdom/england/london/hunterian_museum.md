The Hunterian Museum, inside the Royal College of Surgeons, explores the history of surgery through anatomical specimens, medical instruments, natural history material and art. It grew from the collection of John Hunter, one of the founders of scientific surgery. The displays are fascinating but graphic, so it is best for visitors with a real interest in medicine or anatomy.
