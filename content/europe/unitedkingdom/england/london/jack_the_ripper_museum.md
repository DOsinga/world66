The Jack The Ripper Museum is a small Victorian-house museum near Cable Street, with several floors devoted to the Whitechapel murders and the police investigation around them. Displays recreate period rooms and scenes connected to the case.

It is a niche stop and will appeal most to visitors already interested in Victorian crime history or walking the East End. Booking ahead can save time at busy periods.
