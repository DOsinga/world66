Lambeth Palace has been the London residence of the Archbishop of Canterbury since the 13th century. The complex faces the Thames opposite Westminster and mixes medieval, Tudor, and later buildings behind its walls.

Visits usually require a booked tour, so it is not a casual walk-in sight. Even from outside, it adds useful depth to the Westminster riverfront, where church, parliament, and palace have faced each other for centuries.
