The Leadenhall Building is the City skyscraper nicknamed the Cheesegrater, a sharply tapering Richard Rogers design completed in 2014. Its sloping profile was shaped partly by protected sightlines across London, and it has become one of the more recognisable towers in the financial district.

You see it best from the streets around Leadenhall Market and Lloyd's of London, where the contrast between glass, steel, and older City lanes is at its strongest.
