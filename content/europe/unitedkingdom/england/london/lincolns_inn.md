Lincoln's Inn is one of London's four Inns of Court, a historic legal precinct arranged around quiet courtyards and Lincoln's Inn Fields. The lanes and old buildings give a strong sense of London's legal traditions and make a good short detour between Holborn, Fleet Street, and the West End.
