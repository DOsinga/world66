Little Venice is the canal basin where the Regent's Canal meets the Grand Union Canal, a short walk from Paddington Station. Boats, waterside paths, and quiet residential streets give it a calmer feel than nearby central London.

It works well as the start of a canal walk toward Regent's Park or Camden, or as a short detour while passing through Paddington.
