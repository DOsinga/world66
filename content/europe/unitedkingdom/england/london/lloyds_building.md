The Lloyd's Building is the headquarters of the Lloyd's insurance market and one of the City of London's boldest late-20th-century buildings. Richard Rogers put lifts, ducts, pipes, and other services on the outside, giving the tower its famous inside-out look.

Visitors usually see it from the street rather than inside, but the exterior is a strong architecture stop beside Leadenhall Market and the Leadenhall Building.
