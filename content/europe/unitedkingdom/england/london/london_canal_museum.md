London Canal Museum explains the history of the city's canals from a former ice warehouse near King's Cross. Exhibits cover canal transport, the ice trade, working boats, horses, and the lives of people who lived and worked on the waterways.

It is compact but distinctive, and pairs well with a walk along Regent's Canal or a visit to nearby Islington.
