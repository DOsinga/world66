The London Canal Museum tells the story of the capital's inland waterways from a former ice warehouse near King's Cross. Exhibits cover narrowboats, canal trade, horse-drawn transport, and the huge ice blocks once imported from Norway and stored here.

It is a small museum, but a rewarding one if you are walking the Regent's Canal or want a more workaday slice of London history than palaces and theatres.
