London Central Mosque, beside Regent's Park, is one of the city's main Islamic institutions and a prominent modern landmark with a large golden dome. It is also home to the Islamic Cultural Centre.

Visitors are welcome outside prayer times if dressed respectfully. The mosque is an easy short detour from Regent's Park or nearby Marylebone.
