The London Dungeon turns the darker parts of the city's past into a theatrical walk-through attraction, with live actors, sets, and jump scares covering plague, executions, Jack the Ripper, and other gruesome stories. It is commercial and often pricey, but it can be a memorable stop for families with older children who enjoy macabre history.
