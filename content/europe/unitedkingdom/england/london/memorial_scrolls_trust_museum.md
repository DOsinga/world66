The Memorial Scrolls Trust Museum tells the story of more than 1,500 Torah scrolls rescued from postwar Czechoslovakia and brought to London in 1964. Many were restored and sent to synagogues around the world, while a smaller number remain here.

It is a small, focused museum, best suited to visitors with an interest in Jewish history, the Holocaust, or religious manuscripts.
