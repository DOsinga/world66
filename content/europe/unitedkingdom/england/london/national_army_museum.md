The National Army Museum in Chelsea traces the history of the British Army through personal stories, uniforms, art, weapons, and political context. It is not just a display of military hardware; the stronger exhibits look at how the army shaped, and was shaped by, British society and empire.

It is a good stop for anyone interested in military history, but also for visitors trying to understand Britain beyond the royal and parliamentary sights nearby.
