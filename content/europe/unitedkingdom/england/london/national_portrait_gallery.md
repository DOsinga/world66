The National Portrait Gallery tells British history through faces, from Tudor monarchs and writers to scientists, actors, musicians, and political figures. Its location just off Trafalgar Square makes it easy to pair with the National Gallery, but the mood is more biographical than encyclopedic.

It is one of London's most useful museums for visitors who want a readable route through the people behind the country's public story.
