The Old Vic is one of London's historic theatres, a short walk south of Waterloo station. Its stage history reaches back to the early 19th century, and it has long been tied to major British actors and classic repertory theatre.

For most visitors the reason to come is a performance, but the building and its place in London theatre history make it worth noting while exploring the South Bank and Waterloo area.
