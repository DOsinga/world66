Piccadilly Circus is the bright, busy road junction where Soho, Regent Street, and the West End seem to collide. The Shaftesbury Memorial Fountain, usually called Eros, sits below the famous advertising screens; it is not a peaceful square, but it is one of London's classic orientation points and still worth seeing once after dark.
