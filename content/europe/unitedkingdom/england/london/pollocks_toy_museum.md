Pollock's Toy Museum is a small, eccentric collection of antique toys, dolls, toy theatres, board games, and nursery objects displayed in a warren of rooms above the shop. It feels more like rummaging through a collector's attic than visiting a polished institution.

The museum is best for travellers who like odd London survivals and families with patient children. Some of the doll displays are charming; some are frankly unsettling.
