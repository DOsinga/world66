Pop Brixton is a community project built from stacked shipping containers on a former car park near Brixton station, home to a rotating cast of independent street-food kitchens, bars, small businesses, and event spaces. The food spans the world — Caribbean, Korean, Lebanese, pizza, and more — and the open central yard fills with a young, mixed crowd on warm evenings and at weekends.

It is informal and atmospheric, with DJs, screenings, and events through the week, and a deliberate focus on local startups and traders rather than chains. Come for an early-evening drink and graze across a few kitchens. It complements the older market arcades nearby and shows the newer, container-yard side of Brixton's food scene.
