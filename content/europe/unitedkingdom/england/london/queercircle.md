Queercircle is a small arts space in Greenwich's Design District, focused on exhibitions, talks, and community projects by LGBTQ+ artists and makers. It is not a major museum, but it is one of the more interesting reasons to cross to this newer cultural pocket beside the Thames.

Check the current programme before going. The space works best when there is an exhibition or event that matches your interests, and it combines easily with Greenwich or the riverfront.
