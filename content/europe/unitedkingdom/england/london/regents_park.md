Regent's Park is one of central London's grandest open spaces, laid out with broad lawns, formal gardens, lakes, and long avenues that feel calmer than the parks closer to Westminster. London Zoo occupies the northern edge, while the Open Air Theatre gives the park a strong summer evening rhythm.

It is a good place to slow down between Marylebone, Camden, and Primrose Hill. The boating lake, rose garden, and old trees make it worth more than a shortcut.
