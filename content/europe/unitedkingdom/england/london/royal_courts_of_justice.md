The Royal Courts of Justice house the High Court and Court of Appeal in a vast Victorian Gothic building on the Strand. Its towers, arches, and long stone frontage make it one of central London's grandest civic buildings.

Visitors are often most interested in the entrance hall and exterior, though public court hearings can sometimes be observed. It is an easy stop between the City, Temple, and Covent Garden, and pairs well with a walk through the legal quarter around the Inns of Court.
