The Russian Orthodox Cathedral in South Kensington began as a 19th-century Anglican church and later became part of the Moscow Patriarchate. Its Lombard-style exterior and Orthodox interior make it an unusual religious building in London.

It is worth a short detour if you are already visiting the museums around South Kensington or are interested in London's less obvious church architecture.
