Sadler's Wells is one of London's leading dance venues, with roots reaching back to the 17th century. Its programme ranges from classical ballet to contemporary dance and hip hop, often with better ticket availability than the West End's biggest stages.

The theatre is worth considering if you want an evening built around performance rather than sightseeing. The attached Lilian Baylis Studio hosts smaller productions and student work.
