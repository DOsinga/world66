The Sherlock Holmes Museum recreates the fictional detective's Baker Street lodgings in a late-19th-century townhouse. Rooms are dressed with Holmes and Watson memorabilia, period details, and displays tied to the stories, making it a focused stop for fans rather than a broad London history museum.
