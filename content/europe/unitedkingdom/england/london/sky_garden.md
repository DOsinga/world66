The top three floors of 20 Fenchurch Street — known as the Walkie Talkie for its distinctive silhouette — contain a free public garden with 360-degree views over London. Book online well in advance; walk-in slots are rare. The garden itself is pleasant enough, the bar prices are high, but the views across the Thames to Tower Bridge and the Tower of London are genuinely excellent. Take the lift up and enjoy the river at eye level from up high.
