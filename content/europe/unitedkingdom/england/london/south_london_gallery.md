The South London Gallery is the cultural heart of Camberwell, a free contemporary art space with a genuinely ambitious programme of exhibitions, commissions, and events. Founded in the Victorian era to bring art to working south Londoners, it has grown into one of the city's most respected smaller galleries, showing major international and emerging artists across its original building and the converted fire station opposite.

Beyond the shows there is a good café, a garden, and an artist-designed flat you can stay in, and the gallery runs an active community and education programme. Admission is free, the space is rarely crowded, and it pairs well with a meal on Camberwell Church Street. For contemporary art away from the central crowds, it is one of the best stops in south London.
