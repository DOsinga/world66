Southwark Cathedral sits beside London Bridge, close enough to Borough Market that you can step from food stalls into stone arches and a much older London. There has been worship on or near this site for centuries, and the present building keeps a calm Gothic dignity despite the railway lines and office blocks around it.

It is less famous than St Paul's or Westminster Abbey, which is part of the pleasure. Drop in while exploring Bankside, then come back out into the market for lunch.
