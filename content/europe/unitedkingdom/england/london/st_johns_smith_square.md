St John's Smith Square is a former Baroque church by Thomas Archer, tucked into Westminster a short walk from Parliament. Rebuilt after wartime damage, it became a concert hall in the 1960s and is best visited for classical music performances or for a quiet look at one of Westminster's more unusual church facades.
