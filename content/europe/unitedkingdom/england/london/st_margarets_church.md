St. Margaret's Church stands beside Westminster Abbey and serves as the parish church of the House of Commons. That role gives it a very particular place in Westminster: smaller than the abbey, but closely tied to Parliament and public life.

It is easy to miss in the shadow of its famous neighbour. Step inside if it is open, especially when Parliament Square is crowded and you want a quieter Westminster stop.
