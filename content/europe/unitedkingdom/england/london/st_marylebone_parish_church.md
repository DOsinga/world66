St Marylebone Parish Church is the main parish church of Marylebone, completed in 1827 to a design by Thomas Hardwick. Its portico and tower give the north end of Marylebone Road a more formal face than the surrounding shopping streets.

It is worth a short look if you are already in Marylebone, especially as part of a walk between Regent's Park, Marylebone High Street, and Baker Street.
