St Sepulchre-without-Newgate is a Grade I listed church near the Old Bailey, with roots before the 12th century. Much of the exterior is late medieval, while the interior reflects later rebuilding and restoration.

The church is closely tied to music, with a musicians' chapel on the north side and regular concerts in addition to services. It is a worthwhile short stop while walking the western edge of the City of London.
