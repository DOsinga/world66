Topolski Century is a huge painted panorama by Feliks Topolski, made between 1975 and 1989 as his personal record of the 20th century. The work runs on a monumental scale, crowded with political leaders, artists, conflicts, and public moments filtered through one sharp, restless eye.

It is most rewarding if you like history seen through art rather than through a museum label. Allow time to look slowly; the detail is the point.
