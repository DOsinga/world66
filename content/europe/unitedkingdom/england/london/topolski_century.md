Topolski Century is a monumental work by Polish-born artist Feliks Topolski, painted between 1975 and 1989 as a sweeping visual record of 20th-century people, politics, and culture. It is a compact but unusual art stop on the South Bank, best for visitors who like dense narrative murals and modern history.
