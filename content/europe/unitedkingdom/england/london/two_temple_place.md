Two Temple Place is a late Victorian mansion built for William Waldorf Astor beside the Embankment. The richly worked Neo-Gothic interior is the reason to go: carved wood, stained glass, and elaborate decorative detail turn the building itself into the main exhibit, with temporary shows adding another reason to check opening dates.
