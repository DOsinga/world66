The Undercroft is the famous skateboarding space beneath Queen Elizabeth Hall on the South Bank. Skaters, BMX riders, and graffiti artists have made this concrete pocket one of London's best-known street culture spots.

It is not a formal museum or monument, but it is worth a look while walking along Queen's Walk, especially if you are interested in skate culture or the lived-in side of the South Bank.
