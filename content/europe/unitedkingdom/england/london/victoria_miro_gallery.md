Victoria Miro Gallery is a contemporary art gallery in a converted Victorian furniture factory near the Regent's Canal. It is not a standard big-museum stop, which is part of the appeal: you press the buzzer to enter, then find polished exhibitions in a quiet industrial setting.

It suits travellers already exploring Islington or the canal-side route toward Shoreditch. Check the current exhibition before going, as the value of the visit depends heavily on what is on.
