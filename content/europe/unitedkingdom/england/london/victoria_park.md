Victoria Park, often called the People's Park, is one of East London's major green spaces. It has broad lawns, lakes, a reconstructed Chinese pagoda, and summer events that draw visitors well beyond the surrounding neighbourhoods.

Sunday is the liveliest time to visit, when the park's street-food market adds a local weekend feel. It also works as a calm link between Bethnal Green, Hackney, and the canals.
