The Viktor Wynd Museum of Curiosities is a small cabinet-of-curiosities museum in east London, packed with taxidermy, skulls, odd photographs, ritual objects, and other deliberately strange exhibits. It is best for visitors who like eccentric collections rather than conventional museum interpretation.
