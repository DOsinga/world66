Wellcome Collection is a free museum and exhibition space built around Henry Wellcome's vast collection of medical objects, art, books, and curiosities. Its displays explore health, the body, science, and the ways different cultures have understood being human.

The changing exhibitions are often the best reason to visit, but the permanent material is unusual enough to reward anyone interested in medicine, anthropology, or the stranger edges of museum collecting.
