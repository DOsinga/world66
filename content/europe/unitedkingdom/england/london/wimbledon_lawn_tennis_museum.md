Wimbledon Lawn Tennis Museum tells the story of the Championships and the development of lawn tennis. Exhibits cover famous players, historic equipment, Centre Court traditions, and the tournament's place in London sporting life.

It is the main year-round visitor stop at the All England Club, with tours available separately.
