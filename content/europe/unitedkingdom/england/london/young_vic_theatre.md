The Young Vic is one of London's most adventurous theatres, a short walk from Waterloo on The Cut. It has a strong record for new productions, young directors, and accessible staging, with a programme that ranges from classics to experimental work.

It is worth checking the schedule if you are staying near the South Bank or want a theatre night that feels less polished and predictable than the West End.
