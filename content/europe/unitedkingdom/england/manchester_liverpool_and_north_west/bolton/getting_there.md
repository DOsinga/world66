---
title: 'Getting There'
type: section
---
Bolton is straightforward to reach from Manchester. Frequent trains run from Manchester Victoria to Bolton, taking around 20 minutes, with services running throughout the day and into the late evening. The station is a short walk from the town centre.

By road, the M61 motorway connects Bolton directly to the M60 Manchester orbital and the wider motorway network. The journey from central Manchester by car takes around 25 to 30 minutes outside peak hours, longer during the morning and evening rush. Local buses connect Bolton to surrounding towns including Bury, Wigan, and Chorley, and the Arriva and First networks cover most of the borough.
