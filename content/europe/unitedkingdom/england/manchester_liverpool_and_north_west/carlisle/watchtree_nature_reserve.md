Watchtree Nature Reserve is a community-run reserve on former airfield land west of Carlisle, now managed for wildlife, walking and cycling. Wetlands, open grassland and accessible paths make it a practical outdoor stop rather than a rugged nature expedition.

It is particularly good for families, birdwatchers and adaptive cycling. Check opening arrangements before going, as the reserve has a managed visitor setup rather than open countryside access at all hours.
