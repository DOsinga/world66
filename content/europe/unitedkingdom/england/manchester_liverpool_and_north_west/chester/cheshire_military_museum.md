The Cheshire Military Museum sits within the Chester Castle complex and follows the county's
regimental history from early campaigns to modern conflicts. Uniforms, medals, weapons, photographs,
and personal stories keep the displays grounded in individual soldiers rather than abstract battles.

It is a specialist museum, but a good addition if you are already exploring Chester's Roman and
medieval military layers. The castle setting helps, and the visit is short enough to combine with
the walls, cathedral, and riverfront.
