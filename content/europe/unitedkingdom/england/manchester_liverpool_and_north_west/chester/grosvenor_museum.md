The Grosvenor Museum is Chester's main local-history museum, especially useful for understanding the Roman city behind the modern tourist centre. Its collections include Roman tombstones and artefacts, natural history, paintings, and period rooms.

It is a strong companion to the city walls and amphitheatre because it gives the ruins more context. The museum is central and manageable, making it easy to fit into a Chester day.
