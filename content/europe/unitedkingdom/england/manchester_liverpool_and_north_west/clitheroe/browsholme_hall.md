Browsholme Hall is a Tudor house that has belonged to the Parker family since it was built in 1507. The hall is still privately owned, so visits usually mean joining a guided tour rather than wandering freely.

The appeal is the continuity: old rooms, family history, and a working estate rather than a house frozen purely as a museum. It pairs well with a countryside drive around Clitheroe and the Forest of Bowland.
