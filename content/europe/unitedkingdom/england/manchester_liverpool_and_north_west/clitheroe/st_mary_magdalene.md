St Mary Magdalene is Clitheroe's Anglican parish church. Its tower and east window date from the 15th century, while much of the rest of the building was rebuilt in the 19th century.

The church is a modest but worthwhile historic stop near the centre of town, especially if you are already visiting Clitheroe Castle.
