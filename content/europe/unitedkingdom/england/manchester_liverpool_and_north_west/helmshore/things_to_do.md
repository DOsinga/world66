Helmshore's main draw is the [Helmshore Mills Textile Museum](/europe/unitedkingdom/england/manchester_liverpool_and_north_west/helmshore/helmshore_mills_textile_museum) — one of the best surviving examples of working textile mill machinery in England. Beyond the museum, the surrounding Rossendale Valley offers good walking country, particularly toward [Musbury Tor](/europe/unitedkingdom/england/manchester_liverpool_and_north_west/helmshore/musbury_tor) and the Alden Valley to the west.
