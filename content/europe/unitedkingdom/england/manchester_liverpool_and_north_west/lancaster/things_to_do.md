---
title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Lancaster's history is compact and walkable. The [castle](/europe/unitedkingdom/england/manchester_liverpool_and_north_west/lancaster/lancaster_castle) and the old quarter around it reward a couple of hours on foot, and the [Roman bathhouse](/europe/unitedkingdom/england/manchester_liverpool_and_north_west/lancaster/roman_bath_house) is easy to miss if you don't know to look. For views over the city and bay, the [Ashton Memorial](/europe/unitedkingdom/england/manchester_liverpool_and_north_west/lancaster/ashton_memorial) in Williamson Park is an easy uphill walk from the centre. Down on the quay, the [Maritime Museum](/europe/unitedkingdom/england/manchester_liverpool_and_north_west/lancaster/maritime_museum) addresses Lancaster's 18th-century slave-trading history with unusual directness.
