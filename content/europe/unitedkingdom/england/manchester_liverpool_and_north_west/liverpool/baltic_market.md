Liverpool's original street food hall, opened in 2017 inside the Cain's Brewery Village in the Baltic Triangle. A dozen or so independent food traders share a converted warehouse with communal tables, two bars and a stage for live music and DJs at weekends.

The line-up changes but reliably includes good Neapolitan pizza, a few Asian street food traders, a pie specialist, a Caribbean stall, and a dessert vendor or two. Drinks are local — Liverpool Craft Beer, Love Lane, and cocktails. Open Wednesday to Sunday only; busiest on Friday and Saturday nights.
