Croxteth Hall and Country Park preserves a former country estate on the edge of Liverpool, with a historic hall, working-farm elements, gardens, and broad parkland. It shows a very different side of the city from the docks and music landmarks.

It is best as a half-day outing, especially for families or anyone wanting green space without leaving the urban area entirely. Check which parts of the hall and farm are open before travelling out.
