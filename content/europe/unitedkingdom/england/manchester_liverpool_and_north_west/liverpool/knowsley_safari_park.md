Knowsley Safari Park is a drive-through wildlife park east of Liverpool, with lions, rhinos, zebras, camels, ostriches, and other animals in large parkland enclosures. The baboon section is notorious for car damage, so the park bus is the calmer choice if you are attached to your windscreen wipers.
