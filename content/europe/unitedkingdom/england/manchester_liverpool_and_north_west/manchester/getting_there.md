**BritRail Passes**

BritRail passes give you the the freedom to travel England, Scotland, and Wales at your own pace (Ireland package available).

**Manchester Airport**

Manchester has an international airport with flights to many airports on the continent and in the USA.
 The City's International Airport is less than 30 minutes from the city-centre and the city's second airport, Glasgow Prestwick, is within easy reach with its own purpose-built rail station. A taxi from Manchester Airport to the city centre takes around 25 minutes; the direct train to Piccadilly is quicker and runs every few minutes throughout the day.
