Manchester Arena is one of Britain's biggest indoor venues, built into the Victoria Station complex at the northern edge of the city centre. Its programme runs from arena-scale pop and comedy to boxing, family shows, and touring productions.

It is not a place to visit casually when nothing is on, but it matters to Manchester's live-music life. If you have tickets, the central location makes it easy to combine with the Northern Quarter or a pre-show drink around Victoria.
