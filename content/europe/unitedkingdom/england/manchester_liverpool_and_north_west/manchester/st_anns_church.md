St. Ann's Church is one of Manchester city centre's historic parish churches, set just off the busy shopping streets around St Ann's Square. The building is worth a short stop for its calm interior and long civic role, and it also hosts sacred and classical music events. Check service times before visiting, since sightseeing access may pause during worship.
