The Ribble Way is a 121-kilometre long-distance footpath following the River Ribble from its source in the Yorkshire Dales to its estuary at Preston. The section that passes close to Bolton-by-Bowland runs through some of the finest pastoral scenery in Lancashire — the river winding through meadows below the moorland fells, with the distinctive profile of Pendle Hill visible to the south.

Bolton-by-Bowland makes a good starting point for circular day walks along the Ribble Way, with routes ranging from five to twelve kilometres depending on how far you follow the river before turning up onto the fell. The Skirden Beck, which joins the Ribble near the village, is particularly attractive in spring when the banks are bright with flowers. Maps and route information are available from the Ribble Valley Borough Council website.
