**Sabden** is a quiet village tucked into the southern slopes of [Pendle Hill](/europe/unitedkingdom/england/manchester_liverpool_and_north_west/pendle/sabden/pendle_hill) in Lancashire. It is a good starting point for walking up Pendle Hill from the south — follow the road towards Clitheroe over the [Nick o' Pendle](/europe/unitedkingdom/england/manchester_liverpool_and_north_west/pendle/sabden/nick_o_pendle) and pick up one of the well-marked paths.

The village itself is small but pleasant, with a couple of pubs and a community feel. [Sabden Brook](/europe/unitedkingdom/england/manchester_liverpool_and_north_west/pendle/sabden/sabden_brook) runs down through the valley to the Calder, and the famous [Sabden Treacle Mines](/europe/unitedkingdom/england/manchester_liverpool_and_north_west/pendle/sabden/sabden_treacle_mines) — entirely fictional, but a fine local joke — turn up on pub signs and toffee wrappers across the area. The surrounding countryside offers excellent walking through rolling farmland and open moorland, with wide views across the Ribble Valley.
