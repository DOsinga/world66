---
title: Getting There
type: section
---

Sabden lies between Clitheroe and Padiham, about an hour from Manchester by car.

**By car.** From the M65, take the Padiham exit and follow signs over the Nick o' Pendle. From the M6, head east via Clitheroe. The village is small and parking is limited; the lay-bys above the village give the easiest walking access.

**By bus.** Limited local services connect Sabden to Clitheroe and Burnley. Check current timetables — frequency drops off sharply on Sundays.

**By rail.** The nearest stations are Whalley and Clitheroe, both on the Manchester–Clitheroe line. From either, taxi or local bus into Sabden, around 15–20 minutes.
