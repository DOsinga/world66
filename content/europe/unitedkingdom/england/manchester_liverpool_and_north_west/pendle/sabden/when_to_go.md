---
title: When to Go
type: section
---

May to September is the best stretch for walking — long days, the hill mostly dry, and the heather on top of Pendle in bloom in late August. Spring is cooler and often clearer than summer; lambing in the fields below the hill in April is a pleasant time to be here.

Autumn brings rain and short days but the hillsides go through dramatic colour changes. Winter is sometimes spectacular with snow on Pendle Hill, but the upper paths can be hazardous and the roads over the Nick close in bad weather.
