---
title: Day Trips
type: section
---

Preston is one of the best-placed bases in the north-west for short trips out: the Lake District is an hour north, the Yorkshire Dales an hour east, Blackpool twenty minutes west, and Liverpool and Manchester each within an hour by train. The Forest of Bowland — a less-visited Area of Outstanding Natural Beauty — sits immediately north of the city and has some of the finest walking in Lancashire.
