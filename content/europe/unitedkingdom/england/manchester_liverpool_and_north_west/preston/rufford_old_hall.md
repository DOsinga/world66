Rufford Old Hall is a timber-framed Tudor house near Ormskirk, close enough to Preston to work as a straightforward day trip. The National Trust property centres on a great hall from around 1530, with later wings, gardens, and rooms that show how one Lancashire family estate adapted across centuries.

The great hall is the reason to go: dark wood, carved detail, and the theatrical scale of a house built to impress. The gardens soften the visit, and the site pairs well with a slow drive through the flat, often overlooked countryside between Preston, Southport, and Liverpool.
