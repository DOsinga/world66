Warrington is a large town in Cheshire, standing on the banks of the River Mersey roughly midway between [Liverpool](/europe/unitedkingdom/england/manchester_liverpool_and_north_west/liverpool) and [Manchester](/europe/unitedkingdom/england/manchester_liverpool_and_north_west/manchester). Its strategic position at a crossing point of the Mersey has made it important since Roman times, and today it is a busy commercial centre with a population of around 210,000.

The town centre has the [Warrington Museum and Art Gallery](/europe/unitedkingdom/england/manchester_liverpool_and_north_west/warrington/warrington_museum), one of the oldest municipal museums in the country, housed in a fine Victorian building on Bold Street. The collection includes Egyptian antiquities, local history displays, and finds from the Roman settlement at Wilderspool. Nearby, the [Parish Church of St Elphin](/europe/unitedkingdom/england/manchester_liverpool_and_north_west/warrington/st_elphin) has a spire that is one of the tallest in England. The Golden Gates in front of the Town Hall, ornate ironwork originally made for Sandringham, are Warrington's most photographed landmark.

[Walton Hall and Gardens](/europe/unitedkingdom/england/manchester_liverpool_and_north_west/warrington/walton_hall_gardens), a few kilometres south of the centre, is a pleasant green space with a heritage centre, children's zoo, and gardens surrounding an island mansion. A short drive into neighbouring Runcorn, [Norton Priory](/europe/unitedkingdom/england/manchester_liverpool_and_north_west/warrington/norton_priory) — the most excavated monastic site in Europe — is well worth the detour. Warrington is not a destination in its own right, but its excellent transport links, sitting right on the M6 and M62 motorways with fast trains to both Liverpool and Manchester, make it a practical base for exploring the northwest. The town also has a fierce pride in its rugby league team, the Warrington Wolves, and a match at the Halliwell Jones Stadium is a proper northern sporting experience.
