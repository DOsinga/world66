It is surprising how many visitors to the UK rush from [London](/europe/unitedkingdom/england/london) to [Scotland](/europe/unitedkingdom/scotland) via [York](/europe/unitedkingdom/england/leeds_sheffield_and_yorkshire/york) without a thought of Durham. Yet it is a lovely city with a magnificent castle and cathedral and is home to England's third oldest university.

In his *Notes from a Small Island*, Bill Bryson said: "I got off at Durham... and fell in love with it instantly in a big way. Why, it's wonderful — a perfect little city — and I kept thinking, *Why did no one tell me about this?*" The reason more people don't come is largely the rail schedules — Durham is just twenty minutes from [Newcastle](/europe/unitedkingdom/england/newcastle_and_north_east/newcastle) on the East Coast Main Line, but most expresses don't stop.

[Durham Cathedral](/europe/unitedkingdom/england/newcastle_and_north_east/durham/durham_cathedral) is one of England's — indeed, the world's — finest examples of Norman Romanesque architecture. Founded in 1093, it houses the tombs of St Cuthbert and the Venerable Bede and is a UNESCO World Heritage Site together with [Durham Castle](/europe/unitedkingdom/england/newcastle_and_north_east/durham/durham_castle). The castle, a working university college since 1840, faces the cathedral across [Palace Green](/europe/unitedkingdom/england/newcastle_and_north_east/durham/palace_green_library) in one of the finest medieval civic spaces in Europe. Both can be reached with ease from the city's train station — simply follow the cathedral on the skyline, or take the No. 40 shuttle bus direct to Palace Green.

The [River Wear walk](/europe/unitedkingdom/england/newcastle_and_north_east/durham/river_wear_walk) circles the base of the cathedral peninsula and gives the best view of the whole composition from below. Away from the historic core, the [Oriental Museum](/europe/unitedkingdom/england/newcastle_and_north_east/durham/oriental_museum) on the university campus has an outstanding collection of Egyptian and Asian antiquities (free entry), and [Crook Hall](/europe/unitedkingdom/england/newcastle_and_north_east/durham/crook_hall), a 13th-century manor house with exceptional gardens, offers a quieter counterpart to the tourist trail. If your visit falls on the second Saturday of July, the [Durham Miners' Gala](/europe/unitedkingdom/england/newcastle_and_north_east/durham/miners_gala) — the world's largest trade union event, held since 1871 — is unmissable.
