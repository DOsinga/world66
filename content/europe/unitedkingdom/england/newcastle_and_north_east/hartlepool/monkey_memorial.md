The Monkey Memorial sits on the marina waterfront and commemorates one of England's stranger civic legends. Whether or not a monkey was ever actually hanged in Hartlepool is anyone's guess — the story doesn't appear in print before the 1850s — but the town has wholeheartedly embraced it.

The bronze sculpture shows the monkey at the moment of judgement. It is small and easy to miss; look for the plinth a short walk along the marina from the Andy Capp statue.
