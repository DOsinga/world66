Newcastle is a city that punches well above its weight. Compact enough to walk across in twenty minutes, it packs in more culture, nightlife, and architectural variety than cities twice its size. The [Quayside](/europe/unitedkingdom/england/newcastle_and_north_east/newcastle_upon_tyne/quayside) along the Tyne is the showpiece — the [Gateshead Millennium Bridge](/europe/unitedkingdom/england/newcastle_and_north_east/newcastle_upon_tyne/gateshead_millennium_bridge), the [BALTIC Centre for Contemporary Art](/europe/unitedkingdom/england/newcastle_and_north_east/newcastle_upon_tyne/baltic_centre), and the [Sage Gateshead](/europe/unitedkingdom/england/newcastle_and_north_east/newcastle_upon_tyne/sage_gateshead) concert hall all face each other across the river, with the old bridges and the [castle keep](/europe/unitedkingdom/england/newcastle_and_north_east/newcastle_upon_tyne/newcastle_castle) as backdrop.

The centre is handsome Victorian and Georgian, particularly [Grey Street](/europe/unitedkingdom/england/newcastle_and_north_east/newcastle_upon_tyne/grey_street) — a sweeping curve of classical facades that was voted England's finest street. The [Grainger Market](/europe/unitedkingdom/england/newcastle_and_north_east/newcastle_upon_tyne/grainger_market), a covered market from the 1830s, is still trading and still worth a visit for its mix of butchers, bakers, and cheap lunch spots. Further out, the [Great North Museum](/europe/unitedkingdom/england/newcastle_and_north_east/newcastle_upon_tyne/great_north_museum) covers [Hadrian's Wall](/europe/unitedkingdom/england/newcastle_and_north_east/newcastle_upon_tyne/hadrians_wall) and the region's natural history, and the wall itself is a short drive west.

Newcastle's nightlife is legendary and, unlike in most English cities, concentrated in the centre rather than scattered across suburbs. The [Bigg Market](/europe/unitedkingdom/england/newcastle_and_north_east/newcastle_upon_tyne/bigg_market) is the loud end; the [Ouseburn Valley](/europe/unitedkingdom/england/newcastle_and_north_east/newcastle_upon_tyne/ouseburn), a former industrial quarter east of the centre, is the more interesting one — breweries, galleries, and live music in converted warehouses. The Geordies are famously warm, and you will notice the difference the moment you arrive.
