Two-location Indian street-food restaurant — one in High Bridge, one in Jesmond — that has been the cheapest reliable spot for a sit-down meal in Newcastle for over a decade. Small plates from across the Indian street-food canon: pani puri, bhel puri, lamb kati rolls, dosas. Service is brisk; share a few plates between two.

Lunch deals from £9 a head, dinner roughly twice that. Cocktail list improves the longer you stay.
