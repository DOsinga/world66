The world's first double-deck road and rail bridge, designed by Robert Stephenson and opened in 1849. Trains use the upper deck (and still do — the East Coast Main Line passes overhead every few minutes); the lower deck carries reduced road traffic and pedestrians. The views down the Tyne to the lower bridges from the lower deck are excellent and the cast-iron Y-shaped piers are an engineering classic.

A long walk across with frequent stops to photograph the river. Combine with the Tyne Bridge and the Swing Bridge for a satisfying three-bridge circuit.
