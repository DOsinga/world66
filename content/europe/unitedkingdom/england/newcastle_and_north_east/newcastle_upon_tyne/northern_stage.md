Northern Stage is Newcastle's main producing theatre, tucked behind Newcastle University's King's Gate building at the top end of the city centre. It does not have the Victorian glamour of Theatre Royal or the riverfront scale of the Glasshouse, but it is one of the better places in the city to catch new writing, touring drama, comedy, and politically alert contemporary theatre.

The location makes it easy to fold into a day around Haymarket, the Civic Centre, and the Great North Museum. Check the programme rather than turning up cold: on the right night it can be one of Newcastle's most rewarding cultural stops, while on a dark day the building itself is mostly just a practical theatre complex.
