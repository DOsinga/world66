A parish church for most of its life and elevated to cathedral status only in 1882, St Nick's is the older of Newcastle's two cathedrals. The 14th-century interior is plain by the standard of the great English cathedrals but has a remarkable lantern tower — a stone crown braced by four flying ribs over the central spire, added in 1448 as a beacon for shipping.

Open daily for visitors, free admission. The crypt has a small heritage exhibition. A two-minute walk from the Castle Keep and the Black Gate.
