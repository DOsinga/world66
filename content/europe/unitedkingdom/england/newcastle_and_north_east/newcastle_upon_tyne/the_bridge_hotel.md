A handsome pub on the edge of the Castle Garth, with a beer garden cantilevered out over the cobbled bank above the Quayside. The interior is unspoilt Victorian — mahogany, etched glass, brass — and the upstairs function room is one of the city's longest-running folk-music spaces. Sam Smith's beers at a price that has not really moved with the times.

A short walk from Central Station and the most obvious first pub on a Quayside crawl.
