story: The Town Moor is one of the largest urban commons in Britain — 400 hectares (about 1,000 acres), larger than Hyde Park and Hampstead Heath combined. Freemen of the city still hold ancient grazing rights, and cattle wander among the joggers most months of the year.
---

The Freemen's grazing common that wraps the north side of the city centre, separating Newcastle proper from the Victorian suburbs of Jesmond and Gosforth. Footpaths cross it in every direction; the cattle that share the space have right of way. The Hoppings — the annual ten-day funfair, the largest travelling fair in Europe — sets up here in late June each year.

The Great North Run, the world's largest half-marathon, starts on the Town Moor in early September. Otherwise the place is good for a long walk, sky-watching, or running. The Cluny Whisky Bar at the end of the Newcastle University drag is a useful warm-up stop.
