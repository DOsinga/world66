The arch that says Newcastle. Opened by King George V in 1928, it is the largest single-span bridge in Britain of its kind and the design template for Sydney Harbour. The full sweep is best appreciated from the Quayside cobbles below, or from the High Level Bridge to the west.

Walking across is free and quick — the deck is shared by traffic and pedestrians and the views east down the Tyne and west to the High Level and Swing Bridges are both worth the time. Listen for the kittiwakes nesting under the southern arch: they are protected, and the screaming is part of the experience.
