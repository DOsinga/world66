The Wylam Brewery's tap room and event space occupy the 1929 Palace of Arts in Exhibition Park, the last surviving building from the North East Coast Exhibition of that year. The tap pours the full range of Wylam's beers in a barrel-vaulted hall hung with chandeliers; bookings are usually needed for weekend evenings and rare for weekday afternoons.

Friday tap room sessions, weekend brewery tours, and a regular schedule of food popups (most reliably weekend brunch). Five minutes' walk from the Civic Centre and the city centre.
