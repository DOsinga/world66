---
title: When to Go
type: section
---

Berwick is a year-round destination, though the weather is characteristically northern English — cool even in summer and frequently windy, given the exposed position on the Northumbrian coast. Summer (June to August) brings the longest days and the best conditions for walking the walls and exploring the surrounding country. Crowds are modest by national standards even in peak season.

Spring and autumn are good for birdwatching along the coast and estuary, and the light over the Tweed is particularly good in autumn. Winter is cold and quiet — most tourists have gone, and the town has a more authentic, low-key feel. The annual Riding the Bounds ceremony, in which the boundaries of the town are ridden on horseback, takes place in May and is a good time to see local custom.
