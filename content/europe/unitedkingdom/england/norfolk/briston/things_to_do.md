title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Briston is a working village, not a tourist stop, but it sits in a corner of north Norfolk that is full of interest. The neighbouring estate village of Melton Constable has the ruins of a grand hall and the haunting site of a once-vast railway junction; Stody Lodge opens its azalea gardens for a few weeks each spring; and a string of small parish churches in the surrounding fields reward a slow drive.

For anything bigger, you are quickly into the [Norfolk coast](https://www.nationaltrust.org.uk/visit/norfolk) or the Broads. Holt with its Georgian centre and the seaside at Sheringham and Cromer are all within twenty minutes.
