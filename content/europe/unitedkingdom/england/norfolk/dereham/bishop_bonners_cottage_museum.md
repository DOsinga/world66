Bishop Bonner's Cottage is a small local-history museum in one of Dereham's oldest surviving buildings. The thatched cottage is the attraction as much as the displays, tucked close to the parish church and the town centre.

Opening can be limited, so check before making it the anchor of a day. If it is open, it gives Dereham's history a human scale.
