Diss Mere is the town's defining feature: a small but deep lake lying directly beside the market streets. It gives the centre a quieter rhythm than you expect from a Norfolk town on the A140 corridor.

Come for a short walk, a bench by the water, or a pause before heading into the surrounding lanes. It is not grand, but it is exactly what makes Diss feel like Diss.
