Ant Broads and Marshes National Nature Reserve protects some of the Norfolk Broads' most characteristic wetland scenery: reedbeds, open water, carr woodland, and grazing marsh threaded by slow rivers. It is the kind of landscape where small changes in light, season, and bird movement make the visit.

Access varies by site and conservation needs, so plan around marked paths, boat routes, and current guidance. Bring binoculars if you have them; this is a place for watching and listening rather than rushing from one named attraction to another.
