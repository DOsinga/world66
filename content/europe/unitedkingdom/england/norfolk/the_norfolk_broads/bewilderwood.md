BeWILDerwood is a woodland adventure park near Hoveton, with treehouses, zip wires, rope bridges, storytelling areas, boat trips, bike hire, and marsh walks. It is aimed mainly at families, but it also makes good use of its Broads setting rather than feeling like a generic theme park.
