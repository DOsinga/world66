Fairhaven Woodland and Water Garden is a 130-acre mix of cultivated gardens, wilder woodland, and waterside planting on the edge of the Broads. Its four miles of paths make it a good slow stop if you want the wetland landscape without hiring a boat or tackling a full nature reserve walk.

The garden is especially good for easy wandering: rhododendrons, mature trees, boardwalk-like waterside sections, and quiet views across its private broad. It works well as a gentle half-day break between villages in the Norfolk Broads.
