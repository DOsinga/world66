Horsey Gap is one of the best-known places in Norfolk to see grey seals, especially in winter when pups are born on the beach. The colony is protected by volunteer wardens and viewing routes, so visitors can watch from a distance without disturbing the animals.

The experience is strongest from late autumn into winter, but seals may be seen at other times along this stretch of coast. It is a memorable natural stop in the Norfolk Broads region, pairing well with Horsey Mere, dunes, and the wind-pumped landscapes between sea and inland water.
