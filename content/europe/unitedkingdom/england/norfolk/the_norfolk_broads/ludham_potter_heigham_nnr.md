Ludham-Potter Heigham National Nature Reserve protects a wide, watery piece of the Broads: grazing marsh, reedbed, drainage dykes, and open skies. It is the sort of place where the appeal comes from patience, with birds, insects, and shifting light across the marsh rather than a single big monument.

Bring binoculars and waterproof footwear if you plan to leave the easiest paths. Access can be seasonal or sensitive because this is a working conservation landscape, so check current guidance before setting off.
