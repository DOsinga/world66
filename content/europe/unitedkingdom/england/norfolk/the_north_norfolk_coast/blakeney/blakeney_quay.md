Blakeney Quay is the village's natural pause point. At high tide it feels like a small harbour; at low tide the water drains away into mud, creek lines, and marsh.

It is worth a wander before or after a seal trip. The pleasure is simple: boats, lanes, weather, and the slow shift of the tide.
