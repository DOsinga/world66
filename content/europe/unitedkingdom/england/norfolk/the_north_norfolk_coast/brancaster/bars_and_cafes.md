---
title: 'Bars and Cafes'
type: section
---

Brancaster and the immediate area are better served by pubs than the size of the villages would suggest. The Ship Hotel in Brancaster Staithe has a pub with local ales on tap and a terrace that overlooks the creek — a reliable stop after a walk or a boat trip to Scolt Head Island. The Lifeboat Inn at Thornham, a few kilometres west, is widely regarded as one of the best pubs on the coast: old building, open fires, serious food, and a good selection of Norfolk ales.

Coffee and daytime food options are thinner. The RSPB visitor centre at Titchwell has a decent café, and it is the most reliably open option outside the summer season. Burnham Market, about ten minutes' drive east, has several bakeries and cafés that draw a local crowd and are worth the short detour.
