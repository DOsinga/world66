---
title: 'Getting There'
type: section
---

Brancaster is on the A149 coast road, which runs the full length of the North Norfolk Coast from King's Lynn in the west to Cromer in the east. By car from London the drive takes around three hours; from Norwich it is about an hour. The A149 is a single-track road in places and moves slowly in summer.

Public transport is limited. The Coasthopper bus service (operated by Sanders Coaches) runs along the A149 and connects Brancaster with King's Lynn, Wells-next-the-Sea, and Cromer, with connections to Norwich from those towns. Services run year-round but frequency drops significantly in winter. The nearest railway stations are at King's Lynn (on the line from London King's Cross) and Sheringham, both roughly an hour from Brancaster by bus. A car makes the coast far more manageable, particularly for reaching the various RSPB reserves and walking access points.
