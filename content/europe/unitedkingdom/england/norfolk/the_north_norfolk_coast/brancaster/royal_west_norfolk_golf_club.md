Royal West Norfolk Golf Club occupies a narrow strip of land between Brancaster Bay and the salt marshes, accessible via a causeway road that floods at high tide. The links course is consistently ranked among the finest in England — a true links in the original sense, built on the transition zone between the sea and the land, with no trees, uneven fairways, and the constant presence of wind off the North Sea.

The club is private and membership is not easily obtained, but visitors can book tee times. If you play golf, this is the reason to come to Brancaster. The surrounding landscape of dune and marsh is beautiful, and the walk from the car park across to the clubhouse, with the sea visible through the dune grass, sets the tone immediately. Non-golfers can walk along the beach fringe to see the setting without accessing the course.
