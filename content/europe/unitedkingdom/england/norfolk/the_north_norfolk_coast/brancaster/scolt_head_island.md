Scolt Head Island is a National Nature Reserve lying offshore from Brancaster Staithe, accessible by a short ferry trip that runs in season from the staithe. The island is a classic barrier island: a long ridge of dunes, saltmarsh creeks behind, open North Sea beach in front, and almost no human presence apart from the seasonal ferry landing.

It is an important nesting site for sandwich terns, with one of the largest colonies on the English coast. The western end of the island — where the tern colony is — is closed during the breeding season to protect nesting birds, but the dunes and beach to the east are open and make for an exceptional walk in conditions where you are entirely cut off from the mainland. The ferry crossing itself is part of the appeal: the tidal creeks around Brancaster Staithe are beautiful at any state of the tide.
