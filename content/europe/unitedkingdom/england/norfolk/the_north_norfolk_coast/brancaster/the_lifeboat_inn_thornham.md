The Lifeboat Inn at Thornham, a few kilometres west of Brancaster, is one of the best pubs on this stretch of the Norfolk coast. It sits at the edge of the salt marsh in a creeper-covered building that dates to the sixteenth century, with low ceilings, open fires, and the particular atmosphere of a pub that knows exactly what it is and is not trying to be anything else.

The food is serious — locally sourced, well-cooked, focused on fish and game that reflect the surrounding landscape — and the beer and whisky selection is better than you would expect in a village of this size. In summer the terrace overlooks the marsh. On a winter afternoon with the tide coming in and the light going, the interior is one of the more civilised places in Norfolk.
