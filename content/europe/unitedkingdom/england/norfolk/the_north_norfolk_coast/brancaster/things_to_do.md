---
title: 'Things to Do'
type: section
---

Brancaster sits on the North Norfolk Coast Area of Outstanding Natural Beauty, a stretch of saltmarsh, dunes, tidal creeks, and wide sandy beaches that is one of the finest wildlife habitats in England. Birdwatching is the main draw — the RSPB reserve at Titchwell Marsh, just to the west, is among the best sites in the country, and the surrounding coast holds waders, wildfowl, and migrant passerines in numbers that justify a dedicated trip. Beyond birds, there are long walks across empty beaches, boat trips out to the offshore nature reserve at Scolt Head Island, and the quiet satisfaction of a coast that has changed very little in a very long time.
