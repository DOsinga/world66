Cley Beach is a shingle shore about a mile north of the village, reached by Beach Road or on foot through the marshes. It is not a soft resort beach; it is a big, exposed North Sea edge where the pleasure is walking, watching weather move, and looking along the coast toward Blakeney Point.

The Norfolk Coast Path passes this way, and the beach is the natural start for longer walks. Bird nesting restrictions can apply in spring and summer, so pay attention to local signs.
