Cley Windmill is the village landmark: a five-storey early 19th-century mill standing above the marshes, with the flat coast spreading out behind it. It now operates as accommodation, but you do not need to stay there for it to shape the view.

The best approach is from Beach Road or the raised paths across the marshes, where the windmill, flint village, reedbeds, and sky all line up. It is one of those Norfolk scenes that looks almost too composed to be accidental.
