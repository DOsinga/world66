Holkham Hall is the great Palladian house just inland from Wells, set in a large deer park close enough to combine with the beach. The estate can feel grand without being stiff: house, park, lake, walled garden, trails, and cafes all sit within easy reach of each other.

Even if you skip the interiors, the park gives Wells a strong second act on a day when wind or tide makes the beach less appealing.
