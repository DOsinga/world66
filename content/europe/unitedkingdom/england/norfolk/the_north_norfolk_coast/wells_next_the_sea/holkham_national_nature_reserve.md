Holkham National Nature Reserve covers a large and varied piece of the north Norfolk coast: salt marsh, grazing marsh, woodland, dunes, and foreshore. That mix is what makes the area more than a beach stop. The reserve is good for winter wildfowl, passing waders, and long walks through a landscape where the sea is often heard before it is seen.

Bring binoculars if you have them, but do not worry if you are not a serious birder. The scale of the marsh and sky is the point.
