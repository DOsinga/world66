The Wells and Walsingham Light Railway is a narrow-gauge line running inland from Wells toward Walsingham. It follows part of a former standard-gauge branch line closed in the 1960s, but reopened in miniature form in 1982.

The journey is short and gently odd, which is exactly the charm. In season, small trains carry visitors through the Norfolk countryside and give families a slower alternative to yet another car journey along the coast road.
