---
title: "Getting There"
type: section
---

**By car.** Wolferton sits just off the A149 King's Lynn to Hunstanton road, about 8 miles north of King's Lynn. From the A149, follow signs through the woodland to the village. There is a small unpaved area where you can park near the old station.

**By bus.** The Coastliner 35 between King's Lynn and Hunstanton stops at the top of the village lane on the A149. From there it is a 15-minute walk down through the woods to the station.

**By train.** The nearest railway station is King's Lynn, with hourly trains from London King's Cross. From King's Lynn, take the bus, a taxi (around £15), or hire a bike for the flat run along the coast road.
