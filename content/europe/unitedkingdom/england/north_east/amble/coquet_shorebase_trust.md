The Coquet Shorebase Trust is a charity water-sports centre on the Braid at Amble. Visitors can join guided estuary tours by canoe or sit-on-top kayak, riding the rising tide up past Warkworth to the Hermitage, as well as sunset, dawn and Coquet Island kayak tours. It also hires out paddleboards, kayaks and canoes and teaches sailing, windsurfing and powerboating. The pin is approximate.
