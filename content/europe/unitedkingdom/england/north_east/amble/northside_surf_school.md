Northside has taught surfing on the Northumberland coast since 2006, with group and private lessons on several beaches, Bamburgh and Druridge Bay among them, depending on the swell. It also runs stand-up paddleboarding on the Coquet estuary, coasteering and a three-day surf course. Boards and wetsuits are included. The pin is approximate.
