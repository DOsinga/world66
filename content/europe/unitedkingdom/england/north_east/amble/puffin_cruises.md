This family business has taken visitors from Amble harbour out to Coquet Island for decades. The one-hour cruises circle the RSPB reserve, as landing is not allowed, for puffins, terns and eiders in summer and the grey seal colony all year. Sailings depend on tide and weather, so times are posted only a few days ahead; book at the pod in Amble Harbour Village. The pin is approximate.
