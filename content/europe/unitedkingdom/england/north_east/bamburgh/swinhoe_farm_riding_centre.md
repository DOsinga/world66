Swinhoe Farm is a family-run riding centre on a large farm near Belford, inland from Bamburgh. It teaches beginners to advanced riders, and runs one-hour woodland rides for riders who can canter confidently. It does not do beach rides, and it is closed on Sundays. The pin is approximate.
