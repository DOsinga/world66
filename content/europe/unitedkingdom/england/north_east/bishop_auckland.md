For more than 800 years Bishop Auckland was the country seat of the Prince Bishops of Durham, who ruled the county almost as kings. [Auckland Castle](/europe/unitedkingdom/england/north_east/bishop_auckland/auckland_castle) stands at the edge of the market place, its deer park running down to the River Wear.

Since 2012 a charity called the Auckland Project has turned the old mining town into an unexpected cultural destination. Around the market place are the [Mining Art Gallery](/europe/unitedkingdom/england/north_east/bishop_auckland/mining_art_gallery), with paintings by the miners themselves, the Spanish Gallery, with Spanish Golden Age paintings, and the Faith Museum in a new wing of the castle. In summer, the night-time show **Kynren** retells 2,000 years of English history on a huge outdoor stage across the river.

A couple of miles west, Escomb has one of the oldest complete Anglo-Saxon churches in England.
