Corbridge is an attractive stone village on the north bank of the Tyne, a few miles east of Hexham. Its seventeenth-century bridge was the only one on the Tyne to survive the great flood of 1771. The village has a fortified vicar's pele tower beside the church, several good pubs and delis, and more independent shops than its size would suggest.

Just west of the village is [Corbridge Roman Town](/europe/unitedkingdom/england/north_east/corbridge/corbridge_roman_town), the remains of Coria. It was a supply town just south of [Hadrian's Wall](/europe/unitedkingdom/england/north_east/hadrians_wall) and the most northerly town of the Roman Empire.
