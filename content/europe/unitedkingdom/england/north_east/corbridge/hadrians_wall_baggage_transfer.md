Hadrian's Wall Baggage Transfer, part of the Carrylite service, collects walkers' bags after they set off each morning and delivers them to the next night's accommodation along the Wall. Email your itinerary for a quote; prices are per bag up to 20 kg. The office is in Corbridge and answers 8am to 7pm every day.
