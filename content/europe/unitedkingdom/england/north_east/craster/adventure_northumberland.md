Adventure Northumberland runs small-group coasteering and sea kayaking from its base near Craster, next to the HM Coastguard station (not the lifeboat station). Trips include coasteering at Howick, kayaking under Dunstanburgh Castle and round Cullernose Point, and family kayak tasters. It also leads guided walks in the Cheviot Hills. Wetsuits and safety kit are provided. The pin is approximate.
