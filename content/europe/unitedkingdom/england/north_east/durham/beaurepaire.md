The ruins of the medieval country retreat of the priors of Durham at Bearpark, above the River Browney about three miles west of the city. The Scottish army camped here before the Battle of Neville's Cross in 1346. Reach it on foot by the old lanes from the city or from the Lanchester Valley railway path.
