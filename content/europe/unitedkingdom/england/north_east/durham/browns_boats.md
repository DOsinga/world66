Brown's has hired out traditional hand-built rowing boats on the Wear below the cathedral since 1900, and runs the Prince Bishop river cruiser with sightseeing and evening cruises all year. Rowing boats run from March to late September; no booking is needed. There is no published street address; the pin is on Durham.
