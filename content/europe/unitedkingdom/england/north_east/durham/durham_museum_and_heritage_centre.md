Durham Museum and Heritage Centre fills in the city around the cathedral and castle. Housed in the former church of St Mary-le-Bow, it tells Durham's story through models, objects, and local displays that explain how the peninsula grew into a religious and political stronghold.

It is a modest museum, but useful before wandering the lanes and riverbanks. Visit it as context rather than spectacle: half an hour here can make the surrounding medieval street pattern and cathedral precinct easier to read.
