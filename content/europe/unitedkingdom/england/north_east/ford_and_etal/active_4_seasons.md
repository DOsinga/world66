Active 4 Seasons is Ollie Jay, who has guided and coached paddlers in north Northumberland since 1999. He runs made-to-measure open-canoe trips on the Till from Etal and on the lower Tweed down to Berwick, and sea-kayak trips along the coast and out to the Farne Islands. Bookings are by email only, and he asks you to check his availability page first. There is no published street address; the pin is on Etal.
