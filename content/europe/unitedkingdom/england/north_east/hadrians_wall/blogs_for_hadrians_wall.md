Almost everything written about Hadrian's Wall is by people walking it once from end to end. Very little comes from the people who live and work along it. These two do: the national park team that looks after the central section, and the archaeological trust that runs Vindolanda and the Roman Army Museum.

They lead to a former quarry turned country park below the Walltown crags and to Magna, the fort beside the Roman Army Museum.
