Curius Compass is two local guides based near Hexham. Their Hadrian's Wall Highlights walk meets at 10am at The Sill, takes the AD122 bus to Housesteads and walks back along the Wall. They also run a full-day Grand Day Out, a short tour by car, and a women-only walk of the whole Wall. There is no published street address; the pin is on The Sill at Once Brewed, where the walks meet.
