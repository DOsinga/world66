Hexham is a handsome market town on a terrace above the River Tyne, and the most comfortable base for [Hadrian's Wall](/europe/unitedkingdom/england/north_east/hadrians_wall): the AD122 bus runs from here along the wall in summer, and the trains on the Tyne Valley line connect it with Newcastle and Carlisle.

The town grew up around [Hexham Abbey](/europe/unitedkingdom/england/north_east/hexham/hexham_abbey), founded by Saint Wilfrid in 674. Beneath the church his Saxon crypt survives, built from stones the monks took from the Roman town at [Corbridge](/europe/unitedkingdom/england/north_east/corbridge). Around the Market Place are the Moot Hall and the [Old Gaol](/europe/unitedkingdom/england/north_east/hexham/hexham_old_gaol) of the 1330s, now a museum of the border reivers. There is a farmers' market twice a month and a good spread of independent shops, cafés and pubs.
