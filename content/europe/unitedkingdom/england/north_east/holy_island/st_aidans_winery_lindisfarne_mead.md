St Aidan's Winery makes Lindisfarne Mead on the island from grapes, honey and water from the island's artesian well. Its showroom by the market place has been open since 1968 and gives adult visitors a free sample. It also sells fruit wines, ales and spirits. Check the causeway safe-crossing times before you go.
