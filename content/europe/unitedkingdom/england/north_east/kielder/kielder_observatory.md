Kielder Observatory is a public observatory and educational charity on Black Fell, two miles up a forest track above Kielder Water. Every event is ticketed and must be booked ahead; nothing is open outside event times. The team also runs a satellite site at the Twice Brewed Inn on Hadrian's Wall.
