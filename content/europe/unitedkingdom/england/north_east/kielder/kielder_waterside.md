Kielder Waterside, formerly Leaplish, is Northumbrian Water's visitor centre on the south shore of Kielder Water. You can book instructor-led canoeing, kayaking and paddleboarding, archery and tomahawk throwing, pleasure-boat hire and the lake ferry. The shop opens at 6am for fishing permits and boat hire on the reservoir. The pin is approximate.
