Newcastle's best local writing comes from people who live in a particular corner of it. A novelist back home on Tyneside sends weekly notes on the bookshops, galleries and theatres she actually goes to. The trust that looks after the Ouseburn valley digs into its own oral-history archive. A volunteer history group has spent more than a decade researching one suburb, Heaton, street by street. And one man has photographed the city's buildings, demolitions and new builds since 2005.

Between them they lead to a wartime air-raid shelter under the city, a medieval ruin in a Victorian park, two new bookshops in Heaton and the Byker Wall.
