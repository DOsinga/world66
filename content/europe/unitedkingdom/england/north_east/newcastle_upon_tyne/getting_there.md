---
title: "Getting There"
type: section
---

Newcastle Central is on the East Coast Main Line — three hours from London King's Cross, ninety minutes from Edinburgh, an hour from York. Newcastle International Airport sits eight miles north of the centre and is on the Metro, twenty-five minutes door to door. The A1 and A19 bring drivers in from north and south, and the DFDS ferry from IJmuiden (Amsterdam) docks at North Shields, twenty minutes by Metro from town.
