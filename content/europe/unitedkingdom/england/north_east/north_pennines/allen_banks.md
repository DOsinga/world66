Ancient woodland along the gorge of the River Allen, south of Bardon Mill. The National Trust's Allen Banks estate and the wildlife trust's Briarwood Banks reserve run up the valley. Paths follow the river to Plankey Mill and Staward Peel. The car park is at Ridley Bridge.
