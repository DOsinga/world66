The North Pennines gets far fewer visitors than the Lakes or the Dales, and far less is written about it. These two are the honest count: the National Landscape team, whose blog gives the dales' community projects a voice, and the Teesdale Mercury, the dale's own newspaper since 1854.

They lead to a restored lead miner's well in Allendale and to the Bowes Museum in Barnard Castle.
