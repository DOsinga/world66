Warkworth Castle sits on a loop of the River Coquet above the village of Warkworth, seven miles south of Alnwick. English Heritage manages the site, and the remains are impressive — the great 14th-century keep standing to its full height, the hermitage chapel accessible only by boat along the river, and extensive curtain walls enclosing a large courtyard.

The keep is the architectural showpiece: built to a cross-shaped plan in the 1390s by Henry Percy (Hotspur's father), it is one of the most sophisticated examples of late medieval domestic architecture in England. The rooms are mainly empty but the structure is complete enough to walk through and understand as a building. Allow a full morning to see the castle and the hermitage (boat trips run in season), and if you park in the village you can walk up through the old gateway and over the river bridge for a more atmospheric approach.
