Lindisfarne Castle began as a 16th-century fort on a rocky outcrop above Holy Island. In the early 20th century, Edwin Lutyens refashioned it into a stylish Arts and Crafts house, with a walled garden by Gertrude Jekyll. Access depends on the tides, so check crossing times before planning a visit.
