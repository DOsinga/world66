Most writing about this coast is about Bamburgh and Holy Island. The local writing is about the stretches in between. A photographer posts a picture and a few lines from his runs between Warkworth, Alnmouth and Embleton almost every week. A birder has written up the Boulmer coast for twenty years. Amble's community paper covers its harbour and beaches. And the county's tourism site has a guide to the places visitors miss.

They point to a tidal cove below Howick, the cross on the hill opposite Alnmouth, a hay meadow at Newton Point and Amble's Little Shore.
