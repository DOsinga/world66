A low hill on the south side of the Aln estuary, cut off from Alnmouth when a storm in 1806 moved the river. A wooden cross marks the site of the village's old church, and there are wide views over the village and dunes. It is reached on foot along the coast from Warkworth or along the estuary. The pin is approximate.
