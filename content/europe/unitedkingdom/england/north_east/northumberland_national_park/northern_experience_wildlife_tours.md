Northern Experience has guided birdwatchers and photographers across Northumberland, the North Pennines and the Borders for nearly 20 years. It runs safari days, otter safaris, trips for Kielder ospreys and Lindisfarne seals, photography workshops, North Sea pelagics for white-beaked dolphins and minke whales, and dark-sky evenings.
