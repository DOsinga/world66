Rothbury is a small stone town on the River Coquet, with a long sloping green and the Simonside Hills rising to the south. It is the main gateway to the middle of [Northumberland National Park](/europe/unitedkingdom/england/north_east/northumberland_national_park), and to Upper Coquetdale beyond.

Just outside the town is [Cragside](/europe/unitedkingdom/england/north_east/rothbury/cragside), the house of the Victorian industrialist Lord Armstrong and the first house in the world lit by hydroelectricity. A few miles east, the Norman church of [Brinkburn Priory](/europe/unitedkingdom/england/north_east/rothbury/brinkburn_priory) stands alone in a loop of the river. The [Simonside Hills](/europe/unitedkingdom/england/north_east/rothbury/simonside_hills) make a good half-day walk, with views from the crags over the whole of Coquetdale to the Cheviots.
