Seahouses is where the Farne Islands boats leave from. Several operators run trips from kiosks along the harbour, from short circuits past the seals to landings on Inner Farne and Staple Island in the puffin season. The harbour is also the base for diving on the Farnes' wrecks and seal colonies, and for kayaking and coasteering on this stretch of coast.
