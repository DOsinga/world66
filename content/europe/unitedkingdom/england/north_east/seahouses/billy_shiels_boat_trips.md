Billy Shiel's runs daily sailings from Seahouses harbour to the Farne Islands, weather permitting, with half-day and full-day landing trips, grey seal cruises and sailings to Lindisfarne. Tickets are sold online, at the booking office on Seafield Road and at the kiosk on the harbour (NE68 7RN). Landing on the islands means paying the National Trust fee on top.
