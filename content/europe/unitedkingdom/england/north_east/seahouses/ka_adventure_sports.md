KA Adventure Sports has run water sports on this coast since 2009, from its base in the Beadnell Bay car park, two miles south of Seahouses. It teaches surfing at Beadnell or Bamburgh depending on conditions, and also runs kitesurfing lessons, stand-up paddleboarding and coasteering between Bamburgh and Howick. The pin is approximate.
