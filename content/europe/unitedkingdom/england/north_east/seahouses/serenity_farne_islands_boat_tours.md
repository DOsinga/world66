Serenity sails from Seahouses harbour on Serenity II, a purpose-built catamaran, with grey seal cruises, landing trips to Inner Farne and Staple Island, and sunset cruises. The seasonal booking office is on the harbour; the company's postal address is 7 Osborne Gardens, Seahouses.
