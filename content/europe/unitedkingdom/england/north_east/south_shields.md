South Shields faces Tynemouth across the mouth of the Tyne; the Shields Ferry crosses to North Shields in a few minutes. It is a working seaside town with long sandy beaches, a promenade and some of the best-loved fish and chips in the North East.

On the Lawe Top above the harbour is [Arbeia](/europe/unitedkingdom/england/north_east/south_shields/arbeia_roman_fort), the Roman supply fort for Hadrian's Wall, with its west gate rebuilt to full height. South of the town the coast turns to cliffs at [Marsden Bay](/europe/unitedkingdom/england/north_east/south_shields/marsden_rock), where seabirds nest on Marsden Rock, and the red-and-white [Souter Lighthouse](/europe/unitedkingdom/england/north_east/sunderland/souter_lighthouse) stands a little further on.
