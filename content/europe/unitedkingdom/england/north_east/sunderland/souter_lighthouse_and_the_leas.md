Souter Lighthouse was opened in 1871 and is often noted as the first lighthouse built to run on alternating electric current. The National Trust now looks after the lighthouse and the surrounding coastal grassland of The Leas.

The appeal is partly the tower and partly the walk: sea cliffs, open views, and a working-coast atmosphere just north of Sunderland.
