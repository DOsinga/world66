---
title: Things to Do
type: section
---

Sunderland's main attractions cluster in two areas: the city centre, around the museum and winter gardens, and the north bank of the Wear where the National Glass Centre, the university campus, and St Peter's Church sit in close succession. The seafront at Roker and Seaburn is a fifteen-minute bus ride from the centre and worth the trip, particularly for the pier and the setting of St Andrew's Church.
