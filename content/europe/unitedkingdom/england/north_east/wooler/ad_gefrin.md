Ad Gefrin distils whisky and gin on South Road in Wooler and pairs it with a museum about the Anglo-Saxon royal palace at nearby Yeavering. Visitors book the distillery tour and tasting online, or join other tours, guided walks and afternoon tea at the bistro.
