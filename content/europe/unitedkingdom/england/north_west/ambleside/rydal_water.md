Rydal Water is one of the gentlest beautiful walks near Ambleside. The lake lies west of town between Rydal and Grasmere, with paths along the shore and up onto Loughrigg Terrace for wider views.

This is the walk to choose when the high fells are clagged in or your legs want a quieter day. It pairs naturally with Rydal Mount and can be extended toward Grasmere if the weather behaves.
