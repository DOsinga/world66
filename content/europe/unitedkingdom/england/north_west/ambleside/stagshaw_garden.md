Stagshaw Garden is a small National Trust woodland garden on the slopes above Waterhead. It is at its best in spring and early summer, when the rhododendrons, azaleas, and camellias bring colour to a damp, mossy hillside.

Do not expect formal beds or a grand estate. This is a quiet, informal detour that works well before or after a lake cruise, especially if you want a softer alternative to another fell climb.
