Blackpool Tower is the town's defining landmark, a 158-metre iron structure that rises from the promenade with complete confidence. It is part viewing tower, part entertainment complex, and part civic logo.

Go up for the seafront view if the weather is clear, but do not ignore what is inside. The circus and ballroom are as much a part of the Blackpool story as the view from the top.
