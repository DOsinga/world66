Central Pier is the brash one: amusement machines, rides, food stalls, and a big wheel looking back toward the Tower. It sits in the thick of the resort and makes no apology for wanting your spare change.

It is not refined, but it is very Blackpool. Come for a short wander, a view, and the full soundscape of gulls, machines, music, and families negotiating what to do next.
