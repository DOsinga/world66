Bolton Steam Museum preserves large stationary steam engines associated with Lancashire's textile and industrial history. Seeing these engines in motion, when steaming days are scheduled, makes the engineering far more vivid than static displays.

The museum is a strong specialty stop for industrial-history visitors and families who like machinery. Check operating days before travelling, since the difference between a quiet collection and working engines is significant.
