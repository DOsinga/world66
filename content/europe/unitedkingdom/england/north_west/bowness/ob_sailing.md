A sailing school and yacht charter company on the jetties at Ferry Nab, a short walk south of Bowness. It hires out 31 to 34 ft Beneteau cruising yachts, runs half- and full-day skippered sails, and teaches RYA sail cruising and powerboat courses on the lake. Enquiries go through the phone or a contact form.
