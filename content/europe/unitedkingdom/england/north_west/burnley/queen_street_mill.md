Queen Street Mill is one of Lancashire's most important textile heritage sites, preserving a working
weaving shed from the age when cotton shaped Burnley and the surrounding Pennine towns. Its rows of
looms, engine house, and mill buildings make the scale and noise of industrial weaving easier to
imagine than a static display ever could.

For travellers interested in northern England's industrial story, it is a strong complement to canal
walks, mill towns, and Manchester's larger museums. Check opening arrangements before travelling, as
heritage sites of this kind can have limited days, but the place is worth the effort when
demonstrations or guided access are available.
