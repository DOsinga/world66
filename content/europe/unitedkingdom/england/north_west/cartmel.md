Cartmel is a small village on the Cartmel peninsula, a few miles inland from the north shore of Morecambe Bay, and it punches well above its size. At its centre is [Cartmel Priory](/europe/unitedkingdom/england/north_west/cartmel/cartmel_priory), a huge 12th-century church that survived the dissolution of the monasteries because it was also the parish church. Around it are a little square, a stream, a gatehouse and a handful of pubs and shops, one of which made Cartmel sticky toffee pudding famous.

The village also has a small, much-loved [racecourse](/europe/unitedkingdom/england/north_west/cartmel/cartmel_racecourse), with jump meetings in late spring and summer, and a very good food reputation. Two miles away is [Holker Hall](/europe/unitedkingdom/england/north_west/cartmel/holker_hall), with its gardens and deer park, and Grange-over-Sands, an Edwardian resort on the bay.
