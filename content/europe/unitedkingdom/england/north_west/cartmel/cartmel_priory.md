Founded in 1190, the priory church survived the dissolution because the villagers argued that it was their parish church, and it is still in use. It is far bigger than the village around it, with an unusual tower set diagonally on top of the crossing, carved 15th-century choir stalls and a great east window. The priory gatehouse in the square is also medieval. Entry is free.
