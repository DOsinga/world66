A country house of the Cavendish family, rebuilt in the 1870s after a fire, with grand panelled rooms open to visitors in season. The 25 acres of gardens include a cascade, a labyrinth and one of the oldest lime trees in England, and there is a deer park and a food hall selling produce from the estate.
