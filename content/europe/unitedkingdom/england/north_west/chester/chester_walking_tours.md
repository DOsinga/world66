Chester Walking Tours are run by the city's Guild of Tour Guides. The main Chester Tour sets off every day of the year at 10.30am, with extra 2pm tours from Friday to Sunday between Easter and October, and lasts about 90 minutes. There are also ghost tours, a walk round the full circuit of the walls, and tours of the Rows and the Town Hall. Tours meet by the red pillar box outside the Town Hall on Northgate Street; book online.
