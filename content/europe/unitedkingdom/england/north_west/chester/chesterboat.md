ChesterBoat is a family-run company that has run boats on the River Dee for over forty years, from the landing stages on The Groves below the city walls. The half-hour City Cruise sails daily; on selected dates from Easter to September the two-hour Iron Bridge Cruise goes upriver through the Duke of Westminster's Eaton Estate. It also hires out a picnic boat for up to eight people and runs themed evening cruises.
