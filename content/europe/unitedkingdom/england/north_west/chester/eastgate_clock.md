Eastgate Clock stands above Chester's city walls on an 18th-century gatehouse, with the clock itself added in 1899. It is one of the city's most photographed landmarks and a natural pause while walking the walls or exploring the Rows.

The stop is brief, but the setting is strong: the clock frames the main shopping street below, with the cathedral and the Roman wall circuit both close by.
