Paddle the Dee, the trading name of Dee River Kayaking, runs guided kayak and stand-up paddleboard trips on the River Dee, with instructors qualified through British Canoeing. The Chester trips paddle the flat water past Chester Meadows, the Groves and the Old Dee Bridge; whitewater sessions run on the Dee at Llangollen in North Wales. Most trips start from Sandy Lane Park in Chester.
