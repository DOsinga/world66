Roman Tours walks visitors round the remains of Deva, the largest Roman fortress in Britain, with a guide dressed and equipped as a legionary soldier. There is also a medieval tour set in Chester in 1403, and school sessions run with the Grosvenor Museum. The company has no public premises; tours are booked in advance by phone or email, and the site also gives a landline, +44 1978 761264.
