Walking Food Tours UK is a Chester company that runs guided food and drink walks in Chester, Liverpool, Manchester and Shrewsbury, each with tastings at four or five independent shops, cafes and bars. In Liverpool the Taste of Liverpool tour meets outside The Beatles Story at the Royal Albert Dock; there is also a wine tour and a ciabatta-making class. The office is on the Rows on Bridge Street.
