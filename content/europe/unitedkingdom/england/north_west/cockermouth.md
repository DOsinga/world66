Cockermouth is a handsome market town of Georgian houses and wide streets where the River Cocker meets the Derwent, on the north-western edge of the [Lake District](/europe/unitedkingdom/england/north_west/lakedistrict). William Wordsworth was born here in 1770, and the house where he grew up, [Wordsworth House](/europe/unitedkingdom/england/north_west/cockermouth/wordsworth_house), is at the west end of Main Street.

The town was badly flooded in 2009 and again in 2015, and has since rebuilt its shops and cafés along Main Street and Market Place. It makes a quieter base than [Keswick](/europe/unitedkingdom/england/north_west/keswick) for the north-western fells and lakes: Bassenthwaite, Crummock Water and Buttermere are all within a short drive.
