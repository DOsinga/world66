The Georgian house on Main Street where William Wordsworth was born in 1770 and spent his early childhood. The National Trust has furnished it as it would have been in the 1770s, with costumed guides, a working kitchen and the walled garden running down to the River Derwent, which the poet remembered in *The Prelude*.
