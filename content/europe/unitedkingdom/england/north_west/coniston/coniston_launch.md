Launches leave Coniston pier on three routes round the lake: a 45-minute northern circuit, an hour to Wild Cat Island and an 80-minute southern cruise. Themed 90-minute cruises cover Arthur Ransome's Swallows and Amazons and the Campbells' water speed records. The ticket office is on Lake Road; bookings go through the website or by phone.
