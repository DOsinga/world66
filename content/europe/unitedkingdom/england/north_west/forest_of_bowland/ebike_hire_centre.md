The Ebike Hire Centre is in the Old Vicarage at Tosside, on the edge of Gisburn Forest, the largest forest in Lancashire and the main mountain-biking centre in the Forest of Bowland. It hires out mountain bikes and electric mountain bikes in a range of sizes, sells e-bikes, and serves food and drink. Parking is free and the trails start a short ride away.
