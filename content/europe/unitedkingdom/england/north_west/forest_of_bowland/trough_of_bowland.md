A narrow road climbs from the valley of the Hodder at Dunsop Bridge over the pass and down towards Lancaster, through steep-sided valleys of bracken and heather. A boundary stone at the top once marked the border between Lancashire and Yorkshire. There are laybys and picnic spots by the streams, and paths up onto the fells. The road is narrow and busy on summer weekends.
