Glenridding Sailing Centre is on the spit of land by the lake at the southern end of Ullswater. It hires sailing dinghies, canoes and kayaks, and runs RYA sailing courses and taster sessions. The centre has changing rooms and hot showers, and is open from spring to autumn. The pin is approximate.
