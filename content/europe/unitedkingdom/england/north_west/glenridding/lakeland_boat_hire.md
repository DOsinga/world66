Lakeland Boat Hire operates from Rivermouth at Eusemere, at the northern end of Ullswater beside Pooley Bridge. It hires self-drive motor boats, rowing boats and Canadian canoes by the hour. It opens at weekends and in school holidays, weather permitting, and publishes its open days on a calendar on the site.
