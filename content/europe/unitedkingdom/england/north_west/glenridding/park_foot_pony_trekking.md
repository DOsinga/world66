Park Foot Pony Trekking is on the Howtown road just outside Pooley Bridge, and its treks leave the lakeshore and climb onto the open fells without using roads. Rides suit beginners as well as experienced riders. The stables take bookings by phone between 9am and 5pm or by email, and say they do not answer WhatsApp, text or social media messages.
