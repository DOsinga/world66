St Patrick's Boat Landing hires self-drive motor boats and rowing boats by the hour from its landing on the Patterdale road just outside Glenridding. No experience is needed for the motor boats, and an hour is enough to reach the wooded islands and bays at the head of the lake. Opening is reduced outside school holidays. The pin is approximate.
