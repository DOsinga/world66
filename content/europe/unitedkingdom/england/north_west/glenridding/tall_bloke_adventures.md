Tall Bloke Adventures runs half-day guided kayak trips on Ullswater for groups of two to four, in double sea kayaks chosen for stability, so no experience is needed. Morning and afternoon trips go out to the islands and beaches of the lake's southern reach. Trips start from the gate next to Glenridding Bridge; the minimum age is 14.
