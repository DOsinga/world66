Ullswater Paddleboarding teaches stand-up paddleboarding, wingsurfing and wing foiling with ASI-qualified instructors, and runs guided kayak tours of the lake. It also hires paddleboards, kayaks and canoes with wetsuits and buoyancy aids, and runs archery and rafted canoeing for groups. The site publishes no street address; the meeting point is given at booking.
