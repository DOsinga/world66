A tea garden on the shore of Grasmere, a short walk from the village along Red Bank Road, that hires out rowing boats on the lake. There is no need to book; it opens from 10.30 to 17.00, Friday to Tuesday, weather permitting, and is closed on Wednesdays and Thursdays.
