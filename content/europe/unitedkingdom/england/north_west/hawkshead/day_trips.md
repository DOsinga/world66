Hawkshead is well placed for the southern Lakes. [Coniston](/europe/unitedkingdom/england/north_west/coniston) is twenty minutes' drive west and has Ruskin's house Brantwood and the steam yacht Gondola. [Grasmere](/europe/unitedkingdom/england/north_west/grasmere) and Wordsworth's Dove Cottage are reachable in half an hour by car. The Windermere shore towns — [Bowness](/europe/unitedkingdom/england/north_west/bowness) and [Ambleside](/europe/unitedkingdom/england/north_west/ambleside) — are only ten minutes by ferry.
