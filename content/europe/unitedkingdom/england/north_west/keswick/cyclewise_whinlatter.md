Cyclewise runs the bike shop and hire centre at the Whinlatter Forest visitor centre above Braithwaite, where the forest's waymarked mountain bike trails start. It hires mountain bikes and e-bikes for three hours or a full day, and runs skills courses, kids' camps and private tuition. The company also has a road and e-bike shop in Cockermouth (+44 1900 821998). The pin is approximate.
