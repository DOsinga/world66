Derwentwater Marina sits on the lake shore at Portinscale, a short walk across the fields from Keswick. It hires kayaks, Canadian canoes and stand-up paddleboards, and runs taster sessions and courses in paddling and sailing. There is a watersports shop on site, and the marina also sells boats and outboard motors. The pin is approximate.
