Go Ape's Whinlatter course is strung through the trees of Whinlatter Forest, on the pass road above Braithwaite and Keswick. It runs a full Treetop Adventure Plus course from age six, a smaller Treetop Adventure for younger children, and Sundown Adventures with lit obstacles after dark. Booking is online; the site publishes no phone number. The ground is steep, and parking is in the Forestry England car park, which charges. The pin is approximate.
