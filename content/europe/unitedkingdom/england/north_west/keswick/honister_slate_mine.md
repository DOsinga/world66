Honister Slate Mine is at the top of Honister Pass, between Borrowdale and Buttermere, and still quarries green slate. Visitors can take guided tours into the mine workings or clip on to the via ferrata, a fixed-cable climbing route up the face of Fleetwith Pike that follows the line the miners once used.
