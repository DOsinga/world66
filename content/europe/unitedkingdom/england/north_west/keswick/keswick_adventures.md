Keswick Adventures takes small groups ghyll scrambling and canyoning in the becks around Borrowdale and Newlands, and also runs rock climbing and abseiling sessions, canoeing, kayaking and paddleboarding on Derwentwater. Its base is on Crosthwaite Road on the north-west edge of town.
