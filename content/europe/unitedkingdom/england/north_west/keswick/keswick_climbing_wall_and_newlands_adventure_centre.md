The company runs two sites: the Keswick Climbing Wall at Goosewell Farm, on the lane up towards Castlerigg, and the Newlands Adventure Centre at Stair in the Newlands valley (+44 17687 78463, info@activity-centre.com). Between them they offer indoor climbing and bouldering, outdoor rock climbing and abseiling, ghyll scrambling, canoeing, archery and high ropes. The climbing wall is the wet-day option for families in Keswick.
