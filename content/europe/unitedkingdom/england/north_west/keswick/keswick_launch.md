Keswick Launch runs the passenger launches that circle Derwentwater in both directions, calling at seven jetties: Keswick, Ashness Gate, Lodore, High and Low Brandelhow, Hawes End and Nichol End. Hawes End is the stop for Catbells. The ticket office is on the foreshore at the bottom of Lake Road, where the company also hires out boats, and launches can be chartered for private cruises.
