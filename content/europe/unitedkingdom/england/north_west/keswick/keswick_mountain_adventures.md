Keswick Mountain Adventures is based at Seathwaite Farm, at the end of the Borrowdale road and at the foot of Scafell Pike and Great Gable. It runs rock climbing, scrambling, mountaineering, and mountain walking with navigation training, as well as guided walks for people who want company on the higher fells. The site also gives a landline, 01768 622017.
