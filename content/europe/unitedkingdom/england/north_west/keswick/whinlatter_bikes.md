Whinlatter Bikes is a bike shop on Main Street in the centre of Keswick, despite the name, which hires mountain bikes and e-bikes and takes bookings online. It also sells and repairs bikes. The old railway path to Threlkeld and the climb to Whinlatter Forest both start within a few minutes' ride.
