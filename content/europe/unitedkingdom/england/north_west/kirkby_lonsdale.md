Kirkby Lonsdale is a small, well-kept market town above the River Lune, between the [Lake District](/europe/unitedkingdom/england/north_west/lakedistrict) and the Yorkshire Dales. Its streets of stone houses, old inns and independent shops lead to St Mary's church, and behind the churchyard a path runs to [Ruskin's View](/europe/unitedkingdom/england/north_west/kirkby_lonsdale/ruskins_view), a view over the Lune valley that Turner painted and that John Ruskin called one of the loveliest in England.

On the edge of town the River Lune is crossed by [Devil's Bridge](/europe/unitedkingdom/england/north_west/kirkby_lonsdale/devils_bridge), a medieval three-arched bridge that draws motorcyclists on Sunday mornings and, in summer, swimmers to the river below. The market is on Thursdays.
