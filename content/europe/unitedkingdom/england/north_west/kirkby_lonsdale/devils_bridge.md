A narrow stone bridge with three ribbed arches, high over the River Lune, probably built in the 14th or 15th century. Legend says the Devil built it in exchange for the first soul to cross, and was cheated with a dog. It is closed to traffic, and on Sunday mornings the lay-by and snack van beside it are a well-known meeting point for motorcyclists.
