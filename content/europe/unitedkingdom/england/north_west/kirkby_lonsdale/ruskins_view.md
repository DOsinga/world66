A terrace walk behind St Mary's church looks down on a bend of the River Lune and across to the fells. Turner painted it in 1822, and John Ruskin, who saw the painting, wrote that it was "one of the loveliest views in England, therefore in the world". A path, the Radical Steps, leads down from here to the river.
