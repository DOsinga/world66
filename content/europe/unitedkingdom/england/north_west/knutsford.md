Knutsford is a prosperous market town in Cheshire, 15 miles south-west of [Manchester](/europe/unitedkingdom/england/north_west/manchester). The novelist Elizabeth Gaskell grew up here, and the town is the model for her *Cranford*; the narrow lanes of King Street and Princess Street still have something of that small-town feel, with old inns, independent shops and cafés. Its oddest building is the [Gaskell Memorial Tower](/europe/unitedkingdom/england/north_west/knutsford/gaskell_memorial_tower), built in her honour in an Italianate style.

The main reason to come is [Tatton Park](/europe/unitedkingdom/england/north_west/knutsford/tatton_park), whose gates are at the end of King Street: a neoclassical mansion, formal gardens, a Japanese garden and a deer park of a thousand acres.
