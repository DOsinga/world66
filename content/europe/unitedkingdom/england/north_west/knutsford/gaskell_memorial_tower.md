An eccentric tower and building in an Italianate style on King Street, built in 1907 by Richard Harding Watt in memory of Elizabeth Gaskell, with a bronze plaque of the novelist and a list of her works. Watt, a glove maker turned builder, put up several other unusual buildings around the town. The pin is on King Street.
