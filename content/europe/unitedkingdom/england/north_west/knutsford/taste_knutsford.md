Taste Knutsford is a walking food tour of the town, run from the Knutsford Town Council offices. A guide takes groups of up to 12 through the narrow streets to seven or eight independent food and drink businesses, with tastings that change with the seasons, from charcuterie and tapas to ice cream, afternoon tea and gin. Public tours run twice a month, and private tours are available for groups of eight or more.
