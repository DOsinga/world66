One of the great country estates of the North West, run by the National Trust and Cheshire East Council. The neoclassical mansion of the Egerton family has its original collections; the gardens include a Japanese garden, a fernery and an Italian terrace; and red and fallow deer roam the parkland around two meres. There is a rare-breeds farm and the Old Hall, a medieval hall house. Pedestrian access is from the end of King Street in Knutsford.
