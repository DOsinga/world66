Air Ventures is a BHPA-registered paragliding school working from launch sites around the Lake District, with tandem flights, taster days and full courses through to pilot rating, plus speed-flying instruction. It flies seven days a week when the weather allows. The school publishes no premises; meeting places are given when a booking is confirmed.
