Climbing 4 All is Nick, an IFMGA mountain guide, and Sarah, a mountain instructor, who guide rock climbs and scrambles in the Lake District, usually at a ratio of one client to one guide. They also run skills courses and Alpine preparation courses, and lead mountaineering trips in the French and Swiss Alps. There are no premises; meeting points depend on the crag.
