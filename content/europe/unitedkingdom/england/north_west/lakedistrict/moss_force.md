Moss Force is a waterfall near Newlands Hause above Buttermere. Short paths lead toward the lower and middle cascades, while reaching the upper falls involves rougher ground and some scrambling. It is a compact stop with good views in a part of the Lake District closely associated with Alfred Wainwright's walks.
