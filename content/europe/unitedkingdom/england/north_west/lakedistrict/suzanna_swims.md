Suzanna Swims runs guided wild swimming for individuals and groups across the Lake District: dips in tarns, floats down rivers and lake crossings, combined with walks to reach them. Suzanna is an RLSS open water lifeguard and hill walking leader who has run the business since 2016. The site gives a WhatsApp number for quick enquiries. There are no premises; swim locations are given at booking.
