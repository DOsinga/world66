Lancaster Brewery makes its ales in a former bottling plant on the Lancaster Leisure Park, on the eastern edge of the city. Tours follow the brewing process from the malt silo to the cold store and end with tasters in the brewery tap, which opens at weekends; there are also distillery tours. Book online or by email.
