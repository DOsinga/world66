Lancaster Cathedral is a Roman Catholic cathedral built in stages from 1857 and raised to cathedral status in 1924. Its Victorian Gothic nave, baptistry, and organ are the main reasons to step inside.

It is an active place of worship, so visit quietly and work around services. The building pairs well with nearby Williamson Park and the Ashton Memorial.
