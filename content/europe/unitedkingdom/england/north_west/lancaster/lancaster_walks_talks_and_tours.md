Lancaster Walks, Talks & Tours is a small company owned by a Blue Badge guide, running public and private walking tours of Lancaster and illustrated talks on the history of the North West. Public tours, including evening ghost walks and a tour on the Pendle witch trials of 1612, are sold through Eventbrite. There are no premises.
