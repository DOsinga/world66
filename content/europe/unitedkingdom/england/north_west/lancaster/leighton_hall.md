Leighton Hall is a lived-in Georgian country house north of Lancaster, rebuilt after the original hall was burned for its owners' Jacobite loyalties. The appeal is domestic rather than grand-palatial: furnished rooms, gardens, and a relaxed estate visit, with birds of prey often adding some theatre in the afternoon.
