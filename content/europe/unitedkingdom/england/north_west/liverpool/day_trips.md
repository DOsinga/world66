---
title: Day Trips
type: section
---

**Port Sunlight (15 minutes by Merseyrail).** William Lever's model village across the Mersey, built for his Sunlight Soap factory workers in the 1880s. The Lady Lever Art Gallery alone justifies the trip, and the village itself — Arts and Crafts cottages on planned curving streets — is a UNESCO-tentative-list site.

**Chester (40 minutes by train).** A handsome walled Roman and Tudor city to the south, with a complete circuit of medieval walls, a half-timbered shopping street (the unique two-tier Rows), and a Roman amphitheatre. Easy half-day from Liverpool.

**Crosby Beach and Another Place (25 minutes by Merseyrail).** Antony Gormley's 100 cast-iron figures gazing out to sea on a wide sandy beach. Combine with lunch on the seafront.

**Manchester (50 minutes by train).** Liverpool's old rival, with a different industrial heritage and a rich music history of its own — the Manchester Art Gallery, Whitworth Gallery, Northern Quarter and the football museums.

**Speke Hall (40 minutes by bus).** A Tudor half-timbered manor house six miles south, in National Trust care, with a fine Great Hall and gardens.
