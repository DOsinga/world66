Fab 4 Taxi Tours has run Beatles tours in Liverpool black cabs since 2007, for up to six people per cab. Tours run from two to eight hours, the longest including the Casbah Coffee Club in West Derby. The driver-guides pick up anywhere in the city centre, at the Royal Albert Dock or at the airport; the Jordan Street address is a virtual office, and no phone number is published, so booking is online or by email.
