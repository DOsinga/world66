Liverpool Cycle Tours is an independent company with local guides that runs guided e-bike tours from King's Dock Street, near the Royal Albert Dock. The 90-minute sightseeing ride covers the waterfront and both cathedrals; the Beatles e-bike tour runs for three and a half hours out to the suburbs, and another ride goes to Anfield and Everton's grounds. It also runs walking tours and a historic pub crawl.
