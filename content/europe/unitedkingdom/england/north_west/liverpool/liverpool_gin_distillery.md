The Liverpool Gin Distillery makes Liverpool Gin in a copper still on Castle Street, in the business district behind the Pier Head, and shares the building with the Hawkshead Tap House. Besides the bars it sells gin experiences and a gin school, where you pick botanicals and distil a bottle to your own recipe. It is closed on Mondays.
