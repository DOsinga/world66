The Liverpool Watersports Centre sits on Queen's Dock, a few minutes' walk south of the Royal Albert Dock, and is run by the charity Local Solutions. It hires out sit-on-top kayaks and stand-up paddleboards by the hour on the enclosed dock water, runs lessons and courses, and puts up an inflatable aqua park in summer. Changing rooms, showers and parking are on site.
