The Magical Mystery Tour is run by the Cavern Club and leaves from Anchor Courtyard at the Royal Albert Dock in a painted bus. The two-hour route takes in George Harrison's birthplace, Penny Lane, Strawberry Field and the childhood homes of John Lennon and Paul McCartney, and the ticket includes entry to the Cavern Club on Mathew Street.
