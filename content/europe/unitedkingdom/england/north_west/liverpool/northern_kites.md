Northern Kites teaches kitesurfing, wingsurfing and pumpfoiling on the Wirral side of the Mersey, from Wallasey Beach Club on Coastal Drive and from the Wirral Watersports Centre on West Kirby Marine Lake. Lessons are planned around wind and tides, and the school posts forecasts and spare slots in a WhatsApp group. The site gives a WhatsApp number for bookings.
