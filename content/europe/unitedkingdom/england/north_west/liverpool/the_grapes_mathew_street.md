The Cavern Club never sold alcohol, so for the bands playing there in the early 1960s, the Grapes — diagonally across Mathew Street — was the actual local. The Beatles drank here as often as they played next door, and so did Gerry and the Pacemakers, the Searchers, and the Swinging Blue Jeans; Pete Best came here to drown his sorrows the night he was sacked from the band, and George Harrison picked up a black eye outside over the same falling-out.

A well-known photograph of the four Beatles drinking on a bench was taken here, and both the bench and the wallpaper behind it are still in place, protected under glass. It is squarely on the Beatles trail now, but it's the genuine location rather than a recreation, and still a working pub rather than a museum.
