A small, unpretentious free house named after the Liverpool-born historian and abolitionist William Roscoe, this pub opened in 1830 and has quietly outlasted almost everything around it. It was included in the very first CAMRA Good Beer Guide in 1974 and has appeared in every edition since — one of only five pubs in the country, and the only one in the north of England, to manage that.

It nearly didn't survive to keep the record: a 2015 sale to a property developer triggered a "Save the Roscoe" campaign, run in part by long-serving landlady Carol Ann Ross, that eventually secured the freehold in 2020. Inside it's exactly what the reputation promises — several small rooms, well-kept cask ale, and regulars who have been coming for decades.
