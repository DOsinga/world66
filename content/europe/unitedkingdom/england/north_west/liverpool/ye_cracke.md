A cottage on this narrow Rice Street alley has held a pub since 1852, first as the Ruthin Castle and, by 1892, under its current name — a Liverpool word for a back alley. John Lennon and Stuart Sutcliffe drank here constantly while studying at the neighbouring art college, and it's where Lennon courted his first wife, Cynthia Powell; their tutor sometimes ran informal classes at a table in the corner.

The front bar keeps its original copper-topped counter and holds barely a dozen people, which is most of the appeal. It's a proper Georgian Quarter local rather than a themed stop on a Beatles tour, a five-minute walk from Hope Street and the two cathedrals.
