Lytham St Annes is two towns joined together on the Fylde coast, south of [Blackpool](/europe/unitedkingdom/england/north_west/blackpool) and a world away from it. Lytham, on the Ribble estuary, is the older and prettier: a Georgian and Victorian town with a long grassy green along the shore and a white [windmill](/europe/unitedkingdom/england/north_west/lytham_st_annes/lytham_windmill) as its landmark, and [Lytham Hall](/europe/unitedkingdom/england/north_west/lytham_st_annes/lytham_hall) in its park on the edge of town.

St Annes, three miles west, is a planned Victorian resort with a [pier](/europe/unitedkingdom/england/north_west/lytham_st_annes/st_annes_pier), a sandy beach backed by dunes, and Royal Lytham, one of the courses that hosts the Open Championship. Both towns are quiet, well kept and good for long walks by the sea.
