The watersports centre on Fairhaven Lake, the seafront boating lake between Lytham and St Annes, is promoted by Fylde Council's Discover Fylde. Two-hour have-a-go sessions let you try a canoe, a kayak and a paddleboard, and there are one-day paddleboard and British Canoeing Paddle Start courses, a two-day Paddle Discover course, private lessons and school-holiday camps. Bookings go through the council's tourist information line.
