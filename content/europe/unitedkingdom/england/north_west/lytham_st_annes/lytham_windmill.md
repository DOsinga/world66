A white tower mill built in 1805 that stands alone on the green between the town and the estuary, the most photographed building on the Fylde. It stopped grinding after a fire in 1918; inside there is now a small museum on the mill and the town, open in summer. Next door, the Old Lifeboat House has a lifeboat museum.
