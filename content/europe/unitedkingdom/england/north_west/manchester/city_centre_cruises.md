City Centre Cruises has run passenger boats on Manchester's canals for over 30 years, with two purpose-built barges that board on Liverpool Road in Castlefield. Scheduled cruises include Sunday lunch sailings and Manchester United match-day cruises to Old Trafford; the barges, with a bar and an onboard kitchen, are also hired out for parties, weddings and corporate groups.
