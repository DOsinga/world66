Craft Beer Tour Manchester runs a three-and-a-half-hour guided walk every Saturday at 1.30pm, visiting four breweries and taprooms in the city centre with ten tastings and a talk at each stop. It is run by the Craft Tour Company, whose registered office is in Bolton; the tour meets in Manchester city centre, and booking is online. No phone number is published.
