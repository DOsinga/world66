New Manchester Walks is run by the historian Ed Glinert, who has led several thousand tours of the city. It runs a calendar of public walks on themes from football and music to crime and radical politics, plus private walks and coach tours for groups. There are no premises; each walk has its own starting point, given in the online calendar.
