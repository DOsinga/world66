Manchester's most photographed pub sits alone on a small traffic island, wrapped floor to first floor in dark green, yellow-green and cream glazed tiles that were added in a remodelling around 1900. The tiled band running around the building spells out the pub's name and, on the west face, "Wines & Spirits" — a rare survivor of a style of pub decoration that almost no other British city kept this intact.

Inside, tiled pilasters frame every doorway and the corridor dado continues the tilework from the street. It has been run by the same landlady for over five decades, and its unusual, freestanding shape has made it a recurring filming location, including three episodes of the crime drama *Cracker*. A proper backstreet local, not a tourist set piece, despite the looks.
