The Portico Library and Gallery is a polished slice of early 19th-century Manchester. Long a private members' library, it now opens parts of the building to visitors through its public gallery and events programme.

Come for the atmosphere as much as the exhibitions: the reading room, shelves, and classical details give a glimpse of the city that grew rich on trade, industry, and argument. Check opening details before going, as access can vary.
