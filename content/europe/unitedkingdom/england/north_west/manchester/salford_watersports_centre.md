Salford Watersports Centre, run by Salford Community Leisure, sits on the dock water of Salford Quays, a tram ride west of central Manchester. It hires out stand-up paddleboards and kayaks for hour-long sessions that start with a short lesson, runs open-water swimming, canoeing, sailing and powerboat courses, and has changing rooms, showers and free parking. Evening paddle tours run under the lights of the Quays.
