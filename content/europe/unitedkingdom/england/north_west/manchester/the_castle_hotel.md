A pub has traded from this Oldham Street address since 1776, under a run of names — the Crown and Sceptre, the Crown and Anchor, the Clock Face — before Kay's Atlas Brewery settled on the Castle Hotel in the late 19th century. It is also where John Peel once interviewed Joy Division's Ian Curtis for a local fanzine.

The pub closed and fell into disrepair by 2008, then reopened the following year with a small music hall built onto the back: an 80-capacity room that has become one of Manchester's most reliable places to catch a band before they get big. The front bar keeps a solid cask ale selection and the Victorian fittings intact; the gig room is the reason regulars keep coming back.
