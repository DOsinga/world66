Crossing the bay on foot, from Arnside or Hest Bank to Kents Bank near Grange-over-Sands, is a walk of about eight miles over sand and through river channels. Because of the tides and quicksand it must only be done with the official King's Guide to the Sands, who leads walks on set dates between spring and autumn, often in aid of charities. Dates are published each year; you need to book through the organising charity and arrange transport back by train. The pin is on the sands of the bay.
