A bronze statue of Eric Morecambe in his famous "Bring Me Sunshine" pose, unveiled by the Queen in 1999. It stands on the promenade by the stone jetty, and the paving around it is inscribed with the names of stars who appeared with Morecambe and Wise. It is the most photographed thing in the town.
