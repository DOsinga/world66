A substantial ruin on the banks of the River Eamont, about a mile and a half south-east of the town. The keep dates from the early 13th century, and the castle stands next to the earthworks of the Roman fort of Brocavum. Lady Anne Clifford restored it in the 17th century and died here in 1676. It is run by English Heritage and you can climb the keep for views over the river.
