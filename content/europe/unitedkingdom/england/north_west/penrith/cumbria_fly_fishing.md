Glyn Freeman, an AAPGAI-qualified instructor, guides and teaches fly fishing for salmon, sea trout, brown trout and grayling on private water on the River Eden. Sessions are for one or two people, by the half day or full day, with permit, tackle and tuition included; anglers need their own Environment Agency rod licence. There are no premises; the meeting place on the river is given at booking.
