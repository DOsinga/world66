Lake District White Water Rafting runs guided raft trips on natural rivers. From April to August it is based at Lazonby in the Eden Valley north-east of Penrith, for a seven-mile, roughly five-hour trip down the Eden open to ages eight and up; from August to mid-April it moves to Newby Bridge in the south Lakes. No experience is needed. The pin is approximate.
