One of the largest stone circles in England, with around 60 stones set in an oval about 100 metres across, in farmland near the village of Little Salkeld, six miles north-east of Penrith. Long Meg herself is a tall sandstone pillar outside the circle, carved with spirals. Legend says the stones are a coven of witches turned to stone. Access is free; the narrow lane runs right through the circle.
