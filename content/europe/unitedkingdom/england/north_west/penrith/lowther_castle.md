A huge 19th-century castle built for the Earls of Lonsdale, abandoned and stripped in the 1950s, leaving a dramatic shell of turrets and empty windows. Since 2011 the gardens around it have been replanted and the ruin made safe to walk through, with an exhibition on the family and the estate and a large adventure playground. It is about five miles south of Penrith.
