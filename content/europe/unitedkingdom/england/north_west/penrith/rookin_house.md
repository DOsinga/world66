Rookin House is a working farm at Troutbeck, off the A66 about four miles from Ullswater and eight from both Keswick and Penrith. Its equestrian centre runs pony treks and riding lessons, and the farm's instructors also run quad biking, archery, air rifle and clay pigeon shooting sessions. The pin is approximate.
