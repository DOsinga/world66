Brindle Distillery makes Cuckoo Gin on a family farm in the village of Brindle, between Preston and Chorley. It runs gin tastings, gin-making workshops where you blend botanicals and distil your own bottle, and cocktail masterclasses,, and has its own bar, the Cuckoo's Nest.
