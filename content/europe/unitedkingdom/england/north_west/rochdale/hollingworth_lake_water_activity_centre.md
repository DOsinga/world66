The Water Activity Centre on Hollingworth Lake, below the Pennines at Littleborough, is run by Your Trust, Rochdale's cultural trust. It runs taster sessions and courses in kayaking, canoeing, sailing (with RYA qualifications) and stand-up paddleboarding, provides wetsuits, and hires out pedalos and small electric boats; the Lady Katherine runs passenger trips round the lake. It is an RYA Sailability centre, and opens Tuesday to Sunday from March to October. The pin is approximate.
