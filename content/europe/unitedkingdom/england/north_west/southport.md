Southport is a Victorian seaside resort on the coast north of [Liverpool](/europe/unitedkingdom/england/north_west/liverpool), and a more genteel one than its neighbour [Blackpool](/europe/unitedkingdom/england/north_west/blackpool). Its pride is [Lord Street](/europe/unitedkingdom/england/north_west/southport/lord_street), a long, wide boulevard of glass-roofed verandas, gardens and arcades, and its [pier](/europe/unitedkingdom/england/north_west/southport/southport_pier), the second longest in Britain, runs out across sands so wide that the sea is often out of sight.

The coast around it is a famous one for golf, with Royal Birkdale just south of the centre, and for nature. At Formby, eight miles south, the National Trust looks after pinewoods with red squirrels and big dunes, and inland, [Martin Mere](/europe/unitedkingdom/england/north_west/southport/martin_mere) is a wetland reserve for tens of thousands of wintering swans and geese. Trains run to Liverpool every 15 minutes.
