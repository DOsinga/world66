Between the pinewoods and the sea at Formby Point, the National Trust looks after one of the few places in England where red squirrels survive, with paths through the trees and over high sand dunes to a wide beach. At low tide, footprints of animals and people from up to 8,000 years ago are sometimes exposed in the mud. Formby is about 15 minutes by train from Southport; the reserve is a walk from Freshfield station.
