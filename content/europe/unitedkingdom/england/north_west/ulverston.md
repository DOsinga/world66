Ulverston is a lively market town on the Furness peninsula, between the southern Lakes and Morecambe Bay. Its landmark is the [Hoad Monument](/europe/unitedkingdom/england/north_west/ulverston/hoad_monument), a copy of the Eddystone lighthouse on the hill above the town, built for the explorer Sir John Barrow, who was born here. Stan Laurel was born here too, in 1890, and the town has a statue of Laurel and Hardy outside Coronation Hall and a small [museum](/europe/unitedkingdom/england/north_west/ulverston/laurel_and_hardy_museum) devoted to them.

The cobbled centre has a Thursday and Saturday market and plenty of independent shops. Ulverston is also the start of the Cumbria Way, a 70-mile walk through the heart of the Lakes to Carlisle, and close to [Conishead Priory](/europe/unitedkingdom/england/north_west/ulverston/conishead_priory), now a Buddhist centre with a temple open to visitors.
