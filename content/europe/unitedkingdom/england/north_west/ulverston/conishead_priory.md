A Gothic Revival mansion on the site of a medieval priory, two miles south of Ulverston on the shore of Morecambe Bay. Since 1976 it has been a Buddhist centre, and the Kadampa temple in its grounds is open to visitors, as are the woodland walks down to the shore. There is a café.
