A 30-metre monument built in 1850 in honour of Sir John Barrow, the Admiralty secretary and promoter of Arctic exploration, who was born in Ulverston. It is modelled on the Eddystone lighthouse, though it has never had a light. A steep path climbs Hoad Hill from the town in about 20 minutes, and the views take in Morecambe Bay and the Lakeland fells. The tower is opened on some weekends in summer.
