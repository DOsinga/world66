A standard-gauge heritage line, preserved since 1973, running steam trains with 1950s carriages from Haverthwaite through the Leven valley to Lakeside at the south end of Windermere, where they connect with Windermere Lake Cruises. Haverthwaite station has a tea room and a woodland playground.
