Warrington Transporter Bridge is one of the town's strongest industrial landmarks, a steel structure built to carry loads across the Mersey without blocking river traffic. Its rarity is the reason to care: transporter bridges were never common, and few survive.

Access is limited, so treat it mainly as an exterior industrial heritage sight. It is worth seeking out if you like engineering, old working rivers, or places where Warrington's manufacturing history is still visible in the landscape.
