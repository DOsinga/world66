The Museum of Wigan Life tells the town's story from inside its former public library, a handsome Alfred Waterhouse building on Library Street. Its collections lean into the things that shaped Wigan: coal, mills, sport, music, family history, and the practical lives of a northern industrial town.

It is a modest museum, but a useful one if you want context before walking the canal and Wigan Pier Quarter. George Orwell researched parts of *The Road to Wigan Pier* in this building, which gives the place a literary charge beyond its display cases.
