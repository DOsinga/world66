The former Treetop Trek course in the woods at Brockhole, the national park visitor centre between Windermere and Ambleside, now run by Zip World. It has a full and a mini treetop trek ending on zip wires, Treetop Nets for children and a Treetop Drop. Bookings go through Zip World's central office in Wales.
