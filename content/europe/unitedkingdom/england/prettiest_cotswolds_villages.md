Every Cotswold village claims to be the prettiest, and enough of them make a plausible case that the argument never really settles. This list rounds up five that keep coming up: a row of weavers' cottages William Morris supposedly called the most beautiful in England, a valley village so untouched by modern life that film crews keep showing up, a river running straight through the middle of the high street, and a folly-topped hill above a village nicknamed the "Jewel of the Cotswolds."

Come early or late in the day if you can — all five get busy with coach parties by midday in summer, and the honey-stone charm is easier to appreciate without a queue for the view.
