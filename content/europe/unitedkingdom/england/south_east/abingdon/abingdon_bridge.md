The bridge actually consists of two crossings linked by a causeway across **Nag's Head Island**, with **Burford Bridge** spanning the main Thames channel and **Maud Hales Bridge** crossing the smaller back stream. It is best appreciated from the meadow on the south bank, where you can take in the curve of the river, the spire of St Helen's, and the line of pubs along the wharf.

The riverside path leads east towards **Abingdon Lock** — about a mile's walk along the Thames Path — and west into the meadows of the Ock Valley. In summer, swans, narrowboats, and rowers from Abingdon's two clubs make the bridge one of the most active stretches of river above Oxford.
