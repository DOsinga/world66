---
title: Getting There
type: section
---

Abingdon has no railway station — the line that once served it was closed in 1963 — so most arrivals are by bus or car from **Oxford**, six miles to the north.

The frequent **Stagecoach X3 and X32** buses connect Abingdon to Oxford city centre (about 25 minutes) and continue south to Wantage and Didcot. **Didcot Parkway** railway station, on the main London Paddington line, is six miles south and reached by the same bus routes; it is the obvious arrival point if coming from London.

By car, Abingdon is just off the A34 between Oxford and Newbury. There are several town-centre car parks; the largest is at Charter Quay, a short walk from the market square. The town is also a regular stop for narrowboat hire on the upper Thames.
