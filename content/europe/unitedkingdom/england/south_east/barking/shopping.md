---
title: Shopping
type: section
---

Barking's commercial life centres on the pedestrianised East Street and Ripple Road, anchored at one end by the historic outdoor market and at the other by the Vicarage Field shopping centre. It is local-shopping territory rather than destination retail — bargains, food, mobile-phone shops, and a strong range of African, Caribbean, and Eastern European groceries reflecting the borough's mix.
