title: Things to Do
type: section
---

Barking is no tourist circuit, but it rewards a half-day wander. The headline is the ruined Barking Abbey and the adjacent St Margaret's Church — a thousand-year stretch of English history sitting beside a District-line station. Add the Tudor-era Eastbury Manor House a mile east, a couple of parks, and the East End Women's Museum tucked into the Old Town Hall, and you have an honest afternoon's poking around.
