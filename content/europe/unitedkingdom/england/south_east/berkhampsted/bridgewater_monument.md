The Bridgewater Monument is a neoclassical stone tower built in 1832 for the third Duke of Bridgewater, the canal pioneer who once lived at nearby Ashridge House. It stands in the woods of the Ashridge Estate, so it works well as a landmark on a longer walk.

Climb the 172 steps for wide views over the Chiltern countryside. At ground level there is usually a cafe and National Trust facilities, making it an easy starting point for walks through the estate.
