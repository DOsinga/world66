Blake's Cottage in Felpham, a short walk east of Bognor town centre, is where the poet and artist William Blake lived from 1800 to 1803 — the only time in his life he left London for an extended period. The thatched cottage is a modest, picturesque building on a residential street, privately owned and not open to the public, but visible from outside and worth visiting for those with an interest in English Romantic literature.

The surrounding village of Felpham retains some of its character as the rural coastal settlement Blake would have known. The beach here is quieter than central Bognor, and the walk between the two along the seafront promenade takes around 20 minutes. A trust dedicated to preserving the cottage has periodically opened it for special events.
