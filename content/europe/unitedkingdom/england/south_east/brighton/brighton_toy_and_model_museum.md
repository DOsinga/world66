Brighton Toy and Model Museum fills a set of Victorian railway arches with model trains, toy soldiers, dolls, construction sets, puppets, and miniature worlds. The setting near the station makes it easy to visit even on a short Brighton trip.

The museum is strongest for families, railway-model fans, and anyone susceptible to nostalgia. It offers a playful indoor contrast to the pier, beach, and Royal Pavilion.
