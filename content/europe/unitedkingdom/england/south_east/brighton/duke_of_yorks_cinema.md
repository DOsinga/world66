Duke of York's Cinema has been showing films since 1910, making it one of Brighton's most loved old institutions. Its striped legs on the roof are a local landmark, and the single-screen interior still feels like a proper neighbourhood cinema rather than a multiplex.

The programme mixes art-house films, classics, and mainstream releases. The upstairs bar and balcony make it an easy evening stop if you want a quieter Brighton night away from the seafront pubs.
