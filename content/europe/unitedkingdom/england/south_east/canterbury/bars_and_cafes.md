---
title: "Bars and Cafes"
type: section
---

Canterbury has two universities and a strong pub tradition, which is the explanation for almost everything that follows. **The Foundry Brew Pub** on White Horse Lane is the most reliable for craft beer — they brew on the premises and the kitchen is solid. **The Old Buttermarket** by the cathedral gates is the touristy choice but the building is genuinely seventeenth-century and the beer is fine.

For something quieter, **The Thomas Becket** on Best Lane is a proper Kentish pub with a fire in winter and a beer garden out the back. The medieval **Parrot** in St Radigund's pulls a strong pint. Student bars cluster around St Peter's Place near Westgate Hall — usually loud, usually cheap.

For coffee, **The Refectory Kitchen** in the cathedral precincts is the dependable choice; **Boho Cafe** on the High Street is the day-and-night student favourite for breakfasts, cake, and people-watching.
