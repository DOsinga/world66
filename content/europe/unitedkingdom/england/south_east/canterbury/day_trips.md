Canterbury sits at the centre of east Kent, which is small enough that you can do almost any of it as a day trip. **Whitstable** — fifteen minutes on the train — is the obvious first choice: a low-key coast town with oyster shacks, weatherboarded houses, and a working harbour. **Dover** is twenty-five minutes away and has the white cliffs, the castle, and one of the best preserved Roman lighthouses in Europe. **Ramsgate** and Broadstairs are forty minutes east and offer a slower, more Edwardian coast.

Inland, **Leeds Castle** is forty minutes by car and one of the most photogenic moated castles in England. **Sissinghurst Garden** is an hour west and worth the trip for the Vita Sackville-West restoration alone.

For a longer day, **Sandwich** — half an hour away — is an extraordinarily well-preserved medieval town that most tourists skip, with a working harbour and miles of golf-links beach beyond.
