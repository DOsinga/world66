---
title: "Getting There"
type: section
---

**By train.** Canterbury has two stations. **Canterbury West** is on the high-speed line from London St Pancras (about 55 minutes) and is the faster option from central London; trains also run from Ashford for connections to Eurostar. **Canterbury East** is on the slower line from London Victoria (about 90 minutes) and is closer to the cathedral.

**By car.** Canterbury is just off the M2 motorway, about 90 minutes from central London on a good day. The medieval centre is pedestrianised, so leave the car at one of the **Park & Ride** sites on the edge of town — there are three, with cheap parking and a regular shuttle bus that drops you near the bus station.

**By coach.** National Express runs direct coaches from London Victoria to Canterbury bus station (around two hours), often the cheapest option if you book ahead.

**From Europe.** The Channel Tunnel terminal at Folkestone is 20 minutes by car; Dover for the ferries is about 25 minutes. Canterbury is a natural first or last stop on a southern England loop.
