Chichester Canal runs from Chichester Basin toward Chichester Harbour, with a towpath of about 4.5 miles. It is good for an easy walk or cycle, with water, birds, and flat countryside along the way.

Rowboats can be hired at the basin, and boat trips operate on navigable sections of the canal.
