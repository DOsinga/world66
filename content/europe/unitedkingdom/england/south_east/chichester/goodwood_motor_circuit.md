Goodwood Motor Circuit is the motoring side of the Goodwood Estate, a historic track with a far stronger atmosphere than a modern stadium circuit. Its calendar is built around heritage, with classic cars, track days, and events that turn machinery into theatre.

You do not need to be a racing obsessive to enjoy it when something is on, though you do need to check dates. On quiet days it is more of a specialist stop; during Revival-style events, it becomes one of the most distinctive reasons to base yourself around Chichester.
