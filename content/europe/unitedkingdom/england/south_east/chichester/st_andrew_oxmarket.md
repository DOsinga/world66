This medieval church, deconsecrated and converted to an arts centre and exhibition space, sits down a quiet alley off East Street. The simple interior, with its whitewashed walls and timber roof, makes a fine setting for the rolling programme of local and contemporary art exhibitions. Always worth a quick look if you are passing.
