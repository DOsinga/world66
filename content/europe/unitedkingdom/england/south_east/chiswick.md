Chiswick is a leafy west [London](/europe/unitedkingdom/england/london) neighbourhood with a village feel that belies its proximity to central London — Turnham Green tube station is about 20 minutes from the West End on the District line.

Chiswick High Road and its continuation into Turnham Green Terrace form a lively strip of independent shops, delis, and restaurants. The area has become one of London's better dining neighbourhoods — [La Trompette](/europe/unitedkingdom/england/south_east/chiswick/la_trompette) is a destination restaurant by London standards. A five-minute walk south brings you to the [Thames towpath and Chiswick Mall](/europe/unitedkingdom/england/south_east/chiswick/chiswick_mall), a handsome stretch of riverside houses with a string of pubs — one of the pleasantest waterside walks in London.

[Chiswick House](/europe/unitedkingdom/england/south_east/chiswick/chiswick_house), a Palladian villa built in the 1720s by Lord Burlington, sits in handsome grounds that are free to enter and worth a wander. [Fuller's Griffin Brewery](/europe/unitedkingdom/england/south_east/chiswick/fullers_brewery), the last traditional brewery in London, is also here — tours run regularly. [Hogarth's House](/europe/unitedkingdom/england/south_east/chiswick/hogarths_house), the satirist's former country retreat, is free to enter and close by.
