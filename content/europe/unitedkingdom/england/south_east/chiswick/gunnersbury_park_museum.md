Gunnersbury Park Museum is a local history museum for Ealing and Hounslow, housed in a former Rothschild family residence. The displays cover west London life, local industry, domestic interiors, and the communities around the park.

It pairs naturally with a walk through Gunnersbury Park or with nearby Chiswick sights, especially for visitors who like smaller museums with a strong local focus.
