The London Museum of Water and Steam occupies the former Kew Bridge Pumping Station, a handsome industrial building that tells the story of how London got clean water. Its great strength is the collection of steam pumping engines, some of them enormous, which are run on selected days.

It is one of west London's best specialist museums: practical, noisy when the engines are working, and much more engaging than the subject sounds. Check the operating schedule before you go if you want to see a particular engine in steam.
