The London Transport Museum Depot holds the large objects that cannot fit in the main Covent Garden museum: restored railway carriages, buses, trams, trolleybuses, signs, models, signalling equipment, and other parts of the capital's transport history.

It is not open daily, so check for public open days before going. When it is open, preserved buses sometimes run special rides around the area.
