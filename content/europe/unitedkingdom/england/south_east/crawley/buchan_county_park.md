Buchan County Park is a calm, local-feeling counterweight to Crawley’s airport-town busyness. Its mix of woodland, heath, meadow, and ponds makes it useful for a gentle walk, especially if you want fresh air without committing to a long countryside excursion.

The park is not a headline attraction like nearby Nymans, but it clears the bar for travellers based in Crawley who want something green and uncomplicated. Paths are well suited to slow wandering, and the wildlife interest is strongest around the water and heathland edges.
