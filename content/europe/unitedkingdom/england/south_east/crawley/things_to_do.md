title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Crawley is not a destination in the conventional sense, but it has more to offer than its airport-adjacent reputation suggests. The [Old Town](/europe/unitedkingdom/england/south_east/crawley/old_town) predates the new town by centuries — a genuine medieval High Street with timber-framed buildings and old pubs that survived the 1950s development intact. [Worth Church](/europe/unitedkingdom/england/south_east/crawley/worth_church) is one of the finest surviving Saxon churches in England, dating to around 950 AD. [Tilgate Park](/europe/unitedkingdom/england/south_east/crawley/tilgate_park) offers good walking, a lake, and a nature centre. For a half-day excursion, [Nymans Garden](/europe/unitedkingdom/england/south_east/crawley/nymans_garden) near Handcross is one of the National Trust's best gardens in the south-east.
