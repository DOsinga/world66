---
title: Bars and Cafes
type: section
---

Matthew's Yard, a community arts space near Surrey Street, is the best spot for a coffee or an evening drink — it combines a cafe, a bar, and an event space and has a genuinely neighbourhood feel rather than a chain atmosphere. Boxpark also has bars that spill onto the outdoor terraces when the weather allows. The arts complex around Fairfield Halls has a bar that fills before and after performances.
