Boxpark Croydon stacks repurposed shipping containers five storeys high next to East Croydon station, housing dozens of independent street food traders, bars, and a large outdoor terrace with a screen for live events. The format is deliberately casual — order at the counter, find a seat inside one of the containers or outside in the yard, and work your way through jerk chicken, Korean fried chicken, Ethiopian injera, and wood-fired pizza without committing to a full restaurant meal. Evenings often bring live music or DJ sets. It is the most visited destination in the borough.
