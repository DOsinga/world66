The Clock Tower Museum tells Croydon's story through local history displays, changing exhibitions, and the Riesco Gallery collection. It is part of the Croydon Clocktower cultural complex beside the town hall, so it is an easy stop while exploring the town centre.

The museum is especially useful for understanding Croydon's growth from market town to south London centre, with displays that include local figures such as composer Samuel Coleridge-Taylor.
