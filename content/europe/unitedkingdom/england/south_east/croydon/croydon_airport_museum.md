The original 1928 terminal building at the former Croydon Aerodrome survives as the Croydon Airport Visitor Centre, a small but evocative museum of early aviation. The art deco terminal is largely intact, with original departure boards, control room equipment, and exhibits on the airline pioneers who made the airport Britain's gateway to the world. The site operated from 1915 until 1959, when Heathrow rendered it obsolete; the surrounding area is now housing, but the terminal and its grass airfield buffer zone are protected.
