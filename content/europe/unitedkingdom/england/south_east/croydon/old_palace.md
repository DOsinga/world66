The Old Palace is one of the most underrated medieval buildings in Greater London — a former summer residence of the Archbishops of Canterbury that dates back to the 14th century, with a Great Hall, a Norman undercroft, and a guard room that served royalty for over 500 years. It is now part of Old Palace of John Whitgift School and open for guided tours on selected days. Seeing it requires some advance planning, but the contrast between its mellow Tudor courtyards and the surrounding suburban streets is extraordinary.
