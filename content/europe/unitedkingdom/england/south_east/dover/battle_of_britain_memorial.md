The Battle of Britain Memorial at Capel-le-Ferne occupies a clifftop site between Dover and Folkestone, looking out over the Channel that defined the 1940 air battle. The central seated airman figure is restrained and moving, surrounded by plaques, aircraft replicas, and open sky.

It is a strong stop for anyone interested in Second World War history, but the setting gives it wider force. Wind, sea, and distance to France make the memorial’s subject immediately understandable in a way an indoor display rarely can.
