title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Dover packs a surprising amount of history into a small area. [Dover Castle](/europe/unitedkingdom/england/south_east/dover/dovercastle) with its wartime tunnels, [The White Cliffs](/europe/unitedkingdom/england/south_east/dover/whitecliffs), and the Bronze Age boat in [Dover Museum](/europe/unitedkingdom/england/south_east/dover/dovermuseum) all sit within a few miles of each other. You can comfortably see the highlights in a day, though the clifftop walks to [South Foreland Lighthouse](/europe/unitedkingdom/england/south_east/dover/south_foreland_lighthouse) reward a longer stay. The [Roman Painted House](/europe/unitedkingdom/england/south_east/dover/roman_painted_house) and [Maison Dieu](/europe/unitedkingdom/england/south_east/dover/maison_dieu) are worth an hour each in the town centre. [Samphire Hoe](/europe/unitedkingdom/england/south_east/dover/samphire_hoe), the nature reserve created from Channel Tunnel chalk, makes an unusual half-hour detour west of town.
