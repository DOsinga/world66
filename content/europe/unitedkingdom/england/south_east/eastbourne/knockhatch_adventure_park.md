Knockhatch Adventure Park is a family day out in the Sussex countryside north of Eastbourne. The mix is broad: adventure playgrounds, animals, birds of prey, go-karts, soft play, and seasonal outdoor rides, so it works best when you have children who need a full, active day rather than a single sight.
