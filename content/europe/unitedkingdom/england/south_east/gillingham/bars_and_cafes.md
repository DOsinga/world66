---
title: "Bars and Cafes in Gillingham"
type: section
---

Gillingham's pub scene is the typical Medway mix: a few decent traditional locals scattered through the residential streets, a handful of chain bars on the High Street, and the rougher end of the spectrum after dark on Friday and Saturday. For a quiet pint of well-kept cask ale, head to one of the back-street community pubs.
