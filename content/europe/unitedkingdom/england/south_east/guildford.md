Guildford is a handsome town on the River Wey in Surrey, about 30 minutes by train from London Waterloo. The steep cobbled [High Street](/europe/unitedkingdom/england/south_east/guildford/high_street), lined with shops and pubs and presided over by the gilded clock of the [Guildhall](/europe/unitedkingdom/england/south_east/guildford/guildhall), is one of the most attractive in the region.

The town's most unusual feature is [Guildford Cathedral](/europe/unitedkingdom/england/south_east/guildford/guildford_cathedral), built between 1936 and 1961. It is the only Anglican cathedral built on a new site in southern England since the Reformation, and its simple brick exterior is unexpectedly striking on its hilltop perch above the University of Surrey campus.

The ruins of [Guildford Castle](/europe/unitedkingdom/england/south_east/guildford/guildford_castle) in the town centre sit in pleasant gardens with views over the surrounding countryside. The castle keep dates from the twelfth century and you can climb to the top for a panorama of the town and the North Downs beyond. The [Guildford Museum](/europe/unitedkingdom/england/south_east/guildford/guildford_museum) next door covers the town's longer history; under one of the High Street shops are the foundations of what may be the [oldest synagogue in Britain](/europe/unitedkingdom/england/south_east/guildford/medieval_synagogue).

Guildford has an unexpectedly strong Lewis Carroll connection. The author of Alice in Wonderland spent his last years at his sisters' house [The Chestnuts](/europe/unitedkingdom/england/south_east/guildford/the_chestnuts) on Castle Hill, died in Guildford in 1898, and is buried in [The Mount Cemetery](/europe/unitedkingdom/england/south_east/guildford/mount_cemetery) on the hill west of town.

Guildford is well placed for walking on the North Downs Way and exploring the Surrey Hills, an Area of Outstanding Natural Beauty. The [River Wey Navigation](/europe/unitedkingdom/england/south_east/guildford/river_wey_navigation), one of the oldest man-made waterways in England, offers towpath walks south towards Godalming.
