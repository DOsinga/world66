Clandon Park is a National Trust estate east of Guildford, centred on a Palladian mansion and surrounding parkland. A major fire changed the house dramatically, but the site remains significant for its architecture, restoration story, gardens, and connection with Surrey's landed-estate landscape.

It is most rewarding as a deliberate heritage stop, particularly for travellers already visiting Guildford, Hatchlands, or the Surrey Hills. Check access details before going, as restoration work can affect what parts of the house and grounds are open.
