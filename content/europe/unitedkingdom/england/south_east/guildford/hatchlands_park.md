Hatchlands Park is a substantial National Trust estate east of Guildford, combining landscaped parkland, a Georgian house, and collections connected to music and domestic life. The grounds are the easiest draw, with long walks through open park and woodland.

It works well as a half-day excursion from Guildford, especially for travelers with a car or a deliberate interest in country houses. The estate gives the town's page a stronger day-trip option than another central street or church.
