Loseley Park is an Elizabethan house just outside Guildford, close enough for an easy excursion but rural enough to feel separate from town. The house has the expected Tudor and Stuart associations, while the walled garden gives the visit colour and softness in season.

It is best for travelers who enjoy historic houses at a human scale. Loseley is grand, but not overwhelming, and the surrounding fields keep the approach pleasantly low-key. Check opening days before going, as house access is seasonal and can be limited by events.
