Newlands Corner is one of the classic Surrey Hills viewpoints, looking across chalk downland, woodland, and villages just outside Guildford. It is an easy place to get a lungful of countryside without planning a full expedition.

Footpaths lead into woodland and open slopes, with Silent Pool and longer North Downs walks nearby. It is popular on weekends, but the views and picnic ground still make it a strong short escape from town.
