Shalford Mill is a preserved watermill just south of Guildford, close enough to work as a gentle add-on rather than a full day trip. The building sits on the Tillingbourne, a small river whose mills once powered a string of local industries through the Surrey villages.

The visit is simple: old machinery, timber, water, and a sense of how rural production worked before the commuter belt swallowed the surrounding landscape. It pairs well with a walk from Guildford toward Shalford or with a wider day around the Wey and the North Downs.
