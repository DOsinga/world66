The Spike Heritage Centre preserves the casual ward of the old Guildford Union Workhouse, where homeless men and travelling labourers were given a bed in exchange for hard, degrading work. It is a small museum, but unusually direct: the cells, tasks, and stories make poverty policy feel physical rather than abstract.

This is a good stop if you have already seen Guildford's castle, river, and high street and want a less polished piece of local history. Tours are the best way to visit, since the building needs explanation and the human stories are what give the place its force.
