Watts Gallery and Watts Chapel are among the most rewarding cultural stops near Guildford. The gallery is devoted to Victorian artist G. F. Watts, while the nearby chapel designed by Mary Watts is a remarkable Arts-and-Crafts building covered in symbolic terracotta decoration.

The pair works because the art, architecture, village setting, and cemetery landscape all speak to each other. It is a thoughtful half-day for anyone interested in Victorian art, handmade design, or places where memorial architecture becomes deeply personal.
