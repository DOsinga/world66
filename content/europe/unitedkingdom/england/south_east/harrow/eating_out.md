---
title: "Eating Out"
type: section
---

The flat part of Harrow — around Station Road and Kenton Road — is one of London's best concentrations of South Asian food. The borough has a large Gujarati and Punjabi community, and the restaurants here serve food for a local population rather than a tourist trade. Vegetarian options are unusually good and unusually authentic. Up on the hill, the choice is smaller but there are decent pubs.
