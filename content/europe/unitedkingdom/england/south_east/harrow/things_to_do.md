---
title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Almost everything worth seeing in Harrow sits within a short walk on the hill. Harrow School dominates the ridge, its Victorian and Georgian buildings spread through the village like a small university campus. St Mary's Church at the summit is one of the finest views in outer London. For those with children or an interest in medieval history, Headstone Manor and its moat offer a surprisingly intact piece of the pre-suburban landscape. Down in the flat part of the borough, Harrow Skate Park is a genuine piece of 1970s concrete heritage that still draws riders from across London.
