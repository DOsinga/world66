The Hove Museum of Creativity is a Victorian villa housing the city's decorative and fine art collections, with a particular focus on local history, craft, and early cinema. The permanent collection is modest but well-curated, with strength in ceramics, glass, and local scenes from the 19th century. The early cinema section is the most distinctive — Hove had a significant cluster of film pioneers in the late 1890s and early 1900s, and the equipment and footage on display tell a story that is less well known than it deserves to be.

The museum is free to enter and small enough to cover in an hour. It sits in a quiet residential part of Hove, a twenty-minute walk from the seafront.
