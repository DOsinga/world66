title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Hove's main attractions are its seafront architecture, its beach, and its quieter character compared to Brighton next door. The [Brunswick Town conservation area](/europe/unitedkingdom/england/south_east/hove/brunswick_square) contains the finest Regency terraces in the city; the [Hove Museum](/europe/unitedkingdom/england/south_east/hove/hove_museum) offers a good local history collection; the [seafront promenade](/europe/unitedkingdom/england/south_east/hove/seafront_promenade) runs west from the Peace Statue toward the marina. The [Goldstone boulder](/europe/unitedkingdom/england/south_east/hove/goldstone) and [Everest's grave](/europe/unitedkingdom/england/south_east/hove/everests_grave) are genuine curiosities for those who like their history unexpected.
