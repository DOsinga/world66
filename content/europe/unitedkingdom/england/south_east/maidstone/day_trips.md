---
title: Day Trips
type: section
---

The countryside around Maidstone is the main reason to spend time here, and several excellent day trips are within easy reach.

**Leeds Castle**, five miles south-east, is the most obvious first excursion — one of the most photographed castles in England, sitting on two islands in a lake with extensive grounds to explore. A dedicated shuttle bus runs from Maidstone town centre.

**The Kent Downs** AONB stretches south and east from Maidstone, with good walking in the Weald of Kent through orchards, hop gardens, and quiet villages. The North Downs Way long-distance footpath passes north of the town and connects to the coast.

**Sissinghurst Castle Garden** (National Trust), about 15 miles south-east, is one of England's most celebrated gardens — designed by Vita Sackville-West in the 1930s around the ruins of a Tudor mansion. Worth the detour in any season but particularly in early summer when the Rose Garden peaks.

**Rochester**, about 12 miles north-west, has a Norman cathedral and castle, a strong Dickens connection (he lived nearby at Gad's Hill), and a well-preserved historic town centre. Easy by train or car.

**Hever Castle**, about 16 miles to the west, is the childhood home of Anne Boleyn — a moated medieval castle with good gardens and strong Tudor history exhibits.
