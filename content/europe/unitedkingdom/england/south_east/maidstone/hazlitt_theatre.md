The Hazlitt Theatre is Maidstone's main performing arts venue, a mid-sized theatre seating around 600 people in the town centre, named for the essayist William Hazlitt who was born in Maidstone in 1778. The programme mixes touring productions of West End shows, comedy, dance, and local amateur productions throughout the year.

The theatre is on the High Street in the town centre, making it easy to combine with a meal beforehand. It is a working regional theatre rather than a destination venue, but it keeps a consistently busy calendar and is the main focus of the town's cultural life. The building has been refurbished several times since it opened in 1984 and the facilities are comfortable.
