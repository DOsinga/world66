The Museum of Kent Life is an open-air museum on the edge of Maidstone, built around historic buildings, farm animals, gardens, and rural crafts. It gives a practical sense of Kent’s village and agricultural past, which can be hard to read from the county’s busy modern roads.

It is particularly good for families, but adults who like vernacular buildings and local history will find plenty to notice. Allow time to wander rather than rushing between exhibits. The museum pairs well with a Maidstone visit that otherwise focuses on the town centre, river, or Leeds Castle.
