Stoneacre is a compact National Trust property near Maidstone, centred on a 15th-century yeoman's
house that was restored in the early 20th century with Arts and Crafts ideals in mind. Its scale is
domestic rather than grand, which is precisely the appeal: timber, garden, and Kentish rural detail
instead of a stately-home spectacle.

The house works best for visitors already exploring the Maidstone countryside, Leeds Castle area, or
Kent villages. It is a short visit, but the craftsmanship and garden setting make it a more
distinctive stop than another generic village walk.
