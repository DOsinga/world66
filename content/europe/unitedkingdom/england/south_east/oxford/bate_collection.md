The Bate Collection of Musical Instruments is an Oxford University museum with a large collection of historical instruments, especially orchestral woodwind, brass, and strings. Many pieces are displayed with enough context to show how instrument design changed over time.

It is a focused museum, and that is the appeal. It is best for visitors with an interest in music, craft, or the quieter specialist collections that make Oxford more than its colleges.
