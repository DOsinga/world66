Hertford Bridge is the covered pedestrian bridge linking two parts of Hertford College over New College Lane. Its arched shape has earned it the popular nickname Oxford's Bridge of Sighs, though the comparison is more romantic than architectural.

It is a quick but classic photo stop in the dense historic core, close to the Bodleian Library, Sheldonian Theatre, and Radcliffe Camera.
