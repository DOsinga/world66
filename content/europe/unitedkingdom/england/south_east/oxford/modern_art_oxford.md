Modern Art Oxford is one of the city's main contemporary art spaces, with changing exhibitions of modern art, photography, and installation work. The programme often includes talks and workshops, so it is worth checking what is on rather than treating it as a fixed collection.

The gallery is central, close to Christ Church and the Covered Market, and makes a useful contrast to Oxford's college and museum circuit. It is a good stop for visitors who want something current and compact between the older sights.
