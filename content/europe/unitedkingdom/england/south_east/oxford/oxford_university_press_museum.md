The Oxford University Press Museum tells the story of Oxford's long connection with printing and publishing, from early university printing to the global reach of the press today.

Displays cover printing equipment, OUP buildings, the publication history of *Alice's Adventures in Wonderland*, and the making of the Oxford English Dictionary. It is a small museum, but a worthwhile stop for visitors interested in books, language, and Oxford's working history.
