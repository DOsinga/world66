St Martin's Church in Bladon is best known as the burial place of Winston Churchill, whose grave lies in the churchyard with other members of the Churchill family. The present church is a Victorian rebuilding, but the site has a much older parish history and is a common side trip from nearby Blenheim Palace and Oxford.
