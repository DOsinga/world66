The Ultimate Picture Palace is Oxford's small independent cinema, set in a handsome Grade II listed building off Cowley Road. It is the place to look for art-house films, revivals, and a more local evening out than the multiplexes provide.

It is also a useful excuse to spend time east of the old university core, where Oxford feels more lived-in and less ceremonial.
