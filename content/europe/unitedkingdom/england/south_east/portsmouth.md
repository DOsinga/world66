Portsmouth is Britain's foremost naval city, built on Portsea Island on the Hampshire coast. Its sheltered harbour, protected by the Isle of Wight to the south and [Portsdown Hill](/europe/unitedkingdom/england/south_east/portsmouth/portsdown_hill) to the north, has made it the home of the Royal Navy for centuries.

The [Historic Dockyard](/europe/unitedkingdom/england/south_east/portsmouth/historic_dockyard) is the main draw. Here you can board [HMS Victory](/europe/unitedkingdom/england/south_east/portsmouth/hms_victory), Nelson's flagship at the Battle of Trafalgar, and stand on the spot where he fell. Nearby, the recovered wreck of the [Mary Rose](/europe/unitedkingdom/england/south_east/portsmouth/mary_rose), Henry VIII's Tudor warship that sank in Portsmouth Harbour in 1545, is displayed in its own museum. [HMS Warrior](/europe/unitedkingdom/england/south_east/portsmouth/hms_warrior), the world's first iron-hulled warship, completes the trio of historic ships.

Beyond the dockyard, [Gunwharf Quays](/europe/unitedkingdom/england/south_east/portsmouth/gunwharf_quays) is a waterfront development with outlet shopping, restaurants and the [Spinnaker Tower](/europe/unitedkingdom/england/south_east/portsmouth/spinnaker_tower), a striking 170-metre viewing platform with glass floors and panoramic views across the Solent. [Old Portsmouth](/europe/unitedkingdom/england/south_east/portsmouth/old_portsmouth), at the harbour mouth, has cobbled streets, fishermen's cottages and good pubs overlooking the water.

The seafront at [Southsea](/europe/unitedkingdom/england/south_east/portsmouth/southsea) stretches east from Old Portsmouth, with a long promenade, shingle beach and views across to the Isle of Wight. [The D-Day Story](/europe/unitedkingdom/england/south_east/portsmouth/d_day_story) here tells the story of the Normandy landings, for which Portsmouth was a major embarkation point. [Southsea Castle](/europe/unitedkingdom/england/south_east/portsmouth/southsea_castle), built by Henry VIII in 1544, sits on the seafront nearby. Inland to the east, [Portchester Castle](/europe/unitedkingdom/england/south_east/portsmouth/portchester_castle) preserves the most complete Roman fortress walls in northern Europe with a Norman keep inside.

Portsmouth has daily ferry crossings to France, Spain, Jersey and Guernsey, and the [hovercraft](/europe/unitedkingdom/england/south_east/portsmouth/hovercraft_terminal) to the Isle of Wight takes just ten minutes from Southsea.
