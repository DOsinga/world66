A consistently good modern British restaurant on White Hart Road, in the heart of Old Portsmouth, serving local fish and steaks in a relaxed converted-warehouse setting. The fish is reliably fresh — landed at the Camber Dock five minutes away — and there is usually a creative vegetarian option.

The terrace at the back looks across to the cathedral. Sensible prices for what you get; book ahead on weekend evenings.
