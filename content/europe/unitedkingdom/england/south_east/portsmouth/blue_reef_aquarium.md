Blue Reef Aquarium is a compact seafront aquarium in Southsea, useful on wet or windy days when the
beach is less inviting. Its tanks focus on marine life, reef species, and hands-on family-friendly
displays rather than the naval history that dominates much of Portsmouth.

It is not a reason to travel to Portsmouth by itself, but it earns its place for families already
walking Southsea Common, the pier, and the seafront. Pair it with Southsea Castle or the beach for
an easy half-day away from the Historic Dockyard crowds.
