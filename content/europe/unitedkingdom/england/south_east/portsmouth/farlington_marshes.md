Farlington Marshes is a coastal nature reserve of grazing marsh, lagoons, and pools on the edge of Portsmouth Harbour. It is especially good for waders and wildfowl, with easy walking and open views across the wetland.

Bring binoculars if you have them; this is more rewarding as a slow wildlife walk than as a quick photo stop.
