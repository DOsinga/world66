Funland is Hayling Island's seafront amusement park, with rollercoasters, dodgems, thrill rides, and gentler rides for younger children. It is not subtle, but it gives the island a proper old-fashioned holiday feel and works well if you are combining the beach with a family day out from Portsmouth.
