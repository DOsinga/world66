The city's general museum, housed in a Victorian former military barracks on Museum Road. Permanent displays cover the social history of Portsmouth — domestic life, the docks, the wartime blitz — with a strong section on the city's connection to Sherlock Holmes and Sir Arthur Conan Doyle (Doyle lived in Southsea and started writing the Holmes stories here).

There is also a rolling programme of temporary art exhibitions and a small collection of decorative art. Free entry. Allow an hour.
