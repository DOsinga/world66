---
title: "Getting There"
type: section
---

Ramsgate is 75 minutes from London by high-speed train, making it an easy day trip from the capital.

**By train:** Southeastern's high-speed service runs from London St Pancras International to Ramsgate, taking around 75–80 minutes with some services requiring a change at Faversham or Ashford. Standard trains from London Victoria via Chatham take around 100 minutes. Ramsgate station is about a 10-minute walk from the harbour.

**By car:** From London, take the M2 and then the A299 (Thanet Way) to Ramsgate, approximately 90 minutes without traffic. Parking is available in several car parks near the harbour and beach.

**Ferry:** Ramsgate formerly had a ferry service to Ostend and other Continental ports that made it a significant cross-Channel hub; this service no longer operates. The nearest current ferry crossings are from Dover (about 25 minutes by car) to Calais, or from Folkestone via the Channel Tunnel.
