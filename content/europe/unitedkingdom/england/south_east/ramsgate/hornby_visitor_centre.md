Hornby Visitor Centre is a showcase for Hornby model railways, Airfix models, and Corgi cars. It is part display hall and part shop, but the railway layouts and toy-history angle make it more museum-like than a normal outlet.

It is a good short stop for families or visitors with an interest in British model-making.
