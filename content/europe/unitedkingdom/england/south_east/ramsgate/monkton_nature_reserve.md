Monkton Nature Reserve is a former chalk quarry that has been reclaimed as a small wildlife reserve west of Ramsgate. Chalk grassland, ponds, paths, and fossil interest give it a different texture from the harbour and beach sights on the coast.

It is best for travellers with a car, cyclists, or families who want a gentle outdoor stop away from the seafront. Do not expect a large national reserve; the appeal is local nature, quiet paths, and the way Thanet's chalk landscape shows through.
