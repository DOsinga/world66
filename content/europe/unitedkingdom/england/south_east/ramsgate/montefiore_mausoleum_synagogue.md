The Montefiore Synagogue and Mausoleum form one of Ramsgate's most distinctive historic sites, tied to Sir Moses Montefiore, the 19th-century Jewish philanthropist, and his wife Judith. The synagogue was completed in the 1830s, while the adjacent mausoleum was modelled on Rachel's Tomb near Bethlehem and became the couple's final resting place.

It is a more specialised visit than the harbour or tunnels, but a rewarding one for anyone interested in Jewish history, Victorian philanthropy, or Ramsgate's unexpected international connections. Opening arrangements can be limited, so check ahead rather than assuming it functions like a regular walk-up attraction.
