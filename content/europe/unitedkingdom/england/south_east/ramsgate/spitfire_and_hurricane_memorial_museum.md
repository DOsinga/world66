The Spitfire and Hurricane Memorial Museum at Manston preserves two aircraft types that
define Britain's Battle of Britain memory. The museum's setting on a historic airfield
gives the displays extra weight, especially for visitors interested in wartime aviation.

It is a focused museum rather than a sprawling attraction, but the aircraft and
volunteer interpretation make it worthwhile. Combine it with other Thanet coast stops if
you are based in Ramsgate or Broadstairs.
