St George the Martyr is one of Ramsgate's most visible churches, its lantern tower rising above the town centre rather than hiding in a quiet churchyard. The building has the confident scale of a Georgian and early Victorian seaside town that expected to be noticed.

It is worth a look while walking between the harbour, the town centre, and the residential streets above the seafront. The church will not displace Ramsgate's Pugin sights, but it adds another layer to the town's architectural story.
