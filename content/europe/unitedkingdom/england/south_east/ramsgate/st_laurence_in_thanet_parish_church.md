St Laurence-in-Thanet Parish Church sits away from Ramsgate's harbourfront and points to an older layer of local history. Before the resort and port town expanded, St Lawrence was the parish centre, and the church still carries that quieter, village-like atmosphere.

It is worth folding into a walk if you like old churches, local history, or the less obvious edges of seaside towns. The building's stonework and setting give Ramsgate a deeper timeline than the marina and Regency streets alone.
