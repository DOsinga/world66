Beale Park is a riverside wildlife and gardens attraction west of Reading, mixing animal
areas, landscaped grounds, play spaces, and Thames-side walks. It is especially useful
for families who need a softer, slower outing than a city museum.

The park is outside central Reading, so plan transport and treat it as a half-day
excursion. Its appeal is the combination of open space and easy animal encounters rather
than a single headline exhibit.
