Mapledurham House and Watermill is one of the lovelier excursions from Reading, combining a historic house, old mill, church, and Thames-side village setting. The attraction is the whole ensemble rather than a single room or object.

Opening days can be limited, so check before travelling out. When it is open, it offers a slower, rural counterweight to Reading's urban centre and a good excuse to follow the river west.
