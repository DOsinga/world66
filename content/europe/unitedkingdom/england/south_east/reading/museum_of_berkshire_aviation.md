The Museum of Berkshire Aviation is a small specialist museum near Reading, focused on the county's aviation and aircraft-manufacturing history. Its displays are strongest for visitors who like local engineering stories, prototypes, and hands-on aircraft detail.

It is not a general Reading must-see, but it is a solid niche stop and a good rainy-day option for aviation enthusiasts. Check opening times carefully, as small volunteer-run museums often have limited hours.
