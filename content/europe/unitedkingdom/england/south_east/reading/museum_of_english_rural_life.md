The Museum of English Rural Life explores the history of farming, food, craft, and countryside life in England. Its galleries use tools, vehicles, photographs, and everyday objects to show how rural landscapes were shaped by both nature and work.

There is also a garden and cafe, making it an easy half-day stop for families or anyone interested in social history outside the usual castles-and-cathedral circuit.
