Salters Steamers runs passenger boat trips on the Thames, giving Reading visitors a simple way to shift from shopping streets and railway bustle to the slower rhythm of the river. The value is the journey more than the operator: tree-lined banks, locks, bridges, and the familiar English pleasure of watching towns from the water.

Routes and timetables are seasonal, so check before building a day around it. When boats are running, this is one of the easiest soft adventures from Reading, especially for families or anyone wanting a break from walking.
