The National Archives at Kew holds the official records of the UK government and courts. Its main use is for researchers, but visitors can see changing exhibitions and a small public museum area without applying for a reader's card.

It is a deliberate stop for people tracing family, legal, military, or imperial history, and it pairs naturally with nearby Kew and Richmond sights.
