Whitehorse Hill combines an Iron Age hill fort, the Uffington White Horse chalk figure, and wide views across the Berkshire Downs and Vale of White Horse. The Ridgeway long-distance path passes close by, so it works both as a focused historic stop and as one of the most memorable short walks along this part of the chalk ridge.
