Diggerland is a family amusement park built around construction machinery. The novelty is the point: visitors can ride in modified digger buckets, operate small excavators, and try machinery-themed rides that are hard to find elsewhere.

It is just outside Rochester and works best as a deliberate family stop rather than a casual town-centre sight.
