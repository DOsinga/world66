Bishopstone Station sits west of Seaford and is mainly useful as a starting point for walks toward Tide Mills and Newhaven. Its unusual 1930s station building gives it more character than a simple halt.

Use it if you want to vary a coastal walk or avoid retracing every step from Seaford. The station is quiet, so check train times rather than assuming turn-up-and-go service.
