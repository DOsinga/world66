Seaford sits between several good Sussex day trips. [Eastbourne](/europe/unitedkingdom/england/south_east/eastbourne) gives the grander resort version of the coast, [Brighton](/europe/unitedkingdom/england/south_east/brighton) supplies urban energy, and Lewes is the inland historic town to aim for by train.
