Seaford's Old Town Hall is a modest town-centre landmark rather than a major attraction. It is worth noticing while moving between the station, shops, and seafront because it anchors the older civic core behind the beach.

Do not make a special journey for it. Seaford's real draw is the coast, but small buildings like this keep the town from being only a walking trailhead.
