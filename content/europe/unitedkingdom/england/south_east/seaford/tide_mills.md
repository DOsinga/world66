Tide Mills is the ruined remains of a village and industrial site west of Seaford, on the flat coastal ground toward Newhaven. It is not picturesque in the usual Sussex sense; it is bleak, open, and interesting.

Walk here along the beach or from Bishopstone if you like abandoned places and coastal industrial history. There is little interpretation on site, so read up first or let the atmosphere do most of the work.
