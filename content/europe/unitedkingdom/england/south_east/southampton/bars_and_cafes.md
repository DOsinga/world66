---
title: "Bars and Cafes"
type: section
---

Southampton's drinking scene is in two main clusters. The **Old Town pubs** around Bugle Street and French Street are mostly traditional — exposed beams, well-kept ale, a bit older crowd. The **Bedford Place** strip north of the centre is busier, younger, and has the bulk of the cocktail and craft beer venues. **Oxford Street** between them runs to wine bars and brasseries.

The most distinctive single place to drink in the city is the [Wool House](/europe/unitedkingdom/england/south_east/southampton/wool_house) — Dancing Man Brewery in a 13th-century stone warehouse on Town Quay. The medieval vaulted interior and the brewery's own beers make it worth a special visit. **The Duke of Wellington** on Bugle Street is the classic Old Town pub, in a Tudor building from 1490. **The Platform** on Town Quay does decent pub food with a view of the cruise terminals.

For coffee, **Mettricks** on Guildhall Square is the city's reliable independent. **Caracoli** in Westquay has good pastries. The food halls in **Westquay Watermark** and the older **East Street Centre** both have rotating coffee operators.
