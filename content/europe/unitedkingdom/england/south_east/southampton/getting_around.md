---
title: "Getting Around"
type: section
---

The compact city centre — Old Town, Cultural Quarter, West Quay, and the seafront — is walkable in about 25 minutes end to end. Most visitors will not need any transport beyond their feet for the main attractions.

For longer trips, **buses** run frequently on the main corridors. Bluestar and Unilink are the two main operators. A day pass is around £5 and covers most of the local network. Tickets are bought from the driver or via the app.

**Taxis and ride-share** (Uber operates) are easy enough in the centre. From the railway station to most central destinations is £5–8.

For the **New Forest** to the west and the **Hythe ferry**, the Town Quay ferry terminal is a five-minute walk from the medieval walls. The ferry to **Hythe** runs every 30 minutes and connects with the [Hythe Pier Railway](/europe/unitedkingdom/england/south_east/southampton/hythe_pier_railway) for a quick out-and-back trip across Southampton Water.
