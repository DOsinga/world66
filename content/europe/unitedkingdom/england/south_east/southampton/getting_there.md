---
title: "Getting There"
type: section
---

**By train**, Southampton Central is served by frequent direct services from **London Waterloo** (around 75 minutes), **Bournemouth** (40 minutes), **Portsmouth** (50 minutes), and **Reading** (around 90 minutes). The CrossCountry network connects northwards to Birmingham, Manchester, and the north-east.

**By plane**, Southampton Airport (SOU) at Eastleigh is a small regional airport with flights to a few European and UK destinations. The train station is at the door — five minutes to Southampton Central. Most international visitors use **London Heathrow** (75 minutes by direct National Express coach) or **Gatwick** (about two hours).

**By car**, Southampton sits at the south end of the M3 from London and at the southern terminus of the M27 east-west route. Parking in the centre is mostly in multi-storey car parks; the city centre is partly pedestrianised.

**Cruise passengers** disembark at one of the Mayflower, Queen Elizabeth II, Ocean, Horizon, or City cruise terminals. Most have a shuttle bus into the centre or to the train station.
