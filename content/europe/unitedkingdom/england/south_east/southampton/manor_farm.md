Manor Farm is a working heritage farm in River Hamble Country Park, east of Southampton. It gives visitors a hands-on rural counterpoint to the city's maritime museums, medieval walls, and cruise-port bustle.

The farm is strongest for families, with animals, historic buildings, and seasonal activities that make it more than a quick look-around. It also pairs well with a walk in the surrounding country park if you want a half-day away from central Southampton.
