title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Southampton rewards the curious visitor who looks beyond the shopping centre. The medieval Old Town is a genuine gem — walkable, free, and largely overlooked by travellers passing through on their way to a cruise. A circuit of the [Medieval Walls](/europe/unitedkingdom/england/south_east/southampton/medieval_walls) takes under an hour and gives you a real sense of the city's history. The two surviving medieval gatehouses, the [Bargate](/europe/unitedkingdom/england/south_east/southampton/bargate) and [God's House Tower](/europe/unitedkingdom/england/south_east/southampton/gods_house_tower), bookend it. The [Tudor House](/europe/unitedkingdom/england/south_east/southampton/tudor_house) on Bugle Street and the haunting ruins of [Holyrood Church](/europe/unitedkingdom/england/south_east/southampton/holyrood_church) are the other Old Town essentials.

For the Titanic and Mayflower history that gives the city its emotional pull, the [SeaCity Museum](/europe/unitedkingdom/england/south_east/southampton/seacity_museum) is the central exhibit; the [Titanic Engineers' Memorial](/europe/unitedkingdom/england/south_east/southampton/titanic_engineers_memorial), the [Titanic Musicians' Memorial](/europe/unitedkingdom/england/south_east/southampton/titanic_musicians_memorial), the [Pilgrim Fathers Memorial](/europe/unitedkingdom/england/south_east/southampton/pilgrim_fathers_memorial), and [Canute Chambers](/europe/unitedkingdom/england/south_east/southampton/canute_chambers) all bring out further details of the city's maritime memory. The [D-Day Wall](/europe/unitedkingdom/england/south_east/southampton/d_day_wall) along the old quay is another quietly powerful spot.

For art, the [Southampton City Art Gallery](/europe/unitedkingdom/england/south_east/southampton/southampton_city_art_gallery) has one of England's best provincial collections. For a quirky excursion, take the ferry to ride the [Hythe Pier Railway](/europe/unitedkingdom/england/south_east/southampton/hythe_pier_railway), the world's oldest. The Lutyens-designed [Southampton Cenotaph](/europe/unitedkingdom/england/south_east/southampton/southampton_cenotaph) is the prototype of his more famous Whitehall version.
