A small granite memorial fountain on the corner of Andrews Park, commemorating the eight musicians who played throughout the Titanic's sinking. The original 1913 monument was destroyed in the Blitz; the current replacement was unveiled in 1990 on the same spot. The list of names — Wallace Hartley, Theodore Brailey, Roger Bricoux, John Frederick Preston Clarke, John Law Hume, Georges Krins, Percy Cornelius Taylor, and John Wesley Woodward — runs around the base.

The memorial is small and easy to walk past. Combined with the Engineers' Memorial just metres away, it makes a quiet pendant to the SeaCity exhibits. Free, always open.
