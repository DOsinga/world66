---
title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Warsash is a place to come for a half-day on foot: a walk along the Hamble to the shore, the foot ferry across to Hamble-le-Rice, a coastal path north past the salt marshes, and a quick stop at one of the village pubs before catching the bus back to Southampton. The yachting and merchant navy training keep the village busier than most south-coast villages of its size.
