Hole Park Gardens is a private estate garden near Tenterden, with formal borders, lawns, woodland paths, and seasonal colour. The house itself is not open to the public, so the reason to come is the garden and parkland.

Spring is the best-known season, especially when the bluebells and flowering shrubs are out, but the garden has enough structure for a pleasant walk through much of the open season. Check opening days before setting out, as they can be limited.
