Beaulieu Palace House began as the 13th-century gatehouse of Beaulieu Abbey and later became the Montagu family home. Parts of the house and gardens are open to visitors, with rooms kept in Victorian style and costumed interpretation that gives the visit more life than a simple country-house walk-through.

It is best combined with the National Motor Museum and the abbey ruins on the same Beaulieu estate.
