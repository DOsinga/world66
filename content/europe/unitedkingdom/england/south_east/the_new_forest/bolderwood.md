Bolderwood is one of the New Forest's best-known woodland areas, with walking trails and a public deer-viewing platform near the car park. It is a simple but worthwhile stop for seeing the forest landscape without committing to a long hike.

The deer are not guaranteed, but the easy trails, picnic space, and old woodland make it a good family-friendly break while exploring the national park.
