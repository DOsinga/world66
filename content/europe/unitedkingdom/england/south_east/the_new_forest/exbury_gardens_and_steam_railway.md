Exbury Gardens is a 200-acre woodland garden on the edge of the New Forest, best known for rhododendrons, azaleas, camellias, and spring colour that can be spectacular in May and early June. The narrow-gauge steam railway makes it more than a simple garden walk, winding through parts of the estate that are otherwise harder to reach.

It is one of the stronger planned days out in the area, especially if you want a gentler contrast to forest walks and pony country. Allow time to wander rather than rushing straight to the railway.
