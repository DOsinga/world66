New Forest Wildlife Park is a family-friendly animal park on the edge of the forest. Otters are the headline, but there are also owls, wolves, deer, butterflies, and a changing cast of smaller residents.

It is a good bad-weather or child-friendly add-on to a New Forest visit, especially if the open forest ponies have only made everyone want to see more wildlife.
