Groombridge Place is a moated country house and garden near Tunbridge Wells, set in the kind of Kentish landscape that makes an afternoon disappear quickly. The formal gardens are the main draw, with clipped yews, water, old brick, and views across the estate.

It works best as a gentle half-day trip, especially if you want a quieter alternative to the better-known houses and castles scattered around the High Weald.
