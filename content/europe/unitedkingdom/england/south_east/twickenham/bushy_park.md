Bushy Park is the wilder royal park beside Hampton Court Palace, with wide grassland, old trees, deer herds, and long formal avenues. Chestnut Avenue, laid out by Christopher Wren, runs toward the palace, while the restored water gardens give the park a quieter historical corner. It is large enough to feel rural even on the edge of London.
