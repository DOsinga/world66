The slender brick clock tower in the centre of the village began life as a Victorian water tower, picking up its clock and memorial role only after the First World War. The repurposed structure is one of the village's most distinctive landmarks and stands at a triangular junction near the parade of shops on Brook Lane.

A pause-and-look monument rather than a destination. Visible from anywhere in the village centre.
