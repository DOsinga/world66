---

A small motor launch shuttles foot passengers between Warsash and Hamble-le-Rice across the mouth of the Hamble — a journey of about 200 metres that would otherwise require a 12-mile road trip via Bursledon. The pink-painted boats run continuously through daylight hours during the season, on demand in winter.

The crossing takes three minutes. The ferry is a working piece of village infrastructure — schoolchildren, dog walkers, sailors heading to their moorings — rather than a tourist attraction, but it is a small pleasure to be a part of.
