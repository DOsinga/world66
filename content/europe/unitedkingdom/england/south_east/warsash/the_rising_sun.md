The pub of choice on the Warsash shore, with a wide outdoor terrace looking directly across the river to Hamble. Standard British pub menu — Sunday roasts, fish and chips, sharing boards — done competently. Sit on the terrace at high tide on a summer evening and you have one of the better drinking views on the Hampshire coast.

Get there early on a sunny Saturday or expect a wait for an outdoor table. Reservations taken for the restaurant.
