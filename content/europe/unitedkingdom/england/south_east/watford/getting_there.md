---
title: "Getting There"
type: section
---

Watford is one of the easier London commuter towns to reach from central London. The fast trains from **Euston** to **Watford Junction** take 16 to 20 minutes and run every few minutes; the slower London Overground from Euston via Wembley adds about 15 minutes. **Watford Tube** (on the Metropolitan line out of Baker Street) takes longer but lands you closer to Cassiobury Park.

By road the M1 (junction 5) and M25 (junction 19/20) both serve Watford directly — handy for the Warner Bros. Studio Tour, which has a large car park. From Heathrow, the M25 takes about 30 minutes outside rush hour.

For the Studio Tour specifically there are dedicated coach transfers from London Victoria, and a shuttle bus from Watford Junction (£3 return) that meets every tour slot.
