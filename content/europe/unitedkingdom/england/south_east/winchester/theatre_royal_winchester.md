Theatre Royal Winchester is the city's main live-performance venue, a compact historic theatre a short walk from the centre. It gives Winchester something to do after cathedral hours, with a programme that can include drama, comedy, music, talks, and family shows.

For travelers, the building is worth noting even if you do not book a ticket, but it earns its keep best as an evening plan. Check the schedule before arriving in town; a good performance here can round out a day otherwise dominated by medieval and military history.
