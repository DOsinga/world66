Winchester Science Centre and Planetarium is a family-oriented science attraction with interactive exhibits and a full-dome planetarium. It is designed for doing rather than just looking, which makes it useful for children and mixed-age groups.

The centre sits outside Winchester's historic core, so plan transport rather than treating it as a casual cathedral-side stop. It is best when the planetarium schedule fits the visit.
