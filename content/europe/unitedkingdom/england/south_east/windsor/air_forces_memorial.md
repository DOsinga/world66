The Air Forces Memorial at Runnymede commemorates Allied air force personnel who died in the Second World War and have no known grave. Its long stone galleries record more than 20,000 names, making the site sober rather than showy.

Climb the tower for broad views over Windsor, the Thames Valley, and aircraft approaching Heathrow. The setting pairs naturally with Runnymede and the nearby Magna Carta memorials.
