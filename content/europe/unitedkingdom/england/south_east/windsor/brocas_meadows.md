Brocas Meadows lie across Windsor Bridge on the Eton side of the Thames. The draw is simple: open grass, river light, and one of the best easy views back towards Windsor Castle.

It is a good place to pause between Windsor and Eton, especially if the town centre is busy. Bring a picnic or just use it as a quieter riverside detour.
