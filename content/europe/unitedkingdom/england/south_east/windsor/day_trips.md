Windsor is itself a day trip from London for many visitors, but if you're staying overnight, **London** is 50 minutes away by train and works perfectly the other way round. **Oxford**, an hour west, is the natural pairing for anyone wanting a second helping of English heritage. The river towns of Henley and Marlow, with their own pubs and walks along the Thames, are within easy driving distance.
