---
title: "Eating Out"
type: section
---

Windsor has a fair number of restaurants for its size, mostly priced for tourists and London day-trippers. The high street and the area around the bridge have the obvious chains and a few independents. For something better, walk over the bridge into Eton, where there are a couple of more interesting kitchens, or drive a few miles into the surrounding villages, several of which have good pubs.
