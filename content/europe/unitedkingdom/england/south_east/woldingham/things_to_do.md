---
title: Things to Do
type: section
---

Woldingham's appeal is almost entirely outdoors. The North Downs chalk landscape offers good walking — clear tracks through open farmland and beech woodland, with long views south across the Weald on clear days. Woldingham School's Victorian mansion, the highest point of the North Downs at nearby Botley Hill, and the chalk grassland SSSI at Woldingham and Oxted Downs are the specific draws for visitors who make the trip out from London.
