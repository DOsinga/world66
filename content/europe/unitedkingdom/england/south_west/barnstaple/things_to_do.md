Barnstaple's appeal is partly the town itself — the Pannier Market, Butchers Row, the medieval Long Bridge — and partly its role as a base for the North Devon coast and Exmoor. The town rewards a couple of hours of wandering, but most people are here for what's around it: surfing beaches, the Tarka Trail, cliff walks, and national park.
