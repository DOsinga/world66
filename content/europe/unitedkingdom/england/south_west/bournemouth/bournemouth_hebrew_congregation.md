Bournemouth Hebrew Congregation's synagogue was built in 1905 in an Art Nouveau interpretation of the Moorish Revival style often used for synagogues of the period. It is mainly an architectural stop, worth noticing when exploring the town centre's religious and early-20th-century buildings.
