Bristol Aquarium is a family-friendly stop on the Harbourside, close to the cathedral, M Shed, and the main waterfront walk. Its tanks move from British waters to Mediterranean and tropical habitats, with seahorses, puffer fish, coral displays, and sharks.

It works best as part of a half-day around the harbour, especially in wet weather or with children in tow.
