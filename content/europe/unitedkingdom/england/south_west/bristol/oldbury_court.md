Oldbury Court Estate gives Bristol a broad green escape along the River Frome, with
woodland paths, open lawns, riverside walking, and a popular play area. It is less
formal than the city's showpiece gardens, which makes it useful for a relaxed half-day
outdoors.

The estate works well for families, dog walkers, and anyone following the Frome Valley
paths. Come for the space and shade rather than a single must-see monument, and expect a
local park atmosphere.
