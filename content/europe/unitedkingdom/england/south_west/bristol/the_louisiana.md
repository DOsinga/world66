The Louisiana is a small independent live-music venue and pub near Wapping Wharf, the kind of upstairs room where Bristol's music scene still feels close enough to touch. It does not have the civic scale of Bristol Beacon or the polish of St George's, but that is the point: this is where touring bands, local acts, and first-break performers can make a tiny room feel much bigger.

Check the listings before you go, because the value is entirely in what is on that night. When the bill is good, it is one of Bristol's better low-key evenings: a pint downstairs, a narrow stairway, and a gig where you are never far from the stage. It works best for travellers who would rather hear the city than just look at it.
