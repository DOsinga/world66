Wake The Tiger is Bristol's large-scale immersive art attraction, built around a sequence of themed rooms, installations, and theatrical environments. It suits visitors who enjoy playful, photo-friendly spaces but want something more elaborate than a standard gallery.

The experience is strongest as a shared outing: families, groups, and curious adults can all find details to explore at their own pace. It also fits Bristol's creative reputation, adding a contemporary counterpart to the city's maritime, street-art, and engineering heritage.
