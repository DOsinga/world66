The Cerne Abbas Giant is a huge chalk figure cut into a hillside above the Dorset village of Cerne Abbas. The outline is made by removing turf to expose the white chalk beneath, so the figure stands out sharply against the grass.

The best visit is simple: stop at the viewing area, take in the scale of the carving, then continue into Cerne Abbas for the village and abbey remains.
