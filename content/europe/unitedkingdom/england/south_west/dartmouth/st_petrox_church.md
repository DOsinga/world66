St Petrox Church stands beside Dartmouth Castle at the mouth of the Dart, where the river opens toward the sea. The site has medieval roots, though the building has been rebuilt over time, and the churchyard gives some of the best views around Dartmouth.

It is a short, worthwhile addition to a castle visit rather than a separate outing. Look inside for the historic church fittings, then linger outside for the harbour and coastal views.
