---
title: "Eating Out"
type: section
---

Dulverton is small but eats well. **Woods Bar & Restaurant** on Bank Square is the long-standing favourite, with seasonal cooking, a good wine list, and a fire in winter. **The Bridge Inn** by the Barle bridge is the locals' pub of choice — good ales, ploughman's lunches, and a riverside garden in summer. **Lewis's Tea Rooms** on the High Street is the classic afternoon-tea stop for Exmoor walkers.

For walking food, the **Exclusive Cake Co.** sells excellent take-away cakes and savoury pies; **Mortimer's** is a deli for picnic supplies. Most places close by 9pm and several close one or two days a week — check ahead in shoulder season.
