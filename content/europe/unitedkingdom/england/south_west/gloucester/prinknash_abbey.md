Prinknash Abbey is a Benedictine monastery in the hills above Gloucester, known for its quiet setting and long monastic association. It gives the Gloucester area a contemplative rural stop beyond the cathedral and docks.

Visitors should remember that this is an active religious community, not just a historic attraction. Check access before going and keep the visit low-key, especially around services or private areas.
