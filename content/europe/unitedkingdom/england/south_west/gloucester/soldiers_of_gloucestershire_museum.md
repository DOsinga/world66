The Soldiers of Gloucestershire Museum covers the county's military history through the stories of the Gloucestershire Regiment and the Royal Gloucestershire Hussars. It is a small, focused museum near the docks, with uniforms, medals, personal accounts, and displays on campaigns from the 18th century onward.
