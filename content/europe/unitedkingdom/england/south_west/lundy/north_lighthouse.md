North Lighthouse is the prize at the far end of the island. The walk out is exposed and simple, with sea on both sides and birds using the cliffs below like a city.

It is worth going all the way if weather and sailing times allow. The northern tip feels wilder than the landing side, and the lighthouse gives the walk a clean turning point.
