Luxulyan Valley is one of Cornwall's better short walks if you like industrial history with your woodland. Paths follow the steep, green valley past old watercourses, mill remains, and the impressive Treffry Viaduct, built to carry both a tramway and a leat.

The valley is quiet rather than showy, but that is part of the appeal. Wear decent shoes after rain, and allow time to piece together how much engineering once sat inside what now looks like deep countryside.
