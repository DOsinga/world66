Newton Abbot's railway museum material connects the town to the Great Western Railway and the development of rail travel through Devon. Exhibits are small-scale but locally grounded, with models, photographs, signs, and railway memorabilia.

It is a modest stop, best for rail enthusiasts or visitors already in town. The museum helps explain why Newton Abbot grew as a transport and market centre rather than simply as a gateway to Dartmoor and the coast.
