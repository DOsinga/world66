Plymouth's post-war city centre, focused on Royal Parade and Armada Way, is one of Britain's clearest examples of planned reconstruction after the Blitz. Locals have not always loved it, but the broad avenues, civic buildings, and modernist order are worth a walk if you are interested in how bombed cities tried to rebuild themselves with confidence.
