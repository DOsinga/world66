The Royal Albert Bridge carries the railway across the River Tamar between Plymouth and Saltash. Built from 1854 to 1859, it is one of Isambard Kingdom Brunel's great engineering works, with a distinctive bowstring form that still stands out beside the road bridge.

You do not need long here, but it is worth seeking out if you like Victorian engineering or are crossing between Devon and Cornwall.
