Restormel Castle is a ruined 13th-century castle above the River Fowey, best known for its unusually complete circular shell keep. The walls still give a clear sense of the castle's shape, and stairs near the former gatehouse let you walk much of the circuit.

It is a compact but worthwhile stop for anyone exploring inland Cornwall. The appeal is the simple, readable ruin and the quiet hilltop setting rather than a large museum experience.
