Dinton Park and Philipps House offer a quiet National Trust outing west of Salisbury, with landscaped parkland around a Georgian country house. The house itself is not always the main event; the setting and walks are the reliable draw.

It is best for travellers with a car who want a gentle countryside stop away from Stonehenge and Salisbury Cathedral. Check access before going, as opening arrangements can be more limited than at major National Trust properties.
