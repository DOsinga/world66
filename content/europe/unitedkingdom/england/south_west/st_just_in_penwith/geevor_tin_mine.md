Geevor Tin Mine is one of the clearest places to understand Cornwall's tin-mining coast. The visit combines surface processing buildings, mining exhibits, and a guided section through an early tunnel, so you see both the machinery and the cramped underground work.

Pair it with the nearby mining remains at Botallack and Levant if you want the wider landscape: engine houses, sea cliffs, and the hard edge of Cornwall's industrial past.
