Sennen Cove is one of the easier coastal rewards near Land's End: a sandy beach, surf, and cliff paths with big Atlantic views. It suits travellers who want the drama of the far west of Cornwall without committing to a long walk. In summer the car park fills quickly, so arrive early or come late in the day.
