St Levan's Church is a remote Cornish church near the south-western tip of the peninsula, best known for the split stone in its churchyard. Local legend gives the stone an older, pre-Christian power, later folded into stories of St Levan himself.

The church is worth a short detour if you are exploring the coast around Porthcurno, Land's End, or St Just. It is small, atmospheric, and very much part of the strange old religious landscape of far west Cornwall.
