Woodchester Mansion is a rare chance to see a great house caught mid-thought. Building began in the 1850s and stopped before the interiors were finished, leaving exposed structure, missing floors, and Gothic ambition without the usual country-house polish.

The setting in Woodchester Park makes the visit even better. Check opening days before travelling, because the mansion does not keep daily hours year-round.
