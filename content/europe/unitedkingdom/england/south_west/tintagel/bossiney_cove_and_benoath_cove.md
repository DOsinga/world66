Bossiney Cove and neighbouring Benoath Cove sit below the cliffs east of Tintagel, where the North Cornwall coast turns wild very quickly. They are not broad resort beaches; they are small, tidal coves reached by steep paths, with rock, surf, and a strong sense of exposure.

Check tide times before going, because sand can disappear and access is not forgiving. On a good day they make a fine coastal walk from Tintagel, especially if you want the drama of the cliffs without staying in the castle crowds.
