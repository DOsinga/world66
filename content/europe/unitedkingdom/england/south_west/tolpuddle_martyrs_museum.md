Tolpuddle Martyrs Museum tells the story of the Dorset farm workers whose 1834 conviction and transportation became a landmark in British labour history. Exhibits explain the village, the oath that led to the trial, and the public campaign that followed.

It is a compact but worthwhile stop for travellers interested in social history, especially when exploring rural Dorset by car.
