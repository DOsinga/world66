Victoria Gardens is a small Victorian garden laid out to mark Queen Victoria's Diamond Jubilee. It is planted with exotic trees, shrubs, and flower beds, with enough variety to make it more than just a shortcut through town.

Come here for a quiet pause between the cathedral, the Royal Cornwall Museum, and Truro's shopping streets. It is a modest stop, but a pleasant one if you like old civic gardens.
