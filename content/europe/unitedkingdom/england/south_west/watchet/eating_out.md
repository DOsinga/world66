---
title: Eating Out
type: section
---

Watchet is small and the choice is limited but reliable. The **Pebbles Tavern** on Market Street is the best pub in the centre — a small free house with a serious cider list (more than thirty West Country ciders, several local to Exmoor) and decent pub food. The **Esplanade Club** on the seafront does a no-frills carvery and good views of the harbour.

For fish and chips, **Captain's Galley** on the quay is the local choice. **The Star Inn**, just above the harbour on Mill Lane, has the most ambitious dinner menu in town and a sheltered terrace.
