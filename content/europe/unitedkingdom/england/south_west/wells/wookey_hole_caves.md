Wookey Hole Caves are limestone caverns near Wells, open as a long-running show cave and family attraction. The cave system has a mix of natural chambers, underground water, and stories tied to local legend.

It is close enough to Wells to combine with the cathedral and Bishop's Palace, but it has a different feel: more geological, playful, and aimed at visitors who want a half-day attraction outside the city centre.
