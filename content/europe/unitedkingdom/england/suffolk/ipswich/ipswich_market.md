Ipswich Market is a central street market that is especially useful for a quick, informal meal. Stalls change, but the appeal is the mix: roast meats, baked potatoes, Caribbean food, Tunisian dishes, and other easy lunches.

It is not a grand market hall, but it gives the town centre some life and is worth checking if you are already walking between the waterfront and the old streets around Cornhill.
