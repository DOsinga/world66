A well-regarded pub in the centre of Ipswich with a good range of Suffolk ales and a kitchen that takes pub food more seriously than most. The menu typically features local Suffolk produce — fish, game, and good beef — in straightforward, well-executed dishes. The building is a traditional Suffolk pub interior with exposed beams and not too much renovation.

Busy on match days when Ipswich Town are at home. Worth booking for dinner at weekends.
