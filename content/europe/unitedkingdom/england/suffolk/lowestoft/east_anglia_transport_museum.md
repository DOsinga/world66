The East Anglia Transport Museum is a hands-on vintage transport museum rather than a static shed of old vehicles. Its best feature is movement: trams, trolleybuses, and narrow-gauge trains run on the museum site, giving the visit the feel of a small working system.

It is a strong family stop, but transport enthusiasts will get the most from the details of restoration, signage, and street furniture. Check opening days carefully, as the museum is seasonal and volunteer-run. When services are operating, it is one of the more distinctive attractions around Lowestoft.
