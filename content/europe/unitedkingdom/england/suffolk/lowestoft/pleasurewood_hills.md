Pleasurewood Hills is a long-established family theme park on the southern edge of Lowestoft, in the Gunton area. It has been operating since 1983 and offers a range of rides from gentle children's attractions to more serious rollercoasters, along with a sea lion show and various seasonal events.

The park is the largest in East Anglia and draws visitors from across the region throughout the summer season. It is set in pleasant woodland on the edge of the Broads, which gives it a slightly more relaxed atmosphere than urban theme parks. Opening times and ride availability vary by season — check ahead before visiting.
