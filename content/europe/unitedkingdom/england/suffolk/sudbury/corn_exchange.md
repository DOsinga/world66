The Corn Exchange stands on Market Hill, its Italianate facade of 1841 a reminder that Sudbury was once a serious agricultural market town, not just a picturesque stop on the Gainsborough trail. The building served the grain trade for over a century, and its robust commercial architecture reflects the prosperity of Suffolk farming in the Victorian era.

The building has been converted for community and retail use but retains much of its original character, including the trading floor. It is one of several Victorian commercial buildings that, together with the Town Hall and the medieval churches, give Market Hill its architectural variety and coherence.
