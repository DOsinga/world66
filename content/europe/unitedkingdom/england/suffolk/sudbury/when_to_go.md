---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

Sudbury is a year-round destination, but the Stour Valley is at its most beautiful in May and June (when the water meadows are lush and the hedgerows in flower) and again in October (when autumn colour reaches the river willows and the elm-lined fields). The weekly market on Thursday and Saturday mornings is worth planning around.

Summer (July to August) brings more visitors to Gainsborough's House and the Stour Valley walking routes, but Sudbury never gets crowded. The town is pleasantly quiet compared to better-known Suffolk destinations like Lavenham. Winter is sleepy but perfectly functional — the pubs are warm and the museum is open.

The Quay Theatre's programme runs throughout the year and is worth checking; it occasionally hosts events that draw visitors from well beyond the immediate area.
