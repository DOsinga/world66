The Lapworth Museum of Geology is the University of Birmingham's geology museum, with fossils, minerals, rocks, and displays that explain the deep history of the Earth. Its collections are serious, but the galleries are set up for general visitors as well as students.

It is an easy add-on to the Barber Institute of Fine Arts in Edgbaston, especially if you want a museum stop away from the city centre.
