Ramgarhia Sikh Temple stands near the Jewellery Quarter and is one of Birmingham's visible Sikh landmarks. For visitors, the interest is both architectural and cultural: a gurdwara rooted in the city's large South Asian communities, not just a building to glance at.

Treat it first as an active place of worship. If you visit, be respectful about dress, photography, and access, and use the stop to understand Birmingham as a city shaped by migration, industry, and living religious communities.
