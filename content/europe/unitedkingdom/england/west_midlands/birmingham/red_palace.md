The Red Palace is a narrow red-brick and terracotta building in Birmingham's Jewellery Quarter. It was designed for industrial workshops, with machinery and services built into a compact corner site, and its small tower gives the block a distinctive profile.

It is a quick architectural stop rather than a major attraction, but it fits well into a Jewellery Quarter walk with the area's workshops, churches, and industrial heritage sites.
