The Ackers is an outdoor activity centre in southeast Birmingham, giving the city an unexpected place for dry-slope skiing, climbing, paddling, and instructed adventure sessions. It is practical rather than scenic, but that practicality is useful.

Book ahead and choose a specific session instead of arriving vaguely. It is best for families, groups, or travellers who want an active break from museums and canals without leaving the city.
