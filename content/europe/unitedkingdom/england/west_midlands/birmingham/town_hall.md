Birmingham Town Hall is a classical concert hall facing Victoria Square, better known now for performances than municipal business. Its columns and heavy stonework give the square one of its strongest civic landmarks.

Look for lunchtime organ recitals and smaller concerts if you want to see the interior without committing to a full evening. It pairs naturally with the nearby museum, library, and canal-side centre.
