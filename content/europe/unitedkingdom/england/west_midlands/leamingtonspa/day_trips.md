Leamington's greatest asset as a base is its central position in the English Midlands. [Warwick Castle](/europe/unitedkingdom/england/birmingham_and_west_midlands/warwick) is two miles away — one of the best-preserved medieval fortresses in England and a half-day easily spent. Stratford-upon-Avon is 15 miles south. [Coventry](/europe/unitedkingdom/england/birmingham_and_west_midlands/coventry), with its extraordinary bombed and rebuilt cathedral, is 12 miles north. [Birmingham](/europe/unitedkingdom/england/birmingham_and_west_midlands/birmingham) city centre is 30 minutes by train.
