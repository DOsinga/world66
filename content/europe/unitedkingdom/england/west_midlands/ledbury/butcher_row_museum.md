Butcher Row Museum is a small local-history museum in central Ledbury. Its collection covers the town and surrounding area, including material linked to the Battle of Ledbury in 1645, local trades, domestic life, and an eclectic group of musical instruments. It is best combined with a walk along Church Lane and the Market House rather than treated as a long museum visit.
