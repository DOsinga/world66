Great Malvern Priory is the oldest major building in Great Malvern, founded as a Benedictine monastery around 1075. Its medieval architecture survives as the main historic anchor of a town otherwise strongly shaped by its Victorian spa period.

The priory is worth a deliberate stop for its scale, stonework, and long continuity of use. It pairs naturally with a walk through Great Malvern and the lower slopes of the Malvern Hills.
