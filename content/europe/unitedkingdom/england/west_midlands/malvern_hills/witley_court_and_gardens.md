Witley Court is a ruined country house northwest of Worcester, left roofless after a fire in 1937 but still grand enough to feel theatrical. The shell of the mansion, the formal gardens, and the restored Perseus and Andromeda fountain make it one of the stronger historic-house visits in this part of Worcestershire.

The fountain is the showpiece when it is running, with a central jet that can rise more than 30 metres. Allow time for the grounds as well as the ruins; the scale of the estate is a large part of the appeal.
