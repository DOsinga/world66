Stafford Castle stands on the earthworks of a Norman stronghold, with the ruins of a 19th-century Gothic revival castle on the hilltop. The remains are modest, but the setting gives a clear sense of why this was a defensive site.

Come for the short climb, the open grounds, and the view back toward Stafford. It works well as a low-key outdoor stop rather than a full castle tour.
