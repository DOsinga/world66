Boscobel House is famous for the story of Charles II hiding after the Battle of
Worcester, including the royal-oak episode that became part of English royal legend. The
timber-framed house, garden, and reconstructed hiding-place narrative make it a vivid
Civil War site rather than just another country house.

It lies outside central Wolverhampton, so treat it as a deliberate excursion. For
travellers interested in the English Civil War, monarchy, or old houses with a strong
story, it clears the trip.
