Stratford Butterfly Farm is a walk-through tropical butterfly house close to the River Avon. The main glasshouse is warm and humid, with butterflies flying freely among dense planting, pools, and feeding stations.

Smaller rooms cover caterpillars, insects, spiders, and other invertebrates, making it a useful family stop when the Shakespeare sights need a break or the weather turns wet.
