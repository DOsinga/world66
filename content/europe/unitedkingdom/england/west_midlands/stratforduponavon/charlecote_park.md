Charlecote Park is a Tudor country house set in a deer park between Stratford-upon-Avon and Warwick. The house is managed by the National Trust, with period rooms, outbuildings, and open parkland that make it an easy half-day break from Shakespeare sights.

The appeal is the whole estate: the red-brick house, the river, the lawns, and the deer moving through the park. It works well if you want a quieter historic house close to Stratford.
