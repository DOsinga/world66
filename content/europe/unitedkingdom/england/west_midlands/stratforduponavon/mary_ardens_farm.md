Mary Arden's Farm in Wilmcote connects Shakespeare country to rural life rather than theatre or town houses. The site is associated with Shakespeare's mother and presents timber-framed buildings, farm animals, and the domestic rhythms of the countryside that shaped the playwright's family background.

It is strongest for families and for travellers who have already seen Stratford's central Shakespeare properties. Check current opening arrangements, as the Shakespeare Birthplace Trust has varied access across its sites in recent years.
