---
title: Things to Do in Stratford-upon-Avon
type: section
---

Stratford is a compact town and the main Shakespeare sites are all within easy walking distance of each other. The birthplace, New Place, and Hall's Croft form a natural circuit in the old town; Holy Trinity Church and the Royal Shakespeare Theatre are five minutes along the river. Anne Hathaway's Cottage is a pleasant walk or short bus ride to the village of Shottery.
