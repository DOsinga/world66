The George Marshall Medical Museum is a small medical-history museum in Worcester, with displays that include death masks, a reconstructed apothecary shop, and a Victorian operating theatre. It is set within a hospital training facility rather than a conventional city-centre museum.

It is a specialist stop, but worthwhile if you are interested in the history of medicine or want a quieter museum beyond Worcester's cathedral and Civil War sites.
