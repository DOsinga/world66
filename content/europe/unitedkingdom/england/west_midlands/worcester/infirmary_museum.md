The Infirmary Museum occupies part of the old Worcester Royal Infirmary and turns local medical history into something tangible. Exhibits cover hospital life, surgery, nursing, public health, and the changing relationship between doctors and patients in a provincial English city.

It is a compact museum, but a worthwhile one if you enjoy social history or want a break from cathedral-and-river sightseeing. Its university setting also gives it a lived-in feel: less polished heritage attraction, more working collection with a strong local story.
