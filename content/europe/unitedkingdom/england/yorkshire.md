Yorkshire is England's largest historic county and one of the most rewarding to explore — a combination of post-industrial cities reinventing themselves, expansive moor-and-dale national park country, and a string of small market towns that feel essentially medieval. The accent, the pride, the food (curd tart, Yorkshire pudding, Wensleydale cheese) and the sometimes-belligerent friendliness are all distinctively Yorkshire and not to be confused with anywhere else in England.

The two big cities, [Leeds](/europe/unitedkingdom/england/yorkshire/leeds) and [Sheffield](/europe/unitedkingdom/england/yorkshire/sheffield), are both old industrial centres that have transformed in the past three decades — Leeds into a financial and cultural hub with serious independent shopping and a strong music scene, Sheffield into a young, creative city anchored by two large universities and surrounded by some of the best walking country in England. [Bradford](/europe/unitedkingdom/england/yorkshire/bradford), next to Leeds, has a particularly good curry scene (one of the best in the UK) and the National Science and Media Museum.

[York](/europe/unitedkingdom/england/yorkshire/york) is the historic jewel — a small walled cathedral city with one of the great Gothic cathedrals (the Minster), Roman and Viking remains, the Shambles (a tilted medieval lane), and the National Railway Museum. Easy to walk in a long day.

The countryside is the other reason to come. The [Yorkshire Dales](/europe/unitedkingdom/england/yorkshire/yorkshire_dales) — limestone valleys, drystone walls, sheep-cropped fells, market villages — and the [North York Moors](/europe/unitedkingdom/england/yorkshire/north_york_moors) — heather moorland reaching the dramatic North Sea cliffs — are both national parks with extensive walking and cycling networks. The [South Pennines](/europe/unitedkingdom/england/yorkshire/south_pennines_yo) complete the picture.

Within the Dales, [Swaledale](/europe/unitedkingdom/england/yorkshire/swaledale) is the wildest valley, with [Reeth](/europe/unitedkingdom/england/yorkshire/reeth) at its heart, and [Wensleydale](/europe/unitedkingdom/england/yorkshire/wensleydale) the broadest, with Aysgarth Falls, two castles near [Leyburn](/europe/unitedkingdom/england/yorkshire/leyburn) and the cheese of Hawes. [Settle](/europe/unitedkingdom/england/yorkshire/settle) is the start of the Settle-Carlisle railway over the Ribblehead Viaduct, and quiet [Nidderdale](/europe/unitedkingdom/england/yorkshire/nidderdale) has gorges and caves around [Pateley Bridge](/europe/unitedkingdom/england/yorkshire/pateley_bridge). On the Moors, [Goathland](/europe/unitedkingdom/england/yorkshire/goathland) and [Hutton-le-Hole](/europe/unitedkingdom/england/yorkshire/hutton_le_hole) are classic moorland villages.

The coast runs from the cliff-side fishing villages of [Staithes](/europe/unitedkingdom/england/yorkshire/staithes) and [Runswick Bay](/europe/unitedkingdom/england/yorkshire/runswick_bay), through [Whitby](/europe/unitedkingdom/england/yorkshire/whitby) and [Scarborough](/europe/unitedkingdom/england/yorkshire/scarborough), to the long sands of [Filey](/europe/unitedkingdom/england/yorkshire/filey). Further south are the seabird cliffs of Flamborough, the chalk hills of the [Yorkshire Wolds](/europe/unitedkingdom/england/yorkshire/yorkshire_wolds), and the sand spit of [Spurn](/europe/unitedkingdom/england/yorkshire/spurn) at the mouth of the Humber.

West Yorkshire has more than its cities. [Haworth](/europe/unitedkingdom/england/yorkshire/haworth) is the home of the Brontës, [Saltaire](/europe/unitedkingdom/england/yorkshire/saltaire) a Victorian model village, [Halifax](/europe/unitedkingdom/england/yorkshire/halifax) has the Piece Hall, and [Ilkley](/europe/unitedkingdom/england/yorkshire/ilkley) and [Holmfirth](/europe/unitedkingdom/england/yorkshire/holmfirth) are attractive stone towns under the moors. In North Yorkshire, [Knaresborough](/europe/unitedkingdom/england/yorkshire/knaresborough), [Masham](/europe/unitedkingdom/england/yorkshire/masham), [Thirsk](/europe/unitedkingdom/england/yorkshire/thirsk) and [Malton](/europe/unitedkingdom/england/yorkshire/malton) are all good small market towns to add to a trip.
