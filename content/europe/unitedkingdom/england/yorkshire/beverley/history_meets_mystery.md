The company behind the Beverley Ghost Adventure, in which storyteller Dr Smalls, a Victorian ghost hunter with frock coat, top hat and Gladstone bag, leads walks through Beverley's old streets with tales of poltergeists, ghostly carriages, highwaymen, civil war and plague, with some crowd participation. Public walks are announced on its Facebook page; private performances and bespoke walks can be booked by email or phone. There is no published street address; the pin is on Beverley.
