Bridlington harbour is where the boats leave for Flamborough Head and the seabird cliffs at Bempton, including the Yorkshire Belle, a pleasure cruiser from 1947. Sea-angling trips leave from the same harbour, and the trekking centre outside town rides out over the Wolds.
