The Heidi J is a purpose-built 38ft angling boat skippered by Frank Ridley, a charter fisherman of more than 40 years, sailing from Bridlington harbour. Shared trips run for three, four or six hours, from mackerel fishing in summer to wreck, rough-ground and uptide fishing for cod; private charters can be arranged for families, groups and work outings. Beginners are welcome.
