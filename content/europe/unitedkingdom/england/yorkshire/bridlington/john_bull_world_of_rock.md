John Bull World of Rock is a working confectionery attraction on the edge of Bridlington, focused on the striped sticks of seaside rock that are part of the British coast's old holiday culture.

Tours show how the sweets are made, and visitors can usually try making their own. It is light, family-friendly, and best treated as a rainy-day stop or an easy add-on to the town's beaches and harbour.
