Sewerby Hall and Gardens sits just north of Bridlington in clifftop parkland. The house, gardens, and small zoo make it an easy family stop, but the position above the bay is the part everyone can enjoy.

You can reach it by road, land train in season, or a coastal walk from town. It pairs naturally with Flamborough or Bempton if you are building a full day along the coast.
