A wooded ravine running down to the lifeboat station and a small beach on the south side of Flamborough Head. Paths loop through the woodland from the pay-and-display car park. In autumn, migrant birds arriving from across the North Sea gather in the sycamores. Yorkshire Wildlife Trust's Living Seas Centre is at the top.
