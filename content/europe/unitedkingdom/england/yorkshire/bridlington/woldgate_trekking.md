A trekking centre on Woldgate, the old Roman road a mile west of Bridlington at the eastern edge of the Wolds, with steady horses and ponies for novices and experienced riders. It runs hourly treks, riding lessons, half-day treks and a young riders' club, Tuesday to Sunday all year; book by phone, cash only, 14-stone weight limit.
