The Yorkshire Belle has sailed from Bridlington harbour since 1947, and its schedule is posted and sold online each season. The classic trips run out across the bay to the lighthouse at Flamborough Head; the skipper's own 2.5-hour Bempton Cliffs cruise sails under the chalk cliffs with a full commentary, and from April the RSPB charters the boat for three-hour puffin and gannet cruises, booked through RSPB Bempton Cliffs. The boat boards from the North Pier; dogs on leads are welcome. The phone is the skipper's own, so calls are better than texts, which go unanswered. There is no published street address; the pin is on Bridlington harbour.
