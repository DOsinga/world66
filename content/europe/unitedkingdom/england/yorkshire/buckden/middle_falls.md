Middle Falls is a small cascade above Cray, north of Buckden. It is not a grand waterfall, but it makes a good target on the lower valley circuit when the weather is too poor for the tops.

Pair it with Hubberholme and the lanes around Cray for a quiet walk through the upper dale.
