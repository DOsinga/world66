Filey is a quieter, more genteel resort than its neighbour [Scarborough](/europe/unitedkingdom/england/yorkshire/scarborough), with Victorian terraces and gardens above five miles of sandy beach. At its northern end the bay ends in [Filey Brigg](/europe/unitedkingdom/england/yorkshire/filey/filey_brigg), a long rocky reef good for rock-pooling and birdwatching.

Filey is where two long-distance paths meet: the Cleveland Way ends here and the Yorkshire Wolds Way begins. The coast to the south leads to the seabird cliffs of Bempton and Flamborough Head.
