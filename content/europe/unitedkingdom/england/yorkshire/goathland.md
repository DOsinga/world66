Goathland is a moorland village in the middle of the [North York Moors](/europe/unitedkingdom/england/yorkshire/north_york_moors), with sheep wandering across wide greens. It was the village of Aidensfield in the television series *Heartbeat*. Its station on the North Yorkshire Moors Railway, kept in its 1920s style, played Hogsmeade in the first Harry Potter film.

There is good walking from the village: down to the waterfall of [Mallyan Spout](/europe/unitedkingdom/england/yorkshire/goathland/mallyan_spout), and along the old railway trail to Grosmont.
