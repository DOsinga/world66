Grassington is a small Yorkshire Dales village with a cobbled square, stone lanes, and walking country pressing in from every side. It is pretty enough for a slow wander, but the real reason to come is that you can step out of the village and quickly be in classic Wharfedale: river paths, limestone woods, dry-stone walls, and old lead-mining scars.

The village is a good day out from [Skipton](/europe/unitedkingdom/england/yorkshire/skipton) or a gentle base for the southern Dales. [Linton Falls](/europe/unitedkingdom/england/yorkshire/grassington/linton_falls) is the easy walk, [Grass Wood](/europe/unitedkingdom/england/yorkshire/grassington/grass_wood) gives a wilder limestone woodland circuit, and the [Grassington Moor lead mines](/europe/unitedkingdom/england/yorkshire/grassington/grassington_moor_lead_mines) explain why these quiet hills were once industrial.

It is not a big destination, and that is part of the point. Come with boots, a waterproof, and time for the square afterwards.
