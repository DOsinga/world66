Kilnsey Park is a family-run estate and trout farm below Kilnsey Crag, between Grassington and Kettlewell. Its two spring-fed fly-fishing lakes, each about an acre, are open all year and stocked from the on-site trout farm with rainbow, blue and brown trout from 2lb to over 20lb. It runs introduction and improver fly-fishing courses, parent-and-child sessions and one-to-one tuition, and has a family fishing pond, a café and a smokery. Tickets are booked online.
