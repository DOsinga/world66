Kilnsey Trekking & Riding Centre sits on the edge of Conistone, across the Wharfe from Kilnsey Crag and four miles up the dale from Grassington. It is a BHS-approved centre offering pony treks for beginners, longer trail rides and day rides for experienced riders, lessons and residential riding holidays, including self-guided ones. Book by phone; last-minute places are posted on the website.
