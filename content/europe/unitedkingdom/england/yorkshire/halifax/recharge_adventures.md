A community interest company hiring quality e-bikes, with helmet, lock and a safety briefing, from a collection point at Luddendenfoot, a short walk from Mytholmroyd station and right by the Rochdale Canal towpath and the Cragg Vale climb. Hire is from about £35 a day, open seven days a week, with route suggestions, multi-day and group rates, and optional delivery to Hebden Bridge, Mytholmroyd and Todmorden.
