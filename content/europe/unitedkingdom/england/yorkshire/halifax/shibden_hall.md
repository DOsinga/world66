A timber-framed house of around 1420 in a wooded park a mile east of the centre. It was the home of Anne Lister (1791-1840), a landowner and traveller whose diaries, partly written in code, record her relationships with women in remarkable detail; they inspired the BBC series *Gentleman Jack*. The house is furnished as it was in her day, and the park has a boating lake and walks. Run by Calderdale Council.
