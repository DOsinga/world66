Brimham Rocks is a moorland scatter of strange gritstone formations, shaped by weather into balancing blocks, towers, and ledges. It feels wilder than its easy access suggests, with big Pennine views and enough odd rock shapes to keep both walkers and children entertained.

Wear shoes with grip, especially after rain, and take care around drops and scramble routes. The site is run by the National Trust and makes a strong half-day trip from Harrogate when you want open air rather than spa-town polish.
