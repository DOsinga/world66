Day hire of fully equipped narrowboats, with cooking and toilet facilities, from the marina in the centre of Hebden Bridge, 9.30am to 4pm for up to 12 people. Head east through the locks towards Sowerby Bridge or west past the Stubbing Wharf pub towards Todmorden; you are shown the ropes before you set off.
