Hebden Bridge Picture House is a handsome 1920s cinema close to the centre of town. Its programme mixes mainstream films, independent releases, classics, and community events, making it more useful than a heritage facade.

Go if the weather turns or if you want to understand why Hebden Bridge feels more culturally alive than its size would suggest. The building itself is modest, but the affection around it is real.
