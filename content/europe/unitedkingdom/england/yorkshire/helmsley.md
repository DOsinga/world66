Helmsley is a handsome North Yorkshire market town on the edge of the North York Moors. It has the stone-built good looks of a postcard village, but the reason it earns a day is the cluster of serious sights around it: a ruined castle in town, a walled garden beside it, and Rievaulx Abbey a good walk or short drive away.

The town works especially well if you like combining history with a walk. Start around the market square, climb through [Helmsley Castle](/europe/unitedkingdom/england/leeds_sheffield_and_yorkshire/helmsley/helmsley_castle), then follow lanes and paths towards [Rievaulx Abbey](/europe/unitedkingdom/england/leeds_sheffield_and_yorkshire/helmsley/rievaulx_abbey) and [Rievaulx Terrace](/europe/unitedkingdom/england/leeds_sheffield_and_yorkshire/helmsley/rievaulx_terrace). The moors begin to feel close very quickly.

Helmsley is not large, and it is better for daytime wandering than nightlife. Come for castles, abbeys, gardens, and the pleasure of a Yorkshire town that still has a real market-place centre.
