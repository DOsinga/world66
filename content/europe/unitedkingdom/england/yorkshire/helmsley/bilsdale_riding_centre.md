Bilsdale Riding Centre is on Shakenbridge Farm near Hawnby, a few miles north of Helmsley. It runs regular rides from an hour upwards over moor, woodland and quiet lanes on the western side of the North York Moors, plus activity days, two-day activity breaks and riding holidays for children and adults. Rides are booked online. The pin is approximate.
