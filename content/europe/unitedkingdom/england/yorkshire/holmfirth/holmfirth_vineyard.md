A vineyard on the steep hillside above Holmfirth, running guided tours twice daily at 10.30am and 3pm: a walk through the steep vines and the grape varieties grown there, a look at the winery and a tasting of three of its wines. Tours last 60 to 90 minutes and booking is essential; packages add breakfast, lunch, afternoon tea or dinner in the restaurant.
