Colne Valley Museum preserves a row of weavers’ cottages west of Huddersfield, using domestic rooms and working demonstrations to explain textile life in the Pennines. It is a small museum, but the setting makes the industrial story tangible.

The best visits are on demonstration days, when spinning, weaving, or baking brings the cottages to life. It is especially worthwhile if you are exploring the villages and valleys around Huddersfield rather than staying only in the town centre.
