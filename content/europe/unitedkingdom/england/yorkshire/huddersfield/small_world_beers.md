An independent brewery at Shelley, in the hills south-east of Huddersfield, brewing with water from its own Barncliffe Spring. It runs brewery tours with tastings from the brewery bar for groups of five or more, with food and its Barncliffe chilli available; call or email to arrange a date. The bar is up a flight of stairs.
