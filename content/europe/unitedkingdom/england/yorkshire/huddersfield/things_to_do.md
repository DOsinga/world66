---
title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Huddersfield rewards visitors who go beyond the town centre. Castle Hill is the unmissable landmark — the Iron Age hillfort topped by its Victorian tower is the symbol of the town and the views are exceptional. The railway station is itself a sight worth arriving for, and the canal network offers a quieter, watery perspective on this industrial landscape. The Tolson Museum gives the textile history real texture, and the art gallery consistently surprises.

Beyond the obvious, the surrounding Pennine valleys are some of the best walking country in England. The Holme Valley, Marsden Moor, and the edges of the Dark Peak are all within easy reach, offering everything from strolls to serious moorland routes.
