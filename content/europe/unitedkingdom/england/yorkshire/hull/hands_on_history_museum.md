Hands on History Museum occupies one of Hull's old grammar school buildings, with roots in the 16th century. The museum focuses on the city's past and is especially good for families, with displays on Victorian childhood and local history.

The building itself adds to the visit: Andrew Marvell and William Wilberforce were among the school's former pupils, and the rooms still feel connected to that older Hull.
