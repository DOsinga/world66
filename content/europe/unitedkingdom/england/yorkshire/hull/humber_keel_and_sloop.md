A volunteer-run charity that sails two rare Humber cargo vessels of the National Historic Fleet, the keel Comrade and the sloop Amy Howson. Pre-booked trips of four to five hours leave Hull Marina and head up river, aiming to pass under the Humber Bridge, for up to 12 people at £25 each; groups, families and individuals are welcome, and there are hot drinks and toilets on board. Sailings depend on weather and volunteer crew, so ring the secretary first.
