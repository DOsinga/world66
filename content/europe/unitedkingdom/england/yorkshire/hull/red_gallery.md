RED Gallery is a small independent contemporary art space in Hull, run as a non-profit initiative by local artists. It has served as both an exhibition room and a testing ground for student and emerging work, so shows can feel more experimental than the city's larger civic galleries.

It is not a blockbuster stop, but it is useful if you want to see Hull's grassroots art scene alongside the Ferens Art Gallery and the Fruit Market area.
