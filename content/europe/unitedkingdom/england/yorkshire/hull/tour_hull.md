Local guide Paul Schofield and his team lead informal walks through Hull's Old Town, from Robinson Crusoe to the Land of Green Ginger and Wilberforce, at 2pm on Fridays, Saturdays, Sundays and bank holiday Mondays from April to October, starting at the Queen Victoria statue; just turn up and pay. Historic pub walks, city-centre and Victoria Dock walks, and Beverley town and pub walks can be booked privately all year.
