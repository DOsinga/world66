A family-run charter company on the banks of the Humber offering skippered trips for up to four on a RIB, from a one-hour run past the Hull skyline and under the Humber Bridge to half-day trips out to Spurn Point and the Bull Sand and Haile Sand forts, the Donna Nook seal colony in late autumn, or up the Ouse and Trent. It also runs RYA powerboat courses with Humber Rescue. The company is based in North Lincolnshire and launches by trailer; there is no published street address, so the pin is on Hull.
