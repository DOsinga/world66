Hutton-le-Hole is a very pretty village on the southern edge of the [North York Moors](/europe/unitedkingdom/england/yorkshire/north_york_moors), with stone cottages scattered around a wide green, Hutton Beck running through it and sheep grazing between the houses.

Its main sight is the [Ryedale Folk Museum](/europe/unitedkingdom/england/yorkshire/hutton_le_hole/ryedale_folk_museum). Farndale, famous for its wild daffodils in April, is a few miles north.
