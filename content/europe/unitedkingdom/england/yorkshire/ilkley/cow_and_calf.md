A big gritstone outcrop (the Cow) and a boulder beside it (the Calf) on the northern edge of Ilkley Moor, with the old quarry behind used by climbers. It has wide views over Wharfedale. There is a car park and a café nearby, and paths continue across the moor to White Wells.
