Keighley Bus Museum is a volunteer-run collection of buses, trolleybuses, and transport memorabilia,
with a strong West Yorkshire flavour. It preserves the everyday vehicles that linked mill towns,
villages, and cities before car travel took over.

It is a specialist stop, best for transport enthusiasts or families who like big old machines. Check
opening days carefully, since volunteer museums often keep limited hours, and combine it with the
Keighley and Worth Valley Railway if transport history is the point of your visit.
