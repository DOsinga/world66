Knaresborough stands on the cliff above a gorge of the River Nidd, three miles from Harrogate. The view from the [castle](/europe/unitedkingdom/england/yorkshire/knaresborough/knaresborough_castle) grounds down to the river and the great railway viaduct of 1851 is one of the best-known in Yorkshire. You can hire a rowing boat below the viaduct in summer.

Along the river is [Mother Shipton's Cave](/europe/unitedkingdom/england/yorkshire/knaresborough/mother_shiptons_cave), with its petrifying well, said to be the oldest paying tourist attraction in England, open since 1630. The town has a Wednesday market, a maze of steep lanes and a chapel cut into the rock face.
