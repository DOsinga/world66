Bramham Park is a grand country estate north-east of Leeds, known for its long history, landscaped grounds, and formal gardens. It is a different mood from central Leeds: open lawns, old stone, and a sense of Yorkshire wealth built far enough from the city to breathe.

The estate is also tied to major events, but it is worth knowing as a historic house and garden in its own right. It makes most sense for travellers with a car or anyone already exploring the countryside east of Leeds.
