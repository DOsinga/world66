A three-quarter-acre garden on Merrion Street, opened in 1933 and paid for by Thomas Wade's Charity. It has lawns, trees, benches, a sundial and war memorials, and runs through into the churchyard of St John's, the oldest of the city-centre churches. Entry is free.
