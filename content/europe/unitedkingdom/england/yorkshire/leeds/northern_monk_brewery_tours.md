Northern Monk's home brewery in a listed flax mill in Holbeck runs guided tours of about 90 minutes on weekend afternoons, covering the brewery's story and the brewing process, with three third-pint tasters. The Refectory taproom and garden are in the same building. Private and corporate tours by arrangement.
