Salem Chapel is a distinctive former chapel south of the Leeds city centre. It is best known locally as the place where Leeds United Football Club was founded, giving the building a link to one of the city's biggest civic institutions.

The chapel later became a commercial office, so this is mainly an exterior and history stop rather than a traditional church visit. It pairs easily with the nearby Corn Exchange, Kirkgate Market, and Royal Armouries area.
