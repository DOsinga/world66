St Pauls House is one of central Leeds' more unexpected Victorian buildings, dressed up with minarets, carved stone, and coloured tiles in a lively Middle Eastern style. It was built in the 1870s for a cloth-cutting works, which makes the theatrical facade even better.

You do not need long here. Look up as you walk through the business quarter and it stands out from the surrounding offices.
