Thwaite Watermill is a restored early-19th-century mill on an island between the River Aire and the Leeds and Liverpool Canal. Its machinery and riverside setting make it one of Leeds' stronger industrial-heritage stops, especially for visitors interested in how the city's waterways powered manufacturing.
