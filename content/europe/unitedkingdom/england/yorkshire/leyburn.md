Leyburn is the main market town of lower [Wensleydale](/europe/unitedkingdom/england/yorkshire/wensleydale), with a wide market place and a Friday market. Just west of the town, a limestone terrace called the Shawl gives a long walk with views right up the dale.

It sits between two castles. Two miles south is [Middleham Castle](/europe/unitedkingdom/england/yorkshire/leyburn/middleham_castle), the childhood home of Richard III, in a village famous for its racehorse stables. Five miles west, [Bolton Castle](/europe/unitedkingdom/england/yorkshire/leyburn/bolton_castle), where Mary Queen of Scots was held prisoner, still stands almost complete. The Wensleydale Railway, a heritage line, stops at Leyburn.
