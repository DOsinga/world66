Masham is a small market town on the edge of lower Wensleydale, built around a huge market square. It is best known for its beer: two breweries stand a few streets apart. [Theakston](/europe/unitedkingdom/england/yorkshire/masham/theakston_brewery), brewing here since 1827, makes Old Peculier. [Black Sheep](/europe/unitedkingdom/england/yorkshire/masham/black_sheep_brewery) was founded in 1992 by Paul Theakston after the family firm was sold. Both run tours with tastings.

The town has a large sheep fair every September. The Druid's Temple, a fake stone circle built as a folly in the 1820s, stands in the woods a few miles to the west.
