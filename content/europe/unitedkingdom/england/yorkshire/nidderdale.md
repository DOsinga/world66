Nidderdale lies just east of the Yorkshire Dales National Park. It is a protected National Landscape of its own, and much quieter than its neighbours. The valley of the River Nidd runs from reservoirs high on the moors down through [Pateley Bridge](/europe/unitedkingdom/england/yorkshire/pateley_bridge) towards Harrogate.

Its highlights are the limestone chasm of [How Stean Gorge](/europe/unitedkingdom/england/yorkshire/pateley_bridge/how_stean_gorge), the wind-sculpted gritstone of [Brimham Rocks](/europe/unitedkingdom/england/yorkshire/ripon/brimham_rocks), and the birds of Gouthwaite reservoir. The Nidderdale Way, a 53-mile circular walk, takes in the whole dale.
