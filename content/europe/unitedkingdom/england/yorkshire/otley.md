Otley is a small market town in Wharfedale, the valley of the River Wharfe between Leeds and the Yorkshire Dales. It is a proper Yorkshire town — market on Fridays and Saturdays, an improbable number of pubs, and the [Chevin](/europe/unitedkingdom/england/yorkshire/otley/the_chevin) rising steeply behind it to keep out the urban sprawl of Leeds and Bradford.

Thomas Chippendale, the most celebrated furniture maker in English history, was born here in 1718. J.M.W. Turner came to paint the valley, and some of his Wharfedale watercolours are among his finest early works. Otley doesn't trade heavily on either connection, which is characteristic of the place.

The pleasures are outdoor and straightforward: walking through the Chevin's oak woodland to Surprise View above town, strolling [Wharfemeadows Park](/europe/unitedkingdom/england/yorkshire/otley/wharfemeadows_park) along the river, browsing the independent shops on the high street, and spending time in [All Saints' Church](/europe/unitedkingdom/england/yorkshire/otley/all_saints_church) with its medieval stones and Viking carvings. This is not somewhere you come to tick off sights — it is somewhere you come to relax.
