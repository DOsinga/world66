Run by Otley's Turnip Fish Theatre Company, these are hour-long walking theatre shows mixing horror, local history and jokes, led by actors in character as resident ghosts. The Otley tour runs on Saturday evenings, the Leeds tour starts at the Corn Exchange every Saturday evening, and a weekly Hebden Bridge tour began in August 2026. Tours suit all ages, dogs are welcome, and private and group tours can be booked.
