Nidderdale Llamas is a family-run trekking centre at Kiln Farm in Wilsill, two miles east of Pateley Bridge. You lead your own llama or alpaca on a scenic farm trek or a half-day trek out into the dale, and there are junior, adult and teen, and senior or limited-mobility experiences that need little or no walking, plus group, party and team-building bookings. Treks and gift vouchers are booked online. The pin is approximate.
