Astro Dog runs stargazing evenings from a unit in the courtyard at Low Dalby, in the middle of Dalby Forest. Sessions combine a guided tour of the night sky with telescopes and binoculars, weather permitting, and shows in a 360-degree planetarium dome; events are listed and booked month by month on the website. It also does private hire, birthday parties and school sessions.
