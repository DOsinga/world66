Dales Bike Centre is in Fremington, just across the Swale from Reeth, and claims the largest hire fleet in the Dales: kids' bikes, mountain, gravel and road bikes and full-suspension e-mountain bikes, with e-bikes hired by the full day only, from age 14. Its qualified guides lead rides on the trails that start at the door, and the easy Swale Trail to Keld and back is the family favourite. There is a shop and workshop, the Cakery Café, and bunk, pod and apartment accommodation for riders.
