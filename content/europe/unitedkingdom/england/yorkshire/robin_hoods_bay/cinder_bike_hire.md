Cinder Bike Hire works from the old station workshops on Station Road, right on the Cinder Track at the top of Robin Hood's Bay. It rents Trek e-bikes, mountain bikes and children's bikes, booked and paid for online. Its suggested routes run north to Hawsker (2.5 miles each way) or Whitby (5 miles), or south to Ravenscar, and there are moorland bridleways for something wilder.
