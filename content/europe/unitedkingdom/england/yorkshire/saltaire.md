Saltaire is a model village built between 1851 and 1876 by the mill owner Titus Salt, who moved his woollen works out of polluted Bradford to a site on the River Aire and the Leeds and Liverpool Canal. Around his huge Italianate mill he built neat stone terraces for the workers, with a school, almshouses, a hospital, a park and a church, but no pub. It is so complete that it was made a UNESCO World Heritage Site in 2001.

[Salts Mill](/europe/unitedkingdom/england/yorkshire/saltaire/salts_mill_saltaire) now holds galleries of work by David Hockney, who was born in Bradford, along with bookshops and cafés. Across the river, Roberts Park leads to the Shipley Glen Tramway, a little Victorian cable tramway that climbs to the woods above. Saltaire is about fifteen minutes by train from Leeds or Bradford.
