Bayhire hires mountain bikes, children's bikes and e-bikes for the Cinder Track, the old railway line between Scarborough and Whitby. In Scarborough bikes are handed over outside 22 Scardale Crescent, near the North Bay and Peasholm Park and about five minutes from the start of the track, between 9.30am and 10.30am, and can be returned up to 7pm; helmet, lock and pump are included. There is also a Whitby handover point at Stakesby Vale and a delivery service around the coast. Reserve online.
