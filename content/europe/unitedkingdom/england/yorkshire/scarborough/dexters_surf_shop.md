Dexters is a Surfing England-accredited surf school and hire centre in a shop on the beachfront of Scarborough's North Bay, open all year. It teaches surfing, paddleboarding, kayaking and bodyboarding, offers walk-in adaptive surfing, and hires boards, kayaks and wetsuits by the session or overnight. Guided SUP and kayak tours of the bay, including half-day 'beach and bite' tours, and daily kids' and adult watersports clubs complete the line-up. The shop has changing rooms, toilets and free lockers; phone 9am to 5pm. The pin is approximate.
