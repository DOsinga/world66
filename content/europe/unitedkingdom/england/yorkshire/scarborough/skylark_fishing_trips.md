Skylark is a 35ft wooden boat that sails from the Golden Ball slipway on Sandside, in Scarborough's harbour. Trips run from three to ten hours, from mackerel fishing and inshore winter trips to wreck fishing, and rods and reels are free to use; bait and basic tackle can be supplied. Beginners and children are welcome, with a three-hour trip suggested for under-12s, and you keep what you catch. The boat also does coastal cruises and private hire.
