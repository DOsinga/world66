3 Peaks Cycles is a bike shop, workshop and café on the Market Place in Settle. It hires electric bikes, gravel bikes, mountain bikes and hybrids for riding the lanes and bridleways of Ribblesdale and the Three Peaks; book online, as walk-in hire on the day depends on availability. Bikes must be collected from the shop, as there is no delivery.
