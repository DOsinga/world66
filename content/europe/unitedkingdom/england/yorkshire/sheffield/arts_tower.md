The Arts Tower is one of Sheffield's clearest mid-century landmarks, a tall University of Sheffield block finished in 1965 and now Grade II* listed. Its best-known quirk is the paternoster lift, a continuously moving chain of open compartments that feels like a small piece of engineering theatre inside an otherwise working university building.
