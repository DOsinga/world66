Sheffield's best independent writing is in two Substack newsletters, and both are by people who have lived the subject for years. A local historian who runs walking tours writes two or three pieces a week on the city centre's streets, lost buildings and old photographs. A feature writer who has covered Sheffield's outdoors for thirty years runs a magazine about its rivers, woods, parks and the volunteers who look after them.

Between them they lead to Fitzalan Square, with a medieval lane still running across it, and to Wardsend, an overgrown cemetery above the Don.
