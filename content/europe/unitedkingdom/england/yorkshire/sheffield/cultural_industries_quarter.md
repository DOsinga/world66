The Cultural Industries Quarter sits between Sheffield station and the city centre, where old works, warehouses, and workshops have been folded into the city's creative life. It is not a polished museum district; the appeal is in the mix of brick industrial buildings, galleries, venues, studios, and the nearby university crowd.

Use it as a wandering area rather than a single sight. Site Gallery, the Showroom, the Leadmill area, and the smaller streets around Arundel Street give a good sense of Sheffield's shift from manufacturing city to a place that still likes making things, just in different materials.
