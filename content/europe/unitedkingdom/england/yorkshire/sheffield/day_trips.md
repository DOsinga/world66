---
title: "Day Trips"
type: section
---

Sheffield's location on the edge of the Peak District is its single greatest asset. **Stanage Edge**, a four-mile gritstone escarpment that is a mecca for climbers and walkers, is 30 minutes by car or bus to Hathersage and a short walk further. **Chatsworth House**, one of the great English country houses, is about 45 minutes south-west. **Castleton** and the limestone Hope Valley reach by train in under an hour; from Castleton you can walk into Mam Tor, Winnats Pass, and the Blue John caverns. The cities of Leeds, Manchester, and York are all under 90 minutes by train.
