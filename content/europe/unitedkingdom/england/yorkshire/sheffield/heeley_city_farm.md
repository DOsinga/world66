Heeley City Farm is a small urban farm and environmental centre south of Sheffield city centre, with rare-breed animals, poultry, gardens, and vegetable plots. It is volunteer-run and works as both a community project and an easy family stop.

Come for a low-key break from the city rather than a major set-piece attraction. Children can meet the animals, adults can wander the gardens, and the cafe makes it a practical pause if you are exploring the Heeley side of Sheffield.
