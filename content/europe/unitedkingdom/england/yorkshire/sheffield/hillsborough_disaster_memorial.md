The Hillsborough Disaster Memorial marks the 1989 FA Cup semi-final crush in which 97 Liverpool supporters lost their lives. It is a quiet, sombre place rather than a conventional attraction, but it matters deeply in Sheffield's modern history and in British football culture.

Come only with the right tone: this is somewhere to pay respects, not somewhere to tick off between sights.
