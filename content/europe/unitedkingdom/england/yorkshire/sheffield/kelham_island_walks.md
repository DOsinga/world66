Local resident Anders Hanson leads two-hour walks through Kelham Island and Neepsend, from the area's mill-race origins through its heyday as an industrial powerhouse to the neighbourhood it is now, plus an art and studios walk. Walks run mostly on Sunday mornings and summer evenings and are booked through Eventbrite; private tours on request.
