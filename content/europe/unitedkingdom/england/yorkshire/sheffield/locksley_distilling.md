A small independent distillery in Portland Works, a listed Victorian cutlery works, making Sir Robin of Locksley gin, rums and liqueurs. It runs distillery tasting visits and a gin experience in which you blend your own recipe from over 100 botanicals and distil a full bottle to take home. Over-18s only. The pin is approximate.
