The Norfolk Heritage Trail is a way to read Sheffield's east-side history on foot,
linking parks, industrial traces, old estates, and civic landmarks tied to the Dukes of
Norfolk. It turns scattered sites into a coherent walk rather than leaving them as
isolated curiosities.

Use it when you want Sheffield beyond shops and galleries: the trail gives a sense of
topography, industry, and patronage in the city landscape. Wear comfortable shoes and
treat the route as a flexible framework rather than a race.
