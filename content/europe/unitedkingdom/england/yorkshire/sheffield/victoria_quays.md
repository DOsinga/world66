Victoria Quays is Sheffield's restored canal basin, a small but atmospheric reminder that the city was connected to wider industrial routes by water as well as rail and road. Warehouses, moorings, and brick edges give it a different texture from the shopping streets nearby.

It is not large, which makes it easy to fold into a central walk. Come for a quiet pause, a look at the canal architecture, and a useful counterpoint to Sheffield's better-known steel and hill-country identity.
