Pennine Cruisers runs a boatyard and shop on Coach Street, by the Leeds and Liverpool Canal in the centre of Skipton. You can hire a day boat and drive it yourself, take a 30-minute trip on Leo through the day, or a one-hour trip on the larger Tom, or book Tom for exclusive skippered hire of one to six hours. Trips are booked by phone at the shop, by email or online.
