Skipton Boat Trips sails from Coach Street, just off the High Street, onto the Leeds and Liverpool Canal. The one-hour guided public trips run from March to November, and the two-hour dining cruises include afternoon tea, fish and chips, roast dinners and an evening curry cruise; there is also a storytelling ghost cruise, Santa trips at Christmas and a rail-and-sail ticket. Book online.
