Snaygill Boats is a family firm that builds its own narrowboats at a boatyard on the Leeds and Liverpool Canal at Bradley, on the edge of Skipton. Day boats are hired self-drive for a day's cruising past the hills, fields and woods of the Dales edge; longer narrowboat holidays are also available, the boat being your accommodation. Day boat dates for the season are booked online.
