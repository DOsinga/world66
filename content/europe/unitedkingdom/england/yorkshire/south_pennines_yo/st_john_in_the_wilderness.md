A dark gritstone church of 1838 in the wooded valley of Cragg Vale, on the long climb south from Mytholmroyd. Its vicar from 1821 to 1859, Thomas Crowther, campaigned against child labour in the valley's mills. A plaque inside remembers him. The churchyard is full of the names of local mill families.
