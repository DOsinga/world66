Staithes is a fishing village squeezed into a narrow cleft in the cliffs, where a beck meets the sea, its cottages packed tightly down to a small harbour. It is one of the most picturesque villages on the [North York Moors](/europe/unitedkingdom/england/yorkshire/north_york_moors) coast, and has drawn painters since the Staithes Group worked here around 1900.

James Cook worked in a grocer's shop here as a teenager before going to sea from Whitby. The [Captain Cook and Staithes Heritage Centre](/europe/unitedkingdom/england/yorkshire/staithes/captain_cook_heritage_centre) tells his story and the village's. The Cleveland Way runs along the cliffs in both directions, north to Boulby and south to [Runswick Bay](/europe/unitedkingdom/england/yorkshire/runswick_bay). Cars have to stay in the car park at the top of the village.
