Yorkshire Coast Nature is a small local nature-tourism company of birding guides and wildlife photographers. Its signature trips are pelagic boat trips out of Staithes harbour for seabirds and whales, either a three-hour twilight trip or a five-hour 'Yorkshire Adventure'. It also runs birding discovery days, goshawk safaris, puffin safaris, birding holidays at Spurn, and gannet photography workshops at Bempton Cliffs. Call Richard Baines for wildlife trips, or Steve Race (+44 7767 761260) for photography. There is no published street address; the pin is on Staithes.
