Thirsk is a market town in the Vale of York, with a cobbled square and a racecourse. Alf Wight, the vet who wrote as James Herriot, practised here, and his surgery at 23 Kirkgate is now [The World of James Herriot](/europe/unitedkingdom/england/yorkshire/thirsk/world_of_james_herriot). The fictional Darrowby of his books is mostly based on Thirsk.

East of the town, the road climbs the escarpment of Sutton Bank onto the [North York Moors](/europe/unitedkingdom/england/yorkshire/north_york_moors). The view from the top is one of the finest in England, and the White Horse of Kilburn is cut into the hillside nearby.
