Boltby Trekking Centre is in the village of Boltby, six miles north-east of Thirsk under the western scarp of the North York Moors. It takes riders out onto the bridleways, forestry tracks and moorland above the village on treks and trail-ride days, and runs riding holidays. Book by phone. The pin is approximate.
