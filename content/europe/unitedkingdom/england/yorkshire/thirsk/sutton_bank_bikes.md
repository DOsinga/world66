Sutton Bank Bikes runs the bike centre at the Sutton Bank National Park Centre, 1,000ft up on the edge of the moors, six miles east of Thirsk, in partnership with the North York Moors National Park. It hires mountain, gravel, leisure and electric bikes, runs instruction and guided rides, kids' camps and time-trial series, and has a shop and workshop at the head of the graded singletrack loops and skills area. It is open Thursday to Monday, closed Tuesdays and Wednesdays.
