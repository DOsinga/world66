A five-star BHS-approved riding school on a hill farm off Lumbutts Road above Todmorden, with calm horses and ponies chosen for nervous and young riders. It offers group lessons of up to five riders on Saturdays and weekday evenings, private and semi-private lessons, and pony-care sessions; prices are on the website. Follow the signs on Lumbutts Road rather than the sat nav.
