Wensleydale is the broadest of the Yorkshire Dales, the valley of the River Ure, named after the village of Wensley. It is greener and gentler than [Swaledale](/europe/unitedkingdom/england/yorkshire/swaledale) to the north, and is best known for its cheese, made at the creamery in [Hawes](/europe/unitedkingdom/england/yorkshire/hawes_and_gayle).

Its sights run the length of the dale: Hardraw Force near Hawes, the falls at Aysgarth where the Ure tumbles over wide limestone steps, [Bolton Castle](/europe/unitedkingdom/england/yorkshire/leyburn/bolton_castle) and [Middleham Castle](/europe/unitedkingdom/england/yorkshire/leyburn/middleham_castle), and the market town of [Leyburn](/europe/unitedkingdom/england/yorkshire/leyburn). The Wensleydale Railway, a heritage line, runs along the lower dale.
