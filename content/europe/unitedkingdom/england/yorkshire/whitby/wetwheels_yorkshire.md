Wetwheels Yorkshire is the regional arm of the Wetwheels disability power-boating charity, with its accessible catamaran based in Whitby harbour. Trips are designed so every passenger, including wheelchair users, can come aboard and take the helm: the Whitby adventure runs out between the piers and along the coast for about 90 minutes, and there are Scarborough trips into the South Bay and a weather-dependent wildlife special to Bempton Cliffs. Book online, or contact the office for group bookings of ten.
