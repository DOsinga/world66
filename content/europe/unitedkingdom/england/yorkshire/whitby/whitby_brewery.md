Whitby Brewery brews on the East Cliff, up the 199 steps beside the abbey. Tours are led by a local guide and the £15 ticket includes three third-pint tasters, with the taproom open afterwards; tours are booked online. Under-18s and dogs are not allowed on the tour. The taproom has live music and ticketed events.
