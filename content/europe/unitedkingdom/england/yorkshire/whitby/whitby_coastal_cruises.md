Whitby Coastal Cruises runs the yellow boats that board at Brewery Steps on the harbourside. Summer Queen and Esk Belle III run 20-minute sea trips towards Sandsend every day from 10.15am, with no booking needed; Dash II takes harbour tours up the Esk under the viaduct towards Ruswarp. In September there are two-and-a-half-hour coastal cruises for the whales, dolphins and seabirds, and in summer 45-minute sunset and after-dark twilight cruises. The same firm runs three-hour sea-angling trips on the Coastal Fisher (whitbycoastalfishing.co.uk). The company's registered address is 16 Westcliff Avenue, Whitby YO21 3JB. The pin is approximate.
