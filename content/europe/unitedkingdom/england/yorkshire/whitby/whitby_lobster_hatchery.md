A charity hatchery on Whitby's fish pier, opened in 2021, which rears juvenile European lobsters and releases them into the North Sea. Visitors can see the tanks and learn about the town's potting fleet. It is on Pier Road, a short walk from the swing bridge. The pin is approximate.
