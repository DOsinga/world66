Dr Crank leads two evening walks from the Whale Bone Arch on the West Cliff, opposite The Royal Hotel: the Ghost Walk, with tales of the Screaming Tunnel, the Hand of Glory and the Whitby witches, and In Search of Dracula, on how the town inspired Bram Stoker. Both last about 75 minutes and start at 7pm on the dates listed in the online diary; there is no need to book, just turn up and pay. Call ahead early or late in the season. The pin is approximate.
