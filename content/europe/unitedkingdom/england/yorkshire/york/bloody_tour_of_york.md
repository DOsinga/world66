Costumed guides Mad Alice and Lady Peckett lead walks through York's dark and often ridiculous past: executions, criminals, bloodthirsty monarchs and restless spirits. Alongside the Bloody Tour there is an Execution Tour, a York Walls tour and private tours. Tours meet at St William's College on College Street, just behind the Minster; book online.
