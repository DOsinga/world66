York's independent craft brewery runs 60 to 75-minute tours of its city-centre brewery, beer hall and taproom on Walmgate every Friday afternoon and three times on Saturdays and Sundays, with four third-pint tasters and a pint of your choice to finish. Group, corporate and private tours by email.
