Running since 1973, the Original Ghost Walk leaves every night at 8pm from outside the King's Arms pub on King's Staith for a 75 to 90-minute walk through the city's haunted streets; when the river floods the pub, it meets on Ouse Bridge instead. Book online or just turn up. The pin is approximate.
