Walking tours through York's historic centre that go beyond the usual story to the women, LGBTQIA+ people and people of colour in the city's past, with themed tours on chocolate, Anne Lister, medieval Jewish York and more. Guides are postgraduate students and researchers. Public tours run March to October, private tours all year. There is no published phone number or street address; the pin is on York.
