Intimate small-group and private walking tours of York, departing twice daily at 10.30am and 2pm from Library Square on Museum Street by the York Explore library, a few minutes from the Minster. Tours last about two hours and are priced per group of up to eight, taking in the landmarks and corners few visitors find.
