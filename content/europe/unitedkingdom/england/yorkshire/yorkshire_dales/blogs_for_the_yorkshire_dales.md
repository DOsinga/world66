Almost everything written about the Dales is a route guide to the Three Peaks or Malham. These three are closer to the place. The Friends of the Dales has campaigned for the landscape since 1981 and writes about wildlife and threats to it. The national park authority's news reports small things that guidebooks miss. And a walking writer posted seven circuits through the quieter dales in a burst early in 2025.

They lead to a set of winter floodwaters full of waders near Hellifield, a 1950s AA call box in Wensleydale and Littondale.
