Lost Earth Adventures is an outdoor company whose office is in York and whose guides work across the Yorkshire Dales. Its caving trips come in two kinds: horizontal caves with no ladders or abseils, and vertical trips with ladders, abseils and rope work, each as a three- or six-hour session, either joining an open group or booked privately; a half-day horizontal trip on an open group starts at £69. It also runs gorge and ghyll scrambles, rock climbing days and guided Three Peaks challenges. There is no published street address; the pin is on the Yorkshire Dales.
