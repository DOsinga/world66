An independent brewery at Hunmanby Grange on the top of the Wolds near Wold Newton. Tours start in the bar with the brewery's story before heading into the brewhouse, and include two half pints and a branded glass; they run Tuesday afternoons and Saturday mornings, last about 75 to 90 minutes and must be booked ahead. Combined tours with its sister whisky distillery, Spirit of Yorkshire, can also be booked.
