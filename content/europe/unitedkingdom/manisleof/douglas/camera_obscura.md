Douglas's Camera Obscura is one of those small Victorian survivals that makes the seafront more interesting than it first appears. From its perch on Douglas Head, mirrors and lenses throw a live image of the harbour, promenade, and bay onto viewing tables inside the whitewashed building.

Go for the setting as much as the mechanism. The walk up from the harbour gives wide views back across Douglas Bay, and the camera itself is a neat reminder of the old resort-town appetite for engineering tricks and seaside novelty.
