---
title: "Getting There"
type: section
---

Douglas is reached by ferry from Liverpool, Heysham, Belfast, Dublin, and occasionally other UK ports, operated by the Isle of Man Steam Packet Company. The crossing from Liverpool takes about 2.75 hours on the fast craft or longer on the car ferry. Ronaldsway Airport, about 15 kilometres south of Douglas, has regular flights from several UK airports including London Gatwick, Manchester, Birmingham and Belfast. There are no flights to international destinations beyond the UK and Ireland.
