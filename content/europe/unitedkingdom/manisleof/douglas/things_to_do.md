---
title: "Things to Do in Douglas"
type: section
---

Douglas offers a compact range of attractions centred on its Victorian seafront, the Manx Museum, and the historic Tynwald parliament building. The horse-drawn trams along the promenade are a genuine period survival, not a tourist reconstruction, and the TT connections are everywhere. A day or two in Douglas, combined with trips out on the heritage railways, covers the essential sights.
