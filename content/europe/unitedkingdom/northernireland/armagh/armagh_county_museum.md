Armagh County Museum stands beside the Mall and covers the natural history, social history, archaeology, and art of County Armagh. The displays are compact, but they give useful context for the city and the surrounding countryside.

The museum also has a local reference library with archives, photographs, and maps. It is an easy stop while walking between Armagh's cathedrals and the Mall.
