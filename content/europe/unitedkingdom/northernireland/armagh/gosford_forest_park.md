Gosford Forest Park is a large wooded estate south of Armagh, with walking and cycling trails, play areas and remnants of the Gosford Castle landscape. It gives the Armagh area a practical outdoor day out beyond cathedrals and archaeological sites.

The park is best for families, walkers and cyclists rather than visitors seeking a single monument. Allow time for the trail network, and check facilities if you are planning a longer outing.
