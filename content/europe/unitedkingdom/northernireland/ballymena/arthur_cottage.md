Arthur Cottage is a restored thatched cottage near Cullybackey, associated with the family of Chester A. Arthur, the 21st president of the United States. Its appeal is partly genealogical and partly architectural, showing a rural Ulster domestic setting in a compact form.

The site is modest, so it suits travelers already exploring the Ballymena area or interested in Ulster-American connections. Check opening arrangements before going, since small heritage properties often depend on seasonal hours or prearranged visits.
