---
title: "Galgorm Resort & Spa"
type: poi
---

Galgorm sits in 163 acres of wooded grounds on the River Maine, about three kilometres west of Ballymena town centre. The core of the property is a Georgian manor house, and the resort has grown substantially around it over the past decade or so into one of the most-regarded spa hotels in Ireland. The outdoor thermal spa village — fed by the river and set among woodland — is the main draw: it includes a series of heated pools, steam rooms, saunas, and hot tubs, some of them directly alongside the river.

The spa is open to non-residents as well as hotel guests, though advance booking is essential and the experience is considerably more expensive than a standard spa day. The grounds alone are worth a walk: the River Maine runs through them, and in the right season the landscape is genuinely pretty. There are multiple restaurants and bars on-site, ranging from casual to a fine-dining option.

For visitors using Ballymena as a base, a spa day at Galgorm is a reasonable option on a wet afternoon — there is no shortage of those in County Antrim. If you're considering it as your actual reason to visit the area, it holds up: it consistently ranks among the top spa hotels in Northern Ireland and the Republic alike.
