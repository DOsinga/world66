Belfast's independent blogs are mostly gone or have moved on to other places, so this is a short list. One is the city's own guide to the neighbourhoods beyond the centre. The other is the charity that looks after the ring of hills overlooking the city.

They lead east to the Narnia sculptures and George Best's streets, and up onto Carnmoney Hill with its ancient underground tunnels.
