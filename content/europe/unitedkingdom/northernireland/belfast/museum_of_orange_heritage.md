The Museum of Orange Heritage is not a neutral-feeling museum, and that is part of why it is worth considering. It presents the history, regalia, banners, and traditions of the Orange Order, a subject that has shaped public life, parades, and identity in Belfast and across Northern Ireland.

Visitors looking for a fuller understanding of the city will get more from it than those after an easy attraction. Pair it with other Belfast history stops, and read the displays with the same critical eye you would bring to any institution explaining its own tradition.
