The Northern Ireland War Memorial Museum is a small but worthwhile stop in the Cathedral Quarter. Its focus is Belfast and Northern Ireland during the Second World War, especially the Belfast Blitz, evacuation, home-front work, and the American military presence before D-Day.

The scale is modest, so it fits easily between larger city sights. That is part of its usefulness: in less than an hour, it adds a wartime layer to streets that visitors more often read through shipbuilding, politics, murals, and music.
