---
title: Shopping
type: section
---

Belfast's main shopping is concentrated around the city centre: the Victoria Square mall near the waterfront, the covered arcades off Royal Avenue, and the independent shops of Botanic Avenue in the university quarter. St George's Market on Friday, Saturday, and Sunday mornings is the best shopping in the city — a Victorian covered market selling food, antiques, crafts, and general goods, with a particularly lively Saturday morning food market.
