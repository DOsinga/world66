The SS Nomadic is the last remaining White Star Line ship in the world, preserved in the Hamilton Dock in Belfast's Titanic Quarter. The little ship has had an extraordinary career: Titanic tender, troopship in both World Wars, floating restaurant in Paris, river ferry — before being brought home to Belfast in 2006 for restoration.

The Nomadic can be explored from bow to stern, and the restoration has been done with care. The first-class lounge is particularly fine. The ship gives a more intimate sense of the Titanic era than the main museum does — you can walk the same decks that Titanic passengers walked. Included in Titanic Belfast tickets or separately priced.
