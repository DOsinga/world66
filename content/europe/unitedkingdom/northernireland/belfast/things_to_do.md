---
title: Things to Do
type: section
---

Belfast rewards the curious visitor. The murals and peace walls of the Falls and Shankill roads are unlike anything else in Europe — a living gallery of political art that documents thirty years of conflict with raw honesty. The Titanic Quarter has transformed the old shipyard into the city's most compelling cultural district. And the Cathedral Quarter, with its Victorian warehouse architecture and buzzing bar scene, is one of the most atmospheric urban quarters in Ireland.
