Titanic Distillery and Pump House occupies one of the most evocative industrial buildings in Belfast's Titanic Quarter. The old pump house served the dry dock where the Titanic was fitted out, and the revived site now combines maritime engineering history with a working whiskey distillery.

It is best visited as part of a wider Titanic Quarter walk, alongside Titanic Belfast, the slipways, and the dock itself. The distillery element gives the building a new use, but the real travel value is the setting: brick, machinery, water, and the heavy infrastructure behind Belfast's shipbuilding fame.
