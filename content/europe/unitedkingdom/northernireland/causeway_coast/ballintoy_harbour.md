Ballintoy Harbour is a small fishing harbour about a kilometre below Ballintoy village, at the end of a narrow, steep road down Knocksaughey hill. On the way down you pass the white Ballintoy Parish Church of 1813 and the turning for Larrybane and Carrick-a-Rede. The harbour hosts a dawn service on Easter Sunday every year.

In August 2011 the harbour and village were used to film Lordsport on the Iron Islands for the second season of *Game of Thrones*, so expect fans with cameras. The lane is narrow and steep, so take it slowly or walk down from the village.
