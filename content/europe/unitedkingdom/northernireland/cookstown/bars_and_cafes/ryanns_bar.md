Ryann's is one of the more characterful pubs on Cookstown's main street, a traditional bar with a good local following. Weekend evenings occasionally see live music sessions in the back bar, mixing traditional Irish and country music in the way common to Tyrone pubs. It is a comfortable place to settle in for the evening, particularly after a day visiting the surrounding sites. The bar food at lunchtimes is reliable and straightforward.
