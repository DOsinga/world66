Creggandevesky Court Tomb is a Neolithic burial monument in the Sperrins, north-west of Cookstown. Its stones stand in open countryside rather than a manicured visitor site, which gives the place a quiet, slightly searching quality.

It is best for travellers already interested in archaeology or rural Tyrone. Combine it with Beaghmore Stone Circles or Davagh Forest, and expect a short, low-key visit rather than facilities or interpretation boards on every corner.
