Eve's is a well-regarded cafe on Cookstown's long main street, popular with locals for breakfast and lunch. The menu runs to Ulster fry, sandwiches, soups, and daily specials — straightforward food done well and at reasonable prices. The Cookstown sausage features prominently on the breakfast menu. The cafe fills up on market days and weekend mornings; the atmosphere is busy and friendly in the way that good town cafes always are.
