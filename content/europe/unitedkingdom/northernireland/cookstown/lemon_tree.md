The Lemon Tree is one of the more ambitious restaurants in central Cookstown, with a menu that goes beyond the standard town-centre offering. Mains cover a range of grilled meats, fish, and pasta dishes, with good use of local beef and lamb. The room is comfortable and the service attentive. It works well as an evening option for visitors staying in the area who want something more substantial than a cafe meal. Booking is advisable at weekends.
