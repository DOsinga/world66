Springhill House is one of the most rewarding historic-house visits around Cookstown, combining a lived-in family interior with gardens and short estate walks. The National Trust property preserves a 17th-century Plantation house associated with the Lenox-Conyngham family, and its rooms give a compact introduction to landed life in rural Ulster without the scale or formality of a grand palace.

The visit works best as a half-day stop: tour the house, look for the costume collection, then walk through the walled garden and surrounding woodland. Its setting west of Cookstown also makes it a useful bridge between the town and the Sperrin foothills, especially for travellers who want an accessible heritage site rather than a long mountain outing.
