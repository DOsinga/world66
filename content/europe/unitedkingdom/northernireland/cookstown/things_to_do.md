title: Things to Do
type: section
---

Cookstown's main appeal is the remarkable concentration of ancient and historic sites in the surrounding countryside. [Beaghmore Stone Circles](/europe/unitedkingdom/northernireland/cookstown/beaghmore_stone_circles) to the northwest and [Ardboe High Cross](/europe/unitedkingdom/northernireland/cookstown/ardboe_high_cross) on Lough Neagh are the headline attractions, but [Tullyhogue Fort](/europe/unitedkingdom/northernireland/cookstown/tullyhogue_fort) and [Killymoon Castle](/europe/unitedkingdom/northernireland/cookstown/killymoon_castle) are also worth visiting. The Sperrins and Lough Neagh provide good walking and birdwatching within easy reach of the town.
