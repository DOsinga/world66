Castle Ward is an 18th-century mansion and estate near Strangford, known for its split personality: one side is Palladian, the other Georgian Gothic. The house has furnished interiors and an art collection, while the wider grounds include gardens, older farm buildings, and filming locations used for Winterfell in *Game of Thrones*.
