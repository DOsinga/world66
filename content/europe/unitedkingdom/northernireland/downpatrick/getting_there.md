---
title: Getting There
type: section
---

Downpatrick is about 21 miles south of [Belfast](/europe/unitedkingdom/northernireland/belfast). By car, follow the A24 and A7 from Belfast, or approach from [Newry](/europe/unitedkingdom/northernireland/newry) via the A25 through Castlewellan.

Goldliner buses link Downpatrick with Belfast and Newry, and local buses connect with Strangford, Newcastle, Castlewellan, and Ballynahinch. A car is helpful for the outlying wells, stone circle, and countryside stops, though the centre and Inch Abbey can be combined on foot by determined walkers.
