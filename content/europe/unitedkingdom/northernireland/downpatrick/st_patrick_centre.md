The St Patrick Centre is the modern interpretive stop in town, useful because it does not treat Patrick as a simple postcard saint. The displays explain what can be known, what later writers invented, and why the figure still matters across Ireland.

It pairs naturally with Down Cathedral and the county museum. See it first if you want context, or afterwards if the grave and early Christian sites leave you with more questions than answers.
