The Argory is a handsome riverside house on the Blackwater, preserved with its interiors, stable yard, gardens, and wooded walks. It gives County Armagh and Tyrone visitors a classic National Trust country-house experience without the crowds of better-known estates.

The house is interesting, but the setting is a large part of the pleasure: river paths, trees, lawns, and a slower rural rhythm. From Dungannon it makes an easy excursion for travelers who want architecture, domestic history, and a gentle walk in one stop.
