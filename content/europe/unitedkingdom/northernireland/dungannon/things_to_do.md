---
title: 'Things to Do'
type: section
---

Dungannon is a small market town and a brief visit will cover most of it. The main attractions are the O'Neill heritage at Ranfurly House, the view from the hilltop fort site, and Parkanaur Forest Park west of town. From here you are well placed to explore the Sperrins to the north, the Tyrone landscape of the Clogher Valley west, and Lough Neagh to the east.
