Enniskillen Castle stands on the river where a fortress could control movement between the Erne lakes. Today the complex holds Fermanagh County Museum and the Inniskillings Museum, so the visit covers local archaeology, lake life, military history, and the town's long role as a garrison.

Start here before roaming the centre. The castle gives Enniskillen's calm streets a sharper historical edge.
