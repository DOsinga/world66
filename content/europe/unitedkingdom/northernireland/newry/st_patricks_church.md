St Patrick's Church is one of Newry's more substantial church buildings, with a strong presence close to the town's central streets and canal landscape. It gives architectural and religious context to a place many travellers otherwise pass through quickly between Belfast, Dublin, and the Mournes.

This is a quiet, respectful stop rather than a major monument. It is worth adding to a Newry walk if you are already exploring the town centre, especially alongside the canal towpath and older civic buildings.
