The Abingdon Collection is a private museum near Omagh with an unexpectedly broad mix of classic cars, motorcycles, uniforms, military objects and domestic memorabilia. It is the sort of place that depends on personal enthusiasm, and that gives it more character than a standard municipal display.

The collection is especially good for travellers interested in 20th-century motoring or wartime history. Check opening arrangements before setting out, as smaller private museums can operate differently from public attractions. Combined with the Ulster American Folk Park, it makes Omagh a stronger stop than many visitors expect.
