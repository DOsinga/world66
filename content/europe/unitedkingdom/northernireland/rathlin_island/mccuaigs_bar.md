McCuaig's is the island pub, a short walk from the ferry pier at Church Bay, and the social centre of Rathlin. It serves food as well as drink, and it is the natural place to wait for the last boat back to Ballycastle.

It also holds the island's only cash machine, which is only accessible during pub hours and charges for withdrawals, so bring cash from the mainland.
