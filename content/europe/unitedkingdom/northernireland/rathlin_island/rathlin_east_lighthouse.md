The East Lighthouse stands on the cliffs of Altacarry Head at the north-east corner of Rathlin, first lit in 1856. The RSPB's walking trails at Craigmacagan, at this end of the island, are good for buzzards, ravens and Irish hares.

A short way south are the scant remains of Bruce's Castle, destroyed in 1575 at the time of the massacre of the island's garrison and inhabitants.
