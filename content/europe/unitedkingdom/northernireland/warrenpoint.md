Warrenpoint is a compact seaside town on the southern shore of [Carlingford Lough](/europe/unitedkingdom/northernireland/warrenpoint/carlingford_lough) in County Down, just six miles from [Newry](/europe/unitedkingdom/northernireland/newry). The lough is a narrow sea inlet bordered by the Mourne Mountains on one side and the hills of County Louth on the other, and the views from the waterfront are among the best in Northern Ireland.

The town has a [pleasant promenade](/europe/unitedkingdom/northernireland/warrenpoint/warrenpoint_promenade) along the lough, a small square that fills up in summer, and a lively social scene driven by its proximity to Newry. Warrenpoint hosts a number of festivals through the summer months, making it a livelier stop than its size might suggest. The journey from Newry along the A2 is itself scenic, hugging the lough shore — and a short drive out brings you to [Narrow Water Castle](/europe/unitedkingdom/northernireland/warrenpoint/narrow_water_castle), a Plantation-era tower house guarding the entrance to the river. East along the same coast, [Kilbroney Park](/europe/unitedkingdom/northernireland/warrenpoint/kilbroney_park) at Rostrevor is the trailhead for the walk up to the [Cloughmore Stone](/europe/unitedkingdom/northernireland/warrenpoint/cloughmore_stone), a 30-ton granite boulder dropped by a glacier and explained by local legend as the work of Finn McCool.
