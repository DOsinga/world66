Kilbroney Park sits at the foot of Slieve Martin in the village of Rostrevor, about four miles east of Warrenpoint. It is a forested country park with a campsite, a playground, a small caravan park, and a network of walking and mountain-biking trails that climb up through Rostrevor Forest to the Cloughmore Stone and beyond.

The Rostrevor mountain bike trails are some of the best in Northern Ireland, with red and black routes dropping back to the car park through tight singletrack. For walkers, the same network reaches a Yew Tree said to be one of the oldest trees in Ireland, hidden in a quiet corner of the park. There is a café by the main entrance, useful before or after a climb.
