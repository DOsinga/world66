Aberdeen Art Gallery on Schoolhill is one of the better regional art galleries in Britain, free to enter and usually uncrowded. The collection spans Scottish and European painting from the 17th century onward, with particular strength in 19th-century Scottish work and a notable collection of Impressionist and Post-Impressionist pieces. The permanent collection is displayed across the main building, which was extensively renovated and reopened in 2019.

The building itself is handsome — a 1885 design in Renaissance style with a skylit central atrium used for contemporary exhibitions. The café on the ground floor is good. Open Tuesday to Sunday; closed Mondays. Worth at least two hours, more if you engage with the contemporary exhibition.
