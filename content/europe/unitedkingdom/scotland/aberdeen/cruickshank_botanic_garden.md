The Cruickshank Botanic Garden is a quiet green pocket on the University of Aberdeen campus, close to King’s College and the old granite lanes of Old Aberdeen. It has glasshouses, herbaceous borders, an arboretum, and teaching collections that make it feel scholarly without being stiff.

It is best treated as part of an Old Aberdeen walk rather than a destination on its own. Visit King’s College Chapel, wander the campus, then use the garden as a calm counterpoint to the city’s stone architecture and North Sea weather.
