Girdle Ness is the headland guarding the south side of Aberdeen Harbour, marked by a 19th-century lighthouse and open views over the North Sea. It is a good place to understand Aberdeen as a working port as well as a granite city, with ships moving in and out below the cliffs.

The walk here is exposed, so choose a clear day and bring a windproof layer. Birdlife, sea air, and the sweep back toward the beach make it a satisfying add-on to Footdee or the harbour mouth.
