The Gordon Highlanders Museum is a compact but substantial military-history museum in Aberdeen's west end. It follows the regiment's story through uniforms, medals, weapons, personal objects, and campaign material, connecting local identity with wider British military history.

The museum is strongest for visitors with an interest in Scotland's regimental traditions or family-history links to the northeast. Its garden and tearoom make it easier to treat as a relaxed half-day stop rather than a quick specialist detour.
