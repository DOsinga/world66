Hazlehead Park is one of Aberdeen's largest green spaces, covering former estate land granted to the city by Robert the Bruce in 1319. The park has formal gardens, woodland paths, play areas, and enough space to feel like a proper escape from the granite city.

The Queen Mother Rose Garden and the North Sea Memorial Garden give it more focus than a standard municipal park. It is strongest for families, runners, and anyone who wants a quiet hour away from central Aberdeen.
