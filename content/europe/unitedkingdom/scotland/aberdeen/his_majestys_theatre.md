His Majesty's Theatre is Aberdeen's grand performance house, an Edwardian theatre whose granite exterior gives way to a much warmer auditorium of tiers, plasterwork, and gilded detail. It is one of the best surviving pieces of civic entertainment architecture in the city centre.

The building is worth noticing even if you are not seeing a show, but it is at its best as a working theatre. A performance here adds evening life to an Aberdeen visit and pairs naturally with the Art Gallery and Union Terrace Gardens nearby.
