Johnston Gardens is one of Aberdeen's prettiest small parks, laid out with ponds, little bridges, rockeries and shaded planting. It is especially good in spring and early summer, when rhododendrons and other flowering shrubs soften the city's granite mood.

The gardens are not large, but that is part of the appeal. They make an easy pause west of the centre, particularly if you are also visiting the Gordon Highlanders Museum or walking through Aberdeen's residential west end.
