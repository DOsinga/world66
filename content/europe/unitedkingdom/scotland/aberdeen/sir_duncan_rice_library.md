The University of Aberdeen's main library is a sharp modern landmark on the edge of the old campus. Its striped glass exterior makes a clear break from the granite and Gothic stone nearby.

Visitors can use the public ground-floor gallery, and the building works well as a short architectural detour after King's College and the botanic garden.
