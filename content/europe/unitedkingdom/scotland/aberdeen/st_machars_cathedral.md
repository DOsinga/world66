St Machar's Cathedral anchors Old Aberdeen with the kind of granite weight the city does so well. Parts of the church date to the medieval period, and its twin western towers give the building a fortress-like profile above the Don valley.

Inside, the great reward is the heraldic ceiling, a dense field of painted shields that feels surprisingly vivid against the stone. Combine the cathedral with a walk through the university quarter and Seaton Park; together they show a quieter, older Aberdeen than the oil-city centre.
