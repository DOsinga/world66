The Tolbooth Museum occupies Aberdeen's seventeenth-century former civic building, once used for council meetings, courts, and prison cells. Its stone rooms and old jail spaces give a compact look at the city's municipal history.

It is close to Castlegate and the Maritime Museum, so it fits naturally into a short walk around old Aberdeen. The building itself is as much the point as the displays.
