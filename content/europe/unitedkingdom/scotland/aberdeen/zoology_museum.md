The Zoology Museum sits inside the University of Aberdeen and is best for travellers who like old-school natural history collections. The displays run from tiny preserved specimens to skeletons, taxidermy, skins, and models, with enough odd corners to make it more than a quick academic stop. Pair it with nearby King's College and the Cruickshank Botanic Garden for an easy wander through Old Aberdeen.
