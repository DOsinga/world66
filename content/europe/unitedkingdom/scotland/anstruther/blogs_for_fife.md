The East Neuk gets treated as a stop on the way to St Andrews, and the people who live there say that is the biggest mistake. These three are written from Fife: a travel writer who lives on this coast, a walker who works out her own routes on foot, and the county's guide to free days out.

They find the caves where saints lived, a welly boot garden on a slipway, the saltpans at St Monans and the tidal pools the local swimmers use.
