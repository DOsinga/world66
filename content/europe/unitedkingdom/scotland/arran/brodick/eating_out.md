---
title: Eating Out
type: section
tags: [eating_out]
---

Brodick has more eating options than any other village on Arran, though the choice is still limited by mainland standards. Local seafood — langoustines, crab, and mussels from the Clyde — is the thing to look for, along with Arran cheeses and produce from the island's farms. Most places close or reduce hours in winter.
