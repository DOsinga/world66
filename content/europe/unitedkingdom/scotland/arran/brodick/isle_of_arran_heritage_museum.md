The Isle of Arran Heritage Museum occupies a collection of old farm buildings at Rosaburn, about a mile north of Brodick pier, and covers the island's history from prehistoric times to the 20th century. The exhibits are unpretentious and detailed — farm implements, household objects, a reconstructed smiddy (forge), and a cottage interior that shows what island life looked like before the tourist trade.

The geological displays are surprisingly interesting given Arran's importance to the history of earth science. The museum is a good first stop for understanding what you're looking at as you explore the island, and the admission price is modest. Open April to October.
