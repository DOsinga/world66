Machrie Moor has the best-known group of prehistoric standing stones on Arran, with Neolithic and Bronze Age circles set in open moorland. The walk in is part of the appeal: the stones feel remote without being hard to reach, and the mountain backdrop gives the site a stronger sense of place than many more formal archaeological stops.
