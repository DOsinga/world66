The village pub and the social heart of Whiting Bay. Real ales on tap, honest pub food, and a coal fire in winter make this a welcoming stop after a day on the hills. The regulars are friendly, the atmosphere is unhurried, and there is nowhere better in the village to end an evening. In summer, the outdoor seating catches the last of the light over the bay.
