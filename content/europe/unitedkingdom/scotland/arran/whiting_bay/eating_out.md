---
title: "Eating Out"
type: section
---

Whiting Bay is a small village and the dining options reflect that — limited in number but characterful. The village café is the social hub for walkers and locals alike, and the standard of home baking and simple lunches is a reliable pleasure. Don't come expecting a wide choice; do come expecting honest, well-made food in an unpretentious setting. For a broader selection, Brodick is about ten miles north along the coast road.
