The main café in Whiting Bay, and a dependable stop for home baking, soup, and light lunches. Popular with walkers coming off the Glenashdale Falls trail, who tend to arrive hungry and leave satisfied. The atmosphere is informal and welcoming, and the cake is worth making a detour for.
