A small gallery in the heart of the village showing paintings and prints by local and Scottish artists, with a craft shop attached selling handmade work. A good rainy-day stop, and a pleasant browse even on a fine one. The quality of the work tends to be high for a rural gallery of this size, reflecting Arran's long draw for artists attracted by the light and landscape.
