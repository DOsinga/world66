---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

May to September offers the best combination of weather and daylight for walking — long evenings in June and July, and enough reliable dry spells to make the most of the Glenashdale Falls trail and the moorland above the village. The falls are most dramatic after a period of rain, so a visit in spring or early autumn, when showers are more likely, can be worthwhile. July and August are the busiest weeks on Arran, coinciding with Scottish school holidays — expect fuller ferries, busier roads, and the village café with a queue.

Winter is quiet and, on the right day, genuinely atmospheric: the bay empties, the hillsides are often dusted with frost, and the pub comes into its own. Accommodation is easy to find, prices drop, and the island shows a different, less-visited face.
