Loch Morlich lies in Glenmore Forest at the foot of the Cairngorms, just before the road starts its long climb to the ski area. Long stretches of the shore are sandy beach. In 2009 it became the first freshwater loch to win a Seaside Award, and at 300 metres it is the highest beach ever to get one. On a warm summer day it is packed.

A watersports centre rents kayaks, sailing dinghies and windsurfers, and an easy path of about four miles goes all the way round the loch through Scots pine. The Cairngorm bus from Aviemore stops here.
