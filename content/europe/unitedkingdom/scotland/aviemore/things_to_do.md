Aviemore is the place to sleep and shop. Most of the things worth doing are a walk, bike ride or bus ride away. Many of the big Cairngorms sights, including Rothiemurchus, Loch an Eilein, the reindeer and Cairn Gorm, are listed on the [Cairngorms](/europe/unitedkingdom/scotland/cairngorms) page.
