Ballater station opened in 1866 as the end of the Deeside line from Aberdeen, and it closed in 1966. For decades it was a visitor centre with Queen Victoria's waiting room and a replica royal carriage. On 12 May 2015 a fire almost completely destroyed it.

The building was rebuilt and reopened in 2018. It now houses the village library, a café and a visitor centre. It's in Station Square in the middle of the village, a natural first stop, and the start of the Deeside Way runs from here.
