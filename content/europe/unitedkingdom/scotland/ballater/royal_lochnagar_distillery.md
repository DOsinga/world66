Royal Lochnagar sits on the Abergeldie estate at Crathie, just across the Dee from Balmoral and about six miles west of Ballater. It's one of the smaller Diageo distilleries, a tidy group of granite buildings below the hill it's named after.

Tours run daily and end with a tasting. It's an easy pairing with Balmoral, and a quieter, more intimate visit than the big Speyside names.
