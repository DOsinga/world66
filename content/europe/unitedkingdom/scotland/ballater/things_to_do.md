Ballater's own sights take a morning. Its real value is as a base for upper Deeside: Balmoral, Loch Muick and Lochnagar, the Deeside Way, and a distillery that has supplied the royal family since 1848. [Balmoral Castle](/europe/unitedkingdom/scotland/cairngorms/balmoral_castle) itself is covered on the Cairngorms page.
