The Bridge of Alvah is a single elegant stone arch flung across a steep wooded gorge of the River Deveron, about three miles upstream of Banff. Built in 1772 as part of the Duff House landscape, it remains one of the most photographed spots on this stretch of coast — the river runs dark and fast below, hemmed in by sandstone cliffs draped with beech and oak.

The walk to it from Duff House follows a well-marked path along the river through mature woodland, and takes about an hour each way. The route forms part of the longer Deveron Way and is one of the best short walks in northeast Scotland.
