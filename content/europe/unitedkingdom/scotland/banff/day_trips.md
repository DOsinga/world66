A car opens up most of northeastern Scotland from Banff. South down the A947 you reach [Aberdeen](/europe/unitedkingdom/scotland/aberdeen) in about an hour. East along the coast lie the fishing towns of Macduff, Gardenstown, Pennan, and Fraserburgh — the latter with its huge harbour and the unusual Museum of Scottish Lighthouses. West along the A98 takes you through the painted-house fishing village of Cullen (and its famous Cullen Skink soup), past the ruined clifftop **Findlater Castle**, on to Buckie and then [Elgin](/europe/unitedkingdom/scotland/elgin) with its ruined cathedral.

Inland to the south is Speyside, the densest concentration of whisky distilleries in Scotland — Glenfiddich, Glen Grant, Strathisla, and dozens of others all lie within an hour's drive.
