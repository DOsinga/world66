The Chapel of Cille Bharra is one of Barra's most atmospheric small historic sites, a ruined chapel and graveyard set in the island landscape rather than behind museum glass. The place is associated with Saint Barr, whose name is often linked with Barra itself, and it carries the layered feel of a site used and remembered for centuries.

Come for quiet rather than spectacle. The chapel makes sense as part of a slow circuit of Barra's beaches, causeways, and island viewpoints, especially if you like places where local history is still held in stone walls and weathered graves.
