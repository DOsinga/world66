---

Braemar is about hills, rivers and one very big day in September. The village sights take an afternoon. The rest of your time goes on the upper Dee and the walks that start from the edge of the village. [Braemar Castle](/europe/unitedkingdom/scotland/cairngorms/braemar_castle) and the [Lairig Ghru](/europe/unitedkingdom/scotland/cairngorms/lairig_ghru) are covered on the Cairngorms page.
