---
title: "Eating Out"
type: section
---

Broadford has a handful of reliable options for food, which is more than can be said for most villages of its size on Skye. **Cafe Sia** on the main road is the best daytime choice — good coffee, home-baked cakes, and solid lunches. **The Claymore Restaurant** is a reasonable option for an evening meal, with a menu that leans on local seafood and Scottish beef. The **Co-op supermarket** in the village makes self-catering straightforward; the deli counter sometimes has local cheeses and smoked fish worth picking up for a hill picnic.

If you have transport, the drive south toward Sleat and the Armadale ferry brings you past a few additional options, and [Portree](/europe/unitedkingdom/scotland/skye/portree) has a wider range about 25 miles north.
