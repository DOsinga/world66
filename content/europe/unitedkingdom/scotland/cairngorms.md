The Cairngorms National Park is the largest national park in the United Kingdom, covering 4,528 square kilometres of high plateau, ancient Caledonian forest, and fast-running rivers in the eastern Scottish Highlands. The park takes its name from the Cairngorm mountain range — Am Monadh Ruadh, "the Red Hills" in Gaelic — which forms a high, cold, Arctic-alpine plateau in the park's centre. Ben Macdui, at 1,309 metres, is the second-highest mountain in Britain and the plateau around it sustains plant communities found almost nowhere else in the country at this latitude.

The main access point is Aviemore, a small town on the Spey Valley that has served as the highland sports capital since the ski resort opened on Cairn Gorm in the 1960s. [Aviemore](/europe/unitedkingdom/scotland/cairngorms/aviemore) has all the practical services — train connections to Inverness and Edinburgh, gear hire, accommodation of every standard — and the feel of a resort town that has had a hard time attracting year-round visitors but is making a reasonable effort. The better experiences are outside it: the [Rothiemurchus Forest](/europe/unitedkingdom/scotland/cairngorms/rothiemurchus_forest) of ancient Scots pines, the [Loch an Eilein](/europe/unitedkingdom/scotland/cairngorms/loch_an_eilein) castle ruin, and the high plateau itself via the [Cairngorm Mountain Railway](/europe/unitedkingdom/scotland/cairngorms/cairngorm_mountain_railway).

Wildlife is the park's other major attraction. Red squirrels, capercaillie, ospreys, red kites, and reindeer — the last remaining free-ranging herd in Britain — all live here. The [Highland Wildlife Park](/europe/unitedkingdom/scotland/cairngorms/highland_wildlife_park) near Kincraig has captive populations of species that have vanished from or never reached the wild Scottish landscape: polar bears, wolves, bison. The [Speyside Whisky Trail](/europe/unitedkingdom/scotland/cairngorms/speyside_whisky) runs through the park's northern edge, where the River Spey and its tributaries provide the pure water that feeds dozens of distilleries.

Winter brings skiing and snowboarding on the [Cairn Gorm ski area](/europe/unitedkingdom/scotland/cairngorms/cairn_gorm) and some of the most reliable snow in Britain — though reliable is relative, and the season runs roughly December to April with variable conditions. Summer is walking and cycling season, with the [Lairig Ghru](/europe/unitedkingdom/scotland/cairngorms/lairig_ghru) mountain pass one of the classic routes through the heart of the plateau.
