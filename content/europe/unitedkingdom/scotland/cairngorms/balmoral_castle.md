Balmoral Castle is the royal family's Scottish estate, bought by Prince Albert and rebuilt in full Scottish Baronial style in the 1850s. The turrets, towers, gardens, and mountain setting are the main draw; public access to the castle itself is limited, usually including the ballroom, exhibitions, and grounds. Check the seasonal opening dates before making the long drive across Deeside.
