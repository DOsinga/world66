title: Things to Do

---

The Cairngorms offer two distinct modes: the high plateau for experienced hill walkers and mountaineers, and the more accessible forest and loch country for everyone else. On the plateau, [Ben Macdui](/europe/unitedkingdom/scotland/cairngorms/ben_macdui) and [Braeriach](/europe/unitedkingdom/scotland/cairngorms/braeriach) are the great summits; the [Lairig Ghru](/europe/unitedkingdom/scotland/cairngorms/lairig_ghru) is the classic through-route. The [Cairngorm Mountain Railway](/europe/unitedkingdom/scotland/cairngorms/cairngorm_mountain_railway) gives access to the [Cairn Gorm](/europe/unitedkingdom/scotland/cairngorms/cairn_gorm) summit area without walking.

In the forest, [Rothiemurchus](/europe/unitedkingdom/scotland/cairngorms/rothiemurchus_forest) has ancient Caledonian pines, red squirrels, and excellent paths; [Loch an Eilein](/europe/unitedkingdom/scotland/cairngorms/loch_an_eilein) has a ruined castle on an island. Wildlife highlights include the [Cairngorm Reindeer Herd](/europe/unitedkingdom/scotland/cairngorms/reindeer_herd) roaming free above the tree line and the [Highland Wildlife Park](/europe/unitedkingdom/scotland/cairngorms/highland_wildlife_park) at Kincraig. The [Speyside Whisky Trail](/europe/unitedkingdom/scotland/cairngorms/speyside_whisky) runs along the park's northern edge. [Aviemore](/europe/unitedkingdom/scotland/cairngorms/aviemore) is the main service hub and railway access point.
