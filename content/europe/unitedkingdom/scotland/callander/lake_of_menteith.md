The Lake of Menteith is famous mostly for not being called a loch, but the name is only the start of its appeal. The low, reedy water has a softer mood than the bigger Trossachs lochs, with birdlife along the shore and small islands sitting close enough to feel reachable.

The main reason to come is Inchmahome Priory, a ruined 13th-century monastery on one of the islands. Mary, Queen of Scots was sheltered here as a child in 1547, and the short boat crossing gives the visit a properly removed feeling.
