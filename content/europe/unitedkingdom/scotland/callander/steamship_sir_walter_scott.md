The steamship Sir Walter Scott has crossed Loch Katrine since 1900, sailing from Trossachs Pier through some of the classic scenery of Loch Lomond and the Trossachs National Park. The appeal is partly the old vessel and partly the slow view of wooded slopes, water, and hills from the deck. Check sailing times before making the trip from Callander.
