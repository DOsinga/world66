The red kite feeding station at Bellymack Hill is a short drive from Castle Douglas and one of the easiest places in Galloway to watch kites close up. Feeding sessions bring the birds low over the fields, with a small hide and viewing area for visitors.

It is a simple stop, but a memorable one if you like wildlife. Time your visit for the feeding rather than turning up at random.
