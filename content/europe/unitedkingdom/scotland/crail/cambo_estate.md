Cambo Estate lies north of Crail near Kingsbarns and is best known for gardens, woodland walks, and seasonal snowdrops. It is a softer inland counterpoint to the harbours and exposed coast.

It works well by car or as part of a longer day around Kingsbarns and the coastal path. Check opening arrangements before going, especially outside the main garden season.
