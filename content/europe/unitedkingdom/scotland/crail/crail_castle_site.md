Crail Castle no longer survives as a dramatic ruin, but the site above the harbour is still worth knowing about. It explains the strategic value of this small coastal settlement and gives another reason to look closely at the headland and harbour below.

Treat it as a historical layer rather than a monument. The reward is the position and the imagination required to place a castle back into the village.
