Crail Harbour is the village's essential sight: a small, sheltered basin below stone houses, with boats, creels, and a tight arrangement of walls and roofs that feels almost staged. It is not large, but it is beautifully proportioned.

Come early or late for the best light and fewer visitors. The harbour is still tied to fishing, so treat it as a working place rather than just a photograph.
