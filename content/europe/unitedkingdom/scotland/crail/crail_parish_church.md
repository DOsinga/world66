Crail Parish Church is the village's main historic church, set slightly away from the harbour bustle. Its age and setting make it a worthwhile pause, especially if you are walking the upper part of the village.

It is not a cathedral-scale sight, and that is the charm. Like much of Crail, it rewards attention to proportion, stonework, and the way old buildings sit naturally in the coastal landscape.
