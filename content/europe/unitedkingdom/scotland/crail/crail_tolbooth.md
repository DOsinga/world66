Crail Tolbooth and Town Hall stands on the main street, with a tower and clock that give the village its civic marker. It is a small building, but in a place as compact as Crail it has real presence.

Notice it on the way between the upper village and the harbour. It helps explain why Crail feels more substantial than a simple fishing hamlet.
