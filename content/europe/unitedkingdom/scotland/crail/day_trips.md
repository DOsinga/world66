Crail fits naturally into a chain of East Neuk villages. [Elie](/europe/unitedkingdom/scotland/eliefife), St Monans, Pittenweem, and Anstruther are close by, while [St Andrews](/europe/unitedkingdom/scotland/standrews) gives the bigger cathedral, university, and golf day out.
