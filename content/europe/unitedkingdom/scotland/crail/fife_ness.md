Fife Ness is the eastern point of the Fife peninsula, a rocky coastal area northeast of Crail. It is a good walking target from the village if you want open sea views and a wilder edge to the East Neuk.

Birdwatchers know the area well, and even casual walkers will notice the shift in mood from village harbour to exposed headland. Take windproof clothing; the North Sea is rarely gentle here.
