Kingsbarns Golf Links is one of the high-profile modern courses on this stretch of Fife coast, sitting north of Crail toward St Andrews. Golfers rate it highly for its sea views and championship conditioning.

Non-golfers will mainly register it as part of the East Neuk's wider golf landscape. If you do play, book well ahead and expect prices that reflect its reputation.
