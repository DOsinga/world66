RNAS Crail is the former Royal Naval Air Station east of the village, a windswept military landscape that feels very different from the harbour. It is one for visitors interested in wartime and Cold War traces rather than polished heritage.

The site is spread out and not a conventional attraction, so check access and behave sensibly around old infrastructure. Its appeal is the contrast: fishing village one moment, hard-edged twentieth-century military history the next.
