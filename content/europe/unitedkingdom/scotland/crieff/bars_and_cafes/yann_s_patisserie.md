---
title: "Yann's Patisserie"
type: poi
---

Yann's Patisserie is a small French bakery and café on Crieff's main street that has developed a loyal following among locals and returning visitors alike. It is the kind of place that feels slightly improbable in a small Scottish market town — proper French pastries, good espresso, and a level of care in the baking that stands out. Croissants, tarts, and seasonal pastries are made on the premises, and the quality is consistent.

The café is compact and fills up quickly on weekend mornings, particularly in summer when tourists are about. The atmosphere is relaxed and the service informal. It does not take bookings; the model is simply to arrive and hope for a table, which usually works out in practice.

Yann's is a genuinely good reason to spend a morning in Crieff town centre rather than heading straight to the distillery or the gardens. It exemplifies the kind of quiet quality that makes Crieff pleasant to visit: nothing flashy, but done with evident care.
