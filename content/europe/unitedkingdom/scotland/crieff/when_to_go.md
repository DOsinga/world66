---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

Crieff and Strathearn are at their most spectacular in late spring and summer, roughly May to September. The countryside is green, the gardens are in full flower, and the long Scottish evenings give plenty of time for walking and exploring. Drummond Castle Gardens are only open during this period. July and August bring the most visitors and the warmest weather, though temperatures rarely exceed the mid-twenties Celsius.

Autumn is excellent for the Knock and the surrounding woodland, where beech trees put on a strong colour display through October. The Glenturret Distillery is open year-round, making it a viable destination in any season. Winter in Crieff is quiet and occasionally bleak — the town sees some snow, the days are short, and many visitor facilities reduce their hours — but it has a certain austere appeal for those who enjoy the Scottish countryside in its quieter moods.

The town hosts a number of agricultural shows and events through the summer, including local games and Highland-themed gatherings that give a flavour of Perthshire life beyond the visitor trail.
