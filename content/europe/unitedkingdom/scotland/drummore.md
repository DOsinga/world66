Drummore is a small village near the southern tip of the Rhins of Galloway in southwest Scotland. It is the southernmost village in Scotland, closer in spirit to sea cliffs, farmland, and lighthouse country than to the urban central belt most visitors know first.

The village is tiny, but it makes a useful base for the Mull of Galloway, Logan Botanic Garden, Port Logan, and the quiet roads around the peninsula. Come with a car or bike, since the best sights are scattered along the coast.

This is not a checklist destination. The point is the edge-of-the-map feeling: seabirds, Irish Sea views, low houses around the harbour, and weather that can change the mood of the place in minutes.
