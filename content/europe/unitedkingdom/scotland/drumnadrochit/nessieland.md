Nessieland is the lighter, more child-focused side of Drumnadrochit's Loch Ness tourism. The attraction leans into the monster legend with themed caves, a castle, a summerhouse, play areas, and a miniature railway.

It is not the place for a sober look at the evidence for Nessie, but it can work well for families who want a playful stop before or after the more serious Loch Ness Centre.
