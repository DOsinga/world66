---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

Loch Ness is accessible year-round and has its own kind of dramatic beauty in winter, when the low light makes the dark water even more atmospheric and Urquhart Castle can be visited in near-solitude. Summer (June to August) is the busiest period and Urquhart Castle can have long queues on fine days — booking ahead online is wise.

The midges — small biting insects — are a serious consideration from late May to September. They are worst in calm, humid conditions and can make outdoor activities uncomfortable. Bring repellent. Late spring (April to May) offers good light, fewer crowds, and the midges have not yet arrived in force; this is arguably the best time to visit the area.
