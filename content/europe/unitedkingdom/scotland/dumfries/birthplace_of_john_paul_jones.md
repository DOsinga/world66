The Birthplace of John Paul Jones is a small cottage museum marking the early life of the 18th-century naval commander. Jones is remembered in the United States as a Revolutionary War hero and in Britain as a far more troublesome figure, which gives the modest site a sharper historical edge.

The museum is a quiet rural stop rather than a major attraction, but it is worthwhile for visitors following maritime history, American Revolutionary War links, or the historic villages south of Dumfries.
