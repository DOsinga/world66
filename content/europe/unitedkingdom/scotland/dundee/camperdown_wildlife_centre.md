Camperdown Wildlife Centre sits within Camperdown Country Park on the edge of Dundee. It is a modest wildlife attraction rather than a major zoo, but for families it adds an easy animal-focused stop to a city better known for design, ships, and industrial history.

The surrounding park is part of the draw, giving space for walks and a slower half-day outside the centre. Check current opening arrangements before going, as smaller animal attractions can change their public access more often than museums.
