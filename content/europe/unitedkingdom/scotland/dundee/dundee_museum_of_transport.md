Dundee Museum of Transport is a compact, volunteer-driven museum that covers the city's motoring, tram, bus, bicycle, and engineering past. The collection is strongest when it connects vehicles to Dundee itself: local routes, local builders, and the way transport shaped everyday life around the Tay.

It sits a little outside the waterfront museum cluster, so it works best for visitors with a specific interest in vehicles or enough time to go beyond the V&A, Discovery, and McManus. The informal feel is part of the appeal, with exhibits that often feel cared for by enthusiasts rather than staged by a large institution.
