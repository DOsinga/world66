Mills Observatory sits in Balgay Park, west of central Dundee, and has the pleasing distinction of being a purpose-built public observatory rather than a university facility hidden away from visitors. Its dome, telescope, and small astronomy displays make it one of the city's better low-key stops, especially for families or anyone with a patient interest in the night sky.

The hilltop setting is part of the charm. Even before you look through a telescope, you get a good view back across Dundee and the Tay. Check opening times before going, since the best visits are tied to public observing sessions and weather is very much in charge.
