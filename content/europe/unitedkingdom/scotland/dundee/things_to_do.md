Dundee's main sights cluster in two zones: the redeveloped waterfront south of the centre, where the V&A and RRS Discovery sit beside the Tay, and the city centre proper around City Square and the Howff cemetery. A short walk uphill takes in a sandstone-volcano viewpoint and an excellent local museum. Use the filter bar to browse by category.
