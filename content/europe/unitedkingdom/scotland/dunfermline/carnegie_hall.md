Carnegie Hall is Dunfermline's main performance venue and one of the civic landmarks tied to the legacy of Andrew Carnegie. Its programme ranges across theatre, music, comedy, and community events, giving the town a cultural stage beyond abbey and palace history.

The building is most rewarding if you can see a performance, but it is still worth noting on a Carnegie-themed walk through town. Check the schedule before assuming daytime access.
