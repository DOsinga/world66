Deep Sea World is an aquarium at North Queensferry, close to the Forth bridges. Its main draw is the long underwater viewing tunnel, where sharks and other large fish pass overhead and beside you.

There are also open and closed tanks with smaller marine life. It is strongest as a family stop or wet-weather option, especially if you are already visiting the bridge viewpoints.
