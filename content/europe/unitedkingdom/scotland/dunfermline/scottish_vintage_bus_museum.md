The Scottish Vintage Bus Museum is a specialist transport museum near Dunfermline, run with the enthusiasm that makes volunteer collections worthwhile. Historic buses are the focus, but the appeal is also in the depot atmosphere, restoration work, and the sense of public transport as social history.

Check opening days before travelling, as museums like this are often event-driven. It is best for families, vehicle enthusiasts, and anyone who enjoys seeing everyday machinery treated with care rather than nostalgia alone.
