One of Edinburgh's most characterful neighbourhood pubs, The Bailie sits on St Stephen Street — the slightly sunken, cobbled lane that was once Stockbridge's bohemian heart. The interior is exactly what a proper Edinburgh pub should be: dark wood, comfortable seating, no slot machines and a decent range of ales and whiskies. Good for an afternoon pint or an evening session; the local clientele keeps it feeling genuinely lived-in rather than styled.
