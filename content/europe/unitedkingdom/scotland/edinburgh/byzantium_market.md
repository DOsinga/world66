Beneath Victoria Street, in the stone vaults that run under the Old Town, Byzantium is a covered market of antiques and collectables dealers. The warren of small stalls sells jewellery, vintage ceramics, old prints, military memorabilia, Art Deco objects, and the general category of things that are hard to describe but easy to recognise as interesting. It is the kind of place where you go in looking for nothing in particular and come out with something you had not known you needed. The setting in the vaults gives it an appropriately atmospheric quality.
