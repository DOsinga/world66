Edinburgh Central Mosque is a modern mosque near the University of Edinburgh and the Old Town, completed in 1998 with a minaret and design influenced by Turkish architecture.

Visitors can usually look inside outside prayer times, making it a worthwhile short stop for architecture and the city's contemporary religious life.
