Edinburgh Central Mosque stands just south of the Old Town, with a clean modern form and a slender minaret that nods to Turkish mosque design. Completed in 1998, it serves the city's Muslim community and is usually visible as you move between the university area and the Meadows.

Visitors can generally look inside outside prayer times. Go quietly and dress with the respect you would bring to any active place of worship.
