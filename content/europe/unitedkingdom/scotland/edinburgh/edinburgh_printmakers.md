Edinburgh Printmakers is a visual arts centre focused on printmaking, with free gallery spaces, a shop, and working studios. Exhibitions are usually contemporary and give a good look at the city's active print and design scene.

It is best for visitors with an interest in art or craft rather than a general sightseeing checklist, but the free galleries make it an easy short stop in the west of the city.
