Note: the Elephant House cafe most associated with J.K. Rowling and the writing of Harry Potter is on George IV Bridge in the Old Town. This is a Morningside neighbourhood cafe — a reliable place for coffee, breakfast, and light lunches that serves the local residential area. The food is honest and well-made: good eggs, fresh sandwiches, decent baking. The absence of tourist traffic means it is a more relaxed environment than the famous original, and the coffee is at least as good.
