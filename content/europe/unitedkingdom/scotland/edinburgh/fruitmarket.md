Fruitmarket is a contemporary art gallery near Edinburgh Waverley station. The building's name recalls its earlier use as a fruit and vegetable market from the 1930s into the 1970s.

It is a central, easy-to-visit gallery for changing exhibitions, especially if you are already moving between the Old Town, Princes Street, and the station.
