The Grange Cricket Club ground at Raeburn Place is Scotland's premier cricket venue and host to international fixtures — Scotland T20I matches have been played here regularly and it is a named venue for the ICC T20 Women's World Cup in 2026. The ground is small, intimate, and flanked by trees, with a traditional pavilion. Even for visitors unfamiliar with cricket it makes for a pleasant few hours on a summer afternoon; tickets for international matches are inexpensive and the atmosphere is relaxed. The ground is a few minutes' walk from Stockbridge village.
