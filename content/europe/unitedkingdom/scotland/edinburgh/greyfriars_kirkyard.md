Greyfriars Kirkyard is one of Edinburgh's great atmospheric spaces — a walled graveyard surrounding the 17th-century Greyfriars Kirk, with the castle visible above the roofline. The graves include those of many notable Scots: architects, lawyers, philosophers, and the Covenanters who were imprisoned here in 1679. The small grave of Greyfriars Bobby, the Skye Terrier who reputedly guarded his master's grave for 14 years, is near the entrance and draws visitors from around the world. The more interesting monuments are deeper in: elaborate carved table tombs, the locked Mortsafe cages that protected fresh graves from anatomists.
