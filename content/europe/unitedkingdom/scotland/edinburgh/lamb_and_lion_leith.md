The Port o' Leith was for generations the pub of Leith's seafarers — international sailors whose ships docked at the nearby port. The interior reflects this: low ceilings, wooden booths, and a remarkable collection of maritime memorabilia including flags, pennants, and photographs of ships donated by visiting crews over the decades. The pub maintains the character of a working docks local even as Leith has gentrified around it; the regulars are a mix of old Leith residents and people who have discovered it. Good beer, no nonsense, a genuine sense of place.
