The Museum of Scottish Fire Heritage is a volunteer-run museum inside McDonald Road fire station. Its displays trace Scottish firefighting from rival commercial fire companies through early wooden fire engines to more recent appliances.

It is a specialist stop, but a worthwhile one if you like working museums and civic history. Check opening times before going, as small volunteer museums can keep limited hours.
