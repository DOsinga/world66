Museum on the Mound is a small museum about money, banking, and coins inside the former Bank of Scotland headquarters. The setting on the Mound makes it easy to combine with the Royal Mile, Princes Street Gardens, and the National Gallery, and the exhibits are especially useful for understanding Scotland's banking history and banknotes.
