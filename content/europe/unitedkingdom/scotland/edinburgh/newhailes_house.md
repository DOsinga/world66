Newhailes House is an 18th-century Palladian mansion on the eastern edge of Edinburgh, little changed in its main rooms and best visited by pre-booked guided tour. The house is interesting for its architecture, but also for the sense of an old estate surviving at the edge of the modern city.

The wooded grounds are free to enter and open year-round, making Newhailes a useful low-key stop if you are exploring Portobello, Musselburgh, or the coast east of the centre.
