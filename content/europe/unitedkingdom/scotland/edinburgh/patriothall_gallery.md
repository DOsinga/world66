Patriothall Gallery is a small contemporary art space tucked into a former co-op bakery in Stockbridge. It combines exhibition rooms with working artists' studios, so the place feels more like a live creative yard than a formal gallery. Exhibitions change through the year, and the studio open weekends are the best time to see the building at its busiest.
