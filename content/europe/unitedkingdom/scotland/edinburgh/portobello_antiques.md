type: poi

Portobello High Street and its side streets have a cluster of antiques dealers, vintage clothing shops, and secondhand furniture stores that makes it one of Edinburgh's best areas for browsing. The dealers range from specialist ceramics collectors to generalist junk shops, with the interesting material usually somewhere in between. The concentration of dealers here reflects Portobello's bohemian streak — it attracted artists and eccentrics as it gentrified, and the antique trade is part of that character. Worth a morning's browsing before lunch at one of the High Street restaurants.
