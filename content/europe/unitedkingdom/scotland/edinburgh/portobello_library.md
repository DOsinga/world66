Portobello Library holds a small collection of historical material about the area's history as Edinburgh's premier seaside resort — photographs of the promenade in its heyday, records of the pottery industry that once made Portobello one of Scotland's main ceramics producers, and material about the amusement park and lido that made it a mass entertainment destination. The library is free to enter and the heritage display is modest but informative. Good for understanding what Portobello was before the current wave of gentrification, and for putting the surviving Victorian architecture in context.
