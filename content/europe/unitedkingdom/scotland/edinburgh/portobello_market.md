Portobello High Street hosts regular market days that mix antiques and vintage goods with local food producers and craft stalls. The antiques element is the most interesting part — Portobello has a number of good secondhand dealers, and market day concentrates this into a manageable stretch. The seaside town character of Portobello gives the market a different feel from Edinburgh's city-centre equivalents: more relaxed, less self-conscious, with the beach a short walk away. Check local listings for market dates as these vary seasonally.
