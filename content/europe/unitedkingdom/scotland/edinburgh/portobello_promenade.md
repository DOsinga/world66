The promenade walk along Portobello beach is one of Edinburgh's underrated pleasures — a wide path above the sandy shoreline, facing east across the Firth of Forth to the hills of Fife, with the volcanic profile of Arthur's Seat visible to the west. At low tide the beach extends to a considerable width. The walk is pleasant in any weather; in winter, a brisk walk followed by coffee in one of the High Street cafes is a good Edinburgh Sunday. The promenade runs for approximately two miles between Seafield in the west and Joppa in the east.
