Real Foods has been Edinburgh's leading wholefood shop for decades, with a Stockbridge branch alongside the original Broughton Street store. The shop carries an extensive range of bulk dry goods — grains, pulses, nuts, dried fruit — alongside organic vegetables, natural health products, and artisan foods. It is the kind of shop that has been doing this since before organic eating became mainstream, and the range and quality reflect that experience. Good for stocking up on provisions or finding Scottish artisan food products that are not widely available in supermarkets.
