Ronnies is a Portobello institution: a relaxed burger and comfort food restaurant on the High Street that has been feeding the neighbourhood for years. The burgers are made with Scottish beef and accompanied by proper chips; there are also fish and chip variations and some vegetarian options. The interior is informal, the queues are sometimes long on weekends, and the prices are honest. In a neighbourhood that has a number of good restaurants, Ronnies remains the most straightforwardly satisfying place for a casual meal after a beach walk.
