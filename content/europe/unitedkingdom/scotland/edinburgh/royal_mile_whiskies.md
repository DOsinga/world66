Royal Mile Whiskies is the most serious whisky retailer in the Old Town: a comprehensive selection of single malts from all regions of Scotland, alongside Irish, Japanese, and American whiskies, and a strong section of independent bottlings from small distilleries not widely available elsewhere. The staff are knowledgeable without being condescending and will guide a visitor through the differences between regional styles. The shop offers dram tasting so you can try before buying; the selection of older and rarer expressions is particularly good.
