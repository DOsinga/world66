The Royal Observatory sits on Blackford Hill, a working astronomy site with a handsome late-Victorian main building and wide views back across Edinburgh. It replaced the old Calton Hill observatory in 1896 and still anchors the University of Edinburgh's Institute for Astronomy.

Public access is limited, but winter visitor evenings usually combine talks with a look around the site. Book ahead, and pair it with a walk over Blackford Hill if the weather is clear.
