The best free museum in Scotland, housed in a Victorian building on Chambers Street with a grand Victorian entrance hall. The galleries cover Scottish history from the geological formation of the country to the present, with remarkable objects including the Lewis Chessmen (well, the Scottish half of them), the Maiden guillotine used in Edinburgh, and Dolly the sheep. The natural history and science galleries are equally good. Allow half a day; the rooftop terrace has a clear view of the Castle.
