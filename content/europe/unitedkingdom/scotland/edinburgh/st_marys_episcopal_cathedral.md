St Mary's Episcopal Cathedral is a large neo-Gothic church west of Edinburgh's New Town. Designed by Sir George Gilbert Scott and completed in the late 19th century, it is the senior Episcopal cathedral in Scotland.

Its spires make it easy to spot from the surrounding streets, and the scale of the interior makes it a worthwhile stop if you are exploring the West End.
