The Stockbridge Sunday Market runs year-round in a cobbled car park off Saunders Street, typically with around 40 stalls. It is one of Edinburgh's best food markets: producers sell directly — farmhouse cheeses, smoked fish, sourdough from artisan bakeries, seasonal vegetables, locally made preserves — alongside ready-to-eat street food. The setting in the village centre, a five-minute walk from the Water of Leith, gives it a local neighbourhood character that distinguishes it from the more tourist-oriented city-centre markets. Arrive by mid-morning; the best stalls sell out. Open Sundays from roughly 10am to 5pm.
