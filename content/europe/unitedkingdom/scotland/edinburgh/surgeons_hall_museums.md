Surgeons' Hall Museums are medical museums run by the Royal College of Surgeons of Edinburgh. The permanent displays cover pathology, the history of surgery, dentistry, and the development of medical teaching in the city.

The collection includes human remains and can be intense, so it is not suitable for young children. For visitors interested in medical history, it is one of Edinburgh's most distinctive specialist museums.
