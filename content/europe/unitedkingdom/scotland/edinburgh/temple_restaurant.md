Cafezique is a neighbourhood cafe and bistro that serves Morningside's working population: good coffee, serious breakfasts, a lunch menu that changes with the season and makes proper use of Scottish ingredients. The atmosphere is relaxed and local — the kind of place where the staff know the regulars by name and the menu reflects what is good that week. Evening opening is limited to certain nights; the daytime offer is the main draw. One of the better neighbourhood restaurants in Edinburgh's southern suburbs, without the pretension that sometimes accompanies good food.
