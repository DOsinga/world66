Victoria Street curves down from George IV Bridge to the Grassmarket in a sweep of colourful Victorian shopfronts — greens, reds, yellows — that make it one of the most photographed streets in Edinburgh. The shops themselves are the interesting part: a map specialist, a whisky retailer, a costume shop, a cheese merchant, independent booksellers. The upper level of the street has a pedestrian terrace looking down over the roofline. Worth walking slowly in both directions; the view from the bottom looking back up the curve is especially good.
