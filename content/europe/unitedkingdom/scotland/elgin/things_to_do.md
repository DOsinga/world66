---
title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Elgin's headline attraction is its ruined cathedral, but there is enough else to fill a day. [Elgin Museum](/europe/unitedkingdom/scotland/elgin/elgin_museum) is the oldest independent museum in Scotland, with a strong collection of Pictish stones and Permian fossils. [Gordon & MacPhail](/europe/unitedkingdom/scotland/elgin/gordon_and_macphail) on South Street is one of the great whisky shops in the world — hundreds of single malt bottlings, including rare expressions from long-closed distilleries. [Johnstons of Elgin](/europe/unitedkingdom/scotland/elgin/johnstons_of_elgin) has been weaving cashmere on the edge of town since 1797; the mill tour is worth an hour. [Pluscarden Abbey](/europe/unitedkingdom/scotland/elgin/pluscarden_abbey), six miles southwest in a wooded valley, is a medieval monastery rebuilt by monks who returned to a ruin in 1948 and have been working at it ever since. The [Speyside Whisky Trail](/europe/unitedkingdom/scotland/elgin/speyside_whisky_trail) puts over 50 distilleries within easy driving distance.
