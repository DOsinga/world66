---
title: "Bars and Cafes"
type: section
---

The Ship Inn is the obvious centre of village social life. For coffee and cake, **Sugar Mountain** on the High Street is the most reliable — proper coffee, decent home baking, and a sunny pavement terrace in summer. Out on the road to Anstruther, **The Toll** is a smart pub-restaurant that has built a name on local seafood and bar suppers.
