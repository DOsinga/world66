A short, square white lighthouse at the eastern tip of the bay, built in 1908 by the Stevenson family (the Scottish lighthouse engineers — Robert Louis Stevenson's grandfather and uncles, who designed most of Scotland's coastal lights). It is still operational but unmanned and modest in scale — really just a stout pillar with a lantern — though the position on the rocky headland makes for a fine short walk from the village and a good place to watch evening light coming in over the Forth.
