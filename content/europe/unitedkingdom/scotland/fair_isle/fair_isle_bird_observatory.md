Fair Isle Bird Observatory is the island's great institution, tracking the birds that make landfall here between the Atlantic and the North Sea. Even if you are not a specialist, the daily rhythm of sightings, ringing, and weather watching gives the island a sense of purpose.

The observatory was rebuilt after a major fire in 2019 and reopened as the main place to stay and learn about the island's birdlife. Check ahead, because beds and meals are limited.
