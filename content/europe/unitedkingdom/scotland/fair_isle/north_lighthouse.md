North Lighthouse stands at the island's northeast tip, where moorland ends in cliffs, stacks, arches, and rough water. The lighthouse itself is automated, but the walk out gives fine coastal views and a good chance of seeing seabirds riding the wind.

It pairs well with Ward Hill if you want a longer circuit. Weather changes quickly, so treat even short island walks with respect.
