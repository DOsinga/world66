---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

Falkirk is a year-round destination for its main engineering landmarks — the Falkirk Wheel and the Kelpies both operate regardless of weather, and the visitor centres at both sites provide cover if the Scottish skies open. Summer (June to August) gives the longest days and best chances of decent weather, but expect some rain at any time of year.

The Helix park around the Kelpies hosts events throughout the year and is at its best on a clear autumn or spring day when the light catches the steel sculptures. The Callendar Park is particularly pleasant in autumn when the trees are in colour. If you are visiting Rough Castle Roman Fort, wear sturdy shoes as the ground around the earthworks can be muddy.
