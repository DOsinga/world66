Gordon Chapel is a Scottish Episcopalian church built in the 1830s for the Gordon Castle estate. Where the established Kirk served the village at large, this chapel served the ducal household and the estate's Anglican workers. The building is modest on the outside but contains some fine Victorian furnishings and stained glass added in later decades.

It remains an active congregation and is open for services. The chapel sits on the northern side of the village, a short walk from the main square, and is worth noting as part of the Georgian and Victorian ensemble that gives Fochabers its architectural coherence.
