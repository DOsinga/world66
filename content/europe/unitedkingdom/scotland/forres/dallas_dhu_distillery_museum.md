Dallas Dhu Historic Distillery is a strong stop for travellers using Forres as a base for Moray and Speyside. The distillery no longer produces whisky, which is exactly what makes it useful: the buildings, equipment, mash tun, still house, and bonded warehouse can be read as an industrial time capsule rather than a sales-led tasting room.

The site explains the practical stages of malt whisky production and the role distilleries played in the economy of northeast Scotland. It pairs well with Forres, Brodie Castle, or a broader whisky-country drive, especially for visitors who want heritage context before visiting working distilleries.
