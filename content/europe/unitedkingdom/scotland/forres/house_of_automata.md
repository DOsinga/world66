House of Automata is a small, specialised Forres attraction devoted to mechanical figures and antique automata. It sits somewhere between museum, workshop, and cabinet of curiosities, with delicate moving objects that reward close attention rather than a fast browse.

The appeal is craft: gears, cams, painted faces, and the old pleasure of making a figure come alive through mechanics. It is a niche stop, but a memorable one, especially if you are already in Moray for distilleries, castles, and small-town wandering.
