---
title: When to Go
type: section
---

May and June give the best chance of clear weather on the Ben and the longest evenings — sunset in mid-June is after 10 pm, and twilight lingers until almost midnight. July and August are the warmest but also the wettest and the busiest, with midges making outdoor evenings tiresome. September is a fine month if the weather holds. Winter offers the spectacular snow on Ben Nevis but short, often vicious days; reserve it for experienced walkers and skiers.
