---
title: Things to Do
type: section
---

Foyers is a stop, not a destination. The Falls of Foyers are the obvious draw — a short woodland walk to two waterfalls dropping into a gorge above Loch Ness. Beyond the falls, the quiet south-shore road, the spooky outline of Boleskine House, and the hilly hinterland of Stratherrick reward anyone with time to wander.
