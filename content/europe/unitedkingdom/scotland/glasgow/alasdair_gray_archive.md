The archive of the Glasgow artist and novelist Alasdair Gray, at the Whisky Bond by the canal, with his own furniture and papers. His murals are elsewhere in the city: at Òran Mór, Hillhead subway station and the Ubiquitous Chip. The pin is approximate; open by arrangement.
