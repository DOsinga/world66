Crookston Castle is a compact medieval ruin in southwest Glasgow, standing on a grassy mound away from the city's better-known Victorian and industrial sights. Its remaining stonework hints at the fortified residences that once ringed the Clyde valley.

It is a selective stop, best for castle completists or travellers exploring Glasgow's outer districts. The reward is a quiet historical fragment rather than a fully interpreted attraction.
