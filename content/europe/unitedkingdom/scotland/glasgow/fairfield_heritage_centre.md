Fairfield Heritage Centre is a focused introduction to Glasgow's shipbuilding history, housed in the former Fairfield Shipbuilding and Engineering Company offices in Govan. It explains how the Clyde became one of the world's great industrial waterways and how shipyards shaped working life on the south bank.

The centre is especially useful if you are visiting Govan Old Parish Church, the Riverside Museum, or other Clyde-side sites and want the industrial story filled in. It is smaller than Glasgow's major museums, but its location and subject make it more than a generic local-history room.
