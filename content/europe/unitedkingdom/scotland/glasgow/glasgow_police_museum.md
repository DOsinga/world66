Glasgow Police Museum tells the story of the city's police force, which dates back to 1779 and is often described as the first of its kind in the world. Displays cover famous cases, police equipment, uniforms, and the development of policing in Glasgow.

The museum also includes material on policing around the world. It is a small, specialist stop, but a worthwhile one for visitors interested in crime history or civic Glasgow.
