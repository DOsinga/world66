Glasgow Royal Concert Hall sits at the busy north end of Buchanan Street, where the shopping crowds meet one of the city's main music venues. It hosts orchestral concerts, folk festivals, jazz, talks, and touring performers, with the Royal Scottish National Orchestra among its regular users.

It is not Glasgow's oldest or most atmospheric venue, but it is one of the easiest to use as a visitor: central, well served by public transport, and surrounded by places to eat before or after a show. Check listings if you want a cultural evening without leaving the city centre.
