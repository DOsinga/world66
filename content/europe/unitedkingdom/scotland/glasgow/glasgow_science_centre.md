Glasgow Science Centre is a major family attraction on the Clyde, with hundreds of interactive science exhibits, an IMAX cinema, and Glasgow Tower. The tower rises 125 metres and is designed to rotate from its base, giving wide views across the city when open. It is one of the strongest indoor options in Glasgow for visitors with children.
