House for an Art Lover brings a Charles Rennie Mackintosh design to life in Bellahouston Park, based on drawings he made with Margaret Macdonald for a 1901 competition. The result is part architectural homage, part gallery, and part Glasgow design pilgrimage.

It is not as central as the city's big museums, but Mackintosh admirers should make the trip. The interiors, decorative details, cafe, and park setting make it a gentler visit than many urban architecture stops.
