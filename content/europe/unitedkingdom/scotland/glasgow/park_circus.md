Park Circus is one of Glasgow's most elegant residential set pieces, a sweep of Victorian terraces and crescents above Kelvingrove Park. The area shows the city's 19th-century confidence at domestic scale: stone façades, careful curves, and a hilltop position that turns architecture into theatre.

It is best seen on foot as part of a West End walk linking Kelvingrove, the university, and the park. There is no ticketed attraction here, just urban form done well. If you like cities, that is enough.
