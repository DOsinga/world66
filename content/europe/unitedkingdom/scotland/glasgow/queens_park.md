Queen's Park is the green heart of Glasgow's South Side, laid out on a hill with paths, lawns, sports areas, and one of the city's best everyday viewpoints. From the flagpole area, the view takes in central Glasgow and, on clear days, hills beyond the city.

The park also anchors a lively surrounding neighborhood of cafes, tenements, and independent shops. It is a good choice when you want Glasgow beyond the standard West End and city-centre circuit.
