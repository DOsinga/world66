The Riverside Museum is Glasgow's transport and technology museum, housed in a spectacular building by Zaha Hadid Architects that won the European Museum of the Year Award in 2013. The collection covers Glasgow's transport history in full: locomotives, trams, cars, motorcycles, bicycles, and a remarkable recreated Glasgow street scene of the 1890s–1930s with original shopfronts, a pub, a subway station, and a pawnbroker all assembled under one roof.

Outside on the river, the tall ship Glenlee — a three-masted barque built in Glasgow in 1896 — is moored as a floating exhibit. The Riverside Museum sits on the north bank of the Clyde at the point where several of the city's major shipyards once stood; the museum is partly an act of collective memory for a city that defined itself by what it built on this river. Free admission.
