Scotland Street School was Charles Rennie Mackintosh's last major building, designed in 1906 for a working-class school in the Gorbals. Even when exhibitions are closed or changing, the exterior is worth a look for its bold towers, tall windows, and unmistakable Mackintosh geometry.
