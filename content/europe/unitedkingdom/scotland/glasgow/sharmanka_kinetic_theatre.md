Sharmanka Kinetic Theatre is one of Glasgow's strangest small cultural stops: a darkened room of hand-built machines where carved figures, scrap metal, lights, and music move through short theatrical sequences. It is part sculpture, part performance, part folk-art cabinet of curiosities.

Book ahead if you can, because the visit is usually tied to timed shows rather than open wandering. It is especially good for travellers who have already seen Glasgow's big museums and want something more idiosyncratic, handmade, and hard to classify.
