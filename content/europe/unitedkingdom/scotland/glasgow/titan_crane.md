The Titan Crane is one of the great industrial landmarks of the Clyde, a huge cantilever crane from Clydebank's shipbuilding era. It stands as a reminder that the Glasgow area once built some of the world's largest and most famous ships, with engineering on a scale that still feels bold today.

When open to visitors, the lift to the top gives wide views over the Clyde and the former shipyard landscape. It is a strong stop for anyone interested in industrial heritage, engineering, or the working history behind greater Glasgow's waterfront revival.
