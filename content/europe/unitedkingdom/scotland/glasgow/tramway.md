Tramway is a major contemporary arts venue in a converted tram depot on Glasgow's South Side. It is known for large-scale visual art, performance, dance and experimental work that does not always fit the city's more traditional museums and theatres.

The venue is schedule-driven, so check exhibitions and performances before crossing town. When the programming lines up, Tramway is one of the best places to see Glasgow's current arts scene rather than its Victorian or industrial past.
