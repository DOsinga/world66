The Tron Theatre is one of Glasgow's livelier small performance venues, set in the former Tron Kirk
in the Merchant City. It stages new writing, touring theatre, comedy, and seasonal productions in a
setting that keeps the audience close to the work.

Check the programme rather than turning up speculatively, since the experience depends entirely on
what is on. When the listing suits you, the Tron is a good way to see Glasgow's current creative
scene after a day spent with museums, murals, and Victorian architecture.
