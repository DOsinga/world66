Eilean Munde is the small burial island in Loch Leven, visible from the Glencoe area and tied to the history of the MacDonalds, Camerons, and Stewarts. Its low profile is part of the appeal: a quiet place in a landscape better known for towering ridges and violent history.

Most visitors will see it from the shore rather than land on it. That is enough to make it meaningful, especially if you are tracing Glencoe's clan stories and want a human-scale counterpoint to the mountains.
