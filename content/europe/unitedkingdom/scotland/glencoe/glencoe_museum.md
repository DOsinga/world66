Glencoe Museum is a small local museum in traditional thatched cottages near the heart of the
village. Its displays cover the glen's geology, crofting life, mountaineering, clan history, and the
1692 massacre that still shapes how many visitors read the landscape.

It is not large, but it adds human detail to a place many people experience only through car windows
and viewpoints. Stop in before or after walking in the glen; the objects and stories make the
surrounding mountains feel less anonymous.
