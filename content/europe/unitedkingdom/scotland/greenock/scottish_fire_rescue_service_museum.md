The Scottish Fire and Rescue Service Museum is a compact specialist museum in Greenock, with historic appliances, uniforms, equipment, and stories from Scotland's firefighting services. It is the kind of place where volunteer knowledge can make the visit.

It is not a headline west-coast sight, but it is a worthwhile local museum if you are in Greenock or interested in emergency-service history. Check opening times before going, as small museums can have limited hours.
