type: section
---

Greenock is not a classic tourist destination, but it has more of interest than its reputation suggests. The [McLean Museum and Art Gallery](/europe/unitedkingdom/scotland/greenock/mclean_museum) anchors the cultural scene, covering both James Watt and the broader history of Clyde shipbuilding. The [Watt Library](/europe/unitedkingdom/scotland/greenock/watt_library), founded in 1783, is one of the oldest subscription libraries in Britain. The [James Watt Dock Crane](/europe/unitedkingdom/scotland/greenock/james_watt_dock_crane), a giant cantilever crane by the waterfront, is a striking piece of industrial heritage. The [Free French Memorial](/europe/unitedkingdom/scotland/greenock/free_french_memorial) in Lyle Hill cemetery stands as one of Scotland's more moving Second World War memorials, with extraordinary views across the Firth of Clyde.
