---
title: Things to Do
type: section
---

Helmsdale's strongest sights are compact but memorable. Start at [Timespan](/europe/unitedkingdom/scotland/helmsdale/timespan) for the village's fishing, Clearance and gold-rush history, then climb to the [Emigrants Statue](/europe/unitedkingdom/scotland/helmsdale/emigrants_statue) for the view back over the river and harbour.

With a car, continue north along the A9 to [Badbea](/europe/unitedkingdom/scotland/helmsdale/badbea), one of the most affecting Clearance village sites in Scotland, and [Ousdale Broch](/europe/unitedkingdom/scotland/helmsdale/ousdale_broch), a restored Iron Age tower reached by a short but steep path.
