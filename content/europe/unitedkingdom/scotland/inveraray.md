Inveraray is a small town on Loch Fyne with a carefully staged look. The Duke of Argyll rebuilt it in the late 18th century, leaving a planned town of whitewashed buildings, a church on a traffic island, and a castle that still dominates the view.

The big stops are unusually strong for a town this small. [Inveraray Castle](/europe/unitedkingdom/scotland/inveraray/inveraray_castle) gives the place its aristocratic scale, while [Inveraray Jail](/europe/unitedkingdom/scotland/inveraray/inveraray_jail) turns the old courthouse and prison into a vivid museum. Above town, the [Dun na Cuaich Watchtower](/europe/unitedkingdom/scotland/inveraray/dun_na_cuaich_watchtower) gives the best view back over Loch Fyne.

Inveraray works well as a day stop between Glasgow, Oban, and the western peninsulas. Add [Auchindrain Historic Township](/europe/unitedkingdom/scotland/inveraray/auchindrain_historic_township) or [Crarae Garden](/europe/unitedkingdom/scotland/inveraray/crarae_garden) if you have a car and want the day to stretch beyond the town centre.
