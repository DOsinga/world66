The Bell Tower belongs to All Saints Church and stands in the middle of Inveraray's planned town centre. It is a smaller stop than the castle or jail, but it adds to the town's odd, deliberate layout.

When open in summer, it is worth a short look for the bells, the church setting, and another angle on the streets around the loch.
