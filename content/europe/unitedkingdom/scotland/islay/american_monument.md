The Oa is the rocky peninsula southwest of Port Ellen, now an RSPB reserve where you might see choughs, golden eagles and hen harriers. The road ends at a car park, and a path of about a mile leads to the American Monument, a stone tower built on the cliffs in 1920.

It commemorates two disasters of 1918: the troopship Tuscania, torpedoed by a German submarine off the coast in February, and the Otranto, wrecked after a collision in a storm in October. Both ships were carrying American troops, and hundreds died. The monument was put up by the American Red Cross; the dead of the Otranto were buried in a military cemetery at Kilchoman on the west coast, though almost all the Americans were later taken home. On a clear day you look straight across to Ireland.
