Bowmore's main street runs straight uphill from the harbour to this white, circular church, built in 1767. Inside, the roof rests on a single massive central pillar. It's the most recognisable building on Islay and a simple, light-filled space worth a few minutes.

The church also holds items belonging to the Bowmore-born Rev Donald Caskie, minister of the Scots Kirk in Paris during the Second World War, who as the "Tartan Pimpernel" helped some two thousand Allied servicemen escape occupied France.
