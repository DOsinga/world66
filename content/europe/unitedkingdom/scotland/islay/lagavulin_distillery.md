Lagavulin — from the Gaelic for "hollow of the mill" — has distilled on this little bay since 1816 and belongs to Diageo. Its standard 16-year-old is one of the benchmark peated malts, rich and smoky rather than sharp.

The setting is the best of the three Kildalton distilleries. On the rocks at the mouth of the bay stand the ruins of Dunyvaig Castle, once the naval base of the Lords of the Isles, demolished by the Campbells in 1677. Ardbeg, the third distillery, is a mile further east along the road and the footpath.
