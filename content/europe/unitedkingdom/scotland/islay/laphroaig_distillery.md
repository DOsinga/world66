Laphroaig sits at the head of a small bay just east of Port Ellen, its white buildings and black lettering right on the water. Founded in 1815, it makes one of the most uncompromising whiskies anywhere: peat smoke, iodine and seaweed. It's now owned by Suntory.

It's the first of the three Kildalton distilleries strung along the south coast — Laphroaig, Lagavulin and Ardbeg — linked to Port Ellen by a footpath, about four miles each way. Walking it means nobody has to drive after the tastings. Book tours ahead in summer and during the May festival.
