Islay's winter spectacle is its geese. Barnacle geese arrive from Greenland in their tens of thousands — numbers have topped 35,000 — along with up to 12,000 Greenland white-fronted geese, and the flats at the head of Loch Gruinart are where many of them feed and roost.

The RSPB visitor centre has views over the flats, and two short trails (a woodland walk and a moorland walk) start nearby. Come in October to see the geese arrive, or in spring and summer for corncrakes, hen harriers and otters. The reserve is always open; donations are welcome.
