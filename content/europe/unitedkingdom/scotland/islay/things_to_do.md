---
title: Things to Do
type: section
---

Whisky is the obvious draw — the three peat-smoked distilleries on the south coast are an easy walk from Port Ellen — but Islay rewards a few days. See the 8th-century Kildalton Cross, the island seat of the Lords of the Isles at Finlaggan, the clifftop American Monument on the Oa, and in winter the great flocks of Arctic geese at Loch Gruinart.
