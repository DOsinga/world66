---
title: Eating Out
type: section
---

Options are short and largely centred on [Arinagour](/europe/unitedkingdom/scotland/isle_of_coll/arinagour). The **Coll Hotel** has the only proper restaurant on the island and is justly known for its seafood — Coll crab, langoustines straight from the boats, hand-dived scallops. Sit in the conservatory at sunset for the view across the bay.

The **Island Cafe** opens for daytime food (soups, sandwiches, home-baked cakes) and the village shop sells the basics for self-catering. **First Port of Coll** down by the pier doubles as a takeaway and bunkhouse cafe. Outside the village there is nowhere to eat at all, so stock up before you cycle out.
