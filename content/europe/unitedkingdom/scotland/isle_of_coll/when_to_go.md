---
title: When to Go
type: section
---

May and June are the prime months. Daylight stretches past 10pm, the corncrakes are calling, the machair is in flower with thrift, orchids and bird's-foot trefoil, and midges are not yet a serious problem. Late May into early June is the sweet spot.

July and August give the warmest weather and the highest chance of swimming, but accommodation is fully booked months ahead and the midges arrive in earnest on calm evenings. September is quieter, with the chance of late summer sun and good sea-watching for migrating cetaceans.

The autumn and winter months are for dark-sky enthusiasts and walkers who do not mind weather. The Coll Hotel and the Bunkhouse stay open year-round, but most self-catering cottages close from late October to Easter. Storms can cancel ferries for days at a time — build slack into your dates.
