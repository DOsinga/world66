Cessford Castle is a substantial ruin in the Borders countryside, associated with the Kerr family and the turbulent world of the Border Reivers. The remaining masonry still gives a strong impression of a fortified residence built for a contested frontier.

It is a specialist stop rather than a polished attraction, best for travelers already exploring the Jedburgh and Kelso area by car. The appeal is the scale of the ruin and its historical setting, not visitor infrastructure.
