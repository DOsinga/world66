The cafes in Jedburgh's town centre serve the standard Scottish cafe repertoire: light lunches, sandwiches, soup, and baking — scones, shortbread, tablet. The quality is solid, the portions generous, and the pace relaxed. Useful for a mid-morning break or a quick lunch between the abbey and the Mary Queen of Scots House.
