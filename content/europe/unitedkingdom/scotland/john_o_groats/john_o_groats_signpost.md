The John o'Groats signpost marks the symbolic end of the road from Land's End, even if geography is more complicated than the photographs admit. The modern sign is free to use and has become the ritual shot for cyclists, walkers, charity drivers, and anyone who likes a map-end moment.

It is quick, silly, and worth doing once. Then go to the cliffs.
