---
title: Things to Do
type: section
---

Jura is for walkers, deer-watchers and people who like empty places. Climb the Paps if the weather allows, visit the distillery in Craighouse, and make the long trip north to Orwell's Barnhill and the whirlpool of Corryvreckan. Boat trips from local harbours are the easy way to see the Corryvreckan at full tide.
