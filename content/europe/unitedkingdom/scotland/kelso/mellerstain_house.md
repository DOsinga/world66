Mellerstain House is one of the major country-house visits near Kelso, notable for its 18th-century
architecture and Robert Adam interiors. The house sits in the Borders landscape with formal
terraces, parkland, and long views that explain why estates around Kelso became such powerful
symbols of landed Scotland.

The visit is strongest for travellers interested in architecture and garden design. Floors Castle is
bigger and closer to Kelso, but Mellerstain feels more composed and less crowded, making it a good
second estate for a Borders itinerary that already includes abbeys, towers, and river towns.
