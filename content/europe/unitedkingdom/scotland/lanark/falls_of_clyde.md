The Falls of Clyde are a chain of waterfalls and wooded riverside walks upstream from New Lanark. The path follows the river through the Clyde Valley Woodlands National Nature Reserve, with the best views after rain when the water is high and the sandstone gorge feels properly alive.

The walk is the thing. You can do a short out-and-back from the mill village or keep going through the reserve if you have the legs. Look for dippers on the rocks and listen for the river before you see the next fall.
