Beecraigs Country Park is the main outdoor escape above Linlithgow, with woodland trails, open ground, a loch, and family facilities spread across a large area. It is a good place to trade palace stone and town streets for mud, trees, and views.

The park works for short walks, cycling, picnics, and longer family outings. A car helps, but the reward is space: it feels properly rural while still belonging to the Linlithgow orbit.
