---
title: Things to Do
type: section
---

The park splits naturally in two. Around Loch Lomond, climb Ben Lomond or Conic Hill, take the ferry to Inchcailloch, and walk the grounds at Balloch. In the Trossachs, climb Ben A'an and sail Loch Katrine on the old steamer from Trossachs Pier, using Callander or Aberfoyle as a base.
