Most of Loch Ness's headline sights belong to the villages around it: [Urquhart Castle](/europe/unitedkingdom/scotland/drumnadrochit/urquhart_castle) and the [Loch Ness Centre](/europe/unitedkingdom/scotland/drumnadrochit/loch_ness_centre) at Drumnadrochit, and the [Falls of Foyers](/europe/unitedkingdom/scotland/foyers_loch_ness/falls_of_foyers) on the quiet south shore. The places below sit along the loch between them. A boat trip from Inverness, Drumnadrochit or Fort Augustus is the best way to understand the scale of the water.
