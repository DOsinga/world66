The Land, Sea and Islands Centre in Arisaig is a small local museum covering the area's social history, natural history, and island connections. It is especially useful if you are travelling the coast between Mallaig, Morar, and the Small Isles and want more context than the scenery alone gives you.

Displays include material on local communities, wildlife, and the special operations agents who trained or worked in the area during the Second World War.
