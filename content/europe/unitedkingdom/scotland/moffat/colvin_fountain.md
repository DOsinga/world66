The Colvin Fountain stands in the middle of Moffat's broad High Street. Local businessman William Colvin presented it to the town in 1875, and on top is a ram by the sculptor William Brodie, a reminder that Moffat grew rich as a wool market. The ram is the town's emblem, and the local rugby team are called the Rams.

It takes a minute to see, but you'll pass it anyway. The Moffat Toffee Shop and the narrow Star Hotel are close by.
