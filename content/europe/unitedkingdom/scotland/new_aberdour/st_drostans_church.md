St Drostan's Church stands at the head of the road down to the beach, a modest but ancient building marking one of the earliest Christian sites in northern Scotland. The current structure is largely medieval, but the site has been a place of worship for well over a thousand years.

Inside, an ancient stone baptismal font is the most striking object — rough, worn smooth by centuries of use, and reputedly dating to the foundation of the original church. The churchyard has headstones spanning several centuries. The church is not always open, but the exterior and churchyard are freely accessible.
