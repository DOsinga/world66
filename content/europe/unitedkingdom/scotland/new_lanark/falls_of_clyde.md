The Falls of Clyde are a chain of waterfalls upstream from New Lanark. A short walk follows the river into a steep wooded gorge, where the falls are mixed with mill history, hydroelectric works, and old viewpoints.

Corra Linn is the highest drop, and the valley is also known for wildlife including peregrine falcons and otters. Water levels vary, but the walk is worthwhile even when the falls are not at full force.
