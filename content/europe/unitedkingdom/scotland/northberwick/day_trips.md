---
title: "Day Trips"
type: section
---

North Berwick itself is a day trip from Edinburgh, but the surrounding East Lothian coast deserves more exploration. **Tantallon Castle** and the **Bass Rock** are the essential local excursions. The walled garden village of **Dirleton**, four kilometres west, has an elegant 12th-century castle and one of the best village greens in Scotland. **Gullane** is a few minutes further west, with the famous Muirfield golf course and the long sandy beach. **Dunbar**, 25 km east along the coast, is the birthplace of John Muir and has a busy fishing harbour. For walkers, the **John Muir Way** long-distance footpath runs through North Berwick toward Edinburgh in one direction and Dunbar in the other.
