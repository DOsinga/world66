Eyebroughy is a small island off the East Lothian coast, less famous than Bass Rock or Fidra but part of the same bird-rich seascape that makes North Berwick special. At low tide it can look temptingly close, but the point is the coast and wildlife rather than casual island-bagging.

Treat Eyebroughy as a sight from the shore unless you have proper local tide knowledge. It pairs well with Yellowcraig and the beaches west of North Berwick, especially if you are already looking for quieter coastal views beyond the harbour.
