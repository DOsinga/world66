---
title: "Getting There"
type: section
---

**By train** is by far the best option. ScotRail runs trains from Edinburgh Waverley to North Berwick about every half-hour during the day, taking 32 minutes. The line is a dead-end branch that terminates in the town, a five-minute walk from the High Street.

**By car**, take the A1 east from Edinburgh and turn off at the North Berwick junction onto the A198, about 35 minutes. Town parking is paid in summer but plentiful.

**By bus**, Lothian Country and East Coast Buses run from Edinburgh's St Andrew Square; the journey takes about 75 minutes and is cheaper than the train.

Edinburgh Airport is about 50 km away; transfer via Edinburgh Waverley by tram (or taxi) and connect to the train.
