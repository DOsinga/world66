The National Museum of Flight, at East Fortune Airfield near North Berwick, is one of Scotland's strongest specialist museums. Its hangars hold military, civil, and experimental aircraft, with Concorde usually the headline exhibit for visitors who are not already aviation enthusiasts.

The airfield setting matters: wartime buildings, open space, and large aircraft make the museum feel physical rather than abstract. It is a proper half-day trip from North Berwick or Edinburgh, especially for families and anyone interested in engineering history.
