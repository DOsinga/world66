A conical hill of volcanic basalt rising 187 metres straight out of the East Lothian plain, the Law is the landmark visible from miles away. The climb from the south edge of town takes about 30 minutes on a clear path and is the most rewarding short walk in the area. The summit gives a 360-degree view: the Firth of Forth and Fife coast to the north, the Bass Rock and Tantallon to the east, the Pentland Hills and Edinburgh to the west.

A replica whale jawbone arch stands at the top, replacing a sequence of real jawbones that have framed the view since the 18th century. The walk down the north side passes through gorse and back into town near the harbour.
