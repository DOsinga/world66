A small specialist coffee roaster on the High Street, Steampunk roasts on the premises and the smell is the first thing that hits you walking in. The flat whites are excellent, the cakes change daily, and the breakfast (smashed avocado, eggs Benedict, sourdough toast) is reliable.

The interior is one long room with a few tables and a counter. Queues at weekends; takeaway is faster.
