title: Things to Do
type: section
---

Oban's main draw is its position: a compact, highly functional harbor town that serves as the gateway to the Inner Hebrides. McCaig's Tower on the hill above town, the Oban Distillery in the town centre, and the seafood pier are the immediate highlights. The ferry terminal puts Mull, Lismore, Colonsay, Coll, Tiree, and the island chain within easy reach.
