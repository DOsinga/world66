Orkney is an archipelago of about 70 islands off the northern tip of [Scotland](/europe/unitedkingdom/scotland), and it is one of the most remarkable places in Britain. People have lived here for at least 5,000 years, and the evidence is everywhere: [Skara Brae](/europe/unitedkingdom/scotland/orkney/skara_brae), a Neolithic village older than the pyramids, sits on the shore of the Bay of Skaill with its stone beds and dressers still intact. The [Ring of Brodgar](/europe/unitedkingdom/scotland/orkney/ring_of_brodgar) and the [Stones of Stenness](/europe/unitedkingdom/scotland/orkney/stones_of_stenness) stand in the landscape as they have for millennia. [Maeshowe](/europe/unitedkingdom/scotland/orkney/maeshowe), a chambered cairn, was broken into by Vikings who left runic graffiti on the walls.

The islands are green, treeless, and wind-scoured, with big skies and long summer evenings. The Mainland is the largest island and holds the capital, Kirkwall, with its red sandstone [St. Magnus Cathedral](/europe/unitedkingdom/scotland/orkney/st_magnus_cathedral) — one of the finest medieval buildings in Scotland. [Stromness](/europe/unitedkingdom/scotland/orkney/stromness), the other main town, is a narrow street of stone houses pressed against the harbour, with the sea visible down every alley.

Beyond the archaeology and the towns, Orkney is a place of seabird cliffs, seals, and empty beaches. [Hoy](/europe/unitedkingdom/scotland/orkney/hoy) has the [Old Man](/europe/unitedkingdom/scotland/orkney/old_man_of_hoy) — a 137-metre sea stack. The smaller islands ([Rousay](/europe/unitedkingdom/scotland/orkney/rousay), [Westray](/europe/unitedkingdom/scotland/orkney/westray), [Papa Westray](/europe/unitedkingdom/scotland/orkney/papa_westray), [Sanday](/europe/unitedkingdom/scotland/orkney/sanday)) are quieter still. The ferry from Scrabster or the plane from [Edinburgh](/europe/unitedkingdom/scotland/edinburgh) or [Glasgow](/europe/unitedkingdom/scotland/glasgow) gets you here. Once arrived, you will want to stay longer than you planned.
