Blackhammer Chambered Cairn is a Neolithic stalled cairn on Rousay, with seven stone compartments arranged along a narrow central passage. The concrete roof is modern, but the burial chamber is still a worthwhile stop for visitors following Rousay's cluster of prehistoric sites.
