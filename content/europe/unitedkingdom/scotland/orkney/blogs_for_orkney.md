Orkney is small enough that its writers know it parish by parish. The islands' best-known storyteller takes you to the quiet inner isles, residents write up their own walks in the community news site, and the islands' own board picks the beaches you will have to yourself.

Between them: Scotland's earliest medieval castle on Wyre, a round-towered kirk on Egilsay, a wood in a treeless county, and sand you can walk between the Churchill Barriers.
