A string of four wartime causeways between the mainland and the South Isles, the Churchill Barriers were completed in 1944 to seal the eastern approaches to Scapa Flow against U-boat attack. They incorporated several wrecks of older "block ships" deliberately scuttled in the channels — some of these wrecks are still visible at low tide, rusting hulks protruding from the water.

Driving the barriers is essentially the only road south to the Italian Chapel, Burwick, and South Ronaldsay. Park at the first barrier and walk down to look at the block ships and the wartime concrete and rebar that built it. The beach beside Barrier 1, with its calm lagoon on one side and Atlantic surf on the other, is one of Orkney's most striking spots.
