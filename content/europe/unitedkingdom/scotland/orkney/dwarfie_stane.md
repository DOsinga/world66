Unique in northern Europe, the Dwarfie Stane is a Neolithic tomb cut into a single enormous block of red sandstone in a glacial valley on Hoy. The chamber inside is just large enough for one or two people to crawl into. A massive plug-stone, now lying beside the entrance, originally sealed it.

Reaching the Stane involves a 15-minute walk from a small car park on the road between Quoys and Rackwick. The path crosses moorland and can be muddy — boots are recommended. The setting, between the high cliffs of Ward Hill and the open valley running down to Rackwick Bay, is as atmospheric as the stone itself.
