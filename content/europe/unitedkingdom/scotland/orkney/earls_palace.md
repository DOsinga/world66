A ruined Renaissance palace next to the Bishop's Palace and St Magnus Cathedral, the Earl's Palace was built around 1607 by Earl Patrick Stewart, one of the most notorious aristocrats in Scottish history. The architecture is unusually sophisticated — French Renaissance influences, large windows, projecting bay turrets — and contrasts strikingly with the cathedral's medieval mass.

The palace is roofless now but the structure is largely intact. The combined ticket includes the Bishop's Palace next door. Closed in winter.
