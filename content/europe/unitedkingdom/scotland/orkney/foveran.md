The most consistently good restaurant on Orkney for several decades, the Foveran sits in a hotel on the road south of Kirkwall, looking out over Scapa Flow. The menu focuses on Orcadian produce — beef, lamb, scallops, hand-dived crab, lobster — cooked simply and very well. The wine list is short but thoughtful.

Booking essential, particularly in summer. The dining room has uninterrupted sea views; ask for a window table at sunset.
