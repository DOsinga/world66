One of two Martello towers built on Hoy in 1814–15 to defend Longhope Sound from American privateers in the closing months of the War of 1812, Hackness Tower and the neighbouring Hackness Battery are among the finest preserved Martello defences in Britain. The 12-metre stone tower was designed to hold a gun on its roof firing through a full 360°.

Open in summer. The interior is restored to its early-nineteenth-century condition. Combine with the Crockness Martello tower on the opposite side of Longhope.
