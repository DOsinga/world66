The northernmost Scotch whisky distillery in Scotland, Highland Park has been producing on the southern edge of Kirkwall since 1798. The distillery is one of the few in Scotland still doing its own floor maltings, partly using local Orkney peat, which gives the whisky its distinctive heather-honey-smoke character.

Tours are excellent — small groups, well-paced, ending with a tasting. The standard 12-year-old is widely considered one of the best value single malts in Scotland. Book ahead in summer. The visitor centre stocks rarities not available elsewhere.
