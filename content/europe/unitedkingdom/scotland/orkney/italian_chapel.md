One of the most moving sights in Orkney, the Italian Chapel on Lamb Holm is a tiny chapel that the Italian POWs of Camp 60 created from two Nissen huts joined end to end. The interior, painted in trompe-l'œil to look like marble, brickwork, and stained glass, was largely the work of Domenico Chiocchetti, a Catholic artist from the Italian Dolomites.

The chapel sits beside the Churchill Barriers — the same barriers the prisoners were forced to build — and is visited by an extraordinary range of people, including descendants of the original POWs. A small donation is requested. Allow half an hour. Combine with a visit to the barriers themselves and the famous block-ship wrecks visible at low tide.
