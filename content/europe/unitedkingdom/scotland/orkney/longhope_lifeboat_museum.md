Longhope Lifeboat Museum keeps one of Orkney's strongest maritime stories close to the sea that shaped it. Lifeboats have served Longhope since the nineteenth century, and the museum explains the work, equipment, and local courage behind rescues in notoriously rough waters.

The emotional centre is the 1969 disaster, when the Longhope lifeboat was lost with all eight crew while going to help a drifting freighter. It is a small museum, but on Hoy it carries real weight.
