One of the great cliff walks in Orkney, Marwick Head rises 80 metres straight out of the Atlantic on the west coast of Mainland. The cliffs hold enormous colonies of guillemots, razorbills, fulmars, kittiwakes, and puffins (in season). The walk to the headland from the small car park at Marwick Bay takes about half an hour each way; the path is easy but exposed to the wind.

At the top, the stone tower of the Kitchener Memorial dominates the headland. Combine with the nearby Brough of Birsay — the tidal causeway exposes for a few hours around low tide and gives access to a Pictish and Norse site on the offshore island.
