A Neolithic chambered cairn beside Midhowe Broch on the west coast of Rousay, the Midhowe Cairn is among the largest and most impressive in Orkney — 23 metres long, with 12 burial compartments arranged along a central passage. The site has been roofed by a modern shed that allows you to walk along a raised gangway looking down into the chamber, an unusual and successful piece of presentation.

Combined with Midhowe Broch a few hundred metres away and the smaller Knowe of Yarso further up the hillside, the area is sometimes called Rousay's "Egypt of the North." Reaching it requires the short ferry from Tingwall.
