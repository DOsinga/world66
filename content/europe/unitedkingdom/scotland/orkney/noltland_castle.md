Noltland Castle is an unfinished late-16th-century stronghold on Westray, built to an unusual defensive Z-plan. Its ruined walls, gun loops, and exposed setting make it one of the better historic stops on the island.

The castle is tied to Gilbert Balfour, a turbulent figure in the politics around Mary Queen of Scots, and was later damaged during the Civil War.
