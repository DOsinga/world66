Ophir Church and the Earl's Bu are a pair of medieval ruins on Mainland Orkney, both dating from around 1200. The round church is unusual in Scotland and was probably inspired by the Church of the Holy Sepulchre in Jerusalem, while the nearby remains are linked to the Norse earls of Orkney.

The site is small, but it is a rewarding stop for visitors tracing Orkney's Norse and medieval history beyond the famous prehistoric monuments. Its coastal setting also makes it a pleasant pause on a drive between Kirkwall and Stromness.
