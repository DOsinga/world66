The Orkney Fossil and Heritage Centre on Burray brings together local fossils, natural history, and island heritage. The fossil collection includes material from Orkney and beyond, while other galleries explain the Churchill Barriers and the daily life of the South Isles.

It is a useful stop when driving between the Italian Chapel, the Churchill Barriers, and South Ronaldsay. Some galleries have limited wheelchair access, so check ahead if step-free access matters.
