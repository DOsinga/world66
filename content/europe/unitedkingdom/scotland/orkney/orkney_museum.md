The Orkney Museum occupies Tankerness House, a sixteenth-century merchant's home with one of the best-preserved walled gardens in Orkney. The collection spans the entire history of the islands — Neolithic finds from sites across Mainland, Pictish carvings, Norse artefacts, the maritime and farming history of the modern period — and is presented with intelligence and a strong local voice.

Free to enter. Allow an hour and a half. The garden behind the house is a peaceful spot for a sandwich in summer. The shop is small but stocks unusually good Orkney-related books.
