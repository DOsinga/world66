A genuinely odd and rewarding small museum near St Magnus Cathedral in Kirkwall, the Orkney Wireless Museum houses an enormous collection of domestic and military wireless equipment from the early twentieth century onwards. The wartime communications material — much of it from the Royal Navy bases in Orkney — is the strength of the collection.

The exhibits are densely packed and the explanations are detailed. Allow an hour. Best for people with an interest in technology, World War II history, or social history; otherwise a quick browse.
