The Scapa Flow Museum at Lyness on Hoy tells the story of Orkney's wartime role as the main base of the British Grand Fleet in two world wars. The museum occupies the renovated Royal Naval Oil Pumping Station and Power Station, complete with the original engines and pumps, and includes excellent displays on the scuttling of the German High Seas Fleet in 1919, the loss of HMS Royal Oak in 1939, and the construction of the Churchill Barriers.

Outside the museum the Lyness Naval Cemetery contains the graves of more than 600 sailors from both wars. The whole site takes a substantial half-day to do properly. Accessible by ferry from Houton on Mainland.
