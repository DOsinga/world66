St Boniface Kirk is a 12th-century church on Papa Westray, built over the remains of a much older Pictish settlement and church now threatened by coastal erosion. It is one of those small Orkney sites where early Christian history feels close to the weather and the sea.

The dedication probably reflects the missionary world of Winfrid Boniface, the Devon-born churchman who became an important figure in early medieval Germany. Restored in 1993, the kirk is still used as a church, which gives the place a living edge rather than the feel of a sealed ruin.
