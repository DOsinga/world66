Start Point Lighthouse stands on a tidal islet off Sanday, painted in red and white bands that make it one of Orkney's most recognisable lighthouses. It was completed in 1806, rebuilt in 1870, and later became the first Scottish lighthouse with a revolving light.

There are no tours or interior access, and the causeway can only be crossed for a short window around low tide. Check tide times before setting out and leave enough time to get back safely.
