One of the better contemporary restaurants in Kirkwall, the Storehouse occupies a converted seventeenth-century warehouse a few streets from the cathedral. The menu is short and changes monthly, built around Orcadian seafood, lamb, and seasonal vegetables. The wine list is the most interesting in Orkney.

The dining room has exposed stonework, low beams, and a relaxed atmosphere. Booking advised. Lunch as well as dinner, plus a popular brunch menu at weekends.
