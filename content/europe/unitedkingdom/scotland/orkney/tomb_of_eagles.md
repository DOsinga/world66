A chambered tomb on the southern tip of South Ronaldsay, the Tomb of the Eagles takes its name from the bones of white-tailed eagles found alongside the human remains during excavation. The site was discovered by a farmer in 1958 and remained in family ownership until recent years, with an informal little museum at the farmhouse.

The walk to the tomb across the cliffs is one of the best short walks in Orkney. The current operating status is intermittent — check locally before travelling. The chambered cairn itself, entered by sliding through a low passage on a small trolley, is the most participatory archaeological experience in Orkney when open.
