The Tomb of Unstan is a Neolithic chambered cairn near Stenness, built around 3000 BCE. Its layout combines features seen in both stalled and chambered Orkney tombs, making it an interesting counterpoint to the better-known monuments around the Heart of Neolithic Orkney.
