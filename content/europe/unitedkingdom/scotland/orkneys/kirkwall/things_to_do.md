---
title: "Things to Do in Kirkwall"
type: section
---

Kirkwall packs an extraordinary concentration of history into a small area. The cathedral, the adjacent palace ruins, and the museum on Broad Street can occupy a full morning, and the distilleries on the town's edge extend the day nicely. The surrounding Orkney countryside — Neolithic monuments, wild coast, the scuttled fleet at Scapa Flow — is all within easy reach.
