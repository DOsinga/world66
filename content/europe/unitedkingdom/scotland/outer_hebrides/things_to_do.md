---
title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

The Outer Hebrides offer a remarkable concentration of prehistoric monuments — standing stones, brochs, and burial cairns that predate the pyramids — alongside wild landscapes, working distilleries, and one of the world's most unusual airports. The variety is genuine: you can spend a morning at a megalithic stone circle that rivals Stonehenge, an afternoon watching Atlantic gannets from a clifftop, and an evening at a gin distillery that sources its botanicals from the surrounding sea.
