Peebles is a handsome Borders town on the River Tweed, close enough to [Edinburgh](/europe/unitedkingdom/scotland/edinburgh) for a day trip but with a slower rhythm of its own. It has stone houses, independent shops, riverside paths, and hills that begin almost at the edge of town.

The appeal is split between town and countryside. [Neidpath Castle](/europe/unitedkingdom/scotland/peebles/neidpath_castle) watches the Tweed from a bend in the river, [Dawyck Botanic Garden](/europe/unitedkingdom/scotland/peebles/dawyck_botanic_garden) is one of the Borders' best garden visits, and [Glentress Forest](/europe/unitedkingdom/scotland/peebles/glentress_forest) gives mountain bikers and walkers a proper reason to linger.

Peebles is not as abbey-rich as [Melrose](/europe/unitedkingdom/scotland/melrose) or [Jedburgh](/europe/unitedkingdom/scotland/jedburgh), but it is one of the easiest Borders towns to enjoy without an agenda. Walk the High Street, follow the river, then choose castle, garden, or forest depending on the weather.
