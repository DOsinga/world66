The River Tweed is the thread that makes Peebles feel like more than a market town. Paths follow the water from the centre, with easy loops toward Neidpath Castle and quieter stretches where the town drops away.

It is the thing to do when you do not want a ticket, a timetable, or a drive. Bring sensible shoes after rain; the riverbanks can be muddy.
