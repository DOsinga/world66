Robert Smail's Printing Works is a preserved Victorian printing workshop in Innerleithen, a short trip east of Peebles. Guided tours show the composing room, presses, and office, with the machinery and working routines explained in context rather than treated as static displays.

It is a strong stop for anyone interested in industrial history, graphic design, or the practical side of small-town publishing before digital printing. Visits are normally by timed guided tour.
