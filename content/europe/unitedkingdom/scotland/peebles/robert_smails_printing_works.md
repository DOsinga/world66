Robert Smail's Printing Works is a preserved Victorian print shop in Innerleithen, close enough to Peebles to make an easy Borders side trip. The machinery, type cases, and workshop spaces show how local printing worked before digital files and instant everything.

Visits are by guided tour, which is the right way to see it: the presses and old water-driven mechanisms make far more sense when someone can show them in motion.
