The Black Watch Museum covers the history of the Highland regiment formed from the 42nd and 73rd Regiments of Foot. Displays trace the regiment's campaigns, uniforms, medals, and the darker political origins of its name.

It is one of Perth's stronger museums and pairs naturally with North Inch, where the regiment was first mustered in 1739.
