Huntingtower Castle is a compact medieval stronghold west of Perth, best known for its two tower houses and painted wooden ceilings. It is smaller than Scotland's headline castles, but that makes it easier to read: rooms, stairways, and defensive quirks are close at hand.

The castle is also tied to noble plotting and royal politics, the kind of local history that gives a short visit some bite. It works well as an easy excursion from Perth, especially if you prefer atmospheric ruins and interiors to heavily staged attractions.
