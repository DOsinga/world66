Perth Art Gallery focuses on Scottish art, including modern Scottish painters such as Joan Eardley and Sir William MacTaggart. The collection also includes works by the Scottish Colourists and European art from the 16th and 17th centuries.

It pairs naturally with Perth Museum nearby, giving the city a compact cultural circuit in the centre.
