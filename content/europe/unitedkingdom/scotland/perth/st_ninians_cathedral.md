St Ninian's Cathedral is Perth's Scottish Episcopal cathedral, opened in 1850 during the revival of Anglican worship in Scotland. The building was designed by William Butterfield and developed further into the twentieth century.

It is a worthwhile church stop for visitors interested in Gothic Revival architecture or Perth's religious history. The cathedral sits close enough to the centre to combine with the museum, the vennels, and St John's Kirk.
