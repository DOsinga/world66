Longhaven Cliffs is a rugged coastal reserve south of Peterhead, where high North Sea cliffs create nesting habitat for seabirds and broad views along the Buchan coast. It is a simple place, but the scale of the cliffs makes it more than a local picnic stop.

Travellers should expect an exposed coastal walk rather than a built attraction. Bring windproof clothing, keep well back from cliff edges, and visit for wildlife, sea air, and a sense of the coastline that surrounds Peterhead.
