The Dunfallandy Stone is a compact but memorable Pictish stop just outside Pitlochry. The carved slab shows cross, animal and symbol decoration, the kind of early medieval imagery that makes this part of Scotland feel older and stranger than its Victorian resort-town surface.

It is a short visit, best combined with a walk or drive south of town rather than treated as a standalone expedition. For anyone interested in early Scotland, it is worth the detour precisely because it sits so quietly in the landscape.
