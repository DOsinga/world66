A short track from Frithard Road climbs to this rocky knoll above the village. From the top you look down over Plockton's roofs and harbour and out along Loch Carron towards the Applecross hills.

It takes only a few minutes from the village and is the best place to understand how snugly Plockton is tucked into its bay. Go at sunset.
