---
title: Things to Do
type: section
---

Portree is less about sights than about position. The harbour and The Lump fill an evening; the real things to do are the drives north into Trotternish — the [Old Man of Storr](/europe/unitedkingdom/scotland/skye/old_man_of_storr), Kilt Rock and the [Quiraing](/europe/unitedkingdom/scotland/skye/quiraing) — and south to the [Cuillin](/europe/unitedkingdom/scotland/skye/the_cuillins). Boat trips from the quay head out into the Sound of Raasay for seals, seabirds and, with luck, sea eagles.
