Findlater Castle is a ruin with a superb setting, clinging to a rocky promontory above the Moray Firth. Very little is polished for visitors, which makes the combination of broken masonry, sea cliffs, and coastal weather feel more powerful than many tidier castle stops.

The approach is the point as much as the ruin itself. Treat it as a coastal walk with a historic reward, keep well back from unsafe edges, and avoid poor weather if visibility or footing is bad.
