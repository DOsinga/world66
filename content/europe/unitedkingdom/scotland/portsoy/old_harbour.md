Portsoy's Old Harbour is the village's signature view, a tight basin of weathered stone, fishing-town history, and north-east Scottish light. Its unusual horizontal stonework gives it a texture that photographers love, but it is also a real reminder of Portsoy's maritime past.

Walk the harbour walls, then loop back into the village for ice cream, galleries, or the coastal path. It is a small place, so the reward is in slowing down rather than ticking off a monument.
