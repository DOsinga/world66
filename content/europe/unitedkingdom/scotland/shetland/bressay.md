Bressay sits just across the harbour from [Lerwick](/europe/unitedkingdom/scotland/shetland), close enough that the ferry crossing feels almost casual. That is the trick of the place. A few minutes after leaving town you are among crofts, lochs, ponies, ruined settlements, and roads that climb into open moor.

The island is best as a walking and birding day. [Bressay Heritage Centre](/europe/unitedkingdom/scotland/shetland/bressay/bressay_heritage_centre) gives the island its local story, [Cullingsburgh](/europe/unitedkingdom/scotland/shetland/bressay/cullingsburgh) adds older history, and [Bressay Lighthouse](/europe/unitedkingdom/scotland/shetland/bressay/bressay_lighthouse) anchors the southern coast. The real wild card is [Noss National Nature Reserve](/europe/unitedkingdom/scotland/shetland/bressay/noss_national_nature_reserve), reached by a small seasonal boat from Bressay's east side, where cliffs roar with seabirds in summer.

Bressay is easy by Shetland standards, but it is still Shetland. Bring waterproofs, check the Noss boat before committing to the walk, and leave room for weather to change the day.
