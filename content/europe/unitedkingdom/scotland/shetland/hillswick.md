*Europe > United Kingdom > Scotland > Shetland > Hillswick*

Hillswick is a tiny village in Northmavine, the wild northern lobe of Shetland's Mainland. It is not a town of museums and cafes; it is a base for rough coast, hard weather, and the feeling that the Atlantic has the upper hand.

The reason to come is the land around it. To the west, [Eshaness](/europe/unitedkingdom/scotland/shetland/hillswick/eshaness) has volcanic cliffs, geos, blowholes, and the lighthouse road that gives one of Shetland's best short coastal walks. Nearby [Tangwick Haa Museum](/europe/unitedkingdom/scotland/shetland/hillswick/tangwick_haa_museum) puts the hard old life of Northmavine into a small laird's house, while [Dore Holm](/europe/unitedkingdom/scotland/shetland/hillswick/dore_holm) sits offshore like a sea arch drawn by someone with a sense of humour.

Inland, [Ronas Hill](/europe/unitedkingdom/scotland/shetland/hillswick/ronas_hill) rises to the highest point in Shetland, with Arctic-alpine plants and a Neolithic cairn on the summit. Hillswick works best for travellers with a car, boots, and time to let weather decide the day. If the clouds lift, go straight for the coast or the hill.
