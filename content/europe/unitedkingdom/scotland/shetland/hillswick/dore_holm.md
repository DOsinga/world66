Dore Holm is a small uninhabited islet off Eshaness, famous for the natural arch through its middle. From the right angle, it looks like a horse lowering its head to drink from the sea.

It is a quick viewpoint rather than a long visit, but it gives the Hillswick-Eshaness road one of its most memorable images. In rough weather, watch from a safe distance and let the Atlantic do the work.
