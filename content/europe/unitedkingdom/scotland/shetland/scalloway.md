*Europe > United Kingdom > Scotland > Shetland > Scalloway*

Scalloway is a small fishing village on Shetland's Mainland, six miles west of Lerwick. It was Shetland's capital until the early 18th century, and that older status still shows in the compact harbour, ruined castle, and the way the village feels more historic than its size suggests.

The headline sight is [Scalloway Castle](/europe/unitedkingdom/scotland/shetland/scalloway/scalloway_castle), a roofless tower house tied to the brutal rule of Patrick Stewart, Earl of Orkney and Shetland. Across the street, [Scalloway Museum](/europe/unitedkingdom/scotland/shetland/scalloway/scalloway_museum) tells the local story, especially the wartime Shetland Bus operation that ran agents, refugees, and supplies between Shetland and occupied Norway.

Scalloway works well as a half-day from Lerwick, but it rewards slower travellers too. Walk out to the [Shetland Bus Memorial](/europe/unitedkingdom/scotland/shetland/scalloway/shetland_bus_memorial), continue along the headlands, or drive south over the linked islands of Trondra and Burra for beaches, low hills, and saltwater views.
