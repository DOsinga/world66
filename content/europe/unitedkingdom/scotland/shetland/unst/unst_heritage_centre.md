Unst Heritage Centre is a small but valuable museum for understanding life at the northern edge of Shetland. Its collections cover crofting, fishing, domestic life, textiles, genealogy, and the practical resilience needed on an island this remote.

It works best alongside the nearby boat haven and the wider Haroldswick area. Together they turn Unst from a dramatic map-edge destination into a place with families, trades, migrations, and memory.
