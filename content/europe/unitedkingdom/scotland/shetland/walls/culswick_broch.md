Culswick Broch is an Iron Age fortified dwelling south of Walls. It was once much better preserved, but even as a ruin it gives a clear sense of Shetland's broch landscape.

Drive toward Culswick, then walk up the track. The stonework and coastal setting make it one of the better short archaeology stops on west Mainland.
