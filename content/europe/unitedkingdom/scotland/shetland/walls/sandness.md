Sandness is the small settlement at the end of the A971 west of Walls. The draw is the position: open views toward Papa Stour, a ragged coast, and nearby islets with prehistoric remains.

It is a quiet end-of-road place rather than a formal attraction, but it helps make a good west Mainland circuit.
