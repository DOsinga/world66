---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

June and July are the peak months for almost every reason. At Shetland's latitude — further north than Bergen — midsummer brings near-continuous daylight, with the sky not truly darkening even at midnight. Seabirds are at their noisiest and most visible: gannets, puffins, guillemots, and skuas are all breeding, and the Lumbister moorland holds its best show of divers and waders through May and into July. The otter population is present year-round, but summer's longer light hours make them easier to find.

August is still good but quieter on the bird front as breeding wraps up. Spring arrives late — April on Yell can feel wintry — and autumn brings the first of the Atlantic gales. Winter is genuinely harsh: short days, powerful storms, and a stillness on the land that some people find compelling and others simply bleak. If you visit outside summer, dress for everything and treat the ferry timetables as approximate in bad weather.
