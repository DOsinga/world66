Armadale Castle Gardens occupy the former seat of Clan Donald on the Sleat peninsula. The castle itself is a ruined mansion, abandoned after fire and financial trouble, but the surrounding gardens, woodland paths, and arboretum are the reason to visit.

The Museum of the Isles on the estate gives useful context on Clan Donald and the wider history of Skye and the Hebrides. It is a natural stop after arriving by ferry at Armadale, or while exploring southern Skye from Broadford.
