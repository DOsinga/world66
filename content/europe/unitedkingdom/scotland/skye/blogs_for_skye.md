Very few people who live on Skye write about it, and fewer still are not selling a cottage. These three are the island's own voices: a crofter's diary, the community trust that owns Staffin harbour, and the weekly paper that has covered Skye and the west since the 1970s.

They point to an open-fire restaurant in Dunvegan, the dinosaur beach at An Corran and a new community café in Portree.
