Duntulm Castle is a ruined MacDonald stronghold on the northern tip of the Trotternish peninsula. Built from the 14th century and abandoned in the 18th, it is now a roofless, weathered ruin on a cliff edge facing the Outer Hebrides.

The castle itself is unsafe to enter, so the reason to come is the setting: sea cliffs, open sky, and long views from one of the wilder corners of Skye.
