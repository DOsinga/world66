This small museum tells the unlikely story of Angus MacAskill, born near Dunvegan and later famous in Nova Scotia as one of the tallest strongmen of the 19th century. It is a modest, local place rather than a grand attraction, but it adds a human oddity to the castle-and-coast rhythm of Dunvegan.

Expect photographs, biographical material, and memorabilia tied to MacAskill's life and legend. It works best as a short stop while you are already in the village, especially if you enjoy the eccentric side of island history.
