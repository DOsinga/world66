The Skye Museum of Island Life preserves a row of thatched cottages arranged to show crofting life on Skye around the turn of the 19th century. The rooms are simple, but the setting and objects make the island's older rural life easy to picture. It is a good cultural stop on a Trotternish drive, especially when paired with the nearby landscape sights.
