The Skye Museum of Rural Life preserves a cluster of thatched cottages and croft buildings showing how people lived on Skye before modern comforts arrived. The rooms, tools, and household objects give the island's crofting past a human scale.

It is a short, low-key museum, but a good one if you want context for the island beyond scenery and castles.
