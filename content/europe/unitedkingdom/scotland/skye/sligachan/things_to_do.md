---
title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

There is no village to speak of at Sligachan — just a hotel, a bunkhouse, a campsite, and a junction. What you come for is the view: the dark gabbro spires of the Black Cuillin rising directly out of the glen to the south, and the rounded granite slopes of the Red Cuillin to the east. This is the classic gateway to the high mountains of Skye and the starting point for several of the great walks in Glen Sligachan.
