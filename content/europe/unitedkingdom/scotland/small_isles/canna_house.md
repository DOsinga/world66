Canna House is a 19th-century villa on the island of Canna, preserved with collections tied to Gaelic culture, language, and natural history. The house reflects the work of its former owners, who gathered books, recordings, photographs, and specimens before gifting the property to the National Trust for Scotland.

It is a focused cultural stop on an island better known for walking, birdlife, and sea views.
