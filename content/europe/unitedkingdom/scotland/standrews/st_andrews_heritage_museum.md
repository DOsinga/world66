St Andrews Heritage Museum and Garden tells the town's smaller stories: fishing families, university life, shops, trades, childhood, and the ordinary routines that sat behind the famous cathedral, castle, and golf links. It occupies a 17th-century house, which gives the displays a domestic scale that suits the subject.

The garden is part of the pleasure, especially on a dry day after the wind has had its way with the cathedral ruins. Come here when you want St Andrews to feel like a lived-in town rather than a sequence of headline sights.
