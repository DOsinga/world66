East Sands is the smaller of St Andrews's two main beaches, curving south from the harbour between the town's eastern headland and the golf courses beyond. It is less well-known than the West Sands — fewer tourists find their way here — but for actual swimming and sunbathing it is the better option, having some shelter from the prevailing westerly wind and being closer to the town centre.

The beach is backed by a small park and a rowing club, and the Seafood Restaurant (one of the better fish restaurants in town) looks out over it. At high tide the beach is a narrow strip; at low tide it widens considerably and rock pools appear toward the harbour end. The water is cold by any southern European standard but swimmable in summer — the North Sea reaches around 14-15°C in August.

The walk from the harbour around to East Sands takes you below the cathedral walls, which loom above the path to the east. A longer coastal walk continues south from East Sands along the **Fife Coastal Path** toward the Kinkell Braes cliffs and eventually the East Neuk villages.
