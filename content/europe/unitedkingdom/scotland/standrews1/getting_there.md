---
title: "Getting There"
type: section
---

St Andrews has no direct rail connection. The nearest station is **Leuchars**, five miles north, on the main Edinburgh–Dundee–Aberdeen line. Trains run frequently from Edinburgh (about an hour) and Dundee (about fifteen minutes). From Leuchars, buses and taxis cover the remaining distance; the Stagecoach X24 bus links directly into the town centre.

**By bus from Edinburgh**, the X59 Stagecoach East service runs direct from Edinburgh Bus Station at St Andrew Square to St Andrews in around two hours. It is cheaper than the train-plus-bus combination and drops you in the town centre.

**By car**, St Andrews is about an hour north of Edinburgh via the M90 and A91, or an hour southwest of Dundee via the A91. Parking in the centre is metered and limited; the large park-and-ride at Madras College on the outskirts is a more reliable option during busy periods.

**Edinburgh Airport** is the most convenient international gateway, about 70 miles southwest. Dundee Airport is smaller and has fewer connections but is only 20 miles away.
