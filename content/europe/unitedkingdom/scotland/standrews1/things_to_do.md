---
title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

St Andrews packs an extraordinary amount into a small footprint. The cathedral ruins, the castle cliff walk, the Old Course, and three long beaches are all within twenty minutes on foot. The town rewards slow exploration — duck into the cathedral's museum to see the St Andrews Sarcophagus, climb St Rule's Tower for the view, and walk the coastal path east toward the harbour at low tide.
