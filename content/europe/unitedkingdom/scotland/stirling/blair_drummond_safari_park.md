Blair Drummond Safari Park is one of the most straightforward family days out near Stirling: a drive-through animal reserve combined with walk-around enclosures, play areas, and seasonal talks. The setting is broad and green, so it feels more like a countryside excursion than a city attraction.

It is best approached as a half-day or longer outing, especially with children. Travellers without a car should check transport carefully, since the safari format and location make it less spontaneous than Stirling Castle or the old town.
