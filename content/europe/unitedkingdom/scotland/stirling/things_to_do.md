---
title: Things to Do
type: section
---

[Stirling Castle](/europe/unitedkingdom/scotland/stirling/stirling_castle) is the obvious headline — a strategic stronghold turned royal residence, with the most ambitious restoration project of any historic building in Scotland. Allow half a day. The [Wallace Monument](/europe/unitedkingdom/scotland/stirling/wallace_monument) on Abbey Craig and the [Bannockburn Heritage Centre](/europe/unitedkingdom/scotland/stirling/bannockburn_heritage_centre) two miles to the south fill in the two great battles of Scottish independence and can comfortably be done in a single day.

Within the old town the [Church of the Holy Rude](/europe/unitedkingdom/scotland/stirling/church_of_the_holy_rude) is where the infant James VI was crowned in 1567 — one of only two churches in Britain still in use that have hosted a coronation. [Cowane's Hospital](/europe/unitedkingdom/scotland/stirling/cowanes_hospital), [Mar's Wark](/europe/unitedkingdom/scotland/stirling/mars_wark), [Argyll's Lodging](/europe/unitedkingdom/scotland/stirling/argylls_lodging) and the ruined [Mar's Wark](/europe/unitedkingdom/scotland/stirling/mars_wark) facade trace four centuries of grand townhouse architecture along a single street.

The [Stirling Smith Art Gallery and Museum](/europe/unitedkingdom/scotland/stirling/stirling_smith) holds the world's oldest surviving football and a strong collection of paintings, with no admission charge. The [Old Town Jail](/europe/unitedkingdom/scotland/stirling/old_town_jail) is more touristy but the rooftop platform offers the best panorama in town. The [Stirling Old Bridge](/europe/unitedkingdom/scotland/stirling/stirling_old_bridge) over the Forth is the medieval successor to the wooden bridge of Wallace's battle.

A short walk or bus ride takes you to [Cambuskenneth Abbey](/europe/unitedkingdom/scotland/stirling/cambuskenneth_abbey), the burial place of James III, set in farmland across the river. The [University of Stirling](/europe/unitedkingdom/scotland/stirling/university_of_stirling) campus, built around the grounds of [Airthrey Castle](/europe/unitedkingdom/scotland/stirling/airthrey_castle), is one of the most scenic in Britain. North of the city, [Bridge of Allan](/europe/unitedkingdom/scotland/stirling/bridge_of_allan) is an elegant Victorian spa village worth half an afternoon.
