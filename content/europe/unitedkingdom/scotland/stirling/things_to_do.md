---
title: Things to Do in Stirling
type: section
---

The castle and the historic old town immediately below it are the core of any Stirling visit. A morning at the castle followed by an afternoon walk down through the old town — Argyll's Lodging, Mar's Wark, the Church of the Holy Rude, the Smith museum — covers the main sights without rushing. The Wallace Monument is a worthwhile half-day on its own; Bannockburn can be combined with it as an afternoon excursion south of town.
