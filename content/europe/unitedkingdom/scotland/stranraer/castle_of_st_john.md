The Castle of St John is a thick-walled tower house built around 1510 near the centre of Stranraer. It later served as a military garrison and then as a jail, so the interior tells a more varied story than its compact size suggests.

When open, the tower has displays on the building's past and views from the upper levels over the town and Loch Ryan. It is the one sight in Stranraer you should make time for if you are passing through.
