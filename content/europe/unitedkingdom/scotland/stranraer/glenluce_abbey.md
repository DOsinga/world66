Glenluce Abbey is a ruined Cistercian monastery founded in 1192, about 10 miles east of Stranraer near the A75. The remains are not as grand as the Border abbeys, but the site is atmospheric and easy to combine with a drive toward Newton Stewart or Dumfries.

Check opening details before setting out, as access can vary when masonry inspections or conservation work are underway.
