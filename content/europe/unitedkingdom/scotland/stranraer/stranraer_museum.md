Stranraer Museum occupies the old town hall on George Street and gives useful context for Wigtownshire and the harbour town around it. Exhibits cover local archaeology, agriculture, dairy history, and Stranraer's links with polar exploration.

It is small, free, and central, making it a sensible first stop before heading out around the Rhins.
