Behind a glass door at the southern end of the main street, Login's Well was once Stromness's main source of fresh water for departing ships. Hudson's Bay Company vessels, whalers and other ships bound across the Atlantic topped up their casks here before leaving Orkney.

It's a small thing to see, but it explains the whole town: Stromness existed because ships needed men, water and a safe anchorage on the way west.
