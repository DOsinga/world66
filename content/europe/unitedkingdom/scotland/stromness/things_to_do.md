---
title: Things to Do
type: section
---

Stromness's pleasures are mostly on foot: wander the flagstoned main street and its alleys, see the [Pier Arts Centre](/europe/unitedkingdom/scotland/stromness/pier_arts_centre) and the [Stromness Museum](/europe/unitedkingdom/scotland/stromness/stromness_museum), climb Brinkies Brae, and walk the coast path past the Ness Battery to Warbeth. Western Orkney's great Neolithic sites are a short drive away.
