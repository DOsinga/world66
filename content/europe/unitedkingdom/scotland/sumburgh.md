Sumburgh is the southern tip of Mainland in [Shetland](/europe/unitedkingdom/scotland/shetland), where the airport runway, old stone settlements, bird cliffs, and ferry pier all crowd into a small strip of land. It is not really a town, and it is best treated as a compact Shetland feature rather than a city stop.

The spare-day case is strong. [Jarlshof](/europe/unitedkingdom/scotland/sumburgh/jarlshof) alone is one of Scotland's great archaeological sites, with layers from prehistoric houses to Norse and later farm buildings. Nearby [Old Scatness](/europe/unitedkingdom/scotland/sumburgh/old_scatness) and [Ness of Burgi](/europe/unitedkingdom/scotland/sumburgh/ness_of_burgi) add more Iron Age texture.

Then the road climbs to [Sumburgh Head](/europe/unitedkingdom/scotland/sumburgh/sumburgh_head), with a lighthouse, visitor centre, and seabird cliffs. If you have a car on Shetland and the weather is fair, the area makes one of the islands' easiest half-day circuits.
