Jarlshof is the reason Sumburgh clears the bar. Storms exposed the site in the late 19th century, revealing a dense archaeological stack: Bronze Age houses, an Iron Age broch and wheelhouses, Norse longhouses, medieval farm buildings, and a later laird's house.

Few places in Scotland make the depth of settlement so visible in one walk. The site is exposed, so dress for wind even on a bright day.
