Quendale Watermill is a modest but useful stop if you are driving the south Mainland loop. The restored 19th-century mill explains a practical side of Shetland life: grain, weather, and the machinery needed to make a small farming district work.

It pairs well with nearby archaeological stops because it brings the story forward into more recent rural history.
