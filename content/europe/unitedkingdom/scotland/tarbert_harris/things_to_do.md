Tarbert itself is small — the [distillery](/europe/unitedkingdom/scotland/tarbert_harris/harris_distillery), the ferry and the tweed shop — but it sits in the middle of Harris. Drive south for the great white beaches at [Luskentyre](/europe/unitedkingdom/scotland/outer_hebrides/luskentyre_beach) and Seilebost and the church at [Rodel](/europe/unitedkingdom/scotland/outer_hebrides/st_clements_church_rodel); east through the Bays; or north to [Clisham](/europe/unitedkingdom/scotland/outer_hebrides/clisham), Hushinish and the Scalpay lighthouse.
