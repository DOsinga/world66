Thurso Beach curves along the town's bay, while Thurso East is the reef break that gives the town its reputation among cold-water surfers. When conditions line up, this corner of Caithness feels far more Atlantic than sheltered, with heavy water rolling into a town that otherwise looks modest and workaday.

You do not need to surf to enjoy it. The beach is good for a windblown walk, watching the sets come in, and understanding why people travel a long way to paddle out here. If you are surfing, local advice matters: the water is cold, the reef is serious, and conditions change quickly.
