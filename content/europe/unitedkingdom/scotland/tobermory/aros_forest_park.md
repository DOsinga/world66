Aros Forest Park is the easiest countryside escape from Tobermory. The trails run through woodland south of town, with waterfalls and short walks that work even if you are not planning a full Mull hike.

It is not the island's wildest landscape, but it is useful: close, green, and good for stretching your legs between harbour time and dinner.
