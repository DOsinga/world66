Mull Museum is a compact local museum on Tobermory's Main Street. Its displays cover island life, work, families, and the practical history that shaped Mull before tourism became the main business.

It is the right scale for the town: not a grand institution, but a useful way to give the harbour and surrounding villages more depth.
