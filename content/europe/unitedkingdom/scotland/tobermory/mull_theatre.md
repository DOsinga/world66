Mull Theatre is a 100-seat performance space a short drive south of Tobermory. It is small, but that is the charm: theatre, music, and community events feel close to the island rather than imported for visitors.

Check the programme before building plans around it. When something is on, it can turn an ordinary evening in Tobermory into a memorable one.
