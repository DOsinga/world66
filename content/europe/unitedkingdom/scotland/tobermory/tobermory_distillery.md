Tobermory Distillery is one of the village's stronger reasons to linger. It produces fruity Tobermory single malt, smoky Ledaig, and gin, with tours and tastings for visitors.

The distillery sits close to the harbour, so it fits easily into a day on foot. Book ahead in busy months if a tour matters to you.
