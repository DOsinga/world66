---
title: "Eating Out"
type: section
---

Turriff has a handful of pubs and cafes serving solid Scottish pub food, plus the tearoom at Delgatie Castle for lunches. For more ambitious dining, the wider area has a few hotels with restaurants (the Towie Tavern at Auchterless, the Fife Arms in Turriff itself) that draw locals from across the district. Aberdeen's restaurant scene is 40 minutes south by car.
