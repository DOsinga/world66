The coast road north to [Achiltibuie](/europe/unitedkingdom/scotland/achiltibuie) and then on the narrow single-track road to [Lochinver](/europe/unitedkingdom/scotland/lochinver) is one of Scotland's great drives, with mountain and coastal scenery that is scarcely possible to exaggerate. If you can fit it in, take a boat trip to the Summer Isles from Achiltibuie.
