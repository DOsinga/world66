Isle Martin is a small island just off the coast near Ullapool, close enough to feel reachable but separate enough to make the crossing part of the pleasure. It is community-owned and low-key, with short walks, beaches, and views back toward the mainland and out to the Summer Isles.

This is a fair-weather, slow-travel outing. Check boat arrangements before you plan around it, bring what you need, and leave the island as quiet as you found it. It suits travellers who want a gentle island experience without committing to a full ferry journey.
