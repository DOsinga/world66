Clivocast Stone is one of Unst's small prehistoric markers, a standing stone set in a landscape where archaeology often appears without fences or fanfare. It is not a grand monument, but it adds another layer to an island better known for cliffs, birds, and northern extremes.

Stop if you are already touring Unst by car or bike. The pleasure is in the accumulation: broch remains, old crofts, Norse place names, and stones like this make the island feel deeply inhabited despite its remote position.
