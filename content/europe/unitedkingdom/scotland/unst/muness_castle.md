Muness Castle is a roofless late-sixteenth-century tower house on Unst, standing in a bare Shetland landscape that makes the ruin feel especially remote. It is one of the island's strongest historic stops after the nature reserves and boat heritage sites.

The castle is atmospheric rather than heavily interpreted, so bring a torch if interior access is open and be ready for uneven stone. It pairs naturally with a drive around southern Unst.
