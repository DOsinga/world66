Walls, or Waas, is a small fishing village on the west Mainland of [Shetland](/europe/unitedkingdom/scotland/shetland). It is not a town in the usual sense, but a base for a sparse and beautiful corner of the islands: moorland roads, old archaeology, island ferries, and sea cliffs facing the Atlantic.

The best nearby sight is [Stanydale Temple](/europe/unitedkingdom/scotland/walls/stanydale_temple), a Neolithic enclosure whose purpose is still argued over. South of the village, [Culswick Broch](/europe/unitedkingdom/scotland/walls/culswick_broch) and the Steep Islands add Iron Age history and rugged coastline.

Walls also matters because it links to harder-to-reach places. The ferry to Foula leaves from the village pier, while West Burrafirth, farther north, is the jumping-off point for [Papa Stour](/europe/unitedkingdom/scotland/walls/papa_stour), a tiny island of caves, arches, stacks, and rough Atlantic weather.
