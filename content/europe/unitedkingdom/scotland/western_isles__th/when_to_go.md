---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

The weather is the main variable. The Outer Hebrides sit directly in the path of Atlantic weather systems and can be stormy even in summer. June and July offer the longest days — full daylight until 10pm or later — and the best chances of settled weather. The machair is in flower through June and July, and the seabird colonies on the cliffs are at their busiest.

August is reliable enough and slightly less crowded than July. October through April can be very wet and dark, though winter storms have their own drama — particularly at the Butt of Lewis, where gale-force winds and heavy seas are the norm. The Caledonian MacBrayne ferry schedule does not change much between summer and winter, but flight frequencies to Barra and Stornoway reduce slightly in winter, and the Barra tidal timetable makes forward planning harder in shorter days.
