---
title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Yell rewards those who slow down. The island's best experiences are about patience and attention — waiting on a rocky shore at dawn for an otter, listening for the trembling call of a red-throated diver over the bog, or following a clifftop path until the northwest coast opens into spray and stacks. The Old Haa Museum at Burravoe is the single indoor attraction worth your time, offering a genuine and well-kept record of island life. Everything else happens outside, in a landscape that is austere by any conventional measure and quietly extraordinary once you stop expecting it to be otherwise.
