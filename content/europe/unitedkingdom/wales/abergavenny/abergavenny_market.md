Abergavenny Market is the town's everyday centre of gravity. The Victorian market hall hosts regular general markets, food stalls, antiques, crafts, and seasonal events, and it helps explain why Abergavenny has a stronger food identity than many towns of similar size.

It is not a reason to cross Wales on its own, but it is exactly the sort of place that makes a spare day here feel lived-in rather than purely scenic.
