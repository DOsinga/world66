The Ceredigion Coast Path is Aberporth's best non-beach activity. The path climbs out of the village and gives long views over Cardigan Bay, with cliff grass, coves, and seabirds along the way.

Short walkers can go as far as the next viewpoint and turn back. Stronger walkers can follow the coast toward Tresaith, Llangrannog, or Mwnt depending on time and transport.
