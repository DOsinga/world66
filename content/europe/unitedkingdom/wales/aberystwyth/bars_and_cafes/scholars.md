A Wetherspoons pub occupying a handsome former bank building near the university. The usual formula applies — cheap beer, no music, long opening hours — but the building itself is worth a look, with its high ceilings and ornate fittings still intact. A reliable option for an inexpensive pint and straightforward pub food. Inevitably popular with students and budget-conscious visitors.
