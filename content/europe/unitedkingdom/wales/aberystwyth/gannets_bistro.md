A Moroccan and Middle Eastern restaurant that has been a fixture on the Aberystwyth dining scene for years. The menu covers tagines, couscous, and mezze dishes, with a good selection for vegetarians. The decor leans into the North African theme without being kitsch. Generous portions and reasonable prices make it a reliable choice for an evening out. BYO wine is accepted.
