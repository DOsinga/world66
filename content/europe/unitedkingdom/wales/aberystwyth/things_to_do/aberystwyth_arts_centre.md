One of the largest arts centres in Wales, located on the university campus above the town. The complex includes a cinema screening a mix of mainstream and art-house films, a theatre hosting touring productions and local performances, a gallery space with changing contemporary art exhibitions, and a craft shop. The building also has a cafe with views over the Rheidol Valley. Programming runs year-round, though the schedule is busiest during term time. Worth checking what's on before you visit.
