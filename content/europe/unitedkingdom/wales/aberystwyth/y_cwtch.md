A small, cosy cafe in the town centre that draws a mix of students, locals, and Welsh speakers. The name means "a cosy hiding place" in Welsh, which accurately describes the atmosphere. Good coffee, homemade cakes, and a rotating selection of light meals. Welsh language is spoken and welcomed. Popular enough that seating fills quickly at lunchtime — worth arriving early or accepting a wait.
