The Bulkeley is the white-painted Georgian hotel that dominates the seafront, built in 1832 by Sir Richard Bulkeley to a design by Joseph Hansom. It survives almost unchanged — long sash windows, high ceilings, a grand staircase — and the front bar and lounge are open to non-residents.

Coming in for tea or a glass of wine and a window seat is the easiest way to absorb the building. The view from the lounge takes in the pier and the entire strait, and the dining room — old-school, white-tablecloth — serves good local seafood. Even if you do not stay, it is the most natural rendezvous point in the town.
