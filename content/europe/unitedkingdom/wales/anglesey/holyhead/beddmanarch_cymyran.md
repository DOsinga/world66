Beddmanarch-Cymyran is a protected coastal nature area around the Cymyran Strait and the Inland Sea between Holy Island and Anglesey. The mix of shore, saltmarsh, and open water is best for a quiet walk, birdwatching, and seeing a less built-up side of the Holyhead area.
