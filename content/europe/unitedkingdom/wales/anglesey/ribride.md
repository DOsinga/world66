Rigid inflatables from Menai Bridge. The short trip runs through **the Swellies**, the tidal rapids between the two bridges, and takes children from age four. Longer trips go out to the seals and seabirds of Puffin Island, down the strait to Caernarfon, or all the way around Anglesey in six hours.
