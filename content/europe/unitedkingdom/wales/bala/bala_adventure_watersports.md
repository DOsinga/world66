A family business on the foreshore of **Llyn Tegid**, Wales's largest natural lake, with more than a dozen activities: kayaking, canoeing, paddleboarding and sailing on the lake, and climbing and gorge walking in the hills around it. It is RYA- and AALA-licensed.
