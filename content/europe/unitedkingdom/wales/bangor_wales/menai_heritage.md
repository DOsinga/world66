Menai Heritage is a small community museum devoted to the two great bridges over the Menai Strait: Thomas Telford's Menai Suspension Bridge and Robert Stephenson's Britannia Bridge. It is a good stop if you have already walked the suspension bridge and want the engineering story behind it.

The displays use drawings, models, films, and local artefacts to explain how the crossings changed travel between mainland Wales and Anglesey. It is modest in scale, but the subject is strong and the museum sits close enough to the bridge to make the visit feel connected to the landscape outside.
