---
title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Bangor itself is compact, but the immediate surroundings offer plenty to keep you busy. The National Trust's [Penrhyn Castle](/europe/unitedkingdom/wales/bangor_wales/penrhyn_castle) just east of the city is the headline attraction — a vast Victorian mock-Norman mansion built on slate and sugar money. [Garth Pier](/europe/unitedkingdom/wales/bangor_wales/garth_pier) stretches 450 metres into the Menai Strait and is one of the most scenic spots in north Wales. [Bangor Cathedral](/europe/unitedkingdom/wales/bangor_wales/bangor_cathedral), founded in the 6th century, is at the heart of the city. The [Menai Suspension Bridge](/europe/unitedkingdom/wales/bangor_wales/menai_suspension_bridge), Thomas Telford's 1826 masterpiece, is a short drive southwest. Birdwatchers will find [The Spinnies](/europe/unitedkingdom/wales/bangor_wales/the_spinnies) rewarding. The mountains of Snowdonia are less than half an hour's drive south.
