Boats from Beaumaris pier, daily from April to October. The 75-minute cruise goes around **Puffin Island**, at the north end of the Menai Strait, for the seals, cormorants and, in early summer, a few puffins. The boats carry WiSe accreditation for responsible wildlife watching. Family fishing trips go out for two or three hours.
