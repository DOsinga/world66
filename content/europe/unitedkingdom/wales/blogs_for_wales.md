Search for a Wales travel blog and nearly everything you find also covers Iceland, Italy or the Lake District. These two write about Wales and nothing else. One is a couple who moved here a decade ago and started writing in 2024. The other is a country magazine published from Ceredigion.

For the blogs that cover a single part of Wales, see the lists on Snowdonia, Anglesey, Cardiff, Swansea and Gower, the Brecon Beacons, Pembrokeshire and Cardigan Bay.
