A sandy beach a mile south-east of Bosherston, where the lily ponds drain to the sea, framed by limestone cliffs and backed by dunes. Church Rock sticks up out of the water about 150 yards offshore. It is good for swimming and bodyboarding, and a great place to finish the walk round the ponds.

The National Trust car park on the clifftop has toilets and, in summer, a food van. Not to be confused with Broad Haven on St Brides Bay, near [Little Haven](/europe/unitedkingdom/wales/little_haven).
