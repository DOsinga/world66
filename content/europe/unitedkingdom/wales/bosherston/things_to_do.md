The essential loop takes in the [lily ponds](/europe/unitedkingdom/wales/bosherston/bosherston_lily_ponds), [Broad Haven South](/europe/unitedkingdom/wales/bosherston/broad_haven_south) and [St Govan's Chapel](/europe/unitedkingdom/wales/bosherston/st_govans_chapel). Add [Barafundle Bay](/europe/unitedkingdom/wales/bosherston/barafundle_bay) along the coast to the east, and, when the range is open, the [Green Bridge of Wales](/europe/unitedkingdom/wales/bosherston/green_bridge_of_wales) to the west.
