An uplift bike park on Mynydd Gethin, above Merthyr Tydfil on the southern edge of the Beacons, about forty minutes from Brecon. A shuttle takes riders and bikes to the top, with trails graded from easy to expert. Bike hire and coaching are available, and there are parent-and-child afternoons.
