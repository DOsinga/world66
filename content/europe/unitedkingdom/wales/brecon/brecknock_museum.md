Brecknock Museum is the town's local history and art stop. It is useful on a wet day, but it also helps connect Brecon's market-town streets with the wider history of Powys and the Usk Valley.

The museum is modest, so treat it as context rather than the centre of the trip. It works best before a cathedral visit or after a walk through the old town.
