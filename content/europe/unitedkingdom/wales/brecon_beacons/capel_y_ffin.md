A hamlet at the head of the Vale of Ewyas, on the narrow road over the Gospel Pass to Hay. Its little whitewashed church of St Mary is one of the smallest in Wales. The artist Eric Gill lived and worked here in the 1920s, and two headstones in the graveyard are carved by him.
