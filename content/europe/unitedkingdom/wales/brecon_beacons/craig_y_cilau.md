A nature reserve on the limestone escarpment above Llangattock, across the Usk from Crickhowell. A disused quarry tramway leads along the cliffs, where rare whitebeam trees grow out of the rock. Under the escarpment runs one of the longest cave systems in Britain, explored by cavers rather than walkers.
