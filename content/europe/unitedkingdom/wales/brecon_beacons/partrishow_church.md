The tiny church of St Issui at Partrishow sits alone on a hillside above the Grwyne Fawr valley, reached by narrow lanes from Crickhowell or Abergavenny. It is known for its fifteenth-century carved oak rood screen and its wall paintings, including a skeleton representing Time, and for the holy well in the dingle below.
