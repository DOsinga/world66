Bike hire run by Antur Waunfawr, a group of social enterprises that employ people with learning disabilities. It also has adaptive bikes. The shop is in the Porth-yr-Aur gate in the town walls. From there the traffic-free **Lôn Eifion** runs twelve miles south and Lôn Las Menai runs along the strait. Open weekdays.
