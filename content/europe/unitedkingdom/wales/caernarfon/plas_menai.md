The national watersports centre, on the Menai Strait just north-east of Caernarfon. Taster sessions and longer courses cover sailing, sea kayaking, paddleboarding, windsurfing and wing-foiling, from age eight. The strait's tides make it an instructive, if sometimes demanding, place to learn.
