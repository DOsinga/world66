---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

Caernarfon is most pleasant from May to September, when the days are long, temperatures sit around 18–22°C, and the Welsh Highland Railway runs at full frequency. July and August are the busiest months — castle queues are longer and parking harder — but the town is still walkable.

April and October are quieter and rewarding for the castle and the walks, with the surrounding Snowdonia hills often clearer than in midsummer. Winter is cold, wet, and often windy off the strait; some attractions close or reduce hours, but the castle remains open year-round and looks at its best in dramatic weather.
