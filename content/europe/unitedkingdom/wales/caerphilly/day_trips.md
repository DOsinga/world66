Caerphilly is on the Rhymney railway line and Cardiff is fifteen minutes away by train — a much easier base for the Welsh capital than most travellers realise, with cheaper hotels and a calmer evening. Castell Coch, the Victorian-Gothic fantasy castle on the wooded hillside above Tongwynlais, is a thirty-minute walk south from Caerphilly Mountain or a short bus ride. The Brecon Beacons begin half an hour north by car and reward a full day's hiking; the Big Pit National Coal Museum at Blaenavon, an hour east, offers a underground tour of a real Welsh colliery that pairs well with a stop at Sultan the Pit Pony on the way.
