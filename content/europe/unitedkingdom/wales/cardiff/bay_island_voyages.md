Fast RIB trips from the blue hut on Mermaid Quay, running since 1999. They range from a fifteen-minute spin around the bay to an hour and a half out through the barrage along the coast. The best of them land on **Flat Holm** and **Steep Holm**, the two islands in the middle of the Bristol Channel.
