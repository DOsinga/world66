Cardiff's best local writing is about the city that visitors walk past: the pubs that have been pouring for centuries, the tiled porches of the terraced streets, the hills on the edge of town with the whole city laid out below. These four are written from inside it, by a Cardiffian historian, a volunteer society, the city's long-running culture magazine and the council's own tourism team.

Read together, they will take you out of the Bay and the castle quarter to Llanishen's medieval inn, the porches of Roath, the top of Garth Hill and the sculpture trail in the woods above Tongwynlais.
