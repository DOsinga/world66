An artificial white-water course at the edge of Cardiff Bay, so the rapids run whatever the weather. Rafting goes from family sessions, from age six, to a two-hour Extreme session for over-twelves. There is also tubing, paddleboarding on the bay, an indoor surf wave, and gorge walking out in Waterfall Country at Pontneddfechan.
