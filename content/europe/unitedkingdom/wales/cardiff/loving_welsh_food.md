Food walking tours of Cardiff's arcades, market and independent producers, led by food presenter Sian Roberts. There are also Taste of Wales evenings and Welshcake-making workshops. The Sunday Times named it one of the seven best city walking tours in the UK.

There is no street address; the meeting point is given on booking, and the pin is on the city centre.
