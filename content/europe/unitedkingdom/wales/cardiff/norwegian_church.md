The Norwegian Church is a small white church in Cardiff Bay, originally built to serve Norwegian sailors working in the docks. It is closely associated with Roald Dahl, whose family worshipped here when he was a child.

Today the building is used as an arts centre and cafe, but it still works as a compact heritage stop on a walk around the bay, especially when paired with the Senedd, Pierhead Building, and waterfront.
