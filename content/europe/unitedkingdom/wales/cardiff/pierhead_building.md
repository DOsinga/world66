The Pierhead Building is the red-brick landmark beside the Senedd and Wales Millennium Centre in Cardiff Bay. Built for the docks, it now hosts exhibitions on Cardiff, the bay, and Welsh civic history, with carved dragons and heraldic detail throughout the exterior.
