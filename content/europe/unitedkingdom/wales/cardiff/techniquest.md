Techniquest is Cardiff Bay's hands-on science discovery centre, with interactive exhibits built for experimentation rather than passive viewing. It is one of the city's most reliable family attractions, especially when the weather turns.

The centre fits naturally into a Cardiff Bay day with the waterfront, Wales Millennium Centre, and the Senedd. Adults without children may treat it as optional, but families can easily spend a substantial chunk of the day here.
