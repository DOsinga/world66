The Cardiff Story is the city's local history museum, housed in the Old Library in the city centre. Exhibits trace Cardiff's growth from a small town into a major port, industrial city, and Welsh capital.

Its central location makes it an easy short stop near Cardiff Market, the arcades, and Cardiff Castle.
