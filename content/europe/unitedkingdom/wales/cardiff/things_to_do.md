title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Cardiff packs a surprising range of sights into a compact city centre, from Roman foundations and Norman keeps to world-class galleries and a transformed waterfront. [Cardiff Castle](/europe/unitedkingdom/wales/cardiff/cardiff_castle) is the obvious starting point, but the [National Museum Cardiff](/europe/unitedkingdom/wales/cardiff/national_museum_cardiff) holds one of the finest Impressionist collections in Britain and deserves a full half-day. Down at [Cardiff Bay](/europe/unitedkingdom/wales/cardiff/cardiff_bay), the [Wales Millennium Centre](/europe/unitedkingdom/wales/cardiff/wales_millennium_centre) and the [Senedd](/europe/unitedkingdom/wales/cardiff/senedd) face each other across the waterfront plaza. The [Victorian arcades](/europe/unitedkingdom/wales/cardiff/cardiff_arcades) running through the city centre are a joy to wander.
