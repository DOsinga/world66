---
title: Getting There
type: section
---

**By train.** Carmarthen station is on the West Wales line, with direct trains from Swansea (50 minutes), Cardiff (90 minutes), London Paddington (just over 3 hours), and onward to Tenby, Pembroke Dock and Fishguard Harbour for the Rosslare ferry. The station is on the south bank of the Tywi, a 10-minute walk over the bridge to the town centre.

**By car.** Carmarthen sits on the A40 trunk road from Brecon and the English border (2 hours from Bristol), and the A48 from Swansea (50 minutes). The town has several large pay-and-display car parks within walking distance of the market and castle.

**By bus.** Carmarthen bus station is in the town centre and is served by frequent local buses across Carmarthenshire and Pembrokeshire, plus TrawsCymru long-distance services to Aberystwyth (T1) and Cardiff (T1C). The journey from Cardiff by bus takes about 3 hours and is significantly cheaper than the train.

There is no nearby airport. Cardiff Airport is 90 minutes east by car; Bristol is the main international hub at 2.5 hours.
