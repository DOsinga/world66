Eirias Park is the town's main green space, set back from the seafront on the eastern side of the centre. The park is a Victorian-era municipal park, restored with a Heritage Lottery grant, and contains a boating lake, a miniature railway in summer, formal gardens and a children's adventure playground.

Eirias Stadium, home to RGC 1404 rugby and an outdoor concert venue, sits at the northern end of the park. Big-name acts play here in summer.
