---
title: "Getting Around"
type: section
---

The town centre, seafront and the promenade towards Rhos-on-Sea are all walkable, and the **North Wales Coast cycle route** runs flat and traffic-free along the seafront all the way to Llandudno and Conwy. Bike hire is available in summer from kiosks near the pier and from Llandudno.

**Local buses** run frequently between Colwyn Bay, Old Colwyn, Rhos-on-Sea and the larger neighbouring towns. Routes 12 and 13 cover the bay area; route 14 runs to the Welsh Mountain Zoo from the railway station (free in summer).

For day trips along the coast, the train is fastest: Llandudno is 10 minutes, Conwy 12 minutes, Bangor 30 minutes.
