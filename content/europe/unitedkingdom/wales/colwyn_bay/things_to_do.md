---
title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Colwyn Bay is a small seaside town, so the list is short, but the mix is unusual: a hillside zoo with free-flying birds of prey, a 1950s puppet theatre, a thousand-year-old hall, a chapel that seats six. Add a walk along the promenade or up Bryn Euryn for the view and you have a full day or two.
