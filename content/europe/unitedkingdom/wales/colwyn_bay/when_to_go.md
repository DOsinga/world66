---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

**Late spring and summer (May to September)** are the obvious months — long daylight, the seafront cafes are open, the Harlequin puppet theatre runs daily shows and the Welsh Mountain Zoo has its full programme of bird displays. July and August are busiest, but Colwyn Bay never gets the holiday crowds of Llandudno or Rhyl.

**September and October** can be lovely on the coast, with mild weather and quiet beaches. The promenade and Bryn Euryn walks are at their best in autumn light.

**Winter** is wet and cold and most seasonal attractions close down, though the zoo stays open all year and the coastal walks remain rewarding if you wrap up. Storms occasionally close the promenade.
