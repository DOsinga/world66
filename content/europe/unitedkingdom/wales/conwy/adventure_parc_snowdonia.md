A big indoor and outdoor activity site at Dolgarrog, up the Conwy valley. Indoors there are caving, climbing walls, a ninja assault course and high ropes. Outside there are gorge walking and mountain biking. It was built around a surf lagoon, but the lagoon is not currently on offer.
