title: Bars and Cafes
type: section
tags: [bars_and_cafes]
---

Conwy's pub and cafe scene is modest but functional. The walled town has a handful of traditional pubs that serve local ales and a scattering of tea rooms aimed at day visitors. The best places to sit are those with views of the castle or the estuary — Conwy is small enough that this is not hard to arrange.
