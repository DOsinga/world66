The Conwy Valley runs south into the heart of Snowdonia, making this one of the best bases for exploring the national park. Betws-y-Coed, a popular walking village at the confluence of four rivers, is 15 miles south on the A5 and reachable by train on the Conwy Valley line. [Snowdonia](/europe/unitedkingdom/wales/snowdonia) itself is within an hour's drive, with Mount Snowdon and a range of other peaks accessible from Llanberis and Capel Curig.

[Caernarfon](/europe/unitedkingdom/wales/caernarfon), with its own massive Edwardian castle and well-preserved town walls, is 20 miles west along the coast — a half-day trip that makes a natural pairing with Conwy. [Bangor](/europe/unitedkingdom/wales/bangor_wales), the small university city on the Menai Strait, is a few miles further and the gateway to Anglesey. Llandudno, the Victorian seaside resort, is just 5 miles east and easy by bus or car.
