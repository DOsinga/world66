Conwy's eating scene punches above its size. Mussels, cockles, and lobster from the Conwy estuary show up on menus across town, and the best places make full use of the local catch. The quayside is the place to start looking — a handful of small restaurants and fish stalls line the waterfront with views across to the castle.
