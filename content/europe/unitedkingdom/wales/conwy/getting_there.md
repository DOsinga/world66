---
title: Getting There
type: section
tags: [getting_there]
---

**By train:** Conwy has its own station on the North Wales Coast Line, with regular services from Chester and Holyhead. The journey from Chester takes around 50 minutes. Llandudno Junction, a few minutes east, has more frequent connections and is the interchange for the Conwy Valley line south toward Betws-y-Coed and Blaenau Ffestiniog.

**By car:** The A55 expressway runs through a tunnel under the town, making it easy to pass Conwy entirely without realising it is there. Turn off at junction 17 or 18. Parking in the walled town is limited; the main car parks are just outside the walls near the castle.

**By bus:** National Express coaches on the London–Holyhead route stop at Llandudno Junction, from which local buses run into Conwy. Several Arriva Wales services connect the town with Llandudno, Bangor, and Rhyl.

**From Ireland:** Stena Line ferries run from Dublin to Holyhead, about 20 miles west of Conwy. It is a natural overnight stop if you are travelling between Ireland and England.
