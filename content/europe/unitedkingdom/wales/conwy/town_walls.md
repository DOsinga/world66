The medieval walls that encircle Conwy's old town are among the most complete in Britain. Unlike most medieval fortifications, which have been patched, absorbed into later buildings, or torn down wholesale, Conwy's walls survive almost intact — 21 of the original 22 towers still stand, and you can walk the full circuit of the battlements and look down into the streets and gardens below.

The walk takes about 30 to 45 minutes at a leisurely pace and gives you a completely different perspective on the town. You climb narrow stone steps, pass through archways, and peer through arrow loops at the same views the garrison would have had in the 1280s. The western section, above the mills, offers the best panoramic views of the castle and estuary. Parts of the wall walk are uneven and exposed to the elements — wear appropriate footwear. Entry to the wall walk is free.
