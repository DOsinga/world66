---
title: When to Go
type: section
tags: [when_to_go]
---

North Wales gets a lot of rain, and Conwy is no exception. The wettest months are October through January; July and August are driest but also busiest, with coach parties queuing for the castle and the quay packed on sunny weekends.

May and June offer the best balance — reasonable weather, fewer crowds, and the hills around the Conwy Valley looking their greenest. September is also a strong choice: summer visitors have thinned out and the light is good for photography.

Winter visits are quiet to the point of deserted. The castle is atmospheric in low mist and empty of tourists, and accommodation prices drop significantly. Just pack waterproofs.
