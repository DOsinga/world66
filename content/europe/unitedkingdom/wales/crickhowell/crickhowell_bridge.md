Crickhowell Bridge carries the road across the River Usk on a graceful run of stone arches. It is often described as the longest stone bridge in Wales, and whether or not you arrive for that claim, it is the town's best low-level viewpoint.

Walk it slowly and look back. The bridge, the river, and Table Mountain behind the town explain Crickhowell faster than any visitor leaflet.
