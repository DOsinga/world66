The National Trust's open headland at the far west end of the Marloes peninsula, just beyond Martin's Haven. A path loops round its cliffs to Wooltack Point, with views across Jack Sound to Skomer and, from the point, over nearly all of St Brides Bay to Ramsey Island. Choughs feed on the short grass, gannets and porpoises work the tide races in the sound, and grey seals pup on the beaches below in late August and September.

It is the closest you can get to the islands without a boat, and a good spot for sunset. The national park's Deer Park and Marloes circuit is 5.2 miles and mostly level.
