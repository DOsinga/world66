The southern tip of the Dale peninsula, where a white lighthouse marks the entrance to the Milford Haven, one of Britain's deep-water harbours. There has been a light here since 1714; the present tower dates from 1844. On a clear day you can see Skokholm lighthouse eight kilometres to the west.

The coast path round the Dale peninsula passes the head and Mill Bay, where Henry Tudor landed in 1485, before returning to the village: a good half-day walk.
