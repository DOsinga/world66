---

Dale itself is a place to sail or sit by the water. The real sights are on the Marloes peninsula to the west: the boat to [Skomer](/europe/unitedkingdom/wales/dale/skomer_island) from Martin's Haven, the cliff walk round the [Deer Park](/europe/unitedkingdom/wales/dale/marloes_deer_park), and [Marloes Sands](/europe/unitedkingdom/wales/dale/marloes_sands). South of the village, the coast path loops round [St Ann's Head](/europe/unitedkingdom/wales/dale/st_anns_head).
