Bwlch Nant yr Arian Forest Visitor Centre is a forest and lake site in the hills east of Aberystwyth. Its best-known draw is the daily red kite feeding, which gives visitors a reliable chance to see the birds close to the visitor centre.

The surrounding trails make it more than a quick wildlife stop. It pairs well with other upland sights around Devil's Bridge and the Rheidol valley.
