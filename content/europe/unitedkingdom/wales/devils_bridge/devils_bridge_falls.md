The falls are the reason Devil's Bridge works as a destination. Paths drop from the bridge into the Mynach gorge, with viewing points over a chain of waterfalls before the river joins the Rheidol below.

The walk is short but not casual. Jacob's Ladder and the lower paths are steep, damp, and slippery in places, so wear proper shoes and take your time. The reward is a close look at one of the most memorable gorge scenes in Wales.
