Hafod Estate sits a short drive from Devil's Bridge in the Ystwyth valley. Its old mansion is gone, but the landscape still carries the shape of a late 18th-century estate: woods, bridges, church, waterfalls, and paths arranged for walking.

It is a quieter counterpoint to the falls at Devil's Bridge. Come here when you want a longer wander, softer scenery, and fewer people.
