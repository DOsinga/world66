title: Things to Do
type: section
---

Devil's Bridge is best treated as a compact walking day. Start with the bridges and falls, then choose between the gentler Hafod Estate paths or the rawer mining landscape around Cwmystwyth if you have a car.

The Vale of Rheidol Railway is listed here as an experience, not just a way in. If you are coming from Aberystwyth, build the day around the train times and leave enough space for the gorge walk.
