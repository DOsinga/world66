Carreg Samson is a 5,000-year-old Neolithic burial chamber near Abercastle and the Pembrokeshire Coast Path. Its large capstone rests on upright stones, marking a tomb that once held many burials.

The site is small, but its age, coastal setting, and easy link with a walk along the coast make it a worthwhile stop for travellers interested in prehistoric Wales.
