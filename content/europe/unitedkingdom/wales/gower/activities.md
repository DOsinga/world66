Gower is Britain's first Area of Outstanding Natural Beauty, a small peninsula of limestone cliffs and big beaches west of Swansea. **Llangennith**, facing west at the far end, is the surf beach. **Rhossili** and Worm's Head are the place for coasteering. **Oxwich Bay**, on the sheltered south coast, has the boats and the paddling.
