The *Sea Serpent* runs from Oxwich Bay west along the cliffs of the Gower coast to **Worm's Head** and back, in about two hours, looking for seals, seabirds and, from May to September, dolphins. The booking office is at the top of the beach, next to Oxwich Watersports.
