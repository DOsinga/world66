Oxwich Bay is one of Gower's broad, sandy beaches, backed by dunes, woods, and the slopes below Penrice Castle. The setting is softer than the cliffs at Rhossili, but the beach is long, open, and easy to enjoy for a walk, picnic, or low-key beach day.

The bay also has a sense of history, with a small Saxon church near the headland and old estate landscapes behind the shore. It works well as a gentler Gower stop when the wind is up on the exposed western coast.
