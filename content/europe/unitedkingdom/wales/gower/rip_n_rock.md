Four-hour **coasteering** sessions on the cliffs at the western tip of Gower, under Rhossili Down and towards Worm's Head, with jumps and sea caves. It also runs canyoning on the River Mellte in Waterfall Country. Sessions meet at the National Trust car park in Rhossili.
