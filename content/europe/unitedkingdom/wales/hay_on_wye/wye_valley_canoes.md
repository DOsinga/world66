The classic half day on the **Wye** starts here: launch at Glasbury and paddle five and a half miles downstream to Hay-on-Wye, where you are collected. Longer trips over several days go as far as Ross-on-Wye. It also hires canoes on the Monmouthshire & Brecon Canal, from outside the theatre in Brecon.
