The Offa's Dyke Centre is the practical and historical starting point for Knighton. Its exhibition explains the dyke, the border, and the long-distance path without making you read a textbook before putting your boots on.

It is also useful in the simple ways walkers care about: maps, local information, coffee, and a clear sense of which section of trail suits the time and weather you have. Go here before heading uphill.
