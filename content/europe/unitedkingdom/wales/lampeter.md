Lampeter is a small university town in [Ceredigion](/europe/unitedkingdom/wales), set inland on the River Teifi. It is not as immediately scenic as the Welsh coast, but it has a strong identity: Welsh-speaking, bookish, old-fashioned, and shaped by one of Britain's oldest higher-education institutions.

The core of the town is the former University of Wales, Lampeter, now part of the [University of Wales Trinity Saint David](/europe/unitedkingdom/wales/lampeter/university_of_wales_trinity_saint_david). Its early 19th-century quadrangle grew over the site of a medieval castle, which neatly sums up Lampeter's layered history. Nearby [St Peter's Church](/europe/unitedkingdom/wales/lampeter/st_peters_church) and the [Lampeter Museum](/europe/unitedkingdom/wales/lampeter/lampeter_museum) add enough local texture for a slow town walk.

The reason Lampeter clears the day-trip bar is what sits just outside it. [Dolaucothi Gold Mines](/europe/unitedkingdom/wales/lampeter/dolaucothi_gold_mines), south-east of town, is one of Wales's most unusual Roman sites, where visitors can go underground into workings tied to Britain's ancient gold industry. Use Lampeter as a quiet base for that, rather than expecting a busy attraction town.
