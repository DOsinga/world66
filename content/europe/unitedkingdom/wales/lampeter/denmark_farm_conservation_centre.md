Denmark Farm Conservation Centre is a countryside and ecology centre north of Lampeter, with courses, wildlife-focused activities, and quiet access to the local landscape. It is most useful for travellers who want more than a quick town walk.

Check the programme before going. When events or courses are running, it adds a practical nature stop to Lampeter's otherwise historical set of attractions.
