Lampeter Museum is a small local-history museum near the university campus. Its strength is not scale, but local focus: it gives context on the town, the university, and the Teifi valley communities around Lampeter.

It is a useful stop before or after walking the campus, especially if you want the town to feel less like a pretty bus stop between Aberystwyth and Carmarthen.
