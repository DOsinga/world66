St Peter's Church stands on the hill above the centre of Lampeter. It is a quiet parish church rather than a showpiece cathedral, but it belongs to the same old townscape as the university, bridge, and Teifi valley.

Visit it as part of a walk through town. The church helps connect Lampeter's Welsh name, Llanbedr Pont Steffan, with the older religious and settlement pattern around the river crossing.
