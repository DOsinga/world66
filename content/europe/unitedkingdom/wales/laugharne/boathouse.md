The Boathouse is the Laugharne address most travellers come for: Dylan Thomas lived here with his family in the last years of his life, looking out over the Taf estuary and working in the nearby writing shed. The setting is half the point, with water, mudflats, birds, and shifting Welsh light all close enough to feel part of the room.

Inside, the museum is small and personal, with photographs, manuscripts, family material, and enough context to connect Thomas to the town without turning the visit into a literature lecture. Pair it with the walk past the castle and St Martin's Churchyard for the fullest sense of Laugharne's Thomas landscape.
