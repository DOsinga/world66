Family stables on a 140-acre farm on the hill above Pendine, with 34 horses. Treks last one or two hours. The highlight is the two-hour **beach ride** down to Marros Sands, for confident riders aged sixteen and over, no more than four at a time. Laugharne is about seven miles east.
