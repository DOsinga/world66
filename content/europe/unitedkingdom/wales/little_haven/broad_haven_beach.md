A wide, west-facing sandy beach in front of the village of Broad Haven, with a Blue Flag. It is popular for surfing, windsurfing and sailing, and safe enough for families. At the north end the cliffs are folded and eroded into natural arches and a stack known locally as Lion Rock.

Not to be confused with Broad Haven South, near [Bosherston](/europe/unitedkingdom/wales/bosherston) on the south coast.
