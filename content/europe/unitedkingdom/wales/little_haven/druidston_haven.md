A sandy beach under cliffs up to 80 metres high, about two miles north of Broad Haven along the coast path. There is no road down to the beach, which keeps it quiet. It is popular with swimmers and surfers who make the effort.

On the hillside above is Malator, an earth-sheltered house built into the slope.
