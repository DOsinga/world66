A small cove with a slipway, shingle at the top and sand lower down, enclosed by sandstone headlands. Boats are launched from here, and the inshore lifeboat for the Havens is stationed nearby.

At low tide you can walk north round the point to a larger bay called the Settlands, then past a second point to Broad Haven. Watch the tide on the way back.
