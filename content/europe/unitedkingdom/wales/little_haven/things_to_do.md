The Havens are about beaches and the coast path. [Broad Haven](/europe/unitedkingdom/wales/little_haven/broad_haven_beach) is the main sandy beach, [Little Haven](/europe/unitedkingdom/wales/little_haven/little_haven_beach) the pretty cove, and [Druidston Haven](/europe/unitedkingdom/wales/little_haven/druidston_haven) the wild one to the north.
