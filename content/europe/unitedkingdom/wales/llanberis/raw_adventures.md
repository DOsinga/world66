A guiding company run by Kate and Ross Worthington since 2010, based in an old school at Brynrefail just north of Llanberis. It leads guided ascents of **Yr Wyddfa** (Snowdon) by the quieter routes, the Welsh 3000s challenge, scrambles on the ridges and gorge walking, and trains Mountain Leaders.
