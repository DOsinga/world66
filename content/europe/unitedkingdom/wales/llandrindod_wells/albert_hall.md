The Albert Hall is a small Victorian theatre, not a grand metropolitan hall. That is its appeal: it shows how seriously Llandrindod Wells once took its role as a spa resort and county town.

Check the programme if you are staying overnight or visiting during a festival. Even from outside, the building adds to the town's period texture around Temple Street and the old hotel quarter.
