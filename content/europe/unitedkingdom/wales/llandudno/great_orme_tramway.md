The Great Orme Tramway is a cable-hauled street tramway that has been running since 1902, making it one of the oldest and most unusual surviving tramways in Britain. It runs from Victoria Station in the centre of Llandudno to the summit of the Great Orme, a journey that passes through residential streets, crosses roads, and eventually emerges onto open hillside for the final climb.

The tramway operates in two sections, with passengers changing cars at the Halfway Station. The lower section runs through the town at walking pace, squeezed between parked cars and garden walls — it feels less like a tourist attraction and more like a piece of living urban infrastructure. The upper section is more dramatic: the car tilts at a steep angle as it climbs the hillside, with views opening up over Llandudno Bay and the mountains of Snowdonia. The ride takes about 20 minutes each way, and a return ticket gets you back down whenever you choose.
