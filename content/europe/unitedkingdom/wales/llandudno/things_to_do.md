title: Things to Do

The [Great Orme](/europe/unitedkingdom/wales/llandudno/great_orme) is the obvious headline — a 207-metre limestone headland with feral Kashmir goats, Bronze Age mines, a country park and one of the best coastal viewpoints in north Wales. The [Great Orme Tramway](/europe/unitedkingdom/wales/llandudno/great_orme_tramway) and the [Llandudno Cable Car](/europe/unitedkingdom/wales/llandudno/cable_car) are both attractions in themselves; the Marine Drive toll road around the cliffs is another good way to see the headland.

[Llandudno Pier](/europe/unitedkingdom/wales/llandudno/llandudno_pier), at the foot of the Great Orme, is one of the most complete surviving Victorian piers in Britain — fully intact, still busy, and a good place to walk on a windy day. The [North Shore Promenade](/europe/unitedkingdom/wales/llandudno/north_shore_promenade) runs from the pier two miles around the bay and is the heart of the resort.

The [Great Orme Mines](/europe/unitedkingdom/wales/llandudno/great_orme_mines), reached from the tramway halfway station, are the largest Bronze Age copper-mining complex in the world open to the public. You wear a hard hat and walk through 3,500-year-old tunnels excavated by miners using bone and antler tools. [Kendrick's Cave](/europe/unitedkingdom/wales/llandudno/kendricks_cave), on the lower slopes of the Orme, was the site of remarkable Upper Palaeolithic finds including engraved deer teeth made into a necklace.

The [Mostyn Gallery](/europe/unitedkingdom/wales/llandudno/mostyn_gallery) on Vaughan Street is Wales's main contemporary art space outside Cardiff. [Venue Cymru](/europe/unitedkingdom/wales/llandudno/venue_cymru) is the main theatre and arena venue. [Bodnant Garden](/europe/unitedkingdom/wales/llandudno/bodnant_garden), 15 minutes south by car, is one of the great gardens of Britain and should not be missed by anyone interested in horticulture.
