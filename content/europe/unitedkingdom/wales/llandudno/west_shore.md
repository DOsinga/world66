West Shore is Llandudno's calmer beach, facing away from the polished Victorian sweep of the North Shore and out across Conwy Bay. It has sand, dunes, and open views toward Anglesey, with the Great Orme rising at one end.

Come here when the main promenade feels too busy or when the weather is right for sunset. It is less about amusements and more about space, wind, and the satisfying feeling of seeing Llandudno from its quieter side.
