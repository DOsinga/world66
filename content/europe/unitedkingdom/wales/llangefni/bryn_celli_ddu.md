Bryn Celli Ddu is one of Anglesey's most atmospheric prehistoric sites, a Neolithic burial chamber built over an earlier henge. The low passage and mound are simple, but the age of the place and its quiet rural setting make it a strong stop for travellers interested in ancient Wales.
