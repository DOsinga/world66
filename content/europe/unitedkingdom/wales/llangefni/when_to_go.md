---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

Anglesey has a mild maritime climate, with rainfall spread fairly evenly through the year. The driest months tend to be May and June. Summer (June to August) brings the most visitors to the island's coasts, though Llangefni itself is never particularly crowded.

Spring and early autumn are good times to visit the town: Oriel Ynys Môn has its full programme running, accommodation elsewhere on the island is easier to find, and the countryside around Llyn Cefni and The Dingle is at its best. The museum is open year-round, making the town a worthwhile stop in any season.
