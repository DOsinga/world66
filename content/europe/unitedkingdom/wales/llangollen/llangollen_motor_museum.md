Llangollen Motor Museum is a small, characterful collection of vintage cars, motorcycles, bicycles, and motoring memorabilia just outside town. It is not a slick national museum; its charm lies in the density of objects and the enthusiasm behind them.

The museum makes a good short stop for anyone driving the Dee Valley or visiting nearby Valle Crucis Abbey. Check opening times before going, as small private museums can be seasonal or limited. For petrolheads, it adds a pleasingly eccentric note to Llangollen's canal, railway, and hillfort circuit.
