---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

The International Musical Eisteddfod in the first week of July fills the town and the surrounding campsite with performers and visitors from around the world. It is worth timing a visit around, but book accommodation many months in advance. The rest of summer is busy but manageable.

Spring and autumn give the best walking conditions in the Dee valley — the hills are clear, the river is running, and the crowded narrowboats of high summer have thinned out. Winter is quiet, with reduced canal boat services, but the valley light on a clear winter morning can be extraordinary.
