Running since 2007 from Bridge Street, by the river. **Dee rafting** takes about two and a half hours through the rapids at Llangollen, from age eight. There is also canyoning for adults, gorge walking, and canoe trips across the Pontcysyllte Aqueduct, 38 metres above the river.
