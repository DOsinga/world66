Llangrannog Beach is the village's natural centre: a sandy cove at the bottom of a narrow valley, with cliffs, old cottages, and a couple of places to eat just behind it. It is small enough to feel busy quickly in summer, but that compactness is part of the charm.

At low tide you can walk around Carreg Bica to Cilborth Beach, the quieter neighbouring strand. At high tide, treat the sea and rocks with respect; the coast here is beautiful because it is not tame.
