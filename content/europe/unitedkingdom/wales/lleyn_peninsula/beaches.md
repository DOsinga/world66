title: Beaches

---

The Llŷn Peninsula has some of the finest beaches in Wales, many of them owned and protected by the National Trust. The north and south coasts have very different characters: the north is more exposed and dramatic, the south more sheltered and suited to swimming and sailing.

[Porth Oer](/europe/unitedkingdom/wales/lleyn_peninsula/porth_oer) (Whistling Sands) on the north coast is the most famous beach on the peninsula — a small arc of pale sand that literally squeaks underfoot. [Porth Iago](/europe/unitedkingdom/wales/lleyn_peninsula/porth_iago) and [Porth Colmon](/europe/unitedkingdom/wales/lleyn_peninsula/porth_colmon) are less visited north coast bays worth seeking out. On the south coast, **Abersoch** is the sailing resort, popular with families and watersports enthusiasts. **Porth Ysgo** is a secluded cove reached by a footpath down a cliff — one of the least visited and most beautiful beaches on the peninsula.
