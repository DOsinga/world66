Porth Iago is a small, sheltered bay on the north coast of the Llŷn Peninsula, reached by a single-track lane and a short walk down to the beach. It is National Trust land and sees far fewer visitors than Porth Oer to the west, making it a good alternative on busy summer days. The sand is clean and the water clear. There are no facilities.
