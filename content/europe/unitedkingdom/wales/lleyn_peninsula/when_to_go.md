---
title: When to Go
type: section
---

**July and August** are the peak months. The beaches fill up, accommodation needs to be booked weeks or months in advance, and the roads on the peninsula — especially the narrow lanes to the National Trust car parks — can be slow. That said, the weather is at its best and boat trips to Bardsey Island run reliably.

**May, June, and September** are the best times to visit. The weather is decent, the crowds are manageable, and the countryside is at its most vivid. June is particularly good — long days, wildflowers on the headlands, and the gorse still in bloom. September brings quieter beaches and often surprisingly warm water after a summer of sun.

**Winter** is not unpleasant but many seasonal businesses close, boat trips to Bardsey stop entirely, and the peninsula's intrinsic character — windswept headlands, empty beaches, a strong sense of the edge of the world — is at its most pronounced. If you want Porth Oer to yourself, come in November.

The Llŷn Peninsula is one of the sunniest parts of Wales — it protrudes into the Irish Sea and catches weather from multiple directions, but it also gets more sunshine hours than much of North Wales. Rain is always possible.
