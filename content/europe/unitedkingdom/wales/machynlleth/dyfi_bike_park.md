Downhill rider Dan Atherton's bike park covers about 650 acres of the Dyfi Forest north of Machynlleth, with a Land Rover uplift and trails from red to triple-black. Day passes and coaching are available. It is a place for capable riders, and there is no phone line; book online or by email.
