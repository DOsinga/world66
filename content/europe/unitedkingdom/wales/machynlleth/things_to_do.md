title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

The [Owain Glyndŵr Parliament House](/europe/unitedkingdom/wales/machynlleth/owain_glyndwr_parliament_house) in the town centre has a small museum covering Wales's most significant medieval uprising — it was here in 1404 that Glyndŵr held the first true Welsh parliament. The town's clock tower and Wednesday market complete the compact historic core.

The [Centre for Alternative Technology](/europe/unitedkingdom/wales/machynlleth/centre_for_alternative_technology), a couple of miles north up the Dyfi valley, is the main draw for most visitors. A working sustainable community since 1974, it demonstrates renewable energy, organic growing, and ecological building on a working site with interactive exhibits. The water-balanced funicular railway alone is worth the visit. Allow at least half a day.

[Cors Dyfi](/europe/unitedkingdom/wales/machynlleth/cors_dyfi) nature reserve, 5 km south of town on the Dyfi estuary, is one of Wales's best wildlife sites and home to a pair of ospreys that returned to nest in 2011 — the first wild breeding pair in Wales in 400 years. The viewing platform and bog boardwalk are accessible from the visitor centre.

[Cadair Idris](/europe/unitedkingdom/wales/machynlleth/cadair_idris), Wales's second-highest peak, is 15 km south and makes for a fine full-day mountain walk. The [Mach Loop](/europe/unitedkingdom/wales/machynlleth/mach_loop) — the military low-level flying route through the valleys east of town — draws aviation enthusiasts on days when RAF jets are training. The [Bron Yr Aur](/europe/unitedkingdom/wales/machynlleth/bron_yr_aur) cottage, where Led Zeppelin wrote much of their 1970 acoustic material, is a short walk from the town into the hills.

The Cambrian rail line connects Machynlleth to [Aberystwyth](/europe/unitedkingdom/wales/aberystwyth) to the west and Shrewsbury to the east, making the town an easy stop on a longer Welsh itinerary.
