A Neolithic cromlech on the high ground above the south side of Manorbier Bay. The capstone, four metres by two and a half, rests partly on two uprights and partly on the slope; a third upright has fallen. The chamber is partly sunk into the ground.

The coast path passes right by it, ten minutes from the beach. It is a scheduled monument and free to visit.
