Everything is within ten minutes' walk: [Manorbier Castle](/europe/unitedkingdom/wales/manorbier/manorbier_castle), the [beach](/europe/unitedkingdom/wales/manorbier/manorbier_beach), [St James's Church](/europe/unitedkingdom/wales/manorbier/st_james_church_manorbier) and the cromlech of [King's Quoit](/europe/unitedkingdom/wales/manorbier/kings_quoit). The national park publishes four short walks from the beach car park.
