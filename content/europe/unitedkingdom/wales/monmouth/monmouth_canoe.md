Canoe and kayak hire and guided trips on the lower **Wye**, with put-ins from far upstream at Glasbury down to Tintern Abbey and Chepstow. The stretch below Monmouth runs through the steep wooded gorge of the Wye Valley. River trips start at Easter. It also has camping pods.
