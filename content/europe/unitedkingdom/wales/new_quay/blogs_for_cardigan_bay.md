Cardigan Bay's independent writing is mostly about one thing, the coast path, and the best of it comes from a single local site that has documented every stretch since 2000. The county's own guide adds the wildlife: why New Quay is the place for the dolphins, and where to watch the seals from the cliff top.

Both point you south of New Quay, to the cove at Cwmtydu and the seabird cliffs at Bird Rock.
