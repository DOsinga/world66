The trips that pay for the dolphin research. They carry no more than twelve passengers and record what they see for the Wildlife Trust's long-running survey of the Cardigan Bay dolphins, with a hydrophone on board to listen in. Bookings go through the **Cardigan Bay Marine Wildlife Centre**, on the slipway next to the lifeboat station, which opened in 1996 and is free to visit from spring to autumn.

For the boat in the evenings, the number is +44 7796 135490.
