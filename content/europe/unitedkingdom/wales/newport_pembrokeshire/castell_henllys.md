Castell Henllys is an Iron Age hill fort in the Pembrokeshire Coast National Park, rebuilt with roundhouses and a granary on their original foundations. The reconstruction makes the site much easier to understand than a bare earthwork.

It is especially good with children, but adults interested in early Welsh history will get plenty from it too. The setting is wooded and quiet, with paths around the fort and regular interpretation during open days.
