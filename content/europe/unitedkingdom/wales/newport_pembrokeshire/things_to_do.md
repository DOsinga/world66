type: section
---

Newport's walks go up or along. Up is [Carningli](/europe/unitedkingdom/wales/newport_pembrokeshire/carningli), the hill behind the town, with an Iron Age fort and a view over the whole of Newport Bay. Along is the coast path, past the old port at the [Parrog](/europe/unitedkingdom/wales/newport_pembrokeshire/parrog) or across to [Newport Sands](/europe/unitedkingdom/wales/newport_pembrokeshire/newport_sands). In town, look for the Neolithic dolmen of [Carreg Coetan Arthur](/europe/unitedkingdom/wales/newport_pembrokeshire/carreg_coetan_arthur). The great dolmen of [Pentre Ifan](/europe/unitedkingdom/wales/fishguard/pentre_ifan) is a short drive away.
