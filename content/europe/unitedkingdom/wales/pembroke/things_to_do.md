---
title: Things to Do
type: section
---

Give [Pembroke Castle](/europe/unitedkingdom/wales/pembroke/pembroke_castle) half a day, including the free guided tour. Then walk the millpond and the surviving [town walls](/europe/unitedkingdom/wales/pembroke/pembroke_town_walls), and cross the river to [Monkton Priory](/europe/unitedkingdom/wales/pembroke/monkton_priory). [Lamphey Bishop's Palace](/europe/unitedkingdom/wales/pembroke/lamphey_bishops_palace) is an easy detour on the way to the coast.
