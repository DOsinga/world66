A family business since 2007, running two RIBs out of the Teifi estuary at St Dogmaels, near Cardigan, into Cardigan Bay. Trips range from an hour along the coast to a four-hour offshore run looking for dolphins, seals and porpoises. The season runs from April to October. Enquiries go through the website or by phone.
