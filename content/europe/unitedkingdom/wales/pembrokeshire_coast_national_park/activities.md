The national park is a coastline, not a block of land, and its activities are on and around the water. St Davids is the hub in the north-west, Tenby and Saundersfoot in the south, and Martin's Haven is the departure point for Skomer. The **Pembrokeshire Coast Path** links them all, 186 miles from Amroth to St Dogmaels. Seabird season on the islands runs from April to July; the seals pup in autumn.
