An old slate quarry on the north Pembrokeshire coast, flooded when the seaward wall was breached and now a deep pool of startlingly coloured water, ringed by the ruins of quarry buildings. It is used for coasteering and diving; swimming here needs care. It is a short walk north from the beach at Abereiddi.
