An adventure company on the quay at Cardigan, running since 2011 and part of Adventure Beyond, which dates from 1997. Its sea kayak tours poke into the caves along the coast, the canoe trip goes up the **Teifi gorge**, and it also offers coasteering, paddleboarding and rafting.
