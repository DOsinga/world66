A small sheltered cove on the east side of Dinas Island, between Fishguard and Newport. Above the beach stands the ruined west wall and belfry of St Brynach's church, wrecked in the great storm of 1859. The coast path around Dinas Island links it to Pwllgwaelod on the other side.
