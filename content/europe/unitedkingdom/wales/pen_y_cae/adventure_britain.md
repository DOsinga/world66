The limestone along the southern edge of the Beacons is riddled with caves. This company takes groups underground in **Porth yr Ogof**, where the Mellte disappears into the hill, as well as Bridge Cave and the Little Neath River cave, from age seven. It also runs canyoning and gorge walking down the waterfalls. The base is at Seven Sisters, in the next valley south of Pen y Cae.
