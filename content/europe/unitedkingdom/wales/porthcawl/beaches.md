Porthcawl has three main beaches and they could not be more different.

[Rest Bay](/europe/unitedkingdom/wales/porthcawl/rest_bay), at the north end, is the surfer's beach — west-facing, Blue Flag, with reliable Atlantic swell at the rocky points. [Sandy Bay](/europe/unitedkingdom/wales/porthcawl/sandy_bay) is the family beach in the centre of town, gently shelving and right next to the funfair. [Trecco Bay](/europe/unitedkingdom/wales/porthcawl/trecco_bay) to the east is the longest of the three, backed by the giant caravan park and merging at low tide with Newton Beach.

For a quieter walk, follow the cliff path north from Rest Bay toward Sker Point and Kenfig dunes — within twenty minutes you can be on a beach with no one on it.
