The Portmeirion Dog Cemetery is a small estate curiosity rather than a major sight, but it fits the place perfectly. Its little memorials add a quieter, odder note to a village that can otherwise feel like pure design.

Look for it while walking the estate paths. It will not take long, but it gives Portmeirion one more human detail behind the careful architecture.
