A wide sandy beach that runs south from the harbour wall, with a Blue Flag. It is the village's main asset and less crowded than the beaches in Tenby.
North of the harbour, past the headland, is Coppet Hall, a smaller Blue Flag beach and the start of the walk through the tunnels to Wiseman's Bridge.
