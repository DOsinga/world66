Saundersfoot is a beach village first. The [beach](/europe/unitedkingdom/wales/saundersfoot/saundersfoot_beach) and the [harbour](/europe/unitedkingdom/wales/saundersfoot/saundersfoot_harbour) sit side by side in the middle of the village, and the best short walk heads north along the old coal tramway through the [tunnels to Wiseman's Bridge](/europe/unitedkingdom/wales/saundersfoot/saundersfoot_tunnels). [Colby Woodland Garden](/europe/unitedkingdom/wales/saundersfoot/colby_woodland_garden) is a short drive inland, and [Tenby](/europe/unitedkingdom/wales/tenby) is three miles south.
