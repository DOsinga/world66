Eryri is the busiest adventure country in Britain, and its operators are spread across its towns. **Llanberis** is the base for Yr Wyddfa and the lake. **Betws-y-Coed** and Capel Curig cover the eastern mountains and the forests. **Blaenau Ffestiniog** has turned its slate quarries into zip lines, mines and bike trails. **Bala** has the white water. Guided days on Yr Wyddfa, Tryfan and the Welsh 3000s are worth booking if you are not confident with a map in cloud, which is the usual weather.
