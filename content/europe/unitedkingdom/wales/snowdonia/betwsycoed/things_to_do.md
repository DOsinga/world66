---
title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Betws-y-Coed is a base, not a destination, but the village itself has enough to fill a rainy half-day. Walks fan out in every direction — short loops to waterfalls, longer climbs into the Gwydyr Forest, gentle riverside strolls. Outdoor shops cluster around the high street for last-minute boots and waterproofs.
