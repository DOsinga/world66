---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

Snowdonia gets weather of every kind in every season. May and June are usually the driest and brightest months, with long days for big walks. September can be just as good and less crowded. July and August see the most visitors — the village fills with coaches and the falls car parks back up.

Winter is quiet but the higher walks need real mountain experience and proper equipment. The lower trails through Gwydyr Forest and the riverside paths stay walkable year-round, and the village's pubs and tea-rooms come into their own when the wind is up.
