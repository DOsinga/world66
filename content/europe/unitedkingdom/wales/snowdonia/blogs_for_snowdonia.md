Most of what is written about Eryri is about the same three paths up Yr Wyddfa. The local writing is about the rest: the quieter summits, the valleys with no through road, the river pools you only find by wandering off the path. There is not much of it. These two are the honest number of independent, Snowdonia-only voices we could find that are still writing.

Between them they will take you to Marchlyn Mawr above Deiniolen, along the Nantlle ridge and into Cwm Pennant, places that never make the headline lists.
