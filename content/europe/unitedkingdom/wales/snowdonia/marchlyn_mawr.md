A reservoir in a deep cwm under Elidir Fawr, a few miles north of Llanberis. It is the upper lake of the Dinorwig pumped-storage power station, whose turbines are hidden inside the mountain, but from the path it looks like any other high mountain lake. A loop from the Deiniolen side climbs to it and around it, and sees a fraction of the people on the Llanberis paths across the valley.
