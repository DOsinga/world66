A long, narrow ridge running west from Rhyd Ddu above the Nantlle valley, with views across to Yr Wyddfa and down to the sea. Along the way is the **Jubilee Tower** on Mynydd Tal-y-mignedd, a stone pillar built for Queen Victoria's jubilee that looks like an outsized trig point. The full traverse is a long day and needs transport at the far end; shorter out-and-back walks from Rhyd Ddu work well too.
