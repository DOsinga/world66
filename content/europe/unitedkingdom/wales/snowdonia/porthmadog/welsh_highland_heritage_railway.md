The Welsh Highland Heritage Railway is the smaller, hands-on railway stop in Porthmadog. It suits travellers who want a shorter visit than the full Ffestiniog or Welsh Highland journey, with engines, sheds, and railway history close to town.

It is a selective sight rather than the main reason to come, but it rounds out Porthmadog's unusual concentration of railway heritage.
