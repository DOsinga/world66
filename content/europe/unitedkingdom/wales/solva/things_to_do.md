---
title: Things to Do
type: section
---

Start at the [harbour](/europe/unitedkingdom/wales/solva/solva_harbour) and walk out along it, then climb [the Gribin](/europe/unitedkingdom/wales/solva/the_gribin) for the view. The national park publishes three short walks of about a mile each from the harbour car park. Inland, [Solva Woollen Mill](/europe/unitedkingdom/wales/solva/solva_woollen_mill) is a working mill you can watch at work.
