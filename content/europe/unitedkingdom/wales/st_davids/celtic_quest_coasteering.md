A coasteering company running since 2008, meeting on the beach at **Abereiddy**, a few miles north-east of St Davids. Sessions explore the cliffs around the **Blue Lagoon**, a flooded slate quarry open to the sea. They run twice a day from March to October, with winter sessions too, from age eight. There is no office; you meet on the beach.
