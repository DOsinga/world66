On the cliffs south of St Davids, the ruined walls of a small chapel mark the place where, by tradition, St Non gave birth to St David in the sixth century. Nearby is a holy well under a stone grotto, said to have sprung up that night. A short coast-path walk links it to the little harbour at Porthclais and the beach at Caerfai.
