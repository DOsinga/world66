Running since 1975, this is the operator with the landing rights for **Ramsey Island**, so it is the one to book if you want to walk the RSPB reserve rather than look at it from the sea. Landings run Sunday to Thursday through the season. It also runs trips around the island, and from Fishguard harbour out to Strumble Head, where dolphins and porpoises are common.
