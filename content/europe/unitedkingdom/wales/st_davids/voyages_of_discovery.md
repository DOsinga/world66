Boats circle **Ramsey Island** for its seals, sea caves and seabird cliffs, through the tide race of Ramsey Sound. Longer trips go out to **Grassholm**, whose gannet colony turns the island white, and along the coast to Strumble Head. The company has been running for thirty years, with its booking office on the High Street.
