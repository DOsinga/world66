---
title: "Bars and Cafes"
type: section
---

Swansea's drinking scene splits roughly in three. **Wind Street** is the loud, late, weekend strip — chain pubs, vodka bars and queues for nightclubs. The **Uplands** has a quieter, more student- and local-feeling cluster of pubs along Uplands Crescent, with live music and quiz nights. **Mumbles**, out along the bay, has the most relaxed scene — seafront pubs and gastropubs facing the water.

For coffee, the city has been catching up with the rest of the UK over the past few years. The historic [Kardomah Cafe](/europe/unitedkingdom/wales/swansea/kardomah_cafe) is a Swansea institution. The Uplands has several good independents along Uplands Crescent. Mumbles has a long line of cafes facing the sea; come on a sunny morning with a paper.

Try **Brains** Welsh ale on draught, and look for whisky from the Penderyn distillery up in the Brecon Beacons. The local craft brewery, **Tomos Watkin**, makes a good pint of Cwrw Haf.
