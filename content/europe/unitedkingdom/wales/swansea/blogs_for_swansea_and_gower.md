Gower is small, and the people who write about it best live on it. Here are a village website written by Rhossili's residents, a Swansea designer ticking off every beach on the peninsula one by one, a pair of Mumbles locals who built a community hub because they kept missing village events, and the council's own tourism team.

What they agree on is that Gower's best places are the small ones: a hidden cove reached by a long walk, a rock-pooling bay round the headland, neolithic tombs on the down above the most famous beach in Wales.
