The Egypt Centre, on the Swansea University campus, holds one of the strongest collections of ancient Egyptian objects in the United Kingdom outside London. It is a compact museum, but the artefacts, handling sessions, and volunteer interpretation make it a worthwhile stop for families and archaeology-minded visitors.
