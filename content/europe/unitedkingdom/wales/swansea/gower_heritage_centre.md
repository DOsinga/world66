Gower Heritage Centre occupies a former water-powered corn and saw mill at Parkmill, just inland from some of the Gower Peninsula's best-known beaches. It presents rural crafts, local history, milling, and family activities in a setting that still feels tied to the valley around it.

For Swansea visitors, the centre is a good wet-weather or family stop on a Gower day out. It is not as dramatic as Rhossili or Three Cliffs Bay, but it explains the working countryside behind the scenery. Combine it with a walk to nearby archaeological sites or beaches if you have transport.
