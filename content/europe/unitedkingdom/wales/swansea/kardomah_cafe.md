The Kardomah is a 1950s-style cafe with formica tables, dark wood panelling and a menu of teas, coffees, scones, sausage rolls and full Welsh breakfasts at very gentle prices. The crowd is mostly local and older, but younger fans of Dylan Thomas turn up too.

It is the last branch of what was once a chain of more than fifty Kardomah Coffee Houses across Britain. As a piece of mid-century cafe culture it is hard to beat; come for breakfast, take a window seat, and watch Swansea go by.
