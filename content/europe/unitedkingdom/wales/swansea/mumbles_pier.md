Mumbles Pier is a Victorian seaside pier at the western end of Swansea Bay. Built in the 1880s to bring visitors to Mumbles by rail, it remains one of the area's classic coastal landmarks, with views back across the bay and out toward the lifeboat station and lighthouse.
