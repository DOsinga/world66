A cave in the limestone cliffs between Rhossili and Port Eynon where, in 1823, a human skeleton stained with red ochre was found: the 'Red Lady of Paviland', in fact a young man buried around 33,000 years ago. It can only be reached from the beach at the lowest tides of the year, so for most visitors it is a view from the cliff top.
