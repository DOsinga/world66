---
title: "Shopping"
type: section
---

Swansea's shopping is concentrated in the city centre. The undercover **Quadrant Shopping Centre** and the adjacent **St David's Shopping Centre** between them carry most of the high-street chains. The historic **Swansea Market** inside the Quadrant is the place to buy laverbread, Penclawdd cockles and Welsh cakes from independent stallholders — a genuinely good market by UK standards.

For independents, **Wind Street** and the **High Street** have a scatter of vintage shops, record stores and small galleries. The **Mumbles** village to the west, along the bayfront tram-and-bus route, has more boutique and gift shops than the centre and is a pleasant browse on a fine afternoon.
