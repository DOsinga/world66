---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

Swansea's climate is mild, wet and changeable — typical of the Welsh coast. Average summer highs are around 19–21 °C, average winter highs around 8 °C, and rain is a regular possibility in any month. The Gower Peninsula gets a little less rainfall than the inland valleys.

**Summer (June–August)** is the obvious season. The promenades along Swansea Bay come alive, the Gower beaches are at their best, and the Mumbles is a perfect Sunday afternoon. Expect crowds at Rhossili on hot weekends.

**Spring (April–May)** is good for the gardens — Clyne in particular is spectacular when the rhododendrons bloom. **Autumn** is wet but atmospheric, with mist over the bay. **Winter** is quiet and the Christmas market in Castle Square has a small but cheerful run.

The **Dylan Thomas Festival** in late October and early November is the main literary event of the year. The **Swansea International Festival** in October brings classical music and theatre to venues across the city.
