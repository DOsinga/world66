A proper old town-centre pub, dark wood, fireplaces, decent ale and a friendly crowd. The Coach and Horses has been pulling pints on Upper Frog Street for centuries and is the place locals decamp to when the harbour-side bars get over-run by holidaymakers. Good for a quiet pint, less so for a quiet conversation on a Saturday night.
