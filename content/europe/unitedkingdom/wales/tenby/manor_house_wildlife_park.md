Manor House Wildlife Park is one of the main family attractions around Tenby, with animal enclosures, play areas, and a conservation-minded presentation that makes it more than a simple petting zoo. Its countryside setting also gives visitors a break from the beaches and harbour crowds.

It is best suited to families or travelers spending several days in Pembrokeshire. For a short Tenby visit the coast should come first, but the park is a solid wet-weather or child-friendly alternative.
