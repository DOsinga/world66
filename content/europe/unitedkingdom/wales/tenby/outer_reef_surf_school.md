An AALA-licensed school running since 1999, with surf lessons at **Freshwater West**, Pembrokeshire's most powerful surf beach, at Manorbier and at Newgale. It also teaches paddleboarding, kayaking and coasteering. It has bases in Tenby, Saundersfoot harbour and Pembroke, where the head office is.
