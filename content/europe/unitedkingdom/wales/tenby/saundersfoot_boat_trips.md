A family business at Saundersfoot, three miles north of Tenby. *Ebony May*, a wheelchair-accessible catamaran, goes out for fishing; *Lady Sian* does mackerel trips; and the RIB *Matani* runs sea safaris to the seals around Caldey. Skipper Stuart Denman has fished these waters for thirty years.
