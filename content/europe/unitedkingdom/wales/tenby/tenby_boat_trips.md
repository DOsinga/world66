Hour-long trips from the harbour around **Caldey** and St Margaret's Island, where grey seals haul out on the rocks. They run on a waterjet boat, the catamaran *Island Ranger*, or faster on a RIB. Its sister business, Tenby Sea Fishing (+44 7837 405816), takes the catamaran *Triton* out for family mackerel trips and longer sport fishing.
