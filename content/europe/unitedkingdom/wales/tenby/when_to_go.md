---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

Tenby is a summer town and most things — boat trips, the Tudor Merchant's House, many of the restaurants — operate from Easter to October. July and August are the busiest months; the harbour is packed, restaurants book out, and accommodation prices double.

May, June and September are the sweet spot: warm enough for the beach, dry enough for the coast path, but with room to breathe. Out of season, particularly in February and March, the town hibernates — many places close, but the Pembrokeshire Coast Path walks are at their best when the cliffs are empty and the gorse is in flower.
