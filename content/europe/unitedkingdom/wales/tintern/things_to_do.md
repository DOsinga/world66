title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Tintern is compact, but the best sights are strong. [Tintern Abbey](/europe/unitedkingdom/wales/tintern/tintern_abbey) is the essential visit, while [The Devil's Pulpit](/europe/unitedkingdom/wales/tintern/the_devils_pulpit) gives the classic high view back over the abbey and Wye Valley. For an easier walk, follow the [Wye Valley Greenway](/europe/unitedkingdom/wales/tintern/wye_valley_greenway) or stop at [The Old Station](/europe/unitedkingdom/wales/tintern/the_old_station), a former railway station turned countryside visitor site.
