Usk is a small market town on the River Usk, set between Newport and Abergavenny. It is not a full-day blockbuster, but it has enough history packed into a gentle walk to justify a detour if you are already in Monmouthshire.

The town's strongest sight is [Usk Castle](/europe/unitedkingdom/wales/usk/usk_castle), a Norman ruin above the centre with gardens and views over the streets below. Back in town, the [Priory Church of St Mary](/europe/unitedkingdom/wales/usk/priory_church_of_st_mary) keeps the memory of a medieval Benedictine nunnery, while the [Usk Rural Life Museum](/europe/unitedkingdom/wales/usk/usk_rural_life_museum) gives the place a grounded Welsh Borders texture.

Come for a slow morning rather than a packed itinerary. Usk's appeal is its scale: castle stones, churchyard, river, old shopfronts, and enough local history to make the town feel like more than a pretty stop between larger places.
