Raglan Castle is a substantial medieval castle ruin near Usk, with enough towers, walls, and courtyards remaining to make the layout easy to read. Its scale and setting make it one of the stronger castle visits in Monmouthshire.

The castle was also used as a filming location for the BBC series Merlin.
