Passetto di Borgo is the fortified corridor linking the Vatican with Castel Sant'Angelo. It was built as an escape route for popes and is best known for its role during the 1527 Sack of Rome.

Much of the corridor can be seen from the street while walking between St. Peter's and Castel Sant'Angelo. Access inside is limited, but the structure adds useful context to the Vatican's defensive history.
