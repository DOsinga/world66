Four rooms in the papal apartments, decorated by Raphael and his workshop between 1508 and 1524 — the same years Michelangelo was painting the Sistine ceiling next door. The most famous of the rooms is the Stanza della Segnatura, which contains *The School of Athens*, *The Disputation of the Holy Sacrament*, *The Parnassus*, and *The Cardinal Virtues*.

*The School of Athens* is the great image of Renaissance humanism: every important philosopher of antiquity assembled in a single architectural fantasy, with Plato and Aristotle at the centre. Plato carries the Timaeus and points upward to the world of ideas; Aristotle carries the Ethics and points level to the world of experience. Raphael painted himself into the crowd at the right edge.

The rooms come midway through the standard Vatican Museums route. Photography is allowed; expect crowds.
