The Studio del Mosaico is one of the oldest continuously operating workshops in Europe. It was founded in the 16th century to create the mosaic copies of altarpieces in St. Peter's — the paintings inside the basilica are not paintings at all, but mosaics, made because paint was found to deteriorate quickly in the basilica's humidity.

The studio still produces mosaics for restoration of the basilica's altars and creates the small mosaic portraits of saints, papal coats of arms, and gifts that the Vatican presents to foreign dignitaries and visiting heads of state. The artisans work with a palette of over 28,000 colour shades of glass tesserae, cut to size with a tool called a tagliolo.

The workshop is not open to the public on standard tickets but can be visited as part of specialised guided tours bookable through the Vatican Museums. The visit is short and intimate; you watch mosaicists working on commissions in progress.
