English Harbour is a historic port on the southern coast of [Antigua](/northamerica/antiguaandbarbuda), best known as the site of [Nelson's Dockyard](/northamerica/antiguaandbarbuda/englishharbour/nelsons_dockyard) — the only continuously working Georgian dockyard in the world. The restored naval base, where Horatio Nelson was stationed in the 1780s, is now a UNESCO World Heritage Site and the heart of Antigua's yachting scene.

The dockyard buildings house restaurants, shops and the [Dockyard Museum](/northamerica/antiguaandbarbuda/englishharbour/dockyard_museum), but the real attraction is the setting — stone warehouses and sail lofts arranged around a sheltered harbour, with yachts and historic vessels moored at the quay. [Shirley Heights](/northamerica/antiguaandbarbuda/englishharbour/shirley_heights), the ridge above the harbour, offers panoramic views and hosts a famous Sunday afternoon barbecue with steel band music.

English Harbour and neighbouring Falmouth Harbour together form the centre of sailing in the Eastern Caribbean. [Antigua Sailing Week](/northamerica/antiguaandbarbuda/englishharbour/antigua_sailing_week), held every April, draws racing crews from around the world. [Galleon Beach](/northamerica/antiguaandbarbuda/englishharbour/galleon_beach) and [Pigeon Point Beach](/northamerica/antiguaandbarbuda/englishharbour/pigeon_point_beach) provide the swimming and snorkelling that fills the gaps between history.
