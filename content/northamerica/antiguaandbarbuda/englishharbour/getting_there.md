title: Getting There
type: section
---

English Harbour is at the southern tip of Antigua, about 25 km from the capital St John's. Taxis from the airport take approximately 40 minutes and cost around EC$80. There is no direct public bus service; buses run to Falmouth village nearby, from where it is a short taxi ride.

Rental cars are the most practical option for independent travellers who want to explore beyond the dockyard. Most of the island's rental companies operate from the airport. The road south from St John's via All Saints is direct and well-signed.

For visitors arriving by yacht, Nelson's Dockyard is the official port of entry for yachts arriving in Antigua; customs and immigration can be cleared at the dock.
