---
title: "Bars and Cafes"
type: section
---

St. John's bar scene is unpretentious and fun. Rum is the default drink — try Cavalier or English Harbour, both local brands. Wadadli lager is the local beer. The action concentrates around Redcliffe Quay in the early evening, and moves to the clubs along Nevis Street and the waterfront later at night.
