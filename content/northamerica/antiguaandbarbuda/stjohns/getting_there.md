---
title: "Getting There"
type: section
---

VC Bird International Airport is about 8 kilometres northeast of St. John's and has direct flights from London (British Airways), New York, Toronto, and various Caribbean islands. A taxi from the airport to town takes around 20 minutes and costs roughly EC$45–55. There is no bus directly between the airport and the city centre.

By sea, St. John's Harbour receives cruise ships and also has ferry connections to [Barbuda](/northamerica/antiguaandbarbuda/barbuda). Charter flights connect Antigua to smaller Eastern Caribbean islands. There is no rail service.
