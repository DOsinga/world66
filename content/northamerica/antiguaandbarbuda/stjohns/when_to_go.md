---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

Antigua has one of the sunniest climates in the Caribbean, averaging about 275 days of sunshine per year. The best time to visit is from December to April — the dry season, with lower humidity, cooler temperatures (around 26°C), and reliable sunshine. This is also the peak tourist season, so accommodation prices are higher and the cruise ship crowds are at their largest.

The rainy season runs from June to November, with September and October seeing the highest rainfall and the peak of hurricane season. Rain comes in short, sharp bursts rather than all-day downpours, and prices drop significantly. Sailing Week in late April/early May is one of the Caribbean's great yachting events and fills the island with boats and crew.
