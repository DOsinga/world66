Tahiti Beach is a shallow, clear-water beach near Hope Town on Elbow Cay. At low tide the sandbars and calm water make it especially good for families and easy swimming.

Most visitors arrive by golf cart or taxi from Hope Town. Bring shade and water, as the beach is more about the water and view than built-up facilities.
