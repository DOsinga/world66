Bimini is about big animals: great hammerheads off South Bimini in winter, wild Atlantic spotted dolphins on the banks, and the lemon sharks studied by the [Shark Lab](/northamerica/bahamas/bimini/bimini_biological_station), which takes visitors by appointment. The concrete hull of the **SS Sapona** is the classic snorkel.
