Bimini's big dive operation, built around the animals the island is famous for. In winter the draw is the **great hammerheads** that come in close to South Bimini; the rest of the year there are the wild spotted dolphins on the banks, the concrete wreck of the **SS Sapona**, and stingrays at Honeymoon Harbour. Longer expeditions go to Tiger Beach and for oceanic whitetips. Snorkellers are catered for on most trips.

There is no street address; the pin is on Alice Town.
