---
title: "Eating Out"
type: section
---

Food in Bimini is conch, fresh fish, and not much else — and most of the time that is exactly what you want. Cracked conch, conch salad cut on the spot at small stands along the marina, grilled grouper and snapper from the morning's catch, peas and rice on the side. Joe's Conch Shack and Stuart's are the two informal classics for conch; the Big Game Club marina restaurant covers the more sit-down end.
