title: Activities
type: section
---

Eleuthera is long and thin, with the operators spread along it. In the north, the boats for **Spanish Wells** and Harbour Island leave from the mainland docks at Genes Bay and Three Island Dock. In the south, trips go out to Lighthouse Beach and the Schooner Cays. Most companies here are small and publish no street address; they collect you or meet you at a dock.
