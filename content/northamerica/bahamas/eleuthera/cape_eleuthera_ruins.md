The Cape Eleuthera Resort ruins, at the south-western tip of the island, are a moody walk through a 1970s luxury complex slowly being eaten by the Bahamian bush. Empty villas, a derelict marina, an overgrown golf course, and palms growing through tile floors.

A new sustainable resort and the Island School research centre have set up nearby, but the ruins themselves are unguarded and free to wander. Watch your step; the structures are unsafe in places. The marina has been partially restored and there is a bonefishing dock with views across the cape.
