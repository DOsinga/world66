---
title: "Eating Out"
type: section
---

Eleuthera eats fresh from the sea. **Conch** (in salads, fritters, and chowders), **grouper**, **snapper**, and **lobster** turn up on every menu. The Friday-night **fish fry at Governor's Harbour** is the biggest weekly food event on the island — grilled fish, conch fritters, peas and rice, beer, and music. Smaller fish fries run at Anchor Bay (Gregory Town) on Wednesday evenings.

Beyond the fish fries, look out for **Tippy's** on the beach south of Governor's Harbour for casual lunches; **The Cove Eleuthera** for a more upmarket beachside meal; and the **Rainbow Inn** for an unpretentious Bahamian dinner. On Harbour Island, **Sip Sip** is the long-standing local favourite. Most independent restaurants on the island are family-run and may have shifting hours — call ahead.
