A tour company in South Eleuthera that also works the cruise-ship stop at Princess Cays. Its land tour, **Da Real Bahamian T'ing**, is a day of local food and settlements. On the water it runs boat trips to Lighthouse Beach at the island's southern tip and to the Schooner Cays, and it rents kayaks and fishing gear for going it alone.

No street address is published; the pin is on Rock Sound.
